There is one who says that if heart fat is stuck to the heart and very tightly adherent — they separate it with a knife; and if a blood clot is found there or in the thickness of the heart — forbidden. And needless to say if found in its cavity. {Rama: If the blood clotted in the thickness of the flesh — behold it is like a blood clot and treifah (Or Zarua); see above at end of siman 37 regarding law of heart that adhered to another place.}
