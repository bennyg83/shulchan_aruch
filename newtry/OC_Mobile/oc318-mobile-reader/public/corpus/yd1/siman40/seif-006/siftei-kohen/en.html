There is one who says, etc. Though not brought in Beit Yosef, one who disagrees wrote in language "there is one who says" because it is not reasonable to declare treifah since blow did not reach cavity; and so Maharshal there: were it not for Raavyah's acceptance he would kosher even drop of blood since blow did not reach heart cavity — for even if entire heart flawed, nevertheless did not reach inside — end.
And needless to say, etc. Requires study — behold in every heart there is blood in cavity; and Mordechai's language and Hagahot Ashiri: thus I saw brought before our teacher my father that heart fat was very stuck, cut fat from heart where stuck, saw no flaw outside where stuck, afterward cut where stuck until cavity, saw in thickness not in cavity blood clot and forbade — end (Beit Yosef brings it). Implies he saw in thickness; in cavity saw nothing — there blood clot neither raises nor lowers; and so Orach Chaim general rule 51 law 41: forbade only when found in thickness — see his words; and perhaps Mechaber meant: when found in thickness and did not spread to cavity — treifah; and needless to say if in thickness and spread to cavity — and examine well.
