If a needle is found in the heart even though it is not recognizable from outside — treifah. And the same law if found in terefes of the heart. {Rama: However, if found only in the large bronchus of the heart with the pouch facing outside — that is, toward the side of the heart cavity — if the head of the needle is like a date kernel — kosher (it is my humble opinion in explanation of Bahag) — for we attribute that it entered via the esophagus to the bronchus of the heart, and since its head is downward its end will not puncture and the animal will expel it via coughing and shaking.}
