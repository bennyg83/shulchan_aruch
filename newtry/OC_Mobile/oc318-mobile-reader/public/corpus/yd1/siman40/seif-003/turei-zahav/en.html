However if not found, etc. This he wrote as explanatory on Bahag's words, and it is astonishing — for according to this the latter clause would contradict the former, for he began with placed in small bronchus and specifically its point downward — in two adverse factors then treifah; and in the kosher case he wrote specifically in two favorable factors — placed in large bronchus with pouch downward. And further: why would it be treifah because its end will puncture — in any case now there is no puncture. There are many different explanations; and what appears to me in Bahag's words: "if pouch downward and did not enter" — its meaning: or it did not enter; and we find such often; and in the version Beit Yosef brings in name of Teshuvot HaRashba it is written "it entered" — it appears the word "or" is missing and it must read "or that it entered." Explanation of the simple sense in Bahag's words thus: first we premise that we find a distinction between lung and liver per Beit Yosef in name of Rashba's responsum siman 295: in liver they were concerned with needle found there lest via esophagus entrance its point made a puncture on the way — whenever the point is downward, since it travels a long path; not so in lung, which is close to the canal and from canal to lung is a wide place — therefore permitted in lung if it is fine after examination; and so Rashba in the long Teshuvot HaRashba daf 36. Accordingly we might say in heart too it is close to the canal as our eyes see heart and lung are on one plane — if so we should not be concerned if needle found in heart as in lung; therefore Bahag came and explained heart law: there is distinction — if the animal has presumption to be punctured by this needle, such as placed where it will certainly puncture eventually — this helps concern lest it already punctured upon entering heart, place of entry via canal; since this needle has bad presumption. But if its end is not certainly to puncture — we say leniently from being close as we say regarding lung, since close to canal; and even though regarding lung too its end would puncture for there is no exit — nevertheless kosher in all cases — this case is different, for there it can be clarified by examination, not so in heart. Therefore Bahag said well: treifah only in two adverse factors — found in bronchus of heart inwardly, for then no place to exit and end will puncture — we say perhaps it already punctured; and specifically in first case pouch downward, for it appears fit to puncture — we say when it came it punctured heart and is stricter than lung though close like lung. But if one adverse factor, such as pouch downward and placed inwardly, or it entered large bronchus of heart alone — heart canal going from esophagus to heart and needle did not go into heart at all — we attribute leniently and say it made no puncture. In first distinction — inside and pouch downward — obviously kosher, for impossible to puncture in pouch; but even in second distinction — in large bronchus outside heart with mouth downward — also kosher, for from large bronchus obviously no concern since it is wide as regarding lung; only we might concern lest it went into heart, punctured, and returned backward to bronchus; and though somewhat distant, we might concern from presumption end will puncture — here there is no certain end to puncture, for perhaps it will be expelled via coughing since placed in still-wide place above heart flesh — therefore we do not establish prohibition at all lest it exited from there, etc. Difficulty: why distinguish from liver where Tur siman 41 says treifah with pouch outside because way of esophagus, and here we are not concerned and permit pouch downward? Answer: there it is impossible via canal — in pouch cannot enter this flesh — not so; thin needle forbidden in all cases lest entered via esophagus — so here too concern from doubt. Bahag explains liver as Teshuvot HaRashba long: pouch outside means toward outside of cavity — treifah from puncture in its travel as we cite below siman 41; we do not concern it entered via esophagus. Per our words, when inside large bronchus mouth downward — kosher; but Rama wrote treifah; and per his explanation Moharach z"l wrote: if bone found in heart I accept incident in Ashkenaz ox and sages declared kosher because so in deer heart when blood hardened becomes bone, possibly also in animal — end. I say: if acceptance — accept; if law has answer: even in deer if placed as with needle would be forbidden from end puncturing — why permit if can puncture; what helps us blood of heart hardened; ultimately it punctures like needle — why more kosher than needle; and even if deer kosher — from where learn other species; Rashba siman 52 regarding fell into fire seif 4: vegetables do not disqualify liver of domestic fowl in its species — implies other fowl not in its species no help though both birds; kal vachomer not learn ox from deer; and Tur siman 42: one does not learn species from non-species — therefore appears one should not be lenient without basis from poskim.
