However, etc. So is Rav's view in Bahag's explanation; and Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 12, Derishah, Atzei Tzvi, Bach, and other acharonim explained Bahag's words in several different ways; and Bach concludes: for practical law we are concerned for all explanations — when found inside heart — treifah, whether pouch toward head or toward cavity; in large bronchus pouch toward head — treifah; kosher only in large bronchus pouch toward cavity, specifically when no proof it was inside and returned; but if proof it returned — concern lest upon return heart or heart canal punctured and not recognizable. It appears to me per Rashba who saw Bahag's words are closed and can be explained several ways as explained — he took stringently in short Teshuvot HaRashba and wrote stam needle in heart treifah without distinction; nevertheless in large bronchus pouch toward cavity without proof it was inside and returned — implies all agree kosher. And if bone found in heart — I accept incident Ashkenaz ox and sages koshered because so in deer heart when blood hardened becomes bone, possibly also in animal — end; and see above siman 41.
