A poor person for whom they collected to complete his lack and they left over beyond what he needs — the remainder is his; and if they collected for poor people unstated and left over, they keep it for other poor; and likewise surplus of a captive belongs to that captive; and if they collected for captives unstated and left over, they keep it for other captives; and likewise surplus of the dead belongs to his heirs — surplus of the dead for the dead; and if the distributors saw a pressing need and wished to divert, the authority is in their hands.
