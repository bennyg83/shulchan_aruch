A Torah scholar, etc. As stated in the Yerushalmi at the end of Peah: Everything depends upon the honor appropriate to him. It relates there that Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov encountered a blind man from his town and sat below him. People said: Had he not been a distinguished person, Rabbi Eliezer ben Yaakov would not have sat below him. They consequently provided the blind man with an honorable livelihood, etc.
