One who sustained an orphan and intended it as a mitzvah — when the orphan grew up and claimed from him what he sustained him, he is exempt: {Rama: Even if the orphan had means at that time, unless he specified that he sustained him as a loan; and specifically an orphan — but another, even unstated, we also say he acted as a loan since he has property (Teshuvat Or Zaru'a); and see Choshen Mishpat siman 210 seif 25.}
