See in Sefer Bnei Ahuvah chapter 22 Laws of Marriage halachah 2 s.v. vehineh kashya who wrote if she died after he married her off — all concede the heirs acquired them; and so Masat Binyamin explicitly; and it is difficult — for this is like bridal gifts per Rabbenu Tam who can say I did not stipulate except on condition my daughter enjoy it (and as written in Even HaEzer siman 52 seif 4 in Hagahah); and one must say specifically when the father gives, etc. — see there.
