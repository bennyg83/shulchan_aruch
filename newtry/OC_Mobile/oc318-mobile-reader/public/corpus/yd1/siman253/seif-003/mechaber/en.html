One who had land — if he sells it in the rainy season he sells cheaply, and if he leaves it until summer he sells at fair value — they do not compel him to sell, but feed him from tithe for the poor until half their value, and he should not press himself and sell not at the time of sale (language of Rambam, chapter 9 of Laws of Gifts to the Poor, halacha 17): {Rama: (And some say they do not feed him except until he can sell his lands at half their value) (Tur and many other decisors).} If other people were buying at a high price and he cannot find one to buy from him except cheaply because he is pressed and troubled — they do not compel him to sell, but he eats tithe for the poor and goes until he sells at fair value and all know he is not pressed to sell.
