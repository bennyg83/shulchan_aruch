(Supplement) And some say, etc. This follows Tosafot's explanation there. Rashi likewise explains it this way according to his second formulation, although the textual readings are reversed under his interpretation. According to Rashi's first formulation, he explains it like the Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch, except that he applies it to a poor person; there too, the textual readings are reversed. The statement there, 'Rather, where the price increased, etc.,' applies specifically because he was negligent. Otherwise, even if the property is worth more than two hundred zuz, etc., he may receive. See Tosafot there in the continuation, and the Hagahot Ashri there, s.v. Mi, etc. Thus, there are four approaches here: two explanations of Rashi and two explanations of the Rif, with the textual readings reversed as stated above. (End of supplement.)
