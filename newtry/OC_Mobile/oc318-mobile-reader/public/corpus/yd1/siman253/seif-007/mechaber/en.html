Money collected to redeem a captive who died before he was redeemed — some say it belongs to his heirs, and some say his heirs did not acquire it; and this opinion is inclined in the present time, for we assess their intent that they did not donate with this understanding; and the same law applies if the captive was lost among the gentiles before he was redeemed: {Rama: And see Choshen Mishpat end of siman 283; and the same law if one vowed money to an orphan girl to marry her off and she died — the heirs did not acquire it (Mordechai, chapter Af Al Pi, in the name of R' Chaim and Rosh, klal 20); nevertheless, as long as she lives it belongs to the orphan girl and they must give her immediately and not wait until her marriage; and if she died the money returns; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 253 seif 16 — some disagree.}
