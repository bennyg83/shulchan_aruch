And she died. Bnei Ahuvah writes that if an orphan bride died after marriage, all agree that her heirs acquire the dowry. He distinguishes this from a father's gift conditioned upon his daughter's enjoyment.
For some disagree. Rama rules like the first opinion. Maharibal adds that even if the orphan's heirs seized the funds, they are removed from them because they cannot claim certainty like Raavyah. See Chatam Sofer.
