And there is an authority who says, etc. This applies only where the mitzvah for which the money was collected was performed and money remained. That differs from this case, where we say that the donors' intent was directed specifically to him. We find many such assessments of intent in the Gemara, as in Bava Batra 146b concerning one whose son traveled overseas, and similar cases. There. The Yerushalmi there likewise states: Surplus funds collected for the poor are used for the poor. If they collected for a particular person on the assumption that he had nothing, and it was discovered that he did have means, Rabbi Yirmiyah thought to say that the surplus collected for a deceased person belongs to his heirs. Rav Adda of Chatri said to him: Consider that the donor intended only for that particular person.
