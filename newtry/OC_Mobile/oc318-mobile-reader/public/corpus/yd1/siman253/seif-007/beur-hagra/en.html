So too in the Yerushalmi there: surplus for the poor goes to the poor. If they collected for him on the presumption that he had nothing and it was found that he did have, Rabbi Yirmeyah thought to say that surplus of the dead goes to his heirs. Rav Ada of Chatri said to him: consider that perhaps they intended only for him.
