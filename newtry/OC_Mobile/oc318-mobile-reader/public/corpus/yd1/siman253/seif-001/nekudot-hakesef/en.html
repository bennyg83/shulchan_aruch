(Siman 253, Taz seif katan 2) And he did not examine the sugya, and so forth. The reverse is true: Taz did not examine the sugya, but only Bach's abbreviated quotation of it. One who studies Shabbos 118 sees explicitly that Derishah is correct. According to the Sages there is no difficulty at all, because whatever one intends to eat at the conclusion of Shabbos he may eat on Shabbos itself, as Derishah writes. Rambam likewise states this explicitly in his commentary to the Mishnah at the end of Peah.
