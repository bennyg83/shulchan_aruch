(Siman 253, Taz, seif katan 2) He did not examine the sugya, etc. The reverse is true. It was he who did not examine the sugya, but relied only upon the Bach, who cites the sugya briefly. One who examines Shabbos 118 sees explicitly that the ruling follows the Perishah: according to the Sages there is no difficulty at all, for whatever he wishes to eat at the conclusion of Shabbos he eats on Shabbos itself, as the Perishah wrote. Rambam likewise states this explicitly in his commentary to the Mishnah at the end of Peah.
