Silver. In the Tur it is written that the same applies to a silver menorah and table, which he must sell. The Beit Yosef asks that the opinion holding so was refuted in the Shas. The Taz answers that there is a distinction between times: now that one table serves all who are dining, he certainly must sell it if it is silver; but in the time of the Shas, when each person had a small table before him, it was like eating utensils. The Rosh and other poskim write that if, after he received charity, these utensils came to him, such as by inheritance or find, we do not require him to sell them. Rashi further writes that if it became known he was wealthy at the time he took, beit din comes and collects from him what he took; although it is money with no claimant, nevertheless they collect from him as a penalty.
