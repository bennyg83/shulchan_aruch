It is called peah. Radbaz explains that the two-hundred-zuz limit applies to charity funds or gifts clearly given as charity, because accepting may deprive other poor people. Where everyone knows he owns more but gives him money as an ordinary gift to support his household, he may accept. Charity collectors, however, must not use communal funds for him. If he truly lacks two hundred zuz, they may give generously, provided other local poor are not displaced.
