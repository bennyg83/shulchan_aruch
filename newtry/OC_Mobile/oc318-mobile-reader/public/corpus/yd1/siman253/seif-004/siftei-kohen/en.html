He owes nothing. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that this applies not only when he received from a communal charity fund, but even when an ordinary individual supported him as an act of kindness and gave him all his needs, provided that he had no assets at that time. If, however, he had movable property or land, even if held by another person, he must repay when the benefactor demands payment. The exception is a poor orphan. The Sefer HaTerumos cites a responsum of the Rif that one who supports an orphan boy or girl in his home has no claim against them, even if they possess assets, unless he expressly stated that he was supporting them as a loan. Without such a stipulation, he cannot collect. The Beit Yosef cites this, and these are also the Rama's words in seif 5.
