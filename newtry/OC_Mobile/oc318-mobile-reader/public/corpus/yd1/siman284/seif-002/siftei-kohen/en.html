It is forbidden to write three words from a verse, etc. This is the correct reading.
Nevertheless, in a letter of greetings, etc. The Yerushalmi in the final chapter of Megillah implies somewhat that even in such a case it is forbidden. Some poskim write likewise.
If he ruled the upper line. He may write from that point onward without ruled lines, for the upper line was presumably written straight and serves like ruling for what is below it. Rosh and Tur write this.
He no longer needs to. An actual Sefer Torah, however, requires ruled lines throughout, as stated above in siman 271, seif 5. Hagahot Derishah writes likewise in the name of Maharshal. The remaining laws of a Sefer Torah are explained in Orach Chayyim in Hilkhot Tefillin, so I have been brief concerning them here.
