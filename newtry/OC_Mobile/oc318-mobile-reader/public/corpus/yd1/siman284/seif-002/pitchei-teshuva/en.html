Without ruled lines. See Responsa Teshuvah MeAhavah, volume 1, siman 67, who writes that one may write even many words without ruled lines by writing only two words on one line and only two words on the line beneath it. One may write any number of lines in this manner, unlike one rabbi who was uncertain about it. See there. See Tashbetz, volume 1, siman 2, who writes that R' Maimon, of blessed memory, the father of Rambam, wrote that one who wishes to write without ruled lines should place vowel points above the letters. The people of the Ishmaelite lands followed this practice, but I do not know the reason for it. See there, and see what I wrote above in siman 272, se'if katan 2.
