Forbidden to write three words from a verse — as should be. And that which is written "and if he ruled an upper line," etc. — in Tur it is written: that one does not write without ruling applies specifically to the upper line; but from here onward the upper line is like ruling, as we say regarding tefillin that if he ruled the upper line he no longer needs ruling, since ordinarily people know to train their hand to write on the upper without ruling — end. And it is written in Derishah: it is difficult — what is tefillin's relevance to sefer Torah; and moreover why therefore I say that Rabbenu Tam said the essence of his words is about tefillin as Tosafot and Beit Yosef bring; and what Rabbeinu wrote "since he ruled," etc. — he means Rabbenu Tam wrote so regarding tefillin and Rosh said "therefore I say," etc. — meaning as Rabbenu Tam said regarding tefillin that ruling is needed only upper line, so too here no ruling except upper line, as we say in tefillin and there it is proven from the difficulty Rabbenu Tam proved from it — end; and apart from his honor he was not precise in understanding these matters — not this way; rather the intent is clear it refers to a letter that no ruling is needed when writing a verse name except if in the first line; but if no verse in first line, one writes there without ruling; and when writing a verse in the next line, no ruling needed there either since the first was written without ruling permissibly — it becomes as if there were a verse in the first with ruling for this; therefore he wrote "as regarding tefillin," etc. — no ruling except upper; and on this he gave reason that ordinarily a person knows to train his hand — meaning when no verse in upper and he writes without ruling, nevertheless he knows to write straight; this appears simple to me except the commentator understood Rosh here refers to sefer Torah and learned from tefillin and entered to explain what is not given to write — and it seems that did not come from that tzaddik's mouth but a mistaken student wrote it.
