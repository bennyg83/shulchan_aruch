(There in Shulchan Aruch seif 2) Three words, etc. — so ruled most poskim and later authorities; so Bach; and it appears to me to bring proof for their words from end of Laws of Chalitzah where they say there Abaye wrote "get of chalitzah" and read for himself from "lo" until "lekachtah" so as not to require ruling lines; and so Mar Zutra ruled lines for the whole section even "lo chafatzti lekachtah" — only three words; apparently Abaye and Mar Zutra hold two one writes, three one does not write [and Rosh who wrote that which is said "and read for himself from lo until lekachtah" — I do not know why, for we hold three one writes] — it appears Rosh's intent is not to challenge Abaye and Mar Zutra, for there is one who says in first chapter of Gittin that three one does not write; and moreover Rosh himself ruled so in first chapter of Megillah that three one does not write; rather Rosh's intent is to challenge Rashi's explanation there who explained their reason is because we hold three one writes, four one does not write — and if so they should have written "lo chafatzti lekachtah" without ruling lines (and so one can challenge Nimukei Yosef who also wrote like Rashi — and examine well).
