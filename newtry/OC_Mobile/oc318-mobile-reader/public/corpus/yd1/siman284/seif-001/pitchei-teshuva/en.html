Three words. See responsum of Rashbash siman 482, who wrote that sometimes they write three; four they do not write. For example, the third word does not finish the matter, as "cold water upon" — the matter is not finished until he writes the word "nefesh"; but when three words finish the matter, as "holy water in a vessel" — even though he does not write the word "earthenware," since this is only for additional explanation it is forbidden; and with two words in all forms it is permitted, because it is not clarified whether they are secular or holy words — see there.
