Three words. See Responsa Rashbash, siman 482, who writes that sometimes three words may be written, but four may not. This applies when the third word does not complete the thought, as in “cold water upon,” where the thought is not complete until one writes the word “a weary soul.” When three words complete the thought, however, as in “holy water in a vessel,” it is forbidden even if one does not write the word “earthenware,” since that word merely provides additional clarification. Two words are permitted in every circumstance, because it is not evident whether they are ordinary words or sacred words. See there.
