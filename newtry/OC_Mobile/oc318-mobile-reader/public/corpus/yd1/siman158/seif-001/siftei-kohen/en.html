That there is no war etc. But in time of war they would kill them directly, for we say “the best of the idolaters” etc. — so the Bach ruled, bringing Tosafot who wrote so; and so Beit Yosef.
We do not bring about etc. Meaning there is no mitzvah to kill them even though they transgress the seven Noahide commandments; however where they do observe the seven commandments it implies it is forbidden to cause their death — Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe; and Maharshal in his commentary on Semag proved that the interpretation is that it is forbidden to cause their death — see there; but when you examine the Gemara chapter Ein Mekabel (26b) you will see that his proof amounts to nothing.
Such as shepherds of small cattle etc. Specifically regarding shepherds they were stringent because they are not invalidated by Torah law, and the Sages strengthened their rulings so that they would take heed and repent; but Torah robbers we do help up — Tosafot; and Beit Yosef brings it; and see above siman 62.
Who acted lawlessly in robbery. Because they would graze their animals in others’ fields; and in Eretz Yisrael at the time when most fields belonged to Israelites.
