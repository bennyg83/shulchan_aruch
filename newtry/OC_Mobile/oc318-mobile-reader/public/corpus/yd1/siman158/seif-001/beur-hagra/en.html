Therefore one should not heal them. Avodah Zarah there — 'to exclude [healing] for the purpose of morna,' etc.; and see Tosafot there s.v. 'to exclude,' etc. — that for payment it is permitted; and likewise Rashi there; but Ramban wrote that wherever there is no [concern of] enmity, even for payment it is forbidden — as written there 1: 'R' Yochanan considered saying: nursemaids,' etc.; 'R' Yehoshua of Lod considered,' etc.; 'to assist at childbirth for payment,' etc.; and the case in Gittin [applies] where there is no way to avoid it; and as written there: 'a midwife for payment is permitted,' etc.; and even for free, where there is enmity it is permitted — for we say in Gittin 61: 'we visit the sick,' etc.; and visiting the sick is a form of healing; and likewise 'we sustain,' etc. — and this is what is written: that then, etc. (Supplement) That then even for free, etc. And so it is in Mordechai; but Rambam wrote: specifically for payment it is permitted because of enmity; and so it is implied there in the Gemara — and everything depends on the circumstances (end).
