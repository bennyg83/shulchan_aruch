And terumat ma'aser. He sells it to the priest and takes its monetary value, as stated in the Rambam:
Because he says to the Levite. Regarding the tithe, and to the priest regarding terumat ma'aser: "I come from the authority of one from whom you can take nothing." And why did they say he should not give terumat ma'aser to the priest as terumah gedolah? Because it is written regarding terumat ma'aser: "when you take from the children of Israel" — untithed produce [tevel] that you take from an Israelite, you separate terumat ma'aser from it and give it to the priest; but untithed produce that you take from a non-Jew, you do not give the terumat ma'aser that was separated from it to the priest — rather you sell it to the priest and take its monetary value. Rambam. And the reason he sells it to the priest is because terumat ma'aser has sanctity and is forbidden to non-priests, so he sells it to the priest.
