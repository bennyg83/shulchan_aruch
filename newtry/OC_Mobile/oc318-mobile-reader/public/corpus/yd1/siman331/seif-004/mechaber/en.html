The fruits of a non-Jew that grew on land he bought in Eretz Yisrael—if their work was completed in the hand of the non-Jew and he trod them, they are exempt from all of them. And if an Israelite took them after they were detached before their work was completed and he completed their work, they are obligated. And he separates terumah and ma'aser rishon and terumat ma'aser, and leaves for himself ma'aser rishon and it is his, because he says to the Levi, "I come by virtue of a man" that you cannot take from him.
