From whom you can take nothing. The Rambam concludes there in these words: And why did they say he should not give terumat ma'aser to the priest as terumah gedolah? Because it is written regarding terumat ma'aser: "when you take from the children of Israel" — untithed produce that you take from an Israelite, you separate terumat ma'aser from it and give it to the priest; but untithed produce that you take from a non-Jew, you do not give to the priest the terumat ma'aser that was separated — rather you sell it to the priest.
