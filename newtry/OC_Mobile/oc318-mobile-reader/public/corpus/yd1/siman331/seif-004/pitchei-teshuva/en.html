He sells it to a kohen. See Tur at the end of this siman, who writes that the same applies to all terumat maaser of demai: one may sell it to a kohen and need not give it to him without payment. See Tiferet Yisrael on Mishnah, by the Gaon Av Beit Din of Dessau, Demai 1:1, who cites Rabbi Akiva Eiger as raising many difficulties against the Tur and maintaining that it must be given to the kohen without payment. He repeats this in his name on Demai 4:4 and Orlah 2:1. The author of Tiferet Yisrael resolves those difficulties. See also Responsa Rabbi Akiva Eiger, additions to siman 30, on this subject.
