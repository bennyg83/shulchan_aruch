Just as it is permitted to eat casually from fruits whose work was not completed, so it is permitted to feed them to a beast, animal, and birds as much as he wishes, and to declare them hefker as much as he wishes before he tithes. And if he completed them, even though they were not fixed for ma'aser, he may not declare them hefker nor feed a beast, animal, or birds a fixed meal until he tithes.
