One who exchanges with his fellow—this one to eat and that one to eat—both are fixed for ma'asrot, for behold they took detached. This one to cut and that one to cut—both are not fixed, for sale does not fix a thing whose work was not completed. One took the fruits he exchanged to eat, and one took his exchange to cut them—this one who took to eat is obligated to tithe, and this one who took to cut is not fixed for ma'aser:
