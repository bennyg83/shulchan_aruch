The courtyard. A house fixes produce for tithing by Torah law, as will be explained, while the remaining factors do so by rabbinic law. Scripture says, 'I have removed the sacred portion from the house.' Since removal must be from the house, we infer that produce is not fixed until brought into the house. A courtyard is derived from, 'They shall eat within your gates and be satisfied,' implying that further eating is forbidden. One might ask: If so, why is a house needed, since a courtyard would teach it all the more? The verse is needed to teach that the courtyard must resemble a house: just as a house is guarded, so too only a guarded courtyard fixes the produce.
