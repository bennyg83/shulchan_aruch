**And Shabbat.** See Sha'ar HaMelech, chapter 5 of Hilchot Ma'aser, law 22, who wrote that just as Shabbat establishes for ma'aser, so too Yom Tov — for Yom Tov is also called Shabbat; and furthermore, for the reason Shabbat establishes for ma'aser is that it is written “and you shall call Shabbat a delight,” and if so, for this reason the same law applies to Yom Tov as well, for there is a mitzvah to delight also on Yom Tov; and he brought further proofs; and afterward he wrote in the name of the Meiri of blessed memory, who wrote that Yom Tov does not establish — see there. And so I saw in Sefer Tzelach on Beitzah at the end of chapter 4, who wrote that specifically Shabbat establishes for ma'aser but not Yom Tov; and so is the view of the Penei Yehoshua at the end of that aforementioned tractate; and he wrote the reason: that on Yom Tov we do not find oneg, only simchah — “and you shall rejoice on your festival” — and this simchah is meat of shelamim, a matter to which ma'aser and terumah do not apply at all — see there.
