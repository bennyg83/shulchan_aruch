Terumah and terumat maaser for all of it, and he separates, etc. It is certain that he must separate maaser from all of it, for it is impossible to separate terumat maaser from all of it unless he first separates maaser from all of it. Rather, what we say in the latter clause, “according to calculation,” means that he need give the Levite and the poor only according to calculation, while the rest he mixes with his produce. But terumah and terumat maaser, which are forbidden to non-kohanim, he gives entirely to the kohen. So wrote the Rash in chapter 3 of Challah.
