One who says to his fellow, "Here is an issar for you and give me five figs for it"—he eats one by one and is exempt; and if he combined them, he is obligated to tithe. "Here is an issar for you for twenty figs that I will select for myself"—he selects one by one and eats from a cluster that he selects for himself berry by berry on the tree and eats; from a pomegranate that he selects for himself he picks it on the tree and eats; from a watermelon that he selects for himself he cuts its end in the ground and eats. And if he cut the figs and combined them, or cut the cluster or the watermelon, he is obligated to tithe, for behold he acquired only what was detached. But if he said to him, "Here is an issar for these twenty figs, for these two clusters, for these two pomegranates, for these two watermelons"—he cuts in his usual way and eats casually and is exempt, for they were not fixed by sale, for behold he took them attached:
