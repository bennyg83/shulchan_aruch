For one does not, etc. The Yerushalmi there says: Shmuel said that this follows Rabbi Meir, for Rabbi Meir says that a gift is not like a sale. Rabbi Yose said that it is according to all opinions. I asked the household of Rabbi Yannai, and they said: we were accustomed to give these to those in the field, and they would eat without tithing. What is the reason? It follows the view that they accepted the tithes upon themselves voluntarily. The Rambam follows his own position, for that is his view as written in seif 2.
