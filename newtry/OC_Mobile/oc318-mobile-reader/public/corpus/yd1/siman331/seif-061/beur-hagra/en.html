One may not separate terumah, etc. Produce purchased from another is subject to tithes only by rabbinic law, as stated in chapter 7 of Bava Metzia (88a). One may not separate terumah from produce obligated by Torah law for produce obligated only by rabbinic law, nor the reverse, as stated in the Tosefta: one may not separate from produce of Eretz Yisrael for produce of Syria, and so forth, as explained above in seif katan 21. See also Menachot 31a: he holds that ownership by a non-Jew does not exempt, etc.
