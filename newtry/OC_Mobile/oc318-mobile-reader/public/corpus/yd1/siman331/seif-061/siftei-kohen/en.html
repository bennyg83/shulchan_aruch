One does not separate terumah from purchased produce, etc. Because purchased produce, even from a Jew, is obligated only rabbinically and not by Torah law, whereas what grows in one's field is obligated by Torah law, as it says, "the produce of your seed," excluding what is purchased. It is therefore like separating from what is exempt for what is obligated, or the reverse. Likewise, produce purchased from a non-Jew does not have the same obligation as produce purchased from a Jew.
