Purchased produce. Even produce purchased from a Jew is obligated only rabbinically, whereas produce grown in one's own field is obligated by Torah law, as indicated by the phrase “the produce of your seed,” excluding purchased produce. Separating from one for the other is therefore like separating from exempt produce for obligated produce, or the reverse. Produce purchased from a non-Jew also does not have the same status as produce purchased from a Jew.
