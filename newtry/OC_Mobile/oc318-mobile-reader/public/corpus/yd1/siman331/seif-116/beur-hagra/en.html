And even produce, etc. This follows the Sages who disagree with Rabbi Akiva. As stated at the beginning of Maaserot chapter 5 in the Yerushalmi: one who uproots seedlings, etc. Rabbi Abbahu said in the name of Reish Lakish: this is Rabbi Akiva, for we learned there that one may take from the threshing floor and sow, and it is exempt from maaser until he smooths the pile. Rabbi Chiyya said in the name of Rabbi Yochanan: it is according to all opinions, for the Sages concede to Rabbi Akiva regarding seedlings; wheat has reached completion of work, but seedlings have not. The Rambam wrote this in his commentary there, and this is the meaning of "when is this said," etc.
If he intended, etc. As stated there, "for seed." See the Rambam's commentary there. It is a dispute in the Yerushalmi there: Rabbi Mana said, this was taught only for seed, but if for eating it is exempt. Rabbi Chiyya said in the name of Rabbi Yochanan: there is no difference between seed and eating; it is obligated because this is its threshing floor. He rules like Rabbi Mana, because the plain sense of the Mishnah implies like him.
