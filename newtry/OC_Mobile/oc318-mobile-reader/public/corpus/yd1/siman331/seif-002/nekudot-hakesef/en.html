(Siman 331, in Taz seif katan 1) This requires study regarding Tur. There is no difficulty. Tur's view is correct, for even according to the opinion that terumah in the present era is required by Torah law, challah is rabbinic, since the verse says 'when you come,' meaning when all of you come. Thus 'when you come' refers specifically to challah, and terumah is not derived from it. Even according to the view that terumah is rabbinic, that is for other reasons, as explained by Tosafot and the other authorities, not because of 'when you come.' Raavad in his glosses likewise holds this view.
