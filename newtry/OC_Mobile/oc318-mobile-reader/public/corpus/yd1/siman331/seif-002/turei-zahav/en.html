The Tur objects to the Rambam, who holds that regarding terumah as well we require the arrival of all Israel, saying that this is incorrect because that requirement applies only to challah, concerning which the Torah states, “when you come.” I do not understand the objection. In the second chapter of Ketubot, where this requirement is derived regarding challah, the Gemara states explicitly: even according to the one who says that terumah nowadays is required by Torah law, challah is only rabbinic, for Scripture states “when you come,” meaning when all of you come. Since according to the view that terumah is a Torah obligation we must distinguish between terumah and challah, saying that only with challah did the Torah insist upon the arrival of all Israel, it follows that according to the accepted ruling that terumah nowadays is rabbinic, terumah and challah are equal in this respect. The Tur therefore requires study.
