He sells it to a kohen. See the Tur at the end of this siman, who wrote that the same applies to all terumat maaser of demai: one sells it to a kohen and need not give it for free. See Tiferet Yisrael on Mishnah by the Gaon, av beit din of Dessau, in Demai chapter 1 Mishnah 1, who wrote in the name of the pious Gaon Rabbi Akiva Eiger z"l that he raised many difficulties against the Tur, and his view is that one must give it for free. He wrote further in his name there, chapter 4 Mishnah 4, and in Orlah chapter 2 Mishnah 1; the author resolves his difficulties there. See also Responsa Rabbeinu Akiva Eiger, omissions to siman 30, on this matter.
