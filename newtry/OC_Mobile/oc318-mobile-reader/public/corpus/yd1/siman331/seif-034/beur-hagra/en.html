One who says, etc. See Tosafos in Eruvin, daf 32a, s.v. R' Shimon, and according to Rabbeinu Tam, etc.
And some say, etc. The Tur derives this from Tosafos there, who write in the name of R' Yitzchak, etc.; the Rosh there likewise writes this, following R' Shimshon of Coucy there. But the words of the Tur and the Rama are impossible to reconcile, for in Chullin there the Gemara says, "and he found it separated as terumah," and their words themselves are not understood. I likewise found that the Mishneh LaMelech was astonished by them.
