One who says to his agent, "Go and separate terumah for me," and he went to separate terumah and does not know whether he separated, and he came and found a stack—its presumption is not terumah; we are concerned perhaps another separated without permission: {Rama: And some say that in such a case its presumption is terumah, for we say the presumption is that an agent performs his agency even in a d'oraisa prohibition; but if he does not know whether the stack is terumah or not, it is forbidden to eat from it, for the agent says he certainly did not touch it until he knows it is terumah (Tur in the name of the Rosh and R. Shimon).}
