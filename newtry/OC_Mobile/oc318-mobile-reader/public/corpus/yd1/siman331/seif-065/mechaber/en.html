One who separates terumah from a barrel of wine for wine and it is found to be vinegar—if it is known that it was vinegar before he separated terumah, it is not terumah; and if it soured after he separated terumah, behold this is terumah. If in doubt—it is terumah, and he returns and separates terumah. And likewise one who separates a gourd and it is found bitter, a watermelon and it is found rotten:
