It is permitted, etc. A house establishes produce for tithing by Torah law only after the produce has been completed in the pile. Before smoothing the pile, bringing it into the house does not establish it, because terumah and maaser are stated regarding grain, meaning after final processing. The Sages prohibited a fixed meal from unfinished produce, but casual eating remains permitted. Therefore the circumstances described here do not create a Torah obligation, and the rabbinic restriction does not extend to casual eating.
