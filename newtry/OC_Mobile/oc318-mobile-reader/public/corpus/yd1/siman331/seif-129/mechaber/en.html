Everything exempt from ma'aser rishon is exempt from ma'aser sheni and from ma'aser ani; and everything obligated in rishon is obligated in both of them. And everything that separates ma'aser takes out ma'aser; and everything that does not separate ma'aser does not take out ma'aser. And everyone for whom if he separated terumah his terumah is terumah—so too if he tithed, behold it is ma'aser. And everyone whose terumah is not terumah—so too if he took out ma'aser, it is not ma'aser:
