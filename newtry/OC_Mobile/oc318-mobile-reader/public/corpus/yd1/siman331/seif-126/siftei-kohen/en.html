A vegetable's tithing follows its picking. For it grows from all water and draws moisture at every moment until it is picked.
And if it was picked in the fourth year. One separates maaser sheni from it.
It is like a vegetable. For it dwells on the tree from year to year and draws nourishment at every moment.
And although, etc., it is obligated in maasrot. For regarding shemittah we follow chanatah, as is the law with all other trees. Since this is not considered produce of shemittah, for it reached chanatah in the sixth year, we consider it entirely sixth-year produce.
