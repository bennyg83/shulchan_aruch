A vegetable—at the time of its harvest is its tithing. How so? If it was harvested on Rosh Hashanah of the third year—even though it reached the season of ma'asrot and was completed in the second year, one separates from it ma'aser ani; and if it was harvested in the fourth year, ma'aser sheni. And likewise the etrog alone from the rest of the fruits of the tree—behold it is like a vegetable and we follow its harvest, both for ma'aser and for shevi'it. How so? If it was harvested in the third year after the 15th of Shevat, one separates from it ma'aser ani, even though it was completed in the second year. And likewise if it was harvested in the fourth year before the 15th of Shevat, one separates from it ma'aser ani. If it was harvested in the fifth year after the 15th of Shevat, one separates from it ma'aser sheni—even though we follow its harvest.
