A partnership with a non-Jew is obligated in terumah and ma'asrot. How so? An Israelite and a non-Jew who took a field in partnership—even if they divided the field while standing, and needless to say if they divided the stack—behold this is tevel and chullin mixed in every stalk and stalk of the portion of the non-Jew, even though the non-Jew trod them, and they are obligated from their words. In what case? In Eretz Yisrael, where the ma'asrot are of Torah, and in that of Torah there is no clarification; but if they took a field in Syria, since the ma'asrot there are from their words—even if they divided the stack, the portion of the non-Jew is exempt from everything.
