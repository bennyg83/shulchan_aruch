A non-Jew's partnership is obligated. Even though the produce is still in the non-Jew's possession — and while in the non-Jew's possession we say that a non-Jew has acquisitional rights in the Land of Israel to exempt immediately from tithes, as it is written: "your grain" [deganekha] and not the grain of a non-Jew, and the same applies to "your grain" that is exclusively yours and not where a non-Jew has a partnership share in it — nevertheless we do not exempt the partnership on that basis, by saying that in each and every stalk the non-Jew has a share and that share exempts it. For the grain is as if it were divided, and we apply the verse "your grain" to the Israelite's portion; rather, since we rule that in matters of Torah law we do not apply bereirah, and even if they divided, we do not say it is now clarified that this is the non-Jew's portion and that is the Israelite's portion.
