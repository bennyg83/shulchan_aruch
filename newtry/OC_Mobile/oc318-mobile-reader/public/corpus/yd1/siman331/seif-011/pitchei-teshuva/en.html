And Shabbat. See Shaar HaMelekh, chapter 5 of Hilkhot Maaser, halakhah 22, who wrote that just as Shabbat establishes produce for maaser, so too Yom Tov, for Yom Tov is also called Shabbat. Furthermore, the reason Shabbat establishes for maaser is because it is written "and you shall call Shabbat a delight"; if so, for this reason Yom Tov has the same law, since there is also a mitzvah to delight in Yom Tov. He brought further proofs, and then wrote in the name of the Meiri z"l that Yom Tov does not establish; see there. I also saw in Tzelach on Beitzah at the end of chapter 4 that specifically Shabbat establishes for maaser, but not Yom Tov, and that this is the view of Pnei Yehoshua at the end of that tractate. He wrote the reason: with Yom Tov we do not find "delight," only joy, "you shall rejoice on your festival," and that joy means the meat of shelamim, a matter to which maaser and terumah do not apply at all; see there.
