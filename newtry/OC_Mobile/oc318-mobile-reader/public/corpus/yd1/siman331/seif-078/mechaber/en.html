One who separates terumah gedolah or terumat ma'aser blesses before separating: "...Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to separate terumah." And likewise one who separates ma'aser rishon blesses before separating ma'aser; and likewise he blesses on ma'aser sheni.
