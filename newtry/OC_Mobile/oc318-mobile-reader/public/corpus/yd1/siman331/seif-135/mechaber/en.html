Just as we do not eat ma'aser sheni at this time in Jerusalem, so we do not redeem it there, nor profane it, nor sell it. And if it entered Jerusalem—even at this time—we do not take it out from there, and we leave it there until it rots. [And likewise if he transgressed and took it out from there, we leave it until it rots.] Therefore one does not separate ma'aser sheni in Jerusalem at this time, but takes the fruits out tevel outside the city and separates it there and redeems it. And if he separated them there at this time, they rot. In what case? In ma'aser sheni that did not become tamei; but if it became tamei, one redeems it in Jerusalem.
