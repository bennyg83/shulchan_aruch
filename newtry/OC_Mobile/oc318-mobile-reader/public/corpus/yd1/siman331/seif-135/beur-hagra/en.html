Rather, they take it out, etc.; according to R. Eliezer, etc.; and some, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 3, halachah 6, records that R. Zeira, R. Chanina, R. Yonatan, and R. Yehoshua ben Levi went up to Jerusalem. Produce came to them, and they wished to redeem it outside the boundary. An elder told them that their fathers did not act so, but rather took it outside the wall and redeemed it there. The elder held that the walls are viewed as though they still rise, while the Sages held they are not. The elder followed R. Yehoshua and the Sages R. Eliezer. R. Pinchas rendered it impure and redeemed it, taking both views into account. R. Yaakov bar Idi said in the name of R. Yehoshua ben Levi that the law follows the disciple. The dispute is whether Jerusalem's sanctity ceased in the present era, corresponding to the dispute of R. Eliezer and R. Yehoshua. R. Pinchas was concerned both for R. Yehoshua, that sanctity remains, and for the first Tanna, that produce whose work was completed should not be removed from Jerusalem but returned. R. Yehoshua ben Levi ruled like the disciple, meaning R. Shimon ben Yehudah in the Mishnah. We also rule like the elder that the sanctity did not cease. See above seif 20.
