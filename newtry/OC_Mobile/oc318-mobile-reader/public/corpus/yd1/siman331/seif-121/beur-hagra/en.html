(Likkut) And one who receives, etc. He explains like Rashi in Bava Metzia 101a, s.v. yesh kinyan and s.v. ve-i nami ein kinyan, etc. Tosafot, however, explain there that specifically because there is acquisition, a sharecropper is not like a renter; it is all one matter: there is acquisition, etc., and a sharecropper, etc. See there, s.v. savruha, where they write: so it appears to the Ri to explain, etc. So too Sefer HaTerumah writes, and see there where he also brings the opinion of Rabbeinu Tam. The Rambam rules like Rabbi Yehudah, who does not disagree with the first Tanna, and Tosafot write this as well. Moreover, the Gemara discusses the matter according to him, as stated in Shabbat chapter 15: from the fact that Rabbi Yochanan answers, etc. The Rambam wrote all this in his commentary to the Mishnah there.
