One who wishes to separate terumah and terumat ma'aser at once—he separates one in a hundred and a little more, and says: "One in a hundred that is here—behold it is on this side that I separated, and behold it is chullin; and the remainder of what I separated, which is the slight excess over one in a hundred, is terumah on everything. And the ma'aser that ought to be for this hundred chullin—behold it is on this side that I separated; and this remainder more than the terumah from what I separated—behold it is terumat ma'aser on everything:
