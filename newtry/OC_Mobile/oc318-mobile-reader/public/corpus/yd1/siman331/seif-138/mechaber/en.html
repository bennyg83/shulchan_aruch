One redeems ma'aser sheni only with money; and likewise if he redeemed his ma'aser sheni that he adds a fifth—the fifth shall be only money like the principal. And he does not redeem with money that is not coin but with minted money that has a form or writing on it. And if he redeemed with the language of money and the like, which is called asimon, he has done nothing. And one does not redeem with less than a perutah, because it is like redeeming with asimon. And one does not redeem with a coin that does not circulate at that time and in that place. And he does not redeem with money that is not in his possession. And it is possible that these matters are said only when the Temple stood; but now, when ma'aser sheni is not eaten, one need not be strict about this; nevertheless one should be concerned about the matter, and even at this time one should not redeem ma'aser sheni except on money that has circulating coinage and is in his possession, or profane it on other fruits:
