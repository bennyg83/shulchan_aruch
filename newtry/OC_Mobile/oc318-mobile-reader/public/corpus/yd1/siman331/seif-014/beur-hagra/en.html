One does not, etc. Yerushalmi at the end of Challah: Rabbi Yochanan said, our rabbis in the diaspora would separate terumot and maasrot until the roviyyin came and nullified them. Who are the roviyyin? They translated: Rabbi Zeira. Rav Yehudah in the name of Shmuel: challah of the diaspora and terumah of the diaspora, one eats and afterward separates. Rabbi Ba in the name of Shmuel: they were concerned only for terumah of grain, wine, and oil. Rabbi Hila in the name of Shmuel: they were concerned only for terumah, but for vegetables, even for terumah gedolah they were not concerned, as taught: Isi ben Akavya says, for vegetables it is by their words. See Tosafot Chullin 7a and Avodah Zarah 59a.
And likewise vegetables, etc. There: why did they not decree on vetches? In their shoes they came out with them from Alexandria.
