From vegetables. Even in Eretz Yisrael the obligation is only rabbinic, for Scripture says, “the produce of your seed,” and vegetables are not classified as produce in this sense. They are likewise not obligated in terumah, concerning which Scripture says, “your grain.” When grain or legumes are planted for their greens, the planter's unusual intention is disregarded. Their greens are exempt, while the seed is obligated in terumah and tithes.
