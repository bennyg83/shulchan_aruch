Fruits that reached the season of ma'asrot and were detached and their work is not yet completed—such as grain that was reaped and threshed but not yet winnowed or trodden—it is permitted to eat from them a casual eating until their work is completed. And once their work is completed, it is forbidden to eat from them casually—in what case? When he completes his fruits to sell them in the market; but if his intent was to bring them to the house, behold this is permitted to eat from them casually after their work was completed until they are fixed for ma'aser:
