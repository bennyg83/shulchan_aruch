One who separates part of ma'aser—it is not ma'aser, but like one who divided the pile; but he must take out his ma'aser from this portion. How so? He had a hundred se'ah and separated five of them for ma'aser—it is not ma'aser, and he cannot separate ma'aser on the five se'ah from another place, but from them half a se'ah, which is their ma'aser:
