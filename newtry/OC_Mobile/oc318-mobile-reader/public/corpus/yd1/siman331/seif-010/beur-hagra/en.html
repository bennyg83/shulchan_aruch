An Israelite, and so forth. The Yerushalmi at the end of Demai chapter 6 discusses an Israelite with an am ha'aretz sharecropper in Syria who sends him produce and says it is tithed. We assume he purchased it in the market, provided that species is available there, even if he also grows it in his field. He is believed through the principle that the mouth that created the prohibition is the mouth that permits. Certainly this applies if he sent it without saying it came from his own field. The Rambam therefore states the rule without qualification.
