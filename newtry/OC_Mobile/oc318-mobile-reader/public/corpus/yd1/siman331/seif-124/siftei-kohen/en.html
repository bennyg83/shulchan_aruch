One who gives his field. Planted [and given] to non-Jews in tenancy — the non-Jew accepted upon himself to work it and give the Israelite who gave it to him from the produce whatever it yields, whether much or little — a half, a third, or whatever they agreed upon between them:
He must tithe through them. For they certainly did not tithe. But if he gave [the produce] to an ordinary Israelite [stam Yisra'el], even an am ha-aretz, etc., he need not tithe — since he relies on the majority of amei ha-aretz who do tithe.
