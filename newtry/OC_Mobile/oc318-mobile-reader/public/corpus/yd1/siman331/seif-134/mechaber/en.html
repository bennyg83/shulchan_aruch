And the measure of a perutah is like half a barley grain of refined silver, which is a part of 1/32 of a silver ma'ah, which is an Ashpira Ottomani whose weight is a quarter dirham; thus the perutah is a part of 1/48 of a kate'a and a little more, and one must be precise that the fruits on which he profanes ma'aser sheni are worth at least so much. (And see above siman 294 seif 6.)
