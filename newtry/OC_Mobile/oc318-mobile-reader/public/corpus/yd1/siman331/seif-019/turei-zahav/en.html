And he may leave it and burn it. We do not fear that he may come to derive benefit from it, even if he leaves it in a clean vessel, because nowadays no terumah is eaten. The Tur explains that in the chapter Kol Shaah the Gemara requires impure terumah to be placed in a repulsive vessel lest one mistakenly eat it, thinking it is pure. Nowadays, however, when even pure terumah is not eaten, this concern does not apply. Nor need we fear that a non-kohen will eat it, because every kohen designates a separate place for terumah.
But if it was not rendered susceptible, and so forth. The Tur writes: It is not susceptible to impurity and therefore remains pure. It cannot be eaten, since everyone is impure and an impure person may not eat pure terumah. It also cannot be burned because it is pure, and must instead be buried. It is therefore preferable to render it susceptible before the smoothing of the pile, while the obligation of terumah has not yet taken effect, so that it will become impure and may be burned, lest one come to derive benefit from it. The Beit Yosef asks that shortly afterward the Tur says there is no present concern of mishap because terumah is not eaten. Some answer that where burning is permitted there is no concern, since one will not delay long before burning it. This is incorrect, for he may leave it as long as he wishes. Many other strained answers have been offered. It appears clear to me that the word “immediately” in the Tur is a scribal error. His intent is that if he does not render it impure and must bury it, there is concern that because it is pure he will not place it in the vessel containing impure terumah, which is designated for burning when he chooses, but will leave it separately and may mistakenly eat it. It is therefore preferable to render it impure, thereby permitting it to be burned, and he will at once place it among the impure items designated for that purpose. This is also implied by the Rama, who did not copy the word “immediately.”
And he gives it to a Levi. Although Ezra penalized the Levites because they did not ascend with him, that penalty applied only to the Levites of his generation. The Beit Yosef writes this according to the Rambam.
