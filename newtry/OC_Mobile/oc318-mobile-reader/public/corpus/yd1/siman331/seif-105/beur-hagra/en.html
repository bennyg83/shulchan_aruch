The Yerushalmi here likewise says: R. Yochanan said, with hot water he is liable because it is fixed; with cold water he is exempt because it is casual. He can return it. R. Yochanan in the name of R. Zeira, and R. Yochanan and R. Zeira in the name of R. Elazar, said that even what remains in the flask does not become tevel because he intends to return it, where the work is unfinished. The order of the Mishnah also indicates that the issue is fixed versus casual eating, for the opening case is olives from the vat and the end concerns peeling and rubbing, etc. R. Shimshon explains it likewise.
