The caper-berries, etc. The Yerushalmi at the end of chapter 4 of Maaserot says that caper-berries are given both the stringencies of a tree and the stringencies of seeds. For what practical matter? If the second year entered the third, he separates, redeems, and gives it to the poor. The Raavad objected that this follows Beit Shammai in Berakhot 36, while the Rambam understood that it applies even according to Beit Hillel, since the Yerushalmi asks, "For what practical matter?" So writes the Kesef Mishneh. But this is not compelling, for since the discussion there concerns maaser, it says, "For what practical matter," etc.
