One who completes, etc. Yerushalmi at the beginning of chapter 4 discusses one who pickled or cooked another's produce without the owner's knowledge. This depends on the dispute between R. Yochanan and Reish Lakish concerning one who smooths another's grain heap without his knowledge: R. Yochanan says it becomes tevel, while Reish Lakish says it does not. R. Yudan said: there, the owner could have refrained from pickling. Rambam rules like the first opinion, understanding R. Yudan's statement as a rejection. He therefore writes likewise that one who fixes produce by one of the six factors, etc. See Shach.
