The Maggid Mishneh in Hilchot Issurei Biah chapter 20 explains that although the general rule is that the halachah follows Rabbi Yochanan against Reish Lakish, that rule applies when they dispute independently. Here they determine the law in a dispute among Tannaim, and Reish Lakish follows the Sages. Since the law follows the majority against an individual, his ruling is accepted. The Rashba agrees, and the Kesef Mishneh brings proofs and discusses the matter at length. The earlier statement in seif 1 that the obligation applies in Eretz Yisrael by Torah law, whether or not the Temple stands, refers, according to the Kesef Mishneh, to a time when all its inhabitants dwell there.
