One who purchases produce from a non-Jew in Syria, etc. Syria is treated more leniently, since even one who purchases a field there is only obligated in terumot and tithes by rabbinic law:
And if they had not yet reached, etc. That is, if one purchased land from a non-Jew in Syria with attached produce — if the produce reached the stage of tithing obligation while in the non-Jew's possession, it is exempt; but if it had not yet reached the stage of tithing obligation, he is obligated to tithe rabbinically since he purchased the produce together with the land.
