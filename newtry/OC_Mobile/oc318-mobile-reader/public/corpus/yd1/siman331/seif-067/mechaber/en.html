Ma'aser rishon is permitted to be eaten by an Israelite, and it is permitted to eat it in tumah. Therefore a daughter of a Levi who was defiled by licentious intercourse—they give her ma'aser and she eats. But one who heard that her husband died, or one witness testified to her and she remarried, and afterward her husband came—the sages penalized her that she be forbidden in ma'aser:
