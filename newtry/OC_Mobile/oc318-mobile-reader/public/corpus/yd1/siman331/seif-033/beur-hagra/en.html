It appears to me, however, that the anonymous Mishnah rules that his terumah never takes effect until he brings two pubic hairs. The later statement defining a minor explains the first Tanna, Rabbi Meir, whose view is anonymous in the Mishnah and is so identified by the Yerushalmi. The rule of a minor near majority does not prove otherwise, even if its vows are biblical, because terumah nowadays is rabbinic. Tosafot's text in Niddah is evidently corrupt in light of the Tosefta and Yerushalmi, though their underlying resolution remains necessary. [90] [340]
