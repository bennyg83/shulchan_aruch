At this time, if he wished to redeem ma'aser sheni worth a maneh for a perutah l'chatchila, he redeems it and throws the perutah into the Great Sea; but for other rivers he must invalidate it first. And likewise if he profaned (meaning: if he made it chullin through redemption) ma'aser worth a maneh on worth of a perutah from other fruits—behold it is profaned, and he burns the fruits on which he profaned so that there not be a stumbling block for others:
