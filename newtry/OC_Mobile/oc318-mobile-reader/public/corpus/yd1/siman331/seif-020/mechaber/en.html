Ma'aser rishon that the Levi takes—he separates from it one in ten and gives it to the kohen, and it is called terumat ma'aser. And on it it is said, "And to the Levites you shall speak," etc.: (And the law of terumat ma'aser is like the law of terumah gedolah; however, even nowadays we require giving the full measure) (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rambam):
