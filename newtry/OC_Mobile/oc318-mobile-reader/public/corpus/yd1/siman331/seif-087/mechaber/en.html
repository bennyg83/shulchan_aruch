What is casual eating? Such as one who was peeling barley and eats from what he peels one by one; and if he peeled and put into his hand, he is obligated to tithe. He was threshing handfuls of wheat, winnowing from hand to hand and eating; and if he winnowed into his lap, he is obligated to tithe. And likewise one who takes from wine and puts into a cold dish and eats—but not into a pot, even though it is cold, because it is like a small pit.
