One who eats his produce while it is tevel, and likewise a Levi who eats tithe before its terumah has been separated, is liable to death at the hands of Heaven. Nevertheless, they do not reimburse the owners for the priestly gifts, as it is written, “Which they shall separate for Hashem,” teaching that the recipients have no claim to them until they are actually separated. Outside Eretz Yisrael, a person may first continue eating and afterward separate terumah and tithes.
