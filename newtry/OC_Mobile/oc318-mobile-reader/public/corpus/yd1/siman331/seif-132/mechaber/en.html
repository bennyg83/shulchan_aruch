Maaser sheni is eaten by its owner within the walls of Jerusalem, as it is written, “You shall eat before Hashem your God, in the place that He will choose,” etc. Its obligation applies both when the Temple stands and when it does not. It may be eaten in Jerusalem only when the Temple stands, however, as the verse juxtaposes “the tithe of your grain, wine, and oil” with “the firstborn of your cattle and flocks.” The Oral Tradition teaches that just as a firstborn animal may be eaten only when the Temple stands, so too maaser sheni may be eaten only then.
