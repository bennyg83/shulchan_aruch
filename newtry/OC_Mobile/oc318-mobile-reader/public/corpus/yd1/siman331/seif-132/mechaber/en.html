Ma'aser sheni is eaten by its owner inside the wall of Jerusalem, as it is said, "And you shall eat before the Lord your God in the place that He will choose," etc. And it applies whether when the Temple stands or when it does not stand; but it is not eaten in Jerusalem except when the Temple stands, as it is said, "The tithe of your grain, your wine, your oil, and the firstlings of your cattle and flocks"—from tradition they learned: just as a bechor is eaten only before the Temple, so too ma'aser sheni is eaten only before the Temple:
