This. The Shach wrote: I am astonished. Granted, with pure terumah one may speak of a miserly measure and a generous measure, and it is relevant to say, "if the first separated according to the measure his fellow separated." But with ma'aser, what is meant by "according to the measure his fellow separated"? One must always separate ma'aser by measure. Therefore it appears to me that nowadays, whether with ma'aser or terumah, the second person's terumah is not terumah; end quote.
