Fruits of partners are obligated in terumah and ma'asrot, and they need not take permission from each other; rather, whoever separates terumah among them—his terumah is terumah. One of them separated and his fellow did not know and he came and separated—if they protest against each other, the terumah of the second is not terumah; and if not, if the first separated the full measure, the terumah of the second is not terumah; and if the first did not separate the full measure, the terumah of both of them is terumah. And it appears to me that at this time this is so for ma'aser; but for terumah, since terumah has no measure, whether in this way or that way the terumah of the second is not terumah:
