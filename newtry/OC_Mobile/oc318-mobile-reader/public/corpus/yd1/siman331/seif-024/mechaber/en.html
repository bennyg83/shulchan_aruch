Terumat ma'aser—one does not separate it by estimate but is precise with its measure, and even at this time. A thing that is customarily measured—he measures; a thing that is weighed—he weighs; a thing that can be counted—he counts. If it was possible to count it and weigh it and measure it—the counter is praiseworthy, and the measurer is more praiseworthy than he, and the weigher is more praiseworthy than both of them: (And it is taken not from the surrounding area; and Torah scholars separate terumah only from the surrounding area) (Rambam chapter 3):
