The Sages decreed, etc. Because of wealthy people who would attach their property to a non-Jew in order to exempt it [from tithes]:
Requires burial [genizah]. Perhaps in his heart it was for the sake of Heaven. When does this apply? In the Land of Israel. But a non-Jew who separated terumah outside the Land of Israel — they did not decree upon it, and one informs him that he need not separate, and it is not terumah at all. Rambam, and so too wrote the Levush.
