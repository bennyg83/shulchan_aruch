They decreed. Because of wealthy people [ba'alei kisim] who would attach their produce to non-Jews in order to exempt it from terumah:
Requires burial [genizah]. Perhaps in his heart it was for the sake of Heaven; and the Rambam wrote: when does this apply? In the Land of Israel. But a non-Jew who separated terumah outside the Land of Israel — they did not decree upon it, and one informs him that he need not separate, and it is not terumah at all.
