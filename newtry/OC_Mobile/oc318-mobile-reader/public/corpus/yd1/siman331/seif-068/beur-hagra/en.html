The text says, etc. ; I might think only the tithe of Israel, the tithe of non-Jews and converts, etc.; the text says, etc. ; I have only, etc.; the text says: a tithe from the tithe [ma'aser min ha-ma'aser]. And in the Sifrei there: "and so shall you also set apart" ; why is this stated? Because it says "and to the Levites you shall speak, when you take from the children of Israel" ; Israelites give tithe to Levites, but Levites do not give tithe to Levites. Since they do not give tithe to Levites, one might think they may eat it in its untithed state; the text says "and so shall you also set apart a terumah to Hashem." R. Shimon says: it is not necessary ; just as ḥallah, which does not apply to all produce, applies to the produce of priests ; how much more so tithe, which applies to all produce, should it not apply to the produce of priests? What does the text say "and so shall you also set apart"? The Sages tell us: only the tithe of Israelites and the tithe of their own ; from where? The text says "and so shall you also set apart." The meaning is: the Sages derive from "and so shall you also set apart" that in any case they must separate tithe from their fields and set apart from it terumat ma'aser; while R. Shimon says they need only set apart terumat ma'aser from it:
