Obligated. From the Rambam's words it implies that it is obligated by Torah law. But Rashi explained in the first chapter of Kiddushin that tree tithe is rabbinic, for only grain, wine, and oil are obligated by Torah law. So we say explicitly in chapter Keitzad Mevarchin, that even in Eretz Yisrael itself tree tithe is rabbinic. So too wrote the Raavad. End quote from Taz.
