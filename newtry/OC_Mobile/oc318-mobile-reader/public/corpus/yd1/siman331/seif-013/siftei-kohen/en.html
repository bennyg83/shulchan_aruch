All human food, etc., is obligated in terumah. That is, one is obligated to separate from it the first portion for the kohen, as it says, "the first of your grain, your wine, and your oil you shall give him." This is a general rule: just as grain, wine, and oil are human food, grow from the ground, and have owners, as it says "your grain" and "your wine," so too anything similar, namely human food that grows from the ground and has owners, is obligated in terumah. The same applies to maasrot, for they are equal in this respect.
