It is liable in terumah. Scripture states, “the first of your grain, your wine, and your oil.” Just as grain, wine, and oil are human food, grow from the earth, and have owners, so too anything similar is liable in terumah and maaser. Thus writes the Rambam, implying that the obligation is by Torah law. Rashi, however, explains in the first chapter of Kiddushin, page 3, that tithing tree fruits is rabbinic. The Gemara states this explicitly in the chapter Keitzad Mevarchin, that even in Eretz Yisrael the tithing of tree fruits is rabbinic. The Raavad likewise writes in chapter 3 of Hilchot Terumot that terumah from other fruits is rabbinic, except for grain, wine, and oil.
