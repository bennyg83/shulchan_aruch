Fruits of the second year that became mixed with fruits of the third, or of the third with the fourth—we follow the majority. Half and half—one separates ma'aser sheni from everything, but not ma'aser ani, for ma'aser sheni is stringent since behold it is kodesh, and ma'aser ani is chullin.
