Lees, etc.; and if, etc.; although, etc. This is Bava Batra 96-97, regarding more than the measure, etc.; and the Mishnah taught concerning one who soaks grape lees, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. ha-hu, etc.
And he does not separate, etc. This is Mishnah 4 in chapter 5 of Maaserot.
(Likkut) And he does not separate, etc. The Yerushalmi there, on Mishnah 6, asks: what does "he brings out tithes" mean? It means tithes, but not terumah, for one who separates terumah has in mind the cut-off pieces, the sides, and what is within the straw.
And although, etc. This is Bava Batra 97a: he asked him, etc.
