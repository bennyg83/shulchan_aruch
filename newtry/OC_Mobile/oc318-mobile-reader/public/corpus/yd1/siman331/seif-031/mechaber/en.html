One who separates terumah without permission, or who went down into another's field and gathered fruits without permission in order to take them and separate terumah—if the owner of the house came and said to him, "Take for yourself the fine ones"—if there were fine ones there beyond what he separated, his terumah is terumah; and if not, his terumah is not terumah.
