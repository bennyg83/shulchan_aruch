One who says to one, "Go separate terumah," and he went to separate terumah and he canceled his agency before he separated terumah—if the agent changed his agency, such as that he said to him "separate from the north" and he separated from the south, his terumah is not terumah; and if not, his terumah is terumah. (And there are those who disagree and hold that whenever agency was canceled, even if he did not change, his terumah is not terumah.) (Tur):
