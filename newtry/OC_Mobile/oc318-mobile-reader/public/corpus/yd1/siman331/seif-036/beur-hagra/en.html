If he changed, etc. Yerushalmi there, brought by the Rash. The Rambam's words are astonishing, for in the Yerushalmi they said this only according to Reish Lakish, while Rabbi Yochanan disagrees, as stated in the Gemara at the beginning of Kiddushin chapter 3. Rava there also answers differently according to Reish Lakish. The Raavad already challenged him, and this is what is stated in the gloss, “and some say,” etc. The Tur likewise challenged him. However, the Tur's question why he needs nullification since he already changed it is answered by the Rash there: in such a case there is no objection. One must say so according to Reish Lakish in the Yerushalmi.
