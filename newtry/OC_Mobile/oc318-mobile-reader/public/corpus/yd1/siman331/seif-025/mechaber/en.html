One does not separate terumah gedolah except from the surrounding area. How so? He had fifty se'ah in this house and fifty se'ah in another house—he may not separate from one of them for both of them. And if he separated not from the surrounding area, his terumah is terumah, provided that the separated portion is guarded. But if he was laden with a load of wine or oil and saw that they were breaking and said, "Behold these are terumah for the fruits in my house," he has said nothing.
