And if he separated, etc. See Tosafot, Bava Kamma 115b s.v. “behold,” etc.; and one can say, etc.
And it appears to me, etc. This requires study. Specifically regarding something going to waste, where it is impossible to burn it, but with oil lechatchilah one should not. After the fact, this applies only when the beam of the olive press is wrapped around it, as stated there, and wherever, etc.; see Tosafot there s.v. “and wherever,” etc. It requires study that he wrote plainly. Furthermore, this is specifically with terumat maaser, which may lechatchilah be separated not from adjacent produce; but with terumah gedolah it may not, for that is forbidden by Torah law, as Tosafot wrote in Gittin there s.v. “to separate,” etc. The Mechaber too meant only that after the fact his terumah is terumah. So it is stated explicitly in Tosefta Demai chapter 8: one who had ten barrels of tamei maaser tevel and saw one of them, etc.
