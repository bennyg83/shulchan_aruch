And it contains 146 seifim: Terumot and ma'asrot are not practiced d'oraisa except in Eretz Yisrael, whether when the Temple stands or when it does not stand. And the prophets enacted that they apply even in the land of Shinar, because it is adjacent to Eretz Yisrael and most of Israel go and return there. And the early sages enacted that they apply also in the land of Egypt and the land of Ammon and Moav, because they surround Eretz Yisrael.
