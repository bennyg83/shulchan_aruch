Five [types of people]—if they separated terumah, their terumah is not terumah: a deaf-mute, an imbecile, a minor, and a non-Jew who separated terumah of an Israelite even with his permission, and one who separates what is not his without permission of the owners. But one who separates his own for others—behold this is terumah, and he fixes their fruits; and the benefit of his favor he may give to any kohen he wishes:
