But one who separates terumah, etc. See Tosafot, Bekhorot 11a s.v. “one who redeems,” etc. But the Ritva wrote in Kiddushin that it certainly resembles it, and the earlier question was resolved, although not in its place. See Tosafot, Kiddushin 23b s.v. “for Rav Huna said,” etc., and one can say, etc. So wrote the Ran in Nedarim there, and so wrote the Ritva in Kiddushin there, bringing proof from this case here, as I wrote elsewhere. But the Kesef Mishneh wrote that since the second question in Nedarim there was resolved, that benefit of discretion, etc., the first question was automatically resolved as well. His words are very astonishing, and Mishneh LaMelekh already wondered at them. See Ran in Nedarim there s.v. “but according to the intent,” etc.: “and to me it appears even,” etc.; thus he knew that the benefit of discretion belongs to the one separating terumah, and nevertheless he asked the question.
And the benefit, etc. There it was asked and resolved; learn from it.
