However, etc. For all agree that the formulations "to remove" and "to circumcise" are valid. The question is only why the blessing is phrased with "concerning," as stated in chapter 6 of Berakhot, 38a: "This is understandable if," etc.
Or if he recited, etc. The blessing "to bring him into the covenant" is an addition to the blessing over circumcision, because the father is commanded in this mitzvah more directly than another person, and the blessing over the circumcision is included within it. Nevertheless, initially he should first recite the blessing "concerning the circumcision," as with blessings over fruit, where one first recites the more specific blessing before the more inclusive one.
