(Shulchan Aruch seif 1) He blesses over the cup, borei peri hagefen. In Torat Chaim, end of chapter 10 of Sanhedrin, he wrote that specifically the sandak who holds the child on his knees at the time of circumcision should also hold him at the time of the blessing and drink from the cup of wine. See below, seif 4 in the gloss.
