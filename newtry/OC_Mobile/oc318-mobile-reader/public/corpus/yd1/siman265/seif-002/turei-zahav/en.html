Just as you brought him into the covenant, etc. This is the text in the Rambam and Tur, while the Gemara's version is, “Just as he entered.” According to what the Beit Yosef writes in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach, that those present bless the father that he merit the other mitzvos that a person is obligated to perform for his son, it follows that the statement addresses the father, blessing him that he bring the child to Torah. The version of the poskim is therefore primary. Nevertheless, there appears to be a distinction. Those who can see the father should say, “Just as you brought him in, so may you bring him,” etc. Those standing at a distance, or when the father is not in the synagogue, should say, “Just as he entered,” etc. So it appears to me.
