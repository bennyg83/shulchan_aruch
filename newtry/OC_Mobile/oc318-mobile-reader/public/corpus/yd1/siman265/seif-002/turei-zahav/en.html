As You brought him in, etc. Such is the version in Rambam and Tur; but in the Gemara the version is "as he entered"; and according to Beit Yosef in the name of Ran Menachem — those standing there bless him that he merit the rest of mitzvot a person is obligated to do for his son — end quote; implies it refers to the father and they bless him that he bring him to Torah; automatically the poskim's version is primary; nevertheless it appears there is a distinction — those who see the father say the wording "that You brought him in — so may You bring him," etc.; and those standing far or where the father is not in synagogue say "as he entered," etc. — as above.
