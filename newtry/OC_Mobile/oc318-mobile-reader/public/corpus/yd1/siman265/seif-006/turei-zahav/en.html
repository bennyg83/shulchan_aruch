Into the infant's mouth. The Beit Yosef writes: I heard that when placing wine into the infant's mouth, one forms the shape of the Divine Name Shaddai with his fingers. The custom is to repeat the words, “and I shall say to you,” etc., alluding that through circumcision the child merits both this world and the World to Come, end quote. It is not explained who recites the blessing beginning, “Who sanctified,” etc. The Tur writes that the father, the mohel, or one of those present recites it. Our custom is that the mohel recites it. When two people participate, one performing the milah and one the periah, the one who performs the periah recites it. There appears to be proof from Orach Chayyim, siman 174, where the person who recites the blessing over the wine after the meal also recites the blessing over the spices. Here too, the one who performs periah should complete what still remains before him.
