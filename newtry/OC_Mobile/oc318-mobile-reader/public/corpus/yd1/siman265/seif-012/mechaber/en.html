The custom is to make a feast on the circumcision day: {Rama: And the custom is to take a minyan for the circumcision feast and it is called a seudat mitzvah (first chapter of Milah and Avodah Zarah); and whoever does not eat at a circumcision feast is like one excommunicated from Heaven (Tosafot, chapter Eim Af); and specifically when respectable people are found there; but if disreputable people are found, one need not eat there (same there). Also the custom is to make a feast and drinking on Shabbat night after a male was born — they enter to the infant to taste there, and that too is a seudat mitzvah (Batei Yosef siman 269 from Tosafot, chapter Merubah).}
