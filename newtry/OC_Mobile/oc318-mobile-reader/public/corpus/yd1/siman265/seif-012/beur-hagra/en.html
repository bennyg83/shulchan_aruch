They practice, etc. In Pirkei Rabbi Eliezer there Rabbi Yishmael says Abraham did not delay from all He commanded him; and when Isaac was born, on the eighth day he brought him for circumcision, as it says "and Abraham circumcised Isaac his son when eight days old"; and he brought him as an offering on the altar and made rejoicing and feast — from here the sages said a person is obligated to make rejoicing and feast on the day he merited to circumcise his son, like Abraham our father, as it says "and Abraham made a great feast on the day Isaac was weaned"; and so appears in the first chapter of Ketubot (8a); and Tosafot Shabbat 130a s.v. shesh, etc.; and Bava Kamma chapter 1 and chapter 4 of Sanhedrin (32b).
