On Friday night, etc. The Terumas HaDeshen explains that everyone is then found in their homes, and this is the “celebration of the son” mentioned in the Gemara according to Rabbeinu Chananel's explanation. I found a further support in Midrash Rabbah, Parashas Emor, chapter 27. R' Levi gave a parable of a king who decreed: All guests present here may not appear before me until they first appear before the queen. So too, the Holy One, blessed is He, said: Do not bring a sacrifice before Me until a Shabbos has passed over it, for there are no seven days without Shabbos, and there is no circumcision without Shabbos, end quote. The Derishah cites a responsum of Maharar Menachem explaining the custom to visit the newborn infant on Shabbos: The infant mourns the Torah that he forgot, as stated in the chapter HaMapeles.
