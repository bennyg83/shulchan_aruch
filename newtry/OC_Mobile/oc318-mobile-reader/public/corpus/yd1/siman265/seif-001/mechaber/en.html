The order of circumcision blessings and the law of circumcision on a public fast day. And it contains 13 seifim.
The one who circumcises blesses, "Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us concerning circumcision"; and the father of the son blesses between cutting the foreskin and uncovering, "Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to bring him into the covenant of Abraham our father: {Rama: And if the father of the son is not at the circumcision, some say another person blesses this blessing, for behold beit din is commanded to circumcise him (Rambam in the name of Geonim); and the custom is that one who holds the infant blesses this blessing (Tur, in the name of Raavad and Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Semak); and likewise if the father is present but does not know how to bless (Rabbeinu Yerucham netiv 1, and so Beit Yosef in the name of Avudraham). The father and the circumciser, when they bless, must stand (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Beit HaLevi); but the one holding the infant — when he blesses, the custom is that he sits and blesses. Some say all the people at the circumcision should stand, as it says, "And the people stood at the covenant" (Mordechai, first chapter of Milah, Kol Bo, Rabbeinu Yerucham, Avudraham, and it is taught); and so is the custom, except the one holding the infant who sits.} And if others stand there, they say: Just as you brought him into the covenant, so may You bring him into Torah, the wedding canopy, and good deeds; and the father of the son, or the circumciser, or one of the people blesses over the cup, "Who creates the fruit of the vine"; and some are accustomed to take myrtle in hand and bless upon it and smell, and say, "Our God, King of the universe, Who sanctified the beloved from the womb," etc.; and the custom is that when reaching "in Your blood, live," he puts wine from his finger into the infant's mouth: {Rama: (And when the circumciser blesses this blessing, he first washes his hands and mouth so he blesses in cleanliness) (Avudraham).}
