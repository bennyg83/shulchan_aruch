Who sanctified the beloved one, etc. The text of the berachah is "asher kidesh yedid mibeten," referring to Yitzchak, who was sanctified from the womb for the covenant. The continuation praises the One who placed His statute in his flesh and sealed his descendants with the sign of the holy covenant. Where the father is present, he says the berachah appointed for the father; where he is not present, the beit din or the mohel recites what is appropriate. Regarding naming the child, the custom is to name him at the circumcision, and where the father is absent the mother or those responsible for the child may provide the name. If neither parent is present, the community names him in the usual manner, for the naming is a custom attached to the mitzvah and not an independent requirement that invalidates anything.
