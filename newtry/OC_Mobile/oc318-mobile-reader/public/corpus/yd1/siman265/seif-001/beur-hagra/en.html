Between the cutting, etc. This accords with our text there, "the circumciser," etc., and afterward, "the father of the child," etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. avi. The Ran wrote that any blessing of praise and thanksgiving need not be recited before the act, and this is the reason for the morning blessings. The Rosh wrote that one should bless before uncovering the corona, since the blessing still precedes completion of the act, as stated there, "he circumcised but did not uncover," etc. See Tosafot, Pesachim 7b, s.v. al. The father, etc. For Scripture states, "shall circumcise for you," as with the omer, and as stated in Orach Chaim, beginning of siman 8.
Some say, etc. As stated in the Gemara, "those standing there," etc. All of this is merely a stringency.
Just as you brought him into, etc. This is the version of Rambam and the Tur. It is also the version in the Tosefta, chapter 6 of Berakhot, and in the Yerushalmi there, chapter 9. Our version, however, reads, "Just as he entered," etc.
And the father, etc. From the wording there, "and the one who recites the blessing," etc., it appears that it may be anyone.
Over a cup. See Be'er HaGolah.
And the custom is, etc. As stated in the Midrash Rabbah: Moshe circumcised, Aharon uncovered the corona, and Yehoshua gave the drink.
In your blood, live, etc. This is because the other giving to drink, at the episode of the Golden Calf, led to death.
