The custom is to make, etc. The custom is also to light a candle in the synagogue during the circumcision. This is stated in Sefer Tanya, Hagahot Minhagim, and Maharil, and it is included in the Rama's words at the end of seif 5.
Not to give two children, etc. Meaning, one does not repeatedly give two or three of his sons to the same sandak. Rather, for each son he selects a different sandak. Maharil writes likewise.
Mohalim practiced, etc. "as it is said, exaltations of God in their throat," etc. In responsum Maharam Mintz siman 43 he wrote: this is not found and this derashah is not found anywhere, only preachers and derashan say this; and that verse is expounded in Rabbah on Bereishit and in Pesikta regarding Torah, etc.; and he further wrote there one should not defer for this reason a mourner from praying on the day of circumcision; and whoever objects to a mourner is as one who objects to a person performing a mitzvah and as one who robs the dead if not in a place with fixed custom according to the sages, etc.; and in Hagahot Minhagim he copied his words briefly, but there is an error in the copy; therefore the custom spread that the mohel defers the mourner, and it is not correct; and he wrote there the main reason: because he is occupied with a mitzvah, therefore it is fitting for him to pray.
