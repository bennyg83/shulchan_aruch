He should say with his mouth, etc. The Zohar, Parashas Lech Lecha, states that the father should recite the verse, “Praiseworthy is the one whom You choose and bring near, that he may dwell in Your courtyards,” and those present should recite the conclusion, “May we be satisfied with the goodness of Your house, the holiness of Your Sanctuary.” The Darkei Moshe writes that he had not seen this practiced. Recently, many worthy people who study the Zohar have adopted this practice, with those present reciting the entire verse after the infant is brought into the synagogue.
