One blessing. See in responsum Darkhei No'am part 4 siman 27 who wrote that in Egypt they practiced to bless on each one separately; and after one child's circumcision they say verses "the angel who redeems," etc., to divert attention — and this is an ancient custom; and if they are one person's children the father blesses to bring him in also on each separately — see there; and see in responsum Perach Mor siman 32 part 2.
