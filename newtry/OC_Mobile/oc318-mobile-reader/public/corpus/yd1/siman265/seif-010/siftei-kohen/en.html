And if it is Shabbos they must be prepared etc., per Beit Yosef's explanation of Baal HaItur in Rav Yehudah Gaon's name; but truly it appears from Baal HaItur even if prepared it is forbidden, decree lest he bring earth on Shabbos from outside not prepared; so Bach; likewise implied in Tanya 133 a in Rav Yehudah Gaon's name, on Shabbos do not do so lest he bring earth from outside, end; Bach wrote nevertheless the world practiced leniency with earth prepared from erev Shabbos.
