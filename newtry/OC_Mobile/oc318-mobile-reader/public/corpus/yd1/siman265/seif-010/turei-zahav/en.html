To the circumcised infant. This implies that it is unnecessary to give him the amount required from a cup of blessing, namely, a cheekful. The Beit Yosef likewise writes earlier in this siman in the name of the Rashba: You asked how much must be tasted. Regarding Kiddush, a cheekful is required. Here, regarding other blessings, a cheekful is not required, end quote. This is difficult, for Orach Chayyim, siman 107, states that whenever a cup is required one must drink a cheekful. One may answer that this applies when the cup is required by the Gemara. Here, however, the cup is used only because song is not recited except over wine, as the Beit Yosef writes in the name of the Mordechai. The Rashba's statement refers to such a case.
