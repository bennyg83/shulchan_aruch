And a minor who was born, etc. See above, siman 263, seif katan 4.
Born when he was already circumcised etc. No blessing needed. Tur in Baal HaItur's name; nevertheless bless to bring him into Abraham our father's covenant, and we practice blessing; Mechaber translated Rambam's language implying no blessing at all; so Rashba responsum 329 and Ran chapter Rabbi Eliezer on Milah, likewise no blessing to bring him into Abraham our father's covenant.
But when they return, etc. Meaning also for a valid child, although we do not bless on a safek, nevertheless when they return for tzitzin that impede, etc., one must return and bless, etc., and not say "sustain the child" although he is a valid child, for he already requested mercy for him at first; and so Pri Chadash; and so Maharil; and so in Hagahot Minhagim in the name of Agudah.
