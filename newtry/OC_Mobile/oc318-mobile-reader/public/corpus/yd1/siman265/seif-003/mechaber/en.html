A convert who circumcised before he converted, and a minor born already circumcised — when they draw covenant blood from him, they do not need a blessing; and likewise an androgynous — they do not bless on his circumcision because he is not certainly male: {Rama: But when they return to cut strands that impede the circumcision, he must repeat all the blessings, but does not say, "Establish this child," etc. (Binyamin Ze'ev siman 6 in the name of Agudah).}
