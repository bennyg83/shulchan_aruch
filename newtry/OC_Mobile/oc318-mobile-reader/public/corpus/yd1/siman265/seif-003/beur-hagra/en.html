A convert, etc., and a minor, etc. Regarding a minor, see siman 263, seif 4. All the more so regarding a convert, as stated there, Shabbat 135a: Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar said, "They did not disagree," etc.; "concerning what did they disagree," etc. Nevertheless, covenantal blood must be drawn, as stated in siman 265, seif 1, see there. No blessing is recited over a matter of doubt. See Be'er HaGolah and the Rosh at the beginning of the chapter Kisui HaDam.
(Supplement) A convert, etc., and a minor, etc. The Yerushalmi, chapter 19 of Shabbat, and Bereishit Rabbah, parashah 46, expound: "He shall surely circumcise," to include one born circumcised. A baraita teaches: Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar says that Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel did not disagree that one born circumcised requires the drawing of covenantal blood, because the foreskin is suppressed beneath the skin. Concerning what did they disagree? A convert who converted after already having been circumcised. Beit Shammai say that covenantal blood must be drawn from him, while Beit Hillel say it need not be drawn. Rabbi Elazar son of Rabbi Elazar HaKappar says that they disagreed neither about this case nor about that case, and in both cases covenantal blood must be drawn. Concerning what did they disagree? One born circumcised whose eighth day occurs on Shabbos. Beit Shammai say that Shabbos is desecrated for him, while Beit Hillel say it is not. Sefer HaEshkol also cites the Yerushalmi according to this version. See there. Rabbi Yitzchak bar Nachman said in the name of Rabbi Hoshaya: The halachah follows the words of the student, meaning Rabbi Elazar. (End of supplement.)
