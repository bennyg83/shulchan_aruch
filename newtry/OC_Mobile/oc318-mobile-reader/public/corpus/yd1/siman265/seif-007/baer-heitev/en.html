The firstborn. Shach wrote: we never practice thus, only we do not bless shehecheyanu even if an Israelite circumcised his firstborn son — and so by law; for the Rav derived this from Maharil, and there it implies the opposite that the distinction is only that in every matter one should bless at redemption; and so is the custom (again I found thus in Hagahat Derishah, and so is the main view) — end quote.
