When the father himself circumcises his son, he blesses shehecheyanu; and if the circumciser is another, some say there is no shehecheyanu blessing; and for Rambam, the father always blesses shehecheyanu on every circumcision — and so was practiced in all the kingdoms of Eretz Yisrael, Syria, and its surroundings, and the kingdom of Egypt: {Rama: And in these lands the custom is not to bless shehecheyanu even when the father circumcises his son, unless he circumcised his firstborn son whom he is obligated to redeem — he blesses shehecheyanu at circumcision and does not bless at redemption; but when exempt from redemption, he does not bless shehecheyanu (see Maharil).}
