His intent. Shach questions Rama from the laws of repeated blessings. He concludes that even for two children of the same category a new blessing may be required, especially because ordinary conversation between circumcisions creates an interruption. Bach recommends bringing the children one after another. Taz distinguishes the concern for the evil eye regarding two grooms from two infants.
And he recited the blessing. Shach cites Rabbeinu Yerucham that where two mohalim circumcise two infants, the first recites 'concerning circumcision' and the second recites the concluding blessing, and both blessings cover both infants. This is the preferred practice.
