The beloved from the womb. Rashi explains that this refers to Yitzchak, while Rabbeinu Tam explains that it refers to Avraham, as in the verse, “What has My beloved to do in My house?” “And He placed a statute in his flesh” refers to Yitzchak. “And sealed his descendants” refers to Yaakov. “The beloved of our flesh” refers to the soul. “From destruction” refers to Gehinnom. If the child was born without a living father or mother, the appropriate text of the blessing, “Preserve this child,” is found in the Beit Yosef in the name of the Ritva.
