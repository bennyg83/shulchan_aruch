Beloved from the womb. Rashi explained it refers to Isaac; and Rabbeinu Tam explained it refers to Abraham — as written "what is my beloved in my house." And chok basharo — this is Isaac. And tza'atzavav chatam — this is Jacob. Yedidut she'arenu — this is the soul. Mishchata — this is Gehinnom. And if a child is born orphaned of father or mother — find the blessing text of "sustain the child" in Beit Yosef in the name of Ritva.
