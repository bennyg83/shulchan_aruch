Need not cover, etc. And even according to those in Orach Chaim siman 75 who forbid reciting Shema opposite a minor's nakedness, because we require "and your camp shall be holy" and it is not so here, for we come to repair circumcision we read in him properly "and your camp shall be holy"; and it is not then because of "lest any unseemly thing see in you" as Rosh wrote; and also for a minor we do not judge him the law of nakedness; and one need not cover him at the time of blessing as Rabbi Yonah wrote.
But one should clean him, etc. Although one need not distance oneself from the excrement of a minor until the child is capable of eating a kezayit of grain, as stated in Orach Chaim at the beginning of siman 81, it is nevertheless preferable to clean him before the blessing.
