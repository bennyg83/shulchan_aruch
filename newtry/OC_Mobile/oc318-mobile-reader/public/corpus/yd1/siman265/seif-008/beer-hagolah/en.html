The language of Rambam there in chapter 3. His reasoning concerning a minor is as follows: the need to draw blood of the covenant arises from concern that the foreskin may have been compressed, as I cited in siman 263, and one does not recite a blessing over an uncertainty. A convert who was circumcised before converting is no better than a minor. Raavad challenged Rambam because this is an uncertainty concerning Torah law. The sages of Lunel asked Rambam about this, and he answered that the Sages instituted blessings, and they also said that anyone who recites an unnecessary blessing violates the prohibition, "You shall not take," etc. He brought proof from Sukkah, where the Talmud says that we sit in the sukkah but do not recite the blessing, etc., Sukkah 47a.
