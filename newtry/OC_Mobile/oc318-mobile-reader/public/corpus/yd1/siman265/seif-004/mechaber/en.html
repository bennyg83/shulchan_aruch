On Yom Kippur and the four fasts, they do not bless over the cup: {Rama: (Tur in the name of Baal HaItur, and see below).} Nevertheless, on three of the fasts when the woman who gave birth does not fast, one may bless over the cup and she may taste from it if she hears the blessing and intends not to interrupt with words between hearing the blessing and drinking the cup; but on Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av, when the woman who gave birth cannot drink, they do not bless over the cup; and on Tisha B'Av we also do not bring spices, because of the reason that one does not bless on spices at the conclusion of Shabbat when Tisha B'Av falls on it: {Rama: And some say one blesses on the cup on all fasts and give the blessing to small children (Tosafot in all Ma'aravin and Mordechai Yoma); and so ruled Tur Orach Chaim siman 559; and so is the custom; and on Yom Kippur the custom is to give to the circumcised infant as explained in Orach Chaim siman 621; and some say even without a fast one may do so (Kol Bo); but the custom is not so — only the sandak drinks when it is not a fast.}
