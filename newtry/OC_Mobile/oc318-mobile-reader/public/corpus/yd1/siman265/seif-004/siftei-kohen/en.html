But when they return, etc. Meaning also for a valid child, although we do not bless on a safek, nevertheless when they return for tzitzin that impede, etc., one must return and bless, etc., and not say "sustain the child" although he is a valid child, for he already requested mercy for him at first; and so Pri Chadash; and so Maharil; and so in Hagahot Minhagim in the name of Agudah.
And it is publicized at the time of his circumcision, etc. Maharil writes that they circumcised him in the courtyard of the synagogue near its entrance, unlike children of proper lineage, who were circumcised inside near the entrance.
Because one does not make the blessing on spices, etc. That is to say, because one does not make the blessing on spices on Tisha B'Av that falls on Motzei Shabbos, as explained in Orach Chaim siman 556 — the reason being that smelling spices is for pleasure. The same applies here: it is forbidden to smell the spices in the place where the custom is to take the blood and smell spices at the time of the milah, as above seif 1. This is the author's view, but it is not compelling. For it appears that the reason there is because the extra soul departs at Motzei Shabbos, and the blessing on spices was instituted for the pleasure of restoring one's soul. But that it should be forbidden on every Tisha B'Av to smell spices — this we have not heard.
And so the Mechaber rules in Orach Chaim, siman 559. Seif 7.
