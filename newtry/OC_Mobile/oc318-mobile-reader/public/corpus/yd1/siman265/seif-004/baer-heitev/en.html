They request. The prayer 'Sustain this child' is omitted for a mamzer because Israel does not desire the preservation of mamzerim among them. Those present also do not recite the usual blessing for his future growth. Maharil records additional customs intended to mark the child's status.
And Tishah BeAv. Taz notes that although Orach Chaim permits one within thirty days after childbirth to eat on Tishah BeAv without assessment, Rama records the custom to fast when no great pain is present.

To the child. Taz infers that the child need not drink the full cheekful normally required from a cup of blessing. He suggests that the full measure is required only for cups mandated by the Gemara, whereas this cup accompanies song over wine.
The sandak. Shach writes that in the places he visited, the sandak did not drink; the wine was given to young children.
