And even if it does not have them now but will grow them after a time — we learn it from what is written "that has not jumping legs" with an alef, implying it does not have them now and nevertheless it is permitted. Beit Yosef; Levush; Peri Chadash section 2 — meaning even though it has legs now, we know of this species that it will grow later — permitted. Levush; Kereti section 1; and even if it does not have all four signs and will grow them later — pure. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur end section 2 Shulchan Petach David there.
