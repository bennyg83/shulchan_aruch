(siman 85 seif 1) Signs of locusts — any that has four legs and four wings and its wings cover most of the length of its body and most of its circumference. In the Gemara Avodah Zarah chapter Ein Ma'amidin (daf 37) it brings what we learned in Eduyot chapter 8: what is the dispute between R' Yosi ben Yo'ezer and the Sages regarding a locust called il kamtza whether it is pure; and it explains their dispute whether a majority of any amount suffices — Rashi explained: if they measured it they would find a majority in the narrow part; R' Yosi ben Yo'ezer holds a majority of any amount suffices and the Sages hold we require a recognizable majority — Rashi: a majority visible to the eye; and from this is clear proof for what is written siman 21 there; and from Rambam's words in his commentary on the Mishnayot it appears the halachah is like R' Yosi ben Yo'ezer as we learned in the election "like me"; and there is difficulty on Rambam in his composition and on Taz who omitted this and did not explain what majority we require — one must say that since they were silent that we require its wings to cover most of it, automatically it implies a majority by measurement suffices; and as Taz wrote siman 21 regarding fitness of shechitah — investigate; and for us there is no practical difference since a locust is eaten only with tradition.
