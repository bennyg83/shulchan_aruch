Jumping legs. Shach wrote: and there is no difference whether its head is long and it has a tail or whether its head is not long and it has no tail.
Tradition. Shach wrote: and the hunter is believed as above siman 82 regarding fowl; and Taz wrote: and now the custom is not to eat any locust even when it is known that its name is chagav, because we are not expert in their names.
