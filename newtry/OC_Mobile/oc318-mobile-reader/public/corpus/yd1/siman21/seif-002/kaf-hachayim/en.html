B) And likewise in the veshet: if there is not a majority of its hollow—even if he slaughtered the entire outer skin of the veshet and half the inner hollow—it is not called a majority and it is invalid. Shach s.k. 2; She'erit Menachem there; Shulchan Gavoah om 2; Komatz om 4; Minchat Yehudah om 4. And even if he cut in the inner skin around in a circle from the outside side only, in the thickness of the inner skin and did not reach the hollow of the veshet in a majority of its hollow—in any case it is not called a majority and it is invalid. Komatz there; Minchat Yehudah there.
