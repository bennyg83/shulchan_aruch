Like a stylus. Meaning: diagonally. Like teeth of a comb—Mahari Chaviv explained: he curves and returns and begins; and the Perishah wrote on it that when the knife curves within the siman here and there, it is impossible that uprooting not occur, uprooting the simanim in the curving of the knife—and why is his shechitah valid? It appears to me that outwardly he cuts the simanim around them and curves in that same cut—end. And it appears to us there is no permission in this "like teeth of a comb," for even according to what he explains that he cuts from outside with curved cuts—it is forbidden for us, for he must remove the knife and cut in another place adjacent to it, and we hold: whoever raised the knife even any amount—it is forbidden because of delay; and similarly in what is written "he left this place," etc.—that is without raising the knife at all at the time of shechitah, but was pushed from place to place at the time of shechitah. And see what is written in siman 24.
