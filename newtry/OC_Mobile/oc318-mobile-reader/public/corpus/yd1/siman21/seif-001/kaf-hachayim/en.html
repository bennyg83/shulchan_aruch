A) [Seif 1] How much is the measure of shechitah, etc.; if he slaughtered a majority of one of them, etc. The kaneh is the membrane inside the gargeret that binds the rings together, but the rings themselves are called gargeret; and a majority of one like it means a majority from the beginning of the membrane downward, and not a majority of the gargeret—that is, the thickness of the rings. Shechitot Mahariv. Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash om 1. And the kaneh is thick in front toward the skin side, for there are rings and membrane; but toward the side of the spinal column it is thin, for there are no rings there, only membrane; if so, regarding shechitah we require a majority of its thickness and a majority of its hollow (meaning: we require slaughtering a majority of the hollow from the side of the thick place and not from the side of the thin place); and even if he slaughtered a majority of the kaneh in its thickness and not in its hollow—that is, he slaughtered a majority of the thickness and not a majority of the hollow—or he slaughtered a majority of the hollow and not a majority of its thickness, such as the siman turned over and he slaughtered from the side of the spinal column toward the skin side—likewise it is not called a majority and his shechitah is invalid until he slaughters a majority of the thickness and a majority of the hollow. She'erit Menachem om 2; Tuv Ta'am om 3; Shulchan Gavoah om 2; Minchat Yehudah om 2; Zivchei Tzedek om 1.
