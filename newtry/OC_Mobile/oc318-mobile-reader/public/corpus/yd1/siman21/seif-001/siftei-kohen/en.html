Most of one, etc. Mahariv wrote: the kaneh is the membrane inside the gargeret that binds the rings together, but the rings themselves are called gargeret; and a majority of one like it means specifically a majority of the kaneh from the beginning of the membrane downward, and not a majority of the gargeret—that is, the thickness of the rings—end of his words. And see above siman 24; and therefore it must be likewise for the Rosh—for behold he explained the beginning [of the mishnah in siman 20] requires knowing specifically, and therefore without returning the simanim it is unlike the end; and if so, that which is taught "one who slaughters from the nape—his shechitah is invalid" is because at the nape knowing does not apply, and it is always invalid.
And a majority of two, etc. Maharshal wrote in his hagahot on Tur: if the kaneh was slaughtered entirely and the veshet—the outer skin was slaughtered entirely and the inner skin—a majority of it was not slaughtered—it appears to me to forbid it; therefore from responsum of Maharam of Friedberg siman 27; and so wrote the Bach that he found it written thus.
