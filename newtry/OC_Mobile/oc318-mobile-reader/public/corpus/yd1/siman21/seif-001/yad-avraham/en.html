(Siman 21 seif 1) The proper shechitah that cuts both, whether in animal and beast or in bird, and to this the slaughterer should intend. And in Kerem Shlomo he wrote in the name of Rashi that it is only d'rabbanan that they required both in a bird too because of distancing, etc.; and from this one learns that from the Torah it is only one in a bird—and behold explicitly so in Chiddushei HaRashba chapter HaShochet: from the Torah one siman suffices in a bird, for we say the verse cast [the law] upon the bird between fish and animal; and in animal too, majority of two, as we say there regarding kodshim we do not flay except because there is a mitzvah to flay.
