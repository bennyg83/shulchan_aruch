The measure of shechitah in quantity. In it are 5 seifim.
How much is the measure of shechitah of the kaneh and veshet? The proper shechitah cuts both of them—whether in animal and beast or in bird; and to this the slaughterer should intend. And if he slaughtered a majority of one of them in a bird, and a majority of two in animal and beast—his shechitah is valid, provided that when they measure it they find that what was slaughtered is a majority; and once they find that what was slaughtered is more than half, even by a hair's-breadth—it suffices.
