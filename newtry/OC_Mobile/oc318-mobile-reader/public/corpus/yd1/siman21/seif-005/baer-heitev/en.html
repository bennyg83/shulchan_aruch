And Shach explained: such as when he cut the first half of the kaneh he grasped the kaneh alone in his hand, or half the kaneh was damaged from illness; and it is implied this is valid even for us, for that which we disqualify delay in the beginning minority of the kaneh is because we are not expert in examining the veshet and we are concerned for perforation of the veshet—what is not so here where he knows he did not perforate the veshet, delay does not apply. And Taz wrote: it appears for us in every case treifah if there is a defect in the simanim; and he wrote on this: may the breath of the slaughterers expire—first they tear the skin and curve the cut so the simanim will be visible, and afterward they slaughter; they do not act well. Bach wrote in siman 23: where he grasped the kaneh alone and said "I am certain I did not touch the veshet"—valid; but Taz wrote for us in every case treifah, like bar Oza brought below siman 33 seif 8; and in Sefer Nekudat HaKesef he disagrees with Taz.
