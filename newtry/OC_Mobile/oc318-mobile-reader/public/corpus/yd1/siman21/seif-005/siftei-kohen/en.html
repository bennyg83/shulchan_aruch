And it is known that he did not perforate the veshet. Such as when he cut the first half of the kaneh he grasped the kaneh alone in his hand, or half the kaneh was damaged from illness (Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe); and it is implied from the words of the Rav that this is valid even for us; and so the Taz explicitly, for we hold thus; and the reason is: since it is known he did not perforate the veshet, delay does not apply—for that which we disqualify delay in the beginning minority of the kaneh is because we are not expert in examining the veshet and we are concerned for perforation of the veshet, as below siman 23 in the gloss; not so here. And so the Bach in siman 23: where he grasped the kaneh alone and said, "I am certain I did not touch the veshet"—we hold it is valid. See there.
