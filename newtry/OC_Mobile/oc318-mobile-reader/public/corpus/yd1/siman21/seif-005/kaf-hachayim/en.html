E) And Komatz wrote there regarding veshet: certainly one may not rely on measurement, because it stretches—and at will it widens and at will it shortens—only in the kaneh; see there. However, in our surroundings the Sages of Israel are expert to measure on two kanin that they insert along the length of the veshet as is known, and there is no concern the two cuts stretch equally; on the contrary, one should establish more than the kaneh. And measurement of the kaneh—to press both its sides, etc., one can recognize well; but there must be a majority of its hollow and its circumference. And beware in measuring the veshet lest anything remain, even a little of the membranes of the veshet that sometimes remain from going around—not as is circulated in the mouths of the Sages of Israel that this is from the membranes connecting kaneh to veshet, which do not exist; rather all is included in the category of simanim. Minchat Yehudah in Leket Yosher end om 10; Zivchei Tzedek om 5.
