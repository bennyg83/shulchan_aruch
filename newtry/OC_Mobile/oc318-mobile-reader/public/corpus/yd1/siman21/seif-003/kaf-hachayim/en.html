And if he slaughtered a majority of one of them in a bird, etc. In male geese, in most of them there are like two kanin—that is, sometimes his kaneh in the middle extends like another kaneh, except it is not made in separate rings like the way of the kaneh, and returns and connects to the kaneh; and we wrote below siman 33 om 40 that they are valid—that is, both of them; see there. Nevertheless regarding shechitah: if he slaughtered the bar oza above in the kaneh before it splits and slaughtered the kaneh alone—it is simple it is valid; but if it happened he slaughtered below where it splits and slaughtered only one kaneh—the Pri Megadim end om 2 was uncertain in this and left it tzarikh iyun; but Rav Zevach Shmuel in the she'eilot section siman 10 ruled treifah. And so ruled Mekadesh Me'at siman 33 om 21. And it is implied from his words we require a majority of both; and if he slaughtered the entire first kaneh and half the second kaneh—it does not help; see there. And so in Komatz Kelal 18 om 2; and so Zivchei Tzedek om 3.
