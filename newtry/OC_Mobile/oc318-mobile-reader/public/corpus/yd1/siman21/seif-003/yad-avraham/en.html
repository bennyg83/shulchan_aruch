(Seif 3) He did not slaughter the majority of the siman in one place, etc., that two slaughtered with two knives, etc., and when there is a majority even in the second place it is valid even though the shechitah is not spread out. Even though the slaughterhouse expands outward—nevertheless when cut above or below it does not expand and is not spread out—end Tosafot and Rosh, and Beit Yosef brings them. And it appears to learn from this: if the second slaughtered with a polished knife—it is treifah for everyone, even according to the view of "there are those who validate" that Shulchan Aruch brought in siman 9—for since the first slaughtered first above or below, then there is no expansion when the second slaughtered and the sides are charred from heat of the knife; and there is a practical difference for us in a bird, per what Peri Chadash wrote to validate according to everyone; and in such a case certainly invalid.
