In the veshet. And Shach wrote: it deals with one who did not delay even any amount. And Be'er Heitev wrote: in these lands the practice is to declare treifah if he slaughtered in two or three places even if he did not raise his hand, and even b'dieved treifah; and Peri Chadash disagrees with him in siman 101; and in Avodat HaGershuni siman 16 he ruled: if he said "I am certain I did not touch the simanim" and also no blood came out—valid even if he slaughtered in two or three places, even if he delayed between the first and second. See Shach and Pri Yitzchak siman 23.
