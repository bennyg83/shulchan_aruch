(Siman 58 seif 3 in hagahah) If struck by stone in one place — no examination except there not other organs. Kol Bo and Tur language imply. Tur wrote struck by stone opposite whole body — Rav need not precision; siman 41 seif 63 clear bird struck tree or stone on head — concern membrane only not other organs; siman 32 seif 6 animal struck stick on spine if knots on stick concern knot places; see there s.k. 14 proven Gemara Rashi Ran Tosafot Chullin concern literally crushing that place struck; even in walking concern perforation that organ if treifah from that needs examination — walking does not remove perforation concern for if perforated could still walk — see there.
