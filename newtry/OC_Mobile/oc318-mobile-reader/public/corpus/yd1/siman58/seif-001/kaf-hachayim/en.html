(1) [Seif 1] Fallen animal forbidden, how animal fell, etc., and slaughtered immediately — treifah even if no visible break — concern organs crushed and dislocated. No reason in treifot halacha leMoshe miSinai. Rashi Chullin 42a; Ran.
(2) There. If wall from paunch to place fell ten tefachim — concern, etc. Ox fell to pit five tefachim — also concern since paunch to legs more than four. Kereti and Peleti ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek ot 1.
(3) There. If lying and rolled — height nineteen. Under ten — later authorities' view even if not stood and waited me'eit le'eit — one relying on acharonim should not seize him; however Taz above siman 32 holds under nineteen if cannot walk — concern spinal cord severance; so Noda BiYehudah Yoreh De'ah siman 22; Machberet ot 2; appears from his words if case came to him would declare treifah per Taz — only do not seize one who ruled like acharonim; Yad Ephraim; see above siman 32 ot 42.
(5) There. Or others threw and knew intent to throw, etc. — here deals e.g. nothing to hold — therefore nineteen treifah; nevertheless not fall at once — strains with all force when they want throw — therefore under nineteen permitted; thrown at once — suddenly or legs bound when thrown — concern even under nineteen. Shach s.k. 1; Kaf HaChayim in Hagahot Beit Yosef end ot 8; Peri Chadash ot 2; Peri Toar ot 4; Levushei Prad ot 1; Shulchan HaMelech ot 3; Chochmat Adam general rule 26 ot 5; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3 and 6; Anan HaKohen siman 41 ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek ot 5.
(6) Thrown at once concerned even under nineteen — reasonable we require four tefachim paunch to ground measure; all less seems not crushed. Peri Toar there; Zivchei Tzedek ot 7; Anan HaKohen there; Zivchei Tzedek ot 6; Machberet ot 1 gummed but reason is thus.
(7) Where fell by strong wind — law like others threw. Da'at Torah this siman; Darkei Torah ot 12.
(8) There hagahah. No difference animal and bird, etc. — meaning in bird too if others threw at once concern even under nineteen; if fell on own nineteen as below ot 12 and 13 — see there.
