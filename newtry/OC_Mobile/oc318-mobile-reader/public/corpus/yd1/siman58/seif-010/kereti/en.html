Bound — and Taz cries out at communities that bind animal legs and throw to ground and slaughter; Taz wrote custom stretches rope and is not tied tightly until already fallen to ground; therefore before fall it can still dig nails — needs extra guarding since entrusted to light-minded people God forbid; see Peleti what I wrote resolving Or Zarua words: if carried lamb on shoulder and threw to ground — treifah; but if held lamb's legs in hand and slipped to ground from there — we estimate if from hand grip to ground is ten tefachim — meaning did not intend to throw; but if intended to throw — treifah even without ten.
