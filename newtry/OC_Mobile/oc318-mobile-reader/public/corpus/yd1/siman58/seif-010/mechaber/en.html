An ox that they threw down for slaughter — even though it fell a great fall that has a sound when they throw it down — we are not concerned for it, because it digs in its nails and strengthens itself until it reaches the ground (Rambam's wording there din 12). {Rama: And specifically when they did not bind all its legs when they threw it; but if all its legs are bound at the time they throw it down — we are concerned for it (Mordechai Piskei Dinim in the name of Ra'avyah, and the implication of Sha'arim sha'ar 34, and not like Or Zarua).}
