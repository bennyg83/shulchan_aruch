If it stood, etc. Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe ruled: even if it stood and waited me'eit le'eit — examination is required; and so Or Zarua general rule 56 din 5 in the name of Or Zarua, Semak, and so is implied from the Rav's words in the hagahah seif 6 and other acharonim: for us, who are not expert in examination — standing does not help, and also waiting — only walking.
