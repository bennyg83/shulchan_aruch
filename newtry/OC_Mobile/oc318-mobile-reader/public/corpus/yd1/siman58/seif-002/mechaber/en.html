A bird that a man trampled with his feet, or threw against a wall, or an animal crushed it, or it struck a hard object — we are concerned for it. {Rama: And likewise if a stone or hard object fell on its body; and likewise in an animal if it is a matter where there is concern for the animal (Kol Bo, Or Zarua HaAruch, Tur, Beit Yosef, Ran, Rashba, and Rabbenu Yerucham).}
