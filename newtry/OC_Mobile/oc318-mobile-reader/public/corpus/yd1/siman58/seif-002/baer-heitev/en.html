In less than ten. Taz and Shach challenged: seif 10 Mechaber slaughterhouse no crushing though great fall; Taz wrote perhaps error before Rav; Shach answered: slaughterhouse permitted — estimates, digs nails, has hold; Mechaber deals no hold — nails not applicable; still not fall at once — strains when they want throw with force; therefore under ten permitted; but at once — thrown suddenly or legs bound when thrown — concern even under ten.
