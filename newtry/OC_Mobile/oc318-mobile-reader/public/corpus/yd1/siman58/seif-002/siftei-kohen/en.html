And likewise if a stone fell, etc. Meaning: with a stone there is no difference whether it fell on the animal in less than ten or high ten — all is according to the stone whether it can kill; and so is everyone's view in this, who distinguish: specifically when the animal falls one may say the living being carries itself, unlike a stone; and so Darkei Moshe, and so Maharshal Piskei Dinim siman 62; and not like Bach.
