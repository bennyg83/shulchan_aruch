Whatever stood and walked. Bach explained in seif 9 in conclusion that it is not called walking unless it stood on its own and walked; but if others stood it and it walked — it did not leave the presumption of fallen, even though it walked four amot properly, end of his words — and this is not clear; and so in Sefer Lev Chadash end of daf 163: in walking no examination is needed even if others stood it; and so is proven in the Gemara Zevachim end of daf 74 — see there and examine; rather the main point is as Bach wrote initially and Lev Chadash there: standing is the natural course of the matter; and what Taz wrote that even in standing alone change of appearance in organs does not disqualify — is an error, and Lev Chadash already challenged him there — see there.
Even if a change is found, etc. And Bach disagrees and forbids in this, and his words do not appear correct; and so Maharshal Piskei Dinim siman 65 like the Mechaber (and in Sefer Api Ravrevi he wrote one who is stringent in this for himself — examine well). However this is specifically when a change is found that does not declare treifah in other animals (disqualifying even) not because of the fall, as Rashba wrote in a responsum and the Rav in the hagahah; but if a change is found that disqualifies even not because of the fall — obviously treifah, and it need not be written because of its obviousness; therefore the Mechaber did not explain this (not as appears somewhat from Bach's words, the opposite of this).
