Or others threw and knew intent to throw, etc. — here deals e.g. nothing to hold — therefore nineteen treifah; nevertheless not fall at once — strains with all force when they want throw — therefore under nineteen permitted; thrown at once — suddenly or legs bound when thrown — concern even under nineteen.
