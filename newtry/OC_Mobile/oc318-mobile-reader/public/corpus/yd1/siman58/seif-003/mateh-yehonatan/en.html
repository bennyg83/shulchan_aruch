(Siman 58 in Shach s.k. 1) Paunch to ground four. If pit only six — combine four paunch with six pit — ten; if ox lies and rolls — pit needs nineteen; and wagon — do not throw from wagon with force — Taz. Maharshal challenged Tur Choshen Mishpat siman 410 — answered combining legs death uncommon; payment accident; treifot no difference.
