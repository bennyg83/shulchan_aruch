The fallen animal of which we said we are concerned if it did not stand — it is forbidden to slaughter it until it waits from time to time (me'eit le'eit). And if it was slaughtered within this time — even if they examined it and found it whole in all its organs — treifah. And if it waited from time to time and afterward was slaughtered — one must examine it opposite the entire body cavity, from the head to the thigh; if treifot are found among the treifot the Sages listed, or an internal organ was crushed and its form lost — behold it is treifah; even an organ that if removed is kosher, such as spleen and kidneys — behold this is treifah; except the womb — if crushed, behold it is permitted. And the signs do not need examination, for the fall does not compress them. {Rama: And if it was struck by a stone in one place — it does not need examination except opposite the place where it was struck, and not in other organs (Kol Bo, and so is implied from Tur's language).}
