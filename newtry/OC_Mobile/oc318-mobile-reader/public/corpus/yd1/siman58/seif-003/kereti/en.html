Me'eit le'eit — then defect visible what was not immediately at fall — therefore cannot examine until passes me'eit le'eit; if crushing will be visible.
And its form lost — if crushed organ found in animal we do not concern until we know it fell — so Taz and Peri Chadash, not Bach; Rambam wrote in mishnah explanation.
Except womb — know womb connected by sinews and veins like these organs; by nature sometimes contract sometimes expand as common in womb illness sometimes narrow sometimes wide; therefore even if animal falls etc. easily no damage in womb — when blow pain reaches it expands then contracts without standing and pain marks — cannot damage since organs not one structure; from this reason when gives birth in narrowness and pressure of birth cannot call crushing on child — organs expand at rope pressure; cannot crush child; if so womb has no crushing — two explanations: at fall womb itself not crushed; also fetus in womb at birth not crushed — one reason as written because depends on mother's nature; Rambam explains womb has no organ crushing two explanations as written — Gemara proof fits one explanation no crushing in birth other womb itself — all one consideration; Rambam clear no contradiction Gemara; Mechaber brought Ra'avan on Rambam and two explanations relied on siman 15 — see Peleti.
One place — if struck opposite lung — lung examination required; for us not expert — treifah; likewise fell from roof needs examination — lung too — Shach and Peri Chadash.
