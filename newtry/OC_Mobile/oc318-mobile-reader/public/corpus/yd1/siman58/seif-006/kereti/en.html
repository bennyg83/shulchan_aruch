Walked — if struck by stone opposite internal organs those organs need examination; if struck with stick half spinal cord — we rule above siman 32 examination for spine — here walking does not help at all — Ran and Peri Chadash; see Peleti what I wrote — I left requires study.
And limping — known from fall; if not attribute Shegruna which is common.
When walked — if uprooted its leg to walk — dispute among poskim; Shulchan Aruch follows Rambam and Rif since they did not bring Semak that halacha is not so.
