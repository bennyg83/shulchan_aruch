If it walked — kosher and does not need examination, even though it did not wait me'eit le'eit. In what matters is this said? When it walked a proper gait in its manner; but if it walked and is limping — it needs examination. {Rama: And some say that nowadays we are not expert in examination and one may not permit except through walking (Halachot Gedolot, Tur in the name of Beit Yosef, and all the acharonim); and so is practiced; and it is not called walking unless it walked four amot in a proper gait as it walked before the fall or like other animals (Rashba, Ramban, Ran, and Or Zarua HaAruch). And if it walked and afterward cannot walk — its law is like other dangerously ill animals (Maharik root 61).}
