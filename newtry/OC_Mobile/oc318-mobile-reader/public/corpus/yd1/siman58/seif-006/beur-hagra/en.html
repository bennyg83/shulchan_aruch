And even though in Gemara we say "uprooted its leg," etc., and even R' Yehudah requires walking — nevertheless it implies four amot not required — meaning presumably fit to walk more; but where we see it cannot walk more — then four amot required; and even if cannot walk further — still like other dangerously ill as below; proof from what is stated there "R' Yehudah said R' Yochanan: stood," etc., walked needs no examination; and halacha "where," etc., walked even with examination — no such version in precise books; language changed here said examination unnecessarily — meaning even if cannot walk further still like not fallen; and beginning "needs no examination" deals when did not walk four amot.
