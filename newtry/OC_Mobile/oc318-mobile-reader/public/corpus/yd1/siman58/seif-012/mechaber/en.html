Thieves who stole an animal from the pen — even if they threw it beyond a fence that is very high — we are not concerned, because they aim to knock it down on its haunches so it can run before them; therefore when they return it, if they return it out of repentance — it is permitted, because they aim to knock it down in a manner that they will not lose it; but if they did not return it except out of fear — we are concerned for it.
