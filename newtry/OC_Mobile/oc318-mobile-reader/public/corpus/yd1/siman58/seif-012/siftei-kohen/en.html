Because they aim, etc. It implies: from safek it is forbidden, lest perhaps or presumably they knocked it on its sides; and so Rokeach concludes siman 387 to forbid in such a case, and so is the implication of the Gemara and poskim; however the reason Rokeach wrote there: in sudden fall we are concerned, for even if it fell on its haunches examination is required — requires study, for the implication of the Gemara and poskim does not appear thus at first glance.
