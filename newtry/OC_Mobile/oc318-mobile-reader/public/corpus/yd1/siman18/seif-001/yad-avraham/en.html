(Siman 18 seif 4) A knife that one checked on holacha and did not feel a flaw, and when he brought it back on hava'ah he felt a flaw, etc.: if he slaughtered by holacha alone and did not bring back his shechitah is valid; if he brought back, invalid. It appears to me plainly proven in Talmud, Rashi, Tosafot, Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Rashba, Ran, Mordechai, Tur, Beit Yosef, Shulchan Aruch, gloss, and Shach in this siman that a flaw invalidates in the last minority according to all; for here perforce it deals where he slaughtered the measure of shechitah on holacha alone — meaning there is in the knife measure of two necks, as explained siman 24; and nevertheless if he brought back, invalid — evidently flaw invalidates in last minority; and this is not like Maharitz whom Shach brings siman 23, seif kaf 13 — see there — in whose old age all those poskim were hidden from him.
