Behold it is neveilah — meaning doubtful neveilah; and so Rambam explicitly; and so Shach; and see what I clarified above siman 1 at length, to distinguish between a mikveh that was measured and found lacking, that we declare treifah retroactively for certain and not doubt — see there.
We are concerned: an animal while alive stands in presumption of prohibition; and if there is doubt in shechitah — establish the animal on its presumption, and it is forbidden.
Even in fowl — its measure is soft; and it is hard in reality to make a flaw; nevertheless we are stringent; and Bach wrote: even if he touched the joint-bone. And it seems: even if he slaughtered only the trachea — there is a good doubt: perhaps afterward it was flawed; and even if you say from before: perhaps he touched the flaw before he slaughtered half the trachea — if so the flaw does not flaw; nevertheless it is forbidden, for any doubt in shechitah is forbidden.
