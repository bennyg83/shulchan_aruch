Explanation: doubtful neveilah; and as he writes below, "but rather we are concerned," and so too at the end of seif 11; and so Rambam chapter 1 of Laws of Shechitah and Kol Bo and Semak siman 167 and other poskim; therefore the Mechaber wrote below siman 28, seif 20, that in such a case one covers without a blessing — see there.
But rather we are concerned, etc. Because an animal while alive stands in a presumption of prohibition, and it does not leave prohibition until you know with what it was slaughtered; and since a doubt arose in it, it has not been clarified to you that it was slaughtered properly (as below, end of siman 25). End of Talmud.
