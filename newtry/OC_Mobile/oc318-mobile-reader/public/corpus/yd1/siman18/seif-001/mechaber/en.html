The law of checking the shechitah knife and its flaws. In it are 20 seifim.
One who slaughters with a checked knife and it is found flawed (even from the side) (my humble opinion) — behold it is neveilah; even if it touched the joint-bone one does not attribute that it was flawed by it after shechitah, but rather we are concerned lest it was flawed in the hide, and it turns out he slaughtered with a flawed knife — and even in fowl.
