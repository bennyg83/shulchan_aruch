(Seif 1) One who slaughters with a checked knife and it is found flawed, even from the side. And this does not include knife markings craftsmen make when making the knife, since it is far from the point — so Maharshal; Taz, seif kaf 1; and so above siman 9, letter 6, in the name of several poskim; and see there further from letter 7 onward, laws pertaining to this matter — see there.
