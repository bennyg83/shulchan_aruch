(Seif 1) One who slaughters with a checked knife and it is found flawed, even from the side. And this does not include knife markings craftsmen make when making the knife, since it is far from the point — so Maharshal; Taz, seif kaf 1; and so above siman 9, letter 6, in the name of several poskim; and see there further from letter 7 onward, laws pertaining to this matter — see there.
(There) Behold it is neveilah. Explanation: doubtful neveilah; and as he concludes "but rather we are concerned," etc.; and so in seif 11; therefore he wrote below siman 28, seif 20, that in such a case one covers without blessing — see there; Shach, seif kaf 1, in the name of several poskim; and so Peri Chadash, letter 2; Shulchan Gavoah, letter 3; Kereti, letter 1; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 2; Yad Eliyahu, letter 1. However, Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur, letter 3, wrote: here it is certainly neveilah; and so Shapirdatz, letter 1; and he wrote to distinguish between there and here — see there; however Shulchan Gavoah there agreed with Shach that it is doubtful neveilah; and therefore he wrote for practical law: one who eats its flesh is not lashed and cannot slaughter on the first day; and if he slaughtered he is not lashed on a doubt — see there.
(There) But rather we are concerned, etc. Because animal while alive stands in presumption of prohibition (prohibition of limb from a live animal) and does not leave prohibition until you know with what it was slaughtered; and since doubt arose in it, it has not been clarified it was slaughtered properly, as below end of siman 25 in gloss; Shach, seif kaf 2; Peri Chadash, letter 3; Lechem HaPanim, letter 2; Kereti, letter 2; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 3.
(There) And even in fowl. Even if he also touched the joint-bone — for there are two reasons to be lenient, its measure is soft, and he touched the joint-bone — nevertheless we are concerned lest it was flawed in the hide; Bach; Shach, seif kaf 3; Peri Chadash, letter 4; Lechem HaPanim, letter 3; Beli Yadi, letter 2; Kereti, letter 3; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 4; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 3; Yad Eliyahu, letter 4.
And even — he slaughtered only the trachea, and there is a good doubt: perhaps afterward it was flawed; and even if you say from before: perhaps he touched the flaw before he slaughtered half the trachea — if so the flaw does not flaw; nevertheless forbidden, for any doubt in shechitah is forbidden — Kereti there.
And even — they did not check it many hours after shechitah, and afterward checked and found flawed — nevertheless we are concerned lest it was flawed in the hide; and we do not say it is a double doubt — perhaps on joint-bone flawed, perhaps after shechitah flawed by something else; Yad Eliyahu, letter 3; and to me it seems plain it is not a double doubt, since it does not reverse: the two doubts are after shechitah; and if you say before shechitah — it is neveilah, and we do not make such a double doubt, as written siman 110, laws of double doubt — see there.
