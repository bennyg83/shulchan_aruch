Within the flaw of the fingernail. Avodat HaGolah; and what he wrote in the name of Darkei Shalom siman 4 is a printing error — it should be siman 200; and see there he wrote there is no question to check this shechitah knife on the thumb finger, even though it is the thickest and heaviest of fingers; permission is given to check with whichever hand and finger he feels most, according to his own knowledge and sense of touch; and it happens every day that several slaughterers check on index finger or middle finger, each according to his feeling, even though we did not see one who checks on thumb — perhaps one will be found who feels more on it than other fingers; and doctors in feeling pulse prove: some do not feel on thumb because of the pulse vein there; some feel and feel more on it. And see what Avodat Avodah wrote in Tiferet Shlomo: that which Lechem HaPanim wrote to check specifically on thumb — no concern for this, and we do not practice thus; and he wrote in responsum Avkat Rokhel on a slaughterer who did not feel a catching flaw — one should not remove him for this, since one can attribute that because of fear they commanded him to check before many great ones; but if it becomes clear he has no feeling at all — and so in all places they remove by law — they declare treifah everything retroactively; but if he says, "It is clear to me I slaughtered without flaw" — see there. And it seems treifah retroactively until the hour it was checked before a sage and he saw he has feeling — then they say afterward it deteriorated; and Meir Netivim siman 35 wrote one should not rely on atufra checking alone when felt on avshara, even if slaughtering trachea; and likewise reverse when felt on atufra even if not felt on flesh — forbidden even if slaughtering esophagus alone; Heaven forbid to do so, not like Sefer Gan HaMelech; and he too possibly did not say so except for pilpul — see there.
