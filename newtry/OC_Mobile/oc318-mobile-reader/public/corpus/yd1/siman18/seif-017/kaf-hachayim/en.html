(Seif 3) One must check the knife before shechitah. And we do not bless on knife checking like on shechitah, for any mitzvah whose performance does not complete its work — meaning complete the mitzvah — we do not bless on it; proof from sukkah that we do not bless on making it; and knife checking — its performance does not complete work, for afterward shechitah is needed; Mahari Weil in glosses of R' Yerucham; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur, letter 9; and also lest he reconsider and not slaughter — and it is a blessing in vain; Lechem HaPanim, letter 6; Zivchei Rishon in Musafim, letter 1; and further because we do not bless on any mitzvah that is not the essence of the mitzvah, as written in our words Orach Chaim siman 8, letter 2; and here the essence mitzvah is shechitah.
