A butcher who did not show his knife to a sage (and it was found good) (Tur) — they would excommunicate him. {Rama: And if it was found flawed they would excommunicate him and remove him (Tur); and if it was found good the sage may forgive and he need not excommunicate him (Alfasi's glosses, chapter 1 of Chullin);} and nowadays they practice appointing known men over shechitah and checking, and the sages forgave their honor because they are careful and diligent; and much settling of mind and fear of Heaven is required for checking the knife — do you not see that a person may check two or three times and not feel a thin flaw, and afterward it is found, because he prepared his heart at the end; and the quality of the sense of touch is according to intent of heart (Rabbenu Yonah in Shaarei Teshuvah).}
