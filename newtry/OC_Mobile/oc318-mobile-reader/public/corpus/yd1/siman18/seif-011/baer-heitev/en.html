Shach's explanation: proper checking — avshara, atufra; and we do not say it is an interruption between blessing and second shechitah, for needs of shechitah are not an interruption, as below siman 19. And not specifically many animals — one animal too requires checking after shechitah, lest it was flawed in the hide.
For shechitah. And Levush wrote: if he slaughters for others and did so without permission — he must pay, even if he slaughters for free, as the law of a damager with his hands; and see Choshen Mishpat siman 306. But Taz disagrees and ruled he need not pay, for perhaps on the joint-bone it was flawed; but if he did not check even on the first, which is required for everyone — certainly he was negligent and must pay; not so between shechitah and shechitah, which is only ordinary diligence — if he brought himself into doubt he is certainly exempt from paying — see there; and in Sefer Nehar Shalom he disagrees.
