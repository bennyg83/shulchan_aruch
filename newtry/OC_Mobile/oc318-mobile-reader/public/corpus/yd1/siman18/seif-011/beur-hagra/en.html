(Seif 11) And one who wishes, etc. As is written there: "to invalidate the first," etc. — not from the root of the law, no; and they say: checking is required — good advice, as (Gittin 34b) and Tosafot there 3b, s.v. R' Elazar, and see there 36a, s.v. vehaedim, and Maharam Schiff there — R' Gamliel enacted that witnesses sign the get, as Tosafot there: so they need not witnesses of delivery.
