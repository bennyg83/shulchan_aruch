One who slaughters many animals or many fowl must check between each and every one; for if he did not do so and checked at the last and the knife was found flawed — behold all are doubtful neveilah, even the first. {Rama: And one who wishes to bring himself into this doubt need not check the knife between shechitah and shechitah (Terumat HaDeshen siman 184 and Beit Yosef in the name of Baal HaMaor and Rashba; and see seif kaf 17).}
