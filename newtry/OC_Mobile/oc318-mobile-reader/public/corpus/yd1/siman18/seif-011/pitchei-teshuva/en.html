The utensils are forbidden. Avodat HaGolah; and see responsum Brit Avraham, live part, siman 12, letter 3, in the case of a slaughterer who showed his knife in presumption of good and the sage felt a flaw in it; afterward the slaughterer took and rubbed on forearm flesh and thereby the flaw was removed, and he apologized that certainly there was no complete flaw since it was removed by hand rubbing; and he wrote one should not remove him from presumption of fitness to establish he does not feel, for sometimes honing from stone congeals and sticks somewhere on the knife and thereby is felt like a flaw, and by rubbing the congealing was removed; and it can be that from when he gave the knife in presumption of good it had not yet congealed and was moist, therefore he did not feel; and afterward it congealed and therefore is felt, and this was removed by rubbing — see there; and nevertheless it seems everything is according to the matter.
