The measure of a flaw is any amount. Explanation: if a flaw of any amount is found, it is invalid; but l'chatchila one need not check except avshara and atufra, as below seif 9, because we are not concerned for a flaw of any amount; and so Maharshal chapter 1 of Chullin siman 38, not like Bach who ruled to be lenient that it is not rendered treifah by a flaw of any amount but only by the measure of catching a fingernail — for we have only our custom; Heaven forbid that we be lenient thus; and since he saw that they check on the fingernail, he understood that our custom is that any flaw that does not catch a fingernail is valid — but it is not so, for the custom is plainly to invalidate even a flaw of any amount, as Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe and Maharshal and other acharonim write, and as Rambam and Kol Bo and Ramban and Rashba and Ran and their colleagues hold; only that l'chatchila we are not concerned for a flaw of any amount and it suffices for us with checking the fingernail, as he writes; and so it appears from Taz as I explained; also what Bach said that from Tosafot's words it appears that specifically the measure of catching a fingernail is required — it is not so, and proof: for Kol Bo too wrote like Tosafot, and so Taz — see there.
