Measure of flaw — any amount; see Shach, who wrote even less than catching a fingernail; and that we require fingernail checking — we are not concerned for small flaws, and ordinarily they catch on the fingernail; but in truth if they are smaller we forbid; and so Maharshal and Bach — his view is to declare fit if the flaw is smaller than catching a fingernail; and acharonim disagree; and see Peleti: at first my view agreed with Bach that if he slaughtered only the trachea certainly correct with Bach that if it does not catch on the fingernail it is valid — and with this I settled Rambam's contradiction; but finally I brought that any flaw that is felt is treifah by law, and one may not be lenient at all, and everything is called catching a fingernail.
Any amount — in Lechem HaPanim he gave a taste for a flaw according to a secret that the soul dwells in it — see there; and his silence is better than his speech, Heaven forbid to give a taste for God's mitzvot; and so was the law according to Maharam; and there is no point, for in tastes one gives a stumbling block in error, because many do not hear that secret voice, and Torah provides leniency in these laws.
Even a hair's-breadth — and in error many are lenient and say of the knife that it is not a flaw but in Ashkenaz tongue "ruvikh," and it is for lack of their feeling and knowledge, for ruvikh is small flaws felt with great attention; and one who slaughtered with a knife that is ruvikh is wicked and feeds treifot to Israel in error; and one should excommunicate that this name ruvikh not be mentioned or counted — and it is a complete flaw.
