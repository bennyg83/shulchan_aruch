(Seif 3) The measure, etc. Not like Rav Chisda there — all those in the West and Nehardeans and Rav Sheshet and Rav Ashi do not hold like R' Hoshaya, that all are less than catching a fingernail; and furthermore they said there: it is similar to a thorn — what? And if so, what is the question to him? For it does not catch a fingernail. (Likut) The measure, etc.; and Rosh explains catching the fingernail — that the fingernail feels, as (II Samuel 22:38) "and I girded them from my loins"; and accordingly there is no proof from a thorn, for even a thorn the fingernail feels; and he ruled like Rav Chisda, may his memory be blessed; and my view is to be more stringent than Rashba — end of his words.
And provided, etc. For we call it catching, and as its name — that it catches something — to exclude that it catches nothing; this is similar to the thorn they said there.
Even, etc. From Rav Acha bar Yaakov.
