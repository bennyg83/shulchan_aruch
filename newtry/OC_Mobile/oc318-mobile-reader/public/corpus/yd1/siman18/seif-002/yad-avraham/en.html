(There) If he slaughtered by holacha alone and did not bring back — his shechitah is valid. And it appears to me: if the flaw is at the end of the knife — forbidden, lest he withdrew his hand slightly and did not feel; as Rashba and Ran and Taz wrote, seif 5; and so explicitly in Hagahot Maimonit, chapter 1 of Laws of Shechitah, law 13.
