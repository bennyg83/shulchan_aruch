A hair's-breadth. And Taz and Shach explain: l'chatchila we are not concerned for a flaw of any amount, and one need not check except avshara and atufra. We do not need checking in sun or water — when one passes its point on water, if there is a flaw on its point they feel it in water, and that is even any amount — this is not required l'chatchila; but if afterward a flaw of any amount is found, it is treifah — end of his words.
