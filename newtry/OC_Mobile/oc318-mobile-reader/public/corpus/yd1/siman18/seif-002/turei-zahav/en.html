Provided that it catches in it any amount. In the Gemara many amoraim are cited who were stringent in checking — to check in the sun or in water, for when one passes its point on the water, if there is a flaw in the knife they feel it in the water — and that is even any amount; and Maharshal concludes that it is fitting to be stringent thus, but in the general case it suffices with checking avshara and atufra.
