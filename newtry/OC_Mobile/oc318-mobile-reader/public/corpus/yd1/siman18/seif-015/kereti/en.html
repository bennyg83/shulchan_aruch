Even by way of breaking — and acharonim disagree; and its view: way of breaking to attribute; and I say that before me such an incident occurred, where he struck forcefully on the joint-bone because the minister was forced to take the head and struck forcefully on the joint-bone so the butcher could take it; and afterward he checked the knife and a flaw was found — it was hard for me to rule against Shulchan Aruch; I commanded to hone the knife without flaw and strike twice forcefully on the joint-bone bone, and see if there will be a flaw — behold it is not soft; and that knife accepts flaw. And if not — Shulchan Aruch's words are clear; he struck and a flaw was found — I declared fit according to acharonim.
