(Seif 15) And after shechitah he broke bones in it. According to what Tosafot wrote in one answer: what we answer on it that R' Chiya on that of dipped and ascended — knife deteriorated, animal not deteriorated — there is no place to distinguish, but rather we say thus: even if knife deteriorated, still animal not deteriorated, for perhaps he slaughtered not at the place of the flaw; and we were so in a long knife like two necks full; if so, automatically for us too when he broke bones in it likewise, for even when he broke bones it is hard like dipped and ascended, since he busied with that species it is like breaking bones; and the Talmud's wording must be changed: knife deteriorated; and if so, with a small knife that is not so — forbidden.
