Reuven said to Shimon, "Check this knife," and he checked it twice as required and gave it to Reuven; and Reuven was going to slaughter and Levi took it from his hand and it was found flawed (and Reuven apologizes and says that he still wanted to check it properly) (all in Rashba's responsum siman 494) — his actions prove against him that the checking sufficed for him; and therefore they remove him; and if the matters appear like an error and he is a kosher person — they restore him, provided he accepts upon himself that he will not again do such a thing.
