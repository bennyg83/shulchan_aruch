A knife that has a flaw — it is forbidden to slaughter with it, even if he intends to slaughter not opposite the flaw; and on Yom Tov they practice permitting wrapping a cloth on the flaw, since one cannot hone it; and if it is a time of distress and he has no preparation to hone it, even on a weekday it is permitted to slaughter by wrapping a cloth on the flaw. {Rama: And see above siman 6, seif 1: a knife that has two edges — one must check both sides (Tur siman 6).}
