It is forbidden to slaughter, etc. But b'dieved it is valid even if he did not wrap a cloth on the flaw, if he says, "It is clear to me that I did not touch it," as Rabbenu Yerucham wrote in siman 6; and this is specifically when he knew before shechitah that there is a flaw; but if he did not know and afterward said, "It is clear to me that I did not touch," such as where the flaw is above the middle of the knife and he said, "It is clear to me that I slaughtered only in what is below the middle" — even b'dieved it is forbidden, for anything not dependent on a person, he is not aware, as above end of seif 4; and see siman 6 where the laws of wrapping cloth on a flaw are explained.
