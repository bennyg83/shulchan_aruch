(There) The measure of a flaw is any amount, etc. In Gemara (Chullin 17b) many amoraim are cited who were stringent in checking — to check in sun or in water; when one passes its point on water, if flaw in knife they feel it in water — and that is even any amount; and Maharshal concludes in Yeshuot Yaakov, chapter 1 of Chullin siman 38: these amoraim who checked in sun and water — a stringency they added to check after all doubts; and we conclude the law: with these checkings of avshara and atufra it suffices; but certainly if a flaw of any amount is found in sun and water — it is treifah, even b'dieved — see there; Bach, Taz, seif kaf 2; and so Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef, letter 5: custom to check on fingernail and flesh; but certainly if a small flaw visible in sun and water is found, even if not felt on fingernail and flesh — invalid, Rambam's words — see there; and so Beli Yadi, letter 3; and such is Taz's view, siman 89, letter 13; Zivchei Rishon, siman 10, in Tamidim, letter 2.
