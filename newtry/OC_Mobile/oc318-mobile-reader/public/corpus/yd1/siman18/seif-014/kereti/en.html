His shechitah is valid; and we are not concerned lest when the knife stood a flaw happened, since he did nothing with the knife; however slaughterers say and testify: if the knife stands a while by itself a flaw will happen to it. And in truth in Mordechai and his colleagues only a day or two are mentioned, not more; and in truth one should be stringent if it stood a long time to require checking; but a day or two we have no concern; Yom Tov for two days of exile proves.
And he should hide it, etc.: lest he use it and flaw it, and he forget and slaughter with it and the knife be lost and be in presumption of checked and kosher; and in truth he used it and made it flawed — Rashba.
