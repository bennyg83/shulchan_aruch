If he did not check the knife and slaughtered with it and it was lost after shechitah — his shechitah is invalid. In what case? With an ordinary knife; but a butcher who has a special knife for shechitah and a special place where he always hides it — it is in the presumption of being checked, and if he slaughtered with it without checking and it was lost — his shechitah is valid. {Rama: And it is fitting for every butcher to have a special knife for shechitah, and it is forbidden to do with it any other matter, and he should hide it so that it not be flawed (Rashi and Kol Bo and Hagahot Maimoni, Laws of Shechitah siman 6; and see Tur and Beit Yosef there); nevertheless he may not slaughter with it l'chatchila without checking (Rashba and Beit Yosef there).}
