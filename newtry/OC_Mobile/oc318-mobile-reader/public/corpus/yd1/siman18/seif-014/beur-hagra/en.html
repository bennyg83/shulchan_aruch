If he did not check, etc. As is written: animal while alive, etc., until you know with what it was slaughtered — he was precise: with what it was slaughtered.
(Likut) If he did not check, etc.: from what is written: 50, R' Elazar — all, etc., for appetite; and like Rava, etc.; and all who slaughtered on it, etc., lest with a flawed knife, etc.; and even though he does not leave the permissible, because ordinary knives are not good — forbidden even b'dieved — end of his words.
In what case, etc. On Rashi there 8:2, s.v. one who slaughters, etc., and sometimes, etc. — it implies; but now that he slaughtered without checking there is no prohibition.
(Likut) In what case, etc.: from what is written 8:2: the butcher needs, etc.; one who slaughters, etc. — it should surprise me: why need a special knife for shechitah? For checking is required before shechitah; and it is reasonable there is a difference that checking is not required when he returns and slaughters; and if he slaughtered and did not check and the knife was lost — his shechitah is valid, for establish the knife on its presumption, etc. — see there — end of his words.
And it is fitting, etc. As is written in the Gemara; and Rashi there; and in chapter 9 of Bava Metzia: resting knife, etc., since it is flawed, etc.; and Rashba explains: shechitah knife; and so in chapter 123 of Shabbat (123b).
And nevertheless, etc. As is written (9:1, 32a): in order that the butcher examine, etc.; and other places, even though they required three knives.
