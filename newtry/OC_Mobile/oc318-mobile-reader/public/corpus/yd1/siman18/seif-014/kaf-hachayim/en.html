And our knives dedicated to shechitah — permitted to slaughter relying he will check after shechitah, for b'dieved if he forgets to check it is valid, as below seif 14; Pithei Teshuvah there in the name of Tiferet Moshe. However, in gloss there seif 14 it implies even such a knife forbidden to slaughter l'chatchila without checking — see there; and so Komtzo, letter 1; Zivchei Rishon, letter 1; Yad Eliyahu, letter 7; and so above, letter 18.
