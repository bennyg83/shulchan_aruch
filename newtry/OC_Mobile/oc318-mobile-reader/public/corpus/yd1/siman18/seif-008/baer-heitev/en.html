Like a serpent. And Taz explains: its spine is curved when it raises head and tail and between them like a bow.
L'chatchila. Taz and Shach wrote that even two or three rises and falls are also kosher, not like Bach; but in Beit Yosef he wrote that in our time one does not slaughter with it except in a time of distress, but with two rises and falls it is not permitted even in a time of distress.
