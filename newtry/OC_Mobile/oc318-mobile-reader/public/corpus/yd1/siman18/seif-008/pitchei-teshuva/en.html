L'chatchila. Avodat HaGolah; and see Turei Zahav, who wrote they slaughter with it l'chatchila as Taz and Shach; and so plainly Peri Megadim and Chochmat Adam, general rule 3. But in Sefer Shechitot, Miktav Eliyahu wrote it is fitting and correct to define not to slaughter with a rising-and-falling knife, for in error sensation has diminished; would that our debts go out on a smooth straight knife and not on a mountain-and-valley, for sensation of straight knife is not like rising-and-falling; therefore when there is a flaw in the knife one should not hone only at the flaw place, for then it becomes rising-and-falling, but pass honing along the whole knife length until it stands corrected — see there.
