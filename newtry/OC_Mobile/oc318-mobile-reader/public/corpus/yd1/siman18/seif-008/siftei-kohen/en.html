That rises and falls, etc. It appears that even two or three rises and falls are valid l'chatchila for slaughter; for if you would say that two are forbidden because the second tears — then also with one, the hollow of the knife weakens, as they say in the Talmud; and furthermore, we validate rising and falling even for holacha u'va many times — let us say that on the first time it weakens and on the second it tears; and such is the practice, not like Bach who wrote here a strange matter, not accepted, to forbid two or three rises and falls — see there; so too the Gaon, may his memory be blessed.
