Checking the knife requires avshara, atufra, and three directions — that is, he passes it and brings it back on the flesh of his finger and afterward passes and brings back on his fingernail from three of its directions, which are its edge and its two sides, so that there be no flaw at all; and he should check slowly and with intent of heart so that his heart not turn to other things; and he must shift the fingernail after a bit of checking, lest the fingernail was flawed by the point of the knife; and perhaps there is a flaw on the sides that he will not feel, because it passes within the flaw of the fingernail. {Rama: And he should not check both sides of the knife together, but each one by itself (Mahari Weil).}
