(Seif 2) The measure of a flaw is any amount. Secret of knife checking that it not be flawed — for benefit of the soul dwelling in animal and fowl that shechitah not be invalidated and they not suffer further; Avodat HaKodesh, part 63; Lechem HaPanim, letter 4; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 8; and meaning: aside from what is halachah leMoshe miSinai, there is further benefit for the dwelling soul; and intent of knife checking and shechitah we already wrote in Orach Chaim siman 245, letter 7 — see there.
