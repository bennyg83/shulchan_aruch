The fingernail. And Shach wrote that one should also rinse the knife before checking, because blood sometimes congeals in the checking; and Raavad testified that such happened to him — he checked and did not find a flaw, and afterward when he rinsed in water he found a flaw — end of his words. If he did not check the knife except its point alone and not the sides, and the knife was lost — even if he slaughtered with it many animals, kosher ones, as in Shulchan Aruch daf 22; and it is permitted to check with whatever finger he wishes — Darkei Shalom siman 4. And according to Perishah he wrote that one checks only on the thumb, which is long — Peri Chadash wrote: also moisten the fingernail with a little saliva, for signs in the life of the animal are also moist — even though one feels more without saliva.
