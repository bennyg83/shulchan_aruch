Avshara and atufra. Taz: avshara opposite esophagus, atufra opposite trachea. And he wrote in Gan HaMelech siman 57: if he slaughtered fowl on one siman, such as he slaughtered esophagus and the knife was checked avshara — valid even though felt flaw was atufra; or reverse, slaughtered trachea with knife checked atufra even though felt flaw was avshara — it is fine, for these checkings the Sages required — atufra because opposite trachea, and flesh because opposite esophagus — end; and see responsum Meir Netivim siman 36, who lengthened to distinguish and wonder on this — see there; and see responsum Beit Yaakov siman 21.
The fingernail. Avodat HaGolah; and in Sefer Miktav Eliyahu he wrote one should not check the knife when the hand is cold and chilled or very hot, or the hand is moist either from drink or sweat, even moisture not intended to moisten, for he checked and experienced that in all these he will not feel; also wipe the knife so there be no moisture; also when wind blows in the world do not stand outside to check, for wind is difficult for this — see there.
