Settling of mind. See Sefer Shelah, part 4, page 72, who wrote he saw one chasid who did not eat slaughtered meat because he saw by sense of touch the knife was flawed and the slaughterer did not feel this — see there further; and see responsum Beit Yaakov siman 95, who wrote by law one need not be stringent in this except as measure of piety — be stringent on weekdays and not on Shabbat and Yom Tov, for he nullifies a mitzvah if he does not eat meat — see there; and his words are not compelling; see Orach Chaim siman 288, seif 2, and Talmud Chullin daf 11 end 2.
