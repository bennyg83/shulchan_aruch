He did not feel. And Taz wrote: even though we hold above siman 6, with a long knife that has flaws and a measure of shechitah remains, it is kosher if he slaughtered not opposite the flaw — nevertheless, if there were two flaws or more, there is no permissibility, but it is as if the whole is flawed. The flaw has no measure: whatever is felt on flesh or fingernail, even if they do not enter within at all — behold it is a flaw and invalidates according to all — language of the rabbis, authors of Beit Yosef and Bach, on Tur's words at the beginning of siman 18; and see Sefer HaZichronot.
