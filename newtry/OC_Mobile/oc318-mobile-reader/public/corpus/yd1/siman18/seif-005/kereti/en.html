Even if all are masuchseches — learn from Gemara: a knife that has many flaws — judge it like a saw; and they are in masuchseches; for if in catching — what difference if many flaws or one? One inhibits shechitah. And Taz explains in the name of Baal HaItur: it deals with catching; nevertheless there is a difference: even on the beautiful side one may not slaughter, since there are many — it is like a saw; even the beautiful part is invalid.
