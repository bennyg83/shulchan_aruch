(Seif 18 in gloss) In some places they practice that a person does not slaughter and check for himself. It seems this is one who slaughters for himself and not to sell to others — and this is only from custom's power; but when he slaughters for himself and sells to others — by law forbidden, for suspected to declare treifah fit, as explained plainly in Rambam chapter 8 of Laws of Me'ilah, laws 7, 83, 91; and Tur and Shulchan Aruch, end of siman 65; and he learned this from Talmud Avodah Zarah (daf 38): one does not buy meat in Syria except from an expert; and Rashi there: they are suspected, for they are not careful before skin; but they themselves are not suspected to eat prohibition, as Rama siman 119 in gloss; and it is surprising on one of the acharonim who wrote the opposite: the gloss here deals with one who slaughters for himself and sells to another — this alone is forbidden from custom's power; and I did not grasp his final view; for we cannot say he meant only one established in fitness — if so, there is also no custom.
