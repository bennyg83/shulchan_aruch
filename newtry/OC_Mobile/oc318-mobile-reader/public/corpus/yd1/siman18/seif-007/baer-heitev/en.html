Treifah. And Shach explains: to exclude if there was not shehiyah of the measure of shehiyah between siman and siman; or where he intended and placed the two simanim together one next to the other and slaughtered both — then even in an animal it is kosher; however, certainly l'chatchila one should not slaughter with it even fowl, since l'chatchila one must slaughter two simanim in fowl as in animal; and see below siman 23.
