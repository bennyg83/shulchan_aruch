To exclude: not that there was shehiyah of the measure of shehiyah; or to exclude: where he intended and placed the two simanim together one next to the other and slaughtered both — in this case it is well settled what Bach wondered on Taz, who wrote in the general case "one slaughters," and it further implies even l'chatchila; and below they wrote "one who slaughters with a knife that is not sharp," etc. — here they came only to teach the law of the knife, and it deals with a case where there is no shehiyah, such as where he places the two simanim together one next to the other, as I explained. I again found in Bach siman 23 that he wrote: perhaps according to what they invalidate shehiyah of any amount, everyone who slaughters with a bad knife — the shechitah is always invalid, and there is no measure to the matter — end of his words; and it requires study; and also in Taz, this siman and siman 24, it implies that specifically a measure of shehiyah is required; and so other acharonim wrote in the general case regarding an animal, measure of shehiyah; however, certainly l'chatchila one should not slaughter with it even fowl, since l'chatchila one must slaughter two simanim in fowl as in animal; and so Bach there; and see below siman 23, seif kaf 9.
And see below siman 23, etc. Meaning: there, seif 4, it is explained that one who slaughters with a knife that is not sharp and pauses the measure of shehiyah in the last minority of the first siman — it is invalid; and the reason is explained there: since the majority of the first siman was cut, it is as if the whole was cut, and what he does holacha u'va in the remaining minority is as if he does holacha u'va with its hand or foot; therefore in fowl, whose fitness is in one siman (as below siman 21), shehiyah is not considered — for as soon as he cut the majority of one siman in fowl, behold its shechitah is finished; and even though we hold below siman 23 that shehiyah, even any amount, invalidates, even in the last minority, whether in fowl or in animal — that is specifically actual shehiyah; not so one who slaughters with a bad knife, since he always does holacha u'va — examine this.
