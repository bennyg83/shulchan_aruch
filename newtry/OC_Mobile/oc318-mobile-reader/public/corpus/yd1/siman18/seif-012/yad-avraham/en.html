(Shach, seif kaf 25) But Rambam, etc.: they wrote the reason they excommunicate because he will rely on his shechitah another time and it will be flawed and he will slaughter with it — end; however in Talmud chapter 1 of Chullin (daf 10b) it appears like Rashi and Ran — end. And Lechem Mishneh also challenged thus on Rambam, who gave a reason not mentioned in Gemara. And behold also from Tosafot Bechorot (daf 31) and Rosh, chapter 2 of Negaim, it is proven he too holds like Rambam: showing knife to sage — they did not touch it only for his honor, but also from concern of prohibition; he brings proof: a Torah scholar is trusted to rule for himself in prohibition and permissibility where prohibition was not established — from what they say, chapter HaDar: a Torah scholar may rule for himself — knife checking, and need not show to sage; and if it were that showing knife to sage is only for honor — it would not apply to derive ruling of prohibition and permissibility; rather we learn he holds like Tosafot and Rosh that there is also prohibition; and so from Nemukei Yosef, chapter 10 of Bava Batra — see there. And Shach's difficulty from chapter 1 of Chullin — he already answered in his long book: we do not say a person is trusted in prohibitions except regarding kosher, not regarding invalid; and this one who did not show his knife to sage is not called kosher, for he transgressed sages' enactment, and he is no longer trusted, and we are concerned lest he do prohibition once; and there is support for this: such we find regarding blemishes of firstborn in Bechorot (daf 36): that clear blemish must be shown to sage is for his honor; and nevertheless there (daf 28) if he did not show to sage and it was slaughtered — forbidden, because of decree: we decree on internal blemishes that do not change after shechitah lest blemishes that change; behold even though the beginning of the enactment to show to sage was for his honor and not because of prohibition decrees — nevertheless when he transgressed and did not show to sage, we are concerned lest he come to prohibition. And the same: they said at first to show knife to sage only for honor and did not concern for prohibition; nevertheless when he transgressed and did not show, we are concerned now lest he come to prohibition; therefore they excommunicated him, and sage's forgiveness does not help.
