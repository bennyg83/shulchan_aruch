(Siman 18 seif 12) He must return and check after shechitah. It is difficult for me: behold there is a double doubt that it is not flawed; and even if you say flawed, perhaps on joint-bone it was flawed; and Rama ruled (siman 110, seif 9) that in a double doubt, even where it is possible to clarify, one need not clarify; and even for those who require clarifying — it appears to me this is only if after clarifying we will stand on clarification that it is certainly forbidden or not; but where only one doubt will be clarified, so that afterward there will not be a double doubt but forbidden from one doubt — one may say for all opinions one need not clarify, since now there is a double doubt, and also after clarifying there will not be certain prohibition; with this the difficulty of Shach (siman 110, laws of double doubt, letter 14) is settled — from that of seeing blood due to intercourse: there through clarifying with a tube it will be clarified it is from a source, but still not certain prohibition, for perhaps not all fingers are equal; if so, here with the knife: even if a flaw is found, there will still be doubt perhaps on joint-bone it was flawed — the law is one need not clarify; and it must be that nevertheless in presumption of prohibition, that it is not slaughtered, one must clarify even in a double doubt — and in such a case.
