(There) The measure of a flaw is any amount, etc. And so Shach, seif kaf 4: the custom is plain to invalidate even a flaw of any amount, not like Bach — see there; and so Peri Chadash, letter 5; Peri Toar, letter 3; Lechem HaPanim, letter 5; Chochmat Adam, general rule 3, letter 11; and this is according to law of Talmud and Shulchan Aruch and several more poskim; but our custom is to forbid even any sensation, even though it does not inhibit a hair's-breadth and is not visible to the sun; and also forbid not to slaughter if before shechitah one sees like a flaw of any amount by sun vision, even though it does not inhibit a hair's-breadth and is not felt on fingernail; and in my opinion correct to do so — see l'chatchila by sun vision on knife point, and be precise that he not find even a bit of whiteness, for even though this is not a flaw, nevertheless through shechitah lightly it will flaw there; therefore do not slaughter with knife until there be no sensation at all and its sharpness black to the eye's vision from head to end without white point; whoever practices thus — good for him; Komtzo, letter 3. However, this is certainly plain: Heaven forbid to rely on vision checking alone without fingernail checking — for aside from transgressing Sages' words who require fingernail specifically, they said expert slaughterers: there are flaws not visible in vision and felt in fingernail checking; and one who relies on knife checking by eye vision alone is among those who feed neveilah; Darkei Torah, letter 20; and as appears from poskim' words that vision helps only to be stringent, not to be lenient, Heaven forbid.
