Even though he checked the knife before shechitah, he must return and check after shechitah; and this is when the knife is before us; but if it was lost — his shechitah is valid, since he checked it before shechitah, even if he slaughtered with it many one after another; and if he touched the joint-bone of one of them — one should be stringent and be concerned for all those slaughtered afterward (Tur in the name of the Geonim and Rashba in the name of his teachers and Ran).
