His shechitah is valid — acharonim; Taz and Shach lengthened on this from below seif 15: if it is not known it fell on its point to ground — forbidden from doubt; and they raised the matters that here, since it was lost, the matter already went out in permissibility; and see Peleti, where I wrote one may question from sugya of Gemara; therefore I wrote to distinguish in seif 15: there is only this doubt, that perhaps it fell on its point to earth — aside from this there is no doubt that the knife is in presumption of being guarded; not so here, where the knife is not in presumption of being guarded, for it was lost — and there is a good doubt how the flawed knife happened; therefore it is permitted.
