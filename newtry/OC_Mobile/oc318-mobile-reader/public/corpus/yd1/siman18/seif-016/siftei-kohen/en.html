If he checked the knife, etc. At first glance it is difficult why the Mechaber wrote this law — is it not all the more so from what he wrote in seif 13? And it must be that therefore he was precise and wrote that we say: perhaps it was flawed by something else or he broke bones in it, etc.; and above he wrote only the reason that we say he broke bones in it alone, because above it deals where it is not known whether he broke bones in it or not, as also appears from Rosh's responsum that I brought in seif kaf 21; therefore they say he broke bones in it; but here he informs us: even though it is known clearly that he did not break bones in it, such as where he placed it in a box — nevertheless, since he checked it after shechitah, say it was flawed by something else by itself or he broke bones in it, etc.; and even though above end of seif 14 we are not concerned lest with its standing it was flawed by itself — there, because there is no unseemliness before us, therefore we establish it in its presumption; but here, behold the flaw is before you; perforce they say: with its standing it was flawed by itself by something else.
