And he was not aware. And Shach wrote: here the Mechaber wrote two reasons; and above seif 13 he wrote only the reason that we say he broke bones alone, because above it deals where it is not known whether he broke bones or not; but here he informs us: even though it is known clearly that he did not break bones, such as where he placed it in a box — nevertheless, since he checked after shechitah, say it was flawed by something else by itself; and even though in seif 14 we hold we are not concerned lest with its standing it was flawed — there, because unseemliness is not before us, we establish its presumption; but here, behold the flaw is before you.
