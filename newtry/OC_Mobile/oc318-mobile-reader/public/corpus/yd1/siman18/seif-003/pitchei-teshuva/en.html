He may not slaughter. Avodat HaGolah; and see Tiferet Moshe, who wrote that according to this error with our knives dedicated to shechitah it is permitted to slaughter relying he will check after shechitah, for b'dieved if he forgets to check it is valid, as in seif 14 — see there. And see responsum Zichron Yitzchak siman 103, who wrote: even if he places another beside him to remind him to check after shechitah — it does not help; for even in Orach Chaim siman 275 regarding reading by candlelight on Shabbat they permitted in such a case — specifically for reading, which is a mitzvah, they permitted — see there.
