One must check the knife before, etc. And one may not rely on what he intends to check after shechitah (Tur); therefore the Mechaber doubled and wrote "and if he did not check he may not slaughter," meaning even if he relies that he will check it afterward it is forbidden; and the reason Rashba wrote: lest he forget to check after shechitah, and behold he is like one eating neveilah — Beit Yosef brings it, and so it appears in Tur. And in Perishah, seif 8, he wrote a reason: because it is said "and you shall slaughter them" and "you shall eat" — and it is difficult, for in the Talmud (daf 17b) they derive knife-checking from the Torah: "and you shall slaughter them and you shall eat" — it is obvious; since when it pierces it is treifah, it requires checking by a sage — they must mean thus; and see siman 2, seif kaf 8 [YD].
