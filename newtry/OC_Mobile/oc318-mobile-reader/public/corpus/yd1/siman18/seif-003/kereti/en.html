One must check, etc.; and certainly if there is no knife in presumption of being checked at all — it is plain he may not slaughter with it, for he slaughters treifah; and if he relies he will check afterward — forbidden, lest he forget to check and commits a prohibition. But in a knife in presumption of being checked — nevertheless one must check it before shechitah and not rely on its presumption; and on this Perishah teaches: he made Scripture support: "you shall not eat upon the blood" — for one not checked at all a verse is not needed.
