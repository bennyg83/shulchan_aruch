Avodat HaGolah; and see responsum Nachalat Shiva siman 15 on a slaughterer who ruled on an animal shaking its head and somewhat congealed matter found in the brain; the slaughterer showed it kosher, the membrane exists — since it required a sage's question and to see if brain surrounds the deficiency, concern lest laymen there, not Torah scholars, say in their opinion it surrounds; and possible if it came before the teacher he would declare treifah; and even if truly kosher — nevertheless do not forgive the excommunication; and see there he wrote: what sages forgave their honor in knife checking is toward slaughterers who trust them for shechitah and checking; but forbidden for slaughterer to give knife to check to one who is not their slaughterer — sages did not forgive honor except to appointees from their mouth on shechitah.
