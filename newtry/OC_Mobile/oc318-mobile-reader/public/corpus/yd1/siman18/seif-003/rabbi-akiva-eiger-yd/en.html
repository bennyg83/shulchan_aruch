From that we do not attribute flaw on joint-bone there is no proof, for R' Hoshaya is stringent even when he broke bones, for certainly he makes it unchecked; and what they challenge: if so, sage's checking is required — R' Hoshaya's wording is fine, but this is what they say: for R' Hoshaya's first wording, to invalidate the first, one may say the knife did not become unchecked; but for this wording of R' Chiya, to validate the second — meaning through shechitah it became unchecked; and in truth automatically all the more for R' Hoshaya it is so, for we do not find R' Hoshaya more lenient than R' Chiya — sage's checking required for all, even according to R' Hoshaya; and apparently this is the plain and very spacious way; and I rejected; and see Rashba there: Ramban's view — for R' Hoshaya also one who slaughters with checked knife and it was lost — forbidden, for animal while alive in presumption of prohibition until known; and knife checking inhibits; and difficult for me: if so, difficulty on R' Hoshaya from baraita their kids and chickens were lost — what helps that majority present are experts?
