A knife that one checked on holacha u'va and did not feel that it has a flaw, and when he brought it back on hava'ah he felt that it has a flaw — this is called masuchseches: if he slaughtered with it by way of holacha alone and did not bring back, his shechitah is valid; if he brought back, his shechitah is invalid. And this is when he felt it before shechitah; but if he found his knife good before shechitah and after shechitah found in it a masuchseches flaw and said, "It is clear to me that I did only holacha alone" — his shechitah is invalid. {Rama: And some are stringent that any masuchseches is forbidden until it stands at the very head of the knife if he slaughtered on holacha, and at the very end of the knife if he slaughtered on hava'ah (old shechitah laws and Semak glosses and the above poskim); and since we are not expert where is called the head of the knife, one should declare treifah everything (my humble opinion; also the gloss in Mahari Weil's shechitah and so it appears in Mordechai and Kol Bo and Agudah and so writes the Agur — such is the custom).}
