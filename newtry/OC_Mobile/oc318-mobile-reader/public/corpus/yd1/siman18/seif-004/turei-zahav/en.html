A knife that one checks on holacha, etc. This holacha and hava'ah are not like our holacha and bringing back, where we hold the knife in one hand and its point upward and pass the finger on it — for according to that the matters in this seif would not be understood; rather it deals where he passes the point of the knife on his finger, and so on hava'ah; and in this way one checks the knife, and so it is explained in seif 9 that their checking was in this way; and if so, holacha of checking and holacha of shechitah are one matter. Tur and Rif rule: even in the middle, etc., if the point faces the head, etc.; and Beit Yosef toiled in explaining the matters, and it seems they are as their plain sense: every catching flaw has two points — one toward the head of the knife, namely the upper point, and the second toward the handle, namely the lower point; and on this he said that if the point of the handle side was honed and there is only the upper point, even if it is in the middle of the knife, it is valid on holacha and did not bring back, and so the reverse; and so explained Mahari Chagiz, may his memory be blessed.
