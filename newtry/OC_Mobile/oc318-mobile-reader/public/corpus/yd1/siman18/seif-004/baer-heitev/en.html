On holacha. Meaning: he passes the knife on the finger; and so on hava'ah — Taz. And now they practice passing the finger on the knife — and this too is correct — Peri Chadash.
Invalid. And the reason: anything not dependent on a person, he is not aware; and we are concerned lest the point strike the siman and tear it.
Everything. And Shach wrote: even masuchseches from the side one should declare treifah, since we are not expert — we are concerned lest it not stand at the head of the knife, and the hide and flesh are weakened by the head of the knife, and afterward it is torn by the point.
