(Siman 18, seif kaf 8) For it is in chapter HaGozel, etc. In my humble opinion there: the slaughterer did not bring himself into doubt, for he had no doubt at all; rather he was established like R' Yosi; and if so, even though for Rav there is doubt — that is regarding treifah, but not to take money, for perhaps the law is like R' Yosi; but here he brought himself into doubt. Also what he challenged: here damage is not certain, etc. — in my humble opinion: perforce we do not require damage certain except for gerama; but here he damages with his hands, for he slaughters with his hand with a flawed knife; if so, he should not have brought himself into doubt; and all the more since the way of the knife is to flaw when slaughtering much — behold it is like damage certain.
