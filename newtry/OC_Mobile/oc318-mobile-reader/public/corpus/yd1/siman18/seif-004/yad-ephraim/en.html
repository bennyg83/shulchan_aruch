Like from the kosher. Avodat HaGolah; and he wrote in responsum Ein Mishpat siman 1: an Israelite hired by a non-Jew to sell him kosher and treifot, and the Israelite takes wages of animals' lungs for selling them and the money for himself — in such a case forbidden, for through wages of his toil he buys lungs, profits in body money, and it is not like happenstance, even though he gives him animals alive — he tends them to slaughter and flay; nevertheless Israel has no acquisition in them and treifah is found in non-Jew's possession and happened to non-Jew; when Israel takes them it is like buying to profit; rather sell meat and lungs on non-Jew's purse and take wages from all sale money, even from lung money itself, provided he does not designate lungs for him; and such is permitted, for wages of prohibited food goods are permitted — see there.
