(Seif 4) A knife, etc. As Rashba explains: at the head of the knife toward the head of the knife; and he holds it does not tear except toward the flaw; and the checking is that he passes the knife on his finger, as written in seif 9. (Likut) A knife, etc.: for this is what is written there: "from two," etc. — weakens: weakens — it cuts the hide and flesh, and the flaw strikes the simanim from one direction, etc.; for it is thought: since it says catching, specifically; masuchseches in all is valid; and they answer: it stands, etc., opposite the head of the knife. Rashba; and this contradicts Rashi's explanation: if at the head of the knife itself, even catching — how is it possible the first flaw cuts hide and flesh? And furthermore, if so, what does it say: "a knife that has many flaws," etc. — perforce the second flaw is not at the head of the knife — if so, with one it is invalid; and so there is no question here that he did holacha, etc., and no question that it stands at the head of the knife. But according to his explanation it must be: it stands, etc., for since he said he did holacha and did not bring back — perforce on the incline toward that side that he did holacha; and so Rif brought only the second establishment. And Rosh and Rosh explain like Rashi — end of his words.
