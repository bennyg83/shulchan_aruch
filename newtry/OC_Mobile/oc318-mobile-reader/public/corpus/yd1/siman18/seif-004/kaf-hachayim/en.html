And there are those who practice seeing the knife point with the eye's vision too, aside from avshara and atufra checking, as Darkei Moshe wrote, letter 3; and Zivchei Rishon in Musafim, letter 9; and in letters afterward.
Because all rust is judged like a flaw and rust; and sometimes there is no flaw in knife but from rust it appears like flaw, as I saw and checked several times; and also possible to invalidate from shehiyah; therefore rinse the knife before checking in water; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef, letter 2, in the name of Darkei Moshe; Zivchei Shlomo, letter 2; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 7; and therefore some practice placing a little saliva on the cloth to clean the knife before checking.
