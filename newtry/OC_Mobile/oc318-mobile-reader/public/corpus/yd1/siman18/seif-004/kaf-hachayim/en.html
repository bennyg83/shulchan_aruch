(There) Behold it is neveilah. Explanation: doubtful neveilah; and as he concludes "but rather we are concerned," etc.; and so in seif 11; therefore he wrote below siman 28, seif 20, that in such a case one covers without blessing — see there; Shach, seif kaf 1, in the name of several poskim; and so Peri Chadash, letter 2; Shulchan Gavoah, letter 3; Kereti, letter 1; Zivchei Tzedek, letter 2; Yad Eliyahu, letter 1. However, Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur, letter 3, wrote: here it is certainly neveilah; and so Shapirdatz, letter 1; and he wrote to distinguish between there and here — see there; however Shulchan Gavoah there agreed with Shach that it is doubtful neveilah; and therefore he wrote for practical law: one who eats its flesh is not lashed and cannot slaughter on the first day; and if he slaughtered he is not lashed on a doubt — see there.
