A knife that one checks on holacha, etc. — meaning that he passes the knife on the finger; and so Perishah explains.
And if he brought back. Even if he did not do holacha — his shechitah is invalid, because the point strikes the siman and tears it.
His shechitah is invalid. The reason the poskim wrote: anything that is not dependent on a person, he does and is not aware.
Until it stands at the head of the knife, etc. For otherwise the head of the knife weakens the hide and flesh, and afterward the point tears.
One should declare treifah everything. And even masuchseches from the side; but those markings on the sides of knives — it appears to me permitted, for in a long knife there is space in such a case; and so Maharshal, and Bach brings it; and see above siman 9.
