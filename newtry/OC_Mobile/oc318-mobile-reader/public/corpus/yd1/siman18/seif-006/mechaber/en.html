A sharp knife that was honed and is not smooth but its touch is like the touch of the head of an ear of grain that tangles in the finger — since it has no flaw, one slaughters with it (Rambam's wording there, law 18). {Rama: And we do not practice slaughtering with it, because we are not expert in the matter (Agur and Beit Yosef and Maharshal chapter 1, siman 37).}
