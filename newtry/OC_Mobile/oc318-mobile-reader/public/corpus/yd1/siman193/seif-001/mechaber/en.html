The law of virgin-blood; it contains one se’if. One who marries the virgin has intercourse — the intercourse of the mitzvah — and completes his intercourse, and separates immediately, even if she is a minor who has not yet reached her time to see and did not see, and even if she checked and did not find blood: she is tamei, lest she saw a drop of blood like a mustard seed and semen covered it.
{Rama: And there are those who are lenient if she did not see blood (Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of “there is an opinion”). And the practice is to be lenient if he did not complete intercourse, but only made initial penetration with her, and she did not see blood; but if he had full intercourse with her, he must separate from her even though she did not see blood (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of most poskim). And a ba’al nefesh should be concerned for himself not to play with young girls.} And she needs to stop in taharah, and to check all seven, and she should not begin counting until the 5th day from her having intercourse. And he conducts with her all the laws of niddah regarding distancing, except that a complete niddah it is forbidden for him to sleep on her bed even when she is not on the bed, whereas this one it is permitted for him to sleep on that bed after she stood up from him, even on the sheet that has blood on it.
