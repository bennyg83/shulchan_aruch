Perhaps she saw a drop of blood like mustard and semen covered it. That he did not also write the reason as above siman 192 regarding blood of desire — one can say because here it is like a doubt of doubts — doubt perhaps she did not see; and if you say she saw, perhaps it is blood of virginity; therefore need to seek reason and great concern to be stringent lest semen covered it; not so above; and per my humble opinion and Taz what he wrote; and practice with her all laws of niddah — and so Beit Yosef: forbidden to breach fence of Rishonim and be lenient even a hair's breadth; and one who does so behold he breaches fence and snake will dwell — however I saw they practice giving them to eat together in one bowl after relations of mitzvah — and I did not know from where this custom came. Also Taz wrote in name of Maharal of Prague side of leniency for practical halachah — and so: and although now custom is niddah begins to count from day 6 of her use — nevertheless blood of virginity she begins to count from day 5 — end quote (and if she was claimed on Motza'ei Shabbat she begins to count 7 clean from day 5; and if claimed on Sunday twilight she counts from day 6 — so Taz siman 196 seif 5 — see there); and possibly from this spread here the custom also in this leniency to eat first time from one bowl; but in truth it is against meaning of Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch words; and one who is stringent, praise to him.
