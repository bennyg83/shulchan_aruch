Confession of the sick person and how they speak to him. In it are 2 seifim.
When he is close to death they tell him to confess; and they tell him many confessed and did not die, and many who did not confess died; and by the reward that you confess you will live; and whoever confesses has a share in the World to Come; and if he cannot confess with his mouth he confesses in his heart (and if he does not know how to confess they tell him to say "May my death be atonement for all my sins") (Tur); and all these things are not said to him in the presence of a gentile nor women nor children, lest they cry and break his heart.
