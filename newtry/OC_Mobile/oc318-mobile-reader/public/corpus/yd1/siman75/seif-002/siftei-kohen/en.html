It is permitted to salt them with meat — because they have gravy and all that exude gravy do not absorb — Rashba. And Tosafot and the Rosh and Ran wrote the reason: blood drains away; and even though these poskim do not hold like Rabbenu Tam who holds blood drains in salting, as explained siman 70 in Beit Yosef — nevertheless Tosafot already wrote chapter 25 daf 112b and Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6 and Hagahot ShaDa and other poskim that the reason is because presumably there are hollows in the meat and it does not drain; therefore regarding intestines, which are separate pieces, we say well that it drains; or alternatively — and this is primary — where it exudes gravy we say well it drains; and so Maharshal wrote there, and this reasoning is compelled in several places — investigate.
