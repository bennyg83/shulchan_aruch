Intestines without fat on them — even though blood is not retained in them — it is permitted to salt them with meat: {Rama: And some forbid salting them with meat (Tur in his hagahah), and such is the custom l'chatchila; and b'dieved it is permitted (Mordechai in the name of R' Yoel, Hagahot Asheri, and Or Zarua in the name of R' Eliezer).}
