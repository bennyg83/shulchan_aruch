Such as in the kereis. Shach and Taz and Pri Chadash all as one choke on Perishah whose view is also in crop blood is not retained, even though they said in the Gemara in kereis a drop of blood was found; but for one who forbids kereis he admits blood in crop is presumed forbidden; if so Taz labored in vain to bring proof that also in crop a drop of blood is said — for that view that is precise from 104 admits Perishah. And in truth Taz sought to bring proof since Rashi did not challenge why the mishnah counts treifah in crop pierced — it taught him in animal stomach pierced, from which crop has no connection with piercing; and this is not difficult at all, for certainly crop is not animal stomach — Rashi in Berakhot daf 61b crop grinds — Rashi explained crop in fowl is the melting pot in animal — see there; if so Rashi according to his view that in melting pot one need not pierce through and through, and in crop even with a needle one must pierce through and through — therefore plainly it must teach; but in any case Rashi wrote it is the melting pot in animal, from which one law applies to it; and if there blood is not retained, why presume more in crop? And I did not know what concern testifies more in crop than the aforementioned melting pot; and there is some support from what we say intestines their eating is not human eating — and if one comes to buy he should buy meat — the reason the body's primary creation is soul-power in meat, as we say one who wants to be healed should eat an olive's bulk of meat from the slaughter-house before its soul departs, etc., for there is soul-power and the soul is blood; therefore those without blood have no soul-power and therefore are not fit for health; and this is clear — how could Rashbag say their eating is not human eating — there are those who enjoy this and there are such people and people's tastes in food differ — rather his intent is it is not fit for health like other meat; therefore one should buy other meat. And so Perishah's words are explained; see Ramban on the Torah parashat Vayikra regarding bird burnt-offering, for the mishnah in Kodashim Kodashim teaches fowl takes the zefek and the crop — the intestines meaning crop as mishnah of Abba Yosei who says take the crop — see Ramban and Mizrachi — behold mishnah lists plainly intestines and intended crop alone; therefore Perishah's law is uncertain and do not reject him with both hands; and it matters if there is doubt whether crop was salted — there is to permit it from a doubt perhaps it was salted; and if you say it was not salted, perhaps there is no blood in it per Perishah's view. And in winter days I challenged what Tosafot proved in chapter one of one who slaughters on Shabbos that we say on it forbidden to eat today and they said the reason is because of muktzeh; and Tosafot challenged: he cannot roast or salt it; and from this they proved blood that did not separate is permitted and permitted to eat raw without salting at all; and I challenged: perhaps he wants to eat intestines that need no salting at all since there is no blood in them, and the Gemara needed to forbid because of muktzeh and the like. And now after investigation it appears to resolve simply: and is it clear to us there is no blood? Behold the senses testify that several times blood is found in them; see Ran what is written on this — only it is not clear; and nevertheless permitted — when will he eat after salting and roasting? For blood in the limb is rabbinic and rabbinic doubt to be lenient; and here there is no presumption of prohibition, for blood is not retained in intestines, etc.; and if he wants to eat raw it is simple there is no concern lest there is blood — in any case blood of limbs that did not separate is permitted; and this is impossible — blood of limbs that did not separate is permitted, but blood of limbs that did not separate is forbidden; if so forbidden to eat intestines for it is not clear to us there is no blood in them; and while still alive it is Torah doubt; and Tosafot were precise that permitted to eat raw — otherwise why because of muktzeh like Tosafot's question; and investigate.
