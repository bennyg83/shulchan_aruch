(siman 75 seif 1) When salting the chalchulot one does not salt them on the inner side beyond the food but on the outer side where fat adheres — even though in siman 69 it was explained that l'chatchila we require salting the piece from both sides; here it is different because there is not so much blood in fat as in meat, end of Avodat HaTaharah. And it appears to me to distinguish: what difference if he salts beyond the food — behold it is nothing as the hagahah wrote; and so in Magen Yisrael general rule 15 s.k. 23 — see there. And if he means we must peel the fat and salt it from every side — behold this is like a thick piece that need not be cut in two, as Rashba wrote in responsum and Beit Yosef brings siman 69; and so Beit Yosef siman 105 that a piece salted in its fat regarding kashering need not be salted there when peeling its fat from upon it — see there; and so in Sefer HaTashbetz.
