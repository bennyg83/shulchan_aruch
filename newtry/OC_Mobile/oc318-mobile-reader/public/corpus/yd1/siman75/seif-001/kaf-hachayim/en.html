(1) [Seif 1] Blood is not retained in intestines such as kereis and stomach, etc. — but crop retains blood and its law is like other meat. Or Zarua s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 1. And they wrote — and not like Derishah. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef section 1; Beit Yisrael section 1; Pri Chadash section 1; Menachem Tziyoni on Tur HaChadash general rule 2 law 11; Lechem HaPanim section 1; Likutei Halachos section 1; Kereti section 1; Shulchan Gavo'a section 1; Mishmeret Shalom section 1; Mekor Chaim section 2; Arugat HaBosem section 1; Chochmat Adam general rule 34 law 21; Arukh HaShulchan section 3; Beit Yitzchak in Amud HaZahav section 3; Mekor Chaim section 2; Zivchei Tzedek section 1.
(2) Indeed, if there is any other doubt we follow the lenient as a doubt within a doubt. Beit Yisrael there; Lechem HaPanim there; Kereti there; but Mishmeret Shalom there wrote that for practical law certainly one should not rely on this and make from it a doubt whether it was salted and the like, for Derishah is alone in the matter and one should not be lenient at all; and so Machatzit HaShekel section 2 wrote if there is doubt whether crop was salted one should not be lenient, for Derishah's reasoning is not to make a doubt that he is alone against the many — and not like Peleti; and in Beit Yisrael all the more one should not be lenient without salting and cooking — see there; and so Arukh HaShulchan there; Zivchei Tzedek section 2; and if crop was salted with the gravy inside and cooked — see above siman 69 section 70.
(3) And the same — zefek of fowl retains blood like kereis and requires salting. Mishmeret Shalom there; Beit Yitzchak there; Zivchei Tzedek law 10; and if it was salted with the food and cooked — see above siman 69 section 74.
