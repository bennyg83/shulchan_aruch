Or chayah. That it is included in behemah, as written above, siman 1, seif 1: behemah includes chayah — from where do we know? For it is written: "this," etc.; and from that chapter we derive for treifot there, 42a, according to the one who says it is not chayah; and so we hold there, 58a; and if so, everywhere they said treifot in behemah, the same applies to chayah.
Even though, etc. For otherwise the objection there, 42b, would not be difficult — that it is included in piercing of the membrane — and Tosafot there.
