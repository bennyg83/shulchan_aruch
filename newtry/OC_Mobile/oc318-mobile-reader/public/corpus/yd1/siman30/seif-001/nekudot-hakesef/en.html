(Siman 30, Shach, se'if heh) I wrote that examination is required, for I was drawn after the four Derishah; and now I examined that the words of Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch are correct, for thus said the Rosh: the cord that encircles the pot — meaning that which encircles the pot from inside, and it is literally like a sela, only that a measure more than sela is required, for if it were literally a sela it would not be enough that a sela enter into it — literally in literal [measure]; and if so, when there is a pot more than sela — that is, that the cord encircling the pot inside is the length of a tefach — if so, it is literally like a sela, for when you place that cord on the sela around on its lip precisely, it will encircle the sela; it is found that the width of the sela itself is one-third tefach; and this is clear — not like the Derishah; and examine this.
