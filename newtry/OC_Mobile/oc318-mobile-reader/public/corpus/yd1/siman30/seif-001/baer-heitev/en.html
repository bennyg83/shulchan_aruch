Treifah. And as in Taz: in the Tur it is written ; between in most of the circumference, that is, from its middle and upward, which is below the eyes, and between in most of its height, that is, from the place where it begins to slope and it is above the eyes; and it appears that his explanation gives a sign where the place of treifah begins and says: from the middle of the height of the head and upward ; which is below the eyes toward the ground ; and from that place and upward until the horns: everywhere there is a crush in the circumference in a circle in that place, even a majority of that circle ; treifah. And most of its height ; that is, that it was crushed not in the circle of the circumference but along its height, that is, from the eyes and upward toward the horns: if a hole was made in most of that height, likewise treifah; but below the place where it began to slope there is no reason to forbid in most of the height. And if in its height or in its circumference alone there is not a majority, but in both together there is a majority ; that is, height with circumference ; it is forbidden; for that which was unresolved for us in the Gemara whether most of the circumference or most of the height ; it is possible to say that this too is included in the unresolved question and it was not resolved; and see Peri Chadash and Knesset HaGedolah (Maharam Tiktin).
