(a) [Seif 1] Behemah or chayah where most of the skull bone was crushed. In the Gemara it is stated (Chullin 42b): a skull that was crushed in its majority — treifah; and Rashi explained that it was struck with many blows and did not lose substance and did not pierce the membrane; and so Ri"v — the skull was compressed and shaken by the blows, and it is close to breaking but not actual breaking; and so Shach, se'if alef, and Peri Chadash, section 2, like Rashi. However, Perishah and Derishah expanded on this matter, and from their words it appears that the skull was cracked is called nichbesah, even though the entire skull is whole — see there; and Beit Din, Turei Zahav, section 1, brought further support for Perishah's words from the words of the poskim; therefore he wrote to be stringent, and even if it was not compressed, only cracked alone — whether in most of its height or in most of the circumference — treifah; see there. And so Kereti, section 2, that one should be stringent like all the explanations. And so Shafan Dam, section 1; Chayyei Adam, general rule 15, section 5; Einei HaKohen, siman 2, section 1; Kometz HaMinchah, section 1; Beit Yitzchak, section 1; Zivchei Tzedek, section 1.
