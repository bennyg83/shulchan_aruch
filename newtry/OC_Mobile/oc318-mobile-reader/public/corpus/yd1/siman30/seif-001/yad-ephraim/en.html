Avodat HaGir; and see in Mishna Berurah that he also brought many who permit; and see in responsum of Shevut Ya'akov, part 2, siman 25, on chickens and geese that I raised in my book Eretz Yisrael and in Sulam Lamincah according to the lenient ones; and so printed in Likutei Halachot there; and so I saw in the house of Moharach and his sons and grandsons the geonim to permit. But I saw in responsum of Chavot Yair from his own reasoning that one should be careful ab initio not to buy such geese; and accordingly some teachers practice to rule thus. And it is a stringency without reason, for if so, also among doves we find thus, and we say in the Gemara: a dove they never forbade us; and likewise in Orach Chayyim, general rule 56, it is written; and so turtledoves and doves — we do not find in their species a prohibition; and if it were according to the stringent ones, it would be found in their species of prohibition, and we have no authority to add another sign of impurity that we do not find in the signs of impure birds, end of his words; and his proof is wondrous — what relevance is a sign of impurity here? We come from the aspect of deficiency of the skull. And what is found "in its species of prohibition" — that is, he only said regarding those that do not contain one of the eighteen treifot; and these, since there is deficiency in their skulls, are treifot by circumstance, and through corruption of formation treifot came about in them from birth, like if there is deficiency in another limb from birth that was rendered treifah therein; and see what I wrote above on the words of Noda B'Yehuda on this. And he wrote in Noda B'Yehuda, siman 5: regarding geese in which a hole is found in their skull bones and it is recognizable that it is from birth — (1) if the hole is small that there is no doubt that it would not reach by itself the measure of sela in behemah, I permit; but if it is already large that there is doubt regarding it from deficiency of the skull, I send it to another teacher who permits it, and when there is no other teacher who volunteers to permit it — then b'dieved I also permit it, since from the beginning of its creation one may say treifah of piercing the skull bone means piercing by force. Also one may join to this that one may say the skin seals the hole — even though for Shach, siman 46, in a gullet there is a measure for its deficiency, it does not seal; nevertheless it appears fit to join in such a case — see there; and see what I wrote in my book Beit Ephraim, siman 23, to answer what is written in the book Chiddushei HaRazah on the words of Or Zarua, Turei Zahav, that he raised to forbid; and in Knesset HaGedolah, siman 48; and there in siman 39, siman 426:
And Mishna Berurah wrote in the name of Shulchan Gavo'ah: even if there is some lesion on its head, many slaughterers declare treifah even though the skull bone is whole — they err; rather one removes the scab or shell that is on the head where the lesion is; and if the bone beneath it is whole — kosher; and if not — treifah from safek whether the brain membrane was pierced. And if before checking whether the bone is whole or not the head was lost — kosher because of safek-safek.
