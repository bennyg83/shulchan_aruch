And Mishna Berurah wrote in the name of Shulchan Gavo'ah: even if there is some lesion on its head, many slaughterers declare treifah even though the skull bone is whole — they err; rather one removes the scab or shell that is on the head where the lesion is; and if the bone beneath it is whole — kosher; and if not — treifah from safek whether the brain membrane was pierced. And if before checking whether the bone is whole or not the head was lost — kosher because of safek-safek.
