Behemah ; and likewise bird; only that in a bird there is a difference in waterfowl: with a crack alone it is treifah. The Shulchan Aruch did not wish to elaborate and mentioned only behemah and chayah.
Shenitrotzetz ; it has language of crushing and is close to breaking, and it is not yet broken; therefore it used nitrutz, even though in the Gemara it says nichbesah. And so Radak explained on "kaneh ratzutz"; and many explained language of crack and breakage ; in all of them one should be stringent.
Most of the height, etc. ; many explained; and Taz explained: from the eyes until the horns, the drawn area is height, and the encompassing in that place on the entire skull is called most of the encompassing; and sometimes it was struck in that place high from the eyes until the horns, but on the circumference of the skull it is whole; and likewise the opposite ; most of the circumference of the skull was crushed, but the place of the drawn area high from the eyes until the horns is whole; and one should be stringent and declare treifah in both.
Even though the membrane is intact ; that is, all membranes, lower and upper ; Shach; and it is simple that treifah is not because of the membrane but treifah because of the bone itself; therefore even if it lived twelve months it is treifah, for living does not help for definite treifot; and the words of Maharshal in explanation of Elu Treifot, siman 80, require further study.
