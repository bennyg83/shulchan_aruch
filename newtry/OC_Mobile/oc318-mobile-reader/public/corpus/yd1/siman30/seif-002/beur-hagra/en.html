It lost substance, etc. — Eruvin 7a, and as written in Bekhorot 37b and in Chullin 54b, and in Rashi there, s.v. kesela. And so ruled all the poskim, not like Rabbeinu Tam and his colleagues in Tosafot there, 42b, s.v. ve'amar, and in Bekhorot there, s.v. kedei, and in Tosafot in Eruvin there, s.v. kegon, etc.: (Likut) It lost substance, etc. — not like Rabbeinu Tam who explained that what is written "and likewise for treifah" does not refer except to the spine and not to the skull; and in Tosafot 42b, s.v. ve'ari, etc., and there 54b, s.v. kesela, etc. (meaning that therefore Rabbeinu Tam explains there not like Rashi); and in Bekhorot and so forth — but Rashba and Shitah Mekubetzet refuted his words at length there and brought proof from what is taught, 57b: and in the skull, etc. — R' Yehudah ben haMeshullam, etc. — evidently that also for treifah it is taught there (end of matter).
And if it was pierced, etc. — Rif and Tosafot there, s.v. mitztarfim.
That have deficiency. There in the Gemara, for it is compared to forbidden in the skull, and there is deficiency. Beit Yosef.
That it is one-third, etc. — there 50b, and according to Tosafot there, s.v. lekhashemitach, etc.; and according to his opinion here it must be that what is written there "kesela kosherah," etc., requires a cord a little more than a tefach; and in siman 48, seif 4, he did not write thus; and see Shach.
But less, etc. For we are not stringent except in a matter that requires examination because we are not expert in examination, as written below; but here examination is not needed from what is written; and these treifot in a bird — the blow, etc. — and that is why examination is needed, as written in the Gemara; and in behemah it says there 54a: and these are kosher, etc. — it lost substance, etc. — and that is less than kesela.
And bar oveza. That it too is called oveza, and it is more in the water.
In its teeth. For in its hand — in every place treifah because of drusah. Rashi there, and in the Gemara there 53a.
Or that it was struck, etc. — Tur, and one reason for them because of the membrane.
One places, etc., or, etc. — these two explanations are explained by Rif, and so too Rambam and Tur.
And we, etc. — in Rashi there 52a, s.v. katav behag, etc., and the Mordechai agreed with behag at the end of chapter 4, and wrote: we do not say "it was slaughtered, it is permitted" except in a matter in which one can rely on permission, not so in deficiency of wisdom; and so Hagahot Maimoniyot; and in Or Zarua it is also written that Rashi only wrote for his generation, but in our generations wisdom has declined, etc.; and so all the later authorities agreed; and above siman 57, se'if 8; and see at the beginning of chapter 2 of Niddah.
