One may not rely on him. Explanation: to buy from him.
And some say even from one who is not suspected, etc. Darkei Moshe wrote: however the custom is not so, but we presume every person in a presumption of kosher status; and since the Rav did not write here it appears we should not practice so; and it is correct, especially as we see corruption of generations and I do not presume the generation kosher.
