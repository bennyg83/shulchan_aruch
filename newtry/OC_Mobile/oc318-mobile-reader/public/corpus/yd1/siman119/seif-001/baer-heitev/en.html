To rely. Explanation: to buy from him; but they rely on him when he says regarding others that it is permitted, as the Mechaber wrote in seif 7. Shach:
Regarding prohibition. Shach wrote: some say other prohibitions that are literally of the Torah; but even wine — stam yeinam is only d'rabbanan, for they decreed upon it lest it be definite libation wine; and some say not only wine but even other matters where there is concern of prohibition that are entirely d'rabbanan, such as cheese and bread of gentiles in a place where they practice prohibition, and the like; and from the conclusion of the Rav's words it appears the same applies to milk — one does not buy from him unless he has a presumption of kosher status. And even though the Mechaber also wrote in siman 65 that one does not buy meat from any butcher who slaughters for himself and sells unless he had a presumption of kosher status — one must say a slaughtering butcher is different, for it is very common and the laws of slaughter are numerous and he can easily cause delay, pressing, or other disqualifications; and if he did not have a presumption of kosher status it is forbidden. But according to Rama in truth there is no distinction, and if one dines with him — the Rav's view is that even l'chatchila one may eat with him.
