(1) [seif 1] One suspected of eating forbidden things. A great principle: all laws of suspected persons in this siman do not require testimony before court that the man transgressed — then he would be disqualified for testimony too; even rumor suffices — an incessant rumor makes him suspected. Arukh HaShulchan s.k. 20; similarly Chakrei Lev part 1 Yoreh Deah (167): clear testimony not required — even estimation or rumor suffices. Most common people tithe yet were suspected on tithes because the minority causes suspicion — same in other matters — Rashba (64, 400), Ribash (213), Maharik (root 33), Rashdam (41), other Acharonim; see Chakrei Lev (193); Chayei Adam (1); see responsa Maaseh Ish (13); Zivchei Tzedek below s.k. 116; responsa Parashat Mordechai (7); Devarim Chaim Minsanz (4); Ramatz (12–13); Levushei Mordechai (8). However where enemies spread rumor he is not suspected — Arukh HaShulchan; Maharash Engel (17).
(1) I saw in Yad Yitzchak part 1 (5) that one is not called suspected unless an incessant rumor that he did this prohibition many times and he is unrestrained; similarly Pnei Yehoshua part 1 (16). I disagree: any suspicion he did prohibition even once — not believed; Shach below (18): suspected on prohibition not believed on it but believed on others'; habitual sinner not believed even on others'; Kenesset HaGedolah; Beli; Chokhmat Adam; Zivchei Tzedek.
(2) One may not rely on him regarding them — meaning not to buy from him; but believed to permit others' property as Mechaber below seif 7 (see there s.k.): Taz s.k. 1; Shach s.k. 2; Peri Chadash s.k. 2; Shulchan Gavoah s.k. 3; Zivchei Tzedek s.k. 1.
(3) If one dined with him he may not eat from matters he is suspected about — suspected on forbidden foods forbidden to eat with him even permitted foods lest he feed forbidden; Shabbat 11; 13; Ri'az in Shulchan Shel Rama first chapter Shabbat; Taz end s.k. 4 in Darkei Moshe from Hagahot Asheri; Kenesset HaGedolah; Beli; Maharalnach (121); Niddah 20; Rosh and Ran there.
(3) Peri Chadash end s.k. 6: Darkei Moshe from Hagahot Asheri not from core law — mere stringency and custom of pious; Lechem HaPanim s.k. 6; Shvilei David s.k. 1; question why not cite Shabbat Gemara — perhaps decree only for suspected on tithes common then; not for individual suspected; Bekor Shor on Avodah Zarah 39 — challenged Peri Chadash; Rambam 11:10 — if not careful forbidden to lodge; Mesgeret HaShulchan; Arukh HaShulchan; Eshkol HaShulchan on Rashi Avodah Zarah 38; Gra Bnei Brak Bet David 2; Maharsha responsa (7) — practical leniency; Zivchei Tzedek s.k. 5.

(5) However for buying meat — not from every butcher who slaughters for himself and sells unless presumption of kosher — Mechaber siman 65; Shach s.k. 1 — many slaughter laws, easy to disqualify; Peri Chadash s.k. 1; Shulchan Gavoah s.k. 6; Peri Toar questions Shach — still stringent; Ramatz (12); Makom Shmuel no distinction meat vs other — stringent; Shach: slaughterer for others has presumption — only self-slaughterer needs presumption; Sefatei Daat 1:1; Arukh HaShulchan s.k. 8; Shvilei David s.k. 1; Maharam Schick (12); Chatam Sofer (19); Amudim Or (42); Nehar Efrayim (49).
(6) Nevertheless l'chatchila though we are lenient buying from plain person — fear Rambam in this generation corruption; Taz s.k. 2; Beit Hillel siman 65 — registry requiring rabbinical certificate even for presumed kosher; lost certificate still rely if presumed; Beli; Lechem HaPanim s.k. 2; Kerem Shlomo; especially now; Arukh HaShulchan s.k. 9; Kenesset HaGedolah letter 8 from Rashdam 90 — if reason to fear without presumption, stringent; Beli; Mesgeret HaShulchan; Zivchei Tzedek s.k. 3; Chokhmat Adam 91:1 — buy meat wine milk bread only from recognized presumption lest "before the blind"; responsa Efrakasta DeAnya (70); Ramatz (12); Bekor Shor Avodah Zarah 39; Nahara Mitzrayim.
(7) If one dined with him he may eat with him — not that l'chatchila forbidden to lodge to eat; even suspected for selling may dine seif 2; Mechaber "if one dined" teaches even b'dieved forbidden to eat from his suspected matters; Rama same b'dieved wording; Shach s.k. 4; Kenesset HaGedolah; Peri Chadash s.k. 4; Beli s.k. 3; Lechem HaPanim s.k. 4.
