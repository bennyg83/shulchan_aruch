(1) [seif 1] One suspected of eating forbidden things. A great principle: all laws of suspected persons in this siman do not require testimony before court that the man transgressed — then he would be disqualified for testimony too; even rumor suffices — an incessant rumor makes him suspected. Arukh HaShulchan s.k. 20; similarly Chakrei Lev part 1 Yoreh Deah (167): clear testimony not required — even estimation or rumor suffices. Most common people tithe yet were suspected on tithes because the minority causes suspicion — same in other matters — Rashba (64, 400), Ribash (213), Maharik (root 33), Rashdam (41), other Acharonim; see Chakrei Lev (193); Chayei Adam (1); see responsa Maaseh Ish (13); Zivchei Tzedek below s.k. 116; responsa Parashat Mordechai (7); Devarim Chaim Minsanz (4); Ramatz (12–13); Levushei Mordechai (8). However where enemies spread rumor he is not suspected — Arukh HaShulchan; Maharash Engel (17).
(1) I saw in Yad Yitzchak part 1 (5) that one is not called suspected unless an incessant rumor that he did this prohibition many times and he is unrestrained; similarly Pnei Yehoshua part 1 (16). I disagree: any suspicion he did prohibition even once — not believed; Shach below (18): suspected on prohibition not believed on it but believed on others'; habitual sinner not believed even on others'; Kenesset HaGedolah; Beli; Chokhmat Adam; Zivchei Tzedek.
(1) Even though generally a person does not make himself wicked — if he spread rumor on himself that he transgressed or sold prohibition to others he is suspected; Choshen Mishpat 34:25, 92:5 — not made witness ab initio; likewise for prohibition reliability — not one witness on prohibitions l'chatchila; Hashiv Moshe (22); Ramatz (13); She'el uMeshiv Kama (51); see Haflaah Ketubot 72 on Raavad brought in Rashba and Ran; Noda BiYehudah Even HaEzer 256 — "do not place wicked as witness" only when liable lashes; misleading others not disqualified for testimony — still suspected; see Ramatz (4).
