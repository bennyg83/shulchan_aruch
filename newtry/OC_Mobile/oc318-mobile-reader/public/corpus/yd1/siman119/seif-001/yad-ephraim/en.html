Matters where there is concern of prohibition. Baer Heitev; see Peri Megadim part 2 siman 116: one about whom rumor spread that he had several jugs of stam yeinam to sell to Jews while presumed kosher, and he says the stam yeinam in his hand he already sold to a gentile and it was kosher, and the gentiles contradict him and doubt fell on other wines he brought whether he is believed they were made kosher — ruled that since he was compelled by community decree and judges to clarify to whom he sold his yayin nesech and he says to gentiles in such-and-such village they seized and confessed on their word — we presume him a liar and do not rely on him until he swears a severe oath as the judge sees fit that he sold to those gentiles and all wines he brought into the community were made kosher per our Torah law; if he does not swear he remains suspected and the wine he brought is presumed stam yeinam; responsa Pri Chadash Yoreh Deah siman 14 on reliability of those suspected in prohibitions and many distinctions regarding habitual sinners for appetite in slaughter or other prohibitions.
