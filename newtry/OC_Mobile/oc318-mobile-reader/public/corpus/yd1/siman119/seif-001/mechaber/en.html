One who is suspected regarding a forbidden matter may not be relied upon regarding edible matters. It contains 20 seifim: One who is suspected of eating forbidden things — whether he is suspected of a d'oraisa prohibition or a d'rabbanan prohibition — may not be relied upon regarding them; and if one dines with him he may not eat from his food regarding matters he is suspected about:
{Rama: And some say even from one who is not suspected but whom they do not recognize as having a presumption of kosher status — it is forbidden to buy wine or other things where there is concern of prohibition from him; however if one dines with him one may eat with him (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam chapter 1 of Forbidden Foods and chapter 3 law 21):}
