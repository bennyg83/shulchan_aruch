One who sells, etc. As Yerushalmi chapter 3 Yevamot — R' Yudan asked what proof from their words for tereifah sight, etc.; see siman 39 seif 1–2.
And if he sold from an animal, etc. Meaning seller cannot say bring proof it is tereifah — as muggeret in Bava Kama 99b: since safek tereifah no greater defect — mistaken transaction as if certainly tereifah; unlike animal not checked standing on presumption of permission Chullin 9a; unlike firstborn — Rashi Bechorot s.v. he returns, etc.
