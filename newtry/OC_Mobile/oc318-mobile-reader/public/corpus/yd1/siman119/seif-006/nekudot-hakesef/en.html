I again found as my words in Semak Choshen Mishpat siman 234: only for certain tereifah; but for safek tereifah he need not return money if eaten; and a slaughterer who sold a cow and it became known tereifah — he returns even if already eaten — meaning certainly tereifah; and firstborn differs for an animal is presumed without defect; also firstborn not checked — even if afterward known it had a defect — still forbidden in benefit d'rabbanan as mishnah chapter Ad Kamah and Tur Yoreh Deah siman 310; since it is benefit-prohibition the seller does not deduct from payment and the reason is sensible: even if intact the seller would have to discard and not benefit — therefore no law of sale; as above; and clear from his words even if the seller knew before sale it is safek tereifah he need not return if eaten; if he did not know, even certain tereifah they do not penalize as Shach s.k. 25.
