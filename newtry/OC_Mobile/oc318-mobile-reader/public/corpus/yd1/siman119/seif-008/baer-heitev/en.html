Under oath. The reason: since he is already suspected regarding the oath to transgress, for he is under oath from Sinai; and even though the oath from Sinai is for a past transgression, since he is under oath and also transgressed a sin he is worse still; and further there is no distinction between a future oath or a past oath except regarding monetary matters, but not regarding prohibition — see Choshen Mishpat siman 92 seif 2 and siman 87 seif 15. And it appears all the more he is not believed regarding a lighter matter than it under oath. Maharshal wrote: nevertheless one suspected regarding stam yeinam as in these generations may be believed regarding yayin nesech when he swears on it, only they should specify the oath well that he is not under oath from Sinai, because he is lenient regarding stam yeinam which is d'rabbanan, end of his words; and he wrote he so ruled in practice; and perhaps he did not rule so except regarding stam yeinam because people are lenient about it, but one should not rule so regarding other d'rabbanan prohibitions; and even regarding cheese one should not believe one suspected about it even under oath, for even regarding d'rabbanan prohibitions one may say he is under oath from Sinai for the prohibition "do not turn aside," end of his words Shach.
