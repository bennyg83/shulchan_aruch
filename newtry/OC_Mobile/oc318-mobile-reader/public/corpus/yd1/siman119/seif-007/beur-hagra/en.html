(Lekut) Or does not believe, etc. Like a Samaritan — not only regarding others' property is he not believed, for "before the blind do not place a stumbling block," as first chapter of Chullin (4a) and explicit in Niddah (57a); but even regarding his own it is forbidden, for we establish like R' Eliezer as in Gittin and as above siman 2 seif 9; all the more he is not believed regarding his own because of "before the blind" as above (until here).
And regarding others' property he is believed even regarding that matter to say it is permitted — only regarding prohibition we say so; but regarding monetary matters one suspected regarding one matter is disqualified to testify regarding another even regarding others', as Choshen Mishpat siman 34. And some say here and siman 124 seif 9 the Rav ruled like the Mechaber; Choshen Mishpat 34:205 and Even HaEzer end seif 42 — Rav wrote one suspected regarding sexual immorality is disqualified for testimony about a woman; there the reason is interest in testimony. Difficult: we decide halachah end Yoma and Bechorot 39b like Rashbag against R' Meir; Rashba 64 and Ribash 4 answered anonymous mishnah then dispute; Raavad: Rashbag disputes R' Meir only on blemishes; Demai ch.4 after dark — Yerushalmi Shabbat honor; entering city proves guest leniency; Rambam only when unrecognized; Rashbag believed on another's property; plain person per Rambam not trusted but believed on others unlike suspected; prohibitions even woman and slave believed siman 118:8; Rashba 64, 400; difficult Rama on appetite — see siman 2 s.k. 12.
