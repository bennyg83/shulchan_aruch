One who is notorious for one of the transgressions in the Torah except idolatry and public desecration of Shabbat, or who does not believe in the words of our Sages of blessed memory — is believed regarding other prohibitions; and regarding others' property he is believed even regarding that matter to say it is permitted: {Rama: One who is suspected regarding a matter that does not appear to people as a transgression is not called suspected (Rashba siman 64); however regarding that matter he is not believed. One who practices prohibition in some matter because he holds the law is so, or because of a stringency he adopted — may eat with others who practice leniency regarding it, for they will certainly not feed him what he practices as forbidden (Hagahot Mordechai first chapter of Yevamot and Binyamin Zeev 312 and R' Yerucham end Netiv 3 in the name of Rama):}
