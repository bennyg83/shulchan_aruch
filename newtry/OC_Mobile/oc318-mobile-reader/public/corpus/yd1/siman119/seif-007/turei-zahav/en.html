One who is suspected regarding a matter, etc. Also wondrous is Rama's view to me — Rashba in responsum Beit Yosef brought only for testimony disqualification, as he brings later for cemetery on eve of festival; but for reliability to eat with him or buy from him — permitted in other matters even intentional transgression knowing it is forbidden; if regarding that matter he does not appear as transgression to people, plainly others should not rely since he thinks it permitted — as Rama concludes here; nevertheless what is the practical difference — he is not called suspected.
