Suspected. Shach wrote the reason: because he is a habitual sinner for that matter but not for other matters — plainly if suspected for one matter he is not suspected for other matters, unlike Bach, see there. And Taz wrote Beit Yosef brought here in the name of Rambam that a suspected person and all the more a gentile is believed regarding others' property to testify that he tithed, for a person does not sin without benefit to himself; and he disagrees, for we establish one suspected regarding a matter is neither judged nor testifies, and so one should rule in practice, end of his words.
