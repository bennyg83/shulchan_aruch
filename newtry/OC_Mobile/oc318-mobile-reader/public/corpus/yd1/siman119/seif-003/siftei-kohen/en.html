Not concerned he will substitute — Tur/Rav siman 118:1 deposit with suspected Israelite d'oraisa two seals d'rabbanan one — there suspected to substitute; abbreviated not explaining who is suspected; Bach: there suspected on matters public not lenient (as Mechaber siman 118:8 and here); milk, morayis, bread, cheese deposited with suspected even without seal.
Since not suspected of theft — also suspected to sell because suspected to eat (above seif 1); only for selling he instructs heter; not suspected to steal or substitute by hand.
Innkeeper sometimes ashamed — Taz: Chullin 6 explains innkeeper instructs heter for herself good, guest hot/cold comfortably; Rashba: where known not ashamed of guest as modern innkeepers — permitted; Tosafot opposite — takes good, gives guest bad because she toiled; where he pays wages perhaps permitted; Tosafot Shevuot 48 and Mordechai — agent with wages cannot impose oath for then no heter instruction; even Rambam chapter 9 Sheluchin — even with wages can impose oath lest wages improper — perhaps concedes innkeeper wages.
