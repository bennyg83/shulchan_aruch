What he ate he ate — penalty Rashi Bechorot end Kol Pesulei HaMukdashin; only when known knew forbidden and sold intentionally penalize; else perhaps inadvertent; seif 17 butcher no excuse — prohibition not money; Taz: even safek tereifah ate and returns money, eating not benefit.
He shall pay — since did not cause prohibition. Rashi.
If ate — d'oraisa penalized; d'rabbanan eating considered benefit; money established on Torah law — Sages did not decree prohibition to extract money; Rambam chapter 16 Sales; Yerushalmi Shevi'it — d'oraisa forbidden commerce d'rabbanan permitted; same here sale; see Choshen Mishpat 234.
