One who sells his fellow something forbidden to eat — if before he ate it became known, he returns what he bought from him and he returns the money; and if after he ate it became known — what he ate he ate and he returns the money; and if the buyer sold it to a gentile or threw it to dogs he pays him the value of tereifah; and if it was something not forbidden to eat but of rabbinic origin, or if the produce still exists — he returns the produce and takes its value; and if he ate them — what he ate he ate and the seller returns nothing; and all benefit-prohibitions even of rabbinic origin — he returns the money and there is no law of sale at all.
