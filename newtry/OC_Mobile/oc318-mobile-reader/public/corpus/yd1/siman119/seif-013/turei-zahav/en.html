What he ate he ate, etc. So in mishnah Bechorot end of chapter Kol Pesulei HaMukdashin; Rashi: returns money because of penalty of safek prohibition; appears only when known he knew they were forbidden and sold intentionally we penalize; if not, perhaps inadvertent; and though seif 17 butcher has no excuse — that is prohibition not money; Taz appears even safek tereifah — what he ate he ate and returns money, eating not considered benefit.
Rabbinic matters. If produce still exists — so it must be; this is Rambam's view that in d'rabbanan prohibition once he ate and benefited he does not return.
