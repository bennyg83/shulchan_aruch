To a place where he is not recognized, etc. Maharshal: nowadays generation is unrestrained and if sages see he cannot exile because dependents hang on him they give repentance locally with restriction; one transgression makes him a stumbling block — warn him; nevertheless forbid vessels retroactively in doubt even first time; Shach: even first sale of forbidden removes him; Mechaber seif 17; Choshen Mishpat some say butcher and teacher need presumption before removal — Ribash: there only monetary loss or misled/inadvertent; suspected feeding prohibition — remove immediately; Rashba: butcher tereifah no inadvertent excuse; vessels siman 1; Beit Yosef Choshen Mishpat siman 34 Rambam: inspector for self needs exile after repentance; for others no monetary benefit — repentance suffices.
And what is written in the following seif "we remove him" — Beit Yosef wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 34 in the view of Rambam: if he examines for himself, since he has monetary benefit from it, even if he repented he is not rehabilitated until he goes to a place where he is not recognized; but one who examines for others, since he has no monetary benefit from it, he is rehabilitated through repentance.
