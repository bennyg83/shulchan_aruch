Suspected. Shach wrote: he is not called suspected regarding that matter until he transgresses not for appetite — such as if forbidden and permitted were placed before him he would eat the forbidden; and some say regarding a habitual sinner who eats nevelah for appetite, it is forbidden to eat from his slaughter until he checks the knife for him, as written in siman 2, because knives are often nicked — therefore we are concerned he will transgress for appetite and not trouble to fix it. But here it deals with a matter where there is no concern he will now transgress for appetite; therefore he is not called suspected. Taz wrote: if he is so suspected that even when permitted food is before him he abandons it and eats forbidden — he is like a full gentile; therefore he wrote this Rama gloss has no explanation at all, see there. And Darkei Moshe wrote in the name of Hagahot Asheri that one suspected of a prohibition such that it is forbidden to eat with him — even permitted things, lest he feed him forbidden things as well, end of his words.
