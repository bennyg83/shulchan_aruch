He does not make libation wine. The reason: a gentile's statement is plausible as false; when he says he believes it is for evil inclination's pleasure and our faith is good and true; and when he tells us he is a Jew he says wholeheartedly because our faith is faithful and true and the matter is plain that for all prohibitions he is not believed; only he does not make yayin nesech being truly a Jew; but for reliability in prohibitions since he worships idols for desire plainly not believed.
