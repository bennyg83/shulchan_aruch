One who says to one suspected of eating gentile cheese: buy me kosher cheese from the expert — and he went and brought it and says: I bought kosher from the expert — he is not believed; from so-and-so the expert I bought — he is believed. If he brought him a gift in the name of one of the experts — he is believed that he is not suspected to substitute. In what case? When he is not suspected of theft; but if he is suspected of theft — all the more so he is suspected of substitution. {Rama: (And all this does not apply to one suspected of a d'oraisa prohibition) (Beit Yosef):}
