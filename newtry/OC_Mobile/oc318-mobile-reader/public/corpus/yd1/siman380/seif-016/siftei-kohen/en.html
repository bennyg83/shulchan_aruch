But through others it is permitted. This implies that it is permitted even when no loss is involved, as the Levush states explicitly. Although above, in seifim 2 and 5, work is forbidden even through others, here it is permitted because the principal labor is not the mourner's own; he may receive his wages from the owner. The Rama derived this from the Rosh's discussion of Rashi. The permission applies where the mourner was hired for the work before mourning and others perform it for the employer. It does not permit the mourner to accept new work during mourning and have others do it for his profit. The distinctions stated by the authorities must therefore be maintained.
