For wages. The Taz wrote: do not ask from what was written in seif 16, that if the mourner is hired to others he may not work during his mourning. There it speaks of full labor, which is forbidden to a mourner in every manner. Here, however, it is household work, which is permitted even for the wife herself, as written in the preceding seif; therefore it is permitted even for a maidservant. But in any event the maidservant should not do work that is not for the needs of the house, but for profit, and all the more so she should not leave the house, like the law of a mourner. I saw someone rule regarding a shamash to whom mourning occurred that he should do his work, go to the synagogue, and serve the needs of the community; he did not rule well, for he misunderstood the reason for this permissibility of a maidservant. Rashal wrote in the name of Agudah that a mourner may have bloodlet. Kol Bo wrote that a mourner who performs labor is placed in nidui. End quote.
