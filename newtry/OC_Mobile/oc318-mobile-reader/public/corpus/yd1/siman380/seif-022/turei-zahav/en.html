And likewise a woman who serves, etc. One should not ask: he wrote in seif 16 that if the mourner is hired to others, he should not do work during his mourning days. There, it speaks of full labor, which is forbidden to a mourner in every case. This is not so regarding work for household needs, which is permitted to the woman herself, as written in the preceding seif; therefore it is permitted even for a maidservant. But in any event the maidservant should not do work that is not for household needs but for profit, and all the more so she should not leave the house, as is the law of a mourner. I saw someone rule regarding a shamash to whom mourning occurred that he should do his work and go to the synagogue and serve the needs of the congregation, but he did not rule well, for he erred in the reason for this law permitting a maidservant. Maharshal wrote in the name of the Agudah that a mourner may undergo bloodletting. The Kol Bo wrote that a mourner who does work is placed in nidui.
