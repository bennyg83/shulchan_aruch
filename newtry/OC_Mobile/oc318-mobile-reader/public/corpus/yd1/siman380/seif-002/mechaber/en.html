Melacha — how so? All three first days forbidden in melacha, even a poor person sustained from charity; from then on, if he is poor and has nothing to eat, he does work in private within his house; and a woman may spin on a distaff within her house; but the Sages said, A curse should come upon his neighbors who compelled him to this: {Rama: (As one is permitted to write on chol haMoed, so permitted to write on mourning days, but not another matter) (Kol Bo in the name of R' Meir of Rothenburg and Beit Yosef end of this).}
