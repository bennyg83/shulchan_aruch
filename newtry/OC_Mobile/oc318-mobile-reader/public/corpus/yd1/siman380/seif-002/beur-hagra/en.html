(Gleanings) Every three days, etc. Even though in the Yerushalmi — and the Rosh cited it there — the first Tanna holds two days, the halachah follows Bar Kappara there. And from what is stated in our Gemara — that it is the teaching of Bar Kappara — and similarly all that was stated in the Alfasi (chapter 6; and see below siman 393 seif katan 2): "two days" according to Bar Kappara means three days, and so is the halachah.
