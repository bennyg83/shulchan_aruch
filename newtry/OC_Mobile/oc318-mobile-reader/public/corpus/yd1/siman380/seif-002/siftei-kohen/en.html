And a woman spins with a spindle in her house. Meaning: they permitted her only private and temporary work, such as spinning with a spindle in her house. If that is not enough for her, she does her craft privately inside her house. This is the wording of the Tur.
Just as it is permitted to write on chol ha-moed, etc. As explained in Orach Chayim siman 545; see there.
