There in Tosafot in the name of the Rav's explanation, and so is the implication of the Gemara there; so too it is taught in Masekhet Semachot. So wrote the Rambam there in chapter 5, and so wrote almost all the poskim. The Yerushalmi explains the reason: for all three days the soul hovers over the body, thinking it will return to it; once it sees that the appearance of the face has changed, it leaves and goes. Rabbenu Yerucham wrote in the name of the Raavad that the reason is because the sages said three days are for weeping, and if he engages in labor he will become negligent in weeping.
There in Masekhet Semachot.
