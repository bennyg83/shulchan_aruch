The wording of the Tur, based on the statement of Shmuel as explained by Rav Pappa and Rav Mesharshiya there, page 12a, and following Rashi's first explanation. Even if the work was accepted as a fixed-price contract before the mourning began, it is prohibited because of appearances, lest people say, 'See how this person gave his work to others during his mourning,' not knowing that it had already been contracted to them.
