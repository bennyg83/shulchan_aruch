Detached. For he can do it privately; and all the more so something attached, which he can do only publicly. Shach.
Others. The Levush wrote: it appears that even if it is not a case of loss, it is permitted to do it through others, since the main work is not his, as written in seif 17 regarding one who himself is hired out. The Shach wrote that the master's view appears to be that only there, where he is hired for a month or months, is it permitted, unlike here. According to what I wrote above, that where he himself is hired it is forbidden through others unless it is a case of loss, all the more so here; end quote.
