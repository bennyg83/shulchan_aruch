Baraita, Moed Katan 11b, as the Raavad wrote: because the field owner is a mourner, they should not refrain from working it.
The Rosh, from the implication of the baraita. He wrote in the name of the Raavad that the reason is that land is meant for sharecropping, etc.; since this person was already the sharecropper from before, presumably he does not remove him, and it is like a field that had been rented to him from the outset.
