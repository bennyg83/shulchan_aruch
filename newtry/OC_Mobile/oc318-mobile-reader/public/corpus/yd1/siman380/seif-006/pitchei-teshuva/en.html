Within his house. Raanach, part 2, responsum 93, rules that even if partners agreed in advance that when one becomes a mourner the other will conduct the business and take that week's profit, while the mourner receives another week's profit in exchange, it is forbidden. Since the prohibition is due to appearance, advance stipulation does not help because observers do not know of it. A Jew and non-Jew may make such a Shabbat arrangement because it recurs weekly and people assume an agreement, unlike an unusual mourning event.
