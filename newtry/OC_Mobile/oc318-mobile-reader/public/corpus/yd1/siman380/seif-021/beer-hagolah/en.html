The Yerushalmi, cited by the Rif in chapter 2 of Moed Katan, states that it is forbidden for everyone. In the incident of Maryon son of Ravin and the accompanying discussion in the Bavli, 11b, the Gemara concludes that a distinguished person is different. The Raavad reconciles the sources by explaining that the Yerushalmi concerns public conduct, and the Rambam writes likewise in chapter 5, whereas the Bavli concerns conduct within one's home. See siman 100.
