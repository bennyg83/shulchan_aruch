Forbidden to have his work done by others: {Rama: (even a non-Jew) (Mordechai beginning chapter Mi SheHafkhah);} unless it is a matter of loss, for a matter of loss a mourner is permitted through others; even something forbidden on chol haMoed due to exertion is permitted, even skilled craft: {Rama: Some say if he cannot do through others and it is a matter of loss, the mourner may do himself (Tur in the name of Ramban and Rosh and so Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote); and after the first three days one may be lenient, even within three if the loss is great.}
