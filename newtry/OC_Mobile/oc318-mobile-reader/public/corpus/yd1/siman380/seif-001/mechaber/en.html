Things forbidden to a mourner. And it contains 25 seifim. These are things forbidden to a mourner: melacha, bathing, anointing, wearing shoes, use of the bed; forbidden to read Torah; forbidden to greet; forbidden laundry; obligated in covering the head and overturning the bed all seven days; forbidden to put on tefillin on the first day; forbidden pressing clothes, haircut, joy, and sewing a tear all thirty days:
{Rama: (and all matters of mourning apply both by day and by night) (in responsum of Rashba siman 400).}
