(Siman 281 Taz s.k. 1) His words — in explaining Tur's words — are not plausible; the matter is simple that because of the end clause Tur mentioned "and one does not read in it for endings" and even though one does not read in it, they are obligated to buy it from him, etc.; also what he meant invalid that is not evident — such as not processed for its sake — and similar should be forbidden to read even individually — is not plausible and not comparable to doubtful tereifah, for there specifically with many it is forbidden because we fear mishap; and even according to what we practice to be stringent even in one — that is specifically tereifah where we fear mishap, for it is disgraceful to eat forbidden matter; and similarly Tosafot first chapter of Shabbat 12b regarding Rabbi Yishmael who read and leaned — or because there we fear not everyone knows it is tereifah; or lest it go by itself to another house that does not know it is tereifah; but here since a sefer Torah's way is to be in synagogue and this one reads individually and never in synagogue — the matter is evident it is invalid and there is no misleading; and one can write on the column and similar that it is invalid.
