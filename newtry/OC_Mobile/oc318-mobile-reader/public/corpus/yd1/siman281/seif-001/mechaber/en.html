Who is fit to write a Sefer Torah, and if the scribe said he did not write the divine Names for their sake. In it are 8 seifim.
A Sefer Torah that an apikorus wrote—one burns it. That a Canaanite wrote—one puts it in genizah. Found in the hand of an apikorus and it is not known who wrote it—genizah. Found in the hand of a Canaanite and it is not known who wrote it—some validate it; and some say genizah. And the public is obligated to buy it from him for its value, or a little more, and put it in genizah so he not treat it lightly. And if he wishes to raise its price much beyond its value, they leave it in his hand. And if Canaanites were presumed to plunder Israel's books, we presume those books were theirs and read from them.
