The matter is straightforward. Payment for writing a Sefer Torah is far greater than payment for writing a Chumash. If the parchment was not processed for the sake of a Sefer Torah, the defect can never be repaired to make it valid as a Sefer Torah; the scribe receives only the fee for a Chumash and loses greatly. When the Gemara says that he loses his entire wage, it means the entire wage for a Sefer Torah, and therefore he is believed. Regarding improperly written Divine Names, however, he loses only the fee for those Names, while the Sefer Torah becomes fully valid once they are corrected; therefore he is not believed.
