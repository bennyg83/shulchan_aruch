Informer. See the book Chomot Yerushalayim siman 269, which wrote that this informer is only when he has been established — see Choshen Mishpat siman 388; otherwise since he concluded, etc. However, when one says "I will go and inform" — if afterward he wrote STaM, some say they are disqualified if he then went and informed — see there; and he elaborated greatly on these people who are disqualified from writing STaM.
