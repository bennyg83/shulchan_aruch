Gentiles wrote it. See responsum of Noda BiYehudah second series Yoreh De'ah siman 181 — in name of one carved by a gentile craftsman on the Holy Ark — if burial is required; and he wrote only if the gentile writes for himself is there concern perhaps he wrote it for idol worship; but where he wrote for an Israelite and the matter itself belongs to an Israelite, we are not concerned he wrote for idol worship. And further, necessarily we are concerned only when the gentile knows what he is writing — that it is a Name; but a gentile generally does not know Assyrian script — only an Israelite permits him with ink, and the gentile does no more than monkey action; certainly there is no concern perhaps for idols [he wrote] and burial is not required — see there.
