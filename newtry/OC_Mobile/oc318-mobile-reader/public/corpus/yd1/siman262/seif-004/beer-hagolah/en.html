From the incident of the man who came before Rava, etc., "I heard the child cry," etc., Niddah 42b. Rif and Rosh recorded it in the nineteenth chapter of Shabbat.
Rabbeinu Yerucham, based on the cited incident. He writes that Ramah ruled likewise, since while the child is in the woman's womb, its mouth is closed.
