One born at twilight — we count for him from the night and circumcise on the ninth, which is a doubt whether it is the eighth; and if he brought his head outside to the airlock while it was still day, or they heard him crying — even if he was born days later — we count eight days from the day his head came out or from the day they heard him cry: {Rama: And if he was born at twilight on Friday eve, we circumcise him on Sunday, for we do not circumcise on Shabbat because of a doubt. (Tur.)}
