And to draw covenant blood. See in Ahavat Tziyon; and see in responsum Sha'agat Aryeh simanim 53 and 54, who elaborated on this and concluded that where one was circumcised within its time or after its time at night and did nothing — even after the fact he did not fulfill — nevertheless one need not return and draw covenant blood — see there. And see in Sefer Nofet Tzufim parashat Lekh Lekha; and in responsum Yeri'ot HaOhel siman 13 [and in responsum Knesset Yechezkel siman 42]; and in She'elat Ya'avetz part 1 siman 34 what they wrote on this matter.
