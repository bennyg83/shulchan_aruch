He cries. If the mother was asleep and the infant was heard crying before confirmed birth, the crying does not establish birth leniently. If the eighth day would be Shabbat, he is not circumcised then, because the possibility remains that the head had not emerged. Even on a weekday one should count eight days from confirmed birth.
