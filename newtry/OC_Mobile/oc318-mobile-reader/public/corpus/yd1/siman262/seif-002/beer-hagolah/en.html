Mishnah Shabbat 137a.
A statement of Shmuel there.
An inquiry there that was not resolved in Shabbat. In Yevamot 71b, however, it is explicitly resolved in the Talmud. Rif and the other halachic authorities wrote likewise.
There, in accordance with the baraita. Rashi explains that he was ill, recovered, and regained his strength when the illness passed from him.
From Rav Pappa's interpretation there on the second side of the folio.
