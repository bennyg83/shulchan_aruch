We wait until he recovers. Because of pain or even a slight illness we postpone circumcision until the child recovers, so that he not come to danger. The Gaon likewise writes: any infant who is suffering, whether from illness or from another cause, is not circumcised until he recovers. Nimukei Yosef, cited by Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
Severe pain, etc. It appears that this means in the manner explained in Orach Chayyim, siman 328, seif 9. Nimukei Yosef likewise implies this; see there and carefully consider the proof he brings.
