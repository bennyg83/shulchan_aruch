And then. Turei Zahav wrote: I saw in Bedek HaBayit that he wrote in the name of Rashbatz that nevertheless we do not circumcise him on Thursday, because on Wednesday the circumcised one has pain and one should not cause pain on Shabbat; and per this it seems all the more one should not circumcise him on Friday, for there is greater pain, as written in siman 266 in the name of Rashba and Ramban — end quote. But Shach in siman 266 disagrees and wrote it is proven from the Talmud and poskim that circumcision not at its time is permitted on Thursday, and so appears to be the main view — see there.
Great pain. Shach wrote: it appears this refers to the manner explained in Orach Chaim siman 328 s.k. 9, and so implies Nekudot HaKesef — see there — end quote.
