Because the zealous act promptly. Shevut Yaakov protests cantors who prolong Shabbat or Yom Tov services past midday when a circumcision is pending, thereby delaying the mitzvah. Noda BiYehudah strongly condemns postponing a recovered child's circumcision until Erev Pesach for the convenience of firstborns, because delay violates zeal, risks loss of the mitzvah, may cause Shabbat desecration, and does not necessarily permit the firstborns to eat.
