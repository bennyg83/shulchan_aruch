And to draw covenant blood. Turei Zahav wrote: this gloss is puzzling for two reasons — first, it implies an obligation, and if so even on Shabbat one should return and draw blood, etc., but this is not so, for most poskim do not hold thus; therefore it is plain there is only a stringency here and on Shabbat one should not do so. Further, in what he wrote here at the end "they circumcised within eight and by day he fulfilled" — it implies one need not return and draw blood, etc. — what differs from the beginning, for certainly within eight is no better than at night; and so is proven in siman 264 that within eight is not circumcision at all — end quote. And on this law, if they circumcised within eight, Shach too agreed and ruled that by law one must return and draw blood, etc., and Shabbat is not set aside for this; but if they circumcised at night he did not fulfill even after the fact — one may say he must draw blood even on Shabbat eve.
