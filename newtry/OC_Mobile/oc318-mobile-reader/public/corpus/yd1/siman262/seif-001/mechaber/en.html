The time of circumcision for a healthy child, a sick one, and an androgynous. And it contains 7 seifim. We do not circumcise until the sun has risen on the eighth day from his birth (and from when dawn breaks he has left the night) (Beit Yosef from Mishnah chapter 2 of Megillah); and the entire day is fit for circumcision, but the zealous precede the mitzvah and circumcise immediately in the morning.
