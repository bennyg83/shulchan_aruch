(Siman 262, seif 17, seif katan 3) What he wrote in Rashba's name. There it is cited in the name of Tashbetz, and Bedek HaBayit concludes: “They wait until the next day.” Thus, circumcision on the following day, Friday, is permitted. How then did Taz write that all the more so one may not circumcise on Friday? The primary reason that one does not circumcise on Thursday, because the child will suffer pain, is that on the third day the infant may be in danger and they will need to violate Shabbos. This is like the rule that one does not sail within three days before Shabbos. Therefore, Taz's words in this passage are exceedingly puzzling. Birkhei Yosef, Even HaEzer, siman 248:
