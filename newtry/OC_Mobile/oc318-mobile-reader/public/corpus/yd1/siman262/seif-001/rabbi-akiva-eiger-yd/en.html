Who wrote in the name of Rashba. There the source is cited in the name of Tashbetz, and Bedek HaBayit concludes that one waits until the next day, implying that circumcision is permitted on Friday. How, then, could Taz write that all the more it should not be performed on Friday? Moreover, the source's reason for avoiding Thursday is that the third day after circumcision is dangerous and may require desecration of Shabbat, like the prohibition against setting sail within three days of Shabbat. Taz's words are therefore very difficult. Birkei Yosef.
