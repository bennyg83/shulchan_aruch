When the sun rises. The reason is that at that point he has certainly left the possible period of night. Tur does not mention sunrise, because the verse says "on the day," not at night, meaning at a time that is certainly day and not night.
He must again draw, etc. This gloss is astonishing in two respects. First, it implies an obligation. If so, even on Shabbos he would have to draw covenantal blood again, which is not so. Only Hagahos Maimoniyos holds that one must do so; the other poskim do not, as Beit Yosef writes. Necessarily, this is only a stringency, and on Shabbos one should not do it. Second, the concluding statement here that if he circumcised during the day before the eighth day he fulfilled the mitzvah implies that no renewed drawing of blood is needed. Why should that be better than circumcision at night? Indeed, siman 264 shows that circumcision before eight days is not considered circumcision at all.
