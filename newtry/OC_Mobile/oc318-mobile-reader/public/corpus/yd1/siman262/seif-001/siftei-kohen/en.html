He transgressed and circumcised, etc. "they circumcised within eight," etc. — meaning according to what is written in Darkei Moshe that Hagahot Maimoniyot and Rosh do not disagree that perhaps within eight is better than those circumcised at night — end quote; but in my opinion one cannot say so, as proven explicitly in Menachot end of chapter Rabbi Yishmael (Menachot 72a) regarding that which Rabbi Elazar bar Rabbi Shimon in the view of Rabbi Akiva his teacher said, etc., until "and if it enters your mind that reaped not at its time is valid — why does Shabbat defer it," etc. — see in the Talmud and Rashi; also what Maharshal, Bach, and Perishah wrote that circumcision at night is valid after the fact; and also what Rosh wrote in chapter Rabbi Eliezer of circumcision in the name of Rabbi Shimon that circumcision within eight is valid and one need not draw covenant blood — this is difficult on that Talmud there, for it is proven he did not fulfill, for if it were valid why does circumcision defer Shabbat — let him fulfill by action — see there (and it can be resolved with difficulty). And the proof Rosh brought there from what is said in chapter Rabbi Eliezer of circumcision (Shabbat 137a) — where one preceded and circumcised on Shabbat eve, for Shabbat is not set aside to defer — implies one must draw covenant blood from him since Shabbat is not set aside to defer — is not a proof, for in my humble opinion one may say that although he did not fulfill, nevertheless Shabbat is set aside to defer only for the mitzvah of circumcision itself and not for drawing covenant blood; but in truth circumcision at night did not fulfill even after the fact, as stated in the Talmud there that since Shabbat defers — implies even after the fact it is invalid (also what Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe wrote from the words of responsum of Rashba below siman 264 — it does not appear so there — see there and above siman 264 s.k. 6). And also what Rambam wrote in chapter 7 of Laws of Temidin and Musafin law 6 regarding the omer — its mitzvah is to reap on the sixteenth night whether on a weekday or Shabbat, and the entire night is fit for reaping the omer, and if they reaped it by day it is valid — end quote — is puzzling, for it is stated explicitly in the Talmud in Menachot there that Shabbat defers — reaped by day not at its mitzvah time is invalid; and Rabbi who holds there reaped by day is valid holds Shabbat does not defer, and this is explained explicitly throughout that sugya — and it requires investigation. And for the practical ruling it appears from what is written that whether circumcised at night or circumcised by day within eight he did not fulfill and must return and draw covenant blood from him — and so Bach wrote, except he wrote thus because one should adopt stringency; and according to what is written thus is the main view according to law.
