If he circumcised before the eighth day, etc. If the circumcision was performed before its time, or at night, it does not fulfill the mitzvah of circumcision at its proper time. There is proof from Menachot and from the words of the poskim that circumcision must be by day and on the eighth day when possible. Nevertheless, once the foreskin has been removed, one cannot circumcise again; therefore the child requires hatafat dam brit, drawing covenantal blood, because the first act was not a valid mitzvah act. This is especially so where it was done at night, for nighttime is not fit for milah.
