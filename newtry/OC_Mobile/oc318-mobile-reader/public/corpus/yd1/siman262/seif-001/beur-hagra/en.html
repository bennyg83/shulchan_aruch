One may not circumcise, etc. This is the Mishnah in chapter 2 of Megillah (20a).
During the day, etc. This is explicit in the Torah.
And once dawn has risen, etc. This is the Mishnah there.
If he transgressed, etc. From the statement, “and once dawn has risen, he has fulfilled his obligation,” it follows that before then he has not fulfilled it.
If they circumcised him, etc. This follows from what is stated in Shabbat 137a: they said that Rabbi Yose’s ruling in the first clause concerns such a case, etc., but not, etc. Beit Yosef wrote that these two rulings contradict one another. See Taz and Shach, and see below, siman 264, seif katan 10.
