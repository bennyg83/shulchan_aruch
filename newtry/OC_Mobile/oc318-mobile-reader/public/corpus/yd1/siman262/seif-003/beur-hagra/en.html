Androgyne and one who has, etc. Shabbat 138b — "I am not," etc. "this depends on that" among tannaim, etc.; and Rosh wrote that in Behag they were uncertain like whom is the halachah, therefore circumcise on the eighth and do not desecrate Shabbat for him; and Beit Yosef wrote the same for androgyne, and his words are puzzling, for androgyne according to all is circumcised on the eighth, for the "this depends on that" does not apply to him — what is meant by not desecrating Shabbat is because of a safek, but for the eighth certainly perhaps he is male; and it is possible according to Tosafot and Rosh at the beginning of chapter 8 of Yevamot, who hold he is certainly male, he wrote thus; and Tosafot on Shabbat there s.v. velo safek, etc.
