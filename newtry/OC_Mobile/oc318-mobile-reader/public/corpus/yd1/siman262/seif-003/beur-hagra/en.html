An androgynos and one who, etc. Shabbat 138b: “Is that so? But,” etc.; “this depends upon that,” according to the tannaitic dispute, etc. The Rosh wrote that Behag was uncertain whom the halachah follows; therefore such a child is circumcised on the eighth day, but Shabbat is not desecrated for him. Beit Yosef wrote that the same applies to an androgynos. His words are puzzling, because according to all opinions an androgynos is circumcised on the eighth day. The principle that “this depends upon that” does not apply to him, since Shabbat is not desecrated only because of the uncertainty, whereas on the eighth day he is certainly circumcised, lest he be male. It is possible that Beit Yosef wrote this according to Tosafot and the Rosh at the end of chapter 8 of Yevamot, who hold that an androgynos is certainly male. See Tosafot to Shabbat there, s.v. “and not a doubt,” etc.
