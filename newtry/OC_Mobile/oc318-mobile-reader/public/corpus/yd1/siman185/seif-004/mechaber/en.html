He was having relations with a tehorah, and she said to him, “I became tamei,” and he separated immediately — he is liable for kareis, for his exit is pleasurable to him like his entry. What should he do? He drives his toenails into the ground and waits without thrusting until the limb dies, and he separates with a dead limb. {Rama: And he should be filled with fear and trembling over the transgression that came into his hand (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag). And he should not support himself with her, only support himself with his feet and hands so that he not benefit from her. And if he separated from her while erect, and b'shogeg, not knowing that it is forbidden to separate from her, he should fast 40 days; and they need not be consecutive, only each week 2 days — for example, Monday and Thursday. And on the night of the fast it is forbidden [for him] with wine and meat. And if he cannot fast, he should redeem each day with money that he gives to tzedakah according to the value of the money that he has, for a rich person gives somewhat more than a poor person. And one should be stringent in his repentance, and whoever increases in repentance, it is merit for him (Piskei Mahari, siman 60). And the woman does not need atonement. And if he had relations not at the time of her veset, and afterward after tashmish she found blood — even if it was found on his cloth — it is called “ones,” even if she did not check initially, and they do not need atonement, neither he nor she (Mordechai and Rosh, klal 29, in the name of Maharam).}
