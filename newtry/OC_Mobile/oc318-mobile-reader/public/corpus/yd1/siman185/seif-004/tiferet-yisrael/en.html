Seif 4. Again Imrei Moshe wrote on Rama's responsum words need use in secret and need give fresh excuse that came upon her — otherwise uncertain warning — see there. I wondered matter still difficult how husband can be lashed — behold uncertain warning — perhaps afterward says thought niddah and now clarified from wound and matter clarified by examination she has wound that produces blood; and even if not claim and not clarify — nevertheless uncertain warning and no warning. And if gives such excuse and clarifies by examination — believed; and Gemara says if not giving excuse and clarifying — behold uncertain warning perhaps will clarify she has wound — this is even per view uncertain warning called warning — strained; can say certainly if gives such excuse and clarifies believed; Gemara says if not giving excuse and clarifying — behold uncertain warning perhaps clarifies she has wound; this is explained in Tosafot Yevamot 80 s.v. amar rav if matter revealed retroactively was warning at time of warning — certain even per view uncertain warning not called warning — certain warning (and although Rav challenged in Laws Sanhedrin on Tosafot contradicting himself Niddah 46 — say and I answered tel on correct and therefore true place). See Tosafot Zevachim 76 s.v. pigul venotar who also wrote if matter revealed retroactively was warning — see there; therefore Gemara properly says husband lashes if not clarifying she has wound; and in this manner Ramban also agrees — clear.
