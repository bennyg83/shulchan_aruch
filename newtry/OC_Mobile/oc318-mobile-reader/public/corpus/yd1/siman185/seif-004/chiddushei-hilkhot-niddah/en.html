Forbidden in meat and wine. Mahari Brandeis wrote reason like kodshim night follows day — strained; but reason for fasting on 2nd and 5th is that then is day of judgment below and court sits fixing Ezra and portions stand in assembly of God — therefore fast as known in all books; known judgment begins forbidden at night and conclusion permitted — therefore night before is not in judgment for they do not begin at night unlike when day 2 and 5 ends when judgment is deep and needs settlement and compromise — then they conclude all night for conclusion permitted at night — therefore entire night of 2 and 5 considered like day for judgment; therefore also they afflict themselves not eating meat etc. — see there.
