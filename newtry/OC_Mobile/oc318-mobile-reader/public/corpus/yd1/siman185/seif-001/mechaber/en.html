The laws of a woman who said, “I am tamei,” and afterward said, “I am tehorah”; it contains 4 se’ifim. A woman who is in a chazakah of tamei: it is forbidden for him to have relations with her until she says to him, “I immersed.”
{Rama: And since days passed in which it is possible for her to count and immerse (Beit Yosef), she is believed; even if he sees her garments soiled with blood, she is believed to say, “I passed through a market of butchers,” or “I handled a bird,” and similar (Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham).}
