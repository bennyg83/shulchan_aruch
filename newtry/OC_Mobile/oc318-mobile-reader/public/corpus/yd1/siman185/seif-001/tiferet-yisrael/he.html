Imrei Moshe Hagazal s.k. 1 explained on Mahari Brandeis's question that we properly rely on double doubt even where possible to clarify by inquiry since veset is rabbinic — see there; his words fitting for one who said them to answer Mahari Brandeis's question on Shulchan Aruch; but still question in my eyes per Rif and Rambam and supporters as Ran wrote in Shevuot who hold like R. Yehuda vesetot are Scriptural and Beit Yosef brought this siman — then difficult what R. Yehuda said Niddah 15 doubt saw and doubt immersed — need not inquire — meaning need clarify; and with this no place for Imrei Moshe Hagazal's explanation; indeed as stated this law not clear regarding veset needing clarify initially — no question here; but per Shach who proved thus in general double doubt from Gemara — difficult; seems thus: nevertheless must understand in Gemara per Rif and Rambam who hold like R. Yehuda vesetot Scriptural — what we say there "say R. Yehuda in doubt saw there is double doubt certainly saw — who said doubt does not remove certainty" — initially difficult for R. Yehuda who holds veset — if so even examined and found pure — tamei for presumption she saw and we stone on presumption — likewise as certainly saw — what difference between saw etc.; nevertheless must say as stated although presumption of seeing nevertheless minority do not see as truly R. Zeira said if examined at veset time and found pure — pure; therefore lean this minority to presumption — presumption of purity at immersion time returns to prior presumption of purity and exists for majority unlike certainly saw no majority only presumption of purity of immersion establishes presumption against presumption and remains doubt and stringency — this is correct; therefore since majority against presumption of prohibition and also left her in presumption of purity from start before veset time — certainly strong no need even inquiry of meaning since she has presumption and majority against presumption — requires study; clear.
