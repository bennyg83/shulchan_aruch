Imrei Moshe Hagazal s.k. 2: if she says excuse first thinking she was tamei and now revealed blood from wound — not Rashba's explanation that by great act not believed; and in such excuse Ramban agrees; but inferred from Ketubot she leaves without ketubah — still difficult why leaves without ketubah behold she has such excuse; see what they strained to answer; seemingly in such excuse Ramban agrees nevertheless Gemara properly says she leaves without ketubah for she wants remove money from husband holding doubtful claim he wore garments before clarified blood came from wound and now clarified from wound — say go inquire proof by examination as your words so it is — requires study. And if husband wants remove her — on him to bring proof; and so implies somewhat in Tosafot and Rosh — this is complete proof she wore garments for her niddah — clear. Nevertheless more comfortable to me in Imrei Moshe's words this is complete presumption before she did such great act she examined herself in holes and cracks to see if menstrual blood or from wound — otherwise why husband lashes on her in presumed niddah among neighbors — husband can claim she says from wound and Torah believed her even blood drips and descends — why worse wearing niddah garments than dripping descending blood; must say this is superior presumption one does not wear niddah garments before clearly clarified blood from source — then not believed even if afterward says from wound — therefore husband properly lashes on her — clear. Taz s.k. 2 brought in name of Maharal of Prague explanation on Mahari Brandeis's question above siman 1 regarding butcher believed giving excuse why here not believed — since neighbors did not know excuse unlike above — see there. Question still how husband lashes — entire reason perhaps bad name; and in Imrei Moshe's explanation of Maharal's words since bad name among neighbors wearing garments we presume niddah — can reject saying since this presumption she does not do except certainly niddah — properly lash on presumption though strained; but on Taz and Rav Beit Shmuel who do not hold so and challenged why leave without ketubah — can reject as stated; especially clear why husband lashes — requires study. Mahari Brandeis's question answered Imrei Moshe with straight reasoning: regarding men jumped on who are not proper — never considered forever transgressing — they say husband died or divorced — not so here considered husband and her as niddah relations; per his view this presumption before wearing these garments she examined well if blood from wound or niddah — otherwise here too could think initially thought menstrual blood and afterward revealed wound — same as there; reasonable reasoning for one who said it; with this can also answer Raavad's challenge on Rambam ch. 18 Laws of Forbidden Relations halachah 9 Rambam ruled if woman says tamei etc. although not believed nevertheless if husband died forbidden to kohen; Raavad challenged why excuse does not help like regarding jumped on — per Imrei Moshe's reasoning: granted regarding jumped on although world did not know excuse — world says husband died or divorced; but regarding woman since she belittles herself and says tamei — world says married woman in sin and did not know from excuse — therefore forbidden — requires study.
