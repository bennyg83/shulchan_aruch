(Seif 3 in Hagahah) Said so-and-so sage purified this stain for me and sage contradicts her — not believed. Implies if sage not before us at all and none to contradict her — believed; and so Shach siman 188 she believed to say this blood sage permitted me. Seems must say sage so-and-so permitted me — so we hold regarding firstborn siman 314 seif 7 and 8 — see Taz — not as Shach and Shach in Beit Yisrael and later authorities cited above; and per humble opinion from his words must say sage so-and-so — see there; all this regarding Scriptural stain such as blood on cloth as siman 183; but actual stain even when sage contradicts her — believed as rabbi in responsum and Sefer Keritut — not per Mahari Brandeis.
