Since he did not know, etc. Likewise in Darkei Moshe in the name of responsum of Rosh kelal 19 siman 13; and likewise recorded in Shulchan Aruch; and it is astonishing, for explicitly in the responsum of the Rosh there the opposite — even if he knows he sails the Jew; and: it appears to me the wine is permitted; and proof from the last chapter of Avodah Zarah: that Jew and gentile sitting in a boat and drinking wine; Israel heard the sound of a shofar of the synagogue — he got up and ascended; Rava said: the wine is permitted. I would say the gentile said: now he remembers his wine and comes; and if because of Shabbat — he told me "the prohibition of a convert: when I was among Arameans I said Jews do not observe Shabbat" — and all the more in this case it is permitted, for the gentile will say: now the Jew remembers he did not lock with a key and comes; and according to one opinion, if he did not inform the gentile he sails it is permitted, for the gentile will say: now he comes every hour and trembles; but even if he informed the gentile he sails it is permitted, for the gentile will say: when the Jew remembers he did not lock with a key he will return home and lock, and the gentile trembles, etc. — end; behold explicitly even if he informed he sails it is permitted. And in truth it is recognizable that before the Rav was the responsum of the Rosh old Venice edition printed in the year 512, where this language is written; and all the more in this case it is permitted, for the gentile will say: now the Jew remembers he did not lock with a key and comes; and according to one opinion if he did not inform the gentile he sails it is permitted… when the Jew remembers he did not lock with a key he will return home and lock and the gentile trembles, etc. — end; and these words appear misplaced; and the Rav emended specifically: if he did not inform the gentile he sails it is permitted; but the truth will teach his way that also in the old edition — meaning like the new Venice edition as I copied above; and further that the Rosh brings proof from that Jew and gentile who drank wine, etc., and there even when he informed he sails it is permitted because the gentile will say "now he will remember his wine and come" — as written in seif kaf-tet; and further that according to the reason the Rosh wrote that the gentile will say when the Jew remembers he did not lock with a key he will return home and lock and tremble — certainly even if he informed he sails it is permitted for this reason; and likewise from the words of the Mechaber. Requires study.
