And he informed him of the matter. See the responsa Panim Meirot, part 2, siman 76, which rules that if one sealed the barrels with two seals and afterward the seals were damaged, and the non-Jew came and informed the Jew, the wine is forbidden. This is not comparable to the case here, because the non-Jew is not afraid that the Jew may arrive, since the Jew had already sealed the barrels. See there.
