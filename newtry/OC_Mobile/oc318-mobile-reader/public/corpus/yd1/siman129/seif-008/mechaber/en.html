A Jew who rented a house in the courtyard of a non-Jew, and left wine in it, and closed the door and inserted the bolt into its ring, and forgot and did not lock it with a key, and he departed; and after some days the non-Jew realized that it was not locked with a key and informed a Jew — it is permitted. (Since the non-Jew did not know that the Jew had departed, he feared and did not touch) (a responsum of the Rosh, general rule 19, siman 13).
