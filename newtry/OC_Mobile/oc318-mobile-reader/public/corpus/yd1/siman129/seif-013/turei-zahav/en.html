If it is moist enough to wet another surface. This means that there is enough moisture in the winepress to wet his hand to such a degree that his hand can then wet another object. We are concerned that he may have touched it, since he thinks there is no prohibition in doing so. The winepress therefore requires rinsing and scouring, as will be explained in siman 135. If it is not moist enough to wet another surface, rinsing alone suffices. Certainly that moisture is also actual wine, but it lacks sufficient volume to join with what is already absorbed within the winepress, since it is not moist enough to wet another surface. So wrote Tosafos in the name of the Rashbam. The Rambam concludes after this ruling: This is an additional degree of distancing.
