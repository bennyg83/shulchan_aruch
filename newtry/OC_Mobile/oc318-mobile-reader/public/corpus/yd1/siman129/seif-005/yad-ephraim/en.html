And when they saw that it was wine, they did not touch it. See Baer Heitev and Tashbetz, volume 3, responsum 148. Thieves entered a storehouse and found two sealed barrels and one vat containing pressed raisins intended for wine production. They stole one barrel and opened the other, leaving it behind. If it is clear that no Jews were involved and the thieves were Muslims, the opened barrel should be sold to a non-Jew and the vessel requires kashering. The vat, however, is entirely permitted because no act was performed upon it. Even if non-Jews touched the raisins, the Rashbam permits grapes touched by non-Jews, and all the more so contact by Muslims.
