And when they saw that it was wine they did not touch. See Ba'er Heitev, and in Tashbetz volume 3 siman 148: thieves who entered a storehouse and found two sealed barrels and one pitam with pressed raisins to extract wine from them, and they stole one barrel and opened the second and left it; if it is clear that there was no Jew, only Ishmaelites, he should sell the open barrel to an idolater, and the vessel needs hechsher, and the pitam, since no act was done with it, there is no concern at all, for even if idolaters touched it one should permit it, as is written in the name of Rashbam that if idolaters touched a cluster etc., all the more so regarding the touch of Ishmaelites — see there.
