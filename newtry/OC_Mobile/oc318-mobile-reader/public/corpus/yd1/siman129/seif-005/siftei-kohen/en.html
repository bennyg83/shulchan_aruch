That he did not hand over to him, etc. And even if there are no Israelites in the city — because other gentiles fear this gentile and this gentile fears other gentiles lest they tell the Israelite that he entered. Rosh. And as Beit Yosef; and it appears to me from the poskim that even if he handed him the key simply, we say he handed over only guardianship of the key, and it is not necessary to say “hold this key until I come” — and he brings it. Darkei Moshe.
Even if he is seized as a thief. Both for the entry and for the touch — it is forbidden; and Bach explained "seized as a thief" refers only to opening the barrel, and I do not know why.
And specifically, etc. Meaning: that which we say if he passed and entered it is forbidden, even if he is not seized as a thief for the entry, and we do not distinguish whether he informed him he sails or not — that applies when they cannot see him; but if they can see him it is permitted without informing him he sails, for even though he is not seized as a thief for the entry, since he is seized as a thief for the touch he is afraid, thinking now the Israelite will come and see him, as above siman 128 seif 3.
