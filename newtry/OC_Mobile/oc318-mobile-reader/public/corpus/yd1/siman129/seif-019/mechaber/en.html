Wineskins of wine that were found thrown on the road: if most of the wine-pourers are Jews, they are permitted if they are large wineskins; but if they are small, which travelers commonly carry the like of them, they are forbidden, for we attribute them to the travelers — most of whom are non-Jews. And if there were large and small, all are permitted: the large ones prove regarding the small ones that they are from the wine-pourers, and the majority are Jews; and the small ones came to balance the load.
