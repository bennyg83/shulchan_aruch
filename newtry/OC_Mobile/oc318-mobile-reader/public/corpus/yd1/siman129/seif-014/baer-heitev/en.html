To libate. Taz wrote: it seems this is specifically for Talmudic law when they were very far from ordinary gentile wine and forbade it even for benefit; but now that we are not so far—for it is permitted for benefit, and moreover we have seen many transgressors in sexual immorality; and even those not suspected of prostitution are suspected of drinking ordinary gentile wine—and this is known every day, the prohibition being light in their eyes. Therefore those suspected of prostitution are certainly suspected of drinking ordinary gentile wine and do not beware of his prostitute's contact with his wine. Therefore it seems wine of those sinners in sexual immorality who are gentiles is certainly forbidden for us—and is worse than what they said here regarding an Israelite prostitute, where wine is forbidden because she draws after them.
