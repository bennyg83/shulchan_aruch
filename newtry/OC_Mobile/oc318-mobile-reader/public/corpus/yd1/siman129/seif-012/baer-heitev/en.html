Stopped. A wooden stopper is like an open barrel, because replacing it is normal conduct and not evidence of fear. Ran and Rivash. A sealed barrel, however, is permitted, because if the thieves had opened it they would not have resealed it, since they feared no one.
War. Whether the army belongs to this country or another country. Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
We are concerned. Taz questions why we do not assume that soldiers came only for money. He answers that the case is where people do not ordinarily hide money in wine barrels. Nowadays, when soldiers search for valuables even in barrels, one may be lenient even where sealed barrels were opened, exactly like the law of thieves in seif 11. This applies only during active pillaging, when they are occupied with money rather than drinking. If they remained afterward, opened sealed barrels are forbidden because they likely drank.
