And he informed him of the matter. See the responsa of my grandfather, Panim Me'irot II siman 76, who ruled that if one sealed the barrels with two seals and afterward it became spoiled, and the non-Jew came and informed the Jew, it is forbidden. It is not comparable to the case here, because the non-Jew is not afraid that the Jew will come, since he had already sealed it; see there.
