(Likut) Even if he remained away, etc. The Gemara there, the Yerushalmi, and the Tosefta, as stated there. (End of Likut.)
Provided that, etc., or that he distanced himself, etc. See the Be'er HaGolah. It further writes there that this is taught explicitly in the Tosefta: One who leaves his wine at an inn and enters the city, even if he remains away for a long time it is permitted. If he informed the non-Jew, or the inn was locked upon him, it is forbidden. One who leaves his wine on a ship and enters the city, even if he remains away for a long time it is permitted. If he informed the non-Jew, or his ship sailed far away, it is forbidden.
