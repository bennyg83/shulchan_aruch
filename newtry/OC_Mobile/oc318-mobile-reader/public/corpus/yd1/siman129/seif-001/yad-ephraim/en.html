Avodat HaTalmid and responsum Chayei Olam Yoreh Deah siman 173 on an incident where they all went to synagogue on Yom Kippur night and left an open wine cellar and the maidservant remained in the house; and the synagogue was far from the house about twenty amot; and he expanded on this and concluded to permit in benefit — for exchange we do not concern; and for touch for its sake we concern only for drinking need; and since it is grape juice within three days they did not drink; and combined with several poskim who are lenient — one may be lenient in benefit at least — see there.
