Where there is a roundabout route. See Baer Heitev and responsa Ba'ei Chayei, Yoreh De'ah, siman 173, concerning an incident in which everyone went to the synagogue on Yom Kippur night, leaving an open cellar of newly pressed wine while a maidservant remained in the house. The synagogue was approximately twenty amos from the house. He discusses the matter at length and concludes that the wine may be permitted for benefit. We are not concerned that she exchanged it, nor do we suspect gratuitous touching, but only touching for the purpose of drinking. Since it was newly pressed wine within its first three days, they would not drink it. Combined with the opinions of several lenient authorities, one may at least permit benefit from it. See there.
