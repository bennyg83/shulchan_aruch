A house in which a Jew has wine, and a non-Jew lives below, and the Jew is in the upper story, and there is a skylight from the upper story into the house: and both of them went out in panic to see a groom or a eulogy, and the non-Jew returned and closed the entrance, and afterwards the Jew came — even though the Jew has no passage except through the house and it is impossible to pass without the non-Jew seeing him — the open wine that is in the Jew’s house remains permitted, for the non-Jew closed only with the assumption that the Jew had already entered his home and no one remained outside, and he imagines that he preceded him: {Rama: And this is specifically when they went out in panic and returned, for it is possible to say that out of his panic he returned and did not check carefully regarding the Jew’s entry; but if it was not in panic, the wine is forbidden (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam).}
