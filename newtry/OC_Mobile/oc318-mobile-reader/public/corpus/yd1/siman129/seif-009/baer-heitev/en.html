And an opening. It must be possible for everyone to see through it. Otherwise both sides are forbidden. Shach. See siman 128:3.
In haste. Taz explains that when the Jew leaves hurriedly, the non-Jew assumes that he forgot the wine because of the emergency and fears that he may already have remembered and will return. If the non-Jew sees him in the market while he is returning home, the wine is certainly forbidden whenever the non-Jew locked the door from inside, even though the departure was hurried. If the Jew left calmly, the non-Jew assumes that the Jew trusts him and is not afraid.
