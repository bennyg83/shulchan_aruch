(There in Taz seif kaf-vav) It is astonishing, etc. Perishah and Bach already preceded him in this, and Perishah also concludes it is a scribal error.
(There in Taz seif kaf-vav) For it is stated above seif 12 in Cheil, etc. And it appears to me there is a variant: there, since it is in an inn one returns the cork; but here the reason is as stated in Ran chapter 1 of Avodah Zarah, namely: if he had touched it he would not have bothered to return and seal it, but would take from the wine and go, or bring it to his house — end of his words; and likewise Taz; and specifically when it was sealed, or even corked with wood — for if gentiles found it and opened it, why should they return and cork it, since they left it to be lost, etc.; and likewise Beit Chadash like Rama.
(There in Taz s.k. 27) It appears that this "needless to say" deals with where the vineyard, etc. — this requires study: in such a case it certainly came from this vineyard; and the Talmud only said that thieves do not steal from it and conceal it within it when there are also non-Jews' vineyards.
