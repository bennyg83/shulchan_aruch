The wine is permitted. The gentile fears to touch lest he say "now he will remember his wine and come" — Tur from the Gemara. And according to this explanation it implies that even if the Jew goes to drink and the gentile knows his intent to sail — nevertheless it is permitted because he thinks when he remembers his wine he will immediately repent and come; and likewise it appears explicitly from Rashba and Tur and Raah regarding whether we say "he will remember his wine" in matters impossible to do here — as in the small seif after this; and likewise from the words of the Acharonim. And Rashba wrote in Tosafot HaRosh that even open wine is permitted; and Beit Yosef brings it; and likewise in the responsum of the Rosh kelal 19 siman 13; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it and conclude unlike Ran in chapter HaSocher who wrote it deals here when the wine is corked with a cork — until here his language; and unlike the Derishah seif 28 according to the Tur.
