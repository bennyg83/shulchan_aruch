If a Jew and a non-Jew were on a ship that has wine, and the Jew heard the sound of the shofar blast that they blow to cease, and he entered the city and left the wine on the ship with the non-Jew: if there is a winding route by which he can come suddenly, and there is only 1 non-Jew there, the wine is permitted — even if there is between him and the wine more than 2,000 amot — for we are not, in their eyes, presumed to be proper keepers of Shabbat.
