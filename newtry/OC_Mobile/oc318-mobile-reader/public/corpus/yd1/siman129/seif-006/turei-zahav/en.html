If there is a circuitous route, etc. These are the words of the Rashba. Although we wrote above in seif 1 that in a house among other houses there is no need to distinguish whether there is a circuitous route, because every route is considered circuitous, there is no difficulty. Here, the non-Jew knows that the Jew went to the synagogue and not to one of the nearby houses. Therefore, if there is no circuitous route from the synagogue, he can guard against the Jew's return.
