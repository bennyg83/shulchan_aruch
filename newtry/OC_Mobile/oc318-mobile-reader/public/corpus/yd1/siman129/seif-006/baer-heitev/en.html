Permitted. Shach wrote in the name of Rashba that even open wine is permitted; and it implies that even if the Jew goes to stay and the gentile knows his intent to sail far, even so it is permitted because he thinks that when he remembers his wine he will immediately repent and come. Taz wrote: even though above seif 1, when informing that he sails, it is forbidden — one can say here it is different, for regarding hearing the sound of a shofar or prayer in the synagogue he does not go on his own but suddenly by chance, and therefore his wine is forgotten; the gentile sees this and trembles lest he remember the wine. But when informing that he sails, he certainly consoles himself before leaving and the gentile thinks he intentionally left the wine with him and trusts him — therefore he does not tremble — end of his words.
