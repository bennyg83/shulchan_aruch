Permitted. Shach cites Rashba that even open wine is permitted. Even if the Jew leaves for an extended time and the non-Jew knows this, the non-Jew fears that the Jew may suddenly remember his wine and return. Taz distinguishes this from a planned departure at the beginning of the siman: when the Jew suddenly hears the shofar or prayer and leaves unexpectedly, the non-Jew realizes that the wine was forgotten and fears his immediate return. When the Jew announced a planned departure, the non-Jew assumes that he deliberately entrusted him with the wine.
