The gentile fears to touch lest he say "now he will remember his wine and come" — Tur from the Gemara. And according to this explanation it implies that even if the Jew goes to drink and the gentile knows his intent to sail — nevertheless it is permitted because he thinks when he remembers his wine he will immediately repent and come; and likewise it appears explicitly from Rashba and Tur and Raah regarding whether we say "he will remember his wine" in matters impossible to do here — as in the small seif after this; and likewise from the words of the Acharonim.
וכתב הרשב"א בת"ה דאפי' היין פתוח שרי ומביאו בית יוסף וכן הוא בתשובת הרא"ש כלל י"ט סימן י"ג ומביא בית יוסף ודרכי משה ומסיים בה דלא כהר"ן פרק השוכר שכתב דמיירי כאן שהיין פקוק בפקק עד כאן לשונו ודלא כהדרישה סעיף כ"ח לדעת הטור: **** ENGLISH **** And Rashba wrote in Tosafot HaRosh that even open wine is permitted; and Beit Yosef brings it; and likewise in the responsum of the Rosh kelal 19 siman 13; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it and conclude unlike Ran in chapter HaSocher who wrote it deals here when the wine is corked with a cork — until here his language; and unlike the Derishah seif 28 according to the Tur.
