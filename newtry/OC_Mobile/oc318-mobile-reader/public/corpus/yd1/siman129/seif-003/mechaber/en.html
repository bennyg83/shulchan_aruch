That which it is permitted with barrels that are completely open, without a stopper — this is only regarding a ship or a shop, where he has no permission to touch the barrel itself; but regarding one who is transporting a barrel from place to place, it is not so: even if it is lacking it is forbidden, for we are concerned perhaps he touched, and he will not fear at all, since he can evade and say, “I intended to hold it so that it would not fall.”
