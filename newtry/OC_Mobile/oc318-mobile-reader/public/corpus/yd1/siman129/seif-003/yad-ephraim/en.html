If only one non-Jew is present, it is permitted. See Baer Heitev and Tashbetz, part 3. A person had kosher wine in the bottom of a ship, covered by more than seventy layers of cargo, so that reaching the kosher wine would have required unloading them. The Jewish guard wrote this information to the community and then departed. The ship continued directly from there to the city. There is no concern that the cargo was unloaded and reloaded, and the wine remains kosher. See there.
