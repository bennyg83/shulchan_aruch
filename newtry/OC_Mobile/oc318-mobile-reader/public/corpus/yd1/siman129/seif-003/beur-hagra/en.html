That which is permitted, etc. Rosh there seif 12 and Raavad, etc.; and he also said in the Yerushalmi, etc.; and likewise in Tosafot HaRosh 27.
(Extract) That which is permitted, etc. — Tosafot there in the passage "what he left, etc." — according to Rashi (end). (Extract) Without a cork. Meaning: with a cork, even in the case of one who passes through, it is permitted. Rosh and Ran in the name of Ramban, unlike Raavad who wrote specifically regarding fully sealed barrels — Ran there; and proof — Rosh and Ran wrote from the fact that we include all of them together in "bay" implies it deals with one category, and "passing with a cork" means open ones. But in passing through, etc. As stated in the Yerushalmi and Rosh and Ran brought it; and we say: one gentile carries a barrel and Israel follows him full — forbidden, etc.; and even though we permit there when lacking, here it is forbidden as stated, for we are concerned lest… Rosh and Ran there in the name of Raavad; and as stated in the Yerushalmi there, it is not the end of the matter regarding full ones… Rosh brought it there; and unlike what Ran wrote initially that lacking ones are permitted like the barrel mentioned — (end).
