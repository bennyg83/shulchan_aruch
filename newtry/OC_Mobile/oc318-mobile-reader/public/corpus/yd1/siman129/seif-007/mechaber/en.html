If a Jew and a non-Jew were sitting to drink, each of them from his own wine, and the Jew heard that they are calling to the beit knesset and went there to pray: the wine is permitted if there is a winding route by which he can come suddenly. But if they were drinking together from one wine, and there was wine before them on the table and other wine outside of theirs, and he left the non-Jew alone with what is on the table — it is forbidden. And of that which is outside of the table: that which is within his reach of hands is forbidden, and that which is beyond his reach of hands is permitted. And if he said to him, “Be the one who pours and drinks,” all the open barrels in the house are forbidden, and the sealed ones are permitted — unless he remained long enough to remove the cover entirely and return it and wipe it dry.
