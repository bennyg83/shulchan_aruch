And Rashba wrote in Tosafot HaRosh that even open wine is permitted; and Beit Yosef brings it; and likewise in the responsum of the Rosh kelal 19 siman 13; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it and conclude unlike Ran in chapter HaSocher who wrote it deals here when the wine is corked with a cork — until here his language; and unlike the Derishah seif 28 according to the Tur.
