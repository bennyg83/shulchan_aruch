The wine is permitted. For the reason mentioned; and since the Mechaber did not distinguish, it follows he goes according to his method that he agreed in Beit Yosef with the Tur who challenged Rashba, who wrote that in matters impossible to do here it is forbidden when informing he sails; and he holds that since the reason is "now he will remember his wine and come," there is no distinction in any matter where one can say so — even though impossible to do here he does not want to lose his wine on his account — end. And likewise Bach that the Tur's words are reasonable words (and in responsum Masat Binyamin siman 29 he distinguished that we are not concerned when informing he sails except when he informed explicitly; but here even though the gentile knows he sailed, as long as he did not tell him explicitly he did not rely, for he says "now the Jew will remember, etc." — end; and it is difficult: what of it — safek, he knows he sails; and likewise in responsum Mahari Beit Chad part 2 siman 200 page 93 top of 93: when they went from ship to another ship, need not inform he sails, for he sees he sails; and likewise proven from Raah below that one need not distinguish in this). And Derishah this siman expanded greatly why we do not permit above when he saw he went to the bathhouse and when informing he sails; and he brought the Tur's language and the mishnah's explanation; and he also went down to distinguish distinctions that are not accepted; and likewise Bach here entered with forced interpretations — see there; and it is simply hidden from them the words of Raah in Beit HaBechirah 145b who also challenged Rashba that even impossible to do here is permitted; namely: for prayer in the synagogue he goes, and immediately it is impossible for him here; but these matters are decided from their place, for we say: he heard the sound of prayer — he got up and left; he heard the sound of a shofar — he got up and ascended — this proves this Israel, because he heard these sounds, got up in agitation like a hurried man; and now one can hang and say: because of his agitation he does not remember his donkey — for if he remembered he would not go neither to prayer nor to sit, for he does not lose his donkey now lest he remember — he abandons all and comes to guard the wine; but whoever separates from there without agitation but with settled mind — forbidden, whether bathing, whether prayer — and these are reasonable words and clear matters — end; and they are fit for one who said them; and this appears clear in my humble opinion according to the Tur.
