Within, etc. In the Yerushalmi they sought to say: what is on the table is forbidden within the span of the hands; what is on the counter is permitted within the span of the hands — not so, said R. Ba in the name of Rav Sheshet: just as they established the span of the hands for purity matters, so they established the span of the hands for yayin nesekh; rather, what is said "on the table is forbidden" — even outside the span of the hands; what is said "on the counter is permitted" — only outside the span of the hands.
