Within, etc. The Yerushalmi records: They sought to say that what is on the table is forbidden within arm's reach, while what is on the side table is permitted even within arm's reach. But did Rabbi Ba not say in the name of Rav Sheshet that just as arm's reach was established for matters of purity, so too it was established for yayin nesech? Rather, what was said regarding the table is forbidden even beyond arm's reach, while what was said regarding the side table is permitted, provided it is beyond arm's reach.
Unless, etc. In accordance with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, as above in seif 1.
