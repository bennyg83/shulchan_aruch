And the beams are separate. It is astonishing why a distinction of beams was needed here, for in any case the roofs are separate from each other and the Gemara did not mention a distinction of beams; and it appears "and separate" is like "or separate"; and so it is explicitly in Rambam chapter 13: after mentioning distinction of the courtyard by the heads of the beams, namely: and likewise two roofs where Israel's roof was above and the gentile's below, or they were side by side and there is a partition between them, etc. — end; that also with roofs sometimes there is law of distinction of beams; and this is straightforward; and in all this the reason is the gentile was caught as a thief.
