(Likut) Even though the barrels are open. As stated regarding his pure foods, etc., and in the baraita: he spreads fruit there.
Within arm's reach, etc. There the Gemara raises an objection, etc.: Even though his hand, etc.; come and hear, etc.; come and hear, etc. (End of Likut.)
(Likut) Likewise, if, etc. Although Rabbi Yochanan said there, etc., that applies according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. According to the Sages, even in such a case the pure foods remain pure. So writes Torat HaBayit, and Rambam and the Tur rule accordingly. The Gemara further discusses adjacent roofs. Rambam records that case, but the Tur and Shulchan Aruch omit it because it follows all the more so.
Separated by stakes. Rambam requires this, analogous to a courtyard. Rashba and the Tur, however, do not write that the roofs must be separated by stakes. See the Lechem Mishneh. (End of Likut.)
