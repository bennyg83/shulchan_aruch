Specifically, etc. See the Taz. Torat HaBayit writes likewise at the end of Shaar 4: Some explain this as involving two seals, but this does not appear correct, for if so, what novelty is taught? Furthermore, two seals were required only for an entrusted deposit, where the non-Jew is afraid and therefore troubles himself to reseal it. With a found object, why would he trouble himself? Sealed vessels are permitted because this is like a marauding troop in peacetime, etc. The Ran and Nimukei Yosef in Bava Metzia write likewise. See there.
