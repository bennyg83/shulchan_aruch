By the majority. Even where it is near a city whose majority is Jewish, and even where proximity is compelling. Shach. See Choshen Mishpat 259:6.
With a stopper. Taz writes that Rama's permission where it was sealed with a wooden stopper is incorrect; only clay sealing permits. Nekudot HaKesef disagrees: replacing a wooden stopper is routine in a house, but here, if he had touched the wine, he would not bother resealing a barrel he left to be lost. Bach likewise writes this.
