If the barrel is corked. It implies that if it is not corked it is forbidden, for touch is not feared from passersby; and Bach wondered at the Rav here who was silent like the Mechaber; and above siman 128 seif 4 he wrote in Hagah that nowadays, since they are not idolaters, even open we do not concern lest he touch unless he drinks or has other benefit. Requires study: if so, everywhere the Mechaber wrote forbidden in benefit it should be difficult — for nowadays gentiles are not idolaters, it is permitted in benefit, as above siman 123; and likewise in siman 130 and 131 — in all these simanim it must be that nowadays it is permitted in many places since we do not concern lest he touched to libate where he has no benefit; and the Rav emended nothing there. And likewise in many places. But certainly since the Rav wrote in the main place of the ruling that nowadays it is permitted — it follows that everything the Mechaber wrote is only according to his view from the side of law; and is a merchant to calculate and go? This is straightforward. And above siman 131 seif kaf-vav.
