Likewise Rashba; and Tosafot chapter HaSocher (90a) also wrote in the name of Rashbam to be lenient if it is a public thoroughfare — even a gentile; and he wrote we do not concern lest perhaps he touched, since nowadays libation affection is not upon them; and they conclude on this — namely specifically with a narrow hole; but in a jug or cup with a wide opening it is easy to touch and customary to touch them in the manner of one occupied — end; and Beit Yosef brings them; and Darkei Moshe wrote there is a dispute between Rashba and Rashbam: according to Rashbam it need not be corked, for then even with a wide opening it is permitted; and in Derishah he wrote to distinguish that wherever there is libation affection upon it like according to Talmud law, Rashba requires it be corked even in a public thoroughfare even with a narrow hole; but Tosafot deal with nowadays when there is no prohibition because of libation but lest he exchange for his benefit — permitted when open as long as the hole is narrow; and so appears to me the essence, that also nowadays when there is no libation affection, nevertheless wherever touch is nearly certain it is forbidden even nowadays — as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba at the beginning of siman 128 regarding wine stored with a gentile; and therefore also here with a wide hole it is nearly certain he touched and forbidden even now; and therefore Rama ruled well in siman 128 seif 4 to distinguish between narrow hole and wide in our time.
