Shach wrote: it implies—but if it is not sealed it is forbidden, for one touching is not afraid of passersby; and according to Raavad nowadays that they are not idol-worshippers—even open we are not concerned he touched unless he drinks or has other benefit. What Raavad stated here is because he already wrote in the main place that the law nowadays is permitted—therefore it follows automatically that all Mechaber wrote is only according to his view from the law; and Taz forbids even nowadays. Taz wrote that the main point is as Rama wrote siman 128 seif 4—to distinguish narrow opening from wide nowadays.
