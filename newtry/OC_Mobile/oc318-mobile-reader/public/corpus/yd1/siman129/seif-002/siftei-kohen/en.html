Forbidden. This implies that likewise without informing him he sails, it is forbidden for this reason; and likewise in the responsum of Masat Binyamin siman 29; and he wrote: there is no comparison to one who rents a house in a gentile’s courtyard where the gentile lives, filled with wine, and an Israelite lives in that courtyard — which is permitted even though the key and seal are in his hand (as below at the end of siman 130) — for presumably his household members are many; because in a city the gentile does not rely on the watchman since he may come suddenly and they will not see him from a distance; but in a field he relies properly on the watchman — end; and it appears it is not specifically field and city, but wherever they can see him from a distance and he will not come suddenly — even in a city it is forbidden; for stores imply they are in a city, and even so wherever there are many it implies it is forbidden.
