Stopped with a plug. See Baer Heitev and responsa Beis Yehudah, siman 13. If one left a wine jug closed with a plug in the entrance hall of an inn, in a place where many people enter and leave, and went to the market to purchase bread, the jug has the same law as wine left in a winepress area, because it was placed where many people are commonly present. It is permitted, as stated in siman 129, even if the many people present are non-Jews. See there.
