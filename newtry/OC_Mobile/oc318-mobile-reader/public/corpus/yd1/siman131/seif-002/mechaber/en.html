Non-Jews who buy wine from a Jew and seal it with a seal within a seal, and transport it for several days on a ship by themselves, it is permitted. {Rama: If a Jew and a non-Jew purified the wine of 1 non-Jew in partnership, and the key is in the hand of the Jew: if the wine has 1 additional seal upon it, it is a seal within a seal, as was explained above in siman 130, and we are not concerned for the partnership of the non-Jew (a responsum of Rashba; and see Beit Yosef at the end of siman 130).}
