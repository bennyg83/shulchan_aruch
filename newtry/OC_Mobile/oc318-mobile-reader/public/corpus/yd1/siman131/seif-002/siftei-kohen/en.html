Permitted. They said only regarding one who purifies his gentile's wine that grew in his vineyard who did not trouble himself much and did not expend much, and is not so concerned if the Israelite senses he falsified; but when he transports wine far with great trouble and much expense, and also bought it considering his trouble and expense and did not falsify—Rosh and Tur. Bach wrote: although by law for Taz there is no distinction between one seal and two seals, nevertheless here it is permitted only with two seals as Tosafot imply; and from his words there it implies where the custom is to permit in such a case with one seal, that is the custom. And see responsum of Mahari Bei Rav part 2 siman 208.
