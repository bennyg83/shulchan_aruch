A beriah is not nullified even in a thousand. In it are 4 seifim: Beriah — meaning such as an ant or impure fowl, and gid hanasheh, and eiver min hachai, and an egg that has an embryo in it, and the like — even in a thousand is not nullified; and it does not have the status of beriah unless it is a thing that had life in it, to exclude one grain of forbidden wheat; and likewise it must be a thing forbidden from the beginning of its creation, to exclude pure fowl that became nevelah and an ox condemned to stoning; and likewise it must be a whole entity such that if divided its name no longer applies to it, to exclude milk; and likewise it must be whole:
{Rama: And the essence of gid hanasheh is only on the spoon alone, and it is the width of four fingers; and if it is whole it is called beriah (Arukh general rule 28).}
