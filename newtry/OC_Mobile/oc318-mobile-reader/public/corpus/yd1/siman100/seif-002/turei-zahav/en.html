The fat. Taz and Shach wrote: if the fat is attached to one piece, sixty is required in that piece against the fat; and if not, sixty against the entire piece — for us who say in siman 92 regarding all prohibitions chad k'neged chad.
Melted. Meaning: its importance is lost — nevertheless requires sixty; see above siman 98 regarding not its kind — nevertheless here permitted — end of Taz.
