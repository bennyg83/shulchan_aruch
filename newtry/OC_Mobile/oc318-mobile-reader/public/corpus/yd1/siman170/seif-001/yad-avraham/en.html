(Siman 170 seif 2 in Hagah) And if Israel guaranteed etc. For ribbit not principal permitted. Seems not every guarantee permitted unless accepts pay fixed amount entire loan duration; if accepts pay dinar monthly — also principal as Baal HaTerumot Beit Yosef siman 168 Bahat Halacha seif 13; likewise Taz siman 160 s.y. 3 brought there seif 13 and beginning siman 164; Beit Yosef and Shach this siman Bahat Halacha no distinction guarantor accepts fixed matter or dinar monthly — there explicitly guaranteed principal too heter because lender accepts not sue guarantor first therefore no distinction; where guaranteed ribbit alone permitted though sue guarantor first certainly permit only as I wrote; Bahat Halacha words do not contradict.
