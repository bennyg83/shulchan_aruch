One does not take out the dead close to Keriat Shema — whenever there is no time to take him out to burial before the time of Keriat Shema arrives; and if they began to take him out, they do not interrupt in order to read: {Rama: And one should wait from burying him until they can estimate that most of the congregation has already prayed; and there is no distinction between morning Keriat Shema and evening Keriat Shema; and some are lenient for evening since its time is the whole night (explained in Orach Chaim siman 72).}
