(Siman 358, Taz, seif katan 1) It appears to me that this is not proper, etc. It appears to me that the Yerushalmi discusses a case where no one carries at all. Nowadays, although there is an organized burial society, if one member is prevented by circumstances, the others are certainly diligent and prepared to perform the mitzvah. Furthermore, it appears that the Yerushalmi specifically mentions his sandal, just as regarding Shabbat it is forbidden to go out wearing such a sandal lest its strap break. A securely fastened shoe, however, may be worn outside on Shabbat, as explained in Orach Chaim, siman 301, seif 4. The same applies here, and it is permitted.
