In a place that has shoulders designated to carry the bier, they are forbidden to tie sandals lest one's sandal come undone and he be delayed from the mitzvah: {Rama: There are places where the custom is that mourners leave the house first, then relatives and the bier after them, and distant ones place the dead on the bier and take the mourners and relatives — the bier on their shoulders — and afterward the rest of the people (Kol Bo); some say when they arrive with the dead at the cemetery they stand him every four amot before burial (there and Beit Yosef); and so is practiced nowadays to stand him two or three times before they say Tzidduk HaDin; and on days they do not say Tzidduk HaDin they need not stand him (Maharil).}
