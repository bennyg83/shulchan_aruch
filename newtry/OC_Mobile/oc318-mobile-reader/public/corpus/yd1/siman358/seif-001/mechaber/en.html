Bearers of the bier are exempt from Keriat Shema and prayer. In it are 3 seifim. Bearers of the bier and their replacements and replacements of their replacements — whether those before the bier or those after it — once the bier needs them, they are exempt from Keriat Shema; and the rest who accompany the dead whom the bier does not need are obligated (and see in Orach Chaim siman 72).
