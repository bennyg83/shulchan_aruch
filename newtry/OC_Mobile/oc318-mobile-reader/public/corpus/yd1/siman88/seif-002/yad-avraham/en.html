and regarding one who vowed from some item: Mahari wrote forbidden to place on the table where he eats; and so Peri Megadim; and Chayei Shaul disagrees and permits — since he forbade that item he separates and refrains like pig and nevelah; the case is not comparable to the proof — nevelah everyone refrains; but one who vowed from the item and sees others eating it will come to eat from it — this reasoning is in Torat Yekutiel; and so Yoma (daf 14); and some proof to forbid from that we say to a nazir "go around, we tell the nazir: go around to the vineyard, do not approach"; and so Tosafot Avodah Zarah (daf 6) regarding nazir we are more concerned he will eat nevelah and treifah; however in Bach in Orach Chaim siman 222 he wrote per Tosafot that the stringency of nazir and a woman after childbirth applies specifically to unspecified vows; but for a vow on an item he is not forbidden unless he said explicitly he will eat it — see Gittin (daf 61).
