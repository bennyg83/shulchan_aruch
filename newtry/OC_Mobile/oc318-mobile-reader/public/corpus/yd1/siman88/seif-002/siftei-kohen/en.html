When two people know each other — who are not ashamed of each other and each will eat from his fellow's food.
Even if they are punctilious. So Tur and in Remazim and Beit Yosef in Orach Chaim siman 173 — this is the view of the Acharonim and not like Maharshal there who permits two people who are not brothers even if they know each other when they are punctilious.
And even those who know each other if they made some heker, etc. — Tur's language: and some say if they have one expense they have no permission through the heker they make between them — end; and it is proven from Tosafot and Rosh and those who follow them that "one expense" is not precise but means they know each other which is considered as having one expense and argues with the first explanation; however from Tur in Orach Chaim 173 and from Mechaber here it implies even if they have one expense the heker helps — and so poskim; and so Beit Yosef ruled there and so Tur HaChadash general rule 37 law 9 and Maharshal chapter 25 siman 43 and Acharonim.
A vessel from which they drink, etc. — and Bach ruled if they drink from it it is not a heker even though it is not customary on the table — and Beit Yosef and Rav are primary.
If they placed there a lamp, etc. — meaning when it is not customary for it to be there such as by day and the like; but if it is customary at night in that place it is not a heker.
Because food sticks to the vessel. Therefore even if they are at two tables and even two guests — forbidden — and so Orach Chaim general rule 1 law 14.
