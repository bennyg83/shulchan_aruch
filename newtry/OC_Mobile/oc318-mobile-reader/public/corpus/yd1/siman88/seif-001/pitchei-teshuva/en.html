On the table. Avodat HaTaharah of Maharit who wrote in name of Or Zarua to warn not to light with butter, for we decree lest it drip on the meat — end; and Beit Lechem Yehudah wrote if so with fat candles too; and Peri Megadim wrote: for me this does not apply to butter; with a candle called a torch one should be concerned that by shaking it will spill from the grease — but a fat candle made like a wick we are not concerned lest it drip — and this is correct — end; and in Orach Chaim siman 433 seif 2 it does not appear so — see there; and one may distinguish there because it must be inserted in holes and cracks we are concerned even for a candle made like a wick. And that they did not also write not to light with milk — one may say milk people refrain from and are careful not to keep it near food unlike butter.
