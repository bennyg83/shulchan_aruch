(siman 88 seif 1) Forbidden to place it on the table. Avodat HaTaharah s.k. 2 — and it appears specifically there because on bread a person lives — end; and there is difficulty from Pesachim daf 11 — R' Yehuda checks on the 14th at dawn and at the time of removal and the Gemara challenges there where he does not refrain who decreed, etc. — and we learned one may not pierce an eggshell, etc. — and R' Yehuda permits; and one may say perhaps he will come to eat — and per Shach's words the Gemara's "perhaps" — perhaps what was decreed regarding chametz is because chametz is different because on bread a person lives but not for other things; and one may say the distinction between bread and other things is at the time of a person's meal as regarding challah that Shach brings — all this; but when not at mealtime there is no distinction between bread and other things — if so there regarding chametz inspection which is not at eating time it resolves well from oil to chametz; otherwise there is room to resolve Shach's difficulty that Ran wrote the reason for this meat and this cheese is that each alone is permitted — if so one who sees that he ate will also come to eat, unlike a forbidden thing there is no decree; if so the same for challah even though he refrains, since the kohen sees him eat he will also come to eat; and that regarding one who vowed benefit it is permitted because presumably they hate each other and will not eat from each other — as Rosh wrote — if not it would be forbidden since his fellow also eats.
