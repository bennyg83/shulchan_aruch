Avodat HaTaharah; and see in Shulchan Aruch siman 173 in name of Bach that if they do not know each other — forbidden to eat on one cloth because meat sticks to cheese together on the cloth — end; and if separating with a flask — end in Atteret Zekenim that I am stringent; and Magen Avraham wrote what appears from Tur to permit is on a new cloth or laundered and this eats at one edge and that at another and they do not touch — simple — end Magen Avraham. And see responsum Beit Yaakov siman 12 that one who ate meat is forbidden to sit within six hours with one who eats cheese lest he hand him food and he eat; but if this one eating cheese knows that one ate meat — permitted — end; and in my view it is not clear — what proof he brought from Chullin daf 7 regarding a fellow's wife grinding, etc. — a person is accustomed to eat what is given to him (and what he hung on Tosafot's words is wrong — on the contrary Tosafot wrote on Rashi's forced distinction that it is not reasonable — more common he eats his own than what others give — end); and in truth there is no proof — there a grinding woman — women's way is not to eat and we fear she will forget and eat because she is busy at her work; and even though the answer said because of butter and she said the ox eats from its threshing — meaning permitted butter, for there is no theft; but also for the questioner their way is to eat while grinding and give to her friends as Tosafot from Tosefta; and in all places we are concerned only at eating time like here with two guests; and challah the stranger also eats; and a zav does not eat with a zavah, etc.; but sitting with one's fellow not for eating — it appears there is no concern; and even per his words — specifically one who ate meat has not yet eaten a meal, only ate a little meat casually; but if he already ate his meal and has no appetite to eat at all — no concern; and all the more if he is an honored person — shameful for his fellow to hand him food and he eat — obviously no concern. See there that sitting with a gentile who eats nevelah is permitted — end; and it appears one who ate meat is permitted to deal with dairy foods and we are not concerned lest he eat from them if it is not customary for the preparer to eat from them while fixing; and that Pesachim daf 11 — we say for the Sages he himself returns to burn it, etc. — implies otherwise R' Yehuda agrees that in something he is busy we decree lest he put to his mouth as Rashi there — nevertheless it appears specifically something whose habit is sometimes during work he desires to eat and eats from what he works on; but something not their way — we do not decree; see Magen Avraham siman 222 and what Dagul Mervavah wrote and what I wrote in Shaarei Teshuvah; and so responsum Teshuvah Me'ahavah in name of Kenaf Yonah that one should be lenient — the lenient ones who permit eating cheese after meat are worthy to be lenient — end; and so common talk that household members prepare the evening meal with dairy foods even though they ate meat at noon and no one objects; and nevertheless it appears for foods where the worker commonly eats during his work — there is concern because he is busy at his work he swallows and eats prohibition — all according to circumstance; and if two workers know each other that they ate meat — certainly do not be stringent, for they remind each other as we establish regarding reading by lamplight — end.
