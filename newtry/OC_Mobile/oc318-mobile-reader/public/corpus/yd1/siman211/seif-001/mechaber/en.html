One who stipulates that his vows shall not be vows; it contains four se’ifim. One who says, “A vow that I want to vow shall not be a vow,” and vows — it is not a vow. When is this said? When he first articulated with his lips to say that it shall not be a vow. But if he thought so in his heart, these are matters of the heart, and they do not cancel the vow that he articulated with his lips. And there is one who says that even if he said so in a whisper, these are matters of the heart. {Rama: And that which we say, “All my vows on the night of Yom Kippur” — it is as if they explicitly stipulated; nevertheless we do not rely on this to release without questioning a sage, except for a great need (Mahariv, siman 2).}
