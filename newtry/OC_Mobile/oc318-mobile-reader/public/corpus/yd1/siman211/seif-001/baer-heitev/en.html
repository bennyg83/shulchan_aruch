Shach wrote — it implies even if he remembers the condition at the time of the vow — in such a case the vow stands below seif 2; here it is void because Ran wrote since he stated the condition at the time of the vow; but below the condition and vow do not come together and one can say he uprooted his condition. Perishah wrote — apparently it does not depend on whether he conditions now at the time of the vow or before, but the essence is whether he conditions and explicitly says "tomorrow or after a year I shall vow not to eat meat and I annul it from now" — this is void, for he explicitly conditioned on this vow; unlike when he conditioned unspecified and says "if I vow" — end quote. Turei Zahav wrote he did not know Ran's source for Perishah's words — certainly in this the distinction applies: if he remembered the condition, etc., as written nearby — if he remembered the condition and vowed, behold he uprooted his condition; but here it deals where he conditions specifically at the time of the vow; likewise in Darchei Moshe — end quote (and in Nekudot HaKesef he agrees with Perishah's view — see there).
