It is not a vow. It implies even if he remembers the condition at the time of the vow — in such a case the vow stands below seif 2; here it is void because Ran wrote since he stated the condition at the time of the vow; and thus in Tur; as Beit Chadash wrote; and he ruled thus; and thus in Asheri and other poskim. But below the condition and vow do not come together and one can say he uprooted his condition. Perishah wrote — apparently it does not depend on whether he conditions now at the time of the vow or before, but the essence is whether he conditions and explicitly says "tomorrow or after a year I shall vow not to eat meat and I annul it from now" — this is void; unlike when he conditioned unspecified and says "if I vow" — end quote.
And some say even if he said thus in a whisper. Rosh in Ra'ah's name; and Beit Chadash wrote — however it appears we do not say speech in a whisper is words of the heart except where he does not make heard to his ears; but if he makes heard to his ears it properly counts as uttering with his lips to annul the vow; and thus we rule regarding Kriat Shema and prayer — end quote. It does not imply thus in Rosh — for then Rosh's difficulty returns to its place — he should teach "making heard in a whisper to his ears and his fellow did not hear" — see there. Also from Rosh's language — "but if he annulled in public it is words of the heart evident and his thought helps annul the vow," etc. — it implies specifically in public we require; and for this reason it is not comparable to Kriat Shema and prayer — here at the time of the vow we require words of the heart evident to annul the vow; and when he annulled in public beforehand it is words of the heart evident at the time of the vow; and thus Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 14 ch. 3: "He annuls secretly in his heart every vow he will vow in the future — not annulment and his vow is a vow unless he annulled in a loud voice in public" — likewise Ra'ah on that of Rabbi Eliezer who says even one who wants his fellow to eat — end quote; therefore specifically annulment in a loud voice we require.
