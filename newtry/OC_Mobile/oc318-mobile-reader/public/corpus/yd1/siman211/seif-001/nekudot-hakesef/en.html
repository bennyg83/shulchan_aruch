(Siman 211, Turei Zahav, subsection 1) I do not know the basis for these words, etc. Their basis is evident. It is not specifically when he makes the condition at the time of the vow. The same applies if he explicitly makes a condition regarding the particular vow that he will make tomorrow or at some later time. Since he specifies the vow, it is considered as though he made the condition at the time of the vow. Ran referred to making the condition at the time of the vow only when one states generally, "A vow that I may make in the future." Although this is not conclusive, the Derishah likewise wrote, "At first glance it appears," etc.
