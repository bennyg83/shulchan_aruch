(Siman 211, Taz, seif katan 1) I do not know the place for these words, etc. Their place is evident. The rule is not limited to a stipulation made at the time of the vow. The same applies when one explicitly stipulates concerning this particular vow that he will take tomorrow or later. Since he specifies the vow, it is as though he stipulated at the time of the vow. The Ran speaks of a stipulation at the time of the vow only where one says generally, “A vow that I will later take.” Although this is not conclusive, the Derishah also writes, “At first glance it appears,” etc.
