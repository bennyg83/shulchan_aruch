Beit Chadash wrote — this is specifically where he did not make heard to his ears; but if he makes heard to his ears it properly counts as uttering with his lips to annul the vow; and thus we rule regarding Kriat Shema and prayer. Shach wrote it does not imply thus in Rosh — only annulment in public we require; and one may distinguish for Kriat Shema and prayer; and thus in Rabbeinu Yerucham — annulment is not unless he annulled in a loud voice in public — end quote.
