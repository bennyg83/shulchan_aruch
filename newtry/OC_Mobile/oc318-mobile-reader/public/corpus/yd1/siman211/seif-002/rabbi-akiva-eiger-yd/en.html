(In Beit Chadash.) In a loud voice we require. It appears specifically here, since we judge from the condition in his heart at the time of the vow that he vowed with the condition's understanding — therefore it requires being revealed to all that it not be called words of the heart — through annulling the vow in a loud voice. But if he retracted after the vow within twenty-four he can annul with speech in a whisper without making heard to his ears, for whatever he brings out from his mouth is not called words of the heart; as Ran wrote explicitly in chapter 2 of Kiddushin in the sugya of words of the heart in Rashbam's name. Accordingly I have difficulty with Tosafot Gittin (35) — we are concerned he will annul for her in a whisper — what helps in forbidding her publicly, perhaps she will retract within twenty-four in a whisper; unless we say where the court adjures or forbids him, retraction within twenty-four does not help as prior annulment does not help, as in seif 4 — this detail is doubtful for practical law.
