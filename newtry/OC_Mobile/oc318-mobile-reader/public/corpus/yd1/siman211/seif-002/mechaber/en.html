One who stipulated and said, “All vows that I vow until such-and-such a time shall be null,” and he vowed within the time — if he remembers his stipulation at the time of the vow, his vow stands, for behold he nullifies his stipulation at the time he vows. And if he does not remember his stipulation at the time of the vow, the stipulation stands and the vow is null. And there are those who say that the stipulation does not help to nullify the vow unless he remembers it within the time of speech of the vow, and says in his heart that he relies on the stipulation. And one should be concerned for their words.
