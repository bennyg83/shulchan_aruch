To the vow. Taz disagrees that the remembrance must occur within the brief interval following the vow. In his view, whenever he later remembers the prior condition, he must immediately resolve to uphold it. Nekudot HaKesef disputes this. Maharam Galanti writes that Kol Nidrei also annuls a future nezirut vow, even though nezirut was not mentioned explicitly, because it is included among vows.
