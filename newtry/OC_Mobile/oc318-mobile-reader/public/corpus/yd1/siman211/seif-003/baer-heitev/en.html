To the vow. Turei Zahav disagrees — we do not care at all about within twenty-four of the vow, only that he remember the condition even after much time, he needs within twenty-four of that remembrance to set upon his heart to fulfill the condition, etc. — see there (and Nekudot HaKesef challenged him at length — see there). And here the gloss of the first version applies — "and all my vows," etc.; and it is written in teshuvah of Rabbi Moshe Galanti that likewise in nezirut the law is thus — "all my vows" annuls nezirut even though he did not say nezirut, for it is included in vows.
