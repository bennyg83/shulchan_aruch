These matters are not said, etc. We follow the intent of the forbidding party, as written in siman 210 seif 1; and proof from what is written (25a and in Shevuot) that they make one swear before the court and we are not concerned lest he cancel beforehand; and likewise coerced vows he cancels beforehand per Ran's view of whisper, and even per Rosh's view that here heart-matters help; and likewise chapter 4 of Gitin (34–35) we forbid a woman, etc., and we say (there 35b) "lashon ela," etc. — and if it were so, perhaps she would cancel.
