Ran there in Tosafot's name — according to his fellow's view he swore, as we say in the Gemara there 25a; like one who annuls his condition entirely; and likewise in teshuvah to Ramban siman 248 and siman 280; and Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 7 and Netiv 14; and likewise in Tur Orach Chaim siman 219; and likewise if he vowed and swore to idolatry except vows of ones, as below siman 232.
