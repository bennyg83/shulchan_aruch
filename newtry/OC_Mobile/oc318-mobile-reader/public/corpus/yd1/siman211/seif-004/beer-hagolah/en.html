The Ran there in the name of Tosafot. He swore according to the understanding of the other person, as stated in the Gemara there, 25a, and it is therefore equivalent to entirely nullifying his condition. Responsa attributed to Ramban, simanim 248 and 280, and Rabbeinu Yerucham, netivot 7 and 14, likewise write this. The Tur writes similarly in Orach Chaim, siman 619. The same applies if he vowed or swore by idolatry, apart from vows made under compulsion, as explained below, siman 232.
