One who writes a betrothal document to his maidservant — even if he said to her go out with it to freedom and be betrothed with it — this is not language of release; she is neither betrothed nor freed (there chapter 6 of Laws of Slaves): {Rama: (But if he said be betrothed with it alone, we say he certainly freed her from the start and betrothal took effect) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi, and so Gemara chapter HaSholeach).}
