One who buys a gentile slave from an Israelite or gentile, and a gentile who sold himself to an Israelite or sold his sons and daughters — behold he is like a Canaanite slave, except one bought from a gentile — his body is not acquired except for labor, unless acquired by law of the kingdom (so implied from Tosafot and individual opinion per Rambam and Even HaEzer); and in all, as long as they did not immerse for servitude, his law is like a gentile for everything; and after immersion for servitude he is a slave obligated in mitzvot a woman is obligated in; and with this slave it is permitted to work him with rigor — even though the law is so, from piety and ways of wisdom a person should be merciful, not make his yoke heavy, not distress him, feed and give him drink of all food and drink, not disgrace him by hand or word, not increase shouting and anger, but speak gently, hear his claims.
