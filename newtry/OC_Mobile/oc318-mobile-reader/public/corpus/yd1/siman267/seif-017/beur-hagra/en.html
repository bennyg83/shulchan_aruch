One who acquires, etc. As written in Yevamot 48a.
And likewise a non-Jew, etc. There 46a.
Or who sold, etc. As written in the Torah: "and from their families that they have begotten," etc. Rambam; and in Torat Kohanim: "from them you shall acquire" — their sons and daughters, etc. — as for themselves, from where? The verse teaches "that they have begotten," etc.
But, etc. Gemara there; meaning before he immersed — and if he immersed for the sake of freedom, as written above seif 9.
And in all of them, etc. Keritot 9a.
And after he immerses him. Meaning together with circumcision, as the law of a convert.
And he is obligated, etc. Beginning of Chagigah; and learn from it.
And through this transgression, etc. In Torat Kohanim: "you shall not rule over him with rigor," etc. — lest you say: since you have forbidden us all these things, with what shall we work? The verse teaches "and your male and female slaves from the nations," etc.
And even though the law, etc. As written in the Yerushalmi; and Rif and Rosh brought it in chapter HaChovel, 66; and Maggid Mishneh there: R. Yochanan would eat a caper and give two portions to his slave, and would call upon him the verse "did not He who made me in the womb make him as well," etc.
And he shall not shame him, etc. Niddah 47a.
And he shall not multiply, etc., but only, etc. The words of Rambam; and so it is expressed in the virtuous ways of Job that he praised himself with: "If I had despised the cause of my manservant or my maidservant when they contended with me — did not He who made me in the womb make him," etc. And as written in Bereishit Rabbah, beginning of Vayera: the wife of R. Yosi was quarreling with their maidservant; she shamed her before him. He said to her: Why do you shame me before my maidservant? She replied: but does not Job say "If I had despised the cause of my manservant."
