One who sells his slave to chutz la'aretz, or Syria, or Acre — he goes out to freedom and they compel the second master to write a bill of release; and he loses the money; and even if he said I do not wish to take him to chutz la'aretz, let him be enslaved in Eretz Yisrael — they do not listen to him: {Rama: (And this law applies even now) (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam halacha 9).}
