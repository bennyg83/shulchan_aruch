In six ways, etc. Gittin, folio 9a: The Sages taught, “In three ways, etc.” But there is also a document attached to the ground, etc.; and furthermore, etc.
And they may not sign, etc. Gittin, folio 10b. The Gemara did not count this separately because it is included in the statement that they are valid as witnesses, etc. Rambam, however, did not write that a Jew must sign last, but wrote generally that the Samaritan must be observant, as stated in seif 47; therefore it is not included.
