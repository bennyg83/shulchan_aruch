And they are not written attached. Tur wrote this law in Rambam's name; and Beit Yosef challenged behold Talmud in chapter 1 of Gittin (9) they challenge "but there is attached" — meaning women's bills and slave releases are equal, invalid attached; and it appears to me to resolve the reason there invalid attached is written regarding women's bills — namely in scripture "and he wrote and gave" it should not lack except writing and giving and not cutting; and regarding slave releases there is no explicit teaching in this, rather we derive gezeirah shavah "to her to her" from woman as Rashi explained there; and this gezeirah shavah is not a matter of words — for in chapter 1 of Kiddushin (23) Rashba holds this gezeirah shavah and R' Meir who holds through document by himself and not through others — learn this gezeirah shavah is not derived, for regarding woman it is a bill even through others; and so in chapter HaSholeach (Gittin 41) we say regarding gezeirah shavah to her to her we do not say since the equation contradicts it; and if so it must be that which is challenged in chapter 1 of Gittin "but there is attached" — it challenges according to tanna who learns to her to her; and one can say it is not halachah like him — therefore Tur wrote this law in Rambam's name as above.
