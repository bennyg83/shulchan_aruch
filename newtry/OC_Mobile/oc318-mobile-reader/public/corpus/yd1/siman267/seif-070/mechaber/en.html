A slave whose master married him to an Israelite woman (and all the more if he marries his maidservant) (Tur), or the master put tefillin on his head, or the master told him to read three verses from a Torah scroll before the congregation, and all similar matters one is obligated in only as a free person — he goes out to freedom and they compel the master to write a bill of release; but if he borrowed from his slave, or made him a guardian, or put tefillin before his master, or read three verses before his master in congregation (or married an Israelite woman before him) (Tur), and the master did not protest — he does not go out to freedom.
