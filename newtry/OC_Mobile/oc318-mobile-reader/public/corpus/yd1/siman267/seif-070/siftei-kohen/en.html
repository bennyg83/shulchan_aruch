Whom his master married off, etc. Had he not released him, the master would not have caused him to violate a prohibition through the marriage. See these laws in Even HaEzer 4:2 and my comments there.
And certainly if he marries his maidservant. This refers to a case where his relations were not promiscuous, as explained above. Regarding one who marries his maidservant, Rosh and other authorities hold that she does not even require a bill of release, because only a male slave requires one to prevent aspersion. Ramah holds that a maidservant also requires a bill of release from her master. See Tur and Beit Yosef, and Even HaEzer, simanim 9 and 156:2.
