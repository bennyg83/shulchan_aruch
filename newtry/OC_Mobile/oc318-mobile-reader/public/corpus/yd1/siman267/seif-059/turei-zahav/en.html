But if he wrote half, etc. Rashi and Rosh and Tur explained the reason — perhaps it is one portion he wrote to both and there is remainder — therefore it does not help even if he gave them to both as one. And Ran wrote it is not clear — certainly it implies they acquired the whole between them; and Ramban gave reason because himself is not included in half assets — one who gives half assets to another does not take in every vessel half, nor every field half, rather they appraise assets and he takes the weaker portion; therefore this one whose master wrote him half assets — himself is not included in that half; and Ran wrote on this I do not know whence the rabbi this law that one who gives half his assets to another — why should he not acquire half of every thing; possibly he holds just as his hand is on the lower and he takes the weaker, so too he can reject and say do not take half in every thing; and I have no need for all this — rather our reason half-half does not help because the slave is released only through the document given in his hand and we require no remainder at all; and even though his friend's document proves no remainder by the giver — behold he is released through his friend's document he did not acquire; and when we say he released half and gave half to another through gift he acquired — meaning he gave the gift first; or he writes in release he gave the half to another through gift, the document acquires for the other on behalf of both; and likewise where he wrote in each document "all my assets" — since no remainder they acquired and release each other — end quote. Behold according to Ran it does not help here if he wrote in each document he gives all his assets to so-and-so and so-and-so his slaves — for it is like half-half according to his reason that in each document by itself there should be no remainder; and if so what is written here initially that it helps in this — and it is Rambam's words; therefore he does not hold like Ran but like Ramban I mentioned; and so is proven further from what is written in the seif after this that if the sale or gift to another together with release — it helps this too, not like Ran I mentioned who requires the gift to another first; and it wonders on the rabbi who made agreement between Rambam and Ran — and it is clear he did not.
