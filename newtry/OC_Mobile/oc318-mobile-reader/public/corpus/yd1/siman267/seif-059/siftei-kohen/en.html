They do not acquire freedom. This is derived from the verse, “freedom was not given to her,” teaching that two maidservants or two slaves cannot be released with a single document.
And he gave one person the two documents together. That person acquires on their behalf, so that both slaves acquire simultaneously. If the two documents were not delivered together, the first slave acquires everything, including the second slave, and the second acquires nothing, because everything was already given to the first.
Even with two documents they acquire nothing. Since the slave is included among the property, the master has retained half of him; this is not a valid release. Since he is not released, he acquires none of the property.
