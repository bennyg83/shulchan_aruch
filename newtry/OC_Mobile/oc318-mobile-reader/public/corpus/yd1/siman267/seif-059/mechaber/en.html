One who frees two slaves in one document — they did not acquire themselves; therefore one who writes all his property to two slaves in one document — even themselves they did not acquire; and if he wrote two documents they acquire and free each other. When do we say this? When he wrote in each document all my property is given to so-and-so my slave: {Rama: (and gave both documents to one person together) (Tur).} But if he wrote half my property to so-and-so my slave and half to so-and-so my slave — even in two documents they acquired nothing.
