(seif 7) Requires immersion afterward. Nekudot HaKesef wrote it is only rabbinic; and so Ritva in chiddushim wrote — and further he wrote z"l if his master released him even though he did not immerse, his immersion does not prevent him regarding that if he betrothed an Israelite woman his betrothal is betrothal, etc.; however in Tosafot (47b) s.v. sham ger, etc. — it implies he holds it is biblical.
