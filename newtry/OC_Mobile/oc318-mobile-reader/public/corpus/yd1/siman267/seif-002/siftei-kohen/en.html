“And his mother did not go wrong.” This means that a child rejects a word that is circulating the Shabbat between a house of silver, and so on the column, but in the end of a path, he says, “It is written that there is no Shabbat on the word of the slaves of his mother, and then a child who is told that he is against the Shabbat but in Israel.”
They do not override Shabbat. Rather they circumcise on the first day after Shabbat even though it is the ninth day; and so in Perishah.
