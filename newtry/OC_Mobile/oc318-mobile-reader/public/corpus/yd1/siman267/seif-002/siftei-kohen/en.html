Whose mother did not immerse, etc. This implies that if she immersed before giving birth, the circumcision, which takes place on the eighth day, overrides Shabbos, whether the child was born in the household or acquired with money. The Tur writes likewise. Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, writes at the end of netiv 24 that Shabbos is not desecrated for the circumcision of slaves, even if his mother immersed and afterward gave birth, because the rule that one circumcised on the eighth day is circumcised on Shabbos was stated only concerning an Israelite. Darchei Moshe and the glosses to the Derishah cite this.
They do not override Shabbos. Rather, they are circumcised on the first day after Shabbos, even though that is the ninth day. The Perishah writes likewise.
