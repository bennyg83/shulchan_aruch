Shabbat. Rather they circumcise on the first day after Shabbat even though it is the ninth day; and it implies that immersion precedes birth — Shabbat is overridden for circumcision, whether one born in the house or acquired for money — so too the Tur; but Rabbenu Yerucham wrote we do not violate Shabbat for circumcision of slaves even if the mother immersed and afterward gave birth, for they said "whoever is circumcised on the eighth" applies to circumcise on Shabbat only for an Israelite — thus he wrote; Shach:
