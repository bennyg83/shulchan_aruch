Except for one born in the household, etc. Some rule like the first Tanna, because an individual and the majority, etc., and Rabbi Yirmiyah and Rav Mesharshiya discuss the matter according to his view. Others rule like Rabbi Chama, because Rav Asi holds like him. The Baal Halachos and the Razah wrote that it is uncertain according to whom the halachah follows, and this is also the view of the Rif; see Rashba and Ran. The Rosh and Tur likewise wrote this. It appears to me that even if the halachah follows the first Tanna, it nevertheless remains uncertain whether Shabbos may be desecrated, as stated there: “Is that so? Was it not stated, etc.?” Although this was rejected by saying that the matters depend upon one another, that is only according to Rav Asi. But if we rule unlike Rav Asi, the statement remains in its plain sense. It appears that this is the view of the Rif and Rambam.
