A slave taken from a gentile — they say to him: Do you wish to enter the category of Israelite slaves and be among the fit, or not? If he wishes, they inform him of fundamentals of faith and some light and severe mitzvot and their punishments and rewards, as they inform a convert; and they immerse him like a convert and inform him while he is in the water: {Rama: (And the slave blesses on immersion, not the master) (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter HaChalitzah, Maggid Mishneh chapter 13 of Forbidden Relations).}
