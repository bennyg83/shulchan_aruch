One who writes all his assets, etc. Ran wrote: that which he states "all" — this is the reason that even though a slave is called property, nevertheless when he gives to his slave it does not imply his bill of divorce and gift unless he specified and said "all my property."
Whether he had property, etc. Then plainly he does not go free — for we say since he left him a house or field he left also in the slave; and all property he says means remaining property; but even if he has only that house or field and then perforce it refers only to the slave — nevertheless he does not go free for it is not a valid bill of divorce.
And even if he wrote to him "yourself" except field so-and-so, etc. And Tosafot and Rosh hold in such a case he goes free; and see in Even HaEzer where both views are explained.
