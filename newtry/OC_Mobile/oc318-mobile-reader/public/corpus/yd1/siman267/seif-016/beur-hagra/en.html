A king, etc. As stated there, “and they subject them, etc.” The explanation is that the question is not because of interest, but because of servitude. The Raavad explains like Tosafos there, according to the texts that read “more,” and see what Rabbeinu wrote in his glosses there.
(Addendum) This is permitted, etc. This follows our version, “and they subject them more,” and his explanation that it concerns excessive subjugation. The Raavad, however, objected that it was stated only regarding interest, for the tax was ten dinars and they made them perform labor worth fifteen dinars; this is the meaning of “more,” and it is plainly a sale. Tosafos explain that afterward they would return the poll tax to them, according to the texts that do not read “more”; see there, s.v. “and they subject them, etc.” I cannot understand Rambam's explanation, for what then was the question? See chapter 5 of Hilchos Gezeilah and Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat, siman 369, seif 11, where it is explained regarding the law of the kingdom. End of addendum.
