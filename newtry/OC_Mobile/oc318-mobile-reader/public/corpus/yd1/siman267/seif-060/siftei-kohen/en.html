The slave did not acquire, etc. We derive the law from a woman: just as half of a woman cannot be divorced, half of a slave cannot be released.
But if he sold or gave to another. Meaning, the second half.
Whether it was, etc., he acquired, etc. At the moment of release the slave left the master's possession completely. If, however, the master first released half of him and only afterward sold the other half, the slave did not acquire freedom.
