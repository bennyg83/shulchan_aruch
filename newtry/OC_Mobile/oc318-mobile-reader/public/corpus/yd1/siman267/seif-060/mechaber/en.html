One who frees half his slave by document — the slave did not acquire his half and remains a slave as he was; but if he freed half with money the slave gave him half his value to free himself — he acquired and is half free. When do we say this? When he freed half by document and the other half remained; but if he sold or gave him to another, whether release and sale or gift together or sale or gift preceded release — the slave acquired his half and the buyer or recipient has the other half; and likewise a slave of two partners — if one freed his share, even by document, he acquired and is half free.
