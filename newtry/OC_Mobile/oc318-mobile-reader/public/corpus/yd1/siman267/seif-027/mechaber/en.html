How with principal limbs? A slave circumcised and immersed for servitude — if his master blinded his eye, knocked out his tooth, or cut off one of the tips of fingers of hands and feet, ear tips, nose tip, reproductive tip, or woman's breast tips — he goes out to freedom and needs a bill of release: {Rama: (And they compel the master to write him a bill of release) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham).} But if his tongue was cut — he does not go out to freedom.
