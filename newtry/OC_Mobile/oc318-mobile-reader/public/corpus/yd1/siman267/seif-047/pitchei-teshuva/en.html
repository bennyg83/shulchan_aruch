That he be a Samaritan associate. See Bnei Ahuvah chapter 9 of Hilchot Gerushin halakhah 29 who wrote what Rambam and Mechaber wrote — "that he be an associate" — does not mean we require it be clear to us he is an associate, for then it is difficult in other documents too like the Gemara's question; and further even two witnesses too since they are presumed valid — rather to exclude where they recognize him and know with certainty he is not an associate. And he wrote further what they did not mention that which is in the Gemara requiring the Israelite signed at the end specifically — that is because they hold Rav Pappa departed from the first establishment, etc. — see there [and in a great one's name I heard on this a wise matter — the word "associate" written in Rambam and Shulchan Aruch is an acronym: chasum b'rosh (signed at head). And everything works out].
