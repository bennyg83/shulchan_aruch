Any document with even one Samaritan witness is invalid, except women's gittin and slave release — valid with one Israelite and one Samaritan witness, provided the Samaritan is a companion; and in the present time when Samaritans are like gentiles for all their matters, we learn from them regarding Sadducees, for Sadducees now are like Samaritans then before they decreed they be like gentiles.
