One who commanded at death, so-and-so my maidservant — my heirs shall not enslave her — behold she remains a maidservant as she was, and heirs are forbidden to enslave her; and if he said make comfortable for her, they compel the heirs and they do not enslave her except in work she wishes: {Rama: (And heirs cannot sell her to another, whether he said my heirs shall not enslave her or said make comfortable for her) (Tur in the name of R' Meir of Rothenburg).} And if he commanded and said free her, they compel the heirs to free her.
