The heirs may not enslave her. The Rambam explains that if a dying person says, “Do not enslave her,” the heirs may not require any labor from her, though she is not fully free and her children remain slaves. If he says, “Provide her satisfaction,” she must still work but may choose the type of labor she prefers. The Rif follows this approach. Rashi and the Rosh interpret “provide her satisfaction” more broadly, even allowing compelled emancipation if that alone satisfies her. The practical distinction concerns whether she is exempt from all labor or only from burdensome labor. The Rambam's interpretation, that “do not enslave her” exempts her from labor entirely, appears primary.
