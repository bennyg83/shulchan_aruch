If he stipulated concerning it. This means that she is not divorced until she reaches a specified beit din. Without such a condition, an agent bringing a get that is already in her hand need not say, “It was written and signed before me.” Regarding a slave's own bill, his hand is normally the hand of his master, so the document has not left the master's possession. Nevertheless, when the bill itself grants freedom, his hand and his freedom take effect simultaneously.
