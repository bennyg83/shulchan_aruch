If he stipulated on her. Meaning she shall not be divorced until she reaches court so-and-so — so Tur; for if not this condition he need not say "before you it was written," etc., since the bill is in her hand as written in Even HaEzer siman 142. And what he wrote immediately "his own master" — because his hand is his master's hand — it turns out the bill never left the master's hand at all; and even though one accepts his own release from his master's hand — there we say his hand and his bill come as one.
