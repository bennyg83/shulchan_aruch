If he bought an adult slave from a gentile and the slave did not wish to be circumcised — they roll with him for all twelve months; beyond that it is forbidden to maintain him uncircumcised, but he returns and sells him to a gentile: {Rama: And specifically where he bought him in order to convert him to slavery; but if he stipulated explicitly not to circumcise him (Tur, Mordechai chapter HaChalitzah, Rambam chapter 1 of Milah, chapter 14 of Forbidden Relations, Raavad there); and likewise in these lands where it is forbidden to convert any gentile — it is as if he stipulated with him not to circumcise, and permitted to maintain him uncircumcised as long as he wishes (same in Mordechai there and Rabbeinu Yerucham); and so is the plain custom.}
