It is forbidden to retain him. Tosafot write that Rabbeinu Yitzchak appears to hold that this is prohibited by Torah law, unlike Rivan, who explains that it is prohibited only rabbinically. Bach.
And likewise in these countries, etc. Meaning, if one happens to acquire a slave, he may retain him uncircumcised. Hiring servants is certainly permitted, since the servant is not owned by him. This is also implied by Rashi and Rabbeinu Yerucham regarding a case where the servant fixed the term of his service, for he is then merely like a hired worker; see there. According to this, the Rama's statement, “and so is the widespread custom,” is difficult, because purchasing slaves is uncommon in these countries, and an uncommon matter cannot generate a custom. It must be that he followed Mordechai and Rabbeinu Yerucham, who wrote that this custom had spread in their times.
