The wording of Rambam there, following the first tanna in the baraita, Shabbat 135b. Since he was uncertain whether the halachah follows the first tanna or Rabbi Chama and Rav Asi, we rule stringently: circumcise him on the eighth day, but do not desecrate Shabbat for him. This follows the Rif, Razah, and Halachot Gedolot, as the Tur writes.
