The master can say to his slave: work with me and I will not sustain you; but my slaves are melog property (property like melog of the head — husband eats fruits and leaves principal, no dominion over this) — he is obligated to sustain them: {Rama: And some say this that he can say I will not sustain you applies when years are normal; but in famine year they do not have mercy on him; but he can say: go, your labor is for your sustenance, even if insufficient (Tur, Tosafot, Rosh, Rashba).}
