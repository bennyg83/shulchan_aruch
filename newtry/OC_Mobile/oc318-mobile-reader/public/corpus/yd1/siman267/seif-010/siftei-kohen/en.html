But if he acquired him, etc. Nimukei Yosef writes that if a non-Jew captured him in war, the captor acquires his body. Beit Yosef writes below, page 312, that this applies specifically in a war governed by the law of the kingdom. He nevertheless cites a responsum of Rashba that even when acquired under the law of the kingdom, the body is not acquired. Bach discusses this at length in seif 13 and concludes that the primary view is that even one captured in peacetime, and certainly in war, becomes bodily owned under the law of the kingdom. See there.
