Because of suspicion. The concern is improper seclusion. An Israelite slave is especially suspect because he is greatly subordinate to his mistress and may conceal wrongdoing. This does not prohibit an ordinary hired male servant. He is not owned by her, is presumed observant, and is not so subordinate that he will agree to seclusion. Everyone also knows that ordinary servants must observe the same laws of yichud as other business associates. The Gemara's concern particularly addressed a widow. Where the woman is married, her husband provides an additional safeguard.
