One who acquires a slave — it is forbidden to maintain him uncircumcised, and his immersion, blessing, circumcision, and all laws of male and female slaves. And it contains 85 seifim. It is a positive mitzvah for the master to circumcise his slaves; if the master transgressed and did not circumcise them, beit din is commanded to circumcise him — whether a slave born in an Israelite's house or one bought from a gentile; he is obligated to circumcise him, and they are circumcised only by day. A house-born slave is circumcised on the eighth day; an acquisition with money is circumcised on the day he was acquired — even if he bought him on the day he was born, he circumcises him that day: {Rama: (If one bought the maidservant and another bought the fetus, they circumcise one (Tur); all the more if one bought a maidservant and her child with her, they circumcise one) (Beit Yosef).} And there is acquisition with money circumcised on the eighth, and house-born circumcised on the day he was born: How so? He bought a maidservant and her fetus with her and she gave birth — behold he is circumcised on the eighth; and even though he bought the fetus by itself, behold the fetus is acquisition with money since he bought his mother before birth — circumcised on the eighth. He bought a maidservant for her pregnancy, or bought a maidservant on condition not to immerse her for servitude — even though born in his domain: {Rama: (and she conceived in his house) (Tur)} — circumcised on the day he was born, for this newborn is as if acquisition with money alone and as if acquired today, for his mother is not in the category of Israelite maidservants so the son be house-born; and if his mother immersed after she gave birth, behold he is circumcised on the eighth.
