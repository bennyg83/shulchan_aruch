A positive commandment, etc. Explicit in the Torah.
And they are not circumcised, etc. Even those who are circumcised on day one; and as written above at the beginning of siman 262.
One acquired, etc. Gemara there; and Rambam omitted it — he reads like the She'iltot's text; and as Rashi s.v. hig and Tosafot s.v. kegon, etc.
And if she immersed, etc. From what is written there — and the first tanna does not distinguish; and if it were so, he would distinguish in such a case; but the straightforward reading of the passage does not imply this; and also from what R' Chananel said: 'she gave birth,' etc. — and seemingly why does he not also distinguish regarding one purchased with money? Rather, certainly the ruling of the first tanna satisfies him as well; and see Tosafot s.v. 'for example,' etc.: 'and one can answer that it satisfies,' etc. And the reason of Rava is that he saw R' Chananel disagreed only regarding one born in the house — and the reason is that the first tanna distinguishes between [one purchased] on condition of immersion or not; and necessarily [the case is] where she had not immersed beforehand but afterward; and R' Chananel says: not so — for where she had not immersed beforehand, in both cases [the child] is circumcised on the first [day].
