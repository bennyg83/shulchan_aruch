It is a mitzvah upon beit din to circumcise him. Since the law does not add that if beit din failed, the slave must circumcise himself, it appears that no personal obligation rests upon him. Just as a woman is exempt from circumcising her son, a slave may likewise be exempt. Rabbi Akiva Eiger analyzes Tosafot and Rambam at length and concludes that Tosafot themselves may have been uncertain whether a slave is personally commanded in circumcision or only capable of performing it.
