If he seized it, it is not taken from him. The Gemara leaves unresolved the law of a slave who has already become entitled to freedom because of a lost limb but has not yet received a bill of emancipation, and who is then injured by another person. The question is whether the statutory fine is paid to the master or not paid at all. The Rambam accordingly rules that if the slave seized the payment, it is not removed from him. His statement elsewhere that the master pays the value of a second injury must therefore be understood as applying where the slave already seized it, or after a bill of emancipation was written. The distinction suggested by the Migdal Oz, between two injuries inflicted at once and injuries separated by time, has no basis in the Gemara and should not be followed. The ruling of the Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch is primary.
