Covenant. For were it not for covenant blood the laws of heaven and earth would not stand, as stated "if not my covenant day and night, the laws of heaven and earth I did not place." "Who cuts the covenant" — such is the blessing formula; and Perishah wrote in Maharshal's name this blessing requires a cup, and so Bach; and he wrote further since it is in place of bringing him into Abraham our father's covenant the slave's master blesses him; but Perishah wrote whoever blesses on the cup it may be — end Shach.
