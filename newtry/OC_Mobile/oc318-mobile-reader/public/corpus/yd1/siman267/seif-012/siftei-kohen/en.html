And afterward he blesses, etc. And Perishah wrote in Maharshal's name this blessing requires a cup as above siman 265 regarding milah; and so Bach; and he wrote further since it is in place of bringing him into Abraham our father's covenant the slave's master blesses it; but Perishah wrote since Tur wrote unstated "the one who blesses says," etc. — meaning whoever blesses on the cup it may be — see there.
And to drip from them covenant blood. For were it not for covenant blood the laws of heaven and earth would not stand, as stated "if not my covenant day and night, the laws of heaven and earth I did not place." "Who cuts the covenant" — such is the blessing formula; and Bach wrote since Rosh did not write the blessing's conclusion "who cuts the covenant" — it implies he did not have that text; and for practice it appears in dispute of rabbis one should not conclude "who cuts the covenant" — end quote; but Perishah wrote since it is obvious he did not need to write it; and with this is resolved what Bach wondered on Mechaber that he did not write below siman 268 seif 5 this conclusion — because it is obvious.
