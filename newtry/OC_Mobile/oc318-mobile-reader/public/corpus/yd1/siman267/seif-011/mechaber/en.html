One who has a maidservant not yet immersed for servitude — another may not immerse her without the master's permission: {Rama: For perhaps the master does not wish to immerse her for servitude, for he prefers she remain a gentile who can do his needs on Shabbat; he can also sell her to a gentile; and if another transgressed and immersed her, he must pay the master for damage (Rabbeinu Yerucham netiv 13).}
