And he appraises the slave in money. The slave must issue a debt document obligating himself, as written there in seif 62.
What is written, that the slave must consent to his emancipation, means as follows. In truth he is emancipated through the money, because money alone completes the emancipation without a document. Since the guardian emancipates him through this money, it is considered like a sale, and a guardian has authority to sell him. The minor's consent is required only rabbinically, because the father had trained the child to regard the slave as his property, and otherwise there would be public suspicion and gossip.
