And he transferred his share, etc. The Tur's wording is, “and he first transferred his share.” Rosh and Tosafot likewise write that if the first owner initially released his share, a later transfer is ineffective, because the slave's labor already belongs to himself.
Beit din cannot compel a minor. A minor is not subject to coercion.
And the guardian shall write for him, etc. Bach rules that the father must purchase the slave from his son and pay him from his own money; afterward the father writes the bill of release. See there.
