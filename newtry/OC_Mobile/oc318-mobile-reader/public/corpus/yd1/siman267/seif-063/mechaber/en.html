A slave of two partners — if one freed his share, they compel the second also to free his share; and if he knew his fellow wished to free his share and gave his share to his minor son and afterward his fellow freed — beit din cannot compel the minor to free; but they appoint a guardian, appraise the slave, give part of the value to the minor so he agrees to free, and the guardian frees him with money the minor received and writes a bill of release in his name — specifically when intending to uproot the Sages' enactment; but if he died leaving a minor and afterward the partner freed his share — they do not appoint a guardian to free for the minor, but he works for himself one day and the minor one day until he grows up.
