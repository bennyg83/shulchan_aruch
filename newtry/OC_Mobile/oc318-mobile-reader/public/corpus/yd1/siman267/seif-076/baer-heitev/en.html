Acquired. Taz holds that acquisition here works through lifting, which acquires everywhere; physical delivery hand-to-hand is unnecessary. See Choshen Mishpat 125.
Give. Although 'give' is not normally equivalent to 'acquire' for a gift, emancipation resembles payment of a debt because the master likely frees him in return for past benefit. Gifts, by contrast, are sometimes given gratuitously. Rosh.
