If he said to another, acquire this bill of release for my slave — the slave goes out to freedom even though it did not reach the slave's hand; but if he said give this document to my slave, he cannot retract and the slave does not go out until the document reaches his hand; therefore one who says give this document to my slave should not give it after death: {Rama: And some say if he gave the document to the agent's hand and said give this document to my slave, it is as if he said acquire for him (Tur in the name of Rabbenu Tam, Rosh, Rashba, Ran).}
