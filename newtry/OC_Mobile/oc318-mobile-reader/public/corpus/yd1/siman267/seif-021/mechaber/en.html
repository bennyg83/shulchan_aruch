One who cuts off his fellow's Canaanite slave's hand gives five things to the master; and the slave is sustained from charity even though the master takes Shabbat, for he can say work with me and I will not sustain you; healing — master gives for his healing; and if they assessed healing in five days and strong medicines healed in three, the master takes the advantage even if the slave suffered from strong medicines: {Rama: (And all the more if the master injured him — exempt) (Misharim 53; see seif 37).}
