He pays five categories. Damage, pain, medical expenses, loss of labor, and humiliation. Their laws are explained in Choshen Mishpat, siman 420, and which of them may be collected today is explained in Choshen Mishpat, siman 1. See there.
And certainly if he injured, etc. This is explicit in the Mishnah, Bava Kamma 87a. Rambam records it in chapter 4 of Hilchot Chovel, and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch in Choshen Mishpat 424:3. I do not know why Darchei Moshe cited only Rabbeinu Yerucham. The Mishnah and Rambam there also make clear that if one injures his own Hebrew slave, he is liable for all categories except loss of labor.
