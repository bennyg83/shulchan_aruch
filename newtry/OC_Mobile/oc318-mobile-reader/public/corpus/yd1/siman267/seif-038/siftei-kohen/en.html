Slaves of nichsei tzon barzel, etc. The verse requires “his slave,” one exclusively his. With nichsei melog the husband owns only the produce and the wife has no share in that produce, so the slave is not exclusively his. Slaves of nichsei tzon barzel, however, go free through an injury by the husband but not by the wife, because they are assigned exclusively to the husband and are not hers at all. Bach writes that the Gemara at the beginning of Kesubos, chapter HaIshah Shenaflu, further implies that even slaves of nichsei melog that fell to her before betrothal do not go free through her after marriage; before marriage they go free through her but not through him.
