An Israelite who had relations with a maidservant — even his own maidservant — the child is a slave; and some say if with his maidservant or he consecrated her, the child is free for all matters (Rav Neturai Gaon, Rosh, and see): {Rama: And some say that for other matters he is like free but not permitted to an Israelite woman until he receives a bill of release.}
