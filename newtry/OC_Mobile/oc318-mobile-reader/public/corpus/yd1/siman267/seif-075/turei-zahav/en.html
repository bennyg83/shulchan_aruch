We are concerned that he may have transferred it, etc. The slave is forbidden to a maidservant because of the possibility that he was freed. According to the Ramah he is also forbidden to a free Jewish woman because the matter remains doubtful, while the Rosh treats him as certainly free.
The admission of a litigant, etc. This refers to the slave. The master may retract and continue enslaving him, because the master's earlier statement rested on the mistaken belief that the slave had already received the document. An admission based on such an error is not binding upon the master. If both parties maintain their original statements, there is no dispute to adjudicate, and the slave's own admission prevents him from marrying a free woman. If both retract, he is treated as fully enslaved.
