We are concerned perhaps he acquired, etc. Meaning and forbidden with maidservant; and regarding freewoman according to Rama forbidden with her for he considers this a doubt — automatically we go stringently; but Rosh holds he is certainly a freeman.
Admission of litigant, etc. Tur explains it refers to the slave; and so Rashi explicitly the master can retract and enslave him; and Tosafot gave reason — regarding the master we do not say admission of litigant, etc., for he thinks the slave accepted and acquired but truly he did not accept; and it appears this is as we find in chapter HaKotev that "paid" is precise "will pay" is not precise; and Tur wrote in Rama's name in both they stand in their statement — we need not teach us for neither claimed from the other anything; rather if both retract from their admission we say he is a full slave because master's admission is not considered admission as explained in Tosafot's name; and Beit Yosef wrote according to Rama admission of litigant is like a hundred witnesses on master and slave; and through this he wrote Tur's words not precisely and gratuitously wrote thus.
