We are concerned that he may have transferred ownership to him through another. He is therefore a complete Israelite and obligated in mitzvos, but according to Ran he may not marry a free Jewish woman without a bill of release. Tosafot and Rosh explain that this concern is treated as certainty. Bach explains that because the master makes a definite claim and the slave does not know, the slave's doubt cannot displace the master's certainty; he is therefore permitted to marry a Jewish woman without a bill of release. He must, however, immerse for the sake of release before marriage, for by not saying, “He made me a free man,” he admits that he has not yet immersed.
