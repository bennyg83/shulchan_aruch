One who says I made so-and-so my slave a free man and he says he did not make me — we are concerned perhaps he transferred merit through another; but if he said I wrote a bill of release and gave it to him and he says he did not write or give — admission of a litigant is like a hundred witnesses and behold he is a slave: {Rama: [And if the master returned and said he did not give it] the slave cannot return and say I received it; and if the master stands by his word, they compel him to write a bill of release (Tur in the name of R' Meir of Rothenburg).}
