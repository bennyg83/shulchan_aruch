One who buys a slave from a gentile — the body is not acquired until he immerses him for servitude; therefore if he preceded and immersed for the sake of freedom, behold he is free: {Rama: And nevertheless he must pay his master the money he gave for him (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter HaChalitzah, Maggid Mishneh chapter 13, in the name of Ritva's teacher, Rabbeinu Yerucham, and see Even HaEzer); and if beit din or another immersed him for freedom, he must pay if he knew he was this one's slave (there in Beit Yosef); but if they did not immerse him but advised the slave to immerse for freedom — exempt (Teshuvat Rashba, not like Beit Yosef who wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham disagrees; see Bach).} Therefore one must impose some labor upon him while still in the water so he appear a slave, lest he precede and say he immerses for freedom; but a gentile who sells himself to an Israelite acquires his body immediately and cannot precede to immerse for freedom.
