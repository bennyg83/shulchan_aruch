The body is not acquired to him. For it is written "and also from the children of the residents who sojourn among you — from them you shall acquire" (Lev. 25:45), and our Sages expound: you may acquire from them, but they may not acquire from you, and they may not acquire from one another — meaning regarding the body; only the labor of his hands [is acquired]. Therefore, when the Jew subsequently repurchased him from the non-Jew, he only acquired the non-Jew's right — which is the labor of his hands.
