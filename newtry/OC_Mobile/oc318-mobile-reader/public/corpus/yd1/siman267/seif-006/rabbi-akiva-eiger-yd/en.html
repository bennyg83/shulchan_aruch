(Taz s.k. 10) That he was not precise in this. In Ketzot HaChoshen (siman 249) wrote it escaped Taz Rambam's words (chapter 3 of Zevachim) — "one who gives a gift to a woman on condition her husband have no authority, and to a slave on condition his master have no authority in him — the master acquires and the husband acquires; but one who gives a gift to a woman or slave and stipulated with them the giver in the body of the gift it shall be for such and such — the master did not acquire and the husband did not acquire" — end quote.
