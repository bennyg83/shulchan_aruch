He goes free, etc. Raavad disagrees in his glosses and holds that he does not go free; on the contrary, by annulling the slave's vow the master reveals that he still has authority over him. This also appears to be Bartenura's view at the beginning of the final chapter of Nazir. See above, siman 234, in Tur and Beit Yosef, concerning a slave's vows.
