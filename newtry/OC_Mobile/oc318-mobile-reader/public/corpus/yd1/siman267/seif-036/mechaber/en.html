In all these he does not go out to freedom until the master acts intentionally; therefore if intended to throw a stone at an animal and it fell on the slave and cut his finger or knocked out his tooth — he does not go out; and if he reached into his maidservant's womb and blinded a fetus in her womb — he does not go out, for he did not know he would intend that; but if his master was a doctor and said treat my eye and he blinded — he goes out, for he intended to destroy.
