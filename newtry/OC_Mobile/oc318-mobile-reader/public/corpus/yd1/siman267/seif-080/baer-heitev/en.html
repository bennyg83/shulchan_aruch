The Shach wrote that according to Rashi and the Tur, the same applies if he did not bring him into the non-Jew's domain, but borrowed on him on condition that when the time arrived the non-Jew would take the slave as a pledge, and the time passed and he did not give him the money. Even though the non-Jew did not take him as a pledge and he remains in the master's domain, we fine him and the slave goes free. End quote.
For his debt. If this was against the master's will, or a coercive official arose against him to kill him and he bribed him by giving the slave, the slave does not go free, for there is no reason to fine him when it was done under duress. Shach.
From his labor. Meaning that his labor remains for him. “Except for the mitzvot” means that they should not be nullified because of a mitzvah.
