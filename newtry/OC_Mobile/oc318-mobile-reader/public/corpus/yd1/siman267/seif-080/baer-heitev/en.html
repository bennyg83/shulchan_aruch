Experts. Rosh objects that this is not a Torah-imposed fine. Rabbinic fines may be collected in Babylonia even without ordained judges.
Delivered him. According to Rashi and Tur, even if the slave was not physically transferred to the non-Jew, but was pledged on condition that the non-Jew could take him after default, expiration of the term causes the penalty and emancipation. Shach.
For his debt. If the master was compelled, or bribed an aggressor threatening his life by giving the slave, the slave does not become free because no penalty applies under duress. Shach.
From his labor. Meaning that his labor remains with him. 'Except for mitzvot' means that his work arrangement may not cancel mitzvah obligations.
To a Sadducee. Contemporary Samaritans have the status of full non-Jews. Bach's suggestion that the penalty might not apply to them as it does not to a Jewish apostate is rejected. See also the law of contemporary Sadducees above. Shach.
