He is a free man. He does not return to the second master, because that person redeemed him for the sake of freedom. He does not return to the first master, because the first had already despaired of recovering him. This follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. The Sages require him to serve the first master lest every slave allow himself to be captured in order to escape, but they agree that a slave who fled at personal risk is different.
