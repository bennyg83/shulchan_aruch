Slave, etc. Like first Tanna; and as Rif wrote in last chapter of Bava Batra that not in all places halachah is like Rashbag in mishnah; and as written at end of HaMedir they are amoraim, etc.
One who redeems him, etc. Tosefta: a slave captured and they redeemed him for slavery he shall be enslaved; and his master gives his money for freeman he shall not be enslaved ; Rashbag says whether thus or thus he shall be enslaved and his master gives his money; and this Tosefta is before despair like Abaye; nevertheless we derive from it whoever is enslaved to his first master takes his money even for freeman like Rashbag; and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch wrote "and if," etc. ; because in Gemara Abaye said forever, etc., for freeman, etc., for second master no for he released him for freeman ; evidently without reason of before despair he gets nothing on the slave; and with this Tosafot's question there s.v. le'olam, etc. "likewise," etc. ; teaches he does not give money even for Rashbag ; for Rashbag does not explain except with reason perhaps they withhold, etc.; and he said "just as," etc.; and if because before despair he would give money ; likewise for us who are like Rashbag according to Abaye.
