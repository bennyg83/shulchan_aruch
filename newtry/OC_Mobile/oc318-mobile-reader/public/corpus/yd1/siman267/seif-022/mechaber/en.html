Whatever the slave acquires, his master acquires — whether found object or gift given him, whether from master or another — the other does not acquire except all goes to the master, body and produce; even if he said I give you on condition your master has no power — it is nothing unless he says I give you on condition you go out to freedom with it: {Rama: (Therefore he cannot receive a gift from his master, but from another for the master, and from master to another) (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 5 of Laws of Slaves, Semag siman 86 positive mitzvah).}
