For freedom. Shach cites a view that money given to a slave for food and clothing on condition that the master have no authority does not belong to the master, paralleling a gift to a married woman. Taz rejects the analogy because a slave has no independent hand in either principal or fruits; only a gift expressly conditioned for emancipation escapes the master.
And from his master. Shach emends the gloss: a slave may receive a gift from another for his master, or from one outsider for another outsider, but not from his master for another, because the slave's hand is like the master's and the recipient does not acquire.
