For freedom. Shach wrote in Perishah's name it appears likewise if others gave money to a slave on condition the master have no authority over it but that he eat and dress from it — the master also did not acquire; and as above siman 222 regarding one who gives money to his daughter on condition her husband have no authority over it; and Rambam ruled there if he said on condition your husband have no authority and whatever you wish do with them — it helps; likewise here regarding a slave it helps; but Taz wrote it is not comparable, for a woman acquired the body only the husband eats fruits, unlike a slave who has no hand at all neither in principal nor fruits; if so immediately when he says to the slave "behold I give you a gift" it is like saying to the master himself and he can no longer retract from the master — therefore these expressions do not help regarding a slave, only specifically when he says on condition you go out to freedom with them — end Shach.
