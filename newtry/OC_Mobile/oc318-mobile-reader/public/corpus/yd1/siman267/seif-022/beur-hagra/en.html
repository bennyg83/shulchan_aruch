Whether body, etc. Meaning even though in Gemara there they compared woman and fetus to each other ; no acquisition, etc.; nevertheless one can say in gift to another for a woman the husband has only fruits like all melog property; and as written in Yerushalmi chapter 6 of Ketubot 49 ; they said a woman's found object to her husband ; R' Yehuda so a woman not flee with gold from her husband and say "found object I found" ; let the matter reach you he gives her to another through gift ; a voice goes out for gift, etc.; and if the husband gives her a gift ; also he does not eat fruits as written in chapter 3 of Bava Batra 51b; but here in both whether body whether fruits belong to him as written in Gittin 42a as above ; he did not acquire at all; and Tosafot and Rosh there.
Even if he said to him, etc. only, etc. Like R' Meir according to Rav Sheshet ; sugya in Pesachim there and Sanhedrin 71a and Semag like Rav Sheshet according to Sages; nevertheless halachah is like R' Meir for Rav holds like him in Nedarim chapter 5; and Rosh there; and Tosafot in Kiddushin 23b s.v. veR' Eliezer, etc.; and Rif and Geonim ruled like Sages according to R' Eliezer; and their reason because sugya in most places is like Sages; and Gemara takes the simpler ; Rav Sheshet's; but halachah is like R' Eliezer as their method that his words are stated last is conclusion; and here there is no practical difference ; Avodah HaGolah.
