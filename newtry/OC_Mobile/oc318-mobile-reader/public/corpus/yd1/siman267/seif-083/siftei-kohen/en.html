And they compel the purchaser, etc. It appears to me that here the purchaser does not lose his money, because he acquired the slave outside Eretz Yisrael, and the seller is the one at fault for bringing him there and selling him. Beit Yosef and the authorities write that all the laws in this siman concerning one who sells his slave to a non-Jew or outside Eretz Yisrael apply to a slave who was circumcised and immersed for the sake of servitude.
