No fine. The statutory thirty-shekel fine applies when an ox kills a slave, while ransom applies when it kills a free person. One who is half slave and half free is not fully either category, and therefore neither payment applies.
But a maidservant. A woman who is half free is not compelled to complete her emancipation merely because of the ordinary concern of marriage. If people treat her promiscuously, however, her master is compelled to free her.
Half of the fine belongs to the master. He owns her slave half. The ransom corresponding to her free half would belong to her heirs, but she has no heirs in this circumstance.
