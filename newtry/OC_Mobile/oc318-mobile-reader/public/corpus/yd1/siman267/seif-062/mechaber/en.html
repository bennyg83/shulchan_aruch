One who is half slave half free cannot marry a maidservant or free woman; therefore they compel his master to free him and write a document on half his value; and before writing the bill of release, his labor is for himself; and if his ox killed a person he does not pay: {Rama: neither fine nor kofer.} When do we say this? For a male slave because he is commanded regarding procreation; but a maidservant remains as she was — works for herself one day and her master one day; and if a person struck her hand and it withered and will return — if struck on the day she works for her master, damage to master; if on her day, damage to herself; but if her hand was cut off or anything that will not return — they divide damage between her and master; and if she killed, he gives half the fine to master and is exempt from half kofer.
