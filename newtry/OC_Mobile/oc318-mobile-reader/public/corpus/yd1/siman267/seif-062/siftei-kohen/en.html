Neither the fine nor kofer. The fine is the thirty shekalim that the Torah obligates the owner of an ox to pay the slave's master. Kofer is what the owner must pay if the ox kills an Israelite, as it is written, “If kofer is imposed upon him.” The half-free slave receives neither. Kofer is not paid for his free half because he has no heirs to receive it, and the fine is not paid for his enslaved half because he is destined to be compelled to obtain release, so the verse, “he shall give thirty shekalim to his master,” does not apply to him.
But a maidservant. If she is half maidservant and half free woman, she remains as she was. If people treated her licentiously, her master must release her, as stated below, seif 79.
And if the ox killed her. The owner pays half the fine to her master, because she is not in the same position as a male half-slave, whom the master is compelled to release. He is exempt from half the kofer that would correspond to her free side, because she, like a slave, has no heirs.
