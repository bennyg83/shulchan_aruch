“And if he is his wife.” A. A.D. Day:
In D.C. The name of G. B. and P.D. is a lot:
And if a person struck, etc. There 42a:2 — gored him, etc.; and Tosafot s.v. yom, etc.
And exempt from half ransom. There 43a — it was asked, etc.; R' Acha said worthy, etc.; and this is what is written above "and no ransom"; and likewise half maidservant who is also in doubt of betrothal as written in Avodah HaEzer siman 44; however regarding a slave also he has no fine as above in Shulchan Aruch because of last mishnah.
