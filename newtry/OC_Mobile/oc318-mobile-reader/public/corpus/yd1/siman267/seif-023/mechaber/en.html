A Canaanite slave — whether acquired while still gentile or after his master immersed him — is acquired by money, document, or chazakah, whether from gentile or Israelite; chazakah is using him, such as untying his shoe, putting on his shoe, taking his vessels to bathhouse, dressing, undressing, washing, anointing, or scraping him: {Rama: And specifically these for bodily service; but other work such as sewing his garments — no (Tur and see Even HaEzer).}
