(seif 23) Whether he was acquired from a gentile. Shabbetai HaNatan Mahar Meir Weil z"l challenged me this is against Tosafot Bekhorot (13a) s.v. achizah, etc., who wrote acquisition from a gentile is not acquired through a document; and so challenged in Sefer HaMakneh (22b) s.v. tana af b'chalipin.
