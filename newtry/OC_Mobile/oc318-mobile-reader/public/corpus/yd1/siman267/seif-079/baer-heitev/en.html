A maidservant. The Shach wrote: from this language it implies that even an ordinary maidservant who is treated as ownerless must be freed. But from the Tur it implies that this applies specifically to one who is half maidservant and half free woman, who is fit neither for a slave nor for a free man, but not to an ordinary maidservant, who is fit for a slave. However, Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote like the Mechaber.
