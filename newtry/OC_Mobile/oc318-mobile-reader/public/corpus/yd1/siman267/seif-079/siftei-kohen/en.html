And likewise maidservant, etc. From this language it implies even an unstated maidservant whom they treat with abandonment custom he must release; but in Tur it implies specifically half maidservant half freewoman who is not fit neither for slave nor freeman; but not an unstated maidservant fit for a slave; and so it implies in Gemara chapter HaSholeach (Gittin 43b) — the case there R' Chana bar Ketina — R' Yitzchak: incident with woman eight who was half maidservant half freewoman and they compelled Rabbah and made her a freewoman — like whom? Like R' Yochanan ben Beroka who said on both of them he says "be fruitful and multiply"; Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak said they did not treat her with abandonment custom — end quote; and if it were that when they treat her with abandonment custom even unstated maidservant they compel — if so why did R' Chana bar Ketina take a woman half maidservant half freewoman; and why did he not take "they treated her with abandonment custom"; and the primary matter is missing from the book; rather certainly he teaches specifically because she was half maidservant half freewoman they compelled Rabbah; and what we rule like Ravina who said there (38) that even unstated maidservant one may release — meaning he can release her and they do not compel; and Rabbeinu Yerucham in Mesharim netiv 13 wrote like Rambam and Mechaber.
