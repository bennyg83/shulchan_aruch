One who frees his slave transgresses a positive commandment, for you shall serve them forever; and permitted to free for a mitzvah matter, even from their matters — such as if there were not ten in synagogue, behold he frees his slave and completes ten with him; and all similar; and a maidservant whom people treat with abandon — they compel her master to free her so she marry and the stumbling block be removed (language of Rambam chapter 9 of Laws of Slaves halacha 6).
