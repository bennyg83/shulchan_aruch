A slave acquires himself with money, principal limbs, or document. How with money? One gave the master money and said on condition your slave goes out with them to freedom — once the master accepted money or money's worth, the slave goes out free without the slave's intent; and if another gave the slave money on condition you go out with them to freedom — if the master wishes to accept the money, the slave goes free; if not, the slave did not acquire the money (Rambam chapter 5 of Laws of Slaves halacha 2): {Rama: (And some say he can also acquire himself by chalipin if he has an item for chalipin, such as given to go out with it to freedom) (Tur in the name of Rabbenu Tam, Raavad in hasagot, Rabbeinu Yerucham).}
