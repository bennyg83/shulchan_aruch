His master cannot remove him. The Tur's phrase may imply an obligation to redeem him whenever possible, which is difficult because the slave placed himself in the non-Jew's hands. Rather, it means that if the Jewish master can recover him by paying less than his market value, he may choose to do so. If that is impossible and the non-Jew insists upon full value, the master need not pay, for he can purchase another slave in the market.
