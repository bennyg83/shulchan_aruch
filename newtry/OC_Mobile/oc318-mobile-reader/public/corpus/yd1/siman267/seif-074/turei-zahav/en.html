They compel the heirs, etc. The Shulchan Aruch here retracts the suggestion in the Beit Yosef that when a dying man frees his slave, no bill of emancipation is required after his death. This is no different from one who declares his slave ownerless and dies. The heirs must therefore write the document.
By way of admission. The Tur compares this to the Rambam's ruling above. The Beit Yosef distinguished between them, but as explained, even the earlier ruling depends on an admission. The phrase used does not itself effect freedom; it reveals that freedom had already occurred.
