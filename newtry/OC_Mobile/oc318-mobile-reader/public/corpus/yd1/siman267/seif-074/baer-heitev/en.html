I have made. Bach rules stringently where defective formulas were written: the master cannot compel service, but he is not compelled to write a proper bill of emancipation, and the slave may not marry a free Jewish woman.
The heirs. Beit Yosef wrote that after the death of the dangerously ill master no bill is required, but Bach says he retracted. The ruling here in Shulchan Aruch is primary. Shach.
