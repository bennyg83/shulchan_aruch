If the master wrote only these inferior expressions in a document, he cannot compel the slave to work, but neither do we compel the master to write a valid bill of release. The slave may not marry a Jewish free woman. This is unlike the Shulchan Aruch's ruling that he goes free.
But a person on his deathbed, etc. Derishah notes an apparent contradiction in Beit Yosef concerning whether, when the person on his deathbed dies, the slave requires a bill of release and whether the heirs are compelled to write it. Bach likewise questions Beit Yosef, because Rambam states explicitly in chapter 9 of Hilchot Zechiyah that the heirs are compelled to release him. Beit Yosef himself cites Rambam in Bedek HaBayit and rules accordingly in Shulchan Aruch, retracting what he had written earlier. See below, seif 78, and Choshen Mishpat at the end of siman 256.
