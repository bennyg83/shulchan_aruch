If he wrote in a document I will make so-and-so my slave a free man and gave to his hand — he does not go out to freedom; but if he wrote I made so-and-so my slave a free man, or he is made a free man, or behold he is a free man, or he shall be a free man, and gave to his hand — he goes out to freedom; but if he said orally one of these formulas — he does not go out to freedom. And this is when healthy; but a deathly ill person who said one of these formulas — he goes out to freedom and needs a bill of release to permit him to an Israelite woman; and they compel heirs to write for him; and even when healthy, if they said as admission, he goes out to freedom and needs a bill of release and they compel the master to write for him.
