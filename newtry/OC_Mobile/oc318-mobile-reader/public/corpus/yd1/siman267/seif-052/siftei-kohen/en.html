Just as a woman, etc. Bach writes that Raavad, Rashba, and other Geonim disagree and hold that the signatures must be authenticated; if so, a bill of release likewise requires authentication. Nevertheless, regarding the slave's collection from purchasers of property that his master sold, Rambam would concede that authentication is required. Only regarding ritual status may one be lenient and dispense with authentication. See Rambam's reasoning in chapter 10 of Hilchot Gerushin and Choshen Mishpat at the end of siman 85.
