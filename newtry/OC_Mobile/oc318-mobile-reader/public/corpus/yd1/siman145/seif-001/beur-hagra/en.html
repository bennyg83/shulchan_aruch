Of many. Rambam and Tur — and it is very wondrous, for even of an individual is permitted for this reason that there is no human grasp on it, as we said 59:1; and it is not forbidden for an individual except that he struck them with his hand. And for the one who forbids in detachment — see there 47:1 "If it is forbidden to raise" for an individual; and more wondrous that he hangs "of many" on "there is no grasp" — that of many even detached are permitted, as we said 59:1; and so the Levush, not like Shach; and Shach's words are very wondrous, for there 47:1 it deals with an individual, as we said "Mare struck" etc.; and per my humble opinion I still did not distinguish between many and individual; and so Shach wrote that detached are forbidden — for to what is he bowing?
