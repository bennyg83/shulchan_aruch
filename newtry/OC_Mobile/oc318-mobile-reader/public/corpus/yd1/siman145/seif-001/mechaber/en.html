Which idols of gentiles are permitted in benefit. It contains 9 se'ifim.
Anything that has no human handiwork and was not made by a person — even if it was worshipped — is permitted in benefit. Therefore, one who bows to a mountain — it is not forbidden. And even mountain stones that fell away but are still in their place and were worshipped there are not forbidden. And likewise rivers and springs belonging to the public. And trees that were not planted for idols are not forbidden. Nevertheless, even though the body of the tree is not forbidden when one bows to it, all the branches, leaves, and fruits that it produces as long as it is being worshipped are forbidden in benefit.
