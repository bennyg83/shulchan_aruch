If he exchanged animals for idols. That were his and he exchanged them with gentiles for animals — Rashi; and see above siman 132 and 144.
Such as if he exchanged animals etc. Because of the first clause it mentioned animals; but so too according to this view all exchanged-exchanges of idolatry are permitted, as stated in the Gemara — derived from "for it is herem — it" and not the exchanges of exchanges.
