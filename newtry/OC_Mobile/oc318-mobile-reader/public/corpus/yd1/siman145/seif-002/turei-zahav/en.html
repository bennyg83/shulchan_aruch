Its plating is forbidden. For it is said "you shall not covet the silver and gold upon them."
The accessories of a mountain are like its plating. Because the accessories of an idol are derived in chapter R' Yishmael (Avodah Zarah 52) from the verse "you shall utterly destroy all the places where the nations worshipped their gods, on the high mountains" — and "all the places," if not required for itself since it says "their gods on the mountains" and not "the mountains are their gods," then apply it to vessels — thus even on mountains the vessels are forbidden. But offerings of the mountain are not forbidden, and Tosafot wrote there the reason: for in chapter Kol HaBasar the prohibition of offerings is derived from "and they ate the sacrifices of the dead," and that verse was written regarding Pe'or — and Pe'or was detached.
