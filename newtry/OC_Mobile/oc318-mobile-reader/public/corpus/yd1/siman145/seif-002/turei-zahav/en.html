Utensils of a mountain are like its plating. Because utensils of an idol we derive in chapter R. Yishmael (Avodah Zarah 52) from the verse "You shall destroy all the places where the nations served their gods — upon the mountains" — and these "places", if it is not the subject of the body itself, for behold the mountains themselves are not forbidden, as it is written "their gods upon the mountains" and not "the mountains their gods" — give it the subject of utensils; we learn that even on mountains utensils are forbidden; but an offering of a mountain is not forbidden.
