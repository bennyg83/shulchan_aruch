A stone that one quarried to worship it is forbidden in benefit. If it was already quarried and he painted and designated it to be worshipped — even if he only painted and designated on the body of the stone — he removes what was renewed and it is forbidden in benefit, and the rest of the stone is permitted. And for idol worship, annulment helps even if he did not remove it from there — only what he painted is permitted if he annulled it (Rabbenu Nissim).
