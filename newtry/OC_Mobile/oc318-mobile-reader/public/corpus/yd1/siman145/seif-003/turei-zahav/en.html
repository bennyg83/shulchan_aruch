The house itself was worshipped. For otherwise — only to place an idol in it — we rule utensils of an idol are not forbidden until they are worshipped. But if it itself was worshipped — meaning it is forbidden, for we rule siman 139 regarding a gentile it is forbidden immediately. And what the Tur wrote here "if it belongs to Israel" etc. — meaning in any case a gentile made it, only that he sold it afterwards to Israel; but if Israel made it, it is not forbidden until worshipped — so Beit Yosef. And I saw one who establishes it here regarding an apostate Israelite who is like a gentile in this; and nonsense opens its mouth — his law is like Israel in this, and for all its punishments in the Torah, as we said siman 139.
