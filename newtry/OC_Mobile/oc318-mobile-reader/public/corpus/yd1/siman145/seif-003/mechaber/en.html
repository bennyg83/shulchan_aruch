A house that one built from the outset so that the house itself would be worshipped — and likewise one who bows to a built house — is forbidden in benefit. If it was already built and one plastered it and designated it for idols until it was renewed — one removes what was renewed, and the renewal is forbidden in benefit because he made it for worship, and the rest of the house is permitted. If one brought idols into the house — as long as they are there the house is forbidden in benefit; if he removed them, the house is permitted. {Rama: And this is specifically when the house was not designated for that; but if it was designated for that it is not permitted unless he removed them from there through annulment — meaning, so as not to bring them there again (Tur in the name of Rashi). And for an Israelite, annulment does not help, as above siman 139.}
