Even one sign. Meaning even any slight act; and likewise half a sign, as stated in the Gemara, Rashi, and Tosafot in Chullin (40a); and so in Torat HaBayit 10b.
But Israel does not forbid. Even with a great act — meaning slaughtering both signs in it. Rashi, Ran, and Rosh; and so it appears the Turei Zahav's opinion.
