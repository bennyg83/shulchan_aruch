For his own animal. See Tosafot 46a, s.v. “An animal,” etc. This is also stated at the beginning of chapter 6 of Temurah, “both this and that,” etc. It must refer to his own animal, because another person's animal remains permitted even for sacred use, as stated in Avodah Zarah 54a, “It was taught: an animal that was worshipped,” etc. The passage concerns sacred use, as Rashi explains there from the challenge, “But we learned otherwise,” etc.
He did, etc., even, etc. This is the statement of Ulla and Rav Huna. Even Rav Nachman and Rabbi Yochanan disagree only regarding another person's property, as stated later, “and specifically,” etc. See above, siman 4, for what was written there.
