Animals are not forbidden — for even if one bowed to his animal it is not forbidden. If he did an act to it — such as that he slaughtered it for idols — even one mark it becomes forbidden; and even if it is not his. And specifically a non-Jew forbids a thing that is not his own; but an Israelite does not forbid his fellow's, for he surely does not intend except to distress him. And some say that even if he has a share in it he does not forbid. And if the Israelite is an apostate, or they warned him and he accepted the warning — then he does forbid. (And see above siman 4). (An offering to a house of idol worship and its accessories are forbidden) (Tosafot Nedarim 17).
