A tree that one planted from the outset to be worshipped is forbidden in benefit. If it was already planted and he cut it and carved it for idols — even if he grafted and implanted shoots in the body of the tree and produced branches — he cuts off the branches and they are forbidden in benefit, and the rest of the tree is permitted. A tree under which they place idols — as long as they are under it it is forbidden in benefit; if one removed them from under it, it is permitted, because the tree itself is not the worshipped object.
