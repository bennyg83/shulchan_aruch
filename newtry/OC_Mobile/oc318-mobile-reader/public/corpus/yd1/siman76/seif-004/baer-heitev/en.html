Spit. Because regarding spit we do not say as it absorbs so it releases.
Stopped dripping. Taz and Shach wrote implies only when both removed from fire together and stopped dripping; but still on fire though stopped — or removed while meat still drips — no concern.
B'dieved. Taz: Maharshal forbids spit even b'dieved; without great loss be concerned; if roasted on it — appears forbidden even b'dieved — only blood lenient; absorption sticks — not lenient; Shach: on spit Maharshal's view Or Zarua siman 17 like Rav.
