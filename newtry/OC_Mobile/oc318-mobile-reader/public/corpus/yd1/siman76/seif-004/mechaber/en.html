Some forbid cutting with a knife the roast near the fire that was not salted all the time it is not fully roasted because of blood absorbed in the knife; and some forbid the spit on which unsalted meat was roasted; and some ruled forbidden to leave the roast on the spit after it was removed from the fire after the meat stopped dripping, lest the warm meat return and absorb from it; and some permit all this and so is the custom to permit: (and we are stringent l'chatchila and permit b'dieved) (Mahari and Hagahat ShaDa and Or Zarua general rule 37).
