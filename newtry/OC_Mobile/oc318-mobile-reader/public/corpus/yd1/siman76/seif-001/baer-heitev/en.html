Fire. Shach wrote implies even rinsing not required l'chatchila because of blood in the eye since fire draws all; some say if roasted well all blood exits; else permitted — fire draws and spits; remainder blood of limbs not separated; unlike siman 66 coagulated blood forbidden — here not considered separated since not from life place to place.
Netilah. Shach: for us forbid until sixty as Rav siman 105 (and why Rav did not emend here — relied on custom forbid with sixty in salting and roasting); if clear blood in eye fell and roasted without rinsing — treifah — not as it absorbs so it releases; Taz: blood Mechaber means blood in the eye — excluding blood exuded by salting or roasting.
