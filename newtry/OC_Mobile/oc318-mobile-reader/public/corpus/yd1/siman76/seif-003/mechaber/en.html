If the veins [in fowl] were not pierced at the time of slaughter — forbidden to eat even roasted until he cuts limb by limb and roasts; if he wished to eat from it raw meat — forbidden until he cuts and salts; and if the flesh was pierced from blood vessels he may eat even without melicha, raw or roasted, even all at once; and some were stringent not to eat it all at once even roasted except after cutting the veins and majority of two signs.
