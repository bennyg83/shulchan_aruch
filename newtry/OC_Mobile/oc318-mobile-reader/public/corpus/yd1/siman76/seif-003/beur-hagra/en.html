If veins not pierced, etc. — Gemara 28b; and reason because it is blood that separated — as Rosh wrote there; and therefore "if he wished to eat," etc., also for this reason; and Tosafot 14a s.v. venasvin, etc.
(Lekut) Until he cuts and salts. — as explained since it is cut up, etc.; and specifically also through melicha as written 93a in veins (end).
(Lekut) And if pierced, etc. — for he holds piercing veins or limb by limb not required except because of blood in vessels; and last reasoning holds because of blood in flesh itself — blood that separated as above; and Tur language "and some," etc., even though pierced, etc.;
(Lekut) And if pierced, etc. — from chapter 1 (15b) one who slaughters for the ill, etc., raw; and on Shabbat cannot salt — forbidden because melacha; must be such a case. Tur HaChadash 2 (end):
(Lekut) And if pierced, etc. — Raavad challenged on what is written 93a; and asked why these vessel-threads differ from other blood threads; and wrote other threads differ; but Rashba and Shulchan Panim disagreed since mishnah counts specifically; and wrote piercing veins helps for all threads that pour through that path except these — and piercing veins is only because of threads; therefore if pierced, etc., flesh blood since not separated is permitted except vessel-thread blood enclosed and standing — they only said blood of limbs absorbed in flesh (end).
(Lekut) And some were stringent, etc. — so Tur HaChadash 8a in the name of R' Meshulam and Raavad that piercing veins alone does not suffice until he also slaughters majority of two signs — as written 29a; and if it taught kodshim because, etc., implies all blood requires majority of two signs; not compelled — kodshim fowl suffices with majority of one sign though for blood required; and proof from broken neck also not compelled; and Tur HaChadash challenged them — R' Yehudah only disagrees until, etc., and your question refers where he said in fowl, etc. (end).
And majority of two, etc. — as written 28a until he slaughters trachea and veins; and as written because trachea adjacent to veins — though thought slaughter of trachea alone suffices per conclusion because trachea, etc., requires both even in fowl; and because of blood mentioned trachea since adjacent, etc.; otherwise why did R' Yehudah mention trachea at all.
