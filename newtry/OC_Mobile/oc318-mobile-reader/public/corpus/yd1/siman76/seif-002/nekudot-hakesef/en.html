(Shach s.k. 9) And what is written "or salted without rinsing first and roasted thus permitted" — not to say forbidden l'chatchila even for roast for then no remedy and it would be b'dieved; rather one may say there is remedy — rinse meat and then roast or salt it; and even though above siman 69 seif 2 Mechaber wrote "and some forbid" and Rama wrote so is custom — specifically for pot not for roast as Shach there s.k. 13; and see Taz there s.k. 7; and one may say.
(Taz s.k. 5) In Tur HaChadash did not rule thus, etc. — followed Bach's words; but in my books I wrote Rama in hagahah if did not remain time of salting and roasted thus permitted — also Tur HaChadash general rule 9 law 3 in name of Or Zarua; and wrote on it I already wrote above general rule 5 my view appears to forbid even in such case there is no measure — end; and in general rule 5 there implies he holds thus only set aside his view because of Or Zarua; and thereby Bach's wonder on Rav here removed — also need not say Rav retracted in hagahot from Tur HaChadash as Bach wrote — see there — end.
(s.k. 7) Wondrous, etc. — see Shach s.k. 11 — settled.
