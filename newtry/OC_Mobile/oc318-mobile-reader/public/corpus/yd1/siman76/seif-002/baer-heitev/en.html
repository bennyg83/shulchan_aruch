Rinsing. Explanation: rinsing after salting (before roasting).
Roasting. Shach: if did not rinse before roasting remedied by rinsing after.
Rinsing. Shach: though other blood in eye we do not say fire draws — also considered blood in the eye siman 69 seif 2; may say since not actual blood in the eye fire draws; actually l'chatchila permitted roast without rinsing first — if forbid for roast no remedy — b'dieved; because hagahah custom rinse and salt on spit — wrote b'dieved here.
Remained. Reason then not much absorbed and exits by roasting.
Forbidden. Taz: Mechaber himself did not rule thus in Tur HaChadash — even without remaining forbidden like cooking; so to rule; Shach siman 69 s.k. 13 disagrees for roast if not remained — see there; Rashal wrote women rinse after partial roast before finishing — not prohibition but taste; seems after rinsing do not roast again on same spit — spit treifah and flavors meat — roast without spit as I saw practiced.
Hollow birds. Even though blood separated wall to wall — as it absorbs so it releases.
Meat. Taz challenged — siman 77 permits meat all manners for roast; must say eggs alone or meat with eggs; Shach: on sides — in eggs even b'dieved forbidden; meat l'chatchila forbidden (Rav Meshebarshin heard gloss Rama: no distinction geese hollow and other meat provided not filled with eggs).
Salting. Or salted without time of salting — b'dieved no concern even if turned spit constantly.
Fit to eat. Shach: reason then all blood exited — permitted even l'chatchila roast half to cook afterward; if eat roasted still need roast half; see end siman 69 what Taz wrote how to practice l'chatchila.
After roasting. Shach in name of Tur HaChadash: if roasted without salting must rinse after roasting again; implies same if salted not remained or remained not rinsed after salting; Maharshal always only one rinsing after — Shach wonders how to dispute ancients without proof especially to be lenient (Peri Chadash likewise).
