If he wished, etc. — from what is written chapter 1 (8b) one who slaughters with a knife, etc., rinses; and Ran challenged: derive he needs rinsing anyway as written chapter 25 (113a) "rinse and salt," etc.; and answered there for pot and here for roast; and Tosafot 11a s.v. venichalin, etc.; and though custom to salt roast as written chapter 1 19 (11a) "and they salt meat on it for roasting" — Tosafot 14a s.v. venasvin and Psachim 74a s.v. hai, etc.
And some say roast requires, etc. — Ran in the name of Ramban; and wrote in the name of R' Yitzchak that no question — there suffices rubbing until water comes out without appearance of blood, unlike there requiring great rubbing to remove adhering fat; and so that chapter 2 13a — explain as second version of Tosafot there.
And some say, etc. — Rashi beginning chapter How One Roasts; and in books Menachot 21; and so for roasting; and chapter 1 Beitzah "and they salt," etc.; and this is Rashi's meaning "a little salting" — not like for pot as chapter 1 Beitzah there — only for roast, etc.
But if it remained, etc. — salt absorbs blood in the eye on the meat in it; and regarding blood in the eye we do not say as it absorbs so it releases — as above seif 1; and see above siman 69 seif 2.
And no distinction, etc. — meaning even though permission of roast is because remaining blood is blood of limbs that did not separate as Tosafot wrote — and here blood separated from wall to wall — nevertheless permitted because as it absorbs so it releases; also two pieces permitted to roast together — from what is written 111a "liver on meat," etc.
