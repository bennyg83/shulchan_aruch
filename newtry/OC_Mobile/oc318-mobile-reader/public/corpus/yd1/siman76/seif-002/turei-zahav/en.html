Without rinsing. Explanation: without rinsing after salting before roasting.
But if remained in salt. Explanation: even a little — not full time of salting.
Salt absorbs and forbidden. Meaning the salt; therefore rinse before roasting l'chatchila; if not — rinse after roasting; all deals when rinsed before salting — did not need mention that rinsing since mentioned salting; but without salting no rinsing needed first — on this "some say" apply; printing error in some Shulchan Aruch "does not require salting" — if did not rinse and salted will be explained below.
Without rinsing time of salting. Tur HaChadash did not rule thus himself — no measure; Rashal chapter Shechitah siman 18 — even without remaining forbidden like cooking; so to rule; Rashal wrote what women practice rinse after partially roasted before finishing — not because prohibition but lest roast taste spoil after rinsing.
Between geese, etc. — we do not say considered as separated piece to piece but truly as one piece; moreover even two pieces we are not concerned what separated between them at roast near fire — so Rashal chapter Chullin siman 27; to me appears from Mordechai chapter 25 hollow item not one piece nevertheless permitted in salting and roast as it absorbs so it releases.
Eggs or other meat. Wondrous — in siman 77 permits meat in all manners for roast; must say here eggs alone or meat with eggs; more likely "requires salting first" means full salting l'chatchila — not the little salting mentioned below.
