Meat — and it is permitted to cook it with other meat (Tur HaChadash and Shach). And Taz wrote in the name of Hagahot ShaDa: there was an incident with a spleen roasted without scraping and R' Meshulam permitted, for spleen fat is nullified in the spleen itself and in the permitted fat in it; and Taz concluded nevertheless one should not rely to be lenient from this on ordinary spleen unless it is clear there is sixty in what is permitted against it.
