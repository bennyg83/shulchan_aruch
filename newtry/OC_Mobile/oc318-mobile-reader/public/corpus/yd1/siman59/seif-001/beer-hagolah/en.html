Mishnah Chullin daf 54, and like the Sages.
As Rashi explained there, and so Rambam in chapter 9 of Hilchot Shechitah.
Baraitta there.
Dispute of the amoraim in its explanation, and Rambam ruled in chapter 9 like all the amoraim stringently.
Rambam there, and Rashba wrote: I do not know the reason for the permission, for it is a Torah safek; and so Rosh and Beit Yosef explained his reason: because once skin was removed from one of these three places, it reverts to its presumption — it is a double safek: safek whether the halacha is like that amora, and even if you will say the halacha is like him, perhaps he concedes where skin was removed from that place alone, since all the skin remained; and since it is a double safek, he ruled leniently.
