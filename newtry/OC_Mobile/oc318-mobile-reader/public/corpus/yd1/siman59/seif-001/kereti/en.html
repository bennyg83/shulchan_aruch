Behold it is permitted; and in a time of need, if the width of a selah remained on the spine or on the navel, one may rely on Rashba and poskim to declare kosher — Shach and Peri Chadash; and since it is something entirely uncommon, I was brief.
Kosher — this is Rambam's view, who holds: since it should have been flayed and was not flayed, it is a double safek: perhaps kosher, and even if you will say treifah — perhaps this is not the place that declares treifah in galudah; therefore if removed from the width of the navel and the entire spine — we clarified in the previous s.k. that one may rely that this saves from galudah — if so, conversely one should certainly be stringent.
If removed from all three of them; and if all the vertebrae and all the joints were removed from part of them and no width of a selah remained on the spine — treifah, Shach; and see Peri Chadash; and I was brief in uncommon matters.
