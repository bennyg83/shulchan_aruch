And if there was removed, etc. Maharshal wrote in Piskei Dinim siman 91: it appears specifically when removed the width of a selah along the entire length of the spine, and likewise the width of a selah on all the other places; but a strip less than the width of a selah in those places — there is no concern, even though we require the full width of a selah in those places — that is, when all the skin was removed and in those places we came to save from galudah, therefore we require that the full width of a selah remain in order to save from galudah; but where its skin was not removed except from those places, we do not require the full width of a selah. And so Rashba ruled, end of his words — and this language of Rashba is not clear (in his novellae daf 59a, and Beit Yosef brings it). However, it certainly appears that for all, if skin was removed from part of the spine and all the skin remains, or from part of the joints and all the skin remains — it is permitted; but if all the skin was removed, we require that the width of a selah remain along the entire spine itself (or: the width of a selah on every joint from the joints of its limbs themselves), because there is no other skin there to save except this; but where there is other skin, it joins to save — such as where all the rest of the body's skin remains, the mind does not give that when skin was removed from one vertebra or two in the spine it should become treifah, or from one or two places from the joints; only when all of them or most of them were removed. And so it is reasonable, since Rabbi Yishmael required: if the place of the entire spine was removed, the joints were removed — implying that when the entire place of the spine was removed, or all the joints were removed, there is doubt; not from part of them and most of them like all of them, end of his words. And it implies that he permits only when part of the vertebra was removed or part of the joints were removed; but in any case the majority of the vertebrae and joints were not removed at all; therefore the mind does not give at all to declare treifah because one vertebra or two was removed, or one or two joints; but when all the vertebrae and all the joints were removed from part of them and no width of a selah remained at all — certainly the mind gives to declare treifah, since along the entire length of the spine and all the joints there is no width of a selah at all — so it appears to me.
