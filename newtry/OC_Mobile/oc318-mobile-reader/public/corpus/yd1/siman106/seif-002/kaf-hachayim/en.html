But the piece itself is forbidden, etc. — not specifically when there is sixty in the pot against the absorbed issur that the piece is forbidden; rather even if there is sixty against the whole piece, the piece does not return to its former permitted state. And one might say for one who holds chadichah na'aseh nevelah even in other issurim — but even for one who holds there is no chadichah na'aseh nevelah in other issurim, only in basar bechalav — it does not return to permission.
