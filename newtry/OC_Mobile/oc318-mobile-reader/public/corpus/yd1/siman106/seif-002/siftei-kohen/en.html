That has sixty to nullify the milk. And for us, since chadichah na'aseh nevelah is established in all issurim, and likewise in dabuk issur sixty is required against the whole piece — so here too sixty is required against the whole piece that the milk is attached to; and even so the piece itself is forbidden:
That the milk in it will forbid it. Explanation: when one removes it and it is hot — then the piece will be forbidden on account of the milk in it that does not have sixty to nullify it; but when leaving it in the pot, taste goes equally throughout and the piece is not forbidden. And one should not challenge: it was certainly salted first — otherwise it would be forbidden on account of blood and would need sixty in the dish against all of it, as explained in siman 69 seif 11 — thus it was first forbidden through salting with the milk in it and does not return to permission, as Rashba and Mechaber wrote in seif 1. One might say it deals with d'rabbanan milk or milk of a lean animal that does not forbid in salting except kedei kelipah according to Rashba and Mechaber in siman 64 seif 20 and siman 105. Or it deals where the piece had sixty against the milk and afterward, before cooking, part was cut from it:
And according to what we hold chadichah na'aseh nevelah, etc. Explanation: thus this piece is in any case forbidden on account of dabuk issur; if so, one removes the piece, etc. — for removing it does not harm at all; thus one should not leave it in the pot for nothing.
