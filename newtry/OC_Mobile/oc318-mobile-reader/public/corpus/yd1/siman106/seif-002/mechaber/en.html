A piece that has milk in it that was cooked in a pot that has sixty to nullify the milk — one must be careful not to remove anything from the pot while the forbidden piece is still inside, lest it remain at the end when there is no sixty in the pot to nullify the milk; and likewise one should not remove it first, because the milk in it will forbid it — what is its remedy? Leave it until the pot cools: {Rama: And according to what is established for us that "a piece becomes nevelah" — one removes the forbidden piece from there and the rest is permitted (logical opinion).}
