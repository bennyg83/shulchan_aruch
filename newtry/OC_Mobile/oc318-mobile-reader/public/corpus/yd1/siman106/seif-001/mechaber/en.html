The law how we nullify with sixty. In it are 2 seifim: A piece that absorbed an issur and does not have sixty in it to nullify it — if it fell into a pot — it does not forbid except according to the calculation of the issur in it; that is, if in what is in the pot there is, together with the piece itself, sixty against the issur absorbed in it — what is in the pot is permitted; but the piece itself is forbidden, because the issur in it does not fully leave from it (Rashba and Tosafot chapter 3, daf 100, and Rosh and Mordechai there and Semag daf 50b and Sefer HaTerumah siman 52 and so in the language of R' Efrayim himself in the novelae of Rashba daf 110 and at the end of Sefer Raavan and so Maharshal chapter 3 siman 57 and all the Acharonim) (and so is primary (Gra, Kereti) and not like what was written above siman 92 that even that piece is permitted) — which is not so in a thing that was mixed and dissolved, that if blood or the like fell into permitted gravy and they were forbidden because of its minority and afterward the permitted gravy increased until there is sixty in all of it to nullify the blood — all is permitted, because everything was mixed and intermingled: {Rama: And according to what we practice saying in all issurim "a piece becomes nevelah" — there is no distinction between a moist thing and a dry thing except for this matter; that if what became forbidden was already a dry thing and there is sixty against it — the piece that was first forbidden remains in its prohibition and must be removed from there if one recognizes it; and if one does not recognize it — it is nullified if it is not a piece worthy of honor (Shach, Gra); and if it is a moist thing — everything is permitted once there is sixty against what was first forbidden; and see above siman 92 and 99.}
