But the piece, etc. Even though chaticha na'aseis neveilah does not apply to other prohibitions, regarding the ability to squeeze it out there is no distinction, as the Ran wrote at the end of chapter 25 — that the ability to squeeze it out and chaticha na'aseis neveilah are two separate matters. This is what the Gemara states: "If he holds it is possible to squeeze it out and it is permitted, why etc." — and if they were the same thing, "why" would not be applicable; see there.
