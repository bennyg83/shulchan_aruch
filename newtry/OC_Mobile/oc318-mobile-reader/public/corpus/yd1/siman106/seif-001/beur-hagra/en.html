It is included, etc. As written in 97b concerning the udder: the udder is counted, but the udder itself is forbidden:
But the piece, etc. Although chatichah na'aseit neveilah does not apply to other prohibitions, regarding the possibility of squeezing it out there is no distinction. As the Ran wrote at the end of chapter 25, the possibility of squeezing it out and chatichah na'aseit neveilah are two separate matters. This is what the Gemara says: If he holds that it is possible to squeeze it out and it is permitted, why, etc.? If they were one matter, the question why would not apply. See there. And as written concerning the udder and the udder itself, etc., the Rashba explained: perhaps it did not expel all its milk, and the same applies here:
And not as, etc. There he follows the wording of the Tur, and the Tur follows his own view, disputing the Rashba here and maintaining that the possibility of squeezing it out applies only to meat and milk, as Rashi explained, that all is one matter.
