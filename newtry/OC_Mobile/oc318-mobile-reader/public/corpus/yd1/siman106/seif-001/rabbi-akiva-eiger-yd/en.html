(Siman 106 seif 1) But the piece itself is forbidden. Nevertheless, if it was mixed among other pieces it is nullified, for it is not a piece worthy of honor since the prohibition is not on account of itself, as above (beginning siman 101), and as Shach wrote (here s.k. 4); and it appears it is nullified in the majority, for there is no concern lest one cook and impart taste — for the taste of the piece itself that became forbidden is with the other pieces batel b'mehuto, and there is no taste, as Shach wrote (siman 107); and if perhaps there is not sixty against the taste of the issur absorbed in the permitted — for even for one who does not hold chadichah na'aseh nevelah, nevertheless the piece is forbidden as the Mechaber wrote — we are concerned lest the absorption remained inside; nevertheless one may say the concern is only that something small remains and it does not return to permission since it did not fully leave — see Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba's responsum, and see above (siman 94 seif 6); thus it will not impart taste in the other pieces — investigate; and see in Seder Taharah (siman 88 end s.k. 4):
