(Siman 106 in Shulchan Aruch seif 1) But the piece itself is forbidden, because the issur in it does not fully leave from it — unlike in a thing that was absorbed, etc. And one might say: behold, the Mechaber holds "if it is possible to squeeze it out, forbidden" — thus from reasoning one should distinguish between a piece and a thing that was mixed; for even though it is a thing that was mixed, nevertheless if the issur fully leaves from it — granted, the reason of "if it is possible to squeeze it out" is because the name of issur is called upon it once; unlike in a thing that was mixed it is satisfactory. But regarding "if it is possible to squeeze it out" there is no distinction at all, and one must say: even though we hold "if it is possible to squeeze it out," this means only that something small remains inside and the whole reason that remains in its prohibition is that the name of issur was called upon it once — it does not return to permission; but in a thing that was mixed there is no such reason — "if it is possible to squeeze it out, forbidden":
