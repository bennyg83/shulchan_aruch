(1) [Seif 1] A piece that absorbed an issur and does not have sixty in it to nullify it — if it fell into a pot, it does not forbid except according to calculation, etc. — this is according to the Mechaber's view, who holds we do not say chadichah na'aseh nevelah in other issurim, unlike for us (i.e., Ashkenazim), as the Rav explains afterward in the hagahah. Shach s.k. 1. And see above siman 92 seif 4 and siman 99 seif 3, and in our words there, with Heaven's help.
