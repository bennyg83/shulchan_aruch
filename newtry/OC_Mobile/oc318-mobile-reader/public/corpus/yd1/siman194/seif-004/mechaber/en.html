If she gave birth to a live fetus and afterward miscarried a shilya, she is not concerned for another fetus; rather, she attributes it to the fetus that she already gave birth to, up to 23 days. But if she miscarried first, we do not attribute to that miscarriage the shilya that she miscarried afterward, and she is concerned for the shilya, to give her days of tumah of a female.
