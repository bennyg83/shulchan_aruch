(°) Rema writes in responsum 94 that the custom in these countries is to be lenient with women after childbirth who immersed before forty days following the birth of a male child, etc., particularly since several great authorities wrote that the contrary practice closely resembles a Sadducean custom. See Rambam in chapter 11 of the Laws of Forbidden Relations. Turei Zahav writes that those who act leniently are not considered to have breached an established boundary.
