Within 40. Avnei HaTzedek s.k. 4 in name of Avi Ezri we count 40 days from day of immersion; but for stain requires study; and see responsum Teshuvah Me'ahavah part 1 siman 70 wrote this needs no study at all for we say in Talmud Niddah page 58 Sages did not say regarding stain to be lenient on Torah matter only to be stringent; and if thought to count 40 days from day of her immersion on stain — then you find stains come to be lenient on Torah matter — see there. And implies from complete proof he agrees; and as explained there explicitly; however in Chiddushei HaRim he disagreed also here because within three months of pregnancy even fixed veset as stated siman 189 — plainly can see — see there; and see responsum Noda Biyhudah part Even HaEzer siman 69. [Also in Siftei Tzedek s.k. 7 disagrees; and so answered responsum Chatam Sofer siman 169 Heaven forbid to rely on this at all to be lenient; and miscarriage and she did not know — husband separated from her forever — we are in doubt fetus and she sits 14 days — see there. And all not like Chokhmat Adam kelal 101 din 20 appears followed Avi Ezri's words; and see in books Pithei Teshuvah EH siman 4 seif 14 s.v. lo amrinan and siman 156 seif 4 s.v. vehinich ishto].
For opening of makor. See Noda Biyhudah second YD siman 120 — what they said impossible for opening of makor without blood — no distinction between cause of opening from inside versus from outside such as doctor inserted finger or some instrument and opened mouth of makor; also no distinction whether she gave birth or elderly pregnant or nursing — always no opening of makor without blood — see there. [And see Binat Adam siman 23 wrote on him what he wrote such as doctor inserted finger while washing — also not precise for main bedikah in hefsek taharah she inserts finger deep; or this matter not for opening of makor — for even limb when large reaches only foreskin not cheder as Taz in Rambam chapter 2 Niddah 45 — see there. Also responsum Chatam Sofer siman 179 wrote whether woman checking herself in hospital or midwife who checked — never touched opening of makor which is makor only outer chamber; and in no way can insert finger inside until opens by nature — and this called opening of makor impossible without blood — see there.] And see responsum Teshuvah Me'ahavah part 1 siman 116 in responsum Gaon Noda Biyhudah z"l there wrote what they said no opening of makor without blood — when makor opened and emerged from it lump like fetus or piece not thin as hollow reed straw or she gave birth wind; but when nothing emerged or very thin thing or liquid emerged — we do not say no opening of makor without blood — for otherwise where do you find pure blood at all — behold makor opened — see there further.
