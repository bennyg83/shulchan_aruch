The night. Tur requires separation on the forty-first night after a male birth and the eighty-first after a female birth. Taz writes that since nowadays relations are not permitted during blood of purity, there is no need for this additional separation, which is why the Shulchan Aruch omits it.
The practice. Bach treats violation of the custom to wait forty days after a male and eighty after a female as breach of a fence. Taz strongly disagrees, citing Rambam's opposition to the custom and widespread communities that do not observe it. He rules that one should not punish those who are lenient. Rama likewise records lenient practice in these lands.
