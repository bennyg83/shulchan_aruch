And one may not permit where they practiced to be stringent. Although so is law in all matters enjoyed with prohibition you may not permit — nevertheless needed to inform us here lest you say it was mistaken custom; and also many protested the custom and called foolish custom as explained in Beit Yosef; therefore needed to inform us one may not permit; and see Darkei Moshe and Shach and Taz reason for custom; and Taz concluded one who is lenient in the matter Heaven forbid to impose any punishment — for we see this custom is not fixed; and we see in holy communities deed every day many women nearly majority are lenient; and if miscarried miscarriage none wait 80 days but immerse whenever can — end quote; and so meaning of Rav who also did not write this custom nearby seif 3 regarding miscarriage law; and so Shach there s.k. 4 — and implies regarding miscarriage there is no one stringent to require 80 days and 7 clean days after 14 suffice — end quote. And behold what he wrote to require 7 clean days after 14 days — I do not know why he ruled simply against majority poskim and Shulchan Aruch seif 1 and 3 that even 14 days she did not see in them count toward 7 clean days; and so Rav's view here in Hagahah what Rav wrote "and she counted 7 clean days" — meaning within 7 for male or 14 for female; and so Atzei Arazim; and although R. Tam's view is so — nevertheless in those places that do not wait 40 for male and 80 for female we are not concerned for R. Tam's view for otherwise would need two immersions per his view as explained in Darkei Moshe from Shach's own words s.k. 1; requires study. And Shach concluded further in my humble opinion be stringent to separate from her night 41 and night 81 in miscarriage because of doubt male or female; and see Taz s.k. 2 — no one is concerned.
