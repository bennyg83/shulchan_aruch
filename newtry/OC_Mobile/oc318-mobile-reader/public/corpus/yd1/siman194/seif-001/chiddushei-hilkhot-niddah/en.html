And afterward, etc. and one may not permit where they practiced to be stringent, etc. — Taz, Maharshak in Tiferet Shmuel, and Rama himself in responsum wrote this custom did not spread; and in truth this custom has no reason and essence — how give place to this custom when Tur testified in his days they had relations on blood of purity in all Ashkenaz; and how afterward reversed to custom that even without seeing blood she is forbidden as if seeing? In my humble opinion initially when they wished to abolish without having relations on blood of purity and were not satisfied — woman who saw blood did not show to sages at all for they had tradition these days blood is pure — therefore decreed she should not have relations at all until days of purity pass; and through this preserved from relations on blood of purity — this in my humble opinion is the custom and reason.
