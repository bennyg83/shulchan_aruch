One who gives birth to twins, and one fetus delayed after the other — for example, one [emerged] before sunset and the other after sunset: from when the first emerged she is tamei with tumas leidah, and we count the days of tumah from when the last emerged. And if the first is recognizable as male, and the second is recognizable as female, or it is not recognizable whether the second is male or female, she counts from when the second emerged days of tumah of a female.
