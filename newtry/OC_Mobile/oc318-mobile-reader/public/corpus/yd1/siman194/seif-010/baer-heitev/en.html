Most of it. Biblically, birth impurity begins when most of the fetus emerges. Rabbinically, if a limb emerged and was withdrawn, she is impure from childbirth. She is also a niddah even biblically because opening of the womb cannot occur without blood. Shach and Levush.
Its forehead. Tur and Beit Yosef write 'most of its forehead,' implying that the Mishnah's phrase 'its forehead' means the majority of the forehead. Shach.
