The fetus was cut in her intestines and a limb emerged limb by limb — whether it emerged in order of the limbs, for example, the foot emerged and after it the shin and after it the thigh, or whether it emerged not in order — she is not tamei with tumas leidah until most of it emerged. But if its head emerged entirely at once, behold this is like most of it. And if it was not cut and emerged in its normal manner: from when its forehead emerges, behold it is like a born one, even if it was cut afterward. And this is not only when it actually emerged outside, but even from when it emerged outside the prozdor.
