Within 40. Avnei HaTzedek s.k. 4 in name of Avi Ezri we count 40 days from day of immersion; but for stain requires study; and see responsum Teshuvah Me'ahavah part 1 siman 70 wrote this needs no study at all for we say in Talmud Niddah page 58 Sages did not say regarding stain to be lenient on Torah matter only to be stringent; and if thought to count 40 days from day of her immersion on stain — then you find stains come to be lenient on Torah matter — see there. And implies from complete proof he agrees; and as explained there explicitly; however in Chiddushei HaRim he disagreed also here because within three months of pregnancy even fixed veset as stated siman 189 — plainly can see — see there; and see responsum Noda Biyhudah part Even HaEzer siman 69. [Also in Siftei Tzedek s.k. 7 disagrees; and so answered responsum Chatam Sofer siman 169 Heaven forbid to rely on this at all to be lenient; and miscarriage and she did not know — husband separated from her forever — we are in doubt fetus and she sits 14 days — see there. And all not like Chokhmat Adam kelal 101 din 20 appears followed Avi Ezri's words; and see in books Pithei Teshuvah EH siman 4 seif 14 s.v. lo amrinan and siman 156 seif 4 s.v. vehinich ishto].
