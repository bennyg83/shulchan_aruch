She must be concerned that it was a child. We therefore impose upon her, out of doubt, the impurity period of one who gave birth to a female.
Her immersion did not count. Rather, she requires seven clean days after the 14 days, as above in seif 1. It appears that in the case of a miscarriage no authority requires her to wait 81 days; seven clean days after 14 days suffice, and this also appears from the later authorities. Nevertheless, it seems that a scrupulous person should separate from her on the night of day 41 and the night of day 81 because of the doubt whether the fetus was male or female.
