She is concerned for a fetus. And we treat her as tamei with the tumah of a female birth as a doubt.
Her immersion did not count. Rather, she requires seven clean days and immersion after fourteen days, for perhaps it was a female, but after eighty-one days she does not require immersion even by their ruling. Still, a baal nefesh should separate from his wife on the night of the forty-first day and the night of the eighty-first day, in order to satisfy the view of the one who says that with a female she is impure eighty-one days, as the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham.
