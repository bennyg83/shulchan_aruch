One who miscarries something like an animal, wild animal, or bird, or like fish and locusts and creeping things and crawling things, and any form of a fetus; or a shafir; or a shilya; or a piece that they tore, and there is bone in it: now, since we are not expert in forms, she is concerned for a fetus. And if her 7 nekiyim were completed within 14 days, if she immersed before the night of the 15th, the immersion did not count for her.
