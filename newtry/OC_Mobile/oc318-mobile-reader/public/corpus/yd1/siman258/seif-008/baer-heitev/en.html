Nothing. Since the property is not in his possession, the consecration does not take effect. If he says, 'This field that I am selling you shall become consecrated when I repurchase it,' it takes effect upon repurchase because he could have consecrated it now. If he says this only after the sale, it does not. A mortgaged field may become consecrated upon redemption, though not immediately while subject to the lender's lien. Shach.
