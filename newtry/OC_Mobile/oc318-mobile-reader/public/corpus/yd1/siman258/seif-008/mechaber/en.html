If he has a debt owed by someone and says it shall be for hekdesh or charity — it is nothing; but if he said, "The debt owed me by so-and-so — when it comes into my hand I will consecrate it or give it to charity," he is obligated to fulfill his words to consecrate it and give it when it comes into his hand; and even one who says, "The debt in so-and-so's hands shall be for charity" — if he said so before a court, before the collector, or before the town notables (or an important person in the city) (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Yehudah) — the collector acquires it by the law of the standing of all three, and behold it is charity and he cannot retract, and it is forbidden to divert it.
