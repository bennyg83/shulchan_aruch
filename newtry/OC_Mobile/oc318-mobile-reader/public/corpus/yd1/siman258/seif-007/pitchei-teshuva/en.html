He cannot retract. VeShav HaKohen rules that a charitable pledge is equivalent to delivery to an ordinary recipient and cannot be retracted even immediately. Pnei Aryeh discusses a gift pledged to a poor person who later became wealthy: if the money was already transferred to an agent who acquired it for him while poor, it cannot be withdrawn. Otherwise the donor may seek annulment through genuine regret, though not through the later enrichment as a newly arisen opening. If not annulled, the recipient should pass the gift to another poor person. Chatam Sofer rules that where support was pledged for orphans who later became financially secure, unpaid future installments need not be given, because the pledge was clearly conditioned on need.
