The Rif from the baraita "in your mouth this is charity" in Bava Kamma beginning of chapter 4; and similarly in Shulchan Arukh. Rashba wrote in a responsum on this: even though money that has no claimant — when this poor person claims he says I will give it to another poor person — nevertheless he is obligated to Heaven to give because of "in your mouth this is charity".
