The Rif, from the baraita, 'that which is in your mouth is charity,' at the beginning of chapter 4 of Bava Kamma. The Shulchan Aruch rules likewise. Rashba writes in a responsum that although this is money with no specific claimant, because when one poor person claims it the donor may say, 'I will give it to another poor person,' he nevertheless remains obligated before Heaven to give it, by virtue of 'that which is in your mouth is charity.'
