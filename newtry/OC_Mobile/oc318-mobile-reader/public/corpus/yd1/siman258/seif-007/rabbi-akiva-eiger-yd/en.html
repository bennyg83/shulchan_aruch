(Shulchan Arukh seif 7) Because it is not in his possession. If the robber confessed and wants to return — the view of Tosafot (chapter 1 of Bava Metzia) in the sugya of "the priest overpowered" that he can consecrate it; and similarly the view of Razah (chapter 4 of Bava Kamma) and Ramban in Milchamot (there) disagrees with him. And see in Shitah Mekubetzet Bava Kamma (page 33) "mishnah: from whom" who brought in the name of Ramah that regarding robbery only when they did not bring him before judges; but if he consecrated it and afterward they brought him to judgment and extracted it from his hand in court the consecration takes effect.
