He shall give to both of them together. One hundred to this one and one hundred to that one.
One who says that he will give, etc. Responsa attributed to the Ramban, siman 268, states: Reuven purchased from the city leaders a garden adjoining a synagogue and stipulated in the deed of sale that each year he would give a flask of oil for lighting. A dispute later arose among the townspeople. Some said he should give it to the synagogue adjoining the garden, while others said he should give it to the larger synagogue. It should be divided between the two synagogues according to what is fitting for each, the larger according to its size and the smaller according to its size. This is the language of the Beit Yosef. Darkei Moshe writes: “This requires study. Why should he not give it to the synagogue he regularly attends?” I do not know what troubled him, for the case can involve one who regularly attends both.
