For charity. Mahari ben Chaviv discusses one who consecrated his body or land for the poor in post-Temple times. He is uncertain whether valuation requires ten men including a kohen as in Temple consecrations, and recommends stringency in practice. Valuation follows the person's present worth. One who says, 'The value of one-fourth of my body is upon me,' or vows to give one-fourth of his body, must pay the value of his entire body as assessed like a slave, especially where the vow was made for a mitzvah or in distress.
