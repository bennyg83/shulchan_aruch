One who says, "Give 100, 100 zuz, or a Torah scroll to the synagogue" — they give to the synagogue he is accustomed to in the city where he lives: {Rama: And if he is accustomed to two, they give to both (Tur). One who says to give oil for the lamp — they give to the synagogue and not to the study hall (Beit Yosef in the name of Teshuvat Ramban siman 268).}
