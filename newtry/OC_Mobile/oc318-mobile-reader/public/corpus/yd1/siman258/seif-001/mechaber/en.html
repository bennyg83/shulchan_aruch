The law of one who designates charity. And it contains 13 seifim. One who designates charity — behold he is like one who designates a vow: How so? A sela of charity was placed before him and he said of another, "Behold this one is like this" — (behold this) is charity:
{Rama: A person should not say, "This sela is for hekdesh," but rather for charity (Mordechai, beginning of Avodah Zarah; Beit Yosef end of siman 259 in the name of Hagahot Ashiri there and Kol Bo); nevertheless, if he said for hekdesh unstated, his intent is charity for the poor (Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban and Teshuvat Rashba siman 462); however, if he said his intent is actual hekdesh, there is no remedy to benefit from it and he must ask a sage who permits him with regret like other vows (Hagahot Ashiri there).}
