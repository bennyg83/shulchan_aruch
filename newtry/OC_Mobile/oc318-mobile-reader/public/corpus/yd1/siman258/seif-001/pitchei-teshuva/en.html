One who designates for charity. Maharit holds that a person cannot appoint an agent to consecrate an item, because consecratory speech is treated as words that cannot be delegated. Noda BiYehudah's son, Shivat Tzion, and Shaar HaMelech challenge him, explaining that 'words cannot be delegated' ordinarily means only that the first agent may not appoint a second agent, not that the original agency is invalid.
