One who seizes for charity. See responsum of Mahari"t part 8 siman 127 in the words "however the matters are not". He wrote that a person cannot make an agent to consecrate something for consecration, for speech of consecration are words and are not entrusted to an agent — see there. And see responsum of Noda BiYehudah part 2 Yoreh De'ah siman 147 in a responsum from the author's son who extended to wonder on him: what we say "words are not entrusted to an agent" — means the agent cannot make another agent in his place, but the first agent can be for mundane matters too — see there. And see in his book Shevet Tziyon simanim 94 and 95 what he further wrote on this. And see in Sha'ar HaMelekh end of chapter 6 of Hilchot Ishut what he further wondered on Mahari"t.
