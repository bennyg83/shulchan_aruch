There, from Rav Pappa's unresolved question. Rambam treats it as resolved through an if-you-wish formulation. The Rosh writes in his rulings that since it was not resolved, we rule stringently. The Ran likewise writes this in the name of Ramban, and that Rashba appears to agree. He challenges this from several sugyot and proves that money belonging to the poor is not treated as a doubt in ritual law, but as a monetary doubt, which is resolved leniently.
