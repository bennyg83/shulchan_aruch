He must give. For regarding charity asmakhta acquires, per the rulings of Mahar"a Isralan that Beit Yosef brought; nevertheless Darkei Moshe brings in the name of responsum of Rashba siman 281: one who said if I play I will give the governor such and such and to consecration such and such — since it is asmakhta regarding a common person it is asmakhta also regarding consecration; and it seems this is similar to "acquire yourself and the donkey" that he does not acquire as stated in chapter Mi SheMet and in Choshen Mishpat siman 210.
