He is obligated to give it. Regarding charity, an asmachta creates an obligation, in accordance with the ruling of Maharar Yisrael Isserlein cited by the Beit Yosef. Nevertheless, the Darkei Moshe cites Responsa Rashba, siman 281, concerning one who said, “If I gamble, I will give such-and-such to the ruler and such-and-such to hekdesh.” Since the undertaking is an asmachta regarding the ordinary recipient, it is also an asmachta regarding hekdesh. It appears that this resembles the case of, “You and a donkey shall acquire,” where the acquisition is ineffective, as stated in the chapter Mi SheMeis and in Choshen Mishpat, siman 210.
