For charity. The Shach wrote: likewise regarding any other vow he is obligated to fulfill, for there is no vow or oath by asmakhta as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 207 seif 9. The Taz wrote: nevertheless Darkei Moshe brings responsum of Rashba: one who said if I play I will give the governor such and such and to consecration such and such — since it is asmakhta regarding a common person it is asmakhta regarding consecration too; and it seems this is similar to "acquire yourself and the donkey" that he does not acquire as stated in Choshen Mishpat siman 210 — end quote.
