He said to give, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 4 of Bava Metzia halakhah 2: R. Avahu in the name of R. Yochanan said: one who said to give his fellow a gift and asked to retract may retract, etc. — this you say regarding a rich man; but regarding a poor man it becomes a vow. Rav deposited with Shmaya: if I tell you, give a gift to so-and-so — if poor he gives immediately; if wealthy he consults with me. They brought the Rif, Rosh and Rashba there, and in Choshen Mishpat siman 243 seif 2.
