He must give it immediately. This is because the poor are present. If no poor person is present, he may set the money aside until a poor person comes, as explained above, siman 257, seif 3.
And if he delays, he violates, etc. Meaning, once three festivals have passed. This applies when no poor person was present when he vowed. If poor people were present, he violates immediately, as stated above.
He violates “You shall not delay.” Even though charity is not a sacrificial offering, it is included in the verse, as explained in Rosh Hashanah 6a.
