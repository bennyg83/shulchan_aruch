And even though he does not give him wages except because he deals with his share — nevertheless in that benefit that he invests money to do business with them it is considered as if he takes wages for guarding them. It appears in my eyes if the iska were in a manner that through dealing with the deposit share he has profit in his loan share — for he can buy good merchandise that he could not buy with the lender's share alone; or he must carry the iska's merchandise to a distant place and if he carried only the lender's share the expenses would be hard on him — therefore he must carry also the deposit share so the expense on his share is reduced — in this he need not give him labor wages, for this is preferable to two-thirds in profit, etc. And we find such reasoning in Yerushalmi chapter Mi SheHayah Nasui: therefore a small shopkeeper said a great shopkeeper — what does it mean that he can buy much merchandise through partnership of this one; and likewise in Ketubot (92a): if you bring me for six — all the more it is worth twelve; and Rashi explained one who buys much merchandise — they are cheaper for him more. And the Talmudic sages here do not deal with such an iska; and proof from what they said in chapter HaMekabel regarding an iska that was half loan — if the recipient wishes he may drink beer with the lender's share he may do so; Rava said therefore it is called an iska — for he said to him when I give you it is to deal with it and not to drink beer with it; and Rashi explained it should remain always and they rely on its owner that it not be given for export — end of his words; and it is brought below siman 177 seif 30. And if it dealt there with such an iska certainly it would not enter the mind that he drink beer with the lender's share — for behold he harms the iska giver that he cannot profit so much with the deposit share alone; and further they say there afterward: these two who did business together — one said to his fellow "come let us divide" — his fellow withholds him because of the luck of two being better; and he did not say because we can profit that we can buy good merchandise when the sum is large — perforce the Gemara does not deal with such an iska. But in truth if there were a matter where he has the benefit we mentioned — certainly good reasoning that he benefits greatly from the deposit share and need not give him labor wages — as appears in my humble opinion.
