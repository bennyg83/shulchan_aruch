The lender etc. And in Bedek HaBayit he wrote in the name of Orchot Chayim that only at the time of the loan is it forbidden to do so lechatchilah; but if he already lent on the collateral and now says "if you do not repay me the collateral shall be yours from now" — it is permitted — see there.
There are those etc. This requires examination, for the "some who permit" above seif 14 implies they disagree, since he does not receive money at the end; and it is forced to say that since the collateral was in his possession from the outset it is worse — for Rivash there permits for that reason in one who lent him on his field and said to him "if you do not redeem it by such a time it shall be mine," even though the field is worth more than the debt, since he does not take money at the end; and perhaps one should distinguish between real estate collateral and movables.
