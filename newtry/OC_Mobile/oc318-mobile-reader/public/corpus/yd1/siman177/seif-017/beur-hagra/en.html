The lender, etc. By strict law it is permitted, as stated there, 65b, in the Mishnah: one who lent him money against his field, etc. This applies even without saying 'from now,' as stated there, 66a and 66b, since the requirement of 'from now' is only because of asmachta. One cannot answer that land is different because the laws of overreaching do not apply to land, for there it says that if he mortgaged a house to him, etc., 'at this valuation,' it is forbidden. Also there, 64b, the rule that he may not rent it from him for less was prohibited only because it resembles ribbis. This requires study, however, because it appears to contradict the Mishnah cited above. Rivash, responsum 335, likewise challenged the ruling in seif 14 from that Mishnah, and answered that since the prohibition is only because it resembles ribbis, it applies only where he initially gives money and later receives more money, unlike the Mishnah cited above. The ruling here, however, remains difficult.
