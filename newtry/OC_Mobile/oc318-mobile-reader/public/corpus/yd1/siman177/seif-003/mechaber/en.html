To what does this apply? When he did not set with him at the beginning of the business, rather he gave it to him for half profit. But if he set with him at the beginning of the business wages for his toil in a known matter — even with a dinar — it is permitted (as Beit Yosef explained the words of Rambam). {Rama: And all this is when one gives the money; but if each one gives money, even if only one engages, there is no iska here; rather it is like a simple partnership (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rif and Semag).}
