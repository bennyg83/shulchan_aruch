Or to designate, etc. Rambam; and as written there: 'who designated it,' etc.; and as written there 31b. And if not, etc. — and he does not, etc. See Rashi there s.v. 'who,' etc.; and Tur and Nimukei Yosef wrote that from his explanation it appears that any designation alone suffices — for they divide equally; and such is the view of Rambam and Tur. But Tosafot said that even without any division at all and without notification, one takes half of his fellow's [share]; and as written there in the default case: 'from here on he takes,' etc. — and what is written 'who,' etc. — 'and furthermore,' etc. — are two separate things: 'who designated it for you — and the division is nothing'; and 'furthermore, the place,' etc. — 'and you may not take half of your fellow's [portion].' And so we say in the Yerushalmi: 'a person who gave a hundred dinars to his fellow, and they earned twenty dinars — he took ten for himself, and the other became a principal for them; but people do not do so — they receive and give, and in the end they divide.' And Tur wrote: since such is the custom, even according to Tosafot he does not take half of his fellow's [portion] whenever he did not designate it — for they entered the partnership on the understanding of the custom; and as above beginning of siman 174; and likewise written in the gloss according to the view of Tur and Rashi. But in the Shulchan Arukh it is written: 'the division is nothing' — according to the view of Tosafot; and although Rashba concludes: 'since they are accustomed,' etc. — that is in a place where such is the custom.
