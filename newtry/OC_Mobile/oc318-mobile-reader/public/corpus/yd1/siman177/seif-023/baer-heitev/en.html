The reason is that in the first year there is greater care because it is delicate and must be put in and taken out; and there is an argument to the contrary that on the contrary the second year has greater care because it requires much feed — therefore each has an argument in his favor and withholds his fellow that the iska continue until both these times end — Taz. And he further wrote: Question — one received an iska from his fellow for a sum of money for a time, and after the payment time the recipient withheld the money with him and dealt with them and does not wish to give profit from what accrues after the payment time, because he said that after that time the money was a deposit with him since he has no heter iska with him only until the time and afterward it would be ribbis what he gives him. Another question in Maharam Mintz: one accepted on himself three hundred gold coins principal in an iska and to give profit to the document issuer from that iska one thousand gold coins at payment time on Rosh Chodesh Nisan; and if payment is delayed even partially, the signatory accepted from now to deal with that sum that is withheld with him as that iska and to give profits according to the profit rate above; and afterward payment was delayed some time and the giver came that he give him profits from the profits — meaning from Rosh Chodesh Nisan it became five hundred gold coins principal and he should give him profits also from the thousand gold coins if the law is with him or not. Answer to the first question: it seems clear the law is with the giver and he is obligated to give him profits also from the business after payment time; and the permission from the outset continues afterward as well until he informs after the time that he does not wish to receive further in the iska those monies; and he brought proofs. On the second question, if there will be on the profit there principal after the time — it seems the law is with him, for although the custom is not to take profit from profit, nevertheless since it is mentioned explicitly in the document that from today he accepts upon himself to deal with what is withheld in his hand even part of the payment — it seems clear that in such a case there is no custom — end of his words.
