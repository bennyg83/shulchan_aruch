One who gives an animal to his fellow as issur in asses — must attend eighteen months and examine at twenty-four; within this time each delays the other; offspring — where custom is to divide immediately they divide; where there is no custom the recipient raises them; small cattle examined thirty days, large fifty days; from then on he takes half the profit and half of the other share; need not give his toil wage from the other share; if he comes to divide he must inform his fellow or appraise and stipulate before three; if not the division is nothing (and he takes only half profit as initially) (Tur).
