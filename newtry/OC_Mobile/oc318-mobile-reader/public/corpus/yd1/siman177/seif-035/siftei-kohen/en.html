For a fixed time etc. Bach wrote: specifically for a fixed time; but if he did not fix a time — he can say to his fellow "give me half my profit and half my principal" etc., even though the time for its sale has not arrived according to the custom of the world; and see CM siman 177 regarding these laws.
But just for the profit — we do not heed him. For the luck of two is greater — Talmud.
