Regarding that it is not asmakhta, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 207; and here all agree it is permitted since it is a penalty method, only it is forbidden due to circumvention of ribbit; and here in selling him merchandise, since it is a penalty method, even circumvention of ribbit does not apply; end of Shach's words. Bach wrote: specifically when he stipulated that he will give for it at that time immediately one hundred gold coins; but if the condition was that if he does not give him the merchandise for half a year he will give him for it after the year ends one hundred gold coins; it is full ribbit. Shach wrote it is not necessary except if he stipulated explicitly that if he does not give him for half a year he will give him one hundred gold coins when the year ends and the lender is obligated to wait until the year ends; in this it is reasonable that it is full ribbit; and there is no law of six needed, and likewise Taz's opinion tends this way; see there.
