A third. Taz wrote by way of example: if the loss is twenty-four gold coins, we say; if this sum were profit the recipient would take twelve; now he accepts damage of eight gold coins; and likewise always according to value. For example, a quarter in profit for the recipient; we say if there will be damage the recipient accepts upon himself two-thirds of it, meaning four; and if he wrote that he takes three parts in profit and the giver a quarter, damage comes upon the recipient twelve; end of his words.
