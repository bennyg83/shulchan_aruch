If it is customary to hire a porter, etc. The Tur writes that the amount is assessed according to local custom so that the recipient suffers no loss. The Beit Yosef explains that if the custom is for the recipient to carry the produce on his own shoulder, he will certainly carry it and will not receive a porter's wage. If, however, the custom is that the giver pays the porter, or reimburses the porter's wage, failure to do so would cause this recipient a loss compared with other recipients.
