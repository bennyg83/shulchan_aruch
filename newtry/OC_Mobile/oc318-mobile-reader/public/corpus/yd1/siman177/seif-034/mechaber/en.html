A recipient of issur from his fellow who lost and did not inform him but continued and filled the principal — cannot say "you lose your share of the loss," but the principal is filled from profit; if there is surplus they divide per their terms: {Rama: specifically when he did not inform; but if he informed and said "I will not engage further until we divide profit" — permission is in his hand (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba's students and Noda BiYehudah); for he can retract whenever he wishes as will be explained; a recipient of issur gives tax from profit first and afterward they divide unless they stipulated he can take profit whenever, or he informed to take his share from profit — then if he gave tax afterward it counts as loss (Mordechai chapter HaMekabel).}
