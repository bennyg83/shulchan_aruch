I will not engage, etc. Meaning, he informed him of the loss and said to him "I will not engage until you accept upon yourself that we divide profit from today onward," and the profit will not be encumbered to the principal; the choice is in his hand — and such is the explanation in the words of the students of the Rashba; and it is difficult. And Bach wrote there is no understanding of these words and it is a scribal error; and in my humble opinion as written.
