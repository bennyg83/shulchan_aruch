If etc. Maharshal wrote in chapter HaChovel siman 70 from the language of the poskim: one who lent his fellow money and afterward the lender said "half-profit" — and an accident occurred — the borrower is liable for accidents; and if there was profit — it is all his; and if he gives from himself — it is permitted to accept it, for this is not ribbit since he did not stipulate so at the outset — see there at length.
