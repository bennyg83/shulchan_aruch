Rambam, chapter 5 of Hilchot Malveh, halachah 10. The Maggid Mishneh writes that this is obvious and may be inferred from there. The concern is that perhaps the venture suffered a loss, in which case the investor should have borne half the loss, but the heir does not know the terms between them and collects the entire amount stated in the document without bearing any loss. That is interest.
