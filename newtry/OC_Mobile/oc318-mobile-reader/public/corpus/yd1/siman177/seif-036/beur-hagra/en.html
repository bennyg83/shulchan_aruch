An individual, etc. See Tosafot, 105a, s.v. these, etc.
(Likkut) And the owner of the money, etc. This follows from the Mishnah in the final chapter of Bava Batra, 167b: one may not write a sharecropping contract, etc. Although with a loan only the borrower's consent is required, as stated there, here both parties' consent is needed because the worker cannot retract. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 320 seif 2 in the gloss. The Tosefta of Moed Katan states that one may not write a sharecropping or contracting agreement during Chol HaMoed; Rabbi Yehudah permits it lest another person precede him. See Orach Chaim, siman 545. All the more so, beginning the work itself requires consent even without a written document. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 13 seif 2 in the gloss. (End.)
