For he is liable for half the loss. Meaning that by law he is liable for half the loss because of his loan portion, except that one sixth of the loss in the deposit half is deducted for him as payment for his trouble in dealing with the deposit half. Therefore one third of the loss remains upon him. It appears from here that in one enterprise the recipient has two advantages. But the Tur did not write so; rather, he should have an advantage either in profit or in loss. The Raavad also disagrees with the Rambam on this.
