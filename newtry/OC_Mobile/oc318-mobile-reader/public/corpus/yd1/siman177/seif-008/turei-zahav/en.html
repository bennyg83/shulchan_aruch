For he is liable for half the loss. Meaning, by strict law he is liable for half the loss because of the loan portion. However, one-sixth of the total loss is deducted from the deposit half as compensation for the effort he expends on the deposit portion. He therefore remains liable for one-third of the total loss. It appears from here that in one iska the recipient receives two advantages. The Tur, however, did not write so, but rather that he receives an advantage either in the profit or in the loss. The Ra'avad also disputes the Rambam on this point.
