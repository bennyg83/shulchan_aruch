The lender, etc. This is like one who lent money to another and then lived in his courtyard. Even where the courtyard is not normally rented and the lender would not otherwise rent one, it is forbidden, as stated above in siman 166 seif 3 in the gloss.
Unless he gives, etc. But Rashba forbids, for it is circumvention of ribbis, he knows he will not withhold; and as written seif 2 letter bet "there are matters," etc.; and all the more so in such a case; and therefore the Rav omitted it in the gloss there.
