And all the liability must be, etc. Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 6 of Hilchos Sheluchin, writes in the name of the Sefer HaMitzvos that although one gives an iska to his fellow on condition that the dealer receive three quarters of the profit and the owner of the money one quarter, it nevertheless remains half loan and half deposit. The dealer's additional share of the profit is compensation for his labor.
