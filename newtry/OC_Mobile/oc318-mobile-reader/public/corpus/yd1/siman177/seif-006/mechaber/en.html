He gave money as an iska for half profit, and fixed a known matter [as wages] — even if all the responsibility is on the giver — it is forbidden. {Rama: And some say that if the recipient accepts upon himself all the responsibility, it is permitted (Beit Yosef in the name of Maharik, shoresh 119, and so is implied in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 302), regarding any interest that is by rabbinic law — for example, one who gives in an iska, or other rabbinic interest. But regarding fixed interest, it is not effective even if the lender accepts upon himself all the responsibility. But if he does not accept upon himself all the responsibility, only part of it, even regarding rabbinic [interest] it is forbidden; and one may be lenient in rabbinic interest like this reasoning.}
