Idle. Compensation is assessed according to what an otherwise idle person would accept to manage the investment, not according to the recipient's full lost earnings. The authorities offer several methods of assessment, and Taz explains that the applicable method depends upon whether the recipient ordinarily has a trade.

One third. If profits are divided equally and the recipient receives an additional third as compensation for his labor, the arrangement is permitted where he has another business. If he has no other business, he ordinarily must receive two-thirds of the profit while bearing only one-third of the loss. Bach requires the recipient's genuine consent to any fixed labor payment.
