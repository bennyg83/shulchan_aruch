A third. Shach wrote: this is the Rambam's opinion; but most poskim disagree and hold the recipient takes two parts of profit and accepts half the loss; and likewise is the main view in the Talmud. However, all this is in ordinary places; but where there is a known custom we follow the custom where there is no prohibition in the matter. Taz wrote: and it seems to me that if the recipient already gave the giver his principal and profit (and did not claim labor wages at all, he has no claim against him for this is asmachta and we do not extract in court; but all this is when he has not yet given him his principal — he may withhold himself from the principal for his labor wages; however, if he already gave him the principal and profit and did not claim labor wages) and afterward claimed labor wages from him, and the recipient remains obligated to him from another side for some debt and wishes to deduct his labor wages — it seems he cannot deduct, since at the time of paying the principal he requested nothing and it is complete waiver — end of his words. (And see in Sefer Perach Mateh Aharon siman 21 and siman 116.)
