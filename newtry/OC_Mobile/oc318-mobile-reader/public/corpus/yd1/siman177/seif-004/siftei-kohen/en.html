And if they decreased, the agent loses a third, etc. This is the Rambam's opinion; but most poskim disagree and hold the recipient takes two parts of profit and accepts half the loss; and Beit Yosef brings them. And it is astonishing on the Rav (and the Ettinger) that he did not bring them; and also on the Mechaber one should wonder, for he holds as a rule that wherever the Rif and Rosh agree with one opinion, we rule accordingly; and in Beit Yosef it is written that the Rif and Rosh hold they divide the loss; and all the more so he wrote that likewise is the opinion of the Raavad, Ramban, Rashba, Ran, Nimukei Yosef, and Semag; and so it seems as the main view in the Talmud. However, all this is in ordinary places; but where there is a known custom we follow the custom where there is no prohibition in the matter, as written in Hagahot in the name of the Raavad that Beit Yosef brings.
