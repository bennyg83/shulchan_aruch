(Taz, seif katan 17) I do not know, etc. See the Shach above, siman 160, seif katan 29, where it is explained that there is no difficulty. The words of the Levush here can likewise be understood in this manner. He means that we advance any plausible claim for an orphan, namely that the money was taken permissibly. We would advance the same claim for any other person. This is why the Levush later writes that if one says to another, “You owe me one hundred in fixed ribbit,” and the other says, “This never occurred,” he must swear, etc.; see there.
