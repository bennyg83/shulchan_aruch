(Taz, seif katan 17) I do not know. See the Shach above, siman 160, seif katan 29, where he explains that there is no difficulty. The words of the Ateret Zekenim here may likewise be understood in that manner. His intent is that we advance a possible defense for an orphan, namely, that the money was taken permissibly. We would advance the same defense for another person as well. This is why the Ateret Zekenim subsequently writes: “If one says to another, ‘You have one hundred zuz of fixed ribbis belonging to me,’ and the other replies, ‘This never occurred,’ we administer an oath to him,” and so forth.
