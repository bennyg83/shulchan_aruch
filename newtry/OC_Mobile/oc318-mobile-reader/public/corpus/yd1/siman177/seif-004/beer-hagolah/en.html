The wording of the Rambam in chapter 6 of Hilchot Sheluchin VeShutafin, halachah 2, from the baraita there, as Abaye explains it.
Rashi explains that if he was a bellows-worker or carpenter, one estimates how much a person would accept to desist from such heavy labor and engage in lighter work. This is likewise implied by the Rif. Tosafot explains "an idle worker" as one who is entirely idle and has no other work, estimating how much he would accept to engage in this venture. The Rosh writes likewise. He further writes that here the Sages were lenient regarding rabbinic interest, and a small payment does not appear as interest. The Tur likewise writes this.
