The language of the Rambam in chapter 6 of Laws of Agents and Partners, law 2, from the baraita there and as Abaye explains: (°) Rashi explains that if he was a bellows-worker or carpenter, how much would a person want to take to sit idle from such heavy work and do light work; and likewise it appears from the Rif's words. And Tosafot explained as an idle worker — meaning as one who sits completely idle and has no work at all, how much he would want to take to engage in this business; and likewise the Rosh there. And he further wrote that here the Sages were lenient regarding dust of ribbis, and in a small matter that he gives him it does not appear as ribbis; and likewise the Tur.
