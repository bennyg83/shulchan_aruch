It is ribbit. Even though half the liability is on the lender — it is forbidden because of ribbit ketzutzah if he spent the money in his expenses, even though they came into his hand initially in a permitted manner — Maharik shoresh 119; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it.
If half etc. For we say to him: "if it seems to you that half the profit reached the amount you gave" — and we do not administer an oath for a doubt, but rather say to him "remember, for we rely on your trustworthiness" — Gamirei there; and written in Bedek HaBayit: the reason is obvious, since the claimant does not press a certain claim; however if he wishes to pronounce a general ban he is entitled — end; and it seems to me that even if the claimant presses a certain claim — the lender is exempt without an oath if he says "I took in a permitted manner," as above siman 169 s.k. 73.
