One who gives, etc., if he lent, etc. As above in seif 6, and in Tosefta, chapter 4: one who gives money to his fellow to purchase produce with it for half the profit, and the recipient says to him, 'Here is one maneh, for I cannot attend to every small detail,' it is forbidden.
But if, etc. For this stipulation is void, since it is prohibited, and the original stipulation remains in force. This is what is meant by, 'if,' etc.
