(Shach, seif katan 23) There is an error there, and the text should read as follows: “The Hagahot Mordechai there wrote, and the Bedek HaBayit explained that the reason is straightforward, because he does not assert a definite claim against him. Nevertheless, if he wishes to impose a general cherem, he may do so.” This refers to a case in which the lender asserts a claim against the borrower. If, however, the borrower asserts against the lender a claim concerning money the lender has already taken, it appears to me that even if the borrower makes a definite claim, the lender is exempt without an oath when he says, “I took it permissibly,” as explained above, siman 169, seif katan 73. This is the correct text.
