Not clothing, etc. For everything is in the category of produce except clothing and vessels — the Rosh; and likewise the acharonim; and see above siman 217 seif kaf bet seif kaf lamed, and siman 232 seif kaf chet for the reason. But the Rambam chapter 7 of Shelichin law 7 wrote: he buys whatever type he wishes and does not buy clothing or wood, and likewise anything similar — end of his words; it implies he holds the reason is that clothing and wood are not good merchandise; and accordingly, where it is good merchandise he may buy. But Kesef Mishneh there wrote on the Rambam's words: and it seems to me the reason is specifically produce that he mentioned he may buy, and not something not called produce such as clothing and wood — end of his words. And according to his words it must be that what the Rambam wrote "and likewise anything similar" means similar to this that is not in the category of produce, or every similar thing not in the category of the language he told him. And now it is resolved what he based the reason on "and it seems to me," for behold it is explicit in the Rosh; rather he comes to say it must be so even according to the Rambam — this reason.
