If one gives his fellow merchandise as an iska. Beit Yosef wrote in this siman in name of Rosh's responsum klal 88: if the beginning of the iska was in violation — he sold him wheat for less than the market price in order that he give him money and also did not give him his wages as an idle worker — he shall not take any profit. Beit Yosef explained: if he had given him unspecified he would take labor wages in one of the ways mentioned above in this siman seifim 3–4; but this one who acted in violation — if he had given him his wages as an idle worker it would have mitigated somewhat of the violation and he would take half the profit at the rate at which they were sold in the market when he gave the money as they were sold; and since they stipulated only in violation he shall take no profit at all — end of his words.
