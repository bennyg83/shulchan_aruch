One who says to his fellow, "If I do not pay you by such-and-such time, I owe you from now and for the delay period so many dinars" (more than he borrowed) — forbidden due to interest evasion: {Rama: and some permit if he gives him money and receives produce (Rivash siman 335); and one may be lenient on this; and see siman 163 if he transgressed and did interest evasion whether we extract from him.}
