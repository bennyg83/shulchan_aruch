One who gives etc. He wrote in Bedek HaBayit in the name of Ritva: it is reasonable that if he places the document in the hand of a third party — there is then nothing further to be concerned about and it is permitted.
He may not combine the profit etc. Even if he writes it in iska language — it is forbidden — Derishab; and Derishab further questioned in seif 33 that it is forbidden in any case because he cut a fixed amount, as above seif 6 — see there for his resolution; and it seems to me that is different, for there he cut a fixed amount even if there will be no profit — unlike here; and I later found this in the final treatise.
And the lender writes them. Even the principal alone; and Bach wrote: even though the profit cannot be extracted from him against his will — nevertheless if the recipient wishes to fulfill the condition he agreed to verbally, he may accept from him, since he gave to him as an iska; and if he stipulated with him verbally before witnesses — we consider it as if the iska is explicit in the document and we extract from him against his will — end of his words; and see CM in Tur siman 18 and the commentators there.
