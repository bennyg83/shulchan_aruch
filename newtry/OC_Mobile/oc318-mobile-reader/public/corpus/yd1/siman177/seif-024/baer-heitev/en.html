Shach wrote: and if not, it is forbidden because he fixed an amount, as above seif 6; for it is different there where he fixed on a known matter even if there will be no profit; not so here. And it deals here even if he wrote in the language of iska; also forbidden. However Ritva wrote it is reasonable that if he leaves the document in the hand of a third party, there is no longer concern for anything and it is permitted.
Loan. Even the principal alone. Bach wrote: even though one cannot collect the profit from him by force, nevertheless if the recipient wishes to fulfill the condition he stipulated with him orally he may accept from him since he gave it to him as an iska; and if he stipulated with him orally before witnesses we consider it as if he specified the iska in the document and we extract from him by force. And see Tur Choshen Mishpat siman 18.
