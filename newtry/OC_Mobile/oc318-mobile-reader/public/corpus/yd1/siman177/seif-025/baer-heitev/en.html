We judge. The vav of "and in loss" is a dividing vav as in "or in loss." Hagahot Perishah wrote: and if not, he gives him his wages as an idle worker, as above seif 2 letter dalet — for it is different here where it is written in the document it appears more as ribbis. Beit Yosef wrote: with an ordinary person, if he showed an iska and seized we do not extract from him, for it is asmakhta; but with a prominent person, since it is clear to us he did not stipulate in prohibition, we extract from him.
