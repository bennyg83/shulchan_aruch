A great man, etc. — Beit Yosef and Beit HaTov wrote: regarding another person, if he preceded and showed the issur and seized — we do not remove from him, for it is a mistaken condition; but regarding a great man, since it is obvious they did not stipulate forbidden matter — we remove from him; and Beit Yosef brings Darkei Moshe.
They rule etc. The vav in "and in loss" is a disjunctive vav, like "or in loss."
He receives nothing etc. And one cannot say he should give him payment as an idle laborer (as above seif 2 and 4), for here it is different since he wrote it in a document — it appears more as ribbit — Hagahot Perishah; and likewise proved in Beit Yosef above beginning of siman 176 regarding what he wrote: it can be said that the Rosh wrote so to say that if it were not written in the document Rava should have said he give payment for his trouble as an idle laborer; and our master’s words concern one who does not wish to give as an idle laborer — see there.
