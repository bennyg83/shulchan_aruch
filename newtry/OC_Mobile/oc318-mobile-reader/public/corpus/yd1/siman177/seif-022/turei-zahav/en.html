One who sells merchandise to his fellow, etc. In siman 166 I wrote this law in the name of the Levush, and explained that if he initially used language of a loan it is forbidden. This is also implied here, where the ruling begins with the language, 'One who sells,' etc. For this reason it is permitted here even l'chatchila. It is not comparable to seif 14, where it is forbidden as an evasion of ribbis, because there the transaction is a loan, whereas here it is a sale.
