An agent who died and there are witnesses to movable property from this business — the money owner takes without oath; creditor and wife take nothing from principal nor from the giver's profit share: {Rama: and the giver can take the entire issur from them even though the time with the recipient has not finished (Rambam chapter 5 of Laws of Agents and most poskim) (and see Choshen Mishpat siman 176).}
