He takes them, etc. — and Mordechai wrote even minor orphans too he takes; and I wonder how one can take from minor orphans — for we do not accept testimony on them as in end of first chapter (and Choshen Mishpat siman 110) — end Beit Yosef. And I wonder at his words, for it is possible when testimony was accepted in their father's lifetime — in such case we collect from them as in Choshen Mishpat siman 108 — end Darkei Moshe; and so Bach wrote see there.
Siman 176, seif 19.
