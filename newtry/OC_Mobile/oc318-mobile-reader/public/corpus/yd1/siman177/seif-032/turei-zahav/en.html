And from there onward he takes half the appreciation, etc. Thus it states in the Gemara: from here onward he takes half his own and half of half of his fellow's. Rashi: the recipient shall take a full half for his share since half is his; and in the second half which is his fellow's he takes half of the appreciation as he takes with its mother half wages — and there is no labor wages; and even though half the liability of half his fellow's share is upon him, since he must toil in it because of his half which is full principal and not a loan — for he toils because it is his — therefore Rav Menasya bar Gada took half his own and half of half of his fellow's; he came before Abaye — he said to him: who divided for you; and further: a place where they practiced to raise — and we learned: a place where they practiced to raise he shall raise it. Beit Yosef wrote there are two explanations of the Rashba's students: in name of Rashi they explained that all the time he did not divide properly it is as if he did not divide at all and he takes nothing from his fellow's appreciation share — it results Abaye said two things to Rav Menasya: one that he not take appreciation of his fellow's share because division was not done properly; and second that perhaps they practiced to raise it. In name of Tosafot they explained that even if they made no division at all he takes half appreciation in his fellow's share after the time he is not obligated to tend them — it results Abaye said only one reason that Rav Menasya not take in his fellow's share, namely that they practiced to raise it; but what he said "who divided for you" is not relevant to this but to nullify the division — he said so that his division not stand that the recipient take from Rav Menasya what he divided for him not according to law. And from the Rambam's words chapter 8 of Laws of Partnership it implies like Rashi — for behold he wrote: if he wishes to tend them more beyond this time he assesses them before three and whatever they profit afterward the dealer takes three parts and his fellow a quarter; and if they did not stipulate so — behold they divide offspring equally — end of his words. Behold he did not find he takes in his fellow's share except after assessment before the three; and Beit Yosef wrote likewise is Tur's opinion — for behold he wrote "and if he did not inform him and divided — it is nothing and they are still partners as initially and each takes half" — perforce. And according to this what Tur and Shulchan Arukh wrote initially "and from there onward he takes half the appreciation," etc. — meaning after he informed him; and the end of his words is explanation on the beginning of his words. And in Perishah he challenged him to explain Tur's words so; and he explained he holds Tur like the explanation Perishah mentioned; and what Tur wrote that if he divided and did not inform him he does not take anything of his fellow's share — meaning since he divided and revealed his intent that he does not wish to be his guardian without division, and since the division is void he has nothing in his fellow's share; not so without division and unspecified business that he has according to Tosafot's method. And these matters are slippery — for when he made division it stands and he informed him — nevertheless he was guardian also on his fellow's share; and when he made division and did not inform him and the division is void he shall not be guardian on his fellow's share; and further behold that one who made division not according to law — nevertheless according to his opinion the division stood as we find with Rav Menasya who did not know his division was nothing until Abaye revealed to him "who divided for you"; and nevertheless he was taking in his fellow's share if not because they practiced to raise it according to Tosafot's explanation; and if so it is impossible to say his reason is he revealed his intent that he does not wish to be guardian except on his share and informed according to his opinion — and why do we not rule so in truth for every divider who did not inform; rather like Beit Yosef's explanation of Tur's words that we mentioned — thus is primary. And I wonder more on Mahari Weil who wrote that Beit Yosef too holds like the explanation Perishah mentioned and wondered on him in what he wrote that Beit Yosef did not explain so and did not investigate Bach at all in this — for behold it is written explicitly that Tur and Rambam hold as one method in this; and in Rambam it is explained explicitly that he takes nothing at all in his fellow's share except after assessment before three; and if there were waiver in an unspecified case; and likewise Tur's opinion except Tur holds whoever informed him — it is as if before three; and this is like Rashi's explanation; only simple that Beit Yosef explained as we mentioned; and the matters are clear according to Beit Yosef; and likewise one should explain Shulchan Arukh's words here that are a copy of Tur's language — and the recipient has no right in the giver's share until he first makes division properly before three or informs the giver; and this is clear.
