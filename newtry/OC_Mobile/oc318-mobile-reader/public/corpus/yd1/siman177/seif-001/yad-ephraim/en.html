Further there in siman 33 in Maharam Mintz: they write unspecified and the iska condition orally — forbidden because of rabbinic ribbis and we do not obligate him to pay profit; but if he stipulated before witnesses, or even without witnesses but the meaning of the condition is written on the document "such-and-such principal" — since it writes principal, it implies it was given as an iska and is as if written explicitly, and we obligate him to pay profit as well — see there; and see Taz siman 177 and responsum Shev Yaakov. He wrote in responsum Masat Moshe Yoreh Deah part 2 siman 15 regarding an iska document issued on orphans and it is written in it the iska condition and afterward it is written "and now Shimon agreed by hand to his share of the profit sum sixty-five groszy every year whether it decreased or increased" — and he wrote it is fixed ribbis and we deduct the ribbis he consumed — see there what he wrote to distinguish between what Maharival wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 62; and he also brought from Ravanach siman 15 and Beit Yosef siman 132 and responsum Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 152 and other responsa. And see there what he wrote that even though an oath is written in the document it does not take effect to transgress a Torah prohibition even he himself — and all the more so orphans whom we do not obligate — see there. Further there siman 44: one who combined a condition with ribbis for two months henceforth because he had a prior document that what he would give him would be as an iska — nevertheless this document was made in violation and the borrower shall swear he had no profit in this iska, for thus Maharam enacted in an iska where the recipient is believed in any event regarding profits; unless he claims the borrower admitted to him at the time of writing the document that he already had profit from the prior iska, or he says "I already paid" or "you waived" — the lender swears hesit — see there. See in responsum Beit Efrayim Yoreh Deah siman 41 that it is permitted to give a money changer coin that is not of that country and the money changer obligates himself to give him in Leipzig at the nearby fair to pay in the abundant currency such-and-such sum — because this is not ribbis but he adds collection for trouble, expense, and liability of the road; and likewise in responsum Mayim Rabim siman 38 he wrote to permit for this reason; and he wrote all the more so when paying currency of another country — see there that this is like merchandise; and see in Sefer Bat Einay Teshuvah of the gaon Rav Chaim Rapoport on lending in ritlikh currency to pay from his house gold currency — and the author disagreed with him — namely because the matter is the way of a full loan; and thus I am accustomed to rule in a loan for a time where the lender gives some currency and payment is in another currency considered more valuable at the market rate — to write in the condition the giving of the loan as it is, and to write that if he pays in such-and-such currency he is exempt from the iska because it was done as the way of a loan and payment is in its place; not so with the money changer's negotiation which is the way of sale where the currency is only as fruit and he obligates himself to pay in another place also in currency not of the country — this is only like exchange in merchandise and the way of sale, for they have no known rate and rise and fall and it is permitted for all — see there. And I found in responsum Mahari HaLevi, brother of Taz, that he is stringent in this that the money he says he has in another place must be ready in his hand at the time of making the deal; and even if he has only a little — nevertheless he can acquire that sum for him and tell him explicitly that he discounts for him, etc.; and his reason is that otherwise this is not the way of sale — one cannot acquire what is not in his possession, etc. — see there. And in my humble opinion since it is currency he does not find there — this is like one who sells produce on credit when there is no known rate and he does not stipulate with him for twelve months, etc. — it is permitted; and all the more so here where he discounts for him for expense and liability of the road as written; and it is possible Mahari HaLevi deals when there is a known rate; and what is written there that the exchange rises and falls — meaning over time; and also he deals when he stipulates to pay in the nearby city in a matter where there is no expense and liability of the road — it requires investigation. And there I wrote that there is no permission to give the money owner a condition he has on another that rises more — for even if he writes in the document the borrower's name and he will sell it to the lender according to the law of writing and delivery, or according to the law of the kingdom's courts — nevertheless he does not leave the category of liability and it is forbidden to sell for less unless he writes in it that he delivers the condition and removes himself from liability — see there.
