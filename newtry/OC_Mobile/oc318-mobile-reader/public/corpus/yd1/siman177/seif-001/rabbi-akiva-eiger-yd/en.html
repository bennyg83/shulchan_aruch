(Siman 177, seif 5, in Rama's gloss) He thereby misappropriates the deposit. In my humble opinion, Rashba in the responsum cited by Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat, siman 176, novel ruling 36, and likewise in Rashba's novellae on Bava Kamma, disagrees. He holds that even if one misappropriated part of the business funds, then dealt with the remainder without specifying and earned a profit, the profit is divided between them. See there. This requires further study.
