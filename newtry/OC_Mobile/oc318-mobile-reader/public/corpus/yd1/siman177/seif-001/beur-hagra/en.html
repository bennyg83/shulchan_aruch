And all this not, etc. As written in Yerushalmi: one who carries a bundle from place to place, he said to him "give and I will give you as you give in such-and-such place", if with the giver's liability it is forbidden and the taker permitted; but porters who receive from homeowners set produce for them in the expensive place at the cheap rate, Rabbi Huna said he becomes his agent; they challenged Rabbi Huna: an agent who was compelled is exempt from accidents, not always does he stipulate to be like a borrower. And meaning: not so with one who carries a bundle, since he also does it for his benefit, not so with porters; and there is no need for what is written in the Gemara "it is satisfactory to them that he reveals," etc. (and as written above siman 173 s.k. 34); and there in the mishnah: one does not accept iron flock, etc.; what is iron flock, etc.; Rabbi Yirmiyah asked there: you say a paid guardian is liable for accidents, forbidden; and here you say a paid guardian is liable for accidents, permitted. Meaning: here in iron flock he said since he accepts liability upon it, forbidden; and there with porters you said even though he accepts liability upon it, permitted. And the language of the Yerushalmi is reversed, as is known in many places, and as the Rosh wrote at the end of chapter HaDayanim; and they answered: not always does he stipulate to be like a borrower, therefore there it is permitted since they have no benefit at all, not so here where he takes wages and does it for his benefit, forbidden when he accepts liability upon it. And this is what is written: and even if he accepted, etc.
