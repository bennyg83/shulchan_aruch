One who says to his fellow, "Give me the cut interest you collected from me," and the other says there was never such a thing — they make the defendant take a heset oath (meaning: the Sages induced him to swear to induce confession, for biblically one swears only a guardian's oath and by one witness and when admitting part of the claim (and some disagree (Rabeinu Yerucham in the name of Ram), and see siman 168).
