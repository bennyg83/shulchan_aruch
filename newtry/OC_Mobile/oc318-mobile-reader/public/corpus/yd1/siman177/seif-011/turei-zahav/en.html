However, if he deviated and said, 'I am doing this for myself,' etc. This applies specifically when he says so at the time of the deviation. If he said nothing when he deviated, and afterward claims, 'I deviated for myself,' he is not believed. We retain him in his prior presumption of upright conduct, and say that only now has he become suspect. The Darkei Moshe likewise writes: if he explicitly says when deviating, 'I am doing this for myself,' etc.
