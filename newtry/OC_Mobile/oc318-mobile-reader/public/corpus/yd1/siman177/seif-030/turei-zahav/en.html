Each may prevent the other. The reason is that during the first year there is greater care, because the animal is small and must be brought in and taken out. There is also a contrary rationale, that during the second year there is greater care because it requires much food. Each therefore has a rationale favoring his own position and may prevent the other from terminating the iska until both periods have ended, for each says: I do not wish to give you one-half during one of these periods.
