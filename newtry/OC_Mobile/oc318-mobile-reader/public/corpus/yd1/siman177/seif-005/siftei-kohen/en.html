When it is, etc. The Mordechai in the chapter HaChovel writes that if Reuven lent Shimon money for a fixed period, and after that period the lender told the borrower, 'Let the money remain in your possession as an investment for half the profit,' the managing partner remains liable for accidents just as he was originally liable as a borrower. The Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe cite this. Maharshal likewise writes this in the chapter HaChovel, siman 70. See below, seif katan 38.
The same applies to an unpaid guardian. See CM in the laws of deposit and unpaid guardian.
That all the liability etc. Specifically here since it is rabbinic — it is permitted in such a case; but not in biblical ribbit, as Terumas HaDeshen wrote, which I brought in s.k. 17.
