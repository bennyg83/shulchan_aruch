When he takes wages for the deposit portion in his hand, he is like a paid custodian. It is permitted to give an iska for half profit and stipulate that he will engage only in such-and-such a matter; and if he changed [it], such that all the responsibility is on the recipient, and any condition that he wants — for example, that he stipulates that he will not guard the money except under the ground — and if he changed and lost, all the loss is on the recipient; and if he profited, it is for the middle, even though now he is close to profit and far from loss. It is not considered interest, since if he would not have changed, he would have been close to profit and to loss. {Rama: And it is permitted for the recipient to change initially, and we do not say that by this he is like a thief (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran; and in Hagahos Ashiri, perek Eizehu Neshech; and in Mordechai). However, if he changed and said, “For myself I am doing, and not as an iska,” he is like a thief, and what he did, he did for himself (Rabbeinu Yerucham). Therefore, the giver should stipulate initially that if he sometimes takes a little from the business for his own needs, it will not be called that he “sends a hand” in the deposit by this, for then he is like a thief and all the profit is his, and the money is a loan upon him and it is forbidden to afterward take interest (Beit Yosef in the name of Teshuvas Maimoniyos). And if they practice not to be particular about this, presumably it is as if they stipulated, as I wrote above siman 168; and see below at the end of this siman regarding this law.}
