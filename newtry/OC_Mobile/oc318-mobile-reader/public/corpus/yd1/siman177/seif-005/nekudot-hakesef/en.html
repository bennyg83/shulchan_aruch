(Taz, seif katan 18) The Levush distinguished regarding this. It is clear that the Levush agrees with this view. Regarding a king as well, he writes that if one says, “Here are two hundred zuz so that you will enter the king's profession for three hundred,” the arrangement is forbidden. His point is only to answer why, when one mortgages his house for one thousand gold coins, the arrangement is forbidden. He explains that most people do not ordinarily give such security for a smaller amount. See there.
