If he obligated himself to give the lender such-and-such every week while withholding his money — this is absolute interest: {Rama: even though he wrote thus as a penalty, "if I do not pay you by such time I will give you every week such amount," and even though if he paid on time there would be no interest here at all — nevertheless since he wrote to give him an allowance every week it is absolute interest (most poskim), and such is primary, even though some are lenient and permitted borrowing with interest this way (Mordechai Bava Metzia in the name of R. Yitzchak of Dueren).}
