Each and every week etc. It is not comparable to above seif 14 — where he says “if I do not repay you I am obligated from now for so many dinars” — which is not full ribbit for it does not appear as ribbit to that degree — Beit Yosef; and in the responsum of Maharal ibn Chaviv siman 103 he elaborated and concluded it is biblical ribbit — see there; and in responsum of Mabit vol. 2 siman 51 p. 26c he disagreed, that it is only rabbinic ribbit — see there at length; and from the words of the Mechaber and the Rabbi it appears it is biblical ribbit; and so appears also from responsa of Rabbi Aharon Sasson end of siman 162. However where he did not write for him to give a fixed amount each week — even if he does not pay him until later — such as he wrote in the document that if he does not pay him at the end of Tishrei 60 dinars he will pay him 66 at the end of Cheshvan — there is only a rabbinic prohibition; and likewise Mabit responsum vol. 2 siman 298 p. 149b — see there. A.A. who does not pay for him, but after he has written in a bill that if he is not disturbed by the end of the day, he will pay him an end to the law, but rather a ban on his or her hand, and as such, he will be given to him
