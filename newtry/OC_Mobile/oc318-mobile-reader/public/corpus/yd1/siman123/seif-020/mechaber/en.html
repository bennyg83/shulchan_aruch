A gat that is sealed and full, such that hamshachah does not apply to it, and wine and grape pits descended from it together into a basket in front of the gat, and the wine is strained there: what is in the gat has the status of grapes (not wine), and only what is in the basket has the status of wine. And if a non-Jew touched the basket, it becomes forbidden; and if he returned it to the gat, what is in the gat becomes forbidden because of the mixture of the wine that was in the basket that became mixed into it. And if the grape pits and the skins in the gat have 60 against the wine that fell into it from the basket, it is permitted.
