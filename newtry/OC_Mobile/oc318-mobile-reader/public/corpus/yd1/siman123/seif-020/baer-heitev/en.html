In the press. Since grapes and wine were drawn together into the basket, and only from there did the wine flow alone into the pit, what remained in the press is not thereby called wine. The Rosh compares it to removing grapes and wine together from the press to another place, from which the wine then separates.
Forbidden. The wine in the press is not forbidden through the principle of a connecting stream when none was returned from the basket to the press. See the end of siman 126 and Tosafot, Avodah Zarah 56b.
In the press. Shach writes that everything in the press, wine, grape seeds, and skins, combines toward sixty to nullify wine forbidden through non-Jewish touch, because such wine is nullified in sixty like ordinary non-Jewish wine. Even without seeds and skins, permitted wine combines. The Levush disagreed because wine is the same species, but this is incorrect according to our ruling that the same species is treated as absent and the different species nullifies it. Taz rules likewise.
