That which is in the winepress — since grapes and wine were drawn together into the basket and from there wine alone was drawn to the pit [for the matter is similar to one who takes grapes and wine together from the winepress and puts them in another place, and from there wine was drawn from the grapes — it is clear that what is in the winepress is not called wine just because wine was drawn from that place]. So wrote the Rosh.
It is forbidden — and as to why what is in the winepress is not forbidden even if he did not return from the basket to the winepress through a stream, see the end of siman 136 (and see Tosafot on Avodah Zarah 56b).
That which is in the winepress — Siftei Kohen wrote: all that is in the winepress, both wine and grape-skins and seeds, all combine to nullify the wine that was forbidden by the touch of a non-Jew, which is also nullified in sixty like stam yeinam. And even if there are no grape-skins and seeds, wine is nullified in wine; and as to why the Mechaber did not write so here, one may say he relied on the main law in siman 154. And in the Levush he wrote that wine does not combine since it is the same species — but that is incorrect for us (for we say: remove its species as if it were not there, and what is not its species predominates and nullifies it). See siman 126. End of his words; and so ruled Turei Zahav, see there.
