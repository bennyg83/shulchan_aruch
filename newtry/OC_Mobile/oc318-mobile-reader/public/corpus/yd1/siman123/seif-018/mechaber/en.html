If he filled from the gat a cup of wine, and intended to remove it from the grape pits and the skins (meaning: to remove it, in the sense of “remove your shoe”), it is hamshachah. And likewise if he put a basket into the gat, or into a trodden tub, it is called hamshachah (Sefer HaTerumah and Semag), since clear wine enters into the basket and becomes separated from the grape pits and the skins. But if he took the grape pits together with the wine, it is not hamshachah. And even if a non-Jew took them together, it is permitted to return what is permitted.
