From when is wine called “wine” to become forbidden by the touch of a non-Jew? From when it began hamshachah — that is, from when it flowed on the gat by itself, for the gat is a slope. And if he cleared out the grape pits and the skins and the wine alone flowed from the upper side to the lower side, and the wine alone remained standing — that is called hamshachah, and everything in the gat becomes forbidden, even if he touched only the grape pits and the skins, if there is on them “tofeach al menat l'hatfiach”. But so long as the wine was not separated (until the bottom of the gat — Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot, the Rosh, and Mordechai) from the grape pits and the skins, it is not hamshachah.
