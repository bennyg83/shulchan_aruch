(Turei Zahav seif kaf 12) Rather there is still reason to challenge: behold there is a prohibition for the winepress. Plainly one may say: since it is not called wine regarding touch, a stream is not a connection — as Tosafot wrote regarding "a stream is fit for libation." And even though according to what the yeshiva students erred, that Rav Huna refers to the first mishnah, that also the dripping is not wine, and nevertheless we say there "learn from this that a stream is a connection" — namely that in truth he said it to wonder: learn from this that a stream is a connection; and even in such a case it is not wine. But in truth one may say that in such a case there is no stream; and perhaps Shach (seif kaf 40) intended this.
