Yayin nesech — see responsum Tzemach Tzedek siman 75, who wrote that the same applies to spirits made from grape-skins and seeds of non-Jews, that they are forbidden, contrary to what the masses say. And there it is explained that one may not trust ordinary people who report in the name of others regarding halachah or custom, see there. [And see responsum Mishkenot Yaakov siman 36.]
