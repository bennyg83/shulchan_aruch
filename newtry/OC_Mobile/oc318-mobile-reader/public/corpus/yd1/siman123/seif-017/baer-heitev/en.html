Cleared a space. Taz explains that whenever there is some empty space in the winepress, we are concerned that during treading some grapes may shift and leave an open area into which clear wine flows down the slope, creating drawing within the press. Therefore the press must be sealed and full while the non-Jew treads, so that the grapes have no room to move and clear wine cannot collect. Whenever this siman requires a press to be sealed and full, it means to prevent drawing within the press itself. Shach adds that if wine was drawn outside the press into the pit, this certainly constitutes drawing, and if the non-Jew then touches it, even the wine remaining in the press is forbidden.
Enough to moisten. Otherwise, only the wine he actually touched is forbidden. See siman 126:5 and siman 129:13.
Bottom. This excludes a case where he cleared space only at the top; there is no drawing when grapes remain beneath the wine. Shach.
