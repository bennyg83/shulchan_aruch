A corner. Taz explains: whenever there is some empty space in the winepress we are concerned that at treading-time some grapes may shift so there is open space between them; and since it is a slope, clear wine may flow there into the open space—and this is drawing within the press. Therefore we require the press be sealed and full at gentile treading-time so no grapes can shift and no clear wine flow in their place, above where it is heaped and full or below where there is no exit. So too everywhere they said "sealed and full"—they mean no drawing within the press itself. And Shach wrote: all the more if it drew outside the press into a pit—that is drawing; and if he then touched, even what is in the press is forbidden.
Pushing. Hashem was not arrested, but what he had touched and succinctly concluded:
The bottom — excluding if he did not clear it only from above, there is no drawing since there are grapes beneath the wine. Siftei Kohen.
