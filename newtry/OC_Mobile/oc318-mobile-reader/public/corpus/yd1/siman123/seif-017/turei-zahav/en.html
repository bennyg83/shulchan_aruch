It is a slope, and if he turned aside, etc. It is one matter, and so in the Rosh; and the explanation of the matter is thus: whenever there is a little empty space in the winepress we are concerned that at the time of treading grapes will move somewhat in a manner that there will be empty space in between; and since it is a slope, clear wine will flow there in the empty place, and this is drawing inside the winepress; therefore we require that the winepress be closed and full at the time of the non-Jew's treading, so that there will be no place for grapes to move and for clear wine to flow in their place, neither above, which is heaped and full, nor below is there place to go out; and so in every place in this siman where they said closed and full, they intended that there not be drawing inside the winepress itself, as above, this explanation.
