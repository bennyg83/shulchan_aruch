To the bottom of the winepress. — To exclude: if he only cleared it from the top, there is no drawing here, since there are grapes beneath the wine.
Namely from when it flows over the winepress, etc. — And all the more if it flowed outside the winepress into a pit, that is drawing; and if he then touches it, even what is in the winepress is forbidden — so in the Gemara and poskim.
