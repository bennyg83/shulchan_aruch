It does not have the status of yayin nesech. Beit Yosef wrote that it appears from the words of the poskim that even for drinking it is permitted; and he was challenged from what Rashi wrote "permitted in benefit" regarding aluntit — it appears that for drinking it is forbidden; and he strained to reconcile Rashi's explanation also to agree to permission of drinking; and Chokhmat Shlomo challenged him in Derishah from what Rashi wrote in chapter Elu Metziot end of daf 29: if a Jew cooked it, it is permitted in benefit — and there the explanation of Beit Yosef does not apply. And Chokhmat Shlomo wrote: there we are concerned lest the non-Jew mixed wine into it since it is in his house; and in truth Be'er Heitev properly wrote that if because of concern for mixing wine it would be forbidden also in benefit; and the explicit mishnah there is: morayis is forbidden in benefit, and Rashi explained because of a mixture of wine; and regarding vinegar there in the mishnah Rashi also wrote: if vinegar of a Jew that a non-Jew took, it is not forbidden in benefit — it implies for drinking it is forbidden. Rather plainly for Rashi everything that comes because of wine, even though afterward the taste of wine went away because it became vinegar or some mixtures, does not help for drinking if a non-Jew touched it; but we establish like most poskim that it is permitted even for drinking.
