And wine that congealed, etc. And it is written in Hagahot Ashiri from Or Zarua chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah that wine that congealed has no status of the touch of non-Jews, etc.; and likewise if a non-Jew touched it after it dissolved, there is status of the touch of non-Jews — end of his words; and Darkhei Moshe brings it. And it appears to me that according to what we establish, that the touch of non-Jews forbids in wine that soured because we are not expert when it is called vinegar — as below seif kaf 13 — so too here in wine that congealed, for it is impossible to be expert, and he forbids in it the touch of a non-Jew; and so it appears from the Rosh's words, who wrote regarding vinegar that Rabbeinu Tam was angry at R' Meshullam, who permitted the touch of a non-Jew in vinegar because it is impossible to be expert in the nature of vinegar — some call it vinegar and some drink it and make kiddush on it; and regarding wine that congealed the amora'im disagree in perek HaMokher Peirot when it is called vinegar; therefore if you come to permit the touch of a non-Jew in vinegar, a ruin emerges from it — end of his words. And also Rashi explained in chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah (daf 30 end of side 1) on "wine that congealed" — it soured; and so Rashbam explained in perek HaMokher Peirot (Bava Batra daf 95 end of side 2); and so it is proven in the Gemara there. And it appears therefore the rabbi omitted these words of Hagahot Ashiri that Darkhei Moshe brought; and it further appears there is a copying error in the hagahah and in Darkhei Moshe — " שנקרם " should read " שנקרש " with a shin; and so it appears from what he said afterward "after it dissolved," etc.; and so Bach copied in seif 13 the wording of this hagahah "wine that congealed."
