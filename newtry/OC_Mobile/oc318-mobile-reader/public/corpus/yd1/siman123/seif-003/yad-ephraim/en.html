That a non-Jew touched it — it is permitted. See Baer Heitev; and in Tashbetz part 1 siman 69: wine into which a small amount of cooked wine was mixed, about a third or a quarter, and a non-Jew touched it — whenever the cooked wine changed the taste of the wine mixed into it, it is permitted; and see Bei Chai siman 170 and 171 where a certain sage raised an objection.
