Floating on top. He wrote in responsum Leket Yosher part 1 siman 10, and these are his words: incidentally it is fit to be uncertain regarding those who put wine in a flask and put a little oil at the mouth of the flask so it will not spoil — if a non-Jew touched the oil, what is the law of the wine? And my master my father wrote that perhaps one can compare it to mishnah 4 chapter 2 of Tevul Yom: a tevul yom touched oil — he did not invalidate the wine — end of his words. And see in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 62, who rejected his words, for it is not similar to impurity and it is no worse than touch through something else; but where it actually became mixed in, there is no status of libation; and likewise where the non-Jew did not touch it except through a stream, there is reason to side with permitting — see there further, where he challenged what Leket Yosher wrote further in that responsum, that one should be stringent regarding resin wine of a Jew made from wine that a non-Jew touched afterward — for there is no concern of prohibition here, both because cooked wine has no status of libation, and also the taste changed — see there.
