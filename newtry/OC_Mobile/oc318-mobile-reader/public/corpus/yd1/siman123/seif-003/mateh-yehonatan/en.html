(There) And if there are in the grape-skins and grape-pulp in the winepress sixty like a mustard-seed's amount of wine that fell into it from the basket — it is permitted. Shach seif kaf 41: what he challenges in Rama below siman 134 seif 2 — see there. And there is an answer: behold Tosafot challenge on daf 56b, "But if," etc. — "But is it not presumed that there is in the winepress more than sixty droplets of wine from the press-boards?" And we establish like Rava against Abaye, who holds below that new wine in grapes gives taste; and one should also say that since in the winepress there is also wine with the grapes, it is min b'mino — still there is a difficulty: let us say "remove its type," etc. — see there. And one may say that he holds like Abaye, etc., and he also does not hold "remove," etc. And it appears that Tosafot's words need explanation, for according to Abaye there is no "not its type" here at all; and why did they need to say that Abaye does not hold "remove"? And some want to emend in Tosafot that it should read "and further that he does not hold remove" — this is another answer; but this is strained. And it must be that Tosafot hold that even according to Abaye grapes and new wine are min b'mino; nevertheless there are also grape-skins and grape-pulp there, which are certainly "not its type" against the wine; if so, there is still a difficulty why we do not say "remove." Therefore Tosafot needed to say that he also does not hold "remove," and the intent is: since the grape-skins and pulp float on the wine, when wine falls into them it fell first into the wine without any mixture of grape-skins and pulp; in such a case we do not say "remove," but only when the type is mixed with not-its-type immediately when it falls; but where its type stands upon it without mixture, not-its-type is not present at all — we do not say "remove," but say immediately when it fell there is chad d'raga and "not its type" no longer helps to nullify. And this is what Tosafot wrote, that he does not have "remove" — namely in such a case one should not say "remove," since the wine fell first. And now one should not say that Rama emended anything, because in truth sixty in grape-skins without wine are needed; since wine floats above without grape-skins, it is forbidden first. And if so, there is no reason to emend at all to "combines" also the wine to the sixty — for we say chad d'raga and the wine in the winepress was forbidden first. And it is possible to say that Rama holds that sixty in grape-skins are needed even against the wine inside the winepress, and we say chad d'raga; and the author who wrote anonymously that sixty are needed against the wine that fell — namely he follows his view that we say chad d'raga only in b'chol, and Rama relied on what he wrote above that we say chad d'raga also in other prohibitions.
