The stated reason, lest he touch it, means that we decree before the wine begins to flow on account of the case after it begins to flow, when it is fully wine and becomes forbidden through his touch. Likewise, the objection to Rama's wording, 'or in a vat,' is no difficulty, for the vat mentioned there is analogous to a winepress. This is clear.
