(There seif kaf 14) And it deals with a winepress that is not closed, etc. This is what he said: from the anonymous wording it implies that it deals in every case; and what he said, the reason "lest he touch," etc., is because we decree before drawing lest [one come to act] after drawing, when it is complete wine and forbidden when touched. And likewise what he said — nevertheless there is a slight inconsistency in Rama's words, who wrote "or in a vat," etc. Investigate, for "vat" that he mentioned is similar to "winepress."
