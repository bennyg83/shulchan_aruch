If a non-Jew touched it, it is permitted. See Baer Heitev and responsa Tashbetz, part 1, siman 69. If a small quantity of cooked wine, approximately one-third or one-quarter, was mixed into ordinary wine and a non-Jew touched it, then whenever the cooked wine changed the taste of the wine into which it was mixed, it is permitted. See also Ba'ei Chayei, simanim 170 and 171, where one sage objected strongly to this ruling.
