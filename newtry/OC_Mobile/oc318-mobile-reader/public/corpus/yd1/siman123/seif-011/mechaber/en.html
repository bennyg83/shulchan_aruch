Our lees that one made temed from, and he found only according to their measure: the first time is forbidden if a non-Jew touched them, and the second time is permitted. But the lees of non-Jews are always forbidden, even if he found only according to their measure, even if he made temed from them many times. (Raisin wine — meaning one put water on dried grapes — it is like wine and becomes yayin nesech (Arukh at the end of general rule 22, and a responsum of R. Levi Bach, siman 41).)
