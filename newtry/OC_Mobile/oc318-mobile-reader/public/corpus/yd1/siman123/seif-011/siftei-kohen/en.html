And the second time is permitted. — But according to the view of "some say" in seif 10: it is always forbidden as long as it is of good quality for drinking; and so ruled Peri HaDash and Bach — that according to the Raavad there is no distinction between temed of grape-pulp and temed of grape-skins; and the reason the author closed the matter here is because it follows automatically, since the reason of "some say" is to forbid anything of good quality for drinking because it can be confused with actual wine — if so it is obvious there is no distinction between grape-pulp and grape-skins, for whenever it is of good quality for drinking it can be confused with actual wine.
That one placed water on grapes, etc. — And we do not require that four come from three, for the strength of the fruit within gives such a strong taste, and it is like actual wine — so in the responsum of R' Levi ibn Haviv there.
This is like wine, etc. And it appears to me there is proof for this from what the Rosh wrote in chapter 1 of Ketuvot in the name of Rabbeinu Nissim, and the Tur Orach Chayyim siman 62: in a place where wine is not available at the time of betrothal, one takes raisins and soaks them in water and squeezes them and recites birkat ha-gomel on them — end of his words; and likewise Beit Yosef in Orach Chayyim siman 202: if water was drawn from raisins, it is wine and one recites birkat ha-gomel on it; and likewise in Shulchan Aruch there seif 11. And for everyone, wherever one recites birkat ha-gomel there is status of the touch of a non-Jew, as is proven in Hagahot Ashiri perek HaMokher Peirot and other poskim.
