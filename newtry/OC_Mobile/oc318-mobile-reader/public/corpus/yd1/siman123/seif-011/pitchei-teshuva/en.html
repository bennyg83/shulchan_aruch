Accordingly, charcoal made from grape skins should also be permitted. Dagul MeRevavah agrees. Chatam Sofer objects from Avodah Zarah and discusses oil produced from almonds grown among forbidden grape refuse, concluding that transformation from wine into oil may permit it, especially where no wine flavor is detectable.
Everything in the winepress becomes forbidden. This is the view of Rosh and many authorities, unlike Raavad and Ramban. Shiltei HaGibborim cites authorities who permit everything except wine actually drawn off, and records that the sages of Lunel ruled accordingly. Zikhron Yosef concludes that after the fact, in great loss or pressing circumstances, one may rely on them.
