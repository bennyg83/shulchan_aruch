This follows the Shach's view in siman 114, seif katan 50, where he disagrees with Beit Yosef and understands the Rambam to hold that measuring with the hand leaves the wine permitted for benefit. I find this difficult. If the Rambam indeed holds that measuring with the hand leaves the wine permitted for benefit, and the Rambam omitted that ruling only because he relied upon the incident involving oil, as the Shach writes there, then the Rambam must hold that lack of intent regarding the wine and lack of intent regarding the contact are equivalent. If so, it is impossible to say that treading is forbidden because of his force, since force applied without intent regarding the wine is permitted even for drinking, as is stated explicitly on page 58. This requires further study.
