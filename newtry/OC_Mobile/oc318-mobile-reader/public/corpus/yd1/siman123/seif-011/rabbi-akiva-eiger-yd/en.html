(In Be'er Heitev) Because he is occupied with treading. This is according to his view (siman 114 seif kaf 50) that he disagrees with Beit Yosef, and his view is like the Rambam that measuring by hand is permitted in benefit. And I have a difficulty: if the Rambam holds that measuring by hand is permitted in benefit, and a fortiori this regarding what the Rambam omitted — measuring by hand — for he relied on the case of oil, as Shach wrote there; if so, the Rambam must hold that unintentional wine and unintentional touch are equivalent; if so, it is impossible to say that treading is forbidden because of his force, for behold his force unintentionally regarding wine is permitted for drinking, as is explicit in Noda B'Yehuda. And investigate.
