And it can be poured as a libation. — And we do not require drawing; and even though below seif 17 it is not called wine for libation until it is drawn — that is different there, for there is no way to leave grapes together with wine; whereas here it is customary to leave raisins in the barrel, and there it becomes actual wine, since three days have already passed over it — R’ Levi ibn Haviv there.
