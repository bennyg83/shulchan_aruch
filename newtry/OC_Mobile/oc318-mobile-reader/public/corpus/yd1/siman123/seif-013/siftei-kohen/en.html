Forbidden in benefit. And it is found in the Gemara (Avodah Zarah 32a) that it is forbidden to support with it the legs of a bed; and accordingly all the more it is forbidden to put anything in them, such as dried fruits and the like; and so in Tosafot there. And one should not say to above siman 94 seif 3, that it is permitted to put a cold thing in vessels forbidden from meat and milk, as Tosafot explicitly explained there.
