Wine that congealed. Baer Heitev cites Shach as disagreeing. Upon examination, however, Shach disagrees only with the earlier reading 'formed a crust,' where the ruling was based on vinegar, because before it bubbles we are not expert in determining whether it is vinegar. Once the text is corrected to 'congealed,' Shach does not disagree. Baer Heitev should have clarified this.
