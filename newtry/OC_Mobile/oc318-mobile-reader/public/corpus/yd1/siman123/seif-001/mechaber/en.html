Many laws of yayin nesech (and which wine is forbidden because of yayin nesech). In it are 26 seifim. Stam yeinam of the nations who are non-Jews is forbidden in benefit, and the same law applies to their touch in our wine.
{Rama: This is because of the decree of wine that was poured as a libation to idols. And in our times, when it is not common that the nations pour libations to avodah zarah, there are those who say that the touch of a non-Jew in our wine is not forbidden in benefit, but only for drinking; and likewise their stam wine is not forbidden in benefit. Therefore it is permitted to collect from a non-Jew, in payment of a debt, his stam yeinam, because it is like saving it from their hand (Tur in the name of Rashbam, and the Rosh, and Mordechai). And the same law applies for other loss, such as if one transgressed and bought it or sold it. But l'chatchila it is forbidden to buy it and to sell it in order to profit from it (Hagahot Maimoniot, chapter 8 of Ma'achalot Assurot; and Hagahot Ashiri; and Maharam of Padua, siman 46). And some are lenient even in this, but it is good to be stringent. And see below siman 132 (ibid. in the name of Semag; and Agur in the name of the Geonim).}
