Because of the decree, etc. — Meaning: forbidden rabbinically — because of wine that was poured as a libation to avodah zarah, which is forbidden by Torah; and also because of the decree regarding their daughters, as stated in the Gemara and poskim.
Some say regarding touch, etc. — For he is like a minor (who does not mention the name of idols and their attendants — below siman 124) who does not forbid wine by his touch except for drinking — Tur and poskim.
It is forbidden to purchase it, etc. — See the responsum of Maharam of Lublin siman 50, regarding the law of purchasing stam yeinam.
And it is good to be stringent, etc. — See below siman 124 seif kaf 71.
