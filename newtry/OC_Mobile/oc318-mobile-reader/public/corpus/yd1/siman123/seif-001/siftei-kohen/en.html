And in our time, etc. Bach wrote that even if they pour it before avodah zarah in the manner of libation before the face, nevertheless since we establish that non-Jews abroad are not idolaters but have their fathers' custom in their hands — if so, what they pour as wine to avodah zarah is not called libation, since we call them those who do not know the nature of avodah zarah and serve it; and for this reason also they are not accustomed to pour libations to avodah zarah — meaning they are not accustomed to pour libations constantly, only sometimes — end of his words.
