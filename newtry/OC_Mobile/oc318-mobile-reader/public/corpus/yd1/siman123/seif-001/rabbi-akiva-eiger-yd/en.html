(Siman 123, Taz, seif katan 2) The wording 'and even' seems to require explanation. In my opinion, the simple meaning is that the author first permits taking a non-Jew's wine in payment of a debt because this is like rescuing one's money from their hands so that it not remain with the non-Jew, a stronger consideration than ordinary financial loss. He then adds that even if the non-Jew touched the wine, where there is no rescue from his possession but only ordinary loss, it is still permitted. See Magen Avraham 244:17. [Citation 80.]
