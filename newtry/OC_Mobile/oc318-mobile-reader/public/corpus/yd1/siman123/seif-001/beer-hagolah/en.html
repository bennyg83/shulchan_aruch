Mishnah, Avodah Zarah 29b, according to Tosafot there. The Mishnah discusses ordinary non-Jewish wine, which the Sages prohibited because of intermarriage, as stated in the Gemara there, 36b. They prohibited benefit from it for the reason stated by the Rama in his gloss, namely, that the Sages treated it like actual idolatrous libation wine because it resembles it.
