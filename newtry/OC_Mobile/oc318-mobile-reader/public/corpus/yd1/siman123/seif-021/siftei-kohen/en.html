And likewise to purchase from him, etc. — Since there is no way to draw in troughs; and even though it sometimes happens incidentally that when drawing from that trough to put in a vat there is some clear wine — nevertheless one need not be concerned about this: first, because there are always many grape-seeds mixed with wine; and further, since the non-Jew does not intend to draw the wine, it is not considered drawing, as Tosafot wrote — end of Maharik's words; and Beit Yosef brings it. And similarly Tosafot wrote (Avodah Zarah 55b); and so Hagahot Ashiri from Or Zarua perek Rebbe Yishmael in the name of Ravyah, and Perishah brings it and wrote: it is possible this is specifically in a vat, but in a winepress in any case it is drawing, since it is the nature of a winepress to draw; and so it appears from precision of the Tur's wording, etc.
This is, etc. — See above seif kaf 41.
What is in the winepress does not have the status of wine. — Since the grapes and wine flowed together into the basket, and from there only the wine flowed to the pit — for the matter is similar to one who takes grapes and wine together from the winepress and puts them in another place, and from there the wine flows from the grapes; it is clear that what is in the winepress is not called wine just because wine flowed from that place — so the Rosh.
