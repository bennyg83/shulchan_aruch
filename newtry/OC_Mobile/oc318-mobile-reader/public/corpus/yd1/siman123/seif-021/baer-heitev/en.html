Outside. Shach wrote: but what is in the press or in the vat—even if it drew—is permitted, for gentile contact forbids only wine that went outside; see above siman 125. This is when he did not touch the grapes in the press or vat—e.g. he placed beams on the grapes and the gentile climbed the beams and they were trodden by his force; but if he touched them, plainly it is forbidden because of contact when it drew. But when it did not draw, it is permitted after the fact even if he touched; and even if it drew, it is forbidden only in drinking—for gentile contact forbids only in drinking, as below siman 125.
