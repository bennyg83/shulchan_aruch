One may not tread together with a non-Jew in a gat (or in a tub) (Mordechai and Tosafot, perek R. Yishmael, and Maharik, shoresh 32), lest he touch with his hands and pour a libation, even if he was bound. {Rama: And if the non-Jew trod, anything that was drawn by his force and went out outside becomes forbidden (Beit Yosef); and see below siman 124. And that which we do not tread with a non-Jew is in a tub, or in a gat from which it is the manner to draw out the wine, and we decree before hamshachah because of after hamshachah; but in a trough, from which it is not the manner to draw out, it is permitted to tread with him. And likewise it is permitted to buy from him trodden grapes in a trough, since it is not the manner to draw out from troughs (Maharik, shoresh 32; and see seif katan 46).}
