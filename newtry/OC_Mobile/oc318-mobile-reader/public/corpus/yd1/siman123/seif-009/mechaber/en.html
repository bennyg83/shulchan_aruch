Water that one puts on our grape pits, which were trodden by foot and were not pressed in a wheel and beam: the first and second times are forbidden, even if he did not find according to its measure. But if they were pressed in a wheel, they do not have the status of yayin nesech unless he put 3 measures of water and found 4. And there is one who says that one may not permit the touch of a non-Jew in any temed (meaning: water that one puts on the grape pits and the skins and that absorbs the taste of the wine) that is at all of good quality for drinking, because he confuses it with actual wine.
