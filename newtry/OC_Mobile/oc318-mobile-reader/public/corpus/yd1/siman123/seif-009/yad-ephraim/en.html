In any temed. See Baer Heitev. In Responsa Tashbetz I siman 87, regarding a vat from which the wine had been removed and the grape skins remained, and they placed figs or other fruits upon them to strengthen the power of the wine that comes from the skins and to add to its redness from the first liquid that emerges: if a gentile touched it, one must be concerned, even where there is a majority of figs. But regarding the wine that comes from the water added a second time, one may be lenient.
