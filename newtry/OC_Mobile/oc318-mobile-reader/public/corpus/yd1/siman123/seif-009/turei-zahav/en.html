The first and second time are forbidden even, etc. In the Tur it is written: “It always has the status of gentile contact until it is so inferior that people do not normally drink it”; end quote. Maharshal explained the word “always” to mean even if he found only the smallest amount, but nevertheless it speaks only of the first and second time; end quote. It does not appear so to me. Rather, this is what it means: always, even if it is inferior for drinking, nevertheless it has the concern of gentile contact until it is very inferior, such that it is not the way to drink it at all. The Perishah explained: even if he made temed from it many times; but he was not precise.
Because it may be confused, etc. This implies that even temed made from lees has the same law, for there is one reason. This is the view of the Raavad in the Tur, and so the Derishah ruled. If so, what he ruled in seif 11, that the second time is permitted, is astonishing. According to what we hold in seif 11, which is the view of Rabbeinu Yonah, we do not hold like this “there is one who says.”
