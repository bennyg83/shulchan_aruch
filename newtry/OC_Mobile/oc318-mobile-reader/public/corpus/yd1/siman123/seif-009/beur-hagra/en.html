First time, etc. Orach Chayyim chapter 6 of Bava Batra seif 9; and regarding the touch of a non-Jew, etc.; and it refers to temed of grape-seeds that he wrote above; and temed that people are accustomed to make, etc.; and he ruled like Rabbeinu Yonah there; and in "stopped in a wheel" he wrote like the words of the av beit din, that Rabbeinu Yonah agrees, as he wrote; and regarding the rest he wrote Rabbeinu Yonah's words as his wording; and in seif 11 ; and investigate what he wrote regarding grape-skins, that he wrote "first time" ; he compares to ma'aser; and here also he wrote "second time" and compares to terumah, even though there is reason to distinguish, even though temed of grape-seeds is worse than of grape-skins, as Tosafot 96b wrote, "there is not," etc., and the Rosh there ; this is only when stopped in a wheel; but by foot certainly it is better than grape-skins; nevertheless whence do we distinguish between first time and second time? And likewise here he wrote "even if not," etc.; and in seif 11 he wrote "except its full measure"; and in truth all this is the Rosh's wording there in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah; but investigate, and it appears everything depends on whether there is wine taste; and further investigate what he wrote in seif 11 "our grape-skins," etc., "except," etc.; what he wrote from grape-seeds stopped in a wheel ; specifically he found four; and even though grape-skins are better than grape-seeds, nevertheless in grape-seeds whence did he learn prohibition from birkat ha-gomel, as the av beit din wrote there? If so, the same applies to grape-skins, as he wrote; and the essence was said regarding grape-skins; and Orach Chayyim siman 204 seif 5; and it is possible Rabbeinu Yonah holds one does not learn prohibition from blessing, for in blessings we follow the doubt to be lenient, unlike prohibitions, as he wrote "but regarding blessing," etc.; but, etc.; and he explains temed of hekdesh and terumah and ma'aser in its full measure; and so Rashbam explained there, "first," etc.; and if so he disagrees with the av beit din there; and if so there is a difficulty on that of grape-seeds; and if not, it works as he wrote, that grape-skins do not absorb from the water, unlike grape-seeds; therefore he wrote regarding grape-seeds "even if he did not find," etc.; and likewise what he wrote in seif 11 "forever forbidden" is also not precise, for even though he compared there to hekdesh ; sanctity of the body ; nevertheless there is an opinion entirely for hekdesh that even in a thousand it is not nullified, unlike yayin nesech that is nullified in sixty in sixty parts; therefore specifically when there is wine taste; and Rashbam there, "third," etc., and "even if," etc.; and all this is according to the first reasoning in seif 5; but according to the second reasoning, in six even complete wine is nullified; and Orach Chayyim siman 204 seif 5 in the hagahah.
