Four. The Shach wrote: not literally four, but he means more than three and a half, so that there are six parts water against the wine that emerged, for wine is nullified in six parts water. See Orach Chayim siman 204 seif 5.
In any. The Taz wrote: it implies that even temed made from lees has the same law, for the reason is one. If so, what he ruled in seif 11, that the second time is permitted, is surprising. The Shach wrote that in truth, according to the "some say," it is always forbidden whenever it is fit for drinking, and there is no distinction between temed of pits and temed of lees. The Mechaber wrote without qualification in seif 11 because it is understood on its own: since the reason of the authority who forbids is that anything fit for drinking can be confused with full wine, it is obvious that there is no distinction between pits and lees; this is his language.
