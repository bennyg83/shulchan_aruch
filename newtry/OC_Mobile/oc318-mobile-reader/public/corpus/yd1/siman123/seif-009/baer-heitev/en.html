Four. Shach writes that four is not exact; it means more than three and a half, so that there are six parts water against the wine that emerges, since wine is nullified in six parts water. See Orach Chaim 204:5.
In any case. Taz understands that the same rule applies to water poured over wine lees, since the reason is identical, and therefore questions the ruling in seif 11 that a second infusion is permitted. Shach writes that according to the stringent view it is always forbidden whenever it is fit to drink, with no distinction between grape marc and lees. The Mechaber's unqualified ruling in seif 11 relies on the evident rationale: anything fit for drinking may be confused with ordinary wine, whether made from grape marc or lees.
