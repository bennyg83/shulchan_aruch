And wine that congealed. Be'er Heitev of Maharit, who wrote on this that Shach disagrees. And one who examines Shach will see that this is not so — for he does not disagree except according to what was originally read in the rabbi's words shenikram (with a mem) from the reason of vinegar — that a non-Jew touched it; he disagrees, for with all that does not bubble it is impossible for experts to know if it is vinegar. But after he concluded that it must read shenikrash (with a shin) he does not disagree; and perhaps also in Shulchan Aruch before Be'er Heitev it was written shenikram; in any case Be'er Heitev should have explained this.
