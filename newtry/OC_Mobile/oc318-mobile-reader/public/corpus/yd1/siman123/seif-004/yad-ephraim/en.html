With any grape-water beverage. See Baer Heitev and responsa Tashbetz, part 1, siman 87. If wine was removed from a vat, leaving the grape skins, and figs or other fruit were placed upon them to strengthen the wine produced from the skins and deepen its redness beyond that of the first extraction, one must be concerned if a non-Jew touched it, even if the figs are the majority. However, one may be lenient regarding the beverage produced by adding water a second time.
