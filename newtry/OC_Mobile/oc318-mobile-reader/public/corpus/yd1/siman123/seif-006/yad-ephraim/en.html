When they soaked them in water. See Baer Heitev and responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 75, where he challenges the authority who forbids the lees of non-Jews after twelve months on the ground that without water they are not called dordaya. This appears difficult from Megillah 12b, which states, “like wine resting upon its lees.” See also siman 81, where he discusses the words of Panim Me'iros, who sought to resolve this difficulty and proved that according to Rabbeinu Tam they are called shemarim before water is added and dordaya after water is added.
