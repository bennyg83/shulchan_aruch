(Shach seif kaf 18) Rather he means more than three and a half. I am astonished, for we say on this in the sugya: Rava, according to his reasoning — "all wine that does not carry," etc. — behold here it depends only on the measure of mixing; and so it appears explicitly from Ran's words brought in Beit Yosef, who first wrote that some compare mixed wine to actual temed, which requires a quarter-log of wine; and he contradicts this, for there it is mere foam, but in mixed wine it depends on taste; and some hold it is nullified in six; and likewise explicitly in Rashba. And behold explicitly: if one compares mixed wine to temed, six are not required; for in temed specifically a quarter-log of wine is required, and so explicitly in Rashbam. And what Shach brought as proof from Rashba regarding "stopped in a wheel," etc. — there is no proof, for there it explicitly deals with grape-skins of non-Jews, and likewise grape-skins of non-Jews here, which are already forbidden and are worse. And know that Rashba wrote in that case of "stopped": according to those who forbid in mixed wine up to sixty, here also sixty are required; and behold in temed it is explicit that he poured three and three and a half came — it is permitted. Therefore here it is worse since it is already forbidden. And see in Rashba and it is clear *):
