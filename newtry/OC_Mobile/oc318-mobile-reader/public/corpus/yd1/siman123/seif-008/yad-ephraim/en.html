See there, where he cites Tzemach Tzedek, siman 12, who rules that after the fact one should not permit wine in a place where it is customary to place a basket into the vat to draw clear wine, unless the locality has no winepresses among the vineyards. One need not prohibit the wine merely because priests might come and ask that wine be given to them using small vessels with which they draw clear wine, since their arrival is uncertain. He was also asked about one who purchased wine from a vat fitted with a spigot, and permitted it in a case of substantial loss or pressing need where no other wine was available. For the future, however, it would be proper to place under ban those who purchase wine from such a vat unless they seal the spigot, spread a sheet over the vat, and guard and seal it so that the non-Jew cannot draw wine with small utensils.
