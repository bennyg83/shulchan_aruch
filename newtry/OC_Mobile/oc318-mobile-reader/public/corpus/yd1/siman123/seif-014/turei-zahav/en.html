This is Rabbeinu Tam's view in Tosafot, as Beit Yosef wrote; and investigate, for in perek Kol HaBasar (Chullin 110) it is found that Rami bar Tamri happened to come to Sura and benefited from grape-skins he found that had congealed upon them; and they asked Rav Chisda: perhaps they are from yayin nesech? And he answered: after twelve months it was. And according to Rabbeinu Tam's words there is still difficulty: who told him that those grape-skins were first soaked in water? And one must strain and say it is known that in that city they did not throw grape-skins outside except after first soaking them.
