The grape pits and the skins of non-Jews, and likewise their wine lees: within 12 months they are forbidden in benefit, and after 12 months they are permitted even for eating. And this is specifically when one made temed from them with water initially; but if one did not make temed from them, they are forbidden forever, even if he dried them in an oven. {Rama: And this is stated only regarding pits that were on yayin nesech; but if he removed the pits from the press before the wine underwent hamshachah — as will be explained — they are not forbidden (Beit Yosef in the name of Rif and Rashba). And likewise those who tread the grapes in a barrel — even though the wine floats above them — they are permitted (Kol Bo).}
