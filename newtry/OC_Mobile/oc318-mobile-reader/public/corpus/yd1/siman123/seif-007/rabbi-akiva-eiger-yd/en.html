(Seif 20) There is no [prohibition] on what is in the winepress. Investigate, for he did not bring any dissentient on this; and it appears the Rosh's view on this is singular, for all poskim wrote that Rav Huna refers to the first mishnah. And for us there is a difference according to the first mishnah in a closed and full winepress when he put other wine into it — see there how they strained themselves on this — therefore he does not hold like the Rosh; also in Ramban's words that Rashba brought in Terumat HaDeshen (daf 32a) it is explicit that he disagrees with the Rosh.
