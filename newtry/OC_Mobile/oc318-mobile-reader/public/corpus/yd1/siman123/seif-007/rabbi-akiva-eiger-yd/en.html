There is no prohibition upon what remains in the winepress. This requires study, for no dissenting opinion is cited. It appears that the Rosh's view here is singular, since all the halachic authorities understand Rav Huna as referring to the earlier Mishnah. This has practical relevance for us according to that earlier Mishnah in the case of a sealed, full winepress into which additional wine was poured. See there how they struggled to explain the matter. It is therefore evident that they did not accept the Rosh's view. The Ramban's words cited by the Rashba in Torat HaBayit likewise state explicitly that he disagrees with the Rosh.
