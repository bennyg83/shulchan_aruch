Full — and certainly if not full, it is easy. And “treaded” is not precise — even if not treaded there is reason to forbid; and even when the Jew averted his eyes from it, it is forbidden to take from it — Siftei Kohen. And the Taz wrote: this means if it belongs to a non-Jew, to buy from him; but if it belongs to a Jew, there is no concern lest he drew wine, since he would be caught as a thief — that is, in a place where even clear wine is permitted; see siman 128.
