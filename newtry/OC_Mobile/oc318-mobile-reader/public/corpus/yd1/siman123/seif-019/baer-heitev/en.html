Full. All the more so if it is not full. 'Trodden' is not exact; even un-trodden grapes may be forbidden. If a Jew left them unattended, he may not purchase from them. Shach. Taz explains that this concerns a non-Jew's winepress. If it belongs to a Jew, there is no concern that the non-Jew drew wine, since he would be caught as a thief, in circumstances where even clear wine would be permitted. See siman 128.
