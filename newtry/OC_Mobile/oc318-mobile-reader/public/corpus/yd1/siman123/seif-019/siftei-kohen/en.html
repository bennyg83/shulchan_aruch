And likewise if he placed a basket, etc. — And even if the Jew did not take from the wine in the basket — since the clear wine enters into the basket; so Beit Yosef in the name of Semag; and so appears to be his view here.
A full vat, etc. — And all the more if it is not full; and this is clear.
Treaded. And so in the Tur; and investigate, for in Hagahot Ashiri it implies that even when not treaded one should forbid — see there; and perhaps "treaded" is not precise.
