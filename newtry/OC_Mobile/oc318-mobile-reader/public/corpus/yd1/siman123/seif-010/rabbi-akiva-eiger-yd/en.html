(Shach seif kaf 43) But in truth it appears to me. From the Tur there is no proof, for since measuring by hand is permitted in benefit, so too by foot; but according to the Rambam, measuring by hand is rabbinically [forbidden], and as Beit Yosef concludes — if so, it appears that even by treading with the foot it should be forbidden in benefit. However, since the Rambam did not mention the law of the foot, if so we must say that measuring is called libation; therefore he included it in the wording whether by his hand or by other limbs that are accustomed to pour libations, and the foot is included — and as Shach himself wrote; if so, it appears it is like the hand and being occupied does not help.
