And it was full. Derishah explains that 'full' is not literal. According to Rashi, drawing off occurs only when the skins and pits are deliberately moved aside. Zikhron Yosef relied on this in a pressing situation involving non-Jews handling grapes on a sloped press. He nevertheless condemns the practice of sweeping the skins into one pile after the first pressing, because clear wine then collects and the subsequent handling may forbid it. He develops several additional grounds for leniency where only a small uncertain quantity of wine is involved.
