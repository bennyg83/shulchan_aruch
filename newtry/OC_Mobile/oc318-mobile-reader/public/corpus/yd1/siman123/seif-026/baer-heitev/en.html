Honey. Taz explains that this beverage begins as actual wine and is afterward cooked with spices until it resembles honey. If made by an ordinary non-Jew, it would be fully forbidden because it began as wine. Since Ishmaelites are not idolaters, however, the only concern is intermarriage, and it is forbidden only for drinking. Mead made from bee honey or dates is plainly permitted, as stated at the beginning of siman 114. Taz rejects those who permit drinking wine with non-Jews on the ground that intermarriage is no longer relevant because non-Jews distance themselves from Jews. On the contrary, intoxication may cause complete apostasy and intermarriage, and no distinction should be made.
