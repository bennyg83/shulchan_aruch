Honey, etc. For it appears there is reason to permit, as written in siman 124 seif 6; if so, it is like beer that is forbidden only because of intermarriage; and since it changed with honey, there is no intermarriage concern, as he wrote (41b): "they take out for him," etc.; and at the end he wrote that his intent is to be stringent; and he properly wrote that it is not similar to beer, for food for an ill person — God forbid to permit on the sides of the eighteen, a matter that even if Eliyahu came, etc.; and his words appear from what is written that one does not learn from beer, for specifically in beer they were lenient because it is not an eighteen matter; and in the days of the amora'im they forbade it, as written in Tosafot, "because of"; and proof from what is written 35b: "Abba was," etc., "do not talk," etc.: (Supplement) Honey, etc.: And according to the Ra'ah's view, they brought in Shulchan Aruch siman 113 seif 16 regarding vessels of non-Jews; and the same applies to honey of Ishmaelites, even though it comes from yayin nesech, since it is forbidden only because of intermarriage; but he wrote: since many disagree with the Ra'ah and Sefer HaTerumah includes them, one should not permit — see there; and as written in Shulchan Aruch there in their name; and that which compares to beer is not so, for they were lenient in it; and likewise in Terumat HaDeshen Beit HaShem end of section 1, and these are his words: nevertheless it is more lenient than what was said, that they decreed on them because of intermarriage with bread and roasted foods, etc.; and the reason as we said, that intermarriage does not so apply, that they do not invite him for it, and there is no closeness of mind so much; and the court also does not supervise him, only "Israel are holy," etc.; and this is what he said (41b): "because of what," etc. (end).
