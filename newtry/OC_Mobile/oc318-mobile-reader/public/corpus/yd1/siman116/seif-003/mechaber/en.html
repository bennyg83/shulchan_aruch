He should wash his hands between meat and fish and eat bread soaked in vinegar in order to wash his mouth. {Rama: And some say one need not be concerned for this except when cooking them together and eating them; but to eat them one after the other one need not be concerned (Hagahot Mordechai). And thus they practice not to wash the mouth and not the hands between them; nevertheless one should eat something between them and drink, for this is wiping and rinsing (hagahot Shach siman 75 and they bring it in the margin of Issur VeHeter there).}
