In Sefer Rabbeinu Bachya (parashat Korach) he wrote on the verse "Separate yourselves from the midst of the congregation," and these are his words: and why was it necessary to say "Separate yourselves," and God has in His hand to kill and to give life, and He had no need for separation, for He can kill the many and give life to one among them, as the sages said: two or three hide under one tallit — two die and the middle one is saved; and as written "A thousand may fall at your side" — rather so that the bad air of the plague not cling to them, as stated regarding Lot's wife "And his wife looked back," or because they said: when the attribute of judgment is stretched it does not distinguish between righteous and wicked, etc. — end of his words.
