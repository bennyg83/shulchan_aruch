And he should not put beverages. Baer Heitev and in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 105 — foods and beverages placed under the bed, and likewise other matters explained in siman 116 on which an evil spirit rests — one does not forbid b'dieved, as Tur wrote in the name of Baal HaIttur in Yoreh De'ah siman 6; and so it is proven in Bava Batra daf 58 in Rashi, that on a bed they place foods and vessels underneath, and nevertheless they did not warn a colleague not to eat with a gentile except lest he eat something not properly prepared; but in any case b'dieved there is no concern.
