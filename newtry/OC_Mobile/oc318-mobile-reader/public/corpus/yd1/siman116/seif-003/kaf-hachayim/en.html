D) There. Beverages that were exposed — the Sages forbade them, etc. And regarding these seven beverages that involve exposure and their branches — they are written in Tur, Peri Chadash, and Peri To'ar; and since Shulchan Aruch wrote "and now that there are no snakes," etc., we did not elaborate on them; one who desires should see there.
E) There. And now that snakes are not found among us, it is permitted. And there is no issue here of a matter forbidden by count, for they decreed only because of snakes; and since there are no snakes, the decree does not apply. Tur. Levush. Turei Zahav. Siftei Kohen. Beur HaGra note 1. Aruch HaShulchan note 2.
F) There. And now that there are no snakes, etc., it is permitted. But in western lands there are many places where snakes and scorpions are common; and also here in Jerusalem and its environs, even though they are not very common, nevertheless they are found somewhat; and it is fitting for everyone who fears for his soul to be careful with them. And in Egypt one year approximately they left one type of cooked food uncovered, and all who ate from that dish were greatly endangered; many died, God forbid. And why endanger ourselves in a matter one can easily be careful about? Peri Chadash note 1. And so they practiced in the land of Israel to guard. Birkei Yosef in Sheyarei Berachot note 3, and so too Shelah daf 79 side b that one who guards his soul should distance himself from this. Pithei Teshuva note 1.
