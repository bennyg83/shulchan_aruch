Only when cooking them together. And the same applies if they roasted as one literally one with the other; so it is stated in the Gemara in the chapter How One Roasts explicitly — Mar bar Rav Ashi forbade even in salt; and Rashal chapter 35 siman 15 explained according to Rambam that there it deals with fish roasted with meat literally, therefore they forbade because of danger to life; but not like Rashi who deals with one oven — in that there is no danger. But in my humble opinion, since for Rashi it applies to roasting meat and fish in an oven, one should be stringent even regarding odor; and further Beit Yosef wrote siman 95 that Rambam also explains that which How One Roasts deals with one oven; and if so Mar bar Rav Ashi is forbidden even because of odor because of danger like meat and milk; nevertheless this is not comparable to breads baked in one oven with meat that I mentioned nearby, where there is only meat odor in bread and it is nullified, and there is no afterward concern to eat with fish. And Issur VeHeter wrote klal 39 that one must forbid if they roasted together without a pan under one vessel, or even if in an oven they are adjacent to one another, for in most cases grease passes from one to the other — end of his words.
