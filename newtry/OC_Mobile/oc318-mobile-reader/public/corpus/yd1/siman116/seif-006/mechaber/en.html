It is forbidden to eat foods and beverages that a person's soul was repulsed by, such as beverages and foods into which vomit or excrement or rotten moisture were mixed and the like; and likewise it is forbidden to eat and drink in filthy vessels that a person's soul was repulsed by, such as vessels of the privy and glass vessels into which they let blood and the like; and likewise he should not eat with soiled hands and on dirty vessels — for all these are included in "You shall not make yourselves abominable."
