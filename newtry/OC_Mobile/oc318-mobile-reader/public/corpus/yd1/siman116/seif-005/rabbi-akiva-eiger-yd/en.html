(Seif 5) And not beverages under the bed. And b'dieved they are permitted. Shevut Yaakov (s.k. 5).
(Seif 5 in the gloss) And likewise he should be careful from all matters. One should not drink water after fish, for it is dangerous. Tosafot (beginning of Moed Katan): seafarers do not set sail in a ship until after five days from the renewal of the moon; and likewise they are careful not to let blood close to the renewal; and many great and small crafts require guarding not to do them while the moon is renewing until they say that flax found in a sieve or pot for cooking at the renewal of the moon — he is flogged and will not succeed. Language of Chinuch HaRaah (siman 503).
