Cooked food and not beverages. In Sefer Binet Adam klal 60 he wrote in the name of the Gra, of blessed memory, that the same applies to raw foods like a radish; and if one did so he should throw them in a place where a person will not find them; and he himself wrote on this: nowadays since many tread there, etc. — see there further. And behold what he wrote that the same applies to raw foods — in truth this is implied in the Gemara Bava Batra daf 58, as stated there regarding the bed of a Torah scholar — see in Rashi there. And what he wrote: and if one did so, etc. — see what we wrote in s.k. therefore in the name of Shevut Yaakov.
