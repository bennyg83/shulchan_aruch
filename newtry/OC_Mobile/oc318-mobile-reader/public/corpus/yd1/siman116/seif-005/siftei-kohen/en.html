Not to drink water, etc. And he wrote in Minhagim and in Maharil the laws of the water for kneading matzah: if there is iron in the water all the time of the tekufot, for then the harmful force has no permission; and so people practice to place iron on all beverages and foods. And on foods and beverages that are cooked or pickled or salted they do not place anything, for they say one need not be concerned for cooked, pickled, and salted because of tekufah; and perhaps they derived this from what Rambam wrote chapter 11 of the laws of murder and guarding life, law 9 — sheep water and vegetable water have no issue of exposure of snake venom, and it is from Yerushalmi chapter 8 of Terumot; and if so he holds that likewise in such a case there is no issue of venom of the tekufot (and see in Zohar parashat Yitro page 143 regarding the matter of tekufah); and it is good to be stringent in this to place iron on it.
