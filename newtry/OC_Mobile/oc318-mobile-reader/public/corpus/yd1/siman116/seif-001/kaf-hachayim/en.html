A) [Seif 1] Beverages that were exposed — the Sages forbade them, etc. And even if others drank from them and were not harmed, one should not drink from them, for there is a snake whose venom floats on top, and there is one whose venom bubbles to the middle of the beverage, and there is one whose venom sinks to the bottom of the vessel; therefore even if others drank and were not harmed, one should not drink from them, lest the venom of the snake that drank from them sank. Tur.
G) Do not drink any beverage that was exposed, even by day. Yesh Nochalin. Yismach Yisrael. Lechem HaPanim note 1. Beit Lechem Yehudah note 1. And see in Orach Chaim siman 472 seif 1, and in our discussion there regarding wine for kiddush that was exposed — see there.
