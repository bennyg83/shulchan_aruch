And now that there are no snakes. See in Shelah daf 79 side b that he wrote that nevertheless one who guards his soul should distance himself from this. And a proof: behold Tur wrote them for all the laws — one of them did not remain, etc. And in the conduct of the pious the Gra, of blessed memory, it is also written that he was very careful in this matter — see there his reason. Therefore one who wishes to be careful should see all the laws in Tur.
