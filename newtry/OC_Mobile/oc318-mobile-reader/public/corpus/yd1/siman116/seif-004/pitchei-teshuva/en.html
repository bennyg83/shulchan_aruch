And he should not put. See in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 105, where he was asked if one put foods and beverages under the bed — whether they are forbidden b'dieved; and he concluded there is here only a warning lechatchilah, but b'dieved there is no concern of prohibition; and he proved this from the sugya of the Gemara — see there; and see in s.k. afterward.
