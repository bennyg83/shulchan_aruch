One must, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 8 of Terumot — Rabbi Ami said: one must be concerned for what the baraita is concerned for — not to put coins in one's mouth, bread under the armpit, cooked food under the bed, to insert a knife in an etrog, to insert a knife in a radish. Rabbi Yosei bar Rabbi Bun said: all human sweat is deadly poison except sweat of the face.
