B) All creeping things have venom and one must be concerned for their bite; a snake's kills, and that of other creeping things does not kill but harms. Tur. And the practical difference: if creeping things ate from some fruit or from something from which one must remove the place of their contact.
C) Once in the city of Baghdad, may God protect it, they forgot one cooked dish and did not cover it, and the household members ate from it and many were endangered; and people say that a snake crawled on this dish that kills and left its saliva on it, and therefore the household members were endangered, as Beit Lechem Yehudah wrote in note 2. Even in this time one must be careful not to drink at night because it kills — see there. And this is tested and established that saliva that kills contains venom; therefore it is correct to be careful not to leave cooked food uncovered. Nevertheless, God guards the simple — note 2 — meaning that if one forgot and did not cover, one does not forbid based on the presumption that God guards the simple; and see below note 19.
