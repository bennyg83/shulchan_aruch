Meat and fish. See in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 104, where he was asked about goose fat that became mixed into a fish dish — whether there is concern of danger, for perhaps there is danger only with cattle meat, not so with poultry created from moisture and having scales on their legs like fish; and he answered there is no distinction, and this is evident from the words of the poskim above in siman 9 brought in Baer Heitev there s.k. 4 — see there. And see what we wrote above siman 87 seif 9.
