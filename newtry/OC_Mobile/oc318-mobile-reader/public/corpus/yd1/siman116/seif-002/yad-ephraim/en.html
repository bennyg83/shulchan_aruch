Baer Heitev and responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 104 — fish cooked with goose fat, the fish are forbidden because of danger, for also with poultry and fish there is concern of danger, even though poultry were created from moisture and have scales on their legs; and see siman 70 regarding when poultry and fish were salted together there is no danger in salting, but in cooking it is forbidden; nevertheless if there are sixty one may be lenient, and so Peri Chadash, and so in responsum Chavot Yair siman 64 — see there; and see in Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 70, and there he wrote that Magen Avraham siman 173 wrote that perhaps in this time there is not so much danger; and although one should not rely on him in safek danger in such a case where there are sixty, even though Turei Zahav is stringent, nevertheless since later authorities are lenient and it is nullified in sixty, one should not be stringent at all — see there that in purging vessels there is no concern of danger as Mishnah Yomi wrote klal 57 — see there klal 15.
