And likewise, etc. These are his words — Darkei Moshe in Issur VeHeter klal 39 wrote that lechatchilah it is forbidden to roast meat with fish because of odor, it is nothing; but b'dieved it is permitted, for there is no danger except by way of cooking; and if they are adjacent so that grease flows from one to the other, they are forbidden even b'dieved, as is stated in Pesachim chapter 25 — end of his words. And Maharshal in Orach Chaim siman 35 and in his book Pachad Yitzchak siman 15 wrote that it is permitted to roast fish with meat in one oven even lechatchilah, and there is no issue of odor regarding danger, for specifically when they roast them attached one next to the other it is forbidden — see there. And in Sefer Be'er Sheva daf 101 he elaborated on this and disagreed with him and ruled that even b'dieved they are forbidden because of odor, even if they roasted separately one from the other in one oven; he wrote that even in a place where they practice to permit regarding prohibition b'dieved — odor is nothing and to forbid lechatchilah, as above in siman 108 — regarding danger it is forbidden even b'dieved — see there.
