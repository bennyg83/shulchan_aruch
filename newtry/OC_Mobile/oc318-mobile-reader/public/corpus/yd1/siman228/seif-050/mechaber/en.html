Ambiguous language in an agreement [made] with cherem is judged according to what the majority of the community says was their intent at the time of the cherem. {Rama: And likewise other matters where the public is divided regarding their enactment and cherem; and if they stipulated that every ambiguous expression shall be judged according to so-and-so — he cannot judge according to his estimate of their intent, but only according to what appears to him from the language of the agreement and enactment (Beit Yosef in the name of Rivash in Choshen Mishpat siman 15 in the name of Rashba's responsa). A community that decreed cherem that the public cannot stand by — they need not uphold them (Piskei Maharai siman 292)}
