They follow the majority. In a place with an established custom that an agreement cannot be annulled unless everyone consents, the objection of even one person prevents release. Beit Yosef cites this from Responsa Rashba. He also cites Rashbi that current customs change from generation to generation, and it has never been heard that one individual's protest can block annulment of an agreement. Rather, the majority of community leaders decides. See Beit Yosef's lengthy discussion.
