They themselves may release it. Givat Shaul rules that if a group stipulated that any one member could block release and one later died, the survivors may release it unless they expressly required unanimous consent. See Zikhron Yosef.
Even if they explicitly stipulated. Chavot Yair discusses a communal fine reduced several times after a handclasp oath to accept the leaders' decision. Where the oath clearly aimed to prevent renewed petitions, the person may not request either further reduction or extended payment time. He may ask the leaders to waive the oath and permit a new request, though the leaders need not agree lest communal resolutions become a mockery.
