[*Communal enactments and cherem are void nowadays by royal law*] Vows and cherem that the community imposes are not called al daat rabim even if they said al daat ha-Makom {Rama: (and al daat the community) (Tosafos and Afarkasta)} and they have release without petach and charatah, and they need neither an individual [expert] nor three ordinary men — for it is only like a decree and nidui on anyone who transgresses their decree; therefore they vow and release by themselves. {Rama: (even if they mentioned oath with the cherem) (Ra'sh's responsa)} And if they stipulated explicitly that they cannot release it, or said al daat distant communities; and likewise if they added in the cherem beyond their original custom — such as ta'aniyot (fasts), or inflated [leather] bottles, or extinguishing candles and the like — it is clear their intent was to be stringent and it is not released like other cherem except through inquiry. And if he was placed in cherem or vowed al daat rabim — he has no release. {Rama: And some say even if they stipulated explicitly that he would have no release, nevertheless they can retract and release; and if they imposed cherem that it not be released, they release the later cherem first and afterward the first cherem (Ra'sh's responsa klal 5 siman 7); and see above siman 229}
