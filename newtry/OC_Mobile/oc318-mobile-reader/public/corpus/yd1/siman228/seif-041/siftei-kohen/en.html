One who swore, etc. Responsa Maharam Padua, siman 72, cites Rashba, responsum 74, and explains that if the act and its deadline were stated together as one vow, they form a single matter, and when the time passes the vow lapses. If one swore generally to perform an act and separately fixed a time for completion, they are two matters and do not depend upon one another. See his lengthy discussion.
