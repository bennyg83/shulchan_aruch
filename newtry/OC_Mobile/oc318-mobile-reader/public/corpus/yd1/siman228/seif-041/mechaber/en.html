One who swore to pay his fellow by a certain time, or to invite his daughter to marriage by a certain time — he transgresses (after the time) every day on his oath (since he was obligated even without the oath) (and in this siman in Beit Yosef you will find explained); but if he swore to do the thing on a certain day (and he was not obligated in the matter without this oath) and the day passed — the oath obligation no longer applies to him, and he receives lashes for transgressing his oath, and he is not obligated to do that thing by force of the oath. And there is one who disputes and says he is still obligated to do it by force of the oath (and see in Choshen Mishpat siman 73).
