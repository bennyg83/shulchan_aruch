One who swore, etc. A responsum of Ramban and Rashba explains that where one accepted two obligations, to perform a given act and to perform it by a given time, they may constitute distinct undertakings. Failure at the appointed time does not necessarily eliminate the continuing duty to perform the act, unless the wording made the act entirely conditional on that time. The interpretation depends on the precise formulation and whether the time was merely a deadline or an essential condition of the oath.
