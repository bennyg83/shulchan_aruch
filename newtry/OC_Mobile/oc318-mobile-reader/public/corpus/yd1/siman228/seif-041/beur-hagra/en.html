One who swore, etc. responsum Ramban and Rashba: one who swore, etc. every day, etc. — two matters: accepted to do something without tying to time; accepted to do by set time — not dependent on each other; but if swore, etc. like swore on loaf to eat today and did not eat — does not help to eat later need not eat later as beginning Shevuot 3:2 — learn; this distinction like Yerushalmi Rosh brought chapter 1 Shabbat and chapter 1 Taanit between vow and fast unspecified day versus this day (end); Maharam Padua siman 72 wrote distinction between first and second section — second said his vow and fixed time at once; Rosh general 8 siman 6 wrote even if not demanded within time oath stands because time is spur and essence oath on payment and document language — one would say swore to eat this loaf today, etc. there not obligated to eat that loaf essence oath on time; here obligated to pay essence on payment time to spur — see; Maharam Padua Rosh does not dispute since there document language was thus. Ribash wrote once time passed exempt like swore on loaf, etc. likewise Rashba; even though Rosh wrote opposite his words not plausible; even per his words can be lenient here payment of debt differs; in another responsum wrote to distinguish as Hagahah here whereas not in his case; therefore Rav also distinguished between first and second section — his words astonishing here certainly cannot distinguish except per Maharam Padua or per Maggid Mishneh distinguish said with lamed or with bet — language proves; also wrote some say, etc. which is Rosh's view; likewise Taz challenged.
