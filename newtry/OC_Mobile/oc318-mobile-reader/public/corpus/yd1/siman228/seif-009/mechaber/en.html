They open for a person regarding his own honor — such as one who vowed to divorce his wife — they open for him "If you had known that tomorrow they would say 'what did so-and-so see to divorce his wife?' except that he found a bad name on her and thus he blemishes your children"; or they say to him "If you had known you would need to pay her kesubah, you would not have vowed," and he says yes.
