A Torah scholar, etc. If that person went to study, his becoming distinguished is not considered an unforeseen development, because it is normal for one who goes to study to become a great person. Semag in the name of Tosafot.
Since it is uncommon. He reasons that perhaps the person will not come.
And death is likewise uncommon. Even if he was gravely ill, his death is considered an unforeseen development, because most sick people recover. Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban's responsum. If he was moribund, however, it would not be considered unforeseen, because most moribund people die, as below in siman 339.
