To impose an oath upon those born later. Noda BiYehudah proves that an oath cannot bind people who were not present, even if most of the congregation, prophets, and Urim VeTumim participated.
Upon future generations. Zikhron Yosef permits descendants to repeal ancestral enactments without an opening or regret, just as the founders could alter their own enactments. Noda BiYehudah distinguishes a personal cherem, which cannot bind descendants, from a communal enactment established for all generations.
