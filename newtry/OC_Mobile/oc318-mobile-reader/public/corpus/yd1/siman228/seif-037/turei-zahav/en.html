And see above siman 231. This means that if the person according to whose understanding the vow was made annuls it before the vow takes effect, it is annulled. However, this is not implied by the Beit Yosef here. The Ran explains that some wished to learn from the principle that every woman vows according to her husband’s understanding that one who vows according to another person’s understanding may have his vow released by that person without a sage. The Ran calls this a mistaken ruling. If, however, he explicitly stipulates, “I hereby vow if my fellow wishes,” his fellow may release him. The Beit Yosef also cites the Rivash that saying, “according to so-and-so’s understanding,” does not imply that so-and-so may annul the vow, even when he hears it, unless this was expressly stipulated. Nevertheless, if he swore according to his fellow’s understanding and now says that he intended that person to be able to release him, we accept his statement. It appears that the Rama intended only that the reader consult siman 231 for the law when he explicitly says that so-and-so may annul the vow.
