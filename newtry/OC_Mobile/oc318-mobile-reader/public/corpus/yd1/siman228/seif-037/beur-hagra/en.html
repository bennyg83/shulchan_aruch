One who swore on the understanding of, etc. Rivash, siman 185, writes in substance that one who swore on another person’s understanding may always be released by that person without a sage, just as a husband may annul his wife’s vows, for an express stipulation regarding another is no weaker than the law of a husband. He distinguishes this from a vow made in return for a benefit, where the beneficiary’s consent is required because of reliance and suspicion. Where the wording shows that the other person was granted power over the oath itself, his waiver removes the obligation according to the condition originally accepted.
Unless, etc. As stated in chapter 2, 18b: “Unspecified vows are interpreted stringently, but their explanations leniently.” See below, siman 208, seif 1.
