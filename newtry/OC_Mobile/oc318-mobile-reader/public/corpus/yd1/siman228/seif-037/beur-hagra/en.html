One who swore per, etc. Ribash siman 285 briefly: I was asked, etc. I answered one who swore per can his fellow release him forever not via sage like husband — we say all who vow, etc. and layperson is no lesser than husband with unspecified husband; this ancient view Ramban brought but incorrect — if so suffices by law like case if heard remained silent vow upheld though with husband on day he heard Torah decree for household peace not applicable to other — know we say there 72:1 divorced and returned, etc. even though per husband was — Ramban's view; however even here can argue even at time of hearing cannot nullify — what is written per husband she vows means not in her heart this vow stand if husband objects; but one who says per so-and-so perhaps intent to evade artifice as written 25:1 when they make him swear, etc.; also do not learn from husband's annulment — else should say any language revealing husband's will void; we teach one who says, etc. and husband who said, etc. — certainly Torah decree since even standing screaming vows per intent husband cannot annul — Tosafot; likewise husband annuls even if vowed per many [Ran 73:2 s.v. and some derive, etc.] — Rosh and Tur not so as siman 231 in Shulchan Aruch Hagahah but if, etc. wrote in responsum reason all who vow, etc. is primary reason since relied in many places chapter 3 Yevamot (29:2) chapter 3 Shabbat chapter 10 Nedarim and Niddah 46:2; Tosafot there s.v. as Rav, etc. or because, etc.
