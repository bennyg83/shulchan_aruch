Annulment. Formal annulment by a sage is required together with the community's knowledge and consent; consent alone is insufficient. Ribash writes that where one vowed upon the community's will, even majority consent does not suffice unless all agree. If he says that he intended only the majority, a Torah scholar may be believed, though if he specified named individuals, all must agree. Shach.
To be stringent. Nevertheless, a beit din that granted annulment should not be punished, because most leading authorities permit it and their view appears primary. Bach.
