A community that agreed on one matter and each person swore upon it, and afterward wanted to release — they have no remedy until they all regret and a petach is found for their oath like other oaths of the world; and three men who are not from the people of the city release them. And if their petach and charatah is the same for all, the releasers can release all together; and if not, they release each one according to his petach and charatah. {Rama: And see above I wrote that some say in such a case it is called sworn al daat rabim (Maharik root 182); however there are disputants — it is plausible to them that each of them can regret and ask about his vow even though his fellows do not regret (Rivash siman 462)}
