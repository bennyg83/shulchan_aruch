And above I wrote, etc. The Maharik himself, shoresh 181, addressed this. The Ramban asked how communal cherems made according to the understanding of Heaven and the community can ever be released by a sage, since they are according to the understanding of many. The Maharik answered that the Ramban raised the stronger question, though he could also have asked that the people swore together and thus according to many. Since the Shulchan Aruch already explained in seif 22 that the phrase “according to the understanding of Heaven and the community” is not the technical law of a vow made according to many, but resembles a decree or excommunication, there is no difficulty here either. A community is different, because it imposes the cherem from the outset with the understanding that it may later release the cherem or oath, as stated in seif 27 that they may release it even before the vow takes effect.
