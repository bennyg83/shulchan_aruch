They do not release, etc. Even after the fact, the release is ineffective, as stated by Tur and the authorities.
He may not seek release until Iyar begins, etc. Ran, responsum 50, discusses one who vowed on Friday morning not to be in a certain town on the approaching Shabbos and regretted the vow shortly before sunset. He may seek release before Shabbos, because his vow already obligates him to leave the town before Shabbos. Therefore, late Friday afternoon is considered a time when the vow has taken effect. Ran discusses this at length, and Beit Yosef cites him. See the end of siman 229.
All the more so, one who vows conditionally, etc. See Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 73.
The same applies to one who imposes excommunication, etc. See below, siman 334, seif 31, and what is written there.
But regarding a husband's annulment, etc. See below, siman 234, seif 28.
