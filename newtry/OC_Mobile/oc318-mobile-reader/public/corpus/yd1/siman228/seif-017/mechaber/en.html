They do not release the vow until it takes effect. How so? Behold one who swore he would not eat meat for 30 days from Rosh Chodesh Iyar and regretted — he is not asked until Iyar enters. And even in a place where there is concern of a stumbling block for the one who vowed, they do not release him until it takes effect. {Rama: (And all the more so one who vowed conditionally — they do not release him until it takes effect) (Terumat HaDeshen 112)} And the same applies to one who placed himself under conditional nidui — they do not release him until the nidui takes effect. But regarding hafaras ha-ba'al (husband's annulment) and likewise release of cheremei tzibbur — the vow does not need to take effect. {Rama: (Rashba's responsa and Rivash; and see below siman 234 seif 28)}
