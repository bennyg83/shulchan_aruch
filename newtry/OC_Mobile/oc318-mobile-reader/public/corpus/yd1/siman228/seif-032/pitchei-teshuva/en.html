Who vowed to ascend to Israel. See responsum Rashbash siman 2 who wrote Rosh's reason: ascent itself is only preparatory mitzvah — mitzvah is only dwelling in land as written "you shall inherit and dwell therein" as Ramban wrote; Rashbatz: if so one who swore ascend to Israel can ask on his vow and is not like swearing to fulfill mitzvah which cannot ask on; but if swore dwell in Israel and wants to ask — then do not release him — end quote; see there; Baer Heitev of Maharit above siman 203 s.k. 2.
