If not that they stipulated, etc. Beit Yosef concludes on this Ribash wrote regarding one who transgressed community enactment written therein "whoever transgresses shall be cheremed and nidui" — no doubt by law they practice nidui law on all who know he transgressed; but others who did not practice thus from now — one may say per custom practice they enact transgressor bears his sin but community or knowers do not seize his stumbling block until they proclaim on him — behold as if they wrote explicitly in enactment — end quote; in another responsum wrote thus in name of Rashba — end quote; implies Ribash and Rashba dispute on this; therefore Mechaber omitted end of Ran's words.
