Unless they stipulated, etc. Beit Yosef adds that Rivash discusses one who violated a communal ordinance stating that every violator shall be banned and excommunicated. Legally, everyone who knows of the violation should treat him as excommunicated. Yet because others did not practice this way, the ordinance may be understood according to custom: the violator bears his sin, but the community and those who know are not ensnared until a public proclamation is made. It is as though the ordinance expressly stated this. In another responsum Rivash writes similarly in Rashba's name. This implies that Rivash and Rashba disagree with Ran. The Mechaber likely omitted Ran's concluding words for this reason.
