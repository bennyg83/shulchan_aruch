And relatives. In my opinion, a woman is not included, because relatives are disqualified only toward their relative and remain fit to judge others, while a woman is intrinsically disqualified from judging. The same issue arises with a minor. A thirteen-year-old not examined for signs of maturity may be presumed mature for monetary law, but for vows and chalitzah examination may be required. If only one person must hear the details of the vow, such a youth may be joined with three others because that requirement is rabbinic.
