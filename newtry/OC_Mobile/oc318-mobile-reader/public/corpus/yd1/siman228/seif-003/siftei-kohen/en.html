Three times. This is not exact, for once also suffices. They repeat it only to strengthen the matter. Ateret Zekenim. This implies that after the fact once suffices. Beit Yosef and Bach likewise write in Rambam's name that he need say it only once.
And even while standing, etc. This applies even on Shabbos. Although a court may not judge in this manner on Shabbos, releasing vows is not a judicial proceeding.
And the practice is to release communal bans, etc. This is because everyone gathers on Shabbos, and if they do not release them then, they may be unable to do so. Ateret Zekenim states likewise. Bach, in Orach Chaim, siman 341, rules that one who swore to do a particular act by a fixed time, and the final day falls on Shabbos when the act cannot be done, may seek release even on Shabbos. Shulchan Aruch rules likewise there. See below, siman 234, seif katan 70.
