How is the release done? He says to him three times "it is permitted to you," or "you are absolved," or "it is forgiven to you." {Rama: (in any language he says) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam)} And even while standing, and with relatives, and at night, and on Shabbat — even if it was possible for him from yesterday to inquire about it — provided that it is for the need of Shabbat, such as one who vowed not to eat or to refrain from the enjoyment of Shabbat. And cheremei tzibbur (communal bans) — they are accustomed to release even though they are not for the need of Shabbat.
