Had you known that they would press you, etc. This is not considered an unforeseen development of the kind discussed below in seif 12, because such pressure is common.
And then they release him. Meaning, even without any other opening, they release him through regret alone by formulating the regret as an opening. There is then no concern that he does not understand genuine regret, or that he actually desired the vow until now despite claiming regret. They ask, 'Had you known that you would later regret it, would you have vowed.' and he answers no. Even if he desired the vow until now, since he says that had he known he would later regret it he would not have vowed, the vow is uprooted from its inception and no other opening is needed. Gemara.
