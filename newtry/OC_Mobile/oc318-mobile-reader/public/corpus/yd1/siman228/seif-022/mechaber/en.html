A vow that one vowed of himself al daat one or al daat two — he has release even without their knowledge. {Rama: However if he swore or vowed al daat ha-Makom (Heaven) — its law is like a vow al daat rabim (Ran chapter Shevuos shtayim and Rivash siman 336 in the name of Ramban and Rashba); and some are lenient if he swore regarding a matter of reshus (there in Ran in the name of Yesh Omrim).}
