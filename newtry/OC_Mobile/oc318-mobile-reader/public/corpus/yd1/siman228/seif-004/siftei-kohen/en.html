And since need, etc. Releaser must be settled; but the forbidden one need not be settled at all; however certainly need not stand since not law; now practiced releasers sit and forbidden one stands; appears proof for their words from Yerushalmi chapter Naarah HaMe'urasah — there vows asked only wrapped and sitting, asker sitting and questioner must stand, etc.; even though in our Gemara and poskim we say release standing — that is for regret; but for opening need settled; one may say Yerushalmi deals with opening; now practice to release all via opening as below seif 7 Hagahah; in Gemara Eruvin chapter HaDar page 64 side 2 incident of Rabban Gamliel — forbidden to hire to teach as below siman 242 seif 13 — likewise forbidden to release vows; likewise Keritut page 13 side 2 regarding Rav Acha who had vow, etc.; one may say specifically when releasing via opening needing investigation and settling mind as Gemara concludes there incident of Rabban Gamliel was thus.
