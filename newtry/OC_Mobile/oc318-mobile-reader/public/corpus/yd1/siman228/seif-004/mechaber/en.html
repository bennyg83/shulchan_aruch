In what matters is this said — that they release while standing when they release through charatah without petach; but one who does not regret from the outset and needs to find for him a petach needs to settle the matter and needs great examination, for what "he does not regret from the outset" means is because he had benefit in the vow — such as if his friends wanted to dissuade him, such as that he would eat with them or marry off his son or daughter according to their desire and the like, and through the vow he escaped from them and he did not wish that he had not vowed. Therefore one must find for him a petach that if he had known it would come to this he would have refrained from vowing, even though he would have had to listen to the voice of his friends and go against his own mind; and if he lies to the sage, his release is nothing. And since he needs settling of mind, he needs to be seated.
