The same applies if she converted, etc. This appears in a responsum of the Rosh, klal 34, ruling 1. The Beit Yosef did not cite it here. The reason is that we are witnesses that he did not enter the match with the intention that his family become blemished. It follows, however, that if this occurred to the paternal aunt of the prospective spouse, the law would not necessarily be the same, for we may extend the category only to cases explicitly recognized as a family blemish.
