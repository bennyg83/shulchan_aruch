No permission to release. See responsum Rabbi Levi ben Chaviv siman 3 who wrote: appears distinction between one who vowed in his distress and specified "if God saves me behold I vow such and such" — no release; but when vowed unspecified without condition whether God saves or not — can release him — see there; see Shach s.k. 105 who divides thus and what he wrote in name of responsum Maharam Mat in one who vowed in distress to go to Israel — must say deals also specifying "if God saves me I will go to Israel."
