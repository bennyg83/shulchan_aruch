Some say specifically, etc. Hagahot Mordechai to Shevuot discusses a person subject to a communal ban and says that if he entered while the ban was in force, he was included. If the ban was suspended for a period, however, this was plainly not the community’s intention, and once he entered during the permitted period he remains permitted. A vow partially released is wholly released, and the first permission is not automatically lost when the ban later resumes.
