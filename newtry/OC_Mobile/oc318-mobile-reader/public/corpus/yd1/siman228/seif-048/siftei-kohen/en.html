One who swore, etc. Likewise every Rosh Chodesh whose beginning is two days — not comparable to one who swore pay on Rosh Chodesh Nisan which is full month — you do not obligate pay at day's beginning; here different since days divided and we grasp day of Rosh Chodesh by itself; since so plausible to me should be concerned and stringent — this is like biblical doubt stringency — Rashba wrote and Sema copied Choshen Mishpat siman 73 s.k. 27 in general; for now without reason of doubt to be stringent we rule thus except as stated in Gemara chapter Konam even for leniency we say thus because people call it month-head; explained above siman 210 seif 4; possible Rashba's view to distinguish with strain but not plausible to distinguish; likewise Rosh chapter Konam as written — see there in what he wrote; therefore in Ketubot and documents on first Rosh Chodesh day write "on Rosh Chodesh day," etc. — evidently documents law like vows law in people's language, etc.; per this even without oath if written in document obligated pay Rosh Chodesh Adar or before first Rosh Chodesh Adar — since document language judged like vows as Rabbi Yerucham wrote and Shulchan Aruch Choshen Mishpat siman 42; likewise Beit Yosef Choshen Mishpat siman 73 renewed 15th in name of Rashba's responsum itself — if so obligated pay before first new month — so appears to me.
