Swore not to be tax collector or trustee of city. It appears he benefits from municipal authority which is among things gentiles and converts are not appointed over; also community benefits from him since their work done through him; since he benefits — not asked of town sage — end language of Rashba responsum in Beit Yosef. Darkei Moshe wrote: even though he vowed only this vow not to be tax collector or trustee alone and did not forbid city benefit — nevertheless not asked of town sage for he forbade tax collection and trusteeship bringing him benefit from city; his intent on benefit of tax collection and benefit of trusteeship; since through sage's release this benefit comes to him — behold he transgressed his vow; so appears primary view — end quote; per this Rashba follows Rambam's view as Beit Yosef wrote; Taz wrote — see there in his explanation not like Darkei Moshe wrote; investigate; nevertheless per poskim who forbid due to interest as I wrote s.k. 9 here — in any case not asked — accept.
