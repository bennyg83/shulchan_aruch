(There, seif katan 45) This does not appear so, etc. It seems to me that Ran's view is that if one vows without qualification 'with his friend's intent,' the friend cannot release the vow, because that phrase does not mean that the friend may annul it. But if the vower says that this was his intention, namely that his friend could annul it, it is as though he expressly stipulated so, because vows follow the vower's intention. Rivash likewise holds this view. Examine carefully.
