And the oath was for Jethro's benefit. Shach s.k. 43 disputes and his view: regarding favor even if oath not for companion's benefit — none who permit. See responsum Zera Avraham Choshen Mishpat siman 9 regarding Reuven servant of Shimon whom Shimon expelled from house because all he earned he lost among wicked men; when he saw evil ended he appeased Shimon who agreed on condition he swear per his knowledge not to wear silk garments for ten years; asked above rabbi if he has release via sage not per master's knowledge; ruled since no benefit reaches master can release not in his presence — see there; astonishing he did not mention Shach's words here that even if oath not for companion's benefit — since he accepted favor cannot release without his knowledge. Found in Shaar HaMelekh chapter 6 Laws of Oaths halachah 7 they wondered on this and again ruled conversely Shach's words astonishing, proved from Rashba and Ran regarding vow for orphans oppositely; also brought Maharit part 1 siman 1 regarding Reuven obligated money to Shimon by set time and forbade himself meat and wine if not pay by that time — Raavad wrote he has remedy to ask on his vow even though taught one forbidden benefit from friend, all the more where accepted favor like our case who lent in his distress; our case unlike Moses and Zedekiah since here no benefit to friend in vow's upholding; from his words clear: whenever no benefit to friend in his oath even if accepted favor — all permit without his knowledge — opposite Shach — see there; see responsum Chatam Sofer.
