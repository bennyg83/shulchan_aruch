They do not open regarding the honor of his father and mother, to say "If you had known they would say to your father and mother 'see the offspring you raised — how lightly do your children take vows!' and thus you belittle the honor of your father and mother" — for this reason, that he is not brazen to say he would not have refrained for this. And for this reason they do not open for him regarding the honor of his teacher; but they open for him regarding matters between him and his father and mother — such as if he forbade them from his property, they open for him "If you had known you are obligated in their sustenance, you would not have vowed."
