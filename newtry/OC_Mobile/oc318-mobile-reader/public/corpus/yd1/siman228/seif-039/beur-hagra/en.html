One who swore, etc. As stated at the end of chapter 5.
But, etc. Since he still desires fulfillment of the condition, this differs from the first clause, where it is as though the condition was fulfilled. The Rosh nevertheless rules that the creditor may extend the time. Orach Chaim writes that if he extended the time, the debtor is released from the entire oath, because a vow partially released is wholly released.
