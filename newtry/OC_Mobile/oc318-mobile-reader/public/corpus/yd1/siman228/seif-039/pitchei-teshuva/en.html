His deeds. See responsum Noda Biyhuda second edition Choshen Mishpat siman 204 who wrote: means he converted religion or became promiscuous in forbidden relations or other sins and ugly deeds heard about him — but for one time still does not fall upon him name "corrupted" — see there further; see responsum Darchei Noam Yoreh Deah siman 434 and responsum Shemesh Tzedakah Choshen Mishpat siman 43.
