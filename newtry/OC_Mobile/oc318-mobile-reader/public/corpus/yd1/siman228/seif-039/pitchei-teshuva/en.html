That he say, 'I regard myself as though I received.' Pri Tevuah permits this formula even for waiving a known communal tax or meat levy, because a definite debt may be treated as received and then gifted back. The greater stringency of Rashba concerns an oath, not necessarily an ordinary communal cherem.
To extend it. Noda BiYehudah proves that merely waiving the handclasp or oath does not extend the underlying debt. The creditor must waive the debt itself; the oath then falls away automatically.
