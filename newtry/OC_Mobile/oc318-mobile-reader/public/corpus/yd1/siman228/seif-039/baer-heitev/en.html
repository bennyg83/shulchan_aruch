As though. Beit Yosef explains that the phrase 'I regard it as though I received it' works only where the obligation directly benefits the recipient. If one vowed to his father-in-law not to travel with his wife without permission, the father-in-law's waiver does not substitute for formal annulment merely because he gains some satisfaction. Taz.
