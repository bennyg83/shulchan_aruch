Husband nullifies, etc. Since not dependent on her will — husband nullifies even without her regret; just as her will does not inhibit her nullification — so many's will does not inhibit her — so poskim. Rosh wrote chapter HaSholeach: possible even if husband nullifies his will and gives her permission to vow per others — nothing since woman stam vows per husband and Scripture tied her nullification to husband — husband can retract what he gave permission (likewise Rabbi Yerucham netzach 27 part 2 in name of Yad Moshe); appears even though Rosh wrote thus in language "possible" — nevertheless holds law is thus; therefore wrote there in hints explicitly — otherwise difficult for Rav Huna why she lost ketubah — let husband give her permission; why needed Rosh and Tosafot and Ran there to explain husband's nullification helps even per many — if so proven from Tosafot and Rosh and Ran even if husband gave permission does not help; likewise proven from all poskim who ruled like Rav Huna not like Bach below section 234 who wrote implies Rosh doubtful; therefore Tur did not write, etc.; also omitted Tur's words Even HaEzer siman 96 explicitly even if husband gave permission nullifies — see there.
