One who swears not to enter the community's enactment — it is a shevuat shav (vain oath) and against his will the community's enactment applies to him; and if he does not act like them, he stumbles in cherem of their enactment. {Rama: And even if he swore about some matter not to reveal it and afterward they imposed cherem upon it — he is obligated to tell (Maharik root 110). And no person can say "I did not hear the cherem," for even though they did not hear at all, nevertheless the community's cherem applies to every person (Mordechai beginning of chapter 3 of Shevuos and Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban). A community that decreed cherem on all inhabitants of their city — all are included, even those who do not pay taxes (Ra'sh's responsa klal 6 siman 29). And it is forbidden to transgress the stringencies of the place all the time one is within the city's bounds (Mordechai chapter Kol HaBasar); and see above siman 334 regarding cheremei tzibbur.}
