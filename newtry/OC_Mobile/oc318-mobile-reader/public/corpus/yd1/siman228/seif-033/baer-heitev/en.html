Stumbled. Taz writes that he must seek annulment of his private oath and accept the communal cherem. If the communal enactment was designed to prevent sin, his oath does not take effect at all and requires no annulment. If it concerns monetary affairs, the oath takes effect and must be annulled.
And afterward. All the more if already gave cherem and afterward swore; must tell. Shach; Taz wrote but even without cherem must tell when this one needs testify; behold if not tell, etc.; must be they did not demand testimony; then no transgression as Choshen Mishpat siman 228; but if truly demanded testimony; must tell even without cherem.
