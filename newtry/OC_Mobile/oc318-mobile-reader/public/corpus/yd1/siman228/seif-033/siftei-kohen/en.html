It is a vain oath. Responsa Ramban, siman 280, writes that he must nevertheless seek release from his oath and accept the communal bans and enactments. Beit Yosef cites him. Beit Yosef writes similarly at the end of this siman in Rivash's name.
And even if he swore, etc. Meaning, even if he swore not to disclose the matter and only afterward the community imposed a ban requiring disclosure, his earlier oath does not take effect. Certainly, if the ban already existed and he swore afterward, he must disclose. Maharik likewise writes explicitly that even though his oath preceded their decree, it did not take effect.
And it is forbidden to violate the stringencies of the place, etc. See the end of siman 214, seif katan 7.
