One who swears, etc. This is like swearing to nullify a mitzvah, for Yevamot 21a derives from “You shall guard My charge” that every generation has authority to establish safeguards and protective ordinances.
It is a vain oath. As stated at the end of chapter 3 of Shevuot. Meaning, it requires no release and he is liable for the vain oath, as explained there and in Mordechai at the beginning of Shevuot, and as above, siman 239, seif 4. This requires study, for why should a communal ordinance be stronger than other enactments and rabbinic safeguards? See there, seif 7 in Rama. The distinction may be that the later oath directly contradicts an obligation already binding upon him.
