The ban, etc. See Be’er HaGolah. I do not understand Beit Yosef’s objection, asking what one who excommunicates another has to do with this. If a ban is a vow, does one who forbids another from his property not require release? Although seif 17 distinguishes cases and writes, “The same applies to one who excommunicates,” etc., that is not relevant here. Moreover, Nedarim 7b states that a sage who excommunicated himself may annul it himself, implying that release by another sage is unnecessary. The term used is “annuls,” not “releases,” indicating an annulment like that of a husband, without an opening or regret. The same follows from seif 47, where there certainly is no opening or regret. See above, siman 215.
