If the minister commanded that they impose no cherem except with Reuven's consent — and if they imposed [cherem] it shall be void; and afterward the community imposed [cherem] without Reuven's consent — the cherem takes effect. {Rama: And if they must transgress because of the minister's decree, there is no prohibition in the matter; nevertheless the cherem is in force when one transgresses the minister's decree, and they should strive with all their might and request to nullify the command (Beit Yosef in the name of Rivash) (and see above at the end of siman 334)}
