If he commanded, etc. The responsum states that no person has power to nullify oaths and bans except a father for his daughter and a husband for his wife, etc.
(Supplement) If they require, etc. Vows made under compulsion are permitted, and this too may be considered compulsion, as stated in Nazir 11b: “Alternatively, regarding vows under compulsion,” etc. See Responsa Ramban, siman 273, and Rama below, siman 232, seif 12. The same applies where a dying person commanded under circumstances that made literal compliance impossible. (End.)
