(Shulchan Aruch, seif 20) The one who imposed the oath no longer derives benefit from the matter. Accordingly, if one adjures another to perform a rabbinic prohibition, according to those who hold that an oath takes effect upon a rabbinic prohibition and requires release, the oath may be released. Even without release, the person may not fulfill it by actively violating a rabbinic prohibition, so the one who imposed it no longer gains anything. See Shach below, siman 238, seif katan 20, and Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 4, halachah 2 of the Laws of Creditor and Debtor.
