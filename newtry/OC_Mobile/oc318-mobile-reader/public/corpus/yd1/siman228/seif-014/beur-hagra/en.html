If he did not specify, etc. See Ran, Nedarim 65a, s.v. “where it was.” The Ran and Rosh write that in such a case the release is ineffective even after the fact. One might distinguish that the sage would not initially release it until the vow was specified, but if he did release it, perhaps it would be effective. Yet Gittin proves otherwise in the case discussed here. The reason is that the requirement to specify the vow was instituted because of deceivers, and the court must know whether release is proper.
Nevertheless, etc. Since specifying the vow is required only because of deceivers, this degree of specification is sufficient. See there.
