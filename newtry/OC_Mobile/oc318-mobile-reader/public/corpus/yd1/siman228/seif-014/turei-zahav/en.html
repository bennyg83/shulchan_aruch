Must specify for them the vow and reason. Reason in explaining vow — Rashi in Gemara regarding widow lest she vowed for orphans to forbid all fruits if she enjoyed her ketubah — if she comes afterward in general to release her vow forbidding fruits without completing what she said "if I enjoyed my ketubah" — stumbling block; if she completes whole vow sage does not release; from this we learn everyone who vowed needs completion before sage of all vow words lest if he completes all sage will not release; they also said reason in Gemara due to matter of prohibition — perhaps he vowed prohibiting all fruits if he transgresses some habitual sin — if he does not complete at release time if he transgresses saying only fruit prohibition alone — stumbling block; Yerushalmi brought further one who came before Rabbi Yordan said release my vow, he said what did you vow, he said I vowed not to profit — he said do they act thus, he said I meant gambling — he said blessed be He who chose words of sages requiring specifying vow, for had you not specified your vow I would have released it. Beit Yosef wrote: implies insufficient to explain vow he vowed but must explain also why he vowed; Rashba in responsum wrote must specify reason for which he vowed — that is primary, like chapter HaSholeach and "not profit" case — from this clear we must beware two things: first not omit from vow if conditional like first case; second even without condition not substitute vow language — say as it is like "not profit" who intended to substitute clean language lest sage understand why he vowed — appears this is Shulchan Aruch intent: specify vow and reason — specify vow means not substitute language; reason means not shorten what he said at vow time "if I do ploni sin"; but if he says vow exactly as vowed without substituting or shortening — no hint in Gemara and poskim that releaser asks what reason was in his heart why he made this vow; on contrary proven Yerushalmi conversely — had Rabbi Yordan not found something strange in words of one coming to release who said "not profit" he would not ask why he vowed thus; from question "do they act thus" came to praise sages' words — as if novelty born in that act; if sage must investigate reason in his heart — what novelty; rather clear through difficult question needing investigation from strange matter; unlike other vow without difficulty — through this novelty born we require specifying vow to say language as is without shortening condition — also part of specifying vow. Found explicitly like my words in Tosafot chapter Shum HaYetomim — Mishnah one who consecrates property and wife has ketubah — Rabbi Eliezer: when he divorces her he vows not to benefit lest through trickery he divorces she collects ketubah from consecration and returns; Rabbi Yehoshua: not needed; Gemara: deals with vow made per many — Rabbi Eliezer holds no nullification therefore we forbid vow effective; Rabbi Yehoshua holds nullification exists therefore vow ineffective; Tosafot on Rabbi Yehoshua: need specify vow — if comes before sage will not release; answer: need specify only language vowed but need not explain why vowed — unlike Gittin case priest who married woman in sin — we say he vows she divorces and acts then divorces — must specify and sage does not release since he vows not benefit from disqualified wife — when he specifies sage does not release; here does not prevent release even if hears language vowed. Perishah wrote on Shulchan Aruch here: astonishing we do not practice thus even among precise rabbis — Gemara and poskim do not mention explaining reason; possible to distinguish only strange vow like "not profit" but present vow no concern of prohibition; however Rashi and Rashba wrote generally and proofs show in all cases; therefore do not deviate since Beit Yosef and Rema wrote generally like them; appears because most vows coming before sage to release are fasting or not eating meat etc. to restrain soul — presumably for self-righteousness therefore not customary to ask reason; unlike vowing other matters like not benefiting friend — must know reason; per my words they never intended except beware shortening condition; if he said vow as is — no need ask what was in heart at that hour in any vow — no stumbling block even if we don't know reason since he did not tie vow explicitly to anything — so appears clear to me.
