Sister of betrothed. Baer Heitev; see on this responsum Bach siman 8 and responsum Har HaKarmel Even HaEzer siman 3; see responsum Noda Biyhuda Yoreh Deah siman 69 — Rosh permitted annul matchmaking due to betrothed's sister; a fortiori father's father since sages said until three generations pollution did not cease as written "and on fourth"; also sages said ben Puti, etc. — see there.
