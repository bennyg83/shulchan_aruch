And some forbid, etc. Bach wrote end this siman: astonishing how possible not permitted after the fact — if so difficult for Rav Nachman who said chapter HaSholeach Gittin page 35 side 2 need not specify vow — why not concerned for matter of prohibition, etc.; astonishing on his astonishment — per his words why challenge Rav Nachman halachah not like him — challenge Rav Papa himself who said must specify vow due to matter of prohibition; if you say after the fact not permitted in prohibition matter — why concern for matter of prohibition since in prohibition matter do not go at all to sage to release for never permitted; furthermore Gemara challenges there per view need not specify from Mishnah one who vows and acts — lest sage permit him — what difficulty — never permitted; rather certainly proven there from whole sugya permitted after the fact but not relevant to case before us — there deals throughout that vow is not mitzvah matter or prohibition matter but dependent on prohibition such as said konam fruits on me if I transgressed or will transgress ploni sin; and like konam maintenance on me if I enjoyed my ketubah as stated there explicitly in sugya — therefore in such case we do not permit prohibition matter but permit vow of maintenance he forbade on himself; unlike here where vow is prohibition matter and therefore we permit vow to gamble or do prohibition; such case we have no proof would be permitted after the fact; likewise appears in responsum Rabbi Yitzchak Mizrachi siman 50 — gambling vow and such have no release even after the fact.
