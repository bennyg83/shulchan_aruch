They do not release a vow of a forbidden matter, even if it is only a d'rabbanan prohibition — such as [vowing] for levity/mocking. And if they released a vow of mockery — some permit and some forbid. {Rama: And if there is a stumbling block in this vow, such as that there is concern he will transgress without release — they release him l'chatchila (Hagahos Mordechai on Shevuos in the name of Rabbeinu Tam and hagahah in Semag); and likewise one who vowed not to jest and became insane at times, and when he jokes it benefits him — they release him (Ramban's responsa siman 281); and see above at the end of siman 217 regarding one who vows not to jest, what is its status.}
