In time of distress. See responsum Noda Biyhuda second edition Choshen Mishpat siman 159 regarding woman struggling to give birth who vowed her garment to synagogue to make cloak from it (see responsum Chavot Yair siman 161 if permitted make woman's garment holy synagogue garment) [see below section 282 in Taz in name of Maharil brought also Baer Heitev there] — husband can annul for even if she vowed per many he can annul; only doubt if need annul at all and if vow took effect at all — though garment from father's house nevertheless no less than melog property; in any case can annul for certainly if she lacks this garment must make her another; since can annul — need no opening nor regret — see there.
