Likewise regarding a gambling enactment. Beit Yosef cites Responsa Rashba that if the community included in the ban that anyone who violates it shall be excommunicated, those who already violated it may be released in the same manner as other excommunicated people. It also appears that future excommunications may be released, because the release does not permit the prohibited gambling itself. It removes only the penalty, while the prohibition against gambling remains in force.
