However, one for whom the fast does not harm him or cause him to neglect study; it is obvious that one cannot release it. Shach:
After the fact. Rama follows his view that a vow made upon the will of the public may be annulled after the fact. Shach disagrees and holds that it remains without annulment even then. A vow made during distress means one like Yaakov's vow, 'If Hashem will be with me.' A sick person who vowed not to eat harmful cheese and later regrets it did not make a formal vow in distress and may seek annulment initially.
