Responsa attributed to Ramban, also appearing among the responsa of Rashba. It concludes that Yehudah failed to seek release from his conditional ban because he believed that a conditional ban needs no release once the condition is fulfilled. Makkot 11b, however, teaches that even a conditional ban requires release, deriving this from Yehudah.
Mordechai, chapter 7 of Bava Metzia, in the name of Rabbi Peretz, and Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 14, citing his teachers. Therefore neither Yaakov nor Moshe initially released Yehudah's ban, until Moshe prayed on his behalf.
