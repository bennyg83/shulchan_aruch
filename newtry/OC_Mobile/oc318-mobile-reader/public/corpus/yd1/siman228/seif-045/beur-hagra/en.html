One who excommunicates, etc. See Be’er HaGolah. The view that such a ban cannot be released treats it like a vow made on God’s understanding, which cannot be released, based on the end of chapter 3 of Shevuot: “so that it should not have release,” etc. The first view disputes this as well, as above in seif 22 in Rama.
(Supplement) Some say, etc. Excommunication in this world concerns other people, whereas excommunication in the World to Come concerns Heaven. Therefore release from the former depends on people, while release from the latter depends on Heaven. This is why Yehudah’s bones remained disjointed until the Merciful One released him. Nevertheless, repentance and a competent court may effect release where the original terms allow it. (End.)
