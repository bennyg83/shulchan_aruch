There is one who says: if one congregation made an agreement with cherem to fence a matter and some of them went to another city to dwell and do not intend to return — if that breach is common there, they are obligated to conduct themselves according to the vow they accepted in their first city, and if they transgress they are transgressors. {Rama: And therefore one who left a place where they practice Rabbenu Gershom's cherem not to marry two wives to a place that is lenient — it is forbidden for him to marry two women (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran's responsa siman 53)}
