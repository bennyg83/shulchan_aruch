Since he granted, etc. He did not swear with his fellow's consent and did not incorporate the fellow's will into the essence of the vow. He merely swore not to perform an act without that person's permission. Once permission is granted, no release is necessary. See above at the end of seif 21, seif katan 28. Beit Yosef cites Responsa Rashba and Responsa Ramban, siman 254, concerning one who swore to his father-in-law not to take the latter's daughter away without permission. If the father-in-law gives written permission, he is released from the oath whenever he recognizes the signature. Permission need not be given face to face.
