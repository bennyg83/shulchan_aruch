(Supplement) One who made a vow and regretted it, etc.; how, etc. A vow may be released only through regret, as stated in Gittin 33b: “Did Rabbi Yehudah not say that a sage cannot,” etc. The same is evident in many places. (End of supplement.)
Who is learned, etc., and he, etc. This is the view of the Rosh, chapter 3, siman 3. See Ran, Nedarim 8b, s.v. “and excommunication,” 23a, s.v. “and the release,” and 78a, s.v. “Rav said.” This is unlike Ramban, who requires specifically an ordained sage. Rambam maintains that ordinary judges need not themselves be learned, since the matter is explained to them. See Beit Yosef.
And they also know, etc. So write the Rosh there and the Ran, 78a, in the name of the Yerushalmi.
