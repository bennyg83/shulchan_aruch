The law of hatarat nedarim (release of vows) and the difference between petach and charatah, and how one is called al daat rabim (with the consent of the many). And it contains 51 seifim. One who vowed and regrets — there is a remedy through charatah, and even if he vowed by the God of Israel. How shall he do it? He shall go to an expert sage who is expert and wise; and if there is no single expert, he shall go to three ordinary men who are expert for themselves and wise, and who also know how to open for him a petach and release him. And nowadays there is no expert who is fit to release by himself alone.
