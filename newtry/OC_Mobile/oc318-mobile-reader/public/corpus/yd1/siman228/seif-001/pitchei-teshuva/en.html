No expert may go. Implies if there is an expert, three laymen do not suffice; however not necessarily compelled — one may say therefore poskim wrote "and if there is no expert" because certainly the matter is burdensome with three more than with one, therefore they said when no expert need three specifically; nevertheless three suffice even where there is expert. However in Gemara Bekhorot page 36 side 2 regarding firstborn permission explicit three synagogue members cannot release where there is expert; see Raavad's commentary on Mishnah there who wrote explicitly also regarding vows three do not help where there is expert. Per this appears even though poskim wrote no expert nowadays — nevertheless appropriate to be stringent where there is sage or av bet din not to release with three laymen but join two with rabbi; especially concern Rambam Laws of Oaths chapter 6 law 5 every clear sage in his generation is expert for vows; found in responsum Chavot Yair siman 129 who expanded to prove even where expert three laymen can release; at end brought Ran Nedarim page 23 who wrote explicitly initially cannot due to sage's honor but after the fact his vow is permitted — see there.
