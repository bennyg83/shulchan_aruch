And they understand. The Levush writes, “and they understand somewhat, enough to know what they are permitting.” This indicates that they need not possess deep understanding; it is sufficient that they understand enough to know what they are permitting. This is the accepted practice. Shach.
An opening. Although an opening is not required for every vow, they are not qualified to annul even through regret unless they also know how to annul through an opening. A vow requiring an opening may come before them, and they may not know how to distinguish one vow from another. Perishah.
By one individual. Even two great scholars cannot validly annul a vow. Their annulment is ineffective even after the fact, and the vow must be annulled again before three. Bach; Shach.
