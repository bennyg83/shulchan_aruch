Who have learned and understand. This means that they understand what is taught to them. However, the phrase “learned and understands” used regarding an expert means that he already knows the received tradition from his teachers and also understands the underlying reasoning on his own.
To find an opening for him. The distinction between an opening and regret is explained in seif 7. It is implied here that although the release now brought before them is based on regret, they should nevertheless not release him unless they also know how to release through an opening, for perhaps a case will arise in which an opening is required.
Nowadays there is no expert. This implies that even after the fact, release by one person is not valid. Where the Tur concludes that release may be performed by one person, its text reads, “by fewer than three,” rather than, “by one.” It appears that its intent is to exclude two. This differs from monetary law according to Shmuel, who holds that if two judged, their judgment is valid. Here, even after the fact, two are insufficient, for they are no better than an expert nowadays.
