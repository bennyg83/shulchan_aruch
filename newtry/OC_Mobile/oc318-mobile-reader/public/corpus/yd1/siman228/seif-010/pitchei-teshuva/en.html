Chatam Sofer explains that even Maharam Alashkar permits annulment without the benefactor's consent where the person retracts before beginning the employment or tenancy and has received no benefit. He applies this to a rabbi pressured into accepting a post, combining the absence of benefit, mitzvah considerations, and a credible claim of aversion, though he advises seeking the community's willing consent to avoid public scandal.
