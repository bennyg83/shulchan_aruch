They may release, etc. See below, siman 234, seif 36. This accords with Shulchan Aruch’s view there. See Rivash, siman 185.
(Supplement) They may release, etc. Hagahot Ashri, Bava Batra chapter 2, siman 12, cites Or Zarua. Raavyah disagrees, but the accepted view is that a community may rescind its own enactment through an authority equal to that which established it. (End.)
