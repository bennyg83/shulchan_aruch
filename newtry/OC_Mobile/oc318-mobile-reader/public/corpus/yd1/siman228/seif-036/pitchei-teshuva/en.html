Who vowed to ascend to Eretz Yisrael. Rashbash explains that ascending is only preparation for the mitzvah; the mitzvah itself is dwelling in the Land. Therefore a vow to ascend may be annulled, but a vow to reside there should not be annulled.
Annulment. See Baer Heitev citing Shach and Responsa Ra'anach, part 2, siman 44.
