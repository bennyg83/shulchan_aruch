To ascend, etc. Responsa Maimoniyot, Hilchot Shevuot, chapter 6, writes that even a vow made with public consent may be released when the petitioner's wife is pregnant and travel to Eretz Yisrael would endanger her. We do not tell him to leave his wife. The later authorities likewise cite Maharam of Mintz that if his wife refuses to travel with him, even a vow made with public consent and in a time of distress may be released for such a great mitzvah need.
