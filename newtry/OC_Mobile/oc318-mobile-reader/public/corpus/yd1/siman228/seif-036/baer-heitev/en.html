Annulment. It is written in the responsa of the Maimoni that even if he vowed with the acceptance of the community, he has annulment when his wife is pregnant and there is danger in bringing her to Eretz Yisrael, and we do not say "let him leave his wife." The later authorities likewise wrote that if his wife does not want to go with him, even a vow with the acceptance of the community — in a time of distress he has annulment for such a great mitzvah. Shach.
