For need of mitzvah. Baer Heitev (see responsum Chatam Sofer siman 226 explained there where he struck palm which is more severe — possibly all agree even for mitzvah need do not release — thus resolves Taz's difficulty — see there further); see responsum Noda Biyhuda second edition Orach Chayyim siman 117 who wrote: even per disputants who do not release even for mitzvah matter — specifically when harm to friend in this release, like Moses going to Egypt though mitzvah — loss to Jethro since he was shepherd; otherwise all agree they release. Also regarding essence of law not to release without companion's knowledge — Shach's view s.k. 43: even if oath not for companion's benefit — nevertheless regarding releasing for mitzvah matter all agree if no benefit to friend in this they release him — see there.
