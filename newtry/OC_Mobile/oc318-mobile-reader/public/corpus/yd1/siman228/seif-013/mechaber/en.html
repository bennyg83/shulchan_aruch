Even though they do not open regarding nolad (something born/changed circumstances), they open regarding a conditional nolad — such as one who vowed regarding nezirus before the Temple was destroyed and it was destroyed — they do not open for him to say "If you had known the Temple would be destroyed, would you have vowed at all?" But they open to say "If a person had come at the time of the vow and lied, saying that at that hour the Temple was destroyed, would you have vowed at all?" And likewise all similar cases. {Rama: For that they do not open regarding nolad is only to make from the nolad a petach so that he regrets — for certainly he regrets only from the nolad onward; but if he regrets from the outset, even though he makes [charatah] on account of the nolad, it is called charatah and they release him (from the words of Ra'em that Mordechai brought in chapter Shevuos shtayim, and thus is implied in Semag and Hagahos Maimoni ch. 5)}
