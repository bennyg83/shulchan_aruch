That which we said that one may not, etc. This is Rambam’s ruling and that of the other authorities. Beit Yosef explains that Rambam intends to answer Tosafot’s question in Ketubot 63a, s.v. “with that understanding,” etc. Tosafot also cite proof from the cases released through “Do you still have the same resolve?” and similar formulations, where they did not ask whether the regret resulted from a newly arisen circumstance.
