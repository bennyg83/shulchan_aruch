And relatives. See responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger in omissions to siman 73 who wrote: appears specifically regarding relatives where disqualification is not intrinsic for they are fit to judge others; likewise at night where time causes — unlike women; likewise minors not fit for vow release; even per Tur Choshen Mishpat siman 6 — thirteen years without two pubic hairs fit to judge means unknown if brought signs — specifically for monetary matters; but to be chalitzah judges truly need verification of signs; if so one may say likewise for vow release we do not rely on Rava's presumption and need verification specifically; therefore beware not to take releasers except men whose beards are full. He wrote further in responsum regarding one unwilling to specify vow because he vowed in secret business matter and unwilling to reveal secret to others — has one person to reveal secret to, but he is young and beard not yet full; advice: take four releasers — three whose beards are full and fourth is his secret confidant — before him alone he specifies vow and suffices, for biblically need not specify vow; regarding rabbinic need to specify vow — again rely on Rava's presumption since reached majority age brought signs and this fourth is also among releasers — vow specified to one releaser — see there.
