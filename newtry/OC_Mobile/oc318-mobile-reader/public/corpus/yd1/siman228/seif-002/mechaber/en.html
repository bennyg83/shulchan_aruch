A person may not release l'chatchila in the place of his teacher, nor in a place where there is one greater than him, unless he gave him permission. (Rosh and Ran): {Rama: And they release two or three vows together, to say to him "it is permitted to you"; and likewise they release for two or three people together and they can say "it is permitted to you." (Beit Yosef in this section in the name of Ramban's responsa siman 274) (and see below at the end of this siman seif 46)}
