The Tur in the name of the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchot Shevuot, from the incident in which Ravina had a vow concerning his wife, Nedarim 8b. The Yerushalmi likewise asks whether release may be granted through an interpreter and concludes that it may, because the interested party is present. Rashba rules accordingly. The incident of Yiftach's daughter also supports this, as do Rivash in the name of the Raah, Hagahot Maimoniyot, and Hagahot Mordechai to Kiddushin.
