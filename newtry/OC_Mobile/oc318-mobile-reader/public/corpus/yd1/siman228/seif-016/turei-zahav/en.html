And he may not appoint an agent. The Beit Yosef cites the Rashba that even according to the opinion allowing an agent, release by a written statement is permitted, for his handwriting is better than an agent. The Rivash, however, concludes like the Rambam that a vow is never released except in the person’s presence, and cites Yiftach’s daughter as proof. This implies that even writing is insufficient, for otherwise Yiftach could have sent a written request.
