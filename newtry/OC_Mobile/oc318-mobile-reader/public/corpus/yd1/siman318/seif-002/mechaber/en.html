Two ewes that had not previously given birth and bore two males—both are for the kohen. Male and female—the male is for the kohen. Two males and a female—the kohen takes the weaker one and the second is a doubtful firstborn. And if one of them died, there is nothing for the kohen, for this living one is a doubtful firstborn and the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow.
