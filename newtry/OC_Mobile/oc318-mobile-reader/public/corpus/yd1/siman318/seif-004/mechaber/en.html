One had previously given birth and one had not, and they bore two males—one belongs to him and one to the kohen, and each of them is a doubtful firstborn; and the kohen takes the weaker one. If one of them died, there is nothing here for the kohen, for this living one is doubtful. And likewise if they bore a male and a female, there is nothing here for the kohen, for this male is a doubtful firstborn.
