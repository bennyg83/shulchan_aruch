How many doubts there are in the laws of a firstborn animal. In it are 6 seifim.
A ewe that had not previously given birth and bore two males—even if both their heads emerged as one—it is impossible that one did not precede; and since it is unknown which emerged first, the kohen takes the weaker one and the second is a doubtful firstborn. If one of them died, there is nothing for the kohen, for this living one is doubtful and the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow. And likewise if she bore a male and a female—the male is doubtful, lest the female emerged first; therefore there is nothing for the kohen, for the burden of proof is on the one who extracts from his fellow.
