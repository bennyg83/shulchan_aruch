Doubt. In the Tur it says: it is eaten with its blemish by the owners and is obligated in gifts of kehunah. The reason is that the kohen says: in any event, if it is a bekhor it is entirely mine, and if not, give me the gifts. So too in all cases where we say it is eaten with its blemish by the owners, the law is the same. End quote from Taz. See above siman 61 seif 2 in the Shach there, that the Rambam's view is that in such a case it is exempt from the gifts, since its substitute is in the kohen's hand. So too the Shach wrote here. He further wrote that nevertheless the lean animal is also a doubtful bekhor, is forbidden in shearing and labor, and the kohen eats it with its blemish.
