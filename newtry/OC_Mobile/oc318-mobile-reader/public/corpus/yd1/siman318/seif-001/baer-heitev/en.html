Doubtful. Tur writes that the animal is eaten by the owner after developing a blemish and is subject to the priestly gifts. The reason is that the kohen can argue either way: if it is a firstborn, the entire animal belongs to me; if it is not, give me the priestly gifts. The same applies in every case where we say the owner may eat it after it develops a blemish. Taz. See above, siman 61, seif 2, where Shach states that Rambam exempts the animal from the gifts in such a case because its substitute is already in the kohen's hands. Shach writes the same here. He adds that the thinner animal is also a doubtful firstborn, may not be shorn or worked, and the kohen eats it after it develops a blemish.
