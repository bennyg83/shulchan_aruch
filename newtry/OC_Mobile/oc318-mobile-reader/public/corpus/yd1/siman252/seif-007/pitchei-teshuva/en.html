Saving the man first. See in She'elat Ya'avetz part 1 siman 68 who was asked if a mute gentile creditor comes to take two children from Israel and pass them to his religion and we have means in our hand to redeem one of them for a sum of money imposed on him — one male and one female — who precedes; for at first glance one should be concerned for the female lest she be absorbed among captives since her seed is fit and she has the status of Israel for all matters; and he answered certainly the male precedes — for if to sustain the body the male precedes the female to save his life — all the more; and we had no concern for her seed for several reasons; and even though to take out from captivity a woman precedes — that is an adult and known they will not pass her to religion; and when there is no concern the male will be absorbed among gentiles — see there.
