(Siman 252 seif 1 in Hagahah.) One who vows a sela for charity. Captive redemption is not included at all, and one may not redeem captives with this sela except with consent of the city leaders — Mahari Weil root 7. Taz siman 256 note 4 — Mahari Weil's proof from beginning of Arakhin 6: a sela for charity before reaching the treasurer's hand may be changed to pay later; after reaching the treasurer forbidden to change; we challenge there — is it not so? R' Yannai the treasurer borrowed charity money for his need and repaid; answer: R' Yannai is different, the poor are pleased, for since he delays payment and brings it — Rashi: when he could not repay he told the community there is no money to distribute to the poor and compelled them to charity — profit for the poor; end. If captive redemption were included in charity, how could R' Yannai borrow for himself — fear they demand the money for captives; then cannot say poor are pleased — one, profit reason inapplicable for captives who need only redemption; further they are endangered if delay until community compelled to give — each moment delayed is like shedding blood; why permitted if sela for charity includes captives? And you will say we do not fear captives as uncommon — not so, for we say do not demolish synagogue even leaving money lest captives occur and they take it — all the more for discretionary matters we fear captives; therefore captive redemption not in unspecified charity — Mahari Weil's words stand; challenged at length — no difficulty: R' Yannai in any case needed compulsion as in Talmud and Rashi — then captives not pleased to wait for compulsion — profit inapplicable; when those captives redeemed they will not need the money, unlike poor who do not cease from the land — poor also greatly endangered between them and death; though all captives are in his power, people's intent for unspecified charity is to feed and give drink; and "one who says this sela for charity" means for what the treasurer does — distribute to charity — but for another matter he has no permission except city leaders' consent, as Mahari Weil wrote. Not worthwhile to reject Mahari Weil with mere reasoning.
