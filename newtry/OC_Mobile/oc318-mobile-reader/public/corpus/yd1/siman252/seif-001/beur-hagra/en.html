And there is no, etc. There.
That it is forbidden to sell them, etc. This differs from the first clause, where it is permitted even for the sake of another mitzvah. This follows the Rambam and the reading of the Gemara: in the first clause, 'perhaps an opportunity for a mitzvah will arise for them,' and in the latter clause, 'the redemption of captives.'
(Supplement) But if they have already built it, etc. This is his reading there: 'if they built it, no.' According to our reading, however, it appears that it is permitted even in such a case. See below, siman 270, seif 1, in the gloss. (End of supplement.)
Nevertheless, one who vows, etc. See the Shach in the name of Maharik. His words are not compelling.
