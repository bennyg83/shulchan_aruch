Redemption of captives. See in responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 43 whether a person is obligated to put himself in possible mortal danger in order to save his fellow with certainty — or he is not obligated; but in any case he is permitted to do so if he wishes from piety or from love of his fellow — or he is not permitted to risk himself; and he answered that Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat siman 426 wrote in name of Hagahot Maimon that one must risk himself for his fellow for this is doubt and that is certainty; but one may question and judge on this; and he raised that if both are equal in stature such as both Torah scholars or amei ha'aretz — all the more if the rescuer is a Torah scholar and the one rescued is am ha'aretz — he is not permitted to put himself even in doubt and certain rescue; but if the rescuer is not a Torah scholar like the one rescued — then permitted to put himself but not obligated unless from piety if he wishes — see there; and see in Radbaz part 3 siman 625 that I brought above siman 157 s.k. 16; and see in Chavot Yair in omissions to page 134.
