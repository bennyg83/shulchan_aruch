Redemption of captives. Yad Eliyahu discusses whether one must, may, or may not enter possible mortal danger to save another from certain danger. He distinguishes the relative spiritual standing of rescuer and victim and concludes that such self-endangerment is generally not obligatory, though in some cases it may be permitted as an act of piety.
There is no greater mitzvah than redeeming captives. Shevut Yaakov permits using funds designated for redeeming captives to aid Jews suffering severely during wartime or widespread calamity, because in contemporary conditions they are effectively in captivity and perhaps in even greater danger.
