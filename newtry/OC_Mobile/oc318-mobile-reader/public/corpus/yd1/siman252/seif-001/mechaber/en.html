The law of redeeming captives and how they are redeemed. And it contains 12 seifim. Redeeming captives takes precedence over sustaining the poor and clothing them, and there is no mitzvah as great as redeeming captives; therefore for any mitzvah for which money was collected, they may divert it for redeeming captives — even if they collected for building a synagogue, and even if they already bought the wood and stones and hewed them for the building, which is forbidden to sell for another mitzvah — it is permitted to sell them for redeeming captives; but if they already built it, they do not sell it:
{Rama: (Nevertheless, one who vows a sela for charity — redeeming captives is not included, and one may not redeem with this sela except with the townspeople's consent (Maharik shoresh 7), as below siman 256 seif 4).}
