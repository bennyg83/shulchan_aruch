And his teacher precedes his father, etc. If his father is a Torah scholar, even if he is not equal in stature to his teacher, his father takes precedence, as stated above in siman 242, seif 34.
His mother takes precedence. This is because her humiliation is greater.
She takes precedence over all of them. This means even over the person himself. Although his own life takes precedence over the lives of his father and teacher, as stated at the end of Bava Metzia, based on the verse, "However, there shall be no destitute person among you," which teaches that one's own needs precede those of every other person, and all the more so might one think that his life precedes his mother's, whose obligation of honor is lesser, as stated in the first chapter of Kiddushin regarding fear and honor, nevertheless, for release from captivity his mother precedes everyone, because they may violate her. This is written in Be'er Sheva, page 22c. This implies that the captivity does not involve mortal danger. If there is mortal danger, his own life takes precedence over his mother's.
