One who shuts his eyes from redeeming captives transgresses "you shall not harden your heart," and "you shall not shut your hand," and "you shall not stand idly by your fellow's blood," and "he shall not rule over him with rigor before your eyes," and nullifies the mitzvot "you shall surely open your hand to him," and "and your brother shall live with you," and "you shall love your neighbor as yourself," and "deliver those taken to death," and many such matters.
