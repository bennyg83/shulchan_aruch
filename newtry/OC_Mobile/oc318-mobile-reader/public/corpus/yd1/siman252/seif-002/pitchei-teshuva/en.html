And there is no mitzvah greater than redemption of captives. See in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 84 regarding one who vowed before his death money for redemption of captives and there is an uproar in the world — meaning many from Israel are in great distress — permitted to give them from this money and it is not a change from the donor's intent; even though it is proven in Talmud that captive is less than this matter — specifically in earlier times; but now in our many sins when there is uproar in the world they too are in captivity — it is actual redemption of captives; and perhaps greater than it — see there.
