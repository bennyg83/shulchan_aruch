And he must repay him immediately. Payment may be collected even from the property of minor orphans. Although we hold that the court ordinarily does not act against the property of orphans, as explained in Choshen Mishpat, siman 110, the reason there is that testimony may be accepted only in the presence of the litigant, and minor orphans are treated as absent. Here, however, no testimony need be accepted, since there is a Torah obligation to rescue. Moreover, if the rescuer had to wait for repayment until the orphan girl reached adulthood, no one would ever use his own money to rescue an orphan. So writes Mahari Weil at the end of siman 148.
