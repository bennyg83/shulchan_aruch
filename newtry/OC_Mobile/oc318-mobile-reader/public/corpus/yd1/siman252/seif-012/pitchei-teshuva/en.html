The same applies to a relative. Perach Mateh Aharon rules that relatives contribute according to their means. One relative who voluntarily paid more cannot recover the excess from another, especially if he paid beyond the captive's value.
To repay him. Beit Yaakov discusses whether a captive who objected to being redeemed because he wished to sanctify Hashem's name must reimburse those who redeemed him anyway. See also Yad Eliyahu.
Immediately. Pitchei Teshuva corrects Baer Heitev's citation of Maharam Lublin: even a sinner must ordinarily be redeemed up to his value, since one offense does not make him an apostate, especially without witnesses. The dispute concerns only payment beyond his value when execution is threatened.
