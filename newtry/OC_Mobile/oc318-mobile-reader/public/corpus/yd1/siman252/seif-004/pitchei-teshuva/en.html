More. See in Eshel Avraham in name of responsum Nachalat Yehoshua that where there is concern for death they redeem; and see in responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 43 who did not write thus but that even one standing for execution one does not redeem more than his value — for the first answer in Tosafot end of chapter HaNizakin does not stand on truth — see there; and so in responsum Maharam Lublin siman 15 regarding one who was caught on a charge that he slept with a harlot — whether they must spend much money to save his life; and he answered that by law they need not for one does not redeem captives more than their value; but because of chillul Hashem the community should spend much money on him — see there. And see in Sefer Be'er Heitev s.k. 2 who wrote in name of Gaon Maharshak that Tosafot's words in Gittin were hidden from him that if they seek to kill him they redeem — see there (and what Beit Yosef wrote there as proof — behold one who closes his eyes from redemption of captives transgresses "do not stand on your fellow's blood" — and behold in Orach Chaim s.v. 256 that for a negative commandment one is obligated to give all his money — see there — is not proof per what is written above siman 157 s.k. 4 in name of Pri Megadim and responsum Chavot Yair that specifically for a negative commandment that has an action — see there); but in responsum Kenesset Yechezkel siman 38 he attacked them because this answer of mortal danger applies only if the reason is community distress; but for the reason that they not become accustomed the reasoning is reversed — for if in mortal danger they redeem, captors will want to kill the captives so they redeem more than their value and they will also become accustomed and want to kill; and if so per Rambam and Shulchan Aruch the reason that they not become accustomed is well written — Maharam Lublin wrote the community need not because of pikuach nefesh for captives even in mortal danger more than their value; and what he wrote that because of chillul Hashem the community should redeem — this is because Ri'f and Rosh hold the unresolved question whether the reason is community distress or that they not become accustomed — therefore we say the halachah follows them. And he further wrote all this in other countries that pay attention to money and the reason of becoming accustomed applies; but in these countries of ours it is not so — it needs great examination whether one is not obligated to redeem an Israelite life in mortal danger with all money; and therefore he ruled practically regarding one who sat in the military courtyard near mortal danger that they must spend money on him; but relatives give assessment as the treasurers impose on them and the remainder from the community fund — see there.
