They do not redeem captives for more than their value, because of tikkun ha'olam so that enemies do not hand themselves over to be captured; but a person may redeem himself for whatever he wishes; and likewise for a Torah scholar, or even if he is not a Torah scholar but is a sharp student and it is possible he will become a great man — they redeem him for much money: {Rama: (And whether his wife is like another for valuation or not — see Tur, Even HaEzer siman 78).}
