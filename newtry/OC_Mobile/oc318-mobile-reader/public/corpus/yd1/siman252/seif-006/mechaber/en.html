One who sold himself to a gentile, or borrowed from them and they captured him for his debt — the first and second times they redeem him; the third time they do not redeem him; but they redeem the children after their father's death; and if they sought to kill him, they redeem him immediately even after many times: {Rama: (And a captive who apostatized, even for one mitzvah — such as eating nevelot to provoke — it is forbidden to redeem him (Tur); and see above beginning of siman 251).}
