A pot in which meat was cooked — one may not cook milk in it. In it is one seif: A pot in which meat was cooked — one may not cook milk in it; and if one cooked in it within me'eit l'eit it is forbidden as noten ta'am (and one must estimate against the entire pot). But if it stood me'eit l'eit before one cooked in it, it is noten ta'am li'fegam and the cooked dish is permitted; but the pot is forbidden to cook in it neither meat nor milk: {Rama: But other things are permitted (Rivash siman 126 in name of Semak and in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6). And the law of a pot cover is like the law of the pot itself (Da'at Peh Kadosh and Orach Chaim HaAruch). And some are stringent with a cover to say that even though it is not ben yomo its law is as if it were ben yomo (in Hagahot ShaDaL in name of Kovetz), and so they practice in some places, and so I practice because of the custom (Taz, Shach) and it is a stringency without reason (Da'at Peh Kadosh); but in any case where there is any side to permit anyway, or it is for Shabbat need or loss, one may permit if the cover is not ben yomo as with the pot itself. And if they took a boiling cover from a pot of meat and placed it on a pot of milk — if both are hot, both are forbidden if there is food in the pot of meat and milk; and if the cover is cold and the pot hot, both are also forbidden if it began to sweat under the cover because the bottom prevails (Orach Chaim HaAruch). And if the cover is hot and the pot cold — everything is permitted (there); only the food needs peeling (per Hagahat ShaDaL and not like Orach Chaim HaAruch there) if possible to peel, and if not everything is permitted; and if there was no food in the pot everything is permitted, for it is like two pots that touched one another (all this is Da'at Peh Kadosh per the view of Orach Chaim HaAruch)}
