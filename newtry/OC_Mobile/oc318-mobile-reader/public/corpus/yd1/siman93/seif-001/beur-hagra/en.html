Pot, etc. — Zevachim 96, Chullin 97a, 111b.
And one must, etc. — as written in chapter 3 (97b) it is obvious we estimate regarding the vessel itself, for if, etc.; and this is when it is not known how much it absorbed within me'eit l'eit; and per his view that he wrote at the beginning of siman 98 we do not rely on tasting and estimate with sixty.
But if, etc. — as written at the end of Avodah Zarah 76a the Torah did not forbid, etc.; and even though the pot is forbidden l'chatchila as written decree, etc., but the cooked dish is permitted b'dieved as written 38b hot; and likewise absorbed oil is permitted for this reason; and see Rashi there s.v. noten ta'am li'fegam, etc., and Tosafot s.v. if, etc., and Tosafot 76a s.v. from here, etc.; and Rashba and Ran (see there) explain ben yomo means within me'eit l'eit — not like Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam there that night spoils, for if so why did the Torah require pouring out, rinsing, and breaking for every earthenware vessel — behold when it becomes notar it is spoiled; and one might say it is a Scriptural decree — for if so how do we learn from it that the Torah testified on every earthenware vessel, etc., that hag'alah does not help; granted if we say me'eit l'eit it is possible it will become notar and once the law of breaking, pouring out, and rinsing applies to it, it no longer ceases. Ra'ash and Ran (see there). And Tosafot strained themselves on this see there.
But the pot, etc. — not only meat that absorbed from milk, but even milk — even though absorption of meat already passed me'eit l'eit, nevertheless l'chatchila it is forbidden as written at the end of Avodah Zarah (and see above siman 120 s.k. 4 and s.k. 6).
But other, etc. — for the pot does not absorb forbidden matter in basar b'chalav, for one: the absorption is not forbidden since the taste of meat was already spoiled and the cooked dish is permitted as written in Shulchan Aruch; and see Tosafot at the end of Avodah Zarah 76a s.v. from here. And one may give, etc.; and likewise here milk is forbidden because the beginning of absorption of meat was for enhancement, but other things whose beginning of absorption in basar b'chalav was for detriment.
