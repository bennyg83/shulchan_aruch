But the pot is forbidden to cook in it neither meat nor milk. Because it absorbed from both, therefore it is forbidden to cook meat in it l'chatchila even after me'eit l'eit from this cooking, as it is forbidden to cook milk l'chatchila in a meat pot after me'eit l'eit; however if one cooked meat in it within me'eit l'eit before cooking milk in it — it is obvious the cooked dish is forbidden; but if one cooked milk in it — it is obvious it is permitted b'dieved; but other things that are neither of meat nor of milk — such as vegetables and fish and the like — it is permitted to cook even l'chatchila; and below siman 94 seif 5 the Rav wrote they practice to forbid the vessel that is not ben yomo even for other things, even if only a ben yomo spoon was inserted in it — if so all the more here where meat was actually cooked in it; and one must say that here the matter is stated plainly for the law; and see there in siman 94:
And it is a stringency without reason. However in Taz general rule 80 law 4 he wrote some reason for those who are stringent, because they hold prohibition of steam and odor is stricter than prohibition through taste itself; and Maharshal in Orach Chaim siman 35 rejected a similar reason and from his rejection this reason too was rejected, see there; and he wrote that specifically in those covers that are short at the top and have a kind of hollow as in many covers — then it is impossible to wipe them well clean of steam and boiling that rose there; but in those wide at the top where the hand can control to wipe well — one does not say it is forbidden in substance and permitted, end of his words; and one must say per his words that even though that which is in the short place is a small matter, nevertheless meat steam rises upward up to sixty against it until what is in the cover becomes like nevelah in substance, and afterward the cover becomes nevelah as below siman 98 seif 5 — for perhaps there is not in it sixty times sixty-one slightly less against what is in substance, and it is similar to above siman 92 seif 5 see there; and nevertheless certainly one should not be so stringent with a cover as the Rav wrote, and likewise in Apir Ravrevi daf 61a that in time of need or if there is another side to permit, or a wide cover, or perforated above — it appears to be lenient accordingly; and likewise Darchei Moshe cried out like a herald against this custom, and the end of his words: if my strength avails I would abolish the custom, for it is an erroneous custom with no reason to rely on it, until here.
