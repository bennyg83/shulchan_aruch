(siman 93 in the gloss) And if there was no food in the pot. I have difficulty: behold even if there is food in the pot the pot is permitted — so what did Rama innovate that if there is no food in the pot everything is permitted; and to say "everything is permitted" means even when both are hot — behold in that case certainly the pot is forbidden because of the moisture on the cover, for Orach Chaim's reasoning that it is lenient compared to other iruy does not apply — a single drop nullifies the force of its heat, for since they are pots in what will its heat be nullified — requires further study.
