An expert. Nowadays there are no experts, and three are required, as below in siman 309 seif 2.
To dogs. The Shach wrote that many poskim forbid giving it to dogs, as it is written, “and you shall eat,” not “and your dogs shall eat”; therefore one must be careful in the matter. Regarding a non-Jew too, Maharshal wrote that there is nevertheless a rabbinic prohibition because of disgrace to consecrated items, and one may feed it to him only by way of invitation, where the non-Jew eats with him, but not sell it to him or even give him a raw piece. When it is a treifah, benefit from it is forbidden, as below in siman 307. End quote.
