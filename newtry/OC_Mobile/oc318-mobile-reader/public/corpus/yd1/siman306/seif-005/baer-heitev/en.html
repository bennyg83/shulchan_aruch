To the dogs. Shach writes that many poskim prohibit giving it to dogs, based on the verse, "and you shall eat it," excluding your dogs. One should therefore be careful. Regarding giving it to a gentile, Maharshal writes that there is nevertheless a rabbinic prohibition because of degradation of sacred property. One may permit the gentile to eat it only by inviting him to eat together with the Jew, but one may not sell it to him or even hand him a raw piece. If it is a tereifah, deriving benefit from it is forbidden, as stated below in siman 307.
