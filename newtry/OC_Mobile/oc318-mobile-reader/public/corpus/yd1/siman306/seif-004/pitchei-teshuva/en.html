And he betroths a woman with it. See Even HaEzer siman 28, Beit Shmuel s.k. 60, who writes that what is stated here, "and he betroths with it," means after slaughter, and refers to what was said, "and slaughter." This resolves the apparent contradiction in the Mechaber's words, for there he rules that she is doubtfully betrothed; see there.
