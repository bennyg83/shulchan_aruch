(Siman 306, Taz seif katan 6) Regarding shearing and work. Derishah writes similarly. This shows he holds that the main verse applies only to an unblemished firstborn, while with a blemished firstborn the prohibition is merely rabbinic. Levush likewise, and Sefer Yereim of R. Eliezer of Metz, mitzvah 352, says this explicitly. Semag, Hilchot Bechorot mitzvah 211, and Rambam, Hilchot Bechorot chapter 1, halachah 8, however, clearly treat it as biblical.
(there seif katan 8) From this one can also learn. So it is explicit in Shulchan Aruch Orach Chaim (siman 55 seif 6), and its source is from the Agur, who brought a proof from the law here.
