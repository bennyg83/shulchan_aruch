Whether in Eretz Yisrael, etc. Mishnah at the end of chapter 3 of Temurah: except for bekhor and animal tithe, that if they came, etc. Rabbi Shimon said, what is the reason, etc.; and Bekhorot 36b: “bekhor,” what does Scripture teach, etc.; and in several places.
(Likkut) And it is a mitzvah to consecrate it, etc. Rambam. But the Tur in the name of the Rosh disagrees. The Bach wrote that this requires study. The Rambam's reason is from the sugya in Arakhin 29a, where he ruled like the sages of the Mishnah. The Kesef Mishneh wondered at him: although Ulla holds like the sages, Rav disputes him, and he is his teacher; furthermore, Rav Yehudah acted accordingly. But in the sugya there: and Rabbi Yehudah ben Beteira, that it takes effect on kodshei kodashim, etc., holds like Rabbi Yishmael; and below there: the rabbis need “do not consecrate” for a prohibition, etc., and Rabbi Yehudah, etc. The Rosh followed his approach like the Kesef Mishneh's question, that the halakhah follows Rabbi Yehudah ben Beteira and therefore also Rabbi Yishmael. But the Rambam holds that the halakhah follows the sages regarding bekhor; therefore the halakhah follows the sages of Arakhin too. A proof that the halakhah follows the sages regarding bekhor is that the sugya in the first chapter of Nedarim 13a and the first chapter of Nazir follows the sages of bekhor. So I found in Lechem Mishneh.
And we give, etc. Meaning: although in Chullin 131-132 we say gifts may be given even to a kohenet, and there Rav Kahana said he ate because of his wife, while in Pesachim chapter 3 Rav Kahana said, “if I had not married,” etc., implying he was not a kohen; and in Kiddushin 8a Rav Kahana took, etc. Tosafot there in their second answer explained that he took because of his wife, as with gifts, and so too in Pesachim there. But in Chullin there they wrote like the first answer, that there were two Rav Kahanas. The Rosh proved that pidyon haben is not given to a kohenet, for the Gemara in Chullin there asks against Ulla, etc., and answers, “Aharon,” etc.; and regarding pidyon it is written in Parashat Bamidbar, “and he gave the redemption money to Aharon and his sons.” Human firstborns and animal firstborns are compared to each other. Rosh, end of Bekhorot. What he wrote, “and they are compared,” I do not know where, for the Gemara there 4a says the comparison is only between human firstborns and impure-animal firstborns.
