(Siman 306, Taz s.k. 6) Regarding shearing and work. So the Perishah also wrote. It is clear that he holds that the main verse applies only to an unblemished animal, but with a blemished one it is only rabbinic. So too in the Levush, and so it is explicit in Sefer Yere'im of Rabbeinu Eliezer of Metz, mitzvah 352. But from the words of the Semag, Hilkhot Bekhorot positive commandment 211, and the Rambam, Hilkhot Bekhorot chapter 1 law 8, it is clear that they hold it is Torah law.
