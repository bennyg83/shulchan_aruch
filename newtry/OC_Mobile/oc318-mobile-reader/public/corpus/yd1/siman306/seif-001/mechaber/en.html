The law of a firstborn kosher animal, when and where it applies. It contains twelve seifim:
The law of a firstborn kosher animal applies to males but not females. It applies even when the Beit HaMikdash is not standing, both in Eretz Yisrael and outside Eretz Yisrael. It is a mitzvah to consecrate it and say, "This is sacred," as it is written, "You shall sanctify it to Hashem your God." If he did not expressly consecrate it, it becomes sacred automatically upon emerging from the womb. (It is given to a kohen, but not to a daughter of a kohen.) (Tur)
