The law of a firstborn kosher animal, when and where it applies. This siman contains twelve seifim.
The sanctity of a firstborn kosher animal applies to males but not females, even when the Temple is not standing, both in Eretz Yisrael and outside it. It is a mitzvah to consecrate it verbally, saying, “This is sacred,” as it is written, “You shall consecrate it to Hashem your God.” If one did not consecrate it, it becomes sacred automatically upon emerging from the womb. It is given to a kohen, not to a daughter of a kohen.
