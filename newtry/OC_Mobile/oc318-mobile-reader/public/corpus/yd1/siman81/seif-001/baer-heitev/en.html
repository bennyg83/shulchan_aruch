Its urine — Shach wrote: but donkey urine is forbidden to all even for a sick person without danger, unlike Bach who permits (and in Leket Yosher siman 214 he ruled to permit donkey milk for a sick person with heart disease mixed with the nation's illness because it is dangerous — see there).
Permitted — and even though we rule its flesh is forbidden and everything emerging, etc. — that is for a lav; human flesh is only a positive command; Shach wrote: nevertheless l'chatchila one should be stringent even on human urine because of "do not abominate yourselves"; Mechaber spoke only that it is permitted from the law, end.
