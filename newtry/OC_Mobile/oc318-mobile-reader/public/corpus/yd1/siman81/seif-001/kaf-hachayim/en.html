(1) [Seif 1] Milk of tamei beheimah and chayah, etc. — forbidden from Torah except no lashes. Rambam chapter 3 laws 4 and 6 — see there.
(3) There. Gravy and urine forbidden like flesh — emerging from tamei is tamei. Some permit — hold since not from body only from what entered body therefore permitted. Taz s.k. 1. Maharshal note 1.
(4) Gravy by salting falling into milk dish and cooked — from Torah forbidden in benefit. Shaar HaTziyun note 1; but Chatham Sofer Yoreh De'ah 81 wrote only rabbinic — see there; Beit Din 80 note 1 and siman 87 note 19; therefore permitted in benefit per his view.
(6) There. Urine forbidden. Some permit urine — implies all animal urine even donkey; Rambam's view; but not all poskim — donkey urine since thick like milk forbidden even for sick without danger. Shach s.k. 2. Peri Chadash note 2. Lechem HaPanim note 1. Beit Lechem Yehudah note 1. Shaar HaTziyun note 2; nevertheless do not protest one who rules leniently for sick without danger to drink urine of other animals. Peri Chadash there. Lechem HaPanim there. Beit Lechem Yehudah there. Shaar HaTziyun there.
(7) There. But human urine — all permit; nevertheless stringent because of "do not abominate"; but for sick even without danger permitted. Shach s.k. 3. Peri Chadash note 3. Lechem HaPanim note 2. Beit Lechem Yehudah note 2. Shaar HaTziyun note 3; and all for cure "do not abominate" does not apply. Mahachbar note 4. Zivchei Tzedek note 4; for any reason permitted — so practiced.
