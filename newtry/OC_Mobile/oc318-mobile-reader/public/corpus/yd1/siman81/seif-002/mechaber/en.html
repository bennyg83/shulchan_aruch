Cheeses made from beheimah milk and a treifah is found — if it is treifot that could not have happened to it except now (some versions: that it happened to it now is primary) — such as perforated brain membrane and the like — it is permitted, for we say: now it became treifah; but if it is known that before it was milked it became treifah — such as extra limb in which it became treifah, or the mouth of the wound scabbed over when it is known that three days before slaughter it was — all cheeses made from its milk are forbidden: {Rama: However regarding scabbed wound mouth one forbids only what was made from it within three days; but what was made from its milk before that is permitted (Semak and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Maharai in Hagahot ShaDa and Or Zarua general rule 49 and Agur); but regarding extra limb or other treifot coming from the belly — what was ever made from it is forbidden (Or Zarua there)} and if it was in a herd with other animals and its milk mixed with other milk — we follow the measure of sixty: {Rama: And if there are sixty animals in the herd and we do not know for certain that milk from the treifah is more than one other's milk — we say presumably there are sixty and it is permitted (his own reasoning); and specifically when the milk was mixed; but if the cheeses were mixed they are not nullified, for it is a piece fit to honor guests and even in a thousand it is not nullified (Teshuvat HaRosh and Arukh general rule 49 and Rashba responsum 517):} and if it became treifah through adhesion — we do not forbid cheeses made from its milk because it is a double doubt: {Rama: And some forbid even through adhesion what was milked within three days (Rashba and Ran) and so one should practice if there is not great loss:}
