Now — meaning close to its slaughter.
Scabbed over — Shach wrote: from Mechaber's words it implies regarding scabbed wound mouth one forbids retroactively forever until twelve months, for treifah does not live twelve months; uncertain on Rama who wrote in gloss "however regarding scabbed," etc. — he should have written "some say"; because Mechaber's language itself is not so decisive he wrote simply "however," etc., as is his way in many places.
Ever — Shach wrote: implies even before twelve months; and though treifah does not live twelve months — that is in doubtful treifot; but definite treifah — no; and though I wrote above regarding scabbed mouth even those who forbid everything retroactively forbid only until twelve months, though also definite treifah — there since we do not know for certain it happened before slaughter it is like doubtful treifah; but extra limb from belly born thus — we do not remove from its regularity.
Presumably — because after measure of sixty is rabbinic, for from Torah one in two nullifies, therefore we go leniently presumably, etc.; Taz wrote: and so when there is no sixty only we know there are other much better animals in the mixture, possibly there was sixty in the mixture — we go leniently as siman 98 seif 2 in same kind spilled.
The cheeses — meaning milk was not mixed together but each made cheese separately then cheeses mixed; but if milk already mixed then cheeses made from all — nullified (and if doubt whether made from it alone — lenient, for piece fit to honor is rabbinic — Taz). And one may ask from chapter 6 seif 5 treifah egg needs sixty-one to nullify because eggs large and small — why here sixty suffices; Taz answered: in eggs Rabbanan established one suffices in measure; here they were lenient from presumption all equal. Shach answered further: in eggs because it is a living creature they added one in measure. Taz wrote: if sixty animals in herd even if we did not check others, still we say majority animals presumed kosher. See siman 101 where Taz brings Maharshal that cheese is not called piece fit to honor and he challenged — see there.
Double doubt — Shach wrote: specifically in unspecified adhesions we forbid from doubt because we cannot be expert; but certainly there are many adhesions that are definitely treifot; nevertheless we rule even definite adhesion we permit cheeses before three days like scabbed mouth.
Great loss — Shach ruled even within three days even with great loss one should forbid, for unspecified adhesion is not less than three days; Mechaber did not lean lenient within three only before three; but other treifot like perforated brain membrane or not scabbed mouth — even within three days all permitted per Mechaber, unlike Maharshal who forbids all treifot within three days.
