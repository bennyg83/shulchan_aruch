The hand — Shach wrote: to exclude expressing on the breast or milking into mouth even without touching; and above siman 66 regarding human blood is opposite.
For sick — Shach wrote: sick not precise, rather any who is not healthy but weak.
Danger — appears if danger no longer exists must wean immediately and may not nurse four or five years, for return permitted only due to danger; unlike if weaned from illness unable to nurse then returned — may nurse four or five years; end Shach.
Pregnancy — Sefer Api Ravrevi writes simply if she became pregnant that year, pregnancy counts for the child (and so Taz and Peri Chadash as I wrote siman 57 twenty-four months — see there).
Forbidden things — meaning if she is sick in matter requiring feeding forbidden things, father should not give child to nurse from her but hire another Jewish wet-nurse.
Himself — meaning even though minor eating rabbinic prohibitions father not commanded to separate as Orach Chaim siman 543 — from law; nevertheless separate because harms in old age dulling heart and bad nature — end Shach.
