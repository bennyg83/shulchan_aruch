The Maharshal writes that these laws of a met mitzvah are not practiced nowadays because the land is not ours and we lack authority to bury in every place. Even if one buried the body there, there is concern that non-Jews would later remove it to strip its clothing or to degrade it. It is therefore taken to a designated Jewish cemetery.
