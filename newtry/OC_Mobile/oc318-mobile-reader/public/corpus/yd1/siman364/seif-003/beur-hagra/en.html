(Likkut) Between one field and another, etc. Eliyah Rabbah ch. 4, halakhah 33. (And likewise in the Yerushalmi ch. 7 of Nazir, but there there is an opinion: between a tree field and a vineyard — move it to the vineyard; and so Rambam and Sefer HaEshkol ruled.)
When is this, etc.; and it is not called, etc. Yerushalmi there: one who finds a met mitzvah — he tends to him and buries him in his place. When? When he found him outside the techum; but if he found him within the techum, he brings him to the cemetery and buries him there. R. Akiva said: this was my first service before the Sages — once, etc. And there: what does the verse teach by saying "you shall surely bury him"? From here that it is not considered a met mitzvah until his head and majority are present. And likewise taught in Evel Rabbati (so written in Torat HaAdam — but not in our text): it is never a met mitzvah until his head and majority are found. R. Yosi says: the spine and skull are his majority.
