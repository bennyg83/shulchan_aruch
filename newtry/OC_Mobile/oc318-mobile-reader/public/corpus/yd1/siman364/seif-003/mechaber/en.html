A met mitzvah that a person found in a field and buried there — even without the field-owner's knowledge — it is forbidden to move him, for met mitzvah acquires its place; and whoever finds him must bury him where he found him; and if he found him on the path that must be cleared because of the many lest they shelter over him — if he found him between an unplowed field and a plowed-but-unseeded field, he moves him to the unplowed field; between plowed-unseeded and seeded — to plowed-unseeded; between seeded and orchard — to seeded; between orchard and vineyard — to orchard; and if both are equal, he moves him to whichever is closer; and if both are equally close, he moves him to whichever he wishes. In what case? When he found him outside the techum; but if he found him within the techum, they bring him to the cemetery and he is not called met mitzvah unless he found his head and majority.
