(Shulchan Aruch seif 3) Even when his intent is to move it. The Ran, in his novellae to Sanhedrin, writes that if his intent is to move it, even if he built it for the dead, it is permitted. The case is where he initially designated it for the dead and did not place the dead there at that time, and afterward placed the dead there without another decision. If the first designation was made through an act of construction, it becomes forbidden through the later placement, even though he intends to move the dead. But if the designation was only by speech, it is not forbidden, because the designation was not made at the time of placement. However, if when placing the dead there he designated it for this, or had that intent in mind, it is forbidden even without an act.
