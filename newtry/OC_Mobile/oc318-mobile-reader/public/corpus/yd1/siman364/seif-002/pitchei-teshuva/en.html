To sit upon the stone. Yad Eliyahu 54 gives two reasons for the prohibition: deriving benefit and disrespect to the deceased. Walking over a grave provides no benefit, since walking on ordinary ground would be better, and is prohibited only out of respect; it may therefore be allowed for a mitzvah. Sitting on a grave provides actual benefit, because without the raised grave there would be no seat, and remains forbidden even for a mitzvah.
