For a woman who died in childbirth. Shemen Rokeach, part 2, responsum 13, discusses a pregnant woman who died and whose fetus could not be delivered. He finds no clear source for the agitation customarily made, and the Gemara implies that extracting the fetus after death is degradation. Burial should not ordinarily be delayed. Since current practice attempts every means of delivery, burial may be delayed until the third day while every possible noninvasive effort is made. If the fetus still does not emerge, she should be buried intact; her abdomen should not be opened and her dignity should not be violated.
