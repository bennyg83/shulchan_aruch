If they found a murdered Jew — they bury him as they found him, without shrouds, and they do not remove even his shoes: {Rama: And likewise they do for a woman who died in childbirth, or one who fell and died (Maharil siman 65); and some say they dress them in shrouds over their garments (Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of R' Yitzchak of Dura); and the custom is not to make shrouds for them like other dead — only bury them in their garments and over them a linen sheet like other dead.}
