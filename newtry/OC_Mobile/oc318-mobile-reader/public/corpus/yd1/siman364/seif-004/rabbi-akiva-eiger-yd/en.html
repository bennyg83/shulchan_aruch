(Shulchan Aruch seif 7) But another is permitted. Even if a dead person had already been buried there and was removed, it is permitted to bury another there, for this is not called benefit. Beit Hillel writes this in the name of the Rashba. But the language of the Tur at the beginning of the siman, in the name of Rabbeinu Yeshayah, does not sound this way. See Shevut Yaakov I siman 87; Maharikash forbids burying another there, while Radbaz II siman 747 assumes the view of the Rashba, and Davar Moshe siman 67 is also lenient.
