And some disagree and permit it. They maintain that the Gemara prohibited benefit only from a built grave. The Maharshal writes that the universal custom is not to sit upon a grave. Hagahot Ashri states that anything made for the needs or honor of the deceased is forbidden for benefit, and therefore a broken monument may not be sold. According to this, one may not lean upon a monument, and even walking upon graves would seem prohibited. Yet in Bava Batra 101 the Rashbam explains that pallbearers walked over the surrounding burial chambers when bringing the bier into the courtyard, because Rabbi Shimon did not object to temporary passage. This indicates that brief walking over graves in order to reach one’s destination is not prohibited.
