The earth that they took. Rabbi Akiva Eiger 45 writes that if other earth lies upon it and the prohibited earth may have been nullified in a majority of permitted earth, one may walk upon it even without certainty that the required ratio exists. He suggests that only the amount covering the deceased's face is prohibited as honoring the dead; earth above that may merely level the ground. During exhumation the soils mix and the permitted earth may form the majority. Taz and Magen Avraham in Orach Chaim 526 do not appear to agree. [Citation 50.]
