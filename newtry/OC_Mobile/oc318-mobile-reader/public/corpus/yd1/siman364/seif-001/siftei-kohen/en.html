A built grave, etc. This is derived from the verse, 'He cast its dust upon the grave of the common people,' which compares a grave to idolatry. Just as something attached to the ground is not prohibited as idolatry, as stated above in siman 145, so a grave formed in the ground is not prohibited. A built grave, however, consists of detached materials later attached, and is forbidden like a structure built for idolatry, as explained in siman 145:3.
