To accept. The reason is in Beit Yosef — for this one can forbid his property upon him against his will; and if so, what need is there for him to accept his words? And Turei Zahav wrote this is astonishing, for if so he should have written a stringency — even if he protests explicitly and refuses to accept his words, nevertheless it applies against his will; and why did he write he need not accept — meaning that he is silent. And it appears this is the explanation — if Reuven forbids Shimon from benefiting from his own property, Shimon need not accept his words (and in Nekudot HaKesef he wrote this explanation is not clear, for it is difficult where he speaks; but when his fellow forbids him, the explanation is he cannot forbid him, etc.; rather the matter is simple as Beit Yosef explained — and one must force an explanation, for since he mentioned at the beginning acceptance of words, etc., see there).
