If he placed leaven from dough from which challah was not taken into dough from which challah was taken, and all are one species—if he has flour or dough from which challah was not taken, he separates from it upon this leaven proportionally—meaning one forty-eighth in the leaven (and even if this has no measure of challah, provided he leaves it with the large dough (as implied in Terumat HaDeshen siman 100)). And if not, he separates one forty-eighth from all the dough, since all of it became tevel. And the same law if the leaven is exempt and the dough is obligated. {Rama: Nevertheless l'chatchila he should not rely on this and should not place exempt leaven in obligated dough (Mordechai, chapter Makom SheNahagu). But if he already transgressed and did so, it is permitted l'chatchila to take from that dough and place in another dough to be leaven (Terumat HaDeshen siman 100).}
