Forty-three eggs and one fifth of an egg, etc. Like the numerical value of challah. To allude to the fifth, there is a letter heh at the end of the word challah, for it is impossible to write five in the number forty-three except in the word challah: as soon as you take the number five in your hand, the number thirty-eight remains in your hand, making challah.

Mahari Weil likewise wrote in siman 153 in the laws of Pesach that the measure for challah is a vessel holding a little less than three zeidel, and a zeidel is a quart.
