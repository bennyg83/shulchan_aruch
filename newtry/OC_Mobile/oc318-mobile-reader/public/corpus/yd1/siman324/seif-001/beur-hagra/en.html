The measure, etc. Eruvin, chapter 3.
That flour, etc. Chapter 2 states, "five quarters of a kav of flour," indicating that the measurement is taken in flour. Furthermore, the statement, "they and their bran and coarse bran," would be obvious if measured as grain. The reference in chapter 1 to less than five quarters in grain is phrased that way because of the other species. See Rash, chapter 1, Mishnah 4, the Rosh there, and my comments in Orach Chaim at the beginning of siman 456.
When filling, etc. See Orach Chaim there.
Its weight, etc. See there.
A vessel that holds, etc. Rambam, based on Pesachim 109a concerning the measure of a reviis.
All fingerbreadths, etc. As stated in Menachos 41b, "a Torah-law handbreadth," and in Bechoros 39b, where the Tanna discusses a fingerbreadth. See Rashi there.
