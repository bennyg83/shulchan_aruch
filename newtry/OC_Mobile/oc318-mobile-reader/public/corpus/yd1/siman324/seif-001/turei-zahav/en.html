They fill it with flour. This is the opinion of the Rashba, and he disagrees with the Rosh, who holds that we measure according to the grain, like the omer of manna; automatically with flour he needs more; and therefore he wrote that one should measure with flour heaped. And the Rashba wrote that if so it is impossible for us to know how much was added in the manna after it was ground in the mill; rather certainly we measure only with flour, and therefore heaping is not needed.
