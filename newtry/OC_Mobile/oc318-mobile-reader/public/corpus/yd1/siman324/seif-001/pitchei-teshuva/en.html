Containing forty-three eggs. See Tzelach on Pesachim 116b, who writes that actual measurement demonstrated to him that the eggs commonly found in his locale were only half the volume of the eggs on which Torah measurements are based. He therefore instructed that when flour measured only forty-three contemporary eggs one should separate challah without a blessing, and should not recite a blessing until the measure equaled eighty-six contemporary eggs. I recall seeing the same in the practices of the Vilna Gaon. See Beit Ephraim on treifot, in the responsa section, who discusses this extensively.
