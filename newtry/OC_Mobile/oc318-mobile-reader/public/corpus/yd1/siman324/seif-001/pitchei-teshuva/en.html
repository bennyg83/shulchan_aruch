Containing forty-three eggs. See Tzelach on Pesachim 116b. He writes that his measurements established that the eggs common in our times are only half the size of the eggs used for Torah measures. He therefore instructed that with a measure containing forty-three of our eggs one should separate challah without a blessing, and only when the dough reaches the measure of eighty-six of our eggs should a blessing be recited. I believe I saw the same in the practices of the Vilna Gaon. See Beit Ephraim on treifot, in the responsa section, where he discusses this at length.
