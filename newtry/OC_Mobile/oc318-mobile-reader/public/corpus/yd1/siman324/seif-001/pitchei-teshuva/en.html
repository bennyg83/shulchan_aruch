That holds forty-three eggs. See Tzelach on Pesachim 116b, who wrote that it became clear to him by measurement that the eggs commonly found among us are such that our whole egg is only half of the eggs by which the Torah measures are calculated. Therefore he would warn that for a measure holding forty-three of our eggs, one should separate challah without a blessing, and only when it reaches the measure of eighty-six of our eggs should one recite a blessing; see there. It seems that I also saw this written in the practices of the Gra z"l. See Beit Ephraim on treifot, in the responsa pamphlet, where he discusses this matter at length; see there.
