If it has the taste of grain, it is obligated in challah. Rice is drawn after wheat; so wrote the Atteret Zahav. According to this, it implies that with wheat and rice we follow taste, as the Rashba wrote. But from the Tur it is explicit that the same applies to all five species of grain, and so it is implied in the Asheri, who made the reason depend on the fact that taste is like substance by Torah law.
