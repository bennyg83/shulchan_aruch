(Shach s.k. 18) I challenged the Perishah, but perhaps this is what the Perishah means: specifically leaven from dough, meaning from a large dough that has the measure requiring challah and already became obligated in challah, but its challah was not separated. But a small dough that does not have the measure for challah and became mixed in does not render it tevel, for the dough itself is not obligated in challah. Yet his language is very confused, for he should not have distinguished between leaven and dough, since leaven from a small dough also does not render it tevel, while dough from a large dough does render it tevel. Rather, he should have said: it appears that this is only from a large dough, but from a small dough that was not obligated so long as it lacks the measure, it does not render it tevel. Perhaps there is a scribal error in his words.
