(Siman 324, in Taz seif katan 5) He wondered at Derishah: how can one separate three challot, etc.? Ateret Zahav and Maadanei Yom Tov at the beginning of the laws of challah write that he should separate three challot. Bach, however, writes that spelt is exempt. It may be that Ateret Zahav and Maadanei Yom Tov speak where the combination with the spelt produces the quantity of three challot. Examine carefully.
