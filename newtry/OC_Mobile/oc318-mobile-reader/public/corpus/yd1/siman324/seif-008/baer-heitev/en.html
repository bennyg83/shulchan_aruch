There is. This is rabbinic, due to a decree lest one separate from one dough for another because they are of the same type. The Beit Yosef makes clear that one must separate for each dough from another dough from which challah has not yet been removed. This is correct, for since each dough is obligated by Torah law, a rabbinic decree cannot uproot the Torah prohibition and exempt it from challah. This is the Shach.
