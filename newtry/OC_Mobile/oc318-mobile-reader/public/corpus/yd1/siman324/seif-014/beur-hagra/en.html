Pesachim 48b likewise rejects a stringency that leads to leniency. In earlier times, when dough could be prepared in purity, this prohibition applied fully. Nowadays, however, small portions may be used where necessary so the law of challah not be forgotten, as indicated by the Mishnah concerning the three lands.
