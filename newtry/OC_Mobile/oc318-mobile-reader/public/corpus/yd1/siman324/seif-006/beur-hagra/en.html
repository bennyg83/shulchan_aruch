(Likkut) Or a kav belonging to a non-Jew. This applies specifically when [the doughs] are separated by a bite (nashukh), but when they are blended (balul) it does not interrupt — as stated in siman 330, seif 3, and as stated in the Yerushalmi (ch. 3) there, cited by the Rashbam and the Rosh there: "He should make a kav from here, etc." (end of quote).
