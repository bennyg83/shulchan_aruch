(Shulchan Aruch, seif 12) Concerning those loaves that became mixed. The Rosh, rule 2, responsum 2, distinguishes the case from Menachot 31: when untithed produce becomes mixed, its Torah prohibition is nullified; regarding challah, sourdough imparts flavor and is not nullified, and the loaves are significant and are not nullified. This is difficult, because the rule that significant loaves are not nullified is itself rabbinic. The matter requires study.
