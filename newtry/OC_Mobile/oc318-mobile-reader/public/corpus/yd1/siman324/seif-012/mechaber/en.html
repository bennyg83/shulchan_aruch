One for whom bread from which he separated challah became mixed with bread from which he did not separate—if he has flour, he makes dough of five quarters and mixes it with the loaves in a vessel and separates from it for those loaves that became mixed. And if he wishes to separate from it itself, he must separate a little from each loaf and loaf; or he mixes them all in one vessel and separates from each one upon all until he separates according to the calculation of the loaves—and one more; such as if five from which challah was taken became mixed in ten from which challah was not taken, he separates from six, for then he certainly separated from one upon which challah was not taken. {Rama: A baker who spreads a pie from dough from which challah was not taken and meat is inside—it is permitted to separate from another dough obligated in challah upon this one, and the taste of the dough entering the meat is also released (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum).}
