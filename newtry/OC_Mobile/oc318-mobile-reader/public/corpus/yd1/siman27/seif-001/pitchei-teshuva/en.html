See Taz, who brought Tur's words that it is permitted to invite a non-Jew for intestines; and Peri Chadash's view: even if it became neveilah through shechitah, permitted to sell intestines; but Turei Zahav disputes and holds: if it became neveilah through shechitah — forbidden; and so Peri Megadim in the introduction: if he paused or pressed, etc., or a defective knife — forbidden to sell intestines to a non-Jew, for "is there anything" does not apply; and likewise in perforated esophagus and crop severance. However, in the four neveilot that impart from life — permitted — see there. And see in responsum of Brit Avraham, part Yoreh Deah, simanim 23 through 27, asked on this matter regarding geese that are fattened and become treifah from perforation of esophagus, which is neveilah — how do we sell the geese with intestines to a non-Jew; he raised several correct reasons to permit; and clear from his words: in an animal that became neveilah through shechitah one may not permit — only in a bird there is to permit — see there. [Also in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 19 he brought correct reasons to uphold Israel's custom that they are not careful in this, and also in animal intestines there is no concern — see there.] And see Shach, s.k. 2, what he wrote: but where it was not slaughtered properly — it is obvious, since forbidden to Israel, etc.; and see in Dagul MeRevavah, who wrote: nevertheless permitted to sell them blood that comes out at the time of shechitah while convulsing, even though "is there anything" does not apply; and nevertheless a non-Jew is not commanded on blood from life, as explained in Rambam, chapter 9 of Hilchot Melachim — see there. And see in responsum of Emunat Shmuel siman 14 and responsum of Mekom Shmuel siman 54.
