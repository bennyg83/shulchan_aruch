(Siman 27, Taz, small seif 1) And my brother Mahar R' Yehudah Halevi answered, etc. It is already explained in the gloss of Derishah — see there; he further wrote practical difference emerges regarding oto ve'et beno; and so Beit Chadash. It further appears to me practical difference emerges regarding one suspected of eating treifot — he is not suspected of eating neveilot, which are more severe; and as below siman 119 — see there and examine well.
