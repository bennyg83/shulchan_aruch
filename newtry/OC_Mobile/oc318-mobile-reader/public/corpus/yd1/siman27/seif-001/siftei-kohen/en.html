[Forbidden to eat, etc. Because it is written: you shall not eat upon the blood; and Chazal expounded it is forbidden to eat from an animal before its soul departs; but after it died, it is permitted to eat from it even what was cut from it before it died.]
[Until the animal dies. But after it died, it is permitted to eat it and feed it to a non-Jew — end; Tur; and this refers to the first clause: slaughtered properly; but where it was not slaughtered properly — it is obvious: since forbidden to Israel because of eiver min hachai, since he cut from it before its soul departed — also for a non-Jew forbidden, for a son of Noah is commanded regarding eiver min hachai. And likewise where he cut from a tamei animal while still convulsing, even though he slaughtered it properly — forbidden to give to sons of Noah, for shechitah does not apply to a tamei animal. And so Rashba in Tur HaDeshen, the long one, daf 30, end 2b; and thus: and to invite a non-Jew for intestines, or for flesh separating from the animal before its soul departs — if a tahor animal, which is subject to shechitah, and was slaughtered by Israel, who is subject to shechitah — permitted; but in a tamei animal, even by Israel; and if also in a tahor animal by a non-Jew — flesh separating from it before its soul departs — forbidden to invite the non-Jew for it, and even after the animal's soul departs. And although Rambam, may his memory be blessed, ruled in Sefer Shoftim (in a tahor animal too) for Israel permitted and for a non-Jew forbidden — I do not know the reason for his words, etc. — end; and so Kesef Mishneh, chapter 9 of Hilchot Shoftim; and so in the Talmud and Rashi, chapter HaOr VeHaRotav (Chullin daf 121b). And Maharshal wrote, chapter HaShochet, end siman 17: in an animal of a non-Jew, even if Israel slaughtered it — nevertheless, since he was not commanded regarding shechitah — in his animal shechitah did not permit it for him, and its law is as if a non-Jew slaughtered it; and with this Rama's words are settled, and Semag — see there; and see above siman 62, s.k. 3.]
