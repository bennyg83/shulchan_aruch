Like a synagogue, etc. This is the strict law. Because of the enactment, however, Beit Yosef cites Orchot Chaim that Ra'avad and all the sages of his generation ruled that no person can prohibit his share of a synagogue or of sacred books. If he prohibited it, his prohibition is ineffective. Bach writes likewise, as does the Mechaber in Orach Chayyim, siman 153, seif 15.
And some say, etc. Regarding anything that is not subject to the law of division, meaning a synagogue and the like, there is no law of division and it is permitted, as stated below in siman 226. Maharshal rules likewise in the chapter Shor Shenagach Et HaParah, siman 32, and this appears to be the primary view in the Gemara.
