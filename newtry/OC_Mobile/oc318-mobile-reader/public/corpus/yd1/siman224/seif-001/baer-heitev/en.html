Their enactment. This is the law in principle. As a communal enactment, however, Beit Yosef writes that Ra'avad and all the sages of his generation ruled that a person cannot prohibit his share in the synagogue or sacred books, and if he attempted to do so his prohibition is ineffective. Bach rules likewise. See Orach Chayyim 153:15. Shach. See siman 153.
Division — meaning in the synagogue and the like there is no law of division, it is permitted; and so appears main in the Gemara — Shach; but Taz wrote that in the Gemara it implies in the synagogue there is law of division and it is forbidden, like the first opinion the author wrote; see there and see Orach Chayim siman 153 seif 17.
