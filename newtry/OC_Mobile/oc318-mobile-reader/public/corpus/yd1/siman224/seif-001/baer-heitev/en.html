Their enactment. This is according to the law, but by enactment, as the Beit Yosef wrote, the Ra'avad and all the sages of his generation ruled that a person cannot forbid his share in a synagogue or in books, and if he forbade it, his prohibition is not a prohibition. So too wrote the Bach. See Orach Chayim siman 153 seif 15; end quote, Shach.
Division. Meaning that a synagogue and the like do not have the law of division, so it is permitted; and so appears primary in the Talmud. Shach. But the Taz wrote that in the Talmud it implies that a synagogue does have the law of division and is forbidden, like the first view written by the Mechaber; see there. See Orach Chayim siman 153 seif 17.
