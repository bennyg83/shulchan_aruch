One who said "your property is cherem upon me"; it contains one seif. Reuven who said to Shimon, "I am upon you cherem," or "Behold, you are forbidden my benefit" — Reuven is permitted benefit from Shimon, and Shimon is forbidden benefit from Reuven. And if he said to Shimon, "Behold, you are upon me cherem" — Reuven is forbidden benefit from Shimon, and Shimon is permitted benefit from Reuven. "I am upon you cherem and you upon me" — both are forbidden to benefit from one another. And they are permitted in things that are in partnership for all Israel, such as the Temple Mount and the courtyards; and they are forbidden in things that are in partnership for the members of that city, such as the synagogue, the ark, and the books. And what is their remedy? That they transfer their share that they have in it to another, and it is found that neither of them benefits from that of his fellow. And there is one who says that they are permitted in everything that has no law of division.
{Rama: And one who lent his house to the synagogue — there is in the enactment of Rabbenu Gershom that he is not permitted to forbid it upon one member of the congregation unless he forbids it upon all of them (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim and Kol Bo, siman 116).}
