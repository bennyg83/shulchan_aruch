The majority. And Taz wrote in the name of Maharil: fitting to be stringent that the slaughterer pass his hands within the shechitah cut and feel with touch whether he slaughtered properly, for by sight one cannot check well, since the slaughter-house is full of blood — end.
Forbidden. And Shach wrote: it is neveilah — an animal in its lifetime stands in presumption of prohibition until you know it was slaughtered properly.
Treifot. And Shach ruled l'chatchila one must check after shemuta; and so Peri Chadash.
The cut. This gloss belongs above: before; and there is one who says it refers to slaughtering the majority — we are not concerned lest through convulsion the cut widened. And Bach distinguished between where the entire siman is found slaughtered and where only the majority is found — where only the majority is found, we are concerned. And Shach wrote he has a tradition that certainly the trachea's way is to separate, and we are concerned that easily the majority is made through its convulsion; but when the esophagus is found slaughtered — kosher, for its way is not to tear except in length and not in width — end. And Peri Chadash rejects their words; and the essence is to permit in every case — see Taz. And Hagahot Avraham haChasid wrote in the name of Avraham haChasid: do not check through feathers, lest the majority was not slaughtered, and we require the majority visible to the eye; and further, even if he slaughtered the minority the feather tears and it is forbidden.
