Forbidden. — And it is neveilah — an animal in its lifetime stands in presumption of prohibition and is not permitted until you know it was slaughtered properly — Talmud.
However, one need not check after shemuta, etc. — And in the books I brought up: l'chatchila one should also check after shemuta, as Beit HaGadol holds — only b'dieved if he did not check it is permitted, and it is comparable to the lung, in which treifot are common and one must check it l'chatchila, and b'dieved if it was lost without examination it is kosher, as below siman 39; and the same here — it is common to make shemuta at the time of shechitah, as implied from what is stated in the Talmud and poskim regarding one who slaughters a chicken, at the end of the section, before this; and so Ra'ah wrote explicitly in Sefer HaChinuch, mitzvah 400 — see there. However, since an animal in its lifetime is not in presumption that the simanim were uprooted — one view: if he did not see that the majority were slaughtered, there — an animal in its lifetime stands in presumption that it is not slaughtered, unlike here — and it is kosher b'dieved, as the poskim wrote; and I wrote that Ran too did not disagree with Beit HaGadol except in what he forbade even b'dieved if he did not check after shemuta. And also the view of R' Yonah and Avi haEzri, as Beit HaGadol — were brought in Ra'osh's responsum, klal 20 siman 13; and many times we find that Tosafot and all poskim were concerned for Beit HaGadol's words even where his reason does not seem correct, even to be lenient, because all his words are words of tradition; and all the more so here, where he is stringent and his reason is sound — and all the more so since the great poskim mentioned agree with his view (and even though he forbade even b'dieved, that is because he follows his reasoning that below siman 39 the lung too is forbidden b'dieved; but we hold: lung — l'chatchila requires examination, and b'dieved kosher — so too is the law here). And so it appears in old Hagahot Shechitot that Darkei Moshe brought: one must check after shemuta; and so in later shechitot.
