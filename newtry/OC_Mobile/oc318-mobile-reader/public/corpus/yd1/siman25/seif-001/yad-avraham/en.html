(Siman 25, seif 1) One who slaughters must check the simanim after shechitah, etc.; and if he did not see that the majority were slaughtered — forbidden. And Shach wrote: it is neveilah — an animal in its lifetime stands in presumption of prohibition, etc. And see in Turei Zahav, who wrote: since there is in it prohibition of neveilah, it is permitted to slaughter oto ve'et beno on day one. And in Mekom Shmuel he wrote: forbidden. And so in Peri Megadim: forbidden to slaughter the second on that day, since in any case he purifies from neveilah — it is like treifah, which is shechitah for oto ve'et beno. And in my humble opinion the essence is like Turei Zahav: it is not comparable to treifah, on which there is no name neveilah at all; therefore, even though forbidden in eating, its shechitah is valid shechitah, for we hold: shechitah that is not fit — its name is shechitah. But in this case here, the name neveilah applies regarding eggs and lashes; even according to Turei Zahav, who holds it is not neveilah to impart by carrying — nevertheless it is neveilah regarding prohibition, and certainly it is not shechitah. And so it is reasonable: that which "shechitah that is not fit is shechitah" — we learn from slaughter of outsiders in Chullin (daf 85), and there it was not neveilah at all, as proven in Bechorot (daf 40). But whence do we have that where the name neveilah applies, it is called shechitah? And further: it is not better than arifat eglah, for it appears from Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchot Avot HaTumah, that the name neveilah does not apply to it, and nevertheless one is not liable for oto ve'et beno except in slaughtering and not in breaking the neck, as proven in Ein Yaakov; and so the commentator on Yerushalmi, chapter 9 of Sotah; and all the more here, where it is in presumption it is not slaughtered regarding prohibition.
And Rambam's words appear contradictory: here he wrote it is certainly neveilah even though it is doubt; and in chapter 3 of Hilchot Shechitah, law 18, he wrote: any doubt in shechitah — behold it is doubt of neveilah. And according to what Peri Megadim wrote, siman 18 and end of siman 25: what Rambam wrote, that any doubt in shechitah is doubt of neveilah — that is specifically only in doubt where he paused and pressed — majority of slaughterers properly, and they do not pause and press, and we say rely on minority near presumption that an animal in its lifetime, etc., and it becomes half and half; this doubt is balanced — we establish it on presumption and it is like certainty; and the same here is balanced doubt, for there is no majority of slaughterers who slaughter majority of simanim — see there.
And according to what he wrote — one may learn: in doubt of examination one need not cover, since we consider it like neveilah certainly, even leniently, and we permit slaughtering oto ve'et beno. And this is not like what is written in Binat Adam, who requires covering in doubt of examining simanim.
