However, one need not check; and Shach was lengthy l'chatchila according to Beit HaGadol to check, for it is a minority that is common; and one may also say: rely on minority near presumption. And indeed see Maharsha on Chullin: they derive from a cow that "near minority" is only rabbinic; if so, there is room to be lenient, since one may also say: Rashi's question on the lung — yesterday we ate from its milk and we rely on the majority — how can we require examination according to Beit HaGadol's view that milk of ikkur is permitted? But for Rashi the treifah question stands in its place. And one may analyze: since we ruled above that if the majority was uprooted and the minority connected a little here and a little there — treifah; what does pressing with the finger help? For it cannot come out, since it is connected in part — nevertheless in such a case treifah; and we are concerned for the minority l'chatchila here, since there is presumption of prohibition; and one must say: on all this, that it is minority of a minority — we are not concerned; and it requires study.
And we are not concerned lest through convulsion, etc. — see the latter authorities who were lengthy; and b'dieved certainly kosher; but l'chatchila Maharik's view too is to permit from that R' Zeira checked arrows; and the latter authorities distinguish between slaughtered entirely, or majority, or trachea, or esophagus. And it seems the dispute hangs on Rashi and Rashba: Rashi holds therefore he should not slaughter first at night, lest he not slaughter the majority; and it is difficult — he can see in the morning by lamp-light that the majority was slaughtered; one must say lest through convulsion it widened. And it implies l'chatchila we are concerned; but according to Rashba, who was not concerned for this but lest he delay, etc. — it implies we are not concerned that through convulsion it will widen; on this one may rely on Rashba.
