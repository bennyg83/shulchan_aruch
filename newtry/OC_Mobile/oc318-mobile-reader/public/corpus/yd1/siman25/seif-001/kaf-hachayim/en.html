(1) [Seif 1] One who slaughters must check the simanim after shechitah whether the majority were slaughtered, etc. Even if the slaughterer was quick and fast. Rambam, chapter 1 of Hilchot Shechitah, law 12. Peri Chadash, part 1; Shemirat Shabbat KeHilchatah, part 2; Zivchei Tzedek, part 3; Kometz, part 1; Minchat Yitzchak, part 2; Zivchei Tzedek, part 1. And even if he did holacha and hava'ah at the place of the simanim — he must also check. Zivchei Tzedek there in the name of Ra'osh's responsum. Zivchei Tzedek there.
(2) Fitting and proper to pass his hands within the shechitah cut of the animal to feel by touch whether he slaughtered properly, for by sight one cannot check well, since the slaughter-house is full of blood. Taz, s.k. 1, in the name of Maharil. Olah Yitzchak in the name of the above for the first paragraph, part 1; Zivchei Tzedek, part 1; Ahel Yitzchak, siman 10, in Leket Yosher, part 1; Zivchei Riv, siman 9, on temidim, part 2. But one should not rely l'chatchila on touch of the hand alone — rather we require both sight and touch of the hand. Turei Zahav, siman 1, part 68. And now slaughterers practice: after slaughtering the animal he inserts his finger in the slaughter-house and removes the trachea and esophagus and sees whether they were slaughtered properly — with this there is no further concern.
(3) Slaughterer who checks — his way was to check animal simanim by passing his hand to the slaughter-house and feeling only the spinal column; one may not rely on this examination, for examination requires seeing with his eyes that both simanim were slaughtered, or feeling with his hands on the simanim themselves — unlike the aforementioned examination where he did not touch the simanim at all, for possibly the simanim are at the sides below where he passed his hand; and even though there is an indication from the regular place of the simanim that they are never in the animal beyond where he passed his hand — nevertheless one may not rely on this; and l'chatchila it is simple one may not rely on this — only one must check by touch of both simanim in their cut entirely, or by clear sight. Dagul MeRevavah, part 2; Zivchei Tzedek, part 3.
