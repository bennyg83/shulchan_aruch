See Ba'er Heitev; and see in Shach the reason why b'dieved it is kosher: since an animal in its lifetime is not in presumption that its simanim were uprooted, it is not comparable to where he did not see that the majority were slaughtered — there an animal in its lifetime stands in presumption it is not slaughtered, unlike here. And see in Dagul MeRevavah, who wrote: specifically where there is no irregularity before us; but if there is some irregularity before us, we do not attribute to be lenient; and for this reason they are stringent in doubt and it is called doubt in shechitah — see there.
