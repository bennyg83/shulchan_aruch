(Tur gloss, siman 26, seif 1) The esophagus was perforated, etc. So Tosafot, Ra'osh, and Ran proved, as explained in Beit Yosef; and Maharshal proved: behold here it would be treifah if not that perforation preceded redness; until here his words. And it is difficult: behold here too in perforation, perforation preceded the perforation — rather, even though perforation preceded redness, it is forbidden because of the sides if not that the slaughter-house is wide and it is not singed at the sides; but if it was perforated opposite what was already slaughtered — the slaughter-house being wide does not apply at all — treifah; and this is simple — end.
