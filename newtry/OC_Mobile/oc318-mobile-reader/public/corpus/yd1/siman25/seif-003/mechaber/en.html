It was slaughtered properly and a wolf came and took the intestines and returned them when they were perforated — kosher; and we are not concerned lest at the place of the perforation it was perforated. {Rama: But before we know it was slaughtered properly it stands in presumption of prohibition (Tur according to the Gemara); and any doubt that occurred in shechitah — treifah (Mahariu, laws of shechitah, and it is in the Talmud Chullin daf 10 and 28, and agreed by all poskim in several places).}
