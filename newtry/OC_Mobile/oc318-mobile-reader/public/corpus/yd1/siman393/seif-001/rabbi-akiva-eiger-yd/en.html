(Siman 393, seif 1, gloss) And part of the third day. See Chochmat Adam, Kuntres Matzevet Moshe, letter 4.
(There) to go out at night. After a foot from the market:
(There, gloss) The mourner goes to the synagogue. The law of a kohen mourner who wishes to perform pidyon haben within the first three days is analogous to that of a mohel. If other kohanim are available in the city, he should not go out. After three days he may. Knesset HaGedolah in his work Ba'ei Chayei, Yoreh Deah siman 241.
