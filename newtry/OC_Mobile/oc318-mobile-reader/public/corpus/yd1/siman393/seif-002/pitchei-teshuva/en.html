To the synagogue. Chochmas Adam, in the booklet Matzeves Moshe, siman 8, writes that this is based upon the ruling in siman 376 that there is a mitzvah to pray in the mourner's home. Thus, where ten men are present there, the mourner should pray at home. But where it is impossible to assemble ten and he would thereby lose Kaddish, Kedushah, and Barechu, he may go to the synagogue. However, Magen Avraham, siman 696, seif katan 8 and 10, implies that he should pray alone. Therefore, if a minyan is being held opposite his house in the same courtyard, all opinions permit him to attend. See there.
