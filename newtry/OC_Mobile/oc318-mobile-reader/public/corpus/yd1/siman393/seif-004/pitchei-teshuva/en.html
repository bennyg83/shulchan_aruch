And to trim for the purpose of the milah. See Be'er HaGolah. See the responsa of my great-grandfather Panim Meiros volume 3 siman 57, who also wrote that the Rama refers to his fingernails. He explained that one is permitted to trim them even with scissors — for the poskim wrote that with a ginostri it is forbidden, meaning with scissors; this teaches us that for the purpose of a mitzvah, it is permitted even with scissors. See there further.
