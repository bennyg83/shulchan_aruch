(Ibid., gloss) The mourner goes to the synagogue. The law governing a mohel likewise applies to a kohen who is in mourning and wishes to redeem a firstborn son within the first three days. He should not go out, since other kohanim are present in the city. After three days he may go out. Knesset HaGedolah in Baei Chayei, Yoreh Deah, siman 241.
