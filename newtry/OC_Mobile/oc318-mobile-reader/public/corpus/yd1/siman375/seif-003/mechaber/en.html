If they move the dead from grave to grave — if they buried him in the first grave on condition to remain buried there forever, but afterward decided to move him — they do not count for him except from when he was buried in the first grave, even if they cleared him within seven; but if initially they buried him on condition to move when opportunity arises — mourning begins immediately; and if they cleared him within seven, they return and count from when he is buried a second time; and if they did not clear him until after seven, his mourning already passed and they do not mourn for him again; and if initially they intended to move him from there within seven — they do not begin counting until he is buried in the second grave.
