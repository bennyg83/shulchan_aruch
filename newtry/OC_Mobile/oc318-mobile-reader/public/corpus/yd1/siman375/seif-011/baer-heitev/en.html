Who prayed. If the mourner himself has not yet prayed Maariv, Maharshal and the Bach rule that the day counts toward shivah even if the congregation already prayed Maariv. Although some hold regarding a niddah that she follows the congregation, women generally did not pray and therefore followed the communal day. Here the mourner may say that for him it is still daytime. Alternatively, there the woman sought to create a leniency for herself after the congregation accepted night, whereas here mourning came upon him from Heaven while it was still day. Accordingly, the same day would count even for a woman who had not prayed. This is the primary ruling. If burial occurred shortly before Shabbat and the mourners immediately turned toward the synagogue without sitting on the ground, Friday nevertheless counts as the first day, because turning away from the grave begins the mourning. Raanach II, siman 51. See simanim 51 and 196.
