One who prayed. See the new Responsa Radbaz, siman 268, where it is explained that one who hears a recent report of a death during twilight counts twilight as part of the preceding day and begins the mourning count from that day.
He counts from the following day. See Responsa Chinuch Beit Yehudah, siman 83, who writes that this applies specifically to mourning. For a yahrzeit, if death occurred after the evening prayer while it was still clearly daytime, whether on a weekday or Friday, the yahrzeit is fixed according to the civil halachic day that had not yet ended, meaning the preceding calendar date; see there.
From the following day. Regarding whether he wears tefillin on that following day, see what I write below, siman 388.
