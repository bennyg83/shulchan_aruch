(Seif 2) And if the head of the family goes with him. This means that he goes to the place where the mourner resides and remains there. If the head of the family returns to them, however, even on the seventh day, he counts together with them from the time they turned their faces away from the deceased. See Shach below, seif katan 12.
(Shach, seif katan 1) On the eve of the festival. See Responsa Raanach, part 2, siman 52, and Radbaz, siman 53.
