Who is here. The Shach wrote: from the Ramban's words it is clear that this is specifically if he can come to the grave within three days; but if he cannot come within three days, those at home count immediately, and the head of the household who is at the cemetery counts from the closing of the grave, and they are not drawn after him; end quote. Specifically for stringency do we follow the head of the family, so that they do not count until burial; but if the head of the family is with those who remained at home, it neither raises nor lowers the matter, and nevertheless those accompanying the dead count only from burial. But the Tur's view is that everything follows the head of the household: if he remained here, both groups count from when the dead left. So too appears to be the Ramban's view, etc.; see there.
