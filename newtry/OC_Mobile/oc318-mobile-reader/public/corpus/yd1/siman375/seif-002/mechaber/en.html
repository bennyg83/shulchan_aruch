One whose custom is to send the dead to another country to bury him and they do not know when he will be buried — from when they turn back from escorting, they begin counting seven and thirty and begin mourning (Rambam ch. 1 of Mourning halacha 6); and those who go with him count from when he is buried; and if the family head went with him, even those here do not count until he is buried; and the family head — meaning the household head whom all rely on and all are drawn after him — whether brother, whether minor son; and some say provided he is thirteen.
