From burying him. The Taz wrote: an incident occurred with a Jew who was killed on the tenth of Tevet and thrown into the Rhine River. Before Adar, his son gave up searching for him further, and Rabbi Yaakov ruled that from then he should count seven and thirty. After the days of mourning passed, his father was found in the river and brought to the cemetery, and the elder ruled that he should only tear; the Ramban agreed with him; end quote.
