Or from the time they despaired of burying him. A Jew was murdered and cast into the Rhine on the tenth of Tevet. Before Adar his son despaired of finding him. Rabbi Yaakov ruled that he should then begin counting the seven and thirty days. After the mourning period had passed, the father was found in the river and brought to the cemetery. The elder ruled that the son need only rend his garment, and the Ramban agreed.
