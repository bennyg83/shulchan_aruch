When mourning begins, and for whom they move from grave to grave. In it are 11 seifim. From when does mourning begin? From when he is buried and sealing the grave with earth is finished — mourning begins immediately and he wraps his head; but he does not remove his shoes until he reaches his house (Ramban); and now the custom is to remove shoes immediately after sealing the golel, as in siman 376: {Rama: (and see above siman 372); if he thought the grave was sealed and began mourning and afterward learned he erred — he returns and begins mourning anew (Rosh klal 27).}
