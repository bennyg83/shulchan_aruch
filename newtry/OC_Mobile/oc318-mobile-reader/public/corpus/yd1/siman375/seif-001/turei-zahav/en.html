The sealing of the grave with earth. What the Gemara says, “until the golel is sealed,” Rashi explained as the cover of the coffin. The Ramban explained that this is when they place him in a coffin and seal the cover with boards, intending afterward to place that coffin in a niche or grave; then one counts from the sealing of the coffin. But if they carry him out on a bier or in an open coffin to the cemetery, they count from when the grave is completely sealed. Rabbeinu Tam explained that the golel is the stone placed above the grave to seal it, not to mark the grave. As long as the coffin is exposed, meaning that earth has not yet been placed upon it, even if it was fastened with nails, mourning has not yet begun.
