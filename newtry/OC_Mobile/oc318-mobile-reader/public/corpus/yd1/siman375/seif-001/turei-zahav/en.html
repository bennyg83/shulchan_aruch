Closing the grave with earth. The Gemara says mourning begins when the golel is closed. Rashi interprets golel as the lid of the coffin. Ramban explains that where a body is placed into a coffin that is then nailed shut before being placed into a burial niche, mourning begins when the coffin is closed. But if the body is carried to the cemetery on a bier or in an open coffin, mourning begins only when the grave is fully closed with earth. Rabbenu Tam interprets golel as the stone placed over the grave to seal it, not a marker. Under his view, as long as the coffin remains exposed and has not yet been covered with earth, mourning has not begun even if the coffin itself is already nailed shut.
