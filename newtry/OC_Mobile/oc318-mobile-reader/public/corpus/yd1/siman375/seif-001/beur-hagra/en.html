(Likkut) From when, etc. This is called the closing of the golel. See Tosafot in Berakhot 19b and Semachot, the Rosh, and so it appears in Evel Rabbati chapter 4 from the incident, etc. So too in Shocher Tov: the holy ancestors were not called holy until they were placed in the ground and the golel was closed before them. Likewise in Evel Rabbati chapter 10 and in many places in the Gemara.
And it is completed, etc. So the Rosh concludes; see there, siman 86. Tosafot discuss this at length in several places.
But he is not, etc. As stated in Taanit chapter 1, 13a, and in the Yerushalmi, Berakhot chapter 2, and Semachot, and in the Tosefta, though it is not in our Tosefta but in Evel Rabbati chapter 5: a mourner and one under nidui who are walking, etc.; and as I wrote above in siman 82 seif 4. This is the Rosh there.
If he was, etc. As I wrote above in siman 340 seif 25.
