One who is, etc. This is like tearing, where one adds to the first tear within seven days, etc., as written in the Yerushalmi according to the Mordechai's text there. But his words are astonishing, for in our Gemara we rule the opposite, as I wrote above in siman 340 seif 21. He should have compared this instead to what is stated there in seif 23 regarding one whose dead relatives died, etc. It is not comparable to one who tore and afterward another relative died, etc., for the first tearing has already passed.
