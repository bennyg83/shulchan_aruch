Closing the coffin is equivalent to burial. Ordinarily mourning begins only when the grave is completely sealed with earth. During a siege, however, placing the body in another room and closing the coffin is treated as burial because the family has effectively despaired of ordinary burial. Even if they hope later to bury it in the cemetery, under the present danger this is a complete burial for them, since capture of the city may prevent anything further. This appears from Beit Yosef; Bach offers another reconciliation.
