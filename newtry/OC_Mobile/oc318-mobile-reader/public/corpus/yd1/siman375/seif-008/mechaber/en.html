One whose relative died and he did not know until he came to the place where the dead was or to the burial place — if it was a near place that is a walk of ten parasangs so he could arrive in one day, even if he came on the seventh day {Rama: (and some say even if one came and the head of the house is not home, only that he went for the dead's needs — if he returns within three days it is as if he is home; and one who comes within three counts with them (and so in Tur); and one should be lenient according to this view; and whoever must count with them — even if he later returned home, he counts with them (Rashba siman 377))}; if he found comforters at the head of the household, even if they already rose to stand — since he found comforters {Rama: (meaning they still practice some mourning) (Tur and Rashba in responsum)} — it counts for him and he counts with them the completion of thirty days; and if he did not find comforters, he counts for himself; and likewise if it was a distant place, even if he came on the second day he counts for himself seven and thirty from the day he came.
