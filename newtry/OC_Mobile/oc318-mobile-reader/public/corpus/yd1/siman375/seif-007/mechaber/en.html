One who drowned in waters that have an end, or a report went out that bandits killed him or a beast dragged him — from when do they count? From when they despaired of seeking; if they found him limb by limb and recognize him by bodily signs — they do not count until they find his head and majority, or they despaired of seeking {Rama: (baraita brought by Ramban)}; and if found after they despaired of him — relatives need not return to mourn, but sons — if they were there when he was found, they mourn that day, for it is no worse than gathering their father's bones; but if not there and heard after the day passed, they need not mourn: {Rama: If they sent their dead to another city whose law is to begin mourning when they turn back and they began mourning, and afterward the dead was seized and not buried a long time — they need not interrupt their mourning nor return to mourn afterward, for once their law is to begin immediately they need not interrupt (Mordechai end of Moed Katan).}
