One who has relations with the virgin several times and she saw blood because of tashmish — we always presume it is virgin-blood until she has tashmish one time and does not see blood because of tashmish. And if afterward she sees 3 times because of tashmish, she has been established to be one who sees blood because of tashmish. {Rama: And even if she did not stop seeing one time, if she has no pain at all at the time of tashmish, behold she is like all women, and we do not attribute it to virgin-blood (a responsum of Maharam Padua, siman 10). One from whom blood exits by way of the urethral opening, and he had tashmish with the woman — she attributes it to him (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba). And see above siman 190 (se’if 20).}
