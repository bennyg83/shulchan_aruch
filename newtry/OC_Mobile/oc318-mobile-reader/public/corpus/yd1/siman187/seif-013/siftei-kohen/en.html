And even if, etc. The Bach disagrees and rules that even if she experiences no pain at all during relations, we attribute the blood to hymenal blood. However, Masas Binyamin, responsum 47, discusses the matter at length and rules in accordance with the Rama; see there. Beis Yosef himself also wrote at the end of his discussion, “I am afraid to be lenient unless there are clear indications that it is hymenal blood.”
