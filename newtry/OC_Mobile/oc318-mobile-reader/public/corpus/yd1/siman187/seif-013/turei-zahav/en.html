One who has relations with a virgin. Beit Yosef learned this law from Tosefta: all need examination with a tube except a virgin whose blood is pure — hymenal blood is pure, etc.; and one can analyze: behold the Tosefta specifies a virgin — perhaps all the time she is a virgin; per what they said at beginning of chapter HaTinoket: each according to her law — her time to see has not arrived until the wound heals; per Shmuel as long as saliva is found in the mouth she sees due to relations; and so Rambam like Shmuel ch. 5 Laws Forbidden Relations; and when her time arrives — four nights; and if she saw in her father's house before marriage — mitzvah relations alone and she is no longer a virgin — why is Beit Yosef lenient for all virgins; and further he brings proof from that case in chapter HaTinoket, etc.
