If she has no pain at all during intercourse. It implies if she ceased seeing once, even if she has pain during intercourse, it is seeing blood because of intercourse and we do not attribute to a wound; and so in responsum Emunat Shmuel siman 23; and the reason: that which a woman is believed to say I have a wound within her innards, is when she feels pain and ache in her belly even not during intercourse; see Taz siman 191 seif katan 4; but pain and ache during intercourse is not proven to come from her wound; nevertheless when she feels pain and ache one may rely on examination according to all; and as explained above seif 3 in the gloss.
She is like all other women. And it is explained in the Talmud tractate Niddah, ch. Ha-Tinokot, that there is a distinction between a minor girl or a young girl and a mature woman, between whether her time to see has arrived or not, and these distinctions are explained in responsum Magen Binyamin siman 47; see there and below siman 133.
