(Shach s.k. 30) As if she passed. In Emunat Shmuel he wrote one can say in the simple sense that one finds three consecutive — meaning she had relations three times in one night, and after each relations she wiped herself with a cloth, and in the morning examined the three cloths and found blood on them; in such a case the usage was permitted as above (siman 186 s.a. 2 in the Hagahah).
