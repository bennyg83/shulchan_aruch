A probe. This means a small stick. Were she to insert the stick alone, she would not know whether the blood came from the source or from the sides. She must therefore insert it into a tube, which separates between the stick and the sides. The wad of cotton is used so that the blood will be discernible upon it, and also so that the stick will not scratch her and cause blood to emerge.
It came from the sides, and she is permitted. We do not say that since male organs are not alike, then certainly a tube is not comparable to a male organ, for we do not apply that reasoning stringently. The Rama wrote in siman 177, seif 10, that if the husband transgressed and had relations with her when it was forbidden, and she did not see blood, this is even more effective than an examination with a tube, and it is sufficient.
