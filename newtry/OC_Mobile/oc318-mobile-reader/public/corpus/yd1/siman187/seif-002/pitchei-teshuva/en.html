She has relations three times. Baer Heitev explains this as three total relations with the first husband. Torat HaShelamim understands the Mechaber literally, following Raavad, that the first unexamined occurrence is not counted. Kreti U'Pleiti, Sidrei Taharah, Chokhmat Adam, Noda BiYehudah, and Chatam Sofer debate whether one may rely on this leniency.
