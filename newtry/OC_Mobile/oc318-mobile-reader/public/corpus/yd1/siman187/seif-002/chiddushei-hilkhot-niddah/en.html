Three times meaning still a second time. Magen Avraham in s.k. 2 brought Raavad in Ba'al HaNefesh who explained the first sighting is not included; and Magen Avraham wrote per his view (a) he holds a woman without veset needs no examination for her husband neither before nor after relations for she is not concerned for seeing blood due to relations without prior indication — therefore first sighting is not from the count; but Rambam who ruled even with veset needs examination after relations for we are concerned for seeing due to relations — therefore ruled first sighting is from the count, etc. — end quote; and all his words depend on examination after relations whether we are concerned for seeing due to relations — and this is not so; for with this concern for due to relations — even if we are not concerned, nevertheless we see she saw due to relations — why not be three times presumption. But correct: Raavad proved from this baraita that for her husband no examination needed before relations — from what is taught: she has relations once, twice, thrice — where does first sighting go; rather first sighting does not combine — who says she saw due to relations, perhaps before she had relations she was already tamei and blood was in her womb chamber; therefore when she had relations and examined she finds blood; if so presumption she saw from heat and desire of relations — we establish her thus and forbid to her husband; therefore when she saw once from here onward she is obligated to examine herself always before relations since we see it deteriorated and once blood found after relations — therefore must examine always; and if so when found after relations it is clear she saw at time of relations and forbids her husband; and first sighting does not combine — we say she saw earlier; and from this Raavad proved for her husband no examination needed — for otherwise certainly at first time she examined before relations and it does not suffice otherwise — if so also first relations she certainly sees due to relations; therefore Raavad wrote it does not deal with a woman who has veset and all agree no examination needed beforehand — therefore first is not from count, for end teaches "and if she has veset" — therefore implies first part deals without veset; and Raavad's words are plain and clear; therefore per what we hold always without veset needs examination before relations — if so certainly first sighting is from count — plain and clear. But per this we learn from his words: if she did not examine before relations and saw after relations — we do not establish seeing due to relations — we say she saw earlier; and if so poskim should not have ruled that with veset we hold no examination needed before relations; and so below in Hagahah saw after birth — behold she is in nursing category and even without veset no examination needed; if so even though sees immediately after relations — nevertheless permitted to husband — we say she saw earlier; and it need not be specifically without veset we attribute she saw earlier — behold every hour she is liable to see; but with veset from where say she saw earlier — more reasonable to attribute to relations for nature of heat causes blood to flow from womb as Raavad proved from end — does not deal with veset; seems otherwise we would say deals with veset; nevertheless since she did not examine we do not attribute due to relations; therefore this law is questionable, for also from Rosh's challenge on Rif it is proven not like Raavad — he challenged what relevance Shmuel who said for purity needs examination to Reish Lakish — Shmuel deals with examination before relations and Reish Lakish after; and per above puzzling — since Reish Lakish said yes she does and Rif explained three times to be in presumption of seeing due to relations — therefore needs examination before relations, for otherwise still not in presumption of seeing due to relations — perhaps blood existed earlier as stated; if so from where also before relations needs examination; and it appears he does not hold like Raavad; and as stated we always attribute to common regular matter — we say from presumption due to relations she saw, for nature of relations is so; and see above siman 184 s.k. 10 in Rambam's words appears like Raavad's reasoning — puzzling.
