How does she check? She takes a tube (meaning: a hollow reed of lead) and its mouth is fitted to its inside, and she places inside it a michchol with cotton at its head, and inserts it into that place until the place where the shamash reaches. If blood is found on its head, it is known that it is from the makor and she is forbidden; and if not, it is known that it is from the sides and she is permitted. (Beit Yosef from Rambam.) (And even in this time one may rely on this checking) (Tur and Beit Yosef, according to Rambam and Rosh and Rashba and Rif; and so wrote Ran and Maharam Padua, siman 10).
