With a probe. Meaning a splinter; and Mishnah Berurah wrote there that she need not insert the tube with the probe and cloth; only until the place she can reach; and she estimates herself according to her own assessment that it reached the place the sun crushed; and a woman is believed on this herself, and she need not at all force the tube into a narrow tight place; and we need not be concerned perhaps she did not insert the measure of crushing, for the Torah was not given to ministering angels; end quote.
And she is permitted. It implies even to the third husband; and if nothing was found on the cloth; whether found on the sides of the tube or not found on it at all; she is permitted. And Taz wrote, and some say the fingers are not equal; all the more the tube is not equal to a finger; therefore for stringency some say so; and Rama wrote in seif 10 that if the husband transgressed and had relations with her while forbidden and she did not see; it is better than tube examination and it helps.
