Up to the place reached during relations. Masas Binyamin wrote there in a responsum that she need not insert the tube together with the probe and wad farther than she is able. She estimates for herself, according to her own judgment, that it reached the place reached during relations, and the woman herself is believed regarding this. She need not force the tube into a narrow or constricted place at all. We need not be concerned that perhaps she did not insert it to the full depth of relations, for the Torah was not given to ministering angels, end quote. See there, where he discusses this at length.
And if not, etc. It implies that if blood is not found on the cloth — whether found on the sides of the tube or not found on it at all — she is permitted; and so implies from the words of the poskim; and so Bach end of seif 2, who gives the reason: for if it were from the source it would now also be found on the head of the probe; and so in responsum of Geonim chapter 4 of Laws of Forbidden Relations in the name of Ritzba — it seems to me that even if she examined herself with a tube and found no blood at all — she is permitted to have relations afterward, for perhaps she was healed; for by inserting the tube she is accustomed to see, like with a finger, etc. — and it is obvious.
