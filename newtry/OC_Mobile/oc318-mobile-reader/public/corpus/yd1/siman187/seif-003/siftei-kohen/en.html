The choice is hers etc. However if she examined for the first husband and blood was found on the cloth — she is forbidden to everyone; so wrote the poskim.
One may rely on this reasoning, etc. In my humble opinion, one should not instruct a woman to perform the tube examination while she is still with her first husband, for the Rambam and Rashi forbid it. In my work I wrote that this resolves the difficulty that Tosafos left unresolved on folio 66a. But if we say that the examination is effective with the first husband, that difficulty cannot be resolved; see there. Furthermore, the Raavad and Ramban forbid this examination nowadays, and the Rashba and Rabbeinu Yerucham also wrote that it is proper to be stringent in accordance with them. In addition, if blood is found on the wad, she will become forbidden to everyone. Nevertheless, the Bach, Maharam of Lublin in responsum 52, and the other later authorities are lenient.
And if she felt etc. It seems somewhat that she has an illness and wound, and one may rely on the examination, etc.
