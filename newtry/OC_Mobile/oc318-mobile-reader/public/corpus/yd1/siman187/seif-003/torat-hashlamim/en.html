The choice is in her hand. And that the Tanna did not say this examination is after the third time of the first, good advice is taught: now she is permitted without examination to the second from double-doubt reasoning: perhaps blood did not come from the womb. (And perhaps from this reason we do not require examination, for in the Gemara they permit even when there is remedy of examination as explained in Kuntres HaSfekot; and see nearby seif katan 8.) And even if to the first it came from the womb, perhaps to the second husband it will not come from the womb; but to the first husband forbidden in any case without examination since there is only one doubt, so Semag; and per Bach's words that whenever blood comes from the womb, if so again no distinction between fingers and forces, for in all she will see blood, we must say that which they permit to the second without examination, and so forth.
And one may rely on initial reasoning. And so Bach; and in responsum Maharam of Lublin siman 22; and in responsum Emunat Shmuel siman 27; and he challenged Shach who somewhat questions to be stringent in this, see there.
