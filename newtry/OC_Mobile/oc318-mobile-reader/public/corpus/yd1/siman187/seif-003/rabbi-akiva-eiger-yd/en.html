(Shach s.k. 33) And in my humble opinion the Rabbi's words are clear. And in Noda Biyhudah he wrote to answer: one can say for R. Akiva Eiger holds like Tur below (siman 189) that also veset of jumps requires uprooting three times; or he holds relations is like veset of the body from eating garlic (and as Taz holds s.k. 16) — therefore he holds it is not uprooted in one time unless she had relations three times; and that which tube examination helps — is truly only when found from the side, for sight from the sides; but in relations it is not so — requires three times.
