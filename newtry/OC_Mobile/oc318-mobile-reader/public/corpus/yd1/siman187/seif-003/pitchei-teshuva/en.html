Consecutive. See Chiddushei Dinim who wrote: if she examined herself twice near relations and found blood, and afterward had relations several times without any examination, and afterward had relations once and examined and found blood — it is like three consecutive times and she was established as seeing blood due to relations — see there; and see what I wrote above siman 186 s.k. 2 in his name; and see in responsum Shivat Tziyon siman 38 who was asked about a woman who said she saw three consecutive times blood due to relations, and afterward when the rabbi investigated when the examination was — if at all three times immediately after relations — she said the second time she did not examine immediately because she felt nothing, only the day after immersion she examined and found blood — what is her law; and if the woman is believed on this whether it is retraction from her first words.
