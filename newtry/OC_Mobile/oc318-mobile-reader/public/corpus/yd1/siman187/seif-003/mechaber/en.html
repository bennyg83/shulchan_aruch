If she wants to check herself while she is still under the first [husband] after she had tashmish 3 times, the choice is in her hand and she is permitted to him; and some say that she is forbidden to the first from the third tashmish and onward, even with checking. {Rama: And one may rely on the first reasoning to be lenient (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of the consensus of the poskim and Hagahos Maimoniyos). And if she felt pain and aching at the time of tashmish, according to all opinions one may rely on checking with the first husband (there in the Hagahah, in the name of Ritzba).}
