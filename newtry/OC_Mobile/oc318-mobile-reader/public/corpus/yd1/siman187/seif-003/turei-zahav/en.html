While married to the first husband, etc. They did not say that she must be divorced from the first husband except as a leniency for her. Namely, were she to examine herself while married to the first husband, she might find blood at the tip of the probe and thereby become forbidden to every man. They therefore permitted her to marry another man without an examination. Only after the third husband, when she otherwise has no remedy, should she place herself into this uncertainty and risk finding blood at the tip of the probe.
