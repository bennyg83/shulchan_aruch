She may engage in relations three times. Meaning, with that same husband through whose relations she had already seen blood.
And she may marry another man. For not all male organs are alike.
And she may marry a third man. For she is not established as seeing blood because of relations with every male organ until the third husband. The reason she is not forbidden to the third husband after the first act of relations is that not all degrees of strength are alike. Perhaps the first husband's act of relations was performed with greater force. She is therefore not established in this condition until three acts of relations with each husband.
Until she examines herself. Meaning, by the tube examination mentioned in seif 2.
