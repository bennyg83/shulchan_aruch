Three times. Including the first — meaning two more times; so Perishah; and likewise proven in Tur beginning of siman 187.
Consecutive etc. That is, without an intervening permitted relation in between; but three consecutive nights are not required, as below seif 10 in the note.
On his cloth. For in such a case even if she found blood a long time after relations — it is called seeing due to relations, for necessarily it is due to relations; and so proven in chapter Kol HaYad (Niddah 14a) and in the poskim.
But she must divorce etc. And regarding the ketubah — see Even HaEzer siman 116.
She must divorce and may marry up to three, etc. See Even HaEzer siman 9 and what is written there.
And she is forbidden to her husband etc. It requires investigation whether we are stringent for this reason — that we are not expert — to forbid her to everyone, where she was established thus with three men.
And there is no distinction etc., from when he married her, etc. See above seif 13 — the law of a virgin.
