Immediately in the measure of, etc. Meaning if she saw immediately within this measure even though we say she saw after relations — nevertheless since she was liable for a guilt-offering dependent it is called seeing blood due to relations; but after this measure she is pure even though we say she saw earlier — she is removed from the category of seeing blood due to relations; so implies among poskim. But if she examined immediately after relations and found nothing, and afterward saw even within this measure — it is not called seeing blood due to relations; and all this is simple. And so the Gaon A.M.U. z"l explained at length in a responsum — see there. And I add that so implies explicitly from Raavad in Sefer Ba'al HaNefesh, brought by Beit Yosef: one who sees blood due to relations, etc. — forbidden to have relations; and this is when she examined herself in the measure that she extends her hand under the pillow — meaning so her husband would be liable for guilt-offering dependent; but afterward behold it is like from time to time in niddah who is not tamei retroactively to her husband and is not established as seeing blood due to relations, etc. — end quote; explicitly clear that even though she did not yet examine at all — nevertheless after the measure of extending her hand she is not established as seeing blood due to relations; and further for he says behold it is like from time to time in niddah, etc. — and there it deals where she did not yet examine, for if she examined she is not tamei retroactively from time to time but from deposit to deposit. And per this, for us who hold all near relations is called seeing blood due to relations, as the Rabbi wrote — specifically when known with certainty she saw near relations; but if long after relations she examined herself and found blood, even though we say she saw at time of relations — she is permitted; and all the more a woman who saw three times in the morning on the night of her immersion — when she rose from her bed she found on her garment an abundance of blood — she is permitted. Again the Gaon A.M.U. z"l ruled in his responsum there that even if this woman has relations again once or twice and finds immediately at time of relations — she is permitted; and we do not say the matter was revealed retroactively; rather the first days fall away and from here onward we calculate. And all this is clear — I did not need to write it except to remove from the reader's heart the Rabbi's words who disagreed with the Gaon A.M.U.'s responsum on this matter. And the matter is simple: wherever it is not called seeing blood due to relations, even if she saw many times — she is permitted; and so in words of the Acharonim; and so in responsum Maharam of Lublin siman 22 — no distinction whether she had relations three times or forty and fifty times — see there.
