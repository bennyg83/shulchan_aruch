Three times. Meaning with the first; that is, still a second time; and Shach wrote: but after this measure she is pure even though we say she saw earlier; she is removed from the category of seeing blood due to relations; but if she examined immediately after relations and found nothing, and afterward saw even within this measure; it is not called seeing blood due to relations. And per this, for us who hold that all near relations is called seeing blood due to relations, as Rama wrote; this is specifically when it is known with certainty that she saw near relations; but if long after relations she examined herself and found blood, even though we say she saw at time of relations; she is permitted; and all the more a woman who saw three times in the morning on the night of her immersion; when she rose from her bed she found on her garment an abundance of blood; she is permitted. Again the Gaon A.M.U. z"l ruled that even if this woman has relations again once or twice and finds immediately at time of relations; she is permitted; and we do not say the matter was revealed retroactively that it was also at time of relations; rather the first days fall away and from here onward we calculate. And the matter is simple: wherever it is not called seeing blood due to relations, even if she saw many many times; she is permitted; and so too in responsum Maharam of Lublin siman 102; there is no distinction whether she had relations three times or forty and fifty times; end quote.
Until. In this circumstance, even blood found a considerable time after relations is treated as blood caused by relations. If the husband himself bleeds from the urethra, see Maharit, Yoreh De'ah, siman 30.
