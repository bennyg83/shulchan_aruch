She saw blood. She saw blood. In Tur and Semag wrote in the measure she descends from the bed and examines; Beit Yosef challenged: Rav Ashi was removed with a challenge and we hold the measure is only in the measure she extends her hand under the pillow; and further challenged they said "and she shall examine", this is not so, for the entire measure is for wiping and not examination. On the first challenge Bach explained well, although R. Eliezer was removed with difficulty, nevertheless he was not removed with an answer and halachah is like him; but on the second challenge it is obvious it must be as Beit Yosef wrote: since provisional guilt offering is liable it is called seeing blood due to relations; and although for provisional guilt offering liability we hold we require two pieces, nevertheless liability and prohibition bringing to karet exists here; and so in the Gemara: a woman, why should she examine herself to obligate her husband provisional guilt offering; although there is no karet, nevertheless liability and prohibition of provisional guilt offering exists here; and Minchat Yaakov mixed matters implying in that explanation we hold like R. Eliezer from resolving the second challenge, this is not so; only in the second challenge must be Bach's explanation; and so one can say from what Rambam wrote who holds they are liable for karet, the intent is liability for karet and prohibition of Torah doubt exists here like provisional guilt offering; but nevertheless if so liability is certain for karet.
Three times. Three times meaning still a second time. Minchat Yaakov in seif katan 2 brought Raavad in Ba'al HaNefesh who explained the first sighting is not included; and Minchat Yaakov wrote per his view (a) he holds a woman without a fixed veset needs no examination for her husband neither before nor after relations for she is not concerned for seeing blood due to relations without prior indication, therefore first sighting is not from the count; but Rambam who ruled even with a fixed veset needs examination after relations for we are concerned for seeing blood due to relations, therefore ruled first sighting is from the count, etc., end quote; and all his words depend on examination after relations whether we are concerned for seeing blood due to relations, and this is not so; for with this concern for due to relations, even if we are not concerned, nevertheless we see she saw due to relations, why not be three times presumption. But correct: Raavad proved from this baraita that for her husband no examination needed before relations, from what is taught: she has relations once, twice, thrice, where does first sighting go; rather first sighting does not combine, who says she saw due to relations, perhaps before she had relations she was already impure and blood was in her womb chamber; therefore when she had relations and examined she finds blood; if so presumption she saw from heat and desire of relations, we establish her thus and forbid to her husband; therefore when she saw once from here onward she is obligated to examine herself always before relations since we see it deteriorated and once blood found after relations, therefore must examine always; and if so when found after relations it is clear she saw at time of relations and forbids her husband; and first sighting does not combine, we say she saw earlier; and from this Raavad proved for her husband no examination needed, for otherwise certainly at first time she examined before relations and it does not suffice otherwise, if so also first relations she certainly sees blood due to relations; therefore Raavad wrote it does not deal with a woman who has veset and all agree no examination needed beforehand, therefore first is not from count, for end teaches "and if she has veset", therefore implies first part deals without a fixed veset; and Raavad's words are plain and clear; therefore per what we hold always without a fixed veset needs examination before relations, if so certainly first sighting is from count, plain and clear. But per this we learn from his words: if she did not examine before relations and saw after relations, we do not establish seeing blood due to relations, we say she saw earlier; and if so authorities should not have ruled that with a fixed veset we hold no examination needed before relations; and so below in Hagahah saw after birth, behold she is in nursing category and even without a fixed veset no examination needed; if so even though sees immediately after relations, nevertheless permitted to husband, we say she saw earlier; and it need not be specifically without a fixed veset we attribute she saw earlier, behold every hour she is liable to see; but with a fixed veset from where say she saw earlier, more reasonable to attribute to relations for nature of heat causes blood to flow from womb as Raavad proved from end, does not deal with a fixed veset; seems otherwise we would say deals with a fixed veset; nevertheless since she did not examine we do not attribute due to relations; therefore this law is questionable, for also from Rosh's challenge on Rif it is proven not like Raavad, he challenged what relevance Shmuel who said for purity needs examination to Reish Lakish, Shmuel deals with examination before relations and Reish Lakish after; and per above This requires study, since Reish Lakish said yes she does and Rif explained three times to be in presumption of seeing blood due to relations, therefore needs examination before relations, for otherwise still not in presumption of seeing blood due to relations, perhaps blood existed earlier as stated; if so from where also before relations needs examination; and it appears he does not hold like Raavad; and as stated we always attribute to common regular matter, we say from presumption due to relations she saw, for nature of relations is so; and see above siman 184 seif katan 10 in Rambam's words appears like Raavad's reasoning, This requires study.
