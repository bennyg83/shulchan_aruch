She saw blood. In Tur and Semag wrote in the measure she descends from the bed and examines; Beit Yosef challenged: Rav Ashi was removed with a challenge and we hold the measure is only in the measure she extends her hand under the pillow; and further challenged they said "and she shall examine" — this is not so, for the entire measure is for wiping and not examination. On the first challenge Bach explained well — although R. Eliezer was removed with difficulty, nevertheless he was not removed with an answer and halachah is like him; but on the second challenge it is obvious it must be as Beit Yosef wrote: since guilt-offering dependent is liable it is called seeing blood due to relations; and although for guilt-offering dependent liability we hold we require two pieces — nevertheless liability and prohibition bringing to karet exists here; and so in the Gemara: a woman — why should she examine herself to obligate her husband guilt-offering dependent; although there is no karet — nevertheless liability and prohibition of guilt-offering dependent exists here; and Magen Avraham mixed matters implying in that explanation we hold like R. Eliezer from resolving the second challenge — this is not so; only in the second challenge must be Bach's explanation; and so one can say from what Rambam wrote who holds they are liable for karet — the intent is liability for karet and prohibition of Torah doubt exists here like guilt-offering dependent; but nevertheless if so liability is certain for karet.
