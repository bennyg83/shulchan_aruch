(Siman 187 seif 1) A woman who saw blood due to relations, etc. — she has relations three times, etc. Magen Avraham wrote: a woman who has a veset — the first time is not from the count per poskim' view, for we do not establish her as seeing blood due to relations without prior indication. He further wrote: if the relations were equal in time of month and time of immersion she is concerned only for that night as veset law. And in responsum Noda Biyhudah second siman 49 and some say wrote: that which we forbid a woman who saw blood due to relations three times not to have relations again — some say is only rabbinically; and so in responsum Rashba — Scripturally we are not concerned she will see again, for it is a veset dependent on act and is not called fixed Scripturally. And per this if she saw due to relations within, etc.
