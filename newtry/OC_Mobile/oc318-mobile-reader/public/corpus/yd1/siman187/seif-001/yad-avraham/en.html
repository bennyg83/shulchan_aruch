A woman who saw blood as a result of marital relations, etc., may have relations three times, etc. The Minchat Yaakov writes that where a woman has a fixed veset, the first occurrence is not counted according to the authorities who hold that she requires no examination at all, since we do not establish her as one who sees blood due to relations without some prior indication. He further writes that if the acts of relations occurred at the same point in the monthly cycle and at the same interval after immersion, she need be concerned only for that night, in accordance with the laws of a veset. In responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh Deah, simanim 49 and 51, he writes that the prohibition against a woman who saw blood due to relations three times having relations again is only rabbinic. The Rashba likewise writes that Scripturally we do not presume that she will see again, because this is a veset dependent upon an act and is not considered fixed on a Scriptural level. Accordingly, if she saw blood due to relations during the completion period following childbirth, she does not establish a veset and is not forbidden to have relations after that period ends. He brings proof from Niddah, page 11, where the Gemara states that she does not establish a veset from days of purity to days of impurity. See Beit Meir, Even HaEzer, siman 117, who disputes this.
