She saw blood due to relations. See in responsum Noda Biyhudah second YD siman 61 who permitted one who sees blood due to relations if at the hour she saw due to relations she still had no fixed veset at all, and afterward a fixed veset was established for her — whether in days of the month or interval veset — because one who sees three times due to relations is forbidden from veset matter, for we say she established a veset through the act of relations; and we hold siman 189 s.a. 17 that a veset established through coercion such as she jumped and saw, even if it happened many times — is not a fixed veset in law unless she established it for days; but nevertheless she is concerned for it if it happened three times like an unfixed veset; and from now this is itself the law of a woman who sees due to relations — for what difference between jumping and relations; and if so, since she was not established three times, etc.
