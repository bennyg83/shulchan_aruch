She saw blood because of relations. Noda BiYehudah proposes that where a woman had no fixed cycle when she began seeing from relations, and later establishes a fixed calendar or interval cycle, the new fixed cycle may displace the nonfixed cycle of intercourse. He applies this practically with caution, while Chavot Daat strongly disputes the approach because bleeding from relations may reflect illness rather than only a cycle. Later responsa debate pregnancy, nursing, and whether a first post-establishment recurrence revives the concern.
