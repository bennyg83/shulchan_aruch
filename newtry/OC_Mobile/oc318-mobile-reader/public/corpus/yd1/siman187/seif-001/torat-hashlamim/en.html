She has intercourse three times. Meaning still a second time, so Perishah, Taz, and Shach; and so is proven in Tur end of siman 186; and Rambam in chapter 4 of Hilchot Issurei Bi'ah fixed this explicitly in his language: one who saw blood during intercourse, behold she is permitted to have intercourse when she becomes pure; second time she saw blood, she has intercourse a third time; third time she saw blood, behold she is forbidden to have intercourse, and so forth.; but Tur and the Mechaber who copied the Gemara's language, it appears they specifically say she has intercourse three more times without the first intercourse; and especially the Mechaber whose way is to copy Rambam's language everywhen, and here copies the Gemara's language, implies they specifically say; and do not wonder at this, for Raavad in Sefer Ba'al HaNefesh page 62b wrote thus explicitly: and further understand and know that, and so forth.
