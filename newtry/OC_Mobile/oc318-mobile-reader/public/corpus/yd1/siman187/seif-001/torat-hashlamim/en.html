Shach wrote as follows: and this requires study, whether we are stringent for this reason to forbid her to the entire world where she has become established so with fifty men, end quote. In Sefer Baal HaNefesh it implies that one should not distinguish. But if she did not see close to relations. Shach wrote in the name of his father, the Gaon z"l, that even if she did not examine herself close to relations until afterward, and one could say she saw earlier, nevertheless she is not called one who sees because of relations. But if she examined herself immediately after relations and found nothing, and afterward, even close to relations, examined and found blood, nevertheless she is not one who sees because of relations, for if it were because of relations it would have come initially, end quote in s.k. 1.
