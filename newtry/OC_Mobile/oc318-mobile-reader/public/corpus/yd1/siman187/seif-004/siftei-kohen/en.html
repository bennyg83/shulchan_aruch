Near her veset etc. Perishah seif 11 wrote that we do not deal with the wicked; and per Rabbenu Tam who is stringent above siman 184 seif 10 to separate from her near her veset even for one who goes on a journey — one finds a case here where nearness is permitted, such as if her seeing is at the start of day, then the whole night before is permitted (as above siman 184 seif 2) — end of his words; and it is difficult for me: for the reason the whole night before is permitted is because since her veset is in the day, we are not concerned she will see at night as she is not accustomed to see at night; if so, since we are lenient for that reason, all the more so we should not attribute to leniency that she will not see due to relations, since she is not accustomed to see then; rather here it deals with where she already went ahead and had relations; or alternatively Rabbenu Tam explains "and if she has a veset" like Rashi or like the Mordechai; or alternatively here it deals with a minor or one who has not yet developed signs, where he need not separate from her as long as she has not established it three times, as above siman 184 seif 3; and here he says that if it was clarified that she had relations near her veset — meaning she saw after relations once or twice more at that same time such that she was found to have established a veset three times — she attributes to her veset; and requires careful study.
