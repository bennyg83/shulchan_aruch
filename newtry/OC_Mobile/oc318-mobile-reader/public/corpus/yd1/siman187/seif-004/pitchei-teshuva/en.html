On his cloth. Avnei HaTzedek; and see in responsum A.A.Z. Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 126 — all this deals where found on his cloth prepared to wipe with after relations and he wiped himself with it; but if he did not wipe with his cloth but it was found on his garment — then it is only like any other stain found; and we do not find concern for stains on a man's garment, only on a woman's garment — see there [and see below siman 190 s.k. 2]. And Siftei Tzedek wrote even if found also on her garment one can say it came after the measure of guilt-offering dependent, and what was found on his — perhaps it came from elsewhere — see there [and see what is written below s.k. 38 that one can say so is also Noda Biyhudah's view in blood due to relations siman 87; and see in responsum Chemdat Shlomo siman 21 in a case that happened — a woman sent to ask about stains, etc.].
