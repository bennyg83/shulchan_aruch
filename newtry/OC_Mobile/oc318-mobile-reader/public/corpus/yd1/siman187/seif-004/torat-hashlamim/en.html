If she had relations near her veset. Meaning going on a journey or she passed and had relations; and in Perishah he wrote it deals where she saw at day's beginning — permitted all the preceding night as above siman 184; and Shach wrote on him: puzzling — for therefore permitted all the preceding night since her veset is by day we are not concerned she saw at night; if so since we are lenient from this reason all the more we should not attribute leniently so she not be seeing due to relations since she is not accustomed to see then — end quote; and his words are not compelled, for one can say nevertheless since blood's way is not to come from the source through relations as stated above s.k. 11 — we say relations advanced her veset somewhat; and in order not to forbid her to her husband we attribute to leniency where possible; and see, etc.
We attribute her sighting to her veset. Thus Rambam, Ramban, Rashba, and Tur explained the baraita's language in chapter HaTinoket: if she has a veset she attributes to her veset; but Rashi explained: and if she has a veset for this irregularity that she does not see every hour due to relations but periodically — she attributes to her veset and has relations without examination between veset and veset — end quote. And in truth this law is agreed by all poskim; and the Mechaber did not copy this law here because it is included in the Mechaber's words in seif 1 "if in all three consecutive times," and so in seif 11; and see s.k. 3; and Mordechai wrote in Shevuot: a woman who has a fixed veset time at which she sees niddah — attributes to veset that she can say this blood she sees is pure for her veset has not yet arrived, etc.
