If she had intercourse near her veset. Meaning going on a journey or she passed and had intercourse; and in Perishah he wrote it deals when she saw at day's beginning, permitted all the preceding night as above siman 184; and Shach wrote on him: puzzling, for therefore permitted all the preceding night since her veset is by day we are not concerned she saw at night; if so since we are lenient from this reason all the more we should not attribute leniently so she not be seeing due to intercourse since she is not accustomed to see then, end quote; and his words are not compelled, for one can say nevertheless since blood's way is not to come from the womb through intercourse as stated above seif katan 11, we say intercourse advanced her veset somewhat; and in order not to forbid her to her husband we attribute to leniency when possible; and see, and so forth.
We attribute her sighting to her veset. Thus Rambam, Ramban, Rashba, and Tur explained the baraita's language in chapter HaTinoket: if she has a fixed veset she attributes to her veset; but Rashi explained: and if she has a fixed veset for this irregularity that she does not see every hour due to intercourse but periodically, she attributes to her veset and has intercourse without examination between veset and veset, end quote. And in truth this law is agreed by all poskim; and the Mechaber did not copy this law here because it is included in the Mechaber's words in seif 1 "if in all three consecutive times," and so in seif 11; and see seif katan 3; and Mordechai wrote in Shevuot: a woman who has a fixed the time of the veset at which she sees niddah, attributes to veset that she can say this blood she sees is pure for her veset has not yet arrived, and so forth.
