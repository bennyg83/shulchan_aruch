Of a limb. Avnei HaTzedek; and Tzlach — regarding bedieved if she examined with wood whether the examination helps; and initially it appears it helps, for the reason of scratching is perhaps truly there would be no blood except through scratching blood issues and it would be found on its tip and she would be tamei for nothing; and if so it is only to be lenient for her; but if she examined with wood and nevertheless nothing was found on its tip — it helps. Nevertheless it appears per what is stated above s.k. 7 and below s.k. 14 and 17 that if found on the sides of the tube — more lenient than if nothing found at all; nevertheless in this case she is not given the leniency of found on the sides but her law is as one who found nothing at all, for we are concerned what was found is through scratching — puzzling.
