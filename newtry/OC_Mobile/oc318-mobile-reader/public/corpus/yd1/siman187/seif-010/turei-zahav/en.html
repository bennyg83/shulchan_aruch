During the first act of relations following her immersion. This is not comparable to what the Shulchan Aruch writes shortly afterward regarding concern that she may see blood on the night of immersion. There the concern depends specifically upon that night, and afterward she is permitted. Here, however, the concern is caused by the first act of relations after immersion, even if it occurs a long time after the immersion. Yet this itself is difficult, for it is obvious that she is forbidden like any woman who saw blood during three consecutive acts of relations. We must therefore say that this applies even though there were permitted acts of relations between those occurrences. Although those permitted acts are effective for the law stated afterward, they are not effective here. There they are effective only because we say on their basis that the first blood following childbirth was caused by the childbirth, as explained there. That reasoning does not apply to the night of immersion. See what I wrote in seif 11.
