בביאה הראשונה שafter טבילתה. זה אינה דומה למה שכתב הש"ע בסמוך regarding חשש ראיית ליל immersion דשמה תלוי החשש באותו לילה specifically ואח"כ permittedת אבל כאן איכא החשש מחמת ביאה ראשונה שafter הimmersion אע"פ שהוא זfrom רב after הimmersion but rather דקשה בזה plainly שforbiddenה ככל אשה שראתה ג"פ רצופים ע"כ צ"ל דמיירי אע"פ שבינתיים היו ביאות של היתר וזה מהני בlaw שאח"כ מ"מ כאן לא מהני דגם שם לא מהני but rather דwe say מכח זה דדם הfirst שafter לידה היה מחמת הלידה as explained שם וזה לא שייך בליל immersion and see מה שכתבתי בseif י"א:
Or if she saw after every birth three times, etc. Rema's words cannot be understood unless we say there is a scribal error here. First let us transcribe the view of R' Yochanan in Hagahot Shaarei Dura, from which this ruling is taken: R' Yochanan's view regarding a woman who saw once after each of her births, and then had marital relations without seeing — and this was her pattern through three births — he ruled: since she established a presumption of permitted relations in between, we can say that what she saw once after the birth was due to the sides being struck from the birth, and it is like the wound case in seif 5 above. This is the substance of his ruling there — implying that if there was no established presumption of permitted relations in between, but only three intercourses total — namely one after each birth, after which she saw and had no further relations until after the third birth — it is obviously forbidden, and we do not say all her sightings came from being struck at the sides, even though each was close to the birth. And all the more so: if she had relations and saw three times consecutively after one birth, she is forbidden until she examines with a tube — for here it is more stringent in the two instances after the first [which occurred not close to a birth]; and furthermore, it is implied that if this happened three times after each birth — meaning each time after a birth she saw three consecutively and was thereby forbidden, as we said — and thereafter she transgressed and had relations after those three times after each birth: this does not help her; and certainly examination with a tube does not help — for the tube repair or intercourse without seeing only helps where there is concern that a particular intercourse constitutes a prohibition, and then we say that since she was repaired through the tube, what she saw during intercourse was from the sides; but here, even if we say she was repaired and permitted afterward — that is only until the fourth birth; but after she established a pattern of seeing three times after each birth, intercourse after the fourth birth remains forbidden, for she has established a fixed pattern of seeing three consecutively after each birth, and she will certainly see three times after the fourth birth too, constituting a definite prohibition before us — and here one cannot say that having permitted intercourse in between helps, since she has established a fixed period-like pattern of seeing after birth. What Rema wrote afterward "that where examination with a tube is required, transgressing and having relations helps" — refers to transgressing and having relations after the fourth birth; as Darkei Moshe also wrote explicitly. But it does not help her that she had relations without seeing before the fourth birth — as I said, since she has fixed periods in relation to births, she requires repair specifically after the fourth birth; and this is clear to any who understand. Therefore it seems there is a scribal error here and it should read "and all this if she has already had relations" — and the word "transgressed" does not belong here — and then Rema's words match precisely the words of R' Yochanan. And "or if she saw after each birth three times" means where they were not consecutive and there were permitted relations between them, and on this he said: if she already had relations — meaning in the permitted manner — then we do not say she established a pattern of seeing after each birth, and we attribute it to the sides being struck; and then he properly concludes: "but if she saw three times consecutively, even after one birth, she requires a tube" — and transgressing and having relations does not help her, except regarding relations from then on until the fourth birth — meaning after the third birth she is permitted until the fourth birth. And my father-in-law of blessed memory also wrote that this gloss refers in its first clause to non-consecutive occurrences — and I do not know why he did not explain what "transgressed" means, since there is no transgression here. Therefore it must be as I emended — and the essential law has been clarified with heavenly assistance in all its particulars. And what he wrote "however, in every case, etc." — meaning: here too, in the case of seeing three times — once after each birth — where she did not establish a presumption of permitted relations in between such that she did not see, she is then like any other woman who saw three consecutive times and transgressed and had relations afterward without seeing; it helps her that she need no longer be concerned. But if she had established a presumption of permitted relations in between, there is no concern here. And likewise: if she saw three times after birth in three separate births and then transgressed and had relations after the fourth birth — this helps her; but it does not help her that she transgressed and had relations between births, since she established period-like patterns tied to birth and needs repair specifically after the fourth birth. And similarly, any woman who saw blood from intercourse three times and requires a tube — as stated in siman 186 — how much more does it help her if she transgressed and had relations in a prohibited manner afterward; and this is Rema's reasoning in Darkei Moshe and it is correct.
