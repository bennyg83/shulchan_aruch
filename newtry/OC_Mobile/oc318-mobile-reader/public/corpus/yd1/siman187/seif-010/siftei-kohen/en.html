As if she had seen etc. Meaning as if she went ahead and saw three consecutive times without immersing — for certainly otherwise it is impossible to find three consecutive times, for she must immerse each time; and it is easily understood.
And if it happened to her etc. Meaning: if it happened to her that she saw immediately after giving birth three consecutive times, and afterward she went ahead and had relations in the interim and did not see, and then returned and saw three consecutive times after a subsequent birth and did not again have relations — she is permitted; and likewise even if she saw three consecutive times after each birth, but afterward between each birth she had relations in the interim and did not see, and after the third or fourth birth she returned and saw three consecutive times and did not again have relations — she is permitted; but if she saw three consecutive times after a birth — she is not permitted to have relations afterward ab initio; for we permit only when afterward she went ahead and had relations and was established with permitted relations following the prohibited ones, and we attribute to weakness of childbirth — even though afterward she returned and saw three times after giving birth and did not afterward have relations; but if she had relations afterward and did not see — even if she originally saw three consecutive times not at the time of childbirth — she is pure; for this is superior to the tube examination. Such is the explanation of the Rabbi's words; and so appears in Eitan HaEzrachi as I explained — see there. And this is proven explicitly in the responsum of R. Yochanan there who wrote: at first glance it appears from his saying in the third birth, in this matter without change — implying that in the third birth as well, etc. — implying all births were exactly equal, and the questioner did not come to ask until after the third birth, and in the third birth necessarily they were consecutive — for otherwise it is obvious she is permitted to everyone even without birth, for we specifically require consecutive, as in the responsum there and as above seif 1; and there was no need for the respondent to find a reason for the childbirth permission; if so it implies the first three times as well were consecutive — see there and investigate; and Bach explained the Rabbi's view that she saw after the birth three times but they were not consecutive, etc., and wrote that so implies from R. Yochanan's responsum — but the opposite is true, as I explained; and apart from this, there is no logic to say so, for since she is permitted after the last birth even if they were consecutive — what does it matter whether the first relations were consecutive or not; and all this appears to me clear.
But if she saw three consecutive times after birth we do not attribute to the birth, etc. It seems to me this is specifically if she saw after thirty-three days for a male or sixty-six days for a female; but within this time, since by law it is pure blood as below siman 194 — it appears we do not remove a woman from her husband for this, for even though nowadays they were stringent not to have relations on pure blood — that was for themselves, but not to remove her from her husband; and similarly the Tur and poskim wrote below siman 190 seif 42 regarding a stain: if she lent it to a woman sitting on pure blood — we attribute to her even nowadays; for even though the daughters of Israel were stringent not to have relations on pure blood — for themselves they were stringent but not regarding saving a fellow — end quote; and it appears all the more so here; and further here there is also another side of permission — that due to the weakness of childbirth she saw blood due to relations. It seems to me.
Nevertheless in every place etc. And in the book Apei Ravrevei (93a) he wrote: it seems to me specifically that she went ahead and had relations three times; and in my humble opinion the Rabbi's words are clear — the reason is that this intercourse in which she did not see is superior to the tube examination; and so implies in the responsum of the Geonim that I brought above s.k. 11 — that a finger is like a tube — see there.
