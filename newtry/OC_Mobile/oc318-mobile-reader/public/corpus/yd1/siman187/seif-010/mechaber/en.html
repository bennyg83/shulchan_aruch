One who sees blood at the time of tashmish is permitted to have tashmish a second time when she becomes tehorah. Nevertheless, we are concerned one time after her seeing; for example, if she saw one time or two times on the night of her immersion, when another immersion arrives she must separate on the night of her immersion. And there is no need to separate on the night of a third immersion, for anything that did not establish a veset we are concerned only one time. {Rama: And if she saw 3 times, each time on the first intercourse after her immersion, she is forbidden to her husband as though she saw 3 consecutive times, for it is impossible for her to immerse and have tashmish with him, since she sees each time after her immersion (a responsum of Rashba, siman 1239, brought by Beit Yosef). And if it happened to her that she saw 3 times on the first intercourse after her birth, or she saw after each birth 3 times and in the meantime she did not see, there are those who wrote to be lenient to permit her to her husband, for we attribute the seeing to her weakness still, by virtue of her birth — that the sides were struck by the birth — and therefore she sees close to the birth and not afterward; and we attribute it to birth as we attribute it to a wound (Hagahos Sha”d, siman 47, and the responsum of Rabbi Yochanan). And all this is when she already passed and had tashmish between the first births, such that acts of permission were established after acts of prohibition; but if she saw 3 consecutive times after a birth, we do not attribute it to birth, but she needs the tube-checking (so it implies there). Nevertheless, anywhere she needs checking: if she transgressed and had tashmish and did not see, she is permitted, for this tashmish in which she did not see is better than the tube-checking, as written above (these are my words).}
