Consecutive. Shach wrote — meaning if it happened that she saw immediately after her birth three consecutive times, and afterward passed and had relations in between and did not see, and afterward returned and saw after her birth three consecutive times and did not have relations again — she is permitted; and likewise even if she saw after each birth three consecutive times, only afterward between each birth she had relations in between and did not see, and after birth three or four she returned and saw three consecutive times and again did not have relations — she is permitted. But if she saw three consecutive times after birth — it is forbidden for her to have relations afterward lekhatchilah, for we permit only when afterward she passed and had relations — for she was established with permitted relations after forbidden relations, and we attribute to weakness of birth; even though she afterward returned and saw three consecutive times after her birth and did not have relations afterward — but if she had relations afterward and did not see, even if she initially saw three consecutive times not at time of birth — she is pure, for better than tube examination. Such is the explanation of the Rabbi's words — end quote; and Taz responded to Rama and emends his words — see there; but in Nekudot HaKesef he refutes all his words at length; and Shach further wrote that these three consecutive times after birth — it appears to me specifically she saw after thirty-three days for a male and sixty-six for a female; but within this time, since by law it is purity blood as below siman 194 — it appears we do not remove a woman from her husband for this; for although nowadays they were stringent not to have relations on purity blood — that is for themselves, but not to remove her from her husband; and see above siman 195 s.a. 2. And in responsum A.S.H. question 1 he wrote: since the later authorities erred in explaining this Hagahah, therefore I will explain — it deals where after each birth she saw three times due to relations from the first relations after immersion — meaning near after her birth when she had relations she saw due to relations and again saw no evidence until she immersed after seeing from relations and immersed and saw again due to relations, and so a third time; and afterward she published niddah and saw from herself as women do and immersed and saw no more due to relations; and so it happened for three births that after each birth she saw three times under one relations after immersion and the sightings were consecutive — only that after the three consecutive sightings near each birth she afterward saw like other women not due to relations and immersed and had relations and did not see — permitted to have relations after she immerses after the three sightings of three consecutive times due to relations, for we may attribute to birth. And in Rama's book it is a scribal error and it should read: if it happened that she saw three times in the first relations after her birth and saw after each birth three times, etc.; and the word "or" is a scribal error; and one can ask — how can he forget permitted relations in between, for Rama wrote it deals where she had relations after her immersion that same night several times, and at each relations she wiped herself with a special cloth, and in the morning she examined the cloths and found blood on one cloth and on the others did not find; and so she did three times at each immersion; and this is what Rama wrote "and if she saw three times," etc. — as if she saw three consecutive times — meaning she wiped herself with three cloths and had relations three times in one night, and the next day she examined herself and found blood on the first cloth; and on this Rama wrote that three times after each immersion are consecutive like consecutive in three relations in one night — unlike Shach s.k. 34. And see further there question 23 who wrote there is room to err in Shulchan Aruch siman 187 who wrote one who sees blood due to relations, etc. — for example if she saw once or twice on the night of her immersion — when another immersion arrived she must separate the night of immersion; and it implies even for three times she is not concerned except for the night of immersion and is permitted to have relations on the second night after her immersion since she established only on the night of immersion; and so Taz understood s.k. 14 — and it is a great error to be concerned only for the first night and not for the first relations being on whatever night it may be; since she was established to see three sightings we have no authority to permit relations with her even not on the night of immersion; but certainly if she was not established he may have relations even on the night of immersion since the veset is not for a known day but due to relations; and in Shulchan Aruch's words there is a scribal error and it should read: one who sees blood, etc. — for example if she saw once or twice on the second night of immersion, etc.; and so is the language of immersion. For the girsa of Shulchan Aruch before us it should have read on the night of immersion; or it deals where after immersion at night she does not see and on the second night sees — if so, since she established once she must be concerned for the second night at the second immersion — end quote.
