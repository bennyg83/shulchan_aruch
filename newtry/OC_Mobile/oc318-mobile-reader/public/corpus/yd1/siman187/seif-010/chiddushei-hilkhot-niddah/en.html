Rabbi Emunat Shmuel: can find three consecutive without prohibition — examined three times one night, next day blood on all three; or before green and pure had relations, afterward dried red tamei retroactively; wound without certainly discharging passed three onot near old age — presumption does not see like veset or old age; attributed to wound pure then saw three onot — presumption sees no veset no wound attribution.
