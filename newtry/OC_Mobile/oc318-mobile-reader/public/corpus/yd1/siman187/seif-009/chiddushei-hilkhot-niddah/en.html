If a woman whose flow had ceased sees again. And if a woman ceased seeing, etc. I have room for doubt regarding a woman who sees blood due to relations and is forbidden to her husband, if she aged until women's habit ceased for her, whether permitted; for we see they have no blood and veset and she is no longer established with blood; perhaps nevertheless from affection of relations and desire she will see even in old age; and that she is permitted here is because a gentile says he healed her, only one may not rely on him; and for such a matter we rely on his heels; but on cessation of her veset due to old age perhaps we do not rely; and there is room to be lenient and say: that which Shulchan Aruch and authorities needed to say here we rely on gentile, implies but for his statement that she was healed even though her menstrual blood ceased she would be forbidden, deals where her way was in years and therefore liable to blood and cessation is only coincidence; and therefore at first time bloods would prevail and issue for boiling of bloods where went, and concerned perhaps in heat of relations source opens and bloods flow, therefore needs doctor's testimony; but in old age whose source of blood dried and withered, from where suspect this veset; and for seeing blood due to relations this veset should not be stricter than veset for time and onah to onah requiring separation, and in days of old age need not separate, why here forbidden. And further appears in that we rely on doctor, not while bloods still sealed without reaching her veset time; plainly if afterward she returned to her state and sees blood at her veset, nevertheless rely on doctor who said she was healed for seeing blood due to relations, we see then his healing was so strong to seal blood even her veset ceased for time; and if so no contradiction or proof to the case above; and from Gemara that said unspecified one may have relations, no proof; in first chapter Niddah regarding woman without a fixed veset R. Meir too forbidden to have relations; and clear intent all time she is not old, then returned she has no veset to have; and even in this one can say forbidden to have relations as long as established with blood; but in old age not, This requires study, for I did not find clear words among early authorities on this.
