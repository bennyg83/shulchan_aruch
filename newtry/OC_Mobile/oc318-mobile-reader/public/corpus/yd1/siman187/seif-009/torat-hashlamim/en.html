We do not permit. Meaning we do not perform this healing lekhatchilah to permit through this (or: and if she was divorced and afterward did this healing, we do not return her to marry except if she did this healing while still under her husband, we do not remove her from her husband, end quote Shach). Behold from what he wrote "we do not return her", implies regarding a husband from whom she was divorced for this he says we do not return her; and if so it is difficult how to say nowadays we do not return, and so forth., behold in any case if he divorces his wife for seeing blood from intercourse he may not return her forever even if she was certainly healed, as explained in Even HaEzer in Shulchan Aruch siman 10 and Tur siman 117; and perhaps a scribal error fell in Shach's books and it should read in place of the word, and so forth.
