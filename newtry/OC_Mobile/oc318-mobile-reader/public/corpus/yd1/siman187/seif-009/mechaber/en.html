They frightened her suddenly and a clot of blood fell from her, and she was healed — she is permitted to her husband. But if she again saw because of tashmish, even one time, it is known that she was not healed; and in this time we do not permit by means of this healing (Ramban and Raavad in Sefer Ba’al HaNefesh). Nevertheless, we do not take her out from her husband after this healing until she has relations and returns to her impairment (Raavad there).
