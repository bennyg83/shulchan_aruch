Thus Rama derived in Darkei Moshe end of siman 191 from responsum Mahari Weil siman 25; but in truth Rama abbreviated in the citation, for in Mahari Weil's responsum there he does not decide the matter; and he wrote: and likewise perhaps the woman is also believed when she says I am certain this blood does not come from the place niddah blood comes; and we do not find anywhen explicitly in the Talmud that she is not believed to say it does not come from the womb, end quote; behold Mahari Weil himself is doubtful in the matter; and although for niddah blood the woman is believed as we derive "and she shall count" and as Mahari Weil wrote there in that responsum, nevertheless he is doubtful here from the reason that perhaps the woman is not expert in this matter, or since there is remedy through examination we do not rely on her in such a case, and so forth.
