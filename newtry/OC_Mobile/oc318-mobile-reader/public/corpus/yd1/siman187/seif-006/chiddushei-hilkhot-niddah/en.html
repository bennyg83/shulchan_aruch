She has no fixed veset. She has no fixed veset, etc. Shach in seif katan 22 questioned this, what Shach wrote: if she feels blood comes from source, the explanation is not certainly from source but whenever in examination we attribute from source and it only excludes stain, see there; and in truth Hagahah faithful in name of Shach implies like Shach's words; but in Shach hard to align his words. But Shach challenged: we begin immediately perhaps she is not niddah, his intent is not to challenge it would reverse as Rabbi Shev Yaakov wrote and therefore came to say he contradicts himself, for impossible to begin on the other side, no place for his challenge, not so Shach's intent here; only his intent all is one doubt what he said if from sides or from wound, all blood not from source is pure, if so all one doubt like they said there one coercion and all included in one doubt; and see laws of double doubt ot 11. Nevertheless it appears already explained in laws of double doubt how when there is difference between doubt and doubt even any matter is not called one doubt; and so Maharik, unintentional and intentional not called one, for this liable sacrifice and this exempt. And if so per what we hold woman's source is impure, blood from wound standing at the source defiles evening tumah as Rambam too; unlike if we say from sides, then blood was not in source and does not defile; and this doubt perhaps from wound is not like doubt perhaps from sides, second doubt adds to permit even for purity; and for all not one doubt as explained in laws of double doubt in Piskei Maharai, correct (abbrev.). But I say (b) otherwise behold this woman stands in presumption of permission for purity; and if so with doubt alone whether from source or sides, how can you forbid her in place of presumption; must say since majority of woman's blood comes from source not sides, we attribute to source not side even against presumption; and this for woman without wound at source; but with wound the majority goes that even if from source nevertheless doubt remains and we establish this woman on presumption presumption of permission, Scriptural presumption; if not written worthy to write this law, no challenge to Rama. But if she knows certainly from source, in this I have doubt whether we permit her to second husband per Mordechai who distinguishes between permitting to second husband and examination with a shefoferets where blood found we forbid to everyone for there is double doubt from sides or source; and if you say from source, perhaps not all fingers equal; unlike here with a shefoferet only one doubt from sides impossible for found only on cloth; if so here she knows certainly from source, even for husband only one doubt; if so forbidden forever; and Rama's rulings do not imply; must say he holds as stated seif katan 4 in Shach per Tosafot therefore permit when saw once with second husband because not all forces equal and we establish this examination with probe lightest of all forces; nevertheless This requires study to be lenient where per Mordechai should be stringent, This requires study.
