And she may marry another. See in responsum Noda Biyhudah second YD siman 93 who wrote if she saw profuse blood she may not be permitted to marry another, for the essence of permission that she is permitted to a second man — Semag wrote there is a double doubt: perhaps from the sides, and perhaps the second man's finger will not equal the first; but when it flows profusely and it is certainly from the source — there is no double doubt; and even if in the first two times she did not see profusely, only the third time — the matter is not clear to permit her to another man; and even tube examination is questionable, since through profuse blood that issued from her it was clarified it is from the source — behold she is in the category of all who fill and scatter — see there.
