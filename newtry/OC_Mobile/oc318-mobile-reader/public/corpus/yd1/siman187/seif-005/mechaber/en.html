If there is a wound in that place, we attribute it to the blood of her wound; and if the blood of her wound is different from the blood of her seeing, we do not attribute it to the blood of her wound. {Rama: And all this is for a woman who has a fixed veset; and then we can attribute, not at the time of her veset, to her wound even though she does not know with certainty that her wound produced blood (so it implies from the rulings of Mahara”i, siman 47, and in Hagahos Sha”d). And likewise if she has no fixed veset, and it is a doubt whether the blood came from the makor or from the sides, we attribute it to the wound by virtue of a safek sfeika: perhaps from the sides or from the makor, and even if from the makor, perhaps it is from the wound. But if it is known that it came from the makor, even if she has a wound in the makor, she does not attribute it to the wound if she has no fixed veset, unless she knows with certainty that her wound produces blood (so it implies in Mordechai of this perek and in Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 11 of Issurei Bi’ah; and see se’if katan 22). Nevertheless, at the time of her veset, or from 30 days to 30 days, she does not attribute it to her wound, for if not so she would never become tamei (see there, and in Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer Mitzvos Gadol and Sefer Mitzvos Katan). And ketamim she attributes to it in every case (in Mordechai).}
