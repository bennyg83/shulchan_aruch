Even though she does not know, etc. I did not descend to the end of Rama's intent in his ruling to align with later poskim whom he follows. In the Gemara in chapter HaTinoket: and a woman is believed to say I have a wound at the source from which blood issues — Shulchan Aruch seif 6; Beit Yosef brought dispute: Rashba explains even if she does not actually feel blood seeping — her eyes are not between her wound and she cannot know except through tube examination; rather she feels the wound and is believed to say I have a wound where there is blood — attributes to her wound; Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot require she know certainly the wound discharges blood. Taz: for complete purity to husband Mordechai and Rama require knowing discharge; for not establishing roeh machmat tashmish Rashba suffices even without knowing — rabbinic leniency; at veset time even knowing wound discharges cannot attribute lest never tamei for complete purity — nevertheless Rama permits not establishing roeh machmat tashmish with veset even unknown wound discharge; seven clean days still required per Shach; Bach: Mechaber like Rashba wound alone suffices — combine with veset; Shach disputes Rashba's scope; wound attribution from sides with veset per Mordechai; double doubt without veset per Hagahah; examination at veset time — attribute to veset not wound; Maharil responsum distinctions; all as poskim elaborated.
