(Taz s.k. 10) He elaborated to challenge Rama — and his language is very garbled; and in truth there is no difficulty, for one who examines Piskei Maharai will see to the eyes that initially in the case of a woman who examines in holes and cracks, etc. — even though she does not know her wound discharges blood — from the reason that since she feels at time of examination when touching that place that hurts her somewhat, and in other holes and cracks she does not feel pain at all — there is proof it comes from the wound; and per this reason even in a woman without fixed veset — pure; and therefore he wrote in Shulchan Aruch seif 7 in an unspecified case one may attribute from a wound on that side, etc.; and he did not write this is specifically for a woman with veset not at veset time. But afterward Maharai wrote there further, etc.
(Taz s.k. 16) He continues after his prior words and it was already explained his words do not exist: Hagahah in Beit Yosef s.v. "and what he said this measure," etc. — since on found blood we do not make them tamei — it appears because we can say perhaps it came after relations, etc. And it is difficult per this — for even within the measure of descending from the bed we do not make them tamei certainly but it is a doubt immediately after that day as proven in mishnah and Gemara; but in truth their view is since guilt-offering dependent is liable it is called seeing blood due to relations, as explained in Semag, Raavad, and other poskim.
