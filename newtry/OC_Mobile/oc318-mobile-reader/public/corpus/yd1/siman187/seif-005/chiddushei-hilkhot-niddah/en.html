If she has a wound. If there is a wound, etc., the Mechaber left unspecified without explaining whether the wound discharges blood or not, for he holds mainly like Rashba as Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, and Bach, no need to know at all; and in an unspecified case we say it discharges and blood comes from it. However Rabbi Shach seif katan 24 wrote Rashba's view is not here at all, Rashba only meant she need not know she now feels blood comes from the wound, but nevertheless must know the wound certainly discharges blood; and Gaon Maharal in his responsa agreed with Shach; examining Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba clarifies no need to know if it discharges, he wrote end gate 4 section 4: reasonable even if she does not actually feel blood seeping from the wound; and there in chapter HaTinoket implies for seeing blood from wound no shefoferet needed, "she exhales blood," etc. "how does she examine with a shefoferet," etc. "if she has wound attributes to wound", evidently even without examination attributes to wound. How applies wound in that place unknown if discharges through examination with a shefoferet, necessarily if no blood on cloth wound does not discharge; if blood on cloth still from where know from wound perhaps from source is niddah blood; must say woman knows wound stands on that side in womb chambers, takes shefoferet with cloth on probe tip toward wound, extracts blood, then clean probe elsewhere, no blood elsewhere proves wound discharges. See Shulchan Aruch in name of Maharai responsum seif 7; stronger when wound known. If doubt only whether wound discharges, not whether during relations blood from wound when seeping; no examination with a shefoferet helps if wound certainly discharges, only doubt whether during relations from wound or source. If not feeling now from wound, permitted; what attribution, she knows certainly from wound; must be not feeling now from wound, therefore teaches attributes to wound. Rashba's proofs unnecessary, certainly all Rashba's proofs even if she does not know wound discharges, we say ordinarily wounds discharge; proof from stains mishnah if can erupt and discharge blood, only regarding stains the Sages did not stringent; in examination This requires study, deals healed wound dry scab; fresh wound plainly no doubt. Why Rama and Acharonim require for a woman who sees blood due to relations wound discharges, perhaps only for complete purity; for a woman who sees blood due to relations attribute to any wound since rabbinic concern and always lenient; baraita language "if she has wound attributes" then repeats "believed to say wound from which blood seeps", two laws: attribute so not a woman who sees blood due to relations unspecified; for complete purity need wound that seeps. Mordechai and Gemara requiring knowing wound discharges, for complete purity; not a woman who sees blood due to relations all agree attribute; Maharil responsum all for complete purity not for husband; Shach seif katan 20 all Rama permits with a fixed veset even unknown wound discharge, only not a woman who sees blood due to relations; nevertheless impure needs seven clean days, interdependent; Rama end: at veset time does not attribute lest never impure, deals complete purity; Shach seif katan 25 strained; truth Bach: Mechaber ruled like Rashba wound alone even unknown suffices, why not lenient with a fixed veset; Shach per his view Rashba also requires knowing discharge; per Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, Bach on Rashba, lenient with a fixed veset; Maharil responsum Maharam lenient not at veset even without feeling from wound blood per veset rabbinic. Shach per his view Mordechai "if veset attributes to veset" alone without wound, strained Mordechai; Minchat Yaakov challenged if within veset days permitted why Gemara oath "within veset days coercion"; Maharatz answered Mordechai a woman who sees blood due to relations unspecified from sides or source woman does not feel during relations unlike oath case felt source opened; Chacham Tzvi on Minchat Yaakov great error, oath deals relations with pure woman who felt; Gemara challenged from found on his; Maharam responsum; mishna way two cloths after relations not at veset; Mordechai if wound certainly discharges pure but impure at veset, perhaps veset time only onah; mishna ch.1 woman with a fixed veset two cloths, if finds also pure; woman establishes veset within veset, doubt tumah perhaps establishes three times; habit in time vs established time; coercion if later revealed establishes veset; Mordechai purifies within veset days, not a woman who sees blood due to relations not fixed veset; cannot purify niddah blood with rabbinic presumption; Maharai; Bach and Rama sweeten Mordechai; Mordechai veset with wound not discharging; proof woman with a fixed veset no a woman who sees blood due to relations, treachery with soiled cloth; Mordechai woman with a fixed veset pure; Shevut Yaakov on Mordechai found on his perhaps from compression from sides, worse than woman without a fixed veset.
