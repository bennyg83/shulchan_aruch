If there is a wound, etc. — the Mechaber left unspecified without explaining whether the wound discharges blood or not, for he holds mainly like Rashba as Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, and Bach — no need to know at all; and in an unspecified case we say it discharges and blood comes from it. However Rabbi Shach s.k. 24 wrote Rashba's view is not here at all — Rashba only meant she need not know she now feels blood comes from the wound, but nevertheless must know the wound certainly discharges blood; and Gaon Maharal in his responsa agreed with Shach; examining Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba clarifies no need to know if it discharges — he wrote end gate 4 section 4: reasonable even if she does not actually feel blood seeping from the wound; and there in chapter HaTinoket implies for seeing blood from wound no tube needed — "she exhales blood," etc. "how does she examine with tube," etc. "if she has wound attributes to wound" — evidently even without examination attributes to wound. How applies wound in that place unknown if discharges through tube examination — necessarily if no blood on cloth wound does not discharge; if blood on cloth still from where know from wound perhaps from source is niddah blood; must say woman knows wound stands on that side in womb chambers, takes tube with cloth on probe tip toward wound, extracts blood, then clean probe elsewhere — no blood elsewhere proves wound discharges. See Shulchan Aruch in name of Maharai responsum seif 7; stronger when wound known. If doubt only whether wound discharges — not whether at relations blood from wound when seeping; no tube examination helps if wound certainly discharges — only doubt whether at relations from wound or source. If not feeling now from wound — permitted; what attribution — she knows certainly from wound; must be not feeling now from wound — therefore teaches attributes to wound. Rashba's proofs unnecessary — certainly all Rashba's proofs even if she does not know wound discharges — we say ordinarily wounds discharge; proof from stains mishnah if can erupt and discharge blood — only regarding stains chazal did not stringent; in examination puzzling — deals healed wound dry scab; fresh wound plainly no doubt. Why Rama and Acharonim require for roeh machmat tashmish wound discharges — perhaps only for complete purity; for roeh machmat tashmish attribute to any wound since rabbinic concern and always lenient; baraita language "if she has wound attributes" then repeats "believed to say wound from which blood seeps" — two laws: attribute so not roeh machmat tashmish unspecified; for complete purity need wound that seeps. Mordechai and Gemara requiring knowing wound discharges — for complete purity; not roeh machmat tashmish all agree attribute; Maharil responsum all for complete purity not for husband; Shach s.k. 20 all Rama permits with veset even unknown wound discharge — only not roeh machmat tashmish; nevertheless tamei needs seven clean days — interdependent; Rama end: at veset time does not attribute lest never tamei — deals complete purity; Shach s.k. 25 strained; truth Bach: Mechaber ruled like Rashba wound alone even unknown suffices — why not lenient with veset; Shach per his view Rashba also requires knowing discharge; per Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, Bach on Rashba — lenient with veset; Maharil responsum Maharam lenient not at veset even without feeling from wound blood per veset rabbinic. Shach per his view Mordechai "if veset attributes to veset" alone without wound — strained Mordechai; Magen Avraham challenged if within veset days permitted why Gemara oath "within veset days coercion"; Maharatz answered Mordechai roeh machmat tashmish unspecified from sides or source woman does not feel at relations unlike oath case felt source opened; Chacham Tzvi on Magen Avraham great error — oath deals relations with pure woman who felt; Gemara challenged from found on his; Maharam responsum; mishna way two cloths after relations not at veset; Mordechai if wound certainly discharges pure but tamei at veset — perhaps veset time only onah; mishna ch.1 woman with veset two cloths — if finds also pure; woman establishes veset within veset — doubt tumah perhaps establishes three times; habit in time vs established time; coercion if later revealed establishes veset; Mordechai purifies within veset days — not roeh machmat tashmish not fixed veset; cannot purify niddah blood with rabbinic presumption; Maharai; Bach and Rama sweeten Mordechai; Mordechai veset with wound not discharging; proof woman with veset no roeh machmat tashmish — treachery with soiled cloth; Mordechai woman with veset pure; Shevut Yaakov on Mordechai found on his perhaps from compression from sides — worse than woman without veset.
