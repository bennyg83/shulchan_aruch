(Shach s.k. 20) Because of agunah they were lenient. Otherwise one can say regarding her husband: for the prohibition is only that since she was established to see due to relations — perhaps she will see at time of relations, which is veset matter that is only rabbinic as we hold vesetot are rabbinic — for this we rely to be lenient to attribute to a wound; but for seven clean days we do not rely.
