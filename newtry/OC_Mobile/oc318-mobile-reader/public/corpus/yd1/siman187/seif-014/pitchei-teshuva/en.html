And she is forbidden. See in responsum Noda Biyhudah YD siman 46 who wrote: no examination helps her anymore, even if she examines again and finds on the sides of the tube or finds nothing — she remains forbidden forever; and also on doctors' healing one may not rely after this examination unless she passes a clot of blood — then she returns to permission — see there; and so Chiddushei Dinim — even if she examined again afterward several times and found on the sides — forbidden, for we are concerned perhaps she sees due to desire of relations; and if she transgressed and had relations three times and did not find blood — she is permitted — see there; however this is specifically examination after the third husband; but examination after the first husband has a remedy as below s.k. 18.
