She is concerned. Shach wrote, and it appears to me likewise for the new veset she is concerned — for example if she had relations a second time on 15 Nisan she is concerned afterward for 15 Iyar and is forbidden to have relations on 15 Iyar; and if she did not see on 15 Iyar she is concerned for the interval half a year and again is not concerned for the 15th of the month; and likewise after she had relations the first time she is immediately concerned for the new veset; but for the interval veset — not without a second sighting; and see above siman 189 s.a. 19 and s.k. 22. And Taz wrote, and some say — why did he say in this siman in the Hagahah three times after her immersion, for with two times she must also separate at immersion as here; for there it refers to forbidding her to her husband; and because of this concern that she was not established three times — they do not forbid her forever to her husband, but wherever one can be concerned hour by hour — she is concerned since afterward she will have permission — end quote.
