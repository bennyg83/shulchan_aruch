She is permitted to her husband since she did not establish a veses. Meaning: for this reason she is permitted even during the days of the veses, since it was not established three times — she is only concerned once, and afterward she is permitted to have relations. So wrote the Shach, and so is clear from the words of the Rashba and the language of the author here in seif 10. What the Taz wrote regarding this in seif katan 15 is not understood by me, for his words are superfluous.
However, she is concerned for the last one, one time. The Shach wrote, and it appears to me that the same applies to the monthly veses — she is concerned. For example, if she had relations a second time on the 15th of Nissan, she is subsequently concerned for the 15th of Iyar, and it is forbidden to have relations on the 15th of Iyar. If she did not see on the 15th of Iyar, she is concerned for an interval of half a year, and then she is no longer concerned for the 15th of the month. Similarly, after she had relations the first time, she is immediately concerned for the monthly veses, but she is not concerned for the interval-based veses since it is impossible without a second sighting. However, she is not concerned for the bodily veses when she does not establish it on a known day, for that is like a random occurrence, as explained below siman 189 seif 17. Thus far the Shach.
She is permitted to have relations between one veses and the next. We do not say that since the veses was established three times — such as through relations on Rosh Chodesh or the 15th of the month — therefore the first onah from Rosh Chodesh onward or from the 15th onward is established and she is subsequently prohibited from having relations. For since the veses was established for specific days, immediately upon the passing of that day she is permitted to have relations, as stated above seif katan 28.
Until it is uprooted three times. Meaning she passes and has intercourse, for without intercourse it is not uprooted because it resembles establishment of jumps and days as stated seif katan 37 in name of Shach; and if so it is not uprooted unless the occurrence and time come as explained below siman 189, see there Shach seif katan 50; and this is not comparable for what is stated seif katan 34 that here she is not forbidden to her husband, puzzling.
