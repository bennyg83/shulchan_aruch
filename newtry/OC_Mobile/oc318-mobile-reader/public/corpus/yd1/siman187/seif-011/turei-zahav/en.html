Nevertheless, she must be concerned for the last occurrence, etc. One should not ask why the Rama wrote in seif 10, in the gloss, that she saw three times after immersion, when even after two occurrences she must separate following immersion, as stated here. There the discussion concerns permanently forbidding her to her husband. Because of a concern that has not been established through three occurrences, we do not forbid her to him permanently. Whenever it is possible, however, to observe the concern temporarily, she must do so, since she will afterward have a means of becoming permitted. So it appears to me. I mentioned a similar principle immediately above, in accordance with the Rama's words.
