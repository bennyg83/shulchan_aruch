She is permitted to her husband, for she has not established etc. Meaning: therefore when six months have passed from the last seeing and she did not see — she is no longer concerned at all; and so in Rashba’s responsum brought by Beit Yosef: she is no longer concerned, for anything not established three times is uprooted once; to exclude if she had established — she would need to uproot three times, as in the next case; but certainly even if she established in three equal vestot or by a skip — she is permitted to her husband between veset and veset, as in the next case; and see above siman 189 regarding the law of establishing vestot equally or by skip.
Nevertheless she is concerned for the last etc. And it seems to me the same applies to the monthly veset — she is concerned, such as if she had relations the second time on the 15th of Nisan — she is afterward concerned for the 15th of Iyar and is forbidden to have relations on the 15th of Iyar; and if she did not see on the 15th of Iyar she is concerned for the interval of six months and is then no longer concerned for the 15th of the month; and similarly after she had relations the first time she is immediately concerned for the monthly veset; but for the interval veset — that is impossible without a second seeing; and so we say below siman 189 seif 19 and 22 regarding bodily vestot; and although there she must be concerned for bodily vestot even not at the time of the monthly veset, such as if she yawned once and saw — she must be concerned when she yawns again at whatever time — that case is different, for the bodily veset comes on its own; but regarding a bodily veset that comes through coercion such as jumping — we hold there in seif 17 she is only concerned if she established it on a known day, and not every time she jumps; and relations counts as coming through coercion like jumping; and see above siman 189 s.k. 44.
