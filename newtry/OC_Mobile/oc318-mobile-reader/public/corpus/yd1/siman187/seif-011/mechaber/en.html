A woman who saw because of tashmish, and after half a year again saw because of tashmish, is permitted to her husband, for she did not establish [it] with 3 equal vesatos and not by skipping. Nevertheless, she is concerned for the last one one time: when half a year arrives from the day of the last seeing of blood, she is forbidden for one onah. And if she established a veset for seeing blood because of tashmish 3 times — an equal veset — she is permitted to have tashmish between veset and veset, but on the days of the veset she separates until it is uprooted 3 times.
