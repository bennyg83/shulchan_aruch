It should be attributed to the fact that it came from a wound on that side. Even a woman who does not have a fixed veses — in such a case she is pure. This is also implied from the responsa of Maharai in siman 47, and is also clear and implied from the unqualified language of the author. The Shach likewise wrote in Sefer Nekudos HaKesef, unlike the implication of the Tzamas Tzedek in siman 66, who understood that even in such a case one does not attribute it to the wound unless she has a veses and it is not during the time of her veses. In truth this is not so — and this is straightforward.
