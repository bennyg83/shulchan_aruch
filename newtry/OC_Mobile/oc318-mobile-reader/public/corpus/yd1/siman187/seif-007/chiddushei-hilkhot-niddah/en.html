She says clear to me, etc. Magen Avraham wondered why Rama decides she is believed whereas from responsum Mahari Weil siman 25 he himself did not say decisively except to join with other views; and it appears Magen Avraham himself wrote therefore Mahari Weil was doubtful even though Torah believed her because possible to establish her through examination — and this in Mahari Weil's case where she urinated water and it came with urine — behold examination to clarify through inserting clean cloth in that place as stated siman 191 — see there; unlike here seeing due to relations and she says she feels through compression of relations it comes from sides — how possible to establish that even if she inserts probe and cloth in this it clarifies she is not established seeing due to relations and permitted to her husband; but nevertheless not for this clarified blood comes from sides to purify her from niddah blood and not need seven clean days and pure without immersion; and needless to say thousand tubes do not help purify from niddah — we say that time it did not fit and another time it fits; if so she has no remedy through examination and we rely on her statement for Torah believed her.
