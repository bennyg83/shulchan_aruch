To the third. It is written in Baer Heitev of Maharit z"l: an elderly woman who ceased seeing for a long time and began seeing due to relations and feels pain and ache — she need not tube examination. Beit Yosef question 68 — end quote. And he further wrote in Shevut Yaakov there; but if in her youth she saw three times due to relations — what that she aged afterward does not help — see there; and see in Seder Taharah s.k. 26 who questioned this and concluded requires study for law. Further Seder Taharah wrote: a woman who saw three times due to relations and conceived and gave birth — permitted to have relations afterward after she immerses in her purity days; and if she does not see one can say permitted afterward even after purity days pass, for we hold it is one source; and so wrote, etc.
