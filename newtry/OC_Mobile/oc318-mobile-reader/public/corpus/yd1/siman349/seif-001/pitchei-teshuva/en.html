Among gentiles. See the Baer Heitev of the Rav Maharit z"l. And see in Responsa Even HaShoham siman 30, who concluded that a deceased Israelite is biblically forbidden for benefit, but a deceased gentile is rabbinically forbidden — and for that reason he wrote there regarding one who was dangerously ill and the doctors said to take a bone from a deceased person for some medicine: it is permitted to take a bone from a deceased gentile for this, since it is only a rabbinic prohibition. See there. Baruch, author of Ta'am HaMelekh. Ta'am HaMelekh.
