(Siman 349, Shulchan Aruch, seif 1) A non-Jew, etc., his shrouds are forbidden for benefit. See Nekudot HaKesef and Baer Heitev, who cite Tosafot, Bava Kamma 10, implying that they are permitted for benefit. Shevut Yaakov distinguishes that only shrouds owned by a Jew are permitted, and Beit Hillel likewise rules.
