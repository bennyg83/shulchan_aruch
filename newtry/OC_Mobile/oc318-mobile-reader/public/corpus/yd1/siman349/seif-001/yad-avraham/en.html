(Siman 349, seif 1) A gentile, and so forth, his shrouds are prohibited in benefit. See Nekudot HaKesef and Baer Heitev, who cite Tosafot Bava Kamma 10 as implying they are permitted in benefit. Responsa Shevut Yaakov distinguishes that only property belonging to a Jew is permitted, and Beit Hillel likewise writes this.
