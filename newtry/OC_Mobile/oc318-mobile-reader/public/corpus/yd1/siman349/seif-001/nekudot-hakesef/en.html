(Siman 349, Shulchan Aruch seif 1) Whether a gentile. Beit Yosef in Bedek HaBayit writes this in the name of Responsa Rashba, siman 365. However, Tosafot, Bava Kamma 10a, at the end of s.v. shehashor, implies that the corpse of a gentile is permitted in benefit. The same follows from Maggid Mishneh, Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot chapter 2, who writes in Ramban's name that the flesh of a deceased Jew is prohibited in benefit, implying that the corpse of a gentile is permitted.
