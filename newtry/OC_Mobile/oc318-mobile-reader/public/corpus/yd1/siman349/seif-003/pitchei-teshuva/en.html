When they are attached. See Baer Heitev and Panim Meirot, part 3, responsum 33. A man died with an artificial tooth made from bone of a non-kosher carcass in his mouth. One scholar wished to remove and burn it, comparing it to ornaments of the dead. Panim Meirot rejected this and ruled that it need not be burned; it should remain in place and be buried with him.
