Ornaments of the dead attached to his body, such as a foreign wig and the like, are forbidden like the dead himself (and specifically when attached to the hairs of his body; but if not attached, permitted (as Rashi implies, first chapter of Arakhin); therefore permitted to take rings on the hands of the dead and the like). When is this said—in an unspecified case; but if he commanded that ornaments attached to him be given to his son or daughter or for another need, they are permitted; but his actual hair, even if he commanded about it, is forbidden in benefit. {Rama: A woman going out to be executed—benefit is taken from her hair; even though her judgment is finished, her hairs are not forbidden until she is executed (Tur).}
