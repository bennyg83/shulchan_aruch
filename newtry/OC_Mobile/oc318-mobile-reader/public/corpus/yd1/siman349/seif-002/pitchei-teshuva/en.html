In benefit. See the Be'er Heitev of Rabbi Maharit z"l. Regarding what he wrote, that nevertheless it is permitted to eat mummy, etc., see on this matter Mishneh LaMelekh, Hilkhot Ma'akhalot Asurot chapter 8 law 18, Hilkhot Avel chapter 3 law 1, and chapter 14 law 21.
