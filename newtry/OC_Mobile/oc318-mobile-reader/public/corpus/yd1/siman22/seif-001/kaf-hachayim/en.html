(a) [Seif 1] In a bird one must slaughter the veins, etc. For we taught in tractate Chullin (daf 27a): One who slaughters one in a bird and two in an animal—his shechitah is valid; and a majority of one is like it—R' Yehuda says: until he slaughters the veins; and we say in the Gemara (daf 28b): R' Yehuda said only in a bird, since he roasts it entirely as one piece; but in an animal, since he cuts it limb by limb, he need not. And Tosafot, Rashba, Ra'osh, and Ran wrote: the halakhah is like R' Yitzchak, as we say in the first chapter of Berakhot (daf 8b): take heed regarding veins like R' Yehuda. And the reason Ra'osh wrote is because if he did not slaughter the veins, blood would remain and not emerge—not by salting and not by roasting—all the time the bird is whole—Beit Yosef, see there; and therefore he ruled thus in Shulchan Aruch.
