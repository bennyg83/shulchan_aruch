An animal—even kids and small lambs—nevertheless there is no order to roast whole—Shach.
To roast it whole—it appears that if all the limbs of the animal depend on each other, even though they are cut into many pieces, nevertheless blood will not flow—otherwise how do you produce an animal roasted whole; behold one must puncture behind it and remove the gid hanasheh and gidim hanging on it and gidim of the tail and all membranes until it must mostly be cut into many pieces; and see Rambam in Laws of Korban Pesach chapter 10 halakhah 11 and in Ra'avad's hasagah, and so appears he holds: in order to place the Pesach whole without lack of a limb they permitted the fat of the gid and similar membranes—and it requires study.
And unspecified bird—this was possible in Rama's time, but now it was not and is not done—so Taz.
For it was not aroused, etc.; and Rashba in Mishmeret HaBayit brings proof from what is taught: and they are eaten with soiled hands—from which we learn rinsing is not required, for it is not presumed in blood; and this proof is rejected—for one can say with fruit juice as Ran wrote; and see below siman 69 what Tosafot wrote there: his reasoning is sound—what help is cutting veins if he slaughtered and cut in the simanim and blood did not emerge, how will it emerge in the veins.
