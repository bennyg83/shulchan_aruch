(In the gloss) As it absorbs so it expels, as below siman 76. I did not know this, for there it is explained in the reverse—for according to one view there it is forbidden to leave it roasting on the spit after it was removed from the fire, and that is Rashba's view that after removal from fire we do not say as it absorbs so it expels; and the view of those who permit there holds the spit did not absorb, for fire rests—understand.
