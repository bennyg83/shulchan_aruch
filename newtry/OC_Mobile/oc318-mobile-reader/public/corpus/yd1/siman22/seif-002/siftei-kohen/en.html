An animal, etc. It implies that every animal need not cutting of veins—even kids and lambs that are sometimes roasted whole, since it is not their way to do so—as Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe wrote; and so is the implication of the poskim; and so the acharonim agreed.
But if blood did not emerge, etc. And Ra'ah in Sefer Bedek HaBayit daf 20 wrote that even when blood did not emerge, one must cut limb by limb—see there.
