An animal—one need not puncture the veins at the time of shechitah, because it is not customary to roast it whole; but if he wants to roast it whole, he must puncture its veins at the time of shechitah; and if he did not puncture them, it is forbidden to roast it or cook it whole: {Rama: And if he roasted or cooked it whole, its law is like a bird (Beit Yosef according to several poskim); and an unspecified bird—its veins were slaughtered; and an unspecified animal—its veins were not slaughtered; and all this when blood emerged at the time of shechitah; but if blood did not emerge, we are not concerned for slaughter of the veins, for the blood was not aroused to emerge (Ran, chapter HaShochet, in the name of Rashba—and he is in Mishmeret HaBayit daf 20).}
