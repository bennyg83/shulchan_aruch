And likewise a woman etc. Bach wrote it requires investigation for practice whether to be lenient, since even Tosafot's view is like Rashi, etc. — see there; it implies there specifically when she sees niddah blood in the tissue; but when the pieces fall from her without blood — she is pure.
Even though she saw etc. Bach wrote it implies he holds we do not require that she examined herself at the moment the tissue was extracted — and this is not correct; rather we specifically require that at the moment it fell into the outer chamber she examined and found no blood at all; but subsequently while they were in the outer chamber she kept seeing as long as those pieces were in the outer chamber — and Rabbi Shimshon deemed her pure, etc. — see there.
For we attribute etc. And it is due to a wound; see responsa of Maharam of Lublin siman 111.
And it is when she expelled etc. As in the next case where a creature-form came out of her like peels or hairs, etc.; however perhaps here it is different, since it was recognizable in them that they were pieces of flesh, only that blood was inside them — end of Beit Yosef's words; and in Darkei Moshe he wrote: and according to what he wrote, that here it deals with where it was known that her source was extracted — if so here too Beit Yosef's words are not correct, for even without examination it was known this is not congealed blood but rather that the source was extracted; and he needed to permit only so we not say this itself is the manner of seeing — as above — end quote.
Tosafot wrote at the end of chapter Kol HaYad that he may look at his wife's blood; and Maharshal brings this in his glosses; and likewise Agudah there.
