Avodat HaGoren wrote further Bach invented, etc.; and see in Me'iri and Sha'arei Teshuvah that they also disagreed with Bach; and greater than this wrote in responsum Etz Chaim siman 46 — even if decisor saw while moist and had pure appearance and afterward when dried found impure appearance — pure; and we always follow emergence from body whether to impurify or purify — see there; and see in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 74 who disagreed on this; but Sha'arei Teshuvah wrote his proof is not compelling; also Chiddushei Dinim agreed with Chavatzelet HaSharon on this; and see in She'elot Ya'avetz part 1 end siman 12 who wrote he fears to act per his father's view and his view to be stringent — for if happened it returned to impure appearance after drying — room to rule impure even though already ruled leniently and we are not concerned for disgrace; and brings proof from above siman 52 seif 3 regarding reddened by boiling that we say "it was revealed in its diminution"; and all the more here where no act was done — see there. And what Avodat HaGoren wrote I did not understand, etc. — see in Sefer Nachalat Azriel on Niddah page 20 s.v. Rabbi saw who wrote it is not a challenge — see there. But per my humble view nevertheless Beit Yosef's words here are not understood to me — for in siman 186 there need not see at night at all as Rama wrote there except examination so she know tomorrow — Avodat HaGoren there s.k. 2; and I found in Chiddushei Dinim who sensed this [and must say Taz holds Rama there refers only to examination after relations as stated there s.k. 3 in name of responsum Ahavah who was doubtful on this].
