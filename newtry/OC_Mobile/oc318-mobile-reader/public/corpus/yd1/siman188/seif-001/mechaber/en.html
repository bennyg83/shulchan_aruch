The laws of the appearances of blood; it contains 6 se’ifim. Every red appearance, whether it is very faint or deep, is tamei; and likewise every black appearance. And there is nothing tahor except a white appearance; and likewise a green appearance, even like the appearance of wax or gold, and all the more so the green like leek-green or like grasses (Mordechai). (And likewise the appearance that they call in the vernacular “bluo” is included in green.) And even if it has the consistency of blood and it is very thick — and even if she felt that her makor opened and she checked immediately and found these appearances — she is tehorah. (These are my words, not like Terumas HaDeshen, siman 246, who stammers about this.)
{Rama: And so is the main ruling (Beit Yosef and Tur), unlike those who are stringent to deem her tamei if it has the consistency and it is thick (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 246). (And if she felt that her makor opened and she checked immediately and found nothing, see above siman 190.)}
