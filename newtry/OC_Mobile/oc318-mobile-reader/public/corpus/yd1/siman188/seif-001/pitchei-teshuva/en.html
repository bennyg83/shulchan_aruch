Red appearance. See responsum Givat Shaul siman 63 who wrote regarding stain that at night appeared impure and by day when dry appeared pure appearance — room to be lenient and purify — see there. [And in Sefer Levushei Sharad siman 78 he wrote on this — but if after he saw by day he saw again after an hour and sees it changed from first appearance — room to forbid — see there and above s.k. 3.] [And see in Sha'arei Teshuvah siman 190 end s.k. 93 — his view specifically if at night doubtful to him, etc.; but where established certainly impure through her seeing at night — even though returned and saw by day and has pure appearance — no room to be lenient — see there.] And see in Sha'arei Teshuvah in name of MeKor Chayyim that blood tending to color brown that is like appearance of chestnut shell and like beverage (coffee) is pure — and needless to say regarding stain for it does not tend to redness, etc. — see there; and I also found this written in She'elot Ya'avetz part 1 siman 44 — see there.
