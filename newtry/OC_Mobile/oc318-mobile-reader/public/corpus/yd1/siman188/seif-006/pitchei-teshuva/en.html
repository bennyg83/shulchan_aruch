We rely on her. Avodat HaGoren wrote and likewise woman, etc. — she may not rely on her friend, etc.; and see in Sha'arei Teshuvah who wrote even on herself she cannot rely in this — for example she knows sage thus purified her and must show also now to sage; and see in Tuv Ta'am VeDa'at who wrote even if she brings first blood and says this is what sage thus purified for me — then no concern perhaps she erred in imaginings — nevertheless we do not rely on her — see there; and what Avodat HaGoren wrote — can see blood of his wife — see in Sefer Chokhmat Adam kelal 95 who wrote if question arose to her regarding immersion he cannot rule after presumption of prohibition was established — see there; and see in responsum Chavot Yair siman 121.
