And similarly, a woman who is uncertain about her blood should not rely on her friend who showed her blood and said: "blood like this I also showed to such-and-such a sage and he declared it pure." (However, if she says this stain or this blood was declared pure by the sage, she is believed — see below siman 185 s.3 in the Hagahah.) The Tosafot wrote that a sage may examine the blood of his wife and bring it before the authorities; Maharshal.
