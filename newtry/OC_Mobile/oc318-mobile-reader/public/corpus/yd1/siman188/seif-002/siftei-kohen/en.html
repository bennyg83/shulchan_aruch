We do not rely. And likewise this woman who is in doubt about her blood — she may not rely on her friend who showed her blood and said to her "blood like yours I also showed to such-and-such sage and he deemed it pure" — so Rashi, brought by Bach; however it implies that if she says "this blood or this stain the sage deemed pure for me" — she is believed; and so we say in chapter HaMadir; so implies above siman 185 seif 3 in the note.
Tosafot wrote at the end of chapter Kol HaYad that he may look at his wife's blood; and Maharshal brings this in his glosses; and likewise Agudah there.
