There, in the Mishnah on 19a, in accordance with the Sages and Tosafot's explanation there on 19b, beginning with the words "The green."
(°) Although the halachah follows Beit Hillel, who declare appearances like the liquid of roasted meat and fenugreek water pure, since those colors tend toward red and we are not expert in appearances, we declare them impure. We declare only white and green pure, because they have no reddish tendency at all.
