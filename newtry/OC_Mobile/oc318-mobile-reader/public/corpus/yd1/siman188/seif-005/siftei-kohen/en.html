But if there is upon them, etc. This is not comparable to above, seif 3, where if she saw blood in a piece, even if it was split, she is pure. There the blood is inside the pieces, only within their cracks; here the bloody moisture is on top of the pieces. So writes Maadanei Melech there.
"And specifically," etc. This requires study. No posek is found who distinguishes here between the time of her veset and another time. It is not comparable to above, siman 187, seif 5. There, since she visibly sees blood, we do not attribute it to her wound at the time of her veset, because otherwise she would never become impure. Here, however, she does not see blood, but only dry objects; therefore even at the time of her veset she should be pure. This requires further study. I later found in Api Ravrevei, daf 89b: "It appears to me that since the principal reason is that this is not blood but a creature, she is pure even at the time of her veset. Indeed, even regarding blood from a wound, Rambam and Rashba hold that she is pure at the time of her veset because we rule that veset determinations are rabbinic. All the more so in this case, where according to everyone she is pure."
