But if they have upon them etc. And not comparable to above seif 3, where if she saw blood in a piece even if it split open — she is pure; for there the blood is inside the pieces, only it is in the crevices; but here the blood moisture is on top of the pieces — so wrote the Sefer Ma’adanei Melech there.
And specifically, etc. This requires study, for in Tur and Beit Yosef it is explained that even with things that are dry from their beginning and do not dissolve in water, one rules stringently. So too the Api Rabravi writes, and his words are correct; see there.
