In what case are these matters said — that if they did not dissolve she is tehorah? When they are completely dry, and there is no blood with them at all. But if there is on them any moisture of blood, she is tamei. {Rama: And the same law applies if they were crushed, or some of them dissolved and some of them did not dissolve — for she is tamei (the rulings of Mahara”i, siman 47). Nevertheless, it appears to me that if she checked 3 times everything that she saw, and they did not dissolve at all, she no longer needs to check what she sees afterward in this manner, for she has been established that these things are not blood, but rather come from a wound in her body. And this is specifically for a woman who has a veset, and not at the time of her veset, as was explained regarding a wound above in siman 187.}
