Veset. Shach wrote "requires investigation" — it is not found anywhere that poskim divide here between her veset time or not; and it is not comparable to above siman 187 seif 5 — for there since she sees blood directly we do not attribute to her wound during her veset time lest she never become impure forever; but here behold she does not see blood but dry things; therefore even during her veset time she is pure. And I found again in Sefer Epei Ravrevei — and this is its language: it seems to us that since the main reason it is not blood but only a pure creature, even during her veset time — for even regarding wound blood Rambam and Rashba hold pure even during veset time, for we hold vesetot are rabbinic — all the more in such case pure according to all — end quote.
