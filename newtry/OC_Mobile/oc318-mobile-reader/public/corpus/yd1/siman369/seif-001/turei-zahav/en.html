A kohen is warned not to become impure through a corpse. This includes even a nonviable infant, as stated in the final chapter of Ohalot, since miscarried infants may have been buried there.
The grave-cover. This is the stone placed over the grave as a marker. The supporting stone means smaller stones sometimes placed to support it, as stated by the Tur.
It is not known beneath which one, etc. This implies that if it is known beneath which stone the corpse lies, a kohen may stand over the other stones, provided they are not part of the grave's cover or support and there is no connected overhang transmitting impurity.
