Which impurity the kohen is warned about. In it is one seif. The kohen is warned not to become impure from the dead nor from any impurities that separate from it, nor from the golel and dofek, nor from a limb from the living that lacks enough to raise redness if it were attached, nor from a tree shading the ground whose branches are separated from each other and impurity is beneath one of them and it is unknown beneath which, or stones protruding from a fence and impurity beneath one and it is unknown beneath which; and likewise a field in which a grave was plowed and its place is unknown; and all lands of the nations are forbidden for a kohen to become impure in them:
{Rama: Some say kohanim are forbidden to become impure for a sword that became impure from the dead (Kol Bo in the name of Sefer Yereim and responsum of Rashba in the name of our French rabbis); and some are lenient (responsum of Rashba there in the name of Raavad; and see Tosafot Nazir at length); and so they practiced to be lenient and are not careful about this.}
