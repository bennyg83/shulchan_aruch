A corpse. Even if it fell there, as stated in the last chapter of Ohalot — perhaps they buried stillborn infants in it. Taz (but regarding the impurity of animal carcasses and creeping things, a kohen is not warned against touching them) (Bah):
The golel. It is a stone placed on the grave as a marker. And "dofeik" means that sometimes the stone is propped up with small stones; Tur (and Rashi explained: golel is the cover of the coffin, and dofeik is the side panel. Ketubot folio 4):
That overspreads: (Even though there is only a doubt of impurity here, a kohen is warned about it.) It is proven from here that a tree that overspreads a corpse — one may stand under its branches that do not overhang the corpse, for here it is not forbidden to stand under the tree except because it is not known under which branch [the grave lies]. Perisheh.
