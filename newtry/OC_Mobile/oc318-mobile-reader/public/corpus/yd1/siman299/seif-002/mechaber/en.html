One who makes a garment entirely of camel wool or hares or hemp, and wove in it a thread of wool on this side and a thread of linen on that side—this is forbidden because of shaatnez. {Rama: And some say it is permitted in such a case unless he wove a thread of wool next to the thread of linen; but if they do not touch one another, it is permitted (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh and R' Shimshon). And see below siman 300, for they are accustomed to be lenient.}
