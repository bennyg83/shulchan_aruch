(Siman 353, Shach, seif katan 1) Rather, upon the shoulders. The Nimukei Yosef writes: “Not in a large casket,” etc. That is, not in one so large that it can be carried only upon his shoulder, but rather in a small casket that can be carried at the sides, upon his arms.
