A child of thirty full days goes out in a gloskema (meaning: coffin; Targum: and place him in a coffin — Nimmukei Yosef) carried on side-poles (meaning: wide board; meaning of side-poles: arms); and they stand in a row and say over him the mourners' blessing and condolences. A child of twelve months goes out on a bier; and all who go out on a bier — many accompany him; and one who does not go out on a bier — many do not accompany him; and whoever is known to the many — the many are occupied with him; and whoever is not known to the many — the many are not obligated to be occupied with him.
