(There seif 20) One may not salt meat before removing forbidden threads, membranes, and gid hanasheh; if he salted, removes afterward and cooks. So Tur in name of Rambam; see Beit Yosef here and siman 105, for Rambam gid hanasheh salted or roasted with meat is permitted without peeling; wonder on Taz: per them, thigh roasted with its giddim requires removal, salting requires peeling as siman 105 seif 5 and 9 — see there; and here they brought Rambam as if primary; and some forbid peeling and contradict each other;
