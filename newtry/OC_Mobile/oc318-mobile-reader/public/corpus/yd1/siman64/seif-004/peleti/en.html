Tosevet, membrane, and peeled — so is Tur's language as well; and one may analyze: since his father's view, the Rosh, is that we practice prohibition whether on the arch or on the yeter — as Maharshal and all authors wrote, who practiced like Rebbe — if their practice is to hold the law like R. Yehuda that membrane and peeled alone is chelev, how did Tur rule simply that tosevet is required; it must be Tur holds they do not everywhere forbid membrane and peeled alone, only regarding the yeter; therefore he ruled tosevet is required for everyone.
