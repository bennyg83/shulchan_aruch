(11) [Seif 4] Which is chelev — anything that is tosevet. It is his word that we translate: it is tosevet, when it is a word spread on the innards. Rashi Chullin 59b. Shach s.k. 7. Shaarei De'ah ot 7 — meaning spread and sitting on the innards, namely membrane separates between it and flesh; as Tur wrote, and so Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh 37a explained. And included in the innards are kereis, diaphragm, and beit hakosot. Peri Chadash ot 9; and see below seif 8.
(12) There. Membrane and peeled — a thin membrane is on it, and the membrane is peeled from above the chelev that is not so adherent. Rashi there. Shach s.k. 8. And Tur wrote membrane and peeled — meaning spread on the membrane such that when the membrane is peeled it peels with it. And Bach wrote what Rashi explained is according to R. Yishmael, but according to R. Akiva Rashi would also explain like Tur, that it is lower membrane that chelev spreads on; and he wrote such is the essence. And so Taz s.k. 5. Peri Chadash ot 10.
