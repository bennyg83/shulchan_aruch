(8) There. And some say if its months were completed and found alive, etc. — its chelev forbidden, etc. So too Rokeach siman 364: its chelev and gid forbidden. And so Bach. And so Shach in his Long Tur on Tur. And so here on Shulchan Aruch s.k. 4; and he wrote: and not like Levush, who brought only the first reason. And Peri Chadash ot 6 wrote the main view is the first reason, only we should be concerned for this view since karet is involved; but for gid one should forbid from the outset of law, not like Beit Yosef who wrote only as stringency — see there. And so Shulchan Gavoah ot 6; and so one should rule and be stringent in chelev prohibition, and all the more gid hanasheh. Chochmat Adam kelal 28 ot 2, Binyan Avraham in Shaar 87 ot 4; and see below siman 65 seif 7.
