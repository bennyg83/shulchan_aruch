On the hindquarters — already explained siman 54 that they practice in every animal to leave one rib on each side in the hindquarter portion; and even if extra ribs are found, nevertheless they leave no more than one next to the hindquarters. (Because there are views to be stringent not to eat hindquarter meat, for even a great scholar cannot explain this well from a book.
