One who slaughters an animal and finds in it a fetus of eight or nine months, whether alive or dead — its chelev and gid are permitted. And that which is stated regarding a nine-month fetus that it is permitted applies specifically when it did not spread out upon the ground; but if it spread out upon the ground — forbidden. And some say that if its months were completed and it was found alive, even though it did not spread out upon the ground — its chelev is forbidden, and one is liable for karet upon it, and one removes from it all the threads and membranes forbidden on other animals. {Rama: And chelev of a nevelah is forbidden if the animal miscarried:}
