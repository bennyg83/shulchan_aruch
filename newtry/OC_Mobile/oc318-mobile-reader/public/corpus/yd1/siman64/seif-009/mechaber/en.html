Chelev adhering to the kereis beneath the covering is forbidden: {Rama: And such is the custom everywhere except the children of Rhineland, who customarily permit part of it and they are not stopped, for an elder already instructed them (Hagahot Ashiri, Mordechai, and majority of poskim); and wherever they practice prohibition, its law is like other chelev — to nullify it in sixty (Or Zarua, Long Tur); but one does not forbid the vessels of the children of Rhineland, since they practice permissibility (Chiddushei Agudah):}
