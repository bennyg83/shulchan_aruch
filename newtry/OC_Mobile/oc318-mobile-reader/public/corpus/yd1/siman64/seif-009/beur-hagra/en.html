Chelev that adheres, etc. — for so he explains: what is the heart that covers the innards — tochav, etc., meaning that all three chelevs there. Tochav is what covers, namely the layer spread before it; neqlaf is the heart adhering under the membrane — even wherever there are these three chelevs. And this is what is meant: "the chelev that covers" — that is the layer; "and all chelev that is on the innards" — what adheres to the rumen; and from "all" we derive chelev of the abomasum and intestines. See Mordekhai, chapter 8, where he elaborates proofs there.
(Collection) Chelev that adheres, etc. — for he explains tochav, etc.: tochav means what covers, namely the layer; qerum means what is under the cover and is on the neqlaf chelev; and neqlaf is the heart adhering to the rumen under the membrane — even wherever there is tochav, and there is also qerum and also neqlaf, it is forbidden — not like Rashi, who explains that it is all one thing. And proof that it is forbidden: it was excluded in Torat Kohanim from "chelev of the flanks," and it did not state "chelev of the innards," which would be permitted — all the more so chelev of the flanks; and this is what is meant: "and all the chelev that is on the innards," and from "all" we derive chelev of the abomasum and intestines, and he elaborated there. See Mordekhai siman 484 — dispute of R. Yehiel HaLevi and R. Efraim; and the view of R. Yehiel is as Rashi explains, that it all refers to what covers; but what adheres is under the membrane, and the membrane is covered by flesh — as Rashi stated 93a s.v. "chelev that the flesh," etc. And proof from what is stated below in the Gemara: chelev on the melting-pot, and in Torat Kohanim it is chelev on the innards; and see Rashi there s.v. "the melting-pot," etc., and at the beginning of Me'i Ta'am 1 s.v. "thickness," etc. — Rashi calls it shuman; see there. And Mordekhai wrote siman 483, and the anonymous view that the Rambam follows R. Efraim and Avodah Zarah (end of his words).
Who are accustomed, etc. — that they explain according to Rashi's explanation: tochav, qerum, and neqlaf all refer to what covers; and see there Me'i Ta'am 2.
