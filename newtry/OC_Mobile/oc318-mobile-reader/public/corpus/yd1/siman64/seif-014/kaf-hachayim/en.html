(14) [Seif 5] The alyah is permitted provided one removes, etc. — for the Torah forbade only plain chelev, and this is called chelev of the alyah; but what adheres on the face side of the alyah is full chelev and not in chelev of the alyah, for chelev of the alyah means the alyah itself; and this chelev is the language of the best part. Taz s.k. 6.
