Forbidden by law — it is tosevet, membrane, and peeled for everyone, full chelev:
From custom — where they practiced to forbid like R. Yehiel in membrane and peeled, they practiced to forbid; where they did not practice, permitted, for the essence is like R. Akiva that membrane and peeled without tosevet is not called chelev:
That by law it is forbidden — we follow Babylonians, and they follow R. Yishmael who holds membrane and peeled is called chelev; and see Peleti that I proved specifically we say to forbid, but not in other chelevim that are membrane and peeled:
