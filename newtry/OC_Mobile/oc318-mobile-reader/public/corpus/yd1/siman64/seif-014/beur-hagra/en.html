(Collection) And that on the yeter, etc. — for the Rif, Rambam, Ran, and Rashba ruled that it is entirely permitted, since Rebbe and R. Yehiel permit by ancestral tradition, and priests too practiced leniency, and many Sages ate it; and even though in Babylonia they practiced prohibition, where it was practiced it was practiced, where it was not practiced it is permitted. And the Maggid Mishna wrote: and we in these lands practice like the Babylonians to forbid; and so too Maharil Chaviv; and we have already practiced prohibition in it, etc. (end of his words).
And some say, etc. — that we follow the Babylonians.
