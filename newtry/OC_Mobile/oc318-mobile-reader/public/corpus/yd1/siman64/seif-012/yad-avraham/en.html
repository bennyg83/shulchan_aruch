(There seif 12, gloss) Extra liver lobe — some require removing upper membrane on liver side because of chelev of abdomen on it; some stringent on fat beneath — view of R. Eliezer Maimon and Rabbeinu Tam, brought by Tosafot, Rosh, Mordechai, chapter Gid HaNasheh; they brought proof from Torat Kohanim: perhaps flank chelev forbidden — "it shall be offered" — only fit for offering is forbidden; if so, fat of extra lobe offered with lobe is forbidden. R. Yehiel challenged: if because offered, alyah chelev should be forbidden; if because "chelev of the alyah" is called plain chelev — nevertheless forbidden because offered, etc. Question unfamiliar — we conclude Chullin 117 and Keritot 4 like Rav Zavid: Torah forbade only what is equal among ox, sheep, and goat, offered like kidney chelev; alyah is offered only in sheep, not ox and goat; so Rif, Pesachim, Rashba, Tosafot HaRosh 79, Bahag. If so, Rabbenu Meir and Rabbeinu Tam are correct on extra-lobe shuman offered — their words clear.
