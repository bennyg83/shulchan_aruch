The kidney has two membranes upon it — one is liable for the upper; the lower and the threads in it are forbidden but one is not liable for them; and the whiteness in the kidney — chelev on the kidneys is forbidden, not that inside the kidneys; nevertheless one removes the whiteness inside the kidney and need not dig after it, but some are stringent to dig after it: {Rama: And if he did not dig after it and left some inside the kidney — for everyone it is permitted if cooked thus (Beit Yosef in name of Hagahot Ashiri who so wrote in name of Or Zarua); and specifically what is inside the kidney, but what is on the kidney — for everyone forbidden (Darkei Moshe in name of Rosh, chapter Gid HaNasheh, and so in Gemara); the extra lobe of the liver — some require removing the upper membrane on the liver side because of chelev of the abdomen placed upon it; and some are even more stringent to remove the fat beneath that membrane (Rabbenu Meir and Rabbeinu Tam); but the face side of the lung side requires no nikkur at all (Tosafot HaRosh and Or Zarua). But the custom is to remove and take off two membranes lest one come to err (Sefer HaTerumah and Or Zarua):}
