(11) [Seif 4] Which is chelev — anything that is tosevet. It is his word that we translate: it is tosevet, when it is a word spread on the innards. Rashi Chullin 59b. Shach s.k. 7. Shaarei De'ah ot 7 — meaning spread and sitting on the innards, namely membrane separates between it and flesh; as Tur wrote, and so Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh 37a explained. And included in the innards are kereis, diaphragm, and beit hakosot. Peri Chadash ot 9; and see below seif 8.
