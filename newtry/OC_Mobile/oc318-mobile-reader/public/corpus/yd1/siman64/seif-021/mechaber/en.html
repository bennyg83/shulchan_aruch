A butcher whose way is to pick meat — if a thread or membrane is found after him, they teach and warn him not to treat prohibitions lightly; but if chelev is found after him — if it is the size of a barley grain, they remove him; and if a kezayit is found after him, even in many places, they would flog him with makat mardut and remove him: {Rama: And his return to fitness and his responsum — all according to what the judge sees, whether he transgressed unintentionally or intentionally (Hagahot Ashiri, chapter Gid HaNasheh); and it is a kosher practice that a butcher who sells meat pick it before selling lest buyers stumble over it (Maharik, root 40):}
