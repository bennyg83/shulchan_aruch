(21) There, gloss. Requires demonstration from an expert in nikkur, etc. — meaning after he is expert in nikkur laws, he goes to the rabbi expert in nikkur to see with his eyes how the order and cutting of meat where needed, and to remove giddim and chelevim and place of gid hanasheh; and he takes a demonstration certificate from the rabbi expert in nikkur who also becomes expert; and afterward they give him to pick. And such is custom here in the holy community of Jerusalem: they give to a picker only one who has a written certificate from the rabbi that he is skilled and expert.
