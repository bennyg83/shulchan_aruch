(10) [Seif 3] He extended his hand into the animal's womb and plucked chelev and removed it, etc. — before slaughter; but if he did not remove it, it is permitted through slaughter of the animal. Beit Yosef. Taz s.k. 4. Shach s.k. 6. Peri Chadash ot 8, Lechem Panim seif 3, Shaarei De'ah ot 6, and Kereti ot 7 wrote this depends on the dispute in the previous seif: per the first view slaughter permits; per Rambam it does not. But Beit Yosef wrote even per Rambam's second view in the previous seif, here it is permitted; and so Shach there. And so Peri Chadash there: for everyone permitted. And so Zivchei Tzedek ot 3.
