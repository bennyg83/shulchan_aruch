Strands at the tip (meaning: it is called in Scripture language eitzah, and it is the end of the spine — Arukh; and Rashi explained they call it naka) are forbidden — and they are five: three on the right and two on the left; the three on the right — each splits into two by two; and the two on the left — each splits into three by three; and its one head is connected to the spine. And the heads of the splits adhere beneath the chest at the heads of the ribs, and those heads of the splits are found on the front half of the animal; and if one soaks them in hot water they peel off, and if not — one must dig after both of them: {Rama: And if they are soaked in water it is as if hot and they peel off (Beit Yosef in name of Semak, Kolbo, and Or Zarua HaArukh); and some are lenient with these strands where they are swallowed in the flesh (Beit Yosef in name of Ran — but better to be stringent):}
