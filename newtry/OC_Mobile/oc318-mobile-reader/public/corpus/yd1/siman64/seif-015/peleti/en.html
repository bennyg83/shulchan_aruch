That it is the end of the intestines — and do not challenge that which we say strands of karkasha, meaning in the last chalchalta, does not seal because it is not tight — behold in any case it is impure chelev and does not seal; and one may say chalchalta is long and strands are fixed along the length in each chalchalta; and head of intestine that is forbidden is forbidden only at the end of one amah — if so, the remainder is pure chelev, and it says nevertheless it does not seal because it is not tight — examine; and in vain did later generations of our time struggle in their responsa on this:
But not the shuman adhering, etc. — so too Maharshal and Bach wrote, that thus he infers the Gemara's language "requires scraping" — meaning mere scraping until the membrane; and so Lechem Chamudos wrote; and he criticized the Levush whose language is confused on this; and so the second thread; and so the world practices — only to remove the membrane and leave chelev adhering to the taba'at; and thereby it would be pure chelev and seal the puncture — never was such an open mouth heard, for truly the one who contradicts these Gaonim' mouths is considered like a rebellious elder. And it happened in Prague that a nail was inserted at the head of the intestine, only chelev adhering in the intestine sealed it, and the matter came out kosher that it is permitted to eat and pure and seals; and the report came to the ears of the three Maharar David Oppenheim z"l, Av Beit Din of Prague, and he ruled to forbid, and they cried out on him in the beit midrash; and they said Rama erred in the source of the law, Heaven forbid that it be permitted to eat; and he composed responsa and sent, and part to Yaakov, and most did not heed him, as you will see in responsa Knesset Yechezkel and Shulchan Melech, Laws of Unintentional Sin, except the Rav Baal Shev Yaakov in his responsa held his words that l'chatchila one should forbid, for Rama erred in a printing error in Kol Bo and Rama did not notice the error — and in truth the evil was not only disputing these Gaonim, but also casting aspersion on our ancestors who practiced thus from Rama's time until today eating full chelev and a karet transgression — ancestors eating chelev flesh, Heaven forbid — is this the measure of all our deceased rabbis who toiled to establish ancestral custom without doubt, and this toil turned into bad reasoning saying ancestors sinned and always ate full chelev — would that he had been silent and it would have been wisdom to him. And to clarify briefly: the peg is Chullin 59b on chelev on intestines; Rashi and Rosh on head of intestine in amah — only above the membrane is forbidden because of connection to the layer on the innards; beneath the membrane the membrane interrupts; Baal Shev Yaakov's twists are refuted by Mordechai on spread of abdomen chelev; custom removes membrane only; Baal Shev Yaakov emended Kol Bo wrongly; Chut HaShani proof fails; beneath membrane permitted per Rashi, Mordechai, Rosh; sealing with nail if beyond membrane per Shach; Baal Shev Yaakov retracted per R.
