Only on hindquarters. Shach (and in Be'er Heitev s.k. 7); and see in Noda BiYehudah part 2 Yoreh De'ah siman 41 who wrote on meat between ribs 11 and 12 there is certainly chelev; here meat pickers pick hindquarters, not the butcher; and it is known there is chelev toward the back and toward the belly; and if no expert pickers in the city for hindquarters, leave only eleven ribs, etc., see there. [And see responsa Bnei Shibat Tzion siman 27 questioned why not so practiced; and wrote to explain Maharshal's intent; error in small communities making twelve ribs on the face side; Maharshal counts from the neck including two small ribs with chest — if so when eleven large ribs with marrow end, meat between rib 11 and 12 small rib has chelev; that is Maharshal's intent; if face side larger than eleven ribs, expert needed; where no expert, do not leave more than eleven ribs; leave all three small ribs without marrow for hindquarters — see there].
