It is good to sell it. See Be'er Heitev. See Chatam Sofer responsa siman 303, who concludes that selling it alive to a non-Jew is certainly forbidden, for the non-Jew will without doubt make it a neveilah and it will require burial. But if a Jew slaughters it with valid shechitah and sells it to a non-Jew without checking the lung, that is proper; see there.
