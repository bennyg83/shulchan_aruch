Therefore it is good to sell it, etc. Because perhaps it will later be found treifah. Maharshal in chapter HaMakshah siman 4 disagreed, because an animal stands in a presumption of prohibition as treifah, since we rule that if the lung was lost without inspection it is treifah; as long as it has not been checked, it is forbidden to sell it to a non-Jew. These are his words. But there is no proof from there, for it has already been explained above in siman 39 seif 2 that most poskim permit when the lung was lost without inspection. Even according to those who forbid, I already wrote there in s.k. 8 that their reason is that since Chazal required inspection, they were stringent to forbid even after the fact; otherwise, what did their enactment accomplish? This does not apply here. So too Mahari Weil responsa siman 72 wrote that it is permitted to sell it to a non-Jew, and so wrote Hagahot Mordechai at the end of chapter HaIshah Rabbah. However, in that responsum Mahari Weil concludes in the name of Mahari that it is forbidden to sell it to a non-Jew, and so wrote Maharam of Tikatin in the margin of the Mordechai in the name of later responsa of the Or Zarua; see there.
