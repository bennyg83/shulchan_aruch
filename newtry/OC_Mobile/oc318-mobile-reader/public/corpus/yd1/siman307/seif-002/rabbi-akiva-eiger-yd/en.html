(Siman 307, seif 2, gloss) Therefore it is preferable to sell it. Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 2, printed in Beit Aharon, concludes that it is forbidden to sell it to a non-Jew, lest the non-Jew resell it to a Jew who will shear it or work with it. Even selling it on condition that the non-Jew slaughter it in our presence is forbidden. See there.
