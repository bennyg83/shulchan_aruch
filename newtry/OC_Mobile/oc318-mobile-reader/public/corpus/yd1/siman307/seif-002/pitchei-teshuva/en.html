It is proper to sell it. See Baer Heitev and Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 303. He concludes that selling it alive to a non-Jew is certainly forbidden, because the non-Jew will without doubt render it a neveilah, which requires burial. But if a Jew slaughters it with a valid shechitah and then sells it to a non-Jew without examining the lungs, this is permitted. See there.
