The kohen must keep it and raise it forever until a blemish falls upon it; and he may sell it even to an Israelite even though it is unblemished, and the purchaser observes upon it the sanctity of bechor. This is when the purchaser buys it for his need; but it is forbidden to make merchandise of it—that is, to buy it in order to profit.
