It is forbidden to embroider verses on a tallit — for the reason above that it is forbidden to write even one verse; and further, because the tallit, even though it has tzitzit, is merely a utensil of mitzvah and not of inherent sanctity; therefore one may enter with a tzitzit tallit into the privy and the bathhouse and use it and cover nakedness with it — how can one bring sacred writings to a place of filth and contempt? So wrote Taz; and for this reason they permitted writing verses on the cloths of a Torah scroll, because they are utensils of sanctity and one may not treat them lightly — end of his words. And even though Rabbeinu Yerucham there wrote another reason for the prohibition, namely that it is forbidden to write verses from the Torah — it has already been explained that the custom spread to be lenient in this; and see Rabbeinu Yerucham there, who brought Rambam's responsum that expanded on this.
