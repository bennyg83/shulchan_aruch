It is forbidden to embroider verses upon a tallit. This brings verses of the Torah into disrespect. The tzitzit attached to a tallit are articles used for a mitzvah, not articles of sanctity. One may enter a privy or bathhouse while wearing a tallit with tzitzit, use it for ordinary purposes, and cover one's nakedness with it. How, then, could one bring verses of the Torah into a place of filth and disrespect? The Ateret Zahav writes that for this reason the authorities permitted verses to be written upon the coverings of a Torah scroll, since those coverings are articles of sanctity and may not be treated lightly. Although Rabbeinu Yerucham gives an additional reason to forbid this, namely that one may not write isolated verses from the Torah, it has already been explained that the custom became widespread to be lenient regarding that concern. See Rabbeinu Yerucham there, who cites a responsum of the Rambam that discusses this matter at length.
