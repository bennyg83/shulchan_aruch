It is permitted to bind them together, etc. This is so even though it is impossible to avoid placing the books of the Prophets and the Writings upon the Torah when they are bound together.
Their sanctity is not like the sanctity of, etc. Meaning, if one bound the Torah together with the Prophets and the Writings, the resulting volume does not possess the sanctity of a Torah scroll, because anything added beyond the required form is regarded like something missing from it.
