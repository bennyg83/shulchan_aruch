A scroll. The Rif and other authorities permit writing separate portions because it is a time to act for Hashem. Not everyone knows the text by heart, and not every congregation possesses complete chumashim in the synagogue; otherwise people may neglect the recitation of Shema and prayer. The Bach writes that this is the accepted practice in all lands. Since the Torah may not ordinarily be written as separate scrolls, one likewise may not write a single Torah passage for a child, even for study. A separate chumash is permitted, but not isolated scroll after isolated scroll. Shach. The Taz adds that the Bach's accepted leniency applies for study, because it is a time to act for Hashem. Those who write verses on a wall merely as a good omen do not act properly, for the Torah was given as a complete, sealed text. Reference 17.
