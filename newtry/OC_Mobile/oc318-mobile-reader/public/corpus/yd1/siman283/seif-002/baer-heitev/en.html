A scroll — the view of Rif and other poskim is that it is permitted because of "a time to act for the Lord, they have voided Your Torah," for not everyone knows by heart and not everyone in the world has chumashim in the synagogue, and they would come to nullification of Shema and prayer; and Bach wrote that such is the widespread custom in all lands — end. And the reason one does not write the Torah as a separate scroll is likewise one does not write from the Torah a scroll for a child even for learning, for specifically a chumash by itself is permitted, but not scroll by scroll — end of Shach. And Taz wrote that what Bach wrote that the custom is to permit is in order to learn from it because of "a time to act," etc.; but those who write verses on the wall for some good sign do not act properly, for the Torah was given sealed.
