One may not write a Torah portion as a separate scroll, etc. The view of the Rif, Mordechai, Agudah, and other poskim is that it is permitted because of "a time to act for Hashem — they have voided Your Torah," for not everyone knows it by heart and not everyone in the world has chumashim in the synagogue, and they would come to neglect Shema and prayer. The Bach wrote similarly, and he wrote that this is the widespread custom in all lands.
And one may not write, etc. Meaning, for the same reason that one may not write the Torah as separate scrolls, one may not write a Torah passage as a scroll for a child, even for instructional purposes. A complete chumash may be written separately, but not isolated passage after isolated passage.
