One may not write the Torah scroll by itself, etc. — the view of Rif, Mordechai, Agudah, and other poskim is that it is permitted because of "a time to act for the Lord, they have voided Your Torah," for not everyone knows by heart and not everyone in the world has chumashim in the synagogue, and they would come to nullification of Shema and prayer; and so Bach, and he wrote that such is the widespread custom in all lands.
And one may not write, etc. Meaning, for the same reason that one may not write the Torah as separate scrolls, one may not write a Torah passage as a scroll for a child, even for instructional purposes. A complete chumash may be written separately, but not isolated passage after isolated passage.
