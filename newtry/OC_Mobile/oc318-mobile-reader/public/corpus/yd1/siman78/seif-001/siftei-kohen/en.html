One who coats, etc. — meaning at the time of roasting; investigate. And this coating is not the pashtida that the Rav wrote, for pashtida is forbidden by Torah law as we see that they do not exude from the place where they sit dry in the oven; rather it is what they were accustomed to smear on dough whose batter is soft, and they were accustomed to do around gizzards and intestines that they place on the spit and coat around them with soft dough — so wrote Tosafot, Agudah, Mordechai chapter 25, Semag, and other poskim; if so, for us who are not expert in the matter, even coating with dough whose batter is soft has every law of cooking in a pot, both for leniency and for stringency, as the Taz wrote:
And pashtida, etc. — language of Tur HaChadash general rule 28 seif 2: and pashtida has the law of cooking, and we estimate with sixty, both for leniency and for stringency — that if there is sixty everything is permitted except that piece to which the prohibition adheres, and if there is not sixty everything is forbidden — end of his words:
