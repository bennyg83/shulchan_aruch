In the neighborhood of the dead. The reason is that the Angel of Death drops a drop of the blood of death into the water, and the measure of "neighborhood" is not clarified here. But in Ketubot (28) the Tosafot wrote that a neighborhood means three houses, as stated at the end of chapter 1 of Avodah Zarah. The Rakanat in parashat Vayechi wrote that at the moment of death one should be careful that no limb protrudes from the bed, and he was emphatic about one who is not careful in this.
