One must mourn. See Beit Shmuel in Even HaEzer siman 17, end of s.k. 94, who learned from here that after three days of goses one may testify about him to permit his wife to remarry. I have already written in Pitchei Teshuva there, in the name of Dagul MeRevavah and Me'orei Tzedek, that they objected to him. So too Mishkenot Yaakov responsa siman 69 wrote in the name of his teacher, the Gaon Rabbi Chaim of Volozhin z"l, and he explained there that those poskim who obligate mourning here for a goses after three days hold that one mourns based on a majority even where his wife remains forbidden to remarry; likewise, one mourns in the case of waters without end, unlike the Itur in the name of the Rif's responsum brought below in siman 375. See there, and see what I wrote further in Pitchei Teshuva there s.k. 3 on this matter.
