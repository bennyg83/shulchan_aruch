A grave. The Bach wrote that according to the law it is permitted whenever the sick person does not sense the matter. However, one must be careful on Friday close to Shabbat, lest they not manage to bury him except through desecrating Shabbat; and if they do not bury him, then they will need to leave it open. Therefore we permit them only if we estimate that even if he dies very close to Shabbat and it is impossible to bury him, they will at any rate have enough time to return and fill the excavation comfortably before Shabbat enters; end quote. See Orach Chayim siman 546 seif 11.
