A grave. Bach writes that by strict law it is permitted to prepare a grave so long as the sick person does not know about it. Nevertheless, on Erev Shabbos near Shabbos one must be careful, lest there be insufficient time to bury him without desecrating Shabbos. If he is not buried, the grave would have to remain open. Therefore this may be done only if they estimate that even if he dies immediately before Shabbos and burial becomes impossible, there will still be ample time to refill the grave before Shabbos begins. See Orach Chayyim 546:11.
