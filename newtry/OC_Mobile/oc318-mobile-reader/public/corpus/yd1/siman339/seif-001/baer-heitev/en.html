His affairs. The Ran explains that this means enabling him to give a get or a gift. A kohen may enter the house where he lies, although most moribund persons die. Below, siman 370, it will be explained that a kohen may not enter a house containing a moribund person. This is the Shach. The reason for all actions listed here as forbidden regarding a moribund person is that one may not hasten his death, and all these actions hasten death. This is the Taz. See siman 40 and siman 370.

A grave. The Bach wrote that according to the law it is permitted whenever the sick person does not sense the matter. However, one must be careful on Friday close to Shabbat, lest they not manage to bury him except through desecrating Shabbat; and if they do not bury him, then they will need to leave it open. Therefore we permit them only if we estimate that even if he dies very close to Shabbat and it is impossible to bury him, they will at any rate have enough time to return and fill the excavation comfortably before Shabbat enters; end quote.
