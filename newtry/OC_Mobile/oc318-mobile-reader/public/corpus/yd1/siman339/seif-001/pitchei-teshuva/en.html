My strength. See Baer Heitev and Beit Yaakov 59, as well as Shevut Yaakov, part 1, responsum 13, concerning desecrating Shabbat to preserve even brief life. Shevut Yaakov, part 3, responsum 75, addresses a dying patient whose physicians say he will certainly die within a day or two, but one treatment may cure him entirely or may cause death within an hour or two. Although even momentary life is ordinarily protected, where there is a possibility of complete recovery we do not refrain because of brief life. He proves this from Avodah Zarah 27b and Tosafot. The decision must nevertheless be made with great deliberation, consulting expert physicians and following the majority together with the local Torah authority.
