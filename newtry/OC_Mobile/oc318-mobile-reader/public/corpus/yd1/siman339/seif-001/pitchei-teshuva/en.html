[And see in responsa Shevut Yaakov volume 3 siman 75, concerning a sick person who is in danger of dying, and all the physicians despair of him that he will certainly die after a day or two days, but they say there is still one remedy through which it is possible that he will be healed, and it is also possible the opposite: if it does not succeed he will die immediately within an hour or two; whether we are concerned for momentary life, and sitting and not doing is preferable. And he replied: even though we certainly are concerned for momentary life, even one who is already actually a goses, and for this reason they do not bind his jaws, etc.; and in such a case we hold that it is permitted to desecrate Shabbat because of momentary life, as I elaborated in Shevut Yaakov volume 1 siman 13, etc.; nevertheless in our case, where it is possible that through this remedy he will be healed completely, we certainly are not concerned for momentary life; and a clear proof from the Talmud, chapter Ein Ma'amidin (Avodah Zarah 27b), and in Tosafos there s.v.
