(Likkut) One who is dying. From here through the words "and one does not begin with him" is all from Torat HaAdam in the name of Masechet Semachot, copied in its wording. It is in the first chapter there, although much is missing from our present text.
And they do not begin over him etc. What is written in chapter 8: they do not interrupt Torah study until his soul departs, etc. But the girsa of the Ramban there is: they do not begin tzidduk hadin over the dead until the soul of the dead departs; for all the days that R' Shimon son of R' Akiva was ill, he did not interrupt etc. (end of his words): (Anthology) Seif 1: see the Rosh, chapter 3 of Moed Katan, siman 122. Seif 2: who etc. See there siman 144 (end of his words):
And some say that one does not. This follows the earlier statement that one does not bring certain things in with him. Beit Yosef rejects the proof, because there the reason is specifically not to break the dying person's spirit.
