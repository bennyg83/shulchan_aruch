(Likkut) The dying person, etc. Up to "and we do not begin before him" — all of this is in Torat HaAdam in the name of what is taught [all verbatim]. And it is in chapter 1 there; but much is missing there from the text before us.
And we do not begin before him, etc. As taught in chapter 8: "We do not interrupt Torah study until his soul departs, etc." But the Ramban's text there reads: "We do not begin Tzidduk HaDin over the dying person until his soul has departed — for all the days that Rabbi Shimon the son of Rabbi Akiva was ill, he did not interrupt, etc." (end of note).
And some say that there is no, etc. From the above — "and they do not bring in with him, etc." — but in the Beit Yosef he rejected those words, since there it refers specifically to being with him, lest his heart be broken.
