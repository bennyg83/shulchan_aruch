A woman who gave birth while mourning and needs to bathe — permitted; and likewise one who has dizziness in his head — permitted to wash his head in hot water, for it is no worse than an asthenic person (meaning: one whose body is cold and chilled) who is permitted to bathe his entire body; and not everyone who says he is asthenic do we permit, only one known to be asthenic who conducts himself with delicacy; and if he does not bathe he will suffer greatly and come to illness.
