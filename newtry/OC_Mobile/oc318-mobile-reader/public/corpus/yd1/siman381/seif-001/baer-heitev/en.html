To prohibit bathing for all thirty days. This is the correct reading, as in the Levush, rather than “all bathing for thirty days.” Bathing in cold water is certainly not prohibited. Shach. Or Zarua explains the custom as a concern that one may come to comb his hair, which is forbidden for thirty days. According to our practice, which permits combing as stated in siman 390, bathing should therefore be permitted. Maharshal explains that bathing was prohibited because it commonly leads to haircutting. Taz. See siman 390.
