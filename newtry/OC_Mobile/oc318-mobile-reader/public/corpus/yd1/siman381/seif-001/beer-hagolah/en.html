A baraita, Moed Katan 15b. It derives the rule from, 'Act as a mourner, wear mourning garments, and do not anoint yourself with oil.' Washing is included in anointing. Rashi explains from the verse, 'It came like water within him and like oil into his bones.' The Rambam in chapter 5 places washing before anointing, based on, 'You shall wash and anoint yourself.'
The conclusion of the Gemara, Taanit 13b. [25]
