And needless to say, etc. For even on Yom Kippur, where anointing not for pleasure is forbidden, as written in the Jerusalem Talmud (last chapter of Yoma and other sources), even so it is permitted in such a case, as written in the Gemara in Yoma there.
(Supplement) And needless to say, etc. Jerusalem Talmud (chapter 2 of Berakhot and chapter 3 of Mo'ed Katan): "this statement applies to bathing for pleasure, but bathing not for pleasure — if sores broke out on him, it is permitted." (End.)
