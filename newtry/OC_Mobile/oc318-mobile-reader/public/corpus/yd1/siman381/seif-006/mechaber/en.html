A woman may not apply kohl or rouge on her mourning days, for all these are forbidden like bathing; and a married woman is forbidden only within seven, but after seven permitted in all that she not become repulsive to her husband; a bride who suffered mourning within thirty days of her wedding may adorn herself even within seven; a mature woman who suffered mourning, since she is about to marry, permitted in kohl and rouge; but forbidden in hot bathing of her entire body; a young girl mourning is forbidden even in kohl and rouge.
