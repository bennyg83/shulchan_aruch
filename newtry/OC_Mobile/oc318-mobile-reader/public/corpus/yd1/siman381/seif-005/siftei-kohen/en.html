All the more so she does not wash, etc. Meaning: since immersion, regarding which some say immersion at its proper time is a mitzvah, is forbidden, all the more so washing in order to wear white garments is forbidden. In Masat Binyamin responsa siman 5 he disagreed with the Rav: specifically immersion is forbidden, because we rule that it is not needed now; but washing for the white garments involves a mitzvah need. Therefore, both on Tisha B'Av and during her seven days of mourning, she should wear and spread as usual like the rest of the year. The washing should be altered somewhat, as the Or Zarua wrote, meaning that she should wash only that place and between her thighs, whether with hot or cold water. These are his words.
