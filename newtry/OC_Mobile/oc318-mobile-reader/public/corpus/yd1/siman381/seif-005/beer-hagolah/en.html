The anonymous ruling in the baraita there permits immersion, while another baraita at the end of Yoma records a tannaitic dispute. Tosafot, Beitzah 18b, writes that nowadays all immersions performed by women are not at their original prescribed time, since they count seven clean days because of doubt. In earlier times, when people handled foods requiring ritual purity, immediate immersion was needed so they would not render those foods impure. Today immersion serves only to permit a woman to her husband. Since even if she immersed on Yom Kippur or Tisha B'Av she would remain prohibited to her husband, one does not immerse on Yom Kippur or Tisha B'Av.
