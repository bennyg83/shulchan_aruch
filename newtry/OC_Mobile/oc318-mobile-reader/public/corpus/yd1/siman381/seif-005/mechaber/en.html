A niddah whose immersion time occurred on her mourning days — she does not immerse: {Rama: (Tur in the name of Rabbenu Tam and according to Mahariq root 35);} and all the more she does not bathe to wear white garments; but immediately after seven, even though bathing is customarily forbidden all thirty, nevertheless she is permitted, only she should change the bathing somewhat (Avraham Zacuto end of Laws of Mourning); and she should wear a white shirt and spread a white sheet so she not come to doubt (responsum of Maharil siman 15).}
