Two donkeys that had not previously given birth and bore two males—he gives two lambs to the kohen. Male and female—the male is for the kohen. Two males and a female—one for the kohen, and on the second he separates one lamb and it is for himself. Two males and two females, or two females and a male—there is nothing for the kohen. (And some say he must separate and they are for himself (Tur).)
