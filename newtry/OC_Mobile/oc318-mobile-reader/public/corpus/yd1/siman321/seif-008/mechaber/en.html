A firstborn donkey is forbidden in benefit until it is redeemed; and if he sold it before redemption, its money is forbidden. {Rama: And specifically if he sold it without specification; but if he informed the buyer that it is a firstborn donkey and he sells him what it is worth beyond the sheep with which he redeems it, the money is permitted—even l'chatchila it is permitted to sell it in this manner (as implied in Tur).} And if it died before redemption, or they broke its neck—it is buried, because it is forbidden in benefit; even after breaking its neck, since it was not redeemed. Therefore if he did not redeem it and gave the firstborn donkey itself to the kohen, it is forbidden for the kohen to use it until he redeems it with a sheep and takes the sheep for himself, or breaks its neck and buries it. And kohanim are suspected regarding this matter; therefore it is forbidden for an Israelite to give a firstborn donkey to a kohen unless he redeems the donkey in his presence or breaks its neck in his presence.
