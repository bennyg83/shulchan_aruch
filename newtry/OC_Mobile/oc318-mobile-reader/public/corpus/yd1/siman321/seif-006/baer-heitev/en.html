He recites a blessing. The Taz explains why no blessing is recited when giving an animal firstborn to a kohen, although one blesses over redeeming a firstborn donkey and a firstborn son. A Jew never had a proprietary right in the animal firstborn, for the Torah granted it to the kohen immediately upon birth. It is as though it were born in the kohen's own herd, and the Jew performs no transfer requiring a blessing. In redemption of a firstborn donkey or son, he gives his own money and therefore properly blesses over the mitzvah.
