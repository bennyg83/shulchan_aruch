Immediately when he separated the lamb for a firstborn donkey, the firstborn donkey returned to non-sacred status—even before he gave it to the kohen, and it is permitted to benefit from it. And if it died, it is not buried, and the kohen acquires the sheep immediately; and it is on the owner's liability—if it died in the owners' hands before they gave it to the kohen, they are exempt. Therefore immediately when he separates it, he blesses "Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us concerning redemption of a firstborn donkey."
