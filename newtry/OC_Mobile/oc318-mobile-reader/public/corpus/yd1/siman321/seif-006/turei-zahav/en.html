He recites: Who sanctified us... Why is no blessing recited when giving a firstborn kosher animal to a kohen, as one blesses over redeeming a firstborn donkey or firstborn son? It is not enough to answer that in redemption the owner retains the sacred item and gives another object in its place. Rather, a blessing is appropriate when a person performs a mitzvah with his own property. A firstborn animal never belonged to the Israelite; from birth it is the treasury of Heaven and is effectively born for the kohen. The owner therefore does not transfer his own property and recites no blessing. In redemption of a donkey or son he gives his own money, so he blesses. Tur adds that one need not delay the blessing until the redemption is actually handed to the kohen. [Citation 50.]
