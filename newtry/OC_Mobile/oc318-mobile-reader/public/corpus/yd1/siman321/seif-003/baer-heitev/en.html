A doubt. If the firstborn donkey dies after the attempted redemption but before the owner gives the redemption item to the kohen, he need not give it to the kohen, for he may argue that perhaps the redemption never took effect. If it dies after he gave the item to the kohen, the item remains with the kohen because the kohen is in possession of it and the owner himself gave it to him. This does not resemble a kohen who forcibly seized a doubtful firstborn, from whom it is removed as stated in siman 315. Nor can one object from the Rama there, who rules that a kohen must return an item given by mistake. Here, at the time of redemption the owner certainly owed the kohen a valid redemption item, but mistakenly believed this item was valid, so the kohen may retain it. In a doubtful firstborn, by contrast, there is uncertainty whether any obligation existed. Taz. See siman 315.
