And from when. This follows Rava's answer there, and we rule like the Sages. Rambam, however, writes that from birth until thirty days one should redeem it, while after thirty days he may either break its neck or redeem it, and there is then only a mitzvah to avoid delaying. It appears Rambam did not have the word "rather" in his text. He read Rava's answer as preserving Rav Sheshet's position: the earlier baraita saying one violates after thirty days follows R. Eliezer, while the baraita saying "either redeem it or break its neck" without a transgression follows the Sages. The earlier distinction remains intact.
