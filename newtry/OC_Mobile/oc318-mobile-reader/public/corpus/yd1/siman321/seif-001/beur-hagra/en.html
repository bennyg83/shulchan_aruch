At all times. Bechorot 11a, from the incidents involving Rabbi Yehudah Nesi'ah and Rav Nachman son of Rav Yosef.
From when, and so forth. According to Rava's resolution there and the Sages. The Rambam writes that from birth until thirty days one should redeem it, and afterward may either break its neck or redeem it, the delay being only a neglected mitzvah. He apparently had a different text and maintained Rav Sheshet's interpretation, assigning the baraitot to Rabbi Eliezer and the Sages respectively.
But it is a mitzvah, and so forth. As Yevamot 47b teaches, one should not delay performance of a mitzvah. The Mechilta derives this from, 'You shall safeguard the mitzvot.'
Afterward, and so forth. Bechorot 9b and 10b, excluding the possibility that breaking the neck fully removes the prohibition.
The Kohen, and so forth. Bechorot 11b, Tosafot Menachot 67a, and Bava Metzia 7a. See Rashi there.
