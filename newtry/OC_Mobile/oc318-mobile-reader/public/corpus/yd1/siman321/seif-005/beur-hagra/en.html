And then he can, etc. 9b: and did the Torah insist, etc.
It did not say, etc. Gemara there 11a.
Worth a dinar. Not specifically; even worth a danka, as stated there.
In what case, etc. The Rambam's version there is: this is not difficult; here with a sheep, here with its value. Meaning, what it says “even petruza,” etc., is with a sheep, and what it says “regia” is with its value, that one may not redeem with less than that even if the donkey is worth any amount. So it also implies there, where he said, “how much do I need to give,” implying that he redeemed it according to its value. But according to our version, and the Rosh and Tur, it is the reverse: more than three dinarim is redeemed with a sheep worth three dinarim or with its value, but less than that, even if he redeems with a sheep, he needs only its value, as stated there: the Torah did not say, etc., to be stringent, etc. All this is lechatchilah, but after the fact a sheep of any value works in every case.
