If he has no sheep to redeem it, he redeems it for its value (and then he may redeem with any item (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rambam)) and gives its value to the kohen. Torah did not speak to be stringent upon him but to be lenient—for if he had a firstborn donkey worth ten selaim, he may redeem it with a sheep worth a dinar. In what case is this said? When the value of a firstborn donkey was three zuz and upward; but if its value was less than three zuz, they do not redeem it except with a sheep or three zuz. A generous eye does not reduce below a sela; a stingy eye—half a sela; average—three zuz. {Rama: And they redeem with one sheep many times—such as if he bought it back from the kohen, or in a place where he has doubt of a firstborn donkey whose law is that he separates a lamb for it and it is his (Tur).}
