Wooden vessels and leather vessels, and earthenware vessels — even pitched, and even used for long-term storage — are permitted through hag'alah in a kli rishon. {Rama: And there are those who permit even through iruy — that one pours boiling water into them and rolls them (Tur in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel, and a responsum of the Rosh) — and one may rely on this after the fact (Beit Yosef). And even l'chatchila the practice is to place small glowing stones (or large ones) inside the barrel, and place them upon a funnel and pour boiling water upon them, and it descends into the barrel, and he rolls it, and we consider this as a kli rishon (Arukh, rule 58).}
