That they put in. Meaning that from the start they designated it for this; but not by chance that wine was in it three days. Taz. And Shach wrote: if it is known that wine stood in them three days, pouring does not help for him — this is like kavush as cooked; and in Levush it implies that even if it is known that wine stood in it three days one after another, rinsing suffices with pouring.
