Every place that requires iruy requires that one fill it with water up to all its edges and leave them in it 24 hours, me’et l’et; and at the end of 24 hours he pours them out, and the water is permitted (and some forbid the water) (Mordechai, perek Ein Ma’amidin; and Arukh; and Agudah in the name of Ravyah). And he puts in second water and leaves them in it 24 hours, and so too he does a third time; and even if these 3 days were not consecutive, it is of no consequence. And if the water stood in it several days and he did not pour them out, it counts for him only as 1 day. And if he poured out the water before they stood in it 24 hours, those waters did not count for him. (Strong saltwater that has 1/3 salt and 2/3 water is effective me’et l’et (Tur in the name of the Ramah), like iruy of 3 days) (Tur in the name of Rosh).
