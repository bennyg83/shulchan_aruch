(There in Shulchan Aruch seif 12 in Hagah) That there is a third, etc. — Tur's language; and Ramah wrote it is reasonable regarding salt water where the salt is strong, similar to brine and morayis it is fit; and even according to his words one may not permit except with abundant salt, as explained regarding Shabbat "what is goat salt water — three parts salt," etc. — end quote; and it seems to me it is a printing error and it should read two-thirds salt; and so is in the Gemara Shabbat daf 108b "two-thirds salt and a third water"; so is in all the poskim and Tur, and the Mechaber himself in Orach Chaim siman 321 seif 2; and if so, the Rav's words who wrote a third salt and two-thirds water require study.
