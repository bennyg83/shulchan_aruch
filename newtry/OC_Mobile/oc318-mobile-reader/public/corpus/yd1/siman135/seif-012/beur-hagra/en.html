And the water, etc. See Tosafot there on 33b, s.v. shara, etc.
Some forbid the water. For otherwise one would be deliberately nullifying a forbidden substance. See Shach.
(Likut) And even though not, etc. And likewise in Hagah Acharon perek 2 s"k 22 s.v. "v'chein." "Rabbi says," etc. ; end.
And if they remained, etc. As stated there: ‘and one must empty them,’ etc.
And if he poured, etc. As stated there, a full twenty-four-hour period. The Sages said that all measures established by the Sages require the full measure, etc. See Menachot 103b and elsewhere.
Salt water, etc. Ramah wrote strong salt water, similar to brine and morayis; and Rosh in a responsum and Rashba disagree on this, and wrote specifically brine and morayis, but salt water and morayis we do not have; and Tur wrote that even according to Ramah it is specifically like that in perek 14 of Shabbos (108b, and see Nekudot HaKesef) that there is, etc.
In me'ol le'ol. Rosh there; and the measure that brine stood, etc., and it is reasonable, etc.; and likewise Tur in the name of Ramah; but Rashba in Tur Zahav wrote like the measure that they are heated in fire, for in the Gemara he compares them to burning by fire that they said "now," etc.; and see above siman 105 seif 1.
