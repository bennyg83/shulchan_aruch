Earthenware vessels: if the non-Jew used them, as long as they have not yet become “sated” to absorb, they require milui v’iruy even if they are not pitched. And from when are they “sated”? From the second time and onward. Therefore, if a non-Jew used them a first time and a second time, they require milui v’iruy (Tur). And if one put water into them 3 times and afterwards put wine into them and a non-Jew touched it, it is permitted with rinsing/shaking; but if he put water into them only 2 times and afterwards put wine into them and a non-Jew touched it, there are those who say that it requires iruy. And if he put water into them 1 time and they remained there a large hour, such as a quarter of the day, the absorption from water is like having put water into them 3 times. And if they are pitched, they require peeling the pitch or milui v’iruy, even regarding our vessels that a non-Jew touched while liquid was tofe’ach upon them. {Rama: And after the fact, their law is like stone and wood vessels, as was explained (so the Beit Yosef explains the language of the Tur).}
