Braita daf 33, and according to the girsa that Tosafot and Rosh wrote there in the name of Rabbeinu Tam and his explanation there; and chadashim — meaning that appear as if wine was never in them — nevertheless they are forbidden, lest a gentile put wine in them and the pitch absorbs it, even though one does not put it in for permanence, because pitch absorbs; and likewise Rashba.
