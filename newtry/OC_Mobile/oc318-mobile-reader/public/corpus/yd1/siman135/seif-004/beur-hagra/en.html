Earthenware vessels, etc. This is stated there on 33b, following Rashi's explanation. The Rashba and Tur explain that ‘one time,’ etc., refers to the non-Jew's drinking wine from them. Sefer HaTerumah, siman 166, however, explains that it refers to a Jew first placing water in them. If he placed water in them twice and then wine, and the non-Jew touched it, the wine is forbidden; but if he placed water in them three times and then wine, it is permitted. This is the meaning of ‘and if,’ etc. The Mechaber maintains that water and wine must be distinguished, and therefore states both rulings.
Requires filling and pouring. Rashi there, and as written 1 ; what helps when he came, etc. ; he said "in what case," etc.
And if he put, etc., and they remained, etc. Tosafot there s.v. "kasi," etc.; and accordingly the practice is, etc.; and he wrote in Shulchan Aruch that this means a quarter of the day.
And if they are pitched, etc. As written above seif 1; and the same for all vessels, etc.
And bedi'eved in them. Meaning in ours; and as written in Hagah "there are those who say," etc.
