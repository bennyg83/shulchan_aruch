Earthenware vessels, etc. Even if he does not put them in for permanent storage — and so is the conclusion of most poskim; and so it is in Atzei Zahav.
If he used them, etc. Specifically when there was no yayin nesech in it for 24 hours; but if there was for 24 hours — there is no distinction between sated and not sated; rather it always requires filling and pouring — as above siman 93 seif 6.
Even with ours that the gentile touched them, etc. Namely according to what Beit Yosef explained in Tur's words; but Maharshal challenged on him and explained Tur's words that even l'chatchilah the practice is to permit drinking in it — and it is not comparable to wooden vessels in seif 1; for earthenware, since they are already sated, they no longer absorb; and Derishah brings it; and so Bach wrote.
