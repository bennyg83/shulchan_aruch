And used. Perishah writes that this applies only if the non-Jew used them, but mere touch while they were moist does not forbid. Taz disagrees and rules that touch also forbids. Accordingly, one who sells wine but leaves lees in the empty barrel should not allow the non-Jew to remove them. The barrel still contains enough moisture to moisten another object, and some clear wine remains among the lees; the non-Jew's touch would forbid a barrel used for long-term wine storage, requiring hagalah or filling and pouring. The Jew should remove the lees himself. Nekudot HaKesef agrees with Perishah here, because an unpitched vessel was not subjected to so severe a decree merely through touch.
