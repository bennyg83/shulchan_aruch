Earthenware. Taz wrote: he said to him — behold in an earthenware vessel, even if one does not put it in for permanence it is forbidden, as written in Shulchan Aruch; Perishah resolved in Turei Zahav HaKatzar that he teaches that even in earthenware where there are two adverse factors, pouring helps.
