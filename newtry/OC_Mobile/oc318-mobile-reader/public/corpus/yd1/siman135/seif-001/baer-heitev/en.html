Ours. The Taz explains that there is a distinction between tarred and untarred vessels. "Moist" means moist enough to moisten something else, as Maharshal writes; otherwise it has no power to prohibit, as written in siman 123 seif 17 regarding grape pits. To be lenient. The Shach writes that it is permitted for drinking. This is according to the law of the Shas; alternatively, it speaks where there is not sixty, but if there is sixty it is obvious that in every case it is permitted for us, since we hold that ordinary wine of non-Jews is nullified in sixty. Presumably there is sixty against the peel of the vessel, which is prohibited only to the depth of a peel, as below siman 137. All this concerns wooden vessels and the like, excluding leather vessels, which are soft and whose tar absorbs the wine; according to all views, they require irui.
