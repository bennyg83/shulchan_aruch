and it further appears that in the whole siman and everywhere we say wine vessels are made fit through filling and pouring and the like — this is presumably; but if it is known that yayin nesech stood in it a full day, obviously complete hag'alah is required for each according to its law as if used hot, for we hold kavush is like cooked; and in earthenware hag'alah does not help and breaking is required; however still it requires study, for it is possible to say that even if it is known that me'ol le'ol stood in it — nevertheless since it was forbidden only through cold we say regarding it like cold into cold, and filling and pouring help for it; and nevertheless it appears that other prohibitions absorbed through cold through kavush — making-fit through filling and pouring does not help, and they did not say so except regarding wine, where water weakens the taste of wine, unlike other prohibitions; and what is written in Orach Chaim siman 451 s"k 21 regarding an earthenware barrel into which barley beer was put — it is permitted through hag'alah or pouring for 3 days — possibly they were lenient only when it is not ben yomo, for there is no longer a Torah prohibition;
