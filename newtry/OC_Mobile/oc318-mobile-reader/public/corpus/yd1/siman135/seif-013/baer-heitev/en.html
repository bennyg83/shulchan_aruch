A surface layer. Shach understands this where it is unknown whether wine remained in the vessel for a full day. If it is known that yayin nesech remained for a full day, immersion is like cooking and the entire vessel becomes forbidden. It would then require full hagalah according to its material, and earthenware would require breaking. He nevertheless leaves room to consider that because the absorption occurred cold, filling and pouring might still help for wine. For other prohibitions absorbed through prolonged cold soaking, filling and pouring does not help; this leniency was stated specifically for wine, whose taste water weakens. Filling with wine itself accomplishes nothing. If there is sixty against the vessel's surface layer and wine was placed in it a second time, the second wine is permitted after the first wine already absorbed and nullified the vessel's taste.
The pitch. When one performs filling and pouring, the pitch need not be removed. Tur. See siman 99:7.
