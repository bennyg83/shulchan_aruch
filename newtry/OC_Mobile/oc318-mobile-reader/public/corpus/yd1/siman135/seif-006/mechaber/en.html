Earthenware vessels that are coated with lead, or with a glass-glaze (meaning, a kind of glass vessel that was not fully cooked, and is from sand; Tosafos): if they are green, their law is like natron vessels, because there is in them earth from digging tsarif. But white or black: if they are smooth and have no cracks, their law is like metal vessels; and if they have cracks, their law is like other earthenware vessels that are not smeared smooth. {Rama: That iruy is effective for earthenware is when they were not used with yayin nesech except cold; but if they were used with it hot, earthenware has no remedy. And other vessels require hag'alah, like other prohibitions (Mordechai, perek 2 of Avodah Zarah, in the name of Ravyah).}
