Earthenware vessels, etc. This implies ordinary earthenware vessels of ours, and not merely the type of earth described by the Rosh, whose view is cited by the Tur. Accordingly, one must be careful with those earthenware jugs whose openings are narrow at the top and which are generally designated for wine. One may not drink permitted wine from them when they belong to a non-Jew, because the non-Jew first used them for wine, and rinsing does not suffice. With vessels that are also commonly filled with water, however, there is a doubt in a rabbinic matter, for perhaps their first use was with water, and they are permitted through rinsing. So it appears to me.
