(There, seif katan 15) For in truth, I do not know the reason for this stringency, etc. This presents no difficulty. The reason is that anything left soaking for twenty-four hours causes absorbed matter to be expelled and is comparable to pickling. The Rosh likewise wrote in the chapter Ein Ma'amidin that pickling is comparable to filling and emptying, because during twenty-four hours the water expels everything it is capable of expelling, but not in less than twenty-four hours. These are his words. It is obvious that intermittent soaking does not combine into a twenty-four-hour period. For this reason, we do not require three consecutive twenty-four-hour periods, but rather three separate soakings, one after another. Nevertheless, even apart from this, it may be that if the vessel was subsequently used with permitted food, we do not prohibit the food after the fact. This is not limited to this case, but applies to all wine vessels that were used after the fact without any kashering, because ordinarily there is sixty times the volume of the absorbed surface layer, and it is permitted according to our practice, as explained below in siman 137.
