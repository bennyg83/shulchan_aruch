(Likut) Wherever, etc. Although the Rosh there wrote that this applies specifically to earthenware, and Tosafot there likewise wrote, ‘specifically,’ etc., Tosafot nevertheless concluded there, ‘however,’ etc. (End.)
With water, etc., and some are lenient, etc. The Yerushalmi states in the name of Rabbi Yaakov bar Acha, in the name of Rabbi Yasa: Wine that is forbidden for drinking but permitted for benefit was placed in a jar; the jar itself has the status of the wine. If he emptied it and placed other wine in it, that wine is forbidden but the jar is permitted. Mordechai cites this in siman 1254. Ramban, however, wrote concerning this Yerushalmi: This is a novel ruling for which we find no basis in our Gemara; therefore one should not rule leniently on its basis. See Orach Chaim, siman 452, seif 5, in the gloss, and Ran at the end of perek Kol HaBasar.
