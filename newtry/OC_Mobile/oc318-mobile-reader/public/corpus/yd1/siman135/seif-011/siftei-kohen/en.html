B'di'eved. And Mordecai wrote that yayin nesech vessels that were rinsed with kosher wine are permitted l'chatchilah; and it appears he means: if he rinsed them, it is permitted l'chatchilah to drink in them and no further rinsing is needed; however l'chatchilah it is forbidden to rinse them in wine; and the Rav said that b'di'eved, if he rinsed them, we are lenient to drink in them.
