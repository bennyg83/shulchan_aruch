(There, in Shulchan Aruch, seif 12, in the gloss) Which contains one third, etc. The wording of the Tur is as follows: The Ramah wrote that it is reasonable that salt water whose salt is strong, comparable to fish brine and muries, is permitted. Even according to his view, one may permit it only when there is an abundance of salt, as explained regarding Shabbos: What is strong salt water? Two parts salt, etc. It appears to me that there is a printing error and that the text should read: two thirds salt. This is indeed the reading in the Gemara, Shabbos 108b: two thirds salt and one third water. So too it appears in all the authorities, in the Tur, and in the Mechaber himself in Orach Chaim, siman 321, seif 2. Accordingly, the words of the Rama, who wrote one third salt and two thirds water, require study.
