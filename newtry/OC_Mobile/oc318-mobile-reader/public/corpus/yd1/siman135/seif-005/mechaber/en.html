Natron vessels — which are made from dug-out earth of a tsarif (meaning, earth from which they dig “tsarif” in the vernacular; alum; and in Ashkenazic it is called “beysa,” Rashi) — absorb extremely, and they never become purified, even by aging 12 months, and even when one does not store [wine] in them long-term, and even if their first use was in the hand of a Jew.
