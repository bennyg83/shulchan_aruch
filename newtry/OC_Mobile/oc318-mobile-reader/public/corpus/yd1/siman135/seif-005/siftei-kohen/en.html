Vessels of neser, etc. Tosafos in Avodah Zarah, daf 33b, wrote that there are two kinds of neser. This kind is not the one mentioned in the first chapter of Shabbos, where it states that vessels of neser and earthenware vessels have the same law of impurity. There is also a third kind of neser from which vessels are not made, but which is used for laundering vessels. See there.
From a quarry of tzerif, which is called alum. See Taz and Prisha. Rivash, siman 450, and the Bartenura at the beginning of chapter 2 of Keilim likewise write that this is what is called alum. Rambam there, however, extensively refutes those who say so and explains that it is the substance called tafel in Arabic. See below, siman 159, seif 2.
