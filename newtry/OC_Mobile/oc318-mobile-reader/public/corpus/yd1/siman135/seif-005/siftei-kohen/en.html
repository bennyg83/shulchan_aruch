A vessel of natron, etc. Tosafot wrote [in Avodah Zarah page 33b]: there are two kinds of natron; and this is not so — for in the first chapter of Shabbat it says natron vessels and earthenware vessels have the same impurity status; and there is yet a third natron with which one does not make vessels, only uses to wash vessels — see there.
From a quarry of tzarif. This is called alum; see Taz and Perishah. So wrote Rivash siman 450, and Bartenura at the beginning of chapter 2 of Kelim, that it is called alum. But Rambam there at length rejected the words of those who say so and explained that it is what is called tafal in Arabic. See above siman 159 seif 2.
