For if not, etc. Yerushalmi there, brought by the Rash in his commentary to the Mishnah there: Yerushalmi, "one who smooths his pile," etc. This is what he wrote: "but," etc. There is a scribal error there, and it should read: Resh Lakish challenged R. Yochanan; so it is in Sefer HaTerumah and the other poskim. The Rash's version is "perhaps he will find customers," etc., meaning he will sell everything together, and so wrote the Rosh. But the Rambam's version is "perhaps he will not find," etc., and will make dough. See his commentary there, and so he wrote in his code; so too in Sefer HaTerumah. This is what is written here: "for if not," etc.
