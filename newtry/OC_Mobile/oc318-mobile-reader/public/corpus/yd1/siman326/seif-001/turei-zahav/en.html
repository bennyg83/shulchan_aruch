All this is where neither has the required measure. Then two doughs about which one is particular do not combine even through contact or adhesion. If each has the required measure, one may separate from one for the other only through contact or adhesion. Although elsewhere produce present before him may be separated from one for another without such contact, here the owner is particular that they remain distinct. Terumat HaDeshen 190 and the case of pure and impure dough prove that a connecting strand is required. Levush erred twice: he wrote that adhesion combines sub-measure doughs and that full-measure doughs require no adhesion. In truth, where one is particular, adhesion cannot create a single obligated dough from two sub-measure doughs, but it does allow separation from one full-measure dough for another.
