He separates. Taz explains that this means through actual contact and adhesion. Although in siman 325 he rules that when both doughs are before him one may separate from one for the other without adhesion or even basket combination, this case differs because the owner is particular about keeping them separate. Shach, however, rules that neither touching nor adhesion is required; if both doughs are before him, that is enough, as at the end of siman 325. Nekudot HaKesef also disputes Taz at length.
