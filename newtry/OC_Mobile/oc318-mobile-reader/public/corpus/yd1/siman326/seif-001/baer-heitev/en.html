He separates. The Taz explains that this requires contact through adhesion. Although in siman 325 he ruled that when both doughs are before him one may separate from one for the other without adhesion or even basket combination, here the owner is particular that they not mix. The Shach rules that no contact or adhesion is required; it is enough that both are before him, as at the end of siman 325. Nekudot HaKesef also challenges the Taz at length. See siman 325.
