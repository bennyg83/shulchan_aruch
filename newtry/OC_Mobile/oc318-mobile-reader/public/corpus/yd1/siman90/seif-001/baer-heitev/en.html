By itself. Explanation: Shach — meaning without meat; but it is plain that in water and vegetables and other things it is considered like cooking by itself — investigate.
With sixty. Shach wrote that we do not estimate what came out from it because we do not know how much came out; and see above siman 69 seif 11.
The forbidden. Taz wrote in name of Rashba: the reason the udder is forbidden when there is sixty is that we are concerned lest it did not expel all its milk in the first pot and a bit of milk still remains inside the udder; therefore it forbids again as at first. And Ran wrote it is forbidden only because of marit ayin, and therefore Taz inclines so, see there. (He also wrote there that for us, who hold in all prohibitions noten taam naaseh nevelah, so too even for a d'rabbanan prohibition, and his proof from Rama here.)
The udder. Shach wrote: what Bach wrote that udder is a complete entity like gid hanasheh is astonishing, for it is in the Gemara and poskim that it is not a complete entity except what was forbidden from the start of its formation; and so explained in Rambam that gid hanasheh is a complete entity and udder is not a complete entity, see there.
