To roast. Gemara 111a.
Or to salt it. For salting is like cooking — roasting as written in Pri Chadash (97b).
And some, etc. — Avodat HaGolah.
But b'dieved, etc. — even though in roasting we forbid even b'dieved with meat as Tosafot above that Rosh permits l'chatchila in salting — and even though Rashba forbids — nevertheless Rashba and his supporters permit even in roasting b'dieved according to all as above section 3.
Stomach skin, etc. — Tosafot there 97b s.v. and udder from; and one may say this, etc., unlike stomach as written 109b.
