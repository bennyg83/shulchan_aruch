To roast udder, etc. — meaning l'chatchila it is forbidden to roast or salt udder on the meat but under the meat; and b'dieved even on the meat it is permitted; and on our spits it is forbidden l'chatchila in every case — as explained in siman 73 seifim 4 and 5; and in Beit Yosef he brought dispute of poskim whether there is a difference between tearing in two directions and smearing on the wall or not, see there; and the Rav's view in the gloss for roasting was explained in section 2:
And some permit. Even l'chatchila to salt udder on meat even without tearing at all; and some say for liver, for salting does not expel except blood and not milk — Tur in name of Rosh:
And one may not salt it with meat. And so Maharshal wrote in Isur VeHeter there and in his book chapter 25 siman 57: l'chatchila forbidden to salt it with meat whether under the meat or on the meat; and regarding b'dieved it appears Maharshal's view in Orach Chaim that if they tore in two directions it is permitted even if they did not smear on the wall; and the Rav's view appears that Ramban, Rashba, and Shulchan Paz permit b'dieved even without tearing at all even on the meat; and Rosh, Tur, and Shulchan Paz permit even l'chatchila; and the forbiddance is a lone opinion against those many authorities — and so Maharshal himself in his book there: b'dieved if they salted even without any tearing at all even on the meat it is permitted — and it must be he retracted from what he wrote in Orach Chaim
