The meat. Explanation: that l'chatchila it is forbidden to roast or to salt udder on the meat but under the meat; and b'dieved even on the meat it is permitted; and on our spits it is forbidden l'chatchila in every case; and see above siman 73 seifim 4 and 5.
That permits. Even l'chatchila without tearing at all; and some say for liver, for salting does not expel except blood and not milk.
The matter. Explanation: even without tearing in two directions; and Taz wrote in name of Hagahot Mordechai: an incident with udder and meat that were salted together in a perforated vessel, and afterward they rinsed it in boiling water, and Maharam permitted both of them, end of his words; and a vessel that is not perforated — he mentioned it because of blood in the udder, but not because of milk; and see above siman 69 s.k.
To salt it. Orach Chaim wrote that even to cook it is permitted.
