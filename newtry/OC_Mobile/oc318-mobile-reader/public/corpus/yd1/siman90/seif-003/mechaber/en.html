It is permitted to cut hot udder with a knife used to cut meat (and all the more raw udder even though it is full of milk) (Tosafot Nedarim 51); and likewise permitted to cut meat with a knife used for udder; and the same law to eat this in a vessel in which that was eaten: {Rama: And the same to roast this on a spit on which that was roasted; and the same law permitted to place it in a bowl with roasted meat even if both are hot; for after roasting udder its law is like other meat for everything (Beit Yosef in name of Rashba and Arukh); and specifically if it was roasted properly — meaning they tore it first in two directions and smeared on the wall; but if one transgressed and roasted without tearing or with slight tearing — all these matters are forbidden l'chatchila (Tur and Beit Yosef) and b'dieved all is permitted; and if they tore properly and roasted and afterward found pockets full of milk — no concern (Arukh chapter 11); and likewise if they left it whole with its milk a full day before roasting — permitted (there); and we do not say kavush k'mevushal in such a case (there).}
