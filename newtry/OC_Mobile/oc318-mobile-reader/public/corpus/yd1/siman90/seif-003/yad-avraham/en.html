Baal Nefesh in Dagul Mervavah wrote on him: per his words fowl meat which is also d'rabbanan is also not forbidden in pickling and salting — if so the incident of the fledgling that fell into a fish brine that is not permitted except because of its salting in Chullin (daf 112) is difficult. And he concluded therefore Taz's words are astonishing here — requires study, end. And it was hidden from him what Taz wrote in siman 98 s.k. 5 that he distinguishes between fowl meat where they decreed lest domestic animal, and they strengthened their words like Torah law — and for other rabbinic prohibitions such as min b'mino there is no decree on a decree, see there.
