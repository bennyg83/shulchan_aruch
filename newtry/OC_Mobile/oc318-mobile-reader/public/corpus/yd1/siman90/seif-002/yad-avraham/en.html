(seif 3 in the gloss) And so if he left it whole thus before roasting it in its milk a full day it is permitted and we do not say pickling is like cooking in this case. The reason: chalav shechutah is d'rabbanan. And it appears clear this means it is not forbidden because of pickling in its milk for the reason stated; but nevertheless it has pickling law regarding blood — if it was not salted and pickled a full day it is like pickling without salting as explained above siman 69 seifim 15 and 61 in the gloss, see there. And now it works well that the gloss changed Orach Chaim's language where it says l'chatchila it is permitted l'chatchila to leave it thus a full day — and the gloss used b'dieved language — meaning it must deal with salted meat — if so it is forbidden l'chatchila to salt without tearing; but b'dieved permitted as written
