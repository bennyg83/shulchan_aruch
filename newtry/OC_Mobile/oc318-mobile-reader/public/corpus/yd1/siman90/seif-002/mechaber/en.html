The custom is not to cook it with meat at all, and to cook it without meat — in a pan or pastry requires tearing in two directions and smearing on the wall; and for roasting — tearing in two directions: {Rama: And if one transgressed and cooked it — if torn in two directions and smeared on the wall, there is room to permit b'dieved (Sefer HaTerumah and Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Tur and per Avodat HaTaharah) where there is great loss; but otherwise one should not permit; and that the custom is to tear it and cut it many times in two directions over its entire surface is preferable and more effective than smearing on the wall (Terumat HaDeshen siman 182); and for roasting the custom is l'chatchila to tear it in two directions and smear on the wall (Arukh general rule 18); however if one transgressed and roasted it even without tearing it is permitted if roasted alone without meat with it (Hagahot Maimoniyot and ShaDa and Tur and per Avodat HaTaharah); and if roasted with meat with it — if torn in two directions and smeared on the wall, both are permitted; and if not — the upper is permitted and the lower forbidden (Beit Yosef in name of Tosafot and Rosh and Mordechai and Arukh); and one should not forbid both lest the spit was turned over, for b'dieved we do not establish prohibition (Orach Chaim); but l'chatchila one should not roast it with meat at all; and for a pot without meat the custom is to forbid it l'chatchila (Rashi and poskim); and the same for frying even without meat (Beit Yosef); and even if the udder dried out (Orach Chaim); and if one transgressed and cooked it in a pot alone — b'dieved permitted if torn in two directions and smeared on the wall (Rashi and Ra'avad and ShaDa and Mordechai and per Avodat HaTaharah); however if it dried out — meaning after thirty days — if one transgressed and cooked it even with meat — b'dieved permitted (ShaDa and hagahah and R' Yechiel); and to make pastry from udder without meat the custom permits if they do not bake the pastry on a griddle; but on a griddle its law is like cooking in a pot (Maharai in hagahah and Arukh general rule 18); and some commentators are stringent in every pastry if the udder was not dried first. Some are stringent not to bake udder pastry with meat pastry in a small oven — only one should be placed at the mouth of the oven; and it is good to be careful l'chatchila even though it is only a general stringency (Beit Yosef and Arukh).}
