They practice, etc. — Rabbenu Tam's view in Tosafot is like Rambam's view, only he is more lenient that even for a pot without meat it equals roasting that is permitted l'chatchila through slight tearing; and so through slight tearing it is permitted b'dieved even with meat. But Rashi's view: the whole passage of gid hanasheh deals with cooking without meat and it is forbidden even b'dieved without tearing in two directions and smearing; and what Rav and the baraita said that b'dieved it is permitted in roasting deals — and Rav Nachman disagrees and holds that even l'chatchila tearing is not required in roasting — and the halachah is not like the baraita of Rav; nevertheless tearing in two directions is not required, as R' Eliezer said to his attendant; and Rashi there 111b s.v. he roasted and laws, etc.; and even though Tosafot 109b s.v. it is not write in name of Rashi that for roasting two or four — and so Rashi there s.v.
And if they transgressed, etc. — meaning with meat; and the first part refers; and as written in logical proof that Hagahot Sefer HaTerumah and Orach Chaim in name of Ra'avyah and Or Zarua forbid even b'dieved even through tearing in two directions and smearing; and some say to permit b'dieved because it is a lone opinion.
And what they practice, etc. — tearing in two directions of the Gemara is one direction for its length and one for its width — therefore smearing is required, unlike when all pockets open. There.
And for roasting they practice, etc. — as Rashba wrote per Rashi's view that the whole passage is in roasting; and what was written how one tears, R' Yehuda said, etc., also for roasting; and R' Eliezer disagrees and one should act like R' Yehuda — Avodat HaBayit and logical proof s.k. 2.
However if, etc. — like Rav's first version in the baraita and so Rashi 111a.
And if it was roasted with, etc. — 111a; and as Tosafot wrote there s.v. fatty that when they tore in two directions and smeared it deals; but otherwise even fatty meat is forbidden even b'dieved for the udder — and all the more on the meat, for the meat is forbidden; and this is what was written and if not, etc.
And one may not forbid, etc. But l'chatchila, etc. — as Rashi wrote there s.v. the halachah, etc.
And for a pot without, etc. — meaning even tearing in two directions and smearing, since it will end up being cooked in it with meat afterward — like with meat, for they had a dedicated pot for udder — unlike now when we are not accustomed to this and one will err. Semak and Hagahot Maimoniyot.
And the same for a pan. Also from Torat Chatat.
And even. Meaning even though when dry b'dieved is permitted according to all, nevertheless l'chatchila one will come to switch.
And if they transgressed, etc. — this is permitted according to all.
However if it dried out, etc. — since it is d'rabbanan in such a case they did not decree. There.
But on a griddle, etc. — also for the reason mentioned, unlike without a griddle.
But with a pan, etc. — also for the aforementioned reason; unlike without a pan.
