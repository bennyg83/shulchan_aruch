One who wishes to earn merit should subdue his evil inclination and give generously. Anything done for the sake of Heaven should be from the finest and most beautiful. If he builds a house of prayer, it should be more beautiful than his own residence. If he feeds the hungry, he should give from the best and sweetest food on his table. If he clothes the naked, he should give from the finest of his garments. If he consecrates an item, he should consecrate from the finest of his property, as it is written, “All the choicest is for Hashem.” The source index preserves citation 6.
