A statement of Rav Zutra, Gittin 7b.
The language of Tur, based on Rif and Rosh concerning the Mishnah, "How long must one reside in a city," etc., and the baraita, "Thirty days for the communal soup kitchen," etc., Bava Batra 7b; and based on the incident in which Rava compelled Rav Natan bar Ami and extracted money from him, etc., there and in Ketubot 49b, in accordance with Tosafot's latter answer there. Rambam likewise wrote this in chapter 7 of the Laws of Gifts to the Poor, law 10, as did the other halachic authorities.
