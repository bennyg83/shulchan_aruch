Who is obligated in it and who is fit to receive it. And it contains 8 seifim. Every person is obligated to give charity, even a poor person who sustains himself from charity is obligated to give from what is given to him; and one who gives less than what is appropriate for him to give — beit din would coerce him and strike him with lashes of rebellion until he gives what they assessed for him to give, and they descend to his property in his presence and take from him what is appropriate for him to give.
