They do not assess charity upon orphans even for redeeming captives, even if they have much property, unless they assess upon them for their honor so that a reputation goes out for them: {Rama: And specifically charity that has no fixed amount, or that has a fixed amount on the orphans' property and they can wait until they grow up — such as if they have tevel and do not need to eat now; but if they need to eat now, they tithe and give them terumah, and the same applies to charity that has a fixed amount, such as if they had poor relatives and had a fixed allocation from their father each year and they have nothing to eat except this, or they would go door to door and it would be a disgrace for the orphans — the guardian gives the allocation from their property to their relatives. (Teshuvat R. Yosef Mintz siman 1)}
