A name. The Bach wrote that if they already have a good name, one does not assess them in order to give them greater importance. The Shach wrote that if the orphans' guardian stands before us and says that he does not want them to acquire a name, we do not force them.
From their father. The Shach wrote: it appears that this is specifically where they had a fixed amount from their father; otherwise one should not make a fixed allotment for their relatives, for if not, they would always become obligated to give through that allotment. Rather, the primary view appears to be that one assesses specifically in order to give them a name; but when giving to their relatives so they not be disgraced, one does not assess them for something that has no fixed amount, since no benefit is immediately made for them through this giving, but rather through it one removes the blemish that would later reach them.
