From the women. See in the responsum of Noda BiYehudah kamma part 14 siman 72, who wrote that if she said she gave by her husband's agency and with his permission — she is believed — see there; and likewise in the responsum of Bashamayim Rosh siman 166 — see there; and see in the commentary of Sforno on the Torah parashat Vayakhel on the verse "and the men came upon the women." And see in the responsum of Noda BiYehudah tinyana part Yoreh De'ah siman 158, where a woman was asked who knows her husband is stingy in giving charity and does not give according to his worth, and she is a widow within the house and distributes charity according to wealth, and she knows her husband is particular — whether it is permitted to accept from her; and he answered it is absolute theft and Heaven forbid to accept from her; and even though the court compels for charity, nevertheless who appointed the woman as judge, and a woman is disqualified to judge; and also the court has no permission to take from her without his knowledge; also to permit her in her pregnancy days to give her portion to the poor to pray for her and to make redemption for her — likewise is not correct, for this is not within healing, only if all her family members practiced so it is possible to say the husband too is obligated to do so for her and they compel him on this; but not that she should do this without his knowledge in theft — see there. [And see in Pitchei Teshuvah to Even HaEzer siman 91 s.k. 1.]
A small matter. See in the responsum of Beit Yaakov siman 12 and siman 56, that specifically in charity they take from them a small matter, because it is not the husband's way to be particular about a small matter for a mitzvah matter; but when it is not a mitzvah matter, even a small matter is forbidden — see there.
