Even if he has nothing, etc. Rambam and Tur, based on the statement there, “More than this, Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel,” etc. Hagahot Maimoniyot questions Rambam: selling it does not carry a sign of blessing, etc., but there is no prohibition, just as regarding his daughter. At the end of Bikkurim, the Yerushalmi states: R' Yose said in the name of R' Chiyya bar Va, “They asked Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel whether a person may sell a Sefer Torah to marry a woman. He answered them, ‘No, because of his livelihood,’ and they did not respond,” etc. Why did they not respond? When R' Chiyya came, R' Pinchas said in the name of R' Yochanan, in the name of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, “A person may sell a Sefer Torah to marry a woman and to study Torah. All the more so may he sell it for his livelihood.” Darkei Moshe answers that Rambam's case, where he has nothing to eat, applies only in pressing circumstances.
