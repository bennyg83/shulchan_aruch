A new one. The Bach writes that the prohibition is not limited to selling an old Sefer Torah in order to purchase a new one. The same applies where he initially spent his own money to purchase a new one and afterward wishes to sell the old one to reimburse himself. Only an elevation in sanctity is permitted. When there is no elevation, even though the two are equal and there is no reduction in sanctity, exchanging one Torah for another is forbidden. See Orach Chaim, siman 153.
