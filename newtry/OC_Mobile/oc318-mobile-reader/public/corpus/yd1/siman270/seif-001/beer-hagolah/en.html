A statement of Rava, Sanhedrin 21b, and Rambam at the beginning of chapter 7 of the Laws of a Torah Scroll. Rava derives this from the verse, "Now write this song for yourselves." Since we hold that the Torah may not be written as separate sections, because the Torah was given as a complete unit, Gittin 60a, the verse means that one must write the entire Torah through the song of Haazinu, which is the completion of the Torah.
