A statement of Rava, Sanhedrin 21b, and Rambam at the beginning of chapter 6 of Hilkhot Sefer Torah. He derives it from the verse, “And now, write this song for yourselves.” The explanation is that since we hold that the Torah may not be written in separate sections, for the Torah was given complete, Gittin 60a, this means that they should write the entire Torah until the Song of Haazinu, which is the completion of the Torah.
