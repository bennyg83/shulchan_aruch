Every man. See in responsum Beit Efrayim part 4 siman 62 regarding writing a Torah scroll in partnership whether they fulfill their obligation thereby; and see in Sefer Pardes David of Rabbi David Dishabek z"l Parashat Ki Teitzei that he too was uncertain — perhaps we require it all be theirs to fulfill partnership as stated regarding etrog in Bava Batra 137 — see there (and even though in Orach Chayyim siman 658 seif 7 in the Hagahah he wrote specifically where they did not acquire for the sake of the mitzvah, etc. — that is different there for each one needs it only at the time of taking to fulfill with it, unlike here where each one needs the Torah scroll to be his at all times and all hours — and it is plain); and he greatly wondered at the custom of the world for it is plain in the eyes of all partnerships that they write a Torah scroll in partnership that each one fulfills with it; and again he wrote to prove somewhat the custom of the world that they fulfill in partnership thereby.
