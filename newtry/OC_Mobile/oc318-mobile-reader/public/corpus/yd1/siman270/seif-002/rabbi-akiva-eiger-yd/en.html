(There) He may not sell it. Torat Chaim writes that one should not dedicate his Torah scroll to the community unless he writes another for himself. Once dedicated it belongs to the sanctuary rather than to him, and he no longer fulfills 'Write for yourselves.' This implies that one who owns a Torah scroll in partnership does not fulfill the mitzvah, since after dedicating it to the community he too remains only a partner. See Pardes David, Parashat Ki Tetzei.
