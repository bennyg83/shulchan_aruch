Nowadays. The Perishah writes that today there is no positive commandment at all to write a Sefer Torah. In earlier times they were forbidden to write separate leaves and had to learn from a scroll, but today we are permitted to write individual books in separate leaves. There is therefore no reason to diminish the honor of a Sefer Torah by learning from it unnecessarily, and our obligation is only to write the other sefarim, as the Mechaber states. The Taz writes that although the Rosh's wording appears to support the Perishah, the Beit Yosef's view is correct that even today there is a positive commandment to write a Sefer Torah. How could the positive commandment, "And now, write this song for yourselves," be nullified merely because generations have changed?
