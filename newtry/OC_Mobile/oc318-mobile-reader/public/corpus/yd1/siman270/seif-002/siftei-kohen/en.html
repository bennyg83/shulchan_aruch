Nowadays, etc. — Perishah wrote: it appears this is what Rosh meant — the positive mitzvah is said specifically regarding these and not regarding a sefer Torah, for only in their days did they learn Torah by heart not from writing but orally, and needed to learn from a sefer Torah bound properly and precise in missing and extra letters and cantillation notes, etc.; but in our time, when we have permission to write books page by page each separately — why should we belittle the honor of a sefer Torah in vain to learn from it not for need? It follows that in those generations when they do not learn from it, there is no positive mitzvah in them — end; and so appears the main view of Rosh, and not like Beit Yosef and Bach who wrote that Rosh's view is certainly that there is a positive mitzvah to write a sefer Torah even nowadays, except that there is also a mitzvah to write chumashim of Torah, Mishnah, Gemara, and their commentaries; however Taz too and nevertheless Rosh at the beginning of Laws of Sefer Torah followed Beit Yosef's words.
