Only for a complete body. See Radbaz responsa II, end of siman 732, who wrote that if the shrouds are found whole and it is doubtful whether the body is whole or the limbs have separated, we assume leniently and he may become tamei for him. But if it is known that his limbs separated, even though all his limbs are lying in the coffin, he may not become tamei for him, according to the stringent view in this matter; see there for his proof. See also Mishkenot Yaakov responsa siman 71.
