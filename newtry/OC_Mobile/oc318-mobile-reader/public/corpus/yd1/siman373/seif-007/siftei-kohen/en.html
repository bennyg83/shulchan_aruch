Specifically after he has separated from his deceased, etc. The prohibition is not caused by going to the burial, for then he is occupied with his deceased and may become impure even through other graves. The concern is that on returning he must cross graves after he has already separated from his deceased. The Bach rules that even on the way there he may not add impurity when it is possible to bury at the edge of the cemetery. This also appears to be the Mechaber's view. [Citation 80.]
