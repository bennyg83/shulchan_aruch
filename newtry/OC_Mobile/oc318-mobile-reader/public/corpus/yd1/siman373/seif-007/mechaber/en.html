It is forbidden for a kohen to become impure for the dead even while becoming impure for his relatives; therefore a kohen whose relative died must be careful to bury him at the end of the cemetery so he not enter the cemetery and become impure from other graves when burying his dead: {Rama: (Specifically after he left his dead; but while still occupied with his dead, it is permitted to become impure even for others) (Tur).}
