Only if complete. See Responsa Radbaz II, end of siman 732, who writes that if the shrouds are found intact and there is a doubt whether the body is complete or its limbs have become separated, we are lenient and the kohen may become tamei for him. If it is known that the limbs have separated, however, then even if all the limbs are lying in the coffin, he may not become tamei, following the stringent authorities. See his proof there. See also Responsa Mishkenot Yaakov, siman 71.
