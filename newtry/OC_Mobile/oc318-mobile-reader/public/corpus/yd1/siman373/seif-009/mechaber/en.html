A kohen does not become impure for a limb from the living from his father, nor for a bone from his father's bones; and likewise one gathering bones does not become impure for them even though the spine remains — if anything is missing from it, even if placed beside him, he does not become impure for it, for he becomes impure only for a complete one; and some say this is when the deficiency was after death, but if a limb was missing in his lifetime and he died, he becomes impure for him even though not whole: {Rama: (And some say he does not become impure for one executed, for he is called deficient) (Kol Bo in the name of R' Shimon of Aueria); (and it is proper to be stringent).}
