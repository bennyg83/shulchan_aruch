For a doubtful relative. See Responsa Beit Yosef, siman 130, who writes that the ruling here, that one does not become tamei for a doubtful relative, such as a doubtful nine-month child and the like, applies specifically to the father becoming tamei for the son. The son, however, may become tamei for both possible fathers, because the Torah's decree concerning a father treats the doubt as certainty. See there. In Nachalat Tzvi I challenged this ruling.
