The golel. The Shach wrote: in siman 375 the Tur wrote that there is a dispute between Rashi and Rabbenu Tam as to what the golel is. The Bach wrote that we follow Rashi, who explained it as the coffin cover. The Ramban wrote that according to Rashi, when they place him in a coffin in the house and close the cover with nails intending to put the coffin in the grave, that is considered closure; the Ramban wondered at him, for how is it possible to say that if they sealed his coffin in the house, his son may no longer become tamei for him? It appears to me that when Rashi everywhere explains golel as the coffin cover, he does not mean even if it was sealed in the house. Rather, he means from when the coffin cover is sealed in the grave. Know this, for in chapter HaShoel we say: everything said before the dead, he knows until the golel is sealed, as it is written, “and the dust returns to the earth as it was.” Rashi explains: the body is called dust, and once it returns to the earth as it was, immediately “the spirit returns,” for there is no longer spirit in it. End quote. According to this, even if the coffin is sealed with nails in the house, according to Rashi too his son may become tamei for him until it is sealed in the grave. End quote.
