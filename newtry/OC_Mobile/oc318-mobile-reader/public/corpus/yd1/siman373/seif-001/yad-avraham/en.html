(Siman 373, Shulchan Aruch seif 4) And he may become impure for a woman awaiting yibbum. Atzei Almogim objects from Yevamot 29b and Tosafot there on 56, and I likewise find difficulty from the Rosh to Yevamot 56, where it is clear that without intercourse a yavam may not become impure for a shomeret yavam. Rashi to Sotah 24b, Sanhedrin 69, and Niddah 44b also implies this. I further ask why, regarding annulment of vows, he cannot annul her vows, as stated in siman 234, where she is treated as less than an arusah, while here she is treated more favorably.
