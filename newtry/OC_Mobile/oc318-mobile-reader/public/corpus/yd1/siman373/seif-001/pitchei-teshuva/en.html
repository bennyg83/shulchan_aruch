For minors. See Responsa VeShav HaKohen, end of siman 56, who raises a doubt according to Rambam, Hilchot Avel chapter 2, that a kohen's tumah for close relatives is rooted in the obligation of mourning, and wherever there is no mourning he may not become tamei. If so, it requires study whether a minor kohen may become tamei for his relatives, since a minor is not personally obligated in mourning. Would a minor therefore be more stringent than an adult in this respect? Or perhaps, since Rambam writes that the father must separate the child from prohibited tumah for purposes of education, and education is to train him to fulfill the mitzvah when he becomes an adult, no educational prohibition applies here because as an adult he would in fact become tamei for these relatives. He leaves the matter unresolved.
