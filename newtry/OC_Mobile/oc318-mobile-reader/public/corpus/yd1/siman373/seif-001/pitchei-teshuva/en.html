For minors. Responsa VeShav HaKohen, end of siman 56, raises a question according to Rambam, who explains in Hilchot Avel chapter 2 that a kohen's permission to become impure for relatives is connected to the obligation of mourning. A minor is not obligated in mourning. Does that mean a minor kohen may not become impure for relatives, making his law stricter than an adult's? Or perhaps, since Rambam writes that the father must train the child in mitzvah observance so he will fulfill the mitzvah upon adulthood, no educational concern applies here: once adult, he would specifically be permitted or obligated to become impure for those relatives. He leaves the question unresolved.
