For minors. See VeShav HaKohen responsa, end of siman 56, who was uncertain according to the Rambam, who wrote in chapter 2 of Hilkhot Avel that a kohen's tumah for relatives is because of mourning, and wherever one does not mourn it is forbidden to become tamei. According to this, regarding a minor it requires study whether he may become tamei for his relatives, since a minor is not obligated to mourn. If so, a minor would be stricter than an adult in this respect. Or perhaps according to the Rambam, who wrote that his father must separate him in order to educate him, the education is that he should be careful to fulfill the mitzvah when he grows up; since when he is grown he does become tamei for relatives, chinuch does not apply here. He leaves the matter requiring study; see there.
