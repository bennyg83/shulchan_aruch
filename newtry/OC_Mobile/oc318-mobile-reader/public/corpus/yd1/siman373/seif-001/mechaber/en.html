Which kohen is warned about impurity, for which dead he may become impure, and until when he may become impure. In it are 9 seifim. Just as the kohen is warned not to become impure, so adults are warned regarding minors {Rama: (specifically to make them impure by hands; but if minors impart impurity by themselves, see Orach Chaim siman 343 whether they must be separated) (Rashba siman 128 and Mahariu and Tur in the name of Rambam, who wrote here that separation is not needed).}
