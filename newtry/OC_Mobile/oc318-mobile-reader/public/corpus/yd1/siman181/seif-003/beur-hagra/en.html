He is not liable, etc. The Tosefta there.
And there are, etc. The Mishnah implies that the prohibition applies specifically to the beard, as the Gemara there indicates: 'He is not liable,' etc. The Sages taught: 'The corner of their beard,' etc. This is also evident in chapter 6 of Nazir. See Tosafot, Shevuot 2b, s.v. 'liable,' etc., and Tosafot, Nazir 41b, s.v. 'now,' etc.
