He is not obligated, etc. Tosefta there.
And some [say], etc. For the mishnah implies [it applies] specifically to a beard; and as the Gemara marks there: "he is not obligated, etc." — the Sages taught: "and the corners of their beard, etc." And likewise it is implied in chapter 6 of Nazir; and see Tosafot, Shevuot 2b, s.v. "he is obligated, etc."; and Tosafot, Nazir 41b, s.v. "now, etc."
