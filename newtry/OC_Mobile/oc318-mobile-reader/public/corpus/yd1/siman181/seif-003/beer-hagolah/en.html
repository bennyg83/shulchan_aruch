There, in the name of Rosh, who holds that the Mishnah refers to the corners of the beard. This is also implied by the Talmud and demonstrated in Nazir 58a. The Tosefta's expression means "in a manner resembling a razor." The halachic authorities at the beginning of tractate Shevuot wrote likewise, as did Rabbeinu Yerucham and Semak.
