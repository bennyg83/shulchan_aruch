Regarding encircling a man's head. Above seif 6; and Bach disagreed and wrote that even one who permits encircling a man's head concedes that in destroying the beard there is a rabbinic prohibition to destroy a man's beard; and it is astonishing, for encircling is learned from destruction — just as where there is no "you shall not destroy" because they have no beard, there is no "you shall not encircle"; if so, if they are permitted in encircling, all the more so in destruction — and let it be clear.
