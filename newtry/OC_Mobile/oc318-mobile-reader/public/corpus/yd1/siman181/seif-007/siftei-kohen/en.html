Slaves. Even though their law is like women regarding obligation in commandments — they are obligated in rounding the head; for women are excluded from rounding the head only because it is written "you shall not round the corner of your head, nor shall you destroy the corner of your beard" — these women, since they are not included in destroying the beard, for they have no beard, they are also not included in rounding the head; therefore slaves, since they have a beard, they are not included in this analogy.
