The measure, etc. See Rashi on the Mishnah, s.v. 'two,' etc., and on the Gemara there, s.v. 'one who evens,' etc.
And the entire width, etc. See Beit Yosef, who is uncertain whether liability applies only when one removes the entire area, as also appears from Rashi in the Gemara there. The Tosefta there, chapter 4, states: 'One who plucks two hairs is liable on account of four prohibitions: nazir, metzora, Yom Tov, and rounding.' Thus, liability for rounding is incurred by removing only two hairs. See the Rambam and Bedek HaBayit in the name of the Ritva. Beit Yosef writes that because this is a Torah-level doubt, one must be careful regarding the entire width, etc. See Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
