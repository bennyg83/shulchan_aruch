Pe'ot of the head, etc. This is the language of the Tur; the Rambam wrote that the verse forbade them because idolaters do so, etc.; and it is not explicit, and we do not need a reason for mitzvot, etc.; and it appears that in this he disagrees with the Rambam, for the Rambam attached this law to idolaters, that it is their custom; and if so, sometimes there is permission because of darkei shalom, as we find in siman 178 seif 2; and on this the Tur wrote that behold it is not explicit that this is because of the customs of idolaters, and we do not find permission because of darkei shalom except in a prohibition because of the customs of idolaters; and you will say: since we do not know another reason for this mitzvah, automatically it is as if explicit that this is not so, for we do not need to know the reason for mitzvot, etc. — so it seems to me is the explanation of his words, and not as Beit Yosef wrote, that according to the Tur following the Rambam we do not need to fulfill the mitzvah whenever we do not know the reason — Heaven forbid that the Tur would think so in the opinion of the Rambam.
