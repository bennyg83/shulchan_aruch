You shall not make a bald spot, and so forth. Noda BiYehudah, second edition, siman 80, rules that one may not first cut the beard closely with scissors and then pass a razor over the skin on the theory that no hair of the minimum graspable length remains. Even in that form, the act still constitutes shaving.
Further, in siman 81 he writes that there is no distinction between a razor and a sharp stone that cuts hair, such as pumice-like material. Even with a depilatory paste spread over the face and allowed to dry, one should be careful not to scrape the dried paste away with a knife or other sharp instrument, because this can easily lead to prohibited destruction of the hair. Instead, one should use a wooden stick or smooth bone implement that removes the paste without cutting hairs.
