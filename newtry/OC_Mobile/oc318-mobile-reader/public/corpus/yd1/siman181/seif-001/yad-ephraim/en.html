Further there in siman 81: there is no distinction between a razor and a sharp stone that cuts the hair, such as pigment or pumice-stone. Even with a depilatory like a clay plaster that remains on the face until it dries and afterward they scrape the clay plaster from their faces, one must be careful not to scrape the clay with a knife or with any other sharp object, for one can easily come through it to hashchatah, etc.; see there. Therefore they should take a splinter or a smooth bone implement to scrape, for with this there is no concern of cutting hairs at all; see there.
