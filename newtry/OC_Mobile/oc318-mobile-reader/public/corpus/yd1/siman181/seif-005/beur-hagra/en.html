But it is permitted, etc., and there is. Nizzachon Yisrael end of Makkot, and thus: and there are those who are uncertain whether Israel is warned to encircle for an idolater according to Rabbi Eliezer — similar to a minor; and the Rosh wrote that even though Rabbi Eliezer forbade a man to encircle a minor, because there it is different since he comes to liability; but an idolater it is permitted to encircle him; and therefore likewise to encircle a woman is permitted. But Tosafot Nazir s.v. the above rejected the reason of the Rosh and wrote another reason to permit for an idolater and for a woman — they are uncertain; and in Tosafot Shevuot s.v. the above it implies it is forbidden for a woman, since they wrote that Shmuel is like Rav Adda, etc.
