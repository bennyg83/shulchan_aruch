A tumtum, etc., are prohibited, etc. Meaning, they do not receive lashes. As stated in the Tosefta at the end of Bikkurim, printed in the Talmud after Mishnah Bikkurim: 'In what respects is he like women?' etc. 'He wraps himself and cuts his hair like men,' etc., 'and he does not transgress rounding, destroying, or becoming impure through the dead, like women.' This means that he is not lashed because of the doubt. The Rama's wording regarding an androgynos, etc., differs from that regarding a tumtum, etc.
