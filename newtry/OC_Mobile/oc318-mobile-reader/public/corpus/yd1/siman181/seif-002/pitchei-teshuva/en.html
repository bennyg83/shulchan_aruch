And a minor. See in the responsum Beit Ephraim part 4 siman 62 s.v. and incidentally, who was astonished at Rema in this — that if his intent is that the minor is permitted to go by himself to the idolater who will encircle him without the knowledge of the adult, what is the novelty, for a minor eats nevelah; and it depends on the laws explained in Orach Chaim siman 343. And if he means that Israel is permitted to tell an idolater to encircle the minor — his answer is that since he encircles the minor he is liable, therefore one should forbid telling an idolater; and what he wrote in the name of the Ran, it appears his intent, etc. — see there, who left these words of Rema in doubt.
