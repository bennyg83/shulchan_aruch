The throat. And Bach wrote that one must be careful not to shave under the throat at all, neither with a razor nor with scissors like a razor (however this is certainly permitted when he does not shave completely close to the flesh but leaves some hair, for there is no destruction of the beard here; and likewise in Sefer Zichronot page 39, not to cut hair except by a barber's comb, in which the scissors do not touch the flesh at all; and the Ari z"l did not shave at all, neither with a razor nor with scissors, in any place at all, except for hair on the lip that impedes eating — he would cut with scissors; he was also careful not to touch his beard lest two hairs be uprooted, Heaven forbid, and he would damage and uproot one follicle, Heaven forbid); but Shach adopted the words of the Rav as primary and said not to add to the stringency — end of his words.
