He is not liable for destroying the pe’os of the beard except with a razor; but with scissors it is permitted, even like a razor. {Rama: Nevertheless, they are careful when they cut hair with scissors that he should make the surrounding shave with the upper part of the scissors and not with the lower, lest he do all with the lower part and it be like a razor (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 295). However, it appears that below the throat there is no need to be concerned for this, since it is not the primary place of the pe’os (the author’s words).}
