Destroying the corner of the beard. See in the responsum Noda Biyhudah tinyana part 4 siman 80, who wrote that regarding shaving the corner of the beard, even if there are not hairs sufficient for taking with tweezers, he is liable, for the Sages did not accept this measure except regarding a cow and negaim and a son and daughter; but it is not a general principle for the entire Torah, unlike Rav who said that this measure also applies here; and therefore he wanted to permit shaving the beard first with scissors and afterward to pass a razor on the flesh where the hair was, because initially when taking with scissors there is shaving and no destruction, and afterward at the time of passing the razor there is destruction and no shaving; and he challenged him and wrote that even according to his words there is great demolition here, and you have given your words to measures — today they shave with scissors to the root and tomorrow they shave a full shave twenty-four hours later, relying on the razor that comes afterward, and hairs will remain that have sufficient for taking with tweezers, and also hairs will remain that the scissors did not reach at all, and they destroy with a razor — see there.
But with a razor. Beit Hillel forbids to scrape with a hair-removing stone; and see in the responsum Noda Biyhudah tinyana part 4 siman 81, who wrote that specifically with a stone it is forbidden, because through its sharpening it cuts the hair like that which they call pumice stone; but with an ointment made like a plaster of clay, and the sharpness in it burns the hair — it is permitted, for this is destruction without shaving. But if after the ointment this plaster remains on their faces, it is not scraped with a knife but by hand — see there; and see on this in the responsum Shemesh Tzedakah part 14 siman 61 s.v. only this.
