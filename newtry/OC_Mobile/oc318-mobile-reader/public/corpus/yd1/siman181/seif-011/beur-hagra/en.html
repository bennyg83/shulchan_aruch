And they are in them, etc. That Rashi explained in Makkot there: two under the ear on the protruding part of the cheek outward, and two at the end of the cheek, and one in the beard's lock; and see there in the mishnah s.v. two, and in the Gemara 21a s.v. in two corners of the beard; but in Shevuot he explained that the cheek is wide toward the temples, and Rashi said there is width in it of two corners, and they are four corners, and one in the beard's lock; and Rabbenu Chananel explained the place of connection of the cheek to the temples — two corners as above, and two at the borders of the lip, and one in the beard's lock; and according to him the fifth is in the beard's lock, only that the Rosh wrote there differently from all the explanations; and in chapter 1 of Negaim we learn: what is the beard — from the joint of the cheek until the peak of the throat; and from his words it implies that therefore the fifth is at the head of the throat; and likewise the Remazim; and in the Tur this is Rabbenu Chananel's explanation, and his words are astonishing; and likewise Beit Yosef was astonished at him; and furthermore that it is explicitly taught in Torat Kohanim: the corner of their beard — the end of the beard, the beard's lock; and all the poskim said that under the throat there is no corner at all; and thus Semag: it is good not to raise a razor on the beard, and even under the chin he forbids Rabbenu Tam with a razor, for sometimes the skin of the corner — meaning of the beard's lock — is drawn to him; and Rabbi Yonah wrote for another reason because of passing hair, which is forbidden on the entire body with a razor, as written at the beginning of siman 182; and this is what is written in the gloss here, and even, etc.; nevertheless the words of Rabbi Yitzchak do not appear — what he said from all places of the beard that are not in the place of the corners, that it is permitted even with a razor, and they forbade only on the body that does not grow so much and one does only to beautify it, and it is forbidden because of "you shall not wear," etc. (Likkut) And they are in them, etc. And the main thing is as Rashi explained, and likewise in Orach Chaim (end).
