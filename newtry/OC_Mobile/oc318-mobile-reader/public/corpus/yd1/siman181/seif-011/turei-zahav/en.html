And even under the throat. In the Tur it is written that for Rabbenu Chananel there is one corner at the end of the beard on the throat; and Beit Yosef challenged, for this is not mentioned in the Gemara, only in Rashi according to Rabbenu Chananel's opinion he mentioned, and the beard's lock from below; and we were on the grounds that because the skin of the corner at the end of the bone called chin is drawn to him; and in the Tosefta the Rosh brought it is written: the corners of the beard from the joint of the cheek until the peak of the throat; and if so, at minimum the Tur should have written until the throat, which implies that there is the main fifth corner; and therefore he left it in doubt. And Maharshal elaborated on this to say that the main fifth corner is at the place of the throat itself, and the rest of the hair under the throat is not included in the prohibition at all; and the language of the Tosefta does not imply so, for it says until the peak of the throat and not on the throat at all — it emerges that under the throat there is no Torah prohibition, only rabbinically, for the reason mentioned in Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbi Yonah, that the Sages forbade passing hair with a razor in every place on the body, and even on his arms, because it resembles women's repair; and afterward he wrote in the name of Semag another reason, that it is forbidden for Rabbenu Chananel with a razor under the chin, because sometimes the skin of the corner is drawn to him, etc.; and therefore with scissors like a razor it is permitted there; and see in the next siman in seif 1 what I wrote there.
