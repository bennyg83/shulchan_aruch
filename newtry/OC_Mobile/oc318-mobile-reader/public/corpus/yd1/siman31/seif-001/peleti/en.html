The brain has two membranes, etc.; and many geonim hold we require both upper and lower perforated; Or Zarua wrote for this view: perhaps it resembles the crop — not opposite each other is kosher; Bach disputes; see Peri Chadash s.k. 1. It appears Ra'avan holds like Or Zarua: Ra'avan proved we require both perforated — otherwise what use is inserting the hand and pressing to see if it spurts? Perhaps only the lower was perforated and not the upper — then with lower perforation alone treifah, yet it would not spurt at the upper because the upper was not perforated and seals; and the Ra'osh wondered: the weasel struck the chicken's head from outside — how can the inner hole exist without the outer? An answer is needed, for such a simple matter would not be hidden from Ra'avan's wisdom; but one must say the Gemara said the weasel's teeth are curved. And one who examines by hand certainly does not dispute the reality whether weasel teeth are curved — only he holds one cuts the bone at the weasel's blow and around, and the membrane is exposed — why need "the bone seals"? One cuts the bone and examines the membrane — simple. But it is difficult: perhaps the blow was in a place passing through both membranes not opposite each other; if so, even if it spurts through a hole in the lower membrane, the upper membrane at that place seals — the upper hole is not aligned with the lower. Only what difference in truth if not aligned — kosher, for one protects the other like the crop; but this is as Or Zarua said if we require both perforated; but if the lower alone is perforated — properly difficult: perhaps they perforated not aligned and treifah because of lower membrane perforation, and it cannot spurt because at that place the upper membrane protects and the holes were slanted; and if not aligned — what of it? With lower alone if perforated — treifah; therefore we require both; and since they are not opposite each other — kosher as in the crop; and Or Zarua's law is compelled.
The lower was perforated bore-through — treifah. One should analyze: we say in the Gemara one inserts his hand inside and checks if it spurts; we rule below siman 33: a thorn sat in the esophagus — we are concerned perhaps the outer skin was perforated and healed in the membrane from the blow; why here with thorn and weasel tooth in the outer membrane do we not concern the inner membrane rose and healed from the blow and there is no hole? Do not say the inner membrane is soft and bursts from pressing — for according to the view that lower membrane alone was perforated we do not say the inner is soft and bursts; otherwise it would be difficult for one who examines by hand — he too causes monetary loss to Israel like one who examines with a nail: from pressing the lower bursts and spurts, and in truth the lower was not perforated; therefore one must say they hold the lower is not soft and does not burst. But even though we say the lower is not soft and does not burst — that is the membrane itself; but what was perforated and is only membrane from a blow — that certainly bursts and is soft — no difficulty. From this the many authorities who ruled upper perforated had proof: why does the Gemara challenge for the view upper alone — even for the view lower too it is difficult: perhaps the membrane rose and sealed? And if you say it is soft and bursts — I had not yet brought this to mind; therefore they hold one may indeed say thus, only the Gemara challenges according to the halakhah we hold: upper alone. Accordingly Ra'avan and Ra'osh followed their method: we are not concerned it healed — no difficulty; no proof here; therefore they ruled both must be perforated — examine well.
