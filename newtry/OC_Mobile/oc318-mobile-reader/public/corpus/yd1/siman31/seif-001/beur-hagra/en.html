Perforated, etc. Per Rif and Ra'osh text: upper membrane — and "they say" lower membrane; and Rif ruled like the latter wording per his method; and further Rabban Gamliel and R' Yehuda ben Levi reversed to give a sign.
(Likut) If the lower was perforated, etc. — per Rif text; but our text is until the lower membrane was perforated; and Tosafot HaRosh agreed for halakhah and proof (for the view that lower membrane requires both) from the sugya there 56 — even though one may reject: there necessarily the lower was not perforated without the upper; and some ruled like the first wording stringently — end.
And some, etc. Beit Yosef wrote: Bahag explains the first wording holds specifically upper alone; the latter wording holds even lower alone, and all the more upper. And this is according to the sugya there 56 "it works out," etc. But it appears he ruled like both wordings stringently — as Tosafot wrote there s.v. mar, etc.
