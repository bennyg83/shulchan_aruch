(siman 31 seif 1) The brain has two membranes one upon the other; lower perforated, etc. — treifah. So is Rif's view; and it appears to Ran so is Rambam chapter 6 Hilchot Shechitah. Tur wrote in Rambam's name: not treifah until both perforated — Ra'avan's view. Beit Yosef is truly doubtful of Rambam's view; hidden from him what Rambam wrote in mishnah commentary chapter Aleph Tav explicitly as Ran per his view — from there we learn explanation of his composition; if lower perforated — treifah — meaning lower alone too. So in books, Turei Zahav, and Arukh. This is majority poskim: Rambam, Rif, Ran, Hagahot Ashiri, Mordechai, R' Chananel, Rashba, R' Ovadiah of Bartenura in mishnah commentary, Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit. So explicit in Talmud; Rif, Ra'osh, Rashba, Tur in this matter until bulbs: membrane perforation in any amount; from there onward majority. So explicit Rambam chapter 6 Hilchot Shechitah; Beit Yosef brings siman 32 regarding spinal cord beginning from bulbs outward — majority; above that — any amount — see there — examine. One may wonder on Tur appearing not treifah in lower alone until both perforated; if so how wrote in this matter what R' Yehuda ben Levi said in Talmud (daf 45) until bulbs; and explicitly Ra'osh R' Yehuda ben Levi holds lower alone treifah. Resolve: practical difference if lower perforated there — treifah; if upper also perforated — if upper alone also not treifah — examine.
