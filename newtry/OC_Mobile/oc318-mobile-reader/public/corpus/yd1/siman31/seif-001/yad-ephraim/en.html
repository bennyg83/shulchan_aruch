See Beit HaTov; see Peninei Me'ir siman 54 resolving Beit Yosef's difficulty on R' Yonah who wrote there in Rabbenu Tam's name: skull deficiency with upper membrane — his words wondrous, for in understanding Hagahot R' Yehuda he is Rabbenu Tam — Hagahot Gedolot writes what Bahag concluded is Hagahot Gedolot; writes to be stringent like Rabbenu Tam — very wondrous, for Bahag preceded Rif and Rashi by generations, all the more Rabbenu Tam; but Hagahot R' Yehuda — though final hei missing one must mark at end; so found several times; Mahari Beruna already noted — see there. See responsa Beit Ephraim: in one city slaughterer opened large animal skull, removed brain, saw inside skull from skull bone inward a single long bone, sometimes barley length, prominent beside brain, sharp point, slightly wide at bottom half-finger, narrow above — found many times. I ruled no examination required afterward unless no loss at all where treifot sell like kosher; unlike when loss in treifot — examination afterward prevents slaughter and we fear loss and community pressure. If examined and found defect — do not be stringent saying not expert; visual and tactile examination fine since known place aimed at points; even if cannot aim exact place in lower — may rely on lower examination and good viewing; upper peel unnecessary after ruling spread upper alone kosher in hefseid merubeh — may rely at least not to require upper examination; but if through lower examination hole found — treifah; even if brain membrane lost before examination — treifah from safek it fills whole cavity — concern long bone reached lower and perforated; though some side to leniency — do not act to be lenient.
