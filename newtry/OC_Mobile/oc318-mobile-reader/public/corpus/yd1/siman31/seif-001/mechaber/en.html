The laws of piercing the brain and its membranes. In it are 4 seifim. The brain has two membranes, one upon the other. If the lower one was perforated — if the perforation is bore through completely — treifah; but if only the upper one was perforated alone — kosher. {Rama: And some declare treifah also when only the upper membrane was perforated (Bahag, brought by Hagahot Ashiri and Tur HaDeshen siman 166 and Hagahot ShaDaL siman 81) — and such is the custom unless it is not a place of great loss (his own opinion).}
