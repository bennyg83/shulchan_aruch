(siman 31 seif 1 in gloss) And such is the custom unless in a place of hefseid merubeh. Meaning: perforated through illness where we see it did not control the lower; but through a thorn — even in animal one may say treifah even in hefseid merubeh, lest lower was perforated — see Shach s.k. 5; and in Pri Megadim there.
