Bore-through perforation — treifah. See responsum Beit Ephraim part 4 siman 6: asked regarding a place where a slaughterer opened a large animal's skull, removed the brain, and saw inside the skull emerging inward from the skull bone a single long bone like a barley length prominent beside the brain — found on both sides of the skull; mostly near the horns outside; point sharp; known it presses the brain membrane inward and into the brain itself. Brought before the rabbi — peeled upper brain membrane from skull bone — perforated — declared treifah; afterward in many slaughtered animals this matter was found frequently. Three doubts: (1) require examination initially to open skull and check since minority occurrence? (2) if such points in skull bone — suffice to examine brain membrane whole, or say we are not expert? (3) if examination helps — law if membrane lost or torn before check? He answered first doubt: no initial examination needed even though minority occurrence — a hole in membrane causing treifah is not common; common is finding the defect — unlike lung adhesions commonly treifah. He added reasons: do not be stringent unless no loss at all where treifot sell at profit like kosher. If examined and found this defect — do not be stringent saying we are not expert, since known place aimed at points; even if cannot aim place — do not be stringent; visual examination suffices; upper membrane peel unnecessary. If membrane lost or torn before examination — treifah. See there at length (and see responsum Mishkenot Yaakov siman 15 disputing Beit Ephraim gaon on this matter).
