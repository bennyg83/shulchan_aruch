A) [seif 1] The brain has two membranes, etc. Upper membrane attached to skull inside; lower membrane is soft membrane in which brain rests — Rashi Chullin 45a; so Nahalat Shiva in Hagahot Tosefta ot 1, Zevachim Tosefta ot 1, Chokhmat Adam general 15 ot 6.
C) there. But if upper alone perforated — kosher. Gloss: and some declare treifah, etc.; and such is custom unless hefseid merubeh. Maharshal chapter Aleph Tav siman 11 and Bach forbid even in hefseid merubeh — see there; Taz s.k. 1; Shach s.k. 1; but Peri Chadash ot 1 wrote main thing lenient per gloss in hefseid merubeh; so Turei Zahav ot 1, Zikhron Adam part 3, brief 5 treifot ot 5 from Mechaber ot 3, Zevachim Tosefta ot 4, Chokhmat Adam there, Beit Yitzchak ot 1, Kometz ot 1, Einei HaKohen siman 3 ot 1, Zevach Todah ot 1.
D) However this is ordinary perforation; but if entire upper skin or majority impaired — even if lower has no defect — declare treifah even in hefseid merubeh — Turei Zahav end ot 1 from Shulchan Aruch ot 1, Beit Yitzchak there, Kometz there, Einei HaKohen there, Zevach Todah ot 3.
