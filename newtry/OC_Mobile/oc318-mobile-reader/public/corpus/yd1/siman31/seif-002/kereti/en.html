A little — Shach inferred from the word "a little" that much is treifah; and he gave a measure: we estimate whether so much is missing that in the spinal cord it could not stand — treifah; Peri To'ar agreed; see Peleti: this is incorrect — only "a little" excludes the majority — see there.
And missing from brain — Taz's view: when we require brain surrounding, it is when something is missing from the brain; but if nothing is missing, brain surrounding is unnecessary; and likewise in melting — if the brain surrounds — kosher; Peri Chadash disputes; see Peleti where I agreed with Taz — his words are reasonable.
And likewise if found — and all the more water between membrane and skull — treifah — Shach.
Therefore one must be careful, etc.: if they open and water flows and they do not know whether the brain surrounded — safek issur; and see below siman 56; and one may throw away the head unless an animal with schwindel, where water is more common than lung examination — examination required.
