(there seif 2 in gloss) There was a doubt whether covered by brain or not — treifah. See Tosafot chapter Aleph Tav daf 42b s.v. k'savar ein choshshin, etc.: we establish on presumption from life, etc. — see there; if so here we should establish on presumption brain presumably surrounded water; it appears every animal with water in brain — more common water surrounds; it is like safek derusah where we do not establish on initial presumption.
(siman 31 seif 1 in Hagahot) And such is the custom, etc. See Shach s.k. 5; it appears from his words he explains Mechaber: worm found between two membranes — in truth so in Hagahot Ashiri in the name of R' Chananel; but Mechaber may be explained as Mordechai: worm found inside brain itself — came from outside; so from Tosafot Gittin daf 56 — see there; therefore Rama wrote: if we were expert in examination it would be permitted; and what Shach wrote not comparable to worms on lung, etc. — one may say: certainly if hole also in brain — say after slaughter separated; here we deal only with blood drop — certainly before slaughter separated, for after slaughter no blood drop is made at all; but truth of matter: most poskim do not say "after slaughter separated" except regarding lung, not elsewhere.
