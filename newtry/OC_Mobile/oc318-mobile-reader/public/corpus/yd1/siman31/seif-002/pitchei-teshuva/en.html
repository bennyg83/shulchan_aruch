Great loss. See responsum Har HaCarmel Yoreh Deah part siman 15 explaining in several places leniency in hefseid merubeh — how much is hefseid merubeh. He brought Nachalat Shiva siman 55: general — loss more than one-sixth of value is hefseid merubeh (also in Beit HaTov siman 95 s.k. 4 with slight change — see there). He disputes: if item worth six gold and loss more than one gold — permit for owners' hefseid merubeh; but if worth six hundred gold and loss ninety-nine gold — forbid, saying only slight loss — words of rabbanan like mockery. He also refuted Nachalat Shiva's other words. He concluded: no fixed amount — all per teacher's eyes, time, and person whose loss it is — if great to him, hefseid merubeh — see there. See responsum Maor HaGolah Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 43: doubtful in several matters where they are lenient in hefseid merubeh — such as bubbles adhering in water: if butcher slaughtered two or three animals all with two adhering bubbles — one animal not hefseid merubeh, two or three is — is that hefseid merubeh or not since each alone is not? Discuss to be lenient — see there simanim; from this if three animals of three people — even all came together before rabbi needing ruling treifah — not hefseid merubeh, no leniency; possibly same one animal of partners — not hefseid merubeh to each (see responsum Chasam Sofer Orach Chaim siman 65 brought in Pithei Teshuvah Orach Chaim siman 472 seif 1: permitted in hefseid merubeh only for animal owner and household due to his loss; why should others buy initially? Probably must inform; if sold without informing — possible defect in sale and return; but if no other kosher meat in city except this animal permitted in hefseid merubeh — permitted for all; only scrupulous souls who are stringent on themselves, etc. However from Ra'ak responsum: even butcher selling to others they permit in hefseid merubeh — strained to say dealing when no other meat; then obvious to be lenient for community need; nevertheless certainly must inform as Chasam Sofer; proper in such case to lower price slightly for poor).
