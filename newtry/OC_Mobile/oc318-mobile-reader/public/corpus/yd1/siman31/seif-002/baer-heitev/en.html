Kosher. Shach wrote: even through a little deficiency — in a case where if the same occurred in the spinal cord one holds it and leaves the empty part above his hand and it would stand and not bend — kosher; but if much is missing so that if the same occurred in the spinal cord, if much protruded above his hand it would bend — treifah. And specifically in brain adjacent to the membrane; but if it melted in the middle while the brain surrounds all around — kosher (Maharam Alashkar in the name of Peri Chadash).
Treifah. Meaning: if it were thus in the spinal cord it would not stand — and see above siman 32.
Treifah. Taz ruled: if the brain is whole on all sides and after removing the brain a little water is found in the skull — kosher; for the essence of prohibition in water is lest it is from the brain that melted; unlike here where we see the brain whole. But in water inside the pouch between brain and membrane or between membrane and skull — even if the brain is whole — treifah, for we see it came from the brain. Shach wrote: all the more if water is found between membrane and skull. Peri Chadash disputes Taz and concludes: if water or pouch is found in the brain itself — whether whole or deficient — the brain must surround the water or pouch, for then it is healthy; and if the water or pouch is exposed to the membrane in any manner — treifah; and if placed between membrane and skull — even if the brain is whole — treifah; and all the more if found between membrane and membrane. (Maharam Alashkar.) (And it appears to me we deal when a little of the brain is missing, per Taz.)
Opening. Shach wrote: this is specifically where no defect appears before us; but if there is a defect — such as schwindel, shaking its head — forbidden until permissibility is clarified by opening its head — Taz. And now the custom is in the month of Tammuz not to open any head of small animals such as kids and lambs, because water is common in them and one cannot be careful in opening if they are covered; for this we rely on the majority — Beit Yosef. Once a sheep's head came before us and something like saliva issued from the head near the horn; we cut the head and removed the brain; the bone was whole and we declared kosher, for the moisture came only from the root of the horn — as Nahalat Shiva in the name of Maharshal. If water is found in a lamb's head — Damtrafa in it — and it is unknown which lamb: one must check the spinal cords of the sheep; and any sheep in which water is in the brain in the head and water was in the cord proceeding from brain to spinal column — we presume that head is from that sheep to permit the others — Bach, end siman 101; and Taz wrote it is not worthwhile to permit on this safek — see there end siman 101. Jews who bought meat from a non-Jewish butcher before they knew the animal had water in its brain — afterward when they knew, they returned the meat; one did not wish to return, claiming the membrane surrounded it and therefore the meat is kosher. He ruled in responsum Noda B'Yehudah siman 15 they must put him in nidui, since the law is not clear to all and a question to a sage is required — see there.
