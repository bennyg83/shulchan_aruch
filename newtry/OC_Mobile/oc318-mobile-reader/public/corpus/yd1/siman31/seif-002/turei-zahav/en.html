And missing from the brain, etc. But if the brain is whole on all its sides and after the head was cut and the brain removed a little water is found in the skull — there is no prohibition here; for the essence of the prohibition in water is that we say the brain melted and became water; but here we see the brain is whole — there is no prohibition, and the water came from elsewhere. But in water inside the pouch that lies between brain and membrane, or between membrane and skull — even if the brain is whole — treifah, for we see it came from the brain — as above.
