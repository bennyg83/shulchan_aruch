The brain, etc. — Rambam's wording; and as the Gemara cites Rashba, etc.; and "this became mushy" cannot be explained like by the spinal cord "it swayed," for that is inapplicable here — it must mean decayed; and there "I am not" — etc.; and "this became mushy" means it was crushed, for one cannot say it decayed immediately when slaughtered; and for the law swaying, crushing, and decaying are all one — only Rambam wrote as the Gemara states.
And if, etc. As Mechaber wrote: melted, etc.; and "like water" means like a full pitcher of water; and there "it melted," etc.; and Rif explained: it melted like wax from fire; and when one places it, etc.; and regarding the brain they said: that one melted — it was stated; if so, all the more for crushed; and also they challenged from brain to spinal cord — learn they are equivalent.
And if found, etc.; and the same, etc.; and likewise, etc. For whatever is exposed to the membrane does not return healthy and will perforate the membrane; unlike the brain which is not harmed — for all brain disqualifications are because of the membrane — see Rashi s.v. mipnei kevado, etc.; and all these, etc.
There was a doubt, etc. And we do not say: it was slaughtered — permitted — as written above siman 50 seif 1 in the gloss; and see there.
And nevertheless one need not, etc. See Rashi 12a s.v. pesach; and on this, etc.; and see Gemara 11a; and we are concerned perhaps, etc.; and even though one rejects: it is mere pushing off (see above siman 39 s.k. 1).
