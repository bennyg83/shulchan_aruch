For we presume it did not reach, etc. And even though the worm's mouth faces outward and it appears it was already on the brain — nevertheless it is permitted because of a double safek: perhaps it turned over and never reached there at all; and if you say it reached — perhaps it did not perforate the membrane so much — see Darkei Moshe. And it is wondrous: from a needle in the body cavity we hold treifah because perhaps one of the places was perforated — see siman 51; and behold there too is a double safek; and in Or Zarua the long one he gave another reason: we do not establish prohibition from place to place. On this too it is difficult: for Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba there that whenever a thorn was inserted not in our presence into the heart — even though it did not perforate a bore-through hole — treifah, lest perhaps one of the other organs was perforated and afterward it was inserted into the heart; behold we establish prohibition from place to place. Therefore in my humble opinion: here they did not permit when found outside the cavity except after examination — they examine the brain membrane and find no defect; and although Shulchan Aruch wrote at the beginning we are not expert — that refers to the cavity itself, not to permit through examination alone; unlike here, where there is another factor for leniency: it was found outside the cavity — we may well rely on examination; but if they did not examine the membrane it appears one must forbid even outside the cavity, lest it was in the cavity and came out — as above. And further one may answer: in a needle in the body cavity you must say it made some hole and entered there — if so, one may well be concerned perhaps one of the organs was perforated; unlike here, where it is not necessary the worm made a hole — therefore we do not establish prohibition from place to place; and so is proven in siman 33 seif 9 as written there. If so, one need not examine the membrane here at all; but nevertheless it appears proper to examine the membrane since clarification is possible — we do not rely on safek (we stand) on the safek.
