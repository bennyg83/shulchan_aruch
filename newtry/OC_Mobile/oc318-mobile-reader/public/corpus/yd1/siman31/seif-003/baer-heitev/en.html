Shach wrote: and it is not comparable to worms found on the lung below siman 36 seif 5, for that case is different: there a worm cannot perforate through the lung's constant fluttering without pause while alive; and likewise in the crop end siman 49: its membranes are hard and thick — we are not concerned it perforated them while alive — unlike the brain membrane.
And such is the custom. Shach wrote: even with great loss. Maharshal permits if no trace is found in upper or lower.
Ever. Taz wrote: and even though the worm's mouth faces outward and it appears it was on the brain — permitted, for perhaps it turned over and never reached there; and if you say it was there — perhaps it did not perforate the membrane — a double safek; nevertheless one may not permit except after examination, for it is no better than a needle in the body cavity siman 51, where there is also a double safek; and even though one may distinguish — nevertheless it is proper to examine the membrane since clarification is possible; and here there is another factor for leniency.
