And sacrificial offering, etc. And thus Beit Yosef: that according to Rambam, if it was found in the place of its worship, all is forbidden even if it is not brought on the altar — accordingly these candles too, if they were brought into the place of their worship, they are sacrificial offerings and cannot be nullified — end; and his words are correct, and any person of elevated soul should be stringent. And not only candles — but all things that were in their house of idolatry, there is a concern of sacrificial offering to idolatry, for perhaps they were brought into the place of their worship, and they have no nullification at all according to Rambam — end Bach.
But he threw it, etc. Specifically when its worship is not by throwing; but if its worship is by throwing, he is liable and it is not forbidden — thus wrote the Ran; and this is what Rav Nachman said in Shas: if he threw it before it he is liable and it is not forbidden; and so it is in Rambam, as below in the following seif katan.
