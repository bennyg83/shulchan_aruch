Candles and wax of elilim are forbidden to be used for a mitzvah lamp of Shabbos or Chanukah, or for [lighting] a beit knesset. And likewise, ornaments of the priests (such as the coats that their priests wear) — one should not make from them tallisos or any mitzvah item, because they are repulsive (and see Orach Chayyim siman 154).
