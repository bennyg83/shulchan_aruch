The idol itself and its ornaments depend on its wholeness; as long as it is whole, he worships it and maintains it as an ornament, but once it becomes defective that thought is nullified. This is not so with an offering, for even a small, insignificant item may be offered before it; therefore its prohibition does not lapse if it becomes defective. Taz.
