Anything of which the like is offered “inside”: if one finds it in the presence of elilim, or he finds it inside the partition spread before it, it is forbidden, for we assume that it was brought there as an offering — even water and salt, which are not a manner of honor. And anything that one finds outside the partition: if it is a manner of honor, it is forbidden due to ornament; and if not, it is permitted. And regarding Pe’or and Markulis (meaning, elilim whose worship is throwing a stone, and “kulis” is its name; and they called it Markulis as a derogatory name, meaning “its replacement,” for so “mar” means replacement) — everything that one finds with them, even in a manner of disgrace outside, all is forbidden.
