Kalafim, etc. And in the writings of Mahar"i siman 112 he was uncertain whether it is forbidden to give to schoolchildren the writing implements called Indian ink, quill, and wax; and the Bach wrote there is no prohibition in the matter, but it is proper to be cautious; and likewise Maharshal wrote in Biurei Semag; and Demah siman 121 wrote that it is only when it is known that he wants to write a Torah scroll, but unqualifiedly it is permitted.
