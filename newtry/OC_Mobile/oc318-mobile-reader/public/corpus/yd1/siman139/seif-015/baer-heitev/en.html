Belonging. Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote: likewise their twenty-four [canonical] books written in a foreign language or their other languages—it is forbidden to sell them—for whoever copied them changed the language to undermine and strengthen their faith—he elaborated.
And the strict. Bach wrote: by law it is forbidden; and Maharai was uncertain if it is forbidden to give gentile schoolchildren learning writing—called "voladur" and "volader" and "volish." Bach wrote: there is no prohibition in the matter, only proper to beware; so too Maharshal. He wrote in Darkei Moshe: all this is only where it is known he wants to write a religious book; but unstated it is permitted.
