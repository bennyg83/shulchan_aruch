And some say, etc. Beit HaGadol; but the Ramban wrote that it is not so, but only regarding idolatry itself, as written above seif 2 — because its value is precious, etc.; unlike appurtenances, where affection is not upon them as upon idolatry; and as written in the Tosefta: even vessels that are used with it and in its body — if they stole them and sold them, behold they are permitted.
