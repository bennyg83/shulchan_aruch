Garments that the priests wear when they enter a house of elilim are their ornaments and not the ornaments of the elilim, and they do not even require nullification; but there is one who requires nullification. (Mordechai, perek R. Yishmael, in the name of R' Meir.) (But what they wear for the idolatry itself is called an ornament and requires nullification) (in Semag; and in Hagahot Maimoni, perek 7; and in Mordechai, perek R. Yishmael).
