For since he extinguished them for his own need, etc. It implies that if they were extinguished by themselves — even though he sold or pawned them — they are forbidden; and like the opinion of R' Yitzchak that the Tur and poskim brought. For just as the idolatry itself is not nullified by its sale or pawn, so too its ornaments and appurtenances. And there is a difficulty: in seif 12 the Mechaber brought the poskim's view that it is nullified by sale and pawn unqualifiedly; and afterwards R' Yitzchak's view in the name of one authority; and likewise below siman 146 seif 8 the Rav wrote simply that it is nullified by sale and pawn — requires study.
