When does she recite the berachah of immersion? It contains one se’if. When she removes her garments, while she is standing in her garment, she recites: “Blessed are You… Who sanctified us with His mitzvos and commanded us concerning the immersion,” and she removes her garment and immerses. And if she did not recite the berachah then, she recites it after she enters the water up to her neck; and if the waters are clear, she muddies them with her feet and recites the berachah.
{Rama: And some say that she should not recite the berachah until after the immersion (Tur in the name of Ba’al Halachos Gedolos; and it is Behag daf 85b; and Rashi; and Ramban siman 328; and Sha”d). And so is the practice: after the immersion, while she is still standing in the water, she covers herself with her garment or with her robe and recites the berachah.}
