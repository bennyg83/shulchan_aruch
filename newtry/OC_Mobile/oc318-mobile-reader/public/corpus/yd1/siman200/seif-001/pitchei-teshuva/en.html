After the immersion. See Responsa Har HaCarmel, Yoreh De’ah, siman 25. He writes that even in a place where the custom is not to recite the blessing afterward, but rather before immersion, that applies specifically on a weekday. When a woman immerses on Shabbat, she should follow this practice and recite the blessing after immersion, or recite it quietly before immersion, moving only her lips. See there for his reasoning.
