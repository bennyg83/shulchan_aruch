Turei Zahav wrote: it is possible that in the days of Rama they practiced thus, but now they do not practice so; and we too have heard that women are not careful even with embracing their arms in a separation between heart and nakedness. And it appears this too is according to the law, only that initially one should embrace the arms, for there is no doubt about this — end of quote. And likewise Shach — our women bless in clear water with their eyes outside the water, and their practice is Torah — end of quote. (And Shelah wrote that practical men should caution their wives to immerse and bless, and afterward immerse a second time.)
