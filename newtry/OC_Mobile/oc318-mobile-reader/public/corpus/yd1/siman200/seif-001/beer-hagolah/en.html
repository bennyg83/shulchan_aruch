The Tur in the name of his father, the Rosh, and the Ra'avad, in accordance with the Rif in the name of the Geonim concerning the Baraisa, 'On all mitzvos one recites the blessing before performing them.' Rav Chisda said: 'Except for immersion.' Pesachim, daf 7b. The Rif writes in their name that Rav Chisda said this only regarding the immersion of a convert, because before immersion the person is not yet fit to recite the blessing. Rambam likewise writes this in chapter 11 of Hilchos Berachos.
