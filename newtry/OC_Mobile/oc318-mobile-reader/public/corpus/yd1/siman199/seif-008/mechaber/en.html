On weekdays (and all the more so on Yom Tov), if she washed and examined herself today, and immersed on the night of another day, b’dieved the immersion counted for her, even though the washing and bedikah were not close to the immersion. But if she did not wash at all, the immersion did not count for her, even though she examined herself on her body. And even if she washed immediately after the immersion and combed with a comb and did not find any hair tied, the immersion did not count for her. And needless to say, if she washed in a place of hair and did not examine the rest of her body, the immersion did not count for her, for examination of the body is by Torah law.
