On weekdays, etc. This follows from Niddah 66b: if she immersed and emerged, etc., and if the immersion was close, etc. Otherwise, in every case she need not immerse again. The passage must be referring to inspection of her body, as explained above in seif 6, for this certainly must initially be performed close to immersion. The phrase, ‘something was found upon her,’ also implies that it was found on her body, as explained above. All the more so regarding chafifah: since even initially, where it is impossible, such as on Yom Tov, an interval is permitted, it is obvious that after the fact the immersion is valid. Terumat HaDeshen. This is why the ruling mentions both chafifah and inspection.
