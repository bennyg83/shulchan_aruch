Or she gave her son a cooked dish, etc. And where her son had a cooked dish in hand, or he was dirty with mud, and he took her son by her bare hands, and afterward she immersed and did not examine herself — her immersion did not count, for it is similar to giving a cooked dish to her son — Bach.
But she is permitted to put on, etc. For it is not comparable to giving a cooked dish — for it is not common for clothing to have something moist that would adhere to the woman's body — ibid.
