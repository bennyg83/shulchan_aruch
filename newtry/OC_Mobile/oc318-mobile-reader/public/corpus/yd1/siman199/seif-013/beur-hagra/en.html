If she performed chafifah, etc. Raavad and Rashba. Otherwise, even apart from this concern, the immersion would not be valid, as explained above in seif 8.
If she handled, etc. This is derived from the case in which she gave a cooked dish, etc., as the Shulchan Aruch continues.
Something capable of adhering. Rashba and Tur. This is obvious.
The immersion was not valid, etc. I already wrote in the preceding siman, in the supplement to seif katan 44, that according to the Rambam and Tosafot the immersion is valid. The Beit Yosef attempts to explain the Rambam by distinguishing a substance that adheres, regarding which the Rambam would concede, from the case of a woman who carries her son, etc. His words are strained, because the two cases are not comparable.
