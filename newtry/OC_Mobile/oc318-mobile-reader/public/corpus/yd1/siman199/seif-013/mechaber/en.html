If she washed before immersion, and between the washing and the immersion she dealt with interposing things, or gave her son a cooked dish that is fit to stick to her, the immersion did not count for her — even if she checked immediately after immersion and did not find on her anything interposing — for I say that when she ascended from the water it fell from her, and she needs another immersion. {Rama: Nevertheless, if she checked herself before immersion and saw that nothing stuck to her, she does not need another immersion (Hagahos Sha”d and Mordechai and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba). But it is permitted for her to wear her garments between the washing and the immersion, but she should not take a child near her (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam; and see above siman 198).}
