Her head. Shach notes that Ramban's wording is 'all the hair upon her.' The custom is to comb the hair of the head with a comb and thoroughly separate the rest of the body hair by hand.
Her body. The authorities agree that Ezra's enactment applies only to washing the hair. Women nevertheless accepted washing and rinsing the entire body. Accordingly, several leniencies apply to washing, but examining the whole body for interpositions is a Torah obligation. Shach.
