A woman must check her concealed places and the laws of chafifah on Shabbos and on a weekday; it contains 13 se’ifim. She needs to rinse the armpit and her concealed places with water (and not with other liquids) (Mahari”k, shoresh 159). And she needs to comb the hair of her head well with a comb, so that her hairs not be stuck to one another. And a woman also needs to examine herself and her flesh, and checks her entire body close to her immersion, so that there not be on her anything repulsive that interposes. And she should wash her entire body, and rinse with hot water at the time she washes her body and her hair.
