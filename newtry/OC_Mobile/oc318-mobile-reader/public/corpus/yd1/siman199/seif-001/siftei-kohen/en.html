And to comb the hair of her head, etc. Rambam’s language that Tur brought: Ezra and his court enacted that she should perform chafifah in every place where she has hair in hot water, and comb them or separate them with her hands very thoroughly — end quote; and so Rashba in Torat HaBayit, and Beit Yosef brings it; from their words it appears that what Ezra enacted — that a woman should perform chafifah — is in every place of hair such as armpit hair and pubic hair, not head hair alone; and so Ra’avan siman 326 page 60d: and when she performs chafifah she must comb all the hair of her body — head hair, armpit hair, and pubic hair — end quote; and so implied in Ran and other poskim; and so Bach seif 2; and nowadays they practice combing head hair with a comb and other hair on her body she separates with her hands very thoroughly.
And she performs chafifah on her whole body, etc. The agreement of the poskim is that Ezra's enactment is only for chafifah of hair; but they practiced being stringent to perform chafifah and rinse the whole body — and therefore we are lenient in several things regarding chafifah; but that she must examine her whole body that there be no interposing matter is from the Torah; and see Ran that Beit Yosef brings.
