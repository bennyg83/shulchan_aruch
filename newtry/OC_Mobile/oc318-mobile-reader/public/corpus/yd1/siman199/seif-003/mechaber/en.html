Chafifah must be l’chatchila close to her immersion. And the proper custom is that she begin to wash while it is still day, and be engaged in chafifah until it becomes dark, and then she immerses. And likewise is a proper custom that even though she washed, she should carry a comb with her to the place of immersion and comb there. {Rama: And in a pressing circumstance, when she needs to wash in the day, or when it is impossible for her to wash in the day and she needs to wash at night, she may do so (Beit Yosef in the name of the poskim, and in the name of Rambam, perek 2 of Mikvaos), provided that she not rush to her home and wash properly.}
