Chafifah, etc. As explained above, and in accordance with the interpretation of the She’iltos cited by Tosafot on 68a, s.v. kach, etc. Most poskim rule likewise.
And the custom, etc. This is in order to satisfy both interpretations, that of Rashi and that of the She’iltos. See Tosafot there and the Rosh.
And likewise the custom, etc. As explained above, the practical difference is whether immersion must immediately follow chafifah. Chafifah is performed with a comb, as Rashi writes in Bava Kamma, as cited above.
And in pressing circumstances, etc. The Gemara there distinguishes between a case where it is possible and one where it is not possible. The Rambam explains, in accordance with the She’iltos, that ordinarily she performs chafifah at night, but that ‘not possible’ refers to pressing circumstances.
Or when it is impossible, etc. This follows Rashi’s interpretation, and also the Rambam’s interpretation that the case involves pressing circumstances. Although Rashi did not explicitly explain ‘not possible’ in this manner, he nevertheless agrees with this ruling, as Tosafot write there: according to Rashi it appears that it is permitted, etc.; however, etc.; ‘come and hear,’ provided that, etc. This is also evident from Rashi, s.v. heich, etc. Tosafot there write that it is permitted even without a circumstance beyond her control.
