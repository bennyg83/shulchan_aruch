Chafifah, etc. As above, and per explanation of She'eltot there in Tosafot 68a s.v. kach, etc., and so most poskim.
And the custom, etc. To fulfill both explanations — Rashi and She'eltot explained, and on Tosafot there and Rosh.
And likewise custom, etc. As above — one amora: close to chafifah is immersion; and chafifah is with a comb as Rashi wrote in Bava Kamma as above.
And in time of pressure, etc. There in Gemara 38b — this possible, etc.; and Rambam explains per She'eltot that it is possible to comb at night except he explains "not possible" because of pressure.
Or when impossible, etc. This is per Rashi's explanation and per Rambam because of pressure; and although Rashi did not explain so that it is not possible, nevertheless he agrees to this as Tosafot wrote there — nevertheless per Rashi it appears permitted, etc., rather, etc.; "come and hear," provided, etc.; and as Rashi wrote s.v. heicha, etc.; and they wrote in Tosafot there — even without coercion.
