There in the Gemara, 68a. Ravin and Rava sent this ruling in the name of Rabbi Yochanan.
Tosafot there, s.v. Kach Amru, explaining the words of Rashi concerning the report sent by Ravin. Rashi wondered that it is preferable for the washing to be performed by day, because at night she rushes home. The Sheiltot of Rav Achai, however, writes that it is preferable at night, close to immersion. The Tur cites both views in the name of his father, the Rosh.
Tosafot there. The Rosh likewise writes this.
