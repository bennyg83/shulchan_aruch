If she washed and examined and immersed, and upon her ascending something interposing was found on her: if she immersed within the onah in which she washed, she does not need another immersion; and if not, she needs another immersion. {Rama: Even if the washing was close to the immersion — for example, she washed in the day close to evening and immersed at the beginning of the night — since it was in two onos (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam).} And according to Rambam, in either case she needs another immersion; rather, this one does not need to wash again, and this one needs to wash again. (And this is also the view of Rabbeinu Yerucham.)
