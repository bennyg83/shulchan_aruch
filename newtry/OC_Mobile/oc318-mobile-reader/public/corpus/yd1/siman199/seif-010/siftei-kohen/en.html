If within an onah, etc. And there are poskim who do not distinguish between within an onah but between close to immersion or not — that if chafifah is close to immersion she need not return to comb and immerse; and if not she must return to comb and immerse; and so is Raavad's view in his objections; and their dispute depends on the girsa; and one should be stringent like both views.
And if not, etc. Meaning: even if she examined close to immersion — since she did not perform chafifah close to immersion she needs another immersion; and so implied from Ran's words who wrote: and if you will say, if examination close to immersion is required — even if she did not perform chafifah close to immersion, why does she need to comb and immerse, for she examined her whole body close to immersion? One may say perhaps she did not examine well — but with chafifah there is no concern for this — end quote; and Beit Yosef brings it at beginning of this siman.
