The statement of Rava there, Niddah 66b: If it was close to the washing, and so forth.
The Tur in the name of the Rashba in Torat HaBayit. "Close" means on that same day. He writes that the Razah likewise rules this way.
Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilchot Mikvaot, according to his reading of the Gemara there. Kesef Mishneh and Beit Yosef explain this.
