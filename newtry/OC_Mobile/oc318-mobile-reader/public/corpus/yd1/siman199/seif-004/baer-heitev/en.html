Minhag. And Shach wrote that it is not dependent on custom but rather this is the essence of the law — that she wash and comb well on erev Shabbat and wash and comb on motza'ei Shabbat as well (and likewise if Rosh Hashanah falls on Thursday, Friday, or Sunday, Monday, and her immersion occurred on motza'ei Shabbat or motza'ei Yom Tov, she combs from Yom Tov or from erev Shabbat; and on motza'ei Shabbat or motza'ei Yom Tov they again comb on erev Shabbat — siman kaf zayin. A niddah whose night of immersion was on motza'ei Shabbat and on Friday she was still in her mourning days is permitted to do chafifah on Friday with hot water so she may immerse in her time for her husband (Pnei Me'orot she'elah 8 on erev Shabbat) — Bei Heitev wrote: if her husband was not in the city and came at night when she did not know he would come at night, she is permitted to comb at night and immerse).
