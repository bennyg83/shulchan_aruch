(Supplement) If the night of her immersion falls, etc. This is so even according to Rashi, and all the more according to the She’iltos. Although Rav Huna and Rav Chisda maintain that in such a case she performs chafifah on Friday, the halachah does not follow them, as Ravin sent the ruling that a woman should not perform chafifah then. Although the Gemara states that Mar Yemar taught the halachah, etc., we do not rule accordingly, as the conclusion states: ‘The halachah is that a woman,’ etc. See Terumat HaDeshen. According to Tosafot, s.v. kach amru, however, Mar Yemar’s ruling is authoritative, such as where the night of immersion falls on Yom Tov. This is why Rav Yemar answers that such a case is not found. As stated in Rav Yemar’s discussion concerning the night after two days of Yom Tov, etc., since such a case can occur only on a weekday, it is possible for her to perform chafifah then. It is not because the interval is excessively long, as Rashi writes, s.v. levad, etc. Rashi needed that explanation specifically in Rav Yemar’s reasoning, because Rav Yemar holds that even though the Gemara says that Ravin evidently disagrees, for our ruling the decisive consideration is Ravin’s statement. End of supplement.
