(Extract) If her immersion-night falls, etc. Even per Rashi and all the more per She'eltot; and although Rav Huna and Rabbenu Chananel hold that in such a case she combs on erev Shabbat, halachah is not like them, as Ravin sent — a woman should not comb; and although we say there that Mar Yemar derives halachah, etc., we do not hold so; and as we conclude "halachata — a woman," etc. Teshuvot HaRosh. But per Tosafot s.v. kach amru — that which Mar Yemar [said] halachata is, such as when her immersion-night is on Yom Tov; and this is what "vechidametz Rav Yeimar" — that we do not find such a case; and as written in the matter of Rav Yeimar — for motza'ei two days of Yom Tov, etc., it is possible, etc.; since we find them only on weekdays it is possible she combs — and not because of the reason of too far as Rashi wrote s.v. levad, etc.; and Rashi needed to explain so in the words of Rav Yeimar who holds that even though we say "apparently Ravin does not hold," etc. — but for us, because of Ravin (end).
