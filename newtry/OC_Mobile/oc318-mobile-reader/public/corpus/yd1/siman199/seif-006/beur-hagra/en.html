And if it is impossible, etc. At the time of immersion she must in any event inspect herself again, as the Shulchan Aruch continues below.
And at the time of, etc. Only regarding chafifah, which is rabbinic, do we rule leniently. Regarding inspection, which is required by Torah law, we do not permit any interval. The Gemara there states: ‘She gave a cooked dish to her son,’ etc. It is impossible that over so many days she would neither give food to her son nor eat with her hands. The passage must therefore be referring to chafifah. Ran and Terumat HaDeshen.
And she should rinse, etc.; and likewise, etc. All of this is included within the obligation of inspection.
Even on Yom Tov. As stated in Orach Chaim, siman 326, seif 1, in the gloss. Likewise, according to the Rif and Rambam she may wash her entire body with water heated before Yom Tov, as stated in Orach Chaim, siman 511, seif 2. Here, however, the ruling follows the Rosh and Tur, who are among those who forbid it, as stated there in the gloss.
