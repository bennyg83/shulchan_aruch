Hidden places, etc. on Yom Tov. But not the whole body — Tur and Beit Yosef. And the same on Shabbat — it is permitted to bathe with hot water heated before Shabbat her face, hands, and feet, or other limbs — as long as she does not bathe her whole body — and as explained in Orach Chayyim beginning siman 326. And in responsum Mas'at Binyamin siman 5 he wrote it is possible that in hot water heated on Yom Tov it is forbidden to bathe other limbs besides face, hands, and feet. And he concludes regarding bathing for wearing whites: whether on Shabbat or Yom Tov or Tisha B'Av or Yom Kippur — she dresses and makes her bed as on other days of the year; and bathing she must vary slightly — not to bathe only in that place and between the thighs whether in hot or cold; and on Shabbat and Yom Tov in cold water even her whole body; and in hot water specifically in that place and between the thighs, and specifically in hot water heated before Shabbat and before Yom Tov; and she should also be careful of the prohibition of wringing — not to bathe with a garment but with her hands; and all this is from law. But it seems to me that women have not practiced bathing nor wearing whites on Shabbat and Yom Tov — and possibly because not every woman knows to distinguish between hot water heated before Shabbat and Yom Tov or heated on Shabbat and Yom Tov; and she also does not know to be careful from the law of the prohibition of wringing; and where there is a custom — the custom stands; and where there is no custom, one should permit them as I wrote — end quote. And this reasoning is weak; and in my humble opinion the reason of their custom is that on Shabbat they would not wear whites because then they would need to immerse on motza'ei Shabbat, which would distance the immersion from the chafifah; and the proof is that on Yom Tov they wear whites and it is on Shabbat they practiced not wearing. And regarding wearing whites on Tisha B'Av see Orach Chayyim siman 551 seif 3 in the gloss; and regarding mourning see above siman 381 seif 5 in the gloss.
