If the night of her immersion is on Motzaei Shabbos and it is Yom Tov, such that it is impossible to wash then, she should wash on Erev Shabbos. And likewise, if two days of Yom Tov fall on Thursday and Friday, and the night of her immersion is on Friday night, she should wash on Wednesday, and tie her hairs so that they not become disordered. {Rama: She should also be careful on the days between the washing and the immersion from all filth, and that nothing sticks to her; also from touching cooked foods, or from giving them to her small children, she should be careful if it is possible for her to be careful, if they are things that stick (Tur; and so wrote Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh and Sefer Mitzvos Gadol and Sefer HaTerumah). But if it is impossible for her to be careful — for example, she has no one to do in her place, or she needs to touch them at the time of eating — there is no concern; nevertheless, she should wash her hands each time so that she not come to interposition.} And at the time of immersion, she should examine and check well all her body and the hair of her head, that there not be anything interposing; and she should rinse her concealed places with hot water that was heated, even on Yom Tov. And likewise, she should clean her teeth well at the time of immersion, so that no crumbs and no meat and no bone remain. (And see above siman 197, if the night of her immersion is not on Motzaei Shabbos, whether she may immerse on Motzaei Shabbos.)
