And likewise if two days, etc. In Tur it is written — or if Yom Tov falls on motza'ei Shabbat; and Beit Yosef wondered — all the more it is from the first law of two days Yom Tov on Thursday Friday; and some answer that Tur speaks in the latter portion of two days Yom Tov on Sunday Monday; and still difficult — this is the opening portion. And in Derishah he answered — if not for the latter portion we would have said specifically in the opening portion when Yom Tov falls Thursday Friday and immersion on Friday night she is permitted to immerse since on Yom Tov she can heat a kettle of water to rinse her wrinkles as Tur wrote nearby; unlike when her immersion is on motza'ei Shabbat where on Shabbat it is forbidden to rinse them, and on motza'ei Shabbat there is tumult in her house — it teaches that nevertheless it is permitted. With this it is resolved that in the opening portion Tur wrote "she combs on Wednesday" and did not mention rinsing — because it is possible to rinse on Yom Tov; unlike in the latter portion where he wrote that she rinses and combs on erev Shabbat — end quote. Per his words — one who immerses Friday night and Yom Tov on Friday needs to rinse her wrinkles and concealment on Yom Tov before Shabbat; and his words are correct.
Also be careful, etc. This is from words of Tur; and his intent — although in any case she needs examination and bedikah before immersion on her body and head as he concludes here in Sha"s, nevertheless in any case she should be careful from interposing things on days between from not doing chafifah at time of immersion but she relies on chafifah of Wednesday; and with this everything that was difficult for Beit Yosef on Tur in this is resolved. And Tur wrote — and if she immersed and did not examine herself beforehand — if she was careful to deal with the interposing matter her immersion counted, and if not her immersion did not count — end quote; and his reason — since she already combed and checked her entire body and head on Wednesday and was careful afterward on days between, it was proper immersion. And Maharshal z"l wrote that this "if she did not examine" that Tur wrote does not mean examination of the body — for certainly that was not immersion — rather it refers to extra examination, namely between her fingers, etc.; and his words are astounding — for this is established halachah that b'dieved prior examination helps as Tur wrote in the name of Rashba, and they ruled here in Sha"s seif 8.
