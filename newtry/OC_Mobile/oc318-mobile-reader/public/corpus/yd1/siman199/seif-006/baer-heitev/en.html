Be careful. This applies whenever washing is separated from immersion by one or two days. Shach.
Examine. According to all views she must inspect at the time of washing and again at immersion, and avoid contact with substances likely to adhere in between whenever possible. This is the accepted practice. Shach.
And rinse. She may wash individual limbs with hot water heated before Shabbat, but not the entire body. Shach records a custom not to wash or put on white garments on Shabbat and Yom Tov, possibly because of uncertainty about heating and squeezing. For Tishah BeAv and mourning, see the cited simanim.
