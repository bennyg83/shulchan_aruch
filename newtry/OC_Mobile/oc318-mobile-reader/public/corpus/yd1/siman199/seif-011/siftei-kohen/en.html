In what case, etc. And Bach ruled like the great ones that Rashba brought (and Rabbeinu Yerucham part 3 chapter 5 brings them also in his conclusion), and like Rambam — that even if she dealt with that kind we do not attribute to be lenient until she says "I am certain for myself that it was not on me at time of immersion." And I add that such is Rashba's view in his novellae perek 1 of Chullin, and Beit Yosef brings it; and likewise for all variants in Tosafot Chullin (10a) and Tosafot perek Tinoket (Niddah 66b) and in Mordechai and Agudah there — it implies there is no distinction between she dealt with that kind or not (unlike what Derishah wrote seif 16 that Tur distinguishes thus — that he holds like other answers Tosafot wrote, etc. — that even for other answers there is no distinction — see there and understand). And for one variant they wrote there that this Chullin deals where she did not immerse close to chafifah; and likewise Shulchan Shel David siman 13 explains plainly that even if she dealt with that kind she is impure until she says "I am certain for myself it was not on me beforehand"; and if so there is no one who is lenient in this except Tur per Rashba's view; and Rashba himself is concerned to be stringent; and further in Derishah seif 16 he wrote that also Tur who wrote that I attribute it to the kind she dealt with — meaning she does not need immersion at all but they can attribute; and this is specifically when she says "I am certain for myself" — end quote; and per this also Mechaber's words can be interpreted thus, and such is the main practical law.
But if not, etc. To explain what was already explained in seif 8 that if she did not comb her immersion did not count even if nothing interposing was found on her — only because the opening [of the section] holds it. Derishah. And Bach wrote that we deal where she combed her head but not her entire body, for the body there is only ordinary custom as above s.k. 2; and if she immersed b'dieved her immersion counted; but if something interposing was found on her, even b'dieved her immersion did not count even though she dealt with that kind. And likewise Tosafot and Agudah there in the name of Rabbenu Tam — that this Chullin deals in other places on the body that she did not comb them; and the text teaches "something interposing was found on her" — apparently specifically when something interposing was found on her, for if not so her immersion counted. And what Ran z"l wrote implies their way was to rinse with hot water; and so it appears to me — we say if she immersed close to chafifah she need not comb and immerse; and if it were so that they do chafifah only in place of hair, why would we be concerned perhaps she did not examine well other places on the body as we are concerned when it was not close to immersion (for regarding examination)? All other places on the body are certainly one matter — except their way was to rinse their entire body with hot water — end quote; and Beit Yosef brings it. This refers to "something interposing was found on her" — but when nothing interposing was found on her there is to distinguish between the head and other places on the body — for although she did not comb them, since she examined them her immersion counted b'dieved.
