But. The Bach answers what was written in seif 8, that if she did not perform chafifah her immersion does not count even if no interposing substance was found on her: here the case is that she washed her head but not her whole body, for washing the body is only a custom, and if she immersed bedieved the immersion counts. But if an interposing substance was found on her, even bedieved the immersion does not count, although she had handled that species. The Taz answers that there are three distinctions: if she immersed within the same onah in which she performed chafifah, she does not need another immersion. If an onah passed, she needs another immersion unless she handled the relevant species afterward, and this is where in any event there is only one onah between chafifah and immersion. But if much time passed between them, such as if she performed chafifah on Friday and immersed on Motzaei Shabbat, that chafifah is not called before immersion at all, but far from it, and handling the species afterward does not help. Therefore he wrote here that she did not perform chafifah before immersion, meaning it was very far from it.
