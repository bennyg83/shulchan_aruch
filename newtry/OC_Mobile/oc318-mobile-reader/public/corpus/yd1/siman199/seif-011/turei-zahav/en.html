However, if she did not wash and comb before immersion, etc. So wrote the Tur. Many ask that even if no intervening substance at all was found, her immersion is invalid when she did not first wash and comb, as stated in seif 8. It appears to me that there are three distinctions. If she immersed within the same onah in which she washed and combed, she does not require another immersion. If one onah intervened, she requires another immersion unless she engaged afterward in something that could have produced the substance. This means that in any event no more than one onah intervened between the washing and combing and immersion. If, however, a much longer interval intervened, such as where she washed and combed on erev Shabbos and immersed on Motzei Shabbos, that washing and combing is not considered to have been performed before immersion at all, for it was very distant from it. In such a case, even if she later engaged in something that could have produced the substance, the earlier washing and combing does not help. Therefore, the Tur writes here, “she did not wash and comb before immersion,” meaning that she did so only a long time beforehand, as explained.
