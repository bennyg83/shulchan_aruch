In what case, etc. On Tosafot there s.v. im samuch, etc., and that which he said there "when not," etc., and further Or Zarua, etc.; and difference between this answer and first answer — in this answer "she dealt with it" we hang more, even if she did not immerse close to chafifah, specifically she did not comb at all but only examined ordinarily, which is Torah law; and Rashba and Tur adopt this answer. Nevertheless their words require study — for without combing at all, without this, she needs another immersion as above 8; and therefore Tosafot wrote for taharot and Sha"s. And Rambam wrote the baraita simply and per his method there is no distinction except in chafifah, and he does not need Tosafot's answer; and Sha"s in seif 10 wrote words of Rambam, and here he wrote method of poskim.
