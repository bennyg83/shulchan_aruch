A crop whose roof was punctured with a complete opening in any amount — treifah. And what is its roof? That which stretches with the esophagus when the bird extends its neck. But the rest of the crop if punctured is permitted. {Rama: If the crop was removed — treifah (Beit Yosef in name of most poskim), even if enough remained that food can pass from the esophagus to the crop (as implies Ran on siman 51); puncture of the flesh between the crop and gizzard — treifah (end of chapter 40 and Maharil).}
