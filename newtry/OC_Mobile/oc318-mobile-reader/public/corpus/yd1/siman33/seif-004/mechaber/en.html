The esophagus has two membranes: if one of them was punctured — kosher; if both were punctured — forbidden, even if a membrane grew over it and sealed; and even if they were punctured not opposite one another — forbidden, if both were punctured from one wind (interpretation: that they happen to face one another) (Beit Yosef in name of Ritva). {Rama: All this applies only when it is clear the puncture came from disease; but if there is concern it was punctured by a thorn — even if only the inner was punctured — treifah, for we are concerned perhaps the outer was also punctured; and the esophagus has no examination from outside (Beit Yosef in name of Tosafot, Rosh, Ran, and Or Zarua).}
