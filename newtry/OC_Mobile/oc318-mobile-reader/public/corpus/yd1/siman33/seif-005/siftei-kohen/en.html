**And all the more if it has two esophagi or two tracheae, etc.** The reason is explicit in Rosh's responsum: anything extra is as if removed; and it is as if two esophagi or tracheae were removed; as Taz wrote end of siman 41: if two livers found — treifah — from that reason — see there:
**And if they were reversed, etc.** Maharshal wrote: that which Rosh forbids when both red or both white means entirely red or entirely white; but partially we do not care; and if reversed in majority — requires study — end of his words. And that Rosh wrote "but here, since entirely flawed, it does not stand through the second" certainly implies only entirely; but not partially; nevertheless majority is as entirety in all Torah. Bach:
