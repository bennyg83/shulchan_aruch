And if reversed, etc. Reason in Tur: one is flawed and the other protects it — not like small puncture where friend protects; but here both entirely flawed — not sustained by second. Ran beginning siman 51 wrote: Rashi's "if reversed — treifah" means inner reddened through admonition and treifah, for we say it is derusah as below — found two drops of blood and treifah; Maharach z"l learned from this: even if only part of inner reddened — treifah, for we say derusah; even if predator not established before us. And he further wrote: even if redness found opposite intestines — treifah from this reason — end. Strange words — to cause loss of Jewish money wherever red point found in any flesh of animal saying derusah; if so, asmachta of blood congealed from blow in siman 57 — how find it; he says not where we saw strike, but from congealed blood we say from blow; and in siman 57 we forbid reddened flesh opposite intestines for derusah where doubt of derusah without seeing lion among animals; but without that, no; and siman 57 seif 14: even if neck torn — do not attribute to derusah if predator not seen between them; kal v'chomer for redness in esophagus. And in Teshuvot HaRishonim house 2 sha'ar 2 Rashi: if one reversed, e.g. both red — kosher, for only one corrupted — end; Rashi himself did not concern for derusah from redness; his words would contradict; rather Ran deals only where without that doubt of derusah we saw lion — relies on what we say below found two drops; there doubt of derusah; Rashba responsum 277: some concern doubt of derusah only with clear evidence — end. Both red. Tur: one flawed, friend protects — not like small puncture; here both entirely flawed — not sustained. Maharshal: entirely red or white; partially we do not care; majority — requires study.
