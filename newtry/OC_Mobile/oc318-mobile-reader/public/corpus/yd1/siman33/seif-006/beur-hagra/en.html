What should one do, etc. There 28b. (Likut) And if animal, etc. Not like Rashi 43b s.v. that one, etc.; another view in Teshuvot HaRishonim, Tur, Beit Yosef; some distinguish, etc.; in short Rashi's system: even for puncture no examination outside; derusah examination after slaughter; remedy even animal; Tur in name Ba'al HaTerumah; Ran in name Rabeinu Ephraim; Teshuvot HaRishonim in name R' Yitzchak; but some say animal no examination at all — main; Tosafot puncture has examination even outside; Teshuvot HaRishonim and Shulchan Aruch agree Rashi; another view puncture known place has examination; unknown cannot examine — end:
