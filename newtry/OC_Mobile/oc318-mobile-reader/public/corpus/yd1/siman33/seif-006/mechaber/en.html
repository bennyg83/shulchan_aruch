The esophagus has no examination from outside regarding derusah, which requires examination, because since it is red the redness of the wound is not discernible on it. What should one do? If it is a bird — examine the trachea from outside [and slaughter it], then turn the esophagus and examine from inside; if it is an animal — it has no remedy. {Rama: And for us, who are not expert in examination — even a bird has no remedy.}
