**But we are concerned for doubt of puncture, etc.** It implies Mechaber's view: even if punctured in unknown place — examination from inside. And Lamed-Dalet to what is written in siman 51: needle in body cavity — treifah, concerned perhaps punctured one of organs puncturable in any amount and they have no examination — there we do not know where punctured and puncture in any amount is not discernible; but here at least we know opposite dirty place it was punctured and can examine opposite dirty place. And Rama's view: even in such case no examination for puncture in any amount; but when thorn or needle inserted in esophagus in known place — kosher also for Rama, as in s.k. 7 — except this is called "not known" since must examine along entire length and width of dirty place:
**To examine the dirty place.** Meaning from inside opposite dirty place; for from outside — explained in seif 7 that regarding puncture too esophagus has no examination from outside. Mechaber follows Beit Yosef who brought Hagahot Ashiri in name of Or Zarua: nowadays we are not expert in examination; any bird that comes before us with neck dirty in blood — I forbid. And he wrote on this: it appears to me this is Beit HaGadol's view; but Rashi's view, as explained in siman 57 (that derusah has examination even nowadays) — nowadays too there is examination — end of his words. And in siman 57 end seif 8 he rules like Rashi; as written here in general in seif 6; therefore he also wrote here in general to examine dirty place. And even though Rashi and Mechaber hold puncture has no examination in esophagus — that is from outside because it is red; but from inside it is obvious they hold examination helps. This is simple; except in derusah some, due to haste, did not examine this well — see there. Shach also follows his view written in siman 57 and here that we are not expert in examination of derusah; therefore he wrote here like Hagahot Ashiri in name of Or Zarua and such is custom — clear:
