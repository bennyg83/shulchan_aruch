A bird that comes before us and its neck is dirty with blood — we are not concerned for doubt of derusah (see above siman 57) to examine the entire cavity; but we are concerned for doubt of puncture to examine the dirty place. {Rama: Some forbid any bird or animal whose neck is dirty with blood, for we are concerned for puncture of the esophagus and we are not expert (Hagahot Ashiri on siman 51 in name of Or Zarua) — and such is the custom.}
