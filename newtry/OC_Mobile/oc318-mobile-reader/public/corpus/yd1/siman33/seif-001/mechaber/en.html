The law if the lower cheek was removed and if the esophagus was struck/punctured. In it are 11 seifim. If the lower cheek was removed — kosher, provided it can live through hamara and haleta. {Rama: Likewise a bird whose beak was removed, if it can live through hamara and haleta — kosher (Orach Chaim general rule 54 and Rokeach).}
