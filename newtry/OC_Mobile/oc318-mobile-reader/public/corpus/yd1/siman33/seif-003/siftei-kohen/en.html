**Or puncture of esophagus-crop area, etc. — neveilah.** It is surprising, for we do not find anywhere that puncture of esophagus-crop area is neveilah; in Talmud and poskim it implies only treifah; and so from Rambam cited in the previous s.k.; and when he listed the eight treifos in chapter 1 he did not list what is neveilah and listed puncture of esophagus-crop area — from which it is not neveilah. Also the Mechaber himself wondered in several places on Rambam who wrote in chapter 3 "if slaughtered in esophagus-crop area — neveilah," for esophagus-crop area if punctured is treifah and not neveilah; even though I wrote in siman 20 s.k. 5 that he was not precise here, for certainly even though puncture of esophagus-crop area is only treifah, nevertheless since one slaughtered in the crop area it is invalidated by shechitah and there is no greater neveilah than this; nevertheless it is clear his view is that puncture of esophagus-crop area is only treifah, and here he wrote it is neveilah. And even though Taz in many places writes treifah where it is neveilah, because neveilah is in any case treifah — but treifah is certainly not neveilah; and how much more when the Mechaber himself in many places was precise about this. Also there are many halachic differences between treifah and neveilah even today — requires study:
**And esophagus, etc.** Thus wrote Rambam chapter 3 of Laws of Shechitah halachah 19: if the spinal column was broken and the majority of flesh with it, or it was torn from its back like a fish, or the majority of the trachea was severed, or the esophagus was punctured in any amount in the place fit for shechitah — behold it is neveilah while alive — thus is his language. And it is implied from his words that puncture of the esophagus is neveilah while alive even before slaughter; and so is implied the view of the Mechaber. However, it requires study: in chapter HaShochet (Chullin daf 32b) the gemara asks on this that Rava answered in the mishnah "these treifos are forbidden" it teaches "and some of them are neveilot" (meaning puncture of esophagus and majority severance of windpipe are neveilah) and "some are treifos" — let it also count one who made it a gistera neveilah; and it answers: when it teaches neveilah it means not that it imparts tumah while alive, for it does not teach imparting tumah while alive — thus puncture of esophagus and majority severance of windpipe are not neveilah while alive, but if slaughtered, even though slaughtered in the place of shechitah, it is invalidated by the shechitah and we call them [neveilah in that sense]. And even though it seems there that Rava's answer does not stand in the conclusion, nevertheless it seems there that this law — the default of the Talmud that puncture of esophagus and majority severance of windpipe are not neveilah while alive — is correct; and so is implied in Rashi there; and so in Semag, who wrote puncture of esophagus is treifah — thus it deals while alive, for it is only treifah alone. Even though Rashba in Teshuvot HaRishonim daf 27b and 3 holds that majority severance of windpipe is not even treifah while alive according to Rava — his words are difficult.
