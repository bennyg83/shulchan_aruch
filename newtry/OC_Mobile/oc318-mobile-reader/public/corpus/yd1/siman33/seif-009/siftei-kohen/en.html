**If there is no drop of blood on it from outside.** What is written here in Shulchan Aruch from Tur is an error; rather in Darkei Moshe so — see there Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 3 Laws of Shechitah. Nevertheless requires study: also in Hagahot Maimoniyot he does not decide, for thus wrote R' Yitzchak: if there is drop of blood from outside — certainly the outer was punctured — Sefer HaTerumah — end of his words. And Rav's view there (in Darkei Moshe) is that it refers to "found," as printed; but difficult: for we do not find so in Sefer HaTerumah; therefore it appears it refers to "stuck," as language of "certainly outer was punctured" implies; otherwise should have said "certainly esophagus was punctured." Examine; and gemara teaches: with drop of blood from outside we are certainly concerned; no doubt at all, even for one who rules we are not concerned when stuck. And so in Sefer HaTerumah seif 14 and in Tosafot (daf 43b) in matter beginning "sat" — see there; requires study:
**But if it is stuck in it, etc.** Semak siman 141 wrote: explanation — inserted somewhat into inner skin of esophagus even if complete outside — end of his words. From his language it appears even if stuck and inner skin not punctured on both sides — called stuck; and so Bach:
**Even if there is no drop of blood.** Meaning: neither inside nor outside; and so proven from what Beit Yosef ruled like Rashba and his supporters. And Lamed-Dalet to beit ha-kosot: where no drop of blood — no concern, as below siman 48; esophagus is different because food and drink pass regularly there and wash through; but beit ha-kosot, where they eat and drink calmly — if it were while alive there would be drop of blood found — Ran; and so Rashba in Teshuvot HaRishonim daf 30a; R' Yerucham; and other poskim:
**Concern perhaps esophagus punctured and healed, etc.** Tur's language: healed — punctured and requires examination according to one who holds esophagus has examination. And one who holds healed — language of health: perhaps punctured and healed and examination does not help according to all — until here. It appears difficult why Mechaber needed reason of healed, since he ruled in seif 7 esophagus has no examination from outside — if so, simple to say we are concerned perhaps punctured even if not healed, for no examination from outside. It appears nevertheless he needed healed: otherwise examination would help when separating two membranes and seeing inner also not punctured through-and-through — then obvious outer not punctured at all; but if concerned perhaps healed — treifah; and so proven in R' Yerucham — see there; examine:
**And some permit if no drop of blood from outside.** Meaning: even if drop of blood inside — drop inside does not raise or lower; see Beit Yosef s.v. what our master wrote. But difficult: Shach ruled if concern only inner punctured by thorn — treifah, concerned perhaps outer also punctured; esophagus has no examination from outside. Can say: there Shach wrote Mechaber's view — concerned perhaps healed; and Shach himself so in s.k. 23; he did not write view of "some permit." However, he only informed that practicers of fattening geese relied on them. Difficult why above he gave reason concerned perhaps punctured and no examination from outside — even if there were examination, concerned perhaps healed, as Mechaber writes here. Can say Shach holds concern of healed is remote; we do not concern except for inner puncture, since can say also outer punctured and esophagus has no examination from outside; therefore even without drop inside, reason of perhaps healed makes treifah per Mechaber, as previous s.k. But if examination from outside existed, we would not concern for healed since not punctured before us; therefore above he gave reason no examination from outside: if only outer punctured by thorn or needle in known place — kosher through examination from inside, as s.k. 7 — we do not concern for healed since inner examination on white inner membrane, puncture in any amount discernible. Examine. Further resolve first difficulty: specifically here when thorn found stuck in esophagus — "some permit" if no drop outside; if outer also punctured it would be evident; therefore not concerned perhaps punctured; but when puncture found and no thorn, only concern punctured by thorn — worse, for if thorn before us it would puncture outward; therefore must distinguish: Shach in end seif 23 and this seif 8 and siman 34 end seif 4 concerned perhaps esophagus punctured from outside to inside and examination from inside does not help; here examination helps even from outside despite rule no examination from outside because red — since thorn found stuck, better: if punctured would be evident outside; by law should be permitted without examination, not concerned perhaps punctured since thorn stuck before us; to be stringent requires examination; similar to above siman 24: sign of slaughter dangling, signs not grasped — kosher through examination; impossible dangling become slaughtered — so in several places examination is only stringency — see siman 48 s.k. 32 and 33:
**If not punctured on both sides or no drop of blood, etc.** Simple: printing error; must read: if not punctured on both sides and no drop outside; and so explicitly Darkei Moshe; and so Bach:
**And it would be better, etc.** Shach speaks of practicers who fatten geese and are lenient when thorn sits in esophagus; much to examine; do not be lenient, for main view of Mechaber and supporters: even without drop, even inside — treifah when thorn stuck. If so, better for them not to examine and rely on majority when found stuck, if not punctured both sides and no drop outside — greater leniency; and here certainly d'oraisa prohibition, for majority of poskim hold like Mechaber: even without drop inside or outside — treifah; and so Maharshal siman 51 ot 5. But Or Zarua: here, since thorn stuck before us — called known place; but above deals with concern punctured in unknown place, as written there. And even R' Yehuda wrote: thorn found stuck not called known place — perhaps thorn punctured above and food/drink brought it down, and puncture not where thorn stands — Beit Yosef; but Shach does not concern for this; this is called known place. Shach: both not kosher; what he brought "some permit" only to inform practicers relied on them; his view: since lung adhesions more common — do not rely on majority; examination required; as below siman 39 regarding lung; and where thorn found stuck in esophagus — law agreed per Mechaber, as s.k. 21; and so explicitly Darkei Moshe as explained; and Taz only brought Mechaber's words; Bach cried like a herald against custom of fattening geese — see there; and responsum Maharam of Lublin siman 21: community enacted to examine esophagi; later heads thrown away without examining esophagi — kosher b'dieved; see there at length:
**Along its length or width and it is not stuck in it — kosher.** So Tur; and so Hagahot Or Zarua; and so Orach Chaim general rule 21 halachah 24. Reason in gemara: animals' way is to eat thorns; therefore as long as not stuck in it we do not establish presumption of flaw. And in several places chapter 3 Laws of Shechitah halachah 21 he wrote: specifically when thorn found along length — eating direction; but along width — we are concerned. Must say: there it deals when found in general and we do not know if it was stuck or not — we say presumably it was stuck. And so R' Yerucham netiv 15 ot 5 ch. 3; and Orach Chaim there:
