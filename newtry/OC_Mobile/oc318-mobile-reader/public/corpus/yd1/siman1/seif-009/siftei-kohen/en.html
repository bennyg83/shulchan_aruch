**His shechitah is valid.** Sefer HaZikaron wrote that a blind person who never saw light from birth—even b'dieved it is forbidden to eat from his shechitah; Beit Yosef and Darchei Moshe bring this. On Sefer HaZikaron and other poskim they did not distinguish in this.
**Unless others see him.** In Sheltei Gibborim he wrote in the name of Riaz that even if others see him he may not slaughter l'chatchila—see there.
