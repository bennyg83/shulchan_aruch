Ran according to the Rif, and Tur in the name of Rambam there chapter 4, and Rashba there in Terumat HaDeshen. (°) Maharash said he received kabbalah in his hand from his brother R' Yona z"l that every slaughterer, when he begins to slaughter, should review laws of shechitot and bedikot once each day, and so for thirty days; afterward once each month, and so for a full year; afterward once each season, and so all his days; if he did not do so his shechitah is invalid—end quote. Maharil brought it; Maharshal also in his shechitot u'bedikot.
