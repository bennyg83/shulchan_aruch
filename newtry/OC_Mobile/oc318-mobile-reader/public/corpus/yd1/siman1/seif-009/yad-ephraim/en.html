**Everything according to whether he is a person.** Ba'er Heitev; wrote in Degel Machaneh Efrayim: even for Acharonim—only when not properly at all forbidden from Torah; but if according to words of contradictor it was not proper from stringency, e.g. less than measure or delay in minority—the slaughterer is believed; also room to distinguish one who says "I slaughtered improperly"—whether they contradict him in what he says he slaughtered at such hour and witness says he did not slaughter this animal for it did not move, etc.—slaughterer is believed, for regarding body of slaughter he is believed like two—see there.
He wrote in Tiferet Tzvi siman 72: woman who said slaughterer slaughtered bird for her and slaughterer contradicts her—if the bird is the woman's, slaughterer is not believed to contradict her; if bird of another—even though woman came first and said it was slaughtered kosher—nevertheless slaughterer has migo he could say he did not slaughter; majority would have been believed, since what he said he gave her in presumption of kosher is only on his word. In such case he wrote in Even HaEzer per Ramban: testimony from mouth of witness and first contradicts first is believed. From this a certain sage learned to declare pure for me—see there; therefore also now believed; one may be stringent and forbid—see there; and straightforward that even if bird is woman's—forbidden to one who believes in his heart the slaughterer more than the woman, as explained siman 127.
