**(Likkut) Deaf etc.** It counts here five who are forbidden regarding terumah in chapter 1 of Terumot; and there it counts six: deaf-mute who speaks and does not hear, etc.—five, not etc.; rather there is no law of ba'al keri nowadays and drunk here slaughtering l'chatchila, for there it is because he may not separate terumah from the best.
