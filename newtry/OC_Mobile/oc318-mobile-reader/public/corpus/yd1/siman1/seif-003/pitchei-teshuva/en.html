**And skilled with his hand.** See responsum Nodah B'Yehudah second edition part 14 siman 1, who wrote regarding a slaughterer whose hands tremble—yet he senses a fine defect and also says it is clear to him he does not fail in anything, for the trembling does not harm him—certainly one should remove that slaughterer l'chatchila; but we do not forbid his shechitah retroactively, for he is no worse than one whose way is to faint in seif 3: if he says “it is clear to me” it is kosher—and Shach there is lenient even if silent; only Taz forbids if silent; but one who says “it is clear to me,” all agree kosher—see there.
