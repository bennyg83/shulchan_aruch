**I am certain I slaughtered well.** (♯) Meaning: after they taught him he says so; the reason is that anything not dependent on the person is not his knowledge.
**One whom they know, etc.** (♯) Proof: the Mishnah uses deyavad language regarding deaf-mute, fool, minor—“others see”—from which a grown person is permitted even l'chatchila when others see. Some are stringent: the Mishnah itself needed to teach that even for deaf-mute/fool/minor there is b'dieved permission when others see; and the same for a grown person—only b'dieved; the reason is a gezeirah.
