If they know of someone that he does not know the laws of shechitah—even if he slaughtered before us four or five times with proper, fitting shechitah—and afterward he slaughtered alone between himself and himself, his shechitah is invalid; even if they asked him, “You did such-and-such,” and from his answer it appeared that he slaughtered properly, one does not rely on him; and even if he says, “I am certain I slaughtered well.” {Rama: One whose nature is to faint, and we know he lacks presumption of reliability, and he slaughtered and says, “I am certain I did not faint”—he is believed, since he knows the laws of shechitah (Beit Yosef per the Rambam).} If they know of someone that he does not know the laws of shechitah, they may give him to slaughter if another stands over him, provided he watches him from the beginning of shechitah until its end. {Rama: Some are stringent and forbid giving him l'chatchila to slaughter since he is not expert in the laws of shechitah (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahos Ashrei in the name of Or Zarua and Mordechai), and such is the practice.}
