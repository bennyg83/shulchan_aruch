(3) **There.** "All may slaughter l'chatchila." And you may ask: how did the Holy One blessed be He permit us to cause pain to living creatures to slaughter and eat them—behold it is written "and His mercies are on all His works"? Behold this question was investigated by Ramban, and he answered: this animal that is slaughtered for its benefit was slaughtered—for it ascended from the rank of animal body to the rank of human body. Likewise in Yalkut Reuveni parashat Shemini, siman 138 in chapter "This is the law of animal and bird" in the name of Sha'arei Orah 59a; Beit David Yafeh Lalev part 3 or 1—see there.
