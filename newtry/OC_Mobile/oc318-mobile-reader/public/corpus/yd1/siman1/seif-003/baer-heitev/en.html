**Invalid.** Since he does not know laws of shechitah, perhaps sometimes he delayed and pressed and does not notice; since he is not expert in hilchos shechitah he does not know if treifot came to his hand—Rosh.
**Proper.** Meaning: like another who learned and from his answer it appears he slaughtered properly—even so do not rely on him since at the time of shechitah he did not know unintentionally.
**Shechitah.** Shach explained: if he delayed or pressed he would not feed it to us. Beit Yosef: accordingly if silent as well.
**Its end.** Shach explained: even if he saw he slaughtered one sign well, we do not say since he slaughtered this sign well the second was also slaughtered well.
**And there is.** A slaughterer who is lenient in one law mentioned by poskim to be stringent as it appears to his mind—whether permitted to let him slaughter when others are al gavav—see Beit Yaakov simanim 97 and 121.
**Custom.** Shach wrote: however from the fact he does not know whether he knows hilchos shechitah or not, it appears permitted to give him l'chatchila when others stand al gavav—so too Maharshal.
