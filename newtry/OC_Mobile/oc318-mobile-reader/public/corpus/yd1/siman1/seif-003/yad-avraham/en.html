Know that from Tosafot Avodah Zarah it is proven that even an apostate lehachis is ben brit; and likewise in Ahavat Chesed Even HaEzer siman 109, not like Magen Avraham there; and likewise from the sugya in chapter 2 of Kiddushin; and likewise in responsa Ginat Veradim siman 23; and from this is proof for what Taz wrote at the beginning of siman 2 that he is bar zevicha since he is commanded even though he does not perform. What Magen Avraham proved from what Tosafot wrote in Sanhedrin—one may say there he speaks in the language of ben brit. See Beit Shmuel siman 123 who wrote apostate applies to Torah of gittin; and in siman 141 s.k. 47—not so.
