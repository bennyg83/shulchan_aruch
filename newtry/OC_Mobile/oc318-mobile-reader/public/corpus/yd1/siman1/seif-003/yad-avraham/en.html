(In Shach s.k. 27.) **That a minor is commanded regarding shechitah**, etc.—meaning he is called bar zevicha, and it is forbidden to feed him by hand, and we are commanded not to give him nevelot; as Peri Megadim wrote and likewise in responsa of Maharil—and this is if we say forbidden to feed him by hand from Torah; because of derabbanan he is not called bar zevicha, just as he is not called bar keshira regarding tefillin because chinuch is derabbanan. One may bring proof from what Tosafot wrote in Avodah Zarah 28—that he is called bar brit because we are commanded to circumcise him; otherwise it should be difficult: perhaps a minor is among them, as Tosafot wrote regarding apostate for orlah. And likewise one may say deaf-mute and shotah are ben brit—for otherwise let us say behold there is among them. What Peri Megadim wrote here that they are warned and sons of mitzvot is against the sugya at the beginning of Mi sheHechshich; and Rambam chapter 9 of testimony and beginning of Yevamot that Beit Din is not commanded to separate them; and likewise Magen Avraham siman 489—and it implies there they are worse than minors with da'at. Know that from Tosafot Avodah Zarah it is proven that even an apostate lehachis is ben brit; and likewise in Ahavat Chesed Even HaEzer siman 109, not like Magen Avraham there; and likewise from the sugya in chapter 2 of Kiddushin; and likewise in responsa Ginat Veradim siman 23; and from this is proof for what Taz wrote at the beginning of siman 2 that he is bar zevicha since he is commanded even though he does not perform. What Magen Avraham proved from what Tosafot wrote in Sanhedrin—one may say there he speaks in the language of ben brit. See Beit Shmuel siman 123 who wrote apostate applies to Torah of gittin; and in siman 141 s.k. 47—not so.
