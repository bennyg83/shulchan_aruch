**(Seif 14) (Likkut) Slaughterer etc.** There (Ketubot) 23b “she, since there are witnesses,” etc.; but because of idolatry he turned—no. (end quote)
**One witness in contradiction etc.** As written in the first chapter of Yevamot and chapter 3 of Kiddushin (65b) and first chapter of Keritot.
**And the witness himself etc.** For even if he certainly slaughtered not properly, we only say they remove him—but his shechitah is not invalidated, as written in siman 2 seif 2 in the gloss—and it is from the words of Mahariik here; and even though Mahariik did not write so, rather “perhaps he repented,” his words do not appear correct (Peri Chadash).
