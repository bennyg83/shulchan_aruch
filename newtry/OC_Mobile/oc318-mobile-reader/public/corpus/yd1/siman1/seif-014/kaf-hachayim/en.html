(14) **And even in a place where women are accustomed to slaughter**—this is only for themselves, or even for others by chance, for presumably they can be careful; but they should not be appointed to slaughter for the public, for they are lazy and will make much nevelah; and a butcher who makes nevelah is removed, as in Choshen Mishpat siman 306; and women are prone to this. Peri Chadash or 1; Semak there; Zivchei Tzedek or 5.
