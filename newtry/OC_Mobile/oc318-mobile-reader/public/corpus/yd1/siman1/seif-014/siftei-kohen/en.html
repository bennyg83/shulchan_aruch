**One witness in denial is as nothing.** Meaning: that shechitah itself is permitted to other people, but not to the witness—for he made himself bound by issur; but from here onward his shechitah is permitted even to the witness, for one cannot say he made himself bound by issur from here onward. Specifically regarding a nazir: if he became a nazir, as the witness says, the wine has no remedy—we say (below siman 127) he made himself bound by issur; but here one cannot say so, for even if it were as the witness says, nevertheless he is not absolutely disqualified forever for this; at minimum if he wears black and does the repentance required of him he returns to reliability—and we find he did not make himself permanently bound by issur through his testimony, since the matter depends on the slaughterer’s intent whether for harm or for good from here onward—end of Maharik’s words; and this is the Mechaber’s view. But in responsa Ben Lev and Maharshal, first chapter of Chullin siman 9, and Bach ruled that that shechitah itself is forbidden to everyone. See below siman 127 seif 13, explained at length correctly; and below siman 2 seif 11.
