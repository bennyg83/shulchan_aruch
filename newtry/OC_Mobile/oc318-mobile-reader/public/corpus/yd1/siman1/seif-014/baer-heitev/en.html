**In contradiction.** Meaning: that shechitah itself is permitted for other people, but not for the witness who made himself a piece of prohibition. But from here onward his shechitah is permitted even for the witness—Shach.
**A person.** But Shach and Taz wrote in the name of Maharshal and in the name of responsum of Ben Lev: we do not say a single witness in contradiction is nothing except in testimony of a woman; but in other prohibitions it is one against one in contradiction; an animal while alive stands in presumption of prohibition—therefore this animal is forbidden to all Israel—so Bach, Ben Lev, and Maharshal.
