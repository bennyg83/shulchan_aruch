(10) **There, in the gloss. And likewise the custom**, etc. Likewise Agur; Beit Yosef challenged him that "we have not seen" is not proof. In my humble opinion it appears Agur's view is as Maharik wrote in root 172: regarding custom and the like, "we have not seen" is proof—as the rabbi wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 37. Shach s.k. 1. And in Choshen Mishpat siman 37 Shach expanded there to resolve his words—for here they challenged him—and see what he wrote there in Urim VeTumim that the correct view is as Beit Yosef wrote—see there. Kereti or 4; likewise in responsa Makom Shmuel there that the correct view is as Beit Yosef wrote—see there. And so is the practice: when poskim write "the explanation is: we have not heard and we have not seen"—this is regarding their country and neighboring countries they heard from, but not the whole world; however sometimes possible regarding the whole world, per the appearance from that posek's words.
