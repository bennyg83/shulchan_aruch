**And a shoteh.** See responsum Chavat Shaul siman 7 regarding an expert slaughterer God-fearing who periodically is collapsed—meaning in illness of falling; doctors judged he had weakness of heart that he tastes nothing in the morning whether fit for shechitah or not, as Semag wrote seif 29: all shotim mentioned in Choshen Mishpat siman 35 are invalid also for shechitah, and collapsed are included—for even at the time of his dream there is concern every moment perhaps he is near the hour the illness will collapse him, which is the time of end of his sanity and beginning of his shoteh state, as explained in Rambam and Shulchan Aruch Choshen Mishpat siman 235 and in Semag there s.k. 52. He expanded on this and concluded that certainly for shechitah one needs careful precision more than for testimony, and especially in knife examination; but since the custom there in any case is that two slaughterers go to the slaughterhouse, and even for birds he slaughters alone—it is not common at all that they eat from a bird slaughtered in less than half an hour of rinsing and salting, etc.; and if nevertheless nothing happened to him, we know the time of shechitah was not near his illness of shoteh state, and he is not suspected to hide his illness to feed nevelah God forbid. And in particular if they place one supervisor in his house—for this there is no hospital at all, and it would suffice even if he had no regular cycle at all—all the more since doctors attribute the illness to empty heart weakness—then most of his slaughter will be after he tastes something; therefore the teacher ruled well not to remove him—see there; and it appears that l'chatchila certainly one should not appoint such a slaughterer [see Choshen Mishpat siman 35 seif 9; and there he further expanded whether fit to be shatz].
