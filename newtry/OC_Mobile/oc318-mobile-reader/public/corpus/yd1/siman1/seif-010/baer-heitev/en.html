**To bless.** Taz wrote: it does not help that another blesses and he turns his face elsewhere, since the naked one must intend to hear the berachah and he is forbidden in this l'chatchila. Also one not girded with a belt whose heart sees his nakedness—do not slaughter l'chatchila, for in such a case he may not bless. Also forbidden to slaughter with uncovered head—for in such a case he also may not bless.
