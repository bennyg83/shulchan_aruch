(1) [Seif 1] **All may slaughter l'chatchila.** For we taught: Rabbi says "and you shall slaughter as I commanded you"—teaching Moses was commanded regarding the esophagus and windpipe, and majority of one in a bird and majority of two in an animal (Chullin 28a). Rashi explained "as I commanded you"—learn the mitzvah of shechitah was explained to him orally, for where was he commanded in writing—end quote. Tosafot there wrote: Rabbi expounded the spelling of "as" (ke'asher)—alef one in bird, shin two in animal, reish majority of one like him—majority of one in bird and majority of two in animal. Rabbeinu Tam: retroactively of "as" (ka'asher)—majority of one like him—end quote; and likewise Ran. Tur brought this baraita; Bach wrote to teach that bird shechitah too is min haTorah, and every safek in shechitah is invalid even in bird—not like R' Eliezer ben Yaakov who said bird has no shechitah min haTorah, only midivrei sofrim; per his view one could be lenient where there is safek in bird shechitah, for the halachah is not so, rather like this baraita Rabbi taught—end quote. Likewise Rambam chapter 1 of Hilchot Shechitah law 1 that bird shechitah is min haTorah—see there. Likewise Semag beginning of Hilchot Shechitah; Sefer HaChinuch parashat Re'eh mitzvah 451; Rashba in Torat HaBayit HaKatzar at its beginning; Beit Yosef siman 13; Levush beginning of this siman and beginning of siman 13. Kaf HaChayim part 1 or 1; Minchat Yosef siman 1 or 1; Chochmat Adam kelal 1 or 1; Zivchei Shem or 1, Zivchei Tzedek or 1; Aruch HaShulchan or 1. Likewise Peri Megadim in introduction to Hilchot Shechitah root 1 that bird shechitah is min haTorah, and we follow its safek to be stringent—see there. Likewise the custom: do not distinguish in laws of shechitah between animal and bird, and follow their safek to be stringent. See further below siman 13 or 1.
