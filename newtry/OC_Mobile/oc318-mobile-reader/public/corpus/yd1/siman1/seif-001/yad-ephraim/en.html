**Even** women. See Eshel Avraham in the name of Semak. See She'elot Ya'avetz siman 157. A woman who learned laws of shechitah is kosher to slaughter even for herself; and likewise if he examined the knife and gave it to her and stood al gavav she may slaughter; but they do not appoint her as slaughterer for the public at all—see there.
