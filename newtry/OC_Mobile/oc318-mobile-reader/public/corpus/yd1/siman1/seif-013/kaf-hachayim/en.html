(13) **And if** it happened she slaughtered between herself and herself in a place where they slaughter without kabbalah—she needs examination whether she is expert and says "it is clear to me I did not faint"—then her slaughter is kosher. But if she is not before us, her slaughter is invalid, since there is a defect before us that she deviated from the custom—we do not rely on "most found at slaughter." Semak or 13; Zivchei Tzedek or 2.
