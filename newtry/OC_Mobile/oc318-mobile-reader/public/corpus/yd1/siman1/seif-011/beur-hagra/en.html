**If they placed etc.** Since the congregation forbade him alone, it is like eating without shechitah, and like an apostate for that matter.
**(Likkut) If etc.** For they made him a piece of prohibition for themselves; see siman 62 and Shach (end quote).
**And if the cherem was annulled etc.** And one need not declare them kosher, as written in Avodah Zarah 39a; and likewise a courtyard where they sell, etc.—there.
