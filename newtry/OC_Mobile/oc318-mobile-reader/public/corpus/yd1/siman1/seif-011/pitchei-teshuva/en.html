**Who became bar mitzvah.** See Magen Avraham 39 s.k. 1: in a matter where a minor is invalid d'oraita we require that he be actually an adult—meaning he brought two hairs after he is thirteen years old; see Rama there siman 55 seif 5 that for rabbinic matters we rely on presumption he brought two hairs—see there. From this we learn to judge this case: even after thirteen years and one day forbidden to slaughter for eating, since he is invalid d'oraita—or from Levush's reason he is not bar zevicha—or from Shach's reason he is not bar eidut if another is not standing al gavav until known he brought two hairs or he is thirty-five years and one day (and what Magen Avraham wrote until he is eighteen years old—Acharonim already wondered on this), or his beard became full as explained in Choshen Mishpat 35 and Magen Avraham siman 199 s.k. 7; however to feed dogs, or with others al gavav, and he is not expert and does not know how to train his hands—from when he is thirteen years and one day permitted to slaughter.
