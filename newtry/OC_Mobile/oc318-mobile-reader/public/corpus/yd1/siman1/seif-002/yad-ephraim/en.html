**Unless he took kabbalah.** See Ba'er Heitev; see Chavat Ya'ir siman 178—in a village where the slaughterer fell ill near Yom Tov and one person learned knows laws of shechitah and examination, only he never slaughtered or examined—he may not slaughter except for the joy of Yom Tov: permit to slaughter birds with the sick slaughterer, even though the joy of the festival does not come out in birds—nevertheless the custom of important people who sustain themselves with birds. (In my humble opinion it appears this is not so except for complete joy; but joy of Yom Tov certainly exists, as in Beitzah regarding stocking dovecotes—they come to diminish joy of Yom Tov; see Tosafot Ketubot 5 who wrote that in four chapters they were accustomed to increase in animals and not in birds—see there.) Likewise permitted to slaughter an animal, only if some safek arises that requires removal, and the sick one cannot attend to the whole treifah; see Lechem HaPanim who copied this. What he wrote regarding kaparah—see Chavat Ya'ir who wrote he should accept some fast for kaparah and some sum for charity for publicity that it was not permitted except in an hour of pressure. See there Chavat Ya'ir brought in Lechem HaPanim: regarding one who must give meat to idolaters—one may perform nehirah through an idolater, for there is no concern of mistake here, unlike if he slaughtered without kabbalah—see there.
