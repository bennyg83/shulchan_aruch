**He need not know etc.** Taz. **(Likkut) He need not know etc.** From what is written in chapter 1 “and these are the laws of shechitah,” etc.—it implies specifically those commonly found at all times; and Hagahot Ashri there s.v. ki she'ein, etc. (end quote) **(Likkut) He need not etc.** From that it says there 9a “and these are the laws of shechitah,” and Hagahot Ashri there s.v. ki, etc., and it appears, etc.—meaning every regular matter; and this is what the gloss says “and they examine him,” etc.—meaning he must know all these; therefore they also examine him, etc.; and Hagahot Ashri there s.v. veR' Elazar memitz, etc. (end quote)
**And they examine him etc.** There 9a: “every butcher,” etc., “and these,” etc.; and as explained above in seif 1 that l'chatchila he must be examined.
**And the law of examining the knife.** See Tosafot there s.v. kulhu; and it may be said it deals with etc.—and see what I wrote below.
**And the law etc.** As explained above—the butcher must examine, etc.; and see Rashi there s.v. kulhu tennanhu, etc.
**And if he slaughtered etc. “for all,” etc.** As written in seif 1 in Shulchan Aruch: wherever possible we do not rely on “most found,” etc.
