**That he knows about him.** Beer Hetev; and what he wrote “a father may rely on his son,” etc.—likewise wrote Azriel in responsum Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 3; and greater than this: even if they tested him and found he does not know hilchos shechitah—even though Rama wrote that if he did not take kabbalah we forbid all utensils retroactively—in this case where his father testifies he was expert, we say “now he became defective, learned, and forgot,” and utensils are permitted retroactively—see there. And see in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 44 that l'chatchila one should take kabbalah since he is not his relative—see there; and see further in responsum at end of Sefer Beit Lechem Yehudah siman 1 what he wrote on this.
