**Permitted.** Reason in Taz: even great sages are in doubt on several matters in laws of shechitah; Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote at least he must know that six hesitations combine—that is a regular matter. If he entered a slaughterhouse and saw two slaughtering two animals and said one delayed and he does not know which, and they are silent—Shevut Yaakov ruled question 43: if the witness says “you know there is doubt in the shechitah” and they are silent, render treifah; if he did not say “you know,” even if silent it is nothing. Beit Yaakov siman 4: if he says on permitted “forbidden,” even if he says on forbidden “forbidden,” and does not say on forbidden “permitted”—remove that slaughterer; see Sefer Beit Din siman 6.
