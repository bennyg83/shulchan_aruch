He need not know every subtle distinction in the law; rather, if when asked about a matter he says, “On this I was uncertain and asked,” we consider that he knows well—until he says of something forbidden that it is permitted. {Rama: And they test him on the laws of shechitah as will be explained below at the beginning of siman 23, and on the law of inspecting the knife (°), and on the law of inspecting the simanim after shechitah as will be explained below in siman 25 (Hagahos Ashrei and Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 4 Hilchos Shechitah). And if he slaughtered and is not present before us to test him, nevertheless they check the simanim whether the majority were severed—for whatever can be checked, we check (Ran in the name of Baal Halakhot; common practice per Rosh, see there siman 25 seif 13). All this is in places where they are not accustomed to take kabbalah; but in places where they are accustomed to take kabbalah, such as Ashkenaz and those who follow them, they are not accustomed to check after him at all, as was explained (it is evident).}
