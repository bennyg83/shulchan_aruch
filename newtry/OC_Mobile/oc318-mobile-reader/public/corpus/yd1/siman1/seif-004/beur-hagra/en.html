**Or that were stolen.** Tosafot there, and in Bava Metzia 24b in the name of Tosefta.
**In a place etc.** Gemara Bava Metzia there.
**And also “most,” etc.** Rosh; and it refers to “were stolen,” as written in Avodah Zarah 70a; and see Bava Batra 24a that there it is different—“the majority,” etc.; and here majority Israel does not raise or lower.
**Whether he found them in the market.** Bava Metzia there; and see Tosafot there and in Chullin there. **(Likkut) Whether he found them in the market.** And likewise in Tosefta: his chicken was stolen and he found it slaughtered in the market—for R' Yehudah like found in a refuse heap; for R' Chanina like found in a house; and likewise that baraita above “behold he found a slaughtered chicken in the market.” Halachah is like R' Chanina, since we challenged from it above, and also it is a practical ruling—Terumat HaDeshen (end quote).
**Whether etc.** Like R' Chanina in Bava Metzia there—the law is like him, not like Rambam.
