**"Now he became defective."** See Ba'er Heitev; see Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 91: when found that he does not know laws of shechitah and took kabbalah—one may lighten per Peri Megadim; if he did not take—forbid everything retroactively, and even in safek forbid; and so concludes responsa Beit Yaakov siman 14, etc. Regarding one who pokes who is not expert in poking—it appears to forbid everything retroactively, for they are stringent in poking—see siman 65; see in Beli that his view is there is no distinction between shechitah and poking; see Beit Shmuel and see there siman 52 who wrote regarding a slaughterer who came to anger and slaughtered in anger, then grasped the simanim and went on his way; the second slaughterer showed him the esophagus was not slaughtered; when the first returned and they asked him why he made the animal nevelah, he answered he did not know—from this he believed it was slaughtered properly, and when he grasped the simanim he thought it was the windpipe of the neck and not the esophagus—he ruled a simple matter to dismiss him, since there is no excuse for his words. If he would give an excuse that he knew it was nevelah but did not wish to attribute the deficiency to himself, and his intent was to declare the animal treif through examination of the lung as slaughterers' way—it is not fit to dismiss him. But one who gives no excuse at all—this is complete negligence. And one may say: since he came to anger he came to mistake—this too is somewhat negligence, for "all who are angry," etc.; and in particular as is heard he is not established in kashrut per the versions and bad language—one should be concerned; if enmity is before us until he returns in complete repentance; also he must pay what he made nevelah since they give him wages as explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 66 (see below siman 2 seif 12 in gloss printed there in the name of Even HaEzer who brought this responsa briefly, and there is a printing error: it wrote "and when he did not answer" and it should read "and when he came he answered"). (And think regarding what he wrote: if he gives an excuse that he knew but wished to declare treif through examination—if so he is suspected of money, since by law he must pay; and even though for this his slaughter is not invalid as written below seif 4 regarding stolen kids—nevertheless there is no excuse here, for he makes himself wicked with this claim. And it is possible he holds amirah lebarzel in this—that one may say he truly intended to pay, only secretly or to evade until he has money; and in particular since slaughterers were accustomed to do so from the outset with this intent they accepted upon themselves not to need to shame himself that they establish him unable to train his hands to slaughter properly—and still think per what we hold below siman 27: when it became nevelah in slaughter, forbidden to sell lung and intestines—and if so when the slaughterer conceals from the owner that it became nevelah in slaughter and tells him it became treifah, he will sell intestines to idolaters and transgress lifnei iver—and if so behold the slaughterer causes Israel to transgress lifnei iver, and there is no excuse? And they place this matter on the custom slaughterers do thus, as Shevut Yaakov wrote that such is slaughterers' way. One should wonder why sages did not protest—for hereby he causes Israel lifnei iver. It appears therefore they turned a blind eye—for one must be concerned lest because he will be obligated to pay or from embarrassment he will not inform at all that it became nevelah in slaughter; therefore they left this stumbling block under their hands—for better a small issur by them than a great one. Think.)
