(s.k. 9) **Proof for this from what we taught**, etc. He was not precise for several reasons: one, the Mishnah itself was needed—for even deaf-mute and minor are kosher b'dieved; and likewise adult—only b'dieved permitted. Also, even if you say the precision comes to teach us thus, one may infer: minor—even though he knows laws of shechitah and knows how to train his hands—his slaughter is invalid, as below seif 5; behold adult who knows laws of shechitah and knows how to train his hands slaughters l'chatchila. Also, for all resolutions of the Gemara it does not refer only to deaf-mute, shotah, and minor alone—for we infer from it "adult," etc.—rather it also refers to another matter, as we say "and all who slaughtered"—if you say only regarding deaf-mute, shotah, and minor it refers, it should teach "and if they slaughtered"; rather also another matter, such as tamei in kodshim, and the like. If so, one must say b'dieved because of tamei in kodshim who became impure in chullin slaughters l'chatchila [and from adult who does not know—Shach does not deal with the Mishnah; further] for the resolution we say "all experts," etc.—proves explicitly the opposite: regarding adult who does not know laws of shechitah—b'dieved only, and l'chatchila not—as written in my book; and the other resolution too does not disagree on this.
