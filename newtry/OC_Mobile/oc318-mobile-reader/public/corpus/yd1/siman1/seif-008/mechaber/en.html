One who is drunk to the degree of Lot’s drunkenness has the law of a fool; if he has not reached Lot’s drunkenness, he slaughters l'chatchila. {Rama: Some say a drunk who is accustomed to become violent may not slaughter (Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of Semak chapter 3 Hilchos Shechitah; Behag; Kol Bo in the name of Sefer haTzitzis; Sefer Yereim siman 137).}
