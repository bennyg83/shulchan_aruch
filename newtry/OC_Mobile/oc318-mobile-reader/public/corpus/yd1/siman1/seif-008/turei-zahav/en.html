**Accustomed to come to violent trampling.** Because his limbs are heavy on him from much wine he drank; so one should practice with anyone whose wine is strong on him even if he did not reach Lot’s drunkenness. And although we say in chapter HaDar that a drunk is like a competent person for all his matters—that is for matters dependent on a person’s mind, not here which depends on heaviness of limbs—for certainly he is not competent.
