**And some say, etc.** Meaning: good advice for a drunk not to slaughter l'chatchila because he is accustomed to come to violent trampling; but certainly by law he is like a competent person for all matters, as Tur wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 235. In Levush this Rama gloss was omitted—I do not know why.
**His law is like a fool.** L'chatchila he may not slaughter even when others stand over him; b'dieved his shechitah is valid when others stand over him—specifically.
