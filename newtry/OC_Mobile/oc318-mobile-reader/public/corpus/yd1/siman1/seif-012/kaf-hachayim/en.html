(12) **There, in the gloss. And likewise the custom that women do not slaughter.** So agreed most Acharonim; likewise from there Sefer HaKaneh. Peri Chadash testified he saw women slaughtering; likewise in Sefer Gur Aryeh in his gloss, who wrote: in these Galilee regions I saw women slaughtering with permission, and no one protests them—end quote; river by river and it spread. Birkei Yosef or 4; and the custom here in the Holy Land Jerusalem is that women do not slaughter; and such is the custom in all cities of Eretz Yisrael, Syria, Bavel, India, Media, and Persia.
