**(Seif 12) The slaughterer etc.** As written in chapter 6 of Bava Metzia (81b) and chapter 3 of Bava Batra that migo in place of witnesses we do not say; and likewise here—even though he could say “I did not slaughter it properly”—this is when he stands in his statement; but if he answers his words and says “I did not slaughter it properly,” he is believed—Rashba there; and Tosafot Bava Metzia 50b s.v. mah, etc., and Keritot 12a s.v. amar.
**However etc.** As written in Yevamot 47a; and Tosafot there s.v. ne'eman and s.v. ve'ein eidut, etc.
**(Likkut) However etc.** As written in Ketubot 25b “she made herself,” etc. (end quote).
