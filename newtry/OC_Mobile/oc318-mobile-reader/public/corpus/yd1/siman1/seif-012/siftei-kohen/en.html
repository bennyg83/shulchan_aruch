**For I did not slaughter it.** Meaning: when he stands in his statement and says he did not slaughter at all—he is not believed, because it is a miggo in the place of witnesses. But if he only said “I did not slaughter it for you,” he is believed in a matter he can correct his words—for perhaps he paused or pressed, for anything disqualified in shechitah is not called shechitah—so Rashba in a responsum.
