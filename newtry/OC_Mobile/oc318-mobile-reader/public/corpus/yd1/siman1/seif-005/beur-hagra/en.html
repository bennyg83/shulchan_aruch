**(Seif 5) One who does not hear etc.** Gemara beginning of Chagigah.
**We do not give etc.** As written “and all who slaughtered,” b'dieved, etc.
**And we do not give etc.** As written 12b, and as Tosafot explained there 2a s.v. shema.
**L'chatchila.** Rosh and Ran, as explained, even though he is not expert—not like the view of the gloss s.k. 63, for according to him specifically an expert; and likewise Darchei Moshe.
**But if etc.** In the words of Ravina “all slaughter,” etc.—experts specifically; and nevertheless except deaf-mute and Rashi there s.v. de'afilu, etc.; and as written in the first chapter of Pesachim (4b) and chapter 2 of Ketubot that we do not believe a minor except for rabbinic matters (see below siman 120 s.k. 36, and siman 127 s.k. 29 and s.k. 32).
**And he is called etc.** According to Taz in Bava Batra 155b “if so,” etc.
**And there is etc.** Avodat HaGolah siman 7 s.k. 63.
