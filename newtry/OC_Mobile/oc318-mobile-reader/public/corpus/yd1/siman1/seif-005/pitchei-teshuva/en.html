**But if he did not take kabbalah.** Shach: a slaughterer who apostatized or issued treifah from his hand through negligence—there is room to permit retroactively; see Taz in siman 119 s.k. 15 who forbids retroactively, and likewise Turei Zahav here s.k. 22 and in siman 2 s.k. 32; see responsum Avodat HaGershoni siman 15 who proved like the poskim that regarding a suspect one need not be concerned retroactively—see there. And likewise in responsum Mayim Rabim part 4 siman 23; but at the end of the responsum he wrote to distinguish between one suspected intentionally—where the evil inclination seized him to apostatize—who is not suspected retroactively, which is not so if he did so from laziness, which is worse, and there is concern retroactively—see there; and likewise raised in responsum Brit Avraham part 4 siman 7 that regarding a suspect one need not be concerned retroactively, and on the distinction Mayim Rabim invented he wrote there is room to judge—see there; and see responsum Beit Ephraim part 4 siman 6.
