**Or** stolen. See Ba'er Heitev; Rambam wrote in responsa Avodat HaKodesh (called Pe'er HaDor) siman 112: appointed for the public for slaughter and examination, established for the public that he steals meat and was found with it publicly—even if he repented, you have no greater desecration of God's name than this; therefore he should not slaughter for the public. If he wishes to slaughter in a private house—the permission is in his hand—see there (see Noda BiYehudah part 14 siman 37) and he brought from Peninei Me'orot.
