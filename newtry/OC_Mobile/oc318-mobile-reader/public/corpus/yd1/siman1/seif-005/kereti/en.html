And if the minor knows, etc.—per the Mechaber's view above, that an adult who is not expert slaughters l'chatchila with an adult al gavav—if so, likewise a minor who knows how to train his hands—his law in this is like an adult; therefore Beit Yosef goes according to his view and rules so; but per Rama, who rules like R' Baruch of Eretz Yisrael to forbid l'chatchila—if so, likewise a minor who knows how to train his hands—since he is not expert, he is not better than another, and it is forbidden l'chatchila; straightforward; therefore I did not know in what Shach and Peri Chadash challenged.
Even though he knows—Hagahot Ashri and more authors disqualify even b'dieved—for there is no testimony from a minor in Torah matters; Tur declares kosher b'dieved; see Peleti, where I wrote there is no difficulty for Tur from that there is no testimony from a minor.
Thirteen years and one day—see Peleti, where I wrote they are accustomed to rely on Tur—see there; but a careful person does not let a soft youth in years slaughter until they examine him whether he has signs of maturity, like other Torah matters.
