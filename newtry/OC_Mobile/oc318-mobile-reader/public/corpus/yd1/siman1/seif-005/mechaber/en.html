A deaf person who does not hear and does not speak, and a fool—that is, one who goes out alone at night, or tears his garments, or sleeps in a cemetery, or destroys what is given to him—even one of these, if he does them in a deranged manner; and a minor who does not know how to steady his hands for shechitah—one does not hand them animals to slaughter l'chatchila even if others stand over them. If they slaughtered, their shechitah is valid if others stood over them; and one does not hand them animals l'chatchila to slaughter when others are not standing over them, even if they wish to feed dogs with the meat. If the minor knows how to steady his hands and others stand over him, he slaughters l'chatchila, and it is permitted to eat from his shechitah. {Rama: But if he slaughtered alone between himself and himself, his shechitah is invalid even if he knows the laws of shechitah (Hagahos Ashrei chapter 30; Rashba; Terumat HaDeshen; Or Zarua). For this purpose he is called a minor until he becomes bar mitzvah, namely thirteen years and one day (Beit Yosef in the name of Ittur and Or Zarua in the name of R' Yoel). Some are stringent not to give kabbalah to one younger than eighteen years (Mordechai in the name of Hilchos Eretz Yisrael and Hagahos Alfasi HaChadashim), for then he is an adult who knows to be careful.}
