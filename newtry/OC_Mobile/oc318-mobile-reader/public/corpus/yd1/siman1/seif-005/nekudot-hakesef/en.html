(Taz s.k. 22) **And it is difficult for me**, etc. This question was already answered by Bach at the end of siman 185, who wrote: one may distinguish regarding butcher—it was not possible in another matter, which is not so here regarding niddah—it is possible she will say "I am impure" and she had no clothes of niddah to wear—see there. Also in Rama's responsum [that I brought] below siman 185, small seif 5 [likewise]—see there.
