(In the gloss.) **And some say one may not rely.** It is difficult for me—for since it appears from Ba'al HaItur that one need not know clearly that he will not faint—from the language of fainting "we are not concerned," it implies there is no concern at all; if so, automatically for Ravina, who holds established even though they are not experts, certainly the law is so for us—that all unspecified slaughter is l'chatchila, for even Ravina (who is lenient) (who is stringent) holds that regarding experts we rely l'chatchila on the majority. Only he holds we require established; and in this we hold like the other Amoraim, that for fainting we are not concerned at all. [My student: see the words of our rabbi below seif 3.]
