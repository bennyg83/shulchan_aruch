**If he is expert.** For in such a case we do not need “most found,” etc.—for it is simple at the beginning of Chagigah that it is impossible he finished until he challenged “these ilmei,” etc.
**Slaughters even etc.** Even though in Terumot there we say l'chatchila not, as written in Yerushalmi there—some of them because of berachah, etc.: mute, naked, and ba'al keri because of berachah—and this is when there is no other to bless, as written in chapter 3 s.v. umishum shechitah there is no stringency in both of them, as written “this and this” are like sharp people for all matters.
**(Likkut) One who hears etc.** And in Or Zarua and Hagahot Ashri they forbid l'chatchila—for another cannot bless on his behalf since he may not bless (it appears from the words of our master regarding berachat hashechitah it is like berachat hamitzvot, therefore the Taz was needed; but in Or Zarua and Hagahot Ashri there is no Taz except at the end of their words “if another also slaughters”). And proof from terumah where we taught “the mute,” etc., and Yerushalmi explains because of berachah (and it is strained to say when there is no other at all to bless); and they are in doubt there whether if another also slaughters he can bless on his behalf (and see Orach Chayyim siman 489 s.k. 3 in Likkut).
