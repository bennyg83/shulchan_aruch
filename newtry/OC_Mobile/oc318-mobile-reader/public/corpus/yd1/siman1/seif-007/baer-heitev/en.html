**Expert.** Shach wrote: that which he did not write regarding one who speaks and does not hear “if expert”—here he teaches us he slaughters even l'chatchila, therefore had to say “if expert” (not like Bach and Perishah who answered another matter; see Choshen Mishpat siman 235 and Orach Chayyim siman 121).
**Who blesses.** Shach wrote: do not ask— we hold in seif 3 that one they know does not know hilchos shechitah is permitted to slaughter with another standing al gavav per the Mechaber—why need “expert” here? Answer: here the other is not expert, only knows how to bless; or the other blesses and goes, or stood there and did not see he slaughtered, only heard his berachah; and it deals with that the other also slaughters—for otherwise he cannot bless here—so Bach ruled; see below siman 19 that we require that other intend to extract.
