(In Shach s.k. 37.) **And further, since he is naked he is not permitted to intend in his friend's blessing**—and unnecessary is sheomea keoneh—end quote. Nevertheless regarding this he is as one who answers, for it is forbidden to hear even though hirhur is permitted against ervah, as in Shabbat 150: hearing is forbidden like speech; and likewise Tosafot wrote in Berachot at the end of 20 regarding baal keri. With this what is written in Sefer Avodat Yisrael Orach Chaim siman 75 is resolved.
