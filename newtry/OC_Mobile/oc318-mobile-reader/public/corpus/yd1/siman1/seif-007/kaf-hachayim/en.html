(7) **There. Even women.** Likewise Tosafot at the beginning of Chullin—that women slaughter even l'chatchila, not like laws of Eretz Yisrael—see there. Likewise Tur and Beit Yosef; Taz s.k. 1. Likewise Rambam chapter 4 of Hilchot Shechitah law 4: even women and slaves, if they are experts—these slaughter l'chatchila. Likewise Rashba in Torat HaBayit HaAroch 7b; and in Torat HaBayit HaKatzar house 1 gate 1; likewise Rosh, Ran, Mordekhai, Hagahot Ashri in the name of R' Yitzchak, and Mahariach at the beginning of Chullin. Likewise Semag positive commandments chapter 3 end of Hilchot Shechitah. Likewise R' Yeruham netiv 15 or 5 part 1. Likewise Sefer HaGan. Beit David Hekdesh HaGan in Hagahot Beit Yosef letter 2—see there. Found: per the law, women are permitted to slaughter l'chatchila; only from custom some are stringent, as written below or 11—see there.
