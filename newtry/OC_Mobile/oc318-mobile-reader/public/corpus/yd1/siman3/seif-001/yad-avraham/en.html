**(siman 3 seif 1) Or that he threw a knife to stick it in the wall and shechted as required—kosher.** The same law if it returned backward from the wall from the impact and shechted as required—kosher, for this too is from human force as stated in Sanhedrin (77) regarding murderer. And in first chapter of Chullin he compares shechitah to murderer. And again I found Zichron Yosef in Choshen Mishpat wrote so and proved from Chullin (12b).
