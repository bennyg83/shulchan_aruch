**a) [seif 1] Chullin shechitah does not require intention.** Reason: the Merciful One revealed that meitatek in kodshim is invalid, as written “and he shall slaughter the young bull”—until shechitah is for the sake of the young bull; infer chullin permitted and does not require intention. Chullin 31b. Beit Yosef. Levush. Taz seif 1. But human force we nevertheless require, as written “and you shall slaughter and eat”—what you slaughter you eat, meaning the slaughtering comes from your force. Levush.
