**Shechitah does not require intention. And it has one seif:**
Chullin shechitah does not require intention. Even one who is meitatek be'alma to cut, or threw a knife to stick it in the wall and shechted as required in its going {variant: in walking and majority}—kosher—and that he saw he did not hide {explanation of neitzah and techibah from language of weasel entering holes and cracks} the knife between siman and siman, or under the skin. And if he found feather or hair cut—certainly did not hide. And even if he dropped the knife from his hand or foot without any intention at all and slaughtered—his shechitah is kosher. But if it fell by itself—it is invalid, for we require human force {see above siman 7}. And likewise if the knife was placed in his lap or hand and fell from his hand or lap without intention—it is like falling by itself and invalid.
