When do we separate challah? When he puts in water and the flour mixes with water—and this is when there does not remain in the mixing flour that did not mix with water in the amount of an omer; and if he said behold this is challah on the dough and on the leaven and on the flour that remains, and when all of it becomes one dough this in my hand shall be sanctified as challah—this is permitted; and likewise if he stipulates that challah shall take effect also on flour that will be mixed in later at the time of kneading; and it is good to teach women to stipulate thus (if there is concern that shiur challah will be mixed in later) (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag); and one must be careful not to burn the challah until after all the kneading.
