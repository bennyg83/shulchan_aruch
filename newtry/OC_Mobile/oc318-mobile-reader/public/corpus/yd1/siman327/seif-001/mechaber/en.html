When challah obligation takes effect on the dough. In it are 5 seifim.
One who separates his challah while still flour—it is not challah, and it is robbery in the kohen's hand, and the rest of the dough is obligated in challah; and that flour he separated for challah—if there is an omer in it and he made it into dough, he separates challah from it; and specifically when he says that challah status shall take effect on it while still flour; but one who separates flour and says when it becomes dough challah status shall take effect on it—his words stand.
