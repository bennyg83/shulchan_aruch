And it is stolen property in the kohen's possession. Kiddushin 46 shows that even if the donor knows flour cannot be separated as challah, we do not assume he intended it as a gift. The reason is concern for religious ruin: the kohen may not know the law fully and may treat the flour as valid challah. The Sages therefore made a uniform rule that it is stolen property in the kohen's hands even when the donor is a scholar, rather than distinguishing cases.
