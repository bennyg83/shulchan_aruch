The exterior. According to the 20th chapter of the Shacha, when the exterior is pure, but when the exterior needs a bath, even if it is not in his mouth, according to the Psah, it is permissible to perform a bath for the exterior, but not for the interior, as above, see the verse RA 69:
He shall insert. Rather than wedge the vessels tightly, he should tie them with a string or otherwise use a loose arrangement. Issur VeHeter writes further that the concern applies specifically to vessels that were already filled, because their contents press upon their walls and create a chatzitzah. With an empty vessel there is no concern, since water will inevitably reach the area. However, one should not place many vessels inside another vessel for immersion in a manner that they rest one atop another on their base or mouth. Placing many vessels side by side in a bucket is permitted initially, even if they later fall one upon another in the well, because as they move and roll about the water reaches their entire surface.
