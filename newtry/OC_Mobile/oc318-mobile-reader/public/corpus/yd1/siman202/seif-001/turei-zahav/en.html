These constitute an interposition on utensils. This depends upon whether a person is particular about it, as above in siman 198, seif 1, and the law is rabbinic. This means either that it covers most of the utensil, even though he is not particular about it, or that it covers a minority of it and he is particular about it. So ruled the Rash, in chapter 9 of Mikvaos, and as stated in seif 2.
