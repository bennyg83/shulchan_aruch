Some explain: the clay-like substance, etc. See Rambam in his commentary there, regarding the variant readings. The Arukh writes as follows under the entry mar, concerning the ninth chapter of Mikvaos, which lists substances that interpose: One explanation reads chamar, as in, ‘and mortar served them as clay’; another reads hamor, like myrrh and aloes; and another reads mor istakti, as stated in the Tosefta: ‘myrrh and gum.’ The Rash cites this there on Mishnah 7. The Tosefta teaches: ‘A ring that was placed,’ etc.; ‘myrrh and gum,’ etc.
