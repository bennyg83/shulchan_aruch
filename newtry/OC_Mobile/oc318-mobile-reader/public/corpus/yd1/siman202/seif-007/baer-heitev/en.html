He further writes that this concern applies specifically to vessels that were filled beforehand, because their contents weigh down their walls and create an interposition. With an empty vessel there is no concern, since it is impossible for water not to enter there. Nevertheless, one should take care not to place many vessels inside another vessel to immerse them together, even if they are empty, unless they are not resting one atop another, such as a vessel standing on its bottom or on its mouth. It is permitted initially, however, to place many vessels side by side in a bucket, even if they fall one atop another in the well, because as they roll about in different directions, the water reaches their entire surfaces. End of quotation.
