And they broke, etc. In the Tur it is written that it broke inside the socket. It appears that the explanation is that the socket is made of metal like the utensil itself, and the socket is hollow, and one inserts into it a handle of wood or another material. The handle broke inside the hollow of the socket, and therefore it will have to be removed and repaired anew. Consequently, the handle constitutes an interposition for the immersion of the utensil.
