Anything that one is ordinarily careful about is a chatzitzah; if not, it is not a chatzitzah unless it covered most of it (Rambam perek 3). {Rama: Rust scale stuck to a pot from the outside is like the wall of the vessel, and that is its customary [state], and it is not a chatzitzah (Beit Yosef, end of siman 120, in the name of Rosh).}
