On her garment. Sidrei Taharah and Panim Meirot distinguish modern outer garments from ancient loose undergarments. A stain on an outer garment that cannot physically fold against the genital area does not create impurity. Similar reasoning applies to fixed-position bed cushions.
Above the belt she is pure. Sidrei Taharah questions whether this remains true where deep bending could bring the area opposite the source, unless an actual belt blocks movement. He leaves the matter unresolved.
