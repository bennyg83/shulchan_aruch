From the girdle upward — pure. And also on flesh alone from the girdle upward — pure when she did not flip upside down, even if she did not pass through the butchers' market as above; but here it teaches us that on the garment alone — even if she flipped upside down — she is pure.

Were it to come from the source, etc. And even though below the girdle, not flipped, even on garment alone she is tamei when she did not pass through the butchers' market — when flipped it is different, for it is impossible for blood to drip except as it flows downward; it touches the garment at times and it is impossible for it not to touch the flesh — Rashba.
