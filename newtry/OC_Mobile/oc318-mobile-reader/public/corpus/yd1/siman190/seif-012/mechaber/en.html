If the ketem was found on her garment below the belt, or on the place of the belt itself, she is tamei, even if it was found on the outside. (And there is no difference between whether it was found on her garment in front of her or behind her or on the sides, because garments shift this way and that) (Beit Yosef in the name of Raavad in Sefer Ba’al HaNefesh, and Rashba in Toras HaBayis). But if she passed through the butchers’ market, she is tehorah, even if it was found on the inside and on her flesh. And if it was found only on her garment, from the belt and above, she is tehorah even if she stretched out and lifted her legs, and even if she did not pass through the butchers’ market, for if it had come from the makor it would have been found on her flesh.
