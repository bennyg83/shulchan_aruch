That does not receive tumah, etc. See Rambam Laws of Vessels what are the things that do not receive tumah.
Or any thing, etc. And likewise even something that receives tumah except that its measure prevents it — such as a cloth that is not three by three, as in the Gemara — Beit Yosef brings it; and so poskim; and it is clear from Tosafot beginning of folio 58 and from Rosh chapter HaIshah who wrote regarding mashtatita — even though it does not receive tumah it does receive negaim-tumah; and a vessel that receives negaim-tumah even though it does not receive other tumah does receive stains and she is tamei.
