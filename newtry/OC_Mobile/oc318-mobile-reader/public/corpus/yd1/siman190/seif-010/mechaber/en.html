A ketem that is found on something that does not receive tumah — they did not decree. How so? She checked the ground (or a beit hakisei that does not receive tumah), or anything that does not receive tumah, and she sat on it and found on it a ketem. And likewise, a ketem found on a dyed garment — she is tehorah. (Therefore, a woman should wear colored garments in order to save her from ketamim) (Rambam, and in the Gemara, perek HaIshah).
