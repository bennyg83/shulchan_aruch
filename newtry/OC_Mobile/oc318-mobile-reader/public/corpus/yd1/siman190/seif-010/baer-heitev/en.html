Impurity. See Rambam, Hilchot Kelim, concerning which materials do not contract impurity.
Contracts. Even a material normally susceptible to impurity may not contract it because of insufficient size, such as a cloth smaller than three by three fingerbreadths. Tosafot implies that an object susceptible to tzaraat impurity, even if not to other impurity, is subject to the laws of stains. Shach.
