If she killed a bedbug. A bedbug, called in German a Wanze, or if she smelled its odor, she may attribute the stain to it up to the size of a lupine. Rashi on Beitzah 25 explains that a lupine is a type of round legume shaped like a lentil and as broad as a small coin. I heard from one familiar with foreign terminology that it is what is called turmisin, and that it is as broad as the coin called in our lands a half-patz. Taz and Shach write that in places where bedbugs and such insects are common, one may attribute a stain to them up to the size of a lupine even if she did not actually kill one, because in the laws of stains we follow the lenient approach. See their words at length.
