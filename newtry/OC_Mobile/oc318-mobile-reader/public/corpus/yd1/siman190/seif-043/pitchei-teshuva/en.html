To one counting seven who did not immerse. See Mishnah Lemelech ch. 9 Avos HaTumah halachah 29 who wrote it appears from all poskim specifically zavah within zivah days and niddah within her niddah days we attribute to her; but if zivah days passed though not yet immersed she does not attribute since her first impurity already ceased — only rabbinically immersion required; if we attribute to her we bring her new impurity — why do you see to declare this one tamei and not that; therefore both corrupted — see there further; they brought in Siftei Taz s.k. 78. See responsum R. Akiva Eiger s. 63 regarding married tehorah woman who slept in bed with adult single woman who already saw several times; stain found on bed sheet; he wrote it seems lenient to attribute to the single woman — not only per Levush who wrote plainly even in full counting days who did not immerse she attributes, meaning exactly our view; even per Mishnah Lemelech after seven even without immersion new faces came and she does not attribute to her; nevertheless here though this single woman perhaps did not see for weeks — nevertheless not like zavah after her counting days completed for we hold requires counting either at beginning or end; but without any counting even if clear before Heaven she did not see all seven — nevertheless does not help without counting; therefore by default our single women who do not intend to immerse and do not count for seven clean days intent are zavah who did not count and we attribute to her — see there (see Siftei Taz and Chokhmat David on this matter).
