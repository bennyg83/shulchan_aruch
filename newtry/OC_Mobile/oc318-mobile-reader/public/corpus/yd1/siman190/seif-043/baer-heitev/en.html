And likewise when she did not return and dress it, also when she did not examine the first time before removing it; rather it teaches that even though she returned and dressed it and the garment is with the first, nevertheless the second is also tamei; or it deals where the first examined it before removing it, for now she is not tamei until she returns and dresses it.
