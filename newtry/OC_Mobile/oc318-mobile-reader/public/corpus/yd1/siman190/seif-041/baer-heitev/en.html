Her time. Meaning twelve years and one day. This rule applies to a niddah or non-Jewish woman because the fault is attributed to one already impaired. If the garment was lent to a pure Jewish woman, both become impure. The phrase 'if she examined it' refers to examination when she removed it. Shach.
