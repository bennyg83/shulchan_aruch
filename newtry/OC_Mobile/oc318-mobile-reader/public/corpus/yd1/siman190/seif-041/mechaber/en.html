She wore a garment that was checked for her, and removed it, and she lent it to a Jewish woman who is a niddah, or to an idolater, whose time to see arrived and she saw one time; even though she did not see during the days of the lending of the garment, and afterward a ketem was found on it — she attributes to them and she is tehorah. And the same applies if she checked it and lent it to them, and afterward she wore it and found on it a ketem, that she attributes to them. (This is the explanation of Rashi and Rambam and Tur.) And even if she is counting 7 nekiyim (and see above siman 196, that in the first 3 days of the 7 nekiyim we are not lenient with ketamim to attribute to something else).
