Two women who occupied, etc. Tosafot chapter who sees (Berakhot 58) wrote in Rabbenu Tam's name: they occupied one after another; if first were asked we would declare tehorah since she occupied; for friend's sake we declare first tamei who is completely tehorah, etc. — end quote; Maharach copied: since they come to ask together both tamei — so Tosafot implies; if they came one after another tehorot — does not appear so to me; if so Talmud or poskim should distinguish explicitly as Pesachim ch. 1 regarding mouse and chametz in mouth, etc., brought in Orach Chaim Laws of Pesach. And further: Tosafot certainly did not write one is tehorah but the first — meaning if she came before anything known from second; but after known certainly both tamei even one after another; so I saw in Teshuvos HaRashba long regarding three women as I brought below seif 49; reason appears: here different from chametz for there doubt on mouse in other house or not is heter as Rashi later in Gemara explained — only in two houses they were stringent for perforce one has chametz; here nevertheless lenient one after another since in doubt we go leniently; likewise two paths — if one doubts if walked tamei or not we go leniently establishing on original presumption; therefore lenient also two people one after another; but here with stains where nothing to attribute — rabbinic doubt tamei; no heter except if something reasonable to attribute stain came from there; therefore here with two women and extra sela and unreasonable to attribute impurity to one more than fellow — remains as if nothing to attribute; Derishah also wrote no distinction one after another or together except gave reason only in rabbinic matters we say so; here complete blood sight Torah — he was not precise: one that even in Torah matters we say so Pesachim ch. 1 two paths, etc., and ch. 2 Ketubot regarding permitting woman to husband we say this reasoning; reason we establish on presumption of heter that was before; further Derishah gave reason to be stringent here even one after another: say perhaps from bird came half sela on each and other half her makor blood — also not so; why does Gemara give reason with two women because extra sela — what did you see to attribute to one more than fellow; should say both tamei because each has half from bird; rather clear: whatever before us to attribute we attribute leniently — occupied with whole sela like anything we attribute — we do not say perhaps not all from thing we attribute only part and rest from woman; so too each of two women; except one ruins fellow; if so question returns: why different from chametz; main reason as nearby — as if nothing to attribute; perforce even one after another both tamei.
