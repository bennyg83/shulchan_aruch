And she returned and wore it. It appears that this "and she returned" does not refer to wearing — for that would imply two separate wearings, and if so, why two? Once suffices. Rather, "and she returned" refers to the garment — that she returned and took back the garment from her friend after she had lent it to her, and wore it. The same applies if she wore it from the beginning. See what was stated in seif katan 39. The Shach strains to explain this differently, but according to what was stated, it is clear.
