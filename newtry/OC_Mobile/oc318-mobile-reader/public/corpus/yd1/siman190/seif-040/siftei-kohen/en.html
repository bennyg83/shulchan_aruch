She examined garment, etc. Meaning she examined it first then dressed then removed and found tehorah and lent it to friend; so Tur implies; but without examining garment when removing — no need to teach first tehorah since lent to friend — without examining before dressing per preceding seif likewise Rosh in Teshuvos HaKetzar explicitly examined garment before dressing; Derishah left his words requiring study; Bach explained need for examination before dressing: specifically when examined second time tehorah; but did not examine second time even though examined when dressing — both tamei — end quote; in my humble opinion even Bach's words "even though examined when dressing," etc. — not precise; implies all the more if never examined — both tehorot for not tamei from stain except examined garment as preceding seif; also Perishah deals she did not examine garment second time only examined herself found tehorah; though second examined herself found tehorah nevertheless attribute to second not first — here stain found here it was — end quote; imprecise; in such case both equal — both tamei; we do not say here found here was as Gemara implies (60a) lest you say that stain ruins the other — not teaching; so poskim and Taz below seif 43 both tamei; even if stain found on second first tamei; we do not say here found here was. In my opinion.
