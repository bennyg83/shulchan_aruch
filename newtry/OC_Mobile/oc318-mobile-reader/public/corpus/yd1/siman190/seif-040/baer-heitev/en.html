She checked. The Shach wrote: meaning that she checked it at first, then wore it, then removed it and found it clean, and lent it to her fellow. But if she did not check her garment except when this one removed it, he would not need to teach that the first woman is tahor because she found it clean and lent it to her fellow, for without that too she would be tahor, since she did not check it before wearing it. So too wrote the Taz, unlike the Derishah and Bach, who entered into forced explanations.
