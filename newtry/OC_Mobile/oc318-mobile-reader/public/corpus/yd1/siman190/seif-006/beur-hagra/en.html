And some say, etc. — his reason from what is stated 58a "drops," etc.; he explains small drops are like there 59a; and so all commentators explained as written in Teshuvos HaRashba in their names; Rambam explains the question whether their form proves they are not from the body, as in Siftei Taz: otherwise she is tamei; but commentators explain the question regarding combination whether it equals a garment that does not combine; and this is seif 8 "and some say," etc.; Rashba wrote that in every case it equals a garment except large drops like a lentil — the question arises; he explains like the majority of Rambam whether their form proves as above — this is the first reasoning of seif 8 that did not distinguish; nevertheless Rashba there agrees to the second reasoning of seif 8 in practice.
