But if she stopped a little, etc. Bach proved from Gemara that says is she flowing like river, Rashi explained impossible; answers not dealing with shofe'et but mazlefeft, behold like shofe'et; thus Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote shofe'et or mazlefeft deals with interruption; Taz s.k. 3 challenged and distinguished shofe'et seven days without interruption or less, these words prophecy and nature research to say three days can be shofe'et and seven days not; actually his words plain for certainly to be shofe'et always in abundance of blood impossible to live; but for this need not stop completely only few minutes shofe'et hour two in abundance then discharge stops and mazlefeft little by little; after hour blood returns in abundance and so all days but not stopping completely; thus Gemara question fits per Rambam mazlefeft is like stopped; must say shofe'et like river without interruption; on this questioned well impossible; therefore answers mazlefeft not like stopped; thus plainly exists shofe'et hour two and mazlefeft afterward and returns shofe'et, so it is. These are Shulchan Aruch's words: she was shofe'et several days, meaning sometimes stopped to drip only drop after drop and almost hour returns to flow; or from beginning until end sees only drop after drop, only one sighting; but if she stopped even was shofe'et and stopped and was not mazlefeft between stops, certainly count two sightings.
