(Shulchan Aruch, se'if 31) She found a stain. If she does not know whether it is blood or dye, there is room to rule leniently. Since examination is now impossible, this is not considered an uncertainty caused by a lack of knowledge in such a case, as is clear from the words of the later authorities. It is therefore an uncertainty concerning a rabbinic law. See Minchat Yaakov.
