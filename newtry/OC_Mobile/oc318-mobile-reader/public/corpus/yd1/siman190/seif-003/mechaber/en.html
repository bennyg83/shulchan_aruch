If she flowed for several days, or if she dripped drop after drop without interruption, it is only like one seeing until she interrupts. But if she interrupted a little and again saw 3 times, even in one day, behold she is established as one who has blood, and her ketem is tamei. And there is one who says that her ketem is not tamei unless she saw blood of 3 vesatos. (And one should be stringent like the first reasoning) (this is the reasoning of Rashi and Rashba and Tur).
