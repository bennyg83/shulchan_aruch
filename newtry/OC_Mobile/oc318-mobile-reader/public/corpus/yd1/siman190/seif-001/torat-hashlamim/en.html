That she is tamei. Rambam wrote in ch. 9 Avos HaTumah this impurity is from doubt lest this stain came from the inner chamber; Kessef Mishneh wondered — behold double doubt and we should permit: doubt from her body doubt not from body; if from body doubt from chamber doubt from upper story — tehorah; one may say nevertheless sages were stringent because it is a severe prohibition (and so Gemara 19a implies to be stringent on Torah words per Rashi lest we be lenient on full niddah); explain: since she has nothing to attribute to, they were stringent as if certainly from her body — only one doubt remains — end quote. Taz wrote one may say since majority of blood leaving woman comes from makor this is not considered doubt at all; do not challenge from Rama's double doubt in siman 187 seif 5 for there is wound and perhaps from sides — double doubt refers there; but in my opinion reason for double doubt: majority failed us since majority of women do not see from relations from makor — therefore true double doubt (as explained Ketubot 16 and Bava Batra 99); for this reason considered double doubt here since majority of women feel if they see blood — majority failed; unless for reasons Kessef Mishneh wrote; clear.
