(Shulchan Aruch, se'if 46) A garment that she wore during her days of menstruation and that was laundered, etc. Kreti U'Pleti wrote that all this applies when, immediately upon receiving the garment from the launderer, she locked it in a chest so that no stain could possibly reach it. It then retains the presumption that the stain came from the woman, and she can attribute it only to the days of her menstruation, as explained. If it was not secured in a manner that eliminates the possibility that the stain came from elsewhere, however, this is not considered an examined garment, and she does not become impure because of the stain. We thus find a practical remedy according to Beit Yosef for most women, who do not examine their garments and sheets before wearing or using them, but use them as they are received.
