Unless she has a wound on her body. The Rav wrote in a responsum siman 97 that a woman who has a wound — but white or green appearance was found with the stain — since white and green appearance comes from her body, we also attribute the red to her body and do not attribute it to the blood of her wound. See the responsum at length. The Taz at the end of this siman challenged him, and his opinion is to be lenient in such a case. The Shach agreed in Sefer Nekudos HaKesef — see there.
