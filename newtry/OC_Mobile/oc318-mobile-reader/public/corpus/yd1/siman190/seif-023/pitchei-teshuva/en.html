Siftei Taz wrote: specifically from default we are concerned lest she touched her hand where blood from makor could fall; but if she said clearly I did not touch those places where blood could fall from makor — believed and we do not say it is not plausible. Also appears even per stringent like Rambam (above seif 6) stain on her flesh needs no measure; nevertheless on her hands one may rely on poskim who require measure even on flesh — for otherwise Rashba in his teacher's name: specifically when she examined herself and did not then move her hand; and though we are stringent like Mishnah Yosef, etc. — see there; they also brought Chokhmat Adam there halachah 12; he wrote further it appears to him if found on back of hand above finger joints where there is still room to be lenient one may lean to say specifically until finger joints tamei but rest of back of hand since even with much crawling cannot reach makor — not tamei at all in stains; all the more if she did not check near this or even checked but then moved her hand, etc. — see there.
