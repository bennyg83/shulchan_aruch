She dealt with something red and a black ketem was found on her, or the reverse — we do not attribute to it. In what case are these matters said? Red with black, and black with red. But red with red, and black with black: even if it is not clearly recognizable that it is similar to it, she attributes to it; for example, she dealt with fenugreek-water, or with meat-water, or with a collyrium that is somewhat red — she attributes the red to it.
