On it seven chemicals, etc. Taz s.k. 18 brought what Tur wrote in name of Ramban not to rely on seven chemicals except for taharot; challenged for they were more stringent in taharot than husband, see there; likewise challenged in Perishah, their words without depth for Ramban like Raavad; see Taz and Raavad who hold seven chemicals do not testify it is not blood, though does not pass through being absorbed in garment; for garment taharah they pass seven signs for either if dye plainly tehorah and if blood only absorbed in garment, absorbed impurity does not impart impurity, plain; so Tosafot 62b.
