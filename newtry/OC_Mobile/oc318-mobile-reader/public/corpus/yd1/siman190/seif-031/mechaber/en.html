She found a ketem and she has nothing to attribute to, and the matter is uncertain whether it is blood or dye — she passes over it 7 substances; if it remained as it was, behold it is dye and she is tehorah. But if she does not pass them over it, she is tamei out of doubt. And now we do not have the passing over of the 7 substances, because we are not expert in some of their names.
