Presumption he examined at laundering. Implied from Tur and Rashba like Mechaber's language: even stain before us and it is congealed — nevertheless since presumption examined we do not attribute to before laundering; for relying on congealed or coagulated examination is when not examined at laundering; so explicit Rashba Mishmeret HaBayit 280a R. Yehuda and R. Atta only when not examined — see there; Shach wrote also here we rely on congealed examination; appears follows Ra'ah in Beit HaBechirah 179b who holds so; but in my opinion Tur like Rashba and Mechaber followed as stated.
