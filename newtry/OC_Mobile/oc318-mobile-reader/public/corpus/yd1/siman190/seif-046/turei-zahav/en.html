She must be concerned stringently. Although stains are rabbinic and a doubt is ordinarily resolved leniently, here the doubt arises from lack of knowledge, because she does not know how to identify the shade. Since many people are able to permit this shade, we do not rule leniently merely because she herself is uncertain. So wrote the Rashba. A similar principle is found in siman 98, seif 3, as I wrote there in the name of the Mordechai.
