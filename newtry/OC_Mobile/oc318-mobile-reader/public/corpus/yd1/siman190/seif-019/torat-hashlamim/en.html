And if the wound is on her shoulder. Similarly, if the wound is on her neck in a place from which it is impossible for blood to drip onto her thigh, even if she bends over — such as from the sides and the like — the law is also so. Rather, he mentioned her shoulder because it is a definitive case. This is also clear from the words of the Beis Yosef and the Bach s.v. "Ein machzikin," etc. See nearby seif 16 and seif katan 20.
