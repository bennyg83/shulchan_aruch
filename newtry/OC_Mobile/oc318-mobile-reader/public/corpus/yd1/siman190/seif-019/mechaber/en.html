Just as she attributes to it, so she attributes to her son and to her husband if they dealt with ketamim, or if they have a wound, since it is their way to touch her. But if they were involved with blood and no blood was found on them, she does not attribute to them, unless they were involved with something that is its way to splatter, such as slaughtering and the like. {Rama: And the same applies if she lay in a bed with women who have wounds on their bodies — she attributes to them as she does to her son and to her husband (Mordechai, Hilchos Niddah, and in Sha”d).}
