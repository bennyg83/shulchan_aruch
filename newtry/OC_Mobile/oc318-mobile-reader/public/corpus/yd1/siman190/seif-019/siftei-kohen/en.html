But if they were, etc. It appears this means: it was not known with certainty that blood was found on them after they had been occupied with blood; but if it was known that blood had been found on them — even though now there is no blood on them — we attribute that when they touched her, blood was on them — Bach.
And the same law applies, etc. The reason the Gemara and poskim mentioned her son and her husband — is because these are customarily accustomed to touch her; and so Derishah.
