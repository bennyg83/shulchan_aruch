A woman who was engaged in her work, and blood was found in a place where she passed on something that had been checked for her at the outset (and it receives tumah) (these are my words; and so it is in Rosh, perek HaIshah, and Rashba at the end of Sha’ar 4, and Tosafos at the beginning of daf 58): she should return and engage [in her work] as she did. If it happens that she will pass over the place where the blood was found, she is tamei; and if not, she is tehorah.
