See Siftei Taz who wrote in name of Perishah: even if found on her flesh alone she attributes to son and husband; but at end when blood not found on them but they were occupied with something whose way is to splash — certainly we do not attribute except when also found on garment; but if found on flesh alone — no, for not better than passing through butchers' market — see there; they also brought Chokhmat Adam there halachah 16 (see responsum Chatam Sofer 186 where a Rav questioned Shulchan Aruch's language implying son and husband have all law of wound in her body that even if only could fester one attributes to them; he was challenged whence this for Mechaber; in mishnah chapter one who sees stain it implies only there we attribute even if inflamed and could fester; but for son and husband enough to attribute to wound expelling blood; also from Rambam's language so appears; he answered though it appears so in Be'er HaGolah to Raavad and Ramban's laws — nevertheless Shulchan Aruch's words are correct for practical law as clear from majority poskim Rashba, Semag, Sefer HaTerumot, Mordechai, Semak, Rav Yerocham; also from Gemara there is necessity for this; close to explain Rambam and Raavad and Ramban's closed words agree with majority explicit poskim to be lenient in rabbinic stains — see there).
