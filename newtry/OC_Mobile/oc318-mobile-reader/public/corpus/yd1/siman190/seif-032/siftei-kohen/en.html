But if it does not receive tumah — she is pure, as above seif 10; for there is no distinction between a stain and blood, since she did not feel it emerge from her flesh — she is pure by Torah law; and it is the Rabbis who decreed; and on something that does not receive tumah they did not decree; and so Derishah s.k. 45.
If it should chance that she crosses, etc. Meaning that the place between her legs would come opposite that place; and so Perishah wrote.
