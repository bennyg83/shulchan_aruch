And the measure of a lentil is like four hairs, etc. This is the correct reading — not as printed in books “twenty-four hairs”; the error arose because it was originally written “kud [four] hairs” and the printer assumed it meant twenty-four (kaf-daled), but it is only four (kud).
That it is thirty-six. That is, a gris is thirty-six hairs — meaning six by six in length and breadth.
As they are spaced, etc. Meaning with the gap between them — not as if they are literally adjacent to one another; and see further.
And as long as it does not have this measure. Meaning it does not have a gris and more.
