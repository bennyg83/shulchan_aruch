Whether she is a non-virgin. Maharshal wrote that nowadays every woman who has a husband is concerned about her stains, and even a minor — since even for hymenal blood she counts seven clean days, and one should not be lenient against the custom. See the Shach who challenged him, ruling that one should not be stringent even in our times in any matter regarding a minor's stain, even if found on her body, as implied by the poskim.
