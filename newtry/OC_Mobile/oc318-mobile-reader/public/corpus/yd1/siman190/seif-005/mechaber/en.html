They did not decree regarding a ketem unless it has the size of a gris and more. And the measure of a gris is like 9 lentils (3 by 3) (Tur). And the measure of a lentil is 24 hairs (Agur in the name of Mahari”l: 36 hairs, as they are fixed in a person’s body) (a responsum of Mahari”v, siman 22). And whenever it does not have this measure, we attribute and say it is the blood of a louse even though she did not kill a louse; but when it has this measure, we do not attribute to a louse — whether it is square or whether it is long. And if it happened that she has a gris larger than this measure, we estimate by it.
