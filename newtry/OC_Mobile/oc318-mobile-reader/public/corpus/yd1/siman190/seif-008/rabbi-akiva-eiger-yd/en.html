(Taz s.k. 14) If found on her hands. From this it appears if she passed through and blood found on her hands we do not attribute. I wonder — the reason on flesh alone we do not attribute is the proof it is from her body; if from elsewhere it would also be found on garment — this applies to flesh covered by garment; but on hand it does not apply. But in truth in Tur in Rashba's name it is not so: and found on her hands we attribute to them — end quote; meaning since she was occupied with stains and also found on hands, known that filth is from this occupation; what is also on her body we attribute to hands she touched there — this is not called from place to place for known on her hands is filth from elsewhere. Likewise Perishah explained. One may say if she did and found on hands we attribute; but if blood also on body we do not attribute since filth on hands is certainly not from elsewhere we do not attribute to them what is on body; this too requires some study — nevertheless one may say what is on body is from filth before us on hands; since we judge that filth from elsewhere, since she passed through, automatically this blood on body is tehor like law of blood on hands.
