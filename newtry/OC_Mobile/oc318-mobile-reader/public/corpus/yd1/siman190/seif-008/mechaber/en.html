If there is not in the ketem, in one place, a gris and more — even though there are many drops there, close to one another, such that if we combine them they have more than a gris — she is tehorah; for we attribute each drop and drop to a louse, until there is in it a gris and more in one place. And some say that these words are when they were found on her garment, but if they were found on her flesh, they combine to a gris and more.
