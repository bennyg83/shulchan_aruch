Minor girl. Be'er Heitev in name of Shach; see Shach that from his words it appears even if certainly from her body, any amount without sensation is tehorah since it is only rabbinic and they did not decree on a minor girl — see there; likewise Levush and Minchat Chinuch s.k. 3. But Chokhmat David disagrees and holds we declare stains tehorah only where one can say she passed through butchers' market unawares; but if certainly from her body even without sensation — tamei — see there; see Siftei Taz what he wrote on this.
