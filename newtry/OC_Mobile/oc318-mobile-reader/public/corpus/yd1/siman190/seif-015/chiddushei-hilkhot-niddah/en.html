Two women who occupied. See Taz s.k. 17 strained Bach's words who holds specifically when coming to ask together; but coming one after another two tehorot like two paths; brought proof from Tosafot; Taz rejected his proof; Shach in Nekudot HaKesef agreed with Taz no proof but wrote Bach's law true. Rashba in Teshuvos HaRashba regarding examined ed that is unexamined brought in name of Ramban, his view like Tosafot who explained she prepared cloths for sons and wiped only afterward called unexamined; they challenged if so mishnah three women wore one garment all tamei, why like unexamined ed; answer truly should declare all tehorah only like two paths if came to ask together both tamei one after another all tehorot; Rashba challenged if so lent garment to non-Jewish womanes and fellow does not come to ask, second tehorah; therefore Rashba explained unexamined ed she never examined only takes from hidden place therefore called unexamined ed; but whatever had examination once forever in its chazakah until known departed from chazakah, see there; per this clear matter in dispute; Shach in Nekudot HaKesef wrote Rashba per his reasoning also above siman 111 does not hold like two paths; but per what he raised there comparable to two paths likewise here, see there; his words against Shulchan Aruch for dispute depends if unexamined ed means examined initially as Ramban or never had examination only took from hidden place; since Tur and Mechaber below 158 and Rama seif 36 concluded like Rashba and Shach himself did not speak, plainly law of Rashba also here true and established unlike two paths one after another. Appears Bach said only here two women occupied with bird but not three women wore one garment as Shach in Nekudot HaKesef thought to declare tehorah everywhere coming one after another; distinction clear: here if occupied with bird she says I occupied with bird and found blood, what concern with fellow, I do not know if fellow also saw on her garment, my question does not concern fellow, like two paths this says I walked, what matter fellow's walking; thus coming one after another two tehorot like two paths; but three women wearing one examined garment, one woman says I found stain, unless she attributes to fellow no claim to declare tehorah, only says stain did not come from me only from fellow; in her question she attributes to fellow, like not coming to ask together for cannot declare tehorah until attributes to fellow, what did you see to attribute to this one more; whether fellow here to ask or not, end cannot declare tehorah except through attributing to fellow, clear and correct. Proof from Gemara end who sees stain challenges if after she spread garment lent to fellow she tehorah and second tamei, challenges what difference from two occupied with bird both tamei; Tosafot and Ran explain per Rambam since these were first we would declare tehorah since occupied only for fellow's sake we declare first tamei; wonder why challenged from baraita two women occupied with bird and not from complete mishnah three women wore one garment, per Shach's view there too coming each alone we would declare tehorah and Gemara's question stronger; or matter as stated specifically regarding two women occupied we say so but not others where not applicable coming one after another; distinction above correct, see above siman 111 what written there.
