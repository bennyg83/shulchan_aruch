And it was found. And it is impossible to know the matter through questioning as above; and if they cannot determine whether it is congealed, etc. — both are tamei. Tur and Rashba. The reason: since both are tehorot and one cannot attribute to one more than her fellow — what did you see, etc.; and per this even if lost, for in such a case it is a superior doubt as written above, nevertheless both are tamei since one cannot attribute to one more than the other — unlike Levush who wrote tamei from doubt — end quote Shach.
