(Shach s.k. 61) She should check if congealed or coagulated. And we do not attribute to her saying she did not check it or it was laundered by non-Jewess but laundered by Jewess — presumption she checked at laundering; so too in Tiferet L'Moshe per Shach's intent; see Minchat Yosef and Tiferet L'Moshe. Nowadays we are not expert between congealed and coagulated; even if lost forbidden since when stain found she was forbidden forty days — doubt from lack of knowledge; and by afterward losing she was not permitted; see Pri Megadim in Shulchan Aruch (seif 53 s.k. 76) and Levushi Serad introduction (letter 44).
