If she has a wound on her neck. The Shach wrote: that is on the sides of the neck, for then it is impossible to touch there from the wound unless by her spreading [a garment] and covering herself with it; but on the neck proper, without that she attributes [the stain] to it (see what I wrote in se'if katan 18), as is stated in the Gemara and poskim; and so Beit Yosef wrote s.v. "ein machzikin," etc. — end of his words. And he wrote this for nothing, for it is possible to say that here we are dealing even with the middle of the neck; nevertheless, since we are dealing with nighttime when she is sleeping and was not bending, if so, were it not that she spreads and covers herself, it would not be applicable to attribute [it] to her neck as to her shoulder; and of this sort the Bach wrote in se'if katan 21; and this is also the intent of Beit Yosef, who did not write so regarding this law, only afterwards s.v. "ein machzikin" — and it is easy to understand.
