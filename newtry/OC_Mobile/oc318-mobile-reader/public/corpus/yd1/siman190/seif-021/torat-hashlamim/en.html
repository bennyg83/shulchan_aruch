If she has a wound on her neck. Shach wrote: This means on the sides of the neck, where it is impossible for the garment to touch the wound unless she removes it and covers herself with it. But on the neck itself, she attributes it to the wound even without this, as stated in the Gemara and the poskim; and the Beit Yosef likewise wrote this, s.v. “We do not presume,” etc., end of his words. He wrote this needlessly, for it is possible to say that here the case is even the middle of the neck. Nevertheless, since it concerns a night when she is asleep and has not bent over, then without removing and covering herself with it, it would not be appropriate to attribute it to the neck, just as to the shoulder and similar cases. Bach wrote this in seif katan 21. This too is the intent of the Beit Yosef, who did not write this concerning this law, but only afterward, s.v. “We do not presume.” This requires further study:
