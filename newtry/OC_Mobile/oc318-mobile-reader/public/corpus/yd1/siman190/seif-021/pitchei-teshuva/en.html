She is pure. Dagul MeRevavah notes that Rabbeinu Simchah, Raavan, and Ramban appear stringent regarding colored garments. Teshuvah MeAhavah nevertheless follows the great majority who are lenient, while being stringent where half the stain is on white and half on color. Chatam Sofer accepts Rama's leniency, especially for an outer colored garment rather than one touching the body.
