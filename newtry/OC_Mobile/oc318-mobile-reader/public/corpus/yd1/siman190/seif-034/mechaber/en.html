She checked herself with an ed that was checked for her, and placed it under the pillow or under the cushion, and on the next day blood was found on it: if it is drawn, she is tamei, for its presumption is that it is from the wiping; but if it is round, and it does not have a gris and more, she is tehorah, for this is only the blood of a louse that was killed under the pillow. {Rama: And the same applies if it is more than a gris and there is a place to attribute it to (Beit Yosef, as was explained above regarding what we attribute a ketem to).}
