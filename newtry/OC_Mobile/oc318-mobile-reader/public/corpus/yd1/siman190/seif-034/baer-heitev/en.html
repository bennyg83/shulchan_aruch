The cushion. For one can say that a louse was crushed there. Taz.
Stretched. The Shach wrote: specifically here, when she checked herself, we say this; but with all other stains we do not distinguish between elongated and round, for whenever it is less than a gris she is tahor.
To attribute. (It is implied here that even in a doubt, attribution to anything helps.) The Taz wrote: it appears to me regarding a woman who regularly has blood come out from her rectum for some reason, and she checked herself even with a checked cloth and at the time of checking also touched there, that she may attribute it to that, outside her veset, for there is no Torah-level doubt here, since she did not feel blood from the source. I wrote this because I saw one decisor who ruled stringently in this, and it appears to me that he erred in a simple matter; end quote.
