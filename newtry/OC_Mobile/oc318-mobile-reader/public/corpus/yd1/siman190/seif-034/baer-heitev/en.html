The pillow. Because one may say that a louse was crushed there. Taz.
Elongated. Shach writes that the distinction between elongated and round applies only where she examined herself. For ordinary stains there is no distinction, and anything smaller than a gris is pure.
To attribute. Taz writes that a woman who regularly bleeds from the rectum because of a known condition may attribute blood to that source outside her veset, even when using an examined cloth, if she touched that area during examination. Since she felt no uterine flow, there is no Torah-level doubt.
