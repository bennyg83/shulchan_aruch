An object that does not contract impurity. This applies even when its size causes that result, for example, where it is less than three by three, as these laws are explained in Rambam, Laws of Vessels. Tosafos and the Rosh wrote that if it is an object that contracts the impurity of leprous lesions, it also receives stains, even though it does not contract other impurity. See Shach.
