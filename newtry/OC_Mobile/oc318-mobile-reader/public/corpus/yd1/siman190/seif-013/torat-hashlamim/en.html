Something that does not receive tumah. Even if the measure causes them [not to receive], for example where it is less than three by three, as these laws are explained in the Rambam, Hilchot Kelim; and Tosafot and the Rosh wrote that if it is something that receives the tumah of negaim, it also receives stains, even though it does not receive other tumah; and see in the Shach. And if so, accordingly, it is possible that in a house, a place that receives the tumah of nega'ei batim also receives stains; and see in the Rambam, chapter 5 of Tum'at Tzara'at.
