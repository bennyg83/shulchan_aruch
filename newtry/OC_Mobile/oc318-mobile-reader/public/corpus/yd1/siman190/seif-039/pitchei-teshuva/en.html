To attribute to it. Be'er Heitev brought in name of Taz regarding accustomed woman, etc., until she attributes at her veset time; see Chokhmat David who disagrees one may attribute even at veset time — see there; see responsum Me'ir Netivim s. 57 where a Rav wanted to rule specifically when she was in days of taharah then she has presumption of taharah she attributes to this, unlike in days of anticipation when presumed tamei she does not attribute; he extended to disagree and brought proofs she attributes also in days of anticipation — see there (and I already wrote from this above siman 187 s.k. 23); it seems plain in first three days she does not attribute to this per Shach's view in siman 196 brought in Be'er Heitev there s.k. 9 that even on known wound expelling blood she does not attribute, all the more here (and see above siman 196 s.k. 11 from this).
