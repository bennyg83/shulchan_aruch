So Tur; Rashi; Rambam; greater novelty than first case for though stain found with owner of garment she dressed afterward — nevertheless she attributes to them; see Shach who wrote if examined when removing and lent, etc. — end quote; from his words implies need dressed and removed before examination then lent and returned and dressed; I do not know why need dressing and removing first time for this case; also against Tur, Rashi, Rambam who wrote nothing of this; though Rambam ch. 9 wrote language "and she returned and dressed" — intent after she lent them garment she returned and took from them and dressed; but certainly cannot say we require she already dressed — no logic or reason; possible say therefore need state "and removed" — otherwise why examination since she lent to niddah or idolatress; rather she already dressed and removed and examined garment to see tehorah (possibly Rashba's reason on mishnah regarding lent garment to idolatress or niddah: when she dressed examined garment and removed and lent — end quote; intent if like Rashi and supporters difficult why need examination since she lent — did not want explain dressed twice; therefore establishes mishnah briefly with one dressing; this is first case Mechaber wrote); but also not compelled — can say she examined to dress herself as proved end from beginning; afterward niddah or idolatress came to borrow; never dressed before — requires study.
