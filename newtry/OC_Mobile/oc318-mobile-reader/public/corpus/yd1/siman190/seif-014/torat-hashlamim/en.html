Therefore a woman should wear colored garments. The Rambam wrote "therefore the Sages enacted that she should wear colored garments." Since the Beis Yosef questions his wording of "enacted" — which is seemingly against the implication of the Talmud — and even though the Beis Yosef retracts and resolves his words, his resolution is not compelling. Therefore, the Rav omitted the language of "enacted" etc., and instead states it only as good advice that the woman should do in order to save herself from concern about stains.
