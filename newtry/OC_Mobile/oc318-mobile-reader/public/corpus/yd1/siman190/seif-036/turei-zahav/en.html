And specifically with an intermediate examination cloth, etc. Toras HaBayis HaAroch, as cited by the Beit Yosef, explains that an “unexamined” cloth means one taken from a concealed place where women customarily store and prepare examination cloths, which are not carried through the marketplace or handled near staining substances. But if she took a garment from the marketplace and does not know from whom it came, whether from a non-Jewish woman, a Jewish woman who was a niddah, or a pure woman, or she wiped with a random cloth found in the house, no authority renders her impure even because of a stain. Concerning the intermediate unexamined cloth described above, the Rashba and the Tur in the name of the Rosh disagree: the Rashba declares her pure, while the Rosh renders her impure like any other stain. The Shulchan Aruch rules here like the Rosh. But even the Rosh agrees that a garment taken from the marketplace leaves her pure, for that case is more lenient than an ordinary unexamined cloth.
