She is impure, etc. Beit Yosef cites a dispute among the authorities concerning this. Some ruled leniently, yet even they were concerned to rule stringently in practice. In my work I wrote that the primary conclusion of the Gemara appears to be that according to all opinions, meaning even according to Rabbi Yose, she is impure because of a stain. This is also explicitly indicated by the Jerusalem Talmud at the beginning of the chapter Kol HaYad, where it states: Abba, meaning Rabbi Yose, said, 'A stain, etc.' See there.
