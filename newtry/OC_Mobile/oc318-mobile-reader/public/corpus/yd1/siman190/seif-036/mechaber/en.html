She checked herself with an ed that was not checked for her; even if she placed it guarded in her box and found on it blood, she is not tamei unless there is in it a gris and more (Tur in the name of Rosh, and so it is implied). {Rama: And this is specifically with an intermediate ed — meaning, that its presumption is neither checked nor dirty (these are my words). But if she checked herself with an ed whose presumption is dirty — for example, she took an ed from a place where blood is commonly found, whose presumption is that it already had ketamim — and she checked herself with it and a ketem was found on it, she is tehorah even more than a gris (so it implies from Ritva brought by Beit Yosef).}
