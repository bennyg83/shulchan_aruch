He lent it to a minor who never saw, and this minor wore it after she had intercourse, before the wound healed; or he lent it to a young woman who did not see and she wore it within the first 4 nights of her intercourse; or he lent it to one sitting on dam tohar — he attributes to them, even in this time (Raavad; and Tur in the name of some say; and the same law applies; and Rabbeinu Yerucham according to Rambam; and Ra’ah in Sefer Ba’al HaNefesh). And likewise, if he lent it to one counting 7 [nekiyim] who did not immerse, he attributes to her, and the owner of the garment is tehorah, and her friend who borrowed it is impaired.
