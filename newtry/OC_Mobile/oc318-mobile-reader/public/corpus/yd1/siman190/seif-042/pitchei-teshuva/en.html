To one counting seven who has not immersed. Mishneh LaMelech says attribution applies only during active niddah or zivah days, not after the count ended but before immersion, because that would impose a new impurity. Rabbi Akiva Eiger permits attributing to an unmarried woman who never intentionally counted clean days, since without conscious counting she remains like one who has not completed the process.
