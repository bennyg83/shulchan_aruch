Before the wound healed. In the days of the Sages of the Mishnah, a bride remained permitted until the wound caused by the loss of virginity healed, and a young woman was permitted for up to four nights after the first marital relations. Therefore she is not disadvantaged when another woman's stain is attributed to her. Even nowadays, when the husband has marital relations once and then separates, the stain may still be attributed to the bride, since blood is common in her.
To blood of purity. This means to a woman after childbirth during the days of blood of purity.
