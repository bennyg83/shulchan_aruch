(Seif 42) Likewise, if she lent it to a woman who is counting. This implies specifically during her count. But if she completed counting the seven clean days, although she has not yet immersed, the stain is not attributed to her. See Mishneh LaMelech and Tiferes LeMoshe. Levush, however, rules without qualification that even after she completed her count, the stain is attributed to her so long as she has not immersed.
