Stain found, etc. Bach wrote: difficult either way — if we explain this language per Beit Yosef's explanation here that on her flesh even less than lentil tamei for we do not attribute to food — difficult on Shulchan Aruch's ruling in seif 8 "some say" when found on flesh they combine; afterward plain Rambam's language that he is more stringent on flesh even one drop tamei without combination; if we explain Rambam's language here as clearly from Rashba in Mishmeret HaBayit that Rambam did not say she is tamei in all these except where there is lentil and more; small drops do not combine even on flesh — he is more lenient than "some say" who if total drops on flesh equal lentil and more — tamei; for Rambam no distinction garment and flesh, both small drops do not combine and tehorah even if total more than lentil and more; large if one has lentil and more — tamei — end quote. His words are astounding — how can one say Rashba understood Rambam thus when Rambam wrote explicitly in preceding halachah 6: what difference stain on flesh and garment — stain on flesh has no measure, etc. (Raavad challenged there and R. Yosef and Rav HaMagid settled his words there correctly); also how extract from Rambam's language here less than lentil tamei — he only cited Gemara language and as Gemara can be explained so this language; Bach wrote if we explain per Beit Yosef here on flesh even less than lentil tamei — Beit Yosef here after bringing Rambam's language in this seif brought Rosh's language and wrote: Rosh's language implies he holds stain on flesh even less than lentil tamei like Rambam I wrote above, etc.; he understood Rambam in this seif means Rosh — if so why "and like Rambam's words I wrote above" — should have said "I wrote nearby"; also what "and like Rambam's words," etc. — implies Rambam's words clearer than Rosh's; rather Rosh clearer who wrote we do not attribute to food-blood and Rambam only cited Gemara; what "and like Rambam I wrote above" means he brought above Rambam's words in halachah 6 stain on flesh has no measure; if so conversely from Beit Yosef's words in Rambam's words in this seif no ruling; likewise Rashba in Mishmeret HaBayit did not write Rambam holds only tamei with lentil and more as Bach wrote — certainly not; Rashba there only wrote Rambam also explains Gemara's question not regarding combination but whether we say if dripped from body would be thus; Rashba himself holds there only tamei with lentil and more as Bach wrote — only regarding measure no ruling in Rambam's words at all; now Mechaber's words are clear with no difficulty.
