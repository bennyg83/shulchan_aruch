A ketem found on her flesh, that is long like a strap, or round, or that was drops upon drops; or that the length of the ketem is across the width of her thigh; or that it appears as though it is from below to above — since it is opposite the beit torfah (meaning: a term of disgrace, and it is a euphemism for the ervah), she is tamei, and we do not say: if it had dripped from the body it would not have been such.
