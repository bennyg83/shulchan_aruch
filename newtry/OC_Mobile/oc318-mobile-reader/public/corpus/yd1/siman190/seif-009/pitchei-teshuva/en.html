And measure of lentil. See Sefer Yere'im siman 192 who wrote we are not expert in lentil measure; therefore one must be careful with stains per majority view that food-blood is not abundant, and in a matter not fit to attribute — see there; wonder at poskim who did not bring his words; found in responsum Basheilem Rosh 135 who wrote on stain less than lentil: Rav R. Eliezer of Metz wrote we are not expert in this measure and any matter not proven to be food-blood we do not rely on it; his reason: we cannot separate thickness of blood on the cloth and if there is substance of blood you estimate thus, and if only coloring of cloth you estimate as lentil; and in large blood like lentil you can dye the whole garment — nevertheless I saw all my teachers estimate and are not concerned — see there; see Hagahat Kessef HaRasna there at length. See Siftei Taz end s.k. 61 he also investigated whether blood clumped in one place how to estimate if as now less than lentil or must be stringent to estimate as if spread like dye and would be more than lentil and tamei; he wrote it seems we need only estimate as quantity before us, and likewise reverse where stain appears less than lentil but around it spread from body of stain — nevertheless tamei; such is sages' measure without dispute; but one may say all per eyes of the posek if blood piled in one place we judge as if spread — see there.
