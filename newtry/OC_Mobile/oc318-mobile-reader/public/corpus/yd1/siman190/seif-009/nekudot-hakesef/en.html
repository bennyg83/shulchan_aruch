(Taz seif katan 40) He raised a difficulty. He raised a difficulty against Raavad from Choshen Mishpat, siman 79, and from this concluded that if a long time has elapsed she is impure. The cases are not comparable. There we say that the matter may not have been in his mind, and it is possible that in fact the debt was paid. Here, however, she states with certainty that she knows the truth, and the matter is incumbent upon her. Were she not certainly speaking truthfully, she would be committing a prohibition, and she certainly does not wish to commit a prohibition. In any event, the cases differ for several other reasons as well. This is easily understood.
