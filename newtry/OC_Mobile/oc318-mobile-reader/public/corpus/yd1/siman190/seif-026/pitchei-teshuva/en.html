On her garment. See Seder Taharah s.k. 45 who wrote what is stated in Gemara she wore three garments one on another — if she cannot attribute she does not attribute even on the uppermost — specifically three undergarments that are like undershirts where one can say lower ones folded; but in full garments especially our garments it appears she is not concerned on uppermost for in such garments it cannot be said they folded; again brought in name of Ateret Zekenim responsum Panim Me'irot vol. 2 s. 183 who wrote so except differentiating their garments were all open from neck down but our women's garments are all fitted from waist down — impossible in any way upper garment reaches opposite makor; we do not declare tamei if found on upper garment; never heard declaring tamei for stain on upper garment — end quote.
