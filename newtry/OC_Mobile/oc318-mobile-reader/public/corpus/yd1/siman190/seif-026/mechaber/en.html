She dealt with blood, such that it is impossible that a ketem from it would be more than a gris, and she found on herself [a ketem] like two grisin — behold she attributes one gris to the blood that she dealt with, and one gris to a louse. But if the ketem is found more than two grisin, she is tamei. (Rambam, perek 9; and Rashba and Ra’ah in Sefer Ba’al HaNefesh; and the same law applies.) (And some are stringent and render tamei in all this (Rosh and Tur). Nevertheless, it appears that one may rely on the lenient ones, for with ketamim we listen to be lenient.)
