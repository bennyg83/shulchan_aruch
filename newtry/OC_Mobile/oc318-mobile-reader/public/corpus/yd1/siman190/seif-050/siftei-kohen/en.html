The upper sheet, etc. This refers to the custom of placing two sheets one over the other, with the husband lying between them, so that the upper bed covering will not become soiled by perspiration. Perishah. He writes in the name of Maharshal that this explanation is entirely unsatisfactory, since we see with our own eyes that the lower sheet is also pulled about. Bach likewise writes that nowadays, when we see that the lower sheet also turns over, all the women are impure.
