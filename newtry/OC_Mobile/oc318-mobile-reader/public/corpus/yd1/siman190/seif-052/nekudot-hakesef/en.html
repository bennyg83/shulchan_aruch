(Taz, seif katan 40) He challenged. He challenged the Raavad from Choshen Mishpat, siman 79, and inferred that if a long period elapsed she is impure. The cases are entirely dissimilar. There we say that the debtor may not remember, although it may nevertheless be true that he paid. Here, however, she says with certainty that she knows, and the matter is incumbent upon her. Were she not certainly speaking truthfully, she would knowingly violate a prohibition, and we presume she does not wish to do so. There are also several other distinctions. This is readily understood.
