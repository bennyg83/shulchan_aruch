On the upper sheet. The opinion of the Ra'avad, brought in the Tur and Beit Yosef, is that whenever it is found on the sheet, whether it is the upper or the lower, we are concerned lest she turned over, and all are tamei; and so the later authorities ruled — Maharshal and the Bach and the Shach and the Taz. And the Taz wrote that nevertheless, when it is found on the pillow under the sheet, we are not concerned lest she turned over. And the Rashba wrote in Torat HaBayit, and this is his language: but regarding their own bodies we are not concerned lest they turn over in the course of sleep so as to be intertwined; and the main [reason] for this is because stains are d'rabbanan; but one woman who has no impediment, wherever she finds blood on the bed she is tamei, for the entire bed is her place; and furthermore, from where did this blood come — end of his words.
