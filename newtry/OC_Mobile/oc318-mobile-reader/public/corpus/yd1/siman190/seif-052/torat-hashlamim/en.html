On the upper sheet. Ra'avad's view, cited by the Tur and Beit Yosef, is that wherever a stain is found on a sheet, whether it is the upper or lower sheet, we are concerned that it may have been turned over, and all of them are impure. The later authorities, Maharshal, Bach, Shach, and Taz, likewise ruled so. Taz wrote that nevertheless, if it is found on the pillow beneath the sheet, we are not concerned that it may have been turned over. Rashba wrote in Torat HaBayit: “But regarding their bodies, we are not concerned that they may turn over in their sleep and become entwined. The primary reason for this is that stains are a rabbinic matter. But in the case of one woman, who has no one else to whom it can be attributed, wherever she finds blood on the bed, she is impure, for the entire bed is her place; moreover, where else could this blood have come from?” End of his words:
