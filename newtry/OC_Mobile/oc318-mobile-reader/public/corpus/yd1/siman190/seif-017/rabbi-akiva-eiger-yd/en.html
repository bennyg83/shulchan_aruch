(Seif 17) But if it does not have lentil and more. Even per first view above seif 6, nevertheless since we say it is not from her body one may say it is clean blood and no proof she was occupied. Afterward I saw Tiferet L'Moshe wrote similarly; he also wrote if above on flesh alone and below also on flesh alone we attribute except if above on garment and flesh and below on flesh alone. Likewise Be'er HaGolah (ch. 3). It seems even within first three days of her counting she attributes lower to upper since upper certainly not from her body — known it came from elsewhere as if we knew blood spilled on garment and we do not know how much; plainly we also attribute within first three days. Still requires study for practical law.
(Reference 46 Be'er HaGolah) See below seif 23. In Machazit HaShekel the law there is not similar to this; there since known she was occupied with red, here we do not attribute to black; but here since not known occupation except proven she was occupied unawares — one may say she attributes as if occupied with red unawares, likewise occupied with black unawares; so explicitly in sugya in one variant and so many poskim ruled. But R. Chananel and Rosh ruled also here we do not attribute black to red; dispute brought in Tur. But there seif 23 is arranged baraita and none disputes. See Levush who ruled like R. Chananel and Rosh.
