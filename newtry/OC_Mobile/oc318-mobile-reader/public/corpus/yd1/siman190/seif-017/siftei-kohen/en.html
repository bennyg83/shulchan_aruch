And she knows she did not flip upside down, etc. And even though above seif 11 we say she is not concerned that perhaps she flipped — here is different, for there is additionally another unfavorable circumstance in the lower stain; and if we were to attribute and say she did not flip and it came from elsewhere, and then we would also attribute the lower stain to this saying just as the first came from elsewhere so too the other came from elsewhere and they were soiled at the same time — that would be two lenient attributions, and we are not so lenient — so Taz.
But if there is no, etc. For perhaps it is louse blood; therefore if there is in the lower stain a gris and more — she is tamei.
