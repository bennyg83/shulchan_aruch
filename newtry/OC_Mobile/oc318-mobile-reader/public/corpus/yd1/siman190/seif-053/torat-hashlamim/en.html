Two examined. Beit Yosef wrote: appears from necessity if two attribute to one found tamei — all the more one attributes to two; otherwise need not state this law lest you say reverse since two examined found tamei — perforce one tamei for stain not from her — otherwise two stains should be found; since cannot attribute to both say stain did not come from them at all only from tehorah one — teaches; see Perishah and Levush 289a — see there.
