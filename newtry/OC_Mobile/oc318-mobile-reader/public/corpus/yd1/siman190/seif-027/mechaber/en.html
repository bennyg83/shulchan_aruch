She dealt with less than a gris, and she found on herself a gris and more — she is tehorah, for I say: this ketem is from the dealing with ketamim, and blood of a louse was already there, which joined with it until it returned to more than a gris. And likewise if she dealt with less than a gris and she found on herself like two grisin, she is tehorah. {Rama: And some are stringent and render tamei; nevertheless, if she dealt with blood and she does not know how much, we go leniently and say that there was in the blood the measure of the ketem (Beit Yosef; and so wrote Rashba and Rosh and Tur; and in Lechem Chamudos he wrote that the view of the Rav is that here all are stringent, as he wrote in se’if katan 37).}
