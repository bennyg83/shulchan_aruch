Like four hairs. Meaning its length like growth of two hairs, width like growth of two hairs — meaning thickness (see Perishah that sucking of side hairs also meant); places of hairs themselves — nine lentils in each lentil four hairs together thirty-six hairs meaning six hairs on six in square; each lentil has room for growth of four hairs; so explicit from Torat Kohanim parashat Tazria and mishnah explanation ch. 6 Nega'im and Rambam there in Mishnah commentary and ch. 1 Laws of Tzara'at and clarified there in Kessef Mishneh and Derishah this siman — this is Shulchan Aruch's intent and all poskim; I extended because at end of Tot HaSheni siman 97 he wrote: we learned beginning ch. 6 Nega'im body of baheret like a lentil square — lentil place nine lentils, lentil place four hairs — thirty-six hairs — end quote; perforce he did not mean measure of place standing together on human body for if standing in square thirty-six hairs six by six length six hairs would be place holding five by five lentils twenty-five lentils; rather necessarily: if you count four hairs per lentil together sixteen though actually no more than sixteen hairs four by four; Rambam ch. 1 Tzara'at wrote nega'im like lentil, etc., thirty-six hairs six length six width, etc., specifically square as explained there ch. 4 Houses, etc., and ch. 9 Avos HaTumah regarding niddah stains, etc.; but did not mention hair count — only Beit Yosef wrote for him in YD siman 199; no later posek noticed; in my opinion must be inside and before and inside — second strand clear from his words he understood we say lentil is four hairs meaning only spacing between four hairs without growth and sucking of hairs themselves; therefore wrote they are no more than sixteen hairs four by four; therefore wonders at Rambam and all acharonim who did not notice; but in truth he erred in all this; mishnah language not explicit as his words nor Torat Kohanim; rather as stated and it is plain; all resolves correctly; this needs no inside interpretation.
