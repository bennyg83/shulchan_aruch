Lice. This is vantz in German. Rashba's view is that in places where lice and fleas are common one may attribute to them like lupin even though she did not crushed them; Bach ruled not like him and it is not compelled, and it was already explained that in stains we listen to be lenient — end quote Shach; and likewise Taz. Explanation of lupin: a round legume species, wide like a small coin; and it is explained in Beit Yosef that where we attribute to lice there is no distinction between its length and width or not.
