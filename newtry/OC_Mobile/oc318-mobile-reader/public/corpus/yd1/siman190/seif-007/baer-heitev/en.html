A bedbug. Rashba holds that in places where bedbugs and similar insects are common, one may attribute the stain to them up to the size of a lupine even without knowing that one was crushed. Bach disagrees, but Shach and Taz rule leniently in stains. A lupine is a round, broad legume approximately the size of a small coin. Where attribution to a bedbug is permitted, shape does not matter.
