She examined, etc. This law requires study; what Mechaber extracted thus from Rashi also requires study as I will explain. We learn in Gemara (ch. Kol HaYad 14a): she examined herself with examined ed and pressed on thigh; tomorrow found blood on it — tamei niddah; examined with unexamined ed, placed in box; tomorrow found blood — R. Yehuda says tamei from niddah, R. Chiya from stain (Gemara: even R. Yehuda only with lentil and more). Rashi: tomorrow found on thigh — tamei niddah certain impurity since ed examined clean before examination certainly from her body onto ed and pressed to thigh; we do not say perhaps from elsewhere on thigh and doubtful impurity. On unexamined ed: tomorrow found on ed — can doubt perhaps already on it; therefore not stated here pressed on thigh lest two doubts for leniency. Beit Yosef brought Rashi's words — two doubts and for leniency; per this he wrote here; but no proof from Rashi for several reasons: one — Rashi only said why mishnah omits pressed on thigh because two doubts; if so even R. Yehuda agrees tamei from stain not niddah but should be completely tehorah impossible as I explain; further per Rashi's view possible he holds like Rashba below and as Mishmeret HaBayit implies tehorah completely; further I say this is per his explanation pressed on thigh and found on thigh — in such case possible completely tehorah from two doubts; R. Yehuda agrees only stain not niddah. On ed: found on ed — also doubt as Atzat Tzaddik wrote: only with examined ed; unexamined ed — not double doubt as Rashi explained — end quote; for immediately say perhaps from makor on thigh for cannot fall there from makor as Tosafot wrote there; if so doubt from ed to thigh doubt from elsewhere on thigh; if you say from ed on thigh perhaps already on ed before — true double doubt; but if found on ed not double doubt — cannot say perhaps from thigh perhaps not from thigh and if not from thigh perhaps before already on it — Atzat Tzaddik and Ma'adanei Melech end 275a on ed also doubt perhaps from thigh that had food-blood pressed onto ed; if you say from elsewhere on ed — if even lentil and more tehorah as Shulchan Aruch wrote — end quote; this is no doubt at all — all one; immediately say perhaps from makor on ed; such is not called doubt at all as in laws of double doubt halachah 13 — examine; all the more if pressed on thigh and found more than lentil — no food-blood concern as Tosafot and Rashba in Teshuvos HaRashba; if so wonder how tehorah when ed has lentil and more; since cannot say from food on thigh and poskim explicitly if so only one doubt perhaps already on ed or from wiping — if so Torah doubt and tamei; resembles examined with unexamined ed in box seif 36 tamei with lentil and more; likewise here; all the more first view seif 35 even any amount thigh tamei for we do not attribute on thigh; unexamined ed no better than placed in box; even though from Rashba in Teshuvos HaRashba appears with pressed on thigh even with lentil and more only tamei with examined ed as his view holds like R. Yosi unexamined ed in box completely tehorah even with lentil and more; per our ruling in box tamei likewise here; all this clear.
