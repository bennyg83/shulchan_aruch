She checked herself with an ed that was not checked for her, and she wiped it on her thigh, and afterward blood was found on it — even a gris and more — she is tehorah. {Rama: And all the more so if she placed it after the bedikah in a place where there is something to attribute the ketem to, for then she is tehorah even with more than a gris (these are my words, and Beit Yosef). But if she placed it in a place where blood is not common, she is tamei if it is more than a gris.}
