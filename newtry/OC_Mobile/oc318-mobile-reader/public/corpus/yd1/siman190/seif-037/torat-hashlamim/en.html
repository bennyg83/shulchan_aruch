Reason: double doubt — perhaps ed already soiled; if you say ed was clean when woman took it — perhaps soiled on her thigh; Shach extended to challenge this ruling; wrote Rashi only deals when found on thigh; but from Tosafot Niddah 14 s.v. ulemachar implies even found on ed law is so; Shach wrote this is no double doubt — all one; immediately say perhaps from makor — end quote; such is not double doubt as laws of double doubt halachah 13 — end quote; per Kontras HaSfekot s.k. 20 appears such is properly double doubt since two sides to be lenient; even per Agur Shach cited in laws of doubt halachah 13 regarding knife nicked after slaughter: if perhaps nicked on joint and if you say not on joint perhaps on minority of sign nicked — not double doubt since cannot reverse; if you say perhaps on minority latter not on minority but on first minority — if so certainly nevelah; cannot say perhaps on latter minority and if you say not on latter perhaps on joint — both sides no issur concern — all one; therefore cannot say thus; cannot reverse; nevertheless no contradiction from Agur — specifically such case wrote both sides no issur all one because if you say not on latter minority then necessarily concerned issur earlier nicked; how return say perhaps on joint nicked; we return to first doubt. But otherwise double doubt; one may say immediately perhaps nicked on hide; nevertheless two lenient sides; per Agur's words reversed — nevertheless per Agur if not reverse like Shach there. Here there is double doubt: doubt perhaps ed soiled before woman took; if you say not soiled on thigh nevertheless perhaps ed soiled elsewhere since ed unexamined; seems here need not reverse double doubt at all — first we judge if ed soiled before woman took; then perhaps afterward soiled on woman's thigh; clear; therefore plain do not depart from Shulchan Aruch and other great acharonim' ruling.
