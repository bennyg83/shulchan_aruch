Because we are not expert in some of their names. According to what was stated in seif katan 28, it would appear that nowadays, since examination is not possible, one may be lenient when in doubt whether it is dye or blood. However, the Levush wrote that one should be stringent when in doubt, and one must say that this is like a doubt arising from lack of knowledge. See Chochmas Adam klal 85 seif katan 48 and Kuntres HaSfeikos seif katan 43.
