She checked herself with an ed that was checked for her, and she wiped it on her thigh, and on the next day blood was found on it: if it is drawn, she is tamei even with any amount; but if it is round, she is tehorah if it does not have a gris and more. And some say that even a round [stain] is tamei with any amount. {Rama: And so is the main [ruling].}
