A woman who checked herself with an ed (meaning: a rag; as in “and like a garment of witnesses all our righteousnesses” (Yeshayahu 64:5)) that was checked for her, and it was found on it even a drop like a mustard seed — whether round or drawn — she is tamei. Moreover, even if a crushed louse was found on the ketem, she is tamei. And likewise is the law when she checked with it and placed it in a box, and after an hour she checked it and found on it any blood whatsoever, whether drawn or round — she is tamei.
