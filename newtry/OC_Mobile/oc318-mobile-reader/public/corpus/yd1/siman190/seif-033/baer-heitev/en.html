In a box. Shach wrote: the examined ed above refers to this; and in Perishah he wrote it refers even to an ed that was not examined — and it is difficult, for in such a case all agree that in any amount she is tehorah, for we require the size of a lentil and more, and so all poskim — end quote; and likewise Taz. He wrote the Mechaber did not come to exclude if she placed it in an exposed place that it is tehorah if blood is found on it after time, for this is not plain in the mishnah regarding found on her ed after time that she is tamei from doubt; rather the reverse of the end teaches — namely if she placed it under a pillow or cushion or pressed it on her thigh. His explanation: if found immediately on the examined ed it is plain she is tamei; but even if the blood was not found until tomorrow she is also tamei if it was placed meanwhile in a box and not pressed on her thigh — unlike if pressed on her thigh as in the end — end quote.
