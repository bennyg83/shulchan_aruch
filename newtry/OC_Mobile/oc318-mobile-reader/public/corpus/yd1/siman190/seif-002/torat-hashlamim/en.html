Some say she is tamei. Though no disputant in Beit Yosef, he wrote "some say" since he found no posek except Teshuvos HaRashba; such is his way in Shulchan Aruch as Semak many times; appears regarding women presumed blood ceased such as pregnant and nursing — even if makor opened tehorah if found nothing; likewise provable from Maharai's words in Teshuvos HaRashba siman 247: since sensation is Torah reasoning it is like those who hold vesetot are Torah — even if examined and found tehorah — tamei as in ch. Kol HaYad — end quote; plainly in Gemara 9 that even per those who hold vesetot Torah no examination needed in pregnancy days; likewise here; examine well; all this if found nothing; but if found white or other tehorah appearances — even with sensation completely tehorah in every respect as explained above beginning siman 188.
