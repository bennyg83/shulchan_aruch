On her garments. See above siman 187 s.k. 3 in name of responsum Ateret Zekenim Panim Me'irot that only on the woman's garment did they decree on stains; but if a stain is found on the man's garment, even on his garment after relations, it has no law of stain (and my friend the Rav Gaon R. Yechiel Halevi zt"l Av Beit Din of Valkavisk showed me a supplement omitted by Panim Me'irot — a Tosefta in Niddah ch. 10: found on her son's garment sleeping beside her — she is tamei; and they did not say only on her son but on any person; they spoke of the common case) — unless he wiped himself with it, then the woman is tamei — see there.
