They did not decree regarding a young girl who has not yet reached her time to see — meaning, that she is under 12 — even if she brought two hairs. And likewise, even if she was older than 12, if they checked her and she did not bring two hairs — whether she is a virgin or whether she is one who had intercourse; and even if she already saw twice. But after she saw 3 times, she is concerned for a ketem.
