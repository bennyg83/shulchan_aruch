(Taz s.k. 29) And it is not like the language “not checked.” All the words of the Taz here are astonishing. How can one say that whenever she took it from a hidden place it is called checked and she is tamei? The Rashba is lenient even regarding a cloth with which she examined, if she took it from a hidden place but never examined it, that she is tahor, even with more than a gris. Rather, the essential law is like the Bach: where she never examined it, it is called not checked, and she is tahor with more than a gris. Even according to the Rosh in seif 36, who is stringent, that is regarding a cloth with which she wiped, but not regarding a stain; all the more according to the Rashba, who is tahor even with a cloth. Tiferet LeMoshe agreed to this.
