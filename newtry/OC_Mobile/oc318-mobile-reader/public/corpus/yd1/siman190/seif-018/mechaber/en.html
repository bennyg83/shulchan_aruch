Since ketamim are d’rabbanan, we are lenient in them, and we attribute to anything that she can attribute to. How so? She slaughtered an animal, wild animal, or bird; or she dealt with ketamim; or she sat near those dealing with them; or she passed through the butchers’ market, and blood was found on her garments — she attributes to it and she is tehorah. Even if she was wearing 3 garments one on top of another and it was found even on the bottom one, she is tehorah. But if it was found on her flesh, she does not attribute unless she has a wound on her body; then she attributes to it even on her flesh, if it is in a place that it is possible for blood to drip from there. And even if it healed, if it is possible for it to be scraped and to emit blood through rubbing, she attributes to it, even though now a scab rose over it and it is not dripping (Rashba and Mordechai and Sefer HaTerumah and Sefer Mitzvos Gadol).
