He collects the principal and the interest. For before he converted, the whole amount reverted to being like principal, since from the time it was entered as a debt it is considered as collected.
But not the interest. Even what accrued before he converted. The reason is that since he did not enter it upon him as a loan before he converted, now when he takes interest from him, etc. So wrote the Rosh. The Maggid Mishneh writes that although by strict law it is permitted, nevertheless, as a stringency they followed the entry as a debt.
