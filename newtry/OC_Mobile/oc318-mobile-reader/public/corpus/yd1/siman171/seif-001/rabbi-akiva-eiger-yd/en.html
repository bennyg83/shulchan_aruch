(Siman 171 Shach s.k. 1) That accrued from when he converted. In Gidulei Todah — doubt whether one lent for a year at ribbit of one dinar per month and did not capitalize it in the loan, and afterward gave him twelve dinars, and within that time he converted — whether he must return to him since he did not capitalize; all he gave was full ribbit and he enjoyed ribbit during the time of conversion; or since he already collected them they are not counted as ribbit for him during Judaism.
