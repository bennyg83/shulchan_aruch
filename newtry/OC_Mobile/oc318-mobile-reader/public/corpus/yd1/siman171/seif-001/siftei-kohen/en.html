He collects principal and interest. Even interest that accrued after he converted — for from the time they capitalized it into the loan it is all considered principal, as explained at the end of the preceding siman — Tosafot and the Acharonim.
And not the interest. Even interest that accrued before he converted — Rosh and Tur; and the Maggid Mishneh wrote: by law he can collect what accrued before the conversion, but the Sages enacted to follow capitalization to be stringent — end; and what he wrote "follow capitalization to be stringent" is said as a novelty; and all the more if they did not capitalize at all — it is forbidden even for what predated the conversion; and likewise Bach and Pri Megadim; and not like Perishah.
