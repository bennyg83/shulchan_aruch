If an adhesion emerged from the thin intestines or from one of the other organs whose puncture is any amount and it adhered to the wall or another place — kosher; for we do not find bubble and adhesion except in lung: {Rama: Nevertheless one must be precise, for sometimes many bubbles are found and through them the intestines were sealed, and then it is treifah; and one must examine with straw or feather whether they were sealed (also there).}
