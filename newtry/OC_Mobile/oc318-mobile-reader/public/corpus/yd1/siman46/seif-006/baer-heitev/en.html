To examine. And Taz wrote in name of Orach Chaim: even now one may rely on this; and Hagahot Orach Chaim wrote that one should cut the bubbles and see if there is moisture in them — it is lekuta and treifah; and a chicken sitting on eggs to hatch chicks — one must be stringent with it more than other fowl on account of its weakness — end of hagahot, end of Darkei Moshe; that which is written "if there is moisture it is lekuta and treifah" — we do not rule thus, for Shulchan Aruch already ruled there is no bubble except in lung; and it appears for this reason they did not bring it in Shulchan Aruch — end; and Shach explained: meaning when the flesh beneath is bad, as though it melted, as below siman 50; but when the flesh beneath is good — there is no reason at all to forbid, and so from Maharshal's words explicitly (an incident occurred with a goose that struck its belly; they opened the belly and saw the thin intestines whole and permitted it, since fowl have no rumen, even though the belly skin was punctured; and see Taz siman 49; and see Hagahot Maimoniyot 165).
