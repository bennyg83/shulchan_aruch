And one must examine with straw, etc. So Darkei Moshe wrote in name of Orach Chaim: even now one may rely on this; and hagahot of Orach Chaim wrote one should cut bubbles and see if there is moisture — lekuta and treifah; and the chicken sitting on eggs to remove chicks — more stringent than other fowl on account of weakness — end of hagahot, end of Darkei Moshe; that which is written "if there is moisture — lekuta and treifah" — we do not rule thus, for Shulchan Aruch already ruled bubble only in lung; and it appears for this reason they did not bring it in Shulchan Aruch.
