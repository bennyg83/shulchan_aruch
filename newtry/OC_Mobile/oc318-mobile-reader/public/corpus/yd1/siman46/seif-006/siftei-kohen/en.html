If adhesion, etc. See above siman 37 and siman 42 s.k. 1 in hagahah, and what is written there.
Nevertheless one must be precise, etc. And Darkei Moshe wrote in name of Hagahot Orach Chaim there: one should cut the bubbles and see if there is moisture in them — it is lekuta and treifah; and chickens sitting on eggs to hatch chicks — one must be stringent with them more than other fowl on account of their weakness — end of hagahah, end of Darkei Moshe; and so in Sefer Api Ravrevi; and so I found in Hagahot Shaarei Dura Constantinople, and Maharshal brings it in his Orach Chaim siman 96; and it appears: that which is written "if there is moisture in them it is lekuta and treifah" means: when the flesh beneath is bad, as though melted, as below siman 50; but when the flesh beneath is good — there is no reason at all to forbid; and so from Maharshal's words there explicitly — see his name.
