Be'er Heitev; and see Chavot Yair siman 54: boils in thin intestines, sometimes mucus and lekuta beneath; and my books responsum Beit Ephraim siman 17; and see later authorities: common in them bubbles — i.e., in geese — must check if beneath bubbles flesh whole — kosher; if flesh bad — treifah; and see Mishbetzot on boils in thin intestines black, etc. — see; and see responsum Noda BiYehudah I brought siman 32: do not discard thin intestines without examination — common in geese, especially summer, more than lung adhesions; and Torat Yekutiel: if boil seen in thin intestines and lost — concern treifah; he brought Chochmat Adam; and in my humble opinion everything per circumstances: if in that place many treifot from checking and bad flesh found beneath — when lost, concern; but if majority kosher after check and minority bad flesh causing treifah — appears not so stringent to treifah when lost, for bad flesh causing treifah is not certainly treifah except because not expert in flesh doctor scrapes; therefore if place accustomed to discard thin intestines without check — perhaps they already tried much that bubbles found are not treifah bubbles — therefore did not require checking afterward. And Mishbetzot: more common than lung adhesions — also in adhesions main reason common those adhesions likely to treifah; nevertheless simple: where no such custom, do not institute l'chatchila where majority kosher found; but where majority treifot found — clearly examination needed — treifah bubbles common like lung lost without check, all the more if boil seen and not checked — concern; but where treifah bubbles not common, although bubbles common — permit when lost, all the more if did not check after bubbles — for even in healthy lung we hold no examination needed, as explained beginning siman 37; and perhaps this is Taz's intent: we do not rule thus that bubble only in lung — meaning since lung lenient, no examination needed, for healthy lung itself we hold no examination; and see Mishbetzot: if worms stuck inside thin intestines part and opposite outside boil or wart — treifah — see; and my books s.k. 37 what I wrote on this.
