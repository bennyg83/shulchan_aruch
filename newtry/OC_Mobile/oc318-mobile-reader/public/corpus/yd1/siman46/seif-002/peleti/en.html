If turned over — difficult what they pressed in Gemara Zevachim chapter HaTerumot: in reality of treifah that became mixed — if he knows which — let him come take; if not, how do we know? Perhaps animal tore and intestines turned over and he returned and stitched — if so it is treifah and no one knows. And if we say tear or stitch — its mark is recognizable, even though reality contradicts; and every day they heal tears and wounds in flesh and mark is not recognizable; one may also say: here were many animals that came out and we examined them they were not turned over, flesh healed on return and became mixed with one that was turned over — its mark cannot be clarified, for all were stitched — requires study.
And the same law if they did not come out — and Ra'avyah's view is to permit; and Shach wrote: we have only Mechaber's ruling; and it is obvious who will be lenient; but it is pressed in Rambam: of 70 treifot — among them intestines came out and turned over; came out — why needed? It neither raises nor lowers; if turned over — always treifah; if not turned over — always kosher, even if outside; and forced to say the way of the matter cites they are turned over via exiting — who pressed that it should have said only "intestines turned over"? And perhaps Rambam holds: when they came out they are presumed turned over, even if we did not know they turned over — treifah; and this is the intent of "came out" — presumption intestines turned over — requires study.
But if no stitching found, etc. And one need not examine appearance of this — Ra'avyah holds turned over without exiting — kosher; but per our ruling treifah — if so, what of it they did not come out? Perhaps turned over inside body; and it must be all this when not returned by hand — no concern for turning over; but when returned by hand — there is concern, for one cannot aim as explained in Gemara regarding one who slaughtered for an ignoramus. Therefore when torn we are concerned perhaps human hands were involved and concern for turning over; not so without tear — no concern for turning over, for on return automatically there is no turning over.
