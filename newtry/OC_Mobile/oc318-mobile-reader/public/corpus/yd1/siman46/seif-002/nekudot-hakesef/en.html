(Siman 46, Taz s.k. 6) He copied Maharshal's words in his book Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 59 without attention; and I, in my book, after I proved both in majority of width and majority of length — treifah, as Shach s.k. 15 wrote — I wrote: and so Be'er Heitev; and he wrote that Maharshal also wrote thus in his hagahot to Tur: Maharshal wrote: until majority of four fingers is removed, etc. — he does not mean height alone, but in that place which is kedei tefisah we require that majority not be bored from it, whether in length or width; if bored and majority removed from it — treifah; and so I found — end; behold as I explained — examine; but in his book Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 59 he wrote: and that which says majority of four fingers removed does not mean majority of length — i.e., a strip of length two fingers and something, which is majority; rather majority of its place of adhesion was removed — four fingers; and from all four fingers according to what it is, if majority among all removed — treifah; if not, but majority of length removed or majority of width — kosher — end; and it means: if kedei tefisah is four fingers length and four fingers width — then it is not treifah if a strip removed from its length alone in majority — i.e., width something and length two fingers and something; and likewise not treifah if such strip removed from width alone (for no difference between length and width); rather we require majority removed from all those four fingers — from entire place of adhesion called kedei tefisah must be removed strip of two fingers and something on two and something; such is explanation of his words in his book — and they contradict what he wrote in his hagahot; and one may force and explain his words in hagahot as in his book — examine. And nevertheless his words in his book are not correct at all; and the essence is as written above and as later authorities: both in majority of width alone and majority of length alone — treifah; know that Rabbenu Yerucham wrote "its puncture in its majority" — this is not Maharshal's words, for "punctured" does not apply — end; from this you see he copied what is incorrect and left what is correct.
