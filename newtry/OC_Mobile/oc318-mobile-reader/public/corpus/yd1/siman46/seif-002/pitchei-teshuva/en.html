Turned over. Be'er Heitev (and see responsum Mishkenot Yaakov siman 21, who brought support for Ra'avyah and his colleagues to permit when turned over alone, all who did not come out — see his name). And see responsum Har HaCarmel part 4 siman 19, who wrote regarding hadra d'kanta that its way is to be round, and an animal was found not round but only going up and down; and to appearance of eye it seems excrement and separation within spilled and goes from one to another — treifah; for this is called "found turned over"; and even per Ra'avyah and Maharshal to permit if they did not come out, even if turned over — this is specifically turned over after its creation, for they return to their base; not so when created turned over; and even if already alive — one may not permit for this reason. And not specifically when created turned over like our case; rather even turned over inside from how they were and became entangled and adhered in fowl body or skin with strong membrane — also treifah, even per poskim who did not come out and turned over kosher — for reason they return to how they were; and here we saw strongly stuck and adhered — certainly they will remain so and end up rotting and also puncturing — see his name. (And responsum Kehalat Yaakov siman 4 answered a teacher regarding hadra d'kanta whose order of winding changed somewhat from usual way — whether this is like turned over: in essence of law of "found turned over" and did not come out, Ra'avyah who permitted was already brought; however, in change found in order recognizable from beginning of creation — it appears to all kosher, for we do not find in Talmud they mention special form for hadra d'kanta; if so, all that stand in their base from beginning of creation and did not change from base — no treifot and not called "turned over"; and he is precise in Rashi's language; and from what I wrote it appears proofs of Har HaCarmel book are not compelling at all; and I showed this hadra d'kanta to expert slaughterers here and they said there is only slight change; and sometimes greater changes are found and practice is to permit; and mostly it is great financial loss and Torah spared, etc. — see his name).
Any defect. Be'er Heitev: that which Shach wrote; and Peri Chadash disagrees; and see responsum Sha'arei Yosher part 2 siman 68, who also disagrees with Lechem Chamudos and permits — see his name. And see responsum Har HaCarmel part 1 Yoreh De'ah siman 10 on this matter; and see responsum Toldot Yitzchak siman 8. (And Be'er Heitev in name of Maharshal: chicken whose intestines exited via anus — treifah, for impossible they return to place in order via narrow opening — from this language it implies: even if still outside and not returned at all — treifah, since you do not find "returned" — automatically treifah even if only exited; and perforce it must be explained: mishnah "these are kosher in fowl" that teaches "came out" — means belly torn and they exited there; however Peri Megadim also copied this law in name of Maharshal: chicken whose intestines exited via opening of rectum and were returned — treifah, since exited via narrow place impossible return in order, etc. — end; perforce one may say: exited alone, even via anus — kosher; and mishnah deals with all cases. And I saw in Sefer Atzei Levona Maharshal's words in Peri Megadim explained for amplification he cited "and returned" — even though we see they returned on their own — treifah, all the more if not returned at all; and he brought in Sefer Pri Toar his view: returned on their own kosher even if exited via anus; and wrote Pri Toar also agrees where not returned and still outside — treifah; and mishnah deals where did not exit via anus at all; and he concludes: in any case it is a safek treifah — see his name. However, responsum Nachalat David siman 12 wrote simply: if exited alone, even via anus — kosher; and Maharshal deals only with returned; and so implied anonymously in mishnah, which counts "came out" among kosher cases; and why did mishnah not also count among "these treifot in fowl" — intestines came out via anus? And how did mishnah close to teach intestines came out kosher — lest one err it deals also with exited via anus; and why did Talmud not explain "we taught only when exited via wide place," etc. — rather certainly truth is: in all cases kosher; and it is reasonable Maharshal's words in name of Rabbi Elazar Sefardi are only when he returned by hand, and his view also as Shach; and Chamudei Daniel also: even when returned by hand kosher if he says explicitly they were not turned over; and on this Rabbi Elazar Sefardi said: in fowl, if exited via narrow place, his explicit statement does not help, for presumably when returned by hand via narrow opening certainly turned over — and so he is precise in Maharshal's language in Peri Megadim — see his name. And it appears in great financial loss one may rely on him (and slight support for his words from words of Levush: thin intestines that came out and were not punctured — kosher; he wrote: it implies even while still outside and not returned — kosher; and truly this law was not explained in Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch — "his own words" is his intent: since he does not explain and distinguish from which place they exited — therefore his view is to permit in all cases — and this is "his own words"); however, in such a case one must examine if part of thin intestines that came out did not spoil and rot — common matter. And all this when thin intestines exited; but if only intestine — kerkashta — exited, as when it pushes hard to lay eggs a little of kerkashta comes out and thin intestines are all inside — simple to permit, and no concern at all; and so Sefer Atzei Levona there and responsum Nachalat David there).
