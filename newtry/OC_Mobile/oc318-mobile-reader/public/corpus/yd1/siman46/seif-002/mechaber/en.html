If the thin intestines came outside and returned on their own — kosher; and if one returned them — if they were turned over, even though they were not punctured — treifah; for it is impossible to return them as they were after they were turned over, and it will not live; and the same law applies if they did not come out and were found turned over — treifah (Rambam chapter 6 of Hilchot Shechitah law 15). {Rama: And likewise if the intestines are found between skin and flesh and stitching is found there — there is concern they came out and were turned over; but if no stitching is found there and it is not recognizable that it was torn, and no defect was recognizable in it — kosher (Orach Chaim general rule 55 and Hagahot Shaarei Dura siman 88 in name of Ra'avyah).}
