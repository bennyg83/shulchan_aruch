Question and answer in laws of treifot siman 46. Incident: thin intestines were brought before me, length of cubit from stomach; in middle connected by wide vein to liver; vein cut near intestine; found vein's cavity open through into intestine; recognizable like puncture diagonally from vein's cavity into intestine; recognizable it was created thus from beginning of health; several people testified they know clearly this is way of all animals — thin intestines after exit from stomach some measure connected to liver by vein, not separated until cut; when cut, found hollow and punctured diagonally upward. And I saw several animals slaughtered afterward — found thus. Behold it appears exactly the case brought in Beit Yosef siman 47: incident in city Darom — extra found in intestine exiting stomach after pulled handbreadth or two, and they permitted; said all animals have one vein connected to liver, head in liver, connected to thin intestines after pulled from stomach handbreadth or two — hollow, enters intestine, etc.; only not mentioned whether vein enters open through to intestine cavity or not; nevertheless learn from his language "enters intestine" — implies into real cavity; and if vein not open through to intestine cavity but only attached outside — simple it has no law of extra intestine even if not so in all animals, for not called extra intestine unless serves entry and exit of excrement like intestine; otherwise mere protrusion, as Sefer Tur Beit Shmuel siman 47; but since he permitted only because all animals thus — implies enters intestine cavity really through and through. And that which he wrote everything there: sometimes entire vein remains in liver when liver cut from body and intestine exiting stomach partly with no mark of connection — meaning no mark it was connected; but nevertheless puncture may always be recognizable; or truly in that animal sometimes entire vein remained in liver and intestine part — without puncture; but certainly most times vein remains connected to intestine as clear from everyone's words there inside — open through into intestine cavity; otherwise no reason to declare treifah for extra intestine, as Peri Chadash when entangled in intestine — hollow to intestine only no excrement; if so, vein connected open through to intestine cavity — we have no concern; and this is not intestine puncture, for although organ with cavity created punctured is treifah — that is where not way of most animals; and in this Rashba in responsum Beit Yosef siman 46 was asked on puncture in thin intestines near stomach going on slope, etc., appears created thus; some say most animals thus; he answered treifah, do not heed those saying most animals thus, words have no substance — end. Not needed per Tzitz Eliezer in responsum brought in Tur Beit Shmuel siman 30: Rashba's reason for rejecting people — we do not hang it happened from animal's knocking; if so, certainly in our case puncture open through to vein cavity connected to liver — appears created thus, not from knocking; even per Tur Beit Shmuel: Rashba's reason rejecting people — "we never saw animals thus at all, all the more most" — end; then our case where we saw and heard vein and puncture in nearly all our animals, how forbid for thin intestines punctured — behold most animals are not treifot, for we hold majority animals are kosher; perforce this puncture is not included in thin intestines punctured; and so Tur Beit Shmuel per Rashba: if certainly most animals thus — we must say kosher. And Yeshuot Yaakov on everything there: requires great examination of butchers' assembly who understand matter thoroughly if exactly like their words — Rav said only regarding extra intestine if exactly like extra intestine — i.e., if excrement in it requires examination; but if not like intestine but like vein as way of all animals forever — we never saw nor heard examination needed; if so, would need examine and assemble butchers in all animals where vein found; and Yeshuot Yaakov perhaps said only l'chatchila; and so language "requires" as Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 87; but if already cut or lost and impossible to clarify if exactly like case there — permit in great financial loss at least; for we hold siman 50: anything common to permit more than forbid — even if certainly while alive we hang to permit; and siman 52 s.k. 6: discoloration found on organ and we do not know it fell in fire — we hang discoloration to something else like illness, since falling in fire uncommon; likewise puncture or extra in intestine uncommon and vein common — we hang puncture or extra in place intestine connected to liver to what is common in most animals; and he testified vein itself never one way: sometimes entirely in liver, implying need not cut from intestine; sometimes head in intestine; sometimes length three fingers or more; sometimes connection place finger or more; sometimes very thin — perforce puncture also varies — wider when vein larger, varies by animal size; certainly not defect that sometimes puncture wider, sometimes less. And even in Sefer Shekel: if treifot from birth would appear to permit if lived 12 months or gave birth — since most treifot do not live or bear, and this animal lived and bore — implies not treifah puncture but way of all animals; if treifah puncture — would be born thus and not live and bear; and although certainly treifah we do not permit from living and bearing — because we see it is from minority that live; but when we may hang not treifah — we hang well, and no worse than safek treifah permitted after 12 months; and so Tur Beit Shmuel permitted chickens with protrusions on skull, etc.
