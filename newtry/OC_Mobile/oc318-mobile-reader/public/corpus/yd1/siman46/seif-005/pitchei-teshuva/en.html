Into cavity of abdomen. Be'er Heitev end s.k. 10 in name of Peri Chadash; and it appears he means: in that which is written "anything not stuck to thighs," etc. — in place where there is adhesion to thighs but puncture in place not stuck, such as near omasum or udder; for chalchulit is not stuck everywhere, only on side near spine; but on other side near rectum, or in cow near its udder — not attached at all; and see responsum Beit Ephraim part 1 Yoreh De'ah siman 26: thus understood the questioner, and he answered: this is error — whatever reached place of adhesion between thighs is always kosher; only there is place at end of body cavity and place of adhesion — in this Ran requires majority, and Peri Chadash disagrees and holds its law is also with any amount — see his name. (And Shulchan Aruch wrote: but if punctured in place stuck between thighs — kosher; see Rashi Piskei Avodah Zarah 50a s.v. since thighs hold it up, who wrote: they seal it, for behold it is attached to them — end; perforce it appears: if thighs opposite were also punctured — treifah, as written there in Gemara 48a regarding lung punctured and wall sealed it, and regarding bile punctured and liver sealed it; and so Shach above siman 42 s.k. 1. However, one may say: here reason for treifah in chalchulit punctured is because excrement will fall into body cavity; therefore in place of adhesion kosher that it will not fall into body, as Rashi explains there s.v. in its majority; and in Rashba's comment there and Shach above s.k. 3 — if so, also when thighs opposite punctured excrement will not fall into body since attached around puncture — requires study).
