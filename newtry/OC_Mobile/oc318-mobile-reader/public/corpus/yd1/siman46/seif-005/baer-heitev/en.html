And Shach wrote: thus is the custom if not in great financial loss (and Peri Chadash disagrees; and according to the lenient view, until the majority of its width is removed — meaning the majority of the kedei tefisah between majority of length or width; and Maharshal wrote: specifically the majority among all — meaning from length and from width; and if the majority of length alone was removed, or majority of width alone — kosher; see Shach. Kerkashta found punctured from inside and at the place of the puncture like a protrusion going outside, and the puncture entered into that thickness but was not open through at all; and it appears the skin that was on the outside was the main skin of the kerkashta, its color like skin color, and all the fat on it peeled off and there was no mark at all — only whole skin — and they permitted it in practice (Peri Chadash in name of Radbaz). And that which the Mechaber wrote "in what case" — when punctured into cavity of abdomen — Peri Chadash wrote: I say even if not toward cavity of abdomen, anything not stuck to thighs is called "not in place of adhesion." (Rokeach wrote: in ox — adhesion two fingers; in goat and lamb one finger — kosher (Be'er Heitev)).
