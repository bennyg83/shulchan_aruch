Four fingers, etc. This is the explanation of betada de'tora in the Talmud; and there are poskim who explain betada de'tora as two fingers, and he brings them in Beit Yosef; and so below siman 56 seif 5 in name of "some say" — see his name.
And on a ewe, etc. Ra'avan siman 247 wrote: and on a ewe — meaning a sheep — according to the measure of the outer link of the big finger, i.e., two fingers — end; and in Rokeach siman 285: in ox four fingers in adhesion to puncture, and two for ewe, and one for kid and lamb — kosher; but not in place of adhesion — with any amount treifah — perforce.
Some forbid, etc. In Sefer Lechem 162a he ruled to be lenient and wrote: although in Shulchan Aruch he did not decide — this is because Beit Yosef's words from Rabbi Yerucham (Netiv 5 end letter 10) slipped his mind, who wrote that the essence is like the lenient view, and we must follow the last decisors who are Tur and Rabbi Yerucham — end; and in my humble opinion: Beit Yosef also saw Rabbi Yerucham's words and nevertheless did not decide to be lenient, because Tur and Rabbi Yerucham are disciples of Rosh who always follow Rosh's method — and their decision is no proof; and so Rashba in Mishmeret HaBayit 36 forbids; and so Bach; and so appears Rokeach's view whom I brought in the s.k. before this; however, in place of great financial loss one may permit until its majority, as also Ra'ah in Sefer Beit Hashem there, and so Ran's view is clear per Bach's explanation (except Bach emended Ran's words — in my books I wrote no emendation is needed — see his name); and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 59.
Majority of its width, etc. Tur's language: until the majority of the four fingers is removed; and as Beit Yosef in name of Mahari ibn Chaviv: do not err in that which is written "kosher until majority of four fingers is removed" and think it means majority of length; rather the truth is: in all four fingers — which is kedei tefisah — punctured with any amount kosher; punctured in majority of width treifah; and it means until majority is removed in the place called tefisah, which is four fingers; and so Rabbenu Yerucham in Sefer Adam — end; and it means: do not err to say: since it writes "majority of four fingers" and did not write anonymously, it means it is not treifah by puncture in majority of width but specifically in majority of length of four fingers — i.e., puncture of two fingers and something along length; for if it meant also by majority of width it is treifah, it should have said "until majority of that kedei tefisah is removed," for sometimes majority of width is less or more than two fingers and something; rather the truth is: also by majority of width it is treifah; and that which it says "majority of four fingers" means of that kedei tefisah called four fingers — wherever those four fingers were punctured, i.e., that kedei tefisah, whether in length or width — treifah; and so Rabbi Yerucham Netiv 5 end letter 10, and these are his words: and Rabbi Yonah explained its puncture in its majority, and so is the essence; and per this there are three distinctions: above adhesion of thighs — puncture with any amount; in adhesion of thighs — even if removed, kosher, provided kedei tefisah remains; and that kedei tefisah itself — puncture in its majority — end; behold he did not mention language of four fingers so you not explain in length, but wrote language of that kedei tefisah and wrote anonymously "its puncture in its majority" — implies wherever punctured in its majority — treifah, whether in length or width; and all this is clear — I did not need to elaborate except because some understood Mahari ibn Chaviv's words incorrectly, and also Perishah's words are not correct — see his name; and examine; also in Sefer Lechem there he wrote: and I did not see Netiv 5 end letter 11 who says this is he who explains majority of width — end; and per my words it is impossible to explain Rabbenu Yerucham's words in any way that he means specifically majority of length — clear; and so Darkei Moshe and Bach in name of Mahari ibn Chaviv: treifah even in majority of width, and we do not require majority of length.
