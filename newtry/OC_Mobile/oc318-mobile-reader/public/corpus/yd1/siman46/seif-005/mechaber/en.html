Chalchulit — and it is the last intestine that is straight without bend and excrement exits from it — if punctured in any amount — treifah, like other intestines; in what case is this said? When punctured into the cavity of the abdomen; but if punctured in the place where it is stuck between the thighs — kosher; and even if the entire place stuck between the thighs was removed — kosher, provided something remains of it like the fill of four fingers on a large ox; and on a ewe according to this measure — large according to its size and small according to its smallness; and if in those four fingers it was punctured — some forbid with any amount and some permit until the majority of its width is removed.
