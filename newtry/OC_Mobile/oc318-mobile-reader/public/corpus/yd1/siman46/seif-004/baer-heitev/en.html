Punctured. And Shach wrote in name of several poskim: if the needle lies along its length — one may be lenient in great financial loss; for specifically regarding bile do we concern ourselves, since it has no place to exit and rests within it always — not so regarding thin intestines. And Taz wrote: specifically if the needle lies and is not stuck within them; but if it made some puncture — even according to Rama siman 48 seif 7 regarding melting, that in great financial loss one may permit puncture on one side — here it is stricter, since Ran forbids even when lying and not stuck at all; one may not be lenient so much, since we saw it began to puncture (and if a drop of blood is found outside — even if found — one must declare treifah (Peri Chadash)). Animal in whose thin intestines red spots are found on the side where they eat a grass called kallech — one may permit in Jewish animals (responsum of Mahari Weil).
