Needle found in thin intestines. We are concerned as we are concerned regarding bile: since they travel in a circuit and their membrane is soft — there is concern perhaps food and drink pressed it and it punctured; and with a puncture of any amount they have no examination (as below siman 51) — end, Ran; and in Darkei Moshe he wrote: and it appears to me that one who permits regarding bile (they are Tosafot, Rosh, and Mordechai, as explained in Beit Yosef siman 42 — see his name; however, we hold to forbid regarding bile, as above s.k. 42 — see his name) — also permits here; and so Shaarei Dura siman 93 in hagahah in name of Rabbi Zerachiah; and Orach Chaim general rule 51 law 22: an incident occurred and a needle was found along the wall of the thin intestines and they did not puncture outward, and the elders of the generation permitted it — for its way is to enter via the cavity of the esophagus — end; and in hagahah in Orach Chaim there in name of geonim: kosher if found in thin intestines — anything that is not stuck in the wall of the thin intestines; however, Orach Chaim's view requires study: regarding bile he wrote they are accustomed to declare treifah, and here he permits — end, Darkei Moshe. And it appears to me that Orach Chaim's view is to distinguish: specifically regarding bile we are concerned, since it has no place to exit but rests in bile always — not so regarding thin intestines; and this distinction is in Bach; and he also brings Semak who permits and ruled to permit in great financial loss; and so Maharam of Tikotin in gloss on Ran: it appears to permit in thin intestines when found along their length.
