Were punctured — the same applies if they melted, or the flesh is bad that the doctor scrapes off.
Pure fat — and even a needle stuck — fat seals it; Shach; and see Peleti — there is room to doubt his proof; and a teacher must examine well.
Seals — and the same applies to flesh adjacent to it in its product it seals — to exclude a bone; boils were found in esophagus and it is in that place stuck to flesh, and in flesh there is no lekuta — for the puncture is in the flesh, the flesh protects.
All fat — and Taz wrote; but in spleen it does not seal, since we require thickness of dinar; and see Peleti what he wrote whether this exists in reality.
Tame — and if punctured at head of intestine that requires scraping — whether fat seals — see below siman 84.
At place on heart — see above siman 40 that fat on heart also does not seal per Rambam and Bahag.
Of another limb — Taz's view: flesh seals even of another limb; and see Peleti whom I wrote — on the contrary, even Rambam who holds fat seals even of another limb admits regarding flesh.
