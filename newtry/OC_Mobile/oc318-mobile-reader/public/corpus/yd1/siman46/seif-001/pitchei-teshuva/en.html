Kosher. Be'er Heitev: that which Shach wrote — even needle stuck in puncture; and see responsum Beit Ephraim Yoreh De'ah part 1 siman 23; and see what I wrote siman 49 s.k. 3.
Pure fat. Be'er Heitev: the same applies to flesh, etc.; and see responsum Noda BiYehudah part 1 Yoreh De'ah siman 9, who wrote: nevertheless, if esophagus was punctured near the head where it is stuck to flesh — treifah, and flesh does not seal, because it expanded and hardened and does not remain stuck in its place; as we say regarding puncture not opposite puncture above siman 33 seif 3 — see his name; and see Peri Megadim; and see further in Noda BiYehudah siman 5 what he divided against Taz on this.
