(Siman 46 seif 1) Pure fat seals — kosher. Later authorities wrote: rosh ma'aya be'emtza — although we do not eat it, as siman 64 seif 16 — nevertheless it seals. And the same per geonim' explanation that it is end of intestine, as siman 64. And it is not clear, for explicitly in Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba (daf 38) tame fat does not seal — such as rosh ma'aya be'emtza does not seal; and we may not be lenient against Rashba without proof. And that which later authorities wrote "the same applies end of intestine per geonim" — I wonder greatly: even if so, that it is pure fat — nevertheless it does not seal, since it is made like a hat, as in Talmud and poskim; and Shulchan Aruch this seif: fat on kerkashta, although pure, does not seal. And I happened to have Mishneh LeMelech on Rambam chapter 13 of Hilchot Shegagot and found there the Rav elaborated and challenged: per geonim rosh ma'aya is end of intestine connected to kerkashta but not kerkashta itself; and it does not appear, for Rashba, Beit Yosef, Shulchan Aruch, and Semak do not imply so at all — for they wrote "remove a little from kerkashta"; and so Rambam chapter 7 Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot law 9: some geonim say head of intestine that must be scraped is intestine from which excrement exits, which is end of intestine.
And that which compelled Mishneh LeMelech to say so — because he challenged geonim' explanation that rosh ma'aya is head of kerkashta — if so, why need Talmud and poskim to say fat of kerkashta does not seal because made like hat — behold without this it is tame fat? And therefore he wrote: per them rosh ma'aya is end of thin intestines connected to kerkashta, not kerkashta; and I did not get to bottom of his view — he contradicts what he wrote at beginning there to resolve this per hagahah siman 64 seif 16: rosh ma'aya is forbidden only the membrane on it, not fat under membrane stuck to ma'aya; if so, we are challenged: even if we say it is kerkashta as poskim imply; and without this also challenged: because of tame fat he need not say it does not seal but rosh ma'aya; rather because made like hat the whole does not seal; and so in Kiryat Sefer. Also what astonished Mishneh LeMelech about Rashba's view — I omitted Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba whom I brought.
Again I happened upon Sefer Shev Yaakov siman 26, who wrote exactly as I wrote: rosh ma'aya — whether per commentators that it is beginning of exit from stomach, or per geonim that it is kerkashta — does not seal; and the reason is not equal: at beginning of exit from stomach the reason is tame fat, for even fat under membrane is forbidden; see there, where he elaborated to challenge hagahah siman 64; and at end of ma'aya which is kerkashta the reason is made like hat — even pure fat under membrane; and it is exactly as siman 64.
See Noda BiYehudah Mahadura Tinyana siman 21 regarding puncture found in ma'aya near stomach going on slope, coming from liver simfonot, found so in several animals; butchers say most animals thus; he answered: coincidence would not persist and not recognize place and form; and since this puncture is one form on slope in one place near stomach in several animals — simple it is vein from thin intestines to liver; doctors say its function is liver throws bile via this vein to intestines and thence to stomach to digest; and although Rashba in responsum declared treifah and did not believe butchers in his days — goodness of vein not yet known; therefore nature of puncture at beginning of creation brings into thin intestines drop of bile, but taking out — no nature to expel excrement outward into cavity; therefore when we may hang vein tore at liver removal — kosher; but if certainly tore while alive — forbid; and simple no obligation to search this puncture.
