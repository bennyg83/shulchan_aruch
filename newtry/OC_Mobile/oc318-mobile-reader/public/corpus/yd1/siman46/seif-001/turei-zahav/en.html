And if pure fat seals. The same applies to flesh that seals, as Rambam wrote whom I brought siman 48 seif 2; and Orach Chaim wrote general rule 52: and specifically regarding something whose puncture is any amount; but regarding something that requires a measure for puncture and for what may be lacking — such as puncture of spleen — sealing with fat or flesh does not help — end; and Darkei Moshe brought this in the same language: as with spleen — it does not seal if punctured in its supports, for there is also for its puncture a measure that must be open through — perforce; and he wrote on this in Darkei Moshe: and I do not know what difference there is between puncture of spleen and other punctures, for all need to be open through or punctured into cavity when there is cavity, which is as open through, as Tosafot Piskei Avodah Zarah wrote — end. And I am astonished at him that he did not get to the bottom of Orach Chaim's view, for his words are correct: in every open puncture whose prohibition is any amount — sealing helps if fat or flesh sealed it; for in anything that is not open through, it suffices — as we find in crop that even fat seals it kosher — not so spleen, where kosher is not in what is not open through and remains whole with any amount above; rather there must remain a large part in its supports above — i.e., like thickness of dinar — there sealing with fat or flesh does not help except from spleen flesh itself there must remain like thickness of dinar; and this is what Orach Chaim wrote per Rama's version: the measure that must be open through means there is a measure for what must remain, and without that measure it is as open through; and so for practical law in spleen sealing like thickness of dinar does not help at all — and this is simple in my humble opinion.
