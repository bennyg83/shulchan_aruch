The laws of treifot in the thin intestines. In it are 6 seifim. If the thin intestines were punctured — treifah; and even the mucous membrane (leicha) within them — its sealing is not sealing; and even if it is stuck to them greatly so that it cannot be removed except with difficulty; and if pure fat seals — kosher; for all pure fat seals; tame fat does not seal, except at the place on the heart; and fat on top of the kerkashta (meaning: the intestine attached to the opening of the anus) — even though they are pure, they do not seal; and wild animal fat opposite domestic animal, which is forbidden in domestic animal — it does not seal; and fowl — all its fat seals: {Rama: And it seals only on that limb to which the fat is stuck in its product; but if fat seals a puncture of another limb — it is not called sealing and it is treifah (Beit Yosef in name of Tosafot, Rosh, and Rashba).}
