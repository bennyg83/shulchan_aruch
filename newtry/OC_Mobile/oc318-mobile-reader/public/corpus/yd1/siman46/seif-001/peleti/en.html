And if pure fat seals. Shach s.k. 2 wrote from the incident Tosafot brought: there was a needle in the crop and fat sealed it — kosher; it implies even with a needle pure fat seals — and so later authorities. However, for law there is a response: Tosafot follow their method that they explain regarding esophagus "perhaps healthy, perhaps punctured," and they also hold even in melting that puncture by needle — nevertheless the puncture must be through and through; and even regarding fat we say it seals; but per Rambam and Mechaber above siman 33 we are concerned perhaps a membrane rose and healed; and so Rashi's view in melting, since its membrane is soft as Rosh wrote — if so, all the more so regarding fat, which is soft, we are concerned it punctured and healed — we are concerned perhaps healthy; and especially with needle stuck; and perhaps one may say: since poskim did not mention it, they hold regarding fat — since it is soft and tends to every side — it does not heal in a puncture, for it leaves the puncture and spreads to the sides — requires study. And behold per Rambam flesh also seals — see Lechem Mishneh and Beit Yosef; and one may say: flesh that Rambam cited and did not state generally — to exclude bone that does not seal, as they said regarding lung punctured and wall sealed it — specifically adjacent in flesh, not in bones; if so, we have room for Shach's words above siman 33: thorn in esophagus — we are concerned perhaps healthy, meaning membrane rose and healed; and he challenged: why is this needed — without esophagus it has no external examination? And see what I wrote siman 33 at length. However, per what I wrote it works well: esophagus on one side stuck to flesh and animal skin very attached as is known — if so, what of it? If entire esophagus was punctured, flesh and skin stuck to it in its product seal it; and in skin and flesh there is examination if there is a puncture — for even in fat there is examination, all the more so in skin and flesh; and one may say: truly kosher; and that which we say animal cannot be slaughtered — esophagus has no external examination, and we do not examine skin and flesh — it deals where flesh and skin tore like in Gemara incident of congealed blood, and skin and flesh tore and it died; but per this the Gemara's challenge is difficult: how did Rabbi Meir eat the meat — perhaps he examined skin and flesh? And one may say: esophagus is not stuck to flesh for most of it, only part; if so, must slaughter most of esophagus; and where not stuck must also slaughter; and perhaps it punctured there and has no examination; but if by chance one side — although no examination — nevertheless skin has examination; and this is if the reason is because it has no examination — not so if we say perhaps it punctured and healed, but in skin there is concern and skin examination does not help; and so there is great difference between the explanations; and see what I wrote siman 33 whether we are concerned in other treifot besides esophagus puncture; and in any case in those geese that feed and white boils are found on inner skin and scab as known — without this many opinions permit; and see above siman 33 — Taz leans if it is in esophagus in place connected to skin and flesh and they are whole — one may be lenient, since most commentators did not explain "perhaps healed" but only "perhaps punctured" — examine.
Kosher — Taz and later authorities brought Orach Chaim's view: in spleen sealing with flesh and fat does not help, for in spleen itself we do not require open through, and if punctured and nothing remains like thickness of dinar — treifah; if so, what helps sealing with flesh or fat? It is simple — except I did not know the reality where this applies in spleen; behold on side of rumen in esophagus no puncture disqualifies; on thick side it is stuck to rumen and there is tame fat between rumen and spleen — whence comes pure fat to seal? There is neither flesh nor pure fat; and on second side toward cavity of body — also no pure fat nor flesh from its product, for it adhered elsewhere; in esophagus no sealing — and far from me is this reality; God forbid they toiled in vain on something not discussed — empty peppering. And one may say: there are here two spleens, and Orach Chaim holds like Ra'avan, as written regarding two spleens: in whichever it happens, even if all removed — kosher; if so, there is reality that second spleen is stuck near kosher fat near kanta, etc., punctured and fat seals it; and regarding this spleen it is called baraita "here it was and here it is found," and it teaches us: since its measure is dinar, pure fat does not seal — end. And perhaps Orach Chaim's view is like Ra'ah that spleen in fowl is also treifah; but certainly it requires gold dinar measure as above; if so, there it works well that fat seals, for in fowl all is kosher — therefore he says "not" — examine.
