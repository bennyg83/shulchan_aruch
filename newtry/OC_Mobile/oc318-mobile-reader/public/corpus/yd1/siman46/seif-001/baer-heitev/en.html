Sealing. Shach wrote: the same applies if a membrane rose and sealed — for in all punctured organs the membrane does not seal (and the same if the thin intestines melted — all the more so, and all the more so when much is found, as is common in fowl where the mucous membrane within the thin intestines was dissolved and a defect appears on the skin of the thin intestines — that is treifah (Beit Yosef)).
Kosher. Shach wrote in name of Orach Chaim: specifically regarding something whose puncture is any amount; but regarding something that requires a measure for its puncture and for what may be lacking — such as puncture of the spleen, which must remain like the thickness of a gold dinar — if so, when punctured, sealing does not help, lest it not seal as much as the thickness of a gold dinar. It also appears from the poskim that even a needle stuck in the puncture and pure fat seals it — kosher; for they wrote in name of Rabbi Yitzchak regarding the crop: a needle was found in it, the pocket was punctured and also its flesh, only fat sealed above — and he permitted.
Pure. Shach wrote in name of Darkei Moshe: flesh also seals; however, specifically flesh adjacent to it in its product — otherwise it is no better than fat, which does not seal in such a case, as the Rav wrote in the hagahah; and Taz disagrees and rules that flesh seals even from another limb, with proof from lung that was punctured and the wall sealed it — kosher by Talmudic law — end; (the intestine adjacent to the stomach, which is the beginning of the thin intestines — even though in siman 64 seif 15 it is written that the fat on it is forbidden for the length of a cubit — nevertheless regarding puncture we rely on some geonim who explained that it is at the end of the intestines, and for us at least it certainly seals in any case (Sha'arei Ephraim siman 62); and Peri Chadash wrote on this and it is well written; however, if there is a puncture in the intestine adjacent to the stomach and also at the end of the intestines, and fat seals them — treifah, for necessarily one of them does not seal).
The heart. And it is tarfasha deleva in siman 40; and the reason is that it is not well attached.
They seal. Because they are not well attached — and this is the reason for tame fat (and see above siman 40, that even fat of the heart does not seal).
