He should mix with them. See Sha'ar HaMelekh at the end of chapter 6 of Hilkhot Berakhot, who brings the Rambam, Hilkhot Tum'at Okhlin chapter 16 law 4: fruit juices mixed with the seven liquids follow the majority; if any amount of water is mixed in, the whole becomes liquid, conveys liquid tumah, and renders susceptible. The Raavad there objects and writes that even in a mixture with water, a majority is required; see there. Therefore he wonders at the Mechaber here, who wrote only that one of the seven liquids must be mixed with them, and did not explain that with other liquids besides water, one must mix in a majority for them to render susceptible; see there. However, one may say there is a distinction: if one mixes the liquids with one another, we follow the majority. But if he first kneaded with fruit juice and afterward added one of the seven liquids, nullification does not apply, and even any amount renders it susceptible. Although the Shach wrote that here it specifically means mixing before the obligation of challah takes effect, one may still say it speaks after he poured in the fruit juice but before the flour was well mixed.
