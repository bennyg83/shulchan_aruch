And baked it, etc. The Bach wrote that even if he did not bake it, but at any time reconsidered and decided to bake part of it, whether he took from it and baked it or not, the whole becomes obligated through him. He concludes that in practice one should be precise: ab initio he should separate challah and afterward take that small portion and bake it. However, if he first took that small portion and baked it, he must also afterward separate challah from the whole dough, but he should place that baked dough next to the dough and separate from it and from the dough, etc.; see there at length.
