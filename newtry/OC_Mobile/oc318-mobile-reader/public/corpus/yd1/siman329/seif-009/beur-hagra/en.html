A dough, etc. Mishnah chapter 2. Although the Rosh rejected it from halakhah in his commentary and rulings because of the Yerushalmi brought by the Rash there, where Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi said that it is Rabbi Elazar ben Yehudah of Bartota's view, etc., and we rule like Rabbi Akiva over his colleague, and Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi also holds so, and so he wrote in his responsa to the Rashba, the Rashba answered him that one should not reject an anonymous Mishnah, and several amoraim there hold like the Mishnah; see there.
