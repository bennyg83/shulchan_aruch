It is exempt. See Be'er Heitev in the name of the Shach. The Darkhei Moshe s.k. 2 wrote: the Mordechai in chapter Kol Sha'ah wrote that according to Rabbeinu Tam, dough called lokshen, kreplach, or pastry is obligated in challah, while according to those who disagree with him it is exempt. However, from the commentary of Rabbeinu Yonah in chapter Keitzad Mevarchin it implies that even according to Rabbeinu Tam, lokshen dough is exempt. See the responsa of my grandfather, Panim Me'irot I siman 308, in the middle of the responsum, who wrote that nothing is implied from Rabbeinu Yonah's commentary there. Therefore a God-fearing person should separate without a blessing in order to satisfy Rabbeinu Tam's view; see there.
