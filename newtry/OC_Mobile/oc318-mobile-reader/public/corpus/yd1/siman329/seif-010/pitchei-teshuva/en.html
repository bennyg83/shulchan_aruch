See Shaar HaMelech, end of chapter 6 of Hilchot Berachot, who cites the Rambam, Hilchot Tumat Ochlin 16:4: when fruit juices are mixed with the seven liquids, the majority determines their status, but if they are mixed with even the smallest amount of water, the whole mixture is deemed liquid for purposes of tumah and susceptibility. The Raavad disagrees and requires a majority even when water is mixed in. Shaar HaMelech therefore questions the Mechaber here for stating simply that one need only mix in one of the seven liquids, without clarifying that for liquids other than water a majority would be needed. One can answer that there is a distinction between blending the liquids together first, where nullification follows the majority, and kneading initially with fruit juice and then adding one of the seven liquids, where the later addition is not nullified and even a small amount renders the dough susceptible. Although the Shach says that here the added liquid must be mixed in before the challah obligation takes effect, it can still refer to a point after the fruit juice was poured in but before the flour was fully blended.
