One should be careful not to knead in fruit juice that is not among the seven beverages unless he mixes with them one of the seven beverages (Semak siman 218). {Rama: And see in Orach Chayim siman 298 where I explained what the seven beverages are; and if he transgressed and kneaded without the seven beverages he should not burn the challah but give it to a minor kohen (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag), as explained above siman 322.}
