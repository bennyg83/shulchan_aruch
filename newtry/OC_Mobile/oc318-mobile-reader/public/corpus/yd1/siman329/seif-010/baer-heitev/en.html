Fruit liquids. Or egg liquid. Although the Mechaber rules below that such dough is obligated in challah, it is unclear what should be done with that challah. Although we are all impure through contact with the dead, this challah remains pure because fruit liquids do not render food susceptible to impurity. It may not be burned, and it cannot be eaten by most people because an impure person may not eat pure terumah. It would therefore have to be given to a minor kohen. One should consequently not knead such dough initially unless one of the seven liquids is mixed into it, so that it becomes susceptible to impurity and the challah may be burned. This liquid must be added while kneading, before the obligation of challah takes effect. After the dough has been kneaded and become obligated, no other liquid should be added. Shach. See siman 130.
