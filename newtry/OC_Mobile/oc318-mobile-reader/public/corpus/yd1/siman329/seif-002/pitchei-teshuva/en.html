It is obligated in challah. See Be'er Heitev in the name of the Shach. Regarding what he wrote, "or if he has dough from which challah was not separated," etc., see Levushei Serad, who wrote that the Shach's words here require study. They imply that he means dough that has the measure for challah, from which he should separate challah with a blessing to exempt both; but this is incorrect, for perhaps this dough is not obligated in challah, and it would be separating from what is obligated for what is exempt. He therefore concludes that it is forbidden to separate for it from another dough, unless he has a dough that lacks the measure for challah; then he may combine it with this dough and separate challah without a blessing from whichever one he wishes. In the Yoreh De'ah margin of my grandfather, the Gaon Rabbi N. Hertz z"l, he wrote that the Rosh's exemption applies only to fruit juice other than the seven liquids; but with the seven liquids it is certainly obligated, as implied by the Yerushalmi, the Rosh, and Rashba responsa siman 464. The Beit Hillel was not precise. Levushei Serad too followed those words of the Beit Hillel, and at first glance the Tur's language also sounds this way, but in truth it is not so, as is clear to one who studies the Beit Yosef and the Yerushalmi there. Thus the Tur's phrase, "and my father the Rosh z"l was uncertain in the matter," refers not to wine or honey but only to egg water. See Be'er HaGolah note 30 and examine carefully. So too my grandfather wrote in Panim Me'irot responsa I siman 68, that dough kneaded with honey is obligated in challah according to everyone, since it is one of the seven liquids. As for the statement above in seif 1 that honey-cakes are exempt from challah, and Bartenura explained this as dough kneaded with honey, there it speaks where the dough was initially soft. But if it was initially thick and remained thick, it is obligated in challah. Where the dough is made with sugar, one should separate without a blessing; see there. So too Beit Ephraim responsa, Orach Chayim simanim 11-12, wrote, and the common practice of taking challah from lekach without a blessing is improper; see there.
