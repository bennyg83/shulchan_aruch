One who makes, etc. There in Pesachim. According to Rabbenu Tam this is specifically where its mixture is soft, but according to the Rash and Rambam it applies even to a full dough, as above; this is why it says “one who makes,” etc.
And likewise roasted grain, etc.; and roasted grain, etc. Yerushalmi chapter 1: roasted flour that he made into dough is obligated in challah. Rabbi Yose in the name of Rabbi Yochanan said: this is where he baked it.
