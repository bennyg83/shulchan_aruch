Many authorities disagree and hold that a thick dough is obligated even if kneaded for cooking, because the obligation arises immediately upon kneading. One should therefore separate challah without a blessing, or bake a small portion so that the entire dough becomes obligated, as in the following seif and as Rosh, Tur, and other authorities write in the name of Maharam.
