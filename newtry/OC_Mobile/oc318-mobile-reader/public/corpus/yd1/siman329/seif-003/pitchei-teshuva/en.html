Exempt. See Baer Heitev in the name of Shach; and he wrote in Darkhei Moshe s.k. 2, and these are his words: the Mordechai wrote in chapter Kol Sha'ah that according to Rabbeinu Tam, dough they call lokshen or kreplach or pashtida is liable in challah, and according to those who dispute him it is exempt; however, from Rabbeinu Yonah's commentary in chapter Keitzad Mevarchin it appears that even according to Rabbeinu Tam, lokshen dough is exempt, end of his words. And see the responsum of my grandfather, Panim Me'irot volume 1 siman 308, in the middle of the responsum, who wrote that in Rabbeinu Yonah's commentary there nothing of the sort appears; therefore a God-fearing person should separate without a berachah, to fulfill Rabbeinu Tam's view; see there.
