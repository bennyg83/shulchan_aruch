Exempt. Since she kneaded it intending to cook it, the obligation of challah did not take effect, for the law follows her intention. Many authorities disagree and maintain that when the dough is thick, the obligation takes effect immediately upon kneading even if she intended to cook it. One should therefore be stringent and separate challah without a blessing, or bake a small portion, thereby obligating the entire dough in challah. Shach.
