(There, seif 4) A doubtful bekhor. This is astonishing to me. According to the sugya in Niddah, chapter 3, it is clear that although most offspring are proper, nevertheless since most animals discharge muck one day before birth and this one did not discharge, the majority is weakened. If so, this applies specifically where it emerged and did not linger. The Shulchan Aruch and later authorities should have distinguished this, and it requires great study. I now saw that Sidrei Taharah in his novellae also stood on this point.
