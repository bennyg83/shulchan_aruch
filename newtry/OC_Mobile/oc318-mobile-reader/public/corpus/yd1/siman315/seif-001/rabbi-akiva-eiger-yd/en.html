(There, seif 4) A doubtful firstborn. This is very difficult to me. According to the sugya in Niddah, chapter 3, it is clear that although most births are viable, since most animals discharge fluid one day before giving birth and this one did not, the majority presumption is weakened. But that applies specifically where it emerged and did not remain. If it remained, we say it previously gave birth to a viable offspring and discharged fluid beforehand, and the subsequent offspring is certainly ordinary. Tosafot state this explicitly. Shulchan Aruch and the later authorities should therefore have distinguished these cases. This requires very great study. I later saw that Sidrei Taharah also raised this point in his chiddushim.
