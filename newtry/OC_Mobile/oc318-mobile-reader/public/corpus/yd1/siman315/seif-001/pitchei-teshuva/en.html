A ewe that gave birth. See the responsa of my grandfather, Panim Me'irot III siman 28, who wondered why they did not bring the law of a ewe born from a ewe born from a goat, where the Sages hold that we follow the mother of the mother and this is a nidmeh, and the Rosh ruled like the Sages. Likewise regarding a ewe born from a goat born from a ewe, Rabbi Meir holds that its sheep-status has returned, while the Sages hold that it has not returned, and the Rosh ruled like the Sages. The Rambam too is difficult, for he omitted this law; and although the Beit Yosef gave a reason for the matter, his reason is not sound. In any case the Mechaber should have brought the Rosh's opinion as a "some say," and he leaves the matter requiring study; see there.
