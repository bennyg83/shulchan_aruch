A small animal that miscarried a dissolution—that is, the fetus melted and the discharge emerged—is exempt from bechorah; and one must show it to a wise shepherd who will see whether it is fetal discharge and offspring. And a large animal that discharged a placenta is exempt from bechorah; and the placenta itself has no sanctity and it is permitted to feed it to dogs.
