A ewe that gave birth in the form of a goat, or a goat that gave birth in the form of a ewe—is exempt from bechorah, as it is said "only the firstborn ox"—until it itself is an ox and its firstborn is an ox. And if it had some of its mother's signs, behold it is a bechor and it has a permanent blemish, for you have no greater blemish than the change of its creation. (language of Rambam chapter 5 Laws of Bechoros paragraph 6):
