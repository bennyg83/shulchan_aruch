A ewe that gave birth. See the responsum of my grandfather, Panim Me'irot volume 3 siman 28, who wondered why they did not bring the law of a ewe daughter of a ewe daughter of a goat, for the Sages hold: go after the mother of its mother, and this is nidmeh, and the Rosh ruled like the Sages; and likewise a ewe daughter of a goat daughter of a ewe, Rabbi Meir holds the sheep-nature returned to its place and the Sages hold it did not return, and the Rosh ruled like the Sages. And also one should wonder at the Rambam, who omitted this law; and even though Beit Yosef gave a reason, his reason is not [valid]; and in any case the Mechaber should have brought the Rosh's view in the Shulchan Aruch, and he left it as requiring further study; see there.
