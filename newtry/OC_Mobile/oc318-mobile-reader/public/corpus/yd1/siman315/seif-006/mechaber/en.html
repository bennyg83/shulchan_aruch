A cow that gave birth in the form of a donkey—and it has some signs of a cow—behold it is a bechor for the kohen, since the species of donkey has the law of bechor; but if it gave birth in the form of a horse or camel, even though it has some signs of a cow, behold it is a doubtful bechor; therefore he eats it by the owners, and if the kohen seized it they do not remove it from his hand. And the same applies to a donkey that gave birth in the form of a horse and has some signs of a donkey—this is doubtful bechor.
