An androgynos. Although elsewhere we rule that an androgynos is a distinct creation, neither male nor female, and according to that view cannot be a firstborn, the authorities disagree and some classify it as female. Regarding a firstborn we therefore rule stringently and treat it as a doubtful firstborn. It may not be shorn or worked and may be eaten only after another permanent blemish develops; the androgynous condition itself is not a blemish. See siman 194:8.
