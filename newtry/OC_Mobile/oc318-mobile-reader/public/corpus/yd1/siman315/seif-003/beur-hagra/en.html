An androgynos, and so forth. This is a dispute in the Mishnah and in Yevamot 83a. The Rif understands the ruling like Rabbi Yose of the baraita, which leads here to the later Sages' view, and the Rambam rules accordingly. Rashi and Tosafot understand it like Rabbi Yose of the Mishnah, making the androgynos certainly male and supporting the first Tanna here. Because the leading authorities disagree, the Rosh recommends stringency and treats the status as doubtful.
