(Likkut) And children of the schoolhouse. The Yerushalmi, cited by Rosh and Mordechai in Moed Katan, recounts that Shmuel's father sent him to study Torah. When they later sent him word to perform kindness, he replied, "Are there no graves here that you sent me there?" This teaches that children studying Torah are not interrupted for a funeral. Shabbos 119b likewise says schoolchildren are not interrupted even for rebuilding the Beit HaMikdash.
