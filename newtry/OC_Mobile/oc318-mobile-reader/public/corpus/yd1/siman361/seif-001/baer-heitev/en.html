To go out. Taz writes that this refers to one who neither studies Scripture nor Mishnah, who need not go out to check whether sufficient people are attending to the deceased. This opinion appears to disagree with Rama's statement that nowadays we ordinarily suspend Torah study. According to Rama, even such a person should ordinarily stop. Nevertheless, a rabbi teaching his students should not ordinarily cancel the lesson for this reason. Agudah writes that the custom to suspend Torah study whenever a deceased person is in the city exists lest the deceased's needs be neglected because everyone goes to study. In communities where organized groups are assigned to funeral duties, a person who studies Torah for its own sake and is not ordinarily involved in such duties may continue learning. Shach explains that this concerns a time other than the actual funeral procession. At the time the deceased is taken out, even one who otherwise need not attend must suspend study, as stated in seif 2, unlike Bach.
