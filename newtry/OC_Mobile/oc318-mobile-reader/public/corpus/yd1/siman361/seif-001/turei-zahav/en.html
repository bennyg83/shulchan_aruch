And he does not need to go out and see, etc. This refers to one who does not study Scripture and Mishnah — he does not need to see if there are people attending to all the needs of the deceased, as stated just before this. It appears that this view disagrees with what the Rama wrote — that nowadays we presume that everyone suspends Torah study for a funeral. If so, according to the Rama's view, one should suspend in this case as well. Nevertheless, a rav who is learning with his students should not suspend his study for this, as a matter of presumption. So it appears to me.
