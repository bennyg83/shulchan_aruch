To stand. The Taz explains that one stands out of respect for those attending to the deceased, who are performing kindness, even if the deceased was not a Torah scholar. This teaches that one should stand before a person engaged in any mitzvah. The Gemara in Kiddushin similarly says: See how beloved a mitzvah is in its proper time, for craftsmen stand before those bringing first fruits. Sefer Chassidim, siman 580. See simanim 54 and 580.
