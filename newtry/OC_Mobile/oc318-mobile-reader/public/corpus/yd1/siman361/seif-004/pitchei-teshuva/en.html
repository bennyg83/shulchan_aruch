To stand before him. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Taz. The Taz further indicates that if the deceased is a Torah scholar, one is obligated to stand for the deceased himself, independently of standing for those attending to him. A practical difference arises where those attending to the body are non-Jews. See Responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 54, who challenges the Taz, and see there also whether one must interrupt his work in order to stand before the deceased.
