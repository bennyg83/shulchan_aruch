Stand before him. See the Baer Heitev in the name of Taz; and it is further clarified in Taz that if the deceased is a Torah scholar, one is obligated to stand before him alone. The practical difference is if those attending to him are gentiles — and see in Responsa Yad Eliyahu siman 54 who objected to Taz. See there further regarding whether one must interrupt his work in order to stand before the dead.
