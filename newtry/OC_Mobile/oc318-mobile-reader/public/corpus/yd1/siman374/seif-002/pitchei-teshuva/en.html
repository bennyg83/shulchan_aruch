But not a betrothed woman. Perach Mateh Aharon, part 2, responsum 100, rules that a yevamah awaiting levirate marriage who dies is not mourned by the yavam. Masat Moshe, Yoreh Deah 27, disagrees and requires mourning. Nechpeh BaKesef, Yoreh Deah 1, cited in Birkei Yosef, analyzes the matter and concludes like Perach Mateh Aharon that he need not mourn.
