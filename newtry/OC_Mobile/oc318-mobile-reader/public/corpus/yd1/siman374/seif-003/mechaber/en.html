What is met mitzvah? One found on the road or in a gentile city without grave-diggers, and from where he found him he cannot call an Israelite who will answer and come to attend to him and bury him — it is a mitzvah for him to move from there and not leave the dead, even to go to a city to bring grave-diggers; rather he becomes impure himself and buries him; but if Israelites were near the place of the dead so the finder calls them and they answer and come to bury him — this is not met mitzvah for the kohen to become impure for him; rather he calls them and they bury. If the townspeople came {Rama: (Ramban in Torat HaAdam and Rosh in laws of impurity)} — if he has all his needs, he withdraws his hand. {Rama: Some say if he finds none to bury him except for pay, he is not obligated to hire from his own — rather he becomes impure if he wishes (Nimmukei Yosef ch. HaIshah Rabba in the name of Ritva); a found dead person and we do not know whether gentile or Israelite — we follow the majority found there, if we cannot say every fixed case is half-and-half (Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Rashba).}
