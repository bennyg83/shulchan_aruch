The majority. The Siftei Kohen wrote that the meaning is that we follow the passersby found there, or the majority of towns, and we do not follow the nearest city — even with demonstrable proximity — for anyone who separates does so from the majority. Unless we can say "any fixed [grouping]," etc. — such as where it is known that there were nine non-Jews and one Jew here, and one of them was killed in his place: since this is a case of "fixed," we do not follow the non-Jewish majority; rather it is as if it were half and half. And the Levush wrote: to be stringent — the kohen does not become impure due to doubt, as above in siman 373:8 — end quote:
