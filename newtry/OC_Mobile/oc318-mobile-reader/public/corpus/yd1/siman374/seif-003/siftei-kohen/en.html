That we can say "any fixed [grouping]," etc. Such as where it is known that there were nine non-Jews and one Jew here, and one of them was killed in his place — since this is a case of "fixed," we do not follow the non-Jewish majority; rather it is as if it were half and half. And the Ateret Zekenim wrote: to be stringent, the kohen does not become impure — end quote. That is to say, since it is as if half and half, it is a case of doubt, and we rule stringently: he must be buried; yet nevertheless the kohen does not become impure due to doubt, as above in siman 373:8:
