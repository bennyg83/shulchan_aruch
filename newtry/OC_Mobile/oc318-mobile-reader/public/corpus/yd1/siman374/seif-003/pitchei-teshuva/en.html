His father-in-law and mother-in-law. See Teshuvah MeAhavah I siman 175, who wrote regarding one whose father's wife died during his father's lifetime, that he must observe some mourning until the end of the first Shabbat. If for the honor of one's father-in-law and mother-in-law one displays mourning, though it is only a support, all the more so for the honor of his father's wife, which is included by Torah law in "your father." The Rambam and Shulchan Aruch below, seif 7, were very precise when they wrote: but for their relatives through marriage, such as, etc., and they omitted his father's wife, his mother's husband, his father-in-law, and his mother-in-law, because although these come through marital relationship, he mourns for them because he is obligated in their honor; see there.
