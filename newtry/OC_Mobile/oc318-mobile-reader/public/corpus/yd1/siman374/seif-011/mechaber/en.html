A nasi — even though all become impure for him, they do not mourn for him: {Rama: Some say they do not mourn for a firstborn son or bekhor who died to a person (Kol Bo and Rivash and Beit Yosef brought one who practiced thus and wrote it is an error); and the custom is an erroneous custom — rather they must mourn for them; nevertheless the custom spread in our city that father and mother do not follow their firstborn children to the cemetery; some say in time of plague they do not mourn {Rama: because of panic} (responsum of Maharil siman 50); and I heard some practiced thus.}
