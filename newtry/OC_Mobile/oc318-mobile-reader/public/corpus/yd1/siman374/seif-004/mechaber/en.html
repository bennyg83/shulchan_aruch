All relatives for whom a kohen becomes impure — father, mother, son, daughter, brother and sister from his father who is a virgin, and his wife — mourn for them; and they added brother from mother and sister from mother, whether virgin or married, and married sister from father — who mourn for them even though a kohen does not become impure for them; and just as he mourns for his wife, so she mourns for him — specifically his fit married wife, but unfit or betrothed — not; but for his son, daughter, brother, and sister, even if unfit, he mourns — except his son, daughter, brother, and sister who are mamzer or from a gentile woman, for whom he does not mourn.
