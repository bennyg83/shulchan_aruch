A doubt, etc. See Tosafot to Yevamot 36b, s.v. ha, and what the Mishnah teaches, etc. The Rosh wrote likewise at the end of chapter 2 of Berakhot and in chapter 3 of Moed Katan. So it is explicitly taught in chapter 4 of Semachot: one who is in doubt whether he is his brother or not his brother, whether he is his son or not his son, mourns and is an onen, but does not become tamei.
