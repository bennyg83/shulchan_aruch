A child — all thirty days, and day thirty included, they do not mourn for him even if his hair and nails were complete; from then on they mourn for him unless it is known he is eight months; and if it is clear his months were complete, such as one who married and separated and a live child was born at nine full months — even if he died the day he was born, they mourn for him {Rama: (doubt nine-month of the first or seven-month of the last — both mourn for him) (Mordechai laws of mourning and Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 1 and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot).}
