And the second lived beyond thirty days. The correct text means that one does not mourn the twin who died within thirty days. We do not infer from the surviving twin's viability that the deceased twin was also viable. This is contrary to Levush, who understood that one does not mourn the second twin who died after thirty days; Bach rightly rejects that reading. Or Zarua itself states that where one twin died within thirty days and the second survived, the first is not thereby presumed viable. Rashba's responsum and the gloss to Derishah support this reading.
