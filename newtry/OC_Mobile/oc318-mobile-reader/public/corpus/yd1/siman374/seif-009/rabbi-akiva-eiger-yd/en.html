(Shach, seif katan 9) So too the gloss to the Derishah explains, and this is primary. See Mekor Baruch 31, whose words are difficult to accept. I merited seeing a manuscript of Or Zarua, where it is explicit that one does not mourn for a child who died within thirty days. We do not say that because twins came from one drop and the second lived beyond thirty days, the first was thereby shown viable. It is possible that part of the drop was absorbed immediately and part two days later. No source is given there for this rule. It appears that its source is Nazir 13a, where the question remains unresolved; see Tosafot there, s.v. hipilah.
