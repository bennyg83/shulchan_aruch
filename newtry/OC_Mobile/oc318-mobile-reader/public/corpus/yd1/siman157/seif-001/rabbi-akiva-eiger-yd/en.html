(Siman 157 seif 1) And gilui arayot. And if she is safek eshet ish. See in Sefer Teivat Gome of Peri Megadim (beginning of Vayeishev).
(There in Hagah) And he does not transgress a negative commandment. From words of responsum Chavot Yair (siman 139) it is clear that if it is a negative commandment without an action it is like transgressing a positive commandment and he need not give all his money. And see in Mishbetzot Zahav (siman 446 s.k. 6); however in responsum Rivash (siman 387) it is clear that whoever transgresses a negative commandment even without action must give all he has and not transgress. (See what is written in Choshen Mishpat siman 339 seif 10.) In margin in responsum Radbaz (part 2 siman 145) he wrote that if he is among gentiles and finds nothing to eat except forbidden things even rabbinic prohibition and chelev of gid and the like — he must give all his money to find permitted food and not eat prohibition; and afterwards he is compelled by life-threat and eats the lighter prohibition first; and nevertheless he is not obligated to take money on credit until he has money to repay — lest he not have money and gentiles imprison and torture him and danger can come.
(There) not to fulfill a positive commandment. See in Hanahat Asheri first chapter of Ketubot s.v. umin hasakanah; and see in chapter 5 there s.v. takanta derabbanan; and see in Bach Orach Chayyim (siman 479 seif 8).
