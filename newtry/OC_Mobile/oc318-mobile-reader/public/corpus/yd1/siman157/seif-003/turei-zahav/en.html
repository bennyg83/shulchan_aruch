Permitted to flee to house of idolatry. Rosh concluded there and it seems to heal from asheirah trees — there concern he will be drawn since cure depends on asheirah trees; and even regarding asheirah if he said raise anonymously and brought from asheirah — permitted; and so this that one saves the fugitive into them is not because of elevation in the idol; and further a house not itself worshipped is not called idolatry — end quote; and accordingly for that view Shulchan Aruch brings siman 155 seif 3 forbidding even branches anonymously because of lo yidbak in your hand, etc. — should forbid here too. And siman 149 Tur brings one should not drink from spring flowing before idol even if he dies if not drink — seems to resolve — different there where he goes to do act benefiting him such as speaking with official or similar — therefore forbidden there unlike here where there is sit and do not do — only distancing harm and permitted for pikuach nefesh.
