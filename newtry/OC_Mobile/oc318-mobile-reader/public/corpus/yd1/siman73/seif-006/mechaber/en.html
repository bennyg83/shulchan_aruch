Liver found in roasted fowl is permitted. {Rama: And some say peel a bit around the liver — but this is only a stringency (Mordechai in the name of Rivash and Shaarei Dura and Or Zarua).} And if it is cooked — need sixty against the liver. {Rama: And you have no fowl that is sixty against the liver when it is whole (Shaarei Dura and Mordechai in the name of Rabeinu Efraim and Maharai and Or Zarua general rule 16); therefore if the liver is whole and stuck to the fowl, the fowl becomes a nevelah piece and we require sixty from other things in the pot against all the fowl — and the same if a piece of liver stuck to a piece of fowl and there is not sixty against it (there). For we hold regarding all prohibitions piece becomes nevelah as below siman 92. And if the liver is not stuck, everything in the pot joins to nullify the liver. And if there is sixty in everything — permitted. However the liver itself is forbidden like the heart (section 46 path 15). As explained above siman 72. Fowl stuffed with eggs — if heart or liver found in it, law is as cooked; and we need sixty from the fowl without the filling. And if not — all forbidden. And if stuffed with meat and there are no eggs that harden and block blood — law is as roasting (Or Zarua in the name of Agudah and Binyamin Ze'ev siman 336).}
