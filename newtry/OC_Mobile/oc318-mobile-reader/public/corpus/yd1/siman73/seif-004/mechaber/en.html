If one roasted it with meat in the ovens of the days of the Gemara sages — their mouth above — the liver should be below and not above. And b'dieved it is permitted. And on spits roasted at the fire it is forbidden l'chatchila to roast with meat, even with liver below. {Rama: However if the liver was already salted it is permitted to roast with meat, even on top of meat, for its blood already diminished and it is like meat on meat (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot beginning chapter 25 and Hagahat Shaarei Dura and Mordechai ch. 25).}
