The law of roasting liver. And it has 6 seifim: The liver has abundant blood; therefore l'chatchila it has no remedy to cook it through salting, but one tears it two-and-evening and leaves the cut below and roasts it (that it be fit for eating) (Or Zarua path 15), and afterward one may cook it. {Rama: And if one pierces it many times with a knife it is like tearing two-and-evening (Or Zarua). And likewise if one removes from there the bile and cuts flesh from the liver so blood can flow from there (Hagahat Shaarei Dura). Nevertheless if one did not do so — one removes the vessels after roasting and cooks it (Or Zarua). And all this is for whole liver; but when it is cut nothing is needed (Rabbenu Yerucham section 46). And when one comes to cook after roasting — one rinses it first after roasting before cooking (precision Beit Yosef from Mordechai ch. 25 and Or Zarua). However if one did not rinse and cooked so — permitted (Rabeinu Efraim and Or Zarua there).} And b'dieved it is permitted if it was cooked alone in a pot (without roasting); but the pot is forbidden because it emits and does not absorb. And there is one who forbids. {Rama: And so they practice to forbid everything (Hagahat Semak and Maharai in Hagahat Shaarei Dura), even if the liver was salted before cooking (Rashi's view, unlike Rabbenu Tam).}
