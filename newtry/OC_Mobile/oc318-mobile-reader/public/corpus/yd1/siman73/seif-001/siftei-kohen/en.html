Therefore l'chatchila it has no remedy to cook through salting — even if one wishes to forbid the vessel in which liver was cooked (Torat Chatas general rule 24 law 1; Bach).
That it be fit for eating — see above siman 76 seif 2 hagahah 2 — explained half-roasted; so Or Zarua end general rule 8.
And if pierced many times, etc. — like tearing two-and-evening — we were in b'dieved after roasted by piercing first many times with knife; l'chatchila piercing many times does not help if wishes to cook afterward (Torat Chatas there law 6).
And if removed bile, etc. — cut tendons and bronchi on side where bile hangs and cut bit of liver flesh with them (Torat Chatas sin offering); even l'chatchila permitted if will cook afterward (Torat Chatas there).
One rinses first — because salt sticks; also marit ayin reddens flesh; meat roasted on spit wanting to cook must rinse too (Beit Yosef; Rav end siman 76 seif 2).
And b'dieved permitted, etc. — b'dieved permitted if cooked even without salting alone without meat; if with meat — meat forbidden like pot because liver emits blood and does not absorb even by cooking since busy emitting much blood.
Without roasting — Rav stated plainly; cannot say permitters mean only without roasting but not without salting — not in his words; and per reason emits does not absorb, permitted even without salting — poskim; and from Rav end siman 67 we are not expert scalding — liver much blood; Rav equates statuses — if not expert scalding, b'dieved permitted as if not scalded; liver without scalding forbidden even b'dieved for pot — scalding does not improve — requires further study.
And so they practice, etc. — reason in hagahah Semak siman 205, Maharai, Or Zarua path 15, Torat Chatas — some in Talmud scalding forbidden and we not expert scalding vs cooked; Torat Chatas Or Zarua: pot in which liver eaten is kosher. Rav forbade cooking even b'dieved, permitted scalding; Tur gave no reason for scalding permitted because emits does not absorb as Rav copied — Tur: therefore do not permit scalding; if done b'dieved or cooked alone in pot b'dieved emits does not absorb — applies to end when cooked without scalding; simple; Rav: since practice forbid liver when cooked because not expert scalding vs cooked — same for scalding b'dieved forbidden; difficult — Rav end siman 67 meat scalding b'dieved permitted — say liver different much blood; Rav equates — requires further study.
Even if liver salted before cooking — even if salted as pot measure and rinsed before cooking — not useful because abundant blood — not even tearing two-and-evening and cut below helps (Torat Chatas law 3); if posek permitted after salt tear two-and-evening — we do not overturn practice; if not cut two-and-evening at salting time — overturn and forbid even liver (Torat Chatas); Maharshal conclusion: liver cooked without salting b'dieved emits does not absorb; if no sixty in dish even liver forbidden even salted — becomes nevelah from dish fatty prohibition; Maharshal also wrote liver not forbidden from that reason — requires further study.
