(1) [Seif 1] Liver has abundant blood, etc. — laws of liver roasting and salting — see Pri Megadim, Levush, Chokhmat Adam.
(2) [Seif 2] Scalding in vinegar or boiling water — Geonim forbade l'chatchila; b'dieved permitted — Shach, Taz.
(3) [Seif 3] Cutting for roasting — blood in vessels; Rama: some need no cut — Tur, Semak, Or Zarua.
(4) [Seif 4] Roasting with meat in oven — liver below; spits forbidden l'chatchila; salted liver permitted with meat — Rama.
