Remedy — meaning even if one wishes to forbid the vessels (Shach).
For eating — Shach: appears half-roasted counts as fit for eating.
If pierced — Shach: b'dieved after roasted by piercing many times with knife first; l'chatchila piercing many times does not help if will cook afterward.
Bile — Shach: also cut tendons and bronchi on bile side and bit of flesh — even l'chatchila permitted if will cook afterward.
Alone — meaning even without salting, liver emits blood and does not absorb even by cooking since busy emitting much blood (Shach).
If salted — Shach: even if salted as pot measure and rinsed before cooking — not useful because abundant blood — not even two-and-evening cut below; Torat Chatas if posek permitted after tear and cooked — do not overturn practice, all permitted; if not cut two-and-evening at salting time — overturn and forbid; reason forbid all — some hold scalding forbidden, we not expert scalding vs cooked; bowl eaten from is kosher — Taz: Rama concerned Rambam view liver absorbs.
