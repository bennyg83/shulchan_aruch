Do not salt l'chatchila, etc. — when salting both l'chatchila forbidden b'dieved permitted even liver above — Rav wrote; if meat already salted rinsed — forbidden even b'dieved liver above — not worse than unsalted meat on already salted rinsed meat (siman 70) forbidden — Torat Chatas when salting both.
Custom not to salt, etc. — recognition not like other meat lest cook with meat (Or Zarua); or fire re-absorbs blood (Agur Maharil); if happened salted — rinse before roasting (siman 69); Maharil responsum 334.
If stuck to fowl — this word and Rav cited fowl teaches: in animal no way liver stuck at salting — obviously no view requires peel; found inside not stuck — same Or Zarua explicitly; Torat Chatas end law 10 unlike Taz even not stuck needs peel — s.k. 19.
Practice to rinse, etc. — even if wants to eat roasted only — clear.
However if did not rinse, etc. — if did not rinse at all even before roasting and cooked so — permitted; Darkei Moshe Or Zarua general rule 16; Torat Chatas law 10 — liver blood rabbinic; if did not rinse after roasting — Rav seif 1 wrote; Torat Chatas concludes l'chatchila practice rinse also before roasting — even to eat roasted only — see there.
