Do not salt — as in roasting as said chapter "Keitzad" (Chullin 97b) salting like scalding hot like roast — here Rashi view not relevant. (Likut) Not — because liver abundant blood emits abundantly and meat's trouble to emit is slight — therefore forbade in roasting though other meat permitted for even salting permitted; nevertheless b'dieved permitted — not worse than roasting.
And practice — Shach; so Beit Yosef.
However if — and it is not; b'dieved even roasting permitted as seif 4.
Practice — however; above seif 1 hagahah and when comes.
