One should not salt it l'chatchila on top of meat but beneath it. {Rama: And they practice not to salt liver at all, even alone, and one should not change — only salt it a bit when stuck on a spit or placed on the fire to roast (Hagahat Shaarei Dura and Agur in the name of Agudah). However if it happened that liver was salted — whether alone or with meat, even on meat — all is permitted (Tur and Shaarei Dura and Or Zarua). And some say one should peel a bit around the liver if it is stuck to fowl — but this is only a stringency (Mordechai ch. 25 and Or Zarua). They practice to rinse every liver after roasting because of blood stuck on it (Agur in the name of R' Yitzchak Mullin). However if one did not rinse — it is permitted.}
