And some forbid — see Peleti where I expanded root — Rambam's view and reason of forbidders unlike Rambam who hold scalding forbids and we are not expert between kavush and scalding; if pickled in water from above — as I expanded in strength of heter; but vessel in which liver was pickled is forbidden; also practice to pickle with liver, fat — these forbidden, liver permitted. Liver that waited three days without salting — appears no stringency after roasting; otherwise law of meat three days is only Geonic stringency.
