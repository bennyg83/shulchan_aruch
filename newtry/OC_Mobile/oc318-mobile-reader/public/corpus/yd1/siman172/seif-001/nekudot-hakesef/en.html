(Taz, seif katan 4) Meaning, specifically when he explicitly accepted liability. His words are not compelling. Since deduction of the debt is treated as a sale regarding the produce, it may be treated as a full sale, and we apply the rule that omission of a standard liability clause is deemed a scribal error.
