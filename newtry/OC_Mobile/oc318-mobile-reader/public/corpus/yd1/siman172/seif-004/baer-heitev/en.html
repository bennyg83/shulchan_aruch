For the price. Shach explains that if the borrower agrees to sell the property for the loan amount, or with only a small addition, while it is worth more and the reduction is because of the loan, it is forbidden. Bach classifies this as avak ribbit. Levush writes that although a lender holding collateral may ordinarily defeat a neighboring purchaser's right of first refusal, here he should not, lest that advantage be interest. Taz rejects this, since the borrower loses nothing, and permits it.
