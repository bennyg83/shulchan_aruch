For money. If he fixes the price at the amount of the loan, or adds only a small amount, while the property is actually worth more, it is forbidden. Tur, Rashi, and Tosafot, and this is the view of the later authorities. Nimukei Yosef likewise wrote so, unlike what appears from the Maggid Mishneh, namely that whenever they fixed a known price at the time of the loan it is forbidden even if the property is not worth more. Nevertheless, one should be stringent at the outset.
It is forbidden. The Bach wrote that it is avak ribbit, because contingent ribbit is rabbinic. Darkei Moshe likewise concludes in the name of Rivash, simanim 305 and 367. See above, siman 174.
