For money. Those of the loan's monetary value, or if he adds a little more — nevertheless it is worth more — forbidden; Tur and Rashi and Tosafot, and so all the Acharonim; and so Nimukei Yosef; unlike what appears from the Maggid Mishneh — that whenever he fixed known money at the time of the loan — even if not worth more — it is forbidden; nevertheless it is good to be stringent lechatchilah.
It is forbidden. Bach wrote that it is avak ribbit, for one side of ribbit is rabbinic. So Darkei Moshe concludes in the name of Rivash simanim 305 and 467. See below siman 174.
