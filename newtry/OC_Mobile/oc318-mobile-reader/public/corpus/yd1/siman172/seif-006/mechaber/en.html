One who gives his ruin to his fellow that he should build and live in it until the costs of the expenditure are finished — and if it is worth 3 zehuvim per year, he calculates only 1 zehuv — it is permitted. {Rama: A borrower is permitted to pay the tax from the field that he pledged in the permitted manner, and specifically when the tax is money; but if they give the tax from the fruits of the field — for example, that they give a portion of the growing fruits — it is forbidden (Beit Yosef in the name of Ba’al HaTerumos and Ramban). One who pledged a house that is not his, and the owner of the house came and removed his house from the lender, and [the lender] needed to pay rent to the owner of the house for the one who lived in it: the borrower must return to the lender his money, and also the rent that he needed to give (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba).}
