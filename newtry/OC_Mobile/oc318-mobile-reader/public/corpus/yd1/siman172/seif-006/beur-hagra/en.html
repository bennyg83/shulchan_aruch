But if, etc. See the Be'er HaGolah, and compare the end of the second chapter of Peah: “He harvested half of it and sold half of it,” etc. See the Rambam's commentary there.
One who mortgaged, etc. Meaning, with deduction, which is treated as a sale according to the authorities cited above who permit it.
The borrower, etc. As stated in the first chapter, 14b and 15b: “He is entitled to the money and to the appreciation.” Although it is stated there on 15a, “Is it permitted to lend,” etc., there the matter is a loan, etc. The same applies according to Rav. Nevertheless, here too it may be regarded as a sale. This is the Rashba's view.
