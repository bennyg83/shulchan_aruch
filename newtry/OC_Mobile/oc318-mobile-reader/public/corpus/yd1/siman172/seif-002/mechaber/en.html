This mashkanta: if the lender comes to go back and rent it to the borrower for a fixed matter per year, some forbid (Sefer HaTerumos, and Rivash, siman 305, in the name of Ramban), and some permit (Rivash in the name of some, brought by Beit Yosef in siman 164). And if another person rented it from the lender, he can rent it from him. {Rama: And see above siman 164; it was also explained above regarding if the lender ate the fruits after the time of the mashkanta.}
