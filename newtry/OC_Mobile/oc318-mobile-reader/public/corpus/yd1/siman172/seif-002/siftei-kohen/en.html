This mortgage. Of Sura; but another mortgage — forbidden even if another person rented it, as above siman 164; and likewise Tur in the name of the Rama; and he wrote that if he stipulated thus it is fixed ribbit; and the Rabbi agrees to this; unlike Pri Megadim; and Tur wrote: all the more the custom where the borrower gives collateral for principal and the renter pays in produce — that is fixed ribbit.
He can etc. However it seems to me that they must not have stipulated thus from the outset at the time of the loan — Pri Megadim.
