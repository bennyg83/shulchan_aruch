To rent it. Levush writes that this should not be stipulated at the time of the loan, and limits the permission to a Sura mortgage. In another mortgage, even if a third party rents the property, it is forbidden; if stipulated, it is fixed interest. Taz likewise challenges Levush. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that where money is advanced on houses, the owner continues living there, and a third person obligates himself to pay the lender a fixed annual sum, it is permitted if that third person acts voluntarily. If he acts as the borrower's agent, it is fixed interest.
