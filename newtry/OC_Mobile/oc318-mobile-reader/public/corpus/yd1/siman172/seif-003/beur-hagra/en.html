Every mortgage may be redeemed in installments. The Rashba and Sefer HaTerumos, gate 49, wrote this in the name of the Ramban, based on the question raised in the first chapter of Kiddushin, 20b, regarding houses in walled cities, whether they may be redeemed in installments, and whether this is derived from the phrase “his redemption,” etc. This indicates that without that special issue, property may be redeemed in installments. The reading in the printed texts is, “may not be redeemed,” etc. The meaning is limited to the matter that he proceeds to explain, namely, “and if he brought,” etc. This is unlike the Shach's understanding, for according to him the text should have said, “Nevertheless, if he brought,” etc., in the name of the Rashba. I do not understand the Rashba's proof, because even if we say that it may be redeemed in installments, the partial payment could still remain a deposit. See what I wrote in Choshen Mishpat, siman 74, seif 4.
