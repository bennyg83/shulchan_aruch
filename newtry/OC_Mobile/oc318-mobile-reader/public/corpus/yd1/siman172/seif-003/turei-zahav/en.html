Against collapse or fire, it is forbidden. One should not ask that we rule that an unpaid guardian may stipulate to have the liability of a borrower, as the Rama wrote in siman 169, seif 18. This case is different, because the essential permission of this arrangement is based upon the recipient’s acceptance of responsibility. Therefore, whenever that responsibility is removed from him, there is no permission here, for the lender and borrower remain in their ordinary legal positions. This is unlike siman 169, which concerns agency alone. From here there is a contradiction to the novel permission devised by the Derishah in siman 167. I have already discussed it there at some length; see there.
With a preexisting debt, it is forbidden. It is forbidden even with a deduction, for there is no money here and therefore no effective transfer here. So wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of the Baal HaItur. It appears to me that this applies where he uses only the language of a mortgage. In that case we say: Since no money is being advanced now, upon what can the transfer of the mortgage take effect? This expression, ‘There is no money here and there is no transfer here,’ appears in the first chapter of Kiddushin and in Even HaEzer, siman 50, seif 6. If a man says to a woman, ‘Become betrothed to me with a dinar,’ and gives her collateral until he gives her the dinar, she is not betrothed, because there is no maneh here and therefore no collateral here. Based upon this, it appears to me that just as there, if he says to her, ‘Acquire the body of the collateral,’ she is betrothed, so too here, if he says to the lender, ‘Take possession of the field and acquire it through an act of possession, so that its produce is acquired to you,’ and the lender takes possession accordingly, it is thereafter permitted when accompanied by a deduction, and certainly with a mortgage of Sura. This appears to me.
