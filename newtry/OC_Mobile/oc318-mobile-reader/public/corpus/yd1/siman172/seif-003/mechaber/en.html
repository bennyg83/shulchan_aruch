With this mashkanta: if the lender gives the borrower a small matter per year in exchange for him accepting upon himself some slight repair — for example, breaking a beam, and similar — it is permitted. But if he gives him a fixed amount per year in order that he accept responsibility for collapse or fire, there is one who forbids. {Rama: Any mashkanta is not redeemed in halves; and if he brought him some of his money, the lender eats its fruits until he gives him all of it (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba). That a mashkanta is permitted is specifically when he pledged the field to him at the time that he gives him the money, for it is like a sale; but if he gives it to him on an earlier debt, it is forbidden (Beit Yosef in the name of Itur). And if they made a mashkanta without specifying, and did not stipulate with each other mashkanta d’Sura or b’nichayasa, we follow the custom of the city, and presumably their intent is like the custom of the city; and likewise, if he can remove him, we follow the custom (Beit Yosef in the name of Ba’al HaTerumos).
