And that which it is permitted to taste forbidden matter — Shach wrote: mostly tasting in liver is recognizable and this is not so; and see Peleti what he wrote on this; and from his words it is not difficult to say via a gentile taster; but the world is accustomed to taste by oneself; and in my opinion one should search at the spleen of a bird and near it on the majority a very small gall bladder will be found; and in vain they declare many chickens treifah.
