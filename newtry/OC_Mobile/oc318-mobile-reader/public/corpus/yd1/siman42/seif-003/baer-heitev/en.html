Treifah. And Shach wrote: even though we establish it is forbidden to taste food to know if there is forbidden matter — nevertheless here they established that the matter is uncommon and it is likely one will taste bitter taste. (If there was no gall bladder and before tasting the liver was lost — kosher — Chayei Adam and Orach Chaim siman 16; and similarly Kenesset HaGedolah. Taste its place and bitter taste is found and taste again and bitterness is not found — we attribute that it was wiped off and it is kosher — Peri Chadash in name of Rikanti.)
To rely. And the reason is that in any case the lenient have multiplied regarding missing gall bladder.
