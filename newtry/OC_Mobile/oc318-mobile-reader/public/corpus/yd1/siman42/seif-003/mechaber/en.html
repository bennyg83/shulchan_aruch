If the gall bladder is missing, tear the liver in two and crosswise and taste it with his tongue; if he tastes bitter taste — kosher. And if he did not taste bitter taste, roast it on coals and taste; if there is bitter taste in it — kosher. And if not — treifah. {Rama: And even now we may rely on tasting the liver (Piskei Maharai siman 30).}
