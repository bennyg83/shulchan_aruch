Like date pits without sharp point — permitted per Rambam and his supporters: regarding olive we are concerned because it has sharp point punctured at its base; not so date; if so even if not as large as date, since no sharp point — kosher. And this is Beit Yosef's intent: Tur wrote like Tosafot and Rosh; per Rashi he should have written only if like date without sharp point; and Rashi's wording is corrupted — must read Rashba holds like Rambam; and Bach attacked without noticing gemara; and Peri Chadash sought to align Beit Yosef another way — correct as I wrote. But with all this I did not understand: in previous siman seif 18 it says measure of adhesion is like date pit — these are Rashba and Tur; and see above chapter 88 s.k. 11; and it must be reconciled: if he holds sharp point and can puncture — one law; if sharp point can puncture — if not, no; what is Bach's difficulty that he need not write "like date pits" but only sharp point — for us whenever not like date it is called sharp point; and if he holds sharp point cannot puncture at all — they are two matters; if so even in date perhaps punctured and came; and that there it is permitted since no sharp point even though can puncture — resembles lung; therefore Tur's words are clear; and Bach attacked in vain.
Because mouth of wound scabbed over — from this implies somewhat inflation does not help in gall bladder like lung; but it appears Rashi holds it helps; and thereby Maharsha's difficulty is settled daf 49 in Rashi "olive splits" — Rashi holds one who permits lung permits here too; and Maharsha wondered who forces him — we are in liver here; and per my words it is settled: Rashi holds all difference between lung and liver — this is examination for him, not liver; therefore in cut lung where no examination he also holds distinguish pouch inside outside and bar; and as Rashi daf 48b entry "the law"; and see Rashba; and if so gall bladder that has examination resembles lung not liver; and Rashi ruled well both equal in law; and also what Maharsha wrote: since this gall bladder is stated for one who forbids — nevertheless he permits date; if so he could permit pouch outside, etc.; and why did Rashi write there entry "in every case treifah" — per my words it is settled: regarding gall bladder there is examination; if so two leniencies — examination and adhesion — permit what is not so in liver where only one; and examine.
