Species of birds. See Sefer Tiferet le-Moshe who wrote: those species and likewise deer and rams — if their liver was removed and only olive left in place of its life and not in place of gall — kosher, for essence of treifah is gall as Rashba brought Beit Yosef end siman 41; and possibly even if removed in place of its life — kosher; requires further study; end; see there; and see Peri Megadim beginning siman 41.
