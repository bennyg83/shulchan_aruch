And deer has no gall. Ba'er Heitev what he wrote on rams, etc.; and see Sullam le-Minchah general rule 89 in name of Torat HaAsham: deer and ram are two species, etc.; nevertheless there not found in Darkei Moshe what Shach wrote in his name — Machatzit HaShekel wrote it is omission of Darkei Moshe at end of Orach Mishor on nezirut — see there. And what Ba'er Heitev wrote in name of Mateh Yehonatan general rule 89 on removed liver, etc.; and see Mateh Yehonatan who left requires further study; also in Tiferet le-Moshe he doubted; and Machatzit HaShekel wrote: in these species we do not require remainder in place of gall; and so Kenesset HaMishneh; and so proven from Kometz Bikkurim siman 41 — see there; and so explained Taharat HaShulchan siman 41 who wrote: per our law those species without gall require at least olive in place of its life, etc. — see there; and brought above in Darkei Moshe and here in Darkei Moshe. Machatzit HaShekel was not precise writing on Ba'er Heitev's words and cited Taharat HaShulchan that thus proven from what we were informed deer has gall — per his view would contradict his own words siman 41; but in truth this is Taharat HaShulchan to prove gall law applies for puncture and treifot dependent on it — not matter of removed liver — see there. And see Machatzit HaShekel: if gall found in turtledove or young pigeon or deer at liver like other species — appears kosher. And see Tiferet le-Moshe: found in turtledove and young pigeon and punctured — permit; brought poskim' views siman 35 regarding rose. Machatzit HaShekel wrote: doubt — not similar to rose law; and see Minchat Yaakov general rule 89 who doubts: if no gall in deer in tail whether tasting at its place helps. And in Chagurat Shmuel wrote: does not help; and Machatzit HaShekel wrote: tasting in place or liver where gall at tail or ear does not help.
