(There seif 8) There are species of birds without gall bladder. Rosh beginning Avodah Zarah wrote: some bird species have no gall bladder and one does not declare them treifah; nevertheless do not learn, etc., that every animal species is one — even if one coarse and one fine; but birds are divided in their species and one does not learn these from those; end; see there. And requires further study: he is against gemara beginning of Chullin (daf 70b) and Rashi and Tosafot there explicitly opposite — that geese and chickens are one bird species with turtledoves and young pigeons; but goat is not species of calf — one coarse one fine. And in birds it does not apply to say coarse and fine species — see there; and requires further study.
