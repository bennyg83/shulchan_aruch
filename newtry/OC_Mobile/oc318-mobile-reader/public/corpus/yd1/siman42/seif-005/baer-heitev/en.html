Kosher. And what did not pour to its fellow when first punctured was on account of thickness of the moisture — Beit Yosef. (And this is when there is no taste of its bitterness in the liver; but if there is bitter taste — kosher per many poskim from law of entirely missing gall bladder — Peri Chadash; and in Sha'arei Yeshurun a question — general rule 52 disputes him. Rikanti wrote: an incident came to me — there was a blister like a small pouch coming out near the gall bladder hanging on the duct near it with a finger-width and they did not pour into one another; they punctured the blister and clear water came out; they tasted the water and it was not bitter; they permitted it, for it is not gall bladder but a blister; and there is no treifah of blister except in lung. Ahel Mo'ed wrote: gall bladder that was emptied and the pouch hangs in its place — kosher; we attribute that via the duct the bile was poured and not via puncture at all; and Peri Chadash wrote on this; and they inflate it; and if after inflation no puncture at all is found — there is to attribute that it poured via the duct, rather than to say a puncture in the membrane rose and is not visible.)
