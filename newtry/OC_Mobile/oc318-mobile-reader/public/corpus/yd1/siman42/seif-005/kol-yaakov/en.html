Permitted to change, etc. Implies even not from distress permitted to change shel Rosh to shel Yad; but Derishah siman 32 and Levush there seif 47; and here they wrote permitted only such as he has no shel Yad tefillin and has two shel Rosh — if not so — forbidden — Peri Megadim Orach Chaim ot 1; and be stringent where possible and know Derishah there wrote novel law: only if made four compartments for shel Rosh that were checked — valid and he has no shel Yad — then take hide; if made four compartments for shel Yad initially — invalid — hide-taking does not help; and so gemara Menachot 34 and Levush language; but Shulchan Aruch siman 32 seif 47 implies kosher in every case — Peri Megadim Orach Chaim ot 3.
