The laws of gall-bladder treifot. In it are 9 seifim. A gall bladder that was punctured — treifah. And if it was punctured opposite the liver and the liver seals it — kosher.
{Rama: If the gall bladder adhered to another place — kosher (Mordechai Piskei Avodah Zarah and Isur ve-Heter HaArukh) (and see above siman 46).}
