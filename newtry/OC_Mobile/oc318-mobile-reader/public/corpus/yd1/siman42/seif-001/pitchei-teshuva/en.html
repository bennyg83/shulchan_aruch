Kosher. For adhesion disqualifies only in lung, as Mechaber wrote above siman 37 and below siman 46; and so Bach ruled; and so Taz and Shach. But Yeshu'ot Shemesh Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 48 and 56 forbids adhesion in other organs; and in Taharat HaBayit siman 253 forbids even if adhered to wall — we do not attribute to wall — see there, etc. Shelah daf 74b entry "regarding" in name of responsum Mishpetei Shmuel (it is siman 130) ruled to declare treifah; and he too ruled there. But in responsum Tzitz Eliezer siman 9 he elaborated to dispute and upheld Rav's ruling that adhesion disqualifies only in lung — see there; and so upheld in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 76. And he wrote there regarding thin membranes of calf that adhered to one another: in this all agree kosher; for even in Taharat HaBayit he forbade only adhesion to wall or another place that is adhesion not in order that has no examination at all; not so these thin membranes that rest — even if adhered end to end it is not like lung, for lung goes up and down frequently; but in thin membranes that rest it is like lung-adhesion in order and kosher — see there; and see responsum Chinuch Beit Yosef siman 36; and as I wrote siman 46 s.k. 5.
