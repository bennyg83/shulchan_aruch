A gall bladder that was punctured, etc. And Rashba wrote: where it is possible to attribute to a knife or the slaughterer's hand — we attribute, as above siman 36 seif 5 regarding lung; and Beit Yosef, and it is straightforward. And so the acharonim; and see Taz general rule 88 on laws of gall bladder.
And the liver seals it, etc. And if the liver too was punctured opposite it — we say in the Gemara (daf 48a) treifah; and so the poskim.
The gall bladder adhered, etc. — that is, as the Mechaber wrote siman 37 that adhesion disqualifies only in lung and not in other organs; and so below siman 46. And so Bach there. And the reason is that possibly in other organs it is not adhesion at all but only separation of moisture; and this is not like Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 56, as written in books there.
