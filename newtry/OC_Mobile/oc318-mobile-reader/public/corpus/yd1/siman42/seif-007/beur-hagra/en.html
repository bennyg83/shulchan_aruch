There were two, etc. Shach:
(Likkut) 66 even though, etc., and 67 there were, etc. — we are lenient in such a case because Ra'avyah permits entirely in removal; and all the more in excess; and his proof: we say the liver was removed, etc., and therefore the gall bladder too was removed; and see Mordechai and Hagahot Ashiri chapter 88. Yeshu'ot Shemesh (end).
Gall bladder, etc. Even though regarding lung if they do not pour into one another it is treifah there on account of ducts — not so here, for ducts were removed and it is kosher; and so regarding lung if they do not pour into one another — kosher there — there is no prohibition except because they adhere to one another; not so here.
