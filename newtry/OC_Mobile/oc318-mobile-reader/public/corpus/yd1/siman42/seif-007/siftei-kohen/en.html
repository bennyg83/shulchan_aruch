There is to permit in an animal of Israel. Bach wrote: from Rashba's language it is implied that even in an animal of Israel one should forbid l'chatchila, etc.; and l'fachad — nevertheless the Mechaber understood Rashba thus; but since many poskim permit entirely with two gall bladders, it is reasonable to permit in such a case in an animal of Israel; and so Maharshal Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 12.
