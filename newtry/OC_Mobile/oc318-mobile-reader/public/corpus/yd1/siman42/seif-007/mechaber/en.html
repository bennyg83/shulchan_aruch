If there were two gall bladders in the liver, one here and one here, and one duct pours bile to both of them; and when a person stands the animal upright, bile returns to the place where the duct divides bile and pours to both of them; but when they lie as they stand in the animal's life they do not pour into one another — there is to permit in an animal of Israel. {Rama: A gall bladder recognizable from side to side of the liver — if they pour into one another it is one and kosher (so explained the words of Mordechai that Isur ve-Heter HaArukh brought, general rule 52 dalet-tet); and if they do not pour into one another — treifah.}
