There is to permit in animal of Israel. Bach challenged: Rashba's responsa that Beit Yosef brought wrote stam to be stringent to beware and did not write to permit in animal of Israel; and Shach s.k. 10 wrote therefore he permitted since many poskim permit entirely with two gall bladders. And he wrote well; still Bach's difficulty stands: even Rashba saw with his eyes there is reason to side; for in any case many permit in removed gall; nevertheless he wrote to be stringent; but this permission is per those who say excess is like entirely removed; but Rashba holds like removed and it is puncture — therefore all agree with two gall bladders treifah if somewhat connected — this is gall puncture and all agree treifah; and Rashba follows his view and there is no siding to permit; but for us who establish it is not puncture but like entirely removed — therefore for one who permits removed gall, all the more in excess; and there is siding here to permit.
