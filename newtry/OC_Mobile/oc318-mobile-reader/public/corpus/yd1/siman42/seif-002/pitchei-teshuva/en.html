Missing gall bladder. In Sefer Chamudei Daniel he wrote: once a gall bladder was found whose skin was thick and inside white moisture and it is not bitter — treifah; end; and see Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot s.k. 2 who also doubted this law and declared treifah from doubt — see there; and requires further study whether tasting liver helps in such a case per Rikanti brought in acharonim in incident: small pouch was at gall bladder, tasted and was not bitter, permitted — not two gall bladders but a blister; if so here too perhaps main gall was absorbed in liver and this is only blister; or perhaps only where one is as required do we say so, not here. Further requires further study: if all moisture is sweet and only in part bitter taste — whether to permit in such a case — requires further study; and see s.k. following.
