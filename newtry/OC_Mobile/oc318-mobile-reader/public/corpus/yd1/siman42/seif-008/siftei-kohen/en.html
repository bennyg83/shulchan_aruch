And the deer, etc. But in Sha'arei Shamayim that Maharai composed, sha'ar 10, he wrote that deer have no gall bladder — not considered as removal, for so is the entire species; and so is in Hagahot Sha'ar HaDin siman 88; and so Agudah Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 49: tradition that deer have no gall bladder; and so Or Zarua general rule 52 law 9.
