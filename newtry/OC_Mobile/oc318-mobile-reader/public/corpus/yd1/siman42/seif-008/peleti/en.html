There are species of birds without gall bladder — one must infer from Hagahot HaRosh beginning Avodah Zarah in name of Ra'avyah: he infers from teaching olive in place of gall bladder and not "at gall bladder" — removed gall kosher; see there. And difficulty: there are species without gall bladder — how teach stam at gall bladder? Those without gall — what do they teach? Therefore it teaches in place where gall should be. And if one holds they truly have no gall — no olive needed in place of gall; per his view removed gall kosher; nevertheless we require olive — see what I wrote beginning siman 41. And it must be he holds like Rosh: olive measure specifically in ox and large rams per Rambam; and if so olive cited is in these species; and these species have gall bladder — we find only deer and ram among small game and turtledoves among small birds, not among large species; and see below siman 48.
