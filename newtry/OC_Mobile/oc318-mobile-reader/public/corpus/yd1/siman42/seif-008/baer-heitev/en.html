And the deer. Shach wrote: deer have no gall bladder and it is not considered removal since the entire species is so (Tosafot Chullin daf 112 and in Sefer Sha'arei Shamayim wrote; and some Bach that they have no gall bladder at all and have in intestines like dove and species of quail and sparrow-freedom; and some have gall bladder in stomach and some in ears; and deer and ram — their gall bladder is in the tail; and see Radak Hoshea siman 7). And if the liver was removed from that species — one must leave an olive's amount in the place where gall bladder should be in other species; and the reason: gemara says olive in place of gall bladder and did not say "at gall bladder"; and see Mateh Yehonatan siman 89.
