There are species of birds that have no gall bladder, such as turtledoves and young pigeons; and one does not forbid them since the entire species is so. And a deer has no gall bladder in the liver, but it has one below near its tail. {Rama: One who has before him a bird whose species he does not recognize and no gall bladder is found — one does not say that certainly its entire species has no gall bladder; rather we say that presumably it has a gall bladder until one knows that this species has no gall bladder (responsum Rashba siman 108).}
