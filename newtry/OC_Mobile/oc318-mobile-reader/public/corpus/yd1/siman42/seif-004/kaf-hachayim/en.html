G) Gall bladder protruding between ribs outward between skin and flesh, and whole gall bladder found, inflated it and rose in inflation and no puncture found at all — kosher. Tzitz Eliezer siman 9; and disputed responsum Mishpetei Shmuel siman 130 who declared treifah — see there; and Peri Chadash ot 3 agreed; and so Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 5. Lechem Panim end ot 3. Beit Lechem Yehudah end ot 3. Mishnat Zekenim ot 1. Zivchei Ra'anan ot 119. She'erit Menachem end ot 3. Toldot Zevi ot 5. Ananah there ot 6. Zivchei Tzedek ot 2. Turei Zahav there ot 6. Beit Yitzchak in Taharat HaBayit ot 1. Kometz Bikkurim in laws of other treifot general rule 5 ot 8. And how so? If skin whole from outside; but if punctured from outside side — there is to attribute to thorn from illness and declare treifah. Peri Chadash there. Lechem Panim there. Mishnat Zekenim there. Zivchei Ra'anan there. She'erit Menachem there. Toldot Zevi there. Beit Yitzchak in Taharat HaBayit there. Kometz Bikkurim there. Zivchei Tzedek ot 8; and wrote: and not like Taz there. And in our opinion who permitted: Zivchei Ra'anan wrote: even if punctured from outside — does not emerge from doubt, lest via illness; and therefore wrote: in place of Peri Megadim there is to be lenient — see there. However from rest of aforementioned poskim who are many it is implied: if punctured from outside in every case — declare treifah. And see below siman 49 seif 4 and our words there ot 35.
