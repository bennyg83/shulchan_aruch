See responsum Zichron Yosef siman 4 who brought in name of Rashal: correct to rinse liver at tasting place from visible blood; and wrote on this it is only loss of Israel's money, for Rikanti wrote (brought in Ba'er Heitev s.k. 3): if tasted and bitter found and tasted again and no bitterness sensed — kosher; we attribute first licking removed all bitterness; and therefore concern perhaps he removes bitterness by rinsing and afterward will not sense bitter taste and will declare treifah improperly; therefore rely on Peri Chadash that here it suffices with ordinary licking — see there. And see responsum Shevut Yaakov chelek 1 siman 23 who wrote: that we taste liver is even if pouch too removed (meaning lacking — see Peri Megadim) — not like responsum Hilchot Ketannot — see there.
