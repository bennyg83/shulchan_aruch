And if not — treifah. Ba'er Heitev and what he wrote: in responsum Orach Chaim permits if liver lost before tasting — already Peri Megadim seized: on contrary his view to declare treifah — see there; and see Sefer Bnei Chayya in name of Rav Maharach in Navasht to permit — see there; and responsum Shevut Yaakov chelek 2 siman 66 also wrote in name of Orach Chaim to permit; and so he ruled practically; wrote further he stood on experience many times no bitter taste in liver; saw gall stuck in intestines and fat; sometimes small gall from bird fat not visible; sometimes bitterness poured via stripping intestines — therefore seemed simple: if intestines and fat lost and afterward no gall in liver — attribute stripped with intestines — see there.
