(Siman 62 Shach s.k. 3) And see that in pure animals, where there is eivar min hachai, it is forbidden to give it to children of Noah, etc. Peri Chadash disagrees with what is written in responsa Emunat Shmuel that one does not transgress lifnei iver when he himself is not forbidden in that thing; and so Taz, as Peri Megadim wrote; and so is proven in Tosafot Bava Metzia (daf 10b); and greater than this is proven in Tosafot Chagigah (14) that even where there is a commandment regarding the act, nevertheless lifnei iver applies when his act is for one forbidden in it — such as teaching Torah to an idolater; and so one can learn from what Tosafot wrote in Ketubot (beginning daf 40) that otherwise it should have said that a verse was needed to say that there is upon him prohibition for the one who marries her. I again found that he sensed from Tosafot Chagigah in Sefer Shoshanat HaAmakim general rule 10; and he explained in the name of responsa Bnei Chayya that he distinguishes between that case of handing it to him and between when he feeds him by hand and teaches him, as feeding him. And Rav Shoshanat HaEtz wrote that he did not know from where this distinction. And in my humble opinion, in this there is no sugya of Pesachim (daf 22) that does not establish it in impure and still convulsing, and in such a case where there is no eivar min hachai in Israel and there is for a child of Noah — only we learn that in this there is no matter of giving; and accordingly there is no also what the Tur Bet Shmuel wrote on responsa Emunat Shmuel from the Gemara Chullin (daf 33) that one does not invite an idolater for innards, even though it is permitted for Israel — one can say that inviting to his house is like feeding him by hand.
