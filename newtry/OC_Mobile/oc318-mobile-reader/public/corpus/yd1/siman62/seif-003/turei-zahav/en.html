The slipped limb. Meaning: and not broken — it is written in Darkei Moshe — and these are his words: it is written in Or Zarua: a dangling limb is forbidden even if it returned and was bound well, since once it was forbidden once it has no permission again; we are concerned perhaps it did not heal well enough, end of his words; and it does not appear that if we say since it was bound, certainly it did not go outside — for the matter of permitting the animal, as the Tur wrote siman 55 — even though it is a concern of treifah d'oraisa, all the more so we rely on this for the prohibition of a dangling limb, which is rabbinical, end of his words.
