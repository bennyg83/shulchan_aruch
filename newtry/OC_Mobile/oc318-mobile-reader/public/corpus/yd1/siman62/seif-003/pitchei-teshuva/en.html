The separated limb. See Bach; and see in Shach that the reason for his challenge on Bach is that we hold that for idolaters it applies also in impurity; and if so, it is forbidden to give it to them; and see in responsa Emunat Shmuel siman 14 that he wrote to reconcile their words; and he made a new principle: where it is permitted for him and he forbids only for a child of Noah — it is permitted to give, for it is not similar to giving a cup of wine to a nazir, for even though for him it is permitted, nevertheless the Torah forbade him when he becomes a nazir; but in a prohibition that is permitted for him, like eivar min hachai in impure animals — it is permitted to give to a child of Noah, and he is not warned regarding lifnei iver; and that which he cited in separation the language who is there anything — it refers to the prohibition of giving, thus end of his words, see there. And at first glance there is proof for his words from Pesachim daf 22: and behold eivar min hachai etc. — behold for dogs it is permitted, see there; and see in Tosafot there and in Shach above siman 27; and requires further study; and nevertheless for practical law requires further study.
