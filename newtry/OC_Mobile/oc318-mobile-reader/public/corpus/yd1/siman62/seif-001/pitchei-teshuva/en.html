Forbidden. Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot — what he wrote regarding giving eivar min hachai to a child of Noah; and see on this in responsa Penei Aryeh siman 74 and responsa Zichron Yitzchak siman 108; and see in responsa Beit Efrayim in the introduction to the Yoreh De'ah section what he wrote on this. [And see in responsa Chatam Sofer siman 19 that he was aroused on a new matter according to what is explained in Tosafot Chullin 64a s.v. she'im rakamah regarding eggs — it is a novelty that there is nothing that comes from a living animal and is permitted; if so, it appears one should forbid eggs for a child of Noah in Talmudic times — for regarding Israel the Torah revealed, and not for a child of Noah, like still convulsing that is permitted for Israel and not for a child of Noah; and the Rambam holds chapter 9 of Laws of Kings that even a pure animal fit for Israel — still convulsing is forbidden for a child of Noah, and he has no who is there anything. And even for those who disagree and hold who is there anything — nevertheless in treifah and impurity that are forbidden in any case for Israel, they are forbidden for a child of Noah; and if so, here too eggs are forbidden for a child of Noah — at least treifah eggs; and according to the Rambam all eggs are forbidden. And he wrote that accordingly it is astonishing how they were accustomed in Talmudic times to sell eggs to an idolater, whereas it is explicitly permitted in the Talmud and poskim; see below end of seif 6; and even though one can say because it is common for them in any case etc., nevertheless one should wonder why they give them for free — and this is forbidden even where it is common for them, as is proven from what Tosafot wrote Pesachim 92; however, whole eggs are plainly permitted to give them as a gift, since there is room to suspend that one will seat fowl on them to raise chicks — one fixes them for that purpose, it is permitted; for in anything where there is room to suspend, we rely, as explained in mishnah 8 chapter 5 of Sheviit; and also to give them live treifah eggs one can permit, for perhaps he does not fix them for eating but for work, in the manner of doing many works with treifah eggs, like the work of glue and the like. However, to invite them for foods kneaded with eggs (according to one who holds thus, and according to one who holds thus for the Rambam even in fit animals; and according to those who disagree, in treifot) or that were cooked with eggs — from where do we permit? And one cannot say taste is like essence is not forbidden for a child of Noah — on the contrary, for a child of Noah even in a thousand it is not nullified, and even taste for detriment is forbidden for them; and he left it requiring further study, see there where he elaborated on this.]
In domesticated animal. In Shelah daf 303 he wrote that an animal created through Sefer Yetzirah, as we find in the Gemara that he created a third-grown calf every erev Shabbat — it is permitted to eat it while still alive; and this was the deed of the tribes that Yosef brought, that their daughter grazed to their father, that they ate eivar min hachai, for Yosef thought it was a complete animal, see there; and it appears that nevertheless it is forbidden to eat it without slaughter rabbinically because of marit ayin, as above siman 13 regarding ben peku'ah nine months old that spread over the ground; and one applies all the laws stated there. And according to what is written there in the name of Perishat Derekhim that slaughter is not required, but piercing is required; and if not, it is forbidden because of you shall not eat upon the blood, see there — automatically the same law applies here; and accordingly it is difficult to understand the words of the author of Shelah mentioned above in what he wrote and this was the deed of the tribes.
