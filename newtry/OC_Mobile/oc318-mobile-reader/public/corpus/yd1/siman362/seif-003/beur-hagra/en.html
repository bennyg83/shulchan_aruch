(Supplement) There is no, etc. As written in Bava Batra 100b: "one who sells, etc."; and there, 101b: "but these are planks, etc."; and in the mishnah there: "Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says: everything, etc." — and that is the meaning of "unless, etc." — and he ruled like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. (End.)
