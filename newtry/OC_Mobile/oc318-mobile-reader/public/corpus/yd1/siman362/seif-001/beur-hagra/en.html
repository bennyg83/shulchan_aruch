(Likkut) And if they placed him in a coffin. As stated in the Yerushalmi, Moed Katan chapter 1 and Sanhedrin chapter 6: at first they would bury in trenches, etc., and place them in a coffin. The Rosh brings this, and it is in the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, siman 363 seif 4.
Nevertheless, etc. This is the Yerushalmi at the end of Kilayim, and in Ketubot chapter 12, brought by the Tur.
(Likkut) Nevertheless it is proper, etc. The Yerushalmi there records in Rabbi's will: let my coffin be perforated. The Ramban explains that they should remove the lower board of the coffin and lay him on the earth, for burial in the ground is the mitzvah. One should not say this is only because of love for Eretz Yisrael; rather, even outside the Land it applies, because it says, "and to dust you shall return."
