In a coffin. Gemara (there): Shapur the king said to him etc., that he should make etc.
(Likkut) And if they placed him in a coffin. The Yerushalmi in Moed Katan and Sanhedrin says that originally they buried in temporary cavities and afterward placed the bones in a coffin. Rosh cites it, and Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule accordingly in siman 363, seif 4.
Nevertheless. The Yerushalmi at the end of Kilayim and in Ketubot, cited by Tur. (Likkut) Nevertheless, it is proper. In Rabbi's final instructions the Yerushalmi says, "Let my coffin be perforated." Ramban explains that the lower board should be removed so the body rests upon the earth, because burial in the ground is itself a mitzvah. This is not merely due to affection for Eretz Yisrael, but applies even outside the Land because the verse says, "and to dust you shall return.
