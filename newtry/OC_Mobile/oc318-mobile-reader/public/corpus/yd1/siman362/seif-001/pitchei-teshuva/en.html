If he did not bury it in the earth, he transgresses. Shevut Yaakov, part 2, responsum 101, discusses an amputated leg. Although Ketubot 22b implies that it should be buried, perhaps this is not an absolute burial obligation, and one concerned for danger may not be required to bury it. It must nevertheless be placed in a room inaccessible to kohanim, because a limb from a living person conveys impurity. See siman 369, seif katan 3.
