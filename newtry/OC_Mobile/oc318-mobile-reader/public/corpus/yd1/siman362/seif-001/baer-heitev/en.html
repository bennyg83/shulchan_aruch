Actually. Meaning: even though the coffin is buried in the ground, the coffin should nevertheless not be sealed on every side; rather, the dead should be buried in the earth itself. Levush and Perishah wondered about the present custom to make complete coffins for kohanim, placing a board below on which the dead lies, saying they do so because of the honor of kohanim. On the contrary, it is better to lie on the earth. According to what the Derishah wrote, that nowadays they place shards on his mouth and eyes and that this is in place of earth, it is understandable. Alternatively, it is impossible for the coffins made nowadays not to have holes in the sides, and a hole suffices, as the Tur and poskim wrote. End quote from Shach. One can also say that according to our custom, where we place a pouch of earth under his head, it is considered buried in earth; although the pouch intervenes, since it is of thin linen as is customary, it is not an interruption. For those who place earth of Eretz Yisrael on the face of the dead, it is even more understandable.
