Actually. Meaning that although the coffin itself is buried in the ground, the coffin should not be completely closed on every side. Rather, the deceased should be buried in actual contact with the earth. Levush and Perishah question the present custom of making complete coffins for kohanim, including a bottom board on which the deceased lies, supposedly out of respect for the kohanim. On the contrary, lying directly on the earth is preferable. According to Derishah, however, nowadays we place earthenware over the mouth and eyes, which can count in place of earth. Alternatively, the coffins currently made inevitably contain openings along their sides, and an opening is sufficient, as Tur and the poskim state. Shach. One can further say that according to our custom of placing a pouch of earth beneath the head, the deceased is regarded as buried in earth even though the thin linen pouch separates. This is even clearer for those who place earth from Eretz Yisrael on the face of the deceased.
