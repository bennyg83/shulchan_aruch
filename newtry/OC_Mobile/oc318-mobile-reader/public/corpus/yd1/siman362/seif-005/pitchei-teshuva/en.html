One does not bury a wicked person. See Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 333, concerning one murdered by a killer, whether he should be buried in the graves of his ancestors or be assigned a separate burial place, because some claimed that the four forms of judicial death have not ceased and one liable to beit din execution is not buried with his ancestors. He replied that these claims have no substance, and there is clear proof from Sanhedrin 48a. He adds that had they not asked him he might have left them to their custom, but once the question was brought before him he would not issue such a ruling. Rather, the deceased should be buried in the graves of his ancestors with the honor appropriate to him; see there.
