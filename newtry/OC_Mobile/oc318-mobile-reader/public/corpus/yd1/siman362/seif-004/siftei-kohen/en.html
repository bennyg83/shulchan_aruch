One does not place, etc. The Bach wrote that this is even if it is impossible to bury elsewhere. Therefore one must protest in communities where the buriers do this, that they should not place one on top of another unless there is certainly an interruption of six tefachim. However, if it is impossible to bury him in another way, it is certainly permitted, and so it is implied from the responsum of Rav Hai Gaon brought by the Tur in the next siman; this is simple.
