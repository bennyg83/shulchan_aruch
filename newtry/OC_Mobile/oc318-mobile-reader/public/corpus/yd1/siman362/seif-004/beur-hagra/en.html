One may not place, etc. Toras HaAdam in the name of Evel Rabbasi: one may not place two coffins one atop the other. If one did, the owner of the upper coffin is compelled to remove it, for one may not treat the dead disrespectfully.
If there is, etc. As stated in Bava Basra there concerning deepening the grave.
Six handbreadths. The Tur. Toras HaAdam, however, writes three handbreadths, though it records that Rav Hai was asked and answered that there must be six handbreadths between one deceased and another, three for the required surrounding earth of this one and three for that one. See Rashbam, Bava Basra 100b, s.v. Ushnayim Kenegdo; the same applies above. This is unlike the Rambam's explanation and Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat, siman 217, seif 6. (End.)
