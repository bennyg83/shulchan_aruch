(Seif 3) The wall of the grave separates; its width is six tefachim. See the Rambam, Hilkhot Mekhirah chapter 21, from whom it appears that he rules like the first Tanna and not like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and that he makes no distinction between hard rock and loose earth. Rav Ovadiah Bartenura also writes that the halachah does not follow Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. But from the Ramban and Rashba in Torat HaAdam, cited by the Beit Yosef here, since they write, “therefore one distances enough for the niches to stand,” it appears that they rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, that everything depends on the rock.
