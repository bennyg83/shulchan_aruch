Rava asked Rav Chisda this question in Bava Kamma 96a, and it was resolved there. Rashi explains: one lends his fellow merchandise valued according to a particular currency, so that he fixed a sum of money for him. The Tur paraphrases it as one who lends his fellow a specified sum in currency.
The statement of Rav Ashi there.
There, as in the incident involving Rav Pappa and Rav Huna son of R' Yehoshua, etc. In that case Rav Chisda resolved that he gives him the currency current at that time, because when one-fifth was added there is no gain in the value of the metal after accounting for the loss in smelting and the wage of the minter. Rosh.
The Rif and the Rosh. Rambam likewise writes this in chapter 4 of Hilchos Malveh, and Sefer HaTerumah likewise writes it in section 8.
