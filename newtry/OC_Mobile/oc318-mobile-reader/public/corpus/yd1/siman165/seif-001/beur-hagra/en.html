Such as, etc. In accordance with Rabbeinu Chananel's explanation cited by Tosafot there, s.v. “Until,” and so too the Rif and the other decisors explain.
The same applies to a loan, etc. This is the view of the Rif and the other decisors, unlike the Raavad, who wrote that the ruling applies specifically in the stated case and that the authorities added the loan case because of interest. Sefer HaTerumos already challenged him and wrote: This is a very remote rationale, that one party should profit while the other loses his principal, returning fifty to him in place of one hundred. The Rosh there likewise challenges him.
And if they depreciated, etc. And Rashi there s.v. veha kazilin, etc.
Where they made, etc. As stated in the second chapter of Gittin 19b.
Or courts, etc. As stated first chapter (10b).
And if king decreed, etc. As stated CM siman 369; and even per first reasoning there seif 5 in Hagah ; nevertheless it is coin like land that belongs to kingdom; and see Sefer HaTerumah there seif 8.
