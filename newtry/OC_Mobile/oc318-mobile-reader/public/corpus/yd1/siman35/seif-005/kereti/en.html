That which we permit, etc. — and Pri Chadash also in this his view is to permit, since there is a count — behold on each side; and see Peleti: in a place of loss one may rely on him.
If they are equal on each side — even five on each side according to the acharonim, unlike the hagahah of Mahariu.
And not to declare treifah: and if one ona is found on the left with three cracks, we say to permit, for it is as if there are two and not as if there are three; and the reason: most animals have two on the left, therefore we say presumably this crack is for an ona by itself to equalize it with the majority of the world that mostly have only two.
Only one — meaning to declare treifah; but to permit it is no worse than a crack — Shach.
Wide below — it appears it is only a lump from the lung.
Into the hollow — and even if it does not fill the entire hollow; but if it is larger in quantity than the hollow — treifah — Nekudot HaKesef and Pri Chadash.
But from the front — the split is from the body of the lung and falls downward and will come apart; and it is not similar to extra from the front, for it is not from the body of the lung and "eventually will come apart" does not apply.
And treifah — see Peleti what he wrote here, for it is difficult from the Gemara "lung perforated or missing."
