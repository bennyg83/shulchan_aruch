Except when they place the hand gently on it, etc. So Darkei Moshe: such a case came before me and I permitted it; and so agreed most experts in the city, for the reason it is kosher when there is a hollow there is because it has a place to rest and does not rub itself against the walls of the lung and is not destined to come apart or be perforated; and likewise here, since when they place the hand on it gently it descends into the hollow — the same applies when it reaches the animal's walls it descends into the hollow and need not rub; and behold one of the experts did for me on that lung another split like the first and there was a hollow beneath it, and when they inflated the lung it also rose somewhat because of the inflation and descended when they touched it gently with the hand; therefore we agreed to permit, for this is a baraita — end Darkei Moshe:
But from the front the split, etc. And some hold: extra from the front is kosher, for there the extra is of the ona and did not split from the body of the lung and it is strong; but a split that split from the body of the lung where there is a hollow beneath it, and when they turn the lung the split falls into the hollow — then it appears it split from the body of the lung and is not strong and will eventually come apart and is treifah, as explained — end; unlike Maharam in Machazik Brachah who wrote the Rabbenu deals with a place where they forbid extra from the front, and so they practice to permit extra from the front and to declare treifah a split from the front — and this is from the reason I explained:
Extra opposite the groove of the varda, etc. Meaning: beneath the varda opposite the groove; and so is in Darkei Moshe; and some disqualify extra from the front; therefore they needed to permit through the protection of the groove; and the Rabbenu challenged them: if extra is treifah, the same applies to this; and so is implied explicitly in Darkei Moshe; and the practical difference: in a place where they forbid extra from the front one may not permit this except in a time of distress; but in a place where they permit extra from the front it is obvious that this too is kosher; and so in Machazik Brachah:
And one should not rely on this except in a time of distress and great loss. So it must read; and so is in Pri Megadim; thus Darkei Moshe: and it appears to me that some hold a split with a hollow beneath is different, for the reason of the hollow is not protection alone, but that if it fell inside the hollow it would not be extra at all but split from the lung; therefore the hollow remains beneath it, as I wrote that one expert made one split and a hollow beneath it before me and therefore it is kosher; but in other extra — what does protection of the groove help?
