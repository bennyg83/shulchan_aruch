The right lobe adjacent to the gullet in the narrow of the chest at the neck — the lower one that is upper adjacent to the neck — if on its back is found like a myrtle leaf (tarfa de-asa) — kosher, because such is its way to split; and specifically the right one, but not the left; and some say: specifically above half of it, but below half — treifah. {Rama: That which we permit an extra lobe in the row of lobes — nevertheless we require that there not be more lobes on the left than on the right, such as four on the left and three on the right (Mahariu); but if they are equal on each side — three or four lobes on each side, and all stand in the row of lobes — kosher (there in hagahah); and if in one lobe something like a crack is seen, it combines to permit, but not to declare treifah, for we are not concerned about cracks to declare treifah (so found written); a lobe that split and has one root like finger-thickness is not counted except as one (Mahari Mulen); and it is not called an extra lobe unless it has the form of a lobe; but if on the lung's surface a place is found high, wide below and thick above, not sharp at its head — it is called a lump (gavshushit) and kosher (Mahariu and Beit Yosef in the name of Magid Commentary); and likewise if a lobe is found on the lung's surface and a hollow beneath it, and when they inflate the lung all the lobes fall into the hollow and are level with the rest of the lung (Rabbenu Yaakov Weil and Beit Yosef) — even though it does not enter the hollow except when one places the hand gently on it — kosher, for this is not an extra lobe but a splitting of the lung; and since it has a hollow beneath to rest in, it will not come apart like other extra lobes — kosher (some say); and one may not permit such a split except from the back, where the split rests on the hollow; but from the front the split falls downward and will eventually come apart — treifah (so found written); some say: if an extra lobe is found opposite the groove of the varda — its law is like a split with a hollow (Mahari Mulen in the name of R' Meir the slaughterer); and one should not rely on this except in a time of distress and great loss.}
