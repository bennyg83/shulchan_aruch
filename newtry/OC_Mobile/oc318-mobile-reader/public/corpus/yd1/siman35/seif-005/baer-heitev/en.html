Kosher. And Shach wrote: and likewise more than tarfa de-asa (what he wrote "because its way is to split" means because of the narrowness of the chest) — Maharam.
Treifah. And Taz wrote in examinations there are two explanations on this. One: the half is toward the tip of the ona; but if found toward the root of the ona — treifah; and accordingly this measure of half is in the width of the ona. And some say one also measures in the length of the ona — that is, that half in width that we permitted is not all of it but specifically half its length — that is, opposite the windpipe; but not the other side toward the lung; and so Maharshal explained, and one should be stringent like the latter view (and we practice measuring thus: they take the ona of the left and place it next to the upper ona of the right, and from where the left ona ends onward is called the tip side — that is, toward the head — and kosher; and from where the left ona touches toward the lung is called below, and if the split is there — treifah) — Einayim Yeshayim.
Kosher. And Maharshal wrote: what he wrote in Likutim that one may not permit if five are found on each side is foolishness, and if it comes before me I will buy the finest from it — end (and in the hagahah of Mahariu they declare treifah, and Maharshal and Bach permit, and Pri Chadash wrote: I say that whenever on the right side there are three onot as required, what does it matter if there are more on the left, for it is not called exchange unless there is deficiency on the right; therefore one who is lenient did not lose — see there and in Beit Yosef).
To declare treifah. And Shach wrote: if three onot are found on the left and a crack — it is not like four onot; and it implies that even one healthy one combines to permit but not to declare treifah — for example if on the left there is one ona and three cracks, we say one crack helps to permit and the others count for nothing to consider exchange to declare treifah; and we do not say either way; rather we are not concerned about cracks at all to declare treifah; however Taz wrote: if on the right there are two onot and a crack and there is another suspicious matter on the left — that is, there are three onot on the left — one should declare treifah (and R' Yosef ruled to permit, and Maharshal and Bach forbid, and Pri Chadash wrote this is not primary but one may permit simply, and so R' Yosef and Shach in Knesset HaGedolah).
It. And Shach wrote: meaning specifically to declare treifah; but to permit it is no worse than a crack which is kosher.
And kosher. And Shach wrote: and likewise if round above — also kosher; but if found above or below sharp like a cap — it is extra and treifah — Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
And kosher. For the same applies when it reaches the animal's walls it descends into the hollow and need not rub against the wall — kosher — Maharam. And Taz wrote: but if shorter than the hollow — treifah, for then it is not a split but extra (and Pri Chadash and Shach in Knesset HaGedolah disagree with Taz, for even if it is extra, nevertheless there is doubt it will not come apart; rather it comes to exclude when it was longer than the hollow — then treifah).
And treifah. And Shach wrote: and it is not similar to extra from the front which is kosher, for there the extra is of the ona and it is strong; but a split that split from the body of the lung will eventually come apart and is treifah; but if there is no hollow beneath it, our custom is to permit, for this is extra from the front — Shach, Taz; and Einayim Yeshayim wrote: one must know that a split is treifah only when it is like tarfa de-asa specifically after inflating the lung, for when it is so large then certainly it will come apart; but extra on a bed is treifah even when not as large as tarfa de-asa, since it rubs against the ribs — see there in the law of split siman 1 and siman 4.
Great loss. And Shach wrote: all this is according to those who forbid extra from the front; but in a place where they permit, it is obvious this too is kosher — end. And it requires further study: for behold split from the front is treifah even if extra is kosher, as written above; therefore it is difficult on Rama who wrote its law is like a split with a hollow and one should not rely on this, etc.; and it is possible he holds that since it stands opposite the groove and the groove protects it, its law is like a split with a hollow from the back — and it requires further study.
