Nevertheless we require that there not be more onot on the left than on the right; and Pri Chadash challenged s.k. 94: since there are three on the right, what difference if on the left more? — and he concluded from Rambam — see there; and even though my view tends thus, for the Gemara said exchange treifah — meaning missing so only two on the right and three on the left; if all is exchange, even three right and four left — cannot be Rava's intent; Rava holds extra even in the row is treifah; if so this does not exist; if extra exists — treifah; Rava did not intend this; we hold not like Rava to permit extra, yet we cannot innovate treifos in exchange; in great loss one may be lenient on Rambam — Pri Chadash.
But split from the front falls downward and will come apart; and Shach and Pri Chadash wrote: even for those who permit extra from the front, this is different, for the split is from the body of the lung, and when they inflate the lung the split falls downward and it appears it split from the body of the lung and will come apart and is treifah. And one should not challenge: since the reason is that it split from the body of the lung — then why did the Gemara labor siman 47b to find an explanation for "lung perforated or missing" — let us say in such a case it is missing from the body of the lung and split and it is from the front and treifah, and this is what it teaches that in such a case treifah because it will come apart; and if so, perforation and missing are counted as one, for in conclusion we establish it needs only R' Shimon — also missing means perforated. And see below siman 36 where you will find all these difficulties resolved together; and it appears since this treifah is not because of extra but because it will come apart, even less than tarfa de-asa is treifah because it will come apart; and to say if not so large the lung will not come apart — this is strained, and whence do we be lenient on a Torah prohibition? — unlike Maharatz in hagahot of slaughter who is lenient in this.
