It appears: if on one side, etc. And in Shach I wrote thus in the name of Maharam — see there.
(Taz s.k. 10) And it appears Rama also agrees in this, etc. This is not so, for he did not rule thus explicitly in Darkei Moshe; and this is his wording in my book; and he wrote in Hagahot Ashiri, etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote: and it is a simple matter that there is no aspect of prohibition here; and so Darkei Moshe, that so appears from his words in the hagahah; and so the R"tz; and so they practice in Krakow; and so is the essential law, unlike Maharshal Piskei Tosafot siman 33 and Bach who forbid here — end.
(Taz s.k. 12) That if it was shorter than the hollow, etc. This is not so — ultimately, since it rests in the hollow it will not come apart; and perhaps there is a printing error in his wording — it should read: if it was longer than the hollow, and he holds treifah because it presses on the ribs, as Shach wrote in small seif 36 in the name of Darkei Moshe — that is the reason for a split that has a hollow beneath: it does not press on the ribs; but his wording that concludes "then it is not a split" does not seem to mean this.
