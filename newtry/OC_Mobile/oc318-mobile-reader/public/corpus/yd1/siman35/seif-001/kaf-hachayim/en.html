A) [Seif 1] A lung that was perforated — treifah, because wind exits there and it cannot live. Levush. Zivchei Torah ot 1.
D) A lung that was perforated — treifah. Hilchot Melichah; and all lung treifos in these simanim are all on account of being perforated, for the Sages held that if it was struck by one of those lesions, ultimately it will be perforated; and whatever is destined to be perforated is considered as perforated; therefore all treifos explained here are so. Shulchan Melachim ot 1; Chokhmat Adam kelal 9 ot 1; Beit Yitzchak ot 1; Kometz HaMinchah head of the siman.
