And there are those who practice to declare treifah. And so HaRav and other acharonim that such is the practice; and Bach wrote: some disagree with what was written in seif 4 that an extra ona found at the end of the slopes of the ama is kosher; and so in the hagahah: this is called in the row of onot (and so HaRav) — it is different there, for the onot are as they should be and in our count; and if so this protrusion is no worse than other extra standing in the row of onot; but for one missing ona this protrusion does not complete the deficiency; and so Maharshal divides Piskei Tosafot siman 34; and in Ma'adanei Melech he left this in need of further study.
