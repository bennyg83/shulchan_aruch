If on one side of the lung one lobe is missing, but at the end of the ama on that side there was a large protrusion like a lobe — even though the way of lobes is to be adjacent to one another and here the ama interrupts between them — kosher. {Rama: And some practice to declare treifah in such a case, because all that is below the varda is called not in the row of lobes; and so they practice in these countries.}
