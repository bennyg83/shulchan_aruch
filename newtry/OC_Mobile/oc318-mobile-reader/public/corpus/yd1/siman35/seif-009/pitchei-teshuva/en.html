To declare treifah. Baer Heitev: however Taz wrote, etc.; and see in Dagul Mervavah who wrote that even Maharshal and Taz forbade only when the crack also combines yet there is equal on each side right and left; but if on the right there were two onot and two cracks, even though on the left there were three onot literally — nevertheless since through the cracks there are more on the right than on the left, even according to Maharshal and Taz it is kosher — see there.
