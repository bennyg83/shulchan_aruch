The varda completes, and some say, etc. Beit Yosef and Perishah and Bach wrote: the reason for their dispute — Rambam and his supporters follow their reason who permit extra from the front, evidently extra from the front and the row of onot are one matter; and Rashi and his supporters follow their reason who declare extra from the front treifah — end; and accordingly all the more if no varda is found at all, only extra from the front — it completes for the Mechaber and his supporters; and accordingly the Rabbenu who wrote such is the custom like Rashi and his supporters — not from their reason, for they declare extra from the front treifah, and the Rabbenu in seif 3 wrote they practice to permit extra from the front; and "one should not change the custom" refers only to the reason that we hold missing varda is treifah — if so, if it combines to an ona it is missing; and so is explained in the examination of HaRav in the name of the Rav and R' Avigdor; and accordingly if extra of an ona from the front is in the manner I wrote in s.k. 17 — it completes for all; however it is difficult accordingly: if so, that which the Rabbenu wrote "and some say it does not complete" — that is from the reason they disqualify extra from the front, and immediately he says "and such is the custom" from another reason; also Darkei Moshe did not mention another reason at all. And it appears the Rav did not hold as Beit Yosef and his supporters, but held the reason of the poskim that it does not complete is because they hold that since they do not stand literally in the row of onot they do not complete the deficiency; and so is implied from the words of the Rav that HaRav brought there, who wrote: it may be that it does not combine even though if the varda is missing it is kosher — end, meaning nevertheless it does not combine since it does not stand literally in the row of onot (and Maharam in Sdei Chemed challenged HaRav on this not according to law in matters that are not correct — see there); and so is implied explicitly in Pri Megadim; and so is implied in Igur that he brought in his conclusion the words of Tur: when the varda is missing or extra — kosher; and afterward he brought in his conclusion the words of those who permit extra from the front; and afterward he brought in his conclusion the words of Semak that it does not complete the varda for a missing ona — examine; and accordingly even extra of an ona does not complete — for example if the varda is as it should be and there is also extra from the front with it — it does not complete for us:
