Completes. And Shach wrote: but on the left, if one ona is missing, even if varda is found on the left it does not complete — so all hold; and Bach disagrees.
Custom. And the reason is in Shach: because it does not stand in the row of onot; even though we hold extra from the front is kosher; and accordingly even extra of an ona from the front does not complete; also one may declare treifah and say it does not complete, for if so it is missing varda and treifah — see Darkei Moshe daf 107. And Taz wrote s.k. 13: if extra is found in a bed above the ona adjacent to the spine inside the lung — it is kosher; and it appears to me treifah, for this is certainly included more in "on its back" mentioned in the Talmud (and he also wrote what Rama wrote regarding two vardot with one root that is like finger-thickness — even though there is no finger-thickness there; and a certain sage wrote: nevertheless we require the length of the root like a finger between the two vardot; and it appears to me the measure is the height of the root of both before they branch must be like a finger).
