All this is called exchange and treifah — the acharonim, headed by Maharshal, disagree to permit them, for they have no root in the Gemara; and in truth these can be imagined that the early ones erred in this, and certainly there is room to permit; but it is far-fetched to believe they considered there is a root in the Gemara, for if this exchange were the Gemara's intent, the Gemara should have said first: form and shape of the ona and its place, and afterward say exchange is treifah; but since it did not mention this at all, how can exchange and treifah apply to these exchanges that never appeared in the Gemara? And great is the heart of decisors — Mahariu brought in his responsum, and it is clear it was a received tradition in his hand from ancients, for Torah was transmitted to the sages of Ashkenaz and the valley; they found and fenced and established and accepted to act according to the slaughter of Mahariu — if so, how can one abolish it? Granted, if we could attribute it to error that he thought the Gemara meant thus and erred in understanding — it would be mistaken custom; but this does not enter my mind that such great ones of the generation, from whom Torah literally went out in Ashkenaz and its branches, would err in such simple matters; this is only tradition in their hand from ancients, and they accepted to act thus; if so, it is a complete prohibition to change this custom, since it is not error; and who knows — perhaps they knew many matters in tradition that were not mentioned in Chazal's words, like meat that remained three days without salting, and the like; God forbid to change; therefore many rely on Maharshal and are very lenient — God forbid to be lenient, for behold this breaches the fence of what we received from our fathers, who had tradition to forbid such exchanges — God forbid to say they erred in a simple matter; and do not forsake your mother's teaching.
If two vardot are found — Taz in s.k. 4 challenged: below we ruled extra from the front is kosher — why declare treifah for two vardot? Answer: we do not forbid on account of extra, only since it should have varda designation, all extra is considered as removed and missing varda is treifah — end. And still difficult: for the Gemara says this "between and between" that came, etc. — he thought to declare treifah; he said: all these animals of the baraita have it and call them ayinunita de-varda; and Rashi explained one existed, and from this he explained if missing — kosher, for it was thought to declare treifah as stated; but Rabbenu Ephraim explained one, and certainly only the name was two; nevertheless he permitted. If so, difficult with two vardot found — how do we explain in the Gemara "all these animals of the baraita," etc.? If the explanation is like Rashi that it was only one ayinunita de-varda — if so, Rashi and Tosafot raised that missing varda is kosher; if so, with two vardot found it should also be kosher, for we cannot declare treifah because of extra, for missing varda is kosher; and if they explain like Rabbenu Ephraim with two vardot — the case was obvious it is kosher, for he permitted explicitly because "all these animals of the baraita"; and it is strained to say for us "all these animals of the baraita" does not apply, for it is not common in reality — this is against the poskim's implication; it must be explained like Rashi; nevertheless even according to that explanation missing varda is kosher, nevertheless with two it is treifah, for Ramban holds all extra is considered as removed and it is a perforation in that place; and even here, as considered removed varda, it is as if the lung was perforated; and so Shulchan Aruch below regarding liver: according to Ramban if two livers adhere to the mesentery — treifah, even though removed liver is kosher, nevertheless this is as perforated in the mesentery; and so one may say here. And one may resolve: except I did not know whether Tosafot decided if according to R' Eliezer the case was with two ayinunitot, and from this they decided that missing varda is treifah — whence do we know, since he does not permit extra from the front, one may say therefore he thought to declare treifah because it is extra; but not because removed varda is considered — what of it if varda is missing, since it is not received that there must be varda, only the prohibition is extra not in the row of onot; whence do we add treifos? Rava said five onot and above said or missing and extra treifah, and did not mention varda at all; also somewhat proves it does not refer to missing, for "extra" does not refer to it, and none mention varda; if so, whence did the ruling come to forbid? And there is room to resolve: and behold it is astonishing in Rambam who ruled missing varda or two is kosher and extra from the front also kosher — if so, what reason and for what purpose did he tell him "all these animals of the baraita have it" from Scripture to declare treifah? If because of varda — in every case kosher; if because of extra — extra from the front is kosher. It must be said Rambam holds that permitting all extra from the front is because we see all these animals have it — we learn the count of five onot is not fixed, for varda is also counted as extra ona; and we saw it has one and two; even all extra from the front is kosher; unlike if not for "animals of the baraita have it," extra from the front would be treifah — examine.
