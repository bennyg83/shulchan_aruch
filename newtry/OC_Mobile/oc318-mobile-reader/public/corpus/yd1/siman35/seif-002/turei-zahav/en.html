Onot. Its meaning is language of ear; and ama — language of mother, meaning the large one from which the onot separate and it is the main lung — so Rosh wrote. Ayinunita de-varda: meaning a small ona of varda, on account of its delicacy and redness — Rashi.
The forms of the onot, etc. These laws in this hagahah Maharshal brought and wrote on them: and I am astonished what basis there is for these exchanges and to add treifos on what was not mentioned in the Talmud even by hint, for it only said five onot the lung has — three on the right and two on the left; but all these distinctions — who mentioned them that exchange should apply to them? This is only destruction of Jewish money — end. And my master may he live long wrote: and nevertheless, where there is no great financial loss, one should not rule permission in what they practiced as forbidden, even though by law there is no prohibition — end. And Maharshal wrote in the name of examinations: if the varda is larger than the ona next to it — meaning the ona adjacent to the ama — treifah; and he wrote well, for its name is ayinunita, and if it is so large that name does not apply — end his words. He also wrote: if the ama is above and the onot below — treifah; and it appears to me that where there is another suspicious matter, even though one does not declare treifah on its account, nevertheless it combines with the suspicious matter above and treifah.
And the varda: if missing, etc. The reason: since in some animals there is and in some there is not, one should not declare treifah for any change of vardot. Changed its designation and form — Maharshal explained: when it is drawn out like an ona and not round — certainly for an ona there is no concern if it is not drawn and sharp, for only regarding ayinunita do we say changed designation is treifah, for treifos laws depend on the ways of lung growth, and Chazal established that an ona is sometimes round and sometimes sharp, but varda's law is round — end Igur. And he also wrote: if the ayinunita is entirely round without a groove — treifah, for its designation is known and according to its designation they called it by this name — end.
Whether two vardot are found, etc. At first glance it is difficult: for Rama wrote in seif 3 that our custom is to permit all extra from the front; and it appears here the disqualification is not on account of extra but since there is extra and all extra is considered as removed — if so the varda that should be there is missing; and likewise changed place — meaning it stands on the left side; and Tur wrote in the name of Rosh that it is treifah because of extra; and accordingly we should have permitted, for this is extra from the front; except that we forbid this because the varda is missing in the place where it should be; and accordingly treifos of varda are only when both are in the place of the varda and do not have one root like finger-thickness after inflation; nevertheless if you find an extra ona inside, even in the place where the varda should be, there is no disqualification for two vardot, for the form of the ona is not like a varda, as Tosafot wrote in chapter Eizehu Mekoman regarding that "between and between," etc.; and the same if two vardot are found, one on the right and one on the left — "extra as removed" does not apply since the extra does not stand in its proper place — compare extra of the leg siman 55 seif 4; and from the reason of extra there is no disqualification, for it is from the front and Rama already testified that the custom is to permit all extra from the front — implying even not in the row of onot, as explained.
One root. Magid Commentary in the name of Or Zaru'a: even though there is no finger-thickness there; and a certain sage wrote: nevertheless we require the length of the root like a finger between the two vardot; and it appears to me the measure of the height of the root of both before they branch must be like a finger.
And all these matters, etc. This does not refer to what was said regarding missing or two vardot or changed place — these treifos were mentioned in Rashi and Tosafot Piskei Tosafot; rather it refers to the other laws mentioned in the hagahah. And Maharshal wrote to permit the laws forbidden on account of the pouch that Rama mentioned, for the pouch was not mentioned in the Gemara; and regarding changed designation I already wrote shortly in his name that it is treifah; and regarding the law of inverted that Rama mentioned here — treifah when the varda is inverted — meaning its way is that it has a groove aligned opposite the cut between ona and ama, its back opposite the heart and its face opposite the kidneys; and if inverted, such as the groove opposite the heart and its back opposite the cut — treifah, as written in our examinations; and Maharshal wrote on this: for this there is no reason or smell, since it did not change its form or place, only the groove is inverted — what of it? This is only loss of Jewish money; and if my effort succeeds I would abolish it — end.
