Five lobes (meaning: like ears) the lung has — three on the right and two on the left — when the animal is hung by its legs and its intestines face the slaughterer, for then the animal's right and the slaughterer's right are one; and it has additionally on the right side one small lobe called ayinunita de-varda, and it does not stand in the order of the lobes but is distant from them toward the face; and if one is missing from the right, or from the left, or there were two on the right and three on the left — treifah. {Rama: The forms of the lobes of the right are not like those of the left, and if their form was exchanged — treifah; and likewise when the middle lobe of the right is larger than the lower one — treifah (Mahariu); and if the lobe of the left upper one is larger than the upper lobe of the right — treifah; and likewise if the ama of the right is larger than that of the left — all this is called exchange (chalif) and treifah (Mahar"a of Prague).} And the varda: if it is missing, or if it has two, or if it is found on the left side — kosher. {Rama: And in these lands they practice to declare treifah both when two vardot are found, or if it is missing, or if it changed its place (Mahariu and Or Zaru'a) — and such is customary, for many authorities hold thus and one should not change the custom; and likewise if it changed its accustomed designation and form — treifah (Igur); if two vardot are found in it and they have one root like the thickness of a finger, even though there is a crack in it — kosher (Magid Commentary in Badei Yeshayim) (Or Zaru'a in the name of Ravyah and so Mahariu, and it is in Hagahot Ashiri); and if one is inverted and the other is not inverted — we follow the larger one, and if it is not inverted — kosher (ancient examinations). The varda's way is to be in a pouch; and if no pouch is found for it, or it has two pouches, or it has a pouch and is not placed in it — and even when they place it inside it comes back out when the lung is inflated — treifah (Kol Bo in the name of Rabbenu Moshe bar Yehudah and hagahot of Mahariu and ancient examinations and somewhat from oral tradition — actual practice in Wirmisha in the name of Maharash of Ziria). And all these laws are only in places that practice to declare treifah for two vardot or no varda at all; but in places that permit, all these exchanges are kosher (Beit Yosef). If part of the varda is missing, one should examine the lobes or the ama on the left side (some texts: on the right side); if the missing piece is found — some say kosher, for thus is sometimes found in kids and lambs (so found written); and some permit if it has rose-form at its root like finger-thickness even though it has no rose-form on its head, only that it not be inverted and not have changed its place; and all these matters may be treated leniently since many permit in every regard.}
