And the varda: if missing — this is Rambam's view, who holds it was not received among Chazal that there must be a varda; therefore if missing or extra there is no concern, even if they do not stand one next to the other — all kosher.
If two vardot are found — Taz gave the reason: certainly extra from the front is kosher, only this is extra varda and it is like missing varda; and see Peleti where I elaborated and debated Rambam's words — see there.
Or if missing: and if it is less than tarfa de-asa — kosher. Changed place: its way is to stand below the onot and forward; and the groove in inflation is between the cut of ona and ama; and if the groove is inverted so its height is opposite the cut and the groove opposite the heart — treifah — so Mahariu and Maharshal; and Pri Chadash disagrees — it requires further study.
