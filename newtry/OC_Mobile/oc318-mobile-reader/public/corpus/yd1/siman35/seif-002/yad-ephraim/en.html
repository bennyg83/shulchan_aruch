(There s.k. 4) Except when both are in the place of the varda and are not like finger-thickness between them — Taz in this section.
(Shach s.k. 8) Or its back — must read: and its back.
(Taz s.k. 60) Must be like a finger. And in great financial loss it suffices that there be one root like a finger in its length; and so in its height. And without great financial loss, the entire length of one root must be like finger-thickness in its height.
(Shach s.k. 15) But from the left side — kosher. It implies: on the right, even if the varda does not benefit from two pouches, nevertheless treifah; and specifically when the pouch has a lip at its mouth; but if it has no lip, it is only a protrusion and not called two pouches; nevertheless if varda is found without a lip, it is not called missing pouch — acharonim.
