For the lung. Meaning: ona (lobe) — language of ear; and ama — language of mother, meaning the large one from which the onot separate, and it is the main lung; and ayinunita de-varda is so called on account of its delicacy and redness — Rashi on Chullin.
From the left. And Shach wrote: even if there are four on the right and one is missing on the left — treifah, unlike Torat Chaim who permits in this.
Treifah. And Shach wrote: and likewise also four on the left and three on the right is exchange and treifah.
And treifah. And Maharshal wrote: I am astonished at these treifos that Rama mentioned that were not mentioned in our Talmud except three on the right and two on the left, and he does not declare treifah except the exchange mentioned in the Talmud; and Bach wrote one should rely on Maharshal's words in a place of great financial loss. And so Pri Chadash: we permit in such a case. And if the middle ona is larger than the upper ona of the right — kosher. And likewise if on the right side there are four onot and on the upper ona there was a small ona and the middle ona was larger than the ona adjacent to the ama — kosher — end. And see in Sefer Einayim Yeshayim these laws. Responsum of Noda Biyehudah siman 55 wrote: minor loss is a chicken or dove weight, and anything more than that is great financial loss; and one whose heart misgives him — it appears to me that whatever he lost from capital slightly more than one-sixth is certainly great financial loss without doubt. And one may distinguish between poor and rich, for for him it is not great financial loss except beyond one-sixth — end. And Maharshal also wrote: if the varda is larger than the ona next to it — meaning the ona adjacent to the ama — treifah. And Taz wrote well: for its name is ayinunita, and if it is so large that name does not apply. And he also wrote: if the ama is above and the onot below — treifah; and Taz wrote: it appears to me that where there is another suspicious matter, even though one does not declare treifah on its account, nevertheless it combines with the suspicious matter above and it is treifah.
Two. Even if they do not stand one next to the other — Shach. And specifically when they have two pouches; but if one is placed in a pouch and the second is not placed in a pouch, even if the one in the pouch has no varda designation and the second has — kosher even for us (Darkei Moshe daf 106).
Vardot. And Taz wrote: and even though we hold in seif 3 that extra from the front is kosher, it must be said that here we declare treifah because all extra is considered as removed; and accordingly treifos of varda are only when both are in the place of the varda and do not have one root like finger-thickness after inflation; nevertheless if you find an extra ona inside, even in the place of the varda, there is no disqualification here for two vardot, for the form of the ona is not like a varda; and the same if two vardot are found, one on the right and one on the left — kosher, for "extra as removed" does not apply since the extra does not stand in its proper place — see siman 55 seif 4.
Missing. And Darkei Moshe wrote: if it is less than a myrtle leaf after inflation — treifah.
Its place. Meaning: its way is to stand below the onot and it has a groove; and when they inflate the lung the groove aligns opposite the cut between ona and ama, its back opposite the heart and its face opposite the kidneys; and if inverted so the groove is opposite the heart or its back opposite the cut — treifah; but if the groove is slightly inverted opposite the ona or below toward the ama — kosher — Mahariu. And Maharshal wrote: what of it if the groove is inverted — this is only loss of Jewish money and one should abolish this custom. And Pri Chadash wrote: he wrote well. See in later slaughter examinations; and Bach wrote: where there is no great financial loss one should not rule permission in what they practiced as forbidden.
Treifah. And Shach wrote in the name of Igur: if it has no groove like the designation of the varda — treifah; and if it is as large as the ona adjacent to the ama, or less than a myrtle leaf in inflation, or changed its designation that it is drawn out and sharp like an ona and not round — one should declare treifah; and Shach wrote: it appears to him that even if it is only as large as the middle ona of the right — treifah; however I have seen many examiners lenient in this matter and do not declare treifah except when it is as large as the ona adjacent to the ama; and it appears that even for those who permit extra from the front in seif 3, nevertheless if it changed its designation and is so large — treifah, for it is like a missing varda, for this is an ona and not a varda — end.
Finger. And Shach explained: there is no distinction between large and small animal — one always measures finger-thickness; meaning finger-thickness — that is, the measure of the height of the root of both before they branch must be like a finger.
Crack. In the remaining root, even if both have varda designation — kosher — Maharam.
Inverted. Meaning: that they have one root; for if they do not have one root, even if both are not inverted — treifah, for these are two vardot.
Kosher. And Shach wrote: but if both are equal — treifah and not kosher unless the inverted one is smaller than the non-inverted; further it appears there from the words of HaRav that "changed its designation" has the law of inverted in this. If two vardot are pressed together we press according to our custom even on a thief to examine them.
Treifah. And the reason the acharonim wrote: because then it certainly changed the varda's place slightly; and Shach wrote in the name of Maharshal: even if it has no pouch — kosher; and all the more if it has two pouches or is not placed in a pouch; except that all examiners practice to declare treifah, and it is difficult to be lenient against the custom except in great financial loss; and regarding one who has two pouches he brings in the hagahah: specifically extra on the right side is treifah because all extra is considered as removed; and he wrote such is practiced in Krakow; and Maharam wrote there: and it appears to me that specifically when the pouch on the left has no part of the varda, for otherwise it is forbidden on account of extra varda; but from the words of Taz above it appears even in this it is kosher since it does not stand in its proper place, like regarding the leg.
Kosher. And Shach wrote: even for those who permit extra from the front, nevertheless for two vardot or missing or changed place — treifah. And the distinction by which we know which is extra varda and which is extra from the front: when it has a true varda designation, even if the extra stands on the left side — it is extra varda; but when it does not have a true varda designation in a matter that can be considered extra, one may permit since it does not stand in the place of the varda; and likewise even if it does not stand in its place and has no true varda designation but has a pouch by itself — it is recognizable that there are two vardot and treifah; but if it has no varda designation at all, even though it has a pouch — kosher. And Taz wrote: when two vardot are found — treifah; the reason is all extra is considered as removed; and accordingly it appears one should not declare treifah for two vardot except when both stand in the place where the varda should be and do not have a root like finger-thickness after inflation; nevertheless if you find an extra ona inside, even in the place where the varda should be, and it has no varda form — there is no disqualification here for two vardot, for the forms of the onot are not like a varda; and the same if two vardot are found, one on the right and one on the left — "as removed" does not apply since the extra does not stand in its proper place — see siman 55 seif 5. I found written in Knesset HaGedolah on koy that they call it boful and its way is to have two vardot, and so for most wild animals — and they are kosher; but for other laws they are like an animal regarding adhesion of vardot or varda designation and distance and inversion and all other treifos — only regarding extra of ayinunita we do not declare treifah in them. And Bach wrote: such a case came before him several times and he permitted; if it stands in the place of the varda itself I received from my master may he live long that they practice to declare treifah if it has some varda designation; but if it resembles an ona itself one may consider it extra from the front; and if it has one root — see above in this seif. And what Rama wrote regarding missing part of the varda that one should examine the ama of the left — there is a correction to right; and so in Torat Chaim, for if ayinunita is found on the left side — treifah; and Shach rejects his words — see there.
And lambs. And Shach wrote: from this language it appears at first glance that even in a large animal the law is thus, since it is sometimes found in kids and lambs; but in the gloss HaRav brought it states: and specifically in kids and lambs, but not in a large animal. And regarding the law HaRav wrote there on this: we do not practice thus except that in every change of place we declare treifah, even in kids and lambs; and it appears the reason: since it is attached to the ama it is merely extra and ayinunita de-varda has nothing; and if there is a pouch on it — then it is certainly a varda; but it is a varda by itself. And according to Taz from what he wrote in s.k. 4 it appears kosher if there is another varda on the left side. And he also wrote: R' Liberman the slaughterer of Prague wrote in his examinations that ayinunita requires varda designation, and if it has no varda designation it does not matter according to Mahariu; and in my opinion if he practiced thus he fed treifot to Israel all his days, for I already wrote in the name of all poskim that if it has no varda designation — treifah; but in truth these laws do not contradict each other at all, for I received from my master: when part of the varda is missing and found thus in an ona or ama, they combine and it appears there is varda designation between both; and such Maharam wrote: if two vardot are found with one root not like finger-thickness and no varda designation except through combination — they permit, for it is considered one; and further regarding what remains there is varda designation, as Rama wrote; and so there are those who permit if it has varda designation at its root, etc.
Its place. And Shach wrote in the name of HaRav: if it has one root, even though it became small and nothing remains — nevertheless kosher; and Shach also wrote that in examinations he disputes this law and it is an error, for Rama's words are primary.
Matter. This does not refer to missing or two vardot or changed place — these treifos were mentioned in Rashi and Tosafot; rather it refers to the other laws Rama mentioned — Taz. They practice not to eat the varda because of repulsiveness; nevertheless if it was salted with other meat it is permitted — so from a received tradition. If the varda was found perforated, even though its pouch seals it — treifah — see there siman 16, etc. And that two pouches are treifah — specifically when they have one wall between them; for if we say extra is considered as removed, there is no pouch at all; but distant from each other, each has a pouch by itself — kosher — Knesset HaGedolah. And I heard from an expert examiner: specifically when the ayinunita benefits from both, such as they stand one next to the other and the varda is placed in both; and also each pouch has a ligament at its edges on the lip — and if one of all these is missing — kosher — see there daf 95. If the varda's pouch is tight so the varda cannot exit except through opening, even though there is no adhesion or blister — treifah, and inflation does not help — so from a received tradition daf 173. And Shach wrote in the name of Maharshal in examinations: all they wrote to be lenient, whether in exchanges not mentioned in the Talmud or in the size of ayinunita de-varda — that is only when there is no other healthy suspicious matter; but if there is suspicious matter, even in a matter we permit, nevertheless since there is a side of stringency to declare treifah for someone, even not according to the law, nevertheless in anything it combines to support the forbidders, and we say it is a weakness and treifah.
