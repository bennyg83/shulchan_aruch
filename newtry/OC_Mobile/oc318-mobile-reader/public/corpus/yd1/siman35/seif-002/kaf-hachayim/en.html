B) There. A lung that was perforated — treifah; and the law of a perforated lung is explained below siman 36. And the reason the Mechaber wrote this seif here is to say that the reason for the treifos arranged in this siman is on account of perforation of the lung. Pri Eitz Chaim ot 1; Zivchei Torah there.
E) [Seif 2] Five lobes the lung has, etc.; and below are two large "mothers," one to the right and one to the left. And "lobes" (onim) means ears, for the translation of ear is oni; and ama is called the large one — from the word mother — from which the lobes separate, and it is the main lung. So Rosh regarding two adjacent lobes in the name of R' Tam; and lobes and ama are all called lung; and some explain it is called lung because one sees and examines it in inspection. Perishah ot 1; and in the Gemara Perek Eizehu Mekoman daf 47a they say: why is it called lung (re'ah)? Because it enlightens (me'irah) the eyes — meaning, one who eats it, through the signs that enlighten the eyes, it too assists them. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Turei Zahav ot 4.
F) There. And it is called ayinunita de-varda. Like a small ona (lobe) of varda, on account of its delicacy and redness. Rashi Chullin 47a. Taz s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 2.
G) There. And if one is missing from the right or from the left, etc. — even if there are four on the right, if one is missing on the left — treifah. Shach s.k. 3. Knesset HaGedolah there ot 11. Pri Chadash ot 3. Pri Eitz Chaim ot 2. Lechem HaPanim ot 4. Beit Lechem Yehudah ot 3. Kereti ot 1. Shulchan Melachim ot 23. Levush ot 40. Sha'arei Tzedek ot 3. Zivchei Torah ot 4. Beit Yitzchak siman 35 chavot da'at ot 1. Kometz HaMinchah siman 35 ot 2. Sha'arei Shamayim end ot 2 and in his book Einayim Yeshayim siman 9 ot 2. Zivchei Tzedek ot 1.
H) If the ama is missing — treifah, whether from the left or the right. Turei Zahav ot 2. Mishnat Zekenim ot 2. She'erit Yosef ot 5. Or HaChaim ot 2. Sha'arei Shamayim ot 3. Einayim Yeshayim there ot 3. Amrei Tov on examinations of Mahariu ot 4. Zivchei Shem ot 15. Zivchei Tzedek ot 2.
I) There. Or if there were two on the right and three on the left — treifah; and likewise four on the left and three on the right is exchange and treifah, as the Rav wrote in the hagahah seif 5. Shach s.k. 4. And see below ot 84.
J) There, hagahah. The forms of the lobes of the right are not like those of the left, and if their form was exchanged — treifah, etc. — all this is called exchange and treifah. But R' Shlomo in Yeshu'ot Shelomo Piskei Tosafot siman 54 wrote that all these are not called exchange and are kosher; and so Pri Chadash ot 5, Pri Eitz Chaim ot 3, She'erit Yosef ot 6, Zivchei Torah ot 6; and so Shulchan Gavo'ah ot 6 — in his place the Sephardim do not practice thus; and so Zivchei Tzedek ot 4 — custom in Baghdad, see Einayim Yeshayim, to permit all these exchanges like R' Shlomo and his supporters; and Ashkenaz custom is written in the ot that follows.
