Kids and lambs. Baer Heitev; and see in Shav Yaakov part 2 siman 60 on that which Shulchan Aruch seif 2 in the hagahah: if a piece is missing from the varda — kosher, for sometimes thus found in kids and lambs — they challenged him: certainly presumably in large animals too it is so, for it did not change; and he answered: it may be said when they grow the varda grew and became whole; and it may also be said certainly the shepherds' custom when they sense weakness they do not let them grow, etc.; and see Shach who doubts this leniency; and one may not be lenient except in distress and great financial loss; and see what he wrote there siman 65 regarding liver in calves; and see in my Sefer Beit Efrayim on this.
