That specifically above half. See in responsum Or Zaru'a Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 157 who raised: in the half and above one may permit whether from the front or from the back; but from half downward: if from the back — treifah; but from the front one may permit — contrary to the slaughter examinations of Maharatz who wrote that even from half and above that is kosher refers specifically from the back, but from the front some declare treifah because it will come apart; and so in slaughter examinations of Gevul Binyamin — but this is error and one need not be concerned; and he wrote this is not among permitted matters, etc., for slaughterers learn slaughter and suppose as if given from Sinai, etc.; and he wrote the sages of the city agreed with him to permit extra from the front, since many animals were found thus and there is great financial loss — see there; and see Pri Megadim. [And see in responsum Chavot Yair siman 38 who wrote on this: if it is literally extra from the front — the Bach forbids s.k. 20; and if it stands exactly on the tip and through inflation the root appears to lean from the front — at first glance treifah like other onot whose extra leans toward the back — the same here toward the front; and specifically when the root leans, not merely the tip; however expert examiners here told me: even if the root leaned forward on the upper ona, this must be distinguished while the lung is still inside the animal, for there it is pressed in the narrowness of the chest in its lifetime, and when they remove it it expands and therefore sometimes the root leans forward — therefore one must distinguish this while still in the animal in the place that presses it — and these are matters of reason — end.]
