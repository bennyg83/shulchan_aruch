(Shach s.k. 15) But from the left side — kosher. It implies: on the right, even if the varda does not benefit from two pouches, nevertheless treifah; and specifically when the pouch has a lip at its mouth; but if it has no lip, it is only a protrusion and not called two pouches; nevertheless if varda is found without a lip, it is not called missing pouch — acharonim.
