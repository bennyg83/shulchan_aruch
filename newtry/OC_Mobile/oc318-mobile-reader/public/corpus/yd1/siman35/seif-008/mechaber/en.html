A lung that does not have the cut of a lobe — treifah; and if there is a crack like the appearance of a separation between them, of the measure of a myrtle leaf — whether at their root, whether in their middle, whether at their end — kosher. {Rama: And some permit with a sign or a crack of any size, even though it is not like a myrtle leaf (Rashi and Ittur and Tosafot and Semag and Rosh and Tur and Rabbeinu Yerucham and Tashbetz and Mordechai in the name of HaG and R' Chaim and Rashba and Ravyah); and one may rely on them if there is great loss to declare treifah (should read: to permit). The lower lobe of the right — its way is to be thick and long more than the middle lobe; also it is higher than that of the left; and if it is completely attached to the ama, only a sign of these signs is seen in the place where a lobe should be — it is called a sign and kosher (Mahariu); and the same if another sign is recognized there by which the lobe is somewhat recognized; and even a sign through the usual furrow (simpun) of a lobe — if a furrow is found in the usual place of a lobe, it is called a sign (Magid Commentary in the name of Maharik); and all these matters we examine and estimate after they inflated the lung, because in the animal's lifetime the lung is inflated. Some say: in kids and lambs we do not declare treifah if lobes are not found, even without any sign or crack at all, for such is their growth and their way — in their youth they do not have them, and when they grow the lobes grow with them (ancient examinations of Maharash).}
