It is called a lump and kosher. See Baer Heitev; and see in my responsum Beit Efrayim siman 26: split from the back with a hollow beneath it; and there is another suspicious matter there that combines to be two suspicious matters and treifah; and one may not permit except in onot that split in their width or thickness and have one root in a matter where there is no split there, only divided as I wrote in my book; and above siman 1; or extra on the upper ona in a place that is kosher because its way is to split and this is not included in suspicious matter at all; but split from within a hollow is certainly suspicious matter, not because of concern for extra but because of spoilage and change in the lung; therefore when another suspicious matter combines with it — treifah.
