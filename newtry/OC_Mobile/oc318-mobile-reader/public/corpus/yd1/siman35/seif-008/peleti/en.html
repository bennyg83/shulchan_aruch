Crack — and they raised that we say regarding cracks to permit and not to declare treifah, even in one place, as Shach wrote; meaning we are lenient because since most of the lung is not missing we find; therefore all we can say they are not included in missing, we say; if so, it appears the same law applies to an upper ona that split — the splits complete the count of onot, for even though we are lenient that there should not be extras, nevertheless here there is a count of an ona to complete; and this is simple, better than cracks.
And there are those who permit, etc. — and Maharshal agreed with Rambam; and in truth the Gemara can be understood: we say it resembles sponge — treifah — why is this needed? Behold onot are missing, and explicitly they said missing from five onot is treifah, and here all are missing; and from Maharshal's words it appears he holds when entirely attached one may more permit, for there is no order they should be missing; but for one or two missing it is worse — this is strained; and Shach wrote like Ran: where there is any sign we say they stuck to each other; and this does not help for that which says it resembles sponge — either way: if there is a sign it is truly kosher; if no sign — explain it is missing; and from Shach it appears because of missing any sign helps, unlike for "resembles sponge" where we require crack sign. And in my heart I say: each ona is surrounded by the lung's membrane, and inside is placed like lung flesh; if two onot appear as one, we check if that portion was cut and we find for each a separate membrane — behold it is clear there were two onot, for there are two membranes; if they stuck strongly, it is known that in their adhesion two membranes will be found pressed together — this is a sign there were two onot that only stuck; and there is no invalidity because of missing, for there were two membranes; invalidity is only resembling sponge; through this crack and the like help; but if the onot were cut and there is only one membrane — it is proven it is only one ona and the count is missing and treifah; crack and simpun do not help, for behold suspicious matter is before you — examine. Nevertheless for the law he was stringent and did not rely on crack and simpun if cut and only one membrane remains; and it is strained to be lenient that they stuck so strongly one cannot distinguish one or two membranes — this is difficult in reality that there would be four membranes, for otherwise each ona has two membranes that would be four stuck together and not felt; therefore one should be stringent; and if the membrane is recognizable — obviously there is no missing, only because of resembling sponge; if so, all the above difficulties are resolved — examine.
