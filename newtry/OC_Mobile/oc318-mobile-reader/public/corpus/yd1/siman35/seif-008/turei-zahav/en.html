Maharshal wrote in the name of a certain hagahah: there was an incident in Rinus where they slaughtered a fat cow and when examined found no lung — neither ama nor ona nor varda nor crack, only smooth like one piece of meat; and one examiner prominent among the sages of Austria took a sharp knife, cut the upper membrane, opened gently, peeled gently, separated it all — then they saw the lung like other lungs and permitted it; he said he saw such in Austria; the sages of Austria permitted it — see what I wrote end of section 39.
And if it is entirely attached to the ama, etc. In Hagahot HaRav he wrote to distinguish: specifically from ona to ama we say thus, but not from ona to ona — there these signs do not help; and other hagahot disagree that their law is equal, and such is primary.
