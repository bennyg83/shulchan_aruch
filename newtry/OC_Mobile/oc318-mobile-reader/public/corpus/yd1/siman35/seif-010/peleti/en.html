And if found — it has no known count; and that which the Gemara Chullin 56 did not answer when Chizkiyah said a bird has no lung and the Gemara challenged "but we see it has" and the Gemara answered "not to fall and not to be crushed" and did not answer simply "for the count of the lung" regarding missing count and extra and exchange that have no known count — it is strained to say it chose those two treifos mentioned in the Mishnah, unlike missing and extra which are only statements; and it appears to say: there R' Yehudah and Shmuel dispute regarding a bird whose body slipped whether treifah or needs examination lest the lung was perforated; Chizkiyah said a bird has no lung — behold we need not be concerned for lung perforations that you require examination; if so, how does "not to fall and not to be crushed" relate to the concern lest the lung was perforated? It may be said thus was his intent in "fell from the roof": we are not concerned to examine the lung, nor concerned lest the small bones connected to it for the lung were broken or crushed and perforated; and we do not establish suspicious matter in the lung; if so, the same for slipped body — we do not establish suspicious matter in the lung; and they disputed whether there is more suspicious matter in slipped body than fallen; if so, one may resolve: his intent was not on missing and extra; for what does this matter to slipped body? The statement of Chizkiyah refers to the matter above.
