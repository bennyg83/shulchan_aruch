If lobes were added in their count — if the extra lobe was at the side of the lobes or before the lung, which is opposite the heart — permitted; and if it was on its back, which is opposite the ribs — this is treifah; and this is when it is like a myrtle leaf after they inflated it; but if it is less than this — kosher. {Rama: And some are stringent to forbid any extra lobe that is not in the row of lobes (Hagahot Ashiri and Mordechai and Semak and Mahariu and Tur in the name of Ittur and Or Zaru'a); and so they practice in some places to forbid an extra lobe even from the front (mikama), and they do not distinguish between front and back except if it stands in the row of lobes; and when they inflate it, if it leans forward — kosher; and if it leans toward the back — treifah (Mahariu and Beit Yosef in the name of Magid Commentary); and in these countries the custom is to permit an extra lobe from the front, and one should not change the custom.}
