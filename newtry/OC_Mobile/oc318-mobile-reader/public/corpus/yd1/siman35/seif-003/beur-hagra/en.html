If onot were added, etc. — as written there; and this applies from inside; but from the fact he did not distinguish "from inside" itself — we learn that from inside in every case is kosher.
And it, etc. — from what he wrote "even like tarfa de-asa," etc., and it implies specifically like tarfa de-asa, as written in chapter 2 of Berakhot (15a) and chapter 3 of Pesachim and chapter 9 of Chullin — and from it is the matter that it does not return, etc. (Likut). But if it, etc. — even though he said "even," he means specifically, as written in chapter 2 of Berakhot and from it is the matter, etc., and in chapter 9 of Bava Batra (146a) "Rav said: specifically a dinar," etc. — Tosefet HaRav (end).
And some are stringent, etc. — as explained: but "between and between," etc., and he did not say "and it is not so," for that "between and between," etc. — evidently he did not retract from the first reasoning; and that which he said "and this applies from inside" — he did not wish to elaborate and distinguish within inside itself, and he stated a decided matter.
