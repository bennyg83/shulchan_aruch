Treifah. It appears from the words of the poskim that if extra is found one must inflate to see whether it leans forward or toward the back — Shach. And Shach also wrote: and specifically a protruding extra like tarfa de-asa; but if it bent and goes upward — kosher, since it stands in a permitted place — because it protrudes outward it is not declared treifah (and if extra is found in a bed that is above the onot adjacent to the spine inside the lung, I found written that it is kosher). And it appears to me treifah, for this is certainly included more in "on its back" that the Talmud mentioned — Taz.
