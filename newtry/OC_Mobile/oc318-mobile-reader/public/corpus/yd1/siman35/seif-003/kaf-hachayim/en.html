C) And know that the lung flutters to the heart so it not burn from natural heat; and the lung is like an inflated wineskin — if a small hole is pierced in it, all the wind exits there and it remains as dead; and thus it cannot flutter to the heart and will burn and die; and even though this hole is already sealed by adhesion or a blister or an invalid appearance, and similarly for the other lung treifos explained in these simanim — nevertheless the question is: a scab that arose from a blow is nothing, for ultimately it will peel and the hole remain open, as below siman 36 seif 3; and this is the reason for all lung treifos. Shulchan Gavo'ah ot 1.
