Pouch. Baer Heitev of the Rav Maharit translated in the name of Shach that in great financial loss one may be lenient; and it appears he understood from Shach's words at the end of s.k. 14 that "in great financial loss one may permit" refers also to the beginning of his words regarding missing pouch or not placed in pouch; and in my opinion it appears from Shach's arrangement that it refers only to extra pouch; but regarding those one may not permit even in great financial loss; and so Peri Megadim explained. And see in responsum Pri Tevuah siman 62 that he is in doubt how to interpret Shach's words, and he straddles both sides; and he raised for practical law: if the animal was slaughtered twelve months ago, one may permit in great financial loss even the animals themselves, and all the more cheeses made from them — see there.
