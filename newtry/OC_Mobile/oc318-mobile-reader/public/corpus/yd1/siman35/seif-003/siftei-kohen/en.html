And when they inflate it, etc. From the words of Magid Commentary that Beit Yosef brought and from the words of Mordechai that HaRav brought — it appears that since extra was found, one must inflate it and see whether it leans forward or toward the back; and on this see siman 39:
And if it leans toward the back — treifah. And Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe in the name of Magid Commentary: specifically a protruding extra lobe, but if it bent and goes upward there is no house of concern here, since it stands in a permitted place — because it protrudes outward it is not declared treifah — end of his words; and so HaRav brought in the name of Mordechai; and so is implied from the words of Maharam of Medellin that HaRav brought there; and so Pri Megadim; and he explained: protruding extra means like tarfa de-asa, even though it is rooted in the row of onot, etc. — see there; and so HaRav explained there; and so is in other acharonim:
