A gentile who comes to cut his foreskin because of a wound or boil that grew on it — it is forbidden for an Israelite to cut it because he did not intend for a mitzvah; therefore if the gentile intended circumcision as a mitzvah, it is a mitzvah for an Israelite to circumcise him; and in a place where it is permitted to heal a gentile, it is permitted in every regard (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter Nosein al haAnusah, Rambam, Rashi; and see above siman 158).
