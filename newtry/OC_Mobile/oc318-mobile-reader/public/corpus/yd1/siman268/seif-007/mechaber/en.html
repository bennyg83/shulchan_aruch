A gentile minor — if he has a father, he can convert him; and if he has no father and comes to convert, or his mother brings him to convert — beit din converts him, for it is a merit for him; and one may act for a person's benefit in his absence — whether a minor his father converted or beit din converted him, he can protest when he grows up: {Rama: (And his law is not like an apostate Israelite but like a gentile).} When do we say this? When he did not practice Jewish custom since he grew; but if he practiced Jewish custom since he grew, he can no longer protest.
