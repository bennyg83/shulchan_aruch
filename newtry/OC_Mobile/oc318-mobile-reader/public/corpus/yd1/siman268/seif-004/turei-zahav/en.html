Since immersion of a convert, etc. In the Gemara a reason is given that we do not immerse a convert on Shabbat — for it is like fixing a person; nevertheless Rambam needed this reason, for otherwise it would not be like fixing — for it appears like circumcision, as we find regarding immersion from impurity on Shabbat; and so Tosafot wrote in chapter HaChalitz; and it is surprising on Beit Yosef that he did not write thus.
