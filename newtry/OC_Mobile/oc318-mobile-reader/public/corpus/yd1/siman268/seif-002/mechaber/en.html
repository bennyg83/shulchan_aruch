When he comes to convert, they say to him: What did you see that you came to convert? Do you not know that Israel in the present time are pushed, struck, uprooted, and sufferings come upon them? If he said, "I know and I am not worthy to be joined with them" — they accept him immediately and inform him of the fundamentals of the faith — that is, unity of God and prohibition of idolatry — and they elaborate with him on this matter; and inform him of some light mitzvot and some severe mitzvot; and inform him of some punishments of mitzvot, saying: Before you came, if you ate forbidden fat you were not punishable by karet; if you desecrated Shabbat you were not liable for stoning — but now if you eat forbidden fat you are punishable by karet; if you desecrate Shabbat you are liable for stoning; and they do not elaborate on him nor be exacting with him; and just as they inform him of punishments of mitzvot, so they inform him of their reward; and inform him that by performing these mitzvot he will merit life in the World to Come, and that there is no completely righteous person except one wise in wisdom who performs these mitzvot and knows them; and they say to him: Know that the World to Come is hidden only for the righteous, who are Israel; and that you see Israel in distress in this world — it is good for them, for they cannot receive most good in this world like gentiles, lest their heart rise and they stray and lose reward of the World to Come; and the Holy One, blessed be He, does not bring most suffering upon them so they should not be lost; but all gentiles are destroyed and they remain; and they elaborate on this to endear him. If he accepted, they circumcise him immediately and wait for him until he heals completely; and afterward immerse him in a proper immersion without interposition: {Rama: (And some say he should shave his hair and cut fingernails of hands and feet before immersion) (Tur, Rif, and Rosh).} And three: {Rama: (Torah scholars) (same Tur)} stand over him and inform him again some light and some severe mitzvot a second time, and he stands in the water; and if a woman — women seat her in water up to her neck and the judges are outside and inform her some light and severe mitzvot, and she sits in the water; and afterward immerses before them and they turn their faces and leave so they should not see her when she rises from the water; and bless on the immersion after he rises from the water; and once he immersed, behold he is like Israel — if he returned to his evil ways, behold he is like an apostate Israelite, for if he sanctified, his sanctity is sanctity.
