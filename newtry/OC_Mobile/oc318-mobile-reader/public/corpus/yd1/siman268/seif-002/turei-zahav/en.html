What did you see that brought you, etc. We initially discourage him so that if he is not sincere he will withdraw. R' Chelbo said that converts are as difficult for Israel as a skin affliction.
We wait for him, etc. The Ramban asked why immersion is delayed until after circumcision. He answered that because circumcision is difficult, it is performed first so that an insincere candidate will withdraw. Nevertheless, if he immersed first, the conversion is valid.
Until he heals. The water may damage the wound before it has healed properly.
If he returns to his former ways. The expression means that his old inclination has reasserted itself and led him back to sin.
