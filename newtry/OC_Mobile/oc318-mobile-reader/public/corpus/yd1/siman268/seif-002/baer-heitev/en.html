And they inform him. Beit Yosef writes that failure to inform him does not invalidate the conversion.
They do not increase. Meaning they do not multiply descriptions of punishment or teach him every detail and stringency of forbidden fat and Shabbat, lest his intention truly be for the sake of Heaven. Bach.
An interposition. The Gemara states that anything interposing for ordinary immersion also interposes for a convert. See siman 198 for the laws of interposition. The mikveh must likewise be valid for a niddah's immersion. Shach.
That he shave. Although hair itself is not an interposition, Shach writes that this is the custom for apostates returning to Judaism. After the fact, if he did not shave but had no actual interposition, the immersion is valid.
