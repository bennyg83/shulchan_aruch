And we inform. The Beit Yosef wrote that if they did not inform him, it does not invalidate.
We do not increase. The mention of punishments; and "we are exacting" means that we do not inform him of all the fine details and stringencies in the prohibition of chelev and the prohibition of Shabbat, for perhaps his intent is for Heaven. So wrote the Bach.
An interposition. The Talmud says: anything that interposes in immersion interposes for a convert. See above siman 198 regarding what interposes in immersion. Likewise, he needs a mikveh fit for a niddah's immersion. Shach.
That he shave. (Even though there is no interposition in his hair.) The Shach wrote that such is the custom with apostates when they return to the Jewish faith. However, it appears that after the fact, if he did not shave his hair, provided there is no interposing substance, it is an immersion.
