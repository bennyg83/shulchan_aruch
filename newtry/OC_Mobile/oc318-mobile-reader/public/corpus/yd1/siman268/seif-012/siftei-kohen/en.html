When the convert comes, etc. Tosafos wrote regarding the incident in the first chapter of Shabbos, where a person came before Hillel and said: Convert me on condition that I become the Kohen Gadol, that Hillel was confident that he would ultimately act for the sake of Heaven. From here one may learn that everything depends upon the assessment of the beis din. End quote from the Beis Yosef. Hagahos Derishah wrote likewise.
