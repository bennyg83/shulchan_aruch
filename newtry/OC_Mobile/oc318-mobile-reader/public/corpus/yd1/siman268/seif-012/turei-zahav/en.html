He returned to his former ways. The Baal Halachos teaches that if a convert later abandons Judaism, his wine and similar matters are treated like those of a non-Jew. This does not mean that his wine becomes retroactively forbidden from before his apostasy. Once he entered the Jewish people, he was presumed Jewish for every purpose until his later misconduct became known. The teaching concerns stringencies from that point onward. For lenient purposes, however, he remains a Jew, as the Tur concludes regarding kiddushin.
