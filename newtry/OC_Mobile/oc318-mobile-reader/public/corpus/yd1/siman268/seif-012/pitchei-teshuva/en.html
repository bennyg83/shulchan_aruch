He should immerse. Magen Avraham permits a Jewish apostate who repents to immerse even on Shabbat because the immersion is not Torah-required. Rashbash writes that descendants of coerced apostates returning to Judaism need not undergo conversion immersion or formal notification of mitzvot, but should be welcomed gently. If uncircumcised, they recite the appropriate circumcision blessings and a prayer for divine assistance in complete repentance.
