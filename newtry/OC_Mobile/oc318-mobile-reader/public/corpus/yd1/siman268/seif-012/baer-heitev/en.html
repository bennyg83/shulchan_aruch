Authority. Tosafot explain that Hillel accepted the person who sought conversion on condition of becoming High Priest because he was confident that the person would ultimately act for the sake of Heaven. Beit Yosef derives that the matter depends upon beit din's evaluation.
Returned. Behag writes that if the convert returns to his former ways, his wine is treated as non-Jewish wine. Perishah suggests this may apply retroactively, but Taz rejects that entirely: once he was established as Jewish, no disqualification applies before the later apostasy. The novelty is only that from then on he is treated stringently like a non-Jew, not leniently.
