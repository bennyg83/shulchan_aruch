When a convert comes to convert, they investigate him lest it be for money he will take, or dominion he will gain, or fear he comes to enter the religion; and if a man, they investigate lest he set his eyes on a Jewish woman; and if a woman, lest she set her eyes on Jewish youths; and if no pretext is found, they inform him of the weight of Torah and the trouble in performing it among the nations; so they withdraw — if they accepted and did not withdraw and they see them return from love, they accept them; and if they did not investigate him, or did not inform him of reward and punishment of mitzvot, and he circumcised and immersed before three laymen — behold he is a convert, even if it becomes known he converts for some matter, since he circumcised and immersed he left the category of gentiles; and they are concerned for him until his righteousness is clarified; and even if he returned and worshiped idols, behold he is like an apostate Israelite whose sanctifications are sanctifications: {Rama: (An apostate Israelite who repented need not immerse except rabbinically — he should immerse and accept upon himself words of association before three) (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter HaChalitzah).}
