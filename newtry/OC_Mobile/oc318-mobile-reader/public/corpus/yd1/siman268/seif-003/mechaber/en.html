All matters of the convert — whether informing him of mitzvot, accepting them, circumcision, or immersion — must be with three fit to judge and by day (Tosafot and Rosh, chapter HaChalitzah): {Rama: However, specifically ab initio; but after the fact, if he did not circumcise or immerse except before two: {Rama: (or relatives) (Hagahot Mordechai)} or at night — even if he did not immerse for the sake of conversion but a man immersed for his impurity and a woman for her niddah — behold he is a convert and permitted to an Israelite woman, except accepting mitzvot which impedes if not by day and with three; and for Rif and Rambam, even after the fact if he immersed or circumcised before two or at night it impedes and he is forbidden to an Israelite woman; but if he married an Israelite woman and had a son from her, we do not disqualify him.}
