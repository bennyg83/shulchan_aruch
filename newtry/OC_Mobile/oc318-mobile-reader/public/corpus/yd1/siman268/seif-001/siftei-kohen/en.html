Must drip, etc., and one does not bless, etc. Because we are uncertain whether we drip or not — therefore from doubt we are strict and drip; but one does not bless, for we hold safek berakhot lehakel; see above siman 265 seif 3.
And some say it is not valid immersion. Therefore one should fulfill and immerse again after circumcision — so wrote Atzei Arazim.
