One must draw a drop of blood, etc., and no blessing is recited, etc. Since it is uncertain whether blood must be drawn or not, one should act stringently because of the doubt and draw blood. However, no blessing is recited because of the doubt, for we rule that in cases of uncertainty concerning blessings, one is lenient. See above, siman 265, seif 3.
Some say that it does not constitute immersion. Therefore, one should fulfill the requirement by immersing after the circumcision. Thus wrote the Atteres Zahav.
