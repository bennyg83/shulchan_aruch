If he circumcised himself while still a non-Jew, etc. The Tur cites Rabbeinu Chananel that he has no complete remedy, though his children may be circumcised and enter the congregation. This cannot mean children born from a non-Jewish woman, for they follow her status, nor is the novelty merely that children born from a Jewish woman are valid. Rather, it teaches that they enter the sanctity of the Jewish congregation and are not stigmatized as descendants of a non-Jew.
Blood of the covenant must be drawn from him, etc. The Baal Halachos requires this for a convert who was already circumcised, although a Jewish child born circumcised may not require it. The laws of a convert differ from those of one born Jewish.
