(Siman 268, seif 3) But b'dieved, etc. The Dagul Mervavah wrote that specifically in the case of an adult convert, circumcision and immersion suffice b'dieved without three judges and at night, because for him the principal element is acceptance of the mitzvos. But regarding a minor convert, to whom acceptance of the mitzvos does not apply and whose immersion is the principal element, since he is immersed on the authority of the rabbinical court, according to all opinions it must be performed by day and before three judges. End of his words. There is a proof for the view of the poskim that b'dieved immersion suffices without a rabbinical court from what is stated in the Jerusalem Talmud, chapter 4 of Eruvin, halacha 4: R' Huna in the name of Rav Nachman bar Yaakov asked regarding a convert who immersed after dawn, since had he been awake he would have acquired his Shabbos residence, etc. The meaning is that here too he acquires a Shabbos residence as though he had immersed the previous day. The Korban HaEdah asked how it is possible for a convert to immerse on Shabbos, since we rule that one may not immerse a convert on Yom Tov. It is difficult to say that the question concerns a case in which he transgressed and immersed, because a rabbinical court is required, and how could the rabbinical court make such an error? End of his words. According to the poskim who hold that immersion suffices without a rabbinical court, the matter is well understood.
