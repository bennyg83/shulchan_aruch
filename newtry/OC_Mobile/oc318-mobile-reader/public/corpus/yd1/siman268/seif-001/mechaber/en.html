How they convert a male and female convert, and laws of convert sanctity. And it contains 12 seifim.
A convert who enters the congregation of Israel is obligated in circumcision first; and if he circumcised while still a gentile: {Rama: (Or was born circumcised) (Tur in the name of Rabbenu Asher)} — they must draw covenant blood from him, and they do not bless upon it; and if the organ was cut off, circumcision does not impede his conversion and immersion suffices for him: {Rama: (If he immersed before circumcision — it is effective, for after the fact it is immersion) (Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban, and so Maggid Mishneh chapter 14 of Avodah Zarah); (and some say it is not immersion) (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter HaChalitzah, in the name of Ra'ah).}
