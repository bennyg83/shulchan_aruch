And one does not recite a blessing, etc. For R' Chananel ruled that one need not draw a drop of blood of the covenant; and as written in Shabbat 135a: 'they did not disagree,' etc. 'regarding what did they disagree,' etc.; and see Tosafot there s.v. 'of R' Yosi,' etc. — therefore Rambam and Rosh ruled stringently that one must draw a drop of blood, but one does not recite a blessing — for in cases of doubt regarding blessings, we rule leniently. One who immersed, etc. For ab initio we certainly require circumcision first — as written there: 'until he circumcises and immerses'; and likewise there 47b: 'he recovered — they immerse him,' etc. But post facto, Ramban wrote that his immersion counted — from what is written there 78a: 'a pregnant gentile woman,' etc. But Tosafot there 47b in s.v. 'they immerse him,' etc. disagree with this; and likewise Ra'ah wrote: since he was not fit for circumcision, his immersion counts for him — like a woman; and as above — and after he was born, he is like a complete Israelite even without circumcision, except that we circumcise him like an Israelite infant; and this is what is written: 'and some say,' etc.
