A Samaritan man or woman who comes and says they converted in so-and-so's court properly — he is not believed to enter the congregation until he brings witnesses; and if we see them practicing Israelite ways and performing all mitzvot — behold they have the presumption of righteous converts, even though there are no witnesses testifying before whom they converted; and even so, if they come to intermarry with Israel, they do not marry them until they bring witnesses or immerse before us, since they are presumed gentiles; but one who comes and says he is a gentile and converted in court — he is believed, for the mouth that forbade is the mouth that permitted; and Rambam wrote: when do we say this? In Eretz Yisrael in those days when everyone's presumption there was Israel; but in chutz la'aretz he must bring proof and afterward may marry an Israelite woman — for they were strict about lineage.
