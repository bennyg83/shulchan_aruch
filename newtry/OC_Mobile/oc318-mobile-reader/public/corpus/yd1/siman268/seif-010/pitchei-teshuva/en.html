He must immerse. See Magen Avraham siman 326 note 8 who wrote an apostate Israelite who repented may immerse even on Shabbat since it is not d'oraisa — see there; and see responsum Rashbash siman 68 who wrote regarding children of apostates who are uncircumcised called "anusim" — when they come to repent, do not inform them of light and severe mitzvot and their punishments, do not rush them but draw them with kindness; they need not immersion, and when he circumcises himself bless to bring him into the covenant of Abraham and bless also two blessings on mila as above siman 267 (note 2) "Who sanctified the beloved" and he does not include them in one blessing; afterward say: God of Abraham, please succeed for the servant called by Your name such-and-such, draw Your kindness to him, and as You inclined his heart to complete repentance before You, so incline his heart to love and fear You, open his heart in Your Torah, guide him in the path of Your commandments that he find favor before You, amen — see further there; and see responsum Radbaz HaChadashot siman 344 whether obligated to bless shehecheyanu at time of mila.
