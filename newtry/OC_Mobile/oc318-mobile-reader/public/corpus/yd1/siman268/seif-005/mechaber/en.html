One who circumcises converts blesses, "Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to circumcise converts"; and afterward blesses, "Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to circumcise converts and to draw covenant blood from them" — for were it not for covenant blood, heaven and earth would not endure, as it says, "If not for My covenant day and night, the statutes of heaven and earth I would not have set."
