But he is believed, etc. Language of the Rosh; and that which he says he is believed to disqualify himself — not for this matter that if he had relations with a Jewish daughter he disqualifies her, for we rule a person does not make himself wicked; and furthermore an astonishing matter — he is disqualified and his sons are valid — therefore his testimony is nothing even regarding himself; and if he had relations with a Jewish daughter he does not disqualify her; but for this matter his testimony for himself helps — he makes himself a piece of forbidden matter and is forbidden with a Jewish daughter — end quote; and see Beit Chadash; and see above seif 3.
