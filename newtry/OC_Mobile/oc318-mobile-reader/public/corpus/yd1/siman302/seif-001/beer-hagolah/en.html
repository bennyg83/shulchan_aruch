From the Rambam, chapter 10 of Hilchot Kilayim, based on the statement of Rafram bar Pappa and so forth in Niddah 61b, according to his text there, which does not read the statement, "one who places," and so forth. He follows his own view that even by Torah law one does not require the combination of combed, spun, and woven together, as stated at the beginning of siman 300.
