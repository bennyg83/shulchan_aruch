The Rambam in chapter 10 of Hilchot Kilayim, from the statement of Rafram bar Papa, etc., Niddah daf 61b, and according to his girsa there, that he does not have the girsa of the statement "this one who casts," etc. And he follows his own reasoning, for he holds that even from the Torah it does not require shua, tavui, and nuz, as is written at the beginning of siman 300.
