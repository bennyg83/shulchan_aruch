By Torah law. The Taz writes that this test by dyeing is effective even for a Torah prohibition, as is explicitly evident from the Gemara. The distinction the Tur draws between a Torah prohibition and a rabbinic prohibition applies only to the second option he records there, namely, that one may detach one thread from it and it is then permitted.
