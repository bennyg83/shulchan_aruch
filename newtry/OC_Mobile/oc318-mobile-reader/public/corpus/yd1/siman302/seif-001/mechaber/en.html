The law of a garment in which kilayim was lost. In it are 2 seifim.
A wool garment in which a linen thread was lost, or a linen garment in which a wool thread was lost—he dyes it, for wool and linen do not take the same dye, and immediately it becomes apparent to him and he removes it; and if it is not apparent, behold it is permitted, lest it fell out and went away—for he examined and did not find it. (In what case is this said? With kilayim d'rabbanan; but with kilayim d'oraisa one does not rule leniently in their doubt (Tur).)
