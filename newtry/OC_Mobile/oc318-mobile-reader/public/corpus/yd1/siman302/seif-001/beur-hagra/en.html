When does this apply, etc. The Tur. There he cites the law of Mar Zutra concerning one who, and so forth, and detached it, and on that law he says, "when does this apply," as in the Gemara there. But in the first case this applies even to a Torah prohibition, since he derived it there from a mishnah dealing with a Torah prohibition. The Rambam and Shulchan Aruch omitted Mar Zutra's ruling, as explained above at the beginning of siman 300. See Taz, unlike Beit Yosef and Rama.
