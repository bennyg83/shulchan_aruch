In what case, etc. Tur, and he wrote there the din of Mar Zutra, "this one," etc., "and he tore it out," etc., and regarding this he says "in what case," etc., as is written in the Gemara there. But in the first clause even regarding d'oraisa, for he learned there from the Mishnah that it is d'oraisa. And the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch omitted the din of Mar Zutra, as it appears to me at the beginning of siman 300, and see Taz; and not like Beit Yosef and Rama.
