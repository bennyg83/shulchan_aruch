Even. Since it is known that the Jew seeks hemp, there is concern that the seller says this merely to improve the sale. Therefore, even where no prohibition has been established, he is not believed. This is the Shach.
To permit. This applies only after the fact; initially it is forbidden. This is the Shach.
To substitute. Since he is a professional, he will not damage his reputation when the matter can be verified. This explanation, based on professional reliability, is given by the Maggid Mishneh, Ran, and other authorities. The Rama stated it briefly because it is understood. This is the Shach.
And he burns. The Maadanei Melech writes: I heard that Jewish tailors test hemp by pulling the thread until hair-like fibers extend from it. If those fibers are very long, it is certainly hemp.
