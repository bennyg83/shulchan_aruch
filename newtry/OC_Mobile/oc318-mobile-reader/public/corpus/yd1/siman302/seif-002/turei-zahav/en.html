Not loyal. According to the fact that Israel is asking for a hemp in order to extract it from them:
One may rely to permit. In the Beit Yosef in the name of the Rash he wrote: in a place where flax is expensive, and it is better to sew with hemp because it is a strong thread, the sewing may be permitted, end of his words. And my father-in-law of blessed memory asked on the Shulchan Aruch: why did he rule here to permit with one condition, because flax is expensive, and the Rash stipulated yet a second condition, namely that it is good to sew with it because it is strong? And he strained to answer that the Shulchan Aruch is dealing b'dieved. And it appears to my humble opinion that what the Rash said, that it is strong, etc., is not a condition at all, for certainly in all places it is stronger; rather the main point is if flax is expensive. Except that you should not ask: what does it help that it is expensive — nevertheless there is a reason against it, that flax is better for sewing because it is smoother than hemp, as the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Ran, and as the Rama wrote afterwards. For this he wrote that hemp is stronger for sewing and does not tear in the sewing, and with flax it tears and there is trouble for the craftsman to fix it anew. So it turns out that these reasons are evenly matched against one another: namely, flax has a virtue from the side of its smoothness and a deficiency from the side of its weakness. And therefore the matter depends on its being expensive, for this tips it to heter, and it depends on what is found in the places; but regarding the strength of the thread, certainly in all places it is so. And this is simple to my humble opinion.
But if he gives, etc. The Rama decided to permit in this specifically according to the view of the Maggid Mishneh, for the reason that he is concerned for interchange, since there is room to stand, etc.: for if he does not give him, he has [room] to excuse and to correct his distortion and to say, "I erred, and I thought that this was hemp." Which is not so when he gives him hemp and afterwards sees that it is flax — he cannot correct his actions, for he should have been careful with what he gave him and designated for him a special place. So it appears to me is his reason, and he does not hold the reason that hemp is strong according to the reasoning of the Rash. And that he did not write his words in the manner of a dispute on the Shulchan Aruch is because it is possible that the Shulchan Aruch as well is dealing in this manner.
