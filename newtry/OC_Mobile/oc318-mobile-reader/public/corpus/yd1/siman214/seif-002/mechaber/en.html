Public acceptance applies to them and to their descendants, even regarding matters that the townspeople did not accept upon themselves by agreement, but rather practice on their own to make a fence and safeguard for the Torah. And likewise those who come from outside the city to dwell there — behold, they are like the people of the city, and they are obligated to act according to their enactment, even regarding matters that were forbidden to them in their town because of their custom, when the custom of the city to which they came to dwell does not forbid — they are permitted in them if they do not intend to return.
