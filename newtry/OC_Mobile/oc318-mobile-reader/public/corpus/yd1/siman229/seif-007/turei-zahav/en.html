His condition is nothing. Meaning: I would have said since he stipulated this condition it will not help him any asking — we do not say this; it is proven in the Gemara there is no vow in the world for which asking will not help; so Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rivash; and from the language "for if so they release first" that he wrote here at the end — it implies all who did not say he will not be asked on it, only anonymously that it shall never be released — needs only one release, namely what releases the essence of the oath, and need not release on what he said it shall never be released. And it puzzles me what difference between this and saying "without release" in seif 4; and in my humble opinion Rivash author of this seif holds also regarding saying "without release" — needs only one release, from the reason he did not mention vow again, like Rabbi Shimon that I mentioned in seif 4 per the Ran; or from the reason that all who does not remember any matter about himself that he will not be asked — this is not like another oath; or possible when he was stringent with "without release" — it was as if he mentioned himself that he will not be the one asked, which is not so in saying it shall never be released. And regarding practical matter it appears to me even here he should make two releases, one on what he said it shall never be released, and one on the essence of the oath; there is no difference between this and "without release" in seif 4 — so it appears to me.
