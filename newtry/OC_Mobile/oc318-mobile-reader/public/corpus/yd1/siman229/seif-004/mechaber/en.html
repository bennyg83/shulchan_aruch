One who swore regarding one matter and swore that he not be asked about his oath — he is asked first about the latter oath, that he swore they not release him, and afterward he is asked about the first. And likewise if he swore or vowed not to do a matter without release and without annulment — he is asked first about what he said "without release," and afterward he is asked about the essence of the vow (responsum of Rashba, and so is implied in Beit Yosef, siman 228, daf 272, end of side 1). And likewise a congregation that imposed cherem and stipulated that they not make release, and not release the release until the end of the world — they release first the latter cherem that they imposed not to release, and afterward they release the first. {Rama: One who swore regarding one matter and said that if they ask about his vow he is not a Jew — it is forbidden to ask about his vow; and even if he was asked about it b'dieved, he should be rebuked and lashed, for he tied his faith to his vow and needs atonement and forgiveness (Mordechai, chapter HaUmanin). And specifically in this manner one does not release him; but if he said "if I do such-and-such a matter I will not be a Jew," even release he does not need (Piskei Maharai, siman 192). Nevertheless, it appears to me that one who swore did not act well, and he should be rebuked for this.}
