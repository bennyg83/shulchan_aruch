He swore, etc., and likewise, etc., and likewise, etc. See above siman 228 seif 46. Ran wrote: what is stated "forever" can be released with a single annulment, since it is one utterance; but for "annulment and not annulment of [a previous] annulment" — each requires its own [annulment], as written there above. And proof that all of this can be released by annulment comes from the fact that we do not find in chapter 4 of Gittin (36a) or chapter 3 of Makkot that one cannot release it except via a public declaration — and this also shows that even "forever" can be released in a single [annulment], for if not it would be endless and could never be released, and the Talmud should have stated this. And this is what is stated in seif 5: "he forbade," etc.:
