If he swore regarding one matter and stipulated that they cannot release him except So-and-so and So-and-so who are in a distant land — he regrets that condition, and others release it for him, and afterward others release for him the matter he swore about (responsum of Maimonides; Beit Yosef, siman 228, daf 272, end of side 1; and it is in Tashbetz, siman 8, and in Semak):
