If they placed under ban, etc. A ban and a husband’s nullification do not uproot the vow retroactively, but operate only from that point onward.
Likewise, regarding nullification, etc. This implies that at least part of it is released, as with a husband’s nullification, and this is even more clearly implied by Ateret Zahav. However, this is only according to the Mechaber below, siman 234, seif 36. According to the Rama there, even part of it is not released. It is somewhat difficult why the Rama rules here without qualification in accordance with the Mechaber. One may answer that he relied on the ruling below, where these laws are treated principally. Ateret Zahav labored to explain it this way as well.
