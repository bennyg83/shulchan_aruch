One vowed from one and said regarding another "shall be like So-and-so" — all are forbidden, even up to one hundred. If the first was released, all are released. If the last was released — he alone is released and all are forbidden. If the middle one was released — all before him are forbidden and he and all after him are permitted. And likewise one who vowed and his fellow heard and said within the time of speech "and I," and a third heard and said "and I" — if the first was asked about his vow and released, all are released. If the last was asked and released — the last alone is permitted and all are forbidden. If the second was asked and released — the second and the last are permitted and the first is forbidden. And the same applies to one who vowed from meat and grasped bread upon meat and grasped honey upon bread — he forbade upon himself a loaf twice, or swore upon it twice; even though he was asked about it and they released him, he is forbidden until he is asked about it and they release him a second time:
