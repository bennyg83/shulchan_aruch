Shach wrote: although an oath does not take effect on an oath, nevertheless when the first was released the second takes effect retroactively; and all this if his intent is only to ask about the first; but if he asks about all of them, one release suffices, as above siman 228 s.m. 66; but Taz wrote regarding oath one cannot release with one release, even to release the second before the first one should not be lenient; see there; a matter in the reason {Rama: and in Nekudot HaKesef he challenged him and rejected his proofs, and nevertheless concluded to be stringent; see there}.
