Avodat HaTosafot; see in responsum Radbaz HaChadashot siman 17 on what fools are accustomed to say "as God is true so this is true" — this is a criminal offense close to kefira, for one must not equate the truth of the Creator's existence with the truth of any creature; one should rebuke whoever says it and punish and discipline until he returns from his evil way; but regarding shevuah, if he promises his fellow when saying thus, it appears he is obligated to fulfill his words, and even if you say there is no complete shevuah, it is no less than middot. See further there siman 95.
