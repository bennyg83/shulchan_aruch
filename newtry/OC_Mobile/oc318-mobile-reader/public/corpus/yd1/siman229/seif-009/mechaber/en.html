One who swore not to speak with So-and-so, and afterward swore that if he speaks with him he will forbid himself meat — he is asked first about the first, and afterward about the second, for they do not release a vow or an oath all the time that they have not taken effect. And likewise if he swore not to benefit So-and-so, or to a sage that he ask him about the oath — he is asked first about the first and afterward about the second regarding the sage. And likewise if he swore not to benefit So-and-so and behold he is a nazir — if he asks about this oath, he asks about his oath first and afterward about his nezirut; and likewise everything similar to this:
