He seeks annulment of the first, etc. The second oath does not take effect until he speaks with that person, yet he is presently forbidden to speak with him. He therefore first seeks annulment of the vow obligating him to speak with that person, and when he then speaks with him, the second oath takes effect. The same applies to one who swore not to derive benefit from the sage, etc. See above, siman 228, seif 17, and below, siman 239, seif 13, seif katan 27.
