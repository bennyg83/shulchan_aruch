Maharshadam and Maharshach rule that where one document contains many conditions and concludes with a single oath upon all of them, release from one condition releases the entire oath. Shaar HaMelech discusses whether the same principle applies where part of a vow never took effect from the outset, such as one of two consecrated animals being blemished or impure. Ritva and Ran appear to disagree.
