Or, etc. Ran there s.v. man tana etc. and s.v. vle'inan etc., and likewise Rosh there and likewise Tur "or even if he said to you and to you" is a generalization; Beit Yosef wrote it is not precise, for even without vav it is a generalization — therefore Shulchan Aruch wrote without vav. And no difficulty — Tur holds like Rashi's text in R' Yochanan in Shevuot 38a "all agree except to you" with vav, and likewise Rosh in Nedarim in commentary there s.v. lezeh korban etc.; but Beit Yosef follows his view as he wrote in Beit Yosef below that the main thing is like Tosafot's text in Shevuot there s.v. hacha etc. and the sugya etc. and the book etc. — therefore he wrote without vav as an inclusion, and likewise Tosafot Zevachim 30b s.v. kezayit etc.
And there are no words, etc. Rambam and it is a generalization; and some say, etc. Ran 27a and Ramban also wrote etc. [and likewise Rosh there in piskei — Ramban's words; but in commentary there 66a s.v. konam he wrote like Tosafot] and see there he also ruled like R' Yosi and like Tosafot's text — and his words contradict each other regarding what he wrote in chapter 9 there as above.
