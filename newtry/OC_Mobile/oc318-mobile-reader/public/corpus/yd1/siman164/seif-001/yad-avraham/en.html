(Siman 164 Shulchan Aruch seif 1) One who had field mortgaged in his hand shall not return and rent it to field owner. Implies to another permitted since ribbit does not come immediately borrower to lender as stated siman 160 seif 13; and see what stated there — likewise here if he does not dwell and gives fixed amount each year of mortgage term — he is borrower and forbidden as Taz stated there; and this reasoning Beit Yosef brought siman 168 in name of Beit HaTerumah and Avodat HaGolah there seif 13 — unlike R. Yeruham in name of his teacher R. Avraham ben Ismael whom Beit Yosef brings siman 172 regarding renting mortgage; if another accepts to give fixed amount per year to lender — throw away since ribbit does not come lender to lender — not so; and wonder on Beit Yosef who brought plainly — this contradicts siman 168 in name of Beit HaTerumah — see there; unless he dwells then not borrower since he gives for his dwelling.
