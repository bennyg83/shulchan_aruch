The Shach writes that even if the document states that the property remained in the lender's possession for a long time before he leased it to the borrower, and even if that was in fact so, it is forbidden. The wording "if he rented" indicates that the Mechaber follows the authorities who hold that this constitutes fixed interest.
The arrangement of Sura. This means that he gave it as security in such a manner that he would not need to redeem it from the lender. Rather, the lender would consume its produce for a set number of years and then return it without payment. In this case it is not regarded as a loan but as a lease. The Beit Yosef cites the students of the Rashba that even with an ordinary mortgage, if another person rented it from the lender, the owner of the field may rent it from that person, since another party intervened. The responsum of the Rashba cited afterward by the Beit Yosef, however, indicates that it remains forbidden even when another person intervenes.
