(There in Hagah) And some say what he ate within three is only avak ribbit, etc., but what he ate after three must return — and so is essence. Reason: mistaken forgiveness — seller thinks after three taken by buyer and does not know asmakhta does not acquire; if he knew would not forgive as explained in poskim. In my humble opinion: if seller was particular about land — insisting not sell his lands at end of three years even at fair value — buyer acquired fruits he ate and need not return per Hagah's explanation — Rav Papa's statement ch. Eizehu Neshekh (66b): if particular about land — certainly acquired; Rashi there as stated; Rosh, Tur, Hagah CM siman 207 seif 9 ruled thus — except we were troubled in Rashi due to question perhaps no longer wishes to redeem and concludes transfers ownership — perhaps therefore not concerned to sell lands at value and redeem with that money knowing asmakhta does not acquire; they explain Rav Papa's words; if so even per Tosafot and Rosh land not acquired but fruits eaten necessarily need not return — for if he did not know asmakhta does not acquire then even land would acquire; and if you say he knew asmakhta does not acquire therefore land not acquired — nevertheless fruits need not return since complete forgiveness knowing and silent.
