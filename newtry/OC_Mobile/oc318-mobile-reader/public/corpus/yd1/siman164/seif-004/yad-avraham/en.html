(There seif 4) He lent him on his field and said if you do not give me from now until three years it is mine and did not say from now in case purchase does not stand within three years he shall not eat fruits, etc. — fixed ribbit, etc. He was precise initially to say did not say from now, etc., due to end: if he ate them — fixed ribbit; for if he said from now where purchase stands after three years retroactively — not fixed ribbit but avak ribbit for what he ate within three years — one side of ribbit: redemption side comes to ribbit, other side perhaps not redeemed and land retroactively his and he ate. Explained plainly Rashi Eizehu Neshekh (63a) regarding made his field sale while seller eats fruits — buyer eating forbidden; and necessarily deals with from now — otherwise no one side ribbit and R. Yehuda would not disagree there per Rashi (65a) — see there. Further proof baraita there (63a) and so Beitus ben Zunin acted per sages; as mishnah there (65a) and Gemara establishes plainly mishnah deals from now (66a top) — see there Rashi proves plainly dispute R. Yehuda and rabbis deals from now: per R. Yehuda buyer eats only what stated there; Rashi and Nimukei Yosef; per rabbis he shares — wonder at first glance: rabbis explicitly said (63a) when seller eats fruits permitted — why seller not eat since buyer owes nothing; fine regarding seller sold field and gave partial payment and said when you bring more permitted money acquired from now forbidden to eat lest he bring more and seller ate because waited for buyer — unlike here buyer owes nothing; Tur even though wrote avak ribbit — nevertheless precise not to say from now due to end what he ate after three must return; Hagah only when did not say from now otherwise acquired after three — found to teach in this law seller always permitted; therefore Taz did not mention since obvious buyer forbidden eat within three in all cases even if said from now — only avak ribbit one-sided; and so plainly Rivash's responsum Beit Yosef brought siman 174 — see there; without from now for Mechaber fixed ribbit and for Hagah avak ribbit; and after three with from now acquired retroactively and if fruits still exist give to buyer as siman 174 seif 5 — see there. (There in Hagah) And some say what he ate within three is only avak ribbit, etc., but what he ate after three must return — and so is essence. Reason: mistaken forgiveness — seller thinks after three taken by buyer and does not know asmakhta does not acquire; if he knew would not forgive as explained in poskim. In my humble opinion: if seller was particular about land — insisting not sell his lands at end of three years even at fair value — buyer acquired fruits he ate and need not return per Hagah's explanation — Rav Papa's statement ch.
