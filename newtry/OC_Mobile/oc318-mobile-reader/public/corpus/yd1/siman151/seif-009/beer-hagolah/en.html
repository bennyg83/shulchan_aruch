A rabbinic decree lest he lend it or rent it to a non-Jew, and transgress the negative commandment — as it is written "you shall not do any work" etc. "and all your livestock" — and also because of the "trying out" concern: lest he sell it to him close to Shabbat and the non-Jew tries it while it is loaded, and it hears his voice and goes because of him; and there is an opinion in chapter 24 of Shabbat that he would be liable for a sin-offering. This is not the case via a broker, since he does not lend it, etc., for it is not his; and as for the "trying out" concern — it does not know his voice.
