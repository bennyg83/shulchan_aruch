Permitted to support their poor, etc. It is difficult — for in Gittin chapter HaNizakin (Gittin 61a) the Sages taught: we support poor gentiles with poor Jews — it implies but not by themselves; and why did the Tur not also write "with poor Jews"? I saw in the name of RaShLaL that the Tur holds the Gemara did not mean precisely "with poor Jews" — and it is strained, for when the Talmud's author was not precise in the baraita's wording to say not precisely more than the Tur was precise. It appears to me to be precise: this precision is also in the Gemara there — in the mishnah there they said: one does not protest against poor gentiles taking leket, shikchah, and pe'ah — as the Tur and Shulchan Aruch also wrote nearby, and they did not mention this "with poor Jews"; and it appears to me that all is resolved: in truth there is no difference between with poor Jews or not; rather the baraita was careful not to say that which it said "we support poor gentiles" is a mitzvah in this — therefore it said "with poor Jews," that it is obvious there is also mitzvah with them as with Jews, or even somewhat less — this is impossible, for with Jews there is mitzvah from the Torah and a gentile is only because of darkei shalom alone — it turns out they have no lineage with each other regarding mitzvah; and how does it say "with"? Rather certainly to teach there is no prohibition in the matter — lest you say as with returning a lost object to a gentile where there is prohibition, as in the chapter HaNisrafim, deriving from the verse "so that the watered be sated with the thirsty," and Rashi explained: because he equates gentile with Jew and shows God's mitzvot are not dear to him — for behold he does so also for a gentile; and I would say also regarding supporting their poor thus, not to equate them with Jews — the baraita teaches us there is no prohibition in this if he equates them with Jews since there is darkei shalom in this. Therefore the tanna said here "with poor Jews" — there is no prohibition in equating them in this mitzvah; and since this wording is needed only to teach the matter is not because of mitzvah but permission — it teaches us; therefore the tanna of the mishnah who taught "one does not protest against poor gentiles taking leket," etc. — need not say there "with poor Jews," for he already said "one does not protest"; and this is permissive wording and not mitzvah wording — therefore no need to explain here the wording "with poor Jews." And the Tur who explicitly wrote "permitted to support their poor" — behold he explicitly wrote permissive language — therefore no need to explain the wording "with poor Jews." And this appears to me clear and proper with Heaven's help.
