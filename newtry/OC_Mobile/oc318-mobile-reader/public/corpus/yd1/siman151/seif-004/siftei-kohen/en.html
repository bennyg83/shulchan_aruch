Not to sell. For example where they are suspected of bestiality — so it is in the Gemara (Avodah Zarah end of page 14) and Rashi, Tosafot, and all the poskim — unlike the Eliyah Rabba to whom this was hidden, who wrote the reason that in those places they forbade small animals because of slaughtering them on account of large animals — and this is a decree upon a decree, for large animals themselves are already a distant decree as below; also the entire sugya does not go according to him — see there. I then found in Rashi and the Bartenura chapter Makom SheNahagu (Pesachim 53a) who wrote like the Eliyah Rabba — and I am astonished; and the matter requires investigation in Tosafot there page 14 s.v. amar Rav etc.
Large animal etc. A decree: selling because of lending or renting; and in lending or renting it is forbidden because the gentile performs labor on Shabbat with the Israelite’s animal, and he is commanded regarding his animal’s rest as written "you shall not do any labor" etc. "and all your animals"; alternatively, a decree because sometimes he will sell it on Friday evening close to dark and say to him "try it out" — and it heeds his voice and goes on account of him, making him one who drives his animal on Shabbat.
Broker. An Israelite — these reasons do not apply to him.
