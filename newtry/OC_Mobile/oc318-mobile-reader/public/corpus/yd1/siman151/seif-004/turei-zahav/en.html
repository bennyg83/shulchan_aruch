The leniency was practiced in all. The reason, in the Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer Mitzvot Gadol: the concern of nisuyon does not apply nowadays, since we are not well-versed enough in [reading] the animal’s voice, and it is also not our practice to lend or rent to a gentile. The Ran writes that this is why they are lenient even to buy [an animal] in order to profit from selling it — but nevertheless a person of careful practice should be stringent and minimize. But regarding lending and renting, one must protest those who follow the lenient view.
