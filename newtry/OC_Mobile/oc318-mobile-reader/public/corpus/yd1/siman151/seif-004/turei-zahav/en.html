The Tur wrote in the name of the Ramban: when he sells to a butcher — certainly for slaughter and permitted; and to a landowner — certainly for work and forbidden; and seemingly the precisions contradict each other — one who is neither butcher nor landowner we infer from the beginning for stringency and from the end for leniency. It appears his principal intent: we follow him whether for leniency or stringency — if he is a butcher and most of the world buys for work, we follow him for leniency, and likewise the reverse for stringency; but with one who is neither butcher nor landowner we follow the majority for what they buy.
