We do not sell to them — and not to a Jew suspected of selling to them, and not to a Jewish bandit — anything that can cause harm to the public, such as bears and lions; and not any weapon, and not an anvil (“shtak” in Ashkenazic; so Rashi explained in Avodah Zarah daf 15), and not chains and collars (meaning as in “and they put him in the stocks,” and they put him in collars; meaning iron around the neck), and not chains of iron, and even not a bar of iron. And we do not sharpen weapons for them, and we do not build for them a place in which they judge people.
