Even in a place where they permitted, etc. The wording is very difficult — it implies there is a place where it is forbidden to rent, but behold even in Eretz Yisrael it is permitted to rent as written in seif 8 and in the Gemara Avodah Zarah 21a we say explicitly "even in a place where they said to rent" — from which it implies there are places where they do not rent; and the anonymous view is like R. Meir — for if like R. Yosi, everywhere they rent; see there. And if so for us who rule like R. Yosi, this wording "even in a place," etc. does not apply — rather "and they did not permit," etc.; unless we strain that the mechaber holds as written in the Yerushalmi that Tosafot brought there — specifically in Eretz Yisrael they did not permit except to rent, etc.; see there. Again after much time I found in responsum R. Yehudah ben Chabib siman 78 who addressed the aforementioned difficulty in the Rambam's wording, which is like the mechaber's wording, and answered: certainly in the mishnah the anonymous view is like R. Meir — for no prohibition of rental in any place was mentioned in the mishnah according to R. Yosi, and it is not relevant to say according to his view "even in a place"; but in the Gemara they said that according to R. Yosi it is forbidden to rent them three adjacent houses — and the Rambam wrote it nearby; and regarding this prohibition he wrote "even in a place," etc.; see there.
