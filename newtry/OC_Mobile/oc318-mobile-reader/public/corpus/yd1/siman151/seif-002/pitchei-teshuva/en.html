They can buy. See responsum Emunat Shmuel siman 14, who wrote this permission is not clear to him — for in the Gemara they said the difference is where he has another animal, and they did not say where he can buy another animal; and moreover if so the law of our mishnah "forbidden to sell" would be completely nullified — for if there is not in the whole city one who will sell an animal or frankincense (and according to what is written below siman 160 s.k. 1 in the name of the Melamed, this is not such a difficulty — for we require specifically that the other seller also be a gentile, so the prohibition of lifnei iver should not arise at all per se); and since we found in the Gemara only where he has another animal, we do not permit in other cases; see there.
