Be'er Heitev and in responsum Pnei Yehoshua Yoreh De'ah siman 3: forbidden lifnei iver — for a gentile it is rabbinic prohibition in our time, for Hashem stood, etc., as Rashi explained in Avodah Zarah 12; and therefore in what the Rama wrote in Even HaEzer siman 5 seif 2 — two views regarding selling roosters to a gentile when one knows he is a castrator — one may practice leniently in a place of some need; see there. And Tashbetz part 3 siman 264 wrote: although by law it is permitted for a Jew to cook nevelah for a gentile and hand it to him — for it is forbidden to hand to a gentile only a nursing mother; nevertheless there is ugliness in dealing with nevelah, and there is no enmity concern because he can excuse himself from it; see there. And in siman 266 he wrote: entrails of a gentile's slaughter — forbidden to hand to a gentile because of lifnei iver; and gentiles are called all idolaters, even Ishmaelites, except Israel; see there.
