Even, etc., permanently. Like Tosafot's words there s.v. af, etc.; and some want to bring, etc.; so too the Rosh there and Shulchan Aruch; and he wrote in the gloss: and nowadays, etc. (Likkut) Permanently. As written in the Tosefta: here and there one should not rent, etc.; but one rents to them storehouses and treasuries and inns even though it is known he brings idolatry into them ; the reason must be because it is not permanent; so too the Rosh there even though Tosafot rejected there; and he wrote another reason in the name of R. Chaim: what is written in the Tosefta "here and there" deals only with Eretz Yisrael and Syria, but in chutz la'aretz it is permitted ; and so explicitly in the Yerushalmi: even in a place where they said to rent, etc. ; behold in a place where they are accustomed to sell, one sells and rents even for a residence; see there in Tosafot [and so too the Ran: this "do not bring" is merely an asmachta ; the Torah forbade only that a Jew not bring into his house; therefore in Eretz Yisrael where they are commanded to uproot idolatry the Sages forbade; so too from what is written in the end and in chutz la'aretz one should not rent ; and if the beginning is also in chutz la'aretz, why is the end needed; and in chutz la'aretz. There] (end).
The renter, etc. For rental acquires partially ; meaning what is written "rental does not acquire" is for stringency; and they strained that case in Bava Metzia 56b ; but Tosafot already answered there and in Avodah Zarah 15a and Shulchan Aruch ; specifically for ona'ah; and he wrote in Be'er HaGolah there: and it does not appear; see there [but there is to be stringent according to what the Rosh wrote to permit rental to a gentile nowadays ; although for us rental does not acquire since the hand of nations is strong and in their laws rental for a day is like a sale, etc.; and what is written "the hand of nations is strong" ; it does not appear, for otherwise we act according to their laws as written in the incident of R. Mari bar Rachel (Bava Metzia 108b) "we act according to their laws," etc.; and all the more from a Jew to a gentile it acquires].
