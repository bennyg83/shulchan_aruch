Even in a place where they permitted renting, they permitted only for a storehouse and the like, but not for a dwelling, because he brings elilim into it permanently. {Rama: And nowadays the practice is to rent even for a dwelling, since they do not practice bringing idolatry into their houses (Tur). One who rents a house from a non-Jew — one should be stringent not to leave there the idolatry of a non-Jew (Hagahot Ashiri, at the end of the first chapter of Avodah Zarah).}
