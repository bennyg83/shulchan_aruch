Permitted to inhale. See in Sha'ar HaMelekh chapter 1 of Laws of Forbidden Foods regarding clay dust called "tabak" bought from gentiles — whether permitted to inhale it, for some doubt the matter because sometimes they sprinkle it with stam their wine; and he expanded greatly on this and concluded that even according to the majority of rabbanim who ruled stam their wine is forbidden in benefit — nevertheless permitted to inhale the tabak, for this is exactly like the case of kli sheni that all permit; and the halachah is decided — except the view of "there is one who says" who wrote Rif, and all the more in doubt that perhaps there is sixty against the wine sprinkled — for in this all agree reicha is not significant; and even though all this is only assuming that what they sprinkle is only to give smell — nevertheless still there is concern lest they give wine to strengthen the tabak — if so one cannot permit on account of reicha not being significant, for nevertheless he benefits from the body of issur through which the wine strengthens the tabak; nevertheless there is room to permit in conclusion, since we establish libation wine noten taam in min she'eino mino is nullified in sixty, and in doubt perhaps there is sixty — it is a rabbinic doubt for leniency, since stam their wine is rabbinic, and comparable to above siman 98 in nishpakh — requires study from what he wrote there seif 3 "in what case," etc.; and perhaps he did not write this only as a mere section [however according to what Peri Megadim wrote in siman 94 there s.k. 7 in name of responsum Emunat Shmuel — it fits well; and see what he wrote below siman 123 s.k. 14] — all the more in doubt there are many doubts at the outset of the matter whether stam their wine nowadays is forbidden in benefit, and lest what they put in is only to give smell, and lest there is sixty — see there that he concluded thus for practical halachah:
