Permitted to smell in one's mouth the scent of libation wine through the hole in the barrel to know if it is good: {Rama: But forbidden to taste it even though one does not swallow it (Ribash siman 288); and forbidden to sprinkle libation wine that is forbidden in benefit (in Sha'arim in name of Hagahot Ashiri end Avodah Zarah); but permitted to sprinkle their ordinary wine, for it is permitted in benefit (Arukh general rule 39).}
