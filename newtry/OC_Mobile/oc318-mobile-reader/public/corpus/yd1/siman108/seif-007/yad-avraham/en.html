(Seif 7) Spices of idolatry, kilayim of the vineyard, and orlah — forbidden to smell them. Mahari Mintz there brought poskim that even if there is no eating — forbidden to smell if standing or intending for this; unlike responsum Emunat Shmuel that he brought in Elef HaMagen in Orach Chaim siman 256 [however in Issur VeHeter general rule 39 it is like Emunat Shmuel and his proof from musk and from Tosafot Avodah Zarah]; if so all the more in issur hanaah — forbidden to smell if intending even if not standing for this; and so from what Peri Chadash wrote in Orach Chaim siman 448 to forbid smelling bread of gentiles like Pesach; and so in Hagahot Issur VeHeter; and it appears to this rabbi from what Rosh wrote regarding blessing of enjoyment — liable when intending even if not standing for this; and the same regarding issur — forbidden when intending, for if it were not forbidden he would not be liable to recite a blessing; and as Taz wrote in such a case in Orach Chaim end of seif 17:
