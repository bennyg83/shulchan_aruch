(7) And permitted to roast liver in an oven with a dish l'chatchila, for one who says reicha is significant — for all blood is lean. Turei HaDeshen in rulings and writings siman 76; and Beit Din Beit Yosef siman 97 wrote that its taste does not appear favorable to him. However Mahari Mintz in responsum siman 15 ruled like Turei HaDeshen, and Beit Din Darkei Moshe in this siman; and Shach wrote s.k. 1: that which Beit Yosef wrote that its taste does not appear favorable — this applies to a fatty dish, but when both are lean — certainly Beit Yosef does not disagree — see there; and so too above part 1:
