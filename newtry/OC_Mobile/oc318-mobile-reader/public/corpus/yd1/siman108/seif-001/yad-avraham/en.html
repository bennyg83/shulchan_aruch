(Siman 108 seif 1 in hagahah) If they baked or roasted — Issur VeHeter — under one uncovered pan — forbidden even b'dieved; and the same if they baked in such a case bread with meat — forbidden to eat it with milk; but one after the other — no concern; and even l'chatchila permitted to eat it with milk one after the other, even in something that has a permitted aspect; for according to Rif and Ran in Piskei HaRosh — behold bread has a permitted aspect, for it is possible to eat it by itself, and nevertheless permitted one after the other to eat it with milk; and so proven explicitly in Gemara first chapter of Beitzah (daf 4) — where it becomes weak and issur is permitted even in something that has a permitted aspect; and see Tosafot Pesachim (27b) and in the Gaon's explanation; and one after the other has "it becomes weak and issur is permitted" as proven in Shach s.k. 11 who wrote that so appears in Gemara Avodah Zarah regarding what resolves regarding smell of cumin that it becomes weak — see there and requires study; and see siman 102:
(There) Some say wherever we say reicha is significant — that is when there is not sixty; but when there is sixty — even everything in the oven nullifies the issur; and for need of loss one may practice thus. And one must say that what he wrote "and for need of loss," etc. — refers to what he wrote to be lenient to say that everything in the oven nullifies the issur; for we do not lenient in this unless for need of loss; but not when there is sixty in one piece — this even without loss is permitted, as proven in several places regarding a kid roasted in its milk, or an issur piece that survived with heter pieces — we estimate sixty as explained in Tosafot and Rosh in Piskei HaRosh in name of Rabeinu Tam and in all poskim; and behold Rabeinu Tam holds reicha is significant, and nevertheless for all sixty helps — this is clear; and so Turei Chayim general rule 36 law 7:
(There) Some say an issur that forbids in any amount such as chametz on Pesach — reicha is significant and forbids even b'dieved if the oven is small and closed and Issur VeHeter uncovered, etc. Requires study: for in a place without loss — behold the hagahah wrote above nearby that one should be stringent even in other issurim in a small completely closed oven — why discuss specifically an issur that forbids in any amount? And it appears the hagahah's view is like Magen Avraham in Orach Chaim Laws of Pesach siman 447 — that on Pesach chametz in a small completely closed oven, even in a place of loss one should not be lenient; unless open a little where smoke exits — and in other issurim permitted in such a case even without loss, as the hagahah wrote above; and this appears clear to me — requires study:
