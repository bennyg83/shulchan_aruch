(1) [Seif 1] One may not roast kosher meat with nevelah meat, etc., in one oven, etc. — this is when one of them is fatty, even the kosher one, for the heter goes and seasons the issur through its smell, and then the issur goes and forbids the heter; but when both are lean — permitted even l'chatchila. Beit Yosef siman 97 in name of Rashba. Taz s.k. 1, and he wrote unlike Turei Chayim general rule 35 law 3 who wrote that even when both are lean one should be stringent l'chatchila — see there. Shach s.k. 1. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tashbetz part 3, Peri Chadash part 1, Binyamin Zeev part 1, Chokhmat Adam part 1, Chachmat Shlomo general rule 62 part 1, Zivchei Tzedek part 1; however one who is stringent — side 2. (3) Smell of bread in bread — permitted. Turei Chayim general rule 35 law 3, even if the bread was kneaded with oil. Minchat Yehudah on Turei Chayim there part 11. Specifically oil is called "pounded" and does not season; but fat seasons. Mishnat Chachamim part 1; and see below part 30:
(5) In a place where they practice not to eat bread of gentiles — no concern if baked in one oven, for several reasons: either because there is no smell from bread to bread, or because since its prohibition is only rabbinic — we do not decree. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tashbetz part 20, Zivchei Tzedek part 2:
(6) And our challah, which is only rabbinic — permitted l'chatchila to bake it with bread, even if the challah touched non-sacred bread, if the non-sacred loaf has twice its amount. Issur VeHeter general rule 34 law 24. Knesset HaGedolah there part 21. Lechem HaPanim part 1, Binyamin Zeev end of part 1 — and it appears all the more so permitted to burn challah in the oven in which bread is baked. And see below in hagahah end of siman 122 and in our words in Orach Chaim siman 457 part 40 — see there:
(7) And permitted to roast liver in an oven with a dish l'chatchila, for one who says reicha is significant — for all blood is lean. Turei HaDeshen in rulings and writings siman 76; and Beit Din Beit Yosef siman 97 wrote that its taste does not appear favorable to him.
