(1) [Seif 1] One may not roast kosher meat with nevelah meat, etc., in one oven, etc. — this is when one of them is fatty, even the kosher one, for the heter goes and seasons the issur through its smell, and then the issur goes and forbids the heter; but when both are lean — permitted even l'chatchila. Beit Yosef siman 97 in name of Rashba. Taz s.k. 1, and he wrote unlike Turei Chayim general rule 35 law 3 who wrote that even when both are lean one should be stringent l'chatchila — see there. Shach s.k. 1.
