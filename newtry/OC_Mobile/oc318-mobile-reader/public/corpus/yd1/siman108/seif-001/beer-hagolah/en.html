The language of the Rambam in chapter 15 of Hilchot Maachalot Assurot. This follows the view of his teacher, the Rif, who cites in the seventh chapter of Chullin the dispute between Rav and Levi in Pesachim 76b, and rules like Levi that aroma is not legally significant, though it is initially forbidden. He derives this from the wording "he roasted them," rather than "one may roast them." The Tur likewise writes this in the name of Rashi and the Halachot Gedolot.
