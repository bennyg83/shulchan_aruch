(Siman 108 in Shulchan Aruch seif 1) And even if the issur was very fatty, etc. Shach s.k. 1 — specifically when one of them is fatty, etc.; but when both are lean, etc. — and Rabeinu Tam means that when both are lean reicha is not significant; and proof from Lechem HaPanim that they baked in one oven even chametz and matzah — and it should have forbidden on account of reicha being significant — or that when both are lean reicha is not significant; and Rashi challenged: behold in Lechem HaPanim there is oil, for there is seasoning — and to resolve: it is in Pesachim daf 38 side 2 — they do not fulfill with Lechem HaPanim because it is written guard the matzot and we require matzah guarded for the sake of matzah; rich matzah is excluded, for it is not guarded for matzah but for the sacrifice; Shmuel bar Rav Yitzchak said it is a quarter-log and divided into many loaves — if so, since it is for rich matzah a small matter, likewise for seasoning we say it is a small amount and both are lean — and investigate. [And see in Peleti s.k. 7 that he rejected this reasoning — see there, he explained well]:
