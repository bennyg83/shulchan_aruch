(Siman 108 seif 1 in hagahah) Even in mere dough — permitted. It is difficult for me: if the issur is sharp and the heter is covered in dough — behold the dough itself is uncovered and becomes forbidden through smell; and automatically if there is a dish in that pot of heter — since the dough absorbed smell of issur, the dish will become forbidden through gravy that extracts the absorption from the dough:
