Meat — the Acharonim [added] — specifically when one of them, whether issur or heter, is fatty, for the heter goes and seasons the issur; even though "what became forbidden cannot go," etc. — regarding smell we say that heter seasons issur; but when both are lean — even l'chatchila permitted — unlike Rama in Turei Chayim who forbids even l'chatchila; and see Peleti that one should be stringent l'chatchila:
Behold it is permitted; and that which above siman 96 — bread baked with roast is forbidden to eat with kutach — there it is l'chatchila, for the bread can be eaten for anything — roast and the like — therefore forbidden:
That holds twelve se'ahs — this is as in Talmudic times when they attached bread to the oven wall; if so on all four walls they would attach — if on all four walls together twelve se'ahs could be attached — behold this is a large oven; and such was the tradition of the ancients that a large oven's measure is twelve se'ahs; therefore they did not give a measure for height, for this diminishes the smell, since all the walls join; and if the oven is tall — the oven walls are large and much bread is checked on them; and if the oven is low — the walls shrink and bread attachment; and if so when the measure is twelve se'ahs — everything is included, and there is absolutely no measure for width and height of ovens:
And its mouth open — Peri Chadash's view is to be lenient even if open where smoke exits; and he is correct, for the smell exits through that hole outside and is diminished:
And they do not touch — see Peleti: if they touch — it does not forbid only kedei netilah like hot issur law, but the whole thing is forbidden on account of reicha being significant; and see Peleti:
