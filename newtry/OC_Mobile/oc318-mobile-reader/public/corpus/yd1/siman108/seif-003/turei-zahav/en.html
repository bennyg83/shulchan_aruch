Because it is possible in another manner. In Tosafot last chapter of Avodah Zarah they brought them in Beit Yosef siman 97: the reason is that the gentile will not listen to make a new spit each time he pours issur on it — end of his words. It appears the explanation: behold we buy bread from gentiles and they have only one spit from which they also remove issur; and it is impossible to be careful, for the gentile will not listen if we tell him to always make a new spit — therefore a Jew too is permitted to use it l'chatchila.
