(There seif 1 in hagahah) And if the issur is a sharp thing, etc., even b'dieved forbidden. This law is from Issur VeHeter who wrote: if the issur is sharp reicha is significant — the Gemara needed to establish terumah cumin as permitted because sharp issur weakens — implying otherwise forbidden on account of being sharp; and he challenged: all this is according to Abbaye who holds reicha is significant — but we establish like Rava that reicha is not significant and no change is needed — the reason is sharp weakens issur; and if so, on the contrary it implies even sharp things we establish reicha is not significant; and one may say Tosafot wrote there in the sugya that even according to Rava's daughter reicha is significant, except regarding his daughter Rava permits because it harms him, etc.; and Tosafot challenged why Rava did not also challenge Rav from Pesachim that reicha is significant from terumah cumin — and one may say see there; and at first glance one might say Tosafot's question — certainly according to Rav he could not challenge, for one may say it weakened — but according to Abbaye who holds even where it harms him we say reicha is significant and he holds even weakening does not help — we say reicha is significant; and if so even without Abbaye we are forced to his reason of weakening issur — and Issur VeHeter's words are challenged; but all this is according to Tosafot who hold Rava also holds like Rav and we are forced to distinguish between nevelah meat roasted, etc., and this daughter — for all permit the daughter because it harms him — unlike for us who establish like Rif and his proof, since Rava holds it is nullified we establish the halachah like him and he does not distinguish between nevelah meat roasted and his daughter — and Issur VeHeter's reasoning is unnecessary; and if so Issur VeHeter's proof is thus: since the baraita mentions bread baked and terumah cumin — there is no seasoning at all — it should have stated a leniency that even a thing with seasoning, meaning fat, is permitted according to Rava who according to Rif holds reicha is not significant at all even in a thing with seasoning;
