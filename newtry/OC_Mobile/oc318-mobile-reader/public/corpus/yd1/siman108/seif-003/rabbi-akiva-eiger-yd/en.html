(There s.k. 12) That it returns and emits to food — it appears there is reicha and seasoning for a vessel, only it is not so strong as to return and emit to food; and it is difficult for me in what Rama wrote: unless the pan sweated from both — if they first baked the issur beneath the pan and the pan did not sweat from it, and afterward baked the heter beneath the pan and the pan also sweated — should it not become forbidden, since smell first entered the pan when baking the heter afterward beneath it, and the sweat extracts absorbed issur in the pan?
