(There in hagahah) And some say the issur forbids in any amount, etc. Magen Avraham in Orach Chaim siman 447 s.k. 4 wrote, and Rama in Yoreh Deah that in a place of loss one may be lenient — see there; and one may say specifically when open somewhat, etc.; and also Rif chapter 6 of Chullin forbids, for Rav who said reicha is significant according to his taste because he holds min b'mino in any amount — and we establish in sixty — it implies on Pesach where we establish in any amount for all reicha is significant, etc. — see his words; meaning according to Rav and Levi they disagree whether there is taste of any amount in smell or not — otherwise why does Rif need to say Rav goes according to his taste, etc.; we establish nullified — even if we held min b'mino in any amount in other issurim, nevertheless in smell it would be permitted since there is no smell even any amount — or they do not disagree that for all there is any amount in smell, only according to Rav who forbids any amount he goes according to his taste; and he challenged Rif: behold he does not hold Tosafot's distinction that it harms him — if so since there is any amount in taste anyway, why does Rava permit his daughter l'chatchila — behold Rava also holds min b'mino in any amount; and the question on the Gemara there on Levi from two Pesachim as one — what is the question, behold any amount of taste exists anyway and it is forbidden l'chatchila. And it seems at first glance one answer: let it be a refutation of Rav — for we should not say kid and lamb because it implies on account of mixture of bodies — the verse teaches on account of mixture of tastes — Rav holds reicha is significant; and why did Rif not immediately challenge — he needed kid and lamb that forbid on account of mixture of bodies — if kid and kid it is min b'mino and we follow the name as we establish like Rava it is forbidden on account of smell; and the Gemara's question is according to Abbaye who holds we do not follow the name except after taste — if so Rif's explanation is: Rif wants to rule nullified — meaning according to Rav he is forced to answer the strained explanation of two pots; and the Gemara asked according to Rava who holds we follow the name — there is no need for strained explanation — therefore Rif says if we say we follow the name, nevertheless we forbid reicha only if we say min b'mino forbids in any amount — and we establish in sixty there is no room to forbid on account of smell at all; but in truth the halachah is nullified that reicha is not significant — meaning there is not even any amount — unlike Magen Avraham. However according to Magen Avraham there is another resolution: certainly in smell there is no any amount at all according to Levi and Rav — one may say there is more than any amount in smell — what is any amount here; rather Rif's explanation is: according to one who says min b'mino is nullified because it requires giving taste in another thing — unlike min b'mino that does not give taste in another thing and its issur taste is not discernible — it is nullified also smell is not considered at all and nullified even in min she'eino mino — for in smell there is no taste; and furthermore according to this even bare smell without nullification such as his daughter is permitted; but according to one who says min b'mino is not nullified because taste is not nullified until another taste nullifies issur taste — but min b'mino where there is no other changed taste to nullify issur taste is not nullified — therefore regarding reicha too, even though there is no taste and the taste is not felt, only there is smell — it is not nullified, for in min b'mino the issur taste is also not felt nevertheless since heter cannot nullify it, for all is one taste — it is not nullified; and if so in smell likewise; and according to this explanation even in min she'eino mino Rif holds according to Rav reicha is significant; and by this all questions on Rif are resolved; and according to this on Pesach where the main reason we establish in any amount is because on chametz we are stringent like R' Yochanan that min b'mino is not nullified — certainly also smell is forbidden — and it is clear this was Magen Avraham's approach — investigate:
