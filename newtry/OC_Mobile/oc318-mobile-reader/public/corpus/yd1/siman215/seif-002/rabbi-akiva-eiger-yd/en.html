(Siman 215 se'if 2) These that entered your heart. And behold he is not permitted without release at all, as below (siman 232 se'if vav) — for there it is complete inadvertence regarding his father. But here, he should have known that it is impossible for a year to pass without Shabbatot and Yamim Tovim — Rosh explicitly, and Ran wrote there that below it is a mistake in the essence of the vow, that he never intended regarding his father; but here, by forbidding upon himself a year, he knows that Shabbatot and Yamim Tovim will pass upon him and the vow takes effect — only they open for him "had you known that it is forbidden to fast on the eve of Shabbat":
