In what matters is this said — that he needs release? When they accepted upon themselves in the language of a vow, such as if he said, “Konam, eating on such-and-such days is upon me.” But if they articulated it only in the language of accepting a fast, it does not push aside Shabbat, Yom Tov, and Rosh Chodesh, nor Chanukah and Purim, and he does not need release — even if he accepted upon himself to fast only on Shabbat and Yom Tov alone. (And see in Orach Chayim, siman 568.)
