And even. Bach challenged: what is "even" — on the contrary, all the more so there is no substance in this acceptance, that he accepted upon himself to fast only on Shabbat and Yom Tov. And Shach answered: he properly says "even." According to one explanation, in the first case he did not intend Shabbat and Yom Tov; but even in the latter case where he intended Shabbat and Yom Tov explicitly — what of it? If he encountered them when he accepted in vow-language, it takes effect on Shabbat and Yom Tov if he intended to vow regarding them; and in an unspecified vow if he encountered them, he does not need a release according to Semak.
