And Purim. Since Chanukah and Purim are rabbinic institutions requiring reinforcement, a perpetual vow to fast on those days may be treated more stringently than a one-time fast. Shach reconciles the differing rulings by distinguishing a vow covering every future occurrence from a vow for a limited number of days. He further discusses Rosh Chodesh and the days of Nisan, and concludes that vow-language generally requires annulment, while mere acceptance of a fast may be interpreted according to evident intent.
