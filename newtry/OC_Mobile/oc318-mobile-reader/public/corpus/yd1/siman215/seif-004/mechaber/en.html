One who accepts upon himself in the language of a vow to fast on Shabbat and Yom Tov — the vow takes effect. And likewise one who vows to fast on Sunday or Monday all his days, and Yom Tov or the eve of Yom Kippur fell on them — he is obligated to fast; needless to say regarding Rosh Chodesh. But if Chanukah and Purim fell on them, his vow is null. (And see in Orach Chayim, siman 570, that he did not rule so.)
