Mishnah Nedarim page 15a, and likewise Rambam in chapter 3 of Hilchot Nedarim. And he wrote the reason: oaths do not take effect on a matter of mitzvah, for the one who swears forbids himself from a matter he swore upon, and he is already sworn from Mount Sinai — and one oath does not take effect upon another oath. Unlike a vow, where he forbids the vowed matter upon himself, and that matter is not sworn from Mount Sinai — therefore Scripture said regarding a shevuat bitui "to do harm or good" in discretionary matters, as the Sages expounded; but in vows it says "according to all that proceeds from his mouth he shall do" — it did not distinguish between matters of mitzvah and discretionary matters:
