And they release him from his vow, etc. The Derishah writes that if one vowed to fast on Shabbat or Yom Tov, they release him from his vow, because the vow conflicts with the mitzvah of delighting in Shabbat or Yom Tov. And some say that one does not, etc. This means that although he requires release, he does not receive makkot mardut for having made the vow, for it can be resolved through hatarah. This also resolves the words of Raavyah, for his intent is not that the vow is meaningless, but that after release he is not treated as one who violated a binding prohibition.
