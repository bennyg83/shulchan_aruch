Sitting. Shach wrote: it is not a matter without substance since he mentions the name of the forbidden object; but if he said "the benefit of a sukkah is upon me," it is permitted to sit in a sukkah, for mitzvot were not given for benefit. And see above siman 239 se'if dalet. And Turei Zahav wrote: specifically in vows the law is thus, for since he forbids the object upon himself by saying konam sitting in a sukkah is upon me — he is not as one who vowed to nullify the mitzvah, for he accepted nothing upon himself but forbade the object upon himself (and one does not feed a person something forbidden to him). But an oath forbids himself from doing the matter, and it is not in his power to exempt himself from the obligation of a mitzvah (a vow not to marry a woman fit to bear children — the vow takes effect upon him; but if he swore, his oath is nothing; and if he swore without specification not to marry any woman, the oath takes effect inclusively — Radbaz part 1 siman 1, see there):
And they release.
