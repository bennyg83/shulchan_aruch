(There, Siftei Kohen, subsection 3) Ran. In the name of Tosafot: In the formulation written by the Mechaber, "The sukkah in which I sit," it is obvious that the vow concerns a tangible object, for he prohibits to himself the sukkah in which he sits. Ran adds that even if one says, "Sitting in a sukkah is prohibited to me," the vow is considered to concern a tangible object because he mentions the object. If so, the contrary inference may be drawn: since the Mechaber did not also record such a case, it appears that he does not accept Ran's opinion. This also appears to be the Mechaber's view in siman 213. Rema, however, cites Ran's opinion there in the gloss in the name of some authorities.
