(There Shach se'if kaf gimmel) Ran in the name of Tosafot in this manner: the author wrote "sukkah she'ani yoshev" — it is obvious that it is a matter with substance, for he forbids himself the sukkah in which he sits. But the Ran added: even one who says "sitting in a sukkah is upon me" is called a matter with substance since he mentions the object. If so, on the contrary — from that the author did not write also in this manner, it implies he does not hold like the Ran. And so it appears from the author's view (siman 213).
