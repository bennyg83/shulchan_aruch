However, the Ran's view is like this opinion. The Shach further wrote that according to this opinion, the same applies if he said, "an oath that I will not eat this loaf," and then said, "this loaf is konam upon me": he is liable twice. Likewise, if he swore that he would eat this loaf and then forbade it upon himself by konam, he is forbidden to eat it, because vows take effect upon a mitzvah and upon oaths. So wrote the Ran; end quote.
