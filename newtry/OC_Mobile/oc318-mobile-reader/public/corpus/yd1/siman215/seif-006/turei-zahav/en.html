There are those who say that if he vowed, etc. It appears that the text should read, “And there are those who say,” with a vav. This opinion disagrees with what was written above, that a vow does not take effect upon a negative commandment. Concerning this it states, “And there are those who say,” that it does take effect upon him. The person who arranged the copied text understood that the vav of “and there are those who say” was a marker for seif 6, and therefore arranged six seifim here. In truth, however, all of this belongs to seif 5, and the vav belongs to the word, “and there are those who say.”
