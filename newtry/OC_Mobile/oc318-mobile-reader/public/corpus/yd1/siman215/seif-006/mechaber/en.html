And there is one who says that if he vowed regarding forbidden matters, such as nevelot and treifot, the vow takes effect, and if he eats from them he transgresses “You shall not profane.” But if he associated with them another matter, he is not bound, for his intention is only on the core prohibition — for it is as associating with a forbidden matter. But regarding a matter forbidden d’rabbanan, and he forbade it upon himself in the language of a vow and associated with it another matter — he is bound by the vow and it is forbidden. {Rama: One who vows or swears not to eat matzah on the night of Pesach — rich matzah is not included. But if he vowed not to eat matzah all year, it is possible that rich matzah is included (Rivash, brought by Beit Yosef, end of siman 236).}
