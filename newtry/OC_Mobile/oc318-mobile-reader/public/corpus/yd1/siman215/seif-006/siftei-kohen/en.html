And some say if he vowed from forbidden things, etc. — and likewise if he said an oath that he will not eat this loaf and returned and said konam this loaf upon me — liable twice; and likewise if he swore he will eat this loaf and afterward forbade it to him with konam — forbidden to eat it because vows take effect on a mitzvah and oaths — so Ran there.
But if, etc. — this is even according to the latter view, as in Tur and poskim.
For it is grasping a forbidden matter. However in practice some say we require questioning even for grasping a forbidden matter, as above siman 210.
Rich matzah is not included — meaning permitted to eat it without release; nevertheless one who swore not to eat matzah on Passover night receives lashes and eats matzah on Passover night as explained in Orach Chaim siman 475, and below siman 236 seif 5.
