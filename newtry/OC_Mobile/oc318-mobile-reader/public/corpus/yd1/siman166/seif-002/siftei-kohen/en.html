That it is only dust of interest. For it is not fixed interest except when he fixes at the time of the loan, from what is written "you shall not give" — the Maggid Mishneh.

And that he says thus at the time he extends time for him. But if he already extended time and came within the time and said "dwell in my courtyard for your money which lies idle with me" — it is forbidden — Tur; and it implies it is only dust of interest as I proved in my book, unlike Hagahot Derishah who wrote it is fixed interest.
