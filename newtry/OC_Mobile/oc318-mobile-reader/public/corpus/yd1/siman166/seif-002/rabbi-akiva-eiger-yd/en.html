(Siman 166 seif 2) If it stands for rent. And if lender said lend me and dwell in my courtyard for a dinar — even though worth more, nevertheless not fixed ribbit, for there is no ona'ah on land; unless he said my courtyard rented for four — you dwell for one dinar. So Beit Yosef (siman 164) in name of students of Rashba (siman 376). And per Hagahot Shulchan Aruch CM (seif 27) more than half has ona'ah on land — one can say in such case it is fixed ribbit. See responsum of Ranach (vol. 1 siman 69).
