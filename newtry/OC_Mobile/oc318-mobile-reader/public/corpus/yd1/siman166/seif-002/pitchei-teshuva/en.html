And live in my courtyard. If Reuven rented a house from a non-Jew for ten levonim, then borrowed from Shimon for a fixed time and mortgaged it to him with a deduction of one lavan per day, this is fixed ribbit. It is as though he says, "Lend me money and live in the non-Jew's courtyard, and I will pay." This is from Maharanach I siman 69.
