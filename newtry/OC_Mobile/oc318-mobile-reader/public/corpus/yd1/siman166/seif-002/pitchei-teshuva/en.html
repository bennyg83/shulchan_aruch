And live in my courtyard. Reuven rented a house from a non-Jew for ten levonim. He borrowed money from Shimon for a fixed period and mortgaged the house to him with a deduction of one lavan per day. This constitutes ribbis ketzutzah, because it is as though he said, “Lend me money and live in the non-Jew's courtyard, and I will repay you.” Maharanach, volume 1, siman 69.
