(Siman 166 in Shulchan Aruch seif 2) Lender said lend me and dwell in my courtyard — if it stands for rent: fixed ribbit; if not standing for rent: avak ribbit. Requires study: per Hagah siman 165 it would be fixed ribbit even if not standing for rent in "lend me," for it is benefit of enjoyment he can give to whoever wishes to dwell free; and per those who hold benefit of enjoyment is money — fixed ribbit, as Rambam's view Tur brings. See Lechem Mishneh chapter 5 Laws of Marriage law 6 — understand well; and so explicitly in Rosh, source of above Hagah siman 165: per those who hold benefit of enjoyment is money — fixed ribbit; only per those who hold benefit of enjoyment is not money — only avak ribbit — requires study; end of chapter One Who Betrothes (folio 59) Rosh ruled there like those who hold benefit of enjoyment is money; chapter One Who Borrows seif 17 implies he holds it is not money; see there he wrote so in name of Ramban — requires study, for poskim wrote in name of Ramban benefit of enjoyment is money and Shach brought them CM siman 357. However per Rambam who explained chapter 5 Laws of Marriage and chapter 2 Laws of Theft benefit of enjoyment is not money — no difficulty: how does he hold courtyard not standing for rent is fixed ribbit in lend me? One can say he follows his reasoning who holds any ribbit comes out in court, as stated end of chapter 6 — certainly all the more here — understand well. Similarly Shach wrote CM siman 37 s.k. 9 regarding interested witness — see there: since we do not require monetary interest, even interest of any enjoyment disqualifies — benefit of enjoyment is money for all; see in Lechem Mishneh chapter 5 Laws of Marriage where he expanded. What Lechem Mishneh challenged: strong presumption — we find ribbit without neshech; and so Maharam Schiff. Per Rambam — wonder: he includes all the more neshech every matter, as Mishneh Lamelech wrote — if so behold neshech, etc. Per Tosafot chapter 4 Ketubot there is no prohibition in such case less than perutah value and benefit of enjoyment is not money for Rava, as Shach wrote there; and so Bava Metzia (folio 11 side 2): nevertheless verse does not deal with such case — courtyard not standing, etc.; and as Maggid Mishneh wrote: verse says if you take and if you took return — not applicable here; and as Rosh wrote: neshech without tarbit we do not find even in "throw dinar to sea and lend me maneh" — Ritva wrote in name of Ramban One Who Borrows (folio 69) it is fixed ribbit (unlike Mishneh Lamelech end chapter 5 Laws of Lending who was uncertain) — for he did thus by lender's command and it is as if it came to his hand initially by law of guarantor; and there is also tarbit.
