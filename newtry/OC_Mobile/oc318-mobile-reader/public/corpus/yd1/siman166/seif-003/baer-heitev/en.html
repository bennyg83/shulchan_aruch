That we deduct. In a mortgage, where the property is under the lender's control and the loan was initially made upon it, benefit is not deducted as explained in siman 172. Here it is deducted. Shach.
For the benefit. If the lender rebuilt the ruin for the owner's benefit, the owner immediately owes him money, and living there becomes compensation for waiting. This is forbidden. See siman 172:6. Shach.
