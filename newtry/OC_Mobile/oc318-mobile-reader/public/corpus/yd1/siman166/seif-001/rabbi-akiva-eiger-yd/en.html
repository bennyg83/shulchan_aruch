(Siman 166, seif 2) If the courtyard is ordinarily rented out. If he said, "Lend me money and live in my courtyard for one dinar," then even if the rental value is greater, it is nevertheless not fixed interest, because the laws of ona'ah do not apply to land. This is unless he said, "My courtyard, which rents for four, you may occupy for one dinar." So wrote the Beit Yosef at the end of siman 164 in the name of a responsum of the Rashba, siman 736. According to what is written in the Rama's gloss to Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat, siman 227, seif 29, that when the disparity is more than one-half the laws of ona'ah do apply to land, one can say that in such a case it constitutes fixed interest. See Responsa HaRa'anach, part 1, siman 69.
