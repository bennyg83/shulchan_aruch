One who lends to his fellow should not do labor with his slave, and should not live in his courtyard; it contains 3 se’ifim. One who lends to his fellow should not do labor with his slave, even if he is idle; and he should not live in his courtyard for free, and he should not rent from him for less — even in a courtyard that is not meant to be rented (meaning: it is not intended to be rented out), and [even if] it is a person who does not rent out. And if he already lived in it: since the courtyard is not meant to be rented, he need not give him [payment], even in order to fulfill his obligation before Heaven — even if it is a person who does rent out. And according to Rambam, even if it is a person who does not rent out, it is avak ribbis, and he must return [it] if he comes to fulfill his obligation before Heaven. (And in a courtyard that is meant to be rented it is avak ribbis according to all) (Tur); and see further in Choshen Mishpat at the beginning of siman 72.
