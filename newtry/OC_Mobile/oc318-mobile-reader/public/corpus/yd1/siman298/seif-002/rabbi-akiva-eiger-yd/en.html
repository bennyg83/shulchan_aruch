(Siman 298, Shach seif katan 2) Bach wrote that since it is common. Bach did not state this as a definite permission, but as a legal doubt. He writes that in our lands hemp is found in some places, but only a few can distinguish flax from hemp, and asks whether one may sew it beneath wool when ordinary people cannot recognize the difference. He later inclines to leniency where the stitching is hidden and no Torah prohibition is implicated, because a rabbinic concern in private may be treated leniently, as in Orach Chayyim siman 305, seif 11. In my opinion, there may be room for leniency even regarding a Torah prohibition where the stitching is hidden beneath the garments, based on Tosafot, Shabbos 62a, s.v. vehatanya, that where something is concealed even in the marketplace it may be permitted.
