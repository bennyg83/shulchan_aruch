Things forbidden because of kilayim of garments. In it are 2 seifim.
Forbidden because of shaatnez is only wool of lambs and wool of rams with linen. But wool of camels, wool of hares, down of goats, and all other species are permitted with linen. And likewise hemp and grape-wool and all other species are permitted even with wool of lambs and rams. And the Sages forbade because of marit ayin: silk with wool because it resembles linen; and likewise kelach—it is a species that grows in cities of the sea on stones in the sea and resembles wool—they forbade it because of marit ayin with linen. And nowadays silk is common among us and everyone recognizes it; therefore there is no marit ayin, and it is permitted with wool and with linen.
