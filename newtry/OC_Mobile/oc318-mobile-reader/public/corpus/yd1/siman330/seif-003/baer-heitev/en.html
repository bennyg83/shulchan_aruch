Partner. Specifically when there is an actual partnership — but an arnonit dough is obligated in challah even though [the Israelite] cannot buy out [the non-Jew's share] for money, and even if the Israelite's portion alone does not meet the minimum measure for challah — since perhaps the non-Jew will agree not to take his portion. So ruled the Bach.
Obligated. This implies that even if the non-Jew is standing over [the dough] and it is publicly known that the non-Jew has a stake in it, and even if they purchased the flour together — it is nonetheless obligated. Shakh, and so ruled the Bach.
From within itself. For there is blending in a liquid substance, and in the measure of challah that he separates it is impossible that it not contain some of the Israelite's [portion] — and challah has no minimum measure from the Torah. End quote of the Rosh.
