For he is a partner, etc. This applies specifically when the non-Jew has actual partnership. Dough subject to a royal levy is obligated in challah even when the Jew cannot remove the government's share by paying money, and even when the Jewish share alone lacks the required measure, because the ruler may change his mind and not take it. So rules the Bach.
And if it has the required measure, it is obligated. It implies that if it has the measure it is always obligated, even if the non-Jew stands over it and it is public that the non-Jew's hand is involved, and even if they bought the flour together. So the Bach ruled; see there.
