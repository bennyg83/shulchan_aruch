Two non-Jews, etc., there was, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 3: Two non-Jews who made two kav [of dough] and divided it, then each added to his own [portion from an Israelite's dough] — they are obligated, since they had no time of obligation [as non-Jews] and were not exempt. Two Israelites who made two kav and divided it, then each added to his own — they are exempt, since they already had a time of obligation and were exempted. [Regarding] an Israelite and a non-Jew who made two kav and divided it, then each added to his own — the Israelite's portion is clearly obligated. As for the non-Jew's portion — is the non-Jew's portion obligated on account of the Israelite's portion? The Israelite's portion is obligated and the non-Jew's portion is exempt. See the Beit Yosef.
