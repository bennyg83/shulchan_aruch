That of a Jew is obligated. Because the dough of the Jew, while it was joined to the gentile’s portion, never came into obligation until he later added to it, and therefore it is obligated when he adds. This is not so regarding the gentile’s portion that was joined to the Jew’s portion, which is subject to obligation, for it is as though the gentile kneaded the measure for challah; since the main reason is that he was a gentile at the time of obligation, nothing later helps to obligate it.
