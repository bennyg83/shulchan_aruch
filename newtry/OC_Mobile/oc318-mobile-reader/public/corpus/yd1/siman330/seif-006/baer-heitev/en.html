Obligated. Because the Israelite's dough, while attached to the non-Jew's portion, never came into obligation until he later added to it; therefore it becomes obligated when he adds. This is unlike the non-Jew's portion, which had been attached to the Israelite's portion, which is subject to obligation, and is as though the non-Jew kneaded a full challah measure. Since the essential factor is that he is a non-Jew at the time of obligation, nothing later can make it obligated. End quote from Taz.
