If the dough was between a gentile and an Israelite in partnership and they divided, then the gentile converted and each added to his portion until he completed and added to the Israelite's measure — it is obligated, and the gentile's is exempt: {Rama: Specifically when they made it initially in partnership intending not to divide it and later decided to divide; but if they made it initially intending to divide — even that of the gentile is obligated.}
