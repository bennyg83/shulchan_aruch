She kneaded. For we do not follow the one who rolls, neither for obligation nor for exemption, but only the one whose dough it was at the time of rolling. And the Derishah wrote: and there are places where women buy dough from an idolater baker and take challah from it; it appears that it is forbidden, unless we say that one may rely on this, since d'oraisa money effects acquisition, and they give the money before the rolling; and furthermore, since their way is thus, it is possible that even without giving the money beforehand it is permitted, since the idolater knows the custom of Israel and with this in mind he kneaded from the beginning, so its law is as if they gave it to a Yisrael as a gift before they rolled it. And in Teshuvot of the Mabit he wrote that they do not do well, and the custom should be abolished, end of the words of the Shach. And see above siman 326 seif katan 3, what is written there.
