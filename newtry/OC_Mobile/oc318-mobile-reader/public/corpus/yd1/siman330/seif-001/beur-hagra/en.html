Rosh derives this from the opening clause concerning gentile dough: just as a Jew's kneading does not obligate dough belonging to a gentile in that case, so too ownership remains decisive. At the end of chapter 2 of Peah the Mishnah states that a field harvested by gentiles is exempt, derived by Sifrei from "when you reap," excluding what gentiles reaped. The Yerushalmi there, cited by Rash, limits this to gentiles who harvested for themselves. If they harvested for a Jew, it is obligated. All the more so here, where the exclusion is only from "your kneadings," meaning not those of gentiles. See Menachot 67 and Rosh, responsa klal 2, siman 2.
