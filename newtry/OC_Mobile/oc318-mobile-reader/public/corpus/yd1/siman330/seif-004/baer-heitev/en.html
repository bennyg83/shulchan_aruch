Doubt. The Shach wrote that this is because in a doubt concerning prohibition we rule stringently. One must say this is specifically according to the strict law; but nowadays, when all challah is rabbinic, one is exempt in a case of doubt. It requires study why the later authorities wrote this law plainly. In any event, it appears that wherever there is doubt in a matter of challah, since nowadays it is rabbinic, one should separate without a berachah. End quote.
