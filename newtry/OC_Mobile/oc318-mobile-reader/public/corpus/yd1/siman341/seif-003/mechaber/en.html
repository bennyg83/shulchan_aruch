A place where they practice that designated bearers take out the dead—and after the relatives engaged in burial needs they hand him to them and they bury him—from when they hand him to them the relatives are permitted meat and wine even before they take him out of the house, for he is no longer lying before them. {Rama: A place where they carry the dead from city to city—if the place is near it is as if lying before him; but if a distant place such as two days' walk, permitted until they reach the burial city (Rabbenu Yonah, chapter Mi she-Meto).}
