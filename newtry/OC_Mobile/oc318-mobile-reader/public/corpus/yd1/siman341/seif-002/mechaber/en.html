One to whom a relative died on Shabbat—he eats at motza'ei Shabbat without havdalah and does not pray, not in the morning before burial; and after burial he prays the morning prayer if its time has not passed; but the evening prayer he does not pray for its time has passed; and this is not like one who forgot and did not pray the evening prayer who prays two morning prayers, since at night he was not obligated to pray; and regarding havdalah he makes havdalah after they bury the dead (Maharam, Mordechai, and Hagahot Maimoniyot).
