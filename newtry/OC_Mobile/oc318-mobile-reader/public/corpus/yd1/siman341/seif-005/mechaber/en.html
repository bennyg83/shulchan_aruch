All the time the dead was not buried he does not remove shoe and sandal and is not obligated in wrapping the head and turning over the bed; but forbidden to sit or lie on a bed even inverted. {Rama: And how much more forbidden in use of the bed (Tur in the name of Ramban and Tosafot and Mordechai first chapter of Ketubot in the name of some); and some say forbidden in washing, anointing, joy, greeting, and haircutting and work (Tur); but permitted to go out from his doorway (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 4 in the name of Semak).}
