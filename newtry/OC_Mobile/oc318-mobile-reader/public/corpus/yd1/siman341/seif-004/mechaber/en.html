One who died in captivity and burial was not given—onenut does not apply to the relatives, nor mourning, since they did not despair of burying him; and likewise if the dead's relatives are in captivity, onenut does not apply to them; and likewise one killed on the road, or dragged by a beast, or swept by a river and they did not despair of burying him—neither onenut nor mourning laws apply to the relatives, and they count seven and thirty from the day they despaired of burying him.
