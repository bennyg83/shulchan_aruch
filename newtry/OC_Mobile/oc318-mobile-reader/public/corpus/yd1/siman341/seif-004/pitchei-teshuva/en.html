And he does not recite the blessing of Hamotzi. The manuscript Chammudei Daniel writes that although an onen may eat without a blessing, he must wash his hands both before and after eating. In my humble opinion, this requires study. I later saw that Birkei Yosef, paragraph 5, considers it obvious that an onen must wash, because handwashing was enacted as a safeguard connected to terumah. Since it is a rabbinic decree, there is no doubt that he remains obligated, for an onen is bound by all Torah and rabbinic prohibitions. He cites Mahari Alfandari, who was uncertain, while Responsa Michtam LeDavid agrees that he is obligated. Nevertheless, it appears that the onen should wash without reciting a blessing.
