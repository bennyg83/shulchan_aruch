Maharil wrote in the name of Maharash that if one died on Shemini Atzeret at evening, it is permitted for the mourner to make Kiddush on the following Simchat Torah night, since there is no aninut on Yom Tov. Shach writes that for us who hold that aninut applies on second Yom Tov, likewise at night it is forbidden to make Kiddush. Taz concurs with Maharil; see there.
