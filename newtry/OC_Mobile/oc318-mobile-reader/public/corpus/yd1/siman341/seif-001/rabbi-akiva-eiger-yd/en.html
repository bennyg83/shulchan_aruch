(Siman 341, seif 1) One whose relative died. If a son less than thirty days old dies, the Yerushalmi at the end of the chapter Nosein al HaAnusah implies that the father does not have the status of an onen. See Devar Moshe, siman 83, Bach to siman 353, Birkei Yosef, and Tiferet LeMoshe. The latter remains uncertain and concludes that one should be stringent regarding meat and wine. Regarding Shema and tefillin, the matter requires study. [Citation 50.]
(Shulchan Aruch seif 1) He does not recline. See Orach Chaim (siman 552 seif 7) and Magen Avraham (there seif katan 7).
Not eating meat. Alpha Mandarin is provided if it is permitted in a meat dish and a state in the Constance is thrown out of fire, and by the knees of Joseph:
And he does not welcome the blessing. And his hands are not welcome. From the Alpha Mandarin there:
(There.) And on Shabbat and Yom Tov he eats meat. In the sugya it says: on Shabbat he reclines and eats. See Responsa Bach, vol. 2, siman 5.
(There.) If he wishes. See Responsa VeShav HaKohen, siman 94, who concluded that this applies only to Shabbat, but on Yom Tov he is obligated to eat meat, and at least to drink wine, even on the night of the final Yom Tov; see there.
