(Siman 341, seif 1) One whose relative died. If a son less than thirty days old dies, the Yerushalmi at the end of the chapter Nosein al HaAnusah implies that the father does not have the status of an onen. See Devar Moshe, siman 83, Bach to siman 353, Birkei Yosef, and Tiferet LeMoshe. The latter remains uncertain and concludes that one should be stringent regarding meat and wine. Regarding Shema and tefillin, the matter requires study. [Citation 50.]
