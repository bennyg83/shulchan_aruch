And it contains one seif: If the father did not circumcise his son, beit din is obligated to circumcise him; and if beit din did not circumcise him, he is obligated when he grows up to circumcise himself; and if he did not circumcise, he is liable for karet:
{Rama: And every day he transgresses these prohibitions. (Tur and Beit Yosef.) One does not circumcise a person's son without his consent unless the father transgressed and did not circumcise him — then beit din circumcises him against his will. (Tur in the name of Rambam.) A woman is not obligated to circumcise her son. (Tur.) A father who does not know how to circumcise and there is a mohel who does not wish to circumcise for free but only for payment — beit din should rebuke this mohel, for this is not the way of the seed of Avraham; on the contrary, mohalim are eager that they be given to circumcise. And if he stands in rebellion and the man's hand cannot reach to give him his wages, it is as one who has no father — beit din is obligated to circumcise him, and therefore beit din coerces him since there is no other to circumcise.
