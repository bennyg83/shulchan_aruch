I am. A modern deco with you when I eat:
The expression. Shach writes that Rama's first opinion is missing: an incomplete statement is no vow because it can bear opposing meanings and is not a demonstrative partial formulation. Taz agrees. Bach is more stringent, but this is not the primary Talmudic or halachic view. A statement using oath-language for a vow is formally ineffective, though because people commonly speak this way it should be annulled to prevent disrespect for vows.
