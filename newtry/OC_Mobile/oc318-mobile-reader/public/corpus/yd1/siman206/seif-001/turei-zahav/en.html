It is forbidden to stand within his four amos. It appears that something is missing here, and this is how the gloss should read: “And there are those who say that so long as he did not conclude, ‘that I will not eat,’ or, ‘that I will eat,’ it is nothing.” This is the opinion of the Rosh cited by the Tur. His reasoning is that whenever one says, “I am separated from you,” without concluding the statement, there is room to doubt whether he meant that he would not speak with him or whether he meant to prohibit benefit. Since there is no indication, it is not a valid partial expression of a vow. According to my emendation, what the Rama wrote in seif 3 is readily understood: “It has already been explained that there are those who disagree,” etc., for otherwise it is unclear to what this refers. Therefore, it appears certain as I have emended it.
