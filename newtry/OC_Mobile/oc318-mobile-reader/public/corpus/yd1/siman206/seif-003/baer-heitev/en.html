Ostracized. Shach wrote — the Mechaber's words are doubtful: if menudeh or meshamtina is not vow language, then certainly if he did not complete his words all is permitted; if it is vow language, then even completing "she'ani ochel lecha" is forbidden to eat with him. Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote: "Menudeh ani lecha or meshamtina mimecha — even if he completed and said she'ani ochel lecha it is permitted; nevertheless forbidden to sit within four cubits, even if he said menudeh ani lecha alone or meshamtina mimecha alone," etc. It can be reconciled with difficulty; doubtful — end quote.
Explained. Meaning because he emended in his words as in note 2; nevertheless one can be precise — what is the connection of this to that, for here it is worse; and as Tosafot said in the name of R' Yitzchak that in menudeh and nadina mimcha even if he did not complete his words it is forbidden to benefit, but it appears the Rav followed the Tur's words who equates completely menudeh and nadina to meruchakni; examine; and it appears for practical halachah one should not be lenient — end quote, Shach.
