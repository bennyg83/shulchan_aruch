If he said, “I am excommunicated to you,” or “I am under a ban of excommunication from you,” and he completed his words, “that I will eat with you” — it is not a vow, and he is permitted in everything. And if he did not complete his words, he is forbidden to stand within 4 amos of him. But if he said, “I am under judgment from you,” and completed his words, “that I will not eat with you,” he is forbidden to eat with him. If he did not complete his words, “that I will not eat with you,” he is forbidden to benefit from him. {Rama: And it was already explained that there are those who disagree, and one holds that anything in which he did not say, “that I will not eat with you,” or “that I will eat with you,” is nothing.}
