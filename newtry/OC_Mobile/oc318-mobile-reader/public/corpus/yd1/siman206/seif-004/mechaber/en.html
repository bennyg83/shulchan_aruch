If he said, “Like the vows of wicked people, this loaf is upon me,” or if he said, “Like the vows of wicked people, from this,” and a loaf was placed before him — he is forbidden in it. But if he said, “Like the vows of righteous people, upon me,” or “Like the vows of righteous people, this loaf is upon me,” it is nothing. And even if he said, “Like the vows of righteous people, this loaf is upon me, konam” (meaning: konam is one of the designations of a korban) — it is nothing. But if he said, “Like the donation of righteous people, upon me,” or “Like the donation of righteous people, this loaf is upon me,” it is a vow.
