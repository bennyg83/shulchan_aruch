Like the vows of the wicked. For the way of the wicked is to make vows, whereas the worthy do not ordinarily make vows, though they do ordinarily make voluntary dedications. The reason is that a vow is stated in the language, “It is incumbent upon me,” and the responsibility therefore rests upon the one who vowed. This creates a possibility of failure, for if the item that he designated is lost, he may not give another. This is not so with a voluntary dedication, which is stated in the language, “This item is hereby,” for then he bears no responsibility.
This loaf is a konam upon me. Because what he first said, “like the vows of the worthy,” nullifies what he subsequently said in the language of konam.
