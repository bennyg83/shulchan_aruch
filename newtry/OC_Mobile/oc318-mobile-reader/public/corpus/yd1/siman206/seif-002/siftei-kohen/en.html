And his fellow is permitted, etc. — and if he did not mention eating nor benefit, it is not a vow at all — end of Tur; and he follows his method above. But per Rambam and the Mechaber it is a vow in which he is not permitted to speak with him; and so Bach and Perishah; and it is simple. Therefore the Mechaber omitted the conclusion of Tur's words here; and it is astonishing about Taz who copied Tur's words here anonymously while in seif 1 he copied the Mechaber's words anonymously.
