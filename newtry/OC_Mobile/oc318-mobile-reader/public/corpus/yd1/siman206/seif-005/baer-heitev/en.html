A vow. A vow prohibits the object upon the person, while an oath prohibits the person from the object. Saying, 'I vow not to eat with you,' is oath-language rather than vow-language, yet it is not a valid oath either because he did not use an oath formulation. Shach.
A mitzvah. One who says, 'I vow one quarter of my body,' is understood as vowing to give the value of one quarter of his body to charity, and he is obligated. Responsa Ralnach, siman 3.
