(°) פי' הר"ן דלך משמע לך כלומר נכסי אסורים לך ומשמע משלך: **** ENGLISH **** Rosh there, and likewise Ran; Ramban agrees. But in an unspecified case, yadayim she'einan mochichot are not yadayim — not like Rambam's view in the seif above. Ran explains "to you" — meaning "your property is forbidden to you," and it implies "from your property."
