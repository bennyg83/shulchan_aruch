(In Beit Chadash.) And thus in Rosh's commentary there. He elaborated on this in teshuvot Maharam Alshich (siman 103 and 124); and see Maharitmat (vol. 1, siman 53) who wrote that nezirut is like an oath, for the person is forbidden — only differing from an oath in that in an oath the person is not seized at all, therefore attaching in an oath does not help; but in nezirut the body is seized in holiness, therefore attaching in nezirut helps — see there.
