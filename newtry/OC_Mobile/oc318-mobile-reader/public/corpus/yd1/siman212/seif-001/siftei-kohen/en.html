Am ha'aretz. — and it was already explained above siman 205 that in our time we treat everyone as an am ha'aretz.
For prohibition is written in it, etc. The Raavad explains that a Torah scroll is considered a davar hanadur because korbanot and the Divine Names are written in it, and these have sanctity that comes through consecration. Even detached sheets or portions that contain such matters have this status according to that reasoning. Therefore, when one uses the Torah as the object by which he vows, the vow can take effect because it relates to something in which prohibition and sanctity are written.
