(h) And we will be able to say, “This is the sign of the Lord.”
For behold it is written in it a prohibition, etc. And the Raavad in his hasagot chapter 12 of Mishneh Torah and the Ran chapter 2 of Nedarim wrote the reason — because it contains sacrifices and Divine Names that are like a vowed matter, for through the writing and preparation for holiness they are holy; and accordingly all the more so if he said explicitly "I vow by the Divine Names in it," it is a vow even though his intention is not on a prohibition and vow written in it. And likewise if he vowed on part of the sheets of the Torah, even though prohibition or vow is not written in those sheets, since they contain sacrifices or Divine Names it is a vow.
