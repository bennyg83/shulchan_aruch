BeHaT of Mahari"t z"l in name of Ma'il Tzedakah — see there he disputes Tzemach Tzedek siman 119; Magen Avraham brought it in siman 143 s.k. 6 — if found in place of "their divorces" "their divorce" — need not take out another; and he z"l wrote one must take out another since the error is felt in pronunciation. And see there further in siman 94 — he wrote in a Torah scroll found in verse "and I will take you to another place" was written "go" without hei and with plain kaf — need not take out another. Further in siman 55 in verse "you shall not test the Lord your God" was written "your God" — see there its correction method; and see above siman 275 s.k. 6 further in his name regarding "and all the days of Noah" found written "and they were" — one does not take out another and the reader reads "and it was" — see there. And see responsum Noda BiYehudah second series Yoreh De'ah siman 178 — in a Torah scroll found in verse "behold Esau my brother is a hairy man" with yod — and should be "hair" missing yod — one must take out another. And he wrote further a Torah scroll found in parashat Shemot in verse "for from the water she drew him" wrote "she drew him" — need not take out another — see there. And see responsum Teshuvah MeAhavah part 1 siman 51 — regarding found in order Shemini "and you became impure in them" was written "and you became impure" with alef — if need take out another Torah scroll; he leaned both ways and his view tends more not to take out another — see there. And see responsum Even Shem siman 41. And wrote in responsum Chinuch Beit Yisrael siman 5: the essence does not depend on vav or yod but on change of matter because of the extra letter — example "keves" "keseh" where matter did not change; or in place of "aleh" if written "ali" we do not take out another, and here too matter did not change for we find "ali" as ascent — end quote; and see responsum Meir Netivim siman 13 who disputed him — "ali" in place of "aleh" or vice versa is real change of language, for "ali" is feminine and "aleh" masculine, and need take out another — see there; and likewise challenged in She'elat Ya'avetz part 2 siman 108 — see there; and see further in responsum Chatam Sofer cited siman 4, 5, 6; and see Magen Avraham siman 143 s.k. 7 who wrote in name of Shelah that also in "keves"/"keseh" they take out; and see responsum Basamim Rosh siman 288 in Hagahat Kessef David there what he challenged on this, and wrote even if stringent per his words it applies only if wrote "keseh" in place of "keves," for always the essence name is "keves"; but in reverse one does not take out another — see there. And see responsum Adnei Paz siman 13 — if error found in Shevi'i in such manner that need not take out another Torah scroll — one does not say at hagbahah "and this is the Torah" — see there.
