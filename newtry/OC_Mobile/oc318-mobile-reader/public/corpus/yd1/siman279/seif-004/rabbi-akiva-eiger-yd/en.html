(Ibid., gloss) And when he scrapes. Tashbetz, part 1, siman 149, writes that for any error it is permitted only to scrape, but not to peel. All his reasoning applies only when a Torah scroll is written on whole hide. If one peels the surface, the remaining layer may have the status of duchsustus or parchment, because whole hide is considered gevil only while intact. The result would be a scroll written partly on gevil and partly on parchment, which is invalid even if the latter portion consists of only one word. According to our custom of writing Torah scrolls on parchment, however, this concern does not apply, and peeling is permitted.
