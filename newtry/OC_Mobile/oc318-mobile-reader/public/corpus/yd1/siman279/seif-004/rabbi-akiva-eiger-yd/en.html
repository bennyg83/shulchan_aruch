If there is a repair to delete the boxes and write full:
(Here) and there is a page A. And Durk, who was sincere, but not what he would return and write a whole page. In the name of an extension and the fire:
(There, gloss) And by scraping. Tashbetz, part 1, siman 149, writes that for any error one may correct by scraping but not by peeling. His entire discussion applies where a Sefer Torah is written on gevil, because peeling may leave the remaining layer with the status of duchsustus or parchment rather than gevil, and writing even one word partly on gevil and partly on parchment invalidates. According to our custom of writing a Sefer Torah on parchment, this concern does not apply, and peeling is permitted.
