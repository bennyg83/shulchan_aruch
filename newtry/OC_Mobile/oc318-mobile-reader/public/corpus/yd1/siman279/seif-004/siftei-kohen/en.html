If most of the scroll, etc. Meaning, most of the letters of the scroll have been proofread, while in the remainder there are four errors on each page throughout the scroll, and only one page remains with fewer than four errors. That page should be corrected.
So that the scraped area should not create a break within the word. In such a case the scroll is invalid, because the letters of the word have been separated until the word appears as two words, as explained above in siman 273, seif 4.
