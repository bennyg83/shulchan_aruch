The valid one. Shach wrote: this is when he had not read three verses; but if he read three verses and can stop there — he recites the blessing after it, and they complete the count with the other sefer Torah, as written in Orach Chaim siman 143 seif 4.
The error. And Taz ruled: if the error is found during the reading of the last one called up, he should follow the words of Mordechai — to read the error by heart and complete in that scroll the entire required reading, and not take out another one, due to the honor of the congregation; and even though he will subsequently read for the maftir, nevertheless the honor of the congregation does not apply here, since in any case they pause somewhat for the recitation of Kaddish; and if the error is found during the reading of the maftir, likewise he should not take out another, but rather recite it by heart — end quote.
