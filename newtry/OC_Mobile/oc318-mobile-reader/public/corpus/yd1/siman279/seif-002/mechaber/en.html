If they read from a Sefer Torah in public and an error was found in it—they bring out another Sefer Torah and begin from the place where the error was found, and complete the seven for those who read in error. And if the error was found in the middle of the Torah reading, he finishes his reading in the valid book and recites the blessing after it; and he does not recite the blessing before it, for the blessing he recited before was on the invalid Sefer Torah; and likewise the reading they read in it is valid for them b'dieved. {Rama: And there is no difference whether the error was found in the first Sefer Torah or in the second with which they read the day's obligation for maftir (and see in Orach Chaim siman 143).}
