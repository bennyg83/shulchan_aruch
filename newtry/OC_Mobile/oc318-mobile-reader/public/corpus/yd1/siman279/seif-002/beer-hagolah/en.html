Beit Yosef in the name of his teacher the Mahari ibn Rav, to resolve Rambam's words that he wrote in a responsum that one blesses on a invalid scroll — meaning for public reading which is an enactment of prophets and Ezra; and what he wrote in his composition to the contrary is regarding fulfilling the positive mitzvah to write only one sefer Torah; and since so, one may rely on him post facto; and so Rashbatz wrote in the name of Ran.
