(Supplement) One may not emend, etc. See Tosafot in Bava Batra 134a, beginning with the word "Every," etc., and the statement, "Ri explains," etc. Ramban wrote likewise. He records that many people erased the word "for me" in the passage concerning an etrog because they found the previously cited question of Tosafot difficult. He writes that the Talmud and Jerusalem Talmud explicitly demonstrate that this emendation is worthless, and that conjecturally emending books is a complete transgression for which one deserves excommunication.
