The laws of proofreading a Sefer Torah and not leaving it in error. In it are 8 seifim. A Sefer Torah that is not proofread—it is forbidden to leave it more than thirty days, but one must correct it or put it in genizah. (And the same law applies to other books) (Rashi and Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos and Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 50). And one may not proofread any book by reasoning alone, but only by clear sight that there is an error in it (Rav R' David Cohen, Beit Tov, and ancient cherem).
