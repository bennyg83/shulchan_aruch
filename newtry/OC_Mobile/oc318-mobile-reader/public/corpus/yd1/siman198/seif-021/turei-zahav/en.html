Beit Yosef extended on this — why is there interposition in minority more than majority; and thereby strained to explain deals when some interposing matter on that hanging part; and Mordechai wrote — its majority interposes specifically majority of majority like whole; but its minority does not interpose since stands to separate and already began separating, etc. — end quote; and nevertheless not yet settled why no interposition in minority still stuck when majority separated; and further what is different from hanging limb standing to be cut which is interposition even when majority separated; and Mahari Chagiz gave reason because needs craftsman to cut and I did not know his distinction for after all stands to be cut; and what appears in my humble opinion — certainly no interposition in what still stuck for never exposed there; and as Beit Yosef in his challenge; rather on separated part we say so — if majority remains and minority cracked — crack place is narrow and water cannot enter that crack unlike if majority broke open crack widened and water can enter and is fit for water entry; but in limb and hanging flesh even if majority broke cannot leave hanging — very painful — rather always holds to body as able — therefore not fit for water entry there as above to explain this matter in my humble opinion.
