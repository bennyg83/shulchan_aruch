It interposes. She herself, because she separated — even if no interposing matter was found on her — Darkei Moshe, Taz, Bach and other Acharonim; and not like Beit Yosef's conclusion.
If most of it separated it does not interpose. And it appears that for us who practice requiring a second immersion if she did not cut the nail — the same applies here; or it is possible to distinguish that since by law even if nail remained it does not interpose as long as it is known there was no feces under it, as in the poskim — only they practiced stringency, and that is when the entire nail remained — unlike here; and it is forced; and all the more for the reason that the nail itself impedes.
