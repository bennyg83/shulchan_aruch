Dried mucus. Dried mucus inside the nose is not an interposition in the inner cavity, for the Torah was not given to ministering angels. Water need reach only places that are sometimes exposed, such as the inside of the eye, hidden folds, and creases. One should be careful that no dried mucus remain at the inner edge of the nostril, but material deeper within the nasal cavity plainly does not invalidate immersion. Responsa Menahem Azariah.
