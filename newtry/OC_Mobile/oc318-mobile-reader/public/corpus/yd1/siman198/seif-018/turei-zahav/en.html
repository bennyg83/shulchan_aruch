Excrement under nail, etc. Tur concluded after this — however way of people is not to be particular about this therefore one not particular does not interpose — end quote; and Beit Yosef wrote refers also to dough; and Beit Yosef wondered how he ruled to distinguish when mishnah did not distinguish; and therefore did not bring here in Shulchan Aruch; and Rama in Darkei Moshe explained Tur deals with very minute matter people are not particular about; and as Semag and Semak wrote in name of Rabbeinu Tam — even excrement and dough if so minute minority people not particular about — minority not particular does not interpose — end quote.
Not against flesh interposes. Beit Yosef wrote in name of Semag in name of Rabbeinu Tam — this specifically mud similar to dough sticking greatly like potter's clay; but not other mud and excrement; and know for we learn in Mikvaot these do not interpose — filth on his flesh and excrement under nail — end quote; and did not mention this here in Shulchan Aruch to be so lenient; nevertheless appears to me to resolve what was challenged to Rema in Levush why not careful with this in hand-washing; and per what I wrote it fits well — certainly for hand immersion we rely on Rabbeinu Tam it does not interpose except potter's clay and similar.
