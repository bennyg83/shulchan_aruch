Feces, etc. See Beit Yosef who marvels at Rashba that his words contradict each other, and who distinguished with difficulty between netilah and immersion — see there where he elaborated; and Taz followed him; but in my work I proved no question, for Rashba, Semag, and Semak hold in name of Rabbeinu Tam that non-adhering feces does not interpose at all, and adhering feces interposes not opposite the flesh; and ordinary feces does not adhere; but since adhering feces, similar to dough, interposes — women practiced being stringent to cut their nails at immersion lest there be adhering feces there; but certainly regarding netilah this stringency of cutting nails at every netilah does not apply — I have abbreviated; examine.
