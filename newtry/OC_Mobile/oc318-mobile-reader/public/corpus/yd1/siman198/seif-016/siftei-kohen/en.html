Blood that has begun to congeal, etc. Blood that flowed, etc.; beginning to dry and stick somewhat that when finger inserted a thread is drawn from it; Rashi in Menachot (21a); and implies main distinction is flowed or not but no distinction flowed in flesh or in something else; and so in Gemara there answers this flowed this did not flow see there; and in Behag (88a) I found Rav said; this Sages said moist blood does not interpose; they said only when not cooked; but if cooked by fire whether moist or dry interposes; end quote; and although not found so in our Gemara and from not answering so in Menachot does not imply; nevertheless Behag's words are words of tradition.
