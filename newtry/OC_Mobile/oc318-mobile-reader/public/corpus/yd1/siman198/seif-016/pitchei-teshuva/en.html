Hidden place. See in responsum Noda Biyhudah part Yoreh De'ah siman 64 — woman who had wax ring placed deep in vestibule for healing whether interposes in immersion; and he wrote this is certainly called particular for constantly soiled in niddah days and must remove when she stops for purity to clean and likewise in childbirth days and perhaps also impedes bedikah always in seven clean days and must remove — if so it is matter she is particular about and interposes; however although in Torat Kohanim must be fit for water entry specifically in hidden place they said so but in place called throat need not even fit for water entry; therefore if this woman says it is so deep sun does not reach even at completion of entry then called throat and need not be fit for water entry and need not remove at immersion see there — he ruled so for law not for practice until two more rabbis agree [and in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 152 disagrees with beginning of Noda Biyhudah's words what he wrote called particular because must remove at time of stopping purity and likewise childbirth days — wrote that removal at childbirth is not called particular for that removal is not from concern of particular neither on ring nor body filth but to make room for child; and proof from man's ring without stone not interposing as Magen Avraham siman 161 wrote though removed at tefillin and lulav — only if needed remove at separation of purity this is certainly particular; and what Noda Biyhudah wrote if this woman says so deep, etc. — he added siman 193 even if doubtful and possibly doubt of lack of knowledge expert woman can clarify — nevertheless since danger and great weakness to remove it is called minority and not particular, etc. — may be lenient see there]. And see in Noda Biyhudah second part Yoreh De'ah siman 135 — one rabbi wanted to divide hidden place itself — where water can enter by themselves like mouth if open and nostrils and ear holes except Scripture revealed those need not water entry but still need fit for water entry; but woman's place even outer part since even if she spreads legs water enters only slightly at beginning of place — therefore all inward from where infant sits and is visible need not even fit for water entry; and he rejected his proofs (also from Rama's words seif 43 in hagahah clear not like him) see there he ruled for bedikah of clean days if arrangement that mouth of womb faces cavity of ring and entire mouth of source also faces cavity of ring — then at separation of purity she places cotton within clean linen somewhat thick at least width of cavity of ring and inserts this witness deep through cavity of ring until opposite mouth of source and she has beforehand hefsek taharah until after minchah; and also day 1 she does so and other six days she examines as possible see there at length; and see in responsum Zichron Yosef part 14 siman 10 who also agrees to above Noda Biyhudah; also brought from responsum Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 69 who explained for minority particular we say safek derabbanan lekula (requires study now for in established prohibition we do not say safek derabbanan as above siman 110 laws of stains letter 20 and here established prohibition of niddah tumah — must say he holds like Peri Chadash I brought there and I already wrote there halachah is not so and now safek hefsek log before me) and further because woman said cannot take ring from there due to danger of life except at childbirth pains necessarily takes lest fetus be blocked — therefore called not particular (see what I wrote siman 1 in his name); and for bedikah he ruled she can examine herself like other women with cloth inserting to middle of vestibule near where sun treads and leave there entire twilight at separation for purity and likewise one of counting days morning or evening and also examine left side of womb and though right side and ring place cannot examine — no matter only do what possible to examine all places reachable and other days light bedikah suffices see there at length; and see in responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 60 who greatly extended on this matter and agreed to leniency for interposition and also Gaon of Lissa agreed to permission; however in what he was lenient in Zichron Yosef responsum for bedikah he disagrees since many of our rabbis who are stringent require specifically examination of holes and cracks and implies not examination at all without hidden places; however one thing — there is room to debate and be lenient — one may say what requires examination of hidden places is since per majority cannot insert witness deep until place sun actually treads as Beit Yosef wrote and even at completion of setting sun not exactly at source — concerned zav goes sideways to hidden places and does not meet witness — therefore remedy for our sister to examine with tube (thereby can push cloth more) and cloth inside and push cloth much until enters inward from where sun treads near actual am — this is proof if source zav would drip on cloth and does not require examination of hidden places — so will be bedikah of stopping purity and day 1 of seven clean days; and he concluded may be lenient by above bedikah if great ones of generation agree and even if very lenient only bedikah of stopping purity as above and rely on Ramban that bedikah of seven clean days does not require hidden places — also agrees see there; and see in responsum Bashamayim Rosh siman 156 in hagahah of Kessef David what he extended on this both for bedikah and interposition see there; and see further in She'eilot Uteshuvot Teshuvah Me'ahavah part 1 siman 36 through siman 42 [and in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 192 and 193] what he wrote on this.
