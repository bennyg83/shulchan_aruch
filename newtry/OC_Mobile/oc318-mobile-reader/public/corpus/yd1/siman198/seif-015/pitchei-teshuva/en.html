And likewise law of bandage. See in responsum Shivat Tziyon siman 42 — woman with eye condition and expert doctor commanded no water at all on eyes even outside eyes lest she lose eyesight — how should she conduct chafifah and immersion; questioner thought to provide loose bandage on eyes at immersion time; and although Bach wrote in name of Sha"d regarding ornaments and earrings we decree l'chatchila loose because of not loose and so Rama above seif 1 in hagahah specifically ornaments and earrings always on her, etc. — Rav author does not agree to this remedy; rather even one who holds ornaments and earrings we do not decree because of not loose — there is no benefit to woman if immerses tight; unlike here where main purpose of bandage is to guard water from eyes — concern she will tighten from fear; but there is another remedy — she places hands on eyes and rinses hands first until wet enough to wet another as below seif 28; but placing hands should not be by woman herself for if she presses hands upward wrinkles form and flesh in joint of hand and arm becomes covered as above seif 35 immersion must be as she walks — only another woman descends with woman into mikveh and stands behind her at slight distance not touching body of immersing woman and rinses hands in water and covers eyes of this woman loosely and she immerses; and for chafifah he ruled leniently — sufficient she wash and scrub entire body and head except eyes and suffices careful looking in mirror or examination by another woman that there be no eye-mucus and eyelashes not stuck see there; and see in responsum Maharam Ziskind of Rothenburg siman 18.
