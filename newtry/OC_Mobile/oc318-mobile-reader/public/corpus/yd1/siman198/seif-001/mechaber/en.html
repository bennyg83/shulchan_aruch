The laws of tevilah and its chatzitzah; it contains 48 se’ifim. She needs to immerse her entire body at one time; therefore, there must not be on her anything that interposes, even the slightest thing. And if it is the way of people sometimes to be particular about it, it interposes even if she is not particular about it now, or even if she is never particular about it — since it is the way of most people to be particular about it, it interposes. And if it covers most of the body, even if it is not the way of people to be particular about this, it interposes.
{Rama: And l’chatchila she should not immerse even with things that do not interpose, as a decree because of things that do interpose (Hagahos Sha”d).}
