Sometimes. Zikhron Yosef explains that an item interposes only where she is regularly particular about removing it, such as a tight ring removed whenever kneading dough. Removing it only once after a long interval does not suffice. 'She is not particular now' means only at the moment of immersion, though she is particular on surrounding days.
