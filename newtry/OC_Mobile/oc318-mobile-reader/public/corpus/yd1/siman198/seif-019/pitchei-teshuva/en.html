Planks. See in Baer Heitev in name of Maharam of Lublin; and in Taz who wrote where stones not found possible to fix with planks each not so wide fit for foot treading except by joining many planks then not fit for treading before joining to mikveh, etc. see there further. (And see in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 201 regarding mikveh floor of brick building they placed thick planks so water not seep between bricks and planks interwoven woman to woman by iron and teacher commanded making holes like spout of skin-bottle to fulfill words in Shulchan Aruch and Maharam Lublin — and he wrote to him not correct and if wants stringency of Maharam Lublin what did holes help — are they not fit for treading for holes help nullify vessel-receiving category but not treading category; rather now worse since on stone floor holes made for receiving and are actual vessel (in my humble opinion not understood per his own words siman 206 brought somewhat below siman 201 siman 18 see there); and further concern water entering holes drawn below between planks and bricks perhaps make crawling and disqualify entire mikveh by law as Shulchan Aruch siman 201 seif 50 (also not fully understood see Shulchan Aruch there seif 51 and what he wrote there in his name siman 211 and possibly resolved); and further I wonder if wants stringency of Maharam Lublin how fix with iron which receives tumah and thereby wood also receives tumah because iron supports them, etc. (also difficult per his own words siman 206 brought somewhat below siman 201 siman 28) — therefore appears certainly one wanting stringency should make stone floor without planks at all and lenient did not lose even with irons since made initially for building; but at least should seal holes commanded because of crawling concern which is Torah concern end quote see there); and see in responsum Noda Biyhudah second part Yoreh De'ah siman 138 — mikveh deep and women afraid to descend to immerse — they took chair and placed in mikveh tied with ropes to stand on chair while immersing — one should rebuke them; and although per one reasoning Taz relied for permission of planks one can find permission here too — nevertheless do not add for there are several reasoning to permit unlike bench explicit in mishnah chapter 8 of Mikvaot forbidden — do not rely on external reasoning to permit see there.
