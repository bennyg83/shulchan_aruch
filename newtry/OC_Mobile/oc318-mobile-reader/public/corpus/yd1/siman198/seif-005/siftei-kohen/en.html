And there is no distinction, etc. That is to say, whether two hairs are tied with two other hairs or two hairs tied on their own — in any case they do not interpose. And likewise one hair that was knotted — there is no distinction whether it is tied with its companion one to one or tied on its own — in any case it interposes; as in Beit Yosef in name of Rashba and Ran; and see Bach who elaborated.
And this is when she is particular, etc. And likewise if the way of most people is to be particular — even though she is not particular — as above seif 1; and so analogized in Beit Yosef; and this is simple.
