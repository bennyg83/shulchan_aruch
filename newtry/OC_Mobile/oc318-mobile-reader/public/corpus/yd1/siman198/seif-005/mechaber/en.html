Two hairs or more that were tied together in one knot do not interpose. {Rama: And there is no difference between whether she tied two hairs with two hairs, or she tied two hairs by themselves (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba and Ran).} But one hair that was tied interposes, provided that she is particular about it; but if she is not particular about it, the immersion counted for her, until most of her hair is tied strand by strand individually.
