See in responsum Avnei Nezer part 2 siman 147 who wrote here it deals even if majority of hairs stuck; therefore Mordechai needed another reason and did not write reason because not particular; and he wrote further regarding woman who became ill and doctors said no remedy unless make merlakin and impossible unless powders like flour scattered on head whereby hairs tangle and when rinsed in water those powders cannot let hairs tangle and illness returns — doubtful if she can immerse so for first two Mordechai reasons apply only to those braids made from hairs themselves not this made by other thing; and also last two reasons doubtful in case before us — he ruled not to be lenient unless majority of her hairs remained not tangled then it is minority not particular and rabbinically we rely on expert doctor saying healing through this and it is rabbinic extension; but to do entire head or majority of her hair there is doubt of Torah prohibition in our hands to permit see there. [And see in Taz siman 19 they also brought and wrote his words correct for law and conclude in any case with all hairs or majority of her hairs certainly forbidden l'chatchila by head covering and powders and if she did so it interposes in immersion see there; and see in responsum Chasam Sofer part 6 siman 82 regarding woman wanting to stick hairs with wax for healing — plainly interposes and therefore they did not debate in responsum Penei Meivin and Taz rather by powders and head covering which themselves do not interpose only sticking of hairs interposes and on this they debated whether rabbinic extension; nevertheless they agreed to prohibition; but by wax wax itself interposes and cannot say rabbinic extension — plainly interposes and Heaven forbid to be lenient at all end quote].
