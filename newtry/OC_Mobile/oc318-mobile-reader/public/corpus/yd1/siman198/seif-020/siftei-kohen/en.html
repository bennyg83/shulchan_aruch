She needs another immersion. In truth this stringency is not found in any posek; and on contrary from mishnah and many poskim and Taz appears clear whatever nail is clean certainly does not interpose; only Hagahot Sha"d wrote good to be stringent and immerse again because impossible there not be mud inside; and therefore Rav wrote they practice stringency; and Maharam of Lublin wrote responsum siman 81 — since this is not from law but stringency appears they were stringent only when found she forgot one nail immediately after immersion before sleeping with husband; but if not found until tomorrow not proper to be stringent lest cast aspersion on her relations and even if did not have relations that night matter unseemly and needs no other immersion if no filth found under it — end quote; however in Raavad siman 326 page 60d I found she must cut fingernails and toenails for since she will take them they interpose now — end quote; and this resembles what Rav wrote and since already practiced taking nails, etc. needs other immersion, etc. — implies reason is not because impossible mud remained under nail but nail itself interposes; and so Bach seif 19 — appears even if certain no mud at all should be stringent she immerse again for there is opinion if nail stands to be cut it interposes — end quote; and if so doubtful in this ruling Maharam of Lublin ruled; therefore appears where she can return and immerse even if not found until tomorrow she should return and immerse and it seems to me so is practiced to rule; but where impossible do not be stringent since night passed; and see above siman 197 siman 1.
