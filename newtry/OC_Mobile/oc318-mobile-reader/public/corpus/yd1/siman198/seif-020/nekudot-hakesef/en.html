Also what he ruled on Shabbat she immerses again and is forbidden to cut nail and concluded "I saw one who erred and commanded cutting on Yom Tov through gentile maidservant," etc. — he ruled well and as I will explain. For what he wrote that since assisting is as if doing melachah forbidden on Shabbat and Yom Tov like circumciser above siman 181 — he erred in two ways. One — even cutting with utensil he himself is exempt here Torah prohibition per most poskim and all the more taking in hand per all there is no Torah prohibition as I will explain; and further here assisting does not apply for only in circumcision where one circumcises and one is circumcised is implied as Rashi explained in These are lashed 84b — we learn Scripture thus "do not circumcise" do not let circumcise — or from plural language after Divine warning circumciser and circumcised until here his language; but in other warnings cannot say liable for assisting for assisting has no substance; and so explicitly in chapter HaMetzni'a (Shabbat 93) and chapter 2 of Beitzah 22 — assisting has no substance for Shabbat and permitted even l'chatchila and agreed by all poskim see there; and if so since it implies from poskim in any case there is mitzvah in taking nails as I wrote and telling gentile is rabbinic prohibition and in place of mitzvah they did not decree, etc. — all the more per Raavad and his colleagues I brought in Siftei Kohen siman 25 that nail prevents by law plainly it is place of mitzvah and permitted to tell gentile for even if he himself takes nails there is no Torah prohibition for in chapter HaMetzni'a we say per all one who takes nails for another there is only rabbinic prohibition without utensil and per Rabbis even for himself only rabbinic prohibition; and so wrote poskim and as explained in Tur and Shulchan Aruch Orach Chayyim siman 328 seif 41; and what they wrote there if did not specify majority by hand exempt but forbidden with utensil liable for chatat — likewise did not specify at all by hand exempt as evident in Sha"s except because they stated first "specified majority permitted l'chatchila" they stated also at end "did not specify majority" — or come to teach even if specified partially there is still prohibition and with utensil liable for chatat;
