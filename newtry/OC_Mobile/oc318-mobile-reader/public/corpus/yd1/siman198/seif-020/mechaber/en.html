Specifically dough beneath the nail interposes, but the nail itself does not interpose; and even if it was large and was about to be cut, and it grows and extends beyond opposite the flesh, it does not interpose. {Rama: Nevertheless, all this is specifically when there is no feces or dough beneath it at the time that she immersed. And since they already practiced to cut the nails, even if one nail remained on her hand and she immersed, she needs another immersion (Hagahos Sha”d), and so is the practice.}
