(Taz, seif katan 27) According to Rambam and Ramban I proved. In my humble opinion, this is not proven. One may say that it is common for a person not to loosen it, out of concern that it will fall off, but it is not common to fasten it very tightly, because even a medium attachment prevents it from immediately falling from his hand. Therefore, the Sages did not decree lest he tighten it forcefully.
