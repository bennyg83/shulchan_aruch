And since already practiced taking, etc. In hagahah of Sha'arei Dura wrote in this language — and if she forgot and did not take nails before immersion nail does not interpose provided no mud inside; nevertheless good to be stringent and immerse second time because impossible no mud inside — end quote; and Rashal copied from Maharam's responsum on this that immersion counted all she checks under nails before immersion and no mud and excrement; and Mahari Chagiz wrote appears to him even if certain no excrement she should immerse again for there is opinion if nail stands to be cut it interposes, etc.; and his words astounding for all poskim wrote here only common practice to take nails and not from law; and appears all we find she needs second immersion for forgetting nail — only while she did not have relations with husband before remembering; but if after relations do not be stringent requiring second immersion since only stringency in world; and even if unknown no mud and excrement — implied from Hagahot Sha"d and Maharam's responsum above she needs second immersion by law only while did not have relations with husband but not afterward for even if excrement were there Scripturally does not interpose for majority and particular required — only rabbinic decree on minority particular as beginning of siman; if so here rabbinic doubt; and all the more per seif 18 in name of Semag and Rabbeinu Tam — specifically sticking mud required though not ruled in Shulchan Aruch for law; nevertheless here she already had relations and possibly conceived — if you come to be stringent requiring second immersion you cast aspersion on that child as we find first chapter of Gittin in this language "if you come to be stringent you cast aspersion on her children" — so appears clear in my humble opinion; and again I was told great one answered in responsum as I wrote; and since clarified above taking woman's nails is only from custom they were stringent on themselves — appears what is in Mordechai Hilchos Niddah and Hagahot Sha'arei Dura — if immersion occurred on chol hamoed proper that gentile maidservant shave them for telling gentile is rabbinic prohibition and in place of mitzvah they did not decree; and if no gentile maidservant scrape well under nail — end quote — specifically chol hamoed; but Shabbat and Yom Tov if forgot to take nails she should not tell gentile maidservant to cut for since no absolute mitzvah in taking nails for possible scraping under them alone — why nullify rabbinic Shabbat prohibition of telling gentile; and further it is stringency coming to leniency for even though gentile maidservant cuts nevertheless that Israelite woman assists by lowering hand as if she does this work forbidden Scripturally on Shabbat and Yom Tov like case we find siman 181 regarding circumcision border — circumciser also liable if assisted by leaning toward him to circumcise, etc.; and impossible to guard from this when letting gentile maidservant cut nails without Israelite lowering hand to her; and one may say not so that she should not immerse when this happens Shabbat and Yom Tov — for also on chol hamoed they permitted by scraping nails — why reduce Shabbat and Yom Tov from where no gentile maidservant on chol hamoed; rather clear matter on Shabbat and Yom Tov do not cut through gentile maidservant but scrape well and immerse; and Mordechai did not be stringent to cut through gentile maidservant except on chol hamoed where they were lenient even for her to cut herself; and so we hold Orach Chayyim siman 532 obligatory immersion per all as Rama wrote there; except Mordechai disciple of Maharam who was stringent forbidden to cut nails on chol hamoed except through gentile maidservant as Beit Yosef wrote there — therefore needed cutting through gentile maidservant per his reasoning; and per what we hold siman 532 there to permit cutting chol hamoed without change and Shabbat and Yom Tov do not cut at all but scrape well — so appears clear; and I saw one who erred and commanded cutting on Yom Tov through gentile maidservant and did not intend correctly per law.
