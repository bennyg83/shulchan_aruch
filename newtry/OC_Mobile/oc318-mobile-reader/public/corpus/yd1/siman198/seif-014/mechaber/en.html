Greek mud, potters’ mud, and road-mud that is always found there, even in the summer days — all these interpose. But all other mud: when it is moist it does not interpose, for it dissolves in water; and when it is dry it interposes (Rambam and Sefer Mitzvos Gadol). (Nevertheless, if she is particular even about a moist thing, it interposes) (Rokeach and Mordechai, Hilchos Niddah).
