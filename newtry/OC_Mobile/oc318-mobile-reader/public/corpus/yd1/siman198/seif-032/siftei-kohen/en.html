If it has no rim, etc. Tur did not mention this condition; because he holds Rosh in responsum permitted from two reasons; one we do not say regarding spring that passed over bench for there it deals spring passed to mikveh over bench and Rabbi Shimon explained they decreed on fixed vessels, etc. until this decree does not apply here; and further plain spouts have no rims, etc.; end quote; and Tur holds first answer primary as language implies and second answer wrote only for benefit of matter and therefore even if they have rims permitted; however this is specifically according to Rabbi Shimon and Rosh view; but Raavad, Rashba, and Ran who forbade even detached vessels placed for mikveh; if so not permitted here from first reason; and therefore Mechaber permitted because no rims; examine carefully.
