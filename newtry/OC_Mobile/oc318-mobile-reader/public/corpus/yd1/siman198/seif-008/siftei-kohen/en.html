Kohl, etc. Language of Behag page 85 top of b: when she immerses she should not anoint with oil and should not apply kohl to her eyes — for Shmuel said: applying kohl — if for healing it interposes; if so her eyes appear improved it does not interpose. And the Sages taught: kohl outside the eye interposes. R. Yehudah said all oils do not interpose except oil of myrrh; and the daughters of Israel took it upon themselves not to anoint with oil during immersion at all — end quote.
