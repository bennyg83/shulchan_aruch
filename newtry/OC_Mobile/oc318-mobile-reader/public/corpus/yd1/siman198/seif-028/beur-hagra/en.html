Unless she loosened, etc.; and if, etc. This follows the first Tanna. See the glosses of the Gra. The Torat HaBayit writes this way, but Ramban writes the reverse, that the first Tanna is stringent, etc. He does not have the version implied by Yevamot 46a, 'they loosened it for him,' etc., and by the Tosefta regarding necklaces, etc., as stated above in seif 23. Rather, according to his version, Rabbi Shimon is the stringent opinion, etc.
