It states in mishnah chapter 8 of Mikvaot — one who holds person and vessels and immerses them — impure; and if he rinsed hands in water — pure because moisture on hands connects to mikveh water and no interposition; R. Shimon says he should loosen hands so water enters; Rambam and Ramban explained Tanna Kamma is stricter than R. Shimon — for R. Shimon loose hands suffice and Tanna Kamma insufficient — therefore ruled need rinse hands first specifically; and I already wrote siman 120 seif 2 can rinse hands even in drawn water unlike Rama there; and per their explanation all who rinses hands first need not loosen hands at all even if tightens with force on person and vessel immersed — no concern since already soiled; for if not say so what helps rinsing hands — also after rinsing we decree perhaps will tighten with force; and further proof Rashba in long Teshuvot HaRashba challenged Rambam and Ramban explanation — in many places we do not decree like ornaments and earrings Tur mentioned nearby permitted with loosening; and Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit resolved specifically holding person and vessels to immerse — concern perhaps will hold with force lest fall; but elsewhere why decree gratuitously — if so we see if rinsed hands first no concern afterward even if tightens with force; and Rashba explained R. Shimon speaks stringently — Tanna Kamma holds rinsing alone suffices and R. Shimon holds since accustomed to press hands on person and vessel he immerses — no connection even in moisture touching; and one may say for feet of immerser on mikveh floor mentioned below seif 30 — there water precedes feet and still moisture touching connects to mikveh; but here R. Shimon holds way of holder of vessels to press hands on them lest fall and likewise holds companion and prevents moisture on hands being connection at all; and Tanna Kamma not concerned at all and moisture touching is connection; and halachah like Tanna Kamma — need not loosen; but if loosens plainly helps; and concludes even for Tanna Kamma nevertheless must not press much with force when holding to immerse — so written in Teshuvot HaRashba and Mishmeret HaBayit Rashba himself compiled and Tur agreed to Rashba; and therefore ruled here in Shulchan Aruch like them — suffices whether loosening or rinsing hands first; and Mahari Chagiz wondered why ruled not like Rambam and Ramban — no wonder for worthy to rely on Rashba and Tur especially rabbinic matter Scripturally interposition only majority and particular. And there is further dispute between Rambam and Ramban versus Rashba — for Rambam and Ramban I proved nearby even if holds with force does not interpose all who rinsed hands first; and necessarily weight of vessel must be — here reason "because water precedes" not specifically mikveh water but other water too; and so Teshuvot HaRashba explicitly as Beit Yosef brought — "because water precedes and further there is moisture touching," etc.; and therefore Tur did not write only since moist and did not state since mikveh water precedes her for he holds here like Rambam and Ramban we mentioned.
