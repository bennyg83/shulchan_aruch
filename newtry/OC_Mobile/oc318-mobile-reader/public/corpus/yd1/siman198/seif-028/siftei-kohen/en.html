And if she rinsed, etc. Derishah wrote: from here there is complete permission that when the woman immerses and her companion pushed her into the water who can hold her by hand during the pushing — if she rinsed her hands in water first she need not separate her hands from her; and not like the practice of our women who separate their hands from her during the pushing — end quote. In my humble opinion one should not rule leniently in matters people have practiced as forbidden — for in the mishnah and poskim it is only bediavad permitted but lechatchila who says; and further in many matters they are stringent for niddah beyond the law, even bediavad — all the more here; and further R. Yisrael Brin disagrees and forbids here even if she rinsed her hands; and even Mas’at Binyamin who disagrees with him may agree lechatchila. However regarding a woman who cannot stand on her feet, Mas’at Binyamin there in responsum siman 81 ruled that she should be immersed by two women holding her by the arms and rinsing their hands first in water until it is wet enough to transfer wetness; but there is no remedy to lay her on a sheet and immerse her, for a sheet receives tumah and is forbidden (as below seif 31) — end quote; see there.
In the water, etc. Specifically in mikveh water, but not in drawn water, as above siman 120 seif 2 in the gloss; and so implied in Mordechai end of 71a.
Moist. Enough to transfer wetness — Perishah and Bach; and so in Rashba.
