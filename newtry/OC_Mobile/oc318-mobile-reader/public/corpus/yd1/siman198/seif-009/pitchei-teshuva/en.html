Within three days. Chatam Sofer reasons that full twenty-four-hour periods are required, not partial days. Since the rule of a minority one is particular about is rabbinic, one who is lenient has support, though stringency is praiseworthy.
A woman with scabs. The incident where a woman stood unclothed before a rabbi is explained as occurring after immersion. A firmly attached scab that is painful to remove need not be presumed to have fallen during immersion.
