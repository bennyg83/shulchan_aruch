A woman afflicted with sores, etc. The Beit Yosef explains in the name of the Mordechai and the Rosh that the scab of a bloodletting incision, which bloodletters ordinarily cause to form, has the same law as a scab upon a wound after three days. Within three days, however, it does not constitute a chatzitzah at all. The Maharshal cites a gloss stating: "A scab must be removed for immersion even though it causes her great pain. The wife of Maharar K. had to stand naked before him so that he could see that no scab remained upon her, for he did not trust that she would remove it, since she was young and it caused her pain. The Sar of Coucy did likewise for his wife." End quote.
