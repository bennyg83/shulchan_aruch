Loose. Authorities dispute whether a loose ring invalidates after the fact. Bach strongly rejects such invalidation, except that initial removal is proper in items burdensome to remove. Sidrei Taharah likewise treats the issue as initial stringency, not retrospective invalidity.
And if they are tight. Some suggest that a woman who never kneads dough is not particular about a tight ring. Nevertheless the Shulchan Aruch does not distinguish, so one should not be lenient initially, though after the fact and after relations there may be room.
The same applies to a bandage. Shivat Tzion rejects covering diseased eyes with a loose bandage during immersion, because fear may lead her to tighten it. Instead, another woman should wet her hands, stand behind without touching her body, and loosely cover the eyes during immersion. Chafifah may omit the eyes if they are carefully inspected for discharge and stuck lashes.
