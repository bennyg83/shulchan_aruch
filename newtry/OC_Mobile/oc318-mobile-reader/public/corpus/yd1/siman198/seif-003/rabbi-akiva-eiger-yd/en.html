I do not know what compelled him. Beit Yosef's words are difficult. Since Rabbeinu Yerucham holds that the statement does not refer to eye mucus, his ruling concerning dried material outside the eye must follow Rosh, who interprets Mar Ukva's statement there. Mar Ukva's novelty was precisely that outside the eye only dried material interposes. It is therefore unclear how Beit Yosef could say this applies only to purity matters.
