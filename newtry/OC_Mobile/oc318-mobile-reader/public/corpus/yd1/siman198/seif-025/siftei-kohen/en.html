The immersion counted for her and is not comparable, etc. Taz and Ma'adanei Melech 315a wrote: the reason — in hidden places, since there is room to remain there and adhere in the folds of the body, we say certainly it was not there — for otherwise why would it fall after immersion — end quote; and they strained needlessly, for that is why it is not comparable to body examination and head chafifah, where water entry is required and we attribute stringently; but here water entry is not required (only that it be fit for water entry), therefore we attribute leniently, as below seif 26; and so Raavad; and so explained in Tur and Shulchan Aruch below siman 199 seif 9.
