Such as dog or donkey or am ha'aretz or gentile or pig or horse or metzora and the like — so Hagahos Sha"d; but in Rokeach and Kol Bo it states if a horse encounters her she conceives and has relations, her sons stand well in speech, hear and understand, learn Torah and do not forget, diminish sleep, and further their fear falls on creatures, etc. — end quote; and we learn in Pesachim — one who encounters a woman at time she ascends from obligatory immersion — if he preceded and had relations, a spirit of promiscuity for him; if she preceded and had relations, a spirit of promiscuity for her — what is her remedy? She should say thus: "He pours contempt upon nobles" (Tehillim 107) — and Rokeach brings it so; but in Toledot Aharon he did not cite verse "He pours contempt" but "and loosens the belt of the strong" (Iyov 12) — appears he did not have text in Sha"s "He pours contempt," etc.; and in Yalkut Sha"s this is brought in Tehillim and Iyov — therefore we teach both — end quote Siftei Kohen.
