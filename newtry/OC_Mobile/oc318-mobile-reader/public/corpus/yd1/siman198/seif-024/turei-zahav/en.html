She must clean between her teeth, etc. Although water need not actually enter there, for we expound, "all his flesh," just as his flesh is external, so too only every external part is included, nevertheless the place must be fit for water to enter. For with anything fit for mixing, actual mixing is not indispensable.
And it is a proper custom. It is obvious that if she did not observe this custom, immersion should not be delayed on its account. On Shabbos and Yom Tov the custom is to eat meat, but she must be especially careful to clean between her teeth afterward.
