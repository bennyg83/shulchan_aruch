She needs to clean between her teeth so that there is not in them something that interposes; for if she immersed and something was found stuck in them, the immersion did not count for her. And there are those who practice not to eat meat on the day they go to the place of immersion, because it enters between the teeth more than other food; and even though they check and clean between the teeth, they are concerned lest something remain from it and it is not in her mind — and it is a fine custom. {Rama: And she should not eat between the washing and the immersion (Rosh in Sefer Ba’al HaNefesh). And she should not occupy herself the entire day before immersion with dough or with wax candles, lest it stick to her (Sha”d), and so is the practice.}
