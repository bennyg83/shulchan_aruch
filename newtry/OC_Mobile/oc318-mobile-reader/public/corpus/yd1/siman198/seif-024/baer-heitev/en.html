Immersed. Bach infers that cleaning between the teeth is not an absolute obligation but advice for her own benefit, so that nothing later found there invalidates the immersion. Shach disagrees and rules that she is obligated to clean between her teeth, lest she forget to examine afterward.
And the custom. Taz writes that failure to observe this custom does not delay immersion. On Shabbat and Yom Tov, women eat meat but should clean their teeth especially well afterward.
