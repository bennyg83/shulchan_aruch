“You won’t fall like me.” B.C.:
And if she immersed there is, etc. According to our version of the Tosefta, beginning of ch. 8: if his feet were full of dust and he descended and immersed in a mikveh that has forty se'ah — pure; if he scrubbed or if he fell into hot water — impure. But this is puzzling, for the opposite should be the question; and so too it says in the final clause: a flask that was full of coals and he immersed it — impure; if he scrubbed them or immersed it in hot water — pure. And so it should be stated in the opening clause; and so too copied Rishon le-Zion in ch. 10 Mishnah 4; and so too he compares them both in the Mishnah.
