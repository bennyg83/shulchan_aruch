A plaster, a poultice, and a bandage on concealed places interpose. Even though they do not need the water to enter them, they must be fit and there must not be in them something that interposes. {Rama: Some say that a woman needs to urinate before immersion if she needs to; she also needs to check herself for stool and for urine, so that she will not need to restrain herself and they will not be fit for the water to enter. She also needs to remove the filth of the nose (Sha”d and Raav”an siman 326, daf 60a).}
