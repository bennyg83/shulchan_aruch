(Addendum) Some say, etc. As stated on Niddah 43a, Rava asked concerning a zavah whose discharge was uprooted, etc. The Shaarei Dura cites this. The gloss there writes that no proof may be brought from that passage, because the question there concerns the bodily fluids of a zavah and the laws of purity. If the issue were that she must restrain herself and the place is therefore not fit for water to enter, there would be no proof from there, but only from the case of one who compresses her lips. Nevertheless, his words imply that he agrees with the practical ruling. This requires study, since the Gemara cited above implies the opposite, that the concern applies specifically to purity. (End of addendum.)
