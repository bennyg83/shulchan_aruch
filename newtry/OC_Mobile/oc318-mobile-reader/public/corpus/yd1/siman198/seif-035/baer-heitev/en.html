Planks. Taz wrote — and one should greatly wonder at the custom of our country making planks in the mikveh for the immersing woman to stand on; Levush greatly extended this wonder; and Maharam of Padua preceded him in responsum when Maharar David Vitel asked him why they do so and he answered at length — main point of our construction is that our planks in the mikveh are not considered designated for treading since they are not made to stand on them to immerse, for on the contrary thereby they leave the category of vessel and are considered like roof of house, especially after fixed with nails since initially there was no vessel on them — end quote; and further it appears one can find permission even when not fixed with nails, etc. see there he extends on this and concludes — and all I wrote regarding permission of planks applies also to wooden beams in mikveh found in all our country that one memory rises here and there — end quote; and Maharam of Lublin wrote in responsum — certainly one should not change the custom lest he cast aspersion on the early ones; nevertheless whenever it happens before me they repair or build a new mikveh I always command fixing it with things not fit for treading so it be valid per all — end quote.
