But bends, etc. For in all these, the Sages calculated that when she does so water can reach all visible places of her body that require water entry; and other places are called hidden places that do not require water entry, but we require that they be fit for water entry.
Her immersion counts in it. This requires study, for below in seif 38 the Mechaber rules that if she closed her eyes excessively, her immersion does not count. It depends on the correct textual reading: if the reading there is that she closed her eyes very tightly, then here, where she did not close them in that way, her immersion counts; but if the reading there is simply that she closed her eyes, the rulings are difficult to reconcile. This requires study.
