(Addendum) She should not immerse, etc. This is the wording of the Torat HaBayit and follows its approach. See below, seif katan 44. The Raavad understands that Rabbi Eliezer's statement comes to be stringent and supports Rabbi Yochanan. (End of addendum.)
At the time, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'and a nursing woman,' etc. The Rash also writes this in his commentary to the Mishnah there, as do the other halachic authorities, and they rule like the first Tanna.
And she need not, etc. The Rosh explains the phrase, 'in the manner of her growth,' this way. See Rashi there, s.v. 'she should not immerse,' etc.
(Addendum) And she need not distance, etc. This is the wording of the Rosh and follows his approach, that Rabbi Eliezer came to be lenient and to reject the preceding statements, as explained above in the addendum to seif katan 44. Rashi, however, explains the statement stringently, following his version that reads, 'the halachah does not follow,' etc. In practice, there is no difference between these explanations. (End of addendum.)
