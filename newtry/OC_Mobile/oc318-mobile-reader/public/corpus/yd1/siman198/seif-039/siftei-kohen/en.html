Some say, etc. In Ma'adanei Melech 318a wondered what is different compressing mouth that anonymously ruled immersion did not count — no difficulty for even disputant agrees compressing lips is mishnah as in all poskim; and distinction Ran wrote — what is different compressing lips that mishnah teaches as if she did not immerse — one may say when squeezing eyes wrinkles so much they prevent water entry — end quote; and Beit Yosef brings above regarding scab on wound 221b; and so Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit 129a — squeezing eyes excessively or opening excessively no concern except compressing lips alone — end quote; and so explained in other poskim.
