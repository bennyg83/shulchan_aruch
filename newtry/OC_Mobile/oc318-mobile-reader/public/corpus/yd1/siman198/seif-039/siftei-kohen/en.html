Some authorities say, etc. In Ma'adanei Melech 318a wondered what is different compressing mouth that anonymously ruled immersion did not count; no difficulty for even disputant agrees compressing lips is Mishnah as in all authorities; and distinction Ran wrote; what is different compressing lips that Mishnah teaches as if she did not immerse; one may say when squeezing eyes wrinkles so much they prevent water entry; end quote; and Beit Yosef brings above regarding scab on wound 221b; and so Ra'ah in Bedek HaBayit 129a; squeezing eyes excessively or opening excessively no concern except compressing lips alone; end quote; and so explained in other authorities.
