One should place behind her a Jewish woman older than 12 years and one day at the time that she immerses, to see that no hair of her head remains floating on the surface of the water. But if she has no one to stand behind her, or if it is at night, she should wrap her hair upon her head with wool threads, or with a strap on her head, provided she loosens them; or with hollow chains of threads; or she should tie a loose cloth over her hair.
