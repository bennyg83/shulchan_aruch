Siftei Kohen wrote — doubtful on this; and likewise regarding squeezing eyes excessively in siman 39 — some say immersion counted for her, and this is not per what Mechaber wrote above regarding kohl and blood in a wound that interposes, for these depend on each other per the text in Sha"s and as evident from all poskim — end quote; and Taz wrote — and it is hard for me why he invalidates after the fact; per those who say "because water precedes" we should also say so here; and one may say here there is concern wrinkles on body above water will stick, so when they enter water they are already stuck; per this if she inserted herself until her neck in water first it is immersion after the fact in every case; and it appears correct for every woman to do so for at least there is some advantage if she stays long without awareness — end quote (and Nekudot HaKesef resolves — there when she inserts feet in water her feet already touched water before reaching mud and that water is connected to mikveh therefore no interposition; but here through wrinkles water inside wrinkles will not be connected to mikveh at all — understand well — end quote).
