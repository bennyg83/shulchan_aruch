And require, etc. However she should not recite a blessing when she returns and immerses again — Bach.
And one should be stringent, etc. That is to say, one should be stringent lechatchila that she return and immerse with intention if possible; and so Bach. However if her companion forced her and immersed her — the companion's intention is a superior intention for all, as stated in chapter two of Chullin end of page 31.
If she encountered any of these things, etc. Such as a dog, donkey, an ignorant person, an object of object of idolatryatryater, a pig, a horse, or a leaccording to; and similar; so it is in Sha”D siman 23. But in Rokeach and Kol Bo it states that if a horse befell her she should go up and have relations; for her sons will stand handsome in their speech, hear and examine carefully, learn Torah and not forget, and sleep little; and even more, their fear will be upon people, etc. We learn in chapter Arvei Pesachim (Pesachim 111a): one who meets a woman at the moment she ascends from immersion of mitzvah; if he precedes and has relations a spirit of licentiousness seizes him; if she precedes and has relations a spirit of licentiousness seizes her. What is his remedy? Let him say: “Who pours contempt upon princes and causes them to wander in the trackless waste” (Tehillim 107). And so Rokeach brings it; but Ba’al Toledot Aharon did not cite the verse “who pours contempt upon princes” but rather “and loosens the belt of the mighty” in Job 12; and Pesachim 111 and Tehillim he cited nothing; it appears he had no girsa in the Gemara of “who pours contempt upon princes,” etc.; and Yalkut brings this Gemara in Tehillim and Job; therefore let us say both of them.
Must remove them, etc. This implies whoever did not remove them — even bediavad they interpose, since he is able to remove them.
