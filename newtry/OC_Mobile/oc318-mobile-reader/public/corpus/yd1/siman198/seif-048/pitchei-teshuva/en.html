Without intent. If her husband was ill and she counted seven clean days and immersed only so she could touch and care for him, that immersion also permits marital relations.
She is permitted to her husband. Yom Teruah questions this rule; Radbaz answers. Magen Avraham writes that no blessing is recited in this case.
One should be stringent. Baer Heitev discusses immersion caused by coercion. Chamudei Daniel suggests that coercion by another woman may help, but coercion by a man may not. Levushei Serad is lenient where she first entered merely to cool herself and then formed intent, even without lifting her feet again.
