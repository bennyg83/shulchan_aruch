A niddah who immersed without intention — for example, she fell into the water or descended to cool herself — behold she is permitted to her husband. {Rama: And there are those who are stringent and require her to immerse again (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba and Rabbeinu Yerucham and Rokeach and Hagahos Ashiri), and one should be stringent l’chatchila. There are those who wrote that a woman should be modest on the night of her immersion, and so women practiced to conceal the night of their immersion, not to go in commotion or before people, so that people not sense them; and one who does not do so, it is said about her, “Cursed is one who lies with an animal.” And women should be careful when they go out from the immersion that her friend should encounter her, so that not an impure thing or an idolater should encounter her first; and if these things encountered her, if she is yerei Shamayim she should return and immerse (Sha”d and Kol Bo and Rokeach). And see below at the end of siman 201 whether it is permitted to pour hot water into a mikveh or whether it is permitted to wash after immersion.}
