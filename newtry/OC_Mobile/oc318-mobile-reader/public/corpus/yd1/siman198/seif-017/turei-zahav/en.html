That majority of craftspeople, etc. Beit Yosef in this siman wrote in name of Rokeach — appears if woman touched pot or kettle and flesh swelled slightly this is not particular even though little stuck to flesh; and from this reason I permitted woman who immersed and found blackness in place she touched bathhouse walls black from bath smoke — she needs no second immersion for similar to this we mentioned and it is a fortiori for hands need be cleaner more than body for hands always exposed and she is more particular about their cleanliness more than body; and unlike ink that interposes as Rokeach beginning seif 15 for there actual ink substance interposes unlike this blackness which is mere filth; and similarly Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rashba to distinguish dye from ink as above.
