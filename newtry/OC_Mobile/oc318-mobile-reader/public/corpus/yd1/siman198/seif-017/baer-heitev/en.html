And hair. Dye does not interpose even if it covers most or all of the hair, because it becomes like the hair itself and its color has no substance. It differs from ink, which has substance. Rabbeinu Yerucham, Ramban, and Rashba.
Whose occupation. Beit Yosef cites Rokeach that slight soot from touching a pot is not considered something she objects to, even if a little adheres. Taz therefore permits a woman who immersed and later found a dark mark from the smoky bathhouse wall. A person with two occupations, such as dyeing and writing, who has both dye and ink on the hand, should be treated stringently.
