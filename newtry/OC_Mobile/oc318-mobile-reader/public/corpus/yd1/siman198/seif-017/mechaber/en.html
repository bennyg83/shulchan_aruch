Dye with which women dye their faces and hands and the hair of their heads does not interpose. And likewise, one who is a dyer and his hands are dyed does not interpose. {Rama: And likewise, one whose trade is to be a shochet or butcher, and his hands are always soiled with blood, does not interpose, for most of those in this trade are not particular (Beit Yosef in the name of R”i).}
