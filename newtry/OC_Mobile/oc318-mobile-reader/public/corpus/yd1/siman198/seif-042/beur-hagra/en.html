If they entered, etc. The Tosefta in chapter 8 states: If pebbles or splinters entered the cracks on the underside of his feet, Rabbi Akiva says they do not constitute an interposition because that area is considered a concealed part of the body. It further states there: regarding a plaster, poultice, or bandage upon a concealed part of the body, Rabbi says it constitutes an interposition, while Rabbi Yose son of Rabbi Yehudah says it does not. It appears that Rabbi Akiva follows the view of Rabbi Yose son of Rabbi Yehudah. We, however, rule like Rabbi, as stated in Niddah 66b: although actual contact of water is not required, etc., and as ruled by the Semag.
