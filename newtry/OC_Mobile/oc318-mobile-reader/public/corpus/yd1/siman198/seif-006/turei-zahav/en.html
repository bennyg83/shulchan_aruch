For a married woman it constitutes a chatzitzah. This is because she is particular that it not make her unattractive to her husband. The Beit Yosef writes that the Tur did not need to state the law of an unmarried woman, for it has no practical relevance for us. My revered father-in-law, of blessed memory, explains that it teaches that even a married woman whose husband is not particular about this, like an unmarried woman, nevertheless has a chatzitzah, because we follow the majority of married women. Therefore, he states specifically that for an unmarried woman it does not constitute a chatzitzah, but not for any married woman.
