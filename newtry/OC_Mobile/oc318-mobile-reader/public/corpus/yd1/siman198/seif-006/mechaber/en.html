Hair opposite the heart, and in the beard, that is stuck to itself because of sweat, interposes. But on the head and in the armpit it does not interpose. And in that place, in a man it does not interpose; and in a woman: for a married woman it interposes, for an unmarried woman it does not interpose. {Rama: And those who have a type of braided hair that is stuck one to another, and it is made at night by a demon, and it is dangerous to remove them — they do not interpose (Mordechai, Hilchos Niddah, in the name of Raaviyah; and in Agur; and in Hagahos Sha”d).}
