Stuck to one another. Panim Meirot discusses medically tangled hair produced by powders. Where most hair remains untangled, this may be treated as a minority she does not remove and relied upon for healing. If most or all hair is affected, it should not be permitted. Chatam Sofer rules that wax itself certainly interposes.
They do not interpose. Chamudei Daniel writes that she should remain in the water long enough for it to penetrate thoroughly.
