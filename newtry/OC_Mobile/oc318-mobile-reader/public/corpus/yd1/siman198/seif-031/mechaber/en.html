One does not immerse on vessels; therefore, if there was mud in the place where she immerses, she should not stand on a wooden vessel that receives tumah from its back, nor on planks that are fit for a midras, nor on any vessel fit for a midras and immerse, because of the decree of bathhouses of vessels. If she transgressed and immersed, the immersion did not count for her. But she may place bundles of twigs under her feet because of the mud. And likewise, she should not stand on an earthenware vessel nor on a split log and immerse; even though an earthenware vessel does not become tamei from its back and is not fit for a midras, it is a concern of the chachamim lest she fear that she will fall and will not immerse properly. If she transgressed and immersed upon these, the immersion counted for her. And according to this, a mikveh that has wooden steps (meaning: stairs): if she immersed on the steps, even if they are attached to the walls of the mikveh (a responsum of Rashba), the immersion did not count for her, for they are wooden implements without a receptacle. And one must make in their place a step of stones, and the step should be 4 [tefachim] wide — the place of placement of the foot — so that she not fear to fall from it.
