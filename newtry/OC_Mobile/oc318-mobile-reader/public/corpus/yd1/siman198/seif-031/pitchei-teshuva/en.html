One does not immerse in vessels. Panim Meirot permits copper plates fixed to the floor and walls of a mikveh because before attachment they were unfinished metal forms and did not receive impurity.
Planks. Where stone is unavailable, narrow planks not individually fit for standing may be joined for a mikveh floor. Chatam Sofer criticizes perforated and iron-fastened planks because the holes do not remove treading status and may create concerns of leakage. Noda BiYehudah forbids placing a tied chair in a deep mikveh for women to stand upon.
