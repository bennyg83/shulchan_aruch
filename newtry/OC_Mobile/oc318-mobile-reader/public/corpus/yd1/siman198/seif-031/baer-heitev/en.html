In water. Shach limits this to mikveh water, while Taz permits rinsing the hands even in detached water because the moisture later connects with the mikveh. One must still not grip forcefully. Derishah permits a friend who pushes the woman into the water to hold her after first wetting her hands, but Shach hesitates to permit initially against established custom. For a woman unable to stand, two women may support her by the arms after thoroughly wetting their hands. A sheet may not be used because it is susceptible to impurity.
