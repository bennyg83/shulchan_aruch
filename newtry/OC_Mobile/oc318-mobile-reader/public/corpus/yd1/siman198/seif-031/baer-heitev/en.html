In water. Siftei Kohen wrote — specifically in mikveh water but not in drawn water; but Taz wrote she may rinse her hands even in drawn water (for now that they are connected to the mikveh they are also valid for drawn water that is wet enough to wet another is connection) — and not as Rama wrote in siman 120 seif 2; and he wrote further regarding what Mechaber wrote that loosening helps and afterward wrote that rinsing hands helps even without loosening — nevertheless she must not hold her with force for otherwise rinsing first does not help, only we are not concerned for this — end quote; and Darkei Moshe wrote there is complete permission that when the woman immerses and her friend pushes her in water she may hold her by the hands at time of pushing if she rinsed her hands in water first and need not separate her hands from her — and not as our women practice separating their hands from her at time of pushing; and Siftei Kohen wrote that in his humble opinion one should not rule leniently in matters they practiced as prohibition, for in mishnah and poskim it is only permitted after the fact but l'chatchila who will tell us; however for a woman who cannot stand on her feet he ruled in responsum Maharashbal siman 81 that she should be immersed by two women holding her by the arms and they rinse their hands first in water until wet enough to wet another, but she has no remedy to lie her on a sheet and immerse her for a sheet receives tumah and is forbidden (as below seif 31) — end quote.
