In Hagahot Ashri, chapter Eilu Megalchin, he wrote in the name of Or Zarua: we do not allow the mourner to fast on the first day, but feed and give him drink; even on the fast before Rosh Hashanah, Rashbam did not allow fasting on the day of burial. End quote. Beit Yosef and the poskim. Nevertheless, from this we learn that even according to what we rule, that he may fast if he wants, if the mourner does not want to fast on the days before Rosh Hashanah or similar fasts, he is permitted not to. If the death occurred at midday and he had already accepted the fast, he waits until evening after prayer but does not complete it fully.
