He may eat at night. See the new Responsa Radbaz, siman 424.
And no meal of consolation is required. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, Yoreh Deah, siman 98, who writes that if one buried his deceased at night, the meal of consolation is served that night. If he did not eat at night, it is served the following day. Rama's statement that no meal of consolation is required at night applies only where the first day itself had already been deferred; see there.
