On Shabbat. The Shach wrote: this requires study, for below at the end of siman 399 the Beit Yosef ruled that one provides the mourner's meal on Shabbat, and wrote that such is the simple custom throughout the world, to provide the mourner's meal on Shabbat, and no one was ever heard opening his mouth or chirping against it; end quote. (Perhaps this follows the practice of some who provide meals all seven days, according to the Sephardic custom in many places, because the mourner does not prepare anything for himself due to the burden of his mourning, and also so as not to shame one who lacks food for the seven days when he is idle from his work. Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote likewise. Therefore they provide food even on Shabbat, for the mourners rely on this, and without it he has nothing to eat on Shabbat. But according to those who practice providing only the first meal, with some bread and eggs, we have never heard that they do this on Shabbat, so it appears to me.): ..
