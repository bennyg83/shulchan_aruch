(Likkut) The place, etc. The language of the Ramban in the name of Avot deRabbi Natan: a place where it is customary to comfort mourners with eggs and lentils — they feed them [those foods]; a place where it is customary [to comfort] with meat and wine — they feed them [those foods]. And Jerusalem Talmud, ch. 3 of Berakhot: when R. Yosi died, R. Hiyya bar Ba received condolences, and they ate meat and drank wine; when R. Hiyya bar Ba died, R. Shimon bar R. Yehudah received his condolences, and they ate lentils (so it is in Tohorat HaBayit; and above, siman 242, note 66, our master cited our [different] textual version). They said: this is indeed the custom. When R. Zeira died, he charged and said: "Do not accept upon yourselves today [the duty of] eating — tomorrow [provide] from his own [estate]." And this is what is stated in seif 3: if he wishes, etc. — end quote:
