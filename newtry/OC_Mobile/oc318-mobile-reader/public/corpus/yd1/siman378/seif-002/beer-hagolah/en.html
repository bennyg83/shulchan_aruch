Following the Rosh's explanation that the 'first day' stated by Rav Yehudah means the first meal. A similar expression appears in Pesachim 37a in the statement of Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, 'on the first day,' and so forth. Hagahot Maimoniyot likewise writes in the name of the Semag that in matters of mourning we follow the lenient opinion.
