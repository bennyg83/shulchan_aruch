(Likkut) There were, etc. Jerusalem Talmud (ch. 3 of Mo'ed Katan): "One who sees a Torah scholar who has died is like one who sees a Torah scroll that was burned." R. Abbahu said: "May it come upon me if I tasted anything that entire day." R. Yonah heard that the son of R. Yehudah's son had died; even though he had eaten cheese and drunk water, he declared it a fast for that entire day. The Rosh cited this in ch. 1 of Ta'anit; and so too in the Gemara, beginning of ch. 3 of Shevuot: "like the day that [a Torah scholar] died," etc. — end quote:
