A little during the meal. Ketubot 8b: they began, etc., then restored the original practice. Torat HaAdam explains that wine drunk during a meal does not intoxicate, as stated in the Yerushalmi at the end of Pesachim and cited by Rosh and the other authorities there. This is why the ruling says, but not to satiety.
