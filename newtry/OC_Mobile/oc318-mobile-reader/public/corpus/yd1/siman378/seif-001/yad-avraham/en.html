(Siman 378, Shulchan Aruch seif 9) With meat and wine. Beit Hillel permits a mourner who is also a nazir to drink a small amount of wine during the meal, calling it a mitzvah because the Sages said that wine was created to comfort mourners. His words imply that only wine of Kiddush and Havdalah is forbidden because those obligations are rabbinic, whereas a Torah mitzvah would permit wine. This is astonishing. Even for a Torah mitzvah a nazir’s wine prohibition is not suspended, as Nazir 44 states explicitly. Products of the vine were never permitted to a nazir, unlike tumah and shaving, which are permitted for a met mitzvah and for a mitzvah-required haircut. The Gemara derives this from Scripture.
