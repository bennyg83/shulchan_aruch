An egg of nevelah and treifah — even if it was completed such that similar ones are sold in the market — is forbidden; and even if it was mixed into a thousand, all are forbidden; but a doubt whether it is an egg of nevelah or an egg of a slaughtered animal — it is permitted even without a mixture; but a doubt whether it is an egg of treifah or an egg of a slaughtered animal — it is forbidden: {Rama: And if the chicken was slaughtered and found treifah — the law of eggs is like the law of milk that was milked from an animal (Beit Yosef); and see above siman 81.}
