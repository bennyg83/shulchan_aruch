We wait. Taz wrote: some say to what Rama wrote in siman 57 that we are concerned for mishap even in a doubt of tereifah — there it is different because one must wait twelve months; but here the time is short. But in Beit Yosef he wrote: even regarding fowl one does not wait except a day — immediately one slaughters and sells to a non-Jew, meaning sells to a non-Jew and the non-Jew slaughters afterward — see above siman 57.
Doubt. Shach wrote: but certainly tereifah — no; and see siman 57 and siman 81.
