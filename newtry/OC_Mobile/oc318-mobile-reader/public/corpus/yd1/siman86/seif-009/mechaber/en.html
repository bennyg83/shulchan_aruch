A treifah does not give birth; therefore a fowl that is a safek treifah and laid eggs — one waits with it until it lays all its eggs that it already claims; if it will lay again — it is kosher, and we know this when it paused between the end of one laying and the beginning of another a full twenty-one days, for such is its gestation period; and the same law for a chicken that never laid, born with a safek of treifot — that if it lays after twenty-one days from when it was born it is a safek treifah and the eggs are permitted (Orach Chaim brings it Beit Yosef end siman 57).
