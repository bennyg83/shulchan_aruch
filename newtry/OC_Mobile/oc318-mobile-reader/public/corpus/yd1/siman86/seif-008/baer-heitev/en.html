Rebelliousness. Perishah challenged: for in siman 66 Tur wrote that if it is known the chick is formed they are liable on it from the Torah; and he resolved: there, blood is found inside the egg — therefore forbidden from the Torah because of blood; unlike here where the blood already turned and became clotted and is dust and has no d'oraisa prohibition; and Taz in siman 66 resolved: here it speaks where there is no blood, only formation of flesh — and this is d'rabbanan, end of his words.
