An egg of impure fowl that was cooked with others — if it is in its shell it does not forbid; and if it is peeled it forbids (and if it is pierced its law is like peeled) (Arukh general rule 42 and hagahah ShaDa); and one needs sixty-one permitted eggs corresponding to it to nullify it (and even if it is not whole but only broken) (in Arukh and Beit Yosef in name of Rashba); and if it was cooked in a permitted dish one needs only sixty of the dish to nullify it; and an egg that has a chick or blood forbids what is cooked with it even if it is not peeled: {Rama: And shells of prohibition combine also with permission to nullify the prohibition, and all the more shells of permission (Tosafot Nedarim 15 and Yerushalmi and Bavli — see s.k. 14); and see above siman 99.}
