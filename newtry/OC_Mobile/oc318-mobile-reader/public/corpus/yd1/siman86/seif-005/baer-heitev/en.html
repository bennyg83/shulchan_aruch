Peeled. Shach wrote: it appears that even though the others are also in their shells it is forbidden until sixty-one, and the shell of the permitted combines — by way of example: if there are fifty permitted eggs and the shells hold ten eggs, that is insufficient but sixty-one are required (the reason is that peeled is the power of fire dominating it and extracting the body of the prohibition, and it is like an egg that has a chick in it which forbids even if not peeled — Taz, and Shach agreed). And not like Bach who is lenient if the forbidden is peeled and the permitted is not peeled and permits everything — this does not seem correct at all; see there.
Sixty-one. The reason: many poskim wrote that because there are large and small eggs they therefore added one; and for this reason when it was cooked in a permitted dish one needs only sixty of the dish to nullify it — this is Mechaber's view; but Rambam wrote the reason is because it is a complete creature in itself they made a distinction and added to its measure; and for this reason even in a dish one needs sixty-one to nullify; however for this reason if it became tereifah in a bowl one needs only sixty; and according to Mechaber one needs sixty-one — and one should be stringent for both reasons, end of his words.
Chick. The reason is that the meat is forbidden; and the same applies to blood found in it — a drop of blood that spreads through all of it, as above siman 66, and it is considered as if there is a chick in it — specifically when it is known the blood is in the place that forbids it; but in a doubt of mixtures it is permitted as in siman 66.
Siman 99. Meaning: there it was explained that where there is no great loss one may not combine bones of prohibition to nullify — and the same applies here; and Taz distinguishes that specifically in a dish the shell of prohibition combines with the permitted, but not with ordinary eggs — sixty-one are required to nullify it.
