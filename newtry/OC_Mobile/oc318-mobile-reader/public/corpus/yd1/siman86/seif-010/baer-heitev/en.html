In what case is this said. That it is forbidden to buy from a non-Jew.
They practice. And a sage wrote: nevertheless one should not buy cracked eggs from a non-Jew; see there. And Shach wrote: however, it may be that these poskim who permit speak of a non-Jew who is not signed; but a non-Jew who is commonly signed often mixes with bread — cracked eggs — and we are concerned lest he sell to an Israelite, end of his words.
