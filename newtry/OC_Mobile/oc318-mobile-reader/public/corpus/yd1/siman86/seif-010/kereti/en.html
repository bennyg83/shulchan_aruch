How should he act. Taz: if yolk and white remain visible, signs of such-and-such fowl may help; selling cracked to a non-Jew is permitted per Rashba when signs are known.
And some say. Where bread of gentiles is permitted, selling tereifah to a non-Jew is permitted per many poskim; custom varies — see Peleti and Semak.
And so is practiced. Nevertheless do not buy cracked eggs from a non-Jew — see a sage in general rule 67.
