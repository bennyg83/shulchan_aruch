Bread kneaded with eggs in a place, etc. See above siman 112 and what he wrote there.
And some say, etc. Tur's view appears that even where they are not careful about bread of gentiles, it is permitted to sell him a tereifah egg; and so it appears to me that this is Tosafot in chapter Eizehu Mekoman (daf 64 side 1 s.v. signs), Rashba, Mordechai there, and Ran there explicitly; and so appears Semag; and so from other poskim. The reason for all of them: we are not concerned lest he mix it with bread and sell it to an Israelite; also most of their dough is not kneaded with eggs but with whole eggs, as stated in Shulchan Aruch siman 98 and in Orach Chaim of Maharil in laws of eggs and other poskim. And what the Rav wrote, and so is practiced — is according to what is written in a sage at the end of general rule 67 seif 4 in name of Orach Chaim general rule 42, that so they practice; but in our Orach Chaim it is not written there that so they practice, and I have not seen such a custom; and so it appears explicitly from Maharshal in Pesachim s.k. 88 that it is permitted. However, it may be that all these poskim speak of a non-Jew who is not signed; but a non-Jew who is commonly signed often mixes with bread — cracked eggs — and we are concerned lest he sell to an Israelite; in this Semak and Orach Chaim are concerned; Bach also wrote this reasoning, even though from the Rav's words in that sage and here it appears forbidden to sell to any non-Jew.
