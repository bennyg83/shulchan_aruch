One does not sell an egg of nevelah and treifah to a gentile lest he return and sell it to a Jew. How should he act? He breaks it in a bowl and sells it to a gentile — for then certainly a Jew will not buy it from him. In what case is this said — when we see it broken in his hand that there is concern a Jew sold it to him because it is treifah; but it is permitted to buy from him bread kneaded with eggs in a place where they are accustomed to eat bread of gentiles, since the broken egg is not seen in his hand: {Rama: And some say that now that we are not careful about bread of gentiles, one should not sell a forbidden egg to a gentile even broken, lest he mix it with bread and sell it to a Jew (Arukh general rule 42 and Mordechai in name of Semak); and so is the custom in a place where they make bread with eggs and practice leniency to buy it; but in other places there is no concern.}
