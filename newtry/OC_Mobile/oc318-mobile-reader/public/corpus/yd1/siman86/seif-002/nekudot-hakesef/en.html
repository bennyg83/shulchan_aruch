(Siman 86 Taz s.k. 5) And this is not correct. It does not appear so from Orach Chaim as I will write below; nevertheless some say: bread kneaded with eggs — there the reason is because they rely on the majority; but here, since she broke it, it is like finding it cracked in his hand — we say in end of seif 10 that it is forbidden since there is concern of tereifah that an Israelite sold it to him; and the same here when she broke it — we are concerned lest it was an impure egg; and it appears Orach Chaim and Rama in the hagahah here do not intend this except to exclude eggs of chicken and goose that are more complete when whole — and investigate.
(Hagahah) See Beit Yosef daf 77 side 4 — Rashba wondered why Rif omitted disgusting eggs from the baraita; it appears Rif explains Rashi: disgusting eggs are impure eggs cooked with pure — the pure are permitted as mere moisture and do not impart taste to others; as in Yerushalmi Terumot per Rashi; poskim bring this; Rif states this explicitly in chapter Gid HaNasheh — investigate.
