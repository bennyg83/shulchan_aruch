Meaning chicken eggs. Explanation: we know certainly it was whole and had the form of a chicken egg; but if a non-Jewess brought and broke it before us unintentionally and we had not yet recognized by familiarity that they are chicken eggs, it is forbidden as implied here and in Orach Chaim general rule 42, where R' Yitzchak wrote: and this applies to a chicken egg, but for eggs of other fowl one must examine the signs — end of his words. In truth this is not so, for even bread kneaded with eggs at the end of this siman we say it is permitted; rather in all cases we rely on the majority, except where the non-Jew explicitly says they are from an uncommon species — then signs are required. This is written in a sage in name of Tosafot and Rosh; in name of the Rav the Maggid he also wrote that we buy only chicken and goose eggs; Rama's words here are imprecise.
