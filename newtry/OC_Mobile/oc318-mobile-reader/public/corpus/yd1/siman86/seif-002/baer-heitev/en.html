In signs. Taz wrote: even if a non-Jewess brought and broke them before us unintentionally and we had not yet recognized by familiarity that they are chicken eggs — nevertheless permitted and we rely on the majority; except where the non-Jew explicitly says they are from an uncommon species — then signs are required. And Shach wrote in name of a sage that the custom is to buy bar-goose eggs called enten, some in white shells and some in green shells, because they recognize in them that they are bar-goose eggs whose way is thus.
