Now they practice buying eggs indiscriminately, etc. Because impure fowl are not found among us — it appears even without recognizable familiarity, and he does not say they are from such-and-such fowl and it is pure, and even if he says nothing at all they buy even from a non-Jew, provided both ends are not rounded or pointed, because impure fowl are not found among us. But the Rav the Maggid explained that what they practice buying now from anyone is because they buy only chicken and goose eggs, and those are recognizable by familiarity — end of his words. It appears specifically when he has recognizable familiarity with them it is permitted; and such is Rama's view in his hagahah; and so too in a sage at the beginning of general rule 66 explicitly; and it appears from Rama's words in that sage that for chicken and goose eggs one need not check at all or look for other signs; and so it appears from his words here in the hagahah; and so wrote in Orach Chaim general rule 42 seif 7; and see end of this seif and what he wrote there in s.k. 4.
