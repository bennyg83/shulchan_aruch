The seller. Shach wrote: even an Israelite established in kashrut who says they are from such-and-such fowl and we recognize that that fowl is pure — we do not rely on him; and even if he shows us the fowl and says the eggs come from it he is not believed, for he certainly lies.
Relies. The reason: when he says it is from such-and-such fowl it is a presumption he does not lie because he fears lest they bring eggs of that fowl and they see they do not resemble these and he is shamed; and Taz wrote there is no room to say that since there are raven eggs resembling dove eggs he could evade and say they are dove eggs; nevertheless he fears lest an Israelite set his mind on them that there cannot fail to be some difference between them.
Not. The reason: when they bring eggs of a pure fowl and they do not resemble them, he can say: I did not tell you these are from this fowl but from another fowl you do not recognize, and it is pure.
Tereifah. For most eggs are kosher; and Shach wrote that according to the Rav in his hagahah, even without recognizable familiarity we rely on him when he says they are from such-and-such fowl and we recognize that it is pure.
