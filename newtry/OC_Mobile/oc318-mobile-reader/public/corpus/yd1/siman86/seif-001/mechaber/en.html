The signs of eggs and their laws. In it are 10 seifim: Signs of eggs to recognize that they are from a pure fowl — if both ends are rounded or pointed, or if the white was outside and the yolk inside — it is known that it is an egg of an impure fowl; and even if the seller says they are from a pure fowl, we do not rely on him. And if one end is round and spherical and the other end pointed and spherical, and the white is outside and the yolk inside — it is possible that it is an egg of an impure fowl and possible that it is an egg of a pure fowl; therefore one asks the hunter — a Jew (or a gentile) (Tur and Beit Yosef in name of Hagahot Maimoniyot and so too in name of R' Yitzchak ibn Chaviv) — the seller: if he told him it is from such-and-such fowl and we recognize that that fowl is pure — we rely on him; and if he said from a pure fowl and did not state its name — we do not rely on him; therefore one does not buy eggs from gentiles unless he recognized them and has in them a recognizable familiarity that they are eggs of such-and-such pure fowl; and we are not concerned lest they are eggs of a treifah or nevelah.
