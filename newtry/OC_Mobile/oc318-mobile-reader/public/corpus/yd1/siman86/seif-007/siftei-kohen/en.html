A chick born from an egg of nevelah and tereifah — permitted. The reason is stated in the Gemara and poskim: because the chick is not formed from the egg until after it spoils, and it is like mere dust.
And some say, etc. And Taz omitted this entire hagahah — and rightly intended: why should we be stringent in something found explicitly permitted in the Gemara and all poskim; also in Darkei Moshe and in a sage general rule 64 seif 2 he wrote on Orach Chaim that there is no reason for his words; and so in signs of a sage there, plain like Mechaber.
And l'chatchila one should be careful. Explanation: with this chick that was born one should be careful l'chatchila.
