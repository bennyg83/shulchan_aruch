Permitted. The reason is stated in the Gemara and poskim: because the chick is not formed from the egg until after it spoils, and it is like mere dust.
And some say. Shach wrote: and Taz omitted this entire hagahah and rightly intended — why should we be stringent in something found explicitly permitted in the Gemara and all poskim; and Taz wrote the reason one should be concerned lest he keep it in his house under the chicken to make a chick from it and it will be permitted, and there is concern for mishap lest meanwhile he eat it — as we find this matter stated explicitly in Mishnah chapter 8 of Terumot, that for this reason they decreed that growths of terumah too are terumah, lest he keep impure terumah until sowing time — and it requires study to resolve this in the Gemara, end of his words.
