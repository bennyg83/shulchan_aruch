And some say, etc. And below siman 98 Mechaber wrote: an egg that has a chick in it or a drop of blood that was cooked with others needs sixty-one to nullify its emission — end of his words; and it appears that an egg of impure fowl or of tereifah does not need nullification, and Rama did not emend there at all, because it appears primary in the Gemara and poskim like Mechaber.
