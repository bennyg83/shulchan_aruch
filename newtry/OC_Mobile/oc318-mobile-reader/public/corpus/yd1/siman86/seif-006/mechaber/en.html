An egg of treifah and nevelah — its law is like the law of an egg of impure fowl, that whatever is not peeled and was cooked with others does not forbid, for it is merely like water; {Rama: And some say all eggs have the same law and forbid even when not peeled (Tur in name of Beit HaTaharah and majority of poskim); and so one should practice where there is no great loss; but where there is great loss one may rely on the lenient.}
