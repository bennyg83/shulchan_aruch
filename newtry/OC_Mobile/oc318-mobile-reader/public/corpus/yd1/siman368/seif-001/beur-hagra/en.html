Or to eat, etc.; and one may not, etc. This is frivolity, as stated regarding a synagogue there 28a. See Rashi there, s.v. one may not eat, etc.
And one may not read, etc. Because of mocking the poor, as stated in siman 367.
(Supplement) And one may not stroll, etc. Eliyahu Rabbah, beginning of chapter 14. End of supplement.
Likewise one may not take, etc. From Mordechai, who writes that the incident in Sanhedrin 47b involving Rav's grave, etc., was different because it was for healing, as stated below.
Likewise it is permitted, etc. Proof comes from Chizkiyahu, who cut the doors of the Sanctuary, etc., to save the rest of the Temple. There.
