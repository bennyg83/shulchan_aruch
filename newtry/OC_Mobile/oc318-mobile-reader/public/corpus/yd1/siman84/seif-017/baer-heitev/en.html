"Medical treatment." Taz wrote: from Maharshal he cited R' Yehuda's responsum on an epileptic that it is like a wound of a corpse, and it is permitted to feed him food containing a sheretz if the cure is established; and if not — forbidden; and if a non-Jew nullified the cure in sixty — permitted — end of his words. Chavot Yair siman 102 wrote: a cure for one with greenish complexion to swallow eight lice of his head is forbidden because this cure is not established. One who inhales forbidden matter through his nostrils is exempt even if he enjoys a k'zayit, since eating is not done that way (HaLiku'tim siman 35).
