Therefore one ducks and drinks from them and need not worry, etc. And there is no concern here of lo teshaktzu, since he does not swallow it with the eye, but rather inside the water that he drinks. Bach in Kuntras Acharon. Shach, s.k. 3. Lechem HaPanim, ot 1; Chochmat Adam, klal 38, ot 3. And specifically when they are not repulsive to him; but if they are repulsive to him—it is forbidden because of lo teshaktzu. Taz, s.k. 2. And this means—even though for everyone they are not repulsive, if they are repulsive to him—it is forbidden, as written in the ot following.
