Worms found in flour and the like are forbidden, lest they separated and swarmed on the ground and returned (responsum of Rosh general rule 20 siman 3). {Rama: And the same law applies to salt or other non-liquid items (responsum of Rosh general rule 20; Hagahot ShaDa in name of Maharam; and see Beit Yosef; see s.k. 33). And it is forbidden to sell to a non-Jew lest he return and sell to an Israelite (there; and so Beit Yosef from tradition of Mahari Weil from Maharash).}
