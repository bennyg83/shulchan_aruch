One who filters water or other liquids and worms or yabchushin or mosquitoes were in it — even though they returned into it, they are forbidden, for they already separated; therefore one should not drink liquid in which worms and the like normally grow, and one should not filter it at night with reeds and straw lest they return and fall into the vessel and one come to drink them. {Rama: But it is permitted to filter through a garment or strainer, for they cannot fall through into the liquid; and likewise it is permitted to pour liquids from vessel to vessel since they are always with the liquid — meaning their place of growth (Beit Yosef).}
