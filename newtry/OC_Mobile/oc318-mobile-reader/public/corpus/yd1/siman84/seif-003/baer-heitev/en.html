Return. The Shach wrote that from the Mechaber's words it appears this is obvious if they returned in a vessel — for they had separated; but in the Gemara it appears there is only a doubt lest they separated, and it is possible the Mechaber's view is also so, only that he abbreviated for clarity.
To pour. The Shach wrote: it appears a second vessel has the law of a first vessel, and when they spread on the inner walls of the vessel it is permitted. And some say that separating from fruit to fruit in seif 4 — there that is not their place of growth, but here since it is always with the liquid — meaning their place of growth. And Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote: vinegar that has worms in it — if they separated into the air it is called separated; but from vinegar to a cooked dish it is not considered separation, and they are permitted — end of his words.
