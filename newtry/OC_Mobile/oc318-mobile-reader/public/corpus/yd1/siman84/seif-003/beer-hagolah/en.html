Tur in the name of Rambam, chapter 2 of the Laws of Forbidden Foods, in accordance with the Tanna of the school of Rabbi Yishmael. The Talmud implies that he disagrees with the first Tanna concerning channels whose water flows but does not spring from the ground, in accordance with Tosafot's explanation there and the teaching of Matya ben Charash there. Rashba agreed.
