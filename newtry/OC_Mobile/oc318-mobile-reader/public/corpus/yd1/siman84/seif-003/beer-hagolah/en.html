The Tur, in the name of the Rambam in chapter 2 of Hilchot Ma'achalot Asurot, and like the Tanna of the school of R' Yishmael—as is implied there—that argues with the Tanna Kama regarding charitzin that draw water and do not overflow, per the explanation of Tosafot there and as Matya ben Yehuda taught there; and the Rashba agreed.
