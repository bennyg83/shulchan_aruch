Lest they return, etc.—and "he will come to drink them": so writes the Tur, "and he will come to drink them," and behold they already separated; and it appears at first glance from his wording that this is obvious—that if they returned in a vessel they separated—and likewise from the words of the Mechaber. But in the gemara it appears there is only a safeik lest they separated; one must say that the view of the Tur and Mechaber is likewise, but they abbreviated what is understood.
And likewise it is permitted, etc.—it appears the second vessel has the law of the first vessel; and when they separate onto the interior walls it is permitted. Some disagree regarding fruit-to-fruit in Tosafot—that there it is not "their majority"; but here, since it is always with the liquid, it is "their majority," as the Beit Yosef also wrote. And it is written in Bedek HaBayit in the name of R' Yerucham: vinegar that has worms in it—if they separated to the air it is called separated; but from the vinegar to a stew it is not separating, and they are permitted, end quote—and this is in Netiv 15, ot 27.
