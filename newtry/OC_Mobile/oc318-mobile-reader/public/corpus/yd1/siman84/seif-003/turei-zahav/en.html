It is permitted to pour liquids, etc. And Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe wrote that the second vessel has the law of the first—if they separated on it from inside, it is permitted. And Beit Yosef wrote: and even though Asheri wrote in seif 1 regarding a pit that we are concerned lest they separated on the walls of the vessel—that is different there, for initially they were in a pit and now in a vessel; unlike here, where initially and now it is in a vessel, end quote. And in my humble opinion, there too the Rosh was concerned lest at the time one inserted the vessel into the pit they separated from the pit onto the top of the vessel, or even inside before the liquid entered there—for specifically after the liquid is there, there is no prohibition if they separated from there to the wall inside; but not before that.
