It is permitted to pour the liquids, etc. The second vessel has the same status as the first. If the creatures separate onto its inner wall, they remain permitted. The Beit Yosef distinguishes this from drawing from a cistern: there they originated in the cistern and may have separated onto the vessel before the liquid entered it. Here they began and remain within vessels. In my opinion, even in the cistern case the concern is that, while the vessel was inserted, they moved from the cistern onto the vessel, including its inner surface before the liquid filled it. Once the liquid is already present, movement onto the inner wall is permitted.
