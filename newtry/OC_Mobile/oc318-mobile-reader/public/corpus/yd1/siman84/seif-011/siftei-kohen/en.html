It is forbidden to eat from her inspection. — The language of Orchot Chaim: like the law of a butcher after whom fat was found — and that is as explained above, siman 64, seif 21: a butcher after whom fat was found — he is removed; and see above, siman 119, seif 8.
A woman who was found, etc. — The view of Maharshal in Orach Chayyim and in his book there is that women are not trusted for an inspection that involves effort, since we say they are lazy; and Orchot Chaim and the Mechaber speak of something that involves no effort. But in clarifying legumes, small fish, and other things that involve effort, they are not trusted in a Torah prohibition. And the Rav in Tur Choshen Mishpat there, dalet tet, wrote that the custom is not according to his words — rather, women inspect the legumes and fruits and we rely on them. And even though regarding the clarifying of small fish he wrote in the gloss at the end of siman 127 that a woman is not trusted — here it is different, since the prohibition was not established; and also since there are many sides to be lenient. Therefore, even though we rule stringently, nonetheless we rely on them — and so Tur Choshen Mishpat there wrote.
