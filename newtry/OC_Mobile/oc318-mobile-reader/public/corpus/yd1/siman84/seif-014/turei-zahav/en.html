It is permitted to grind them. The worms flee during grinding to the walls of the mill funnel, and even if some are ground, they become nullified.
And every worm that he sees, etc. The remainder is permitted because of a double doubt: perhaps no additional worm is present, and even if present perhaps it dissolved during baking. This is not intentionally nullifying a prohibition, because the intention is to bake the bread, not to nullify the worm.
