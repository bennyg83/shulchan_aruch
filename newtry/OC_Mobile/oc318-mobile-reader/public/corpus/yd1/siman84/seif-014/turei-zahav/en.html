It is permitted to grind them—for the worms flee at the time of grinding from there to the walls of the hopper; and even if they are ground, behold they are nullified.
And any worm one sees, etc. It appears that the reason the rest is permitted is that here there is safeik safeik: safeik there is none there; and if you say there is—safeik it dissolved at the time of baking. And here there is no nullifying prohibition lechatchilah—for our intent is only to bake the bread and not to nullify the worm—in my humble opinion.
