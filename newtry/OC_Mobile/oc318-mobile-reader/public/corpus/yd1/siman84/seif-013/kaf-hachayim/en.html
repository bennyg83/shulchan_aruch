(13) There. And if they separated from the place of their majority—such as behind the pit, or on the lip of the vessel from outside. The language is not corrected; and in Tehilla l'David for the Rashba it is written: behind the vessel, or on the lip of the pit—and that is corrected. Nevertheless, it appears that sometimes a vessel is found whose lip is wide, and one need not worry that they separated onto its lip. Shach, s.k. 6. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz, ot 5; Pri Chadash, ot 5; Shulchan Petach David, ot 6.
