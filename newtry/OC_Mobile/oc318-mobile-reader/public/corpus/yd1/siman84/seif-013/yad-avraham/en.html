So too the Rashba in responsum 494, and the Ran, chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah, regarding hag'alah. And in Sefer Sha'ar HaMelekh, Hilchot Ma'achalot Asurot, he challenged—for from the words of Tosafot and Ran in Pesachim (30a) it appears it is forbidden even though one does not intend to nullify the prohibition in order to benefit; and per Pri Chadash in Orach Chayyim, siman 453, to resolve the difficulty—that hag'alah is different, for his intent is to separate the prohibition from the permitted, and in such a case all permit.
