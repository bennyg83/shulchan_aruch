Nevertheless one must inspect them, etc.—and the Shach explained: internal inspection is not required, but external inspection is required—for if it separated outward behold the worm is forbidden; and therefore inspection is required. From this I wondered at the words of the Taz, who wrote s.k. 12, that flour and raisins dried do not require inspection after milvein, since the prohibition is only after separating—there is safeik safeika: for this is external inspection, for the milvein stir on top and inspection is required to remove them—and this is impossible; therefore everything is forbidden, and particularly in hot days milvein are common; and behold this is more common than lung inspection that requires inspection; and also after adhesions inspection is required, even though there is safeik safeika: perhaps there is no adhesion, and if there is perhaps it is kosher—why should we not require inspection here? And particularly in milvein it does not apply at all "lest they separated"—for their entire matter is to stir and move and be moved; and they are always judged as separated and standing; and therefore in hot times inspection is most necessary; but in cold days they are not common and inspection is not required. And behold, likewise in the case of a woman who made Paladin Marziyani, and afterwards worms were found at the seller's and in the hands of other buyers who bought milvein—and the Taz permitted: one can say they formed afterwards, and there is safeik safeika: safeik they were not there initially; and if you say they were, perhaps they dissolved, end quote. And in truth it is as I wrote: if in hot times this stirring is common in them—certainly one should be stringent, for we say certainly they were there and inspection is required; and now that we find in Ma'achalot Asurot from the Torah let us say they formed afterwards—unlike in days that are not so hot until it is very common—it appears correct as the view of the Taz. And what Pri Chadash attacked on him—that in a short time the milvein would form—I do not know; and will he say this stirring was forever, and these fruits—he will concede they formed in time; and if so, one moment before the time it was not there and afterwards it was—and if so, perhaps this is the moment the stirring was born and came into being, and before it was not; and according to Pri Chadash it must be it was forever, which is impossible. But in my opinion safeik safeika is not needed—for why should we not say establish fruits in their presumption, and now it deteriorated? And is this better than treifah of an animal—and this is the sheretz found in a alley that Tosafot raised in Chullin: we say now it is found and it was not retroactively; and even for those who disagree—we were that there the sheretz is found; but here in these fruits they are not found—only we are concerned from the Torah; let us hold retroactively for Chullin everything is permitted, and we do not hold everything retroactively; and even for those who disagree one can distinguish between tumah and issur—in any case it is not worse than treifah of an animal. And from the Torah to be stringent also in the second safeik that the Taz gave—perhaps it dissolved—and Pri Chadash wrote: in bees it does not apply, and if it melted behold its quantity still exists; and also he did not speak correctly regarding the aforementioned Rav: small milvein, controlled by the heat of fire, are a vessel of themselves and like nothing and as if they have no substance; therefore even in this, since it is not recognizable—and if so from the Torah it is nullified—only rabbinically clear is not nullified—there is safeik derabbanan lekula. And here we did not establish its prohibition—raisins themselves have presumption of heter, and it is like something spilled in rabbinic prohibition where we depend leniently—even though prohibition was established because of the stew, heter was established; and likewise here; and particularly most authors hold regarding establishing prohibition we also say safeik derabbanan lekula; and therefore the correct ruling is as I wrote.
