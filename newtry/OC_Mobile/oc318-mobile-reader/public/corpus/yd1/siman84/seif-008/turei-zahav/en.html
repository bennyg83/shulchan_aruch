All kinds of fruit that commonly become infested while attached, etc. This implies that fruit or flour that becomes infested only after detachment requires no inspection when no defect is visible. The insect is prohibited only if it separated, creating a double doubt: perhaps no insect is present, and even if present perhaps it did not separate. A double doubt permits even a Torah prohibition.
