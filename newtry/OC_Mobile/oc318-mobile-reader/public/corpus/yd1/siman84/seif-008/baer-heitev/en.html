Attached. The Shach wrote: in Asheri and Rashba it is found that beans, lentils, and legumes are accustomed to become wormy while attached; however, in other poskim it is found that legumes and the like are not accustomed to become wormy except when detached — and it appears they relied on this nowadays, that we are not accustomed to check legumes; even within twelve months we only put them in a cold pot and pour off whatever rises to the top, and cook the remainder in boiling water — even though Maharshal ruled that one must check each and every one individually; and people are lenient in this. And it is also possible that nowadays they are not accustomed to become wormy while attached — for this matter changes according to place and time; and one who is stringent in this — may he be blessed. And the Taz wrote: from the Mechaber's words it appears that regarding something whose way is to become wormy only when detached, one need not check; and therefore there is no obligation to check flour or dried fruit such as raisins and the like if there are small mealworms there — for there is no prohibition except after they separated, which is a double doubt; and in a double doubt it is permitted even regarding Torah law — end of his words. And certainly that initially one must check, and it is not a double doubt once it has been presumed so; however, if one transgressed and cooked them — there is still a doubt lest it dissolved and was cooked — it is permitted (and see Shach s.k. 31). Nevertheless, everything changes according to place and according to time — there are years when they become wormy and years when they do not; therefore a scrupulous person should be concerned for himself lest he come to stumble in a Torah prohibition — and so in Beit Yosef. And I saw practical people who were not accustomed to eat raisins, almonds, and rice — see there.
To discard. Meaning: regarding those whose way is to become wormy — although one need not check them inside since they remained twelve months and the worm inside them decomposed and is like mere dust — nevertheless one must check them to discard the worms found among them outside; but those fruits whose way is not to become wormy at all — certainly no checking is needed at all, even externally.
Month. The Taz and Shach wrote: within twelve months this checking does not help — for the Ran wrote that those that became wormy while attached do not have a nature to float upward like those that become wormy when detached. And Maharshal wrote: specifically regarding legumes, beans, and the like this remedy helps; but regarding fruit that have pits inside — even when detached we are concerned lest they separated onto the pits; therefore boiling and checking do not help them. And this is not the Shach's view — see there.
That is common. And anything common — one may not rely on checking the majority; and it is similar to checking the lung in responsum 39.
