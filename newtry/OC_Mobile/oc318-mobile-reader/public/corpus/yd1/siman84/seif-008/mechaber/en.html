All fruit species that normally become wormy while attached may not be eaten until one checks the fruit inside lest there be a worm; and if the fruit remained twelve months after being picked, one may eat without checking, for any creature without a bone does not survive twelve months; nevertheless one must check to throw out worms found among them outside or on the fruit; and even after throwing out those found outside, one should be concerned that when placed in water in the pot they may come out and swarm (translation: "the sheretz that swarms" — rachasha derachish) in the water or on the pot walls or on the fruit; therefore one coming to cook after twelve months should place wormy fruit in cold water — the wormy and perforated rise to the top; afterward place in a pot of boiling water so any remaining worm dies immediately. {Rama: Checking a majority does not suffice — one must check all, for it is a common minority (Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen and responsum siman 274).}
