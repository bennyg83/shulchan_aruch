The Shach wrote: even if we do not know that its way is to become wormy while attached, since it is wormy before us it is forbidden; and according to Rema in seif 4 it must be — meaning when not perforated outward, for if perforated outward, even if it became wormy while detached it is forbidden [as the Rosh wrote in seif 4] — and this is obvious.
