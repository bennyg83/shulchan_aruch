A fruit that became wormy, etc. — Even if we did not know that its way is to become wormy while attached — since it is wormy before us and it is possible to doubt that it became wormy while attached, it is forbidden. And according to the Rav in the gloss at the end of seif 4, one must say this is specifically where it was not pierced outward; for if it was pierced outward — even if it became wormy when detached, it is forbidden. And this is straightforward.
