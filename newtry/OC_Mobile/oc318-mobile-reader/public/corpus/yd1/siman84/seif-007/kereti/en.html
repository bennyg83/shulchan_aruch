Fruit that became worm-infested, etc. Even when this type of fruit is not normally subject to infestation, the Acharonim nevertheless ruled stringently and forbade it. See Pleiti. According to the Rama, who permits cheese made from an animal later found to be a treifah when the cheese was produced within the first three days, because we say that the defect arose only now, here too we may say that the infestation arose only now, after the fruit was detached, and it is permitted. However, regarding the worm itself there is room for concern, since it has no presumption of prior permissibility; see there.
