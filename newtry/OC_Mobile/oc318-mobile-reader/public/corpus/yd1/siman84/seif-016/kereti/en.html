For some permit it. This is the view of Raavyah, who maintains that separation applies only to things that grow from the ground. With meat, cheese, and similar foods, the concept of separation does not apply. Pri Chadash objected that those who ruled this way misunderstood Raavyah's words, but his objection is not precise. See Pleiti, where I clarified Raavyah's position and brought proof from the Gemara. Therefore, every conscientious person should be stringent for himself, but one should not protest against those who follow the lenient practice, for they have a sound authority upon whom to rely.
