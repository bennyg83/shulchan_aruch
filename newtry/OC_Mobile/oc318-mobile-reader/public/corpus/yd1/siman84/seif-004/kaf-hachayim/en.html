[Seif 1] The verse states, "This you may eat, from all that is in the water," meaning that in seas and rivers, if it has fins, you may eat it, and if it does not have fins, you may not eat it. But in vessels, even if it does not have fins, you may eat it. Chullin 66b. Beit Yosef. Levush. Taz, s.k. 1. Chochmat Adam, there.
