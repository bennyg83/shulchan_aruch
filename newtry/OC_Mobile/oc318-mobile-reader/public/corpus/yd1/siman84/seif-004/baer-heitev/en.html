Outward. (For we are concerned lest they separated.) The Shach wrote: and some say regarding Sheratzim that, like a vessel in seif 1, we are not concerned for this — for here, since its hole is open outward, there is somewhat of proof that they separated and returned; or else it is the way of worms to spread outside onto the fruit and return — and not in a vessel.
