Worms that grow in detached fruit are permitted, for the Torah forbade only sheretz haShoretz al haAretz; when is this said — when they did not separate from the fruit; but if they separated from the fruit — even if they did not reach the ground but died in the air before reaching the ground; and even if only part separated, or separated only on the surface of the fruit or on the pit inside, or from fruit to fruit — forbidden; and some forbid even if it died inside the fruit and separated afterward. As long as it is found inside the fruit — even if its hole is open outward we are not concerned it separated and returned (and some forbid if its hole is open outward) (Mordechai Avodah Zarah; ShaDa in name of Raavan and R' Aviyah in name of R' Yoel brought in Terumat HaDeshen siman 171) (and so is practiced) (Terumat HaDeshen there and Hagahot ShaDa).
