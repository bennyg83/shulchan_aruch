Even if it did not reach the ground. See Pleiti, where I wrote that this means it entered the air after leaving the fruit completely, and one caught it in his mouth from the air. However, if it emerged only partly beyond the hole into the air, while the remainder was still inside the hole, that is considered its dwelling and it is permitted; see there.
Even if its hole was perforated, the Acharonim permit it, and I ruled likewise in Pleiti; see there. Even if the hole opened to the outside, Pri Chadash wondered why there should be concern when only part of it emerged into the air. See Pleiti, for this presents no difficulty.
