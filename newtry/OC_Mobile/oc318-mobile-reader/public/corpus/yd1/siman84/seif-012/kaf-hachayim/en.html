(12) There. [Rama: gloss.] But the prohibition against drawing with a vessel, and so forth, applies only to pits, shichin, and caves. But it is permitted to draw from a large vessel into a small vessel, for even if they later separate in the second vessel, they remain permitted. Shach, s.k. 4; Beit Lechem Yehuda, there; Lechem HaPanim, there; Mishbetzot Zahav, ot 4; Chochmat Adam, there; Zera Yaakov Tzvi, ot 9. And this is not like the Peliti, who forbids; see below, seif 3, at the end of the gloss.
