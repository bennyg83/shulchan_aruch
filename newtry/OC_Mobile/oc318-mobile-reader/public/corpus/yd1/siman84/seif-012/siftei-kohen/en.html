Its way is to keep intact the whole things embedded within it. — But cut things that fall into it — its way is to quickly dissolve and consume them. So wrote the Rashba in responsum siman 80; and so R' Yonah in chapter Keitzad Mevarechin wrote that the nature of honey is to restore the piece of forbidden food that fell into it back to honey; and even the Rosh there implies he only disputes this on the grounds that perhaps even though it dissolved it gives a pleasant taste, as Bach wrote in Orach Chayyim siman 216 — but he agrees it dissolves in honey. And even though here nothing practical follows from this — for a cut ant is not called a "creature," as below siman 102, seif 6 — nevertheless, regarding a sheretz that is not nullified even in the size of a lentil, as below siman 104, seif 1 in the gloss — in honey it is nullified; see there.
