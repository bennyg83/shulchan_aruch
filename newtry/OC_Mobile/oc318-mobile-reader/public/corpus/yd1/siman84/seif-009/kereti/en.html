And some say. Concerning worms found in fruit after twelve months, where the concern is that perhaps they separated and crawled, the Shach is lenient, and his ruling is correct.
A food from which they cannot be removed. An incident occurred in which a seller sold raisins to a certain woman, and she prepared a dish from them. He then sold some to others as well. The raisins remaining in the seller's possession and those held by the other purchasers were inspected, and all were found to be infested with milben. It was not known whether the raisins that had been in the woman's possession were likewise infested. The Taz permitted her dish on the basis of a double doubt: perhaps those raisins were not infested, and even if they were, perhaps the insects dissolved. End quote. Nekudos HaKesef and Pri Toar challenged this ruling. In truth, if this occurred during an unusually hot period, when infestation is extremely common, the food is forbidden. Otherwise, as I concluded in Pleiti, I agree with the Taz that it is unquestionably permitted.
