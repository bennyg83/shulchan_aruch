If one transgressed and cooked within twelve months without checking — if one can check, one checks; if not, it is permitted; and if some are found in the pot, one discards them and eats the rest (for there is no fruit without sixty against the taste of the worm within) (responsum of Rosh in name of Maharam). And some say this applies only when one or two are found, but three or four — all forbidden. {Rama: And specifically food that cannot be strained to remove worms; and since the whole is presumed wormy, all is forbidden; but if one can strain and remove found worms, the rest is permitted (Rashba siman 150); see below end of siman 100.}
