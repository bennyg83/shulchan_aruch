Provided that it crawled. Pri Chadash maintains that it is forbidden even if it merely moved. See Pleiti for what I wrote about this at length, and for my explanation of Rashi's intent. It appears explicitly from Rashi's words that actual crawling is required. Nevertheless, one should be stringent; see there.
Like a black dot. Although there was no crawling here, the Shach inferred from this that the Rama forbids the worm itself even though it did not separate. Since it has not yet been cooked and the black dot can still be removed, we are stringent. However, if it was already cooked together with the black dot, the food should not be forbidden after the fact.
