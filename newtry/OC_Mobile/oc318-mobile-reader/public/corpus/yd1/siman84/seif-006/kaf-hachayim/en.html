(6) And anything that people ordinarily find repulsive is included in the prohibition of lo teshaktzu. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz, end of ot 4; Pri Chadash, ot 3; Lechem HaPanim, there; Beit Lechem Yehuda, ot 1. And if it is repulsive to him personally, even though it is not repulsive to everyone, it is certainly forbidden to him, and lo teshaktzu properly applies to him. Pri Chadash, there; Lechem HaPanim, there; Beit Lechem Yehuda, there. And see below, siman 104, ot 3, and siman 116, seif 6.
