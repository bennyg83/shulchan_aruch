Worms that grow in fruit while still attached are considered like sheretz haShoretz al haAretz and forbidden even if they did not separate — provided they swarmed; but worms found in beans and peas under the shell while the shell covers them from outside — when the shell is removed and yabchushin are found underneath, they are permitted because they lie in a narrow place and we do not call them sheretz haShoretz al haAretz as long as they did not swarm; but those found in stalks are forbidden because they have room to swarm. {Rama: Worms in mushrooms and truffles have the same law as other fruit and we do not say they are not considered attached to the ground because we do not bless on them borei pri haAdamah (logical opinion, unlike Or Zarua). And sometimes a black dot is found in fruit where the worm begins to form and one must remove it deeply, for it is forbidden like the worm itself (responsum Rashba siman 275).}
