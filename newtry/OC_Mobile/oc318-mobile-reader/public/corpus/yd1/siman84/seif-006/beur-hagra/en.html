Worms, etc. Per Shmuel at the beginning of that perek, chapter 58, who holds like him; and Tosafot there, s.v. deika, etc.:
And it, etc.—for we require "that creeps." There:
(Likkut) And it stirred, etc. But in Terumat HaDeshen, part 2, he argues on this and wrote: for per Shmuel it is like that which grows on the ground; and thus a cucumber that became wormy without specification, etc.; and thus in the gloss: and sometimes, etc. (end):
But those found, etc. — like a cucumber, etc. Ibid.
Worms that grow, etc. As written in Berachot 40b, Nedarim 55b, Mirva, etc.:
And sometimes, etc. So too the Rashba holds; and he holds we do not require that it stirred, unlike the view of Tosafot and the Rosh and the Shulchan Aruch in siman 275:
That it begins, etc. — like an egg in which blood is found in the yolk, which is forbidden; and like a mouse whose flesh is partly mixed with impure flesh — all the more so, for the purpose of the prohibition, as stated in chapter 1 of Yom Tov (7a): it proliferates, etc. Ibid.
