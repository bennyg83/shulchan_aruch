Types of birds, etc. — And Maharshal, chapter Ha'or veHaRotev, siman 9, ruled like Mordechai in the name of Rabbenu Tam — that they are permitted through shechitah; but the consensus of the poskim is like the Mechaber, and so too the view of the latter authorities. And so wrote Orchot Chaim at the end of klal 9 in the name of many poskim, writing that so is our custom; and so ruled Tur Choshen Mishpat at the end of klal 46.
