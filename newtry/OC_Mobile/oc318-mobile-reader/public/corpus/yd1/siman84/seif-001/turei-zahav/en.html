In the Tur. The Tur writes that the Rambam permits only creatures found in cisterns, while the Rosh does not say so. In my opinion, the Rambam also follows the school of Rav. The dispute concerns channels that neither spring naturally nor flow. The school of Rav treats them like cisterns, while the school of R' Yishmael prohibits them because their large size differs from an ordinary vessel. Flowing channels, however, are prohibited according to everyone, even if they do not spring from the ground. The Rambam's doubled language, “they do not spring and are confined like vessels,” teaches that lack of a spring is insufficient; they must also be stationary. Cisterns are mentioned because they are ordinarily confined, whereas channels vary. The Rosh's wording does not explicitly equate flowing channels with springs, but the Ran indicates that the main novelty is precisely that flowing water is treated like spring water.
