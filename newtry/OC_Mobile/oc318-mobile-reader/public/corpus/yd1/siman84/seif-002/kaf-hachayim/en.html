(2) There. And in pits—shichin, caves, etc. A bor is round. A shich is long and narrow. A me'arah is square and covered with a roof, except that it has an opening. Charitzin are wide and square like a me'arah, and are not roofed, but their entire mouth is open. Neitzin are short below and wide above. Rashi at the beginning of 72b, and the Ran. Bach.
