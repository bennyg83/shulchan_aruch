Marriage. The Bach wrote that this is the explanation: if brothers or sisters are making marriages for themselves, even though one is greater than the other in wisdom, they should not advance the younger in years to marry because he is greater in wisdom; rather, they should advance the elder in years to marry, etc.
Before. It should read: provided that he too is somewhat learned, as in the first clause, and all the more so; and it also speaks where the young man is wiser than he, for otherwise it is obvious. So wrote Maharshal and the Perishah.
