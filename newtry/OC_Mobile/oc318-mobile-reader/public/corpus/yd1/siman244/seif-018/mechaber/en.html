If they were together — a Torah scholar greatly advanced in wisdom who is a youth, and an elder greatly advanced in age who is somewhat wise in a court of law or of Torah — they follow wisdom to seat the wise youth at the head and speak first; and at a feast or wedding feast they follow old age to seat him at the head; and if the Torah scholar is greatly advanced in wisdom and the elder is not greatly advanced in age — everywhere they follow wisdom; and if the elder is greatly advanced in age and the Torah scholar is not greatly advanced in wisdom — everywhere they follow old age, since he is somewhat wise; and if neither is advanced — neither in wisdom nor in age — the elder takes precedence everywhere.
