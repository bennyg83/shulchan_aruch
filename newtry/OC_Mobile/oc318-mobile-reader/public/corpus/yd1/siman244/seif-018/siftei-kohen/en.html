Or regarding marriages, etc. Bach explains that if brothers or sisters are to be married, even if the younger is greater in wisdom, the younger should not be married first for that reason. Rather, the elder in years should be married first.
The elder takes precedence everywhere. This too requires that the elder possess some wisdom, all the more so. It also concerns a case where the young man is wiser, for otherwise the ruling would be obvious. So write Maharshal and Perishah. Ran cites those who disagree and maintain that where neither is exceptionally distinguished, it is treated as though both are distinguished: in a study setting wisdom determines precedence, while at a banquet age determines precedence.
