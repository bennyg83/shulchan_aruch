"And if the wise man is, and if the old man is gone." Madammar and he is a sailor and he is a sailor, and the other is not prepared for everything else:
And if there is no, etc. — there is no need that he be equal to their error in extremes, for then why does he say "and he is distinguished," etc. — it should have said "in such a case"; rather certainly we follow everywhere after one of them and we do not know which; and Rashbam wrote it is reasonable after the elder; and Beit HaGadol in his name, and all this the Rosh also wrote.
