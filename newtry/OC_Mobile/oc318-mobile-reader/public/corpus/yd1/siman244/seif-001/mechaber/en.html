Standing and showing honor before a Torah scholar even if he is not one's teacher. And it contains 18 seifim.
It is a positive mitzvah to stand before every Torah scholar — even if he is not elderly but is young and wise, and even if he is not one's teacher: {Rama: (Only that he is greater than him and fit to learn from him) (Tur in the name of Rambam and Ran, first chapter of Kiddushin).} And likewise it is a mitzvah to stand before old age — that is, seventy years: {Rama: (Even if he is an am ha'aretz, provided he is not wicked) (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot, Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 6, Mordechai first chapter of Kiddushin, Rabbeinu Yerucham, Ran, and Rabbeinu Tam).}
