(Siman 244 seif 1) A positive mitzvah to stand before every sage, etc.; and even if he is not his teacher. And he wrote in the gloss: only that he is greater than him and fit to learn from him. And in the chapter "if he knows his fellow is greater than him" he is obligated to treat him with honor; and Tosafot wrote even if he learned nothing from him. Perforce this is general honor; but to stand — however only when he is also greater than ordinary people, as Shach wrote.
