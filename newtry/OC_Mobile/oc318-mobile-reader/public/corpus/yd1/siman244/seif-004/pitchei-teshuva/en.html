One does not stand before them — Lechem Mishneh wrote in chapter 6 of Laws of Torah Study that this law applies even to an outstanding teacher; and see in Arba'ah Turim Even on Rambam there who disagrees and holds that specifically for a teacher who is not outstanding, or for another sage, or for an elder, these laws apply; but for an outstanding teacher who is included in "fear," one must stand before him even in the bathhouse, and so for one's father; and that craftsmen are not obligated or permitted to stand — that is specifically before a sage or a teacher who is not outstanding, but before an outstanding teacher they must stand; therefore Rambam did not bring these laws in the earlier chapter that deals with an outstanding teacher; and so Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote these laws in siman 244 and not in siman 242 — see there.
