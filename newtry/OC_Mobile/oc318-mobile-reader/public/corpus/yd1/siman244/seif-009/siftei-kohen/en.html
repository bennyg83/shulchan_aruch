He does not stand, etc. It implies it is forbidden to stand until he reaches within his four amot, for if to inform us he is not obligated — it was already explained above seif 2; and although for nasi and av beit din and muvhak teacher one stands as far as his eye sees — evidently there is honor even in a distant place; there certainly since we obligate standing before him as far as his eye sees there is honor; but for an ordinary sage, since there is no obligation, the matter is not proven that he stands from before him, rather it appears as if he stands for his need. Bach.
