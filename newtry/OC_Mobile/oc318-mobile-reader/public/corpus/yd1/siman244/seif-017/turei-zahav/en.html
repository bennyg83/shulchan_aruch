They enter and sit, etc. This "they enter" does not mean stepping on the heads of the holy people — they did not permit this except for the need of the holy people; rather this is the meaning: their father has permission to take his sons with them at the time of their entry and hold his sons at their place of sitting; and they also said in the Gemara that even at a feast they seat the children beside the elders because of their father's honor. So it appears to me.
