Av Beit Din. The Shach wrote that in our time there is neither nasi nor Av Beit Din. Although communities accept heads of yeshivot and an Av Beit Din, they have only the law of a sage. Now the practice is to stand only before a head of yeshiva and an Av Beit Din, and perhaps because one should not distinguish between one sage and another, they made their measures equal: anyone who holds a yeshiva or is Av Beit Din is called a sage for whom one stands. This still requires study; end quote.
