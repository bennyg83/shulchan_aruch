The head of the beit din, etc. Semak, siman 32, writes that the laws of a nasi and head of the beit din are not needed today. This implies that in our times there is no one with the formal status of nasi or head of the beit din. Even when communities appoint heads of yeshivos and heads of batei din, they have only the status of a sage. For this reason the custom today is not to form a row for the head of a beit din, as discussed below in seif 15. Nowadays the custom is to stand only for a rosh yeshivah or head of a beit din. Perhaps, to avoid distinctions among sages, the practice was standardized that anyone who heads a yeshivah or serves as head of a beit din is treated as a sage for whom one stands. This still requires study.
