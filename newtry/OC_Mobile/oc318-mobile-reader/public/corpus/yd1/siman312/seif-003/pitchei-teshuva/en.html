Blemishes on Yom Tov. [The details of this law are explained in Orach Chaim siman 498 seif 9. And what Taz wrote in s.k. 9: one should learn from this that if there was a clear issur, for example liquid issur fell into liquid heter in less than sixty, or dry into dry and there is no majority of heter, on erev Yom Tov, and afterward more heter was added on Yom Tov and the issur was nullified, one may not rule on this on Yom Tov, for it is exactly like a bechor, and one may not call a sage about this on Yom Tov, end of his words; see there. And see Sefer Korban Netanel, Beitzah chapter 3 letter 33, who wrote upon him: I have never seen elder rabbis who were concerned for this, and the case is not comparable to the proof at all; see there. And see Peri Megadim above siman 99, Siftei Da'at s.k. 11 on this.]
