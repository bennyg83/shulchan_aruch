Blemishes on Yom Tov. The details of this law are explained in Orach Chayim siman 498 seif 9. As for what the Taz wrote in s.k. 9, that one can learn from here that if there was a definite prohibition, such as forbidden liquid falling into permitted liquid in less than sixty, or dry into dry without a permitted majority before Yom Tov, and afterward more permitted substance was added on Yom Tov and the prohibition became nullified, it is forbidden to rule on this on Yom Tov, for it is exactly like a bekhor, and one may not call a sage for this on Yom Tov, see there. See Korban Netanel, Beitzah chapter 3 note 33, who wrote against him that he never saw the elder rabbis concerned for this, and the case is not comparable to the proof at all; see there. See also Peri Megadim above, siman 99, Siftei Da'at s.k. 11, regarding this.
