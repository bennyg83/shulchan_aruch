There is no wise man who sees himself. The reason for my suspicion is that there is no man who has written, and that he is not a man who is cursed, and that he is not allowed to do so; I have seen that in the midst of Hashem’s wrath, and that he will not be able to do so
I subsequently saw that in chapter 2 of tractate Nega'im, R' Shimshon also raised this difficulty and distinguished between where the prohibition was already established [itchazek issura], etc. — and by this it is also resolved in the case of one who examines himself: since [the animal] was slaughtered and thereby permitted, the prohibition had not been established beforehand. And if you would say: if so, he would not be trusted regarding the slaughter even for himself — since in its lifetime an animal stands under a presumption of prohibition; regarding this we rely on the majority [who hold] that those engaged in slaughter are generally expert, as Mordechai wrote at the beginning of [tractate] Chullin — see there.
