The law of one who takes wages to inspect a firstborn. In it are 3 seifim. One who takes wages to be an inspector of firstborns—they do not slaughter based on him unless he was a great expert and the sages knew of him that there is none like him, and they fixed wages for him for inspection and examination; and they must give him his wages whether the blemish is permanent and he permits it, or whether it is not permanent. And they do not give him wages for the animal except once—and he inspects it forever whenever they bring it, so that he not come to suspicion.
