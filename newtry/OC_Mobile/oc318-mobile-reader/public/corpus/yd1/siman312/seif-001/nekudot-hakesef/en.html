(Siman 312, Taz, seif katan 1) If so, the Tur requires study, etc. The Derishah already preceded him with this question against the Tur. The Rosh is likewise difficult, for his words in chapter Elu Metziot conflict with his words at the end of chapter Ad Kamah. The Maadanei Yom Tov already raised this issue and left it requiring study. The Taz offered an answer. I could have answered more effectively as follows: At the end of Elu Metziot, regarding unloading an animal, the Rosh distinguishes between whether the owners were present. When they are present, one is paid only for complete loss of idle time. Since the owners were present and no stipulation was made, he receives nothing for the effort of the additional labor; see there. The Tur writes likewise in Choshen Mishpat, siman 265. In the cases of a firstborn animal and testimony, the owners are present, and therefore the expert receives only compensation for being completely idle. This would appear to answer the question. However, the Rosh’s wording in chapter Ad Kamah, “and now he does not receive payment for the testimony,” does not imply this. This is also difficult according to the Taz and requires study.
