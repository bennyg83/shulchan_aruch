(Siman 312, Taz seif katan 1) If so, Tur requires study, and so forth. Derishah already preceded him with this question on Tur, and the same difficulty applies to Rosh from his words in Perek Elu Metziot compared with his words at the end of Perek Ad Kamah. Ma'adanei Yom Tov already noticed this and left it unresolved. Taz offers an answer. I could have answered more effectively as follows: at the end of Perek Elu Metziot, regarding unloading an animal, Rosh distinguishes whether the owners are present. When they are present, the helper is paid only for completely idle time, because since the owners are there and he did not stipulate, he receives no additional payment for the labor itself. Tur rules similarly in Choshen Mishpat siman 265. Accordingly, with a firstborn animal and testimony, the owners are present, so he receives only compensation for complete loss of time. This seemed to me a possible answer. Yet Rosh's language in Perek Ad Kamah, "and now he does not receive payment for the testimony," does not fit it well. This is also difficult according to Taz. The matter requires study.
