From his head etc. This is not precisely stated, for it was explained in the preceding siman that it is forbidden to round his head and beard — rather it means from the top of his body to the end of his body — Perishah.
That it is permitted etc. For certainly he intends it for healing, since if for beauty — on the contrary it is disfiguring for him; and perhaps even if he explicitly states he is doing it for beauty — we take no account of him but say certainly he is doing it for healing — Beit Yosef; and likewise Bach, except that his words appear to disagree regarding one who explicitly says he is doing it for beauty — see there.
