Witness adornment. Turei Zahav wrote that it implies adornment and ornamentation is forbidden, but if one does so because of sun or cold and rain there is no prohibition; and Bach wrote that one should forbid this, and one who listens to him will come to a blessing, for there are many stumbling blocks, Heaven forbid, through this when they walk together without distinguishing man and woman; and regarding Purim joy see Orach Chaim siman 696.
