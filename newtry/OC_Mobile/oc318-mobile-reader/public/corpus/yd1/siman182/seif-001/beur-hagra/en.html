One who passes a razor, etc. According to Peri Chadash in Nazir 58b, R. Mikal, etc., like a razor — and according to this version it is forbidden only in the armpit and pubic area with a razor, but with scissors even like a razor it is permitted, and the rest of the body even with a razor is permitted, for the Gemara's difficulty was only on what permits the entire body even in the armpit and pubic area; and Tosafot there 59a s.v. that man, etc., and thus is the version of the Arukh. But the version of Beit HaGadol is from the baraita, etc., he receives lashes, because Rav said regarding other limbs, and regarding other limbs who permits; and it is taught: passing a razor is not from the Torah but from the Sages; rather because Rav said with scissors, and behold Rav also like a razor, etc.; and therefore other limbs are equal to hair in the armpit and pubic area, that they are forbidden with a razor and permitted with scissors like a razor. But in the fixed halakhot 33, rather because, etc., only because Rav said, etc., and he did not retract from the first change that in the armpit and pubic area they are forbidden even with scissors like a razor; and Tosafot there s.v. the above and s.v. Rabbah bar bar Chanah, etc., and even though they wrote there that even though he does not learn only, one can say he explains "rather" — nevertheless he does not hold so; and see there s.v. he asked, etc., and in Yevamot 45a s.v. not, etc., and likewise the Rosh, even though in Yevamot he wrote like the above words of Tosafot that one can say he explains like "rather nevertheless," in Makkot he wrote like the words of Tosafot in Yevamot, and thus is the opinion of the Rambam, and these are the words of the Shulchan Arukh; and Tosafot there 58a s.v. I see, etc., and s.v. that, etc., it implies, etc.
They would prepare him, etc. According to the first version of Rabbi Yochanan there, because of the baraita of passing hair, etc., like him; and the Gemara challenges from it to Rav, from which we learn that we hold like her, as the Rif wrote at the end of chapter 4 of Eruvin and Shulchan Arukh. Rambam and Tur and Shulchan Arukh; but Semag ruled like the last version of Rabbi Yochanan because Rabbi Avahu is like him, as explained above, and we hold like Rabbi Avahu (even in the baraita as written in Yevamot 37 seif 1), and see in Semag.
In another case, etc. From what is explained that one, etc., they left him, etc., it implies that the others practiced leniency; and this is what is written in the gloss, and even, etc., only, etc., and likewise the Ran; but the opinion of the Rambam is that nevertheless ab initio it is forbidden, only that they do not prepare them and they did not heed them in the matter.
