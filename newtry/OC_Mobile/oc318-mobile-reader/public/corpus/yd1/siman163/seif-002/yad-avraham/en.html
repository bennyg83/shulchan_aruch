(Seif 2) Behold he had wheat and made loan on him, etc., "go make them for me wine at market price now if he has wine" — permitted; if not forbidden. Know this deals where wheat is charged to him in value saying such is wheat value give me wine — like loan forbidden when he does not have; even if initially gave money for wheat; but when says plainly so-and-so seah wheat I have with you give me so much wine — permitted even if no wine as giving money now for wine. All explained in Talmud, Rashi, Tosafot, Ran, Nimukei Yosef, Beit Yosef, Hagah siman 175 seif 6 — see there; plainly proven Tosafot Eizehu Neshekh (62b) s.v. lakach, etc.: if initially when fixed on wheat he did not have and fixed in prohibition — forbidden afterward to give wine at wheat value as now appreciated even if he has wine.
