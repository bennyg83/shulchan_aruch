The law of one who sets a price of wheat to obligate him at today’s rate; it contains 3 se’ifim.
One who was owed money by his fellow, and said to him, “Give me my money, for I want to buy wheat with it,” and he said to him, “Go and make it for me at today’s rate, and you will have with me wheat as a loan”: if he has wheat corresponding to the amount of his money, it is permitted, even if the market rate was not set; but if not, it is forbidden, even if the market rate was set (Nimukei Yosef in the name of Ramban, and Maggid Mishneh there; and so wrote Ramban and Rashba). (And the seller is believed to say that he has [wheat], and no proof is required for his words) (Hagahos Mordechai, perek Eizehu Neshech), as above siman 162.
