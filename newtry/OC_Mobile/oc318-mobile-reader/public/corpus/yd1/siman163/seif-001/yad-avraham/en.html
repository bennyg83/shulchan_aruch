(Siman 163 seif 1) One who was creditor in money, etc., "go make them for me," etc., "you will have wheat on loan with me if he has wheat," etc., "permitted even if price did not go out." And gives cheaply — he cheapens for him. Beit Yosef in name of Nimukei Yosef and Ramban. Question: above siman 160 seif 2 Mechaber wrote if gives more at repayment unstipulated — forbidden; and see there seif 4 s.k. 14 — if so since gives cheaply it is ribbit on money he lent. Also proven Rambam ch. 8 Laws of Lending regarding field guards who pay rent in wheat cheap at threshing floor — forbidden and presumably price not yet out — nevertheless forbidden to give cheap; all the more here it is loan — must say we permit here only he gives at cheap price customary to fix at this price like gleaning price as Tosafot Eizehu Neshekh (63a), Rosh, Tur siman 175; and since customary to fix thus not recognizable he cheapens due to loan — even if now gave money would cheapen thus; similar below siman 173 seif 2 — see there; unlike field guards who gave much cheaper due to advance rent payment stipulation — forbidden in all cases as stated there siman 160 seif 2 s.k. 14 — see there.
