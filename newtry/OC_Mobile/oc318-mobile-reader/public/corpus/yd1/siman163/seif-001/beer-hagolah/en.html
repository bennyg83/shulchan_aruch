A baraita of Rabbi Oshaya, Bava Metzia 63a, using Rambam's language in chapter 4, law 10, of the Laws of Creditor and Debtor.
Rashi explains that the purchaser acquires it from now. Although he has not taken physical possession, when the price rises, it rises in his domain and the arrangement does not constitute interest, since if the seller seeks to retract, he is subject to the curse imposed upon one who fails to honor a transaction.
Since the seller does not possess the goods and the purchaser has not now given him money with which to buy them, the arrangement resembles interest.
