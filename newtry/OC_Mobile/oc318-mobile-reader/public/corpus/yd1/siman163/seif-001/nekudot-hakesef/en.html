(Siman 163, Taz, seif katan 7) It appears obvious to me, etc., and it resembles what is written in siman 174, seif 2, etc. He was not precise. There, since it is a sale, the buyer properly consumes the produce. Here, however, the borrower first said, “Lend me one hundred,” so there is certainly an evasion of ribbit, or actual rabbinic ribbit according to the Ramban. This is readily understood.
