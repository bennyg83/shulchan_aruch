(Siman 163, Taz seif katan 7) It appears obvious to me, and it resembles what is written in siman 174, seif 2, and so forth. This is imprecise. There, because the transaction is a genuine sale, the purchaser properly eats the produce. Here, however, where he first said, "Lend me one hundred," there is certainly an evasion of ribbit, or actual ribbit according to Ramban.
