He said to him, “Lend me a maneh.” He said to him, “I do not have a maneh; I have wheat for a maneh.” And he gave him wheat for a maneh at the market rate, and then he went back and bought it from him for 90 (and they are still worth 100) (Beit Yosef in the name of Tanna D’vei R' Yishmael). If in the end he repaid him the maneh with produce, it is permitted; but it is forbidden to repay with money, because of subterfuge of interest, since he initially said to him, “Lend me a maneh.” And if he transgressed and did so, behold he extracts from him 100 in beis din, for there is not even avak ribbis here. {Rama: And some say that it is avak ribbis (Tur in the name of Ramban and Rosh). And all this is only when they did not stipulate initially upon this; but if they stipulated initially to buy from him for less, it is full-fledged interest (Maggid Mishneh, perek 5 of Hilchos Malveh V’Loveh, in the name of Ramban and Rashba; and so it is in Nimukei Yosef), even if he repaid him produce it is forbidden (Nimukei Yosef, perek Eizehu Neshech).}
