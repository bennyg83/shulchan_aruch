In produce. This applies only after he performed meshichah on the wheat or acquired it through another complete kinyan. Otherwise it appears as interest, even if he first requested a wheat loan and later repays the money in produce. Whether produce stipulated for repayment may instead be repaid in money depends upon the dispute in siman 175:6.
A maneh. But if he said, 'Lend me wheat,' it is permitted. Rosh and Tur.
Complete. Bach understands the prohibition as resembling fixed interest rather than being literal fixed interest. Taz writes that if the purchase and resale occur at separate times, it is fully permitted. Nekudot HaKesef disagrees where the transaction began as a request for a money loan, viewing it as an evasion of interest or avak ribbit.
