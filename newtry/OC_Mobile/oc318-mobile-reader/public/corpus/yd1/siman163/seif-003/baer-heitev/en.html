Complete. The Bach writes that the language of the poskim implies that this is only like complete ribit, but not actual complete ribit. The Taz writes that it is obvious to him that the prohibition in this seif is where it was all done at one time. But if, an hour after he bought the wheat for one hundred, he returned and sold it to him for ninety, it is completely permitted, and resembles what is written in siman 174 seif 2; see there. In Nekudot HaKesef he writes that this is not precise, for there, since it is a sale, the buyer properly consumes the produce; but here, where he first said, "lend me a maneh," there is certainly an evasion of ribit, or avak ribit according to the Ramban.
