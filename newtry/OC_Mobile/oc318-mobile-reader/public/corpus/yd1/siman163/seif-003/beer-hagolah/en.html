From Abaye's interpretation of the Mishnah in accordance with what Rav Safra taught in the baraita concerning interest from the school of Rabbi Chiya, etc., there on 62b. Although that interpretation was rejected, its underlying reasoning was not rejected. This is the consensus of the halachic authorities.
Tur in the name of Rambam, chapter 5 of the Laws of Creditor and Debtor. Rashba likewise ruled this way based on the language of the baraita. See also Maggid Mishneh and Nimukei Yosef.
