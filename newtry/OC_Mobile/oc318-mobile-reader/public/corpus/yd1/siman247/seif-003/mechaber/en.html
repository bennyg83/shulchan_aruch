Whoever has mercy on the poor — the Holy One, blessed be He, has mercy on him: {Rama: And a person should put it in his heart that he seeks his sustenance every hour from the Holy One, blessed be He; and just as he asks that the Holy One, blessed be He, hear his cry — so he should hear the cry of the poor. Also he should put in his heart that he is a revolving wheel in the world, and in the end a person will come — he or his son or his son's son — to this measure; and whoever has mercy on others — they have mercy on him. (From the wording of Tur)}
