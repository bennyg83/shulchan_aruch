In giving maaser. See in She'elat Yaavetz part 1 siman 3, who challenged the Rema, for from his words it appears he speaks of maaser kesafim that is practiced nowadays; but in truth this is not said regarding maaser kesafim, for it is not permitted to test with it, for it is only charity — see there; and likewise the Gaon author of Shelah, and he is in the Amsterdam edition page 242, that specifically regarding maaser tevuah it is permitted to test, but not regarding maaser kesafim — see there; and likewise agreed in Sefer Mishnat Chachamim Laws of Foundations of the Torah page 17 side 1 — see there.
