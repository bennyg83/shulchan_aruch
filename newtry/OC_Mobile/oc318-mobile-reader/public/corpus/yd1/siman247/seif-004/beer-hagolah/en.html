Language of the Tur from the incident of Shmuel and Avalt — Shabbat page 157 side 2. The Rokeach wrote in siman 217: what the poskim rule, charity on behalf of the dead on Yom Kippur — they have a support, for it is written in the portion of Tetzaveh "the kapparot once a year atones," and next to it "and they shall give each man kapparah for his soul"; and he gave a reason for both: what does it help the dead that the living gives charity on his behalf; and he wrote that if given on behalf of a wicked person it does not help; and see in Orach Chayyim siman 621 what is written, and the Rema also brought it in siman 249.
