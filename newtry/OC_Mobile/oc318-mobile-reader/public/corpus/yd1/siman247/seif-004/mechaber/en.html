Charity pushes away harsh decrees and in famine saves from death, as happened to the woman of Tzarephath: {Rama: And it is tithed, and it is forbidden to test the Holy One, blessed be He, except in this matter, as it says, "Test Me now in this," etc. (Tur from the Gemara beginning of Taanit.)
