(Likkut) The custom, etc. Even though in ch. 15 of Shabbat (114a) R. Yannai said to him, etc. — in the Yerushalmi (end of Kilayim and ch. 12 of Ketubot) they said: R. Yoshiyah commanded to dress him in white sewn garments; they said to him, "Are you better than your master?" He said, "And am I ashamed of his deeds?" R. Yirmiyah commanded to dress him in white sewn garments; they gave him, etc. — and from here the custom spread to dress all in white.
