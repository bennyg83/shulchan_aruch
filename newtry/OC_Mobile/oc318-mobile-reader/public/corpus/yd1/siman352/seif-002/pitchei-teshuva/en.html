In white garments. The Levush specifies white linen garments, as do the Tur and Rambam. Rambam implies that even the sewing thread should be linen and no other material. Yalkut Reuveni records a mystical virtue in burial solely in linen garments, without any other fabric, even a single foreign thread. See Tzemach Menachem and Ahavat Yonatan.
