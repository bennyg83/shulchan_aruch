In white garments. The Levush reads: "in white linen garments." So too the Tur in the name of Rambam, and in Rambam, Hilchot Avel, chapter 4, it appears that even the thread used for sewing should be linen and not another material. Yalkut Reuveni, Parashat Miketz, states that there is a great mystical secret in wearing a linen garment: every deceased person clothed in linen garments, even if he died outside Eretz Yisrael, has every accuser transformed into an advocate. The virtue applies when linen is worn without another garment. Even an infant whose father has repeatedly lost children through Lilith or an evil spirit is, by virtue of these garments, protected from them. The segulah of linen garments is when one wears linen alone and no other material with it; if another kind is mixed in, even a single thread, it invalidates the effect. See Tzemach Menachem, Parashat Shelach, and Ahavat Yehonatan on that parashah.
