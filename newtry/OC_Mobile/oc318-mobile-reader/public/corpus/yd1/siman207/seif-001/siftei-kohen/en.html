Synonyms of vows, etc. — Rambam wrote in chapter 1 of Laws of Vows, law 16: there are places whose people are playful and corrupt the language and designate one thing by another — there one follows the designation; how so — any synonym of korban is like korban: one who says "these are upon me, konam, konach, konas" — these are synonyms of korban; and so all similar cases — one follows the language of the people of that place at that time — end. And so Semag in negative precepts, Rama, Ramban, and Ran at the beginning of Niddah in the name of the great sage R. Yitzchak bar R. Chisdai: we are taught that not only in gentile languages that are complete languages is one who vows in them bound by his vow, but even in languages that are not complete, such as these konam konach — which are holy tongue but corrupted — one might think one who vows in them is not bound since it is not a complete vow language in itself — it teaches that since gentiles speak them, even though it is not from their root language but from corruption in their hands, it is a complete vow; and all the more other gentile languages — end. And such are the words of Tur and the Rav; and see siman 237 in the gloss.
