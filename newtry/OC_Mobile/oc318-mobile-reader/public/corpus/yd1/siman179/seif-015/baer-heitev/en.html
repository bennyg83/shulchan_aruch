The eyes. The Bach wrote in the name of the Rambam that even sleight of the eyes that is not through sorcery, but only through appearance and stratagems by quick movement of the hand, is forbidden.
Formation. The Levush wrote: but for this one needs holiness and purity, which, because of our sins, is not found in these generations. About this it is said, "one who uses the crown passes away"; therefore one who refrains is blessed.
