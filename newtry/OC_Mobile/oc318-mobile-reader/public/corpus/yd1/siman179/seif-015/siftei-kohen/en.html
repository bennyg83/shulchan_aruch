And through Sefer Yetzirah it is permitted. To do ab initio, for the holy names are and the Holy One blessed be He gave in them power that the pious and prophets can act through them, and the one who acts through them shows the greatness and might of the Holy One blessed be He His name; only that they should engage in them in holiness and purity, and for the sake of sanctification of the Name or for the sake of a great mitzvah — which is not found in these generations, woe to us; and even in their times we find Yeshayahu was punished for this, and all the more so in our time that it is impossible to conduct in purity and holiness, and the Merciful One requires the heart — end of his words; Pri Chadash, and his words are correct; and likewise it is found in many authors of hidden wisdom that one should not use the holy names except for the sake of a great mitzvah; and one who uses the crown in vain; and likewise the Rav wrote below siman 246 end of seif 21, and one who uses the crown in vain; some say this is one who uses the holy names; also in accepted Kabbalah books it is explained that great is the punishment of one who uses His name — end; one who refrains will be blessed.
