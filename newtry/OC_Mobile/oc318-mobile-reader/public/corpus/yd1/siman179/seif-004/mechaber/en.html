House, child, and woman: even though it is not nichush, it is a sign — if it succeeded after this 3 times or not (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashi). {Rama: And likewise, it is permitted to say to a child, “Pesok li pesukecha” (Tur). Some say that a person is permitted to make for himself a sign regarding something that will come in the future, like Eliezer the servant of Avraham did, or Yehonasan (Tur and R' David Kimchi), and there are those who forbid (Rambam and Semag). And one who walks in innocence and trusts in Hashem — kindness will surround him.}
