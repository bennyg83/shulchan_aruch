House etc. Rashi explains: he built a house, or a son was born to him, or he married a woman — although there is no divination that is forbidden to divine and rely upon, there is such a thing as general omens; for if he succeeds in his first business after he built the house, or the child was born, or he married the woman — it is a sign that he will continue to succeed; and if not — he should not make a habit of it excessively, for there is reason to fear he will not succeed.
And likewise permitted etc. It implies the view of the Rabbi and poskim that even to take action and rely on it for the future based on a verse is permitted, for it is considered a minor form of prophecy.
