The practice is that one does not begin on the 2nd [day] and on the 4th [day], and one does not marry women except at the filling of the moon. {Rama: Therefore the practice is also to begin learning at Rosh Chodesh, for even though there is no nichush, there is a sign (Semak, siman 136). Regarding something that a person knows is against the mazal, he should not do it and should not rely on a miracle; however, one should not investigate after this because of “Tamim tihyeh” (a responsum of Ramban, siman 286), as was explained.}
