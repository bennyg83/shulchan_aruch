From diviners. Mishkenot Yaakov seeks to justify those who consult sorcerers for a sick person. The Zohar may follow Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai, who holds that Noahides are commanded regarding sorcery; since practical halakhah does not follow him, the issue may be limited to verbal consultation and the requirement to be wholehearted with Hashem, like consulting astrologers. This may be permitted for illness, especially danger to life, though one who guards his soul should distance himself from them.
