Regarding diviners. See Beit HaTalmid; and see in the responsum Mishkenot Yaakov siman 39, who learned merit for those who practice leniency to inquire of sorcerers for the sake of a sick person — one can say that the Zohar on this follows his view that Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai held in the Gemara in Sanhedrin that a descendant of Noah is commanded regarding sorcery, etc.; but we hold not like Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai — if so, there is only a prohibition of speech and the prohibition of "you shall be wholehearted" like asking Chaldeans; perhaps for a sick person it is permitted, especially for a sick person where there is danger; nevertheless one who guards his soul should distance from them — see there.
