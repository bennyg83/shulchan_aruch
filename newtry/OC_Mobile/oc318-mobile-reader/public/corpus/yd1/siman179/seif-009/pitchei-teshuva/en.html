A child who was harmed. Baer Heitev limits the prohibition to cases without danger to life. Be'er Sheva permits placing a Torah scroll upon a woman in difficult labor and reciting verses because of the danger. Chinukh Beit Yosef objects to such degradation of the Torah scroll and allows bringing it only to the doorway of the room, so that the merit of Torah protect her, not as a magical cure, and certainly not placing it in her hands or opening it before her.
