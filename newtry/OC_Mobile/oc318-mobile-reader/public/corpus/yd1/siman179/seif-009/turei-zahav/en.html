An infant who was afflicted, etc. For then he requires healing, and words of Torah were not given for healing the body, but for healing the soul. This is not so regarding a healthy person, who needs only protection so that he will not become ill; it is permitted to protect him through words of Torah. For this reason, we recite Krias Shema upon one's bed, to protect him during the night. It is obvious that if an infant who was afflicted is in mortal danger, it is permitted even to heal him through words of Torah, as stated in seif 8.
