A child, etc. The Yerushalmi, chapter six of Shabbat, end of halakhah two, states: One may not recite a verse over a wound. One who recites over a mandrake is prohibited. Come and recite this verse over my son, who is terrified; place tefillin upon him so that he will sleep, this is prohibited. But was it not taught that they would recite the Tehillim against afflictions in Jerusalem? Rabbi Yudan said: Here, after one has been afflicted; there, before one has been afflicted. This accords with the Gemara in Shevuot cited above.
