One who burns incense, etc. Gemara there: one who burns incense for a demon; and Rivash wrote because of a medium; and likewise in the Gemara there; and Beit Yosef disagreed with him and wrote it is a scribal error and it should read because of idolatry; and Beit Yosef's words are astonishing, and Shach (besides that Shach's words are strained, behold in Keritot 3b it is found in Rava's words: burning incense for a demon in order to bind him).
One who learns, etc. End of chapter 7 of Shabbat, and it is explained there that specifically from a sect, but from a sorcerer it is permitted for everyone; and Rivash wrote that from both it is forbidden; and Beit Yosef wrote that because he is uncertain whether the halakhah is like Rava or like Shmuel — and his words are astonishing.
