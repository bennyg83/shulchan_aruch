One who burns incense, etc. The Gemara there discusses one who burns incense to a demon in order to bind it. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that the liability is because of the prohibition of an ov, and this is also the wording of the Gemara there. Beit Yosef disagrees and writes that it is a scribal error, and that the text should read that he is liable because of idolatry. Beit Yosef's words are astonishing. See Shach. Apart from the fact that Shach's explanation is strained, Keritot 3b explicitly records Rava's statement, 'One who burns incense to a demon in order to bind it.'
One who learns, etc. This is stated at the end of chapter seven of Shabbat. It is explained there that the prohibition applies specifically when one learns from a heretic, but learning from a sorcerer is permitted according to all opinions. Rabbeinu Yerucham writes that learning from either is prohibited. Beit Yosef explains that he was uncertain whether the halakhah follows Rava or Shmuel. Their words are astonishing.
