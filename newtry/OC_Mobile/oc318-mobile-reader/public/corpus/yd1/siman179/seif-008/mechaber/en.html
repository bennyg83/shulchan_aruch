One who whispers over a wound or over a sick person, and spits, and afterward reads a verse from the Torah, has no share in the World to Come. And if he does not spit, there is at least a prohibition. But if there is danger to life, everything is permitted. {Rama: And some say that all this is forbidden only when he reads the verse in Lashon HaKodesh, but not in the vernacular (Rashi in the name of his teacher). However, regarding spitting, it is good to be careful in any case, particularly if they mention the Name, for he has no share in the World to Come (so is implied by the Tur according to the view of R' Yitzchak).}
