And spits. One may not mention Hashem's Name over spitting. The custom of spitting before reciting 'and we bow' is different, because everyone knows the spitting expresses contempt for idolatry and is followed by praise of Hashem. Rashi limits the case to whispering after spitting; Tur holds that even spitting afterward is included.
In a foreign language. Bach forbids incantations in a foreign language just as in Hebrew. Shach disagrees, though initially one should avoid every possible concern; where avoidance is impossible, the leniency may be relied upon.
