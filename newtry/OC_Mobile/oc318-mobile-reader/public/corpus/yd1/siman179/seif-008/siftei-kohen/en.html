"He has no share in the World to Come." Because one does not mention the Divine Name over spitting — Gemara. It implies when reading a verse without the Name, even regarding spitting there is only a prohibition; and likewise it appears in the Tur in the name of Rabbi Yochanan and Beit Yosef; and likewise it appears from the words of the Rav and the author abbreviated in the understood sense, not like the Pri Chadash who wrote that it appears he spits on verses of Torah, etc.; but one can say he speaks of verses of Torah that contain names; and likewise it appears from the end of his words — see there.
And some say, etc. Bach wrote, and it is astonishing, for in the chapter Kirah regarding bathhouse and privy Abaye said that matters of holiness — even to say them in secular language is forbidden; therefore it appears regarding whispering too that what is forbidden in the holy language is also forbidden in secular language; and such is the implication of all the poskim who did not bring this of Rashi in the name of his teacher — end of his words. And it is astonishing how it entered his mind to reject the words of Rashi and his teacher, whose words are not relevant here at all, for matters of holiness means a matter of sanctity such as instruction or some matter of Torah — forbidden to say in secular language; but the Name in the holy language is a name, but in secular language it is not a name at all; and consider yourself, for behold it is permitted to erase a name written in secular language, such as "Gott" in Ashkenaz language, or "Bog" in Polish and Russian language, and similar; and that which the poskim did not bring this of Rashi in this manner — we find many such; therefore I say certainly ab initio one should be careful in everything possible, as the Rav also wrote; but where it is not possible — it is not possible.
