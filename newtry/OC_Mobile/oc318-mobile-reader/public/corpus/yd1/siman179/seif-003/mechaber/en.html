One who says, “My bread fell from my mouth,” or “My staff fell from my hand,” or “My son calls me from behind,” or “A deer stopped him on the way,” or “A snake passed on my right,” or “A fox on his left,” and whoever one of these occurred to him makes from it a nichush — not to go out on the road, or not to begin in a labor; and likewise those who practice nichush with a weasel and with birds and with stars; and likewise one who says, “Do not begin to collect from me — it is morning; it is Motzei Shabbos; it is Motzei Rosh Chodesh”; and likewise one who says, “Slaughter this rooster that crowed like a raven, and this hen that crowed like a rooster” — it is forbidden. {Rama: Some say: if he does not state the reason why it is a mitzvah to slaughter the hen, but rather he just says, “Slaughter this hen,” it is permitted to slaughter it when it crowed like a rooster (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Avraham; and it is in a responsum of Maharil, siman 118). And so is the practice.}
