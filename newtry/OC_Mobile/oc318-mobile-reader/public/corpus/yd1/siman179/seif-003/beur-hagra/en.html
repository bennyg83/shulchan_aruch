Some say, etc. Maharil wrote in siman 118 that Maharav told him this in order to justify the common custom. His words are as harsh as vinegar to the teeth, for this would amount to deceiving the Omnipresent. Maharil himself, however, wrote that the custom rests on the textual version in Shabbat that reads throughout the entire discussion, 'It does not involve the ways of the Amorites.' Accordingly, the statement there, 'with regard to all of them,' etc., 'except for these,' refers to everything listed there. This is why the Gemara cites from the entire Tosefta only those practices. This was also Ramach's version, as Kesef Mishneh records in his name. Rashi likewise had this version before him, but he deleted it, as he explains in the comment beginning, 'It involves.' Rashba also writes in siman 413 that this is the version found in the manuscripts. See there.
