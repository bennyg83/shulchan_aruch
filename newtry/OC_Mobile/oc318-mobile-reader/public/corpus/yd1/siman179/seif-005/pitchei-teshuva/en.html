One who whispers over a wound. In the responsum of Avnei Nezer Panim Me'irrot part 1 siman 36 he wrote regarding a child who could not sleep, and the women said there is a remedy to cut the nails of his hands and feet and some of his hairs and place inside an egg and place on a willow tree — that it is forbidden to do so. And his proof from Rashi Chullin 77b, that anything not directly on the sick person is forbidden — see there well. And from this for all things that are done far from the sick person — forbidden. And in my humble opinion, the explanation of Rashi z"l requires investigation, for if so what does he challenge from that which they seal with pitch — see there and investigate; and see in Rashi Shabbat 67 and in the Rosh there; and see in Orach Chaim siman 301 seif 27.
