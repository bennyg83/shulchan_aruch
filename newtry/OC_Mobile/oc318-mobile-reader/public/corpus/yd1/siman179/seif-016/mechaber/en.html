The act of shedim is forbidden, and there is one who permits to inquire of them regarding theft. {Rama: And similar to this (Beit Yosef and Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 11 of Hilchos Gezeilah). And through an oath that one adjures them through Names, some permit in any case (Beit Yosef in the name of Nimukei Yosef, and there in the above gloss). Nevertheless, most who engage in this do not depart from them in peace; therefore one who guards his soul should distance himself from them (Beit Yosef).}
