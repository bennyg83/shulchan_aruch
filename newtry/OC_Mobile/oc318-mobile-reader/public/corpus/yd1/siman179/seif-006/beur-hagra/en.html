And even though, etc. Rambam, and likewise in his commentary on the mishnah in chapter 4 of Avodah Zarah; but all who came after him disagreed with him, for behold many charms are mentioned in the Gemara; and he followed philosophy, and therefore wrote that sorcerers and names and charms and demons and amulets — all is false; but they already struck him on his skull, for behold we find many incidents in the Gemara — therefore names and sorcerers she said a word and stopped it for a raven they said, etc. (Shabbat 81b, Chullin 105b), and in chapter 4 of deaths and in the Yerushalmi there the incident of Rabbi Eliezer and Rabbi Yehoshua and ben Beteira, and likewise Rabbi Chananel and Rabbi Eliezer who created a third-grown calf, and Rabbi Yehoshua who said there and established it between heaven and earth (Bekhorot 8b), and likewise Avishai ben Tzeruyah (Sanhedrin 95a), and many similar; and they said (in chapter 4 of deaths, Chullin 7b): why are they called sorcerers, etc. And the Torah testified "and they shall be as serpents," and see Zohar there; and likewise amulets in many places — too much to enumerate. And philosophy led them astray in most of their learning to interpret the entire Gemara allegorically and uproot them from their plain meaning; and Heaven forbid I do not believe in them, neither from them nor from their multitude; rather all the matters are as their plain meaning, only that there is inner meaning in them — not the inner meaning of the philosophers who are externality, but of the masters of truth.
