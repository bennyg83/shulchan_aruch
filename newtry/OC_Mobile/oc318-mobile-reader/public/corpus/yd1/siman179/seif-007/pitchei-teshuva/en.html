Sleight of hand. Amudei Kesef forbids turning a vessel as a charm to improve a candle's burning, even on weekdays, because of the ways of the Amorites. Chokhmat Adam strongly condemns wedding entertainers who perform illusion or sleight of hand, treating the act as a Torah prohibition and those who hire them as violating lifnei iver. Watching is also forbidden, though one may watch a non-Jewish performer.
