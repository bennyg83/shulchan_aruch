Holding the eyes. See Beit HaTalmid; and in Sefer Amudei Kesef manuscript he wrote regarding the matter that they practice to turn a vessel so the candle burns well on Shabbat — there is no concern because of kindling, since he does not touch the candle; but there is concern in this because of the ways of the Amorites, and one should not do so (meaning even on weekdays); and see in Sefer Chochmat Adam kelal 75, who protested against the entertainers who do sleight of hand at weddings, called Taschenspieler, that they transgress a Torah prohibition, and it is a mitzvah to stop them — they transgress "before the blind"; also forbidden to look; but if the performer is an idolater — permitted to watch — see there.
