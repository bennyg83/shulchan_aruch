However, to write verses, etc. The reason is as stated at the beginning of the chapter Kol Kisvei: those who write blessings are considered like those who burn a Sefer Torah, because if a fire breaks out on Shabbos it is forbidden to save them and to exert oneself on their behalf on Shabbos. Although nowadays blessings are written in siddurim, that is because "it is a time to act for Hashem"; this does not apply to amulets. So wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba.
