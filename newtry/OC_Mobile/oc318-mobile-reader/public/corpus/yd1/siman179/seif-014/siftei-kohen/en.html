And there are those who permit, etc. See in Darkei Moshe who challenged what Beit Yosef wrote, that the proof he brings from Raavad from Shmuel who went to the cemetery, etc., is not a proof, since it was through adjuration; and thus: this rejection is nothing according to the conclusion that Rabbi Yerucham brought, who wrote that through adjuration also it is forbidden; and if so, necessarily what Shmuel did was for the spirit and not for the body, as Raavad's words — end of his words. And in Pri Chadash he wrote that what Beit Yosef wrote, that the distinction between spirit and body is garbled, for if he inquires for the body itself without spirit — behold the body alone has no carrying — end of his words; in my opinion it is not garbled according to the masters of Kabbalah, and in the Zohar in many places, etc. — see there.
