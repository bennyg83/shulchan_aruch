And all the more so. This is because of "you shall be wholehearted" — forbidden to inquire of them; but one who does an act of divination and omens and sorcery — it is a Torah prohibition, as Maharai wrote there, and it is simple; and it is explained from his words there that it is permitted for a sick person to inquire of sorcerers and diviners, since the prohibition is only because of "you shall be wholehearted," etc.; and Maharshal in a responsum siman 3 and in the chapter Kol HaBasar siman 13 brings him and elaborated and concluded that it is forbidden for a sick person to inquire of diviners and sorcerers where there is no life danger, even if there is danger to a limb; and if his illness came through sorcery or chance and an evil spirit — permitted — see there; and similarly Bach wrote in this section, and likewise Beit Yosef in the name of the Zohar, that it is a great prohibition to inquire of sorcerers even for a sick person.
