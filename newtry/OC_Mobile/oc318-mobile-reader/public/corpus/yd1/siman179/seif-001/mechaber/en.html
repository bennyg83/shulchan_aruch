That one may not practice kishuf, onen, or nichush; it contains 19 se’ifim. One may not inquire of those who gaze at the stars, nor with lots.
{Rama: Because it is stated, “Tamim tihyeh im Hashem Elokecha” (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos on the Rif, and in the name of Sifri). And all the more so, it is forbidden to inquire of soothsayers, and of menachashim, and of mechashfim (Piskei Mahari, siman 96).}
