Diviners. Shach cites Maharshal that a sick person may not consult diviners or sorcerers where there is no danger to life, even if a limb is endangered. If the illness itself arose through sorcery, an evil spirit, or a similar unusual cause, consultation may be permitted. Bach and the Zohar likewise treat inquiry of sorcerers as a grave prohibition. A later responsum records a lenient view for contemporary inquiries because present-day claims of sorcery are regarded as empty.
