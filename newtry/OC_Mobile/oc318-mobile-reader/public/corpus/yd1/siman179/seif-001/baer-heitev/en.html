Regarding diviners. Shach wrote in the name of Maharshal that it is forbidden for a sick person to inquire of diviners and sorcerers where there is no life danger, even if there is danger to a limb; and if his illness came through sorcery or chance and an evil spirit — it is permitted; and similarly Bach wrote in this section, and likewise Beit Yosef in the name of the Zohar, that it is a great prohibition to inquire of sorcerers even for a sick person — end of his words (and in responsum Nishmat Chayim siman 15 he inclined to permit the multitude who inquire and seek nowadays of sorcerers regarding theft or whether the sick person will live or die, since the Torah forbade only ancient sorcery; however nowadays there is no sorcery in the world and it is all vanity — see there).
