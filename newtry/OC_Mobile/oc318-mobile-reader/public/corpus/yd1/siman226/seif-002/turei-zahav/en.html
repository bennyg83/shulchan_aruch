They would compel the one who made the vow, etc. We are concerned that his fellow may stumble because of his vow. The Tur cites two opinions. The Ramban holds that we are concerned only that the person who made the vow may stumble, not that the person prohibited by it may do so. The Rambam holds the opposite: the one who made the vow will remember his vow and be careful, whereas the person prohibited by it, who did not utter the vow himself, may forget. Therefore, the one who made the vow must sell his share.
