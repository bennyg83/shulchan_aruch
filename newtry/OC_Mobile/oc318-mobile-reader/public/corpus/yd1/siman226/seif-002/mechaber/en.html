When both forbade one another — they are forbidden to place in it an oven, stoves, a mill, and chickens. If one of them was mudar hana'ah from his fellow — he is forbidden to place an oven, stoves, a mill, and chickens, and forbidden to enter it if it has a law of division; and his fellow is permitted in everything.
