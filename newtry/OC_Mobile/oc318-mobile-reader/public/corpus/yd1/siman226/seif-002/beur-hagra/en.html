There was one qua. Thor and as in both of them, Nadrin. B:
It is forbidden as a. Same as C:
And they compel the one who vowed, etc. The ruling is stated without qualification in accordance with the Rosh, whether he himself vowed or his fellow prohibited him. Rambam, however, applies it specifically where his fellow prohibited him. The Ramban, Rashba, and Ran challenged Rambam and wrote the opposite, as the Gemara states there, 'This too is reasonable,' etc. Rambam, however, relied upon the Jerusalem Talmud, as is his practice. See the Ran there, s.v. 'if,' etc., and the Rosh in his rulings.
