Forever. It is written in Hagahot Asheri that something that has not yet come into the world one may forbid upon himself by konam, just as one may forbid the fruits of his fellow; but in full hekdesh, no. And upon his fellow he cannot forbid something that has not yet come into the world even by konam — see there siman 234 seif 1. And he wrote in teshuvah of Maharam Matz in siman 1 regarding one who vowed to give a tithe from the profit that would come from his loan, and afterward he retracted and regretted — it appears he may retract because the profit had not yet come into the world. However, if he had said "this money — whatever profit comes from it I will give a tithe from it," the hekdesh takes effect since the money has come into the world. And likewise if he had lent a gentile two gold coins for three years and accrued interest with him immediately and gave him a document — it is possible that from this he cannot retract, etc. And see there siman 258 seifim 7 and 8. And Turei Zahav siman 215 s.k. 4 disagrees with this law regarding a vow to give a tithe, etc., and wrote that we do not rule thus but as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 212 seif 8 — if he says when I take it I will give it to hekdesh, it is upon him as a vow-name and he must fulfill his vow, end quote — see there.
