Not yet in existence. Hagahot Ashri writes that one may prohibit upon himself by konam something not yet in existence, just as he may prohibit another's fruits upon himself. Full hekdesh does not take effect upon something not yet in existence, nor can one prohibit it upon another by konam. Maharam discusses a vow to tithe future profit and allows retraction where the profit did not yet exist, unless the existing capital was itself designated as the source. Taz disagrees and rules that a vow to consecrate property upon acquisition creates a binding personal vow.
