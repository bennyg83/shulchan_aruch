Like imra. Like a sacrificial lamb; like dirim — the pen of a korban; like Yerushalayim — for the walls of the city and its towers are from the surplus of the chamber treasury — Rosh. And Ran explained “like Yerushalayim” as like the offerings in Jerusalem.
If he vowed by theirs, etc. The Rav's words are not precise, for it seems that if one vowed by the table or by the holy vessels of the Temple, the vow takes effect, since these are matters that became forbidden through a vow-like consecration. But if he vowed by vessels of a synagogue or similar items, this depends on whether they were consecrated in a way that creates actual prohibition. For an am haaretz, however, one should be stringent and require release from the vow, because he does not distinguish between one form of consecration and another.
