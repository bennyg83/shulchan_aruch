If he vowed, etc. — as explained in Shulchan Aruch, or as one of all, etc.
But if, etc. See Taz and Shach, and so it is in Shulchan Aruch siman 237 seif 6; and his proof from what is written there, 14b: one who vows by the Torah has said nothing, for he cast it upon the ground; and the explanation is that this "one who vows" means one who swears; and that is what is written in Be'er Heitev, etc.; and as is written there [note] 1.
