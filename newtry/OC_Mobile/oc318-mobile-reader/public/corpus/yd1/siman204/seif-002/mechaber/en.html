It is not specifically associating with a korban; rather, the same applies to anything holy with sanctity of the mouth — such as when one says, “This loaf is like a ewe,” “like rams,” “like the altar,” “like the Sanctuary,” “like Jerusalem,” or “like one of all the utensils of the altar” — this is a vow. {Rama: If he vowed “like the table in the Temple,” it is a vow; but if he said “like a table” without specification, and did not say “like the holy table,” this is nothing. Nevertheless, with an am ha’aretz one should release it so that he not be lax with vows (Hagahos Mordechai to Shevuos in the name of Maharam; brought in Beit Yosef, siman 237).}
