Unspecified — Taz and Shach wrote that the Rav's words refer only if he attached to a thing, such as if he said "this loaf is upon me like the sacred table," or even "like a table" unspecified is also forbidden like a lamb and birds; but one who vows "on the table" without attaching to anything, but only says in oath language "I vow" or "I swear on the table," whether sacred or unspecified — it is not an oath at all; and Rama's words here are like an error from before the ruler — end of his words.
