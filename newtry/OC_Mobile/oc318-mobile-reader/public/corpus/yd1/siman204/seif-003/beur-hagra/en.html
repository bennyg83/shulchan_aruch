(Likut) I heard, etc., and I like you, etc. — Rambam's language; but RAN wrote in chapter 3 of Shevuot that if he said "like you" he does not need twenty-four, etc.; and as written in siman 239, see there; and likewise at the beginning of Nazir we say that if he said "I am like this one" he is a nazir, and so is the custom (end).
