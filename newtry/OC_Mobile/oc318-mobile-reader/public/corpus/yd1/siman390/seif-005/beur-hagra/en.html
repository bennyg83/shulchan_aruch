(Likkut) A woman, etc. In Evel Rabbati, as cited in Torat HaAdam: everything that applies to a man applies to a woman, except haircutting. Tosafot, however, explain all this as referring to applying depilatory paste to the lower area, and the Ramban agrees; this is why it says, "and some forbid," etc.
And some forbid, etc. See Tosafot there. In Torat HaAdam he wrote in the name of Tosafot that the haircutting which is permitted means the lower area, etc.; and so Tosafot wrote in Moed Katan 18a, s.v. u-ve-ginustari, etc.
