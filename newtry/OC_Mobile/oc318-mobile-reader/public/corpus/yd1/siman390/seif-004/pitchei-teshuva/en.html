Three months. See the responsa Noda BiYehudah Orach Chaim siman 14 at its end, where he wrote that when they said the measure of rebuke is three months, this applies to those who cut only their head-hair, but for those who shave their beards (by scissors or by application) the measure of rebuke is a shorter time. And so wrote in the responsa Chatam Sofer siman 347 that for Ashkenazim who are accustomed to shaving their beards, the measure of rebuke is thirty days, and if thirty days of [mourning] for his father and mother coincided with the national mourning period from the Seventeenth of Tammuz, he may cut a minimal haircut on the eighteenth of Tammuz such that he would no longer be fit for rebuke; see there. And see there further regarding whether if the time of rebuke arrived on Chol HaMo'ed one may cut hair.
