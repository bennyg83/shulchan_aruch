(Shulchan Aruch seif 4) Until his friends rebuke him. This helps even to allow shaving on the thirtieth day, as in Eliyah Zuta siman 548. If they rebuked him within thirty days, it is obvious that it does not help, for this is no less than ordinary mourning. Even if a festival occurred within thirty days, we do not say that the festival cancels the thirty so that rebuke should help; see siman 389 seif 5. See Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chaim 531 s.k. 10 and 548 s.k. 10. In Eliyah Rabbah 132 s.k. 4 he writes that one who marries within thirty days, even for a father or mother, may shave; the Mishbetzot Zahav there leaves the law requiring study. See Shemesh Tzedakah, Orach Chaim siman 25.
