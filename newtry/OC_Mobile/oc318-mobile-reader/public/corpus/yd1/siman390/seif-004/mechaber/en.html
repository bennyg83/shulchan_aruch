For all the dead he shaves after thirty days; for father and mother until companions protest to him: {Rama: Festival does not help if festival came before companions protested (Tur); and see Orach Chaim siman 548; measure of protest has dispute; custom is three months (Maharai, Agudah, Maharil, Ran); and in these places they do not cut hair for father and mother all twelve months (Maharil partial custom), unless for need such as hair weighed on him or he goes among gentiles and is disgraced by his hair — permitted to cut (so Avraham Zacuto answered).}
