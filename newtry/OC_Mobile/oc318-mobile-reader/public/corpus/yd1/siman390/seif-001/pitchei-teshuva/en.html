It is forbidden to cut the hair. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh Deah, siman 213, who writes that if a brit milah occurs and the mourner is the sandek or mohel, he nevertheless remains forbidden to take a haircut. This is not comparable to Rama below, siman 391:2 in the gloss, citing Mahari Weil, for several reasons; see there.
To shave his hair. Chamudei Daniel, manuscript, writes that it is permitted to apply bankes, meaning cupping treatment, during the thirty days of mourning if there is significant need, even though the head must first be shaved for the procedure. The same applies on Chol HaMoed.
