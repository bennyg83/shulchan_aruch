This follows the view of Ri Migash and Behag cited by Tur. Tosafot write likewise. I still require study, because Beit Yosef writes that as practical halachah we follow Rambam in chapter 5 and Raavad, who hold that this leniency was said only regarding Chol HaMoed and not mourning, as taught in Evel Rabbati.
