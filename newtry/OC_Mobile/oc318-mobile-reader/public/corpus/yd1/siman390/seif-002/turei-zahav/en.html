Whether the one shaving. This means that if at the time of shaving the one shaving is told that a relative died, he may finish his work because of human dignity.
In this siman one seif is missing, namely seif 5, and this is how it should read: A woman is permitted to remove hair after seven days. Gloss: And some forbid even for a woman, and this is the essential law. I received that this is found in the Shulchan Aruch of the Rama, written by his own hand, of blessed memory.
