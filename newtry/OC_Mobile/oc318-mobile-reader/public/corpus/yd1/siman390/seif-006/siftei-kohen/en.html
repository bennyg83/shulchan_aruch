To comb, etc. A seif is missing here, since the text marks seif 4 and seif 6 but not seif 5. The Levush's division of seif 5 into two is not correct. A new seif should be inserted, as in some editions: 'A woman may remove hair after seven days. Gloss: Some prohibit this even for a woman, and this is the primary ruling.' This appears in the introduction to Semak. The laws of haircutting and combing must be distinguished, and the readings of the editions and later authorities should be consulted.
