And the festival does not help. This is also written by the author in Orach Chaim siman 548 seif 9 and below siman 399 seif 4. It seems that similarly the festival combined with a rebuke does not help if he encountered [the haircut] before thirty days, and this is also clear from above siman 389 seif 5 regarding ironing. However, in the responsa Noda BiYehudah part Orach Chaim siman 14, on the question of whether the time of rebuke arrived during Chol HaMo'ed, he discussed this at length and concluded that on Chol HaMo'ed one certainly may not permit it, and on erev Yom Tov if his friends rebuked him it is more appropriate to permit it even within thirty days — but all this appears [to him] as a ruling for practice but not for actual implementation until he reconsidered the matter, end quote. And it is surprising that he did not at all recall the words of the Shulchan Aruch in siman 389 mentioned above, for it requires no further discussion since ironing and haircutting are equal. Subsequently I found in the responsa Teshuvah Me'Ahavah part 1 siman 124 that he expressed astonishment at him regarding this and elaborated to bring many authorities who forbid it, and concluded that those leniency-ruling authorities regarding within thirty days for [mourning] a father and mother on account of the festival, relying on the Noda BiYehudah mentioned above, shall bear [responsibility for] what is theirs — may my portion not be with them; see there.
