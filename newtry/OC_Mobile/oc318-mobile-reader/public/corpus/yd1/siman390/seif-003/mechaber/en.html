All those they said permitted to shave on chol haMoed — if one occurred before mourning and he entered mourning immediately, forbidden to shave; but if one occurred and two mournings pressed one after another, he shaves as usual, whether with razor or scissors, even within seven; and one whom mournings pressed one after another may lighten his hair with razor but not scissors: {Rama: (provided done in private) (Beit Yosef in name of Ramban).}
