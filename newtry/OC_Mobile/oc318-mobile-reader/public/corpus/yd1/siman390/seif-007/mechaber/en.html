Just as forbidden to shave all thirty days, so forbidden to take nails with a tool; but with hands or teeth permitted, even within seven; and a woman whose immersion came after seven within thirty — if she takes nails with hands or teeth she does not take them well; rather tell a gentile woman to take them with razor or scissors: {Rama: Not specifically a gentile woman — same law for Israelite woman; he used chol haMoed language (his own reasoning).}
