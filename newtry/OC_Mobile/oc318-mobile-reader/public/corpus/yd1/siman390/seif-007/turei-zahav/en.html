If she is prohibited from cutting them herself, then having another Jewish woman do so should also be prohibited because of “before the blind.” It appears that another woman is required only so that it be done unusually, as stated nearby, since she cannot remove them properly for immersion with her hands or teeth. Yet I am astonished at the Shulchan Aruch. In seif 5 it rules, following the Rif and Rambam, that a woman may remove hair after seven days. Why then may she not cut her own nails normally? Nails are no more stringent than hair. Moreover, even according to the Rama, siman 393 permits cutting nails for the purpose of circumcision. Why should cutting them for the mitzvah of immersion not be permitted according to all opinions? We have not observed women having another person cut their nails during the thirty days.
