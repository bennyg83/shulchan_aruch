One who vows and retracts within the time of speech, or others persuade him within the time of speech and he accepts their words (within the time of speech of his vow) (the view of the Rav; and with this the objection of Raavad against Rambam is resolved, and not like Beit Yosef) — it is not a vow; and this is when he says so with his mouth. But if he said, “I retracted in my heart,” it is nothing.
