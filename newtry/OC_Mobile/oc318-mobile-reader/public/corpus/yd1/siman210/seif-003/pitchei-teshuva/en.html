That they annul it. Perach Shoshan discusses immediate retraction from a dream-vow. He distinguishes dream-excommunication, where Heaven has distanced him and great stringency applies, from a dream-vow, where some require no annulment at all. Chatam Sofer rules that a dream-vow not to eat cooked matzah during 'this Pesach' applies only to the coming Pesach and includes even soaked matzah; despite being made 'on the understanding of the public,' it may be annulled before ten men where the person does not wish to maintain it.
