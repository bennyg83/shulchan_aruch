Within twenty-four of his vow. But not within twenty-four of their protest; and we do not say since he retracted within kedei dibbur of their protest and they protested within twenty-four of his vow — this counts as twenty-four. Likewise we say in chapter Shevuot HaEidut regarding retraction and witnesses' denial — here others are not relevant to his vow that their protest should support his vow to retraction, as Raavad wrote; not like Beit Yosef who did not know his intention. It appears Rambam and Tur also agree, as Darchei Moshe wrote and brought; likewise Beit Chadash; and Kesef Mishneh himself concludes end of chapter 2 of Hilchot Shevuot.
