Within toch k'dei dibbur, namely the time required to say, "Shalom alecha, rebbi." Perishah.
Within toch k'dei dibbur of his vow, but not merely within toch k'dei dibbur of their protest. We do not say that because he retracted within toch k'dei dibbur of their protest, and they protested within toch k'dei dibbur of his vow, the entire sequence is considered one toch k'dei dibbur, even though a similar rule is stated in chapter Shevuas HaEdus regarding retraction and the denial of witnesses. Here the others have no legal participation in his vow that would connect their protest to his vow and thereby connect his retraction, as Ra'avad writes. This is unlike Beit Yosef, who did not understand his intention. It appears that Rambam and Tur likewise agree to this. Darkei Moshe writes this, and Derishah cites it; Bach writes likewise, and Kesef Mishneh himself concludes so at the end of chapter 2 of Hilchos Shevuos.
