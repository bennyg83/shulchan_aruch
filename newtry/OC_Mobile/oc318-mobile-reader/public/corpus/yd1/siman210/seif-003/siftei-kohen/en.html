Within twenty-four — meaning "peace upon you, Rabbi" — Perishah.
Within toch kedei dibbur of his vow. But not within toch kedei dibbur of their protest. This is the view of the Raavad, for the retraction must attach to the act of the vow itself; once the time of speech after the vow has passed, the later protest of others does not reopen the ability to retract.
