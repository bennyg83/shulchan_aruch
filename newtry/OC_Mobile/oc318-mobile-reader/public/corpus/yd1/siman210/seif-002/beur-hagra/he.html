הנודר כו'. דאפי' בהקיץ בעינן פיו ולבו שוין כ"ש כאן דאין כאן לא פה ולא לב:
וי"א כו'. כמ"ש פ"ק דנדרים (ח' א') נידוהו בחלום כו' וכגירסת הרמב"ם אלא דל"ג רק עד י' דידעי למקרי. וסברא הראשונה ס"ל דלא דמי לשם דשם סי' קללה הוא משמים וצריך קירוב: **** ENGLISH **** And some say, etc. As in chapter 1 of Nedarim (8a) — "they ostracized him in a dream," etc.; and Rambam's text, except he only counts up to ten who know to read. The first reasoning holds it is not like there, for there it is a curse from heaven and requires drawing near; but one who swore — even awake we require mouth and heart equal — all the more in a dream where there is neither mouth nor heart. "And some say" — this is Rashba, who wrote for mere concern one should be stringent even in an oath in a dream; it appears to my humble opinion — if a woman swore with self-affliction, annulment by her husband on the day he hears within twenty-four hours applies, lest this oath — which is a mere stringency — be preferable to an oath while awake; and even though for requiring ten initially they were more stringent than awake where three suffice — nevertheless one should not learn stringency except what Magen Avraham wrote — the husband cannot annul since they swore him from heaven; and I found Maharal of Prague who answered practically like Rosh — no annulment at all.
