And it appears according to this view that even if they came back and annulled it in a dream, it is nothing, as there regarding niduy — for it is impossible to dream without idle matters and perhaps the annulment itself was idle matters.
One must give weight to their words. — And so is the custom. And it appears the same applies to a woman who vowed in a dream, that she requires ten just like a man, and her husband cannot annul it for her on the day he heard it, for this vow’s law is like niduy — end of Bach; and so is the practice.
