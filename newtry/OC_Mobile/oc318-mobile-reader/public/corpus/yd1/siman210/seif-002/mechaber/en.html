One who vows in a dream — it is nothing, and he does not need questioning. And there are those who say that they should release him with ten who know how to release (and they release him through regret, as if he vowed while awake) (responsum of the Geonim). And one should be concerned for their words. (However, if he does not treat vows lightly, ten who know how to release — they release him with three, like any other vow) (Rashba, siman 268).
