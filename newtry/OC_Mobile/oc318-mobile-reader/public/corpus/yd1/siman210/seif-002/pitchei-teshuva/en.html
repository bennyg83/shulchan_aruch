One who vows in a dream. See teshuvah of Shemesh Tzedakah, Yoreh De'ah part siman 5, who elaborated on this [and see teshuvah of Radbaz vol. 1 siman 99 — one who dreamed he swore to repay his debt on a certain day; he answered that here even Rosh agrees, since repaying a creditor is a mitzvah — therefore heaven showed him to stir his soul to mitzvah — therefore he must repay his debt that day or strive that the lender say "behold you are as if received" — see there; and it is implied from his words that for this annulment of vows does not help, for we do not come upon it from vow reason but heaven imposed to stir his soul; and see in s.k. afterward].
