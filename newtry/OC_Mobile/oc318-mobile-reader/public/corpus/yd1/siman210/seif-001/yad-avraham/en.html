(Siman 210, seif 3) One who makes a vow and retracts it, etc., within toch k'dei dibbur of his vow. See Shach, seif katan 7: within toch k'dei dibbur of his vow, but not within toch k'dei dibbur of their protest. See the responsum of the Rashba cited in Choshen Mishpat, siman 80, that toch k'dei dibbur of another person's speech is not considered part of one's own speech.
