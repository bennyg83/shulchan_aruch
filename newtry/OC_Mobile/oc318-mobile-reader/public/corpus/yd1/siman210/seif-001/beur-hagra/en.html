And if he vowed, etc. His intention is to answer the commentators' difficulty from what is written in Nedarim 25a — "and even though we hold," etc.; and we likewise say there (25a) "he told him when one swears with intention," etc., "and we," etc.; but there we say it implies the mitzvah of tzitzit, etc.; and specifically a matter far from its meaning one cannot say; but one who says explicitly "with my intention" — then even all twenty-four that are in its meaning — see there. And in Shevuot 29a, and in such a case Rambam deals: "One who swore and said, etc., 'with your understanding I swear' — this is not yad," etc.; likewise Tosafot there 26b, "gamar." "And if we would say," etc. But unspecified — a condition applies, as in the tractate — Rabbi Akiva would swear with his lips and annul in his heart; and all the more one who vows by himself — a condition was in my heart, as in chapter 6 of Tosefta of Taharot — one is believed about himself: how? They said to him "you vowed" and he said "a condition was in my heart" — we listen to him; likewise in Torat Kohanim (Vayikra) — they said to him "you are a nazir," etc. (and see above siman 232 s.k. 35).
