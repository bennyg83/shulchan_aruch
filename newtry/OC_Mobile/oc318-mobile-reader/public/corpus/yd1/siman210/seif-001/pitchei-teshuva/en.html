Alone. Avodat HaTodah in Beit Chadash's name; and see teshuvah of Chavot Yair siman 15 who questioned this — his view: even if he intended to produce unspecified bread, permitted in barley bread; only he feared disputing several early and late authorities whose view is like Beit Chadash. He also wrote — if his thought was for unspecified bread and he uttered wheat bread, whether in error or intentionally — plainly permitted in barley bread; and perhaps even if his intention is clear it does not help even to be stringent in what is not in the vow's language at all; and even though below siman 218, one who vows wool and flax "upon me in a load" and sweated — permitted to wear and forbidden to load, though for Rosh it implies wearing — it is different there since nevertheless gentiles' way is to say "sweated" in this language — see there further.
