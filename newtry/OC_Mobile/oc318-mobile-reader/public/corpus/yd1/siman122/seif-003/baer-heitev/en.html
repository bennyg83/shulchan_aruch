Sharp. Issur VeHeter wrote: if he cooked a sharp thing in a meat pot that is not ben yomo and afterward cooked milk in it within twenty-four hours, the food is permitted; and we do not say the sharpness in the pot causes it to emit now for improvement; and the same applies if he cooked a sharp thing in a forbidden pot that is not ben yomo (and Nachalat Binyamin emends that it should read that it is ben yomo — see there) and afterward it stood within twenty-four hours from when the sharp thing was cooked in it — permitted; and likewise if he cooked a sharp thing in a ben yomo meat pot, if he cooked in it afterward within twenty-four hours from when the meat was cooked in it, even though it is not me'et le'et from when the sharp thing was cooked in it and when the sharp thing was cooked in it it was ben yomo — permitted; but if forbidden matter was first cooked in it, even if afterward a non-sharp thing was cooked in it, only that it was ben yomo at that time — we count afterward me'et le'et from the time of the second dish, as above siman 94; and see in siman 103 seif 7 in the hagahah regarding heating water in it — see there.
