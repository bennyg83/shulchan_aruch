(There, Taz s.k. 8) And furthermore Ra'ah's words are astonishing, for behold in a gentile's vessel as well it is permitted b'dieved, etc. His words are astonishing, for Ra'ah holds exactly like Rabbi Shalom of Bunburk; therefore for a gentile's vessel here, since it is a Jew's vessel we say establish it on its presumption, as Rabbi Shalom wrote; and the same applies that for Rabbi Shalom, if he returned them the same day standing is required, except Rabbi Shalom deals there where it already stood in a gentile's hand and he returned it the next day — therefore it is permitted immediately. And also his further words written here are not necessary at all; and see Shach on this.
