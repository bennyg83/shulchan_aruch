L'chatchila. See in Minchat Yaakov general rule 85 s.k. 64, who brought that in responsum Nachalat Yaakov he wrote in the name of the sages of Venice: this is specifically in metal vessels, since there is remedy through hagalah; not so earthenware that requires breaking — it is considered b'dieved and vessels are permitted after standing overnight; and he elaborated to challenge this from many Talmud sugyot and poskim; nevertheless he concludes there in the name of a great one that in irretrievable loss one may permit in earthenware — see there; and in my humble opinion it is difficult to permit even in irretrievable loss, for Hagahat Asheri wrote at the end of tractate Avodah Zarah on that which says: decree lest ben yomo because of ben yomo: but one should not say the reason is because it is l'chatchila and nosen ta'am lifgam is forbidden l'chatchila, for since they require breaking it is considered b'dieved — end of his words; behold explicitly even in earthenware breaking is required. I again found in Pri Megadim siman 103 who also raised this; and see Pri Megadim in Orach Chaim in conduct of rulings Issur VeHeter order 3 sections 25 and 26 what he wrote on this; and see above s.k. 3.
