(Siman 122 in Shulchan Aruch seif 2) And nevertheless the Sages forbade to cook in it l'chatchila, etc. Rashba proved this law from the Gemara in Pesachim 39a: Rabbi said pots on Pesach — break them, etc. — see there; and it is impossible that l'chatchila it is permitted to use a pot that is not ben yomo — why break them, leave them until after me'et le'et and use on Pesach? Or l'chatchila it is forbidden. And one may say: behold Rashba holds that wherever something is forbidden in any amount, nosen ta'am lifgam is also forbidden even b'dieved; if so nothing is compelled from chametz on Pesach that is forbidden in any amount — if so nosen ta'am lifgam is also forbidden even b'dieved; not so other prohibitions. One may say: a pot that is not ben yomo is permitted to use l'chatchila. And one may say his proof is from Shmuel, who holds mivshei aku"m is batul in sixty even chametz on Pesach; and this is certainly that even though Rashba holds to forbid nosen ta'am lifgam in prohibitions of any amount and the like, but where mivshei aku"m is in sixty, even if mixed in its kind and it is nosen ta'am lifgam, it does not forbid, for certainly since it is for spoilage there is no importance of its kind here; if so according to Shmuel all prohibitions are permitted to use l'chatchila — why did Shmuel say leave them until after Pesach and make, etc.? Rather certainly nosen ta'am lifgam is forbidden l'chatchila.
