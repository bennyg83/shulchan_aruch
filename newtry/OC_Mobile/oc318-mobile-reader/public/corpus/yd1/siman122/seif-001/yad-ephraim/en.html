Baer Heitev; and in responsum Noda BiYehudah vol. 2 siman 36 regarding Kaffen drinking vessels of gentiles in Kaiserheim — one should forbid drinking from them, for certainly they are ben yomo, for at every hour they drink milk of prohibition in them; and one should not rely on Rashal and Taz who are stringent, and Shach is stringent regarding earthenware l'chatchila; therefore in a city where there are Jewish vessels it is forbidden l'chatchila; and if he pours from a Jew's vessel, even b'dieved it is forbidden; and on the road at an inn where there are no other vessels it is considered b'dieved; and in the city one should be stringent even where impure milk is not common; and one who forgets has a mitzvah to separate from prohibition — see there; and it requires investigation, for all drinking from Kaffen is through pouring from a first vessel; if so, even on the road at an inn one should be stringent and pour from the first vessel to another vessel not regularly used every day, for we say in seif 10: stam vessels are not ben yomo, and from there one may pour to the Kaffen even if the hand still recoils; and if there is no yad soledes bo, everything is permitted even in the city, and through pouring, for presumably the vessels are clean and rinsed.
