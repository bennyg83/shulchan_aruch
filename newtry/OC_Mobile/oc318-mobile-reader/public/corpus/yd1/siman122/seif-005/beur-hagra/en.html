Prohibition, etc. As written in Avodah Zarah 33b: a dilemma was raised, etc.; and Tosafot there d.h. he saw, etc.; and Rashba wrote: because the vessel's use is cold and its absorption is only a small amount, and they use it abundantly, and it is impossible to come, etc.; but wine is forbidden because its prohibition is in any amount; and see there 33a in the Mishnah, not like what the Rosh and Ran wrote that the reason is because it spoils, for behold we say there 33a: wine in water is nosen ta'am; rather one may say there is no visible prohibition to absorption — spoiled spoils in other beverages; and see in siman 103 seif 4; but Rashba does not hold to distinguish; and with this Tosafot's challenge in Pesachim 40a d.h. why leave them is resolved: and one might say, etc.; even though chametz use is abundant, nevertheless the chametz in it is small, for it is impossible that everything is nosen ta'am, since heter is involved, as Tosafot wrote at the end of Avodah Zarah; and this is what he says: like the pot from what was stated in Pesachim, and the barrel from Avodah Zarah. But Tosafot there follow their views, for they do not hold Rashba's reason; and therefore they forced themselves there; and Rashba did not want Tosafot's first explanation there — it is difficult, for Rava is uncertain in Avodah Zarah whether Rav holds nosen ta'am lifgam is forbidden; and likewise the second explanation — behold jugs can be kashered through hagalah. And greater than this: even visible prohibition of something they were not concerned in such a case, as written in the last chapter of Terumot: a storehouse from which terumah wheat was removed, etc.; and likewise a barrel of oil, etc.; and Rashba siman 222 — except Rambam end of Laws of Terumot wrote: a pot in which terumah was cooked — if he washed it in water or wine it is permitted to cook non-sacred food in it; and he wrote in Kessef Mishneh that Rambam wrote in a responsum he learned from the Mishnah in Terumot there: the pourer sold to the buyer — evidently even the small amount visible is permitted, all the more so what is absorbed; and even rinsing is not needed in cold, only to use hot requires rinsing; but Raavad already challenged from the sugya in Zevachim 96b: Rabbah bar Ulla, etc.; and otherwise from the Torah to be lenient in terumah; and even in terumah nowadays that is rabbinic like chelev and forbidden fat and in Yerushalmi there: a pot in which terumah was cooked — kasher it three times in hot water and it suffices; Rabbi Ba said: one does not learn from it to nevelah; Rabbi Yosi asked before Rabbi Ba: terumah is punishable by death and nevelah by lashes — and you say thus? He said to him: according to the one who said they accepted ma'aserot voluntarily — meaning they are rabbinic; Rabbi Yoshti bar Shunam asked before Rabbi Mana: we learned if he bent and squeezed it is terumah — and you say thus? He said to him: here through fire it was kashered and emerges — evidently hagalah is required; except that for all opinions hagalah three times suffices; and he challenges there from if he bent and squeezed, and does not support from the beginning: the pourer sold, etc. — evidently we are lenient; except it is not because of leniency, for even in Torah prohibitions the law is so, because small prohibition is permitted when used abundantly; not so to kasher, for we measure in all of it in much absorption.
But if it is, etc. As written in Pesachim there: decree lest he come, etc. — evidently if its way is to use what forbids, forbidden in every case; and some say regarding jugs, as written there it is merely incidental, and like first entry; and we say in chapter 25 (111b): a bowl in which he salted, etc.; even though it is forbidden only in that quantity that is a very small matter; and this is what he says: like a bowl, etc., and even, etc.
