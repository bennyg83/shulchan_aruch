A small amount of issur that was absorbed into a utensil whose manner is not to be used with a small amount of heter in a way that this would give flavor into the heter, such that one would use it — such as a large pot or a barrel and the like — it is permitted to use it l'chatchila even though it is ben yomo, because it is impossible to come to a situation of giving flavor. But if it is a utensil that is used with a small amount, such as a bowl and the like, it is forbidden to use it, for we do not nullify issur l'chatchila, even a small amount, and even absorbed issur.
