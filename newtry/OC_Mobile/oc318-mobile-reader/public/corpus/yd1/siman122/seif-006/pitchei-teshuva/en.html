Baer Heitev siman 104; and see in responsum Be'er Sheva siman 30, who wrote that even though the poskim equated them regarding that they are in presumption of not being ben yomo — nevertheless there is a great distinction between them: we do not say stam vessels of ours are not ben yomo except when the vessel owner is not before us to ask him; but in a gentile's vessel, even though the gentile is before us, one need not ask, because he is not believed and his speech neither raises nor lowers — see there.
