This is agreed upon by the halachic authorities, apart from the Rambam. It is derived from Avodah Zarah 75b, which teaches that all vessels used before kashering are nevertheless permitted according to the view that a detrimental flavor is permitted. It is also derived from the discussions concerning non-Jewish pressed residue and cooked oil, where the Gemara states that if the concern is absorbed non-Jewish flavor, a detrimental flavor is permitted. Tosafot explains there that this is a double doubt: perhaps the vessels were used yesterday rather than today, and even if they were used today, perhaps they were used with something that imparts a detrimental flavor or with something that imparts no flavor. The Tur likewise writes this in the name of the Rosh.
