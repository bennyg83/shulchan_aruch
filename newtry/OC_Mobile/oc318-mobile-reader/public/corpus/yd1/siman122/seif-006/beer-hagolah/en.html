Agreed among the poskim except Rambam, from that in Avodah Zarah 75; and all of them that were used in them, etc. — permitted according to the one who says nosen ta'am lifgam is permitted; and from that which we say regarding gentile cheese and regarding Aramean oil, etc.: if because of gentile purgings nosen ta'am lifgam is permitted; and Tosafot explained there: because it is a double doubt — doubt whether they used it today or yesterday; and if you say they used it today, perhaps they used it in something that spoils or that is not nosen ta'am; and so the Tur in the name of the Rosh.
Tur in the name of Rashba in Torat HaBayit, from that in Chullin 6: since he said to her make from my dough, it is as if he mixed with his hands.
