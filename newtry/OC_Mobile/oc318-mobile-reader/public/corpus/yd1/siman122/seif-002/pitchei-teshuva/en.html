Decree. See in responsum Noda BiYehudah second series vol. 4 siman 51: whether also when forbidden from a rabbinic prohibition the law is so; and he wrote there that by law it is permitted l'chatchila, for it is a decree upon a decree; except one should not be lenient in this since it is not found in poskim that they should write this law — see there; and in truth it is found thus in Taz below siman 137 s.k. 7, who wrote explicitly permitted — see there (however Taz in his view in siman 90 s.k. 12 wondered at Issur VeHeter who forbids pickled in milk of a slaughtered animal, for it is a decree upon a decree; and Nachalat Binyamin already challenged him there from Tosafot beginning of Kol HaBasar — see there; and this is Issur VeHeter's view that Taz brought there, also what he brought in siman 137 — see there) [and in responsum Chacham Tzvi siman 75 it is clear that we decree in such a case a decree upon a decree; in a common and regular matter we decree g'l'g from that which they placed on the top of the reed or top of the stalk, etc. — see there; and see above s.k. 3 in the name of responsum Brit Avraham]. And at first glance there is clear proof that one should not permit a pot that was ben yomo from a rabbinic prohibition from the funnel of Rabbi Ami in Chullin 111: and blood that he salted is only rabbinic — why did she break it, leave it until tomorrow and it will be permitted? (and see Shach above siman 23 s.k. 19 and siman 57 s.k. 47 and responsum Avodat Avraham Panim Me'irot vol. 1 siman 23 — investigate); or it is forbidden; and in Ran there it is clear thus, who wrote on this incident: from here one can prove that nosen ta'am lifgam is forbidden l'chatchila, for if not, leave until tomorrow — end of his words; and therefore he intended this, that even in rabbinic prohibition a pot that was ben yomo is forbidden, for otherwise what does it teach us — behold the Talmud is arranged at the end of Avodah Zarah; however one may distinguish between whether the prohibition became known before it was ben yomo or after; and see above seif 110 in laws of double doubt section 19 — investigate.
