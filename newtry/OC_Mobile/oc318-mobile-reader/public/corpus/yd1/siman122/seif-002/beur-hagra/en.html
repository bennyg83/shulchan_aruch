A pot, etc. Chapter 3 of Pesachim, chapter 6 of Nazir, and chapter 5 of Avodah Zarah, 67b and 76a. It is permitted even rabbinically, as written there at 36a, 38b, 39a-b, and 75b, as explained. Tosafot wrote in Chullin 8b, s.v. “The slaughterer,” that the Gemara says in Avodah Zarah there concerning oil, and likewise concerning honey: What need is there to be concerned? A substance that imparts bad flavor is permitted. The mishnah that forbids oil cannot hold that bad flavor is forbidden, for if so, why did they permit honey?
