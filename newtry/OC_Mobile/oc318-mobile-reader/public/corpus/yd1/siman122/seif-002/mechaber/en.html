A pot that is not ben yomo — that is, it stood from time to time since the issur was cooked in it — is noten ta'am lifgam. Even so, the Sages forbade to cook in it l'chatchila, a decree because of a ben yomo pot, whether it absorbed from issur and one comes to cook heter in it, or whether it absorbed from milk and one comes to cook meat in it, or the reverse.
