And we are not concerned b'dieved. Baer Heitev; and in responsum Beit Chadash siman 116: an eating vessel whose use is hot in a gentile's hand a day or days — its law is like other gentile vessels and requires hagalah; and if he used it b'dieved the food is not forbidden; and if it did not stand in a gentile's hand except half a day and a Jew took it from him to use the same day — the food is forbidden even b'dieved; but if it stood in his hand me'et le'et, then the food is permitted b'dieved; but forbidden to cook in it forever, rather hagalah is required, and earthenware — breaking; and if it stood in a gentile's hand only for an hour, like part of the day — then a Jew leaves it overnight and it is permitted to use even l'chatchila and need not kasher; but forbidden to use within me'et le'et; and if he used within me'et le'et — through the dish the food is permitted; and there are further distinctions in responsum of the Rosh and in Torat Chayim general rule 59 — see there.
