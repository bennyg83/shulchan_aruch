Utensils of a meal that are in the hand of a non-Jew, and he speaks innocently and says that they are new, and it appears that it is as he says — it is permitted to purchase them from him. {Rama: And some are stringent and say that he is not believed, and one should not buy utensils from a non-Jew except when he sells many utensils and one buys a utensil among the utensils (Rashba in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah); and so is the practice l'chatchila. But in a case of pressing circumstances, such as when one is a guest in a non-Jew’s house and has no utensils, the practice is like the first opinion, and so is primary; and see below siman 137.}
