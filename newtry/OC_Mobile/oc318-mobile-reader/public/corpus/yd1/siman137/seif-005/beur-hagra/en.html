If he placed, etc. Meaning, although the Yerushalmi says that if he emptied it and placed other wine in it, the wine is forbidden but the jar is permitted, that refers to soaking for three days, as stated in Orach Chaim and above, siman 135, seif 11. Even there Ramban disputes the ruling, as stated there. Without such soaking, however, it is plainly not permitted. On 33a and 33b the Gemara says that only brine and fish brine have this effect, but other beverages do not.
