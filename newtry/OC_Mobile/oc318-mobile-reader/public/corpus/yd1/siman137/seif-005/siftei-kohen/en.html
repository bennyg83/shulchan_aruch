We do not permit, etc. And Beit Yosef; and even though Rosh in a responsum permitted because initially emission of absorption of the flask in wine was nullified — meaning according to the lenient authorities stam yeinam in sixty; and I already wrote siman 134 we do not rule except like those who say it forbids be'mashehu — so he wrote; and even though we rule batel in sixty — the Rav ruled like the Mechaber, for batel in sixty is due to loss as I wrote siman 135 s.k. 15 — unlike here where he puts wine ab initio; and not like Eitan HaTzvi who wrote that for us it is permitted ab initio to put wine another time; and it is possible he deals with a time of pressure and loss requiring the vessels — and if he does not give these vessels there will be loss in the matter. Eitan HaTzvi further wrote: even according to those who say stam yeinam in any amount is permitted after the fact by irui as above siman 135 seif 12 — and I already challenged him there s.k. 27; however it appears Rosh permits only when the wine stood in the vessel kashered by wine as with water in the manner explained siman 135 — if not, kashering with wine is not better than water — so it seems; and nonetheless investigate, for in Rosh's responsum there (klal 19 law 4) he did not write it is permitted to put wine another time except after 12 months — and teaches where 12 months aging is needed it does not harm what softened during 12 months in wine; and we count 12 months from the first forbidden wine used — as above seif 135 regarding water — see there in Rosh's responsum explained thus explicitly; but to kasher by wine from this Rosh did not mention there at all; and it is possible Beit Yosef understood from reasoning that since regarding 12 months the law of wine is like water, likewise regarding other kashering such as scrubbing and irui three days — see Rashba's responsum siman 503.
