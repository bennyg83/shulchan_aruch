Mishnah, Avodah Zarah 29, according to the sages.
Tur in the name of his father the Rosh there in his rulings: cold with cold emits or absorbs in twenty-four hours. But with a vessel into which wine is put for keeping, just as the vessel becomes prohibited through a non-Jew if he put wine into it temporarily, so too the Jew's wine is prohibited in it even temporarily.
There in the name of the Rashba; I also marked it above in siman 134 seif 5.
