A peel's thickness. Baer Heitev cites Shach. Chacham Tzvi permits in every case where the prohibition penetrates no more than a peel's thickness. See also Panim Meirot and Shaar Naftali, and above siman 135.
Because of stam yeinam. Magen Avraham applies the same law to distilled liquor. Perach Mateh Aharon appears otherwise, but Noda BiYehudah states that his ruling is not reliable and rested upon several special leniencies. Shev Yaakov distinguishes wooden or earthenware vessels from metal vessels, where the absorbed wine may become impaired, and permits in great loss distilled liquor produced in a non-ben-yomo copper vessel previously used for stam yeinam.
