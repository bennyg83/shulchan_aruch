Forbidden vessels. See Ba'er Heitev and responsum Shev Yaakov vol. 3 siman 73 — wine barrel of gentile that he made stam yeinam into stam yeinam ashes does not forbid after the fact after overnight even without irui and hagalah even though stam yeinam itself does not spoil due to overnight as there siman 137 — nonetheless stam yeinam ashes changed by boiling of fire and it is only steam becomes like other beverage and becomes lefegam; and likewise in Perach Mateh Aharon. And what Magen Avraham wrote siman 491 s.k. 40 and appears in Tosafot HaRosh, etc. — there is no meaning; and Magen Avraham siman 497 s.k. 25 left it in investigation and without doubt removes from certainty that responsum Perach Mateh Aharon — therefore these vessels have remedy by hagalah also the reeds in it by whitened stones and boiling water — so he wrote; but in Noda BiYehuda mateh siman 67 he wrote wine placed in barrels previously used stam yeinam ashes of stam yeinam and they sense in this wine taste and smell of stam yeinam ashes — one should be strict and forbid and nosen ta'am lefegam does not help as Taz in Orach Chaim siman 442 and Magen Avraham siman 491 — for though there it deals with another matter overnight unlike here — one may say stam yeinam ashes spoils wine by its nature; nonetheless who will decide if lefegam or leshvach. And also from nullification in sixty — one may say there is no nullification; Asher Lemelech siman 137; and all the more stam yeinam ashes which is strong and sharp and bitter certainly absorbed in vessel entirely; and all the more here they sense in wine taste of stam yeinam ashes and our eyes see it was not nullified — therefore difficult to be lenient even Birkei Yosef — see there.
