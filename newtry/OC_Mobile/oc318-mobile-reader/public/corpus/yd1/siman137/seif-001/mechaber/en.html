A law of kosher wine that was placed into a vessel that was not made kosher; it contains 6 se’ifim. All vessels that are forbidden due to wine of a non-Jew, into which a Jew placed wine before he made them kosher — it is forbidden for drinking and permitted in benefit, even if it is dry, as long as 12 months have not passed over it (but if it is a vessel used for long-term storage, it is forbidden immediately) (Tur in the name of Rosh). And if it is a vessel that is not used for long-term storage (and it is dry), it is not forbidden unless the Jew’s wine stood in it 24 hours. And if in the wine there was mixed a pitcher of water: if there is in the water 6 times corresponding to the “peel” of the jug, it is permitted even for drinking. {Rama: And see above siman 134, that even if it has no water, it is batel in 60; and there is no difference between vessels forbidden due to yayin nesech and whether they are bnei yoman or not (Tur; and a responsum of Rosh; and R' Peretz at the end of Avodah Zarah).}
