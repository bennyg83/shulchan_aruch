It is permitted to salt in them. Noda BiYehudah rules that a boiler previously used to cook distilled liquor made from stam yeinam may not initially be used for grain liquor. The permission to place salt, vinegar, or brine in wine vessels concerns cold absorption and does not prove that cooking other liquids is allowed. Here the distilled wine flavor may improve the later liquor. What was already produced may nevertheless be permitted in great loss, because in other prohibitions we do not apply chatikhah na'aseit neveilah in great loss, and the boiler absorbed a mixture of large amounts of water with the forbidden residue, so sixty is needed only against the forbidden portion of the absorbed contents.
