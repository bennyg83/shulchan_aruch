And likewise permitted to salt in them. This is Rashba's responsum that Beit Yosef brought and gave three reasons: one, salt does not emit from vessel; and further impossible to reach giving taste that nullifies prohibition be'mashehu; and likewise Ra'avad, etc.; and further since occupied emitting blood did not absorb — so he wrote; and superficially from first reason salt does not emit — automatically if in a way that certainly emits — namely stood overnight — forbidden; and we already proved opposite that in other beverages permitted even overnight; rather it appears Rashba means: one, it does not emit; and if you say it emits — nonetheless does not emit so much to reach giving taste; and if you say emits much — nonetheless did not absorb flesh since occupied emitting blood; and if you say this reasoning of being occupied emitting — it helps only regarding blood but not absorption of other prohibition such as nevelah, etc., where flesh salted with it became forbidden as explained siman 69 — Beit Yosef already copied siman 105 end Rashba's responsum and added another reason: and further only absorbs what clings like fat or brine but blood is thin;
