Likewise, it is permitted, etc. Salt does not extract absorbed taste from a vessel; see above, siman 105, seif 12. Furthermore, it cannot impart flavor, just as one may place water and other beverages there for this reason; see above, siman 122, seif 5. In addition, while the substance is occupied with discharging, it does not absorb. It absorbs only something that adheres, such as fat or brine, but not blood, which slides off, and likewise not the wine discharged here. See above, siman 70, seif 3. Beit Yosef here and in siman 105, citing a responsum of the Rashba.
