Beverages. Because they nullify the taste of wine and we do not decree other beverages on account of wine because it is merely incidentally — Gemara; and accordingly it is forbidden to put beverages in them on a regular basis, and so we rule above siman 122 seif 4 in Hagah regarding other forbidden vessels.
