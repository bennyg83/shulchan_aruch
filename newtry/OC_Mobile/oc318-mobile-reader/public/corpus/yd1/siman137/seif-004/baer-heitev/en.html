Beverages. They weaken the taste of wine, and the Sages did not decree concerning other beverages because such use is incidental. Accordingly, it is forbidden to store other beverages in these vessels permanently, as ruled in siman 122:4 concerning other forbidden vessels. Shach. Taz writes that even if the beverages remain twenty-four hours they are permitted, because the absorbed wine impairs them. Although detrimental taste is ordinarily forbidden initially, here the absorption occurred cold, and the Sages were lenient, as in siman 138:3. The distinction may be that the stricter ruling concerns actual yayin nesech, while here the case is ordinary non-Jewish wine.
For salting. Bach disagrees and rules that initially one may not salt food in them, as in siman 105:12, and after the fact it is forbidden if the food remained a full day. Shach rejects this: ordinarily there is sixty against the vessel's absorbed surface layer, wine's taste is weakened in other beverages, and all the more so in salty food. Taz likewise rules that the Mechaber is correct and they may even initially be used for salting.
