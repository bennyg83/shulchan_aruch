Of his own accord. Shach wrote: even though we rule that a gentile is believed bedei'eved in a rabbinic prohibition as written siman 98 seif 1 — one may say that there we deal with a matter that did not become presumed forbidden, unlike here where certainly these vessels were used with forbidden wine; and even though above siman 69 seif 10 he wrote that if a gentile put meat in a pot and it is unknown whether he rinsed it, he is believed bedei'eved since blood he salted is only rabbinic even though it became presumed forbidden — one may say that there since there is also the reason that gentiles are particular about honor to rinse the meat, therefore we rely on him — so he wrote. Siman 138.
