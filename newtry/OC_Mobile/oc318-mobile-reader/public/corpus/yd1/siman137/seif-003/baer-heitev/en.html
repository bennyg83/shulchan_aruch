Like wine. Taz writes that since ordinary non-Jewish wine is nullified in sixty, this ruling is needed only to teach that if the combined first and second batches of wine contain sixty against the vessel's forbidden surface layer, all is permitted, similar to the ruling regarding water. Nekudot HaKesef disagrees: the first wine alone must contain sixty. Otherwise it becomes entirely forbidden, and the second wine later requires sixty against all of the first wine. See siman 134:3.
