Like wine. Taz wrote: according to what we rule that stam yeinam is nullified in sixty, we do not need this ruling except if there is among everything — namely wine one and wine two — sixty against the peel of the forbidden vessel, everything is permitted; and similarly as he said afterwards regarding water (and in Nekudot HaKesef he challenged him, for certainly wine one alone needs sixty, for otherwise wine one becomes nosen ta'am and afterwards wine two needs sixty against all of wine one — for necessarily we do not hold regarding wine in wine that there is no nosen ta'am, rather because since it is forbidden be'mashehu unlike where it is forbidden due to there not being sixty; and so we rule above siman 92 seif 4 that even dry in dry there is nosen ta'am, if not in a place of great loss — so he wrote) — and see below siman 134 seif 3.
