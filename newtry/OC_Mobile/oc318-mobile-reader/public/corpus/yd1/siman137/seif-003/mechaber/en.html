Wine that was placed into jugs of a non-Jew that were not made kosher, and afterwards he emptied that wine and mixed it with other, more abundant wine that he had in other kosher jugs: if there is in the second wine 60 corresponding to the first wine, everything is permitted even for drinking. And if there is in the second wine water in the measure of 6 parts corresponding to the forbidden “peels,” everything is also permitted for drinking, even though there was no water in the first wine.
