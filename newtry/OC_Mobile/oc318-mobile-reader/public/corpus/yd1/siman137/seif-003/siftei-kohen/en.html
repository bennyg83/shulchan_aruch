In the second wine, 60, etc. For since the first wine was not forbidden except in drinking, it does not forbid in any amount — Rivash there; and as above siman 134 seif 2. And the reason it does not suffice to have 60 against the peels — behold the wine in vessel 1 is certainly much more than the peels, and the peels also only forbid in drinking — this is because since before the wine was absorbed in the peels it was forbidden in benefit, the peels forbid in any amount; and even if there were 60 in wine 1 against the peels — wine 1 is forbidden in drinking; only since it does not forbid except in drinking it does not forbid wine 2 except without 60; and see above seif 3.
In the second wine, water, etc. And we do not say chatichah na'aseh neveilah — as above siman 134 seif 16.
All is permitted, etc. And if there is not enough to nullify, the vessels too are forbidden as if he had put forbidden wine in kosher vessels — so it implies in Rivash there.
