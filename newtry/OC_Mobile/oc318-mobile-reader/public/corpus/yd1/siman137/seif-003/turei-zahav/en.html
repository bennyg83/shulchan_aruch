In second wine sixty like first wine, etc. This is according to Rivash's ruling siman 133 — master of this law who holds even stam yeinam forbidden be'mashehu; and likewise Shulchan Aruch ruled siman 134; but according to what we rule there batel in sixty we do not need this ruling except if among everything — wine first and second — sixty against peel of forbidden vessel everything permitted; and similarly as he said afterwards regarding water; and it is astonishing on Rama who did not write anything about this.
