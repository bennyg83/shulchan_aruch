And we do not say the first is null. Since it is cold — so Atzei Zahav; and it is not clear, for if so even according to one who says stam yeinam in any amount it should be permitted; and likewise Rashba and Tur wrote explicitly it is forbidden b’di’eved; rather certainly even though it is cold, the winepress is like a vessel into which they put wine for permanent storage that is forbidden immediately from cold; rather the reason is that the peel of the hole is nullified in 60 — for whatever comes out through the hole always has 60 against the peel; for Rashba and Tur themselves only forbid because the wine comes out clear and is forbidden in any amount; but if it came out with the pits and skins they would permit because there is 60 against the peel of the hole; and if so, for us who hold stam yeinam is null in 60 — in every case it is permitted; and this is clear.
