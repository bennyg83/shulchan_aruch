If one trod grapes in the winepress of a non-Jew that was not made kosher, it is permitted, for the pits and skins have 60 corresponding to the “peel” of the winepress. But it is forbidden to take the wine out through a hole through which the non-Jew’s wine runs, for the hole is forbidden, and the wine that exits from there is clear, without the mixture of the pits. {Rama: And if he transgressed and took it out through the hole, it appears that according to what we rule that stam yeinam is batel in 60, as above siman 134, here too all the wine is permitted, and we do not say that it becomes forbidden “first is forbidden and then the next,” and see below siman 99.}
