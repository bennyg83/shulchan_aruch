The family. It is degrading for the family if its members are not buried together in the same place according to their custom. Shach. Although one who instructed that he not be eulogized is obeyed, that differs because he gave the instruction at the time of death. Here his agreement was induced by money. Alternatively, there the matter concerns only himself, while here the burial or eulogy place also affects his heirs and household, who will eventually be eulogized there, creating a blemish for them as well.
