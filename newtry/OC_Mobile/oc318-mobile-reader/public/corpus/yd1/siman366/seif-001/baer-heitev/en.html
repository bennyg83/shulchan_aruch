The family. It is degrading to them if members of the family are not buried together in their customary place. Shach. Although the instruction of one who requested that he not be eulogized is followed, that case differs because he gave the instruction at the time of death. Here, financial pressure compelled him. Alternatively, there the matter concerns only his own honor, whereas here his heirs and household will also be eulogized in that place and would suffer disgrace. See Tosafot, Bekhorot 52a, s.v. Ba'in Bnei Mishpachah.
