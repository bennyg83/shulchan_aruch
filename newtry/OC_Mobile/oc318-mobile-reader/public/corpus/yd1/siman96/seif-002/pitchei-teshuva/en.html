See Shach who challenged Rama that from his words it appears even when it is known the knife was ben yomo, but doubt whether it was cut with it or through presumption and half-and-half — permitted; but this is not so — specifically with kreyn there is room to permit such a case, not with radish; see there. See in Knesset HaGedolah who challenged him — this vegetable has a presumption of permissibility, establish the matter on its presumption it was not cut with a forbidden knife, and even for Torah law we follow leniency; see there. See in responsa Toldot Yitzchak siman 17 who challenged him — we say establish on presumption specifically where presumption arose from itself, but if the presumption is because of another matter of doubt of mixed prohibition, we do not say establish on presumption, and he elaborated (see responsa Shivat Tzion siman 32 that his view is not so; see there); nevertheless he brought that Rama's words are correct, unlike Shach, because with cold too there are several sides to be lenient; see there.
