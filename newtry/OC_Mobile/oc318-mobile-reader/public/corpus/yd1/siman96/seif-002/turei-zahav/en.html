And sour fruits. Turei Chayim general rule 61 wrote in the name of Issur VeHeter that sour apples are not called a sharp thing; and it appears that in a case of loss one may rely on this for the reason I wrote nearby in the name of Maharai. And that liquid they make in Russian lands, soaked with water, that they call borscht — it appears plain it is called a sharp thing even if not very sour, still like weak vinegar, in chapter 25 regarding chalita, for we require the fruit itself.
