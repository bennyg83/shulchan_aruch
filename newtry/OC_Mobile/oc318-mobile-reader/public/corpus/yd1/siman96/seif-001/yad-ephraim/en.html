Avodat HaTaharah s.k. 10: Taz wrote borscht liquid is a sharp thing; Beit HaLevi wrote in siman 95 it is not a sharp thing, they can drink it raw unless very strong like vinegar; see there. He wrote in responsa Makom Shmuel siman 89 regarding borscht liquid that mostly is not very sour, and cooked in a dairy vessel not ben yomo and poured into a meat vessel and cooked meat with it — there is room to permit, for even though Taz says it is sharp, nevertheless Rama's view in hagahah and in Darkei Moshe siman 95 that sharp thing does not help noten taam bar noten taam, and he brought proof from Rashba's responsa and Semak there proves the opposite, and he elaborated; in my book I stood on all this and brought Minchat Kohen, Peri Chadash, Peri Megadim, and other poskim, and I brought that borscht which is sharp should be forbidden as Rambam and Taz, and likewise Magen Avraham siman 451 that with sharp thing noten taam bar noten taam does not help; and now I saw in printed Shulchan Aruch with Avnei Nezer on Yoreh De'ah siman 96 challenging Magen Avraham. He wrote in Makom Shmuel siman 90 regarding liquid made from turnip called (ruiti ribin) or (burekish) pickled in water — cooked in a not ben yomo dairy vessel and poured into meat vessel and cooked with meat; if cooked in water first to soften, sharpness is nullified even if very sour; and even though Taz is not lenient with sour apples except in case of loss, here one may be lenient in every case; see there end of his words it appears to be lenient even if not cooked first (there is some contradiction head to end — I abbreviated — it appears all according to whether very sour until comparable to sour fruits, and then if not cooked first one should not be lenient except in case of loss, as above — primary like poskim to be stringent with sharp thing even with noten taam bar noten taam, and in need see there, and such a case one may be lenient here too). See there in the name of Prague sages who ruled with food of onions with meat insulated on Shabbat in not ben yomo dairy pot — permitted because the world's way is to cook those onions from erev Shabbat before insulating in the meat pot to sweeten them — if so sharpness nullified, and it is plain.
