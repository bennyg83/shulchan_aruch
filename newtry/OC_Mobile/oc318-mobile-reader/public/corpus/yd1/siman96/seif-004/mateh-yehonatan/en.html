(There seif 4) Lemon juice that non-Jews bring is permitted because they bring many together, and even if some became forbidden, etc., they are nullified in others, etc. SheLaH challenged: why not say in each case chein nafsho, and afterward the knife absorbs from the cut ones and forbids all cut with the knife? One may say sharpness only absorbs or emits, but emits and absorbs together — no; if so, if the cut ones became forbidden, the knife no longer has power to absorb; if so it is well to rule as Shulchan Aruch ruled.
