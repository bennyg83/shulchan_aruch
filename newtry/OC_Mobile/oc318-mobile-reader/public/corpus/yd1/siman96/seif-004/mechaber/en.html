Lemon juice that non-Jews cut and extract its essence, which they call lemoni"sh zafet — some forbid them because they cut them with their knives and through its sharpness they absorb; but some permit because they cut many and fill a barrel from them, and one may say the knife taste is nullified in the first and second (Shulchan Leket); and in some places they eat pickled cabbage even though it is sliced and cut, for they cut them after pickling (and those who are stringent with sharp preserves and lemon juice should be consistent, for otherwise there are two contradictory matters) (Mahari"v): {Rama: But other things that are not sharp, such as dry apples or dry fruit and the like, are permitted in every case; and likewise turnips are permitted in every case.}
