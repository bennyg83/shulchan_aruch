(There s.k. 9) One does not need sixty only against the knife. At first glance it appears sixty against the knife's peel helps, for we hold below (siman 120 s.k. 7) that through insertion it is permitted to cut a radish, and plainly insertion emits only what is in the knife's peel; if so proven the radish emits only what was absorbed in the knife peel, and sixty against the knife peel suffices. However it is difficult: that which makes for improvement even when clean forbids the amount for removal — behold in the knife peel it is minimal and rinsing suffices as Shach wrote (siman 94 s.k. 28); and since sharpness and pressure emit only what is in the peel, rinsing should suffice for the radish like not ben yomo and well scraped, for even nevertheless sharpness makes for improvement what is in the knife peel — to Shach it appears and proven that sharpness and pressure emit more than what is in the knife peel; if so why does knife insertion help for cutting radish? Requires study.
