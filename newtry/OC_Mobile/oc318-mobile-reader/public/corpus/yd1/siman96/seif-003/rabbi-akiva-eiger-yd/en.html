(There s.k. 5) From the Gemara l'chatchila forbidden. That is since absorption spreads only the amount for removal; but if one cooked greens with meat, tasting helps l'chatchila to cook with milk — so explained Shach in his long book; accordingly what Shach wrote — and that he did not emend, etc., language "all forbidden" implies no remedy — nevertheless through tasting there would be remedy to remove the amount for removal and cook the rest with meat, for if we fear the radish absorbed throughout, tasting would help; one must say Rama's reason we are not expert and do not rely even on Jewish tasting except b'dieved; Shach's language requires study.
