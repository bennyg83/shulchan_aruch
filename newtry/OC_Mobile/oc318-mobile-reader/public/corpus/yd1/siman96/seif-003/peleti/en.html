Spice, etc., ben yomo, etc. — Shach wondered: even not ben yomo too sharpness for improvement, as below siman 103; it appears difficult to Mechaber in the Gemara that says fish that came up in a bowl permitted to eat with kutach — with ben yomo pot, when not ben yomo all permit; and radish cut with knife appears also ben yomo like the bowl; perhaps the Gemara's intent even when not ben yomo — the Gemara should inform us we do not say specifically like the bowl; therefore he holds two actions: we do not say regarding sharpness to make for improvement, and also for noten taam bar noten taam we do not say it has no such power; and investigate from below siman 103 where noten taam bar noten taam is not needed because it is prohibited.
