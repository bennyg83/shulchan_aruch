In the thick head. Be'er Heitev seif 1 in name of Taz: even in thin place at beginning of thick head — treifah. So responsa Mahari of Beit HaLevi kelal 5 siman 55; so Pri Chadash; see responsa Avnei Nezer Orach Chaim part 2 siman 121 who ruled where around hole is skin recognizable as from its nature — so in thick place all acharonim agreed treifah, not like Taz seif 2; but where not actually in thick — kosher — see there. [See responsa Noda BiYehudah siman 16 explained plainly even in such case one must declare treifah — only investigated mixture there — see; even so all who are lenient relying on Avnei Nezer — we do not exalt them, for all Noda BiYehudah's words there revolve on what he decided at beginning of responsa there that one may not rely at all on Taz's permit of this hole entirely because disputants are many, acharonim and gedolei Yisrael; he himself in later responsa strongly upheld Taz's permit and below seif 2 I also brought many gedolei Yisrael agreeing with Taz to permit even actually in thick — plainly lenient in case not actually in thick did not lose.]
