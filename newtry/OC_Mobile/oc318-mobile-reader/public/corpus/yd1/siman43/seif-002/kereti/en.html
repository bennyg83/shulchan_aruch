Perforation through and through in calves at the thick head — perforation through and through and skin surrounds it and it is in one place and recognizable it was created so: Taz and Taz permit; many disagree, headed by Shach in Nekudat HaKesef and Pri Chadash; and I inclined Peleti that I elaborated on this. If not always in one place — certainly treifah; but if always in one place I never permitted in this — only leave to rule one whose heart does not strike him; and where there is no man one may rely in great loss on Taz and his support.
Like thickness of gold dinar from the body of the spleen and not from pure fat that seals — and the like — Taz.
Treifah — see Peleti that I wrote in the name of acharonim: if punctured from kereis to spleen the spleen does not seal the kereis, for impure fat and membranes forbid and separate and they do not seal — see Peleti in explanation of Rashi's words that he wrote punctured via needle; although he explained the matter "insert before us" — nevertheless what did Rashi want with this.
"And even if cut off" — see Peleti that I elaborated in pilpul on "even"; for Baal HaItur's view is to permit if cut even in the hilum — see there and in explanation of Beit Ephraim's words.
With change of flesh — but change of appearance does not disqualify unless it fell to light — and this is simple.
Even murky and putrid — even though he cited; but one law: clear water with murky — see Peleti what he wrote.
And the flesh around it whole — see Peleti that I wrote he intended Taz's words: if the spleen is whole on every side outside and only afflicted inside — even if no gold dinar, because since whole the name "hole" does not apply to it and we learned "punctured" in the mishnah; and if the flesh is bad only the membrane on it is whole — that helps only if the flesh of the spleen too is whole on every side — see Peleti.
