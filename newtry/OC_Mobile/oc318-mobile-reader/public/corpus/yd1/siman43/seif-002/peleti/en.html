If punctured in the thick head, etc. — Taz wrote regarding calves that at the thick head there is perforation through and through and skin surrounds it; in every calf found thus in one place, and people say thus is the way of most calves there — Taz ruled to permit, for this is not a hole but deficiency and kosher; and in responsa Taz he explained his words: in hollow things a hole applies even if created so, but in a flat thing like a leaf a hole does not apply if created so — what difference to me if created deficient at the end of the spleen or in its middle. Taz agreed to permit. Shach in Nekudat HaKesef and Pri Chadash ruled to forbid. One argument: who says it was only at creation and afterward through weakness became so healthy until it looks created — this can be answered since we see all in one directed place, a sign it was made so in nature; if by illness and accident it would not always be in one directed place. In Rashba's responsa siman 483 intestinal holes were not directed in all animals in one place — therefore no proof it was a hole in the small intestine that Rashba doubted whether made afterward by illness or before; there it was not directed in one place, but in the body of the heter there is no hole if created so — I am uncertain from two proofs. One: Rashba wrote if two spleens and one attached to its fellow in hilum — according to "considered removed" with the place of its attachment — treifah, as if removed from hilum and as if hole and treifah; though from creation considered removed, nevertheless treifah — this can be rejected: "considered removed" is tradition from the Sages, and tradition is that for Rabbi extra spleens cause weakness as if in that attached place something was missing and removed somewhat by hand — treifah from weakness in two spleens attached where they make treifah for the animal as if called so now and treifah; and they were precise from that he said "considered removed" and not "considered deficient." Second proof: Tosafot and Rosh and authors were precise that mishnah did not enumerate removed heart, stomach, etc. — all the more from punctured; and mishnah "punctured the omasum" — Rabbi says even if removed — implies more to permit in punctured than removed; they challenged from "if so" and entered with difficulty. Seemingly simple: certainly if punctured treifah, all the more removed — not necessary, for Rashi wrote punctured is stricter than removed because through hole it continues and adds pain and much illness in the body — this applies to hole by hand and illness, but created with hole Rashi's words do not apply, there is no pain and illness — plainly one may say if punctured treifah, all the more removed; so in heart and stomach and the like which are hollow organs where hole applies even at beginning of creation, as Taz wrote, the poskim's "all the more" applies even not from pain — but in spleen where hole at creation does not apply, as Taz, only in category removed; if you find hole in spleen only through illness and thorn — here there is no "all the more" for removed as Rashi. However one may say conversely: from Tosafot's precision from Rashi who wrote therefore punctured treifah and removed spleen kosher because it adds illness and pain — created with hole from "if so" wherever "punctured" is said even if created so, for there is no pain — necessarily Rashi holds hole at creation does not apply to spleen, as Taz; and this requires further study. Truth: I always inclined to be stringent in doubt of law; only this I gave my heart: as Taz and Taz wrote, all was in calves and many such questions came in calves; in oxen and cows such a question was never mentioned — only once. It is astounding: if common in calves, it should be common in oxen and cows, for a calf becomes an ox when it grows. From this I judged: when the animal grows the hole heals and deficiency fills and flesh returns as before — therefore not felt in a large animal; hence a sign it is not absolute treifah — how would it heal if treifah, and how always membrane from a blow; rather nature did not complete creation and deficiency is found, and in many days deficiency fills — very conceivable to naturalists; therefore my view tends to permit; nevertheless I never permitted all my days — only leave others to permit whose heart does not strike them; where there is no man one may not permit except in great loss and great investigation needed.
Perforation through and through. Rashi explained: if there remained like thickness of gold dinar and needle did not come out. Bach wants to save from difficulty that it would be needle in body cavity — explained hole via kereis and spleen attached seals it. True Rashi did not hold as below siman 46 seif 1 in hagahah that another organ does not seal, as appears from Rashi daf 49b s.v. tarfesha; nevertheless impure fat separates between spleen and kereis and it is like a hat and does not seal, for there is space on the hole and it is not tight inside the hole — what does it help that spleen is attached and seals? Therefore all agreed we deal with needle in body cavity inserted before us — no further concern for other inner organs; true for law, but I am not released: why did Rashi need needle inserted before us and not simply punctured through illness and putrefaction as daily practice? I said much and did not succeed that it be close to Rashi's intent; what appears most: difficult for Rashi the Gemara language "if there remained like gold dinar kosher" — implies to permit it came with dinar remainder kosher; in truth reversed — unspecified hole is perforation through and through everywhere in all treifot; if not Gemara words, even any remainder like egg membrane would be kosher — Gemara was stringent to require dinar thickness; what does it teach kosher — as if coming to teach us a permitted prohibition? It should say: and if dinar-thickness did not remain treifah — do not say regarding perforation through and through. Therefore Rashi holds we deal with needle; Rashi holds in melting organ since it has only one membrane we fear perhaps it healed — not regarding perforation through and through; if so also in spleen hole with needle one need not perforation through and through at all — we fear perhaps it healed; therefore Gemara in spleen only says if dinar-thickness remained kosher — and teaches much to permit: we do not forbid hole that is not perforation through and through; examine well; Rashi's words are clear.
