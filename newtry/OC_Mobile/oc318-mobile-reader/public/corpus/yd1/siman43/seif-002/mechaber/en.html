The spleen: one end is thick and the other thin, like the creation of the tongue. If it was punctured in the thick end — if the hole is a perforation through and through — treifah. If punctured with a hole that is not a perforation through and through — if there remains beneath it like the thickness of a gold dinar — permitted; less than this is considered as if perforated and treifah. But if the thin end was punctured — kosher. {Rama: And even if it was cut off in the thin place — kosher (Tur). If the spleen was torn, or putrefied, or afflicted with a change of flesh — its law is like punctured (Shaarei Dura and Hagahot Asheri). And so everywhere the flesh is bad and rotted — its law is like punctured. And if there is water in the spleen — even if murky and putrid — if the flesh around it is whole — kosher (Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai and Hagahot Asheri, and see above siman 50).}
