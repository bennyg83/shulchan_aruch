(Shulchan Aruch seif 5) Two spleens found. Sefer Taz siman 98 permitted with two spleens one small punctured in hilum like Barda siman 35; so Shulchan Aruch siman 35 and 44; difficult from Chullin 55 Rav Avira from Rava: only removed spleen kosher but punctured treifah; Rabbi Yosi bar Avin challenges cutting from fetus permitted from spleen and kidneys forbidden eating — animal itself permitted; what does he challenge Rav Avira — perhaps mishnah deals with two spleens and permit recognizable e.g. smaller than other and cut from permitted spleen; per Taz no treifah disqualifies permitted organ; one may say therefore mishnah does not deal cutting from excess spleen — if so why teach cutting forbidden in eating — reason because said in animal "that you shall eat" expounded whole not deficient — see Rashi; meaning only when cutting from required organ must be whole animal and he lacks by cutting; but cutting from excess organ not relevant to forbid — not whole applies; so Tosafot there 67 regarding horns in animal not forbidden because "that" whole not deficient — nothing deficient; if so necessarily only one spleen deals.
