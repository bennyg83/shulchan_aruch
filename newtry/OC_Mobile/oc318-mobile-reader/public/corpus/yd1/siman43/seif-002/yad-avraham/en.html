(Siman 43 seif 2) The spleen, etc. — perforation through and through treifah. See Taz: I was asked calves found, etc., I answered to permit, etc.; so Mateh Yehudah kelal 89 wrote to permit hole created in organs without cavity; so Taz siman 71; Noda BiYehudah siman 17 lenient on hole created in spleen because novelty as Beit Yosef — you have in it only its novelty — punctured by thorn or illness; nevertheless in great loss rely on Taz in his case; but large ox implies in Kerem Shlomo do not be lenient. Mateh Yehudah also wrote to permit in great loss internal deficiency in spleen as Taz this siman and Kerem Shlomo.
