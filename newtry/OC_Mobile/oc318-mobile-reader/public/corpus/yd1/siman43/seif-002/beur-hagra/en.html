"And even if it was cut off," etc. Mishnah at end of chapter 4 of the spleen, etc.; and it is not necessary for the latter version there 55a except even for the first version — namely according to the view that we do not distinguish between hilum and loose end; but according to us the mishnah is in the loose end, as Ran wrote; and it is implied from the hagahah words that if cut in the hilum it is invalid as above — even for the latter version, for according to us this is unnecessary; and all the more the first version — see Ran.
(Likut) "And even if cut off," etc. — in Beit Ephraim he wrote even if cut in the thick place kosher, like the latter version; but in Teshuvot HaRashba he disagreed, for in his responsa he followed the stringent (end of his words).
"Afflicted," etc. See above siman 50.
"And if water," etc. Peri Megadim and Shach wrote specifically if dinar-thickness remained; but Pri Chadash wrote since whole on every side it is unnecessary; and so regarding punctured.
