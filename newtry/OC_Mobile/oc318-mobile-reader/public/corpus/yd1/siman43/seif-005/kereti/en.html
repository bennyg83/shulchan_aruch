Two spleens — see Taz: if two spleens found and known which spleen is correct and which is excess and punctured in hilum — kosher; and he learned this from kidneys; and there too Maharshal obtained — see Peleti that I elaborated.
On the thick side, etc. — this is the Ramban's view who holds excess is considered as removed like a hole in that place — and punctured in hilum is treifah.
