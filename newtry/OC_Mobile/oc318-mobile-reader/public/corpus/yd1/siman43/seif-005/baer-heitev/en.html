Great loss. The reason of the lenient: for according to what we hold "all excess is considered as removed" — as if both were removed and the spleen removed is kosher; and this is the view of Rashi, Rambam, and Rosh. Bach and Maharshal ruled one must be stringent and declare treifah even in great loss. Shach ruled like Rama's view who holds "all excess is considered as removed" means from the place where it is attached — as if that place was removed — and it is as if the spleen was punctured in its hilum; and this is the Ramban's view. Animal that had two spleens and one of them was punctured in the hilum — Taz Taz siman 98 ruled: if recognizable which is primary and it was not punctured — kosher; if not recognizable — treifah from doubt; and Pri Chadash wrote in every manner one must declare treifah.
