See Taz challenged Rambam who explained what Levi taught all treifot Sages enumerated in animal corresponding in fowl — refers to mishnah treifot not Amora treifot; treifot of kidney and spleen they did not enumerate in animal in mishnah. Taz challenged we find this language Gemara 54 Rav Pappa's house struck kidney killed — came before Rabbi Abba — you have only what Sages enumerated — that certainly not mishnah alone but all Amoraim enumerated; if so whence to distinguish and explain other matter. It appears to me Rambam's explanation necessary from sugya Piskei Avodah Zarah 57 Rav Shemuel thigh dislocated in fowl kosher; Tosafot forced there implies specifically fowl permits not animal — challenges beraisa Levi all treifot; per our master it is spacious Levi refers only mishnah treifot and dislocated thigh not in mishnah; afterward I saw Rashba responsa 294 and Turei Chayim so himself; further appears this Levi beraisa arranged from Tannaim before (see Rashi beginning Beitzah); therefore "Sages enumerated" in mishnah and beraisa specifically; not like Rabbi Abba "Sages enumerated" who was Amora unlike Sages in beraisa — only Tannaim mentioned in mishnah and beraisa; further Niddah 18 even Amora language calls Sages only mishnah and beraisa not Amora sugya; so Be'er Sheva and Turei Chayim chapter 35. With this also Pri Chadash's difficulty on Rambam from Piskei Avodah Zarah 56 comparing fowl lung to animal for treifot though not similar for cutting testicles — we would also compare spleen of fowl to animal for treifot stringently; per what I said to me Levi treifot of spleen not mentioned in mishnah and beraisa unlike lung treifot mentioned in mishnah.
