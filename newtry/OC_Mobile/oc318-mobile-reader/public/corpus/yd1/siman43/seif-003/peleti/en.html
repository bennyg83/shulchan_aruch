All attached to kereis called thick and no more — in Lechem Mishneh wrote after attachment to kereis also thick and rests on kereis but not attached; Gaon author of Kli Yakar and sages of Prague agreed; he too in number to forbid; afterward retracted — most authors unlike him, Ran and ShDa wrote what attached to kereis called thick and no more; truly per what I wrote and found in Perishah — reason treifah not from spleen but affliction called to kereis — clear all depends on attachment to kereis; nevertheless I incline lenient since issued from mouths of gedolei hador to be stringent; Lechem Mishneh also said for halacha not practice; conversely since after attachment place also thick — if primary what attached to kereis how Gemara stam in hilum — there is hilum nevertheless kosher; how stam in hilum Rashi in its thickness above — therefore if spleen came not punctured through and through only remainder and remainder below in extreme thinness until no doubt no dinar-thickness remainder — I declare treifah — called thick; but if remainder only we are not expert dinar measure need specifically until half — in this plain affliction more than half; if on thick side not attached I permit — two doubts: perhaps dinar-thickness, perhaps not; if you say no doubt which is thick place perhaps loose end and hole does not disqualify at all — likewise reverse — must permit.
