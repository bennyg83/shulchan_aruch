And Maharshal wrote Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 85 that it is a great leniency to uproot the principle they said: everything that makes treifah in an animal — the corresponding in fowl; and the opposite is reasonable — everything is called hilum in fowl. And in Maharil he did not permit affliction in the entire kidney of fowl even though its form is not at all like that of an animal, but because they are closed in bone and the intestines do not reach there — if so, spleen of fowl which is in the place of intestines is treifah like in an animal; nevertheless since Rashba already ruled leniency, an elder has taught leniency — I do not forbid those who permit and rely on his ruling; but Heaven forbid for us to permit ab initio, end of his words. And Maharshal corrected Rambam's words: since they said in the Gemara "treifot that the Sages enumerated in an animal — corresponding in fowl," namely what they enumerated in the mishnah — and spleen and kidney were not enumerated in the mishnah. And it is difficult — for we find this language again in the Gemara (daf 54): in the house of Rav Pappa they struck the kidney and killed it; it came before Rabbi Abba; he said to them: is there adding to the treifot? You have only what the Sages enumerated — and that certainly does not refer to the mishnah alone but to all that the Amoraim enumerated. If so, from where to distinguish and explain there a different matter? Also Ra'ah in Beit Habechirah wrote to forbid in fowl — therefore every person of spirit should be stringent for himself in this. It appears to me that what they forbade through putrefaction or affliction on the thick side is specifically when it begins from outside and goes inward; but if the spleen outside is whole and afflicted inside — there is no prohibition, for it is not stricter than lung where we say internal deficiency is not called deficiency — and know from that they said in the Gemara: kosher in lung, invalid in kidney — namely putrefied; and they did not say so in spleen where invalid in it what is kosher in lung, namely regarding internal deficiency. And so appears Maharshal's view in what he cited in his name in siman 44 seif 2; nevertheless I fear to be lenient since it is not explicit in the poskim.
