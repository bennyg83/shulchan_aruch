Reached majority. The Bach rules like Maharam that if a minor reaches majority during the seven days, he counts seven and thirty from the time he reaches majority. If he reaches majority after thirty days, the restrictions of seven and thirty no longer apply to him. See there. The Taz discusses this law at length and concludes that in practical halachah the Shulchan Aruch's ruling appears correct: a minor is exempt from mourning, for its reasoning is sound and clear. Our great earlier teachers left us room, with Heaven's help, to merit clarifying this matter. See siman 50.
