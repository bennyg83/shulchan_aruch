See Chatam Sofer responsa siman 342, who wrote regarding a father-in-law whose family suffered a misfortune at a time of plague, may the Merciful One protect us, and they did not mourn then because of fear, as above at the end of siman 374: certainly, if the fury passed within thirty days, they must mourn, as written in Nechalat Shiv'ah responsa in the name of the Gaon Maharam Shapira, that a sick mourner, if he recovered, must mourn within thirty days. Even the Rosh, who disagrees with Maharam of Rothenburg regarding a minor who became an adult, concedes here; see there.
