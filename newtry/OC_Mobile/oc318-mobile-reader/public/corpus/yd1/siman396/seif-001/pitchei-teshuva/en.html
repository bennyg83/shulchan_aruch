But if he treated it lightly. Radbaz, new responsa 521, discusses one who heard that his father died, but a relative falsely told him the report was untrue, and two days later admitted it was true. The relative acted improperly and caused him to miss the Torah-level mourning of the day of burial, since recent news is like the day of burial. Legally, however, he counts only from the first report. [Citation 7.]
