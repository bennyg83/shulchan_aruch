Bach writes that where he was prevented by circumstances, such as being dangerously ill and unable to rend, or lacking clear and settled awareness, and after seven days he returned to health and clarity, that later moment is considered his moment of acute grief. This differs from one who acted inadvertently or deliberately and allowed the moment of grief to pass while healthy and obligated. Here he was not truly able to experience or act upon the grief until he recovered and regained a settled mind. At that time the obligation of rending takes effect.
