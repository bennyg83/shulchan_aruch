(Siman 396, seif 1) Except for rending. Tiferet LeMoshe writes that this means if he observed mourning but failed to rend, he does not rend after seven days. But if he observed no mourning at all, in circumstances where he must later complete the full thirty days, he also rends when he begins observing the seven-day laws. See there. However, the ruling of Knesset HaGedolah in Responsa Ba'ei Chayei, Yoreh Deah siman 239, indicates he disagrees and holds that in every case he does not rend after the time has passed.
