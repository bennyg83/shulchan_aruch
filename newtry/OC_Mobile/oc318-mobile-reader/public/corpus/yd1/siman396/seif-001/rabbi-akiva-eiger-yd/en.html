(Siman 396, seif 1) Except for rending. Tiferet LeMoshe writes that this means that if he observed mourning but did not rend his garment, he does not rend after seven days. If he observed no mourning at all and must therefore complete the full thirty days, he also rends when he begins the laws of the seven days. See there. The ruling of the Knesset HaGedolah in his responsa Ba'ei Chayei, Yoreh De'ah 239, indicates that he disagrees, and holds that in every case he does not rend afterward.
