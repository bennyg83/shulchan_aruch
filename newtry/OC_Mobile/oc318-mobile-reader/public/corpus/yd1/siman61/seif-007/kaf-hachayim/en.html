(29) [Seif 7] Give them to a kohen chaver; if no chaver — to am ha'aretz. If a kohen am ha'aretz took them — it appears we do not remove from his hand for he already acquired them; that which we say not to give am ha'aretz when there is a chaver — is l'chatchila; but b'dieved if given or he took — he acquired. Radbaz on Rambam chapter 12 of Hilchot Terumot. Erech HaShulchan 5. Zivchei Tzedek 17. Darkei Teshuvah 32.
