That he receive immediately from his acquaintances. Perishah brought Rashi in chapter Kol HaGet: since he is his acquaintance, all kohanim divert their attention from him — see there; per the matter here assigning to a Torah scholar who takes all gifts of those who slaughter — none of this is needed, for can one not make an agent to receive gifts on his behalf — the Torah scholar receiving acquires through the kohen's agency; but Rashi wrote there they never reached the kohen's hand — agency does not apply; likewise here if this Israelite assigns for the kohen before gifts reached another's hand — agency does not apply; nevertheless he acquires through agency — plain.
