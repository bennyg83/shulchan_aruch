A kohen who has acquaintances who regularly give him their gifts may assign them to an Israelite so that he receive them immediately from his acquaintances — but only if the assignee is a Torah scholar and the hour presses upon him, and the assigning kohen is not a servant in that house to whom he assigns the gifts, lest he assign against his will (Rambam chapter 9 of Hilchot Bikkurim); and the same law applies if the kohen serves in the house of the homeowner and assigns to a Torah scholar guest lodging in his teacher's house with gifts that his teacher regularly gives him — it is forbidden (Rashi and Tur):
