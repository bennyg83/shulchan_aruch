Kohen who has acquaintances — see Peleti I clarified; certainly if gifts came to that Torah scholar's hand he plainly acquired — he is the kohen's agent and one's agent is like oneself; but even if he sold before they came to another's hand — agency does not apply — nevertheless Chazal made him as assignee since his acquaintance is known to all — diverted attention and he acquires from hefker; so Rashi.
May assign to Israelite — and if the Torah scholar retracted in any way he did not acquire — behold agency was cancelled; also if he died, likewise for the heir.
