And there are those who say, etc. Rashi and Tosafot 136b, and Rashi in chapter 1 of Shabbat (10b) — one reason for reishit hagaz and gifts — we derive giving from terumah as 131a; first reasoning was custom — in this they practiced, in this not; see reason in Ran and proof R' Nehorai himself wove a cloak and all amoraim practiced gifts as written; see Rashi and Tosafot 138b s.v. only, etc. — but Rambam challenged why need a verse for reishit hagaz from gifts — they are not comparable; therefore must hold halacha like R' Elai; so is primary. (Likutim) see Mordechai chapter HaZeroah and Hagahot Semak seif 66 s.v. they asked Rashi, etc., and he answered, etc., and whoever gives — blessing upon him, etc. (end).
