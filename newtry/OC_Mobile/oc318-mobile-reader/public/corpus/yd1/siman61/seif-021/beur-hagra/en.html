And there are those who say, etc. Rashi and Tosafot 136b, and Rashi in chapter 1 of Shabbat (10b) — one reason for reishit hagaz and gifts: we derive giving from terumah, as [stated] 131a. The first view holds it is custom — in this they practiced, in this not; see the reason in Ran, and the proof that R. Nehorai himself wove a cloak, and all the Amoraim practiced gifts, as written; see Rashi and Tosafot 138b, s.v. "only, etc." But Rambam challenged: why does reishit hagaz need a verse from [analogy to] gifts? — they are not analogous; therefore he must hold the law follows R. Elai; and so is the primary [view].
(Supplement) See Mordechai, chapter HaZero'a, and Hagahot Semak there, seif 66, s.v. "they asked Rashi, etc. — and he answered, etc. — and whoever gives, may blessing come upon him, etc." (End.)
