And so they practiced — we hold like R' Elai in reishit hagaz as the world practiced reishit hagaz like R' Elai; Maharsha challenged Rashi who explained in the mishnah foreleg and cheeks in land and outside land — because of chullin and kodashim he mentioned it — he challenged: this very clause teaches outside land unlike our conclusion; one may say Rashi holds since the mishnah teaches reishit hagaz in land and outside land — all the more priestly gifts; therefore in the mishnah of foreleg it is redundant — therefore Rashi explained because of kodashim, etc. — end. They wrote: if one wishes to be stringent and give to a kohen to fulfill a positive mitzvah — no arrogance; I truly do not know practical people stringent in many mitzvah details and stringencies — why do they not return to fulfill the biblical positive commandment which most authors hold applies in the diaspora and most of Chazal in the Gemara practiced; perhaps because we have no known patrilineal kohen, only presumptive kohanim (see responsum Chasam Sofer and what Magen Avraham wrote on this). Indeed my famous pious son-in-law Rav Binyamin Wolf Frankenthal z"l at his son's pidyon haben — the excellent Bach Rav Zelig took his animal for the slaughterer to buy meat for the meal; he wished to give gifts to the kohen who redeemed his son and said: why fear — only presumptive kohen — I redeem my son from him; he regretted perhaps it is in the category of not giving all gifts to one kohen for pidyon and this — therefore gave gifts to my famous father-in-law Rav Yitzchak Shapira z"l who ate them for his wife my mother-in-law Mrs. Matti daughter of my grandfather the leader Rav Mordechai Cohen z"l; I doubt whether customary to give to a kohen without acceptance to practice this mitzvah even in diaspora; one must always fulfill this mitzvah as regarding Maariv — optional and similar — requires further study, for most householders lack animals to slaughter; many gave to complete scholars who went to Eretz Yisrael with money appointed agents to buy animals and separate priestly gifts — foreleg, cheeks, reishit hagaz — one's agent is like oneself; I doubted since he cannot act, path is far — how appoint agent; Tosafot Nazir on separating challah — I elaborated elsewhere — all hearts seek God.
