(Seif 9) One should not divide among many kohanim, etc. It implies if one wishes to give all gifts to one kohen he may. So Rambam chapter 9 Hilchot Bikkurim halacha 17 explicitly. And it implies even for all his days. It is difficult from Eruvin 63: one who gives all his gifts to one kohen brings famine to the world. One may say: specifically all gifts in his house — otherwise it would say "all his gifts"; Rif's text. But stam "gifts" everywhere means foreleg, cheeks, and maw as Rambam wrote in the introduction there. It appears Rambam derived from Bava Batra 123 and see Chiddushei Aggadot. See Tosafot Kiddushin 27 that it is proven one may give one item entirely to one kohen but not two gifts in one year — see there. And in my work on Rambam I elaborated.
