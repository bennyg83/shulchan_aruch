A bechor before it came into the kohen's possession that became mixed with other animals — one must give the gifts from them, for the kohen may say regarding each one: give me its gifts; and if you say it is the bechor, give me all of it. But a bechor that was given to the kohen in its blemished state and he sold it to an Israelite and it became mixed with other animals — if many people slaughter them, all are exempt, for each can say the bechor is mine; and if one person slaughters all of them, they exempt him only for one of them:
