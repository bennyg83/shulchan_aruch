(9) [Seif 3] The cheeks are from the joint of the cheek, etc. — and the cheeks are the lower cheek; Scripture calls them "cheeks" in the plural because there are two joints, right and left. So is in Pesikta and Sifrei, unlike Targum Yonatan ben Uziel who wrote upper and lower cheek. Perishah 1. Perishah 4. Mechaber 3 — and he wrote this translation is not from Yonatan ben Uziel. She'arim Shemen 1; and wrote as in Gemara Zivchei Tzedek 5.
