(Seif 11) In a place where they do not recognize he is a kohen, etc. Perishah wrote: where they do not recognize he is a kohen, raising hands does not help; in Gemara chapter HaZeroah it deals when he was already presumed a kohen and raising hands suffices so priesthood is not forgotten — per Rashi there. So too Peri Megadim in name of Bach. Requires further study — from Rambam chapter 8 of Avodah Zarah and idolatry seif 6 it is proven raising hands helps for priestly gifts even if not presumed. However one may say it helps only rabbinically, not d'oraita, and priestly gifts are d'oraita even now, lest one come to nullify the mitzvah of giving. Shulchan Aruch there implies it helps even for eating priestly gifts in our time — unlike Perishah and Bach. Perhaps Taz's explanation "not recognizing" literally as Peri Megadim — no clear proof from Rashi; requires further study.
