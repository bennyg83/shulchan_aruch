Roasted — as written "for anointing and greatness" as kings eat; if so, how may he treat them disrespectfully by giving to dogs? One must say per Maharshal — not permitted to dogs except when spoiled; also selling gifts to an Israelite — no mitzvah to eat roasted; Perishah. Ostensibly one may say "for anointing and greatness" is only rabbinic and asmakhta — "for anointing" is not written regarding foreleg and cheeks; see Rambam — it is a new mitzvah; but Tosafot Bekhorot chapter Ad Kamah 26b wrote in all gifts "for anointing" is written even foreleg and cheeks as here — requires further study what they teach, for the Gemara is on these gifts eaten only roasted as written "for anointing," etc. — rather they teach it is d'oraita, proof we decree to treat as profane; these bodies rabbinically we do not decree.
