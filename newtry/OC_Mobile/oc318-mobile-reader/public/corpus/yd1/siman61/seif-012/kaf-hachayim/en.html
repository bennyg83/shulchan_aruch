(27) A nine-month fetus that spread on the ground — since it requires slaughter as in siman 13 — obligated in gifts per all; however it appears since it spread on the ground it does not require slaughter d'oraita but rabbinically for appearance — if the homeowner does not wish to give gifts we do not remove from his hand; if the kohen seized — also we do not remove.
