(27) A nine-month fetus that spread on the ground — since it requires slaughter as in siman 13 — obligated in gifts per all; however it appears since it spread on the ground it does not require slaughter d'oraita but rabbinically for appearance — if the homeowner does not wish to give gifts we do not remove from his hand; if the kohen seized — also we do not remove. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagah Taz 5. However Perishah there wrote what is permitted through its mother's slaughter — even if it spread on the ground requiring rabbinic slaughter — exempt from gifts; so Kereti 7; Ya'avetz on this seif. Aruch HaShulchan 15.
