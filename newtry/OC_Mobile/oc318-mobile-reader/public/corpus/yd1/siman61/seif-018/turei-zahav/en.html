In half the gifts. For it is written "if sheep" — we expound even partial sheep; from the father he is exempt — perhaps we are concerned for father's seed; therefore obligated in half and exempt in half; but Tur understood per Rosh that he is obligated in all gifts from this reason — for half is obvious; why need a verse unless to include all gifts; Shach challenged Rambam who wrote koy from animal, etc. — "separate" means separation in any case; Beit Yosef wrote Rambam holds koy is its own species and all agree; Rashal wrote Tur's correct text regarding koy or from animal and bird — Rambam's "separate" is unnecessary since one does not give.
