Took by weight — since they profited, obligated to fulfill Heaven; buyer likewise must pay kohen and cannot waive since they are in existence and not yet eaten; Perishah in Ran's name: gifts not separated are as separated; if after death kohen seized animal per those who hold benefit of use is not money — kohen acquires from hefker; if benefit of use is money — owner's hand is upon it; I elaborated in Choshen Mishpat Ohrim uTumim; see Peleti.
