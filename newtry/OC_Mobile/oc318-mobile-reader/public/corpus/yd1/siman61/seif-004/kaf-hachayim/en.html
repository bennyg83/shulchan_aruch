(18) And the same if kohanim were lenient regarding wool and skin on the cheeks — it would likewise be so. Taz s.k. 7. Knesset HaGedolah there end letter 15. Perishah 9. Kereti 4. Mateh Yehonatan 7. (19) It appears if one kohen comes and says he does not wish to waive — we do not listen to him since they practiced leniency toward him. Mahari Korkus in chapter 9 of Hilchot Bikkurim din 14. Erech HaShulchan 1. Aruch HaShulchan there. Zivchei Tzedek 10. Darkei Teshuvah 23.
(20) And in a place where the custom is known not to leave — it is forbidden for one kohen to leave, for he is like a kohen assisting in the threshing floor — as Perishah wrote 43. Darkei Teshuvah 21. It appears this is when it is a significant matter; if not significant — we have no concern.
