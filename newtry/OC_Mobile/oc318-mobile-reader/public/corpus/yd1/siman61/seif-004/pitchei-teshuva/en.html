If there is no kohen there. See in responsa Radbaz Chadashot siman 300 who wrote: specifically when there is no kohen there does one appraise them in money; if there is a kohen there — it is they, not their value. And specifically one appraises in money — meaning to appraise them at their worth, for the language "ma'aleh" everywhere means thus; but less than that is robbery of the tribe. And specifically also to appraise them first and then eat them, but not to eat them and afterward appraise in money. See there at length.
