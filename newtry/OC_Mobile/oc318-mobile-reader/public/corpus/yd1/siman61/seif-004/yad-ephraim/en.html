And found treifah — exempt. Avnei Nezer s.k. 5; see Perishah and Peleti; even treifah by later stringencies exempt since kohen also observes that stringency — would be for his dog; written "give to kohen not to his dog" as in Chullin; Meshiv Tzedek wrote treifah obligated per Rambam and Semag — he answered from reishit hagaz and Niddah 91 and Chullin slaughtering cow with live nine-month fetus; Tosafot Pesachim 33 may reject as he wrote; Peri To'ar obligated by rabbinic stringency — see Peleti; Perishah on slaughtering kosher and finding live nine-month fetus even if spread on ground — exempt from gifts; Kereti — see there.
