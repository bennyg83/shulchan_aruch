(33) A kohen who marries women in sin or intentionally becomes impure through contact with the dead — one does not give him gifts even if there is no other kohen; rather appraise them in money and give to a kohen who becomes available later. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagah Taz 23. Mateh Yehonatan 9. Shulchan Gavoah Machaneh Ephraim 3. Erech HaShulchan 7. Zivchei Tzedek 21. Darkei Teshuvah 33.
