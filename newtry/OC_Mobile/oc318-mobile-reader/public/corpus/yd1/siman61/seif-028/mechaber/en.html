One who slaughters his fellow Israelite's animal — the obligation is upon the slaughterer to give its gifts to the kohen; and if he slaughtered a kohen's or gentile's animal, he is exempt: {Rama: However, the benefit of use belongs to the owners, and he may give them to any kohen he wishes (Beit Yosef in name of Ran); and it is permitted for the owners to take a dinar from the kohen to give him the gifts; and likewise an Israelite may give the gifts to his daughter's son the kohen (Beit Yosef in name of R' Yitzchak and in the chapter "Until when").}
