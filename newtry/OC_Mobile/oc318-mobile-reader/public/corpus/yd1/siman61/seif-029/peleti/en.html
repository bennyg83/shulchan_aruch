To reconcile: either condition or reservation — doubt; see chapter 2 Nazir; therefore buyer is muchzak in the animal — I claim it was a condition, condition void — gift on Torah matter, sale stands, burden of proof; but land — buyer is not muchzak, seller is muchzak — land remains in owner's possession — seller claims reservation, not gift on Torah — if buyer wants to uphold sale he upholds condition; Shach may also intend this — his language unclear; Perishah wrongly said he spoke incorrectly; one may say per chapter HaZahav: gift on Torah matter, condition void — specifically definite uprooting; in doubt all agree condition stands. In gifts certainly uprooted — gift to give him gifts — not coming to giving at all; unlike seller of field — many contingencies before fruit, tithe, etc. — not definite uprooting; also I do not know the essence of tithe mitzvah from Torah to separate and give Levi and kohen — if owner or another gives — not uprooting what Torah said.
