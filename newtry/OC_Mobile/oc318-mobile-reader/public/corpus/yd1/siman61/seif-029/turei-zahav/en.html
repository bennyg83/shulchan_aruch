It is a condition. Meaning the condition helps since he reserved the gifts for himself; unlike when he says on condition — not reservation language but mere condition, ineffective — gift on Torah matter; see Choshen Mishpat 212 — I challenged Tur and Shulchan Aruch against each other; more astonishing on Rambam who wrote same here explicitly and in Hilchot Maaser chapter 7 — Cohen sold field on condition tithe is mine forever — Rambam and Gemara in HaMocher et HaBayit and Tosafot and Rosh disagree whether on condition is reservation — I challenged this before great ones without satisfaction.
