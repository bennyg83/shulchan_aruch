It is a condition. Meaning a full condition — 'except' is a reservation:
On condition the gifts are mine, etc. And Rosh wrote the same regarding one who sells a field and said on condition the tithe is mine — the law is thus, for we hold 'on condition' is a condition and not a reservation; if so, it is a gift on what is written in the Torah and his condition is void; but Rambam, even though in Hilchot Bikkurim chapter 9 din 11 he ruled one who sells on condition gives to any kohen he wishes, nevertheless in Hilchot Maaser din 19 he wrote they are his since he said on condition — it became as if he reserved the place of the tithe; it appears he holds that since the Gemara in chapter HaMocher et HaBayit regarding one who sells a field said when he said on condition he reserved for the place of tithe, clearly halacha is thus, and only regarding gifts since he gave the whole animal to the Israelite we do not say he reserved; unlike a field where land remains in the owner's possession; see Ramban's novellae, chapter HaMocher et HaBayit daf 54, top 13a:
