(1) [Seif 1] A man is obligated to give the kohen the foreleg, etc. — of an ox and sheep; and the same for all domesticated pure animals, excluding undomesticated animal as below seif 17. Shach s.k. 1; Perishah letter 1; Kereti 1; Shaarei De'ah 1; Beiur Avraham in Shaarei Perishah 7 letter 1; Beiur Tziyon 1; Zivchei Tzedek 1; whether male or female. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagah Beit Yosef letter 66. Mahariik in hagahah on Shulchan Aruch. Shulchan Gavoah letter 3.
