(1) [Seif 1] A man is obligated to give the kohen the foreleg, etc. — of an ox and sheep; and the same for all domesticated pure animals, excluding undomesticated animal as below seif 17. (33) A kohen who marries women in sin or intentionally becomes impure through contact with the dead — one does not give him gifts even if there is no other kohen; rather appraise them in money and give to a kohen who becomes available later.
