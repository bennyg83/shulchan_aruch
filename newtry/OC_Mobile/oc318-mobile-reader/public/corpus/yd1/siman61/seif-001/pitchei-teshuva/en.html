To give to the kohen. See in Sha'ar HaMelech chapter 3 of Hilchot Ishut halacha 5 who brought that the Rav, author of Perishah in Mayim Chayim siman 4, was uncertain whether regarding pidyon haben and other priestly gifts we say giving against one's will counts as giving, and since the Israelite gave to the kohen even if the kohen did not wish to receive — has he fulfilled giving, or not; he resolved that giving against one's will counts as giving even for pidyon haben and other priestly gifts; in Sefer Kol Yaakov on Rambam's language 4:57 he challenged this — see there.
