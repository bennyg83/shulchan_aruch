(15) There. That one may not scald nor flay the skin, etc. — meaning even if he wishes to scald and give the kohen the wool separately, or flay the skin and give the skin separately — he may not, since he does not know what pleases the kohen — perhaps the kohen prefers to scald, or prefers more to flay the skin. Mateh Yehonatan 5. Darkei Teshuvah 16. And see below letter 18.
