Before he separates, etc. Ran wrote that gifts that were not separated are considered as if they were separated, and if a whole slaughtered animal comes into the kohen's possession he acquires its gifts; and if another ate them afterward, he is liable — but specifically when a slaughtered animal comes to the kohen; but while alive, not, for the obligation has not yet taken effect — it is like one who separates his dough when it is still flour, which is not challah, and it is robbery in the kohen's hand; and so too Maharshal, chapter HaZeroah, seif 4:
