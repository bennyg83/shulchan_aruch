Tosafot's view is that there is a prohibition l'chatchila; Perishah brought that according to Tosafot what is the novelty of Yechezkel who praised himself for not eating before gifts were separated — Tosafot in 36 rejected that Yechezkel was a kohen and a kohen may not eat tevel; it appears Yechezkel the prophet was in Babylonia where priestly gifts do not apply as halacha is decided, yet he was stringent not to eat without separating gifts; from this one may learn to be stringent in the diaspora as a pious person like Yechezkel.
