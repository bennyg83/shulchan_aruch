To snatch the gifts. For it is written "and he shall give" — not that he take by himself; and even to ask verbally — as written regarding sons of Shmuel "they turned aside after gain" — meaning they asked with their mouths.
When there are many kohanim. Meaning that until now there were enough gifts for kohanim; therefore they would wait until the slaughterer gives; here there are many kohanim and gifts are insufficient — one might think since not enough for each, if he waits it is better to present himself to take from love of mitzvah — it teaches the modest withdraw their hands; they learned this from the showbread — before the curse there was enough for each double portion, and after the curse the modest withdrew since not enough; likewise here with few gifts and many kohanim.
In a place where they do not recognize him. This is learned from what is said in the Gemara about Abbaye who said that in any case where many kohanim gather he too comes to take lest priesthood be forgotten; they challenged: but he would be presumed via the platform for priesthood; they answered he was forced that time — Rif and Rosh explained illness of bowels; Rashi explained set times for students who disturbed him from raising hands when the congregation gathered; Tur summarized all this in "place where they do not recognize him" — in any case priesthood is not recognizable to all; thus what was challenged on Tur against the Gemara is resolved.
