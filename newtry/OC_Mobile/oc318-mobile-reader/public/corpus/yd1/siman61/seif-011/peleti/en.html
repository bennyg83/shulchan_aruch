In a place where they do not recognize. In the Gemara 133 they challenge: let him spread his hands; they answer: that time he was forced. Therefore it should not have been written only where he can raise hands — unnecessary; Perishah forced himself here. It appears he holds: what challenge to spread hands to the platform all year — Rashi plainly says this is great trouble, and some know from this and some do not; rather we require raising hands not as a sign he is kohen, for taking gifts is also not a sign — perhaps his wife is a kohenet as above, or the opposite sign as on Erev Yom Kippur when many gifts are taken and all take and he does not — they judge he is not kohen and profanes priesthood; therefore to avoid suspicion he takes. The Gemara challenges: if so how did Abbaye leave all year without raising hands — how avoid suspicion they say because he does not raise hands though it is a positive mitzvah with a blessing — certainly profanation; the Gemara answers: this is not difficult regarding a Torah scholar — they know now he was forced and prevents this — no suspicion, unlike withholding gifts where there is suspicion fulfilled through an agent; but not that raising hands is the sign — Shulchan Aruch's words are correct.
