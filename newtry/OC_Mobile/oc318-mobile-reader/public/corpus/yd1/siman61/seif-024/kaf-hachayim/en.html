(24) And if a slaughtered animal came to the kohen's hand he acquires its gifts — gifts not separated are as if separated; if another ate them afterward he is liable — it is the kohen's money. Shach s.k. 2. Shaarei De'ah 2. Beiur Avraham in Shaarei Perishah 7 letter 7. Zivchei Tzedek 12. Specifically when slaughtered comes to the kohen; but while alive — not, for obligation has not taken effect — like separating challah from flour that is not challah — robbery in the kohen's hand. Shach there. Beiur Avraham there.
