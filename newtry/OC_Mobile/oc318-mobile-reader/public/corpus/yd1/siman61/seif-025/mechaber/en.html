One who slaughters for a kohen or for a gentile is exempt from the gifts; and one who partners with them must register; but with a gentile, if he sits with him in the slaughterhouses, he need not register: {Rama: In a place where they are accustomed to slaughter with a gentile — if a treifah is found it remains for the gentile, and if a kosher one is found the Israelite takes it — he is exempt from the gifts (Mordechai in name of Ra'avyah).}
