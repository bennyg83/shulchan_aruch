Slaughter for kohen — meaning three weeks, not more — then the kohen himself is obligated and partnership does not help.
And one who partners with them, etc. — but Israelite partners are obligated, for it is written "those who slaughter the sacrifice" plural — see Peleti.
Must register — Rashi explained a known mark that this meat is meat — he is not obligated in gifts, lest they suspect him; and even if part of gifts fell to Israel's share he is exempt — "sheep" even partial sheep does not apply here — one in animal nature, one in meat nature; Rambam explains he marks meat for Israel and gifts for kohen — by marking the matter is public that it belongs to kohen and no suspicion.
Sits in slaughterhouses and calls out — known it relates to gentile; for kohen it does not help per previous seif that if established in slaughterhouse he is obligated — Perishah raised thus and it is correct to escape difficulties.
Where they practice, etc. — the essence: if the animal now before kashrut is clarified belongs to gentile only if found kosher Israel takes — exempt; but if essentially Israel's animal and only if treifah gentile must buy from Israel — obligated in gifts; see Perishah who found a leniency if royal tax to buy every liter for a pruta or two — like partnering with gentile; Perishah was stringent and he is correct.
