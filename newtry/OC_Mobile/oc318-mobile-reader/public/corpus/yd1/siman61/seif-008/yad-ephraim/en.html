And Yeshuot Yaakov wrote that even if there is a full kohen one may give to the kohenet; and if there is a kohen am ha'aretz and she is the wife of a chaver, it is better to give to the chaver's wife, for the chaver's wife is like a chaver; and if there is a kohen chaver they are equal; and the same for kohen am ha'aretz in such a case , see there. And see Mishna Berurah who brought the words of Rambam and Semag that a challalah does not eat; and Kenesset HaGedolah challenged this, for also a challal kohen does not eat, and he brought from the fourth chapter of Derech Mitzvotecha who wrote that it deals with a kohenet who had intercourse forbidden, called challalah because she was profaned from kehunah , this does not apply to a kohen who is not profaned for this; but if born from disqualified kehunah who is a challal, he does not eat; and so in Kiryat Sefer to the Maharit; and see in Maggid Mishneh chapter 9 of Hilchot Bikkurim that challalah , not only does she not take, but even if we took from her, since she had intercourse with a disqualified person her sanctity ceased , see there; and Mishna Berurah brought this.
