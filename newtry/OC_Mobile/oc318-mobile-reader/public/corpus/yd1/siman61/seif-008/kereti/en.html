To a kohenet — but not a challalah — Rambam.
To her husband — and if his wife revealed she does not wish to give to her husband, one should not give — for the essence of giving is that his wife benefit; Perishah; and giving a gift to the kohen's agent — requires further study, for Sifrei writes "to him" to himself — not to his agent; but a husband buys on behalf of his wife — a husband's hand is like his wife's hand, and better than an agent as we find in several matters; therefore one should not learn from his wife; not as Perishah.
