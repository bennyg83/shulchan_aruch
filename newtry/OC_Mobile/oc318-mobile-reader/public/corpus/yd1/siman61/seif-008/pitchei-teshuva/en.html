To a kohenet. See in Maharshal Yesh Sotah chapter HaZeroah seif 6 who wrote that even if there is a full kohen one may give to a kohenet; and if there is a kohen am ha'aretz and she is the wife of a chaver, it is better to give to the chaver's wife; and if there is a kohen chaver they are equal; and the same for kohen am ha'aretz in such a case — end of his words — see there.
