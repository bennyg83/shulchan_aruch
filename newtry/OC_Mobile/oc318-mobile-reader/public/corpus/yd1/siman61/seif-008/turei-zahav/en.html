To a kohenet, etc. For "give to the kohen" includes even a kohenet.
And one who gives them to her Israelite husband, etc. This is the Talmudic language, and poskim derived from it all the more that her husband is exempt; it is astonishing on Tur who wrote "not only that but her husband is exempt on her account" and did not write the main amplification that he receives from others on her account — many force explanations and it does not settle with me; it appears this exemption Tur cited means "he exempts" — and now it is clear that he teaches one can exempt others when receiving from them on her account.
