Doubt whether they are obligated. For we are in doubt because it is written 'from the people' — whether Levites are called 'people' or not; and it appears to me it is proven from the Gemara and poskim that they are exempt only from giving, because perhaps they are not called 'people'; but plainly if a Levi seized gifts from an Israelite, they remove them from his hand, for the verse writes 'and this shall be the judgment of the kohanim,' etc., and Levites are not called kohanim; and Beit Chadash seif 17 ruled they do not remove from his hand; and so Pithei Teshuva was uncertain in this; in my humble opinion it is clear as I wrote:
Need not return them to him, etc. So Tur and Rambam ruled; and likewise in safek bechor Rambam wrote chapter 5 of Hilchot Bekhorot and Tur brings below siman 315 they do not remove from his hand — and one should not descend to Beit Chadash's view who challenged Rambam's source to distinguish; indeed Tur can be challenged against himself, for in siman 315 he ruled they remove from his hand and did not write only 'there are those who disagree' as here; and one should not answer as Beit Chadash for Rambam that there the doubt is for the whole world whereas here the doubt is whether we hold this tanna who calls them 'people' or not — in chapter Beit Kor and chapter HaSho'el regarding a bathhouse rented for a gold dinar per month it is proven one cannot distinguish thus — see there; and so explicitly in Asheri, chapter 1 of Bava Metzia, regarding comparing bathhouse law to tekef kohen — in both, seizure after the doubt arose does not help — see there; and it appears to me to distinguish: specifically in safek bechor where the doubt is in the substance of the matter they argued about, therefore from doubt the kohen has no connection — they tell him bring proof it is bechor and take it, and as long as he brings no proof we establish prior presumption and presumption that most animals are not bechorim; unlike here where the doubt is whether Levites are obligated or not, and in the substance there is no doubt — only from another side the kohen can tell the Levi: as long as you bring no proof you are not called 'people,' the gifts are mine; and this distinction is clear in Mishmeret HaBayit for Rashba daf 66a, except I explained it more; and so appears Rabbenu Yerucham's view to distinguish between here and bechor — for here he wrote only 'there are those who disagree' and in siman 315 he concluded 'and so is the halacha'; and this is Pithei Teshuva's view that here he brought only the Mechaber's view and in siman 315 wrote halacha to remove from hand; in Sefer Lechem daf 241 end 1a he challenged him and in my humble opinion I wrote as above;
