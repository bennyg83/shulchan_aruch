Levites — doubt, etc. Shach plainly raised: doubt whether Levites must give gifts from their animals from "and this shall be the judgment of the kohanim from the people" — whether Levites are included or Torah did not obligate Levites but Levites take portions from Israel the slaughterer — not sensible at all — "kohanim" is written — are Levites called kohanim? R' Yehuda holds this was the corruption of Shmuel's sons who took gifts though they were Levites not kohanim — therefore "they turned aside after gain"; Perishah stood on Tosafot Bava Metzia 6 s.v. etc. who wrote in chapter HaZeroah: ben Levi who snatched gifts is a criminal — implies not obligated to pay; no proof — perhaps when he ate as the Gemara says "one who damages priestly gifts," etc.; from this it appears Tosafot hold a Levi taking gifts has some entitlement and seizure — therefore they challenged tekef kohen we remove — very difficult, no hint of doubt — what does a Levi have seizing Israel's gifts — never sensible, no place for seizure; Perishah wanted to emend Tosafot — achieved nothing; therefore it appears Tosafot challenged why Rav scolded ben Levi: not only does he not give but takes by weight and not honorably — head of the snatching Levi to return full robbery — what does he have with priestly gifts, he is a Levi — what connection — not only does he not give but takes by weight — what connection; Tosafot hold this Levi snatched not from Israel but from other Levites who did not know the law and sent gifts via children — this Levi snatched saying Levites wrongly give gifts, they despaired, he acquires from hefker; therefore Rav said: not only is he lenient with himself by not giving and they do not protest his hands taking by weight what other Levites were stringent to give — Tosafot challenge how can he seize from doubt perhaps Levi is obligated and it belongs to kohen — how seize what he has no right to — worse than tekef kohen — they answered he already ate and could not extract; if existing they would remove — what does he have in another's — no right — they belong to the kohen sent to him — examine in Knesset HaGedolah why Rav scolded — per my view Tosafot intended this — clear.
And some say he must return — Ran's view: we hold tekef kohen we remove; in my work on Choshen Mishpat Sefer Ohrim uTumim on tekef kohen I called Kuntres HaTefisot — I elaborated Ran erred on Rambam from tekef kohen we remove; seemingly astonishing — Rambam ruled tekef kohen we do not remove — there I clarified Ran thinks Rambam said only regarding bechor where no sanctity and a divine portion is mixed — see there. They challenged Tur with Ran's challenge — Tur himself ruled tekef kohen we remove yet here ruled if kohen took from his hand he need not return; Shach answered: these are the words in Mishmeret HaBayit — here no doubt in the gifts themselves only in recipients whether they may acquire — therefore he acquired — essence of his words; forced for law as I clarified in Kuntres HaTefisot. If we sharpen Tur's language: "if the kohen took from his hand" — not "if he snatched"; "from his hand" specifically not "from him" or "from his possession" — given hand to hand — need not return — seizure with permission — given to him — seizure with permission all agree even after doubt arose helps — see Rosh Bekhorot and Terumat HaDeshen — find all — requires further study — clear in Tur's intent.
