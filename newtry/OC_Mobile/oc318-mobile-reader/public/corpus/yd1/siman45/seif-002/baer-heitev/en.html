To declare treifah. And all the more removed; and so Tosafot chapter One Who Hens (daf 48): if punctured the urinary chamber or removed — treifah. Oxen that urinate blood must inspect the membrane where the urine exits whether it was punctured — if not punctured, permitted to eat (Hagahot Lifnei Periyah). And Maharshal wrote they found a needle stuck in cluster of eggs and declared treifah because it was in the body's cavity and perhaps one internal organ was punctured.
