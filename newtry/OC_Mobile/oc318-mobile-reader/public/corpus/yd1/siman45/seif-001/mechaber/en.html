The law if the placenta was removed. In it are 2 seifim. Cattle or bird where the placenta was removed but the fetus rests in it — kosher.
{Rama: And all the more if it was punctured — kosher (one opinion). And some are stringent to forbid if punctured or dissolved (there; Rokeach; and above seif 3; Hagahot Sha'ar HaDin siman 88 in name of Or Zarua; Orach Chaim general rule 55). And one should be stringent if not in a place of great loss.}
