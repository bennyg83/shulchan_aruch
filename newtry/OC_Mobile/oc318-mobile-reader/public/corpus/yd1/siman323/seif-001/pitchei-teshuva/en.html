And there are those who disagree. See Shach s.k. 3, who brought the words of the Maadanei Melekh, who wrote to prove from the words of the Rosh to permit, and concluded: therefore one who relies on permitting with a majority has not lost. The Shach challenged him extensively. See Responsa Mishkenot Yaakov siman 69, who wrote to explain that the words of the Maadanei Melekh are correct, clear, and justified as practical halachah. Therefore he too, of blessed memory, concluded that one who is lenient has not lost; see there.
