(Siman 323, seif 1) It is considered that he may eat it with a non-kohen. This refers to produce that became obligated as grain. It also applies to a minor kohen under nine years old, because we do not suspect that he experienced a seminal emission. If it is known that he did, he is forbidden. Magen Avraham, siman 457, seif katan 9, writes this in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham. I also saw that Raavan, siman 54, writes so. Eliyah Rabbah there cites it in the name of Raavan. See Chok Yaakov there.
