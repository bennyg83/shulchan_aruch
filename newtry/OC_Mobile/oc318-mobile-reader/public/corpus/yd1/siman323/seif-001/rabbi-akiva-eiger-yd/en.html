(Siman 323, seif 1) It is considered permitted to eat it together with a non-kohen. This refers to one who is a tevul yom because of a seminal emission, and likewise a minor kohen under nine years old, where we do not suspect he experienced an emission. If we know he did, it is forbidden. Magen Avraham, siman 457, seif katan 9, writes this in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham. I saw that Raavan, siman 54, writes similarly. Eliyah Rabbah cites Raavan there. See Chok Yaakov there.
