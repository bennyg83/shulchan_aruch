And the lender shall be responsible etc. And if the lender does not trust the borrower regarding loss of principal or profit — he should write in the iska document that the borrower is not believed regarding loss of principal except on the testimony of established qualified witnesses (as below siman 177 s.k. 17); and regarding profit he shall not be believed except by a stringent oath per the document holder's wish; unlike those who were accustomed to write in the document that regarding profit also he is not believed except by witnesses — for that is complete interest; so wrote the Taz — see there where he elaborated on this and many laws; and so the Bach wrote; and it implies specifically here where it is an iska document; but in an actual loan which is Torah-level interest — it is forbidden to stipulate even on the principal that he shall not be believed except by two qualified witnesses — as below siman 177 s.k. 17.
And if he stipulated with him etc. And Perishah wrote that from the proof of the Maharam from whom this law's source comes, it is proven that even without stipulating at the outset — any small wage suffices; and it is not comparable to iska below beginning of siman 177 where he gives him wages as an idle worker — for there he engages with half his own and half the lender's together; which is not so here, for when he engages there is no loan at all here — rather it is like advance or belated interest; and therefore even without stipulation any small amount suffices — see there.
