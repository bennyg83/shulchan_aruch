In what manner it is permitted to lend money on condition that he will conduct business with it for profit; it contains 1 se’if. A person may lend his fellow a maneh on condition that he will conduct business with it for the lender’s profit until it becomes 2 manehs, and it shall be in the lender’s responsibility until that time. And when they become 2 manehs, the 2 manehs return to the lender, and from then onward all the profit is for the borrower — provided that he gives wages for his labor until it becomes 2 manehs. And if he stipulated with him from the outset, even with anything at all that he stipulated to give him as wages for his labor, it suffices.
{Rama: And the same law applies the other way around: that he lends to him initially for the needs of the borrower, and afterward he will conduct business with it for the needs of the lender, and he gives him [wages] (Beit Yosef).}
