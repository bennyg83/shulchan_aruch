Regarding other prohibitions: one may receive medical treatment in a case of danger even in the manner of their benefit; but when there is no danger, in the manner of their benefit it is forbidden, while not in the manner of their benefit it is permitted — except for kil’ei ha-kerem and basar b’chalav, which are forbidden even not in the manner of their benefit, and are permitted only in a case of danger. {Rama: And see above siman 123: there are those who say that all prohibitions of benefit that are by rabbinic law are permitted for medical treatment even for a sick person who is not in danger (Ran, perek Kol Sha’ah, in the name of “there are those who say,” and Rivash siman 45). And even yayin nesech in our times is permitted for medical treatment, and to make from it a bathhouse, even though it is in the manner of their benefit — provided that he does not eat or drink the prohibition, since there is no danger. And all the more so, it is permitted to sprinkle wine upon a fire, for even for a healthy person it is permitted, for smell is not a matter (Arukh, rule 39). It is permitted to burn a sheretz or other forbidden matter and eat it for medical treatment, even for a sick person who is not in danger, except with wood of idolatry (Arukh, rule 32). And any sick person whom we feed prohibition needs the medical treatment to be known, or by the word of an expert (ibid., rule 59). And we do not permit any prohibited matter to a sick person if he can do the medical treatment with something permitted as with the prohibited matter, even though he needs to wait a bit before he finds the permitted [substance], since there is no danger in the matter (Beit Yosef and a responsum of Rashba, end of siman 134).}
