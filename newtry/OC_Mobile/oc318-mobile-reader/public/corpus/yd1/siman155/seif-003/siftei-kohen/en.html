From other benefit-prohibitions one may be healed etc.
Not in the normal manner of enjoyment etc. For medicinal purposes; but not for medicinal purposes, even not in the normal manner of enjoyment is forbidden — the Rosh chapter Kol Sha'ah; and it is simple; and this is regarding benefit-prohibitions, but eating-prohibitions are permitted — as stated by the poskim.
It is permitted to be healed etc. Provided one does not eat or drink the forbidden matter — there; and so the Rav wrote nearby; and that is specifically in the normal manner of eating; but not in the normal manner of eating — the Mordechai and Agudah at the beginning of the chapter Kol She’hu in the name of Ra’avyah wrote it is permitted; and the Mordechai further wrote there that for this reason it is permitted to eat raw fat; however a healthy person should be careful — end; and see responsum R. Eliyahu ibn Chayim siman 112.
Even illness etc. Even in the normal manner of enjoyment.
Nowadays. Meaning that gentiles nowadays are not idolaters, as above siman 123 and 124 and many other places; and the Rashba who forbids in a responsum (brought by Beit Yosef beginning of siman 123) making a bath from their ordinary wine — he deals with the Gemara's law; and so the Rivash siman 55 wrote that ordinary wine has the same law as wood of idolatry — and that is by the Gemara's law; and it is relevant where the gentiles there are idolaters.
And to make a bath from it for a sick person. But not for a healthy person — for anointing is included in drinking — Or Zarua there.
That it is permitted to sprinkle etc. See above end of siman 108 s.k. 25.
Or other forbidden matter. Even if it is from benefit-prohibitions — for we rule that all that is burned its ashes are permitted, except for wood of idolatry and yayin nesech — Rivash siman 225; and so appears the Rav's view.
The remedy is known, etc. See Orach Chaim siman 328 and 618.
