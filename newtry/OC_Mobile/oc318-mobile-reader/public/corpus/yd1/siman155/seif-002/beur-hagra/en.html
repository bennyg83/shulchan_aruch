A gentile, etc. As written in chapter 2 of Pesachim (25a): with everything one may be healed except asherah wood; and what is written from water — in the Yerushalmi R. Yonah was ill with tzamarurin, etc.; Tosafot brought in Avodah Zarah there s.v. she'ani.
But if, etc. Tosafot in the aforementioned s.v. from Yerushalmi chapter 14 of Shabbat (and see above siman 157 s.k. 26).
P.S.A. The fire there:
(Likkut) And some forbid, etc. So in Yerushalmi chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah. The Ran there and Be'er HaGolah, and so too the Rav in siman 157 seif 1 in the gloss: and every idolatry prohibition and sorcery, etc. (Likkut) And some forbid, etc. In Yerushalmi chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah: R. Yaakov bar Idi in the name of R. Yochanan — with everything one may be healed except idolatry and sorcery; R. Pinchas asked: until when? When he said to him "bring me wood of idolatry" and he brought him; "bring me leaves" unspecified and he brought from idolatry — shall we learn from this? R. Yonah was ill with tzamarurin; they brought him from the male of Chovi and he drank; R. Acha brought him and he did not drink; R. Mana said: if R. Yonah had known from where, he would not have drunk. So too in chapter "Eight sheratzim"; but Tosafot have another version: R. Pinchas asked, and also another version "shall we learn from this"; and Tosafot there s.v. she'ani (end).
