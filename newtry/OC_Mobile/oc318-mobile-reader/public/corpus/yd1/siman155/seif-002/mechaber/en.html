A non-Jew who came to heal a Jew and said to him, “Take from these waters,” or “from such-and-such a tree,” that are of elilim — it is forbidden. But if he said to him, “Take from these waters,” or “from this tree,” and he did not mention to him the name of elilim — even though those waters and that tree are found only of elilim — it is permitted, since he did not attribute the healing to the fact that they are elilim; and some forbid even this, even when he said to him, “Bring leaves,” without specification, and he brought him from the trees of an asheirah.
