Conditioned it. Bach writes that the prohibition applies even when he does not say so explicitly, if it follows from his words. There are two cases. First, if other water or trees are readily available and he says, 'Take from these,' it is forbidden even without specifying those belonging to idolatry. Second, if only these are available and he explicitly says, 'Take from these of idolatry,' it is forbidden. Permission exists only when no alternatives are available and he does not identify them as idolatrous. Shach writes that the Mechaber would agree.
Even. The authorities agree with the first opinion, and Issur VeHeter rules likewise. Shach.
