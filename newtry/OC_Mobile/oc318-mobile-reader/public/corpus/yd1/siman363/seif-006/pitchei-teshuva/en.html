A coffin from which the deceased was removed. Teshuvah MeAhavah, part 1, responsum 164, permits benefit from a coffin found too small for the deceased and replaced, and certainly permits placing another deceased in it. Since it was never suitable for burial with the first deceased, merely placing him in it temporarily and awkwardly for transport did not prohibit it. Har Avel, branch 11, disputes this from the Ran, who indicates that even temporary designation and use can prohibit. The case of a bier is different because it is designated for many deceased persons.
