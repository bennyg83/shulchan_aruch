(Supplement) One may not gather, etc. Torat HaAdam in the name of Eliyahu Rabbah concludes: in a place where the custom is to do so, they do; where the custom is not to do so, they do not. Concerning this it is said, 'Do not move an ancient boundary.' He explains that the custom refers to the Yerushalmi's case after the flesh has decomposed. This is the meaning of seif 4, 'a place,' etc. End of supplement. [Citation references: 50.]
