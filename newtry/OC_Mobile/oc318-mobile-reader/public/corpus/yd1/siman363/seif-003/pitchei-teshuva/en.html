Or it is an incidental grave. Birkei Yosef records a case in which the seven leaders of a city sold a burial plot to a woman through an explicit document identifying its boundaries and markers. Later, a man unknowingly buried a stillborn infant there. It appears that the infant may be removed, because a body placed in an incidental grave may be relocated. Responsa Rabbi Yechiel Bassan, siman 21, ruled in precisely such a case that removal is permitted; see there. The source index also preserves citation 106.
