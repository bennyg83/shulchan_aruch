One does not transport a dead person from a city that has cemeteries to another city except from outside the Land to the Land: {Rama: Or they transport him to the place of ancestral graves (so implied in Or Zarua); and if he commanded to transport him from place to place, or commanded to bury him in his house and not in the cemetery — they heed him (same there); and it is permitted to put lime on him to consume the flesh quickly and transport him where he commanded (Rashba siman 369).}
