We do not move, etc. The reason is that disturbance is difficult for the dead, because they fear the Day of Judgment. A supporting verse is, "I would have slept; then I would be at rest," and in Shmuel it says, "why have you disturbed me to bring me up," etc. And within his own, etc. Meaning, to move him in order to bury him with his ancestors, because this is his honor. The Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe write that for this honor one may move him, but not for another honor. To Eretz Yisrael it is permitted. For this is his atonement, since the soil of Eretz Yisrael atones, as it is written, "and His land will atone for His people."
