The prohibition of moving the dead and bones from their place. In it are 7 seifim. One does not move the dead and bones — not from an honored grave to an honored grave, nor from a disgraceful grave to a disgraceful grave, nor from disgraceful to honored, and a fortiori from honored to disgraceful; but within one's own — even from honored to disgraceful is permitted, for a person anticipates resting beside his ancestors; and likewise to bury him in the Land of Israel is permitted; and if they placed him there on condition to move him, it is permitted in every case; and if it is not guarded in that grave so there is concern lest gentiles remove him or water enter, or it is a found grave — one must move him
{Rama: (Some practice placing Eretz Yisrael earth in the grave) (Or Zarua) (and this custom has support) (Midrash Tanchuma parashat Vayechi)}.
