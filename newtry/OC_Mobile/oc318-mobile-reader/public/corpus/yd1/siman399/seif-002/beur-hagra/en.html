(Likkut) But because he has no public display, etc. The Yerushalmi in chapter 3 says: one who buries his dead three days into the festival counts seven after the festival. During the first three days the public attends to him; during the last four days the public does not attend to him. What is the law of showing him a face? Rabbi Yaakov bar Idi said in the name of Rabbi Chanina: did they not say that there is no mourning on Shabbat? Why then did they say to show him a face? Is it not because of honor? Here too, it is because of honor. This is from the Beit Yosef.
