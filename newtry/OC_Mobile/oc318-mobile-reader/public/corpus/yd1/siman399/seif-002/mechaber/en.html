One who buries his dead during festival on chol haMoed — observes onen laws until buried and after burial observes private matters: {Rama: (nevertheless permitted to be alone with wife (Beit Yosef in name of Raavad and Rosh); see above siman 383 and Orach Chaim siman 548);} festival counts toward thirty completing thirty; even if buried on Sukkot — we do not say Shemini Atzeret cancels; and they occupy themselves comforting during festival: {Rama: All work permitted for him on chol haMoed as matter of loss may do himself; if not matter of loss done through others in their houses and slaves work in private in his house (Beit Yosef in name of Rabbeinu Yerucham);} after festival he begins counting seven; when seven of death complete even if seven mourning days not complete — work through others in their houses and slaves in private; many do not comfort after festival from days comforted during festival; but show him consideration — if buried last three festival days, work through others last three mourning days; in places making two days count seven from last Yom Tov day since from their words it counts and count afterward six days only.
