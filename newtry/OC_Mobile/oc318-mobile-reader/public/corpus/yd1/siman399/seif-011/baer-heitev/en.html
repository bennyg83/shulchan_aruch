Twenty-two. The Taz wrote: it is difficult; Shemini Atzeret should be counted as a festival in its own right also for nullifying thirty, since the seven were nullified before the festival and the festival counts as seven, making fourteen, just as we say regarding Yom Kippur in seif 10. The Rosh answered that here is different, because he did not practice the law of thirty at all. Although he practiced the prohibitions of ironing and haircutting, those are forbidden even without mourning because of the festival. It requires study to answer this according to the Ramban, who holds that in mourning there is an additional prohibition beyond the festival prohibition. If so, why should Shemini Atzeret not nullify the decree of thirty, since he already practiced the law of thirty with the added prohibition? It appears that nevertheless there is a difference from ordinary thirty during the week: there, the essential law of ironing and haircutting is recognizable, whereas here only the added aspect is recognizable, and that is not such strong proof. Therefore it is enough to say that the festival counts toward the thirty, but not that it is like actually having practiced part of the thirty so that Shemini Atzeret would completely nullify the rest of the thirty. End quote.
