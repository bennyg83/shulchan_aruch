The Rosh explains that Shemini Atzeret does not completely cancel the thirty-day restriction even though the mourner already observed the restrictions of laundering and haircutting on Chol HaMoed, because those activities were prohibited independently by the festival and therefore were not acts of mourning. According to this reasoning, a new law could arise according to Rabbeinu Tam, who holds that one who shaved on the eve of a festival may shave on Chol HaMoed. If someone shaved before the festival and then a close relative died one hour before the festival, his haircut prohibition on Chol HaMoed would arise only from the thirty-day mourning restriction. If so, perhaps Shemini Atzeret should completely cancel the thirty days. Noda BiYehudah leaves this unresolved because the Gemara makes no such distinction. See also the preceding responsum there.
