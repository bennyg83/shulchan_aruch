And this is the custom. Baer Heitev cites Terumat HaDeshen. Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chaim 548:12, questions later authorities who cite that view, since Darchei Moshe expressly writes that according to our custom, bathing is forbidden throughout thirty days and has the same law as haircutting. It is permitted only when the eighth day falls on Shabbat, and this is the custom. He rules stringently in practice like the Rama in Darchei Moshe. Teshuat Chen 32 likewise concludes stringently.
