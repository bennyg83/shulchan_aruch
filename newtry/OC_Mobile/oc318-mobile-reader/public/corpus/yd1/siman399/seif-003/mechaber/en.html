If he buried his dead seven days before festival and observed seven-day decree — festival nullifies thirty-day decree; even if seventh fell erev festival — part of day like whole counts both ways; permitted to launder, bathe, and cut hair erev festival: {Rama: Close to dark (Tur); on erev Pesach permitted all after noon from time of slaughter onward; better to cut before noon since others forbidden after noon (responsum R' Yehudah Mintz siman 74);} and if eighth fell Shabbat erev festival — permitted launder, bathe, cut erev Shabbat; if did not cut erev Yom Tov or erev Shabbat — permitted after festival since thirty nullified; but on chol haMoed do not cut since could cut before festival; if seventh fell Shabbat erev festival — forbidden cut erev Shabbat, permitted after festival; and likewise chol haMoed since could not cut before festival.
