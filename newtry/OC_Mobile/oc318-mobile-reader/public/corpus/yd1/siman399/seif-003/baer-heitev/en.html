Pesach. The Siftei Kohen wrote that this ruling the author derived from the responsum of Mahara"i Mintz, and it requires analysis — for on the contrary, from there it is implied that regarding washing and bathing, Erev Pesach is equivalent to other Festival eves, where it is only permitted close to nightfall; but he wrote there that on Erev Pesach one may have a haircut before midday, since others too are forbidden to have a haircut after midday, because it is the time for slaughtering the Pesach offering, as written in Orach Chaim siman 548, see there, and it requires analysis — end of his words:
