And to cut hair. Meaning that although haircutting applies all thirty days, as above at the beginning of siman 390, here the decree of thirty is canceled from him.
Close to nightfall. What is marked here as from the Tur is an error. In the Tur he did not write in the name of the Riva except that if the first day of mourning or the sixth day falls on the eve of a festival, it is permitted specifically close to nightfall. Rather, it appears to me that this is from Hagahot Ashri, brought by the Beit Yosef in Orach Chayim siman 548: even if the eighth or ninth day falls on the eve of a festival, he must wait until close to night, for then it is recognizable that it was permitted because of the festival. So wrote Raavyah.
And on Erev Pesach, etc. The Rav derived this from Mahari Mintz responsum siman 4, but it requires study. On the contrary, it is implied there that Erev Pesach is equal to every eve of Yom Tov regarding laundering and washing, that they were permitted only close to nightfall, for he concludes there: if so, he may cut hair before midday, and it is not comparable to washing, which is possible after nightfall, etc.; see there. It is stated there only that on Erev Pesach one should shave before midday, since others are forbidden to shave after midday, because work is not done on Erev Pesach after midday, since it was the time of slaughtering the Pesach, as explained in Orach Chayim siman 548; see there. I saw in the Beit Hillel that he copied the Rav's words and omitted the statement that on Erev Pesach everything is permitted, and this requires study.
Since others are forbidden to cut his hair. For work is not done on Erev Pesach after midday, and if so he would have to shave himself; therefore it is better before midday. So it is in Maharam Mintz responsa there, unlike the Atteret Zahav, who wrote: since many others practiced a prohibition to shave after midday, he too should practice the prohibition, etc.
