See Baer Heitev of Maharit of blessed memory, who wrote in the name of Shevut Yaakov siman 90 that nevertheless it is forbidden to wear whites and Shabbat clothes and to return to his place, unlike Be'er Heitev who is lenient in this; see there. And see Sefer Beit Lechem Yehudah, who wrote like Be'er Heitev and not like Shevut Yaakov. And see responsum Teshu'at Chen siman 33, who likewise concluded so, but not for his reason; see there. And see Sha'arei Teshuvah in Orach Chaim there, who likewise wrote that one who rules to be lenient is not a mistaken ruling, for he has what to rely on, for also the Bach in his new responsa siman 12 permits this; and perhaps if Shevut Yaakov had seen the Bach's words he would have set aside his view and ruled the halacha like Beit Hillel. Nevertheless regarding wearing Shabbat clothes it appears one should be stringent if they are new and the ironing is still very noticeable; and if he has no others, perhaps one may be lenient in this as well, end of his words; see there.
