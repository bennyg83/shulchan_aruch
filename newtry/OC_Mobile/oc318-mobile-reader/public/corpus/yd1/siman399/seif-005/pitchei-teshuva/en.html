And so is the custom. See Baer Heitev in the name of Terumat HaDeshen, and Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chaim 548, seif katan 12, who questions the later authorities for citing Terumat HaDeshen. Darkei Moshe explicitly writes that according to our custom, which prohibits bathing throughout thirty days, bathing has the same status as haircutting and may be permitted only where the eighth day falls on Shabbat. Eliyah Rabbah concludes that practical halachah should follow Rama's stringent ruling in Darkei Moshe. Responsa Teshuat Chen, siman 32, also discusses the matter at length and concludes stringently. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, Yoreh Deah, beginning of siman 296, and Responsa Panim Meirot II, siman 40, by my ancestor, concerning whether the synagogue attendant should announce that people go to greet the mourner before Barechu.
