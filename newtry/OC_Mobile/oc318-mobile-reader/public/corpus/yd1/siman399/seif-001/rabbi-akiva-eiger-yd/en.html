(Siman 399, seif 2, gloss) He may perform it himself. Although a mourner may not personally perform labor to prevent a loss, as stated above in siman 380, mourning is stricter than Chol HaMoed only because during mourning the work can be done by others, for whom it is entirely permitted. Here, during the festival, the others are also restricted because of the festival, so there is no difference between doing it himself and through others. The clause “if it is not a matter of loss” must refer to the period after the seven days from burial have ended.
