Therefore they do not involve themselves in comforting him, and the festival counts toward the count of thirty. This is where he learned of the death during the festival, even though he did not know before the festival. This is what Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote, that the festival counts toward the thirty, for it is no worse than one who buries his dead on the festival. These are his words. It is not as the Beit Yosef wrote, that it speaks where he learned of the death before the festival. Perhaps he spoke imprecisely, or it is a scribal error and should say "during the festival."
