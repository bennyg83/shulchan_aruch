Private matters. See Dat Esh responsa siman 7, who wrote that this category of private matters means specifically marital relations. Torah study, however, is not forbidden to a mourner except because of joy, as it is written, "the precepts of Hashem are upright, gladdening the heart." On a festival it is not forbidden to a mourner, because festival joy is Torah law and mourning is rabbinic. He therefore permitted a mourner within seven days to read when called to the Torah on Shemini Atzeret. Furthermore, the ancient custom is that all members of the community are called to the Torah on Shemini Atzeret; if this person were in the synagogue and not called to the Torah, it would be public display.
