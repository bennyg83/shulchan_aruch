Private matters. Responsa Dat Esh, siman 7, writes that the festival rule of private mourning refers specifically to marital relations. Torah study is different: its prohibition to a mourner is because Torah gives joy, as the verse says, "the precepts of Hashem are upright, gladdening the heart." On a festival, the Torah-level mitzvah of festival joy overrides this rabbinic mourning restriction. He therefore permitted an aveil within shivah to receive an aliyah on Shemini Atzeret. In addition, the ancient custom is to call all community members to the Torah on Shemini Atzeret, and refusing the mourner an aliyah in public would itself publicize mourning.
