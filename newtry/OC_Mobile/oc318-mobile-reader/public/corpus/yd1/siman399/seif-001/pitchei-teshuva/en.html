Private matters. See Responsa Dat Esh, siman 7, who writes that the private mourning practice retained on a festival is specifically marital relations. Torah study, by contrast, is forbidden to a mourner because of joy, as the verse says, "The precepts of Hashem are upright, gladdening the heart." On a festival Torah study is not prohibited to the mourner, because festival joy is a Torah obligation while mourning is rabbinic. He therefore permitted a mourner within seven days to be called to the Torah on Shemini Atzeret. In addition, ancient custom is that all members of the community are called to the Torah on Shemini Atzeret, and if he were present in the synagogue and did not receive an aliyah, his mourning would become publicly apparent.
