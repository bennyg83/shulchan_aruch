See the Be'er Heitev of Rabbi Maharit z"l, who wrote in the name of Shevut Yaakov siman 90 that nevertheless he is forbidden to wear white garments, Shabbat clothing, and return to his place, unlike Beit Hillel, who is lenient in this; see there. See Beit Lechem Yehudah, who wrote like the Beit Hillel and not like Shevut Yaakov. See Teshuat Chen responsa siman 33, who also concluded this way, though not for his reason; see there. See Sha'arei Teshuvah in Orach Chayim there, who also wrote that one who rules leniently has not erred in ruling, for he has upon whom to rely, since the Bach too, in the new responsa siman 12, permits this. It is possible that had Shevut Yaakov seen the Bach's words, he would have retracted and ruled like Beit Hillel. Nevertheless, regarding wearing Shabbat clothes, it appears proper to be stringent if they are new and their pressing is still very noticeable. If he has no others, perhaps one may be lenient even in this; these are his words, see there.
