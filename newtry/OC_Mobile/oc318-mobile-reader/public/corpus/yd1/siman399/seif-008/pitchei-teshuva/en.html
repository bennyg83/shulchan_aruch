Before the festival. Noda BiYehudah, Orach Chaim 14, writes that according to the Rosh, Shemini Atzeret does not completely cancel the thirty-day restrictions because laundering and haircutting during Chol HaMoed are forbidden independently of mourning. According to Rabbeinu Tam, however, one who shaved before the festival may shave during Chol HaMoed. It would follow that if one shaved before the festival and then suffered a bereavement one hour before it, his haircutting prohibition during Chol HaMoed stems only from mourning, and Shemini Atzeret should cancel the thirty days entirely. He leaves this requiring study because the Gemara made no such distinction. See the preceding responsum there.
