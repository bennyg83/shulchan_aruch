The Taz wrote: I find it appropriate to mention here what I have seen in several places — when they bury a deceased on the first day of Yom Tov through non-Jews, Israelites come and return the earth into the grave over the deceased until the final layer (zivula batraiyta), and this is an absolute prohibition in my view, for it is a full labor and involves the prohibition of building, and it is obviously forbidden by Israelites. Therefore one must be careful to have this done by non-Jews for any trouble that should not come — end of his words:
