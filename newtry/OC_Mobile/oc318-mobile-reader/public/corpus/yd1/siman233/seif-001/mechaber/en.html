The age for vows for a male and for a female. It contains one seif: A minor boy of twelve years and one day, and a minor girl of eleven years and one day—if they know in whose name they vowed and swore, their vow is a vow and their oath is an oath, even though they have not grown two pubic hairs. And we examine the thirteenth year for a male and the twelfth year for a female; and before there are twelve for a male and eleven for a female, even if they know in whose name they vow, it is not a vow.
{Rama: And we rebuke them and strike them so that they do not accustom their tongues to vows and oaths; and if it is a small, light matter in which there is no affliction of the soul, we tell them to stand by their vow and oath so that they do not accustom themselves to this. (Rambam chapter 12 of Hilchot Shevuot, Semag, and Kol Bo)} And from thirteen years and one day onward for a male, and from twelve years and one day for a female—even though they do not know in whose name they vow, their vow is a vow.
