(Likut) Even though, etc. This follows from what is stated there: "No, Rav Hamnuna derived it from the matter itself," etc. For if that were so, why would it be necessary to infer it indirectly, and to explain that the reason concerning the later age, etc.? It should have been inferred from the matter itself, since the first clause likewise concerns one who produced the signs, and nevertheless its law is not the same as that of the later age. However, the Rambam's ruling that even after thirteen years this applies although he did not produce the signs is perplexing, for the Gemara states explicitly there that it applies specifically when he produced them.
