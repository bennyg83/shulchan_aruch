And see in Sefer Petach HaBayit siman 11 who wrote: requires further study — if a thorn stuck before us and immediately came out, such as when a thorn entered from a tree within the wall of the animal with sudden force and immediately the animal itself distanced it and the thorn remained on the tree and similar — to what shall we compare it: if we say since we know it was opposite the heart and now after slaughter a defect is found in the heart in a manner that did not enter the cavity, and when we see this defect is from the thorn — then we are no longer concerned perhaps it perforated one of the other organs; and even though Bach and Taz and Shach required that the thorn be entirely stuck — this is to exclude that the thorn be stuck only in the heart alone, or in the wall alone, or inside the cavity; but if the thorn already came out — kosher; or perhaps no distinction, and requires further study in this — end, see there further.
