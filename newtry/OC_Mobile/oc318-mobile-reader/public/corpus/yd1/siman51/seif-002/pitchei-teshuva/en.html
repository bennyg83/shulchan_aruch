We are concerned perhaps it perforated. See responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 109 who declared kosher a thorn found in the body cavity, and meat from it was salted with much other meat; and there was great loss and need of a mitzvah meal; and he declared kosher the rest of the meat already cooked from the reason of twofold safek that perhaps one of the inner organs was not perforated; and if you say it perforated — perhaps we hold like Ra'avyah that salting forbids only to the depth of a peel, and he declared kosher even without peeling since it was already cooked and impossible to peel; see there. And see responsum Noda BiYehudah part 2 Yoreh De'ah siman 20 in the middle of the responsum — he greatly disagreed, and wrote that this safek that perhaps we hold like Ra'avyah is not considered a safek at all since many poskim disagree with him. And also what Shevut Yaakov wrote that kosher even without peeling since already cooked — it is not so; see there at length (and see responsum Chatam Sofer siman 93 who elaborated to uphold Shevut Yaakov's words above, because Ra'avyah's view is not a singular view but the view of most rishonim and their view is primary in the Talmud — therefore it is simple we may rely on them in great loss and time of pressure with combination of another safek or other leniencies, as the incident of Shevut Yaakov without hesitation; see there.
