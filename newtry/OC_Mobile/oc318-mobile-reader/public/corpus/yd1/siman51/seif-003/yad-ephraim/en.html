Such as a piece of chiltit. See Be'er Heitev what Shach wrote in name of Maharit; and in responsa Avkat Rokhel our Rabbi Beit Yosef answered Maharit and wrote that it was testified before him that this grass is also called chiltit — it resembles mafis that eats only the leaves, for its way is to eat all the grass until the root; and it is found eating the root in which are the leaves and in it is the power of chiltit and sharpness; and also even another grass causing that action — treifah, for it did not specify specifically a piece of chiltit but as an example. Therefore Rambam wrote "and the like"; and also even safek perforated that Rambam wrote "examine" is according to Behag; but Rashi holds there is no inspection for intestinal children, and so Rosh and Ra'avad; and even for Behag — only incidentally; but when found every day four or five with lesions, and sometimes most slaughtered or all — who will rely on inspecting them; and also nowadays we are not expert; and also they have lesions and tend to liquefy — this is like nitmasmes, and flesh that the doctor scrapes, as Mordechai wrote in name of Rabbeinu Tam that we are not expert; therefore this lesion from intestinal children is like perforated, and afterward one of the slaughterers testified that when they feel this lesion it liquefies and the intestine is found perforated; and it was explained from what I wrote that everything found with such a lesion in the intestines in these animals — whether from eating grass or another cause — is a perforation and treifah. Afterward when butchers saw that some permit — my words were not accepted and they fed from those animals. And the hand of God was on the people and several of those who ate became ill from that illness itself that the animals had, for they had bloody diarrhea, and ten souls died, God have mercy; the survivors reached death's door; and when the sage saw thus he returned and confessed to my words, and we agreed to forbid any animal in whose intestinal children any lesion is found, however small; and so this custom spread several years; and I wrote this as a memorial for generations — said Yosef Karo.
And again he brought that Radbaz also wrote there is prohibition and danger, and whoever feeds Israelites from this animal is a partner of Amalek and agent of the Satan according to experience; and so signed on this Mahar R' Yisrael in Mohal Meir, see there. And see in words of A.A.Z. in Talmud Torah — it appears he intended in holy spirit to what our Rabbi Beit Yosef wrote, see there. And behold in Maharit's responsum part 1 siman 256 it is explained that he saw Beit Yosef's answer on this who wrote to forbid because it eats the root which is the artery etc., and he disagrees: even if it eats the root — kosher, for it is a piece that perforated; and he also wrote that mostly red spots are found in intestinal children but there is no lesion and liquefaction — rather healthy like other intestinal children, and not like flesh the doctor scrapes. And regarding what Beit Yosef was angry that he permitted where he forbade — he answered that this was said about that animal itself; but that he extended the prohibition to all animals that one sees lesions in their intestines from eating that grass — it appears to me this is not in the category of "a sage forbade and erred"; see there. And in truth "a sage forbade" requires examination in responsum Panim Me'irot part 1 siman 6 — God willing it will be explained in siman 243 at length. Behold Maharit part 2 s.k. 194 wrote that although he proved this grass is not chiltit — nevertheless the sage Mahari Karo practiced and taught inspectors to declare treifah animals in which a red mark is found from eating that grass; and because of peace I was silent all these years, for the stringency was in Ishmaelite animals; and now this year the ramson occurred in month Shevat which is the season of the grass, and they do not let Jews slaughter for Ishmaelites, and Jews buy animals to slaughter — loss of Israel's money; I returned and added to take on all I wrote, for in the Gemara treifot were mentioned only regarding a piece of chiltit that drips from the tree called chiltit like komam etc. that perforated the intestinal children — meaning a piece, not the root which is the artery, as the sage wrote; and in these red spots and stains — this is not a perforation nor destined to perforate, for it appears to the eye that the place of the stains is strong and healthy like other intestinal children, and the place of the stain is not liquefied nor wormy that they should be close to perforation etc.; and even in chiltit itself Rambam wrote: if within three days — see there. And I am uncertain whether what Beit Yosef wrote that it is life-danger and Maharit confessed was after the second ruling or the first, and he was silent because of peace and again afterward added a second time his hand to permit and did not heed this matter that Beit Yosef wrote that several souls were endangered — and this is astonishing, for certainly he should have been concerned for the stringency that danger is more severe than prohibition; and there is somewhat to my mind on the essence: whether there is concern for nitmasmes or not; and wherever there is safek that it will be flesh the doctor scrapes — forbidden, for Maharit does not permit except when in the place of the mark its place is healthy and strong like other intestinal children. And in A.A.Z.'s words in Talmud Torah it is explained: if only from one side — permit in place of great loss, since it is not wormy; and it appears one may scrape the mark, and if skin remains that is whole and beautiful — permit in great loss. And see in Machazik Berachah what A.A.Z. brought in Talmud Torah siman 40 seif 5 who wrote: who will tell us chiltit does not puncture immediately, for it came to its interior and is not recognizable through inspection; and he wrote on this that Rashba in Torat HaKodesh escaped him — the reason that everything standing to perforate is considered as perforated implies it does not perforate immediately; and I wonder — Rashba comes to say that even if you say it does not puncture immediately, nevertheless the venom in it weakens and throws treifah into all, and it will ultimately perforate; and so in Talmud Torah there: chiltit by its nature weakens intestinal children and they are destined to perforate — behold like reddened flesh opposite intestinal children that is treifah even if slaughtered immediately, as Rashba wrote in responsum siman 346; and so is implied from what Beit Yosef forbade animals eating grass etc.; and A.A.Z. took the first reason according to Rashi's language implication that treifah is because the piece actually makes a perforation — therefore he said it punctures immediately; and again he wrote that even if you say it does not puncture immediately, nevertheless since illness began to rule etc. — this is according to Rashba's view; and nevertheless he challenged Peri Chadash that even with a needle that does not actually cause illness — nevertheless we say everything standing to perforate etc.; and this is his view, and his proof is clear — all these indications in the baraita "thorns ate them" etc.; I shortened. And see Peri Chadash that in eating a piece, even if slaughtered immediately — treifah; and so agreed in Beit Lechem Yehudah and Maharach there.
