In a bird, many acharonim forbid, for they have no force through rubbing against a wall — the needle entered with its cavity; and one may deduce: if so Levi should have taught more in a bird — needle found stuck in flesh in kupiah l'gav — treifah, for certainly one of the inner organs was perforated. And if it is not taught — since treifot of perforation of inner organs are taught, what difference — does it come to count? Levi comes to inform us that in a bird there are more treifot than in an animal, and this too is included; and there is to reconcile; and requires further study. However, everything according to appearance, as Kereti wrote; if so, impossible to teach more in a bird.
