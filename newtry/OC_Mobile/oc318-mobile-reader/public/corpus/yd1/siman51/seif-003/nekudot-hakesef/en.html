(Taz s.k. 4) I do not know how they filled their heart to disagree with Bach to be lenient without proof; and also the language "because the way of an animal is to rub against walls" implies thus — otherwise it should have said "because it is possible it rubbed against walls." And also what he wrote in name of Maharshal — behold he did not examine only his words in Piskei Avodah Zarah siman 76; but in truth he was brief there in the matter, and wrote as we wrote above siman 47 in name of Rosh; and in siman 47 he mentioned animal — see there.
