The law of a thorn or needle found in an animal. In it are 4 seifim. A thorn or needle that perforated into the body cavity of an animal or fowl — we are concerned perhaps it perforated one of the places whose perforation disqualifies in any amount, and it is treifah. Therefore, all the organs regarding which we said that if perforated from one side it is kosher — such as the heart, the thickness of beit hakosot, and the kurkavan — if the perforation is from the outside only when done through illness; but if through a needle or thorn — treifah. {Rama: And if it is a safek whether done through illness or through a thorn and needle — kosher (Beit Yosef in name of Ohel Mo'ed). And see above siman 49; all the more so there is to declare kosher in such a case when skin and flesh were perforated until the cavity.} However, if it perforated with a thorn before us and the animal was slaughtered and the thorn is found stuck before us from the wall of the animal into one of the organs — the matter appears that it did not perforate in another place; therefore if it struck the heart and the like and did not perforate into its cavity — kosher.
