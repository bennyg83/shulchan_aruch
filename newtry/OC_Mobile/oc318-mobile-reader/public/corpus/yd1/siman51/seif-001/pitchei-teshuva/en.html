That perforated into the cavity. See responsum Chinuch Beit Yehudah siman 35: an animal in which no defect was visible from outside, and after the skin was flayed a perforation was found in the flesh between rib and rib opposite the lung; and they judged it that through goring of another animal or a tree it was struck with force until skin and flesh were pushed inward until the flesh tore; and nevertheless no mark was made on the skin and it did not perforate — and they declared kosher since it was an Israelite animal; see there.
