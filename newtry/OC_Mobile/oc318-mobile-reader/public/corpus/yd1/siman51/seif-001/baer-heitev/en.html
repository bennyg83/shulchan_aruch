Treifah. Taz wrote in siman 51 that we do not say it is a twofold safek that perhaps it did not reach the inner organs, and if you say it reached — perhaps it did not perforate; for here it is worse, since in any case it made a perforation and we must be concerned perhaps one of the inner organs was perforated.
In perforation. Maharshal wrote: nevertheless inspection is required opposite the perforation. And in siman 49 seif 4 Taz disagrees with this hagahah and ruled: if it is a safek whether perforated through thorn or illness — we hang to be stringent; see there.
Kosher. As Darkei Moshe: even if we did not see at first where it perforated, but all that we saw is that the needle entered from outside opposite the heart and is now stuck in the heart — we hang that it perforated only opposite it and not elsewhere, and it is kosher. And Shach wrote that so is the essence, and it is explained in Mordechai that there is no distinction between kupah l'bar and kupah l'gav, nor between ketinita and elimta.
