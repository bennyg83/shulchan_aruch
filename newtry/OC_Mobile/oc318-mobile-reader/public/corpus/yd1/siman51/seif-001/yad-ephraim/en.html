Through needle or thorn — treifah. Be'er Heitev. And he wrote in Shevut Yaakov siman 25: in a goose in which two round pieces of lead called (schroit) were found inside opposite the breastbone in the body cavity — to declare treifah, lest while marking with a destructive vessel it struck the goose with force as it perforated the bone — one must be concerned also for inner organs; but if the scratches were large like beans and there is great loss — one may be lenient through inspection of all organs puncturable in any amount, and we are not concerned they healed; see there. And see in Noda BiYehudah part 1 siman 25 that I brought: he wrote that such inspection is difficult and one should be stringent even in great loss. And through siman 48 — what responsum Pri Yitzchak siman 12 wrote regarding a round perforation found between the ribs, and they said it was from horn-goring and declared kosher; and see responsum Beit Yaakov siman 13 who brought in Likkutei HaKabbala that inspection of inner organs is required; see there. And in responsum Tzitzat Tzvi siman 9 who wrote to permit from twofold safek — perhaps through illness and perhaps inner organs were not perforated; and see responsum Pri Tevuah siman 10: it is known that an ox gored the animal with its horn opposite the intestinal children, and a protrusion of skin appears swollen and prominent like a large apple; and his view is to be stringent since through the goring this large protrusion came out; and if there is no blow in the flesh — from where this swelling; therefore he wrote to be stringent that inspection of inner organs is required, and we are concerned for those who forbid lest it healed; see there. And his words are not clear — is it that through force of goring the flesh aches there and causes swelling in that place, and because of this we establish a defect in inner organs; and this is different from a perforation where we see the goring caused the perforation — behold this is like a needle found in the body cavity; not so here where there is no defect that through goring a perforation was made, for the skin is whole; all the more when visible from inside that in this place like wings of inner organs — no mark of a blow on the ribs — plainly there is no concern at all.
