(1) [Seif 1] Thorn or needle that perforated into the body cavity of animal or fowl, etc. And needless to say if a needle or thorn is found in the body cavity and no defect is recognizable in skin and flesh — treifah, for presumably it came through the esophagus; but even if known that it came from outside through skin and flesh — treifah. Turei Zahav ot 1; Mechaber ot 1; Eliyah Rabbah siman 33 ot 1; Beit Yitzchak in Amud HaZahav ot 1; Zivchei Tzedek ot 1.
