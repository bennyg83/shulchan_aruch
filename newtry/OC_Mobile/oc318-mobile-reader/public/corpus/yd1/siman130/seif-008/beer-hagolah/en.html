A baraita there in Avodah Zarah 31a. Rashba writes in the name of Ri HaZaken that this does not mean one must seek out the seal and examine it closely. Rather, it means that if, upon seeing his seal again, he recognizes it, the item is valid. If he does not recognize it, the item is forbidden, for this clearly demonstrates that the seal was altered and forged, since he no longer recognizes it. Tur wrote likewise.
This is the name of the Lord, and he is called the Lord
