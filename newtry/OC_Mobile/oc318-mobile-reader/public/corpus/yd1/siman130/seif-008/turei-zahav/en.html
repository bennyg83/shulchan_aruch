And likewise if the mouth of the skin is narrow, etc. In the Tur it is written: tie and seal the mouth of the wine skin and put it in a sack with the skin's mouth downward and tie the sack above — two seals; the skin's mouth upward is not two seals; and in the Gemara it is also: skin in a strap — tie and seal its mouth downward, etc.; it appears straightforward this "and seal" does not mean another sealing as I explained regarding "a barrel on its mouth smeared and sealed" — for here the sealing was conditional on the tying itself: granted regarding plastering there is no distinction since once it dried there is no bother to make another and wait until it dries — unlike regarding tying: whatever is not a strange matter is not a sign at all, for an ordinary tie certainly has no bother at all to untie; rather we certainly require a strange tie — as written nearby "two strange ties, etc."; therefore they said in the Gemara: if he tied and sealed — meaning he made a recognition in the matter of the tying — automatically it was one seal; and its mouth downward was seal two; or what he folded the skin's mouth inward — with this are also the Tur's words we mentioned explained: the tie of the skin was seal one because it is strange — he made it in the manner of sealing; if the skin's mouth was downward and the sack tie above — meaning an ordinary tie that is not strange — two seals; and as we mentioned, so it appears to me to explain the Tur's words also here; settled what Beit Yosef inferred on the Tur here; and according to this putting in the sack was no seal — only the tie and inverting the skin's mouth or folding its mouth inward was two seals; and it appears the reason is: whenever the skin is in a sack and its mouth upward, immediately when he unties the ordinary sack tie the skin's mouth is exposed as if without sack — therefore when the Tur wrote "but he folded the skin's mouth inward" — even though its mouth is upward in the sack it is two seals — likewise if it is without sack at all; and as Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch wrote here from Rambam's language who did not mention putting in a sack at all and did not write "even though its mouth is upward" but since at the beginning he said if it is in a sack upward it is not a seal — here he said it is permitted here even though there is no seal because of putting in the sack; and Maharach ruled because of the Tur's language that putting in a sack is required at least; and it appears to me since from Rambam it is clear we do not require a sack and Shulchan Aruch brings it — we have no reason to dispute his ruling, since in the Tur it is not proven a sack is required; and that Shulchan Aruch seif 1 wrote "put the skin in a sack, etc." — there it deals with wood as explained there, and one should concern lest he draw wine through its boards; and nevertheless Rama concludes there one is accustomed to be lenient; but if the skin is of leather and its stitches are not visible from outside — it appears clear we do not require putting in a sack, only two seals as I explained; and the tie was also a seal — namely it is somewhat strange from other ties as we proved above.
