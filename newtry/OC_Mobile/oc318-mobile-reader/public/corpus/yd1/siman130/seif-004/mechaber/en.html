What is a seal within a seal? If one closed the barrel with a vessel that is not tight, as is the way everyone closes, and he smeared it with clay — this is 1 seal. If the vessel was tight, and he smeared upon it from above — this is a seal within a seal. And so too, if the mouth of the wineskin was narrowed, this is 1 seal; if he turned the mouth of the wineskin inward and narrowed it upon it, this is a seal within a seal. And likewise, any alteration that is an alteration from matters that are not the way of everyone is like 1 seal, and the smearing or tying is a second seal.
