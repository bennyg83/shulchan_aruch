And likewise any unusual change, etc. And the responsum of Masat Binyamin siman 29 wrote: the tying of the wagon covers — even though there is no more unusual knot than that which is tied at the head of the wagon, passed back and forth many times along the entire length of the wagon and at the end of the wagon a second time — nevertheless it does not constitute a seal, for that which we rely on an unusual knot or seal is because he would not trouble himself to forge, or he fears; not so here, for he is compelled to untie the knots and open the wagons every day to take his food from within the wagons — end; see there.
