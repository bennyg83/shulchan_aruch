In the hand of Israel — the key and seal. Avodat HaTalmid and Teshuvot HaTashbetz part 3 siman 89: a gentile purifies his wine through Israel and leaves it with a gentile in his courtyard; and at night the guard closes with key and seal — in the keys in those lands that can easily be forged, two seals are required — see there. And in responsum Mahari Katzabi siman 7: an apostate who made his wine in purity and closed it in a storehouse through his Jewish wife on condition he not enter there — only his wife; and the apostate lives with his wife in Israel's courtyard renting a house atop the storehouse; and the key is in the landlord's hand only when he needs to sell wine the wife takes the key and closes and returns the key; and he wrote: even according to the stringent who hold regarding went out and sailed that key and seal are required — nevertheless since he stipulated he not enter and his wife guards it and also the Jew lives there and the key is in the Jew's hand and he troubled to tread in purity and to spend expenses; and also he is apostate to Ishmaelites and he himself is a decree; and even one who is stringent regarding a suspect is more than a gentile — that is regarding depositing; but lest he touched — in such a case, since it is in Israel's possession and locked — even though generally a key is not considered like a seal, here one can say it is permitted and pure — see there.
