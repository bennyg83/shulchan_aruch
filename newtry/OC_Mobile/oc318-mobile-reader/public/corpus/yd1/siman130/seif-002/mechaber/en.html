If one deposited it in the hand of a non-Jew with 1 seal, it is forbidden for drinking and permitted in benefit, provided that he designated for him a corner. {Rama: And there are those who wrote that after the fact one may permit even with 1 seal (Beit Yosef according to Rabbeinu Tam, and in Arukh, rule 22). And he need not recognize his seal unless he sees that his seal is damaged, for then it is forbidden (ibid.); but there is no need to check for this, and so is the practice to be lenient; and see above siman 118.}
