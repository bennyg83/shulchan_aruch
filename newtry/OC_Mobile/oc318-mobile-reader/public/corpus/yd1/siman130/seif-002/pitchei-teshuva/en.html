See in responsum Noda BiYehudah tinyana Yoreh Deah siman 72 regarding an incident where a Jew sent with one gentile with wine on the road and the Jew was not with him; and when he came with the wine the gentile said the seal was damaged by force when the wagon fell — and he ruled the wine forbidden; for even though Rama wrote that if one can hang that it was hidden, etc., it is permitted b'di'eved — that is where one can hang that the gentile did not notice and did not know at all the seal was hidden; but in our case the gentile knew it was hidden — even if we believe it was hidden by force, afterwards the wine rests in his possession and it is like deposited with a gentile without a seal. However this is specifically when sending with the gentile from place to place and on the road it was damaged; but if he deposited with the gentile and the Jewish depositor is present here and the gentile can tell him — we say presumably he immediately told him, for he fears and says in his mind: if I wait until the Jew comes he will suspect me that I damaged it intentionally to reveal — therefore he immediately runs and informs; and nevertheless he did not decide to be lenient except b'di'eved and in a manner that scholars agree with him — see there; and see in responsum Shivat Tzion siman 31 (and in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 254).
