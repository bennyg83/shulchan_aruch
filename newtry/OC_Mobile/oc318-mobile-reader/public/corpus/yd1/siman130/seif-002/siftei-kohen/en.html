Even with one seal. Specifically a seal; but sealed with a stopper is not b'di'eved like one seal except in a case of great loss, as above siman 129 end of seif 1 in Hagah; and likewise one key is not like one seal, as written in siman 118 s.k. 21.
Damaged, etc. And specifically when it appears it was tampered with intentionally by a person, as below seif 8 in Hagah; and such appears in Or Zarua general law 22 din 11 explicitly; and even a single damaged seal is permitted if it does not appear that a person damaged it — see there.
