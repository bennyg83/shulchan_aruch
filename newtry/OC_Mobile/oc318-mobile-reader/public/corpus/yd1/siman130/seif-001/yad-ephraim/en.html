See Noda BiYehudah Mahadura Tinyana siman 72: wine a Jew deposited with a gentile sealed with two seals as required by law; but then it was fermentation time and because of the strength of the boiling that fermented greatly the force of boiling pushed the body outward and the seal was damaged; and the gentile went and informed the Jew to come and repair his wine — one may be lenient b'di'eved, since he deposited in his house and it was in his hand to inform, and presumably he immediately came to inform and does not delay to remove himself from suspicion — see there regarding an incident where the Jew was with wine on the road and the Jew was not with him; and when he came with the wine the Jew said the seal was damaged on the road when the wagon fell — one must forbid, since even though he sees it was damaged he has no way to inform him and afterwards it remained open in his hand — therefore forbidden — see there. And see in responsum Pnei Moshe part 2 siman 76: just as this question the questioner asked — the seal was damaged because of fermentation — was asked before him and he ruled to forbid, since Israel relied on two seals and the wine in a gentile's cellar — he does not tremble at all; and even though in Beit Yosef siman 58 he wrote that even where there is concern he drank through the tube one may permit where there are other sides for permit — but in our case there is no side of permit, etc. — see there; and it appears from his words that even b'di'eved his intent is to be stringent.
