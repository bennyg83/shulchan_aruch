And we are not concerned lest he draw wine from between the boards. For we do not hold like the first Tanna of Rashbag in chapter HaSocher who holds we are concerned for an orphan — Ran explains he will make a new hole; rather like Rashbag who is concerned only that he open the body, etc.; and it implies from here that if it was clear he drew wine from between the boards it is forbidden; and even though in siman 124 seif 24 Rama wrote regarding a gentile who drew wine by piercing a barrel with a knife — what is in the barrel is permitted — it is different there, for the knife is still stuck there; but if they already sealed it — certainly forbidden even for us, for at time of sealing he touches there where the wine comes out; and likewise from what Rama wrote nearby "and if he found a knife in a barrel, etc." — it implies specifically while it is still there; and likewise here in Darkei Moshe he wrote: if he found a thorn stuck there one may be lenient, etc.
