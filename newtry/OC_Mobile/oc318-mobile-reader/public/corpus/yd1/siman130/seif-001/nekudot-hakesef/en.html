(Siman 130, Taz, seif katan 1) That case is different because the knife is still inserted, etc. This is incorrect, for the Rama's words do not imply that. When the Rama writes here that we do not suspect that he will remove wine from between the boards, he means in a manner that would forbid the wine in the barrel, such as drawing it through a tube, touching it incidentally as discussed at the end of siman 128, or touching it when he returns to seal the hole. The Taz himself writes in seif katan 2 that in all these cases, since he touches the wine, the wine remaining in the barrel is also forbidden. Siman 124, however, concerns a case in which he did not intentionally touch with his hand the wine in the barrel. See what I wrote in Shach, seif katan 3. This is the primary explanation: the Rama states the law for practical halachah, with a difference for us where no loss is involved. His words must similarly be reconciled in several places, as I wrote in Sifsei Kohen in several places. See there.
