(Siman 130, Taz seif katan 1) There it is different because the knife is still inserted, and so forth. This is incorrect. Rama's wording here does not imply that. When Rama says that we are not concerned lest the gentile draw wine from between the boards, he means in a manner that would prohibit the wine in the barrel, such as drawing it through a siphon, touching it inadvertently as explained at the end of siman 128, or touching it when he returns to close the hole. As Taz himself writes in seif katan 2, in all these cases, once he touches the wine, the wine in the barrel is also prohibited. Siman 124, however, concerns a case where he did not intentionally touch the wine in the barrel with his hand. See what I wrote in Shach, seif katan 3. This is the primary explanation of Rama's ruling and has practical relevance for us where there is no significant loss. Rama's words in several places must likewise be understood this way, as I explained in Siftei Kohen.
