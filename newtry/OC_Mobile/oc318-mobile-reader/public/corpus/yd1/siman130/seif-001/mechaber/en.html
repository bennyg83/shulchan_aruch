Laws of sealing wine; it contains 10 se’ifim. It is permitted to deposit and send wine in the hand of a non-Jew if it is sealed with a seal within a seal, or with a key and a seal; and this is specifically regarding an earthenware barrel, but not a wooden barrel, because they can remove wine from between the staves and it will not be noticed; and certainly regarding wineskins, for they can easily remove wine from between the stitches. And they have no remedy except that one should place the entire wooden barrel or the entire wineskin into a sack that has no stitching on the outside, and seal the mouth of the sack.
{Rama: And there are those who permit even a wooden barrel and do not suspect that perhaps he will remove [wine] from between the staves (Mordechai at the beginning of perek Ein Ma’amidin; and Beit Yosef in the name of Semahot Gedolot, Semag, Rashba, and Or Zaru’a; and Arukh), and so is the practice, provided that one is careful that the bung is sealed properly, and likewise that there is no spigot there; rather, he should cut off all the spigots, and place leather upon the bung, and the spigot should be fixed with nails, and he should write letters, half upon the leather and half upon the spigots, so that if the non-Jew lifts the leather he will not know how to return it and align the leather as at first (Mordechai, perek R. Yishmael, and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot). And if one found a knife in the barrel [indicating] that he removed wine from between the staves, see below siman 124.}
