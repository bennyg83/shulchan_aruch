Standing beside. Avodat HaTalmid and Noda BiYehudah Mahadura Tinyana siman 73: one who rented a cellar from a Jew and the Jew also has his wine in that cellar; and Israel sealed his wine with his seal; and once Israel lacked several measures of wine from one barrel; and from then the Jew took the key in his hand, etc.; and once Israel forgot to seal some barrels and came to the cellar and found the Jew standing beside the unsealed barrels; and found the barrels lacking; and asked him how he came without a key; and he showed he has another key he had last year and lost and now found; and he ruled to forbid the open barrels — for they are lacking before you and presumably he took from them; and even if he took through an unbent nursing woman there are sides of permit for what remains in the barrels — nevertheless one should concern for a bent nursing woman in a place where it is common; and also concern he took much and filled with his inferior wine; and even b'di'eved in such a case one does not permit — see there.
