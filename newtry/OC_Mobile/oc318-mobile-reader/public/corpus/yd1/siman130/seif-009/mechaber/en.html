If a Jew rented or bought a house in the courtyard of a non-Jew and filled it with wine: if the Jew lives there, then even though he left the wine in the courtyard and there is neither seal nor key upon it, it is permitted — even if the non-Jew also lives in that courtyard. But if the Jew did not live in that courtyard: if the key and a seal were in the Jew’s hand, it is permitted, and needless to say with 2 seals; but if not, then even though the wine is in that house that he rented or bought, it is forbidden if the non-Jew lives in the courtyard. And if the non-Jew does not live in the courtyard, it is permitted unless he was found standing beside the wine.
