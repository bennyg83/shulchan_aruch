See Baer Heitev of Rabbi Maharit, and Responsa Maharam Galanti, siman 123, concerning one who consecrated money so that its profits would support a yeshivah in a particular place in Eretz Yisrael. In the interim, because of sin, that place was destroyed. The administrator nevertheless exerted himself, restored the place, and reassembled the dispersed members of the yeshivah. The donor then sought to retract and redirect the funds elsewhere. It was ruled that he may not change the designation, because he expressly designated the funds for those maintaining a yeshivah in that place; see there.
