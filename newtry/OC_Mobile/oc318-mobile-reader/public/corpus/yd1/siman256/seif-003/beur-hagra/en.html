Because it is not, etc. This follows the explanation of the Rif, the Rambam, and the other authorities. See Tosafot there, s.v. 'and it is distributed,' etc. In the Yerushalmi to Peah, however, the matter is stated in accordance with Rashi's explanation: the tamchui is administered by three because its distribution is immediate.
