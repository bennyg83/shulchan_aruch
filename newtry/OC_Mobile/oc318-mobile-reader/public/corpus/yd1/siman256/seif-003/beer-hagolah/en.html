This too is from the statement there of Rabbi Elazar in the name of Rabbi Yitzchak, "Any fast day," etc., Sanhedrin 35a. Rashi explains that they were accustomed to distribute charity on the nights of fast days, and the poor relied upon receiving it. In a place where it is not customary to distribute charity in this manner, however, the poor do not rely upon them, and those who delay are not considered as though they shed blood.
