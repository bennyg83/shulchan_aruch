They may not change even for Torah study — in Mahariwe siman 27 he explained the reason: although elsewhere it is permitted to change, nevertheless here where he says they should give his money to the poor for a weekly allowance, the poor of the city already acquired it; and he brings proof from what Rambam wrote chapter 5 of Laws of Guardians: but if he said to these poor or these captives, behold it is acquired by them and it is money that has claimants — end; and see siman 259 seif 6 where I proved that Tur's language in the name of Rosh — that they may change it even for optional matters — is specifically regarding fund and tamchui that is a fixed matter and if they lack they will collect again; but if a case occurred that they needed to collect for the poor, such as for clothing need or poor came and they collected for their name, they may not change for another purpose even for other poor — end. Maharik wrote in root 7: two who agreed to give equally to charity and captives occurred to them and one wants to collect according to wealth and the other claims they already agreed to give equally to charity and redeeming captives is also included in charity — he ruled one must compromise whether it is included in language of charity unspecified; nevertheless in people's language it is not included; and charity is a vow and in vows we follow people's language, for in people's language they call charity the needs of the poor — food, drink, clothing and the like; and from this he derived another law that Rama brought siman 252 seif 1 regarding one who says, etc.
