Community needs — meaning even for optional matters per Rabbenu Tam there, and not like R. Yehuda Migash who wrote specifically for a mitzvah matter. The Rosh brought proof for Rabbenu Tam from chapter 1 of Arakhin that once it was discontinued it is permitted to change even for optional matters — and see siman 259 seif 54 and Yerushalmi end of chapter 2 of Shekalim "they do not protest the hands of the administrators" — but there is no proof from there, for there too it is for the need of another mitzvah; and that of Arakhin too is no proof as R. Yehuda Migash wrote, for there it is different since everyone who vows to the Temple on behalf of the community vows — therefore they themselves can sell even for drinking beer as stated in chapter 4 of Megillah.
And even though he did not, etc. As stated: R. Ashi said "I stipulated", etc. — and all the more so the community itself.
And similarly regarding the gabbai, etc. His words are puzzling, for a gabbai is certainly forbidden to change it as stated in Bava Batra there from Marish who would do, etc., and in Arakhin there "once it came to the hand of the gabbai", etc. — I am not, etc., but rather it refers to when the community gave him authority, and as stated: since he listened to what Shmuel said to him, etc.
(Likkut) And similarly regarding the gabbai, etc. As stated in Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Megillah halakhah 1: R. Chiya bar Abba went outside and they gave him perutot to distribute to orphans and widows; he went out and distributed to the rabbis — must he set aside others in their place? R. Bibi bar Mamal said he must set aside in their place; R. Zeira said he must set aside in their place; R. Ila said he need not set aside in their place; R. Yasa in the name of R. Elazar said he need not set aside in their place, etc. And similarly the Gaon explained what is stated in chapter 1 of Bava Batra "and to change it", etc. — that afterward he must give others in their place for that matter — and Avraham ben Shmuel included it among the 613 mitzvot; nevertheless the conclusion of Yerushalmi is that he need not set aside in their place (end quote).
And similarly regarding an individual, etc. As stated in Tosefta chapter 2 of Megillah: one who pledges charity before they acquired, etc. — only with their consent, and as stated in siman 251 seif 5 — see there.
And likewise if he specified, etc. — from what is written in chapter 8 of Bava Kamma: the poor of Pumbedita finished, etc.; and Rambam wrote "and already acquired by them," etc. — plainly one may not change; there; and in Tosefta that Rashbam brought in Bava Batra 43b, and Rosh there siman 66: "One who says give," etc.
And if he cannot, etc. As stated in Bava Kamma 111a "the employer", e.g. he went out, etc., and the conclusion, etc. — likewise here, since they went out and did not command to retract, it retracts for him that he may do as he wishes. There.
