The daily distribution is collected every day, and the fund from Friday eve to Friday eve. The daily distribution is for poor people of the world, and the fund for poor people of that city alone. The people of the city may make a fund into a daily distribution and a daily distribution into a fund, and change them for whatever communal needs they wish, even if they did not stipulate thus when they collected: {Rama: And specifically when they did not stipulate at the outset; but if they stipulated, they may not change without the agreement of the majority. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba.) And the townspeople may remove treasurers and appoint others in their place. (Maharik shoresh 80.)}
