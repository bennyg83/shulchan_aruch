One who resided, etc. This follows the reading of the Rif and the other authorities. The same reading appears in the Tosefta to Peah there and in the Yerushalmi.
When does this apply, etc. This follows from the Gemara's challenge there concerning a donkey-driver and a camel-driver, etc. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 163, seif 2, in the gloss.
Likewise, the residents, etc. Because they intend to settle there permanently.
