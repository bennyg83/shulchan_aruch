The charity fund and the daily food distribution — how they are collected and divided. And it contains 6 seifim.
Every city that has Israelites in it is obligated to appoint from among them charity collectors — known and trustworthy people who go around to collect from each one what he is fit to give and his fixed assessment; and they divide the money every Friday eve and give to each poor person enough for fourteen meals, one for each day and one for each night.
