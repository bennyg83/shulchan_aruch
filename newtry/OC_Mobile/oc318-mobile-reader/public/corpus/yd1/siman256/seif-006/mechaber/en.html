One who went on a business trip and the people of the city assessed charity on him for going there — he gives to the poor of that city; and if many went and charity was assessed on them, they give, and when they return they bring it with them and sustain the poor of their city with it: {Rama: And since they must give because of suspicion, therefore if they have poor relatives in another city they may give to them there. (Maharik.)}
