To build it. Shach infers from the Tur and Mechaber that even if the building itself is not worshipped, one may not build a wall for it because this benefits the idol housed inside. The Gemara and Rashi imply likewise. Bach permits where only an idol is placed inside the building, but Shach rejects his view.
His own. His share of the original wall counts toward the distance. If the wall was two cubits thick, he measures his one-cubit half and withdraws an additional three cubits into his property. Rashi and Ran. Tosafot writes that even according to the view that fragments are not worshipped, the case may involve idolatry belonging to a Jew, or belonging to a non-Jew but worshipped by a Jew. The Yerushalmi establishes it where he bowed to every stone.
