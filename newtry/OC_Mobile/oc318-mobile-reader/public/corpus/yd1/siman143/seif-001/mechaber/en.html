A law that one should not build his house adjacent to a house of idolatry, and in what manner it is permitted to benefit from idolatry; it contains 6 se’ifim. One whose house was adjacent to a house of elilim, and it fell, is forbidden to rebuild it. How should he act? He draws inward into his own property and builds, and he fills that space with thorns or filth so that he not expand the house of elilim. If the wall was jointly his and of the elilim, it is judged half and half: his half is permitted in benefit, and the half of the elilim is forbidden (and if he cannot distinguish, all is forbidden in benefit) (Tur). Its stones, its wood, and its ashes are all forbidden in benefit. (Nevertheless, it is permitted to sell them his portion wherever it is) (Ran, perek Kol HaTzelamim).
