And the goodwill etc. It implies this is like those who explain it as payment; and so the Magen Avraham wrote; and therefore he ruled strictly — even if others also share in the goodwill with the priests it is forbidden. And the Rambam chapter 7 law 17 — since he explains goodwill as kind words provided one gives no payment — ruled leniently: if it belongs to her and to others permitted, since kind words are only rabbinically forbidden we rule leniently; and so wrote the Ran and Kesef Mishneh there; and so Bach seif 4.
To its worshippers. Meaning the gentiles who worship that idolatry; and so explained Rashi and the Ran; and it is simple — unlike the Magen Avraham who explained it as its attendants in the garden and bathhouse.
And one may rely etc. And the Bach ruled strictly where this profit would not reach the priests if not through this Israelite who gives it; but if he would not buy those fruits or bathe in the bathhouse — gentiles would come and buy fruits and bathe in the bathhouse — it is permitted — see there; and see above end of siman 148 and 149.
