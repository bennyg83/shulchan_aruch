A dome. Taz derives that one may not construct windows for a house of idolatry, meaning the room containing the idol. Hostility does not permit this, because one can excuse himself by saying that Jewish law forbids it, as Tosafot writes concerning ornaments of idolatry.
His wages. Idolatry is created only with the final hammer blow. That final blow is worth less than a perutah, and wages accrue continuously from beginning to end. Therefore he may receive payment for each earlier portion of work. Taz.
The hall. Taz writes that permission applies only when he merely assists others in building. To build it alone is forbidden according to all. As practical conduct, one should distance oneself from ugliness and refrain even from assistance. Beit Yosef cites Ri that one who willingly sells his house for idolatrous use must cast the money into the Dead Sea; if compelled, the proceeds are permitted.
