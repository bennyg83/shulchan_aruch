They combine, etc. Specifically when they belong to one person, but not to two women, as in the next siman.
On a board, etc. This is an unresolved question. The Mechaber wrote simply that they do not combine, since nowadays all challah is rabbinic, and as above in siman 323 we rule leniently.
Therefore when he combines them, etc. This refers to a board, for when the entire loaf or dough is above the walls of the vessel, it resembles a board and does not combine.
That every loaf be, etc. Above the walls of the vessel; if part of it is inside, it combines, for it does not resemble a board. So wrote the Beit Yosef, while the Bach disagrees; see there.
And there is one who says, etc. I do not know why he wrote this in the name of "there is one who says," for no one disagrees in the matter, and the Tur too stated this law plainly. Maharil in the laws of Pesach wrote that if one does not have a vessel that holds all of it, he should place it in a cloth and also cover them with the cloth, and this is called a proper combination.
