In every case. The Shach wrote: it appears that the same applies to two doughs where one has the required measure and one does not. Even touching is not required; it is enough that they are adjacent. He separates from the dough that has the measure the amount of challah, and also separates from it an additional one forty-eighth for the dough that lacks the measure. No basket combination or covering is needed, as above in siman 324 seif 11. End quote.
