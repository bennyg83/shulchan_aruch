Who has not yet reached adolescence. As explained below in siman 190 seif 2.
If she ceased for three onot. Meaning ninety days, even if her veset was every second month or her interval veset was every thirty-five days. An adult woman would require three repetitions of her own pattern, but here three average onot suffice. She is no longer concerned for her former veset, and if she later sees once, she does not immediately resume concern for the prior pattern. Shach and the other later authorities.
Even if she again saw during those same intervals. This means not only if she did not see during those intervals, did not see at all, or saw only once, but even if she returned to the former pattern twice, she still need not be concerned until it becomes established again three times.
Until she again establishes it three times. The phrase implies that she must see in an established pattern, namely three accurately corresponding sightings. Taz infers from Rashba that a young girl or elderly woman is not concerned at all for a non-established veset, even after three irregular sightings. Taz nevertheless writes that the Shulchan Aruch does not agree and that after three sightings she must observe even a non-established veset, while only the former established veset requires full reestablishment. Nekudot HaKesef challenges him.
