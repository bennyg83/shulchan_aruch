A young girl who has not yet reached the time to see, and she is small — that she has not reached the days of youth — even if she brought two hairs; and likewise, even if she reached the days of youth, if they checked her and she did not bring two hairs: she establishes a veset like other women — with 3 seeings for the other vesatos, and with 4 for a veset of intervals. However, there is a difference between her and an adult: even though she was established as one who sees and established for herself a veset, if she stopped for 3 onos beinoniyos, which are 90 days, and she did not see, she is not concerned for the first veset at all, and she returned to her earlier status; and even if she again saw in those onos in which she was accustomed (meaning: practiced) to see, she is not concerned until she again establishes it 3 times, since she is not one who is subject to blood, and it became revealed that the first bloods were a chance occurrence. If she saw 3 seeings from 3 precise onos, not lessened and not increased, it became revealed that the first skipping was not a departure of blood, but rather a change of veset. Therefore, the first seeing from which she began to skip combines with the last 3 seeings, and they are 4 seeings and 3 intervals between them, from 90 to 90. But if she lessened or increased such that the seeings were not precise, then it is impossible for the first ones to combine, and until she sees 4 precise seeings she does not establish a veset of intervals.
