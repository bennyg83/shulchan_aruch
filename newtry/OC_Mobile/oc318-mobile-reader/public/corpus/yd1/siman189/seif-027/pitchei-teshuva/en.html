Or time alone. See Seder Taharah who wrote: what we say for time alone it is not uprooted — for this matter if day arrived three times and she did not yawn and did not see — nevertheless concerned afterward if day arrived and she yawned; but for this matter it is uprooted i.e. need not separate entire day as long as she did not yawn from concern lest she yawn for three Roshei Chodesh passed and did not yawn — see there; and so Chiddushei Dinim — see there.
