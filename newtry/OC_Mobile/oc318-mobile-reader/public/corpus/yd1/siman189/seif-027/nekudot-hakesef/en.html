(Taz s.k. 43) What he explained in Rashba and Rav Gidal — his words are correct; but what he raised for law of girl who saw three times concerned for unfixed veset — not logical at all; for how did Rashba and Tur and Mechaber and Rama write plainly she is not concerned for unfixed veset at all; rather all Rashba's analysis is only to prove law of girl equals other women for impurity — first sight joins; distinction for fixed veset that also for girl first sight joins; but when she has no fixed veset she is not concerned at all lest she see, as appears from plain words of Rashba and Tur and Acharonim in several places; and even though when she saw third time she impurifies me'et le'et — that is regarding if she saw she impurifies retroactively; but whatever she did not see she is not concerned lest she see since she did not fix veset — general rule girl is not concerned for unfixed veset — examine.
