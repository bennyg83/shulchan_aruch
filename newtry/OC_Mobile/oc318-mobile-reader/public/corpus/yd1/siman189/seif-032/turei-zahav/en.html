And the fourth sighting was on the 2nd of the month. Another version reads: on the 20th of the month. He stated the case in this manner rather than as above, namely that she saw three times on Rosh Chodesh and also on the 2nd of the month, to teach a greater novelty. Although the Rosh Chodesh sighting was established first, one might think that she need be concerned only for Rosh Chodesh and not for the 2nd, which may merely be a continuation from an already open source. He therefore teaches that she must be concerned for both.
