And a fourth on the 2nd of the month. So in all books; Derishah and Perishah say it must read with bet; in some Mechaber books it explicitly says "on the second" but that is incorrect; further on the 2nd of the month there are two consecutive days that always follow the beginning of the sighting, as above seif 13 in the Hagahah; the essence as in most Tur books and Bach and Taz on the twentieth; and so in Ba'alei Nefesh 56 end 1a: "I will enumerate for you in which matter a woman establishes veset within veset — e.g. she established veset three times from Rosh Chodesh to Rosh Chodesh and on the fourth saw on the twentieth of the month and Rosh Chodesh — behold this woman established two vesetot on Rosh Chodesh and on the twentieth; but Rosh Chodesh is within eleven days of the twentieth sighting — no matter, for she establishes from the beginning; and though at first she does not establish veset within eleven, if established from the beginning it is established and not uprooted until uprooted from her three times" — end quote; Raavad there further: another matter where she establishes veset within veset — e.g. she saw on the 15th of this month and 16th of this month and 15th of this month and 16th of this month — when she triples in each she establishes two vesetot each month as fit — end quote; explained above s.k. 21.
