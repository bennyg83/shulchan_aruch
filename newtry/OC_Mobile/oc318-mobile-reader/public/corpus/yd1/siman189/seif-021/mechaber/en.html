She yawned on Rosh Chodesh and saw, and again yawned within the days of the month — she is concerned for that yawning, and she is forbidden to have tashmish until she checks; for every veset, whether of days or of body, she is concerned for it with one time. And one must be concerned lest she establish a veset for yawning without a known time. But if she checked and it was found that she did not see, she is no longer concerned for yawning alone; but she is concerned for Rosh Chodesh, lest she establish for Rosh Chodesh.
