And she is forbidden to have relations until she examines herself. This is difficult. In seif 4 of this siman, if she had not established the veses three times, did not examine herself, and did not see, once the onah passed she was permitted. One cannot answer that there the ruling is only after the fact while here it is initially forbidden, for there, regarding an established veses, the beginning of the seif states, “she is forbidden to have relations until she examines herself,” using precisely the same language as here. Nevertheless, there it says that for a non-established veses she is permitted, which implies even initially. It appears that yawning is worse, since it is a woman's natural manner to yawn at the time of her sighting or close to it. There is therefore an actual indication before us that she may be tamei, and this is more stringent than another non-established veses. As for seif 26, which states, “just as an established veses of days requires three uprootings and an examination, so too a bodily veses,” implying that a non-established bodily veses does not require an examination, this is not a valid inference. The examination is mentioned there to teach the greater novelty that even after she has uprooted an established veses, she still requires an examination. The text was not intended to imply anything about a non-established bodily veses.
