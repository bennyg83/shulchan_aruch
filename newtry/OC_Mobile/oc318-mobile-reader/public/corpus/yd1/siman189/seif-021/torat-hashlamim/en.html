She may not engage in marital relations until she examines herself. Bach explains that initially, during the veset, she may not engage in relations and must examine herself so that she may be permitted afterward. Although if the time passed and she did not examine herself she is permitted without an examination, initially she should examine herself during the veset. Perishah offers several explanations and adopts this one. Taz rejects it, but his objections are not compelling. Nekudot HaKesef gives a somewhat different explanation and also challenges Taz. Bach and Perishah's explanation remains sound.
