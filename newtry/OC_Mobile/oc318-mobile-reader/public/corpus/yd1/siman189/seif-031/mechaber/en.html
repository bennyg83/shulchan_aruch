If she again saw in small onos in which she was accustomed to see, she returned to her first establishment: if it is a veset of intervals, she returned to her earlier status if the interval is as she was accustomed at first; and if in the other vesatos, even one time she returns to her earlier status, for it became revealed that the first skipping was not a departure of her blood but rather a chance occurrence. And in this, the law of the old woman is more stringent than the law of the young girl who has not yet reached the time to see.
