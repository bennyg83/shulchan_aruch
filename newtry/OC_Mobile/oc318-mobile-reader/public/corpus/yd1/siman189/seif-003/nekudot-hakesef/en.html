(Taz s.k. 11) This accords with one opinion, etc. This is imprecise, for here, according to all opinions, the first sighting is included in the count, as I wrote in the Shach, s.k. 22. His statement that she must see once more on the first of Elul is also incorrect, for she saw on Rosh Chodesh in an advancing pattern. According to his reasoning, she would therefore have to see once more on the first of Tishrei. Yet this too is incorrect, as I wrote there; see there.
