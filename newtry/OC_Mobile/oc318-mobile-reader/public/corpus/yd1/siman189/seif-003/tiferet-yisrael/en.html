and likewise Tosafot version; and left requires study; and truly great difficulty; per my view one can say Tosafot and Rambam agree to Shulchan Aruch law that if she fixes once skipping veset no further concern — only Rambam deals sight on 17th continued two days and on 18th saw without continuation as usual once and once; and even though we rule not like Ramban only we always follow beginning of sight — nevertheless Gaon already wrote s.k. 17 Tur holds like Ramban regarding skipping veset from majority is rejected — month veset is common, skipping uncommon; if so Rambam holds thus (and possibly for this Tur ruled above like Ramban without dissent since Tosafot and Rambam here hold like him) — certainly if we see she was never accustomed to extend veset two days until that 17th sight and also on 18th saw without continuation — even if we say she fixed skipping veset as we truly rule we follow beginning of sight and 15th permitted uprooted by skipping sight — nevertheless concern lest she see next month also on 18th and then clear she fixes equal month veset and we follow end of 17th sight so she can fix on 18th for common equal month; but if 18th passed and she did not see and saw on 19th — return to law we follow beginning of veset — this is Tosafot and Rambam intent concerned for 18th lest she see; if she sees on 18th certainly fixes month veset and we follow end of sight; but for Gemara law if she skipped without continuation we do not concern for 18th at all — Tosafot and Rambam agree; and no dispute at all on Gemara version and sugya; therefore no need to bring Beit Yosef their view; or Tosafot and Rambam deal if woman accustomed always to see at day beginning — no question whether before or after sunrise — then forbidden only that night and day before permitted; but Shulchan Aruch already wrote s.k. 87 if he had relations in adjacent onah and she saw attributes to her veset; and see Gaon Perishah's view attributes corruption, etc.; and Rambam holds since concern perhaps she saw previous month that day perhaps also this month — but if she certainly fixes skipping veset without doubt Tosafot and Rambam agree to Shulchan Aruch law — requires study; or plainly deals she always skipped to next day only on 17th unclear before or after sunrise and on 18th saw as usual by day — we say presumptively she fixed skipping veset and uproots first to permit 15th and 17th sight was after sunrise and habit comes in time — nevertheless Tosafot and Rambam hold still not free from concern lest she see next month on 18th and fix common month veset for perhaps still no fixed skipping veset since sight was before sunrise — why uproot fixed 15th — say from 18th for this; but if she sees next month on 18th certainly concerned and forbidden both; but for Shulchan Aruch law dealing without doubt she saw for skipping — Rambam and Tosafot agree; and this settles Hagahah "likewise" on Rambam — no need explain this is arranged Gemara;
