Only its hour. This means, for example, that she always saw at the twelfth hour of the day, but the days were not fixed, for once she saw on the twentieth and another time on the twenty-second. Thus she has established only a veset tied to a particular hour, and she need separate only during that hour. This is derived from the case in which she would always see when returning from immersion, and the instruction was, 'Have relations with her on the riverbank.' The meaning is that she would always see in the middle of the night upon entering the city, and he permitted them to have relations before entering the city, because before that time she was permitted. One must distinguish this from the ruling above, siman 187, seif 10, regarding a woman who always saw on the second night after immersion, where she must separate on the second night after her third immersion. There, the specific hour of the night was not fixed, for once she saw at midnight and once before it, and therefore the entire night remained in doubt. See what I wrote above in the notes.
