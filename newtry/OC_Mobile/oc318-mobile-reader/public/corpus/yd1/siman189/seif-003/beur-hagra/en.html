If she established, etc. This follows from what is stated there, 63b: “It was taught: How so? Rabbi Yose says, days and hours constitute a veset,” etc. This implies specifically a combination of days and hours, as stated there, “from the twentieth day to the twentieth day,” etc. It is only in this respect that Rabbi Yehudah disputes Rabbi Yose. This also explains the statement there, 66a, “Go and engage in relations,” etc., because it was before the hour at which she was accustomed to return home, while her immersion was not on a fixed day.
