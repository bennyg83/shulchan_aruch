She is forbidden for the entire onah of the veset. Beit Yosef writes this in the name of Rosh citing Raavad. Yet above, at the end of siman 184, Beit Yosef cites Raavad that a veset caused by an incident, whether or not it has a fixed day, forbids only its actual time. Taz discusses the contradiction at length. The language of Rosh and Raavad in Baalei HaNefesh does not support Taz's answer, and Shach challenges him in Nekudot HaKesef. It appears more likely that Rosh stated this ruling on his own. When Beit Yosef says that Rosh wrote all this in the name of Raavad, he refers to the other laws mentioned there, not necessarily this one. Rama's ruling in siman 184, that a veset dependent on bodily change forbids only its actual time, concerns a sign that does not occur on a known day.
