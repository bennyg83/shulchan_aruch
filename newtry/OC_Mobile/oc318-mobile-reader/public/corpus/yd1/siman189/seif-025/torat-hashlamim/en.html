Forbidden entire veset onah. So Beit Yosef in name of Rosh in name of Raavad; however above siman 184 Beit Yosef brought Raavad's view veset coming by chance whether she has fixed day etc. — likewise forbidden only for its hour; see Taz who extended to resolve; but plain language Rosh and Raavad in Sefer Baalei Nefesh is not so; Shach in Nekudot HaKesef also challenged; though strains greatly to resolve Raavad's words; but truly more appears Rosh wrote his own reasoning in this law forbidden entire onah (and Beit Yosef wrote Rosh likewise in name of Raavad because Rosh concluded at end all this Raavad's precision; but actually does not refer to this law only other laws mentioned there in Rosh (as explained in Sefer Baalei Nefesh) and not Raavad's view above; and Rabbi ruled above siman 184 seif 2 body veset dependent on bodily change — concerned only at veset hour — deals not coming at known time — clear.
