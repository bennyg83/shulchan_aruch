From when her pregnancy is recognized, and a nursing woman throughout 24 months, is not concerned for the first veset; even if she had a fixed veset and it arrived within this time, she does not need checking and she is permitted to her husband. And even [if] they flow and see blood in those onos in which they were accustomed to see, it is only by chance. After the days of pregnancy and nursing passed, they return to be concerned for their first veset. How so? If she had a veset of days: if she was accustomed to Rosh Chodesh, she is concerned for the first Rosh Chodesh that she encounters, and likewise anything similar. And likewise is the law if she had a bodily veset or for a known time. But if her veset was an interval-veset, it is impossible to be concerned until she again sees; if she again saw, even one time she is concerned for the day of interval that she was accustomed to interval.
