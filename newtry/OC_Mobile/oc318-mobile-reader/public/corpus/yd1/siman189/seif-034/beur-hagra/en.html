A pregnant woman, etc. There, 9a, the Gemara asks him, etc., and says, “So too, her blood,” etc. This implies that she requires no examination at all and is not concerned for the veset at all. The statement in Pesachim 72b concerning relations close to the time of her veset does not refer to a pregnant woman. Thus write the Rosh and Torat HaBayit in the name of Tosafot. Alternatively, that passage concerns a woman whose pregnancy was not yet recognizable. Thus writes Torat HaBayit.
From when her pregnancy is recognizable, etc. As explained above in seif 33.
And a nursing woman. This is derived from the law of a pregnant woman, as explained above in seif 28 and in seif 33. This is the meaning of “even if they repeatedly experience a flow,” etc., as stated in the baraita according to Rabbi Meir, as explained above.
After they passed, etc. This follows from the statement there, “during all the days of pregnancy,” etc., and from what is stated there, 9a: “The reason is only because her head and limbs are heavy,” etc. The same applies afterward, and likewise to a nursing woman. See the glosses of the Gra.
