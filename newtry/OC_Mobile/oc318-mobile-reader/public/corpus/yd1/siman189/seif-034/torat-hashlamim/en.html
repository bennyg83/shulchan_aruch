She must be concerned for the first Rosh Chodesh. Even if she did not see then, she must be concerned for the second Rosh Chodesh and likewise the third, because the pattern is not uprooted in fewer than three contrary occurrences. Shach in the name of Beit Yosef and the later authorities.
That she encounters. The Mechaber rules like Rashba because Beit Yosef writes that this is the implication of the Gemara and provides its rationale. Shach wonders why the Mechaber rules stringently against Tur and Ramban, who hold that she need not be concerned unless she saw once.
It is impossible to calculate concern. There is no prior sighting from which to measure an interval. See what I wrote above in seif katan 52.
