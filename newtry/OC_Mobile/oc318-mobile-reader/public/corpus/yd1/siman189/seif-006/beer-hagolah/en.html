From the dispute of Rav and Shmuel there, 64a. Rambam, chapter 8, rules like Shmuel, as does Ramban; the Tur writes so in their names and states that this was also the conclusion of his father, the Rosh. Rashba likewise appears to agree. Although we generally rule like Rav in matters of prohibition, they hold that here the halachah follows Shmuel because the Gemara states that the baraita supports him precisely.
