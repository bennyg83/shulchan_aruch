Just as she establishes a veset by interval of equal days and of unequal days, so she establishes on days of the month and on days of the week — equal and unequal. How so? She saw 3 times on Sunday, or on Thursday; or on the 1st of Nisan and the 1st of Iyar and the 1st of Sivan, or on the 5th of Nisan and the 5th of Iyar and the 5th of Sivan — she established for herself a veset on Sunday, or on Thursday, or on the 1st of the month, or on the 5th of it. Even though one month is full and one is deficient, we are not precise about this.
