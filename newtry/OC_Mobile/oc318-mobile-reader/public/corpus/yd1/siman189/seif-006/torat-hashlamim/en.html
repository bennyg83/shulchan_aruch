She saw three times on Sunday. For example, she saw on Sunday and again three weeks later on Sunday. She thereby establishes a veset for Sunday every three weeks. So write Perishah, Bach, and Shach. One should not ask why this must be taught, since she has also established equal intervals. An interval veset would be established only after the fourth sighting, because the first sighting is not itself an interval. By contrast, the weekday pattern is established after three sightings, since the first sighting already occurred on Sunday, as Beit Yosef writes.
