(Taz s.k. 39) His distinction in Raavad's words is incorrect — since we say its law is like day veset alone why not also be concerned before the onah; and further below poskim and Tur who wrote she must separate entire onah like day veset alone — implies day veset alone truly has such law; and further Raavad there also said but before veset permitted and if she examined at veset time and did not see — permitted after veset — appears before that i.e. permitted after veset only that there he said even if she has fixed day for those signs but no fixed veset i.e. she yawned or sneezed much this Rosh Chodesh afterward and did not see — if so she has fixed day for those signs and afterward yawned two Roshei Chodesh in a row and saw afterward — must be concerned for yawning veset alone; but here body veset itself comes at known time and is fixed — then we say even though yawning also fixed its law is like day veset alone i.e. body veset fixed three times at known time — when that day of yawning arrives she must separate from husband entire onah like day veset alone — end quote Rosh in name of Raavad brought Beit Yosef; appears specifically fixed as explained; and so Maggid Mishneh ch. 8 as explained brought Beit Yosef below beginning daf 337. What Rabbi Maggid wrote — he is imprecise — per what he wrote he is precise. However from Rashba responsum 438 and Shulchan Aruch above siman 187 seif 11 — appears even unfixed veset must separate entire onah — Beit Yosef's view and as Siftei Kohen s.k. 78; and so appears from plain words of Tur and Shulchan Aruch here; and so appears primary for law.
