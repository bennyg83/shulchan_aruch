(Taz s.k. 28) At first glance, this is contrary to the Gemara, etc. He concludes that Ramban had a different version of the Gemara than the Rosh. This is difficult, for the Tur cites Ramban's ruling without qualification. Rather, the primary explanation is as I wrote in the Shach, s.k. 40. When the Tur and Ramban write, “If, after changing once or twice to the thirtieth day, she then saw at the end of twenty days, etc.,” they mean that she saw at the end of twenty days after having seen a third time after thirty days. Accordingly, although she did not see three times on the twentieth day, the twentieth-day veset was not uprooted, because she saw only twice on the thirtieth day, while the third sighting occurred after thirty days. Therefore, when she then saw at the end of twenty days following the sighting that occurred after thirty days, the twenty-day veset returned to its place. The Perishah also explains it this way; see there.
