She did not establish a veset. Even for those who are stringent in seif 7 — Bach wrote: even if she returned and saw on the 19th of Tammuz she did not establish a veset for skipping since the skips are not equal. Nevertheless it appears: since the sighting on the 16th of Iyar from the sighting on the 15th of Nisan gives an interval of 32 days equal to the interval of the 18th of Sivan from the 16th of Iyar, which is also 32 days — Nisan full and Iyar deficient — she is concerned for this interval; and when she sees on the 19th of Tammuz, which is also an interval of 32 from the 18th of Sivan, she established a veset of three equal intervals of 32 days in four sightings — end quote; and this is obvious.
Since she alternated etc. Even if she had a fixed veset beforehand for the 14th — we do not say since she now saw on the 15th and 16th she established a veset; rather we require she see three sightings besides the 14th sighting; therefore this one who alternated to the 18th did not establish a veset — so is proven in the Gemara 64a.
