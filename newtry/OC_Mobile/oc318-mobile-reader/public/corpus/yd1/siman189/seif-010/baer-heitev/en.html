That she skipped. The Shach wrote that even if she previously had a fixed veses on the 14th, we do not say that since she now saw on the 15th and 16th she has established a veses — rather we require that she see three sightings aside from the sighting of the 14th. Therefore this woman who skipped to the 18th has not established a veses. The Bach wrote that even if she returned and saw on the 19th of Tammuz, she has not established a veses for skipping since the skips are not equal. However, it appears that since the sighting on the 16th of Iyar from the sighting on the 15th of Nissan has an interval of 32 days, equal to the interval from the 18th of Sivan to the 16th of Iyar which is also 32 days (since Nissan is full and Iyar is deficient), she is concerned for this interval. When she sees on the 19th of Tammuz, which is also an interval of 32 days from the 18th of Sivan, she has established a veses of three equal intervals of 32 days with four sightings.
