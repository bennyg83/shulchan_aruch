She did not establish a veset. Since the pattern was intermittent, this is true even according to the stringent opinion in seif 7. Nevertheless, she must be concerned for the nineteenth of Tammuz, because there are two equal intervals. From the fifteenth of Nisan to the sixteenth of Iyar is thirty-two days, and likewise from Iyar to the eighteenth of Sivan, since Nisan is full and Iyar deficient. If she then sees on the nineteenth of Tammuz, which is likewise thirty-two days after the eighteenth of Sivan, she establishes a thirty-two-day interval veset through three intervals and four sightings. So write Bach and Shach.
