She is concerned. Avodat HaTosafot in name of Taz: also here concerned for day 1 of Rosh Chodesh. Seder Taharah and Chiddushei Dinim already disagreed: here she saw on 20th of Nisan no concern for onah beinonis which is from interval reason and already stopped by sight of 20th — needless to say Perishah's Bach brought in Avodat HaTosafot s.k. following that in every interval veset when she stopped by sight count is from adjacent sight — certainly for onah beinonis likewise; even per Taz who disputes there — nevertheless for onah beinonis reason is as Ran wrote because forever will not see — certainly count from adjacent sight; Bach's words were correct — see there. Regarding dispute between Taz and Shach on onah beinonis — Taz holds onah beinonis is not month veset but day 30 to her sight and if month deficient both same day but if full also concerned for prior day for onah beinonis; Shach holds onah beinonis is always day of month and measure of onah beinonis is day 31 as default full month. Chatzot haShavuah responsum siman 114 extended to challenge Shach and agreed with Taz; likewise Kiryat Sefer and Peltei agreed — see there; see Chiddushei Dinim who agreed with them that onah beinonis is not month veset but essence as Shach — day 31; if so opposite — if full month both same day and if deficient also concerned for day after for onah beinonis — see there. Know even per Shach must be distinction between onah beinonis and month veset: for month veset as long as not fixed and time passed and she did not examine — permitted without examination as appears seif 4; but for onah beinonis forbidden until examines as explained there — you find month veset without onah beinonis e.g. if she saw in between or has another fixed veset and afterward changed to another day — so Acharonim.
