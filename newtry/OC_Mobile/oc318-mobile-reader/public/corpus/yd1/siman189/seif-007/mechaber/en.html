How does she establish on days of the month by skipping? For example, she saw on the 15th of Nisan, and the 16th of Iyar, and the 17th of Sivan — she did not establish a veset until she sees on the 18th of Tammuz, for the first seeing does not combine, since the intervals are not equal. Nevertheless, if she had a veset before she began, and afterward she changed and saw by skipping 3 times, she established a veset by skipping, since even the first in the skipping, she saw it by skipping from the veset established for her (Rambam and Ramban like Shmuel; and Tur according to Rosh, but in perek Shor SheNagach Arba’ah VaChamishah Rosh ruled like Rav, and so it is in the Remezim there). And some say that even though she saw only on the 15th of Nisan and the 16th of Iyar and the 17th of Sivan, she established a veset, and she is concerned for the 18th of Tammuz and the 19th of Av, and so forever; and one should be concerned for their words and be stringent.
