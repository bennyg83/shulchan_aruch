In Tammuz. And then she establishes a veset in months for skipping, and afterward must be concerned for 19th of Av and 20th of Elul, and so forever. Shach.
The first. That is, the first skip itself was uniform with the remaining skips; for example, she was accustomed to see on the twentieth day and changed to see on the 21st, 22nd, and 23rd: the sighting on the 21st also counts from the reckoning, since it too was a uniform skip like the others. This is contrary to the Drisha who wrote that the first skip need not be uniform with the remaining skips, which is entirely incorrect. So wrote Taz and Shach.
And to be stringent. Taz wrote there is reason to wonder, for even according to the first view there is one stringency; namely if she has concern for two vesetot and one of them is not uprooted unless the second becomes fixed, as stated seif 13 at the beginning of the Hagahah; if so, according to the latter view here where skipping veset became fixed three times, concern for the second veset is automatically uprooted and she is no longer concerned for it; but according to the first view it is not yet fixed and she is concerned for both; therefore Bach wrote that for this matter we rule like the first reasoning, namely to be stringent in both; and this is correct, and I have room to examine Tur on this who wrote that Rosh ruled like Shmuel that it is the first reasoning here and in chapter Shor sheNagach 4 and 5 Rosh wrote on daf 8 that we rule like Rav here; requires study.
