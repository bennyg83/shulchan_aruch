From the count. Avodat HaTosafot: what he wrote however if she had veset before, etc.; and elsewhere wrote likewise conversely she saw Nisan, Sivan, Av, Elul, Tishrei — concerned for equal veset and even if previously four alternations and afterward returned and saw three consecutive months — alternation veset uprooted and order veset fixed. He further wrote alternation veset once fixed e.g. saw Nisan, Sivan, Av and afterward did not see three consecutive months i.e. Elul, Tishrei, Cheshvan — nevertheless concerned for Kislev and 34th for interval veset in Avodat HaTosafot below s.k. 33, etc.; and this veset is not uprooted until she sees in three alternations i.e. six months — see there.
