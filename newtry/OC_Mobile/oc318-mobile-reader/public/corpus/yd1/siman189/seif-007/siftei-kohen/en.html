Since there is no etc. For in the first there was no name of “skip.”
Nevertheless if she had a veset, etc. Meaning: even if her veset was in a greatly spaced skip on the 12th or 13th of the month — but when she had no fixed veset, a greatly spaced skip does not suffice unless the skip is equal with four sightings — so Derishah siman 12; and so appears at first glance: if you say specifically where she had an equal veset for skipping — now that her veset was on the 14th — plainly there are four sightings; and even without a veset, since she saw four times in a skip — and so appears in Tur that Shmuel holds she did not establish her veset except with four sightings, etc.; and even for Shmuel if she had a veset, etc. — see there. But the difficulty is: whence this? For in the Gemara (64a) it only says when her veset was equal to her present skip; but when her veset was not equal — one may say: since she alternated she did not establish a veset, as below seif 12; and so is proven below seif 15 that in such a case she did not establish a veset for skips, as written there in s.k. 42 — see there; and so is proven from Rashba and Taz below seif 27 s.k. 68 regarding "but if she decreased or increased," etc. — see there; and see Tosafot first chapter of Niddah 9b s.v. pachatah, etc. — investigate well. Therefore it appears it deals where her veset was equal to her skip; and so Bach on Tur's words — but he informs us of a greater matter: even if she had a veset beforehand, three sightings afterward suffice — to exclude the other view that holds like Rav who establishes with three sightings; and for Rav, if she had a veset beforehand, two sightings afterward do not suffice — as stated explicitly in the Gemara: Rav says to the accustomed woman, I am different (Rashi: since her veset is fixed for the 15th we count the sighting of the 15th with the sightings that passed over her, for she saw at her time, and two sightings afterward do not suffice; but if I say: I am different where the sighting on the 15th was the beginning of her sighting, or she had no veset before, or she had a veset from the 20th — end quote; meaning: since she had a veset for the 20th which is later than the 15th, one cannot say we discard the 15th for her subsequent veset, for her veset has not yet arrived). If so there is no contradiction: just as for Rav it is worse if she had a veset — we do not combine the sighting of her veset so there would be three sightings — likewise for Shmuel we should not combine so there would be four sightings; and we should say we judge the sighting of her veset with the sightings that passed over her — but this is not in the Gemara in the baraita there, that for Shmuel the sighting of her veset combines so there are four sightings; but when she has no fixed veset the Tur did not need to inform us, for plainly there are exactly four sightings. It appears to me.
One should be concerned for their words and be stringent. Bach wrote: that is, to be stringent for both; therefore she is concerned for the veset of interval which she established in two sightings and we say she has not yet established a veset for skipping — like the first opinion and as below — end quote; meaning she is concerned for the 17th of Tammuz because of the interval veset, and for the 18th thereof because of the skip veset; and if it was established in three sightings it is uprooted only three times, as is the law of a fixed veset; and so Raavad in Ba'alei Nefesh end of folio 57; and for sightings we take the stringency of Rav and the stringency of Shmuel, and she is concerned on the fourth for the 17th — for per Rav's view she is only concerned for the 18th — end quote.
