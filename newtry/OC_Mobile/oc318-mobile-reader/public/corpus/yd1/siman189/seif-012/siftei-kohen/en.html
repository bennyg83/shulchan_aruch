Veset ha-sirugin, etc. It does not mean she saw at the beginning on Rosh Chodesh Nisan, second on Rosh Chodesh Sivan, third on Rosh Chodesh Av — for then plainly she established a veset, for whence that veset ha-sirugin and not veset ha-shaveh, for she never alternated? Nevertheless one may say it is still called veset ha-sirugin since ordinarily women have a veset from thirty to thirty; if so, this woman who saw from two months to two months alternated from the way of other women. But more likely it deals even where she saw at the beginning on Rosh Chodesh Adar and afterward on Rosh Chodesh Nisan — even though now she saw month to month, if she again saw on Rosh Chodesh Sivan and Av she established a veset for alternations, for now we see she alternated from two months to two months; and so appears explicitly in Ba'alei Nefesh for Raavad, brought by Beit Yosef, who wrote that in veset ha-sirugin even Shmuel concedes the first sighting counts. And this is what the Mechaber said: the first sighting counts — s.v.; and in the Gemara regarding Rav says to the accustomed woman I am different it implies the dispute of Rav and Shmuel is not specifically where her first sighting was thus, but likewise where she has no fixed veset; and Rashi explained there explicitly, and I brought his language in s.k. 18; and so from Taz above seif 6. If so, when we say here Shmuel concedes in veset ha-sirugin the first sighting counts — even if this was not her first sighting but she simply had no veset before — similar to what Rav concedes to Shmuel. Nevertheless where she had a veset before — e.g. she saw on Rosh Chodesh Shevat and Adar and Nisan and then alternated to see on Rosh Chodesh Sivan and Av — it appears for all opinions the Nisan sighting does not count and she did not establish a veset for alternations but for Rosh Chodesh; and even though we say in the Gemara if she had a veset on the 20th and afterward saw in a skip on the 21st, 22nd, and 23rd Shmuel concedes the 21st sighting counts — because there she skipped from her fixed veset on the 20th and saw on the 21st, and there the skip applied to her; but here since she established a veset from Rosh Chodesh to Rosh Chodesh, on Rosh Chodesh Nisan no alternation applied but veset ha-shaveh — investigate.
