And a “veset of irregularity” (veset hasirug): the first seeing is included in the count according to all opinions, even though she spaced out her seeings; but regarding concern for its veset at the beginning, it is equal to skipping, for she is concerned only from Rosh Chodesh to the next adjacent Rosh Chodesh. For example, she saw on Rosh Chodesh Nisan; she is concerned for Rosh Chodesh Iyar. And if she did not see until Rosh Chodesh Sivan, she is concerned for the adjacent Rosh Chodesh Tammuz. And if she did not see on Rosh Chodesh Tammuz, she is not concerned for Rosh Chodesh Av even though they are an interval of 2 months like the first interval, because the pause of the second month nullified the seeing of the first month; and the seeing of the third month is the beginning of a veset, and she is concerned for the adjacent Rosh Chodesh and no more.
