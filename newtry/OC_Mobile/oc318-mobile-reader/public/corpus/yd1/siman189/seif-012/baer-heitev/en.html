From the count. Meaning Shach: for example she saw initially on Rosh Chodesh Adar and afterward on Rosh Chodesh Nisan — even though now she saw from month to month, if she again saw on Rosh Chodesh Sivan and Av she fixed veset for alternation, for now we see she alternated from two months to two months. Taz explained this means she saw on Rosh Chodesh Nisan, Sivan, and Av and fixed for Rosh Chodesh alternation — afterward she is concerned only for Rosh Chodesh Tishrei and not Elul, and so forever; here Rosh Chodesh Nisan is from the count for all, for in it there is also veset relevance since it is Rosh Chodesh and it is recognizable — see there. However if she had veset before, e.g. she saw on Rosh Chodesh Shevat, Adar, and Nisan and then alternated to see on Rosh Chodesh Sivan and Av — then Nisan sighting is not from the count, for since she fixed veset from Rosh Chodesh to Rosh Chodesh, on Rosh Chodesh Nisan she did not place alternation but equal veset.
