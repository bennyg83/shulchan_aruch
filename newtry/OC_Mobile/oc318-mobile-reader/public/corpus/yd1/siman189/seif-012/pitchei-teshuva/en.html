From the count. Chavot Daat discusses alternating-month cycles and how a later regular monthly cycle can uproot a skipping cycle. Such a cycle is uprooted only by three missed alternating intervals, totaling six months.
For Rosh Chodesh Av. Chavot Daat notes that the Mechaber's solar-month example is imprecise because the intervals are equal. The novelty applies where month lengths create unequal calendar dates but equal intervals.
