That she establish one veset. The Tiferet Devorah wrote that this is specifically with a concern for a monthly veset and a concern for an interval veset; then, once one is established, she no longer concerns herself with the second, since it is impossible for both to be maintained. But where both are concerns for a monthly veset, such as where she saw three times on Rosh Chodesh and the third time she also saw on the twenty-fifth, she is concerned also for the twenty-fifth, even though the veset of Rosh Chodesh has already been established, since it is possible for both to be maintained, for a woman can establish a veset within a veset, as in seif 32. The same law applies to a veset of skipping and a fixed veset; see there.
