An intermittent veset. Shach writes that it is called intermittent because most women see every thirty days, whereas this woman sees every second month and has therefore departed from the ordinary pattern. It appears more correct, however, that the case applies even if her first sighting was on Rosh Chodesh Adar and the next on Rosh Chodesh Nisan. If she later sees on Rosh Chodesh Sivan and Av, she establishes an alternating-month pattern, for we now see that she skips every other month. Shach's printed text is difficult and has several corrupt readings. The correct reading appears to be that in the older Krakow editions: her first sighting was on Rosh Chodesh Adar, and where the text reads afterward on Rosh Chodesh Sivan, it should read Nisan. With this correction, Shach's words are sound, for although she initially saw on consecutive months, the later sightings in Sivan and Av combine with the Nisan sighting to establish the alternating pattern.
