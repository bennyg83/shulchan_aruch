Alternation veset. Shach wrote therefore called alternation veset since default women default from thirty to thirty — this woman saw from two months to two months alternated from way of other women. But more appears deals even if she initially saw Rosh Chodesh Adar and then Rosh Chodesh Sivan — even though now saw month to month if she again saw Rosh Chodesh Sivan and Av she fixed alternation veset for now we see she alternated two to two months — end quote Shach; and truly his words puzzling at first sight since initial sighting was Rosh Chodesh Adar and then Rosh Chodesh Sivan — how wrote now saw month to month — behold from three months to three months; and further against Shulchan Aruch who wrote initial alternation sighting was Rosh Chodesh Nisan; per his words she never saw Rosh Chodesh Nisan. In printed Shach corrected and wrote: even if she initially saw Rosh Chodesh Iyar — but also no correction for then difficulty where alternation fixation from one alternation sight; she saw Rosh Chodesh (Nisan) Iyar Sivan and once skipped to Rosh Chodesh Av; also still against Shulchan Aruch; and more wonder on both versions how Shach concludes if she again saw Rosh Chodesh Sivan — until now deals she saw in Sivan; therefore appears primary as old Krakow editions initial sighting Rosh Chodesh Adar but where wrote and then Rosh Chodesh Sivan must emend Nisan, etc.; if so Shach's words correct — even though initially saw Adar and Nisan consecutively month to month — if afterward saw Sivan and Av — Nisan sight joins and here three skips; only if she had three-times fixation month to month then Nisan sight does not join and needs one more alternation sight to fix veset; per this Shach's words explain well — see his words at length.
