How does she establish it? For example, if she sees 4 times, with 3 equal intervals between them; for example, she saw today, and at the end of 20 days another time, and again at the end of 20 days, and again at the end of 20 days — and this is called a veset of intervals. Therefore she needs 4 seeings, for the first seeing is not included in the count, since it is not part of an interval. And even before she established it 3 times, she is concerned: immediately after she saw one time, at the end of 20 she is concerned from now on when 20 arrives. And likewise for a seeing of days — that it is on known days of the month — immediately after she saw one time on a known day of the month, such as the 21st or the 25th of it, she is concerned for another time on that day, and it is forbidden to have tashmish the entire onah; and the same applies to other kinds of veset that she must be concerned for likewise, except for a veset of skipping, as will be explained in this siman (these are my words, and it is obvious). And they only said that it must be established 3 times with regard to uprooting, for since she established it 3 times, it is not uprooted with fewer than 3 times; for as long as she has not uprooted it 3 times, she must be concerned for it. But to become forbidden, even one time she is concerned for it a second time; nevertheless, even though she is concerned for it, it is uprooted with one time, even if she established it 2 times — for if she saw 2 times on a known day and the third time she did not see, she is no longer concerned for it.
