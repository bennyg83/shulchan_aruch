(Supplement) How, etc. From the Mishnah there, 63b, which states, “If she was accustomed,” etc., and “she changed three times,” etc., it is evident that she must repeat the pattern three times according to the intervals. One cannot answer that a woman who was already accustomed is different, for if so, when the Gemara there, 64a, challenges from “If she was accustomed,” etc., why did it cite this? It is necessarily different because she was already accustomed, as is clear from the Mishnah. Furthermore, from the subsequent teaching, “A woman does not establish,” etc., it is evident that there is no distinction between the establishment of the veset of this woman, who was previously accustomed, and that of another woman who was not previously accustomed. Thus writes Torat HaBayit. See Tosafot to Bava Kamma 37b, s.v. The day, etc. Although Shmuel said there, 63b, “They stated,” etc., that follows Rabbi, as Tosafot there explain, s.v. For days, etc., and as stated there in the Gemara. When the Gemara says there, “These are the words of Rabban Gamliel,” etc., it means that this Tanna holds accordingly, for Rav and Shmuel dispute there, 64a, and one says, “until she repeats it three times,” etc. The anonymous Mishnah there likewise follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, and the halachah is expressly decided in the chapter HaBa al Yevimto, 64b, in accordance with Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel regarding vesetot. Thus writes Torat HaBayit. See Tosafot 63b, s.v. This, etc., and 11a, s.v. She jumped, etc. (End of supplement.)
