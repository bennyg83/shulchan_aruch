(Likut) How, etc. From what is stated in mishnah there 63b "she was accustomed," etc. "she changed three times," etc. — evidently she must triple in intervals; and it challenged there 64a "it established her accustomed," etc. "what does it call her accustomed — behold necessarily accustomed from mishnah"; and further from what we teach afterward "a woman does not fix," etc. — evidently no distinction between establishment of this accustomed one and another not accustomed.
