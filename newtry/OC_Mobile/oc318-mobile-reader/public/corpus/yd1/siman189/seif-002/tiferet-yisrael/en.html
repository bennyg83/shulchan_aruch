Seif 2 in Shulchan Aruch seif 3. If she fixed veset for hours — Raavad derives this from ascending from ritual immersion — her husband on the riverbank, etc.; and I in my poverty cannot understand this proof, for it is clear in Gemara the reason is her friend recognized her; and therefore permitted her husband on the riverbank before city entry — as Rashi and Tosafot wrote; if so one can say besides possible jumping veset — more possible they only attributed to friend's recognition; and possible Raavad did not explain so in Gemara; but the wonder Gaon Imrei Moshe wondered s.k. 3 on Mechaber who ruled uprooted at once even without examination — one can say behold Rif and Rambam and even Raavad hold husband coming from road had relations with her even while sleeping since vesetot are rabbinic; and above siman 184 reason for matter; if so in this veset contrary to majority of women who do not intend by hours and also not explicit in Talmud and poskim — Mechaber relied on those great ones who do not require even initial examination — they certainly hold here established in presumption of purity — need not even initially examine — one can say.
