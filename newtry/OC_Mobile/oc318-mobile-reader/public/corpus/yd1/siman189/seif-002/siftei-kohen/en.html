From the moment she saw. A second time at the end of twenty days from the first sighting she is concerned from then onward; and likewise when the twentieth day arrives a third time.
And likewise with sightings by days etc. That is, every woman when she saw the first time must be concerned a second time for that same day — such as if she first saw on the 21st of Nisan she must be concerned afterward on the 21st of Iyar; and if afterward she saw a second time she must also be concerned for the interval veset, as below seif 13 in the note.
