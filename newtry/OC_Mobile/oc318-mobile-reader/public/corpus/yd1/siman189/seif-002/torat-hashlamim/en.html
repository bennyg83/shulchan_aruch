At the end of twenty days. Meaning, twenty days after the first sighting.
By one occurrence. Even an average interval, although established by only one occurrence, is uprooted by one contrary occurrence. This is the implication of the Gemara, Tur, Rashba, all the poskim, and the Mechaber here. This is unlike Olat Tamid, who writes in seif 13 that the average interval is exactly like an established veset and is uprooted only after three times. He was not precise, and Shach already challenged him in seif katan 30; see there.
