And 17th of Sivan. Per first view Mechaber brought seif 7 — necessarily deals she had fixed veset initially as above; so Perishah; and this appears Tur and Mechaber intent wrote saw three times in three months skipping — implies all sights must be in skipping; if she had no fixed veset first sight in skipping is not called first sight as explained above; if so also in this law concerned for stringency of both views as s.k. 14 and Shach challenged Perishah wrote implication Tur and Rashba here both Rav and Shmuel suffice three sights — end quote; his words not compelled at all; no implication in their words at all; can explain their words as stated; and from where lenient against R. Chananel brought in Tosafot Niddah 64a s.v. itmar — clear for Shmuel even in such case need four sights; especially since this law learned from R. Chananel's words as Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh 174a wrote.
And concerned forever. Bach seif 11 wrote likewise three times in two months returning in cycle is also fixed veset — e.g. saw 15th Nisan 16th Iyar 15th Sivan 16th Tammuz 15th Av 16th Elul — afterward concerned 15th Tishrei 16th Cheshvan and so forever; so in Sefer Baalei Nefesh Raavad daf 96a; and that poskim stated three months not two months (see Bach and Taz to resolve this difficulty) — appears for emphasis they stated even if third-time skip three times and one might say first fixation uprooted and fixation for skip month and concerned 18th Tevet 19th Shevat and so forever — teaches nevertheless all one fixation i.e. for cycle skipping — clear.
