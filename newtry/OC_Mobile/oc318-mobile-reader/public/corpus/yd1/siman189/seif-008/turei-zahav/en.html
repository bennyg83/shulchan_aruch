She saw three times in three months, etc. Can state briefly e.g. 15th Sivan 16th Tammuz then 15th Av 16th Elul — she fixed afterward in one month on fifteenth and in second on sixteenth and so forever; but teaches leniency even if skip is three days i.e. 15th 15th 17th — we follow them and concerned in third month on day 17 even though between day 15 only two days; and automatically know if skip more than three days i.e. saw four times in four months three times skipping — also we follow them; stated three times for this is measure she was presumed in — automatically same law for more than this. So appears plain.
