She saw three times in three months, etc. He could have stated the case briefly, for example: she saw on the 15th of Sivan, the 16th of Tammuz, then the 15th of Av and the 16th of Elul. She has established a veses to see in one month on the 15th and in the next month on the 16th, and so on forever. Rather, he teaches a greater novelty: even if the progression is separated by three days, meaning 15, 15, and 17, we follow the pattern, and in the third month she must be concerned for the 17th, even though two days separate it from the 15th. From this we automatically know that the same applies when the progression is greater than three days, namely when she saw four times in four months with three progressive changes, for there too we follow the pattern. He mentioned three occurrences because this is the minimum by which the pattern becomes established, and the same obviously applies to more than this. This appears obvious to me.
