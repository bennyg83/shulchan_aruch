She saw 3 times in 3 months by skipping, and again saw in those same skips; if she conducted herself so 3 times, this is a fixed veset of circular skipping. How so? She saw the 15th of Nisan, and the 16th of Iyar, and the 17th of Sivan; and again in a circle she saw the 15th of Tammuz, and the 16th of Av, and the 17th of Elul; and again she saw the 15th of Tishrei, and the 16th of Cheshvan, and the 17th of Kislev — she established for herself a veset of circular skipping, and she is concerned forever: the 15th of this month, and the 16th of this month, and the 17th of this month.
