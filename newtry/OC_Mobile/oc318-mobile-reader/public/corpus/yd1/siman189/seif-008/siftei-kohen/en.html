She saw three times, etc. Perishah wrote seif 14: for Shmuel we require that she also see on the 18th of Tammuz, and afterward in the three months following the 16th, 17th, and 18th, and likewise in the third three months per Tosafot's explanation of Rosh Chodesh, etc.; and if so Tur here deals with Shmuel where she has a veset — end quote. Difficulty: Tur's plain words imply here for all opinions three times suffice in every matter; and so appears in Terumat HaDeshen HaKatzar 64a. Rather it appears: since the poskim rejected Rosh Chodesh's explanation, they hold here that for Rav and Shmuel three times suffice — since she saw three times on the 15th and three on the 16th and three on the 17th, it is as if she saw three times on equal days of the month, as above seif 6 — investigate; and so appears explicitly in Bach siman 11 as he explained — see there.
And she is concerned. 15th of Tevet, 16th of Shevat, 17th of Adar, and so forever. Bach wrote seif 11: likewise with three times in two months and it returns cyclically — also a fixed veset; for example she saw on the 15th of Nisan and 16th of Iyar and 15th of Sivan and 16th of Tammuz and 15th of Av and 16th of Elul — she established her veset for cyclical skip and is concerned afterward for the 15th of Tishrei and 16th of Cheshvan and 15th of Kislev and 16th of Tevet, and so forever — end quote; and so is explained in Sefer Ba'alei Nefesh, and I brought it below s.k. 72.
