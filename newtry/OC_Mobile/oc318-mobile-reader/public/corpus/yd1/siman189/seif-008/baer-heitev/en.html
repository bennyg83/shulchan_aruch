Bach wrote likewise three times and two months returning in cycle is also a fixed veset — for example she saw 15th of Nisan, 16th of Iyar, 15th of Sivan, 16th of Tammuz, 15th of Av, 16th of Elul — she fixed her veset for skipping in cycle and afterward is concerned for 15th of Tishrei, 16th of Cheshvan, 15th of Kislev, 16th of Tevet, and so forever. Shach wrote that here both for Rav and Shmuel three times suffice, for since she saw three times on 15th, three times on 16th, and three times on 17th — it is as if she saw three times on equal days of the month, unlike Derishah who wrote that for Shmuel we also need on 18th of Tammuz and afterward in the three months after it 16th, 17th, and 18th — end quote.
