(Taz s.k. 32) And apparently, etc. They preceded him in Shach s.k. 59. {Hagahah:} They wrote in Hagahah Rashba and Tur and other poskim hold like Talmud law — woman does not fix veset in niddah days nor zivah days: how if she saw on 1st of month and 5th of it which is within niddah days of first sighting — even if she did so three times she did not fix veset for 5th of month but for 1st of month; and if she saw on 1st and 15th (likewise 18th which is still zivah days — so Perishah) she did not fix for 15th because 15th sight is within zivah of 1st sight — when she saw from open source as I explained; but from closed source e.g. twice on Rosh Chodesh and third time on 25th of month and on Rosh Chodesh — even though third sight was within niddah days of 25th sight she fixes on Rosh Chodesh because first two sights were from closed source and extra blood of 25th sight hastened — also concerned for advance day lest another veset fixes — end quote (and even though above seif 13 Hagahah we say if she fixed veset she is no longer concerned for unfixed veset she had — different here since third Rosh Chodesh sight is not considered full blood since from open source. Hagahah of Derishah and so Bach). Mechaber and Rabbi omitted all this — appears because Tur and poskim in name of Ramban: since nowadays daughters of Israel were stringent on themselves not to separate between niddah and zivah days (as above siman 183 in Tur and Beit Yosef — see there) — likewise for veset fixation there is no distinction; therefore if she saw from 15th to 15th she fixed veset necessarily. But truly Ramban said only for stringency that she fixes veset even in niddah and zivah days but not to be lenient — certainly he would not divide on the Talmud; already explained in several places laws of stringent unfixed veset from fixed; also one without fixed veset must be concerned for month veset and interval unlike fixed — as above seif 13; and so appears in Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh and Maggid Mishneh Ramban said only to be stringent not lenient; and so Perishah seif 93: if so also now be stringent to fix in zivah and niddah days; distinction for uprooting that even if uprooted twice must be concerned for third — end quote; from extra language "in our time be stringent" also distinction for uprooting which is stringency not leniency — evidently only to be stringent we hold no distinction; but to be lenient Heaven forbid divide on Talmud; if so Mechaber and Rabbi should have brought these laws; nevertheless we learn wherever this siman says she fixed veset — if for leniency it is specifically not in niddah and zivah days — end quote.
