She jumped on a known day, such as on Rosh Chodesh or on Sunday, and she saw on it; and it happened so on 3 Rosh Chodesh or on 3 Sundays — she established for herself a veset, and she is concerned for each time that she jumps at that time. But if afterward Sunday arrived and she did not jump, or she jumped on Monday, she is not concerned, for she established only for jumps of Sunday.
