Whose time to see has not yet arrived. According to Rambam, chapter 4, halachah 1 of Hilkhot Mishkav uMoshav, it appears that an elderly woman who saw twice after ninety days conveys impurity retroactively for twenty-four hours. See Kesef Mishneh there, who supports Rambam's ruling from the Mishnah. Accordingly, there is a substantial distinction between an elderly woman and a girl whose time to see has not yet arrived. I am surprised that the Mechaber departed from his usual practice and did not mention Rambam's opinion at all. Perhaps he holds that although she conveys impurity retroactively for twenty-four hours, this is still treated like an unfixed veset, for the original veset has nevertheless been uprooted, and an elderly woman need not be concerned for an unfixed veset. This requires study.
