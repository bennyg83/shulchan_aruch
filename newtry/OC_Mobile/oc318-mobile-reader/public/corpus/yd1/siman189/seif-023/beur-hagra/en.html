She ate, etc. Gemara there; and Beit Yosef wrote from Rosh's and Mordechai's words it appears by three types of eating she fixed veset. His words are puzzling, for Rosh's language is like Gemara's and in Mordechai appears the opposite — he wrote in siman 17 "all day forbidden to have relations," etc. "but a woman whose veset is for days but for other signs," etc. "she yawned sneezed," etc. "or ate garlic or ate peppers," etc.; and so in Tosafot HaRosh 168b; and there is accustomed to see in compound days — and action how: e.g. she jumped on one Shabbat and saw and jumped on one Shabbat and saw, or ate peppers on one Shabbat and saw and ate peppers on one Shabbat and saw, etc.; therefore poskim omitted it included in law of jumps and hold like Rashba that it is not fixed except in days like law of jumps; and Gemara did not mention there except to divide between them and other vesetot that are on one and two; and this is in three; but for us all is in three since decided like Rashbag in chapter HaBa al haYevamah — no need for this.
