All these bodily vesatos that are established by means of an occurrence: one does not combine with another; rather, anyone who yawned 3 times and saw established a veset, but if she yawned one time and sneezed two times, they do not combine. {Rama: She ate garlic and saw; she ate onion and saw; she ate peppers and saw — some say that she established for herself a veset to see through eating any hot foods (Rosh, perek HaIshah, and Mordechai at the beginning of Shevuos). And some say that all this — that she sees through food — has the law like seeing through jumping and other act that she does, which is called seeing because of oness; and she does not establish a veset except with days (Beit Yosef according to Rashba). And some say that its law is like a veset of seeing through an occurrence in her body, and she establishes it even without equal days (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos).}
