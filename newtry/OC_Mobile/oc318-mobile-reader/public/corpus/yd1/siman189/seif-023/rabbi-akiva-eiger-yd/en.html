(Seif 23 in Hagahah) she ate garlic and saw. Plainly appears specifically in such case since she saw in three sharp types; but if she ate garlic twice she fixed only for garlic — as goring ox: ox, donkey, camel is muad for all; but ox that gored twice is muad only for oxen; if so automatically also ate garlic twice and saw and ate onion and saw — not fixed at all. Per this I have difficulty — appears we divide between body veset where one is not fixed by another; but for sharp foods we combine all — why then state if she yawned once and sneezed twice; likewise for sharp foods eating there is no fixation as stated — should have stated yawned once and sneezed and saw and head and limbs heavy on her which are three different matters — she did not fix — requires study.
