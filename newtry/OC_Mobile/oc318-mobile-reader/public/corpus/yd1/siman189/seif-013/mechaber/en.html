A woman does not establish for herself a veset even if she saw 3 Rosh Chodesh one after another, unless they were all in one onah — in the day or at night. And if she saw 3 times in the day and the fourth at night, or 3 times at night and the fourth in the day, she is concerned for day and for night, because of concern for the first veset and because of concern for the second, which is the last. But if she saw sometimes in the day and sometimes at night not in order (and she did not establish either of them 3 times), or she saw the first in the day and the last 3 at night, or the first at night and the last 3 in the day, or 3 in this and 3 in that, she is concerned only for the last one. {Rama: A woman who saw is concerned for the day-of-month veset and for interval until she establishes the day-of-month veset 3 times, or an interval-veset 4 times, or until one of them is uprooted. How so? She saw on the 1st of Nisan and the 20th of it; she is concerned for the 1st of Iyar because of Rosh Chodesh Nisan. She saw on the 1st of Iyar, or she did not see on it — she is concerned for the 9th of Iyar, which is the day of 20 from the seeing of the day of 20 that she saw. She saw on the 9th of Iyar, or she did not see — she is concerned for the 20th of Iyar, lest she established for herself a veset of the 20th of the month, for she saw the 20th of Nisan. And so she is concerned forever until she establishes one veset as is its law; then she is not concerned for the second, which was not established; or until one of them is uprooted, then she is not concerned for it even though the second was not established (all in Tur in the name of Ramban). And she is not concerned for a skipping-veset until she establishes it. How so? She saw the 15th of Nisan — she is concerned for the 15th of Iyar. If she did not see on the 15th of Iyar, she is not concerned for the 16th of it. If she saw the 16th of it, she is concerned for the 16th of Sivan, and she is not concerned for the 17th of it. If she saw the 17th of it, she is concerned for the 17th of Tammuz, and she is not concerned for the 18th of it. If she saw the 18th of Tammuz, she established for herself a skipping-veset for days of the month, and she is concerned for the 19th of Av (this too is from the implication of Tur). And likewise in this manner for interval and skipping, for there is no difference between them, except that some say that in the skipping of the month, the first seeing is included in the count, as was explained. If she saw the 15th of Nisan and her seeing continued 4 days, and on the 16th of Iyar she saw and her seeing continued 3 days, and in Sivan she began to see on the 17th of it: some say that she is concerned for skipping and for an equal-day veset, for she “tripled” to see 3 times on the 17th of the month (Tur and Ramban). And some say that there is no equal-day veset here at all, for we always go after the beginning of the seeing; and so is the main [ruling] (Beit Yosef in the name of Razah and Rashba and Rosh and Raavad in Sefer Ba’al HaNefesh, brought by Bach at the end of se’if 20).}
