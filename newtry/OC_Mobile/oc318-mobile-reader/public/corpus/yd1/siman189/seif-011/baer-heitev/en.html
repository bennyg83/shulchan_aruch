Shach wrote from Derishah's words it appears that even if she has a fixed veset on 15th of the month and afterward saw on 16th and 17th; Rav holds she fixed a veset for skipping; and specifically if she has veset from 20th to 20th and changed by skipping Rav holds she did not fix veset for skipping; and this is not so, for explicitly in the Talmud even if she has veset for 16th of the month we judge 16th as veset of the day before it; and so Rashi explained there explicitly; end quote.
