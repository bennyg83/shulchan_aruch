Once or twice. Derishah wrote siman 19: meaning for Rav once and for Shmuel twice; but for Rav, if she saw today and at the end of the 31st and at the end of the 32nd — even though there are only two skips — he holds she established a veset for skips unless we say she had a veset first from the 20th to the 20th and changed and saw on the 30th and afterward on the 31st — that is two skips, and nevertheless she does not establish a veset for skips according to Rav, for Rav did not say two skips suffice except when they are on the 15th, 16th, 17th — then the recognizable days of the month cause them to combine with the 15th sighting for a count of three — end quote. It appears from his words that even where she has a fixed veset on the 15th alone and afterward saw on the 16th and 17th, Rav holds she established a veset for skips since the days of the month are recognizable. Investigate: where she has a veset from the 20th to the 20th and changed with skips — Rav holds she did not establish a veset for skips since her veset was not on recognizable days of the month — but this is not so, for explicitly in the Gemara (64a) regarding Rav says to the accustomed woman I am different (Rashi: since her veset is fixed for the 15th we judge the 16th sighting for the veset after it [before it]); and Rashi explained there explicitly — see there and above s.k. 18.
