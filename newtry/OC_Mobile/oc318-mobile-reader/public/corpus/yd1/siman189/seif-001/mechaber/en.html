The laws of a woman who has a fixed veset and who does not have a fixed veset; it contains 34 se’ifim. Any woman who does not have a fixed veset is concerned for the 30th day from her seeing, which is an onah beinonis for ordinary women. And if she has a fixed veset for a known time, from 20 to 20 or from 25 to 25, she is concerned for the known time.
