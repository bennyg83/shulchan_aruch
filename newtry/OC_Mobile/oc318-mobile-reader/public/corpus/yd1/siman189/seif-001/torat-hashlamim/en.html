She must be concerned for the thirtieth day. This means the corresponding day of the new month, as stated below in seif katan 30. She must also be concerned for the interval veset until one of them becomes established according to its law, as explained below in seif 2 and seif 13 in the gloss. These are the words of Shach. The concern for an interval veset applies only when she saw twice, for otherwise the concept of an interval veset does not yet apply. Shach himself writes this several times in this siman.
She must be concerned for the known time. She is forbidden to engage in marital relations then, as explained above in siman 184.
