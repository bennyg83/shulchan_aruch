She is concerned for the thirtieth day etc. That is, for that same day of the month, as written below s.k. 30; and similarly she is concerned for the interval veset until she establishes one of them as required, as below seif 2 and seif 13 in the note.
She is concerned for the known time. And she is forbidden to have relations for the entire onah of the veset, in the manner explained in siman 184.
