Seif 1. See what Gaon Imrei Moshe s.k. 1 challenged — behold there are three sightings and two equal skips in a week; if so why not say veset for skipping in week; and see what he wrote; and in that seif itself Gaon was doubtful in that reasoning; and per my weak view one can resolve: it deals she was accustomed to see previously on that day of week of fixed veset and changed to see on another day of week but did not fix veset; and afterward began seeing by interval as stated; if so even Rav agrees three skips in week are needed for accustomed is different and in between she already clearly fixed interval veset — requires study.
