(Siman 189, Shach, seif katan 19) That she established it twice. This wording is puzzling, because the two intervals are not equal, since Iyar is deficient and Nisan is full. The Bach explains the Tur's statement that according to Rava the alternating veset is established and she must be concerned for the eighteenth of Tammuz. The purpose of mentioning the eighteenth is to teach that she is concerned only for that date and not for the seventeenth, even though the interval from the seventeenth of Iyar to the seventeenth of Sivan equals the interval from the seventeenth of Sivan to the seventeenth of Tammuz. This means that we disregard the second interval, which was thirty-one days. According to Shmuel, however, the veset has not yet been established, and she must be concerned for the seventeenth of Tammuz because of the thirty-one-day interval. Since we follow the stringencies of both Rav and Shmuel, she must be concerned for both the seventeenth and the eighteenth of Tammuz. Yet even apart from this, according to Shmuel she must be concerned for the seventeenth of Tammuz because she saw on the seventeenth of Sivan and must observe the corresponding calendar date once.
