Just as she is concerned for a veset of days with one time, so she is concerned for a veset of body with one time. How so? She yawned one time and saw — when she yawns another time, she is concerned for it. And just as a veset of days that is not fixed is uprooted with one time — for even if she saw two times on a known day, if the third time arrived and she did not see, it is uprooted completely — so it is a bodily veset. And just as a veset of days fixed 3 times requires uprooting 3 times and checking, so it is a veset fixed in the body. And from when is its uprooting? From when an occurrence happens and she does not see. If the occurrence was at a known time, it is not uprooted unless the occurrence came 3 times at its time and she did not see; but with the occurrence alone, or the time alone, that she did not see them, it is not uprooted.
