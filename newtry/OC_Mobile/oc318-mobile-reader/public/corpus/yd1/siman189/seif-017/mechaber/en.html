Any veset that was established because of an oness (for example, she jumped and saw), even if she saw in it several times (if she did not establish them for days) (Tosafos and Rashba and Rabbeinu Chananel, and it is obvious below in se’if 18), is not a veset, for because of the oness she saw. (Nevertheless, she is concerned for it like a veset that is not fixed) (Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 8). If she jumped and saw, jumped and saw, she established for herself a veset for days without jumping. How so? She jumped on Sunday and saw blood, and after 20 days jumped on Sunday and saw blood, and after 19 days jumped on Shabbos and did not see blood, and after Shabbos she saw without jumping — behold, Sunday after 20 has been established, for it became known that the day caused it and not the jumping, and this day has already been established 3 times; and similarly any such case.
