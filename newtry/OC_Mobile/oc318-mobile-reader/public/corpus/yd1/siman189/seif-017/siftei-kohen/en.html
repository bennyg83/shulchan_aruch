Even if she saw with it many times. On various days — since she did not establish it for specific known days such as Rosh Chodesh or the first day of the week as below seif 18 — it is not a veset.
And yet she is concerned for it. For it occurred so three times — so it is in Hagahot Maimoniot and Beit Yosef brings it; and Darkei Moshe; and it seems to me he came only to exclude that she need not be concerned every time she jumps when it has not occurred three times; but for the monthly veset and the interval she must be concerned even one time — such as she jumped and saw, and afterward at the end of twenty days returned and jumped and saw — she must be concerned for the interval veset; and if afterward she jumps at the end of twenty she must be concerned for it the whole onah, for is it not that she saw from twenty to twenty without a jump? She must be concerned for twenty, and because she jumped at first should it be diminished? For now too she jumped on twenty. And furthermore since by jumping on a specific day three times she establishes a veset as below seif 18 — if so with one instance she is also concerned; and proof for this from above siman 187 seif 11 regarding one who saw on account of relations from half a year to half a year — she is forbidden to have relations when half a year arrives from the last blood sighting; and likewise if she jumped on the 15th of Nisan and jumped on the 15th of Iyar she must be concerned for that day for it is a monthly veset with a jump, since the monthly veset equals the interval veset in all respects; but if she does not subsequently jump for that veset she need not be concerned, for what she saw initially was through a jump; and though regarding yawning below we say she is concerned for the monthly veset even without yawning — yawning is different, since initially she saw without duress. So it seems to me, investigate.
