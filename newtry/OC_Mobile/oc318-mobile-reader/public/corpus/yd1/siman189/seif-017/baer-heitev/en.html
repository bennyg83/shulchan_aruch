For days. Known such as Rosh Chodesh or day one of the week as below seif 18. Shach.
The external cause. Taz explains that jumping is an imposed external cause, whereas yawning is a natural bodily symptom associated with the onset of bleeding. Therefore the rules differ without contradiction.
She is concerned. Since it occurred three times; so Hagahot Maimoniyot; and it seems to me it comes only to exclude that she need not be concerned every time she jumps when it did not occur three times; but for new veset and for interval she must be concerned even once; e.g. she jumped and saw and after twenty days returned and jumped and saw; she must be concerned for interval veset; and if afterward she jumps at end of twenty she must be concerned all that onah that she not see from 20th to 20th without jumping {Rama: behold now also she jumped}; and further since by jumping to a known day three times she fixed veset as below seif 18; if so even once she is concerned; and proof from siman 187 seif 11 regarding seeing due to relations; see there; and even though regarding yawning below we say she is concerned for new veset even without yawning; there is different since initially she saw not by coercion; end quote Shach.
Fixed. {Rama: even without a jump} But Rashba ruled we attribute one sighting on Shabbat to yesterday's jump and she fixed veset for day one and for jumping but not for days alone {Rama: and so Rabbi Maggid in name of Ramban since vesetot rabbinic we are lenient}. Wonder on Mechaber omitted this; should rule leniently vesetot rabbinic; nevertheless leniency becoming stringency per Mechaber view fixed veset for days alone if afterward did not see three times on day one uprooted veset; per Rashba did not fix veset only days and jumps veset not uprooted thereby since did not jump; not seeing because did not jump; but if she jumps that day concerned as below seif 26; end quote Shach.
