Like unfixed veset i.e. if it occurred three times — so Shach s.k. 48 — see there; appears Raavad's view above seif 7 seif 3 if she fixed veset for hours i.e. by immersion and incident — derives from woman who saw ascending from ritual immersion; therefore ruled uprooted in one hour even without examination; but begins concerned for her hour — from this reason even though does not fix for jumps — nevertheless since presumed concerned initially like unfixed veset; per this also here if jumps though not in equal days — nevertheless were in equal hours — initially need not be concerned except that hour i.e. if jumped that hour and similar; but not before or after; Mechaber abbreviated and relied above seif 3; appears Ran in his Niddah novellae disputes — how permitted this woman to husband on riverbank — always sees ascending from ritual immersion; resolve similar to jumping veset — not fixed; difficulty behold begins concerned like unfixed since accustomed to see; Maggid Mishneh resolved Raavad there was specific hour and he commanded relations before that hour; if so disputed matter; Shach s.k. 48 if composite veset in days and jumps — concerned when veset arrives and jumped even before fixed three times — need not extend for clear in Baalei Nefesh — plain and clear if did not jump that day need not be concerned and we do not say perhaps she fixes veset for days alone like below other body veset; in Perishah s.k. 159 appears also here concerned — possible wrote per Tur's view fixes veset for jumps alone — if so jumps law equals other body veset; but for us distinction in fixation — also here can divide.
