Every veset, etc. This follows Tosafot's explanation there, s.v. Rather, etc. See also 63b, s.v. She ate, etc., “One may say,” etc. The Rashba likewise rules this way in Torat HaBayit; see there, 173a. This is also the view of Rabbeinu Chananel and the other poskim. The Baal HaMaor and the Tur, however, follow Rashi.
She jumped and saw, etc. This follows the latter version, which he understands as disputing the first version. All the poskim, however, write that it does not dispute the first version, for the latter version concerns a case in which she did not jump at all on Shabbos. The words found in our text of the latter version, “and on Shabbos she jumped but did not see,” are a scribal error copied from the wording of the Rambam. They are not found in the other textual versions. Rather, the Rambam explains that the latter version also concerns a case like that of the first version. The Rosh, Rashba, and the other poskim write that these two versions do not disagree. This is also the unqualified ruling below in seif 2.
