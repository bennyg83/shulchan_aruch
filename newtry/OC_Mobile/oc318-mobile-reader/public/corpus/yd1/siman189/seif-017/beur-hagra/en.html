Every veset, etc. Per Tosafot there s.v. rather, etc.; and see there 63b s.v. she ate, etc.; and one can say, etc.; and likewise Rashba decided in Tosafot HaRosh — see there 173a; and so R. Chananel and Shitah Mekubetzet; but Raavad and Tur like Rashi.
She jumped and saw, etc. In the latter version and he explains he disputes the first version; but all poskim wrote he does not dispute the first version — in the latter version it deals where she did not jump at all on Shabbat; and what is in our version in the latter version on Shabbat she jumped and I did not see — is a scribal error; and from Rambam's words it was copied; but it is not so, for in all their versions it is not present; but Rambam explains the latter version also like the first — that she jumped and saw; but Rosh and Rashba and Shitah Mekubetzet wrote they do not dispute these versions; and likewise anonymous below seif 2.
