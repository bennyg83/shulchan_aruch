Similarly, the law of uprooting a Rosh Chodesh veset is as follows: How so? She was accustomed to see on Rosh Chodesh, and Rosh Chodesh passed for her and she did not see — she is concerned for Rosh Chodesh until 3 Rosh Chodesh pass for her and she did not see. If 3 Rosh Chodesh passed for her and she did not see, she is not concerned for them. If she again saw on Rosh Chodesh, the veset returned to its place.
