That three Roshei Chodesh passed. See responsum Noda biYehudah second part chelek 14 siman 86 asked: if two Roshei Chodesh passed and she did not see and she became pregnant and pregnancy and nursing days passed and Rosh Chodesh arrived and she did not see — does this uprooting join with first two uprootings to count as three uprootings; he answered per Mechaber below seif 34 and per Ramban brought in Shach there — plain since she must be concerned also for uprooting it counts uprooting and joins with first two; but per some great teachers Tur brought there you do not find this law at all; therefore in our case if after nursing she still did not see at all or saw once not at veset time and veset time arrived and she did not see — need not be concerned for first veset; necessarily per Mechaber and Ramban it counts three uprootings and per some great teachers otherwise need not be concerned as long as she did not see at that veset; if afterward she sees at that veset — for all at once returns to establishment since she still did not fix another veset — see there.
