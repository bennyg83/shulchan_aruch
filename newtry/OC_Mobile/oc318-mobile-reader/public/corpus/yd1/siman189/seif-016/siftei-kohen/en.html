And it passed, etc. — she is concerned for Rosh Chodesh, etc. Specifically here veset ha-chodesh is thus; but veset ha-hefsek — e.g. she was accustomed to see on the 32nd and afterward it passed and she did not see on the 32nd — she need not be concerned for the 32nd interval that she was fit to see, i.e. on the 64th day of her sighting — so Maharshal and Bach s.k. 24 — see there.
They passed over her, etc., she returned, etc. And the same applies if she changed her sightings three times to unequal sightings and then returned and saw on Rosh Chodesh — the veset returned to its place, and three times are required to uproot it — Beit Yosef and Acharonim; and it is obvious.
