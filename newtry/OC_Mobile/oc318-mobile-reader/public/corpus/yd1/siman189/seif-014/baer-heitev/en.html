Rosh wrote: even though three times she did not see on day 20 {Rama: meaning twice she saw on day 30 and the third time saw after 30 and again returned to see on day 20 — so explained in Nekudot HaKesef}, day 20 is not uprooted since she did not see day 30 — for if a woman skips one veset two or three times and does not see in between at another time and returns to see at veset time, the veset is not uprooted. Nevertheless he wrote Tur holds not specifically where she did not see on the thirtieth the third time but even if it has not yet arrived — nevertheless whenever she returns and sees on 20th she returns to first establishment and is not concerned for 30th even though not yet uprooted by not seeing on it; nevertheless this seeing on 20th which is first establishment day counts for uprooting two sightings of 30th — this requires study for it does not appear so in the Talmud — end quote Shach. Taz wrote to resolve: there is a distinction between days of the month — the Talmud deals with that — but Mechaber deals with interval of days from day 30 to day 20; therefore he wrote after she changed once or twice — see there at length.
