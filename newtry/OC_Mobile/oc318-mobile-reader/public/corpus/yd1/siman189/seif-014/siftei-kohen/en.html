And if after she changed, etc. — the veset returned, etc. Even though three times she did not see on the twentieth day the twentieth was not uprooted since she did not see on the thirtieth — if a woman skips her veset once or twice or thrice and does not see in between for another time and returns to see at veset time the veset is not uprooted — Rosh; and so Gemara and Rashi; Derishah s.k. 68 and Bach in last pamphlet on Tur (which are the Mechaber's words); Maggid Mishnah 296b: Tur holds not specifically where she did not see on the thirtieth the third time but even if it has not yet arrived — whenever she returned and saw on the twentieth she returned to the first establishment and is not concerned for the thirtieth even though not yet uprooted by not seeing — end quote (and so Perishah s.k. 68); this explanation requires study — for if so even if the first establishment was not on the twentieth she uprooted the thirtieth veset since she afterward saw on the twentieth she uprooted her interval and changed to twenty and need only be concerned for twenty-day interval, as above s.k. 31; in baraita, Gemara, and Tosafot we do not say she must be concerned for the thirtieth except where she did not change her interval to twenty; and although Rambam ch. 8 Avot HaTumah 8 wrote similarly — accustomed to see on the twentieth and changed to twenty-second, both forbidden; twentieth arrived and she saw — twenty-second is permitted for she returned to her fixed veset and twenty-second uprooted since not established three times — end quote; and likewise there deals with twentieth and twenty-second of month as there explicitly; therefore if she did not have fixed veset for twentieth of month, twenty-second of month would not be uprooted by seeing on the twentieth, as above s.k. 31; but Taz deals with intervals as they wrote when the twentieth day arrives for sighting of thirtieth, etc.; and proven from what they wrote afterward (seif 16) as uprooting of interval veset so uprooting of month-veset — plainly until now veset of intervals; if so even if she did not return to first establishment only changed her interval again she need not be concerned for thirty-day interval. It appears to me.
