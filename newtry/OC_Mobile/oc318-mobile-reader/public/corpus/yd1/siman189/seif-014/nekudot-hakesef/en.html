(Taz, seif katan 28) At first glance, this contradicts the Gemara, etc. He concludes that the Ramban had a different textual reading of the Gemara from that of the Rosh. This is difficult, because the Tur cites the Ramban’s ruling without qualification. Rather, the primary explanation is as written in the Shach, seif katan 40. When the Tur and Ramban write that after her interval changed once or twice to day 30 she then saw on day 20, they mean that she saw on day 20 after a third sighting that occurred after day 30. Although she had not seen three times on day 20, the day 20 cycle was not uprooted, because she saw only twice on day 30, while the third sighting was after day 30. Therefore, when she again saw on day 20 following that later sighting, her day 20 cycle returned to its place. The Perishah explains likewise; see there.
