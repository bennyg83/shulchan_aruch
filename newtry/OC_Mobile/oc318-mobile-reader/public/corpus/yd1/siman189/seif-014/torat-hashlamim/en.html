She was accustomed to see day twenty. Tur and poskim wrote per Talmud law woman does not fix veset in niddah days nor zivah days; they extended these laws; Mechaber and Rabbi omitted all this — because Tur and poskim in name of Ramban wrote since nowadays daughters of Israel were stringent not to separate niddah and zivah days — likewise for veset fixation no distinction — all plain and clear; in Nekudot HaKesef Hagahah challenged Beit Yosef and Rabbi wrote Ramban said only for stringency she fixes veset even in niddah and zivah days but not to be lenient — certainly would not dispute Talmud; already explained in several places laws of stringent unfixed from fixed; also one without fixed veset must be concerned for month veset and interval unlike fixed; and so Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh and Maggid Mishneh in name of Ramban said only to be stringent; if so Mechaber and Rabbi should have brought these laws — end quote; per my view Beit Yosef well intended — need not be so stringent in rabbinic vesetot nowadays; know main reason Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh wrote if you divide between niddah and zivah days they would need to learn gates of niddah and zivah days — appears explicitly in no case divide — clear; see s.k. 52.
She saw at end of 20th. Meaning this woman accustomed to interval veset from 20th to 20th — afterward changed and did not see until interval of 30th; and likewise second time did not see until 30th; and when 30th arrived did not see — only after 30th saw once; and afterward saw distant from that sight 20th — returned to first establishment even though three times did not see on day 20th — day 20th not uprooted since day 30th not fixed — if woman skips one veset two or three times and not fixed in between for another time and returns to see at veset time — first veset not uprooted until she fixes another veset (so Gemara and Rashi and Rosh intent; and Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh; and Shach in Nekudot HaKesef; Taz's wonders on Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch removed) — and even though even if first establishment not on 20th — interval of 30th uprooted since she changed once in interval before fixing as Shach s.k. 40 (but month veset distinction as Shach there) — nevertheless comes to teach even though did not see three times on day 20th returns to first establishment; and in Nevertheless daf 426b wrote not specifically when did not see on thirtieth third time but when interval of 30th not yet arrived — immediately after second sight on day 30th saw on interval 20th returned to first establishment and not concerned for 30th even though not yet uprooted by not seeing — nevertheless this seeing on 20th which is first establishment day counts for uprooting two sightings of 30th — end quote; see Shach who left this explanation in difficulty.
