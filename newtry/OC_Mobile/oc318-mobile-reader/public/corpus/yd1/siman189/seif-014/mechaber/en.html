She was accustomed to see on day 20 (and she has for this a fixed veset), and she changed to day 30 — this and that are forbidden. And when day 20 arrives from the seeing of day 30, she is forbidden because of the first veset; and if she does not see on it, she is concerned for day 30. If she changed two times to day 30, this and that are forbidden. If she changed 3 times to day 30, day 20 becomes permitted and day 30 becomes forbidden. And if, after she changed one time or two times to day 30, she saw at the end of 20, the veset of 20 returned to its place and 30 became permitted.
