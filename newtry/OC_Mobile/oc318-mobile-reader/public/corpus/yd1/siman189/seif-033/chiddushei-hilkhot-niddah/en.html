So long as the miscarriage has the halakhic status of childbirth regarding blood of childbirth and blood of purity, she has the status of a nursing woman. This follows from Tosafot, Niddah 36, s.v. 've-shavu' and s.v. 'de-leika.' It is also evident from Ramban, who infers that the rule that a nursing woman is impure only from the time of her sighting was not stated because she is, by Torah law, a woman sitting during her days of blood of purity, whose impurity begins only from the time of sighting. He did not explain that the novelty concerns a woman who miscarried. It follows that anyone who has the law of blood of purity also has the law of a nursing woman. This is clear.
