The child died. Kreti U'Pleiti, Sidrei Taharah, and Chavot Daat extend nursing status even to miscarriage, and some even to a false pregnancy. During pregnancy and nursing, a pattern may be used leniently to define concern, though not necessarily stringently as a permanent fixed cycle.
Nevertheless she is concerned. Sidrei Taharah writes that a pregnant or nursing woman need not observe the ordinary thirty-day expectation from an isolated sighting, but she must observe one calendar or interval concern arising from it.
