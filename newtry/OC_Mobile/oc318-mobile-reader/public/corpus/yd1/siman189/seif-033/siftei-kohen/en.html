She does not establish, etc. Bach wondered why he ruled leniently — Tur brought Ramban to teach stringency as his words — end quote; this is no wonder, for we rule in Choshen Mishpat siman 25 seif 2 wherever Acharonim dispute Rishonim we rule like Acharonim; but if Rishonim's words are not well known we need not rule like Acharonim, for perhaps they did not know the Rishonim's words and if they had heard they would have retracted (see responsum of Maharam Alashkar siman 94); especially here Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen disputes Ramban explicitly, brought by Beit Yosef; Tur did not see Terumat HaDeshen as proven in many places, as Beit Yosef also wrote in this siman; further Raavad and Ritva agree with Rashba as in Beit Yosef — Ramban is alone against them; Ritva wrote such is the custom, brought by Beit Yosef; we say wherever halachah is loose in your hand follow the custom; further vesetot are rabbinic and we go leniently in many places.
