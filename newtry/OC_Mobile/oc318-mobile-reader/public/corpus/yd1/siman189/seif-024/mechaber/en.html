And all of them, one is concerned for them only for their hour. How so? If she was accustomed to see with the beginning of the veset, she is immediately forbidden for the entire duration of the continuation of the veset. If she was accustomed to see at its end, she is forbidden only at its end. In what case are these matters said? When all the seeing is contained within the veset; but if not all the seeing is contained within the veset, but it continues also after the veset, she is forbidden from the beginning of the veset until the end of one onah.
