An onah. Meaning a day or a night, as above siman 184 seif 2. Although there in seif 6 we say that if her veses extends two or three days, she need only separate for the first onah — one can say that in any case she is prohibited for one onah. It also appears that certainly if the sighting begins even before the veses, she must separate for that entire onah before the veses and for the entire veses period. It is possible that here she is prohibited for the entire onah even after the veses as well — this requires further investigation. Thus far the Shach.
