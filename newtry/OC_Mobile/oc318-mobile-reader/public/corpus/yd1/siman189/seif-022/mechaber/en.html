And likewise is the law for days: if she yawned today and yawned at the end of 30 — if she yawns even not on day 30, she is concerned for that yawning, lest she establish for yawning alone. If she yawned within 30 and did not see, she no longer needs to be concerned for yawning alone, but she needs to be concerned for the end of 30; and likewise she needs to be concerned for the fixed day in the month in which she yawned, lest she establish a veset for days.
