She transgressed. Taz wrote: even though in siman 186 he ruled that a woman without veset needs examination before relations, here it deals where she already has a veset only she changed it now to a new veset and did not establish it three times {Rama: and in Nekudot HaKesef he wrote this means even at the beginning of establishment; but one can say there it deals where she has no veset at all and each time is presumed seeing; but here she has a veset only it is not fixed}.
