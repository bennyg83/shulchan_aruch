(Taz s.k. 18) Wishes to prove that even though she saw on Rosh Chodesh Iyar she is concerned for 9th of it for interval of twenty — his words are not clarified; what he wrote that we do not find a sighting in changed time uproots initial concern — is not a matter, for here since she saw on Rosh Chodesh she changed her interval to twelve; since she did not fix interval veset it is uprooted at once. What he further wrote — I say likewise in such case she does not fix veset for there are not three equal intervals; since she saw on Rosh Chodesh she uprooted veset of 20th that was suspended; and Perishah agrees — see there, examine and forget. What he wrote further clear proof from Tur end of this seif regarding saw twice on Rosh Chodesh — not precise, for there it is month veset and Bach and Perishah agree, as seif 30 states; and his challenge on Bach is well intended, nevertheless his law is true and what he proved is opposite — not proof at all; for there she had fixed day on 20th — therefore if she would see on 20th we would not say she changed interval by seeing ten days later and would need concern ten days after day-ten sighting i.e. day 20th — unlike here where interval veset was not fixed. Since all his proofs are void — return to law: if she saw on Rosh Chodesh Iyar she is not concerned for 9th of it, as appears from Ramban's plain words, as he himself wrote that Rama's "if she did not see" is leniency — does not settle, etc.
