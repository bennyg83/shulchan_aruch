(Siman 189 in Taz s.k. 5) Here the discussion is of one who already has a veset, etc. This is incorrect, for it implies that the discussion is even at the beginning of establishing a veset; rather one must say that there the discussion is of one who has no veset at all and is always presumed to be a woman who sees; but here she does have a veset, only it is not fixed.
