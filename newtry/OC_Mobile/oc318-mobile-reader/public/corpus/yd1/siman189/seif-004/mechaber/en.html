There is another difference between one that she established 3 times and one that she did not establish 3 times: one that is established — even though its onah passed and she did not feel — is forbidden to have tashmish until she checks and finds [herself] tehorah. And one that she did not establish 3 times: if the time of the veset arrived and she did not check and she did not see, since its onah passed she is permitted. And an onah beinonis, which is at 30 days, has the law of a fixed veset (Rashba).
