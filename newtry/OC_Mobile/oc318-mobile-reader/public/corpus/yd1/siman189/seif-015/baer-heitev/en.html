The veset. Taz wrote: I have difficulty; since here it deals with interval of days as stated above, how find here that she returned to see on veset day? But her veset is interval of total days; one sighting joins its fellow; and here she did not see a long time and you have no sighting to count interval from it; etc.; his conclusion: truly what Shulchan Aruch wrote on veset day is imprecise; end quote. In Nekudot HaKesef he explained meaning she should see one sighting and afterward a second on day 20; then first veset returns to its place for everything and need not be concerned for new veset and has other laws of fixed veset; all this clear; end quote.
