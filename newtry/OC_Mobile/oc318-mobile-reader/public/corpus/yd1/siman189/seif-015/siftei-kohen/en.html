Uprooted veset, etc. Question: since she saw twice in a skip of two days she should be concerned for the 36th day? Answer: since she did not see in skip except twice she need not be concerned at all, for it is already explained that veset ha-dilug as long as not established three times she is not concerned — Beit Yosef; Perishah s.k. 51; this is for Shmuel; but per what we say above seif 6 like Rav — likewise here she must be concerned for veset ha-dilug since she skipped twice, as s.k. 8; Bach challenged Beit Yosef — Beit Yosef wrote she saw twice in skip, etc. — but not so, for there are only three sightings on 30, 32, 34 and only one skip as Ramban wrote: interval veset in skip is established only with five sightings — even though she had veset to see on the twentieth and skipped to thirtieth, the twentieth sighting does not count since not equal to skips from 30 to 32 to 34, for she skipped from twentieth to thirtieth and you need five sightings on 30, 32, 34, 36, 38 — then veset is established in three equal skips; if she only saw on 30, 32, 34 there is only one skip — end quote; his words are puzzling, for when she saw on 30, 32, 34, 36 there are five sightings — 20, 30, 32, 34, 36 — and she established veset even for Ramban who rules like Shmuel; and for us who are stringent like Rav she established when she saw on 30, 32, 34, as explained — even though twentieth sighting does not count since not equal to her skip, nevertheless since she intervalled from twentieth to thirtieth, whenever she returned and saw on 32 she skipped two days — if so the first skip is on 32; and this is clear; and in any case proven explicitly from Derishah, Beit Yosef, and Bach above that the thirtieth sighting is not considered a skip at all even though she skipped from veset she was accustomed to see on the twentieth and skipped and saw on thirtieth — since the two-day skip is not equal to 32 and 34 there is no skip on it; hence contradiction to Perishah above seif 8 small — see there, investigate.
And she has no veset at all, etc. However she is concerned for the last interval — that is day 34 — as above s.k. 36 and several other places; and so Bach. Furthermore, Beit Yosef and other Acharonim wrote she is concerned for the onah beinonis; and I already wrote in s.k. 30 that this means the days of the month — such as if her 34th day falls on the 15th of this month, she is then concerned for the 15th of the next month; and she is also concerned for the 34th day; and now it is clear why Taz and the poskim did not write that she still must be concerned for the 34th day for the interval and for the onah beinonis — because it is included in what I wrote: that every woman who saw must be concerned for the monthly veset and the interval until she establishes a veset, etc.
And the same applies to one who ceased seeing three onot. Provided she did not change in between with three other sightings; and if she then returned to see on the day of the first veset — she returned to its first establishment, and three times are required to uproot it.
