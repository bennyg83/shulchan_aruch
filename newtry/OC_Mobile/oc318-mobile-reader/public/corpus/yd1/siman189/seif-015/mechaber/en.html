She changed her seeings and did not make them equal — for example, she changed one time to day 30, and the second to day 32, and the third to day 34 — the first veset is uprooted and she has no veset at all. And if she again saw on the day of the first veset, the first veset returns to its first establishment, and she is concerned for it always until it is uprooted from her 3 times. And the same applies to one who stopped seeing for 3 onos and afterward again saw on the day of the first veset.
