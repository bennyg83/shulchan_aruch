She changed, etc. There, 64a, the Gemara challenges: “She saw,” etc.; “she alternated,” etc., and establishes the case as one in which she was accustomed, etc. It is therefore evident that this applies even though she already had a veset.
And if she returned, etc. As explained above in seif 14, and as stated there, “for her regular course,” etc. Meaning, so long as she did not establish another veset, one occurrence restores her original veset.
