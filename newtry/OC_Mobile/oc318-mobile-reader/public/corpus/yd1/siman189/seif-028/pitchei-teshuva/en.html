And likewise fifth and sixth. See responsum Nodah biYehudah Yoreh Deah siman 61 doubted if intent is specifically "and likewise fifth and sixth" i.e. then month also fixed twice; but immediately on fourth when she saw on 20th need not be concerned for 20th at all since she has fixed Rosh Chodesh veset — need not be concerned for unfixed at all as above seif 13 Hagahah if she fixes one veset she is not concerned for second; or perhaps specifically when at time unfixed veset occurred she changed and did not see in fixed veset and even though not yet uprooted at once — since at least she did not see on it must be concerned for this from month even though unfixed — if afterward fixed veset returns to place again need not be concerned for unfixed even though not yet uprooted because initially it came only when fixed did not come in its time — if so now fixed returned one can say this month will not come again; but if month came when fixed not uprooted as here saw twice on Rosh Chodesh and also on 20th of month — one can say what she again saw on fixed Rosh Chodesh does not help her for from beginning Rosh Chodesh veset did not cease and nevertheless she saw on 20th of month — if so here must immediately be concerned except Shulchan Aruch deals she fixes i.e. both truly fixed — therefore wrote "and likewise fifth and sixth" and left in doubt — see there; and see below s.k. 11 in name of Chiddushei Dinim and below siman 187 s.k. 1.
