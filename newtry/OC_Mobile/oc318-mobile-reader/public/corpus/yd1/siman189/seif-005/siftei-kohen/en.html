Such as she saw today, etc., she established her veset, etc. It implies that with four sightings she established a veset for skipping — specifically per the view of those who are stringent in seif 7 below; and so Perishah seif 37; and so appears in Tosafot and Rosh end of chapter HaIshah regarding what they wrote that Rav holds the first sighting does not count except where the first sighting was on the 15th of the month, since her sightings correspond to the count of days of the month; but if the first sighting was on Rosh Chodesh and the second on the 15th of that same sighting, and the third on the 16th of that same sighting — Rav concedes she did not establish a veset, for the first sighting does not count — end of Tosafot; and Beit Yosef brings this on 192c end s.v. how she establishes by days of the month with a skip, etc. It implies that if she saw four sightings even if her sightings do not correspond to the count of days of the month — she established a veset for skipping per Rav; and accordingly for those poskim in seif 7 who follow Shmuel, here she did not establish a veset for skips until five sightings — as Ramban wrote, brought by Tur: such as she saw once and afterward saw a second time on the 29th of the first sighting, and afterward a third time on the 30th of the second sighting, and then the 31st, and then the 32nd — at the third sighting she began to skip and the name skip applied to her; and per what I write below seif 8 and 19 that for law one should be stringent like both views — the same applies here.
And she saw a second time at the end of thirty etc. And the same applies if she skipped retrogressively — such as she saw a second time at the end of twenty-nine and third at the end of twenty-eight; in any case where her skip is equal she established a veset for skipping; and this is obvious; and so implies in Tosafot first chapter of Niddah 9b s.v. pachatah, etc.
She established a veset for skipping etc. And she is concerned afterward for day 33 and afterward for day 34, and so forever.
Whether the skip is large. Such as she skipped from 29 to 31 and then to 33 and then to 35 — she is again concerned for 37 and then for 39, and so forever; and so Rashba in Mishmeret HaBayit 169b, who wrote that so wrote the Rishonim, and unlike Ra'ah in Beit HaElohim there.
That in any case she is equal etc. To exclude where she skipped one day and afterward two days, or vice versa.
