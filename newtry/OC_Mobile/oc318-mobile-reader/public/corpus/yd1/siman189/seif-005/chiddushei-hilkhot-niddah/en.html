For example she saw, etc. See Shach s.k. 68: he explained this is specifically for Rav but not Shmuel — for interval veset Rav too concedes four sightings, and Shmuel counts five. In truth Rosh and Tosafot are explicit thus; only Rosh's words need explanation — he wrote Rav does not say the first sighting counts only where first sighting was on the 15th of the month since sighting time counts for month days; but if first on Rosh Chodesh, second on fifteenth for this sighting, third on sixteenth for this — Rav concedes she did not establish veset for sighting one does not count; and so proven in chapter Shor HaNogach 4 and 5 from goring on 15th, 16th, 17th of this month per the dispute — end quote. These words appear incomprehensible: what proof from goring ox more than here regarding veset — are not the Gemara's words here the same as there without difference? Rather Tosafot challenged from "he saw ox gore ox and did not gore" implying even with interval three suffice — see there; room to say per Shach below s.k. 30 that onah beinonis women's way to see is veset ha-chodesh without distinction — if so one may say certainly she herself is a sighting, and even for interval veset Rav holds two sightings suffice in total and Shmuel four — why phrase "she saw on 15th of this month" which is veset ha-chodesh and not "she saw on Rosh Chodesh and 15th for this and 16th for this" which has more disputes and is the mishnah case — accustomed to see on the 15th, etc., dealing with interval veset not month? Must say perforce interval needs more sightings — four for Rav and five for Shmuel; the Gemara abbreviates language and therefore used month-veset. Rosh's law is compelled; one may say therefore month-veset since it is the common onah among women and common matters are stated since women's way is thus — therefore Rosh brings proof from goring ox stated likewise; in oxen it does not apply — strained to say ox specifically teaches this; rather the dispute was not stated regarding ox but Gemara compared ox to veset and Talmud's way is thus — therefore another explanation needed: Tosafot challenged how we require four sightings for intervals when "ox gore ox and did not gore" implies mued with three — they resolve per R. Yitzchak: regarding woman we do not want to establish her as seeing — she is otherwise presumed seeing — all is to know limited time of sighting day; with one sighting you do not know limited time until second sighting and time is fixed with such-and-such interval she sees; unlike ox goring — main matter is establishing presumption of goring; first goring counts as goring and with three gorings established for three; if gored three consecutive days established mued for all days — only since gored sporadically mued for sporadic days; sporadic skipping is not what makes mued — rather sporadic diminishes so not mued except days he skipped; unlike woman — if she saw consecutive day after day there is no veset; all veset we judge is through intervals and you too require three intervals — plain and clear. Accordingly Rosh's words are correct: he needed proof this is not interval veset — if we say month-day does not cause sight at all what matter if month full or deficient or day 15 or 16 until many days early before flow — if day 15 arrives she is presumed to see at her time — almost impossible per naturalists; they said only nature of woman to interrupt between sighting and sighting limited time which is interval veset; therefore "she saw 15th of this month and 17th of that" deals by way of parable in equal months full and deficient or month like 12 — behold interval from 15 to 16 and 16 to 17 also skip one extra day; and he says first sighting counts alone without interval for whatever name — month does not cause blood and no time for "her way at its time"; if so proven interval veset is like Rav in such case and Shmuel requires four sightings. But if month-veset causes sight and tradition was with Chazal that shofar or moon's course causes sight — no difference early or late, when that time arrives she sees — this is veset ha-chodesh Chazal said; plainly when Rav and Shmuel disputed it is month-veset where first sighting counts immediately setting boundary and veset time; unlike interval where first sighting counts nothing until another; Rosh was compelled: month-veset specifically because month-day causes veset not depending on interval; therefore Rav suffices with three — "she she she" meaning month-veset meaning interval for month-day does not cause sight; if so proven also for interval three suffice as Ra'ah in Beit HaElohim — he elaborated there; one may say until here Shmuel's view until she triples in skip — month-veset which per this is one law with interval — Shmuel holds only one skip from 15 to 16 is one interval and 16 to 17 one skip; Shmuel requires at least two skips for presumption in skips per R. Yehudah; even R. Shimon ben Gamaliel concedes since there are sightings establishing like they said to Rav — see Ra'ah there. In ox goring on 15th of month it has a name as regarding goring — immediately has name like veset ha-shavua or month for all; if gored afterward on 16th behold sporadic and on 17th second sporadic — two sporadic skips; even Shmuel concedes established for gorings in sporadic months day by day — how does Gemara compare sporadic ox goring in months to Rav and Shmuel on veset — learn even here law is month-day causes veset and sight needs no interval — even if flows many days early or ceased long among women when month time arrives liable to see — that is time women's way comes; equal law to goring ox — goring too has limited name; this law is proven — we say specifically month-veset since stated in months; but for interval need additional sightings for Rav and Shmuel — investigate.
