Bach's language is thus on what Tur wrote for Rava skipping veset is fixed and concerned for 18th of Tammuz — he needed to say concerned for 18th Tammuz to say concerned only for 18th Tammuz but not 17th Tammuz even though second interval from 17th Iyar to 17th Tammuz equals interval from 17th Sivan to 17th Tammuz — end quote; i.e. we are not concerned for second interval which is 31 days; if so for Shmuel who did not yet fix — concerned for 17th Tammuz for interval of 31; and for us we rule like stringencies of Rav and Shmuel and must be concerned for 17th and 18th Tammuz. However in any case for Shmuel concerned for 17th Tammuz since she saw 17th Sivan — for law to be concerned once for day of month i.e. 17th Tammuz.
