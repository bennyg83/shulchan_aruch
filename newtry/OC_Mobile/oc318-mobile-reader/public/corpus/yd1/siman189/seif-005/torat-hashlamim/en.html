Sometimes the interval will be. Beit Yosef writes that this is evident from Rashba. Raavad likewise states it explicitly in Baalei HaNefesh, folio 25b, and writes that the same applies if she decreased in reverse order, such as to twenty-nine days and afterward to twenty-eight. Shach writes similarly in the name of Tosafot. Shach further writes that if she first saw after twenty-nine days, then after thirty-one, thirty-three, and thirty-six, she must next be concerned for thirty-nine, and so on. This is the Mechaber's intent in writing, whether she increased the skips, and so forth.
She established a veset of progressive dates. She must then be concerned for the thirty-third day, afterward the thirty-fourth, and so on. These are the words of Shach. She may likewise establish a repeating progressive pattern in this manner, as explained shortly in seif 8. This entire seif follows the second opinion in seif 7. According to the first opinion, five sightings are required here, as Shach and Taz write.
