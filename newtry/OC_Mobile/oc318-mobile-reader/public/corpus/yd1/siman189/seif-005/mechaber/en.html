Sometimes the interval by which she establishes the veset is by skipping; for example, she saw today, and a second time at the end of 30, and a third on the 31st, and a fourth on the 32nd — she established a veset of skipping for intervals. Whether she increased her skip greatly, or whether she increased only one day, she established a veset of an equal skip; for in any case that she makes her seeing equal, she establishes for herself a veset.
