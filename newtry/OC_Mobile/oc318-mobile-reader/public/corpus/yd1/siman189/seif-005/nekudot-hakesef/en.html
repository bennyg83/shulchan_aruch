(Taz s.k. 24) And I was astonished at their words, etc. There is no difficulty. The case is that if she sees a fourth time afterward, she will establish an advancing veset. Since until then it had not yet been established, they did not call it fixed, unlike a regular veset that had already been established. His other proofs that Ramban was concerned to rule stringently in accordance with Rav are likewise not compelling. This is simple.
