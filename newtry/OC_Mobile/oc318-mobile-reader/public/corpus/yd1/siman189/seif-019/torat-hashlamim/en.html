Many times in succession. Meaning, for an hour or two during the time when she normally sees. Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh and Tosafot.
She need not be concerned. She need not be concerned at all, even as for a non-established veset. The rule in seif 21 that she must be concerned for a non-established veset applies where the two factors have not yet become jointly established. So write Derishah, Shach, and Taz, and this is also implied by Rama's gloss, unlike Beit Yosef, whose words are difficult.
