There is [a woman] who establishes a veset through occurrences that happen in her body; for example, that she yawns — meaning, like a person who stretches out his arms because of heaviness, or like a person who opens his mouth because of heaviness, or like a person who produces a sound through the throat; and likewise if she sneezes downward, or feels in the mouth of her belly and in the lower part of her intestines, or if pangs of fever seized her, or her head and her limbs are heavy upon her — in each of these, if it happened to her 3 times and she saw, she established for herself a veset, such that each time she feels them she is forbidden to have tashmish. Nevertheless, with yawning or sneezing of one time, the veset is not established unless she does so many times one after another; and if it happened to her 3 times that each time she did so many times, this is a veset. And all these vesatos of her body have no known time; rather, each time this occurrence happens to her, that is the veset. And if a bodily veset comes at a known time, such as from Rosh Chodesh to Rosh Chodesh or from 20 days to 20 days, she established for herself a veset for time and for the concern of the veset, and she is concerned only for both together: if the time arrived and the feeling did not come, or the feeling came not at its time, she is not concerned. {Rama: This is specifically when she established for herself a veset for both together; but at first, she is concerned for each one on its own, because she does not know which of them she will establish, as was explained above regarding a skipping-veset and days, or interval and days, and so will be explained below.}
