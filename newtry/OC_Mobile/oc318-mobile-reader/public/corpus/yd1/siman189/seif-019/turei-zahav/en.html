A sound through the throat. This is caused by the food she ate.
At the opening of her abdomen. This refers to the area opposite her navel, and to the lower part of her intestines near the womb.
That each time, etc., she is forbidden to have relations. One should not ask that even a non-established veses forbids relations, as stated in seif 26. Here the text means only that this bodily event causes the prohibition of relations. From this we automatically know the distinction between an established and a non-established veses: the former requires three failures to uproot, while the latter is uprooted by one.
If it occurs outside its time, she need not be concerned. The Beit Yosef explained that she need not be concerned as for an established veses, but she must nevertheless be concerned as for a non-established veses, as stated in seif 26. The gloss that follows, however, states that this applies specifically where she established a veses for both together, etc. This implies that it disagrees with the Beit Yosef and holds that here she need not be concerned at all. It has become clear to us that the yawning creates no concern apart from the designated days, since she never once saw after yawning on another day, while that day was established specifically together with yawning. Before the establishment he was stringent only to give it the status of a non-established veses.
