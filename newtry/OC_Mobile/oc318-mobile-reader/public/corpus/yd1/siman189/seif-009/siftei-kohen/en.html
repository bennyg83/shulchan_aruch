She established for herself a veset, etc. Here too Derishah explained seif 15: this is for Rav or Shmuel where she had a veset beforehand and changed; meaning: here since her sightings are always equal on Rosh Chodesh, Shmuel too concedes, as above seif 6 regarding she saw from Rosh Chodesh to Rosh Chodesh; what difference whether from one Rosh Chodesh to two or from one to three? And so Bach: here even Shmuel concedes; and so Raavad in Ba'alei Nefesh explicitly, brought by Beit Yosef — this is veset ha-sirugin where three sightings suffice for all, as below seif 12; and this is clear.
