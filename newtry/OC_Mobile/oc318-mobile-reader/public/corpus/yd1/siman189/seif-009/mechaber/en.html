She saw on the 1st of Nisan, and on the 1st of Sivan, and on the 1st of Av — she established for herself a veset for Rosh Chodesh by skipping. But if she saw on the 1st of Nisan and on the 1st of Iyar and on the 1st of Tammuz, and on the 1st of Sivan she did not see, she did not establish for herself a veset.
