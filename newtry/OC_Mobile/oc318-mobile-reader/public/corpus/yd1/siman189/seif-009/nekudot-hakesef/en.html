(Taz s.k. 38) Difficulty, etc. No difficulty, for here certainly cannot say she is concerned except until she yawns; therefore he said forbidden to have relations until she examines i.e. immediately after yawning forbidden; but if long time passed after yawning and she did not feel — permitted since not fixed, as appears below seif 26. What he said ex post facto here lekhatchilah — has no understanding; what ex post facto and lekhatchilah applies here.
