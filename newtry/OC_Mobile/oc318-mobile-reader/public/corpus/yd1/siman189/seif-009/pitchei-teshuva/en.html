She fixes veset. See responsum Nodah biYehudah second part Yoreh Deah siman 83 in name of a great one who wrote: even though Shulchan Aruch wrote this law regarding saw three Roshei Chodesh — likewise interval veset which is not fixed veset even in three equal intervals if they are not equal in day or night onah; if so what is in Talmud and poskim that woman fixes equal day interval veset is also equal onah intervals; hence there is room to ask what is law of veset at beginning of interval veset before fixing three times — for month veset it is clear in Shulchan Aruch she is concerned for last one if second was at night concerned again at night; but interval veset — if she saw on day 1 and afterward at end of four weeks saw on night 1 — appears need not be concerned at end of eight weeks on night 1 even though equal in days for vesetot we look only at onot and second sight is distant from first fifty-six onot; if so at end of eight weeks concerned on Shabbat day; he disagreed and divides: for separation in that onah we do not judge fixed veset unless divided in onot — none is considered fixed; but for interval calculation we count distant day by its number whether first sight was day or night — we do not look at this and say when distant day arrives from first sight such is her way to see — certainly if her way was to interval something else we do not look whether that thing from which interval derives was day or night — see there; his words need explanation; appears intent to divide: specifically month veset we do not consider how distant from prior sight for month veset is not from interval reason only that day of month causes; if so what to judge whether night and day count as one is for separation here since divided in onot not fixed — cannot say that day of month causes since other sight was at night; but interval veset we count how distant from prior sight by day count — still his words need seasoning.
