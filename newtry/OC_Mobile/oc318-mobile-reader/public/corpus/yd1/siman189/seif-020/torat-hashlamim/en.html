The yawning of the previous day caused it. This differs from the end of seif 17, where we say that the day caused the sighting and not the jump of the previous day. Jumping is an external compulsion, as Derishah explains. Olat Tamid and Maadanei Melech distinguish similarly, and Mishmeret HaBayit explicitly makes this distinction. Shach, Taz, and Responsa Chut HaShani likewise discuss it. Chut HaShani initially rejects the distinction for lack of a source and later offers a strained answer based on Beit Yosef and Rambam. That cannot be correct, because according to Rambam she does not establish a veset through yawning at all, as Maggid Mishneh explains.
