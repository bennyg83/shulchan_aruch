She yawned 2 times on Rosh Chodesh and saw, and afterward she yawned not on Rosh Chodesh and saw — it became clarified that Rosh Chodesh does not cause, but rather the yawning. And likewise if on the third time she saw on Rosh Chodesh without yawning, it became clarified that the yawning does not cause, but rather Rosh Chodesh. But if she yawned 2 times on Rosh Chodesh, and the third time she yawned on the 29th of the month and did not see, and on Rosh Chodesh she saw without yawning, she established for herself a veset for yawning of Rosh Chodesh, for the yawning of yesterday caused the seeing of Rosh Chodesh.
