(Shach, seif katan 5) The yud of Yehudah, yoducha achecha. This is the text in Hagahos Maimoniyos. It explains that since 'Yehudah atah' begins an open section, the final line of the preceding column must necessarily begin with the words Yehudah atah, the entire line containing those two words, and the next column must begin with yoducha. See there. The Tur, however, writes that the yud of Yehudah should be at the beginning of the line. Minchas Shai states the same, and this is the scribal custom.
