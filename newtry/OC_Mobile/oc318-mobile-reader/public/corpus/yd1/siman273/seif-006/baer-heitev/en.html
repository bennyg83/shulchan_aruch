The Bet-Yod-Heh, Shin-Mem-Vav mnemonic. These mnemonic letters designate words that should begin columns. Authorities differ over the exact vav. Hagahot Mordekhai states that failure to place these at the head of a column can invalidate even after the fact. Taz sharply condemns stretching a final kaf until it resembles a dalet. The leg of a final kaf must be twice the length of its roof. To repair such a letter, it must be erased completely and rewritten, because merely scraping part of the roof would be engraving the interior.
