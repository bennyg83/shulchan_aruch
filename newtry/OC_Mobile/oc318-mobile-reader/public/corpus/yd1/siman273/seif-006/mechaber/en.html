If he forgot to write the entire Name, he hangs it between the lines; but part of the Name on the line and part hanging is invalid. And in other words, if he forgot—he writes part of the word on the line and part above. {Rama: Some scribes are meticulous to write yud-hei shin-mem-vav at the tops of the columns, and some meticulous ones write vav at the top of each column and call it vavei ha'amudim. And one need not be meticulous in all this; and even the more meticulous should not diminish or expand the writing at times because of this (Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 7), for all these matters have no root on which to rely (Beit Yosef).} (°)
