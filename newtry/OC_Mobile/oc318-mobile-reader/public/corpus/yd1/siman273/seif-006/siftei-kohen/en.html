One need not be exact in all this. Hagahot Mordechai in the Minor Laws records that Maharam wished to abolish the scribal custom of beginning every column of a Megillah or Torah scroll with vav, except for the mnemonic letters the mnemonic sequence Bet, Yod, Heh, Shin, Mem, Vav and the vav of “and I shall call heaven and earth to witness,” because forcing a vav to the top leads scribes to distort letter dimensions. Nevertheless, I found that if the words represented by the mnemonic sequence Bet, Yod, Heh, Shin, Mem, Vav do not begin a column the scroll is invalid even post facto, whereas the other initial vavs are only a preferred practice. Thus, regarding the mnemonic sequence Bet, Yod, Heh, Shin, Mem, Vav one must indeed be exact.
