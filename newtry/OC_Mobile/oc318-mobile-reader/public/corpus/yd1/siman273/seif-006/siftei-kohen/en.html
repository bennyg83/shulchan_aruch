BiYah ShMo. These are: the bet of Bereishit; the yud of "Yoducha achecha"; the heh that comes after them in Parashat Beshalach before the Song; the shin of "Shemor lecha," though some use the shin of "Shoftim veshotrim"; the mem of "Mah tovu"; and the vav of "Ve'a'idah bam." So appears in Hagahot Semak, siman 155, and Hagahot Maimoniyot. However, Mordechai cited below appears to indicate that the vav in BiYah ShMo is a different vav, besides the vav of "Ve'a'idah.
And one need not be particular in all this etc. And in Hagahos Mordechai in Hilchos Ketanos he wrote that the Ram wrote: that which the scribes are accustomed to write the vavs of the columns in a megillah and a sefer Torah — if I had the power I would abolish them; and one should not begin any column with a vav except the letters of BiYah Shemo, and similarly the vav of "va'a'idah bam"; for through this they come to corruption, that they widen and shorten letters improperly in order to constrain a vav at the head of the column, end of his words. However I found that BiYah Shemo even b'dieved is pasul if it was not written at the head of the column, but vavs if they were not written above are kosher, for specifically l'chatchila we require [them], end of his words. And Beit Yosef brings it (and in Ma'adanei Melech at the beginning of daf 79 he understood that this "however" is from the language of Beit Yosef who wrote thus — see there). It is explicit that regarding BiYah Shemo one should be particular.
