(Shach seif katan 5) The yud of "Yoducha achecha." So appears in Hagahot Maimoniyot. He writes that because "Yehudah atah" begins an open parashah, the final line of the preceding column must begin with "Yehudah," the entire line containing the two words "Yehudah atah," and the next column beginning with the yud of "Yoducha." Tur, however, writes that the yud of "Yehudah" should begin the line. Minchat Shai says the same, and this is scribal practice.
There, the shin of Shoftim. Hagahot Maimoniyot wonders how this is possible, since Shoftim is a closed parashah. How can it begin at the head of a line?
