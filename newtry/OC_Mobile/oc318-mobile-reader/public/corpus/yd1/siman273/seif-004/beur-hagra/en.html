If there occurred, etc. This follows from the expression there, "If there occurred," etc. There is no difficulty from a four-letter word, where two letters would be written beyond the column. Writing up to half the word outside is permitted, and the teaching informs us that only a majority is forbidden. Rosh, however, ruled that three letters are always forbidden outside the column. The text therefore discusses a five-letter word and does not discuss a four-letter word, as explained above, because two letters are always permitted. See Orach Chayim, siman 32, se'if 33.
