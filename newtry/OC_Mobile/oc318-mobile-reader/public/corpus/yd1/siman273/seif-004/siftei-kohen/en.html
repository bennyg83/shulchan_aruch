If he can write half of it, and so forth. This implies that even more than three letters may be written outside the column provided they are not the majority of the word. Bach rules this way. In my opinion, however, the primary ruling is to be stringent like Rosh and Tur: only with a four-letter word may half be written outside the column. Three letters outside the column should not be written even when most of the word remains inside. Semak and Hagahot Maimoniyot say regarding tefillin that one should not write three letters outside the line, deriving this from Sefer Torah. Beit Yosef cites them in Orach Chayyim siman 32, and Tur and Mechaber rule this way there in seif 33. It is surprising that Mechaber appears to contradict himself. This requires study.
