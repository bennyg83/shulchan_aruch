If he can write half of it, etc. This implies that even more than three letters may extend outside the column, provided they do not constitute most of the word, and the Bach rules accordingly. In my humble opinion, however, the primary ruling is to be stringent like the Rosh and Tur. Only half of a four-letter word may be outside the column. Three letters may not be outside even when most of the word remains within. This is also the view of the Semak and Hagahot Maimoniyot regarding tefillin, who forbid writing three letters outside the line and derive this from a Torah scroll. The Beit Yosef cites them in Orach Chaim 32, and the Tur and Mechaber rule likewise there in seif 33. The Mechaber's rulings therefore appear contradictory, and the matter requires study.
