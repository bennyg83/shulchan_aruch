If a five-letter word came to him, he should not write two inside the page and three outside the page, but rather write three inside the page and two outside the page. And if there is no room to write three inside the page, he leaves the place partly empty and does not stretch the letters to make them larger than their fellows in order to fill to the end of the page (Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of the Rambach). And a three-letter word—one may write two of it outside the page even though most of it is inside; but if it has only two letters, one does not write them outside the page. {Rama: And even if the two letters are from a large word and appear like a word by themselves, one does not write them on the margin (Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai, who so wrote in the name of Semak).}
