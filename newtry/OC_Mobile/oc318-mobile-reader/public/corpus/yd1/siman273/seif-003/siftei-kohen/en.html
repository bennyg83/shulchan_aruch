And even, etc. From the Tur, the Shulchan Aruch, and the other authorities it appears that the concern is only with writing an entire word outside the column, but not with writing two letters of a larger word outside it. This is explicit in Rashi, the Beit Yosef, and the Rosh regarding a two-letter word: he may not cast it between the columns or write it beyond the ruled margin. Although two letters of a five-letter word may extend beyond the column, an entire word may not. It follows that two letters belonging to a longer word are permitted outside the column.
