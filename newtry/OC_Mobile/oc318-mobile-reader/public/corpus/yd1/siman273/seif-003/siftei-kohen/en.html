Even, and so forth. From Tur, Shulchan Aruch, and the other poskim it appears that the concern is only writing an entire word outside the column, not two letters of a longer word. Rashi, Beit Yosef, and Rosh say explicitly regarding a two-letter word that happened to fall at the end of the line: one should not throw it between the columns, meaning one should not write that entire word outside the ruled margin. Although one may write two letters outside the column from a five-letter word, an entire word was not permitted. This clearly implies that two letters from a larger word are permitted.
