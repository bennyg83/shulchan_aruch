Four lines. See in Dagemar below siman 275 who wrote in name of Tosafot that these four lines must be in the middle of the page specifically; but to finish one book at the end of the page and begin the next book at the head of the page — even if he leaves four empty lines he is not permitted — see there. However in Tashbetz part 1 siman 175 he wrote that what the sages required leaving four lines between one chumash and another is only for the mitzvah alone and not to invalidate; therefore it is reasonable there is no stringency whether these four lines are in the middle of the page or at its beginning or end or partly at end and partly at beginning — in any case even initially it is fine; however that after the fact does not invalidate — this is if he added or subtracted; but if he did not leave any space at all it is certainly disqualified — see there. Also in responsum Teshuvah Me'ahavah part 1 siman 65 he extended on this and brought clear proof from Talmud Menachot 30 that there is no stringency at all in this; and wrote that the Yerushalmi Tosafot brought there is also not for invalidation but only for initial mitzvah — see there. [And so ruled in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 255 concluding: for halachah I say — although initially we should not act against Rabbenu Tam in Tosafot, nevertheless when the scribe reaches the end of a chumash and the world presses him he should not reduce the writing so as to finish in the middle of the page — for that is like after the fact; it is not proper to condemn the Torah scroll now with such letters because of finishing a chumash at the end of the page which is a future disgrace and a general concern lest they cut it; and all the more now when there is no way to use chumashim made in a scroll at all — a person should never come to cut a Torah scroll into chumashim even if he finishes the chumash at its end — see there.] And see in Noda B'Yehuda tinyana part 4 siman 179 in responsum from the author's son regarding a Torah scroll where the scribe at end of Sefer Shemot "bekol maseihem" at end of the sheet did not leave even one line and above began Sefer Vayikra and also left no line; and he wrote that Torat Emet in mouth of Tashbetz above that only initially must leave four lines and after the fact not required; however if he did not leave space at all certainly disqualified even after the fact; and he raised: since there are thirteen words after the name Havayah baruch hu — if possible to engrave all thirteen words and write them in fine script so the bottom line remains completely empty — valid for all; and if impossible then leave at least in the bottom line space of half a row or at minimum enough to write gimel pei aleph which is open per Rambam's view — see there.
