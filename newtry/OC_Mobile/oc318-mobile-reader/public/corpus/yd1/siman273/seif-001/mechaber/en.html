The measure of the margin and spacing between line and line and between page and page. In it are 6 seifim. The measure of the margin below is four fingerbreadths in size; and above, three; and between each page and page, two. Therefore one must leave at the beginning and end of each sheet the width of a finger, and space for sewing; and it is found that when the sheets are sewn together there will be between each page and page in the entire book the width of two fingerbreadths. And between line and line—as the full width of a line. And between each chumash and chumash—four blank lines without writing, and one begins the chumash from the beginning of the fifth line. And one leaves at its beginning and end enough to roll on the pole, and also the two fingerbreadths that remain between the pole and the page.
