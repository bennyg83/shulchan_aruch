In thumbbreadths. This follows from the Jerusalem Talmud's statement there: for ordinary sacred books, one must leave a margin of two fingerbreadths above and three below; for a Torah scroll, three above and one handbreadth below. This also appears in tractate Soferim, chapter 2, law 4. The text there contains a scribal error and should read, "three portions of a handbreadth." Since the first clause speaks of a handbreadth, the latter clause likewise refers to portions of a handbreadth.
