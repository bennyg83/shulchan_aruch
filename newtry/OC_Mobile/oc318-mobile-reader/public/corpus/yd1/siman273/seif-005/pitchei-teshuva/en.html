To write half inside the page. Shach — it appeared to him primary to be stringent like Rosh that three letters not remain even if the word is large; and he challenged that Mechaber's words contradict each other — Mechaber ruled here like Rambam that even many letters outside the line are permitted if half remains within the line; and in Orach Chayyim siman 32 seif 33 ruled like Rosh; and so Magen Avraham challenged there s.k. 46; and see in responsum Knesset Yechezkel siman 40 who wrote for practical difference that truly it appears to decide that a word with six or seven root letters like metusha'el shevatayim mahalalel pelishtim kaftorim and similar — certainly we depend on majority and if half is within the line it is permitted; however a word whose first letter is for action (meaning for use) like levhemtecha veha'avartem veshinantam letotafot — in this one rules like Rosh since the remaining half is half with the action; per this in a Torah scroll with a hundred words like metusha'el whose root is six or seven letters — valid if half remains; unlike in tefillin impossible to find this for we do not find a word in all four parshiyot with six letters without action except vehivosei and words hotzi'anu la'avoteinu — and these words impossible to find at end of line — see in Levush Orach Chayyim siman 35; therefore in Hilchot Tefillin he wrote plainly three letters unlike Torah scroll — end quote. And his language is somewhat garbled — what he said at beginning of his words like shevatayim pelishtim kaftorim — there are no six root letters and his intent is that the entire first half of the word is from the root; and what he said like levhemtecha veha'avartem veshinantam — even though there also the last letter is for action — nevertheless he holds the last letter is considered more the essence of the word since they already sanctified the word.
