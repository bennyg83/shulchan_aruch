Or if one shredded, etc. — this entire seif is Rambam's wording except "or" is Mechaber's gloss; in Rambam: "and he made from them alone," etc. Kesef Mishneh explained at length; I cite gemara first then Rambam in Niddah 61: R' Acha b.R. Ivya in name of Mar Zutra — flax thread on wool cloak, removed, unknown if removed — d'oraisa sheatnez requires combed, spun, woven; Rashi: combed in brush together, spun together, woven — weaving rabbinic without combing/spinning; if unknown if removed — permitted. Rav Ashi: say or combed or spun or woven; halacha like Mar Zutra since Torah combined in one phrase. Tosafot challenged Rashi spun-together — kilay in tzitzit? tekhelet combed in brush, spun alone; if woven means woven, how "or combed or spun" without woven? Answer: prohibition by combing/spinning alone without garment connection until woven; Torah forbade garment — why verse? Rosh HaIsha: combed without weave not a garment. Perush Rambam: d'oraisa each of combed, spun, woven alone means twisted together afterward — verse needed for tzitzit threads spun alone. Beit Yosef challenged: we hold all three together — why rule combed alone kilay? Tur emended Rambam "this is not kilay" — cannot, we learned lebedim forbidden as combed — means not d'oraisa only certainly kilay; main text kilay — Raavad text likewise. Difficult: Rambam says lebedim d'oraisa kilay — perhaps "until combed spun woven" with or, one suffices; end implies all three together — I say combed alone kilay, spun alone not unless woven — Kesef Mishneh wonders: gemara only all together or each alone. Proof from mishnah end kilayim? tanna on Scripture sheatnez, sha explicit, lest whole word drags; separate tet from naz — Rambam correct broadly; his mishnah explanation as Beit Yosef — each Torah kilay; Niddah statement is commentator not Talmud — Rambam like Rav Ashi or combed; "halacha like Mar Zutra" not Talmud — Tosafot challenge if need all together why tzitzit verse? Rambam proved in Hilchot Kilayim; we hold one suffices here too, Mar Zutra does not drash tzitzit verse, we hold like Rav Ashi. Rambam's order: wool joined flax — d'oraisa kilay once combed/spun/woven together; if combed each and made lebedim — kilay; shred and spin together and weave — kilay; sew wool to linen even in silk thread; wool garment with linen threads or reverse; tie or braid wool and flax threads; wool and flax in bag rolled together — kilay; braid of wool tied to braid of flax though strap between — if folded wool and linen garments and tied — kilay, "wool and flax together" — once united forbidden. Four sections: three from sheatnez, one with verse yachdav; first three from sheatnez — sha together, lebedim, then spin add weave for garment; nun — connected by spinning; separate section for all connections including braid and fold; Rambam explains nun as any joining including spinning; not only Rashi woven; per Rambam each of three forbidden alone; Raavad and Tur like Mar Zutra require all together.
