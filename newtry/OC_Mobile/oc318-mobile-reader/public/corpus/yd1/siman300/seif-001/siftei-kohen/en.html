And made them into felts, etc. Most poskim hold that felts are forbidden only rabbinically, and stiff ones are entirely permitted, as below in siman 301; see there seif 2 in the gloss.
Even if he sewed them with silk and hemp threads, etc. It appears that although the Rambam derived this law from his own reasoning below in seif 5, as the Beit Yosef brought in this siman from his language in the Mishnah commentary, nevertheless we rule that this is forbidden according to everyone, since the linen and wool are next to each other and are merely joined by silk and hemp. So too it is explicitly proven in Derishah seif 10 from what he wrote in the name of his teacher; see there. So too it is implied below in seif 5 regarding "although it is possible that the hemp thread will enter," etc.; examine carefully.
