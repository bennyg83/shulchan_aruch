Which kilayim is d'oraisa and which is d'rabbanan. In it are 7 seifim. Once wool joined with linen, if there is a side of joining in the world, it is forbidden because of shaatnez. How so? Wool and linen that were mixed together and combed them, and made felt from them; or mixed and spun them together and wove a garment from that spinning; or sewed a wool garment with a linen one—even if he sewed them with silk or hemp threads; or sewed a wool garment with linen threads or a linen garment with wool threads; or tied wool threads with linen threads or braided them—this is shaatnez.
