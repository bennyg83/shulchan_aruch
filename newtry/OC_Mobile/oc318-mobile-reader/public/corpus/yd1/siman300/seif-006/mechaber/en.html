One who leads animals and inserts ropes into its legs, some of linen and some of wool—this is permitted even though he wraps them on its leg. But if he tied them all, they become shaatnez and it is forbidden to wrap them on its leg. (And some permit, since he does not intend to) (Hagahos Ashiri and Rabbenu Yerucham, Netiv 59) (and see below siman 301).
