He tied them. A single knot is sufficient here because afterward he wraps it around his hand. Taz. Shach writes that the authorities who prohibit hold that this is unlike siman 301, seif 6, because here it is a psik reisha: from the way he holds them, it is impossible that his hand will not become warm. Bach explains this, unlike Perishah, who left the matter requiring study. See siman 301.
