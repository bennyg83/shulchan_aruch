And it is forbidden for him to wrap them around his hand. For his hand becomes warmed by them, and it is comparable to wearing.
And some permit, etc. Those who forbid hold that this is not comparable to below, siman 301 seif 6, for here it is a psik reisha, since by holding them it is impossible that his hand will not become warmed. So wrote the Bach. So too it is in Shiltei Gibborim, chapter Bameh Behemah, and he is brought in Ma'adanei Melekh, p. 194a, who agreed with him, unlike the Perishah below, siman 301 seif 1, who left it requiring study.
