A garment, etc. Tosefta: a wool shirt that tore may be fastened with a linen thread, and a linen shirt that tore may be fastened with a wool thread; but if he sewed them, they are forbidden because of kilayim.
And likewise it is permitted, etc. The Rosh in Niddah there. See Tosafot, Shabbat 57b at the end of s.v. “ein,” and at the end of the first chapter of Yom Tov 15a at the end of s.v. “ella,” etc. But the Rambam forbids even with sackcloth, as written in seif 5, and the same applies in such a case.
(Likkut) And likewise it is permitted, etc. This is learned from the Tosefta written in Shulchan Aruch: a wool garment that tore, etc. See the Rosh s.k. 24. Although he sews, etc. This is learned from the case in seif 4, that it is permitted, etc., according to the first view there. The reason is that they are not themselves attached to one another; the same applies here.
And likewise, etc. Learned from the above.
