The Taz wrote: it is difficult, for the implication here is that there is a prohibition in joining via a knot if it were such that one cannot pull out the ends of the tears without untying the knot; and above in seif 2 the Rama wrote that even two stitches without a knot do not constitute joining; and likewise in reverse — and one must say that there [with wool and linen threads] the wool and linen themselves are not joined, only through the sewing thread, therefore we require both — two stitches and also knots; unlike here where the prohibition is that if one were to knot the wool garment together with the linen garment itself; and in any case all agree that if one makes holes in the garment itself and joins them with thread through a permanent knot it is forbidden; and many stumble in this by knotting the fringe of a garment to a linen cord and tying a permanent knot on one side — end quote.
