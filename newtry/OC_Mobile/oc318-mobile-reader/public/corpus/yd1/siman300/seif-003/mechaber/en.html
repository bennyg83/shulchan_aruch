A wool garment that tore—it is permitted to join it with linen threads and tie it, but not to sew it. {Rama: And likewise it is permitted to place wool in a linen pillow or cushion even though all around it is sewn and one cannot remove the wool—it is like a linen sack filled with wool whose mouth is sewn, for there is no shaatnez in this (so is in the Tur). And even if the sewing is with wool itself, it is permitted, for one can take the wool from there without undoing the sewing. But in wool garments of shaatnez, it is forbidden in such a case, for one cannot take the garments from there without undoing the sewing (own reasoning, and Mordechai chapter BaMah Ishah and Tosafot first chapter of Beitzah). And likewise it is permitted to join wool sleeve-houses that have knots or buttons in linen sleeve-houses that have holes and insert the knots or buttons into the holes, since they are loose and one can remove them with one's finger without tearing the holes—even if one leaves them thus always. But if they are so stuck that one cannot remove them without tearing the holes, it is a joining and forbidden (Terumat HaDeshen siman 256).}
