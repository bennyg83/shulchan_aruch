It is permitted to wear, etc. This ruling requires explanation. Why should a permanent knot be permitted, and how can that fit with the condition that the garments not be joined by two stitches? My father-in-law the Bach therefore disagrees with the Shulchan Aruch's reading and explains the Tur differently. The central distinction is whether the two garments can be removed separately or only together. Temporary joining intended merely for wearing does not create kilayim when each garment remains independently removable, but stitching or tying them so that they function as one permanent garment is forbidden. The number of stitches, permanence of the knot, and the wearer's intention are evidence of whether a true connection has been formed. One should therefore be stringent whenever the garments have been joined in a durable manner, even if the physical attachment appears slight.
