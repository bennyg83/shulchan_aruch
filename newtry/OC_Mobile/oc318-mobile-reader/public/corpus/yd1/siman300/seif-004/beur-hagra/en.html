It is permitted. The Tosefta states: a person may wear two tunics even if his belt is wrapped around them externally, provided he does not twist the cord and tie the two garments together across his shoulders. Rosh explains that this means tying the two garments together in such a way that they cannot be separated without untying the knot. This is the source of the opinion that prohibits. Beit Yosef infers from Responsa Rashba that even where the garments are held by the belt, the prohibition applies only when tied together in this manner, and Rashba understands the Tosefta accordingly.
And it appears to me. This follows the latter opinion, as explained below.
Some say etc. Yerushalmi there: a shoe of zerev — there are places that zerevin wool from inside. Like this: R' Zeira commanded R' Abba bar Zamina, and he should tell Bar Rashin that he should not sew him a shoe with linen, but with a strap. He admits regarding this, that he forbids strips of wool upon linen, that it is permitted, for it is a clasp of itself, and it comes down for it. They asked before R' Ila: may one sew a shoe with linen? He said to him: like R' Zeira, for R' Zeira said it is forbidden. R' Ila admits regarding this, that one who wears wool socks over linen socks, that it is forbidden, for he does not remove the upper without removing the lower. And that is what he wrote, and it is not similar etc. And it is implied from the Yerushalmi according to the latter reasoning mentioned above in the Shulchan Aruch: anything that one cannot take off is forbidden even with a undershirt, and as the Rosh wrote, as it appears to me.
