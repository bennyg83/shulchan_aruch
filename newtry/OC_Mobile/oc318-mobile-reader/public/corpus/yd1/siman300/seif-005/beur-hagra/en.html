Although, etc. These forms of kilayim are rabbinic, and a doubt concerning them is treated leniently. This accords with their view that the threads must be combed, spun, and woven together. See siman 301, seif 2. Toras HaBayis gives another reason. See the Shach; it is not clear to me. See also the end of siman 299, seif 1.
According to the Rambam, etc. He explains the Mishnah literally, "He shall not tie," as he writes in his commentary. So too the Tosefta cited above in siman 299, seif 2, and the Mishnah concerning a sack and basket. This is the meaning of "even if he placed," etc. In his commentary he cites the Sifrei's exposition of "together," under all circumstances. This is also the meaning of the opening of this siman: once they are joined, etc.
