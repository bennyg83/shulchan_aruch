And according to Rambam. Rambam explains the Mishnah literally, that one may not tie them together, as he writes in his commentary. This also accords with the Tosefta cited above in siman 299, seif 2, and with the Mishnah concerning the sack and basket. This is why Mechaber writes, "and even if he placed," and Rambam writes in his commentary that Sifrei derives from the word "together" that any joining is included. This is also the basis of the opening rule of this siman, "once they are joined.
