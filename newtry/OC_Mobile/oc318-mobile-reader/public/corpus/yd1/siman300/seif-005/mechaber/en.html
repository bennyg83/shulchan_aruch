One strap of wool and the other of linen—it is forbidden to gird with it, even if a leather strap is in the middle, because he ties both ends together when girding with it. But if in its middle there is wool in one place and linen in another and they are not adjacent to one another, it is permitted. And according to this, it is permitted to attach hides sewn with linen beneath wool garments, even though it is possible to insert the hemp thread of the sewing that joins the hide with the garment into a thread of the sewing of the hide (R' Yerucham and Terumat HaDeshen siman 297). And for the Rambam, all this is shaatnez d'oraisa—even if he placed wool and linen in a sack or box and rolled them, it is shaatnez d'oraisa. (And the custom follows the first view) (Tur, and likewise Ramban and Rashba siman 300 and Terumat HaDeshen there).
