And likewise, etc. — Yerushalmi; and Rosh brought mishnah 10 — R' Yirmiyah asked, etc.; and wrote there above "in father and son he asked him," etc., and specifically, etc.; likewise Rosh there and in Niddah; likewise Rashba in responsum in name of Rosh — but he explains R' Yirmiyah's question without joining with two stitches, only when belted together; R' Yosi answered in wonder "and they are attached" — not yachdav except when joined with two stitches; but this is not a connection; R' Chagai asked he himself — how combine in kilayim? It happened — wearing wool leggings on one leg and linen on the other; R' Yosi said in wonder not necessary — there were no wounds on his head; he gave a wool patch on this and linen patch on that; R' Yosi said in wonder you are forbidden only attachment — meaning he asked if permitted to wear one wool stocking and one linen on two legs without connection; and two patches in two places on the head — he answered in wonder only connection with two stitches; with two stitches need not belt both, for he did not say "belt," etc., but per view it should forbid even without connection at all — sewing. Beit Yosef — see there in the responsum in manuscript.
