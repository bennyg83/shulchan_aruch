They combine, etc. For example, a piece of linen cloth is attached to a sack and a piece of woolen cloth is attached to a basket, and the two pieces are then joined together with two stitches. They combine even though each is attached to a different utensil, and it is forbidden to cover oneself with the sack or basket. We do not say that each attachment stands independently. Tur and the other commentators. Bartenura and other commentators write that the same law applies when the pieces are attached to other utensils; their status is like that of a sack and basket.
