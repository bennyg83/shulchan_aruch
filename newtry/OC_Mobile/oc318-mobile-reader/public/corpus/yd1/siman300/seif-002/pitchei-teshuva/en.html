With two stitches and a knot. See Magen Avraham siman 11 seif katan 13, who wrote that even according to this view that one stitch — a knot does not help, that is one knot; which is not so with two knots: even if he inserts the needle once, according to everyone it is a joining, for if not so, kilayim in tzitzis — where would you find it? See there. And so he wrote in the teshuvah of Chavos Yair siman 143, and he wrote further that even though this view holds that even two stitches require a knot, nevertheless three stitches, and all the more so more, do not require a knot — see there further. And see the book Chomos Yerushalayim siman 101 what he wrote on this.
