With two stitches and a knot. See Magen Avraham 11:13, who writes that even according to the view that one stitch does not help together with a knot, this refers to one knot. With two knots, however, even if the needle was inserted only once, all agree that it constitutes a connection, for otherwise how could kilayim ever occur in tzitzit? See there. Chavot Yair 143 writes likewise, and adds that although this view requires a knot even with two stitches, three stitches, and certainly more, require no knot. See also Chomot Yerushalayim 101.
