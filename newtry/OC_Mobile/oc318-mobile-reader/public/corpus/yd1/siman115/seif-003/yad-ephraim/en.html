That it is a carcass. Beit HaTefutzot and responsum Mayim Rabim YD siman 20, 21, 22: regarding one who made cheeses in a flock and went and bought dry rennet from gentiles who practice drying them in their skins with smoke and sun, and made cheeses from them and forbade them because rennet of a carcass stood within twenty-four hours and is pickled like cooked — see there; and he forbids even if before he curdled the cheeses he removed the skins from the rennet, for one may not distinguish in this, for this rennet with which he curdled became entirely new, since it is pickled like cooked with the skin of the carcass before the skin dried; and see in Shach and Peri Chadash siman 87.
