(There) Milk of a gentile that was forbidden does not help, etc. See Shach seif katan 8 who wrote: specifically stam milk; but when we know, etc.; and he is from Tosafot chapter one of Avodah Zarah; and this is not compelled, for one may well say that all this is according to their view who hold that wherever there is no concern of mixture they did not forbid gentile milk at all; and if so, if we know he sets it for cheeses and there is no concern of mixture it is permitted; not so for us who establish that even if there is no concern of mixture, once it comes to a Jew while it is still milk it is also forbidden because they do not distinguish; if so, even if the gentile's intent was to make cheese and there is no concern of mixture, nevertheless since it came to a Jew while it was still milk it is forbidden even without any mixture at all — understand well.
