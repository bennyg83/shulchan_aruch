(Siman 115 seif 3 in the hagahah) Also forbidden even if there is a distinguishing mark. For one might say: in this place they practiced like those who forbid, and not because they fix it in that place in the manner of the prohibition — end of Maharil's words. And Turei Chayim wrote: accordingly even one who travels to a place where they practiced leniency is forbidden when his intent is to return; but it seems they practiced to be lenient in this, and leave Israel alone — end of his words. And that which we forbid one who travels to a place of prohibition even when his intent is to return — that is because we are concerned lest one say that in that place they do not make it in a permitted manner; and accordingly one who travels from a place of permission to a place of prohibition and brings butter with him from his place and his intent is to return is permitted from the outset. Minchat Chinuch.
