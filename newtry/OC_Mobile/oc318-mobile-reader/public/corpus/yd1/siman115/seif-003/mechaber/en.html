One does not protest against the people of the place who practice leniency with butter of a non-Jew; and if the majority of the people of the place practice prohibition, one should not change. And in a place where there is no custom — if he cooked it until the milk residues went away, it is permitted. {Rama: It is also permitted to cook it initially so that the milk residues go away (Beit Yosef). And if a non-Jew cooked it, it is permitted, for stam their vessels are not ben yomo (Beit Yosef according to the poskim). And if one goes from a place where they do not eat it to a place where they eat it, he eats there with them; but it is forbidden to bring it with him and eat it in a place where they practice prohibition with it unless it has a distinguishing mark that it is from permitted places. And one who goes from a place where they practiced leniency to a place where they practiced prohibition there — it is forbidden to eat it there (Tur). And some say the same if it was brought from a place of leniency to a place of prohibition, it is also forbidden even if it has a distinguishing mark (responsum of Maharil siman 35); and such is the practice.}
