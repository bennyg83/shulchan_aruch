1) [Seif 1] Milk that a gentile milked and a Jew does not see it is forbidden, etc. — it makes no difference whether he milked for his own need or for a Jew's need. Tur. Taz seif katan 1. Shach seif katan 1. Peri Chadash ot 1. Halachah Pesukah in seif 1. Chacham Tzvi klal 67 ot 1. Aruch HaShulchan ot 2 — meaning that with milk for his own need there is reason to permit, for certainly he did not mix impure milk and spoil his milk since it does not curdle; and nevertheless it is forbidden. Taz there. Aruch HaShulchan there.
