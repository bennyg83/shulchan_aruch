(Siman 115, seif 3, Rama's gloss) It is likewise forbidden even if it bears a distinguishing mark. For one may say that in this place they followed the opinion that forbids it, and not because there they prepare it in a manner involving the prohibition. End of the words of Maharil. Toras Chatas wrote that according to this, even one who goes to a place where the custom is to permit it is forbidden where he intends to return. However, it seems to me that the custom is to be lenient in this, and leave Israel to their practice. End of his words. The reason we forbid one who goes to a place where it is forbidden, even when he intends to return, is that we are concerned that perhaps in that place they do not prepare it in a permitted manner. Accordingly, one who goes from a place where it is permitted to a place where it is forbidden, and brings the butter with him from his own place and intends to return, is permitted whichever way you look at it. Minchas Yaakov.
