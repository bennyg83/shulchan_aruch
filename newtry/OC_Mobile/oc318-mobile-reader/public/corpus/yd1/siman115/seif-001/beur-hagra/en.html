lest עירב etc.. גמ' שם:
If it is known, etc. There in the Gemara: if, etc.; and Avodah HaGolah seif katan 3.
And he knows, etc. Tur: without this he is not afraid.
And l'chatchila, etc. Meaning: even if it is known he has no impure thing in the flock; and as stated above that one who sits outside is required; and they said there on account of mixing and not actual mixing, for behold there is no impure thing there; and furthermore we are not concerned for this since he gains nothing except that he is not careful that impure milk should not remain in the vessel — as the Mordechai and Semak wrote.
And they practiced, etc. We learn from the above; and meaning: even if he sees at the beginning of the milking.
However b'dieved, etc. Meaning: even if he did not see in the vessel; and also that he milked in a vessel whose way is, etc.; but he did not see at the beginning of the milking — even b'dieved forbidden, as Darkei Moshe and Turei Chayim wrote, and as the Gemara and Shulchan Aruch wrote as above; and Shach wrote that even to see in the vessel — even b'dieved forbidden, as Semak and Mordechai wrote, that for this reason a Jew must sit as above; and so Issur VeHeter wrote, not as Darkei Moshe wrote in his name.
And maidservants, etc. Like a Jew sits, etc., he is afraid; and so here he is afraid.
And even a minor Jew, etc. Avodah Yomi siman 130 in the name of the Rashba; and so the Mordechai chapter 2 siman 474 on what the Gemara says (folio 38): one places a Jew with meat, etc., a woman stirs a pot, etc.; and the Mordechai wrote in the name of Ravyah: and we are not concerned for switching and mixing as explained in the Yerushalmi — for example the child sits and guards.
However if, etc. As above in Shulchan Aruch: specifically a Jew sits, etc., even though he goes out and comes in; and Shach who ruled even if he was not there for part of the milking it is forbidden, not like the Rav.
Milk of a gentile, etc. Like all rabbinic prohibitions as above end of seif 113; and even the Raah concedes here since there is concern of a Torah prohibition.
But not, etc. For it is forbidden only from custom; and like bread of a gentile.
Milk of a gentile, etc. Gemara there; and the other answer: even if you say he sets, etc.; and so butter, as is written in seif 93 — see there; and even if one cooks the butter — and this is what is meant by and whatever, etc.
