They forbade cheeses of gentiles because they curdle them with rennet of their slaughter that is nevelah; and even if they curdled them with herbs, it is forbidden. {Rama: And such is the custom and one should not breach the fence (Beit Yosef) unless in a place where they practiced leniency from olden times. And if a Jew sees the making of the cheeses and the milking, it is permitted (Agur chapter "Sho'el"). And such is the widespread custom in all these lands; and if he saw the making of the cheeses and did not see the milking, there is room to permit b'dieved, for one need not be concerned lest impure matter was mixed into it, since he made cheeses from the milk, for impure matter does not stand and certainly the non-Jew did not mix into it since his intent is to make cheeses (hagahot Ashiri Pesachim and Tur). Nevertheless it is forbidden to eat the milk thus (there).}
