And even, etc. For they already decreed on all cheeses of gentiles, whether they curdled it with a forbidden substance or curdled it with a permitted substance — a decree because they curdle it with a forbidden substance — language of the Rambam. Understandably they did not distinguish between one cheese and another, since it challenges there: if so dry should be permitted old should be permitted; and perhaps the same applies only when they forbade it completely they forbade it and did not distinguish; and the Gemara challenges: why did they not distinguish? And they said there: a new decree, etc.; hence they forbade on account of a decree and it is forbidden by vote; and so all the poskim, not as Tosafot there d"h chada, etc., wrote. And one should not compare to other matters that were permitted when the reason was nullified, that they did not forbid l'chatchila except according to the matter; and Rivash siman 194; and in milk he sometimes distinguishes forbidden and sometimes permitted; and so with sheep and other matters; and he does not distinguish with cheese — and Hagahot Maimoniyot.
And if the Jew sees, etc. So too Maharam siman 474 in the name of Tosefta chapter 5: bread that a gentile baked without Jewish presence and cheese that a gentile curdled without Jewish presence is forbidden; and in our Tosefta the matter of cheese is missing in the first section; but Maharam wrote there that it is not specific, for behold in the latter section it says: bread that a Jew baked even though the gentile kneaded it, and cheese that a Jew curdled even though the gentile worked it is permitted — hence Jewish curdling we require; and so with bread — necessarily one cannot say that in the presence of a Jew it helps, as the Gemara says: specifically one of three labors is required.
And if he saw, etc. For therefore other reasons are needed in cheese there; and Tosafot folio 35b d"h ligvinah, etc., and there one d"h lefi, etc.; and all this is according to his method; but according to what is written above, specifically that a Jew curdled. (Collection) And if the Jew, etc., and if he saw, etc. According to Tosafot there d"h lefi, sight of making the cheese alone suffices, for all the concerns there are on account of curdling except for Rabbenu Levi; and now we are not concerned for Rabbenu Levi as Tosafot wrote d"h chada; rather according to Rashi there d"h lefi one must also see the milking on account of Rabbi Chanina, as is written in the second answer and the other answer; and Tosafot there d"h ligvinah, etc.; and see what is written in seif 93 (end).
Nevertheless, etc. As above seif 66; and meaning: here even if the gentile's intent was to make cheeses; and as is written: even if you say, etc.; and it implies even if the gentile's intent is to make cheeses it is also forbidden.
