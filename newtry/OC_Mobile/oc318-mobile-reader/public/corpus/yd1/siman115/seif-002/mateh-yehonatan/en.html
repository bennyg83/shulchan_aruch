(There in the hagahah) And if a gentile milked some of the animals, etc. See in Shach seif katan 13 where he elaborated to give a reason why that milk which was milked before the Jew's arrival was not forbidden; and it is possible that certainly if that milk that was milked after the Jew's arrival stands alone in a vessel, in truth even Rama would agree it is forbidden; only it deals with milk already mixed with milk milked after the Jew's arrival; if so one may say that through mixture they did not forbid so extensively because they do not distinguish.
