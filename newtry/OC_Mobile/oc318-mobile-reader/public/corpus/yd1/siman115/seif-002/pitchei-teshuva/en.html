Cheeses. See in responsum Makom Shmuel siman 74, who wrote regarding cheeses that come from overseas cities, colored with fat of a tamei fish called walpish, that they are kosher because the fat is notein ta'am lifgam — see there.
The gentiles. See in the new responsa of Radbaz siman 552 regarding cheese and milk of Samaritans — see there.
And so is the custom. See Be'er Heitev in the name of the Shach; and see in responsum Noda B'Yehudah Tinyana Orach Chayyim part siman 37, who decided like the words of the Rama and not like the Shach and Taz and Peri Chadash; and so instructed the Minchas Yaakov and Peri Toar, and he wrote that so the custom spread. And if the keivah is of a Jew, even though the gentile puts it into the milk, it is permitted according to all, even l'chatchila — see there; and see in the book Tiferet L'Moshe who also wrote that all the proofs of the Shach are not compelling, and all the reason in the Gemara in the prohibition of cheeses of gentiles does not apply if a Jew sees, and so it is implied from all the poskim — see there.
