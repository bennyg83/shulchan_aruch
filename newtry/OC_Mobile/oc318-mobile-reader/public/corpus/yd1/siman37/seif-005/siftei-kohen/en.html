Even though they do not pour, etc. In Hagahot Maimoniyot it is explained it is more reasonable to permit when not pouring — he wrote it appears to permit if not pouring, etc.; and so R' Shmuel HaLevi ruled and saw they do not pour and permitted — end; and Beit Yosef brings it; and the reason is when pouring, since it happened so much it resembles poured like a pitcher and there is concern for bronchi (and so Mahariu, Mahara"k, and other acharonim); and one must say the Mechaber holds it is more reasonable to declare treifah when not pouring because it appears like two adjacent bubbles; and it appears to him thus from Hagahot Maimoniyot there; and people practiced to permit bubble across whether pouring or not, for it does not appear like two adjacent bubbles pouring to one another — end; it appears the doubt was it appears like two adjacent — and that is specifically when not pouring — requires study.
Kosher, etc. And written in Beit Yosef: however where it stands opposite bronchi it appears we are concerned for Rambam's words and examine the bronchus between them — end; and the Rav implies he declares treifah and examination does not help; and so from other acharonim who closed their words; and so Maharshal Perek Aleph Tav siman 37 — in large bubble (full of mugla or murky water; but clear water Maharshal permits in great loss through examination — see there) in place of bronchi and across — expert examination does not help even in great loss since we are not expert in examination — end; and so from the Rav's words — even in great loss he declares treifah since he did not distinguish; but difficult, for above siman 36 seif 7 regarding lung poured like a pitcher he wrote even murky and putrid kosher through examination in great loss; and one may say the Rav holds here this bronchus examination is pouring into vessel and seeing white threads — if melted; if not, not melted as there — and specifically poured like pitcher without bubble; but here since there is bubble and bubbles raise rot inside, perhaps bronchi melted inside and rotted until appearance of moisture in bubble changed — therefore impossible to see white threads in vessel — therefore treifah in every case; and I proved below s.k. 28 from Hagahot Mordechai and Mahara"k — see there.
Thin, etc. Meaning in general in animals we say these are their measures; but if we see bronchi outside these measures — treifah — so Semachot; but in Lev Chaim daf 156a it is written it implies we do not care to fear bronchi at edge outside these measures.
And even clear water. And Maharshal there permits clear water in great loss through examination; and wrote his reason: it is reasonable in clear water they did not melt; and further it is extra stringency and most poskim did not mention it; also Rosh and Tur did not mention — end his language; and it appears to me bubble across has place in Gemara and agreed by all poskim — lung poured like a pitcher where bronchi stand; if so all the more bubble across where it stands in place of bronchi; and if so what is lacking because it protrudes — after all there is internal deficiency like poured; and on the contrary bubble is worse than poured, for it protrudes and lung membrane does not cover it (and poured deals even in clear water as all poskim prove — see there); and so Maharam from whom this law's source — he derived this from lung poured like a pitcher as in responsum of Maharam (Prague edition) siman 66 and Mordechai and Teshuvot HaTaz and Mahara"k root 36 and other acharonim; and so found in hagahot in name of Mahariu in his work to declare treifah bubble across even in clear water; and Maharshal brings it there; and so Agudah; and so proven explicitly from Rashba and Ran whom Beit Yosef brings — they challenged Rambam that every bubble requires examining bronchus beneath it, learned from poured like pitcher — not clear that only for poured because inside flesh melted; but in large bubbles between skin and flesh we do not fear bronchus puncture — end their language implies explicitly in bubbles inside across, not between skin and flesh, we fear bronchi; and what Maharshal wrote most poskim did not mention law of bubble across — it is nothing, for it is included in lung poured like pitcher and is worse; and Beit Yosef ruled thus that across requires examination — as I brought his language at beginning s.k. 26. And truly at first glance I wondered on Hagahot Maimoniyot that custom permits bubble across — behold it is worse than poured and melted lung; and on what Mahara"k inclined to be lenient in bubble across since Semag, Semak, Rashba, and other poskim did not mention it — behold it is included in poured and melted and is worse; and it appears clear even Hagahot Mordechai and Mahara"k only say to permit through bronchus examination; and accordingly the Mechaber also wrote in general to permit and necessarily deals through examination as s.k. mentioned; and forbidders in Hagahot Mordechai hold here examination does not help, unlike poured; and so Mahara"k wanted to forbid even through examination — as written for the Rav's view there.
But not pouring, etc. And the reason: then it is not similar to poured like a pitcher and there is no concern for bronchi.
Even murky, etc. And even though above siman 36 seif 7 hagahah wrote specifically in great loss one may permit murky and putrid — there because poured like pitcher from side to side and from concern for bronchi as s.k. 1; but here since they do not pour to one another — not similar to poured like a pitcher and no concern for bronchi at all.
Bubble under varda, etc. And so Mahariu and examinations of acharonim; and Maharshal Perek Aleph Tav siman 38 brought divided views and decided even in varda one declares treifah only when no flesh thread surrounding.
Tinnir, etc. And Maharshal there siman 37 disagreed because treifah across is extra stringency; and we do not add on it; and nevertheless one may be lenient in tinnir for we find no bronchus concern except in poured; and so examinations of acharonim and Semachot — such is practice.
