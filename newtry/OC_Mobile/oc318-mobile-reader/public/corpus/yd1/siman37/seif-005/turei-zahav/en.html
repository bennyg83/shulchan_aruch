And even clear water. His words are astonishing: in adjacent which is Talmudic law he wrote above one follows custom; and in slopes wrote law in clear water like adjacent; and here in across law which is only custom as poskim say — he decided to be stringent; and Maharshal wrote in name of Rosh of Vienna: since it is universal acceptance — do not be stringent in clear water; but water must be clear, not murky, sweet not putrid or salty, for murky, putrid, salty ruin bronchi and lung does not return; his words appear to permit even without bronchus examination, for clear water does not spoil; and further since extra stringency and most poskim did not mention, unlike slopes mentioned in Tosafot in name of BeHag — end; and so is primary; and Moharach z"l also permitted in clear water; except in name of Maharshal he wrote to forbid even clear water — possibly he found thus in some manuscript there from him; but as I brought here in his name it is from his book Perek Aleph Tav siman 37.
And there is bronchus, etc. I saw many examiners err in explaining this law — it was difficult: he wrote recognizable across — from whatever soul, if truly recognizable across what does he teach treifah; if not why truly treifah — I said to write explanation: the matter is bubble in slopes kosher if flesh thread surrounds — still treifah in that bubble from bubble recognizable across; rather necessarily to say there it does not disqualify from across since no bronchi as above seif 5 measure two fingers from thin place i.e. slopes; and on this he said here in varda it is not so, for even in thin place under varda there is bronchus at varda's groove; therefore if bubble found there — treifah if recognizable across even if flesh surrounds, since bronchi there — like its thick place; unlike other onot of lung which do not disqualify in their thin place since no bronchi; and if bubble in varda in thick place or its root — all the more treifah if recognizable across; but without recognizable across no treifah at all in varda like rest of lung. I saw Maharshal brought this law in name of Likutim and wrote it appears one should not be stringent in varda more than rest of lung, since many geonim permit even without varda at all — if so proven bronchi of lung are not primary for life to depend on them — end; his words very astonishing — he wanted varda bronchi stricter than other lung bronchi — we only came to say since there is bronchus even in thin place, law is to forbid when recognizable across; and so correct practically to be stringent as we wrote; and he said well here recognizable across to teach here forbidden from across like rest of lung; and we learn further: every bubble in slopes that was kosher if flesh surrounds — must see if it spreads down lung more than two fingers from slopes — then treifah from across; examiners must be warned — so simple and clear.
Recognizable tinnir across, etc. Also here his words astonishing: why in adjacent and slopes whose treifot are clear to all — kosher in tinnir as above a fortiori across; and Maharshal also wrote he found written treifah but we do not heed, for reason I wrote; and so is primary practically; also in Machberet HaChayim testified they practice thus explicitly to permit.
