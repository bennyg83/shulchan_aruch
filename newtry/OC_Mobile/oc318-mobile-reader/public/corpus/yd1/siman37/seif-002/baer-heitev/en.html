Treifah. And so Taz and Shach: even bubble of clear water; and all the more complete adhesion adhered elsewhere emerging from bubble — pressing and feeling do not help; even from tinnir — it appears treifah; pressing and feeling do not help; even in onot we do not say lining protects.
Treifah. And Taz wrote: from this examiners learned to declare treifah wherever two flaws; and not similar to hanging adhesion from tinnir kosher as below siman 39 seif 8 in hagahah; and must say because of tinnir no flaw there at all; and in hagahot R"tz on examinations: hanging adhesion from bubble with clear water — kosher, for clear water not flaw; and it appears treifah. Taz wrote further: bubble emerging from adhesion between ona and ona below halfway — not called two flaws, for below halfway no adhesion; but if window there — though we permit as below siman 39 seif 4 — nevertheless poskim forbid even in window; and here further bubble flaw — at least two flaws treifah; and bubble from adhesion between ona and ama below halfway even without window — some hold that adhesion treifah — nevertheless here two flaws. Incident: lung adhered to pouch, adhesion full blood until around adhesion to pouch all blood appearance — I declared treifah, worse than two flaws — pressing does not help (large bubble like nut on middle ona of right on ribs, protruding outward, no lung adhesion except root that was adhered — rest outside; Shach siman 25 declared treifah and Pri Chadash agreed, but not for my reason). And Shach wrote: if bubble skin changes to lung appearance even on protrusion or other flaw — kosher, for lung appearance — only internal deficiency.
