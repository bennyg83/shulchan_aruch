(There seif 2) If an adhesion hangs out from a blister. See Shach s.k. 4. And the acharonim hold that the law of two flaws that they forbade regarding lung has no root in the Gemara; and according to Razah it is proven in the Gemara that two flaws in lung are treifah — for Razah wrote that which R' Nachman [said] lung adjacent to the lining is kosher — not because we attribute the blow to the lining, but since it adhered to the lining next to it it is as if in its proper place and kosher; accordingly it is difficult on "it raised tzemachim" — why treifah? For tzemachim alone are kosher; and because it adhered to the lining — behold it is in its proper place; nevertheless one must say it is forbidden on account of two flaws.
