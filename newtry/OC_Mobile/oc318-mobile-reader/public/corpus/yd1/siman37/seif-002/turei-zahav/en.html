If adhesion hangs out, etc. Maharshal explained the reason: since there is no adhesion without puncture in such a case where flaw is before us and adhesion emerges from it — end; and hanging adhesion — see siman 39 seif 8. And it appears all the more if there is complete adhesion — adhered elsewhere and emerging from bubble — treifah; and what they practice to press and feel adhesions does not help here, as siman 39 seif 13 — for hanging adhesion is permitted for all, yet forbidden when emerging from bubble; a fortiori adhered adhesion whom many poskim do not permit with any pressing; it is simple one must declare treifah for all where there is further flaw — emerging from bubble, and even from tinnir — it appears treifah and pressing does not help as siman 39 seif 8.
And the same bubble standing, etc. From this examiners learned to declare treifah wherever there are two flaws; and not similar to hanging adhesion from tinnir which is kosher as siman 39 seif 8 — tinnir has no flaw there at all; and in hagahot of R"tz on examinations he wrote hanging adhesion from bubble with clear water — kosher, for clear water is not a flaw at all; and it appears to me treifah here, for Tosafot Perek Aleph Tav daf 43 "he will split" wrote clear water is a flaw and kosher — found here treifah from force of two flaws; and Moharach z"l also wrote likewise one who permits two adjacent bubbles in clear water as seif 3 — the same kosher here; and it appears different there, for only one flaw — whatever you say clear water is flaw is kosher, you have no disqualification even from adjacency; unlike here two flaws, each independent — hanging adhesion is flaw by itself and bubble of clear water is flaw by itself — combination declares treifah; and so I saw in Machberet HaChayim on examinations — R"tz wrote his law in name of Azrieli; this matter is not in Azrieli's book at all, for I searched and it is not there. It appears bubble emerging from adhesion between ona and ona below halfway — this is not called two flaws, for below halfway there is no adhesion on it at all; however if there is window — even though we permit as below siman 39 seif 4 — nevertheless since poskim declare treifah even there in window, and here there is further bubble flaw — at least called two flaws and treifah; and so if bubble emerges from adhesion between ona and ama below halfway even without window — since some poskim hold that adhesion treifah though we do not hold thus — nevertheless here called two flaws — so appears to me. Incident before me: lung adhered to pouch and adhesion full of blood until where adhesion to pouch all around filled with blood appearance — I declared treifah, for here worse than two flaws — pressing and feeling do not help, for I say it is only spreading of this blood's moisture from lung punctured and blood exited from its bronchi — spreading of moisture certainly occurs even if not punctured — unlike this blood; and even though it does not bubble — treifah, for examination does not help to permit where prohibition is doubtful — as siman 39 seif 4.
