(Siman 37 s.k. 2) Emerging from the bubble. And likewise if the upper skin of the bubble was punctured. Shulchan Aruch HaRav seif 18.
(Shach s.k. 2) All the more for us. Meaning that even though Raavad forbids only without adhesion — nevertheless for us who hold adhesion to the lining is forbidden in every case — here where it emerged from the bubble, even though it adhered to the lining, nevertheless since there are two flaws, adhesion is stricter than hanging — pressing and smoothing do not help; and we judge it for puncture and therefore forbidden for us, for lining closure does not help. (One may distinguish: specifically in hanging, since according to the side of concern we judge it for puncture there is no further lenient side; but in adhesion to the lining that goes away through pressing — even if you fear puncture — nevertheless by law lining closure helps; except we are stringent for we are not expert whether it is properly matted, or perhaps it is not in a place of protrusion literally; if so in any case even for actual puncture it is not certainly treifah; if so where there is no certain puncture, only on account of two flaws we fear puncture in such a case — we are not stringent. Nevertheless Shach holds it is reasonable to declare treifah. And that Shach used the language "all the more" — meaning all the more it is called two flaws, for adhesion through pressing is more of a flaw than hanging without pressing.) And he wrote greater than this from Maharshal, etc.; if so if we judge for puncture there is no lenient side here, for it does not adhere well — and examine.
(There) Unlike a protruding bubble. And from the reason: even in tinnir it is forbidden — from the side of the adhesion itself, if it is adhered like any adhesion adhered — emerging from tinnir — pressing and lining closure do not help, for it does not adhere well. And see in Maharival (part 3 siman 107).
(s.k. 2 in hagahah) Such as on a protrusion. It appears specifically on a raised protrusion; but beforehand it is not a flaw. Pri Megadim in the general rule of two flaws ot 6.
(Taz s.k. 3) This is not called two flaws. Here according to Rashi's view that there is no adhesion without puncture — except that the ona's lining helps — we say well that even though emerging from bubble which is two flaws and indicates puncture — in any case we know there is puncture; except the ona seals (except according to Maharshal brought in Shach s.k. 2 that on account of protruding bubble it does not adhere well). But according to Tosafot's view the ona does not seal, rather the adhesion did not come from puncture — if so one may say emerging from within the bubble which is two flaws — we judge for puncture and it is forbidden; except one may say it is not certainly two flaws, for perhaps the adhesion emerges from another ona; if so if there are two bubbles opposite one another and adhesion emerges from both bubbles — treifah according to Tosafot; and likewise with two bubbles and adjacent and adhesion emerging from them in order — treifah.
