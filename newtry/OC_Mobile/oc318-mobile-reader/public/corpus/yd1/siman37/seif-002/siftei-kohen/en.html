If an adhesion hangs out, etc. As Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Raavad that if an adhesion hangs emerging from the bubble — forbidden, and even in an ona we do not say the lining protects it, etc.; as Beit Yosef wrote, and I do not know the reason for the matter, for behold we say lung punctured and lining seals it — kosher; and Rokeach wrote similarly that if there is adhesion of a thread in a bubble — declare treifah; and it is possible he speaks not in a place of protrusion — end; however below siman 39 he brought Raavad's words at length and it is explained there his reason: we say in the Gemara specifically when it is matted in the flesh it is kosher when the lining seals; but here it is not matted and did not adhere, etc. — see there; and so Bach and Lev Chaim daf 155a explained; and Beit Yosef himself retracted in his Beit Yosef book and Bach and Lev Chaim did not see his words in Beit Yosef — see there; and accordingly all the more for us who hold the lining never protects, as written in hagahah siman 39 seif 18 treifah; and so the Gaon z"l wrote in responsum and expanded in clear great proofs greater than this from Maharshal Perek Aleph Tav siman 44 who wrote even nowadays kosher as matted; and ruled in siman 28 like Raavad because we do not say lining protects — and this is their protrusion; except regarding division of lung which then adheres well — unlike protruding bubble.
Emerging from the bubble. From "and sealed" it implies even a bubble of clear water; and so from Maharshal Perek Aleph Tav siman 28; however one may say they follow their reason who declare treifah adjacent in clear water; and so the R"tz wrote in the name of the Azrieli that clear water is not considered a flaw neither for adjacency nor for hanging adhesion emerging from it and not adhered elsewhere; and such is custom — end; and so Bach wrote that for one who permits adjacent in clear water, all the more he permits hanging adhesion from bubble in clear water; and Maharam in his Beit Yosef book challenged the Azrieli and R"tz on this and his view is to declare treifah in adhesion emerging from bubble of clear water even for one who permits adjacent in clear water; and all his words are not compelling at all — see there; also what he wrote in the name of Mahariv and Maharam to forbid — no proof, for it is clear in their words they forbade in adjacency; and see responsum Ben Lev part 3 siman 100.
And the same applies to bubble, etc. Meaning as adhesion emerging from bubble is treifah because it is two flaws — if so the same on protrusion and the like; and what is protrusion was explained siman 35 seif 5 in the hagahah.
On protrusion. But if it changes to lung appearance — kosher — end; Machatzit HaShekel wrote in several places; and it appears he means if the skin of the bubble changes to lung appearance even though it stands on protrusion or other flaw — kosher, for since it has lung appearance it is only internal deficiency.
