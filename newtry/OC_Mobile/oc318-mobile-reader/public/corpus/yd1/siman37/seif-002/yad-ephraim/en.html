Sound discipline treifah. Avodat HaTaharah; from Taz's words all around filled blood appearance (I wrote scribal error — should be all around it blood appearance, etc.); in my responsum Beit Ephraim part 2 manuscript I wrote: adhesion inside protruding bubble below halfway between ona and ama with window, bubble not covered, salty water inside, adhesion passed through proper pressing, checked in lukewarm, did not bubble — see my laws of two flaws seif 6; from there see leniency in such case in great loss — requires study, specifically bubble not of clear water; even of clear water where custom stringent on hanging even from clear water; but where custom lenient on hanging from clear water — lenient here in slight loss. What I wrote there seif 5 in murky water — stringent — refers to ona passed through pressing; also as I think missing in print and omission — should be: if bubble of murky water where custom stringent on bubble i.e. murky water — stringent and forbid. See my books: pillars of world Beit Ephraim and Magen Shmuel permit below halfway even without pressing, all the more with pressing; though my books' implication I did not rely to lenient on adhesion ona to ama with window — nevertheless per reason I wrote there flaws do not oppose and pressing applies — I permitted in great loss and Shabbat honor; but per one who asked question — no pressing, only detachment from one side — since Shach's father and Shach stringent — who will dare be lenient — see there. See my responsum Beit Ephraim siman 19: thin hanging adhesion on upper ona, they pressed adhesion off lung, wiped blood after pressing, blood exited afterward, still did not bubble in test, great loss, many animals declared treifah — simple complete treifah, permitter liable karet, vessels forbidden; even if blood did not return after wiping; to eye whole and fine after removing adhesion, test does not bubble — treifah: if no puncture — whence blood? Even without adhesion, drops of blood on lung — wipe, four drops return — certainly treifah though test there does not bubble; even make doubt red sweat — do not make; vanity of vanities, especially after slaughter obvious no drops if membranes whole — simple throughout Israel to forbid when lung exudes blood; a fortiori with adhesion as question; test not bubbling does not permit — see there; but if did not watch lung immediately and blood found — can attribute to hand feeling — room to permit as I wrote Beit Ephraim siman 1 and Kaf HaChaim there — see there. Once I happened in that place; slaughterers told great distress: common on upper ona thin red hanging adhesion; when pressed off lung blood on lung — great loss. They said certainly adhesion adheres inside only because place narrow and adhesion very thin detaches from connection, remains adhered to lung — I ordered if occurs bring before pressing; they did — I saw adhesion on place where bronchus near membrane, membrane very thin no hold in flesh; slaughterers quickly press by hand — even walking lightly, membrane weakness makes fine puncture from snatching thin adhesion — blood exits; I ordered cut entire upper ona, placed in lukewarm water bowl, pressed and felt gently quarter hour to soften adhesion, removed, ordered expert press adhesion not quick customary way but much patience — through softening came off easily, no mark, no blood; inflated upper ona through bronchus, lukewarm test, no bubble — I permitted. I said how great sages' words — lukewarm softens adhesion; as Azrieli responsum siman 39 s.k. 15 — test: leave in lukewarm to soften; Ran's reason for lukewarm on lung adjacent to lining — if you ask all explanations — through soaking in lukewarm it tears and puncture revealed; Taz thought must leave lung in lukewarm and soak. Though in my work I wondered on Taz and Azrieli and poskim — Ran's primary intent not remove adhesion with weak-mouthed knife lest spoil and wrongly treifah — leave in lukewarm to soften and detach itself, then know if puncture — siman 39. Nevertheless Ran teaches soaking in lukewarm adhesion detaches easier from lung; I wrote so expert slaughterers hear — if occurs do not hurry feeling; though saliva on adhesion at pressing time — still wait for softening action; saliva spreads sideways — better do as I wrote. If no expert to inflate upper ona alone — do not cut, place whole lung in large vessel water floating on adhesion, feel gently long as estimated softened; good not remove by pressing until lift from water — lest blood drop unnoticed; if adhesion fully removes no blood, test inflate lukewarm no bubble — permit as I wrote; do only expert God-fearing slaughterer gathering all experts — must be careful on lukewarm and heat not contract, and inflation proper; since incident came — good inform public gate, for Torah spares Israel's money.
