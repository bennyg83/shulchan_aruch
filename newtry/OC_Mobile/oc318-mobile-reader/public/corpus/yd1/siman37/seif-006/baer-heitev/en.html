Treifah (we do not surround bubbles — bubbles change — Shach; see Api Ravrevi). Taz wrote: but if he felt — rely lenient as siman 36 seif 5; must examine ribs; also incident Beit Yosef siman 39 — two saw slope bubble, inflated, agreed no surround; others inflated, permitted, tore, said better strength — Bach wrote for us unexamined animal forbidden; Taz: we can answer no contradiction, one inflates better — permitted; Moharach wrote unexamined forbidden — contradiction; but as I wrote no contradiction; siman 39 seif 5, 17 — even contradiction kosher (Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe).
