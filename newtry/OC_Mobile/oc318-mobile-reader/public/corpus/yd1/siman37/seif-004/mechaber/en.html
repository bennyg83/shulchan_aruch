A blister found in the slopes of the lung — they practice to forbid if there is no flesh whatsoever surrounding it on every side {Beur HaGra} (and specifically a blister; but tinnir is kosher in the slopes of the lung) (his own view and Mahari and Hagahot Maimoniyot). And slopes means the bottom of the ama or ona, but not the cuts between ona and ona. {Rama: And some say that wherever there is a sharp point it is called slopes of the lung (Mahariu, Semak, and Kol Bo); therefore one should declare treifah even above in the place where the lung adheres to the spine if it is opposite the cut of the onot where the lung is still sharp and called slopes; but opposite the onot — kosher, for there it is not slopes, for there the lung is wide (in examinations). And one may follow custom in this; and if there is no thread of flesh surrounding, they inflate it and feel toward the side of the blister — if through this a thread of flesh surrounds — kosher (Beit Yosef) {Shach} {Taz}: Provided the surrounding flesh has lung appearance {Baer Hetev} (Bedek HaBayit in the name of Mahara"k) {Shach}: And if there was no flesh appearance surrounding and afterward examiners came and inflated and felt, and through their expertise and strength they inflated well and felt, and made it that there was flesh surrounding — kosher (Beit Yosef siman 39 in the name of responsum of Rivash and an incident with the Rosh).}
