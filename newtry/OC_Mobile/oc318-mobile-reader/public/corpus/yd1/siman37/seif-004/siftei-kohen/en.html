In slopes, etc. This law has no root in Gemara — only from BeHag; poskim strained to find reason to settle his words because all his words are received tradition; and primary reason as Sefer HaChinuch and Semag and Hagahot Mordechai and other poskim — because it appears like two adjacent bubbles and concern for marit ayin; therefore they permitted tinnir in slopes.
Any amount. Implies even one hair-thread; and so Maharshal wrote; and so in examinations of acharonim.
And it must be surrounding, etc. And so in examinations of acharonim; and implies to exclude other appearances even permitted appearances below siman 38; unlike R' Tanchum at end of examinations of R"tz and Maharshal there who wrote in one hagahah that flesh-thread lung appearance is not needed; and he agreed since one hair suffices — if so certainly this has no lung appearance; and further since many geonim permit bubble in slopes — one may be lenient — end.
And if there was no surrounding, etc. Bach answered R' Yosef son of Rosh on an incident: two examined lung and saw bubble in slopes and agreed no flesh surrounded after inflation; afterward examiners came, inflated, permitted, tore bubble and said those who inflated before had better strength than first — therefore flesh surrounded; establish two against two; animal slaughtered in presumption of permission stands; rely on them that flesh surrounded and permitted — so Beit Yosef brought siman 39 and Beit Yosef agreed. However for us who are stringent to declare treifah animal not examined — animal stands in presumption of prohibition and forbidden; and so Maharshal Perek Kama of Chullin siman 21 — end. One may wonder he skipped R' Yosef son of Rosh's words — Beit Yosef brought his language there; also bubble in slopes was not mentioned except from the Gaon, not in Gemara; also "establish two" — if so even without reason of establish two — permit from reason that bubble in slopes is only from Gaon; and we do not add on it — specifically where we know certainly no surrounding thread; and further there is no contradiction in witnesses — perhaps last forgot better than first; and Maharshal deals explicitly with contradiction in adhesion, not slopes; also according to what below siman 39 s.k. 42 Maharshal's words do not exist — all the more here permitted, for we hold animal slaughtered in presumption of permission stands; and so Darkei Moshe brought responsum of R' Yosef and Beit Yosef in general.
