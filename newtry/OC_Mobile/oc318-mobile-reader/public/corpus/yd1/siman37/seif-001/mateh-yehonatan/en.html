(Siman 37 in the hagahah) And the custom is to permit. Shach s.k. 1. And even though above siman 36 seif 4 regarding lung poured out he ruled in the hagahah that only in a place of great loss one may permit murky and putrid — one may say that there there is also concern for bronchi that require examination whether they melted, unlike here where the lung was not so impaired. Hagahah of Maharash HaKohen n"y m"tz of the community of Vilna.
