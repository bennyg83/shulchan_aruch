And many permit even murky, etc. Meaning even in bubbles of mugla that are murky and putrid — likewise; and so is implied from poskim and acharonim and is simple; and even though above siman 36 seif 7 regarding lung poured out the Rav ruled in the hagahah that only in great loss one may permit murky and putrid — one may say that there too there is also concern for bronchi requiring examination whether they melted; and see s.k. 26 and s.k. 30.
