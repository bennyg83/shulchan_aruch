Adjacent, etc. forbidden. Rashi explained the reason: we hold they are adjacent only on account of puncture in the lung that raised the blisters; and Raah agreed in Beit Yosef daf 34; and Rambam ch. 7 explained because the matter is close that there are punctures between them and they have no way of examination; and Rashba and Ran and Rabbeinu Yerucham explained since they are adjacent to one another, because each presses its fellow, eventually the skin will puncture and it is as if punctured now; and in those types that are kosher — two adjacent bubbles — these reasons do not apply.
If they pour. Maharshal wrote Perek Aleph Tav siman 27 in the name of Maharil that even when pouring examination is required to see if each has a pouch by itself; if in two pouches with a break between — treifah, for since there is puncture between them that pour to one another — we fear puncture of lung, for from place of lung the blister arose, only afterward they drew near — and it is like membrane that arose from blow on lung and is not membrane — end; and so in Hagahot Mordechai — two adjacent bubbles have no examination even when pouring; and so implied in examinations of R"tz regarding what Machatzit HaShekel wrote: every bubble has a pouch and lies in it; and two bubbles appearing as one — puncture one and they pour to one another — certainly lung punctured between the two bubbles and treifah, etc. — see there (except he did not mention examination is required l'chatchila); and so appears the Rav's view and as written s.k. 33 and in Machberet HaChayim he wrote from Tosafot that from break of two hairs it is not considered adjacent — implies if no proper gap and they pour — kosher; his words are astonishing, for on the contrary from their writing "not considered adjacent" implies when no two hairs between — considered adjacent; and regarding adjacent it is certain even pouring — treifah, for otherwise Rava and poskim need not say one appearing as two; and so necessarily as we said.
Tzemachim, etc. They are called in Shas and poskim tinnir; and in responsum of the Rav simanim 5, 6, 7 a dispute was found with Maharshal — Maharshal's view: tinnir means hard with a little moisture, for if no moisture at all it is sealed as explained above siman 37 seif 9; and the Rav's view: even if no moisture at all, only completely hard like stone — nevertheless since its appearance resembles mugla appearance it is tinnir, as written in hagahah siman 36; and see there in responsum where he explained his reason; and so in examinations of acharonim.
Hard. Bach wrote: it appears the same when full of air — kosher in adjacent — end; and it appears proof, for poskim who declare treifah in clear water in adjacent only forbid from the reason the language of bubble applies — "water requested," as in Rosh; if so when full of air one cannot say thus.
And the same applies to morsa, etc. In all books this hagahah is written after "and these are when there is mugla or water in them"; therefore Lev Chaim daf 157a wrote in the name of Mahariu: morsa on lung — meaning lung membrane covers it and it is not higher than lung flesh — its law is like bubble with mugla that is treifah — end; and I did not stand on the end of his view, for the matter is simple it is a scribal error and this hagahah belongs after "but if they are tzemachim," etc. (and he answered: thus I hagah'd also in my Shulchan Aruch many years ago — end); and so explicitly in Mahariu and Darkei Moshe and Maharshal there and Bach — it is not a bubble to forbid in adjacency when lung membrane covers it, etc.; and Maharshal agreed there with correct reasons — see there; and so in Taz and Api Ravrevi and other acharonim.
That lung membrane covers, etc. Maharam wrote in Beit Yosef: morsa means what they call in Italian the appearance of moisture inside like precious stone called turquoise; they grow between skin and flesh even though membrane covering them has no lung appearance — only morsa appearance — kosher; but bubbles are normally inside flesh and are rot of flesh — therefore there flesh thread surrounding is required, etc. — see there; and it is not clear — if so if lung membrane does not cover them, are they adjacent and treifah? And so Rashba and R"tz whom I brought s.k. 28 — bubbles of Gemara are those growing between skin and flesh — "two adjacent" treifah; also we find no posek who distinguished — rather truth of the matter used term morsa, for presumably they are not accustomed to be higher than lung flesh; and so Maharshal Perek Aleph Tav siman 27 explained.
And it is not high, etc. And he wrote in Torat HaShelamim there: but if higher than measure of tarfa de-asa and has form of ona — forbids on account of extra — end; his words are astonishing, for what connection to extra of ona; rather simply he means if high — forbids on account of adjacency; therefore even if no tarfa de-asa and no ona form — forbids in adjacency.
Like two hairs. And Maharshal there ruled we require gap width of kesh; we are not expert between one hair and two; and poskim agreed to be lenient.
Lung appearance. Meaning to exclude mugla appearance; for even if among permitted appearances that will be explained siman 38 — since mugla quartered beneath it — it is adjacent.
And sweet. To exclude bitter or salty which are like murky — Mahariu there; and found in examinations of acharonim — see there Ram siman "what is clear water": whatever has a pouch by itself and peels from lung and no air exits when puncturing the bubble; and bubble skin is white inside — end; and it appears this sign was needed because Mahara"k root 36 wrote in name of Tosafot Rif that nowadays we are not expert between clear and murky — therefore they gave a sign even for us; and Semachot strained on this.
Custom, etc. Or according to matter of loss. And Maharshal there disagrees with custom and his view appears one may not permit even in great loss; and truly for law the words of forbidders are primary; and also according to primary reason that one presses its fellow — no distinction; and Bach wrote one who is stringent for himself — examine; therefore it appears what the Rav wrote "or according to loss and time of pressure" — specifically where both exist; not as Pri Megadim who wrote "or time of pressure."
Their law like adjacent. Meaning where they practice prohibition in clear water in adjacent — the same in slopes; and where they practice permission; but regarding surrounding thread it is not similar, for all agree in slopes one hair suffices as s.k. 22; and see s.k. 28.
Bubble on groove. On upper ona of right in place of groove — so in Machatzit HaShekel there.
For it is like two bubbles, etc. And accordingly in clear water one may be lenient where lenient; and Semachot wrote another reason — concern friction of bubbles on small ribs and gargeret — good to be stringent.
Between two onot — kosher. For there is gap between them; and even for reason one presses its fellow — nevertheless since onot are normally separate in animal's life they do not press one another.
