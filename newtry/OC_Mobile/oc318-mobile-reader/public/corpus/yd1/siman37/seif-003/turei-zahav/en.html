Adjacent to one another. Rashi's reason: we hold they are adjacent only from puncture in lung that raised these blisters; Rashba and Ran — since adjacent they press and eventually puncture — as if punctured now. Except what Rama wrote shortly bubble and tinnir kosher — implies like Rashi's view, not Rashba.
One and kosher. Maharshal wrote in name of Maharil — still requires examination whether each has pouch by itself — treifah.
But if they are tzemachim, etc. Meaning there is no moisture at all — this is called tinnir.
And the same morsa, etc. This hagahah belongs elsewhere even though adjacent kosher; Maharshal gave reason morsa kosher — not called bubble but protruding outward; and implies there is mugla in this morsa — nevertheless does not forbid in adjacency.
And it must be in what separates. Meaning not kosher when there is gap unless lung appearance; but if mugla appearance — like adjacent; Machatzit HaShekel: if upper skin of bubble peeled — lung skin — treifah, for lower skin is bubble skin from blow — even single bubble, even clear water.
And one may follow custom, etc. And where no clear custom — certainly forbid, for Beit Yosef wrote in name of most poskim to be stringent; and Maharshal ruled stringent in all cases.
