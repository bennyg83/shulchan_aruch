Forbidden. And the reason: they are adjacent only from puncture in lung.
And kosher. Taz in name of Maharshal: still requires examination whether each has pouch by itself — treifah even if pouring (and this is what they ask: how find lung-appearance break between bubble and bubble yet treifah though pouring: if punctured and did not pour and afterward inflated this one and fellow rose in inflation, or put water in one and fellow filled — kosher like two marot siman 43 — Kenesset HaGedolah daf 114).
Tzemachim. Meaning hard tzemachim is tinnir and no moisture at all (and same full of air kosher in adjacent — Shach and Bach).
Lung. Taz wrote this hagahah belongs elsewhere even adjacent kosher; Maharshal's reason: morsa kosher — not called bubble but outward protrusion; implies mugla in morsa — nevertheless does not forbid adjacent. Shach wrote: some explain morsa is called in Italian moisture inside like turquoise appearance, grow between skin and flesh — membrane has no lung appearance only morsa — kosher; not clear — if no lung membrane covering — adjacent treifah? And wrote: specifically when lung membrane covers and not higher than lung; but if high — forbids in adjacency even without tarfa de-asa or ona form.
Lung appearance. Taz explained: but if mugla appearance — like adjacent treifah; and found written: if upper bubble skin peeled — lung skin — treifah, for lower is bubble skin from blow — even single bubble, even clear water.
And sweet. Shach explained: exclude bitter or salty like murky; sign what is clear water: pouch by itself, peels from lung, no wind when puncturing, white skin inside (Pri Chadash: when examining bubbles poke with thorn not knife — iron spoils water; receive only in vessel not hand — see Api Ravrevi). Shach wrote this sign helps even for us; nevertheless requires study; Semachot strained.
Distance. Shach wrote Maharshal disagrees with custom — view one may not permit even in great loss; Bach likewise; therefore appears what Rav wrote "or according to loss and pressure" — specifically where both; not as Pri Megadim "or time of pressure."
In adjacent. Meaning where prohibit clear water in adjacent — same in slopes; where permit — permit; but surrounding thread — not similar, for all agree in slopes one hair suffices.
Treifah. Meaning bubble on upper right ona in groove place.
Adjacent. Shach wrote: accordingly in clear water lenient where lenient; however another reason — concern friction on small ribs and gargeret — good to be stringent (and if membrane covers bubble even on groove — no grain-friction concern, for meat covers bubble and covering meat must have lung appearance. Rama's distinction between two bubbles and bubble with tinnir: tinnir punctures bubble even without pressing; unlike two bubbles that do not puncture each other except through pressing; therefore kosher between onot with gap. Rama bubble and tinnir adjacent kosher — Pri Chadash unclear: since adjacent bubbles treifah from pressing, all the more bubble and tinnir — tinnir hard and punctures; Shach forbids; Kenesset HaGedolah brings in name of several poskim to permit — and so practice in examinations).
