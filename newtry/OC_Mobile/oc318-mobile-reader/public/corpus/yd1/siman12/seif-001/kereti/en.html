Into the pit—for thus did ancient idolaters: they collected blood into the pit and there they sat and performed sorcery and divination with it.
A pit—and even if it is not clean—nevertheless there is marit ayin.
A sloped place—and even if there is a garbage dump there, so that he could slaughter there—nevertheless the Sages permitted it.
A slope and to slaughter toward a vessel and from there the blood goes down into the pit—one should not permit, for they only permitted on a ship where it is impossible otherwise.
And in the market—it appears in a place where the city's ministers are strict and fine if they make a garbage dump in the market; certainly even in the market it is permitted when there is no marit ayin at all.
