They may not give her less than one hundred zuz. The charity administrators are compelled to borrow this amount. If money is available in the fund, they give her even more than one hundred zuz, in accordance with her dignity. Ran writes this, and Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, and Perishah cite him. It appears that they are not obligated to borrow more than one hundred zuz.
One hundred zuz. It appears that this measure, like all the measures in seif 4, applied only in their time. Nowadays, he is given what is appropriate, as stated below in siman 253, seif 2.
