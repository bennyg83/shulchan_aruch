The individual. The Bach wrote that an individual too is obligated to give him all that he lacks if his means allow, as above at the beginning of siman 249, and the proof is from Hillel the Elder, who took for a poor person of noble descent a horse to ride, etc. The Shach wrote that this is not conclusive, for one may say it speaks where the public was not with him, or where the public lacked the means to help him.
