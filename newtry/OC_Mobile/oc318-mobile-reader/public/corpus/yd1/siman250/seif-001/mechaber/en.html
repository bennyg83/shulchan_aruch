How much is fit to give to each and every one. And it contains 5 seifim. How much do we give to a poor person? Enough for his lack that he lacks — how so? If he was hungry, they feed him; if he needed clothing, they clothe him; if he had no household vessels, they buy him household vessels; and even if he was accustomed to ride a horse and have a servant run before him when he was wealthy, and the poor person — they buy him a horse and servant; and likewise for each and every one according to what he needs. What is fit to give him — bread, they give him bread; dough, they give him dough; a bed, they give him a bed; what is fit to give him hot bread — hot; cold — cold; to feed him into his mouth — they feed him. If he has no wife and comes to marry — they marry him off, rent him a house, furnish him a bed and his utensils, and afterward marry him a wife:
{Rama: And it appears that all this is with charity collectors or many together; but an individual is not obligated to give a poor person enough for his lack except he informs his distress to many; and if there are no many near him, the individual gives him if his hand reaches. (Beit Yosef, and not like the apparent meaning of Tur.) And as explained in siman 249.}
