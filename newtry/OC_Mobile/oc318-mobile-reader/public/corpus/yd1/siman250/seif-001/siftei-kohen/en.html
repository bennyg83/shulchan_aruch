But an individual is not, etc. Bach writes that an individual too is obligated to provide everything the poor person lacks if he has sufficient means, as stated above at the beginning of siman 249. Rambam likewise writes in chapter 7 of Hilkhot Matnot Aniyim: “You are commanded to give charity according to what the poor person lacks. If he has no clothing,” etc. In the chapter Metziat HaIshah, Kesubos 67, the Sages taught: “They said about Hillel the Elder that he purchased a horse for a poor man of noble descent to ride,” etc. It would therefore appear that every person with sufficient means is commanded to provide a poor person who does not solicit door to door with what that poor person lacks. None of this is conclusive, however. One can say that all these sources concern a situation in which the public is not present with him, or the public lacks sufficient means to assist him.
