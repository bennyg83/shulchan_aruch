One in two is nullified. See Pri Chadash who concludes that any amount more than half suffices; and see in Sefer Menachem Yehudah general rule 31 and general rule 39 — his view that d'rabbanan specifically requires double; and see Pri Megadim in the introduction who wrote that in d'rabbanan issur even Menachem Yehudah agrees that double is not required; and even in d'oraisa issur, one who is lenient with any amount more, like Pri Chadash — one should not object. [And see in responsum Chasam Sofer siman 99 from this, and brought somewhat below siman 110 s.k. 4] — and see in responsum Chinuch Beit Yosef siman 80 that his view is like Pri Chadash that double is not required even in Torah issur; and see there that he disagreed with one sage who explained Shach's words who wrote one in two is nullified — meaning in the majority; and even if there is no majority except in those two which are smaller than the issur, etc. — meaning that the two pieces together are smaller than the issur and there is only a majority in number, i.e. two parts against one, and not majority by volume — even so nullified; and he disagreed and explained that he meant that in those two there is also majority by volume, that the two are larger than the one — only double is not required; and he elaborated on this and wrote the practical ruling: if one said he has at home seven pieces — six heter and one issur — and went away, and we find seven pieces mixed and one of them is larger than the six remaining — we do not say it is nullified in six and not in sixty if the large one is larger in quantity than all six; and likewise if he said he has one heter piece of seven liters and six issur pieces each one liter, and went away, and meanwhile one came and cut the large piece into seven parts and they became mixed — nullified in the majority; and according to the words of that sage — not nullified, for even for leniency one large is nullified in two small ones, even though combined they are not like the large one — how much more so for stringency that the large heter piece is nullified in six issur pieces — see there further:
