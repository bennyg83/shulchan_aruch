(Siman 109 in the hagahah seif 1) And some are stringent to throw one out, etc. — and I saw Pri Chadash in Orach Chaim siman 447 who challenged those who say so, from what we taught in tractate Orlah: orlah and likewise terumah ascend in one in two hundred and removal is not required — and the same in dry when nullified in the majority, even in issur of benefit; and he wrote on Rashi's explanation, which is incorrect, who explained in chapter 1 of Beitzah regarding bundles of fenugreek of kohanim that they ascend in one in two hundred — meaning one of them rises and the rest are permitted by burning; and it appears this was hidden from him what Mordechai wrote on this in chapter 1 of Beitzah regarding nullification in the majority, that one must throw one of them out; and it is not comparable to terumah where everything is mixed together and one cannot eat the issur alone without mixture of heter — therefore removal is not required except on account of robbing the tribe — end. And with this one may also resolve the difficulty on Rashi's explanation regarding fenugreek from that mishnah in Orlah that removal is not required — it deals with things where one cannot eat the issur alone without mixture of heter, unlike pieces and bundles of fenugreek:
