The Rashba in Torat HaBayit. This follows the version before the Beit Yosef, as he wrote in the name of a responsum of the Rashba, who instructed that the original wording in Torat HaBayit be corrected. The Tur copied this ruling, and the Ran likewise wrote it in the seventh chapter of Chullin.
