Beit Yosef concludes Shach; and perhaps the hagahah refers to what he wrote that we require one in two — see there. But behold proof that the source of the hagahah is from Beit Yosef, and there it is stated explicitly thus at the end of the siman — see there; that he challenged what ShaDa wrote that in d'rabbanan issur one in one suffices — only there is no distinction in this; see Maharsha chapter 3 of Pesachim; and it appears that Rambam chapter 13 of Hilchot Terumot halachah 11 [and so chapter 6 of Hilchot Bikkurim law 12 — see there Kessef Mishneh that I aimed at his view with God's help] regarding terumah of chutz la'aretz which is d'rabbanan — one in one suffices, and he relied on Yerushalmi that Tosafot brought in Menachot (67:) and Raavad in hasagot disagrees and holds majority is required — and so appears to me in the Gemara Pesachim (98) where we say there "do not treat lightly the measures of the rabbis," and Rashi explained: even a matter whose issur is d'rabbanan — do not treat its measure lightly; and so poskim and Taz siman 98 seif 6 — it implies he means all measures, whether measure of sixty in wet or measure of majority in dry — do not treat lightly; and Rambam follows his reasoning that also regarding sixty in wet he treats lightly d'rabbanan issurim and holds the issur is from the set number — except challah siman 90 and siman 98 seif 8; if so, the same regarding nullification of dry in majority — we do not treat lightly, and requires study. It appears to me: if one in one became mixed and afterward one piece was broken into two — forbidden, lest the issur was broken; and even if afterward a heter piece was added — for if it had not been broken it would be permitted, as written in the hagahah siman 92 seif 5 that we do not say "the piece was lost" in dry; and if heter was added — permitted; but here forbidden, lest issur was broken and there is no majority of heter here; and as written siman 101 seif 6 that we do not rely to be lenient — here likewise — see there and requires study. And it appears further: if a piece worthy of honor became mixed with two heter pieces — we hold siman 101 that it is not nullified — and afterward one of them was cut into two — forbidden perforce: if it is heter, behold the issur is still complete, meaning standing and not nullified; and even if you say the issur was cut — one may not permit, for there is no majority of heter pieces to nullify it, for an issur piece increased, and we hold it returns and revives even in dry siman 99 seif 6 in the hagahah; and even if it increased from within, as proven in chapter 5 of Terumot and Beit Yosef at that seif regarding a se'ah of terumah that fell into less than a hundred of heter — they ground it and left over, etc. — see there and requires study. And what is written siman 101 that all that are cut are permitted — meaning heter pieces remained in majority, as proven there that he permitted only perforce — see there; unlike when there is no majority, there is no perforce.
