Nullifying issur. Since each one is permitted by itself — Ran. Forbidden to nullify issur on Shabbat or Yom Tov, for there is no greater repair than this; but permitted to estimate with sixty even if it became mixed on Erev Shabbat and he did not know until Shabbat.
