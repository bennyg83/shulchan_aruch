The prohibition of oto v'et beno is only through shechitah, as it says "you shall not slaughter"; but if he pierced (nichur—the meaning of nichur is inserting the knife in its nostrils and cutting) the first, or it became neveilah in his hand, it is permitted to slaughter the second; therefore a deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor who slaughtered the first among themselves—it is permitted to slaughter the second after them, for most of their acts are defective: {Rama: And if others see that they slaughter properly, it is forbidden to slaughter after them (Tur); so it appears to me.}
