(9) And if father and son slaughtered in one day—responsum Zikhron Yitzchak siman 101 wrote permitted to eat on that day the one slaughtered last, since matter doubtful whether applies with males as above seif 2; if so double doubt: perhaps does not apply and perhaps halachah follows those who say if transgressed and slaughtered, permitted that day to eat them; and even though in matter that will be clarified double doubt does not help, nevertheless since prohibition not for all world only for slaughterer, we do not judge as matter that will be clarified as Maharshal wrote Yevamot chapter HaAreil siman 34—see there; however Pithei Teshuvah "or" 2 wrote on Zikhron Yitzchak's words: we do not rule like Maharshal—for we hold below siman 102 seif 4 in hagahah one who vows from matter is called matter that will be clarified, and behold even now forbidden only to vower and nevertheless called matter that will be clarified—end; and so Shofar below siman 102 "or" 2; Zivchei Tzedek "or" 6.
