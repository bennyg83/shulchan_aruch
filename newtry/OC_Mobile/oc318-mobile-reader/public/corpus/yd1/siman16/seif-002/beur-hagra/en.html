(Seif 2) And if known, etc. From doubt we are not concerned—therefore he stated there 83a "I sold the mother, etc." Rosh; and see there.
(Likut) And if known with certainty, etc. Specifically such a case; and this is what he stated "I sold the mother" and no difficulty father; and so throughout the chapter because it is uncommon to be known. Rosh 82 (end).
For the matter is doubtful, etc. As written end of Ketubot halachah follows R' Yehudah in mules; and we say in Chullin there 79a we doubt; and so R' Eliezer and Rabbis there hold.
