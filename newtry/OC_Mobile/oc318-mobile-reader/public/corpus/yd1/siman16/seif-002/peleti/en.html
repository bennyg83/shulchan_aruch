This is doubtful—we doubt whether concerned for father's seed; and there is doubt whether permitted to slaughter the father at bein hashemashos—and this too is doubtful, as written in seif 4; and if so it is double doubt: doubt we are not concerned for father's seed; and if you say we are concerned, lest yesterday or tomorrow is the day; and see Ran Sukkah—it appears at bein hashemashos at edges of day it is not doubtful, for we depend it is day—and the matter requires study and is not its place; and I elaborated in Magen Avraham.
