(Seif 10) And if the fetus went out alive after its mother's shechitah and spread on ground—they do not slaughter it in one day; and if he slaughtered he does not receive lashes. Plain: if mother found treifah—for regarding fetus it is not shechitah and requires d'oraisa shechitah as siman 13 seif 3; and regarding oto v'et beno it is shechitah of treifah shechitah—for we hold like R' Meir in sugya chapter Kisuy HaDam (daf 86) as Bach and Acharonim s.k. 9; and if so if he slaughtered offspring that day—he receives lashes.
