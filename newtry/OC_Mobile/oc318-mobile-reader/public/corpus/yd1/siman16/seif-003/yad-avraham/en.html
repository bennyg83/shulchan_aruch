(Seif 12) Animal whose mother or daughter was slaughtered that day and became mixed and must slaughter from them that day—pen them, etc. See siman 110 seif 6 in hagahah; and he was precise to say "and must slaughter" etc.—for otherwise forbidden, for it is matter that has permission tomorrow.
Shach wrote the reason it is not nullified in majority: live animals are important and not nullified, as below siman 110; and he did not analyze that it is matter that will be clarified; and practical difference: if he transgressed and slaughtered them intentionally—forbidden for that person to eat that day as seif 3, and not nullified for him if this mixture mixed into a second mixture—for we hold siman 110 seif 8 in hagahah forbidden in matter that will be clarified; and if it were not matter that will be clarified, even though live animals not nullified and does not help if he slaughtered intentionally—for he is nullifying prohibition l'chatchila as siman 110 seif 2 and siman 99 and siman 101 seif 6; nevertheless nullified in sixty like other prohibitions—for what is permitted for others by nullifying prohibition as siman 99 seif 5 is not matter that will be clarified, since for him forbidden forever as s.k. 102; and even though I wrote it deals for need and without need permitted even in matter that will be clarified as proved there siman 110 seif 8 in hagahah—nevertheless specifically dry mixture in dry where there are two doubts: perhaps of permitted mixed into second mixture; and if you say of prohibition, perhaps what one eats is not prohibition; but when mixed in cooking, certainly no second doubt and forbids in majority; and further per R' Yehudah and Shulchan Aruch there only mixture of three permitted—see there and examine; and one may say Shach's words: he holds it deals where mixed before mother or daughter slaughtered so prohibition never recognizable—and such we hold not matter that will be clarified in Orach Chaim siman 320, as Noda Biyehudah Maarechet Chadash siman 8 per Magen Avraham Orach Chaim siman 513—but this is not agreed as Magen Avraham Laws of Me'ilah wrote, as I wrote above siman 15.
And know: if he slaughtered the mixture b'shogeg—permitted even for him, for we do not penalize shogeg like intentional where he did not transgress d'oraisa—for from Torah one in two nullifies, as stated explicitly in sugya Gittin (daf 54) and Beit Yosef brought siman 99; and so Taz there siman 101 seif 6 and siman 110 seif 2: if slaughtered b'shogeg, nullified and we do not penalize shogeg like intentional unless he transgressed d'oraisa, such as not through mixture in seif 3 and Orach Chaim siman 318 regarding cooking on Shabbat b'shogeg forbidden that day—see there.
And I saw responsum Chayei Adam siman 133 who wrote: per Rambam there is no prohibition when he transgressed and slaughtered, as Gemara Chullin (daf 109) "here there is no prohibition of nullification"—neither because of live animal importance nor matter that will be clarified; for it applies only in thing forbidden in eating or benefit, unlike here where impossible prohibition of slaughter l'chatchila.
