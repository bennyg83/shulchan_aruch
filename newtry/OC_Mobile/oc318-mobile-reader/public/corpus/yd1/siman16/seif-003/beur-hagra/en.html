(Seif 3) If he transgressed, etc. As written beginning of chapter "both are valid and the second valid"; and there 115a "oto v'et beno is not forbidden, etc."
And there are those who forbid, etc. From that which they say there 82a: tanna—if the first preceded, etc.—implies otherwise he did two prohibitions and may not eat in Israel; and what mishnah states "valid" is by Torah law, and even rabbinically after that day; but for that day forbidden like act of Shabbat; and as written 14a his shechitah is valid and they said in Gemara forbidden in eating that day; and so there 146a—also by Torah forever, as written there also on act of Shabbat; "but from now," etc.
