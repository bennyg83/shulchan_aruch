Permitted to eat them, etc. In the Gemara they say: even though it is written "you shall not eat any abomination," and we expound "anything I made abominable to you"—here it is different, for since the Merciful One forbade it lacking time for the Most High—that is, before the eighth day—from this it is permitted to an ordinary person here lacking time; and one view is in Beit HaGadol: he holds to forbid in any case on that day because of penalty for doing a transgression similar to cooking on Shabbat—and the Gemara did not exclude except that it is not forbidden forever from force of "any abomination."
