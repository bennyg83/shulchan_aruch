And there are those who forbid. See responsum Zikhron Yitzchak siman 101 who wrote: if he slaughtered father and son in one day—permitted to eat on that day the one slaughtered last, since the matter is doubtful whether applies with males as above seif 2; if so double doubt: perhaps it does not apply and perhaps halachah follows those who say if he transgressed and slaughtered, permitted that day to eat them; and even though in matter that will be clarified double doubt does not help, nevertheless since prohibition is not for all the world only for the slaughterer himself, we do not judge it as matter that will be clarified, as Maharshal wrote Yevamot chapter HaAreil siman 34—see there; and in my humble opinion we do not rule like Maharshal—for we hold below siman 102 seif 4 in hagahah one who vows from a matter is called matter that will be clarified, and behold even now forbidden only to the vower and nevertheless called matter that will be clarified; and see on this responsum Noda Biyehudah Tinyana part Yoreh Deah siman 53 [and see Pri Megadim below siman 102 in Shaarei Teshuvah s.k. 2].
