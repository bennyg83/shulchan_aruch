But the first in any case is permitted—for at the time they slaughtered it no prohibition was created at all; and the reason the last is forbidden—the Ran wrote is because of a penalty (as explained in Orach Chaim siman 318 regarding Shabbat); and it appears specifically we penalize him, but for others it is permitted; and so appears Perishah's view, and so appears the language of Beit HaGadol that the Rosh brought: one who slaughters oto v'et beno may not eat the last on that day—end of his words; and so appears from the proof the Rosh brought for this law from Rav Yosef—see there.
