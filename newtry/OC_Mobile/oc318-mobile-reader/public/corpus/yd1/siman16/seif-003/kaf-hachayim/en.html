(4) There. Whether permitted to slaughter father or offspring at bein hashemashos—for double doubt: perhaps we are not concerned for father's seed; and if you say concerned, lest yesterday or tomorrow is the day—and the matter requires study. Peleti "or" 1; but responsum Zikhron Yitzchak siman 101 attacked him: for we hold in matter that will be clarified we do not permit from double doubt; if so for oto v'et beno prohibition double doubt does not help—for it will be clarified after dark—see there. Pithei Teshuvah "or" 1; but b'dieved if already slaughtered at bein hashemashos—they are permitted even per view of those who forbid in seif 3; Zivchei Shamayim "or" 1; Zivchei Tzedek "or" 2.
(5) [Seif 3] And there are those who forbid on that day, etc. And so agreed Perishah "or" 3; Semag "or" 3; Shofar "or" 3; Shulchan Gavoah end "or" 4; Machber "or" 5; handful "or" 6; Zivchei Ratzon siman 16 "or" 3; Zivchei Shamayim "or" 2; Zivchei Tzedek "or" 3.
(6) And even if slaughtered b'shogeg—forbidden. Peri Chadash "or" 3; Kereti "or" 3; Semag there; Birkei Yosef "or" 4; handful there; Zivchei Brit there "or" 7; Zivchei Tzedek "or" 4; and this shogeg they are stringent about specifically one who says permitted; but shogeg who did not know it is oto v'et beno—penalty does not apply. Shenot Chaim "or" 3; Daat Torah "or" 4; Rabbenu Yerucham "or" 8; and see below "or" 11.
(7) And this is specifically for the slaughterer himself forbidden, but for others permitted. Shach s.k. 3; Peri Chadash there; Lechem HaPanim "or" 4; Beit Lechem Yehudah "or" 1; Shofar "or" 3; Zivchei Brit there; place of Samuel in chiddushim of laws for Yoreh Deah this siman; however Semag there wrote to be stringent even for others; and so Erech HaShulchan there; handful there; Zivchei Rash there; Mani "or" 3; but Zivchei Tzedek "or" 5 wrote: since for unspecified Shulchan Aruch for him also permitted, we judge one may be stringent for him but for others permitted; however one who slaughters and sells to others should be stringent that they not benefit from his act, as Semag there; and so handful there; Mani there; and in hot places where if left until tomorrow it will spoil—it appears one may be lenient to sell that day.
(8) There hagahah. To eat the last. But the first in any case permitted—for at slaughter no prohibition was created; and reason last forbidden—Ran wrote because of penalty, as in Orach Chaim siman 318 regarding Shabbat. Shach there; Peri Chadash there; Zivchei Brit there.
