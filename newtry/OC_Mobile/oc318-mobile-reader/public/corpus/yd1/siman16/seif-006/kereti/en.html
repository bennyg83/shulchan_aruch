On seller to warn buyer—all year obligated to warn, for they are certainly slaughtered; and if he did not inform—mistaken transaction, for he can claim slaughter tomorrow; and so Shach.
Erev Pesach—not first day of festival, because they are busy eating in sukkah, etc. Tosafot.
In what case, etc.—Peri Chadash elaborated: if purchase was on one day mother and son only two days before Yom Tov—whether must inform; and since among us this does not apply, I did not copy his words to debate; and Peri Chadash wrote: if known they slaughter on one day in our time—must inform; and this is plain.
