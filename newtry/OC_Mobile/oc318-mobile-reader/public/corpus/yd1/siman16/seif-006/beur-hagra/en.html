(Seif 6) On the seller to warn, etc. Tur; and his view: all year must warn; but in four seasons even if first sold without specification must inform; and also mistaken transaction; unlike other days of year one could say "I did not sell you for slaughter today."
And if (he did) not inform, etc. From that which it says must inform the seller and not the buyer—and more one must warn the one causing another to stumble; but certainly the buyer need not be concerned, for we do not establish prohibition; and it is double doubt if it has a mother and it exists and was sold, and today sold; and this is what he stated "whether bought from non-Jew"—otherwise one would have to forbid buying from non-Jew forever, even all year lest sold today for slaughter, and a non-Jew has no testimony.
In what case, etc. Per Rashi there s.v. for law, etc.
