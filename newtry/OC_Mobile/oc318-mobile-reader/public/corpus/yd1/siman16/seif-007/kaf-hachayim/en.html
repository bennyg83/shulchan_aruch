(7) And this is specifically for the slaughterer himself forbidden, but for others permitted. Shach s.k. 3; Peri Chadash there; Lechem HaPanim "or" 4; Beit Lechem Yehudah "or" 1; Shofar "or" 3; Zivchei Brit there; place of Samuel in chiddushim of laws for Yoreh Deah this siman; however Semag there wrote to be stringent even for others; and so Erech HaShulchan there; handful there; Zivchei Rash there; Mani "or" 3; but Zivchei Tzedek "or" 5 wrote: since for unspecified Shulchan Aruch for him also permitted, we judge one may be stringent for him but for others permitted; however one who slaughters and sells to others should be stringent that they not benefit from his act, as Semag there; and so handful there; Mani there; and in hot places where if left until tomorrow it will spoil—it appears one may be lenient to sell that day.
