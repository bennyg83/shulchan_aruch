Whether permitted to slaughter father or offspring at bein hashemashos—for double doubt: perhaps we are not concerned for father's seed; and if you say concerned, lest yesterday or tomorrow is the day—and the matter requires study. Peleti "or" 1; but responsum Zikhron Yitzchak siman 101 attacked him: for we hold in matter that will be clarified we do not permit from double doubt; if so for oto v'et beno prohibition double doubt does not help—for it will be clarified after dark—see there. Pithei Teshuvah "or" 1; but b'dieved if already slaughtered at bein hashemashos—they are permitted even per view of those who forbid in seif 3; Zivchei Shamayim "or" 1; Zivchei Tzedek "or" 2.
