And if he slaughtered at bein hashemashos first on fourth day—whether permitted to slaughter second at bein hashemashos beginning fifth day; and per Tosafot chapter 2 of Shabbat daf 34b—permitted in any case: if this bein hashemashos is day, also second is day, or both night—and then in any case there is no one day; but per Rashi there and Rambam chapter 1 of Laws of Offerings—it could be one day and one night, and therefore forbidden.
