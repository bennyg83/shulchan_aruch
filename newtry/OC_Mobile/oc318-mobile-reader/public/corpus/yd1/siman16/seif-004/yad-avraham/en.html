(Siman 16 seif 4) And if he slaughtered the first at bein hashemashos of night of fifth, he may not slaughter the second until night of sixth. For perhaps at bein hashemashos it is night and therefore forbidden to slaughter all of fifth day; and it appears this is with mother and offspring—for the whole seif deals with this since it mentions lashes; and with father and son there are no lashes as seif 2; but if father slaughtered at bein hashemashos of night of fifth, he may slaughter son that day—for double doubt: perhaps at bein hashemashos it is day and night does not follow day; and perhaps oto v'et beno does not apply at all with males, for we doubt whether applies with males or not as seif 2; and such double doubt helps even in matter that has permission, as Shach wrote siman 110 s.k. 55; and as I wrote double doubt s.k. 14; and measure of bein hashemashos see Orach Chaim siman 262 that it is like quarter hour before stars; and see what I wrote in chiddushim Shabbat (daf 34).
