The "one day" stated in oto v'et beno—the day follows the night. How so? If he slaughtered the first at the beginning of the night of the fourth day, he may not slaughter the second until the beginning of the night of the fifth day; and if he slaughtered the first at the end of the fourth day before bein hashemashos (twilight), he may slaughter the second at the beginning of the night of the fifth day; and if he slaughtered the first at bein hashemashos of the night of the fifth day, he may not slaughter the second until the night of the sixth day; and if he slaughtered on the fifth day, he does not receive lashes.
