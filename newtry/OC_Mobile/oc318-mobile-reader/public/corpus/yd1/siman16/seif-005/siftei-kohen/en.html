If it is attached after her, presumption she is its mother. Meaning: for the prohibition; but for lashes, attachment does not help until we know with certainty she is its mother; and so Maharshal chapter Oto V'et Beno siman 8; and so explicitly in the Talmud in Bekhorot (daf 24a): even attached after her and nursing from her—it is forbidden and he does not receive lashes; and this is what he wrote in seif 2 "the prohibition of oto v'et beno applies with females—for that one is certainly its offspring"—meaning for lashes, as appears there; and see above siman 15.
But from doubt one is not concerned. Even for the prohibition—even if it greatly resembles the mother in appearance or in other bodily signs—so too Maharshal there.
