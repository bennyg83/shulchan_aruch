(Seif 10) Permitted to slaughter the pregnant animal. If mother found treifah—there is remedy for offspring if its months completed, permitted to slaughter it—plain forbidden to slaughter offspring that day for oto v'et beno; however even if offspring treifah, in my humble opinion one may argue permitted to slaughter offspring—for we hold shechitah that is not valid—its name is shechitah, meaning because shechitah helps purify from neveilah status, unlike here where even if fetus died it would not impart tumah as neveilah, for regarding this mother's shechitah helps, as stated explicitly Chullin (daf 75b); and if so slaughter of treifah fetus did not help at all, and one may say in such case it is not called shechitah.
