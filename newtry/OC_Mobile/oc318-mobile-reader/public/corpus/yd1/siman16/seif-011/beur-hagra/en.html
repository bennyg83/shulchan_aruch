(Seif 11) Non-Jew, etc. For there is no testimony of non-Jew, as written chapter 4 of Yevamot (47a) "for your words you are a non-Jew," etc.; and what is written end of Yevamot: specifically to enhance his sale he is not believed—here he is believed because sale in his hand and believed through migo if he wished he would not sell or not sell cheaply; and this is what he stated "after he sold them," etc.
And if believed to him, etc. As written regarding single witness chapter 3 of Kiddushin (66a): even though not believed, if you believe him, etc.; and likewise anyone not believed.
