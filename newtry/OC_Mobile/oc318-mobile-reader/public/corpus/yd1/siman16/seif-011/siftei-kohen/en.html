He is not believed. For a non-Jew is not believed in any testimony, as below siman 127 s.k. 19; and Atzat HaZahav wrote the reason: to enhance his sale he says so; and he did not aim well—for here enhancing his sale does not apply at all; if it applied he would not be believed even before he sold them; rather for this he is believed before he sold them through migo that if he wished he would not sell them or would cheapen them; but where enhancing his sale applies, even if he has migo he is not believed—as explained in responsum of Rashba siman 118, source of this law; however from words of Rosh and Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoniyot I brought in siman 127 there it is explained: where it is in his possession he is believed even where one could say he says so to enhance his sale—and see there.
If he is believed to him—it is forbidden. Even if not speaking incidentally—so in responsum of Rashba there; and see these laws in siman 127 s.k. 14.
