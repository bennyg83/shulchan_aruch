His possession. And Taz wrote: but as long as he did not sell, he is believed—for enhancing his sale does not apply here he said so; however from words of several poskim it appears: as long as in his possession he is believed even where one could say enhancing his sale.
Forbidden. And Shach wrote: even if not speaking incidentally; and see below siman 127; and if at time of sale he said so—believed; and if it appears he does so to enhance his sale—nothing; and see siman 127 where in his possession he is believed nevertheless.
