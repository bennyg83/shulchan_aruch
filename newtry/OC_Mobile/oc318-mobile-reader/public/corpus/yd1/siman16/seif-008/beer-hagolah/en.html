Statement of Rav Chisda there 79 regarding "sheep the Merciful One said" and its son—all.
Rambam's language there chapter 12; and Tur brought it and wrote it appears permitted l'chatchila; and Rashba wondered on him in chiddushim and Terumat HaDeshen and concluded there: if sheep the Merciful One said—even deer lashes too; if sheep the Merciful One said and not deer—whence prohibition; and that which we say regarding he-goat that came on doe—for prohibition we must explain: he-goat that came on doe and bore daughter and daughter bore son, as we established at the beginning—end.
And so also in Rambam there law 9; and it is astonishing—for we hold like Rabbis who doubt whether concerned for father's seed, as Rambam wrote there law 11 and Beit Yosef brought above seif 2; and further Rambam wrote in chapter 9 of Laws of Kilayim similarly lashes; and Kessef Mishneh concluded there: better to read "he does not receive lashes."
