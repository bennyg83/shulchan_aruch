Taz and Shach wondered on this law and wrote: did we not say in the Gemara we doubt whether concerned for father's seed—why lashes here, lest not concerned and this female had no father's seed at all and was entirely deer; requires study; and practical difference even for us in what they slaughter—for then disqualified for testimony from Torah, as we hold Choshen Mishpat siman 34; and likewise kiddushin of woman with non-Jew siman 42; and in my responsum I wrote on the margin a correct answer: specifically regarding this law Rambam holds thus because of scriptural decree.
