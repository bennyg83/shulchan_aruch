A deer that came upon a goat and gave birth—and he slaughtered the goat and its son—he receives lashes; but a goat that came upon a doe—they are forbidden to slaughter her and her son; and if he slaughtered, he does not receive lashes; if that daughter of the doe was female and gave birth to a son, and he slaughtered the female daughter of the doe and her son—he receives lashes.
