(Seif 10) After, etc. There 74a; and like Sages; and in Tosefta chapter 4: one who slaughters the animal and found in it nine-month live fetus, etc., and liable for oto v'et beno per R' Meir; and Sages say: not forbidden for oto v'et beno, as it says "it and its son you shall not slaughter"—that which requires shechitah is forbidden for oto v'et beno, and that which does not require shechitah is permitted for oto v'et beno.
