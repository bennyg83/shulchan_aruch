It is hard for me—for oto v'et beno is matter that will be clarified and we do not say whatever is separated from majority, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 513 s.k. 13; and I found responsum Noda Biyehudah Tinyana part Yoreh Deah siman 8 in hagahah of Maharam Ben Chaviv who brought this challenge in name of his father in his book Tzelach on Beitzah 4:10—and he may his light shine wrote to settle per Orach Chaim siman 320 (and so below siman 102 seif 4 in hagahah): any prohibition not recognizable before it became mixed is nullified even though matter that will be clarified—one may say it deals where mother mixed before daughter's slaughter so prohibition not recognizable before mixture; and what Shulchan Aruch wrote "animal whose mother was slaughtered, etc., and became mixed"—intent: already mixed before slaughter—end; indeed I saw responsum Chatam Sofer siman 133 who wrote: in such case where mother mixed with others first and afterward slaughtered daughter—permitted afterward to slaughter from mixture without penning, for a thing that has permission is plainly not nullified except even importance of live animal does not help after mixture when no prohibition existed at mixture time; and he brought from Tosafot Temurah daf 30 end s.v. and the other: it appears sufficient in our case to separate one animal and slaughter all others—for since prohibition was not clear before mixture only after mixture prohibition arose, we rely on selection; and all the more if he slaughtered first one from mixture, permitted afterward to slaughter daughter; and see Chatam Sofer further he doubted essence of this law even if mixed after slaughtering daughter—and his view one in two nullifies and need not pen at all, even though live animal and matter that will be clarified—nevertheless Sages were stringent only in thing forbidden because of itself, unlike here where nothing happened to this animal only because mother or daughter slaughtered today—not stringent with live animal importance and matter that will be clarified; and Beit HaGadol goes by reasons that he holds to forbid eating last that day and therefore Beit HaGadol combined two laws reciprocally and Rosh brought them (chapter Oto V'et Beno siman 5)—unlike Rambam's view as sugya goes in chapter 25 daf 115 that in eating permitted—if so plain no place to say live animal importance and matter that will be clarified after no forbidden thing at all only Merciful One warned not to slaughter it; and if mother became mixed, nullified in majority and no prohibition name on mixed thing at all; and further since we exist, etc.—see there; and see further Noda Biyehudah there what he wrote to settle why here in oto v'et beno we rule pen them and in Orach Chaim siman 496 seif 2 ruled in case of two found three forbidden and did not write pen them, and wrote several answers [and see responsum Chasam Sofer seif 37 and examine].
