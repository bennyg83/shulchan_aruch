(Seif 12) How is it remedied; and if first the mixture occurred and afterward he slaughtered the mother of one from the mixture. One may say nullified in majority since prohibition was born in the mixture, even important thing and live animal nullified like matter that will be clarified below (siman 102)—so Chatam Sofer (siman 133); and ostensibly hidden from sugya Temurah (daf 30) "take out one for the dog and the rest all permitted"—why not challenge to permit all, for dog's portion nullifies prohibition in majority in the rest—for prohibition was born in mixture; and perhaps one may say: according to those who hold there is selection, it is as if possible to clarify the prohibition—for they can take one and say this is the prohibition; therefore no nullification rule; and with this resolved Tosafot's challenge there: according to Rabbenu Tam how does he challenge plainly, for we hold one in two—and per my words the challenge is in any-case reasoning: at minimum need forbid only one—for if one in two, all permitted by nullification; and if there is selection, take out one and rest permitted; and therefore the law should be that even per Chatam Sofer above, nevertheless must take one of them and set aside until tomorrow—for since we hold in rabbinic matters there is selection—and this is because we doubt whether law of selection exists or one in two, as Ran wrote chapter 3 of Gittin; and if so here perhaps there is selection and possible to clarify prohibition and not nullified at all, as I wrote; and when we come to this, one may say even without Chatam Sofer's reasoning and as implied from Rama's ruling who did not write so, only regarding matter that will be clarified (siman 102): one may say since never called prohibition name on it, no reason to say wait until eaten with prohibition; but regarding important thing in any case not nullified—nevertheless permission exists by taking one of them—for from Torah all permitted by nullification, only rabbinically forbidden for live animals not nullified and we rely on selection as we hold in rabbinic matters; and specifically when mother slaughtered and afterward became mixed—for there selection does not apply, as Tosafot Temurah wrote like any prohibition mixed in permission; but when prohibition born in mixture, permitted by taking one of them. And with this also resolved Tosafot Temurah's challenge: therefore he challenges "take out one"—according to view we hold in rabbinic matters there is selection; and since from Torah nullified, it is rabbinic; and resolved above challenge.
