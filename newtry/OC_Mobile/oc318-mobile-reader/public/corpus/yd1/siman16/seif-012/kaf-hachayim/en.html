(12) [Seif 4] The one day stated in oto v'et beno, etc. This R' Shimon ben Zoma expounded: said in Bereishit "one day" and said in oto v'et beno "one day"—as the one day in Bereishit, the day follows the night, so the one day in oto v'et beno, the day follows the night; but in kodashim the night follows the day, as written "on the day of his offering he shall eat, he shall not leave of it until morning"—apparently the night after it is day of his offering until morning. Chullin chapter 3 81a.
