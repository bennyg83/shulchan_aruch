Whether whole, etc. Language of the Tur — meaning: not only when whole, according to the tanna of a litra of pressed figs, which otherwise would not be nullified, as stated above siman 86 seif 3; but even when beaten, and like water and salt in dough (Beitzah 39a) — there.
And even if there is doubt, etc. Gemara there.
And it was mixed, etc. Tur; and as Mordechai wrote in seif 3 of Avodah Zarah, and he derived from this that a double doubt is forbidden regarding something that has a permitted aspect; and as stated above siman 110 seif 8 in the hagahah; and Rosh in responsum part 7 of Avodah Zarah wrote that a double doubt helps; and all the more so in siman 293 seif 3 in the hagahah — only because of one doubt in the mixture — see there beginning general rule 2; and it depends on the dispute in siman 110 seif 9 in Shulchan Aruch and hagahah; and not like Rashba who wrote "and if it was mixed" refers to certainty specifically; but in Orach Chayim siman 513 stam, like the opinion of Rashba.
And if it was mixed, etc. As written in Nedarim 58a — everything that has a permitted aspect, such as tevel, etc.; and we say in chapter 3 of Challah that tevel forbids, etc.; and there 52a regarding one who vowed, etc. — even though it is something that has a permitted aspect; and as written there 59a; and so in Yerushalmi there; and we learn from this: this general rule was Rashba's because of R' Yehoshua — everything that has a permitted aspect, such as tevel; and what he wrote regarding hekdesh and chadash — the Sages did not give a measure except in its kind in any amount and not in its kind in sixty; and everything that has no permitted aspect, such as terumah, etc. — these vows, what do you do with them? As something that has a permitted aspect or as something that has no permitted aspect? Reasonably we act as something that has a permitted aspect, for we learned there that the elder uproots the vow from its root, etc.; and this Mishnah does for them as something that has no permitted aspect, for we learned that one who vowed from a matter and it was mixed — if there is sixty in it, it is forbidden; resolve in its kind, not in its kind; and Tosafot chapter 5 of Yevamot 38a s.v. "and let it be nullified," etc.; and one can say, etc.
However, etc. Tosafot there 38a s.v. "because," etc.; and one can say, etc.
And in Orach Chayim, etc. There seif 3 — anything for appearance, etc. — meaning: everything that comes to fix, whether for appearance or for taste, is as in its kind; but on account of appearance without something that has a permitted aspect it does not forbid; and Rashi in Zevachim 74a s.v. "and where," etc.; and even though they raised a doubt in Bava Kamma 101a whether appearance is significant and it was not resolved — poskim ruled it is a doubt, as Shulchan Aruch Choshen Mishpat end of seif 363; and there Rava distinguishes between d'oraisa and d'rabbanan — it appears that d'oraisa appearance is significant, and all the more so for R' Yochanan in Niddah 62b — one can say even d'rabbanan; and Tosafot there s.v. "from leniencies," etc.; nevertheless all this is in prohibition of benefit; and as written there "benefit that is visible," etc. — appearance helps, unlike Peri Chadash who erred here in all this.
