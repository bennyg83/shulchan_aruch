Something that has a permitted aspect. See what Peleti wrote — why did it enter his mind to be lenient in a doubt of chadash on 16 Nisan — see there.
Even if there is doubt, etc. This is regarding an egg laid on Yom Tov after Shabbat, where there is a Torah prohibition — then a double doubt does not help, for there is doubt in the item itself and sixty in the mixture; but regarding an egg laid on ordinary Yom Tov, which is only a rabbinic decree of Yom Tov after Shabbat — we are not stringent in a double doubt; so is the opinion of Magen Avraham; and see what Peleti wrote in resolving the Gemara.
Not in its kind — regarding something that has a permitted aspect all agree specifically in its kind; and the reason — see what Peleti wrote in the name of Ran.
Nullified in sixty — and everyone follows the name, since it is forbidden in any amount without sixty.
If one whitened it — and Peri Chadash disagrees, that appearance in a rabbinic matter has no substance; and see what Peleti wrote — his words are not correct, for everyone agrees that since it came for appearance it is as its kind.
