(Siman 102 seif 1 in the hagahah) However, if one whitened it in food or placed it in a pot to fix, it is not nullified. The reason poskim wrote: everything that needs one another is called for this purpose its kind, like water and salt in dough stated in the Gemara and poskim at the end of Beitzah (daf 39) and in Orach Chayim siman 513 — see there at length.
