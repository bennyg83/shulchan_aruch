The law of something that has a permitted aspect. And it has 4 seifim: Everything that has a permitted aspect, such as an egg laid on Yom Tov that is fit for tomorrow — if it was mixed with others, whether whole or beaten, it is not nullified even in a thousand; and even if there is a doubt whether it was laid on Yom Tov and it was mixed with others they are forbidden; and if it was mixed with what is not its kind it is nullified in sixty (Tur and Sh"D and responsa of Rosh and R' Yerucham and Hagahot Sh"D and Rashal and Rokeach and as stated Smag and Smak in laws of Yom Tov and likewise Or Zarua general rule 26 in the name of Smag and Taz general rule 43 and not like Bach): {Rama: However, if one whitened it in food or placed it in a pot to fix the pot, such as one filled it with chicken, it is not nullified (Hagahot Sh"D and Arukh); see Orach Chayim siman 513.}
