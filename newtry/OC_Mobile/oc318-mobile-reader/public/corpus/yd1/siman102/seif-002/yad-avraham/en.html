(Seif 3) A vessel that became forbidden through absorption of prohibition and was mixed — it is nullified in the majority and we do not judge it like something that has a permitted aspect. This is specifically that it absorbed a prohibition that has no permitted aspect — it is not like something that has a permitted aspect what becomes permitted through hagalah, because one must expend outlay, etc.; but a vessel that absorbed a prohibition that has a permitted aspect — see Shach and poskim.
