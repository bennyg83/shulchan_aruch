There are those who say that they only said something has a permitted aspect when the permitting matter will certainly come in any event, or if the permitting matter is in his hand to do without loss; but something that is not in his hand and it is not certain that the permitting matter will come — it is not in the law of something that has a permitted aspect; therefore an egg of doubtful tereifah that was mixed with others is not in the law of something that has a permitted aspect, because the permitting matter is not certain and is not in his hand.
