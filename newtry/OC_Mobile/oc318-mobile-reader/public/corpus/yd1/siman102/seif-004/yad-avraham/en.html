(There in the hagahah) If it remains forbidden for one forever even though permitted for others, such as one who cooks on Shabbat — it is not called something that has a permitted aspect. And see Magen Avraham in Orach Chayim siman 318 — it deals with mixing after Shabbat; for if on Shabbat it is literally something that has a permitted aspect after Shabbat relative to others; and similarly regarding what is written in siman 318 — see there.
