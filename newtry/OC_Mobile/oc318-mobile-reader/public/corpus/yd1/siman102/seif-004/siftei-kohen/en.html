That something that has a permitted aspect is not nullified, etc.; but its taste is nullified. And that if one whitened the food or filled it with chicken it is not nullified (in the first seif) — because the substance of the prohibition is considered present and it is not considered dissolved and not in existence except when not recognizable in appearance but only in taste — end of Torat Chatat there rule 2 in the name of Maharai; however it requires investigation also in this, for in my humble opinion from the Gemara Nedarim (daf 98) and Yerushalmi chapter 6 of Nedarim and Tosafot and Rosh and Ran in Nedarim there — something that has a permitted aspect, even its taste is not nullified in its kind — see there; and so is implied from what Maharam and Rosh wrote in responsum beginning general rule 2 on beer of new grain — the grain is nullified in sixty in water, and what we say that something that has a permitted aspect is not nullified refers to in its kind, etc.; and so is implied from Rambam, Semag, and Mechaber below siman 116 seif 10 regarding wine forbidden by vow mixed in other wine — even a drop in a barrel forbids all because one can ask about his vow; also what Torat Chatat wrote there that so is Taz Orach Chayim siman 513 in the name of Rabbeinu Tam is not necessary, for in my humble opinion the Tur there only said that in not its kind we do not say something that has a permitted aspect is not nullified, as implied in Beit Yosef and Bach there — see there; and this is what the Tur wrote here in the name of Rabbeinu Tam: that something that has a permitted aspect is not nullified specifically when mixed in its kind, but not in its kind taste is nullified in sixty — end of his words; thus in its kind even taste is not nullified; also what the Rav wrote in Torat Chatat that so is Orach Chayim general rule 25 — it is not so, for he ruled there in rule 4 like Rambam regarding wine above; and likewise regarding vessels absorbing prohibition he wrote there rule 19 like Rashba and Mechaber seif 3; and what he wrote there in rule 17 that where the substance of the prohibition is not in existence and there is only taste it is nullified — refers specifically to not its kind: "and that something that has a permitted aspect is not nullified refers specifically to in its kind; but in not its kind it is properly nullified in sixty even if one does not recognize it and the substance of the prohibition remains inside, etc.; and all the more so where the substance of the prohibition is not in existence and there is only taste it is properly nullified in sixty" — and so we learned in Yerushalmi that the Sages gave a measure for something that has a permitted aspect only in its kind in any amount and not in its kind in sixty — end of his words; thus it deals specifically with not its kind; and at first glance one could reconcile his words in the hagahah to say wherever the substance of the prohibition is still in the mixture, even if not in existence, such as wine in wine, etc., it is not nullified; but where a piece of prohibition fell and they threw it out whole — we hold one must nullify its emission as above siman 98 seif 8 on account of taste exiting from it — in such a case it is nullified; and now we can reconcile some proofs above dealing where the body of the item was mixed there and therefore not nullified; but from his words in Torat Chatat there it appears there is no distinction, for he brought so from Orach Chayim siman 513 dealing explicitly where the body of the item was mixed — see there; and so is implied from Maharai's words he brought there that I brought at the beginning of s.k. 7 — investigate; and he also brought this rule from Hagahot Alfasi chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah in the name of Mahariach — and there it states explicitly specifically where there is substance of the prohibition, but where there is no substance that dissolved it is nullified in sixty — end of his words; and so is implied in Darkei Moshe; and in truth for practical law it appears even if removed whole its taste is not nullified, as implied from all those proofs above; and so is implied from Rabeinu Yerucham part 4 that one who vowed from meat and it was cooked with meat — eggs are forbidden in sixty, for although it is something that has a permitted aspect, since it is not in its kind — end of his words; and otherwise it is strained to distinguish whether taste remains there or not — until you eat it in prohibition you eat it in permission. I found again in Bach who also ruled there is no distinction and it is never nullified, only he was brief — see there; and see in the next s.k.:
And likewise if its prohibition is not on account of itself, etc. All seized on the Rav in this, for something that has a permitted aspect — what difference if absorbed or not, ultimately there is permission for its prohibition; and what he brought from Terumat HaDeshen siman 170 is on the contrary implied explicitly the opposite, as Maharshal wrote in Orach Chayim and in his book Yoreh Deah siman 88 — not so there, rather a piece worthy of honor: if the prohibition is not on account of itself but on account of prohibition absorbed in it, even though it is worthy of honor it is nullified, as poskim wrote at the beginning of siman 101; but regarding something that has a permitted aspect it is implied explicitly in Torat HaBayit that it is not nullified even if the prohibition is not on account of itself, for he considers there a piece that stood three days without salting as something that has a permitted aspect — if not because for roasting it was never forbidden — see there; and so in Hagahot ShaDa in the name of Maharai, and so in Be'er Sheva — see there; and on the contrary, since poskim wrote regarding a piece worthy of honor that absorbed a prohibition it is nullified because nevertheless the prohibition absorbed in it is not worthy of honor — it is implied that regarding something that has a permitted aspect, where the prohibition will have permission, it is not nullified.

That was not recognizable, etc. Such as a vat full of grapes in clusters and there was much clear wine before Shabbat — permitted to take wine on Shabbat even though more constantly exits from the grapes, because it nullifies in what already exited and there is no something that has a permitted aspect since wine never had a name by itself; and likewise a barrel of whole grapes filled with water for tamad — grapes burst later and all wine exits — permitted to draw on Shabbat to drink, for the little wine exiting on Shabbat mixes and nullifies in wine already there that was never once recognizable alone — and similarly all such cases — there.
Such as chametz on Pesach, etc. And nevertheless some say in Orach Chayim siman 447 seif 9 that if chametz mixed dry on Pesach, even in a thousand it is not nullified; their reason as Mordechai and Rashba wrote: they did not say dry-with-dry is nullified except for prohibitions that forbid up to sixty, etc.; and that is why he wrote there seif 2 chametz mixed six hours and more does not forbid in any amount but its law is like other prohibitions; and if it were something that has a permitted aspect, even by taste also; and as Maggid Mishneh chapter 1 of Laws of Chametz in the name of Yerushalmi; however one can say in this that nevertheless it is nullified not in its kind like other prohibitions that have a permitted aspect; and what is not nullified on Pesach even not in its kind is on account of the severity of its prohibition, as Rambam chapter 15 of Forbidden Foods rule 12 wrote.
And there are those who disagree. And therefore what some say in Orach Chayim siman 447 there that chametz on Pesach dry-with-dry one against two is nullified — meaning not in its kind; for if in its kind it would be something that has a permitted aspect; and therefore it deals where there are sixty in the permitted pieces against the forbidden piece — otherwise even in other prohibitions it would not be nullified, since if cooked they impart taste, as below siman 109 s.k. 9; however one can say the two reasons the Mechaber brought in Orach Chayim also disagree whether chametz is something that has a permitted aspect; and there are those who disagree; and Torat Chatat there rule 4 ruled like the first reason, and Maharshal disagrees.
