(Seif 4 in the hagahah) One who vowed from one matter and it was mixed afterward. See in Peri Megadim in the introduction to Laws of Yom Tov who wrote: one who vowed benefit and it was mixed — one can say it is nullified, for when we say something that has a permitted aspect is not nullified, that is for eating, not for benefit; and likewise for moving one can say it is nullified — see there; and so Tzelach: granted for eating and benefit, but for moving, etc. — see there.
