And even if there is doubt whether it was laid on Yom Tov, etc. — in this matter something that has a permitted aspect is stricter than other things that are not nullified, for there we are lenient in their doubts if the prohibition is certainly d'rabbanan — we say in his doubt it is permitted like any d'rabbanan doubt, and it will not harm him if it is an important item; but here, even in a d'rabbanan prohibition doubt, it is forbidden since it has a permitted aspect. Taz s.k. 3. And so Peri Chadash letter 2 — something that has a permitted aspect, even in a d'rabbanan doubt, and even in a thousand is not nullified, since there is permission for its prohibition. Levushei Srad letter 4. Chokhmat Adam general rule 53 letter 19. And so is the opinion of the Shulchan Aruch in what it wrote: "and even if there is doubt whether it was laid on Yom Tov," which is a d'rabbanan doubt, and it was mixed with forbidden eggs. However, see in our words in Orach Chayim siman 513 letter 6, where we wrote that there is permission in a d'rabbanan doubt mixed with others, only that we wrote there to be stringent like the Shulchan Aruch and its reasoning, only for great need or great loss one may rely on the permission — see there.
