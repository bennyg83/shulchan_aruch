It will be distant. The Gemara says there is no rending without seven days of mourning, and one might have thought that here he does not rend, since he will not observe seven days of mourning after the festival — for it will then be distant. The ruling teaches us that this is not so, for they only said "there is no rending without seven days" in a case where he heard during the festival itself — that he cannot rend, and after the festival it will be distant and he will only observe one day of mourning — in that case he does not rend even after the festival. But if he heard a recent bereavement on the intermediate days of the festival, when he can rend, he rends on the intermediate days — even though after the festival it will be distant and he will only observe one day of mourning. Nevertheless, now is the time for rending for him, since the festival interrupts only the mourning and not the rending.
