It will become a distant report, etc. Even according to our practice above in seif 31, not to tear for other relatives during Chol HaMoed, that custom applies only where one can tear after the festival. But where the report will become distant after the festival and he will then be unable to tear, no custom exists, and one should follow the law and the majority of the Geonim. Maharshal writes that one who hears a distant report does not tear during the festival, even for his father or mother. Thus writes Bach.
