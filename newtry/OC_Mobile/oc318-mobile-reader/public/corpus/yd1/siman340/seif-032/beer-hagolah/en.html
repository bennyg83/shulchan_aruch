The wording of the Rambam is based on the statement of Rabbi Chiya bar Abba there. He incorporates two explanations: Rashi's explanation, that 'inside' means in the chamber and 'outside' means before the people, as also ruled by the Semag and Sefer HaEshkol from the wording 'like the rim of a mortar'; and the explanation of Rav Yitzchak ibn Giat, that he places his hand beneath his garment and tears, as also ruled by the Ramban, since the Gemara says 'from within and from without' rather than 'inside and outside.' In the version of Rashi before us, the explanation accords with the Rambam. [401]
