And Torah scholars are exempt. See the Shach s.k. 13, who wrote that from the words of the Raavad and the Rosh it appears that even Torah scholars are obligated; see there. See Agudat Ezov, in the pamphlet Alon Bakhut, derush 1, who objected to him and wrote that they said Torah scholars are exempt from this tearing only for an upright person who is not as learned as they are, because this is like an elder for whom it is not fitting to his honor. The law of tearing is like the law of standing and showing honor, where a greater person does not stand before a lesser one. But if the deceased was a sage equal to them, even Torah scholars are obligated. See there, where he brings proof that it is impossible for anyone to disagree and say that Torah scholars are obligated to tear for an upright person lesser than themselves, for Megillah 28 relates the case of one who would teach halachot, etc.; see there. In my humble opinion this can be rejected; see Rashi there and Sotah 22, and see Tosafot Gittin 31b, and examine carefully.
