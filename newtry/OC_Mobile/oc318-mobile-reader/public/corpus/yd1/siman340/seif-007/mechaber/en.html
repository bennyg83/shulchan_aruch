For a sage and a Torah student whom they ask a halachic matter anywhere and he answers—even after burial on the day of report if within thirty days—and one tears at his eulogy (if he did not tear on the day of report and the eulogy time passed he does not tear again) (as implied in Tur and Beit Yosef); and they tear for him until hearts are exposed; and Torah students everywhere already practice tearing a handbreadth for one another even if equal and neither teaches the other. {Rama: Some say one does not tear for a sage unless he is his rabbi or they know from his teachings that he innovated—he is his rabbi (Rosh, Hagah Maimoniyot in the name of Ram)—and so they practice to be lenient in these lands.}
