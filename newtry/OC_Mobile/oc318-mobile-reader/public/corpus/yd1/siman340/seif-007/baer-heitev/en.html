At the time. The Bach wrote: however, this is specifically when his eulogy is within thirty days; but after thirty days they do not tear even at the time of his eulogy. So wrote the Rosh in the name of the Ra'avad. Shach.
Who innovated. The Shach wrote: I am astonished that they did not practice tearing even for a sage about whom they know from his teachings that he innovated, and even for one greater than he, and they have nothing on which to rely. Perhaps they hold that one is called a talmid chacham only when he knows a halachic matter in every place, even in tractate Kallah, as stated in the Talmud. The Agudah already wrote in the first chapter of Chullin that now, because of our sins, there is no talmid chacham, since one does not know even tractate Kallah; and this requires study; end quote.
