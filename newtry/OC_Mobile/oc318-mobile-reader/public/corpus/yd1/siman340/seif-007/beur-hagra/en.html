And for a Torah scholar, etc. This is the Yerushalmi, brought by the Rosh: just as one tears for sages, so one tears for a Torah scholar. Who is a Torah scholar, etc.? See there, siman 106.
Even after, etc. This follows the incident of Rav Safra, where they thought: what was, etc.; and we say on 24a that they said to Shmuel, Rav has died, etc., and he said to Rabbi Yochanan, etc. So too in the Yerushalmi brought by the Rosh there.
If he is, etc. This is 20b and follows Rabbi Mani, as written below in seif 18.
If he did not tear, etc. So it appears from the Gemara there: they thought, what, etc.; he said to them, we learned that a sage, etc. But without this, no.
And the custom has already spread, etc. This is the language of the Rambam.
And some say, etc. So the Rosh wrote there in the name of the Maharam, from what is stated there: moreover, every day his teaching, etc. But the Rosh challenged him, for on the contrary, from there the opposite is proven, since it says "moreover," etc. And what is stated in Bava Metzia regarding Torah scholars in Babylonia has already been explained by Rashi there as a tear that may not be resewn, like the law of one's teacher.
