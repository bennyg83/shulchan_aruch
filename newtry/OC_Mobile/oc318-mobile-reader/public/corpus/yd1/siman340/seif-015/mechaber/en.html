For all the dead he bastes (uneven stitching) after seven and mends after thirty (and there is no distinction whether a festival intervened) (Hagah Maimoniyot in the name of Raavyah); for father and mother he bastes after thirty and never mends; and a woman bastes immediately because of her honor. {Rama: All the time bastening is forbidden, even joining the head of the tear with a needle; and some places are stringent even for other dead not to basten within thirty, and then even joining the head of the tear with a needle is forbidden (Mahariv siman 6).}
