The festival. Although a festival cancels the decree of seven and thirty, that is only regarding mourning. But regarding all matters of tearing, we require an actual seven and thirty. So wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot. However, nowadays the custom is to mend the tear once mourning has ceased. So too below in siman 399, where the poskim write plainly that the festival cancels the decree of seven and thirty, it implies that every decree belonging to seven and thirty is cancelled in every respect. End quote from Shach.
