But on chol ha-moed one tears. See Mishneh LaMelekh, Hilkhot Avel chapter 11 law 1, who wrote that if he died on the first day of Yom Tov, he may not tear on chol ha-moed; rather the tearing is postponed until after the festival, when he begins mourning. What we learned, that only the close relatives of the dead tear, refers to one who died on chol ha-moed, for since it is the moment of emotional heat, tearing was permitted for him. But if he died and was buried on Yom Tov, since the tearing was deferred because he could not tear on Yom Tov, it is likewise deferred until the time of mourning; see there. So too Magen Avraham siman 547 s.k. 3 wrote in the name of Maharam of Lublin.
