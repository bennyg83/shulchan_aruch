Except that the woman, etc. Regarding rending for a father or mother, when all garments must be rent, a woman first rends the lower garment and turns the tear behind her, and then rends the upper garment. This prevents her chest from being exposed and the men present from improper thoughts. Although the Rama soon writes that the custom is not to rend the undershirt, exposing the undershirt itself also involves immodesty similar to exposing her chest. So writes the Bach in seif 10.
