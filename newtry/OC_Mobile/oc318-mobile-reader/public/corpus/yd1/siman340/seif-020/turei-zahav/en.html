From within the basting. He wrote at the end of this siman, following Rashi and the Alfasi, and this is his language: molel — he rubs the two ends of the tear between his fingers and winds them together like the winding of book-scrolls, and inserts two or three stitches there. sholet — a wide stitch. loket — he gathers in his hand the entire tear, its beginning with its end, and inserts a needle two or three times. sulam — like the rungs of a ladder, sewing two stitches and stopping and sewing again below, leaving a small gap between one stitch and the next. End of his language. Therefore one who rends from within these [temporary closings] has not fulfilled the obligation, since without this it was already a form of rending.
