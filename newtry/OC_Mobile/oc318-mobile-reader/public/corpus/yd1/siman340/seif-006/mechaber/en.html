For any kosher person not suspected of any sin or neglect of any mitzvah and not of bad reputation—even if not great in Torah—even if he did not stand at departure of soul, he is obligated to tear for him provided he stands there between death and burial; and Torah students are exempt from this tearing. {Rama: Some say one is not obligated to tear for a kosher person unless he stood at departure of soul, but he must weep and mourn him (Ran, Hagahot Maimoniyot, Raavyah)—and so they practice to be lenient.}
