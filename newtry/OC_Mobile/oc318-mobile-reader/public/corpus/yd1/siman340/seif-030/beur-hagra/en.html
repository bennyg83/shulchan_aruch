(Likkut) We do not, etc. Although in Moed Katan 26b we say that mourning is separate, etc., that means only that this rule does not always apply, namely to follow the lenient opinion even when it is a lone view against the many. Rather, mourning is like other rabbinic laws. Even though it is derived from the verse "and your heads," etc., that is only an asmachta, for there is no mourning by Torah law at all except the first day of mourning according to the Geonim; and even on that point others disagree with them. Tearing, the other practices, and the decree of thirty days are all rabbinic. This is from Torat HaAdam; see there.
