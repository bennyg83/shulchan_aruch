(There, in Taz seif katan 15) From here there is some proof that a minor who has reached the age of education, etc. In my humble opinion this applies specifically to tearing. Rosh at the end of chapter Elu Megalchin records the dispute of his teacher Maharam over one who becomes thirteen during the seven days of mourning, whether he must observe seven days. This shows that while he was a minor it was obvious that he did not have to observe mourning. Tur and the Mechaber below in siman 396 likewise imply this; see there.
