Some say that he fulfills, etc. Semak writes: it was taught in Masechet Semachot chapter 9 that one who tears below or at the sides does not fulfill his obligation; R. Yehudah says he does. It appears that the law follows the Sages. Nevertheless, the common practice is to tear below, perhaps relying for other relatives upon R. Yehudah.
