The place of tearing is anywhere at the neck opening in front; but if he tore behind (or at the edge of the garment) (Tur) or at the sides he has not fulfilled. {Rama: Some say he fulfills if he tears at the edge of the garment, and so they practice to be lenient to tear at the edge of the garment (these customs are found in Semak, Mahariv, Chidushei Agudah, and Kol Bo) when tearing for other dead whom one does not mourn for; but for dead who are his relatives and he mourns them, he does not fulfill with this tearing and must tear at the neck opening, and so they practice.}
