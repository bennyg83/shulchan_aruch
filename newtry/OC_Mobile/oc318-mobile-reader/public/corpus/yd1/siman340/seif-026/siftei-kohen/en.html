He has fulfilled the obligation of tearing, etc. But if he was told that the person died and tore, and the person had not yet died, and afterward died, he has not fulfilled the obligation. Ramban in Torat HaAdam, cited by Tur.
The obligation of tearing. Regarding the seven days of mourning, one might reasonably say that since mourning took effect and was observed, although some of the days were mistakenly observed, they count, for mourning has no makeup period. Ramban states that this matter requires study; Tur cites him. See below, siman 396.
