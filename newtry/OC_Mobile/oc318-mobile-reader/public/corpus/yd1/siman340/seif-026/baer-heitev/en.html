The first. But if he was alive the first time, he has not fulfilled the tearing, for it is after tokh kedei dibbur. Taz. The Shach wrote: regarding the seven days of mourning, one might nearly say that since mourning took effect on him and he practiced it, even though some of the days were mistakenly nullified, they count for him and there is no compensation for mourning. This matter requires study. The Ramban discusses it, and the Tur cites him. See Radbaz I, siman 195, and see Knesset HaGedolah.
