Only one who rends for whichever relative of his died need not rend again, even if he learned the identity only after twenty-four months within the time needed for a brief utterance. The Rama writes that if he was told merely that a relative died, without knowing which one, and he rent without specification, then afterward learned who it was, he fulfilled the obligation of rending because retroactive clarification applies.
