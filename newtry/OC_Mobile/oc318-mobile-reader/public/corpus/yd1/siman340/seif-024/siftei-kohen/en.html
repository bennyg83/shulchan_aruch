But if they told him, etc. The Bach rules that in such a case he has not fulfilled the obligation of rending. An unspecified rending means that one rends for his deceased relative, whoever that relative may be, and then he need not rend again even if the identity became known only after the brief interval of speech. See there and siman 234:33.
He has fulfilled his obligation. The Darchei Moshe cites Nimukei Yosef in chapter Elu Metziot: if he was told without specification that a relative died, he rent without specification, and afterward learned who it was, he fulfilled the obligation, because retroactive clarification applies.
