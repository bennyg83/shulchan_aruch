For his father and mother, etc. Maharshal writes that the custom is to rend on the left for a father or mother and on the right for other relatives, such as children or siblings. Maharil records a case in which he instructed his son to rend on the right for his mother. My father-in-law the Bach reasons that since rending for parents must expose the heart, and the heart is on the left, it should be done on the left. This explains the custom, though the basic law and later additions to a prior tear follow the details stated in this siman.
