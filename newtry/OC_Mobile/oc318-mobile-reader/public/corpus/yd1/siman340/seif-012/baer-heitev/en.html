The outer coat. A responsum of Masat Binyamin identifies this as the garment called a mantle, but rules that the other upper coat must be torn for both a parent and other relatives. The Taz considers this astonishing and writes that one should not even be stringent to tear that other coat. Such a stringency can produce a leniency regarding other relatives, for whom only the uppermost garment must be torn. It is certainly incorrect to tear only a garment that is not legally subject to tearing. Nekudot HaKesef agrees with Masat Binyamin.
