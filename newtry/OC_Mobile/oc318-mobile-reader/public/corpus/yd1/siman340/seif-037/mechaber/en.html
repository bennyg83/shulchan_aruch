One who hears a blessing of God's Name, even a euphemistic blessing (Rambam chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah), even if said in a foreign language it is like a euphemism (Nimukei Yosef, chapter Four Deaths)—obligated to tear, provided he hears it from an Israelite (and some say nowadays an apostate is like a gentile) (there); and even hearing from witnesses how so-and-so blessed—he must tear and witnesses need not tear again; one who sees a Torah scroll burned, or tefillin, or even one scroll from the Prophets or Writings—tears two tears, specifically when burned by hand as in the incident that occurred.
