The matter of tearing: its measure, place, for whom one tears, and in what time one tears. In it are 39 seifim. One to whom a relative died who is among those one must mourn for is obligated to tear for him; and he must tear standing; and if he tore sitting he has not fulfilled (and must tear again) (Tur in the name of Ritz Giat, Rosh, Hagahot Maimoniyot, and Beit Yosef according to Raavad, Rif, Ramban, and Mordechai); (and l'chatchila he should tear before the face of the dead is covered) (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak).
