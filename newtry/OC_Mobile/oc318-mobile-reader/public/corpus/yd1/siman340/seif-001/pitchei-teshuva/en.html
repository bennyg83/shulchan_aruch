He is obligated to rend. Birkei Yosef writes that ideally another person should perform the tearing, because the mourner feels greater embarrassment and anguish when another tears his garment while he remains silent and accepts Hashem's judgment. He cites R. Yosef Molcho, manuscript responsum 88. If the other person tears more than required, he is liable because he should have been careful. Even when tearing for himself, one may not tear beyond the legal requirement because of bal tashchit.
