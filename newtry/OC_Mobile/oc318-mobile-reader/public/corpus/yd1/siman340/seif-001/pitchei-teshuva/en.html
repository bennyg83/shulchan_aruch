He is obligated to tear. Birkei Yosef wrote that every tearing is preferably done by another person, for he is embarrassed by him, and there is greater anguish when another tears his garment and he remains silent and accepts upon himself the judgment of the Blessed One. Mahari Malko, manuscript responsum siman 88; see below seif 13. He further wrote: if his fellow tore for him excessively, he is liable, for he should have been precise. Even when tearing by himself he may tear only according to the law, for otherwise he violates bal tashchit.
