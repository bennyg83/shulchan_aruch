He is obligated to rend. Birkei Yosef writes that every act of keriah is preferably performed by another person, for one is embarrassed before him and there is greater anguish when another person tears his garment while he remains silent and accepts the judgment of the Blessed One. This is from Mahari Malko, manuscript responsum, siman 88. See also below, seif 13. He further writes that if another person tore more than the required measure, that person is liable, for he should have been careful. Even when one rends his own garment, he may not tear more than the prescribed amount, for he thereby violates the prohibition of needless destruction.
