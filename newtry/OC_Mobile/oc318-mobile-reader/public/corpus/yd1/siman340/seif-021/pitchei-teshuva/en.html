A Torah scroll that was burned. See Tiferes LeMoshe, who writes that it requires study whether one tears for a burned volume of Talmud, Midrash, or the like. Since these works were permitted to be written because 'it is a time to act for Hashem,' and for that reason they may be rescued from a fire, perhaps tearing is required. See there, and see above, siman 270, seif 2.
