For example, when they gathered. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Shach, who writes that the tear may be fully repaired the next day. His words are astonishing, for it is explicit at the end of this siman that a keriah made for evil tidings is among the tears that may never be fully repaired, and so is the law in the Gemara. I found that Dagul MeRevavah also questioned the Shach on this point. The Gaon Rabbi David Dessa, in his commentary to the Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Moed Katan, likewise expressed astonishment.
