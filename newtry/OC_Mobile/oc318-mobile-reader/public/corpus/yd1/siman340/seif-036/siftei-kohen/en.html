They tear, etc. Scripture states, 'David took hold of his garments and tore them for Saul and for the people of Hashem, because they had fallen by the sword.' Beit Yosef writes in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham that the verse indicates an obligation to mourn the entire day, and therefore he may not loosely stitch the tear until the next day. Darkei Moshe cites this. It appears that he may likewise mend it permanently the next day.
