For all deceased relatives, etc. Maharshal writes that the custom is to rend on the left side for a father or mother, and on the right side for other relatives, such as sons and brothers. The Bach states in seif 12 that this is the communal custom. This is only custom, however. By strict law, the side used for parents should also be used for other relatives, as indicated by the Gemara, the authorities, and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch below in seif 22. Alternatively, once one has already rent one side, the custom did not require rending the opposite side for a later relative, and adding to the existing tear suffices.
