His garments. Maharshal wrote that for a father and mother one tears on the left side, and for other relatives on the right side. The Bach wrote that this is reasonable, for since for his father and mother he must tear until he reveals his heart, and the heart is on the left, he must therefore tear on the left to reveal his heart; and so they practice in the communities. Nevertheless, this is by custom, but by law it implies that on the side where one tears for his father and mother, one also tears for other relatives, as implied in the Talmud, the poskim, and the Tur and Shulchan Aruch below in seif 22; see there. Perhaps there, since he already tore on one side, they did not practice stringency to tear on the second side, and adding to the tear suffices; end quote, Shach.
