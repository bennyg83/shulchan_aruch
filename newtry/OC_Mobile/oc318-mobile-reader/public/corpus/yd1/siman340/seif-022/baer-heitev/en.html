The Bach wrote that if in the first tearing that he tore for his son, he separated the edge in front of the lip, he does not need another tearing — it suffices with what he adds to the first tear. But this does not appear correct according to the halachah, as is proven from the words of the poskim that even if he separated it, he still needs another tearing. See above seif katan 23. Thus far the Shach.
