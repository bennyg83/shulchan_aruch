He tears another tear, etc. The Bach wrote that if in the first tear, which he tore for his son, he separated the border, he need not make another tear, and it is enough to add to the first tear. This does not appear correct in law. Even according to his explanation, it is proven from the Ramban that according to the Rambam, even if he separated the border, he must make another tear. So too the plain meaning of his words implies, and the Ramban wrote about him that he aimed well. Rabbeinu Yerucham ruled likewise, and so wrote the Nimukei Yosef in the name of the Raavad. This also appears to be the view of the Rav: although above, seif 13, he wrote that the custom is to separate the border for all dead relatives, here and in seif 23 he states plainly like the Mechaber, as does the Atteret Zahav.
