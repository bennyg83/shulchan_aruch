For example, when they assembled. See Baer Heitev in the name of Shach, who writes that the tear may be mended the next day. His words are astonishing, for it is explained at the end of the siman that a tear made upon hearing evil tidings is among those that may never be fully mended. This is also explicit in the Gemara. I found that Dagul Merevavah raised this difficulty against Shach, and the Gaon Rabbi David Dessa, in his commentary to Yerushalmi, Moed Katan, chapter 3, likewise questioned him.
