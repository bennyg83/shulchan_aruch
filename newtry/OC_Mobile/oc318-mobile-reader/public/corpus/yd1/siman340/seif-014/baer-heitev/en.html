To change. The Shach writes that this restriction applies on weekdays. On Shabbat, even one mourning a parent changes clothing and does not tear them. If he has no change of clothing, he turns the tear to the back. See siman 389, seif 3. This applies when changing weekday clothing, but the custom is to observe the restriction with Shabbat clothing. The Bach cites Maharam Menachem of Metz that old garments known to have been worn before the parent's death may be worn after seven days without tearing. See siman 389.
