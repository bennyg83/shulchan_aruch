For his father or mother, if he changes, etc. This refers to weekdays. On Shabbat, even a mourner for a father or mother changes garments and does not rend them. If he has no garment into which to change, he turns the tear to the back, as stated in the Gemara, page 24a, and ruled by the Rambam in chapter 10. This is also implied below in siman 400. This applies only when changing into weekday garments. Regarding Shabbat garments, the prohibitions of mourning are observed as explained below in siman 389:3.
