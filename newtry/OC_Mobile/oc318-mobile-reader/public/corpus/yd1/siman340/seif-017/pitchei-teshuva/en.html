From Israel. See the book Mishnat Chakhamim, which wrote that even though one who hears from the mouth of a non-Jew need not rend — if an Israelite came and said he heard from a non-Jew who blasphemed, since he now hears the blasphemy from the mouth of the Israelite he is obligated to rend. And likewise the one who himself heard and repeats the blasphemy — since at the time he heard it from the non-Jew he was not required to rend, he is now obligated to rend. See there.
