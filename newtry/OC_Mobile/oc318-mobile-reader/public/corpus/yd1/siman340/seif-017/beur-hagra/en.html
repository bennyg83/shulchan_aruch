And one stitches it, etc. Ramban wonders why he may not mend it on that very day, for Eliyahu Rabbah chapter 9 says one who tore for honor may mend immediately.
For his primary teacher he loosely stitches on the next day. The Yerushalmi relates that when R. Yonah died, R. Chiya bar Ba instructed that shoes be worn the next day. According to the view that one may loosely stitch that day, one may also fully stitch that day. We rule that mourning is observed one day, so he loosely stitches the next day. It requires study why Rambam says the same regarding a nasi, since in siman 374:11 he writes that for a nasi, etc. Ramban likewise leaves this unresolved.
