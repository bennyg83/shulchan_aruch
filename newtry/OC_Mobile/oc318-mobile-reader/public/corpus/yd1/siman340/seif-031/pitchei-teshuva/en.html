But on Chol HaMoed one rends. See Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Avel 11:1, who writes that if a person died on the first day of Yom Tov, the mourner may not rend on Chol HaMoed. Rather, the keriah is deferred until after the festival, when mourning begins. The Mishnah's statement that only relatives of the deceased rend on Chol HaMoed concerns one who died on Chol HaMoed itself. Since that is the immediate moment of grief, keriah was permitted. But where the deceased died and was buried on Yom Tov, since keriah was deferred because it could not be performed on Yom Tov, it remains deferred until the time of mourning. See there. Magen Avraham, siman 547, seif katan 3, states likewise in the name of Maharam Lublin.
