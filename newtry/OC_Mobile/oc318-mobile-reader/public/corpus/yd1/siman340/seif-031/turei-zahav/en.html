Only on his father, etc. Since the Mordechai cites opinions that one does not tear at all on Chol HaMoed, therefore they distinguish between a father and other relatives. But in a place where there is no custom, one should tear for all of them on Chol HaMoed, for the primary view is like the opinion that one tears on Chol HaMoed, as implied by the Mishnah. My father-in-law z"l wrote: even though the custom is not to tear for other deceased on Chol HaMoed, that applies when it is possible to tear after the festival; but where one heard recent news during the festival and after the festival it would become distant news — so that he could not tear after the festival — in this case there is no custom, and one should follow the halachah that one tears on Chol HaMoed. Thus far his words. He further wrote in the name of Maharshal: it appears to me that where one heard distant news, one does not tear during the festival even for a father or mother; rather, one tears after the festival. Since mourning has already ceased from him, the tearing is not significant enough to override the festival. Thus far his words.
