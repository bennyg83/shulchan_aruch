Custom. In Responsa Masat Binyamin he wrote that this means throughout the kingdom of Poland one should tear for all of them, but in Ashkenaz they practiced not to tear except for father and mother, as the Rav wrote in Orach Chayim siman 544 seif 6. The Bach wrote: although the practice is not to tear on Chol HaMoed for other dead relatives, that is where it is possible to tear after the festival. But where one heard a recent report on the festival and after the festival it becomes a distant report, so he cannot tear after the festival, there is no custom in this, and one should grasp the halachah that one tears on Chol HaMoed. He further wrote in the name of Maharshal: it appears that where he heard a distant report, one does not tear on the festival even for father and mother; rather, he tears after the festival, for once mourning has ceased from him, the tearing is not so severe as to override the festival; end quote.
