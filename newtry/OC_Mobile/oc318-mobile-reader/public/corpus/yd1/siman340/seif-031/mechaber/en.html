One does not tear on Yom Tov, even second-day Yom Tov of the diaspora, even close relatives of the dead; but on chol ha-moed one tears for each as appropriate—if standing at departure of soul, or if a kosher person or sage, each as explained. {Rama: Some practice not to tear on chol ha-moed except for father and mother, and for other dead tear after the festival (Terumat HaDeshen siman 288); and where there is no custom, one tears for all.}
