Regarding what he wrote in the name of the Taz, that even on the day of a near report, etc., I saw in the Yoreh De'ah glosses of my grandfather the rabbi z"l that Maharam Binyamin responsa siman 79 does not agree. As for what he wrote in the name of Nekudot HaKesef, Dagul MeRevavah also wrote so, that this is explicit below in siman 356. The matter of chinuch does not apply here, for by doing so you would interrupt him from Torah study; but tearing does not interrupt Torah study.
