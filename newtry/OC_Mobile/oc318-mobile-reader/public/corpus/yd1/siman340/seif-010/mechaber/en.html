His afrekusin (outer garment)—he need not tear; some explain it is the sweat garment stuck to the body; some explain it is the upper garment called cape; and the practice spread not to tear the cape for any dead even father and mother, and to tear for father and mother also the shirt called camisole (Kol Bo in the name of his rabbi poskim and Semak per Arukh). {Rama: In these lands they do not practice so but do not tear the linen shirt that is the sweat garment nor the upper cloak; but other garments he tears for father and mother, and for other dead the upper garment under the cloak (Semag in the name of French rabbis).}
