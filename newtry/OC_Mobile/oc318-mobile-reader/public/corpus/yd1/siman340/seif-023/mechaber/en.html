One to whom two died as one or report of two at once tears one tear for both; he tore and afterward another died (within seven) (Tur)—he tears a separate tear, whether adding in that same tear and tearing another handbreadth or distancing three fingerbreadths and tearing a handbreadth; after seven he adds to the first tear any amount; father or mother and another relative—he tears first for father or mother to the heart and distances three fingerbreadths and tears a handbreadth for the other dead; father died and he tore and after seven another relative died—he adds to the first tear and bastes the lower and not the upper; another relative died and he tore and afterward father or mother died—whether within or after seven he distances three fingerbreadths and tears from the side at the garment edge because he must distinguish hem from hem and tear until the heart; father and mother died as one tears one tear for both.
