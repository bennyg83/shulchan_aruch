In the Tur in the name of the Ramban, he ruled accordingly — not like Rashi's explanation that the reason it resembles a Torah scroll that was burned is that he could still have learned; if so, there would be no need to rend for a woman, since one could object: how can one rend for the future? Rather, the reason is that it is merely an analogy to a Torah scroll that was burned, which is a great loss and great anguish, and therefore one rends also for a woman. And the Maharashal wrote that according to Rashi it seems one rends for a minor. But in the chapter "HaOreig," Rashi, the Mahari Em, and the Rosh explained: there is no empty person in Israel who does not have Torah and commandments — and if so, this is the reason one rends also for a woman but not for a minor, as above. End quote. And my father-in-law z"l wrote that nevertheless, for a minor who studies Scripture, everyone agrees one must rend. End quote.
