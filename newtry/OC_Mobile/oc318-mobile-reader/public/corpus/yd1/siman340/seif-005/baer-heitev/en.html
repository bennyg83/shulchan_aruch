But in Perek HaOreg, Rashi and the Rosh explained that there is no empty person in Israel who does not have Torah and mitzvah, and therefore one tears for a woman too, but not for a minor. The Bach wrote: however, for a minor who studies Scripture, one must tear according to everyone (and in the book Erekh Lechem he wrote in the name of Radbaz siman 508 that one tears even for a male minor but not for a female minor; he further wrote in the name of the same authority, siman 1193, that if one stood at the time of the soul's departure on Shabbat, he is exempt from tearing on Motza'ei Shabbat).
