(Likkut) And some say that one does not, etc. From what is stated in the Tosefta, Sanhedrin chapter 14: minors of an idolatrous city who were led astray with it; Rabbi Eliezer says they are killed, and the Sages say they are not killed. The Sages must disagree only regarding whether they are killed, but regarding mourning they agree that one does not mourn for them. The first view holds that this is no proof, for there the minors are somewhat grown and actually worshipped idolatry; but a minor who does not know his right from his left is considered a Jew. Moreover, an idolatrous city is not comparable to other cases, for a minor who worshipped idolatry is not killed even according to Rabbi Eliezer. This is from the Mordechai, chapter Hayu Bodkin.
