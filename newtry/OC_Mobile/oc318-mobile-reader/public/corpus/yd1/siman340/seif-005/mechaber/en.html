One standing at the time of departure of the soul of a man or woman of Israel is obligated to tear. (Tur in the name of Ramban and Beit Yosef also according to Rashi.) And even if at times he committed a sin out of appetite, or refrains from doing a mitzvah because of trouble. {Rama: But one who regularly commits sin—one does not mourn him (Mordechai end of Moed Katan); and all the more an apostate to idolatry (there and poskim, and clear from Shas); and some say one mourns an apostate killed by gentiles (Hagahot Ashiri and Azulai); and likewise a minor apostate who apostatized with his father or mother is as compelled (Mordechai, Rashi, Beit Yosef siman 345); and some say one does not mourn (there in the name of R. Chaim)—and this is primary. Those who separate from community ways—even though one does not mourn them, one mourns their children (Azulai) (and see above siman 345).}
