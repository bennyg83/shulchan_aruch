(Likkut) One who rends in a borrowed garment, etc. The Yerushalmi, chapter 13 of Shabbat, on what is stated in seif 28: "one who rends on Shabbat" — they asked before Rabbi Yosi: is it not so that Rabbi Yochanan said in the name of Rabbi Shimon ben Yehozadak: stolen matzah — a person does not fulfill his obligation with it, meaning because of a commandment fulfilled through a transgression? He replied: there the act itself is a transgression, but here he commits a transgression and so we say: one who carries matzah from the private domain to the public domain does not fulfill his obligation with it on Pesach. And the Ramban wrote — and it may be inferred that one who rends a stolen garment does not fulfill his obligation (end of note).
