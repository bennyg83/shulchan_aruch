Or a woman. Baer Heitev cites Radbaz, part 2, responsum 838, concerning one present at a death on Shabbat. Tiferet LeMoshe rules that he must rend after Shabbat, provided burial has not yet occurred, and derives also that one rends for a minor girl, contrary to Radbaz. Chatam Sofer 323 disagrees, holding that if one did not rend at the proper time, he does not rend later unless renewed grief arises, as indicated by Moed Katan 25. Radbaz further writes that one who was with the deceased must rend even if he turned his face away at the moment of death, so long as he could have seen it. One who could not see, despite being in the same house, need not rend.
