(Likkut) Just as, etc. This follows Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar, for in the Yerushalmi, in the first chapter of Shabbat and the first chapter of Avodah Zarah, it says: in Shabbat, mourning, and idolatry, the halachah follows Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar. According to this text, which is our text, one may say that it does not refer to all rulings of Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar, but only to his statement in the Tosefta at the beginning of Avodah Zarah, which has practical relevance also for Shabbat and mourning. But the Halakhot Gedolot, the Ramban in Milchamot at the end of Moed Katan, and Torat HaAdam bring the text: in klal gadol, in mourning, and before their festivals, the halachah follows Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar. Tosafot in Avodah Zarah 21b, s.v. arisa, bring it, unlike Tosafot in Moed Katan there, s.v. kakh. This is also the reason for seif 11, where he also rules like Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar. See Tosafot there, 22b, s.v. achar, etc. So too Halakhot Gedolot, the Yerushalmi, and the Ramban there cite it regarding Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar's rule that resewing is forbidden; see also what the Ramban wrote further on this.
