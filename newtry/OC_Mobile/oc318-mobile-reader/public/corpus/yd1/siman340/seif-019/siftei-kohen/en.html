He must inform him. This is because of a mistaken sale. There is no need to inform him merely because of the prohibition, since even when the garment was sold without specification the buyer may not mend it, just as explained above in siman 16 regarding an animal and its offspring. This is the Ramban. The Bach distinguishes the cases: everyone knows the law of an animal and its offspring, whereas not everyone is versed in the laws of rending.
