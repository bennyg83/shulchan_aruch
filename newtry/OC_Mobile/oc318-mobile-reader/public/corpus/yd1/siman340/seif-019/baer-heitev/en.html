To inform him. Because of a mistaken purchase. But because of prohibition, he need not inform him, for even where it is unspecified it is forbidden to mend it, as above in seif 16, like the law of oto ve-et beno. So wrote the Ramban. The Bach wrote that this is not comparable to oto ve-et beno, for there everyone knows the law, whereas here not everyone has learned the law of tearing.
