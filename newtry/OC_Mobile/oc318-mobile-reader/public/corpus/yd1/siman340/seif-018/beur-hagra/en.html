Forever, etc. In the Yerushalmi Rabbi Mana said: "Is there tearing without mourning? For one's father or mother, even on only one of them, how much must one tear?" This implies that the obligation applies forever. Although the incident of Rabbi Chiya, who did not tear, appears otherwise, as Tosafot write on 20b, that is according to Rabbeinu Hananel's explanation. Ramban did not accept that explanation, but followed Rashi, that they asked regarding the father and mother of his teacher. See there. See also Rosh, siman 81.
