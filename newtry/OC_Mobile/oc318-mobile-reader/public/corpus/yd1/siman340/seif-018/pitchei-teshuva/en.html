The Shach writes that even if the witness does not repeat the exact blasphemous words, the hearer must rend, but one who merely hears from someone that another blasphemed, without the report coming from an actual witness, does not. Mishnat Chachamim notes that Rambam, Semag, and Tosafot disagree with the Yerushalmi and require rending only when the blasphemy itself is repeated. Since rending is rabbinic, their lenient view should be followed. According to them, the Yerushalmi's distinction between a second and third reporter also falls away; where the exact blasphemy was repeated, even one who hears it from a third person must rend.
