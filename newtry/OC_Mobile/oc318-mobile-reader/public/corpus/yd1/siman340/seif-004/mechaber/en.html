Just as one tears for his relative whom he mourns, so he tears in the presence of his relative for a dead person who died to his relative—how so? If his son's son died or his son's brother died, or his son's mother died, he is obligated to tear in his son's presence; and likewise he tears for father-in-law and mother-in-law, and a woman tears for her father-in-law and mother-in-law. (And they do not practice so now, as will be explained below siman 374 regarding mourning).
