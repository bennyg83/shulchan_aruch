And in Choshen. The Shach wrote that this is not difficult at all. There it means the borrower requested from the lender; if so, the lender intended only the borrower's benefit, to fulfill his request, and can say: I lent it to you only so you would go and return. Here, however, the lender wrote to his fellow, etc., meaning the lender came on his own to benefit the borrower so that he could fulfill the mitzvah of comforting mourners. If so, each day the mitzvah rests upon him. End quote.
