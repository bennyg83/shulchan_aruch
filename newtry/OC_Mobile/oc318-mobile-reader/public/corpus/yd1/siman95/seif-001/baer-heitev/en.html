Well washed. Shach wrote this is not to say that stam we establish regarding anything cooked in it that there was clinging fat, as Rosh and Taz wrote in seif 3 regarding rinsing — that even if it is not ben yomo we establish on it clinging fat and that it was not well scraped, for only regarding rinsing do we say stam there is clinging fat on them because now he is rinsing them; but when it was cooked in a pot we say stam there was no clinging fat on them. And what Mechaber wrote "well washed" is to exclude when it is known clearly that it was not well washed.
Taste. Explanation: the meat imparts taste to the bowl and the bowl to the fish and still all is heter. And Taz wrote and these are his words: (and all this is in Bach; but regarding prohibition, even many times noten taam bar noten taam is forbidden, for there the concept of noten taam bar noten taam of heter does not apply). One may learn from this that there is no danger in eating fish that has taste of meat from what was cooked in a ben yomo meat vessel, for they said in the Gemara that many ate fish that came up in a bowl, etc.; and see siman 116 what he wrote there until here is his language.
