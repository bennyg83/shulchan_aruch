If they were cooked — l'chatchila one should not cook them in a meat pot intending to eat them with kutach; but if he cooked in a meat pot, it is permitted l'chatchila to eat them with kutach according to the plain language of the Gemara; and even if he transgressed and cooked them in a meat pot intending to eat them with kutach, we do not penalize him — Peri Chadash.
