(Siman 95 seif 1) Fish that were cooked, etc., in a meat pot — if it was not well washed it is forbidden to eat them with kutach. One must say that because of the opening he mentioned kutach and not specifically — for likewise it is forbidden even by itself if there is not sixty against the grease of the meat because of danger, as below siman 116 seif 2, see there; some say even sixty does not help, for danger is stricter than prohibition. And see what he wrote in siman 89 that if one ate them by themselves it is permitted to eat cheese immediately afterward even if there was not sixty.
