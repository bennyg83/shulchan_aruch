and he brought there Issur VeHeter chapter 34 and Turei Chayim general rules 57 and 58 and Hagahot ShaDa general rule 57 — an incident occurred in Mainz, etc., that it appears like Rama, and he rejected this — who will tell us that in pouring he deals with, etc., and he expanded and wrote at the end that Beit Yosef and Turei Chayim did not bring this Hagahot ShaDa, and he raised practical halachah that in a place of slight loss one need not forbid if they poured from a ben yomo dairy vessel or the reverse with water to which yad soledes bo applied, even if the vessel onto which they poured was soiled — so too; and it appears that so is also Shach's view; and in my innovations I brought Taz's reason that they consider the splash of pouring as if it were in the vessel from which he pours; and Minchat Yaakov challenged this, for in prohibition we do not say connected flow for b'dieved, as he wrote in general rule 23; and I wrote that in metal vessels where hagalah is possible it is like l'chatchila, as it appears in Shach siman 103; and there I wrote that perhaps one who holds roasted is permitted agrees in this that specifically the food that has no remedy is b'dieved, unlike the vessel; and in responsum of Masat Binyamin siman 20 he wrote there is no reason to distinguish between food and vessel, and so in practical halachah this siman s.k. 5 he also wrote, and we do not know from where the custom that food is permitted and vessel forbidden — behold the entire essence of this prohibition is as Taz wrote, etc.; and according to what he wrote it works out, and so explained in Shach siman 94 s.k. 34 and he explained Shach's words there who wrote one may not permit the vessel for the reason of Mordechai that grease spoils in honey, etc.; and however I wrote there that accordingly there was room to permit in every case that has no remedy through hagalah; and in Shach siman 94 s.k. 15 it appears that even in every case we require three noten taam, see there; and in siman 103 in Nekudot HaKesef I shortened here; and it appears to me for practical halachah that in earthenware considered like porcelain there is room to permit after it was within twenty-four hours since there is slight loss and it is like food; but in a vessel where hagalah is possible — one purges and what of it; and see in Maharam in Lublin general rule 33 regarding kli sheni.
