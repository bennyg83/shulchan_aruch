Fish that were cooked or, etc. So wrote Ran and Rambam and Rif, unlike the view of Ribban in name of Rashi who wrote in Tosafot s.v. halachta and brought proof from a cucumber that one cut, etc.; and even though noten taam bar noten taam — according to the second explanation in Rashi who distinguishes that through its sharpness it absorbs from the mouth of boiling fish and this is its absorption, but when they were cooked it is no worse than a cucumber; and Tosafot wrote; however for the first explanation in Rashi, because of the oil on its surface there is no proof. And Semak and Rosh permit when cooked and forbid when roasted, because in cooking there are three noten taam — from the pot to the water and from the water to the vegetable, for most of the taste spreads in water, which is not so in roasting; and proof from what they said one does not smear the oven, etc.; and in Tosafot they rejected this proof too, for an oven cannot be cleaned well and it is visible, and so too here; and this is why he wrote well washed — to exclude a bowl that is not well washed; but stam well washed — for this is the difference between a bowl in which fish came up and a cucumber cut with a knife, as above; and so too that which they permit in chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah boiled oil and hot — learn that stam they rinse them; and Tosafot in Chullin 8b s.v. the slaughterer, etc., and in Avodah Zarah 38b s.v. if, etc.
