When they were cooked — Mechaber and Rama brought the view of those who say whether they came up specifically or even when they were cooked; and I found support for those who say specifically they came up, for in the Gemara at the end of Avodah Zarah we say every day purging is made from one to the other, and the Gemara challenges: sin-offering and peace offerings — this is eaten in one day and that in two days, etc. And it answers: sin-offering slaughtered today and again they cook in it peace offerings slaughtered yesterday that completed their times together, and again they cook peace offerings slaughtered today when the time ended, etc. And one must understand what the Gemara sought here in cooking two types of peace offerings — yesterday's and today's — and it did not say immediately after cooking the sin-offering to cook today's slaughtered peace offerings; and why do I need cooking yesterday's peace offerings? However according to what the Tosafot supplement for one who says when cooked it is forbidden — one must say there regarding a sacred vessel we establish it on a Torah basis to nullify species in its species in the majority, and this is certainly to rely on the majority — there is some hesitation until they said regarding something that has a permissible aspect — therefore it is not nullified: why should you rely on the majority — wait until it is permitted; And from this it is proven that when cooked it is forbidden, for if permitted there is no nullification of leftover at all, for it is noten taam bar noten taam of heter as Tosafot wrote; and certainly according to Rambam that rinsing was immediately before it became leftover — if so the Gemara that challenges heter that comes to prohibition means even though now it is permitted, nevertheless ultimately it becomes prohibition; if so according to this view even noten taam bar noten taam of heter is not called, as regarding chametz on Pesach according to many authors — and this is permitted; but according to the conclusion that it is heter that when its prohibition time arrives it no longer exists in substance — even this is noten taam bar noten taam of heter, and it is simple; but apart from Rambam and his supporters the matters are clear as stated.
