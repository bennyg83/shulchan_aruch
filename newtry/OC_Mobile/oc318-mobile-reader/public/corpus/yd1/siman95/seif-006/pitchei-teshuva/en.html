Eshel Avraham challenged this law; and in responsum of Knesset Yechezkel I expanded on this and answered the challenge of the authors on Shach — behold they are Maharam in Lublin general rule 58 s.k. 26 and Korban Netanel s.k. 4 and Chavot Tzvi in responsum siman 101 and responsum of Knesset Yechezkel siman 28 — all challenged Shach; and I established Shach's words here in seif 4 as correct, and at the end I wrote it appears Mechaber's intent is not that ash can spoil the prohibition, but his intent is specifically here in meat and milk ash helps to weaken taste so it should not apply afterward there in Bach, and as we find in siman 87 in congealed milk of the stomach, see there in Shach s.k. 41; and with this Shach's challenge is removed, also Taz's challenge from Maharil, see there. And see further in She'elat Ya'avetz part 1 siman 156 and responsum of Makom Shmuel siman 74 and responsum of Tzemach Tzedek siman 47.
