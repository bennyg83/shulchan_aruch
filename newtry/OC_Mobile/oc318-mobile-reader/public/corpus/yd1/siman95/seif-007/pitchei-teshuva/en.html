Noten taam lefegem. Be'er Heitev s.k. 18; and see in responsum of Noda Biyehudah second edition part 14 siman 57 regarding the matter of sourdough starter that they make from lye of borax — whether it is permitted to place it in dough so the dough will leaven; and he answered that even though the Gaon author of Tzemach Tzedek testified regarding borax that it is very spoiled, nevertheless perhaps the borax is spoiled and the lye perhaps is not spoiled, for every good taste of milk entered the lye; however there is room to permit, for it is known that the sourdough starter itself must be placed in a burning oven and it becomes all fire within it, and this is no stricter than burnt offerings whose ash is permitted; and see there further that even if the work of the sourdough starter were in another manner that was not actually burnt, nevertheless b'dieved the dough that was leavened through it is not forbidden for several reasons, see there.
