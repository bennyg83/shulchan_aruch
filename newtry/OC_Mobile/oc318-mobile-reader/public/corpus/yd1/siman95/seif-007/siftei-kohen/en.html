Salt placed, etc. There is no taste because it is noten taam bar noten taam, for salt is a sharp thing and makes it as if visible, as the Rav wrote in the gloss seif 2 and Mechaber siman 96 seif 3 regarding spice; rather the taste is that salt has no power to emit taste from a vessel, as below siman 105 seif 13; and accordingly, even in a forbidden bowl it is permitted.
