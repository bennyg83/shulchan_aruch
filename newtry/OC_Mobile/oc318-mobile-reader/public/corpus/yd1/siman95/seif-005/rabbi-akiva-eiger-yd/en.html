(Shach s.k. 20) That pouring does not cook so much. One may say: even if pouring does not cook to emit and absorb from vessel to vessel, nevertheless we judge that at first the grime in a dairy vessel absorbed from a clean meat vessel — pouring emits to food — and afterward when he pours again it absorbs the nevelah grime into the vessel; and from taste one may say that in a forbidden vessel, even if clean, even if we say pouring does not emit and absorb — nevertheless in the first pouring the water became nevelah from absorbing forbidden vessels, and in the second pouring it absorbs the nat bar lichtmile water into the permitted vessel; and even according to those who do not hold nat bar lichtmile in moist food, nevertheless the water with the absorbed matter in them is absorbed in the permitted vessel; and in this one may say that in water that became forbidden through pouring they themselves are permitted since it cannot be peeled; and that which Shach wrote that the clean nat bar lichtmile returns and forbids — that is when the vessel itself became forbidden and forbids since the peel is recognizable, see Shach above (siman 91 s.k. 5); but nevertheless the first time in a meat vessel and dairy vessel where one is not clean — one may say the grime permitted substance became forbidden the measure of a peel and forbids in the second pouring to the permitted vessel.
