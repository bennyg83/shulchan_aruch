For through ash it is noten taam lefegem. And according to this there is no prohibition if lye called zeif made from milk fell into a dish, for the lye has ash when they make it from milk, and requires study regarding this ruling, for above in siman 87 Rama wrote that the vessel in which they make water for head-washing, etc., and it is customary to mix meat and milk there, end of his words; and since noten taam lefegem, there is no basar b'chalav prohibition at all. And I also found in Rashal's words chapter 25 siman 94 and these are his words: and I found in name of Maharil that once a child came and said a piece of lye made from milk fell into the pot, and he said one should not believe the child, end of his words — it appears that if it certainly fell, the lye forbids; and behold lye is made from ash with milk and is noten taam lefegem, and requires study for practical halachah.
