Ash. Shach wrote: this law requires study, for it is not found in any posek, and he brings proof from poskim that proves the opposite, see there. And Taz wrote: according to this law there is no prohibition if lye called zeif made from milk fell into the dish, for the lye has ash when they make it from milk, and requires study; for in siman 87 Rama wrote that the vessel in which they make water for head-washing is accustomed to mix meat and milk there, end of his words; and since nat bar lichtmile there is no basar b'chalav prohibition at all. And I also found in Rashal's words: and I found in Maharil's words that once a child came and said a piece of lye made from milk fell into the pot, and he said one should not believe the child — it appears that if it certainly fell, the lye forbids; and behold it is nat bar lichtmile, and requires study for practical halachah (and Nachalat Binyamin wrote one may distinguish that here he speaks only of reducing clinging fat on the surface of the vessel through ash, unlike in the substance of the milk, see there).
