(There in the gloss) And some are stringent with roasting and cooking, etc. And one may analyze: how did Rama rule without qualification that it is forbidden — it implies even in a measure where there is no taste it is forbidden; and why, since there is no meat taste here at all, why should it be forbidden? And one may say that Rif explains that which we say this fish that he sealed meat in it is forbidden to eat with kutach — even though we hold smell is not a matter — and Rif answered that even though smell is not a matter, nevertheless something exists; and these fish, since they can be eaten without kutach, are like something that has a permissible aspect — forbidden in something, since in any case there is some taste and it is forbidden; and so too here is comparable.
