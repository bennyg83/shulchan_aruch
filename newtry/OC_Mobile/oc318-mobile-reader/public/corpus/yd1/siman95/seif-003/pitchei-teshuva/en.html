And the water — they treat it with prohibition. See in Turei Chayim end of general rule 58 who wrote: one may learn from this if one stuck a ben yomo meat spoon into a pot of milk that is not ben yomo full of water, or the reverse, etc. — the water, however, is forbidden; and Maharam in Lublin wrote at the beginning of general rule of taste: one who sticks a spoon that is not ben yomo into water — they are forbidden — that is without heating for drinking; if so when heated for drinking, and requires study for practical halachah, see there.
