Meat bowls that were rinsed in a dairy cauldron in hot water that yad soledes bo applies to them — even if both are ben yomo — permitted, because it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of heter; and this is when he says it is clear to me that there was no clinging fat on them; and if there was clinging fat on them — there must be in the water sixty corresponding to the substance of the fat on the surface of the bowl: {Rama: And some forbid even if there is no clinging fat on them (Tur in name of Semak and Semag and Semak and ShaDa and Rif and Tosafot and Mordechai and Ran and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Piskei Maharai and Orach Chaim) unless one of the vessels is not ben yomo from absorption of a kli rishon; and then all the vessels are permitted; and the water is treated with prohibition l'chatchila; but if both are ben yomo and he rinsed them together in a kli rishon — all is forbidden; and so they practice and one should not change; and specifically when they were rinsed together and in a kli rishon; but if they were rinsed one after the other, or in a kli sheni — even together — all is permitted (Arukh general rule 34); and if he poured from a kli rishon of meat onto a dairy vessel — its law is like a kli rishon and it forbids if it was ben yomo; but if he poured boiling water that is neither of meat nor of milk onto meat and dairy vessels together — even if clinging fat is on them — all is permitted, for pouring is not literally like a kli rishon such that the vessels onto which he pours will absorb one from the other (there). And if a dairy bowl is found among meat vessels — we are not concerned lest they were rinsed together in the manner that is forbidden (Hagahot ShaDa and Orach Chaim).}
