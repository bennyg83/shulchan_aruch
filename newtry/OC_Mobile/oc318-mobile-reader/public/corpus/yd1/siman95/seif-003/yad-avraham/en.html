(There) But if it was a sharp item, such as if they cooked sharp things in a meat vessel, etc. — if they ate it with milk it forbids even b'dieved. One must say it is not specifically with milk that it forbids — for even if they placed it in a dairy vessel it forbids even b'dieved, for there is no noten taam lefegem and no noten taam bar noten taam of heter, for a sharp item emits and behold first taste is absorbed in it; and if they placed it afterward in a vessel of the second species it also emits from it taste like the first, and behold meat and milk substance are absorbed. And requires study in Eseret Yisrael parah chapter 22: Maharam in Lublin wrote general rule 57 that stam a pot and bowl have sixty in the dish against the grime, only in rinsing we do not say so; and if so in such a case there is no relevance to what he wrote that stam vessels are clean, except when they did not use them abundantly — Beit Shmuel disagrees on him.
