An egg that was cooked in water in a dairy pot — permitted to place it inside poultry, even l'chatchila; but if it was cooked in a pot with meat — even in its shell — forbidden to eat it with kutach: {Rama: And some are stringent with roasting and cooking to forbid noten ta'am bar noten ta'am (Ribban in name of Rashi and Arukh general rule 34 — Mordechai brought it and Or Zarua); and the custom is to forbid l'chatchila; and b'dieved permitted in every case (Arukh); and specifically to eat with milk and the meat itself; but to place them in their vessels — permitted l'chatchila (Issur veHeter HaArukh); and so they practiced; and likewise if they did not cook or roast at first but only came up in a meat vessel — permitted to eat them with milk itself and likewise the reverse (his own reasoning); and likewise if the vessel in which they were cooked or roasted was for spoilage such that it was not ben yomo, they practice permissibility l'chatchila to eat them with the other species (there in Arukh); and all this when the food is not a sharp item; but if it was a sharp item, such as if they cooked sharp things in a meat vessel — even if it is not ben yomo, or if they ground spices in a meat mortar — if they ate it with milk it forbids even b'dieved until there is sixty against the meat absorbed in them (Arukh general rule 24, and so appears in responsum of Rashba siman 249 and Beit Yosef siman 96 in name of Semak and Hagahot ShaDa siman 62 in name of Maharash and Agur in name of Mordechai); and nevertheless a sharp food item is not called such on account of a small amount of spice in it, only if the whole thing is a sharp item and the majority is like its entirety; and see above siman 96.}
