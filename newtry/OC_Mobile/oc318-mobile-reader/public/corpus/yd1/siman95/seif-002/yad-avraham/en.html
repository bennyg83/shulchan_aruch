(Seif 2 in the gloss) And likewise if the vessel in which they were cooked was for spoilage — they practice permissibility l'chatchila. This is the intent of R' Yerucham that Beit Yosef brought in name of s.v. and these are his words: and if it is not ben yomo it is permitted to eat that dish with cheese, end of his words. And in order Beit Yosef challenged him that he rules exactly like the gloss. And so too Hagahot Maimoniyot — Darkei Moshe brought them above siman 4 regarding two spoons that were stuck in a pot that is forbidden to use l'chatchila — noten taam bar noten taam is forbidden l'chatchila in cooking, as Shulchan Aruch wrote in siman 4 above seif 5; and Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote that if the second spoon was stuck after twenty-four hours from the first insertion, it is permitted l'chatchila to use it for the other species, end of his words. And since so wrote R' Yerucham and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Issur VeHeter and Darkei Moshe and the gloss and Taz — one need not heed Rashal who forbids in this. Also his proof to forbid roasted b'dieved even if they already placed it in milk — from that he cited came up and did not cite roasted which is a greater stringency, and Maharam in Lublin brought it — I did not understand, for he said only that it is not permitted l'chatchila with milk like came up, and so is in the gloss siman 4 above seif 5 and in Darkei Moshe there as he wrote there.
