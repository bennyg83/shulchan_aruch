And the custom is to forbid l'chatchila, etc. And fowl meat — I am uncertain whether one should be stringent with it, since it is entirely d'rabbanan, or not; and it would appear that since Tosafot were uncertain whether they were cooked or only came up, and he wishes to bring proof from an oven plastered with tallow, etc., and he did not explain that kutach is different because it is only d'rabbanan — for Tosafot hold kutach is d'rabbanan as I wrote above siman 91 — that Tosafot hold in this there is no distinction between d'oraisa and d'rabbanan.
