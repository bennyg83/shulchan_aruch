And b'dieved permitted in every case. And specifically when cooked, for there are three noten taam since the main taste goes in the middle of the water; but when roasted it is forbidden — Shach. And accordingly also when cooked — even though the fish are permitted, nevertheless the gravy is forbidden, for it is like roasted — Peri Megadim and Chokhmat Adam; and Chokhmat Adam also wrote that when roasted is forbidden is specifically when one roasted fish on a spit or in a vessel in which meat was roasted, but if one roasted fish in a pot in which meat was cooked it is permitted, since meat taste was absorbed in the vessel through cooking — considered from the outset like three noten taam; and if one cut hot fish with a knife that cut meat it is like roasted, for it is strained and hot cooks, see there; and this law of cutting fish with a knife Beit Yosef wrote in siman 96 in Hagahat Beit Yosef note 5, and see in responsum of Pri Tevuah siman 22 in the gloss who disagrees and wrote one need not be concerned, and also Peri Megadim in siman 97 in Meshivtzot note 3 left this law requiring study, see there.
