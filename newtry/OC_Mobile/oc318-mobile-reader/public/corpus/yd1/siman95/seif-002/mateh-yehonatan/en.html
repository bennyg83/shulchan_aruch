(There seif 2) Egg, etc. — but if it was cooked in a pot with meat it is forbidden to eat it with kutach. See Acharonim who wondered, for the essence of this law is only for permission — at first glance, from where would we say that an egg is worse than a meat pot, for it is explained above at the beginning of siman 93 that it is forbidden to cook milk in it? And one may say according to Rashi's words at the end of chapter 25, and these are his words: and milk found congealed on the hide of the stomach that they salt on its hide — it appears to me a full prohibition, etc., and some permit it and bring proof from fish that came up in a bowl that we ruled permitted to eat them with kutach because it is noten taam bar noten taam, etc., and they are as far from each other as east from west, etc., but milk salted in meat that imparted taste to each other — both become prohibition, etc., end of Rashi's words. And one may analyze: how does he compare to fish that came up in a bowl — behold here it is a second taste in prohibition, for as soon as taste comes from meat from the stomach it is immediately taste in prohibition? And one must say that Rashi holds according to the view of R' Meir that Rashba brought, that even if the second taste is in prohibition it is also permitted, for we do not say taste of prohibition except for taste coming from the substance of prohibition and not taste from taste even if it is in prohibition; if so, accordingly it is difficult: that which is taught, a pot in which meat was cooked — one may not cook milk in it — behold this too is taste from taste, and nevertheless it is forbidden; and one must say that specifically regarding a food item that has taste and emission from itself — if so, taste that emits afterward is not full taste but taste from taste, unlike a vessel that has no taste and emission from itself — if so, it is not taste from taste at all but all is first taste, for what is absorbed in the pot is only as if all is enclosed within the pot; therefore a pot in which meat was cooked may not have milk cooked in it, for it is first taste; and accordingly Shulchan Aruch needed to inform us that even in an egg it is forbidden — to exclude the view of Rashi and those Tosafot above, for there in siman 93 regarding a pot it is forbidden because all is like first taste, but here behold it is taste from taste and would not be forbidden — he informs us that it is forbidden, and it is clear. [Gloss of Maharav Maharash HaCohen may he live long, and see in Peri Megadim in the introduction to laws of basar b'chalav that he investigated whether second taste is d'oraisa or d'rabbanan and wrote that for us, who hold taste of prohibition is from Torah as we derive from purgings of idolaters, it is proven that second taste too is from Torah — see there; however for the above it is not proof, only for taste absorbed in the pot, but for taste absorbed in food it is not proof, requires study. End of gloss.]
