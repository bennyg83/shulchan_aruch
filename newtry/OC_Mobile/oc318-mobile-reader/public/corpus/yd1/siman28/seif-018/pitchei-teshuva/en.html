Kill by stabbing or uproot. [See responsum Noda Biyhudah, second series, part 4, siman 10: asked regarding a man whom God enriched with broad inheritance, forests and fields with chayot — whether permitted to go himself to hunt with burning reed, or forbidden because of tza'ar ba'alei chayim or bal tashchit; answered: by law no prohibition, for all that man needs — no tza'ar ba'alei chayim; tza'ar ba'alei chayim applies only to its pain and leaving alive, not to killing; and no bal tashchit, for he benefits from hide and does not destroy path; further, essence of bal tashchit prohibition is not destroying what man can benefit from; but thing with no loss to anyone — no bal tashchit; these chayot while alive have no benefit, only in death in hides and flesh — certainly no bal tashchit; nevertheless unseemly trait — cruelty; not his craft; also prohibition putting himself in danger; also causes mention of sins; one whose livelihood is such hunting — no choice; but one whose main intent is not killing — forbidden to do so — see there; and see responsum Shemesh Tzedakah, part 4, siman 18 and 57 on the above matter.]
