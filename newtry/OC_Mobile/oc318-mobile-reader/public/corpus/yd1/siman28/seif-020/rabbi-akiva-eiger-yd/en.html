(Seif 20) And if safek treifah found — cover without blessing. See Torat HaDeshen who expanded: we are not concerned lest they see him covering and eat the flesh and say he covers from safek. He wrote: also not proof from covering koy — there different, for signs of chayah in horns and hooves are also needed. Nevertheless since we do not find this concern except regarding deaf-mute, imbecile, minor — there most actions defective — we do not infer other safekot from them — end. And I wonder why he wrote this as decisive without proof — for it is compelled from Tosafot's difficulty there s.v. sippa: how from their blood or bird from safek forbidden to act for covering — positive and negative commandment — they needed their answer that sugya holds in safek certainly obligate covering and we are not concerned they will say excellent shechitah; if so, also for bird permit — see there. Proven explicitly: in safek obligate covering. And more: conclusion Rabbanan fed up with the matter disagree — behold Chachamim obligate in shechitah of deaf-mute, imbecile, minor in covering; although their shechitah forbidden from safek — nevertheless obligate covering and no concern they will say excellent shechitah; and R' Meir who exempts also permits for bird; therefore he holds most actions defective; why exempt from covering. But for this matter of safek obligating covering we find no one disagrees with Rabbanan — clear proof without answer. And I saw Pri Megadim explained Bach's intent: even in safek without strong presumption of prohibition — exempt from covering by law, since forbidden from safek not in "that may be eaten." This too is astonishing: Chachamim obligate deaf-mute, imbecile, minor in covering, as above; and presumably those Chachamim of R' Meir are mishnah Chachamim earlier: slaughtered and treifah found — exempt from covering — from verse "that may be eaten" as there in sugya. And more: even if we force those Chachamim are not these Chachamim and hold treifah must cover; therefore safek in shechitah must cover from safek — nevertheless the question itself: why do Rabbanan disagree on the first part from difficulty of questioner on bird from covering? Perhaps truly those Rabbanim are they who expound "that may be eaten" — therefore exempt in safek since not permitted to eat; if so, every safek must cover from safek lest not treifah and in "that may be eaten" — clear. Further Pri Megadim wrote there: even rabbinic treifot exempt from covering — nevertheless not in "that may be eaten." Hidden from sugya Chullin (daf 27b): they challenge Necher and Ma'aker, etc. — nechirah is its shechitah; nevertheless rabbinically forbidden in nechirah. However this can be rejected: they challenge well: this mishnah of Necher is the end of mishnah "slaughtered and treifah found" — first teaches they disagree on treifah, then teaches Necher and Ma'aker — all exempt; therefore challenge well: one who obligates on treifah who does not expound "that may be eaten" — only nechirah is not shechitah at all; if no shechitah for Torah matter, say nechirah is its shechitah — only forbidden rabbinically — only like treifah must cover. Similarly they challenge further: teach "slaughtered and needs blood — how act? necher or ma'aker" — also on this path: since requires specifically necher and not suffice to make treifah — therefore holds treifah must cover; therefore say nechirah is its shechitah. However, proven from sugya Chullin (daf 86a): not only "go out and trample" which is shechitah of R' Eliezer, etc., but even "go out and necher," etc. — "as I commanded you" — these have no shechitah from Torah — would have been obligated in covering although treifah exempt; reason of R' Meir: verse "that may be eaten" — as said there explicitly. Proven: rabbinic prohibition does not remove mitzvah of covering.
