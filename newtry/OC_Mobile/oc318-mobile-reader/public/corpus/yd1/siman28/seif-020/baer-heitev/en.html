In this. Bach disagrees with Mechaber and ruled: if safek found — what is exempt from covering; Taz disagrees and agreed with Mechaber; and so Maharshal.
To cover. Shach wrote: must bless — not like Bach who rules cover without blessing. Likewise shehiyah, darsah, haladah, hagramah in minority in simanim — must cover with blessing (Shach). One who slaughters for dogs or for idolaters' eating — must cover. Minhag in the name of Shach.
