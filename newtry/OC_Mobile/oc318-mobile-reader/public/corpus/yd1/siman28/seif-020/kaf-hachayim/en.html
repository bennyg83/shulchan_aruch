And they cannot receive blood in vessel to cover at night, for one does not slaughter into vessels — Tur; reason as above siman 11 seif 3 lest they say receiving blood to throw to idolatry — see there. Bach wrote: nowadays b'dieved if slaughtered on Yom Tov can receive in vessel even l'chatchila; nevertheless better place earth and pebbles in vessel before receiving — see there; Beit Din Shach s.k. 10. Derishah wrote: perhaps here too concern before observer he took earth to cover koy blood by sage's command and they will permit its milk; and so Darchei Shalom; Minhag in Hagahot Tashbetz part 14; Likutei Halachot part 3; and Shem Chadash agreed part 4; Turei Zahav in Orach Chaim siman 498 s.k. 34; Minhag Tziyon, general 23 in Asheret part 10; Zivchei Rambam siman 19 in additions part 4.
