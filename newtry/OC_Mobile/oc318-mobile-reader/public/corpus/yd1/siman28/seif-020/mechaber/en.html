One who slaughters a chayah does not cover until he checks the lung; and if a safek treifah was found, he covers without a blessing. The same applies to every disqualification due to doubt — such as when we are concerned perhaps the hide was damaged; and all similar cases. {Rama: But something that by law is kosher, only they are stringent about it — such as if he cut the entire spinal column and similar — they are obligated to cover (Terumat HaDeshen siman 184).}
