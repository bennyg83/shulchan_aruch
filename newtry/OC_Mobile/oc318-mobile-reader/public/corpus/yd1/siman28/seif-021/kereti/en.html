The Sages enacted for him — we did not find in Gemara, only Geonim; therefore many raise objections; nevertheless one who wants to be stringent may; one who wants to be lenient certainly should not protest, for he has Geonim to rely on — perhaps for tikkun olam: they saw breaches eating without covering at all — they enacted this so kisuy mitzvah is remembered; without enactment travelers in western lands deserts would transgress this mitzvah with their hands — therefore for blessing, enactment of Geonim is found — many berachot on Geonic enactments; therefore we do not depart from Rama's words; but all this is western lands and surroundings where one travels long before earth or vessels — no wood with him; in our country where prepared earth is available even if lacking a day or two — do not rely on this and nullify kisuy hadam — clear.
