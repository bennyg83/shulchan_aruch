One who has no, etc. So in the baraita there 88b: one traveling in the desert, etc.
(Likut) One who has no, etc.: from mishnah 87b "and one does not slaughter," etc.; and first chapter of Yom Tov 5:1 "if so, what is different," etc. — "not necessary" he says, etc.; and there 2 (1 in mishnah) "and in a boat," etc.; and Chullin 31a "and behold covering is required," etc. — end.
As loss, etc. There in the baraita.
They enacted, etc. Because of bal tashchit; and that they said in the Gemara this enactment of burning, etc. — there when it equals, then there is no bal tashchit, as in chapter HaChovel (Bava Kamma 91b): if worth more for building, permitted to cut — and so Rambam Hilchot Melachim chapter 6 (and Semag lavin 129); and that he blesses — since when he absorbs in the garment it is beginning of covering although he does not complete until later. This enactment of Geonim is forbidden when possible through the Gemara's enactment; therefore they did not teach there this enactment of Geonim; and this is what "and not equal," etc. — specifically in such a case — Darkei Moshe.
And bless, etc. And although he has not yet fulfilled covering — like on checking chametz we bless on bi'ur, as Ra'osh wrote (chapter 1 of Pesachim siman 10): the beginning of burning is it.
He launders, etc. As written (87a): blood mixed in water if there is appearance, etc.
