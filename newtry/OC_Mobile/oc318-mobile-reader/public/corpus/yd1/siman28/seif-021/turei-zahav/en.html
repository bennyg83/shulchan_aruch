The Sages enacted for him, etc. Beit Yosef brought Mordechai's words in the name of Geonim and challenged them: if so, the tanna should have taught this enactment before what he taught about burning gold; and further, how can he bless on covering with earth — behold he does not cover with earth, only absorbs in his garment? Therefore he wrote these words have no substance. Rama in Darkei Moshe answered the first difficulty: the tanna deals when the bird equals the gold or garment burned, and Geonim deal when it does not equal; therefore impossible otherwise, and they made this enactment. He did not answer the second: since when absorbing blood in the garment it is the beginning of covering and he can bless although he does not complete covering until later — like checking chametz, etc. This too is nothing: unlike bi'ur chametz where bi'ur is impossible without checking first, so at the time they commanded bi'ur, checking was included; unlike here where no man does so — only this person because he has no earth; certainly one cannot say this is included in the original command; on the contrary one should say he should not slaughter at all since he has no earth to cover. Maharach answered the second difficulty: one does not bless on covering blood with earth, but bless on covering the blood, as in Tosefta — the essence of the blessing formula is so; and study is needed: even so, we require covering be with earth as explicit in the verse "and cover it with earth," only need not mention it; as I noted above seif 2 in the question. Therefore it appears clear we should not practice this enactment; and so I saw Rashal in his book, chapter Kisuy HaDam, siman 17: it appears these words have no substance; although Or Zaru'a brought them, if it were truly Geonim's words, Rif or Rambam would not have omitted them — end. And Levush also wrote one does not bless on this covering, neither at the beginning nor end — implying he was only strict about blessing, but the essence of covering one may do; and I say: one who has no earth to cover should not slaughter at all, since this enactment does not exist, as Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote here — in my humble opinion clear.
