One who has no earth to cover should not slaughter. {Rama: Rather he should wait until he has earth (Tur); and if he travels in a desert or on a ship and the bird is not worth the loss of a garment he would burn to ash, or gold he would grind — the Sages enacted for him to slaughter and to absorb the blood in a garment or sandal, and bless; and when he reaches a place of earth he launders the garment or sandal so the blood comes out and covers without a blessing (Mordechai in the name of Geonim); and so is practiced (Or Zaru'a and Raavan).}
