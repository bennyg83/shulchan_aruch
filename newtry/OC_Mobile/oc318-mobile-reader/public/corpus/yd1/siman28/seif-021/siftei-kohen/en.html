The Sages enacted for him, etc. Beit Yosef rejected these words: if so, the tanna should have taught this enactment before "burn his garment," for there is also bal tashchit; and further, since he does not cover with earth, how can he bless on it? Therefore it appears these words have no substance — end. And Rav in Darkei Moshe answered the first difficulty: the tanna deals when the bird equals the gold or garment he burns, and the Geonim deal when it does not equal; therefore it is impossible otherwise, and they made this enactment. And he did not answer the second: since when he absorbs blood in the garment it is the beginning of covering, and he can bless even though he does not complete covering until later — like checking chametz where we bless on bi'ur chametz although we do not burn until tomorrow (as explained in Orach Chaim siman 432); and the same here, etc.; and afterward I found in Or Zaru'a that he brought the Geonim's words and expanded on reasons and wrote one may rely on them — end Darkei Moshe; and so I found in Raavan who brought their words for halakhic ruling; and Maharshal, chapter 25, siman 17, followed Beit Yosef to reject the Geonim's words; and such rejection returns and appears wrong after it is explicit in great poskim mentioned; and their words are also settled — Maharshal himself brought the Geonim's words anonymously in his Hagahot to shechitot of Mahariu (indeed you will find several times in his book he retracts his first words). And so Bach. However what Darkei Moshe compared this law to checking chametz — I do not hold so: there the reason is checking is the beginning of bi'ur, and bi'ur is impossible without checking, and checking is tied to bi'ur — unlike here. It appears to me to add also the reason Bach wrote: the blessing was enacted only on covering blood, not on earth, as in Tosefta and Yerushalmi and poskim; and although we practice saying "with earth," it is for ease of speech; but here we establish the law, and therefore when he absorbs in his garment one does not say "with earth"; and also in the Geonim's language it says "he blesses on the covering" and no more; and Atzei Erez wrote: it appears one should not bless l'chatchila when absorbing in his garment, nor afterward when laundering the garment; and had he seen Rav's words in Darkei Moshe he would not have written so.]
