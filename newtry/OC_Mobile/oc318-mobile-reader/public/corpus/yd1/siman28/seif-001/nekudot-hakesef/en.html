(Siman 28, Taz s.k. 12) He wants to reconcile the words of Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Maharam — and it is forced; the matter is clear as I wrote in my Mareh Makom: Hagahot Maimoniyot deals when they were mixed together, for there they wrote thus on what Rambam wrote there: if blood was mixed in wine or in blood of behemah we view them as if they were water — if it is possible that the appearance of blood would be seen, he must cover it in this measure as if it were water — and Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote on this: obligated to cover, as the plain mishnah; and so my teacher ruled on one who slaughtered a kosher hen and again slaughtered another and it became neveilah — he must cover — end of his words; behold as I explained.
