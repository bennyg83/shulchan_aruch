Prepared in his hand. For it is written "that hunts" — in any case; if so, why is "hunts" stated? The Torah teaches proper conduct: a person should not eat meat except with this preparation — so Rashi explains: as if it is hunting that is not prepared for him, meaning he should not eat meat frequently.
A mitzvah in itself. And one can say from what Ra'osh wrote and Tur brought, siman 20: the mitzvah of covering completes the mitzvah of shechitah; in truth it is a mitzvah in itself, only related after the mitzvah of shechitah, like tefillin of the head with tefillin of the arm. And one should not challenge: why did Rama rule in Hagahah siman 19 seif 4 that one may speak between covering and shechitah — what is different from speaking between tefillin and tefillin? One can say: regarding tefillin the blessing of the head applies also to the arm, as we noted in laws of tefillin.
