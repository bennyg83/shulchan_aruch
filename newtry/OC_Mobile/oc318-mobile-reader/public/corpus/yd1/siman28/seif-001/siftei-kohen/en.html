Whether they were prepared in his hand. — Such as geese and chickens — for one might say from what is written "that hunts" it would exclude geese and chickens and similar that stand ready for hunting; therefore the verse teaches "hunting" in any case — end of his words.
And covering is a mitzvah in itself, etc. — See above siman 19 s.k. 6.
Kosher, etc. — It implies even for himself; and so Darkei Moshe; and so Beit Yosef and Maharshal, chapter Kisuy HaDam, siman 1, and other latter authorities — not like Bach who forbids for himself. However, where the people are lax in the mitzvah of covering — they do not cover properly, or do not bless on covering or on shechitah — one should penalize and obligate lashes; and also forbid shechitah to the transgressor because of erecting a fence; so appears from Maharshal's words there; and see above siman 19.
