Covered. And so Taz wrote: although Ra'osh wrote the mitzvah of covering completes shechitah — one must say in truth it is a mitzvah in itself, only related after shechitah like head tefillin after arm tefillin. And one should not challenge why Rama ruled in Hagahah siman 19 that if he spoke between shechitah and covering it is kosher — unlike speaking between tefillin and tefillin; one must say regarding tefillin the head blessing applies also to the arm, unlike shechitah and covering; from "kosher" stam it implies even for himself. And Bach forbids for himself — from Maharam.
