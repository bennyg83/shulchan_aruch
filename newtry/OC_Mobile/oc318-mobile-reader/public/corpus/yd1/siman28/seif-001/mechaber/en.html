The laws of covering the blood. In it are 24 seifim. One who slaughters a chayah or bird must cover its blood — whether on his side now or whether they were prepared in his hand. {Rama: And covering is a mitzvah in itself (so appears in Hagahot Ashiri, chapter Kisuy HaDam); but the shechitah is kosher even if he acted willfully and did not cover (Beit Yosef, and so Chayei Adam, and so Kol Bo).}
