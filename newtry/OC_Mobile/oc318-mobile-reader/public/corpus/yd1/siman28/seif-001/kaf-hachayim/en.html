A) [Seif 1] One who slaughters chayah or bird must cover its blood. The reason is written in Midrash Rabbah, parashat Bereishit: because they buried Abel and the Holy One blessed be He gave them reward — two blessings they bless on them: one for shechitah and one for covering blood — see there; meaning: as they buried him and covered his blood, so their reward that their blood requires covering — measure for measure.
D) If after he slaughtered he consecrated the blood — he must cover because mitzvah of covering preceded consecration — so we learned in Tosefta. The same if he consecrated the chayah or bird after slaughter — Rambam chapter 14 of Hilchot Shechitah law 3; Minhag in Hagahot Tashbetz part 5; Zivchei Tzedek part 3.
E) There. Whether on his side now, etc. For Sages taught: "that hunts" — I have only one who hunts; hunted ones standing by themselves — from where geese and chickens? Teach "hunting" in any case. If so, why "that hunts"? Torah teaches proper conduct: a person should not eat meat except with this preparation — Chullin 83a. Rashi: except with this preparation — as if hunting not prepared for him, meaning do not eat meat frequently lest he become poor — end; and Beit Yosef brought it; Taz s.k. 1.
F) There in Hagahah. But shechitah is kosher, etc. Even for himself; and so Darkei Moshe; and so Beit Yosef and Rashal, chapter Kisuy HaDam siman 1, and other latter authorities — not like Bach who forbids for himself — Shach s.k. 3; Pri Chadash part 3; Likutei Halachot part 2; Kereti part 1; Shem Chadash part 2; Zivchei Tzedek part 4.
