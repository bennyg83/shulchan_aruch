A) [Seif 1] One who slaughters chayah or bird must cover its blood. The reason is written in Midrash Rabbah, parashat Bereishit: because they buried Abel and the Holy One blessed be He gave them reward — two blessings they bless on them: one for shechitah and one for covering blood — see there; meaning: as they buried him and covered his blood, so their reward that their blood requires covering — measure for measure.
