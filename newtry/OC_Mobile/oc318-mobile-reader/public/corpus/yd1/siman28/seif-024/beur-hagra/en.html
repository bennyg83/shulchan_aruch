Therefore, etc. End of chapter 3 of Pesachim (47b).
And one does not, etc. Not like the Gaon wrote to cover with snow, as written (Iyov 37:6) "for to snow He says 'Be earth'" — for it is not written "Be dust"; and further: the Holy One commanded it should be on earth — for it is not written it should be called earth; and also when it melts and warms it is water, and one covers with water.
