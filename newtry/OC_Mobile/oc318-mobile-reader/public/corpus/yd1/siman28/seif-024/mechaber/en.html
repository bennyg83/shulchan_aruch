One does not cover with desert dust because it is salty land and does not grow. {Rama: Therefore one does not cover with moist earth from water such as Mitoneta (Tur, Semak, Semag); and one does not cover with snow (Beit Yosef and Or Zaru'a in the name of a Gaon, not like Mordechai in the name of a Gaon).}
