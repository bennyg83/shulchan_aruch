Moist. Shach wrote: it implies here moist earth does not grow — and this is not so, for in chapter Elu Overin (Pesachim daf 46) explicit it is fit for sowing and grows; rather the reason we do not cover with moist earth is we require fine earth fit to be counted, as written "if a man can count the dust of the earth," etc.; therefore it appears "therefore" that Rama wrote is a scribal error and must read "and so." Rambam end Hilchot Shechitah: permitted to cover with dust of an ir hanidachat; and the same with dust of idolatry and other issurei hana'ah, because mitzvot were not given for benefit (Maharam).
