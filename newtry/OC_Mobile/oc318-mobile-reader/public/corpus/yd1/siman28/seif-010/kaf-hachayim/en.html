J) There. On covering blood with earth. Although we do not find anywhere mentioning the detail in the blessing except generally — it would suffice "on covering blood" alone; nevertheless here different, performing two mitzvot — below and above — learned from "with earth"; must say "with earth" for two mitzvot — Taz s.k. 3. But Rambam and other great ones brought formula "on covering blood"; and Rashbatz in Yavin Shemuah. And Kitzur Halachot wrote law is as Chayei Adam: custom when standing to say "on covering blood" and no more. But Shach s.k. 29 wrote their custom to say "on covering blood with earth"; in his long book brought so from Rokeach, Geonim, Tashbetz, Maharshal — see there; and custom of land of Tzvi and Egypt like Maran and his colleagues to say "on covering blood with earth." Neharah Neharah and Pestia. Machazik Berachah part 2; however what he wrote "and so custom," etc., to say on kisuy hadas with earth — meaning also say word "with earth"; but regarding word "blood" with hei — on the contrary Shulchan Aruch's formula without hei — this is per another version written in part 6 afterward.
