And another saw — obligated to cover. Tur gave reason: positive commandment on all Israel; but in Gemara derived from verse "say to Bnei Yisrael" — warning on all Israel; see Peleti what he wrote. I also wondered: if one acquired the mitzvah of covering, can he honor another with this mitzvah? From mohal who has a son in Eretz Yisrael to honor another to circumcise — I raised. To instruct another to cover or circumcise as agency is permitted — agent of man is like himself; but by way of honor or as agency forbidden — like Or Zaru'a and Shach.
