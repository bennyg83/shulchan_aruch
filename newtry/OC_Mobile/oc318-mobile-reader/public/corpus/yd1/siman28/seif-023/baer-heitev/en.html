In them. Shach wrote: it does not help when one places plants and they grow — rather we require specifically seeds sown in them grow.
Burned. Shach wrote: but if burned one covers; and the same for all those one does not cover — if burned one covers with their ash. One does not cover with sulfur and salt, nor ground pepper; but with ground cinnamon one covers (Minhag in the name of Shach).
