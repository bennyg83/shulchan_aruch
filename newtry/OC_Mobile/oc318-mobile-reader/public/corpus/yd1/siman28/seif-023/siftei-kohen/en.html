And shavings of pottery that carpenters plane in a plane and cut wood in it, and it leaves fine shavings like dust. Shichur — Rashi: ground charcoal; and so Arukh, entry shachor; and Rambam explained: soot of the kiln. Kocholet is dye put in the eye. Nekoret hapasulim — Rashi: dust scraped from millstones.]
