KB) [Seif 4] Buffalo — they practice not to cover its blood. It is re'em mentioned in the verse — Beit Yosef; Levush; Zivchei Tzedek part 14; it is the species called in Arabic jamus; larger than ox and similar — so Ra'avad on Amos siman 5 verse 22; and Yeshayah siman 1 verse 11; and Ralbag Melachim 1 siman 1 verse 10: re'em is a species of fat large cattle — end; and in root shoresh mara: species of large cattle; and Rambam chapter 1 Hilchot Ma'achalot Assurot law 8: such as wild ox and re'em which are ox species — end; Beit Din Zivchei Tzedek part 16; see below siman 80 seif 3 and siman 297 seif 7.
