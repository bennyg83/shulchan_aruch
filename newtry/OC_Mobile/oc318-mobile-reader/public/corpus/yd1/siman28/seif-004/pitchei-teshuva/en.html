Obligated to cover. Pri Chadash wrote; and so agreed Pri To'ar: cover without blessing because "no rejection regarding mitzvot" is an unresolved question not clarified; see Magen Avraham siman 485 s.k. 6 — his view appears he covers with blessing here; see Sefer Be'er Yaakov what he wrote and raised like Pri Chadash; however he wrote: proper to slaughter another bird first and bless on covering, then cover this one that became uncovered; but if he has no other bird — certainly cover without blessing — see there.
