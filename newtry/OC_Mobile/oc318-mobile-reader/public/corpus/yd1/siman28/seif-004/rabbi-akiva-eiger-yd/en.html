(Seif 9) One covering for all. Explained in sugya Rabbanan hold: bird does not need to teach that covering is required for chayah separately and bird separately — only if it comes to divide; and R' Yossi holds division is from its blood; therefore if it comes, he covers each separately; see Tosafot there explanation of their words: even R' Yossi is uncertain whether concerned for father's seed; and according to the side that we are concerned — proven he holds like Rabbi Yonatan: no need to divide, as explained beginning Orach Chaim — behold one depends on the other; nevertheless Talmud seeks to answer the other side that we are not concerned for father's seed — like Rabbi Yoshiah; therefore answers: from its blood; and although still somewhat difficult: one could say R' Yossi's view that he covers chayah separately and bird separately is from safek, since uncertain whether concerned for father's seed — uncertain whether like Rabbi Yoshiah or R' Yossi; therefore here too stringent: perhaps like Rabbi Yonatan and no need to divide bird — must say R' Yossi holds that by law certainly must cover each separately; nevertheless difficult for law: after we rule safek whether concerned for father's seed — we are uncertain in law whether like Rabbi Yoshiah or R' Yossi; rule here too stringently: must cover each separately. Afterward I found in Az Nidberu he stood on this in responsum of Maharam of Lublin and forced it. And why is it hard for me below (siman 259 seif 17) in Hagahah: one who made his son swear not to borrow without Reuven and Shimon's permission, and one died — can borrow with the other's permission; source from Rashba; and for law we are uncertain whether halakhah like Rabbi Yonatan or Rabbi Yoshiah from safek whether concerned for father's seed — behold one depends on the other; and study is needed.
