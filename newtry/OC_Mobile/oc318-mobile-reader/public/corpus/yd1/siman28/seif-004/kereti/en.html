To cover it without blessing — although below regarding kilayim the Mechaber wrote plainly it is a species of behemah and Rama did not correct; Rama was stringent here because he can slaughter a bird — Peri Chadash. And this is insufficient, for then on Yom Tov he would slaughter since you hold it certainly behemah and not cover; better to say Rama relied below on that case which is in the category of safek chayah — and so the matter there.
