And he must designate it with his mouth. Meaning the earth below he must designate with his mouth for the purpose of covering where he found loose earth; otherwise he accomplishes nothing; and likewise when he places it for loose earth above it counts as designation — so appears from Rashi's explanation, chapter HaShochet (Chullin daf 31), and so in Agudah; and one authority holds: if he found loose earth and slaughtered upon it, that suffices; but earth above — all agree he must place it himself, as in Tosafot at the beginning of chapter 25; and so appears in Rabbenu Yerucham; and it appears they only disagree regarding the preferred mitzvah; but b'dieved it is plain for all he need not place it himself even for earth above, for "the wind covered it" in seif 11 exempts from covering — and it is a complete mishnah.
