(Seif 11) And if it became uncovered again — obligated to cover — without blessing. And for koy in such a case one can say no need to cover — double safek: perhaps behemah; and perhaps rejection regarding mitzvot. And one can argue: the two safekot did not arise together — immediately when he slaughtered he became obligated in covering from safek; afterward when wind covered, second safek arose whether rejection regarding mitzvot; and study is needed.
