So he does not come to a blessing in vain. Meaning: if a disqualification is found in the simanim there is no covering at all; and if the knife is found defective, although he must cover from safek that perhaps the spinal column was damaged, as written afterward — nevertheless in both there is no obligation of blessing; therefore he should not cover before checking simanim and knife. And one can ask: why do we bless the shechitah blessing before shechitah lest treifah be found — for we require the blessing precede the act; and even though for netilat yadayim and immersion we bless after the act — there it is different, for otherwise impossible, as a person is not fit, as in siman 4 seif 1; here we rely on the majority that are kosher. In Derishah he wrote in the name of Rashal: the shechitah blessing is different — it helps purify from neveilah status immediately. And it is astonishing: if so, why did they say regarding one who slaughters where a defect arose — slaughter without blessing, as in siman 19 seif 1? If it purifies from neveilah, etc. Rather one must hold we only bless on what is permitted to eat from it.
