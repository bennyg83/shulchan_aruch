Required, etc. As in Yerushalmi (chapter 9 of Berachot): when does one bless on shechitah? R' Yossi says before shechitah; Yossi ben Nahorai says after he slaughtered — lest the shechitah become neveilah; and although we do not rule thus and are not concerned for this — here, since possible, I am cautious; and Shach.
