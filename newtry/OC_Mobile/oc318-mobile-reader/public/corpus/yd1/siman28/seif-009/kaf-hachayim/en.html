I) [Seif 2] Must bless before covering, etc. As Pesachim 7b: all mitzvot bless before performing. If he did not bless before covering — he does not bless after covering — Shem Chadash and Turei Zahav part 3, unlike Pri Chadash. And Mishnah Berurah agreed part 3 because of safek berachot. Zivchei Rambam in Tamidim part 7; Mani part 2; Zivchei Tzedek part 7; and from those poskim it appears even within kedei dibbur one does not bless after covering; although Kometz part 2 holds one may bless within kedei dibbur of covering; and Mani's view leans in Likutei Halachot part 7; nevertheless we hold safek berachot lehakel even against Maran and even against majority, as Orach Chaim siman 18 part 7 — see there; rather bless in heart or without God's name and kingdom. See above siman 19 part 6.
