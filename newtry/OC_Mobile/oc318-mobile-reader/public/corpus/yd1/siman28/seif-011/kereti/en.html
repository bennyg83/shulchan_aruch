Exempt from covering — for it is covered and standing.
Became uncovered again — see Pri Chadash: that he must cover is from safek whether there is rejection regarding mitzvot — for when the wind covered it, it was rejected for him; and from safek we do not bless — Peri Chadash.
If he himself, etc. — if he covered he is not obligated to watch at all whether it becomes uncovered later, for he fulfilled his mitzvah; therefore they cover in streets and places many pass and tread there — and covering is not fulfilled at all.
