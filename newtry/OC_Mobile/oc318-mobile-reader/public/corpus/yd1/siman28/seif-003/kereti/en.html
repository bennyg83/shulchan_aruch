And if he slaughtered them, etc. Ra'osh wrote: receive blood in a vessel and cover at night; one does not slaughter into a vessel — on this there are many difficulties. Therefore the essence for all difficulties: if it is received for kisuy dam they will consider it certainly chayah and permit its milk; see Peleti. Therefore even if he has prepared earth — nevertheless forbidden on Yom Tov because of permitting its milk.
