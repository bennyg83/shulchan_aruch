L) And if he said only "on covering blood" and did not say "with earth" — he fulfilled. Chayei Adam there; and simple, since several Geonim hold even l'chatchila one says only "on covering blood" without word earth, as above part 10; if so, even where practiced to say "on covering blood with earth," if he did not say "with earth" — plainly he fulfilled.
M) And before blessing say for sake of unification, etc.: "Behold I come to fulfill positive commandment of covering blood, as it is said 'and pour out its blood and cover it with earth,' to repair the root of this mitzvah and all 613 mitzvot included in it in the supernal place. And may it be pleasant," etc.
P) [Seif 3] Koy which is safek chayah and kilayim from behemah and chayah must cover its blood, etc. It implies both ram on she-gazelle and gazelle on she-goat — both must cover and do not bless; and so Bach; and so Derishah part 1: even ram on she-gazelle whose mother is chayah must cover and not bless; and so Rav Mateh Asher siman 18: even ram on she-gazelle whose mother chayah — also do not cover on Yom Tov and do not bless — unlike Rashal, Taz, and Shach; rather like Bach, Derishah, and colleagues — see there; and so Sefer Beit Meir; Zivchei Tzedek part 12.
Live) There. And one does not slaughter them on Yom Tov. Since must cover and on Yom Tov may not cover — do not slaughter l'chatchila — Shach s.k. 8; Pri Chadash part 7; Likutei Halachot part 6; Zivchei Tzedek part 10.
K) There. And at night if recognizable he may cover. And they cannot receive blood in vessel to cover at night, for one does not slaughter into vessels — Tur; reason as above siman 11 seif 3 lest they say receiving blood to throw to idolatry — see there. Bach wrote: nowadays b'dieved if slaughtered on Yom Tov can receive in vessel even l'chatchila; nevertheless better place earth and pebbles in vessel before receiving — see there; Beit Din Shach s.k. 10. Derishah wrote: perhaps here too concern before observer he took earth to cover koy blood by sage's command and they will permit its milk; and so Darchei Shalom; Minhag in Hagahot Tashbetz part 14; Likutei Halachot part 3; and Shem Chadash agreed part 4; Turei Zahav in Orach Chaim siman 498 s.k. 34; Minhag Tziyon, general 23 in Asheret part 10; Zivchei Rambam siman 19 in additions part 4.
KA) Koy lacking a limb — permitted to slaughter on Yom Tov and receive blood in vessel and cover at night, since we do not offer lacking limb to idolatry — She'erit Shmuel end part 3; Zivchei Tzedek part 15. However according to reason we wrote above part 18 — appears even in such case not l'chatchila.
