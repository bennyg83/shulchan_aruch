From behemah and chayah. This is also included in koy; and Rashal wrote: specifically when chayah mounted behemah we say one does not slaughter on Yom Tov, since we are uncertain whether we are concerned for the father's seed and perhaps we are not concerned; but when behemah mounted chayah he must cover certainly, and there is no safek — for gazelle and even partial gazelle we say, as we said above siman 17 seif 8: sheep even partial sheep; and therefore one slaughters it on Yom Tov; and so explicit in the Gemara, chapter Oto Va'et Beno, conclusion of Rav Pappa: when a ram mounted a she-gazelle he must cover certainly, for gazelle and even partial gazelle we say; and you find safek only in gazelle mounting she-goat, where there is safek whether we are concerned for father's seed and it would be partial gazelle; and although Tur, Rambam, and Shulchan Aruch wrote here plainly "from behemah and chayah," they relied on what is written in Oto Va'et Beno that sheep even partial sheep — here too; and therefore explain it deals with gazelle mounting she-goat; and in Perishah he also challenged from here on Oto Va'et Beno and answered: regarding Oto Va'et Beno Scripture revealed sheep even partial sheep, but here Scripture did not reveal — there is room to doubt perhaps only for full chayah is covering required — end; and he did not examine the Gemara we cited that is explicit for one who holds sheep even partial sheep also holds even partial gazelle for covering — he is obligated; and Maharach wrote: Tur holds like "some say" later in the Gemara in the baraita of koy: it is from she-goat and gazelle; and this "some say" does not hold we say even partial gazelle, although regarding sheep we say so. I cannot rest on this either, for certainly Rav Pappa who said in the conclusion that you find regarding covering only gazelle mounting she-goat saw and knew this baraita of "some say," and nevertheless explains it does not deal with ram mounting she-gazelle; for otherwise Rav Pappa would argue on the baraita from reasoning — and so is proven from what they said in the Gemara before: "koy — one does not slaughter on Yom Tov — what are we dealing with?" If you say ram mounting she-gazelle — whether according to Rabbanan or R' Eliezer — to slaughter and cover gazelle and even partial gazelle, etc.; since they challenge plainly thus, it implies it is plain they equate sheep even partial sheep with gazelle even partial gazelle; rather the clear truth is Rashal's view I cited.
