And one does not slaughter them, etc. The Ra'osh wrote to receive the blood in a vessel and wait until night and then cover it; and Tosafot: one does not receive blood in a vessel — see there. It is difficult for me: we say in Beitzah (daf 8b): when he comes and crushes, a positive commandment comes and pushes aside a negative commandment; and similarly when while covering he crushes — the Gemara challenges: behold Yom Tov is a positive commandment and a negative commandment, etc. How can the positive commandment of covering push aside the positive commandment of Yom Tov? It is possible to fulfill both: receive blood in a vessel and cover at night after Yom Tov passes and fulfill both. And if one may not receive blood at night — that is only a rabbinic decree, and from the Torah it is permitted; how can it push aside a Torah negative commandment? On this the Sages did not uproot Torah law; and study is needed l'chatchila. It must be said with difficulty: certainly the essence of kisuy hadam is to cover immediately when blood leaves the slaughtered animal, and if he delayed he nullified the mitzvah; and the Ra'osh who suggested receiving blood in a vessel and covering at night is at least better than nullifying kisuy hadam entirely; but if he receives in a vessel and covers at night he does not fulfill kisuy hadam properly, therefore it could push aside a negative commandment also to fulfill properly. And similarly we say in Menachot (daf 72): burning fats — its time is all night, nevertheless it overrides Shabbat because a mitzvah at its time is dear; and so here with kisuy hadam — dear is the mitzvah at its time to cover immediately, and it overrides Yom Tov; and study is needed.
