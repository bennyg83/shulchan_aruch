[And one does not slaughter them on Yom Tov. Since he must cover them and on Yom Tov he may not cover them, he should not slaughter l'chatchila.]
[He does not cover its blood. Even if he has earth prepared — because the observer will say: certainly it is a chayah, for otherwise they would not trouble to cover its blood on Yom Tov, and he will come to permit its milk — end; so Taz in Orach Chaim siman 498, seif 8; and this is the conclusion of the Talmud, first chapter of Beitzah (end of daf 8), and agreed by all poskim (and so Rashba wrote in Sefer Avodat HaKodesh, Beit HaMikdash, gate 2, law 2); and so Levush there — not like what Atzei Erez wrote here: one does not slaughter koy and kilayim on Yom Tov lest he move earth on Yom Tov not for a need, lest it be a species of behemah — end; and he was not precise, for even if there is prepared earth where there is no prohibition of moving, it is forbidden for the reason mentioned.]
[And at night if its mark is recognizable he may cover it. And they cannot receive blood in a vessel to cover at night, for one does not slaughter into vessels — end; so Tur from Ra'osh and Ramban: forbidden because it appears as receiving blood for idolatry, as above siman 11, seif 3. And that they did not permit through placing a little earth in the vessel, as above siman 11 — because there too there is concern before the observer that he will say he took earth to cover and will come to permit its milk — so Derishah. But Bach wrote: and it appears to me that in our time (meaning there is no way of idolaters in this, as above siman 12, seif 12) that even l'chatchila one could slaughter into vessels like Or Zaru'a; only we are cautious) b'dieved if he slaughtered on Yom Tov he can receive blood in a vessel even l'chatchila, to fulfill kisuy hadam properly, and there is no prohibition by law to be cautious from receiving; nevertheless it is better to place earth and pebbles in the vessel before receiving — end; and in truth his words are difficult: why did the Talmud and poskim not teach us this enactment of earth in a vessel? Perhaps they only permit through a little earth in a case where one cannot say he will cover with it; and by law it would be forbidden since there is only a little earth, because of suspicion of idolatry that it is still fit for sprinkling; but nowadays when there is no way of idolaters in this, it is permitted even without earth at all; only stringently he should place a little earth — and examine well.]
[And kilayim from behemah and chayah, etc. It deals with a gazelle that mounted a she-goat — we are uncertain whether we are concerned for the father's seed or not; therefore we are uncertain whether to cover and do not bless, lest we are not concerned — not like what is written in Sefer Zivchei Tzedek: the reason he does not bless is a decree lest he permit its milk and he was not precise; and study is needed. But if it were plain to us that we are concerned, he would be obligated to bless, for he must cover it from the Torah, for we hold gazelle and even partial gazelle; and regarding the mother's seed none disagree that plainly we are concerned; and see above siman 16 s.k. 17 from this.]
