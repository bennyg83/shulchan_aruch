And Taz and Shach: that he does not bless on kilayim from behemah and chayah deals with gazelle mounting she-goat, since we are uncertain whether we are concerned for father's seed; but behemah mounting chayah he must cover certainly and there is no safek and must bless — for gazelle and even partial gazelle we say; regarding mother's seed none disagree that plainly we are concerned, as we said above siman 16 — Shach.
Its blood. Shach wrote: even if there is prepared earth — because the observer will say certainly it is chayah, for otherwise they would not trouble to cover on Yom Tov and will come to permit its milk.
He may cover it. Shach wrote: nowadays on Yom Tov one may place a little earth in a vessel and receive blood into it until night. That regarding koy we require covering from safek — unlike other safekot; see responsum of Maharam of Lublin siman 65.
