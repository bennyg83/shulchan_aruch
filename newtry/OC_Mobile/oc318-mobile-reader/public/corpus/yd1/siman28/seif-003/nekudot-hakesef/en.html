(s.k. 17) He challenged Rama. And his words are not compelled; also what he challenged Beit Chadash is not difficult at all, for in truth he covers afterward with earth — only the blessing was not enacted l'chatchila on earth; and from Levush there is no proof that Darkei Moshe did not see it; he also did not see Ramban's words that I brought in Shach s.k. 29; and we must reconcile the words of the Geonim, for all their words are words of tradition.
