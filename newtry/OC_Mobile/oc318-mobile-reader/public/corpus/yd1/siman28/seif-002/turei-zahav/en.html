Covering blood with earth. Although we do not find anywhere mentioning the detail of the mitzvah in the blessing except generally — and it would suffice to say on covering blood alone — nevertheless here it is different, for one performs two mitzvot: covering below the blood and above; and this is learned from "with earth"; therefore one must say "with earth" to indicate the two mitzvot, as above; and see what is written siman 120 seif 3.
