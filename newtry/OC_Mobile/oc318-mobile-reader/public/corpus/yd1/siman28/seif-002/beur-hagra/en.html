Obligated to bless before, etc. As written (Pesachim 7b and conclusion): all mitzvot one blesses before performing them; and in Tosefta last chapter of Berachot and Yerushalmi there: the slaughterer needs a blessing for himself on shechitah, and the one who covers needs a blessing for himself on kisuy dam.
One who slaughtered, etc. As written at the end of chapter Kisuy HaDam: in reward that Avraham said "and I," etc.; and in reward Avraham said "if," etc.; and they bless as they bless on all mitzvot — tzitzit, sukkah; and Orach Chaim siman 23; and Shach here.
