Blood with earth — so they raised and so they practiced: although they cover with other things, all are offspring of earth and return to earth; and in Scripture it says "and cover it with earth."
Shehecheyanu — and Shach argued: many mitzvot like tefillin and tzitzit — we only bless when specifically required, or mitzvah with joy, or coming from time to time — unlike covering. It appears: mitzvot where obligation is placed on a person to do, like tzitzit and tefillin — one does not bless shehecheyanu; and although for tzitzit if he lacks four corners he is not obligated, nevertheless at the time of wrath he is punished; but he is not obligated to procure himself to slaughter and cover — most of Israel do not slaughter and cover; therefore if such a mitzvah comes to his hand he should bless with joy — so it appears to me.
