B) There. Must cover its blood. He should hasten to cover immediately, for we hold a mitzvah at its time is dear; however if he did not cover immediately and covered afterward — he fulfilled the positive commandment as required, only he did not treat the mitzvah at its time dearly. And if he did not cover immediately and covered after a short time — still called mitzvah at its time, only not among those who hasten mitzvot. Machazik Berachah, Orach Chaim part 1, Zivchei Tzedek part 1.
C) There. Must cover its blood. Also with warm ashes permitted to cover — Taz Orach Chaim siman 498 s.k. 15. And Da'at Kedoshim at end of this siman wrote: do not cook blood or salt before covering; therefore proper the ashes not be yad soledes bo. And Gidulei HaKodesh wrote there part 6: on this some Torah students wondered — opposite Taz above; and wrote to settle: specifically lower earth one does not cover with warm ashes, for necessarily he waits a bit before covering above and at time of upper covering blood already cooked by lower ashes; but upper earth permitted with warm ashes — see there; and Beit Din Mani in Likutei Halachot part 157. Zivchei Tzedek part 2 wrote there Zivchei Tzedek: therefore warn slaughterers not to absorb shechitah blood in kiln that has fire and ashes that are yad soledes bo.
I) [Seif 2] Must bless before covering, etc. As Pesachim 7b: all mitzvot bless before performing. If he did not bless before covering — he does not bless after covering — Shem Chadash and Turei Zahav part 3, unlike Pri Chadash. And Mishnah Berurah agreed part 3 because of safek berachot. Zivchei Rambam in Tamidim part 7; Mani part 2; Zivchei Tzedek part 7; and from those poskim it appears even within kedei dibbur one does not bless after covering; although Kometz part 2 holds one may bless within kedei dibbur of covering; and Mani's view leans in Likutei Halachot part 7; nevertheless we hold safek berachot lehakel even against Maran and even against majority, as Orach Chaim siman 18 part 7 — see there; rather bless in heart or without God's name and kingdom. See above siman 19 part 6.
K) There. On covering blood with earth. And so formula in Tur and shechitot of Mahariu, end siman 8; Levush; Yeshuot Yaakov, chapter Kisuy HaDam siman 12; Shem Chadash part 3; Chayei Adam, general 8 part 4; Kometz part 2; Ahel Yitzchak siman 24 part 11; Mani part 2; however in Tosefta Berachot chapter 6 part 16 formula "on covering the blood" with hei; and so Rambam chapter 14 Hilchot Shechitah law 1; Lev Avraham siman 108; Rokeach siman 380; Minhag in Hagahot Tashbetz part 8; Ben Ish Chai, parashat Vayelech part 3; Neharah Neharah and Pestia.
N) With earth — beit with segol, not shva. Mishnah Berurah part 3; Mani part 2; Zivchei Tzedek part 9.
O) There in Hagahah. One who slaughtered first time blesses shehecheyanu on covering. However Pri Chadash part 5 wrote: for law, since Tosafot and Rashba agree not to bless and their words appear primary; and safek berachot lehakel — so we rule, unlike Hagahah — see there. And Likutei Halachot part 2; and responsum Ba'ei Chayei, part 4, siman 22: world does not practice to bless, for not a mitzvah with joy — unlike the Rav from Shulchan Aruch — see there. Turei Zahav siman 4 part 7; also Rikanti wrote on Rama's words: not practiced and not mentioned in Talmud — Zivchei Tzedek part 7; and Shomer Emunim part 5: certainly do not enter safek berachah; and Kometz part 3: to exit dispute, take other vessel or new fruit and bless on it and intend also for covering; if not — do not bless — safek berachot lehakel; and Mani part 2; and Zivchei Tzedek part 11: custom in Baghdad not to bless shehecheyanu on covering. However where practiced to bless — do not neglect them, for where there is custom we do not say safek berachot lehakel, as Orach Chaim siman 7 part 2 — see there.
Good) There. And does not bless. Lest it be behemah and blessing in vain, and transgress "do not take," etc. — Levush.
KG) There in Hagahah. And some are uncertain in buffalo, etc. They say it is koy — Darkei Moshe part 1 in shechitot of Mahariu; Beur HaGra part 7; therefore do not slaughter on Yom Tov like koy.
KD) There in Hagahah. Therefore good to cover without blessing. And mitzvah to heed Geonim to cover as proper covering, only not bless from safek lest this not be the buffalo of Geonim's words — Pri To'ar part 6; Zivchei Tzedek part 15.
