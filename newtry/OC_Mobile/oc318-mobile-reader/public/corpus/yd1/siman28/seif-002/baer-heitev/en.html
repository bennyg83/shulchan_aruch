And Shach wrote another reason: because shechitah is in his hand and he is not commanded to slaughter, for he can always be without meat or have another slaughter; but regarding covering the obligation is on every person who sees blood to cover, as below seif 8. However study is needed why we bless on covering more than shehecheyanu on tefillin, mezuzah, kiddushin, or other mitzvot one begins; he left it in study; and Peri Chadash ruled not to bless shehecheyanu — safek berachot lehakel. And Taz wrote: that one must say on covering blood "with earth" and not say plainly on covering blood — because here one performs two coverings below and above, learned from "with earth"; therefore must say "with earth" to indicate two mitzvot; and see siman 120 seif 3.
