(Likut) Even if, etc.: from what Shach wrote "covering that is not certain," etc. — safek issur even in such a case, as in first chapter of Yom Tov (8); and Tosafot there s.v. kisuy, etc.; and for the language "because of permitting its milk," etc. And some, etc.: and so Ra'ah wrote, only he wrote even for koy on Yom Tov the law is so, and we do not rule (so) as in Orach Chaim siman 498 seif 8, and so in the Gemara there they challenge from "one does not slaughter," etc.; "if so," etc.; rather Rava said kiln ash; and so Rav Yehudah; and so Rashi there s.v. ve'ein da'ato, etc.; Rami son of R' Yossi: do not say kiln ash was his intent, etc.; and Rav Yehudah — rather prepared earth for all; only because of permitting milk; and so in Maharshal; and here he was uncertain and expanded there and concluded one never covers — baraita teaches plainly; and so Rava who said it is a disgrace, etc.; and so those Tosafot; and so essence of what Shach wrote "covering that is not certain" — evidently one law for them — end.
