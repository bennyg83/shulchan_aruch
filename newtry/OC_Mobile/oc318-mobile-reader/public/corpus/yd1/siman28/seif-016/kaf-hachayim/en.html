P) [Seif 3] Koy which is safek chayah and kilayim from behemah and chayah must cover its blood, etc. It implies both ram on she-gazelle and gazelle on she-goat — both must cover and do not bless; and so Bach; and so Derishah part 1: even ram on she-gazelle whose mother is chayah must cover and not bless; and so Rav Mateh Asher siman 18: even ram on she-gazelle whose mother chayah — also do not cover on Yom Tov and do not bless — unlike Rashal, Taz, and Shach; rather like Bach, Derishah, and colleagues — see there; and so Sefer Beit Meir; Zivchei Tzedek part 12.
