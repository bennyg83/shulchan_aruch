G) However where people are lax in mitzvah of covering — not covering properly, or not blessing on covering or shechitah — penalize and obligate lashes; also forbid shechitah to transgressor because of erecting a fence — Shach there; Likutei Halachot there; Shem Chadash there; Shomer Emunim part 3; Zivchei Tzedek part 4; Zivchei Tzedek part 5; and so above siman 19 part 1.
H) And even only that slaughterer lax in this mitzvah — penalize and lashes and forbid meat to transgressor because of erecting a fence — Shem Chadash there; Shomer Emunim there; Zivchei Tzedek part 6.
