Blood that spurted and that is on the knife — if there is no other blood there except it, he must cover and drag it, so as to place earth also below; but if there is other blood there he need not cover that, for one need not cover all the blood; therefore one need not wait to cover until all the blood has come out. {Rama: However he should wait until drops begin to fall so he covers some of the lifeblood (Maharil in the name of Maharak, and ancient shechitot in the name of Maharash).}
