(Siman 28, Taz s.k. 12) He wants to reconcile the words of Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Maharam — and it is forced; the matter is clear as I wrote in my Mareh Makom: Hagahot Maimoniyot deals when they were mixed together, for there they wrote thus on what Rambam wrote there: if blood was mixed in wine or in blood of behemah we view them as if they were water — if it is possible that the appearance of blood would be seen, he must cover it in this measure as if it were water — and Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote on this: obligated to cover, as the plain mishnah; and so my teacher ruled on one who slaughtered a kosher hen and again slaughtered another and it became neveilah — he must cover — end of his words; behold as I explained.
(s.k. 15) And it is astonishing, etc. It is not difficult at all; you overlooked the words of Maharshal, chapter Kisuy HaDam, who ruled in truth in a matter where a defect arose — that one blesses before shechitah — and this is like Rashi's view (and it also appears you overlooked Rashi's words too): since when he removes it from neveilah status he can bless; and he brought Maharshal there; and Beit Chadash at the end of siman 19. However in Siftei Kohen siman 19 s.k. 2 I wrote that from Yerushalmi it appears like Or Zaru'a — see there.
(s.k. 17) He challenged Rama. And his words are not compelled; also what he challenged Beit Chadash is not difficult at all, for in truth he covers afterward with earth — only the blessing was not enacted l'chatchila on earth; and from Levush there is no proof that Darkei Moshe did not see it; he also did not see Ramban's words that I brought in Shach s.k. 29; and we must reconcile the words of the Geonim, for all their words are words of tradition.
