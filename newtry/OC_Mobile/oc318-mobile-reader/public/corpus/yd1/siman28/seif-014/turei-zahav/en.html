He slaughtered bird, etc. Tur wrote this law in the name of Ba'al HaItur; Beit Yosef wondered: behold this law is a baraita in chapter Kisuy HaDam; it appears he learned from it — since Ba'al HaItur in Tur mentioned first exemption of bird or chayah and in the obligation part mentioned only chayah, not as Shulchan Aruch translated here. Certainly Ba'al HaItur's intent: when slaughtering bird upon behemah there is no covering because its blood is minimal; and so I saw Rashal wrote — and it did not settle for me: are all birds equal or all behemot equal? Explicitly in Sotah (daf 16): regarding bird blood mixed in water, not all birds are equal; and here everything depends on the measure if there was water, as seif 13; and Tur's intent appears thus. One can challenge the reverse: why did Rif, Ra'osh, and Rashba in Torat HaBayit not bring this law of slaughtered chayah and afterward behemah, etc., or the reverse, since explicit in the baraita? It appears their intent: in the baraita Rabbi Yonatan ben Yosef says: slaughtered chayah and afterward behemah, etc.; since it teaches "Rabbi Yonatan says," the first tanna disagrees and holds one is not obligated even when slaughtering chayah upon behemah, since there is no earth below under chayah blood; or vice versa: even slaughtering behemah upon chayah there is covering — he drags behemah blood and covers chayah blood, as Tosafot challenged on Rabbi Yonatan ben Yosef; therefore Rif and Ra'osh did not bring Rabbi Yonatan's words, since many disagree with him; and Ba'al HaItur holds halakhah is like Rabbi Yonatan; therefore Tur brought this law in Ba'al HaItur's name — clear and correct.
Obligated to cover. Meaning: as it is; and even though there is no earth below under chayah blood, nevertheless that suffices; and Tur explains the reason in the Gemara: since nevertheless it would have been fit initially to place earth below, and whatever is fit for mixing — mixing does not block it. Tosafot challenged: why not drag it and cover with earth below like blood that spurted in seif 15? And they conclude in astonishment; and Levush wrote on this he must cover provided there is loose earth below that will be absorbed in the end; and if no loose earth below he drags it all as it is and places loose earth below and above, etc. — all this is a great error and he did not examine the Gemara at all, only wrote from his own mind erroneous things.
And it became neveilah in his hand. Beit Yosef wrote this law in Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Maharam: obligated to cover; and Beit Yosef challenged what is written regarding slaughtering behemah upon chayah that one is exempt from covering. Rama answered here: it deals when he does not know whether the last blood covered the first; we do not say stam it did not cover it, and therefore he must cover. And one may wonder: how can such a rule be given to obligate a blessing stam — are all chayot or birds equal to one another? In my humble opinion another way (Rambam) [Maharam] wrote this law specifically when it became neveilah in his hand: the reason — whatever left in the second bird's shechitah before it became neveilah is blood fit for covering, and at that time it is called shechitah blood; and although after it became neveilah retroactively it is blood unfit for covering, nevertheless it was already nullified when it had a name fit to cover; with this we do not follow the measure whether in water there would be appearance of blood, but this blood was nullified in the blood that was there first. One can compare this to moist chametz mixed before Pesach in wine, for we hold it does not return and revive, as in Orach Chaim siman 467, since at the time of mixture it had a name of permission; so here too initially it had a name of permission. Therefore Maharam said properly: stam when he sees it became neveilah he throws it from his hand immediately — if so, there is no blood for the lower to nullify; but if it already became neveilah at the beginning of shechitah and he continues slaughtering and extracting blood, certainly one does not bless on this covering as long as we have not clarified there is so much that if mixed in water there would be appearance of forbidden blood, as seif 13 — in my humble opinion clear.
