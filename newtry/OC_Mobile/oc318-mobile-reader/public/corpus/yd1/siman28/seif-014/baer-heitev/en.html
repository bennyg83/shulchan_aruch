To cover. Shach wrote: and even though there is no loose earth below, whatever is possible we do. Taz wrote the reason: since nevertheless it would have been fit initially to place earth below, and whatever is fit for mixing is not blocked. Tosafot challenged why not drag and cover with earth below like blood that spurted in seif 15 — and they conclude in astonishment. Bach wrote: with bird upon behemah exempt from covering because bird blood is minimal; Taz and Shach disagree — everything depends on the measure if there was water, as seif 13.
To cover. Taz disagrees with Rama who wrote stam he must cover — how can such a rule obligate blessing stam? The essence depends: if initially blood left before it became neveilah, although afterward retroactively unfit blood, it was already nullified; compare to moist chametz mixed before Pesach — does not return and revive since at mixture it had permission; but if already neveilah at start of shechitah while continuing — certainly no blessing on this covering until clarified there is enough that in water would show blood, as seif 13 — clear, end.
