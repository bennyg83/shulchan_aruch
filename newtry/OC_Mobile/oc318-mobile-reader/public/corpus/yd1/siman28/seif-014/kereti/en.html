Exempt from covering — when he cannot drag the blood; but if he can drag behemah blood he drags and covers chayah blood — Rashba.
Or bird, etc. — Bach's view: with bird no need to cover; Taz, Shach, and Pri Chadash challenged him; what Beit Yosef wrote to settle the difficulty is forced; see Peleti where I expanded settling Tur; and there is room for Bach's words; therefore cover without blessing.
Obligated — even though no earth below, since he could have placed earth below — whatever is fit for mixing is not blocked; so Gemara beginning Kisuy HaDam — see there; Atzei Erez wrote it deals when there is earth below — latter authorities challenged against Gemara; see Peleti where I settled his words — there is not so much novelty.
And became neveilah, etc. — Rama wrote this distinction to settle why obligated for chayah and behemah but exempt bird upon bird; Rama's answer is forced; therefore Shach: the case was not one upon the other, only mixed at the side — forced; and what is Maharam's teaching? See Peleti where I wrote proper answer to Maharam. Earth below is nevertheless not me'akev; see Peleti what I expanded on this.
