(Seif 7) He must drag the blood. This is Rashba's words who learned from blood on the knife explained in sugya he drags it; and that of seif 14 slaughtered behemah and afterward chayah — obligated to cover — explained sugya covers only above and need not drag — this is truly Tosafot's difficulty, left in difficulty; see Shach s.k. 20. In my humble opinion one can say: in truth the law is equal — meaning Ra'ah's view: plain to Talmud that blood on knife requires dragging, because there is no concept of covering on the knife; one cannot say covering without loose earth suffices because fit for mixing — therefore when slaughtered behemah and afterward chayah no need to drag, only they challenge on kodshim to exempt entirely since no earth below and not fit for mixing — on this they challenge: nevertheless drag like blood on knife — no covering on knife, drag and cover. According to Rashba one can say covering on knife also applies, only Talmud's precision from "obligated to cover" and does not teach "he covers it with earth" — at first glance difficult: this precision can be inferred also from baraita body behemah and afterward chayah that teaches "obligated to cover"; and it appears the precision is only because it does not teach "he covers it with earth" — implying dragging because of earth below; but from baraita slaughtered behemah one can say he relies on why it teaches chayah and therefore behemah exempt from covering and need not drag; if so, proven in behemah and afterward chayah he is obligated without dragging; and if chayah and afterward behemah — reason like "the wind covered it." One can say in truth where no earth there is no covering; and from exempt it follows they do not require dragging in covering at all; therefore he inferred only from blood on knife where one might think he relies on dragging — should have taught plainly "he covers it with earth"; therefore one can say our version is so: initially they challenge: though impossible below, etc. — was it not taught slaughtered behemah? meaning: if obligated to cover, it is dragging; likewise to drag; and even if you say he covers without dragging — likewise do above; and on this they answer it does not deal with dragging; there it suffices with covering above — whatever is fit for mixing; but for kodshim where R' Eliezer: for mixing entirely exempt — on this they challenge from blood on knife — dragging; likewise to drag. In truth since compelled from blood on knife he drags it — likewise slaughtered behemah and afterward chayah he drags it; and that "and afterward behemah" exempt — whatever is covered even in something that is not earth is exempt from covering, for nevertheless it is covered. "The wind covered it" — even in something that is not earth; and examine well.
