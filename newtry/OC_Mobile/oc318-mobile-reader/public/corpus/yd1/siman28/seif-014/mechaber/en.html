If he slaughtered a bird or chayah and then slaughtered a behemah upon it — exempt from covering; but if he slaughtered a behemah and then slaughtered a chayah or bird upon it — obligated to cover. {Rama: If he slaughtered a bird or chayah and afterward slaughtered another and it became neveilah in his hand: if he knows the last one's blood covered the first's, exempt from covering; but stam (without such knowledge) he must cover (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 14, plain rule that he must cover, and he distinguishes if he knows etc., so these do not contradict the case of slaughtering behemah upon it where one is exempt).}
