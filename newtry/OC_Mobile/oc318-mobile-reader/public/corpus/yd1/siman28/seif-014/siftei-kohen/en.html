[Exempt from covering. For behold behemah blood is above — what will he cover? Rashi.]
[Or bird, etc. And Bach wrote: with bird upon behemah exempt from covering, since bird blood is minimal — and it does not seem right; and what Maharshal wrote, chapter 25, siman 14, is not found — on the contrary Maharshal's words there appear the opposite; and what they forced to reconcile Beit Yosef's difficulty on Tur who wrote in the name of Ba'al HaItur — behold it is a complete baraita — his answer is forced — see there. What seems right: all poskim did not bring this baraita at all — it appears they hold we do not rule like this baraita, because Tosafot wondered on it: "slaughtered chayah and afterward behemah — exempt from covering" — say: let him drag it and cover it; and likewise behemah and afterward chayah — let him drag and cover, like blood that spurted and on the knife — end; and poskim hold to settle this wonder: this baraita holds that even for blood that spurted and on the knife one need not drag, only cover in place, as Maharam ben Natan wrote and Beit Yosef brought; if so, we who hold for blood that spurted and on the knife one must drag — we do not rule like this baraita; therefore Tur wrote this law in the name of Ba'al HaItur who holds this baraita is halakhah and must distinguish unlike blood that spurted and on the knife: here is different since behemah blood is above — what will he cover, as Rashi wrote — behold it is as if all mixed together; and likewise when chayah blood is above he is obligated to cover — the same: if there is loose earth below, as Atzei Erez and Levush wrote, page 193. Or: even if there is no loose earth below, perhaps he need not drag, because if he waits until he drags and it dries, chayah blood will mix with behemah blood; and before it dries plainly he cannot drag it and all will mix together; therefore better to cover in place. This is Tur's view; and perhaps poskim agree; and that they omitted this baraita is because it seemed plain; and this baraita was not brought in the Talmud except incidentally — see there.]
