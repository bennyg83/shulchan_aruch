"Is permitted." The Issur ve'Heter wrote that this ruling speaks of when there are sixty [parts] of permitted food against the prohibited part; and what the Mechaber wrote, 'if it is of the prohibited type,' means that even the prohibited item is included within this doubt — namely, that perhaps it was cut.
"We attribute it." Even though everywhere we follow the majority, nevertheless here we say that anything that has changed from its original state is attributed to the majority, and it is like the rule of 'anything that separates is attributed to the majority,' etc. Annotation of Shaarei Dura.
