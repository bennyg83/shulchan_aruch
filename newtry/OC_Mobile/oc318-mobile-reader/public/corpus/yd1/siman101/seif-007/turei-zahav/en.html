And Or Zarua wrote in general rule 25 that this law is only when there are sixty in the prohibition against the heter.
One may not rely to say, etc. Even though in every place we follow the majority, here it appears we should say the opposite: everything that changed from its former state is from the majority, and it is like everything that separated from the majority is presumed separated — and the forbidden item, which is the minority, did not change from its former state. So Maharai in Hagahot ShaDa. But one should not say the reason that every fixed item is like half against half, for here before you are two fixed portions — the cut portion and the portion that remained uncut — if so, it is more fitting to say the forbidden is in the cut portion, for this portion is the majority against the second fixed portion. Therefore one must say as Maharai resolved, that the reasoning is that it is in the remaining portion.
