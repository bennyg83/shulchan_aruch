7) Ibid., in the Rama's gloss. "And if it is doubtful whether it is worthy of honor or not, we rule leniently." Because the law of a piece worthy of honor — meaning the decree that it is not nullified by a majority — is d'rabbanan, and in a doubtful d'rabbanan case we rule leniently. And this leniency applies specifically to a doubt as to whether it is a piece worthy of honor; but if it is certainly a piece worthy of honor and there is a doubt as to whether it is forbidden, it is not nullified, as below in siman 110, seif 9 — see there.
