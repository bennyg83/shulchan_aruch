If one piece was cut — one may not rely to say that the forbidden one was cut and is nullified and all of them shall be permitted; rather only that one which was cut is permitted in any case: if it is of the forbidden, all of them are permitted; and if it is not of the forbidden, behold it is permitted; and even if a majority of them were cut one may not rely to say that the forbidden is from the majority that were cut — rather all the cut ones are permitted and the whole ones are forbidden.
