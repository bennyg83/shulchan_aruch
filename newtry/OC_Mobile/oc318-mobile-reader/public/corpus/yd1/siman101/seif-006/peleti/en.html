Not in its kind. Derishah uPerishah challenged Or Zarua that worthiness of honor applies only in its kind, citing beriah. The Mishnah in Avodah Zarah is the source; Acharonim in siman 110 wrote "in its kind" is not precise. Rambam in Orlah commentary: everything listed is in its kind with any amount; not in its kind requires sixty. He rules nevelah is nullified in slaughtered animal for tumah — important items are not nullified even for tumah; in not its kind, importance does not help. Cut fish may still be one kind called "piece of fish" unless very thin — see Or Zarua and Peri Chadash on siman 98.
