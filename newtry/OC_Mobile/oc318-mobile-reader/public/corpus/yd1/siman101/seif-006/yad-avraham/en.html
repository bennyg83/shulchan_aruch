(Seif 6 in the hagahah) And there is no distinction whether mixed in its kind or not in its kind. It may be not in its kind, e.g. fish kinds with the same appearance — siman 83 seif 4. Or Zarua and Derishah disagree whether cut fish are one kind; the hagahah follows that cut pieces can be two kinds — Gemara in Pesachim supports this. Peri Chadash on siman 98 agrees with Or Zarua for whole fish; cut fish may remain two kinds by name — see Tosefet Yom Tov on kilayim regarding camel wool with sheep wool.
