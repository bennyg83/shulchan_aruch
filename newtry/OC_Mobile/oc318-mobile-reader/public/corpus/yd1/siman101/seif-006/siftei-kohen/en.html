And the Torat Chatat wrote in general rule 40 at the end: and it is implied in Or Zarua that the ruling that it is nullified if crushed means that no significant item remained there; but if a piece worthy of honor remained, even though it is nullified on account of beriah, nevertheless it is still considered significant and is not nullified on account of a piece worthy of honor — and this is sound reasoning, end of his words. And there is no distinction whether they were mixed in their kind, etc. Meaning that a piece worthy of honor, even when not in its kind, is not nullified even in a thousand — unlike Or Zarua who wrote that a piece worthy of honor is nullified only in its kind.
