This that beriah and a piece worthy of honor are not nullified — only while they are whole; but if they were cut or crushed until their form was lost they are nullified and are no longer considered; and even if they were crushed after they were mixed with the permitted food (and even after the mixture became known) (Hagahot Sh"D and Darkei Moshe, unlike Or Zarua) — and this is when they were crushed without intent to nullify the prohibition; but if one intended for that it is forbidden to crush if it is his own, and likewise for one who crushed on his behalf (and there is no distinction whether they were mixed in their kind or not in their kind) (Darkei Moshe and implication of poskim, unlike Or Zarua).
