And there is no distinction whether they were mixed in their kind. Explanation: regarding something that has a permitted aspect in siman 102 we say that not in its kind — even regarding something that has a permitted aspect — is nullified; and Or Zarua wrote in general rule 25 that a fortiori regarding other things that are not nullified, such as beriah and a piece worthy of honor, their doubt is permitted, as the Shulchan Aruch wrote at the beginning of siman 100, that if they were mixed not in their kind their importance does not help and they are nullified. And he disagrees with the Rama, for Rashba and the Shulchan Aruch in siman 100 regarding a pot of vegetables in which a beriah was lost — everything is forbidden — from which we learn that even not in its kind beriah is not nullified. And in truth his own words contradict one another, for in general rule 43 he wrote many laws regarding worms that forbid in mixtures, and the Torat Chatat wrote in general rule 40 that something that has a permitted aspect is different, for the importance is not on account of the prohibition itself but because there will later be permission for it — the Sages did not want it nullified; and everything mixed not in its kind — the permission is not called on the forbidden item but on the name of the item it was mixed into, and it is as if it has no permission — end of his words. And in siman 102 seif 1 I wrote in the name of Ran a more correct reason. Nevertheless I do not know how this hagahah applies here regarding crushing and nullifying the piece through cutting; and it appears there is a scribal error here, and its place is at the head of the siman in what the Shulchan Aruch wrote that it is not nullified — and here it has no place at all.
