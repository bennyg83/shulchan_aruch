But if, etc. On this basis Torat Chatat challenged Or Zarua regarding cheese mixed with treifah milk; Peri Chadash and Minchat Yaakov explain Or Zarua as dealing with much treifah milk that was worthy of honor. I do not understand their reading of Or Zarua on cheese curdled in forbidden stomach — perhaps taste of meat absorbed in stomach reabsorbed in milk becomes a prohibition on account of itself; Or Zarua may hold curdling in basar b'chalav does not nullify. Nevertheless, non-Jewish cheese is a prohibition on account of itself because the Sages decreed it — we do not say taste follows the majority for absorbed blood in meat. Rashba's question on fish brine remains; the Sages forbade the brine path, not only the brine. Taz on curdling in neveilah skin is puzzling — sixty is required in milk as explained in siman 87.
But the peel, etc. Shach wondered why the Rama derived this from Tosafot in Chullin — only chein nafsho in half a piece is found, not worthiness of honor on a peel. Rabeinu Yerucham holds a peel can be a piece worthy of honor; if squeezing is possible it remains forbidden forever — unlike Tosafot who link piece becoming nevelah with squeezing. We hold like Rabbeinu Tam that a peel does not forbid wet mixtures without sixty; therefore worthiness of honor on a peel in dry mixtures has no place. Ramban ruled well.
