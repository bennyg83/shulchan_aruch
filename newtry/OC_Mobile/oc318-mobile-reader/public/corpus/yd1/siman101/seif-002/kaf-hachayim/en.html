2) And if you ask: how is it considered a piece worthy of honor — is it not neveilah or tereifah? — this is not a question at all. For not only is neveilah and tereifah fit to be served before a non-Jewish guest, but even a piece of something prohibited in benefit, such as basar b'chalav, is also considered worthy of honor — since, if we were to say that it is nullified and permitted, it would then be worthy of honor. Therefore it is not nullified for this reason. Aruch HaShulchan ibid.
