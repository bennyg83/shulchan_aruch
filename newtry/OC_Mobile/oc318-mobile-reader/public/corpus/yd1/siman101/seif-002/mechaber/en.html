It does not have the law of a piece worthy of honor unless its prohibition is on account of itself, such as nevelah and basar b'chalav; but if it became forbidden because it absorbed taste from a prohibition and there was not sixty in it to nullify it — even for one who holds the piece itself becomes nevelah — it does not have the law of a piece worthy of honor: {Rama: And even a piece that was not salted is nullified, for its prohibition is not on account of itself but on account of blood absorbed in it (there and Taz siman 110); every piece that was forbidden only on account of a peel is nullified, for a peel is not worthy of honor (view of Tosafot and Hagahot Sh"D).}
