(Seif 2 in the hagahah) Every piece forbidden only on account of a peel is nullified, for a peel is not worthy of honor. Shach cites Lev Chaim: why forbidden on account of itself if it absorbed from another prohibition? And in Minchat Shai he left it requiring study. It can be forbidden on account of itself at the cut site — see Gemara regarding the fetus extending its hand, and detached limb siman 55. Cooking on Shabbat forbidden only on account of a peel is an example. Shach's case of hot meat into cold milk requiring sixty against the peel is difficult for those who hold dry nullification — see siman 105. Piece forbidden on account of peel nullifies in dry majority — Mordechai, Rabeinu Yerucham, Beit Yosef siman 105.
