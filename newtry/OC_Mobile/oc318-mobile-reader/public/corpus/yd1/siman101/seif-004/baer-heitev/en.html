"The gizzard." The Taz wrote that for us, the outer membrane of the calf's gizzard is considered a piece worthy of honor.
"Fat." The Shach wrote that Maharshal disagrees with this [and wrote that the outer membrane of the calf's gizzard is considered (a piece worthy of honor)]. Since these laws vary according to place and time, as will be seen below at the beginning of siman 110, I have been brief in this matter, and everything depends on the judgment of the decisor whether it is considered significant in that place or not; and if there is doubt, we are lenient. The Taz wrote that one may not be lenient regarding the skin of goose fat, even if it was cut into strips (called gribn), for even so it is called a piece worthy of honor. (And in Nekudat HaKesef he disagrees with this, for one does not honor a guest with one piece alone but only with many together, and if so, each individual piece by itself is not worthy of honor — see there.)
