(There in seif 2 in the hagahah) And even a piece that was not salted is nullified, for its prohibition is not on account of itself, etc. Sha'ar Dura in chapter 40 wrote that a nevelah piece mixed with kosher pieces, none of which were salted, is not nullified if it was worthy of honor, even though it lacks preparation — after nullification it still needs salting; nevertheless lacking preparation is not like a chicken in its feathers. An incident came before Rabbeinu Tam who ruled thus; Hagahot ShaDa concludes, but I saw great authorities who say even unsalted nevelah is nullified. All this is because they hold it must be worthy of honor before Israelites specifically; if we say when nullified it would become worthy, they argue about unsalted pieces. The Mechaber in Darkei Moshe seif 1 implies one need not require worthiness before Israelites — even worthiness before a non-Jew suffices; unsalted nevelah not deveined that was mixed remains significant if it was worthy of honor. Peri Megadim and Noda BiYehudah discuss whether deveining is a great preparation like feathers; the Mechaber may disagree with Ran who requires worthiness before everyone. See Noda BiYehudah Yoreh Deah siman 16 and the author's long hagahah.
