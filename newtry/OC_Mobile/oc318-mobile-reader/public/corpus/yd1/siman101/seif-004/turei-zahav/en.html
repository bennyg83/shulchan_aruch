Fat of the stomach lining, etc. Maharshal wrote there in that siman that the outer membrane of a calf's stomach is considered, although other intestinal organs are not considered; and the Shulchan Aruch already wrote at the beginning of siman 110 that everything depends on place and time, and among us the outer membrane of a calf's stomach is important.
But the skin of goose fat, etc. In Or Zarua there in the gloss he brings that if the goose skin was cut into strips its significance is lost. Maharshal was puzzled there: since if cut it is not important, from what aspect would it be important when whole, etc.; and also in my humble opinion the opposite is astonishing, for the main honor is in the goose skin after they cut it into pieces for frying — our eyes see that in every place they honor important people with those fried pieces. End of my words: certainly it is a piece worthy of honor even when cut. And it appears therefore that the Rama did not bring in the Shulchan Aruch, and in Darkei Moshe, this distinction, although the Torat Chatat brought it — it is not the main point, and one may not be lenient in this — so it appears to me.
