"Similar." The Taz wrote that it implies that they must also be equal in appearance. And even though regarding the fat of the gizzard the appearance of the fat alone suffices, here it is different — since it is possible to verify that they are also aligned in the cut, this is also required. The Issur ve'Heter wrote that nowadays, when we are not so expert in this, we cannot permit in such a case unless there is a large and obvious difference. The Bach wrote that he received this teaching from a certain sage: if fluid was found in the head of one sheep that would render it treifah, and it was not known from which sheep the head came, one should examine the spinal cord of the sheep — and whichever sheep has fluid in its head will have fluid in the cord extending from the brain to the spine, and we attribute the head to that sheep in order to permit the others. End of his words. The Taz concluded that in this matter it is not worthwhile to permit a doubtful treifah status on this basis, since it is not found in the Gemara or the decisors.
