Similar and well aligned. It implies that in appearance they must also be equal; and even though regarding the fat of the gizzard, the appearance of the fat alone suffices, here it is different, since it is possible to clarify also through their being well aligned in cutting — this too must be clarified, in my humble opinion. And Or Zarua wrote in general rule 52: now that we are not so expert, we have no permission to permit in such a case unless there is a great and recognizable difference — end of his words. And Maharshal, may his memory be blessed, wrote, and this is his language: and I received from one scholar who received from the mouths of scholars: if water is found in the head of one sheep that was tereifah in it and it is unknown from which sheep — one should examine the spine-cord of the sheep, and every sheep in which water is found in the brain in the head will have water in the cord extending from the brain to the spine, and we presume that the head is from that sheep to permit the others — end of his words. And in my humble opinion there is nothing worthwhile in this matter to permit a tereifah doubt, since it is not found in the Talmud and poskim.
