Gizzard, etc. It is implied from the language of the Mechaber that even the gizzard of a goose is included; and so is implied from the words of other poskim. But Maharshal wrote in his rulings and in his book Piskei HaGahot siman 50 that a gizzard is not nullified; and so is found in the name of Maharam in the hagahah below beginning "Maharam forbade a needle found in a gizzard," etc., that he ruled thus — end of his words (and after him the Bach followed). And incidentally he did not examine well, for one who examines there in siman 54 in that hagahah will see that Maharam's ruling applies specifically to a piece that absorbed a prohibition and there is not sixty in it to nullify it, etc. — see there at length.
