(There in the hagahah) every piece that was forbidden only on account of a peel, etc. See Shach s.k. 6 that he means according to Mordechai, who apply a piece worthy of honor regarding a peel — see there; but one who examines Mordechai's words will see that he brought this law in the name of Riva and Rashba, and according to them they hold regarding hot meat that fell into cold milk that one needs sixty against the peel; unlike us, who hold like Rabbeinu Tam that one does not need sixty against the peel and it does not forbid the mixture at all — it is obvious that we do not apply a piece worthy of honor to it. Therefore the Rama wrote well.
