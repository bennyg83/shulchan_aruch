"Other." The Taz wrote in the name of Maharshal that the gizzard of a goose is considered a piece worthy of honor, and it implies that the gizzard of a chicken is not a piece worthy of honor. Maharshal further wrote that the sages of Krakow ruled that the goose breast meat called bilak — even though the skin was removed from it — is still worthy of honor, and he disagrees with this. The Taz's conclusion is that it is called a piece worthy of honor on the grounds that any piece currently worthy of honor, even though it was formerly even more significant, does not have its significance nullified. The Levush wrote that those goose livers that turned white due to the fat are certainly significant and are not nullified. The main point in this matter is that it depends on the place and time.
