Forbidden in benefit, etc. It is explained in the Gemara in Avodah Zarah (daf 74a) that prohibitions of benefit — such as basar b'chalav and the like — if they are a piece worthy of honor, forbid their mixture also from benefit even in a thousand; and so is it among the poskim. And see Ran there. And see siman 109 s.k. 5. However, it is permitted to sell them to a non-Jew except for the value of the prohibition within them, as I wrote below siman 110 s.k. 2:
