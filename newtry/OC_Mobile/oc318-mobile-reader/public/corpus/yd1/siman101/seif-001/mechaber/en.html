The law of a piece worthy of honor. And it has 9 seifim: A piece worthy of honor has the law of beriah — even in a thousand it is not nullified — and even if it is forbidden in benefit, since if it were nullified it would be permitted and worthy of honor:
{Rama: And even if it is forbidden only d'rabbanan it is not nullified (Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban); and if it is a doubt whether it is worthy of honor or not we follow the lenient view (Arukh general rule 25) even if it is forbidden d'oraisa.}
