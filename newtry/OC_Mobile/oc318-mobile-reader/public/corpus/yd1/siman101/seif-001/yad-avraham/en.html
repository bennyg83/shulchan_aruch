(Siman 101 seif 1 in the hagahah) And if it is a doubt whether it is a piece worthy of honor, we follow the lenient view. The Taz's words here require study — he wrote regarding non-Jewish cheeses that they are not nullified because they are worthy of honor unless known they were not curdled in neveilah stomach, implying even a doubt of basar b'chalav forbids them. I did not understand his conclusion, for it is a doubt whether forbidden on account of itself — most poskim hold non-Jewish cheese is not forbidden because of stomach curdling. Taz himself in siman 81 is lenient on doubtful treifah milk cheese. Peri Megadim explains Taz as dealing when there is not sixty against the stomach — still puzzling. See Beit Yosef siman 115 and Peri Chadash.
