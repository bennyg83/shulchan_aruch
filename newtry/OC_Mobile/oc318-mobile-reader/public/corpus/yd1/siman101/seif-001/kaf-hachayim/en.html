1) [Seif 1] "A piece worthy of honor, etc." — meaning it is fit to be served with honor before guests. Chullin 100a. Tur. Levush. Kneset HaGedolah in Hagahot HaTur, note 1. And we require that it be fit to be served with honor before guests of the sort that distinguished people are accustomed to serve it. Maharshal, Perek Gid HaNasheh, siman 23. Kneset HaGedolah ibid. Beit Lechem Yehudah note 1. Mishbetzot Zahav note 1, who wrote that this means honored and important guests. Beit Yitzchak in Amudei Zahav, note 3. Aruch HaShulchan note 1.
