"From benefit." The Shach wrote that it is explained in the Gemara that prohibitions involving benefit — such as basar b'chalav and the like — if they are a piece worthy of honor, prohibit their mixture also from benefit, even in [a ratio of] one thousand. However, it is permitted to sell them to a non-Jew, except for the value of the prohibition contained within them.
"Leniently." For this stringency regarding a piece worthy of honor is only d'rabbanan. And see what was written at the beginning of siman 100.
