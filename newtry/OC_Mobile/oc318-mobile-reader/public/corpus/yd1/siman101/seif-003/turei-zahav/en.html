And there are those who disagree in all of this. Explanation: and it is reasonable to them that what they said "worthy of honor" does not mean that it must be worthy as it is now, but because of its measure they mentioned it — that it is large like this, worthy to give before a guest after it is cooked.
Except for a chicken in its feathers. Because it lacks a great preparation — it implies that if they already removed the feathers but have not yet taken out the innards and opened the belly, this is not considered lacking preparation and it is not nullified. And a sheep whose skin was not removed — it is obvious that it is nullified, a fortiori from chicken feathers; and so Maharshal.
But if they were already singed. Even though their hooves were not removed, one can trim them and it is not considered lacking great preparation.
Head of poultry. Maharshal wrote in the chapter of Gid HaNasheh siman 53 that the head of an animal is not considered, only the head of a calf — and certainly the tongue of an ox is considered, for Abraham honored the angels with three tongues.
