Except chicken in its feathers, etc. Shach challenged the Rama implying a counted item applies; Minchat Yaakov explained the Rama did not mean that. A sheep in its skin is obviously like chicken in feathers — see Peri Chadash and Semak. The Gemara's challenge in "all sacrifices became mixed" — R' Papa answers like the litra of pressed figs — does not resolve for sheep in skin or birds in feathers. Rabbeinu Tam permitted mixed chickens because they are not a counted item when sold in feathers. In our time fowl are not sold in baskets — feathers and skin do not count — see Semak.
