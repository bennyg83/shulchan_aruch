For after it became mixed, since at the very moment it was mixed it was not yet a piece worthy of honor, it has already been nullified and is no longer significant enough to prohibit everything.
And similarly a whole lamb — even if they have already been cooked, since before cooking it would not in any case be considered a piece worthy of honor. And if so, one must say that even if one has removed from it the sinews, the forbidden fats, and the membranes, it is still called a whole lamb with respect to nullification.
A head, etc. But a head of a large animal implies that it is significant. The opinion of the Maharshal is that it is not significant. However, if the tongue is within it, the tongue is not nullified due to its significance, and the head does not diminish the tongue's significance.
