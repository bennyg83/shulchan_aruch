(Seif 3) And a whole sheep, etc. is not considered worthy of honor. See Chavat Daat who proved from Pesachim that a whole sheep is not nullified and is a piece worthy of honor — five Paschal hides mixed, a blemish found, all go to burning and require a second Pesach even before sprinkling, because at sprinkling time they were not fit. If a whole sheep is not nullified, this is difficult. Answer: they are forbidden because of invalid emurim mixed, not because of meat mixture — what is offered on the altar is not nullified; therefore they require second Pesach d'oraisa. Chavat Daat's rejection is answered. See Yad Malachi and Noda BiYehudah siman 16 on effort like chicken in feathers.
