There is no fowl in which there is not sixty against its heart, and it is permitted even if stuck to the fowl. {Rama: And every fowl has sixty even without head and lower legs (Piskei Maharai siman 91, R' Yitzchak Mintz, and Or Zarua) — as is customary to remove them, meaning until the lower knee; therefore if the fowl is whole, all is permitted. And if it is not whole and there is not sixty in it against the heart stuck to the fowl — some say the piece becomes nevelah and we require sixty in the pot against all the fowl (Tur in the name of Yesh Omrim, and so in poskim), and so they practice. And even when there is sixty in the pot, the fowl is forbidden since there is not sixty against the heart stuck to it. And if the heart is not stuck to the fowl, the entire pot joins to nullify the heart. And see below siman 92 regarding law of piece becomes nevelah. And you have no animal that is sixty against its heart.}
