No fowl, etc. — if no sixty need combination to nullify heart; even though Mechaber no absorbed prohibition, still need combination to nullify at sixty; but in fowl certainly sixty — no further combination, heart nullified in minority.
Against, etc. — even if not salted, has sixty; goose when skin removed not presumed sixty.
And there is not sixty — Maharshal: if heart not stuck in offspring only by cooking shrank and stuck — Maharshal absorbed prohibition; Acharonim disagree — such is main.
Some say ein hana'asur — because stuck hurries to swallow from you — Acharonim; but Rishonim decree sometimes that piece alone in gravy and absorbs — therefore require sixty in that piece alone.
