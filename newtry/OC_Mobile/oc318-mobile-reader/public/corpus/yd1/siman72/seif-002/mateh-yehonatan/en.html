(There in hagahah) Even when there is sixty heart itself forbidden — see Shach s.k. 7 reason dries inside, etc. It appears reason: Rav follows his view siman 69 seif 7 that even b'dieved need salting two sides — here heart salted one side forbidden even b'dieved — clear.
(Siman 72 Shach s.k. 3) And it appears distinction if after heart salted it fell into gravy — permitted, etc. — see there. These Shach words are closed: his foundation is shita explaining whole "all flesh" — heart tears all in roasting but cooking forbidden; difference roasting vs cooking because heart is shia on inner side in cavity — shia and does not absorb; unlike outer side that absorbs. If so in roasting permitted since inner does not absorb and what exited — fire draws from source unlike cooking — even if inner does not absorb, since blood in pot, outer absorbs; therefore Shach "if fell to gravy permitted" means fell to inner side in gravy and cut place not in gravy; but if fell outer side in gravy or cut place in gravy — forbidden.
