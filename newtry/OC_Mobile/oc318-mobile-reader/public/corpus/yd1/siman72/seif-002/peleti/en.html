"As it absorbs so it releases" — see Acharonim who brought: even though regarding blood in the eye "as it absorbs so it releases" does not apply, nevertheless the view of heart shia supports that its law is like emission blood or blood dries inside heart. Acharonim wrote distinction regarding other meat salted with it; distinction only if blood in the eye fell on heart from outside while roasting on fire — forbidden, and "as it absorbs so it releases" does not apply, only shia helps — all heart even from outside is shia as Tosafot wrote; and truly main rule is this view. And with this Rashba's and Yesh Mefashshim's question in Chullin is resolved — they wanted to innovate heart shia from Scripture to Gemara and reject established "as it absorbs so it releases"; Gemara held heart shia does not help to prevent absorption entirely — requires further study.
Until there is sixty against heart — Acharonim included that even if heart was salted to emit, nevertheless require sixty against entire heart because salted only one side and also against its cavity for we do not know how much it holds — see there. And I wonder on fowl: why estimate against cavity more than kezayit which is rabbinic — only third of egg; Gemara is set that only regarding fowl heart whose blood is not more than kezayit one does not violate. I would doubt: not all birds equal — large birds have more than kezayit blood; Gemara distinguishes animal from fowl because animal violates when there is kezayit blood in heart, and does not distinguish fowl from fowl because fixed fact: some fowl lacks kezayit unlike animal — forced; also what does Mishnah teach that one does not violate — reason no kezayit is obvious, we heard less than kezayit has no karet and lashes. I thought Mishnah teaches stam fowl has no kezayit in heart and stam eater does not violate — but per view there is difference among fowl, cannot rely on stam and must investigate kezayit — Mishnah unspecified which species. Possible per Rabbenu Tam: taste is primary until heter becomes full prohibition and eater of kezayit is lashed — even without kezayit blood, heart flesh receives taste and joins kezayit — Mishnah teaches not shia, etc.; but per most poskim unlike Rabbenu Tam, only R' Chaim Kohen — no lashes — difficult. Must say general rule: Chazal established no fowl heart has more than kezayit, any species big or small — therefore no need to estimate on fowl more than sixty times kezayit (third egg) — requires further study.
