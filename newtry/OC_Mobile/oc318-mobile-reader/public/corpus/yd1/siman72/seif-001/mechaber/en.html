The law of salting the heart and lung. And it has 4 seifim: The heart — blood gathers inside it at the time of shechitah; therefore one must tear it before salting and extract its blood and salt it afterward; and then it is permitted even to cook it (Or Zarua general rule 15). {Rama: And some are stringent to cook it only by roasting.} And afterward one cooks it (Shaarei Dura).
