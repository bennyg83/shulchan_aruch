(Siman 72 seif 2 in hagahah) And some stringent in salting forbid other meat salted with it. In my humble opinion should not be stringent — Gemara Pesachim 74: "heart is different — shia" — does not absorb at all even outer side; inner not shia but absorbed well. Nevertheless "as it absorbs so it releases" — must be shia inside does not absorb blood. Therefore other meat with it — like upper windpipe (Mordechai all flesh). In salting and roasting we say shia even for us (Shach, Shafan Damim). Tosafot, Mordechai, Rashi responsum: roasted/salted/cooked heart — discard heart, rest permitted — shia did not absorb or emit. Beit Yosef. Even though we do not hold shia in cooking, in salting/roasting we hold shia — permit other meat — requires further study. Ran: only blood shia not fatty prohibition; if cooked with meat without sixty — meat forbidden, heart forbidden from meat fatty prohibition becoming nevelah — Beit Yosef; wonder ein hana'asur; Ran's reason in all flesh; we hold Ran fatty prohibition no shia; distinction if heart salted/roasted with nevelah.
