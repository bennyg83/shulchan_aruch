(1) [Seif 1] Heart gathers blood at shechitah, etc. Keritut 22a variant readings — Kenesset HaGedolah Hagahat Tur section 1: Rashi/Rashba Torat Chatas 76b — heart has two bloods: absorbed in flesh like other limbs; and gathered at shechitah — karet. Rif/Rosh reverse: external blood is limb blood; blood always enclosed in cavity is nefesh blood — karet. Therefore three bloods in heart. Kenesset HaGedolah: heart blood clear, external congealed — Shulchan Aruch section 1.
