And custom to be lenient. Ba'er Heitev; responsum Penei Yehoshua 100: fowl salted with heart, rinsed, resalted in non-perforated vessel with heart inside, pressed in gravy — forbid what is in gravy — first salting emitted; cannot combine outside gravy to nullify (siman 105 lenient outside gravy); if spreads upward — sixty — Rosh; cannot join nullify.
