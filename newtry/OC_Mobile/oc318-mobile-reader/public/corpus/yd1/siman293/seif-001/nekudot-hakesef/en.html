(Siman 293, Shach s.k. 2) As is evident explicitly in chapter "the place where they practiced," etc. And in my humble opinion nothing is evident — for in chapter "the place where they practiced" we hear Rabbi Yehuda who said so regarding the graft; but R. Yosi and R. Shimon dispute him in chapter 2 of Shevi'it, and Gemara brings it in first chapter of Rosh Hashanah 10, and in Yevamot chapter HaAreil (Yevamot 83a); and halachah was decided in Gemara chapter HaAreil like R. Yosi and R. Shimon who say two weeks; and so all poskim, and Rambam chapter 3 of Laws of Shemittah and Yovel; and if so the words of Tur HaShalem require study.
