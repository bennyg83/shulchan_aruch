That was sown after the omer. The same applies if it was sown before the omer but did not take root until after the omer.
And it had not yet begun to take root. The Gemara raises the question whether one may gather grain lying in the ground at the time of the omer and eat it. Perhaps it is regarded as though it were lying in a vessel, unlike grain that failed to take root before the omer in seif 3, which is forbidden because of its later growth, a concern that does not apply here. Or perhaps, since it lies in the ground, it is regarded as growing and is forbidden because of the doubt. This applies specifically where it was sown after the first omer. If it was sown before that omer, then once it became old grain, it is plainly permitted to gather it. Although the omer does not permit it while it lies in the ground, it was already permitted beforehand.
