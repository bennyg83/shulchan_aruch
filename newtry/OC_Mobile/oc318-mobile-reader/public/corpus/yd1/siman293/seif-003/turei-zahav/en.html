Continue after Pesach. In this column, it is written that the days of the working-star torture was during Pesach P.D. and there were no stars at the time to circulate in their days of torture and yet gave the point that the Grad was a pyre of winter heat and ice was a hard and closed land that had been shown to the Rashi that had not been planted until the Pesach:
But one may not rule for others, etc. — it is not worthwhile to justify on account that we see sages of Israel, most of them, are not careful about chadash in any year, and it is not applicable to say of them "better they be inadvertent," etc.; and Mahari Chagiz already toiled on this matter to find permission from Yerushalmi, from which R. Yitzchak author of Tosafot proved there is prohibition even in gentiles' [grain]; and afterward all the great ones followed and said he has another explanation of Yerushalmi not like R. Yitzchak's words. We have no business with this; even if we had a correct explanation of Yerushalmi, we may not raise our head against all the poskim. Nevertheless it appears according to my humble view to teach adequate merit on the permission in this, for this prohibition of chadash outside the Land is a dispute of tannaim in the first chapter of Kiddushin (38a): the first tanna of the mishnah holds chadash does not apply outside the Land, as written therein "in all your dwellings," meaning only after inheritance and settlement; and R. Eliezer holds chadash applies everywhere — "your dwellings" wherever you dwell implies; and R. Yishmael and R. Akiva also disagree, R. Yishmael like the first tanna and R. Akiva like R. Eliezer. And in Rosh's responsum he wrote R. Yishmael and R. Akiva hold like R. Eliezer — and this is an error, for we explicitly say in the Gemara: Abaye said, who is the tanna who disagrees with R. Eliezer? R. Yishmael; and one must say R. Akiva holds like R. Eliezer. And it is astounding on Beit Yosef who copied this responsum and did not notice the error that contradicts the entire world — requires study.
