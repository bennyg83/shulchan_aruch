The omer came. BeHaT wrote: and took root before the omer — meaning three days; and this is from Tur HaShalem that Shach brought; and see in Dagul MeRevavah who wondered on this, for we hold like R. Yosi — meaning two weeks, as below siman 294 seif 4; and again see that Shach himself in Nekudot HaKesef challenged Tur HaShalem on this — see there. And see in Chatam Sofer siman 100 and siman 284 and siman 286, who wrote to settle Tur HaShalem's words with good reasoning. And see there wondrous Hagahah in She'elot part of BeHar; also in Mishkenot Yaakov siman 64 he wrote to settle Tur HaShalem's words here — that his words are correct and clear — see there.
