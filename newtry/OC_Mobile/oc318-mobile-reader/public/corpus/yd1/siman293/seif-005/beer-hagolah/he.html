Eliezer; and so Rif and Rosh there and at end of Pesachim; and Rambam in chapter 10 of Laws of Forbidden Foods; and like R. Akiva in baraita there — for the Tanna of mishnah at end of tractate Orlah is like him; and like the rabbis of Rav Ashi who do not eat new grain on the night of the sixteenth when the seventeenth dawns — they hold new grain outside the Land is Torah law; and Ravina who said "my mother told me" also holds so — there Menachot 68b, and I cited it above from this; and Rosh wrote in responsum general 2 that so they ruled in all the compositions.
