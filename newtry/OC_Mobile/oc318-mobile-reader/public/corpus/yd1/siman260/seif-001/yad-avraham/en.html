(Siman 260) And this mitzvah is greater than other positive commandments. Beit Yosef wrote it appears this is apart from the mitzvah of tzitzit, for Tur in Orach Chayim siman 24 wrote tzitzit is equivalent to all mitzvot — i.e., all 613; if so what is special here about milah that it is greater than other positive commandments — it must be apart from tzitzit which is greater; and he does not hold like those who say in Nedarim (32a) that milah is equivalent to all mitzvot — and this is incorrect, for how can tzitzit be greater than milah which has karet and overrides Shabbat, unlike tzitzit; and moreover in Sifrei parashat Shelach it says: you shall see them and remember — it teaches that whoever fulfills tzitzit is elevated as if he fulfilled all mitzvot; and it concludes a fortiori for all Torah mitzvot — if so on the contrary it implies tzitzit is lighter than other positive commandments; and so in Menachot (44a) — see Tzafnat Paneach; nevertheless fulfillment is considered as fulfilling all mitzvot, not that it is greater than them; and for this Tur in Orach Chayim intended; and we find this language in Bava Batra regarding charity that is equivalent, etc., and regarding Shabbat in Yerushalmi; and so regarding Torah study.
