One who vows conditionally, or one who ties his vow to others' consent; it contains one seif. A vow and an oath are not released except through an expert or three ordinary men — for even if he specified and stipulated at the time of the vow or oath when he wishes that they annul it from its root, or he tied it (to another) that they annul it when he wishes, it is nothing; for immediately when the vow takes effect he has no release except through an expert or three ordinary men; and even if he tied it to another's mind and they upheld it, and he is asked again about the upholding — it is not effective. {Rama: But if they did not uphold it, and immediately when they heard he said it should not be a vow — the vow is void (so is implied from the words of Rosh, general principle 10, siman 3, and from the words of Tur). And all the more if he stipulated explicitly that his fellow may annul it — his fellow may annul it any time before the vow takes effect (Rivash, siman 486 and 482). And see above, siman 228, se'ifim 37 and 38.} However, if he vowed for a set time, he does not need release; when the time arrives it is void automatically:
