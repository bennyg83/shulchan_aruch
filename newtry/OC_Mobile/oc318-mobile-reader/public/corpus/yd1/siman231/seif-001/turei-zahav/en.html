One said to his wife: "Give me a handshake that you will not remarry after my death; and I too will vow for you that I will not marry another after your death." His vow does not take effect even if he has already fulfilled the commandment of procreation at the time of the vow — and from now his wife's vow also does not take effect, for she only vowed conditionally, that if he will fulfill his vow and handshake she will likewise; and a handshake can be released, and in addition for clarity's sake they shall release it for her.
