Seven clean days. See Shach who explains laws of niddah and zivah; and now all are stringent per R. Zeira who was stringent even for one who sees a drop like mustard to require seven clean days. Know that from days of R. onward there was stringency for people of the fields — or per variant in Rosh for people of Syria — meaning all the more in other lands: if she saw one she sits six and it; and likewise two and three she sits seven clean days — in this all doubt was removed; afterward R. Zeira was concerned lest sometimes she counts seven clean days and it is interrupted and she thinks it is like beginning of sight and sits six and it — but truly she needs seven clean days completely; and in this one may say Rosh's words regarding "they sought to marry and reconciled" requires seven clean days — cannot say due to R. Zeira's stringency for in Rava's days R.
