In possession. We do not presume that because the kohen is his shepherd, the owner surely granted him rights in the courtyard so that the kohen would acquire the firstborn immediately upon its birth. Although the owner may wish to perform this mitzvah even at his own expense by raising the kohen's firstborn animals in his courtyard, as in the case above of two people who deposited animals, we do not presume such willingness where it causes a loss. The owner therefore remains in possession, and the burden of proof rests upon the claimant. Shach. Reference 5.
