(Siman 213 se'if 1) However, from the rabbis — only when one forbids upon oneself, but not when one forbids a matter that has no substance upon another. And likewise speech and walking are no longer in the world, and one cannot forbid a matter that is not in the world upon another; Rashba in Chiddushei Rashba there, and so he derived from the language of the Rambam (chapter 23 of Hilchot Shevuot) "one who vows from matters," etc. "since he forbade himself through them" — end quote.
