(Siman 379, Shulchan Aruch seif 5) For zimun, whether with three or with ten. See Be'er HaGolah, who writes that the reason he had not seen the mourner's blessing added to the fourth blessing of Birkat HaMazon is that people relied upon the view of the Tosafot in Ketubot 8b that the mourner's blessing is recited only in the presence of ten. This also appears from siman 361. References: 80.
