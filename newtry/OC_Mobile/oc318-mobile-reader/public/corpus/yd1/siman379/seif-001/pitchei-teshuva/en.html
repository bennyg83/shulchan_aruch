Grace after Meals. She'elat Yaavetz, part 1, responsum 74, writes that the custom of giving the cup for Grace after Meals to the mourner is not mentioned by any authority. It arose because the Sages say that the mourner reclines at the head, making him the leading participant at that meal, and the leading person recites the blessing even if he arrived last. This applies only during the seven days, not throughout the remaining twelve months. See also siman 375, seif katan 3.
