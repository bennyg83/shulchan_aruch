Birkat HaMazon. See She'elat Yaavetz I, siman 74, who writes that the custom of giving the cup of Birkat HaMazon to a mourner is not mentioned by any posek. The practice arose from the statement of the Sages that a mourner reclines at the head. Since he is given the position of honor among those present, he has the status of the greatest person at that meal, and the rule is that the greatest person leads the blessing even if he arrived last. This, however, applies only during the seven days of mourning. During the rest of the twelve months he has no priority for this purpose. See there further and what I wrote above, siman 375, seif katan 3.
