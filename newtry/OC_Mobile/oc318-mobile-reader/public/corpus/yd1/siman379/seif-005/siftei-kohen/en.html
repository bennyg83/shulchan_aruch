But he joins, etc. From the words of the Mordechai it appears that at the first meal, the mourner's meal, which the mourner is forbidden to eat from his own food, the mourner does not join for birkat hamazon. So too the Rokeach wrote in siman 313, that when they recite birkat hamazon at the time of the mourner's meal, the mourners are not counted, as we say in the first chapter of Ketubot. That is, we say there, 8b, that for the blessing in the square the mourners are not counted. Rashi explains there that this refers to when they feed the mourner the first meal from others' food, as stated in Moed Katan: they would feed him in the square, etc. It seemed to them that the same applies nowadays, even though there is no square, for the matter depends on the mourner's meal. Although Tosafot wrote in Megillah 23b that a mourner joins for birkat hamazon, perhaps they agree regarding the first mourner's meal and speak only of the rest of mourning.
