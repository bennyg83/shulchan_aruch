E) There — or to the appearance of flesh, etc. There are appearances that if it changed to that appearance — treifah; for it is simple to Chazal that whatever is seen on its skin with this appearance — the skin has already spoiled and will eventually puncture; and for this reason even only something changed to this appearance — it is like something punctured somewhat and treifah. And there are appearances (below seif 4) that even if the entire lung changed to that appearance or in several places — kosher; for it is simple to Chazal that it returns healthy; and even if it has many different appearances one from another and all are from kosher appearances — it is kosher.
