Or green like kartei. And Maharash wrote there: so is found in hagahah in the name of Maharil — general rule: green called in foreign tongue green or blaw — kosher; and green called geyl — treifah — end; and so appears to be primary — end of his words; and so wrote the acharonim; and it is simple.
Kosher. And he wrote in examinations of the R"tz: kosher appearances are not kosher until they whiten somewhat through inflation; and it is not clear; and so R' Meir challenged in Beit Hashem; and so Maharshal Piskei Avot siman 39.
And some say if there is a wound, etc. And Maharshal Piskei Avot siman 21 permitted even if there is a wound in the wall.
Treifah. And the R"tz wrote in the name of R' Tavil and Maharak: here even if it changes to lung appearance through inflation — treifah, since it came on account of a blow that was struck in the wall; and so Bach; and R' Meir in Beit Hashem challenged this; and so appears primary; and also from the words of Rosh and Rabbenu Yerucham it appears thus at first glance; and also since in any case many poskim permit even if it does not rise in inflation; and so is the view of Maharshal; and as written nearby — one should not be stringent.
