Baer Heitev; and see in responsum Givat Shaul siman 86: an incident came before him — a lung was found with a wrinkle and deficiency upon it, and it rose in inflation in a kosher manner, as explained above siman 36 in the hagahah — only there was a wound in the wall opposite it, namely the rib broke and rejoined — and he wrote one should permit even not in a place of great loss; and it is not similar to redness, for redness is worse than deficiency and wrinkle. And afterward he saw in responsum Bach siman 130 who wrote on this to declare treifah — and he, may his memory be blessed, challenged him and raised as he ruled at first to permit; and those who give rulings agreed with him — see there.
