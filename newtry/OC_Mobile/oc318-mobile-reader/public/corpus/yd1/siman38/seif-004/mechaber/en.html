If it is not black as ink but like the appearance of blue (which is called blaw in Ashkenaz, similar to the sky) (Mahariu), or green like kartei, or like the appearance of the liver (or like the appearance of the spleen) (Beit Yosef and in his words Maharak), or that it is red even at ultimate redness — kosher. {Rama: And some say if there is a wound in the wall opposite the red lung — treifah, and one must examine afterward (Tur, R' Yerucham, Rosh in the name of R' Meir, and this is the view of Rabbenu Ephraim, and Beit Yosef in the name of Baal HaEitan; and the rest of the poskim did not distinguish, and it is implied from their words the opposite, and some explicitly disagree — see there); and so is practice; even if it is entirely so; and likewise if it has many shades of these kosher appearances — kosher.}
