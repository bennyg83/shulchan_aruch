The laws of lung appearances. In it are 5 seifim.
A lung in which is found any amount black as ink, or that is (similar) to the appearance of flesh, or that is green — amarillo in foreign tongue (and in Ashkenaz they call it geyl) — whether it is like the appearance of egg yolk, whether like saffron, whether like shusta — treifah; and likewise if it is like the appearance of ophta that is white as a valley — forbidden. {Rama: And some say this means that it is like the appearance of palm scar tissue.} {Rama: And some say that even egg-white color is treifah (Mahariu) (Arukh entry kachal, Rokeach, and so is practice).}
