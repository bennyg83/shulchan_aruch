Saffron. R' Meir explained in his commentary: whether saffron itself or things dyed from it — see there.
Shusta. It is also a yellow-green color; and the poskim and acharonim wrote that green like gold or wax, or like the wing of a peacock, or like the pallor of a sick person's face called in foreign tongue geyl zokht — all are a species of yellow-green geyl and treifah; and above siman 44 s.k. 9 and below siman 188 s.k. 4 it is explained that geyl like an etrog — its law is like wax; and the same applies here — treifah, as implied in Tosafot and poskim, for they have one law.
Egg-white color. Darkei Moshe wrote: so is found in the name of Maharil — meaning egg-white after it was cooked; and likewise egg yolk; and it appears that for yolk he means that in both cases here it is treifah; but for white he means specifically after it was cooked, but while still alive it is only clear and translucent and not white; and so is implied in Semachot.
