Or that it is green. Maharshal wrote: so is found in the name of Maharil — furthermore there are other appearances where vegetation is upon them and treifah; and they are greenish like the wing of a peacock called in foreign tongue pavoya, and like wax, and like gold, and like the pallor of faces called geyl zokht; general rule: green called green or blaw — kosher; and that called geyl — treifah; but what I found additionally: appearance like a toad called kreyt shtayn, or like an eye, or like weinkshil — treifah; and it is not clear, for weinkshil appearance — behold red is kosher in every regard — end of his words.
