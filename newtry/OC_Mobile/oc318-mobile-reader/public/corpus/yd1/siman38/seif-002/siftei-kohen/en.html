To a permitted appearance. And Maharshal Piskei Avot siman 39 wrote: so is found — one must return specifically to the actual appearance of the lung; and he agreed; and so is brought in examinations of the R"tz; and R' Meir in Beit Hashem challenged him; and so is explained in the words of Rambam, Mordechai, Kol Bo, and Rashba — that even if it returns only to a permitted appearance it is kosher; and so is in the Taz.
