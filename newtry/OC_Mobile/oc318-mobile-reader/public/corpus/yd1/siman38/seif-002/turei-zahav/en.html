To a permitted appearance, etc. It implies: even though it does not whiten like the actual lung appearance but only changed to a permitted shade; and Maharshal explained in the name of one hagahah: disqualifying appearances must be specifically through inflation itself like lung flesh, and if not — treifah; and Maharshal wrote on this: it is well written — since even for actual whitening it entered the mind to forbid, as the Ra'em wrote in the name of Rashi; rather we grasp the words of Rabbeinu Hai — that all appearances that return through inflation are kosher; therefore presumably we are not lenient to permit except for actual whitening like the lung's appearance at the outset, and not in another matter — end of his words. And in my humble opinion: behold also in a kosher appearance there is somewhat of a flaw, as the Tur wrote (but) [regarding] "if it has many shades" — this teaches us: since it was spoiled — treifah; it teaches us that not so — rather every forbidden appearance must be clearly kosher without any flaw.
