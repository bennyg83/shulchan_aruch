White as a valley. [See in responsum Chasdei David siman 34 regarding lungs of sheep found upon them like a clear shiny appearance showing white; they cut inside the lung flesh in depth and there it whitened more and more until reaching good flesh. He wrote: I reviewed all acharonim' books after Shach and all forbid. In Orach Mishor, Tuv Ta'am, and Pri Tevuah — and in my opinion there is no need for the reason that we are not expert in ophta appearance and white valley, but rather without it: since Tuv Ta'am raised that all appearances not explicitly explained for permission are forbidden; and although Noda Biyehudah disagrees — in the pamphlet of proofs at the end of Pri Tevuah siman 53 he wrote the Gaon retracted; and it was fit to retract, for we are not expert in flesh that the doctor scraped, and every change of appearance not explained to permit — we must be concerned perhaps it dissolved (as written siman 43 s.k. 5 and siman 50 s.k. 3); if so, simple that even clear white is disqualified; but according to the language of the question — on the skin it is only clear like whiteness, and in lung flesh it whitens more — then one should peel the skin and see if afterward it remains in its whiteness, for one may attribute that the wound is inside the flesh and through the flesh its clearness appears on the skin; and in such a case we say regarding a disqualifying appearance that is on account of pus beneath — kosher — see there.]
