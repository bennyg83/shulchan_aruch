Baer Heitev; and see in Noda Biyehudah siman 13 on the law of change of appearance in flesh — he wrote: if even part of the flesh is flawed, even though it dominated only in part of the limb and the change of appearance is on the entire limb — treifah; and I brought in my book 38 s.k. 58 and in Shulchan HaTahor there siman 101; and there I brought that the Gaon, may his memory be blessed, tore his rulings in his own handwriting in the margin of his Yoreh De'ah and returned and conceded to my words — I, the humble author — who am stringent regarding change of appearance alone when there is concern it is flesh that the doctor scraped, for behold it is like dead flesh; and one may not say there is no flaw in the flesh — only change of appearance alone — see there; and see in Noda Biyehudah there: change of appearance in a boil, and specifically black appearance — certainly one should declare treifah; but only slightly raised is not considered a flaw in flesh if it was not more changed than other flesh to be soft or somewhat dissolved — see there. And if a woman crushes morah who says there was a boil there and she denies it — she is not believed to forbid, even though she came to teach before him — then she is believed, as explained siman 185 — namely on what she says in his name; but when there is denial in the body of the matter — it is not better than one witness in the world. And see there: he wrote that on account of change of appearance alone also there is no place to permit except in great financial loss; for one must be concerned the change is on account of fell to light, even though we did not know, as Shach wrote siman 52; and nevertheless, since it was already permitted and vessels were also mixed — one should permit — see there siman 14.
