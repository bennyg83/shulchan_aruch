(Shulchan Aruch seif 3) There are those who say that these appearances do not disqualify except when the change is in the skin on account of it and not on account of pus or congealed blood — kosher. Behold the Mechaber wrote this in the name of "there are those who say" — follow his reasoning, as he wrote in Beit Yosef at the end of the siman: although Ran wrote these matters in the name of Ra'ah and did not disagree — I did not see any of the poskim who disagreed on this — end. But one may wonder on him — he himself wrote in this siman in the name of Rambam: all these appearances do not disqualify, etc., and one rubs by hand, etc. — and this is what Shulchan Aruch wrote seif 2. And Beit Yosef wrote on this: for this reason Demars — perhaps moisture is on the lung that changes its appearance; and when one rubs by hand upon it it passes and the lung remains in natural shades — end; behold Rambam also holds thus. And further — what is different from a case where moisture is found in it that is permitted, that in the animal's lifetime it could emerge through coughing and shaking, as written siman 36, and Shach there s.k. 26? And similarly in Hilchot Gedolot and Rashba in Torat HaBayit (daf 36) and Tur and Hagahah siman 40 regarding a needle in the heart — see there s.k. 3 in the hagahah. And so is proven in my humble opinion in the Talmud, Rashi, Rosh, and Rashba in Torat HaBayit and Shulchan Shel Avraham regarding lung that reddened — kosher, as written seif 4; and even redness is not a flaw, for the red comes on account of its congealed blood; only if the skin is not punctured from within — we learn: if the change of appearance came from pus or congealed blood — kosher; and this is clear. And see Maharshal Piskei Avot siman 23; and see in responsum Noda Biyehudah siman 10 — he concludes one should permit all appearances not mentioned in the Gemara; and all permitted appearances in great financial loss we do not consider suspicious matter relative to two suspicious matters; only for redness one should be stringent.
