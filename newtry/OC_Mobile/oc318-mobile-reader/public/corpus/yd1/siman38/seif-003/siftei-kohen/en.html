There are those who say, etc. And that which is written below "and likewise if it has many shades of these kosher appearances — kosher" — implies that disqualifying appearances disqualify when the change is in the skin on account of them; but in Beit Yosef daf 157b he did not write thus; and so all acharonim in their examinations; and Bach: where the change in the skin is not on account of the appearances themselves — they are kosher.
