The law of a place where there is wine and a non-Jew was alone there. In it are 5 seifim. A non-Jew who was alone with the wine — even in our domain, even for a short time — is forbidden in benefit. But one who is known to us not to worship avodah zarah: it is permitted to leave wine alone with him in our domain for a short time, such as the time it takes to walk the distance of a mil, or more, even in a city that is entirely non-Jews, and even if he informed him that he is leaving; but we do not entrust wine to him in his home. And even in our homes it is forbidden if it is for a long time; and if one transgressed and deposited it, it is forbidden for drinking. (Non-Jews outside of Eretz Yisrael, even though they do not worship avodah zarah, nevertheless we do not leave wine alone with them — Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba.)
