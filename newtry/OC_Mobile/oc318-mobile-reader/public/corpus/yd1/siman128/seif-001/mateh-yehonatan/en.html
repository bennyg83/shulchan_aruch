(Siman 128 in Shulchan Aruch seif 2 in the gloss) And likewise if he found that the gentile is holding the latch, etc. See above siman 124 in Turei Zahav s.k. 33, who wrote that this law also applies to gentiles of today, for it is not touch through something else; rather we are concerned lest he touched the spurt itself; and Shach s.k. 8 at the end wrote: however for us, since gentiles nowadays are not idolaters, it is permitted even for drinking — see there; and according to his words all of Rama's words in this gloss are not for practical halachah for us — and this is a great forced reading; therefore it appears that certainly Shach's law is correct, that we do not say he certainly touched the spurt, for it is possible he did not touch at all; and regarding this it is not a regular matter; and Rama deals when the barrel is open from above as if he could touch from above — stam it is permitted, for we say he was seized as a thief and is afraid; unlike if the latch is in his hand, and we say he himself removed the latch — then we see he is not afraid, we are concerned lest he touched from above, and the wine is forbidden even nowadays since he is not afraid:
