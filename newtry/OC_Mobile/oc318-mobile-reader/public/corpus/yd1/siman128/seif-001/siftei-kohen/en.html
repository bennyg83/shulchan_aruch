This implies that benefit in all cases is permitted; and such appears in Beit Yosef; but Bach challenged him — it is only permitted in benefit when it is known he did not touch; otherwise it is forbidden even in benefit, as Tosafot wrote, that he is not concerned about the touch of a gentile; and likewise in the Rosh and the Tur, that the reason one who deposited with him is forbidden is because we suspect perhaps he exchanged it with his own, and his own is certainly forbidden since he is not concerned about the touch of a gentile.
