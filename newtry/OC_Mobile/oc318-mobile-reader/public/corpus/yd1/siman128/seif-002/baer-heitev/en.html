Sealed. Since Rama rules in siman 130:2 that one seal suffices after the fact even when wine is entrusted, all the more so here. Rama states the Mechaber's ruling without qualification because he relies upon siman 130, the principal location of this law. Taz and Shach.
A seal. Even if the barrels are open. Rashba. Bach writes that this applies only where the house opens to a public domain and Jews pass there; otherwise the non-Jew does not fear discovery. See siman 131.
Customary. We do not suspect that he inserted his finger and touched the wine inside, or touched wine that flowed outside through the spigot, thereby forbidding the wine inside through a connecting stream, since no defect is visible. Accordingly, nowadays it is permitted even for drinking in a case of loss, because this would be indirect, unintentional non-Jewish touch. Shach. Taz disagrees and forbids all the wine even in a major loss. Nekudot HaKesef supports Shach.
