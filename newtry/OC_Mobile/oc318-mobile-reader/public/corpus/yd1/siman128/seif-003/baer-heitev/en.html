With a lock. Beit Yosef cites Rashba that this applies only where it is known that the non-Jew locked himself inside. If this is unknown, the wine is not forbidden merely because there is an interior lock. This applies by day; at night we assume he locked himself in. Likewise, holes in the door permit only by day, but not at night. Rama, end of siman 130.
