And even if an Israelite lived in that house. Since the gentile has ownership in the house — Tur; and in Rashba it states: since the gentile has ownership in the house or in the wine; and see above at the end of simanim 130 and 131; however that which we require it be known he closed — that is by day; but at night it is assumed he closed when he has ownership in the house, and it is forbidden as below at the end of siman 130 in the gloss; and likewise holes in the door do not help at night.
