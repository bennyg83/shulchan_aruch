If he was caught as a thief. If Israel finds it, Israel will be captured as a thief by the Governor and he is not a god:
If caught as a thief upon entering, the wine is permitted for drinking. This is the view of R' Yitzchak cited in the Tur; and in Rashi's name it cites that the matter depends on whether he was caught as a thief regarding touching. Beit Yosef already wrote that Rashi did not say this; and therefore Maharshal emended the attribution: if caught as a thief upon entering, it is permitted — even if not caught as a thief regarding the touching; and if not caught as a thief upon entering, it is forbidden — even if caught as a thief regarding the touching. And Rashi explains: since he entered without permission, he certainly entered for the purpose of wine-libation. This is still not settled in my mind — what [Maharshal] wrote: "even if he was not caught as a thief regarding the touching" — how can such a case arise? For it is impossible to touch without entering, and since he entered he would be caught as a thief. And it seems one might explain: even if he was not caught as a thief regarding the touching — this is when the wine was lying in an open place, and since he also did not fear being caught regarding the touching — yet even so the wine is forbidden — and now the wine is in a concealed place and he was flustered and panicked upon his entry and did not touch.
