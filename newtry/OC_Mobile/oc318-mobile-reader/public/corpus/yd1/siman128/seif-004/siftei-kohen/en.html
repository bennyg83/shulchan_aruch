Permitted for drinking. Even though he is not seized as a thief for the entry — since he is afraid lest an Israelite come and see him touching — the Rosh; and that is specifically when the gentile knows that the touch of gentiles is forbidden to an Israelite and his wine will be lost thereby, as above seif 2; but otherwise the concept of fear does not apply; and such we say at the beginning of siman 115.
Seized as a thief. Wherever the expression "seized as a thief" is mentioned, it means that if they find him he would be seized as a thief — as immediately below in seif 5.
Unless there is reason to suspect he touched to drink. And even so, it is permitted in benefit in a case of loss nowadays — as above at the beginning of siman 123.
