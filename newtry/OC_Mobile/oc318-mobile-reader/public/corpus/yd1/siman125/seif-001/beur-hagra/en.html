He took, etc. Folio 60a; and 72b.
For drinking [only]. The Rosh there, folio 60a, in the name of Rashi; and the Ran there in the name of Rashi; see the Rosh there. But in our Rashi there it is written that it is forbidden in benefit [altogether]; and likewise at the end of the chapter in the Gemara's conclusion it writes "and so forth, etc."; and so wrote Tosafot there, 60a, s.v. "a non-Jew, etc." — and see Tosafot, 58b, s.v. "but, etc." And whatever, etc. On account of the continuous stream — and even though there, 72b, we say "what is inside, etc." — that is according to what was thought [by way of] refuting Rav Huna's [position]. Rashba and other later authorities; and so wrote Tosafot, 56b, s.v. "gargotin," in the name of Rashi; and so holds Rambam, as written in siman 124, seif 18: "he took, etc. — it is forbidden." But Rashi there, 72b (s.v. "what is inside, etc.") and Tosafot there, s.v. "his force, etc." did not write thus; and so wrote the Rosh and Mordechai. And that is the meaning of "and some say, etc." There is [a case where], etc. He ruled completely according to the first view — except that in [the case of] a great loss he ruled that a continuous stream is permitted in any case, as stated above at the beginning of siman 126; and that is the meaning of "as stated below, etc." Or that he did not, etc. See what is written above, siman 124, seif 18 — and it is puzzling, as stated there; and so too I found that the Shach here was puzzled by it. See there, seif 14; and there, seif 24, in the gloss: "wine that was lying, etc."; and below, seif 4; and in Tosafot in several places.
