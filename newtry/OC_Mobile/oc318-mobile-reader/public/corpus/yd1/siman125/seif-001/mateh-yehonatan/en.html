(Siman 125 in Shulchan Aruch seif 1) A non-Jew took a utensil of wine, etc.; and what remains in the utensil is also because of stream — see Shach seif kaf 1 what he challenged from above in siman 124 seif 14 that he ruled what remains inside is permitted, and that is because they did not stringently forbid his force in stream; and see above what Ba'er Hetev wrote to resolve this difficulty; and what he further challenged from below siman 126 seif 5, that we say wherever it is forbidden only for drinking we do not say stream is connection — and here too it is forbidden only for drinking as the Mechaber wrote — if so stream connection does not apply; and it appears to me that according to reason by Torah law one should distinguish in wine inside the utensil whether below it is forbidden in benefit or forbidden only for drinking — for if it is connection and is as though everything is mixed, what difference prohibition of benefit or prohibition of drinking; and that Rashba and also the Mechaber wrote below siman 126 seif 5 that what one pours from it is permitted — that is since there is no non-Jew's touch of a handbreadth in the utensil, and it is forbidden only because the Sages forbade using a utensil of wine of a non-Jew as explained in the Talmud and poskim; if so regarding where its prohibition is because of mixture with yayin nesech — if so we properly say stream is connection and is as though everything is mixed; but here where it is forbidden because the utensil is of yayin nesech, therefore forbidden to use it — such a case we do not say stream is connection, that what is above would be considered as below in a utensil of yayin nesech; therefore they did not forbid what is above; but here where the wine is forbidden because of his force and what is above is forbidden because of mixture, etc.
