The law of wine that comes through the ko’ach of a non-Jew, and the ko’ach of his ko’ach. In it are 11 seifim.
If a non-Jew took a utensil of wine and lifted it, and the wine came out — even though he did not agitate it — it becomes forbidden for drinking, since the wine came through his ko’ach. And what remains in the utensil is also forbidden because of nitzok. (And there are those who say that we do not forbid nitzok regarding the ko’ach of a non-Jew — Tur, the Rosh, Mordechai, and Ran in the name of Rashi and Tosafot, and Raavan, and Responsa Maimoniot, Ma’achalot Assurot, siman 11. Nevertheless, one should be stringent unless there is great loss, as below siman 126.) But if it was without intention, for he did not know that it was wine (or he did not lift the utensil — Beit Yosef, siman 124), everything is permitted even for drinking.
