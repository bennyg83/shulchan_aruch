Water. And even one who holds that a stream is a connection — this is specifically wine to wine; but water to wine, no; and accordingly, even if he touched the water, it is permitted. Shach.
To mix. And even in a barrel, when it is known that he intends to mix it, it is forbidden. Shach.
Permitted. For it is a rabbinic doubt to be lenient; and Ran wrote that in a cup, even if he poured only a little water or too much, it is forbidden, since people's practice differs in mixing between much and little — mixing is so called — end of his words; and it appears that in these lands where the practice is not to mix in a cup, it is permitted unless it is known his intent was to mix; and if he knew it was wine, presumably he intended to mix. Shach.
