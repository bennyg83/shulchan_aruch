And he did not know it was wine. But if he knew it was wine, presumably he intended to dilute it; and likewise Ran that in a cup the matter appears, that even if he poured only a little water or too much it is forbidden, since people's practice differs in dilution between much and little — dilution is so called — end of his words; and it appears that in these lands where the practice is not to dilute in a cup, it is permitted unless it is known his intent was to dilute.
But if he intends to dilute, etc. As Beit Yosef — it deals with a cup; but in a barrel Ran and Rashba wrote that even if intending to dilute it is permitted, since it is an uncommon matter they did not decree at all — end of his words; and it is strained; and also in Ran and Rashba nothing appears except that they wrote even if he gave water for proper dilution for drinking it is permitted, because it is not customary to dilute a barrel in one day, and in an uncommon matter they did not decree at all; and it means presumably we are not concerned for dilution; and since he did not intend to dilute it is permitted, for an uncommon matter they did not decree; but if it is known he intended to dilute — forbidden.
