(Likut) In which there is wine, etc. According to R' Yitzchak's explanation in Tosafot there s.v. what is, etc.; and likewise Ran explained in the name of others; and he wrote what is written: even though the wine drips, etc. ; it deals with wine in the winepress; and he wrote that so in the Tosefta we learn there: a non-Jew who brought grapes in baskets and dardoriyot to the winepress ; even though they were crushed in the winepress and wine drips on the grapes ; permitted; and he wrote but Raavad explained the concern is because of wine in the baskets and dardoriyot spilled by the non-Jew [and because of his force, and these are his words: and according to this view, etc., and see above Pri Megadim] in pouring the grapes into the winepress; and even though it is not wine that was not yet drawn ; nevertheless l'chatchila forbidden because of "go, go"; and according to this view Raavad ruled that if a Jew assists the non-Jew it is permitted even l'chatchila, since in complete wine it is forbidden only l'chatchila because of decree as written in seif 3 ; it would be decree upon decree; and these are his words: even though there is, etc.; and if, etc.; and Ran wrote according to Raavad's explanation even in our winepress where wine does not float on the grapes ; l'chatchila forbidden; unlike according to Tosafot's explanation; and likewise the practical difference of assistance as mentioned (until here).
Even though there is, etc. Gemara there and Tosafot there s.v. what is, etc.
