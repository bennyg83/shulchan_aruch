A non-Jew who brought grapes in baskets and in dardoriyot (meaning, per Rashi: small tubs) and threw them into a winepress in which there is trodden wine: it is permitted b'dieved, even though there is in the dardoriyot wine that dripped from the grapes in them. And if a Jew assists the non-Jew in throwing them into the winepress, it is permitted even l'chatchila.
