If wine is flowing from the barrel on a wagon into the vat, and the non-Jew lifts the wagon so that the wine will flow more nicely, it is permitted. {Rama: But if it would not have flowed without the non-Jew’s lifting, it is forbidden. The same law applies if a Jew is pouring from one utensil to another and a non-Jew comes and shakes the Jew’s hand: if the wine would have flowed anyway, it is permitted (the reasoning of the Rav to distinguish, in what Mordechai wrote in the name of R. Peretz); and if not, it is forbidden. A non-Jew who came to unload a barrel of wine from the wagon and wine came out by his ko’ach outside — it is permitted, for it was without intention, since he is occupied with his work (Mordechai, perek R. Yishmael).}
