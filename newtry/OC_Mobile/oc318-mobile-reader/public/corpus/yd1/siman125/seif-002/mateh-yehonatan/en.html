(There in the hagahah) And there are those who say we do not forbid, etc. This law is from the Rosh; and see in Beit Yosef that he challenges the Rosh's words against each other, for in perek HaSocher et HaPo'el siman 25 he wrote that because of stream it is not forbidden, for they did not stringently forbid so much the force of a non-Jew to forbid what is connected to him through stream; and in perek R' Yishmael siman 4 he brought in the name of Rashi, and these are his words: even according to the one who says stream is connection, nevertheless stream of a stream is not connection, etc.; and if so if the non-Jew emptied wine from a jug into a cup, the wine in the cup is forbidden because of the force of the non-Jew and the wine in the jug is forbidden because of stream; and if afterward a Jew drew wine from the barrel into the jug, the wine in the barrel is permitted — end of his words; if so the Rosh holds the wine in the jug is forbidden because of stream even though the wine in the cup was forbidden only because of his force — from this we learn he holds stream connection applies even to his force; and one may resolve that certainly the Rosh holds also the wine in the jug is not forbidden, for we do not say stream connection in his force; and that which he wrote there according to Rashi's words is because it is difficult for him by Torah law to distinguish between his force and other matters; and perforce one must say that since his force is lighter than other yayin nesech and we do not say stream connection where prohibitions are lighter, the Rosh challenged whence he knows this; therefore he brought the incident of Rashi who did not forbid stream of a stream — meaning wine forbidden because of stream is a lighter prohibition and we do not say stream is connection; if so the same law applies to wine forbidden because of his force — examine carefully. And what he wrote in his language there that the wine in the cup is forbidden — he only copied Rashi's language but he himself did not hold thus — examine carefully.
