The wineskin full or lacking. See Be'er Heitev and responsum Avkat Rokhel siman 62: a full vessel of wine was placed on a shelf, and beneath this vessel was another full vessel of wine; a non-Jew was walking in the house, and as he walked along the top of the shelf the upper vessel shook from the shaking of the shelf, spilled, and fell into the lower vessel — the wine is permitted even for drinking, for what shook from his force is permitted, as we say regarding the wineskin; and also what fell through shaking into the lower vessel was not forbidden, for even if he threw a pebble with his hand it is not forbidden — see there.
