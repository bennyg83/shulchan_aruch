(Seif 6) And if a Jew assists. In the source of the law in Terumat HaDeshen and Terumat HaKodesh it appears for practical halachah that it is explained this is when there is no wine in the winepress but it is forbidden from concern for wine on the grapes in the dardoriyot that exchanges with complete wine; in this if a Jew assists it is permitted, since even in complete wine by law it is permitted, for the force of a non-Jew and a Jew are mixed in it. But wine in the pit that is forbidden for Ramban because lest he touch with his hand the wine in the pit — in this there is no advantage in the Jew's assistance — see there; and if so the Mechaber who stated a winepress in which there is wine, which implies he holds like Ramban, and on this he says and if a Jew assists — requires study.
