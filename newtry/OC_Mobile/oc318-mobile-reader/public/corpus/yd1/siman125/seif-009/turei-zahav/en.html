The mouth of the wineskin in his hand. This is Rambam's view that one who holds an open utensil and agitates — forbids; perforce here he must hold the mouth of the wineskin in his hand, that it not be an open utensil — so Beit Yosef. But it is difficult for me, for it was already explained that also the Rosh holds he forbids in agitation through lifting; and here it deals with lifting, for he carries the wineskin; and it appears since he carries in the manner of walking it is not called the way of libation in this; and likewise I saw in Derishah in the name of Rashal that he wrote also Rambam concedes here it is permitted for this reason; but one cannot distinguish thus in Rambam's view, for if so why was it needed here that the mouth of the wineskin be in his hand; rather for the Rosh one may distinguish thus.
