And even if the non-Jew could pour by himself, etc. Meaning: and the Jew cannot pour by himself — so Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen; and on this Tur wrote in the name of Rashba that even a non-Jew assists a Jew — and why is the non-Jew made primary, etc.; meaning: even though the non-Jew has more force, nevertheless now that the Jew also bears in his force — who will tell us that in that force needed for this load there is in it a greater share of the non-Jew than the Jew, since in this load both have force; also Ran ruled to be lenient when the non-Jew can and the Jew cannot; and all this specifically b'dieved; but l'chatchila forbidden.
