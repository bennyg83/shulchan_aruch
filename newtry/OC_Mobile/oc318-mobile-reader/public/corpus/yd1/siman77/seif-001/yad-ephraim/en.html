Avodat HaTaharah and hagahah brought Magen Avraham on zefek or gizzard remaining with food — see there; see Kenesset Yisrael siman 27 disagrees and permits; Meshiv Tzedek siman 69 s.k. 14 also inclined to permit — for practical halachah requires further study; seems forbid only in great loss; see above siman 75 s.k. 8. If piece of animal hide fell in stew — Levushi Serad forbids until sixty like meat cooked without salting — blood in hide; Taz there disagrees — I say do not forbid hide blood at all — Scripture speaks only of flesh blood; Tosafot Chullin 64 — rabbinic decree on fowl blood; but do not decree hide blood because of meat blood — clear to me; we forbid fowl hide because of blood — fowl hide is soft and eaten like meat; not animal hide — not called meat, no blood prohibition.
