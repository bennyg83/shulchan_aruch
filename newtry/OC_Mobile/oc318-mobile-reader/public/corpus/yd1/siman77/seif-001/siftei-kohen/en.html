And all the more so if the inner was salted and not the outer, etc. — view of Rav: even if the inner was salted and remained for the time of salting it does not absorb from the outer, for fire draws and absorbs from the outer and expels — so in Darkei Moshe and Tur HaChadash general rule 9 law 3 from Or Zarua; and in my book I elaborated and proved with clear proofs that all poskim agree that where the inner was salted and remained for the time of salting and the outer is unsalted or did not remain for the time of salting — the inner is forbidden because regarding the inner we do not say as it absorbs so it releases, since it has no blood of its own to exude, and the inner absorbs and we do not say in such a case fire draws to it; but if the inner was not salted it is forbidden only when the outer was salted and did not remain — as Mordechai wrote in the name of R' Yoel and Rabbenu Tam chapter 25 — then the inner absorbs before roasting; and even though we hold above siman 70 that meat salted on unsalted meat — salting afterward helps, and all the more roasting — here it is different since it is inside; and I wrote there that Semak siman 205 and Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Tosafot, Beit Yosef, and Rav in Tur HaChadash understood Hagahot Maimoniyot applies where the outer was salted and remained — which is not the case; and moreover Tosafot wrote that in such a case it is permitted, and so implies from the Gemara from what they challenge from coating of bar goose — see there and investigate. And I further proved there that Semak and Hagahot Maimoniyot agree; and Or Zarua beginning general rule 9 also applies in such a case (also I wrote there Mahari Trushan agrees in all these laws — see there); and Rav in Tur HaChadash and Darkei Moshe understood Or Zarua on a different matter — very forced; but when inner equals outer — both salted and remained, or both unsalted, or even both salted and did not remain — permitted in every case as Mordechai states explicitly; but when inner unsalted and outer salted, remained, and rinsed — obviously permitted; and as Taz wrote regarding outer we say as it absorbs so it releases even though it does not exude its own blood; and even though regarding meat salted on already salted rinsed meat we hold above siman 70 salting and roasting afterward do not help — here it is different since it exudes immediately at the time of absorption; therefore if inner salted and did not remain and outer salted, remained, and rinsed — or already exuded all its gravy — the outer is forbidden for it absorbs also from the inner before roasting — all this I brought there to reconcile the poskim and it is clear; and so is Maharshal's view in all this in Or Zarua end gate 4 and in his book chapter of Chullin siman 29 — see there; and as you will find in Maharshal's words that where inner salted and did not remain and outer remained — the outer is permitted — must explain his words that outer was not rinsed and did not exude all its gravy, though somewhat forced in his words, for certainly outer is not better than other meat salted on already salted rinsed meat or that exuded all gravy — forbidden even for roasting as explained above siman 70; and Maharshal also wrote so in several places:
