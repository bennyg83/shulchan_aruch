And even if the outer was salted. Explanation: and it exuded all its blood; Beit Yosef wrote unlike Hagahot Maimoniyot who forbids when outer salted and not inner because outer is not busy to exude; and Tur HaChadash general rule 9 ruled like Hagahot Maimoniyot and so Mordechai; but when inner salted and not outer — all agree permitted because fire draws blood. Rashal wrote chapter of Chullin siman 29 that when both salted, even if did not remain, or inner salted and did not remain and outer not salted — all permitted; prohibition only when outer salted and did not remain and inner not salted — then inner alone is forbidden.
For as it absorbs, etc. — and it is not comparable to meat that was salted and fell into gravy which is forbidden — there exudation is after time of absorption.
Outer alone. Explanation: and rinse them.
It does not release blood in the inner. Explanation: Rashal — and these are his words: it does not release blood inside, meaning of the inner side of the outer itself, even though inner was salted, end of his words; and though language does not imply so, one should be concerned for his words for practical halachah; and it is not comparable to what he ruled siman 69 permitting b'dieved when salted on one side — here worse since filling inside seals on all sides.
If both were not salted. Explanation: full salting, each alone until fit for pot in all its laws if meat with eggs in filling. (This belongs to Tur.) See Tur: it is written to distinguish between animal, etc.; Beit Yosef wondered we do not find this language in Scripture, etc. — all this can be answered in one answer; and we further analyze Tur's language "it is written" whereas in many places Tur begins with verses without saying "it is written" — nevertheless here he said "it is written" — and though not a great precision, incidentally we answer within our words. Rather Rav saw the derashah Beit Yosef mentioned only that derashah is found in two places — end of Shemini and end of Kedoshim — and Rashi explained in both verses; but certainly the primary derashah is from the verse at end of Kedoshim "and you shall distinguish," etc.; and what Rashi explained in Shemini is another matter — see there; primary learning is from Kedoshim; in Shemini he also explained similarly; but primary intention of "it is written" is the law Rashi explained that one must know and be expert — not to bring verse language only; and what Beit Yosef wondered why not bring verse "between the animal that may be eaten" — that comes for itself as we said; and Darkei Moshe also wrote this is only Rav's language not the verses.
