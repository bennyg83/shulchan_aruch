How does one circumcise? One cuts the foreskin — all the skin covering the corona until the corona is revealed; then one tears the soft membrane beneath the skin with a fingernail and turns it here and there until the flesh of the corona is seen; then one sucks the circumcision until blood comes from the distant places so that danger not come; and some places have the custom that the mohel sucks with his mouth, and in other places with a pipette.
