Tur in the name of Rambam from the implication of the Gemara that says "this is the reason only according to Rav," etc. — Menachot page 42 amud 1 — so too there; and in Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rabbi Menachem that the reason is because we do not find in the Torah circumcision for its sake; and he further wrote it is possible Rambam too concedes one must draw covenant blood from him, only one need not return and cut; and so Rabbi Menachem wrote.
(°) Explanation: regarding obligation to circumcise him specifically through an Israelite; but if Israelites circumcised him within eight properly he fulfilled, as Rama wrote siman 262 seif 1; and if gentiles circumcised him properly within eight — according to Rama's ruling one must draw covenant blood from him.
(°) Explanation: because it is mere verbal acquisition; but an oath is effective, as written afterward in the name of Rosh.
