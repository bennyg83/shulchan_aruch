To give precedence. Shach questions Rama. The Mechaber already gives precedence to an adult Jewish male. If Rama means that a woman should never circumcise even when no adult Jewish male is available, the phrase 'and so is the custom' is inappropriate, because such a rare case does not establish a custom.
Remain. If shreds of foreskin remain, the circumcision must be completed properly by removing them or performing periah. If nothing remains, covenantal blood must be drawn. See siman 262.
A formal acquisition. Even without a binding acquisition, the principle that the remnant of Israel does no injustice applies, and one who retracts may be called wicked. Shach.
