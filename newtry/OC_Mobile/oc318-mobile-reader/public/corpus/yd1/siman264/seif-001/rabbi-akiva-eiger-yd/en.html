(Siman 264, proof 1) His brothers died from circumcision — it implies that an apostate regarding foreskin, even if not to provoke, is unfit to circumcise. Peri Chadash in his book part 5 (section 3) challenged this; his view is that anyone who is not [circumcising] to provoke is fit to circumcise, as he is called "bar zevichah" above (siman 2 seif 2). See responsum Beit Yaakov (siman 104) and in Even HaEzer (Orach Chayim siman 289).
