To circumcise. See in Beit HaTov in the name of Maharit; and so Shach in Choshen Mishpat end of siman 382 that one may not give to another mohel, and one who does so nullifies a positive mitzvah, etc. — see there; see in responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 51 who wrote that according to Shach the father must circumcise his son himself and not others with him, whether cutting or uncovering — see there where he elaborated and challenged Shach and concluded the father-mohel may give to another mohel — see there; and so Tashbetz above siman 28 s.k. 14 that one may honor another in the mitzvah of circumcision and covering; and so Peri Megadim there; and see in responsum Beit Efrayim part Choshen Mishpat and responsum Orach Chaim siman 458; and see in Sefer Nachalat Azriel on Chullin page 87 what he wrote on this.
