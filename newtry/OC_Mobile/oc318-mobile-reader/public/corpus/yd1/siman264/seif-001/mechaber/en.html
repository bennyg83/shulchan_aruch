Who is fit to circumcise, with what one circumcises, and how one circumcises. And it contains 6 seifim.
All are fit to circumcise, even a slave, woman, and minor; and an uncircumcised Israelite whose brothers died from circumcision — if there is an adult Israelite who knows how to circumcise, he takes precedence over all of them (and some say a woman does not circumcise (Semak and Hagahot Mordechai), and so is the custom).
