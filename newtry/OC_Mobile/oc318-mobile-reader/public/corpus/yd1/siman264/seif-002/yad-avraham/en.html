(There) If he gave the circumcision and sandek to one person it is forbidden to retract. However if he retracted it is a retraction and acceptance of a thin kinyan does not help here, for it is mere verbal kinyan — Shach in the name of responsum of Rosh kelal 2. And he further wrote in the name of Darkei Moshe it is possible Rosh spoke only that the court cannot compel him, but nevertheless he agrees with Ramah that there is in it because of "a remnant of Israel," etc., "they shall not speak falsehood," and it is forbidden to retract and permitted to call him wicked — end quote. And it appears he did not see Rosh's additional responsa in print, who wrote explicitly in kelal 102 that one may retract and there is no lack of faith in this; and it is possible it was not yet printed in Shach's days; and it is explained there the same in every matter where kinyan does not apply, such as a matter of the heart — there is no lack of faith. However for this matter a matter of the heart is better, for kinyan helps in what is customary, such as regarding tenancy as written in responsum kelal 13 that tenancy kinyan is not from Torah, only because it is a matter of the heart they adopted to make kinyan; but here according to Rosh custom does not help because it is verbal kinyan. Or this is an uncommon matter and in such a case there is no custom; and it appears Maharshal too intended this in chapter HaChovel — specifically here there is no custom, and not like Ba'al Kitzur in siman 226 and Rabbi Eliezer who brought there Mordechai's words in chapter HaBoneh in general that one may acquire a matter of the heart.
