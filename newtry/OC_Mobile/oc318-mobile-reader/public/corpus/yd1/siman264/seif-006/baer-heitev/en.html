Shach wrote: this means one should trouble with the child and push the skin back and tie it there around the gid above the atarah if it can stand there and not return downward; and if this is impossible one need not cut anything — and so Bach wrote; and he further wrote: however, where one was born circumcised but not completely — such as the skin covers most of the height of the atarah or most of its circumference — even if they already drew covenant blood from him on the eighth day of his birth, since afterward even when he becomes aroused he appears only partially circumcised — part still covers most of the height of the atarah or most of the circumference — he must be circumcised and cut all flesh from here and from here until he appears fully circumcised at the time of arousal; and Beit Yosef in the name of a Spanish sage wrote: examination at arousal was stated only for children who are fleshy and pendulous who were first circumcised properly; but for all others the atarah must be exposed even not at the time of arousal — end quote; and to me the sage's words require investigation, for Yerushalmi implies that even without circumcising properly they also examine him at arousal — and it requires investigation — end quote Shach; and in Nekudot HaKesef he also wrote he found proof for his words from Rambam's commentary on the Mishnah end of chapter Rabbi Eliezer of circumcision — see there; and Taz wrote: since many times there is concern the mohel errs at the time of circumcision and thinks he circumcised properly and uncovered properly, but in truth it is not proper — therefore at every time there should be other mohalim present who look well at the uncovering whether it is proper, for there is room to err — sometimes very thin skin is found after the cutting as Limel wrote in his responsum, and he uncovers that skin and the main uncovering remains — therefore one must be careful as stated — end quote.
