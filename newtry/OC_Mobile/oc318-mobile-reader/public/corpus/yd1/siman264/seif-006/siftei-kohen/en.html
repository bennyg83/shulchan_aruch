“We need to repair the meat. It is ostensibly to say that the flesh is not cut, but in the words of the Rambam, a word appears that nothing should be cut from the rod which appears to be circulated at the time of the ornament, but that it should be corrected, i.e., to the small, and to push the skin back, and to link it there around the rod, if it is impossible to cut and to it is impossible
Since he was circumcised once properly — meaning: and one should say according to above seif 5 that the majority of the corona must be revealed, because there he had not yet been circumcised according to its law; but here, since he was already circumcised according to its law by Torah, even if he did not appear circumcised at all, even when he becomes an adult he would not be called uncircumcised except rabbinically, since he appears circumcised only at the head of the corona and below a bit appears circumcised, we call him circumcised; and so wrote Tur siman 264, and Bach wrote. However, where he was born circumcised but not completely, such that skin covers the corona in most of its height or most of its circumference — even if he already let blood of the covenant on the eighth day of his birth — since afterward even when he becomes an adult he appears only partially circumcised, still covering most of the height of the corona or most of its circumference, he must circumcise him and cut all the flesh from here and hence until he appears fully circumcised at the time of examination; and certainly one need not cut anything except when they circumcised him initially according to its law; but one who was not circumcised at all, his law is like a baby who was circumcised and has strands covering most of the height of the corona that impede circumcision — for thus it seems to me regarding one born thus, as if the foreskin was cut at the time of circumcision and strands cover most of its height — both this and that, his skin was never cut in any way according to its law. And in Beit Yosef in the name of a Spanish sage it appears he was more stringent, that he wrote that the examination at becoming an adult is stated only for children who are heavy and dangling who were circumcised initially according to its law; but for all others the corona must be revealed even not at the time of examination, etc.; behold he was stringent that for one not circumcised initially according to its law one must cut flesh until the corona is revealed even not at examination; and so I ruled in practice — end. And to me the words of this sage require study, for we learn in Yerushalmi chapter R. Eliezer from the baraita: these are the strands that impede circumcision — R. Avina in the name of R. Yirmiyah said: when it covers most of the height of the corona, etc.; R. Tavi in the name of R. Shmuel said: we examine him at the time he becomes an adult — end; thus even without circumcision according to its law, Shmuel too said we examine at becoming an adult; requires study.
