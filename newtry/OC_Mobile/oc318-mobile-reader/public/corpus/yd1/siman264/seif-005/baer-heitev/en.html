The corona. Beit Yosef records a dispute whether the corona means the entire flesh at the head of the organ or only the raised circular ridge. For circumcision, most of both the flesh and surrounding ridge must be exposed. If remaining skin covers most of the height in one place or most of the circumference of the ridge, he remains uncircumcised and must be circumcised again on a weekday. See siman 266 for Shabbat.
