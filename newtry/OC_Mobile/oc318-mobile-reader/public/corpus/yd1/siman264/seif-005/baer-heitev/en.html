The corona. The Beit Yosef brings a dispute as to what is called the corona: whether it is all the flesh at the head of the membrum, or only the raised ridge that surrounds between that flesh and the membrum. He concludes that for the mitzvah of circumcision one must uncover both: most of the flesh and most of the surrounding ridge. Taz. The Shach wrote: it appears that whether the shreds cover most of the height of the head of the membrum in one place, or the flesh or some of its shreds cover most of the circumference of the ridge, which is the primary corona, even if it does not cover most of the height of the head of the membrum, he is as uncircumcised as he was, and one must circumcise him again if it is a weekday. If it is Shabbat, see above siman 266 seif 2.
