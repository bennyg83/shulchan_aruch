Nevertheless, if, etc. Meaning, even if he withdrew his hands, unlike on Shabbos. This accords with Rashi, 133b, s.v. one who circumcises on Shabbos, and the Tur likewise wrote this. It is unlike the Rambam, who explains that even on a weekday he need not return. That interpretation is untenable in light of the Gemara's statement there, "Who is the tanna who taught: if he withdrew," etc., and also its statement there, "but here, where we require this," etc.
