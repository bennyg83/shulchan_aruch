One places in a Sefer Torah two poles—one at its head and one at its end—and what he left of margin at its head and end he wraps on the poles and sews them with sinews; and two fingerbreadths should remain between the pole and the page. (And in a time of pressure when it is impossible to sew it with sinews, if it was sewn with silk around the poles—they read from it; however, if it is possible to repair it with sinews, even though it was already sewn with silk, they do not read from it until it is repaired) (Terumat HaDeshen siman 51).
