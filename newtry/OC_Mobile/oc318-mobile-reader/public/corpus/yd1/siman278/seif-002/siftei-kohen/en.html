And he should sew it with sinews. It is forbidden, however, to glue the sheets around the rollers, because after the glue has remained for many days it dries and comes loose from the rollers. Mahari Weil writes this in Dinim VeHalakhot, siman 63. Darkei Moshe concludes in his name that it is nevertheless permitted in pressing circumstances, but this does not appear in Mahari Weil there.
