(Siman 278, Taz s.k. 2) And I wonder greatly, etc. In my humble opinion certainly even with two stitches suffices, and he did not write like Shabbat and shaatnez — rather regarding that the stitches be permanent; in that matter stitches are considered regarding Shabbat and shaatnez. And what Tur HaShalem wrote five or six is not precise — he meant to exclude that we do not require it joined in majority; and so appears from the question's language there. And it is known the questions in Tur HaShalem were arranged by Baal Tur HaShalem himself. And it is also possible if it is not joined in five or six stitches the joining is not recognizable; and although for Shabbat and shaatnez two stitches suffice — there the joining is for stringency; but certainly to read ab initio in such a Torah scroll it is not proper to do so. And what he wrote "further I wonder from Gemara Eruvin chapter HaOr VeHaRotav," etc. — in my humble opinion there, whatever tore in majority is nullified from tallit law since it was a complete tallit and the body of the tallit tore; but here only the stitches tore. And further there, since majority tore it is not considered as originally for impurity we require a vessel as originally, as we explained there; and one who examines Rabbenu Shimshon's explanation chapter 28 of Kelim mishnah 10 will see that without this in my humble opinion; and from his words it appears he did not examine there, for he wrote "from Gemara Eruvin chapter HaOr VeHaRotav," etc. — behold it is a complete mishnah in Kelim.
