He should not sew the entire sheet, etc. This is so that it will not tear. When he leaves no unsewn portion, he pulls forcefully while fastening it to the Sefer Torah and it tears. When he now pulls it forcefully and it begins to stretch and is about to tear, he stops pulling it further. Rashi. Bach writes that since this is only a rabbinic enactment intended to prevent the Sefer Torah from tearing, failure to do so does not invalidate the Sefer Torah; this concludes his words. Ran rules likewise, and Beit Yosef and the other later authorities cite him.
