The law of sewing a Sefer Torah. In it are 4 seifim.
One sews a Sefer Torah only with sinews of a pure domestic animal or wild animal; and one should not sew the entire sheet from its head to its end, but rather leave a little at its head and a little at its end unsewn, and all the rest sewn from head to end; and one sews from the outside (Terumat HaDeshen siman 50).
