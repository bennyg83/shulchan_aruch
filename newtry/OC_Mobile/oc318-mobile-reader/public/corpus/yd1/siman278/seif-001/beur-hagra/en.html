And he sews from outside. Mishnah chapter 2 halachah 11; and likewise in Yerushalmi there — halachah of the mishnah that they write on hides, etc., and sew with sinews; and when he sews he should sew like this stitch, except they explained it refers to a Torah scroll that tore — as stated above there "and they attach with glue," etc.; and nevertheless they agreed the same applies in such a case.
