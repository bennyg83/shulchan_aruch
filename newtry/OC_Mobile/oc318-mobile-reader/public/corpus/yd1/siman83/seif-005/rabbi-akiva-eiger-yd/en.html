(siman 83 seif 5 in the hagahah) And so is the custom not to buy them, etc. But if a non-Jew testified before him that he saw them lying with impure ones and he says I do not believe you — even according to those who are lenient below (siman 127 seif 3) in the hagahah that where it can be clarified he is believed — nevertheless here that by law even when lying together it is permitted, he is not believed. Terumat Chesed. And even if he says he saw that they salted together — by law it is forbidden; nevertheless here there is nothing to clarify. Menachem Tziyoni.
