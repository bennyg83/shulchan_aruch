With impure ones. See Sefer Tiferet Lemoshe who wrote: specifically when lying with definite impure ones; but if we do not know they are impure, only we do not recognize them and forbid from doubt since we do not recognize head and spine of pure ones — the pure are permitted, for it is a double doubt; see there.
And a doubt to be stringent. Avodat HaTaharah in the name of Shach: and specifically in salting, etc.; and see responsum Noda BiYehudah Yoreh De'ah siman 26 middle of responsum who disagrees and proved even through pickling and cooking it is permitted d'oraisa, but concludes nevertheless for practical ruling one should be stringent and say through cooking it is certainly d'oraisa as many Rishonim appear; but through pickling it remains not d'oraisa; see at length [and see Sefer Beit Yehudah what he wrote on this].
