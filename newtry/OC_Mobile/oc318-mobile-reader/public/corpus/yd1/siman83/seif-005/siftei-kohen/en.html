Initially, and after they expelled all their brine they mixed together — Rosh and other poskim; with this one need not be troubled that below siman 91 seif 5 Mechaber and Rama ruled that even after salting time it is considered boiling and forbidden even b'dieved — for certainly here brine in fish is minimal and since it remained salting time they already expelled their brine; especially brine of fish is rabbinic; Bach wrote even permitters only when lying with impure but not touching them — end of his words; see siman 70 s.k. 11; investigate:
And some forbid, etc. The reason: since they lie with impure ones we say presumably they were initially salted together; Taharat Chesed general rule 20 section 4 in name of Or Zarua: if there are sixty of pure against impure, buy l'chatchila; Darkei Moshe siman 58: no difference between brine of fish and brine of nevelah — always forbidden d'oraisa even nullified in sixty; see there:

Because there is no way to salt with them. For the way herring are brought from afar in barrels dedicated to them alone, and certainly after many days from salting they placed these near them — Maharai there:
One need not be stringent to search, etc. We do not establish prohibition; and even where it can be clarified — some say in siman 1 hagahah that although most are presumed regarding shechitah, one may not rely l'chatchila on presumption where one can clarify (also Or Zarua) — here we do not permit fish by presumption but on the contrary we say from where shall prohibition come:
Even herring. Meaning: permitted when lying together salted; nevertheless in their brine after salting there is no difference between other fish and herring, for our eyes see the brine is sharp and strong and certainly absorbs — worse than brine mixed in water that is not considered boiling, above end of seif 7:
As long as one does not see. Meaning: if they fell in; but if we sometimes see in a non-Jew's house that he soaks them thus, we are not stringent to beware, for we say it is happenstance — Terumat Chesed there in name of Maharai:
One may permit in every case. Meaning: whether other fish, herring, salted, or floating — so Taharat Chesed; Maharshal in his book forbade even salted until sixty even b'dieved because after time of expulsion they are forbidden in salting — not so all poskim; they did not practice so; s.k. 7; plain that if they fell a full day — twenty-four hours — like pickled, forbidden even b'dieved; Beit Yosef and Taharat Chesed; even doubt if floating twenty-four hours — forbidden, for we rule pickled is d'oraisa, below siman 105:
