Broad head and spine. See responsum Noda BiYehudah second edition siman 29 in responsum from the author's son who wrote that all agree that even one who holds head and spine is a clear sign to permit admits there are pure fish without head and spine; therefore if scales are found it is permitted even if head and spine resemble impure; one sage disagreed — Rav Shmuel Neru replied and upheld his words; see further he rejected the sage who said spine must be wrapped in sinews so it not detach easily from the fish — there is no such thing; see there.
