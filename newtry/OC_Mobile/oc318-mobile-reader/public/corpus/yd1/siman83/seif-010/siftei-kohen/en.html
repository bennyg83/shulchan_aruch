An impure fish found in the innards, etc. Rosh wondered Ahilot — dog ate dead meat, how long in innards three days, fowl and fish until falls in fire — considered digested — permitted; distinguish impurity and prohibition; R' Moshe: chewed vs swallowed whole — this mishnah not digested; Tur: pure swallowed impure forbidden — not digested while whole; it appears if not whole after swallowing we say certainly digested since swallowed whole; but while still whole after swallowing not digested — wrote not digested since whole — not 'since swallowed whole'; Ahilot comparison; wheat in crop Pesach some permit digested — Rosh agreed do not learn prohibition from impurity; Beit Yosef after R' Moshe: understood Tur means except chewing — between Rosh's answers both: chewed — digested; Tur: Ahilot about swallowing whole — impurity digested — prohibition different; R' Moshe: Ahilot when chewed; here and there digested; investigate; first answer primary:
