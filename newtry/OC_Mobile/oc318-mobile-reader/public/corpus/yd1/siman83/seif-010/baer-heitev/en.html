Forbidden. Taz wrote in the name of Tur that it is not considered digested since it is whole; and he explains that whatever was chewed in teeth and swallowed is called digested, but when swallowed whole, even if now somewhat crushed, it is not called digested; practical difference: wheat found in a bird's crop on Pesach is not considered digested even if somewhat crushed (see Orach Chaim siman 467 that some permit, for it is considered digested). Shach wrote: however if found after it came out, it is permitted, for it is considered digested and is mere dust.
