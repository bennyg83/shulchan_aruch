A pure fish found, etc. — Mishnah wording "found" — even if we did not see swallowing (Rav Ashi).
Whether found through digestive tract or swallowing or womb — Rav Sheshet and R' Papa; all the more Rav Ashi.
Whether it came out — Tosafot there: no proof from majority; permitted in womb — most spawn in species.
