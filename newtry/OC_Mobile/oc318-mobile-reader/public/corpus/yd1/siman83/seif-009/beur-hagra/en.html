A kosher fish that was found. The Mishnah's wording is "that swallowed," whereas the Mechaber says "that was found," meaning that even if we did not see it swallow, the ruling follows Rav Ashi there.
Whether, etc., whether, etc. The Gemara there, according to Rav Sheshet and Rav Pappa, and all the more so according to Rav Ashi.
Whether it emerged, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. "Most," etc.; and there is no proof, etc.
