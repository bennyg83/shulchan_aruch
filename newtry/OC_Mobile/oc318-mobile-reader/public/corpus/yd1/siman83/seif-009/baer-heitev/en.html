In the innards. Meaning: even though we did not see that it swallowed.
Pure. Shach wrote: and some apply this to water that an animal drinks, for we said above siman 81 that it is forbidden because there it is different since the body's moisture mixes in; however for what is found in the womb, Shach holds it is also permitted; Taz forbids according to Rashi; we are not concerned for that view because most that spawn spawn in their species, therefore permitted in the womb — we say fish enters, fish exits.
