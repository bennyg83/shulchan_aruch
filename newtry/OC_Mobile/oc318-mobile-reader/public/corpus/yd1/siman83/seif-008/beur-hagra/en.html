(Addendum) Fish eggs, etc. This follows the approach of Ri and the other authorities, that the identifying signs of fish are likewise not of Torah authority, according to the Gemara's answer in Chullin there and the similar implication of the Yerushalmi cited by Tosafot there, s.v. "Rava said," and in Chullin there, s.v. "Signs," etc. Raavad, however, explains it in accordance with Rabbenu Tam, and Rabbenu Chananel writes likewise. Rashba writes that this also appears from Rashi in Chullin there, s.v. "This is the correct reading," where he says, "these signs of eggs," etc., meaning specifically bird eggs. Rashba writes that Rif holds that fish eggs require no identifying sign at all, because they are certainly kosher, according to the answer there, "Remove embryos from here." Therefore, in both Chullin and Avodah Zarah, Rif wrote only the signs of fish entrails.
The same applies to the entrails. This is the view of all the authorities except Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch.
