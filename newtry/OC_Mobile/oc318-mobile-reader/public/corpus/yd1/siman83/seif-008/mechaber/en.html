Fish eggs — if both their heads are rounded (meaning round and thick like pitchers), or pointed — impure (and the same law applies to innards) (Tur) — one rounded and one pointed: one asks the Israelite seller; if he said I salted them and removed them from a pure fish, one eats based on his word; and if he said they are pure, he is not believed unless it was a person established in kashrut. And now the custom has spread to buy fish eggs unconditionally, whether whole or crushed, even from a non-Jew, provided they are red; but the black ones one does not eat at all.
