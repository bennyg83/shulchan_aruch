(siman 83 Taz s.k. 5) That this is considered b'dieved, as we find regarding bread of non-Jews that we eat and are not concerned for their vessels because it is b'dieved — it is not similar at all, for there it is permitted d'oraisa from the Torah, and also rabbinically permitted b'dieved because it imparts adverse taste in ordinary vessels that are not used the same day, as below siman 122; therefore bread of non-Jews is b'dieved, but here that we say the fish is impure, what difference is there b'dieved or l'chatchila — it is a doubt of a d'oraisa prohibition; rather the correct view is as written in Shach s.k. 6.
