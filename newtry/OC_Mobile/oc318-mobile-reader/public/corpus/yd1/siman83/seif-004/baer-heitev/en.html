Match. Shach wrote: it appears that if they only fit together well it is permitted and one need not see from the cuts that all is one fish; and so in Perishah that this inspection may be relied upon even nowadays; and see above siman 101 seif 9.
Together. Shach explained: nevertheless the kosher pieces were not forbidden because brine of fish is rabbinic and we say that after salting they mixed them together; nevertheless if all are salted together the rest are forbidden and we do not say that since these with scales are permitted, presumably the rest are also of this species — rather it is a d'oraisa doubt to be stringent, and the brine is forbidden in any case; see below siman 114 seif 9.
And spine. Explanation: two things are required.
