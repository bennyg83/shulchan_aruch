If one found pieces of fish and one of them has scales — if the pieces match one another, all are permitted, for we presume they came from one fish; and if they do not match, those in which scales were found are permitted and the rest are forbidden, even if all are salted together. {Rama: And if a fish is found there that has a broad head and spine, it is permitted to eat it, for certainly it had scales; but if the fish is whole and we do not see scales on it, one may not rely on the head and spine.}
