Peleti on fish signs, salted fish, head and spine, kulyatis, innards, eggs, and digestion — see Hebrew for full discussion; main conclusions: fish signs according to Rambam are rabbinic safek; whole fish without visible scales forbidden; salted pieces with scales permitted; head and spine when salted and broad head permitted; brine rabbinic; kulyatis establishes brine per Rambam; innards need "I salted them" per Rambam unlike other poskim; non-Jew not believed for "I salted them"; red eggs permitted, black forbidden; pure fish in impure fish permitted even in womb per majority; impure in pure forbidden if whole — not digested.
