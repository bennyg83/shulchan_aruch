Kulyatis. Shach wrote: according to Rashi and other commentators kulyatis is a species of small fish that grows by itself in brine of pure fish; and if there is brine of impure fish, kulyatis does not grow in it; Rambam and other poskim explain kulyatis is a pure fish — since there is one small pure fish in the brine we presume the brine pure; from Mechaber's words it appears he follows Rambam primarily; and nowadays it is plain we cannot rely on Rashi, for we do not recognize that species.
Open. The reason: a person is apt to take from this and from that, therefore it is considered mixed.
Forbidden. The reason is in Shach: since their bodies are mixed in their brine we decreed that if we permit their brine they will eat their bodies; but pieces recognizable as pure are permitted, for we do not forbid them because they absorbed from brine — by law it is permitted — rather we forbade by decree. And he further wrote: although Mechaber brought this view as "some say," it is compelled in the Gemara.
