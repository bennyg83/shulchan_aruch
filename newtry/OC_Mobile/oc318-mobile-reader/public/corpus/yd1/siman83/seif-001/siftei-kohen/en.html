And one scale, etc. Meaning: in any place it suffices with one scale, and we are not concerned that perhaps with pure fish it became mixed and a scale fell from them and attached to this one, since it is firmly attached and appears part of its body; and the Yesh Omrim in the hagahah: since it does not stand in the special place we are not concerned it came from pure fish; all this is Ran and Maggid Mishneh; and even in the first reasoning the Maggid Mishneh raised that certainly any unknown species where there is concern scales attached — forbidden from doubt and we follow Torah stringency; nevertheless even in other places it will be recognizable if they are from its body — end of his words.
