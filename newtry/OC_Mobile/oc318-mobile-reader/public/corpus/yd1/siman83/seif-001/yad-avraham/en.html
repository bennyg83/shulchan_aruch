(siman 83 seif 5 hagahah) Brine of impure fish, etc. — perhaps they were not salted together. In Maggid Yehoshua general rule 20 s.k. 4 he expanded to prove that brine of fish is rabbinic even through pickling for twenty-four hours; and not like Taz and Shach there; and so too Nachal Eshkol siman 26; investigate.
