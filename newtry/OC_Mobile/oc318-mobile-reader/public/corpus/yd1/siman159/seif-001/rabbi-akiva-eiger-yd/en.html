(Siman 159 Shach s.k. 3) because of lo tashich. According to Ramban it is permitted to lend to him for he is not called "your brother" since they would lower him; but the mumar is warned not to take interest from him. If so, a lender who gives interest to a mumar transgresses only lifnei iver. But according to Tur's view the permission to lend is because they were not commanded to sustain him with this loan — the lender also transgresses a negative commandment; and so is practical law regarding lending at interest to an informer — for Ramban it is forbidden for he is called "your brother" (for what they would lower him is only because he is a rodef); and according to Tur one can say it is permitted for nevertheless we do not read in him "and your brother shall live with you"; and see responsum Chacham Tzvi (siman 63) and Shevut Yaakov (part 2 seif 73).
