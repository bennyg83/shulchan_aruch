From him. Because of lo tashich and because of lifnei iver lo titen michshol.
To be stringent. The Bach writes that with a mumar out of spite who has not left the community, one should nevertheless be stricter than with a mumar who left the community, and one may not lend to him with interest. In responsa of the Geonim it is stated that if he is a mumar who abandons permitted food and eats forbidden food, then he is permitted, but with a mumar for appetite it is forbidden. Nevertheless, one should be stringent. Thus the Shach.
