To be stringent. Bach wrote — and regarding a mumar le'hach'is who has not left the category, nevertheless one should be more stringent with him than a mumar who left the category; and one may not lend to him at interest. And in responsum of Geonim it states that if he is a mumar who leaves permitted and eats forbidden — then it is permitted; but a mumar for appetite is forbidden — end quote; and nevertheless one should be stringent. Shach.
