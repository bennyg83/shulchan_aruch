From him. Because of the prohibition, 'You shall not cause interest,' and because of lifnei iver.
To be stringent. Bach writes that concerning an apostate who sins defiantly but has not formally left the Jewish people, one should be more stringent than with one who has left the community, and should not lend him at interest. A responsum of the Geonim permits lending at interest to one who deliberately rejects permitted food for forbidden food, but forbids lending to one who sins from appetite. Shach concludes that one should nevertheless be stringent.
