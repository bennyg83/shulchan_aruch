And it is forbidden to borrow from him. On account of "you shall not charge interest" and on account of "before the blind do not place a stumbling block."
And it is good to be stringent etc. And for a mumar le-hachis who has not left the community — even though he was among those whom we lower down, as above siman 158 — nevertheless one should be more stringent with him than a mumar who left the community, and one should not lend him at interest — Bach; and even though his words are not compelling. Also in responsum of the Geonim, Prague press siman 284, I found a question regarding lending at interest to apostates — the Rif's response: I heard from my teachers that if he is a mumar who abandoned what is permitted and ate what is forbidden, then it is permitted; but for a mumar le-te'avon it is forbidden — end; nevertheless one should be stringent.
