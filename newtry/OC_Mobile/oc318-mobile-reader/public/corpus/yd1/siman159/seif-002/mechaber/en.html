A meshumad — it is permitted to lend to him with interest, but it is forbidden to borrow from him with interest. {Rama: And some are stringent even regarding a meshumad — to lend to him (Mordechai and Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of Ravyah, and Rashi, and Semag, and in Maharil); and it is good to be stringent if it is possible to evade him.}
