Tosafot in Avodah Zarah 26a: since there is no mitzvah to sustain him, etc., the verse "that your brother may live with you" does not apply to him. Rosh likewise wrote this in the fifth chapter of Bava Metzia, as did Nimukei Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, Ramban, and Ran, and Hagahot Maimoniyot and Mordechai in the name of Ri.
