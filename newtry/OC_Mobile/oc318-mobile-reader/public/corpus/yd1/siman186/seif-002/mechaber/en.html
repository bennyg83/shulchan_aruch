If she does not have a fixed veset: the first 3 times require checking before tashmish, and after tashmish — he with his cloth and she with her cloth. And if she was established in those 3 times that she does not see blood because of tashmish, then she no longer needs checking at all — neither before tashmish nor after tashmish. And according to Rambam and Rosh, as long as she has no veset she needs checking forever, before tashmish and after tashmish; and Rambam requires that the husband also check himself after tashmish. {Rama: And they need not check themselves after each and every tashmish that they do in one night; rather, they wipe themselves all night with a cloth, and on the next day they need a bedikah; and if she found blood, she is tamei (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam, perek 4). If she wiped herself with a cloth and it was lost, she should not have tashmish until she checks herself, since she has no veset (there).}
