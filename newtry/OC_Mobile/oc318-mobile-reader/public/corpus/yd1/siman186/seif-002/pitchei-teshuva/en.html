They must examine beforehand. Shach rules that no examination is required and states that this is common practice. Torat HaShelamim says established lenient custom should not be changed, but otherwise one should not disregard the Mechaber and Rama. Chavot Daat explains that even lenient authorities exempt only a full internal examination; wiping and retaining the cloth for the relevant interval is still proper. If she once saw blood on a night of relations, even not immediately after relations, she must examine afterward until reestablished as not seeing from relations.
