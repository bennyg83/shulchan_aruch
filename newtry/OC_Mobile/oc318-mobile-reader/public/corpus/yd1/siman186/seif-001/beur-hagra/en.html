“A woman.” According to R.C.
And Rambam etc. who explains from there specifically for purity — meaning before relations; therefore Gemara specifies "and when she passes," etc., and does not specify beginning of mishnah "has relations with witnesses," etc.; and so from challenge there 12a "R. Eliezer bar Memel taught," etc., and does not challenge from mishnah there 14a "modest women" — for mishnah there is for her husband and so entire chapter there; but R. Ami said here even for purity — challenges properly; and from there 12a "may a woman examine herself at veset time," etc. — specifically at time of chatat and asham; and likewise question of R. Zeira in such case after relations; and from there 16b "for it is taught," etc. — also before relations; but all poskim dispute him — and so in Hagahah "reasoning," etc.
