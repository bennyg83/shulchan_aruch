And the Rambam, of blessed memory, etc. He explains that the Gemara's statement that this applies specifically to matters of purity refers to an examination before relations. Therefore the Gemara comments on the clause, 'and when she passes,' etc., rather than on the opening clause of the Mishnah, 'she has relations with examination cloths,' etc. Likewise, when the Gemara on 12a challenges from the teaching of Rabbi Eliezer bar Memel, etc., it does not challenge from the Mishnah on 14a concerning modest women, because that Mishnah addresses her relationship with her husband, as does that entire chapter. Rabbi Ami's statement, however, 'Here it applies even to matters of purity,' is properly challenged. The question on 12a, 'May a woman examine herself within the interval,' etc., refers specifically to the interval in which liability for a sin-offering or guilt-offering is possible, and Rabbi Zeira's question similarly concerns an examination after relations. The teaching on 16b, 'When was this taught,' etc., also concerns an examination before relations. All the other authorities disagree with him. This is the meaning of the gloss, 'and the reasoning,' etc.
