The laws of a woman’s checking, whether before tashmish or after tashmish; it contains 5 se’ifim. A woman who has a fixed veset does not need checking at all — neither before tashmish nor after tashmish. On the contrary, she should not check in the presence of her husband at the time of tashmish, so that his heart not knock. Rambam, of blessed memory, requires checking after tashmish: she with one cloth and he with one cloth, and to look at them lest she saw blood at the time of tashmish; and according to his view, the modest women check themselves even before tashmish. (And the first reasoning is the primary one, and so is the practice) (Rokeach and Hagahos Maimoniyos and most teachers).
