To teach. The Taz writes that after three days a teacher may study halachah with his students, because this is included in the category of one whom the public needs. The breath of schoolchildren studying Torah is more precious than the study of adults. It is also clear that a mourner may perform functions connected with the Torah reading, such as rolling the scroll, because only study itself is forbidden on account of its gladdening the heart. A gloss records that Rav Chaim was a mourner on a Monday or Thursday, Selichot were recited in his presence, the Torah was read, and he rolled the Torah scroll.
