It is forbidden to read Torah. See Chacham Tzvi responsa siman 100, who wrote that the reason is because he can learn the harsh passages in Jeremiah and the laws of mourning. Therefore, on a festival, when it is forbidden to study such matters, he may learn anything, since it is not proper to sit idle; see there. This requires study, for below in siman 399 it says that on the festival he observes private matters, and consequently he is forbidden in Torah study, as written in siman 400, since Torah study is a private matter. However, according to what I wrote there in siman 399 in the name of Dat Esh responsa, this works well. See Sha'arei Teshuvah in Orach Chayim siman 554 s.k. 1, who wrote in the name of Machazik Berakhah that on Tisha B'Av a mourner may be called to the Torah and read the haftarah, since these are harsh matters and everyone is forbidden in Torah like him; see there.
