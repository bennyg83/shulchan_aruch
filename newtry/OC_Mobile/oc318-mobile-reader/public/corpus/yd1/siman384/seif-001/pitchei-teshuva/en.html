It is forbidden to read Torah. Chacham Tzvi, siman 100, explains that a mourner is prohibited from Torah study because he can instead study the sad passages of Yirmiyahu and the laws of mourning. On a festival, when those topics may not be studied because they conflict with festival joy, he argues that the mourner may study any Torah rather than sit idle. This requires study, because siman 399 says private mourning practices continue on a festival, and siman 400 treats Torah study as a private restriction. According to the approach cited there from Responsa Dat Esh, however, the matter is resolved. See Sha'arei Teshuvah, Orach Chayyim siman 554, seif katan 1, in the name of Machazik Berachah, that on Tisha B'Av one may receive an aliyah and read the haftarah because all are then studying sad passages and the mourner is no different.
