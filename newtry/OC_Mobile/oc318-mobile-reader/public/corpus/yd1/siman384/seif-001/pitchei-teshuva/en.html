It is forbidden to read Torah. See Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 100, who writes that the reason is that a mourner can study distressing passages in Yirmiyahu and the laws of mourning. Therefore, on a festival, when those subjects are themselves inappropriate to study, he may study other Torah, because it is not proper for him to sit idle. This requires study, because below in siman 399 it is stated that private mourning practices remain in force on a festival, and Torah study is itself considered a private matter, as stated in siman 400. However, according to what I cite there in siman 399 from Responsa Dat Esh, the point is resolved. See Shaarei Teshuvah to Orach Chaim 554, seif katan 1, in the name of Machazik Berachah, that on Tisha B'Av a mourner may receive an aliyah and read the Haftarah, since all are prohibited in Torah study and the readings themselves concern distressing matters; see there.
