To pray. See Responsa Zichron Yitzchak, siman 6, who writes that during all seven days a mourner should not recite Pitum HaKetoret or the order of korbanot described in Orach Chaim siman 1. He derives this from the law of Tisha B'Av in siman 559:4. See Magen Avraham, siman 50, seif katan 1. Chomot Yerushalayim, siman 14, cites the Tevuot Shor as likewise ruling that throughout the seven days a mourner should not recite the korbanot passages, on the ground that a mourner does not send his offerings. The author questions this and inclines to permit it, even during aninut after burial where the status is more severe, so long as he does not recite the accompanying "May it be Your will" prayer. This itself requires study, because they did not mention the reason given by Zichron Yitzchak.
