It is forbidden for him, etc. The Bach wrote: this is surprising, for in siman 400 regarding Shabbat the Rav wrote that if there is no other kohen there besides him, it is permitted to call him, while here he wrote nothing. This is no difficulty. Here the reason for the prohibition, that a mourner is forbidden in words of Torah, is what he comes to teach, and he is not discussing the law of mourning on Shabbat. But there, where the entire siman deals with the prohibition of mourning on Shabbat, he says that he may go up because otherwise it would be public mourning, and public mourning is not practiced on Shabbat. So it is explicitly implied there; see there.
