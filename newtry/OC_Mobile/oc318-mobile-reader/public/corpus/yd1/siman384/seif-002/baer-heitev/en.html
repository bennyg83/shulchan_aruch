Kohen. The Bach wrote: this is astonishing, for in siman 400 regarding Shabbat the Rav wrote that if there is no other kohen there, he may be called, while here he wrote nothing. The Shach wrote that this is not difficult at all. Here he comes to teach the reason for the prohibition, that a mourner is forbidden in Torah study, and he is not discussing the law of mourning on Shabbat. There, however, where the whole siman deals with the prohibition of mourning on Shabbat, he says he may be called up, for otherwise it would be public mourning, and public mourning does not apply on Shabbat. This is explicit there; see there. End quote.
