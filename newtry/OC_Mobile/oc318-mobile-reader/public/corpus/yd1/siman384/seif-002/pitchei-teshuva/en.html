To pray. Zichron Yitzchak 6 writes that throughout the seven days a mourner should not recite Pitum HaKetoret or the order of offerings listed in Orach Chaim 1, based on the law of Tisha BeAv in siman 559:4. See Magen Avraham 50:1. Chomot Yerushalayim 14 cites Tevuot Shor that a mourner should not recite the offerings because a mourner does not send sacrifices. He questions this and inclines to permit it, even for an onen after burial, provided the accompanying prayer of supplication is omitted. It requires study why he did not cite Zichron Yitzchak's reason.
