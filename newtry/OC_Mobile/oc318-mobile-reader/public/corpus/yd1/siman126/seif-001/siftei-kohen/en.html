One who pours, etc., until what is in the lower vessel. This is Rambam's language chapter 17 of Forbidden Foods law 11; except the Mechaber inserted in his words what is forbidden in benefit and came to exclude wine that is forbidden only for drinking, to which nitzok does not apply, as below seif 5; and see there siman 132 s.k. 18 and s.k. 24 and siman 134 s.k. 3.
