The law of wine that became mixed with yayin nesech, and the law of nitzok as a connection. In it are 7 seifim.
One who pours wine into a utensil that contains the wine of a non-Jew that is forbidden in benefit — all the wine in the upper utensil becomes forbidden, for the nitzok column connects the wine in the upper utensil with the wine in the lower utensil, and we view it as though the wine of issur is mixed with the wine of heter. But if he poured and then cut off the flow, such that before it touched the issur the stream descending from the heter stopped, it is permitted.
