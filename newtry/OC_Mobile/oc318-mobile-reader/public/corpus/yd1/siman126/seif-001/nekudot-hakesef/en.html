(Siman 126 in Taz s.k. 1) And further that his words here are astonishing, etc. According to Rabbenu Meir that he said certainly we consider great loss; and if so it is not stam yeinam at all; for if you say we consider minor loss and in such a case we establish it is stam yeinam — if so immediately when you are stringent to say it is stam yeinam, how will you sell it afterward to a gentile; for many authorities forbade selling stam yeinam in our time to a gentile; and some say for other matters — where one may sell to a gentile with minor loss it is not considered great loss because there is no prohibition in selling for everyone; but here it is forbidden to sell according to some poskim; rather certainly it is not applicable here to say it is minor loss but it is like great loss; and it is plain that where there is great loss it is permitted even for drinking; and accordingly the latter reason also applies for us — investigate.
