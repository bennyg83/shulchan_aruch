A winepress from which the wine descends into the pit, and a non-Jew touched the descending stream: if the grape pits and the skins and the grapes that are in the winepress have 60 against the stream, what is in the winepress does not become forbidden. {Rama: For we say, “remove its own kind as though it is not [there], and what is not its kind is greater than it and nullifies it.” Wine lees that were not made into temed, and 12 months have not passed over them, are called min b’mino with wine (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba).}
