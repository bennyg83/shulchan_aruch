Sixty against it. In some books it is written here hagahah — see there siman 134 seif 5; and likewise in Shulchan Aruch printed in Amsterdam; and meaning that there he ruled that the measure of nullification is that there should be six parts against it; and the rulings contradict each other; and likewise Bach challenged in his last pamphlet and ruled that six parts suffice; and he would have emended here to six; and likewise in Shulchan Aruch printed in Ancona; but in Taz he wrote explicitly sixty twice over — requires study.
