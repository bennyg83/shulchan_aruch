To mortar. Shach wrote; but on the ground — they too sprinkle on the ground before their idolatry — Rashba; and Levush wrote: if one pours wine on the ground and a gentile touched that splash, the splash is forbidden from the touch and what is inside is forbidden through nitzok — end of his words.
Outside. Levush wrote that even if the gentile who pours it touched it, everything is permitted, for since he himself pours it to loss, his intent is nullified and there is no intent of libation at all.
