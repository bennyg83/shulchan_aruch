One who pours wine into a utensil of a non-Jew that was not rinsed, and there is not in it liquid that is “tofeach”: that which he poured into it is forbidden for drinking, but that which he poured from it is permitted even for drinking. {Rama: And if there is in it enough “tofeach al menat l'hatfiach”, it is forbidden because of nitzok (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham and Semag, and Tosafot and the Rosh, last perek of Avodah Zarah). However, it does not forbid more than if it were actually mixed into it; and if it is their stam yeinam, it is nullified in 60, as will be explained in siman 134.}
