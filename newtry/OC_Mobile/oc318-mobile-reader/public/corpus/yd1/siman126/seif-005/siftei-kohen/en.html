Forbidden for drinking. As below siman 137.
Permitted even for drinking. For they did not say nitzok is connection except one who pours into stam yeinam forbidden in benefit — Rashba, and likewise Rabbenu Yerucham; and it appears that even though all their touch and stam yeinam in our time is permitted in benefit, as above siman 123, nevertheless we say here nitzok is connection since by law it was forbidden in benefit — and it is plain.
Enough to rub. And if it is a vessel one keeps permanently, even a revi'it alone is connection — Rabbenu Yerucham, and Beit Yosef and Darkhei Moshe bring it; and see there siman 123 minor seif 32 and below siman 157 s.k. 4; and see Maharshal in his responsum siman 76.
