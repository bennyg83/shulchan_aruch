For drinking. For they said nitzok is a connection only when pouring into stam yeinam forbidden in benefit; and it appears that even though all their touch and stam yeinam nowadays is permitted in benefit, as above siman 123, nevertheless we say here that nitzok is a connection since by law it was forbidden in benefit — and it is plain. Shach.
To rub. Rabbenu Yerucham wrote: and if it is a vessel one keeps permanently, even a revi'it alone is a connection; and see there siman 123 seif 17 and siman 137 seif 1 [A Jew and gentile sat to drink and the gentile drank and the Jew did not rinse the cup and there was a revi'it with intent to rub and the Jew returned and mixed from the flask into the cup — everything is forbidden even what is in the flask, for we establish nitzok is a connection — Radbaz siman 200 — until here one must be careful to wash the cup well after the gentile drank].
