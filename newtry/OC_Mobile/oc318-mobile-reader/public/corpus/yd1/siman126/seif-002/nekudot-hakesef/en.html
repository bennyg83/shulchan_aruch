(Hagahah in Shulchan Aruch seif 5) One who pours wine, etc. And these are his words Beit Yosef; and it appears to me that even if there is in it a revi'it of liquid it forbids because of nitzok, even though there is not in it enough to rub; for Rav Huna said: nitzok, katfres, and a revi'it of liquid are connection for stam yeinam; and Rashi explained: a revi'it of liquid without a revi'it with intent to rub — if he rubbed them with his hand, etc.; but Rabbenu Yerucham wrote that a revi'it with intent to rub is required to forbid because of nitzok; and likewise it appears from Semag's words, as written in siman 123 that according to his view it is not connection for stam yeinam until there is a revi'it with intent to rub; and likewise Tosafot, etc.; and accordingly the Mechaber wrote in Shulchan Aruch: and there is not in it a revi'it of liquid — he poured into it forbidden for drinking, and he poured from it permitted even for drinking; and he wrote "and there is not in it a revi'it of liquid" — meaning that if there is a revi'it it is forbidden what is in the vessel he poured from because of nitzok according to Rashba's reasoning above; and in siman 123 Beit Yosef wrote on Tur who wrote regarding if he emptied grape seeds, etc., if there is in them a revi'it with intent to rub, etc.; Beit Yosef wrote: and what he wrote if there is in them a revi'it with intent to rub — likewise Semag; and at first glance it appears plain that if there is not in them a revi'it with intent to rub, what if he touched them; rather in the chapter of the hired worker Rav Huna said: nitzok, katfres, and a revi'it of liquid are connection for stam yeinam; and Rashi explained: a revi'it of liquid without intent to rub; and it appears to me that one must say Semag holds like Rabbenu Tam who ruled nitzok is not connection and the halachah is not like Rav Huna; but according to the one who ruled nitzok is connection — likewise a revi'it of liquid is connection even though there is not in it enough to rub; or alternatively Semag explains that the revi'it of liquid of Rav Huna is a revi'it with intent to rub — end of his words Beit Yosef; and accordingly he wrote there in seif 17 if there is in them a revi'it with intent to rub, etc. (This is for the second answer; for if for the first answer the halachah is not like Rav Huna and they hold nitzok is not connection — for us who establish nitzok is connection — no need for enough to rub; rather perforce for the second answer Rav Huna also deals with a revi'it with intent to rub) and if so his words contradict each other, for it appears here that a revi'it alone is nitzok; and perforce one must explain Rav Huna also deals with a revi'it alone, as written above — requires study.
