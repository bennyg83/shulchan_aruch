One should be stringent. All the later authorities object to this custom and call it mistaken. The Shach writes in the name of the Raavan that the practice not to eat with her until she completes the seven clean days is proper and commendable, for at that stage there is no longer a habituation to sin, but rather a habituation to mitzvah. Since she is then able to immerse, she will not abandon what is permitted and choose what is forbidden.
