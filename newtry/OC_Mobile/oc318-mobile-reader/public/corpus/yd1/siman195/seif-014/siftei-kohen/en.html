And some say, etc. Darkei Moshe's language: whether during her niddah days or during her whiteness days — and the beginning of this siman has already been explained; however I found a gloss in Mordechai in the name of Ravyah — and his language: after the whiteness days there is no habit of transgression, and it is good to eat with her so that she will want to immerse when she is able — end quote; and regarding this I saw leniencies during whiteness days; and it does not seem to me one should rely on this; and Ravyah is alone in this — end of Darkei Moshe. And Bach wrote that even according to this those who are lenient err, for he only permitted on the 7th day after the whiteness days so she will want to immerse and also there is no habit of transgression for there is no concern she will be violated on the 7th day since she immerses in the evening; and also this is a weak reasoning, and one does not listen to an individual to permit a prohibition publicized in all works; therefore one should preach publicly that those who eat together from one bowl during whiteness days are transgressing. And I found in a gloss: men who eat with their wives during whiteness days without the household members knowing — it is a disgrace and they are transgressing rabbinical words; and all communities accepted and made a ban on this — R. Chiya — end quote; and this gloss is in Maharshal's glosses, brought by Derishah. And Ra'avan wrote at the end of siman 335 — there are those accustomed not to eat until the completion of seven clean days — and it is proper and becoming, for then there is no longer a habit of transgression but rather a habit of mitzvah, for since he can immerse he would not abandon the permitted and eat the forbidden — see there where he elaborated.
