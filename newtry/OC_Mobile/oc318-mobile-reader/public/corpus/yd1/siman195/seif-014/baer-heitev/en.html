To be lenient. The Bach wrote that they have no one on whom to rely for leniency, and it is proper to teach publicly that it is forbidden. The Taz wrote: it further appears to me that one should be more stringent during the white days, for if we permit him some greater leniency there is concern that he will come to habitual intimacy, since he sees that she is not so tamei. I also found that Torat HaBayit wrote this reasoning in the name of the Ra'avad; end quote.
