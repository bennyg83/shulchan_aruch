All these distances must be kept, whether during the days of her niddah or during the days of her whitening, which are all the days of her counting; and there is no distinction in all these between one who sees actual blood and one who finds a ketem. {Rama: And some say that we need not be stringent in the days of her whitening regarding the prohibition of eating with her from one bowl (a gloss in Mordechai in the name of Raaviyah); and so is the practice to be lenient in this, and one should be stringent.}
