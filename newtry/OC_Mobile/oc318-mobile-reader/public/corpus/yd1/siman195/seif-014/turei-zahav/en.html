And so the practice is to be lenient. My father-in-law, of blessed memory, wrote that they have no authority upon whom to rely, and it is proper to teach publicly that this is forbidden. So too wrote the Derishah in the name of the Maharshal. It further appears to me that one should be even more stringent during the days when she wears white, for if we permit him some additional leniency, there is greater concern that they will come to habitual intimacy, since he sees that she is not so impure. I also found that the Toras HaBayis wrote this reasoning in the name of the Ra'avad.
