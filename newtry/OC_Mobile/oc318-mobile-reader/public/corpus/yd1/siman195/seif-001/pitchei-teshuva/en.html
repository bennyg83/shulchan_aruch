And he should not joke. Birkei Yosef forbids smelling his niddah wife's perfumes, even though the Torah attests that Israel is 'fenced among roses.' Precisely for that reason, a further fence is required regarding roses. Taz likewise forbids smelling her perfumes even after she has removed them and they rest on the table. Chamudei Daniel adds that even where some leniency exists before travel near her expected time, conduct that creates intimacy with a niddah remains forbidden.
