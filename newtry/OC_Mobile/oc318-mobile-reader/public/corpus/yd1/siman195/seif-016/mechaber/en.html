If a woman is ill and she is a niddah, it is forbidden for her husband to touch her in order to attend to her, for example, to lift her, to lay her down, or to support her. (And some say that if she has no one to attend to her, it is permitted in every case (Hagahos Sha”d and Hagahos Mordechai, perek 1 of Shabbos, in the name of R”m); and so is the practice if she needs it greatly.)
