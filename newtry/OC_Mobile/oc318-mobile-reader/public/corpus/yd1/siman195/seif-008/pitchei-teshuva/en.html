On a bed. See Chokhmat Adam kelal 102 — he wrote likewise regarding pillows designated for her — forbidden — see there. And if so must say all the more regarding those designated for him — she is forbidden [to lie on them] as Taz wrote s.k. 6; and per this what Taz wrote s.k. 7 — niddah woman may lie on her husband's cushions — deals when not designated for him; or also deals not in his presence — for although he is on her bed forbidden also not in her presence — nevertheless she on his bed — it appears permitted not in his presence; and so appears from Taz's words s.k. 6.
