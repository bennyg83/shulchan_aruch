Throwing. See responsum at end of Minchat Yaakov — he wrote forbidden for niddah woman to remove feather from her husband by blowing; and brings proof from Amemar who took wing from man — he said "I disqualify you from judgment" — see there; and see responsum Har HaKarmel YD siman 18 who rejected this and inclined to permit — see there; also Hagahot Kaftor VaFerach wrote not to be stringent in this; he wrote further there he saw practiced that they throw something upward and not toward his wife — intending to throw to her hand and she extends her hand and receives it — and there is room to be lenient — see there [but in Siftei Tzedek he wrote that although room to be lenient from essence of law — nevertheless do not permit; and whoever is stringent in such matters — Heaven help him — see there].
