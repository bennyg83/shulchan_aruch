He shall not drink, etc. It implies even though they refilled it — such as she drank half and it was then refilled — it is forbidden, for he is still drinking from her leavings; but if she drank the entire cup and it was refilled, it appears it is permitted; and so implied by Bach; and even though Beit Yosef wrote we are accustomed to immediately rinse the cup between her drinking and his — and it is a proper custom — end quote, we are not accustomed to this.
