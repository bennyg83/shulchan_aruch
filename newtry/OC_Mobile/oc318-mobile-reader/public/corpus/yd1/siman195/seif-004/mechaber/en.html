He should not drink from the leftovers of a cup from which she drank. {Rama: Unless another person separates between them (Tur in the name of Sefer Mitzvos Gadol); or if he poured from this cup into another cup, even if it was returned to the first cup (Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of Ra’ahm, and Rokeach siman 318, and Mordechai and Agudah, perek 1 of Shabbos). And if she drank and he does not know and wants to drink from her cup, she does not need to tell him not to drink (there). And she is permitted to drink from a cup from which he drank (also there). And if she drank from a cup and went away, some say that it is permitted for him to drink what remains, for since she already went away, there is no affection here (in the aforementioned booklet).}
