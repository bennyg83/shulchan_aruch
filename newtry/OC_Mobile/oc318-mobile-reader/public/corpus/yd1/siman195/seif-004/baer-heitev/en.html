From leftovers. It means that even if they returned and refilled it — for example she drank half and refilled it — it is forbidden, for nevertheless he drinks from her leftover; but if she drank the entire cup and refilled it, it appears it is permitted; and although Beit Yosef wrote they should rinse the cup between his drinking and her drinking — we do not practice so — end quote Shach (and one who is stringent to rinse — Heaven help him).
