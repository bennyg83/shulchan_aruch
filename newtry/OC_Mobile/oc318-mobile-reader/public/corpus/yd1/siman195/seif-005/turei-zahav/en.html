He shall not sit on bed, etc. It implies even mere sitting without lying — for in sitting too there is sinful thought; and from sinful thought he will come to habitual transgression; so Beit Yosef; and in some books it is in Tur here "he shall not lie" and Maharach z"l wrote scribal error in text of "he shall not sit" — since above end siman 193 Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote "he shall not sleep" and it does not appear there teaches regarding canopy — even sleep there permitted; but this certainly also sitting applies — sinful thought — for Mordechai wrote and Rama brings nearby — forbidden to sit on long bench, etc.; and all the more sitting on her bed where there is more sinful thought; and it appears all the more she shall not sleep on his bed — for more sinful thought in lying and standing; but mere sitting is permitted for her on his bed — for she is not accustomed to him so — so it appears to me. And touch when touching sheet soiled with blood — no prohibition even though some are careful about this — and it is confusion.
