He should not sit on the bed designated for her, even not in her presence. {Rama: And it is forbidden to sit on a long bench that wobbles and is not attached to the wall when his wife, a niddah, is sitting on it (Mordechai, perek 1 of Shabbos, in the name of Tzafnas Pa’aneach in the name of Rashi). And there are those who permit when another person separates and sits between them (Agudah, perek HaTinokes, and Terumas HaDeshen, siman 251). And likewise he should not travel with his wife in one wagon or in one ship if he is going only for recreation, such as to gardens and orchards and the like; but if he is going from city to city for his needs, it is permitted even though he and his wife are alone, provided they sit in a manner that they will not touch one another (all this in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 251).}
