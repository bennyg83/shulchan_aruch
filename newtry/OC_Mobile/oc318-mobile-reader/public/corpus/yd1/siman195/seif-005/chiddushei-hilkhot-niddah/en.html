All labors, etc. unless she makes some distinguishing sign, etc. One must analyze against Shulchan Aruch's concealment — for Rashi explains "on exchange" meaning in days of counting; and Tosafot explained certainly no difference between niddah days and counting days — only deals in their days when they count between niddah days and counting days; if so in our time when there are not two immersions we should forbid even by making distinguishing sign; and it appears Beit Yosef holds Rashi explains "in counting days" with another reason — namely from Rambam's words in Laws of Blessings chapter 6 halachah 18 regarding they permitted from cloth for terumah eaters; and per what Kessef Mishneh wrote there it appears to his view like Shmuel and Rav — Shmuel's sister's children in Israel would eat chullin in purity; therefore Shmuel said to Rav "act according to law to eat from cloth"; if so difficult for Rashi — how did Shmuel eat from what his niddah wife passed to him when he ate in purity? On this Rashi explained "in counting days" — meaning she already immersed from her niddah only because of stringency of R. or R. Zeira she needs another seven days; and this is regarding prohibition of relations — they were stringent on daughters of Israel for themselves; but regarding purity law is as before — she was already purified and there was no concern of impurity in these eaters at all; and I say Rashi's words are clear and Shulchan Aruch's words are correct — regarding prohibition of affectionate closeness plainly no distinction even during her niddah days it is permitted by distinguishing sign.
