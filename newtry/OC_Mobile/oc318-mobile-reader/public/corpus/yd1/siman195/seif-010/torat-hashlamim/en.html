Except for mixing his cup. See the Bach and the Shach, who write that placing a bowl on the table is likewise forbidden during her days of niddah. This applies specifically to a bowl designated solely for her husband, but not when other members of the household eat from it. So wrote the Be'er Sheva, page 112b. The Taz writes that since the Sages mentioned only mixing the cup and did not also forbid preparing his food, they evidently understood that food preparation does not create affectionate closeness and is merely an act of service.
