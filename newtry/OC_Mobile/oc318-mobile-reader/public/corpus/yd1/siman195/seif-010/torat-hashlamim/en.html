And see Bach and Shach who wrote likewise placing bowl on table is forbidden during her niddah days — specifically bowl designated for her husband alone; but not when household members eat from it; and so Be'er Sheva page 112b; and Taz wrote — since we say only "except mixing the cup" and did not also say "she prepares food" — certainly sages knew there is no closeness of mind here — only act of servitude.
