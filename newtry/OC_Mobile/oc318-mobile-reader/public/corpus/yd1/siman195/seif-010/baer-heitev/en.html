In his presence. Bach wrote that although mixing not in his presence — if she places it on the table in his presence it is forbidden; and all the more when he knows she mixed the cup — for since she placed it on the table in his presence it is considered as mixing in his presence as well and is forbidden — end quote; and Shach wrote his words are not compelling; also from words of Rosh beginning Ketubot it implies that not in his presence there is no prohibition at all; also what he forbids her to bring the bowl with food onto the table does not appear correct — for even regarding mixing the cup there is prohibition only in the cup designated for her husband alone where there is affection; but to place the bowl on the table from which all household members eat — there is no concern even though her husband also eats with the household from that bowl — for there is no affection here — and so we rule; but to bring a bowl designated for her husband alone is forbidden — for even by passing alone Semag forbids when there is no distinguishing sign; and per this it appears he is stringent also regarding other beverages — end quote.
