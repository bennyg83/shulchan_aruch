From mixing the cup, etc. Bach wrote in name of Darshot Maharash of Austria — not according to law householders do — they allow their wives to carry bowls and the like onto the table immediately as with mixing the cup — end quote; however per Hagahot Maimoniyot in name of Ra'avad — mixing without passing or passing without mixing is permitted even without distinguishing sign — likewise regarding bowl where there is only passing it is permitted; and so Rashba wrote (and Beit Yosef per his view) — mixing wine in water specifically is forbidden but other beverages or mixing from vessel as we do — permitted; likewise regarding bowl — no concern; and further it appears even if you say placing bowl on table is like mixing cup — nevertheless even regarding mixing cup prohibition is only in cup designated for her husband alone where there is affection; but to bring bowl onto table from which all household eat — no concern even though her husband also eats with household from that bowl — for no affection here — and so we rule; but to bring bowl designated for her husband alone — forbidden; and as proven from Tanna of house of Eliyahu; and from what Semak wrote — even by passing alone when no distinguishing sign — forbidden regarding food and drink — end quote; and per this it appears he is stringent also regarding other beverages.
In his presence. Bach wrote that although mixing not in his presence — if she places on table in his presence it is forbidden; and even Hagahot Maimoniyot who specifically when both mixing and passing exist forbids — agrees to this; and all the more when he knows she mixed the cup — for since she placed on table in his presence it is considered mixing in his presence as well and is forbidden — end quote; and his words are not compelling; also from Rosh beginning Ketubot it is proven not in his presence there is no prohibition at all — see there.
