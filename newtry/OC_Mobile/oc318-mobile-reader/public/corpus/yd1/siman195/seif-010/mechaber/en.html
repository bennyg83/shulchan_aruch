All labors that a wife does for her husband, a niddah does for him, except for pouring the cup: it is forbidden for her to pour the cup in his presence (Beit Yosef, and so it implies from Mordechai, perek 1 of Shevuos, and so it implies from the words of the poskim), and to place it before him on the table, unless she makes some distinguishing sign — for example, she places it on the table with her left hand, or she places it on the pillow or on the cushion even with her right hand.
