Fitting for her, etc. — so they remember always she is niddah — possibly specifically on weekdays; but Shabbat clothes differ from weekday clothes — and will she have special Shabbat clothes for niddah? With this one can resolve why Tur and Mechaber cited the reason "so they remember"; and in Ketubot page 65b the reason is stated — so she not be repulsive to her husband; and Rashi explained — if in days of purity she wears clothes of niddah days — Maharshal already left this in doubt; and I say — certainly if it were custom in Israel to always wear whether niddah days or purity days one garment — no concern of repulsiveness at all; but because they should remember — they practiced wearing special clothes; only there regarding one who feeds his wife through a third party who does not eat with him except from Shabbat to Shabbat — and we interpret what is "she eats" — relations even for donkey-driver and ship-captain as Tosafot wrote; if so on Shabbat she does not wear niddah clothes so there is distinguishing sign; and on weekdays in any case he does not come to her — for feeding through third party — not applicable that they remember; only since already custom to have special clothes for niddah days — if she changes on this she will be repulsive to husband; and I say — or one can say even though also on Shabbat she wears niddah clothes — nevertheless above reasoning is certainly true — were it not for custom to change garments — repulsiveness not applicable; only that she should wear is mere warning; but we have no power to compel husband on this; if so difficult why there they compel him to leave her worn-out clothes to cover in niddah days; on this Gemara says — since custom is so — there is repulsiveness here; and since she has repulsiveness — they compel husband; also she can compel him to give her worn-out clothes so she not be repulsive; and she treats garment like ornaments; and I say — requires study.
