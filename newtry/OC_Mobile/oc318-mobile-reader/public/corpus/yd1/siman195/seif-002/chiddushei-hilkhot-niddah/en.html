He shall not eat. Some say it is forbidden for him to eat from leftovers of her food as it is forbidden to drink, etc.; and Shach challenged in s.k. 8 — for all the more drinking — for drinking is learned from eating — see there; and one can distinguish — certainly eating has affection like drinking; only regarding drinking mutually there is affection and here there is no recognizable sign — for at one time impossible to drink together; but not so regarding eating from a bowl — when she is pure both eat together from one bowl; if so during her niddah when she eats alone and leaves him a portion in the bowl — behold there is recognizable sign — for otherwise they would eat together at once; and there is room to permit these eating leftovers; and he informs in name of Magen Avraham that nevertheless it is forbidden; and requires study; afterward he found so in Sefer Yad Eliyahu regarding drinking as well — and it pleased me.
