He shall not touch her. In responsum Noda Biyhudah second YD siman 122 — they asked regarding a man and woman living in a village where there is no Jew or Jewess besides the couple alone — whether her husband is permitted to stand over her when she immerses to see that she is entirely under water; and whether he may support her with his hands to push her under water; and he wrote that from reasoning it appears — since touch is forbidden (per Shach s.k. 20) and likewise gazing at covered places lest he come to habitual transgression at that moment — we are not concerned — for we do not forsake permitted matter that immediately at that moment she is permitted upon rising from water; therefore if impossible otherwise there is room to permit — see there. And see responsum Shemesh Tzedakah YD siman 67 — he wrote one whose wife died rachmana litzlan while she was niddah is permitted to touch her — see there.
