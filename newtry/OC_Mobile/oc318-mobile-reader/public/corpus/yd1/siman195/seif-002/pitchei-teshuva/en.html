To look. See the discussion whether a husband may watch his wife immerse to ensure that her entire body is under water, or support her with his hands. Since touching and looking at covered areas are ordinarily forbidden, this is permitted only where no other practical solution exists, because she becomes permitted immediately upon emerging. Shemesh Tzedakah also permits touching one's deceased wife while she is a niddah.
Throwing. Some forbid a niddah from blowing a feather off her husband, while Har HaCarmel and Kreti U'Pleiti permit. Throwing an object upward rather than directly toward her hand also has grounds for leniency, though Sidrei Taharah says one should not permit it in practice and praises stringency.
