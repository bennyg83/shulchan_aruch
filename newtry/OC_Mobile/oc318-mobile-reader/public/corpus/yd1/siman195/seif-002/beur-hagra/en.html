He should not touch, etc. First chapter of Shabbos, 13b: Heaven forbid, etc.
He should not hand, etc. See Tosafot to Shabbos there, and Ketubot 61a, s.v. 'it is exchanged,' etc. The Rosh, however, wrote that the teaching of the other Tanna is a proof, since he said, 'Perhaps you brought,' etc. This shows that one must be careful, etc. See there. Regarding pouring a cup, the Rashba wrote that the prohibition is not limited to her placing it directly into his hand. It is forbidden even for her to place it on the table, as stated in seif 10. The Amoraim would make a change and would not place it directly on the table, etc.
