If her husband is a physician, it is forbidden for him to feel her pulse. {Rama: And according to what I wrote, that we practice leniency if she needs him, for he attends to her, all the more so it is permitted to feel her pulse if there is no other physician and she needs him and there is danger in her illness (thus Beit Yosef inferred from the wording of Ramban, siman 127). And see Orach Chaim siman 88, whether it is permitted for a niddah to enter the synagogue and to pray.}
