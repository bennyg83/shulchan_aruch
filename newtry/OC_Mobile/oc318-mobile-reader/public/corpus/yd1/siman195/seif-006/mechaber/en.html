He should not sleep with her in a bed, even if each one is in his own clothing and they do not touch one another. {Rama: And even if each one has his own mattress (Beit Yosef, unlike Rabbeinu Yerucham). And even if they lie in two beds and the beds touch one another, it is forbidden (Mordechai, perek 1 of Shabbos, in the name of R”m).}
