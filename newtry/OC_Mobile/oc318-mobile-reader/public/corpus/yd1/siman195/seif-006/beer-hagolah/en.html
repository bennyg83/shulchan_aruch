From the Mishnah, Shabbat 11a: A zav may not eat together with a zavah. Raavad there in chapter 11, in his gloss, writes that Rav Acha likewise ruled this way. Maggid Mishneh cites Ramban and Rashba, and Ran states that it is implied by the baraita in the Gemara. The Tur and the other authorities likewise rule this way.
