He should not sleep with her. See the Bach and the Taz, who quote a gloss stating that a niddah may lie on her husband's sheets, but she should be careful not to lie on sheets upon which another man slept, lest she become pregnant from his semen. Although the child would be legitimate, since no forbidden intercourse occurred, one must nevertheless be concerned for proper lineage and for the decree lest the child later marry his paternal sister. None of these concerns applies to a niddah lying on her husband's sheets. See Chelkat Mechokek, Even HaEzer, siman 1, seif katan 8.
