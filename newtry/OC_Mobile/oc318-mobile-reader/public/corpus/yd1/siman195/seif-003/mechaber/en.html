He should not eat with her at the table unless there is some change — that there is something that separates between his plate and her plate: bread or a pitcher, or each one should eat with his own cloth. {Rama: And some say that this, that a separation is needed between his plate and her plate, is specifically when they do not eat in one bowl when she is tehorah; but if they eat in one bowl when she is tehorah, it suffices, when she is a niddah, if she eats in a bowl by herself, and no other distinguishing sign is needed (Hagahos Ashiri in the name of R”i, and Hagahos Alfasi); and so is the practice. And some say that it is forbidden for him to eat from the leftovers of her food (found in a booklet of Hilchos Niddah), just as it is forbidden to drink from the leftovers of her cup, as will be explained.}
