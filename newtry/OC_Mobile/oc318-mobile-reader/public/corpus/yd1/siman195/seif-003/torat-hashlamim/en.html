If she eats from a separate bowl. It is forbidden to eat from one bowl even if it contains small pieces and he eats one while she eats another. If, however, the bowl contains large pieces and each person places a piece before himself on a separate utensil and eats from there, there is no prohibition, since the eating does not follow immediately upon taking from the common bowl. So wrote the Taz.
