And he shall not receive from her hand. See responsum Yad Eliyahu end siman 65 — he wrote a niddah woman is not permitted to hold a candle in her hand so her husband drinks tobacco or warms himself from that candle or lights another candle from it; and proof from mishnah 11 chapter 5 — they prepare for a woman whose hands, etc.; and Ra'avad explained because vapor joins, etc. — see there; and see Teshuvot HaTaz chapter 3 siman 58 and siman 201 — he wrote permitted to take from hand of infant because "a living being carries itself" and she does nothing — only the infant itself emerges from mother's lap and comes to his father — see there. And it appears if the infant is small or sick or bound — forbidden — for then not applicable to say "living being carries itself" as Magen Avraham wrote siman 308 s.k. 70 and 71 — see there. And he wrote further there siman 58 — to touch her garments while she is wearing them one should distance; but when not on her — permitted — for lying and sitting of hers was forbidden only for purity — see there.
