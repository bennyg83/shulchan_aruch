Touching each other. See MeKor Chayyim s.k. 33 — he wrote from Mordechai's language (from which the rabbi extracted this matter) it implies specifically when feet of the beds touch each other it is forbidden — for then man and woman lie face to face and can gaze at each other and can come to habitual transgression; not so if head of one bed touches feet of the other — and all the more head to head — they cannot see each other's face — permitted; but nevertheless the world practices like concealed language of Rama — that in all cases when they touch each other it is forbidden; but with any separation between bed and bed — permitted — end quote; and see Chokhmat Adam kelal 116 din 5 — he wrote possibly if beds are attached to wall — permitted — see there. And to me it appears Rama did not say this except specifically regarding their beds that were not surrounded with boards above as implied above siman 190 s.k. 25 and below siman 301 s.k. 2 — and then when beds touched each other it appeared like one long bed; but regarding our beds made as a writing desk surrounded with boards above — permitted. [And see Ikarei Dinim siman 21 ot 46 — he brought in name of Sha'ar HaMayim siman 1 — he wrote although Maharam Alashkar in responsum 10 siman 21 permitted sleeping under canopy with niddah wife when each has separate cover individually — nevertheless correct to be stringent unless with hanging sheet partition between beds that she no longer sees her; and proof for prohibition without this see Teshuvot HaTaz chapter 3 siman 42.]
