I found in a gloss to an old Semak, in the name of Maharar Peretz, that a niddah may lie on her husband's sheets. Women are careful, however, regarding sheets upon which another man lay, lest she become pregnant from another man's semen. Why, then, is she not concerned that while she is a niddah she might become pregnant from her husband's semen and the child would be a child conceived in niddah? He answered that since there was no forbidden intercourse, the child is entirely legitimate, even if she became pregnant from another man's semen, for Ben Sira was legitimate. Regarding another man's semen, however, we are particular because of determining paternity, and because of the decree lest he marry his paternal sister, as stated in Yevamos. End quote. This was copied from the work of my father-in-law, of blessed memory.
