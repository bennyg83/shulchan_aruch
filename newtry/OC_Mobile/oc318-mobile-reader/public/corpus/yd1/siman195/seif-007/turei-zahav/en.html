I found in an old gloss on Sefer Mitzvot Katan in the name of R' Peretz: a niddah woman may lie on her husband's sheets, and women are careful about sheets that another man has lain on lest she become pregnant from another man's semen. But why is she not concerned about becoming pregnant in her niddah state from her husband's semen, which would make the child a son of a niddah? He answered: since there is no prohibited intercourse here, the child is fully legitimate even if she becomes pregnant from another man's semen — for was not Ben Sira legitimate? Rather, the reason we are particular about another man's semen is because of the need to distinguish paternity [havkhanah], and the decree lest he marry his half-sister from his father's side, as stated in Yevamot — end of quote, transcribed from the book of my father-in-law of blessed memory.
