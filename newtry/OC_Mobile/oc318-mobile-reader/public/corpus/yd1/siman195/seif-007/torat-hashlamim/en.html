Even the heel. So is in Talmud chapter 2 of Nedarim — "whoever gazes at heel of woman — it is as if sons not proper are born to him"; R. Yosef said — and regarding niddah wife R. Shimon ben Lakish said — heel that is taught is in place of filth that he intends opposite heel — end quote; if so difficult on Tur who is language of Mechaber who wrote likewise regarding covered places — but there it says specifically heel. And what Beit Yosef and other Acharonim wrote — Tur holds all covered places on her are included in place of genitals; and so Rashba in Teshuvot HaRashba — from heel we learn every covered place — end quote; but in truth this too is astounding — for in Talmud he is precise and says specifically in place of filth that he intends opposite heel; and also language "even the heel" that Tur wrote is not precise apparently. Therefore it appears simple — view of Rashba and Tur — Resh Lakish does not refer at all to matter of R. Yosef; and both disagree in explanation of baraita of R. Yosef — R. Yosef establishes baraita on actual heel and niddah wife; and Resh Lakish explains baraita regarding ordinary woman even if not niddah — and what is heel — place of filth. It emerges per R. Yosef he says actual heel; and nevertheless forbidden to gaze since it is covered place; and likewise all covered places; and so I found in Sefer Ba'al HaNefesh of Ra'avad page 49a who explained sugya of sugya so; and concludes and wrote — end quote — and we rule like both to be stringent — forbidden to gaze at heel of niddah wife; and from heel we learn every covered place on her; and forbidden to gaze at place of filth even if not niddah — end quote. And this appears also view of Rashba and Tur — their words are correct and clear; and we need not forced explanations of Beit Yosef and Bach and Perishah in this — see there.
