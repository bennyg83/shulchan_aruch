**(Collected) But the relatives, etc.** From the Mishnah cited above and Yerushalmi there, and in Tosafot chaf 1 s.v. shekvar, etc., and there 24b s.v. ella, etc. (72b).
**And likewise for Chanukah, etc.** From that he distinguished at the end — women on the festival, etc., on Rosh Chodesh, etc. — and did not distinguish at the beginning; and it is not required.
**Even on the second day of Yom Tov, etc.** — for they did not say (Yoma 6a) that the Rabbis equated it to a weekday except for what is needed for it alone; from that he said "even to cut myrtle for him," etc. — implies specifically everything for his need.
