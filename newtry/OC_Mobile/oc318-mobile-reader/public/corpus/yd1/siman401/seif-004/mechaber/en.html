They do not pass shoulder on chol haMoed and do not give meal of recovery except relatives of deceased; but relatives give meal; and same for Chanukah, Purim, and Rosh Chodesh; when giving meal they give only on upright beds; but on Yom Tov, even second Yom Tov — no tearing, passing shoulder, or meal of recovery; on chol haMoed they give meal for everyone for a sage into the street as they give for relatives — all like his relatives.
