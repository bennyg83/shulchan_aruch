Women on chol haMoed wail — meaning all answer as one; but do not clap hands; on Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, Purim — wail and clap but do not lament — meaning one speaks and all answer after; for deceased buried — no wailing or clapping; these for ordinary person; but for Torah scholar — on chol haMoed, Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, Purim — wail and lament as on weekday; these in his presence; not in his absence; and day of tidings even distant is like his presence.
