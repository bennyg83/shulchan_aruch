**On erev Shabbat.** (Even though on the evening of) [but on the evening of] Rosh Chodesh and erev Chanukah they say Tzidduk haDin even after chatzos — (not) [for it is] not preferable to erev Shabbat; so too Levush — and it implies that likewise they do not say it on erev Yom Tov after chatzos. Shach.
