**After chatzos on erev Shabbat.** (Even though on the evening of) [but on the evening of] Rosh Chodesh and erev Chanukah they say it even after chatzos — (not) [for it is] not preferable to erev Shabbat; so too Atzei Chaim — and it implies that likewise on erev Yom Tov after chatzos they do not say Tzidduk haDin.
