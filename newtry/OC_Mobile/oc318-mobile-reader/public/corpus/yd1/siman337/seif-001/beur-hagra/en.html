(Likkut) Is ill, etc. Mo'ed Katan 26b, and tractate Semachot, cited by Torat HaAdam: if one of his relatives dies, they do not inform him lest his mind become disturbed; and they do not rend his garment, and they do not weep, and they do not eulogize before him, lest they break his heart; and they silence those who wail before him. In the Tur it is written "the comforters before him," and this is not in the text before us (end of note).
