Rashi there and Shita Mekubetzet from the law of a pot that is not ben yomo — we call it noten ta'am lifgam; specifically nevelah itself we learn issur until unfit for a stranger's eating; but issur of mixtures we follow taste — whatever spoils the dish somewhat is permitted.
Rashba.
Ran in the last chapter of Avodah Zarah: since one enjoys it in its full quantity more than one suffers from its spoilage.
Statement of Rav Yehudah who said Shmuel there daf 67.
Tur and so Rambam chapter 15 of Forbidden Foods and Rashba because all the more so if it spoils at the end it is forbidden — all the more if it spoils intending later to impart beneficial taste.
