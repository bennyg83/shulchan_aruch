Even spoils somewhat — and if neither beneficial nor spoiling taste: Shach forbids; see Peri Chadash; requires study — Shach's proof from Yerushalmi on terumah olives with hullin olives or terumah liquid in hullin olives — neither beneficial nor spoiling forbids; can reject: no majority in heter; with majority perhaps permitted — requires study. See Orach Chaim on four spices: Tur wrote not noten ta'am but for sharpness; Peri Chadash: truly senses taste but not noten ta'am in dish — Razah: new taste and third taste.
And if there is no substance, etc. — see Shach s.k. 5 citing Rashba in Terumot; with this could reconcile Rosh for end of night spoils — pot needs pouring in kodshim if cooked partially in vessel — perhaps kodshim stringent lest absorption spreads in whole vessel unlike terumah; if so for other issurim noten ta'am lifgam permitted — Rashba's question remains; perhaps absorbed issur in vessel is majority — forced Rashba must say taste is not majority; for halachah perhaps forbidden piece agrees they forbade only ben yomo; when not spoiling, taste like substance forbids in majority in not its kind; but Rashba's view seems to disagree with Tosafot — see there. Truly Tosafot hold even taste is considered majority — gid in sixty, therefore permitted pot chatat then shelamim — kodshim establish d'oraisa on majority; when always cooked there is majority, whole vessel emits and forbids even b'ein d'oraisa; possible for halachah nevertheless when spoiling dish, not taste at all and no substance in what they forbade — when not spoiling, taste like substance forbids in majority in not its kind; Rashba's view seems to disagree with Tosafot.
Spoiled and finally beneficial — see Shach citing Or Zarua: if eaten while spoiling, permitted; Peri Chadash challenged; I brought for myself Yerushalmi: hot beans and cold — what? Yerushalmi: therefore hot beans forbidden because their way is to cool — not that simply because they will eventually be beneficial they are forbidden; what is not their way is certainly permitted as Shach wrote — now permitted; but what is their way, since one eats hot and leaves to cool — certainly forbidden; Yerushalmi emphasized "their way is to cool" — like initially praiseworthy; otherwise not; Or Zarua ruled correctly.
