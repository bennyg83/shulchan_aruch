See responsum Kenesset Yechezkel siman 21 — from his words we learn: if they cooked in issur pot a dish the issur imparts beneficial taste, then cooked a dish the first issur is noten ta'am lifgam — second dish forbidden, since first dish was forbidden and piece became nevelah, now first dish imparts taste in second since first does not spoil second dish; see responsum Shvut Yaakov Givat Shaul siman 18 who challenged and raised second dish permitted — forbidden cannot be stricter than forbidding — see there at length; also in my responsum 20 I challenged Kenesset Yechezkel and raised to permit; see also responsum Teshuvat Chen siman 23.
