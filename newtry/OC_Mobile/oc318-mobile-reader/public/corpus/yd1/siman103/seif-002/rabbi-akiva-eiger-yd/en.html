(Shach s.k. 12) Nevertheless it spoils as in the Gemara. It seems permitted to add up to sixty and then add salt and spice that impart beneficial taste — not nullifying issur l'chatchila, since now while spoiling the issur is nullified in majority, for in noten ta'am lifgam we come from nullification in majority as Rashba wrote — only after beneficial taste it is like recognizable issur taste; therefore whatever one adds beforehand so afterward issur not recognizable — not called nullifying issur; similar to law below (s.k. 9 section 2); later found in Pri Megadim (in Siftei Da'at s.k. 6 third law) who considered this and doubted distinguishing this from below (siman 100) — requires study for halachah.
(Shulchan Aruch siman 103, Shach s.k. 2) And so for every issur thing. See Peri Chadash, etc.; he also wrote in responsum Dat Ash siman 13.
(There) Even though afterward water was heated. From this wording it seems at least twenty-four hours from milk cooking is required, not sufficient from prior meat cooking alone — otherwise what novelty that water was heated — without that it is already within twenty-four hours from milk cooking; so agreed in halachah in Pri Megadim here and above (siman 94 in Shach s.k. 2) at length.
(Shach s.k. 18) Not required per Beit Yosef. Shach's intent: root of distinction whether night passed before heating water or after — if water heated first, we judge water is noten ta'am lifgam immediately, requiring twenty-four hours from heating; if night passed first before heating, at heating we say since noten ta'am lifgam rabbinically, sixty rabbinically perhaps already spoiled by night's sleep; though water forbidden to drink from safek, absorption in it is forbidden but water body is rabbinic safek; if night between meat and milk cooking, at milk cooking time milk is noten ta'am lifgam in ben yomo d'oraisa safek — d'oraisa safek; since we judge milk noten ta'am lifgam, requires twenty-four hours from milk; if water heated afterward, water too noten ta'am lifgam — between milk and water no new spoilage — truly we were stringent from safek milk noten ta'am lifgam — cannot afterward be lenient on water saying milk not noten ta'am lifgam — needs careful study.
