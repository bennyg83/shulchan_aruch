(Shach s.k. 12) Nevertheless it spoils as in the Gemara. It seems permitted to add up to sixty and then add salt and spice that impart beneficial taste — not nullifying issur l'chatchila, since now while spoiling the issur is nullified in majority, for in noten ta'am lifgam we come from nullification in majority as Rashba wrote — only after beneficial taste it is like recognizable issur taste; therefore whatever one adds beforehand so afterward issur not recognizable — not called nullifying issur; similar to law below (s.k. 9 section 2); later found in Pri Megadim (in Siftei Da'at s.k. 6 third law) who considered this and doubted distinguishing this from below (siman 100) — requires study for halachah.
