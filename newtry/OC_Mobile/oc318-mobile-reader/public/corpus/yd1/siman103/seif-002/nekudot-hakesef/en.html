(There s.k. 8) And we do not rule, etc. — scribal error; should read "and we rule"; so in Shach s.k. 14 [perhaps before him Taz had "we do not rule"].
(Siman 103, Taz seif katan 6:) And I wonder why the Rama chose, etc. The Rama aimed well, for perforce this is also the intent of Issur VeHeter, that the spoon absorbed from a spoiled prohibition. Otherwise there are only two transfers of taste, and we rule above in siman 95 that nat bar nat is permitted only where there are three transfers of permitted taste. Issur VeHeter HaArukh itself also wrote that even with utensils we require three transfers of taste, and I brought this in Shach, siman 94, seif katan 15; see there. Also, what Levush wrote, "but if he stirred the initial food with a prohibited spoon," etc., is a scribal error and it must read "with a permitted spoon." His intent accords with the Rama's view, for otherwise what difference does it make that he first inserted it into another pot; in the end it is a prohibited spoon, and this is clear.
