This spoil need not, etc. Rashi there 67a s.v. "anything that is not," etc.; and proof for his words from pot not ben yomo — we call it noten ta'am lifgam though it does not spoil the dish so much that it is inedible; and they say there: pot ben yomo too — impossible that not, etc.; and even R' Shimon disagrees only because he holds it does not spoil at all, as Tosafot there s.v. "and the other," etc.; and so oil and honey and a kortov of cress in chapter 2 — they permitted because of noten ta'am lifgam, and all are eaten.
And some say, etc. Ran there: although we derive from nevelah and whatever is fit for a stranger is called nevelah even if slightly spoiled — because it is eaten b'ein and therefore must spoil from human eating; but mixture whose body is nullified in majority, even rabbinically forbidden only because of taste — taste is like essence d'oraisa, and they decreed from it for not its kind; therefore whatever spoils somewhat is permitted because it is not noten ta'am — on the contrary it spoils; but without majority it is forbidden because of the body as if b'ein; and what is written "if there is no substance," etc.
And some, etc. Ran there: still one must say how we derive from nevelah; and it seems because nevelah forbidden only when fit though slightly spoiled because one benefits — unlike when unfit, since one does not benefit at all; and so here one does not benefit because it spoils; therefore I am inclined to say: if the issur was enlarged, etc., like nevelah.
When stated, etc. Refers to seif 1; and as in the Gemara there.
Or spoils, etc. See above siman 95.
Some say even though, etc. As at the end of Avodah Zarah — decree on pot that is not, etc.; all the more here lest one cook something noten ta'am lifgam; see siman 99.
And if they cooked, etc. Like spoiling; and at the end it imparted beneficial taste.
Against the issur in it. But not against all of it, for piece does not become nevelah since it was permitted, as stated siman 94 seif 6 and siman 98 seif 5; but a different spoon — Beit Yosef, etc.
But if, etc. For it is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of the permitted.
And so in a thing, etc. For here it does not apply; and afterward it imparted beneficial taste; see siman 81 seif 8.
