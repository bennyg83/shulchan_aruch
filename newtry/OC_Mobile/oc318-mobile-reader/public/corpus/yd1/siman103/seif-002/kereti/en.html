Even spoils somewhat — and neither for spoilage nor beneficial taste: Shach forbids; Peri Chadash permits; see Peleti if heter is majority — even if issur neither spoils nor benefits, nevertheless permitted.
Spoiled and finally imparted beneficial taste — Shach to permit; Peri Chadash forbids; see Peleti there is a difference if it stands to impart beneficial taste — then like beneficial and forbidden.
In the spoon, etc. — this is noten ta'am bar noten ta'am of the permitted, for it spoils; even if the spoon absorbed beneficial taste from the issur body, nevertheless for those who hold even if cooked we permit — noten ta'am bar noten ta'am.
