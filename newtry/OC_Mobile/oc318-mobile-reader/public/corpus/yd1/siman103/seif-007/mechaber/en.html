A pot absorbed from meat and milk cooked in it together or one after the other — before one night passed, if water was heated in it, it is considered ben yomo until it rests twenty-four hours from when water was heated in it: {Rama: but if a night passed — permitted; and if a night passed between cooking meat and milk, even though afterward water was heated immediately, the law is the same; and so for all issurim as we hold for all issurim piece becomes nevelah (Beit Yosef and the Rav's reasoning); and see siman 92; and where there is loss one may permit in such a case for other issurim in every matter, only that there be twenty-four hours from time of cooking the issur.}
