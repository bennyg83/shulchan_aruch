Night. Specifically night causes taste to spoil, as poskim state — Shach [and see above siman 94 s.k. 16 what is written].
The water. The reason is in Beit Yosef: since some hold after one night noten ta'am lifgam, and even though we do not rule so to permit the water, nevertheless it helps here not to count as nevelah from heating the water; Shach wrote: it is clear that if water was heated within one night, afterward twenty-four hours from heating is required; unlike Beit David who is lenient; if so, also what the Rav wrote "if one night passed between meat and milk," etc., is not required per Beit Yosef — investigate.
Issurim. Taz wrote: meaning even before the night's sleep, since the water itself will be considered nevelah in other issurim — dispute in siman 92; in such a case we rely on the lenient (see Peri Chadash). Shach wrote: here "where loss" means slight loss; for great loss otherwise permitted from the view that we do not say piece becomes nevelah in wet issur absorbed in wet heter, as above siman 92 seif 4 in hagahah — see there.
