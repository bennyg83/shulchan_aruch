Pot, etc. Water is noten ta'am lifgam and returns and is absorbed in the pot. Tosafot end Avodah Zarah 76a s.v. "ben," etc.
From ben yomo. Per his view that noten ta'am lifgam only in ben yomo.
But if, etc. Semak wrote many are lenient since no issur body is ben yomo — no concern; and Rashi and R' Tam in Tosafot there hold whatever passed a night is not ben yomo; even though we do not rule like them, in such a case since many are lenient, one may rely. Beit Yosef.
And where loss, etc. Where many are lenient we rely on those who hold no piece becomes nevelah, not in ben yomo. Beit David.
Loss. Meaning slight loss; for great loss otherwise we say wet in wet piece does not become nevelah.
