(Shach s.k. 18) Not required per Beit Yosef. Shach's intent: root of distinction whether night passed before heating water or after — if water heated first, we judge water is noten ta'am lifgam immediately, requiring twenty-four hours from heating; if night passed first before heating, at heating we say since noten ta'am lifgam rabbinically, sixty rabbinically perhaps already spoiled by night's sleep; though water forbidden to drink from safek, absorption in it is forbidden but water body is rabbinic safek; if night between meat and milk cooking, at milk cooking time milk is noten ta'am lifgam in ben yomo d'oraisa safek — d'oraisa safek; since we judge milk noten ta'am lifgam, requires twenty-four hours from milk; if water heated afterward, water too noten ta'am lifgam — between milk and water no new spoilage — truly we were stringent from safek milk noten ta'am lifgam — cannot afterward be lenient on water saying milk not noten ta'am lifgam — needs careful study.
