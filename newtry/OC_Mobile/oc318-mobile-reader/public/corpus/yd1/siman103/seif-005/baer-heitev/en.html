Rinsed. Shach wrote: above siman 95 I wrote that stam vessels cooked in them — we say they were clean and no fat stuck; therefore "it must be rinsed," etc., meaning unless known clearly it was not clean and not rinsed — we teach that stuck fat is not noten ta'am lifgam after twenty-four hours; and those who permit even cooked before rinsing — their view is that smearing issur on the vessel face also spoils after twenty-four hours; see siman 122 seif 3; Beit David and Maharshal [and we rule like the first view that what is b'ein does not spoil even after many days — acharonim].
Permitted. Shach and Maharshal disagreed: since there is substance b'ein, taste in a pot that is not ben yomo imparts beneficial taste and all is taste from substance — his words are not plausible.
