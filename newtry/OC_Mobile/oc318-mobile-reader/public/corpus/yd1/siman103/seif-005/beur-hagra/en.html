(Lekut) And it must be, etc. From what is written (Chullin) 8b "slaughtering with a knife," etc. — even though stam knives are not ben yomo except because stam knife is not scoured [and so Ra'ah in Beit David — see there]. Latest view: as Tosafot there s.v. "one who slaughters" and Rosh there — ben yomo deals; but Hagahot Mordechai there s.v. "and if he slaughtered," etc., did not write so — Rashi's words above. Tosafot and Rosh's words are difficult: why challenge there and resolve because of fat drippings, etc. — new, etc. — stam knife is not ben yomo, why force it (and perhaps Ra'ash chapter 2 Avodah Zarah siman 35 "when slaughtering," etc.). So too Beit David there: in new, etc., or in old and scoured, etc.; but if not scoured, presumption on knife that fat is on the face, and whatever is b'ein we do not say it spoiled in not ben yomo, etc.; and so stam in Shulchan Aruch and hagahah in many places (end).
(Lekut) And it must be, etc. So too Beit David and Beit HaLevi; and what is stated in chapter 2 Avodah Zarah regarding a kortov of cress — even though they said noten ta'am lifgam is permitted nonetheless — Ra'ah explained even b'ein fat drippings in cress spoils; Beit HaLevi explained stam artisan's knife is clean since the shopkeeper scours his tools — see there (end).
(Lekut) And it must be, etc. Tosafot 8b s.v. "one who slaughters," etc.; Mordechai there and Semak answered because of fat drippings on knife walls; Rashi 112a s.v. "sharpness," etc.; Tosafot there; since Tosafot force known ben yomo here, they hold one may permit (end).
