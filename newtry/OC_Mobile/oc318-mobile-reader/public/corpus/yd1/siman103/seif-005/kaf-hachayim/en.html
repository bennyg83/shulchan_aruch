If not spoiled in themselves, etc. — it seems if spoiled in themselves, such as rotted, nullified in majority; and Beit David means things important from the outset like impure bird and nevelah piece, davar sheb'minyan, etc., that rotted afterward — nullified; but things repulsive in themselves like mice, flies, mosquitoes, sheratzim and crawlers — even though they have spoiling repulsive taste, not nullified. Minchat Kohen in Sefer HaTa'aruvot chapter 1 section 9 page 73b. Peri Chadash section 1. Lechem HaPanim section 6. Beit Levi section 3. And not like Panim Me'irrot volume 2 siman 67 who understood Rama permits these too — see there. Also not like Arukh HaShulchan section 11 who wrote Rama permits worms repulsive in themselves, flies, lice, etc., lost in a dish and unrecognizable — completely nullified — see there, against all poskim and Rashba whom Shulchan Aruch ruled siman 104, clear in Beit Yosef: specifically if deficient or crushed nullified in majority, not when repulsive in themselves — see there. Also not as explicit in Beit David general rule 80 law 24. And see above siman 101 section 32.
