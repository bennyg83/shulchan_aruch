And it must be rinsed, etc. Above siman 95 I wrote stam vessels cooked in them — we say they were clean with no stuck fat; therefore "it must be rinsed," etc. — unless known clearly not clean and not rinsed — we teach stuck fat is not noten ta'am lifgam after twenty-four hours; those who permit even cooked before rinsing hold smearing issur on the face also spoils after twenty-four hours; as Beit Yosef and Tur siman 122 first view; so appears Beit Yosef; so below siman 122 seif 3 only first view; so Beit David and Maharshal Pesachim siman 64.
And if there is sixty, etc. So Beit David there in the name of Or Zarua (beginning general rule 33); so from Rashba and Mechaber above siman 95; Maharshal Pesachim disagreed: since substance b'ein, taste in pot not ben yomo imparts beneficial taste and all is taste from substance — not plausible.
