Every pot that is not ben yomo — its taste is considered spoiling and it does not forbid; and it is called ben yomo whenever it did not rest twenty-four hours after issur was cooked in it; and once twenty-four hours passed after issur was cooked in it, it is not called ben yomo; and if one cooked in it when it is not ben yomo, the food is permitted, for it is noten ta'am lifgam — provided it was rinsed so no fat is on its face; for if not rinsed, it forbids, and behold it is like an issur piece that did not spoil; and some permit even if cooked in it before rinsing: {Rama: and if there is sixty against what sticks to it, all permit, since the pot is not ben yomo — and so is customary (Or Zarua).}
