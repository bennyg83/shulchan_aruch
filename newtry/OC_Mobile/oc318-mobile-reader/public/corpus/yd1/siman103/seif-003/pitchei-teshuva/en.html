See responsum Noda B'Yehudah volume 14 siman 51 — wine cooked in ben yomo meat vessel, stirred with hullin ben yomo spoon — wrote wine should be permitted though hagahah below seif 4 meat or milk in wine is noten ta'am lifgam — meaning milk in curdled form; nevertheless since milk itself does not forbid wine except through mixture with meat and ben yomo — meat spoils; but vessels we forbid per siman 95 seif 3 hagahah — we do not count noten ta'am bar noten ta'am lest milk and meat drops mixed without water middle — vessels forbidden as hagahah here — see there; see Beit Yehudah who challenged forbidding vessels.
