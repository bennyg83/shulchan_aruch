(3) There. Every thing whose taste spoils does not forbid, etc. — and even in meat and milk spoiling taste is permitted. Rambam chapter 15 of Forbidden Foods law 40; and so is proven from Shulchan Aruch and poskim: in all issurim, even ben yomo and kilayim of vineyard, noten ta'am lifgam is permitted since they did not distinguish; they wrote simply: whatever spoils does not forbid — unlike Pliti siman 87 section 15 who wrote in ben yomo noten ta'am lifgam is forbidden. Chokhmat David section 1; so Shafan David above siman 87 section 18 refuted Pliti; Chokhmat Adam chapter 1 section 73 also refuted Pliti and wrote Rambam's words were omitted — see there.
