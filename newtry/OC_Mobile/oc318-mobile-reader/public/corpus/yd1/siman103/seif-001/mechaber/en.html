The law of noten ta'am lifgam. In it are 7 seifim: Every thing whose taste spoils does not forbid its mixture — even if its taste is not spoiled in itself, that b'ein it is flavorful and praiseworthy, but it spoils its mixture — permitted:
{Rama: however important things like beriah or the like — if they are not spoiled in themselves, even though they spoil the dish, they are not nullified even in a thousand (and so wrote Arukh general rule 32).}
