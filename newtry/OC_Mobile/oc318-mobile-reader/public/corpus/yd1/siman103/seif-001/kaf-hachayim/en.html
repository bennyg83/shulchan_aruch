(1) [Seif 1] Every thing whose taste spoils does not forbid, etc. — we derive from the verse "you shall not eat any nevelah to the stranger," etc. Avodah Zarah 67b. Beit Yosef. Taz s.k. 1. And see below section 8.
(2) And even if praiseworthy at first and later spoiled — permitted. Rosh chapter Ein Ma'amidin; and so agreed all poskim — Beit Yosef.
(4) There in hagahah. However important things like beriah, etc. — when spoiled in themselves their importance is nullified even if cooked and not recognizable — the body too is nullified in mere majority, for the stringency in mixtures through cooking more than pickling is because of taste, and here there is no issur taste since the issur is putrid as if no issur substance, only taste alone; but if praiseworthy in themselves, even though noten ta'am lifgam in this dish itself, not nullified even in a thousand since beriah or the like among things not nullified — all is like issur body without mixture. Taz s.k. 2. Lechem HaPanim section 2. Beit Yitzchak section 3. And what Zivchei Tzedek section 2 wrote — the Mechaber brought this in Beit Yosef from Rashba, etc. — not clear, for this is another matter from Or Zarua as appears in hagahah — as appears to one who examines well.
