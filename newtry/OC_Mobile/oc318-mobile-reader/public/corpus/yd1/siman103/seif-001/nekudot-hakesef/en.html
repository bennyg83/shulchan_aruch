(Shulchan Aruch siman 103, Taz s.k. 4) He wondered why Rama wrote, etc. Rama intended well, and Or Zarua's intent is likewise: the spoon absorbed from spoiling issur; otherwise there are only two noten ta'am, and we hold above siman 95 that noten ta'am bar noten ta'am is permitted only where there are three noten ta'am to the permitted; so too Or Zarua itself requires three noten ta'am even in vessels — I brought this in Shach siman 94 s.k. 15 — see there. Also what Levush wrote: "but if they stirred the first dish with a spoon of issur," etc. — scribal error; it should be "spoon of heter"; intent is Rama's view, otherwise what difference if first inserted into another pot — end is still issur spoon — clear.
