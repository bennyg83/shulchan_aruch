Spoils. Shach challenged: but in siman 122 the Mechaber ruled that stam vessels of non-Jews are not ben yomo; if so, permitted anyway; one must say he did not bring Rambam's words here except to teach that meat spoils oil and honey.
Loss. Taz wrote: even with great loss one should be stringent; see siman 94; Shach wrote: it appears the Yesh Omrim agree oil spoils — see there, that he wrote in the name of all poskim that meat and fat impart beneficial taste in oil whether raw oil or cooked.
