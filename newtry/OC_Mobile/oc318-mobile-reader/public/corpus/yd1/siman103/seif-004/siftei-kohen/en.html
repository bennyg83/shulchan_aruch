Oil and honey, etc. This is Rambam's wording chapter 17 of Forbidden Foods; Kesef Mishneh wrote there: he holds stam vessels of non-Jews are ben yomo; so Beit Yosef below siman 122; if so here permitted anyway; one must say he did not bring Rambam's words here except to teach meat spoils oil and honey; see siman 114 seif 7.
And some say, etc. — only liquid made from honey, etc. It seems in oil too the Yesh Omrim agree it spoils (and Eshel Tzvi wrote oil and honey, etc., even b'ein spoils oil and makes rancid, etc. — the Rav omitted in hagahah — see there). Wonder: these are Maharil in Hagahat Sh"D siman 22 — there explicit that all the more in oil it imparts beneficial taste even when cooked; so all poskim as I will explain from Maharil who brought responsum of R' Yitzchak in Mordechai Pesachim and Ein Ma'amidin: fat drippings b'ein is spoiling taste in honey; he wrote: however the incident was in a ben yomo pan in which they cooked honey and forbade — perhaps liquid made from honey; with this the Mishnah that permitted honey deals; R' Yitzchak brought proof: if because of non-Jewish absorption, etc.; in R' Yitzchak's responsum in Mordechai it is clear in oil he agrees it imparts beneficial taste — he brought proof spoiling in honey from Mishnah chapter Ein Ma'amidin forbidding non-Jewish oil, Shmuel explained because zefes of their vessels forbids them, etc.; therefore they only discussed noten ta'am lifgam in oil; but in honey complete noten ta'am lifgam even mixed with non-Jewish zefes or meat and milk — end. Meaning: in those Mishnayot permitted honey because of non-Jewish absorption — no concern, noten ta'am lifgam permitted; if so perforce in honey zefes b'ein is noten ta'am lifgam; in oil beneficial taste (and this Tanna may hold vessels ben yomo. Or: since spoilage in oil is only because not ben yomo, forbidden; and Tosafot Chullin 8 top: spoilage in oil only because not ben yomo — see there) — not like Ran in what he wrote siman 22 explaining R' Yitzchak that regarding absorption in wine, wine absorption in honey and impure meat — end; not like the Rav in responsum 53 who extracted from Maharil to distinguish in oil cooked or not — all poskim as I explained, not like Rav (and what Or Zarua wrote in the name of Mordechai that fat in oil is noten ta'am lifgam — not found in our Mordechai, perhaps a student mistakenly emended Mordechai — end; wonder, for clear from Mordechai from R' Yitzchak's incident and Mordechai Pesachim brought R' Yitzchak's incident and proof from Mishnah in harvest — clear as all poskim wrote — all hidden from the Rav). He answered: Gemara deals with cooked oil like honey; but in uncooked, noten ta'am lifgam — clearly not so as I explained; poskim explain like Rashi: if because of absorption, it is wine mixture — throughout Avodah Zarah we concern only wine mixture; but zefes in oil always noten ta'am lifgam whether raw or cooked.
