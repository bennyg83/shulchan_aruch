Oil and honey of a non-Jew — even though cooked, permitted because meat spoils the oil and makes it rancid; and so for honey: {Rama: and some say meat does not spoil honey itself, only liquid made from honey (Maharil in Hagahat Sh"D siman 22 and many more poskim — see s.k. 13); and where there is no great loss one should be stringent. Meat or milk in wine is noten ta'am lifgam and permitted (Hagahot Ashiri chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah).}
