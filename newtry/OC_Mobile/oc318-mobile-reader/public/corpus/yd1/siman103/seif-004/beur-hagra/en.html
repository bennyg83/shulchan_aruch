Oil and honey, etc. Rambam's wording: oil of a non-Jew is permitted, etc., not because of non-Jewish absorption, for meat spoils the oil and makes it rancid; and so honey, etc., for this reason; and explained what is stated there 38b "this oil," etc., if because of absorption, etc.; and there 39b "and honey," etc., if because of absorption — because meat spoils them; but stam vessels — some hold we say they are ben yomo and absorbed, siman 122; therefore "even though cooked" — and as stated "cooked oil," etc., if because of, etc.; and for us who hold stam vessels are not ben yomo, Shulchan Aruch wrote this to teach meat spoils them.
And some say, etc. As Beit Yosef challenged: the world cooks meat in honey and it imparts beneficial taste; but Rashi and Tosafot there s.v. "if they specified" — stam vessels are not ben yomo; nevertheless Tosafot wrote there honey spoils; but in oil Tosafot Chullin 8b s.v. "one who slaughters" wrote oil imparts beneficial taste; and so in the Mishnah they forbade oil and permitted honey because honey spoils even b'ein, unlike oil; later they permitted oil because stam vessels are not ben yomo; and so Ran explains absorption regarding oil, wine absorption regarding honey and impure meat; and so from all poskim; Beit Yosef's question — Bach answered: when the main dish is meat it imparts beneficial taste, unlike fat drippings that spoil.
(Lekut) And some say, etc. From Beit Yosef's question — not applicable, for even b'ein beneficial taste spoils like in flasks 33b per Rosh; though there 33a we say wine in water, etc. (end). (Lekut) And some say, etc. Per what is written: honey b'ein imparts beneficial taste but what is absorbed spoils, like wine in water and other liquids for beneficial taste and in absorption for spoilage, as Avodah Zarah 33b and as above siman 122 seif 5 (end).
(Supplement) And some say, etc. See what is written there: honey in its visible form improves flavor but absorbed honey worsens it; and likewise wine that is in water and other beverages improve in visible form but worsen when absorbed — as written in Avodah Zarah 33b; and see what I wrote above in siman 122 seif 5 (end).
