To honey. Avnei HaTzedek of Maharit s.k. 12; see responsum Yad Eliyahu end siman 36, 45, 66.
Milk in wine. See end Tosafot Shabbat and responsum Noda B'Yehudah volume 14 siman 51 — milk in curdled form — see there; Pri Megadim in Shach s.k. 13: milk in a kortov; but milk in curdled form see siman 121 seif 1 hagahah — seems beneficial taste — meaning there Rama wrote custom to put wine in vessels whose walls have milk stuck because wine's nature, etc. — apparently milk beneficial taste in wine, otherwise permitted. Truly not proof — one can say if because noten ta'am lifgam would be forbidden l'chatchila as in many places; but because wine's nature, etc., permitted even l'chatchila; so explicit there in Rabbenu Yerucham from whom that custom derives. [Found in Beit Yehudah challenging Pri Megadim: more plausible both milk in kortov and curdled spoil; poskim wrote only milk unspecified, not whether kortov or curdled — both equal — see there].
