[Against the first prohibition. See Baer Heitev. See Sefer Levushei Serad, siman 47, who wrote that it appears to him that one does not require sixty against the entire prohibition, but only proportionally, for some of the prohibition also remained in the initial food, as the Taz wrote in siman 92, seif katan 24; here too it did not become neveilah, since it impairs, etc. He further wrote: but if the prohibition gave beneficial taste in the initial food, afterward one requires sixty against the entire pot (see Shach at the beginning of siman 93). However, if there is some doubt, such as if it spilled and we do not know whether there was sixty against the pot, but it is at least known that there was sixty against the prohibition, even if not against all of it but proportionally; or alternatively, that prohibition was a doubtful prohibition and was not known until after it fell into the initial food, and afterward in the second dish there is sixty against the prohibition proportionally, it is permitted. Perhaps this requires specifically that it not become known until the second dish was cooked. This matter requires consideration; see there for the reason.]
