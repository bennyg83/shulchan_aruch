If he did not slaughter the mother but tore her open; and likewise if he slaughtered her and it became neveilah in his hand, or was found treifah: if the fetus (found in it) nine months alive requires shechitah for itself and is permitted through it (the mother). And if it is nine months dead or eight months—even alive—behold this is forbidden. {Rama: And now we do not permit any fetus found in an animal if the mother is treifah (Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Semag end of prohibitions 136, Orach Chaim end of kllal 55); and his own shechitah does not help, for we are concerned perhaps it is not nine months old.}
