(Seif 3) And it became neveilah, etc. All the more so from treifah—for neveilah is like tearing, as written in chapter 5 (chapter 1 seif 2) and chapter 6 (chapter 5 seif 1) there.
And if it is, etc. As written there according to the view of those who permit—it forbids; and here we do not need "four simanim," etc.
