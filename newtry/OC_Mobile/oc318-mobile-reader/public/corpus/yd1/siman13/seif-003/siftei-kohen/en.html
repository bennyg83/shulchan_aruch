Requires shechitah. D'oraisa; and all other treifot disqualify it—so is plain in the Talmud and poskim; and see below siman 16 seif 9: regarding oto v'et beno there is a distinction between one who slaughters and it becomes neveilah versus one found treifah; and regarding kisuy hadam in siman 28 seif 17 there is no distinction as here; and see in the Talmud chapter Kol HaBasar (daf 88).
Behold this is forbidden. For its mother's shechitah did not avail to purify it, and it needs shechitah; and since it is nine months dead or eight months alive—which is considered like dead—shechitah does not help for it, and it is forbidden.
If the mother is treifah. But if she is kosher it is kosher—even nowadays; for even if it is eight months old, its mother's shechitah purifies it; and so too where its mother too did not require shechitah—it is plain that even if the mother was found treifah the fetus does not require shechitah and is kosher even nowadays.
For we are concerned perhaps it is not nine months old. But surely when one waits until the beginning of the eighth month from its birth—then it emerges from doubt of miscarriage; and as below siman 15—even if its mother was treifah it is permitted through its own shechitah; and so too in Perishah seif 12.
