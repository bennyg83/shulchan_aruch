Requires shechitah for itself—for "four simanim the Merciful One koshered"; and behold it is like a yelud and has shechitah.
And is permitted through it (the mother)—and if it has treifot it is forbidden like other behemot, for behold it is entirely like a yelud.
Behold this is forbidden—for it has no shechitah at all.
And now there is no permission, etc.—but if the mother is kosher, then for it—that the offspring is a complete miscarriage—its mother's shechitah permits it from "all in behemah you may eat."
For we are concerned perhaps it is not nine months old—and if it waited eight days from the beginning of its birth it is permitted—so wrote the Shach, Pri Chadash, and Perishah; and see Peleti where I extended in contradicting their words—and in any case it is forbidden even if it waited a long time.
