And the Shach explained: this shechitah is d'oraisa, and all other treifot disqualify in it; and see below siman 16 seif 9: regarding oto v'et beno there is a distinction between slaughtering and it becomes neveilah versus found treifah; and regarding kisuy hadam in siman 28 seif 17 there is no distinction as here; and see in the Talmud chapter Kol HaBasar daf 88.
Treifah. And the Shach wrote: but if she is kosher it is kosher—even nowadays; and so where its mother too did not require shechitah—even if the mother was found treifah the fetus does not require shechitah and is kosher even nowadays.
Nine months. And the Shach wrote: but surely when one waits until the beginning of the eighth month from its birth—then it emerges from doubt of miscarriage; as below siman 15—it is permitted through its own shechitah.
