And likewise if he slaughtered her, etc. In the Tur he wrote: even if he slaughtered her, etc.; and his intent is not to exclude—for since the beginning of the shechitah was proper it helps; and so regarding treifah—there is nevertheless proper shechitah that helps to purify it from neveilah; and Beit Yosef explained in the language of "even" that it is attached to the end, and there is no need for this.
A nine-month-old alive, etc. For we say in the Gemara: four simanim the Merciful One koshered in the verse "of the behemah you may eat"—that if one wishes he eats it through its mother's shechitah; and if its mother is treifah he slaughters for that nine-month-old alive that is permitted, either through two simanim of its mother or through its own; and therefore if it is nine months dead or eight months alive—for it has no shechitah of its own and its mother's shechitah too does not help it, since the mother was found treifah—it is forbidden.
