Forbidden—for it is written in the Torah regarding the split creature that has two backs and two spines, and it is not in reality that it could live even a short time; and the Torah only mentioned it if found in the womb of a behemah—a fetus that has this, so that the mother's shechitah should not permit it—and it is forbidden. Gemara—and that which they permit a dissolved placenta through the mother's shechitah and we are not concerned perhaps it is split and dissolved, or the form of a dove and shechitah does not permit—one must say: since it is not common, they were not concerned for this.
