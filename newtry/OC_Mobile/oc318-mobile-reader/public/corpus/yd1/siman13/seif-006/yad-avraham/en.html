(Shulchan Aruch) And if it came upon a daughter pakuah like him—behold his son, etc., like him. And it is proved in the Talmud, Rashi, and Tosafot at the beginning of chapter HaMakishah (daf 69a) that even a nine-month-old live ben pakuah that has a prohibition emerging in one limb—as written in the siman afterward: that it came upon a daughter pakuah like him that also has a prohibition emerging in one limb—for that limb has no remedy through shechitah, as explained in siman 14. Nevertheless, that offspring which it engendered has no prohibition in this limb—for its seed confounds—see there.
