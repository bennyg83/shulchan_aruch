Animals that do not require shechitah. In it are 6 seifim. Behemah, chayah, and fowl require shechitah; fish and locusts do not require shechitah. {Rama: And it is permitted to eat them dead, or to cut off a limb from them and eat it; but it is forbidden to eat them alive because of bal teshaktzu (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 1, Hagahot Mordechai, Tosafot).}
