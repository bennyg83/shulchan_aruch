Chayah and fowl, etc. Chapter 2, R' Yehoshua in the name of R' Yishmael, chapter 2, etc., until "two years" regarding behemah.
Fish. There and in chapter 5 of Keritut.
And locusts. Keritut there and chapter 2 of Avodah Zarah: the Sages taught "locusts," etc., there 35a; a gentile who kindled, etc.; and chapter 9 of Shabbat.
And it is permitted, etc. Tosefta chapter 9 of Terumot: a person may eat fish and locusts whether alive or dead and need not be concerned.
But forbidden, etc. Gemara chapter 9 of Shabbat; and Tosafot there s.v. delo; and Tosafot Avodah Zarah 38a s.v. le'olam.
