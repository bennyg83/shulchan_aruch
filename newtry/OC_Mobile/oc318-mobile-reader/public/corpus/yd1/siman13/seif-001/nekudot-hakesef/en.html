(Siman 13 s.k. 3) This language is superfluous, etc. I needed to say: what does "permitted in eating" teach—through shechitah? Or perhaps other treifot do not forbid it, but nevertheless it requires shechitah like at the end—the verse teaches us.
(s.k. 4) And one should consider, etc. And in my humble opinion even b'dieved it is forbidden—for so is explicit to the Rosh, Ran, and other poskim, and Beit Yosef brings them; and so it appears from the Tur and Mechaber, who wrote regarding other treifot "do not forbid"—from which we infer shechitah forbids it even b'dieved; and the proofs he brought from ma'alin and gittin are not proofs at all—and examine.
