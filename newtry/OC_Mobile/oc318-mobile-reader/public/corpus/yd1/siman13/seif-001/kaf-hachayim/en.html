(a) [Seif 1] Behemah, chayah, and fowl require shechitah. Explicit in the Torah (Deuteronomy 12:21): "and you shall slaughter of your cattle and flock"; and chayah is derived in the first chapter of HaShochet (Chullin 27a) from what is written regarding disqualified consecrated animals: "only as the gazelle and deer are eaten," etc.—the gazelle and deer are compared to disqualified consecrated animals: just as disqualified consecrated animals require shechitah, so gazelle and deer require shechitah. And fowl is derived from what is written: "whoever hunts and traps animal or fowl and pours out its blood"—teaching that pouring the blood of fowl is like pouring the blood of chayah. Beit Yosef. Levush. And see further above siman 1, section 1.
