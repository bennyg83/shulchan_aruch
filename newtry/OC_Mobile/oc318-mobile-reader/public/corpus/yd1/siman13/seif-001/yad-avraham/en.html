(Siman 13 seif 1) Chayah and fowl require shechitah. Maharam wrote: according to the view that melikah for a fowl is not from the Torah, it is not neveilah at all—for it is all one nechirah—unless it died by itself or he struck its head, then it is neveilah; but in such a case it is nechirah—and examine. And it is difficult: if so, why was a verse needed for "all their sin-offerings" to include the fowl sin-offering that is eaten—for one might have thought it is neveilah, as explained in Zevachim (daf 44)—and I found he sensed this in Be'er Yitzchak. And see further in Torat Kohanim chapter 6 Vayikra, where R' Shimon also derives from another verse to permit melikah of kohanim.
