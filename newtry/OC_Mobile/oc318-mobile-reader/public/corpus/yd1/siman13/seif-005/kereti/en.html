Even if its hooves, etc.—already explained above in seif 2 that on the contrary, if its hooves are found cloven, even rabbinically it does not require shechitah; if so, one must understand what "even" means—and the Acharonim extended, and what appears close to the simple sense: certainly if a kosher behemah is found in the womb of a behemah, even if its hooves are cloven, nevertheless it is kosher—only if the form is of an impure behemah and its hoof cloven is found in a behemah, then even though we hold in tractate Bekhorot "a camel born from a cow is kosher"—that is only to kosher it; but that the mother's shechitah should permit it—no; for it is the form of an impure animal and it has no hooves; and Scripture says "hoof"—to teach that nevertheless it is kosher since it is the form of a behemah, and the boundary of behemah is that it should have hooves.
