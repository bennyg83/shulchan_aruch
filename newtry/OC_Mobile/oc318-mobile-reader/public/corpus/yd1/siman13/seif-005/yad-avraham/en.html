And behold the reason for this law is explained in the poskim—that is, because from the father's side it is already slaughtered, and from the mother's side it was not yet slaughtered; and it is as if in the midst of its shechitah—and therefore it has no remedy through shechitah. And it requires study: therefore regarding an offspring whose father and mother are both sons of cloven pakuot who do not require shechitah at all, and it is not cloven—for it requires shechitah by rabbinic decree—from one side, father or mother, it is already fully slaughtered, and from the other side it is not slaughtered rabbinically—whether its own shechitah helps. And one may be lenient here, since without this, according to Rashi, Rif, and Rambam, Rav Mesharshia's view does not exist—one may be lenient in such a case.
