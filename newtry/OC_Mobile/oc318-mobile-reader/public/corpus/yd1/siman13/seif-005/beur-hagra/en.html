(Seif 5) And some say, etc. Therefore he cited the form of a dove—for everything depends on the form of a species that has a hoof; and Tosafot there s.v. almah, etc.; and in Yerushalmi chapter 8 of Terumot: one who slaughters a behemah and finds in it a pig—it is permitted in eating; a dove—forbidden in eating, as it says "behemah—in behemah you may eat," and not fowl in behemah.
Even if its hooves, etc. Not precise—for regarding this there is no one who argues; it is the Gemara in Arukh there; "if so, closed," etc.—behold the tanna taught, etc., "hoof," etc.; and so there 68b: "two hooves you may eat," etc., "one," etc.; and this is what the Rambam and Shulchan Aruch mean: "what has a hoof"—and Tosafot did not add except that even if it has no hoof at all—end of his words.
(Likkut) Precise, etc. For they raised a dilemma in Niddah 23b: if the form of a person was found and it was not resolved—and for stringency. Teshuvot HaRashba (end of his words).
