What has a hoof. For it is written "every one that parts the hoof," "of the behemah you may eat"—the matter depends on the hoof, and it does not suffice that the species of behemah has hooves—this is the Rambam's view. And "some say" is the view of Tosafot and the Rosh: even if it has no hoof at all, if that species has hooves it suffices.
