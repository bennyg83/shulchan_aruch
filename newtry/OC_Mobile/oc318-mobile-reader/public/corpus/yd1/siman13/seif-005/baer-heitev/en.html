Hoof. And the Shach explained: everything depends on the form of a behemah: if it has the form of a behemah, even if it has no hooves at all it is kosher; and if it is the form of a fowl, even if it has two hooves like a behemah—even so it is treifah. But this hagahah is astonishing—for behold in seif 2 it is written that cloven hooves are more significant: even if it spread on the ground—see there.
