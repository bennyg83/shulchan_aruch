Or to cut off a limb from them—the Pri Chadash wrote that so it implies from the Gemara too: "all that you are commanded regarding its blood, you are commanded regarding its limbs," etc. And one may be precise a bit: if so, behold the Gemara challenges: and for R' Yehudah why does he need a verse?—the prohibition of eiver min hachai applies to a gentile; and what is the difficulty—perhaps it comes to permit fish and locusts? Rather, in truth no verse is needed for all this—for in the beginning of chapter Kol HaBasar it is explained that fish flesh is not called flesh either in Torah language or in people's language—"only flesh with its soul, its blood you shall not eat" is written regarding behemah and cattle.
