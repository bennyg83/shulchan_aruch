(Seif 1 in hagahah) And it is permitted to eat them dead, or to cut off a limb from them and eat it. In Pri Toar he attacked what the Pri Chadash wrote: the reason regarding fish that a limb from a live animal is permitted is because we say in chapter 5 of Pesachim: "all that you are commanded regarding its blood, you are not commanded regarding its limbs." And this is not proof—for this derivation is only to include impure species that are forbidden; and whence do we know the verse also comes to teach us a leniency to permit a limb from a live fish? And it was hidden from him that in Sanhedrin (daf 59b) they derive to permit a limb from a live creeping thing from the verse of Noach—for their blood is not distinct from their flesh, that afterward it is because of blood. If so, all the more so fish, whose blood is entirely permitted. And what Pri Toar wrote: if we say the verse comes to permit fish—one could ask for R' Eliezer: perhaps for this it comes and not to forbid impure species—there is no question; for how can we say for leniency and not for stringency? And further, for leniency we learn from Noach's verse.
