Shechitah. And the Taz wrote: likewise there is no prohibition of eiver min hachai; and likewise no issue of oto v'et beno; and it is permitted to slaughter it on the day its father or mother was slaughtered.
Shechitah. And the Taz wrote: this shechitah is by rabbinic decree, lest one confuse it with other behemot; however b'dieved we need not be stringent, and it requires study for practical law; and in Nekudot HaKesef he ruled to forbid even b'dieved.
It (the mother). And the Shach explained: such as other treifot that are not due to slaughter, which are not publicized so much—they do not forbid it; but treifot due to slaughter, such as shehiyah or derisah, etc.—are as if he did not slaughter at all; for since they required shechitah for it, it needs proper shechitah. However according to the Taz b'dieved one does not disqualify it.
