(s.k. 6) And some are uncertain, etc. Meaning: that it was pakuah, etc.—here he followed the Pri Toar and erred in this, as the Shach wrote in s.k. 7; and I showed this matter in Lublin at the German fair the Taz for the first chapter before several rabbis of the generation, and the matter reached him and he wanted to correct this on a page he printed at the end of his work, where he wrote—and this is his language: but afterward I saw in Maharshal's words that this is not needed, only the son alone, son of ben pakuah, etc.—and these words are astonishing: why need he see Maharshal's words? Whoever has a heart to understand will see the commentator errs in the opposite direction; also it did not help at all—for in any case he did not say in letter tet, meaning that of "cloven son of cloven mother" that I mentioned regarding "and some are uncertain" in seif 2—end of his words—he followed the Bach here to bring proof for his law, as the Shach wrote in s.k. 14; and these words are not corrected, as the Shach wrote there; and therefore one must also retract from this and say this is not proof.
