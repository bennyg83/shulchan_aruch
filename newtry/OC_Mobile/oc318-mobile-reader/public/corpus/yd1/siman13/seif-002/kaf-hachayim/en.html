(b) There. Fish and locusts do not require shechitah. Regarding fish it is written of them: "if you gather all the fish of the sea for them"—an ordinary gathering suffices; and this is written in a place where shechitah is mentioned regarding behemot. And locusts are equated to fish, as written "and to every living creature that swarms in the waters—these are fish; and to every creature that crawls on the land—these are locusts." Taz s.k. 1. And these are the words of Beit Yosef—see there.
