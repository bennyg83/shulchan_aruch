Eight months. See She'elat Yaavetz part 1 siman 25, who wrote that all distinctions said regarding a large animal between eight months and nine months apply also to a small animal between four months and five months—for a small behemah gives birth at five months—see there.
Nine months dead. See responsum Radbaz Chadashot siman 190: his view is specifically if found dead it is permitted in eating; but if it died after it went out into the air of the world—even though it did not spread on the ground—it is forbidden in eating. And he wrote that the Ravyah's view is to distinguish also regarding one that was torn: its mother's shechitah permits only what occurred inside it as treifot, but what occurred afterward—not. But I concede to him regarding dead but not regarding torn—see there.
Requires shechitah. Av Beit Din; and see responsum Bashamayim Rosh siman 283, who wrote one does not bless on its shechitah—see there; and see Pri Megadim and Av Beit Din below siman 19 s.k. 1.
Does not require shechitah. See Parshat Derachim on Derech HaAtarim derush 1, who wrote shechitah is not needed for it, but nechirah is nevertheless needed—for it is no worse than one that convulses—below siman 27—see there.
Some other strange matter. See Hagahot Ashiri in Chullin chapter HaBehemah HaMakishah what he wrote there: a canopy is so, and people wonder like that of Abaye who said all agree regarding cloven ben pakuah that it is permitted, etc.—see there; and it requires study for practical law; and see what I wrote below siman 16 s.k. 4.
