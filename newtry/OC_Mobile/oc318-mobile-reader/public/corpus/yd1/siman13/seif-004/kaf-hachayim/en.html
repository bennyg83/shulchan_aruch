(d) There in hagahah. Or to cut off a limb from them and eat it. And we would say they are not forbidden because of eiver min hachai—such as cutting a limb and rinsing it well and eating it, for there is no bal teshaktzu in it. Pri Chadash section 1, Shulchan Aruch HaRav section 1, Zivchei Tzedek section 5; and one need not wait until the fish or locust dies from which this limb was taken, as required for a slaughtered behemah—as below siman 27—for they do not have so much life as something that requires shechitah. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. Mishnat Hasidim section 3, Beit Av section 17 section 3. But one must wait until the limb dies. Minchat Yosef section 1.
