(Seif 4) A nine-month-old alive, etc., and it grew and came upon a regular behemah and engendered—that offspring has no remedy through shechitah. This is the statement of Rav Mesharshia in chapter HaMakishah (daf 75); and the Rif and Rambam omitted this. And Beit Yosef wrote: I do not know why; and in my humble opinion they explain like Rashi there—that Rav Mesharshia argues against Rav, who said "four simanim the Merciful One koshered"; for according to Rava, its own shechitah would help. And therefore, since they ruled like Rava, who is later, Rav Mesharshia's statement is rejected and we do not hold like him. And similarly in the Ran, siman 14.
