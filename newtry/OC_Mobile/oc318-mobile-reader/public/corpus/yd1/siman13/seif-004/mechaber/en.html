A nine-month-old live fetus found in the intestines of a slaughtered kosher animal: if it grew and came upon a regular animal and engendered—that offspring has no remedy through shechitah. And if it came upon a daughter of ben pakuah like him—behold his son and son's son until the end of all generations are like him, and all require shechitah by rabbinic decree, and treifot do not disqualify them.
