Torat HaAdam cites a baraita: between attending a funeral and a circumcision, circumcision takes precedence because this is a mitzvah for the living and that a mitzvah for the dead. Between a funeral and a bride, the bride takes precedence. Between a house of mourning and a banquet, the banquet takes precedence. If one has limited means, however, supporting the house of mourning takes precedence. Between a mourner and groom, the mourner takes precedence because the groom eats and feeds others, whereas the mourner does not eat until others feed him. See Beit Yosef.
