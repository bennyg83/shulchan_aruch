A met mitzvah takes precedence. Tur concludes that a met mitzvah even overrides Torah study and Megillah reading. Rosh cites this from the Gemara in the second chapter of Ketubot. Although even an ordinary deceased person can override Torah study in appropriate circumstances, Tur specifically mentions a met mitzvah because the Gemara there discussed additional priorities unique to it. Yevamot defines a met mitzvah as one who calls and no one answers. Tosafot in Nazir 43 explain that if the deceased has relatives who would answer his call, he is not a met mitzvah. See below siman 374, seif 3, where the formulation differs.
