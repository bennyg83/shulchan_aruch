Forbidden. Because he goes and gives thanks before an idol on its festival day.
A thing that endures. Until its festival day — that he sees it before him on the festival day and goes and gives thanks — Rashi.
And if it is a loan with interest, etc. Tosafot wrote without qualification that with a loan on collateral too it is like saving from their hand, for many times debts are lost even with collateral, end quote. Beit Yosef wrote that the reason appears to be because loans nowadays are with interest. To me it appears the reason is because nowadays their hand is strong over us, and a non-Jew can easily fabricate a claim and deny the amount of the loan and the like. So too it implies in Ateret Zekenim; see there. However, nowadays in every case it is permitted for these reasons stated below at the end of this siman. So too wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham.
