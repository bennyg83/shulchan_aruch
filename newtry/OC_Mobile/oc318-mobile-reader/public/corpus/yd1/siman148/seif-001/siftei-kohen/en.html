Forbidden. Because he goes and gives thanks before an idol on its festival day.
A thing that endures. Until its festival day — that he sees it before him on the festival day and goes and gives thanks — Rashi.
And if it is a loan with interest, etc. Tosafos write without qualification that even a loan secured by collateral is considered rescuing property from their hands, because many times such loans are lost despite the collateral. End of their words. The Beit Yosef writes that the reason appears to be that contemporary loans are made with interest. It appears to me, however, that the reason is that nowadays their power over us is strong, and a non-Jew can easily fabricate a claim and deny the amount of the loan and the like. This is likewise implied by Olat Tamid. See there. In any event, nowadays the transaction is permitted in every manner for the reasons stated below at the end of this siman. Rabbeinu Yerucham likewise writes this.
