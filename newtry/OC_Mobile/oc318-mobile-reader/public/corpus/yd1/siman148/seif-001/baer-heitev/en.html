That endures. Selling them an enduring item is forbidden because they rejoice to possess it on their festival. Buying an enduring item from them is also forbidden, even though losing the item pains them, because they retain the money. It is therefore permitted to sell them a non-enduring item, since on the festival they will have neither the money nor the object. Taz.
And to lend them. Borrowing applies to an object that must be returned in its original form. Lending applies to money, which is given for expenditure.
With collateral. Tosafot writes generally that even a loan secured by collateral is considered rescuing one's money from their hands, because such debts are often lost despite collateral. Shach suggests that this is because their political power over us enables a non-Jew easily to make false accusations or deny the debt. Nowadays, however, all such transactions are permitted, as stated at the end of the siman.
