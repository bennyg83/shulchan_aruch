A law of the festivals of elilim; it contains 12 se’ifim. Three days before the festival of non-Jews, it is forbidden to buy from them or sell to them an item that endures, but it is permitted to sell them an item that does not endure until the day of their festival, such as vegetables and a cooked dish. And likewise, it is forbidden to lend and to borrow, and to lend them (without interest) (Tur and the poskim), and to borrow from them, and to repay them, and to collect from them a loan that is with a promissory note or on collateral. But a loan by word of mouth one may collect from them, because it is like saving from their hand; and in their time, when their power is strong, even with a promissory note it is considered like saving from their hand. And if it is a loan with interest, even on collateral it is considered like saving from their hand.
