To greet first. Taz asks what was exceptional about Rabban Yochanan ben Zakkai, of whom the Gemara says no one ever greeted him first, even a non-Jew in the marketplace, if halachah itself forbids initiating a greeting. Semak explains that the restriction concerns 'shalom,' one of Hashem's names, or a blessing invoking Hashem's Name. Another ordinary greeting is permitted. Rabban Yochanan ben Zakkai's distinction was that he initiated even other greetings. Taz.
