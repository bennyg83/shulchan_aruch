To precede. Taz wrote: at first glance it is hard—for we find in the Talmud in Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi that no man ever preceded another in peace, even a gentile in the marketplace—what is Rabbi Yehoshua's novelty? The law is so! I later saw in Semak: specifically peace which is the name of the Holy One, blessed be He, or blessing in the language of the Name; but another blessing there is no concern. Accordingly Rabbi Yehoshua's case is resolved—he preceded peace to a gentile in the marketplace, meaning even in another blessing language; and this matter is correct.
