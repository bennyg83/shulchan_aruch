(Collected) Regarding Rabbi Eliezer, etc. The Rambam and the other authorities. The Rosh writes there, siman 1, as do the other authorities; see Rashi 7b, s.v. “In the Diaspora,” etc. He further writes that we fear them, and therefore there is no distinction between Eretz Yisrael and the Diaspora. See there, 11b, s.v. “In the Diaspora,” etc. The other authorities, however, write in accordance with the Rambam, as above (end).
