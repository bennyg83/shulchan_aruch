Non-Jews who are going to their festival in the outward direction: it is forbidden to conduct business with them; but those who are coming [back] are permitted, provided that they are not bound together, for if they were bound, perhaps their intent is to return. But if a Jew is going there in the outward direction, it is permitted to conduct business with him, while on the return it is forbidden. And if a Jew is an apostate to idolatry, even on the outward direction it is forbidden.
