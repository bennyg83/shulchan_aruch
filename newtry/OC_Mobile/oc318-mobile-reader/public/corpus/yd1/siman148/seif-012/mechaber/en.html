There are those who say that all these matters were said only in their time, but in our times they are not expert in the nature of elilim; therefore, it is permitted to conduct business with them on the day of their festival, and to lend them, and all other matters. {Rama: And even though they give the money to the priests, they do not make from it an offering or ornament of elilim; rather, the priests eat and drink with it. And furthermore, there is here concern for enmity if we separate ourselves from them on the day of their festival, while we dwell among them and need to conduct business with them all year; therefore, if one entered a city and found them rejoicing on the day of their festival, he should rejoice with them due to enmity, for this is like flattering them (all in Tur). Nevertheless, a ba’al nefesh should distance himself from rejoicing with them if he can do so without causing enmity in the matter (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran). And likewise, if one sends a gift to a non-Jew in our times on a day that they have a sign, and his gift will reach them on that festival, if he can, he should send it from the eve; and if not, he should send it on the festival itself (Terumat HaDeshen siman 195).}
