It is forbidden to send a gift to a non-Jew on the day of his festival unless it is known that he does not acknowledge elilim and does not worship them. And likewise, if a non-Jew sent a gift to a Jew on the day of his festival, he should not accept it from him; and if he is concerned due to enmity, he accepts it and throws it before him into a pit or into a place of destruction, in an unusual manner.
