One who slaughters, etc. Gemara, first chapter of Pesachim 7b; and Yerushalmi, chapter 9 of Berachot: one who slaughters must say "Blessed are You... upon the shechita."
Before. Gemara there and Yerushalmi there, and like Rabbi Yochanan, and as written below.
And if he slaughtered, etc. Tosafot on Zevachim 31b, s.v. shehashechitah, etc.; and Rosh and Shulchan Aruch; and in chapter 2 of Berachot (15a)—and not regarding a berachah, etc. (Likut) And if, etc.—chapter 1 of Terumot, five, etc.; and Ittur there (end of his words).
And if he slaughtered, etc. Yerushalmi there: when does one bless on it? Rabbi Yochanan said: before the shechita; Yossi ben Nehorai said: after he slaughters—lest the shechita become invalid from now; one examines—the presumption is that the intestines are kosher; it appears that there is a defect, but according to all, after examination where there is no defect we hold like Rabbi Yochanan, and as written in Pesachim there that we are not concerned lest the shechita be ruined.
