Kosher. And the Bach wrote that one should penalize him not to eat from it himself; but for others it is permitted. Ben Paku'a that spread out on the ground and requires shechita from doubt—he blesses on the shechita (Peri Chadash); and he concludes in kaf alef that if there is no expert there for examination, it is permitted to make it nevelah without a blessing; but if there is one expert there for examination, he must slaughter and examine, even if his intention is only to feed gentiles, for it is fit for him.
On the shechita. And the Taz wrote: and some are accustomed to slaughter with it a kosher shechita, and this is preferable.
On the shechita. And the Shach wrote: some wish to say that even on a kosher behama, if he forgot and did not bless before shechita, he blesses after shechita; but the Shach disagrees on this and wrote: wherever one must bless and did not bless initially, he has no remedy; and the Peri Chadash wrote that within the time of speech after shechita he can bless; if he is obligated to slaughter for a gentile and is not expert in laws of shechita, he is not permitted to bless, since he will not end up eating from it—see responsa Sha'ar Efrayim siman 57; and if he spoke he need not bless again, even if he spoke within the shechita itself; nevertheless l'chatchila he must be careful (Maharam Tiktin).
