(Seif kaf tet) One may say "those who say," etc.—this is not so, for the agreement of the poskim is like the first view; and so all the acharonim. In Siftei Kohen, seif kaf gimmel, one must pass the pen over "and further clear proof: they taught," until "and so generally where this reason does not apply he does not bless"; and so one must read: and so it appears from the words of R' Yonah, chapter "Three who ate" there—that the same applies to all other berachot that are before doing them: if he did not bless he does not bless afterward, see there; and also in Piskei Rekanati siman 72, etc.—thus it should read. And further I now found proof for my words from what Kol Bo wrote and the Beit Yosef brings in Orach Chaim siman 432: if he did not examine before he begins to examine, he blesses all the time he has not finished his examination—for every mitzvah that has duration, wherever he blessed before the end of the mitzvah, it is "before doing it," end of his words; and so Rabbeinu the Rambam there in the hagahah: specifically all the time he has not finished his examination he blesses, but not afterward; and so it appears from the words of the poskim regarding Birkat Eirusin, see there.
