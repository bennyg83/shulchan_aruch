One may bless one [blessing] and discharge his fellow—it appears that initially permission is given to do as he wishes; and so in Orach Chaim siman 8, seif 5, regarding tzitzit: either each for himself or one discharges his fellow; but in laws of milah siman 288 he wrote regarding two circumcisions for two infants—one blesses and discharges the second; and a reason is needed why this differs from that; and it is strained to say: since milah is done for many in assemblies and there is more "multitude with the glory of the king," like Beit Hillel's reason in the chapter "Elu Devarim" regarding a candle—that one blesses for all; not so in other matters where it is not usual to be in a multitude—they were not so concerned; and still requires further study.
