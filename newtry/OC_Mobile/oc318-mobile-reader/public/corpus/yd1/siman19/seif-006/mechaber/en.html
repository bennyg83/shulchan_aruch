One who blesses with the intention of slaughtering one animal and afterward they brought him more—he covers the blood of the first and blesses on it, and blesses on the second shechita but not on the covering; and this applies when they brought them to him before he blessed on covering the first. (The Beit Yosef's opinion, and Rashal, siman 205, seif 8:) {Rama: And some say that if they brought him of the same species that he slaughtered first, he need not cover the first and bless on the second shechita (Tur in the name of Ba'al HaItur) (and such is the essential ruling; see in Orach Chaim siman 206, seif 1):}
