(6) And if he forgot and slaughtered and did not bless before shechita—he does not bless after shechita (Shach, seif kaf gimmel; ShakhneHag in Hagahah Tashbetz, section 4; Peri To'ar, section 3; Kereti, section 3; Shemen Chafetz, section 1; Pri Chadash, section 2; Shulchan Gavoah, section 3; Orach Chaim, siman 4, section 2; Chokhmat Adam, general rule 5, section 1; Pithei Teshuvah, section 3, in the name of responsa Tiferet Tzvi siman 3; Zivchei Ra'anan in Temidim, section 4). And the reason: since he was fit to bless beforehand and pushed himself aside—for since it was pushed aside it was pushed aside (Zivchei Ra'anan there). And even within the time of speech after completing shechita he does not bless (Kereti there; Shemen Chafetz there; Orach Chaim there; Zivchei Ra'anan there; Zivchei Shalom, section 1; Zivchei Tzedek, section 2). And although there is an opinion that he blesses—they are not difficult, for one does not bless because he holds so. However, if possible, he should return after another behama or bird to slaughter and bless on it and intend to discharge this one too (Shemen Chafetz there; Lechem Yehudah there; Zivchei Ra'anan there; Zivchei Tzedek there).
