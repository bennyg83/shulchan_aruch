To slaughter many—see Peleti that I raised: if it becomes treifah there is a safek whether he must bless anew; therefore he should leave it for another slaughterer to slaughter, and then he can slaughter.
And if he spoke, etc.—it is an interruption; even though we hold regarding shofar that speaking between one tekiah and another is not an interruption—for there it is one mitzvah; here there are two mitzvot.
It is not an interruption—and the acharonim agreed it is an interruption, for they are two mitzvot, and it is like tefillin of the arm and of the head; and since it is a safek in berachot, if there is another slaughterer he should slaughter another and bless and intend to discharge him—and thus he exits the safek.
