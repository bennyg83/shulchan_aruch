One who slaughters must bless beforehand, etc.—meaning we are not concerned lest it become invalid in his hand, nor lest treifah be found—for we establish the presumption of kashrut, and the slaughterer does not desire nevelah (Peri To'ar, section 2; Zivchei Tzedek, section 2).
