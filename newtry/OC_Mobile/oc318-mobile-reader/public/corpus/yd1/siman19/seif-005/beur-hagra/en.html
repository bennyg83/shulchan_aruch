He must take care, etc. Because of a berachah that is not needed, as written in Yoma 70a and in Sotah 41a.
(Likut) If he wishes, etc.—because of a berachah that is not needed, as written in Yoma 70a; old Tosafot there, s.v. mishum, etc.—from here one may learn, etc.; and Tosafot on Chullin 87a, s.v. umichsei, etc. (end of his words).
And if he spoke, etc. As written in chapter 3 of Menachot (36a) regarding tefillin.
He must cover. As written there—Rabbi Yehudah in the mishnah, chapter 6, 2b; and even though we do not hold like Rabbi Yehudah, because one berachah suffices, as written in the Gemara there; and Tosafot, s.v. umichsei.
But on, etc., because, etc. As stated—Rabbi Yehudah concedes, etc.; here it is possible to slaughter, etc.; and the same here; and this is what is meant by "but if," etc.; and as stated regarding shechita.
And some say, etc. For it is not similar to tefillin—for there are two obligatory mitzvot; but here, if he wishes he slaughters and if he wishes he does not—it is like speaking in the middle of a meal.
