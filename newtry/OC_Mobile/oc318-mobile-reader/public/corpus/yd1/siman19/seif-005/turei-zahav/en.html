And if he spoke he must, etc.—for he holds that between one shechita and another it is like [one who] spoke between [putting on] tefillin and tefillin, where he must return and bless; therefore he must complete the first shechita and cover its blood; and there is a printing error—it should say: he must cover the blood of the first shechita and bless on it, and bless again, etc.
Because the shechita is not an interruption. Explanation: between the coverings—for his intention was on the second covering at the time he blessed on the first, and there is no interruption here, for it is possible to slaughter with one hand and cover with one hand; and the blessing on the second shechita is also not an interruption, since he did not speak idle words of Chullin as we find in Yoma.
To return and bless. Explanation: on the second covering, as with an interruption between slaughterings.
And some say that speech, etc.—he holds that it is different between tefillin and tefillin, for there are two obligatory mitzvot, and since he began them it is not proper that he interrupt and occupy himself with speech; but here, if he wishes he slaughters and if he wishes he does not slaughter—he is not obligated to return and bless, and it is like speaking in the middle of a meal; therefore he need not cover the blood of the first at all, but he slaughters more and covers everything at once; and since the Shulchan Aruch did not decide, we hold like those who say that a safek in berachot is lenient; therefore one must take care not to speak between one shechita and another so as not to bring himself to a safek.
