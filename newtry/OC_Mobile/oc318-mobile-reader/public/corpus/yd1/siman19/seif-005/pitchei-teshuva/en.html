He should bless at a distance of four amot. See in Tashbetz, part 2, siman 43, that his view is not so—for one does not bless on a mitzvah unless it is prepared before him to do immediately; and if there is no concern lest the butcher change his mind and not slaughter, therefore it appears he must slaughter first and immediately after shechita go outside and bless the berachah in a pure place—for since they bless, it is fine; and so one should do for the blessing on covering. Nevertheless he did not decide this except for halachah and not for practice, see there. And see in Magen Avraham siman 166, seif kaf gimmel, who also disagreed with the Rambam for another reason—because the berachah must be specifically in its place, and even the final berachah must be specifically in its place—all the more so the first berachah; and in the Agudah there it deals where everything is in one house, see there. And see on this in responsa Tiferet Tzvi, part Yoreh De'ah siman 3, at length.
