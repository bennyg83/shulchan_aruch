He must cover the blood of the first shechita, etc.—for since he spoke about other matters that are not required for the shechita and must return and bless on the second shechita, he must complete the mitzvah of the first shechita; therefore he covers and blesses again on the second shechita, but on the second covering he does not bless, since at the time he blessed on the first covering his intention was to slaughter and cover more; and the shechita is not an interruption, for it is possible to slaughter with one hand and cover with one hand; and the blessing on shechita is also not an interruption, as we find in Yoma—so too all the poskim.
And some say that speech, etc.—and the agreement of the poskim is that it is an interruption and he must return and bless; and so too Maharshal there siman 9, and so wrote Darkei Moshe, and so in Sefer Lechem, and the Ittur, and all the acharonim.
