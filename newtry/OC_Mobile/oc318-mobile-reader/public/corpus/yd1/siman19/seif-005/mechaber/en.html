If he wishes to slaughter many [animals], he must take care not to speak between one shechita and another about a matter that is not required for the shechita; and if he spoke, he must cover the blood of the first shechita and bless again on the shechita, but he does not bless on the second covering. {Rama: Because the shechita is not an interruption; but if he spoke in between, it is an interruption and he must return and bless (Kol Bo, Ashrei, and all the poskim).} And some say that speech between one shechita and another is not an interruption.
