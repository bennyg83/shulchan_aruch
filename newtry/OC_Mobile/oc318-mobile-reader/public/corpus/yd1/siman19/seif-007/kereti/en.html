And l'chatchila, etc.—and the acharonim wrote that a permanent slaughterer is as if his intention is on all they bring him; and so it is customary among Jews that they slaughter korbanot and bring them to one another and the slaughterer does not bless; but even in this they act lightly, interrupting with speech and the like between one shechita and another—and it is fitting to protest.
