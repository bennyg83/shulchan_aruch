They brought him more. Explanation: of another species—according to Ba'al HaItur nearby; and for the Rosh and other poskim, even of the same species.
He need not return and bless. This is the Semag's view; and the Beit Yosef gave a reason: whatever is still before him, he is fixed to slaughter; but when he finished it is a departure and comparable to what we say: one who finished eating is forbidden to eat until he blesses; and even though we do not hold so there, a meal is different, for it is usual to continue from eating to eating; but regarding shechita, when he finished it is a complete departure; and the Tur disagrees on this and holds that what remained before him from the first ones does not avail, since at the time of the blessing they were not before him; and the Beit Yosef decided like the Semag.
And l'chatchila it is good, etc. Rashal wrote: and for a city slaughterer, always consider his intention to be on others; and whenever they bring before him and he did not slaughter in the meantime, he need not return and bless, end of his words—it appears even of two species.
