To slaughter. And the Shach wrote: the same applies if one was before him to slaughter and after he blessed, before he slaughtered, they brought more before him.
For him. The Shach wrote that a permanent slaughterer—certainly his intention is on all they bring him, even of two species, and he need not bless as long as he did not slaughter in the meantime (Rashal and Bach). And if he has many birds to slaughter and blessed at the beginning of slaughter and another comes to take from those birds to slaughter— that other must bless (Hilkhot Ketanot siman 115).
