He must take care not to speak between the blessing and the shechita about a matter that is not required for the shechita; and if he spoke, he must bless again. (But it is permitted to speak between the shechita and the covering [of the blood].) (Mordekhai, chapter 25, in the name of Sefer HaTerumah.) (Nevertheless, it is good not to speak.)
