Between the shechita and the covering. For he holds that covering is a mitzvah in its own right; but some say that covering completes the shechita; therefore the Rambam wrote that it is good to take care not to speak, since he is in the middle of the mitzvah; even though if he spoke he need not bless again—even within the shechita itself—nevertheless l'chatchila it is proper to be careful in this.
