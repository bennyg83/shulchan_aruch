Interruption. And the Taz wrote: but on the covering he is obligated to bless, because he forgot and thought he would not slaughter more; and that which in seif 5 regarding interruption through speech one need not bless again on the covering—there his intention was initially at the time of the blessing on covering on what he would cover afterward, for he knew he would slaughter afterward (Maharam Tiktin).
Blessing. And the Shach wrote: but according to what we hold below siman 28 seif 20 that one must examine the lung before covering—if so he will interrupt greatly between one shechita and another and will need to bless again; in my humble opinion he should slaughter the behama first and afterward cover the chaya.
