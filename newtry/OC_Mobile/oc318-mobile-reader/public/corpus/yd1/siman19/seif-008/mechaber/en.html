If he was slaughtering an animal or bird and his intention was to slaughter more and he forgot and covered [the blood] and blessed—when he returns to slaughter he need not return and bless on the shechita, for the covering is not an interruption. {Rama: And the same law applies if he slaughtered a chaya (or bird) and wishes to slaughter a behama—he covers the blood of the chaya and slaughters the behama without a blessing (Kol Bo):}
