And he forgot and covered, etc.—and regarding the second covering see what the Ittur wrote, for they are words of reasoning.
He covers the blood of the chaya, etc.—and according to what we hold below siman 28 seif 20 that one must examine the lung before covering, if so he will interrupt greatly between one shechita and another and will need to bless again; in my humble opinion he should slaughter the behama first and afterward cover the chaya; and it is possible to say regarding slaughtering between one chaya and another when he changed his mind—that he must cover the first chaya first, for there it is different since he must return and bless; and so it appears in Ashrei and the poskim; not so here—and all this is possible if he does not hold the law below siman 28, or it is possible he did not descend to these laws and came only to teach us that covering is not an interruption, and we learn from it regarding a bird that he mentioned there too; and so it appears from his words there—and examine this.
