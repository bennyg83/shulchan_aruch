To slaughter more and he forgot and covered, etc.—thus it should read.
He need not return and bless on the shechita. And regarding the blessing on covering he did not mention anything, as he mentioned above regarding the law of interruption through speech or through changing his mind—that one need not return and bless on the covering; for there his intention at the time of the blessing on covering was on what he would cover afterward, for he knew he would return and slaughter; but here he forgot and thought he would not slaughter more—certainly he must bless afterward on the second covering, which is a new mitzvah that came to him, as stated.
And the same law if he slaughtered a chaya, etc. This deals with one who at the time of the blessing on shechita also intended on slaughtering the behama, or the behama was before him at the time of slaughtering the chaya—according to the Semag nearby; therefore he need not bless on slaughtering the behama; rather it teaches us that he covers the blood of the chaya first before slaughtering the behama.
