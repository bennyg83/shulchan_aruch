Or a dragon and it resembles a serpent etc. This form was venerated among them because they attribute it to one of the divisions of the celestial sphere, and it is the form of the lunar node — end Rambam there; and similarly wrote the Mordechai, whom Beit Yosef brings.
If the vessels are honored they are forbidden. And it appears that if they are neither honored nor dishonored but middling, they are forbidden — for if they were honored they would certainly have been made for idolatry, as the Tur wrote; if so there is here only a single doubt. And the Tur wrote below in this siman: it appears all those forbidden when found are forbidden even sunken, since we suspect perhaps made for idolatry, even sunken there is concern — end; but R. Yerucham wrote one authority wrote and so in Tosafot — they forbade only honored ones when protruding — end; brought by Beit Yosef.
That are on silks etc. The Tur's language: some say silk garments and embroideries are considered dishonored since they are for wearing; and they interpret "shira'im" as a bracelet; and the Rambam counted them among the honored; and it is good to be stringent — end; and since the mechaber did not bring any explanation, it implies his opinion is to be stringent.
Kumkumot. Small metal pots — Rashi. Hot-water heater. Meaning a vessel for heating water. Below the water. The bottoms and sides; above the water — meaning the rim of the vessel — Rashi.
But when it is known these vessels were not worshipped. Even though they worship those forms when on the vessels, permitted; and obviously even per this view if it is known these vessels were worshipped, even the dishonored ones forbidden.
And there are some who say etc., even though we do not know these vessels were worshipped. Since this form is certainly worshipped, we say presumably even the dishonored ones were worshipped, unless we know certainly these vessels were not worshipped — then even per this view even the honored ones are permitted. And per this view there is no distinction between honored and dishonored except in the unqualified case — meaning most gentiles are accustomed to worship it; but where it is not known if this form is worshipped there at all, or where it is known it is not worshipped in that place, or it is not known that most gentiles are accustomed to worship it — even honored ones permitted. All this is proven from the Ran.
And the custom is like the first view. Meaning: regarding forms that gentiles worship nowadays, we follow the first view — investigate.
And nowadays, when gentiles do not worship these forms etc. Refers to above — sun, moon, and dragon form — for nowadays permitted for benefit to the finder, even per the last view, since gentiles do not worship those forms; but one must not leave them because of suspicion.
But forbidden to leave them with him because of suspicion. Even nowadays; and as the Rav wrote nearby — one must not leave them.
