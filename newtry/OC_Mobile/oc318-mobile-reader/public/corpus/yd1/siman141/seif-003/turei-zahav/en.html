And dragon form permitted to make. So is the set Talmud chapter "all images" (Avodah Zarah 42) at its end: "but if one makes dragon form — who forbade? Behold it is written 'do not make with Me gods of silver'" — these are not dragon; and I wonder on the Beit Yosef who wrote the source of this law — because it is not among things mentioned in the Gemara for prohibition; and Rashal gave a reason why dragon was not included in "do not make" like heavenly servants — because the dragon in the heights flies in air and is not in heaven. And Maharach challenged from Mordechai in the name of R. Elyakim the Beit Yosef brought at end of this siman in what we say "all faces permitted except human face" — behold "all" is not precise, for sun, moon, and dragon forms are in the prohibition since they are considered painters and worshippers — end; appears dragon also forbidden to make because of "do not make with Me" like sun and moon (and difficulty) [and no difficulty] — there it refers to leaving them, for it explicitly concludes "since they are considered painters and worshippers" — and this applies to a gentile who made it for this and Israel finds it, as stated chapter "all images" (Avodah Zarah 42); and although R. Elyakim there deals with Abaye who establishes it in making — his intent there is: even according to Abaye who is lenient and establishes it even in making and a fortiori to leave it completely permitted — nevertheless "all" is not precise etc.; so one must say there for one who investigates his words.
