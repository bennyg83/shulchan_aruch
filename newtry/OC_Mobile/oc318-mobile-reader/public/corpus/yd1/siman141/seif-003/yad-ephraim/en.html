And see there how his father the Gaon, called from the congregation of Sephardim, had in his hand to merit them to receive the light of His countenance, and his peaceful men could not seduce him and turn him from the Supreme opinion to allow drawing his portrait and anointing with oil; even though in color depiction most decisors as all hold there is no prohibition concern — all the more half a face alone there is no concern in such a case — nevertheless he did not want, although they pleaded much because of his piety and holiness; and they did not rest; and through a wondrous craftsman all depiction of his awesome face was made with tremendous alacrity not in his presence; and the light of his countenance did not fall and nothing of his likeness was lacking except the breath of life; and forms were made from it etc. — see there.
