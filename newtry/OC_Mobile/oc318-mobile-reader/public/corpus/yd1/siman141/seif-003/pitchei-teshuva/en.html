For the sun. [See Baer Heitev. And see in responsa Chatam Sofer siman 129 regarding a synagogue made with a round glass window like a sun form with protruding sparks around the circle like sun sparks at sunrise, and within it is written the name Havayah, and on its side to the east "from sunrise to sunset praised be the name of God"; and he wrote that they did two evils in error: it is explained in Shach siman 141 s.k. 8 that a sun circle — although finding is permitted for benefit, nevertheless at least the maker transgresses Torah prohibition and one who leaves transgresses rabbinic prohibition because of suspicion; and in public too it is ugly; and here it is worse, for it is written explicitly on its side "from sunrise" etc. — behold he says he intends the sun form itself; and worse than what Turei Zahav wrote (in Beit Yosef s.k. 23) regarding zodiac forms in prayer books; and even Nekudot HaKesef there admits here; and this secondly — even if not written on its side "from sunrise", nevertheless this is anthropomorphism etc.; and especially in a place of holiness and in the east where all bow toward it upon entering the synagogue door — certainly ugly; therefore it appears to me one who can abolish this will merit healing in His wings — end.]
