For the sun. Chatam Sofer condemns a round stained-glass synagogue window designed like the sun, with rays around it, a divine name inside, and the verse 'From the rising of the sun.' Making the solar image may violate Torah law, retaining it raises suspicion, and the inscription proves intentional depiction of the sun. Its placement in the east of a synagogue, toward which people bow, makes the matter especially objectionable. He urges its removal.
