If the utensils, etc. This follows Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, since the Gemara's discussion proceeds according to his view.
The silken garments. This is the Rambam's view. The Ramban, however, wrote that silken garments are considered degraded because they are made to wear out. This is also stated in the Jerusalem Talmud, whose reading is “bracelets,” and this is likewise the reading of the Rif, Rosh, and the other authorities, in accordance with Rashi. The Jerusalem Talmud states: Rabbi Karuspedai said, “This is a garment of degradation.”
Coins. From Tosafot there on "that upon" etc.; and so in the Tosefta chapter 7.

If it is certainly known that they are not worshipped, even honored items are permitted. Rather, we are dealing with an unspecified case.
