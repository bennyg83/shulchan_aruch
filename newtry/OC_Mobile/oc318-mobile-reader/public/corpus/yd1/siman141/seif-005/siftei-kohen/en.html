Forbidden to leave it. For he is leaving a protruding form which is forbidden because of suspicion; but permitted to sign with it, for the impression made becomes sunken.
Even if a gentile signed for him. A protruding form is forbidden because of suspicion; and already explained in s.k. 17 that likewise forbidden to tell a gentile to sign in a ring whose seal is sunken, even if he does not want to leave it, because telling a gentile is rabbinically forbidden; nevertheless needs investigation why the Rav wrote this law since the mechaber wrote it in seif 4; and perhaps one could have distinguished between the gentile making it with his own hands and making it with his seal — the Rav teaches us no; and this is forced.
