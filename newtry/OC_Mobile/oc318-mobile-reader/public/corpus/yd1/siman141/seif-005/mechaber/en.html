A ring that has upon it a seal that is the form of a man: if the form is protruding, it is forbidden to keep it, but it is permitted to seal with it. And if the form is recessed, it is permitted to keep it, but it is forbidden to seal with it, because the imprint will make the form protruding (the language of Rambam, Hilkhot Avodat Kochavim 3:11). And even if a non-Jew sealed for him, it is forbidden to keep it (so it appears in Mordechai and Tosafos). And the same applies to other forbidden forms (Tur; and so wrote Ran in the name of Rif; and so it appears in all the poskim).
