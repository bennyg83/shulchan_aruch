To leave it. A ring with a protruding seal may not be retained because it contains a protruding image, but one may use it for sealing because the resulting impression is recessed. Shach.
The non-Jew. As explained, it is likewise forbidden to instruct a non-Jew to seal with a ring whose seal is recessed, even if one does not intend to retain the resulting protruding image, because instructing a non-Jew is rabbinically prohibited. Shach.
