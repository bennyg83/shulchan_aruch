To form. All the more so to retain them. Beit Yosef cites Rabbeinu Elyakim that one should not make such forms in a synagogue, lest it appear that people bow to them. Bach prohibits individual forms of a lion and ox because these correspond to constellations in the lower firmament, Leo in Av and Taurus in Iyar. Shach responds that this reasoning would forbid each of the twelve zodiac signs individually, though no such practice is known. Rather, the prohibition of the constellations refers to all twelve together, like the four faces together. Taz criticizes those who depict the zodiac in prayer books for the prayer for rain. Nekudot HaKesef disagrees, since the drawings are educational and not complete forms.
