To form. And all the more to leave them. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of R. Elyakim: one should not form in a synagogue these forms, lest it appear as bowing to them. And Shach wrote that the Bach in Kuntres Acharon forbids a lion and ox form; and his claim is that the constellations are in the lower abode, and the form of a lion and ox stands each alone in the month of Av and Iyar — end of his words. And accordingly all twelve constellations are forbidden to make, even each one alone — and we never heard of anyone careful about this. Therefore it appears that when we say constellations are forbidden, we mean all twelve together, similar to four faces together. And Turei Zahav wrote that those who depict in prayer books for rain the form of constellations — they do not do well, since they intend to make the form of constellations. (And in Nekudot HaKesef he challenged him and wrote the matter is simple to understand and rule; and further it is not a complete form.)
