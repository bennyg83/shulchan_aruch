Forms of animals and beasts. It appears even a lion permitted; and so the Rashba ruled in responsa siman 167 — see there; and Shiltei HaGibborim copied his words; and so appears somewhat in Tosafot Avodah Zarah 43a on "do not make" — see there. And the Bach in the last pamphlet forbade; and his claim: behold constellations and stars are in the lower abode and forbidden; constellation of lion stands alone in month of Av and ox in Iyar — forbidden to make — end of his words. And per his words all twelve constellations forbidden even each alone; and we never heard anyone careful; and also in Mordechai and Agudah chapter "all images" in dispute of R. Efraim and R. Elyakim — Maharshal's reason that standing in month of Av alone does not forbid lions — see there; therefore it appears when we say constellations forbidden, we mean all twelve together, similar to four faces together — so it appears to me; and see in Rashba's responsa siman 325 and Bedek HaBayit and responsa Maharit part 2 siman 30 page 12:4.
