All images etc. In the Mishnah Rabbi Meir and Sages dispute — R. Meir holds since that form was worshipped in one place we decree other places on its account; and Sages did not decree unless he has a sword etc., as written here in Shulchan Aruch; and nevertheless it appears even if he has a sword it does not help for prohibition except for a form we know they worship such a form — similar to R. Meir's prohibition because of decree; and if so there is no distinction whether that form is human or other form. And in the Tur below in this siman it is written "human form standing" etc. "in its hand a staff" etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote here it appears from his words specifically human form, but not other forms — his intent: for human form the reason is known they worship it, not so other forms; but if we had clarification they also worship other forms, the law would be the same; therefore the Rambam wrote and Shulchan Aruch copied here "and in the hand of the form" etc., and did not mention human form for the reason we said. And it appears what the Gemara says: Rav Yehuda said Shmuel said regarding kings' monuments — there too it teaches idolatry, known they worship it; as Rashi wrote. And Maharach wrote: there is no reason for what Shulchan Aruch wrote plainly "form" — difficulty: is it his? Behold this is Rambam chapter 7 Laws of Avodah Zarah; and the intent is to what he said.
