That we presume. Shach explains this where it is known that no one bowed to the image. Pri Tevuah permits utensils engraved with idolatrous forms through compounded doubt: perhaps the image was nullified, and perhaps it was made merely as a memorial. Additional grounds include that the image is sunken, sale may constitute nullification, and ordinary eating utensils are not honored vessels. Rabbi Yitzchak of Krakow agreed.
