When making. Shach writes that all the more so retention is forbidden. He previously explained that the phrase 'but not making' does not refer to a dragon, because even a complete dragon may be made and only retention is forbidden because of suspicion. Yet Tosafot appears to distinguish completeness only for a human image, implying that a dragon is always forbidden. Semag rules that even an incomplete human image is forbidden. One who is stringent in all this is praiseworthy.
