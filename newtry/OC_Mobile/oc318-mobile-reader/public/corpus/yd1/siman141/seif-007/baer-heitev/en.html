In making. Shach wrote: and all the more in leaving; and in s.k. 63 I wrote that "this and not in making" does not refer to a dragon, for regarding a dragon even a complete form is permitted to make, and the prohibition is only to leave because of suspicion. Nevertheless difficulty: from Tosafot chapter 25 43b on "it is different R. Gamliel" it appears explicitly that specifically for a human we require a complete form, but for a dragon there is no distinction and it is always forbidden. And in Semag he ruled that even for a human there is no distinction and it is always forbidden; one who is stringent in all this — praiseworthy — end of his words.
