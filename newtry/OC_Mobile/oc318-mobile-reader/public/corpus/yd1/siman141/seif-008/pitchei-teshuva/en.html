And stars. See in responsa Dibrei Yosef siman 8, asked if they painted on the inner doors of the synagogue sanctuary star forms in spices and gold dust — if there is any side of permission to maintain them; and although this is neither protruding nor sunken, and as Maharam said, nevertheless from the Rambam's words it appears depiction in color has one law like sunken, and one should be stringent per his words (and not as he said in s.k. 5 in the name of Knesset Yisrael). And even according to decisors that in public there is no suspicion — that is specifically where there is no holiness like that of R. Gamliel who had in his attic; but in a synagogue which is a holy place designated for prayer — plainly we are more concerned for suspicion than an individual in his house; therefore one must erase them and remove the stumbling block. And do not answer me from that monument in the synagogue etc. — it is different there, for it was placed under compulsion etc. — see there where he extended; and per my humble opinion it does not appear so from the Gemara's words there in Rosh Hashanah 24, which challenges "and do we suspect suspicion" — "behold that synagogue" etc.; and answers "public is different" — there it is explicit plainly the main permission is because public is different; if so it appears explicitly not like his words. [And it is possible to reconcile; therefore one should be stringent; and without this, Shach already wrote in the name of R. Yerucham that although in public there is no suspicion it is ugly; and see in Pri Megadim Orach Chaim siman 90 s.k. 6 from this.]
