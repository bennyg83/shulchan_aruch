And stars. Divrei Yosef rules that painted stars on the inner doors of a synagogue sanctuary should be removed. He treats painted images like sunken ones according to Rambam and argues that suspicion is especially severe in a sacred place. Pitchei Teshuva questions this from the Gemara, which expressly distinguishes a public setting, but notes that Rabbeinu Yerucham still calls such decoration unseemly even where suspicion is absent.
