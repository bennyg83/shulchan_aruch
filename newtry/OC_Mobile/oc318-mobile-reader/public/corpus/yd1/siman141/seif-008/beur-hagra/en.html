At the measure etc. Rashi there; and Tosafot Yoma on "the above".
And even without etc. As we said in Menachot 25:1 "it comes with gold, it comes with cups" etc. ; plainly without this there was a menorah; and as forbidden in all metals because they are not essential.
And not even etc. For height too is not essential; and as we said in Zevachim 62:1 the altar's height is not essential; and although height is written in the verse ; all the more a menorah where it is not written; and we do not hold "House, Temple form" etc. ; specifically at its length measure etc.; as above, there it is not recognizable except at the measure; and so from that he distinguishes between seven branches and eight ; it appears seven in all cases forbidden; and see there in Maharik who extended.
