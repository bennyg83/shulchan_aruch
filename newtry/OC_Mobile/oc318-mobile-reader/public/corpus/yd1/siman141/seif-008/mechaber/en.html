One should not make a house in the form of the Heichal, according to its height, length, and width; an achsadra in the form of the Ulam; a courtyard in the form of the Azarah; a table in the form of the Shulchan; and a lamp in the form of the Menorah. But one may make one with 5 branches, or 6, or 8; but one with 7 he should not make — even from other kinds of metals — and even without cups, knobs, and flowers, and even if it is not 18 tefachim high.
