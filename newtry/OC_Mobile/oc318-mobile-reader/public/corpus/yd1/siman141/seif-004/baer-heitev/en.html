Faces. Rashi explained: faces of ox, human, lion, and eagle for one living creature — likeness of the holy creatures — end; and therefore after Yechezkel came and turned the ox into a cherub, it is forbidden to make their four faces together, for they are included in "do not make with Me" — the servants before Me. Alternatively, even now when there is no ox in the chariot, nevertheless it is forbidden to make four faces with an ox among them as there was when the verse was said. Shach.
For ornament. And some say for a gentile — and both are words of the Living God, for since the verse was particular about making, there is no distinction — Shach. And Turei Zahav wrote: he asked him — why do I need a four-faced form together? Behold even a human form alone is forbidden. The Ran answered: he thought that because he combined other forms with the human form it would be permitted — he informs us that nevertheless it is forbidden — end. But it is difficult: if so, if he made a human form with one or two faces of the four, it would also be permitted, for there is neither human form alone nor four faces; and this does not appear to permit such a novelty, and the decisors would certainly not be silent about it without informing that it is permitted. Rather it appears there is a difference regarding if he diminished — it does not help if he corrected the human form alone, and he must diminish all the forms — end of his words. (And in Nekudot HaKesef he wrote that it was omitted that as the Ran's words, Tosafot in Rosh Hashanah 24b and the Ramach wrote, and the Kesef Mishneh brings it.)
To leave them. Because of suspicion; and likewise forbidden to tell a gentile to make them even if he does not want to leave them, because telling a gentile is rabbinically forbidden even regarding a matter that is not of Shabbat. Shach.
Allowed. P.S. with pleasure, provided that they do not have them in his hands. “Third:
Sunken. For also in the firmament they are sunken. And Shach wrote: it appears that all forms we forbid are forbidden only in a complete form — such as a human form with two eyes and a complete nose and the whole body and the like; but not half the depiction as some painters paint one side of the form — and this is not forbidden — end of his words. And Turei Zahav wrote in the name of the Ramban: all that is forbidden from the Torah because of "do not make with Me" — meaning the four faces in the heavenly abode, and the likeness of angels, sun and moon etc. — there is no distinction between sunken and protruding, and all are forbidden. And for halacha, although from the mechaber's words it does not appear so, nevertheless one should not be lenient at all against the Ramban; also the Tur concludes so, and so the Ran — end of his words.
