And likewise human form alone. Derived from "do not make with Me" — "with Me" — likeness I show prophets — meaning human. And if you challenge: above we said four faces together forbidden — derive from human? Tosafot already answered in Avodah Zarah (43a) and Rosh Hashanah (24) on "do not make with Me" — it comes to obligate even finding human form who completed to four faces; and so answered in hagahah Semak siman 162 — "or to transgress two prohibitions"; and in Rosh Hashanah they further answered: he thought if he made four faces it is worse than human alone; and so answered the Ran and Ramach — Kesef Mishneh brought them laws of idolatry; and see s.k. 30.
Forbidden to make them even for ornament. And so Rambam chapter 3 and in old authors' books written even for a gentile; and so in the Tur; and both are words of the Living God, for since the verse was particular about making, there is no distinction between making for ornament or idolatry — forbidden.
Forbidden to leave them. Because of suspicion; and it was already explained in s.k. 17 that likewise forbidden to tell a gentile to make them even if he does not want to leave them, because telling a gentile is rabbinically forbidden even regarding a matter that is not of Shabbat.

However in protruding etc. Meaning: in protruding specifically forbidden both in leaving and making; but in sunken permitted to make them; and all the more to leave; and as written in seif 5 regarding a ring with human form — if its seal protrudes permitted to sign, if sunken permitted to leave; reason: forbidden only similar to them that are protruding; and from Torah what is depicted on a wall and board — although flat, called sunken since not protruding like themselves; therefore sun, moon, and similar forms that are also sunken in the firmament forbidden even sunken. The Magen Avraham wrote: specifically those forbidden only in making because of "do not make with Me" — in finding permitted because there is no way to make them for idolatry — not forbidden in making except protruding; but sun and moon customarily worshipped — forbidden in making, finding, protruding, and sunken; for in finding forbidden lest worshipped; and since we suspect perhaps idolatry was done, even sunken there is concern; and in making although no Torah prohibition except protruding since they appear protruding in firmament — nevertheless forbidden to make even sunken because of suspicion of idolatry — end; and his words are wondrous, hanging a baraita on what is not taught; and what relevance finding permitted to sunken permitted, and vice versa. Investigate yourself: if there is proof in four faces together or human made for worship forbidden in finding — shall we say therefore forbidden in making and leaving even sunken? But we forbid only protruding; and vice versa sun and moon nowadays permitted in finding — shall we say permitted sunken? Certainly not; and further already explained in s.k. 8 sun and moon literally — their circle permitted for finder and nevertheless forbidden to make even sunken — see there; and further wonder how he changed the known that sun and moon appear sunken in firmament — as Tosafot, Rosh, Ran, Semag, Tzeda Laderech, Shiltei HaGibborim, and other decisors wrote — he wrote they are protruding; and it seems the Tur misled him — the Tur wrote: it appears all those forbidden in finding forbidden even sunken, since we suspect they were made for an idol, even sunken there is concern — end; he thinks meaning all those forbidden in finding because customary to worship them — sun and moon although protruding (if sunken anyway forbidden) forbidden sunken even in making; and the matter is simple this interpretation does not exist; rather meaning wherever we forbid in finding — meaning because there is proof for an idol they were made — no distinction in finding between protruding and sunken; since we forbid for finder because made for idol, if so forbidden for finder both sunken and protruding; but making and leaving do not depend at all on what is forbidden in finding or not; and as he wrote regarding making and leaving everything depends on the thing itself — if human and similar that is protruding, forbidden only protruding; if sunken forbidden even sunken; and it is simple — I did not need to extend except to remove from the reader's heart in his book: And it appears all forms we forbid are forbidden only in a complete form — such as human form with two eyes and complete nose and whole body and similar; but not half the depiction as some painters paint one side — it appears to me.
