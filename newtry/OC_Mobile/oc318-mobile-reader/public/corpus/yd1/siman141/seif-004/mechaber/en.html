It is forbidden to draw forms of the realm of the Shechinah, such as 4 faces together; and likewise forms of serafim, ofanim, and malachei ha-shareis; and likewise the form of a man alone — all these it is forbidden to make, even if they are for ornamentation. And if a non-Jew made them for him, it is forbidden to keep them. {Rama: However, if one finds them, they are permitted, except for the sun and moon, which it is the manner of non-Jews to worship, or where there is proof that he made them to worship them — for then it is forbidden like all images, as was explained at the beginning of the siman (Tur).} In what case is this said? Regarding a protruding form; but a recessed form, like those that they weave into a garment, or that they draw on a wall with pigments, is permitted to make. But the form of the sun, moon, and stars is forbidden, whether protruding or recessed. And if they are for the purpose of learning, to understand and to teach, all are permitted, even protruding (and some permit for many, for there is no suspicion) (Tur in the name of Rosh).
