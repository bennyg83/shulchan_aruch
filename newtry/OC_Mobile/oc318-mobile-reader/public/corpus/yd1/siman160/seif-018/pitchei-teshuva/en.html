Of orphans. See responsum Maharit part 1 siman 144 who wrote permitted for orphans to lend orphans' money of other orphans at rabbinic interest; and all the more when document issued on orphans that father borrowed at rabbinic interest from other orphans we extract from orphans to orphans — see there; see responsum Maharshach part 3 siman 2 in name of Mahara of Monsheim that even though permitted for orphans to give money at rabbinic interest, nevertheless if father gave at rabbinic interest and died orphans cannot collect rabbinic interest though permitted — see there; see Mishneh Lamelech chapter 4 Laws of Lending who brought and rejected his proof; nevertheless concludes for law, etc.
