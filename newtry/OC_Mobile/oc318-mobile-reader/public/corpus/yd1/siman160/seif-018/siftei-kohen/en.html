Permitted. Nevertheless if the recipient claims "I received only half in profit and half in loss" (and not close to profit and far from loss) — the Mordechai wrote in the name of Ra’avyah and R. Baruch that the recipient is believed by a migo of "it never happened" or "I returned it to you"; and so is the main ruling, unlike Ra’avan there; however where the orphan is in possession — such as he has collateral — the guardian is believed if he claims close to profit and far from loss; or if the guardian is not before us we argue the orphans’ claim up to their value — so in responsum Maharil siman 36; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it.
As long as the orphan has not come of age etc. And the same for an adult whose father died and who does not know how to manage his affairs — he is considered an orphan; however one whose father does not want to support him and manage his needs does not have the status of an orphan, and it is forbidden to invest his money close to profit and far from loss — Terumat HaDeshen there; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it; and Beit Yosef wrote that widows — even though generally they do not know how to manage their affairs — do not have the status of orphans, for they manage like other women and we do not require them to manage like men — end; and Darkei Moshe brings it.
