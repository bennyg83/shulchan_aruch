The lender must be careful not to benefit from the borrower without his knowledge, as long as his money is in his hand, even regarding something that he would do for him even if he had not lent to him. But if he benefits from him with his knowledge, it is permitted regarding something that he would do for him even if he had not lent to him, provided that it is not something public — for example, to live in his courtyard and to use his slaves.
