(Seif 13) Nor may he say. Regarding the expression, "Whoever gives will not lose," the Nimukei Yosef and the Hagahot Maimoniyot write in the name of the Ramban that it is forbidden. This is also the view of the Rashba. Rabbeinu Yerucham, in part 1, writes that some prohibit it and others permit it. These are the words of the Beit Yosef.
