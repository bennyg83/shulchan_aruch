One should not do labor for his fellow on condition that his fellow will afterward do labor with him that is heavier; and even to do with him the same labor is forbidden if it is at a time when it is heavier — for example, this one weeds with him in the “garid,” and this one weeds with him in the “revi’ah.” {Rama: And if one lends money to his fellow for a certain time in order that he will return and lend to him another time for the same amount of time, some say it is forbidden (Mordechai, at the end of perek Eizehu Neshech; and a responsum of Rosh, klal 108, siman 16), and it is not comparable to doing labor with him and then doing labor with him again, for regarding a loan he is taking the wage of the loan. And there are those who say it is permitted if he is not lending to him for a longer time than he lent to him (Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer HaTerumos, and Mordechai in the name of Maharam). And see below siman 177.}
