A minor is forbidden even if not dependent, because the father remains obligated to support him. Although a minor not dependent upon his father is considered an adult regarding found property, here the father effectively receives the benefit because he must support the child if the child lacks funds.

The permission applies only when the transaction is carried out through a Jew. Through a non-Jew it is forbidden, because the rule that there is no agency for sin does not apply when the agent himself violates no prohibition, and descendants of Noach are not prohibited from interest.
