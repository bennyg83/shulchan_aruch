It is written in Masat Moshe, Yoreh Deah, responsum 18, concerning trustees of charitable funds who lent money at fixed Torah-prohibited interest. The borrower initially refused to pay, but they compelled him, and the proceeds had already been distributed to poor people throughout the world. Since the borrower has no party from whom to recover the interest, the trustee is not personally obligated to reimburse it from his own funds. See there.
