That you give a zuz to so-and-so. Chavot Yair permits paying a third party a monthly fee to serve as guarantor where the lender receives no benefit and genuinely requires security. If the arrangement is a contrivance to enrich that person, it resembles 'give a dinar to so-and-so and I will lend you' and is forbidden. Once the guarantor has paid and extends time to the borrower for compensation, the payment may become fixed or rabbinic interest according to the authorities.
