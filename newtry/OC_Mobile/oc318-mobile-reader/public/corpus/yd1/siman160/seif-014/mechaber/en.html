It is forbidden to say, “I will lend you a maneh on condition that you will give a zuz to so-and-so” (or to hekdesh), even if that so-and-so is a non-Jew, even though he does not owe him (Tur, siman 169). And interest that is “fixed” (meaning, he fixed and stipulated to give such-and-such because he is lending to him) is interest that is by Torah law. (And there is no difference whether the lender said so, or whether the borrower said so of his own accord and he lends to him because of this) (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba).
