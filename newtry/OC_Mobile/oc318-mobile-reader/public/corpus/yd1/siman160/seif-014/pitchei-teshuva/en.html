It is fixed interest. Rabbi Akiva Eiger questioned whether the third-party recipient must return what he received. Tiferet Tzvi rules that he need not, because it was given as a gift in response to the borrower's instruction; the lender's claim is against the borrower under guarantor principles. If the recipient was expressly told that the payment was being made in place of interest owed to another, return may be required.
