(In the gloss) But rabbinic interest is permitted. If a person entered into partnership with orphans, and the administrator lent the funds, some with rabbinic interest and some through a fully permitted arrangement, one may say that the partner may take from the profit earned through the fully permitted arrangement an amount corresponding to what the orphans take from the profit earned through rabbinic interest. In rabbinic matters, we maintain that retroactive designation is effective. See Responsa Maharit, part 1, siman 116.
