(Gloss) But rabbinic interest is permitted. If one entered a partnership with orphans and the guardian lent the funds, some through rabbinic interest and some through an entirely permitted arrangement, it may be argued that the partner may take from the fully permitted profit an amount corresponding to what the orphans take from the profit generated through rabbinic interest. For in rabbinic matters we maintain that retroactive clarification applies. See Responsa Maharit, part 1, siman 116.
