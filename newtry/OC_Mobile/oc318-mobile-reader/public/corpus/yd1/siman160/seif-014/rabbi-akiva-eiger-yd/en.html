(Seif 14) or to hekdesh. And even to say lend you one hundred and give one hundred twenty to hekdesh forbidden.
(Taz s.k. 8) and if not important person no prohibition. See Ran (first chapter Kiddushin) who wrote that which with Levi they acquire with utensils of seller — in that benefit receiving from him even if not important person — nevertheless benefit less than perutah; and in chalifin they acquire with utensils though not perutah works even non-important; and that for Rav they do not acquire with utensils — learn even important person because gives intending to return not considered receiving benefit — see there; if so one can say Rav does not disagree with Levi and even non-important called at least receiving, etc.
