(Siman 160, Taz, seif katan 4) And what he wrote afterward, 'and certainly he gave it to them as a gift,' meaning that he returns and gives it to them, etc. He strained without need, because the latter clause discusses a case in which he borrows from them. Likewise, what he wrote that 'it is forbidden to lend' means to arrange a loan from them, etc., is incorrect. Its meaning is straightforward. In truth, he confused the wording of the Tur with that of Shulchan Aruch. In the Derishah he wrote this concerning the Tur, which did not state, 'and likewise to borrow from them.' But in Shulchan Aruch, everything follows its plain meaning. This is clear.
