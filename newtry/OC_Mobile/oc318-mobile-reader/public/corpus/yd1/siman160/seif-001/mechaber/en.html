The severity of the prohibition of interest and how far one must be careful regarding it; it contains 23 se’ifim. One must be careful regarding interest, for how many prohibitions were stated regarding it; and even the borrower, the one who gives it, the guarantor, and the witnesses transgress. {Rama: And there is no difference between whether he lends to a poor person or to a rich person (Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 4 of Hilchos Malveh V’Loveh). And that which the borrower transgresses is specifically regarding interest that is by Torah law; but regarding interest that is by rabbinic law, he transgresses only because of lifnei iver, etc. (Ran, perek Zeh Borer; and Nimukei Yosef, perek Eizehu Neshech; and so wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham, nesiv 8, cheilek 1).}
