In responsum Tiferet Tzvi siman 48 — and Taz — if lender said I lend you and give maneh to so-and-so — fixed ribbit; then if borrower gave recipient via gift has no claim on recipient and claims commanded via guarantor law (in my humble opinion requires study — since act fulfilled he truly lent — no claim via guarantor only can claim assess lender's benefit how much would give man to heed him give so-and-so maneh — this benefit is fixed ribbit he received must return); but if gave and said I give you because I need give so-and-so for ribbit — properly returns if rabbinic, etc.
