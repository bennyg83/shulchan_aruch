The borrower who gives it. Mishneh LaMelech discusses whether a borrower violates lifnei iver when the lender could lend the same money at interest to another Jew. Penei Moshe is lenient by analogy to handing wine to a nazir who can obtain it independently. Mishneh LaMelech disagrees because another borrower would himself violate lifnei iver and may retract. Even according to those who prohibit rabbinically where the sinner could act independently, the rule may differ for an apostate whom one is not obligated to restrain.
