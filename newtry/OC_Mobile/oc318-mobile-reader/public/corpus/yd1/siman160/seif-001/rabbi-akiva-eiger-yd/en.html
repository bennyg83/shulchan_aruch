(Siman 160 seif 1) And how many prohibitions stated regarding it. Lender transgresses six prohibitions: your money shall not (negative commandment) at interest; and at increase (negative commandment); you shall not be to him as creditor; do not take from him interest; and do not place; lifnei iver; and borrower transgresses two negative commandments lifnei iver; and Hagah Maimoniyot wrote in our version in sugya borrower transgresses negative commandment and to your brother negative commandment; and Mishneh Lamelech wrote and Maharash in responsum (siman 162) uncertain if borrower transgresses from time of loan or only time of giving — see there.
(In Hagah) but rabbinic ribbit lifnei iver. And Maharit in one who swore to give ribbit — oath takes effect and must give — so Rashba in responsum (siman 738) brought in Beit Yosef this siman; however in responsum Ran that even rabbinic law like all who swore to transgress rabbinic — though oath takes effect they do not permit transgressing rabbinic in positive commandment — see there; see responsum Turei Zahav kelal 3 seif 17 — even per Rashba borrower obligated to give but lender not permitted to take and court prevents him from taking; and how is this if rabbinic or not in this, etc.
