(Siman 160, seif 1) And how many negative commandments were stated concerning it. The lender transgresses six negative commandments: "You shall not give him your money with interest"; "nor your food for increase"; "You shall not be to him as a creditor"; "Do not take from him interest or increase"; "You shall not impose interest"; and lifnei iver. The borrower transgresses two negative commandments, lifnei iver and, according to the version before us in the sugya cited by the Hagahot Maimoniyot, "Do not impose interest" and "Do not lend your brother with interest." The Mishneh LaMelech writes that Maharshal, in responsum siman 162, is uncertain whether the borrower transgresses from the time of the loan or only from the time the interest is actually given.
