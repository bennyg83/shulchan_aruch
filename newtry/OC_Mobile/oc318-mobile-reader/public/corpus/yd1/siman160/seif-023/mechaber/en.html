One who lends money on condition that any labor that comes to his hand he will give it to the lender to do — it is forbidden. {Rama: And according to the one who says that benefit is considered money, it is called fixed interest (so the Beit Yosef inferred from the language of Rosh), since they stipulated initially to that; and even if they did not stipulate initially, if he is not accustomed to do so otherwise, it is forbidden. And likewise, any benefit. But if each one sometimes returns a favor to his fellow — sometimes the borrower to the lender, and sometimes the opposite — it is permitted (Hagahos Mordechai; and Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 5 of Hilchos Malveh V’Loveh).}
