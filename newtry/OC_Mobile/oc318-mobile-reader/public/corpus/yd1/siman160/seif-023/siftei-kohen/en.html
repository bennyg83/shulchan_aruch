On condition all work, etc. Meaning lender is craftsman and borrower not craftsman but gives to some craftsman; if needs work forbidden to lend on condition all work that comes to his hand he give lender; but if borrower craftsman and lends on condition do work cheaply — plainly fixed ribbit for all; and so responsum Beit Yehudah part 2 siman 23 page 9b — even where already owed and extended time for this if fixed matter and known rate — fixed ribbit.
