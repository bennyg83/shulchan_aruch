Craft/work. Shach wrote — meaning the lender is a craftsman and borrower is not a craftsman but gives to some craftsman; if he needs some work it is forbidden to lend on condition all work that comes to his hand he give the lender; but if borrower is craftsman and lends on condition he do work cheaply — plainly fixed ribbit for all, even for one who says below siman 166 seif 2 regarding courtyard it is dust of ribbit, since they did not stipulate at giving money — here agrees specifically for dwelling in courtyard he gives nothing though standing for rent is dust of ribbit; but if fixed to give him — no; and all the more for us who hold even courtyard is fixed ribbit; see Orach Chayyim siman 170 seif 3 — end quote; and Taz wrote were it not for the holy mouth of Rosh I would say complete permission, and clear proof from Talmud brought in Shulchan Aruch siman 172 seif 4 — if lender said to borrower if you sell your land do not sell except to me at its value and on that condition I lend you — permitted, and this is exactly this case (and Nekudot HaKesef rejected his proof that one can distinguish — see there).
