(Seif 13) One may say to another: Here is a zuz; lend ten dinars to so-and-so. It appears to me that this does not apply only when he gives the zuz immediately before the loan. Even if he gives it after the loan in exchange for extending the borrower’s time, it is permitted, provided he does not later recover it from the borrower. This differs from the case cited by the Taz and later authorities, where one says, “For as long as so-and-so does not repay the money you lent him, I will give you one dinar each month.” That is prohibited because every additional delay in repayment creates an additional interest obligation, so the third party is effectively lending the principal as well. Here, by contrast, he gives a single zuz once. After writing this I found the point explicit in Tosafot, the Ran, and the Mordechai to Kiddushin 6b, s.v. de’arvach. See there carefully, and see Tosafot to Menachot 90b.
