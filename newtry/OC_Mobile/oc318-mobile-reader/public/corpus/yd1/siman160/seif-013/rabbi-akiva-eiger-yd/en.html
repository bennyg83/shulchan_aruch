(Shulchan Aruch, seif 18) With orphans' money. Mishneh LaMelech, citing Maharit, writes that this applies even to orphans inheriting from orphans. If their father lent at fixed interest and died, the heirs may not collect even interest accrued after his death, which is more stringent than a loan made from orphan funds without stipulation. If orphans lent at fixed interest and did not collect until adulthood, they may collect what accrued while minors, but not what accrued afterward, even if the entire amount was capitalized while they were minors. Maharikash 161, however, permits orphans to collect interest accruing after their father's death on a document that fell to them. He further writes that where the borrower undertook to repay the lender's successors, the orphans may recover fixed interest through the courts as though it had been given after the father's death. Bigdei Terumah writes that an insane person has the status of an orphan because a guardian is appointed over his property. Mishneh LaMelech adds that the same applies to a deaf-mute.
