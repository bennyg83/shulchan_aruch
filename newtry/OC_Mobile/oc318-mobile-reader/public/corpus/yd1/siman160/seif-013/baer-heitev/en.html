And the borrower. Taz writes that if a third party tells the lender, 'As long as the borrower has not repaid you, I will give you one dinar each month,' this is a complete prohibition. See siman 169:16.
From the borrower. All the more so, the lender may not first give a gift to a third party as payment to arrange a loan, where the third party then transfers that gift, or slightly more or less, to the lender. This is full interest. Taz.
He may persuade him. Beit Yosef cites many authorities who permit even where the borrower persuaded the third party, because the third party gives from his own funds and the borrower does not reimburse him, so he is not the borrower's agent. One may rely upon these lenient authorities because the prohibition is rabbinic. Shach.
