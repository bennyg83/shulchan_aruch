It is permitted to say to his fellow: Here is a zuz, etc. It appears obvious that this is permitted only in this manner. If, however, he says, “For as long as that person does not repay you the money that you are lending him, I will give you one dinar each month,” this is an absolute prohibition. Although the ribbis does not pass directly from the borrower to the lender, the one who undertakes to pay the ribbis is considered as though he also borrowed the principal. For as long as the borrower does not repay the principal, the obligation of ribbis rests upon him, and it follows that responsibility for the principal likewise rests upon him. The Beit Yosef writes this reasoning in the name of the Baal HaTerumos. You will find it in siman 169, seif 16, in our comments.
Provided that he does not go back and take it, etc. It appears obvious that all the more so the borrower may not first give the donor a gift as payment for arranging a loan for him from the lender, whereupon the donor gives that gift, or slightly less or more, to the lender. This is absolute ribbis. So too wrote the Perishah.
