Also that the borrower should not persuade him to give it to the lender on his behalf, but rather he should do so on his own without the borrower's knowledge — Tur; and Beit Yosef brought many poskim that even if he persuaded him it is permitted, since he gives from his own, and the borrower does not pay him so he is not made his agent; and he wrote: regarding the law, one who wishes to rely on the words of those who permit is entitled to do so, since it is a rabbinic prohibition and their reasoning is sound.
