It is permitted to say to his fellow, “Here is a zuz, and lend 10 dinarim to so-and-so,” provided that he will not return and take it from the borrower. And also, the borrower should not say to the lender, “So-and-so will give for me.” And there are those who say that it is also necessary that the borrower not persuade him to give to the lender in order that he will lend to him.
