Took. For an ordinary person, once the loan was made through a prohibited arrangement he may not collect the profit even if the borrower earned much. For orphans, the illegal stipulation is disregarded so as not to harm them, and the arrangement is treated as if made in the most favorable permitted manner. Taz therefore allows the orphan to take half the actual profit, even beyond the stipulated amount, after deducting compensation for the recipient's labor. Levush limits recovery to the stipulated amount, but Taz strongly disagrees. Shach writes that the orphans cannot take all the profit merely because the stipulation was illegal, though some responsa imply that if the recipient receives no profit, all may go to the orphans.
