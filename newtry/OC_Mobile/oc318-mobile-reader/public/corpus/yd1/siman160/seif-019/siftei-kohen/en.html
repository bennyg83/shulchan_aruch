If the borrower took as his share as much profit as he stipulated to give them — it is permitted; and if he did not give them he is obligated to give them; but one cannot say that if he earned only as much as he stipulated to give them, they take all the profit — for since they stipulated in prohibition, is their power thereby improved? — Beit Yosef; and so the Taz wrote; and he wrote: it appears to me the same applies if he has more profit than what the fixed interest is — he need not give the orphans more than the fixed interest; but in responsum R. Aharon Sasson at the end of siman 162 he challenged Beit Yosef, for one who examines the Mordechai it implies that even if he does not take any profit he gives everything to the orphans; and so appears also from the responsum of Maharil that Beit Yosef brought — see there.
