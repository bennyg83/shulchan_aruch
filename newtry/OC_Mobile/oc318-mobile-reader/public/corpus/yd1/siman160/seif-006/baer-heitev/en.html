Because of. Taz writes that although delayed interest is forbidden even without an explicit condition, here the gift precedes the loan and is forbidden only when expressly connected to it. Shach writes that even when explicit, it is only avak ribbit and cannot be recovered through beit din.
Substantial. Beit Yosef writes that even if the borrower regularly gave substantial gifts before, a substantial gift shortly before the loan may be forbidden. If the gift is separated from the loan by a long interval, there is no concern when given without explanation. If explicitly connected to the loan, even a small gift is forbidden despite a long interval. If he was not accustomed to give it, even an unspecified gift is forbidden. Maharshal writes that anything clearly given only because of the loan is treated as a substantial gift. Shach concludes that all depends upon the circumstances and the giver's evident intent.
