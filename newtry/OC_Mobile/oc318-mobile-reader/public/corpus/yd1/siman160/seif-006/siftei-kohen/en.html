And he specified etc. This refers to advance interest and belated interest; and Beit Yosef wrote it requires study — if he specified, whether it is fixed interest or dust of interest; and Darkei Moshe brings it; and the Taz wrote it is dust of interest and cannot be extracted in court; and so appears to be the main ruling as below siman 166 s.k. 7-8.
Or he is etc. Beit Yosef wrote it is possible even if he was previously accustomed to a large gift — it is forbidden because it is a public matter comparable to dwelling in his courtyard and using his servants as below seif 7; and Hagahot Derishah brings it.
A substantial gift, for ordinarily it is as though he explicitly stated that it is because of the loan. This is the correct reading. But a small gift given without specification is permitted. This is unlike the law above in seif 4, where an unspecified gift is forbidden, because there it is given at the time of repayment. So wrote the Rosh and the students of the Rashba. It implies that where the borrower intends the gift as interest, even without saying so it is forbidden. Sefer HaTerumah likewise writes this, and the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe cite it. See also the Bach. The Beit Yosef writes that this applies only when the gift is given relatively close to the loan, but where a long time has passed there is no concern at all, and even a substantial gift is permitted if it is unspecified. It appears that if he explicitly states that it is because of the loan, even a small gift is forbidden no matter how much time has passed. The Beit Yosef further writes in the name of Hagahos Ashri and Rambam that even an unspecified gift is forbidden when the borrower had not previously been accustomed to give such gifts. Hagahos Derisha cites Maharshal that anything which it is known he would not otherwise have done at all is considered a substantial gift and is forbidden. It appears to me that everything depends upon the circumstances and what is apparent from the giver's intent. On this basis one can say that the poskim do not disagree.
