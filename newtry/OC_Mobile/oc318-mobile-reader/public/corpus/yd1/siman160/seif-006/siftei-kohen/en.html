And he specified etc. This refers to advance interest and belated interest; and Beit Yosef wrote it requires study — if he specified, whether it is fixed interest or dust of interest; and Darkei Moshe brings it; and the Taz wrote it is dust of interest and cannot be extracted in court; and so appears to be the main ruling as below siman 166 s.k. 7-8.
Or he is etc. Beit Yosef wrote it is possible even if he was previously accustomed to a large gift — it is forbidden because it is a public matter comparable to dwelling in his courtyard and using his servants as below seif 7; and Hagahot Derishah brings it.
