It is forbidden to advance the interest or to delay it. How so? If he set his eyes to borrow from him, and he would send him a gift — (and he explained: in order that he will lend to him (Tur; and so wrote the Beit Yosef, in the name of Rosh); or if it is a large gift, for presumably it is as if he explained to him) (there, in the name of Semag) — in order that he will lend to him, this is “advanced interest.” If he borrowed from him and returned his money, and he would send him a gift on account of his money that had been idle with him, this is “delayed interest.” And if he transgressed and did so, this is avak ribbis.
