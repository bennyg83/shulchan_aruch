It is permitted to borrow with interest because of pikuach nefesh. {Rama: And see below siman 169, and in siman 173, that it is forbidden to borrow with interest for the needs of the community, even if it is not fixed interest; all the more so, with fixed interest it is forbidden. And it is not like those who in some places were accustomed to be lenient to lend and to borrow for the needs of the community with fixed interest; they have nothing upon which to rely, unless we say that they consider the needs of the community as pikuach nefesh or for the needs of a mitzvah, as will be explained below siman 172 — but one should not rely on this except for a great need (most of these glosses are the words of the author).}
