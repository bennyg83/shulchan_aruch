(Shulchan Aruch seif 20) and fed the orphans. Implies specifically fed; but if still existing they extract from them; and in Rivash's responsum brought in Beit Yosef clear regarding minors even if still existing do not extract since return only because of mitzvah not lien on property — as below s.k. 61 seif 5 — orphans minors not subject to mitzvah performance; see there; nevertheless appears there specifically when gave minors; but when ribbit still in guardian's hand extract from them; and so Mishneh Lamelech; seemingly we hold one may benefit minor — if so when guardian received for them, etc.
