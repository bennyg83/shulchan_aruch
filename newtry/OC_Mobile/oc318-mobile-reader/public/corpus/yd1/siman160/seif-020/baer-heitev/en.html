Believed. Shach — it appears even if the lender already collected the ribbit he is not believed to say it was hekdesh and he gave it to hekdesh; and if it is fixed ribbit he must return it; and in Maharam's responsum brought by Mordechai it appears the guardian is believed with oath to say they are orphans' money; and if the borrower claims I do not believe you — it is a claim of perhaps and he does not even need oath; it seems to distinguish — specifically when claiming it was hekdesh money he needs proof lest everyone claim hekdesh or other mitzvah money; but regarding orphans' guardian who is known as guardian this does not apply; see CM siman 34 seif 12; and in Maharam responsum regarding Reuven who had charity money of individuals and lent via gentile with document without collateral and Shimon claims regarding ribbit he gave saying it was not charity but his — Reuven believed even without oath that it was charity money — end quote; it seems there different for it was known charity money was in his hand, therefore permitted lest he leave permitted and eat forbidden; nevertheless it seems he is believed without oath only because borrower does not claim certainly; but if borrower claims certainly even though charity money was in his hand he still needs oath; and although below siman 169 s.k. 205 lender exempt without oath — there different for he speaks permissively that he lent and permitted lest he leave permitted and eat forbidden; but here since it became clear he lent at ribbit only saying orphans' money — in such case he needs proof — end quote Shach; and Taz wrote that he is not believed except with proof — it seems plain that as long as he has presumption of orphans' money he is believed before orphans with oath that ribbit came to them and they lose what he returned of this ribbit.
