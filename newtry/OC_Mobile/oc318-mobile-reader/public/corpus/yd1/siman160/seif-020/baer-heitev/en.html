Exempt. Baal HaTerumot writes that it may be recovered through beit din. See Shaarei Yosef, siman 61.
Believed. Shach writes that even after the lender collected interest, he is not believed merely to claim that the money belonged to hekdesh and was transferred there; fixed interest must be returned. A known guardian for orphans, however, may be believed with an oath that the money was theirs. Where charity funds were known to be in his possession, he may be believed that the loan used those funds, especially when the borrower has only an uncertain claim. If the borrower asserts certainty, an oath is required. Taz writes that where the guardian remains in possession of the orphans' funds, he is believed with an oath on their behalf that the interest belonged to them, and they bear any loss caused by its return.
