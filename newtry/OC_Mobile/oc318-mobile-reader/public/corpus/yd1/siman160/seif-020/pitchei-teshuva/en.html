Custom to be lenient. Avnei Nezer in name of Shach; see responsum Noda BiYehudah part 14 siman 40 who wrote thus should be practiced — though per Rashi needs court because forget in their hand to declare recipient's property against orphans then needs important court and now we have no important court therefore not practiced thus; and this reason of custom Shach brought — nevertheless main law like those who disagree with Rashi; and wrote thus I practiced several times giving orphans' money close to profit far from loss and wrote in iska document with recipient that this orphans' money received close to profit, etc. — see there.
