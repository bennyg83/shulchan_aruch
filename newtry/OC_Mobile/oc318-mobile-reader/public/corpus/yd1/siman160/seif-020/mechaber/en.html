If the apotropos transgressed and lent the money of orphans with fixed interest and fed the orphans [with it], they are exempt from paying, even when they grow up. {Rama: And the apotropos also is exempt from returning it (a responsum of Rashba), and the same applies to money of hekdesh for the poor or other mitzvos (Beit Yosef in the name of a responsum of Rashba). However, the lender is not believed to say that the money was money of hekdesh, except with proof (Rivash, siman 465). And see below siman 169 whether an apotropos is permitted to borrow from a non-Jew for the needs of orphans.}
