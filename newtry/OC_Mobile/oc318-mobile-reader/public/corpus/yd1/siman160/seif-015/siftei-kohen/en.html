Some say etc. And the Bach wrote that there is no prohibition here, but rather it is a scribal error in R. Yerucham — it should read "that he give me four dinars" — and it teaches us that even if the borrower did not say "give him four dinars so that he will lend to me," but rather persuaded him to give me four dinars and because of this lent to him — it is forbidden, for it appears as if he was his agent — see there; and one should not corrupt the books for nothing; rather the reason of the matter is that since the lender desires the agent's benefit and wishes that so-and-so give him four dinars, accordingly it is forbidden for the agent to say to him to give him the four dinars, for he thereby benefits the lender through his agency — and this is straightforward.
