(Taz, seif katan 16) His words are extremely astonishing. In my humble opinion, the Levush's words are correct and clear. The borrower never undertook to pay more than the amount that had been fixed. It is therefore inappropriate to say that it is as though they stipulated a condition obligating him in something he never wished to undertake. Rather, regarding the amount he did agree to pay, there is an external prohibition of interest resting upon the obligation. Concerning that amount we say that it is as though they had stipulated a permitted arrangement, thereby removing the prohibition of interest. This is clear.
