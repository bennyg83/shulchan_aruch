(Taz, seif katan 16) His words are exceedingly difficult. In my humble opinion, the Levush's words are correct and clear, for the borrower never undertook to pay more than the amount stipulated. It is therefore inapplicable to say that it is as though they stipulated that he become liable for something to which he never agreed. Rather, regarding the amount he did agree to give, there is merely a prohibition of interest resting upon it. In such a case we say that it is as though they stipulated in a permitted manner, thereby removing the prohibition of interest. This is clear.
