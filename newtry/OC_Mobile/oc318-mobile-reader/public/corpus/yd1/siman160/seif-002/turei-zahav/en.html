His property collapses, etc. In the Baraisa at the end of the chapter Eizehu Neshech it states: Not only that, but they make Moshe Rabbeinu into a wise man and his Torah into truth, meaning by way of irony, for they say, “Had he known that there is profit in the matter, he would not have written it.” At first glance this is astonishing, for did he not know that there is substantial profit for one who lends with ribbis? It appears that the explanation is that they say there is substantial profit for the borrower through the money he takes, for he earns much with it, and therefore there is no prohibition upon him if he pays ribbis. This is what those who lend with ribbis say.
