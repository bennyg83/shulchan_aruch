And it is as though, etc. Toras Kohanim, parashas Behar: “I am Hashem your God, Who brought you out of the land of Egypt.” On this condition I brought you out of Egypt, that you accept upon yourselves the mitzvah concerning interest. Whoever acknowledges the mitzvah concerning interest thereby acknowledges the Exodus from Egypt, and whoever denies the mitzvah concerning interest thereby denies the Exodus from Egypt.
(Extract) And in Elki, etc. Gemara there; and Tosefta chapter 6; and Yerushalmi chapter 5 concludes ; learn that one who lends at interest denies fundamentals ; see there (end).
