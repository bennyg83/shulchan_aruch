(Siman 160 Taz s.k. 4) And what he wrote afterward and certainly he gave them as gift — meaning he returns and gives them, etc. — strained to explain end deals when he borrows from them; and what he wrote forbidden to lend — meaning to make loan from them, etc. — not so, he explained plainly and in truth the words of Tur were exchanged with Shulchan Aruch — in Derishah he wrote thus in Tur's words unlike Shulchan Aruch, and also to lend from them; but in Shulchan Aruch everything follows plain meaning — requires study.
