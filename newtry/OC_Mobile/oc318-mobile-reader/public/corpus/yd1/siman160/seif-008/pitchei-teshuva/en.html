Ribbit devarim. See She'elat Yaavetz part 2 siman 47 who wrote important person forbidden to accept gift from one who lent him while still his debtor, since we hold regarding woman in such case betrothed — learn monetary law applies as giving money; all the more ribbit for we do not require money, even ribbit devarim forbidden. What is called important person — one who does not accept gift from anyone else; if careful with money must be careful here; if does not accept anything even portions of food and drink must be careful here too — see there.
