(There, Shach, seif katan 8) And the Beit Yosef wrote that the matter requires study. The Beit Yosef's uncertainty exists only according to the authorities who hold that prior and subsequent interest are forbidden even when nothing was stated explicitly. According to them, one may say that where it was explicitly connected to the loan, it constitutes fixed interest. According to the gloss here, however, which rules that subsequent interest is forbidden only where it was expressly connected to the loan, there is no room for uncertainty.
