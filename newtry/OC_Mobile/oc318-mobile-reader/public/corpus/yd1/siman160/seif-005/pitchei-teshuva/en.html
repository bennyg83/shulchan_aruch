I give it to you as gift. See responsa Teshuvah Me'ahavah part 1 simanim 54–58 regarding one who says to his fellow I hereby give you a maneh as complete gift from now forever on condition you give me after thirty days one hundred twenty — is there concern of Torah ribbit or rabbinic or ribbit evasion; and all poskim wrote is when it is complete loan and condition was on ribbit to give via gift unlike here where no loan and no remembrance of loan at all — he answered at length and his view tends there is no Torah or rabbinic ribbit.
