I give it to you as a gift. Teshuvah MeAhavah first inclined to treat a gift of one hundred on condition that the recipient later give one hundred twenty as no more than an evasion of interest, because no loan was mentioned. After correspondence, he accepted that it is Torah-level fixed interest recoverable through beit din. Knesset HaGedolah proposed a lifetime-annuity structure through an unconditional gift followed by a separate annual obligation, but She'elat Yaavetz questions this and regards excess recovery as at least an evasion of interest.
