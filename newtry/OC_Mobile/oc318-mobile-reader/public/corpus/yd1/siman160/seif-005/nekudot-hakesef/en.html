(Seif katan 16) I have not understood the full intent of their view, etc. There is no difficulty with Rashba. He means that the claimant need not swear before payment, even if the other party says to him, 'Swear to me,' as I wrote in Shach, seif katan 30. What he also wrote concerning the Levush, that its words are extremely astonishing, presents no difficulty. On the contrary, in such a case even an ordinary person who is not an orphan need not swear. The Levush therefore stated precisely that specifically in such a case he collects without an oath, as I wrote in Shach there. See below, siman 177, seif 9.
