But, etc. This follows from what is stated in Bava Kamma 94b: "The Sages taught: robbers and those who lend with ribbis," etc., meaning that the recipients should waive repayment because otherwise people would be deterred from repentance, as stated there. We see that waiver is effective. Rambam. See there.
(Likkut) But if, etc. Rambam's proof is from the beginning of chapter 9 of Bava Kamma, concerning robbers, etc. Sefer HaTerumah and Hagahos Maimoniyos rejected his proof, because there the Sages confiscated the claim, etc. Nevertheless, Rambam's ruling is correct from what is stated there: "Since they do not accept it, why do the offenders return it?" The answer is: in order to fulfill their obligation toward Heaven. This shows that he fulfills that obligation by offering it and having the other party decline to accept it. Necessarily, the reason is waiver. End.
