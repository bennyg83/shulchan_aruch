Forbidden. Parashah challenged in CM siman 232 — money one bought from fellow and found surplus within what mind does not err need not return — we say he gave gift or robbed and swallowed in account; one can say there deals with sale but in loan we attribute to ribbit not gift; but difficult — Tur (and Mechaber there seif 2) wrote same in loan; it seems there different for he does not mention before him giving surplus which is not so here saying accept from me more than my debt only not mentioning as ribbit, etc.
(b) "We have given him only, and he does not say to him."
