If he lent, etc. Although the Gemara presents the matter as a question, we nevertheless rule that the original status governs. This follows from 62a, where the Gemara asks concerning a se'ah, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. “According to our law,” etc.
(Likkut) If he lent, etc. The Mordechai and Hagahos Ashri write that the matter remains doubtful, in accordance with the wording of the Gemara. Nimukei Yosef at the beginning of the chapter, however, rules like the Rashba and the Shulchan Aruch. He explains that the Mishnah therefore teaches a case of two se'ah for three, which is ordinarily fixed interest, without distinguishing whether the commodity later increased in price or not.
