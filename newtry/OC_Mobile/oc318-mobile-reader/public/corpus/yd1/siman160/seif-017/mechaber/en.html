Talmidei chachamim who lent one another food items, and he gave him more than what he borrowed from him — up to a fifth — this is permitted, for it is known that he gave him only as a gift. {Rama: And some permit even if they stipulated initially that way, provided it is a small matter (Hagahos Ashiri, and so is implied by the Tur, and so appears to be the view of Maggid Mishneh). Nevertheless, talmidei chachamim should not accustom themselves to this, because of the masses, so that they not learn from them (Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 4 of Hilchos Malveh; and Semag, lavin 193).}
