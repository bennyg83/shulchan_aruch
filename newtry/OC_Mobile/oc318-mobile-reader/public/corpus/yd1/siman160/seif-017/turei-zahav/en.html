This is Rambam's language and holds not permitted even Torah scholars to fix initially; and though Gemara Shmuel said to Avahu bar Ihi lend me one hundred peppers for one hundred twenty peppers and Rashi explained long good to do so — learned stipulated initially; Rambam explains long in this matter — if good and proper to do so by my sight and automatically took choice initially; hence great wonder why Tur wrote beginning siman 160 if borrower gives more willingly Rambam permits — for he did not stipulate, etc.
Even if they stipulated, etc. Beit Yosef brought Hagahot Ashiri — if not Torah scholar even stipulated initially as gift forbidden; Beit Yosef explained stipulated to give as gift better than not stipulate at all; brings proof afterward from Ashkenazi responsum written distinction in ribbit for stipulated initially as gift, etc.; per this Rama's language even does not settle; but in truth wonder at Beit Yosef — that Ashkenazi responsum is Maharil siman 37 — deals with one who had orphans' money at fixed ribbit and fed step-orphans, etc.
