(Seif 16) One may say to another person. If one says, "Here is a zuz, and tell your wife to lend me money from her nichsei melog," it requires analysis whether this constitutes fixed interest or rabbinic interest. If he says, "Here is a zuz so that you will waive your rights to the produce, leaving the property in her control so that she may lend me from her nichsei melog," one may say that this is forbidden as an evasion of interest. See Responsa Lechem Rav, siman 202.
