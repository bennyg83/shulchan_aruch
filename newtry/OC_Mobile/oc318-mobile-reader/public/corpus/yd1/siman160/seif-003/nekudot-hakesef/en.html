(s.k. 11) He challenged Rama — and we have no difficulty, for granted where borrower did not receive money himself from lender — since we say no agency for sin it is as if he borrowed from agent and ribbit agent sent to lender is not ribbit for he did not borrow from lender at all, and we do not say since he borrows from him via agent it is as if borrowed from him himself — no agency for sin; but when borrower received money himself — behold he sends him ribbit for the loan and one cannot say here no agency for sin for presumption this ribbit is lender's — and so explained.
