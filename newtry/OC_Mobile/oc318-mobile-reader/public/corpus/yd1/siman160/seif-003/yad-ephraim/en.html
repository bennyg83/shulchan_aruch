He wrote. Responsa Masas Moshe, Yoreh De'ah, siman 18, discusses guardians of consecrated funds who lent the money with fixed ribbis. The borrower initially refused to pay, but they compelled him to do so, and the funds were already distributed to impoverished people throughout the world. Since the borrower has no person from whom he can recover the payment, the guardian is not obligated to reimburse him from his own pocket. See there.
