(Seif 4, in the gloss) It is permitted only by way of sale. The Beit Yosef writes in the name of the students of the Rashba that this distinction applies to a loan, but here, since the transaction is by way of sale, as long as he did not state that the addition was compensation for the use of his money, it is permitted, because he merely sold to him at a reduced price. It appears to me that this refers to a case like the incident in the Gemara, where he purchased from him by way of advance pricing, and when the produce was delivered he gave him additional produce. If, however, one already owes money, there is no distinction between a debt arising from a loan and one arising from a sale. Whenever he pays more than he owes, it is forbidden. The language of the Shach, seif katan 4, does not appear to indicate this. Examine carefully.
