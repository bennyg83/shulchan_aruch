Even to zavim, etc. What is stated there, “and it is forbidden to zavim,” etc., refers to the first challah, as he wrote in his commentary; so too the Rash wrote there in his first explanation. But the second is eaten even by them, as stated in Bekhorot 27a: Shmuel said that terumah of the diaspora is not forbidden, etc.; therefore a niddah, etc.; and if there is no, etc., an adult kohen may eat it. He explains that what it says there about two challot is where there is no minor kohen, as stated there in the Gemara, and this is what is stated in seif 5: challah of the diaspora, etc. But Tosafot there and at the beginning of Chullin chapter 8 wrote that there one always separates two challot, and what it says “and one who immersed that day,” etc., and “it is forbidden to zavim,” etc., refers to the second challah. The sugya in Bekhorot concerns a diaspora place distant from Eretz Yisrael, where the law is as the Rambam wrote; according to the Rambam there is no distinction throughout the diaspora.
