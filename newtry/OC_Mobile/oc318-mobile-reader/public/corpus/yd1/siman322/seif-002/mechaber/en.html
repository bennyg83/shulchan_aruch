They are obligated in challah from the Torah only in Eretz Yisrael alone, as it is said, and it shall be when you eat of the bread of the land; and at a time when all Israel is there, as it is said, in your coming — the coming of all of you, and not the coming of some of you. Therefore challah in this time — even in the days of Ezra in Eretz Yisrael — is only by their words (rabbinic).
