Challah outside the land — even though it is impure, since its main obligation is rabbinic — is not forbidden in eating except to a kohen from whom impurity issues from his body: those who had seminal emission, zavim, zavot, niddot, and yoledot. But other impure persons through contact with impurity — even impure persons are permitted to eat it. Therefore, whether in Syria or outside the land — if he wishes to separate one challah he separates one forty-eighth and it is eaten by a minor who has not yet seen keri, or a minor girl who has not yet seen niddah; he need not separate a second. So too if there was a kohen gadol who immersed from his seminal emission or his ziva — even though he did not wait for sunset (and some say he must wait for sunset) (Ran, chapter Elu Overin) — behold, he is permitted to eat the first challah and need not separate a second outside the land: {Rama: Some say that since challah is not eaten in this time in Eretz Yisrael, in other places too they need separate only one challah and burn it (Tur in the name of R' Yitzchak and other poskim). So is the widespread custom in all these countries — they separate only one challah without measure and burn it, as they did when separating two challot, when the challah of light had no measure; nevertheless the custom is to take an olive's bulk (Maharil). One who eats challah — even outside the land — blesses first on the species he wishes to eat, then blesses Who has sanctified us with the holiness of Aaron and commanded us to eat terumah (Kol Bo, and it is from the words of Rambam at the end of Hilchot Terumot). Some say when burning challah they make a separate fire for it, for an Israelite is forbidden to benefit from it (Maharik, and so in Tosafot, chapter Kol She'ah). The custom is to throw it into the oven before baking the bread.}
