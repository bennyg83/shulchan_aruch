The custom. The Maharshal writes that the reason we do not presently practice giving challah to a minor kohen is that nowadays we do not regard any kohen as certainly established in his priestly lineage, and we do not know which kohen is truly a kohen. Nevertheless, on matzot some have the practice of giving it to a minor kohen, though not during the rest of the year. One must therefore distinguish between Passover and the rest of the year. Shach. The Mahari Weil likewise writes this in the laws of Passover, and so write the later authorities.
