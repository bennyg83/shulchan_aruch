The established custom. See Baer Heitev, who writes that we are not expert in verifying priestly lineage. Rivash, siman 94, writes similarly, as does Maharshaldam, Even HaEzer, siman 235, regarding a captive woman and a chalutzah marrying a kohen. See Maharit, part 1, siman 149, and Chut HaShani, siman 17, who argue extensively against Maharshaldam's position. Shevut Yaakov, part 1, siman 93, rejects their objections and upholds Maharshaldam. Mabit, part 1, siman 219, writes similarly, as does Sheilat Yaavetz, part 1, siman 155, which I cited above in siman 305, seif katan 9. See also Responsa Knesset Yechezkel, siman 56; Responsa Heishiv Rabbi Eliezer, siman 8; Chavat Yair in the additions to page 154 and on page 247; Responsa Zikhron Yosef; and Beit Ephraim, Orach Chaim, siman 5, and Even HaEzer at length.
