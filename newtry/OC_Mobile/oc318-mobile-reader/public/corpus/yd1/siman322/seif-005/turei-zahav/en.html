He need not separate a second challah outside Eretz Yisrael. This applies where a ritually pure kohen is available. If no such kohen exists, the primary challah is burned and a second challah is separated for a kohen to eat even if impurity issues from his body and he has not immersed. Because the primary challah was already burned, the second exists only so the law will not be forgotten and has no inherent sanctity. When only one challah is separated for a pure kohen, however, it is the primary challah and requires purity.
