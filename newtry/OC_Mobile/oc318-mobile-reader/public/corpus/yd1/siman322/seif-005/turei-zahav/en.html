And the widespread custom is... and they burn it. Maharshal writes that nowadays challah is not given even to a minor kohen, and certainly not to an adult who immersed, because priestly lineage is not established with certainty. Mahariv likewise writes that those baking on the first night of Pesach should designate the small cake as challah only when separating it and should not move it afterward, even if a minor kohen is present, because we do not treat contemporary kohanim as certain.
