And the baker. Even if he makes a small dough he separates one forty-eighth; and a householder, even if he makes a large dough, such as for his son's feast and the like, separates one twenty-fourth, so as not to distinguish among doughs. So wrote the Rambam, and it is from the Yerushalmi. See above at the beginning of siman 328.
It became tamei. The Beit Yosef wrote below at the beginning of siman 328 that nowadays, when there are no pure foods and every dough is tamei, even a householder's dough is always one forty-eighth, even in a place where they give challah to a kohen.
