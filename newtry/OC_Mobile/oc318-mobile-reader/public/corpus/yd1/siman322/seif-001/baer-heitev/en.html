And the baker. And even if he makes a small dough, he separates 1 from 48; and likewise a householder, even if he makes a large dough, such as for his son's feast and the like, separates 1 from 24, so as not to distinguish among doughs. So wrote the Rambam, and it is from the Yerushalmi; and see above the beginning of siman 328.
Became impure. Beit Yosef writes below at the beginning of siman 328 that nowadays, when laws of ritual purity are not practiced and every dough is considered impure, even the householder's challah is always one forty-eighth, even in places where challah is given to a kohen.
