The difference between challah of Eretz Yisrael and challah outside the land. It contains 5 seifim.
It is a positive mitzvah to separate terumah from the dough and give it to the kohen, as it is said, the first of your doughs you shall raise up terumah. This "first" has no measure from the Torah; even if he separated an olive's bulk he exempted the dough. One who makes his entire dough challah has done nothing until he leaves some. By rabbinic law they separate one twenty-fourth from the dough. A baker who makes to sell in the market separates one forty-eighth. If the dough became impure unintentionally or under duress — even a homeowner separates one forty-eighth.
