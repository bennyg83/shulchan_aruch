And this reishit, etc. Sifrei: “like the terumah of the threshing floor,” etc. You compare it to the terumah of the threshing floor, which is unspecified; should I compare it to terumat maaser, which is explicit, and have it taken as one tenth? Therefore Scripture says, “like the terumah of the threshing floor, so shall you lift it,” teaching that I compared it to terumah of the threshing floor, etc.
