The measure of their length is from the place where they begin to be a junction until they spread in the flesh — in a large animal, 16 fingerbreadths; and some say the measure is 4 fingerbreadths. And in a small animal the Sages did not give a measure, only by appearance and by touch: as long as they are white, hard, and thick, they have the law of junction of sinews; but when they begin to soften, or they are small and thin — they do not have the law of junction of sinews; and in a place where they are not so white, only somewhat white and glisten like glass — it is not junction of sinews.
