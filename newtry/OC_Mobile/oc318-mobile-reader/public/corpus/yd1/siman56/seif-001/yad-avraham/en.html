(Seif 7) Junction in animal: three, one thick two thin; thick alone cut — permitted, for two remain. It implies if thick and one small entirely cut — treifah. And even though Mechaber ruled majority of thick and majority of one small — kosher, this is because we do not follow majority in these sinews; and so is clearly proven in Rashba in Torat HaAdam 42 end; see there; and so clear in Rambam's explanation of mishnayot chapter HaBehemah HaMekasheh; and not like Peri Chadash who wrote for Rambam if majority of thick and majority of one small severed — treifah — and it is not so; Shach and Peri Chadash were stringent in this law.
