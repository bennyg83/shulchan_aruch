(1) [Seif 1] Junction of sinews was removed or severed — meaning severed from each other in the middle; and it teaches not only this but also that: not only removed where nothing remains — which is obvious forbidden — but even severed while still connected there, only torn — nevertheless forbidden. Perishah ot 2; Kaf HaChayim in Hagahot Taz ot 1; Shulchan Zedek ot 2; Machberet ot 1.
(3) There. Or if severed — treifah. And if not entirely removed and not cut, only uprooted from the knee and folded from the bone upward — Beit Yosef's view to declare kosher; and so Levush. Perishah ot 1; Peri Toar ot 1; Or HaChaim ot 1; but Bach wrote it is treifah, not like Beit Yosef; and so Taz s.k. 1; Shach s.k. 1; Kaf HaChayim in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 1; Peri Chadash ot 1; Levushei Prad ot 1; Binyan Olam ot 1; Shulchan Zedek ot 1; Shulchan HaMelech ot 1; Zera Avraham ot 406; Machberet ot 2; Chochmat Adam general rule 24 ot 12; Levushei Serad siman 6 seif 2 ot 3; Beit Yitzchak part 1 ot 3; Anan HaKohen there ot 2; Zivchei Tzedek ot 2; and he wrote this is Rashbatz's view in Sefer Yavin Shemuah daf 70a.
(4) However — this is specifically when the entire length measure was folded; but if part of their measure still adheres to the bone — kosher. Levushei Serad there ot 4; Beit Yitzchak there; Anan HaKohen there; Zivchei Tzedek ot 3.
