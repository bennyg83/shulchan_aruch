Junction of sinews was removed — the acharonim, especially Taz, agreed with Ra'avad; and as he explained: if they exist but only folded from their place of connection upward — treifah; and Taz strained: from where did Ra'avad learn his law. And it appears simply the Gemara challenged: but it teaches severed — and Rashi explained as above in his name that it need not teach cut of bone with junction, but teaches removed junction alone; if so it should teach junction removed, and all the more cut; and it is difficult, for severed was needed: if junction folded and the knee was cut — how do we know treifah, for in junction nothing happens until declared treifah because of them, for they only folded and the bone was cut — we did not hear in the mishnah that it declares treifah; therefore severed had to teach that bone cut also declares treifah; the Gemara did not challenge immediately; thus folding junction also declares treifah; and in removed junction it is unnecessary — no difference in cut bone. And Rashi's words tend thus: arukum without junction of sinews — bone broke and he did not write plainly cut and junction folded; rather we learn treifah. However one may say when the place where they were connected folded, it was also cut — then impossible to return and be healthy; behold dependent on air — treifah; even though only because of bone cut there is no treifah per Rif and Rambam; but if the connected place remains and only junction folded — one may say kosher, for they can return and live by healing and adhere as before; and from all this no proof to forbid, for my proof was only when knee was also cut where they were attached; requires study, for we do not find Ra'avad's law in any author; according to what is said, Ra'avad's view is somewhat acceptable. However there appears proof from Rashi to the contrary: Rashi brought proof from the dispute of Shmuel and Rav on broken bone whether bone permitted — halacha like first version; and words tend that Rashi holds Shmuel also holds like first version, for strained to say Rav and Shmuel argue in those two establishments; and difficult for Shmuel how to resolve Gemara's challenge: why not say junction removed; and arukum without junction is unnecessary (and precision: it teaches severed — no difficulty for them); and Rashi explained bone broke and junction remain — for Shmuel no need thus, for Shmuel on broken animal permits; thus for Shmuel specifically severed; if so difficult how you find severed without junction; thus what Rashi wrote "broke" is not precise — for example he used it; and truly one may answer for Shmuel the other change from mishnah: from arukum and junction and above arukum and junction; and precision "from arukum and above" — they were not so precise; for Rashi for us no need in that term how say above junction — if broke in place of junction we would not know what it had; mishnah could not hide this law; therefore Rashi does not hold that explanation; but for Shmuel fine to say thus — no difficulty; if so in place of junction what of it — for Shmuel truly kosher, no concern — examine. (And truly Maharam of Lublin addressed whether arukum and above means above junction — what of place of junction — see there.) And his view appears: in place of junction the bone there is not treifah because of itself — requires study; and he is truly against all poskim; one should not challenge broken and no flesh covering — for then junction hangs in air, that bone is as non-existent, nothing for junction to rely on — namely folded; one may answer that bone is not non-existent for everything, only for life-treifah; for am ha'aretz we hold as connected — we rule only commandment of examination; and one may say the same for junction — one may not learn treifot from each other. Tur brought in this siman Ramban's view like Rif: middle bone when cut above junction — kosher if junction cut and cut is above bone with junction and thrown — kosher; Beit Yosef wrote we do not find his view in poskim; it appears to me thus Tosafot 57 s.v. why need, etc.: they challenged why mishnah teaches animal whose leg was cut, etc., and we say likewise in bird; Tosafot challenged: if only in bird kosher not animal — difficult for Levi who did not count, etc.; they challenged from question that Rav holds like Rashba who holds cut legs kosher like tanna of Beit Hillel earlier siman 54 — no question here. Therefore he challenged: since Rav said likewise in bird — we learn halacha here. Also on Levi no question, for in dispute they do not ask, as Tosafot 56b s.v. ibaya, etc.; and Rashba permits even in animal — must say Tosafot hold Rashba argues only on cut legs that can heal, not on removed junction; and proven from Niddah 24 sealed body — R' Zechariah until knee, R' Yannai until navel, R' Yosi until navel place — Gemara says they argue whether treifah lives; R' Yosi like Rashba holds cut kosher — do not invent new dispute; but per above, fine in sealed body even junction missing — Rashba explained. (And if so proven from Rambam created missing junction — treifah like missing leg — examine.) However understand Tosafot 42: why not challenge from animal whose junction was cut — and tanna should include in animal whose leg was cut; difficult for tanna but for Rashba's view difficult from junction — Rashba does not argue; must say certainly from junction alone no challenge — one may say not counted among 18 treifot only what are definitely treifah; but can return to kashrus if cut above junction per Rambam — only Tosafot's question: from arukum below upward everywhere you cut — treifah — cannot return forever when cut above — obviously treifah; therefore challenges to count junction and Gemara answers since Rashba holds cut kosher — Ramban's law returns to its place and is not counted; examine. And truly per Maharshal's distinction above regarding kidney between human removal and illness — possible here too, rejecting Rashba and Ran arguments; therefore God forbid rely on Ramban; but possible to join other sides since in my humble opinion Tosafot agree — examine. Gemara language supports Ramban: it challenged: is there something that if lifted from above severs and lives, and from below severs and dies; and see Rambam chapter 8 din 54 — matters are not similar; therefore Ramban holds Gemara's challenge thus: treifah returns to kashrus — from below treifah, then cut above and kosher. Tosafot Avodah Zarah and Zevachim challenged why Gemara in Noach took kosher animals — perhaps Noach himself treifah; Tosafot's difficulty: behold it lived long afterward; what challenge — they wrote what seemed treifah was external treifah; one may say at taking of animals legs broke in junction or were cut — treifah as all treifot, then Noach cut above and healed from treifah and lived long — perforce not like Ramban, does not return to kashrus — examine.
