(Siman 56 in Shach s.k. 1) And likewise even if not entirely removed, only uprooted from the knee and folded from the bone upward — treifah, etc. It must be said this means they folded upward until nothing at all remained connected along the length of the bone; for otherwise if they folded only a little from the bone and above remained connected — also treifah; if so for Ula who holds arukum is recognizable opposite it on a camel, and if the middle bone broke even protruding outward — also kosher for him — how kosher, for from the break upward the connection of sinews was severed; if so in any case treifah because junction of sinews was removed; rather it must be since they still remained connected on the part of the bone that is connected, it does not become treifah thus.
