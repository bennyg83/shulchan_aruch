Junction of sinews in an animal: three — one thick and two thin. If the thick one alone was cut — permitted, for behold two remain. If the two thin ones were cut — permitted, for behold the thick one is larger than both of them, and behold the entire junction was not removed, only its minority. If the majority of each of them was cut — treifah (Rambam's wording, chapter 8 of Hilchot Shechitah din 18). {Rama: The thick sinew is the one they separate when hanging the animal, and it rests upon the two and they are attached to flesh and bone (Mordechai in the name of Ra'avyah).}
