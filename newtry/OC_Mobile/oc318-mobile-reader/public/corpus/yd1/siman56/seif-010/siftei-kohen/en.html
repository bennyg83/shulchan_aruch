Some permit. Without examination, for since it healed well it is proven there is no defect here; and those who forbid wrote the reason: since if this bird were brought before us examination would be required, therefore now too one may not permit without examination. And for us, since we are not expert in examining birds — treifah. And it appears to me unlike above siman 55 seif 12: if it healed, bone to bone adheres — kosher; for there it is not in the place of junction of sinews, and treifah depends on the bone coming into open air; therefore once it healed it is kosher, for if it had gone into open air it would not return to heal; unlike here where there is concern for severing one of the sinews — so other poskim distinguished.
In examining birds, etc. It implies; but in animals we are expert even when broken, since there are only three and they are large — so Darkei Moshe, and so appears in Tur HaChayim there — see there and in simanim; and even though in Or Zarua general rule 50 din 3 (and Bach brings it) he wrote: specifically when there is no clear defect before us, such as swelling and clotted blood; but regarding clear defect of breakage, even in animals we cannot be expert — nevertheless it appears to the Rav to be lenient in animals, since in any case there are poskim who permit even without examination; and so Maharshal chapter HaBehemah HaMekasheh siman 16: in animals we are expert even when broken and bound (and see above s.k. 10). However it appears this is specifically when it returned and was bound; but if still broken, since there is such a great defect and most poskim require examination — one should forbid even in animals; and so from Maharshal's words there.
