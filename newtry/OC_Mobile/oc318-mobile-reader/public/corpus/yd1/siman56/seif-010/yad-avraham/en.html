(Seif 10) Bone broke in place of junction and bound and healed well — some permit if flesh appearance on fracture did not change; and for us treifah; specifically bird; but in animal we are expert even if broken and bound; specifically when bound; but if still broken — forbid even in animal, Shach. One may analyze: if still broken without concern for junction — treifah because of broken bone if skin and flesh do not cover majority; and for us not expert in any treifah matter as written siman 55 seif 5 in hagahah; it appears here he deals where no defect in skin and flesh outside, as Mechaber wrote if flesh appearance did not change — then kosher even for us in great loss as hagahah there; and if because concern for junction — treifah even in great loss. Such acharonim answered here what Shach meant: all that bound, even if fracture pieces do not adhere — kosher in animal.
