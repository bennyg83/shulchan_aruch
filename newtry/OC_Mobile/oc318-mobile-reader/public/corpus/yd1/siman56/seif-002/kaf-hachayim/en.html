(7) And know — also in a bird the primary junction is outward, and one must be stringent in all explanations and examine outward and inward, and they are more than 16 in both. Shulchan Pardes ot 2; Machberet ot 5; Levushei Serad there ot 9; Beit Yitzchak part 2 ot 1; Binyan Olam in Shulchan Aruch siman 87 ot 3; Zivchei Shamayim ot 5; Anan HaKohen there; Zivchei Tzedek ot 6; and what Yeshuot Yaakov wrote that in a bird all hold junction is inward toward body cavity not outward — his words were rejected in responsa Me'il Tzedakah siman 37; he raised that wherever there is decay in a bird in place of junction, whether from cavity or all sides of leg — forbid even in great loss and honor of Shabbat — see there; and Beit Din of Machberet there; Pithei Teshuvah ot 1.
(8) There in hagahah: but the first explanation is primary — meaning: although for law one should be stringent in both explanations, in great loss where there is still a side of explanation to be lenient one may rely on the first explanation that they are specifically outward and not inward.
