The junction of sinews in an animal is on the middle bone facing outward, where the tail falls upon it; and some say they are facing inward toward the body cavity of the animal. {Rama: And one should be stringent according to both explanations (Beit Yosef in the name of R' Yosef ibn Chaviv, and so Maharam Alashkar responsum 45); but the first explanation is primary (it is the view of Tosafot, Rashi, Rosh, and the great authorities of France and Ashkenaz).}
