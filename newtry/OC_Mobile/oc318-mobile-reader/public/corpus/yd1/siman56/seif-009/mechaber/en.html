A sign for the place where junction of sinews ends in a bird is like their sign in a small animal. {Rama: And one expert in estimating may estimate in every bird according to its size and according to its smallness — as long as they are hard, it is junction of sinews; and in a place where they soften, it is not junction of sinews (Piskei Maharai siman 141). And one not expert in estimating should estimate the width of a finger in a bird from the place where they begin (there in the name of Maharil). And junction of sinews never exists above half the middle bone (Rokeach siman 404). And we are not expert in examining junction of sinews of a bird, because it is difficult to examine and easily becomes treifah; therefore everywhere there is a blow in the place of junction of sinews, even if it is only swollen and the blood is clotted — since examination was required and we are not expert in birds, it is treifah (Mordechai chapter HaBehemah HaMekasheh, Or Zarua HaAruch, Agudah, and the acharonim).}
