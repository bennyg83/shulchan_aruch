In a bird. But in an animal the measure is as above.
And junction of sinews never, etc. Requires study: since junction of sinews never exists above half the middle bone — if so, one not expert should always estimate at half the bone and not the width of a finger; and so in simanei Tur HaChayim general rule 7 din 10 plainly; and the measure of the length of junction of sinews is on the middle bone from its half downward — junction of sinews; from its half upward — not junction of sinews, end of his words (and this is Rokeach siman 404, and he argues on Maharil who estimated the width of a finger); and it implies in simanei Tur HaChayim there that even in an animal we say thus, and so in Tur HaChayim there it appears to him that on this every person may rely, whether in bird or animal (and so Rokeach siman 404); and so Bach, that on this measure one may rely whether in bird or animal — see there.
We are not expert in examining junction of sinews of a bird. Since they are many — Taz; but in an animal, whose sinews are large and are only three, one expert should examine — Darkei Moshe and Tur HaChayim in the name of Or Zarua, and so Bach; and so I wrote in s.k. 13 in the name of Maharshal, not like Taz who omitted the word 'bird' and made a separate seif and wrote plainly that we are not expert in examination, and even if it is only swollen, etc. — see there, where it appears from his words that we say this in animals too — and it is not so.
Even if it is only swollen, etc. And Maharshal wrote in Or Zarua and in his book chapter HaBehemah HaMekasheh siman 15: indeed everything is according to the swelling, and the authority must before declaring treifah cut the swelling and examine it — if it appears from within the swelling that there is decay and dissolution, and there is concern that one of the strands was severed — then declare treifah, end of his words. And Bach wrote: possibly the Rav said only regarding swelling; but regarding change of appearance alone, such as bluish or greenish or yellowish — in such cases there is no defect at all, and without swelling we declare entirely kosher, and one need not cut and see if there is decay and dissolution; nevertheless it is good to be stringent and examine by cutting, etc., end of his words. And similarly Taz wrote: it appears to me specifically when the blood was greatly clotted until the sinews are almost unrecognizable because of the blood; but small blood clots — why declare treifah when no defect is seen in the sinews; if we come to declare treifah all that come before us swollen and with blood clots, even a little, we would need to declare treifah the majority and majority of majority — and this does not appear to me; rather inquiry and investigation of a God-fearing sage is required even in our time — so it appears to me, not like Sefer Maggid David who challenged him in lip-words but unnecessarily.
