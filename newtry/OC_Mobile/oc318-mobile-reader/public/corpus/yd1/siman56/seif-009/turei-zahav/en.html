The width of a finger. Maharshal wrote this is correct according to Rashi who explained on the large ox four fingerbreadths (second view in seif 5); but according to what we rule 16 fingerbreadths like Rambam (first view in seif 5) one finger is not enough; and at minimum in a bird two fingerbreadths, end of his words. And what Rama wrote "and junction never, etc." — meaning whether very small bird or large animal, for there the measure does not apply.
Where there is a blow in the place of junction of sinews. So is Maharshal's conclusion: indeed everything is according to the swelling; the authority must before declaring treifah cut the swelling and examine — if decay and dissolution appear from within, concern that one strand was severed — declare treifah, end of his words; implying without decay there is no prohibition even if flesh appearance changed.
