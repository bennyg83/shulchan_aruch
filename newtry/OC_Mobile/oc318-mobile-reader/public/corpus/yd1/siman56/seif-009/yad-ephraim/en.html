Even if only swollen. Be'er Heitev; see responsa Bach siman 133 copied in Likkutei HaKabbala; see Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 64 that one should not rely on examining junction only as poskim wrote in swollen and clotted blood — see if no decay and defect in junction; but not rely on actual junction examination — see there; see Mateh Yosef end 38b on bird whose legs were in place of junction at their binding, greenish in foreign language; he declared kosher in great loss, for we rely at time of binding, and since blood was not clotted and not swollen — kosher, for Bach brings in Or Zarua that even if flesh under skin changed we declare kosher, all the more when no change visible in skin and flesh.
