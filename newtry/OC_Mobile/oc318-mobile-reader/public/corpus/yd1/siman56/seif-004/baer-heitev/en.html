Opposite. (Even though junction of sinews spread until the heel, they already hardened and became like bone, and treifot of junction apply only where soft.) And if the bone broke in the place of the hock itself, Sha'ar HaDin siman 86 wrote treifah — concern perhaps one of junction was severed; and Bach wrote: even though junction begins above the hock, nevertheless we are not expert to distinguish that one strand was not severed above, and one may not be lenient in practice against Sha'ar HaDin, end of his words (and so Shach; see Peri Chadash).
