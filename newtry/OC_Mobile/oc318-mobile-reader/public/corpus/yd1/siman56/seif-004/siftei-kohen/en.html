Opposite the hock — kosher. And in Sha'ar HaDin siman 86 he wrote: if the bone broke in the place of the hock itself — treifah, for there is concern perhaps one of the junction sinews was severed, for there is the junction of sinews; and even if the bone did not break and protruded outward — treifah, for we are concerned for severing one of the sinews, end of his words. And Bach wrote at the beginning of siman 55: and even though in siman 56 it will be explained that the beginning of the sinews is above the hock, and if severed opposite the hock — permitted — nevertheless the Rav was stringent because we cannot distinguish that one of the sinews was not severed above, and one may not be lenient in practice against Sha'ar HaDin, end of his words. And also Maharil in laws of forbidden fat wrote like Sha'ar HaDin, and so is implied in Rashi Avodah Zarah (daf 57a) regarding those baskets that were perforated — Rashi explained: birds whose legs broke at the knee below or above, etc., and they examined junction of sinews, etc. — implying that at the hock itself, which is the arukum, examination of junction of sinews is also required; and if so, for us who cannot be expert — treifah. And that which poskim and Tur wrote plainly that opposite the hock is kosher — possibly they deal with a case where there is no concern for junction of sinews above, such as where it was cut before us only opposite the hock; but where the arukum broke and there is concern for junction of sinews — treifah; not as appears somewhat from Maharshal's words chapter HaBehemah HaMekasheh siman 11, who permits in such a case — see there.
