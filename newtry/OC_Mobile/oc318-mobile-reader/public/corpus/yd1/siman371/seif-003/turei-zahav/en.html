The house is tamei and they are tahor. Since each one contains only half a k'zayis, which is half the required measure — the house is tamei because the tumah will ultimately exit through there, and we view it as though the two half-k'zeisim are placed in the house. The tumah does not enter the rooms since their doors are locked. We do not apply lavud stringently. At first glance this implies that we do apply lavud leniently, which is astonishing — for if so, you would never find tumah exiting or entering through an opening, since we would say "it is like lavud" and it would be as though sealed. I examined the source: Mahariv wrote as follows — "R' Yaakov asked me whether we apply lavud regarding ohel ha'mes — for example, two houses adjacent to each other in construction less than three tefachim apart, and a person died in one of them. I answered him that we do not apply lavud. The proof is from chapter 1 of Eruvin regarding the hide of the donkey-pack, and from Sukkah chapter HaYashein regarding roof-beams that are not aligned — see the Gemara and Rashi there." Thus far his words. I examined chapter 1 of Eruvin: Tosafos wrote there that regarding tumah we do not apply lavud — and they made no mention of stringency at all. And in chapter HaYashein (Sukkah 22a) we say: "but if there is not an opening of a tefach between them and above them, it is tahor." Rashi explains: the same applies when the beams are not aligned — above is tahor and below is tamei, since if there is a tefach of space, it conveys tumah along its entire length below. Thus far his words. This implies that the tumah descends downward and renders tamei what is in the lower area, since there is no barrier there. Now if we were to apply lavud leniently, why would what is below become tamei? Even though we do not apply "throw it down," it should still be as though sealed and constitute a barrier to render what is below tahor! Rather, certainly regarding tumah we do not apply lavud at all. Accordingly, one must say that what the Rama stated here — that we do not apply lavud — means even stringently.
