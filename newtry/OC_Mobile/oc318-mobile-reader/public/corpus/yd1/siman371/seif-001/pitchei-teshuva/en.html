To enter. See Be'er Heitev in the name of the Rokeach. See Magen Avraham in Orach Chayim siman 343 seif 2, who challenged him: in any case it is permitted, for swallowed taharah does not become tamei, and he left the matter requiring study. See the new responsa of the Radbaz, siman 200, who also raised this difficulty against the Rokeach and answers that the Rokeach speaks of a woman whose time to give birth is near, so there is concern that the fetus may put out its head and thereby be considered born, no longer swallowed taharah. Therefore he wrote that even so we are not concerned, since there is still a double doubt. See there further. See the responsa of my relative Givat Shaul siman 53, who also answered this way, and see Noda BiYehudah Tinyana, Yoreh De'ah siman 207, She'eilat Yaavetz II siman 177, and Chatam Sofer responsa siman 354.
