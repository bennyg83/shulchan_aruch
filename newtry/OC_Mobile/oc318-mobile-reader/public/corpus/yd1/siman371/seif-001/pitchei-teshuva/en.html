To enter. See Baer Heitev in Rokeach's name. Magen Avraham, Orach Chayyim siman 343, seif 2, asks why any concern is necessary, since a fetus absorbed within its mother does not contract tumah, and leaves it unresolved. Radbaz, new responsa siman 200, raises the same question and answers that Rokeach speaks of a woman close to childbirth, where there is concern that the baby's head may emerge. At that point the child is considered born and is no longer protected as absorbed purity. Even so, Rokeach rules leniently because there remains a double doubt. Givat Shaul, siman 53, answers similarly. See also Noda BiYehudah, second edition, Yoreh Deah siman 207; She'ilat Yaavetz, part 2, siman 177; and Chatam Sofer, siman 354.
