(Siman 371, seif 1) Even if he did not nullify them. See Maharsha to Bava Batra 20a, who writes that according to Tosafot there this applies specifically when the opening is sealed with a utensil that is not susceptible to tumah. If it is sealed with food, then even if the entire opening is filled, the food must be expressly nullified there. I later saw that Tiferet LeMoshe here cites these words of Maharsha.
