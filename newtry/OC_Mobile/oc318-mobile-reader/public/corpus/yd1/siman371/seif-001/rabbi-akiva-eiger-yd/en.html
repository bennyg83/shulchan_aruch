(Siman 371 seif 1) Even if they did not nullify it. See Maharsha (Bava Batra daf 20), who wrote according to the view of Tosafot there that this is specifically when he seals with a vessel that does not receive tumah, but if he seals with foods, even if he seals the entire hole, he must nullify them afterwards. Afterwards I saw that the Tiferet l'Melech here brought these words of the Maharsha.
