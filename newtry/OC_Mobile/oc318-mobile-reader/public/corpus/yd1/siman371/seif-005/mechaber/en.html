It is forbidden to approach within four amot of the dead or of a grave — in what case? When the grave is not defined by partitions ten tefachim high; but if defined by partitions ten tefachim high or in a ditch ten tefachim deep, one need not distance except four tefachim {Rama: (and a kohen may stand beside a house with a dead inside and touch its walls, provided nothing shelters over him) (responsum of Rashba siman 336).}
