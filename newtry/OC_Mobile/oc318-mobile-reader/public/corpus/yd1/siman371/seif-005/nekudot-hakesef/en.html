(There) This is impossible, for closing the entrance is by means of a key, and it does not interpose, as stated above at the beginning of this siman, seif 1, which I brought in the name of the Rambam, etc. He follows his approach above at the beginning of the siman, and I have already written above that this is not so. On the contrary, the Rambam implies that it does interpose. One may also say that the Derishah speaks where the door can stand without support, as is the case in most houses. What he further wrote, “and moreover, the door is hung by hinges, etc.,” I do not know what “moreover” means, for this is the same point. In truth, all of this is not so, as I wrote above. Also what he wrote, “and we have never heard, etc.,” is not a valid point. That is because it is impossible for him to be closed in a room like one sitting in prison, without entering or leaving and without others entering and leaving to him. Furthermore, one may say that our teachers speak of a place where it is impossible to seal the holes.
