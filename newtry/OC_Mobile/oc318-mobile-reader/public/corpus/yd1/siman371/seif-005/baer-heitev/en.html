To approach. The Derishah proves from here that even within four cubits of a corpse lying before us, a kohen may not approach, either because the corpse occupies four cubits or as a decree lest he touch the corpse itself. This rejects the practice of allowing a kohen delivering a eulogy to stand within four cubits. The Shach explains that those who are lenient distinguish between a corpse resting permanently in a house, where a kohen may not enter its four-cubit area, and a corpse lying on a bier being carried out. When it is set down briefly during Tzidduk HaDin, it does not establish a fixed place and therefore does not occupy four cubits. The Rokeach makes the same distinction.
