A courtyard surrounded by projections and porticoes and impurity in one of the houses — if all openings and windows of the houses are locked, impurity goes out under the projections and porticoes {Rama: (and the reason is since ultimately impurity will go out that way, we view it as if it already went out; therefore some are stringent for kohanim to walk through the city gate from which the dead will ultimately go out (Piskei Maharai siman 25 and Kol Bo); and some permit (there citing some authorities); and one who is lenient does not lose where they did not practice to be stringent)}; and if a window or opening opened on the other side and those on the courtyard side are all locked, impurity does not go out under the projections; and if one of them also opened toward the projections, impurity goes out from the house under the projections; and likewise if impurity is under the projections, it enters from beneath them into the house; therefore roofs projecting outward above the doorways of houses and impurity in one of the houses — all houses whose doorways are open are impure: {Rama: Two roofs that do not touch each other — even if less than three between them, they are not joined for impurity, for we do not say lavud to be stringent (Mahariu in his rulings); and if one was above the other, even if not touching, we say chavot rami — as if touching (Nimmukei Yosef laws of impurity for Rif); kohanim cannot compel the dead's relatives to remove the dead so they may enter their houses except where it is customary to remove all dead (responsum of Maharil siman 65).}
