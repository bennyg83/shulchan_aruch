A law of the money of idolatry, what is its law; it contains 2 se’ifim.
The proceeds of an elil in the hand of a Jew who sold it are forbidden. But if a non-Jew sold it — even if he sold it in order to worship it — the money is permitted; and this is specifically when it is known that he sold it to do his needs with its proceeds, but if it is not known, one must be concerned that perhaps he sold it to buy with its proceeds other elilim, and the money was set aside for elilim and is forbidden (and see at the end of siman 132).
