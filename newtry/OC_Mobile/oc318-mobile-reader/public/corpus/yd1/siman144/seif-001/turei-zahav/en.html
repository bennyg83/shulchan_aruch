In the hand of a Jew who sold it. As it is written: "and you shall be placed under a ban like it" — whatever you benefit from it shall be like it; but this does not apply to idolatry itself.
One should be concerned that he may have sold it. At the end of siman 132 it is implied that the prohibition applies only when it is definitively known that he sold it in order to purchase idolatry needs; and possibly he relied on what is written here. However, in the Mordechai, perek Even HaMeabed, it is written that it is permitted to receive the money if he did not say "wait until I sell the idol."
