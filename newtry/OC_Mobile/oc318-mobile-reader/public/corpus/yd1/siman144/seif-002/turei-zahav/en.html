And the gentile sold them to another, etc. So wrote Tosafot in the name of Rabbi Elchanan, and the Beit Yosef brought them. Tosafot concluded: “And Ri further says that even if the gentile did not sell them, but since the gentile drew them to himself before giving the money, for we hold that a gentile’s meshikhah acquires, etc.” The Tur and Shulchan Aruch also wrote this law at the beginning of siman 132, as I brought there the source-reference for this. If so, it is astonishing that the Shulchan Aruch here ruled permissively only where he sold them to another.
