The Shach concludes that this raises a difficulty with the Mechaber, who states without qualification that the money is permitted. The Taz writes: In siman 132 the Mechaber rules that even if the non-Jew did not sell them, once the non-Jew pledged them to him before paying the money, it is permitted, since we hold that meshichah by a non-Jew effects acquisition. Accordingly, it is difficult why the Shulchan Aruch rules permissively here only where he sold them to another. See siman 132.
