They are permitted. R. Yerucham writes that this clearly means permitted specifically to others, but to that Jew himself they remain forbidden forever as a penalty. This makes the Mechaber's unqualified statement that the money is permitted difficult. Shach. Taz writes that in siman 132 the Mechaber rules that even if the gentile did not sell them, but merely gave them as collateral before paying the money, it is permitted, because we rule that a gentile acquires through meshichah. It is therefore difficult why the Shulchan Aruch here permits only where the gentile sold them to another.
