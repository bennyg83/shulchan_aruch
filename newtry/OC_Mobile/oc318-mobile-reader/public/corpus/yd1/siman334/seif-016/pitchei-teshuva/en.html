By rabbinic enactment. See the Siftei Kohen (Shakh), and see the responsum of my ancestor the Panim Me'irot, part 1, siman 90. See there what he writes — that one who became obligated in excommunication and wishes to pay a fine is not exempt from the excommunication according to the strict law; nevertheless, everything depends on the judgment of the court, if they see that he repents wholeheartedly it is appropriate to be lenient, since they are not required to excommunicate him publicly for this. See there. Accordingly, it appears that if the matter was such that they were required to excommunicate him publicly, as in seif 47, it is impossible to be lenient with him.
