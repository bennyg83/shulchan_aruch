(Siman 334 seif 43) One who treats lightly even one matter of the words of the Sages. See the Shach s.k. 70, who writes in the name of the Ran to distinguish between a matter whose essence is rabbinic and a matter whose essence is Torah law. See Pri Chadash, Hilkhot Yom Tov siman 496, and Korban Netanel at the beginning of Perek Makom Shenahagu, who wrote to reject the Ran’s distinction. With God’s help, I resolved his words in my work on the Rambam, Hilkhot Talmud Torah chapter 6 law 14.
