The sage.
One who calculates.
Against one who.
The sage. This is where he is fit to issue rulings and labors in Torah. Beit Yosef in the name of the Kuntresim.
Who calculates. The Shach wrote: it appears that this is specifically when he calculates and establishes the calendar orally, as they were accustomed to establish it in Eretz Yisrael when the Temple stood. But to calculate seasons and constellations as is customary nowadays is, on the contrary, a mitzvah, as we say: whoever knows how to calculate seasons and constellations and does not calculate, Scripture says of him, “and they do not regard the work of Hashem,” etc.
For one who. If he comes to beit din to complain that the other placed him in nidui unlawfully, beit din places the other in nidui. So wrote the Levush. It implies that as long as he does not come to beit din to complain, beit din does not attend to placing him in nidui. Kesef Mishneh wrote in the name of Tashbetz in responsum that here it speaks of one who is accustomed to doing so, in which case beit din attends to placing him in nidui. It implies that where he is accustomed, even if the injured party does not complain, beit din attends to placing him in nidui. End quote from Shach. Reference 40.
