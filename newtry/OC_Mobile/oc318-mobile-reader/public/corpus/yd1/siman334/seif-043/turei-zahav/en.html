One who mentions the Name of Heaven in vain. Some ask why he did not count one who failed to place this person who mentioned the Name in nidui. It appears that there is no obligation to place him in nidui, because perhaps the listener thinks that the one who mentioned the Name does not know that there is a prohibition in the matter. They said that the listener must place him in nidui only if he knows clearly himself that the one mentioning the Name knows that there is a prohibition in the matter.
One who places in nidui someone who is not liable to nidui. The Hagahot Maimoniyyot wrote that this is specifically where he knows that the person is not liable to nidui, like the case of Reish Lakish in Moed Katan. But a teacher who placed someone in nidui because he thought this person was liable to nidui, and erred, we do not place the teacher in nidui. Maharshal wrote that some say this is specifically if the one wrongly placed in nidui is a Torah scholar, but not with an am haaretz. And regarding one who places a Torah scholar in nidui, there is no proof from the case of Reish Lakish, for perhaps the person whom Reish Lakish placed in nidui was greater than he.
