For twenty-four matters they impose nidui on a person, and these are they. (1) One who disgraces a sage even after death. (2) One who disgraces an agent of bet din. (3) One who calls his fellow slave. (4) One who treats lightly one matter of words of scribes—and needless to say of words of Torah. (5) One whom bet din sent and they set him a time and he did not come. (6) One who did not accept the law upon himself—they impose nidui until he gives. (7) One who has in his possession something harmful—they impose nidui until he removes the damage. (8) One who sold his land to a non-Jew by force—they impose nidui until he accepts upon himself every coercion that may come from the coercer to his fellow Israelite owner of the boundary. (9) One who testified on an Israelite in courts of idolaters by coercion and took money from him through testimony not according to law—they impose nidui until he pays. (10) A priest butcher who does not separate the gifts and gives them to another kohen—they impose nidui until he gives. (11) One who profanes the second festival days of exile even though it is custom. (12) One who does melacha on erev Pesach after noon. (13) One who mentions the divine Name in vain or swears by it in idle matters. (14) One who brings the public to eat sacred food outside. (15) One who brings the public to profanation of the Name. (16) One who calculates years and fixes months outside the Land. (17) One who misleads the blind. (18) One who prevents the public from doing a mitzvah. (19) A butcher from whom treifah issued under his hand. (20) A butcher who did not show examination of his knife to a sage. (21) One who hardens himself to render judgment. (22) One who divorced his wife and made partnership or business dealings between them that bring them to be intimate when they come to bet din—they impose nidui on them. (23) A sage whose reputation is bad. (24) One who imposes nidui on one not liable for nidui. {Rama: And for nidui matters they do not need testimony and clear proof but assessment of the mind on truth of what the claimant claims certainly, and then even a woman, even a minor is believed if the mind gives that the matter is true (Maharik root 120).}
