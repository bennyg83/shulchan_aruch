For twenty-four matters, etc. This is the Gemara's language at the beginning of chapter 3 of Berachot, 19a. Rambam gathered them from scattered places in the Bavli and Yerushalmi. Raavad and the glosses object that many more are found, as stated in seif 44. Beit Yosef answers that all those are included in the fourth category, one who disparages, etc. His answer is difficult, for if so Rambam should likewise have omitted many of the twenty-four.
(Supplement) For twenty-four matters, etc. See Tosafot Kiddushin 28a, s.v. one who calls, etc., and Ran Nedarim 7b, s.v. and if not, etc., for, etc. End of supplement.
And for this purpose he need not, etc. As stated in Moed Katan 17a, a Torah scholar may execute judgment, etc. See Rosh there. Bava Kamma 112b likewise states concerning an agent of beit din, etc. [Citation references: 40.]
