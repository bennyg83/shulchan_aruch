Menudeh to his city is menudeh to another city (even if larger than it) (Tur). Menudeh to another city is not menudeh to his city. {Rama: But he is menudeh to other towns like the city that imposed nidui on him (Tur). Townspeople who made cherem on anyone who comes to dwell in their town—it does not apply to one who comes to dwell there unless he has a rabbi in the town, for then he must fulfill his rabbi nidui; but they may enact among themselves to fence themselves not to marry or do business with him (Mordechai chapter Lo Yachpor).}
