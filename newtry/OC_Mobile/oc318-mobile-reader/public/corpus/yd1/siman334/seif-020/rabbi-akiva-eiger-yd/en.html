(Shach s.k. 46) According to all they may not release him. It appears to me that this is the Shach’s intention: the Rosh gives the reason that release is only in his presence because of his honor, and according to this reason, if they released him not in his presence, after the fact it is effective. The Rosh then brings those who explain that the reason is concern for suspicion, and according to that reason, even after the fact he is not released. The Shach means that according to all, that is, even according to the reason of suspicion, they may not release him by notifying him. This is implied, because the Rosh gives a practical difference regarding after the fact and cites some explainers stringently, but does not also give a practical difference that according to the first reason there is greater stringency regarding notice. It must be that even according to those explainers, release by notice is not effective. This is correct, with God’s help. Still, regarding after the fact there is no proof from the Rosh; perhaps notice is effective after the fact. Yet since release without later notice is not effective after the fact, perhaps because at the time of the release it was not in his presence and did not take effect, later notice cannot repair it, similar to Tosafot, Gittin 33b, s.v. libdar ibdurei.
