Menudeh to a rabbi is menudeh to the student (but he is not menudeh to other sages) (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam). Menudeh to a student whom they imposed nidui for his honor is not menudeh to the rabbi, but he is menudeh to the rest of the people (who are not sages, but not to sages even younger than him) (Beit Yosef from implication of Rambam wording). And specifically when menudeh not in the rabbi presence; but in the rabbi presence it is contempt of court, and if he argues with his rabbi he is well called wicked (a rabbi who decreed cherem and nidui in some matter and his students cannot stand in his decrees need not fulfill) (Piskei Maharai siman 222).
