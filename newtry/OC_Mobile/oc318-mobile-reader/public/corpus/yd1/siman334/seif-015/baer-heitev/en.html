To the others. Even if they are lesser than he. This is the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe.
To the sages. The Shach cites many authorities who maintain that one placed under a ban by a student is banned regarding all other sages of equal rank who are not greater than that student. Accordingly, one banned by a teacher is certainly banned regarding other sages. This applies only where the student is a Torah scholar whose honor warrants a ban, as stated below in seif 18.
Disrespect. The disrespect was committed by the student, and the student himself requires a ban.
To uphold. The Rama likewise rules above at the end of siman 228 regarding a communal decree. See siman 8.
