If they imposed nidui on one person for transgressing a transgression and the ruler decreed punishment on whoever upholds the nidui, they must enter possible punishment to uphold our religion; but if they imposed nidui on matters between him and his fellow, such as that he brought him to gentile courts, we are not obligated to enter danger of punishment. {Rama: And see above siman 228 s.k. 67 also siman 232 seif 12. Even though a person is obligated to protest transgressors and whoever does not protest when he can is caught in that sin, nevertheless a person is not obligated to spend his money for this; therefore they practiced to be lenient from protesting transgressors when there is concern they will stand on our bodies and property (Mahariv siman 157).}
