Therefore the custom is to be lenient. Even more so is written in the Derishah in the name of Maharamatz — that Maharam wrote that in our times when the bans of excommunication are improperly administered, we do not excommunicate everyone who deserves excommunication, even for severe transgressions. For even by the strict letter of the law, the Sages did not impose stringencies lest it prevent him from repenting; all the more so in our generation, when scoundrels shame a person over such things, and sometimes a person cannot control his spirit and it could lead to bloodshed, etc. Thus far his language.
