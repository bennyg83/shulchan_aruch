Nezifah is when a great person rebukes him saying how impertinent is so-and-so and the like; and its law is that he should hide and sit in his house and be ashamed and not show his face to one who shamed him, and diminish in laughter, speech, and business, and afflict himself before all who see him; but he need not distance from people neither in eating and drinking nor in greeting, and need not practice any mourning laws, and need not appease one who was impertinent toward him, and need not have nidui released—rather once he behaved as nezuf, when the time of nezifah passes he is permitted automatically. {Rama: And even if the great one said nothing to him, but he knows himself that a great person harbored resentment against him, he must practice nezifah on himself (responsum of R. David Cohen siman 22).}
