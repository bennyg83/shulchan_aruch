Reuven who owes Shimon money and refuses to pay—if Shimon imposed nidui on Reuven that he pay, his nidui is nidui. {Rama: And some say a person does not judge himself to impose nidui on his fellow for monetary matters (there root 25 in the name of Maharam and Raavad), and such is primary; all the more if his fellow says he is ready to obey the law (Maharam siman 129).}
