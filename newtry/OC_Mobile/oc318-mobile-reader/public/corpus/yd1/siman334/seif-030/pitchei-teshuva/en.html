Conditionally. See Tashbetz, part 2, siman 20. He writes that in this case even if the person under the ban is a Torah scholar, regarding whom we rule that a scholar may place himself under a ban and release himself from it, as below in seif 33, he cannot release himself from a ban that took effect because of his prior acceptance of a condition. If a Torah scholar could release himself in such a case, why did Yehudah not release himself from his own undertaking? The Raavad writes likewise. Tashbetz concludes that the matter requires study.
