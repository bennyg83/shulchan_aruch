That which they say regarding a menudeh without specification that he is forbidden in laws of menudeh—specifically menudeh to bet din, who is menudeh to every person, whether he did not come to bet din or did not obey the law, or for contempt of court, or for a transgression—whether bet din imposed nidui or any person imposed nidui, his law is equal whether in his city or another city; but menudeh partially, such as a student whom they imposed nidui for his honor, and likewise menudeh to another city whom they imposed nidui for their honor—he is not forbidden in all laws of menudeh, but people distance him to shame him; but mourning laws do not apply to him.
