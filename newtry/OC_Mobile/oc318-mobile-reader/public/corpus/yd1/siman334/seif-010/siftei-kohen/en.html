Ramban may hold that these are not automatic restrictions, but that beit din may add them. According to what was explained above, only one excommunicated for sin is excluded from zimun and a minyan, not one excommunicated for monetary disobedience. The Mechaber therefore teaches that in a monetary case beit din may expressly bar him from zimun and communal prayer.
