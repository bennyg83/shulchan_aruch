Regarding its statement that tefillin are permitted, Tiferet LeMoshe writes that this follows from Rabbi Eliezer ben Hyrcanus, whose son Hyrcanus came to remove his tefillin although he was under ban. This conflicts with Orach Chaim 38, which says that one under ban and a metzora may not wear tefillin; that text must be erroneous. Moed Katan leaves the question unresolved, and in a doubt he remains obligated. See also Shemesh Tzedakah, Yoreh Deah 3.
