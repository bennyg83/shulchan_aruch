And wearing shoes. See Baer Heitev regarding the statement that tefillin are permitted. Tiferet LeMoshe writes that this is also implied by the incident of Rabbi Eliezer ben Horkenus at the end of chapter 4 of Sanhedrin, where his son Horkenus came to remove his tefillin even though Rabbi Eliezer was under a form of separation. This is unlike the statement at the end of Orach Chaim, siman 38, that one under a ban and a metzora may not put on tefillin; that text must contain an error. In Moed Katan as well, the Gemara asks whether one under a ban may wear tefillin and leaves the matter unresolved. Because of the doubt he remains obligated. See Responsa Shemesh Tzedakah, Yoreh Deah, siman 3.
