The menudeh—one does not sit within four amot of him except his wife and sons (Ran) (and some say also members of his household are permitted) (Tur and Terumat HaDeshen siman 276) (and one may be lenient). And one does not eat (or drink with him) (Tur). And one does not invite him for zimmun, and one does not include him in anything requiring ten. {Rama: But if they did not impose nidui on him explicitly, even though he is a criminal or transgressed communal enactments, they include him in the minyan of ten to pray with him (Rivash siman 172 and Mordechai beginning of Shevuot shenayim and Hagahot Alfasi chapter Elu megalechin); and even an actual menudeh whom one does not include in the minyan—nevertheless it is permitted to pray while he is in the synagogue (Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba responsum and Rivash siman 173); and what they practice to expel him from the synagogue is so individuals who need to distance from four amot of him are not pressed (there in Rivash).} And forbidden in laundry, haircut, and wearing shoes (Raavad and Rosh) like a mourner. And permitted in words of Torah—he studies and they study with him, and they are paid and they pay him; and the one under cherem does not study and they do not study with him, neither is he paid nor are they paid for him. And likewise forbidden to benefit him more than his livelihood (Rashba responsum); but he studies for himself so he not forget his learning, and he opens for himself a small shop for his livelihood. And permitted to speak with the menudeh and the one under cherem unless bet din explicitly strengthened on him. {Rama: And nevertheless he should not increase words with him and should not speak with him except for need, as one speaks with a mourner (Hagahot Alfasi and Rivash cited). In a place where it is forbidden to sit within four amot of him—whoever sat there first and the menudeh came there need not leave (Beit Yosef in the name of Kontres). And forbidden to enter his house as it is like four amot of him. And there is no distinction between standing and sitting (there).}
