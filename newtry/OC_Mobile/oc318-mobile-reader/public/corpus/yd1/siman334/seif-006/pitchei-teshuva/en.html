But not for the sages. See Be'er Heitev in the name of the Shach, who disagrees with this. This is difficult, for if so, why does the Mishnah teach that one under nidui by the teacher is under nidui to the student? Why mention the student specifically; he is under nidui to all sages. I found in the new responsa of the Radbaz, siman 378, that he too concludes that one placed under nidui by the teacher is under nidui to all sages, and he writes that the Mishnah's case of nidui to a student means even if the student became wise and greater than his teacher; see there. From this it follows that the Mishnah's statement that one under nidui to a student is not under nidui to the teacher likewise speaks even where the student is greater than his teacher, and this requires study.
