If they imposed nidui on him in a dream, it is nidui and release is required; and even if they released him in a dream it is nothing; and even if he knows who imposed nidui on him he cannot release himself; and ten people who review halachot are required to release him. If he did not find effort after them until a parasang—if he did not find, they release him even ten who review Mishnah; if he did not find, they release him even ten who know how to read Torah; if he did not find, they release him even ten who do not know how to read; if he did not find in his place ten, they release him even with three (and some say he has release only through ten who reviewed halachot or Mishnayot (Tosafot), and nevertheless it suffices if they release him one after another) (Tur in the name of Rosh).
