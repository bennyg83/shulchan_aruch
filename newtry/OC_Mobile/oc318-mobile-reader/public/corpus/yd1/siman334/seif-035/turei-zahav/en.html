Ten are required, etc. The reason is that one must be concerned that he was placed under nidui by Heaven, and the Divine Presence rests upon every gathering of ten. He may therefore be released by such a beit din from the nidui imposed upon him. This is stated in the Rosh.
If he did not find ten in his locality. Here he is not required to travel as far as a parsah, because ten do not possess so great an advantage over three as to require that effort.
Those who study halachot or Mishnayot. The Gemara states the order in the reverse, those who teach or study. Here, however, the ruling addresses the after-the-fact case and means that at minimum the ten must study halachot, or at least Mishnayot.
