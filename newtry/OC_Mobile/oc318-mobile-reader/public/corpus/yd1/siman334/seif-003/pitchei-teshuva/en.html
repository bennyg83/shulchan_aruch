That his sons should not be circumcised. Birkei Yosef cites Makom Shmuel that Maharam teaches that even if the father himself is a mohel, the court may decree that he not circumcise his own sons. Birkei Yosef questions this. Perhaps no such decree may be imposed, just as he is not barred from prayer, Shema, or tefillin. The Knesset HaGedolah's proof from the Ramban that he may be barred from reciting Kaddish for his father is not decisive, because Kaddish, zimun, and communal prayer require others. The ban may exclude him from communal participation, but not from a mitzvah he can perform entirely by himself, such as circumcising his son.
