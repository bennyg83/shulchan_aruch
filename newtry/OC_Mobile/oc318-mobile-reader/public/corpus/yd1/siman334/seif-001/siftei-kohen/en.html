For a matter of issur, etc. See above seif 43 whether one places in nidui for a rabbinic issur.
They place him in nidui immediately. It implies even without warning; however in the booklets Beit Yosef brought it implies that even for a matter of issur they did not practice nidui except after warning.
And nidui is not less than thirty days. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the booklets: there is a difference — if a sage placed him in nidui for his honor and he went away they do not release him until after thirty days per their view; and per our view seven days — for since he went away presumably he wants them to release him after this time; and so it seems there is a difference for one who persists in his rebellion — we say to place him in nidui a second time they place in nidui immediately and repeat after seven and cherem after fourteen — for one depends on the other; so it seems to me for law but not for practice because I did not see any of the early authorities who wrote thus — end quote; and meaning: certainly one should not explain that the court has no power to reduce nidui to less than thirty days — for it is not so as below seif 5.
