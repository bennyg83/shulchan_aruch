And even if there is concern, etc. See Be'er Heitev. See Tashbetz responsa II siman 47, whose view is also like Nekudot HaKesef, and so wrote Shemesh Tzedakah responsa, Yoreh De'ah siman 48, Chavot Yair responsa siman 141, She'eilat Yaavetz I siman 79 at the end, and the new Radbaz responsa siman 187. The Radbaz concludes: nevertheless, the leader of the generation must be measured in such matters, for not all people are equal and not all transgressions are equal, and all his deeds should be for the sake of Heaven; see there. So too Chatam Sofer responsa siman 322 concludes, and he wrote there: however, one must doubt a case where there is concern that the person will convert his religion and take his minor children with him; perhaps we should be concerned for the minors who did not sin. His view is that one should distinguish: a person with false beliefs, where it is close to certain that he will teach his children after him, and then the children too will be included in "all who enter her shall not return," we are not concerned for the children. For this reason it seems to me that the cursed sect who believe in Shabbetai Tzvi, may his name be blotted out, if Heaven forbid their hearts are not turned for good, we pursue them together with their sons and daughters, etc. But with an ordinary recalcitrant or sinner, who says that we should not be concerned for his children? He discusses this at length and concludes that the matter still requires study and deliberation; see there.
