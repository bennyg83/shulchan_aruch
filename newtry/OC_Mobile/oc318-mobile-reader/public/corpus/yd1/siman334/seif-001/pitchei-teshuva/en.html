Even if there is concern, and so forth. See Baer Heitev and Responsa Tashbetz, part 2, siman 47, who agrees with Nekudot HaKesef. Responsa Shemesh Tzedakah, Yoreh Deah siman 48; Chavot Yair, siman 141; She'ilat Yaavetz, part 1, end of siman 79; and Radbaz, new responsa siman 187, likewise. Radbaz concludes that the leader of the generation must act with patience in such matters, because people differ and transgressions differ, and all actions must be for the sake of Heaven. Responsa Chatam Sofer, siman 322, reaches a similar conclusion but raises a further doubt where there is concern that the sinner will apostatize and take his minor children with him. Should concern for innocent children alter the response? He distinguishes between a person holding corrupt theological beliefs, where it is nearly certain he will teach the same beliefs to his children and they too will be drawn irreversibly into them, and an ordinary stubborn transgressor, where perhaps concern for the children should matter. He therefore writes that regarding the accursed sect of believers in Shabbetai Tzvi, if they do not repent they may be pursued together with their children, but regarding an ordinary rebellious sinner the issue remains one requiring further study and caution.
