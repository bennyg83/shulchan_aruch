How they practiced toward one under nidui or cherem, and for what they impose nidui, and the law of its release. In it are 48 seifim. One who transgresses a forbidden matter—they impose nidui on him immediately. {Rama: But for monetary matters they do not impose nidui on him until they warn him three times, namely 2, 5, 2 (Tur) from the beit din, as will be explained in Choshen Mishpat siman 11.} And nidui is not less than thirty days; and if he does not repent they repeat nidui on him after thirty days; and if he does not repent they wait for him another thirty and place him in cherem; even if a festival occurs within the thirty days it does not annul it. This is said when nidui is thirty days in their nidui; but our nidui is like their nezifah which is seven days, and our nezifah is one day. {Rama: And they impose nidui on one liable for nidui even if there is concern that through this he will go to bad ways—one need not be concerned about that (Piskei Maharai siman 138).}
