They count thirty. Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the kuntresim that there is a practical difference: if a chacham placed nidui on him because of his honor and he went away, they do not release him until after 30 days according to them, and according to us 7 days, for since he went away, presumably it is satisfactory to him that they release him after this time, end of his words. And the meaning is that one should not explain that beit din does not have the power to reduce the nidui to less than 30 days, for that is not so, as below seif 6. Shach:
To be concerned. Taz writes that one should not act in accordance with this gloss, and he brings several proofs and arguments against it. Nekudot HaKesef responds that all of Taz's proofs can be rejected, because where beit din lawfully places a person under nidui, we need not fear the possible consequences. Otherwise the authority of Jewish law would be nullified.
