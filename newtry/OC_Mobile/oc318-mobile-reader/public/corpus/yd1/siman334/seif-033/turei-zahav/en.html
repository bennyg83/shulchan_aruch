In the form of an oath. For example, he says, 'I swear that I shall be under nidui if I perform a certain act.' Thus writes the Beit Yosef.
And that which he writes, that release is required. The Beit Yosef cites a manuscript stating that at the time of release they should say: 'Because so-and-so was placed under nidui in his dream, let him now be released; may neither suffering nor curse come upon him.' They should then recite, 'May Reuven live and not die,' 'Make known to me the path of life,' and 'Hashem, I have heard Your report,' and recite the passage beginning 'And He passed' three times.
