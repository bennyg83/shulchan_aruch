An elder who imposed nidui for his own need—even as halachah—is not nidui. {Rama: Meaning he did not intend for honor of Torah but to profit money that he give him to release his nidui (Tur in the name of Avi HaEzri), or he did it from hatred, such as when we see he imposed nidui on one and others who did like him he did not impose nidui (Terumat HaDeshen siman 374), and all similar cases.}
