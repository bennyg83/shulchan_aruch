Intended. In Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of the Ramakh, he explained that this is where he did not disgrace him at all, but merely failed to honor him as he is obligated to honor him, through rising and deference; in that case his nidui is not a nidui. Maharik in the name of Maharam explained that it speaks where he placed him in nidui because he withheld his money unlawfully; although he placed him in nidui according to halakhah, his nidui is not a nidui. So too wrote the Rosh in responsum. End quote from Shach.
