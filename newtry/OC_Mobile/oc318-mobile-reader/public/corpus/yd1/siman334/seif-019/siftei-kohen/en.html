An elder who placed under nidui, etc. Meaning a Torah scholar who placed someone under nidui: even though he did so according to law, for one who acts that way is liable to nidui, nevertheless since he did not intend for the honor of Torah, it is not nidui, as the Rav proceeds to explain.
Meaning that he did not intend, etc. In Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of the Ramakh, he explained that this means where the person did not disgrace him at all, but merely did not honor him as he was obligated to honor him by standing and showing respect; in such a case his nidui is not nidui. Maharik in the name of Maharam explained that it speaks where he placed him under nidui before his teacher, as the Raavad and Mechaber wrote above in seif 15. He further answered that it speaks where he placed him under nidui because he was unlawfully withholding his money; although he placed him under nidui according to law, his nidui is not nidui. So the Rosh wrote in a responsum. See below seif 46.
