A Torah scholar, etc. In the responsum of the Rosh it states: a Torah scholar or a communal prayer leader, etc. — and it appears that this refers to a case where the prayer leader excommunicated him as an agent of the court.
Until they excommunicated him. This refers to a case where he was excommunicated lawfully. And likewise in the Taz the text reads: "he who shamed him, a wicked man, until he excommunicated him" — and so too it is in the responsum of the Rosh — implying that the meaning is: he shamed the sage to the point that because of this he was excommunicated, and after he was excommunicated the wicked man said "on the contrary" — and if so, it is certainly a lawful excommunication, since one who shames a sage is excommunicated, as stated below, siman 243.
