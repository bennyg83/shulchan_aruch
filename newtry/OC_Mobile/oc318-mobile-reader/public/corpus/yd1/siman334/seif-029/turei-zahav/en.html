Only in his presence. The Ran wrote that the reason is suspicion: at times they will release him, but the excommunicated person will not know, and when people do not treat him with nidui, they will come to say that they are treating nidui lightly and will not be concerned for the nidui of the Sages afterward. But when they placed him in nidui not in his presence, people will say that just as they placed him in nidui not in his presence, so they released him not in his presence; end quote. From this we learn that the shamash should announce that so-and-so is clear of the cherem, also because of suspicion, lest one who was exempted from the cherem not hear. So it appears to me.
