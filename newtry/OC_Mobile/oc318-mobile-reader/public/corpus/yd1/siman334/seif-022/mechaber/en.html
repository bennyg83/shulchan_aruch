They practiced not to impose nidui on one who transgressed a communal enactment with cherem and nidui until they proclaim on him. {Rama: One who transgressed the enactment of Rabbenu Gershom may his light shine—if he transgressed unintentionally he need not release; if they released him and he transgressed intentionally, any synagogue that are agents of Rabbenu Gershom release him when he repents, for we assess Rabbenu Gershom intent that from the outset he meant to treat him with nezifah and nidui according to their view (Hagahot Mordechai Bava Kama in the name of Rashi responsum); but in a matter he explicitly decreed shall be in nidui forever, then he is in nidui forever if he transgressed intentionally (same place there).}
