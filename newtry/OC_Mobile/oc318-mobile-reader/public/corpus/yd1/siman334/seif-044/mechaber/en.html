A sage who ruled to permit in waters that have no end is liable for nidui; and likewise one who transgresses his vow; and any bet din that attends to him for a question—he is bar shamtah; and likewise those who do melacha while there is a dead person in the city who has not yet been buried—they are liable for nidui unless there are burial societies in the city; and likewise one who makes kal vachomer or gezeirah shavah to uproot something from the Torah; or asks something that is impossible; and likewise one who has a grievance against his fellow in one matter and delivers him to the king or to a judge—they place him under cherem until he brings him and they stand him before bet din; and likewise a Torah scholar who persists in dispute against the majority; and likewise one who rules a ruling in his rabbi's place.
