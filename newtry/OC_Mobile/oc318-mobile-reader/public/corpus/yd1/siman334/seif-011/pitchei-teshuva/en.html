It has no release. Rashbash 396 discusses a community placed under ban in a dream. He rules that one who is himself under ban may perhaps release others. Ten leading members of the congregation should therefore release the community together. Then ten others should release those first ten. If twenty learned men are unavailable, the first ten may be released one at a time by nine of their number joined by a tenth person, rotating until all are released. The Ramban and Rosh's limitation concerning a husband annulling vows for two wives applies only because Scripture says he shall annul 'her,' not her and another; ordinary people may release many at once. See also siman 234:29.
