(Seif 6) To reduce the nidui from thirty days. And likewise to reduce its restrictions, whose details are set out in this siman in the second law. Rika”sh. The source is in the Beit Yosef, who quotes the Kuntresim in the name of the Raavad: the restrictions of one under nidui, namely not sitting with him, not eating with him, haircutting, laundering, and shoes, apply only to an unspecified nidui, or where they said that he is in nidui except for such-and-such matters, in which case whatever they excluded is permitted and for the rest he remains like a full menudeh. But if they specified the nidui, everything follows their wording; if they specified that he is forbidden in such matters and for such days, only what they specified applies. Although the Kuntresim wonders what the source is that they may lighten the nidui, we have only the Raavad’s words. It requires study why the Mechaber omitted this point, that they may lighten the details of nidui.
