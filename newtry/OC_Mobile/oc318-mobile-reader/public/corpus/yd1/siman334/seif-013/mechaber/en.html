That which they say unspecified nidui is thirty—this is when imposing nidui they say so-and-so shall be in nidui thirty days; and nevertheless if he repented—if the nidui was for monetary matters or contempt of court and he appeased his litigant, they release him immediately; and even if he did not repent, some say they release him after thirty days provided he comes to bet din and asks that they release him; but if he treats nidui lightly and does not ask that they release him, they leave him in nidui until he dies; and some disagree (and hold that even after thirty they do not release him until he repents (Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam), and within thirty they do not release him even if he repented from what he did) (implied in Beit Yosef per Rambam view).
