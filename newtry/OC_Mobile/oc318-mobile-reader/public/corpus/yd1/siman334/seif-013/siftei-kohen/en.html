They say, "So-and-so shall be in nidui," etc. That is, when they place him under nidui without specification, we say that he shall be in nidui for thirty days.
If the nidui was because of money, etc. In the Tur, in the language of the Raavad, it says: or because of a transgression, etc. It seems to me that this is not precise, for if so, how could he then write that even if he did not retract, they release him after thirty days? How could they release him if he did not retract from the transgression? It is also explained at the beginning of the siman that if he did not retract, they wait another thirty days, etc. It would be forced to say that there it speaks where he does not come to court and request that they release him. Also in the Raavad's own language in the Asheri he did not write "or for a transgression," but wrote that in monetary matters they release him after thirty days even if he did not appease his litigant. So too it is in Rabbeinu Yerucham, netiv 14. It seems to me that "even if he did not retract" does not refer to a transgression. It appears that for this reason the Mechaber omitted "or for a transgression."
And there is one who disagrees with this. The Beit Yosef says this is the Rambam, whose words he copies below in seif 47, that it remains until the sage is appeased. It requires a little study why he brought the first view here, since below he rules anonymously like the Rambam. Perhaps he holds that a sage is different.
