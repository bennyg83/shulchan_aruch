(Seif 1, gloss) But not more. Rivash. I found in Responsa Maharam Alashkar, siman 90, that even when the tear extends beyond three lines it should be sewn. He cites a responsum of Rashbatz that rules so, and states that Rivash himself informed Rashbatz that he had retracted his responsum. See there. He also cites a responsum of Rabbi Shimshon of Sens validating even four or five lines. I found this responsum of Rabbi Shimshon of Sens in the glosses to Mordechai, Gittin. There is no doubt that had the Rav seen these authorities, he would not have written as he did.
