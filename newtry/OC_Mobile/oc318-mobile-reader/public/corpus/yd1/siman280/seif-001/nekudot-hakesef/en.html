(Siman 280, Taz seif katan 1) His words do not appear correct, for from the words of the poskim it is implied that sewing is required. Similarly implied by the Rivash siman 31, who wrote: if the old tear was two stitches and the new one within three — which can be repaired by sewing, etc. The Beis Yosef cites this, implying that even with two stitches one needs the repair of sewing. The proof he brought from the responsum of Maharik is not precise, for there the reason was that it is not invalid without sealing; therefore sealing was not required. But here one can say it is invalid without sewing.
