(Siman 280, Taz seif katan 1) His. His words do not appear correct, because the poskim imply that stitching is required. Rivash, siman 31, likewise writes that if the old tear was two lines and the new tear is within three lines, they can be repaired by stitching, as Beit Yosef cites. This implies that even a two-line tear requires the corrective act of stitching. Taz's proof from Responsa Maharik is not precise. There, because the item is not invalid without sealing, it does not require sealing. Here, however, it can be said that without stitching it is invalid.
(Seif 1 in the gloss) But not more. Rivash. I found in Responsa Maharam Alashkar, siman 90, that even if the tear exceeds three lines it may be stitched. He cites a responsum of Rashbatz ruling this way, and reports that Rivash himself sent word to Rashbatz that he had retracted his earlier responsum. He also cites a responsum of R. Shimshon of Sens permitting even a tear of four or five lines. I found that responsum in Hagahot Mordechai to Gittin. There is no doubt that had Rama seen these poskim, he would not have written otherwise.
