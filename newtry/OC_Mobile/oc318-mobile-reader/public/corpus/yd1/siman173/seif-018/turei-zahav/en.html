There are those who permit lending, etc. I see a stumbling here — for the language of this passage implies that those who permit are disputing Rivash above, but that does not appear to be so; for in Maggid Mishneh it is transcribed from a responsum of Binyamin Ze'ev siman 364, as follows: regarding the practice in this kingdom where at the fair of Cologne they give 12 dinars and receive in Worms or Mainz on the return 13 dinars — and Rabbeinu inquired of Rashi, who said: it is permitted only if the lender takes the merchandise that the borrower purchases with that money, and the lender or his agent transports it at the lender's liability until the place where the lender receives the money and gives him 13 dinars; but without this it is forbidden — end of quote. And it seems obvious to me that Rivash also agrees with this — for here there is no loan at all but a complete sale: the lender acquires the merchandise there, and upon returning home from the fair resells it to the other party; but as for the law of Rivash immediately preceding this — it is obvious that all agree it is forbidden, for there it is a loan, and one might have thought to permit it on account of the liability — and on this Rivash rules it is forbidden. This seems to me clear and simple.
