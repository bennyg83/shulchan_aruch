(°) Tosafot explains the example as a cow or garment. In my humble opinion, since he gives the money without specifying a particular cow or garment, and their value is known neither when he receives the money nor when he returns it, the rule of an established market price does not apply. The Sages therefore did not decree, because it does not appear as interest, since he may give him whichever cow or garment he chooses, even one of lesser value.
