To lend a gold dinar worth 20 dinarim on the responsibility of a ship that goes across the sea, such that he will give him upon its return 24 dinarim, is forbidden. {Rama: And some permit to lend someone 12 dinarim, that he will buy merchandise with them at the fair, and that he will give the lender, when he returns home, 13 dinarim — provided that the lender accepts the merchandise and brings it to his house, and responsibility for the road is on the lender; for it is like he has a share in the profit of the merchandise since he accepts responsibility; and so in the days of Rashi they were accustomed to be lenient (Binyamin Ze’ev, siman 364).}
