Forbidden. Radbaz prohibits lending one hundred for one hundred twenty conditioned on a ship arriving safely, where the lender would lose principal if it failed. If the loss condition is an unenforceable asmachta, the lender never truly bears the risk and the excess is fixed interest. Where the contingency is fully acquired from now, he concedes that it is only avak ribbit. If goods rather than money are transferred under a genuine present-risk sale, it may be permitted.
