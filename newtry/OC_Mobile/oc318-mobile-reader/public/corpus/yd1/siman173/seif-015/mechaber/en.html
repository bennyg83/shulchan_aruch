One who has merchandise that is sold here cheaply and in another place dearly, and his fellow said to him, “In the cheap place, give it to me, and I will bring it to the expensive place and sell it there, and I will use the money until such-and-such time, and I will repay you according to what it is worth there”: if responsibility in transport is on the buyer, it is forbidden; but if responsibility is on the seller, it is permitted, provided that he gives him wages for his exertion for bringing it to the expensive place. And if the seller is an important person, such that they exempt the buyer from the customs tax on his account, he need not give him wages for his exertion.
