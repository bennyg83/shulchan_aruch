It is forbidden to buy the fruit of an orchard before it is finished and ripened, because this that he sells now for 10 is fruit worth 20 when it finishes; it turns out that the addition is because of the credit. (And it is not comparable to the gourds above, which are permitted, for it is not the manner to sell orchard fruit in that way) (Maggid Mishneh, perek 9 of Hilchos Malveh, and Nimukei Yosef, perek Eizehu Neshech). But it is permitted to buy a calf cheaply and leave it in the seller’s hand until it grows, for if it dies or becomes weakened, it is in the buyer’s domain, and death is always common.
