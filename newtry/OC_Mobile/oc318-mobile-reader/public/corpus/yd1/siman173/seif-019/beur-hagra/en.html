To give, etc. He does not do this because the money was advanced, for he would have done the same even had the buyer paid only after the ship arrived. Rather, it is because the hundred litrin aboard the ship are highly exposed to loss, and he therefore sells them for a small amount. This is permitted even when the money is advanced, as stated in siman 173, seif 10: 'But it is permitted,' etc. In seif 18, however, he does not give him anything from the ship but pays from his own pocket, so it is a loan. Although he accepts responsibility for the ship, that is irrelevant, because it is a different matter. (End of supplement.)
