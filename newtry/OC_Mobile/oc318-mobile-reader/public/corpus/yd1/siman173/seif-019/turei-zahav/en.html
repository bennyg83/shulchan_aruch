To one who will guarantee 100 litrin, etc. There is no loan here, but something resembling a sale, for he accepts responsibility for 100 litrin of money. Even if the insured first gives him 20 litrin and later, when the loss occurs, receives 100 litrin from him, this is not considered an advance payment in which he gives 20 in order to receive 100 later. It is known that the 100 is not paid because the 20 was advanced, but because of the loss for which the guarantor accepted responsibility. The first 20 litrin were already acquired by the recipient. Furthermore, this resembles a penalty by which one obligates himself. So concludes the Rivash there. The gloss above in seif 4, concerning 'Release me from 12 dinars,' likewise implies this law.
