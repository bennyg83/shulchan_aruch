To sell him at credit for 12, since they are now worth 12 as any buyer would pay for them; only the seller in the market for less is one who discounts because he needs money — Beit Yosef. And see the responsum of Mabit vol. 2 siman 116 p. 54a: a dispute arose among the scholars of the generation regarding one who lent his fellow money on condition that he purchase known merchandise from him at more than its cash value — whether it is ribbit ketzutzah or avak ribbit; Maharai ibn Lev and his colleagues hold it is ribbit ketzutzah, and Mabit disagreed with them — see there for many distinctions at length.
