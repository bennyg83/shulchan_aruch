For a set time. For if they did not stipulate to give at a fixed time — there is no loan here, it is merely an agency; and even if they wish to take trouble and also accept responsibility — what of it, since there is no loan — Beit Yosef and Perishah.
And a distinguished person etc. When he accepts responsibility and also gives him payment for his trouble — even a distinguished person is permitted — Rivash; Beit Yosef brings it.
And some permit etc. There is no dispute here — for Rambam and the Mechaber also agree to this, as implied by the Maggid Mishneh and Beit Yosef — rather it speaks of a case where he does not give him payment for his trouble; and likewise below seif 18 where the Rabbi wrote "and some permit" — they do not dispute the first reason; and this is so in many places (also what is marked in Shulchan Aruch — Tur in the name of Rashi — is not precise, for Rashi permits even without responsibility and without payment for trouble, as Rivash and the Maggid Mishneh wrote).
