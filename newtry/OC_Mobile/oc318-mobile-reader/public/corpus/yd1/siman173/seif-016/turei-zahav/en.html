Some permit this for produce even when accepting liability. So wrote the commentators in the name of R' Tam and Ramban; and the reason is explained in Nimukei Yosef and Rivash siman 306: even though one might have thought to prohibit this, since from the moment he received the money and thereafter, had he needed to spend it he would have spent it — and therefore he accepts liability for the return journey with the produce and gives it to him at the cheaper price on his return — nevertheless it is permitted, for it is not compensation for the loan: even though he used the money on the way there, he is like a money-changer with whom a deposit was left, who repays it there; rather the reason they do this is for those two reasons mentioned above — they reveal to them the price and discount for them — and they are merely his agents; except that a guardian can accept upon himself to be like a borrower — and therefore he accepts liability for the merchandise on the return trip — end of quote. And one should not raise a difficulty from what is written in seif 15 — that even if one gives him compensation for his trouble, it is only permitted if the liability is on the seller — for there the case is different: there the merchandise involved is not produce, and there is no revealing of a market price there in truth. And furthermore it seems that specifically with a buyer of produce do we apply those two reasons — that the seller benefits from the fact that this buyer purchases merchandise for him, since when others see money in his hand many will rush to him; therefore this benefit comes in place of his accepting liability. But in the earlier case with the seller, the buyer gains nothing from people who come to purchase, since whoever comes to buy has no concern — he can buy in many places and need not ingratiate himself to anyone; and at the beginning of chapter Ein Mafkidin it says: "people's money does the brokering" — meaning the drovers themselves bring the grain to the money-holder — and thus the seller gains nothing; therefore the buyer must accept liability.
