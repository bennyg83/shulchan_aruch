If wheat in the city was 4 se’ah-measures for a sela, and in the villages 6 for a sela, it is permitted to give a sela to a merchant so that he will bring 6 se’ah-measures from the village for a certain time — provided that they are in the responsibility of the buyer if they are stolen or lost on the road. And an important person is forbidden to do this; and with types of merchandise it is forbidden for every person, because types of merchandise are not common like produce. {Rama: And some permit with produce even when responsibility for the road is on the merchant who brings them (Rambam and Rivash and Maggid Mishneh; and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and Maggid Mishneh), provided that he gives him wages for his exertion and labor.}
