If he sold him merchandise for 12 on condition that he will pay him after some time, he can say to him, “Pay me immediately and I will take from you only 10.” {Rama: And this is specifically when the sale was already concluded for 12 and the buyer already acquired it (Beit Yosef in the name of Nimukei Yosef and Mordechai). But before the sale was concluded, if he said to him, “Pay me now 10,” then it is forbidden to take 12 after some time if the buyer does not want to give now (explaining the words of Nimukei Yosef mentioned, according to Beit Yosef).}
