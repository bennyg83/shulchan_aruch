One who goes to milk his goats, to shear his sheep, to remove [honey] from his beehive, and said to his fellow, “That which my goats will produce, that which my sheep will shear, that which my hive will extract — sell to you the measure for such-and-such,” and he lowers [the price] for him because of the advancing of the money, it is forbidden. But if he said to him, “All that my goats will produce, and my sheep will shear, and my hive will extract — whether much or little — sell to you for such-and-such,” it is permitted, for it is as close to loss as to profit.
