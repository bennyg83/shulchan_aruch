The wording of the Rambam in chapter 8 of Hilchot Malveh, halachah 4. The Maggid Mishneh writes that he appears to have derived this from the Yerushalmi concerning the flax of Rav Chisda, in accordance with his own view and that of the Rif, who did not rule like Rav Nachman that a deferred-price sale is permitted.
