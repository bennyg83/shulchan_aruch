To a money-changer etc. Tur wrote in the name of the Rama: specifically a money-changer whose practice is such; but with another person it is forbidden unless he says to him explicitly by way of purchase and sale; unlike what Beit Yosef wrote — that Tosafot and the Rosh do not hold so.
A minted dinar. That is legal tender; and see the responsum of Maharam Elshaker siman 50.
