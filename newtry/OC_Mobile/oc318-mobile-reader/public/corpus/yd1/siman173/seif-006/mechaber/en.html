If his donkey drivers and his laborers were demanding from him in the market, and he said to the moneychanger, “Give me a stamped dinar (meaning, a dinar that has an image and goes out in circulation) to pay them, and I will give you more than its value — un-stamped coins that I have at home,” it is permitted, provided that he has all the value of the dinar. {Rama: And it appears to me from the words of the Asheri and Tosafos that he need not have all the value of the dinar in his hand, rather even a small amount of it, like the loan of a se’ah for a se’ah above siman 162; and so it is in some editions of the Tur, and this is correct in my opinion. And all this is permitted only [when exchanging] a dinar for coins, for it is by way of buying and selling; but coins for coins, which is by way of a loan — even if he has all of it — it is forbidden (Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and Rivash).}
