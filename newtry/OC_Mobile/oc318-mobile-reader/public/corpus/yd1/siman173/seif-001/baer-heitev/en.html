The seller. Taz writes that even if the buyer intends to leave the merchandise until prices rise, it is forbidden, because at the time he takes it the transaction is already prohibited.
At the higher price. Baal HaTerumot writes that if the seller did not say, 'If you pay now the price is this, but later it is more,' and merely fixed delivery in Iyar at the Iyar price, the transaction is permitted whether the future price rises or falls, even if it usually rises by then. With land and other items lacking a fixed market price or subject to onaah, an ordinary sale is permitted unless the seller expressly sets one price for immediate payment and another for deferred payment. If done improperly, the prohibition is only avak ribbit because it arose through a sale. Shach.
