The Baal HaTerumot writes that whenever he did not say, "if you give from now," etc., but simply said, "in Iyar you will give me according to the Iyar rate," whether that time will be expensive or cheap, it is permitted, even though prices normally rise in Iyar. Even if it now has a fixed market price, it is permitted. With land, since land has no law of onaah and nothing comparable is sold in the market, whenever it is done in the manner of buying and selling and does not appear as ribit, it is permitted, unless he explicitly stated, "if from now, for such-and-such, and if at such-and-such later time, for such-and-such," for then it is forbidden even if the buyer has not yet taken possession of the land. The same applies to anything without a fixed measure and onaah. However, if he transgressed and sold even where it is forbidden, it is only avak ribit, for since it is only in the manner of sale, it is rabbinic. Thus the Shach.
