If the seller is wealthy. This means that he sells him an item that is expected to rise in price, at the price for which the buyer now acquires it from him. With respect to the seller, who is wealthy, there is no benefit, for he could leave the merchandise until the time of the higher price. Nevertheless, it is forbidden, for we follow the buyer. Had he possessed money, he could have bought it cheaply now, and since he lacks money, he takes it at the future higher price. It appears obvious that even if the buyer also wishes to leave it until the time of the higher price, it is forbidden, for in any event, at the time he takes it from him he takes it through a forbidden arrangement. In siman 169, at the beginning of seif 18, I cited this law; see there what I derived from it.
