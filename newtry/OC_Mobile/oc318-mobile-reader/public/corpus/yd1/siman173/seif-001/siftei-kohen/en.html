And he does not need money etc.
And he sells him something that will appreciate — as it will be at the time of the price rise — for now the wealthy man has no profit from this transaction, for had he not acquired it he would have held it in his hand until the time of the price rise; nevertheless it is forbidden, since if the buyer had money he would buy at the current rate, and now in payment for the waiting of the money the buyer takes at the future higher rate — Talmud; and see the responsum of Mabit vol. 2 siman 114 p. 53b.
To pay dearly at a fixed time etc.
Beit HaTalmud wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Tam: whenever he did not say to him “if you give now etc.” but said to him simply “in Iyar you will give me at the rate of Iyar” — whether that time will be expensive or cheap — it is permitted, even though they tend to be expensive in Iyar; and Beit Yosef brings it; and it implies that even if they have a known rate now — it is permitted.
That he should not say explicitly etc. And Rambam wrote that if he transgressed and sold with "if from now at such-and-such etc." — it is avak ribbit; and Beit Yosef wrote that since it is only by way of sale — it is only rabbinic.
