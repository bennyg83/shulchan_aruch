That it has no known market price. Chatam Sofer permits a transaction where a genuine liquor merchant bought on credit at a higher price and later resold below cost for immediate cash. This differs from a sham intermediary who buys solely to provide money, and from one who takes goods on credit only to resell immediately at a loss. Where the buyer genuinely trades in that commodity and the facts do not prove an interest arrangement, it is permitted.

Foreign coins without a fixed local valuation resemble property without a known price, and payment elsewhere compensates for transport, expense, and risk.
