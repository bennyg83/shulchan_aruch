It is permitted to advance money on wine at the time of the grape harvest, on condition that he will give him good wine in Nisan. And likewise, it is permitted to advance money on a known barrel of wine and stipulate with the seller that if it becomes cheaper or more expensive until the time of selling the wine, it will be in the buyer’s domain, and if it turns sour it will be in the seller’s domain. But if the buyer does not accept upon himself responsibility for the cheaper price, it is forbidden, even if he pulls it into his domain.
