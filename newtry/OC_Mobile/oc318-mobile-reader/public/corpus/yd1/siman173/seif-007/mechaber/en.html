If he buys an item worth 12 for 10 because he advances the money: if it is in the domain of the seller, only that it is not accessible to him until his son comes, or until he finds the key, it is permitted. But if it is not in his domain, it is forbidden — even if he has of that item on credit in the hands of others. {Rama: And the seller is believed to say that he has [it] (Hagahos Mordechai, beginning of perek Eizehu Neshech). And all this is permitted only when it is unspecified; but if he explains, “If you give me now I will give you for 10, and if after some time, for 12,” it is forbidden (Tur in the name of R' Yeshayah). And all this is regarding something whose appraisal is known; but if its appraisal is not known, even if he does not have it, it is permitted (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos and Rosh) — provided it is unspecified, but not if he explains (there).}
