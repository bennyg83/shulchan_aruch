(Shach, seif katan 15) He may not reduce the price for him. Tiferet LeMoshe writes that it is effective if he already possesses the amount for which he reduces the price, together with a little more. Thus, if he sells six for five, it is permitted when he possesses goods worth one and slightly more, for he transfers the entire quantity to him for one perutah, while the remainder is a permitted obligation to supply at market value. See there. Responsa Mahari Halevi, brother of the Taz, siman 4, writes similarly. Nevertheless, he must not reduce the price excessively on the quantity already in his possession, lest it be evident to all that the reduction is given because of the remaining loan from the dinar.
