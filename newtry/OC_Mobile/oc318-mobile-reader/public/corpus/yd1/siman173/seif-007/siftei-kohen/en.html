To sell for less — and specifically when the market rate has been established; but if the rate has not been fixed — even for equal value — it is forbidden to sell when not in his possession, for perhaps the price will rise and it would be ribbit, as below siman 175; and likewise Rivash siman 306; and clearly so from Tosafot (63b) s.v. "va'amar lo" etc. — and this is obvious; and accordingly where we permit when their value is not known — even when he does not have them — it is specifically when the market rate has been established, and not as implied by Pri Megadim.
