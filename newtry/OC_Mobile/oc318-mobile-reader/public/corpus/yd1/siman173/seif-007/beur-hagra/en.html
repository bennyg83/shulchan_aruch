All this, etc. The Tur cites Rabbi Yeshayah, contrary to the view of those who disagree. The latter view derives from Baal HaTerumot, who answered Tosafot's question there, s.v. 'for he said to him,' etc., by explaining that the case is one in which he explicitly fixed the higher deferred price, which is prohibited as a deferred-price arrangement, as stated there. His second answer accords with Tosafot's answer there. Rabbi Yeshayah's words are correct, however, for otherwise why does the Gemara on 65a challenge Rav Nahman from the Mishnah? He owned land. His words require study.
