The house of, etc. The Geonim explained Rav Nahman's deferred-price arrangement as stated in the gloss. This is the meaning of the gloss's statement, 'All this applies when he fixed the price,' as stated there: 'There he fixed,' etc. Rav Hama's arrangement, however, is permitted even when the higher price was fixed, as Tosafot write, s.v. 'and the halachah.' They explain it as stated here. According to their explanation, the merchants are pleased that the goods remain under my authority because they want them to remain under my authority for purposes of customs duties. Since they remain under my authority with respect to unavoidable loss, the arrangement is permitted.
