A barrel of wine that is worth now a dinar, and he sold it to him for 2 until the summer, on condition that if a mishap occurs to it, it remains in the seller’s domain until the buyer sells it — this is permitted, for if it is lost or breaks he pays nothing; and if he did not find to sell it and profit from it, he would have to return it to the owners. And likewise, if he sold it to him for 2 and said to him, “The excess over 2 is because you are exerting yourself to sell it; and if you do not find to sell it as you wish, return it to me,” this is permitted, even though if it is lost or stolen or turns sour it will be in the buyer’s domain. {Rama: And all this is when he fixed a fixed amount for the price of the barrel that he is selling; but if he sells to him without a fixed amount — for example, he sold him 7 barrels and said to him, “Pay me for each barrel according to the rate at the time you take each barrel” — it is permitted, even though now it is not worth so much (Hagahos Mordechai in the name of Rav Hai; and so is implied from a responsum of the Rif that the Beit Yosef brought).}
