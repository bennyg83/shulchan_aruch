Responsibility. Without genuine seller responsibility, the transaction is not a sale but a loan, and the excess is interest. Taz. Shach adds that even responsibility only for the money received may forbid, whether the seller transfers part or all of the debt.
The seller. The same applies if the seller accepts responsibility in case collection becomes impossible because of a royal decree. Responsa of Rashba.
An office. Ramban explains that one gives two hundred gold coins now so another will assume a royal office worth three hundred. This is forbidden because the advance payment causes him to discharge a greater obligation.
