It is permitted to sell them etc. Hagahot Asher wrote that we require the lender to release from the debt and the buyer to acquire it in the presence of three; and if he has a document on it he should hand it over to the buyer and write to him “acquire for yourself it and all its liens” (as written in CM siman 66); and if the borrower is a non-Jew and the seller said to the buyer “I forgive the non-Jew all that he owes me and I will not inform him, and trust me and I will give it to you and I will not acquire it for myself but for your benefit, and from now I am free from you” — it is permitted — end; and Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe bring it; and see above siman 169 end of seif 18.
Provided etc. It implies that even if he accepts responsibility only for the money he received from the buyer — it is forbidden; and likewise Darkei Moshe; and so is the implication of the poskim; and Terumas HaDeshen siman 303 wrote there is no distinction between one who sells part of his debt and one who sells all of his debt — Darkei Moshe.
Forbidden etc. Specifically the case (above) where the borrower gives the lender himself less than he owes to be released from his debt — that is permitted, as it is like purchasing from him; but the borrower is forbidden to give another person less than his debt so that that person will pay his debt to the lender when the time comes — for this is full ribbit, as written above siman 169; and likewise Rashba in responsum siman 686 etc. — Darkei Moshe.
