And smoked is not like cooked. Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe wrote; but it appears that even though a Jew made them, if a non-Jew cooked them it is forbidden, similar to large fish below — end of his words. And it appears it appears to refer also to small fish; but in Issur ve-Heter, general rule 43, halachah 16, he wrote that small fish — even if a non-Jew smoked them and afterward cooked them — are permitted, for smoking is equivalent in all its kinds to salting — see there. And accordingly, for those who permit in seif 12 large salted fish that a non-Jew cooked, the same applies to large smoked fish that a non-Jew cooked — they are permitted; and so the Bach in a later pamphlet.
