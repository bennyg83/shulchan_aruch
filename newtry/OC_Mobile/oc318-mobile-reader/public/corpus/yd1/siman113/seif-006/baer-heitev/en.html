Behold, the Taz and Shach challenged the Rama that this ruling is against the Gemara — see there. And one must say that here we deal with a case where it could not be fully cooked without the aid of gentiles, but as maachal ben drusai it could have been cooked by a Jew alone. And the Taz wrote that in any case we establish that there are disputants whom the Rama brought below, that lighting a fire or stoking coals is effective.
