6) Chestnuts (called in Arabic kastana) that a gentile picked are permitted, for they are eaten as they are when raw, even though most of their eating is by roasting and cooking. Birkei Yosef there, note 8, in the name of Radak. However, Peri Chadash, note 22, brought a dispute whether they are eaten raw; and therefore we follow this according to place and time, as written in the previous note: if most of that place eat them raw, there is no [prohibition of] bishul akum; if not, there is bishul akum.
