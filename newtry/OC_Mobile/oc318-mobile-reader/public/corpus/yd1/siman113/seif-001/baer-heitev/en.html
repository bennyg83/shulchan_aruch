"Rises [on the king's table]." The Shach wrote that from the words of the Mechaber and the Rav in Darkei Moshe it appears that even if it was not changed from its natural state by fire it is forbidden; but in truth the essence of the Gemara appears that it is not forbidden because of bishul akum except when it was changed from its natural state by fire. And the Issur ve-Heter wrote that gizzards, crop, and innards — even though we said above in siman 87 that their food is not human food and is not a piece worthy of honor — nevertheless they are forbidden because of bishul akum; and herbs and mushrooms rise on the king's table as relish, and there is in them [the prohibition] of bishul akum — end of his words.
