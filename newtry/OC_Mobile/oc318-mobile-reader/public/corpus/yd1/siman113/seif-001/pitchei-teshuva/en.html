The Avodat HaTaharah of Maharait wrote that the essence of the decree was because of [intermarriage] feasts, etc.; and see in Sefer Tiferet Moshe, who wrote that according to this reason the cooking of an apostate is permitted, for there is no [concern of] feasts (see above, siman 112, what is written there); except that there was another reason explained in Beit Yosef, lest he feed him forbidden things; and accordingly, the cooking of an apostate is also forbidden, for he is like a denier of the entire Torah and lest he feed him forbidden things — see there. And accordingly, the same applies to an apostate who desecrates Shabbos in public, or [denies] the entire Torah except for two [commandments], whose law is like a non-Jew, as above siman 2 seif 5, and below siman 119 — his cooking is likewise forbidden.
