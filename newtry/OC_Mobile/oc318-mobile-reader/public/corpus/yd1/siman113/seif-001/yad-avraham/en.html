(Siman 113, seif 16.) Vessels in which a non-Jew cooked, etc. Even according to those who require kashering — if it is an earthenware vessel, one purges it three times and that suffices, because this prohibition has no Torah root. In Be'er HaGolah he showed his source from the words of the Rashba — investigate: in Niddah, chapter of HaSefinah, he wrote in the name of the Rashba that even in a matter that has no Torah root they did not permit except for terumah, which is a rabbinic prohibition that is not equal in all respects. And so there in the name of the Rambam, who wrote that one does not learn from terumah to other rabbinic prohibitions. And see in Ketzot HaChoshen in explanation of Yerushalmi Pesachim, who challenged that in Yerushalmi there Rav said thus regarding chametz on Pesach for cooking — hagalah in boiling water is effective three times, even though it has a Torah root.
