Avodat HaTaharah; and responsa Avkat Rokhel siman 134: wheat that gentiles parboil until it reaches maachal ben drusai and return and dry them to make groats — and again they are not eaten except through further cooking — they are permitted, since they are dried so much that they are not eaten except through further cooking and the first cooking is not recognizable in them — see there. And see responsa Mayim Rabim, volume 4, from siman 15 through siman 19, on permitting chestnuts and lupines that gentiles cooked in their intestines, that there is no [prohibition] of bishul akum or gentile vessels; and there a responsum from one who challenged this, and the author returned and answered him — see there.
