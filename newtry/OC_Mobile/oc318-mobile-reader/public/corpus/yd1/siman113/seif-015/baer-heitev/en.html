"Dates." Issur ve-Heter wrote: however, these pears and apples — even though most of their eating is by roasting and cooking, if a non-Jew roasted and cooked them they are permitted, for they are nevertheless also eaten raw; and moreover most of the essence of their eating is raw — until here. And he wrote in the Taz that those large nuts that they spice with their bitter peel and their pits are forbidden because of bishul akum, for they are not eaten raw and rise on the king's table — end of his words.
