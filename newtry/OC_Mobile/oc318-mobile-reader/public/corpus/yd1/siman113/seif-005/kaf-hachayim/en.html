5) And even herbs and mushrooms have [the prohibition of] bishul akum, for they rise as relish. Issur ve-Heter there. Torat Chatat there. Darkei Moshe there. Shach there. Knesset HaGedolah there. Chochmat Adam, klal 66, note 4. However, Peri Chadash at the end of note 3 disagrees and wrote that herbs and mushrooms do not rise on the king's table and there is no [prohibition of] bishul akum; and so agreed Arukh HaShulchan, note 10. And Peri Toar, note 7, wrote that in our places herbs and mushrooms rise on the king's table and are for improvement, as Issur ve-Heter's view; and what Peri Chadash rejected from the Gemara is no proof, for nowadays they do rise — and everything is according to the place. And so Minchat Yitzchak in Sulam at the end of Torat Chatat, general rule 71, halachah 16. And so Birkei Yosef in Shivhei Yosef, note 9, in the name of Radak, that everything is according to place and time. And so Chochmat Adam there. And see below, note 10 and note 11.
