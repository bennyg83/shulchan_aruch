An egg, etc. The reason is that eating it is by pressing; and even those who permit in seif 12 with large fish agree here, and with bitter dates in seif 15 — for here they are not eaten except by great pressing; and so the Tur explicitly concludes "by great pressing." Darkei Moshe wrote in the name of the gloss of Alfasi, chapter of Ein Ma'amidin: those large nuts that they spice with their bitter peel and pits are forbidden because of bishul akum, for they are not eaten raw and rise on the king's table — end of his words.
