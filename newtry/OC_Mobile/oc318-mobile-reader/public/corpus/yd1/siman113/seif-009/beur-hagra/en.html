If they cooked it, etc. As written: everything that was cooked as maachal ben drusai, etc., even though he placed it on a tile; and the same applies to the reverse.
And there are those who permit, etc. As written there: until the beginning and end are, etc.; and only for leniency they said "everything," etc., like approximations of cooking as above; and the first version holds this is called "finishing"; and nevertheless he relied on this view in the Shulchan Aruch on erev [Shabbos], etc.
(Collection) And there are those who permit, etc.: according to the first view as written, and a Jew turned it over — it should have said until it did not reach maachal ben drusai; but in Terumat HaDeshen 90b he decided it is forbidden from what is written: this non-Jew singed, etc. — it appears that if he intended for cooking it is forbidden, even though it is impossible that by singeing it becomes fully roasted (until here).
