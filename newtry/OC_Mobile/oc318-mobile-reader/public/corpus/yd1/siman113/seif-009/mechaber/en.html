If a non-Jew cooked it as maachal ben drusai and a Jew finished it — there is [reason] to forbid, unless it is erev Shabbos or erev Yom Tov, or there is great loss in the matter. (And there are those who permit in every case) (Tur in the name of the Rosh, Ran, Rif, Maharam, Issur ve-Heter, and Semak siman 223 — R' Yakar did so in practice; and so in Kol Bo; and so in Semak, Mordechai, Agudah, chapter of Avodah Zarah, and Rae in Sefer Beit Hashem; and Semag daf 57 side 2; and so received from Maran and R' Yerucham, section 17 chapter 7 — for so agreed most poskim, and not as the Beit Yosef challenged in Beit Hashem on R' Yerucham in this — see there). And so is the practice.
