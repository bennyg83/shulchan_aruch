10) A food eaten as it is when raw, and one person cannot eat it except roasted or cooked — his view is nullified before all people, since most of the world eat it as it is when raw, and it is permitted for him too; and likewise the reverse: if the majority do not eat it as it is when raw, even though this person eats it as it is when raw, there is [the prohibition of] bishul akum, and for him too it is forbidden. Responsa of the Rikash called Ohalei Yaakov, siman 58. Peri Chadash, note 3. Minchat Yitzchak in Sulam at the end of Torat Chatat, general rule 71, halachah 16. Lechem HaPanim, note 2.
