Except for bread. In the Ran it is written the reason that heating the oven for bread is a special labor, and Beit Yosef brings it — end of his words. It appears his explanation is that for bread one must kindle the oven and remove all the coals before baking the bread, which is not so for other dishes — one kindles the fire while the dish cooks and constantly adds; therefore a Jew's action is not so recognizable.
And there are disputants, etc. I already wrote what appears to my humble opinion on this; and likewise on what the Rama wrote here, and even stoking without intention — I wrote below that Issur ve-Heter disagrees, and Darkei Moshe wrote that the same applies to blowing on the fire with the mouth — it is effective.
