Avodat HaTaharah; and see in Sefer Tiferet Moshe, who wrote that nevertheless, if a non-Jew cooked it as maachal ben drusai and removed it from the fire and returned — whether that non-Jew or another non-Jew — and finished it, it is forbidden. And some say regarding small fish that a non-Jew salted and returned and roasted them — below seif 12, that for salting they did not decree; but here the beginning and end are in the hand of a non-Jew — what difference is there whether in one action or that he took it in the interim? However, if a Jew took it from the fire and a non-Jew returned it — requires study; so too his conclusion — see there.
