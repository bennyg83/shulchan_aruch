"Forbidden." Specifically of fish or raw fat of meat; but fat of meat or of goose that has already been rendered has no [prohibition] of bishul akum, for there is no cooking after cooking. Shach.
"In bread." The Taz wrote, and some say [this refers to] bread kneaded with eggs in siman 112, because there the flour is primary and the eggs are secondary; not so here, since the fat is still visible and is not nullified.
