There are those who permit with our maidservants, and there are those who forbid, even b'dieved (responsum of the Rashba, siman 68). {Rama: And b'dieved one may rely on the words of those who permit (Arukh, general rule 43, and glosses of ShaDa). And even l'chatchila they practice leniency in a Jewish home where maidservants and slaves cook in a Jewish home, because it is impossible that one of the household will not stoke [the fire] a little (there).}
