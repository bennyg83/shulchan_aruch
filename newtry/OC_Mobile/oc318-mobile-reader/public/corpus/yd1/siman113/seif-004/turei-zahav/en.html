With our maidservants. He explains: those acquired by us, and we are warned about their labor on Shabbos from the Torah; they are not included among this. Not gentile maidservants hired to a Jew for a term; but in Issur ve-Heter, general rule 43, he wrote to permit with these too b'dieved and to rely on R' Avraham whom the Tur brings, that in a Jewish home there is no [prohibition of] bishul akum, even though we do not rule like him.
For it is impossible that he will not stoke, etc. Darkei Moshe wrote that we learn from here that even if he did not intend to permit through this stoking but did it without intention, it is permitted; and so too in Kol Bo — end of his words; and so too the Rama here below; and in Issur ve-Heter, general rule 43, he wrote: but one should be careful that this stoking should help repair the fire and help hasten its cooking by a Jew — end of his words.
