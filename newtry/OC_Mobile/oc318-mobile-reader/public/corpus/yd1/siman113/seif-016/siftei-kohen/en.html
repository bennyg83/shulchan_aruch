And one cannot challenge: behold, he wrote there halachah 17 that b'dieved they practice to permit even a dish cooked in a Jewish home — one may say as written in the gloss at the end of Sefer Issur ve-Heter: even though she cooked in a Jewish home, if she cooks for herself it is worse; and although inside Turei Chayim he permits in a Jewish home because it is impossible that one of the household will stoke — one may say that if she cooks for herself there is no custom at all that one of the household will stoke:
