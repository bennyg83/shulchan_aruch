Vessels in which a non-Jew cooked before us things that have [the prohibition of] bishul akum require kashering. And some say they do not require it. And even according to those who require kashering — if it is an earthenware vessel, one purges it three times and that suffices, because this prohibition has no Torah root. {Rama: A non-Jew who cooked for a sick person on Shabbos — it is permitted after Shabbos even for a healthy person, and there is no [prohibition] of bishul akum in it, for in every such case there is publicity (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ran, who so wrote in the name of the Rae).}
