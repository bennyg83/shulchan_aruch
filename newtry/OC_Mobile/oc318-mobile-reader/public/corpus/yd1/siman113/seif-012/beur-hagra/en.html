And there are those who permit. As the Rif and Rosh wrote in the name of a Gaon: Abaye refers only to an egg; but for fish we return to the general rule, and his words are not, etc.; and moreover his teachers were not like Rabbeinu Yonah; and so he wondered in Terumat HaDeshen.
And so he ruled, etc. As written in the Shulchan Aruch: but, etc., they are not, etc. — and this is from Terumat HaDeshen, who distinguished between them and small fish; and as written in the Gemara in small fried fish; and as written in Semak.
