That a non-Jew cooked it, etc. — whether cooking of a pot or cooking of roasting; for roasting too is called cooking, as stated in Divrei HaYamim: "and they cooked the Pesach"; and so it is proven in the Gemara from the case of a gentile who singed the head of his ship, and from the case of a Jew who placed meat on coals. And so the Rashba in Torat HaBayit. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur, note 1. Peri Chadash, note 1. And so it is proven below, seif 12.
