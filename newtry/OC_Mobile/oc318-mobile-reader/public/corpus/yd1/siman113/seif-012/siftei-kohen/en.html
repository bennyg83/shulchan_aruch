Small fish, etc. Tosafot and other poskim wrote that this means even somewhat large; for if completely small — behold, we say in the Gemara that even if small fish were cooked, there is no [prohibition of] bishul akum in them, for they do not rise on the king's table. The Bach wrote that when small fish do not rise on the king's table, that is with small fish whose growth is large, and those fish in their small state are not significant and do not rise on the king's table; but small fish whose growth is always small are significant and rise on the king's table; and he brought proofs — see there:
