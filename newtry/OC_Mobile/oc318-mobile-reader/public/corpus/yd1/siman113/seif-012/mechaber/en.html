Small fish that a Jew or a non-Jew salted — behold, they are as though partially cooked; and if a non-Jew afterward roasted them, they are permitted. But large salted fish are not eaten except by pressing; therefore, if a non-Jew roasted them, they are forbidden. And there are those who permit. {Rama: And likewise anything eaten raw by pressing — if a non-Jew cooked it, its law is like large fish (private opinion). And salted meat is not eaten at all as it is raw, and it is forbidden if cooked by a non-Jew (Tosafot, chapter of Chalitzah).}
