(Siman 333, seif 5) He is responsible for it. This applies specifically while some of the produce still exists, so that he can yet fulfill the mitzvah. If all of it was lost and he can give only its value, it is property for which there is no claimant, and he is exempt, like one who damages priestly gifts. See above, siman 61, and Mishneh LaMelech, Laws of First Fruits 10:8.
