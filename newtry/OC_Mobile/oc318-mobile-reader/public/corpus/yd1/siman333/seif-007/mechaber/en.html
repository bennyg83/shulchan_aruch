One who buys the shearing of a gentile's flock after the gentile sheared them is exempt from reishit ha'gez; but if he bought the flock for shearing, he is obligated even though the shearing grew in the gentile's possession, and even though the flock returns to the gentile after shearing he is obligated. (Some disagree and say exempt if he did not buy the flock for shearing) (Rashi's view and Tur).
