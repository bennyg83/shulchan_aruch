For their shearing. Meaning that he bought the fleece while it was still attached. Even though he did not buy the body of the sheep, only the fleece, he is obligated, despite the fleece having grown in the non-Jew's possession and despite the principal sheep belonging to the non-Jew. Those who disagree hold that he is obligated only if the body of the sheep belongs to him at the time of shearing. End quote from Shach.
