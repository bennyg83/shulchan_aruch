How much, etc. This follows Beit Hillel, Rabbi Dosa, and the Sages, for the sugya of the Gemara there, 137b, follows them, unlike Rabbi Yose bar Rabbi Yehudah and Rabbi. So rule the Rambam, the Rosh, and the other poskim, not as appears from the words of the Rif.
And this is when it will be, etc. This follows Rav, Shmuel, and Rabbi Yochanan, for Rabbi Yochanan does not disagree with them.
And it shall be, etc.; and if, etc. This follows Rav, for on this point Rav and Shmuel disagree, and the halachah follows Rav in matters of prohibition. This is unlike what the Kesef Mishneh wrote, that they do not disagree. All this is the wording of the Rambam and follows the view of the Rif; but the Rosh and the Tur rule like Rabbi Yannai. The Rambam and Shulchan Aruch require study for omitting that the measure given to one kohen is one sixtieth.
