If he had five sheep and sheared only one and sold its shearing, and afterward sheared a second and sold its shearing, and afterward sheared a third and sold its shearing—all combine for reishit ha'gez and he is obligated after several years (and likewise if he sold some of the flock after he began to shear); and he may separate from the new for the old and from this for that. (If he had two types, bad and good, he does not give from the bad for the good but gives from each according to its measure) (Tur). But if he had one ewe and sheared it and afterward bought a second and sheared it and left its shearing, and afterward bought a third and sheared it and left its shearing—they do not combine.
