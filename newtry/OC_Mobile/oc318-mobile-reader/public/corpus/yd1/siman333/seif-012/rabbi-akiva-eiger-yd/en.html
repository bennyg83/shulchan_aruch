(Seif 12) And he should separate from new for old. See the Ran, brought in the Beit Yosef, that since the world practices like Rabbi Ila'i regarding reishit ha-gez, that it does not apply outside Eretz Yisrael, one can no longer be lenient to separate new for old. According to Rabbi Ila'i, who derives reishit ha-gez from terumah regarding outside the Land, we likewise derive from terumah that one may not separate new for old or from one species for another. See the sugya. Nevertheless, regarding what the Rabbis are stringent about, that new and old and different species combine for obligation, one separates from this for that, because either way: according to the Rabbis one may separate from this for that, and according to Rabbi Ila'i they do not combine and there is no obligation. See Mutzal MeEsh responsa siman 57, p. 30b.
