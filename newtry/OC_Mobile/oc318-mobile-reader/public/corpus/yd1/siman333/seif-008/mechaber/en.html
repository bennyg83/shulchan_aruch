One who buys the shearing of his fellow's flock—if the seller left a little of his flock to shear, the buyer must separate from what remains for everything, even though the seller had not begun to shear; and if he left nothing, the buyer must separate; and if there were two types, such as white shearing and dark shearing (meaning red), and he sold this type and left the other type—this one gives for himself on what he bought and that one gives for himself on what he sold. {Rama: If one bought the shearing of his fellow's flock and the seller left a little shearing for himself, he is obligated; if he left none of them, the buyer must give; and if he specified to him "I sell you everything," both are exempt (Tur).}
