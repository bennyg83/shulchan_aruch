One who had many shearings of reishit ha'gez and wishes to divide among kohanim should not give each less than the weight of five sela of whitened wool for a small garment—not that he whitens and afterward gives, but he gives him wool of the shearing while it is soiled (meaning dirty) so that after whitening there will be in it five sela or more, as it says "you shall give to him" that there be in it a fitting gift. (But he is not obligated to give only five sela from everything) (Tur).
