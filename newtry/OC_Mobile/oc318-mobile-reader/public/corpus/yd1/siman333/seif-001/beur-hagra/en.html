Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak did not say this as a matter of law, for the halachah certainly follows all the Tannaim and Amoraim who disagree with him; and Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak himself imposed a penalty regarding what is tamei, and the same applies to reishit ha-gez, for they have one law, and likewise regarding immersion and kilayim. Rather, he stated a custom. See the Rosh at the beginning of the chapter, and Tosafot 136b, s.v. ke-Rabbi Eliezer, etc.; their words imply like the first view, and this is also the opinion of all the poskim.
