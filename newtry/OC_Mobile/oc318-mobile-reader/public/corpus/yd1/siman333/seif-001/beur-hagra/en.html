And some say. Tur. Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak did not state this as strict law, because the halachah certainly follows the many Tannaim and Amoraim who disagree with him. Rav Nachman bar Yitzchak himself imposed a penalty regarding impure gifts, and reishit hagez follows the same law. The same is true of immersion and kilayim. Rather, he was describing custom. See Rosh at the beginning of the chapter and Tosafot 136b. Their words imply the first opinion, which is also the view of the poskim generally.
