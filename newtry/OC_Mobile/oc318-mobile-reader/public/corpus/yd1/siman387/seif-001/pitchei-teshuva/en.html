The ground. Responsa Divrei Yosef, siman 61, discusses a mourner within shivah on Erev Yom Kippur who wishes to eat the pre-fast meal seated on a chair or bench. He rules that one who is lenient has not lost, unlike Magen Avraham in siman 606, who appears stringent even during that meal, since the mourning rule extends only until nightfall. Responsa Shemesh Tzedakah, Orach Chayyim simanim 10 and 12, writes similarly.
