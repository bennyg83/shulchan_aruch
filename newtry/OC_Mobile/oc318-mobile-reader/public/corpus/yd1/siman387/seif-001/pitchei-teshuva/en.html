The ground. Divrei Yosef responsa siman 61 wrote that regarding a mourner within seven days, on Erev Yom Kippur, who wishes to eat the final meal seated on a chair or bench, one who is lenient loses nothing. This is unlike the Magen Avraham, whose words in siman 606 imply that he is stringent even during the meal before the fast, since he says "until nightfall"; see there. So too Shemesh Tzedakah responsa, Orach Chayim I siman 10, wrote, and see there siman 12.
