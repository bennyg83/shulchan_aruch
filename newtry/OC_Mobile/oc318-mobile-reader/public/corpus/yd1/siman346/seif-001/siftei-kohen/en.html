He should not remain there. Because he causes disgrace to the deceased, since people will say, "That hanged person is the brother of so-and-so." Derishah writes that once the body is buried, it is permitted. Bach writes that this applies specifically where the hanged person is within the city. In our lands, where hangings occur outside the city in open fields, it is permitted to remain in the towns. Therefore, this law effectively does not apply nowadays.
