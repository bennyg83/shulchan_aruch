Provided that it is arranged as a half-brick over a brick. Meaning, a 'brick' is the full measure of three letters, while a 'half-brick' is half that measure. The brick is therefore larger than the half-brick, and the lines must be written in the corresponding pattern of a half-brick above a brick. This applies to the Song at the Sea. In the Song of Ha'azinu, however, one does not write a half-brick over a brick; rather, every line is divided into two parts, as stated below in seif 5.
