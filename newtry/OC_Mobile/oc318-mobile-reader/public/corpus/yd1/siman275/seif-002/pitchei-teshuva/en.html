Closed made open. See responsum of Noda BiYehudah second series Yoreh De'ah siman 170 — in a Torah scroll found in parashat Devarim in a closed parashah between "and you turned Mount Seir many days" and "and the Lord said" — the scribe ended "many days" in the middle of the bottom line and wrote the word "and He said" at the head of the page, and it was found made open — what is its law; and he brought words of R. Azriel in responsum Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 24 who ruled to erase the word "and He said" at the head of the line and write it at end of the bottom line and it will be a closed parashah; and although part will remain at the head of the line we do not care since it is not nine letters' measure. And he wrote Derishah ruled thus; but if the word "and He said" is too wide so that after dragging part remains for three words "wine" — one cannot do so, only make another correction — drag the closed mem of the word "many" and extend until less than nine letters remain, drag the word "and He said" and rewrite at same head of line but leave a little space at head of line to write three letters tightly — see there.
