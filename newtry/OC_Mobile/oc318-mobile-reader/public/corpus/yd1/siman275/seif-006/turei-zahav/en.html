Not fewer than forty-eight. The Perishah cites an explanation that forty-eight corresponds to Israel's forty-eight journeys, as in the verse, “Moshe wrote their departures according to their journeys,” while sixty corresponds to the six hundred thousand Israelites. The difficulty is that the Tur permits any number between forty-eight and sixty. It appears that the beginning of the sacred measure is forty-eight and its completion is sixty, so every number within that range belongs to the same sacred proportion. Fewer or more are improper.
And some say forty-two. The mnemonic is the phrase “Hashem bam Sinai bakodesh,” whose numerical association indicates forty-two.
“And she took a horse.” This is also the reading in the Tur, but it would make the line disproportionately long relative to the surrounding lines. I found a corrected reading: “And she took, after her, a horse.” This is correct, and the text here should be corrected accordingly.
