And beneath it five lines, beginning: 'Vayavo,' 'Vayedaber,' etc. The Rambam's text reads: 'Vayavo, ledaber, asher, hazot, asher,' etc. The Ma'adanei Melech, page 81, column 4, writes that the Tur's version was copied incorrectly, and expresses surprise that the Rama and Rabbi Mordechai Yaffe trusted the printers without checking and reproduced the error as it appears in printed editions of the Tur. It is surprising that he did not raise the same difficulty regarding the Song at the Sea, where they write that the five lines beneath the song begin 'Vatikach, sus, vayetze'u, vayihyu.' The word 'vayihyu' does not occur there at all, and the verses cannot be divided into lines in that manner. The Rambam's text reads: 'Vatikach, achareihem, sus, vayetze'u, vayavo'u.' See there. See also the Rambam for numerous additional laws and customs concerning the writing of a Torah scroll.
