If he wrote plene what should be defective, or defective what should be plene; or wrote a word that is kri ukhetiv according to its kri, such as writing "yishkavenah" instead of "yishgalenah," or "uvetachorim" instead of "uvecholilim," and the like—it is invalid. {Rama: And there are other matters scribes are accustomed in, transmitted person to person, such as large and small letters, points, and crowns. And they are also accustomed regarding the count of lines to make them not fewer than forty-two, and some say forty-eight, and not more than sixty. And they are accustomed at the heads of lines above the Song at the Sea five lines, and these are their beginnings: haba'im, bayabashah, ham, met, bemitzrayim. And likewise below the song five lines, and their beginnings: vetikach, sus, vayetze'u, vayavo'u. And above the Song of Haazinu six lines, and their beginnings: ve'aidah, achari, haderech, be'acharit, lehach'iso, kehal. And below, five lines, and their beginnings: vayavo, vayedaber, asher, hazot, [asher]. And all these matters are for the mitzvah; and if one changed, he does not invalidate (all from the language of the Tur).}
