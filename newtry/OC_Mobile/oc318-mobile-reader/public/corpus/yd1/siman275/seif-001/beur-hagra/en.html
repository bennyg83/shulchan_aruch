A section, etc., must be stored away. This is the text in tractate Soferim, chapter 1, law 14.
Some say that it is permitted, etc. The statement that it must be stored away is because one may not initially reduce the amount of writing, as stated above in siman 273, se'if 2.
(Supplement) Some say, etc. Rambam disagrees, and Rivash likewise rules this way in siman 7. See there at length and examine Rashba's proofs there.
