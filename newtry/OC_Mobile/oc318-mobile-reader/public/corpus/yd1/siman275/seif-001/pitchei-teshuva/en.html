A closed section. Authorities discuss a Torah scroll containing an unintended blank space where no section break belongs. Bnei Yonah permits leniency where the blank exists because the parchment could not be written upon, such as at a seam or tear. Chatam Sofer distinguishes interruption within a verse, which may be repaired, from an erroneous section break between verses, which may require replacing the sheet. Numerous responsa analyze specific cases in Yitro and Pinchas.
