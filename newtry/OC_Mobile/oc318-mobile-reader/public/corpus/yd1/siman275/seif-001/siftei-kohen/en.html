It is forbidden to cut out the Name, etc. One may not cut a Divine Name out of parchment in a disgraceful manner, for even when the intent is to preserve the Name, the act of cutting around it can appear as damaging or treating it lightly. If there is a hole or tear near the Name, one must repair the parchment with honor, and not cut the Name away as though it were an ordinary word. A patch may be placed where needed if it is done respectfully and in accordance with the laws of writing and repair, but one must be careful that the Name itself not be erased, scraped, or diminished. The same applies to a matlit, a patch: it may be used to support the parchment when necessary, but not in a way that degrades the sanctity of the Name or makes it appear detached and discarded.
