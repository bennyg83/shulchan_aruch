For he has no [permission] to cut the Name and make holes in the sheet. At first glance it appears the reason it is forbidden to make holes in the sheet is forbidden; and so appears in Taz; and one cannot say so, for if so, why specifically when encountering the Name — even other words should be forbidden because of disgrace of making holes in the sheet. And further Rosh's language in responsum general 3 siman 7 who wrote "heaven forbid to perforate the mentions," etc. — implies because of honor of the Name it is forbidden; and likewise in responsum of Maharal ibn Chaviv siman 2. And certainly Rosh did not forbid because the Torah scroll remains invalid afterward, although the questioner was concerned from his language there; nevertheless Rosh certainly was not concerned as appears from his language, etc.; and in my opinion Rosh forbade because of honor of the holy Name — end quote. And it is strained to explain what the rabbi wrote "and to make holes in the sheet" — comes to exclude if one removes the thin upper hide — for since Rosh forbade because of honor of the Name, and the rabbi too — all the more forbidden to remove the thin hide from the parchment, for then it appears more disgraceful and close to erasure; and likewise Maharal ibn Chaviv there; therefore it appears the rabbi wrote "and to make holes in the sheet" to exclude if the Name was written initially on a cloth attached in a hole — then permitted to remove the cloth with the Name, for specifically when cutting the Name from the sheet in manner of making a hole it is forbidden as disgrace to perforate the Name; but when writing on the cloth — permitted to remove for repair need. And so must explain words of R.
