The forms of an open parashah and a closed parashah, and the form of song. In it are 6 seifim. A parashah that he made closed, or a closed one that he made open—he should put away (genizah). {Rama: And some say it is permitted to correct it like other errors (Rosh, Rashba, and Rivash); and so scribes are accustomed to correct it if he can erase without harming a Name, for he may not cut out the Name and make holes in the sheet (Rosh, general rule 3).}
