Closed. Rambam likewise invalidates a scroll where blank space was left where no section belongs, or where no space was left at a required section. Bach understands Tur as validating extra blank space after the fact, but Shach and Taz reject that reading.
To repair it. One may not initially compress the writing merely to create the proper space between sections. After the fact, however, one may erase before or after and reduce the lettering. Taz understands Rashba to permit such correction once the mistake already occurred.
Holes. Shach explains that the prohibition against cutting around a divine name is due to the name's honor, not merely because holes are made in the parchment. Other words may be treated differently. If the divine name was originally written on a patch over a hole, the patch may be removed for repair, because only cutting out the name itself is forbidden.
