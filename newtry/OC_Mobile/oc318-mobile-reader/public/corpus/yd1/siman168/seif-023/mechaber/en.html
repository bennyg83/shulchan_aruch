A Jew who said to a non-Jew, here is your wages (or: I will split profit with you) (as is implied in Tur) and lend my money with interest — it is forbidden, for they are on the Jew's responsibility. And a non-Jew who said to a Jew, here is your wages and lend my money with interest — it is permitted, but it is forbidden to do so because of marit ayin. (However if the matter is publicized among many it is permitted.)
