(Taz s.k. 37) Rather Rama decided, etc. This is not that Rama wrote thus in Darkei Moshe in the name of Beit Yosef, as Siftei Kohen s.k. 73 wrote — see there. Also what he wrote "and it appears thus is proven from what is stated in perek HaRibbit," etc. — he was not precise, for there it is a ribbit document; but here he claims "I took it permissibly," and likewise what he wrote that Rama learned from that CM matter — is not a valid point, for there he does not swear and take but pays and afterward swears an oath after payment; and further, specifically there where he claims he did not take at all, unlike here where he claims "I took it permissibly" — one can say he did not abandon a permitted path and eat forbidden matter, as Siftei Kohen wrote — see there.
