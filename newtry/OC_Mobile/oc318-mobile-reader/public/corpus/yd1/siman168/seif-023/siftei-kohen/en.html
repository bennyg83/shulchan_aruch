Forbidden due to appearance of impropriety. It appears that also with a deposit — forbidden even though not on his responsibility — due to appearance of impropriety. And some say it is only permitted when he fixed his wage as a known amount whether he profits or not; but if he told him “I will share profits with you” — forbidden even though he has no share in losses. And some permit since it does not come to him on account of his money; and so Rambam — end of Tur; and Beit Yosef; and Bach wrote “and what Tur wrote — it appears also with deposit, etc.” — this is not how it appears from other poskim, etc. — and I have not arrived at the bottom of his reasoning; and it is straightforward.
