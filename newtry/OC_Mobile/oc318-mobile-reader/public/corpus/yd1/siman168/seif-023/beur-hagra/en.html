Or [I] disagree, etc. In the latter clause: a non-Jew who said to an Israelite, etc. — the poskim dispute a case like this: some forbid, since one can say he has a share in the profit; and some permit, since the principal belongs to the non-Jew and responsibility for it does not fall upon [the Israelite] — and as written in Sifri: "your silver" — excluding the silver of others. Sefer HaTeruah and Tur. But here everyone agrees it is forbidden. And in the latter clause he omitted and did not write the view of those who forbid; but in Sefer HaTeruah he agreed with those who permit, and likewise the Tur.
