Or I will divide, etc. In the latter clause, where a non-Jew said to a Jew, etc., the poskim dispute a case of this kind. Some forbid it because the Jew has a share in the profit. Others permit it because the principal belongs to the non-Jew and the Jew bears no responsibility for it, as the Sifrei expounds from "your money," excluding another's money. See Sefer HaTerumah and the Tur. Here, however, all agree that it is forbidden. In the latter case the Shulchan Aruch omits the prohibiting opinion. Sefer HaTerumah accepts the lenient view, and this is also the Tur's view.
