And likewise, if they presented him before the non-Jew and he said, “Place them on the ground,” and he did so and withdrew, and the second took them from there, it is permitted. {Rama: And some say: if the non-Jew said to give them to the second Jew by way of a deposit, and afterward it will be a loan in his hand, it is permitted, since it did not leave the first [Jew] as a loan (Beit Yosef in the name of the students of Rashba and Nimukei Yosef). And if the second Jew did not make a loan with the first Jew, only with the non-Jew, and the non-Jew told him to receive them from the first Jew, and the first Jew is only like the non-Jew’s agent, it is permitted even if he gives the interest to the Jew (there, in the name of Hagahos Mordechai).}
