(Taz seif katan 14) Gratuitously. He expressed surprise at Beit Yosef and Bach, but his interpretation of Mordechai is strained, as is evident to every careful reader. There is no difficulty at all, because once he admitted it, his admission is equivalent to one hundred witnesses, and the non-Jew acquired it through that admission. This is comparable to the ruling below in seif 13, where even witnesses who testify that the item belongs to a Jew are ineffective once he had first admitted that it did not belong to a Jew. Examine this carefully.
