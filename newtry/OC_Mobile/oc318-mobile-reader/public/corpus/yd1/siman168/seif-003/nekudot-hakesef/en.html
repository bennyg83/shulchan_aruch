(Taz seif katan 14) For no reason did he wonder about the Beis Yosef and the Bach, and his words in explaining the Mordechai's words are forced, as is apparent to any reader. There is no difficulty at all, since once he admitted, his admission is equivalent to 100 witnesses, and the gentile acquired rights through his admission. It is comparable to below seif 13 — that even if witnesses testify that it belongs to the Jew, it does not help, since he initially admitted that it does not belong to the Jew. Examine this carefully.
