A non-Jew who pledged collateral to a Jew for his debt, and the non-Jew pledged that collateral to a Jew with interest — if the pledged Jew redeems it, it is forbidden to take interest from him. But if the liability for the debt and interest is not on the collateral alone but also on the non-Jew, it is permitted. And likewise if the pledged Jew did not know that the pledger non-Jew intended to pledge it with a Jew, it is permitted to receive principal and interest from the Jew's hand. {Rama: A Jew who owes a non-Jew a debt with interest, and the non-Jew transferred that debt to another Jew — if the non-Jew receives the interest and gave it to the second Jew, it is permitted; but it is forbidden for the second Jew to receive from the first Jew's hand because of the stringency of ribbis (responsum of Rashba siman 964).}
