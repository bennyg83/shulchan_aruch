(Taz seif katan 37) Rather, Rama decided, etc. This is incorrect, for Rama wrote this in Darkei Moshe in the name of Beit Yosef, as I wrote in Shach, seif katan 73. See there. He was likewise imprecise in writing that the ruling appears to be proven from the passage in the chapter HaRibbit. There, the document itself records interest, whereas here the claimant says, 'I took it permissibly.' His further statement that Rama derived the ruling from the case in Choshen Mishpat is also incorrect. There, the claimant does not swear and collect. Rather, he pays and afterward takes an oath administered after payment. Moreover, there he claims that he took nothing at all, unlike here, where he claims that he took it permissibly. It may therefore be said that a person would not abandon a permitted course and consume a forbidden item, as I wrote in Shach. See there.
