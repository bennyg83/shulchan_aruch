To take. According to the earlier lenient view, it is permitted. Shach.
Knew. There is no element of agency here.
To receive. If the Jew already owed the non-Jew principal and interest, another Jew may assume both. The case discussed is where only principal had accrued when the non-Jew transferred the debt. Taz explains that a complete sale to the second Jew would be forbidden and therefore understands the transfer as security. Nekudot HaKesef explains it as a complete acquisition that nevertheless creates no interest because the second Jew has no direct claim against the first under either legal system.
