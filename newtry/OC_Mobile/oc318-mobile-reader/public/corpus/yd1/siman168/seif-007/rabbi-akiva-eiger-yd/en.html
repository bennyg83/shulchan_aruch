(Seif 7) And the borrower does a prohibition. So too Rosh in a responsum (klal 108 siman 11). And in his rulings he quoted R. Yitzchak's language that there is no prohibition for the lender. However, in a responsum (there siman 18) he quoted R. Yitzchak's words that there is no prohibition neither for lender nor borrower. And likewise it was copied in Be'er HaGolah. Puzzling.
