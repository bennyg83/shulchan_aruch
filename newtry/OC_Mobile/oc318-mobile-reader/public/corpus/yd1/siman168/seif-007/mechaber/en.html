A Jew who said to a non-Jew, “Borrow for me money with interest from a Jew in your name,” and the lender did not know that it was for the Jew: it is permitted for the lender, but the borrower does a prohibition (Rosh and Tur according to Rashi). (And some are lenient because there is no agency for a non-Jew; and it was already explained that one should not be lenient except in a place where they are accustomed to be lenient) (there, according to Rabbeinu Tam).
