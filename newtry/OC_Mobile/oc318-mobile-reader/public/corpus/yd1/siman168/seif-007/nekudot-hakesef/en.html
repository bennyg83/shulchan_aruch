(Taz seif katan 24) I was greatly astonished by these words, etc. His own words are astonishing, while the words of Atzei Levonah are clear. Since the arrangement is regarded as though he sold him the collateral, when he later takes it back from him with interest, it is as though he repurchases it from him for that amount. The excess money that had remained with the first Jew had therefore been in his possession without charge. The proof he brought from the deferred sale of Rav Pappa is entirely irrelevant. There, he sells them the liquor for more than its present value, and that constitutes interest. Here, however, since he sold him the collateral, he is now receiving the value of his collateral, for it is as though he repurchases it from him. This is clear, and such is the established practice.
