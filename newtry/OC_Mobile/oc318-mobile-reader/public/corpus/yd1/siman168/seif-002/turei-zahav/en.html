The interest into the first Jew's hand. Not specifically interest but principal too ; meaning after the non-Jew received from the first Jew and gave to the second Jew, he told the first Jew: be in my place to receive my money from the second Jew, principal and interest; and the first Jew did so. This is explained in Hagahos Mordechai that Beit Yosef brings: the Baraisa "they presented before a non-Jew ; permitted" discusses where the non-Jew told the Jew receive my money from the first Jew and he is my agent ; but you cannot sue him except through me ; and it is permitted even if he gives interest to the Jew ; end quote; in the Hagahah there it does not say interest but simply "receive my money" ; meaning even principal; and it is a clear matter there is a scribal error ; how can the second Jew be agent when he returns money to the first Jew and gives him interest, for this is the sugya's topic "and I will pay you," etc., implying the first Jew receives even in the conclusion; and likewise proven in Hagahos Mordechai's conclusion that he is only a depositary, etc. ; see there; and this applies if he receives principal too; but if he receives only interest what novelty is there. Rather the main point as my teacher z"l wrote in the name of great ones: there is a scribal error and it should read: he told the first Jew receive my money from the second Jew, etc. ; meaning as I stated: after the non-Jew received and gave to the second Jew he says thus to the first; and he need not mention this since it refers to the beginning where the second Jew told the first "and I will pay you" ; and this applies also to the conclusion that in this formula it is permitted if the non-Jew gave and took by hand first. But in Tur it is written "even if he gives interest to the first Jew," etc. ; implying it does not discuss appointing the first Jew as agent to receive principal from the second; nevertheless for the law Tur too holds it permitted when the non-Jew gave and took. And what Hagahos Mordechai wrote "you cannot sue him except through me" ; means there shall be no responsibility on you; and even though below seif 23 it is explained forbidden because of marit ayin ; this case is different, for the loan did not go Jew to Jew but only after the first Jew completely departed; and see what is written in the Hagahah shortly on this.
