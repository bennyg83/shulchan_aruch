(There) And not as it appears at first glance, etc. In my humble opinion it appears as it does at first glance, and the Responsa Rashba is also speaking that way. Nevertheless, there is no prohibition of ribit here, because it speaks of a case where the second Jew cannot claim from the first, such as where the gentile transferred the debt to the second Jew under their laws and not under our law, yet the gentile still cannot retract from the first Jew. Examine carefully.
