(Ibid.) And not as it would appear at first glance, etc. In my humble opinion, the matter is indeed as it appears at first glance, and the responsum of Rashba also addresses such a case. Nevertheless, there is no prohibition of interest here, because the case is one in which the second Jew cannot bring a claim against the first, such as where the non-Jew transferred the debt to the second Jew according to their law but not according to our law. Even so, the non-Jew cannot retract from the first Jew. Examine this carefully.
