It is the trickery of the wicked. One may not create a false heter merely because the collateral belongs to a non-Jew when the Jew himself fixed the interest. If he says, 'Lend upon this non-Jewish collateral and collect such-and-such interest when the non-Jew redeems it,' ordinary intent is permissive. If he says, 'Lend me at interest upon these non-Jewish collaterals,' it is fixed interest. Shach. Derishah is stringent where the first Jew borrows less than the collateral's original loan; Taz disagrees, while Nekudot HaKesef supports Derishah.
