A Jew who comes to his fellow and says, lend me money on these collaterals that belong to a non-Jew, and he lent him and the money is for the Jew's need and he pays him interest from his own, or says to his fellow, lend me money with interest and I will send you collateral through a non-Jew, and the non-Jew brought the collateral to the lender and conveyed the money to the borrower — this is the trickery of the wicked, and he should not take any interest at all.
