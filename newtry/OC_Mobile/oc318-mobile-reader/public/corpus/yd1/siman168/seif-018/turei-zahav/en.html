I sell you all power,. etc. Levush wrote: for this reason it is permitted for a Jew to pledge a non-Jew's collateral with another Jew ; he explains more than what is pledged with him and interest shall be only what the non-Jew gives him; for we say he sold all collateral to the second and what he lent this one more he lent free without interest ; end quote. I greatly wondered at these words ; how can you say the surplus is interest-free loan; immediately when the second Jew fixed with him at less than all the gold the non-Jew promised, the first profited that surplus; and if he did not take principal from the second only as given to the non-Jew he would not give such interest ; why give the second afterward; necessarily for what he lent more than he lent the non-Jew; and there is complete ribbis; and even if no loss, there is ribbis from preventing the agent's profit; and though this is obvious I bring clear proof from Talmud perek HaRibbis (65a): Rava Pappa said my document is permitted ; Rashi: he was a date-broker and sold cheap in Tishrei at Nisan rate and waited until Nisan ; why? I do not lose, I could store until Nisan, I do not need money, I do a favor for the buyer. Rava Sheshet told Rava Pappa: why do you follow yourself ; follow them, the buyers; if they had money they would take now as now; now they have no money they take at future rate ; and halakhah follows Rava Sheshet as stated beginning siman 173. Behold the buyer has no loss ; if he wished he could hold until payment time worth that amount; even if buyer holds until that time there is prohibition though no loss, since one side could profit and he did not ; we consider him a paid watchman; likewise here though borrower has no real loss since non-Jew gives that interest from smaller sum as from larger ; nevertheless if he took from Jew smaller sum he would profit and he forfeits that profit for extra loan ; a fortiori in Rava Pappa's document which is sale where ribbis prohibition is rabbinic they were stringent; all the more here loan where prohibition is Torah ; therefore it appears fixed ribbis extractable by judges; do not honor rabbi over colleague; let this permit be suppressed and not done among Jews.
And some say that even if he does not sell it, etc. This is the opinion of the Rosh cited by the Tur, who permitted it even if he did not explicitly sell it. His intent is that even though he did not state the sale expressly, we assume that he intended it. See what I wrote in seif 19.
And if they stipulated from the outset in it. It is Torah and I need to learn ; for this ruling seems puzzling, since main permission in this law is we grasp it as sale; and any sale with condition he is obligated to return when he returns the money as stated perek HaRibbis brought at beginning siman 174 ; sold field and said when you return to me, etc. ; did not acquire; likewise here if they stipulated there is no sale; and so is Tur and Shulchan Aruch's language in this seif ; wrote the second wished to return and take from the first, etc. ; implies not that he wished not; he should have written stipulation helps ; great novelty. Further proof from Maimoniyos Mishpatim siman 30: it happened once Rabbenu Tam had collateral from a non-Jew and needed money and to give collateral to lend on it; he asked how to be permitted ; the non-Jew borrows from you and owes you interest, etc.; he answered I sell you all collateral and do not trouble me further if you wish and I exempt you and you are not obligated to return collateral like this Rabbenu did ; end quote. Behold on the contrary he had to say he is not obligated to return collateral; and if there is permission when they stipulated he could also stipulate ; why diminish his right for free; who asked this of him; rather certainly main permission is from this; and so Rosh's words responsum klal 108 siman 5: we can say if the broker told the Jew this collateral of the non-Jew ; acquire the collateral for you; and if the non-Jew comes give it to redeem; I have no dealings with you nor you with me ; possibly permitted, etc. ; end quote; behold he insisted specifically no dealings regarding returning collateral to redeem; all this refutes Rama's ruling here whose source is Terumas HaDeshen siman 303; and I abbreviated its language: asked Reuven who fixed with non-Jew borrower on Nisan 100 dinar for 112 by year ; 12 dinar interest per hundred by Sivan ; came to Shimon to pay half the debt ; 50 half principal ; and pay interest on all as from non-Jew as long as he does not return principal and interest and authority in Reuven's hand to return after month or two months ; how act permissibly; answered all interest fixed with non-Jew is considered Reuven's principal lent to non-Jew ; all 112 dinar are Jew's principal; Mordechai further wrote if Jew has collateral from non-Jew with interest permitted for another Jew to redeem thus ; first sells to second so interest from here on is on his collateral; if after time first wishes to return and redeem ; permitted to give second principal and interest accruing on collateral while in his hand; second returns and sells to first to be his as initially; brought the case siman 174 gift that returns ; not sale; per this wrote no remedy to transfer fixed document with non-Jew to fellow for weekly interest ; since debt fixed with non-Jew all debt principal and interest is Jew's money; turns out weekly ribbis he takes not from non-Jew but from Jew's money as wages for advancing money before due date ; certainly if he wished to buy debt completely now for less than written amount calculating interest until fixed time ; such case completely permitted, etc. But if seller does not intend complete sale but to return and buy when he has money ; must act so he is not completely secure in complete condition to return but secure he will not change his promise. Now explain the question: if he wishes buy entire debt in Sivan for 102 dinar ; all his from now; if they wish the manner in the question ; redeem only first principal on condition second agrees to return and sell if bought in Sivan for 102 and returns in Av for 104 for what accrued while debt owned ; end quote. From this Rama learned only where he first fixed interest it is forbidden afterward to make complete condition; but without fixing permitted with complete condition. And fixed-interest law Rama wrote at end of Hagahah; and must understand Terumas HaDeshen's view ; since truly even without prior fixing permission is only through sale first and end; and since obligated to return it was not sale from outset; if Terumas HaDeshen had some distinction he would have written it; and it appears his view: without fixing sale comes only to remove first lender's right to what accrues weekly from non-Jew; but need not transfer anything ; what does he transfer, it is something not yet in existence, interest born each week; therefore only self-removal applies even if he removes for a time and stipulates if he has money he returns and sells; but with fixing then sale comes to transfer to second what is already the first Jew's complete right; therefore sale must be such second is not obligated to return to first if he has money ; for this nullifies sale meant to give second complete acquisition. So I explain; but we already proved from other poskim that even on plain non-Jewish collateral sale is required such that he not be obligated to return when he has money; and I wonder Terumas HaDeshen did not bring them. In any case it appears to be stringent as poskim we cited stated; and every conscientious person should act with second lender's willingness to return and redeem if redeemed without non-Jew's knowledge ; so it seems.
