Under the responsibility of the non-Jew. This means that if the debts are lost, the non-Jew must repay them to the Jew like any borrower. It is not sufficient that he merely accept the responsibility of a guardian, even that of a borrower-guardian, since he did not thereby become a borrower of the debts themselves. This is from the Beit Yosef in the name of Sefer HaTerumos.
Under the responsibility of the Jew, it is forbidden. The Rivash writes in siman 305 that this constitutes fixed ribbis. So wrote the Darkei Moshe.
