A Jew's money is deposited with a non-Jew and he lent it to a Jew with interest — if they were on the non-Jew's responsibility it is permitted to take interest; and if they were on the Jew's responsibility it is forbidden. (And if he lent them without the Jew's knowledge, they are presumably on the non-Jew's responsibility and it is permitted) (Mordechai and Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba and Beit Yosef in the name of students of Rashba).
