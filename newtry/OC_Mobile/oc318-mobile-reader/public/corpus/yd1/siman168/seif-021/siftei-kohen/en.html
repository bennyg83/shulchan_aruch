On the non-Jew's responsibility, etc. Specifically we require the non-Jew lend them from the Jew's hand and the non-Jew accepts that if the debts are lost he bends to the Jew as any borrower's law; but if he accepted responsibility like other guardians, and even obligated himself for accidents when they are lost as long as they stand intact — forbidden and he does not become borrower from now.
