If they are on the Jew’s responsibility etc. This is specifically when he says to the non-Jew "I will lend them to a Jew with interest at my responsibility — if the debts are lost I will repay you." But if he tells a particular Jew "I will lend them to him with interest and if you do not find him with assets at repayment time I will pay you principal and interest" — permitted as the law of a guarantor. And if he said "give me your money and I will lend them to a Jew with interest, and if the debts are lost they are your own; but if a mishap occurs with them while still in my possession before I lend them to the Jew — behold they are mine" — permitted, for this is not called being a borrower on them from now but is called a guardian — end quote Beit Yosef in the name of Beit HaTalmud; and Darkei Moshe brought it. However all of this is forbidden due to appearance of impropriety.
