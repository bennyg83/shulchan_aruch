(Siftei Kohen s.k. 40) And likewise it appears from Rashba's responsum. In my humble opinion there is no proof from there, for there it discusses a broker who cannot obligate others through his admission. And likewise Maharshal wrote plainly in a responsum there. And even if only one witness says it belongs to the Jew, it is forbidden for him to take interest, for one witness is believed in prohibitions; but the broker himself — either from one witness or from the law of one witness, since his words contradict each other. Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba's responsum.
