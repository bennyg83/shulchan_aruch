(Siftei Kohen s.k. 52) And according to Ran. This is puzzling — for also according to Ran it is permitted for a Jew to be a contractor on behalf of a Jew who borrowed from a non-Jew, as is clear below (siman 170) in Beit Yosef. And Bach already wondered thus on Beit Yosef in responsum Lechem Rav and in responsum Mahari Halevi, brother of Taz (siman 5).
