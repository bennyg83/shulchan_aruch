And I will pay him, etc. For he gives it to the non-Jew as his agent. This is comparable to one who says to another, “I will lend you on condition that you give the interest to the non-Jew,” which is obviously forbidden. Even if the lender owes the non-Jew nothing, since the borrower gives the interest to the non-Jew as compensation for the loan and at the lender’s direction, it constitutes fixed ribbis. So wrote the Tur.
That he should give me the principal and interest, it is forbidden. Since he received it from the hand of the Jew, it appears that he gives the interest to the non-Jew as the Jew’s agent. So wrote the Tur. The essential principle here is that there is no agency for a non-Jew, whether a non-Jew acts for a Jew or a Jew acts for a non-Jew.
