A Jew who said to his fellow, take this money and lend it to a non-Jew with interest — if the responsibility for the money is on the agent when conveying or when bringing it, it is forbidden for the lender to take interest from the agent's hand or from the agent of the agent's hand, even up to a hundred [links in the chain]. And if the responsibility is not on him but like other agents — if he guards unpaid he guards unpaid; if he guards for wages he guards for wages — it is permitted.
