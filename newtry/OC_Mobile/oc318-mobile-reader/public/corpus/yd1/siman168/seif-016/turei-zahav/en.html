If responsibility for the money rests upon the agent. The Beit Yosef explains in the name of the Baal HaTerumos that the agent may not accept responsibility even merely for the interest, such as by saying, “As long as the non-Jew does not repay the principal, I will give you the interest.” Since whenever the non-Jew fails to pay the principal this Jew must pay the interest, responsibility for the principal also effectively rests upon him. See what I wrote in siman 170 concerning the difficulty this presents for the Rama there; the Levush cites that ruling there in the name of “some authorities.”
