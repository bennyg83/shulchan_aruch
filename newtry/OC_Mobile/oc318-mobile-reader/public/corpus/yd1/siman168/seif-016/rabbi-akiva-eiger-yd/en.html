(Seif 16) If responsibility for the money rests upon him. This is not comparable to the ruling of Mahari ben Lev in a responsum, that one may receive money from another, assume all responsibility, and give him all the profit earned from the funds, because it is not deemed a loan. The distinction there is that he seeks no benefit for himself, whereas here, had an opportunity for profit arisen, the gain would have belonged to him. Thus writes the Derishah. See Beit Yosef, who permits spending the money in consideration of the benefit that he assumes greater responsibility than the law requires; see there. The essential ruling of Mahari ben Lev appears to me to be explicit in Responsa Ran, siman 72, cited by Beit Yosef below at the beginning of siman 177. See also Shulchan Aruch there, seif 19.
