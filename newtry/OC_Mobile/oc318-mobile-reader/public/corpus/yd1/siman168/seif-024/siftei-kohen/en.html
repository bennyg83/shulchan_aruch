"Guardian over his property." Bach wrote: specifically over all his property; unlike when he did not become overseer or guardian except to lend his money with interest, as he forbids "take your wage and lend my money with interest," etc.; and from the students of Rashba and Beit Yosef who wrote regarding "take your wage" that in his craft thus it is permitted — it appears explicitly not over all property; and possibly even Bach means only when the matter is not well known; and so it appears from the end of his words concluding because not everyone knows he became guardian or overseer of the non-Jew since he does not conduct other business of the non-Jew's property.
