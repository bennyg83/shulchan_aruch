The lender is not, etc. Bach and Taz wrote: this is specifically by default; but if the lender said you shall be my agent, etc. — obviously the agent can tell the lender do not sell it because the non-Jew is silent, as above and similar. Pri Megadim wrote: it seems specifically in such a case where the agent brought collateral with him at loan time as we said; but if he did not bring collateral with him but the non-Jew sent him to bring money and afterward would give his collateral — then responsibility for collateral was never on the agent, only the agent was the Jew's agent as above siman 13; the agent can say do not sell it lest it harm me because the non-Jew is silent, etc.; and if he sold it and the agent came to harm thereby the lender must compensate — it seems to me — end quote.
