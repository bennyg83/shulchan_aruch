(Shach, seif katan 64) It is permitted in every manner. I do not understand this. The basis of the permission is that reliance is placed solely upon the collateral, and the increase derives from the non-Jew's property. It may therefore apply only when he says generally, 'Lend such-and-such upon it, and when the non-Jew comes to redeem it, take such-and-such interest.' In that case we say that he transferred the object to the second Jew as collateral, as the Shach writes in seif katan 58. But when he says, 'Lend me,' he personally undertakes to pay the interest from his own pocket, whether or not the non-Jew pays. It may therefore be that he is the actual borrower and the transaction is forbidden. This requires study.
