A Jew who borrowed from a Jew with interest for the non-Jew's need on the non-Jew's collateral, and after a time the lender wishes to sell it and the agent says, do not sell it for the non-Jew is silent — the lender need not heed his words, for the lender has nothing to do with the non-Jew. And if the agent fears [the lender], let him save himself and redeem the collateral or add collateral for the lender so he will not lose through the delay.
