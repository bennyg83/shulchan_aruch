A Jew who said to his fellow, "Borrow money for me from the non-Jew with interest" — if he gave him collateral to borrow upon it: if the non-Jew's responsibility is on the collateral alone and not on the agent at all, it is permitted for the agent to take the interest and give it to the non-Jew. And if he did not give him collateral, it is forbidden unless he said to him, "Borrow money with interest from the non-Jew on my account," and the non-Jew trusted him and he relies on the borrower and not on the agent. {Rama: And see below siman 170 whether an agent may be a guarantor. Some say that communal leaders are permitted to borrow from a non-Jew with interest for the needs of the community even though they are agents of the community and even though the responsibility is upon them and they return and take from the community — it is permitted, for it is like a guardian of orphans that is permitted in such a case; and thus they practiced to be lenient even though there is no clear permission here (Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Rashba). Nevertheless it is forbidden afterward to take partial payment from part of the community who borrowed for the needs of the community and deduct from them afterward more than they borrowed, for the interest falls upon the others and it is as though they borrowed from one another with interest (also there).}
