If the responsibility etc. Tur did not mention that the non-Jew's responsibility should be on the collateral alone; rather he wrote simply that the non-Jew relies on his collateral. And it seems to me this discusses where the collateral is good — for then by default he relies on the collateral alone and not on the agent; and the Ran and Mechaber discuss where the collateral is not so good; and therefore Beit Yosef wrote he must say to him "I am not obligated to you at all, and your responsibility shall be only on this collateral" — it seems to me.
On the collateral. And not on the agent — meaning he shall not sue him as a borrower; and per the Ran, even as a kablan-guarantor he must not sue him — Beit Yosef; and see above siman 170.
The agent is permitted to take the interest. And it is not comparable to above seif 1, for there the Jew initially borrowed for his own need and then lent to a second Jew; and further here the non-Jew relies on the collateral alone; unlike Hagahot Derishah seif 36 — see there; and see in responsum Maharam Galanti siman 10 and in responsum Mabit vol. 2 siman 72.
That the community leaders etc. See in the responsum of R. Eliyahu Ibn Hayyim siman 57.
