To the agent. This differs from seif 1, where the Jew originally borrowed for himself and then lent to another Jew. Here the non-Jew relies only upon the collateral. Shach.
Permission. Taz explains that the non-Jewish lender knows the loan is for the community and relies upon the community if the named borrowers cannot pay.
The interest. Meaning the interest earned by those who pay is imposed upon the others to complete. Taz.
