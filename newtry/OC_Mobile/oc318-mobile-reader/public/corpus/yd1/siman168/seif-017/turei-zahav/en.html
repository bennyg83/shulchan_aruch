They are permitted to borrow from the non-Jew. Although the trustees are like guardians over the community, it should nevertheless be forbidden, for it is forbidden even for a guardian of orphans. So wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba at the end of this siman, concluding: “This is like one who says to another, ‘The maneh that you owe to so-and-so, lend it to me, and I will give one maneh per year to your creditor.’ I see fixed ribbis here.” End quote. The Rama’s reason for permitting it here is that he does not regard it as fixed ribbis, and since it is for a mitzvah purpose, the trustee is treated like a guardian of an orphan. It also appears that the non-Jewish lender knows that the loan is for the community’s needs and relies upon the entire community if these trustees are unable to pay.
