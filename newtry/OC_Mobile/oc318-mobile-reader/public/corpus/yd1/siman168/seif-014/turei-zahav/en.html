And some say the borrower must pay from his pocket. This is Avi HaEzri's view. It appears his explanation: if he wants the lender to give him collateral and he does not wish to pay all interest he fixed ; he must pay from his pocket or leave the collateral; but if he mentioned the non-Jew's name he must give collateral without interest. Thereby resolved what Beit Yosef wrote that Rosh disagrees with this law ; even when collateral responsibility is on the agent forbidden, all the more to take interest from borrower if non-Jew silent ; end quote. And it seems to me there is no dispute; Avi HaEzri too holds responsibility is on the agent, only the agent wants to extract collateral without interest ; on this he said pay from pocket or leave collateral with lender. And I wonder at Beit Yosef ; behold Rosh wrote shortly that if lender does not wish to wait in selling collateral lest he lose in waiting, the agent must add collateral from himself; if so a fortiori the agent has no power to compel lender to give collateral without interest ; simply this is not in responsibility category; responsibility is if lost or stolen from agent the loss is lender's; but here lender does not wish to give collateral from his hand until he received principal and interest ; in this all agree. My teacher z"l challenged ; behold the first view Shulchan Aruch wrote borrower does not pay from pocket is written in Mordechai in the name of Aviasaf; and Aviasaf is Avi HaEzri ; Rabeinu Yehudah; and from this he entered explanations and distinctions; examining Mordechai shows no difficulty ; he wrote laws in the name of Rivba and among them this law borrower does not pay from pocket; and concluded on some of Rivba's laws there is response; afterward wrote Aviasaf meant Aviasaf wrote one should challenge some of Rivba's laws ; clearly he said this on this law and disagrees as we saw in his commentary. He further challenged: since main permission is the Jew sends his fellow to lend his money to the non-Jew ; why must he pay from pocket if non-Jew silent; and he extended to answer. And in my humble opinion it is no difficulty ; even though he is his agent, since he entered the matter he accepted not to harm him in relying on collateral; therefore must ensure no loss ; laws of causative damage; when non-Jew silent certainly no interest and he should have informed this non-Jew is silent; therefore it seems if agent did not know this non-Jew is coerced and learned afterward ; no obligation on him and sender must give collateral without interest ; why lose since he did not sin ; so it seems. Levush wrote: it seems specifically when agent brought collateral at loan time; but if he did not bring collateral at loan time but non-Jew sent to bring money and afterward would give collateral ; then responsibility was never on agent, only agent was Jew's agent as I wrote above; agent can say do not sell lest it harm me because non-Jew silent; if sold and agent harmed, lender must compensate ; so it seems ; end quote; he was not precise ; Rosh explicitly discusses sending to bring collateral from non-Jew on lender's responsibility as Tur cited explicitly in his name; and nevertheless concluded if agent fears non-Jew he adds collateral to Jew as Shulchan Aruch cited seif 15 ; end quote; his words have no place in my view; and whenever agent knew non-Jew is coerced and did not inform lender he cannot afterward extract collateral from lender without interest for he caused his own loss ; so it seems.
