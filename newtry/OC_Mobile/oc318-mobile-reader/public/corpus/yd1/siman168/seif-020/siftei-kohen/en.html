Permitted to lend etc. For what difference does it make that the collateral does not belong to the non-Jew — is it forbidden to lend to a non-Jew on a Jew's collateral? And even if the non-Jew lent to the Jew with interest — it is permitted for a Jew to lend to the non-Jew on that collateral with interest and to receive from the Jew the interest the Jew owes the non-Jew; for the second Jew did not lend to the first anything but to the non-Jew, and it is in his place that he receives the interest from the Jew's hand — Tur and the Rosh.
