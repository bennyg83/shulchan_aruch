A Jew's collateral in a non-Jew's hand — it is permitted for a Jew to lend against it with interest. And some forbid unless the Jew, owner of the collateral, says: acquire from now the collateral if I do not give you until such-and-such day. {Rama: And according to the first view, even from the first Jew it is permitted to receive interest (Tur in the name of Rosh). And if the non-Jew has a debt against a Jew and made with him liens to give him all year thus and thus interest, or that he eat the produce of his field if the borrower lacks [means] to discharge the non-Jew and return his money — it is permitted for another Jew to buy it from him. And if he can discharge him, it is forbidden to buy it from the non-Jew (so is implied from responsum of Rivash that Beit Yosef brought).}
