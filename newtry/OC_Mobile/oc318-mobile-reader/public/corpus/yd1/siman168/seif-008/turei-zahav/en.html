And the non-Jew went and borrowed, etc. Since he deviated from his instructions, there is no agency here, and the Jew is considered as though he borrowed from the non-Jew himself. The Beit Yosef wrote that there is a basis for permitting this for the borrower but not for the lender. There is no reason for such a distinction, and the Darkei Moshe already disagreed with him. So too, the unqualified ruling here permitting it implies that it is permitted even for the lender.
