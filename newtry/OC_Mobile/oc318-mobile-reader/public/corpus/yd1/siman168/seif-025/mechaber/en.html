Reuven who said to Shimon, you took interest from me hand to hand, and Shimon replies, no — I took only through a non-Jew — he is believed without an oath. And if he replies, I do not know if you ever gave me interest — he swears that he does not know. {Rama: And likewise with other claims between borrower and lender where this one claims he lent to him in prohibition and this one claims he lent to him permissibly — the lender is exempt even without an oath (as is implied in poskim, and see Beit Yosef). And this is specifically when the borrower comes to extract from the lender, such as he had collateral or already paid him (Beit Yosef). But if the lender comes to extract from the borrower, the borrower swears and is exempt. And if there is a promissory note in the lender's hand, he swears and takes (reasoning of Beit Yosef from implication of Mordechai). And see above siman 105 and below siman 177.}
