Believed without oath. For a person does not abandon a permitted path and do forbidden matter as we say regarding prozbul Choshen Mishpat siman 67 seif 33 ; a person is believed to say I had a prozbul and it was lost.
And if he responds I do not know,. etc. Beit Yosef wrote the same in claims "there never was such matter"; and per this resolved what Choshen Mishpat siman 82 seif 12 wrote regarding claiming this debt is ribbis ; must swear heses ; meaning claims I do not know; and nevertheless must understand why truly obligated to swear in these claims more than claim I took permissibly ; difference is only acquisition alone without obligation from that; since if he admitted he took he still has claim to exempt that he took permissibly; Choshen Mishpat siman 93 seif 9 heses only on claim where admission obligates money, etc.; a fortiori here even if admits can exempt saying I took permissibly; appears insufficient to say I took permissibly without specifying permission how ; lest he not know how to make permit; example Choshen Mishpat siman 67 Rosh never saw anyone succeed with claim prozbul I had and lost because they did not know prozbul at all; further proof Rosh klal 108 siman 7 Reuven borrowed from Shimon 100 gakshish, etc. ; lender claimed borrower had gakshish; ruled we do not say he abandoned permitted path because it did not enter mind gakshish would become expensive ; unlike prozbul, etc. ; end quote. Likewise in all permits possibly he thought matter would not need permit and thereby done forbidden; therefore if he explicitly says done permissibly as we can investigate if matter where no concern it did not occur to him he would need permit ; therefore if says I do not know or silent there is this concern and he swears he does not know or is silent. Thereby resolved Beit Yosef's challenge why believe claim I do not know with migo I took through non-Jew ; answered we do not say migo to exempt from oath; not so ; Choshen Mishpat siman 93 explicitly Rosh says we say migo here; and per my words it fits ; possibly he is not clear on that permit; and per this path appears what Rama wrote afterward "and likewise in other claims," etc. ; meaning he specifies permission how, not simply; and even regarding prozbul they said specifically must say so, not suffice "I sue permissibly"; and even for one not wanting to explain thus saying simply I took permissibly suffices ; nevertheless practically one should act thus now, for we see many breach ribbis prohibition and it is not considered anything until almost inapplicable to say he did not abandon permitted path ; he does not care to act permissibly at all; and this we learn from Rosh changing law for shemitah and not giving credibility to his generation saying prozbul I had though explicit in Talmudic sages' days ; likewise ribbis in our case must at least investigate how done permissibly; and if judge sees unclear he administers oath ; so it seems.
And if there is a document in the lender's hand ; swears and takes. In Mordechai's words Beit Yosef brings it says he takes without oath; but Rama decided in any case he is not presumed from the document like one presumed in collateral taking without oath; and it appears thus proven from what is stated perek HaRibbis (62a) all agree a document standing to collect is not as collected; nevertheless the document helps that he swears and takes; and Rama learned from Choshen Mishpat siman 82 seif 10 we mentioned.
