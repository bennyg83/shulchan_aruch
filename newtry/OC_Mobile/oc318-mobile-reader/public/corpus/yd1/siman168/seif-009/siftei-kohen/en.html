And deposits them with the borrower etc. The borrower uses them or sells them, but the borrower commits to return to the lender them or their value — there.
However, if the lender, etc., he is entitled to do so. This applies specifically in such a case, where the Jew originally gave the item to the non-Jew in order that the non-Jew pledge it. But if the Jew merely lent his vessels to the non-Jew, or even gave them to him to pledge temporarily on condition that he return them, then when the non-Jew pledges them to another Jew for a fixed period he is a robber, and the second Jew must return them to the first Jew. The Mordechai writes that this is like a robber who takes an item from one person and gives it to another, as explained in Choshen Mishpat, siman 369. The Bach likewise writes this. The Beit Yosef and the Rama below in seif 11 distinguish the cases differently.
