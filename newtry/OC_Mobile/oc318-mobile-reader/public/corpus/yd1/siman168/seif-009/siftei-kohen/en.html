"However if the lender," etc., "the authority is in his hand." Specifically in such a case where the Jew initially gave it to pledge; but if the Jew lent his vessels to the non-Jew, or even to pledge them temporarily and return them to him — then the non-Jew who pledged them to a Jew for a term is a robber, and the second Jew must return them to the first, as Mordechai wrote — he is like a robber who took from this one and gave to that one (CM siman 369); and likewise Bach.
