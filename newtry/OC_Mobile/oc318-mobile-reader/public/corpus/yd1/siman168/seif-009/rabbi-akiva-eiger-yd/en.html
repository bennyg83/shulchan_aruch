(Taz, seif katan 14) I later saw Responsa Maimoniyot, Mishpatim, siman 31. No proof may be brought from there, because the rationale of the Baal HaItur is that a debt, since it is not presently extant, cannot be transferred even through an admission, and even to remove a monetary claim, as he proves there from the case of a dying person who said, 'I lent to so-and-so'; see there. But regarding an extant object, where transfer through admission is effective, it may likewise be effective to remove a prohibition.
