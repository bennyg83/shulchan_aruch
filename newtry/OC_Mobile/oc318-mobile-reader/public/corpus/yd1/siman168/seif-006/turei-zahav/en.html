Even though the non-Jew gave the money, etc. The reason: even though we hold there is no agency for a non-Jew above seif 1 — that is for leniency; but here for stringency we say there is agency for a non-Jew. So Rashi's view; and "some are lenient" is Rabbeinu Tam's view that even for stringency we do not say agency for a non-Jew, even in unsecured debt without collateral — so Beit Yosef wrote for Rosh's view; and Tur brought Hagahos Maimoniyos that they disagree and hold even for Rabbeinu Tam it is not permitted except with collateral and not unsecured; and see what I wrote in seif 12 for the law.
