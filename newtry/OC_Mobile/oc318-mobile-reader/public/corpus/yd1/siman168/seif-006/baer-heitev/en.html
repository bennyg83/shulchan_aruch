With consent. Behold, the non-Jew is the agent of the Jew for stringency and it is forbidden rabbinically. And those who are lenient hold that even for stringency we do not say there is agency for a non-Jew, even in an unsecured debt without collateral. But the Tur brought Hagahos Maimoniyos that they disagree and hold that even according to those who are lenient it is not permitted in this case except with collateral — end quote Taz. And the Tur wrote that even according to this view, if the second Jew says he receives it as the agent of the first, it is forbidden, for he is the agent of the Jew. And Bach wrote that this is specifically when the first Jew made him an agent; otherwise one cannot say he is his agent without his consent. And Siftei Kohen agreed to this, unlike Beit Yosef — see there.
