(Taz seif katan 20) He objected. His objection to Bach and Atzei Levonah was improper. What the Tur wrote in the name of Rosh, that the borrower should add collateral, is an independent point, as Beit Yosef wrote. It does not refer back to the opening case in which responsibility for the collateral rests upon the lender. The proof is that this ruling appears in the responsa of Rosh, klal 108, siman 7, and there it is not stated that responsibility rests upon the lender. See there.
