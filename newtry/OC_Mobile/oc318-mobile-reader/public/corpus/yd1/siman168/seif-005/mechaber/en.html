If a Jew lent to a non-Jew with interest, and the non-Jew lends that money to a Jew with interest, and the first Jew then goes back and takes interest from the non-Jew, it is forbidden. {Rama: And this is specifically when the non-Jew lent to the second Jew as the agent of the first Jew, and according to the one who says there is agency for a non-Jew, for stringency (and so is implied in the Tur, and Rashi and his companions). And even if there is no agency for a non-Jew, for stringency, it is discussing where the Jew coerces the non-Jew to give him interest, and he knows that the non-Jew will coerce the second Jew to extract the interest from him, because it appears like interest. But if the non-Jew has collateral from the second Jew such that he does not need to coerce him, and it was not done as the agency of the first Jew, according to all it is permitted (Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai and Hagahos Maimoniyos, perek 5 of Hilchos Malveh), as will be explained shortly.}
