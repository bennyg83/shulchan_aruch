It discusses where the Jew coerces, etc. This is Hagahos Maimoniyos' view that Beit Yosef wrote distinguishes unsecured debt from collateral as Beit Yosef wrote; and it is in Maimoniyos' responsum to judges siman 33 in the name of Maharam; and proof from Terumas HaDeshen who forbid borrowing through a non-Jew ; therefore they distinguish unsecured from collateral; but other poskim such as Semag and Semak explain Terumas HaDeshen discusses where responsibility is on the Jew; if so no distinction between unsecured and collateral; and likewise Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh and Tur that they do not distinguish thus; and see what I wrote in seif 12 on this law.
