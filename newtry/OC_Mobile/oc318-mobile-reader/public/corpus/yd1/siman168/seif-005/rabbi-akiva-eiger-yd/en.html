Since both know of the matter. Rabbi Akiva Eiger questions why the additional rationales of acquisition and lack of credibility are needed. According to Rabbeinu Tam, absence of agency should itself permit the transaction; according to Rashi, lack of the lender's knowledge may likewise suffice. Ramban, however, appears to prohibit not because of agency but because the collateral remains the Jew's property and the interest accrues upon it.
