There is agency for a non-Jew etc. That is to say, a person’s agent is like himself; and although we hold that this is specifically for Jew to Jew, but not for non-Jews — neither their agency to us nor ours to them — that is by Torah law; but the Sages stringently ruled that the non-Jewish agent is as the Jew himself who sent him; and for the one who says there is no agency for a non-Jew even rabbinically — the non-Jew himself is not like the Jew himself.
That a Jew coerces etc. And nevertheless it is forbidden only when he lent in the first Jew’s agency; for otherwise it does not appear as ribbit, since the non-Jew borrowed for his own need from the outset — and see below seif 21.
And he did not act in the Jew's agency etc. That is to say, per Rashi; but per Rabbenu Tam — even if he acted in the Jew's agency it is permitted, as below seif 9 in the Hagahah, since there is no agency for a non-Jew.
