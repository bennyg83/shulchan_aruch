On the first day. See Be'er Heitev. Eliyah Rabbah in Orach Chayim siman 38 s.k. 4 wrote that there is room to doubt a case where he heard after praying Ma'ariv, for we rule above at the end of siman 375 that that day does not count and he counts from the next day: does he put on tefillin on the second day, which is called the first for him? From the words of the Taz below in siman 402 seif 11 it implies that he does put on tefillin. See Chomot Yerushalayim siman 264, who wrote that it does not imply so there, and his view is that he should put them on without a blessing and cover them; see there.
