After sunrise, etc. Some authorities hold that the prohibition applies only on the first day, and on the second day tefillin may be put on immediately, even before sunrise. The Bach rules accordingly. One who hears recent tidings has the same law as on the day of burial and may not put on tefillin, as implied by the Ramban and the Mechaber below in siman 402:2, and as ruled in Masat Binyamin 82.
