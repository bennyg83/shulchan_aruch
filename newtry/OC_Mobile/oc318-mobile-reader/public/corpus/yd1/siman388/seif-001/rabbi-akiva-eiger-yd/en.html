(Siman 388 seif 1) On the first day. This means the day of burial, even if he died yesterday; see Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chaim 38 s.k. 3. If he died and was buried on Yom Tov, he puts on tefillin on the first day after Yom Tov, since he already observed private mourning. In the diaspora, where there are two days of Yom Tov and the last day of Yom Tov counts for him as the first day, it is obvious that he puts on tefillin on the day after Yom Tov. See Magen Avraham 38 s.k. 5: on the second day, if new faces come, he does not put them on until they leave; if he already put them on, he does not remove them. See the Darkhei Moshe here in the name of the Mordechai, that on the third day, even if new faces come, he puts on tefillin. In my humble opinion, the Mordechai’s words are astonishing, for Rabbi Eliezer says only that from the third day onward, if new faces come, he does not remove them; from the sugya, who said that as a matter of law on the third day he puts them on? This requires study.
