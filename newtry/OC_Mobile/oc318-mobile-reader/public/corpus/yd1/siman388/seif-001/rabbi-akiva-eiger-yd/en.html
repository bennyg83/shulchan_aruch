(Siman 388, seif 1) On the first day. Meaning the day of burial, even if death occurred the previous day. Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chayyim siman 38, seif katan 3, writes this. He further writes that if death occurred and burial took place on Yom Tov, tefillin are worn on the first weekday after Yom Tov because private mourning practices were already observed. Outside Eretz Yisrael, where two days of Yom Tov are observed and the last day counts as the first day, it is obvious that tefillin are worn on the next day. Magen Avraham there, seif katan 5, writes that on the second day, if new people arrive, he does not put on tefillin until they leave; if he already put them on, he does not remove them. Darkei Moshe here cites Mordechai that on the third day, even if new people arrive, he puts them on. In my opinion Mordechai's words are difficult, because R. Eliezer says from the third day onward that if new people arrive he need not remove them. This does not prove that he initially puts them on in their presence. This requires study.
