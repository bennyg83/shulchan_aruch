Baer Heitev, Orach Chayyim siman 38, seif katan 4, cites Maharit Tzahalon that exemption from tefillin on the first day applies specifically when death and burial occurred on the same day. If death occurred one day and burial another, the mourner is obligated in tefillin even on the first day of mourning. Dagul MeRevavah cites and rejects this, concluding that even a day that is only the day of burial carries the tefillin prohibition. Responsa Zichron Yitzchak, siman 2, writes that his father, author of Mishnat Chachamim, likewise rejected Maharit Tzahalon's position.
On the first day. Baer Heitev cites Eliyah Rabbah, Orach Chayyim siman 38, seif katan 4, who is uncertain about one who receives a death report after praying Maariv. Since the final seif of siman 375 says that this day does not count and counting begins the next day, is the following day treated as the first day for tefillin as well? Taz below, siman 402, seif 11, appears to say tefillin are worn. Chomot Yerushalayim, siman 264, argues that Taz does not clearly imply this and recommends putting on tefillin without a blessing and covering them.
