It is forbidden to put on. See Be'er Heitev in Orach Chayim siman 38 s.k. 4, who wrote in the name of Maharit Tzahalon in his novellae to chapter Elu Megalchin that specifically when the day of death is the day of burial is he exempt from tefillin on the first day; but when the death was on one day and the burial on another, he is obligated in tefillin even on the first day. See there. See Dagul MeRevavah, who also brought this and objected to it, concluding that even if it is only the day of burial, tefillin are forbidden; see there. See Zikhron Yitzchak responsa siman 2, who wrote that his father, the Gaon author of Mishnat Chachamim, also objected to the view of Maharit Tzahalon mentioned above; see there.
