See Baer Heitev to Orach Chaim 38, seif katan 4, which cites Maharit Tzahalon in his novellae to Eilu Metziot that exemption from tefillin on the first day applies specifically when the day of death is also the day of burial. If death occurred on one day and burial on another, the mourner must wear tefillin even on the first day of mourning. Dagul MeRevavah cites this and disagrees, concluding that even a day that is only the day of burial is forbidden in tefillin. Responsa Zichron Yitzchak, siman 2, writes that his father, author of Mishnat Chachamim, also challenged Maharit Tzahalon's position.

Eliyah Rabbah to Orach Chaim 38, seif katan 4, raises a doubt where one heard the report after praying Maariv, so that under the rule above at the end of siman 375 that day does not count and the mourning count starts from the following day. Does he wear tefillin on the next day, which is called his first counted day? The Taz below, siman 402, seif 11, appears to indicate that he does wear them. Chomot Yerushalayim, siman 264, argues that the Taz does not clearly imply this and recommends putting them on without a berachah and keeping them covered; see there.
