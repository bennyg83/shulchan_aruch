First. The Taz wrote that even on the day of a close report of death, it is forbidden to put on tefillin. So wrote the Ramban, and the Beit Yosef cites him below in siman 402. End quote.
The sun. The Shach wrote: some poskim hold that it is forbidden only on the first day, but on the second day it is permitted immediately, even before sunrise (once the day has become light); and so ruled the Bach; end quote (and see Beit Hillel).
