The first day. Taz writes that even on the day one hears a recent report of a death, he may not put on tefillin. Ramban writes so, and Beit Yosef cites it later in siman 402.
Sunrise. Shach writes that some authorities hold tefillin are prohibited only on the first day, while on the second day they are permitted immediately when daylight begins, even before sunrise. Bach rules this way.
