One who swore that he would not marry a woman within three years, and he transgressed and married — if he said language whose meaning is that he would not perform the act of marriage with her, it is as one who swore on a loaf and ate it: he is questioned and they release him. But if he said language whose meaning is that she would not be his wife, they do not release him until he divorces her, like the days on which he treated vows lightly. (And it has already been explained that thirty days suffice.) (Responsum of Rashba; and responsum of Ramban; and Rivash regarding the halacha.)
