It is like an oath. Avodat HaGershuni derives that where a community banned appointing a rabbi with local relatives, then violated the ban and appointed one, they may retain him because the prohibition addressed appointment, not continued tenure. Zikhron Yosef similarly permits studying from Talmud volumes printed in violation of a printing ban, since the ban prohibited printing, not later use. He compares Jericho, which Jews inhabited after it was rebuilt in violation of Joshua's oath.
