He swore that he shall not marry, etc. — and the oath takes effect in such a case even though he nullifies the mitzvah of procreation, as below siman 239 seif 8 in the gloss; and see there what I wrote in the name of Or Zaru'a that the oath does not take effect; and according to this one may be lenient here that he need not divorce her.
That he was asked and they annul, etc. — and he is released from his oath, as below siman 238 s.k. 20.
