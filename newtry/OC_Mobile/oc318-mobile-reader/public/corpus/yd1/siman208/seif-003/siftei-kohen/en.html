Few days. Meaning he observed permissibility few days; but many days — meaning he observed permissibility many days — thirty days suffice. However, this is according to Beit Yosef; and for halachah we rule like those rabbis who permit thirty days. But one should not set his heart to examine Tosafot chapter 2 of Nedarim and Rosh there and Rabbeinu Yerucham, Netiv 14 ch. 7, who ruled even in many days he must observe prohibition like all days he observed permissibility; and thus appears Tur's view, who wrote unspecified must observe prohibition like days he observed permissibility, and did not distinguish — not like Perishah's gloss explaining his words "few days." If so, since all these rabbis forbid, and Raavad and Rivash, as explained in Beit Yosef — one must not be lenient.
