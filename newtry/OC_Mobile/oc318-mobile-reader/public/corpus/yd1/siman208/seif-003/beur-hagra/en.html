When does this apply, etc. This follows Rabbi Yosi, because the halachah follows him against Rabbi Yehudah, as explained in the fourth chapter of Eruvin, 46b and 47a. The Rambam rules likewise. Although one could distinguish that this applies specifically to naziriteship, because an unspecified term of naziriteship is thirty days, unlike unspecified vows, which have no fixed term, as stated there on 4b, 15a, and 28a, and it is therefore possible that Rabbi Yehudah would agree regarding vows, nevertheless the Tosefta at the end of the first chapter of Nedarim records their dispute regarding vows as well. See Tosafot, s.v. 'who,' and so rule the other authorities, unlike the Rivash. The Tur, however, rules in accordance with Rabbi Yehudah. It appears to me that he intends to rule like Rabbi Yehudah entirely, as Tosafot write there. Although at first glance it would appear that the halachah follows Rabbi Yosi, nevertheless, since Nazir 32a cites Rabbi Yehudah's view anonymously, refers to it as the view of the Sages, and states that the halachah follows it, the Tur rules accordingly. He likewise rules this way in siman 334, seif 28, even though an unspecified term of excommunication is thirty days, just as an unspecified term of naziriteship is thirty days, as stated in the third chapter of Moed Katan, 16a.
