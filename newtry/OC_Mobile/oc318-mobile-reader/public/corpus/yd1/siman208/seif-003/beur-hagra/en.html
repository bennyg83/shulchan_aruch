Where does this apply, etc. Like Rabbi Yosi — halachah is like him against Rabbi Yehuda, as in chapter 4 of Eruvin (46b); likewise Rambam. Even though one may distinguish — specifically in nezirut where unspecified nezirut is thirty days, unlike unspecified vows where there is no fixed term, as explained 4b and 15a and 28a there. Perhaps Rabbi Yehuda agrees; in Tosefta chapter 1 of Nedarim their dispute is taught in vows. Tosafot "mi"; likewise Shulchan Aruch — not like Rivash. But Tur ruled like Rabbi Yehuda; it appears his view is to rule like Rabbi Yehuda entirely, as Tosafot wrote there — though apparently halachah is like Rabbi Yosi, nevertheless since in Nazir 32a Rabbi Yehuda is cited anonymously and called "the Rabbis, halachah is like him," likewise in siman 334 s.k. 28; even though unspecified niduy is thirty days like nezirut, as in chapter 3 of Moed Katan (16a).
