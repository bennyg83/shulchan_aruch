Mishnah, Nedarim 18b.
According to the Rosh's second explanation of the Mishnah's statement that doubtful expressions are interpreted leniently. The Tur rules likewise, and this also appears from Responsa Rashba, which states that if the expression was unspecified it is forbidden, meaning if he spoke without knowing what he intended.
The Rosh there, because the Mishnah does not state that release must be sought.
The Tur, based on the Mishnah there, following Rabbi Yose; the Rambam likewise rules in chapter 2.
