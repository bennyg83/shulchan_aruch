The laws of unspecified vows and one who vows by cherem; it contains five se’ifim.
Unspecified vows — to be stringent. How so? If he said, “These fruits are like salted meat,” or “like libation wine” — there is in its meaning salted meat of consecrated items, or libation wine for Heaven, which is a vow, for behold he associates with a matter subject to vow; and there is further in its meaning salted meat and libation wine of idolatry, which is not a vow, for behold he associates with a forbidden matter — we suspend it to be stringent. And if he says that his intention was like salted meat and libation wine of idolatry, he is believed, and he does not need questioning, even if he is an am ha’aretz. And if the majority of the people of the place call consecrated salted meat “salted meat” without specification, and libation wine for Heaven “wine” [libation wine] without specification — he is not believed.
