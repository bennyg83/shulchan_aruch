And he need not inquire, etc. From the end of the mishnah "and if they inquired," etc. — the question is not here but specifically there, because its meaning is to be more stringent than to be lenient, as Ran wrote there; and Tosafot "stam," etc. According to Rambam, even one who attaches to a forbidden matter explicitly needs inquiry for an am ha'aretz, as above siman 205 — he need not teach here, for otherwise inquiry is needed; and Shulchan Aruch's words are doubtful; and Shach strained on this.
And if the majority, etc. Then it is like a vow in cherem, etc., since its meaning is more to forbid, as above; and as in the mishnah 62b, "everything according to," etc.; and there 49a, Mar because, etc.; and there 52b, 53a; and Shach.
