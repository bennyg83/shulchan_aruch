In the cherem of the sea. — meaning in the nets of the sea.
Behold myself a korban. — for it implies he consecrated himself, and he afterward said “I only vowed with one bone,” etc.
And if he is an am ha'aretz, etc. — and it was already explained above siman 205 that in our time we treat everyone as an am ha'aretz.
Nevertheless regret suffices. — And Bach ruled that regret does not suffice, rather one must open an opening for him from another place, as above siman 205 seif 1.
We do not penalize, etc. — since a query is required only rabbinically.
Except grain. — but if he did not specify "except grain" it is a vain vow and not a vow at all, as below siman 232 seif 5.
And he transgressed it, etc. — but if he wants it annulled in order to transgress, some say we do not annul for him even if there is a stumbling block in the matter, as below siman 228 seif 15, s.k. 27.
