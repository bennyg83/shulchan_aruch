Because of excommunication. Shach wrote — it is astonishing, for below siman 334 s.k. 28 he wrote if he treated lightly his niduy and cherem he must observe prohibition like the days he observed permissibility before they annul him. It appears to answer — if he treated lightly the laws of niduy, not observing what a menudeh must observe, there is only a rabbinic matter; but if he accepted something in niduy or cherem and transgressed that matter, it is biblical, for niduy and cherem themselves are biblical; therefore in siman 334 it deals where he treated lightly niduy and transgressed that for which he was ostracized, or accepted cherem or niduy — this is the true law — end quote.
