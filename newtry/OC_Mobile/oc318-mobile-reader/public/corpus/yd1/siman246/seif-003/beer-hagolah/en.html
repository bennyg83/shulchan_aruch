From Rabbi Chanina ben Dosa, Taanit 25a; Rabbi Yehoshua, Berakhot 28a; and Hillel, Yoma 35b. Rambam further writes that some of Israel's greatest sages were woodcutters, water carriers, and blind, yet they studied Torah day and night and transmitted the tradition person from person back to Moshe Rabbeinu. Kesef Mishneh explains that the woodcutter was Hillel, the water carrier was Rav Huna, Ketubot 105a, and the blind sages were Rav Yosef and Rav Sheshet.
