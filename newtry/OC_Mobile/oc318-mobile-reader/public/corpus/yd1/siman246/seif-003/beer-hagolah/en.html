From the statement of Rabbi Chanina ben Dosa — Taanit beginning page 25 side 1, and Rabbi Yehoshua in Berakhot page 28 side 1, and Hillel in Yoma page 35 side 2; and he wrote further there: great sages of Israel were among them woodchoppers and water-drawers and blind men, and nevertheless they engaged in Torah day and night, and they were among those who transmit the tradition person from person from Moses our teacher; and the Kesef Mishneh explained there — woodchoppers is Hillel, water-drawers is Rav Huna (Ketubot page 105 side 1), and blind men are Rav Yosef and Rav Sheshet.
