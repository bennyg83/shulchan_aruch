And the custom, etc., if he has no, etc. This follows from the beginning of chapter 13 of Ketubot concerning the judges who issued decrees, etc., and the teachers of kemitzah, etc. See Tosafot there, 105a, s.v. 'the judges who issued decrees,' and Bekhorot there, s.v. 'What,' etc.: 'Come and hear, even if he has,' etc. The Rosh cites this there. The Jerusalem Talmud in Nedarim likewise says: 'Do we not see that teachers take payment?' Rabbi Yose son of Rabbi Bon answers: They receive compensation for lost time.
