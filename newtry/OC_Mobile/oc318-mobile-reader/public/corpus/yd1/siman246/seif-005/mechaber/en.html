A place where they practiced to teach Written Torah for pay — it is permitted to teach for pay; but Oral Torah is forbidden to teach for pay; if he did not find one to teach him free, he learns for pay — and even though he needed to learn for pay, he should not say, "As I learned for pay so I will teach for pay," but teach others free. And what they practice today to teach everything for pay — if he has no means to sustain himself, it is permitted; and even if he has means, if it is idle pay it is evident he leaves all his business and burden — it is permitted: {Rama: (And all rabbinic novelties — that is, what was enacted by rabbinic decree — it is permitted to take pay to teach them) (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 1).}
