One asked regarding the subject. Rabbeinu Nissim explains that a student who asks about Pesach laws within thirty days before Pesach is considered to be asking an immediately relevant question and is answered first. There is also a monetary consequence. Bava Metzia teaches that when a teacher is presently obligated to teach his students, he is considered “borrowed” to them, so if they borrow his property and it is lost by accident they may be exempt as a borrowing while the owner is in service. Rava is considered in their service only when he cannot change the subject, such as during the public festival lectures. Since a teacher must teach Pesach laws during the thirty days before Pesach, during that period property borrowed from him may likewise have the law of borrowing while the owner is in service.
