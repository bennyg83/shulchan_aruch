Regarding this matter. The Taz wrote in name of the Ran that if one student asks about Pesach laws thirty days before Pesach, this is asking about the matter and they attend to him first. And I wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 336 that there is another ramification regarding asking questions from owners, etc.; see there.
For an a fortiori inference. Shach records an alternate reading that priority is given to aggadah. See there.
