A woman who learned Torah has reward, but not like a man's reward, because she is not commanded and does; and even though she has reward, the Sages commanded that a man not teach his daughter Torah, because most women's minds are not directed to learning and they extract words of Torah to frivolity according to the poverty of their minds; the Sages said: whoever teaches his daughter Torah — it is as if he teaches her lewdness. When do we say this? Oral Torah; but Written Torah — he should not teach her ab initio, and if he taught her it is not like teaching lewdness (Rambam and Semag, not like some books of Tur): {Rama: Nevertheless a woman is obligated to learn laws pertaining to women (Agur in the name of Semag); and a woman is not obligated to teach her son Torah, but if she helps her son or husband engage in Torah, she shares reward with them (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 1, Semag).}
