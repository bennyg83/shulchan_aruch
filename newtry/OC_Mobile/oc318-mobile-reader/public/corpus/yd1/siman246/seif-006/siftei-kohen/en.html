Because he is not commanded and does. And greater is one commanded and does than one not commanded and does (and the Tur wrote) [and Tosafot wrote] the reason — because one commanded always worries to nullify his impulse and fulfill the commandments of his Creator — meaning one who is commanded: if so there is punishment for transgression and his impulse overcomes him and he always worries to nullify his impulse; but in one not commanded there is no punishment here.
