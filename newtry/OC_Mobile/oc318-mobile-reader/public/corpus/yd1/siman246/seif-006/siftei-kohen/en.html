Because he performs it although he is not commanded. One who is commanded and performs is greater than one who is not commanded and performs. Tosafos explain that the reason is that one who is commanded is constantly anxious to subdue his inclination and fulfill the commandment of his Creator. That is to say, because he is commanded, violation carries punishment, his inclination resists him, and he must continually strive to subdue it. One who is not commanded faces no such punishment.
