One does not say to him, 'Health.' This is because it interrupts study in the beis midrash. The Perishah writes that perhaps this applied specifically in their days, when they would not even look outside their books, and certainly would not converse. Nowadays, however, when people are in any event not careful about this, one may say, 'Health.' End quote.
