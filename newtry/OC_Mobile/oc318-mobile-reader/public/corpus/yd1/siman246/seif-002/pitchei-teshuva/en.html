To set him time. See in responsum Orach Chayyim siman 155 s.k. 1, who wrote that in Beit Rabbi he wrote in name of Maharatz that he judged the case without taking wages — this counts as establishing times; but for wages, and likewise teaching children for wages — it does not count. And see there he is uncertain regarding one who studies in a yeshiva at a fixed time and receives a stipend — whether it counts. And see there what they said "all who review halachot," etc. — a minimum of two halachot. And see in Magen Avraham siman 215 s.k. 5 who wrote that when studying blessings in the Gemara he should say them without mentioning the Name; and see in She'elat Yaavetz part 1 siman 81 that his opinion is he may say with mentioning the Name; and see in the responsum there in name of Beit Rabbi that elderly rabbis in the Land of Israel were accustomed not to mention it; and likewise in Machatzit HaShekel in name of Maharam — see there.
