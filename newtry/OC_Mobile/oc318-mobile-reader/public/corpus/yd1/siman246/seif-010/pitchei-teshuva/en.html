That he contemplate. See in responsum Orach Chayyim siman 3 s.k. 1, who wrote a segulah to be saved from contemplating words of Torah in the bathroom — in name of the Ari that he should first say "be honored, departed ones," etc.; although he wrote that nowadays they are not accustomed to say it — nevertheless one should say it for this purpose to be saved from contemplation of words of Torah — see there; and perhaps these words were said only for individuals whose Torah is their craft and not for others, lest it appear as haughtiness — end quote; and in my humble opinion it appears that showing haughtiness applies only to something done before the many; but not here. [I have now seen that also the Beit Yosef in Orach Chayyim there in name of Abudraham wrote likewise that it appears as haughtiness — see there.]
