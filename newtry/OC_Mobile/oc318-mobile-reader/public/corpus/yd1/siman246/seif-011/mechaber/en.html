The student should not be ashamed before his fellow who learned the first or second time and he did not learn even after many times — for if he is ashamed in this, he will enter and leave the study hall having learned nothing; therefore they said: the bashful does not learn, and the impatient does not teach. When do we say this? When students did not understand because of depth or because their minds are short; but if it is evident to the rabbi that they are lax in Torah and neglect it and therefore did not understand — he must be angry at them and shame them with words to sharpen them; concerning this they said: throw bitterness at the students; therefore it is not proper for a rabbi to act lightly before students, nor jest before them, nor eat and drink with them, so his awe is upon them and they learn quickly.
