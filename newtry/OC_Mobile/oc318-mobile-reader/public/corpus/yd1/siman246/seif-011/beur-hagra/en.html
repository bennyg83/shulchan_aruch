Therefore it is not proper, etc. As stated there: Conduct your leadership with authority. According to our reading and the Aruch's explanation: Do not demean your authority before the students; rather, lead them with a firm hand. Avot also says, 'and pleasant with youth,' and Shabbat 30b states: Every student who sits, etc.
