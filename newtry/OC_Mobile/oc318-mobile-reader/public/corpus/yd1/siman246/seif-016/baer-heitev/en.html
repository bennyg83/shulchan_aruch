Sleeping. In Beit Yosef he cites what is stated in the Talmud regarding Rabbi Zeira, who replied "I did not sleep in the synagogue — neither a fixed sleep nor a casual sleep." One must say that casual sleep he observed only as an act of piety, but strictly it is permitted, as stated in Orach Chayyim siman 151 seif 3 — so ends Sh"akh:
