One may not sleep in the beis midrash, etc. Beis Yosef cites the statement in the chapter Bnei HaIr: They asked Rabbi Zeira through what merit he lived long, and he answered, among other things, that he never slept in the beis midrash, neither a regular sleep nor a temporary sleep. It must be that he refrained from temporary sleep as an act of piety, but according to the law it is permitted, as explained in Orach Chayim, siman 151, se'if 3.
