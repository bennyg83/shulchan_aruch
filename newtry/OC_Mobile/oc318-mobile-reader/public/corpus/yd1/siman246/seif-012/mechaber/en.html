They do not ask the rabbi immediately when he enters the study hall until his mind settles upon him; and the student, when he enters, does not ask until he sits and rests; and two do not ask as one; and they do not ask the rabbi about another matter but only the matter they are engaged in, so he not be ashamed; and the rabbi may mislead students in his questions and in acts he does before them to sharpen them and to know if they remember what they learn; and needless to say he may ask them about another matter they are not engaged in to rouse them.
