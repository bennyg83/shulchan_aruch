One who wishes to merit the crown of Torah should be careful with all his nights and not lose even one in sleep, eating, drinking, talk, and the like — but in words of wisdom and Torah study: {Rama: For a person learns most of his wisdom only at night (language of Tur from Rambam); and a person should begin learning at night from 15 Av onward; and "he who does not add will have taken away" (Nimmukei Yosef, chapter Yesh Nochalin, Gemara, Rambam — not in Rambam there daf 121b, end of Taanit, evening).}
