He should be careful all his nights, etc. The Perishah wrote: it seems to me "all his nights" as its plain meaning — that even on nights of the tequfah of Tammuz when they are short, likewise he should study; one who increases and one who decreases — in any case he should study a little. And what is written (Eruvin 65 side 1) "night was only created for sleep" — we establish it (Tosafot there) on nights of the tequfah of Tammuz; it seems to me he meant that then his main night should be for sleep; and in the manner they said "the moon was only created for study" — we establish it on nights of the tequfah of Tevet when they are long; and certainly then he must also sleep and not study all night — but he meant to say the main thing should be for him then for study because of the length of the nights; and see in Orach Chayyim siman 238.
[Joseph. He will die without his time.”
