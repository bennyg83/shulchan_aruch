One who wishes, etc.; and not, etc. As stated in Eruvin 65a: Night was created only for Torah study, etc.; and Avot speaks of one who stays awake at night, etc. See the Be'er HaGolah and seif 24; see also Tamid 32b.
(Additional note) One who wishes, etc.; for there is no, etc. The Rambam writes: The Sages said that the song of Torah is heard only at night. This appears in Midrash Shir HaShirim on the verse 'His locks are curled.' Rabbi Yochanan says that the song of Torah is heard only at night, based on 'She rises while it is still night.' Reish Lakish says that Torah must be studied by day and by night, based on 'You shall meditate upon it day and night.' Reish Lakish nevertheless said: Rabbi Yochanan taught me well that the song of Torah is heard only at night, for when I would labor in Torah by day, it would become illuminated for me at night. See also the end of Menachot on 'A song of ascents: Behold, bless Hashem,' and the Rambam.
