For anyone who studies in a synagogue, etc. This is not precise, since the sanctity of a beis midrash is more stringent than that of a synagogue, as stated above in se'if 7. We also find in Berachos 8a that Rabbi Ami and Rabbi Asi, although they had thirteen synagogues, prayed only where they studied. It does not say that they studied where they prayed. Perishah.
