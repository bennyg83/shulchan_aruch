And some say, etc. — and Ran further distinguishes: for soft teachings the students stand; for requests they sit; and in Ein Yaakov, chapter "One who reads standing," he wrote that when they repeated they all stood, for they did not need to analyze; but in reasoning where they need to analyze, the rabbi sat and others stood; sometimes they all sat — when they needed to analyze and toil in halachah — end.
And he did not write them, etc. — and see in Orach Chayim siman 145, who wrote laws of the translator regarding Torah reading.
