One does not ask, etc.; if two asked, etc.; if the questioners were, etc. All of this appears in the Tosefta there.
And some say, etc. This follows from Berachot 27b and Bekhorot 36a: 'The questioner stood,' etc. See also the Jerusalem Talmud, chapter 10 of Nedarim, cited by Tosafot to Bekhorot there, s.v. 'The questioner stood.' The author's words require study, for he writes there 'halachot and aggadot.' One must say that these authorities disagree with the Tosefta. The Shach's words are surprising: he writes that this applied after Rabban Gamliel, but then what is the meaning of 'one does not ask'?
