To stand. The Shach wrote that Tosafot wrote this only regarding the incident of Rabbi Tzadok, where Rabban Gamliel said to the questioner, etc.; and that was certainly according to the law, for Rabban Gamliel was before the destruction of the Temple. In this even the Rambam agrees, as he wrote at the beginning of chapter 4 of Hilchot Talmud Torah: originally the teacher would sit and the students would stand. But after Rabban Gamliel died, illness descended to the world and they would study Torah while seated. If so, here the Rambam speaks in our time, when students do not ask while standing, and in this no one disagrees with him. This requires study; end quote.
