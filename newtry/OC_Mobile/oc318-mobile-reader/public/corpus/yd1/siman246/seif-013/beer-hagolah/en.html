In name of the Rambam, from what we say "if we say merit of Torah" — it is not commanded and does; and as Rashi explains there Sotah page 21 side 1; and the statement of Rabbi Chanina — Kiddushin page 31 side 1 and Avodah Zarah page 3 side 1; and Tosafot wrote the reason — because one who is commanded always worries to nullify his impulse and fulfill the commandments of his Creator.
