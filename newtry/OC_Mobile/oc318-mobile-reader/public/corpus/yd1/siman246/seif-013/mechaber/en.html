They do not ask standing and do not answer standing: {Rama: (And some say when asking a halachic matter one must stand) (Tosafot, chapter Pesulei HaMukdashin, Yerushalmi).} Nor from a height, nor from afar, nor from behind the elders; and they ask only in a matter; and they ask only in awe; and one should not ask about more than three laws.
