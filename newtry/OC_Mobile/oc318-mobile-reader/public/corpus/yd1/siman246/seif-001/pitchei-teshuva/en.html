He must claim time. See in Mishnat Chachamim Laws of Torah Study page 44, who brought in name of his father the Gaon that he was uncertain regarding one who has a fixed amount to study each day — which pages — and due to some constraint was prevented one or two days: whether he is obligated to make up on another day by studying more each day according to his ability in order to complete his vow; or shall we say it is literally as if he vowed on that day specifically, like a sacrifice fixed for its time — if its day passed its sacrifice is void. He then resolved for himself that specifically the night after he is obligated to complete the duty of that day and not on another day; and he himself questioned this, and his opinion tends that he is obligated to make up even on another day, and must learn this matter before his other learning so that he not be lax and come to transgress his vow — see there.
