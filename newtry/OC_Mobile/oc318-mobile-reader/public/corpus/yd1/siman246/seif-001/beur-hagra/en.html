He is obligated to establish, etc. As stated in Shabbat 31a: 'Did you set fixed times,' etc. This refers to one who has an occupation. See seif 4 regarding how, etc.
By day and by night, etc. Menachot 99b, where Ben Dama asked, etc.
And in pressing circumstances, etc. This follows Rabbi Yochanan there and Nedarim 8a. The Ran does not understand it this way there. It appears that he maintains that our sugya follows Rabbi Yose in the Mishnah, as is evident there from his words, etc.
And one who cannot, etc. Bereishit Rabbah, chapter 99, teaches that Zebulun is mentioned before Issachar because Zebulun engaged in commerce and sustained Issachar, who engaged in Torah. Therefore Scripture placed him first, as it says, 'It is a tree of life to those who support it,' etc. Moses likewise said, 'Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out,' because Issachar's tents are yours, since you enable him to dwell in them. Vayikra Rabbah, chapter 25, similarly expounds that the verse does not say 'to those who toil in it,' but 'to those who support it.' In the future, the Holy One, blessed be He, will make a shade and canopy in the Garden of Eden for supporters of mitzvot beside Torah scholars. This is also the meaning of 'Shimon, the brother of Azariah': although Shimon was greater, he was named after the brother who supported him. The same principle applies to Zebulun and Issachar.
