That every person is obligated in Torah study and how they learn for pay. And it contains 26 seifim.
Every Israelite man is obligated in Torah study — whether poor or rich, whether sound in body or suffering afflictions, whether young or very old; even a poor person going door to door, even one with a wife and children — he is obligated to set a time for Torah study by day and by night, as it says, "And you shall meditate on it day and night": {Rama: (And in time of distress, even if he read only Shema morning and evening — "they shall not depart from your mouth" — we read this regarding him) (Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 1, Semag positive mitzvot 12).} And one who cannot learn because he does not know at all how to learn, or because of troubles he has — he supports others who learn: {Rama: And it is considered for him as if he learned himself (Tur); and a person may stipulate with his fellow that he will engage in Torah and the fellow will provide his sustenance and they divide the reward; but if he already engaged in Torah he cannot sell his share for money the fellow will give him (Teshuvat Or Zaru'a netiv 2 from Gemara Sotah).}
