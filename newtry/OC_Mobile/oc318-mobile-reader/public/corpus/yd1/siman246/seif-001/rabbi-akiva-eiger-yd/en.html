(Siman 246, seif 1, in Rama's gloss) Toldot Adam VeChavah. Netiv 2, based on the Gemara in Sotah. He writes there: “It is reasonable that one who engages in it has lost his reward, because he has already forfeited his portion. So write the commentators”; this concludes his words. See Responsa Maharam Alshakar, siman 101, in the name of Rav Hai Gaon. See also Esh Dat by R' Chayyim Alfandari, parashat Vayelech.
