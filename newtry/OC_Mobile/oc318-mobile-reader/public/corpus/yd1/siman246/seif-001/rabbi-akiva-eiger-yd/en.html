(Siman 246 seif 1 in the Hagah) Tav — path 2 from the Talmud Sotah. He wrote there: it is reasonable that one who occupies himself [with Torah but not for its own sake] has lost his reward, since he has already nullified his share — so the commentators wrote. See Responsa Maharam Alshaker (siman 101) in the name of Rabbeinu Hai Gaon; and see Sefer Eish Das of R' Chaim Alfandari (parashas Vayeilech).
