It is forbidden even to mention its name, etc. The Tur states that it is forbidden to mention an idol by name needlessly. The word 'even' teaches two points: not only is taking a vow in its name forbidden, but even ordinary mention is forbidden; and although the Gemara forbids telling another person to wait beside a certain idol, the Rosh explains that a name recorded in Scripture may be mentioned. Thus a name that has become merely the ordinary name of a place, or whose idolatrous significance is no longer recognized, is treated according to the distinctions explained by the authorities. Needless mention intended to honor the idolatry remains forbidden. [Citation 143.]
