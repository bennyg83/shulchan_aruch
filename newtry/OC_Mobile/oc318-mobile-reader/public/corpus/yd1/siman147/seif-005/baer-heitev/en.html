Your god. Taz writes that this ruling is imprecise. It was derived from the law of shemittah, where one may use this expression to a Jew because it refers to the Holy One, blessed be He. But Heaven forbid that one address a gentile directly in this form, for that would strengthen the gentile in his idolatry. Rather, one should say, "May God assist you." Bach likewise establishes this as the halachah.
