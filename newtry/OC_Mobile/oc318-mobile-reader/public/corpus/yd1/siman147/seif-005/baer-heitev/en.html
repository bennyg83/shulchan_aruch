Your God. The Taz wrote: this derivation is imprecise — for it was derived from the laws of the Sabbatical year, where that phrasing applies as addressed to the Jew, referring to the Holy One blessed be He. But Heaven forbid to say to a non-Jew in the second person, which would be strengthening the hands of the non-Jew in idolatry. Rather one should say: "May God be your help." And so the Bach established as the practical ruling.
