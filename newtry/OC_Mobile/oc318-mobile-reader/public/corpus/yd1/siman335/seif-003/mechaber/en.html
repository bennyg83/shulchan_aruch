One who visits the sick should not sit on the bed nor on a chair nor on a bench, but wraps himself and sits before him, for the Divine Presence is above his head. {Rama: And specifically when the sick person lies on the ground, for the sitter is higher than he; but when he lies on a bed it is permitted to sit on a chair and bench (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran, Hagahot Maimoniyot, Tosafot, and Hagahot Alfasi); and so is the practice.}
