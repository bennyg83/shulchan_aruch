They tell him to issue instructions, etc. Torat HaAdam in the name of Masechet Semachot, although it is not in our version. Its text there says: when a person takes to his bed, they enter and tell him, 'Say neither words that give life nor words that cause death. Perhaps you lent to someone, or someone lent to you; perhaps you deposited with others, or others deposited with you.' They do not enter on the first or second day, but on the third. If he is delirious, even on the first. Relatives may enter at any time, for visiting the sick has no fixed measure. [Citation references: 50.]
