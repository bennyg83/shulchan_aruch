For intestinal illness a man does not attend a woman, but a woman attends a man. {Rama: Some say one who has a sick person in his house should go to a sage in the city to ask mercy for him (Nimukei Yosef, chapter Yevamot); and so they practiced to bless the sick in the synagogue to give them a new name, for changing the name tears up his decree; comforting mourners takes precedence over visiting the sick (Kol Bo).}
