Rather, he should not visit, etc. The Bach wrote that regarding escorting an enemy, there is no concern that he will rejoice at his misfortune, since this is the end of all people. But regarding comforting a mourner or visiting a sick person who is his enemy, there is such concern. However, everything depends on the nature of the enmity and on the people who are enemies.
