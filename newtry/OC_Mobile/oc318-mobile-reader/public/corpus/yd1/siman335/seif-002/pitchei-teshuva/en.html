To bless the sick. See Baer Heitev of the Rav Maharit z"l, who wrote in the name of Maharil that one should not bless a sick person when he is in another place. See Responsa Nachalat Shivah, siman 59, who disagrees and acted in practice to bless a sick person on Shabbat in the synagogue even though the sick person was not in that city, but in a settlement more than an hour's walk away. He brought proof from Gittin that even if one left him old or sick, he gives it to her on the presumption that he is alive. One great authority wrote to uphold the words of Maharil, and he returned and replied to him; see there from siman 76 to siman 81.
