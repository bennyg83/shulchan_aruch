Even a great person goes to visit a lesser one, even several times a day, even one of his own age; and whoever adds is praiseworthy, provided he does not trouble him. {Rama: Some say an enemy may go to visit a sick person (Maharil 197); it does not seem so to me except that he should not visit the sick or console—but if he is his enemy so he not think he rejoices in his misfortune and has only distress. So it appears to me (Shas, chapter 23).}
