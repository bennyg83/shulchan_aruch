In whatever language he wishes. The rule that ministering angels do not attend to Aramaic does not apply here, because the Shechinah is with the sick person, as it says, “God supports him on the bed of illness”; therefore at that time there is no need for angels.
Only in the Holy Tongue. This implies that the angels do not attend to any other language, except specifically the Holy Tongue. This is the view of the Rif, brought in Orach Chaim siman 101. However, there he wrote in the name of the Rosh that only to Aramaic do they not attend, but other languages are like the Holy Tongue. If so, it is difficult regarding the Tur: why did he state here without qualification against his father, and require specifically the Holy Tongue? It appears that because a sick person needs abundant mercy, he wrote here that one should act according to all opinions.
