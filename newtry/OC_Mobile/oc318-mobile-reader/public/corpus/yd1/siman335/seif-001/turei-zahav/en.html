The relatives and the friends enter immediately. My father-in-law of blessed memory explained the reason: so that his mazal not be harmed by imposing upon him the name of a sick person, as we say in Nedarim (daf 40), Rava, on the first day that he became weak, said: do not reveal to people, so that his mazal not be harmed etc.; but relatives and friends who always enter his house, there is not so much of a sensation; and therefore when the illness springs upon him, even the distant ones enter immediately:
