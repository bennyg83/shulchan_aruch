Relatives and close friends enter immediately. My father-in-law explains that this avoids worsening the patient’s fortune by publicly establishing him as ill, as Rava said in Nedarim 40 on the first day of his illness, “Do not reveal it to people, lest my fortune be impaired.” Relatives and friends, who enter his home regularly, create no unusual indication. If the illness strikes suddenly and severely, even more distant people may enter immediately.
