It is a mitzvah. Sotah 14a, Shabbos 127a, Bava Kamma 100a, Bava Metzia chapter 2, and Nedarim 39b to 40a.
The relatives. Masechet Semachot and the sources cited below. The Yerushalmi in Gittin and at the end of chapter 3 of Peah, cited by Rosh, teaches that with an ordinary illness close relatives enter immediately and more distant people after three days. If the illness comes suddenly or dangerously, all enter immediately. A story tells that R. Huna, R. Pinchas, and R. Chizkiyah visited R. Yose after three days, and he said to them, "Did you wish to fulfill the Mishnah with me?" Meaning colleagues and close associates are considered like relatives.
