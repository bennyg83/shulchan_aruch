A commandment, etc. Sotah 14a, and chapter 18 of Shabbat (127a), and chapter 9 of Bava Kamma (100a), and chapter 2 of Bava Metzia, and chapter 4 of Nedarim (39–40).
The relatives, etc. Tractate Semachot, and as stated below; and the Yerushalmi, chapter "HitkabEl," cited by the Rosh there, s.15, and end of chapter 3 of Pe'ah, cited by the Rosh in chapter 9 of Bava Batra, s.18: "Who is considered dangerously ill? Anyone upon whom the illness came on suddenly — relatives may enter immediately, and those more distant after three days. And if the illness came on suddenly, both may enter immediately." And it is told: R. Yona, R. Pinchas, and R. Chizkiyah went to visit R. Yosi after three days. He said to them: "Did you think to uphold the teaching?"— meaning that colleagues are like relatives.
