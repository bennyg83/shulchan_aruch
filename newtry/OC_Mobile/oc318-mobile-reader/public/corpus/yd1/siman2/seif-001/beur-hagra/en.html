**Even, etc., and even if he does not worship idols, etc.** For the Stam teaches so; and all this is Rambam's wording, and he needed this for his view that he wrote the reason: “And you shall call and eat of his altar”—from which we learn his sacrifice is forbidden—and we do not learn from there except regarding idol worshipper; therefore he needed this that they forbade him in all cases. But R. Yitzchak wrote because it says “And you shall slaughter and eat”—one who is fit to slaughter, eat of his altar; and so explicitly in Tosefta beginning of Makshirin, and he need not all this—and so in the gloss there he wrote like R. Yitzchak's words.
**(Likkut) Even, etc., and even, etc.** Like in Yoreh Deah as stated siman 124 seifim 1 and 2 and Avodah Zarah 29b; and per Rambam because of “and eat of his altar,” and the same here as with stam yayinam; and per R. Yitzchak he need not all this (end quote).
**And even if others supervise, etc.** As above for both reasons; and also from what the Mishnah teaches “and all of them who slaughtered,” etc., and afterward teaches “non-Jew shechitah,” etc.—implies in every case.
**(Likkut) And even if others, etc.** Chullin 3b and Tosafot there s.v. kasever, etc. (end quote).
