**He is not believed.** For he is already suspected regarding the oath, for he is under oath—Mahari Sinai is on the shechitah—so Beit Yosef in the name of Ohel Moed. One might ask me: from Choshen Mishpat siman 34 we only disqualify for testimony for transgressing an issur with lashes; regarding transgressing an oath we say there he is disqualified, even for transgressing a cherem. Per what is written here—that for every transgression we say he transgresses what he swore at Sinai—we should disqualify everyone who transgresses an issur even without lashes, since here his oath does not help him.
