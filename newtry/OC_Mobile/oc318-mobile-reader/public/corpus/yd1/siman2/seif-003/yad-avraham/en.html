**Uncertain:** in this siman seif 65 Mechaber wrote mumar to provoke even for one matter his law like Kuti; so Kessef Mishneh and Be'er HaGolah there seif 65 that one who kicks at one mitzvah—his shechitah nevelah—examine. Yet regarding mumar regarding orlah Rashi wrote “kicking,” etc.—uncertain. Perhaps he holds mumar regarding orlah means he does not circumcise from pain of suffering—that is for appetite. So found explicitly in Kessef Mishneh and Maharshal—examine.
