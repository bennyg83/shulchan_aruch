**(seif 3) (Likkut) Mumar, etc.** See Rashba who compelled with strained proofs (above siman 119 seif 22); and uncertain—for is not the Gemara arranged in chapter 3 of Sanhedrin (27a) that mumar who eats nevelot for appetite—per Rashi disqualified—and they disagree regarding mumar who eats nevelot to provoke; and even in this we rule like Abaye; and at the beginning of Bava Metzia they were uncertain whether we say miggo he is suspected, etc.—and this is regarding that money where we say he should separate through oath, as R. Yitzchak Chasid wrote Ketubot 18b s.v. uvchulei (and Tosafot Bava Metzia there 5b); and also we say “perhaps a loan,” etc. (end quote).
**Even if he swore, etc.** For he is like suspected regarding the oath for that matter, for he is under oath, etc.; and as we conclude in chapter 1 Bava Metzia (6a) that we say miggo he is suspected on money, etc., regarding that money—except because of an old loan.
**(Likkut) Even if he swore, etc.** For he is disqualified for oath as written Choshen Mishpat siman 92; and even b'dieved as written siman 87 there (end quote).
