**(in Or Zarua) Provided they know about him, etc.** Famous difficulty: Rav Ashi establishes in mumar; per us “and all who slaughtered” challenges why not establish in mumar like Ravina's establishment “everyone slaughters” examining knife and giving—in case they know he is expert; otherwise may not slaughter; if slaughtered they test him; not before us; others see—kosher. Per my view to settle: on contrary from this sugya proven with mumar must know he is expert. In sugya Tosafot s.v. mati lo amar keshe'amtah challenged presumption: Rava said below one teaches Kuti one mumar; their answer not understood—per my view Rosh challenged what helps majority found—say rely on minority not experts for animal issur presumption; answered it is minority of minority; therefore even if majority experts—not minority of minority; we say rely on minority for presumption. We find law rely on minority for presumption d'rabbanan from Yevamot (119): Rava said first part presumption for levirate marriage, etc.; Rishonim explained Rava said first and last parts all agree even per R. Meir and Rabbanan—in first part even Rabbanan “do not marry” for we say rely on minority for presumption—examine well. If so for version Stam of Shas there—plain mishnah R. Meir—opposite: per Rabbanan of R. Meir never rely minority for presumption; thereby Rav Ashi per his view who orders Stam of Gemara as Tosafot Chullin (3b) holds in Yevamot for Rabbanan of R. Meir never rely minority—therefore with mumar too need not know expert. Per halachah we hold like Rava in Yevamot—even Rabbanan say rely on minority for presumption stringently—Rambam holds with mumar when not minority of minority must know expert; thereby Tosafot difficulty well settled: Rava should have established here in mumar; he follows his view that we say rely on minority for presumption; therefore with mumar must know expert; should establish here in mumar to gain “and all who slaughtered”—speaks of mumar in case they know expert—as stated; Rava answers per Abaye's words; he himself establishes in mumar as such; thus explicit halachah of Rambam with mumar must know expert; also settles Tosafot beginning Chullin s.v. shema yekalkelu that Rava and “and all who slaughtered” also on mumar—without checking afterward if expert; examine.
