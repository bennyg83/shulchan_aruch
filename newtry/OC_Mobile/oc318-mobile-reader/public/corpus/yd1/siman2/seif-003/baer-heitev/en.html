Taz challenged this: for in Choshen Mishpat siman 34 the Mechaber ruled one is disqualified for testimony only if he transgressed issurim with lashes; why not say so for all transgressions even without lashes, because he is under oath from Sinai and transgressed the oath—end. It appears there is no difficulty—for here he is under oath to slaughter with a fine knife, and he would be believed even without an oath; rather necessarily, since he eats nevelah he is like one suspected for that matter; therefore also with an oath he is not believed, since he is suspected for that matter.
