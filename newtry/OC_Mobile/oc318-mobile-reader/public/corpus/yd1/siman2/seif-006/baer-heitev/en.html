**required.** Where they did not examine the knife neither at beginning nor end—his shechitah is invalid. Shach wrote: this is not comparable to seif 1 regarding stolen chickens that are permitted—for there it is possible to say that although he is suspected of theft, perhaps he gave another to slaughter; Taz wrote: per this reason it is a double doubt—perhaps he gave another; and if you say he slaughtered, perhaps he examined the knife well at first—end.
**Rambam.** Shach explained: “mumar” speaks of a repeat offender disqualified for testimony—meaning he transgressed only once and is not a repeat offender—see Choshen Mishpat siman 34.
