**And according to Rambam one must.** It implies even to forbid—where they did not examine the knife for him neither at beginning nor end his shechitah is forbidden. That which above siman 1 seif 4 regarding lost kids or stolen, etc.—they are permitted—Derishah resolved: there he is not established as a thief but stole this time and is disqualified for testimony; therefore knife examination is not required even per Rambam. From Tur’s wording there—even though suspected of theft, etc.—it implies he is established as thief. Moreover, it must be in majority Jewish thieves, as it teaches “Jewish thieves”; and what Bach wrote here in last pamphlet—he already commanded to erase it himself, and the Gaon Kessef Mishneh resolved: it is different in siman 1 for we do not know for certain he slaughtered, and it is possible that although suspected of theft he gave to another to slaughter.
**And specifically a mumar, etc.** That is, he is a repeat offender and disqualified for testimony—meaning he transgressed only once and is not a repeat offender—see Choshen Mishpat siman 34.
**But if he is disqualified for testimony, etc.** In Torat Chaim it is written: it appears to me from Rava’s wording, etc., that specifically when he is mumar and accustomed to this then knife examination is required, but not for disqualified for testimony, etc.—end of his words. So Kessef Mishneh: the Rambam’s reason is that Rava surely said “Israel examines and gives him” only regarding Israelite mumar and not disqualified for testimony, etc. It appears to me this law depends on what we say below siman 119 seif 7: one suspected in a matter may testify for others but not for himself. Now there is no contradiction between his words in Kessef Mishneh and Beit Yosef—for in Kessef Mishneh he speaks of others and in Beit Yosef of himself. That is, Beit Yosef wrote: since he is suspected for that matter he must examine the knife for him, etc.—implies it depends on the law of suspected for that matter. Rabbenu Yerucham’s wording that his law is like mumar for this is not so precise therefore. And see below siman 126 seif 18.
