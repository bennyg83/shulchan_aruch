**(seif 6) Mumar for one of the rest, etc.** As stated: mumar for one matter is not like, etc.; and specifically for that matter as stated “since he tramples it,” etc.
**(Likkut) Mumar, etc.** And such is the essence—for we rule mumar for one matter is not mumar for another; and as stated “mumar to eat heart,” etc.; and Horayot 11a we challenge on it “but Rava said all agree,” etc.—implies even mumar to provoke for chelev is not for blood, even for appetite—for he says only here one who eats nevelah for appetite, etc.—apparently even for appetite and nevertheless specifically for that matter; and we do not establish initially mumar for chelev, etc.; and even for shechitah it would be kosher for mumar to provoke for another matter without any examination—only because he is like non-Jew. And from what R. Eliezer said “mumar for what,” etc.; and per Rambam what is the challenge “for what”—should have taught for appetite? Plainly per Rava's words one can say to include; and similarly regarding lost kids Tosefta teaches “stolen”; and great strain to say it is known he did not steal only once and is not mumar for theft [and see Rosh chapter 1 Chullin siman 17 and that case of lost, etc., majority of city thieves are Jewish, and so above Shulchan Aruch siman 1 seif 4]—and although one can say with strain all this (Taz and Shach), nevertheless truth guides his way (end quote).
**(Likkut) Mumar, etc.** And proof from what is written Chullin 3b: Abaye does not say like R. Eliezer, etc.; and if so, establish in mumar for other transgressions—for in this certainly not like him; baraita is arranged; and even uncircumcised and even Israelite mumar. And from what Rav Ashi said “mumar for what—to eat nevelot for appetite”—no difficulty: plain mumar for appetite; no need for Rava; no need to say he mentions for inclusion—for wording “mumar for what” does not imply so; and with this I explain Tosafot 50b s.v. mati, etc., and their resolution is uncertain what difference; and further “per his words,” etc.—he does not hold thus; rather necessarily he holds this is regarding Kuti; but certainly that which is written “this regarding Kuti”—all per his first words and per Abaye's Mishnah there is no difficulty and their shechitah kosher b'dieved; and mumar for another transgression permitted l'chatchila without any fix—and first regarding Kuti and challenges to establish also first regarding mumar and for that matter as you heard and as baraita “both” and even uncircumcised, etc., and even, etc. (end quote).
**(Likkut) Mumar, etc.** And so in Tosefta and as stated siman 1 seif 4 “or stolen,” etc.; and also “majority of thieves,” etc.—apparently even if mumar for theft (end quote).
**And according to Rambam one must.** For he holds Rava mentions for that matter to include, etc., and he compelled this from what is inferred in baraita “everyone slaughters,” etc., mumar regarding orlah with mumar for that matter; and says “let us say it supports him,” etc.—implies all need fixing; and so Kuti that is taught there.
**And specifically, etc.** So Rambam; and he learned from “mumar” implies specifically mumar for that; and uncertain on Beit Yosef and gloss who wrote “but,” etc.—for per Rambam there is no distinction, and Rambam did not mention at all mumar for that matter; his rule is mumar for one transgression; and if so he should have distinguished—rather there is no distinction; and with this the gloss's words above seif 2 are justified (Shach seif 11).
**(Likkut) And specifically, etc.** Tosafot 12a s.v. harei, etc. (meaning per Rambam's view there must be one who is not mumar for theft—and see there seif 17) (end quote).
**(Likkut) But if disqualified for testimony, etc.** This has no dawn (end quote).
