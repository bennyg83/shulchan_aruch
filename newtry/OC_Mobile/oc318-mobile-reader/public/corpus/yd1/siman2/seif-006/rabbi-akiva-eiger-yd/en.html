**(seif 5) Desecrating Shabbat in public.** In Teshuvot Beit Yitzchak vol. 3 issues 43 and 47 wrote: desecrators of Shabbat—only regarding land work; uncertain. Here regarding desecrating Shabbat in melacha d'rabbanan—if d'rabbanan like mumar to entire Torah and shechitah forbidden d'rabbanan—Acharonim disputed: Hagahot Beit Yosef holds mumar; Peri Chadash in siman 101 disagrees; per my view in Tosafot Chullin (14a) they inferred even intentional slaughter in public—shechitah kosher—for not established as mumar for one time. Examine Ran's comment Chullin who wrote per Tosafot reason first shechitah slaughtering for idol worship or Shabbat in public not forbidden because not mumar until completion of shechitah; proof from chapter Orlah: first for his table second for idol worship liable for orlah; if so first shechitah mumar shechitah—why do rabbis obligate—he is not slaughtering but mere piercing; rather certainly not called mumar on him except from that shechitah onward. Some reject: Shabbat unlike idol worship—for slaughtering for idol worship he does not worship except at completion of slaughter; but slaughtering on Shabbat he desecrates Shabbat at beginning of shechitah for he wounds—therefore even first shechitah mumar. This does not satisfy me: on Shabbat too not liable until completion of shechitah—for beforehand only spoilage in wound and we hold exempt; they also reject: perhaps shechitah of mumar although forbidden in eating, nevertheless not like idol worship shechitah to impart impurity in carrying—for his offerings remain holy. If so even if became mumar in first shechitah, nevertheless rabbis obligate for orlah because slaughter is called slaughter since purifies from nevelah—end Ran. Although Ran rejected first explanation with two hands—nevertheless we find here Kodesh Kohanim so Rambam in Mishnah commentary on this mishnah slaughtering on Shabbat wrote explicitly: intentional shechitah forbidden because at beginning of shechitah he becomes mumar—examine. **We find** three views: Rambam—slaughtering in public on Shabbat is mumar shechitah, becomes mumar at beginning; Ran—shechitah kosher, mumar only at completion—for beforehand only spoilage; third view last explanation in Ran we call “there are those who reject”: in first shechitah whether idol worship or Shabbat is mumar shechitah forbidden in eating and does not impart impurity in carrying. In this there is stringency side: first Shabbat shechitah forbidden in eating; leniency side: even second shechitah not impure in carrying. We also learn from Ran: where imparts impurity from nevelah—all agree not shechitah but like piercing. Where forbidden in eating and not impure—full shechitah that R. Shimon and rabbis disagree. Apparently clear proof for Peri Chadash that for d'rabbanan Shabbat desecration one is not called mumar—from what Ran raised first Shabbat shechitah in public permitted—not mumar until completion—for beforehand only spoilage; nevertheless it is d'rabbanan issur—clear Ran holds for melacha d'rabbanan not mumar. From Rambam Mishnah commentary no contrary proof—one can say he holds beginning of shechitah is d'oraisa melacha of spoilage to fix; nevertheless I find clear proof Rambam too holds like Ran it is spoilage. Therefore becoming mumar at beginning of shechitah is for melacha d'rabbanan—mumar d'rabbanan as Hagahot Beit Yosef—Peri Chadash view. Per this one can resolve Tosafot Ketubot difficulty why mishnah lists butcher on Shabbat if we could establish slaughtering for idol worship at completion he worships—also before decree could establish lost kids case; and from what we conclude regarding scapegoat majority take bird forbidden and Elijah ravens brought—no difficulty for Rambam if not hold thus. Per Rambam proof from gid hanasheh: they slaughtered slaughterhouse for them and so took also gid—examine Tosafot s.v. kama deamarin they were careful then in shechitah although not commanded—difficulty: they knew Yosef and household Israelite—thought gentile; what helps man of house slaughtered—gentile slaughter nevelah not fit to slaughter; proven they knew Yosef household not idol worshippers and d'oraisa their shechitah kosher. See Maharsha who resolved per this difficulty Pharaoh gave them slaughterhouse they slaughter—this is not language of pay bearing. For Tosafot and Rosh second version not specifically slaughterhouse but piercing—examine. [Long sugya on Shabbat slaughter, kleb baal mam, gonif kis, Rambam Hilchos Geneivah, Derishah, Tosafot Bava Kamma—condensed conclusion:] Per my view decisively Rambam like Ran: at beginning of shechitah only spoilage—not liable for death until completion; if Rambam commentary says forbidden at beginning because mumar—mumar from d'rabbanan melacha as Hagahot Beit Yosef—Peri Chadash. Can resolve Tosafot Ketubot: even though teaches at completion he worships—could establish like scapegoat; from majority bird take forbidden. Per first Rishonim only that shechitah permitted because completed before becoming mumar; from Shas challenge we learn mumar imparts nevelah impurity and like piercing—challenge works even per Rambam mumar shechitah; nevertheless challenges well since imparts impurity and like piercing—even R. Meir would not be shechitah; challenge in private and slaughtering proper; if first shechitah mumar shechitah forbidden in eating and not impure—wonder remains established in public butcher and per R. Meir is shechitah like all slaughter R. Shimon disputes—great wonder; apparently proves Rambam view. Must understand Ran: at beginning he is spoilage “to fix”—per Tosafot Shabbat (106a) s.v. chutz mechoveil: for R. Yehudah even complete spoilage in wound exempt; only choveil ve'tzarich lechalko liable; R. Yehudah exempts here too. Tosafot distinguish R. Yehudah between choveil vetzarich lechalko and all who slaughter in two ways—or in choveil repair not immediate at time of spoilage unlike slaughter where repair immediate at spoilage to remove from nevelah. If we judge beginning of shechitah to obligate—depends on two answers; per first answer liable since normal to begin shechitah for its end; per second answer need repair at time of spoilage—at beginning of shechitah repair not yet come until completion—like choveil vetzarich lechalko exempt; therefore Ran's reason as Tosafot second answer. If like Ran—even if public and R. Meir obligates—not mumar shechitah since not liable until completion—for beforehand spoilage; rabbis exempt—we establish man chachamim R. Shimon. Still challenges per R. Shimon obligates at beginning and becomes mumar before completion—mumar shechitah; proves Rambam view even for us mumar shechitah; since R. Meir obligates—must be private; challenges well. If only d'rabbanan mumar from spoilage melacha—sugya Bava Kamma still hard: for dalet and hei we follow d'oraisa shechitah in our challenge—why chachamim exempt per those who say Shabbat act d'rabbanan; still challenges established in public butcher and per R. Meir only d'rabbanan mumar and chachamim R. Shimon d'oraisa mumar. Therefore for us too d'oraisa mumar; since R. Meir obligates—private; challenges well. If like Tosafot first answer Shabbat choveil vetzarich lechalko exempt because not normal, etc.—would not be d'oraisa mumar at beginning since repair not immediate; as stated. **Nevertheless** no challenge from this to Tosafot second answer Ran—it is clear entire Ran dispute whether mumar at beginning of shechitah only per reason first shechitah not mumar shechitah because mumar only at completion and completion in fitness—therefore Shabbat slaughter if mumar at beginning completion by mumar and like different shechitah. But per Tosafot Chullin first time not mumar; per their proof from two holding knife—clear what he slaughters afterward also not forbidden; therefore reason not called mumar except habitual repeat offender; certainly Shabbat slaughter in public even if from beginning full melacha—not habitual for this one act—not called repeat offender for this—clear as sun in my humble view. If so fine to say choveil vetzarich lechalko exempt since repair not immediate; truly also at beginning of shechitah spoilage—nevertheless Shas would not establish in public butcher—for even R. Shimon who liable in choveil vetzarich lechalko would be liable at beginning and become mumar before completion—still not called mumar shechitah since even what he slaughters after permitted. Apparently proof for Tosafot: Tosafot wondered Ketubot wording why generality butcher on Shabbat—R. Meir obligates in proper shechitah a fortiori; I explain baraita states generality obligates in every case even butcher intentional public—if taught only idol worship slaughter one might say only there since at completion he worships; not so Shabbat one might think since mumar at beginning disqualifies shechitah; teaches nevertheless liable dalet and hei; with first time not habitual not mumar; all per Tosafot Chullin. Per Rishonim only that shechitah permitted because completed before mumar—from challenge we learn mumar imparts nevelah like piercing; from not establishing public butcher—all hold mumar; as stated. Since R. Meir obligates—private; challenges well. **Still** resolve Tosafot difficulty another way: Sanhedrin (74a) burglar sugya—halachah drown in river; see Ramban Milchamot: only when broke while still in tunnel; if exited then broke—liable for not kleb baal mam at break time. Same thief: slid hand, made pocket, received, threw immediately—liable; if threw to another place—liable for at throw time not kleb baal mam. See Bach Choshen Mishpat siman 351 Rambam agrees; deduced from Rambam Hilchos Geneivah chapter 3 thief stole purse on Shabbat dragging until from reshus hayachid to reshus harabim and lost there—exempt; emphasized “and lost there” immediately not afterward. So Tosafot Bava Kamma (70b) s.v. keman. Derishah siman 351: Tur regarding burglar in name of R. Yeshaya requires specifically broke while in tunnel not wrote first regarding thief sliding hand—implies distinguishes: only burglar when exits from tunnel presumption death liability ceases forbidden to kill, no further kleb baal mam; unlike thief purse death liability for Shabbat still applies not ceased—therefore even drowning afterward exempt. Examine Derishah siman 357 thief sold on Shabbat if sale in issur etc. exempt both issurim together; same butcher on Shabbat for idol worship exempt; wrote Derishah Rabbenu did not write here too death and payments together because not together—in truth not together: death liable immediately at first siman of shechitah; nevertheless exempt payments since death liable on that act initially not monetary again on same act (as siman 351 and his words on thief purse). If from Tur words on Shabbat butcher must conclude even though liable death at beginning slaughter not liable payments—Ramban view cannot settle end Bava Kamma sugya. Wanted to reject Derishah intent for Ramban: Ramban holds death only at completion slaughter—for beforehand spoilage per Ran; only from Tur wording not writing butcher on Shabbat death and payments together built foundation Tur holds not spoilage not together—still exempt payments. Strained; from plain language must conclude liable at beginning slaughter; also insufficient for Ramban end sugya challenge. Per R. Shimon liable choveil vetzarich lechalko—all the more liable at beginning shechitah—proves Rambam; since R. Meir obligates—private; challenges well. If only d'rabbanan mumar from spoilage—Bava Kamma still hard for dalet hei we follow d'oraisa shechitah; those saying Shabbat d'rabbanan—still public butcher R. Meir only d'rabbanan mumar R. Shimon d'oraisa. Therefore us too d'oraisa mumar; R. Meir obligates—private. If Tosafot first answer choveil vetzarich lechalko exempt—not normal—would not be d'oraisa mumar at beginning. **Nevertheless** no challenge to Tosafot second answer Ran—dispute only whether mumar at beginning per first shechitah reason; Tosafot Chullin first time not mumar; two holding knife proof; not habitual; Shabbat public even full melacha—not habitual one act—not repeat offender—clear. Choveil vetzarich lechalko exempt repair not immediate; beginning spoilage—Shas would not establish public butcher—even R. Shimon liable at beginning become mumar—still not mumar shechitah since afterward permitted—proof Tosafot Ketubot. Per Rishonim only permitted because before mumar; challenge mumar nevelah like piercing; not public—all mumar. R. Meir obligates—private. **Another way** resolve Tosafot Ketubot: burglar sugya etc. [Derishah/Tur/Ramban thief distinctions repeated briefly]. Per Maharshal one witness contradicting with kleb baal mam animal forbidden—denial since knows not permitted on word with one witness—believed regarding money. **End:** three views summarized—Ran: d'rabbanan Shabbat not mumar; Rambam: mumar d'rabbanan; Peri Chadash via Ran's first-shechitah reasoning.
