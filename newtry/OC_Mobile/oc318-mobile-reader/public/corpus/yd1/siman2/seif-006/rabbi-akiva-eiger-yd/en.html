**(seif 6) And per Rambam requires.** I wonder: if so how say Abaye not like Rav Ashi—does not hold Rava's view; nevertheless holds mumar for one matter if not for that matter permitted at least via knife examination—for baraita explicitly teaches even mumar; if say like Rambam with mumar for one matter also knife examination—still establish mishnah “everyone slaughters” even mumar for one matter in case examining knife, etc.—great difficulty—examine.
**(Shach seif 23) But** it appears also Rashba's view and Taz inferred: when standing over, must be expert; going in and out must know expert—for perhaps not with Israelite's knowledge and he does not know forbidden for Israelite so will not fear. Therefore difficulty for Rava establishing Kuti going in and out and we conclude “and all who slaughtered”—why not establish advantageously “everyone” even Kuti going in and out in case they know expert; otherwise may not slaughter; if slaughtered they test; not before us; others see—kosher—examine.
**(there seif 24) Knife examination not required.** Ran in his novellae wrote one can say actually Kuti requires knife examination—thereby we expand settling Shach seif 2 difficulty why not say everyone like Abaye and Rava did not say—still difficult: establish Kuti standing over or going in and out in case examining knife and giving; if not examined—they check knife afterward.
