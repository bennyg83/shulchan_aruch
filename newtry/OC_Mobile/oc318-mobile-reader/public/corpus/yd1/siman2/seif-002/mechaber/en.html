A mumar who eats nevelot for appetite—a Jew examines the knife and gives it to him, and it is permitted to eat from his shechitah even if he slaughters between himself and himself—provided they know about him that he knows the laws of shechitah. And if he did not first examine the knife for him, it is forbidden to eat from his shechitah until he examines it at the end; and one does not give him l'chatchila to slaughter even if a fit person stands over him without the fit one first examining the knife for him, relying that he will examine it at the end. {Rama: One who slaughtered and brought out a treifah once from beneath his hand—if he was not established as such, it is permitted afterward to eat from his shechitah; nevertheless in this matter they judge according to what the judge's eyes see in the slaughterer whether he has already failed and there are indications to the matter—they remove him (Maharik shoresh 33).}
