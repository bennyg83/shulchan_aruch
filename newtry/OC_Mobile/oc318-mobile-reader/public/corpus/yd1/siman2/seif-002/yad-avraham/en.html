**I saw** in responsa Panim Me'irot vol. 2 siman 41: one who ate meat and afterward it became known it was treifah—he need not bring offering. He brought proof from Yoreh Deah siman 185 regarding sun not at her time she need not offering, considered ones. One can reject: there reason as Noda BiYehudah wrote in Mateh Efrayim Choshen Mishpat siman 96: woman need not examine herself so his heart not pounding and separate from marital relations; but here he should have waited. Also Choshen Mishpat siman 19 Maharik: mishnah Pesachim chapter 6 they slaughtered and found treifah in hiding—exempt; no proof—for there reason error in mitzvah matter and follows R. Meir who exempts even though did not do mitzvah, as Tosafot there (73); even Rambam there exempts from ones because could not recognize—unlike here if he had checked could have known before eating, liable for not checking and not waiting. Only proof from Tosafot Beitzah (25) is correct proof: implies only what he ate before skinning and cutting is considered unintentional, not what he ate after. Must distinguish from Tosafot Bava Kamma (26) regarding chelev: even if never recognized it was chelev he is liable—must say there it was recognizable to someone; not so here. And not recognizable to anyone.
