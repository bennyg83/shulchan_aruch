**(seif 2) And permitted to eat from his shechitah.** See Rashba and Ran who wrote we are not concerned perhaps majority simanim not severed—for majority who want to slaughter kosher slaughter majority simanim and immediately after shechitah check; if see majority not severed return and finish shechitah immediately—not called great effort—examine. We should judge for our times that we forbid delay—all the more concern perhaps majority not severed first and needed to check simanim and found majority not severed—although afterward finished majority, nevertheless there was some delay; must say specifically if we know he delayed we forbid, fear they cannot be expert in measure of delay; but since in truth per majority majority simanim severed immediately—only without checking simanim forbidden d'rabbanan as Tosafot (9a)—therefore not so concerned for delay. Still difficult: found meat where majority of city Jewish—should forbid concern slaughterer checked simanim and majority not severed; since we forbid any delay and slaughterer knows no remedy by finishing—he withdrew hand and did not return to finish majority since no benefit; double doubt: perhaps did not give another to slaughter; if he slaughtered, perhaps examined knife well first. Only one doubt Rambam stringent. Plainly with mumar one can say if sees majority not severed returns and finishes immediately—even though wants to eat biblically forbidden, does all possible in permitted way and finishes so as not to eat d'oraisa issur and at least permitted by law; no doubt we fear delay—so above. But found meat—great concern slaughterer due to delay forbidden and unwilling to eat does not finish; possible our times truly not permitted except found slaughtered and checks simanim see majority severed—then for law even where no concern meat hidden from eye like lost kids with presumption—even so requires found slaughtered seeing majority simanim severed. **I was asked** why Rif omitted Rava's statement mumar eating nevelot for appetite examines knife and gives him—we find no disputant except where Abaye said not like Rav Ashi does not hold that Rava; halachah like Rava; especially as challenge to Rav Anan resolved, support remains for Rava from baraita even Israelite mumar—we have no other establishment except to include mumar for that matter; all Rishonim wrote this proof. Wonder increases on Rif for omitting this Rava. I answered per Rashba and Ran above: even if did not check simanim forbidden—say presumably mumar too checks simanim; if finds majority not severed returns and finishes immediately—examine sugya Chullin (9a) what if not checked—R. Eliezer b. Antignonus in name of R. Eliezer b. R. Yannai treifah forbidden to eat; mishnah taught nevelah and imparts impurity in carrying, etc.; Tosafot there: majority times slaughters well and permitted d'oraisa—therefore not stringent to make nevelah—end; so Rashba in comment: majority times majority of simanim severed; but issur depending on act majority not depending on act—in such case we do not follow majority as Bechorot—therefore rabbis establish on original issur presumption; but impurity presumption not from outset—end. Tosafot and Rashba hold d'oraisa even without checking permitted; therefore one can say we rely on above reasoning mumar checks immediately and finishes before measure of delay—in truth if simanim checking d'oraisa we would not rely, for some effort to check simanim and finish immediately; since d'oraisa permitted because majority times majority severed—for d'rabbanan we rely. **Nevertheless** although Tosafot and Rashba hold only d'rabbanan, we find Rambam wrote doubtful nevelah d'oraisa—one can say reason: in truth it is majority but majority depending on act which is not majority; although from Rashba's language this “majority depending on act” is only d'rabbanan—Rambam may not hold so. What Tosafot and Rashba proved from R. Eliezer not impure in carrying—Rambam may hold they truly disagree R. Eliezer and baraita whether d'oraisa or d'rabbanan; therefore one can say Rif holds no support for Rava from baraita teaching “even mumar”—one can say even though baraita plain mumar for that matter, baraita holds like R. Eliezer simanim checking only d'rabbanan—therefore rely mumar checked simanim and finished immediately; but per what we hold like baraita impure in carrying—simanim checking d'oraisa—we do not rely on mumar's checking. Add spice: Rif seems challenged—R. Eliezer b. R. Yannai is amora why not challenge from baraita impure in carrying; in Teshuvot Shor (siman 25 letter 23) wrote there was power for one view to disagree on baraita; strained—law of mumar depends on this; R. Eliezer b. R. Yannai tanna supporting baraita even Israelite mumar—since we rely on mumar proves simanim checking d'rabbanan; per this one can say Rif rejected Rava's statement: Rava follows his view in Bechorot that even majority depending on act is majority—therefore simanim checking only d'rabbanan—therefore rely on mumar; but for us who hold like Ravina in Bechorot—majority depending on act not majority—simanim checking d'oraisa required—we do not rely on mumar—examine. Although still difference in Rava's matter whether immediately after mumar shechitah we checked simanim and majority severed—permitted, not concerned for delay and “does not leave permitted”—nevertheless l'chatchila cannot give him to slaughter lest he become drunk and not check simanim afterward and cannot bring Rava's law in full form—no challenge if Rif omitted Rava without extracting practical consequence.
