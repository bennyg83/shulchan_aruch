We should judge for our times that we forbid delay—all the more concern perhaps majority not severed first and needed to check simanim and found majority not severed—although afterward finished majority, nevertheless there was some delay; must say specifically if we know he delayed we forbid, fear they cannot be expert in measure of delay; but since in truth per majority majority simanim severed immediately—only without checking simanim forbidden d'rabbanan as Tosafot (9a)—therefore not so concerned for delay. Still difficult: found meat where majority of city Jewish—should forbid concern slaughterer checked simanim and majority not severed; since we forbid any delay and slaughterer knows no remedy by finishing—he withdrew hand and did not return to finish majority since no benefit; double doubt: perhaps did not give another to slaughter; if he slaughtered, perhaps examined knife well first. Only one doubt Rambam stringent. **(in Or Zarua) Provided they know about him, etc.** Famous difficulty: Rav Ashi establishes in mumar; per us “and all who slaughtered” challenges why not establish in mumar like Ravina's establishment “everyone slaughters” examining knife and giving—in case they know he is expert; otherwise may not slaughter; if slaughtered they test him; not before us; others see—kosher. Per my view to settle: on contrary from this sugya proven with mumar must know he is expert. In sugya Tosafot s.v. mati lo amar keshe'amtah challenged presumption: Rava said below one teaches Kuti one mumar; their answer not understood—per my view Rosh challenged what helps majority found—say rely on minority not experts for animal issur presumption; answered it is minority of minority; therefore even if majority experts—not minority of minority; we say rely on minority for presumption. We find law rely on minority for presumption d'rabbanan from Yevamot (119): Rava said first part presumption for levirate marriage, etc.; Rishonim explained Rava said first and last parts all agree even per R. Meir and Rabbanan—in first part even Rabbanan “do not marry” for we say rely on minority for presumption—examine well. If so for version Stam of Shas there—plain mishnah R. Meir—opposite: per Rabbanan of R. Meir never rely minority for presumption; thereby Rav Ashi per his view who orders Stam of Gemara as Tosafot Chullin (3b) holds in Yevamot for Rabbanan of R. Meir never rely minority—therefore with mumar too need not know expert. Per halachah we hold like Rava in Yevamot—even Rabbanan say rely on minority for presumption stringently—Rambam holds with mumar when not minority of minority must know expert; thereby Tosafot difficulty well settled: Rava should have established here in mumar; he follows his view that we say rely on minority for presumption; therefore with mumar must know expert; should establish here in mumar to gain “and all who slaughtered”—speaks of mumar in case they know expert—as stated; Rava answers per Abaye's words; he himself establishes in mumar as such; thus explicit halachah of Rambam with mumar must know expert; also settles Tosafot beginning Chullin s.v. shema yekalkelu that Rava and “and all who slaughtered” also on mumar—without checking afterward if expert; examine.
**(in gloss) One who slaughtered and brought out treifah, etc.** Per my view if while animal not yet removed still on presumption of kashrut witnesses came he slaughtered improperly and he denies he slaughtered properly—in this he is not disqualified. One can say he does not want to feed treifot; since he knows they do not permit on his word because witnesses contradict him—therefore he denied them to maintain himself as artisan who did not spoil his shechitah; if witnesses had not come he would not truthfully say animal kosher—especially if first one witness said he slaughtered improperly and he denied slaughtered properly; afterward another witness joins first testifying not slaughtered properly. Per Maharshal with one witness contradicting animal forbidden—one can say his denial since he knows they do not permit on his word with one witness contradicting—therefore denied so he need not pay if hired—for regarding money he is believed against witness.
