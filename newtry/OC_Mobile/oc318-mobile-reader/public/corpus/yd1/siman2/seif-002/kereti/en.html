For appetite when permitted and forbidden before him—leaves forbidden, takes permitted; to trouble after if not available—like issur does not trouble.
Examines knife: public effort; when has knives does not leave unblemished and slaughters blemished—even thousand blemished and one fine, slaughters with fine.
Between himself and himself: lest delayed or pressed—not concerned for lifnei iver.
By himself: see Peleti I wrote if no benefit from slaughtering for others whether permitted—appears forbidden, no distinction.
Until examines at end: estimation he had examined knife at shechitah—engaged in shechitah; else what helps examination—perhaps afterward knife came to him—Rashba, Peri Chadash.
At end: see Peleti proof for Rashba—even mumar need not know expert; joins with others.
May not give: Kessef Mishneh principle—if forgot treifah forbidden l'chatchila to rely lest drunk and eat treifah; here if drunk cannot rely l'chatchila; Ran: onlookers fear he slaughters without examination thinking kosher—Bach stringent: examine knife only before public.
Examine at end: if not examined at all—all require before; not rely after except here mumar said kosher—we disbelieve; cannot rely on others' examination—teaches not.
If not established: Shach challenged; law with him—cannot explain actions perhaps error unintentional—kosher; if established sometimes—not attributed to error.
