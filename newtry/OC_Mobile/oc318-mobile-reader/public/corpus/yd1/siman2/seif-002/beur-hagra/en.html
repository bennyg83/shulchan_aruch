**(seif 2) (Likkut) Mumar, etc.** Like Rava—and although Abaye disagrees, halachah is like Rava; also Rav Ashi holds like him; and a baraita supports him; and also the baraita “everyone slaughters”—therefore Rav Anan was established. Terumat HaDeshen (end quote).
**Even if he slaughters, etc.** Tosafot there s.v. bodek—from what is said this leniency regarding Kuti and not regarding mumar—examine; and in the Gemara there “and all of them who slaughtered,” etc., except he did not examine, etc.; and see Rashi s.v. leyivdekeih and s.v. shochet l'chatchila, etc.
**Provided they know, etc.—for in him there is no need for “majority found,” etc.** Ran and Mordechai and Terumat HaDeshen and in Darchei Moshe.
**(Likkut) Provided they know, etc.** For that which we say “majority found,” etc.—meaning that if not so he would not slaughter but give to another; unlike this one who eats nevelot for appetite (end quote).
**And one does not give, etc., relying, etc.** Tosafot there in Or Zarua and in the Gemara there: but if he did not examine he may not slaughter; and if, etc.—specifically b'dieved.
**Even if a fit person stands over him, etc.** Tosafot in Or Zarua and Gemara there; and if the knife is not available, etc.; and the Mechaber combined together “and there is no… even if…,” but Tur wrote only the latter alone as Tosafot's words—except the Mechaber learned from Tosafot and Rosh there who wrote the reason: since b'dieved it is forbidden, one does not give, etc.; and the same in such a case where fit stands over him, since b'dieved forbidden as stated; and if the knife is not available, etc.
**(Likkut) One who slaughtered, etc.** From what is written siman 18:1 “this butcher,” etc.; and some explain here, etc.; and it implies they remove him only because he did not show a sage, as when found fine (end quote).
