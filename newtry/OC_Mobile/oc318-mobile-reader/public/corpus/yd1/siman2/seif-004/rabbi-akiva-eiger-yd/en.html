**(seif 4) Believed to say.** See Taz and Shach require saying so-and-so expert—and clear with plain “so-and-so slaughtered for me” suffices since we believe him that so-and-so slaughtered—we then judge that so-and-so among majority found are experts. Know per my view this permitted only with animal slaughtered in his hand saying so-and-so expert slaughtered for me—in this fear applies and not “perhaps another animal that expert slaughtered for him but not this one”—still fear perhaps they will ask so-and-so and he will recognize by presumption it is not what he slaughtered. But with piece of meat in hand saying this is from animal so-and-so expert slaughtered for me—does not help, perhaps expert truly slaughtered some animal for him but this meat not from it; no fear since cannot be clarified.
