**(siman 2 seif 4) Believed to say an expert slaughtered for me.** Tur and Mechaber and gloss did not mention saying “expert so-and-so” because even without this he is believed—for he is mumar for appetite who does not leave permitted and eats forbidden, and certainly bought from expert. (See Beit Yosef and responsa Rashba who brought proof we say he does not leave permitted even where there is effort of going, from Chamtzan of transgressors permitted immediately because they exchange; he wrote all the more believed to say regarding another's that it is kosher.) But without this, even “expert so-and-so” does not help for Torah issur as Beit Yosef end siman 119 and gloss there seif 19. No contradiction to what is written here: there speaks of suspected one not concerned with that mitzvah at all even without appetite, as gloss there in that siman seif 2—examine; but here speaks of one who transgresses for appetite—not called suspected and does not leave permitted, etc. All this appears plain view of Taz, Beit Yosef, and gloss here and siman 119—they do not contradict each other. Taz and Shach conclude one must also say “expert so-and-so” as there in that siman—for there is also reason of fear. This does not contradict what gloss there wrote: for Torah issur it does not help—meaning regarding suspected one; not so here. However in Rashba responsum siman 287 it implies one need not “expert so-and-so”—not from fear he permits, but whoever is not troubled like knife examination is believed; see Tiferet leMoshe there: specifically for d'rabbanan suspected one is not believed.
