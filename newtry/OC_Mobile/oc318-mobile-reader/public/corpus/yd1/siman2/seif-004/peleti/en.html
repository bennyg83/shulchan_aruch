And says he slaughtered with fine one: one should consider Rashba who wrote this language—must understand why his statement needed: whatever we can clarify we clarify to ask whether with this he slaughtered. Also afterward says from expert slaughtered—Bedek HaBayit challenged why statement needed, is his statement believed. Answer in Mishmeret HaBayit: must say “expert so-and-so” lest fear lie if asked. So Terumat HaDeshen; proof from demai agent must say from so-and-so expert. Plain expert does not help; “does not leave permitted” does not help here—expert not so common—end his words. One might ask: also with non-Jew we find siman 86 believed if says from so-and-so's poultry. Still hard on Tur who copied Rashba “believed to say Israelite expert and fit slaughtered for me”—implies no name; how much more Shulchan Aruch “expert slaughtered for me”—forbidden even per Rashba source. On Rashba “and says I slaughtered with fine one”—Bedek HaBayit wondered statement accomplishes nothing; essence depends whether known fine knife known to him—else suspect at shechitah time did not know. Rashba answered: not full credibility; we say found here, therefore rely partially—for believed to say I checked simanim after shechitah properly, most slaughterers slaughter well as required; here too may rely since examined knife before us; not after day or two but for its hour—even after hour if fine knife in hand rely on his shechitah—end briefly. Also puzzling: surely we permit not from credibility but majority as Rav wrote; if so even without statement believed since examined knife before us. Stumbling block in Tur and Shulchan Aruch requiring plain “expert slaughtered for me.” No challenge on them since did not see Rashba's Terumat HaDesden—we merited to see; therefore no permission except “so-and-so expert” mentioning name—novelty; see more siman 119 seif 19.
Experts: if butchers in city majority Jewish butchers—certainly need not his statement lest only non-Jew butcher; Israelite butcher permitted—presumably from butchers as Chamtzan of transgressors who exchange. If no majority but there are butchers—also permitted. But poultry—not common to buy from butchers—must say “from so-and-so butcher I took.” Simple; last words somewhat confused.
