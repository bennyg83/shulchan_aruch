**believed.** Shach wrote: when we know the fine knife was with him when he slaughtered—for he does not leave the permitted and eat the forbidden; but if after shechitah these knives were found with him he is not believed if he had leisure to go bring another knife—and see below seif 2. Also what the Mechaber wrote, “and he says he slaughtered with the fine one,” is not precise; rather we believe him in general even if he says nothing, only because wherever one can stand on the matter and clarify more, we clarify—so Perishah. And per what is written below seif 14 regarding expert—that it must be “expert so-and-so”—if so he must say explicitly.
**for me.** Shach wrote: specifically he must say “expert so-and-so”; there is no distinction between animal and bird; and in any case if there is an expert in the city he is believed to say an expert slaughtered for me—not like Bach. Regarding animal meat: if there are Jewish butchers he is believed to say he took from the slaughtered ones—and specifically when slaughtered meat was commonly found that day so cheap like nevelah meat.
