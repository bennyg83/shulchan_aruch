**They remove him.** See responsum Chavot Yair in omissions to page 21: he was uncertain regarding one removed from being shochet u'bodek through one witness with some indications, and nevertheless he knows himself he lied in testimony—whether permitted to slaughter at home or when coming to another place that does not know what was done to him; and even if true he failed and knows it was unintentional; and even if removed per two witnesses—some say when there are no onlookers who know it is permitted. See there; see what is written below siman 27 seif 7 in name of Bechor Shor on this matter. See responsum Rama siman 2 brought in Taz below siman 185 seif 2—examine.
