**Mumar to provoke, etc.** Maharshal wrote chapter 1 Chullin siman 6 that even once he is like a non-Jew—see seif 20. It implies the Mechaber’s view that he is literally like a non-Jew and his shechitah is nevelah by law; so Ran and other Acharonim and Perishah 96; and so appears from Tosafot and Rosh who wrote: from “And you shall slaughter and eat” we derive what you slaughter you eat—meaning one who is fit to slaughter, excluding a non-Jew and one who eats nevelot to provoke—end of their words. It implies a mumar to provoke is excluded from the verse like a non-Jew—not like Bach who wrote his shechitah is forbidden only d'rabbanan and is not nevelah, etc., without proof. And see below siman 158 seif 2: mumar to provoke is an apikoros; regarding apikoros Rambam chapter 4 wrote he is like a non-Jew and his shechitah is nevelah—see Bach end siman 7.
**Mumar to idol worship, etc.** Know there is dispute among poskim here; the essence is as those who say: mumar to idol worship even in private, even only once, is mumar to the entire Torah and his law is like a non-Jew. So too one who desecrated Shabbat once in public. However in that shechitah he does not become mumar—for example slaughtering for idol worship, or slaughtering on Shabbat—this shechitah is not forbidden because of mumar shechitah but because of slaughtering for idol worship as below siman 4, and likewise because of slaughtering on Shabbat in siman 11. So appears Maharshal chapter 1 Chullin siman 7.
