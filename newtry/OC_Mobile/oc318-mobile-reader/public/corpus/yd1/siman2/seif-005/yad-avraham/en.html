**(seif 5 in gloss) And one who is not concerned with shechitah, etc.**—his law is like mumar to provoke—end. It appears this is specifically when already established he transgressed three times; and even so it is a question for Rav Ashi in Temurah (27) whether we say one does not leave permitted and does forbidden once established three times in issur; but if not established three times in issur, plainly there he said he does not leave, etc., if he did not transgress to provoke even though not for appetite as that case in Temurah—and if so one may permit his shechitah. And although in Rif manuscript it is written implying from the sender that wherever he leaves permitted, etc., he is mumar to provoke—as Peri Megadim chapter 2 Avodah Zarah. One can say there regarding reviving him specifically; and even from first view since he renounced, one cannot revive him. Nevertheless he still has more credibility than mumar to provoke and will not leave permitted in future. It appears Ran too who wrote eating nevelot not for appetite does not help without standing over—means after three times specifically, but he holds even three times helps with standing over. In Rif manuscript he challenged from sender; per what is stated one can say matter of reviving is not credibility.
