A mumar to provoke—even for one matter—or who is a mumar to idol worship or to desecrate Shabbat in public, or who is a mumar to the entire Torah except for these two—his law is like a non-Jew (R. Yerucham). {Rama: And one who is not concerned with shechitah and eats nevelot without appetite—even though he does not do it to provoke—his law is like a mumar to provoke (thus the Beit Yosef inferred from the words of the Rosh).}
