**in public.** Hagahot Beit Yosef in name of Hagahot Beit Yosef: in public means before ten Israelites. He also wrote that even transgressing d'rabbanan issur—Peri Chadash disagrees; see Mishnat Chachamim Hilchot Avodah Zarah 57b who also disagrees, because we hold one suspected on a light matter is not suspected on a severe matter; if so all the more he does not become mumar from light to severe. If so regarding Shabbat in chapter Eruvin—which speaks regarding eruv techumin that carrying without eruv is d'rabbanan—it is fine that we say if he is established to desecrate Shabbat even in d'rabbanan issurim he is like non-Jew regarding that eruv does not help; but to be mumar for all of it he is not until he desecrates d'oraisa issur—examine. He also brought proof that even if he transgressed Shabbat prohibitions and on this is not mumar for all until he transgresses capital court death—examine.
