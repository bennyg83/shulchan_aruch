And also they examined knives: Shach wondered as Tosafot wrote explicitly for Kuti no knife needed lest perhaps Israel examine knife—not so for suspected who does not fear saying “I am Israelite”—so Chochmat Shlomo challenged. Appears Mechaber and certainly Tosafot followed Rabbeinu Tam below siman 118: more to be stringent with two seals when Israelite suspected from non-Jew who does not fear, as Tosafot here. But per Mechaber who does not hold Rabbeinu Tam's view and non-Jew is tamar—also this reasoning here that suspected does not fear and Kuti fears—does not exist; like suspected with no distinction. Therefore Mechaber holds there Kuti has three fears: or takes knife before intending to slaughter alone to examine then cannot evade damaged joint; or takes from him after slaughtered and examines; third gives him olive-size meat to eat and he will not eat—for he does not eat issur. Unlike mumar for appetite who does not fear for this—for he eats nevelot. On these fear matters Mechaber did not rely for Sadducees suspected of eating treifot—as authors wrote Mechaber did not want to permit—examine. Also one can say disputants of Rabbeinu Tam explain Gemara challenging Rava why not say like you heard; Tosafot challenged: teaches one for Kuti one for mumar; they explain Gemara difficulty: Abaye does not hold knife examination at all—holds majority knives presumed examined; Rava challenges as Abaye and did not mention knife examination for Kuti—implies not concerned for damaged knife; why not say like you heard also for mumar no knife examination needed; answers per Abaye's words; per his view knife examination required; therefore Mechaber correct—examine.
