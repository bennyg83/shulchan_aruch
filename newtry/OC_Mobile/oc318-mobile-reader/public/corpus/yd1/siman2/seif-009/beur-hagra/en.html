**(seif 9) Sadducee, etc.** For their law now is like Kutim before they decreed on them, as written in several places that Sadducee did not heed Sages' exposition; and so Kutim; and see Rashi chapter 1 Avodah Zarah 21b s.v. Kuti; and so Rambam chapter 6 Hilchos Avadim halachah 6; and Shulchan Aruch below siman 267 seif 47.
**And others stand over them.** Rambam—and meaning: but cutting olive-size meat does not help, for it is a dispute and we rule like first Tanna there 4a—Terumat HaDeshen and Tur. But Rambam in Hilchos Avadim there and Shulchan Aruch siman 267 there ruled like R. Eliezer; and per first view Gitin 10a; and although one can say it also comes like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel and he was not established even in Gitin; and what is written there “always R. Eliezer” for benefit of the matter he mentions—even per R. Eliezer it comes—nevertheless like first Tanna impossible to establish; and see Rashi and Tosafot there.
**(Likkut) And others stand over them.** Not precise—for even going in and out suffices, for we rule like Rava; and so siman 69 seif 10 (end quote).
**And also they examined knives.** His own opinion—and all Acharonim already wondered on him; and Tosafot there s.v. bodek; and so all poskim that it is not required except standing over (Ritva explains Kuti requires also knife examination—and he is alone).
**Informer (moser), etc.** As stated Gitin 45b: moser, etc., “and you shall bind,” etc.; and the same here for R. Yitzchak's reason that it says “and you shall slaughter.”
**And some permit.** For it is not comparable to there—for here even woman and slave and all disqualified are believed, unlike there as stated “and woman and slave,” etc.; and uncertain—for there they are not commanded on tefillin, as Rashi there s.v. eved; rather per Rambam one may permit, for he derives from verse “and you shall call,” etc.; and see siman 119 seif 10.
