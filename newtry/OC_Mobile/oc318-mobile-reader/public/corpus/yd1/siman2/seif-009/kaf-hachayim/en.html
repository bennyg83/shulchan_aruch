**i) There. Mumar eats nevelot, etc.** Meaning witnesses testified he ate nevelah intentionally even once; needless to say where they saw him seeking kosher and not finding and ate nevelah—plainly called for appetite. Even if they saw him eating nevelah plainly intentionally—we attribute to appetite that he did not find kosher and craves meat. Same if nevelah commonly cheaper than permitted and he spared money and ate nevelah—we attribute: if permitted were as common as forbidden he would not leave permitted and eat forbidden—and called for appetite. Shulchan Malachim letter 2. Zivchei Tzedek letter 4. See Shulchan Aruch letter 4 wrote specifically mumar for eating nevelot—even once examines knife and gives him; mumar for treifot once does not need knife examination—examine. Meaning if they know he knows that slaughtering with damaged knife is nevelah and he does not eat nevelah—notwithstanding.
**b) There. Non-Jew shechitah nevelah.** But permitted in benefit. Beit Yosef. Levush. Bach. as customary in Hagahot Beit Yosef letter 1 Peri Chadash letter 2 Peri Toar letter 2. Shulchan Malachim letter 1. Mishnat Shlomo letter 1. Shulchan Gavoah letter 1. Zivchei Torah letter 1. Zivchei Shlomo letter 1. Zivchei Tzedek letter 1. Meaning: when we did not hear he is heretic; but if we heard—forbidden in benefit. Beit Yosef in name of Baal HaItur. Bach.
**c) And so** shechitah of heretic who denies Torah of Moshe Rabbeinu—nevelah and permitted in benefit as Rambam chapter 4 and Rashba in comment. But heretic who became priest for idol worship whether gentile or Israel—plain thought toward non-Jew and his shechitah forbidden in benefit as taught chapter 1 Chullin: shechitah of heretic for idol worship. Peri Chadash there. Zivchei Torah there. Even its hide, bones, horns, hooves, and fat forbidden in benefit. Shulchan Malachim there and Turei Zahav letter 4. Nevertheless b'dieved where it came to Israel's hand one may be lenient in benefit where loss, in his plain shechitah that he slaughtered for his table and we did not hear he is heretic for idol worship—for they are not so attached nowadays even priests said plain shechitah is for idol worship. Specifically if priest slaughtered; but if killed—permitted in benefit for they do not worship idol except via shechitah path. It also appears to me: in places where their custom is to stab and kill for food and we saw priest slaughtered—proven he does for idol worship; in such case forbid in benefit. Shulchan Malachim there.
**d) There. Even if he is a minor.** One could say his thought is not toward non-Jew; even if he knows how to train hands and is expert—in such case minor Israelite kosher, non-Jew is nevelah. Shach letter 1. Peri Chadash letter 3. Zivchei Torah letter 2. Zivchei Tzedek letter 2. Yad Eliyahu letter 2.
**e) There. Even if he is a minor.** And even if Israel examined kosher knife for him and stood over him and saw he slaughtered well—it is nevelah. Shulchan Malachim letter 1. Yad Eliyahu letter 2.
**g) There in gloss. Such as ger toshav.** Or from nations that do not worship idols; or convert who circumcised but did not immerse properly. Shulchan Malachim there.
