**forbidden.** Shach wrote: even eating an olive-size from this meat.
**over them.** Shach wrote: if the Israelite only goes in and out, then there must also be an expert—for perhaps not with the Israelite's knowledge, and he does not know this is forbidden for an Israelite so he will not fear him.
**knives.** Karaites nowadays—their shechitah is forbidden even when an Israelite stands over them and saw he slaughtered well—responsa R. Betzalel siman 3.
**like a mumar.** Shach wrote: like mumar to the entire Torah—not like Taz who ruled only like mumar for appetite. Hagahot Beit Yosef distinguishes: an informer not established for that, only temporarily—then b'dieved his shechitah is kosher; but an informer established for that—shechitah invalid even b'dieved. Peri Chadash in the last pamphlet disagrees.
