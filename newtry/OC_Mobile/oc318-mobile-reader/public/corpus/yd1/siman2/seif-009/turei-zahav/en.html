**Unless they slaughtered and others, etc.** And one might say: are they not fit to slaughter—for they were established in mitzvot of shechitah? Rashba wrote on this: if he is expert his shechitah is kosher when Israelite stands over him, even going in and out. It is hard: since he stands over him, why need expert? Beit Yosef resolved: there is concern lest he delayed, etc. One might wonder: how is this different from Israelite who has not learned laws of shechitah where an adult standing over him helps? Rather it is simple as Beit Yosef wrote initially: he mentions expert because of “going in and out” that he mentions at the end—there specifically we require expert.
**Informer (moser)—his law is like a mumar.** It appears this means like mumar for appetite above.
