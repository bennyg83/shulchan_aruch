A Sadducee and Boethusian (they follow Tzadok and Boethus, students of Antignus ish Socho, who went out to bad culture and do not believe in the Oral Torah)—their shechitah is forbidden unless they slaughtered and others stood over them, and also examined the knives for them. {Rama: An informer (moser)—his law is like a mumar and his shechitah is invalid (Hagahos Alfasi).
