**required.** Hagahot Beit Yosef until his shechitah is invalid—and this is Shach's words. See responsum Beit Yaakov siman 138 who disagrees: his view is specifically l'chatchila Rambam requires knife examination, but b'dieved his shechitah is kosher—examine. See responsum Pa'er HaDor to Rambam siman 112; responsa Radbaz haChadashot siman 24; Noda BiYehudah Choshen Mishpat siman 37 [see responsum Chasam Sofer siman 8 regarding shochet and shochet in one city where his wife's sister became pregnant between her teeth and said from him she conceived; afterward the cantor himself came and said before the congregation and rabbi av beit din that this matter is true that he came upon his wife's sister; but the next day another spirit was with him that he did not say so except to dismiss from his position, etc. He wrote: even if we suppose his later words are true, nevertheless he is a disgraceful contemptible person who was brazen to say about himself before congregation of Israel and before rabbi av beit din that he transgressed karet issur of forbidden relations—even if he says teshuvah he would be brazen; how much more only per confession. How can there be credibility for a person who for such a small excuse disgraces and brazenizes himself so much—what will he do if monetary loss or general honor occurs? Such a disgrace is disqualified for testimony as Choshen Mishpat siman 34 seif 18; how can he testify on shechitah and inspection? The mind cannot bear establishing such a disgrace before the ark, how much more believing him on shochet u'bodek—examine. What Hagahot Beit Yosef also wrote is not comparable to siman 1, etc.—see responsum Mishkenot Yaakov end siman 6 what he wrote on this.]
