**Their shechitah is forbidden.** Rashba wrote in Terumat HaDeshen that even cutting an olive-size of meat and giving him does not help when he slaughtered between himself and himself, because shechitah is not written and whatever is not written—even if established—we do not rely on it, as said in Shas regarding Kutim—necessarily. So in his novellae 3a in Ramban’s name: the halachah follows the first Tanna of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel. But Rambam wrote: Sadducee and Boethusian—their shechitah is forbidden; and if he slaughtered before us it is permitted, for they are not like idol worshippers but are not believed to say they did not disqualify. It implies cutting an olive-size and giving helps when he himself will eat; so Tur—this is Rambam’s view as Perishah and Bach wrote. It appears his reason: since Abaye and Rava said regarding Kutim before the decree that cutting an olive-size helps, and so the whole sugya at the beginning of Chullin implies; and from the Stam of Shas there “Rav Ashi did not say like both of them—he holds Kutim are converts of lions” and did not say he holds like first Tanna of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel—apparently the halachah is fixed like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel.
**And others stand over them.** Rashba required that there also be an expert; Beit Yosef wondered at him—since an Israelite stands over him, why need an expert? Therefore he did not mention expert here. But it appears Rashba’s view too: with Israelite standing over him one need not have an expert, for he is no worse than a minor who knows how to train his hands above siman 1—only when Israelite goes in and out he required expert, lest not with Israelite’s knowledge and he does not know this is forbidden for Israelite so he will fear him. So Maharshal there siman 6, Bach, and so Darchei Moshe.
**And also they examined knives for them, etc.** Uncertain whence this—for even Rashba who is stringent wrote the law of Sadducee and Boethusian is like Kutim before the decree; so Kessef Mishneh himself chapter 4 Hilchos Shechitah; so Rambam chapter 6 Hilchos Avadim; and Mechaber below siman 267 seif 47 that Sadducee and Boethusian are like Kutim before decree. Regarding Kutim before decree Tosafot 3a and Rosh wrote: knife examination is not required, for Israelite mumar—for he knows no Israelite relies on him and fears lest they examine the knife after him, therefore he is careful not to slaughter with damaged knife; but Israelite mumar does not fear they will not examine after him since they hold him as Israelite—end of their words. Uncertain again—I found in Levush he also wondered on the Mechaber here—examine. However all this is by law; but in responsa R. Betzalel siman 3 he wrote this is specifically in Tzidon of former times when they had several good measures; but these Karaites of our time who corrupted their deeds and also do not eat from Israelite shechitah—their shechitah is forbidden even with Israelite over them who saw he slaughtered well—examine.
**Informer (moser)—his law is like a mumar.** For the entire Torah—so in Hagahos Alfasi in the name of Riaz, brought by Darchei Moshe.
**And see below siman 119.** Meaning there seif 10 the Mechaber ruled his shechitah is kosher; so Maharshal there.
