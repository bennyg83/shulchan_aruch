**(seif 7) Mumar regarding orlah—his law like mumar for one transgression.** Explained in previous seif his law does not require knife examination except per Rambam. Mumar regarding orlah—Rashi chapter 1 Chullin (5) explains kicking at this mitzvah—implies for provoking; so Tosafot except not mumar for that matter and for appetite—if for provoking plainly invalid—end. Implies explicitly applied to what we establish in mumar regarding orlah nicely even for provoking.
