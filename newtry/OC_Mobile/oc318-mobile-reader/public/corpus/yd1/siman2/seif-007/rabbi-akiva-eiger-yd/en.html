**(Shulchan Aruch) Except these two.** This is Rabbenu Yerucham's words; plain proof: otherwise only mumar to entire Torah would be specifically on entire Torah—otherwise he is mumar from idol worship or Shabbat; nevertheless I wonder: perhaps mumar to entire Torah includes even Shabbat except idol worship—only was not in public, for from Shabbat alone one is not mumar since not in public.
