**(seif 11) If there were, etc., and needless to say, etc.** Avodah Zarah; but in Tosefta there: Israel and non-Jew who held the knife and slaughtered—even one above and one below—their shechitah kosher; and so Acharonim—examine their words.
**(Likkut) If there were, etc.** It is not so—for if so, that which we say 41a “come learn two,” etc.—why need a name for Israelite mumar? (Tosafot 14a s.v. hashochet; and one can explain, etc.; and further we say, etc.; but this source of law in Shulchan Aruch is from Rashba and his view is not like Tosafot as stated siman 4 seif 3—and see Shach); and so in Tosefta; and Hagahot Ashrei wrote it siman 65 s.v. vetania, etc. (end quote).
**Shechitah of ape, etc.** Tosefta there: shechitah of ape is invalid, as it says “and you shall slaughter”—and not that non-Jew slaughtered, and not that ape slaughtered, and not that it slaughtered itself.
