**(seif 18 in gloss) Ape shechitah invalid.** See Magen Avraham (siman 159 seif 14) who challenged: here plainly ape invalid; there regarding netilat yadayim brings two views and permits b'dieved; per his answer here d'oraisa we are stringent; if so should forbid only from doubt and regarding orlah forbidden to slaughter after him. Per my view initially no difficulty: even if for netilat yadayim they permit as called human force—here not fit to slaughter—like non-Jew shechitah; clear in my humble view.
