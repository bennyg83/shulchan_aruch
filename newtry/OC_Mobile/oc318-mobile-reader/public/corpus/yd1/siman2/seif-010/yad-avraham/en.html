**(seif 10) If the unfit began with half the kaneh and the fit finished—it is kosher.** So Hagahot Ashrei beginning Chullin; concluded we hold it is not for shechitah but only at end—implies per view that shechitah applies from beginning to end it would be invalid. One might say it challenges view that shechitah from beginning to end—for here is explicit baraita brought in Shas chapter HaOr veHaRotav (121) that if non-Jew began with something not making treifah and Israel came and finished—it is kosher. Rather learn: all agree kosher, for non-Jew shechitah is only ape act and no worse than if half kaneh damaged. See Rashi Chullin (20). Further clear required proof: R. Yochanan holds shechitah from beginning to end chapter 2 Chullin (29), and he himself holds chapter 1 Chullin (19b) non-Jew began half kaneh and Israel finished—kosher—examine. This is irrefutable proof. Rambam too although ruled chapter 1 Hilchos Shechitah halachah 18 shechitah from beginning to end, nevertheless ruled this baraita of HaOr veHaRotav chapter 4 Hilchos Shechitah. This is clear. Words of Hagahot Ashrei above cannot be settled unless we emend: “but in something making treifah invalid”; although we hold it is not for shechitah, etc.—these are Rava's words chapter 2 Chullin (29); so Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen and Beit Yosef here—examine.
