**(seif 10) If the unfit began, etc.—when is this said, etc.** Avodah Zarah and Gemara there 121b: non-Jew slaughtered in place of, etc.; and in Tosefta beginning Makshirin: Israel who slaughtered and non-Jew removed—shechitah through his hand, his shechitah invalid; Israel who slaughtered two or majority of years—his shechitah kosher; non-Jew who slaughtered and Israel removed—shechitah through his hand, his shechitah kosher; slaughtered with something that does not make treifah and Israel came and finished—it is permitted in eating.
