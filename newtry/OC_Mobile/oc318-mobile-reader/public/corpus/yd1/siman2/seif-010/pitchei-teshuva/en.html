**But if he is disqualified for testimony.** Shach seif 20; see Tiferet Yisrael on Mishnayot from Gaon av beit din of Dessau on Demai chapter 4 mishnah 1: he wondered on Shach who wrote that even in shechitah for others he is believed—it is not so, for one suspected is believed for others only when not opposing presumption; but not in shechitah where there is presumption of “mother's house”—he is not believed even for others. The reason they said in the mishnah there “he said to him after he is not believed,” etc.—examine [see responsum Chasam Sofer siman 22: his view accords with Shach; he wrote in Noda BiYehudah siman 1 wondered on him what reason. But the reason is simple: he is suspected only that he fears monetary loss to himself when it becomes nevelah in his hand he will not separate from issur; but for bodily effort he is not suspected—therefore believed to slaughter for others without knife examination. There they asked regarding a slaughterer who confessed in illness that for about four years while standing and they press him to hurry slaughter among birds and animals—many times he felt flaw in knife before shechitah and slaughtered with it because they hurried him and his heart did not prevail against his inclination to delay people; in several places they took fat from that place—what is law of fat and utensils? He answered: certainly if known by witnesses everything he slaughtered is presumed issur as not properly slaughtered, although one can say he only transgressed issurim for appetite not to be ashamed before standers mocking him for lengthening knife honing—but always majority of times he honed knife well alone so this would not happen. Nevertheless since he did so several times and is accustomed—even if only transgresses for himself to eat nevelot, his shechitah invalid even b'dieved for others, for since he tramples it he will not trouble again; how much more here destined to feed nevelot to others from shame he will not trouble—and all already slaughtered presumed issur. But in this case no testimony only he testifies on himself—even if we suppose per some Acharonim confession path is believed, nevertheless the mouth that forbade what he slaughtered with damaged knife is the mouth that permitted them that people did not stand over him and press him and knife was fixed at home like most slaughterers; if we had examined knife for him we would eat from his shechitah—so these are as if we examined knife for him, for he said in confession specifically when pressed his knife was damaged and he intentionally slaughtered with it from pressers not otherwise; found at least majority slaughtered properly; all that separated is permitted; do not forbid fat and utensils retroactively (it appears he means utensils of other places that did not take from that place only incidental fat; but utensils of that place certainly issurim all twenty-four hours if we decide he is believed; as he wrote siman 4 brought above end seif 1). Even if we decide he is believed since he said path of teshuvah and especially this too is unclear if to train—examine. And as stated siman 1 seif 6 and end seif 18.]
