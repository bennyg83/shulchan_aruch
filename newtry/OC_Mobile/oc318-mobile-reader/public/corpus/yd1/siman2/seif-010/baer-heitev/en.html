**invalid.** Shach wrote: implies if the fit person slaughtered the majority it is kosher—for it is no longer rendered treifah; unlike delay, which disqualifies even in the minority at the end.
**windpipe (kaneh).** Shach wrote: even if the fit person slaughtered half the kaneh and the unfit completed it to majority—it is also invalid.
**valid.** Shach wrote: even though we hold delay of any amount is treifah in kaneh or veshet—nevertheless this law is found where he grasped the kaneh alone in his hand, for it is kosher even per us, as at the end of siman 21—all per Shach; Taz disagrees there—examine; Nekudot HaKesef disagrees with Taz.
