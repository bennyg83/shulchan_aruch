**j) There. Israel examines knife and gives him, etc.** Regarding whether must examine knife after shechitah—dispute as Beit Yosef wrote—examine. If knife before us must examine also at end after shechitah; if knife not before us or b'dieved did not examine after—kosher. Taz seif 2. Shach seif 4. as customary Hagahot Beit Yosef letter 9. Shulchan Malachim letter 5. Shulchan Aruch letter 4. Zivchei Tzedek letter 5. So l'chatchila must examine simanim after his shechitah; b'dieved kosher. Shulchan Malachim there.
