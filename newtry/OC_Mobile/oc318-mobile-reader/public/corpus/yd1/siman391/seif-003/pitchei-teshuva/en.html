After thirty days. The Bach holds that only a mourner for a parent is restricted within thirty days, while one mourning other relatives may attend even within thirty days. The Shach, seif katan 3, disagrees. Noda BiYehudah, Yoreh Deah 100, finds the Bach's view compelling: if a reciprocal social meal is forbidden to a mourner for a parent throughout twelve months but permitted to other mourners within thirty days, the same distinction should apply here.
