Provided that it is after thirty days, etc.
The Bach permits a mourner for other relatives to attend within thirty days in certain cases, comparing it to bringing an orphan bride or groom to marriage, but the Rama's language does not support this.
The distinctions between a wedding meal, instrumental music, serving as an attendant, and merely entering to offer greetings must be followed as stated by the authorities.
The prevailing custom is to be stringent during the thirty days except where a specific mitzvah need and the recognized exceptions apply.
