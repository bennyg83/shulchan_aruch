To enter the wedding canopy not at mealtime to hear blessings — some permit and some forbid, rather stand outside the house to hear blessings: {Rama: But he may not enter the house at all when they stand there with groom and bride merriment, and so Ashkenaz practice (Tur); in these lands, all this in a house where they eat, drink, and rejoice; but in a canopy in synagogue where they bless betrothal and marriage and there is no joy at all — permitted immediately after seven (Hagahot Maimoniyot); and some forbid until thirty (there in name of Raavyah), and so it appears to me; and some places are strict that mourner stands all twelve months outside synagogue to hear blessings (Hagahot in Maharil); nevertheless mourner may bless betrothal and marriage under canopy in synagogue; and likewise may bring groom in as in our custom that two men bring groom under canopy; and may wear some Shabbat garments when bringing in, provided after thirty (Da'at Emet), and so is custom; some permit mourner to eat at wedding or circumcision with attendants provided not in place of joy such as another house (Kol Bo and Beit Yosef in name of Semak); and some forbid (Hagahot Ashiri); custom is only mourner serves if he wishes and eats in his house from what they send from the meal; some permit mourner to eat at fish meal after wedding since after they ceased saying joy is in his home it is recognizable there is no joy and permitted (Beit Yosef in name of Rokeach); some say forbidden to eat at meal at night if father or mother died the next day (Hagahot Minhagim in name of Mahariw).}
