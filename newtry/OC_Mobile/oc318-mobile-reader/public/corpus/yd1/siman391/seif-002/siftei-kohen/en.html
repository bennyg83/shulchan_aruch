Companionship joy [etc.]. [Meaning] that they form a bond of companionship with one another — eating and drinking together today and going back to eat with the other tomorrow:
Twelve months. And even if that year is a leap year, twelve months suffice. So wrote the decisors:
But within thirty days it is forbidden. This implies even regarding other relatives. And so it is implied in the Taz, and so it is stated explicitly in the Ra'avad cited in Sefer Torat HaAdam — the source of this ruling — see there; and likewise in the language of the Ra'avad in the Rosh; and likewise regarding the greeting of peace above siman 385 and regarding marriage below siman 392 — there is no distinction within thirty days between [mourning for] one's father and mother and other relatives.
