For all the dead he may enter a banquet house after thirty days; for father and mother after twelve months, even if the year is leap year permitted after twelve months; however joy of friendship that he was obligated to repay immediately — permitted immediately after seven; but if not obligated to repay — forbidden to enter until thirty; for father and mother, even if obligated to repay — forbidden until after twelve months: {Rama: But for a mitzvah gathering such as marrying orphan to orphan for Heaven's sake — if he does not eat there the matter will be cancelled, permitted after thirty; but within thirty forbidden for any mitzvah meal in the world (Tur in name of Raavad); but a mitzvah meal without joy permitted to enter, such as pidyon haben or circumcision meal — permitted to eat there even within seven provided he not leave his doorway (Terumat HaDeshen siman 251); and some forbid circumcision meal (Mordechai); custom is not to eat any meal all twelve months if outside his house; within the house they are lenient to eat at his house at circumcision, and all the more other meals without joy; but wedding meal one should be strict, so it appears to me; but one who is sandek or mohel may wear Shabbat garments until after circumcision and permitted to enter circumcision to eat if after thirty even if circumcision not in his house (Beit Yosef in name of responsum of Maharil).}
