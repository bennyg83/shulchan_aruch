And in a company, etc. This is in Moed Katan and the Yerushalmi, brought by Tosafot there, s.v. u-le-simchat, etc., and in Ketubot 4a, end of s.v. aval, and Yevamot 44a, end of s.v. shani. The Raavad explained that it applies specifically in such a case, for another mitzvah meal is more stringent than an ordinary optional meal, as seen from what is stated there regarding a social celebration, etc.; and specifically after thirty days. Similarly, the Yerushalmi permits a mitzvah company and sanctification of the new month only when it cannot be done without him, such as eating the Pesach offering. This is why it says, "and if not," etc. See the Rosh, siman 89. The Nimukei Yosef explained it as referring to a meal that has no joy, such as a brit, etc., as stated in the first chapter of Ketubot, 8a: come and hear, but a meal, etc.
(Likkut) And in a company, etc. But the Ramban disagrees and writes that it is specifically like the Yerushalmi: sanctification of the new month, eating kodashim and the Pesach offering, etc.
And some forbid, etc. As stated at the beginning of chapter 19 of Shabbat: "I rejoice over Your word," etc. See Tosafot in Moed Katan, s.v. cited above.
