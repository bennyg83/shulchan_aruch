(Shach, seif katan 3) And the Bach did not explain it this way. In my humble opinion, we see that in seif 2 a social meal during the thirty days is permitted for other relatives, whereas for one's father or mother it is prohibited throughout twelve months. Thus the law is that within thirty days for other relatives there is greater leniency than after thirty days for a father or mother. Since marrying off an orphan is permitted during the twelve months, all the more so it is permitted within thirty days for other relatives. I later obtained Sefer Dagul MeRevavah and found that I had independently arrived at his view on this point.
(Siman 391, seif 2, in the gloss) At any meal whatsoever throughout twelve months. Regarding a siyum of a tractate, see Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 119.
