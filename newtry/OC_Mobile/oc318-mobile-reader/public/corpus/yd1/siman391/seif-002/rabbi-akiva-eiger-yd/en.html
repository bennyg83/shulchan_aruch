(Shach s.k. 3) And the Bach did not explain so. In my humble opinion, we see from seif 2 that a social meal within thirty days is permitted, while for a father or mother it is forbidden for all twelve months. Thus we rule that, regarding other relatives, the period within thirty days is more lenient than the period after thirty days for a father or mother. Since marrying off an orphan is permitted within twelve months, it is all the more so permitted within thirty days for other relatives. Later, the Dagul MeRevavah came into my hand, and I found that I had matched his view.
