With joy. See what I wrote above in siman 376 s.k. 2 in the name of Har HaKarmel responsa siman 20, that in havdalah which a mourner makes within seven days over a cup, he should not say the verses of joy that precede it, but should begin with the blessings; see there. See Magen Avraham siman 426, who wrote that a mourner in the seven days of mourning should not recite kiddush levanah; but if he will not complete his mourning until the tenth of the month, he should not wait until the night of the eleventh, and should recite it during his mourning; see there. In Shevut Yaakov II siman 11, he wrote that nevertheless he should not recite kiddush levanah until after the first three days of weeping, and even if when the three days of weeping end it will be the night of the twelfth of the month, he should wait; see there. In Sha'ar Ephraim responsa, Yoreh De'ah siman 95, he wrote that only if the time will pass and he will no longer be able to recite kiddush levanah may he recite it during mourning; but when there is still time to recite it, he may not recite it during mourning. See there.
