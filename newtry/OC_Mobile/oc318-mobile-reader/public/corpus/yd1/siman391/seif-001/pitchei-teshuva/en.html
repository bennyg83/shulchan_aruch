With joy. See what I wrote above in siman 376, seif katan 2, from Responsa Har HaCarmel, siman 20, that an aveil making Havdalah during shivah should omit the introductory verses of joy and begin with the blessings. Magen Avraham, siman 426, writes that a mourner should not recite Kiddush Levanah during the seven days. If, however, his mourning will continue beyond the tenth of the month, he should not wait until the eleventh night but may recite it during shivah. Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 11, says he should nevertheless wait until after the first three days of crying, even if that means reciting on the twelfth night. Sha'ar Ephraim, Yoreh Deah siman 95, writes that Kiddush Levanah is permitted during shivah only where the opportunity would otherwise expire completely; if there is still adequate time afterward, it should not be recited during mourning.
