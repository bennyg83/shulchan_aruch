Vessels. The Shach explained: on the back of vessels, not into them.
Need not be concerned. And the Bach concludes that at the head of the ship—even slaughtering inside the sea or in a vessel—it is permitted, for it is impossible to soil the ship's sides outside; but standing inside the ship—even though he soils the ship's sides, they are washed in their movement in water. And we do not hold thus; rather even at the head of the ship one must also slaughter on its sides or on top of a vessel.
The heart. And the Shach wrote: in Minhagim it states they give him the liver; and I have now seen the practice to eat from the feet—end of his words; and see above siman 116. And the Kaneh wrote: one should not slaughter a goose at a time when demons have power and dominion—and that is the month of Shevat—for the killer will be killed.
