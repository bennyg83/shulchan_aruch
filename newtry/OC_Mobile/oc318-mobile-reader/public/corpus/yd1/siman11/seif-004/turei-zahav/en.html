If he was on a ship, etc. In the Tur it is written first: one slaughters in every place, even on the roof and at the head of the ship—and so is in the baraita; and it teaches us we are not concerned lest they say he slaughters on the roof for the host of heaven, and similarly at the head of the ship lest he slaughter for the minister of the sea. And Rashi explained that then even collecting in a vessel is permitted, for all know he does so only so as not to soil his roof or ship. Tosafot challenged him from the chapter HaShochet that one must slaughter specifically on the ship's sides—from which we learn on a ship even without a vessel it is forbidden—and so the Ran; and such is the Tur's view, who did not permit on a ship except on top of vessels—and then even if it pours afterward to the sea and not into them there is no concern; but directly into the sea without the ship's sides is also forbidden. It turns out collecting blood in a vessel is forbidden everywhere, and on a ship there is no permission to slaughter to the sea except on top of a vessel or on the ship's sides—and such is also the Shulchan Aruch's intent; and so we hold, although Mahari Chai toiled to resolve Rashi from Tosafot's difficulty—nevertheless for halachah we have only the conclusion of Tosafot, Ran, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch; and if he transgressed and slaughtered in forbidden manner, see end of siman 12.
