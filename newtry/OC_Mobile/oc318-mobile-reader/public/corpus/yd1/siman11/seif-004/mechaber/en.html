If he was on a ship and has no free place in it to slaughter, he may slaughter on top of vessels and the blood pours and goes down into the water; or he extends his hand outside the ship and slaughters on its sides and the blood pours and goes down into the water, and he need not be concerned. {Rama: Some slaughterers are careful not to slaughter any goose in Tevet and Shevat unless they eat from the heart, for there is a received tradition that there is one hour in those months—if one slaughters a goose during it, the slaughterer will die unless he eats from it; and the custom is to eat from the heart (Teshuvot in the name of R' Yehuda HaChasid).}
