If he was on a ship—the conclusion of most poskim is not like Rashi who permits at the head of the ship to slaughter directly, for it is known to the ship's carpenter he slaughters to the sea. And similarly Rashi's view at the head of the roof—permitted to slaughter into vessels—and all workers omitted this, and one should not be lenient—and not like the Bach.
On top of vessels—here are two explanations: one explanation that he slaughters on a vessel placed on a slope, part on the ship and part outside the ship, and when slaughtering facing outward the blood pours outside into the sea—and this is correct for all. And another explanation that he slaughters behind the vessel even though blood descends into the vessel—and see Peleti what I wrote that even according to this one should not permit except at sea but not in another place.
From the heart—and sometimes they eat from the liver or leg; and in Prague from the fat of the essence; and see Peleti one need not be stringent on this, for it is in the category of ways of the Emorites, and included in "you shall not practice divination"—let it be a wonder; and the matter came from the mouth of the pious R' Yehuda, and in his days there were many diviners and sorcerers with geese, as known in story-books that R' Eliezer of Germiza came to nullify their power. And it is possible this too was to nullify their power; but in our time we have not heard this matter, and certainly one need not be stringent; and to eat for a mitzvah meal—Shabbat, brit milah, wedding—it is obvious he may slaughter, and he is a guardian of mitzvot, etc.
