He should not slaughter. And the reason is in the Shach: lest he delay or press and not notice; but b'dieved when he says "it is clear to me I did not delay and did not press," it is valid—and it is not comparable to above siman 6, for even if he says "it is clear to me I did not press" it is invalid, lest he press another time—for there it is common to press since the animal's neck is raised, which is not so here. And if he began to slaughter by candlelight before he slaughtered the majority of the simanim and it went out, he does not need it and finishes his slaughter without delay—Beit Yaakov siman 106.
Torch. And the Shach wrote: a lamp that has two wicks; and similarly two lamps—even if braided—have the law of a torch; and see Orach Chaim siman 433 seif 2; and the Magen Avraham siman 275 s.k. 3 permits l'chatchila with a single lamp. And Peri Chadash permits by moonlight when it shines well. And Radbaz is stringent l'chatchila.
