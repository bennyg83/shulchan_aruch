He should not slaughter. — Lest he delay (shehiyah) or press (derisah) and not notice; but b'dieved, when he says "it is clear to me that I did not delay and did not press," it is valid—Rashba. And it is not comparable to above siman 6 s.k. 9 regarding the neck of an animal raised high—even if he says "it is clear to me that I did not press," it is invalid, lest he press another time, for there it is common to press since the animal's neck is raised; or alternatively, here the reason is as Rashi wrote—that perhaps he did not slaughter the majority and he does not know, and if so there is no concern lest another time he not slaughter the majority, for every slaughterer must inspect after slaughter whether he slaughtered the majority, as below siman 25; and see there. And it appears to me that even according to Rashi's reason it is forbidden l'chatchila to slaughter by day in a dark place, for we are concerned lest he pause in idol worship (avodah zarah) and forget and not inspect the simanim by daylight—and the Bach did not write thus; see there.
