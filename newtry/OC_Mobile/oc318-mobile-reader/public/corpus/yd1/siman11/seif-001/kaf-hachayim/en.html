(a) [Seif 1] One may always slaughter both by day and by night; in what case are these words said—when there is a torch opposite him, etc. Or by day in a dark place he should not slaughter, etc. And the reason Rashi wrote in Chullin 13b: l'chatchila he should not slaughter at night lest he not slaughter the majority and he not know. And the Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh the short wrote: lest he press, lest he make chaladah, and he not notice. And according to all views, both for Rashi and for the Rashba, l'chatchila he should not slaughter whether at night or in a dark house; and b'dieved his slaughter is valid. Taz s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 1.
