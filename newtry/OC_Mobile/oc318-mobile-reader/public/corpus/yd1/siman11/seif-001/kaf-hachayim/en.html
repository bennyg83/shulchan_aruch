(a) [Seif 1] One may always slaughter both by day and by night; in what case are these words said—when there is a torch opposite him, etc. Or by day in a dark place he should not slaughter, etc. And the reason Rashi wrote in Chullin 13b: l'chatchila he should not slaughter at night lest he not slaughter the majority and he not know. And the Rashba in Tosafot HaRosh the short wrote: lest he press, lest he make chaladah, and he not notice. And according to all views, both for Rashi and for the Rashba, l'chatchila he should not slaughter whether at night or in a dark house; and b'dieved his slaughter is valid. Taz s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash note 1; Shomer Emunim note 1; Kometz note 1; Zivchei Tzedek note 1.
(c) There. And if he slaughtered, his slaughter is valid. Meaning he saw afterward by candlelight or by daylight that he slaughtered the majority according to Rashi; and we know the Rashba's view—such as he sensed he did not delay and did not press and did not make chaladah, as the Taz and Shach wrote there. And we are not concerned lest the cut widened afterward, as the Taz and Peri Chadash wrote there—not like the Bach. And so Zivchei Tzedek note 3; and see below siman 25 seif 1 in the second hagahah and in our words there b'siyata diShmaya.
