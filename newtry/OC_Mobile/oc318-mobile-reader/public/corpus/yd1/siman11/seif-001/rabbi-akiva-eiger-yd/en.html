(Siman 11 seif 1) And if he slaughtered. If it went out in the middle of slaughter, he wrote in responsum Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 61 that it is worse—for the slaughterer is startled during slaughter and makes delay and renders it treifah (there).
(There) And two candles count as a torch. See Orach Meshur on Darkei Moshe on Yoreh De'ah that even if they are not attached it is called a torch; and similarly for havdalah—and I copied it in the margin of my Magen Avraham (siman 298 s.k. 4).
