And two candles count as a torch. See Ba'er Heitev; and he wrote in the mishnah of R' Akiva at its end daf 92: permitted to slaughter l'chatchila at night opposite two lamps that are not braided or attached (there); and see what A.A.Z. wrote in Shach and Tosafot Shabbat that their flames must touch each other (there). And see in Sefer Bnei Chayya who challenged the proof of Peri Chadash who wrote there is no proof from "the moon is like three" for slaughter—for when a person reads in a book he cannot see by moonlight as by a lamp; and regarding demons it is different, since its light is inclusive and somewhat similar to day they are not seen (there). And see what A.A.Z. wrote in Tosafot Shabbat on this that the sense will testify moonlight is not like torchlight in approximate place—end of his words. And see Berit Yosef and Machatzit HaShekel who agreed to forbid l'chatchila by moonlight (there). And he wrote in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 61 regarding one who slaughtered at night and the lamp went out in the middle of slaughter—even though at night without a lamp his slaughter is valid, this is worse—for through extinguishing the lamp he is startled and makes delay and renders it treifah (there). And see what was printed from Even HaEzer who copied this and the language is garbled (there).
