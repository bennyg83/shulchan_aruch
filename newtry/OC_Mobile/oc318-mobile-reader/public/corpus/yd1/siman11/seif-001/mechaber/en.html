At what times one may slaughter. And the law of one who slaughters in water. In it are 4 seifim. One may always slaughter both by day and by night; in what case are these words said? When there is a torch (avuka) opposite him. But if there is no torch opposite him, or by day in a dark place—he should not slaughter; and if he slaughtered, his slaughter is valid. (And two candles count as a torch) (Maharil and Agudah).
