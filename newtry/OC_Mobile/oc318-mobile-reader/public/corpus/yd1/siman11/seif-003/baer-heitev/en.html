And the Taz wrote: specifically in a vessel turbid water helps, for then it proves he is not acting to collect blood; but if the water in the sea is turbid it does not help, for they still say to the minister he is slaughtering. And Beit Yosef wrote: a God-fearing person should be careful not to collect blood in a vessel after slaughter in order to sell to a gentile (there). And Peri Chadash in Kuntres Acharon disagreed and wrote: the Sages only forbade at the beginning of slaughter into a vessel, but after he removed his hand from slaughter we are not concerned for marit ayin.
