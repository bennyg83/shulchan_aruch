Into seas—even turbid rivers—for what difference? They will say to the minister of the sea or river that he slaughtered.
And not into vessels: Rav Beit HaLevi challenged what is practiced—after slaughter they collect blood in a vessel and sell to gentiles for their work; and the above sage challenged that this is into a vessel—and Rav Peri Chadash in Kuntres Acharon wrote he acted halachah l'ma'aseh and permitted; and so they practice in these countries. And see Peleti why; and if possible to receive it through a gentile, it is better (there).
