One may not slaughter into seas and rivers, lest they say to the minister of the sea that he is slaughtering; nor into vessels, lest they say he is collecting the blood to cast it for avodah zarah. And if there is water in a vessel—then the blood is not fit for casting—if the water is clear he should not slaughter in it, lest they say he is slaughtering toward the form visible in the water; and if it is turbid, it is permitted. And similarly, if there is earth in a vessel, it is permitted to slaughter in it.
