One who slaughters on Shabbat, etc. Even intentionally (and in public) his slaughter is valid—for as he wrote above siman 2 s.k. 17, one does not become a gentile through that slaughter but only from the first slaughter and onward; nevertheless whether unintentional or intentional, his law is as explained in Orach Chaim siman 318, and so wrote the Bach; and similarly in Shiltei HaGibborim there—Riaz—not like the Magen Avraham.
