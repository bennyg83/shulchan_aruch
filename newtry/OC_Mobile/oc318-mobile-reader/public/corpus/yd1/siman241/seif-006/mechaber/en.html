Whoever disgraces his father and mother — even with words, even with a hint — behold, he is included in "cursed by the Almighty," as it says, "Cursed is one who disgraces his father and mother," and beit din may strike him for this with lashes of rebellion and punish as appropriate: {Rama: One who became liable to take an oath to his son in beit din — they do not impose upon him an oath that contains a curse, for this comes to curse his father; rather they impose an oath that contains no curse. (Tur in the name of Rambam)}
