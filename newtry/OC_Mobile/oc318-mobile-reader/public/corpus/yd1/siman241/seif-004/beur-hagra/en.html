(Likut) If he did, etc. — likewise the Rif in chapter 2 of Yevamot from that in Sanhedrin; but Tosafot in Yevamot there s.v. she'asah, etc., explain that Sanhedrin too deals with doing repentance; and likewise Mordechai in Yevamot there in name of Rabbeinu Tam that the question there deals with doing repentance, for otherwise Rav Sheshet would not have said "and who permitted them," since he is not doing an act with you; and the baraita of one going out to be executed — we establish it there where they do not refuse to leave; but without doing repentance there is no distinction between prohibition and obligation, for "doing an act with you" is not written regarding obligation that the Gemara challenges from it, rather regarding prohibition; and the Gemara's question is in truth that even l'chatchila it is permitted (end quote).
