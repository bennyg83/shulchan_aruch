If a thorn was stuck in his father, he should not remove it, lest he come to make a wound in him; and likewise if he is one who lets blood or a physician — he should not let blood for his father nor cut a limb for him even though he intends for healing: {Rama: In what case is this so? When there is another to do it; but if there is no other to do it and he is suffering — behold, he lets his blood and cuts for him as they instruct to do. (This too is in Tur and Rambam chapter 5 of Laws of Rebels)}
