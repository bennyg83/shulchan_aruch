The laws of one who strikes and one who curses his father and mother. And it contains 9 seifim. One who curses his father or mother — even after their death — is liable for stoning if there are witnesses and forewarning; both a man and a woman who cursed are liable. In what case is this stated? When they cursed them with the special Names; but if they cursed them with a substitute name, he is liable only for a negative commandment, like one who curses an ordinary Israelite:
{Rama: And likewise one who strikes his father or mother during their lifetime — whether man or woman — is liable for strangulation; and specifically if they made a wound in them; but if they did not make a wound, it is only a negative commandment, like one who strikes an ordinary Israelite. (Tur)}
