(Siman 342, seif 1) And he observes the seven days of wedding celebration. If a festival occurs during those wedding days, Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, in the later supplements, letter 17, cites Responsa Edut BeYaakov, siman 11, that the festival interrupts the seven days. In my opinion this requires study. Since mourning has not yet taken effect, the festival should not cancel it. I do not have the responsum available to inspect, so practical halachah requires further study.
