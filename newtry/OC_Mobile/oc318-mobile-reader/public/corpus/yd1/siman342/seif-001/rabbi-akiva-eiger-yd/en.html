(Siman 342 seif 1) And he observes the seven days of celebration. If, within the days of celebration, a festival occurs, it interrupts the decree of the seven days, as Kenesset HaGedolah writes at the end, in some likkutim, note 17, in the name of Responsa Edut BeYaakov siman 11. But in my humble opinion this ruling requires study, for plainly, since mourning has not yet taken effect upon him, the festival should not cancel it. The responsum itself is not in my possession to examine, and the practical law requires study.
