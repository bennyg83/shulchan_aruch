Partners who chose judges with the power of a cherem to uphold what they decreed upon them, and one of them submitted a moda'a that he did not accept the chosen ones upon himself except so that what he owed him would be verified—for he feared lest he deny—and he began to say he has nothing in hand; and after the chosen ones decreed he showed this moda'a and said he does not wish to accept except what the congregation's judge rules in Torah law—since he accepted upon himself with a cherem, it is forbidden for him to overturn their judgment.
