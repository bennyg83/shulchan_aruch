He said, "Wine is upon me as a korban because it is bad for the stomach." They said to him, "Behold, aged wine is good for the stomach." He said, "Had I known, I would not have vowed." And even if he said, "Had I known I would have said the new is forbidden and the aged permitted"—he is permitted regarding aged and new. But if he said, "Had I known I would have said all wines are forbidden to me except the aged"—he is permitted only regarding the aged. And similarly in all comparable cases.
