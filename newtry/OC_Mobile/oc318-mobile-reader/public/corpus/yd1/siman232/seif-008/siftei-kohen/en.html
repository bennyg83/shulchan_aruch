When he changed his wording, etc. In that case the force of the vow is weakened. If he neither changes nor maintains his original words, but simply says upon approaching and seeing his father and brothers, 'Had I known that my father and brothers were among them, I would not have vowed,' then all the more so the entire vow is void.
