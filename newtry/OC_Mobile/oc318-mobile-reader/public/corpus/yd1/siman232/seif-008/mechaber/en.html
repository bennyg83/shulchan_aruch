When is this stated? When he changed his words—that he first said, "All of you are forbidden," and finally said, "Had I known my father was among you I would have said, 'So-and-so and so-and-so are forbidden and father is permitted';" or when he first said, when he saw them from afar and did not recognize them, "They shall be forbidden to this one and that one," and afterward changed his words to say, "Had I known my father was among you I would have said, 'All of you are forbidden except father.'" But if at the time of the prohibition he said, "All of you are forbidden," and also at the time of retraction he says, "I would have said all of you are forbidden except father;" or if at the time of prohibition he said, "They shall be forbidden to this one and that one," and at the time of retraction he says, "I would have said so-and-so and so-and-so are forbidden and father is permitted"—since he did not change the language of the vow between the time of prohibition and the time of retraction, the others are not released.
