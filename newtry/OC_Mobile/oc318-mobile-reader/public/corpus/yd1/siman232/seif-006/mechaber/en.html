What are vows made in error? He said, "Konam is this loaf upon me if I ate and drank," and he remembered that he ate and drank; or "if I eat and drink," and he forgot and ate and drank—since he was mistaken at the time of uttering the vow from his mouth or at the time when it was to take effect, and if he had known the matter he would not have vowed—it is not a vow. And similarly if he said, "Konam, my wife shall not benefit from me that she stole my purse," or "that she struck my son," and it was found that she did not steal and did not strike. And similarly if he said, "Konam I shall not marry so-and-so because she is leprous," and she was beautiful at the time of the vow, so that if he had known she was comely he would not have vowed; but if she was leprous and became beautiful, the vow takes effect. {Rama: And similarly one who says, "If so-and-so were present at this matter I would give a thousand zuz to charity," and he was present—this is a vow in error. (Mahariv in Dinim ve-Halakhot siman 59)}
