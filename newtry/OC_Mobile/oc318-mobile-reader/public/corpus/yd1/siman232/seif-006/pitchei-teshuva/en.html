My purse. Rosh Yosef follows the view that clear circumstantial proof is equivalent to explicit wording, unlike Taz.
It is an erroneous vow. Mishkenot Yaakov distinguishes a genuine mistake from a reckless wager based on mere suspicion. Where one knowingly entered a doubtful contest and pledged money to charity, the vow is binding; charity and consecration are not defeated by asmachta in such a case, especially where there was a formal kinyan and the money was deposited with a third party.
