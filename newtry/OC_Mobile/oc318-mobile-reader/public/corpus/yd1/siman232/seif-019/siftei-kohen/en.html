And they heard that he died. Even if he died or repented only after the vow, no release is necessary. This differs from the case above in seif 6, where an unattractive woman was beautified and the vow took effect. An unattractive woman is not ordinarily expected to become beautiful, so he did not intend her to become permitted if she later became beautiful. Here, however, an evil person may repent or die. We therefore assess that when he said, 'Her father is evil,' he meant only for as long as he remained evil. Thus the Beit Yosef writes above in the name of Rivash citing Rashba, and the Ran writes likewise. See what I wrote at the end of siman 229.
