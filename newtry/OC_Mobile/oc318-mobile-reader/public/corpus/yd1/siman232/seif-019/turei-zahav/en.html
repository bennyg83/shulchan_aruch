Like one who made his vow dependent upon, etc. The Perishah asked why the law of seif 6 was not included here. There is no difficulty. There, the circumstance upon which he made the vow dependent was false, such as his statement that she stole his purse, and it was therefore a mistaken vow. Here, the circumstance was true when the vow was made, but was later nullified. See what I wrote in seif 7 concerning, “She was ugly and became beautiful,” etc.
Who died. This does not mean that he died before the vow, for then it would be a mistake. Rather, he died after the person vowed and made the vow dependent upon him. The basis of the vow was then nullified, and the vow is void without release.
