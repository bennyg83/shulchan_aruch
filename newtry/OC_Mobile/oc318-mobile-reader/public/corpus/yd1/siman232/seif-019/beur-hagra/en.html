One who specified, etc. The Rosh writes that the halachah follows Rav Huna because Rabbi Yochanan was refuted. This is not conclusive, as the Rashbam writes in Bava Batra 53b in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel: since the Gemara says only 'this is difficult,' rather than 'it is refuted,' the opinion is not rejected. The Rif and Rosh write likewise at the beginning of chapter 4 of Berachot, as does the Aruch, and this is the view of the other authorities, unlike the Rashbam. Here, however, the ruling follows Rav Huna because the Yerushalmi likewise states his view, as the Ran and Rosh write. See the Rosh's rulings, whose version reads: 'and the Sages agree with him.'
