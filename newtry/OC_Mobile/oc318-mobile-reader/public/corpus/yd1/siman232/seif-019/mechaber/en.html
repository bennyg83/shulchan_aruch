One who specified at the time of his vow upon what he vowed—behold, his vow is attached to that matter; and if the matter for which he swore was not fulfilled, he is permitted. For example, if he said, "I will not marry so-and-so because her father is wicked," and they heard that he died or repented—he does not require annulment, for it is as if he specified that he vowed on this condition; and since it was voided, the vow was voided. (See above siman 220 seif 95 in the gloss.)
