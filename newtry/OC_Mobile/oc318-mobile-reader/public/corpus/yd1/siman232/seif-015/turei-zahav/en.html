There is no validity to that oath, etc. Although regarding vows to murderers and tax collectors in seif 14 we require him to think “today,” there the murderer and tax collector do not torture or beat the person. They merely wish to seize his fruit. Here, however, where there is torture and beatings without limit, the oath is entirely void. Even if they made him swear according to the understanding of the Omnipresent, blessed is He, it is ineffective. So it is written in Maharam’s responsum cited by the Beit Yosef.
