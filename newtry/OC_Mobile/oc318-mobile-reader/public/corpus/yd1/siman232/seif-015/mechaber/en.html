One who compelled his fellow and tortured him with various afflictions until he swore to give him such-and-such money—there is no substance to that oath, vow, or cherem. (And all the more so if he accepted a cherem or oath upon them under compulsion of death—it is nothing.) (So it is implied in Mordechai chapter HaNizakin, and similarly Maharadach bayit 10 responsum 17, and not like responsum of Mahariv siman 53 and Piskei Maharai siman 73.)
