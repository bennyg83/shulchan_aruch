Actual coercion. This applies even if he did not nullify the oath mentally. Taz and Shach. Mahari Weil limits the rule to coercion to give property; an oath not to reveal information remains effective.
Death. Shach hesitates to be lenient even under a clear threat of death, because severe coercion may cause a person to commit fully to the undertaking. Darkei Moshe therefore recommends formally annulling the cherem.
