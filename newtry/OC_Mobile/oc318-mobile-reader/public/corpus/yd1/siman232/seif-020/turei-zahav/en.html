Did you vow anything except to honor me, etc. Gemara page 63 asks without this reason would not be vow — would be vow of exhortation like seif 6; answers not exhortation — said to him I am not a dog benefiting from you not you from me; Rosh wrote since wants gift from him presumably also benefits him much; likewise end said as if received — Gemara said not a king benefiting you not you me; Rosh wrote presumably benefits much since demands great gift — before you this law specifically manner Rosh mentioned accustomed to benefit and likewise end accustomed to benefit him; though Tur and Shulchan Aruch did not mention — nothing missing — presumably all who vow thus accustomed as Rosh language and Levush omitted Gemara words made difficulty from logic and answered distinction receiving gift vs inviting to eat — not halachah per Gemara mentioned.
