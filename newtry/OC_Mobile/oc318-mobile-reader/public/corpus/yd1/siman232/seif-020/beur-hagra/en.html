And even if the person who imposed the vow is not, etc. See Ran, Nedarim 24a, s.v. klum, etc., and the Yerushalmi cited there, etc.
Some say specifically, etc.; and some say, etc.; one who said: Konam, etc. See Ran there, s.v. hareini, etc.
And every condition that is, etc. This belongs to the second opinion, according to which every condition qualifies even when its fulfillment does not require an affirmative act. See Ran there, 24b, s.v. ule'inyan halachah. This is the meaning of the words, 'and every,' etc.
