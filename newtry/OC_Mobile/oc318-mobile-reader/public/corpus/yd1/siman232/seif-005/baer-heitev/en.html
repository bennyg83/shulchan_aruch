But if he forbade himself only a few fruits; he is not believed unless he was under duress, as below seif 14. Shach:

And afterward he did that thing; some say forbidden since it could be he did not violate the condition; it appears what Rama wrote "some disagree" applies here too; if he said except meat all agree it is not a vain vow; all the more if he said except grain since grain includes the five species; end quote, Shach.
