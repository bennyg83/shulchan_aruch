If he said, "Konam are all the fruits in the world upon me"—it is not a vow, since it is impossible for him to be without all the fruits; it is an invalid vow (neder shav). And similarly if he said, "Konam are all the fruits in the world upon me if I did not do such-and-such a thing"—it is not a vow. {Rama: But if he said in future tense, "If I do such-and-such, all fruits shall be forbidden upon me"—some say it is forbidden. (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag and Tosafot chapter Ha-madir beginning of Ketuvot 71a and Hagahot Mahariach.) And similarly if he forbade upon himself all fruits except wheat—some say it is a vow. (Semag and Mordechai chapter 3 of Shevuot.) And there are those who disagree. (Teshuvot Maimuni end of Hilchot Nedarim)}
