He wrote a document against him. Noda BiYehudah and other responsa discuss whether written declarations without an orally uttered oath create a binding oath, and whether the obligated party is believed to explain that his commitment depended upon conditions not fulfilled. Monetary admission rules do not automatically govern questions of oath prohibition.
