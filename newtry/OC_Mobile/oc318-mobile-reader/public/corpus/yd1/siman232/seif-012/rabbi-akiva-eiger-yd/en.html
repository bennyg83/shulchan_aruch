What are coerced vows? Ribash distinguishes bodily coercion, coercion present when the vow takes effect through failure of a condition, and the principle that he never vowed with such circumstances in mind. Rabbi Akiva Eiger questions how the Gemara's proof works according to Ribash, because the Mishnah may instead rest upon the exclusion of a coerced person from the law of oaths.
