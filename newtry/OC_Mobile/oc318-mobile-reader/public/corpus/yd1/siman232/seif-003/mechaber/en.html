One who invites his fellow to eat with him and he refuses, and he forbids him if he does not eat with him; or who says to him, "Konam that I derive benefit from you if you do not lodge with me and eat warm bread and drink a cup of cold water," and he refuses—this is a vow of urging. But if Reuven invited Shimon to eat with him and they were also inviting him elsewhere and he wished to eat more with Reuven and said to him, "He forbade me from your property if I do not eat with you so that they will not press me in the other place"—this is a full vow. {Rama: And one may not derive a lesson from the laws of vows of urging to another place, for you have in them only what the Sages said. (Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham)}
