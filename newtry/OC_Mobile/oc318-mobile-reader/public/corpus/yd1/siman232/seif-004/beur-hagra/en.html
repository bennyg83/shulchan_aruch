Konam, this loaf, etc. As the Gemara states there concerning oaths. See Rosh there, s.v. i nima, etc.
Or a wall, etc. As stated at the beginning of chapter 7 of Chullin and at the beginning of chapter 2 of Tamid.
But if he maintains, etc. This is from the Yerushalmi cited by the Rosh there; see there. At first glance, the Yerushalmi implies that this applies specifically to an exaggerated oath, and the Tur indeed writes so in siman 239 regarding exaggerated oaths. Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, explains that the same applies to vows, as with vows intended merely to encourage.
