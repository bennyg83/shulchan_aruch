Vain vows, etc. Ran wrote two types of vain vows — one with exaggeration like those who left Egypt; one without exaggeration like snake with beams on back — also permitted because we assess his intent not to forbid — if so would attach prohibition to impossible condition; rather intended vain words; one who attaches fruit prohibition to exaggerated matter (meaning excluding without exaggeration — even certainly nonexistent permitted since still vain) — e.g. fruits forbidden by oath or konam if I did not see on road like those who left Egypt and did not see so many people to say exaggeration like Egypt — fruits forbidden; as Beit Yosef; I do not plumb his depth — is there limit; one may see hundred people and say exaggeration like Egypt; Tosefta Nedarim ch.2 vain vows: if I did not count on his Passover shank weighing ten litra, if I did not drink wine worth gold dinar — end quote; difficult — Ran means specifically when saw many people and upholds words to say with multitude I saw like Egypt — since fit to say exaggeration on multitude — intended exaggeration; but if did not see multitude intent truth fruits forbidden; no proof from Tosefta — there also exaggeration saying shank heavy counted and exaggerates ten litra weight and fine wine drank; unlike few people where exaggeration inapplicable; as Ran concludes; Mishnah Nedarim vain vows permitted saying if I did not see on this road like those who left Egypt — specifically when saw great number possible exaggeration like Egypt; otherwise speaks truth fruits should be forbidden — end quote.
