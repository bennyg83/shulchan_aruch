What are empty vows? Such as if he said, "Konam is this loaf upon me if I did not see the likes of the Egyptians," (or) "if I did not see a snake like the beam of an olive press," or "a wall reaching to the heavens"—(it is not a vow,) for such is the way of the world to exaggerate and he did not intend it as a vow. (And if he stands by his words that he said he intended it as a vow, it is a vow.) (Ta'anit 11, and similarly implied below in Tur siman 239 regarding an oath.)
