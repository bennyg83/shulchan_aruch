And similarly if he saw from afar people eating his fruits and did not recognize them and said, "They are upon you as a korban," and when he came near them he saw they were his father or brother, whom he did not wish to forbid upon them—it is not a vow, even though he did not specify, since the matter proves that a person does not forbid his fruits upon his father and brother. And even if strangers were found with them, since the vow is void regarding his father and brother, it is void also regarding others.
