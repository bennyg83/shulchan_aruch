Even though he did not specify, etc. Since he was mistaken about the very basis of the vow, for he never intended it to apply to his father, his mouth and heart were not aligned, and the vow is void on its own. Therefore, in the case of 'Konam, my wife,' he must state explicitly that she stole his purse. Otherwise, even if that was his motive, since he did intend to prohibit his wife there was no mistake in the essential vow. Ran.
