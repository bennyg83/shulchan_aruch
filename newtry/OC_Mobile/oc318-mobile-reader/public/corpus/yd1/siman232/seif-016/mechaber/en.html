One who betrothed his daughter and vowed to give with her a sum of money (and swore), and afterward the debts owed to him were ruined so that he has nothing with which to pay—not debts, lands, or movable property, except his dwelling house and utensils of use—he is exempt, for you have no greater compulsion than this. {Rama: Reuven and Shimon who lived in one city and both swore to marry their sons to one another, and afterward Reuven and his son left the city—Shimon is exempt and need not bring his daughter after him. Also Reuven is exempt if he needed to leave, for it is called compulsion. And similarly if the son swore and his father needed to leave and the son has no sustenance except his father. (Beit Yosef siman 228 274b in the name of Ramban siman 278.) And see in Choshen Mishpat siman 73 seif 8.}
