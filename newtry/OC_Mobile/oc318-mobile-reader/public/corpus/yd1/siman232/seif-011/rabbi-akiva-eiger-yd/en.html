(Ibid., seif 8) When he changed his words. The Rosh writes: It requires study in the case of a person who vowed regarding two things, meat and wine, neither of which was before him, and later found grounds for regret regarding one of them. Do we say that the entire vow is released, since no substitution is applicable here, or do we say that the entire vow is released only where one item is exchanged for another? This is cited by Beit Yosef here. See the Shach above, siman 229, seif katan 6, in the name of Rivash.
