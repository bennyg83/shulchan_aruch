Who vowed to a sick person. See Shevut Yaakov, Choshen Mishpat, end of siman 168, and Tiferet Yisrael to Peah 3:7.
If they did so because of the sick person's urging. Shevut Yaakov treats a promise to gather people for Torah study during the first year after a dying wife's death as a full vow made for her spiritual benefit. Annulment may therefore be unavailable without compelling justification. Since no number was specified, two participants suffice.
