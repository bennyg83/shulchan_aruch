A woman, etc. See Orach Chaim, siman 339, seif 4; and Choshen Mishpat, siman 254, seif 1.
(Supplement) A woman who vowed, etc. if they did, etc. Even though the mishnah does not discuss vows made under duress specifically — only where duress occurred afterwards — the mishnah speaks to a stringency; and certainly [the same applies] at the time of the vow. And as written in Nazir 11b: "and alternatively, in vows, etc." And he wrote: as for [the case of] Zedekiah — it was not on account of duress that he acted, but rather to honor him more. See siman 14. (End.)
