A woman, etc. See Orach Chaim 339:4 and Choshen Mishpat 254:1.
Gloss: A woman who vowed, etc.; if they acted, etc. Although the Mishnah discusses unavoidable vows only where the compulsion arose after the vow, it states that case to teach a greater novelty. Certainly, a vow made under compulsion from the outset is also excluded, as stated in Nazir 11b: 'Alternatively, regarding coerced vows,' etc. Tzidkiyahu, however, did not swear because of coercion, but in order to show greater honor to Nevuchadnetzar. See seif 14. End of gloss.
