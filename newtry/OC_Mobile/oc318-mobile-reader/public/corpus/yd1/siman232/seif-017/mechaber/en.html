One who swore to dwell in his father-in-law's place—if they do not detain him, it is not compulsion if he or others on his behalf circled and threatened him not to go to his father-in-law's place. {Rama: A woman who vowed to her husband in his illness not to marry after his death, or the reverse (R' David Cohen bayit 17 and in Ramban siman 40); or one who vowed to a sick person something—if they did this because of the sick person's pressing so that he not lose his mind over him, it is like a vow under compulsion; but if he did not do so for this reason but for another matter, it is like a vow according to his fellow's intent. (There.) And see above siman 228.}
