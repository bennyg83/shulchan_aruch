(7) [Seif 3] If punctured around its circumference thin punctures, etc. Meaning specifically punctured around its circumference exactly; but punctures without deficiency along the length of the kaneh do not combine to the majority of the circumference — for it would only be as split; therefore punctures without deficiency never disqualify except when standing on the gargeret's circumference in its majority. Shach s.k. 5. Levush ot 5, Zivchei Tzedek ot 8, Bayit Yitzchak ot 4.
