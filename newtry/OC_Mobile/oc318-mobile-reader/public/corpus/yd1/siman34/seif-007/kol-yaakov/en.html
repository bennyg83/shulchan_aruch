G) Seif 3. One should not do so except one who, etc. I wrote in my humility after seeing all acharonim' words — truth of the matter: Maran did not write one may place them only one established in piety, but per poskim' words it is only a safek; and since the ruling spread like Rashi and Rambam — one who comes to be concerned from Rabbeinu Tam's safek must be renowned in piety — not so now that our rabbi the Ari z"l revealed both are required, even if not renowned in piety one may place them, for his soul desires to fulfill the mitzvah properly; and already in the land of the deer many years most sages and their students place two pairs, and also baalei mikra and mishnah — some place two pairs. Machberet ot 2, Bayit Yitzchak there ot 3.
