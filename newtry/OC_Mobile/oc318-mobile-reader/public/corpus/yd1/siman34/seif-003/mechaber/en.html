If punctured around its circumference with thin punctures adjacent to one another like the punctures of a sieve — if they have no deficiency they combine to a majority of its cavity; and if they have deficiency they combine to an issur — that if there is an issur in the punctures together with the intact portion between them — forbidden; and this is when the punctures are so close that there is not between puncture and puncture a full puncture; but if there is between them more than this measure — what is between them does not combine.
