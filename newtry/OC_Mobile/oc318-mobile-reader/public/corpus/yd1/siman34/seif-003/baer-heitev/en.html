And Shach explains: specifically that it was punctured around its circumference exactly like this; but punctures that have no deficiency along the length of the kaneh do not combine to the majority of the circumference — for it would only be as though it split; therefore punctures without deficiency never disqualify except when they stand on the circumference of the gargeret in its majority; but punctures with deficiency apply only when standing in the middle or along the length of the gargeret — for on the circumference, even without deficiency, they disqualify. And therefore the Mechaber's wording is ambiguous: he opened "if punctured around its circumference — if they have no deficiency," etc., and "if they have deficiency," etc. — implying that with deficiency also refers to around the circumference — and this is not so; and it must be said thus: if they have no deficiency — then it is not treifah on a slight circumference except we require that the kaneh encircle it in its majority; but if there is deficiency — if there is an issur in the punctures — treifah even though they do not encircle the majority but some punctures stand on the circumference and some along the length — for it is possible when there are many punctures the size of an issur that they encircle a little of the kaneh — end of his words.
