The intact portion between them — Shulchan Aruch's view: in punctures without deficiency the intact portion between them does not combine — not like Ran; and Shach wrote thus is the view of Rashi, Rashba, Tur, Rosh; and Peri Chadash wishes to be stringent like Ran; and one should not reject Shulchan Aruch's words, especially since for Rambam the intact portion does not combine — meaning in punctures with deficiency; and it appears this is Rif's view; and therefore he explains the question on a bird refers to the mishnah: a puncture deficient like an issur — and uproots the question from its place stated on knafah puncture and attaches it elsewhere on that mishnah; and this is the reason he holds: if knafah puncture — how does he say "one folds it and places it," etc. — behold there is intact portion between them and there is no covering to render treifah, for the intact portion does not combine; and therefore Rif explains it on that mishnah: deficient in one like an issur — and for this "one folds it" applies well, for there is no intact portion inside; and covering of the kaneh testifies how much was deficient — examine.
Forbidden — and we say in the gemara: an issur is like more than an issur; and the gemara inferred: "and ad" is not included; and therefore all ruled a narrow issur is treifah and does not permit except when less than an issur; and nevertheless Rambam in explanation of the mishnah wrote explicitly a narrow issur is kosher; and Peri Chadash already wondered; and one must force that Rambam explains the gemara thus: the issur stated in the mishnah does not refer to this issur that is kosher — only the mishnah's intent teaching "like an issur" means more than an issur itself is kosher; and the gemara inferred: therefore he holds "ad" and "ad" are not included — for if in Hillel's view how can one say the mishnah teaching "until how much may it be deficient — until an issur" means more than an issur, and "ad" is included? If so, much would enter — everything is included in "more"; and there is no limited measure; or "ad" is not included; and if so, whatever is more than an issur is not in the leniency and nothing is permitted except a narrow issur — and these are Rambam's words — and understand.
