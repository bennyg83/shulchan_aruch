**And if they have deficiency,** etc. — **that if there is an issur in the punctures with the intact portion between them,** etc.From that he wrote regarding punctures with deficiency — "if there is an issur in the punctures with the intact portion between them," etc. — and did not likewise write at the beginning regarding punctures without deficiency that the intact portion between them also combines to a majority — implies he holds in punctures without deficiency the intact portion does not combine even if there is not between puncture and puncture a full puncture; and it also implies from the Mechaber's words there is no distinction between animal and bird regarding combining the intact portion — only in punctures with deficiency: even in an animal the intact portion combines to an issur when there is not between puncture and puncture a full puncture; and when there is no deficiency — even in a bird the intact portion does not combine to the majority of its gargeret's circumference; and so I proved in my books that this is the view of Rashi, Rashba, Rosh, Tur, and Rabbeinu Yerucham.
**If punctured around its circumference,** etc.Meaning specifically punctured around the circumference exactly like this; but punctures without deficiency along the length of the kaneh do not combine to the majority of the circumference — for it would only be as though split; therefore punctures without deficiency never disqualify except when standing on the circumference of the gargeret in its majority — so too Rosh: punctures without deficiency combine to a majority if standing on the circumference of the gargeret — end; but punctures with deficiency apply only when standing in the middle or along the length of the gargeret — for on the circumference, even without deficiency, they disqualify, similar to severance; and we derive nothing from "if they have deficiency." And therefore the Mechaber's wording is ambiguous: he opened "if punctured around its circumference — if they have no deficiency," etc., and "if they have deficiency," etc. — implying with deficiency also refers to around the circumference; and Tur's wording is more ambiguous: he wrote "and if punctured around its circumference thin punctures adjacent to one another like sieve punctures — if they have deficiency they combine to an issur — that if there is an issur in the punctures with the intact portion between them — treifah; and if they have no deficiency — the measure is a majority" — until here his language; and therefore it must be said thus: "and if punctured around its circumference" — then the law is thus: if there is an issur in the punctures, etc. — treifah even though they do not encircle the majority but some punctures stand on the circumference and some along the length — for it is possible when there are many punctures the size of an issur that they encircle a little of the kaneh; and if they have no deficiency — then it is not treifah on a slight circumference except we require encircling the kaneh in its majority; and so interpret the Mechaber's words — and understand.
