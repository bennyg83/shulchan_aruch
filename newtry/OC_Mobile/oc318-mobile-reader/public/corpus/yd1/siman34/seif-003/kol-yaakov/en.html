C) There: "And for shin-dalet in the third house," etc. Nevertheless one must write them in the order they are written in the Torah; and if he changed — invalid, whether shin-yud or shin-dalet; therefore in shin-yud, which are on one parchment, one writes Kadesh and V'haya ki y'viacha and leaves part of a parshah, and V'haya im shamoa, and writes parshat Shema, and afterward writes parshat V'haya im shamoa. Tur and Beit Yosef, Magen Avraham s.k. 2, Taz, Raz Siman ot 3, and so acharonim; and see our words above siman 32 ot 169.
