H) They were accustomed to wear Rabbeinu Tam tefillin at minchah per writings of Maharachav z"l; and some kabbalists of our generation in Hendzin hold otherwise — for according to their secret Rabbeinu Tam tefillin alone do not fit; nevertheless since explicitly stated the Ari z"l practiced thus before making Shmusha Rabbah tefillin, and Maharachav practiced thus — certainly their reason stands with them and we do not know. Berkei Yosef ot 2.
