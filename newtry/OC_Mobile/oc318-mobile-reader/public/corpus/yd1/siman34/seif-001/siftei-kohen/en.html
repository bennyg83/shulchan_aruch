If the gargeret was severed — the majority of its cavity. — The wall of the kaneh is thick from above and at the side of the neck it is only a thin membrane, and it is not forbidden until the majority of the cavity is severed; and the thickness of the wall of the kaneh does not complete to a majority — so it is in the Talmud and the poskim.
Or above this. — Meaning above the place fit for slaughter as well — the law is thus that the gargeret is severed in its majority; and that which was stated above in siman 24 seif 15 and Taz on ikur — that even if only a minority remains connected in one place it is kosher — there we deal where the sign was not uprooted except from its place of connection in the jaw; but here, since the defect is in the body of the sign itself — in a majority it is treifah; but below the place fit for slaughter will be explained in this siman seif 8 that in any amount it is treifah.
It is forbidden. — Bach's view: for Rambam, if the gargeret was severed in its majority and was not slaughtered — it is only treifah from life; and when slaughtered it becomes nevelah; and this is as stated in siman 33 s.k. 4 — see there.
