(1) [Seif 1] If the gargeret was severed — the majority of its cavity, etc. The wall of the kaneh is thick from above and at the side of the neck it is only a thin membrane, and it is not forbidden until the majority of the cavity is severed; and the thickness of the wall of the kaneh does not complete to a majority — so in the Talmud; and Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash ot 1, Levush ot 1, Bayit Chadash ot 1; and likewise if severed from below upward so there is a majority of its cavity — even though the majority of thickness remains — treifah. Peri Chadash there. Zivchei Tzedek ot 1; however in the large ring the majority of the circumference is the majority of the cavity. Rashdam. Zivchei Tzedek there; and he meant because all is thick and not like the kaneh at the side of the neck which is thin; and see above siman 21 ot 1.
