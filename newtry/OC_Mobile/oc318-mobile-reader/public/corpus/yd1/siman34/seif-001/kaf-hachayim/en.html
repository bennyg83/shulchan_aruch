(1) [Seif 1] If the gargeret was severed — the majority of its cavity, etc. The wall of the kaneh is thick from above and at the side of the neck it is only a thin membrane, and it is not forbidden until the majority of the cavity is severed; and the thickness of the wall of the kaneh does not complete to a majority — so in the Talmud; and Shach s.k. 1. Peri Chadash ot 1, Levush ot 1, Bayit Chadash ot 1; and likewise if severed from below upward so there is a majority of its cavity — even though the majority of thickness remains — treifah. Peri Chadash there. Zivchei Tzedek ot 1; however in the large ring the majority of the circumference is the majority of the cavity. Rashdam. Zivchei Tzedek there; and he meant because all is thick and not like the kaneh at the side of the neck which is thin; and see above siman 21 ot 1.
(2) Incident that they brought before us a chicken after slaughter and saw in the kaneh below the place they slaughtered one place where the gargeret was removed — meaning the hard ring — and nothing remained except the membrane connecting them — the soft membrane before the rings, called kaneh as above siman 21 ot 1; and that membrane was whole on every side with no defect; and it appeared so from the beginning of its creation; and we declared it kosher as Maran z"l wrote we require majority of its cavity — and here the entire cavity was intact; and as Tur z"l wrote: if the majority of its cavity was not severed — even if it is majority of circumference, etc. — kosher — see there; and although the entire ring was missing — it matters not, for halachah is as those who say majority of its cavity; and we permitted publicly. Zivchei Tzedek ot 1.
(3) There. Or above this, etc. Meaning above the place fit for slaughter as well — the law is thus that the gargeret is severed in its majority; but below the place fit for slaughter — in any amount — treifah, as below seif 8. Beit Yosef. Taz s.k. 1. Shach s.k. 2. Peri Chadash ot 2, Levush ot 2 and ot 3, Bayit Chadash ot 2, Sha'arei Tzedek ot 2, Zivchei Tzedek ot 2, Bayit Yitzchak ot 1; and see above siman 20 seif 1 what place is called below the measure of slaughter in bird and animal — see there.
