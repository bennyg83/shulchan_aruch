**And one places it on the opening of the cavity of the kaneh,** etc.Implies the intact portion between them also combines when there is not between puncture and puncture a full puncture; nevertheless this is specifically in punctures with deficiency — in an animal we estimate as an issur and in a bird by if it covers; but in punctures without deficiency — then the intact portion between them does not combine, as stated in s.k. 6; and we estimate also in a bird by the majority of the circumference of its kaneh.
**And some say we estimate the large,** etc.Regarding knafah puncture all agree we estimate by if it covers — for when the large is estimated according to its size, etc., and the intact punctured portion is considered — its measure of deficiency would be very small, as in s.k. 6; but in deficiency in one place — always we estimate the large according to its size and the small according to its smallness; and it is explained in the Rosh's words that likewise everywhere we estimate in an animal — such as deficiency of the skull as a sela in siman 30, and likewise removal of the liver where we require two olives remain in an animal as below siman 41 seif 2 — we estimate in a bird according to the proportion of the measure in an animal (and we deal with a bird that was slaughtered on the ground and checked and found the brain membrane was not punctured — nevertheless if the puncture is so large according to the proportion of the measure of a sela in an animal — treifah; and see siman 30 and examine).
**In a bird, if punctured,** etc.Meaning punctures with deficiency — whose measure in an animal is like an issur, and in a bird there is not in its gargeret like an issur; and if we estimate according to the proportion of the measure of an issur in an animal — its measure of deficiency would be very small; and when the intact portion between them also combines — there would be a great stringency; therefore its measure is "if it covers," etc.; but in punctures without deficiency — since we require they stand on the circumference of the gargeret — there is no distinction between animal and bird, for always each one combines the majority of its gargeret's circumference — so Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham.
