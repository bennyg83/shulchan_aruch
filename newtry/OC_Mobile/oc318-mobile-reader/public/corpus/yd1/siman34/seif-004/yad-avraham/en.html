(Seif 4 in hagahah) And if a gargeret of a bird was deficient — some say we estimate like knafah puncture (Rif and Rambam). I have proof for their words from the Yerushalmi chapter 3 of Niddah: R' Zeira in name of Shmuel — an incident came before R' Yosi: one year it was punctured a full needle; and he answered: do we not know which is its majority? — end; and hence they were doubtful whether we estimate puncture by proportion of issur measure in an animal; and it is possible to estimate; and he answered there is no concern — for by its majority we estimate, and this we know — and understand.
