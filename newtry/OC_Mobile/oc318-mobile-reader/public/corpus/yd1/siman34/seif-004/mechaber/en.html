In a bird, if punctured knafah-style — one cuts the punctured place and the wing and places it on the opening of the cavity of the kaneh; if there is enough to cover the majority of the kaneh — forbidden. {Rama: And if a gargeret of a bird was deficient — some say we estimate like a punctured knafah (Rif and Rambam); and some say we estimate the large according to its size and the small according to its smallness, proportionate to the measure that is in an animal as an issur (Rashi and Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham).}
