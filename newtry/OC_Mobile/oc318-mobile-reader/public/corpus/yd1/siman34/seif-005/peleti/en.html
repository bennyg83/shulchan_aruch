Hanging and standing, etc. — this is Rashi; but Rif explains as stated in the next seif — open puncture; and if so one wonders on the Mechaber who brought two views together — if this is not that; and it must be said he adopts Rashi as primary — open puncture and issur in its thickness like Rif; one who remembers my name was not mentioned in the mishnah at all; and therefore he ruled like Rashi — since the indentation still stands we require a spacious issur; but this open puncture — no reason to forbid, for thickness of issur was not mentioned; and nevertheless he is concerned for Rif's words since they emerged from his mouth, and an unattributed ruling also to forbid; but the essence is Rashi's explanation; and therefore he permits when the opening hangs and stands — it is forbidden only when a spacious issur enters and exits. And it appears Rif follows his method that he holds the bird's question refers to deficiency like an issur — impossible to estimate in a bird as an issur, as above; and the Sages gave another measure to cover the majority of the cavity of the kaneh; and still difficult in indentation like a door in a bird — what? — so that an issur enters spaciously — behold in a bird there is no measure of issur at all; therefore Rif explains open puncture so that an issur enters in its thickness — and this is possible even in a bird that issur-thickness exists in reality in a bird like an animal; and to my humble opinion — unlike Rashi who explains in a bird on knafah puncture, but in issur-deficiency we estimate by proportion as the Rosh wrote; if so likewise in indentation like a door we estimate by proportion — and understand. And in truth one must understand: why in all matters we estimate in animal and bird everything according to large and small, and here in issur-thickness in indentation like a door animal and bird would be equal in measure as implied from poskim and Shach; therefore Rif and Rambam considered this measure said regarding a bird — and from this the opposite: in an animal more is required and the proportion is inverted — examine.
