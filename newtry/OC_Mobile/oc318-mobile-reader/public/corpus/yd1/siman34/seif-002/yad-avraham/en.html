(Siman 34 seif 2) We are not expert in the measure of an issur; therefore we estimate it by the majority of the width of the cavity of the kaneh. Taz and Shach conclude the measure of an issur is more than the majority of the cavity of the kaneh in an animal — to resolve Rama's difficulty. And Shach's words are difficult — for in s.k. 7 he brought the Rosh's and Rabbeinu Yerucham's words that prove explicitly the opposite — that the measure of an issur is less than the majority of the cavity in an animal; for they wrote in the bird's question: many punctures and he asks — in an animal its measure is like an issur, and in a bird there is not in its gargeret like an issur; and if we estimate according to the proportion of the measure in an animal — its measure of deficiency would be very small; and if the intact portion combines — there would be a great stringency; therefore we estimate by covering the majority of its kaneh's cavity — end. And if you say the measure of an issur in an animal is more than its majority of cavity — then according to the proportion of this measure in a bird also it is more than the majority of its kaneh's cavity — and how did they write that if estimated by proportion of issur in animal its deficiency measure would be very small? Proven explicitly the measure of an issur in an animal is less than the majority of its cavity; and if so by proportion in a bird also it is smaller than the majority of its kaneh's cavity; therefore we estimate by covering the majority of its kaneh's cavity, which is less than its deficiency measure by proportion. And in one puncture we estimate by proportion which is less than this, as Rama hagahah s.k. 4 in name of "some say." And Shach did not notice this; and what Shach wrote in s.k. 4 that so it is proven in Rabbeinu Yerucham that the measure of an issur is more than a majority — there is no such thing, for he wrote explicitly as stated — and examine.
