Therefore we estimate, etc. For even in a bird it is kosher — all the more so in an animal whose life-force is greater.
And according to what, etc. Meaning in Shulchan Aruch regarding a bird "if," etc., and in the hagahah he wrote "and if," etc., and "some say we estimate," etc. — and this is the view of the Rosh, who wrote: in the bird's question what is the difficulty? Only regarding knafah — for if we estimate it also according to its size, it would disqualify with a little; hence an issur is less than a majority.
