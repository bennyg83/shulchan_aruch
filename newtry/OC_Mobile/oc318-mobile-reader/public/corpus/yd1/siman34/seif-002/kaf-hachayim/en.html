(6) [Seif 2] If punctured with a puncture that has deficiency like an issur, etc. Hagahah: and according to what will be explained shortly, etc. Therefore it appears he meant the majority of the width of a bird's kaneh's cavity, etc.; see Taz s.k. 3 and Shach s.k. 4 who disagree with our rabbi's words cited and hold we estimate by the majority of the width of an animal's kaneh's cavity — see there; but Turei Zahav ot 2 agreed with our rabbi z"l that we estimate in a bird's kaneh — see there; and Bayit Yitzchak ot 5, Pri Megadim siman 8 ot 7 agreed. Turei Zahav siman 38 ot 6; and Turei Zahav ot 7 further wrote we estimate in a medium bird and goose — and similar is called medium — see there; and so Bayit Yitzchak in Amud HaMa'ariv ot 9, Pri Megadim there.
