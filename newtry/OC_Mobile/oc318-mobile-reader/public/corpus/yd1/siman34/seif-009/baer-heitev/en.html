And Shach wrote: it implies from Rama's words that if it can be attributed that because he slaughtered in two places it happened — we attribute even in deficiency; and even though above siman 24 seif 19 — if a ring is found on the knife, etc. — nevertheless we say every safek in slaughter is invalid; even if the ring was cut from one side — it must be said: perhaps here we deal where the ring was not lacking but two half-rings stuck to one another with the skin between them — for it is not the normal way that rings are cut in two around their circumference and connected together by the connecting skin; but certainly with the knife in the way of slaughter that went back and forth it happened, and through rolling of the gargeret they were cut in two around their circumference; or here we deal where the gargeret was lacking in a curve like this; or the skin between the rings was lacking; or when they stretch the kaneh the deficiency happens opposite the cut of the skin — as implied explicitly from Rashba's words that in such a case we attribute to the knife — end; since so it is, there is proof it happened from the knife; nevertheless with difficulty there is to permit in such a case — who knows to stretch properly.
