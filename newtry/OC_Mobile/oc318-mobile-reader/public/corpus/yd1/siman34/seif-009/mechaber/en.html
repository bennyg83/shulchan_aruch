If one found a gargeret that was severed or deficient and it is not known whether it happened while alive (and cannot attribute that it happened because of slaughter, that he slaughtered in two places) (Beit Yosef in name of innovations of Rashba) — one cuts it in another place and compares whether they are equal — kosher; and if not — forbidden; and we compare only ring to ring or bar to bar, but not ring to bar nor bar to ring. {Rama: And we are not expert in examination and there is to declare treifah in every matter; and all these laws of puncture and deficiency in gargeret apply only when it is known that it is from illness (Beit Yosef and Orach Chaim general rule 51); but if it happened from a thorn or needle — for us who are not expert in examining the esophagus — treifah in every matter, for we are concerned perhaps the esophagus was punctured, which is no less than if the neck were soiled with blood as explained above siman 33.}
