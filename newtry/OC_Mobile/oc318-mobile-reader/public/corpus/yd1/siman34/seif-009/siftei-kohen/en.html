**And cannot attribute that because of slaughter,** etc.Implies that if one can attribute that because he slaughtered in two places it happened — we attribute even in deficiency; and even though above siman 24 seif 19 — if a ring is found on the knife — nevertheless we say every safek in slaughter is invalid; even if the ring was cut from one side — it may be said: perhaps here we deal where the ring was not lacking but two half-rings stuck together with the skin between them — for it is not normal that rings are cut in two around their circumference and connected by connecting skin; but certainly with the knife in the way of slaughter it happened, and through rolling of the gargeret they were cut in two; or here we deal where the gargeret was lacking in a curve; or the skin between the rings was lacking; or when stretching the kaneh the deficiency happens opposite the skin cut — as Rashba states explicitly that in such a case we attribute to the knife — see there; since so it is, there is proof from the knife; nevertheless with difficulty there is to permit — who knows to stretch properly.
**Only ring to ring,** etc.Thus Tur: bar chulya is half a ring downward from where they begin to be thin; and some say chulya is the ring and bar chulya is the skin between the rings; and it is good to heed both views — end. And Rashi wrote chulya is three rings — so too Rosh.
**And we are not expert in examination.** Likewise regarding healthy lung in siman 36 seif 5 that we are not expert in such examination — see there. And in Sefer Efi Ravrevi he wrote: however if there is still some side to permit — one may be lenient through examination.
**When it is known that it is from illness.** Or that he grasped the kaneh alone in a manner he knows certainly he did not touch the esophagus — so Beit Yosef; and see end of siman 21 and siman 23 s.k. 15.
