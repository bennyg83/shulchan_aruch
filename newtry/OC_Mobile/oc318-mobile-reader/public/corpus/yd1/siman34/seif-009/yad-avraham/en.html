(Seif 9) Found a gargeret severed and unknown if from life, and cannot attribute that because of slaughter he slaughtered in two places. Implies if one can attribute it happened from slaughter in two places — we attribute and it is kosher; even though according to Rama's hagahah siman 23 that delay disqualifies in any amount in animal and bird; and likewise per the Mechaber end siman 21 that a bird's delay is minimal — if so impossible to permit slaughter in two places unless he knows it did not delay at all, as acharonim end siman 21; nevertheless here it seems permitted because of safek after slaughter it happened without slaughter in two places. (You may say: because he slaughtered in two places — perhaps after completing slaughter of most of the two signs he slaughtered again in the kaneh above or below; and furthermore this is extra stringency to disqualify for any delay, and in safek we are not stringent — and understand; and see acharonim siman 23 and end siman 21.) Shalom.
