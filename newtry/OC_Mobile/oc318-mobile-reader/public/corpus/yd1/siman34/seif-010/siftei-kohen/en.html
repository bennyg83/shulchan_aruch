**And one to the liver.** Meaning the large duct in the liver — and it is called the great hepatic duct; but the small ducts in the liver will be explained in siman 41; and likewise the small ducts in the lung in siman 36 seif 6.
**It is usual to have small thin punctures,** etc.And Beit Yosef wrote: I heard the reason that those veins are mouths of the ducts and are not punctures — end; and Darkei Moshe brings it; and hearing is not like seeing — for I saw with my eyes and it is simple; and therefore if there are punctures not in the place of the mouths of the ducts — treifah.
**One inclines to the heart.** And this is the large duct that goes out from it to the lung.
