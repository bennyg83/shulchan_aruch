The kaneh at its end, from where it enters under (the chest), splits and from there divides and becomes three heads — one inclines to the heart and one to the liver and one to the lung, and all three are treifah with any amount. {Rama: However, in the heart-kaneh, which is fat descending between the folds of the lung and the heart, it is usual to have small thin punctures — and these are their exits and are not called punctures — and kosher (Hagahot Ashiri chapter 1 and Rokeach and in Sha'arim sha'ar 31).}
