Human blood — if it separated from him it is forbidden because of mar'it ayin; therefore, if one bit bread with his teeth and blood came from his teeth onto the bread, he must scrape it off; but what is between the teeth — one sucks it out. {Rama: And all blood of fish and human blood — since by law they are permitted — do not forbid their mixture (Maimoniyot chapter 7 of Forbidden Foods in the name of Rashba, and he is in Tosafot HaRosh house 3 gate 5).}
