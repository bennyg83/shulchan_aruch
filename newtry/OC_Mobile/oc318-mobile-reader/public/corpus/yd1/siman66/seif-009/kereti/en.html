Permitted — and Rambam in chapter 3 of Forbidden Foods ruled that even without scales it is permitted; and Ra'avad and Maggid Mishneh already challenged and strained themselves greatly; and I elaborated in my novellae — but because it is lengthy I did not copy it; and what is closer is that Rambam holds: if he puts in the blood and afterward drinks it in this — there is concern for mar'it ayin and scales are required; but if he puts in the blood and drinks it himself in this — there is no mar'it ayin and scales are not required.
