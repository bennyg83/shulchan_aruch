(1) [Seif 1] Blood of beheimah, chayah, and fowl, etc.; and likewise blood of koy — one is liable. Kerisos 21a; Tur and Beit Yosef; Levush; Shach s.k. 1; Peri Chadash note 1. In Levush note 1 and this is for those who say it is a species in itself; but for us, who rule it is a doubt — chayah or beheimah — in any case one is liable for its blood.
