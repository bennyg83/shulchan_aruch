Chayah and fowl, etc. — their blood too is forbidden; only that which we say in Kerisos — an extra verse to forbid blood of koy — is according to those who say it is a species in itself; but according to what we rule, that it comes from beheimah and chayah — it is obvious it is forbidden, for whether chayah or beheimah blood is forbidden — so Peri Chadash; therefore Shulchan Aruch omitted it; and it is well written — only one may wonder why they wrote it regarding the hagahah:
