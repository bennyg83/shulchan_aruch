Pri Chadash s.k. 10 elaborated that there is doubt in every place blood is found — even on the chalmon, perhaps the halacha is like Ra'ah, etc. — and according to his words there is no certainty except when found on the knot of the chalmon and also on the chelevon, when all forbid; and immediately after he wrote (and Be'er Heitev did not copy this) that Maharshal's leniency when found on the chalmon does not appear — see there, and his words contradict each other; and see Peri Megadim, who wrote that perhaps Shach retracted and holds the essential view is like the Geonim. And see Nachalat Azriel on Chullin daf 62, who explained his words: here the halacha is not like Maharshal, who is lenient on the chalmon because he holds the essential law is like Ra'ah; if so, one may be lenient b'dieved as truly Peri Chadash wrote (brought in Be'er Heitev of Mahari"t s.k. 3) — Shach wrote that all the later authorities hold not like him, only that chalmon is more severe; but in mixtures always be lenient because of doubt — see there. And what Be'er Heitev wrote in the name of Taz — a case that came before me, etc. — appears all this is according to his view below siman 98; but according to Shach there s.k. 8 it appears that not its type is forbidden; and so I found in the gloss of Yoreh De'ah of the Gaon R' Yaakov Berlin — but Peri Megadim wrote that Shach too may agree here — see there.
