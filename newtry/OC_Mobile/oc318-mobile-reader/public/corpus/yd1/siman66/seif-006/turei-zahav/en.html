Maharshal wrote: this is according to his view of Rashba; but according to what we rule below, that even roasted and hot into hot is considered cooking and forbids up to sixty — here too it forbids up to sixty, end of his words. And in siman 105 seif 9 I wrote Maharshal's conclusion that it is so; and it appears one should not be stringent here except if the blood is in a place that certainly forbids; but if found in a place that is not forbidden by law, such as on the chalmon not on the knot — one should not be so stringent here to forbid the hot food; and as Maharshal wrote below that he was lenient in other matters — all the more so here, where many hold that hot in hot does not forbid more than the measure of a peel, and the peel of an egg helps here, for a peel is not required:
