(Likkut) If found, etc. Rashi there 64b s.v. beitzim muzarot, etc.; and Rashba agreed and wrote that in them in every place one discards the blood and eats the rest (end of his words):
And some say, etc. This is the view of Rashi and the aforementioned Tosafot — that they do not distinguish; rather according to their view even in the chalmon it is forbidden only when found on the knot; only that Rosh and Rashba wrote one should be stringent like both views — and this is the 'some say' here:
And some are stringent, etc. A decree lest one find on the knot of the chalmon, which is forbidden d'oraisa, as explained above seif 2:
And from this spread, etc. Meaning even not on the knot — and see below. (Likkut) And from this spread, etc. But one may say for another reason that Ra'ah explained the opposite — in chelevon forbidden and chalmon permitted; and he wrote that this is what is stated there: Rav Geviha said to him, etc. — meaning Abaye is alone against everyone — see there where he elaborated; and so in Yesh Seder one should be stringent like his words — but it is forced; and also in the Jerusalem Talmud Tosafot brought there s.v. vehu, etc. — it is clear like Abaye's words — see there (end of his words):
