(3) Musk, which they call musk — it is from a chayah whose name is known, and it has a kind of hump on its neck where blood first collects like blood and afterward dries and becomes musk. Some say it is forbidden to eat because of concern for blood; some say permitted, for it is mere secretion; and even though its beginning was blood — afterward. Now we follow; and as Knesset HaGedolah elaborated in Hagahat Tur note 2 and concludes: for practical halacha the common people are accustomed to permit musk in eating, and daily practice is to throw musk into broth or sweets for good fragrance — see there. And likewise in the indexes at the end of Kol Bo Levi siman 106.
