And from this spread the custom; and the later authorities wrote that not for this reason but from the law, for they were concerned for Ra'ah and his supporters, who hold: found on chelevon forbidden, on chalmon permitted; and Peri Chadash established the essential law like Ra'ah, for naturalists testified that all embryo formation is in the chelevon, and the chalmon is sometimes found inside the embryo's crop — see there. And in truth it is difficult for me to say that all the Geonim, Rif, and Rambam, who were leading naturalists, erred in this; therefore it appears that all of Ra'ah's proof from 'we learned the opposite' and Abaye interpreted for us — meaning Abaye erred like the Geonim — but in truth we learned the opposite; but this is extremely forced, since the correct view is like nature the opposite — why would Abaye argue thus without reason; he would always be the one who disputes whoever formed, investigating if it is possible; how would Abaye err in something obvious and further reverse the early authorities; the Gemara should have said 'and Abaye, who interpreted for us.' But it certainly appears the truth is as Ra'ah said: the essence of formation is in the chelevon, and the chalmon does not promote or demote embryo formation at all; but the matter is that most blood found in an egg comes for embryo formation, and otherwise blood is not common there at all. Blood comes to the egg before formation began, and afterward from it formation begins; therefore when blood is found in the chelevon, where the essence of embryo formation is — who will say formation already began? Perhaps it has not yet begun; and even if you say it began — perhaps it was here and found there; and from where do we know 'he cast the flaw into the whole of it'? But when found in the chalmon — there is no place of formation at all, and to attribute that it was not formation blood at all is meaningless; therefore we say that l'chatchila the blood was in the chelevon and also caused embryo formation — 'he cast the flaw into the whole of it'; and afterward when formation finished in the chelevon and the blood already did its work in formation and 'cast the flaw into the whole of it' — even the blood descends from chelevon to chalmon in its short connection to the chelevon, for certainly the chalmon too assists in forming the offspring — otherwise what is its nature and function, and it was not created in vain; if so, it makes sense that initially they differed like Ra'ah's view, but Abaye understood as written and therefore 'Abaye interpreted for us' must be the opposite in the chalmon; but in the chelevon we do not rely on formation, for when formation came the blood turned to flesh; but in the chalmon it is a remainder of formation that was in the chelevon — if so, this is all the poskim's view; and according to our version in the Jerusalem Talmud it is likewise — chalmon is more severe than chelevon; and such is essential:
