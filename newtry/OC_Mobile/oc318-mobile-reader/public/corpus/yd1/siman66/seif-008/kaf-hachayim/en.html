(8) And Kol Bo wrote that when blood is found in an egg in the place that forbids it — even if it is a day-old egg it is forbidden, for the Sages did not distinguish. Beit Yosef; Torat Chesed general rule 62 end of din 1; and he wrote such is the custom — Shach s.k. 6; Pri Chadash note 6; and he concludes there — Shach: the later authorities' view appears that one should not distinguish; and it is implied he means that wherever blood is found in a day-old egg one should forbid. However Menachem Tziyoni on Torat Chesed there note 5 wrote: if found on the chelevon not at the place of the knot — one may permit in a day-old egg — not like Shach; and Beit Din — Pri Chadash there — wrote one may lean thus where there is financial loss in the matter.
