(2) A place where they place salt in nevelah blood, and if a person says to bring him salt made in permissibility from outside — it is forbidden in its visible form, such as to immerse an egg or radish in it; but permitted to put it in a cooked dish l'chatchila; and if there is something else in the salt along with the blood — it is 'this and this cause' and permitted even to eat the salt in its visible form; and all such cases we rely on a non-Jewish taste-tester.
