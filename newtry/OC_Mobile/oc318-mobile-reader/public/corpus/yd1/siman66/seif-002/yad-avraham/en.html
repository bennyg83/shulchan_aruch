(Siman 66 seif 2) Blood of eggs — if known that it is marukem ha'afroach one is liable; but if not known, etc. — the Sages forbade it. Meaning it is not forbidden from the Torah unless the form of the embryo is recognizable in this blood; but if the form of the embryo is not recognizable, even if found in the chalmon — according to Shulchan Aruch seif 3 the entire egg is forbidden because we are concerned for formation; nevertheless its prohibition is d'rabbanan — and this is the first resolution in Tosafot that Rosh in Perek 9 and Beit Yosef bring. And Tur's wording is slightly different: 'but if it is known that it is not marukem ha'afroach, etc. — forbidden by rabbinic words' — meaning in the chelevon according to Shulchan Aruch's view, that we are not concerned for formation there at all; nevertheless forbidden d'rabbanan. It is proven from this that in the chalmon, where we are concerned for formation, it is forbidden d'oraisa — and this is the second resolution in Tosafot and Rosh; only that Tur adopted the stringency of both — see there and in Beit Yosef. And I wrote this to exclude what Be'er HaGolah wrote — that Shulchan Aruch's words are like Tur's second resolution; and it is not so, for I already clarified the difference between them; and likewise Taz — Shulchan Aruch ruled like the first resolution in Tosafot that in every place its prohibition is d'rabbanan — requires further study.
