(4) Nevertheless civet, which is the sweat of the civet cat — it appears to me forbidden to all for eating, for one cannot say it dried, for it remains moist; and accordingly one must be careful not to eat civet or that it was put in foods; and likewise forbidden to smell it and recite a blessing, as Shaarei Dura wrote: everything forbidden in eating — one does not smell or bless on it; and Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahat Tur in Orach Chaim there note 8; and likewise in the indexes at the end of Kol Bo Levi there. And Beit Din — Shvut Yaakov in this siman at the aforementioned note 4. However, what is written that everything forbidden in eating — one does not smell or bless on it — is not the poskim's view; rather even if forbidden in eating, one may bless and smell it, since it is not forbidden in benefit — as we wrote in our words in Orach Chaim there note 23 — see there.
