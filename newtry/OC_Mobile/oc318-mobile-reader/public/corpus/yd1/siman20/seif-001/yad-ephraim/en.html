See Ba'er Heitev; and he wrote in Peri Megadim ch. 1 s.k. 1 regarding one who slaughtered a calf: he began close to the head itself from the trachea and slaughtered diagonally, and in that same diagonal he also slaughtered the esophagus, and they brought the head split in two—since it is fitting that the cut in the esophagus that lay in it was longer than the grasp of a hand in width, there is to validate—see there; and see in the responsum he brought in Peri Megadim and answered on it. And his view is that the crop is only the place attached to the cheeks themselves, and if something more is seen to the eyes it is permitted—see there; and see in Chokhmat Shlomo. And he wrote in Shevut Yaakov ch. 1 siman 48: a slaughterer who is left-handed and trains his hands to slaughter does not slaughter in the exact middle of the neck but from the sides—that is, diagonally—and nevertheless he cuts the simanim and does not touch the spinal column; and some people murmur about him that he should be removed—and he answered that even according to Tosafot who are stringent about slaughtering from the sides—that is when he also cuts the spinal column etc.—unlike here, where he slants sideways to slaughter diagonally and cuts only the simanim; Tosafot too agree it is permitted l'chatchila, and even if you say l'chatchila one should not do so, nevertheless one does not remove him for this—see there.
