The place of shechitah in the neck. In it are 4 seifim.
The place of shechitah in the neck is in the trachea on the side toward the head from hat slope downward—and it is before the hat begins to slope and rise—and that is, one must leave some of the wheat grains; and they are at the end of the trachea above, within the large ring, like two grains from the body of the cartilage (meaning: translation of badal ozn—the hardness in the ear cartilage) and they are called wheat grains; and if he slaughtered within the wheat grains and left any amount of them above, it is kosher, for behold he slaughtered from hat slope downward; and if he did not leave any of them but slaughtered above them, behold this is hagramah (meaning: hagramah is the knife's deviation outside the place of shechitah toward the head or toward the body) and it is invalid. {Rama: And l'chatchila one should slaughter below the large ring (Mordechai chapter Eizehu Mekoman and Mahariu laws of shechitah; see s.k. 1).} And its measure below is until the head of the wing of the lung when they inflate it and it rises upward until the place where its head reaches in the trachea—then that is the place itself that it had touched when the animal was alive and was grazing in its normal manner when it stretches its neck to graze, without forcing itself to stretch its neck excessively. {Rama: And in a bird, below in the trachea like the esophagus (Ran in the name of Ra'ah) (see s.k. 4 for the entire seif).}
