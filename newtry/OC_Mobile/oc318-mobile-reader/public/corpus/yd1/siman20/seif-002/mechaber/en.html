In the esophagus, the place begins from where when one cuts it, it contracts, until the place where hair begins and it starts to become perforated—if he slaughtered above this place, called the crop of the esophagus, or below this place, which is the beginning of the intestines, his slaughter is invalid. And the measure of the crop of the esophagus that is not fit for shechitah above—in animal and beast—is so that one can grasp it with two fingers. {Rama: And some say: the width of four fingers (Tur in the name of Rashi and Mahariu); and it was received from the ancients that in every animal and beast the measure is until the place where the animal's or beast's ear reaches when they bend it (gloss in Mahariu and Or Zaru'a in the name of some commentaries and Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari Chai).} And in a bird, everything is according to its size, large or small. {Rama: And there is no difference between a dove and other birds—for all of them their measure is equal (agreement of all poskim) (thus proved Darkei Moshe that yonah in the Gemara is a place-name, not a bird—to exclude Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari Chai).} And below until the zefek. {Rama: And a bird that has no zefek—until between the wings (Beit Yosef); and since we are not expert in these measures, it is proper to slaughter in the middle of the neck along its length, for then one exits doubt (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak).}
