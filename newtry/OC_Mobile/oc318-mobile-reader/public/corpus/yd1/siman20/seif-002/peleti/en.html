His slaughter is invalid—and so wherever it says invalid, it means nevelah; and Shach and Peri Chadash raised that certainly in the crop of the esophagus there is no nevelah, only it deals with one who did not yet slaughter it; and since this shechitah is not called shechitah, behold it is as if it died by itself—and obviously it is nevelah; therefore Peri Chadash raised: if he returned and slaughtered it afterward properly, this shechitah purifies immediately from nevelah and behold it is only treifah and not nevelah, for the crop is only treifah and shechitah purifies from nevelah—and this is correct and one should not doubt; see what I wrote above siman 33 at length on this—only what Peri To'ar wrote in s.k. 5, and so K'li Muor's view: if majority of the trachea was severed above in a place not fit for shechitah—it is only treifah; this matter tzarich iyun in my humble opinion, for besides what Peri Chadash already stood on—why did the Rambam not count among the 70 treifot a severed spinal column above the place of shechitah, as he counted perforation of the crop and esophagus, that it is only treifah and not nevelah, and he distinguished this from that? Also we say Chullin daf 19: he was derore a third and slaughtered a third—Rabbi Yehuda said kosher, for life departs with shechitah; and so this reasoning is said in several places; and it is difficult: if you say in severing the trachea not in the place of shechitah it is not nevelah but treifah—we learn life does not depart in that severing, for it is only treifah; if so, how does life depart in deroring a third and slaughtering a third, and how can shechitah be validated—for the third he slaughtered in the place of shechitah there is no majority, and it is kosher; and the third he slaughtered above the place of shechitah—there life does not depart, for above the place of shechitah it is only treifah; if so, from every side life does not depart at all, and in the Gemara they said life departs—how can shechitah be validated if he did not make an act of nevelah? Therefore it is clear: since majority of the spinal column was severed and majority is like the whole in all the Torah, it is as if removed and the spinal column was severed—and nevelah—and understand. And what the Rambam wrote in chapter 3 of laws of shechitah halakhah 22: spinal column severed, majority of its cavity in a place fit for shechitah—it is nevelah—K'li Muor wanted to infer from this that not in the place of shechitah is treifah; this inference is not a complete inference, for we establish that even in a place fit for shechitah we require majority of its cavity and majority of thickness does not suffice; and see Ramban in his Milchamot chapter 1 of Chullin in the sugya "one who slaughters within the ring"—see there; and this inference is not worth rejecting the aforementioned matters, whose foundation is in clear proof—and this is clear.
