(Siman 20 seif 3) And the same applies to one who slaughters from the nape. And without holding and he slaughtered the spinal column and simanim without flesh—there is to examine for practical halakhah whether it is treifah or nevelah from the reason of chaladah (slaughter by hiding), for in the sugya we say: Ze'ira said: we too taught etc.; Rabbi Yehuda said: because he inserts (machlid); Rava said: because he presses (dores). The one who says because he presses—what is the reason? The other says because he inserts. He tells you: chaladah—what is it? Like a weasel that returns to the foundations of houses. If so, for us in such a case without majority flesh, it depends on the dispute of Rabbi Yehuda and R. Chiya bar Abba: for Rabbi Yehuda it is chaladah and invalid in shechitah; for R. Chiya bar Abba with melikah and bringing it is only treifah from breaking the spinal column before the simanim—and see Semag; and it is below (siman 24 seif 9) one must be careful, etc.; and its foundation is from that which Rashi explained in Zevachim in "because he inserts"—it is explained he holds halakhah is like Rabbi Yehuda, or he is in doubt and it is nevelah. And nevertheless it appears in my humble opinion we establish like Rabbi Yehuda, for we say: the one who says because he presses holds melikah and bringing is invalid; and we establish melikah and bringing is kosher, as Rambam wrote (chapter 6 of laws of korbanot)—it is proven the reason is because he inserts; and even though we establish like Ze'ira, nevertheless we conclude that the mitzvah of melikah is without majority flesh, and therefore we need the reason of inserting. And see in Tosafot in the sugya who wrote two explanations in "because he inserts": for their first answer—this is with a knife that is not wide specifically; and for their second answer—it is chaladah because he slaughters the simanim from below upward. For both answers I wonder: if so, how do we challenge the one who says because he presses—why does the other not say because he inserts? For their first answer it is difficult: since he is in doubt about melikah and bringing invalid, he should have mentioned more the truth "because he presses," for invalid even with a wide knife. And for their second answer it is difficult: perhaps this one who says holds even with return, as the sons of R. Chiya hold above; if so, the reason of pressing is a decided matter, since he holds melikah and bringing invalid; but the reason of chaladah is not a decided matter, for with return of simanim that he slaughters the simanim properly there is no chaladah. And similarly I wonder on what Rashba wrote in chapter 2 (daf 30): they say in the house of Rav "under the skin" etc.; and they say in the house of Rav Rabbi Yehuda said because he inserts—it is resolved that under the skin is chaladah [and this is not like Tosafot's second answer above]; and he answered: Rabbi Yehuda is sharp, for there is no support for Ze'ira, for perhaps "because he inserts" means perhaps in truth under the skin is chaladah. But according to the truth that exists for Ze'ira there is no proof it is chaladah—see there [and at first glance it is wondrous: for even according to the conclusion that exists for Ze'ira, we conclude the mitzvah of melikah cuts the spinal column and spinal process without majority flesh; if so, the difficulty of "let a knife purify it from nevelah" still applies for me, and we need the reason because he inserts. One must say Rabbi Yehuda did not know this baraita of melikah without majority flesh, as Rashba wrote in the sugya that R. Yehuda and R. Elazar did not know this baraita. However I wonder: if so, for practical halakhah it should be ruled that under the skin is certainly chaladah, for according to the truth that the mitzvah of melikah is without majority flesh, if so one who performs melikah with a knife becomes impure in the tent, and a knife does not help to purify from nevelah—for it is certainly impossible to say that with a knife usually majority flesh is also cut; if so, how do we challenge in the sugya (daf 28a) "and if you say there is no shechitah for a bird from the Torah, let a knife help him" etc.—for according to what we establish like Ze'ira it is nevelah before reaching the simanim; therefore the challenge is from the fact that the mitzvah of melikah is without majority flesh and he performs melikah with a knife also without majority flesh. If so, we need according to the truth the reason of Rabbi Yehuda and Rava. And since we establish melikah and bringing is kosher, it is proven the reason is because he inserts, as said; if so, it is proven for practical halakhah that under the skin is chaladah—and tzarich iyun]—if so, what do they challenge: the one who says because he presses—why does the other not say because he inserts? For since according to him in truth it is invalid because he presses, he did not want to say because he inserts, lest it be inferred from him that under the skin is chaladah—and tzarich iyun.
