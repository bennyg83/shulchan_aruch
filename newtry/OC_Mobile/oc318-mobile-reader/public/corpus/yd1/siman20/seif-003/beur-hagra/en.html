Must, etc. From Tosafot there s.v. hashochet etc.
And that is, he returned, etc. Thus they inferred from the words of Tosafot cited above, who said a decree lest he not return; and if b'dieved kosher, the decree would not apply; and what the Gemara says "and if you would think specifically with return" etc.; and the mishnah there—with knowing he cut simanim before the spinal column; then return on the sides is not needed, unlike the nape where it is impossible; and what Shulchan Aruch wrote "and know" means: or he knew; and what he wrote "and the same applies to one who slaughters from the nape" refers only to return—Shach. But the main thing is as the view of teshuvah ha'arokhah and Ran: from the sides kosher even without return, as the sugya there; and he wrote in teshuvah ha'arokhah there that even l'chatchila it is permitted even without return, even though he also cuts the spinal column—nevertheless it does not become treifah from this; and he did not mention b'dieved except because of melikah, for even b'dieved it is invalid; but Ran and Rambam wrote b'dieved specifically—see there; and Rashi wrote as the words of teshuvah ha'arokhah.
