Specifically, etc. See Rashi 63a s.v. 'what does "he took" mean,' etc. — but all the Geonim wrote 'and what does "he took" mean,' etc., and they read: 'Rava said: we deal with one who comes to incur a debt in lieu of them'; and as R' Eliezer taught, etc.; and Rashba explained: that wine is also forbidden only when he [the seller] does not have [it], unless he came initially with a loan — as written there: 'for example, if he was a creditor,' etc.; and even regarding wine — specifically [when] he is a creditor — not like Rashi; and this is what is written: 'and what does "he took" mean,' etc. — and he said: 'one who comes to incur a debt,' etc. — even if he came initially with coins [paying cash], if he recorded the price of the wheat as a loan against him and afterward designated wine in payment — it is also forbidden when he does not have it. And it must be said that according to Rashba's interpretation, what is written 'one who comes to incur a debt,' etc.; 'and as taught,' etc. — means: 'or as taught,' etc. — and these are two [different] interpretations; and on the second interpretation [the Gemara] objects: 'and what,' etc.; and see Nimukei Yosef there.
