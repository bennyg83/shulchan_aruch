One who set a price on produce, and it became more expensive at the time of repayment: he can appraise the produce that was set at money-value and give him other produce; but he cannot give money. And there is one who says that he can give money. {Rama: And he can set a price on the market rate for the other produce, as if he gives him money now; and this is specifically where he did not appraise the first produce as money-value, rather he says, “So-and-so many se’ah-measures of wheat I have with you; give me with them such-and-such wine or other item.” But if he appraised it as money-value, such that he says, “So-and-so is the value of the wheat; give me with it wine,” it is forbidden, for it is as if he lent him money, for we do not set a price on them when the money is not present except when he has [the money] (so the Beit Yosef inferred from the language of Ran and Nimukei Yosef), as was explained in siman 163.}
