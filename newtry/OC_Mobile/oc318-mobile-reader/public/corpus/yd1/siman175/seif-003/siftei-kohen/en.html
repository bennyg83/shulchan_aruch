He may fix a price. For gleanings — that he will give him from gleanings at the rate of gleanings.
But he may not fix a price with the owner of the house. That he will give him even from gleanings four for a sela, for presumably the owner of the house gives from superior produce; but it is permitted to fix a price with the owner of the house that he will give him three for a sela — and see Derishab.
