Gatherers. This differs from new and old grain, which are one species and expected to equalize. Gatherers' grain consistently has its own market price, so each may contract according to its own price. Taz.
With a homeowner. A homeowner who agrees to supply four seah for a sela is presumed to give superior grain, and therefore may not fulfill with gatherers' grain. He may contract to give three seah for a sela. Taz and Shach.
