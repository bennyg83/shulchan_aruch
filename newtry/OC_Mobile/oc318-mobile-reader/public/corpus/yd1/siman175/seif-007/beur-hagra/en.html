(Extract) And if he was agent, etc. Per Rashi there; but Rif, R. Chananel, and Geonim explained another matter: in Milchamot there; and so R. Chananel: this man gives money to his father-in-law or another person to buy merchandise for his father-in-law's house — it became cheap and giver wishes to retract and agent says not to retract — R. Pedat ruled Israel cannot retract until recipient of payment accepts; and he challenges: let it be resolved, etc. — cannot retract at all, for all agent did bridegroom accepts against his will; why need I sent you to repair, etc. — he has no right to stipulate conditions from his mind; and some say that case of one who bought without responsibility (Bava Batra 169 side 2) — he is negligent; and so Rashba in Geonim's method; but R. Nissim wrote: when agent acquired, not yet cheap; afterward cheap and bridegroom does not wish to accept at marriage time except at current rate; and he challenges: let it be resolved, etc. — from moment he took it he acquired it, agent of man is as himself, etc.; and per this possibly on essence of law it does not apply to Rashi (end).
