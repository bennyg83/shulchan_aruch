Do not challenge: behold this is close to profit and far from loss — for this reason we require siman 173 seif 13 he accept responsibility for cheap rate; resolved in Tosafot chapter Ribbit (folio 64 side 2): matter beginning "this is close to profit" — in fixing on fruits one cannot say close to profit, etc., since if seller wishes he eats those fruits and gives him others; and we do not require "he has" — only for permit; meaning at time of fixing; unlike that case siman 173 where known clear barrel he bought from him.
