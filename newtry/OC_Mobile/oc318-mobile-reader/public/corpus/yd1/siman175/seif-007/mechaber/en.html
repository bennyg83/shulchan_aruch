Once the rate is fixed, it is permitted to set [a price] according to the high rate. How so? If wheat was selling 4 se’ah-measures for a sela, and he set with him that he will give him wheat at the low rate: if afterward they rose [so that it is] 10 se’ah-measures for a sela, he gives him 10 se’ah-measures for a sela. If he gave him the money without specifying and did not set with him at the high rate, and they became cheaper, he gives him according to the rate that they were worth when he gave him the money. And one who retracted receives “mi she’para” [a curse for reneging]. But if he was an agent for others, he takes only according to the low rate, or he returns the money, and neither the agent nor the one who sent him receives “mi she’para.”
