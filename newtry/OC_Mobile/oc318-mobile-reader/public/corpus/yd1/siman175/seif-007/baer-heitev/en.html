The low price. This does not appear as interest, because had the buyer retained his money he could have purchased grain cheaply. Perishah.
Who paid. This refers to a transaction established only by words, without another kinyan, as in Choshen Mishpat 204 and 209. Taz explains that this is not considered close to profit and remote from loss, because the seller may consume these particular fruits and deliver others. This differs from siman 173, where a specific barrel was purchased.
