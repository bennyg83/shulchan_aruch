Cheap. And he did not want to be able to buy cheap grain. Retirement:
That he paid. This means that he did not obligate himself with another kinyan, only with words, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 204 and siman 209 seif 6; see there. The Taz writes: do not ask that this is close to profit and far from loss, and for that reason in siman 173 seif 13 we require that he accept upon himself responsibility for depreciation. Tosafot answer that with advance payment for produce, the concept of close to profit does not apply, since if the seller wants he may consume those fruits and give him others. This is unlike the case in siman 173, where he bought a known and defined barrel from him. See Choshen Mishpat siman 182 seif 6 and siman 208.
