In the city. Meaning a large city. The buyer may say that had he retained his money, he could have purchased cheaply. We therefore view him as having purchased the grain before it appreciated. Because this is a commercial transaction, the Sages did not decree even without meshichah. Taz.
Once it is established. If a market price is established in a metropolis, nearby towns may rely upon it when they obtain their supplies there. If they do not, one follows the price of the place from which they actually obtain supplies. Where several towns depend upon a city without a fixed price, one follows the larger supplying city. Beit Yosef.
Rabbinic. Since the arrangement is a sale, its prohibition is only rabbinic. Shach.
