And some say, etc. Per Tosafot folio 63 side 2 s.v. ufoskin, etc.; and we hold like Rava who is later; but first reasoning holds as Rashi explained there; and Rashba explained from what is stated we say: what is different — their value, etc.; and they fix, etc. — here as if he has money they fix at market rate — likewise if he brings other fruits they also fix as he could fix at their value; and this is what he wrote: what is different — their value, what is different — them; and per this Rava's matter does not apply to R. Yochanan's matter at all.
