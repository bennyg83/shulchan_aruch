A house that has many doorways—even if one is not accustomed to go out and come in except through one of them, one must make a mezuzah on every doorway. {Rama: Since they were made for entry and exit (implied in the Tur). A cellar that has a doorway from the street where they bring in wine in large barrels, and has a small doorway from the house through which they always enter and exit—if the small doorway is fit for a mezuzah, it is obligated and the large one exempt; and if the small one is not fit for a mezuzah, the large one is obligated and the small one exempt (Beit Yosef in the name of an Ashkenazic responsum, and it is in the writings of Maharil siman 99).}
