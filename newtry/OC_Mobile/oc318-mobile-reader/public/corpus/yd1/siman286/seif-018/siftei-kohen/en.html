If the small doorway is fit for a mezuzah. Meaning that it is ten handbreadths high and four handbreadths wide, as explained below in siman 287. Since people constantly enter and leave through the small doorway, it is the principal entrance. The large doorway is exempt because it is not regularly used for entering and leaving, but only occasionally, such as when wine barrels must be brought through it. This is not a fixed use, and the large doorway is subordinate to the small one. If the small opening lacks the minimum dimensions required for a mezuzah, however, then although it is used, it is merely like a hole, and the large opening is the principal doorway.
