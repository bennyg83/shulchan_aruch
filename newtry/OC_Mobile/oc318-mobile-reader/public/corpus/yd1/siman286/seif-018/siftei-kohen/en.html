If the small opening is suitable for a mezuzah. That it has a height of ten and a width of four, as below siman 287 — since they always enter and exit through that small opening, and that is the main use; and the large one is exempt because there is no main entry and exit there — only occasionally when one needs to bring in wine in barrels — and that is not a regular use; it is nullified relative to the small opening. But when the small one does not have the minimum measure suitable for a mezuzah — even though they use the small one, it is merely like a hole, and the large one is the main doorway.
