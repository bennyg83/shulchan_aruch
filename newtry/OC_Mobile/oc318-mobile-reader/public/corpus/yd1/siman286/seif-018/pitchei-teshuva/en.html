Thirty days. Re'em wrote: if he stayed in the house twenty-nine days and left and afterward returned — there is room to doubt whether on his return on the first day that day joins the twenty-nine preceding days and completes thirty days, and there is room to side on this. Rabbi Beit David Yoreh De'ah siman 146. And it seems to me requires study on what is written regarding tallit, that Nissim Gaon wrote in Hilchot Tzitzit, brought in Orach Chayim siman 14 seif 3 in gloss: if he returned it within thirty days and again took it — it does not join, for we require thirty consecutive days — see there; and now that responsum is not before me. And see in Nekudot HaKesef what I wrote: that exemption from mezuzah for thirty days means even if he wants to dwell many years he is not obligated to fix until after thirty — and not as implied from the siddur of the Gaon of Lisa; see there further whether if he afterward buys the house he blesses again.
