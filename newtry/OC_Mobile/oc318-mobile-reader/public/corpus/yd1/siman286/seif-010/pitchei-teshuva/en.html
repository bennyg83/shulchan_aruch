The shops in the market. See Yad HaKatanah on Rambam chapter 2 of Hilchot Mezuzah letter 21, who wondered about this: if the shop has some merchandise that is always placed there, it is truly like a treasury house — and even if he removes the merchandise at night and leaves nothing, nevertheless it is right to obligate it, as in a study hall where we say since they sit in it all day it is a dwelling; and he concluded that the Tur and Shulchan Aruch’s intention regarding shops is those made on market day in large cities, where the shops stand in the market only for the duration of the fair, and afterward they are dismantled or stand empty without any use — which is truly like a Sukkot booth during the festival. And the Gaon Rabbi Yaakov of Lisa wrote in his prayer book regarding shops in the house that have one doorpost from the ground to the lintel and one doorpost that does not reach the lintel but the wall is recessed about a cubit or more — if the doorpost that reaches the lintel is to the right of the entrance, one makes the mezuzah on that doorpost; and if the small doorpost is on the right of the entrant, if it is ten handbreadths high one makes the mezuzah on it, and if it is not ten handbreadths high one places it in the wide part — so it seems to me — end quote.
