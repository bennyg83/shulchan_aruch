In Tur he wrote: and so did R' Meir of Rothenburg — a mezuzah for a bathhouse; and he said when he would sleep there for a midday nap, an evil spirit would frighten him before he fixed a mezuzah in it. And Bach wrote: it is correct when he fixes a mezuzah on the doorway of the house and blesses upon it, his intention should also be on fixing the mezuzah in the bathhouse; and he should fix them one after another — first on the house doorway, then on the bathhouse doorway; and this blessing applies to both.
