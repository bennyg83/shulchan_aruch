Some say, etc. This is unlike a synagogue, discussed above in seif 3, which is not obligated in mezuzah unless it contains a dwelling. A study hall, however, resembles a dwelling because the students sit there from morning until evening.
But one should not recite a blessing over it. The Bach writes that when one affixes a mezuzah at the entrance of his house and recites the blessing, he should also intend for the mezuzah that he will affix at the entrance of the study hall. He should affix them consecutively, first at the entrance of the house and then at the entrance of the study hall, and the blessing will apply to both.
