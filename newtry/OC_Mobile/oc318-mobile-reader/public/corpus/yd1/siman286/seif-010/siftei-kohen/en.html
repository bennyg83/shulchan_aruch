[And some say, etc. And unlike a synagogue above seif 3 that is not obligated in mezuzah unless it has a dwelling house — a study hall, since the students sit in it from morning to evening, is comparable to a dwelling.
[But one does not bless over it. And Bach wrote: it is correct when he fixes a mezuzah at the house entrance and blesses over it, his intention should also be on fixing the mezuzah in the study hall; and he should fix them one after another — first at the house entrance and then at the study hall entrance; and this blessing applies to both.
