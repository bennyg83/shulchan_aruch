(Shach, seif katan 25) He should then affix it again without a blessing. Why should he affix it without a blessing? Even according to the view that the doorway was obligated from the outset, once the mezuzah was removed, replacing it should require a blessing. Perhaps the Shach follows the authorities in Orach Chaim 5 concerning one who removes a tallit intending to put it on immediately: because his intent is immediate return, it is considered as though he never removed it. The same may apply to a mezuzah removed with intent to replace it immediately.
