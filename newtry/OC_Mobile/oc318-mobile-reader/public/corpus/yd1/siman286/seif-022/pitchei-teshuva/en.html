One who rents a house. Rabbi Akiva Eiger rules that a rented storehouse for straw or wood requires a mezuzah, because the rejected minority view does not create a practical rabbinic doubt. He suggests, however, that cases where the Mechaber expressly records a lenient opinion may be treated differently for a renter.
Exempt from mezuzah for thirty days. Eshel Avraham and Rav Meshulam permit one who wishes to act stringently during the first thirty days to affix a mezuzah with a blessing, comparing a borrowed tallit.
Thirty days. Birkei Yosef questions whether twenty-nine days followed by departure and return combine. Pitchei Teshuva compares the rule of a borrowed tallit, which requires thirty consecutive days. The exemption applies even where the renter intends to remain for years, until the thirtieth day. He also discusses whether purchase of the house afterward requires a new blessing.
