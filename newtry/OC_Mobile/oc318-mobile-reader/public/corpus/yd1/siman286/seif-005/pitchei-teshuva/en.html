To cover it. Yad HaKetanah warns that covering is only for occasional unavoidable filth. One may not establish a permanent degrading use opposite the mezuzah and rely upon a cover.
To remain visible. Magen Avraham permits a glass cover with an additional covering. Chomot Yerushalayim argues that Taz agrees and objects only to glass alone. He questions whether intentional visibility weakens the effect of the partition, though Pitchei Teshuva finds the argument weak.
