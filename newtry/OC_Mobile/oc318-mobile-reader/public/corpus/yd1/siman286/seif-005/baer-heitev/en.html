To cover. Taz rules that fear of theft or disgrace is not a reason to abandon the mitzvah, since the mezuzah can be embedded or otherwise hidden from outside. Even where pigs are common, it may be placed in the upper third of the doorway.
Visible. Taz says the mezuzah should always have a cover, because repeated touching can erase the divine name. A glass cover allows the name to remain visible and also protects it in rooms with ordinary filth or bathing. In a bedroom used for marital relations, an opaque wax covering is required.
