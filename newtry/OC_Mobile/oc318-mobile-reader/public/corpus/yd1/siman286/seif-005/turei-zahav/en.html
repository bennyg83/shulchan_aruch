Such as where children are found. Beit Yosef, in the name of Semag, wrote: even at the house entrance in a place where the beginning of the upper third is above ten handbreadths, one may place it, and even though pigs are found there — end quote; and from what he wrote "it is good to cover it" — we derive that the essential mitzvah is not for it to be visible. From this I derived that those people who do not make a mezuzah on account of saying that thieves steal it from there and treat it with contempt — this does not save them, for they can place the mezuzah there in a manner that it will not be exposed at all from the outside, even against the name of Sha-dai — for that is at any rate better than nullifying the mitzvah entirely.
