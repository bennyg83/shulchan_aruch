It seems to me one should not make it visible without any cover at all, for from this follows transgression — each time they handle it on entry and exit a little of the name Sh-dai is erased until completely erased after much time, as we see in most houses; rather insert there a piece of glass, and thereby the name will be visible; and thereby is voided also the claim of those lenient in making a mezuzah because filth is found around that place sometimes or women bathe there casually — for with this it is covered even though visible; and proof from that which is in Orach Chayim siman 76: excrement in a lantern — permitted to read Shema facing it even though he sees it through its sides, because the Merciful One depends on cover and behold it is covered; and the same here conversely — since the name is covered there is no prohibition, for the matter does not depend on seeing; and a fortiori in what is above ten handbreadths, which is like another domain, as Semag wrote that Beit Yosef brought; and one would not need even glass cover — but this is the better side; and in a room where husband and wife have relations — cover the mezuzah with wax as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Semag in the name of Riv'a regarding if filth is found there; and so one should do in the aforementioned room, for here glass cover is not sufficient since there is marital relations there; and it is written "no nakedness of anything shall be seen in you" — as we say nakedness in a lantern forbidden to read Shema facing it — so it seems to me; and see what is written in siman 287 seif 2.
