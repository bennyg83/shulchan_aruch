One who rents a house. See responsum Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 66, who wrote: likewise one who rents a house of hay and wood is obligated in mezuzah, even though according to furnishings it would seem exempt, since a renter is exempt by Torah law it is a rabbinic doubt — perhaps the halacha is like Rambam that house of hay and wood is exempt; nevertheless since in Shulchan Aruch and acharonim they did not bring Rambam's view — to be concerned not to bless — therefore it seems his words are rejected from halacha and we do not equate it to a rabbinic doubt — see there; and per this it seems somewhat that those mentioned in Shulchan Aruch as the lenient view — such as a place where women lie in childbirth, and a gatehouse if no house opens to it, and a study hall in seif 10, and a house without doors in seif 15 — possibly exempt for a renter.
