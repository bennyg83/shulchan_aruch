Dividing it. It is explained in the Tur: that is, if it is in such a way that they are not separated from each other — if this one opens, the other opens as well; but where there is one large gate and next to it a small gate in the manner of the wealthy who do not want the large gate to be open, but always use the small one, opening the large one only occasionally — in that case two mezuzot are required — end quote, Taz.
