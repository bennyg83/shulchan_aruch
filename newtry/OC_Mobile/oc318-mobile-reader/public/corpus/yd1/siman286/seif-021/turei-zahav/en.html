The pillar does not divide it, etc. It is explained in the Tur: that is, if they are connected in such a way that they are not separated from each other — if this opens, the other also opens; but where there is one large gate and next to it a small gate in the manner of the wealthy who do not want the large gate to be open, but always use the small one and only occasionally the large one — in that case two mezuzot are required.
