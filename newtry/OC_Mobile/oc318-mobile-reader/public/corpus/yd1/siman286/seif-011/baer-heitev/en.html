Exempt. Because they are a temporary dwelling; and Taz wrote: it seems to me even if they sit in them every day they are exempt, for since they are not there at night they are called temporary, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 140 seif 8 regarding legal possession; but a shop within his house that opens to the public domain — it is obvious it requires a mezuzah at its entrances — end quote.
