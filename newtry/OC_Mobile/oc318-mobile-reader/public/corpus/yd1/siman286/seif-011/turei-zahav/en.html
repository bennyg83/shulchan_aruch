The shops in the market are exempt. They are not dwellings; and it seems to me even if they sit in them every day they are exempt, for since they are not there at night they are called temporary, as we find in Choshen Mishpat siman 140 seif 8 regarding legal possession; and Perishah left this as requiring study; but it seems to me as I wrote, and it is unlike a house of hay or a storehouse where the use relevant to it has no distinction between day and night — so it seems to me. But a shop that is within his house that opens to the public domain — plainly it requires a mezuzah at its entrances.
