It is obligated in mezuzah. In the Diaspora, after thirty days; in Eretz Yisrael, immediately. This teaches that we do not say that because the house belongs to a non-Jew it is entirely exempt from mezuzah. All this applies only where there is no danger in affixing a mezuzah in the non-Jew's house. Where danger exists, it is exempt, as explained above in seif 8.
