The bathhouse. See responsum of my ancestor's ancestor Panim Me'irot part 1 siman 87 — that if they made a physical change in the body of the bathhouse and uprooted the furnace and the like and made it a house — it is permitted to dwell in it and to bless and to place a mezuzah at its entrance — see there. And in Sefer Chamudei Daniel manuscript: a hall-house from which they sometimes make a bathhouse once or twice a year — obligated in mezuzah, only one must cover it with wax (see Ateret Zekenim note 5), and likewise it is permitted to pray inside it — see there.
