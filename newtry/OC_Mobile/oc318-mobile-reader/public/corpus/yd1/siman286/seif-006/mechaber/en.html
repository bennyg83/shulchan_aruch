A portico—that is, a place that has three walls and a ceiling—even if it has two doorposts in the fourth direction, it is exempt, because the doorposts are to support the ceiling and not for mezuzot (words of the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchos Sefer Torah, halachah 3). But if it has a partition also in the fourth direction, even if it is low, or if it is made of windows upon windows, it is obligated.
