Of Israel and non-Jews. See the responsum of our Rabbi Akiva Eiger siman 66, who wrote: if they purchased a house that has no law of division and sometimes this one uses all of it and sometimes the other uses all of it — there is reason to rule it obligated in mezuzah, per what Ran wrote in Nedarim chapter 8 that in such a case we rule that there is bereirah — and this one uses his share and that one his share; see there carefully; and see above siman 226 and in Orach Chayim siman 658 seif 7 in Hagahah.
