And if he fixed it before hanging the door, he has not fulfilled his obligation when he places the door afterward, for this would be "it shall be done" and not "from the already done" — so writes the Levush; and to satisfy both opinions, he should hang the door upon it immediately and then fix it; and if it is impossible to hang the door immediately, he should fix it without a blessing, or first bless over another and have in mind this one as well, and when he hangs the door upon it he should remove it and do so again — and thus he fulfills everyone's view — end quote, Shach.
