Some exempt. If one affixed the mezuzah before hanging the door, he does not fulfill his obligation by subsequently installing the door, because this is a case of "you shall make" and not something already made. So writes the Ateret Zekenim. To satisfy both opinions, one should hang the door immediately and then affix the mezuzah. If it is impossible to hang the door immediately, he should affix the mezuzah without a blessing, or recite a blessing over another mezuzah while intending that it apply to this one as well. When he later hangs the door, he should remove this mezuzah and affix it again without a blessing, or recite a blessing over another mezuzah while intending for this one as well. In this manner he satisfies all opinions.
