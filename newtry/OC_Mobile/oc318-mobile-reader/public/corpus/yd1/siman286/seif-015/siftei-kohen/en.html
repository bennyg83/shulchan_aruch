And if he fixed it before hanging the door, he has not fulfilled his obligation when he places the door afterward — for it would be "it shall be done" and not "from the already done" — end quote, Ateret Zekenim. And to satisfy both opinions, he hangs the door upon it immediately and then fixes it; and if it is impossible to hang the door immediately, he fixes it without a blessing, or first blesses also on another and has intention also on this one; and when he hangs the door upon it, he removes it and refixes it without a blessing, or blesses on another and has intention on this one as well; and thereby he fulfills everyone's view — study it.
