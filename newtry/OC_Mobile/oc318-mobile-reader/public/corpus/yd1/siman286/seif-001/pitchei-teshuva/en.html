A cattle stable. Yad HaKetanah explains that modern stables are generally exempt because of excessive filth. Poultry rooms may be obligated where sufficiently clean and otherwise structurally qualified; where filth is occasional, the mezuzah should be placed outside or covered.
Owned by a Jew and a non-Jew. Rabbi Akiva Eiger discusses jointly owned property without division, used alternately by each partner. Since each may be viewed as using his own share through bereirah, there is reason to obligate a mezuzah.
