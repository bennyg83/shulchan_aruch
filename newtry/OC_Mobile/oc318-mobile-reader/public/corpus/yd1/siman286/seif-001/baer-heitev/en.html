Countries. Meaning regions surrounded by mountains and valleys for many parasangs and accessible only through gates, such as Hungary. Rashi.
A cattle shed. Although it is a place of filth, the contamination is not severe. Derishah states the rule: where sanctity or significant filth is present, an actual permanent dwelling is required; where neither applies, such as a hayloft, frequent entering and leaving can make it a dwelling. A poultry house is a built room for geese and chickens. Shach.
Are obligated. See Sema in Choshen Mishpat 427.
Exempt. Because part belongs to non-Jews, and also because of danger lest they accuse the Jew of sorcery. Shach.
Some are non-Jews. Even if most residents are Jewish, the gates are exempt because of danger. This also explains why Jewish streets in certain cities had no mezuzah on their gates. Another reason is that non-Jews would remove and disgrace it. The same applies to rented taverns or shops where non-Jews regularly enter and may take the mezuzah, unless it can be securely protected. Shach.
