Places obligated in mezuzah. In it are 23 seifim. These are the places obligated in mezuzah: gates of houses and gates of courtyards of countries and towns, cattle sheds, attics, and storehouses of wine and oil, and a woman's house, and a partners' house—all are obligated.
{Rama: And specifically when the partners' house is of Israelites; but a house of an Israelite and a non-Jew is exempt from mezuzah (Mordechai, first chapter of Avodah Zarah). And likewise courtyards or towns where some non-Jews live are exempt from mezuzah (Chidushei Agudah, first chapter of Yoma).}
