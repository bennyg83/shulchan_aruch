Cattle stable. Here he did not write "if women bathe there" as he wrote regarding the cattle house in seif 2 — because in the cattle house the cattle are not there constantly; but in the stable there is animal dung there constantly, so women do not bathe there; and so Tosafot wrote in Yoma (folio 11) — and nevertheless it is obligated in mezuzah; and it is unlike a privy where the impurity is great. And it appears that here too the mezuzah needs to be covered, as I wrote in seif 5.
Of Israel and non-Jews — exempt. For a partnership we include from what is written "your days may be multiplied" — and regarding a non-Jew one cannot say so.
That some non-Jews live there. The reason is explained there — because of danger, meaning they will say he is making sorcery for them, as stated in Yoma folio 11. And from this one can derive regarding all city gates to exempt them due to the danger of the ruler.
