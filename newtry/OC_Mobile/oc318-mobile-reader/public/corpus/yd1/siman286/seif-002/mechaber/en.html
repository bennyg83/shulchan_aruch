A straw house, a wood house, and a cattle house are obligated; and if women bathe in them, since they stand there naked, it is not fitting for the Divine Presence to be there—no mezuzah. {Rama: And specifically these; but an actual room—even a place where a man and woman sleep and engage in relations—is obligated in mezuzah (in a responsum of Maharil, and so Beit Yosef). And some are lenient and say that wherever women lie, it is exempt from mezuzah (Beit Yosef in the of Semak and Kol Bo and Mordechai end of Hilchos Mezuzah). And it appears to me that where the door is from inside and when they lie there they close the door and the mezuzah is outside, it is obligated according to all (own reasoning in Darkei Moshe, and so in responsum of Maharil siman 98).}
