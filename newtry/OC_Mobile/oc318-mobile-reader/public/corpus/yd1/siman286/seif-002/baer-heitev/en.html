And he wrote in the Maadanei Melech: nevertheless there remains to clarify — if that room has another room beyond it and there it is not used by women and husband for marital relations, but it is a room obligated in mezuzah except that the hinge and door revolve around it — and if so the opening of the doorway is from outside into the women's bedroom, etc. — it appears to me that one should fulfill the mitzvah of mezuzah in the room under discussion by fixing it behind the door — end quote. And Bach wrote: the custom has spread to make a mezuzah only at the house entrance adjacent to the public domain, because women bathe in the winter room in winter and in the rooms in summer and stand there naked, etc. But recently scholars began making mezuzot for all house doorways; however, one must be careful not to make a mezuzah in a place where one regularly bathes or launders dirty children's clothing — end quote. Nevertheless it appears one should fix mezuzot at all doorways and cover them as below seif 5. Shach.
