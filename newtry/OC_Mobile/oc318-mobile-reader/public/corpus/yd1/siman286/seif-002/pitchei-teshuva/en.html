Cattle stable. See Beit HaTov; and see in Yad HaKatanah chapter 2 of Hilchot Mezuzah ot 12, who wrote: that which nowadays nowhere do we practice to make a mezuzah on a stable — must be because in their days it was somewhat clean and their impurity was not great; but nowadays we see plainly their impurity is great. Nevertheless in lulim — a built room for geese and chickens — it seems certainly obligated in mezuzah, provided it has four cubits by four and a ceiling and the other conditions, for their impurity is not like a stable; and if we see it is somewhat great — at least if the door is inside and the mezuzah outside — fit to make a mezuzah there; and all according to what the eyes see — see there.
