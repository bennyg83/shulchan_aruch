Specifically these, etc. Meaning, specifically these places, which are not actual dwelling rooms. A room that is an actual dwelling, however, is different.
Some are lenient and say, etc. The Bach writes that the same applies where women commonly bathe in the winter room or in another room, as our women do, since bathing is impossible unless they are unclothed, which constitutes disrespect to sacred writings. Therefore the custom spread not to affix a mezuzah except at the entrance of the house adjoining the public domain, because women bathe in the winter room during winter and in the other rooms during summer, standing there unclothed. Recently, however, scholars have begun affixing mezuzot to all the doorways in the house. They must nevertheless take care not to affix a mezuzah in a place regularly used for bathing or for washing children's soiled clothing. Nevertheless, it appears proper to affix mezuzot to all the doorways and cover the mezuzah, as stated below in seif 5.
It is obligated according to all opinions. Maadanei Melech writes at the end of folio 78 that the following question still requires clarification. Suppose that beyond this room there is another room that is not used by women for marital relations, and that inner room is obligated in mezuzah, but because of the direction of the hinge and the door, the doorway opens from outside into the women's bedroom. It appears to me that the mitzvah of mezuzah should be fulfilled for the room under discussion by affixing the mezuzah behind the door, as explained below in siman 289, seif 2. See there at length.
