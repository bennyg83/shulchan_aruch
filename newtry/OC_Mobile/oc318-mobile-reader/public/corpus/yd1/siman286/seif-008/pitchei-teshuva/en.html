To be visible. See Beit HaTov in the name of Taz, who wrote only in a room, etc.; and see in Magen Avraham siman 40 s.k. 2 who wrote that ostensibly it refers even to spreading a cloth over it, for in one vessel plainly forbidden; and on this he disagrees, and his view is that glass cover helps and another cover over it — see there; and see in Chomot Yerushalayim siman 277 who wrote that Taz also concedes to this and forbids only glass cover alone; and Tosafot plainly forbid even not glass — one vessel. It seems that without glass it is considered a tent by itself, for we deal with ten handbreadths high and fixed in the wall and more important. And on what Taz wrote that in a place of filth glass cover helps — he wrote requires study: since he intends the name be visible, one could say it is worse, for he himself requires revelation and it is not so much considered a partition — see there; and this reasoning is weak.
