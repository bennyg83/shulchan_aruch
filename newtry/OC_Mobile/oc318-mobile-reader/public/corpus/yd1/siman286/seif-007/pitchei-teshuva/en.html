Covering. See Yad HaKatanah chapter 3 of Hilchot Mezuzah letter 13, who wrote: all this is when it occasionally happens by chance that something impure is there and it is compelled to be opposite the mezuzah — that is, when it cannot be otherwise — then the law is to cover; but heaven forbid to permanently do anything disgraceful opposite the mezuzah and rely on the cover, such as regularly laundering dirty children's clothing before it, or fixing a vessel before it into which all dirty water is poured — as is unfortunately common among us; and of this it is said "for he has scorned the word of the Lord" — and his punishment is great, etc. See there.
