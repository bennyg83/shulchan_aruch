Exempt for thirty days. See Eshel Avraham what he wrote: if he wants, etc.; and see in Eshel Avraham Orach Chayim siman 14 s.k. 2 who plainly permits to be stringent and bless, from that which is in Shulchan Aruch there seif 3: a borrowed tallit is exempt from tzitzit for all thirty days; and Magen Avraham wrote there in the name of Tosafot and Rosh: one who blesses did not lose — see there; and so ruled in responsum Rav Meshulam siman 1 — see there; and see in Nekudot HaKesef what I wrote on this.
