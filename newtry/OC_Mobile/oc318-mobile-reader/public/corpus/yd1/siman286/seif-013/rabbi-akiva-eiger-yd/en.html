(Seif 13) Four cubits by four cubits is exempt. Meaning that in itself such a small room is exempt from mezuzah. But if there is a small inner room opening from a larger room, the doorway leading from the large room into the small one is obligated by virtue of the large room, since one exits the large room through that doorway into the small room. The small room is treated like open air, just as an exit from a house into the street still requires a mezuzah. This appears correct to me, with Hashem's help.
