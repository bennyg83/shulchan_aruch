If it contains enough area to form a square, etc. The Rosh maintains that a house is not obligated in mezuzah unless it is actually square, four cubits by four cubits. Since the Beit Yosef writes that we follow the Rambam, as only the Rosh disagrees with him, the Mechaber cites only the Rambam's view here. In truth, however, Rabbeinu Yerucham at the end of Sefer Adam also agrees with the Rosh and writes that this was accepted as the primary view. It therefore appears that one should affix the mezuzah without a blessing, or first recite a blessing over another mezuzah while intending that the blessing apply to this one as well.
