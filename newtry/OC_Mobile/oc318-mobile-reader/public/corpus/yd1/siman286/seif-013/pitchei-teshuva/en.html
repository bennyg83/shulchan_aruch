See responsum Har HaKarmel chelek 4 siman 23, who wrote: in a room called "kitchen" where they enter to light winter-house ovens and keep lulim of chickens — obligated in mezuzah even though not roofed at all; nevertheless since its way is to be unroofed, it is like courtyard gates obligated in mezuzah for this reason; but if it is a place of filth — exempt, since it is not a dwelling, as Shach s.k. 2 wrote; but if filth is only sometimes — obligated in mezuzah, only must cover it, and even though the door interposes — see there. And see in responsum Arba'ah Turim Even siman 14 regarding what is in most places — roofed rooms with mezuzah, and on Sukkot they open the roofing and cover with branches while the mezuzah is fixed in the door; and after Sukkot they throw the s'chach and re-roof as before while the mezuzah remains as it was — whether they do well or not. And one great authority's view: there is here an issue of ta'aseh velo min he'asuy — therefore afterward must take the mezuzah from there and fix it anew and bless on it; and so he ruled to be stringent if the door broke and they gave it to a craftsman to repair — when returning and fixing it on the gate, must remove the mezuzah and fix anew due to ta'aseh velo min he'asuy. The author of the work disagrees: first, this sukkah never left obligation of mezuzah, for if due to sukkah being temporary dwelling — behold this room is permanent dwelling all year, and therefore we do not exempt the festival sukkah except when made for the festival; even when made permanent dwelling it is exempt, called temporary dwelling; but this room that is permanent all year — certainly also on Sukkot days it is not exempt. And one should not exempt it because it is unroofed — behold its way is thus and it is like a courtyard. And further, disqualification of ta'aseh velo min he'asuy is only when initially made invalid; but if initially made valid in a room obligated in mezuzah, even if the room was later exempt from mezuzah, such as removing roofing, etc. — nevertheless when repaired afterward it is valid and there is no issue of ta'aseh velo min he'asuy — see there at length.
