And he wrote, etc. But Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: specifically when he needs, etc. This is as he wrote above in siman 286, where he wants to eat. Since the time of Minchah has arrived, it is forbidden to eat until he prays, and the obligation of both prayers, Mussaf and Minchah, falls upon him; then Minchah comes first. But if he does not want to eat now, even though the time of Minchah has arrived, it is not called that the obligation of Minchah has fallen upon him, and he prays Mussaf. If so, on Yom Kippur, when it is forbidden to eat, it cannot be found that he would need to pray Minchah first.
And the Tur wrote that in any event they must confess ten times. This corresponds to the ten times that the Kohen Gadol mentioned the Name. He confessed three times: once over his bull for himself and his household; a second time over his bull for the kohanim; and a third time over the goat for all Yisrael. In each confession he mentioned the Name three times: 'Please, Hashem, I have sinned,' etc.; 'Please, with the Name, atone'; and he concluded, 'as is written in Your Torah: for on this day,' etc., 'before Hashem you shall be purified.' This makes nine times. The tenth time was at the lottery over the two goats, when he said 'for Hashem as a sin-offering' while placing the lot on the goat that was for Hashem. This makes ten times. Corresponding to them, one must confess ten times. He counts the ten confessions as follows, and this is his language: There are places that have the custom, excluding what he wrote there previously in the name of the Ba'al HaIttur, that the shaliach tzibbur does not confess for each prayer except once, and the congregation once. This makes eight times, meaning in the four prayers of the day, and two times in the evening, one at Minchah and one at Ma'ariv. End quote. Although at Ma'ariv itself they confess twice, once by each individual in his prayer and a second time with the shaliach tzibbur, it is possible that the custom of those places was not to confess at Ma'ariv except each individual in his prayer. So it also somewhat implies previously there in the name of the Ba'al HaIttur.
And at night he does not confess, etc. But it seems more correct that he counted only the confessions that are within the prayer. At night, the confessions said with the shaliach tzibbur are not within the prayer, because the shaliach tzibbur does not repeat the prayer at Ma'ariv. Since the Ma'ariv confession is within the selichos, he did not count it.
The Tur further wrote in the name of Rav Natronai the number of selichos: at Shacharis, seven; at Mussaf, five; at Minchah, three; and if there is time, he says five. End quote. This is a smaller number than what other authorities wrote, which the Tur brought; see there.
