1) [Seif 1] It is proper to shorten the piyyutim, etc. For Shacharis, they rise early and go to the synagogue, arrange the blessings, and recite Pesukei D'Zimrah as on Shabbos. In Sepharad, the custom is to add many Tehillim relating to the theme of the day. It is proper not to prolong them excessively, so that Shema may be recited in its proper time. Rav Amram wrote that the shaliach tzibbur stands before the lectern and says Nishmas. If there is a custom to add passages in Yotzer Or, Ahavah Rabbah, and Emes V'Yatziv, they have permission to do so, and this is the custom in Ashkenaz. However, the custom of Toledo, where they do not add to them, is more correct. Tur. We have already written above, siman 51, sec. 50, that this is the custom of the Beit El congregation, may its Rock and Redeemer protect it, who follow the teachings of the Arizal, that they do not add to them, see there. See further in Shulchan Gavoah what he wrote concerning this matter. See citations 140.
