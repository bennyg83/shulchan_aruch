[1] And selichos, etc. Thirteen times 'Vaya'avor,' corresponding to the Thirteen Attributes (Avi Ezri). When there is a milah, see siman 621. A Torah scholar may not occupy himself with Torah and omit praying the prayers, selichos, and requests with the congregation (Kenesses HaGedolah). It is a mitzvah on Yom Kippur to study mishnayos from tractate Yoma with their commentaries. It is also proper to study, at the end of Yoma, the teachings that discuss the virtues of repentance (Shelah).
