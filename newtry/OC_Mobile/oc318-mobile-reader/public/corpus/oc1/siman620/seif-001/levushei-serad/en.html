Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1: They should skip Avinu Malkeinu. For if they delay until seven hours, the time of Minchah has already arrived and one must give it precedence, as explained in siman 286. But the Rady disagrees, holding that one need not give the Minchah prayer precedence except when the time of Minchah Ketanah has arrived; at Minchah Gedolah one need not give it precedence unless one wants to pray Minchah immediately as well, such as when one wants to eat.
