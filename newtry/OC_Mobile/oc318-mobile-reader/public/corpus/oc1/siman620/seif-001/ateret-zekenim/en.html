It is good to shorten. On Yom Kippur they say 'Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbos,' but they begin with 'It is good to thank Hashem' (Maharil). Nevertheless, the custom is to say even 'Mizmor Shir LeYom HaShabbos,' because Yom Tov too is called Shabbos, and so is the custom. There are places where the kohanim go up to the duchan on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur even at Shacharis. Sometimes they do not say Avinu Malkeinu at Shacharis on Yom Kippur, in order to advance the Mussaf prayer (Maharal) before the time of Minchah Gedolah. Nevertheless, even if the time of Minchah Gedolah has arrived, even so we advance Mussaf before Minchah (Bach, siman 286). When the Rosh saw that they were delayed, he would pray Mussaf alone in order to pray it in its proper time.
