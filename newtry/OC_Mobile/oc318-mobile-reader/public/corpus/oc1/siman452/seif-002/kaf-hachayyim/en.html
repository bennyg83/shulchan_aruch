2) Regarding how long a utensil should remain in the hagalah vessel, Rif in Perek Kol Sha'ah writes that it should remain until it expels what it absorbed. Ran writes likewise there, as do Rambam, chapter 5, law 23; Raavad in Sefer Tamim De'im, siman 25; and Rashba in Torat HaBayit, bayit 4, shaar 4. Radbaz, in Leshonot HaRambam, siman 241, likewise demonstrates that this is Rambam's view. Tur, however, writes that the custom is to insert them and remove them immediately, and writes likewise in Yoreh De'ah, siman 121. Mordechai, Perek Kol Sha'ah, siman 579, writes likewise, as cited by Beit Yosef. Levush, seif 2, also writes so. Peri Chadash, at the end of seif 6, writes that the custom of Israel is Torah. Taz, seif katan 2, explains that "immediately" is not literal; the utensil must remain at least briefly. Kol Bo likewise writes that one must not hurry to remove the utensil, so that the boiling water has time to enter and expel what was absorbed. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 4; Ben Ish Chai, Parashat Tzav, letter 12, write likewise. See below, letter 14.
