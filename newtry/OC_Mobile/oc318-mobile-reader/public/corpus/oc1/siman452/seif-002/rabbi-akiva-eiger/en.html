All the time they are boiling they did not absorb from hagalah water — meaning regarding water: while absorbing they do not expel — what absorbs in this boiling does not expel in this boiling; but absorption already expelled as explained in Tosafot; and if they cease boiling even if boil again — nevertheless at time of boiling they absorb in vessel what absorbed when ceased; since did not absorb in this boiling it is like old absorption — examine well.
