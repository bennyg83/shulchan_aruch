(א) One should be careful — and per Rama in Yoreh De'ah siman 95 seif 3 in the hagahah, even b'dieved it is forbidden.
(ב) To kasher — and even before the fifth hour, since it is because of meat and milk.
(ג) Together — meaning one inserts them at one time into the pot; the reason is that meat and milk expulsions mix together and are absorbed in the vessels and become forbidden.
(ד) One of them etc. — ben yomo is permitted, for it receives from what is not ben yomo only impaired taste; and what is not ben yomo is also permitted, for the taste absorbed from ben yomo is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter and is not considered taste; and after hagalah one may use each for what he wants — meat or milk.
(ה) Is not ben yomo — and the same even if both are ben yomo but there is sixty against one vessel, for then its expulsion is nullified and it is as if it does not exist. All this during the rest of the year and because of meat-milk prohibition, or on erev Pesach before the fifth hour; not so afterward — one must be careful in each that it not be ben yomo or that there be sixty against all, as in s.k. 13.
(ו) Forbidden vessel — meaning even though one kasher it itself one must be careful that it not be ben yomo from when forbidden food was used in it, or that there be sixty in the water against it, lest it become forbidden if it again absorbs its expulsion.
(ז) Any ben yomo vessel — meaning whether from meat to milk or chametz to matzah; even if one kasher it alone and before the fifth hour and there is sixty, because of a decree lest one kasher a forbidden vessel with less than sixty, for there hagalah does not help all the time it is ben yomo, for it returns and absorbs from hagalah water. And even counters one should scrub well and wait after scrubbing twenty-four hours so they not be ben yomo, and dried well from cold water lest they be cooled from boiling ones; and be careful to pour on them quickly, not by throwing in air, for that is not considered irui; and be careful to pour on them from the vessel in which one boiled the water, but not draw with another vessel from the pot and pour on them to kasher, for that is kli sheini — if one kasher without a white-hot stone — unless one leaves the vessel with which one draws inside the pot until the water raises boiling in a small pot, then it is properly kli rishon [Magen Avraham in the name of Maharil]; and see what we wrote above siman 451 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 114 in the name of Mahariw.
