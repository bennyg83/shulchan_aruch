(2) Regarding how long vessel stays in pot when kashering — Rif wrote chapter Kol HaShaah that one leaves them inside until they expel; and Ran there; and Rambam chapter 5 halacha 23; and Raavad in Sefer Tamid siman 25; and Rashba in Terumat HaDeshen house 4 gate 4; and Radbaz compelled in Rambam wording siman 141 — see there. But Tur wrote practice is to insert and remove immediately; and Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 121 — see there; and Mordechai chapter Kol HaShaah siman 479 and Beit David Beit Yosef; and Levush seif 2; and Peri Chadash at end seif 6 wrote custom of Israel is Torah. However Taz s.k. 2 wrote Tur's words insert and remove immediately appears not literal immediately but at least some waiting; and wrote so in Kol Bo one must be precise not to hurry removing vessel from pot so there is time for boiling to enter and expel what was absorbed; and so Orach Chayyim 1, Responsa 7, Orach Chayyim 4, Ben Aharon chapter 96, Orach Chayyim 12; and see below Orach Chayyim 14.
