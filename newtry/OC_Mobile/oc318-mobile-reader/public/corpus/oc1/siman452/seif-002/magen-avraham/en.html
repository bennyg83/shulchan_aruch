One must be careful. Regarding the law after the fact, see Yoreh De'ah, siman 95, seif 3. Together. Meaning that he inserts them at the same time into the hagalah vessel and there is not 60 in the water against any 1 of them. Any ben yomo utensil. Even before the fifth hour, as a safeguard because of a utensil containing actual prohibited absorption, for which hagalah is ineffective while it is ben yomo if the water lacks 60, since the utensil can reabsorb from the hagalah water and the rationale of a secondary transfer of permitted flavor does not apply to actual prohibition. Even tables should be scrubbed thoroughly and left 24 hours after scrubbing so they are not ben yomo. They should be dry from cold water, lest the boiling water be cooled. One must pour upon them quickly and directly, not by casting the water through the air, which is not considered proper pouring. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 92. One should pour directly from the vessel in which the water was boiled and not draw it into a second vessel and then pour, because that is a kli sheini. If the drawing vessel remains inside the boiling vessel until the water in it itself boils, it has the status of a kli rishon. [Maharil] Mateh Moshe writes that hagalah should be performed 3 days before Pesach; all this depends upon custom. Sefer Chasidim, siman 730, states that a Torah scholar expert in hagalah should perform the hagalah.
