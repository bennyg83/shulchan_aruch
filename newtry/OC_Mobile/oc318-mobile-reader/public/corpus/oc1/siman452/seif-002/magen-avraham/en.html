(א) Together — meaning one inserts them at once into the pot and there is not sixty in the pot against any one of them.

(ב) Unless one of them — or there is sixty against it; all this during the rest of the year or on erev Pesach before the fifth hour; not so afterward — one must be careful in each that it not be ben yomo or sixty, as explained.

(ג) Any ben yomo vessel — even alone and before the fifth hour, and even if there is sixty, because of a decree regarding a forbidden vessel that hagalah does not help all the time it is ben yomo, for it returns and absorbs from hagalah water and noten taam bar noten taam does not apply. And even counters one should scrub well and wait after scrubbing twenty-four hours so they not be ben yomo, and dried from cold water lest they be cooled from boiling ones; and be careful to pour on them quickly, not by throwing in air, for that is not considered irui (see Yoreh De'ah siman 92); and be careful to pour from the vessel in which one boiled the water and not draw with another vessel from the pot and pour on them to kasher, for that is kli sheini (and Avnei Nezer Yoreh De'ah 92 elaborated on this); however if one leaves the vessel with which one draws inside the pot until the water raises boiling in a small pot — then it is properly kli rishon (all this in Maharil); and Mateh Moshe wrote to kasher three days before Pesach; and all this depends on custom. Written in Shaarei Chaim siman 470: a Torah scholar expert in hagalah should kasher.
