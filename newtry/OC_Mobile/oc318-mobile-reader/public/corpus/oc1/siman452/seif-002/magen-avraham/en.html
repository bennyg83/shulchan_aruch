Together. Meaning that one inserts them at one time into the cauldron, and there is not sixty times [as much] in the cauldron against any one of them.
Any ben yomo vessel. Even before the fifth hour, because of a decree regarding a vessel of issur, for which hagalah does not help so long as it is ben yomo, since it returns and absorbs from the hagalah water, for a giving of taste after a giving of taste does not apply to it. And even tables: one should scrub well and wait after the scrubbing twenty-four hours so that they not be ben yomo, and so that they be dry of the cold water, lest the boiling [water] be cooled by them; and one should be careful to pour upon them with alacrity, and not by throwing through the air, for that is not considered pouring (see Yoreh De'ah siman 92); and one should be careful to pour from the vessel in which one boiled the water, and not to draw with another vessel from the pot and pour upon them to do hagalah, for that is called a kli sheini (and see the Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 92, who elaborated on this); however, if one leaves the vessel with which one draws inside the pot so that the water raises its boil, it is properly called a kli rishon (all this in Maharil). And Mateh Moshe wrote to do hagalah three days before Pesach; and all this depends on custom. It is written in Sefer Chasidim, siman 730: a Torah scholar who is expert in hagalah is the one who should do the hagalah.
