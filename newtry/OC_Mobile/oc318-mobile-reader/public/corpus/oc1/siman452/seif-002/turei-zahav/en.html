(א) One of them is not ben yomo — one may say that when not ben yomo it is forbidden l'chatchila, for it receives impaired taste. One may say that here it is not certain the vessel will absorb since it is busy expelling — therefore there is no certain l'chatchila prohibition; but if it already finished expelling completely it certainly absorbs immediately when returned to the pot — then there is l'chatchila prohibition to kasher one place twice, as written.

(ב) Therefore the practice is not to kasher — meaning even if one kasher one vessel.
