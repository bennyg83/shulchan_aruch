(א) Together — meaning that one inserts them at one time into the pot and there is not sixty in the pot against any one of them. For b'dieved see Yoreh De'ah siman 95 seif 6. And see siman 509 in Magen Avraham small seif 11 the reason for those who practice not to kasher a dairy vessel to eat meat in it or vice versa.

(ב) Ben yomo — or there is sixty against it. All this is during the rest of the year or on erev Pesach before the fifth hour; but afterward one must be careful in each of them that it not be ben yomo or that there be sixty, as explained.

(ג) Forbidden food — that there must be that it is not ben yomo or sixty in the water against it, and then it is permitted to kasher even a forbidden vessel first.

(ד) Any vessel — even alone and before the fifth hour, and even if there is sixty, because of a decree regarding a forbidden vessel that hagalah does not help for it all the time it is ben yomo, for it returns and absorbs from the hagalah water, and noten taam bar noten taam d'heter does not apply to it {Rama: (And Shach Yoreh De'ah siman 95 asks from what we say there: a meat bowl rinsed in a pot of milk is permitted; and he answered that there it is b'dieved; and Peri Chadash rejected his view — is it not noten taam bar noten taam d'heter? Rather he concludes that the stringency l'chatchila is per the view of poskim that taste of two substances mixes at once in the water)}. Magen Avraham wrote: even counters one should scrub well and wait after scrubbing twenty-four hours so they not be ben yomo, and they should be dried from cold water lest they be cooled from the boiling ones; and one should be careful to pour on them quickly, not by throwing in the air, for that is not considered irui {Rama: (see Yoreh De'ah siman 95)}; and one should be careful to pour from the vessel in which one boiled the water and not draw with another vessel from the pot and pour on them to kasher, for that is called kli sheini; however if one leaves the vessel with which one draws inside the pot until the water raises its boiling, it is considered properly kli rishon — see Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 92 end of his words in the name of Maharil.
