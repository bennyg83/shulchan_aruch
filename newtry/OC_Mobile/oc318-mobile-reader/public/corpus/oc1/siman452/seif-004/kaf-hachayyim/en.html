4) There. Before the fifth hour. At that time there is a secondary transfer of permitted flavor. Beis Yosef; Taz, subsection 1. How does this occur? The chametz imparts flavor to the utensil, the utensil imparts flavor to the water, and the water then imparts flavor back into the bowl, while the prohibition of chametz has not yet taken effect. Mordechai, chapter Kol Sha'ah, siman 576; Levush, se'if 3. Although some maintain that since it retains the designation chametz, it is considered to have absorbed a prohibited substance, and Maran took their view into account above in siman 451, se'if 4, here he relies upon the lenient authorities because some authorities independently permit all these matters. Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 1. See above, siman 451, subsection 39.
