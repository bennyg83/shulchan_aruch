(4) There — before fifth hour — for then noten taam bar noten taam d'heter ben yomo Taz s.k. 1. And how is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter: chametz gives taste to vessel, vessel to water, water returns and gives taste to bowl — yet chametz prohibition not yet arrived. Mordechai chapter Kol HaShaah siman 476. Levush seif 3. And even though some say chametz since named on it is called prohibition taste and our master zl above siman 451 seif 4 concerned for their view — nevertheless here he relied on lenient views because anyway there is some view permitting all these matters.
