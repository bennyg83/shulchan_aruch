(א) Liquid — and the same applies in water with ashes called in Ashkenaz language lye; so too Beit Yosef and Peri Chadash Yoreh De'ah siman 95 s.k. 19; therefore that hagalah they do in their homes in a pewter vessel with white-hot stones with lye water is not properly called hagalah, especially since they do it when they are dirty; therefore one must be careful with those large vessels that one does not insert into the pot — one should clean them well with water alone — Kaf HaChayyim. And in Be'er Heitev which I annotated only in water, and wrote on it those mixed with ashes called lye — there it seems from his wording that the essence of hagalah is in lye water and he was not precise.

(ב) To kasher more — and b'dieved it is permitted as with other liquids; requires study — Kaf HaChayyim. Orach U'Mussar wrote that one may not use hagalah water of forbidden food l'chatchila even if there is sixty against the vessel, because it is like nullifying prohibition l'chatchila since one wants to use the water.
