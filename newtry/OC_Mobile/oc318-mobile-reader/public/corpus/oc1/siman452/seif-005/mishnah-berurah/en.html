(א) One may kasher in them — or in hot-spring waters.
(ב) In any liquid — for their nature is not to expel absorption.
(ג) However b'dieved — because some dispute that liquids also expel like water, and we rely on them for b'dieved; and poskim wrote the same regarding water mixed with ashes called lye, or what smiths practice heating the vessel via wine lees or vinegar-stein mixed in water — one may not kasher in them l'chatchila, only in water alone; b'dieved hagalah helps in them; and see in Peri Megadim whose view is that if it is before Pesach one should kasher a second time in water alone; however if Pesach arrived it is permitted l'chatchila to use this hagalah.
(ד) One may kasher no more — for they no longer have power to expel absorption; and it is possible that even b'dieved this hagalah does not help.
(ה) In that water — and even if one kashered in them vessels that are not ben yomo, the law is the same.
