But that they not cease boiling etc. — the reason is lest they return and absorb what they expelled as Beit Yosef wrote; and also that one not insert vessels until water boils — Beit Yosef forbids; and see Beit Yosef that after prohibition time one may not put vessel in water before boiling even if leaves there until boils — I abbreviated since Terumat HaDeshen siman 138 and Agur wrote their wording: custom is when inserting one vessel water ceases boiling — therefore wait in each vessel until water bubbles; up to here; and one must warn kasherers, for they practice great lightness and kasher quickly one after another without checking if boiling rises; and one may learn merit for them, for way of kashering is only spoons and bowls used by irui where hagalah in water at yad soledes bo alone helps; proof from poskim of Maharai siman 151 wrote regarding matter sufficient with hagalah by irui — this hagalah also helps; already explained siman 451 seif 5 that vessels mentioned above are kashered by irui; nevertheless in copper pots and things used via kli rishon it appears to me from Terumat HaDeshen that even b'dieved this hagalah does not help at all — not as appears from responsum of Rashal siman 26.
