5) There. Before the fifth hour. This means before the beginning of the fifth hour, namely, by the end of the fourth hour. Since chametz is at least rabbinically prohibited from the fifth hour, as stated above at the beginning of siman 443, its flavor is no longer considered a secondary transfer of permitted flavor.
