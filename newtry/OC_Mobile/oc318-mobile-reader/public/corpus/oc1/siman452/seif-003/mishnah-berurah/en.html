(א) Into a vessel — meaning a perforated basket or net; in a non-perforated vessel one may not kasher even one vessel, even when it floats and does not touch the bottom, unless one leaves them in the pot until the water raises boiling in the vessel one wants to kasher.
(ב) If they touch one another — for at the place of contact boiling does not reach, even though regarding tevilah there is no chatzitzah; regarding boiling it is different.
