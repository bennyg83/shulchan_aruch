3) One might ask: According to the opinion that intentionally nullifying a prohibition is forbidden under Torah law, how did the Torah permit kashering the utensils of Midian with boiling water? This appears to intentionally nullify the prohibition absorbed in them within the kashering water. The answer is that the rule against intentionally nullifying a prohibition applies only when one intends to mix a prohibited substance into permitted food in order to benefit from it. Here, one intends to kasher the utensil and derives no benefit from the prohibited substance, so it is permitted. Ran, chapter Ein Ma'amidin.
