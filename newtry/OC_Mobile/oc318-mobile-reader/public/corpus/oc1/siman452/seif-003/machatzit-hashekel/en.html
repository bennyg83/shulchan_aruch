(א) (s.k. 3) However etc. — for Rabbeinu Yehuda also agrees to this even before the time of prohibition hagalah does not help in water at yad soledes bo unless it raises boiling.

(ב) Forbidden to boil them a second time — meaning if one kashered the vessels when the water was boiling, nevertheless one should be careful that the water not cease boiling all the time the vessel is in it, for after the water ceases boiling the vessel again absorbs from the hagalah water, even though the vessel is not ben yomo and there is also sixty in the water; nevertheless l'chatchila where possible one may not rely on this. And as written in s.k. 6; and even if one's intent is to boil them again — then again they will expel what the hagalah water absorbed — one may not rely on this where there is no need. Also one must be concerned not to be precise as required regarding boiling again, since he knows they already boiled once and will not consider that they again absorbed hagalah water.

(ג) And so too Rashal in responsum siman 26.
