If they touch one another — for at the place of contact boiling does not reach, even though regarding tevilah there is no chatzitzah prohibition here; regarding boiling it is different — so Mordechai in the name of Rokeach; but because of vessel expulsion prohibition there is no concern even if they are ben yomo, for it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter — before six, like fish that rose in a meat bowl that is permitted to eat with kutach, as Yoreh De'ah siman 97.
