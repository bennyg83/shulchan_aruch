And the same applies to a pot in which one kashered vessels and wants to use it on Pesach — one must make a lip for it or a white-hot stone — Taz. A vessel whose use is from the outside, like a bowl that one sometimes inverts on a pot, or a vessel with which one draws from the pot — it has no remedy in this manner, for the outer side was not kashered; therefore one must insert it entirely into the pot — Magen Avraham. A vessel whose mouth is narrow so one cannot scrub inside, or that has cracks — forbidden in hagalah — Maharil; and see above siman 451 s.k. (43) [45].
