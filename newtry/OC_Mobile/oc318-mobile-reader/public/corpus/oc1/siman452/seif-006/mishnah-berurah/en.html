(א) A large vessel etc. — meaning one wants to kasher it and is concerned perhaps there is chametz absorption on its lip where the water does not reach; and the same law for a pot after one kashered vessels in it and wants to use it on Pesach — one must make a lip or white-hot stone; but initially before kashering the vessels and kashering the pot first from the prohibition in it — one need not make a lip.

(ב) One makes a lip at its mouth — meaning around its mouth and not on it directly, for the vessel mouth must be exposed so boiling water reaches there.

(ג) With clay — and during the rest of the year when one wants to kasher from prohibition one makes a lip of dough. The reason for making the lip is because it is impossible that boiling not splash once from chametz cooked in it onto the lip from above, and perhaps at hagalah time boiling water will not splash enough to expel chametz absorbed in it; therefore one makes the lip so water reaches there. All this in a vessel that cannot be inserted into a kli rishon of chametz but is used with boiling inside — therefore this remedy suffices; but a vessel used from outside, like a bowl sometimes inverted on a pot, or a vessel with which one draws from the pot — has no remedy this way, for the outer side was not kashered; therefore one must insert it entirely into the pot. A vessel whose mouth is narrow so one cannot scrub inside, or that has cracks [called rarerin] — forbidden in hagalah.

(ד) Or one takes — meaning one fills it well at first.

(ה) A white-hot stone — for cold cools the water.
