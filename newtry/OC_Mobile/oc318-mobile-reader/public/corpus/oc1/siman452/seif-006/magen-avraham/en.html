(א) One makes a lip — because it is impossible that boiling not splash on it once, and perhaps at the time of hagalah it will not splash on it; therefore one makes a lip; and even though the water drips through the clay — we hold as it absorbs in sparks so it expels in sparks (Gemara); and Maharil wrote: if it is a vessel with which one draws boiling water it has no remedy in this manner, for the outer side was not kashered; therefore one must insert it entirely into the pot. A vessel whose mouth is narrow so one cannot scrub inside, or that has cracks — forbidden in hagalah — end of his words. And it appears to me the same regarding bowls with which one covers the griddle — the outer side also needs hagalah.
