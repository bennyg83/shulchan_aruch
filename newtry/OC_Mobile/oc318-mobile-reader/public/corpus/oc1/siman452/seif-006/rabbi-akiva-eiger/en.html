In Tiberias waters one kasher in them — this is not novel; rather Orach Chayyim wrote if forbidden in Tiberias waters and things appear possible to kasher via hot water in kli rishon — hagalah via fire kashering helps for all — end of his words; explained novelty that can kasher even via hot water and we do not say need hagalah water specifically in Tiberias as it absorbs so it expels; and in Beit Yosef: one may kasher in them or in hot-spring waters.
