(7) Incident where bread piece found in pot they were kashering — asked if need re-kasher vessels; Pri Megadim brought Raavad siman 195: for those who hold not chatzitzah — obvious not vessels, for bread taste expelled in water nullified in sixty; and for those who hold chatzitzah also no need re-kasher vessels, for we did not see they accepted chatzitzah except in substance not taste; and even though practice to kasher kneading vessels of lenient etc. — that is l'chatchila etc.; and since it is custom we do not add stringency; however if found clearly their custom forbids even b'dieved by taste alone — then certainly must re-kasher these vessels — Pri Megadim see there. Zachor L'Chaim end siman 451. And see above siman 447 Orach Chayyim 59 and Orach Chayyim 76.
