(א) (s.k. 7) One must be careful etc. — see Yoreh De'ah, meaning there the Mechaber wrote: a meat bowl rinsed in a pot of milk etc. even though both are ben yomo — permitted, for it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter; and Shach challenged from Rabbeinu Yehuda's words here. Therefore he wrote there for b'dieved and here he wrote one must be careful l'chatchila.

(ב) And this is that l'chatchila one is concerned for Rama's view there who wrote that in such a case taste of two substances is forbidden — the bowl received meat taste and the heter expels taste into the water, and the pot received milk heter taste and expels taste into the water; if so in the water there are two tastes of meat and milk mixed and the second taste is forbidden and they return and are absorbed in the vessel; if so here b'dieved depends on the dispute of Rabbeinu Yehuda and Rama there.
