(s.k. 1) If etc. nevertheless permitted — meaning that if one kasher after the time of prohibition in such a manner hagalah does not help, for in such a case the hagalah water becomes forbidden from the expulsion of the vessels kashered in it; and after the vessel finishes its expulsion they return and absorb from the hagalah water; and even though the water did not cease boiling one may not rely on this, as written below; nevertheless before the time of prohibition there is no concern because it is noten taam etc. of things cooked in a chametz vessel and not etc. as should be.
