(א) The fifth hour — meaning from the beginning of the fifth hour until the end of the fourth hour, for since from the fourth hour it is forbidden in any case d'rabbanan, it is no longer called noten taam bar noten taam d'heter. {Rama: (And regarding the reason of chametz some say it returns and reawakens; and for b'dieved see Yoreh De'ah siman 95 seif 3)} Acharonim.

(ב) Beit Yosef — that even though the vessel is ben yomo and there is not sixty in the water against it, nevertheless it is permitted because it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter, which is not so after the fifth hour; and even though above in siman 447 seif 5 the Rama forbids things cooked in their vessels and does not permit from the reason of noten taam bar noten taam — here there are many taste transfers from food to vessel and from vessel to water and from water to vessel, and it returns and expels from the vessel into the cooked food during Pesach — Magen Avraham. {Rama: (And Kaf HaChayyim wrote that in cooking we forbid l'chatchila noten taam bar noten taam, and it is like l'chatchila since it is possible to wait)} Kaf HaChayyim.

(ג) Whose absorption is slight — such as spoons and cups; one must be concerned that they will absorb from what the bowls and pots expel.

(ד) And he does not leave them — this phrase has no explanation or relevance at all. See Kaf HaChayyim who resolves it.

(ה) Dispute — the words of the Rama are puzzling, for the Rabbeinu Yehuda also agrees to this, except that after its prohibition, once the water ceases boiling it is forbidden to boil them a second time, for all the time they are boiling they did not absorb from the hagalah water; and if they ceased boiling they absorbed; but before the time of prohibition one may boil them a second time and kasher in it, for it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter. Magen Avraham and Kaf HaChayyim.

(ו) That he insert — so that it not absorb from the hagalah water.

(ז) And last — meaning when one kashered it at the beginning or at the end.

(ח) In any amount — because of chol ha-moed he needed this reason, for on Yom Tov itself, even on other Yamim Tovim, it is forbidden to kasher, as written siman 509 Taz.

(ט) Six — meaning not only after six but even after four one must be careful, as written s.k. 1.

(י) The matters etc. — meaning one should see that the vessels not be ben yomo; and if they are ben yomo there must be sixty in the water against it; and if one kasher many vessels one after another there must be sixty against all of them, for the prohibition returns and reawakens; and if one vessel absorbed meat and the other fish it does not return and reawaken since they are distinct tastes; and one should kasher first vessels whose absorption is slight and afterward vessels whose absorption is great; and one should not leave them too long; and the water should not cease boiling; and one should kasher the pot first and last. Magen Avraham wrote: it appears to me that if the pot is not ben yomo one need not kasher it first, for it is no worse than the vessel itself; and if one kashered it at the beginning one need not kasher it at the end, for the vessels are not ben yomo; nevertheless the practice is to be stringent in all stringencies even before the fifth hour — so too Bach. Maharil and Mateh Moshe and Minhagim wrote that one should kasher three days before Pesach. Shaarei Chaim siman 470 wrote: a Torah scholar expert in hagalah should kasher — this is correct — end of Kaf HaChayyim.
