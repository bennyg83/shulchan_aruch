(א) Before the fifth hour — for then it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter; and in some Tur manuscripts it is written before six.

(ב) If the vessels ben yomo — meaning they will return and absorb from what they expelled; and similarly if one leaves them too long. It implies that leaving a little there is no concern. Tur wrote: the practice is to insert and remove immediately — it appears he does not literally mean immediately but at least some waiting is needed; and so in Kol Bo one must be precise not to hurry removing the vessel from the pot so there is time for boiling to enter and expel what was absorbed; and there is no concern at all for leaving a little, especially since one kasher before the fifth hour.

(ג) That they not cease boiling — and if they ceased, then one boils again and kasher in them, and the pot is not forbidden from what the water ceased.

(ד) Until Pesach when chametz is in any amount — because of chol ha-moed he needed this reason, for on Yom Tov itself, even on other Yamim Tovim, it is forbidden to kasher, as siman 509.

(ה) One must be careful in all etc. — requires study, for there is another matter not mentioned here — if one kasher part of the vessel twice; in this one need not be careful if one kasher before the fifth hour, as siman 451 seif 9; automatically after prohibition one must be careful in this; and how is it possible to be careful, for in the place one holds the vessel by hand while kashering water does not enter there, and all the more if one holds with tongs one must rotate the vessel and return it to the pot — and if so it is impossible not to kasher one place twice, impossible to narrow this; Beit Yosef brought that one should narrow per his ability, and truly this narrowing is impossible; therefore one must kasher then in a net called hovev, whereby one can kasher in one time since water enters all of it; but if one kasher after prohibition by hand or tongs hagalah did not succeed at all even b'dieved, for it returns and reawakens; and one may say if the vessel is not ben yomo one may kasher even by hand, for it is noten taam lifgam and permitted until night — this is not so, for in any case noten taam lifgam is forbidden l'chatchila as Yoreh De'ah siman 103; also not leaving inside does not help, for since nothing remains in the vessel to expel — it already expelled the first time — it certainly absorbed even immediately when inserted again; but if there is sixty in the pot water against the vessel one kasher, it helps also for kashering the vessel twice; but thereby one cannot kasher two vessels, for sixty would be needed against both, as Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba even afterward; and one may say it is nullifying prohibition l'chatchila, for one does not intend to nullify prohibition of what is absorbed but on expulsion of what is in it; and for this reason it appears also that one who comes to kasher a forbidden knife in a pot on the fire that is large and has sixty up to the knife may take a kosher pot for this and it is not nullifying prohibition l'chatchila; and per my words it resolves why he did not mention here kashering part of a vessel twice, since he mentioned here sixty against the vessel — not so above siman 451 seif 9.

(ו) Before six — meaning per the girsa I mentioned in s.a. that reads fifth hour; and the distinction between hagalah and libun that in hagalah something always remains.
