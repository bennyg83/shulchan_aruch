For Rabbeinu Yehuda also all explicitly agree in Beit Yosef in this siman in name of Geonim and Talmidei Chachamim without any disputant that boiling helps only when it raises boiling — see Peri Chadash here; and Mechaber siman 451 seif 63 regarding knives kashered in kli rishon even not at fire suffices when yad soledes bo as Magen Avraham s.k. 7 there — must be because primary use of knives is kli rishon even though sometimes also kli rishon — he held zl siman 451 we follow any vessel by majority use — therefore from complete law suffices to kasher in kli rishon; and in Gemara also stam in boiling; but nevertheless stringent on knife because much adheres and requires kli rishon; and in any case does not require raising boiling — see Chayei Adam s.k. 7; but vessel whose use is kli rishon and requires hagalah by law must raise boiling — so must be; not as I saw someone confused here with knife law above; however for law per what we hold solid item law is kli rishon all time it is yad soledes bo as Acharonim Yoreh De'ah siman 34 and Magen Avraham siman 447 s.k. 9 — therefore knives cutting hot solid items — their law as all things used in kli rishon and must raise boiling and does not suffice otherwise — examine well.
