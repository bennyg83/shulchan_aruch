(א) If the vessels ben yomo — that even though the vessel is ben yomo and there is not sixty in the water against it, nevertheless it is permitted because it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter, which is not so after the fifth hour. It is difficult — for above in siman 447 seif 5 the Rama forbids things cooked and did not permit from noten taam bar noten taam; and why does he permit here l'chatchila? One may say that here since there are many taste transfers from food to vessel and from vessel to water and from water to vessel — it is permitted.

(ב) Whose absorption is slight — such as spoons and cups; one must be concerned they will absorb from what bowls and pots expel.

(ג) However many dispute etc. — the words of the Rama are puzzling, for Rabbeinu Yehuda also agrees to this except that after its prohibition, once the water ceases boiling it is forbidden to boil them a second time, for all the time they are boiling they did not absorb from hagalah water; and if they ceased boiling they absorbed; but before the time of prohibition it is permitted, for it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter; and so in Peri Megadim; and so Rashal in responsum.

(ד) And similarly if one leaves them too long — one must be concerned that after finishing expulsion it will again absorb from hagalah water.

(ה) And similarly so that one need not be careful — that the water not cease boiling.

(ו) One must be careful in all etc. — meaning one should see that the vessels not be ben yomo; and if they are ben yomo there must be sixty in the water against it; and if one kasher many vessels one after another there must be sixty against all, for the prohibition returns and reawakens (Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe in the name of Rashba siman 462); and in Orach U'Mussar Shaarei Nitzchon wrote that b'dieved it is permitted, for some say also in food it does not return and reawaken, and if so in vessels one may be lenient; and so the Acharonim and Shach Yoreh De'ah siman 99 disagrees with them — see there. And it appears to me that if one vessel absorbed meat and the other fish it does not return and reawaken since they are distinct tastes, as written Yoreh De'ah siman 98. And one should kasher first vessels of slight absorption and afterward of great absorption; and one should not leave them too long; and the water should not cease boiling; and one should kasher the pot first and last. It appears to me that if the pot is not ben yomo one need not kasher it first, for it is no worse than the vessels themselves; and if one kashered it at the beginning one need not kasher at the end, for the vessels are not ben yomo; nevertheless the practice is to be stringent in all stringencies even before the fifth hour — so too Bach. And requires study why the Rama specified after six, for even after four one must be careful as stated, for even though the prohibition is d'rabbanan, nevertheless noten taam bar noten taam does not apply — see siman 447 seif 3 that regarding something containing only chametz taste we do not say it returns and reawakens — not as one who asked.
