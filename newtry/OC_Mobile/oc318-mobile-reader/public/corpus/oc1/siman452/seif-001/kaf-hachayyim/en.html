1) [Se'if 1] One should be careful to kasher with boiling water, etc. The procedure is as follows. One takes a large cauldron filled with water and brings it to a vigorous boil, until the boiling water reaches every surface of the cauldron, inside and out. One places into it all the utensils one wishes to kasher, inserting them individually, one after another. Levush. Regarding a large utensil that cannot be inserted into the cauldron, see below, se'if 6.
