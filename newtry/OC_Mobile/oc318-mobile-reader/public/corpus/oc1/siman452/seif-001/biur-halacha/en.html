(א) So that one need not be precise etc. — see Mishna Berurah. Several Acharonim [Mateh Yehuda, Maamar Mordechai, Peri Chadash and others] questioned the Mechaber's words that imply after the time of prohibition one must be careful in all these — from the fifth hour until night; but truly this is not so — if it is not ben yomo or there is sixty in water one need not be careful at all in the other matters; what difference if one kasher two types of vessels or leaves them too long or other matters considered, since there is sixty or not ben yomo — even if absorption returned in the vessel it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter; one cannot say the Mechaber meant only before fifth hour no care needed and after fifth hour care in some of them — sixty or not ben yomo or not leaving too long or water not ceasing boiling — for if ben yomo and no sixty to nullify expulsion, even if careful not to leave too long or water not ceasing — still forbidden per poskim [Rashba siman 463 and Tosafot] that always absorbs; and Gra in note 12 explained Mechaber wrote all for clarity and applies even to lenient views on these reasons alone when not ben yomo and no sixty since not leaving long and water did not cease — no absorption; nevertheless before five is better to kasher when no care needed at all; but for law no difference in these matters — even if careful they do not help after prohibition time; essence depends on sixty or not ben yomo — then even if left too long or water ceased afterward we do not care.

(ב) Must be careful in all etc. — see Taz who wrote another matter requiring care — not to kasher part of vessel twice, as above siman 451 seif 9, such as large vessels that cannot enter pot at once — do not rotate by hand in water lest return and insert water over part already kashered once and expelled all its expulsion, and now when returning to water it reabsorbs what already expelled; even if insert only half vessel and remove after expulsion and return other half and narrow so not insert one part twice — very difficult narrowing; and with tongs also impossible to be careful — therefore Taz advised kasher in net or perforated vessel so hagalah in one time, or sixty in water against vessel then expulsion nullified in water and even if reabsorbs no matter; but thereby cannot kasher many vessels in pot as today's custom where expulsions join and need sixty against all vessels [and another advice per Acharonim Mateh Yehuda and Maamar Mordechai: vessel not ben yomo is noten taam lifgam; and what Taz doubted that noten taam lifgam forbidden l'chatchila — they challenged him: only regarding pot that absorbed good taste initially then impaired; not so in hagalah where even if reabsorbs in vessel only impaired taste — and practice is lenient].

(ג) That one need not be careful in them etc. — Rama and Mechaber in this entire seif follow one view that while chametz is permitted it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter and no care needed in anything; see Or U'Mussar, Peri Chadash, Biur HaGra that all this is per poskim who hold chametz is called heter taste; but per those who hold chametz is called prohibition taste — and Mechaber above siman 451 seif 4 ruled thus — then no difference between before fifth hour or whole day; see Peri Chadash who challenged poskim against each other; accordingly one of two matters I brought in Mishna Berurah — vessels not ben yomo or kasher large pot with sixty to nullify vessels kashered therein — is fundamental law requiring care even when kashering during permitted chametz time; and in our country where all city vessels kasher in one large pot, sixty cannot be against all vessels since all expulsions join as Magen Avraham wrote — therefore careful not to kasher any vessel unless passed twenty-four hours since chametz use; and some are stringent when wanting to kasher intestines from chametz to use them chametz three days prior [Maharil and Mateh Moshe].
