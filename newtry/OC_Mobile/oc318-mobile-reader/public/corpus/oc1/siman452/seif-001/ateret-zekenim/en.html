(א) One should be careful to kasher etc. It appears that even if one kasher before the fifth hour, and even if the pot is not ben yomo, one must kasher the pot in which one kasher first and last — not as the Rosh; and so is the practice; do not forsake etc. And even in this leniency of the Rosh that before the time of prohibition one may kasher even ben yomo vessels even without sixty in the water because it is noten taam bar noten taam d'heter — it appears one should be stringent regarding leaving twenty-four hours, as Rabbeinu Yehuda, for since Semag wrote regarding fish that rose in a meat bowl that it is forbidden l'chatchila to cook them in a meat vessel to eat with milk — and similarly regarding chametz.

(ב) However many dispute etc. It is difficult regarding the words of the author of the hagahah, for this wording is from responsum of Geonim, and Beit Yosef wrote on it that it is not explained literally; and so in Terumat HaDeshen — see there; and Kol Bo wrote one must be careful that the large pot in which one kasher not cease boiling while vessels are inside etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote all this when one kasher after the time of prohibition, and on this he was silent here; nevertheless all agree hagalah does not help if water is not boiling — if so why did Rama write many dispute — requires study. Forbidden to libun a spit after six hours since chametz prohibition already applies — so in derash of Mahararash.
