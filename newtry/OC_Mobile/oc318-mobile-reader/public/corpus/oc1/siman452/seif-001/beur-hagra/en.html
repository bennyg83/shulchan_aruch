(א) Seif 1 — one should be careful to kasher before the fifth hour etc. So too Tosafot Chullin 108b s.v. shenafal and Avodah Zarah 76a s.v. mikan and Rosh and Mordechai chapter Kol HaShaah that before the fifth hour it is still heter and noten taam bar noten taam d'heter and need not be concerned in all this; and even per Tosafot Chullin 111b in name of Rashi only rose but not cooked — see there s.v. halachta here there are three noten taam from chametz to vessel and vessel to water and water to vessel and all agree as siman 447 seif 5 hagahah; however etc. and even though stringent there l'chatchila general stringency there specifically; not so after four hours when prohibition applies and like pot in which meat cooked one may not cook milk etc. for second taste is in prohibition as Rosh there s.k. 29; and same for many noten taam specifically in heter; and Rava does not dispute Abaye there 110:11b — Rava said when absorbs etc. only first taste; see Tosafot s.v. amar; and Yoreh De'ah siman 95; then must be careful as written; but per Ramban chametz is called prohibition even before prohibition time — no difference; even before prohibition time must be careful; and Ran chapter Kol HaBasar on Tosafot above before four hours; and possible to distinguish etc.; and Terumat HaDeshen house 4 beginning Shach that even before fifth must be careful in all per his view chametz not called heter; and as written not as Magen Avraham s.k. 1 for in prohibition no difference between two noten taam or more; see 123b; and see Rashba siman 462; already explained siman 451 that Rif, Rosh, and Shulchan Aruch follow Ramban.

(ג) Or if they are — if hand of his son was given etc. in which he would not take hand in water sixty against vessel — fix seeded bad text with: Or if vessels ben yomo — if there is sixty in water against vessel it is permitted.

(ד) And similarly if one kasher etc. — meaning even per Rashbam who Tosafot wrote there: as we say absorbs absorbs expels does not expel all time absorbing — and same expels expels absorbs does not absorb all time expelling — so Raavyah and Rosh chapter 2 seif 7; and even though Tosafot rejected this view — per Rabbenu Tam he does not expel at all current absorption — we could say opposite; but per Rashbam to say all time busy expelling does not absorb is certainly true view as Raavad chapter 2 Pesachim; and we find this view in many places; but what they are stringent is in such case where vessel of slight absorption quickly expels from other vessel and then absorbs — and all time they are ben yomo and no sixty in water against them; and entire this seif until end deals only with such case where we are stringent because of Rabbenu Tam who disputes Rashbam — for him expels always; and if we held view Tosafot rejected then permitted in one vessel if kasher once even leaving very long; but Tosafot already rejected; and general rule per Rabbenu Tam no remedy after fifth except not ben yomo or sixty; per Rashbam helps if not leaving long.

(ה) And similarly if one leaves — per above view that all time expelling does not absorb — forbidden to leave more than measure of its expulsion as Tur wrote.

(ו) And he does not leave — meaning per Rashbam there in Tosafot absorbs absorbs etc. all time absorbing does not expel; when full from absorbing then expels; and he said there when rests again expels — meaning when rests from absorbing then again expels; and answers when preceded and removed before full from absorbing and does not expel at all even from another absorption — not as Rabbenu Tam there; and Rashbam wrote there therefore must leave in hagalah long time until etc.; and Rif and Rosh chapter 2 and Rambam; and Orach Chayyim in name of Raavad; and Rabbenu Tam challenged him; and Tur in name of Raavyah on Rif who knows to estimate etc. perhaps leaves inside and return and absorb — therefore Hashem-fearing person etc. before fifth hour etc. — meaning then may leave much inside per Rif and Rashbam.

(ז) And similarly so that — per Rabbenu Tam view all time expelling does not absorb; and Gemara says there specifically fell into boiling pot then absorbed etc.; and same for expelling as above; and challenges six seifim when rests etc. — meaning from its boiling when preceded and removed; however many dispute etc. — meaning as Semak wrote hagalah at yad soledes bo does not help except boiling that raises boiling; and Magen Avraham; and Kol Bo in name of Kol Bo; and six seifim when rests etc.; but appears boiling also not needed at all before fifth hour for we hold yad soledes bo in twenty-four in kli rishon; but here Semak reason as Chullin fell into boiling pot and therefore not to exclude yad soledes bo for otherwise all permitted; and he said however many dispute hold Semak reason because as absorbs in boiling so expels in boiling — so Beit HaGolah in plain Shulchan Aruch; and implied chapter Kol HaBasar 2 in boiling and kli rishon; and one may reject for Gemara Shabbat pot and kettle passed from boiling and there yad soledes bo suffices; but truth shoots his way plain Shulchan Aruch as Beit Yosef Kol Bo; and he said not cease etc.; and also not insert etc.; and as fell into boiling pot — meaning to exclude that afterward reboiled; and as above perhaps absorbs; and Beit Yosef Kol Bo see there.

(ח) And yes to — per Rabbenu Tam all time expelling does not absorb; Gemara fell into boiling pot then absorbed; same for expelling; challenges when rests from boiling when preceded and removed; however many dispute Semak hagalah at yad soledes bo only boiling raising boiling; Magen Avraham; Kol Bo; six seifim when rests; appears boiling not needed before fifth for yad soledes bo in kli rishon; Semak reason Chullin fell into boiling pot; not to exclude yad soledes bo; many dispute Semak reason as absorbs in boiling so expels; Beit HaGolah plain Shulchan Aruch; chapter Kol HaBasar boiling and kli rishon; reject Shabbat pot passed from boiling yad soledes bo suffices; truth plain Shulchan Aruch Kol Bo; not cease; not insert; fell into boiling pot exclude afterward reboiled; perhaps absorbs; Kol Bo see there.

(ט) End — it absorbed from vessels in which water stood inside a long time; and all the more if he kashered first; and when there is not sixty in the water against the vessels and they are a first vessel — if they are not a first vessel the taste is spoiled and it is taste-of-taste-of-permitted even though taste-of-taste l'chatchila forbidden; nevertheless in such a case of taste-of-taste-of-permitted it is permitted even l'chatchila; and there must be sixty in the pot against all the vessels — then one need not hagalah at the end; and likewise Hagahah above 'or if there is,' etc. — meaning when kashering one vessel; but with many vessels sixty is required against all vessels — not like Rosh chapter 2 seif 7 who wrote each vessel, etc., as Rashba wrote in siman 262: although they said in chapter 2 of Orlah terumah elevates, etc., and orlah elevates orlah there to ascend in two hundred, and terumah in one hundred — but if added until taste-of-taste it is forbidden as Yerushalmi there end law 4: four groats cooked with lentils of hullin and there is no taste-of-taste in them — a groat of the arousing kind forbids — do not let it be greater than an olive-bulk; as you would say regarding an olive-bulk: you see the permitted as though it is not that prohibition — if there is taste-of-taste forbidden, and if not permitted; and here you see the permitted as though it is not that prohibition — if taste-of-taste forbidden, and if not permitted — meaning as though it did not mix at all and returns and awakens; thus it said: a groat of terumah forbids; and as Bikkurim 22 and 23: found an olive-bulk and it returns and awakens; and likewise Tosafot Avodah Zarah 73a:1 what 'first first' nullifies up to taste-of-taste; and Bikkurim there and other places; see Yoreh De'ah siman 99 seif 6 in Hagahah.

(י) And if etc. then etc. — and does not help what there is sixty in water; and also does not help what not ben yomo for noten taam lifgam on Pesach forbidden as siman 447; and per his view wrote after six hours noten taam lifgam permitted; and nullified in sixty as seif 2.

(כ) Must be careful — meaning there should be sixty in water or not ben yomo; but all permissions of not leaving much and not ceasing etc. do not help if were ben yomo and no sixty in water — Tosafot already rejected; and Rashba siman 463 this view always absorbs — therefore no remedy except as above; and Shulchan Aruch wrote thus for clarity that even lenient views nevertheless must be careful etc.; therefore good to kasher before fifth hour but not for law.
