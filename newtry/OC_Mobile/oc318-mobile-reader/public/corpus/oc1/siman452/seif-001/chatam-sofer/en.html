Taz s.k. 11 — see general rules Pesach 52; and see Mishnah 10 chapter 5 of Machshirin; and see well Rambam chapter 7 Laws of Food Impurity halacha 2; and one may compare this to impurity — smoke rising from hot water as liquid is considered as Rambam wrote; and presumably also upper part anyway does not conduct chametz expulsion from lower with it upward as we find similarly regarding blood that does not percolate upward; therefore Taz was precise and wrote no prohibition except regarding impurity [and also noten taam bar noten taam d'heter — it appears there is custom to rely on this — see].
