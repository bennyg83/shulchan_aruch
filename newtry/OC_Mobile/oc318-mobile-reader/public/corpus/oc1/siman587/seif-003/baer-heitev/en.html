And midway. The Maharil wrote: all the people should take care not to spit during the teki'ot, and likewise not to whisper, so that all the people hear the shofar; and it is proper that the young children remain at home, for specifically with the Megillah we do [bring them] because of joy; and if it is impossible to leave them [home], the woman should keep them with her in the women's synagogue so that they not interrupt. And the Taz wrote: those standing outside at a distance (if it appears clear to him that he heard the sound, he has fulfilled [his obligation]) see there; and see Halachos Ketanos, siman 276.
