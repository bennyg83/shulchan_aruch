Se'if 3, "the one who hears." There is a dispute in the Yerushalmi; and according to the interpretation of the Ramban that the Rosh cited in chapter 4 of Rosh Hashanah, se'if 8; and likewise the Rashba compelled [the conclusion] according to this interpretation, for if [it were] according to the first interpretation, that [it] refers to our Mishnah "He blew the first [blast] and prolonged [it]," etc., then even the first teki'ah would not count for him, since he interrupted in between, as is written in siman 590, se'if 8. And see the Magen Avraham in the name of the poskim that the halachah follows the first view; and as the Rambam and other poskim wrote, for we hold in every place like the opinion that was established last in the Gemara.
And some say. See the Rosh there.
And the same applies. For according to the first view, which is the anonymous Tanna of the Yerushalmi, the Gemara regarding a pit deals with [a case] where there is not the measure of a teki'ah; and according to R' Abba, even if it has the measure of a teki'ah.
