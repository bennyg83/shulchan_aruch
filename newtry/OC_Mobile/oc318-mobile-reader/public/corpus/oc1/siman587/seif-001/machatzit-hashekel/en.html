(1) (s.k. 1) The pit — meaning it is a building underground etc. In mishnah chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah which teaches pit and cistern: Rashi, Rambam, and Raavad explain a place enclosed by partitions on the ground; and likewise mishnah 2 chapter 4 of tractate Bava Batra — Raavad explains there too that it is on the ground. Rashbam explains there: pit — they dig in hard ground that holds water without building. Cistern — they dig a pit in loose earth and build for it a stone wall — end quote; and this is as Magen Avraham wrote. And Tosefta there in Bava Batra wrote that from Rambam's words in his work chapter 25 of Laws of Sales it appears he holds like Rashbam's explanation — see there; and Magen Avraham proved from that Shulchan Aruch writes "within the pit or cistern" and it is Rambam's language and they did not also translate cistern; nevertheless it must be he holds here too cistern means building under the ground, i.e. cistern. And likewise must be he holds for Rosh who disagrees with Shulchan Aruch per his explanation in the Gemara that those standing outside the pit certainly did not fulfill, for certainly they only heard kol habarah (reverberating sound). But for those standing inside the pit there is a difference whether they heard kol habarah or shofar sound:

It cannot be that cistern means building on the ground; if so what is different in the synagogue from cistern, for per Rosh those standing outside did not fulfill.
