Magen Avraham, seif katan 4, writes so, as does Bach. Taz, at the end of seif katan 3, disagrees. Logic also supports Taz: the clear sound affects the adjacent air, which in turn affects the air next to it. Before that clear sound reaches the ear, it has already become confused with the echo of the cellar, and the sounds are not distinguishable. Why, then, does one who blows the shofar while ascending from a pit fulfill his obligation? One may answer that even if the precise instant cannot be measured, once he first brings the shofar outside, before the clear sound can travel back into the pit, his ears have already emerged, for he is blowing while ascending. This is readily understood.
