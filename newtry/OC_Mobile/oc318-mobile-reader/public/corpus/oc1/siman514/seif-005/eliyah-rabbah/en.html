[5] [Levush] So that it be distanced, etc., and go out, etc. Meaning: that it remain so; but if he draws the oil a bit away from the wick so that they can grasp the wick and draw it out a bit more than it was at first outside the oil, and he again tilts the oil toward it, it is permitted so that it burn well (Taz). And it seems he did not see Maharil, who wrote that the synagogue sexton cannot fix the synagogue candlesticks while they are burning, for by his touching, oil will diminish from them; specifically after they have gone out he should again fix and light them; until here. It is permitted to walk with a burning oil lamp in his hand even if he moves it about and perhaps he will draw oil from the wick, for it is not a psik reisha; however, to tilt the lamp to one side to draw [oil] is a psik reisha and forbidden (Maharil there). And according to this it would be possible to permit through cutheans, for a psik reisha is permitted with him, as written at the end of siman 253 and siman 277, seif 3; and see siman 276, s.k., and siman 680.
