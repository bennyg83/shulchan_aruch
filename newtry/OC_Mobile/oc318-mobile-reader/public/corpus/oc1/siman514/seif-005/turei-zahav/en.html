But of the synagogue, etc. Rashal wrote: even in his house, if he lights for honor of Yom Tov, even if he already blessed on Yom Tov lamps, it is not called purposeless unless he does so to show his wealth — end of his words; and it appears to me that also this is not called purposeless, since he intends to show wealth on this day and certainly intended for honor of the day. And it is written in Tur that at every hour, even by day, there is mitzvah in lighting lamps in the synagogue.
