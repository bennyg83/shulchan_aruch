But of the synagogue, etc. But near dark, even though it is not certainly night, it is permitted to light a lamp even though it is not for a mitzvah — for behold he benefits from it immediately, and there is no reason to forbid it because it appears that he lights for the need of night (Beit Yosef, Maharar"m"i). And what he wrote regarding a purposeless lamp — it is forbidden to light — that is to say, even if he lights it for the honor of Yom Tov, nevertheless it is forbidden; only a synagogue lamp is permitted because of the honor of the house of our God (Beit Yosef and Rabbeinu Yeruham).
