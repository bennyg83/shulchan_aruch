[7] [Levush] Rather he does outside, etc. His words are not precise in this, for at the beginning of seif 3 he wrote Tosafos' reason [Beitzah] daf 22 that it immediately extinguishes; and Rosh challenged them from a tube at the beginning of siman 265 that constantly drips to the lamp's light — why is it extinguishing when removing a little; therefore he wrote another reason, namely specifically when he does outside, etc.; if so he should not have written in the gloss what he wrote inside, or he should have written them as a dispute. Behold Bach at the beginning of siman 265 challenged why forbidden with a tube and answered like Tosafos' reason mentioned above that it immediately extinguishes, etc.; and I wonder — behold Rosh challenged Tosafos' reason from a tube; and perhaps it is reasonable to him like Yam Shel Shlomo siman 21 to answer Rosh's difficulty — we are concerned with a tube that he will take all the oil, the dripping will stop, and it is like extinguishing; or like Taz's answer that we decree: when removing from a tube he will also remove from oil in the lamp itself. And in Magen Avraham seif katan 19 he challenged those who permit cutting the lamp with fire from the tube — see there; and there is no difficulty, for it is reasonable to him the answers mentioned above and like Tosafos; and further, even for Rosh one may say it is permitted like increasing wicks to kindle, as he himself wrote seif katan 6 — but nearby I will clarify also on this to forbid; and in Olas Shabbat he wrote: I do not understand Rashal's words — what of it if he takes everything, behold the oil in the tube is not attached to the oil in the lamp, and when he takes the tube he does not extinguish the light — until here; and in my humble opinion Rashal's words are simple: since it constantly drips drop by drop, the lamp constantly burns with the drip, and as long as he does not take everything it still drips and the light remains clear as when he does not take; but when he takes everything the dripping stops and the light is extinguished; but I have difficulty on Rashal's answer from Pesachim daf 11 where R' Yehuda who permits with a tube is challenged — R' Yehuda decreed lest he eat from chametz when he checks inside Pesach, lest it is different with a tube that they did not decree so much lest he eat everything, unlike chametz where even in part it is forbidden; and for Taz's answer mentioned above it is simple that there is difficulty; requires study.
