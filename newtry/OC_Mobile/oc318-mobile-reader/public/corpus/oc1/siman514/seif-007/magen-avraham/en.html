One does not reduce. And Tosafos explained that they place straw in the air of the lamp so the sides not fall together, and when dry they remove it — forbidden to remove it on Yom Tov because it fixes a vessel; and so Yeshuot Shalom; and Rambam wrote: two vessels joined together at the beginning of their making, such as two lamps or cups — one does not break them into two, for it is like fixing a vessel — end of his words; implying if they did not join at the beginning of making but afterward joined incidentally, permitted to open them; deals with 506.
