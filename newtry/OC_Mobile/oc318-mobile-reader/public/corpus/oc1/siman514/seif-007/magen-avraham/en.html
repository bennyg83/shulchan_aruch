One may not separate them. Tosafos explain that straw was placed within the hollow of the lamp so that its walls would not collapse together, and after it dried the straw was removed. It is forbidden to remove it on Yom Tov because one thereby fashions a utensil. Yam Shel Shlomo rules likewise. Rambam writes: 'Two utensils that were joined together when they were first made, such as two lamps or two cups, may not be separated into two, because this resembles fashioning a utensil.' This implies that if they were not joined when first made, but later became attached to one another incidentally, it is permitted to separate them. See the end of siman 340.
