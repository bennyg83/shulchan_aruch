10) There. Or the pot. This extinguishing is not considered food preparation, even when he has no other place to set the pot. Extinguishing the piece of wood does not contribute anything to the food but merely prevents damage. Although this appears no less than preparatory work for food that could not have been performed before Yom Tov, which we rule is permitted as stated above, siman 509, in the gloss, this opinion holds that not all preparatory work for food was permitted. Only acts more directly connected to preparing the food were permitted. Mishnah Berurah, subsection 4.
