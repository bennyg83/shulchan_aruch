And it — Taz brought Yeshuot Shalom chapter 4 siman 11: a candle cut with fire for two lamps when not yet lit — and do not say it is for the wick, for the viewer says he intends lighting generally, unlike cutting in the middle of wax which appears he intends two lamps and fixing a vessel; nevertheless permitted; and what he wrote that the second side should not light — forbidden — he challenged him either way; and if possible the second side not light, what does it teach — obvious it is fixing a vessel like a knife; Magen Avraham 8; and it will be explained there; and what Taz permitted on a burning wax candle to light on the second side to melt wax to hasten extinguishing as he wrote letter 16 — such is comparable to increasing wicks; Eshel Avraham letter 9 sided to be stringent; and it will be explained in Magen Avraham. And what Tosafos permit cutting a candle with fire Beitzah 22a s.v. ve-ha-mesatef — it appears they explain in this matter to hasten extinguishing, and Rosh would agree to this, for otherwise forbidden like a knife; and for Magen Avraham letter 8 one may say it deals when the second side does not light, per Rama's wording in seif 3 to shorten it — see there; as said.
