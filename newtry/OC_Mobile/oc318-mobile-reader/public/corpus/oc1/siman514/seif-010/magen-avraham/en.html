Permitted to remove. From Rambam's wording it implies he holds permitted to remove even with a utensil, since the manner is to flick it with his hand — it is not fixing a vessel; but forbidden to cut the head of the wick even by hand, since the manner is to do it with a utensil — it is fixing a vessel, as Beit Yosef wrote; but Yeshuot Shalom wrote: the world is careful not to trim at all, and one should not be lenient with them lest they come to trim with a utensil like tongs; and permission is only to flick with a finger — end of his words; and although Rosh wrote permitted to extinguish what prevents the lamp's lighting, perhaps by hand it is forbidden; and so it is reasonable, for extinguishing is permitted only for food for souls need, and we do not say "from when permitted," etc.
