(Seif katan 10) And permitted, etc. — it appears to me he inferred thus; i.e. Maharil, who is the master of this law, inferred thus.

And requires study, for in a bonfire, etc. — i.e. on the contrary it is proven that here dealing with a lamp, even without wind it is forbidden; for in a bonfire by law even common wind is permitted; and nevertheless forbidden when there is common wind, for we decree common wind lest it not be common; all the more a lamp where when there is common wind it is forbidden by law, for common wind also extinguishes a lamp — one should forbid even now when there is no common wind, decreeing lest common wind come, for it is common; and see Shabbos daf 120.

And what he wrote siman 276 seif 2: and even if there is no one there, etc. — that is Maharil's proof as above.

Therefore when there is no one there, etc. — it is like a decree for a decree.

And the custom, etc. — and the opposite is the case: carrying to his house is forbidden even without common wind, as above; and to insert it in sand on a weekday is permitted per Rama who wrote on "and some permit to cut," etc., and so is the custom; and that is Tosafos' view, and for them also inserting in sand while burning is permitted as Magen Avraham wrote; and in truth against Magen Avraham seif katan 7, even for Rosh it is permitted since he does not take anything from what burns; and all this specifically when he does not make a hollow, i.e. the sand is not hard but soft, or the hollow was already made erev Yom Tov, as Magen Avraham wrote above siman 498 seif katan 32.
