[10] A wax candle, etc. Kenesses HaGedolah, siman 314, wrote: it is forbidden to heat a wax candle to stick it to a menorah, for it is smearing; until here. And Magen Avraham wrote upon him: and it appears to me that if he does not rub it, it is not smearing; rather it is forbidden to stick it as a decree lest he smear; until here. And I wonder: for Maharil wrote in the name of Or Zaru'a that it is forbidden to stick it by its middle to the wall so that it go out when it reaches there, for he is liable because of extinguishing; until here. And possibly Maharil deals with when he does not heat, for then there is no place to decree because of smearing; and Kenesses HaGedolah and Magen Avraham deal with sticking it to a menorah so that it stand well and not fall, and when it reaches [the end] it does not go out.
