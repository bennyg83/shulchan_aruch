[10] Wax candle, etc. Knesset HaGedolah wrote siman 514: forbidden to heat a wax candle to stick it in a lamp because it is smoothing — until here; and Magen Avraham wrote on it: it appears to me that if he does not rub it, it is not smoothing; only forbidden to stick as a decree lest he smooth — until here; and I wonder — Maharil wrote in the name of Or Zarua that it is forbidden to stick it in its middle to the wall so it extinguishes when it reaches there, for he is liable because of extinguishing — until here; and perhaps Maharil deals when he does not heat it, for then there is nothing to decree because of smoothing; and Knesset HaGedolah and Magen Avraham deal with sticking it in a lamp so it stands well and does not fall, and when it reaches it is not extinguished.
