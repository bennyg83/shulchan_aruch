Lest he come to use. Since per Rosh's words that even if he takes it from there it is not called using the tree but a decree lest he climb the tree to take it — end of his words; requires study, for it is found in Eruvin daf 32: when one takes his eruv from atop a tree, behold he uses the tree — see there; and one may answer: he challenged to decree lest he climb the tree, for in any case it deals when placed below ten handbreadths and he need not climb; and further one may answer: we should change — a decree for a decree: climbing itself is a decree lest he detach; and see siman 528 (deals with 514 seif katan 6).
