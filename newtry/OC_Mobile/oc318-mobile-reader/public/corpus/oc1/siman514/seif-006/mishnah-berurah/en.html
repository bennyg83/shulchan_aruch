{36} Lamp, etc., from erev Yom Tov — for on Yom Tov obviously forbidden, for placing is included in use; but even if he placed it erev Yom Tov intending it remain there on Yom Tov — forbidden, for since on Yom Tov one may move the lamp, we are concerned he will come to take from there; and this too is included in use that the Sages forbade; and as above siman 516 seif 2 in the gloss; and this is what the Mechaber concluded "lest they come," etc.; and this law was already repeated above siman 276 seif 4.
