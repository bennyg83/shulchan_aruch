{36} A lamp, etc., from erev Yom Tov: for on Yom Tov it is obvious that it is forbidden, for placing [something upon it] is included in use; rather, even if he placed it from erev Yom Tov with intention that it remain there on Yom Tov, it is forbidden, for since on Yom Tov it is permitted to move the lamp, we are concerned that he come to take from there, and this too is included in the use that the Sages forbade, as above in siman 336, seif 2, in the gloss; and this is what the Mechaber concluded, "lest they come, etc."; and this law was already taught above in siman 277, seif 4.
