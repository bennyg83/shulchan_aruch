Lamp — Taz extended; and see letter 9 letter 10; and Magen Avraham letter 7; and it will be explained nearby. And here I will mention what is done with wax stock (or called statzik): they wrap it in a metal vessel with something like scissors in the vessel that cut in wax and prevent burning more; at least to do this before lighting, for after lighting per the Mechaber and Rosh's view one should forbid this; but for Mishna Berurah one may say permitted — Magen Avraham 8; and nevertheless many hold like Rosh — proof from tube; and see Olas Shabbat. And because of fixing and weekday acts with a utensil, do not forbid all this, for he does not cut the wick, only wax alone — see seif 8.

And behold the Mechaber's wording is exactly Tur's wording; but Tur concluded causing extinguishing is permitted, and the Mechaber omitted it intentionally, explaining Tur's words before lighting specifically (since Tur wrote he wants to light it) — if so, no causing extinguishing at all here; and on the contrary, when lit there is no repair at all, even to place only something that prevents on it like a knife and the like; and even on a weekday one may say forbidden, behold causing extinguishing is forbidden; and from the precision of our master Maran the distributor in Beit Yosef it appears that for Rosh it is obvious to him that to cut with fire of wax while burning is forbidden like a tube; but to place on it something preventing outside it is doubtful; and from Tur it appears to him forbidden also this; and therefore Beit Yosef returned and wrote: and so from Rosh that this too is forbidden when lit; and the explanation of "outside it" that he wrote is in a cloth that caught fire — although fire will pass and reach the whole cloth, nevertheless the cloth was not set aside and is fit for lighting, only fire fell on it by accident, therefore permitted causing extinguishing such as placing something preventing outside it, i.e. outside the place of fire; unlike wax, even if he places on the wax something preventing outside the wax, nevertheless wax and wick were both prepared for lighting — all who diminish from one of them while burning, such causing extinguishing is forbidden; and certainly this is from what Rosh wrote: he does not touch the burning object but does something outside it, etc., when the flame reaches there; and he did not write he does no act — it should be understood as said.

And in the holy words of our master Taz — requires study, for from his words it appears after lighting permitted to place around it, not by fire; and "outside" must mean he does no act, only places on it; and how did Taz write therefore to light it — if already lit, forbidden to shorten it, behold placing around permitted; and one may say with difficulty like what is done with scissors or zwengel that Eshel Avraham brought — therefore specifically before lighting he cuts in the body of wax; requires study; and what he wrote to reconcile the tube — deals with 265 and Darkei Moshe there and here; and in Olas Shabbat: if he takes the whole tube it still does not extinguish; and Eshel Avraham; and see Pesachim 11 and Shabbos 42 the sugya; and it will be explained in Magen Avraham 7. And in Yeshuot Shalom chapter 2 34 it implies he holds like Beit Yosef that for R' Yehuda and Tur after lighting, even on a weekday forbidden to place — there is no "outside" except diminishing wax; distant tallow piece is permitted because not attached to wick, unlike a candle; but Taz holds to distinguish between acting on the body of wax — cutting in zwengel — see Or Zarua in Levush, and already explained. If so, one who wants to be stringent l'chatchila like Yeshuot Shalom per Rosh and per Beit Yosef's understanding of Tur and per the simple meaning of the Mechaber — all that was lit, even only something preventing on the wax alone, forbidden, and even to insert in sand on a weekday, and all the more zwengel or scissors to cut some wax — forbidden per this; as I wrote.
