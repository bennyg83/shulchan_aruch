(s.k. 6) To add, etc.: it is forbidden, as written in siman 314, seif 11.
And as stated in the Mishnah, folio 146a: a barrel that was pierced, one should not place wax upon it because he smears. Said R' Yehudah: an incident came before R' Yochanan ben Zakkai and he said, I am concerned for him regarding a chatas; and Rashi explained: perhaps he smeared and it was not on his mind. And Magen Avraham holds that R' Yehudah does not disagree with the first opinion; and so wrote Tiferes Yisrael; rather he brought an incident that occurred, and an incident is decisive.
It implies that it is permitted to move it. For if not so, even where there is no concern of smearing, without that it would be forbidden to move it to place upon a barrel because of muktzeh; and the reason is as Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 499, s.k. 9: for he can place a wick upon it and it is fit for lighting; see there.
