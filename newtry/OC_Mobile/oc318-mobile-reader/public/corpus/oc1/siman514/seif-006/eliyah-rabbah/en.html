[6] [Levush] And all the more so to take [oil], etc. This requires further study, for behold in Agudah, chapter 2 of Beitzah, he wrote that one who takes [oil] who is liable because of extinguishing deals with Shabbos, for on Yom Tov he could extinguish and take the oil; until here. And so one may explain Rashi's commentary on Beitzah 22: that the questioner asks about one who tilted in order to extinguish without need to take oil, and he does not bring the law of one who takes [oil] except to learn from it that when he removes oil it is extinguishing. However, Avodat HaKodesh, Rokeach, and Rabbeinu Yeruham forbade regarding one who takes [oil]; and so it implies in the other poskim; and this requires further study, that Beis Yosef and the Acharonim did not remark on this. And greater than this, Avodat HaKodesh wrote: it is forbidden to place on Yom Tov an eggshell tube, and even of earthenware, full of oil and pierced, upon the mouth of the lamp so that it drip, as they said on Shabbos; see the beginning of siman 265, as a decree lest he take [oil].
