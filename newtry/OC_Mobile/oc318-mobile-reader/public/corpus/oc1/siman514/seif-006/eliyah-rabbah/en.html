[6] [Levush] And all the more to remove oil, etc. Requires study, for in Agudah chapter 2 of Beitzah the one who removes wrote he is liable because of extinguishing — it deals with Shabbat, for on Yom Tov he could extinguish and remove the oil — until here; and so one may explain Rashi's explanation [Beitzah] daf 22 — the questioner asks why he tilted to extinguish without need to remove oil, and does not bring the law of removing, only to learn from it that when he removes oil it is extinguishing; however in Avodat HaKodesh, Rokeach, and Rabbeinu Yeruham they forbade removing; and so appears in other poskim; and requires study why Beit Yosef and Acharonim did not note this from it; and greater than this Avodat HaKodesh wrote: forbidden to place on Yom Tov an eggshell or even earthenware full of oil pierced at the mouth of the lamp so it drips as they said on Shabbat — see beginning siman 265, a decree lest he remove.
