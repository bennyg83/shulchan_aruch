To extinguish. Shiyurei Kenesses HaGedolah in siman 601 forbids drinking the tobacco called tobak because of extinguishing. And Magen Avraham wrote to forbid because of mugmar, which is not equal for every person; see there. But in Responsa Darkei Noam, Orach Chaim, siman 9, he wrote that it is permitted and there is neither [concern of] extinguishing nor mugmar; see there. And see above, siman 502, s.k. 7, what he wrote there. And to cut the tobak on Yom Tov is a complete prohibition. And see siman 504.
