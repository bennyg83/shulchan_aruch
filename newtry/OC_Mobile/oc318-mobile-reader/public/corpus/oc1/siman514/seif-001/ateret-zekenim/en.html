And one may not extinguish the chip of wood, etc. However, if he has a place outside he throws the chip outside; and if it goes out, it goes out — for behold his intent is not to extinguish. And some say only if it is possible to cook the pot without extinguishing, and as Rama wrote afterward. Some permit covering the fire on Yom Tov with ashes so that tomorrow they will find fire on Yom Tov; and some forbid, for it is similar to extinguishing coals (Beit Yosef, Tosafos, Agudah).
