(Taz s.k. 5) And it requires further study: what is different, etc. NB: and it requires further study — for certainly not for nothing does he take burning wood on this side and rest on that side. If because on this side the pot will scorch from much fire, or on the second side there are no woods sufficient for his need to cook the pot and he has no other fire — it is found that he needs ochel nefesh, and extinguishing for the need of ochel nefesh is permitted like kindling; but a burning wick — there is no ochel nefesh in it.
