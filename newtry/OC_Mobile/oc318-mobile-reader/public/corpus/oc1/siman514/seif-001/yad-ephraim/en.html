(Siman 514) In Taz seif katan 2 regarding a knife they made permission that we do not instruct thus — seemingly there is no understanding in these words, for on Shabbat obviously extinguishing is forbidden; if so, the partition for relations — even per what Taz concludes, making a partition on Yom Tov because extinguishing is permitted by law — nevertheless on Shabbat obviously forbidden per Maharam; and see siman 515 in Magen Avraham seif katan 2. And in my humble opinion a scribal error fell in Taz's words, and these words from what Maharam wrote until the word l'chatchila are not in place here, only at the end of the seif katan where he also wrote turning a vessel, etc. — and that too is garbled wording; therefore it appears to me there one must emend these words, and it should read: and what Maharam wrote in Mordechai chapter Kirah to make a partition specifically before Shabbat before books so he does his needs on Shabbat — it is a stringency l'chatchila; this too turning a vessel is permitted for relations as siman 276 at the end — see there — so it should read; and he means: since he has remedy by turning a vessel on the books, therefore there is no complete benefit in making a partition that is permitted; but requires study, for in siman 515 it is found forbidden to make a partition before a lamp or books for relations or needs; and per Taz's words it must deal where impossible to turn a vessel; if so, it must be said Maharam made a stringency as if it were impossible for him to turn a vessel on the books; and from Darkei Moshe's words that Magen Avraham brought siman 515 who wrote in general to forbid, and so in general in the gloss — it appears he holds that although possible to repair another way, nevertheless since he still needs some repair and he makes the repair through the partition, forbidden to make it on Shabbat; and the same on Yom Tov whenever the repair comes through the partition — see there; and possible thus Taz's wording must be read; and what Maharam wrote must be emended; and per this one must make a partition even on Yom Tov itself, since by law permitted to cover, this is not a complete repair; and what he wrote Maharam, etc., means: although there it is not so much repair, for possible to go outside for needs or turn a vessel and the like, nevertheless Maharam was stringent l'chatchila — so appears his intent; requires study; and at the end of Taz's words there is emendation; and on Shabbat too turning a vessel is permitted; and possible afterward should read the words "and what Maharam wrote," etc., until the words l'chatchila.
