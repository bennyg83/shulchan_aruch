(א) Or, in order to be used, he has no advice in order to turn the candle to another place, and he is not allowed to be permitted by the High Court and the High Court, and even if he does not have any other place, and he is not allowed to circumvent in any of his mitzvot, and he is not allowed to turn away from it He said, “And I will make it easier for him to rely on him, and he will not be able to make him look at him, and if he does not want to be able to do so, then he will be able to rely on him, and if he is able to make him sick, he will not be able to make him clean.”

But if he has another house, etc. — see in Beit Yosef who brought Mordechai's view in the name of Rabbeinu Elchanan who is lenient when he will not have another house to dwell in on Yom Tov; and it appears Rama did not rely on him to be lenient in this, for it appears to him that this is far from the matters of food for souls.

Forbidden to extinguish, etc. — see in Peri Chadash siman 496 who rules like Rashba and Ran that just as for applying eye salve it is permitted on the second day of Yom Tov, so too regarding extinguishing a fire or a lamp because of smoke it is permitted — see there; and in Korban Netanel his words are brought and he disagrees — see there. And in truth Beit Yosef in siman 496 also brought there Ran's lenient words and wrote that Tur and other poskim disagree on him; and so in Darkei Moshe the long; and also Yeshuot Shalom siman 31 extended to attack Ran's words on this in letter 31 — see there [and what he wrote in letter 30 "on the second day of Yom Tov" should read "even on the second day of Yom Tov"]. And I found in Meiri in his novellae that he brought views on this and rules like those who forbid — see there; and also Rashba, although among the lenient, concludes that one should be concerned and forbid; therefore certainly it is proper to be stringent in all this unless in the manner Chayei Adam wrote as I copied above.
