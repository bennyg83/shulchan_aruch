It is permitted to extinguish it. Nevertheless, he should not extinguish it directly, but should cast it outside. Rosh. The Bach rules likewise. See siman 502, seif 2.
He will have no place. But if he can eat, even in the non-Jew's house, it is forbidden to extinguish it. It appears to me that the same applies if his eating utensils will burn and he has no others, in which case it is permitted to extinguish the fire. Knesses HaGedolah writes in siman 608 that it is forbidden to smoke tobacco because this involves extinguishing. It appears to me, however, that it is forbidden because of mugmar, since it is not something enjoyed by everyone, as I wrote in siman 511.
