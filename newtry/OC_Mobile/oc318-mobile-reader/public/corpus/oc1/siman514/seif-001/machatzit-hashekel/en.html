(s.k. 1) And one does not extinguish, for not all preparatory acts, etc. — meaning: although we hold in siman 519 like Rabbi Yehuda, who permits preparatory acts that cannot be done from erev Yom Tov, nevertheless we do not rule thus. And here it implies that by law it is forbidden, even though it was not possible to do it from erev Yom Tov. Therefore Rabbeinu Yonah wrote in the name of Ramban, who wrote thus: that from the Torah, preparatory acts that cannot be done from erev Yom Tov — it is permitted to do them on Yom Tov; nevertheless the sages were strict and did not permit preparatory acts that cannot be done from erev Yom Tov except in a place where it appeared to them to be lenient. And Rama, who wrote that some permit if it is impossible to save, etc. — it implies that for him this is a complete permission and we rule thus. This one holds that this is ochel nefesh itself and is permitted even per the rabbanan; Rabbi Yehuda holds that all preparatory acts are forbidden — and as Rabbeinu Yonah wrote in the name of Ran and more poskim.
