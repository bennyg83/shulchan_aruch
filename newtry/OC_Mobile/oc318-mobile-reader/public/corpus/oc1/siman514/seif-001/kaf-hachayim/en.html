(1) [Seif 1] It is forbidden to extinguish a fire on Yom Tov. For the Torah did not permit it except kindling that cannot be done for food for souls otherwise, and we say: from when it was permitted for need, it was also permitted not for need (see above beginning siman 511). But extinguishing is a primary melacha and remains in its prohibition like Shabbat. Levush. And see above siman 278 note 1 and note 2 on which extinguishing one is liable; and further see siman 514 seif 26 and our words there in its place.
