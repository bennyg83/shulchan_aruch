[8] In another lamp, etc. And the same law applies to this lamp itself — Taz challenged what is written siman 502 seif 2 that it is permitted to take wood from one side of a bonfire and place it on another side, and we do not say that immediately it extinguishes like here; and he answered that there it is different in one bonfire — for there the reason is since he does not intend for extinguishing; if so, we may say the same here with a wick he takes out to place in another fire; and what difference whether in this bonfire from end to end or another bonfire adjacent to it; and it seems whoever is lenient there, the same here with a wick; requires study on Rama; and Rashal ruled for both of them — one lenient and one stringent.
