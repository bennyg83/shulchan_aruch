One does not cut, etc. — and if it is permitted to disconnect by hand and make them into two, it was not explained in Acharonim; and seemingly from "cutting" it implies specifically with a utensil, as Yeshuot Shalom wrote in this sugya and in Or Sameach seif 10 — only that since the Gemara needed to find permission through fire, it implies in other manners it is forbidden, for specifically there it appears he intends for lighting as Rashi explained, unlike here where in any case he makes it into two and it appears as fixing a vessel, like the Gemara's question regarding a knife, etc., and as Rashi explained there — except Rambam chapter 4 explicitly permits specifically with a utensil by hand; and it appears he derived his words from what we say in the Gemara "he presses by hand" and he did not explain like other commentators that it refers to the first statement of three to be stringent on "one does not enlarge the wick" except what ascends there, and "one does not cut" — upon this the Gemara says he presses by hand; and so I found in explanation of the Mishnah who explained thus — see there; but one may say that for most commentators who did not explain thus, perhaps they do not hold like Rambam, and in every case it is forbidden due to fixing a vessel since he wants this; and see Nishmat Adam section 5 of Shabbos in melacha of tearing and cutting — examine; and nevertheless one may say that also for others it is permitted due to the wick being somewhat spoiled through disconnecting, since it disconnects entirely and he must enlarge anew; and further, perhaps without a utensil it is only fixing in an abnormal manner; and see Rashi s.v. mema'akeh; and perhaps he too explains like Rambam; and in Ravyah I also found he wrote one does not cut it with a knife, and perhaps also per him by hand it is permitted; requires study.
