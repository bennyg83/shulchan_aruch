(s.k. 8) That is, etc. That the other side not light is forbidden because of fixing a vessel, as written in seif 8; and that is when he needs both of them.
And in Taz there is a difficulty: how is it possible that he cut by fire and not light the second part unless he extinguishes it? And in Sefer Chemdas Moshe he wrote that it is found by flickering little by little until one part disconnects from its fellow and they do not both light; see there.
But here, where he intends only to shorten it, etc. And Sefer Chemdas Moshe wrote upon this: behold nevertheless it is a psik reisha. And in my humble opinion there is no difficulty according to what Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 308, s.k. 36, in the name of Maggid Mishneh regarding making a vessel, as follows: it appears that whenever he does not intend, it is not proper to say it is a psik reisha and to hold him liable, because when he intends he does melacha, and when he does not intend he does no melacha at all, for he does not want to make a vessel of it. It resembles trimming a splinter: whenever he does not trim it to pick his teeth he is exempt, for whatever is because of fixing a vessel, one who does not fix it is exempt, even according to R' Yehudah who holds liable for something unintentional; end of his words. And there it implies that even a rabbinic prohibition is absent; and the same law applies here. And that it is forbidden by means of a utensil because of fixing a vessel, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 9: one must say that since its way is by means of a utensil they were stringent; and see Magen Avraham s.k. 20.
And according to this view, etc. This does not refer to what was written before this that Rama disagrees with Yam Shel Shlomo; rather it is a matter by itself and refers to the words of Shulchan Aruch. That is to say: according to this view, namely the view of those who permit that Rama wrote, do not err that Rama's intent is to add upon the Mechaber's words who permits placing around it something that prevents burning, [as if] Rama added that it is also permitted to cut it by fire; but Rama agrees that we require placing around it specifically before lighting. Upon this Magen Avraham wrote that this is not so, for the view of "some interpret" that Magen Avraham wrote is the view of Tosafos, who hold that the prohibition of taking [oil] is because he dims its light; and according to them, even while it is burning it is permitted to cut it by fire, all the more so to place around it something that prevents burning even while it is burning.
And see what I wrote above: even according to the first reason, namely Rosh, whom the Mechaber ruled like. Granted that according to Rosh, when it is burning it is forbidden to cut from it by fire; nevertheless to place around it something that prevents burning is permitted even while it is burning, since he does not take from the burning object it is permitted; not like Beis Yosef's understanding of Rosh's words, as above in s.k. 7.
