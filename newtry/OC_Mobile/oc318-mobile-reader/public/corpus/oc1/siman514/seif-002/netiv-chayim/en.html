(Magen Avraham seif katan 7) For otherwise there would be no difficulty. Editor's note: and one should not say this is a dispute and we rule like the Rabbis that causing extinguishing is permitted when there is loss; if so, where there is no loss it is forbidden only rabbinically — if so, why does the baraita teach one who removes is liable; it must be said even per Rosh's words that where it says liable, it is rabbinic lashes as Tosafos wrote — it appears and looks like extinguishing; otherwise what is this that he says "and we hold like the Rabbis" — even for R' Yehuda who concludes in chapter 22 that R' Yehuda does not forbid causing extinguishing from the Torah, as written "you shall not do any melacha" and this is not doing — see there. But this proof may be rejected, for we find that Rosh who wrote "and we hold like the Rabbis" is only citing Tosafos' wording, but he himself holds liable as the baraita teaches from the Torah; and per him, even if we rule like R' Yehuda, there is difficulty; only per this, what does he contradict — Tosafos' explanation from the tube that does not contradict the lamp's light: there the reason is causing extinguishing where there is no loss; there it does not teach liable but we learn Rosh holds: we who rule like the Rabbis — causing extinguishing even without a place of loss.
