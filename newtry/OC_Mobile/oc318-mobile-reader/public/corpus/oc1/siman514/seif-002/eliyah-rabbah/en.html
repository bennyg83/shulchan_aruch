[2] Another house, etc. One may eat in a gentile's house (in Mordechai in Beit Yosef it does not imply so, and it requires study). And it appears to me that the same law applies when one's eating vessels burned and he has no other vessels — it is permitted to extinguish (Magen Avraham). And likewise when there is much fire and it damages the food (Taz siman 512 s.k. 3). And see siman 502 seif 1 in the Hagah, which implies specifically at the hour when one fixes the food it is permitted to extinguish, and not before then (Magen Avraham siman 507 s.k. 8).
