Or. See Taz, Beitzah 22a; see letter 1; and Beis Yosef: according to the view that it is the law but we do not instruct [to act so], covering with a sheet on Yom Tov; and according to the Ramban's view that by rabbinic law it is forbidden, even according to R' Yehudah it is forbidden; and see siman 315, and Rambam regarding a sheet that sees the lamp; see there. And it appears at first glance that for machshirin we also say mitoch, for marital relations [are permitted] through mitoch; see Kesuvos 7a, and see Bach. However, according to what appears in siman 518 in Magen Avraham at first glance, and Levush here, that we do not say mitoch except for carrying out and kindling, not for other melachos according to the law, if so one may say a sheet is forbidden on Yom Tov; and on the second day of Yom Tov, Eshel Avraham letter 4 inclined to be lenient on the second day of Yom Tov; see there; and see Ohr Zarua there: behold, on the first day of Yom Tov it is forbidden even for mitzvah relations.
