To tilt the lamp, etc. Pieces of tallow placed on a lamp — permitted to take one from the pieces distant from the wicks, and although he causes extinguishing, it is permitted only in a place of loss as written siman 514 seif 22; it is different there, for when fire reaches there it actually extinguishes; but here he only prevents it from burning — permitted even for Rosh (Yeshuot Shalom siman 28); and even though Bach wrote in the name of Rashal to forbid after his book — we follow his later book; and so seif 3 in the gloss and siman 502.

To add wicks. And likewise it is permitted to fold a wax candle so it burns quickly — see seif 9. Forbidden to heat a wax candle to stick it in a lamp because it is smoothing as written siman 514; and it is written in Sefer HaZichronot that it is forbidden to touch wax lest he smooth (as Knesset HaGedolah siman 514). And it appears to me that when he sticks it in the lamp it is not smoothing, for smoothing is a derivative of erasing — if he does not rub it, it is not smoothing; only forbidden to stick as a decree lest he smooth, as in the mishnah chapter 23 of Shabbos; but permitted to move it, for they did not decree lest he smooth, for we taught: and one does not put wax on it — implying it is permitted to move it — end of his words.
