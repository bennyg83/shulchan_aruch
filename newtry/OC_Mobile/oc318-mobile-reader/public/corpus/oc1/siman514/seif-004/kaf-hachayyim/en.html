4) There. Even if he sees his house burning. Even if his house will burn and he will have no other house in which to eat, extinguishing is forbidden. This is the view of Shulchan Aruch, which rules according to Rif and Rambam, as explained in Beis Yosef. See there. Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 2 of Beitzah, siman 30, rules likewise. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1, also writes this according to Shulchan Aruch. See there. Rama, however, in Darkei Moshe, subsection 1, disagrees with the aforementioned words of Beis Yosef and permits extinguishing if one will have no other place in which to eat. This is why Rama presents this permission here in his gloss to Shulchan Aruch as an alternative opinion. See below, subsection 23.
