(4) There. And even if he sees his house burning. And even if his house will burn and he will have no other house to eat in — forbidden to extinguish. So is the view of Shulchan Aruch who ruled like Rif and Rambam as explained in Beit Yosef — see there; and so Yeshuot Shalom chapter 2 of Beitzah siman 30; and so Or Sameach note 1 per Shulchan Aruch's view — see there. Only that Maran in Darkei Moshe note 1 disagrees with Beit Yosef's words mentioned and his view is to permit to extinguish if he will not have another place to eat there — and this is what he wrote here in the gloss of Shulchan Aruch this permission in the name of some; and see below note 23.
