{27} That same day: for if he wants to prepare for another day, even for the second day of Yom Tov, it is forbidden.
{28} To cut the head of the wick: meaning the burnt [charred] wick; and this is not considered fixing a vessel in that he removes the coal; but to cut with a utensil from the body of the wick, it is reasonable that it is forbidden, as the Mechaber stated plainly below at the end of this siman.
{29} And remnants of oil and a wick, etc.: that is to say, to exclude the view of the poskim who hold that if they went out on Shabbos it is forbidden to light them afterward on Yom Tov, and likewise if they went out on the first day of Rosh Hashanah it is forbidden to light them on the second day; it teaches us that it is permitted. And see above at the end of siman 501 in Mishnah Berurah what we wrote on this in the name of the Acharonim, that ideally it is good to be stringent. And in any case one should see to light the wicks from the other side.
