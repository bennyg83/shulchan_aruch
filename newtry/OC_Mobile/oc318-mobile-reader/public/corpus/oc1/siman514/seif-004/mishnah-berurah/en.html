(א) At the same time, Daam wants to prepare for another day even for a second sentence:

{28} To cut the head of the wick — meaning the wick that is burning; and one does not consider this fixing a vessel in what removes the coal; but to cut with a utensil from the body of the wick it is reasonable that it is forbidden, as the Mechaber summarized below in this siman.

{29} And leftover oil and wick, etc. — i.e. unlike poskim who hold if they went out on Shabbat, forbidden to relight them afterward on Yom Tov; and if they went out on the first day of Rosh Hashanah Yom Tov, forbidden to light them on the second day — it teaches permitted; and see above end of siman 501 in Mishna Berurah what we wrote on this in the name of Acharonim that l'chatchila good to be stringent; and in any case one should light the wicks from the other side.
