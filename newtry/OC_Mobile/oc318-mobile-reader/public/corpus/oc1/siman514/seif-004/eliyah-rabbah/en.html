[4] [Levush] In order to have marital relations, etc. Even for mitzvah relations and turning a vessel over on the lamp — permitted even on Shabbat (Rashal siman 40). A partition is permitted on Yom Tov; and Taz extended to be lenient like Rokeach when he has no partition or turning a vessel over to extinguish the lamp; and it appears to me to be lenient on the second day of Yom Tov in exile: one — behold Ran brought it, Beit Yosef siman 496 permits; and so Avodat HaKodesh daf 42; and likewise to extinguish because of monetary loss like applying eye salve — see there; and further I saw in Shibolei HaLeket daf 38 he ruled like R' Yeshaya that it is permitted for relations and because of monetary loss even on the first day of Yom Tov; and so ruled at the end of Sefer Tanya; and Agudah chapter 2 of Beitzah in the name of Ravyah and Rashba.
