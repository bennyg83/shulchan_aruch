[4] [Levush] In order to have marital relations, etc. Even mitzvah relations; and overturning a vessel upon the lamp is permitted even on Shabbos (Maharshal, siman 30). A partition is permitted on Yom Tov; and Taz elaborated to be lenient like Rokeach when he has no partition or overturning of a vessel to extinguish the lamp. And it appears to me to be lenient on the second day of Yom Tov of the Diaspora: first, behold the Ran, whom Beis Yosef brought in siman 496, permits; and so Avodat HaKodesh, folio 42; and likewise to extinguish because of monetary loss like applying eye-salve; see there. And further, I saw in Shibolei HaLeket, folio 38, that he decided like R' Yeshayah that it is permitted for marital use and because of monetary loss even on the first day of Yom Tov; and so he ruled at the end of Sefer Tanya; and so wrote Agudah, chapter 2 of Beitzah, in the name of Ra'avyah and Rashba.
