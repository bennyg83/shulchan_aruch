Wax candle. This seif is Rosh and Tosafos' view who hold that causing faster extinguishing is not permitted except when the cause is something outside the lamp, as written here placing around something inhibiting kindling; but he may not diminish kindling by taking from its body any part — that is extinguishing; and Rosh proved this: if you say even in this permitted, difficult from what is written regarding a tube on the mouth of the lamp that is forbidden, lest he remove from it — because extinguishing will hurry afterward, since it does not contradict the lamp's light now, for the lamp is always full of oil because from the tube it constantly drips to the lamp's light; even if he removes a little from oil in the tube it does not contradict the lamp's light; rather the reason is because it causes faster extinguishing — oil and wick both cause lighting, and one who diminishes from one hurries extinguishing and forbidden even for the Rabbis of R' Yehuda who permit making a partition in vessels even full, even new, and perhaps they will crack and extinguish the flame — nevertheless here forbidden; therefore forbidden on a wax candle except to place around it, but not to cut from it at all, even by fire; therefore Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote here "he wants to light it" — for if already lit, forbidden to shorten, for it is extinguishing; and Mahari Chagiz challenged: if it is not yet lit, why did they write to place around it specifically — behold even to cut from it permitted, for there is no causing extinguishing here; this is no difficulty, for regarding a knife obviously forbidden to cut in every manner as in the mishnah "cut with fire at the mouth of two lamps" — only by fire must he cut from it, and he does not want this, for he wants a candle piece remain to light another time; if he lights it by fire he must light all and nothing remains; therefore he has no remedy except before lighting to place around something inhibiting the light; this is simple; and those who permit are Tosafos who hold prohibition is only if it immediately contradicts the lamp's light like one who removes from oil, or tilts oil and distances it from the wick; but if it does not contradict the light now, only hastens extinguishing afterward — permitted, even if he takes somewhat from its body; and one may answer for Tosafos' view on Rosh's difficulty from tube prohibition on the lamp's mouth: since if he removes from oil in the lamp, all agree forbidden, since at the time of taking it contradicts the lamp's light; therefore they also forbade oil in the tube, lest he remove from it and thereby come to remove also from oil in the lamp — what difference between this removal and that; so it appears; and Rashal answered on the tube: lest he remove all the oil — this is strained in my humble opinion; and it appears to me: if he wants to tilt a wax lamp's fire downward a bit so the wax melts and he returns it upward, and does this many times so quickly all the candle burns — and likewise tallow candle permitted even per Rosh, although he caused extinguishing through the body of the candle — nevertheless permitted, for through this tilting he kindles more and comparable to increasing wicks mentioned nearby in the gloss. Mordechai chapter Kol Kitvei wrote: some permit on Yom Tov to place a knife or Yom Tov-movable object on a wax lamp not at the place of its lighting, for there is only causing extinguishing; but R' Yoel explained that even on Yom Tov causing extinguishing is permitted only in a place of damage specifically — end of his words. Rashal chapter 2 wrote permitted to insert a burning lamp in the synagogue in sand on a weekday so it not burn more than what goes outside; and although Mordechai wrote R' Yoel wrote even such we do not permit except in damage — not clear, for it deals with actual causing extinguishing that already extinguished the burning fire, similar to the dispute of R' Yehuda and Rabbis in chapter Kol Kitvei; but what does not actually extinguish, only when it reaches there it has no more to burn and finishes by itself — permitted l'chatchila per Tosafos' words, etc.; however to permit l'chatchila does not look right to me against Rosh's words, since he diminishes the body of wax that is burning; but to take a tallow piece from the lamp while burning — permitted even l'chatchila and even for Rosh — end of his words; and in Hagahot Ashiri chapter 2 wrote one should be careful not to take a tallow piece even distant from the wick — forbidden; and this is unlike Rashal's words per Mordechai chapter 2; but the primary view appears like Rashal, for we find permitted to take wood that fire did not catch, as siman 502 seif 4; and in my humble opinion inserting the candle in sand on a weekday is also permitted for Rosh, for he forbade only when diminishing oil; and likewise wax; but here he does not diminish, only by inserting in sand it cannot light because sand falls on it — this is exactly like what Tur wrote permitted to place around the lamp something to cause extinguishing — likewise in this insertion in sand; but to place the insertion below in water — this appears forbidden, for it is actual extinguishing and worse than new vessels with water where we hold like the Rabbis who permit, and nevertheless R' Yoel forbade not in a place of damage — all the more here; nevertheless it appears in slight need, even if not great need, one may be lenient even in water, for we did not find R' Yehuda's colleague on this, as above.
