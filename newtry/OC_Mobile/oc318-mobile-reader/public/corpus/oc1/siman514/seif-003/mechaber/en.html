A wax candle that one wishes to light on Yom Tov, and one is concerned it will burn down entirely — one may place around it before lighting it something that prevents it from burning, so that it will go out when the flame reaches that point.Some permit cutting a wax candle with fire — meaning one lights it also from below in order to shorten it — and this is the custom. But cutting it with a knife is forbidden. [Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 6, Hagahot Asheiri, Mordechai, and Tosafot ch. 1 of Beitzah] It is permitted to place] a lamp in a spot where the wind blows in order for it to go out, but it is forbidden to place it there if the wind is already blowing.
