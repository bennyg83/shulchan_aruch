(Seif katan 3) Permitted, etc., he throws it outside. (Rosh) — I did not find in Rosh, nor do I know the source of this law; and perhaps it is a scribal error and should read Yerushalmi. For Rabbeinu Yehuda brought Mordechai's words who wrote on that which is taught: for the house not to become smoky it is permitted — Yerushalmi explains: when there is no air; but when there is air, he throws it outside — end of his words.
