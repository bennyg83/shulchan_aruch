(1) He may place around it, etc. But after he lights it — not so, Beit Yosef and Maharar"l: l'chatchila one should be careful; and my teacher in Bach permitted. But in Levush he brought the view of Beit Yosef and it implies that this is his view. But the Rav in the gloss that he wrote: and some permit cutting a wax candle — for even after it is already burning it is permitted to cut with fire; all the more so to place around it something that prevents burning — so in Darkei Moshe; and so is proven from the indication of the place. If so, he disagrees with Beit Yosef, and this is not per the implication of Levush. However, if he cuts a burning wax candle by lighting it in the middle so that it burns quickly, he should leave it to grasp the light well in the wick so that it tears by itself and he does not tear it by force (Maharar"m"i).
