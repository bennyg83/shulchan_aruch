(1) That he light it. Therefore one should warn those who have a zwengel that the candle burns only until it reaches the zwengel — not to place it therein on Yom Tov unless before they light, per Maharil. But Magen Avraham wrote as follows: Rosh wrote that although causing extinguishing is permitted as written siman 514 seif 22 — that is when he does not touch the burning object but only causes something outside the lamp, as written here that he places around it something that inhibits kindling; but he may not diminish kindling by taking from its body any part such as oil and wick, for both cause lighting and one who diminishes from one of them hastens extinguishing; therefore it appears to me that Rosh forbade only taking from the body of the burning object; but when he places something outside it, it is permitted; and so is implied from his wording and from Tur's wording, who did not write the word "before he lights it" — implying even after lighting it is permitted; therefore Rama wrote "and some permit," etc., that he argues on the first reason. And the remaining piece is not forbidden due to muktzeh, for it was not muktzeh from erev Shabbat; and also he could have caused it extinguishing — if so he did not remove his mind from it — Arsi"r Ra"t. And nevertheless it appears to me that a mitzvah candle lit on Yom Tov — even though it went out — it is forbidden to benefit from it and eat it, since it was set aside for its mitzvah like sukkah decoration — end of his words. Magen Avraham.

(2) That they light. Explanation: specifically in order to light both as one; but if he acts so as to disconnect the first part so the other side will not light — forbidden — Yeshuot Shalom. But from Rama's wording it implies that even if he does not need both, only that he acts to shorten it — permitted; and per this view it is obvious that it is permitted to insert a candle in sand even on a weekday when it is burning — Magen Avraham; and so too Taz see there. And see siman 498 seif katan 18 — I wrote in the name of Magen Avraham that it is forbidden to insert on a weekday because he makes a hollow — see there; and so is Shelah's view. And Taz permits in slight need to insert the candle even in water — see there.
