One does not enlarge, etc. — see Mishna Berurah; and it is reasonable that even by hand it is forbidden; see Chayei Adam in general 95 that twisting by hand is also liable, and even less than the measure — behold it is forbidden from the Torah; and so is proven from Shulchan Aruch's wording that wrote "but he presses by hand in order to harden it" — implying that to enlarge l'chatchila even by hand is forbidden; and see Or Sameach.
