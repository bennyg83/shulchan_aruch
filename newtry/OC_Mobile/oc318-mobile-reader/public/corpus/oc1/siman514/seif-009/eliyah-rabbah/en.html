[9] To add wicks, etc. And the same law applies that it is permitted to fold a wax candle so it will burn quickly — see seif 9 (Magen Avraham). And in truth this is included in "some permit cutting a wax candle," etc., that Rama and Levush wrote in seif 3. And behold the view of Rabbi Shlomo Luria there: per the reason of the Rosh above, it is forbidden to cut even by fire; and so Bach wrote, and so it implies in Magen Avraham that I brought. But the view of Taz in s.k. 7 is that the Rosh agrees in this that it is permitted, for it is like adding wicks so it will burn quickly — see there where he elaborated; and likewise the implication of Levush in seif 3, who wrote that this is not grama, etc.; and Rama wrote that such is the custom. Taz wrote: it is permitted to tilt a wax or milk candle downward a bit so the wax will melt and one returns it upward, and one does this many times so that quickly the whole candle will burn — for through this tilting it burns more and it is comparable to adding wicks, until here. And my mind is not at ease with this permission, since drops of wax or milk go from it to loss; and adding wicks is different, for all of it is burned. And see the s.k. after this. And furthermore I saw this: I found in the book Avodat HaKodesh daf 43: to add wicks — if to add light, permitted; and if to hasten its extinguishing, forbidden, for this causes extinguishing not for the need of ochel nefesh, until here. And likewise it implies in the book Maharil daf 51. And according to this, Ran that Beit Yosef brought also deals with increasing light; and likewise Ran's words are proven clearly to one who examines, that he brought proof from burning coals, and there there is no intent. And Rama, Levush, and acharonim who wrote in the name of Ran that even so that it will be extinguished is permitted did not analyze the source of the law. And now it is automatically clear that it is forbidden per the Rosh; whereas for Tosafot it is permitted, since it does not extinguish immediately. Therefore it appears to me to forbid cutting by fire and adding wicks l'chatchila. And furthermore behold Hagahot Maimoni, Mordechai, Hagahot Ashiri, and Or Zarua forbid.
