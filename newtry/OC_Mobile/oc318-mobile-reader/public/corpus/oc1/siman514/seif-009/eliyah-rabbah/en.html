[9] To add wicks, etc. And likewise it is permitted to fold a wax candle so that it burn quickly; see seif 9 (Magen Avraham). And in truth this is included in "some permit to cut a wax candle, etc." that Rama and Levush wrote in seif 3. And behold Maharshal's view there: according to Rosh's reason above, it is forbidden to cut even by fire; and so Bach wrote; and so it implies in Magen Avraham that I cited. But Taz's view in s.k. 7 is that Rosh agrees in this that it is permitted, for it is like increasing wicks so that it kindle quickly; see there where he elaborated; and likewise the implication of Levush in seif 3, who wrote that this is not mere cause, etc.; and Rama wrote that such is the custom. Taz wrote: it is permitted to tilt a wax or tallow candle a bit downward so that the wax melt and return it upward, and so he does many times so that the whole candle burn quickly, for through this tilting he kindles more and it resembles increasing wicks; until here. And my mind is not settled with this permission, since drops of wax or tallow go from it to waste; and increasing wicks is different, for everything is burned; and see the s.k. after this. And further, see this that I found in Sefer Avodat HaKodesh, folio 43: to increase wicks, if to increase light it is permitted, and if to hasten its extinguishing it is forbidden, for this causes extinguishing not for the need of ochel nefesh; until here. And so it implies in Sefer HaManhig, folio 51. And according to this, the Ran whom Beis Yosef brought also deals with increasing light; and so Ran's words are proven clearly to one who examines, for he brought a proof from meat on coals, and there he does not intend. And Rama, Levush, and the Acharonim who wrote in Ran's name that even so that it go out is permitted did not carefully examine the source of the law. And now it is automatically clear that it is forbidden according to Rosh's reason; whereas according to Tosafos it is permitted, since he does not extinguish immediately. Therefore it appears to me to forbid cutting by fire and increasing wicks ideally. And further, behold Hagahos Maimoni, Mordechai, Hagahos Ashiri, and Or Zaru'a forbid.
