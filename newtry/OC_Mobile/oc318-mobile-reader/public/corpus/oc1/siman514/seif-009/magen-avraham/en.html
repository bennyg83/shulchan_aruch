One may not braid them. The same applies that it is forbidden to undo their braid, or to undo a braided wax candle. See siman 303, seif 26.
When no lamp is burning in it. When a lamp is burning and one removes oil from it, he diminishes its fuel and thereby extinguishes, because the light is immediately dimmed. Tosafos. The Rosh writes that even if the light is not dimmed, he is liable because he removes part of the burning substance itself, as stated at the beginning of siman 265 regarding one who takes oil from a perforated vessel that drips into a lamp, who is liable for extinguishing. According to this, it is likewise forbidden to cut a burning candle. This poses a difficulty for the authorities who permit cutting it in seif 3. Tosafos, however, imply that one can distinguish between Shabbos and Yom Tov; see there.
