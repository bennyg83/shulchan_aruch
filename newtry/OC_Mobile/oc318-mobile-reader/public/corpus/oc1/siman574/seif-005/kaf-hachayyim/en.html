5) [Seif 2] He must fast with them. Even if he ate that day before arriving in the city, nevertheless he must complete the fast with them and may not eat. Eliyah Rabbah, os 5; Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 2. All this applies if he is able to cause himself discomfort, but regarding a weak person one may be lenient in this matter. Nevertheless, he certainly may not eat publicly. Mishbetzos Zahav, ibid.; Mishna Berurah in Shaar HaTziyun, os 4.
