Except for the night of immersion. These are the words of the Beis Yosef: We learn in the Yerushalmi: those who long for children engage in marital relations. Said R' Yose: only on the day she immersed. And there is reason to wonder, for they need to engage in relations continually until they know that she has conceived; and it appears to me that it refers to what was said above, that it said it is forbidden to engage in relations, and R' Yose said: except for the night of immersion, which is permitted, end quote. And on this basis he ruled here in the Shulchan Aruch. And this is a great forced reading, to interpret "only" like "except," for he should have said "aside from the day she immersed"; and further, the Tosafos asked how Yocheved was born between the walls, and other commentators too were pressed by this; he should have said simply that it was the night of immersion, as the Shlah wrote according to the ruling of the Shulchan Aruch. Therefore it appears to me [to take] the words as written: that R' Yose holds that even those without children are permitted to engage in relations only on the night of immersion, for he holds that a woman does not conceive except close to her immersion, as is stated in the Gemara in Niddah; and so wrote the Matnos Kehunah on [Bereishis] Rabbah, Parashas Noach, chapter 31; and so wrote the Agudah in explaining the Yerushalmi, and ruled like him. However, from the plain sense of our Gemara and the poskim it is implied that those without children engage in relations always, for we do not hold like R' Yose; but to permit [for others] on the night of immersion has neither root nor branch; and so it appears in all the poskim, who did not write this leniency.
