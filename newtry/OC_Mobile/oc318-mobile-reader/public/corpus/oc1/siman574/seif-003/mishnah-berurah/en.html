Eats a great deal. Rather, he must stop that meal immediately when he remembers, and even if he did not accept the fast upon himself (Magen Avraham).
It is forbidden to treat himself to luxuries. That is, even regarding that concerning which we are lenient to eat outside the techum, nevertheless he should not indulge in his eating, for if he comes to the city his face will be ruddy (Magen Avraham). And there are Acharonim who interpreted it according to its plain sense, that he should not indulge in luxuries in other matters; and as above in siman 568, se'if 12.
