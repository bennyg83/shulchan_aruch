Eats a great deal. Rather, he must stop eating immediately when he remembers (Tosafos; Beis Yosef); and even if he did not accept the fast; see siman 568.
And even not in their presence. This the Mordechai wrote in the name of the Raavyah, who holds that it is permitted to eat privately, only that he should not indulge himself in luxuries; but according to what the Beis Yosef wrote, that we hold like the Tosafos that it is forbidden to eat at all, if so, why does the Rama need to write that he should not indulge himself in luxuries? And one must say that the Rama teaches us that even outside the techum he should not indulge himself in luxuries, for if he comes to the city his face will be ruddy.
