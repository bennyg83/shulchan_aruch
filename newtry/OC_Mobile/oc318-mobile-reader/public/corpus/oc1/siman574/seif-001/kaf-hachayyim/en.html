1) [Seif 1] He must observe all the fasts that they accepted upon themselves. The residents of his city impose upon him the stringencies of the place from which he departed. Rashi, Taanis 10b. It appears that this means even though he did not personally accept the fast upon himself, but the residents of his city accepted it upon themselves. Bach. However, regarding one who explicitly accepted upon himself to fast, it is obvious that he is obligated in all the stringencies, and there is no distinction here between whether he intends to return or not. Taz, s.k. 1; Ateres Zekeinim; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 1.
