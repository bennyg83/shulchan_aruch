1) [Seif 1] He must observe all the fasts that they accepted upon themselves. The residents of his city impose upon him the stringencies of the place from which he departed. Rashi, Taanis 10b. It appears that this means even though he did not personally accept the fast upon himself, but the residents of his city accepted it upon themselves. Bach. However, regarding one who explicitly accepted upon himself to fast, it is obvious that he is obligated in all the stringencies, and there is no distinction here between whether he intends to return or not. Taz, s.k. 1; Ateres Zekeinim; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Mishbetzos Zahav, os 1; Mishna Berurah, os 1.
2) Ibid. He must observe the fast, etc. This refers to a case in which he was in the place where they were fasting at the time the fast was accepted, for otherwise the decree of his community certainly did not take effect upon him, since he is not there on the day of the fast. Taz, ibid.; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. This refers to a case in which they decreed a fast because of a calamity that it should not come, or as an act of repentance. In such a case, it is relevant whether he was there when the fast was accepted or not. However, regarding a fast observed by a particular community because of a decree that had occurred in the past, such as the 20th of Sivan in Poland and the like, no acceptance is required at all, for it is considered a communal fast. He then has the law of one who travels from one place to another, and they impose upon him the stringencies of the place from which he departed if he intends to return. Eliyah Rabbah and Mishbetzos Zahav, ibid.; Mishna Berurah, ibid.
3) Ibid. He must observe the fast, etc. Even if he intends to return only after the calamity has passed, he fasts and completes the fast, for since he intends to return, he is already considered like the residents of the place from which he departed. Shulchan Gavoah, os 2.
4) Ibid. He must observe the fast, etc. One who travels from a place where they are observing the 13 fasts for rain explained below in siman 575 to a place where they are not fasting is obligated to observe all 13, even if he was there only at the time they began. Gevuras Ari; Pischei Olam, os 1.
