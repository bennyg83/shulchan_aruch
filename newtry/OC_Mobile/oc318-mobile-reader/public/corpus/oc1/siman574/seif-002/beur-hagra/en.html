Se'if 2: Even if his intent, etc. Analogous to the latter clause, concerning [one who came] from a place where they are fasting, which deals with a case where he intends to return, as above; and the reason is that one must share with the congregation in their distress.
However, etc. And that is what [he] wrote: he fasts with them; and there is no difficulty from [the wording] 'stipulates,' being analogous to the latter clause; and as written in Chullin, folio 110a.
