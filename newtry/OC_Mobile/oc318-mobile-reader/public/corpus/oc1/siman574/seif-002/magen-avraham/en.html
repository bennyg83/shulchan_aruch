Even if he intends to return. Even though regarding other matters of eating it is permitted to eat privately if he intends to return, even in the city, here it is different, for one must share with the community in their distress (Mordechai; Beis Yosef). And if so, it is possible that when they fast for a distress that has already passed, such as a day of [commemorating] a decree, it is permitted to eat privately if he intends to return; and this requires further study.
