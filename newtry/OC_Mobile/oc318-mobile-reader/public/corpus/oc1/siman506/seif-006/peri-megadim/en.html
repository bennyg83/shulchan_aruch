According to our ruling, however, this applies specifically to challah outside Eretz Yisrael, where one may eat first and separate challah afterward. To separate on Yom Tov from dough rolled before Yom Tov is forbidden. This requires study. Perhaps his reasoning is that challah in Eretz Yisrael is more lenient in this respect: if one rolled the dough before Yom Tov and forgot, he may separate challah on Yom Tov in accordance with Rabbah, since it was not included in the decree, while the father of Shmuel disagrees only regarding challah outside Eretz Yisrael. Moreover, the discussion of produce that is tevel nowadays concerns produce outside Eretz Yisrael. All of this requires study.
