{33} It is forbidden to bake it. That is, with dough; and with flour it is forbidden even to knead. And it is unlike an animal half of which belongs to a Jew and half to a non-Jew, which one may slaughter on Yom Tov, as above in siman 498, se'if 11; for that case is different, since it is impossible [to get] a kezayis of meat without slaughter; but here it is possible to divide beforehand and toil only in his own share.
{34} On Yom Tov. That is, even in one oven, and even in a small oven; for the Mechaber ruled below in siman 507, se'if 6, that it is permitted to bake a full oven of bread even though he needs only one loaf and the rest will remain for a weekday, because the bread bakes well when the oven is full; for that case is different, since all the bread belongs to the Jew and he has authority to eat each one, this or that; and further, perhaps guests will happen upon him and all are fit for Yom Tov. But here, where half belongs to a non-Jew, he is forced to leave that share; and if so he toils in kneading and baking the non-Jew's dough merely for the added improvement of his own bread in general, and it is forbidden.
