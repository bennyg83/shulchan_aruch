(In Shulchan Aruch 'and combine them together') If he rolled two doughs from erev Yom Tov without challah measure in each and they joined on Yom Tov — apparently one could say since challah obligation was not before Yom Tov but now on Yom Tov obligation came by joining, permitted to separate on Yom Tov like kneading on Yom Tov. But it appears per Rashi's reason: although forbidden to separate on Yom Tov because could have separated yesterday — not here since permitted to roll and bake for hot bread — rabbis did not decree; if so without this hot-bread reason permitted to roll today even though obligation came today and could not separate yesterday — not comparable to terumot and ma'asrot that became tevel yesterday, for anyway forbidden to knead yesterday and separate, only since permitted to delay kneading until today for hot bread they did not decree; if so two doughs less than measure kneaded erev Yom Tov — although obligation came today, can combine from yesterday and hot-bread permission does not apply here — no separating challah on Yom Tov. But Beitzah daf 12b on plucking sheaves on Yom Tov: if so we find terumah entitled to its separation, etc.; for Rabbi Yehuda too you find case entering to make dough, decided to pluck on Yom Tov — barrel in days, Rashi: plucking on Yom Tov separates terumah on Yom Tov, see there clear even without hot-bread reason permitted. Perhaps Rashi only gives reason for one who rolls dough on Yom Tov separates on Yom Tov due to challah lacking in decree 'we do not separate' since permitted to knead on Yom Tov for hot bread therefore became tevel on Yom Tov and separate challah — they did not decree; but for one who rolls dough truly separates only if rolled on Yom Tov — not from this reason; rather because not applicable that should have separated yesterday — obligation now comes properly separates; not like sheaves case where entitled since no obligation place before Yom Tov. Still DNA joined today in basket to death leaf needs dough now plucks from nights and because of this permitted today terumah obligation comes now therefore entitled also to state. Not like our case where eating need already done yesterday, never needed separate challah, what renewed today needing challah is combining cause not needed today — no permission because obligation came today. Found in Chok Yaakov siman 497 s.k. 9 wrote perhaps permitted thus combine on Yom Tov and separate since not tevel until now like gemara plucking sheaves, see there. Ateret Tzvi disputes and writes from Tosafot, Agudah, Kol Bo implies even when obligation via combining forbidden separate on Yom Tov; though in my humble opinion not required, nevertheless main for ruling. See Beit Yosef siman 217 on Tosafot Beitzah and Mordechai implies when not joined in basket; see Darchei Moshe, Bach, Pri Chadash, Kaf HaChayim wrote: if neither has measure and obligation via basket combining meaning can eat-and-continue as Taz wrote; more authors; per Chayei Adam disputing Taz there, per his view if joined on Yom Tov can separate — then means when obligation via basket combining and joined on Yom Tov no need these remedies, may separate on Yom Tov; but wording does not imply; see Chayei Adam s.k. 6 there; nevertheless b'dieved, etc.; but if doughs lack measure and he ate — even b'dieved separation afterward fails since did not combine — not obligated challah at all — left from each one piece and combine, end quote; I do not know what master says, for since did not combine not obligated at all and eats in permission — why need separate piece by piece, requires further study.
