Even a first sifting. For Rashbam ruled that a change helps even with a first sifting; but Rashi ruled that it does not help, for it is an av melacha, and it is possible from yesterday, and it is forbidden by rabbinic law. Therefore Tosafos wrote that by means of a non-Jew one should be lenient with some change; and that is like a second sifting by means of a Jewish woman.
