{28} And it is tamei. And likewise terumah and challah nowadays, when we are all tamei met.
{29} He should not bake it. Because it is forbidden to eat; and likewise it is forbidden to move for this reason. And even so, immediately when he called upon it the name challah and it is still in his hand before he set it down, he may move it to any place he wishes, and leave it there until Chol HaMoed, and then burn it.
{30} For one does not burn, etc. And even though burning tamei challah is a mitzvah, nevertheless it does not override Yom Tov, which is a positive commandment and a negative commandment.
{31} He leaves it until evening and burns it. And it implies at first glance that he burns it at night; and see in Biur Halacha what I wrote on this. And all this is with challah of Eretz Yisrael, which is forbidden to the tamei, or in chutz la'aretz in places where they do not practice feeding it to a kohen who is tahor from keri-impurity [namely an adult who immersed, or a minor less than nine years old], or where there is no kohen before us; but if they practice giving it to a kohen and there is a kohen there, it is permitted for him to bake it; and it was explained above in siman 457.
