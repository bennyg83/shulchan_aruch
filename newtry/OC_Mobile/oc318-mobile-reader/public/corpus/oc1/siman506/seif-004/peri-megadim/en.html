See Peri Chadash. It appears that even through a complete deviation from the ordinary manner, the first sifting is forbidden for a Jew, although according to Ran it is prohibited only D’Rabbanan, as written here and in seif katan 2. Although siman 495 permits certain melachos when performed with a deviation, this melachah is ordinarily performed for many days at once and is therefore forbidden like harvesting and grinding. Through a non-Jew, perhaps even the Peri Chadash would agree that it is permitted, though it is also possible that he forbids it. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 2, in the name of Maharil, that on Yom Tov one may not purchase bread baked by a non-Jew, even according to the opinion that permits this on Shabbos, as stated in siman 325, lest one come to say that sifting is permitted on Yom Tov. This requires study, for I have not found Magen Avraham or the other later authorities citing such a novel ruling. The practical halachah requires study.
