(s.k. 4) Who wants, etc. At the time of kneading, etc. Meaning: in such a case the two reasons for prohibition written in s.k. 2 do not apply: not the reason that it is done for a long time ahead, for the way of a second sifting is only at the time of kneading and not for a long time ahead; and also the reason that melachos before kneading were not permitted: nevertheless in such a case there is no melacha, for there is no bran in it, and also it is at the time of kneading; and the same law applies to Kneading itself, which is permitted.
