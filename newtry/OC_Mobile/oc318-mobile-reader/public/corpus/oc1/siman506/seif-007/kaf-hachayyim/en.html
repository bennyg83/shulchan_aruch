7) There, in the gloss. It is permitted to take flour, etc. This implies that making a depression applies to sand and earth when the material is hard, even if detached from the ground. Accordingly, those who on Yom Tov insert a wax candle into hard sand, or remove it from there, do not act properly, because they make a depression. Ran writes that it is forbidden to bury an egg in loose earth unless the earth is so loose that when the egg is placed upon it, the egg enters by itself. Tosafos write likewise. Shabbos 113a, however, implies that one may remove an inserted object even though this makes a depression. Magen Avraham, siman 498, subsection 32. See there.
