(In Magen Avraham s.k. 12) And combine them together — explanation: they should be in proximity, etc.; Shulchan Aruch there in Yoreh De'ah ruled like Ra'ash; but in Nekudot HaKesef siman 326 brought view Semak and Semag and Rash in one view that basket combining needed also for enclosing; I elaborated in responsa Beit Efraim part Orach Chaim siman 44, see there.
