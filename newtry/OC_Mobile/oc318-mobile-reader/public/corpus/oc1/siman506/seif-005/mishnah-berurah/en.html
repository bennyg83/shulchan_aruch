{32} And we are not concerned that from this, etc. Meaning: since there is not so much effort as there is with small loaves in forming each one, and therefore one might have thought to be concerned that he will bake more than he needs, and that is in a manner that is forbidden, such as in a large oven or in several batches; for in a small oven and in one batch, even baking more than he needs is no problem for us, because the bread improves thereby, as below in siman 507, se'if 6. And likewise it is permitted for him to bake very thin cakes, even though he toils much in forming and kneading each one.
