But dough, etc. See Baal Hamaor; and the Rif brought a proof that the sugya of chapter Mashilin, folio 34a, follows him; and the Razah and the Rosh rejected [it], for there it is challah of Eretz Yisrael, but here regarding challah of chutz la'aretz they disagree, since one can eat and afterward separate; and the Ramban wrote: first, if so the Gemara should have said 'one who kneads in chutz la'aretz'; and further, it depends on the dispute of R' Yehuda and R' Shimon bar Abba in Shabbos folio 142 regarding raising [tithes], since one can set his eyes, etc.
Rather he eats. And so it is in the Yerushalmi; Hagahos Maimoniyos, see there; and as written there: he sets his eyes, etc.; and even according to the one who forbids there, here, where one does not even need to set his eyes, he agrees.
And if he wishes, etc. And it is similar to what is written there, folio 8b: it was stated only when there is no, etc.; and in chapter 2: one immerses a vessel upon its waters, etc.; a niddah uses a device, etc.; and all the more so here where there is only thought. And as written in Pesachim chapter 3: one who kneads dough, etc.; R' Eliezer: do not call a name, etc.; and even dough half of which is of a non-Jew would be permitted if it were not that one can divide at kneading, which is not so here. And see there folio 12b: R' Yehuda said: Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel did not disagree that one brings with, etc. And all the more so in such a case, that even others agree.
Se'if 4. One who separates, etc. Mishnah, Pesachim chapter 3; and see Rashi.
Se'if 5. It is permitted. According to the explanation of Tosafos there, s.v. 'That is numerous,' etc.
