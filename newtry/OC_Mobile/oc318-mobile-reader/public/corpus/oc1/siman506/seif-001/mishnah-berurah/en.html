{1} One does not measure flour, etc. For since it is not a need of bread, and it will not spoil if he does not measure, the Sages did not permit it, for it appears like one measuring in order to sell.
{2} In order to knead. And if he takes flour for a dip or the like, for which a measure is needed, one can say it is permitted [Peri Megadim].
{3} Rather he should take by estimation of the mind. And if he takes with a measure that he uses on a weekday, he must lessen a little or add a little.
{4} And it is permitted to take, etc. Meaning he need not pour from the flour vessel into his kneading-trough; rather he may thrust his hand into the flour sack even though a hollow is made there; for specifically regarding earth we are concerned about a hollow [above in siman 498, se'if 16], and not regarding produce and food items.
