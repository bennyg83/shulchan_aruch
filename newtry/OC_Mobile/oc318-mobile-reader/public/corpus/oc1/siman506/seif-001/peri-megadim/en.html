One may not. See Taz, and Beitzah 29b. The words “of the school of Shmuel” are a scribal error and should read “Rabbi Shimon.” See Beit Yosef. The Bach and Taz offer a somewhat similar resolution, though in a different manner. Nowadays it is forbidden to measure, and we also protest against one who does so, for there is no distinction permitting challah to be given generously. See Tosafot, Rosh, Beit Yosef, and Peri Chadash. It is permitted to take it with the measuring vessel itself while reducing the amount slightly; see Bach and Tosafot there. What the Levush writes, that measuring is a primary category of melachah, requires study, for it is merely a weekday activity. See Beitzah 29a, where Rashi explains, “He should not measure with a measuring vessel,” and the rule that one may not measure barley for his animal. Measuring is not listed among the primary categories of melachah on Shabbos. See siman 323. This requires study.
