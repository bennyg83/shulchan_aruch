(Taz, s.k. 2) For this could be done yesterday, etc. The Ran wrote so in the name of Rashi's explanation on that of 'one does not do a second sifting,' for there the reason that it is done for many days does not apply, like grinding and reaping, for it is not the way to do a second sifting for many days. Therefore he wrote the reason that it is possible [to do it] on erev Yom Tov. And in truth a change helps in this, and it is like pounding salt above in siman 504. And on this the Ran wrote that others explained that the reason one substitutes [and forbids] a first sifting is because it is forbidden since his way is to do so for many days. But regarding the reason of 'one does not sift,' the Ran did not write the reason of 'it is possible,' but rather the reason as simply as with reaping: because the way is to do it for many days, or from the verse from kneading onward.
