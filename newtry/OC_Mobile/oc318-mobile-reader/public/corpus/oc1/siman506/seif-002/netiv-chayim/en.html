Marginal note: And this is not clear, for those poskim are reliable enough [to rely on]: Rabbeinu Chananel, the Rosh, Baal HaTerumos, the Raavad, the Ran, and the Raavan, who ruled leniently to separate challah and give it to a minor kohen, or to an adult who immersed for his keri-impurity, and even to a tamei kohen, for he can nullify it in a majority and may eat, as we hold in the laws of challah, siman 323. And if there is no kohen, he eats and continues [eating], and leaves less than a kezayis, and covers it with a vessel until Motzei Yom Tov, like other chametz that remains if he has no non-Jew, in siman 444, s.k. 5. And according to the ruling of the Magen Avraham it is difficult, for he himself uproots it from challah with his hands, as it appears to me for the ruling of the law.
