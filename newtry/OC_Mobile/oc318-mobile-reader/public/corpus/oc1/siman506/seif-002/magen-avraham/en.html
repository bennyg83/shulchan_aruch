One does not sift, etc. Because it is a melacha done for a long time ahead (Ran). And Yam Shel Shlomo wrote as Tosafos wrote at the beginning of Beitzah, that all melachos are permitted only from kneading onward; see siman 495.
Some are stringent. For it appears like borer, which is an av melacha like sifting; but one who does a second sifting, meaning he sifted a second time, is not [doing] meraked, for it is apparent that everything comes out and there is no bran there (Rashi). And if so, with our flour which has already been sifted at the mill and has no bran, it is possible that it is permitted to sift it a second time; and see Shabbos chapter 7.
Who wants to sift it a second time. For that is his way to do at the time of kneading (Yam Shel Shlomo).
Upon a table. For on a weekday his way is to sift over a kneading-trough; and if so, one whose way on a weekday is to sift over a table needs a different change.
