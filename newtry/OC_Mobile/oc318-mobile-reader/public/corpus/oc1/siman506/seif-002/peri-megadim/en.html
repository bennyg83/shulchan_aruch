One may not sift. See Taz. Sifting once in order to separate the flour from the bran is a primary category of melachah on Shabbos and Yom Tov. See siman 510 regarding a sieve and sifter, which are forbidden even on Yom Tov. If so, the melachah was permitted only D’Rabbanan, as discussed in siman 495. Some maintain that according to the Torah only melachos from kneading onward were permitted. See Magen Avraham, seifim katanim 2 and 5; with Hashem’s help, this will be explained further there.
