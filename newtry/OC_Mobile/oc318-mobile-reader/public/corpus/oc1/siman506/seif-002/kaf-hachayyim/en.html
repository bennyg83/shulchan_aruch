8) [Se'if 2] Flour may not be sifted, etc. This is because sifting is a melachah ordinarily performed for an extended period. Ran. Yam Shel Shlomo writes in accordance with Tosafos at the beginning of Beitzah that only melachos from kneading onward were permitted. Magen Avraham, subsection 2. See above, siman 495, subsection 24, where we already recorded the dispute whether melachos performed before kneading are forbidden under Torah law or rabbinic law. See there. Eshel Avraham, subsection 2, writes that according to the first reason, that the Sages decreed lest one sift for an extended period, it is forbidden even if it could not have been done before Yom Tov, just as harvesting and grinding are forbidden. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 3, and Mishnah Berurah, subsection 5, rule likewise. See above, siman 495, subsection 21.
