One may not sift flour, even with a variation [from the usual method]. However, if it was sifted yesterday and a pebble or wood chip fell into it and one wishes to sift it a second time, it is permitted even without variation. And if nothing fell into it but one wishes to sift it a second time in order that the bread be nice, a slight variation is required, such as [sifting] on top of a table. {Rama: Rema: And there are those who say that it is permitted to remove the pebble or wood chip by hand (Rashba). But there are those who are strict and forbid it [Maggid chapter 3]. Rema: And similarly it is permitted to do it through a non-Jew, even the initial sifting, if he varies it slightly [Beit Yosef].}
