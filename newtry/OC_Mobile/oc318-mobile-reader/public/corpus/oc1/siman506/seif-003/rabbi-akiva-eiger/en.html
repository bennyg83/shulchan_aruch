(There) For challah of Eretz Yisrael is also forbidden to eat from. At first glance this is not so much a proof, for one can say that specifically challah of Eretz Yisrael, where this is a complete act of fixing, is forbidden; but challah of chutz la'aretz, which is not a complete act of fixing, since there is a permit to eat in it, and as the Rosh and those who assist him hold that Rabbah held from this reason to permit, one can say that even though Avuha deShmuel considers it fixing, nevertheless it is lighter, and it is forbidden only where it is possible to eat by means of eating and continuing; but where that is not possible, it is permitted to separate. However, since we hold that challah of chutz la'aretz is forbidden to separate, and that is according to the view of the Rif, and it implies that we hold that Rava permits even with challah of Eretz Yisrael, and it is proven from chapter Mashilin that the halachah follows Avuha deShmuel, as the Rif wrote, if so we find no grounds for leniency at all to say that challah of chutz la'aretz is not called fixing, and it is entirely like challah of Eretz Yisrael. And a proof for this is from what the Mordechai holds, that if one rolled dough on erev Yom Tov it is forbidden to bake it; thus even where it is impossible to eat without separating, nevertheless it is forbidden to separate. Nevertheless one may argue that since many poskim are lenient with challah of chutz la'aretz to separate where it is not possible, we rely on those poskim; and for this, on erev Pesach that falls on Shabbos, one should separate and feed it to a minor kohen less than nine years old, or to an adult who immersed for his keri-impurity.
