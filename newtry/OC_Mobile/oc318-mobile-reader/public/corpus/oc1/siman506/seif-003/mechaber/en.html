One who kneaded dough on Yom Tov may separate challah from it and bring it to the kohen [it is permitted even if he separated it the day before]. But dough that was kneaded before Yom Tov—it is forbidden to separate challah from it on Yom Tov. {Rama: Rema: Rather, he should eat and leave over a bit, and tomorrow separate challah from what was left over. And it is permitted to bake the bread by means of eating from it and separating challah afterwards [Beit Yosef]. And if he wishes, he can knead another dough on Yom Tov and combine them together, and separate from that dough also for what he kneaded before Yom Tov [Hagahot Maimoniot chapter 3, Mordechai chapter Elu Overin, and Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot, first chapter of Beitzah].}
