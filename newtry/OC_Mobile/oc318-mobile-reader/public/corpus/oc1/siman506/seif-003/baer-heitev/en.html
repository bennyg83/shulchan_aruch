Challah. But it is forbidden to leave over only enough for challah alone (Beit Yosef). And the Magen Avraham wrote: And automatically, on erev Pesach that falls on Shabbos, if one forgot to separate challah on erev Shabbos from chametz bread that he baked for Shabbos, it is forbidden to eat from it, for he will need to dispose of the chametz, and if so it turns out he ate tevel retroactively. And one should not say: let him give the leftover to a non-Jew as a gift, and the non-Jew will return it to him after Pesach and he will separate challah, for it is forbidden to give on condition, and if so perhaps the non-Jew will eat it and it turns out he ate tevel; therefore it appears to me that they have no remedy, end of his words.
