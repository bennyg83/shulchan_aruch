(There) And if he wishes he may knead more dough, etc. — it appears he means challah measure, for although Tosafos in Beitzah and Mordechai in Pesachim wrote in their words one matzah, meaning measure, as explained there that immediately in kneading matzot each has measure, see there; and with less than measure it appears forbidden, since he could eat it without separating except that he causes recognition by hand through intentionally combining it, and no challah obligation came today at all since he does not need hot bread in measure — forbidden to combine intentionally and obligate challah; and perhaps it is worse than rolling on erev Yom Tov; and likewise it plainly implies from the end of Mordechai's words who wrote incidentally that obligated matzah — implying it became obligated by itself; and likewise explained from Magen Avraham seif katan 12 who wrote and combine them together, meaning they should be adjacent, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 325. And therefore he means: because in this dough he bakes today there is also measure, as explained there that whenever both have measure one needs only adjacency, per the Rosh; and what Shach wrote there seif katan 6 that even if one has measure and one lacks measure, one needs only adjacency and not basket combining and covering — meaning also that one without measure need not combine to become obligated, for it comes from large dough that had measure and was obligated in challah, as I brought above from siman 324 seif 11 explained regarding removing chametz, etc., see there Shach seif katan 19 if it has flour, etc.; and therefore adjacency suffices, etc.; and likewise explained explicitly in siman 325 and siman 324 that Shach copied from it, and he need not have written this. Only because in Machatzit HaShekel siman 457 he wrote it is a printing error in Shach as above that only vessel combining is needed, and in Machatzit HaShekel through its cold and its washing he thought it deals with dough kneaded initially with less than measure — obvious in this it is not obligated except through basket combining; therefore he emended Shach. But in truth one may say: what lacks measure means there is not measure now, but nevertheless there is already challah obligation upon it from when at rolling the dough had measure. And I found in Hagahot HaRif in Semak laws of resting on Yom Tov daf 81: and if he forgot, etc., he returns and kneads on Yom Tov challah measure and combines them in a vessel with that kneaded on erev Yom Tov and separates; and he brought it to his mind on the fifth day of Yom Tov, see there. And I saw in Machatzit HaShekel siman 457 for Tosafos first chapter of Beitzah — Beit Yosef brought them: they wrote there are two enactments, etc., to knead and bake only a small dough less than measure on Yom Tov, etc.; in my humble opinion this is not so, for a small dough is not mentioned in this; only Rashi and other poskim wrote small piece regarding challah on Pesach — make a small piece; but here this is not mentioned — he must knead challah measure and combine them with what he has from erev Yom Tov; and adjacent suffices and he separates from the obligated and exempts in his thought also the other. However what Magen Avraham wrote that adjacency suffices requires examination here, and it will be explained shortly.
