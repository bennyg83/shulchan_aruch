(s.k. 9) From the remainder — challah; but forbidden, etc. — this is also forbidden on chol, as Ra'ash wrote: regarding challah it is written 'first,' therefore we require recognizable remainder — meaning after separating challah remainder remains. So explained in Yoreh De'ah and Rama inferred 'separates from the remainder' — implies some of the remainder, not all. However Rabbi Yosef challenged Tur who first brought Rashba that for challah of the Diaspora even for Yom Tov need one may separate challah on Yom Tov since he eats and continues and afterward separates — the separation is not called fixing; but Rif and Rashi forbid. He also wrote Rashba in Rashi's name that even what he eats and leaves for challah is included in 'we do not separate terumot and ma'asrot'; since there is a dispute of authorities, better not to separate but eat and leave, end of Tur. Rabbi Yosef wrote: although for Rashba in Rashi's name to eat and leave is also forbidden, our Rabbi did not heed that view as it is a great stringency; and perhaps this fixing our Rabbi wrote helps also per Rashba in Rashi's name, etc.: that 'eat and leave' Rashba means he eats all the dough and leaves only for challah alone or slightly more — that is like actual separating; but 'eat and leave' our Rabbi wrote means he leaves much more than challah measure — then it is not like separating challah on Yom Tov, for on motza'ei Yom Tov one separates challah from much dough. So our Rabbi's wording is inferred, end of Rabbi Yosef. It appears the latter view is primary in Rabbi Yosef's eyes; therefore one who wants to satisfy also Rashba in Rashi's name must leave much more than challah measure. But Pri Megadim challenges Rabbi Yosef and explains Rashba in Rashi's name forbids only per Rashi who holds on chol it suffices if he leaves only minimal challah — he holds then recognizable remainder is not required; and on that basis he wrote on Yom Tov forbidden in such a case as separation. But for us who hold like Ra'ash that even on chol one must leave slightly more than challah measure, in such a case Rashba in Rashi's name admits also on Yom Tov permitted as it does not appear like separating; and if so one need not say Tur's words that if he leaves slightly more than challah measure he satisfies all views and need not leave much more than challah measure.
