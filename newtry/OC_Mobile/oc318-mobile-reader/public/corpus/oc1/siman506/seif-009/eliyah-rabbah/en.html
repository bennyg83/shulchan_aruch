[9] Even if he separated it yesterday, etc. And it implies even terumah that was raised last night (Beit Yosef). And the Taz wondered: it is impossible to separate terumah on Yom Tov, until here. And this is no difficulty at all, for I would have thought that if one transgressed and separated, we hold that it is a [valid] separation; and further, in the Gemara [Beitzah] folio 12 [13] it says 'most of terumah'; Rashi explained that there is terumah that one separates on Yom Tov; see there and be precise. Also regarding what he asked upon Beit Yosef in that he wrote that the halachah follows Beit Hillel regarding challah, when everyone agrees: necessarily he was not precise, for he wrote this only regarding terumah and not regarding challah, for Beit Yosef immediately wrote: and challah and gifts, which all permit to bring, etc. Further he prolonged at the end of siman 512, that they understood that the Yerushalmi and our Gemara disagree, and they asked upon Beit Yosef; and a clear proof for this is from Agudah, who strongly attacked Beit Yosef who understood that Mahari Weil forbids in this, which is against the Gemara folio 12. And in my humble opinion one can say: in the Gemara, specifically what is permitted to separate on Yom Tov is what is permitted to bring; gifts are [permitted] even if raised yesterday, since it is permitted to separate on Yom Tov; and what he compares to challah is when one kneads on Yom Tov, when it is permitted to separate; but if one kneaded on erev Yom Tov, when it is forbidden to separate on Yom Tov, even if he separated on erev Yom Tov it is forbidden to bring it; and terumah that he permits to bring, the discussion is where one transgressed and separated it on Yom Tov, as above. However, for the law, Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch also wrote that it is permitted even if he separated yesterday; and see Avodas HaKodesh, folio 39.
