(ט) Even if he separated it from yesterday, etc. — and it implies even terumah that was separated last night (Beit Yosef); and Taz wondered: but is it not impossible to separate terumah on Yom Tov? Until here. And it is no difficulty, for we could say when he transgressed and separated, we maintain it is a separation; and further in the gemara [Beitzah] daf 12 [13] it says most terumah — Rashi explained there is terumah one separates on Yom Tov, see there and requires further study. Also what he challenged Beit Yosef for writing halacha follows Beit Hillel on challah — behold all agree perforce it is not precise, for he wrote this only on terumah not challah, for Beit Yosef immediately wrote challah and gifts that all permit to bring, etc. He also elaborated at end of siman 572 that they understood Yerushalmi and our gemara disagree and challenged Beit Yosef; clear proof from Agudah — the rabbi strongly challenged Beit Yosef who understood Mahari Ibn that he forbids this, against gemara daf 12; in my humble opinion one may say in our gemara specifically what is permitted to separate on Yom Tov is that one may bring gifts even if separated from yesterday, since one may separate on Yom Tov; and what he compared to challah is when kneading on Yom Tov one may separate, but if kneading erev Yom Tov when forbidden to separate on Yom Tov — even if he separated erev Yom Tov it is forbidden to bring; and terumah that permits bringing refers when he transgressed and separated on Yom Tov as above; nevertheless for practical ruling Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch wrote it is permitted even if separated from yesterday; see Avodat HaKodesh daf 39.
