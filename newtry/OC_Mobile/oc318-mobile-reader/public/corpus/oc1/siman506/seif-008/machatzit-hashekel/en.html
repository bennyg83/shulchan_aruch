(s.k. 8) And tomorrow, etc. Perhaps the skin bottle will burst. For we learned in a mishnah regarding demai, and it is brought in Eruvin folio 37 and in other places: one who buys wine from among the Cutheans before they decreed upon their wine; and since they are suspect regarding 'before a blind person you shall not place a stumbling block,' and they do not separate terumos and maasros from what they sell to others; and he is on the way and has no vessels into which to separate terumos and maasros. R' Meir holds that we say bereirah; therefore it is permitted for him to say: two logs that I am destined to separate (for example, if the total is one hundred logs) are terumah (at the amount of an average eye), and likewise he says regarding the other tithes that he needs to separate; and relying on this it is permitted to drink from the wine on Shabbos, and what remains at the end is clarified retrospectively that it is the terumah and maaser. And R' Yehuda disagrees and says: even if we say bereirah, nevertheless one should not say [so], perhaps the skin bottle will burst and the wine that was to be terumah and maaser will spill, and it turns out retrospectively that he drank tevel. And R' Meir is not concerned for this, for it is an uncommon matter. But here, even though at the time of eating he does not commit a prohibition, nevertheless at the end he certainly will not be able to separate challah, for he must dispose [of chametz]; therefore according to all it is forbidden, lest he eat tevel retrospectively.
For it is forbidden to give on condition, as written above in siman 448, se'if 3.
Perhaps he will eat it, etc. And it is similar to 'perhaps the skin bottle will burst.' And here according to all we are concerned, for it is a common matter that the non-Jew will eat it.
