[8] And to bring it, etc. And likewise on Shabbos when it is baked, for it is muktzeh; and see in the Taz what he wondered upon Maharshal, that the mishnah in Demai chapter 4 is according to Beit Shammai, but according to Beit Hillel it is permitted to give him in his house, and therefore the poskim did not mention this at all, until here. And it is surprising regarding his wonder, for in Piskei Tosafos, first chapter of Beitzah, specifically when he is regularly accustomed with him it is permitted to give him in his house; and so wrote the Rambam, Hilchos Shabbos chapter 23, law 16. And if we distinguish between Shabbos and Yom Tov, then it is difficult against the Rash and the Yerushalmi of Demai there; and be precise. And even if there is no public domain between the kohen's house and his house, it implies it is forbidden. And the view of the Taz is that even in a public domain it is permitted for a Jew to take it out for the need of a kohen, just as moving is permitted, until here. And this is not clear, for it is similar to siman 518, se'if 3, that it is forbidden to take out for the need of Cutheans; and moving of foods is different; and so it implies in siman 512, se'if 3, regarding animal food.
