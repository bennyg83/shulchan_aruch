(6) [Levush] Whether with the right etc. Sultana Belula wrote: one should not stab the fruit on which he blesses with a knife, because with the right hand is the main of life — and then there are two opposites in one matter; end. And Sefer Chassidim siman 109 wrote: one who says to his friend "pass me the book" — he should take it with the right and not with the left.
