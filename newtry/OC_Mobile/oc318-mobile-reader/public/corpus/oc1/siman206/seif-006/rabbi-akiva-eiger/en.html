Seif 6, in the gloss. Only if he had not intended it. If he took a cup of beer before the meal intending to drink it and also to drink during the meal, and the cup spilled, then according to those who hold above in siman 174 that bread discharges beverages consumed during the meal, the original blessing did not apply at all to the beer he would drink during the meal. He must therefore bless again when he drinks before the meal. See Tosafos Shabbos at the end of siman 271.
