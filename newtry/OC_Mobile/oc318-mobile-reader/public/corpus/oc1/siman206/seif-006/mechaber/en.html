If one took fruit in his hand to eat and blessed on it, and it fell from his hand and was lost, or became repulsive, he must return and bless again — even if more of that species was before him when he blessed on the first one.Only if his mind was not on it to eat it [Hagahot Maimoniot ch. 4, Kol Bo, Agur, Abudraham, responsum Maharil siman 92].} And he must say baruch shem k'vod malchuto l'olam va'ed for having uttered Heaven's name in vain. And if when it fell he said baruch ata Heaven and did not say Elokeinu, he should finish and say limdani chukekha so it appears as reading a verse, and there is no uttering Heaven's name in vain here. But one standing by a spring blesses and drinks even though the water he drinks was not before him when he blessed, because he intended this from the outset. {Rama: And see above seif 1 regarding if he blessed in error what the law is.] וצריך לומ' ברוך שם כבוד מלכותו לעולם ועד על שהוציא ש"ש לבטלה ואם אמר כשנפל ברוך אתה ה' ולא אמר אלהינו יסיים ויאמר למדני חוקיך שיהא נראה כקורא פסוק ואין כאן מוציא ש"ש לבטלה אבל העומד על אמת המים מברך ושותה אע"פ שהמים ששותה לא היו לפניו כשבירך מפני שלכך נתכוין תחלה: הגה ועיין לעיל סעיף א' אם בירך בטעות מה דינו:
