Seif 6: If he took, etc. This is the wording of Rambam, and it is also the view of the Rosh, based on the Yerushalmi there. He resolves the question stringently.
Or it became repulsive. Rabbeinu Manoach.
Only, etc. This is the view of Raavad and Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, who resolve the question leniently. Maharil also holds this view. See Darchei Moshe and siman 209, seif 2, in the gloss.
