He blessed and afterward they brought before him, etc. Kesef Mishneh wrote that this is learned from the law mentioned in seif 6, which is from the Yerushalmi — likewise Kesef Mishneh perek 4 of Berachot:

Of another species whose blessing, etc. Beit Yosef so explained himself, but we already proved in Yoreh De'ah siman 19 that we do not hold thus; and what he brought as proof from the words of Rashba — that one need not bless on fruits since he blessed on bread — is not relevant to this, for there certainly everything drags after bread which is the main thing, even if the bread was not before him; and also regarding the same species it appears one does not discharge from a blessing except regarding a person whose way is to continue in eating fruits more than what is placed before him; but regarding one who thinks in his mind that he will eat this amount and whose way is not to eat except what is before him — if they brought before him more, it is a resolution and he must bless; and this is simple reasoning and no proof is needed:
