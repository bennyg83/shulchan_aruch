One does not bless on food or beverage until they bring it before him. If one blessed and afterward they brought it before him, one must bless again. But one who blessed on fruits before him and afterward they brought him more of that species, or of another species whose blessing is like the blessing of the first — he need not bless.And it is good l'chatchila to have one's mind on everything they will bring him [Beit Yosef, Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 19].]:
