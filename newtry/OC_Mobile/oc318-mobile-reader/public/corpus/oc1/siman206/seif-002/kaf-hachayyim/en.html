2) There: Borei Pri HaAdamah, he fulfilled his obligation. The Tur rules accordingly. Tosafos there, Rashba, and Rosh write the same, as stated in Beis Yosef. Beis Yosef nevertheless writes that the Rambam in chapter 8 rules that he did not fulfill his obligation. Kesef Mishneh there, chapter 8, law 10, however, writes that one textual version of the Rambam states that he did fulfill his obligation; see there. The version before us likewise says that he fulfilled his obligation. The later authorities agree that he fulfilled his obligation, and this is our practical ruling.
