Seif 2. If both a fruit of the ground and a fruit of the tree were before him, etc. Note: See Magen Avraham in the name of Re'em, who rules that if one first recited the wedding blessings, he must recite them again after the blessings of betrothal. At first glance, this would appear to apply only according to those who hold that chuppah before kiddushin is ineffective. According to those who hold that it is effective, perhaps the blessings need not be repeated. One may answer, however, that even if the chuppah itself is effective, the blessings associated with joy certainly do not apply before kiddushin, since the parties have not yet firmly committed themselves and may still withdraw. There is therefore no complete joy to which the blessings can attach. Thus, even according to the opinion that chuppah before kiddushin is effective, the blessings apply only after kiddushin, because the legal acquisition effected by the chuppah takes force only after the kiddushin. Examine this carefully; in my humble opinion, this is correct.
