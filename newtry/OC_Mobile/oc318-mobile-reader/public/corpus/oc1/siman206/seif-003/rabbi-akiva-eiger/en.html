Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. He writes that Abudraham disagrees. See Responsa Perach Shoshan, which distinguishes that there the delay was necessary for the reading, in order to roll the Sefer Torah and read.
Seif 3, in the gloss. More than kedei dibbur refers to silence. Speech, however, constitutes an interruption in every case, and one may not answer amen, Kaddish, or Kedushah. If he did answer, the matter depends on the dispute between Taz and Magen Avraham above, siman 25, seif 10. See Eshel Avraham, siman 51, seif katan 6.
