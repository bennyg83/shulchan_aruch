The laws of interruption and error in blessings on fruits. And it contains 6 seifim:
If one blessed borei peri haAdamah on tree fruits, one has fulfilled one's obligation. But if one blessed borei peri haEtz on ground produce, one has not fulfilled one's obligation. Therefore if one is in doubt whether the fruit is tree produce or ground produce, one blesses borei peri haAdamah. And on everything — if one said shehakol, one has fulfilled one's obligation, even on bread or wine.
