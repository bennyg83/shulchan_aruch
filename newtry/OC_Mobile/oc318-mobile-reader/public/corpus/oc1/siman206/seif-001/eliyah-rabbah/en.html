If he recited over tree fruits, etc. Magen Avraham writes that where something grows on a tree but receives Borei Pri HaAdamah because the fruit is not fully developed or because it is not the principal fruit, if one recited Borei Pri HaEtz he has discharged his obligation. This is difficult. Why then do the Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule in siman 202 seif 2 regarding unripe grapes, which are not the principal fruit, that if one is uncertain he should recite Borei Pri HaAdamah? The same is stated at the end of that siman. According to Magen Avraham, Borei Pri HaEtz should also discharge him, since it grows on a tree.
