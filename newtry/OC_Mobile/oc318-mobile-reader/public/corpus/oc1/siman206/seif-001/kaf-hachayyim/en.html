1) [Seif 1] If one recited Borei Pri HaAdamah over tree fruits, he fulfilled his obligation. The reason is that the tree's principal source is the earth. Berachos 40b; Beis Yosef; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. Even if he intentionally recited Borei Pri HaAdamah over tree fruit, he fulfilled his obligation. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1.
