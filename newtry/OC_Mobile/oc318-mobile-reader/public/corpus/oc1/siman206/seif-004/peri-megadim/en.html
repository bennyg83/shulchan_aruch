Ateret Zekenim showed there in sign 4, and Magen Avraham explained that regarding a woman too there is concern of ervah, and therefore we require pressing to the ground; but her heart seeing ervah has no bearing on her — therefore in water, when her hair is covered, it is permitted even though her heart is also in the water, for she has no concern that her heart sees her nakedness, etc. And the Mechaber wrote it is forbidden to bless naked, and a woman, etc., and even if he is not naked, etc. Regarding a man who is naked — his nakedness: even though his whole body and heart are covered, it is forbidden if all his hair is exposed. And regarding a woman, pressing to the ground helps so she not be in ervah; but not regarding a man, for it is impossible to cover his nakedness with the ground, etc. But the dress changed — and even if he is not naked, etc. But regarding a woman it implies that a woman too leaves concern that her heart sees her nakedness, and pressing to the ground helps for this too — see there on this:
