4) There: Therefore, if he is uncertain, etc., he recites Borei Pri HaAdamah. Olas Tamid, paragraph 1, writes that since the Rambam disagrees and holds that one who recited Borei Pri HaAdamah over tree fruit did not fulfill his obligation, it is preferable to recite Shehakol, thereby fulfilling his obligation according to all opinions; see there. Rishon LeTziyon, folio 14, writes the same; see there. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 4, however, writes that since the Rambam's view is unclear and some textual versions state that he fulfilled his obligation, the primary ruling follows Maran; see there at length. Shaarei Teshuvah cites this. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 1, agrees and challenges Olas Tamid; see there. The later authorities concur: Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Chayei Adam, rule 58, paragraph 4; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 1; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 56, paragraph 2.
