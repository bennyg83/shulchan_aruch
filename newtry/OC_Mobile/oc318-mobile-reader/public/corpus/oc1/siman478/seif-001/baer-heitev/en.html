(א) One may not eat. And after the fact, if he ate he need not return and eat afikoman again. And from the Mechaber's writing "one may not eat" it is implied that to drink is fine, as they drink the other two cups; only after the cups it is forbidden to drink intoxicating beverages specifically, as written in siman 481; and so too in Taz siman 479 seif kaf bet. And such is the essential ruling — not as Magen Avraham wrote plainly that just as it is forbidden to eat, so it is forbidden to drink; that is not the agreement of the poskim. Chok Yaakov.

(ב) In two places. Even in one room he should not eat it at two tables. And specifically for afikoman, which is a commemoration of the Pesach eating; but for the rest of the meal it is permitted to eat in two places. Magen Avraham.
