(1) [Levush] Not to eat, etc. And the same applies — it is forbidden to drink (Magen Avraham). And Chok Yaakov holds like Tur siman 481, that only after four cups is it forbidden to drink intoxicating beverages — see there. And in my humble opinion to decide: drinking is forbidden because it impairs the taste of matza; some say as Shevilei HaLeket wrote and as taught in a baraita — but for other beverages, even if intoxicating, one forbids only after four cups. And that which Chok Yaakov wrote that Taz siman 479 seif 2 implies one may drink anything after afikoman — it is difficult, for it contradicts what Taz wrote in seif 3 that if he ate afikoman at home it is forbidden to drink here, end quote. And one cannot say the reason is like eating in two places, for it is strained to say drinking is like eating for the law of two places — requires study.
