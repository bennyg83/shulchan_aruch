One may not eat. And after the fact, if he ate, he need not go back and eat afikoman; see above, siman 477, s.k. 5. And from the fact that the Mechaber wrote 'one may not eat,' it is implied that drinking is fine; and as the Tur wrote in siman 481 regarding those who still drink the remaining two cups, only after the cups is it forbidden to drink specifically intoxicating things, as all of this is explained below explicitly in siman 481; see there. And so it is implied in the Taz, siman 479, s.k. 2; and this is the main ruling, not like the Magen Avraham, who wrote simply that just as it is forbidden to eat, so it is forbidden to drink; and that is like the view of the Rif cited in the Tur, siman 481, and that is not the consensus of the poskim.
In two places. And the Magen Avraham wrote: even in one house, at two tables.
