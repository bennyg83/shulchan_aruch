(א) One may not eat. And the same applies — it is forbidden to drink; see Tur siman 481 and Beit Yosef.

(ב) In two places. Even in one room he should not eat it at two tables (as is clear in the Gemara); and it is implied specifically for afikoman, which is a commemoration of Pesach eating, but the rest of the meal is permitted in two places; see seif 2; and see siman 484.
