(Seif 2) One who slept during the meal and awoke does not return to eat, etc. NB: see Tosafot Niddah 63a, who wrote to explain the intent of the Gemara in Pesachim 'asleep but not asleep, awake but not awake'; and see on this in responsa Beit Elohim in my compositions on Orach Chayim from year 5597, 99a, for the community of Berazan — see there and examine.
