(1) One may not eat — so that the taste of the matza of afikoman, which is a mitzvah, not pass from his mouth through the taste of that food. And after the fact, if he ate anything afterward, he should return and eat a kezayit of matza set aside for afikoman.

(2) Anything — and regarding drinking the poskim dispute; one should be stringent with intoxicating beverages, and l'chatchila it is proper to be careful from every beverage except water and the like, as explained below in siman 481 in Mishna Berurah seif kaf alef.

(3) And he should not eat it — the afikoman; but for the rest of the meal we have no concern.

(4) In two places — and the same in one room, part at this table and part at another table, for it is like two places. The reason: afikoman is a commemoration of Pesach, and Pesach is not eaten in two places, as it says "in one house shall it be eaten."

(5) That it is no better — he brings proof that for matza too they are stringent about two places, like Pesach.
