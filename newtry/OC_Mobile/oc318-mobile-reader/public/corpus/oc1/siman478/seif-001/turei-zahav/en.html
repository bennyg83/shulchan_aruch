After afikoman, etc. For we taught: one may not conclude after the Pesach offering with afikoman — meaning, once they finished eating the Pesach they should not uproot themselves from their place to eat elsewhere; and even in the same place they should not eat anything afterward. And afikoman is for us in place of the Pesach; therefore once they began eating it they should not uproot from place to place to eat, and even in the same place — so too the Tur.
