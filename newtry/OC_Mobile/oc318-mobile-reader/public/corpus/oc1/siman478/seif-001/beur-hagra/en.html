(א) Seif 1, 'anything.' In accordance with Rabbi Yochanan there, who adds to [the ruling of] Shmuel — for [Shmuel] had only prohibited types of meat, and ardilaya is a type of fowl. Ran.

(ב) 'And he should not eat it.' In accordance with Rav, as Shmuel and Rabbi Yochanan add to [the ruling of] Rav, and as the Ran wrote.

(ג) 'That it is no better.' Meaning not like Tosafot there who wrote that Rava's case does not apply to matzah in our time, and the baraita that it exists is in Pesach specifically; and the incident of Rava in Taanit who learned from Pesach — but for us who hold all this applies also to matzah, as stated in seif 2; and all this is because of stringency lest they uproot — see Rashbam there, all the more here where it also applies to matzah.
