(א) He does not return — for it is like eating in two places.

(ב) They dozed — that is, asleep but not asleep, awake but not awake, such as one calls him and he answers but does not know how to return reason (Gemara).

(ג) It is not an interruption — not like Maharil who wrote that it is an interruption, for all eating of this night is like his Pesach (Darkei Moshe).
