(Siman kaf bet) In two places, even in one room, etc. As is clear in the Gemara 86a: we taught — two groups eating in one house — these turn their faces elsewhere and eat, and these turn, etc. (Rashi explained they may turn their faces even though it appears like two groups, for even two groups in two houses is permitted.) And the kettle in the middle (Rashi explained it has hot water for diluting wine — permitted to be between the two groups so the server can conveniently take for the second group's needs). And when the server stands to pour (for one of the group who is not eating with them) he snaps his mouth and turns his face until he reaches his group (Rashi explained lest they suspect him of eating in two groups). The Mishna deals with one room, as is proven; and nevertheless the server must snap his mouth lest they suspect him, etc. — thus even in one room it is forbidden at two tables.
