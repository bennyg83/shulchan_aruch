One who slept during the meal and awoke does not return to eat. Members of a group — if' some of them slept during the meal — return' and eat. If they all fell into deep sleep and awoke, they may not eat. If they all merely dozed, they may eat.And all this applies only if they slept after they began eating the afikoman; but if they slept before that, it is not an interruption (Tur and Rabbeinu Yeruham}.):
