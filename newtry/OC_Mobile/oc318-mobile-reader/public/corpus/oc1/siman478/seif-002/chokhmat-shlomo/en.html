(Se'if 2) One who slept during the meal and awoke does not resume eating, etc. Note: See Tosafos, Niddah folio 63, what they wrote to explain the intent of the Gemara in Pesachim, 'dormant and not dormant, awake and not awake'; and see what I wrote on this in a responsum, with Hashem's help, in my work on Orach Chaim, year 5616, folio 99a, for the holy congregation of Berzan; see there and look carefully.
