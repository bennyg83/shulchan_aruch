(6) During the meal — see the Rama's words in the gloss.
(7) He does not return to eat — for sleep is considered an interruption, and the Sages made it like eating in two places.
(8) They return and eat — for since the rest of the group were awake, the interruption is not counted even regarding those who slept.
(9) They dozed — that is, they did not sink into full sleep; and if one asks him where he placed this vessel, and when they remind him he placed it in such-and-such a place he remembers and says yes or no — this is called dozing.
(10) But sleep before that — that is, during the meal.
(11) It is not an interruption, etc. — meaning we are not concerned for this interruption since it is not during afikoman. And regarding whether he must bless HaMotzi again because of the sleep, see above siman 178 seif 7 and Mishna Berurah there.
