That some of them slept. In the Tur and Rokeach, siman 283, and in the commentary of the Rashbam, and the Bartenura, and the Rosh, and the Agudah, chapter Arvei Pesachim, it is explained that whenever some of them fell into a deep sleep, they may not eat, unless it was that some of them only dozed; and they thus explained the words of R' Yehuda as referring to the first clause; and so too is the plain sense of the Gemara there. However, the Rambam and the Raavad explained that R' Yehuda refers to the latter clause, and if some of them fell into a deep sleep they may eat; and the Mechaber ruled anonymously like them. And see the Levush, who ruled anonymously like the first view, which is the view of the Rosh and those who agree with him; and so it appears to be the main ruling.
But sleep before this. Even during the eating there is nothing wrong in it; not like the Maharil. So he wrote in the Darchei Moshe.
