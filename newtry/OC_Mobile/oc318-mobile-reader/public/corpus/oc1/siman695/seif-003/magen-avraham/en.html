(א) He says on the miracles. And if he forgot on the miracles in Grace after Meals they return him, for he may not eat without it [Shelah, Rashal in name of R' Yehuda Pollak, Mateh Moshe]; nevertheless his words require study, for is he better than prayer which is obligatory and nevertheless they do not return him — all the more in Grace after Meals; therefore it appears if he already ate one meal that day they do not return him, for if he wishes he need not eat more; as Beit Yosef siman 488 in name of Tosafos — see there; and though Rashal did not write so, it appears what is written main law according to halacha, for per law it appears we never return him, for we did not find he must eat bread on Purim and he can exempt himself with one kind of delicacies; therefore in this case where he already ate it is simple not to return him and concern for doubtful blessing is void; and so in Mordechai: on parchment he ate Purim meal on erev Purim, prayed, spread cloth and kiddush, in Grace after Meals says on miracles — end; in Mateh Moshe and Minhagim they wrote since he did not pray Maariv it implies if he prayed he again does not say on miracles — so Shelah and Maharil responsum 56; therefore it appears he blesses Grace after Meals first then prays Maariv to remove dispute and also lest he become drunk and not bless Grace after Meals.
