(א) s.k. 63 and if he began etc. as in Ne'ilah prayer, though for Rav in Yoma ch. 7 and for Tanna Kamma ch. 8 that one prays at night — see Rosh there in name of Yerushalmi; nevertheless he prays that of Shabbat as written in chapter 2 of Shabbat Yom Kippur that falls on Shabbat etc., and though Rav does not hold R' Yehudah ben Lakish not because of night.

(ב) For after beginning etc. The main thing is whatever extends the breath of Shabbat or falls on it we mention that of Shabbat, therefore on Shabbat night he prays two of Shabbat and so mentions in Grace after Meals of Shabbat as Tosefta, and so in Ne'ilah mentions of Shabbat since he has not yet prayed Maariv and Shabbat sanctity still; not so one who prays two of weekday since he already prayed first of weekday — per this there is no difference whether he began, for even if he did not begin as above. Per Yerushalmi who finishes at night one could say we follow the beginning; but for our gemara at end of Yoma that even to begin at night is a dispute, since it says one may not immerse and as above.

(ג) There is who. As written in chapter 4 of Berachot: he erred and did not pray Minchah on erev Shabbat etc. on Shabbat he prays two of weekday, and in chapter erev Pesach they finished first cup and bless on it Grace after Meals, and they said in Tosefta of Berachot he mentions of Shabbat in Grace after Meals though it is a weekday meal — clearly we follow now; and s.k. first holds prayer is only makeup since Minchah time passed and he separates in the first but in Grace after Meals he blesses on what he ate from before and since he has not yet prayed weekday he says me'ein hame'orah. Maharil siman 56, meaning to answer the Rosh's second difficulty who challenged from that Tosefta that case is different since Shabbat already entered unlike here where all this he has not yet prayed Shabbat sanctity upon him.
