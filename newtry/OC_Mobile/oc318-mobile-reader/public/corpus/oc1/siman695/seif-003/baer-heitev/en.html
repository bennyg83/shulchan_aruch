(א) The miracles. And if he forgot about the miracles in Grace after Meals they return him, for he may not eat without it — Shelah, Rashal, Mateh Moshe. Taz and Magen Avraham wrote this is specifically the first meal he eats on Purim, for the main reason is he has obligation of his meal that day as written "days of feasting and joy"; if so the first meal he eats that day with which he fulfills Purim meal — we need not hang the law on what we call the meal after Minchah the Purim meal; if he already ate a first meal that day they do not return him — see there. In this vein he wrote in name of Maharal of Prague he ruled unlike Shelah and Maharshal, rather when he reaches haRachaman he says haRachaman He will do miracles for us — see siman 682.

(ב) First. See siman 488 seif 10 from what is written, and so in Mordechai: on parchment he would eat Purim meal on erev Purim, pray, spread a cloth and recite kiddush, and in Grace after Meals say on the miracles — end of his words; in Mateh Moshe and Minhagim they wrote since he did not pray Maariv it implies if he prayed he again does not say on the miracles — so too Shelah and Maharil in responsum siman 56; therefore it appears he should bless Grace after Meals first and then pray Maariv to remove himself from dispute and also lest he become drunk and not bless Grace after Meals. Magen Avraham.
