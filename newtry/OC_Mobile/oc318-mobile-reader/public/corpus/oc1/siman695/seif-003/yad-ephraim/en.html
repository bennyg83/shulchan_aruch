In Taz s.k. 1 a person must become intoxicated etc. Many wish to answer and explain what the intent is here etc.; to me the younger it was explained in a night vision that the intent is the main obligation of the feast is he be steeped in joy as written "and wine gladdens a person's heart", and because he be steeped in joy the joy of the Blessed One is his strength and he gives thanks and hallel to the Blessed One for the miracle from breadth of heart, per what they said gather wine etc.; therefore he should not become drunk more than enough that his mind be confused and he not recognize the force of the miracle at all; thus he said a person must become intoxicated on Purim until he does not know etc. — this "until" is and not "until" in general, meaning great is obligation of drinking here to become intoxicated until the boundary he does not know etc.; from this boundary onward it is nullification of intent of obligation the sages obligated to become intoxicated in order to give hallel and thanks, for since his mind is so confused he does not know between cursed is Haman etc. — clearly he has no mind and understanding to praise and glorify the force of the miracle; I considered it in the morning and saw it is correct and settled what gemara relied on the incident of Rabbah and R' Zeira who made Purim meal together etc., which superficially is an act to contradict; some poskim wrote the intent is from this one learns the halacha is not like Rava here; per what is written one could say on the contrary it brings support, for what they obligated days of feasting and joy has a boundary not to become drunk more than enough, for we precede Rabbah and R' Zeira etc.; and since they did not guard from crossing the boundary the matter came to danger — therefore one must be careful not to become intoxicated only until this boundary he does not know etc. and not cross.
