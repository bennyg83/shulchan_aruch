(א) A person is obligated etc. — one could ask how did Chazal obligate what is mentioned in Torah and Prophets in several places that intoxication is a great stumbling block; one could say because all miracles done for Israel in days of Ahasuerus were through feasting, for initially Vashti was removed through feasting and Esther came, and so the matter of Haman and his fall was through feasting, therefore the sages obligated to become intoxicated enough that the great miracle be remembered through wine drinking; nevertheless all this is for the mitzvah and not to prevent [Eliyahu Rabbah].

(ב) Until he does not know etc. — thus Meiri: a person must increase joy on this day in eating and drinking until he lacks nothing; nevertheless we are not commanded to become drunk and diminish ourselves from joy of mitzvah, for we were not commanded joy of frivolity and foolishness but joy of pleasure reaching love of the Blessed One and thanks for miracles He did for us — see there what he explains gemara words. Thus Chayei Adam: since the entire miracle was through wine, therefore sages obligated intoxication and at least to drink more than usual to remember the great miracle; indeed one who knows about himself he will treat a mitzvah lightly in Grace after Meals or will not pray Minchah or Maariv or will behave lightly — better he not become drunk and all his actions for the sake of the mitzvah — end of his words.
