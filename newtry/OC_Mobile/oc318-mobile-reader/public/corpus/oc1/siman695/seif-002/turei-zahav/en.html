(א) A person must become intoxicated etc. Many wish to answer and explain what the intent is here and it was not settled; in my humble opinion to explain thus: a person must give praise to the Blessed One for double good He did for us — first punishment He brought on Haman, and had Mordechai not been blessed but saved with all Israel we would have been satisfied; rather He blessed Mordechai with very great honor and extra good — therefore he said one must become intoxicated until he does not know our superiority of cursed is Haman which is his humiliation to blessed is Mordechai which is our second superiority; the intent from saying until he does not know implies before his intoxication he knew and gave thanks for this — they intended he should not cease giving thanks for this in joy until he comes to not distinguishing anymore, and then he is exempt from this in my humble opinion.

(ב) And some say he need not become drunk etc. The statement of Rava in gemara until he does not know etc. is rejected by this statement, since in gemara he brings on this Rabbah who slaughtered R' Zeira — we learn from gemara conclusion one should not do so much; Beit Yosef in name of Ran in name of R' Efraim.
