Seif 2. A person is obligated to become intoxicated on Purim until he does not know the difference between cursed is Haman and blessed is Mordechai. Note: see what I wrote to explain this in my work on Orach Chayim from the year 5589, page 224b, and in the derashah for Parashas Zachor in the year 5593 in my novellae Kehillas Yaakov; see there for a wondrous explanation of this, with Hashem's help, and examine it carefully.
