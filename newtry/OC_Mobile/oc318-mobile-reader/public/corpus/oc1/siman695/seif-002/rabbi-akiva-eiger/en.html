Seif 2 in the gloss. Because of the joy of Purim, he is exempt from paying. At the end of Responsa Maharam Mintz he wrote: It is found in Responsa Rabbeinu Toviah in the name of the Riva that any food the young men snatch because of the joy of Purim, even without permission, from the time of Megillah reading until the end of the Purim meal, which is two nights and one day, has no prohibition of theft, and they should not summon them to beis din for this. This is the end of his words. It is brought in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 110.
Seif 4. Two portions. In Turei Even, in Avnei Shoham, he is uncertain whether, if one sent two portions to a poor person, he can count them for both purposes: to fulfill mishloach manos, since a poor person is also included in “a man to his fellow,” and to fulfill gifts to the poor. If he gives another gift to one poor person, he has fulfilled the obligation; see there.
