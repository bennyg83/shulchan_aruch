A little in Torah. So wrote Mahari Weil from that which we learn in first chapter Rav Ashi was sitting before R' Kahana; he said why did the rabbis not come to the beit midrash etc.; he said they are busy with the Purim meal — we learn the Purim meal pushes aside Torah study; nevertheless since they expounded "this is Torah" — mitzvah to engage in Torah before the meal and it protects him that no mishap occur at the meal — end of his words.
