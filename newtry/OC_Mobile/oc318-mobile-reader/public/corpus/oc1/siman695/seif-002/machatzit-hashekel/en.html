Seif katan 2: Night. Even on the night of the fourteenth. He used the language “even” because of the common custom to increase the meal on the night of the fifteenth, as the Rema wrote in the gloss. But in truth there is more of a mitzvah on the night of the fourteenth than on the night of the fifteenth. On the night of the fifteenth, the main reason is that we follow the beginning of the meal, which was by day, as Terumas HaDeshen wrote in siman 110.
