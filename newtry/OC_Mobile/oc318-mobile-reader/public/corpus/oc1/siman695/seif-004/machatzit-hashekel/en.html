Seif katan 4: Torah scholar. It appears to me that his intent is, and so on. See what he wrote in siman 604. There the Magen Avraham asked from what we say in Pesachim, page 68, that Mar son of Ravina would sit in fasting the whole year except for Shavuos, Erev Yom Kippur, and Purim. The commentators disagree about its meaning. Some explain that all year he would fast if he had a bad dream, as a Torah scholar, except on these three days, when even a Torah scholar would not fast. If so, this proves that even a Torah scholar should not fast on these three days. But others explain it simply, that he fasted the entire year, day after day, except on these three days. If so, it is not speaking at all about a fast for a dream, and there is no proof that a Torah scholar should not fast on these three days. Now above, in siman 604, he wrote that one should not fast a fast for a dream on Erev Yom Kippur; therefore he must hold like the first explanation. Here, however, he wrote that one may fast a fast for a dream on Purim; therefore he must hold like the second explanation. These are two contradictory positions; see what the Magen Avraham wrote there. Therefore he wrote here that the Rema's intent is to fast until after Minchah, for in such a case the Rashba permitted it above according to all explanations; see there, and also siman 552. See the beginning of siman 570; this is how it should read. Meaning, there the dispute of the authorities is explained regarding whether an oath took effect, and therefore he concludes there that he should annul his oath and not fast.
