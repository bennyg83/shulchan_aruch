(s.k. 4) Torah scholar. It appears to me his intent etc., and see what is written siman 694. He challenged there Magen Avraham: behold we say in Pesachim daf 68 Mar brei d'Ravina would sit in fast all year except Shavuot and Purim and Yom Kippur — and they disputed its explanation: one says all year he would fast if a bad dream befell him he would fast — Torah scholar — except these three days that even a Torah scholar does not fast; then it is proven even a Torah scholar may not fast these three days. But one says simply he fasted all year day by day except these three days; then it does not refer to Torah scholar at all and there is no proof not to fast Torah scholar these three days. Behold above siman 694 he wrote one may not fast Torah study on erev Purim — therefore he holds like explanation one, and here he wrote one may fast Torah scholar on Purim — see that he holds like explanation two; behold two contradictory matters, and see what is written there Magen Avraham; therefore he wrote here Rama's intent is to fast until after Minchah, for thus Hagahot Rashba permitted above for all explanations — see there and also siman 552. See Rosh siman 570 as should read, meaning there is explained dispute of poskim whether a vow fell and therefore he concludes there he may annul his vow and not fast.
