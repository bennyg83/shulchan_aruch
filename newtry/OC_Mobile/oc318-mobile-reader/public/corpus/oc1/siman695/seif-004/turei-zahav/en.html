(א) Kind of food. And not clothes or other things and drinks at all eat so much. And he was appointed in the scroll of the D.C. on the other hand:

(ב) Exchange with his friend. In gemara they say Abbaye bar Avin and R' Chanina bar Avin exchange their meals reciprocally; Ran explained neither was able to send and leave for himself, therefore each sends his meal to the other to eat Purim meal and fulfill mishloach manot; this way is written here; Rashi explained they exchange meals — this one eats with that one on Purim this year and second year his friend feasts with him; Beit Yosef challenged Rashi's explanation: if so they did not fulfill mishloach manot ish lere'ehu; you could say they sent portions ish lere'ehu — what does it teach? End of his words; in my humble opinion for Rashi it was a novelty they said explicitly this one will eat with this one and then reverse, and one could say it was like a loan and there is no manot here since gift specifically — it teaches nevertheless they fulfilled, meaning this year this one fulfilled by inviting his friend and his friend sent to another person, and second year the reverse; but one must be exact: why did Rashi not explain truly all on one year and both fulfilled? Perhaps one could say then it was truly complete loan since all see immediately he receives payment and there was condition between them — not so if in two years it would not appear like payment this year and not that; example for this we find (Yoreh Deah) siman 170 s.k. 2 in Hagahah: one should not say to his friend come eat with me what you fed me, for it is like paying his debt and appears as if he loaned him, and concern he will feed him more and there is interest; but permitted to say come eat with me and I will eat with you another time, and permitted to eat with him even a greater meal — end. Behold at second eating it is forbidden to mention it is payment — so too here then it was loan in my correct opinion.
