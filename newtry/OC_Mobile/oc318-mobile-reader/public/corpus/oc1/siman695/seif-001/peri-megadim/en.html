Regarding the Purim meal, gifts to the poor, and mishloach manos, one can say that intent is required. In siman 475, and in Magen Avraham, seif katan 14, regarding matzah, since he derives benefit from it he has fulfilled the mitzvah even though he did not intend. One can say that the meal is primarily a remembrance of the miracle that occurred through a meal; therefore it requires intent. This requires analysis.
