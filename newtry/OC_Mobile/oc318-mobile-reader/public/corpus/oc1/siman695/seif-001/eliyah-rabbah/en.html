[1] Levush: To become intoxicated, and so on. If you ask how Chazal could obligate something that is mentioned in the Torah and Neviim in many places as a great stumbling block, namely intoxication, one can answer that all the miracles performed for Yisrael in the days of Achashverosh came through a feast. At first, Vashti was removed through a feast, and Esther came in her place; and likewise the matter of Haman and his downfall came through the wine feast. Therefore the Sages obligated becoming intoxicated to the extent that the great miracle is remembered through drinking wine. So too Responsa Maharil, siman 56, wrote that one must become intoxicated as a mitzvah, but it is not indispensable.
