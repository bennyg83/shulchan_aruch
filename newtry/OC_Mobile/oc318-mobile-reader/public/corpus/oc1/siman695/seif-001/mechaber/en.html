Laws of Purim meal. Containing 4 paragraphs One who does the Purim meal at night does not fulfill his obligation. {Rama: Rema: It is a mitzvah to have many feasts during Purim (Tur), but one fulfills his obligation with only one feast (Mordechai) Rema: Nevertheless, one should be happy and add more during his meal even at night (Teshuvat Mahariv).}
