(א) On seas. Even beyond twelve mil they are not d'oraisa, because they do not resemble desert encampment; therefore we follow leniency. R' Moshe Alshakar siman 108 wrote permitted to descend from ship by ferry unless in a place where they were stringent; some forbid ferry — see Turei Zahav on Darkhei Moshe. See siman 339 seif 7.

(ב) Above. Magen Avraham wrote: if he went out to land once after dark he acquired residence there and has only two thousand from there — s.v., in the name of Rashbatz.

(ג) He went out — meaning his ship sailed from there beyond two thousand.

(ד) And needs. Likewise if non-Jews pushed him into the city — the whole is like four cubits; see siman 405; and if he did not go out from where he reached below ten — two thousand cubits, and his measure ended in half the city — forbidden to walk more, as siman 403 and 400. One who comes on Shabbat from outside techum — permitted to walk on the following Yom Tov beyond four cubits. Beit Yosef; see Magen Avraham.
