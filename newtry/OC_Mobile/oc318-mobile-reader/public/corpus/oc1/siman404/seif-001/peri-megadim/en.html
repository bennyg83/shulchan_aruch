And there is not — Taz; and likewise one pillar, part of it within the techum and part outside the techum — it is forbidden to ascend it and go outside the techum if it has four by four in a square; if it is long and does not have a width of four, it is a doubt; see Levush — it implies that on dry land even within twelve mil its doubt is treated stringently; and specifically in seas and rivers, where the essential techum even beyond twelve mil is d'rabbanan, its doubt is lenient. But on dry land, since beyond twelve mil for many Poskim is d'oraisa, even within twelve mil they relied on a verse and its doubt is stringent. In the opening essay we wrote from this reliance if we go stringently; but from what Rabbi Zalman wrote in the Hagah that beyond twelve mil its doubt is stringent — it implies that within twelve mil even on dry land its doubt is lenient; Eliyah Rabbah on this.
