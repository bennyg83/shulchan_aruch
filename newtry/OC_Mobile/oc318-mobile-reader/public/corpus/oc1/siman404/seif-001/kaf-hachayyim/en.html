1) [Se'if 1] One who travels beyond the techum at a height greater than ten handbreadths, etc. Whether the laws of techum apply above ten handbreadths is an unresolved question in the Gemara, Eruvin 43. The halachic authorities disagree whether we rule stringently or leniently. Several great authorities write that any aspect of techum that is of Torah origin is treated stringently when doubtful, while any aspect that is Rabbinic is treated leniently when doubtful, as explained in Beis Yosef and as the Shulchan Aruch rules here.
