There seif 2 — "Even a bathhouse or oven if he hired them out year after year and the matter became publicized" etc. Editor's note: It is difficult for me — why should publicity help here? For we hold in the Talmud and poskim that one who sells his field with witnesses collects from mortgaged property, and the reason is that one who sells publicly sells so that buyers will jump on the sale; accordingly it appears the same for one who hires out his field — he too has publicity from that reason itself, for even in a loan we say he has a rumor apart from the reason that one who borrows in secret borrows; if so, in hiring where that reason does not apply, certainly he has a rumor like in sale; and if so, since he has a rumor — why did they forbid hiring out to a Samaritan? Perforce even where he has a rumor the Sages forbade; and if so, why should publicity help here? And one cannot say that the rumor of sale and hiring is not considered publicity — for if so, why do we repossess from buyers if it is not considered a rumor. And it is possible to distinguish the matters: with buyers, since otherwise it is everyone's way to investigate the matter whether there is a protest, therefore even in selling with witnesses there is not complete publicity to be known to all without investigation — nevertheless there is some rumor, that at least if one investigates the matter afterward it will become known to him; therefore we say specifically in a loan which is in secret, and even if one investigates the matter it will not become known to him — we say he does not repossess from mortgaged property; but in selling, at least there is some publicity in the matter, and if one investigates it will become known to him the truth — therefore he repossesses from buyers who should have investigated and not bought, and if he did not investigate he caused his own loss. But on Shabbat we require that the publicity be that it become known automatically to all without any investigation, for an ordinary person — why should he investigate? And if he sees he will come to suspect him; it is not incumbent upon him to search for his merit — here we require great publicity, and in this he has no publicity except if he did so year after year. And being so, this requires further study on the poskim who ruled that a field for sharecropping is permitted — whence do we know thus? For one could say what the Talmud says "but his field to a non-Jew is permitted" means only in hiring, and that is from the fact that hiring has a rumor — and therefore in a bathhouse we do not rely on this alone; nevertheless if this view is joined that "Aris — his sharecropping he does," there are two dependencies for leniency: one that at least the majority will have a rumor and it will become known that it is in hiring; and if we come to be concerned for the minority who will not know from this rumor, still one could say they will say it is in sharecropping since he works for sharecropping; but if it is not in hiring but only in sharecropping — then he has no rumor, for sharecropping has no rumor; and if so, on this leniency alone that they will attribute it to sharecropping, perhaps we do not rely — for still we are concerned for several people who will not know this and will err that he is a day-laborer; and whence do we know to be lenient and say that on this alone too we rely — perhaps we do not rely on this view except together with the advantage that he has a rumor; and this requires further study:
