(2) Because it is called etc. Explanation: that by law it is permitted to hire out to Samaritans that they give the Jew such-and-such for a year, or that they give the Samaritans a share in the bathhouse profit — and this is sharecropping; and if so the Samaritan works for his own benefit, only it is forbidden due to marit ayin because it is called etc.; and according to this outside the boundary it appears permitted to all as below siman 244 in a field. And what he wrote (according to) [Olah Shabbat] that for Ra'avad who holds that where a Jew (uses) [benefits from] a Samaritan's melacha it is forbidden — one should be stringent in sharecropping of a bathhouse; until here. It is not so, for he did not write thus except (because) [when] all the profit is for the Jew but he gives the Samaritan payment for his melacha for a year — then it is not applicable that a Samaritan works for his own benefit, for he has his own hiring even if he does not work; which is not so in sharecropping of a bathhouse where the Samaritans have a share in it even though the Jew (uses) [benefits] — it is permitted since the Samaritan works for his own benefit:
