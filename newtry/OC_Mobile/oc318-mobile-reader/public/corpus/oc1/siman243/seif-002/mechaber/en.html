Even a bathhouse or oven — if he hired them out year after year and the matter became publicized thereby, such that it is not his custom to hire workers but rather to hire them out; and likewise if the custom of most of the people of that place is to hire them out or give them in sharecropping — it is permitted to hire them out to a non-Jew or give them to him in sharecropping.And even in a place where it is forbidden — if the bathhouse or oven does not belong to the Jew but he rented them from a non-Jew and returned and hired them out to a non-Jew, it is permitted, for the Jew's name is not called upon it [Or Zaru'a in the name of the Geonim]. And likewise if there is a bathhouse in an apartment building and only those in his building bathe in the bathhouse and they know that he hired a non-Jew — it is permitted [Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari Weil and R' Chaim and Or Zaru'a]. And if he transgressed and hired it out in a forbidden place — some say the rental payment is permitted [Beit Yosef in the name of the Geonim], and'] some say it is forbidden [Mordechai, first chapter of Shabbos, and this is the essential ruling] [and see siman 245]:]:
