Even for a bath house or an oven: if he rents it out year after year and this matter is publicized that the workers are not getting wages, rather they are rented, or similarly if the custom of the place is that most people rent or stipulate that the profits go according to percentages, then it’s permissible to rent it out to a non Jew or to have profits go according to percentages.RAMA: Even in a place where it is forbidden, if the Jew is hired to work the bath house or oven by a non-Jew, then he rents it out to a non-Jew, it is permissible, since the property isn’t recognized to be owned by the Jew. Similarly, if there is a bath house in living quarters and only those that live there use the bath house, and they know that they hired a non-Jew, then it is permissible.
