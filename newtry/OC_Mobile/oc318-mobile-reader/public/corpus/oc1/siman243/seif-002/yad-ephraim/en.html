In Taz note 2 he wrote thus: we hold in every case forbidden as explained in siman 247 etc. — his words are wondrous, for there the reason is because when they know the non-Jew does not take a share in produce it appears to people like a day-laborer and forbidden due to marit ayin; and here it discusses where it is publicized that his way is to hire in contracting — automatically marit ayin is not applicable; and so explained in Bach that he brings proof only regarding that even though the Jew has profit it is permitted by law like mill in sharecropping — and automatically that here too where it discusses publicized it is permitted in a field — certainly marit ayin is not applicable — and automatically that also in oven it is permitted and we are not concerned for what the Jew profits — it follows automatically. And it appears Taz holds according to what is written in siman 244 note 2 that Tosafos brought Yerushalmi that contracting specifically in detached but not in attached; and so for Rambam — and we learn that Eretz Yisrael land even in a field etc. see there; and this view one could say that in attached even by law contracting is forbidden not because it appears like a day-laborer — and if so even publicized that their way is to hire in contracting — forbidden; and per the other view written there they inclined to permit in a field etc.; if so obviously in publicized there is permission as written.
