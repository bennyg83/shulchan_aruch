Like a bathhouse. Responsa Nachalah LiYehoshua, siman 13, was asked about one who built a furnace in his house for manufacturing glass and employed Ishmaelite workers, whether they may work on Shabbos, since he would suffer a loss if the furnace cooled on Shabbos. He concluded that if the ordinary practice is to rent it out, even on a daily rental basis, it is forbidden to lease it under an arrangement resembling a tenancy, because it is comparable to a bathhouse. This is also the view of the Semak and Ramban. There is no basis for permitting it unless the owner gives the craftsman an actual share in the furnace itself, and the craftsman receives a portion of the glass corresponding to his share in the furnace. See Responsa Aderes Eliyahu, siman 1.
