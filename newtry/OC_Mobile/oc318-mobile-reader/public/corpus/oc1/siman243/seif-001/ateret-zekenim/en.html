A person should not hire out etc. And some say that if a Jew never lived in it, it is permitted to hire them out to a non-Jew or give them to him in sharecropping, and there is no concern for suspicion, for the Jew's name was never called upon them — only a non-Jew acquired them and returned and hired them out to a non-Jew (Beit Yosef in the name of Ran). And if he transgressed and hired it out in a forbidden place — some say the rental payment is permitted and some say it is forbidden, and this is the essential ruling. However it appears that specifically when he gives him payment for Shabbat day alone; but if he baked in it for many days, since it is through absorption with the other weekdays of Shabbat and likewise all the melacha is imposed on the non-Jew — all agree it is permitted b'dieved (my teacher in Bach):
