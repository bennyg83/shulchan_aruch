In Mechaber seif 1 — "but a field is permitted." Editor's note: And in our time there is no field in sharecropping but only day-laborers, and this requires further study whether to hire it out if a Jew's name is called upon it; and all the more so that it is forbidden through a deceptive sale on Friday eve and he returns and buys from him after Shabbat — for they permitted only with an animal and with detached produce and with great loss and b'dieved, but l'chatchila and with attached field — no. And see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 57 and siman 59; and there in siman 59 he was asked regarding one who rented from the count [Briahoviz] a cooking house, rent and a hired non-Jew worker for a year, for such is the way of all who do hiring and toil — the questioner sought to find a leniency to cook also on Shabbat to save him from very great loss if his work ceases on Shabbat; and after he extended to collect every side of permission in this case he concluded and thus he wrote: nevertheless no ruling for permission shall emerge from this, and for this we pray that our livelihood be in permission and not in prohibition; and the intent is not without theft and robbery, for that is not in the hands of Heaven; but the intent is things forbidden and we must permit them on account of loss of our livelihood — may the Merciful One save us from these and the like, end of his words; and see there well in the entire responsum]:
