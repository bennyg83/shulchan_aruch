(Note 1) Because etc., that by law etc. — meaning: Shulchan Aruch forbade only due to marit ayin. By law it is permitted in one of these two ways: either he hires it out to a non-Jew — in such a case certainly the non-Jew works for his own benefit, for all the profit is his; or he gives the non-Jew a share etc., for even though the Jew also benefits, nevertheless it is permitted from the side of law as Rama wrote in his gloss — nevertheless the non-Jew works for his own benefit on account of his share:
