Such is the custom etc. — one should examine whether the meaning is that most of the world give in sharecropping, and so too Rosh there in his language at the end of the first chapter of Avodah Zarah; but if the custom is equal to hiring workers like sharecropping it is forbidden — lest they attribute it to sharecropping; or perhaps in such a case it is also permitted. And what they said in the Gemara "Arisa — his sharecropping he does" means that it is also customary to give in sharecropping — to exclude a bathhouse, for it is not customary to give in sharecropping; and as Ran wrote at the end of the first chapter of Avodah Zarah, because his expenses are great and his payment is small, and everyone knows that on account of the Jew he works; and from the language of Rambam it implies as we wrote above in the name of Rosh, who wrote in chapter 6 of Laws of Shabbat halachah 15: a thing upon which a Jew's name as owner is called and it is not the custom of most people of that place to hire it out or give it in sharecropping — it is forbidden to hire it out to a Jew, etc.; it implies that for permission we require a majority; and so it implies from the language of Shulchan Aruch in seif 2:
