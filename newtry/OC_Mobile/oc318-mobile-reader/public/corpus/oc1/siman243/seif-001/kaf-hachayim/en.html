(1) [Seif 1] "A person should not hire out a bathhouse" etc. — meaning: that by law it is permitted to hire it out to a non-Jew that he give him such-and-such whether the non-Jew heats it or does not heat it; or that he give the Samaritan a share in the bathhouse profit — and if so the Samaritan works for his own benefit as written regarding a field, and this is sharecropping; only it is forbidden because it is called by his name. But to hire them out to a non-Jew for days that the non-Jew gives him each day such-and-such — forbidden by law, for it is like hiring for Shabbat. Magen Avraham note 1. Eliyah Rabbah section 2 — and it is obvious that if he hires a non-Jew on condition that he not do melacha on Shabbat and he listens and does not do melacha on Shabbat — it is permitted. Beit David siman 197. End of section 5 Shabbat section 15:
