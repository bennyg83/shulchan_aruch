Under Taz — that is, from what is said in Yoma 28b: on a cloudy day the sun is everywhere — according to Rava it implies that when it is not cloudy, in shade it is permitted provided one not approach the sun; it implies that in shade too, wherever one is close to the sun, the shade is also hot and will heat the dough; and specifically when distant it is permitted.
