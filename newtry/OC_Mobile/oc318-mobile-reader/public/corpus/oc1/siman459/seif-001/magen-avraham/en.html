Lest it be cloudy. Meaning lest suddenly clouds come; but it is permitted to knead in the shade under the open sky, for when clouds come and the sun is covered he will notice the matter immediately (Terumat HaDeshen).
And likewise one should be careful. For this reason, lest it be cloudy; but at bein hashemashos there is no concern (Terumat HaDeshen; Darkei Moshe); see siman 455, se'if 1. And one should not leave one sheet many times on the matzos, lest dough stuck to it and became chametz; see se'if 4. And in the responsa of the Rashba, siman 124, he wrote that the Rambam sent the matzos to a bakery much farther than fifteen houses.
