Lest it become cloudy. Meaning: lest suddenly clouds come; but it is permitted to knead in shade under the open sky, for when clouds come and the sun is covered he will immediately perceive it (Terumat HaDeshen).

(2) And be careful. From the 9th Dashma it will be cloudy, but between the suns there is no feeling (15th DM) Esi' 15th SA: And a sheet should not be placed many times over the unleavened bread, because dough will stick to it and it will chametz.
