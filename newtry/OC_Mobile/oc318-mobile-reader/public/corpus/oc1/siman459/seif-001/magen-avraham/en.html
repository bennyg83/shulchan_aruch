Lest it become cloudy. Meaning: lest suddenly clouds come; but it is permitted to knead in shade under the open sky, for when clouds come and the sun is covered he will immediately perceive it (Terumat HaDeshen).

(ב) And yes, be careful. Hashem’s Word says, “But there is no way for the sun to be seen in the heavens, and it is not to be seen in the heavens; and it is not to be put on many times that the Hashem has been infected with him, and that the Hashem has sent him a lot more to the ground.”
