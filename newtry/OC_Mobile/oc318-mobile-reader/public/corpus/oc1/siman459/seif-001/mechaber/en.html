THE PLACE AND ORDER OF MAKING THE MATZOT: SIX SECTIONS One should not knead in a sunny place and on a cloudy day, it is forbidden under any open sky because on a
cloudy day, the sun is everywhere. REMA: and because of this, our custom is to be cautious to avoid kneading opposite an open window even where there is no sun, as it may become cloudy and one wouldn't realize. Similiarly, there are those who are careful when moving the Matzot to the oven exposed to the sky, and similarly [when moving] the water and flour. . One should not approach the oven when kneading because of the heat of the oven.(T"H siman 193) (Haghaot Maimoni perek 5) in the name of SMA"K and MAHA"RIV)) ולא יתקרב ללוש אצל התנור מפני חום התנור:
