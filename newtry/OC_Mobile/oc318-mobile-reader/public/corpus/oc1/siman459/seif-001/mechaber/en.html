The place and order of kneading matzot. And in it are 6 seifim:
One should not knead in a place of sunlight, and on a cloudy day it is forbidden under the entire open sky, because on a cloudy day the sun is everywhere.And therefore they are accustomed to be careful not to knead opposite an open window even where there is no sun, lest it become cloudy without his realizing it (Terumat HaDeshen siman 193). And similarly one should be careful not to) carry the matzot to the oven under the open sky, and similarly the water or the flour (Hagahot Maimoni ch. 5 in the name of Semak and Mahariv}.} And one should not approach to knead near the oven because of the heat of the oven.) ולא יתקרב ללוש אצל התנור מפני חום התנור:
