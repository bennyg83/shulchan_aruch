(s.k. 1) Lest, etc. "He will notice," that is to say, by means of the shade passing. Which is not so when one bakes opposite a window, where there is no shade: there is concern lest it become cloudy and he not take it to heart. And Chok Yaakov wrote: and if the window is sealed, even with a glass partition, it is permitted to knead there.
And in Chelkas Mechokek he disagrees with Chok Yaakov on this.
