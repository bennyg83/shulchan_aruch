So that they not gather. But in a place of a slope they are absorbed into the ground quickly (Rashi); and therefore one should not pour them onto a stone floor. And nevertheless this requires further study: what does it matter if they become chametz, seeing that he pours them and has declared them hefker, as is written at the end of siman 444? And one may say that l'chatchilah one must be careful that they not come to chimutz; and see the end of siman 445.
And the utensils, etc. And the peels need not be rinsed (Maharil).
At the time of production. Not like the practice of the world, who do not scrape them until after production; for even though they do not sit idle for the measure of a mil, nevertheless l'chatchilah it is forbidden, as is written in se'if 2; therefore one should stand over them who will scrape every utensil continually at the time it is set down.
By means of a non-Jew. For the scraping is a shevus, and it is a shevus of shevus, which is permitted in a place of a mitzvah, as is written in siman 307, se'if 5.
That the dough bake. And nevertheless it is forbidden to eat the dough on the other side, for in the cracks it was not baked well (Maharil). And it is difficult: if so, how is he permitted to bake it? And perhaps according to the strict law it is permitted for eating, and it is only an ordinary stringency not to eat; therefore it is permitted to bake it. And it appears to me that one should bitul the chametz before it becomes chametz, or give the utensils with the dough as a gift to a non-Jew. And if he needs to bake a second time on Yom Tov, obviously it is permitted to rinse them, but it is forbidden to scrape them, as is written at the end of siman 323. Some plate the kneading-board with a metal sheet, and some make it of smooth stone so that it cool (Zichronos, folios 22, 25). A vessel that cracked at its rim: Maharil ordered to widen the crack and scrape it well and hagalah, and to seal the cracks and smooth them to make them even, or widen it to the width of a finger; but one should not stick a copper piece onto it (Darkei Moshe). And also in the peel there should not be a crack or groove, etc. (Mahariv). The sheet must be changed between one kneading and the next; and if one works on a board without a sheet and there are holes in the board, one must seal them so that dough not enter them (Maharil; Darkei Moshe); see what I wrote in siman 458, se'if 18, regarding washing the sheet.
