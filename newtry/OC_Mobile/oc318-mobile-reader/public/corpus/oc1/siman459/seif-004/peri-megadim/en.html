One who is shaping [the edges]. See Taz; and see Bach, who ruled that she must always cool her hands even when there is no covering, for the hands warm from the force of the kneading; and Rava, who said in Pesachim 42b that she needs two vessels when she kneads and bakes, refers to when she alone kneads and needs one vessel to cool her hands, see there. And the Taz agreed with the Maggid Mishneh's and the Ran's interpretation. And see Magen Avraham 8; and Peri Chadash was stringent where one sees that the hands warmed, that cooling is required, and one should be stringent. And sometimes they take helpers and day and night they do not rest, and the hands are very hot; certainly it is forbidden. And if Hashem wills, in Magen Avraham this will be further clarified.
