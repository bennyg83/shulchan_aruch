If one kneaded after the time when chametz becomes forbidden, these waters together with the other waters with which they wash the kneading trough — one should not spill them except in a sloped place so that they not collect in one place and leaven.And the vessels with which they form the matzot and the knife with which they cut the dough — one should scrape them constantly at the time of production so that dough not adhere to them; and after production one should rinse them and dry them well to return and use them a second time (Maharil). And this is preferable to what people are accustomed to scrape them, for it is impossible to clean them well by scraping alone; and one who is strict will scrape them and afterward rinse them (his own opinion). And on Yom Tov it is permitted to do this via an akum; and if there is no akum, one should put them in the cold or place them in the oven so the dough on them will bake. And kneading troughs into which one can place something — it is permitted to rinse them, and similarly all comparable cases. And one should be careful with a trough in which one kneads that there be no hole or crack from which one cannot pick out the dough after kneading, and it will leaven and afterward when one returns to knead in it it will mix with the dough (Maharil). And one should not place the trough on a pillow or wool cover at the time of kneading, for it heats the trough; but on leather it is permitted (Mahariv).):
