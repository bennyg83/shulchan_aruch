(24) One who is shaping [kituf] - That is to say, she smoothes the face of the cake with water.
(25) That cools - For one must cool the hands at the time of kneading l'chatchilah, because the hands warm from much work and the dough warms from the hands.
(26) Rather she should have, etc. - And in these lands they are accustomed not to trim the matzos with water [Darkei Moshe].
(27) And she did not cool - And the same applies if she transgressed and trimmed the matzos with water with which she cooled her hands: it also appears at first glance that it is permitted b'dieved. And know that there are poskim who hold that we do not require at all cooling the hands at the time of kneading even l'chatchilah, and they required cooling the hands l'chatchilah only if the one who kneads also bakes, because as she draws near the oven her hands are warm, and when she kneads afterward the Sages feared that she warm the dough with her hands; and they wrote that such is the custom not to be stringent. However, all this is in an ordinary case; but if she feels that her hands warmed, according to all opinions she must cool. And even in an ordinary case it is good l'chatchilah to be stringent, for such is the view of several early authorities.
