One who is shaping [kituf]. Meaning she smoothes the face of the cake with water. And in these lands they are not accustomed to trim the matzos with water.
That cools. The Bach agrees that she must cool her hands while working with the dough even if she is not busy with the oven; and so wrote Shiltei Giborim. However, the view of the Ran and Maggid Mishneh is not so: so long as she is not warming from the heat of the oven and afterward touching the dough, she need not cool at all (and b'dieved one should not forbid; later authorities). And likewise the custom is not to cool the hands when working at kneading the dough; and so it appears to me; Chok Yaakov, see there.
