And if. See Taz: and even if he worked on it the whole time, so long as one sees that part of it became chametz with such work, then it too is chametz; so he wrote, of blessed memory. And some say that four milin forbid even with work, and this will be explained, if Hashem wills, in Magen Avraham. And see Responsa Maharam of Lublin, of blessed memory, siman 105; and Chok Yaakov letter 15 brought [the ruling that] wherever there is a sign of chimutz in part of a dough, the entire [dough] is forbidden.
