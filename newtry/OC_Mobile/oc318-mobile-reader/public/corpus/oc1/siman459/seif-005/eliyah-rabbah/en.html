[5] [Levush] uncovered; rather he should cover with a cloth, etc. And one should not leave one cloth many times on the matzos, lest dough stuck to it and became chametz (Magen Avraham). And b'dieved, when he carried the matzah, or the water, or flour, uncovered, or stood in a warm winter-house, one should not forbid unless they became hot, as is written at the beginning of siman 455.
