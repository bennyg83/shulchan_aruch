A quarter. See Taz: ten parasangs are forty milin; divide ten hours among forty, and you find a quarter of an hour; and further, two hours, each [divided] into twenty parts, so that for each mil [there is] a quarter and a twentieth of an hour, that is eighteen minutes. And in the Rama [the measure is] a third of an hour minus a thirtieth of an hour; all is one. And see Yoreh De'ah, siman 69, se'if 6, in Shach letter 25, and that is the measure here; and see Magen Avraham letter 3. And what is written in Terumat HaDeshen in [siman] 123, that idle periods do not combine: that is, the work one does cancels the idle time, and that means real work; but scraping [dough from cracks] does not cancel the idle time; but if one scrapes continuously without interruption, even if he spends a mil on scraping, it is permitted, see there. And see Magen Avraham 4, and there this requires further study: what he wrote, that scraping is only in one place, is difficult, for if so when one is busy scraping for the measure of a mil it would be chametz; and rolling too is not [done] throughout the entire dough. And proof from the drop-by-drop [case] that he brought there in Terumat HaDeshen implies that there is not such significant work as to cancel the idle time. And if Hashem wills, in Magen Avraham this will be further clarified.
