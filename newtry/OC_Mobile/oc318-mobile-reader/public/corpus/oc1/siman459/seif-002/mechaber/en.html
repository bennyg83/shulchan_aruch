One should not leave the dough without occupation even for a single moment; and all the while they are occupied with it, even the entire day, it does not leaven. If they left it without occupation for the measure of a mil, it becomes chametz. The measure of a mil is a quarter of an hour and one twentieth of an hour.And one should be strict to hurry regarding making matzot, for there is concern that the delays will combine to the measure of a mil, or that it will be in a hot place where it leavens quickly (Hagahot Maimoni ch. 5 and Mordechai perek Elu Overin).} And after they occupied themselves with the dough and it warmed in their hands, if they leave it without occupation it leavens immediately. If they began with two doughs as one and one leavened, it is known that the second also leavened even if one does not see in it signs of leavening. If it leavened to the point that there are cracks in it — even if the cracks did not intermingle with one another but one goes here and one goes there — it is complete chametz, and one who eats it is liable for karet. If there are no cracks in it but only that it paled (meaning: its appearance changed toward white — Aruch) — its face like a person whose hair stands on end — one who eats it is exempt. {Rama: And if the matzah was formed and it appears it will not come to leavening, it is permitted to break it and return to form it so that the occupation will nullify the leavening; however, it is good to be careful l'chatchila (Maharil in the name of Agudah and Rokeach).) ואחר שנתעסקו בבצק ונתחמם בידים אם יניחוהו בלא עסק מיד יחמיץ ואם התחילו בשתי עיסות כאחד והחמיצה האחת בידוע שהחמיצה גם השנית אפי' אין רואין בה סימני חימוץ ואם החמיצה עד שיש בה סדקים אפי' לא נתערבו הסדקים זה בזה אלא אחד הולך הנה ואחד הולך הנה הוי חמץ גמור והאוכלו חייב כרת ואם אין בו סדק אלא הכסיפו (פי' נשתנה מראיתו ללובן. ערוך) פניו כאדם שעמדו שערותיו האוכלו פטור: הגה ואם המצה עשויה וירא שלא תבא לידי חימוץ מותר לשברה ולחזור לעשותה כדי שהעסק יבטל החמוץ מיהו טוב ליזהר לכתחלה: מהרי"ל בשם אגודה ורוקח):
