[2] [Levush] And some forbid, etc. In Shulchan Aruch he ruled like the first opinion, and the Bach like those who forbid even in a case of shogeg, because they are the majority of the poskim, namely the Rosh, Rashba, Rabbeinu Yeruham, Sefer Yere'im, and Semag. And likewise Nachalas Tzvi wrote that all the poskim forbade except the Rambam; and nevertheless he concluded that b'mezid it is forbidden and b'shogeg it is permitted. And to me this requires further study: for in the Remazim it is clear that it is permitted b'dieved, and likewise in Sefer Yere'im, who omitted "not in the sun." And from Semag and Rabbeinu Yeruham there is no proof, for he wrote "if she passed and kneaded with these," etc.: one may say that refers to water, as Maggid Mishneh, chapter 5, was precise [in saying] that kneading is only with water; and all the more so here, where he used the language "kneaded with these," meaning with the waters with which they knead, whether heated by fire or heated by the sun, but not in the sun itself; and so I found explicitly in Tanya. And what he wrote in the name of the Rashba I have not seen. And regarding benefit it appears to me that in every case it is permitted; see siman 455, s.k. 8.
