(1) [Seif 1] If he drank wine in the amount of a revi'it etc.—see below siman 128 seif 38 regarding Nekudot HaKesef, who wrote specifically if he drank the revi'it at one time; but if he drank in two times or put a little water in it, it is permitted; and if he drank more than a revi'it, even if it is diluted and even if they drank in several times, it is forbidden—see end matter; and it seems the same applies here regarding prayer; and likewise Magen Avraham and Machatzit HaShekel seif kaf-alef; Eliyah Rabbah ot 1, Peri Megadim Eshel Avraham ot 1.
