Beit Yosef cites Rosh concerning the incident of Rabban Gamliel's sons, who returned from a feast and said that they had not recited Krias Shema. He told them that if dawn had not risen they could still recite it, implying that after dawn they could not, although one who was compelled may recite until sunrise. Rosh answers that Rabban Gamliel's sons were not compelled, but were drawn after the feast. Beit Yosef discusses the explanation at length. I wonder why he did not explain it simply: throughout the night they were not intoxicated, but neglected Krias Shema because of their desire for the feast, unlike the pair of scholars of Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, who were intoxicated throughout the night and were therefore compelled.
