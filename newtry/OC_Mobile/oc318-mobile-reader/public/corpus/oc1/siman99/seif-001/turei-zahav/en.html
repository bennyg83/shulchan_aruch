And in Beit Yosef he brought the words of the Rosh on the incident of the sons of Rabban Gamliel who came from the wedding hall; he said to them, "We did not recite Shema"; he said to them, "If dawn has not risen" etc.—which implies that after dawn they are not permitted to recite; and even though one under duress recites until sunrise. And the Rosh explains that the sons of Rabban Gamliel were not under duress but rather their hearts were drawn after the feast; and Beit Yosef elaborated in explaining his words. And I wondered why he did not explain them per the plain sense—that all night they were not drunk but rather neglected on account of desire for the feast; unlike the pair of scholars of R' Yehuda ben Levi, who were drunk all night and were under duress.
