If the time for prayer passed, he makes it up with the following prayer, as in the case of an inadvertent omission, even if he began drinking after the time for prayer had arrived. Some say that if he began drinking after the time for prayer arrived, he is deemed deliberate and has no compensatory prayer; see below, siman 108. He may, however, recite the other blessings even while intoxicated, although regarding excrement there is no distinction. This is the ruling of my teacher in Bach.
