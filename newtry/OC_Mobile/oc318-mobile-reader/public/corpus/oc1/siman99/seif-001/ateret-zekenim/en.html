If the time for prayer has passed, he completes it in the prayer after it, as is the law for one who acted unwittingly—even if he began to drink after the time for prayer arrived. And some say that if he began to drink after the time for prayer arrived, he should be judged as one who acted intentionally and he has no compensatory prayer (see above siman 108); but other blessings he may recite even though he is drunk, although for relieving oneself there is no distinction (my teacher in Bach).
