A se'ah amount for a revi'it. For Tosafot there s.v. shetah—some books read etc., and so too Rambam and Tur, and even Tosafot concede this.

And one requires etc. Tosafot there s.v. shikor, and likewise siman samekh-samekh ayin-vav and resh-samekh tzadi-bet.

And even etc. And proof of the law of compulsion (ones) for him from what is written 9:1 regarding that pair etc.; and for intentional [drunkenness] he does not pray after wine, as Rif and Rashba wrote there.

But etc. Yerushalmi beginning of Terumot, and Tosafot brought it there s.v. shikor, and Rosh in chapter 8 of Berachot siman 9, and thus: "One who has drunk should not pray" etc.—which "one who has drunk"? Anyone who drank a revi'it; "drunk"—one who drank more. There they said: anyone who cannot speak before the king. Rabbi Zeira asked before Rabbi Eliezer: one who is drunk—may he recite a blessing? He said to him: "And you shall eat and be satisfied and bless"—even when drowsy it is not needed, for it is not fit that he recite Shema. And this is what he said, and the law of Keriat Shema etc. is stringent. But it seems there is a textual error in the Yerushalmi, for they said there: a certain pious man asked Elijah—what about a naked man who recites Shema? He said to him: there shall not be seen upon you anything indecent—a matter of nakedness, nakedness of speech. And Chizkiya taught: whether for reading or for blessing. And all this is not understood, for they said even terumah he cannot separate because of the blessing, and likewise even before the nakedness of his fellow; and also "nakedness of speech" is not understood—why did he not say "anything indecent" according to its plain sense? Rather it must be said it is a textual error, and it must say "one who is drunk—what" etc., and likewise in Rosh chapter 1 of Terumot they brought it and the word "naked" is not written at all, and it refers to one who is drunk; and Chizkiya disagrees and says that whether for Keriat Shema or for blessing it is forbidden; and he also disagrees with Rabbi Asi who said "and you shall eat" etc.
