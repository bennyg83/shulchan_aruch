A revi'is. This applies specifically when he drank it all at once. Semak, siman 133, writes that wine consumed during a meal does not intoxicate. See siman 473, seif 2, where some say that even wine consumed before the meal does not intoxicate. See Magen Avraham.
An abomination. It is as though he worshipped idolatry. If he refrains from praying while intoxicated, he is saved from every trouble. Gemara.
Inadvertent. Even if he began drinking after the time had arrived, because he thought he would still have time afterward, it is deemed an unavoidable circumstance. Magen Avraham. Olas Tamid writes likewise. See Taz and Levush.
Prayer. Levush writes that some are lenient regarding Krias Shema.
