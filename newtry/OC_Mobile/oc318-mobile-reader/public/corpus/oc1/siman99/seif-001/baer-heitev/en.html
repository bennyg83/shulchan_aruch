Revi'it. Specifically when they drank it at one time; Semak wrote siman 133: wine within the meal does not intoxicate; and in siman 473 seif 2. Some say that wine before the meal also does not intoxicate (see Magen Avraham).

An abomination. And it is as if he worships idolatry; and if he does not pray at the time of drunkenness he is saved from every trouble. Shas.

Unwitting. And even if he began to drink after its time arrived, when he thought he would have hours afterward, it is considered compulsion (ones); Magen Avraham and likewise Eliyah Rabbah; and see Taz and Levush.

Prayer. And the Levush wrote that some are lenient regarding Keriat Shema.
