(seif kaf-alef) A revi'it amount—in siman 128 seif 38 he wrote that if he drank a revi'it of wine he may not lift his hands [in priestly blessing], and wrote there that this is specifically if they drank at one time but not in two times; and Magen Avraham holds likewise here. And it is further explained there that if he put a little water in it, even if they drank at one time he may lift his hands—and likewise here; except he further wrote there that specifically for a revi'it the law is so, but for more than a revi'it, even if they drank in two times or put a little water in it, it is forbidden—and likewise here.

And in siman 473 seif 3 "some say" etc.—meaning, in Beit Yosef he brought "some say" thus, but not in Shulchan Aruch there.

One may not rule—and ruling and prayer share the same law. And see in Yoreh De'ah siman 242 seif 13 in the gloss, and in Choshen Mishpat siman 7 in Sema seif kaf-yud-alef.
