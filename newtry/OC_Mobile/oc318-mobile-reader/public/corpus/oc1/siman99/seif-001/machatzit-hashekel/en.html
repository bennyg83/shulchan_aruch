(Seif katan 1) The measure of a revi'is. See siman 128, seif 38, which states that one who drank a revi'is of wine may not recite Bircas Kohanim, specifically when he drank it at one time, but not in two installments. Magen Avraham holds that the same applies here. It is further explained there that if he mixed in a little water, he may recite Bircas Kohanim even if he drank it at once, and the same applies here. There it is also stated that this leniency applies only to a revi'is, but if he drank more than a revi'is, he is forbidden even if he drank it in two installments or mixed in a little water. The same applies here.
