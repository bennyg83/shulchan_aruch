Walking a mil—specifically while walking on his feet; but one riding does not dispel the wine except one who rides three mil (Eruvin page 64, Bach, Hagahot Maimoni). And some wrote that if he drank a revi'it, one may be lenient—even riding one mil dispels the wine; but if he drank more than a revi'it, then the road does not dispel it, whether he is walking or riding, even many mil; so is the implication of the Talmud, and likewise.
