(12) Traveling a mil—whether walking or riding; and some say that for a rider we specifically require three mil.

(13) Increases his intoxication—specifically a little sleep; but much sleep weakens it [Magen Avraham in the name of Kesef Mishneh].

(14) But a rider—for he does not have so much exertion.

(15) Weakens the wine—and this is when he rode three mil; but nevertheless everything depends on the quantity of wine, for if he drank a great deal, traveling three mil does not weaken it.
