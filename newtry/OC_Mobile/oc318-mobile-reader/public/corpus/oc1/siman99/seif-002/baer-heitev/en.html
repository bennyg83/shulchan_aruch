Sleep intoxicates him. This applies specifically to a little sleep, but much sleep dissipates the wine. Kesef Mishneh, Hilchos Biyas HaMikdash.
Riding. One who is riding requires three mil. See Magen Avraham, Chavos Yair responsum 177, Bach responsum 140, Halachos Ketanos, part 1, responsum 183, and Yad Eliyahu responsum 8. The latter rules that only wine disqualifies one from prayer, while one who consumed other intoxicants may pray unless he is so intoxicated that he cannot speak before a king. See there. At the end of the responsum, however, he writes that since Bach did not distinguish in his responsum between prayer and issuing halachic rulings, and was also stringent regarding our wines, one should not be lenient. Even other intoxicants are forbidden, since an elder has already ruled, although according to the strict law there is room for leniency. See there.
