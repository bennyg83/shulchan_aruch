His intoxication. Specifically a little sleep; but much sleep dispels it, as explained in Laws of Entering the Sanctuary.

Riding. And riding requires three mil; see Magen Avraham and in responsa of Chavot Yair siman 177. And in the responsum of Bach siman 140 and in Hilchot Ketanot vol. 1 siman 183 and in responsum of Yad Eliyahu siman 8—he ruled that for prayer he is not forbidden except wine, but other intoxicants are permitted unless he is so drunk that he cannot speak before the king, at the end. Except he wrote at the end of the responsum that since Bach in the responsum did not distinguish between prayer and ruling, and also was stringent regarding our wines, therefore one may not be lenient that even other intoxicants are forbidden, since the elder already ruled, although by law one may be lenient, at the end.
