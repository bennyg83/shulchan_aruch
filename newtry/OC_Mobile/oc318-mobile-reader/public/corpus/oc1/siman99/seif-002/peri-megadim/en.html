But. See Taz and Magen Avraham, seif katan 5. His intent is that the Gemara's statement, 'Now that you have reached this conclusion,' is merely a rejection, while the first answer remains primary. This is necessarily so because Rabban Gamliel said, 'Did we drink a revi'is of Italian wine?' According to the second answer there is no distinction, and even an ordinary revi'is would require three mil when riding. Rather, the later statement only provides another way to resolve Rami bar Abba's difficulty, while the halachah follows the first answer. The Gemara's statement that Italian wine intoxicates 'more' indicates much more than a revi'is of ordinary wine. Therefore Tur and Halachos Gedolos rule that for one who rode after drinking more than a revi'is, one mil suffices, while Italian wine requires three mil. Such wine is not common among us. Rif and Rosh, who do not mention this, hold that one should not be lenient regarding riding after more than a revi'is. Their omission of the rule that three mil suffice can be explained because one who drank a great deal, though not formally intoxicated, may be in a worse state than one who drank a revi'is of Italian wine, so even three mil may not suffice; Italian wine itself is not common. As for the statement that after the fact he discharged his obligation through sleep, I do not understand it, since sleep disturbs him all the more. Perhaps it is a scribal error and should read that after the fact one who had merely drunk discharged his obligation, for although he should not pray initially, after the fact his prayer is valid.
