But Taz and Magen Avraham (5)—and his intention is that now that we have come to this it is rejected, and the essence is like the first version; and therefore it is correct from what Rabban Gamliel said: "Did we drink any Italian wine?" And the latter version: there is no distinction, and even plain wine—a revi'it, riding three mil—rather it implies that this is what is now possible to resolve the difficulty of Rami bar Abba; and the essence of resolution one: from what is said there, Italian wine intoxicates—from the term "more" it indicates much more than a revi'it; and therefore the Tur and Halakhot Gedolot ruled that riding with more than a revi'it—a mil helps, and Italian wine three mil—and this is not found among us. Rif and Rosh, who did not mention this, hold that one should not be lenient with more than a revi'it while riding; and that which they did not write that three mil help—one may say that certainly if he drank a lot, even though he is not drunk, nevertheless it is less than a revi'it of Italian wine and three mil do not help; and Italian wine is not found, etc. And indeed what he wrote that after the fact one fulfills with sleep—it is questionable whether sleep all the more so agitates him, etc.; and it is possible it is a scribal error and one must say: after the fact one fulfills with one who has drunk moderately, for ab initio he may not pray—that after the fact it is fine.
