(2) There—if he drank wine in the amount of a revi'it etc.—meaning, even though he is accustomed to wine such that if he drinks a lot he is not in the category of drunk but can speak before the king, and we do not call him drunk but shatui—even so if he drank a revi'it he should not pray ab initio; and as our master explained in Beit Yosef, Tur's words who wrote shatui is anyone who can speak before the king, but nevertheless ab initio he should not pray even if he drank only a revi'it—see end matter in Bach and examine well; Birkei Yosef ot 1, zekher leTzadik ot 10, Sha'arei Teshuvah ot 1; however if he began to pray he does not stop—Eliyah Rabbah siman 128 ot 67.
