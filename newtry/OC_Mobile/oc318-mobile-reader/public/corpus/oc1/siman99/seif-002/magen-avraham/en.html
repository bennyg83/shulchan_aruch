Sleep intoxicates him. This applies specifically to a little sleep, but much sleep dissipates the wine. Kesef Mishneh, Hilchos Biyas HaMikdash.
Riding. The Gemara states that one who is riding requires three mil. Hagahos Ashri and Lechem Chamudos, Berachos chapter 8, siman 32, write likewise, as does Ran in the chapter HaKorei Omed regarding Bircas Kohanim. Tur implies that riding is equivalent to walking. It is possible that Tur agrees that after more than a revi'is, three mil are required.
