That sleep increases his intoxication—specifically a little sleep, but much sleep weakens it (as the fifth [chapter] of Bias Mikdash).

Rider. It is found in the gemara that a rider requires three mil; and similarly Hagahos Maimonios and Levush, chapter 8 of Berachos siman 32; and similarly the Ran, chapter HaKorei Omed, regarding lifting of hands; and in the Tur it implies that riding is like walking—and it is possible that he agrees that with more than a revi'it he requires three mil.
