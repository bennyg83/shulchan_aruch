Seif 2: This applies when walking. This is the Gemara's ruling and is Tur's wording in the name of Halachos Gedolos; Semag writes likewise. They omitted the Gemara's requirement of three mil. It appears that they follow the view of the Geonim stated in seif 3, that the decisive factor is whether the wine has dissipated. Rabban Gamliel therefore did not say to walk three mil as an absolute measure, but to walk until the wine dissipated; it happened to dissipate after three mil. They also omitted the Gemara's later qualification, perhaps because they regard it as merely a rejection. Rif, Rambam, and Rosh omit the law of riding entirely, while Hagahos Ashri there records the Gemara's full discussion.
