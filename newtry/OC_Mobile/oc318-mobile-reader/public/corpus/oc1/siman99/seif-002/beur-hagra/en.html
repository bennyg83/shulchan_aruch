Seif 2: and this means while walking etc. Gemara there, and this is Tur's wording in the name of Halachot Gedolot, and so Semag; and they omitted from Bavli that we require three mil—and it seems he holds like the opinion of the Geonim, as written in seif 3, that only one who is shatui etc., and therefore Rabbenu Gershom did not say travel three mil but until his wine is dispelled—and it happened that in three mil it was dispelled. And they also omitted from their words "now that you have come" etc., and it is possible they hold it is a postponement (dechuyah); and Rif and Rambam and Rosh omitted the law of riding entirely, and Eliyah Rabbah there wrote all the words of the Gemara there.
