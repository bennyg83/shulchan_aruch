[3] The law of Krias Shema. Some are lenient because prayer differs, as it is a request for one's needs. Levush. He may recite blessings. This is derived from 'You shall eat, be satisfied, and bless,' meaning even in a state of satiation. Regarding excrement, however, Bach writes that all blessings have the same law as prayer. See siman 185, where I cited my Eliyah Rabbah on Yoreh Deah, from which additional laws may be derived.
