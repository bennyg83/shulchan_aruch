And therefore "we are not careful" etc.—this refers to one who has drunk moderately, for whom there is no prohibition except ab initio; but regarding one who is drunk such that his prayer is an abomination, one must certainly be careful even for our [weaker] wines; and one who prayed while drunk must return and pray, as Tosafos wrote in chapter HaDar; and Rabbenu Yonah wrote in the name of Mordechai that nowadays we are not careful because in any event we are not so careful in intent—this refers to other matters we heard, such as one who comes from the road that he should not pray per the gemara's words; and similarly R' Yehuda did not pray on a hot day, as Beit Yosef wrote afterward in the name of Hagahos Maimonios. But not the prayer of a drunk person, which is an abomination—and there is a deficiency in Mordechai; and it was already corrected in the Mordechai Krakow edition; and Maharach, may his memory be blessed, elaborated on this that one must return and pray in this; and they already exaggerated in the gemara regarding the punishment of one who prays while drunk, and they said that one who is careful in this—the troubles are closed before him; and they derived it from a verse; and from the positive you hear the negative, heaven forbid—therefore extra caution is required in this.
