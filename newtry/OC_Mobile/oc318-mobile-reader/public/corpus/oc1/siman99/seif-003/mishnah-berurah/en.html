(16) And therefore "we are not careful" etc.—this refers to one who has drunk moderately, for whom there is no prohibition except ab initio; but regarding one who is drunk such that his prayer is an abomination, one must certainly be careful even according to our [weaker] wines, and he must return and pray [Bach and Taz].

(17) "That we are not concerned"—this is to justify the custom; but nevertheless it is not correct [Peri Megadim]. He wrote in Yesh Shalom, chapter 2 of Beitzah, and on Yom Tov one may pray Minchah even though he drinks a little, for it is impossible to wait until his wine passes—and all the more so nowadays, when in any event we are not so careful in intent, that one need not be strict about moderate drinking.
