(3) There—a revi'it amount etc.—wine within the meal does not intoxicate, Semak siman 133; and some say wine before the meal also does not intoxicate, Beit Yosef siman 473; Magen Avraham and Machatzit HaShekel seif kaf-alef, Razah ot 2; and Peri Megadim wrote in Eshel Avraham ot 1 that everything is according to what he understands and his mind; and if he ate a joyous meal such as a Yom Tov meal—even though he drank only at the meal—one may not rule, Magen Avraham there, in the name of the gloss of Semak; and Machatzit HaShekel wrote there that ruling and prayer share the same law; and also in this Peri Megadim wrote there that everything depends on what sort of person he is; and see in Yoreh De'ah siman 242 seif 13 in the gloss.
