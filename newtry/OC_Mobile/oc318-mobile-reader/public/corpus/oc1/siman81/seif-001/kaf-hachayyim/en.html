(a) [Seif 1] A child who reached the stage of one like him, etc.; even though he has not yet eaten grain at all, we distance from his excrement and urine, for once he reached this stage his excrement putrefies. Beit Yosef. Levush. Eliyah Rabbah ot 1. Taz s.k. 1. And he concluded that Bach's words do not appear at all, and Shulchan Aruch's ruling is clear and established; see there. And likewise Shaarei Teshuvah in Maamar Mordechai ot 1 and Hagahot Beit Yosef. And likewise Eliyah Rabbah there that one should be stringent like the Mechaber's words. And Rav David Arameh in commentary to Rambam z"l concludes like Maran's words; see there. Maamar Mordechai ot 2. Chasam Sofer ot 1. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 5 ot 5. However, Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef ot 2 brought Bach: if he did not eat a kezayit of grain, or even if he ate and it is known this excrement is not from grain, we do not distance; and it implies agreement. Ateret Tzvi ot 4, Nachalat Tzvi likewise. Peri Chadash: initially be concerned for the Mechaber; after the fact if he read or prayed he fulfilled and need not repeat. Machatzit HaShekel ot 2, Siddur Beit Yehudah ot 29. Shaarei Teshuvah ot 1. Chasam Sofer. Rav Zundel. Kaf HaChayyim laws of prayer conduct:
(b) There; can eat a kezayit of grain, etc. All five species are called grain: wheat, barley, spelt, oats, and rye. Rashi Sukkah 42b, and Beit Yosef, Taz, and Magen Avraham there. And Taz s.k. 2 wrote that even if he ate the grain in kinds of cooked food and porridge. And the later courts. And see Mishnat Zahav ot 2. And in food made for an infant that they call pap, we estimate according to what an adult can eat; a peras of pap. Magen Avraham s.k. 2. Eliyah Rabbah in Hagahot Beit Yosef. Ateret Tzvi ot 2.
