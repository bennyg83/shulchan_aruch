A minor who has reached the category of one like him, etc.—see Mishna Berurah what he wrote, that if he is weak, etc.—this is from Magen Avraham's words, and the reason is that we follow his view; and it seems all the more that we follow his view to be stringent; therefore if we see that this minor can eat an olive of grain within the time to eat a peras, and others his age have not yet reached this measure, nevertheless by law one must distance from his feces and his urine. What I wrote at the end, the practical difference between lekhatchilah and bedieved, stems from Sha'arei Teshuvah in the name of Machatzis HaBashekel—see there; only that he wrote the law regarding reading, and in my humble opinion regarding reading one should be stringent to go back and recite the paragraph of Shema again, since there is no concern of a blessing in vain, and it is also from the Torah.
