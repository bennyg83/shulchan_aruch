Grain—Ba'er Hetev, and what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of the old Ba'er Hetev, likewise in Tosafos Hagahah on Shabbos. And see Pnei Meiros part 2 siman 225, that Magen Avraham's view is that since in its place the impurity is great, it is forbidden to others too because of the impurity of the smell—see there. And see Machatzit HaShekel, who wrote that this refers certainly when there is no smell—for otherwise it would be forbidden because of the smell, and what he wrote there is not implied. And see responsa Beit Yehudah siman 35—a baby sitting on a potty with clothes folded down and nothing of the potty visible, we read Kerias Shema in his presence and educate him, etc., since it is covered—see there. And there in siman 39 he wrote that regarding general blessings the Tosafos were uncertain regarding feces. And see Levush siman 185 regarding herdsman; regarding nakedness it is obvious it is forbidden, etc.—see there.
