[2] An olive-sized portion of grain, etc. The gloss to Semak, siman 57, states that the law regarding the food made for an infant called papa requires study. The Magen Avraham writes: It appears to me that we measure according to the amount of papa that an adult can eat within the time required to eat a peras. The Taz writes: This applies even to cooked foods such as porridge. The Maharshal writes that it is improper to recite Krias Shema near an infant, since an infant ordinarily handles refuse. Moreover, infants do not clean themselves, and excrement at the anal opening is forbidden.
