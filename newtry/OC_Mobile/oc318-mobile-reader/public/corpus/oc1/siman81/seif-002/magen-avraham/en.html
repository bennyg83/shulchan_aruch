And if it is impossible, such as, etc. It is difficult—he should turn his face and the feces would be behind him. Granted, in the Gemara it was said regarding prayer that one must stand facing Jerusalem, but here regarding Shema it is difficult. And it seems to me that the case is where there is feces on both sides and before him is a river; and you would say he should walk behind it until the feces passes and then turn his face away from it—one may say they did not trouble him to turn backwards since it is possible to place it to the sides.

To the place where he stopped. Since it was not within his four amot, we do not say he should have checked; and if he waited long enough to complete all of it, see siman 65.
