Olive—Taz: Rashi according to his view requires that he actually ate—that feces of these species putrefies; he said well that the Torah prohibition applies specifically to bread, but the same applies to porridge. Rather he proved to Rashi the dissenting view too, that "from when he ate" is to know the measure; and the same if he never tasted—and nevertheless the Torah prohibition applies specifically where he could eat bread. We learn that even if he reached only the ability to eat porridge, we distance; and the measure is in grain, for he can eat other foods and not grain—and specifically regarding grain Chazal estimated here. See Magen Avraham.
