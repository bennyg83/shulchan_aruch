(2) There—he can eat an olive of grain, etc. All five species are called grain: wheat, barley, spelt, oats, and rye. Rashi Sukkah 42b, and Beit Yosef, Taz, and Magen Avraham brought it there. Taz wrote seder katan 2 that even if he ate the grain in kinds of cooked dish and porridge. Later authorities. See Machatzit HaShekel letter 2. And in food made for a baby called "pappa" we estimate according to what an adult can eat—a peras of pappa. Magen Avraham seder katan 2. Eliyah Rabbah in Hagahot Beit Yosef. Eliyah Rabbah letter 2.
