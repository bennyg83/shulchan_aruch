And they answer after him. It seems to me that if the shaliach tzibbur says [Barekhu] silently and one did not hear Barekhu from him but only heard the congregation answering, one says Amen after them as written in siman 139 seif 6 in the hagahah.

And the shaliach tzibbur returns and says. It is written in Agudah: if there are not ten there besides him, if he did not answer with them he does not fulfill his obligation.

While he prolongs. Specifically during the melody; but when he says the words they should say nothing [hagahah of Yein Nitzan and Levush]. And see siman 68 and end of siman 125.
