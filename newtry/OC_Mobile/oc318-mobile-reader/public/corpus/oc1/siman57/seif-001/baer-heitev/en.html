And they answer. And if the shaliach tzibbur says [Barekhu] silently and one did not hear Barekhu from him but only heard the congregation answering, one says Amen after them as written in siman 139 seif 6 in the hagahah. The reason the shaliach tzibbur says "Barekhu" and not "N'varekh" as in zimmun—see Tosafot Yom Tov on chapter 7 of Berakhot mishnah 3. And in responsa Matteh Yosef part 2 siman 15.

And the shaliach tzibbur returns and says. And if there are not ten there besides him, if he did not answer with them he does not fulfill his obligation. Agudah, Magen Avraham.

Extending. Specifically during the melody; but when he says the words they should say nothing. And if the chazzan does not extend in Barekhu, it is better that they be silent and have intention for what the shaliach tzibbur says so that they may answer "Barukh the Omnipresent ha-mevorakh l'olam va'ed." Levush and hagahah of Yein Nitzan: {Rama: Those accustomed to say "At the Omnipresent Elohekha tira" when the shaliach tzibbur reaches "et"—Maharshal wrote this is not accepted and is not from the foundation of wisdom.}
