And the congregation, etc. It is good that the congregation say so when the chazzan prolongs in the melody on one letter; therefore if he does not prolong in Barekhu but only separates the letters and words, it is better that they be silent and have intention and listen to what the shaliach tzibbur says (Maharam of Padua). Nevertheless they should not say the verse "At the Omnipresent Elohekha tira" (Maharshal in responsa siman 64). And the shaliach tzibbur returns and says "Barukh the Omnipresent ha-mevorakh l'olam va'ed"; and likewise for Amen yehei shemei rabbah the shaliach tzibbur must return and say it.
