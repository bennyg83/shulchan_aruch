דין ברכו וענייתו. ובו ב סעיפים:
Laws of Barekhu and its response. In it are 2 sections: The shaliach tzibbur says "Barekhu et the Omnipresent ha-mevorakh," and they answer after him "Barukh the Omnipresent ha-mevorakh l'olam va'ed," and the shaliach tzibbur returns and says "Barukh the Omnipresent ha-mevorakh l'olam va'ed."The practice is that the shaliach tzibbur prolongs in Barekhu and the congregation say "Yitbarakh' v'yishtabach," etc., while he prolongs in Barekhu (Tur).
