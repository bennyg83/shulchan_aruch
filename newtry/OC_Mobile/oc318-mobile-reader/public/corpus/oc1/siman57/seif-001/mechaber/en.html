דין ברכו וענייתו. ובו ב סעיפים:
Laws of Bar'khu and its responses comprising 2 sections: The prayer leader says: "Bar'khu et Adonai ha-mevorach", and they [the congregation] answer after him: "Barukh Adonai ha-mevorach l'olam va'ed", then the prayer leader returns and says: "Barukh Adonai ha-mevorach l'olam va'ed".Rem"a: And some have the custom that the prayer leader lengthens "Bar'khu", and the congregation say' "Yitbarakh v'yishtabach..." while he is extending "Bar'khu" (Tur).
