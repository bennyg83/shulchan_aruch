Magen Avraham s.k. 1—in the hagahah it is indeed so as written in that hagahah; but in Shulchan Aruch siman 124 in Hagahat Tashbetz it is explained that if the congregation heard, they say "Barukh Hashem ha-mevorakh" and answer with them even if they did not hear Barekhu—for this reason. And I have seen in Pri Megadim that Ohr Zaru'a stood on this and distinguished: when hearing from an individual in siman 139 one does not answer with him, unlike siman 124 where one hears from the congregation and answers with them—and he challenged this from it.
