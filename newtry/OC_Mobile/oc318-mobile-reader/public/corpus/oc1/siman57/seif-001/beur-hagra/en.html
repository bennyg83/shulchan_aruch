"Ha-mevorakh," etc.—per R. Yonah, for so is the conclusion of the Gemara there.

And they answer, etc. As written in the mishnah: as he blesses so they answer, etc., and the same applies here.

And he returns, etc. As written in Beit Yosef there mishnah 6 paragraph 2: he returns to where he stopped, per Rif's explanation there. And although they said in Yerushalmi that Shmuel said he does not remove himself from the category of those answering—the words [of Barekhu]—behold the one who reads Torah says, according to R. Abin: since he says "ha-mevorakh" he does not remove himself from the category of those answering; likewise when they say in Birkat haMazon "N'varekh" he still returns, etc.

And the practice, etc. But Rambam and the Geonim did not write it, and likewise Tur challenged it as written in Sotah 40a; therefore Shulchan Aruch omitted it. But in the name of Rosh he wrote to reconcile that they say while he prolongs, etc., as written here; and [Rosh] wrote a sign for the matter: the sages concede, etc., and see above siman 128 seif 26.
