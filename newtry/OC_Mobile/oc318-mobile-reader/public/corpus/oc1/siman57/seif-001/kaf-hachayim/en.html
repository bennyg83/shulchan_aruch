(1) [Seif 1] The shaliach tzibbur says "Barekhu et the Omnipresent ha-mevorakh" and they answer after him, etc. And if the shaliach tzibbur says [Barekhu] silently and one did not hear Barekhu from him but only the congregation heard and answered, one says Amen after them as written in siman 139 seif 6 in the hagahah—Magen Avraham s.k. 1; Beit David 11 in Hagahat Tashbetz; Ma'amar Mordechai letter 1; Shulchan Arukh HaRav 1; Chayei Adam 30:8; Chochmat Shlomo letter 3. However Pri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav letter 1 challenged Magen Avraham's words above from Mor Uktzia in hagahah siman 56 seif 1 and end of siman 124, which imply there that one who hears from the congregation answers with them; and what is written in siman 139 seif 6 that they answer Amen there is different, for one hears only from the shaliach tzibbur, unlike here where one hears from the congregation—see there. And likewise Ohr Zaru'a challenged in siman 139 letter 9 and wrote that specifically one who hears only from the shaliach tzibbur answers Amen, but one who hears from the congregation answers "Barukh," etc.—see there. And likewise R. Zalman agreed letter 2; Siddur Ba'al haTanya in laws of Barekhu letter 1; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 15 letter 8; Ben Ish Chai parashat Vayigash letter 17.
