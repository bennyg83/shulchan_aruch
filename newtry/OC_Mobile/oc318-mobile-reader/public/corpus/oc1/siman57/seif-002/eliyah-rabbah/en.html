[2] "While he prolongs," etc. Levush explained: he prolongs in the melody on one letter, but when cutting the words one should intend to hear the shaliach tzibbur. Maharshal siman 64 wrote: when the shaliach tzibbur says Barekhu I say "Yitbarakh" until each blessing and praise, and when he reaches the Name I say "Barukh shem kevod malkhuto l'olam va'ed," when he reaches "ha-mevorakh" I say "Yehi shem Hashem m'vorakh me'atah v'ad olam," and when he finishes "ha-mevorakh" I answer "Barukh Hashem ha-mevorakh l'olam va'ed" standing before I sit—until here. And likewise Magen Avraham wrote; and by this it seems the shaliach tzibbur must also prolong on the Name and on the word "ha-mevorakh." And I have found those accustomed to say "At Hashem Elohekha tira" when the shaliach tzibbur reaches "et"—Maharshal wrote this is not accepted and is not from the foundation of wisdom.
