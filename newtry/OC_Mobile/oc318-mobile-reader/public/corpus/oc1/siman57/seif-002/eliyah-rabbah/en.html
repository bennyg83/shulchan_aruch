[2] While he prolongs, etc. Levush explains that this means while he prolongs a melody on one letter, but while the words are being articulated one should concentrate on hearing them from the chazzan. Maharshal wrote in siman 64: When the chazzan says 'Barechu,' I recite 'May He be blessed' through 'every blessing and praise.' When he reaches the Divine Name, I say, 'Blessed is the Name of the glory of His kingdom forever and ever.' When he reaches 'the blessed One,' I say, 'May the Name of Hashem be blessed from now and forever.' When he finishes 'the blessed One,' I respond, 'Blessed is Hashem, the blessed One, forever and ever,' while standing, before I sit. Thus far his words. Magen Avraham writes likewise. According to this, it appears that the chazzan must prolong both the Divine Name and the word 'the blessed One.' I also found that regarding those who customarily say, 'Hashem your God you shall fear,' when the chazzan reaches the word 'es,' Maharshal wrote that this practice is neither from Kabbalah nor founded upon wisdom.
