Derashos Maharil, order of the Haggadah, likewise writes: 'One should hold the three matzos. The upper one is for HaMotzi, the third provides the second whole loaf, and the broken one represents poor person's bread. One recites HaMotzi, lets the third matzah slip from the hand, and while holding the broken piece together with the upper matzah recites Al Achilas Matzah. Both blessings should be recited before breaking them.' Chok Yaakov, subsection 2, cites this and writes that the later authorities agree. Shelah, page 142b, likewise writes that this satisfies every opinion and doubt. See there. Mateh Moshe, siman 645; Chok Yosef, subsection 2; Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsections 4 and 5; Ben Ish Chai, parashas Tzav, subsection 34; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2, write likewise.
