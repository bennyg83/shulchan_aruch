(ו) There. He grasps them — all three for lechem mishneh and broken for lechem oni (Magen Avraham seif 2). So Maharil's drashot Seder Haggadah: grasp three matzot — upper for HaMotzi, third for lechem mishneh, broken for lechem oni; bless HaMotzi, set aside third, bless on broken with upper grasped for eating matzah, both blessings before breaking — end; Bach Chok Yaakov seif 2: Acharonim agreed; Shelah 142b; fulfills all views.
