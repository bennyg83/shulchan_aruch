[Levush] Pesach and matzah, etc. So explained Rashi, Tosafos, the Rosh, Tur, and Mordechai in chapter Arvei Pesachim, and in Zevachim folio 79. And Lechem Mishneh already challenged the Rambam, who wrote that they ate only matzah and maror together and not Pesach; and Chok Yaakov wrote that they relied on him to say one wraps matzah and maror and not to mention Pesach. And in truth, from what the Mordechai and Terumas HaDeshen wrote, that they ate three olive-bulks of matzah, maror, and charoses, it is implied that they did not eat Pesach with it, for if so there would be four olive-bulks; and this requires further study. And the difficulty from Yoreh De'ah siman 98 regarding prohibitions nullifying one another will be clarified there.
