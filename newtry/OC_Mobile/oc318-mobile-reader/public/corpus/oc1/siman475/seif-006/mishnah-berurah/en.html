(40) And he returned and ate, etc. And the same applies even if he did not interrupt in between, but only delayed in eating the kezayit more than the time of eating a peras: they do not combine together, and he does not fulfill.
(41) He fulfilled. That is after the fact; but ab initio he must eat a kezayit together and swallow.
(42) Between one eating and its fellow. The wording is not so precise, for he must not delay from the beginning of the first eating until the end of the last eating more than the time of eating a peras, for that is not counted within the category of eating.
(43) And see above, siman 612. That is the measure of a peras.
