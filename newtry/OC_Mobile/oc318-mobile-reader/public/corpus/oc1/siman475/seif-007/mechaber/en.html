There is no obligation to eat matza except on the first night alone. And even on the first night one fulfills with a kezayit.And the custom is to make the three Seder matzot from an issaron, in commemoration of the loaves of the thanksgiving offering, and they make a mark on them to know which is first or second or third, and they place the first on top, the second in the middle, and the third on the bottom for the korekh; and if he changed the order he did not invalidate (Tur in the name of Rosh). And they bake them in order as well (Kol Bo and Avudraham).
