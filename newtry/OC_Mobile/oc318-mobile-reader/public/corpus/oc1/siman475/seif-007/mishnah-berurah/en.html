(44) Only on the first night. For it is written "in the evening you shall eat matzos"; but all the other nights and all the days he is only warned not to eat chametz. And even though he is obligated to eat bread on Yom Tov, as is proven in siman 188, he can fulfill that with matzah ashira, namely that which is kneaded with fruit juice; but lechem oni he is obligated according to the Torah only on the first night; and because of the doubt of the day, for us he is obligated also on the second night.
(45) Alone. And in the name of the Gra they wrote that in any event there is a mitzvah to eat matzah all seven days, only that it is not an obligation.
(46) And they were accustomed to make, etc. See Sefer Beis Meir, who questions the custom; and in several places today the custom has fallen into disuse.
(47) A mark. And one should not make letters for identification, for when one breaks it, it is erasing on Yom Tov.
(48) One of them. Meaning: even if the third broke, and if so they now advance the third before the second, nevertheless because of priority for the mitzvah one need not bake another.
(49) He fulfills with a kezayit. See se'if 1 and in the Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 9.
