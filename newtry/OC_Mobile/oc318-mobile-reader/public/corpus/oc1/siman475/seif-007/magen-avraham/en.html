(א) On the first night. And nevertheless he is obligated to eat bread every day, only he fulfills with rich matza (Ran chapter 2 of Sukkah); see siman 188 seif 7, and see siman 471 seif 2.

(ב) From an issaron. Darkei Moshe wrote that Mahariv wrote that if he has many children so that the measure of challah is not sufficient for him, it is permitted to knead two doughs less than the measure and combine them; and Maharil forbids unless he limits himself to take an issaron, end of Darkei Moshe's words. And it requires further study, for Mahariv wrote to combine them at the time of challah and to make from both of them three matzot so that he can give a kezayit to each, and thereby there is no prohibition at all; and so too in Hagahot Semak, and so one should practice; see siman 456.

(ג) He fulfills with a kezayit. See siman 482, and see there — not for its own sake.
