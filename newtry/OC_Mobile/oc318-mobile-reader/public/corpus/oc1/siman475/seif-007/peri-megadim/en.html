for Rabbis l'chatchila require each separately and b'dieved together — so Tosafot s.v. ella and Eliyah Rabbah letter zayin — see there; concludes there in our time eat first matzah alone and maror alone, then together — remembrance for the Temple like Hillel; Bach wrote now there is no mitzvah at all even d'rabbanan even for Hillel to eat matzah and maror together since there is no Pesach — see there; and this is Rashi and Rashbam who wrapped all three together for Hillel, unlike now without Pesach therefore say remembrance for the Temple; and obvious if he spoke between blessing and wrapping he need not return and bless; Tosafot s.v. ella bet wrote therefore now need each alone then both together — if eating both at once, maror d'rabbanan now nullifies matzah d'oraisa; if eating matzah first alone then matzah of rabbinic origin nullifies maror d'rabbanan now; and they wrote nevertheless if halacha were clear like Hillel, etc. — they meant Rabbis and d'rabbanan — one may explain they wrote it appears halacha like Hillel in Temple times wrapping matzah and maror (without Pesach), and so now wrapping is d'rabbanan; impossible now even for Hillel since maror d'rabbanan nullifies matzah d'oraisa — therefore even Hillel needs now l'chatchila each alone then both together d'rabbanan like Torah law; and on this they wrote nevertheless if halacha were clear like Hillel there would be another enactment — eat matzah first then matzah and maror in wrap, and matzah would not be optional d'rabbanan requiring wrap per Rambam who wraps matzah and maror without Pesach — therefore for us wrap is d'rabbanan like Torah law; but uncertain like whom is halacha — perhaps Rabbis, and matzah of rabbinic origin nullifies maror d'rabbanan — therefore need first each alone then both together lest Hillel; and if challenged 'remembrance for the Temple' — it is d'rabbanan mitzvah; impossible to say only d'rabbanan remembrance — rather one may say they say remembrance for the Temple in the wrap to inform that now one may not go out with wrap alone, which nullifies maror d'rabbanan to matzah d'oraisa; and because remembrance for the Temple there they would fulfill everything in wrap alone, both d'oraisa — as stated; nevertheless even now matzah d'rabbanan requires wrap for Hillel; and specifically l'chatchila each alone then wrap; but not reverse, for how bless on eating matzah and maror nullifies — impossible except first fulfill matzah d'oraisa (even for Hillel now) and maror d'rabbanan; with this Taz, Eliyah Rabbah, and Chayei Adam are also explained — as I wrote, in my opinion.
