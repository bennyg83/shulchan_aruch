Even without this reason, one might have thought to obligate him to eat again because while he was mentally incapacitated he did not know that it was Pesach and that he was obligated to eat matzah, as stated above in seif 4. This is not sufficient, however, because he likewise did not think that it was an ordinary weekday or that the food was not matzah; one might therefore have thought that he fulfilled his obligation. The stated reason is consequently necessary. This is clear. See Lechem Mishneh, chapter 2 of Hilchos Shofar.
