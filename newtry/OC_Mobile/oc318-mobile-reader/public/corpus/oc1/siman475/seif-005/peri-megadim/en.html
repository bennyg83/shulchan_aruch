(א) Third matza to fulfill mitzvah in wrapping — Taz. So Rosh Pesachim 116a. We wrote for Rif two matzot suffice whole and broken; we make three as seif 7 Hagahah; even for Rosh HaMotzi on whole — then two suffice whole until HaMotzi, bless HaMotzi on lechem mishneh, then break second and bless eating matzah; perhaps must break before saying 'this poor bread' siman 473 seif 6; Levush there says Haggadah on lechem oni and children ask why hide half for afikoman.

(ב) It appears if on first night he has only two whole matzot he leaves them whole until HaMotzi, for lechem mishneh required — so Rosh Pesachim 116a distinguishes Pesach from other Yom Tov; breaking before 'this poor bread' is custom as Levush from Beit Yosef 473 Kol Bo — not in Talmud; Rambam chapter 8 law 6 breaks when saying HaMotzi; though we do not practice so, when impossible lechem mishneh certainly preferable as we say; see siman 482 one who is ill.
