(s.k. 5) A kezayit of maror. And even if he ate the whole plant intact, for in such a case some say in siman 214 that even less than a kezayit has the law of a kezayit to bless an after-blessing on it.
For one does not fulfill with the roots; and if so, one cannot eat it with the roots, for the optional roots come and nullify the maror of the mitzvah, as written in s.k. 13; and since he must remove the roots from it, it is no longer called a "whole item," as written in siman 210.
