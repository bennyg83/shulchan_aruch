Other authorities reverse this. The Shulchan Aruch therefore writes that one should hold all of them while reciting the blessings, thereby satisfying all opinions, as explained in Tur and Beis Yosef. See there. The Arizal's opinion agrees with those who recite HaMotzi over the whole matzah and Al Achilas Matzah over the broken matzah, as stated above in siman 473, subsection 117. See there for the mystical rationale. This is also the opinion of the later authorities, as stated in the following subsection.
