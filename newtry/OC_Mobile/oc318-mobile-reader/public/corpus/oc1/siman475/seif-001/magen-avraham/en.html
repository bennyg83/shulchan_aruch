(א) He should wash his hands. And even though he washed for the vegetables, as written in siman 473, nevertheless since he will recite the Haggadah we are concerned lest he diverted his attention and touched an unclean place (Gemara). And the Mordekhai wrote that if at the first washing he intended to complete the entire meal without diverting attention he need not wash a second time, end of his words. And it appears to me that one should not intend this, so as not to nullify the enactment of the Sages who enacted washing twice on this night (Beit Yosef). And I do not know where we find this enactment, for we say: one who washed his hands at the first dipping must wash his hands at the second dipping, and the reason is written alongside: lest he diverted his attention — and if so, when he did not divert attention he need not wash. However, he needed to bless immediately at the first washing; see siman 158 seif 7 that he must intend that he washes for eating, and see there seif 2.

(ב) And he should grasp them in his hand — all three — so that there will be lechem mishneh and a broken piece because of lechem oni (Bach, Maharshal, Shelah).

(ג) The upper one — even if it split on Shabbat, so wrote in Darkei Moshe; see siman 274.

(ד) A kezayit from each. For the broken piece of HaMotzi also requires a kezayit, and so is in the Tur; see siman 167 seif 61. Darkei Moshe wrote in Terumat HaDeshen siman 139 that one must eat the kezayit together and swallow it, and so too the Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafos, end of his words — it is implied from his wording that when he swallows one kezayit in one gulp; and so too in Terumat HaDeshen there, who wrote on that which we say we wrap all three in one gulp — the Mordekhai asks how one can hold the swallowing chamber so large, for a kezayit is half an egg, and one can say that when it reaches the swallowing chamber it is crushed, and therefore one can hold so much; thus it is proven from here that one should not swallow the kezayit of matza little by little, for otherwise the question would not be difficult at all, for one could well put all three olive-bulks together in his mouth and chew them together and swallow little by little; rather the mitzvah is to swallow together, and the same applies for us for one kezayit of matza or of maror, end of his words — it is implied that he holds that when he swallows one kezayit in one gulp. And that which is written in the Shulchan Aruch that he should eat them together means that he should put them in his mouth in one gulp. And Maharil wrote that one may eat little by little provided he does not delay in his eating more than the time to eat a peras; but in seif 6 it is implied that only after the fact he has fulfilled his obligation, but l'chatchila he must eat together, and so too in Chullin chapter 7 that one must swallow together; however for maror one may rely on Maharil if it is difficult for him to swallow together; see siman 172 seif 69.

(ה) A kezayit of maror. And even if he ate the whole stalk, it is not called a stalk to fulfill with less than a kezayit (as written in siman 210), for one does not fulfill with roots (Piskei Maharai siman 245).

(ו) Without leaning. And if he ate it while leaning he has fulfilled (as is clear in the Gemara).

(ז) And he dips it in charoset. And so too Bach and Maharal of Prague and Taz, and where it is customary it is customary.

(ח) He should not speak. And if he spoke he need not return and bless again (Bach); see siman 492 seif 63: it appears to me that for this he must swallow the two olive-bulks together as stated above, unless it is difficult for him.
