(Shulchan Aruch seif 1) And he blesses on eating maror. Note: in Yoreh De'ah siman 328 ruled one blesses to separate terumah or challah and it is two contradictory matters; appears because challah nowadays d'rabbanan even in Land when not all Israel upon it as siman 322; many differences between d'oraisa and d'rabbanan challah; to teach challah we separate now is d'rabbanan we bless per those who bless to separate challah.
