Further laws of the Seder. It contains 7 seifim: He washes his hands and recites the blessing over washing the hands. He takes the matzot in the order in which he placed them, the broken matzah between the 2 whole ones, holds them in his hand, and recites HaMotzi and the blessing over eating matzah. He then breaks from the upper whole matzah and from the broken one, from both together, and dips them in salt. {Rama: The custom is not to dip them in salt on the first night, because fine bread does not require salt.} He eats them together while reclining, a kezayit from each. If he cannot eat approximately 2 kezayitim together, he first eats the portion associated with HaMotzi and afterward the portion associated with the blessing over eating matzah. He then takes a kezayit of maror and immerses all of it in charoset, but does not leave it there, so that its bitter taste will not be nullified. For this reason he must shake the charoset off it. He recites the blessing over eating maror and eats it without reclining. He then takes the third matzah, breaks from it, wraps it together with the maror, and dips it in charoset. {Rama: Some say that it should not be dipped, and so it is stated in the customs, and this is what I have seen practiced.} He says, 'In remembrance of the Temple, according to Hillel,' and eats them together while reclining. From the time he recites the blessing over eating matzah, he should not interrupt with anything unrelated to the meal until he has eaten this sandwich, so that the blessing over eating matzah and the blessing over eating maror also apply to this sandwich.
