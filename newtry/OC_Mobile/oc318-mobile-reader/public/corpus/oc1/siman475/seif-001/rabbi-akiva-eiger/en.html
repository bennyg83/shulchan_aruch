Magen Avraham seif 5: for one does not fulfill with roots; even though one who eats non-kosher bird without kezayit is liable for non-kosher creature; even though bones are not included in prohibition, since he ate he is liable on meat for non-kosher creature — nevertheless here impossible to eat roots for they are nullified to maror as we say maror and matza eaten together how say nullified; so Machatzit HaShekel. Nevertheless in my opinion one can argue: there they say slight matza enters maror nullified and lacks from its measure; but here ultimately in some maror not nullified in roots he fulfills non-kosher creature rule since he eats all — examine.
