In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 1, And he wraps it with the maror. See Shaagas Aryeh, siman 60, that from the Rosh's words it is implied that maror requires a kezayit because one blesses "al achilas maror"; and if so, one could say that the wrapping (korech) does not require a kezayit. And he challenged this and brought proofs that one is obligated to eat a kezayit when there is a Pesach offering according to the Torah, and nowadays by rabbinic law, without relying on the reason of the blessing; and likewise for the wrapping that is done as a remembrance of the Temple according to Hillel, even though one does not bless on it, nevertheless it is not less than a kezayit; see there.
