(א) He grasps them. All three: the upper for HaMotzi, the third for lechem mishneh, the broken for lechem oni; bless HaMotzi, set aside the third from his hand, bless on the broken while grasping the upper for eating matzah, bless both blessings before breaking them. Maharil and Acharonim.

(ב) The upper one. Even if it split on Shabbat (Darkei Moshe above siman 474).

(ג) In salt. And he is in writing:

(ד) HaMotzi. As above siman 472 seif 8. Magen Avraham wrote at minimum l'chatchila swallow a crushed kezayit at once; b'dieved even little by little if he does not delay between them longer than eating a peras; and likewise maror — even l'chatchila if difficult to swallow together — end.

(ה) Selecting. To the death of the es inside and do not welcome until after the first harvest, eat:

(ו) Taste. One should not soak maror beforehand in water, for it is pickled (Magen Avraham siman 473 seif 14). Chok Yaakov is lenient — see there.

(ז) Leaning. If he wishes to lean he may.

(ח) In charoset. So Bach, Maharal of Prague, Taz, Chok Yaakov, and Acharonim — dip in charoset; Magen Avraham wrote where customary it is customary.

(ט) Like Hillel. Our formula is so: Hillel when the Temple stood wrapped matza and maror and ate, etc. Taz wrote it should read wrapped Pesach, matza, and maror and ate — so Rashi and Ari. Chok Yaakov wrote now there is no Pesach so one should not mention Pesach lest speak falsehood since one has no Pesach — therefore do not change our formula — end.

(י) He speaks. If he spoke he need not return and bless (Bach, Taz, Magen Avraham).
