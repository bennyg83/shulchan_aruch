(א) And he takes the matzot, etc., and grasps them. Magen Avraham. And as above siman 473 and as Gemara says he holds two, etc.

(ב) And he blesses, etc., and afterward. Per Rishonim in Tosafot Berachot 39b s.v. hakol that one blesses HaMotzi on the whole and on eating matzah on the broken lechem oni; but all on the broken — not because one does not make mitzvot bundled as bundles, as chapter Afikoman 102b and R' Menachem blessed all on the broken saying it is not called bundles here like kiddush cup where one blesses wine blessing and kiddush; and if one has only lettuce one blesses borei peri haadamah and on eating maror at first dipping; R' Yitzchak removed himself from dispute and did as written here — Rosh wrote.
