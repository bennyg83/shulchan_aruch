(ב) At first dipping borei peri haadamah — Magen Avraham's view: if one has only tamcha called horseradish, no blessing of enjoyment at all, only on eating maror; even in our case eating before meal since not normally eaten as is; Chok Yaakov wrote bless since fit to eat with vinegar etc. (Chasam Sofer in glosses siman 473); per his view bless properly also borei peri haadamah for this reason. Magen Avraham's words are somewhat difficult: if not fit for eating at all why fulfill maror obligation per poskim requiring fit food (perhaps fit via some preparation is called edible); especially per our custom eating crumbled it is fit; we see many Ashkenazim dip it with bread all year — therefore at minimum all do not fulfill as above siman 202 Mishna Berurah seif 5; perhaps also since Torah obligated eating it tonight it is significant eating, for Torah considered eating it as is — therefore bless appropriate blessing borei peri haadamah; in any case all certainly must bless; Gaon Ra'akiva in his novellae leans so — see there.
