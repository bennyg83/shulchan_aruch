Magen Avraham seif 10: how exempt horseradish here since must bless shehakol; in my opinion one can say if he blessed borei peri haadamah he fulfills since fruit of ground; but since when cooked it is not considered to bless on living thing, explicit blessing shehakol suffices; nevertheless one can say b'dieved he fulfilled per Magen Avraham above siman 202 and Ozer. Also what Magen Avraham wrote — I think should not bless at all as siman 202 regarding pepper — I did not know: there they say by law shehakol suffices but not that one does not bless at all except dry pepper where there is no food — horseradish certainly not comparable.
