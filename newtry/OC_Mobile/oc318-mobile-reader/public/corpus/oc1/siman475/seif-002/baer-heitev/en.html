Vegetables. And it is good to seek other vegetables, to remove oneself from the dispute in the Gemara; and if he has no vegetables, he should take something else whose blessing is Borei peri ha'adamah, even if it is a type of turnip. Magen Avraham.
Ha'adamah. And the Magen Avraham wrote: specifically, if he takes that type of maror that is fit to eat raw, one blesses on it thus; but what it is not the way to eat raw, such as tamcha that they call horseradish (chrein), one should bless on it only "al achilas maror" and not Borei peri ha'adamah, as with peppers that are clarified in siman 202, that one does not bless on them; end of his words; see there. And Chok Yaakov disagrees with him and wrote that on all types of maror one blesses Borei peri ha'adamah even if it is not eaten raw; therefore at the dipping one blesses Borei peri ha'adamah and "al achilas maror"; see there.
