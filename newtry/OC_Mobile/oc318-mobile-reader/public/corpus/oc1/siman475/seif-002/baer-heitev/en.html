(א) Vegetables. And it is good to return to the rest of the fruit of my soul’s follicles, and if he does not have vegetables, he will take another thing in his mouth. M:

(ב) haadamah. Magen Avraham wrote only if taking that type of maror fit to eat raw does one bless on it; what is not normally eaten raw like tamcha called horseradish — bless only on eating maror, not borei peri haadamah, like pepper explained siman 202 where one does not bless on it — end; see there. Chok Yaakov disagreed and wrote on all types of maror one blesses borei peri haadamah even if not eaten raw — nevertheless bless at dipping borei peri haadamah and on eating maror — see there.
