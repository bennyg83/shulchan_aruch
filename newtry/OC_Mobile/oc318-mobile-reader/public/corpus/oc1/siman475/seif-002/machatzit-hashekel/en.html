(Seif bet) And he holds them, etc.: all three from Shulchan Aruch language implies: when holding the upper and broken, on one he blesses motzi and on the second eating matzah; and since some say to bless on the whole motzi and on the broken eating matzah, and some reverse: therefore he holds both and blesses both blessings one after another (and why Taz wrote what Shulchan Aruch states 'if unable, etc., eat from motzi first' is not precise, for one does not know which is motzi); but Bach, Rashal, and Shelah wrote one must eat all three because of lechem mishneh: why should Pesach night be lesser than other Yom Tov in this?: only a broken piece is also required because an impoverished person's custom is to break.
