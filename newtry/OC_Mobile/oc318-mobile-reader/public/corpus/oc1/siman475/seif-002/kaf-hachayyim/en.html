2) Regarding the practical law, although initially we wash twice, in pressing circumstances when water is unavailable, or for an ill or elderly person who suffers from the cold, one may rely upon Mordechai, who is a great authority. It is possible that Rabbeinu Beis Yosef agrees in such a case, provided that the first washing is performed according to all its laws and with a blessing. Shulchan Gavoah, subsection 1.
