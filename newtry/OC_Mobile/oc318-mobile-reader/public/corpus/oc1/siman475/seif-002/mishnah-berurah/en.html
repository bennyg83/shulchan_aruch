(25) If he has no vegetables, etc. For ab initio the karpas must be of a species that is not of the five kinds of maror, as above in siman 473 in the Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 20.
(26) And "al achilas maror." For in this case one cannot act as at other times, to eat without a blessing and bless "al achilas maror" after a kezayit of matzah; for after he has already filled his belly with it earlier without a blessing, it is not proper to bless afterward. Therefore he should first bless "al achilas maror" and eat a little of it for the sake of karpas (and he cannot eat it for the sake of the mitzvah of maror, for ab initio we require matzah and then maror, as it is written "upon matzos and bitter herbs," etc.). And nevertheless the blessing is not in vain, for he blesses it in order to eat the maror after the matzah; and the Haggadah is not considered an interruption, provided he does not interrupt with other speech.
(27) And at the second dipping he should dip it, etc. That is the eating of maror after the kezayit of matzah: he should dip it in charoses, for since he eats this maror in order to fulfill the mitzvah of maror, he must dip it in charoses, as with maror in general. And for this reason he must also eat a kezayit.
(28) Without a blessing. For he was discharged by the blessings Borei peri ha'adamah and "al achilas maror" that he blessed over the first eating of maror. And know that many poskim disagree with this, and according to them it is preferable in this case to intend at the first eating of maror to fulfill the mitzvah of maror, since he then blesses "Who has sanctified us," etc.; and according to them he is obligated then to eat a kezayit and to dip in charoses according to the law of maror. And the second maror that he eats without a blessing is merely for the sake of karpas, and he is not obligated to eat a kezayit, and he need not dip specifically in charoses; and vinegar or salt water also suffice, as above in siman 473 regarding karpas.
