Se'if 2. If he has no vegetables for the first dipping except maror, etc. Marginal note: behold, regarding the law whether for maror [as karpas] any amount suffices, or we require a kezayit, see what I wrote in my novellae to tractate Sukkah, chapter 2, folio 27a, in my composition on Orach Chaim of the year 5616; and I brought there a proof that any amount suffices; see there. And see also in the introduction to my composition Sheyurei Taharah what I wrote there on this law; see there, and examine carefully.
