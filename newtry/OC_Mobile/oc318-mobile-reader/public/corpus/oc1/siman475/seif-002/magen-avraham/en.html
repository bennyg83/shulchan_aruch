(א) If he has no vegetables, etc. And it is good to seek other vegetables to remove himself from the dispute in the Gemara (Kesef Mishneh in Maggid Mishneh). And it appears to me that if he has no vegetables he should take something else whose blessing is Borei peri ha'adamah, even a type of pastry.

(ב) At the first dipping with Borei peri ha'adamah. It requires further study for us, since we are accustomed to take karpas for maror — if so, how do we bless on it with Borei peri ha'adamah, for it is not fit to eat while raw (as written in siman 210)? Granted, when one blesses on other vegetables with Borei peri ha'adamah it is not difficult how the karpas is discharged thereby, for one must bless on it Shehakol — one can say as some poskim write that the blessing of HaMotzi discharges it. And further it appears to me that one who eats karpas does not bless on it at all, for it is not the way to eat it as it is, as written in siman 202 regarding pepper; therefore it appears to me that if he has no other vegetables he says from the outset only the blessing on eating maror, as above.
