If he has no vegetables, etc. And it is good to seek other vegetables, to remove oneself from the dispute in the Gemara (so it is in Hagahos Maimoniyos). And it appears to me that if he has no vegetables, he should take something else whose blessing is Borei peri ha'adamah, even a type of turnip.
At the first dipping, Borei peri ha'adamah. This requires further study for us, since we are accustomed to take horseradish (chrein) for maror; if so, how do we bless Borei peri ha'adamah on it, for it is not fit to eat while raw (as written in siman 205)? Granted, when one blesses Borei peri ha'adamah on other vegetables, it is not difficult how the horseradish is discharged thereby, for one must bless Shehakol on it; one can say as some poskim wrote, that the blessing of HaMotzi discharges it. And further it appears to me that one who eats horseradish does not bless on it at all, for it is not the way to eat it as it is, as written in siman 202 regarding peppers; therefore it appears to me that if he has no other vegetables, he says from the outset only the blessing "al achilas maror," as above.
