In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 6, If he ate about half a kezayit, etc. And in Responsa She'elas Yaavetz, part 2, siman 18, he wrote that if on the night of Pesach one has only half a kezayit of matzah, he is not obligated to eat it at all, for "eating" implies a kezayit, etc.; see there. And so wrote in Sefer Bnei Chayei, siman 485. And Machatzis HaShekel cited on this a responsa of a certain sage, and he too, of blessed memory, elaborated at length, and his view is that just as regarding prohibitions a half-measure is forbidden according to the Torah, so regarding a mitzvah there is at least some mitzvah; and see below, siman 482, what is written on this.
