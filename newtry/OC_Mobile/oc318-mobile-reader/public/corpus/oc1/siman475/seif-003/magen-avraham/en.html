(א) If he swallowed matza he has fulfilled. But l'chatchila he must chew it, for we require the taste of matza (Rashbam). And if you ask, if so even after the fact he should not have fulfilled, as written in siman 461 seif 4 that one does not fulfill with cooked matza because the taste of matza is lacking — one can say that it is different there, for when it was cooked it is no longer in the category of matza upon it and it is as if he ate another type that is not matza; but here there is matza, only he does not sense it in his mouth; therefore he has fulfilled.

(ב) For the taste of maror is required — he should feel the bitterness in his mouth.

(ג) He has fulfilled for matza. And we do not say that maror nullifies matza since he did not chew them as one; but one who swallows matza and maror as one has not fulfilled, for maror nowadays is d'rabbanan and maror comes and nullifies matza. And with this is resolved that which they eat at first each one separately and afterward wrap them together, for it suffices when they wrap together — rather the reason is so that maror not nullify matza. And if you ask, if so let him eat matza first and afterward matza and maror — one can say that then matza would be optional and maror a d'rabbanan mitzvah, and matza would nullify matza for maror (all this is in the Gemara and Ran).
