One washes one's hands, etc. Even if one did not divert attention from guarding one's hands while reciting the Haggadah and Hallel, one must nevertheless wash again for the meal, without a blessing. When washing for dipping a vegetable, one did not intend that the washing should also cover the meal. A washing performed for food dipped in liquid does not cover eating bread unless one specifically intended this at the time of washing, as stated in siman 158. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there. See se'if 7 there and our words there, with the help of Heaven. Nevertheless, as stated above, it is initially improper to avoid diverting attention from the hands for this purpose.
