For maror taste is required — in mouth he should feel bitterness, commemoration of bitterness; if he does not feel, no commemoration; not so matza — though taste required, if he cooked does not fulfill siman 461; there we do not require feeling matza taste in mouth, for Torah did not command feeling, only eating alone; swallowing is called eating. Levush expanded on this difficulty and Lechem Mishneh's words. In formula zecher lemikdash keHillel one can say he wrapped Pesach matza maror — so Rashi in Gemara on Hillel who wrapped at once, i.e. Pesach with them.
