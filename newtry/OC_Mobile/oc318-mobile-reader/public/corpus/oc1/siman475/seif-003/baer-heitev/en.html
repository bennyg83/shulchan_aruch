(ב) He fulfilled. We do not say maror nullifies matza since he did not chew them together; but one who chews matza and maror together does not fulfill, for maror nowadays is d'rabbanan and matza d'oraisa, and maror is nullified by matza as in Gemara. Magen Avraham — see there.
