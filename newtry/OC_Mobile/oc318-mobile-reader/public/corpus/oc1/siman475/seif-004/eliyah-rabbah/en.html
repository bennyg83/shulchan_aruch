Only maror, etc. When they take horseradish (chrein), one should not bless Borei peri ha'adamah at all, only "al achilas maror," for it is not eaten as it is while raw, and furthermore it is not eaten as it is, like peppers in siman 202 (Magen Avraham). And Chok Yaakov wrote that the blessing over enjoyment is like a blessing over a mitzvah in siman 487, and therefore one does bless Borei peri ha'adamah on it.
