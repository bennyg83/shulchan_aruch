Seif 8 — an alleyway in which they made a partnership. Magen Avraham. And above siman 372, seif 2. And according to this, 7:2, and the Mishnah and Gemara there, and Tosefta chapter 6: partnership of an alleyway requires that the eruv chatzerot be with them in the alleyway; eruv chatzerot requires that it be with them in the courtyard, as stated in the Gemara chapter 5:2, and Rashi there.
