Forbidden. See Magen Avraham, and know that those courtyards that are forbidden to carry in — even in one house it is forbidden from room to room, as stated in siman 370, seif 2 and 3. And one must warn about this in places that have no lechi or beam. And according to our practice, since we place the eruv in the synagogue — if the rectification breaks in that alleyway, all the alleyways are forbidden even if they have their own separate rectification, unless they can bring the eruv to them. Therefore, in a place where there is no lechi or beam and they are forbidden to carry in the alleyway, and there are not two winter houses in one house — they should not make eruv chatzerot on Pesach, for it would be a blessing in vain. Magen Avraham, there.
