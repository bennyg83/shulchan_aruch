Semag and Semak who also wrote that one may not use beneath the beam unless it is four wide — and the Beit Yosef explained it deals with a public domain and nevertheless requires four wide. And they disagree with the Rashba who permits even to a public domain when not four wide. And on this Magen Avraham wrote: they too deal with karmelit, but to a public domain even when not four wide it is permitted.
