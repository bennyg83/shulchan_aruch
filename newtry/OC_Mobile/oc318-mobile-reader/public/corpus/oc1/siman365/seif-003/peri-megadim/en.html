And even, Taz. Eruvin 8:1, and the Rambam chapter 17 of laws of Shabbos 18, etc. And he asked: why did the Rambam not mention in chapter 17 of the alleyway the distinction regarding many people passing through? See letter alef — one cannot say it is not halachah, for from what he wrote that a courtyard through which many pass is a public domain — behold, in an alleyway many nullify by implication. And what he wrote regarding its length exceeding its width requires further study — for in siman 363, seif kaf-zayin, for leniency lechi helps. For stringency — perhaps in a courtyard where they do not leave so much room for many to walk — not like an alleyway. And many people passing through do not nullify. And some say even according to the Rambam in an alleyway thus. And see there — it is simple. And to me it requires further study. And it implies even directly open — permitted in the courtyard even though many pass through and they did not make an eruv with the alleyway residents — the courtyard residents may carry in the courtyard even when directly open and many pass through, and the alleyway residents who did not make an eruv with them do not restrict. And it implies even when breached a long time and the alleyway residents pass through the courtyard and have a hold in the courtyard to pass there — nevertheless one may say as Tosafos 7:2, s.v. de'i amar 7 wrote: likewise in reverse — the alleyway has a way to the public domain from another direction. And see there what the Rosh wrote that side-sections remained — meaning: even if they pass through the courtyard. And see there. And for what I wrote "if not" in Magen Avraham it will be further explained.
