And even if many people pass through it. Even though above regarding an alleyway many people passing through disqualifies, here regarding the courtyard we do not pay attention to many people passing through. And so too is the wording of the Rambam: a courtyard through which many people pass, entering through this breach and exiting through that breach — behold, it is a full public domain, end quote. And it is implied that regarding an alleyway the Rambam agrees. And it requires further study why he did not mention this distinction regarding an alleyway, as the Tur wrote. And this courtyard means a square one — for if its length exceeds its width, it is like an alleyway, as stated in siman 363.

On the courtyard's side. Such that if the alleyway wall were equal in length to the courtyard wall, it would appear like a long alleyway extending to the public domain opposite it and open, and forbidden — therefore the alleyway and the middle of the courtyard wall must be such.

And a courtyard belonging to an individual is forbidden. For sometimes the individual will take counsel and build houses in the width exceeding the alleyway and align the length of its walls with the length of the alleyway wall — automatically it becomes also on the courtyard's side as above.
