(There in Magen Avraham, seif kaf-tet.) And know that those courtyards, etc., even in one house — note: this means when it broke on a weekday. But if it broke on Shabbos, to carry in the house or on oneself it is permitted, for Shabbos — since it was permitted, it was permitted for this matter. And so he raised in Sefer Tosafot Shabbos. He further wrote there that on Shabbos it is permitted through a non-Jew, for it is better that one shvut violation be done than that Jews do many violations.
