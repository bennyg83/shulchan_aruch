[3] To one standing in the alleyway, etc. This requires study, because one standing in the alleyway can never see the projections, as stated by the Magen Avraham. The Perishah writes that he found a parchment version of the Tur reading, 'to one standing in the courtyard.'
[4] Visible not from that, etc. This applies where three handbreadths separate them, so we do not apply lavud, as stated in the Gemara on page 9.
[5] [Levush] For that case is different, etc. This must be said according to the Beit Yosef's reading of Rashi. According to Chochmat Shlomo and Tosafot, there is no difficulty at all, as written in siman 363, seif 13.
