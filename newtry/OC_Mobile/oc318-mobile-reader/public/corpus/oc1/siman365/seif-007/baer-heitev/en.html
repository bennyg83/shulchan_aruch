Surrounded. For it is like one courtyard, and when the rectification fell there is no interruption between them and the non-Jews, and it is like a courtyard that did not make an eruv, for we say on Shabbos: since it was permitted, it was permitted, for behold the outer partitions remain, as stated in siman 374, seif 2. And see in siman 363, seif kaf-vav, and see Magen Avraham.

Standing. Meaning: for example, in a place where non-Jews are common who spoil the eruv on that night.
