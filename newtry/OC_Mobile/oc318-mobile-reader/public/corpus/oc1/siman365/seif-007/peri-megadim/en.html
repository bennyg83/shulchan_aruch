Forbidden, Taz. The principle: whenever it was breached to another domain — from public domain to karmelit and the like — we do not say "since it was permitted, it was permitted." Not so when breached to its own domain — like courtyard to courtyard that did not make an eruv together and the like — we say "since it was permitted, it was permitted." And see letter chet. And "if not" in Magen Avraham letter 13 will be further explained.
