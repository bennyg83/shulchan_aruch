Surrounded by a wall is permitted. For it is like one courtyard, and when the rectification fell there is no interruption between them and the non-Jews, and it is like a courtyard that did not make an eruv, for we say on Shabbos: since it was permitted, it was permitted, for behold the outer partitions remain, as stated in siman 374, seif 2 (there). And see siman 363, seif kaf-vav. And the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the old Mordechai: if a partition between an alleyway and a courtyard was breached on Shabbos it is forbidden, since their usage is not the same, there. And see seif 3.

Standing to be demolished. Meaning: that it is nearly certain the non-Jews will break it.
