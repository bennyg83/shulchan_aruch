(31) Whose beams or lechis were removed — meaning: its lechi or beam, for an alleyway has only one beam and one lechi. And the same applies when tzurat hapetach was removed — according to our custom that we make tzurat hapetach.

(32) Forbidden from that point onward — to carry except within four amos. And likewise forbidden to take out from the house there. And we do not say here "Shabbos — since it was permitted, it was permitted" as in siman 340, seif 2, since the partitions are not present.

(33) Surrounded by a wall is permitted — for behold through the wall the entire city becomes a full public domain, and the alleyway's rectification was only to remove non-Jewish residents from upon them. Therefore even when the alleyway's rectification was nullified on Shabbos it is permitted — it is like two courtyards that did not make an eruv together and the partition between them fell, for we say "Shabbos — since it was permitted, it was permitted" because the outer partitions remain. And so too in this case. And the Taz wrote: specifically "it was settled and afterward enclosed" — as in ordinary towns of ours, for then it is a public domain. But if it was enclosed and afterward settled — it has the law of karmelit rabbinically, and they made rectification in the alleyway to permit carrying, and the rectification fell — it is forbidden, for behold it lacks a partition and is open to karmelit.

(34) Intended to be demolished — such as in places where non-Jews are common who spoil them on Shabbos eve, and it is nearly certain they will spoil it.

(35) And if there is doubt, etc. — this refers to when it is not intended to be demolished.

(36) We rule leniently — for we establish it in its presumed status and now it broke (Beit Yosef).
