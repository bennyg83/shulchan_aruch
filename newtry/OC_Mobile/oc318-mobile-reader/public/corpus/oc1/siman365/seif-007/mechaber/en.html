An alleyway whose beams or lechis fell on Shabbos — even though it was permitted for part of Shabbos, it is forbidden from that point onward, whether it opens to a public domain or a karmelit. Some say this applies only in a city not surrounded by a wall,' but in a walled city it is permitted.And one may rely on this to be lenient. All of this applies when it was fit to stand the entire Shabbos, but if it was already intended to be demolished from before Shabbos, it is forbidden after it collapses. If there is doubt whether it collapsed before Shabbos or not, we rule leniently. (Terumat HaDeshen, siman 73.).
