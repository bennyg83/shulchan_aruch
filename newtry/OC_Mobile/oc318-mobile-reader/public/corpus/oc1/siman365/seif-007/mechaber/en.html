An alleyway whose beams or lechis fell on Shabbat — even though it was permitted for part of Shabbat, it is forbidden from that point onward, whether it opens to a public domain or a karmelit. Some say this applies only in a city not surrounded by a wall, but in a walled city it remains permitted. {Rama: Rema: One may rely on this to be lenient. All of this applies when the arrangement was fit to stand the entire Shabbat, but if it was already intended to be demolished from before Shabbat, it is forbidden once it collapses. If there is doubt whether it collapsed before Shabbat or not, we rule leniently.}
