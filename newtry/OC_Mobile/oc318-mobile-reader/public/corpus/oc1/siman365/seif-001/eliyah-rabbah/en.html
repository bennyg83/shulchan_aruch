(1) (Levush) And the same applies if not, etc. So the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashi, and it requires study — for Tosafos, s.v. cholkim: since no post of four remained, it is like the beginning of an alleyway and requires a post of four amos. And so it appears from Rabbeinu Yehonatan and the Tur, and so ruled the Bach, Levushei Yom Tov, and Magen Avraham.
