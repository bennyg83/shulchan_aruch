(Seif kaf-alef) If, etc., for the beginning of an alleyway, etc. — meaning: for it is explained in seif kaf-bet that the reason we require that a post of four remain is lest they abandon, etc. But when a post of four remains it is called a beam across the alleyway.

That post of four handbreadths has by itself the law of an alleyway, for the measure of the continuation of an alleyway is four handbreadths. And if so, there is over the breach there an opening — for even if they abandon the alleyway opening, nevertheless over those four handbreadths there remains the name of alleyway since it has the measure of continuation of an alleyway. And the Gemara asks: but we hold the measure of continuation is four amos, as stated in siman 363, seif 33, that one must erect a post of four amos. And it answers: there — the beginning of an alleyway to make it now an alleyway requires four amos. But here — the end of an alleyway, meaning it already has the name of alleyway but was breached — if so, it suffices when only four handbreadths remain, for the name of alleyway still remains on it. And if so, if nothing remains but he now erects a post there, it is also the beginning of an alleyway and requires four amos.
