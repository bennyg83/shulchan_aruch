Laws of an Alleyway That Was Breached. Contianing 8 Sections An alleyway that was breached on its side toward its end — if a post four handbreadths wide remains at its end, it is permitted, provided the breach does not exceed ten handbreadths. If no four-handbreadth' post remains, it is forbidden unless the breach is less than three handbreadths.
