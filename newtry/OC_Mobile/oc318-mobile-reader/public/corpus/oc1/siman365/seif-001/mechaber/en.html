דין מבוי שנפרץ. ובו ח סעיפים:
The law of an alleyway that was breached. And in it are 8 seifim: An alleyway that was breached with a breach on its side toward its head — if a post four handbreadths wide remains standing at its head, it is permitted, provided the breach does not exceed ten handbreadths. If' no four-handbreadth post remains, it is forbidden unless the breach is less than three handbreadths.
