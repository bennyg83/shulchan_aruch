If something remains standing. But if nothing remains standing, one must erect a post of four amos, for the beginning of an alleyway requires four amos, as stated in siman 363, seif 13 (end quote).

Forbidden unless, etc. For even though less than ten amos is called an opening, here we are concerned lest the alleyway residents abandon the alleyway opening and go through this opening to shorten their way, and the rectification is nullified — for a beam is required across the alleyway and there is none. But when a post of four handbreadths remains, it is called a beam across the alleyway. And see what I wrote in siman 363, seif lamed-dalet.
