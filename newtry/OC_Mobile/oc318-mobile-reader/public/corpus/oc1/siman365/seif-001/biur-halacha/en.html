That was breached, etc. — to a karmelit or public domain (Gra and Beit Me'ir).

If something remains, etc. — see Magen Avraham who wrote that if nothing four handbreadths wide remains standing and one wishes to erect it, a post of four handbreadths does not help — for this is like the beginning of an alleyway and specifically a post of four amos is required. And behold, the plain view is like Tosafos and not like Rashi, because thus the wording of the Rambam and Tur instructs — they wrote "if something remained." And what the Avnei Nezer wrote that thus also appears from Rabbeinu Yehonatan is not clear — on the contrary, since he copied Rashi's wording it implies he holds like Rashi. Nevertheless, it is not entirely clear, for Or Zarua and also the Rashba in Avodat HaKodesh, gate 2, letter 1, and also Rabbeinu Yerucham copied it explicitly as the law like Rashi. And from the wording of the Rambam and Tur who wrote "if something remained" there is no proof that they intended this precisely — and know that the Beit Yosef copied Rashi's wording and in the Shulchan Aruch wrote "if something remained."

And if no post of four remained, etc. — see Mishna Berurah for the reason. And know that it is evident from the Gemara's flow and poskim that it is forbidden in this even if ridges obstructing passage remain — nevertheless we are concerned for this lest they shorten the walking path through this opening.

Forbidden unless, etc. — see Mishna Berurah. And it is explained in the novellae of the Rashba and Ritva that at the place of the breach the rectification of lechi and beam does not help when that rectification was not made for the breach. And if tzurat hapetach helps — it appears prima facie that it depends on the textual variant in the Shulchan Aruch siman 363, seif lamed-dalet at its end — for according to what we wrote stated in the name of Tiferet Shmuel, we read Rava: it does not help what one makes as tzurat hapetach at the place of the breach, lest they abandon, etc. (And what is explained in the novellae of the Rashba and Ritva that tzurat hapetach helps when breached at its head — perhaps that case is different, for "lest they abandon" is not applicable there as stated in the Gemara.) And it is possible that since one made tzurat hapetach it is considered closed and is as if there was a post of four at its head. And regarding whether tzurat hapetach helps when made at the alleyway opening — it depends on the dispute per the variant there — Rava: it helps (and the law there derives from Or Zarua). However, according to what the Beit Me'ir proved from the Ritva — not like Even HaEzer but the statement of R' Ami and Rav Asi (who forbid when breached on its side and no post of four remained) deals even when the alleyway opening was made as tzurat hapetach — if so it is proven that they hold it is forbidden in every case. Therefore this law depends on the dispute of the early authorities, and it requires further study for practice.
