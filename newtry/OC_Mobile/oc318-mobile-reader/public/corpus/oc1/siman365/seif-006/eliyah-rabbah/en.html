(6) Both beneath the beam, etc. So the Shulchan Aruch wrote because the Beit Yosef emended the Tur's words — but he holds the Rosh is like the Rambam who forbids even with a beam. But Levushei Yom Tov wrote that in chapter 1, siman 8, it appears he holds the Rosh permits with a beam — until here. And according to this, there is no need to emend the Tur, and so it appears to me as the main view — for I found in the Agur, siman 745, that he copied the Tur's words for halachah that he holds the Rosh permits. And in our version I also found in Rabbeinu Yerucham, student of the Rosh, daf 94. And Tziyun LaDerech ruled thus, and it is known that Tziyun LaDerech follows the Tur. And so it appears in Shiltai Gibborim. And what the Rosh wrote "and so it is reasonable" appears that he refers to rejecting the Rabbad's first proof and not to the main law — easy to understand. And since later authorities perhaps are lenient thus, we rule thus, especially since I explained that this is the view of the Rosh and Tur. And also since it is a d'rabbanan matter.
