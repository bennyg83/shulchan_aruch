In Magen Avraham, seif kaf-dalet: "and one should be stringent" — note: see in Even HaEzer on this, and his words require further study, as stated there in the margin. And see well the responsa of Chacham Tzvi, part 6, siman 82. And indeed in the responsa of Maharit, part 1, siman 94, it is obvious to him with clear proofs that our alleyways have the law of a courtyard to be lenient — see there. And we do not abandon the obviousness of Maharit because of the doubt of Magen Avraham — and had he seen it he would have retracted.
