In Magen Avraham, seif kaf-gimel: "on the post or if there are four handbreadths, etc." — so it should read. And for this the Tur and Rabbenu Yerucham intended, etc. — meaning they wrote simply "breached at its head" at four and did not write that specifically at a corner, as explained in the Gemara — for otherwise we say it is an opening. And so Tosafot proved from that which we say regarding an alleyway four amos wide making a post, as explained above siman 363. And the Beit Yosef wondered at the Tur on this — see Taz, seif kaf-alef. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote that the Tur and Rabbenu Yerucham deal with when the beam is placed on the post — therefore when there is a gap of four handbreadths from the wall it does not help, for a beam across the alleyway is required. Not so in the Gemara making a post — it deals with rectification by lechi at the post (and what he wrote "rectified by lechi at the side of the houses" is a scribal error — it should read "at the side of the posts" — see siman 363, seif kaf-lamed-vav, and Tosafos in Eruvin there). And with lechi it properly helps even though it is not adjacent to the alleyway itself, since it is at the place of the large opening to the public domain — not so with a beam, for we require it across the alleyway itself and this is distant four handbreadths from it. And on this he asked: if so, at a breach of three it would be forbidden since three is not lavud and then there is no beam across the alleyway. And he answered: he holds like that view in siman 363 that less than four requires no rectification — therefore whatever is not distant from the wall we view it as if closed there and it is as if the beam is placed across the alleyway. Not so when it is distant four handbreadths — therefore specifically when breached on its side we are concerned lest the alleyway residents abandon, etc., as stated in seif kaf-bet. But at its head, since they do not shorten their way, we are not concerned for this as explained in the Gemara and Tosafos — see above siman 363, seif kaf-chet. And that which Magen Avraham did not establish there also with beam rectification dealing with beam placed on alleyway walls from end to end — meaning: there it deals with an alleyway twenty amos wide and a beam from end to end does not help at all since it is wide — perforce when coming to reduce with posts, etc., and we count the beam's fitness from the posts onward — this is impossible since the post is more than four handbreadths distant from the alleyway wall — therefore Magen Avraham wrote it deals with lechi. And that which the Gemara answers on daf 5 "at a corner" and does not answer thus that the beam is placed on the post — because he holds that in every case it deals with this, for otherwise there is no need to distinguish between its side and its head — let it distinguish within its head itself between rectified by lechi or beam, and even with a beam if placed from end to end — nevertheless it forbids in every case, and this is at a corner — not so the Tur: one may say he relies on what is stated in siman 363 regarding rectification of posts learned from there that we do not care at its head less than ten at all — and perforce here with beam placed on the post. And nevertheless it requires further study on the Tur who omitted the law of a corner explained in the Gemara — see Beit Yosef who wrote on the Rosh that he relied on what they said in several places "an opening at a corner they do not make" — and this is strained even regarding the Rosh, and all the more regarding the Tur that it is not reasonable to say thus. And regarding emending Magen Avraham's words to read "rectified by lechi at the side of the posts" — prima facie one may say this emendation is unnecessary but according to their plain meaning: what does this lechi help adjacent to the alleyway wall since here is a small opening and the main lechi erection is at the large alleyway opening — even though lechi is for partition, nevertheless the main partition we need at the place of entry and exit from the alleyway to the public domain — and so it is stated explicitly: if they abandon the opening and enter through the small one, the lechi rectification made in the alleyway is nullified, as Rashi wrote there explicitly, and likewise above siman 363 — therefore the lechi must stand at the place of the large opening through which they exit from the alleyway to the public domain. And indeed in the Gemara dealing with an alleyway fifteen amos wide that distances two and makes a post of three and a half — one may say the lechi is erected at the second alleyway wall wider from the post ten amos. But the Rif, Rambam, Rosh, and all poskim wrote this rectification also for an alleyway twenty amos wide making two on both sides of the alleyway, as explained above siman 363 — if so perforce the lechi erection is not adjacent to the alleyway walls themselves but at the side of the posts — meaning: either at this post or at the second post, the place where there is the large opening ten amos wide. And Magen Avraham hinted to siman 363 — therefore he wrote rectified by lechi at the side of the posts. And see in Sefer Tosafot Shabbos who wrote what Magen Avraham wrote: lechi at the side of the houses — I do not know why it would be permitted even adjacent to the closure, there. And in my humble opinion adjacent to the closure at the place of distancing is obviously not helpful, and also adjacent to the wall is not helpful as stated, since the main alleyway opening is elsewhere — only that it should be erected adjacent to the post at the place of the opening there. And if so, it must be emended "at the side of the posts" as stated. And see Eliyah Rabbah siman 363, seif mem-bet in the name of the Rashba in Avodat HaKodesh who wrote: lechi or beam permits it and the air from the posts to the walls requires nothing, for behold it is permitted through standing exceeding breach, end quote — explained as stated that the lechi stands at the post at the place which is the wide opening of ten. And nevertheless from the Rashba's words it appears not like Magen Avraham's words regarding the Tur and Rabbenu Yerucham — from their words it implies even the beam on the post is fine since it is considered as if it is across the alleyway itself because the air between the post and wall is permitted through standing exceeding breach — there.
