Pass through. Meaning: a filthy place or a place spoiled with mud, and the public does not pass through that partition — Rashi. And all the more so if ridges remain that obstruct passage — we say in the Gemara on daf 5 that a courtyard whose breach is ten amos, as stated in seif 3. And it requires further study regarding our alleyways whether they have the law of a courtyard for this matter. And the same applies in a walled city that was breached with a breach of four handbreadths and many people pass through it — according to our ruling that it has the law of a courtyard, it requires further study whether it would be permitted, and one should be stringent. Magen Avraham. And see further in Magen Avraham in siman 363, seif kaf-vav, at length.
