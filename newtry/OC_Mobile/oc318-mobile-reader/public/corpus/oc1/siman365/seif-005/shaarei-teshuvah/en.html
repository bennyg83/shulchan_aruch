Forbidden. See Ba'er Heitev. And see below siman 366, seif 10, who also wrote in the name of the Taz. And in Even HaEzer he sided to permit if nothing separates them from the eruv except karmelit — for at twilight it is fit to bring the eruv to them. And in the responsa of Noda B'Yehuda, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 10, he wrote that it does not apply — for twilight does not help to acquire an eruv except when the entire Shabbos it is as if they dwell there and it is designated for that house. And since the entire Shabbos the domain where the eruv rests is separate from that house, and also the beginning of the eruv is so — twilight acquiring an eruv is not applicable. And also Even HaEzer did not decide this and left it requiring further study. And see there siman 39: in a city where the synagogue was sealed by the government minister for the king's tax and the eruv is placed there — one should be stringent. For although regarding the prohibition of government seals on land one may say it is not d'oraisa, and if so the eruv is fit at twilight (and although he himself wrote that twilight does not help — here is different: if he would act at twilight to nullify the seal it would be designated for the entire Shabbos and is comparable to siman 394 regarding his shoe, there) — nevertheless, since no hand will touch it to open the seal of the king's agents, and one may say to permit because it is in their power to pay the tax — therefore lest they say "since money is lacking we do not say since it was permitted" — let them make another eruv, there. And see in the responsa of Chacham Tzvi, siman 111, who also wrote regarding an open city where they are forbidden to carry in the city — even adjacent houses with an opening between them may not carry based on the eruv in the synagogue; they also may not recite the blessing on the eruv in the synagogue since it permits nothing. And even if two houses open to the courtyard of the synagogue — since they are forbidden to carry in the alleyway before the synagogue courtyard, there is no law of partnership to place it in the synagogue, as the Rama wrote in siman 366 regarding the reason for the custom — only the law of eruv that must be placed in a dwelling, and it would be like placing it in a gatehouse, etc., and it is not an eruv. And the open houses are forbidden to carry in the synagogue, and one who recites the blessing on this eruv recites a blessing in vain. And see in Even HaEzer who wrote the reason we place the eruv in the synagogue: it must be done lest they be forbidden to carry from the synagogue since they acquire places there and it is part of their property, there — several novel laws.
