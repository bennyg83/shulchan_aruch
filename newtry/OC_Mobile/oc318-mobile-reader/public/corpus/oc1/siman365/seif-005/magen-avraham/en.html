One should not sit at the head of the alleyway. And the same applies that one should not sit at the lechi (Gemara). And it is possible that when there is a tzurat hapetach it is permitted, for it is called a recognizable distinction. However, in our tzurat hapetach, which is very high, it is forbidden, for there is no recognizable distinction. And even though we have no public domain, nevertheless it is forbidden, as the Rosh wrote regarding a courtyard.
