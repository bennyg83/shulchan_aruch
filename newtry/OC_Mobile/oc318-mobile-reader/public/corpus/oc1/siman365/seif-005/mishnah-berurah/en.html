(28) Although one may use, etc. — meaning: specifically at the side of the beam and when needed one may carry one's objects there. But one should not sit there permanently with an object in hand, etc. (Mordechai).

(29) Beneath the beam — and the same applies that one should not sit at the lechi (Gemara). And it is possible that when there is tzurat hapetach it is permitted, for it is called a recognizable distinction. However, in our tzurat hapetach, which is very high, it is certainly forbidden, for there is no recognizable distinction. And even if the alleyway opens to a karmelit — which is only a rabbinic concern — it is also forbidden because it is common to ask (Magen Avraham and Maggid Mishneh).
