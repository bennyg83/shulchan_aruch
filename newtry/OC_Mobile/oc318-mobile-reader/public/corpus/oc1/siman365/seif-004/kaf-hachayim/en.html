(d) There: if a post four handbreadths wide remains standing at its head — it is permitted. For it is the measure of the length required for an alleyway — the large opening is not nullified. And even though the measure of the length of an alleyway is four amos — that is when an alleyway is first made. But here it was already an alleyway and merely became closed; four handbreadths in what remains suffices. Taz there. But if nothing remains standing, one must erect a post of four amos, for the beginning of an alleyway requires four amos, as stated in siman 363, seif 13. Tosafot, Magen Avraham, seif kaf-alef. Turei Zahav, part 2. And he wrote: and not like Rashi's explanation (who holds that even at the beginning of an alleyway four handbreadths suffice, and it is brought in the Beit Yosef). And what the Levush followed from Rashi's explanation is not correct. And so in Avnei Nezer, part 1, letter alef, chapter 1, letter chet, he wrote it requires further study on the Levush and brought several explanations who hold like Tosafot — see there. And so too the Rabbenu Zalman, part 1, chapter 1, letter alef, letter chet.
