Seif 4 — opposite the lechi. The view of Tosafos in the sugya: if the lechi is four wide, even when open to a public domain it is forbidden to use opposite it. And the Ramban in Milchamot wrote to permit, and Taz agreed with this. He wrapped thin lechis less than three, and when open to a karmelit one may use up to the inner edge of the innermost one. And see Magen Avraham above, siman 363, seif 112.
