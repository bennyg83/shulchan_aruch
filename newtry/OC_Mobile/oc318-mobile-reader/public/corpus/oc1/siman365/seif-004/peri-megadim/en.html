And the courtyard, Taz. The general principle: a courtyard is always permitted even when directly open and many pass through and they did not make an eruv with the alleyway residents — the courtyard residents may carry in the courtyard, for direct openness and many passing through do not harm a courtyard. And the alleyway residents who did not make an eruv with them do not restrict. And it implies even when breached a long time and the alleyway residents pass through the courtyard and have a hold in the courtyard to pass there — nevertheless one may say as Tosafos wrote: likewise in reverse. And see there what the Rosh wrote. And for what I wrote in Magen Avraham and "if not" it will be further explained.
