(25) And opposite the lechi — meaning: whether the alleyway's rectification is with a beam or lechi, one may use opposite them. However, some poskim hold specifically when this place opposite the lechi did not have the measure of four by four handbreadths — then it is nullified relative to the interior and one may use beneath it and likewise bring in from there inward. But when this area has four by four, and all the more so more — this place is considered karmelit regarding that it is forbidden to carry in it except within four amos, and likewise to take out from it to the public domain or bring in to a private domain is forbidden. And some poskim do not distinguish in this and hold that even when it has four by four it is also nullified relative to the interior.

(26) But when open to a karmelit, etc. — even though generally karmelit is lighter than a public domain, nevertheless for this matter we say: like finds like and is stirred — meaning: the karmelit between the lechis, even if originally it did not have karmelit measure since there were not four by four handbreadths, finds its kind outside it and is stirred from its sleep and strengthened to combine with the karmelit at its side — and there karmelit applies to it and it is forbidden to use beneath it and also to bring it inward. Not so when open to a public domain which is not its kind at all — it does not combine with it and is nullified relative to the interior.

(27) Both beneath the beam — and in Sefer Avnei Nezer he sided to be lenient to use beneath the beam even when open to a karmelit. And so ruled in Sefer Even HaEzer. And especially if the beam is four handbreadths wide and fit to receive plaster — certainly it is permitted even when open to a karmelit, for the opening of the ceiling descends and closes and is considered a partition.
