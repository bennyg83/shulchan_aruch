"Divine Name and kingship." The Amidah does not include an explicit formula of kingship in each blessing. Beit Yosef answers in the name of the Rosh that the first blessing, because it contains the words "the great God," is regarded as containing kingship, and all the subsequent blessings follow the first. Rambam writes in chapter 11 of Hilchot Berachos that one who performs a mitzvah without first reciting its blessing acts as follows. If the mitzvah is an ongoing one, such as tzitzit, tefillin, or sukkah, he recites the blessing afterward while the mitzvah is still being performed. But if he slaughtered, covered the blood, performed a brit milah, or completed another comparable act without a blessing, he does not return and recite the blessing after the act has ended. See Magen Avraham. See also Even HaOzer, who questions the Shulchan Aruch's ruling that if one omitted the word "ha'olam" he must repeat the blessing.
