Name and malchut. And that in the prayer of eighteen they did not enact malchut — Beit Yosef answers in the name of the Rosh that the first berachah, since it contains "the great God," is considered like malchut, and all the later ones are dragged along after the first. Rambam wrote chapter 11 of Berachot: one who performs a mitzvah and did not bless — if it is a mitzvah whose performance still persists, such as tzitzit, tefillin, and sukkah, he blesses after he wrapped himself; but if he slaughtered or covered or circumcised without a blessing, he does not return and bless after the act; and so with everything similar — see Magen Avraham. (And see in Sefer Even HaEzer who wondered on Shulchan Aruch's ruling that if he skipped the word haOlam he must return and bless.)
