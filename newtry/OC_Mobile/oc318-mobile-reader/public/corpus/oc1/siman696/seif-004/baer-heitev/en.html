To hear. This requires study, for the Beit Yosef ruled in seif 5 that he should not leave the doorway of his house. If so, he should have written this in the language of "there are those who say," etc. Magen Avraham.
Mourning. The Bach, Maharshal, and Prishah testified that the custom is to observe mourning, and so wrote Maharil. Now the world follows the words of the Rama. It appears to me that one should be careful regarding all of them, except for wearing shoes and sitting on the ground, because of people; and all the more so it is forbidden to see all kinds of joy. Magen Avraham.
In private. He should pray in his house and afterward go to the synagogue to hear the Megillah, Tur, and so it appears in the Levush. However, it implies in the Tur that in the morning he goes to the synagogue for prayer; see Yoreh Deah, siman 393, Magen Avraham. The Taz wrote: it appears to me that since there is no public mourning on Purim, one should not call out in the synagogue on Friday evening before Barchu to go toward the mourner, for one should not mention a mourner there in public; rather, he should go to the synagogue without calling.
