Note: In the sefer Chemed Moshe he challenged the Rashba, that it should be permitted to marry on the Yom Tov of Atzeres, since it is one day. He forgot that one does not marry women on Yom Tov, for it is like acquiring an acquisition, as above in siman 339, and in Magen Avraham there, s.k. 5, and in Shulchan Aruch, Even HaEzer, siman 64. The Talmud in Moed Katan refers to Chol HaMoed and not to Yom Tov itself, and this is obvious.
