(א) Nevertheless he goes to synagogue. It requires study, for Rabi ruled in Sefer HaZekhirim that he not leave his house — if so he should have written in the language of "some say" etc.

(ב) And some say there is no mourning etc. Bach and Rashal and Perishah testified the custom is to mourn; and so Maharil; and now the world follows Rama's words; it appears to me one should be careful in all except removing shoes and sitting on the ground because of people, and all the more forbidden to see all kinds of joy.

(ג) Private matters etc. He prays in his house and afterward goes to synagogue to hear Megillah (Tur and so in Levush); however it implies in Tur that in the morning he goes to synagogue for prayer — see Yoreh Deah siman 393.
