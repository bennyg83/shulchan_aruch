Permitted Orach Chayyim on Yom Tov one learns "in your festival" and not "with your wife" — unlike Purim; Magen Avraham letter 18 agreed one may not marry on Purim except canopy on the 13th and feast permitted at night — see there. It implies 14th for walled cities and 15th for villages; perhaps Magen Avraham would concede permitted as siman 695 seif 2 in Hagahah and Orach Chayyim on 14th and 15th one may not marry in name of Mordechai and Mahari Weil — see there.
