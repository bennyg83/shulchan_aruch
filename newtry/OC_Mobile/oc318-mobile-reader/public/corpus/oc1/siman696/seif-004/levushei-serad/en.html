Before Barchu, for Shabbat sanctity is still upon him; but after Barchu he returns home — unlike here, since on Purim in Rama's words, if he went before Barchu he will be there after prayer and Megillah as if he were in the synagogue from the outset; unlike on a weekday he must pray Maariv at home and afterward go to synagogue as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 8.
