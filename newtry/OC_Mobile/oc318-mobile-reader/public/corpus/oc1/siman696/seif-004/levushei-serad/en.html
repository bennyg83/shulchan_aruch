S.k. 10. To go to the synagogue. Before Barchu, because the sanctity of Shabbos is still upon him, except that after Barchu he returns to his house. This is not so here, since Purim is on Motzei Shabbos; if he went before Barchu, he will remain there after the prayer and the Megillah, just as if he had been in the synagogue while it was still day. This is not so on a weekday, when he must pray Maariv in his house and afterward go to the synagogue, as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 8.
