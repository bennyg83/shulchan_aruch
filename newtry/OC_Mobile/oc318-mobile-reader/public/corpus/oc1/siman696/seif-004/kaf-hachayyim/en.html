4) One who had been building his house through a non-Jew before Purim should suspend the work on Purim, because the matter is publicly noticeable. Beit David, siman 495; Birkei Yosef, os 3; Erekh HaShulchan there; Shaarei Teshuvah, os 1. In Chikrei Lev, volume 2, Yoreh De'ah, in the addenda to Orach Chayyim, siman 20, he permitted building a store on the 14th and 15th, and he acted accordingly; see there. Construction of a synagogue and another mitzvah matter is permitted. Ruach Chaim, section 1, os 10, seif 1, and in his sefer Moed L'Chol Chai, siman 31, os 31.
