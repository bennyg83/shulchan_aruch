In Shulchan Aruch, seif 1. Not to do work, etc. Behold, in Hagahos Maimoniyos, in the name of the Sheiltos, he wrote that one who does work is excommunicated, from the incident where Rav cursed that person who planted flax. So too wrote the Razah, and so wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chayyim. It appears that the other poskim disagree. See Pri Chadash, siman 496, seif 2.
