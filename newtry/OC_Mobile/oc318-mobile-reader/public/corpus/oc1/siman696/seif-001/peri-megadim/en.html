And there he rejects that it deals with villages — see there. And see siman 568 regarding erev Purim: at night until sunrise it is permitted even if they practiced not to do, and it appears all the more Purim; and the same if there is loss in the matter it is permitted, for anything of joy is permitted on Purim; and in Eshel Avraham he brought Ran's words in Megillah: it was writing Yerushalmi teaches Purim is permitted in melacha on the 14th in walled cities; and Ran z"l explained except the 15th on Shabbat and they read on the 14th etc. — all the more villages that advance entry are permitted in melacha on the day they read; in Magen Avraham we wrote from this in letter 2 — this is reasonable for villages. And Yerushalmi holds one should not distinguish between villages and cities; and it appears he holds not in its time for villages requires ten for delay — how is it relevant he will prepare ten and not intend, unlike cities on the 14th one could say it was its time and need not interpret thus.
