The laws of eulogy, fasting, and doing work on Purim. It contains eight seifim:
On Purim, it is permitted to do work. In a place where they are accustomed not to do work, one does not do work. And nowadays the custom in every place is not to do work, as the Kol Bo writes. One who does work never sees a sign of blessing from that work, unless it is a building of joy, such as a wedding house for his son, or a royal arbor, which is permitted. Gloss: It is permitted to do any mitzvah labor, such as writing rulings of halachos. It is also permitted to do even full labors for the needs of Purim, as in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 112.
