In every place. See the beginning of siman 468, that through a gentile it is permitted, and see siman 554, seif 22.
He does not see. See the Rambam, from whom it implies that this applies even in a place where they are accustomed to do work. So too wrote the Re'em in his name in his explanations to the Sefer Mitzvos Gadol. But in the Beis Yosef it implies that this is specifically in a place where they are accustomed not to do work.
A sign of blessing. For example, if he sows something, it does not sprout, as the Beis Yosef writes. But the Re'em wrote that he does not profit, though he also does not lose; and it implies there that he had a different text in the Gemara.
Rulings of halachos. And the plain meaning of verses, a letter of greeting, a memorandum of his debts, and similarly anything that does not require great analysis, as cited by Mateh Moshe, Beis Yosef, and Orach Chayim.
