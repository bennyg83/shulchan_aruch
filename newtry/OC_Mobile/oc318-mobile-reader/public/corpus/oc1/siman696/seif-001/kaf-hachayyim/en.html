1) [Seif 1] It is permitted to perform melacha on Purim. Although it is written, 'days of feasting and gladness and a festival,' they did not accept upon themselves a prohibition against melacha. At first it is written, 'gladness and feasting and a festival,' but afterward it is written, 'to make them days of feasting and gladness,' while 'festival' is not written. Nevertheless, there are places where the custom is to prohibit the performance of melacha. Megillah 5b; Beit Yosef; Levush.
