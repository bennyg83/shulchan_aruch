(טו) He does not leave from there etc. — this law Rokeach wrote, and in his place they would eat the third meal before Minchah; therefore he says when he comes to pray Minchah he does not leave from there; but for us he must go home after Minchah to eat the third meal, and at night pray Maariv and read Megillah at home if he has a minyan; and if not, he goes to synagogue while it is still day and is there for prayer and Megillah reading.

(טז) From his house's door — only to hear Megillah reading if he has no minyan at home, as above. Behold the Mechaber who copied Rokeach's words follows his view b'siyata dishmaya that mourning applies on Purim; but Rama already wrote above that we practice like poskim that mourning does not apply on Purim; per this, even if he has a minyan at home he goes to synagogue to hear Megillah in public [and to leave his house's door for discretionary matters — it implies from Peri Megadim one should be stringent]; all this by day, for "days of feasting and joy" is written; but at night if he has a minyan he prays and reads Megillah at home, and if not he prays at home and goes to hear Megillah in synagogue, and for reading alone he does not leave his house's door. See Darkei Chayyim that on day 15 he does not go from house to synagogue even for Shacharit prayer; nevertheless regarding removing shoes and sitting on ground it implies from poskim that even on day 15 it is not required. Levush wrote: Orach Chayyim justified the law and does not say Kaddish except before a sage in his presence after they eulogize him; and even though Orach Chayyim 395 they say Kaddish.
