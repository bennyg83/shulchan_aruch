5) Ibid., Rama's gloss. Nowadays the custom everywhere is not to perform melacha. It appears that commerce is permitted, since it brings him joy. Taz, s.k. 1. So too wrote Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos Beit Yosef, os 5. However, he wrote that although we reason by analogy, we should not act upon it to permit commerce on Purim in a place where the custom is not to perform melacha. Yet if one transgressed and did so, beit din does not penalize him, unless it is necessary to establish a safeguard so that people not become lax in performing melacha. All of this depends upon the judgment of beit din; see there. So too wrote Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, os 3. It appears that if beit din sent him a warning not to conduct business and he disregarded it, beit din may place him under a ban. Ruach Chaim, os 2.
