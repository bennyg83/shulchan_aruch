[5] [Levush] They pray in their houses etc. — it appears to me from Rama: since mourning doesn't apply, permitted to go to synagogue even for prayer; Matot Moshe ruled only at night before days of drinking and joy entered, go after prayer; by day permitted for prayer — Tur seems so, unlike Hagahot Maimoniot understood; perhaps Levush distinguishes since Rama questioned Shulchan Aruch words here unlike siman 101 where ruled not practiced — therefore distinguishes: matters of joy like drinking joyfully and not sitting on ground don't apply, but not leaving house applies except for Megillah; perhaps same for removing shoes entering house — I explained in my Yoreh Deah book Rosh's language in Bava Kamma; should rule Shach testified in name of R' Shlomo Luria and Bach they received all mourning practices. I wondered there on great acharonim who challenged Maharam of Rothenburg contradicting himself — see there; found in Maimonides responsum end Shoftim he brought both and implies for ruling no mourning, for custom he put on shoes for Megillah then returned to mourning and removed shoes; also possible Shulchan Aruch wrote in Yoreh Deah for law and for custom; shoe removal perhaps may lighten — Magen Avraham wrote forbidden to see all kinds of joy — till here; not necessary days of joy are — see s.k. 8.
