It is stated in the Maharil that when someone was a mourner during the year, he went on Motzaei Shabbos to the beis haknesses and stood at the entrance of the beis haknesses until they said Barechu, and afterward returned to his house. This shows that it is permitted to go to the beis haknesses, all the more so on Purim, since some say that mourning does not apply. The Levush wrote that one does not recite Tzidduk HaDin except for a sage in his presence; after they expound about him, they say Kaddish.
