To marry. According to what is written at the beginning of siman 546, that we rule like the opinion that one does not mix one joy with another joy, here too it is forbidden to do so. Therefore he should make the chuppah on the thirteenth. Magen Avraham, see there. See Sefer Gufei Halachos, and what I wrote in Even HaEzer, siman 62, s.k. 10.
You shall not steal. According to this, it is permitted to recite a blessing on what they snatch from one another. But the Shelah wrote that this is not the teaching of the pious, and one who guards his soul should distance himself from garments of kilayim and from snatching from his fellow. See Taz and Bach in Yoreh Deah, siman 182, who are stringent about this at length. So too the Knesses HaGedolah ruled to forbid masks on Purim; see there. See Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 247. Therefore, one who fears the word of Hashem should distance his sons and members of his household from all such prohibition and looseness. See further in Devar Shmuel, siman 153, an open rebuke regarding those who study the book Maseches Shikorim; see there.
