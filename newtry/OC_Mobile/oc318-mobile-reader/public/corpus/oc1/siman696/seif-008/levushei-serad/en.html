Magen Avraham, s.k. 13. As is written in siman 548, seif 3, that an individual positive commandment according to the Torah, namely mourning on the first day, overrides a public positive commandment that is rabbinic, namely the joy of the second day of Yom Tov. If so, the same applies here: aninus, to prohibit meat and wine, is rabbinic, and is deferred before Purim, which is a positive commandment according to the Torah; all the more so since it is also a public positive commandment. Consequently, he did not need to give the reason that mourning is an individual positive commandment.
