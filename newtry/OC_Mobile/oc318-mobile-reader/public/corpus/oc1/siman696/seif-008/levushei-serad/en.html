Magen Avraham s.k. 13 as written siman 688 seif 3. For individual d'oraisa positive mitzvah, namely mourning day one, overrides communal d'rabbanan positive mitzvah, namely joy of two festival days — if so, here too aninut to forbid meat and wine is d'rabbanan and is deferred before Purim which is d'oraisa positive mitzvah; and all the more since it is also communal positive mitzvah — therefore one need not give the reason that mourning is individual positive mitzvah.
