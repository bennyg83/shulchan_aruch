(8) It appears to me that even for a Jew they fine only one who does melacha b'mezid; but b'shogeg when he thought melacha was permitted they do not punish him, for shogeg is not like mezid. Knesset HaGedolah there s.k. 3. And if he is b'mezid, even though they do not put him in nidui since it is doubtful in the dispute of poskim — nevertheless they may fine him, for all he profited from that melacha he gives to the poor, all per the judge's view. Rokeach s.k. 3. And so in his book Malach siman 31 s.k. 34.
