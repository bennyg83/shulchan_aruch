One may marry a woman on Purim, {Rama: Rema: both on the fourteenth and on the fifteenth. All the more so, one may redeem one's firstborn (Tosfot to ch. 1 of Moed Qatan). The custom of wearing masks on Purim, and of crossdressing, is totally permitted because of its innocent and joyful purpose, as is the wearing of shaatnez. While some would prohibit it, our practice is as I have already said. So too those who playfully rob each other do not violate "Thou shalt not steal'', and such is our custom. However, you may do only as local elders permit. (Responsum of Mahari Minz #17).}
