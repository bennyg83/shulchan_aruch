Permitted to marry. On a festival it is written "and you shall rejoice in your festival" and not "with your wife," and not on Purim; and further it is one day, and because of one day a person does not delay himself, and as those who say because of cancellation of procreation (Beit Yosef in name of Rashba); superficially it implies here that granted on a festival we decree lest he delay until the festival to become intoxicated and the seven days of feasting come out on the seven days of the festival — but on Purim because of one day he will not delay — requires study, for we say in gemara it is permitted to marry on erev the festival, for because of one day a person does not delay himself (Rashi and Ran: since if he delays further he cannot do on the festival) — if so he should not set up the marriage on erev the festival, and this is not relevant regarding Purim; and it is possible Rashba's reasoning is he speaks of his own soul alone, only he seized the gemara's language; nevertheless it is difficult from where he learned this, and it is possible his proof is from that it is permitted to marry on erev Shavuot and we are not concerned he will delay the marriage until after Shavuot, for it is only one day — rather per force since it is only one day he is not concerned to delay, for in any case he must do seven days of feasting. Levush wrote per what I wrote siman 688 seif 6 one should not make the Purim feast on Shabbat — if so here too forbidden to make a wedding feast on the 14th per force; in my humble opinion it is not comparable, for there the reason is if so the Purim feast would not be recognizable; but here what matter if the wedding feast is not recognizable; see what I wrote there; but if it is difficult, this is difficult, for we hold one may not mix joy with joy as I wrote beginning siman 696 and we derive from scripture — if so how permitted to mix wedding joy with Purim joy? One could say: therefore Rashba concluded these matters are d'rabbanan and we follow leniency — meaning for those who say one may not mix, in truth it is forbidden; but since for the other view it is permitted, we follow leniency; if so per what I wrote beginning siman 696 we hold like those who say one may not mix joy with joy — here too forbidden; therefore it appears he should make the canopy on the 13th as above.
