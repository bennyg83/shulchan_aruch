Portions. Even within the seven days; however, one should not send delicacies that are made for joy. Sefer Chasidim 713. Mateh Moshe wrote that one does not send to a mourner even something that is not for joy, and so it appears from the words of the Rama. In Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah he wrote that a mourner is exempt from mishloach manos within the seven days, and after the seven days he should send privately to one of his relatives or to one of the poor. It is obvious that if the mourner is poor, it is permitted to send him money, for it is no worse than tzedakah. Magen Avraham, see there.
