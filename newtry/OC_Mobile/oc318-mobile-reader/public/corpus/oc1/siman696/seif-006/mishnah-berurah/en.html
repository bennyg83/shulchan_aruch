Is obligated to send portions. However, he should not send anything joyous. This is the language of Sefer Chasidim: one whose father died on Purim, in the following year he sends portions, even though it is the yahrzeit, and in that year he sends money or meat to the poor, only not delicacies made for joy.
But one does not send to a mourner. Even something that is not of joy. If the mourner is poor, it is permitted to send him money even within the seven days, for this is no worse than tzedakah.
