[6] He should not go out at Minchah, etc. Maharil wrote that when he was a mourner during the year, he went on Motzei Shabbos and stood at the entrance of the synagogue until they said Barchu, and afterward returned to his house, end quote. If so, on Purim all the more so it is permitted to go for Barchu and to remain in the synagogue until after the Megillah. Magen Avraham. Regarding what Magen Avraham asked in s.k. 6 against the Rama cited above, that he should have written it in the language of "there are those who say," it appears to me that he holds that what is written in the Shulchan Aruch, that he should not leave the doorway of his house, refers to when he has a minyan in his house; that is why he did not conclude that he reads by himself, as the Rokeach wrote in the Beit Yosef.
