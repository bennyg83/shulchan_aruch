There is one who says that a mourner is obligated to send portions. Gloss: But one does not send to a mourner throughout all twelve months, as the Maharil writes, and as will be explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 385; see there. If there is no one in the city except the mourner and one other person, that other person is obligated to send to the mourner in order to fulfill mishloach manos, unless the mourner waives his portion, as the Mahari Beirav writes.
