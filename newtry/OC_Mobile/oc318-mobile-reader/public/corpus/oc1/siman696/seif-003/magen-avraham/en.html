Forbidden in eulogy. A place where the custom is that on the seventh day they go to the grave to lament — forbidden on Purim except the mourner alone goes there with one chazan and says hashkavah to him; even on erev Purim it is forbidden to go there lest people think mourning ceased because of Purim (R' Shimon Levi 13, Knesset HaGedolah).
