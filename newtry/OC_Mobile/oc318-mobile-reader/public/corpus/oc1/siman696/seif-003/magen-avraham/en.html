They are forbidden in eulogy. In a place where the custom is that on the seventh day they go to the grave to lament, they are forbidden to do so on Purim. Rather, the mourner alone should go there with one chazzan, who should recite the Hashkavah for him. Even on Erev Purim it is forbidden to go there, so people should not think that the mourning was interrupted because of Purim, as in Rav Shmuel Levi, 13:25.
