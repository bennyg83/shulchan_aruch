In eulogy. In a place where the custom is that on the seventh day they go to the grave to lament, it is forbidden on Purim. Rather, the mourner alone should go there with one chazzan, who should recite Hashkavah for him. Even on Erev Purim he should not go there, so people should not think that the mourning was interrupted on Purim.
