S.k. 9. As is written in siman 548, seif 2, regarding the last day of Yom Tov, that it counts toward the mourning count because it is not Yom Tov except by rabbinic enactment, all the more so Purim is deferred according to the opinion that mourning is observed. Therefore, even according to us, who hold that it is not observed, it counts in any event.
