And as written in Yoreh De'ah — see Taz; and Rama wrote some are lenient nowadays regarding offering greeting; and Taz holds that is the custom; and in truth nowadays they offer greeting to a mourner for a father or mother within thirty days, and even per Beit Yosef there; and there is no reason for this unless we say this is not the greeting forbidden to a mourner — see there in Eshel Avraham.
