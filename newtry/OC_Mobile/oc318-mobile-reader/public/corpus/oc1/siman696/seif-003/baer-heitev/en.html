In eulogy. A place where the custom is that on the seventh day they go to the grave to lament — it is forbidden on Purim except the mourner alone goes there with one chazan and he says hashkavah to him. Even on erev Purim it is forbidden to go there lest people think mourning ceased because of Purim. R' Shimon Halevi siman 13, Knesset HaGedolah.
