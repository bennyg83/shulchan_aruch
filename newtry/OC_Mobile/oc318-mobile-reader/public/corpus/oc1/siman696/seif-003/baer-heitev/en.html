In eulogizing. In a place where the custom is that on the seventh day they go to the grave to lament, it is forbidden on Purim; rather, the mourner alone should go there with one chazzan, who should recite Hashkavah for him. Even on Erev Purim it is forbidden to go there, so that people not think that the mourning was interrupted because of Purim. R. Shimon Halevi, siman 13; Knesses HaGedolah.
