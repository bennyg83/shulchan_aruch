[7] That the mourner, etc. In the Beit Yosef he wrote this in the name of the Rosh and the Mordechai. In the sefer Beis Hillel to Yoreh Deah, siman 400, he was not precise; see there. One whose father died on Purim, in the following year he should not neglect mishloach manos. In that year, on that same Purim, he sends meat or money to the poor, but not kinds of delicacies or any joyful item, end quote. It appears to me that he holds that he is obligated specifically in gifts to the poor, but not in sending portions to the wealthy, which is not done with money, as above. So too wrote the Riaz in Shiltei Giborim, folio 398, and he concluded: nevertheless, after the seven days he sends privately to one of his relatives.
