(א) Do a single. And he said, “I will give thanks to him.”

(ב) And all the more he is obligated. Rama's words require great study: granted in meat and wine it is permitted because of Purim joy; but Shema and prayer — the reason he is exempt is he should be free for the deceased's needs; if so what is different about Purim from weekdays? And even if one says he holds that at night he is obligated in Shema since his law is like second day of Yom Tov as written siman 688 seif 5 — nevertheless it is difficult how we learn this in what he wrote in meat and wine, what relevance does this have to that — requires study.

(ג) Specifically at night. It requires study why he should be permitted at night in meat and wine, for behold there is no obligation at all to eat at night as Rashi wrote siman 695; if so how did he write communal positive mitzvah etc.; and it is puzzling on the 14th siman 341 he ruled that even on Shabbat if he wishes not to eat meat the permission is in his hand, as Terumat HaDeshen wrote: he is not obligated to eat meat and wine etc.; what they forbade on a weekday is lest he continue after his pleasures and be nullified from burying the deceased — end of his words (and see siman 552 seif 10 that when prevented because of mourning it is forbidden); if so there is no positive mitzvah at all even by day; and it is possible to say Purim is different since it is written therein joy, and there is no joy except with meat — nevertheless it is difficult, for Yom Tov too joy is written therein and there is no obligation to eat meat except when the Temple stood — see siman 529; therefore it appears to me at night in any case he should not eat meat, for in any case there is prevention as written siman 695 seif 2 in Hagahah; and what he wrote "it appears to me specifically at night" refers to Megillah.
