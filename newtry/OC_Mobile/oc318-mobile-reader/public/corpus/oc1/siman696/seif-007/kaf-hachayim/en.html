He does not see a sign of blessing etc. — it implies a sign of blessing is what is not fitting; behold we call him and do not put him in cherem for him; and so is the view of most poskim; but Rav Acha Gaon in Sheiltot — Hagahot Maimoniot and Razah in Sefer HaMaor and in Orach Chayyim wrote we put him in cherem. Birkei Yosef s.k. 5. Orach Chayyim s.k. 2. For the matter of practice he agreed in Knesset HaGedolah (in Hagahot Beit Yosef end s.k. 2) that nowadays they practiced everywhere not to do, and if one did they do not put him in cherem and do not fine him, only he does not see a sign of blessing. Shulchan Gavoah s.k. 4.
