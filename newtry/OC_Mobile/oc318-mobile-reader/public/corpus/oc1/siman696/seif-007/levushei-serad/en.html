For the law of portions is like the law of asking peace, and he wrote there on the 14th in s.k. 3 that on Shabbat there are places that permit asking peace of a mourner and there are places that forbid — therefore in places that practiced permission, portions too are permitted — see there; and the same here on Purim, whose law is also like Shabbat regarding mourning: portions are permitted in a place where they practice permission to ask peace on Shabbat, for according to them it is likewise permitted to ask peace on Purim — therefore portions too are permitted. But Taz s.k. 3 understood Rama's intent here on what Rama wrote there in s.k. 2 that nowadays they are lenient in asking peace of a mourner after thirty days and there is no reason for this unless we say the peace we practice is not called asking peace as in their days — see there; therefore Taz was puzzled on portions, there is no such permission — therefore he wrote one should not rely on this etc.; but per Magen Avraham's explanation there is no difficulty, for Rama's intent is to permit portions on Purim in places that permit on Shabbat asking peace of a mourner, for he holds that even asking peace as in their days was permitted on Shabbat — simple.
