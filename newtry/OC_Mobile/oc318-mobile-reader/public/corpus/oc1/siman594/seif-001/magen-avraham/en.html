His fellow does not, etc. Unless there are ten; Tur. So too the Rambam wrote in Hilchos Tefillah, law 4, that a shaliach tzibbur does not discharge fewer than ten. In law 9 he wrote that he discharges only one who is not expert. So too wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham, nesiv 3, part 7; see there. And see what I wrote in siman 59, seif 4.
