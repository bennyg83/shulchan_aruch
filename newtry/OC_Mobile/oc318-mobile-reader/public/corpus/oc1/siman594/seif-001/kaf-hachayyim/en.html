1) [Seif 1] An individual who did not sound the shofar, his fellow may sound it for him, etc. This is as with all mitzvos, through which one person may discharge the obligation of his fellow. Tur. This follows from the law of mutual responsibility, for all Jews are responsible for one another, as written above, siman 273, os 31, and siman 167, os 127, see there.
