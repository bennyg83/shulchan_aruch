(3) They practice now with fruits, etc. — Olas Tamid wrote thus one should practice with other beverages such as burnt wine and the like in which one is not exempted by his fellow's blessing — and the world does not practice and error is in their hands — until here. And according to what I explained above that by law it helps in all things, only the matter depends on the custom of establishment — if so it is possible to establish for them nowadays on other beverages like wine; and also behold it appears b'dieved when intending through hearing one fulfills even with fruits as in the after-blessing above. And see above siman 167 seif 13 — Taz and Magen Avraham wrote that nowadays beer and mead that they call mead are like wine that one blesses for all; and it is obvious that if they came within the meal even with fruits one blesses for all — for since hasseivah helps for bread it also helps for other things.
