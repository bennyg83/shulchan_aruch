3) There: Nevertheless, sitting is required. Regarding incense, even sitting is unnecessary. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 5. Likewise, blessings of praise and blessings over mitzvos require no established framework. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1. Regarding Birkas HaMazon, even if they did not establish themselves or recline, one person recites the blessing for all. Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 2, and Machazik Berachah, paragraph 3. Erekh HaShulchan, paragraph 3, however, cites several authorities who hold that one person recites Birkas HaMazon for all only if they established themselves together; see there.
