(s.k. 3) They need to separate — and if it comes at the end of the meal, etc. After-blessing — meaning after they withdrew their hands from bread — then one must bless on the fruits that were brought, also after-blessing — and then one blesses for all; but before they withdrew their hands from bread we do not find one must say after-blessing on fruits.

And in siman 177 seif 2 thus. Meaning it is explained there this law is not found among us to withdraw our hands from bread — and see in Magen Avraham s.k. 7 the reason.
