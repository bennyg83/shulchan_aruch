Regarding fruits. See Baal HaTanya; and in Beit Rabbi they were uncertain: one who ate pat kasnin and fruits in the measure of their law — does one bless and include both — if he could fulfill one who ate pat kasnin alone — and they were uncertain if one who includes in his blessing also peri ha'adamah can fulfill, lest for himself it is an interruption; and he brought there Radbaz siman 561 mentioned siman 100, and more proofs that the listener can intend to fulfill in what concerns him and he does not care about what the blesser adds that he blesses for himself. And if they were sitting and one ate fruits that are not of the seven species and one drank — the drinker can exempt his fellow through borei peri haGefen; and he brought Garden of Roses — if burnt wine and spice were before him, the shehakol of the burnt wine exempts the shehakol of the spice; and he wrote in Dvar Shmuel siman 295: when one fulfills through his fellow's blessing, he must intend from the beginning of the blessing to its end, such as kiddush and the like — he should not answer Baruch Hu uVaruch Shemo, for one who hears is as one who answers and his mouth and heart are aligned in reciting the blessing; and if he answers Baruch Hu uVaruch Shemo it is as an interruption and addition and change from the formulation coined by the Sages. And in Beit Rabbi he wrote in Shoshanim L'David that he rebuked one who disagreed with him etc., and it is proper to be careful; but one should not protest those who answer Baruch Hu uVaruch Shemo. End of his words.
