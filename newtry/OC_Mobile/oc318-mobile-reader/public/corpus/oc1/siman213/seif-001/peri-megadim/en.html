Rashba, in responsum 196 attributed to Ramban and cited by Beit Yosef, writes that there is no zimmun over fruit initially, but after the fact one fulfills through hearing, because hearing is equivalent to answering. Nowadays, when people are lax about the blessing, one should act this way even initially: one person should bless, and everyone should listen and intend to fulfill their obligation. The statement that one may rely on this opinion does not apply to an after-blessing if one delayed longer than the time needed to eat a pras and did not eat a kezayis within that period. In such a case he may not recite the after-blessing. See siman 210 concerning this.
