(1) And that which we say, etc. — and b'dieved Beit Yosef and Bach wrote in the name of Rashba that one fulfills through hearing even in the after-blessing — see there; and nowadays that people are lax in it the Taz wrote one should bless in a loud voice so others fulfill through him. Darchei Moshe wrote: it appears bread is not literal only, rather the same law applies to types of porridge made from the five grains, as Rosh wrote above siman 178 regarding things requiring blessing in their place — until here his language. And study is required on the law: from Levush's language that he wrote there is no zimmun except on bread because of its importance — it does not imply thus, for importance is only on bread as above siman 178 seif 3; and also behold there are poskim above that specifically actual bread requires blessing in its place. And that which Beit Yosef and Levush wrote plainly that wine is like fruits requires study — for I found in Semak that he ruled wine regarding after-blessing is like bread; again I saw in Ra'avan's responsum siman 196 and Rabbeinu Yonah and Semak like Beit Yosef's words. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: also the authors of Shulchan Aruch omitted this that they brought before him wine and good oil to smell, that he blesses on the wine first — because we already said in siman 177 seif 2 that nowadays the law of things that come after the meal does not apply — until here. And above in siman 174 and 177 it is explained that Levush holds that even in our times this custom applies, and therefore Tur wrote; also it is difficult for me — for behold even not within the meal this law applies when both came at once. And to my humble opinion it appears: behold Ma'adanei Melech wrote daf 54 — Tur's reason is because wine causes a blessing for itself, therefore it is more important; and likewise Darchei Moshe wrote; and if so one may say we already heard from what Shulchan Aruch and Levush wrote siman 211 seif 3 that wine precedes even olives; and according to this it is difficult on Tur who also wrote thus in siman 211. Also it is difficult for me on his reason — for if so other beverages do not precede the blessing of smell, and it does not imply thus from Beit Yosef's words at the end of this siman; and likewise it is explained from Olas Tamid which I will bring siman 296 s.k. 6 that every blessing of eating and drinking precedes the blessing of smell; and likewise I found explained in Rashba's novellae daf 33 and Kol Bo daf 21 who wrote the reason is because bodily pleasure entering the body is more important than smell; one may also give a reason that eating and drinking — the body and soul enjoy from it, which is not so with smell. And perhaps also Darchei Moshe and Ma'adanei Melech did not write the reason of important wine except against oil which is for smell and for anointing as we say in the Talmud end of chapter Keitzad Mevarchin — therefore we require specifically the reason of important wine; and study is required.
