On all fruits and other things — see in Magen Avraham who sides to say that on spices (mugmar), even though it is a blessing of enjoyment, it does not even require sitting to exempt one another; and he brought proof from the light blessing that one blesses for all, as explained in siman 298 seif 4, and there too sitting is not required per his words. See in Peri Megadim who questions his words — light is different, for it is a blessing of praise and is like blessings on mitzvot, unlike here; and in truth similarly Rashba wrote in that sugya of light see there (and see further there Tosafot R' Yitzchak and Rosh there). However in truth also regarding light itself it is not clear, and in Shitah Mekubetzet there it states explicitly that at least sitting together is nevertheless required see there; and possibly more — also Rashba did not be lenient except regarding reclining [and what Magen Avraham questions — that sitting means they sat together with fixed intent for this, and at the synagogue they were initially engaged in study — in my humble opinion nothing is lacking, for even if they sat of themselves and afterward wanted one to exempt the other, it also helps with other things that do not require reclining, for at least there is some fixing; and in this manner is the Mechaber's intent in "nevertheless sitting is required." See Rashi Berachot 42a s.v. badukh ploni etc. "but they sat of themselves" etc. — behold per Rashi what we say in the mishnah "they were sitting" means of themselves, and on this the Talmud says other things besides bread and wine do not require reclining, and poskim wrote sitting is nevertheless required — meaning sitting of themselves. And what is practiced today to exempt in the synagogue with the light blessing even though all the people stand — this is because they also bless havdalah with the light, and since fixing works for havdalah it also works for this, as Perishah wrote there in siman 298; unlike when they bless only on the light alone, possibly sitting is properly required] and particularly regarding mugmar, which is a complete blessing of enjoyment, certainly one can say Rashba agrees to Shitah Mekubetzet that at least sitting is nevertheless required — requires study for practical law:
