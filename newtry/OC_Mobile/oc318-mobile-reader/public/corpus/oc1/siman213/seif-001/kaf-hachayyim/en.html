1) [Seif 1] One person discharges another, etc. This means that when one recites a blessing and the other listens and intends to fulfill his obligation, while the person reciting likewise intends to discharge him, and the listener hears the blessing from beginning to end, he is exempt and fulfills his obligation through the other's blessing. Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. This follows what is stated below in seif 3.
