1) [Seif 1] One person discharges another, etc. This means that when one recites a blessing and the other listens and intends to fulfill his obligation, while the person reciting likewise intends to discharge him, and the listener hears the blessing from beginning to end, he is exempt and fulfills his obligation through the other's blessing. Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. This follows what is stated below in seif 3.
2) There: Nevertheless, sitting is required. This means at one table or upon one tablecloth, as stated above in siman 167, seif 11. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2, Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1, and Chayei Adam, rule 5, paragraph 19, write the same. Without one shared table, even establishing themselves in one location is ineffective. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2.
3) There: Nevertheless, sitting is required. Regarding incense, even sitting is unnecessary. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 5. Likewise, blessings of praise and blessings over mitzvos require no established framework. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1. Regarding Birkas HaMazon, even if they did not establish themselves or recline, one person recites the blessing for all. Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 2, and Machazik Berachah, paragraph 3. Erekh HaShulchan, paragraph 3, however, cites several authorities who hold that one person recites Birkas HaMazon for all only if they established themselves together; see there.
