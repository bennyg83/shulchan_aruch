Seif katan 1: Even, etc. Tosafos, page 42a, at the end of s.v. hesivu, write that this implies that for incense even sitting is unnecessary.
He wrote this based on his own reasoning. Since the benefit reaches everyone simultaneously, they are joined together and reclining is unnecessary. The same reasoning shows that sitting at one table is also unnecessary.
This is also implied by siman 298, seif 14: if they were sitting in the beis midrash and light was brought to them on Motzaei Shabbos, one person blesses for everyone.
Nevertheless, one person blesses for everyone. The reason must be that the benefit from the light also reaches everyone simultaneously, like incense. This proves that for this reason even sitting is unnecessary.
