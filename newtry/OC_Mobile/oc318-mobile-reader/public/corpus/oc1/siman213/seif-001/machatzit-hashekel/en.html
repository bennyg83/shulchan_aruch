s.k. 1 Even, etc. Tosafot wrote daf 42a end s.v. hasseivu, etc. — it implies in the final blessing even sitting is not required.

He wrote so he explains himself. Since through pleasure coming to all at once they have connection and need not hasseivah — if so likewise need not sitting at one table.

And likewise siman 288 seif 14 — they were sitting in the synagogue and they brought them light on motza'ei Shabbat — one blesses for all.

And nevertheless one blesses for all. And therefore the reason is because the pleasure of the light also comes to all at once like in the final blessing — plainly from this reason even sitting is not required.
