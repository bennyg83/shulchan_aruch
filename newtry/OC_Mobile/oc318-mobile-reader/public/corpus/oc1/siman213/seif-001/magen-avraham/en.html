Even without hasseivah. It is in the Gemara that their way was in great feasts that guests gather and sit on benches and drink until all gather; and then they ascend and sit in the place of the meal; and in the first place each blesses for himself — for since their minds are to uproot it is not called establishment at all and is less than sitting — Tosafot wrote; and if you ask what is different in the final blessing that hasseivah is not required — one may answer in the final blessing all enjoy together, unlike in eating where they enjoy one from another — end of his words; it implies in the final blessing even sitting is not required; and likewise siman 288 seif 14 — for there it was not sitting, for sitting means they sat together with establishment of intent to eat and the like, and there they were engaged in learning and nevertheless one blesses for all; and likewise in Terumat HaDeshen there.

With sitting. On one table as written siman 177 seif 11.

They need to separate. And if they came at the end of the meal one blesses for all the after-blessing since zimmun came to them through bread (Beit Yosef Rashba) siman 177 seif 2.

For there is no zimmun. And if one blessed and the others heard they fulfilled (Beit Yosef Rashba) siman 167 seif 13 — see what is written.

Hasseivah does not help. And if they came within the meal one blesses for all — for since hasseivah helps for bread it also helps for other things (Gemara).

In fruits. And the same in other things except bread and wine (Bach Darchei Moshe); and ostensibly it implies our beer has the law of wine for this matter — for they also are accustomed to establish on it to drink without a meal; and likewise mead; and likewise in Darchei Moshe; and therefore he was precise and wrote here they practice now with fruits, etc.; and Bach wrote that even on good wine one blesses each for himself — since some say nowadays there is no establishment even on wine; and this is Agudah's language in Chullin siman 108: a simple custom in Ashkenaz that on wine one blesses for all — because they are accustomed to establish on it; but if two or three enter one person's house and they give them wine to drink casually — each blesses for himself — end of his words; and one should not deviate from the custom — see siman 208 seif 16.
