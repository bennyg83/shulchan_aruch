Question. At one table — see siman 167 seif 11. When he wants to drink to his friend [l'chaim] he should bless first, for the blessing beforehand is the Holy One's and afterward his own:

For fruits. If one blessed and the others heard — they fulfill. Beit Yosef, Rashba. Taz wrote, and it appears to me that nowadays since they are lax regarding the concluding blessing, one may rely on this view and he should bless the concluding blessing in a loud voice and the others fulfill through him:

On fruits. And likewise in other things besides bread and wine; and apparently it means our beer has the law of wine regarding this matter, for they too are accustomed to fix on it to drink without a meal, and likewise honey water that they call mead, and so in Darkei Moshe; therefore he was precise and wrote here "they now practice on fruits" etc. And Taz plainly ruled that now beer and honey water are in the category of bread and wine that one blesses for all — see there.
