(2) And some say for us, etc. — Rama's language: and some say in all things except bread and wine hasseivah does not help, and the same is sitting for us; and therefore they practice now with fruits that each blesses for himself — until here. It is clear that also for them there is a distinction — and this is Ra'avad's view that Beit Yosef brought siman 174 — he rules like the latter language of Rabbenu Yonah — see there. And Olas Tamid implies he understood Rama brought it from Rashba's words in this siman; and therefore he wondered — for to Rashba himself this reasoning does not appear, and on the contrary Rama should have ruled like Tosafot and Mordechai and Semag who hold that even for wine hasseivah does not help, and the same is sitting for us — until here and he was not precise. And in any case this requires a resolution why he did not rule like Tosafot and Mordechai and Semak; and also behold Beit Yosef wrote above siman 174 that Ra'avad's words are isolated against all poskim. And it appears to my humble opinion Rama wrote according to Ra'avad because he saw they practice with fruits that each blesses by himself and with wine they do not practice thus — and he supported the custom to Ra'avad's words — and this is "and therefore they practice," etc. And behold Malbushei Yom Tov challenged Levush: what reason did he not write like Rama's words; and likewise in Lechem Chamudos daf 53 he wrote R' Mordechai Yafe erred here. And to my humble opinion Levush aimed well — for it is difficult for him to rely our custom on isolated Ra'avad; rather he holds we establish like Tosafot and Mordechai and Semag that by law hasseivah helps in all of them (and so I found explicitly in Semag daf 111; and one may wonder greatly on Bach siman 174 who wrote Semag holds like Ra'avad that by law hasseivah does not help — also in any case it is wondrous). Rather in that which Semag and his supporters wrote we are not accustomed to establish ourselves on wine, etc. — Levush holds this is specifically in their days; and likewise it implies from Bach siman 174. But nowadays, since they practice to establish on wine — its law is like bread; and therefore with wine they practice one blesses for all.
