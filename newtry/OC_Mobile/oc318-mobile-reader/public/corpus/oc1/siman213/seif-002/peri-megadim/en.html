And know regarding kinds of porridge that some say the blessing after eating that is a kind of threefold blessing is d'oraisa — see Rif in Taz ot 3; if so, one who ate a little cannot fulfill for one who ate to satiety of the seven species who ate a kezayit of the kinds — it refers to the view that the kind-of-three blessing is d'oraisa — see siman 186; and all these paths follow the view that women's Birkat HaMazon is d'rabbanan — likewise the kind-of-three blessing — Magen Avraham there ot 1; if so, one who ate more blesses; and a male thirteen years and one day.
