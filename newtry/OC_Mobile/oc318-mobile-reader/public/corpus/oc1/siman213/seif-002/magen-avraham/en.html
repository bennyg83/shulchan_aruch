And then they fulfill. This implies that if the person reciting the blessing does not eat or drink with them, they do not fulfill their obligation through hearing him even after the fact, because his blessing is in vain. This is also stated by Beit Yosef and by Mabit, part 1, siman 109, contrary to Behag. This is not comparable to siman 209, seif 2, where the person acted unintentionally; see there. See also siman 219, seif 4. When ten people perform one mitzvah, it is preferable for one person to bless for everyone. See siman 298 and siman 8, seif 5. This applies even if the obligation did not arise for everyone simultaneously. Mabit, part 1, siman 180. See there that some disagree, and see Yoreh Deah, siman 265, seif 5.
