In Shulchan Aruch: when Rosh Hashanah falls [on Shabbat], etc.; and see Tashbetz vol. 3 siman 176 and in Chayei Adam siman 248 and in Yavin Shemuah to Rashbatz introduction to the article on chametz; and see Beit Yosef from there, in the name of Baal HaItim that in places where they say Aleinu on Shabbat, then at minchah one should say Aleinu first — for the early rabbis enacted it first — and afterward Tzidkatcha; and likewise in Shakhnehog siman 622 that even though Tzidkatcha is frequent, nevertheless Aleinu is said in the morning and at minchah and Tzidkatcha only at minchah; and in Beit Yosef siman 643 he challenged this from Sukkah daf 3 that the halacha is like Rabbah in Beit Hillel that time comes first and then sukkah because time is frequent, etc. — see there; and in responsa Kenesset Yisrael siman 50 and see siman 622.
