They say Tzidkatcha. Taz, Av Beit Din — another reason that it is Rosh Chodesh — see Levush. Therefore on Yom Kippur they say it; but for us, since it is impossible on Yom Kippur as in siman 582, it should be that one does not arouse judgment, as written "Your judgments are a great deep" — see Levush. And see Bach 596 that Rosh Hashanah is called a festival and it is written "and you shall rejoice in your festival" — see there. However on Yom Kippur, since one cannot say Tzidkatcha when it falls on Shabbat, therefore the reason is so as not to arouse judgment as Levush wrote; and see Eliyahu Rabbah in 599.
