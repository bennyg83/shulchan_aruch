They say. Tzidkascha; see Turei Zahav and Beis Yosef. There is another reason, that it is Rosh Chodesh; and see Levush. According to this, on Yom Kippur they would say it. For us, who do not say it on Yom Kippur, as in siman 652, one must say the reason is in order not to awaken judgment, for it is written regarding it, Your judgments are the great deep; see Levush. See Bach, siman 596, that Rosh Hashanah is called a chag, and it is written, You shall rejoice on your chag; see there. However, regarding Yom Kippur, where Tzidkascha Tzedek is not said when it falls on Shabbos, the reason must be in order not to awaken judgment, as the Levush wrote. See what I will write, with Hashem's help, in siman 599.
