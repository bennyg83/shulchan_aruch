3) Ibid., gloss. Our custom is not to say Ashrei, etc. This is in order to hasten, so that the time for the Minchah prayer will not pass. It is sufficient that the Torah reading interrupts between the Mussaf prayer and the Minchah prayer, just as there is an interruption between Yotzer and Mussaf. Ashrei and U'Va L'Tzion are left until after Minchah, so that they will form an interruption between the Minchah prayer and the Ne'ilah prayer.
