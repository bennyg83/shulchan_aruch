Se'if 1, For Minchah he says Ashrei, etc. See the first chapter of Berachos.
And Uva LeTziyon. See the last chapter of Sotah.
And one does not say, etc. For on Shabbos we say it on the basis of the Midrash, 'and songs of wine-drinkers,' etc.
And I, etc. As above, siman 292, se'if 1.
And we do not, etc. See above, siman 108, se'if 2: after he says, etc., and likewise, etc. This is based on what is written at the end of chapter 4 of Berachos: How long should he wait, etc. So too here; but at Minchah he need not wait, because they interrupted with the reading.
