The order of the Minchah prayer. It contains four se'ifim.
For Minchah one says "Ashrei" [and] "U'va LeTziyon," and we do not say "Va'ani Sefilasi," even if it falls on Shabbos. {Rama: And we are not accustomed to say "Ashrei" [and] "U'va LeTziyon" before Minchah, but only before Ne'ilah; and so wrote some of the [early] authorities (Mordechai; Hagahos Maimoni, end of [Hilchos] Ahavah and end of Hilchos Yom Kippur; Kol Bo; Minhagim). And we do not say "Ein KeElokeinu" on Yom Kippur (Minhagim).}
