Forbidden relations. Tosafos in Megillah 31 wrote that the reason is because the women are adorned in honor of the day. In the Midrash it is found: Just as the Holy One, blessed be He, warned not to uncover nakedness, so He should not uncover the nakedness of his sins. The Taz wrote the reason: a person's soul desires forbidden relations more than all other transgressions; therefore we warn about this on Yom Kippur, which is a holy and awesome day; see there. Similarly, the death of Aharon's sons is read to teach that one should not be lenient with commandments in which warnings are not found, as with Nadav and Avihu. Kol Bo.
With Yonah. Because it contains the power of repentance, and that one cannot flee from Hashem. Levush.
Over the Torah. Meaning: our custom is not as the Shulchan Aruch wrote. Rather, we do not say at all 'Al HaTorah' or 'Al HaAvodah'; we only conclude 'Magen David.' If there is not enough time, he should skip Avinu Malkeinu in order to begin Ne'ilah at its proper time; see siman 620. It is better to say a few selichos calmly than many in haste. Later authorities.
