And we take out a Torah scroll and three [men] read in the parashah of arayos [forbidden relations], until the end of the parashah; and the third is maftir in [the book of] Yonah, and concludes "Mi Kel kamocha," and blesses before it and after it. And if it falls on Shabbos, one makes mention in it of [the theme of] Shabbos and concludes with [the theme of] Shabbos. {Rama: And we do not say "Al haTorah ve'al ha'avodah" at Minchah (Hagahos Maimoni, end of Hilchos Yom Kippur; Maharil; their custom; Hagahos Mordechai).}
