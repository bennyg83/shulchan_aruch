[2] [Levush:] For his soul, etc. People stumble in this all year, and also on Yom Kippur, when women adorn themselves in honor of the day, as we say in Yoma 19, that nowadays on Yom Kippur many virgins were violated in Nehardea (Abudraham). From the Gemara mentioned above I asked, in Eliyahu Zuta, against what Mahariv wrote in simanim 191 and 192, that on this day they are like angels, over whom the evil inclination has no control, and therefore one who commits a transgression on it has a doubled and redoubled sin. End quote. But in the Gemara there, what does the Holy One, blessed be He, say? 'Sin crouches at the opening.' Rashi explains: the evil inclination causes him to sin against his will. And what does the Satan say? It says: On Yom Kippur the Satan has no permission to prosecute. This shows that it does have control, causing them to sin even against their will, only that it has no permission to prosecute. On the contrary, it seems to me that it has more control then, so that the prosecution will arise on its own. Even regarding prosecution, the Rokeach wrote in siman 216 that only for transgressions committed on that day does it have no permission, but for those of the whole year it does prosecute.
