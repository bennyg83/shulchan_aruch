Se'if 2, And three read. Beginning of chapter 3 of Megillah.
And if it falls. As written in Shabbos 24: And the halachah is not, etc., even according to the authorities who rule like Rav Gidel.
And one does not say 'Al,' etc. This is against the Gemara in Shabbos there. But the Hagahos Mordechai there wrote in the name of Rabbi Shimon bar Baruch that it is not comparable to Shabbos. The Torah reading and haftarah at Shabbos Minchah are because of the sanctity of Shabbos, and therefore we say 'Al HaTorah,' etc., and 'for the day,' etc. But the Torah reading and haftarah at Minchah of Yom Kippur are only because of the fast, as on every fast day. A proof for his words is that on Shabbos, which is more severe than Yom Kippur, we do not read a haftarah at Minchah.
