4) The custom of Sephardic communities is to recite the passage of the Akeidah through "and I will remember the land." Afterward, they say the chapter of Tehillim, "For the conductor, upon the Gittis," etc., the passage of the daily offering, Pittum HaKetores, Ashrei, U'Va L'Tzion, and Kaddish. They then take out the Sefer Torah, say Kaddish, and pray Minchah. This is also the custom of the pious members of the Beit El congregation, may its Rock and Redeemer protect it. See citations 14.
