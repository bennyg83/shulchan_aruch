(14) They do not raise their hands, etc. - See siman 129; all their details are explained there.
(15) He raises his hands - See above, siman 129 in the gloss, that for this reason some have the custom to say 'Elokeinu ve'Elokei Avoseinu,' etc. The same applies that one should then say 'Sim Shalom' and not 'Shalom Rav,' as explained at the end of siman 127, for they have the same law. If one kohen went up to the duchan and the other kohanim uprooted their feet at Retzeh but have not yet gone up, it is good that they leave the synagogue and not go up with him [Birkei Yosef].
