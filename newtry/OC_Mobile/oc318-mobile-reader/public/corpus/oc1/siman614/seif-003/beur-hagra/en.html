Se'if 3. A woman after childbirth, etc. As above, for the sugya of the Gemara there follows Rabbi Eliezer.
All thirty days. See Rashi there, 73b, on the Mishnah, comment beginning "she may wear shoes." And see the end of chapter 18 of Shabbos, 129a: "they make a fire, etc." There he explains that all thirty days are as it says there: for a woman after childbirth, thirty days, etc., regarding immersion.
And the sick person, etc. There: they initially thought, etc. It must be speaking of a sick person who is not in danger, since they initially thought to distinguish. One must say that cold is dangerous for him. And even according to the Rambam, who forbids there according to his textual reading, nevertheless here it is permitted, like anointing.
And likewise one who, etc. For only that which is for pleasure is forbidden. See Shabbos chapter 6, 61a, and what I wrote below.
