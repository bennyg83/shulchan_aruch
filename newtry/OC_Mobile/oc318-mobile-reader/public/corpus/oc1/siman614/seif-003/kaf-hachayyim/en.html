3) Ibid. But if he is ill, etc. For he does not do so for pleasure. Levush. Although we wrote in the preceding section that even when his intent is not for pleasure he nevertheless derives pleasure, one can say that this applies specifically to a healthy person who anoints his skin. This is not so for a sick person or when applying it upon scabs, for he does not derive pleasure from it, but only alleviates his suffering. One can further say that for a sick person or for scabs, since he does not do it for pleasure, the Sages did not decree against it even if he incidentally derives pleasure from it.
