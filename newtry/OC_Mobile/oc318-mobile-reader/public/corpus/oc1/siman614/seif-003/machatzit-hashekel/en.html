(א) (Note 3) And it is permitted, etc., sometimes, etc., from Shulchan Aruch's wording and likewise from responsum Terumat HaDeshen 149 it implies permitted for everyone; Terumat HaDeshen also brings Mordechai's words. It should be said Mordechai's intent is also to permit for everyone, because some are cold, some find cold hard by nature — therefore permitted for everyone, as presumably one standing on pillows needs it and does not intend pleasure.
