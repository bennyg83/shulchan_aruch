4) Ibid. Or if he has scabs on his head, it is permitted. This applies specifically in a place where even a healthy person commonly anoints himself on weekdays. But in a place where a healthy person does not commonly anoint himself on weekdays, it is forbidden to anoint over scabs, whether on a festival or on Shabbos, because it is evident that he does so for healing, as stated above siman 327, seif 1 in the gloss.
