Rain. The Taz wrote that it appears that when rain is falling, one needs to be a delicate person. But if there is mire and mud on the ground, everyone is like a delicate person in this regard; see there. The Magen Avraham wrote: it requires analysis what people now rely on to be lenient, for not everyone can say, 'I am delicate,' as written in siman 640; see there. Especially since they wear socks, they do not have so much distress walking in mire and mud. Therefore one should protest if he knows they will accept it; see there.
Permitted. From the Nimukei Yosef it implies that specifically a sandal without a heel is permitted, but not a shoe. The Shelah wrote that he should switch right to left and left to right; see there. The Magen Avraham wrote: perhaps even among non-Jews it is forbidden, even though there is concern that they will mock him, since it is according to the Torah; see there. See siman 611.
