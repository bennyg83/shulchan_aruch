Shulchan Aruch se'if 2 To go out with them. This means that the Ramban holds that since it is permitted to wear these types on Yom Kippur because they are not shoes, it is therefore forbidden to go out with them into the public domain, for since they are not shoes they are a burden. The Ran answers that specifically on Shabbos, when one can walk with leather shoes, these types are a burden. This is not so on Yom Kippur, when everyone wears these types, for then they are the normal manner of clothing and not a burden. With this you will understand the words of the Bach in Magen Avraham subparagraph 2: for practical law he holds one should be stringent not to wear them on Yom Kippur, lest they are a shoe; and one who is delicate and wears them should be concerned lest they are not a shoe, in which case they are a burden, and he should not walk in them in a place that has no eruv.
