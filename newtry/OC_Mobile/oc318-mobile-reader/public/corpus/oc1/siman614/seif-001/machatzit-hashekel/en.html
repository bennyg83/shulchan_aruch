(s.k. 1) And even if he does not suffer — what Magen Avraham wrote regarding washing that it is permitted to remove filth. It should be said Magen Avraham holds as was permitted in siman 613 seif 1 — washing to remove mud and excrement; likewise to remove filth is permitted, since both are not for pleasure. But Taz on siman 613 wrote washing to remove filth is forbidden.
