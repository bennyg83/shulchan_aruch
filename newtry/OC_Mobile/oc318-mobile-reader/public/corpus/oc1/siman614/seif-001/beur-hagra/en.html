Se'if 1. And even if it is not, etc. That is, even though regarding a mourner they said in the first chapter of Ta'anis, 13b, "and if to pass, etc.," here it is forbidden, as stated in the Yerushalmi, last chapter of Yoma and first chapter of Ta'anis: on Yom Kippur, both anointing that is for pleasure and anointing that is not for pleasure are forbidden; on Tisha B'Av and public fasts, anointing that is for pleasure is forbidden, while that which is not for pleasure is permitted.
Even, etc. For if not, what novelty is he teaching us? Even eating is permitted.
