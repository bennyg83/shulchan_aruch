In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2, at the end of his words: 'and so is the custom.' The primary practice is to walk the whole day in socks and stockings, as is also written in Pri Megadim. When one walks in the street, he should walk in felt shoes made in a way that he feels the ground beneath his feet, for this is indicated by the language of the Rambam, and as Maharam Ash wrote in part 2, siman 25, where his words are correct; see there. See below, se'if katan 5.
