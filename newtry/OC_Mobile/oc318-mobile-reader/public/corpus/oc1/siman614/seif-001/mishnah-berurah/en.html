(1) And even if it is not, etc., the filth - Meaning, even though it is not for pleasure, it is forbidden.
(2) Scabs - This is specifically in a place where people ordinarily anoint during the week even a healthy man. But in a place where healthy men do not ordinarily anoint during the week, it is forbidden to anoint over scabs, both on Yom Tov and on Shabbos, for it is evident that it is for healing, as above in siman 327, se'if 1 in the gloss.
