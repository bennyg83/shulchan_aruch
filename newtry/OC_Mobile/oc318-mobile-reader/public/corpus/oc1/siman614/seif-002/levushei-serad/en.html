Taz subparagraph 1 That Rabbi Meir and Rabbi Yosi forbid. For he holds it is a shoe, and Rabbi Yosi forbids because he holds that it is not a shoe and is a burden. Even so, Rabbi Yosi agrees that on Yom Kippur it is forbidden. This proves that anything that protects is forbidden. He answers: actually the amputee's wooden leg, which is made of wood, is called a shoe, and that is why it is forbidden on Yom Kippur. But one made of other materials is not a shoe and is permitted.
