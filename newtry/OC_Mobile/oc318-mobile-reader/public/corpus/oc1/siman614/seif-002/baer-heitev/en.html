Sandal. One removes shoes while it is still day and goes to the synagogue barefoot. The Bach wrote: I saw from my teachers that they went completely barefoot, without stockings. The Magen Avraham and Taz permit going with stockings, and so is the custom. See the Taz, who discussed this at length and concluded that according to all opinions there is no permission to wear shoes even for a mitzvah matter. Wearing cloth stockings or socks is permitted, and there is no doubt about it. Because of our many sins, many of the common people, and even some learned people, walk in the street with shoes, and when they come to the synagogue they also walk with shoes; only when they reach their place do they remove them for as long as they stand there, and no more. These people walk in darkness and are called sinners if they do not listen to the voice of their teachers. End quote. See se'if katan 6, what he wrote there.
Cushions. During Shemoneh Esrei it is forbidden to stand on anything. Later authorities.
