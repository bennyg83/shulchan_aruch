It is forbidden to put on a sandal or shoe of' leather, even with only a heel patch and the like; even of wood covered with leather is forbidden; but of reed, straw, cloth, or other materials is permitted, even to go out in them in public thoroughfares. (One may stand on pillows and cushions of leather; nevertheless one who is stringent is blessed (Mordechai Yoma and Terumat HaDeshen 149).):
