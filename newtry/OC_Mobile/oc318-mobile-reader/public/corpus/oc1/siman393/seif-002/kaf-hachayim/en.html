(ב) There: and if he forgot and did not make eruv etc. — and if he forgot from Yom Tov and remembered on erev Shabbat and erred and thought it permitted to make eruv on Yom Tov that falls on erev Shabbat and made eruv — requires study whether after the fact the eruv helps or not; Maharsha siman 1; and it appears if it was second day of Yom Tov and he erred and made eruv, one may be lenient since we are expert in fixing the month and it is only a rabbinic enactment, and eruv too is only rabbinic enactment; and one may say two rabbinic matters — and if so one may be lenient if he already did so after the fact unknowingly, and there is some need in the matter.
