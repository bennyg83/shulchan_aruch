But if he forgot and did not establish an eruv, etc. If he forgot before Yom Tov, remembered on Friday, and mistakenly thought that one may establish an eruv on a Yom Tov that occurs on Friday, and he did establish one, it requires examination whether the eruv is effective after the fact. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1. It appears that if this occurred on the second day of Yom Tov and he mistakenly established the eruv, one may rule leniently. We are expert in determining the calendar, and the observance of the second day is only an enactment of our Sages. The eruv itself is also only an enactment of our Sages, making this a twofold Rabbinic issue. Therefore, if he already acted without knowing the law and there is some need, one may be lenient.
