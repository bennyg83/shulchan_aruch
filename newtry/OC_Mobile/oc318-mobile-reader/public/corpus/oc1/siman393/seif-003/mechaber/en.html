If one made an eruv for two people—for one person while it was still day and that eruv was consumed during twilight, and for another person during twilight—both have acquired their eruv. For the one whose eruv was consumed during twilight, we consider it night; for the one whose eruv was placed during twilight, we consider it day. However, if one made an eruv during twilight and it was consumed during twilight, it is forbidden.
