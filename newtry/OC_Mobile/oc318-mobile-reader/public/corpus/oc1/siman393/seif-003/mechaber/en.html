If one made eruv for two people — for one while still day and the eruv was eaten during bein hashemashot, and for another during bein hashemashot — both acquired eruv. For the one whose eruv was eaten during bein hashemashot' — we consider it night; and for the one who placed his eruv during bein hashemashot — we' consider it day. But if he made eruv for him during bein hashemashot and his eruv was eaten during bein hashemashot — it is forbidden.
