(א) (יג) Made eruv for two — meaning two appointed him agent to make eruv on their behalf:

(ב) (יד) We consider it night — and eruv already took effect:

(ג) (טו) We consider it day — and he placed at his time; and even though this person became agent for both and they contradict each other, nevertheless since eruv chatzerot is rabbinic speech, we lean leniently for each one because rabbinic doubt is lenient [gemara]:

(ד) (טז) But if he made eruv etc. — for in this entire case we do not lean lenient to split bein hashemashot itself for one person — meaning for placing to make it like day and for eating to make it like night:
