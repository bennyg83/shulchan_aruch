3) There. He should establish an eruv conditionally on Yom Tov, etc. Rambam writes in chapter 6 of Hilchos Yom Tov, halachah 15, that nowadays, when we are expert in determining the calendar, one may not establish an eruv conditionally, whether an eruv tavshilin, eruv chatzeiros, or another eruv. See Beis Yosef, who writes that Ran and Rashba challenge this ruling. Birkei Yosef, subsection 1, also questions Rambam from the Gemara, for they were expert in determining the calendar and nevertheless made conditional stipulations on Yom Tov observed outside Eretz Yisrael. See there. The later authorities likewise agree with the Shulchan Aruch's ruling that one may make a conditional stipulation during the two days of Yom Tov observed outside Eretz Yisrael and establish an eruv conditionally, even nowadays when we are expert in determining the calendar.
