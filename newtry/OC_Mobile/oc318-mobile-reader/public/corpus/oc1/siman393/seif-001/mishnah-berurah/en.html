(א) One does not make eruv etc. — because it appears like fixing on Yom Tov for tomorrow's need; and if he forgot and made eruv, there is room to be uncertain whether it helps [Peri Megadim]:

(ב) Yom Tov that falls to be on erev Shabbat — whether two days fall Thursday-Friday or Friday-Shabbat; rather he leaves from Yom Tov:

(ג) And if he forgot and did not make eruv — explanation: Yom Tov of the world, not erev Shabbat:

(ד) Conditionally — and without a blessing. Berakhot siman 528:

(ה) Let this be the eruv — for Shabbat's sake [Taz]:

(ו) And tomorrow he will say — on that bread: lest if he speaks on another bread he must also guard the first bread that it remain until night of Shabbat falls, lest yesterday was weekday and his eruv took effect and it must remain until entry of Shabbat:

(ז) On two festival days of diaspora — for from Torah only one day is holy and the second is due to doubt:

(ח) And see below siman 528 — for there it is explained the prohibition of placing eruv chatzerot on Yom Tov that falls on erev Shabbat applies even if he placed eruv tavshilin:
