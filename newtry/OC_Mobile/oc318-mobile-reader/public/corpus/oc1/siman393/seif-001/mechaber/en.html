Laws of eruv on Yom Tov that falls on erev Shabbat, and the law of bein hashemashot for eruv. Containing 3 seifim. One
does not make eruv chatzerot or shitufei mevoot on Yom Tov that falls to' be on erev Shabbat; and if he forgot and did not make eruv before Yom Tov, and two festival days fall on Thursday and Friday — he may make eruv on Yom Tov conditionally, saying: if today is weekday, let this be the eruv; if today is holy, my words are nothing. And tomorrow he says: if today is holy, I already made eruv from yesterday; if today is weekday, let this be the eruv. And this applies to two days of Yom Tov of the diaspora; but for two days of Rosh Hashanah, no — for they are considered like one long day (and see below siman 528). (.
