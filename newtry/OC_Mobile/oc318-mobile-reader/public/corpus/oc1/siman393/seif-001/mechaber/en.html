One does not make eruv chatzeirot (courtyard eruv) or shitufei mevoot (alleyway partnership) on a festival that falls on Friday.
But if one forgot and did not make an eruv before the festival,' and two festival days fall on Thursday and Friday, one may make an eruv on the festival conditionally, saying: "If today is a weekday, let this be an eruv; if today is holy, my words are nothing." And the next day one says: "If today is holy, I already made an eruv yesterday; if today is a weekday, let this be an eruv." This applies to the two festival days observed in the diaspora, but not to the two days of Rosh Hashanah, for they are considered like one long day. (See below, section 528.) (.
