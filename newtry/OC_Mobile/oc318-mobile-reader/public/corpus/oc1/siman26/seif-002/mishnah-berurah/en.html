(3) He recites the blessing “to put on.” See Peri Megadim, who cites additional authorities who maintain that even over the tefillin of the arm alone one recites both blessings. The later authorities, however, agree that the practical halachah is that over the tefillin of the arm one recites only the blessing “to put on,” especially since many early authorities maintain that even when putting on both tefillin, one recites only a single blessing. If he put on both tefillin without reciting a blessing, the law is that he may recite the blessing so long as they remain upon him. If he first recited the blessing for the tefillin of the head and afterward for the tefillin of the arm, he does not recite another blessing. Initially, however, even if he already put on the tefillin of the arm without a blessing, he should recite the blessing over it before putting on the tefillin of the head.
