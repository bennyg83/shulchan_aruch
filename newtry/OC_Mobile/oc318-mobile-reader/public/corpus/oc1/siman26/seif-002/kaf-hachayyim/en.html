2) There: He puts on the one he has and recites the blessing upon it, etc. If he has only one Rashi tefillah and the other is Rabbeinu Tam, he should put them on, because the arm- and head-tefillin do not prevent fulfillment of one another. If the arm-tefillin follows Rabbeinu Tam and the head-tefillin follows Rashi, he first puts on Rabbeinu Tam's arm-tefillin without a blessing, then puts on Rashi's head-tefillin and recites the blessing 'concerning the mitzvah of tefillin.' So writes the Beit Oved Siddur, laws of putting on tefillin, section 34. We likewise wrote in our humble work Kol Yaakov, siman 34, section 13, because this is the primary ruling according to Arizal, unlike Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 2; see our discussion there.
