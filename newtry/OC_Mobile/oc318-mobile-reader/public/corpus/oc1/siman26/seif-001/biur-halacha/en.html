He dons what he has, etc. Even head alone only — do not worry "between your eyes there shall be two"; even at Shema/tefillah may wear one — not failing "bind them" when he cannot don both. If expecting second before Shema time and he wears only for Shema/tefillah — wait for second, do not recite Shema with one. If his way is to wear all day and now wants to avoid bittul — may don immediately and bless; for Shema — wait for second.

And has no constraint — Mechaber speaks lechatchilah permitting one; bedi'eved even if intentional only one — fulfilled that mitzvah [Beit Yosef].
