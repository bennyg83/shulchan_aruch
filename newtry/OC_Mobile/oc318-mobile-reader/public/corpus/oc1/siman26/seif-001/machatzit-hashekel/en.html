(s"k 1) Any constraint, etc. Rosh on Menachot — arm/head not me'akevot; R' Chananel: only when has both; if only one — me'akev lest neglect buying missing — later R' Chananel retracted: always not me'akevot. Rosh challenged: if has both why only one? — therefore onus case (travel). "As long as between your eyes two" — see siman 28:2. Magen Avraham: whence Rosh allows head alone in light onus? — Rosh means not cancel arm forever but don later.

Light onus we permit. Do not ask "between your eyes two" — Rosh did not waive that in light onus; his view: not severe prohibition (Nekudot Yosef — if encountered head first, don head not to pass over mitzvah though violates "two between eyes" — light prohibition; we don't hold so, 25:16, but proves it's light).

And see siman 38:9 — do not say travel exempts like groom's preoccupation; there 38:9 distraction different — must settle mind (Magen Avraham 12).
