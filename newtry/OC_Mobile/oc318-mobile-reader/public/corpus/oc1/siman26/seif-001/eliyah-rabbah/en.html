Wearing implies even right hand not used if cannot use left (weaker than itur yad, siman 27:7). Mekhilta Bo — Abba Yosei: right called "yad" — "Israel stretched out yad yeminam"; "al yadekha" — place gid on right when cannot on left. Seems like "others" (Menachot 36 — yadekha includes gid); halachah like first tanna — yadekha is left (Magen Avraham on Sifrei Va'etchanan; Beit Ya'akov disputes — "ein lo bahen" different, yadekha as he wrote; Tosafot — no dispute). Mekhilta needs explanation — why "what to learn yadekha" from "yad means right"? Rather: really drasha on left as in Shas; "what to learn yadekha" — yad implies right, therefore "bring gid on right"; hence "yad." In our Shas — gid from yadekha not yadekh; even chatzitzah — place on that left hand; understand. Rama teshuvah 122 — no left hand — place on right? Extended not to don at all; omitted above; Beit Ya'akov 149 — Beit Yosef 591, Ittur — goes on right (not itur yad). Maharam/Rashba citations misquoted — see Maharam Rothenburg 12, Rashba 1000.
