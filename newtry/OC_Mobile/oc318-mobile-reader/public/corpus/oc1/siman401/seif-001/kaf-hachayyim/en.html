1) [Se'if 1] He establishes his Shabbos residence at his location. This applies if he is outside the city's techum. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1. If he slept within the city's techum and intended to enter the city, however, he is regarded as one of the city's residents. He may walk throughout the city and two thousand amos beyond it in every direction, as explained by Rambam in chapter 27, halachah 10.
