(1) [Seif 1] Afterwards they pour the third cup. And one requires rinsing and washing; and even one who was not careful all year — if he saw the cup is clean, nevertheless on this night he should be extra careful to beautify the mitzvah — Maharshal in responsa siman 88, Bach, Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz, Eliyah Rabbah letter 1. However the Taz s.k. 1 wrote in the name of Maharshal: even one who is not careful all year to see if the cup is clean, etc., and it implies he meant that even one not careful all year to see if it is clean, on this night he should be extra careful to look; and if it is clean he need not rinsing and washing — so too Chok Yaakov letter 1 and so too some Acharonim; and it is possible they had a different version in Maharshal; but in the Maharshal responsa in our hands it is as Bach and Knesset HaGedolah and Eliyah Rabbah wrote, that it implies even if it is clean on this night he should be extra careful and do rinsing and washing — see Eliyah Rabbah. And see above siman 483 seif 1 and in our words there letter 4 that we wrote per Zohar and Tikkunim the cup of Birkas Hamazon requires rinsing and washing even if it is clean — see Eliyah Rabbah. And also see above siman 473 letter 1 and siman 474 letter 2.
