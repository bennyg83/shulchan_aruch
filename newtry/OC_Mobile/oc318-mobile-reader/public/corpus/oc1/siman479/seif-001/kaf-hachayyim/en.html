1) [Se'if 1] Afterward, the third cup is poured. It requires rinsing inside and washing outside. Even one who throughout the year is not careful to do so after seeing that the cup is clean should be especially careful on this night in order to beautify the mitzvah. Responsa Maharshal, siman 88; Bach; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur; Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. Taz, subsection 1, however, cites Maharshal as saying, 'Even one who throughout the year is not careful to see whether the cup is clean,' and so forth. This implies that even one who is not careful throughout the year should be especially careful on this night to inspect it, but if it is clean, rinsing and washing are unnecessary. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1, writes likewise, as do some later authorities. It is possible that they had a different version of Maharshal. In the Responsa Maharshal available to us, however, the text accords with Bach, Knesses HaGedolah, and Olas Shabbos, implying that on this night one should be especially careful to rinse and wash it even when clean. See there.
