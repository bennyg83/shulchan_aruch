(א) And he drinks it while reclining — and if he did not drink while reclining, it is explained in siman 472. Maharshal wrote: one requires rinsing and washing — this is the view of all Acharonim; and even one not careful all year to see if the cup is clean, nevertheless on this night he should be careful for hidur mitzvah.

(ב) And he does not drink wine, etc. — the reason in Yerushalmi is lest he become intoxicated and be unable to complete the Hallel; and they challenged: but is he not already intoxicated? They answer: wine during the meal does not intoxicate. Rashbam wrote; and it should be said that the same applies to wine before the meal that does not intoxicate — from this it appears to me he may not drink mead called ma'ad either, since it too intoxicates; but other beverages that do not intoxicate are permitted.

(ג) It is a mitzvah to respond after zimmun — Tur wrote on this, and so it was stated in Midrash Tehillim: one who recites Hallel needs three, that one says to two Hodu; and Avudraham wrote they do not practice thus; and if because of Hodu, three suffice with his wife and sons who reached education — end. Explanation: Rosh wrote at the end of chapter Afikoman there is proof Birkas Hamazon requires a cup specifically with three — for if even alone, why did they say obligation of four cups on Pesach night? The Birkas Hamazon cup every day requires a cup — rather certainly with three specifically it requires a cup, and on Pesach one must return after three to have four cups; afterward Rosh rejected this proof, for nevertheless there are four cups together on this day; therefore Tur wrote in his name and the world did not practice thus. Explanation: from Birkas Hamazon side, even alone needs a cup; and also for Hodu his wife and sons suffice; and it appears what Tur wrote mitzvah to return after zimmun — he did not intend at all for Birkas Hamazon, for he already ruled in siman 482 that Birkas Hamazon requires a cup even alone; rather certainly from Hodu alone he said so, as he concludes in the name of Midrash Tehillim — that they call him to zimmun, and the one who comes to zimmun should not eat, drink, or assist in blessing zimmun, for he already blessed; only hear Hodu and answer with them and return home; and this too is Shulchan Aruch's intent writing mitzvah to return after zimmun — zimmun for Hodu; and as Rama wrote on this that the greatest says Hodu; and although Rosh wrote wife and sons suffice, nevertheless it is better with three obligated men for this; but for Birkas Hamazon zimmun it is not proper to call one from another house to bless with him if he did not eat here — for on what will he assist to bless? And even if he wishes to drink here to join zimmun it is not proper: if he ate afikoman at home he may not drink here; if he did not eat afikoman at home it will be forbidden afterward to eat afikoman at home since he already blessed — therefore the practical case is only that he not eat afikoman at home but eat it here, and he need not bless motzi here a second time as siman 484 seif 2; and see siman 484; and this is not forbidden because of two groups, for this is only once he began afikoman; but from the mitzvah side of eating at the meal there is no prohibition of places, as Rama in siman 478 — changing before this is not an interruption, and the same for changing place; after he eats afikoman here he blesses with them and remains until completing the seder; if he wishes he may return home after Birkas Hamazon and say Hallel with them and drink the fourth cup — if he has three at home besides himself for zimmun; if not, certainly forbidden to ruin his zimmun; and per what Maharshal wrote, the complaint of Maharshal and Maharal of Prague is settled, who bring that he would need to bless again at home — they do not now practice eating afikoman in another house; also not required for Birkas Hamazon zimmun, for we practice cup even alone; and if for Hodu, Rosh already wrote wife and sons suffice; and trouble to call from another house is not required unless he has at another table an obligated man — join him for Hodu only — and no more; so it appears correct per our custom.
