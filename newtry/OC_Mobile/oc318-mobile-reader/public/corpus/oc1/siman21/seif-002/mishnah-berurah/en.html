(9) That wore out — See Pri Megadim, who wrote that a tallis used for a mitzvah whose tzitzis tore should not be made into pants, for that is degrading use; and even a tallit katan is forbidden.

(10) And one may not, etc. — So the text should read [Ateres Rosh; and so implied by Gra].

(11) To wipe oneself, etc. — meaning, even to wipe mud off one's foot [as is clear from Ran on chapter 3 of Shabbos].

(12) Degrading use — but non-degrading use is permitted. While tzitzis are still on it, if one may use the tallis for non-degrading purposes: Olas Tamid permits, holding that the Mechaber forbids only the tzitzis in se'if 1, whereas Taz forbids the tallis too — Pri Megadim agreed, and wrote that a tallis of prayer designated for that purpose is stricter like tzitzis. But Turei Zahav holds plainly that any non-degrading use is permitted even while tzitzis are on it, for it is ordinary clothing and one may sleep in it at night, as below.

(13) Throw them away — meaning, in the trash; and even those who forbid the tzitzis themselves agree to this.
