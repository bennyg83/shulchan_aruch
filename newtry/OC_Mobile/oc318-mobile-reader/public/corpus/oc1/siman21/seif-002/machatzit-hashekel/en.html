(Seif katan 2) Not to, etc. The writings of the Arizal, etc. Taz writes that the permission to enter the privy while wearing tzitzis applies specifically to a tallis katan. A tallis gadol, whose initial making was for the sake of the mitzvah, is forbidden. The same applies to a kittel; see there. “He had a mezuzah, etc.” The Gemara likewise implies this. Meaning, one should not say that David was particular only about a mitzvah upon his body, unlike mezuzah, which is not upon his body. The Gemara implies that even if he had had a mezuzah, that would have sufficed for David.
Seven each day, etc. Meaning, seven mitzvos: tefillin of the hand and tefillin of the head are two mitzvos, the four tzitzis are four mitzvos, and mezuzah is the seventh mitzvah.
This implies that he was naked of all of them, even of mezuzah, because the Gemara brings the account that when he entered the bathhouse, etc., in connection with the statement “seven each day,” which includes mezuzah.
