(Se'if katan 2) "Not to sleep," etc. In the writings, etc. Taz wrote: that which is permitted to enter the bathroom wearing tzitzis applies specifically to a tallit katan; but a large tallit whose making was begun for the sake of a mitzvah is forbidden — and the same for a kittel. "He had a mezuzah," etc. — as is clear in the Gemara, etc. Meaning: do not say that David was particular only about a mitzvah that is on his body, unlike mezuzah which is not on his body. And therefore it implies in the Gemara that even if he had a mezuzah, it would have sufficed for David.

"Seven each day," etc. Meaning: seven mitzvos — namely tefillin of the arm and of the head, two mitzvos; and four tzitzis, four mitzvos; and mezuzah, the seventh mitzvah.

It implies he was naked of all of them, even mezuzah — since the Gemara brought: when he entered the bathhouse, etc. — behold, "seven each day," etc. — mezuzah is included among them.
