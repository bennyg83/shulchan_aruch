But according to what is stated in se'if 2, "tallisos used for a mitzvah — one separates from them," etc. — all the more so one must be careful with tzitzis not to throw them in the trash. Birkei Yosef, letter 1, and Levushei Serad on Taz, se'if katan 3, wrote: that which is permitted here to throw tzitzis in a degrading place applies specifically to tzitzis of a [regular] garment; but tzitzis of a tallit used for a mitzvah — it is obvious the tzitzis are not less than the tallit itself, rather they are even more important — certainly. Pischei Olam brought this, letter 2. We find that here it deals with tzitzis of a garment, as Levushei Serad wrote. That one may throw them in the trash is the law, as Birkei Yosef wrote. But according to se'if 2, worn tallisos used for a mitzvah — one separates from them — all the more so one must be careful with tzitzis, even of a garment, not to throw them in the trash; and all the more so tzitzis of a tallit made for a mitzvah, such as ours which we wear a tallit in order to fulfill the mitzvah of tzitzis — the tzitzis are not less than the tallit, as stated in se'if 2, "one separates," etc.
