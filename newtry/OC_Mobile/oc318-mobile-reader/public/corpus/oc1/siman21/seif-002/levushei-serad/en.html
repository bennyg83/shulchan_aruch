(There) "Even on Chol haMoed — here you have it." Meaning: even on Chol haMoed it is forbidden when it fell, even though neither reason alone applies — there is no muktzeh on Chol haMoed, and there is no disrespect when it fell. Nevertheless, the combination of reasons forbids, because there is somewhat of muktzeh on Chol haMoed since it was already set aside from Yom Tov, and there is also somewhat of disrespect when it fell; therefore, even though one reason alone is insufficient to forbid, the combination of both forbids.
