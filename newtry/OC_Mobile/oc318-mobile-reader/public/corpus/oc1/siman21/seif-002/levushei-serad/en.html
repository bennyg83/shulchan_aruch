Ibid. Even on Chol HaMoed, etc. Meaning, even on Chol HaMoed it is forbidden when it fell, although neither reason applies fully. There is no muktzeh on Chol HaMoed, and when it fell there is no direct degradation. Nevertheless, the combination of the two considerations creates a prohibition. Some aspect of muktzeh remains on Chol HaMoed because it had already been set aside from Yom Tov, and some aspect of degradation remains even after it fell. Although neither reason alone would suffice, together they prohibit it.
