[4] Not to sleep, etc. The later kabbalists wrote that many are punctilious to sleep in tzitzis so as not to be without the mitzvah of tzitzis even for a single moment — even when sleeping with one's wife, and it is permitted to use one's bed while wearing it. Magen Avraham challenged the proof of Mahari Luria: David said, when they saw him naked in the bath, "Woe to me that I am naked of mitzvos" — but he did not say this at night when he lay down; he challenged: at night he had a mezuzah — see there. In my humble opinion, his proof is from the nights of Sukkos, when one must sleep in a sukkah and it is exempt from mezuzah, as stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 286; however, this is not proof, for one could say the sukkah itself is the mitzvah. Rather it seems to me his proof is: when one sleeps with his wife throughout the year he is exempt from mezuzah; but Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 286 that if a door separates, everyone agrees he is obligated in mezuzah. It is possible that since a door separates, it is as if he is naked of the mitzvah.
