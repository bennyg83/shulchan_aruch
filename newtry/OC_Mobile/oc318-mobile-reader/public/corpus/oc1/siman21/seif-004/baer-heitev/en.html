His tzitzis. Concerning him it is said, “I shall sweep it with the broom of destruction.” Matzas Shimurim explains at length that the prohibition is not because of degradation of the mitzvah, but because the tzitzis may be stepped upon and severed. Magen Avraham questions this from siman 23, where dragging them over graves is forbidden because of mocking the deceased, implying that elsewhere dragging is permitted. He likewise asks from the account of Ben Tzitzis HaKeses; see there. Yad Aharon resolves this according to Taz's distinction, cited above in seif katan 3 regarding entering the privy. The same distinction applies here. Regarding a tallis gadol designated solely for prayer, it is improper and undignified for its tzitzis to drag upon the ground. The case of a cemetery and that of Ben Tzitzis HaKeses concern tzitzis attached to ordinary garments. See Shiurei Knesses HaGedolah.
