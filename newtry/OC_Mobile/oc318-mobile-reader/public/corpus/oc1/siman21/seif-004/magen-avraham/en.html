Not to drag. Concerning him it is said, “I shall sweep them with the broom of destruction.” This is difficult. Siman 23 states that if they drag over graves it is forbidden because of mocking the deceased, implying that elsewhere dragging is not prohibited. Furthermore, the account of Ben Tzitzis HaKeses, whose tzitzis dragged over cushions and bedding, appears to imply that other people's tzitzis dragged over the ground. The same is implied by the end of siman 10; see there. It appears to me that the graves were raised above the ground, and Rabbi Yonasan's tzitzis dragged over them. Rabbi Yochanan prohibited this because of mocking the deceased. There was no degradation of the mitzvah, because they dragged only upon an elevated place. Regarding Ben Tzitzis HaKeses, one may reject the inference and say that other people's tzitzis did not drag upon the ground at all. Alternatively, only dragging while standing still is degrading, because it appears as though he is sweeping the house with them. If they drag incidentally while he walks, there is less concern.
