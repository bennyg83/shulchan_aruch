Debsi, 23:20, that a dam is dragged on the graves, it is forbidden because it mocks the Resh, which means that there is no prohibition in the grave, and also, apparently, from Damari, ben Tzizit Hessat, whose tassels were dragged by Gerim and Kassut, which apparently means that other people's tassels were dragged on the land, etc. The graves were high above the ground and were Rabbi Yonatan's tufts. They were dragged over them and forbidden because they mocked the Resh, but because they were despicable from Mt. Licha, since they cannot be dragged. But in a high place and on the back of the tsitzit of the hessat, one should postpone the dishel after it. They would not be dragged on the ground at all.
