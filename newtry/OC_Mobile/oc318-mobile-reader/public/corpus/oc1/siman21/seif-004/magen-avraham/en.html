That one not drag them. Of this it is said, "and I will sweep it with the broom of destruction" [Agur]. He asks: siman 23 states that if dragged over graves it is forbidden because of "mocking the poor," which implies there is otherwise no prohibition in dragging. It also seems from "Ben Tzitzis the pillow-cover" that his tzitzis dragged over pillows and cushions — implying others' tzitzis dragged on the ground — as stated at the end of siman 10. It seems the graves were raised above the ground and R' Yonasan's tzitzis dragged over them; they forbade because of mocking the poor, but not bizuy mitzvah, since they drag only over a raised place. Regarding Ben Tzitzis the pillow-cover, one may answer others' tzitzis did not drag on the ground at all; or, dragging while dressing is disrespectful as if honoring the house with them, but if they drag while walking there is no concern.
