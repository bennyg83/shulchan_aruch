(14) To the bathroom — specifically the small four-cornered garment worn all day; but tallisos used for a mitzvah, designated only for prayer — it is not proper to enter the bathroom wearing them. Urinating in them is permitted. Those who go on Yom Kippur to relieve themselves while wearing a kittel must remove the kittel, since that garment is designated only for prayer.

(15) Not to sleep — In the writings of the Ari z"l it is written kabbalistically that one should sleep at night in a tallit katan.

(16) To a non-Jewish launderer to wash — the tallis with tzitzis: rather, untie the tzitzis and then launder them.

(17) To sleep in them — and likewise to give to a launderer; however, some are stringent and do as stated above.
