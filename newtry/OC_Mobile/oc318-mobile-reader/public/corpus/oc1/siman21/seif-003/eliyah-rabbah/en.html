[3] It is permitted to enter, etc. Taz wrote: it seems this applies specifically to a garment made for regular wear. But our tallisos used for a mitzvah, which are only for prayer — it is not proper to enter the bathroom wearing them, even though there is no proof for prohibition; nevertheless, one does not fall short of derech eretz, for we say: "Garments in which one cooked a pot for one's master — one should not pour a cup with them." Likewise those who go on Yom Kippur to relieve themselves must remove the kittel, for that garment is designated only for prayer — so wrote Taz, end quote. I found in Sefer Chassidim, siman 997, as follows: Saul, when he relieved himself, did not have a head-covering on him; therefore when people relieve themselves in the bathroom they remove the head-covering, and likewise a tallit that has tzitzis on it — this requires study.
