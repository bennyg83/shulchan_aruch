It is permitted to enter the privy while wearing tzitzis. Gloss: All the more so, it is permitted to sleep while wearing them. Some write that the custom is not to sleep in a tallis that has tzitzis, and also not to give it to a non-Jewish launderer for washing. All this is so that mitzvos should not become degraded in one's eyes. Nevertheless, the custom is lenient and permits sleeping in them. Kol Bo.
