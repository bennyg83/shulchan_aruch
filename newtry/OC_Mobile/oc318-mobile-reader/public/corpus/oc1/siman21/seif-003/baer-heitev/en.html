Into the privy. Taz writes that this applies specifically to a four-cornered garment worn beneath one's clothing. Regarding talleisim designated for the mitzvah, however, it is improper to enter the privy while wearing them. Likewise, those who go to relieve themselves on Yom Kippur while wearing a kittel must remove it, since that garment is designated solely for prayer. See there.
Not to sleep. The writings of the Arizal state, based upon Kabbalah, that one should sleep at night wearing a tallis katan. See Eliyah Rabbah for his resolution of Magen Avraham's question against the proof of the Arizal.
