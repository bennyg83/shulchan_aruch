To the bathroom. Taz wrote: this applies specifically to the four-cornered garment worn under one's clothes; but a tallit used for a mitzvah — it is not proper to enter the bathroom wearing it. Likewise, those who go on Yom Kippur to relieve themselves while wearing a kittel must remove the kittel, since that garment is designated only for prayer.

Not to sleep. In the writings of the Ari z"l, there is a kabbalistic secret that one should sleep at night in a tallit katan. See Eliyahu Rabbah for how he resolves Magen Avraham's difficulty on the Ari's proofs.
