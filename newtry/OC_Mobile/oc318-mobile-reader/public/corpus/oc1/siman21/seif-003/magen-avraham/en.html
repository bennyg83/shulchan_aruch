Not to sleep. Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: we never heard of anyone who was particular about this. The Ari's writings say kabbalistically one should sleep at night in a tallit katan. He brings from Menachos 43a: when David saw himself naked in the bathhouse he said, "Woe to me that I am naked of mitzvos." Why did he not say this at night when he was also naked? Rather, because he was lying in a tallit katan — his words are kabbalah and we accept them; but halachically there is an answer: at night he in any case had a mezuzah at his doorway, unlike the bathhouse — as is clear in the Gemara: "Israel is beloved, for the Holy One surrounded them with mitzvos — tefillin, tzitzis, and mezuzah — about which David said, 'Seven each day I praise You.'" When David entered the bathhouse and saw himself naked, etc. — it implies he was naked of all of them, even mezuzah, as above. See siman 18, se'if 1.
