Not to sleep. Beit Yosef writes that we have never heard of anyone who was particular about this. The writings of the Arizal state, based upon Kabbalah, that one should sleep at night wearing a tallis katan. They bring proof from the statement in the fourth chapter of Menachos: when David saw himself naked in the bathhouse, he said, “Woe to me, for I am naked of mitzvos.” Why did he not say the same at night, when he was likewise naked? It must be that he slept wearing a tallis katan. These are words of Kabbalah, and we accept them. As a matter of halachic reasoning, however, one may answer that at night he at least had a mezuzah at his doorway, unlike in the bathhouse. The Gemara likewise implies this. It states: “The Jewish people are beloved, for HaKadosh Baruch Hu surrounded them with mitzvos: tefillin, tzitzis, and mezuzah. Concerning them David said, ‘Seven times each day I praise You.’ When David entered the bathhouse and saw himself naked, etc.” This implies that he was naked of all of them, including mezuzah. See siman 18, seif 1.
