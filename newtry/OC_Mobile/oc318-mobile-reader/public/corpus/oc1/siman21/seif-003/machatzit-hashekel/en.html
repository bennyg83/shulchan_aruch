(Se'if katan 3) "Not to drag," etc. As is clear from what Magen Avraham stated, siman 10 — meaning what Magen Avraham wrote there, se'if katan 13, in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah: he gave the reason that they were lenient not to place tzitzis on our long garments — for otherwise they would tear constantly and need repair constantly; one could say the Torah did not obligate this. It is also possible that the tzitzis were dragged on the ground and this caused prohibition.

And in our view, "the graves," etc. "And the tzitzis of R' Yonasan," etc. — meaning: this siman 23 is from the Gemara: R' Yochanan and R' Yonasan were walking in a cemetery, and R' Yonasan's tzitzis were dragged over the graves; R' Yochanan told him it is forbidden because of "mocking the poor." Regarding Ben Tzitzis, etc. — but it is still not settled what is stated in siman 10.
