(Seif katan 3) Not to, etc. The same is implied by what is written at the end of siman 10. Meaning, Magen Avraham there, seif katan 13, cites Beit Yosef's reason for the custom not to place tzitzis upon our long garments: if they had tzitzis, the strings would constantly break and require constant repair. One may say that the Torah did not obligate a person in such a case. According to Magen Avraham's present concern, however, another reason should suffice: the tzitzis would drag upon the ground and cause a prohibition.
