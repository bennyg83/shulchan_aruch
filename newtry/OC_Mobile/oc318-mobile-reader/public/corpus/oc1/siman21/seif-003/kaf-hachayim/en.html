(3) There: But as long as they are attached to the tallit it is forbidden to use them, etc. — even if he is not wearing it, since it stands ready to fulfill a mitzvah with it. Olas Tamid, letter 2; Taz. Pri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav, letter 1; Shitilei Zetim, letter 2; Pischei Olam, letter 3 — and not like Taz, se'if katan 1. Likewise it is forbidden to tie anything with tzitzis, even at night — even according to Rambam z"l in siman 18 — for it is comparable to shofar and lulav that stand ready for next year, which are forbidden for mundane use, as Beit Yosef wrote; and all the more so this. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 1; Pischei Teshuvah, letter 4.
