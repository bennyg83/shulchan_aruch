It is permitted. See Taz, who writes that a tallis designated for prayer, like ours, may not be brought into the privy. Only a tallis katan worn beneath one's clothing is permitted. This implies that if it is worn over one's clothing it is not permitted, though the custom is not careful about this. I did not understand what he writes regarding the Mechaber in seif 1. His words imply that according to the Mechaber one may use tzitzis even for a degrading purpose, while only the tallis is forbidden for a degrading use. Levush omitted Rama's gloss in seif 1 and cited the ruling concerning worn-out talleisim. It appears that Rama in his gloss is stringent and forbids even discarding them into refuse, although burial is unnecessary. Darkei Moshe in the name of Kol Bo implies that treating them disrespectfully is forbidden; see there.
