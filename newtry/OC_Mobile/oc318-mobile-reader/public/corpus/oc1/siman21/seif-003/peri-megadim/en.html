Permitted — see Taz, who wrote that a tallis designated for prayer like ours is forbidden to enter a bathroom; only a tallit katan under one's garments is permitted — implying above one's garments, no; but we do not practice thus. What the Mechaber wrote in se'if 1 I did not understand — for from his words it seems the Mechaber permits even degrading use of tzitzis, and forbids degrading use of tallis. The Levush omitted the Rama gloss in se'if 1 and brought "tallisos," etc.; and apparently the Rabbi in the Rama gloss was stringent — even to throw them in the trash is forbidden, only they do not require genizah; and in Rama's wording in the name of Kol Bo it implies not to practice disrespect toward them — forbidden — see there.
