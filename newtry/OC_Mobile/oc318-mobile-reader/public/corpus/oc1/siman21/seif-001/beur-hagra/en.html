Se'if 1, "that became detached," etc. Meaning: in the Gemara there it deals with this, as we explained — like a Chanukah lamp, sukkah, and [covering] blood in chapter 2 of Shabbos, and other mitzvos such as etrog and the like. We also say at the end of Rosh Hashanah: "But water of the feet is forbidden," etc. And in a baraisa it lists there sukkah, lulav, and shofar.

But as long as they are attached, etc. As stated in chapter 2 of Shabbos: their common factor is blood. See Sefer Tanya, 32b.

And some say, etc. For it is taught that they are thrown away on their own — the opposite of sacred objects of mitzvah use, which are buried. But in tractate Tzitzis we say: one may make them a shroud for the dead, or a goad for a donkey.

And there are those who are punctilious, etc. From that which we say, "since one mitzvah was already done with them," etc. — evidently one must treat them with honor. This is what he meant. See also siman 20, and Magen Avraham and Taz.
