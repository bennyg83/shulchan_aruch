The law of what to do with tzitzis that became detached and with old tallisos. Containing 4 se'ifim: Tzitzis strings that became detached may be thrown in the trash, because it is a mitzvah that has no inherent sanctity (i.e.,
tzitzis are a mitzvah only by virtue of being attached to a four-cornered garment). However, as long as they remain attached to a tallis, it is forbidden to use them — for example, to tie something with them or the like — because of bizuy mitzvah (disrespect to a mitzvah).<small>[Some say that even after they become detached, one should not treat them disrespectfully by throwing them in a degrading place, but they do not require genizah (ritual burial) (Kol Bo, Laws of Shabbat). Some are punctilious to bury them, and one who is stringent and careful in mitzvos — a blessing shall come upon him (Maharil, Laws of Tzitzis) (and see below, siman 664, se'ifim 8–9).]</small>[ ']:
