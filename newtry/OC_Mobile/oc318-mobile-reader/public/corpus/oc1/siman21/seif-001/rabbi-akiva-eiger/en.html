Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2 Rather, because he was lying in a tallit katan. Accordingly it is proven that the view of the Rosh, siman 18, is that day garments at night one is obligated. For if per the Rambam one is exempt — if so, a man who was lying at night in a tallit katan — behold, nevertheless he is naked of mitzvos, for he has no fulfillment of the mitzvah of tzitzis since he is exempt, and tzitzis at night [do not apply].
