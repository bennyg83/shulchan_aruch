Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. He had a mezuzah. See Mordechai at the end of Hilchos Mezuzah, cited by Beit Yosef in Yoreh Deah, siman 286, that if a husband and wife sleep in a room, the room is exempt from mezuzah. Rama cites this in his gloss there, seif 2. The words of the Arizal are therefore correct. Accordingly, there is some support for what Rabbi Yaakov Emden writes in his siddur, that one should have marital relations while wearing a tallis katan. This proof may be rejected, however, because even without that he was not devoid of a mitzvah, since the marital obligation itself is a mitzvah. The case cannot concern one who was merely lying in that room without relations while there was no mezuzah. He must therefore have been lying there wearing a tallis katan. Consider this carefully.
