In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2: He had a mezuzah. N.B. See Mordechai at the end of the laws of mezuzah, brought in Beit Yosef, Yoreh De'ah siman 286: in a room where a man sleeps with his wife, he is exempt from mezuzah; Rama brought this in his gloss on Shulchan Aruch there, se'if 2 — and the words of the Ari z"l are correct; this requires study. Accordingly, it seems somewhat as R' Yehudah Eibeschutz wrote in his siddur, that one may use one's bed while wearing a tallit katan. One may reject this: in any case he would not be "naked" from mitzvos, for marital relations is itself a mitzvah. And one cannot apply this when he was lying in that room without marital relations and there was no mezuzah — therefore he was lying in a tallit katan; consider this carefully.
