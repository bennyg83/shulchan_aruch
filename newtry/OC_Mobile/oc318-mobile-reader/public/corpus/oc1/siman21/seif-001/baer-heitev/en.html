Taz writes that the same applies to s'chach from a sukkah: it is proper not to use it afterward for something that is not respectful to the mitzvah that was performed with it. It is permitted to use the tallis after removing its tzitzis, provided that the use is not degrading. This is obvious. Responsa Rama, siman 9, writes that it is forbidden to make a tallis for a mitzvah from ornaments used by idolatrous priests, or to purchase from them beams and stones for building a synagogue. It is, however, permitted to wear a garment with which non-Jews adorn themselves.
