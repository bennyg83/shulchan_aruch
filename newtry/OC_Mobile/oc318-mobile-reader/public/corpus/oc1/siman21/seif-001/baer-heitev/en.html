That became detached. The same law applies as stated earlier when one attached them to a garment — mere designation is not a [halachically significant] matter.

To the trash. Maharil would place them inside a book as a marker, or use them for some mitzvah purpose. Taz wrote similarly regarding sukkah sechach — it is proper not to use it for a purpose that is not in honor of the mitzvah that has passed. (It is permitted to use a tallit after untying the tzitzit from it, provided the use is not degrading — this is straightforward. Rama wrote in a responsum, siman 9: It is forbidden to make a tallit used for a mitzvah from ornaments of pagan priests, or to buy from them beams and stones to build a synagogue; but it is permitted to wear clothing that they adorn themselves with.)
