(1) [Se'if 1] Tzitzis strings that became detached, etc. Olas Tamid wrote, letter 1: "The same law applies as before when one attached them to a garment — mere designation is not a matter." Ma'amar Mordechai wrote on this, letter 1: his words are not precise — it does not come from "the same law," but from an a fortiori: even regarding something that has an element of sanctity, designation is not a matter and it is permitted, as below siman 42 and in many places. Here, regarding strings that became detached, because they contain no sanctity, the a fortiori applies that they are only objects of mitzvah use, etc. Nevertheless, one should be stringent not to treat them degradingly, since they were spun and twisted for the sake of tzitzis. Likewise if one placed tzitzis on a tallit and has not yet worn it, one should be stringent not to treat them degradingly — so wrote the Acharonim. See Sha'arei Teshuvah, letter 1, and Sdei Chemed, Kol Yaakov on siman 42, letter 4.
