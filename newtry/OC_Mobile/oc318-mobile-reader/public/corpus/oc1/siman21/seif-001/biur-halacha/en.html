But as long as they are attached, etc. — See Mishnah Berurah; this is from Beit Yosef. Olas Tamid and Ateres Rosh brought his words and Pri Megadim agreed with them — not like Taz, who wrote that everyone agrees it is permitted. See below, siman 638, se'if katan 2 and Magen Avraham, regarding a sukkah built year after year: his words there imply that throughout the year, even when Sukkos arrives, as long as one does not sit in it, it has only a mere designation — and it is plainly evident that he holds like Taz, that it is still permitted to use it, and this is not bizuy mitzvah, unlike Beit Yosef. See Sha'arei Teshuvah, who cites Birkei Yosef in the name of Mahari Molcho, who also forbids there; but he rejected his words based on Magen Avraham. It is surprising he did not mention that Beit Yosef would also hold like him; one may only permit there because it is as if one stipulated that he is not separating from it, as Sha'arei Teshuvah wrote. One may also say that Magen Avraham distinguishes: tzitzis stands ready every day to fulfill the mitzvah with it, therefore even when removed from the body the mitzvah is not nullified from the garment — unlike a sukkah after the seven days of the festival, when its mitzvah is nullified from it until the next year; therefore even if one's intent is upon it, it has only mere designation. Accordingly, at least regarding lulav and shofar, Magen Avraham would also disagree with Beit Yosef.

To tie, etc. — See Pri Megadim, who wrote that the prohibition of bizuy mitzvah applies even to every rabbinic mitzvah; see there.
