The meals. For it appears in the Midrash on Beha'alosecha and in Megillas Taanis that they established them for feasting and joy because the work of the Mishkan was completed at this time; Darkei Moshe. So wrote most of the poskim. The Beis Yosef wrote in siman 420 that according to all opinions one provides the mourner's first meal on Chanukah; see Yoreh Deah, siman 378. The poor have the custom to go around to the doors on Chanukah, and Sefer Chanukas HaBayis wrote a reason for this.
