(1) (a) From the beginning of the eight days of Hanukkah - because in the Second Temple, when the kings of Reshaah reigned, they passed decrees on Israel and abolished their religion and did not allow them to engage in the Torah and Mitzvos, and they spread their hands with their money and their daughters and entered the temple and breached it and defiled the purity, and it was very difficult for Israel to face them and put a great pressure on them until the the Omnipresent of our ancestors had mercy on them and delivered them from their hand and saved and strengthened the Hasmonean sons of the high priests And Israel was slaughtered and saved from their hands, and the kingdom returned to Israel for more than two hundred years until the second destruction, and when Israel overcame their enemies and perished in 25 Bakhsh, it was and they entered the temple and did not find pure oil in the temple except for one jar that was placed with the seal of 23 and it was not to be lit except for one day and a miracle was done and they lit the candles of the campaign for 8 days until they crushed olives and brought out pure oil. And because of this, the sages of that generation decreed that these days that begin on the 25th of the 25th year should be days of joy and praise, and they light the candles in the evening at the doors of the houses every night of the eight nights to show and reveal the miracle.
