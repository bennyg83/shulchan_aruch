The poor youths have the custom to go around to the doors on Chanukah, and in Sefer Chanukas HaBayis he wrote a reason for this. The women. Specifically women, because the miracle was performed through them; Mateh Moshe.
While the lamps. In Mitzvas Chanukah he wrote: This means as long as the synagogue lamps are burning, which is until about midnight. End quote. In a place where they practiced a prohibition all day, one should not be lenient, because of permitted things that others practiced as forbidden, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 214. So ruled the Bach, Mitzvas Chanukah, and Tur. See what I wrote in siman 534, se'if 2. In Shiltei HaGibborim he wrote that their custom should be abolished, and it is enough on the first and eighth day. End quote. So too it is in Maharil.
