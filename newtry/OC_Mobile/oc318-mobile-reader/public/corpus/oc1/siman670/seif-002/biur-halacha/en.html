And the custom is to say songs and praises, etc. See Responsa Maharshal, siman 85, who wrote that it is proper that the joy be mixed and blended with the joy of Torah, and do not neglect your fixed learning. End quote. Because of our many sins, there are people who, instead of songs and praises with which it is proper to praise Hashem for the miracles He did for us, increase in card-playing. The holy books greatly protested this, and one who guards his soul should distance himself from it.
