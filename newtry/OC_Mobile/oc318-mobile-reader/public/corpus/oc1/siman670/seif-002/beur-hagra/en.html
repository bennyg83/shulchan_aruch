And some say that there is a bit, etc., because on those days, etc. In Pesikta Rabbasi, and it is brought in Yalkut Melachim, siman 184: So too you find regarding the work of the Mishkan. Rabbi Chanina said: on the twenty-fifth of Kislev the work of the Mishkan was completed, and it remained folded away until the first of Nisan, as written, 'On the first day of the first month you shall set up the Mishkan.' Yisrael were murmuring against Moshe, saying: why was it not set up immediately? Perhaps some defect occurred in it. But the Holy One, blessed be He, thought to delay the joy of the Mishkan until the month in which Yitzchak was born, as it is written, 'knead and make cakes,' and He said to him, 'I will surely return,' etc. Now Kislev lost, for the work had been completed in it. The Holy One, blessed be He, said: It is upon Me to repay. With what did the Holy One, blessed be He, repay it? With the dedication of the Chashmona'im, etc. See Magen Avraham in the name of the Midrash on parashas Beha'alosecha and Megillas Taanis; see Midrash Rabbah, parashas Naso, chapter 13, 188a. So too the Yam Shel Shlomo ruled in chapter 7 of Bava Kamma, siman 37, and wrote that anything one does in order to give praise to the Omnipresent, or to publicize the miracle or the mitzvah, is all a mitzvah meal. A proof is from what is written there, that Rav came to the house of Yeshu'a HaBen; see Tosafos there. And we say in chapter Get Pashut that Rav did not benefit from an optional meal, and likewise from what the Gemara says, 'and they made them Yamim Tovim,' and likewise in Megillas Taanis, 'and they established them as Yamim Tovim,' etc. See there, where he discusses this at length. This is what he wrote: 'and the custom is so,' and then, etc.
