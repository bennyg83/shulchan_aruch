(S.k. 2) While, etc. That the synagogue lamps, etc. In the Beis Yosef he wrote the reason for the prohibition, and this is his language: so that they will have a recognition that it is forbidden to use their light. End quote. According to this, it would have been enough to forbid labor as long as the lamps in the house are burning, and so wrote Sefer Eliyah Rabbah. One must say that they added a stringency upon a stringency.
Because of things that are permitted, etc. Even with a matter that has no aspect of prohibition in itself, to say that they decreed this because of that. So wrote the Beis Yosef according to the view of the Tur.
And see what I wrote in siman 134; so it should read. And in Shiltei HaGibborim in Hagahos Mordechai, chapter Bameh Madlikin.
That one should abolish, etc. And it is enough, etc., for the Sages distanced idleness because of boredom and immorality. It is enough on the first day and on the eighth day, like the law of Torah regarding Pesach and Sukkos, where labor is forbidden in a place where they are expert in establishing the month only on the first day and eighth day. So it is there in Shiltei HaGibborim, and so it is implied in Maharil.
However, in Shiltei HaGibborim it is explained that the prohibition applies only to women, and so wrote the Pri Chadash. But from Maharil it implies that men also practice a prohibition, and so it is implied in the Bach.
