2) Ibid. The eight days of Chanukah begin. Therefore, these days are called Chanukah, meaning that they rested on the 25th of Kislev. The word Chanukah is also an acronym for: eight lamps, and the halacha follows Beit Hillel, who say that on the first day one kindles one, and from then onward one continually adds. Abudraham. Shulchan Gavoah, os 3. One can further say that they are so called because the dedication of the Altar took place during those days, as written below in the gloss, and therefore they called them the days of Chanukah. One can further say that since the Temple service had ceased because of the enemy and was then restored, they called them the days of dedication.
