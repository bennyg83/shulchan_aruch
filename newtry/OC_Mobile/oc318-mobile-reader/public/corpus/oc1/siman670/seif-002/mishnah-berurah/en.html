(6) That they did not establish them for feasting and joy - But rather for praise and thanksgiving. The reason appears to be that here they did not establish joy as on Purim, because on Purim the decree was to destroy and kill the bodies, which is the negation of feasting and joy, but not the souls; even if, Heaven forbid, they had converted their religion, he would not have accepted them. Therefore, when the Holy One, blessed be He, saved them from him, they established that we praise and laud Him, blessed be He, also through feasting and joy. This is unlike the episode of Antiochus, who did not decree upon them to kill and destroy, but only troubles and forced conversions in order to make them convert their religion, as we say: to make them forget Your Torah and remove them from the laws of Your will. If Yisrael had submitted to them, to be conquered under their hand, pay them tax, and return to their faith, Heaven forbid, they would not have sought more. But the Holy One, blessed be He, strengthened the hand of Yisrael and they defeated them. Therefore they established them only for praise and thanksgiving, meaning: since they wanted to prevent us from this, to deny His religion, Heaven forbid, and with His help, blessed be He, they did not bring their plot to fruition and our hand prevailed, therefore we thank and praise Him for being our God and not abandoning us from His service; Levush.
(7) For on those days, etc. - That is to say, on the twenty-fifth of Kislev, as appears in the Midrash, that the work of the Mishkan was completed on the twenty-fifth of Kislev, but the Holy One, blessed be He, waited to erect it until Nisan, when Yitzchak was born. The Holy One, blessed be He, said: It is upon Me to repay Kislev, and He repaid it with the dedication of the House of the Chashmona'im. Also there, in the days of Antiochus, they made the Heichal impure and performed the dedication of the House during these eight days in the House.
(8) Songs - The same applies to the marriage of the daughter of a Torah scholar to an am ha'aretz: if they say songs, it is a mitzvah meal; Magen Avraham in the name of the Mordechai.
(9) Then it is a mitzvah meal - That is to say, through this combination. So ruled the Maharshal and the Bach like the 'some say.' The Maharshal wrote that anything one does in order to give praise to the Omnipresent, or to publicize the miracle or the mitzvah, is all a mitzvah meal.
(10) For Yehudis fed him - She was the daughter of Yochanan the Kohen Gadol, and there was a decree that every betrothed woman be first violated by the officer. She fed the head of the enemies cheese to intoxicate him, cut off his head, and they all fled.
