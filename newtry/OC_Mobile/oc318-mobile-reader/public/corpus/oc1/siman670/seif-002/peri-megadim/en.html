"That they do not." See Taz. The Mechaber omitted the phrase "all day," etc. Taz concludes that where women have the custom to refrain from melacha throughout the entire day, this may perhaps apply for the entire day. Maharil implies that even men refrain while the Chanukah lights are burning. On the first and last day, in places where the custom is to prohibit melacha throughout the entire day even for men, they should not be permitted to work. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 11, and Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. There is also a reason to refrain while the lights are burning, as a visible reminder that one may not derive benefit from their light. See Tur.
