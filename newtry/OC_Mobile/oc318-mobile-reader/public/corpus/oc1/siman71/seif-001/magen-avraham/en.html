Exempt from Keri'as Shema. And immediately when they begin to throw earth upon the deceased he recites and prays; and so did Maharil. And it seems that before chatzos he prays Shacharis as written in siman 89; but Birchos HaShachar it seems he should not say, since at the time of his obligation he was exempt, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 341 seif 2 regarding Havdalah.

They do not protest against him. The Levush challenged what is written regarding his married sister that they protest against him from reciting even though the husband is occupied with her burial; and it can be said that here it is different since he established another man in his place — it is as if he is occupied himself; and further it is possible that even regarding his married sister, if he wanted to recite they do not protest.
