Is exempt from Krias Shema. As soon as they begin casting earth upon the deceased, he should recite Krias Shema and pray; Mahari Weil acted accordingly. It appears to me that, before midday, he should pray Shacharis, as stated in siman 89. However, it appears to me that he should not recite Bircas HaShachar, since he was exempt when their obligation took effect, as stated in Yoreh Deah, siman 341, seif 2, regarding Havdalah.
We do not prevent him. The Levush asks: Why, in the case of his married sister, do we prevent him from reciting even though her husband is attending to her burial? One may answer that here it is different, because he himself appointed another person in his place, so it is as though he is attending to it himself. It is also possible that even regarding his married sister, if he wishes to recite, we do not prevent him.
