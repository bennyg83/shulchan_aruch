Turei Zahav, se'if katan 1: “In the name of the Yerushalmi.” According to the reason of preserving the deceased's honor, the rule also applies in this case, so that people should not say that the deceased is treated lightly in the eyes of the onen, who is eating meat. The Rosh's statement that the Sages did not distinguish between cases is merely an additional reinforcement of the ruling, though the matter still requires further resolution. According to the reason that someone must bear the deceased's burden, however, the rule does not apply in this case.
