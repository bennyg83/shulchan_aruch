In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1: Since at the time of the obligation. This requires study: What does a 'time of obligation' mean regarding blessings over benefit? Perhaps the statement refers to Baruch She'amar and Yishtabach, or to 'Who sanctifies Your Name publicly' according to those who recite it. The other blessings, however, apparently should be recited. See Machatzis HaShekel and Shaarei Teshuvah, who write that the three blessings beginning 'Who did not make me' and the Torah blessings may be recited throughout the day. See Kerem Shlomo, citing later authorities, for the order of his prayer as explained in every circumstance. All these sources imply that he recites Baruch She'amar and Yishtabach, unlike my revered father and teacher, of blessed memory. From the Gaon Chasam Sofer.
