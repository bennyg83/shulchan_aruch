A Mourner and Those Occupied With a Deceased Person are Exempt from the Recitation of the Sh'ma - containing 7
S'ifim One who's relative died, for which he is required to mourn for them: even if it is' not his responsibility to bury them, he is exempt from the recitation of the Sh'ma and from Tefilla [i.e. the Amidah]. And even if he wants to be strict with himself and to recite [it], he is not permitted [to do so]. But if one has someone who will take care of the needs of the burial for him, and he wants to be strict with himself to recite it, we do not protest against doing so [and see Yoreh Deah 341].[' ]:
