A mourner and those occupied with a deceased person are exempt from Keri'as Shema — containing 7
seifim. One whose relative died for whom he is obligated to mourn: even if he' is not responsible to bury him, he is exempt from Keri'as Shema and from tefillah. And even if he wants to be strict with himself and recite, he is not permitted. But if he has someone who will exert himself on his behalf in the needs of burial, and he wants to be strict with himself and recite — they do not protest against him [and see Yoreh De'ah 341].[' ]:
