(א) Exempt. And when the time for Minchah had already arrived before his relative died, and he was occupied with the burial until after Maariv — he raised in a responsum in Yoreh De'ah siman 42 that one cannot pray Maariv twice, for the obligation does not fall upon him except at the last moment of the day, whereas at the earlier time there was no obligation upon him; rather he fulfills his obligation when he prays Arvit once. Maharam and R' Chaim Esayel ruled that he is obligated, and he brought many proofs from the poskim, and likewise from the Gemara — from what we say: one who was lame on the first day [of the festival] and was healed on the second day [still] recites Havdalah.

(ב) They do not protest. See Tosafot in Yoreh De'ah siman 41 — when one prays under the mistaken impression that he is obligated, they are obligated to protest against him. And proof from the chapter HaPo'alim in Bava Kamma 91a — Rabba: they stole his ox in a tunnel; we returned it to them, they did not accept it because he had acquired it through monetary payment. And Tosafot challenged — are they not nevertheless obligated to pay when he seeks to fulfill his heavenly obligation? And one can explain that they thought they were obligated by law, and if they had known they were exempt by law they would not have returned it — so it seems.
