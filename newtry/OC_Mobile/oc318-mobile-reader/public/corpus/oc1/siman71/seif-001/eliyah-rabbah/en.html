[1] Exempt from Kriat Shema, etc. Immediately when they begin to throw earth upon the deceased he recites and prays Shacharit if it is before midnight, and not Birchas HaShachar since at the time of its obligation he was exempt (Magen Avraham). See siman 89 — I wrote there to pray Shacharit until half an hour after midnight; see Yoreh De'ah siman 341 that if its time passed it is not made up in the adjacent prayer; see end of siman 93.
