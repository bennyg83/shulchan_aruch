12. Is exempt. From Krias Shema, prayer, and all mitzvos, because he is occupied with the mitzvah of guarding the deceased from mice. Even aboard a ship, we are concerned for mice. [Gemara.] He may not be stringent with himself at that time, even if he sits beyond four amos from the deceased. [Magen Giborim.] See what we wrote above in siman 38.
