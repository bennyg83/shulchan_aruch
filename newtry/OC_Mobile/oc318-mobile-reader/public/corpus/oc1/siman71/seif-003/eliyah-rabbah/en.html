[3] And if he was on Shabbat, etc. — Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rabbenu Yerucham: if he does not wait at the boundary for burial needs he is obligated also at evening; and if he waits he is exempt even at evening. And on second Yom Tov its law is like weekday. And on first Yom Tov if he wishes to bury that day through gentiles its law is like weekday; if not like Shabbat — until here. And in my humble opinion here it deals where he waits at the boundary for burial; therefore exempt at evening. But in Yoreh De'ah 341 it deals where he does not wait — obligated also at evening; and Shulchan Arukh ruled like Rabbenu Yerucham.
