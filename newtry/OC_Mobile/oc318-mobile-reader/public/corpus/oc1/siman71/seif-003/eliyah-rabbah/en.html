One who guards the deceased, etc. Even on a ship, for we are concerned about mice, as stated in the Gemara.
Even if it is not his deceased, etc. Meaning, for if it were his deceased, even if he is not guarding him he is exempt, since burial is incumbent upon him, as above.
Exempt. Nevertheless, certainly whenever he need not exert himself at all, he should fulfill both obligations. From the principle: because of good, do not call it bad, Beit Yosef.
