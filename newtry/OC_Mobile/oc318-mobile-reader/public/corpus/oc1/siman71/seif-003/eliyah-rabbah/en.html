If he does not go to wait until dark, etc. So the Tur wrote in Yoreh Deah. See Beit Yosef, who challenges the words of the Rosh and Tur against one another, for in this siman he wrote, if he is able to go and wait until dark, implying that even if he does not actually go and wait, since he could go and wait he is exempt. The Bach answers that in Yoreh Deah, siman 341, it discusses other mitzvos, where he is exempt only when he needs to go and wait until dark; but here the Tur discusses Krias Shema, which requires great concentration, and therefore even if he does not need to go and wait until dark, he is exempt. He concludes that this is what we rule, unlike the Shulchan Aruch. This is astonishing, for when one can go and wait until dark, it is not the time for Krias Shema; even when one prays the Motzaei Shabbos prayer on Shabbos, nevertheless one does not read Krias Shema until nightfall, as the Tur himself wrote later in siman 293. So I found in the Derishah, who wrote that the Tur wrote this law regarding other mitzvos, not regarding Krias Shema, for it is not the time for Krias Shema. End quote. This is unlike the Taz, who ruled that an onen should read the evening Krias Shema while it is still day. Regarding the main difficulty, the Derishah wrote at length to resolve it, and this is the essence of his words: the Rosh and Tur hold that whenever people need to go and wait until dark for this deceased, even if he himself does not wait until dark, he is exempt, like a weekday case, where even if he has others to attend to the needs of burial, he is exempt. Therefore the Rosh and Tur wrote here, he can go and wait until dark, meaning even though he himself need not wait until dark. The same applies when no one at all waits until dark, but they ride quickly after Shabbos to bring what is needed for the deceased. Nevertheless, since the deceased's needs are not in the city and it is fitting to wait until dark, he is exempt because of the honor of the deceased. But when they do not need to wait until dark at all, for all the deceased's needs are in the city, then he is obligated in Krias Shema and all the mitzvos. Therefore the Tur wrote in Yoreh Deah, if he needs to wait until dark. End quote, in brief. Examine this well, for everything is resolved, and this is how it is proper to rule.
