(5) There. Exempt from Kriat Shema and tefillah. In a place where they practice that special bearers take out the deceased, and after the relatives were occupied with the needs of the burial they hand him over to them and they bury him — from when they hand him over to them the relatives are permitted in meat and wine even before they removed him from the house, since he is no longer imposed upon them, Yoreh De'ah siman 341 seif 3. And he wrote there the Birkei Yosef note 20 — that the same applies, that one may bless and pray, for just as it is permitted in the Yerushalmi in meat and wine in the chapter Ve'Eilu Megalchin in the ruling of Rabbenu Yerucham on one who died in captivity — that when they hand over to the bearers the aninus ceases; and so is proven from the words of the Maggid Mishneh at the beginning of the chapter Mi SheMeto; and so is implied from the Talmudim there; and likewise the rabbi Maharikash in his glosses — that for the other laws of mourning too his law is like meat and wine, so far. And likewise Mahari Ayash there in Sefer Shevet Yehudah, see there. And they brought Shulchan Aruch HaRav p. 281 side d and Sha'arei Teshuvah in this siman note 1. And likewise the Mishbetzos Zahav note 5, and these are his words: and it appears that the same applies — that it is permitted to pray and recite Kriat Shema without tefillin, so far.
