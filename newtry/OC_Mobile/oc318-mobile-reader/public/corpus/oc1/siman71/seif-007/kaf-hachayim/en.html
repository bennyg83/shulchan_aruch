And if he has someone who will exert himself on his behalf, etc. — they do not protest against him; and so Maran z"l ruled without qualification in Yoreh De'ah siman 341 seif 1 — for from what is written there "and some say he is not permitted," it is implied that per Maran's unqualified ruling he is permitted and they do not protest against him, see there. And so explained Olas Tamid and Eliyah Rabbah and Birkei Yosef note 2 on the words of Maran z"l; and Eliyah Rabbah and Birkei Yosef z"l wrote that in this there is no place for what the Lechem Chamudot challenged on Maran z"l at the beginning of the chapter Mi SheMeto, see there. And likewise Avodas HaGershuni siman 62, see there. And if so, per the rule transmitted to us that wherever Maran z"l rules without qualification and afterwards writes "and some say," his intention is to rule like the unqualified ruling as written in siman 13 note 7 — here too his intention is to rule as written here, that if he wants to be strict they do not protest against him if he has someone who will exert himself on his behalf; and likewise Eliyah Rabbah in the note mentioned. And therefore, since we accepted upon ourselves the reasoning of Maran z"l and Maran so held — one should not protest against one who wants to be strict; and so ruled Siddur Beit Yosef in the laws of Kriat Shema of Maariv note 8 and in the laws of aninus note 1. And further see what we wrote in siman 55 note 13.
