15. Krias Shema. The same applies to reciting Kaddish and other sacred matters. The reason is that doing so would mock the helpless deceased.
16. Or in a cemetery. See above, siman 45, seif katan 1 in the Mishnah Berurah, regarding entering a cemetery while wearing tefillin. The same law applies here.
17. He has not fulfilled his obligation. The Sages penalized him because he transgressed their words, even if he acted inadvertently. The Raavad disagrees and holds that he need not recite Krias Shema again, since the location was intrinsically fit for recitation and the prohibition applied only because it would mock the helpless deceased. He therefore fulfilled his obligation after the fact. As a matter of practical law regarding Krias Shema, the later authorities agree that one should take account of the Rambam's view and recite it again. If he recited a blessing or prayed, however, he does not repeat it. Regarding the blessings of Krias Shema, the Peri Megadim remains uncertain; see there for his reasoning.
