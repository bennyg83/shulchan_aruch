(15) Shema — and the same applies to saying Kaddish and other holy matters; and the reason is because of scoffing of the wicked.

(16) Or in a synagogue — see above in siman 45, note 1 in Mishna Berurah what I wrote there regarding entering it with tefillin; and the same applies to this matter.

(17) He has not fulfilled his obligation — the Sages penalized him since he transgressed their words, even if it was unwitting; and the Ra'avad disagrees and holds that he need not return and read, since that place was fit to read in, and only because of scoffing of the wicked he fulfilled his obligation after the fact. And for practical law regarding Shema, the later authorities agreed to heed the words of the Rambam to return and read; but if he blessed or prayed, he does not return. And regarding the blessings of Shema the Peri Megadim is uncertain — see there his reason.
