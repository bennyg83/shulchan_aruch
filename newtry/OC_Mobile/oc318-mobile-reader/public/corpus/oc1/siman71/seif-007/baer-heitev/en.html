The burials. See above siman 45 seif kaf alef from Shulchan Shelah. And see Magen Avraham on this siman seif daled.

He did not fulfill his obligation. Even if he was inadvertent, etc. And the Ra'avad disagrees and holds there is no returning to recite. And they ruled there regarding the blessings of Kriat Shema we hold like the lenient view, for we rule doubt regarding blessings leniently. And thus ruled Peri Chadash that for Kriat Shema one must be concerned for the Rambam's words, but if he blessed or prayed he does not return to pray and bless, for the essence follows the Ra'avad there. See Taz on this siman seif hei and above siman 45 seif alef, and understand carefully.
