He has not fulfilled his obligation. This applies even if he acted inadvertently, as is evident. The Raavad disagrees and maintains that he need not recite it again. The Olas Tamid rules that regarding the blessings of Krias Shema we follow the lenient view, because the established rule is that in a case of doubt concerning blessings, we rule leniently. The Peri Chadash likewise rules that regarding Krias Shema one should take the Rambam's view into account, but if he recited a blessing or prayed, he does not repeat the prayer or blessing, because the Raavad's view is primary.
