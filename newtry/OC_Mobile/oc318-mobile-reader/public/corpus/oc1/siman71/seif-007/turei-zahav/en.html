Or in a synagogue — meaning, or within four cubits of a grave; and therefore even after the fact he has not fulfilled his obligation. But in a synagogue outside four cubits of a grave — even though it is forbidden ab initio, as written in siman 45 per the Tur — nevertheless after the fact he certainly fulfills his obligation. And in the Tur the word "or" is missing here; and so it should read: or within four cubits of a deceased.
