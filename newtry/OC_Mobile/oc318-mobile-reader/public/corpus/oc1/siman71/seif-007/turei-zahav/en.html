Or in a cemetery. Meaning, or within four amos of a grave. Therefore, even after the fact he has not fulfilled his obligation. If, however, he was in a cemetery but more than four amos from a grave, although it is forbidden at the outset, as stated in siman 45 according to the Tur, nevertheless, after the fact he certainly fulfilled his obligation. In the Tur the word 'or' is missing here, and the text should read: 'or within four amos of a corpse.'
