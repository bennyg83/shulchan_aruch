[4] His law is like a weekday, etc. Meaning on the day of mourning, but on the night before it which is Motzei of the first day of Yom Tov — the Taz's view in Yoreh De'ah there is that his law is like Shabbos; and the Shach's view is like a weekday, and there I expanded; and the main view seems like the Taz, and so ruled Magen Avraham in siman 548.
