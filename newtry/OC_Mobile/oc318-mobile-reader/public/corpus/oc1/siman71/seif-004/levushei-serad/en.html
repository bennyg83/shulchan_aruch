(Magen Avraham note 1) Havdalah. It is true that there he ruled in Shulchan Aruch that one makes Havdalah after the burial until the third day; and if so the Magen Avraham brings proof to the contrary — but the intention of the Magen Avraham is because the Beit Yosef brings a dispute there — that the Rosh and his supporters hold he is exempt from Havdalah since at the time of his obligation he was exempt; and he brings proof from Chagigah — one who was lame on the first day of the festival and was healed on the second day is exempt from the pilgrimage offering even though the pilgrimage offering applies all seven [days] — nevertheless all of them are compensations of the first day; and since he was exempt on day one he is entirely exempt. So too regarding Havdalah. And other poskim disagree, and the reason is because here there are no compensations at all, rather the obligation of Havdalah extends from Motzei Shabbos until the third day and not in the category of compensations at all — for why does one pray until chatzos even though in the morning he was exempt? Not because of compensations, but because the time of tefillah extends from morning until chatzos; and therefore he ruled there in Shulchan Aruch that one makes Havdalah. And these poskim hold, and the Magen Avraham holds, that Birchos HaShachar is comparable to the case of the lame one, since we say in Shas — when he heard the voice of the rooster, etc.; and so in all of them — only that the poskim wrote, brought in Shulchan Aruch siman 46, that nowadays because hands are not clean and also because of the evil eye they instituted to arrange them in the synagogue — if so it is called compensations since the obligation applies on an action such as hearing the rooster; and by law immediately when he hears he must bless, as seems to me the intention of the Magen Avraham — requires investigation.
