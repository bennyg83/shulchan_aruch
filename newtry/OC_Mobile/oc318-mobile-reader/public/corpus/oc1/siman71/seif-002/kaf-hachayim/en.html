Even if he is not responsible to bury him, etc. — for example his sister died married and she has a husband who is occupied with her burial, or his brother who has sons who are obligated to bury him — nevertheless he is exempt. Tur, Beit Yosef, and Levush, and not like Rabbenu Tam. And even if he was in another city and they informed him before the burial he is exempt. So ruled Maran z"l in Beit Yosef and in Yoreh De'ah siman 341 seif 1, see there. And so is the accepted practice according to Maran z"l whose rulings we accepted, and not like the Bach who wrote to permit if he is in another city, see there. And likewise the Maamar Mordechai note 1, and see there what he rejected from the words of the Bach and the Peri Chadash, see there; and so is the opinion of Yafeh LaLev note 1. And likewise Rabbenu Zalman note 1.
