Its law is like a weekday, etc. This means during the day. But on the preceding night, which is Motzaei the first day of Yom Tov, the view of the Taz in Yoreh Deah there is that its law is like Shabbos, while the view of the Shach is that its law is like a weekday. I discussed this at length there, and the Taz appears primary. So the Magen Avraham also ruled in siman 548.
