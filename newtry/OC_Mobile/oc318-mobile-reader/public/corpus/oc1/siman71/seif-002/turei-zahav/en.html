But if so, etc. — it appears to me that since he has an obligation at that time, he may read the evening Shema while it is still day, even though the congregation does not read Keriyas Shema except at night; nevertheless, that he will be exempt at night — he should not nullify from himself the yoke of the kingdom of heaven, all that he can read before night. For every day we read the evening Shema even while the day is still great; therefore this one can rely on this to read on that Shabbat before Shabbat departs, as above.
