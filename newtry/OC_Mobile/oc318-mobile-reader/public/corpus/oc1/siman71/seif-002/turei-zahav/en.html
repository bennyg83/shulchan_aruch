But if, etc. It appears to me that, since the obligation rests upon him at that time, he should recite the evening Krias Shema while it is still daytime. Although the congregation does not recite Krias Shema until night, nevertheless, since he will be exempt at night, he should not thereby lose acceptance of the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven when he can recite before nightfall. After all, every day we recite the evening Krias Shema even while it is still fully daytime. He may therefore rely upon this and recite it on that Shabbos before Shabbos ends. This appears correct to me.
