Evening. Taz wrote: since he has obligation at that time he should read evening Keriat Shema while still day, even though the congregation reads only at night — nevertheless that he will be exempt at night should not nullify the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven as long as he can read before night; for every day we read evening Keriat Shema even while day is still great — he may rely on this to read that Shabbat before Shabbat ends:
