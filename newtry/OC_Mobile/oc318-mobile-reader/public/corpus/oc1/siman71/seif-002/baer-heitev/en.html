Evening. The Taz wrote: it seems to me that since he has an obligation at that time, he should recite the evening Kriat Shema while it is still day, even though the congregation does not recite Kriat Shema except at night — nevertheless, that he will be exempt at night should not cause him to nullify upon himself the yoke of the kingdom of heaven; all that he can recite before night, for every day we recite the evening Kriat Shema even while the day is still great — therefore this one can rely on this to recite on that Shabbos before Shabbos goes out, etc. And in Shevut Yaakov volume 1 siman (100) [8] he disagrees and wrote that one who examines the Beit Yosef will see that the obligation at evening time refers only to other mitzvos and not to Kriat Shema; and even if we say it also refers to Kriat Shema, nevertheless Maariv he certainly should not pray — for since he wants to pray Maariv as a weekday prayer, therefore it is a weekday for him and onan status applies to him, and an onen is exempt from prayer, etc. See Bach and Magen Avraham. One who began to pray or recite Shema and suddenly became an onen — whether he should finish or stop. Shevut Yaakov vol. 1 siman 7 ruled he should not stop but should finish, since that an onen does not recite Shema or pray is only rabbinic.
