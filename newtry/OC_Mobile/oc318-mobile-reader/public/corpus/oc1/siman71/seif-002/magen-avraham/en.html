Obligated also at evening time. And the Bach ruled that he is exempt from Keri'as Shema which is recited with awe; and it requires investigation, for the Rabbeynu Yosef wrote — in any case, if it deals with the Keri'as Shema of the morning its time already passed, and the Keri'as Shema of Maariv its time has not yet arrived on Shabbat, for behold its time is after ninety [minutes]; and therefore it deals with other mitzvos. And it is possible it deals with our custom that we practice reciting by day; and nevertheless in what is written "everyone recites" and prays at night.
