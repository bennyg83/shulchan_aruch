He is obligated even toward evening. The Bach rules that he is exempt from Krias Shema, since it is recited with awe. This requires study, for the Beit Yosef wrote that, whichever way one approaches it, if this refers to the morning Krias Shema, its time has already passed; and if it refers to the evening Krias Shema, its time has not yet arrived on Shabbos, for its time begins after the emergence of the stars. It must therefore refer to the other mitzvos. It is possible, however, that it refers to our custom of reciting Krias Shema while it is still daytime. Nevertheless, on Motzaei Shabbos everyone recites Krias Shema and prays at night.
