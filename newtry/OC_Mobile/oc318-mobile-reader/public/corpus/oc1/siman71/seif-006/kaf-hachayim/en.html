And even if he wants to be strict, etc., he is not permitted — one who began to pray or to bless Birkas HaMazon and became an onen: whether he must stop or complete his tefillah or his blessing — see Birkei Yosef note 1 who expanded on this and brought the dispute of the poskim — some say he must stop and some say he need not stop; and in the end he left the matter undecided and did not decide, see there. And also Shulchan Aruch HaRav p. 277 side c weighed this matter and brought the words of the Birkei Yosef mentioned and did not decide, see there. However, the rabbi in Siddur Beit Yosef in the laws of aninus note 8 decided per the view of Maharadaf z"l that he stops, see there. And I say that one who wants not to stop — we do not neglect him since he has upon what to rely, only he should direct his heart to heaven and settle his mind to have kavanah in his tefillah or his blessing. Afterwards I saw that so ruled the Chesed LeAvraham note 6.
