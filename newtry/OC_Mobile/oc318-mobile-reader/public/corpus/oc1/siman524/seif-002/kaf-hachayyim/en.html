If a person rode on Yom Tov to save property, even if he went beyond the techum, since we rule that techumin are rabbinic and riding is forbidden only lest one cut a branch, he should not be disqualified from giving testimony. He is, however, lashed and placed under a ban. If he is a Torah scholar, he is not placed under a ban. Rabbeinu Yerucham in the name of a Gaon; Beis Yosef; Chemed Moshe, subsection 5. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, writes that Rabbeinu Yerucham's statement, 'he is lashed and placed under a ban,' means one or the other, in accordance with Rambam in chapter 1 of Hilchos Yom Tov.
