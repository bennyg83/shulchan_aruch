1. Some things that are forbidden on a holiday, 2 Seifim: We
do not climb a tree, ride on an animal, swim in water, slap the hand on the thigh, clap hands, dance, make legal judgments, get engaged, get married, divorce, perform a Levirate marriage, do halitzah, make a cherem vow, or separate trumah or tithes. Rem"a: Regarding redeeming the first born, the rule on a holiday is the same as the rule on Shabbat. See above in chapter 339 paragraph 4. ':
