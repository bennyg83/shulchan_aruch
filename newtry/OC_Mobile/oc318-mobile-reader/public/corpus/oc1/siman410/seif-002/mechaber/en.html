What we said about one who acquires Shabbat rest at a distant place by setting out on the road — he need not go out and walk into a field; even if he descended from an upper story to go to that place and before he left the courtyard gate his fellow turned him back — he has set out and acquired Shabbat rest. And anyone who acquires Shabbat rest at a distant place need not say "my Shabbat rest is in such-and-such place" — once he resolved in his mind and set out on the road even a little he has acquired Shabbat rest there. Needless to say, one who went on foot and stood at the place where he acquires Shabbat rest need not say anything at all — once he resolved in his mind he has acquired.
