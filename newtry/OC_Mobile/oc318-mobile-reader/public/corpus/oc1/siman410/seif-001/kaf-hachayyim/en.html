1) [Se'if 1] At a place known to him. This means that the location is specifically identifiable, such as by a tree, fence, or the like, as stated in the preceding siman, se'if 11. See our words there in subsection 73.
2) There. Although he did not reach, etc. This applies if it was possible for him to reach that location before the onset of Shabbos had he run, as stated above in the preceding siman, se'if 11. See there.
3) There. Although he did not reach, etc. Another person told him, 'Remain here overnight, for it is hot,' or, 'Remain here, for it is cold.' Gemara.
