Townspeople who sent one of their number to bring their eruv to a known place, and he set out on the road and his fellow turned him back and he did not bring their eruv — they have not acquired Shabbat rest at that place since their eruv was not placed there, and they may only walk two thousand cubits in each direction within their town. But he has acquired Shabbat rest there, since he was traveling and intended to rest there and set out on the road. Therefore he may walk to that place the next day and walk from it two thousand cubits in each direction.
