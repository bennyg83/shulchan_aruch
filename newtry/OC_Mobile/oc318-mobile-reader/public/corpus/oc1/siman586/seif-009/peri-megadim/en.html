If (on Taz: it is not comparable to a crack in se'if 9, for here the seal) even though it is of its kind; mixes in some other sound with it; therefore if its majority is not whole from the place where the mouth is placed, four thumb-breadths do not avail. And the same applies to a punctured shofar that they did not seal, with which one should not blow ab initio (four thumb-breadths likewise do not avail; [but] if its majority is whole, one can say we blow ab initio with a punctured shofar) see Magen Avraham 1; and with Hashem's help it will be clarified there on this.
