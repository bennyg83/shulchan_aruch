In Taz s.k. 10. Since there is in any case a sealing, etc. in my humble opinion it is simple that Beis Yosef also admits this: that it is possible that through the sealing the sound return to what it had been, since originally it was sealed and through the hole it became open, and when he returns and seals, it can well be as initially. Nevertheless Beis Yosef holds that it is impossible that after the sealing it be like before the sealing, since originally it was open and now that he made it sealed; this is something impossible: that the sound of the shofar that was sealed be like when it was pierced; and therefore it is invalid, since it also did not return to what it had been before it was pierced.
