20) There. One who steals a shofar and blows with it — he fulfilled [the obligation], etc. However, there is an opinion that a stolen shofar is invalid — and that is such as when one stole an entire shofar from its owner, for that is forbidden; but if he stole a horn and shaped it into a shofar, he owes him money and it is kosher, as explained in Beit Yosef — see there. And Bach, Taz. And nafka mina: if afterward another shofar was designated for him, or he obtained permission from the owner of the shofar he stole from — he should return and blow with it in order to fulfil according to all opinions; but he should not bless, since it is clear that b'dieved one fulfilled with a stolen [shofar], and even though he did not bless at first on a stolen shofar, as written in the section that follows, and see below Orach Chayyim 24.
