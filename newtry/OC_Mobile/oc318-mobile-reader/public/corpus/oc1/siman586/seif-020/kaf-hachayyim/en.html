20) Ibid. One who steals a shofar and sounds it fulfills the obligation, etc. However, there is an opinion that a stolen shofar is invalid. This refers specifically to one who stole an already-fashioned shofar, which is forbidden. If he stole a horn and fashioned it, however, he owes only its monetary value, and it is valid, as explained in the Beit Yosef; see there. Ateres Zekeinim cited his words. A practical difference arises where another shofar later became available to him, or where he obtained permission from the owner from whom he stole the shofar. He should sound again in order to fulfill the obligation according to all opinions, but he should not recite a blessing, since we rule that b'dieved he fulfilled the obligation with a stolen shofar, even though he did not initially recite a blessing over the stolen shofar, as written in the following letter.
