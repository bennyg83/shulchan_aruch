(77) [Inner] exceeding the outer on both its sides, and he put the inner, etc. For then we say that only the inner sound is heard.
(78) Only equal to the outer, etc. For if it is a little shorter than the outer he has not fulfilled according to everyone, for in the end the sound emerges from two shofaros.
(79) And he blows in the inner; he has fulfilled. And Taz wrote that the Mechaber also admits this: that from the essence of the law if both are equal, then it is possible that they hear the inner sound which is kosher; only because it is hard to make them exactly equal (for possibly the outer protrudes a little) therefore he required that the protrusion of the inner be recognizable.
(80) And that is provided it does not change its sound. Meaning: even if only the inner sound is heard, nevertheless possibly the sound was changed through the outer covering it, and it turns out the sound comes from the force of two shofaros and is invalid.
(81) And if not; invalid. This refers to the beginning of the seif; meaning: if the inner is not exceeding, it is invalid because the wind of his mouth presses in both of them and it is the sound of two shofaros, and the Merciful One said one shofar. And from all this we learn that if he blew with two actual shofaros he certainly has not fulfilled.
