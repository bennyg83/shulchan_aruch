(s.k. 20) A hole, etc. (And even ab initio, etc., for like with like, etc. and so it appears in Sukkah, etc.) for one might have thought: since the male growth does not have upon it the name "shofar" (for if they removed it and pierced it, it is invalid) therefore if they did not remove it and pierced [through] it, one might think it is an interposition between [him and] the shofar; and even though after the fact it is not an interposition, nevertheless ab initio [one should] not. It teaches us that even ab initio we say: like with like does not interpose. And so Rava said in Sukkah folio 37b that it is permitted to insert the lulav into the bundle of hadas and aravah that were tied, and we are not concerned that through the insertion lulav leaves will fall and there will be an interposition, for like with like does not interpose. And so we hold in siman 651. Thus even ab initio we say: like with like does not interpose.
