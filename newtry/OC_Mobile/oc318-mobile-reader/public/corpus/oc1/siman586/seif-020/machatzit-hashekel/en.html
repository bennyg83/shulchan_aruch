(section 20) A hole, etc. — and even l'chatchila, etc. — something of its kind, etc., and so too in Sukkah, etc., for it would have seemed that since the remembrances are not upon it there is no name of shofar — for if they removed it and pierced it, it is invalid; if so, if they did not remove it and pierced it, it would have seemed that there is chatzitzah between the shofar. And it is — b'dieved it is not chatzitzah; nevertheless l'chatchila not. It teaches us that even l'chatchila we say: something of its kind is not interposing. And so said Rava in Sukkah daf 37b: it is permitted to insert the lulav within the binding of the hadas and aravah that were tied. And Lechem Chamudah: through the insertion its lulav leaves will fall and there will be chatzitzah. Something of its kind is not interposing. And so we rule siman 649 — behold that even l'chatchila we say something of its kind is not interposing:
