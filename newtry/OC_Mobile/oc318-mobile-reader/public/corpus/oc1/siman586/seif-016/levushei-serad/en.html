For the Rama wrote here one opinion, which holds that even after the fact he did not fulfill the obligation if it was nullified on Yom Tov. If so, he should have glossed there in Yoreh Deah that one does not recite a blessing. But the Tur and the Beit Yosef hold that the issue of Reish Lakish applies only with respect to rejection in mitzvos, meaning only if one would initially take this item that was rejected and then became fit.
