(16) In the gloss: likewise a shofar from a tame animal is invalid. Although we taught in Niddah ch. 6: whatever has horns has hooves — apparently a tame animal has no horns — how do we find a shofar from a tame animal? Peri Chadash already wrote in this siman and Yoreh Deah siman 80 that we find a dispute in ch. These Terefot (Chullin daf 59b) — Rabbis and R' Dosa; and per the Rabbis there is a tame animal and chayah tame that have horns, and the mishnah in Niddah and the sugya in ch. Bava Metzia go like R' Dosa; and we conclude like the Rabbis — see there and Beit David Hametivi. And likewise in Otzar (that sefer) letter 1; and see Magen Avraham s.k. 3.
