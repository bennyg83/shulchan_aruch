The thickness of the shofar on the inner side. Meaning: he places the gold on the thickness of the shofar (what he places its edge to his mouth) even though the sound does not change because of the gold, nevertheless it is invalid, since the sound emerges first from that circumference of gold and afterward from the shofar itself; and this is not in the category of "he added onto it any amount," for there the sound goes from the shofar first and afterward from the gold addition; and it teaches us that even so it is invalid. And accordingly one cannot learn from here [the law of] if he distanced the shofar and blew into it (for there the sound does not emerge first into any [foreign] thing) except according to the second interpretation that disqualifies because of an interposition: [then] it is also invalid when he distanced [it]. And for this reason the Tur wrote "and they learned from here that if he distanced etc." after writing the second interpretation; and this is somewhat not like the interpretation of Beis Yosef.
