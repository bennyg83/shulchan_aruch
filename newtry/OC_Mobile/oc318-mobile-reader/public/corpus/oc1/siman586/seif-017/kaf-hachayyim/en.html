17) Ibid., Rama's gloss: Likewise, a shofar from a non-kosher animal is invalid. This is because it is stated, 'So that the Torah of Hashem shall be in your mouth,' meaning that everything used for the Torah of Hashem must be from that which is permitted in your mouth. Although it comes from a neveilah or another prohibition, it is nevertheless called 'in your mouth,' because its species is permitted in your mouth, as we say regarding tefillin and a Sefer Torah. The same applies to every sacred article, which must be from that which is permitted in your mouth. Levush; Ateres Zekeinim; Magen Avraham, s.k. 3. However, the Ran in the chapter Ra'uhu Beis Din wrote that the statement in the chapter Bameh Madlikin, folio 28, that only the hide of a kosher animal was deemed fit for sacred articles, and a shofar is also a sacred article since it comes for remembrance, is not a conclusive proof. Therefore, the matter requires further study; see there. The Beit Yosef and Olas Shabbos, letter 1, cited his words. Therefore, Olas Shabbos wrote there that if no other shofar is available, one should sound such a shofar without a blessing. So too wrote Mateh Ephraim, letter 3, and Mishna Berurah, s.k. 8.
