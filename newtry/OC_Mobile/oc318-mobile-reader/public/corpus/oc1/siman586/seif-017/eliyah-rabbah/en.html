[17] Levush: Even if most of it is split, etc. Nachalas Tzvi objects to Levush that according to the opinion requiring most of it to split, namely Maggid Mishneh in chapter 1, it is forbidden even when glued. In my humble opinion, this is not compelled upon examination. It also appears that Levush follows Tosafos, Rosh, and Rabbeinu Yerucham here, and Magen Avraham rules likewise. I find it difficult, however, that Shulchan Aruch did not cite what Beit Yosef writes, that Ran implies that even after gluing, the slightest split is invalid. Perhaps this was omitted because it is only an implication. Yet the law is explicit in Ran in the name of Rabbeinu Yonasan. It therefore appears to me that when another shofar can readily be found, one should not be lenient in this at all.
