17) There — gloss. And likewise a shofar from an impure animal is invalid. Because it is said: "so that the Torah of God shall be in your mouth" — everything in the Torah of God must be from what is permitted in your mouth. And even though it is of neveilah or another prohibition, we apply to your mouth [the rule] "from what is permitted in your mouth," as we say regarding tefillin and a Torah scroll; and the same applies to every heavenly craft that is permitted in your mouth. Levush. Taz. Magen Avraham section 3. However, the Ran in the chapter "They saw" of Bava Batra wrote that this which we say in the chapter of Bava Metzia (daf 28): "They were not qualified for heavenly craft except from a pure animal" — and a shofar too is heavenly craft, since it comes for remembrance — is not a complete proof; and therefore the matter requires further study — see there. And Bach, Beit Yosef, and see there Orach Chayyim 1. And therefore the see-there wrote that if there is no other shofar, one should blow with such a [shofar] without a berachah. And likewise Mateh Efraim Orach Chayyim 3, Mishna Berurah Orach Chayyim 8.
