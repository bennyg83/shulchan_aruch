A statement of R' Abahu, Rosh Hashanah 16.
A statement of R' Levi there, folio 27.
Mishnah there.
Tur in the name of the Ramban.
My relative, the rav R' Mordechai Krashnik, posek of the holy community, of blessed memory, raised a difficulty from that which we learned in Niddah, chapter 6: Whatever has horns has hooves, and it implies there that this is [accepted by] everyone, as Tosafos wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Tam in Chullin folio 59; and if so, it is not a non-kosher animal. And it appears to me that we are dealing with [a case] where a non-kosher animal bore it, and that which comes from a non-kosher [animal] is non-kosher, as we learned in the first chapter of Bechoros.
