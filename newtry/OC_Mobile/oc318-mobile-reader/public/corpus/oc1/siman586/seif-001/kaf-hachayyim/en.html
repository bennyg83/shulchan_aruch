1) [Seif 1] The shofar of Rosh Hashanah, etc. This excludes a communal fast, on which they also sound a shofar, for there it need not be from a ram or curved. Mishna Berurah, s.k. 1. Alternatively, it excludes Yom Kippur of the Jubilee year, regarding which there is an opinion that it must be straight, as explained in the Beit Yosef.
