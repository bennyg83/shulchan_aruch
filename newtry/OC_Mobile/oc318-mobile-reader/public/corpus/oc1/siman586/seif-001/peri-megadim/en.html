Of a ram (on Taz and on Magen Avraham 1: that a bent [shofar] is required ab initio) specifically bent; but of a ram it is only a preferred mitzvah; and if so, other species that are bent, and a ram that is straight if it happened so, or bent that he made straight (is that more of a mitzvah, and need we other species? And what he wrote in the commentary of the Rambam of blessed memory) see Beis Yosef in the name of Orchos Chaim: goat and he-goat and yael-goats are included in "sheep"; nevertheless there are other species whose horns are hollow (a minority of the horns of wild animals and the like) and [those are] invalid according to the Rambam of blessed memory, for they are not a sheep species at all; and according to the Rosh and Tur they are kosher. And it is difficult: how did he write that the Rambam [holds] like the Rosh and Tur? Also, apparently "ayil" with a kamatz under the yud is a wild-animal species ("ayal taarog al afikei mayim") whereas a male ram of sheep has a chirik under the yud; and this requires much further study. And [in] Parashas Re'eh, "ve'ako," we translate as "vayeala," as Rashi of blessed memory explained: "shtainbok"; and the "yachmur" of Tehillim is a scribal error; and see Tosafos, Rosh Hashanah 27b, s.v. "of a yael" Rashi of blessed memory explained: yael, a wild animal, "steinbok"; and in Rashi I did not find "wild animal"; and see Chullin, "goats of be'ala" (see there. And what he wrote "sheep") apparently one can say that "sheep" includes ewe and goat; but I do not [hold so], for "tzon" includes ewe and goat, and likewise "seh" includes both, but "keves" implies specifically the ewe species; and this requires further study. And see Kesef Mishneh, chapter 1, halachah 1, of Shofar, in the name of the Kol Bo (see there).
It emerges that there are grades in a shofar: of a ram is a very preferred mitzvah (bent, and a male ram of Yitzchak) and sometimes a ewe has a horn, and [that is] straight; see Tosafos, Rosh Hashanah 27b, and Yom Teruah there. And other species that are bent are preferable to a straight ewe; but other species that are straight (one can say a ewe is preferable, for there is some remembrance. (3) Goats and yaels) goats of be'ala. (4) Other kinds. (5) A shofar that became tamei, when there is no other (one can say he blows without a blessing; see Magen Avraham 3 and Olas Shabbos. (6) A cow and an ox) after the fact completely invalid; and the same applies to those that are not hollow, for they are not a "shofar." And see Responsa Chacham Tzvi of blessed memory, siman 34, who wondered at Taz of blessed memory regarding what he wrote: a ram after a "seh" of sheep (that this is a wild animal, yud with a kamatz; "eilei" with a chirik is a sheep) see there.
