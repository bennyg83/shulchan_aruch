Of a ram. Taz, Magen Avraham 1: l'chatchila we require specifically bent; of a ram — only a preferred mitzvah. And if so, other species — bent and ram straight: if it happened so, or bent that he made more straight than the mitzvah requires — other species are required. And that which is written explicitly in Rabbeinu Moshe z"l in Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chayyim: deer and ibex and wild goats are included in sheep. And nevertheless there are other species whose horns are hollow — a minority of animals' horns, and the like — and invalid according to Rabbeinu Moshe z"l, for there is no species of sheep at all; and according to Rosh and Tur it is kosher. And it is difficult how he wrote that Rabbeinu Moshe [agrees] like Rosh and Tur; also apparently "ram" in kamatz-yod is a species of wild animal — "ayalah ta'arog al afikei mayim, eil zachar" — "keves" with yod in chirik, and tzarich iyun greatly. And chapter "Re'eh" — and aku in Targum, and ya'ala as Rashi z"l explained: Steinbock; and Tosefta [Rosh Hashana] 27b s.v. shel ya'el — Rashi z"l explained: ya'el is an animal that walks in Bekaa; and in Rashi I did not find "chaya." And see Chullin, goats in Aleph-Lamed-Alef, see-there. And that which is written "keves" — apparently one could say that keves includes ewe and goat; and I would say that tzon includes ewe and goat, and likewise seh includes both; but keves implies only specifically the species of sheep, and tzarich iyun.
