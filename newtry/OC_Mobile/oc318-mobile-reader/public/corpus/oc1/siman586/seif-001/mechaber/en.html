The Shofar of Rosh Hashana should be from a ram,
bent; under extreme circumstances, all shofars are kosher, whether straight or bent, however the mitzva is better fulfilled with a bent one rather than a straight one; the shofar of a cow may not be used; similarly the horns of most wild animals, as they are a bone and have no hollow inside, may not be used. ((Similarly, the shofar of a non-kosher animal may not be used (Ra"n Chapter 3 Rosh Hashana).):
