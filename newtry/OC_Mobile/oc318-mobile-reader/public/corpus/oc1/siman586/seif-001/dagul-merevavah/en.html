"Of a non-Jew" refers to the [avodah zarah itself], to exclude [a case] where the accessories were of avodah zarah belonging to a Jew. But regarding the accessories themselves we do not care even if they belonged to a Jew, since the avodah zarah is not of a Jew. And see in Yoreh De'ah (siman 146), and in Beis Yosef there at the beginning of the siman, what he wrote in the name of the Ramban and the Raavad.
