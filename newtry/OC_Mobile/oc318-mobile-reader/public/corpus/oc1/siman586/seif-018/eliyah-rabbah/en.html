[18] Some say that it is not, etc. The first view appears primary among the poskim. Nevertheless, initially one should be stringent. Even according to those who validate a split extending through most or all of the shofar, one should be stringent initially where it was glued with a different material, which appears worse. It also appears that even the stringent opinion is stringent only where the sound changed. If the sound did not change, it is no worse than sealing a hole with a different material, which many poskim validate.
