Thickness of the shofar. See Taz. Behold, as it appears to one who studies Beis Yosef, Bach, and Prisha, one will see three interpretations in the baraita of Rosh Hashana 27b regarding "the place of placing the mouth" and "not the place of placing the mouth." The first interpretation: the hollow of the hole at the narrow place where he places [his] mouth (the hollow is called "mouth," and he places its edges from the outside onto the shofar) is called the place of placing the mouth; and the thickness of the shofar itself is as its simple meaning; at the narrow place, the thickness of the wall of the shofar. And when it says "in its thickness" with a bet, one may say that he put a gold ring inside into the hollow at the narrow place, and it turns out he added to its thickness, and it is invalid because the sound, as soon as it enters, enters into the gold not into the shofar; and [likewise] if he plated it with gold entirely from within. And here it teaches us that also at the place of placing the mouth it disqualifies; and not at the place of placing the mouth it is kosher; one may say even if he put gold on the actual thickness of the shofar it is also kosher; and it is not called "he added onto it any amount" except on the wide side, not on the narrow; and an interposition also does not disqualify; and if so, if he distanced the shofar from his mouth, one may say it is kosher, and as it appears, this is the interpretation of the Ran, of blessed memory. The second interpretation is the interpretation of Beis Yosef on the Tur and Rosh: placing the mouth on the thickness of the wall of the shofar is invalid because it is like adding onto it any amount, whether on the wide side or on the narrow side it is invalid; and what the Tur wrote "in its thickness" is as if he wrote "on its thickness," and see Beis Yosef who explained thus. And if so, according to this interpretation one may say that whether he plated a ring around the hollow within it is also invalid, and even plating on the thickness of the wall is also invalid; and "not the place of placing the mouth" means outside, immediately adjacent to what he inserts into his mouth, and he added to its thickness from outside; it is kosher if its sound was not changed by a small amount of gold; [but] if it was changed by a small amount of gold it is invalid in every place. And according to these two interpretations one may say: if he distanced the shofar from his mouth it is kosher, for an interposition also does not disqualify. The third interpretation is "some explain" in the Tur: at the place of placing the mouth, even from outside it is invalid, for the mouth (placing its lips outside) the gold interposes between his lips and the shofar; and they learned from here that if he distanced the shofar from his mouth it is invalid; but according to the first two interpretations one cannot learn this. And the Mechaber copied the baraita and explained in it two interpretations: one appears to be the view of the Tur and the Rosh (he did not explain [like] the Ran; and what he wrote "toward the inner side" means the actual thickness of the shofar) the thickness of the wall of the short [end] that he places in his mouth, from the beginning of the outside toward the inside until the hollow, and it is invalid for he added onto it any amount as on the wide side. And according to the Taz the reason is that first the sound [hits] the gold on the lip of the thickness of the wall of the shofar and afterward [enters] the shofar, and that itself is the narrow [end] (and this he wrote somewhat unlike Beis Yosef, namely what Beis Yosef wrote [that] it is invalid because of addition, and the Taz holds because the sound first goes to the gold, etc.). And the second interpretation is the interpretation of "some say" in the Tur, and the Mechaber wrote them in order to learn his law in seif 19: if he distanced the shofar and blew in it, it is invalid (namely out of doubt) and he blows afterward without a blessing; and see Peri Chadash. And he omitted the interpretation of the Ran, of blessed memory, for the Mechaber admits that this is invalid simply whenever he put gold into the hollow at the narrow place, for it is [like] trumpets and not a shofar. And see Beer HaGolah notes 50 and 60 (it implies that he explains "some explain" as (1) the Ran and (2) the Tur) I did not understand; and see Levush; it appears he explains as written here. And see Sefer Kapos Temarim; and see Prisha and Peri Chadash; and I, the poor one, according to my strength, have explained.
