18) If a valid shofar later becomes available to him, he should sound it as well without a blessing, because the invalidity of a shofar from a non-kosher animal is not entirely clear, and perhaps he already fulfilled the obligation through the shofar from the non-kosher animal. However, if he sounded a horn of an ox or cow, or the horns of wild animals that have no inner core, and a valid shofar later became available, he should sound it with a blessing, because those are certainly invalid. Therefore, even if he has no shofar other than one from an ox or cow, or the horns of such wild animals, he should not sound them at all. Mateh Ephraim, ibid.; Mishna Berurah, ibid.
