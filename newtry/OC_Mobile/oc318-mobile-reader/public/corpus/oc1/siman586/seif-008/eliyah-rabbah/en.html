[ח] [Levush] he should not blow, etc. And in time of need it is permitted to blow for himself, as written in Yoreh Deah siman 221 seif 13 Bach; and this is the language of Rabbeinu Yerucham: he said konam this shofar is upon me — I blow with it; and with this is resolved Ran's difficulty why Rava [Rosh Hashanah] 28b also ruled one forbidden benefit from a shofar — since he already said one forbidden benefit from his fellow is permitted. And per what I wrote it is resolved, for it teaches us he himself is permitted; and truly for the girsa that forbids one can say it teaches us it forbids. And further Ran in Nedarim 16a wrote if he said konam sukkah upon me — since he did not mention sitting in the sukkah he speaks of benefit and it is permitted — end quote; and the same applies to shofar and requires study; see Sefer Beit Hillel Yoreh Deah siman 221 that he did not look at Bach here — he and his son in the gloss.
