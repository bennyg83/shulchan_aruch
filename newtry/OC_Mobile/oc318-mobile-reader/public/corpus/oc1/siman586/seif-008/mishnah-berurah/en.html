(37) Cracked, etc. And specifically cracked, which keeps increasing, as below; but if chipped it is kosher with any amount. Nevertheless if he saw it before Rosh Hashana it is better to repair it so that it not appear defective.
(38) Invalid. And specifically when cracked from side to side, even though there is no deficiency in it and also its sound did not change.
(39) With any amount. Because due to the wind and the strength of the blast the crack keeps increasing until it cracks entirely; and whatever stands to crack is as cracked.
(40) Unless he tightened, etc. For the thread holds it so that it not split further.
(41) And [measure] remained, etc. And many Acharonim are lenient even if it did not remain, so long as its sound was not changed through the tightening.
(42) Toward the mouth. And like the first opinion below in seif 9.
(43) In its majority. And because its majority is like its entirety, and even if it cracked only from one side; but if its majority did not crack, even if a measure of tekiah did not remain, it is kosher. And in a time of urgency one may rely on this opinion, which is the main one. And if he blew with a shofar cracked in its minority and afterward found a kosher shofar, he should go back to blow with it and not recite a blessing.
(44) And if he glued it, etc. And specifically with glue we permit, for it is as if it is whole; but if he tightens it with a thread or cord it does not help, since its majority cracked.
(45) Kosher. That is, even if only any amount remained that did not crack, it is kosher when he glued it [Magen Avraham and other Acharonim].
(46) He glued it with glue. And this is not called "not of its kind," for cracked differs from pierced, for the glue is not recognizable between the glued [edges]. And if the crack is wide and the glue is recognizable, certainly one should be stringent about this, for it is like sealing with what is not of its kind. Nevertheless it is possible that if its sound was not changed through the glue from what it had been initially, one should not be stringent when its majority remained, for also regarding pierced and sealed in this manner it is kosher according to many poskim, as above in seif 7; and this requires further study.
(47) Of itself. But with glue it is like sealing with what is not of its kind, and its law is as written in seif 7. And the first view is the main one. Nevertheless if its sound changed, one should be stringent (not in a time of urgency) to go back and blow, and without a blessing.
(48) Invalid. For then there is no name of shofar upon it, since it cracked entirely.
