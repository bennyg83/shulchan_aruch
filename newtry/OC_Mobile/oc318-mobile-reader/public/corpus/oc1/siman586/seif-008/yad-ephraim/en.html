And so wrote the Rosh explicitly, and thus: etc. I did not understand his proof from the Rosh, for there it deals when its sound changed after the sealing as well; but in truth also before the sealing there was a change of sound, and specifically regarding the cause of the emergence of the sound one may distinguish between of its kind and not of its kind. Again I saw in the Rosh that there is a scribal error in Taz, and there it reads: "no other matter causes the change of sound," etc., meaning, [regarding] not of its kind: since there is sealing, also after opening we say that through the sealing the change was made; [but] since no other matter causes the change of sound, we have no concern about it; and the words of Taz are correct.
