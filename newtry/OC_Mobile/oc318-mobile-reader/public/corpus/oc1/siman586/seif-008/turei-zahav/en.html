Because due to the wind and the strength of the blast the crack continues and increases until it cracks, etc. And it appears to me the reason: a crack is worse than pierced, and there is a deficiency there: for regarding pierced there is a place for the wind caused by the blast to enter there through the deficiency and exit outward, and it does not strengthen to crack the shofar; which is not so regarding a crack, where there is no place for the wind to exit because of the gap, and therefore it strengthens and cracks the crack further. And also regarding a crack it does not crack further unless cracked in its length; but cracked across its width does not strengthen and crack further across its width, for it is not so in its nature; and the crack that is now across its width [comes] because there was some flaw in the hide, therefore across its width it does not disqualify unless it was already cracked in most of its circumference, as above.
And a measure remained, etc. This deals with [a case] where it impedes the blast, and it is similar to gold plating in seif 16; for this reason we require a measure. But if the sound does not change because of this, I do not know a reason or basis for requiring a measure.
