In Taz s.k. 18. The thickness of the shofar, etc. in my humble opinion the citation is placed in error, and one needs to cite what he wrote afterward: "And some explain that its thickness at the narrow place etc.," which is from the words of the Rosh; and what he wrote afterward, "except according to the second interpretation etc." meaning the interpretation that the Tur brought there, which is from the words of the Ramban that the Maggid Mishneh brought; and upon this the Tur wrote "and they learned from here etc." see there and in Beis Yosef and you will understand. And the first view here is the Ran, whose language is explained explicitly as his very wording; and the intent is that the thickness itself is as if divided in two, and the partition on the side of the hollow is called the place of placing the mouth; and see Peri Chadash. And I saw in Peri Megadim who wishes to force into Taz's words that the first view is the Rosh, and it is not so; and the main [view] is as I wrote.
