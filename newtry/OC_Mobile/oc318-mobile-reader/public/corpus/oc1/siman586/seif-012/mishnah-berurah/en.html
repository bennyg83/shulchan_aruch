(60) Whether he inverted it in the manner, etc. And the reason is that it is written "and you shall sound a shofar of teruah" we require [it] in the manner of transfer: namely as the ram transfers it on its head while alive.
(61) Rather that he widened, etc. That is, through boiling water; and also the reason is that we require that it be as the manner of its growth, that the short side face the person's mouth.
(62) At the wide place. Meaning: even with a shofar made properly; and all is for the aforementioned reason.
