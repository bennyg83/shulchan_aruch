[12] Levush: But if it split, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov writes that all the more so it is invalid if it became notched, for the Mishnah in the chapter “Eilu Mumin” teaches that a notch involves a deficiency whereas a split does not. It therefore did not need to state the law of a notch regarding shofar. One must consequently inspect shofaros carefully, because many have a slight notch at the mouthpiece due to saliva, and such a shofar is invalid. In my humble opinion, however, it is possible that a split without a deficiency is worse here than a hole with a deficiency, because where material is missing we do not fear that the defect will continue to widen, even when it runs lengthwise. This is also implied by Taz. Although Levush writes that the law concerns a round hole, perhaps he means a hole without a deficiency; this wording is absent from Shulchan Aruch. Malbushei Yom Tov’s proof from blemishes is certainly not decisive, because there all holes are disqualifying.
