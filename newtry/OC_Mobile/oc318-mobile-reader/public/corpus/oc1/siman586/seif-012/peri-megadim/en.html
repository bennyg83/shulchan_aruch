And there remained. See Taz regarding what impedes the blast: one may say that by means of a cord [wrapping] it is like what Magen Avraham wrote in note 11, and it is worse than [a case of] pierced and not sealed: for even though that which impedes the blast is [still] kosher, therefore here we require a measure of four thumb-breadths; but if it does not impede [the blast], no measure is needed at all. But the view of Peri Chadash is that if its sound did not change, no measure of tekiah is needed; and if its sound changed, [it is] not [valid] for the measure of tekiah. And see Magen Avraham note 11, and God willing it will be clarified there; and see Levush; it implies that the reason is that by means of a cord it is not [really] cracked, therefore we require a measure of four thumb-breadths even though its sound did not change.
