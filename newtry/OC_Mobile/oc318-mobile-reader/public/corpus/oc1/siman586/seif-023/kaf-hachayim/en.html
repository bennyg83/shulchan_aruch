23) And it appears — it is permitted to take a friend's shofar without his knowledge and bless on it, as written siman 649 seif 5. And even though a shofar spoils somewhat through its blowing, behold a talit also spoils somewhat, and nevertheless it is permitted, as written above siman 14. And there is no concern that he will blow in it extensively like with books (above siman 14 Orach Chayyim 31) — for why would he blow more than his obligation? Magen Avraham there. Eliyah Rabbah there. Razin Orach Chayyim 5, Chayei Adam there. And nevertheless one may blow a hundred blasts with it, since they are accustomed to blow in this measure. Mateh Efraim Orach Chayyim 5, Mishna Berurah there. And it implies — however, for learning it is forbidden to take without the owner's knowledge unless he knows his friend is not particular about this. Chayei Adam there. And that which it is permitted to take a friend's shofar, etc. — that is to blow with it in the place where it rests; but to remove it from his house to the synagogue, or the reverse, is forbidden, lest he be particular about it and it is theft, as written above siman 14 seif 4 and in our words there Orach Chayyim 19 and 22 — see there. And likewise Magen Avraham siman 649 section 16 regarding lulav — see there. And likewise Mishna Berurah in this siman in Shaar HaTziyun 20.
