S.k. 5. "Sitting in a sukkah upon me as a konam," or even if, etc. (thus it should read. And what Taz wrote that Beis Yosef wrote etc.) see in Beis Yosef who wrote that in Tosafos of Rosh Hashana they wrote that in the second chapter we say regarding a sukkah; and it appears that also here one should read: in the name of Tosafos of Rosh Hashana who wrote that in the second chapter of Nedarim, etc.
