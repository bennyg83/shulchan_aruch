One who steals. Baer Heitev. And Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of R' Yitzchak Malkho in a manuscript responsum: if he blew without permission and it cracked, he is liable to pay, for he is a borrower without the owner's knowledge; yet he has fulfilled his obligation, for we hold that one who stole a shofar and blew has fulfilled his obligation; and if he took it with permission, he is exempt, for it is [like an animal that] died because of the labor; and regarding the blowing itself after which it cracked: if immediately upon finishing [the blast] he stopped, he has fulfilled his obligation; and if he completed the tekiah with that same shofar; according to our Babylonian Gemara he has fulfilled his obligation, and according to the Yerushalmi he has not, for its beginning requires its end (see there).
