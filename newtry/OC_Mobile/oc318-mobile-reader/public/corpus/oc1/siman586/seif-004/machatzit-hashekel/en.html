(s.k. 4) And he blew, etc. (and see the end of siman 454 regarding stolen matzah).
And nevertheless one does not recite a blessing over it; and see the end of siman 691 regarding Megillah; that one also fulfills with a stolen megillah for this reason, that there is no theft in the sound; and nevertheless he wrote there that one does not recite a blessing over it, as regarding sefarim that the Rama wrote in siman 14: that even a little one should not read in them, lest he read in them a great deal and they tear.
