This is a separate matter, for the Olas Shabbos asked why a shofar from a non-kosher animal is invalid, since we do not rule in accordance with that statement that only something from a kosher animal is fit for the service of Heaven, for if so, why would tefillin require the verse, that the Torah of Hashem shall be in your mouth, from that which is permitted in your mouth? The Magen Avraham answered that we derive the entire Torah from tefillin.
