(s.k. 10) With any amount, for as long as he blows the crack increases, etc. Granted regarding a punctured [shofar], where there is a place for the wind leaving his mouth to exit, it has no force to crack the shofar further; but regarding a crack, where there is no place for the wind to exit, it cracks it more. But regarding its width we require specifically its majority, as written in the following se'if: Taz wrote the reason (that it is not the nature of a shofar to crack across its width, so why should we say it will crack more? And what cracked a little now) necessarily an accidental cause brought it about, that it fell to the ground and the like (which is not so along its length. And that we are concerned along its length that it will crack more) even though one may say "what do we care" the Bach wrote in the name of the Re'em that we are concerned that it will crack further during the tekiah and he will not know and will err that he has already fulfilled [his obligation].
