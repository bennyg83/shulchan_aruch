There is [room] to permit; on Taz: this is the view of the Rosh; that the Ran refers to [a case where] it hinders the tekiah, as written in letter 6. And here is view 1: its sound returned as it was before the hole; and the same if the sound did not change on account of the hole and they sealed it of its kind (it is kosher if the sound is as it was. And according to the Rosh) even if its sound did not return to as much as it was before it was pierced, and all the more so if it returned to as much as it was before it was pierced. And it is possible: if it was pierced and its sound did not change, and when they sealed it of its kind its sound changed; the Rosh would concede that such a case is invalid. And the reasoning of Taz of blessed memory: anything of its kind is not [ruled as] "a shofar and something else," from the language of the Rosh of blessed memory; but when of its kind, even if its sound changed, we do not reckon "something else" [as] causing, etc. (it appears at first glance that even after the sealing the sound changed, and before the sealing it did not change) it is kosher when of its kind. The Rosh further challenged view 1: why, when its sound did not return to what it was before it was pierced but only to its sound as it was after it was pierced, is it invalid according to view 1? And on this Beis Yosef and Taz answered according to his method; but according to the Rosh of blessed memory, of its kind it is kosher in every case, as stated above, apparently.
