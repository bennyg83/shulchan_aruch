There is [room] to permit. Taz: this is the view of the Rosh, that the Ran refers to [a case where] it hinders the tekiah, as written in letter 6. And here is view 1: its sound returned as it was before the hole; and the same if the sound did not change due to the hole and they sealed it of its kind — it is kosher if the sound is as it was. And per Rosh — even if its sound did not return to as much as it was before it was pierced, and all the more so if it returned to as much as it was before it was pierced. And it is possible: if it was pierced and its sound did not change, and when they sealed it of its kind its sound changed — the Rosh would concede it is invalid in such a case. And the view of Taz z"l: everything of its kind is not a shofar; and another matter from the language of the Rosh z"l — but of its kind, even if its sound changed, the reading is not "another matter causes the change of sound," etc.; it appears that even after the sealing the sound changed, and before the sealing the sound did not change — it is kosher when of its kind. The Rosh further challenged view 1: why when its sound did not return to what it was before it was pierced — only its sound as it was after it was pierced — why is it invalid according to view 1?
