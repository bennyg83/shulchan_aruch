(57) He glued fragments of shofaros. That is, even if he took fragments of whole shofaros in their round and glued them (and even glued them by themselves, as above in seif 8) and even if it does not impede the blast.
(58) Even if there is, etc. And he added onto it to beautify it so that it be long, or so that it give a greater sound than it had been, and even so it is invalid; and the reason is that the Merciful One said one shofar and not two shofaros.
