S.k. 12. And it is similar to gold plating, etc. it appears he means: like there, through the gold its sound was changed, so too here through wrapping outside it a thread or cord one may say it causes a change of sound. Nevertheless if there is a [sufficient] measure, since there is the status of a shofar upon it without the lower part, one should not compare it to gold plating, for it appears that merely remaining a measure of tekiah above the plating toward the mouth does not help; whereas here we do not attribute the change of sound to the thread or cord to say that it is the sound of a shofar and another matter; rather we say the change of sound is because of the crack and we do not care, as written in seif 9 regarding cracked across its width and remaining, that it is kosher even if it impedes the blast; which is not so if a measure of tekiah did not remain: since without the thread and cord upon it the status of shofar departs from it, and only through the thread and cord with which it was tightened does the status of shofar appear again upon it; and since its sound did not return to what it had been, we are concerned for the change of sound like sealed with what is not of its kind and the like (so wrote Magen Avraham s).k. 11.
