And likewise horns of most animals etc. have no name in holy tongue as shofar, for only hollow horns with masculinity inside, from which the sheath is peeled, are called shofar; invalid like cow. Tur. R' Zvi ot 1. Even horns of kid-goat that mated with gazelle are invalid, and all the more gazelle from goat; hybrid from permitted species such as ibex and sheep is valid.
