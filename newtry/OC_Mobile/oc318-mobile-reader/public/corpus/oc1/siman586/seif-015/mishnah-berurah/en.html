(66) He did not remove its core, etc. For when it is attached to the animal a bone protrudes from the head and enters into it, and when they make a shofar they remove it from within; and this [person] did not remove it, but pierced it from its beginning to its end.
(67) Kosher. And even l'chatchila it is permitted to do so, for like-kind does not interpose, and this is not in the category of two shofaros since it was attached from its beginning; and correspondingly if they removed it and pierced a hole in it and returned it, it is invalid.
(68) Such as that [only] a hole was made in it; invalid. For "shofar" is from the language of a tube that is hollow, and whatever is not hollow by its nature is not called a shofar.
