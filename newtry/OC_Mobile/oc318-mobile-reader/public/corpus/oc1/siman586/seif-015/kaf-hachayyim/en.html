Likewise, the horns of most wild animals, etc. In Lashon HaKodesh they are not called shofar, for only hollow horns that have a core inside, whose outer shell peels away from the core, are called shofar. They are invalid like the horn of a cow. Tur; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 1. Even the horns of the offspring of a female goat upon which a gazelle mated are invalid, and it goes without saying that the offspring of a gazelle upon which a male goat mated is invalid. However, the horn of a hybrid produced from two valid species, such as one born from ibexes and sheep, is valid. Siddur Amudei Shamayim; Pis'chei Olam, letter 4.
