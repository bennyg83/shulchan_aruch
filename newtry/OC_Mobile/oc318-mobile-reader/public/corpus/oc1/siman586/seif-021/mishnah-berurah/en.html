(82) We do not desecrate, etc. Because desecration of Yom Tov involves a positive commandment and a negative commandment, and shofar is only a positive commandment and does not override.
(83) Because of a shevus. For the Sages equated their words to those of the Torah and upheld their words in a case of sitting and not doing, in a place of a positive commandment.
(84) How, etc. And the same applies that we do not bring it from outside the techum, and one may not go outside the techum in order to hear the sound of a shofar; and the same applies to every rabbinic prohibition, such as if a heap of stones fell upon it, that it is forbidden to move the stones in order to take it; and likewise if he needs to repair the shofar with a cut and the like, that it is forbidden by himself even with a tool that craftsmen are not accustomed to repair with (for there is in this only because of a shevus) it is also forbidden.
(85) And he does not float upon the face of the water, etc. And for this reason likewise it is forbidden to cross in a ferry that they call a "pram," or in a small boat, in order to bring the shofar or to blow there; however if it is impossible in another manner, the view of Chayei Adam is that it is permitted if the gentile will transport it and the Jew does not assist at all; and see at the beginning of siman 639 what we will write on this.
(86) And through a gentile it is permitted, for it is, etc. And accordingly it is possible even where he has a shofar, only that it is not of a ram; that it is permitted to bring through a gentile a shofar of a ram, for the mitzvah is with a ram.
