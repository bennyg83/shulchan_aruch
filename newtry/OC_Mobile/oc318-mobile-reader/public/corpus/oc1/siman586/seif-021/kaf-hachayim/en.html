21) There. One who steals a shofar and blows with it — he fulfilled [the obligation], etc. And nevertheless one does not bless on it. Magen Avraham section 4. Eliyah Rabbah Orach Chayyim 7, Chayei Adam siman 141 seif 2, Razin Orach Chayyim 4, Chayei Adam kelal 140 Orach Chayyim 18, Mateh Efraim Orach Chayyim 4, Mishna Berurah Orach Chayyim 9. And even if he acquired it through despair and change of ownership, one could say he should not bless — Eliyah Rabbah Orach Chayyim 4, and likewise Maharikash, for just as we say below siman 649 regarding a stolen lulav — even if he acquired it through change he should not bless on it — the same applies here. However, Peri Chadash disagrees and wrote that this case is different, for the berachah is only on the hearing, and in hearing theft is not relevant — it is fine to bless on it in every case, see there. And likewise is the view of Mateh Efraim there. But Mateh Yehuda brought the words of Maharikash and the aforementioned Peri Chadash and wrote that the main [view] is not to bless, see there. And likewise Hanhagot siman 2 on the words of the aforementioned Peri Chadash that it is not obligatory, and he concluded: nevertheless for practical halachah it should be only a doubt in berachot — one may be lenient, see there. And likewise Mishna Berurah there that even if there was despair and change of ownership, or change through action, likewise it is not fit to bless. And see above siman 25 Orach Chayyim 86 and below siman 649.
