3) Ibid. Its mitzvah is with a ram's horn, and curved. The same applies to every type of sheep, meaning even a female, for they are included in this category so as to recall the Binding of Yitzchak. Indeed, in siman 583 it is written that one eats the head of a sheep, and that is in remembrance of the Binding of Yitzchak. Nevertheless, those who are meticulous customarily seek specifically a ram's horn, since a male more closely resembles the ram of Yitzchak.
