(seif 3) But from idol worship of gentiles and also from gentiles' idol-worship accessories one does not blow; if he blew he fulfills etc. Note: reason explained in Gemara from commandment not to benefit; known from Bach that rabbinic commandments are given to benefit; from Tosafot words.
(There seif 5) Yom Tov of Rosh Hashanah on Shabbat do not blow shofar. Note: I questioned Rosh Hashanah on Shabbat if passed or forgot and blew one or two not finishing third — finish third or not; perhaps Chazal established their words where possible not to nullify; if nullified cannot repair; if not nullified perhaps finish; requires study.
