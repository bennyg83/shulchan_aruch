Rather when it was annulled from erev Yom Tov — see Mishna Berurah what we wrote, that if it was not annulled at the time he sanctified the day, etc. The source of our words is from Rashi's language in Sukkah daf 33a s.v. v'lulav misukah lo yalif, and likewise there, in column 2 s.v. hig, see there. However, in truth it is difficult — for behold the time of the lulav mitzvah begins from dawn onward, and if so even if it was annulled at night it is as before the time. And I found the Baal Aruch LaNer stood on this there, and see there that he elaborated on this.
