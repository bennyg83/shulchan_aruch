(10) Of a gentile; of a Jew. Such as that he worshipped the shofar. And the same applies when he used it before his idol, that he blew before it.
(11) That is not nullified. Meaning: since it can never at any time be permitted, and therefore it is destined for loss (to be burned or brought to the Dead Sea) behold it is as if it does not exist, and it is no better than a kosher shofar that merely lacks the measure of its size, which is invalid; and all the more so here.
(12) But etc. of a gentile. Such as he found a shofar of a non-Jew's idol, or borrowed it from him.
(13) He should not blow. Because it is repulsive. And even when it was nullified it is also forbidden l'chatchila according to several poskim.
(14) He has fulfilled. Even before it was nullified; and the reason is that since it is possible to nullify it according to the law of a non-Jew's idol, which a non-Jew can nullify, it is not appropriate to say regarding it "behold it is as if it does not exist," for it may never come to burning at all; and if because he benefits from things forbidden for benefit; that too is not forbidden, for fulfilling a mitzvah is not called bodily benefit, but rather it is a royal decree. And it is obvious that if he has no other shofar he blows with it l'chatchila so as not to neglect the positive commandment.
(15) And that is provided he did not intend to acquire it. At the time he found it and lifted it from the ground; rather he lifted it only in order to blow with it; and see Biur Halacha.
(16) For it becomes a gentile[-idol] of a Jew. And even though a Jew did not worship it, nevertheless it has no nullification since now it is of a Jew. And some wrote that this refers only to an idol of a gentile, but not to accessories of an idol: for regarding accessories of a gentile's idol, even if they came into a Jew's hand we are not so stringent with them and they have nullification, as the Mechaber ruled in Yoreh Deah siman 146 seif 2; and some are stringent also regarding this.
(17) Only when nullified. The reason is as above: since it is forbidden in benefit, behold it is as lacking from its measure, which is invalid; and even though it is possible to nullify it, nevertheless it has not yet been nullified and stands in its prohibition.
(18) Before Yom Tov. For if it was not nullified at the time the day was sanctified, if so it was not then fit to blow with (according to this method that it is invalid to blow with before nullification) and was then deferred; and if so even if the gentile nullified it afterward on Yom Tov and it became permitted, nevertheless perhaps there is dichuy regarding mitzvos and it was already invalidated. And regarding the practical law we hold like the first opinion, which is the view of most poskim: and even without it being nullified at all he may blow with it if he has no other; only if afterward he finds a kosher shofar he needs to blow with it anew to fulfill this opinion, and without a blessing.
