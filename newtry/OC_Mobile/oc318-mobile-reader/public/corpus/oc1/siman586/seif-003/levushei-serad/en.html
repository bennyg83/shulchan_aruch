Magen Avraham seif katan 3 That it refers to a wild animal. Meaning, horns are a distinguishing sign between a kosher wild animal and a non-kosher wild animal, for a non-kosher wild animal has no horns. They are likewise a sign distinguishing a wild animal from a domesticated animal, since this is recognizable through split horns and the like, as explained there. But a non-kosher domesticated animal does have horns, except for a pig, which has no horns. In my novellae to Chullin, in my work Kad HaKemach, I discussed this at length.
