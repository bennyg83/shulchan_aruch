And that is provided he did not intend to acquire it. Meaning: he takes it from the gentile by way of robbery, and his intention is not to acquire it so that it be his, but only so that he blow with it now; and regarding this the Tur wrote: and even though it is stolen, behold we say that one fulfills [the obligation] with a stolen [shofar]; and so [is written] explicitly in the Remazim. And my master and teacher, of blessed memory, wished to explain this of a shofar of ownerless property, and twisted the matter from its plain sense, and wrote against Beis Yosef (and may his Master forgive him) and that is not according to justice, for he is a man whose Master assists him.
