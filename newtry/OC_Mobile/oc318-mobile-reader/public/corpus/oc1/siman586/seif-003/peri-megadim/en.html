And it (on Taz: the Bach challenged Beis Yosef, [holding] that if the shofar has owners, it is not that a Jew acquired it) even if he intended to acquire it, that is nothing; rather it deals with [a case] of ownerless [property], and "stolen" is not precise. But Taz explains according to its plain sense: that the Jew did not intend to acquire it, only to steal [use] it; but if a non-Jew gives him the shofar as a gift, then it becomes of a Jew, and its measure is crushed. And see Magen Avraham 5, who explains differently; and with Hashem's help it will be clarified there.
