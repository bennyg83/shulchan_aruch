Taz in Bach challenged Beit Yosef: if the shofar has owners, it is not that if a Jew acquired it — even if he intended to acquire it — it is nothing; rather it deals with [idolatry] of ownerless and stolen, in his view. But Taz explains according to its plain sense that the non-Jew did not intend that the Jew acquire it, only to steal it; but if a non-Jew gives him the shofar as a gift, then it is of a Jew, and its measurements [apply].
