[3] Levush: That it should be bent, etc. Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah gives another reason in the name of a disciple of Rashba: so that one will remember to keep his eyes lowered during prayer, just as the shofar is bent downward. See siman 585, seif 1, which states that the mouth of the shofar should be turned upward. Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah assumes simply that the re’em is a non-kosher animal, and this also appears to me from Ran at the beginning of the chapter “Ra’uhu Beis Din,” who writes of animals whose horns are not hollow, such as the re’em and the deer. Magen Avraham, however, writes that it is kosher, because “te’o” is translated as “reima.”
