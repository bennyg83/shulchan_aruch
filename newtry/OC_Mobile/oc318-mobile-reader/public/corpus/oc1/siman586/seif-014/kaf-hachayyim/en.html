As to whether the re'em is a kosher species, see Pri Chadash and Mateh Yosef, who wrote that it is a kosher wild animal, unlike Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos Beit Yosef, who wrote as a simple matter that it is a non-kosher species; see there. So too wrote Magen Avraham, s.k. 3. Knesset HaGedolah himself also retracted, as written in the summaries of his responsa on Yoreh De'ah, printed at the end of the sefer Kol Ben Levi, siman 179; see there. Machazik Berachah, letter 2, cited this.
