In Taz s.k. 10. And behold regarding the explanation of the Rosh's words in Rosh Hashana concerning pierced and he sealed it, they struggled with this; that his words contradict: what he wrote "alternatively like R' Natan, and its majority was diminished," and again he asked according to the latter wording that the Rabbis admit when its majority remained, and he was not comfortable establishing it as "its majority was diminished." See Responsa Mahari Halevi, brother of Taz, siman 28; and my ancestor Maharsha emended that it should read as Tosafos wrote: "alternatively R' Natan, and with what is not of its kind" and this requires further study, for in Asheri he wrote in his difficulty that presumably it deals even of its kind, and if so one cannot answer that it comes like R' Natan and with what is not of its kind; and Tosafos in truth did not write this in their difficulty. Therefore it appears to my humble opinion that the Rosh there wrote that if he did not seal it, it is kosher, for all sounds are kosher, only that the sealing detracts, for it is the sound of a shofar and another matter; and accordingly it appears to me that this is the reason of R' Natan who distinguishes between of its kind and not of its kind: that of its kind it is not detracted because of the sealing and is not called another matter, since it is of its kind, which is not so for not of its kind, for it is the sound of a shofar and another matter. But all this is when its majority remained, which produces a shofar sound (only that it is not as it had been) [then] sealing of its kind helps, since without the sealing it was kosher and the sealing does not detract since it is of its kind. If so, when its majority was diminished, without the sealing there is no shofar sound here but mere puffing, as we say that it makes a barking sound; even of its kind it is invalid. And accordingly the Mishnah is fine like R' Natan: that whenever its majority remained, this is not called "impeding the blast" (what do we care that before the hole there was another sound, since all sounds are kosher) we have only to judge as it is now after the hole, that this sound was kosher and did not impede the blast at all, for the sound as it is after it was pierced is called a shofar sound, and it is not called "impeding the blast" except when its majority was diminished. (And what the Rosh wrote that even if its majority remained it is "impeding the blast" that is when we explain as its plain sense that "impeding the blast" means the sound is not as it was before it was pierced.) And accordingly the Mishnah properly comes like R' Natan: for the Mishnah that says "impeding the blast" means its majority was diminished (for if its majority remained, we do not call it "impeding the blast"; and he properly says that if it impedes the blast it is invalid) namely even of its kind, for R' Natan deals only specifically when its majority remained, then it is kosher of its kind; and that is what he means: if not that it does not impede the blast (namely that its majority remained) and all sounds are kosher, and there is no [problem] because of shofar sound and another matter that is of its kind, then it is kosher. But according to the other wording, that when its majority remained even not of its kind they are kosher, if so necessarily the sealing does not detract to disqualify because of shofar sound and another matter, for they permit even not of its kind; and if so even when its majority was diminished one cannot say that the disqualification is that the sealing detracts, for the sealing does not detract, for otherwise it would be invalid also when its majority remained; and since the sealing does not detract, one may say that it helps to permit, for through it a shofar sound is made; and therefore the reason is that since its majority was diminished it went out from the category of shofar and repair does not help it. And if so one cannot explain "if it impedes the blast" as "its majority was diminished," for we do not come upon it because of impeding the blast, but because it itself is not in the category of shofar; from the principle that all sounds are kosher in a shofar; and therefore when its majority remained is what is dealt with; and if so when its majority remained the Rabbis hold it is kosher. And also one may say that certainly it is not reasonable to permit sealing of its kind if its majority was diminished and there is no shofar sound at all here, that we should say it helps to permit it because of the sealing; and specifically regarding whether the sound through the sealing assists, then we say of its kind is not called shofar sound and another matter; but that the essence of the sound be through of its kind that seals it; it is not reasonable to permit. And that is in truth the reason of the first wording: that of its kind does not help when its majority did not remain; for in the first wording they hold that if its majority did not remain the shofar sound departed from here; and it is not reasonable that the latter wording dispute this; and therefore when he establishes in the latter wording "its majority was diminished," and even so permits of its kind; necessarily in the latter wording they hold that also when its majority was diminished and the sounds are not spoiled so much that it not be considered a shofar sound; rather also when its majority was diminished one may say all sounds are kosher; and therefore he distinguishes between of its kind and not of its kind, for assistance of what is not of its kind causes spoilage; but specifically when its majority was diminished, since it was broken so much, and its breaking proves upon it that it was broken so much that [it is like] the breaking of a vessel, the sealing is considered like the body of the shofar and is not nullified to it, which is not so when its majority remained, which is still a vessel: even though the hole impedes the blast, nevertheless its importance has not departed from it, for all sounds are kosher, and the sealing is nullified to the body of the shofar and we do not reckon it as shofar sound and another matter. And this is necessary: for if you say that a shofar sound whose majority was diminished is not considered a sound like in the first wording (if so why does of its kind help for R' Natan, who is reasonable like the first wording, to make from an invalid sound a kosher sound) for this sealing does not help at all; and therefore in the latter wording he holds that there well is a sound when its majority was diminished, only when its majority was diminished it is a shofar and another matter. And one should not say that the Rabbis hold not like R' Natan in this, and hold that when its majority was diminished its sound is not considered a shofar sound and therefore invalid even of its kind according to the Rabbis, for why make a dispute in this between the Rabbis and R' Natan, for simply one may say they hold that of its kind also detracts; and also if so it would be a dispute in fact: the Rabbis hold this is not a shofar sound and R' Natan holds this too is a shofar sound. It is fine to distinguish in this between the wordings: one holds that R' Natan and the Rabbis both hold it is not a shofar sound when its majority was diminished, and the latter wording holds that the Rabbis and R' Natan both hold it is a shofar sound; but if we establish the dispute of the Rabbis and R' Natan thus, it would be like a dispute of fact. And since we must say in the latter wording that there well is a shofar sound when its majority was diminished, only that they hold that since it has no importance the sealing detracts; (and accordingly one cannot establish the Mishnah like the Rabbis and when its majority was diminished..) one cannot establish the Mishnah like the Rabbis and when its majority was diminished, for if so what is this that it takes "impeding the blast" for it does not depend on this at all, for this is not called "impeding the blast," since all sounds are kosher; and if you say that "impeding the blast" means the blast that was before it was pierced, if so even when its majority remained it impedes the blast that was before the hole, and the Mishnah disqualified "impeding the blast"; and therefore [in] the wording the Rabbis permit when its majority remained, and if so it is difficult: the Mishnah is like whom? And the words of the Rosh are fine: that in the first wording we can well say that the Mishnah is like R' Natan and when its majority was diminished, for "its majority was diminished" and "impeding the blast" are one matter; which is not so in the latter wording, one must explain that "its majority was diminished" does not detract the sound more than when [majority] remained, and in both one may say all sounds are kosher, and the inferiority of diminished versus remaining is regarding that it is not considered important relative to the sealing, if so one cannot establish the Mishnah like the Rabbis, for the Mishnah makes the matter depend on impeding the blast.
