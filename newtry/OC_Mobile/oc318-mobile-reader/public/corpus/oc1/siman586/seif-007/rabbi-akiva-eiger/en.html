Magen Avraham section 24. And he resolved that it deals outside twelve mil. It implies that also on Yom Tov outside twelve mil it is d'oraisa. And see responsa Bach HaChadashot siman 7 and in the last pamphlet there siman 36. And nevertheless I have a question: for from d'oraisa it is permitted by the law of within [the techum], like taking out a shofar to blow with it. And tzarich iyun. And in Sefer Tashbatz part 2 matter 213 it is explained that he holds that also on Yom Tov techum is d'oraisa. And it appears to me there is proof to infer thus from the words of the Rambam chapter 8 halachah 10 of Laws of Eruvin and in Maggid Mishneh: regarding techum there is no distinction between Yom Kippur and Yom Tov.
