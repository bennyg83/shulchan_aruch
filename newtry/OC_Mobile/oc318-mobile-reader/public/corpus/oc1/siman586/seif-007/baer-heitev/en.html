Pierced. Even if a measure enough for a tekiah remained in it, because its sound is defective. Kol Bo; Magen Avraham. And see Taz; and it is obvious that a cracked [shofar] is not included in this, and its law is explained afterward.
Time of need. It should read: the tightness of the seal, for it is forbidden to remove it on Yom Tov, since otherwise he will remove it from there. A shofar that they scraped thin and made iron hoops for it is kosher to blow with. Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 77.
