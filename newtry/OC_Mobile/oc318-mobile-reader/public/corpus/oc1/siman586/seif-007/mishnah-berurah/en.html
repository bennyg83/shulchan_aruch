(26) If it was pierced, etc. I preface this seif with a short introduction, as follows. It is taught in the Gemara: Our Rabbis taught: If it was pierced and he sealed it, whether with of its kind or not of its kind, it is invalid; R' Natan says: of its kind it is kosher, not of its kind it is invalid. And the poskim wrote that we hold like R' Natan; and we say there that that which R' Natan permits of its kind is specifically when its majority remained that was not pierced. And behold regarding the explanation of the sugya there are opinions among the Rishonim: the Rambam and those who assist him explain that R' Natan refers to [a case] where it does not impede the blast (namely after sealing, its sound returned to what it had been initially before it was pierced) and even so it is kosher only of its kind; it turns out we require three [conditions] for excellence: it does not impede the blast, and of its kind, and its majority remained; but if one of these is lacking it is invalid; this is the first opinion brought here. And the Rosh and those who assist him hold that R' Natan refers only to [a case] where it impedes the blast, and even so it is kosher of its kind; and when it does not impede, even not of its kind it is kosher if its majority remained. And automatically according to this explanation it is a leniency, for we require only two (namely of its kind and its majority) and even if it impedes; or its majority and it does not impede, even if not of its kind. And this is what the Mechaber concluded below: and if it is a time of urgency, etc., namely then one may rely on the Rosh and those who assist him.
(27) Even though its sound changed it is kosher. For all sounds are kosher in a shofar, as above; and that is even l'chatchila according to the Mechaber's view. And the poskim wrote that even if a measure of tekiah did not remain in it (namely a full tefach without holes) it is also kosher. Nevertheless the Acharonim wrote that if the majority of the shofar was pierced, even if a full measure of tekiah remained in it, it is also invalid, for its majority is like its entirety; and see Biur Halacha.
(28) However, etc., for some say, etc. They hold that a pierced shofar is invalid like a shofar that cracked, and we should be concerned for this opinion l'chatchila, and even if a measure of tekiah remained in it above toward the mouth that has no hole at all. Nevertheless it implies that also according to this view that is specifically where the hole is felt in the blast, that the sound is flawed through it and its sound changed; but where its sound is clear, such a hole is not significant, and they blow with it according to everyone.
(29) And if he sealed it, etc. Meaning: even though when he did not seal it it is kosher according to the Mechaber, when he sealed it it is worse: for when he sealed with what is not of its kind, if so he does not hear the sound of a shofar alone but the sound of a shofar and the sound of another matter mixed with it; and likewise when he sealed with of its kind and its sound did not return to what it had been initially, behold we hear that the sound of this shofar is not the sound of the first shofar but from the sealing, and the sealing is not nullified, and it too is like the sound of a shofar and another matter mixed with it.
(30) To what it had been initially. Before it was pierced; and all the more so if its sound resembles only the sound of before the sealing.
(31) If [majority] remained, etc. For if its majority did not remain whole it is invalid according to everyone (even if he sealed with of its kind and its sound is as it was initially) for then the name "shofar" is nullified from it and it became a shofar only through this sealing, and it turns out it is a shofar and another matter.
(32) Its majority whole. Meaning: in the length of the shofar toward his mouth.
(33) A time of urgency, etc. And specifically when it was not noticed until Yom Tov, for then it is forbidden to remove the sealing, for it is like repairing a vessel, since through this he validates the shofar; but if it was noticed before Yom Tov he should remove the sealing, and its law is as if he did not seal it, which is kosher [Acharonim].
(34) That no other shofar is available. But if he can obtain another shofar (even if he already blew with a shofar that has two [conditions] for excellence) he should go back and blow, and not recite a blessing [Peri Megadim].
(35) There is [room] to permit, etc. For we rely on the poskim who hold that wherever there are two [reasons] for the good it suffices; and see in Peri Megadim who wrote that it appears he may recite a blessing, and especially according to what Peri Chadash and the Gra wrote, that the main [view] is like this opinion.
(36) And likewise there is [room] to permit, etc., if its majority remained. But when its majority did not remain whole, even if there are two [reasons] for the good, that he sealed with of its kind and its sound returned to be clear as initially before it was pierced; it does not help even according to this opinion, for the reason we wrote above. And where there is majority alone, or there is sealing of its kind alone and he has a doubt about the sealing whether its sound returned to what it had been or not; one should be stringent by law, for it is a Torah-level doubt.
