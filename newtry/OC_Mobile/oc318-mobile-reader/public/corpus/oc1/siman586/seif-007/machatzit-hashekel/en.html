(s.k. 7) One who is forbidden by neder, etc. (as is written in Yoreh De'ah, etc.) Bach. Meaning: here it implies that specifically another person is permitted to blow for him, for mitzvos were not given in order to derive benefit; but he himself should not blow, for there are people who enjoy their own blowings, and that is benefit beyond the mitzvah. And in Yoreh De'ah it wrote that he himself too is permitted to blow; and the Shach there asked that the words of the Shulchan Aruch here and there contradict each other. And in truth the Bach wrote that this is a dispute among the poskim; and he wrote that in a time of need we rely on the poskim who permit that he himself too may blow a mitzvah-tekiah.
