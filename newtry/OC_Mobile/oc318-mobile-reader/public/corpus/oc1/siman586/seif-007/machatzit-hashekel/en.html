(s.k. 7) One forbidden benefit, etc. As written in Yoreh Deah, etc., Bach. Meaning here it implies specifically another person is permitted to blow for him, for mitzvot were not given for benefit; but he himself should not blow, for there are people who enjoy when they themselves blow and bodily benefit cannot be permitted because of the reason mitzvot were not given for benefit. And in Yoreh Deah it is written that he himself is also permitted to blow, and Shach there challenged that Shulchan Aruch's words here and there contradict each other; in truth Bach wrote it is a dispute among poskim. And he wrote in time of need one may rely on the permissive rulings that he himself is also permitted to blow only a mitzva tekiah.
