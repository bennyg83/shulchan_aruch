The Rambam, at the beginning of chapter 1 of Hilchos Shofar, wrote that all shofaros are invalid except for the horn of sheep, and everyone challenged him regarding this. Tur. However, the Orchos Chaim wrote: It is possible that the Rambam's intent was only to exclude the horn of a cow, while the horns of a goat and a male goat are included in the category of sheep, and likewise the horns of ibexes; end quote. The Beit Yosef cited his words. So too wrote the Kesef Mishneh there, although he wrote that this is not the implication of the Rambam's words. The Taz, s.k. 1, cited his words; see there. The sefer Nechpeh BaKesef, folio 184, wrote that in a place where they accepted the rulings of the Rambam, if they do not have a curved sheep's horn they should not recite a blessing over other shofaros; see there. Birkei Yosef, letter 1, cited his words and wrote that this may be questioned. Since there are those who explain the Rambam's words as holding that other shofaros are valid, they may recite a blessing. Although Maran's explanation, and that of those who agree with him, regarding the Rambam's view appears correct, nevertheless the matter has not left the realm of doubt. Since the other poskim plainly hold that one recites a blessing, and there is at least some doubt that the Rambam also holds so, as the other commentators explained him, this is not included in their acceptance to rely upon the Rambam; end quote.
