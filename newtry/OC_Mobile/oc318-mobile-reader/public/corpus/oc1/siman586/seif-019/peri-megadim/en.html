And it appears to me that even etc. See Taz: for the Mechaber also admits that on the wide side, called the upper end (which is wider, [extending] from his mouth upward (see siman 585) there is no advantage whether the inner protrudes or is equal to the outer, only that the outer should not protrude more; but on the narrow side at the place of the mouth, then the inner must protrude a bit beyond the outer, for otherwise he inserts both into the mouth and the sound emerges from both of them, and the Merciful One said one shofar; and see Levush. And even when the inner protrudes on the narrow side, he should place in his mouth only the protrusion alone, as stated.
And know that there are disqualifications of a shofar according to the Torah, and there are rabbinic ones (such as when four thumb-breadths did not remain, in seif 9) for according to the Torah there is no [minimum] measure for a shofar; therefore if there is no other shofar, only one invalid by rabbinic law, he should blow with it. See Magen Avraham, and God willing it will be clarified there.
