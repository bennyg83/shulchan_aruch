19) [Seif 2] One who steals a shofar and sounds it fulfills the obligation, etc. For it is stated in the Yerushalmi: What is the difference between a lulav and a shofar? R' Assi said: Regarding a lulav it is written, 'You shall take for yourselves,' meaning from your own property. Here, however, it is written, 'It shall be a day of sounding for you,' in any manner. The Rambam wrote in chapter 1 that the mitzvah consists only of hearing the sound, and the law of theft does not apply to a sound. The Beit Yosef cited his words; see there.
