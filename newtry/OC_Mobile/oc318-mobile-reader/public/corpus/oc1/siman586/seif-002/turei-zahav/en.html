One who steals a shofar, etc. In the Yerushalmi: what is the difference between this and a lulav? Regarding a lulav it is written "And you shall take for yourselves" of your own; but here it is written "A day of teruah shall be for you." Nevertheless its meaning appears: that regarding a lulav it is written next to the taking, implying that the taking should be of your own; and likewise regarding tzitzis it is written "And they shall make for them" we expound that the making be of theirs and not stolen; which is not so here, where it is written "A day of teruah shall be for you," with "for you" adjacent to teruah, and the making of the shofar is not mentioned at all, meaning that the teruah should in any case be for you from whatever side it may be, without any making at all.
