(9) One who steals a shofar, etc. And it is not similar to a stolen lulav, matzah, and tzitzis, because the mitzvah of shofar is only the hearing alone, and in hearing a sound there are no laws of theft, for in his hearing he does not touch the shofar at all; therefore even though he blew with it with a prohibition of theft, since in the essence of the mitzvah (namely the hearing) there is no prohibition of theft, he has fulfilled his obligation. And all this is when he already blew; but l'chatchila it is forbidden to blow with it against the will of the owner of the shofar, and even if the owners despaired of it; and all the more so one should not recite a blessing over it because of "and the robber blesses..," etc. And see above siman 11 seif 6 in Biur Halacha: that even if there was despair and change of domain or change of deed, it is also not appropriate to recite a blessing. And the Acharonim wrote that it is permitted to take one's fellow's shofar without his knowledge and blow with it, for it is pleasing to a person that a mitzvah be done with his property, and certainly he would consent; and he may blow with it all the hundred sounds even though that is not by law, since they so customed; certainly he will not protest this.
