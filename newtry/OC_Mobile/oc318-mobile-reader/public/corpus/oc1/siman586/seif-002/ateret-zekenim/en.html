One who steals a shofar — even if the owners have not despaired, etc.; however l'chatchila even if they despaired the owners should not blow with it — so is the implication of the poskim who wrote stam b'dieved he fulfilled, but l'chatchila when they wrote "not" it does not imply there is no distinction between despair and no despair. And some say a stolen shofar is invalid (Hagahot Ashiri end of Rosh Hashanah in name of Or Zarua); and specifically if he stole a finished shofar it is invalid, but if he stole a horn and fashioned it — he owes him money and it is kosher, as the Yerushalmi explains.
