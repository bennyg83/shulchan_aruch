Marginal note: It is a wonder in my eyes how the words of the Rosh in Rosh Hashanah, chapter 3, siman 12, escaped his notice (that he wrote: even when it is not of its kind, if the sound is as it was before it was pierced) even though before the sealing it was hindering the tekiah; it is kosher. And still more: does he not rule here in the Shulchan Aruch, "And likewise one should permit when they sealed it not of its kind, if its majority remained and its sound returned to what it was at first before it was pierced" (and that is the view of the Rosh, upon whom he relies in a time of need) see there.
