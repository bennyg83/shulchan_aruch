If he sounded it, he fulfilled his obligation. For there is no theft in sound. See the end of siman 454. Nevertheless, no blessing is recited over it; see the end of siman 691. It appears to me that one may take another person’s shofar without permission and recite the blessing over it, as stated in siman 649, seif 5. Although a shofar deteriorates slightly through sounding, a tallis likewise deteriorates slightly and it is nevertheless permitted, as stated in siman 14. There is no concern that he will sound it excessively, as there is regarding sefarim, for why would he sound more than is required for his obligation?
