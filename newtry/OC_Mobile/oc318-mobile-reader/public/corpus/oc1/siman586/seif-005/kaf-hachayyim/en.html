5) All of this applies only on Rosh Hashanah itself, when the time for sounding has arrived and they are deliberating as to which shofar is more fit to sound. Before Rosh Hashanah, however, when the obligation of sounding has not yet taken effect, one may purchase a goat's horn even though a ram's horn is also available, but it must be purchased for a high price, and he knows that there will be no ram's horn in his city on Rosh Hashanah. Even so, there is no obligation to purchase the ram's horn for a high price. Rather, it is governed by the law of enhancing a mitzvah up to one-third, but no more, as explained below regarding the lulav. Bach; Ateres Zekeinim. Mateh Ephraim, letter 1; Mishna Berurah, s.k. 5.
