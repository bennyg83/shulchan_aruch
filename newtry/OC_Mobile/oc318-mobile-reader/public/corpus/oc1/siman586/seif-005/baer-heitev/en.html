Another. And in a time of need it is permitted to blow for himself. Bach.
Konam. This is the language of a neder, whereby he forbids the object upon himself, and nedarim take effect on a mitzvah-object as on an optional matter, as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 215; consequently, if he said that he will not hear the sound of the shofar, that is the language of a shevuah, whereby he forbids himself regarding the object, and a shevuah does not take effect on a mitzvah-matter (see Taz).
