(ב) But if he said etc. Nedarim 16b — see Tosafot Rosh Hashanah and as Ran and Rosh wrote there: Rava does not argue with Abaye except when he said benefit of a mitzva — mitzvot were not given for benefit; but when he said sitting etc. it is profane, since he specified to forbid himself, as one who forbids himself to throw a pebble into the sea even though there is benefit in it. Ran wrote: if he said konam sukkah upon me, it is permitted to sit in sitting of a mitzva since he did not specify as above; and this is what is written here konam for its tekiah etc. — he specified explicitly for its tekiah.
