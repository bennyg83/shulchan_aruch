Ibid. That the re'em is kosher. He wrote this because Tosafot wrote there in Rosh Hashanah that the shofar of a re'em is invalid because it is called a horn, as it is written, 'and his horns are the horns,' which implies that without this reason it would be valid even though it is a non-kosher species, which would prove like the Olas Shabbos. Therefore he wrote that in truth it is kosher, for in Parashas Re'eh, when the verse enumerates the kosher wild animals, it writes there, 'the dishon and the te'o,' and Targum Onkelos renders dishon as reima. Furthermore, according to one explanation in Tosafot in Chullin there, horns are a sign of a kosher wild animal.
