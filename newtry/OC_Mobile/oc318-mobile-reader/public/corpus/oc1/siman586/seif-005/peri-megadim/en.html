Konam; on Taz: that if he says regarding one person [alone], whether "konam" or a shevuah, it certainly takes effect, for he did not swear to cancel a mitzvah, since it is possible to hear from another person; and if he forbids in an inclusive [formulation] as well, one can say it takes effect when [the mitzvah can still be done] by remaining seated [passive]; and see Eliyah Rabbah letter 9 and [siman] 529, se'if 7 (see there).
