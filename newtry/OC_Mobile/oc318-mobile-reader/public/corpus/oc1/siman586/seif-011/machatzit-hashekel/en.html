(s.k. 11) And there remained, etc., as the Bach wrote explicitly regarding a hole. And for this reason Taz in truth wrote that the Shulchan Aruch deals specifically where its sound changed through the tightening; its law is like one who plated it with gold on the outside in se'if 16; and see Peri Chadash, who also wrote that he did not know the reason for this law.
As if they sealed it somewhat, meaning: certainly it is not a complete seal; for if so it would be a seal not of its kind, which is invalid, as written in se'if 7 regarding a hole; rather regarding this it is [treated as] a seal, [so] we require that a measure enough for a tekiah remain.
