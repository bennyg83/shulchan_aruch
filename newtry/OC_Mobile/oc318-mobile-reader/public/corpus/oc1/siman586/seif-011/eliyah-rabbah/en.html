[11] Levush: But after the fact, etc. Shulchan Aruch does not say this. Rather, it states: “If it is a pressing circumstance and no other shofar is available, etc.” It therefore appears that if another shofar is available, even after he already sounded this one he has not fulfilled his obligation and must sound a valid shofar afterward. It also appears that if the greater part did not remain, then even if the hole was sealed with the same material and its sound returned to what it had been before the hole was formed, it remains invalid even in a pressing circumstance. This requires study.
