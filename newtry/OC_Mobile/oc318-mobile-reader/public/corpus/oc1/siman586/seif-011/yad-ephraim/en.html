In Magen Avraham s.k. 18. At the wide place (see siman 11); and see there in Magen Avraham in the name of Bach that Tosafos were in doubt about the matter; and see what I wrote at length in my book Beis Ephraim on the section of the lungs, siman 36, and in Korban HaEdah there; and accordingly even if there are not four thumb-breadths at the wide place, only at the narrow place, one may also blow with it when there is no other shofar, only that one should not recite a blessing over it.
