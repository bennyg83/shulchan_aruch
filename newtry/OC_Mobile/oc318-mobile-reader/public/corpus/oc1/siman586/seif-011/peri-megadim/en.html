Even to any extent; on Taz: the distinction is that when it is pierced, there is a deficiency; therefore some say one should not blow with it, because of "offer it, please, to your governor" then its sound is defective; see Magen Avraham letter 8, and with Hashem's help it will be clarified there. And if they sealed it of its kind it is kosher, provided there is not a crack of any extent in it; and even without sealing it is kosher, for the wind has a place to exit and does not crack it entirely, so that we should not say "whatever stands to crack is as if cracked" (which is not so regarding a crack. And see Levush, who wrote "a round hole") it appears at first glance that even though there is no deficiency, [as] by a nail and the like, so long as the hole is round the tekiah has no force to crack [it], which is not so when it is truly lengthwise. And according to Taz there is no distinction except when there is a place for the wind to exit, it does not crack; and possibly even without a deficiency, so long as there is a hollow in the hole, it does not crack.
