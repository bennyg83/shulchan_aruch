The horn of a cow is invalid, etc. The horns of the buffalo are invalid for use as a shofar, for the buffalo that we call by that name is the wild ox, which we rule is a domesticated animal; see Yoreh De'ah, siman 80. If so, it is obvious that it is invalid. She'eilas Yaavetz, volume 1, siman 50; Shaarei Teshuvah, letter 3. This is the species called in Arabic jamus. So wrote Ibn Ezra on the Twelve Prophets, Amos 5:22; see there. His words were cited in Zevachei Tzedek on Yoreh De'ah, siman 28, letter 16. See there, where he brought further support for this; see there.
