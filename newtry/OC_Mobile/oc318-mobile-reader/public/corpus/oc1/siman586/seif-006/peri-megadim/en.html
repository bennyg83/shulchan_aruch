If it was pierced; on Taz: Rosh Hashanah folio 27b; and see Beis Yosef at length. And in a time of need when there is no other shofar, even with two favorable factors it is kosher; of its kind, and its majority intact, even though it hinders the tekiah; or its majority [intact] and it does not hinder. And when not of its kind, one may rely on the Rosh who permits; and it appears that this is with a blessing. And in the Levush: after the fact, if he blew he has fulfilled his obligation; and in Eliyah Rabbah and Or Zarua letter 7: if there is another shofar he returns and blows with it, and it appears without a blessing. Further there: if its majority did not remain, even though there are two favorable factors, it is invalid even after the fact (see there).
