(In Shulchan Aruch seif 6) Or very thin; in the Gemara it is "or needs [repair]," and Rashi explained: dry, etc. and in the Tur he wrote: or in whatever manner it may be; and the Mechaber omitted this, and one must say that it is included in what he wrote "very thick."
In Magen Avraham s.k. 9. It should read: it deals with a case where he tightened the sealing and it is forbidden to remove it, thus it should read; meaning that above in siman 314 it is [stated] that if it was tightened it is forbidden to remove; and possibly what is written "it appears to me" is a scribal error and it needs to be "it should read: that he tightened, etc."
