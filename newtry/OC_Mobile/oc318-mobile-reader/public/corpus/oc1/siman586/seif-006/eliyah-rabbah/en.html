[6] Levush: He should not sound it, etc. This implies even after the owners have despaired of recovering it, and Bach rules accordingly. It appears that this applies specifically where he stole a shofar. If he stole a horn and fashioned it into a shofar, it is permitted, as stated in siman 649. This requires study, however, for perhaps it was already called a shofar before it was fashioned. Regarding the blessing, see siman 649.
