Across its width. See Taz: whatever is [only] in its circumference; the crack does not keep widening further. And apparently according to the first opinion in seif 8, that if cracked lengthwise in its minority it is invalid; how can one conceive of the width of its circumference without any [crack] in length? And who can decide that it will not crack lengthwise? And to say that lengthwise it is apt to crack of itself by chance-development, and the crack continues and lengthens through blowing (which is not so across the width, where it is apt [to crack] only through a person's striking, and then we are not concerned, etc.) this is forced; and one may say [so].
