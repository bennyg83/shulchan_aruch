13) A person sounding the shofar came with a curved horn in his hand to sound, prepared it in the manner of those who sound the shofar, and held it ready to recite the blessing and sound it. In the meantime, a Jew emerged holding a ram's horn. The designation of the first shofar, through his having prepared and readied it, is of no consequence, and he should sound the ram's horn. The same applies whenever a more choice means of performing a mitzvah becomes available to him before the mitzvah takes effect. Birkei Yosef, letter 2.
