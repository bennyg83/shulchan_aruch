S.k. 21. And if he put gold, etc. (meaning: according to that interpretation one may ask how he stated plainly that not at the place of placing the mouth it is kosher) for it is outside and depends on whether its sound changed, etc. and upon this the Rosh answered: yes indeed, if it would change it would be invalid; only that ordinarily because of gold of any amount that he made for placing the mouth, its sound does not change (see the Rosh there); which is not so according to the first interpretation, where "not the place of placing the mouth" refers to the outer side of the thickness of the shofar, if so it has no relation to the case of plating it from outside, which refers to the length of the shofar; otherwise there is no difficulty.
