[13] Levush: Some permit, etc. This requires study, for Shulchan Aruch implies that all agree to this. Perhaps Levush reasoned that since Rosh and Tur did not mention it, they prohibit it. In my humble opinion, there is a kal vachomer to permit it. Ran maintains that even a split of the slightest amount remains invalid after gluing, yet he nevertheless validates it when bound with a cord. All the more so should Rosh and Tur, who validate a slight split when glued, validate it when bound.
