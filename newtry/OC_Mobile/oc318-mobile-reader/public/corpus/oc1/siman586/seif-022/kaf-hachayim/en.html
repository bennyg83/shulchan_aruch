22) However — it is clear that even if one acquired a stolen shofar, he should not blow with it if he has another suitable one, lest it become his advocate his prosecutor, as we say regarding lulav in Yerushalmi derech perek lulav hagazul. Peri Chadash. Mishna Berurah in Shaar HaTziyun 19. And regarding blessing, Peri Chadash's view appears to be to bless, and likewise Mateh Efraim there. But other Acharonim hold one does not bless, as written in the previous Orach Chayyim.
