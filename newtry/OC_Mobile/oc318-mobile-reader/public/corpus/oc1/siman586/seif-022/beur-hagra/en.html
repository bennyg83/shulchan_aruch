Seif 22: If a non-Jew, etc. According to Hagahos Ashri, this applies specifically in such a case, because it is not muktzeh, since it is permitted to another Jew, and it may therefore be moved, as explained above in siman 515. It may likewise be sounded, because mitzvos were not given for personal benefit. This accords with Rashi, who forbids use because one may not benefit from melachah performed on Yom Tov, and also with Tosafos, who forbids it lest one instruct the non-Jew, for there is no concern that one will sin in order to perform a mitzvah through a transgression. Mordechai, however, permits it even where it is muktzeh, based upon the third chapter of Pesachim, as explained below.
The same applies if, etc. Muktzeh is permitted for the sake of a mitzvah, as stated in Pesachim 48a: “One might think that he should not bring, etc.” Nor is it invalid because it is a mitzvah performed through a transgression, just as a stolen shofar is not invalid. Mordechai, chapter 3 of Sukkah. See what I wrote above in siman 655.
