(87) Outside the techum — meaning even outside twelve mil, which is d'oraisa according to some poskim; and if so, according to them it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to bring it, for they did not permit in the previous seif only something that is d'rabbanan. Nevertheless, if he brought it, even for the sake of a Jew, it is permitted to blow with it. And the reason: for behold something that comes from outside the techum is permitted in moving, and it is forbidden only in eating or benefit — namely to use it, as written in siman 495. And this that one blows with it a tekiah of a mitzvah is not considered benefit, as we wrote in siman 586. And nevertheless it is forbidden to move it in the city street whose entrances are not fixed in the form of a doorway outside four cubits, as explained in siman 495 [but if they are fixed through eruvin as required, it is considered like a house and permitted to move throughout]. A Jew who sent a shofar to another city through a non-Jew before Rosh Hashana, and the non-Jew was delayed and brought it on Rosh Hashana from outside the techum, and the people of the city did not know they would send them a shofar — one may not move it, and even blow with it if possible with another; and in a time of need there is [room] to permit. And the source of this law was explained at the end of siman 495 in the gloss, see-there, in Mishna Berurah:

(2) (Peak) If a foreigner did something, etc. - even for Israel and Meiri that the foreigner did from his own horn, it is not assigned to him, but it is forbidden to shake it or even stick in it, it is assigned to him, and even if he did it on the first day of Israel, it is also forbidden on Monday, per the law, an egg that was born on the first day of Dasura also on Monday, etc. And it is permissible to blow it so as not to invalidate the M.A. and it is permissible to blow it even on Monday and the 7th that are held in his hand, it is permissible to blow it per the custom, but if the obligee blows the shofar and puts the shofar out of his hand, he must not shake it in order to blow it per the custom [Meta Ephraim].
