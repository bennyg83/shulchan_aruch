Is it fitting that the very object that became his accuser should become his advocate, as we say regarding a lulav in the Yerushalmi at the beginning of the chapter Lulav HaGazul? Pri Chadash; Mishna Berurah in Shaar HaTziyun, letter 19. Regarding the blessing, Pri Chadash appears to hold that one should recite it, and so too wrote Mateh Ephraim there. However, the view of the other later authorities is that one should not recite a blessing, as written in the preceding letter.
