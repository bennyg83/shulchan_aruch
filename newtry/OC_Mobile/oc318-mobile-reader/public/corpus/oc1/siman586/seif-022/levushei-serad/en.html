Ibid. That if he says, 'dwelling in a sukkah is konam upon me,' or even if he says, 'konam that I will not dwell in a sukkah,' this is the correct reading. Although in the formulation 'konam that I will not dwell in a sukkah' he forbade himself upon the sukkah, nevertheless, since he mentioned the term konam, which is a vow, we rearrange the wording he stated and say that his intent was to forbid the object upon himself, and it takes effect even regarding a mitzvah matter.
