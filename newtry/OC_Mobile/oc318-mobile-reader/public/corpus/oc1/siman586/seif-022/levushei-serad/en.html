There [Ran] that if one says sitting in the sukkah upon me konam, or even if he says konam I will not sit in the sukkah — so it should read; and although in the language konam I will not sit in the sukkah he forbade himself on the sukkah, nevertheless since he mentioned the language konam which is a vow, we attribute to the language he said; and we say his intention is to forbid the object upon him, and it takes effect on a matter of mitzva.
