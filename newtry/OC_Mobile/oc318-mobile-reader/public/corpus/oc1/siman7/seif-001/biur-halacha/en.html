To pray immediately—see Mishna Berurah in the name of Aruch HaShulchan, and see Peri Megadim, who was uncertain whether the opportunity for keri might arise; another doubt according to our teacher: one who wants to taste before prayer something whose dipping is in liquid—there are two uncertainties: perhaps the halacha follows those who say that on something dipped in liquid one must bless Asher Yatzar, and furthermore perhaps the halacha follows those who say that all day one must bless before prayer—see there. And Aruch HaShulchan agreed that he may bless Asher Yatzar if this occurred after he relieved himself for adults, and not because of a double uncertainty—for in this case his intent is not to bless; rather, because otherwise the opinion of the majority of poskim is that for prayer he may bless in this case.
