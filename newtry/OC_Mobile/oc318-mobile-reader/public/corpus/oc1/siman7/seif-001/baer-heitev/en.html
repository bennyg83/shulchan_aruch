Asher Yatzar. Rashal wrote in a responsum, siman 97: One who ate something that obligates a concluding blessing and forgot, and urinated, and remembered before coming to bless Asher Yatzar—which of them comes first? He answered that Asher Yatzar comes first, because it is more frequent—see there. And likewise in Orach Tzadikim. One who drinks a laxative, who from the outset has in mind to evacuate his bowels until the filth is cleared from them—for certainly his mind is on this and he did not distract his mind; therefore he should not bless until after he finishes evacuating—Leket Yosher, part 1, siman 86. And the author of Yad Aharon disagrees with him and holds that he should bless each time—see there. He wrote in Heichal HaKodesh that one who comes out of the bathroom must pour water on them three times, and Magen Avraham disagrees with him—see there.
