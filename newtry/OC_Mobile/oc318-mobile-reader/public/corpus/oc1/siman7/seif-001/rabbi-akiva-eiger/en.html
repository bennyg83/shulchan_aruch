{Rama: Seif 1:} One blesses Asher Yatzar. If one ate something and became obligated in a concluding blessing and relieved himself — he blesses Asher Yatzar first, and afterward the concluding blessing. Magen Avraham siman 165, seif katan 3 in the name of Maharshal. If he urinated before Birkas HaMazon — there is doubt whether he should not precede Birkas HaMazon, which is Biblical, or nevertheless more frequent takes precedence. And it is possible it depends on the requirement of the Gemara in Zevachim — frequent and holy, which of them takes precedence. One may say Biblical regarding Rabbinic is like holy, and the Rambam ruled that holy and frequent — one may precede whichever he wishes. See Tzelach Berachos 44b s.v. Ribi"l and Rif Pashtos s.v. shehayin, and in responsa Noda B'Yehuda siman 39. And there is doubt for practical ruling.
