1) [Seif 1] All day when he relieves himself, etc. He blesses Asher Yatzar. One who ate something that obligates upon it a concluding blessing of Borei Nefashos Rabos and forgot, and urinated—he should bless Asher Yatzar, for it is more frequent. And furthermore, the blessing of Asher Yatzar has no minimum measure, but a concluding blessing has a measure of a kezayis and a revi'is; therefore it is fitting to give precedence to the blessing of Asher Yatzar—Orach Tzadikim, seif katan 1, in the name of Rashal in his responsum, siman 97; Eliyahu Rabbah, siman 165; and likewise Magen Avraham there, end of seif katan 3, in the name of the Noda B'Yehuda; and likewise Shulchan Aruch HaRav in this siman, letter 2; Eliyahu Rabbah, seif katan 2; Rebbe Tzaddok, letter 7; Aruch HaShulchan, siman 165, letter 1, in the name of Mateh Moshe, siman 365; and likewise Ra'avad Kaful in this siman; and likewise the compiler in Hilchos Berachos, letter 1, siman 2 of Yoreh De'ah, letter 6; Ruach Chayyim, letter 1; and our teacher the Penei Yitzchak, may he live long, set 1, letter 10; and likewise the rabbi the son of Avraham Chaim, may he live long, parashas Vayeitzei, letter 13. And if he urinated before Birkas HaMazon—in the novellae of Ra'avad Kaful there he wrote that it is uncertain which he should precede; but from the words of Ruach Chayyim there it appears that the blessing of Asher Yatzar comes first—see there. And so one should conduct himself, for we are concerned lest he forget.
