Or. See Taz. I explained in siman 4, ot 1, that even according to our practice we can apply the Rosh's reason in the morning. The rest of the day is different, because it is uncommon for one's hands to be soiled precisely when he wishes to pray; and for Torah study they did not institute this blessing, but only for Keriat Shema and prayer, as the Rama writes in his gloss there.
