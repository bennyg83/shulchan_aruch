The law of blessing Asher Yatzar all day after urination — it contains four clauses.
All day when one relieves oneself, whether minor or major matters, one blesses Asher Yatzar and not on hand-washing, even if one wishes to study or pray immediately. Rema: If his hands were soiled such that he rubbed them [clean], he does not bless on hand-washing (Semag siman 27, 49).
