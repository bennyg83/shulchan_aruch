דין לברך ברכת אשר יצר כל היום אחר הטלת מים. ובו ד סעיפים:
כל היום כשעושה צרכיו The law of blessing Asher Yatzar all day after urination — it contains four clauses. All day when one relieves oneself, whether minor or major matters, one blesses Asher Yatzar and not on hand-washing, even if one wishes to study or pray immediately.If his hands were soiled such that he rubbed them [clean], he does not bless on hand-washing (Semag siman 27, 49).
