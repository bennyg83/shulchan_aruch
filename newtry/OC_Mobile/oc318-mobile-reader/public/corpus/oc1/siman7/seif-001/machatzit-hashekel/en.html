(Seif katan 1) To bless twice. And Bach, etc.: Orchos Chayim wrote in the name of the Gaon to bless twice, and he brought proof from prayer—for we establish that one who erred and did not pray Shacharis prays Mincha twice. Behold, completion is required; the same applies here. And therefore Bach [says] that here it is different, for this is a blessing of thanksgiving, and once suffices, as when one eats, etc.

And see Taz, what he wrote to reconcile. And regarding siman 178, if he changed his place while eating.

However, specifically regarding morning ruach ra — meaning that this is what the Zohar deals with.

And see Magen Avraham below, end of siman 165, who brought in the name of Maharshal, who wrote in a responsum, siman 97: If one ate something that obligates upon it a concluding blessing, forgot, and urinated before blessing the concluding blessing—he must bless Asher Yatzar first, for it is more frequent, for it is possible for him a day or two days without a concluding blessing—and this means without eating; but to urinate in any case he must—and if so, he must bless then Asher Yatzar. And furthermore, every concluding blessing requires a measure, and Asher Yatzar does not require a measure; and if he discharged only a single drop, he must say Asher Yatzar, as written at the end of the siman. And a blessing that does not require a measure is more important and must be given precedence.
