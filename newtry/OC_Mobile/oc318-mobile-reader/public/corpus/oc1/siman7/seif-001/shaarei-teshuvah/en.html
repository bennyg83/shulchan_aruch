Asher Yatzar. Ba'er Heitev wrote regarding one who drinks a cup of laxative, etc. — Shalmei Tzibbur wrote the custom is widespread to bless Asher Yatzar each time — see there. However it appears that with strong diarrhea where he feels he will immediately need to evacuate again, it is forbidden to bless, as below siman 92, where even Bedi'eved it is forbidden — see there. And so is explained from what Maharshal wrote in the name of responsa 20 to Mahara Nachum, who were asked about one who relieved himself or urinated and did not bless Asher Yatzar — how long does he have to bless? He concludes that if he remembered before he began to feel the urge, obviously he blesses; if he remembered after he began to feel the urge, he does not bless; and certainly so here. And what he wrote regarding leaving the bathroom — see Eliyah Rabbah, evident from siman 165 there is no need; and see siman 227. Nevertheless some are stringent on themselves, as written above siman 4 in the name of Machatzis HaShekel, that elderly rabbis do thus — to wash three times after leaving the bathroom — see there.
