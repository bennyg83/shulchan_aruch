[1] He blesses, etc. It is impossible that he did not touch the filthy flesh—this requires study, for below, at the beginning of siman 170, it is explained that it is possible with minors; and likewise it is explained below, siman 164. Also in Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch this reason is not mentioned, but rather because of cleanliness or because of preparation. And according to this it would seem that even without having touched a filthy place he must perform netilah, and so it appears from Bach. Sefer Chasidim wrote, siman 462: when one takes a wipe in his hand to wipe himself and sees that he did not need it and did not relieve himself for adults or minors, and removed the wipe from his hand—even so he should not touch books until he washes his hands.
