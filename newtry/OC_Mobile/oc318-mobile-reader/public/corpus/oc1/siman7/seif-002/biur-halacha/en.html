One who urinated and did not rub—this seif does not discuss prayer, for there one must perform netilah even without having urinated at all. And know further that if he rubbed, he is required by law to perform netilah; and if he rubbed with one hand, he is not required by law except for that hand, as is evident in the tractate Yoma 30a: the rabbis taught, "The halacha is at a meal," etc.—see there—and the second [hand] is only because of preparation.

Or because of preparation—see below, siman 91, seif 2, and it is uncertain; perhaps to this the Gra directed what he cited. And see chapter 1 of Shabbos, Rashi Levi, page 10a, from which the law of Shulchan Aruch flows.
