One who urinated and did not rub [his hands], etc.—even netilas yadayim he does not need. And likewise in Semag: seven things require netilah after them—the one who comes from [attending] the dead and from the bathhouse, and one who cuts his nails, and one who removes his shoes, and one who touches his feet, and one who combs his head, and one who kills a louse (Mordechai, Berachos). See above, siman 4, seif 18, who counted several other things that require netilah—end quote.
