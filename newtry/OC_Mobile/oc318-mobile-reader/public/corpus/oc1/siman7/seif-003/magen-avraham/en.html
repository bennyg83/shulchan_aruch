“To bless twice.” Bach wrote that it is a berachah of praise and once suffices, like one who eats twice and makes one blessing after both; so too Shelah in his rabbi’s name—see there—and one may be lenient in a doubtful berachah, siman 178 and gloss. Sefer Heichal Hakodesh writes that one leaving the privy should pour on the hands three times, as cited in the Zohar. However, specifically for morning ruach ra they say the kabbalistic “bat chorin” is particular about three pourings—so there is no proof from the Zohar for other cases.
