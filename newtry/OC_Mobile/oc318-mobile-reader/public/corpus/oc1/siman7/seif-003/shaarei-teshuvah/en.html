Twice. Ba'er Heitev. And he wrote in She'eilat Ya'avetz part 1 siman 15 that although one may reconcile the words of the Gaon, nevertheless it appears one should not bless except once; and greater than this — if he did not bless Asher Yatzar until he needed to urinate again, he again has no [obligation] to bless on the first until he urinates again and blesses once Asher Yatzar and no more — see there. And see seif katan 1 what he wrote in the name of responsa of Mahara Nachum — thus is the ruling. And he wrote in Birkei Yosef that one who urinated, reconsidered, and urinated a second time, and afterward was uncertain whether he blessed even once — if not, he should not bless at all, lest perhaps he blessed; and per the Acharonim one blessing suffices, and now he would bless an unnecessary blessing — see there. And Machatzis HaShekel wrote the reason that here there is a double doubt, and therefore to require a blessing — see there.
