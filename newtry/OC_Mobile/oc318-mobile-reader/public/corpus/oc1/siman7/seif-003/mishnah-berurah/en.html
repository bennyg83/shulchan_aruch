(6) Twice — as we find regarding prayer, that if one erred and did not pray Shacharis, he prays Mincha twice. But many Acharonim disagree with this ruling and hold that prayer is different, for it is in place of a sacrifice and compensation applies to it; not so with Asher Yatzar, which is a blessing of thanks — one gives thanks once for many times, like one who ate twice and diverted his attention in between, who blesses Birkas HaMazon once for both. And one may be lenient in a doubtful blessing. Lechatchilah it is proper to be careful to bless Asher Yatzar immediately after urinating, lest he need to urinate again afterward; and the same applies for major needs.
