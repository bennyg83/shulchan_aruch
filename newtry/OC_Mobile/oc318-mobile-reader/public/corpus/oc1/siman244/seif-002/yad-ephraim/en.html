In Magen Avraham, seif katan 2, “privately” is the correct reading. His intent is that where the work is private, even those who know that it belongs to a Jew do not suspect him. They judge him favorably and assume that the gentile is acting permissibly under a fixed-price arrangement or the like. Public work is different. Even if the Jew lives among gentiles, we remain concerned for guests and members of his household. Since they see him directing that public work be performed on his property on Shabbos, they may falsely suspect him and assume that he hired the gentile by the day while claiming that the arrangement was contractual, as Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 4.
