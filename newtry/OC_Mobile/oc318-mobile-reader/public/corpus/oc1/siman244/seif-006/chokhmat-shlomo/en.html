There, seif 6: A Jew who purchases the customs rights and rents a non-Jew, etc. Note: Regarding a case in which townspeople rent the bathhouse to a Jew for the entire year, and whether the renter may sublet the Shabbos days to a non-Jew, see my work on Even HaEzer, ninth edition, siman 192, in a responsum to the province of Wallachia, where I discussed this incidentally. Regarding one who sells something to a non-Jew through a permitted sale for Shabbos, whether it suffices to write one bill of sale, take the item back on Sunday, and give it to him again the following Friday, repeating this each week, or whether a new bill of sale is required every Friday because of concern that the prior obligation was waived, together with all the laws and details arising from this question, see my work on Choshen Mishpat, third edition, siman 287, in a responsum to the community of Kolomyya, where I discussed it at length, with Hashem's help.
