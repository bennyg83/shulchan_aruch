Taz, seif katan 2: The correct reading is: “If they investigate the matter, they will find that it is so.” This means that they need not discover that the truth is a sharecropping arrangement, for in fact it is a rental. Rather, since rental is even more readily permitted than sharecropping, it is permitted, as I wrote at the beginning of siman 243. Taz therefore writes that after investigation they will at least find that the non-Jew receives a share of the produce. Even if they do not learn that he takes all the produce, no improper consequence results, for sharecropping is also permitted. Here, by contrast, since contracting is less readily permitted than sharecropping, we are concerned that observers will investigate and discover that the Jew takes all the produce. They will then know that the non-Jew is not a sharecropper and may suspect that he is a day laborer.
