Seif 5: If he hired, etc.; and there is one who says. This differs from fixing a price and from contracted work discussed above, because the Jew does not profit from the work the non-Jew performs on Shabbos. Rambam derived from the law of sheep placed in a pen, Moed Katan 12a, that a worker hired for a year is permitted, as stated in siman 537, seif 14.
Provided that he does not calculate. See Tosafos, Kesubos 64b, s.v. demichzei, etc.
And not. This follows the Yerushalmi cited above.
Specifically. For otherwise it is regarded as a case where no fixed price was set, as explained there.
