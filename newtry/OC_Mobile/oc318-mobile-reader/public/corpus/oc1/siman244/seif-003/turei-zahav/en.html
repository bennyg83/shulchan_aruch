It is proper to be stringent, etc. In siman 344 Tur wrote the law of techum Shabbat that whatever is forbidden on Shabbat within the techum is forbidden there even outside the techum, since one can walk there there is suspicion; and he wrote there that even if the house was built in prohibition via a non-Jew one should be stringent not to enter it, end of his words; and his reason is explained in Beit Yosef there per the Gemara in the chapter Mi she'Hafach that Mar Zutra built a storehouse for him as kablan outside the techum; R' Safra and R' Huna bar Chinena came and did not enter it; some say he also did not enter; and Shmuel said kablan outside techum — a prominent man is different; and one says his supporters helped him with straw in the storehouse so it is forbidden; and this certainly refers to Shabbat outside techum, etc.
