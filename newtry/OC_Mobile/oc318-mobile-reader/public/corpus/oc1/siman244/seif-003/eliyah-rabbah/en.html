[3] Levush: To build him a house, etc. Levush changed the wording of the Shulchan Aruch, which says “his house,” repeating the possessive in order to teach that even if it stands in a place where it is not known to belong to him, it is prohibited because of his neighbors. This is unlike the bathhouse at the end of siman 243, where the profits belong to the non-Jew. Perhaps the Shulchan Aruch also did not mean its wording precisely.
