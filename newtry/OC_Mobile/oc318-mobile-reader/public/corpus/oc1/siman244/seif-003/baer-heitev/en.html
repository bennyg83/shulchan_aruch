It is proper to be stringent. Bach writes that regarding contracted work one may rely on Rabbeinu Tam after the fact, and it is permitted. The same applies if he prepared the stones, in which case it is permitted to set them in place. Magen Avraham; Taz. (Eliyah Rabbah proves that the meaning of Bach is that it is proper to be stringent in accordance with the Shulchan Aruch.)
