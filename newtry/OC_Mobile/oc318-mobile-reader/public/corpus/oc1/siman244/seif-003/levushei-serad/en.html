Magen Avraham, seif katan 1: “Meaning, there is no concern.” His intent is that the wording of the Shulchan Aruch, which follows Rambam, supports Mahari Abuhav's view cited by Magen Avraham in siman 243, seif katan 2. Rambam therefore writes that the non-Jew works “for himself,” teaching that the Jew derives no benefit from it.
