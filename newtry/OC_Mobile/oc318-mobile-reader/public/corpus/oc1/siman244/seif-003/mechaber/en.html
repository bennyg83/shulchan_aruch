If non-Jews built a house for a Jew on Shabbos in a prohibited manner, it is proper to be stringent and not enter it. {Rama: If, however, the Jew stipulated with the non-Jew that he not perform melacha for him on Shabbos, and the non-Jew nevertheless performed it against the Jew's wishes in order to complete his work sooner, there is no concern. [Mordechai, beginning of chapter Mi SheHafach; Rabbeinu Yeruham; Beit Yosef; see also siman 543]}
