(23) Known to belong to a Jew. This does not mean that the work is done on the Jew's premises, for then even ordinary movable work would be forbidden, as stated in siman 252:2. It refers to work done elsewhere. Magen Avraham and the other later authorities explain that this applies specifically when a ship is built in a visible and public place. If it is done privately, one need not be stringent, as is evident from siman 252:3.
