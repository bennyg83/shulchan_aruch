Seif katan 3: Meaning: The wording of the Shulchan Aruch, “to perform work for him on Shabbos,” could mislead one into thinking that it is prohibited by law only where he expressly hired him for Shabbos. Therefore, Magen Avraham explains that this is not the intent. Rather, whenever he hired him by the day and the non-Jew also works on Shabbos, it is prohibited by law, for it is as though he expressly hired him for Shabbos as well.
