4) There: This applies in private, etc. 'Private' means that Jewish people do not see that non-Jew while he performs the Jewish person's work. If non-Jews see him entering a non-Jew's house on Shabbos, this is not considered public. R' David HaLevi in Shemen HaSimchah, folio 17b. The author there, folio 20a, agrees; Petach HaDevir, paragraph 4.
