Seif 1: He contracts, etc. The Mishnah regarding hides given to be tanned, etc.; and there in the Gemara, “One may not send letters,” etc. Tosafos, s.v. unless, writes that the same applies to hides, etc.; see there. See also Avodah Zarah 21b, Tosafos s.v. arisa, citing the Yerushalmi in the first chapter of Shabbos concerning craftsmen, etc., that whether the work is on detached materials or attached to the ground, it is permitted only when contracted for a fixed price.
When does this apply? In private. This follows the Yerushalmi cited above: work on detached materials is permitted specifically in the non-Jew’s house, whereas work attached to the ground is permitted only in another city. See also the Gemara in Moed Katan, as explained below.
