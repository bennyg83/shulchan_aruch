The maftir. He wrote in Shelah in the name of the Geonim that one should not remove the book of Prophets until after the blessing so he sees and blesses on what he reads as maftir; and I do not find a reason for this, end of Beit Yosef's words. But also in Sefer Tanya it is likewise; and likewise Magen Avraham Talmud Torah; for in a Torah scroll too it is forbidden to remove the Torah scroll until after the blessing.
