The maftir may not, etc. When he blesses after the haftarah they may not remove the book of Prophets so he can see and bless on what he reads as maftir (Beit Yosef in the name of Shibulei Leket). So it appears regarding everything one blesses on — the item must be before him and very close to him, as with tefillin of the hand; and likewise with the cup of Havdalah and the hadas that the Sages said one takes in the right and this in the left — in the dispute of Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel it implies that at least the item not so needed in the left hand should be prepared before him (Ateret Zekenim in the name of his teacher from Maharash in Maharal, may his memory be blessed):
