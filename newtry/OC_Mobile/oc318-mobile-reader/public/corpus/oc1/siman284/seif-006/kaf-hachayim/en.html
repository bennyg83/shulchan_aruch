(6) There. They read the haftarah in the Prophets, etc. — in Aggadah one says he is not saved from his harsh punishment until his son says Kaddish or reads the haftarah in the Prophets. And Rivash wrote in responsum siman 115 that from this the custom spread for the son of the deceased to say Kaddish at the end every twelve months and also to read the haftarah in the Prophets, end of Rivash's words.
