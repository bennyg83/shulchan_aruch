Paragraph 6: The maftir does not, etc. Tur, siman 236, explains the custom in Rosh's name: we pray, 'Hashem shall reign forever,' and that the verses 'At evening time there shall be light' be fulfilled; He will illuminate for us and darken for our enemies. This is somewhat related to the conclusion immediately before the conclusion.
Since we say, 'May Your Name be exalted,' etc. In our version, near the conclusion of the fourth post-Haftarah blessing we say, 'May Your Name be exalted in the mouth of every living being, continually, forever and ever. Blessed are You, Hashem,' etc. 'May Your Name be exalted' relates to the conclusion because it resembles its opening words, 'Blessed are You, Hashem.'
As we find regarding the Kohen Gadol on Yom Kippur in Yoma 68b: he reads from the Torah scroll Acharei Mos and Ach Be'asor, rolls the scroll, and recites by heart Uve'asor from Sefer Bamidbar. See the reason there on page 70.
