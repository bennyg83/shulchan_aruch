In the Prophets. The reason is that once they decreed not to read in the Torah and they enacted to read in the Prophets related to the parashah 21 verses, as they would read in the Torah 21 verses three times for each of the seven who read in the Torah. Therefore the maftir blesses seven blessings corresponding to the seven who read in the Torah. And what they read in Prophets and not in Writings is because one does not find there a matter similar to the subject of the Torah parashah. Levush wrote: I wondered that I did not see practiced writing haftarot as a proper book, for it seemed to me one does not fulfill at all with haftarah reading in printed chumashim since they are not written with all the laws of a Torah scroll or megillah, end of his words. And Taz and Magen Avraham disagree with him and permit reading the haftarah in printed chumashim.
