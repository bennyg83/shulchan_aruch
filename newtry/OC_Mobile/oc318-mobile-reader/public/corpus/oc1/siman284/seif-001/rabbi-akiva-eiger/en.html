Seif 1 in the gloss 'after they read in the Torah' — if they read in a Torah scroll and in the middle found a mistake and there was no other Torah scroll to complete the reading, one may say that nevertheless we say the haftarah, since they read in a Torah scroll — for even though it was found disqualified, nevertheless what they read is called a reading; for if there is a Torah scroll they do not return and read what they already read.
