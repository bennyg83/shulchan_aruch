In the gloss "and he answers Amen after them" — it implies two are required: that he hear and they answer Amen; even though in siman 213 seif 2 it is explained one fulfills through a blessing by hearing like answering even without answering Amen — here it is deficient because we cannot say hearing like answering since he did not read in the Torah.
