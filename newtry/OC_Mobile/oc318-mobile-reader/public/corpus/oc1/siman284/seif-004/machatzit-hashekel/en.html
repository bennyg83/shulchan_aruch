Paragraph 4: Like, etc. According to Rambam he does not recite another blessing. Rambam nevertheless agrees that the successor must begin where the first reader began. Their dispute is explained in siman 585: if one began blowing shofar and could not finish, another completes it without a new blessing.
This is also implied by siman 692, where Rema writes that one may bless over the Megillah reading and another may read. If the reader became unable to speak mid-reading, Magen Avraham there, paragraph 2, compares it to siman 585. The first blower blessed for everyone: all listeners must bless for hearing, but his blessing discharged them. The second blower who continues when the first cannot finish is therefore considered to have blessed himself. The same applies to Megillah reading. This differs from Torah aliyos, where listeners are not obligated in the blessing and the first reader did not discharge the newly called person; according to one view, the latter must bless again.
