(א) (ז) One who does not know, etc. — meaning the minor read in the Torah initially for the purpose of maftir and afterward it was found he does not know how to say the haftarah; and likewise with an adult in such a case another may say the haftarah and its blessings — for even though they enacted that the maftir read in the Torah first out of honor for the Torah, nevertheless b'dieved we say there is also honor for the Torah evident in that they read in the Torah first for the purpose of maftir, for behold they interrupted with Kaddish beforehand; and Peri Megadim:

(ב) (ח) But l'chatchila, etc. — meaning one does not call for maftir one who afterward will not know to read the haftarah too. And in our time when the custom in many places is to write Prophets on parchment in a scroll like a Torah scroll — if so the reader in the Prophets fulfills for the entire congregation like Torah reading; therefore even l'chatchila it is permitted to call for maftir even one who does not know to read in the Prophets himself — for he says all the blessings and the shaliach tzibbur reads the haftarah:
