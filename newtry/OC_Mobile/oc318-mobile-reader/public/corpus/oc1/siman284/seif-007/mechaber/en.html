On a Shabbat where the parshiyot are joined we read the haftarah of the' second parashah.And see later siman 428. It is customary after the Torah reading to mention the souls of the departed and to bless those involved in the needs of the congregation, each place according to its custom (Shibulei Haleket; Hagahos Mordechai). We are accustomed to say Yekum Purkan and there is no violation of making a plea on Shabbat. We also are accustomed to say Av Harachaman; on every day where we do not say "Tzidkatcha tzedek" we do not say it; and likewise when there is a wedding or circumcision; and there are places that do not say it when they bless the New Month except during the days of counting the Omer; and in all this one follows the custom.
