Paragraph 7: Those engaged, etc.; that they pray, etc. Every Jewish congregation customarily prays each Shabbos for the welfare of its government. The text includes, 'May He cast its enemies beneath it.' This cannot mean enemies in another kingdom. If two kings fought, Jews in each kingdom would pray for their own king to cast the opposing king beneath him, yet Jews live under both kings and pray oppositely; one prayer would necessarily be vain. Rather, each congregation prays that the king subdue enemies within his own country. The text should read: 'It is proper to recite memorial prayers for men,' and so appears in Knesses HaGedolah.
