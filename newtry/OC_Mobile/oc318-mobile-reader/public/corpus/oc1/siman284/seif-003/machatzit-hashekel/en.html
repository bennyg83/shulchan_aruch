Paragraph 3: He may, etc. Since Kaddish was recited before the maftir read from the Torah, it is evident, etc. This differs from siman 282, where Kaddish follows the seventh reader; there, on the contrary, concern exists for those entering, as Magen Avraham writes above in siman 282.
For this is its honor, etc. Although the maftir must first read from the Torah and here the person reciting the Haftarah did not, this is proper. The reason for prior Torah reading is not to equate the Prophets with Torah, as above siman 282:15; here, precisely this division honors the Torah, for people will say, etc. Nevertheless, Rema writes not to do this initially because Rivash disagrees.
