He must have intent. Therefore, it is a mitzvah for those called to the Torah to recite the blessings aloud, so that the entire congregation can hear and answer amen.
They answer amen after them. By answering amen it is considered as though one had recited the blessing himself. One should not answer amen after the words “true and righteous” in the blessings following the Haftarah, because that is not the conclusion of a blessing. Likewise, one should not answer amen after the words “which are spoken in truth” in the blessing before the Haftarah, because this forms one blessing with the continuation ending, “Blessed are You, Hashem, Who chooses the Torah,” which is its conclusion.
