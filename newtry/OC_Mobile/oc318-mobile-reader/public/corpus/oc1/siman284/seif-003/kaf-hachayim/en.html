(3) Levush wrote: I wondered all my days that I did not see practiced in any place to write the haftarot as a book like they write the megillah, for it seemed to me one does not fulfill in the congregation at all with haftarah reading read in printed chumashim since they are not written with all the laws of a Torah scroll or megillah, end of his words. And likewise in Hagahot Yosef Nasi. And I say the custom of ancients is primary as written in Beit Yosef siman 144 in the name of Tashbetz — what they skip now from prophet to prophet is because our haftarot are written in booklets and one can mark to find quickly, etc., see there. And it is in Gittin (daf 60) that it is forbidden to write the haftarah because of what is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 283 seif 2 — except because of "it is time to act for Heaven, they have voided Your Torah" — meaning the congregation cannot afford to buy them complete prophets; and if so now that printing has been revealed and books are cheap, it is forbidden to write haftarot — for it is simple that printing is superior writing as Maharshal wrote siman 96 and Mishna Berurah and Levush Chayim. And if because they are written on paper — Hagahot Beit Yosef 283 wrote other books are written in all kinds of inks and do not require ink; if so, likewise they are written on paper as Tosafot wrote there; and likewise one does not need a scroll. And as in responsum of Rashba siman 287 — specifically in a Torah scroll it is forbidden to bless when written in booklets; but for haftarot we are not particular, see there. Magen Avraham beginning of the siman; and likewise Taz s.k. 2 Orach Chayyim paragraph 1; Rabbi Zalman paragraph 4; but the Arizal paragraph 3 wrote some pages and certainly he holds specifically parchment is required, as in responsum of Rama siman 134; and further certainly handwritten is better than printing; and further that printed names are not written for sanctity like handwriting. And he concluded: nevertheless those who do not have haftarah written on parchment should not read from printed chumashim for haftarah alone — for since it is considered as if written, it is better they read from a complete printed book of Prophets since each congregation can afford it, see there.
