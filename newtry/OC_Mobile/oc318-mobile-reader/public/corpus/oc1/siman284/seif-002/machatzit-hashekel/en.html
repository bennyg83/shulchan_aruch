Paragraph 2: After them, etc. After the word vatzedek; that is, the vatzedek in the blessings after the Haftarah. Although printed texts place a break between vatzedek and the following word ne'eman as if vatzedek ends a blessing, it does not. Seven blessings were instituted for the maftir corresponding to the seven Torah readers: two over his Torah reading, one before the Haftarah, and four afterward. If vatzedek ended a blessing, there would be five afterward. In earlier times they paused after vatzedek to recite praises of God, but the word always remains in the middle of a blessing. This applies only to vatzedek after the Haftarah. One certainly answers amen after vatzedek in the blessing before the Haftarah, because it concludes that blessing.
