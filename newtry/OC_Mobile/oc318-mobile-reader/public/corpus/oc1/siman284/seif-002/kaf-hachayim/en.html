(2) They enacted for the maftir seven blessings corresponding to the seven who read in the Torah: three before the haftarah and four after it — three before it, two of them on the Torah reading and one before the haftarah "who chose good prophets," etc., and opens and concludes with "Blessed." And four after it: the first opens with "Blessed" and concludes with "Blessed"; and what they interrupt when reaching "His words are true and just" is not because it is the end of a blessing — for they enacted only seven; and if it were the end of a blessing there would be eight; rather as stated in the Gemara they were accustomed to say words of praise and appeasement. The second is "Have mercy" and concludes with "Blessed" and does not open with "Blessed" like a blessing adjacent to its fellow. And likewise the third which is "Make us rejoice" and concludes "Blessed are You Heaven, Shield of David" as in chapter Arvei Pesachim (Pesachim 117b) — in prayer a horn of salvation sprouts, in haftarah Shield of David. The fourth on the Torah and concludes "Blessed are You Heaven who sanctifies the Shabbat." Tur. And Taz brought it s.k. 1 and wrote therefore one does not answer Amen after "true and just" since it is not the end of a blessing, see there; and likewise Magen Avraham s.k. 2. And likewise in the blessing before the haftarah one does not answer Amen after those said in "true" since it is not the end of a blessing — only after "and in Your prophets true and just."
