And two may not, etc. Likewise Rivash — meaning they should not say the haftarah by alternation, meaning one of them reads one verse and the second is silent until the first finishes the verse, and afterward the second reads another verse; for the maftir who read in the Torah, out of honor for the Torah, must read the entire haftarah alone; therefore even if one became silent the second must begin from the start and bless afterward:
