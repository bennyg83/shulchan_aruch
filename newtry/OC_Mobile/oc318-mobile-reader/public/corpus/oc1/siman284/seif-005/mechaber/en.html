If the maftir lost his ability to speak in the middle of the Haftarah, the person who comes to replace him and complete [the reading] should not begin from the place where [the reader] ceased. Instead, he should begin from the place where the first began, as is done with the Torah reading.Two people may not say the haftarah at the same time, because (of the principle) 'two people should not use the same keli'. (Rivash).
