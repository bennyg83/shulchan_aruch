That two voices, etc. — see Mishna Berurah s.k. 11; and see Shaarei Ephraim gate 9 letter 63 who wrote, and these are his words: the main law is that the one who ascended for maftir alone says the haftarah and the congregation also says after him quietly; and in our countries they practice that everyone says the haftarah together with the maftir aloud — and this is from lack of knowledge, and it is proper to abolish the custom. And l'chatchila one should practice that the maftir says the haftarah aloud like the reader in a Torah scroll and the congregation says after him quietly; and there should be ten standing next to him who heard the voice of the haftarah recitation. And likewise Eliyahu Rabbah sympathized with the practice and saved the haftarah recitation: since when the blessings began, do they want to stop saying the haftarah? And it is not beautiful what they do if they say in the Gemara you do not hear a voice of things from the patters; and sometimes factors that you are also a brother — the nearby mountains do not hear; and even if their voice mutters and is not heard, they must be treated: as soon as the maftir finished the haftarah and the blessing of the haftarah that he has not yet finished will be long until the end of the blessed maftir, and then he finishes the haftarah statement — the maftir must be careful not to be careful with the Hostage Force until the voice of the masses is completely broken:
