We say the section of the daily offering (parshat hatamid). And on Shabbat we say the verses of the Shabbat offering alongside the section of the daily offering. And it has one seif.
And we say the section of the tamid. And there are those who say the order of the arrangement (seder hama'arachah) and "Master of the worlds, You commanded us," etc. And if it is impossible to say it with the congregation, one may say it in one's house and return to read the section of the tamid alone with the congregation. And the second time one should intend it like reading in the Torah (Tur).} On Shabbat we say alongside the section of the tamid the musaf Shabbat verses, but not on Rosh Chodesh and Yom Tov because one reads musaf in the Torah from the verses of musaf in the Torah reading. {Rama: And there are those who mention musaf Rosh Chodesh as well, and so is the practice—in order to publicize that it is Rosh Chodesh; and so it is below in siman 421 (Tur). And those who are exacting have practiced to sway at the time of reading the Torah, as an illustration of the Torah that was given with trembling; and likewise at the time of praying "because of all my bones they shall say, O Lord, who is like You?"
