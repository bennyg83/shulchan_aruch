The daily offering. It should be recited while standing, corresponding to the offerings, which were brought while standing. See above, siman 1, seif katan 12. One should take care to recite it with the congregation together with the chazzan, because the daily offering was a communal offering. Derech Chochmah.
To sway. This refers to Pesukei DeZimrah. During the Amidah one should not sway. Responsa Menahem Azariah, siman 113. Knesses HaGedolah cites that some pious individuals would move gently only at the conclusions of blessings, in fulfillment of, “Before My Name he was humbled.” Maharil writes that he would sway during prayer from front to back and from back to front. Darkei Moshe writes that Recanati, in parashas Yisro, gives a kabbalistic reason for swaying during prayer. Magen Avraham concludes that one who acts according to one view may do so, and one who acts according to the other may do so, provided he concentrates.
