The section of the offerings one says standing, like the offerings that were offered standing [Orach Chayyim]. And one should intend the second time, etc.—for the relief of the matter, so it should not be as if offering two daily offerings.

Verses of musaf. Even though they have not yet prayed Shacharit and it is forbidden to precede musaf to the tamid, nevertheless they have already said the section of the tamid; and if one considers the recitation of the verses as a sacrifice-offering, the recitation of the tamid verses is also considered a sacrifice-offering.

In order to publicize. And he relied on this on the Mishnah at the end of Shekalim that they would place the limbs of Rosh Chodesh on the altar to publicize that it is Rosh Chodesh, at the end of Sukkah.

To sway. That is, in the verses of song (pesukei dezimra); but in prayer one should not sway [Magen Avraham in responsa, siman 113]. And in Sefer Yeshu'os Meshicho, part 1, siman 33, he wrote that some commentators used to sway gently only at the conclusions of blessings, to fulfill "for My name is becoming calm." And in Maharil it is written that he would sway in prayer—from before him to after him and from after him to before him—on Shemini Atzeret. And in Divrei Moshe he wrote that the Rakanat on parashat Yitro gave a kabbalistic reason to sway in prayer, and likewise in the Zohar, etc. And "one who acts like this master acts, and one who acts like that master acts"—provided that he has proper intent.
