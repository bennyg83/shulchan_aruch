And they say the parashah of the Tamid; and before the parashah of the Tamid one reads parashat Bereishit every day—parashat HaYom and the day after it—as they used to do in the Temple; and afterward they say parashat HaKiyor, and afterward parashat Terumat HaDeshen; and afterward one says the parashah "And this is what you shall do on the altar" in parashat Tetzaveh until "Speak to you there," because it is similar to parashat HaTamid; and afterward one says parashat HaTamid as written in our siddurim; and afterward "And he shall slaughter it on the side of the altar" etc., "This is the Torah" etc.; and one says after it the verse "And it shall be accepted for the the Omnipresent" etc.; and afterward Abaye arranges the altar service etc. (Tur); and afterward one says parashat "Altar, incense burner" until "for your generations" etc.—the signs of the incense and its making—for after the offering of the Tamid they burn incense (Beit Yosef siman 1). Nevertheless on Shabbat and Yom Tov one must say Pitum HaKetoret also after the conclusion of the prayer as we practice; and the reason is that it drives away the kelipot so they do not cling to the prayer in the abundance that descends through the prayer (Lechem Min HaShamayim). And afterward one says parashat Olah, Minchah, Shelamim, Chatat, and Asham before Birkat Kohanim if one has time. And some say parashat Zeit Zach in parashat Emor and parashat HaNerot in parashat Behaalotekha (Siach HaSadeh). And it seems this is not its place but rather for Maariv before Minchah, for thus it is written: in the morning when he prepares the lamps etc., and when Aharon raises etc. between the evenings etc.—and it was not omitted from anyone to light the lamps in the morning; only Rambam chapter 4 of Laws of Temidim uMusafin holds that their lighting is their preparation, but all the later authorities disagree and say the lighting of the lamps between the evenings and "and he prepared them" refers only to fixing the wicks—and so is the opinion of Rashi and Onkelos at the end of parashat Tetzaveh, Raavad end of chapter Avodat Yom HaKippurim, Hilchot Temidim uMusafin, and responsum of Rashba siman 69. And afterward one says the order of the avodah etc., and so was the avodah of the Tamid, with the Omnipresent's help.
