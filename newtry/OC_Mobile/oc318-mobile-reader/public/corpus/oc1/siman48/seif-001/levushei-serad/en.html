Shulchan Aruch: And if it is impossible to say it with the congregation—since the congregation does not say the entire order—he should say the entire order first in his home; and nevertheless he must say the section of the tamid again with the congregation, because the tamid was offered with the congregation. One need not be concerned about violating "do not add" as though offering two daily offerings, for recitation alone does not constitute "do not add"; and for the relief of the matter one should intend it like reading in the Torah a second time.
