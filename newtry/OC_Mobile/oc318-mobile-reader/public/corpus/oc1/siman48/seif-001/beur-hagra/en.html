Siman 61 and they say etc.—see below beginning of siman 50.

And some say etc.—as stated at the end of Taanit and at the end of Megillah and at the end of Menachot.

And one should intend etc.—see Magen Avraham; and this is the language of the Tur.

On Shabbat etc.—see Magen Avraham, meaning not like Baal HaManhig and Avi Ezri.

But not etc.—unlike on Shabbat as stated above siman resh peh-gimmel; and for this reason they read parashat HaTamid. Kol Bo.

And some say etc.—as stated at the end of Shekalim and at the end of Sukkah.

And they are accustomed etc.—see Zohar chelek gimel, leaf 208 side 2, leaf 219 side 1.
