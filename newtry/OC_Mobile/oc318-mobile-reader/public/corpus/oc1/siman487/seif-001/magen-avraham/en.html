And he retracted. That he went back and said "Who sanctifies Yisrael and the seasons."
After he already knows that it is Yom Tov. Only that he erred in his speech; but if he erred and thought it was Shabbos, and remembered within toch kedei dibbur, he has not fulfilled his obligation, as is written in siman 209. But most poskim hold that even though he did not know it was Yom Tov, he has fulfilled, since he remembered and concluded according to law; see there. Nevertheless it is implied here that if he did not remember and concluded with the [formula] of Shabbos, he has not fulfilled; and that is specifically when he did not mention [the formula] of Yom Tov in the middle, but if he mentioned [the formula] of Yom Tov in the middle, he has fulfilled. For he already ruled in siman 268, se'if 4, that even if he prayed a weekday Amidah, he has fulfilled if he mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos in the middle; and all the more so here, where he concluded "Who sanctifies the Shabbos," for Yom Tov too is called Shabbos. And so it is somewhat implied in se'if 3: if he said Ya'aleh Veyavo, he has fulfilled, meaning even if he did not conclude with [the formula] of Yom Tov he has fulfilled; and if so, if he concluded "Who sanctifies Yisrael and the seasons," even if he did not mention [the formula] of Yom Tov in the middle, he has fulfilled. And this requires further study, for we have an explicit Mishnah: where they said to conclude, one is not permitted not to conclude; and a beraisa teaches: the general rule of the matter is that everything follows the conclusion of the blessings. And it is still difficult: since the Sages fixed for Shabbos a blessing of its own, how does he fulfill if he prayed weekday [Amidah] and mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos and did not conclude with [the formula] of Shabbos? And one must say: since by strict law one should pray eighteen [blessings] on Shabbos, only that out of honor of Shabbos the Sages did not impose the burden on him, therefore if he prayed weekday [Amidah] and mentioned [the formula] of Shabbos, he has fulfilled. And it is implied in Pesachim folio 117 that if on Yom Tov or on Shabbos he concluded "Who sanctifies Yisrael," he has fulfilled; and that is specifically in prayer, but in Kiddush he has not fulfilled.
And one does not say the blessing me'ein sheva. For it was instituted only because of damaging spirits, and on Pesach it is a night of watching.
