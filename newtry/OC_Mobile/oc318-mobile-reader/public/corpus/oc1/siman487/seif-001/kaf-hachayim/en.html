A) [Section 1] The daily schedule is Arabic, etc., and Korin Kash, etc., and seal and spread and say a verse, and Moshe spoke the times, etc., and this is not the break between a gala and a prayer, a long, bloody redemption. And so on as 33 in the Hagat, we used to say after we lay down on the nights of the 19th, these are the holy days of the the Master, and on the nights of Ra'ah, and on the day of your joy and your days, and on the night of Yahweh, because on this day he will atone for you, etc., and so on.
