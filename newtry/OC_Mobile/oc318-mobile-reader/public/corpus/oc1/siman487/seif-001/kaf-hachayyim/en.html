1) [Seif 1] The order of the evening service, etc.: They recite Kerias Shema, etc., conclude the blessing, spread over the congregation, and recite the verse ‘Moshe declared the appointed festivals,’ etc. This does not constitute an interruption between redemption and prayer, for it is considered an extension of the blessing of redemption. Levush in this siman and in siman 582. Knesses HaGedolah similarly writes in Hagahos HaTur that the custom is to recite after Hashkiveinu, on Yom Tov nights, ‘These are the appointed festivals of Hashem, holy convocations’; on Rosh Hashanah nights, ‘On the day of your rejoicing and on your appointed festivals’; and on Yom Kippur night, ‘For on this day He shall atone for you,’ etc. Peri Chadash writes likewise. Chok Yosef, paragraph 1; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 194b. Although Levush writes that on Yom Tov nights they recite the verse ‘Moshe declared,’ each author wrote according to the custom of his locality; in some places the custom is to recite both verses.
