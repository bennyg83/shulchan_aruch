After etc. This is the words of Tosafos at the end of chapter 1 of Berachos, who ruled stringently. And therefore one must know that it is Yom Tov, for then the beginning of his blessing is proper; for if not, even though he finished properly he has accomplished nothing, only that his conclusion was not proper, and for that toch kedei dibbur helps. And Tosafos's reason for ruling stringently, even though we say there on folio 21a regarding a doubt etc., is because the law follows R' Yochanan there who said "would that etc."; and Tosafos explained there, s.v. "and R' Yochanan etc.," that it deals with a doubt; and the reason is as written at the end of Rosh Hashanah, that an unnecessary blessing is forbidden only by rabbinic law. But Ba'al Halachos Gedolos and other poskim wrote there from what is said there, "what is the reason [regarding] Kerias Shema etc.," that it is proven that in every doubt of rabbinic blessings we are lenient; and the words of R' Yochanan they explain even regarding a certainty, as is written in chapter 4 of Pesachim and regarding a freewill offering of Torah, and as is written there.
