If one forgot to say Ata Bechartanu (the special holiday blessing) and instead said Ya'aleh V'Yavo (the insertion for festivals), he has fulfilled his obligation. Gloss: Even if it was Shabbat, if he mentioned Ya'aleh V'Yavo, he has fulfilled his obligation (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim). However, if he said Ata Bechartanu and included Shabbat within it, he still needs to go back and mention the festival specifically in Ya'aleh V'Yavo (Responsa of Mahari”l). Nonetheless, if he did not mention the festival in Ya'aleh V'Yavo, he does not need to repeat the prayer (Beit Yosef). If a prayer leader forgot to mention the festival in the Shacharit (morning) prayer, see Siman 156.
