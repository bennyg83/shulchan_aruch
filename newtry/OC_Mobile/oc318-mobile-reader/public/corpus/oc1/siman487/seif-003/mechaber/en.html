They take out two Torah scrolls and read in the first five men (and if they add on Yom Tov see above siman 482) in parshat Bo from "Draw out" until "from the land of Egypt by their hosts," and the maftir reads in the second in parshat Pinchas in "in the first month," and the haftarah from Yehoshua "At that time"; and they pray musaf and do not mention rain from now on (and see above siman 114 seif 3), and so they rule not to ask for rain from now in Birkat HaShanim.And it is customary on every Yom Tov in musaf to say the musaf verses of that day after "through Moses Your servant, as it is said.":
