(s.k. 3) And one does not say etc. Because of damaging spirits etc.: as is stated in Shabbos folio 24b; and this is Rashi's language there: because of the danger of damaging spirits, for their synagogues were not in the settlement. And on all other weekday nights they were occupied with their work, and at the end of their work they pray Ma'ariv in their homes; but on Friday night they come to the synagogue, and they were concerned that there are those who do not hurry to come and linger after the prayer; therefore they lengthened the congregation's prayer; end quote.
