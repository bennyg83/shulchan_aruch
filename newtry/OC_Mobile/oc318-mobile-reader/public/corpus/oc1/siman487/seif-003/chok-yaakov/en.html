He must mention in Ya'aleh Veyavo. The view of many later authorities is that one should not mention [the formula] of Shabbos in Ya'aleh Veyavo at all; and the Taz ruled that even though in Birkas HaMazon and likewise on Rosh Chodesh one should not mention [the formula] of Shabbos in Ya'aleh Veyavo, nevertheless in the festival prayer one should mention it; and see there where he elaborated on this. And a shatz who forgot to mention [the formula] of Rosh Chodesh: see above, siman 472 (and see above, siman 270, that one does not say Bameh Madlikin when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos; and see there).
