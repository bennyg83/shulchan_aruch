[4] And Vehasienu etc. R' David Abudarham wrote: some interpret it from the language of nasi, meaning You have exalted us and made us great; and some interpret it as from how they raise signal-fires, meaning illuminate Your countenance and answer us; and some interpret it from "Pharaoh will lift up," meaning that our remembrance should rise before You for good; and some interpret it from "and I carried you on eagles' wings," praying before Him by way of parable that the time come when He command the clouds to carry them at every festival to Jerusalem; and the correct [view] is from the language of mas'es, whose sense is a gift, meaning send us a gift of the blessing of Your festivals. He further wrote: "And Yisrael who sanctify Your Name will rejoice in You," and some read "who love Your Name," and that is a mistake, because one must say something that parallels the conclusion immediately before the conclusion; end quote. And with this the words of R' David Abudarham that I wrote in siman 468, s.k. 13, are somewhat resolved.
