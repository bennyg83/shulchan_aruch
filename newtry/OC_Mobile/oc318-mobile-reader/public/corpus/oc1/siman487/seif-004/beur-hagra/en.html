Se'if 4, on the first night etc., and likewise. Yerushalmi; and likewise in chapter 3 of Shekalim and chapter 1 of Berachos and chapter Arvei Pesachim; and Tosafos of Berachos 14a, s.v. "days etc.," brought it; and in the Tosefta, and Tosafos there brought it; and in Masseches Sofrim chapter 20, halacha 9, and the Ran in chapter Arvei Pesachim brought it.
And all this etc. For it was not instituted for those who are expert, as is written in the Tosefta at the end of Pesachim: townspeople who have no one to read the Hallel for them go to the synagogue and read the first chapter and go and eat and drink and return and come and finish all of it; and if that is impossible for them, they finish all of it. And so it is implied in the aforementioned Yerushalmi there and there: if he heard it in the synagogue, he has fulfilled etc.
