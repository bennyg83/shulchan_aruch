With a blessing at the beginning and the end. The reason for this custom is explained above in the Tur at the end of siman 473: because they do not recite a blessing before the Hallel that they read at the time of the Haggadah, therefore they read the Hallel in the synagogue so that he not need to bless at the time of the Haggadah. And if so it is somewhat difficult: why do they bless at the time of the Haggadah after Hallel Yehallelucha, that is, after Hallel HaGadol, and why do they not rely on what he blessed in the synagogue Yehallelucha, as with the first blessing? And one can resolve it with difficulty.
