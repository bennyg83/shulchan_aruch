The shatz does not recite Kiddush. For there is no poor person in Yisrael who has no wine. However, it appears to me that even if there is no wine in the city except in the synagogue, one should not recite Kiddush in the synagogue; for throughout the year too many say not to recite Kiddush in the synagogue, as is written in siman 269, except that they follow the custom of the early ones, and on Pesach there was never a custom to recite Kiddush in the synagogue.
