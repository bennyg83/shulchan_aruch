The shatz does not recite Kiddush in the synagogue. Even if wine is not available in the city except in the synagogue, one should still not recite Kiddush in the synagogue; for throughout the year when they recite Kiddush in the synagogue it is only to uphold the custom of the early ones, and on Pesach there was never a custom to recite Kiddush in the synagogue; end quote of the Magen Avraham; and not like the implication of the Taz. And one should not quibble and say that in a place where wine was not available, the early ones' custom too was to recite Kiddush in the synagogue to exempt many guests of their obligation; and see above, siman 269: one can say that something uncommon, that there not be wine in the city, or something called "local wine," regarding something uncommon the notion of custom does not apply; and see the Shach at the beginning of se'if 14; and see in Kol Bo, siman 90, who elaborated greatly in explaining the reason that they do not recite Kiddush in the synagogue.
