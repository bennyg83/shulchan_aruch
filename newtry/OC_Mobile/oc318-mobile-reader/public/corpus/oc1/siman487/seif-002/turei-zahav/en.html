The shatz does not recite Kiddush in the synagogue. The reason in the Tur is because there is no poor person in Yisrael who has no four cups, and if so every person recites Kiddush in his home; end quote. He wrote this according to the custom that throughout the year they recite Kiddush over wine; but the Tur's own view in siman 269 is that if he had the power he would abolish it. And according to the Tur's reason, if he is in a place where there is no wine in the city for the whole congregation, only for individuals, one should recite Kiddush in the synagogue to exempt one who has no wine.
