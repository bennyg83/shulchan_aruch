See Taz and see Peri Chadash; and our custom is that they do not mention [the formula] of Shabbos in Ya'aleh Veyavo; and the same applies on Shabbos of Chol HaMoed, they do not mention in Ya'aleh Veyavo [the formula] of Shabbos, for it has the reason of the Rosh and the reason of the Mordechai; and on Yom Tov of Shabbos he already mentioned Shabbos in the opening. And see Magen Avraham 5; and God willing there it will be explained. What the Mechaber wrote: if he forgot Ateh Bechartanu and said Ya'aleh Veyavo, he has fulfilled; and this requires further study: if he forgot Ya'aleh Veyavo and said Vehavi'enu, and remembers "Shabbos and the festivals of Your holiness," whether he has fulfilled, or whether we specifically require that he mention the particular of the Festival of Matzos etc.; and if he mentioned so, one can say he has fulfilled.
