(א) A mavo whose side, etc. It appears here that the sea is considered a partition — and it requires study, for Tosafos 22b wrote: and one might say one cannot make reshut hayachid since mountains surround it, and in Do not make reshut hayachid that surrounds it is a river Diglat — and some say a partition not made by man is not considered a partition and many come and nullify their partition, end of his words; see end of this siman, and one must say here he speaks where ships do not pass there to the port, for there the sea itself is a partition, and the same for other waters where ships do not pass — they themselves are a partition as written siman 356 and so in the Gemara 24b and Tosafos one does not make, etc., that the river itself is a partition, and so in Aruch entry Ozel; and per what is written siman 358 specifically when the riverbank is 10 deep, and so Rashi and Rivash here — but if the riverbank is a slope and when one walks the slope is still not 10 high it is not a partition, as written in this siman:

(ב) The sea rises as a sandbar. For such is the way of the sea — it shortens and becomes ground the width of a parsah, and partitions are nullified and they also carry in mavo'ot as initially (Rashi); and in any case it implies that even though the sea shortens, nevertheless a slope remains ten deep and is a partition — only the concern is lest they carry from the mavo to the place where the sea dried, which is karmelit even enclosed by the sea partition since it was not enclosed for dwelling, as written siman 365 seif 3; but when the sea exists there is no concern, for the sea is not fit to carry with the mavo, end of his words; and if so according to him complete partitions are required on the sea side, and tzurat hapetach or lechi or korah do not help as Eimimar ruled in Sura in his responsum; and the Tur holds like Rashi's view; if so correction of the rest of the mavo suffices; and it implies from Rashi's, Rosh's, and Tur's words that in other rivers we are not concerned lest a sandbar rise; and so Tosafos 4, 24 — see there; and know that in any case we require that the river be adjacent literally to the head of the mavo; but if distant from the houses ten amot correction is required, for that place is breached; and so Tosafos 4, 24 s.v. let them make tzurat hapetach and s.v. let them make lechi — see there; Constantinople even though breached to the sea in several places more than ten — it is as closed (R' Yehuda the Castilian Orach Chayim 94; and so Shach 92); see siman 345 seif 66; and in Rivash siman 345 he wrote a mavo pass-through to karmelit requires tzurat hapetach; and that case where one side is sea — it was not pass-through at both ends since it had three partitions, only on two sides it was somewhat breached on one side to sea and one to garbage heap, and the sea bank was ten deep, etc., end of his words; but the implication of poskim is that even fully pass-through to sea is permitted; and see Rashi siman 364; if so in our rivers whose way is to dry greatly in summer — forbid, see siman 365 seif 63; and know that if they freeze in rainy season the partition is nullified and it is forbidden to carry:
