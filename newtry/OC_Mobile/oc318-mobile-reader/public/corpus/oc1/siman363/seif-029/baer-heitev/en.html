The sea. And in our rivers whose way is to dry greatly in summer — it is obvious that it is forbidden, Magen Avraham, see there; and in the Taz he questioned in the community of Steinitz in the land of Mehrin — they practiced moving objects on Shabbat without an eruv relying on the city being surrounded by a river around and they acquired permission from the lord — and he expanded in his analysis and ruled to forbid that they make an eruv as is customary in all communities not surrounded by a wall; however, if on the riverbank on dry land there is a place of a slope ten high over four amot as written siman 366 — we consider that slope in place of a partition, end of his words; and Magen Avraham wrote: and know that in any case we require that the sea be adjacent literally to the head of the mavo; but if it is distant from the houses ten amot correction is required, for that place is breached; and in Constantinople even though breached to the sea in several places more than ten — it is as closed, Knesset HaGedolah in the name of R' Yehuda the Castilian; and see Magen Avraham; and know that if they freeze in rainy season the partition is nullified and it is forbidden to carry; and see in responsum of Chacham Tzvi siman 37; (and see in Eben HaEzer who expanded to prove that frozen water does not nullify partitions, see there in Maginei Eretz printing Propes):
