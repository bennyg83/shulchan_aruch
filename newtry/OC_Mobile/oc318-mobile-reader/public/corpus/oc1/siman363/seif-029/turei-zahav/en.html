Lest the sea rise as a sandbar. — it will be explained in the responsum that I answered to the community of Steinitz — this is the text of the question: in the community of Steinitz in the land of Mehrin they practiced moving objects on Shabbat without an eruv relying on the city being surrounded by a river around and they acquired permission from the the Master — is it fit to do so or not. Answer: it appears it is not correct to do so for two reasons. The first is explicit in the Gemara and poskim and the second although not explicit is nevertheless clearly proven in the Gemara and poskim. The explicit reason is stated in Eruvin daf 8: R' Yehuda — an incident of a mavo one of whose sides ends at the sea and one at a public garbage heap — the matter came before Rabbi and he said neither prohibition nor permission; prohibition he did not say for partitions stand; permission he did not say lest the garbage heap be removed and the sea rise as a sandbar; Rashi explained sandbar of sand and stones at its bank for such is its way and the width of the sea shortens and ground of a parsah or more is made; Beit Yosef in Orach Chayim siman 363 in the name of Magen Avraham Meirat Einayim wrote we are concerned lest the sea rise as a sandbar; and there is dispute among poskim whether we hold this concern — as explained in siman 363 and Beit Yosef ruled to be lenient and Rama brought the stringent view — if so certainly you should be stringent in this concern lest part of the waters at its bank be covered by sand and stones and they carry there. Second: it appears here all hold to prohibition even though they said at the end of the thrower in tractate Shabbos daf 100: a pit in reshut ha-rabbim ten deep and four wide full of water — if one threw into it he is liable; full of fruits — if one threw into it he is exempt — why? water does not nullify partition; fruits nullify partition etc. — it teaches us that through water partition is not nullified; nevertheless since the way of water here is to freeze and ice in cold season — behold at those times there is no partition at all and it is obvious forbidden to carry; and this concern does not apply to sea that does not freeze; but in that of making pasin 4:22 in what Magen Avraham wrote surrounding it the Euphrates on this side: and one should not learn permission from the mishnah of chapter Window (Eruvin daf 78) that taught: a ditch between two courtyards ten deep and four wide — they make two eruvin and not one; even full of straw or hay, full of earth or stones — they make one eruv and not two etc. — it teaches us that through filling with straw partitions are not nullified since he will take the straw from there for his animal — likewise we would say since frozen water will eventually melt and become flowing water as at first partitions are not nullified; and one may say on this: perhaps here it is different since freezing happens by itself not by human action unlike filling with straw; if so perhaps it helps here what will eventually be removed; but there is no question for behold we find in chapter He May Not Dig (Bava Batra daf 20) regarding reduction of window that brings there: he removed grass and placed it in the window or grass that grew by itself in the window — it is not reduction; behold before you that growing by itself has no advantage to nullify more than coming there by human action; and likewise we may say freezing of water is considered as if the pit were filled with straw that does not nullify the pit; but all this is not so for Tosafos there in chapter Window explained that filling with straw that does not nullify partition is because that straw is taken on Shabbat and is not muktzeh; and they bring proof from what we say there afterward that even a pouch nullifies a person etc.; and that fruits nullify partition even though taken on Shabbat is d'oraisa and certainly nullifies; but for eruv which is rabbinic one may distinguish between taken on Shabbat and not taken on Shabbat — perforce we learn from this that whatever is certainly there on Shabbat — forbidden to take from there such as straw prepared for bricks not for animal feed — is called certainly nullification of partition and even though certainly afterward he will take it — nevertheless every Shabbat this is called nullification of partition; likewise here and all the more since frozen water will certainly stand all Shabbat; and further proof from chapter Window (Eruvin daf 77a): Rav Yechiel covered a bowl — meaning adjacent to the wall and reduced the height of the wall less than ten from the bowl to the head of the wall from his reduction; and they challenge there: and why — it is taken on Shabbat; and they answer: it is not needed for he attached it to the ground for that bowl and they will not move it on Shabbat; and they challenge: what difference from muktzeh that he hid in straw that is forbidden to take on Shabbat if part of it is not exposed; and they answer: it is not needed for we require recognition and a basket; behold before us that specifically what is fit to take on that Shabbat is not considered to nullify partition; but not what is forbidden to take on Shabbat even though certainly he will take it after Shabbat through recognition and basket — nevertheless now it is considered nullification of partition; if so all the more here since freezing is accustomed to stand many weeks on the water; and further proof for prohibition from the initial enactment that the Rabbis enacted recognition in lechi and korah for closed mavo from three sides — Rashi explained at the beginning of Eruvin in the first mishnah: the Rabbis decreed in mavo lest they confuse with complete reshut ha-rabbim; and likewise in karmelit the Rabbis decreed on them lest they confuse with reshut ha-yachid as stated chapter 1 of Shabbos daf 6; and all our domains are karmelit as all poskim wrote — if so here when water freezes and people walk there like in the rest of the city and sometimes snow covers until the water underneath is not recognizable at all — it is obvious the Rabbis decreed more lest they confuse with reshut ha-rabbim for there is no recognition at all here. And one may say: only on those Shabbatot when water freezes would carrying be forbidden in the entire city; but on other Shabbatot not — this is not so for we said in that of concerned lest sea rise sandbar that I brought above that Rabbi did not wish to say permission even though partitions stand from the reason lest sometimes there be no partition there — all the more here that most of the year certainly there is decree even all year because of those Shabbatot; and further proof from mishnah of chapter Window (Eruvin daf 78) regarding ditch — if one placed a board four wide — if they wish they make one eruv; and from this one may not learn permission; for if they freeze in rainy season partition is nullified and forbidden to carry; and see in responsum of Chacham Tzvi siman 37; (and see in Even HaEzer who expanded to prove frozen water does not nullify partitions — see there in Maginei Eretz printing Propes): however if on the riverbank on dry land there is a slope ten high over four amot as written siman 366 — we consider that slope in place of a partition, end of his words:
