(א) Ends at the sea — meaning that the mavo had two sides from houses and courtyards, and its two heads east and west — one ended at the sea and one ended at a public garbage heap ten tefachim high from the ground; therefore it needs nothing, for the sea is also considered a partition ten tefachim deep at its edge, and water does not nullify the partition, as written siman 356; and Magen Avraham wrote that here it deals with a place where ships do not pass there to the port, for otherwise many come and nullify the partition; but several Acharonim disagree on this and hold that even when ships pass there, the partition at the sea edge is not nullified thereby. [However, when one head of the mavo ends at a river and there is a bridge over it where many walk to the other side, it appears from several Acharonim that through the bridge the partition is nullified and tzurat hapetach must be made at the place of the bridge.] And all this is specifically when the sea or river edge was ten tefachim deep; but if the sea and river edge slopes and continues, if along the slope four amot it is still not ten tefachim high, it is not a partition, for it is like level ground. And all this when the river is immediately adjacent to the head of the mavo; but if it is distant from the houses more than ten amot, correction is required, for that place is breached; and if it freezes in winter months, then the partition is nullified and it is forbidden to carry; and some Acharonim hold the partition is not nullified by freezing. And if the river edge is high and ten tefachim collect within four amot up to the place of freezing, certainly there is room to be lenient:

(ב) Is not made to be cleared — for all day they throw their refuse and manure there and its height will not diminish; but an individual's garbage heap, although now it is ten tefachim high, tomorrow it will be cleaned and its height will be lacking; and if we permit now to carry, they will also carry later when its height is lacking:

(ג) And we are not concerned, etc. — meaning also we are not concerned lest the partition on the sea side be ruined and it be found that the mavo has no fourth partition:

(ד) The sea rises as a sandbar — for such is the way of the sea, that it raises sand and stones at its edge and shortens and becomes land the width of a parasang, and the partitions are nullified and they also carry in mavo'ot as at first [and all this when there is no slope except at the place of the water alone; but if on its edge there is a slope that collects ten tefachim within four amot, this is considered a partition in every regard]; and Acharonim agreed to be stringent like this view, and specifically for seas; but for rivers we are not concerned that a sandbar will rise, and we consider the river edge a partition when it has ten tefachim height [however rivers whose way is to dry in summer, and when they dry their slope does not have ten tefachim high within four amot — one should forbid without correction even when not yet dry, for we are concerned it will dry without awareness]. And in any case we require that the river be immediately adjacent to the head of the mavo; but if it is distant from the houses more than ten amot, correction is required, for that place is breached; and if rivers freeze in winter months, then the partition is nullified and it is forbidden to carry; and some Acharonim hold the partition is not nullified by freezing. And if the river edge is high and ten tefachim collect within four amot up to the place of freezing, certainly there is room to be lenient:

(ה) And there is no partition here — and therefore correction is required like any mavo breached on the fourth side, by tzurat hapetach or lechi or korah.
