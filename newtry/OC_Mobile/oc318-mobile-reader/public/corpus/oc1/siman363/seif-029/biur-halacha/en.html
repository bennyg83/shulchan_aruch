(א) A mavo whose one side, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah and it is according to Peri Chadash; and the same if the mavo had only one side toward the south built with houses and courtyards and the east and west sides were sea and refuse and the north side was repaired with lechi and korah — against reshut harabbim too it is permitted per the first view [Beit Yosef and Eliyah Rabbah]; and know further that whether for the first view or the second stricter view there is no difference whether the mavo was open to karmelis or reshut harabbim, as in the poskim:

(ב) And there are those who disagree and hold we are concerned, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah that for a river for all opinions we are not concerned lest a sandbar rise; and know all this if the river does not surround the city on all sides — then it is considered a partition opposite it to permit carrying in the city, and in the breached places tzurat hapetach will repair; but when it surrounds the city on all sides — behold this enclosure was made before the city was settled and it is as a rakafef more than beit se'atayim that was not enclosed for residence — forbidden to carry in it even if afterward they settled in it, as above in siman 358 [unless they extended the enclosure around the city afterward after they settled in it; and even if initially it was also enclosed and only in one place it was not 10 deep in an area slightly more than 10 amot — that is as a breach and they completed also in that place until no measure of that breach remained — it is considered as if the entire enclosure was afterward, as above in siman 358 see there]; and even if now a partition is made on this enclosure it also does not help as in 358 there unless one closes in one place in width more than 10 amot and afterward opens [Mordechai at end of siman 363 and Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav s.k. 20 see there what he further wrote on this]:
