**29.** An alleyway whose one side ends at the sea and whose other side ends at a public refuse heap requires nothing, since a public refuse heap is not expected to be cleared away, and we do not worry that the sea will deposit silt (*shirton* — a dry patch like an island from which the water receded and which becomes fit for planting). {Rama: Some disagree and hold that we do worry that silt may rise and there would be no partition.}
