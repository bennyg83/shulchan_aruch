32) There. Provided that it is one handbreadth wide. Although the Shulchan Aruch writes that a lechi of any width suffices, that applies when the lechi stands independently. If it is attached to the wall, it is recognizable only if it is one handbreadth wide. Olas Shabbos, subsection 4. This follows the distinction made by the Shulchan Aruch in se'if 2 between a strip made deliberately and one left as a remnant of the wall. However, we already wrote there in subsection 14 that some authorities hold that any width suffices even when a remnant remains. According to them, the same applies here. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 4, has already challenged the Levush's distinction between the two cases. See there. Nevertheless, we wrote there that initially one should forbid carrying when the remnant is less than a handbreadth, in accordance with the Shulchan Aruch and the authorities who agree with him. The same therefore applies here.
