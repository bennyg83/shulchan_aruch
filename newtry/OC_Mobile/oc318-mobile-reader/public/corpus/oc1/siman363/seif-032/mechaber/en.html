**32.** Just as we ruled that a lechi may be placed in the middle of an alleyway, the same applies to placing a beam in the middle. All of this applies where the inner section has the proper status of an alleyway — its length not less than four cubits, its length exceeding its width, and houses and courtyards opening into it. If not, two minimal posts on either side, or one four-handbreadth post on one side, is required.
