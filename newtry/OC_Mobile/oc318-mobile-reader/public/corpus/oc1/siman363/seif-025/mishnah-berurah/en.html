(א) Bent on two, etc. — meaning that the pegs stood on the two sides on a slope and their heads protrude above the height of the walls:

(ב) And he placed the korah on them — meaning, and here there are two deficiencies: first, the korah is not placed on the wall itself but only in the air above on the pegs; second, the korah is shorter than the area between the two walls:

(ג) And the pegs are not one tefach — for if the pegs were one tefach wide, we would not care that the korah itself is short, for the pegs themselves are counted as korah:

(ד) Valid, etc. — for in that the pegs are not higher than the mavo walls by three tefachim, we say throw it down, meaning as if the korah were knocked down and lowered to the walls; and even though it is still short, since the inclination of the pegs is not three, and therefore the korah is not distant three tefachim from the walls, we say lavud:

(ה) Invalid — for in the measure of three we do not say throw down for korah, nor lavud:
