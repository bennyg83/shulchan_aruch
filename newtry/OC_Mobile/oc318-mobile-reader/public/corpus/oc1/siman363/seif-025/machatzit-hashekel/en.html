(א) (s.k. 25) Two, etc. — for anything that does not, etc. — and the reason in a courtyard more correction is required than in a mavo — for in a courtyard pas four tefachim or two pasin are required, and in a mavo lechi of mashehu suffices — for in a courtyard there is not abundance of residents:

(ב) One may be lenient, etc. — meaning even though Rema in the gloss was stringent in our mavo'ot since there are no houses and courtyards open into them — on this he wrote that it is possible nowadays that there is abundance of residents in a mavo even though there are no courtyards and houses open — one may say mavo law and lechi or korah suffices:
