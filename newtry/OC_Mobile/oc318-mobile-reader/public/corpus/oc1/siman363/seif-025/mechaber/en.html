If one drove two bent pegs on the two walls of the mavo and their bend inclines into the mavo and placed the korah on them, and the pegs are not a tefach — which is the measure of korah to be considered as korah — if they are not higher than the walls of the mavo three [tefachim] and their inclination is not three — it is valid, for we view as if they are on the walls of the mavo; and if there is in their height or inclination three — it is invalid:
