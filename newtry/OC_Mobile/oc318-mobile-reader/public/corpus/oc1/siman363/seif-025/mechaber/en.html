**25.** If two bent pegs were driven into the two walls of the alleyway, curving inward, and the beam was placed on them — and the pegs are not a handbreadth wide (which would make them count as a beam): if they are not three handbreadths higher than the walls and their bend does not extend three handbreadths, it is valid, since we consider them as if they were flush with the wall tops. If they are three handbreadths higher or their bend is three handbreadths, it is invalid.
