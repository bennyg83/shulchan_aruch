(א) Spaced. — and in the conclusion metsumtsamot — and in both we go stringently:

(ב) It is not permitted by korah. — for korah is for recognition and above twenty there is no recognition for the eye does not rule over it; but lechi helps for we hold lechi is for partition even though the mavo is very high it is not needed:

(ג) Must hollow etc. — the reason it does not suffice with a tefach like above twenty — Rashi explained: there it is stated for the side that is already called a side only needs to reduce somewhat for slight recognition suffices; but here that now he makes it a side — less than ten is not called a side — it requires that this side be visible to the measure of mavo qualification:

(ד) And some disagree on this. — they are Rif and Rambam who hold that since they are open to each other it is as if all are one courtyard and mavo requires two courtyards open into it; the first view is Ra'osh and Tur Mordechai and Rashba shows where he erred here:

(ה) They have the law of courtyards. — accordingly we would need to correct a pas of four on one side or two mashehuyin on each side as above in courtyard — but it appears they are not accustomed to do so because of open mavo that requires specifically tzurat hapetach:

(ו) And perforce one must beware etc. — and it appears since one must place reeds below at height ten, one need not tie the rope above on the reeds but may tie the rope even on the roof from this side and that, since the essence of tzurat hapetach is from the reeds below; also from the Gemara we conclude that the upper reed need not touch the reeds — if so perforce it is tied elsewhere; and one need not beware now how the rope is tied on the side of the reeds — for above siman 362 seif 11 we said it does not help — meaning there those reeds themselves are the reeds for tzurat hapetach or there are also reeds above — namely above from the roof and below other reeds — in this one must beware they are in place of doorposts and the way of an opening is only the lintel on the doorposts; unlike if above there are no reeds — only tzurat hapetach through reeds placed below at height ten for tzurat hapetach and above tied on the roof since one cannot place long reeds there from the rope to the ground — then one need not beware how it is tied above on roofs but only that they stand aligned under the rope — for behold there are no doorposts but only what is below at height ten and it is considered as if the rope rests on them literally as stated above to decide this rabbinic matter: and it appears further that if above the reeds placed below there is a roof interrupting between the height of the rope and the reeds there is prohibition in this for it is not tzurat hapetach since the roof interrupts between the rope and the reeds — and we do not make tzurat hapetach in this manner except one must open a large hole in the roof above the reeds in a manner that they are aligned under the rope without interruption; and above I wrote in seif 7 regarding lechi from smeared plaster: and it appears further that if under the rope on the wall projections extend up to ten tefachim near the ground — one may well rely on them on erev Shabbat in place of lechi reeds;
