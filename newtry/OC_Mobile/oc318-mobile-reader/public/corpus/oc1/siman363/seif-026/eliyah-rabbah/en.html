[26] A korah wide, etc. Explanation: wide 10 tefachim — since it hangs 10 tefachim it leaves the category of korah like a machatzlet and it is a partition under which goats pass through and is forbidden as Rashba wrote; it teaches us it is permitted since the korah is visible and is not covered by a machatzlet — there a korah is on it. And what he wrote even if it extends below 10, etc. — we would have thought since it hangs below 10 from the ground there is no category of korah on it as he concludes.
