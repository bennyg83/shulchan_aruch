(א) Spaced — See in Mishna Berurah that we go stringently, and although it is a rabbinic matter and we should go leniently in its doubt — nevertheless it is not called a doubt due to lack of knowledge [Chavot Da'at]; but if another doubt joins it is possible to be lenient [Peri Megadim]. And it is wondrous that the later authorities did not mention Rashba's words in Avodat HaKodesh that his view for a mavo is we always measure constricted see there:

(ב) Except with tzurat hapetach — Behold in Eliyah Rabbah he brought that in width more than 10 it helps when making a pas 4 or two pasin of mashehuin like in a courtyard, and he brought a source for this from Rashba; and see in Beit Me'ir who wondered on this, for also in a courtyard itself this does not help when its opening is more than 10 as explained above in seif 2, and also in Avodat HaKodesh itself he wrote thus — and therefore what he wrote in sha'ar 1 ot 4 the mavo lacked all these, etc., rather with a pas 4, etc., does not refer to width more than 10:

(ג) It is not permitted with korah — Meaning even if 4 wide. And if 4 wide and strong to receive plaster it is permitted and not because of recognition but because the edge of the ceiling descends and closes — as long as the opening of the mavo is not more than 10; it further appears that if it were 7 wide even if not strong it is valid since whenever it is 4 wide the eye dominates [Avodat HaKodesh and brought in Eliyah Rabbah; and in Peri Megadim he wanted to emend from 7 to 4 and it is wondrous, for 4 does not help as he wrote at the beginning of his words; and the reason for his emendation was it was difficult for him what advantage of 7 — however now that we merited Chiddushei Rashba on Eruvin published, he wrote there on 3 a proper reason on this see there]. And know further what he said it is not permitted with korah — this is if he wants to place it above on the walls as usual, so the korah is found above 20; but if he wants to place it in the hollow of the mavo he has a remedy also with korah — insert pegs in the two walls of the mavo below 20 from this side and that and place the korah on them; and what is written in seif 4 and he must place it on the walls of the mavo — comes to exclude that he should not place it from outside as he concludes there; but it need not be specifically on the walls, and so is proven at the beginning of Eruvin in Rashi s.v. yima'et he will lower, and so in Rif and Rosh and Rashba at the beginning of Avodat HaKodesh and Ritva there [although he explained there further that diminution includes what the Gemara 4b said diminish the hollow by raising the ground, but he also agrees diminution includes also lowering the korah as Rashi there see there] — all wrote he can lower the korah that was initially placed at the height of the mavo walls that was above 20 and place it in the hollow of the mavo within 20:

(ד) But it is permitted, etc. — And all the more it is permitted with tzurat hapetach, and even if that opening was also wide more than 10 — lechi does not help for this, tzurat hapetach helps [Tosafot Shabbos in the name of Tosafos]:

(ה) With lechi — And in Beit Me'ir he strongly questioned this law, and his view is superficial reading of Rambam's and Tur's language that just as korah does not help for this, so lechi does not help see there his reason:

(ו) In a building a tefach wide — See in Mishna Berurah that the same with earth — so Magen Avraham, and it is according to what Bach agreed see there; and it is obvious his proof from Sukkah that it helps to diminish it with earth — and in my humble opinion there is not so compelling proof, for there one pours earth over the entire area of the sukkah and it is not so applicable to say lest it diminish; and although we rule a sukkah suffices with two complete walls and a third wall of a tefach suffices and can through trampling pour from the earth outward — nevertheless since it is a single person we are not concerned for this; but in our matter where the entire width of the earth will be a tefach and all residents of the mavo trample that place it is reasonable at first glance that even Abaye agrees we decree lest it open from its measure — and this is the reason of Rif and Rambam who mentioned diminution through korah, as R' Yitzchak wrote; but nevertheless for the law it appears we should not deviate from Magen Avraham's words that Rashi and Rashba and Razah and Or Zarua also copied that earth helps:

(ז) Two courtyards — Whether they were on one side or a courtyard on this side and another courtyard on the opposite side; and the same if the two courtyards were on the middle closed wall that is opposite the open wall opposite it and the two sides were only walls without houses and courtyards [Darkei Moshe in the name of Or Zarua]:

(ח) And for each courtyard two houses — And each house is not less than 4 amot [Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Avodat HaKodesh]:
