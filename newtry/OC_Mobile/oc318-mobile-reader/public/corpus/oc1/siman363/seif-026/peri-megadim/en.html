And what — Taz: in 364 bent mavo like a gimmel or like a chet is like open even though there is not width ten amot between reshut ha-rabbim and karmelit or in its bends; and here Ra'osh wrote specifically when there is more than ten amot from the pas until the middle wall (until three partitions and a fourth wind open in the east he places in the east pas four one; and if from the pas until the western wall more than ten amot etc. — tzurat hapetach is required as in 364); and Taz wrote that here it is not literally like bent mavo; and one may say we view as if the place from the pas goes straight until the western wall — and two mavo'ot are on the side of this pas and on the side of the two they also go equally and straight — and in neither is open; unlike when there is more than ten amot from the pas until the western wall — if so there is no opening between the middle mavo and the mavo'ot at the sides — therefore then it is like bent mavo; and therefore what Rambam wrote and some say specifically when there are ten amot — it is one view; rather more than ten amot:
