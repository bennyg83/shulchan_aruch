Seif 26: Less than ten tefachim spaced. I am in doubt whether this general rule of the Sages' measures to be stringent with spaced and compressed is like a manner of doubt in which amot we measure and each time we take stringently; and if so when mavo is four wide metsumtsamet per the other view 28 — its length of mavo four metsumtsamot and height nineteen metsumtsamot suffice to permit it with lechi — from necessity if we measure the mavo spaced it needs no correction since its width has no measure of four spaced; and if we measure metsumtsamot — automatically its length and height are measure; or this is from the essence of the law that where amot and tefachim are mentioned for leniency the explanation is spaced; and where stated for stringency — namely metsumtsamot; and as if the Sages explicitly said mavo four tefachim metsumtsamot requires correction with lechi and must be height nineteen spaced and length four spaced; and likewise in Kilayim fence breached in ten — namely metsumtsamot; and in such a case if distancing vineyard four metsumtsamot helps — since if we measure spaced automatically permitted because of fence since there is not eleven spaced; and it appears to me proof from Tosafos Eruvin 5:1 s.v. and if in four etc.; and even though four that R' Yosi says etc. — why did they not simply challenge that the four R' Yosi says is spaced for all barley for stringency is spaced; and if the measure of houses and courtyards open into it that opening measure four is also spaced — if so Tosafos did not challenge at all; for it is fine to say that the four R' Yosi says is literally four; and nevertheless its length is more than its width for the four is spaced and its width in four is metsumtsamot; and they challenge well: how do you find it — behold it requires open into it opening measure four spaced; perforce according to side one above; and if so we should not have challenged the three views that the four is spaced; for one may say the four R' Yosi says is according to its width four — if its width four metsumtsamot also its length in four metsumtsamot; and in the manner of from necessity as above; and they challenge well that in such a case where there are four metsumtsamot how do you find it; to this they challenged that also when its width four metsumtsamot it requires its length more than four metsumtsamot for behold it requires its length more than its width:
