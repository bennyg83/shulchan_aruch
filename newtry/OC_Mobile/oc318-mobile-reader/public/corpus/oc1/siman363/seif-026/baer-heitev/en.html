Under. The Taz wrote: it appears to me that since one must place reeds below at height ten, one need not tie the rope above on the reeds but may tie the rope even on the roof from this side and that, since the essence of tzurat hapetach is from the reeds below; also one need not beware how it is tied above on the roofs but only that the reeds stand aligned under the rope; and it appears to me further that if above the reeds placed below there is a roof interrupting between the height of the rope and the reeds there is prohibition in this, for it is not tzurat hapetach since the roof interrupts between the rope and the reeds, and we do not make tzurat hapetach in this manner except that one must open a large hole in the roof above the reeds in a manner that they are aligned under the rope without interruption; and it further appears to me that if under the rope on the wall projections extend up to ten tefachim near the ground — one may well rely on them on erev Shabbat in place of lechi reeds; and likewise the projections must be exposed above under the rope, end of his words.
