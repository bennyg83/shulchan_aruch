[17] [Levush] And if it is 4 amot wide, etc. And the same law if he paved with lechayim less than less than 3 over a length of 4 amot, and specifically when the outer lechi does not protrude at all; but when it protrudes somewhat outside the mavo it is permitted. If they were distant from each other 3 tefachim one may use up to the outer end of the outer lechi, and if the entire lechi stands, etc. (Avodat HaKodesh daf 6):
