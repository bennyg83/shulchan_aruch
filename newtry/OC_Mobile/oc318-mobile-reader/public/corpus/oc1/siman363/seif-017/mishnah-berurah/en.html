(א) One tefach wide — and although it requires measure to receive upon it a brick that is one tefach and a half wide, one can join a quarter tefach from it in mortar here and a quarter tefach from it there [Gemara]:

(ב) To receive a brick, etc. — meaning bricks, each of which holds in its length three tefachim and in its width one tefach and a half, lying upon it along its entire length as the measure of the width of that mavo's opening [and nevertheless, if the mavo opening was ten amot wide, it need not be fit to receive bricks along its entire length; rather it suffices if it can receive bricks along a length of nine amot and two mashehuyin, which are eighteen bricks in their length, for a korah of nine amot and two mashehuyin permits a mavo of width ten amot by the law of lavud — meaning that it should not reach less than three tefachim to this wall and less than three tefachim to that wall, as below in siman 361]; and the reason it must be fit to receive bricks is that otherwise there would not be so much recognition that people of reshus ha-rabbim would think they placed it there temporarily; but when it is healthy and strong to receive such building, it is recognizable to all that building placement was intended for this purpose:
