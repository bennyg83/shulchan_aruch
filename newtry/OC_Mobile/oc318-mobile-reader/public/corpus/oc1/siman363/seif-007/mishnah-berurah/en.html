(א) A lechi — even fragments, etc. — meaning attached, provided it can stand in an ordinary wind [Levush]. And to exclude a korah, for we require specifically that it be strong enough to receive a brick, as below in seif 17:
(ב) Are valid for it — and the same for stones protruding from the wall and separated from one another less than three tefachim — they are an actual lechi, provided that between all of them there is a height of ten tefachim:
(ג) And therefore they make a lechi, etc. — for it is no worse than fragments of fragments, which are permitted for lechi; and the same for tzurat hapetach, for we require that lechayim be made on this side and that side; if he fears the wind will knock them down, it is permitted to make them by plaster smeared on the wall on this side and that side, and the reed above should be aligned upon them; and nevertheless we require that there be some substance at least like a thread; but appearance of plaster alone, or a mark in lime called krayid, or another color — does not help [Taz and Shach]:
(ד) That it not be erased more, etc. — meaning by dogs and pigs that are accustomed to rub against walls and erase from the walls the plaster smear near the ground; then it no longer helps, neither for lechi nor for tzurat hapetach:
