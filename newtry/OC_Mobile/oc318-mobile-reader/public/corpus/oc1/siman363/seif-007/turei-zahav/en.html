And therefore they make lechi from smeared plaster etc. — and even though in Terumat HaDeshen he wrote that lechi that if wind blows on it cannot stand is invalid — and here certainly the plaster cannot stand — there it deals with lechi that is valid by itself for mavo without tzurat hapetach that requires some measure; but here what they make from smeared plaster is for tzurat hapetach that requires only one reed from here and one reed from here suffices in whatever amount; and this is the difference in Terumat HaDeshen siman 74; and accordingly Rama's words are not resolved that wrote and therefore they make lechi etc. for it implies he deals with the law of lechi without tzurat hapetach and this is not so; and below siman 362 seif 26 it is explained explicitly that this refers to tzurat hapetach that we make. And in that responsum: nevertheless it is astonishing that he saw that smearing plaster smeared on the wall sometimes comes to ruin because dogs are accustomed to rub against walls and erase the plaster smearing from the walls below more than three toward the ground and it is no longer fit for lechi nor partition for it is a partition that goats penetrate — end of his words; it implies that in any case in this smearing there must be some actual substance and somewhat thick and it does not suffice with the appearance of plaster visible on the wall alone; and so too in that responsum initially that at least it must be thick like a cloak cord; and so too Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Ra'osh that part of it must protrude — I wrote this because I saw one teacher who commanded to mark on the wall in place of lechi with a type of white earth called kreid — and he erred greatly in this, especially in this matter of plaster smearing that does not fit the law well as Terumat HaDeshen himself wrote there that he strained to uphold the practice in this; and do not add to it — there is only folly and nonsense in this:
