Seif 6: It is required that the lechi. I question this — for behold this is included in seif 10. And why state twice:

And behold in the essence of the section of Dikah — one should study from where; in such a case where there was another lechi and it fell on Shabbat — it does not help, lest we say they did not rely — only they did not know from yesterday about this lechi, or they did not carry on the previous Shabbat through this lechi, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 9; but in such a case where there was another lechi there — one may say since we rule on Shabbat once permitted always permitted — only when lechi fell, since there is no partition, we do not say once permitted always permitted; if so lest since this lechi is a partition — only recognition is lacking — one may say it is called they brought it for partition and we say once permitted always permitted; and from that in Eruvin 15 side 1 — Rav sat in that mavo — they showed him and he stood in his place — there is no proof; for Rav according to his view that even when partitions exist we do not say once permitted always permitted; and conversely here according to R' Huna who does not hold as Mar — that when partitions exist we say once permitted always permitted — there is to rely on the palm tree; and perhaps since the baraita also teaches its korot fell — we rule korah is because of recognition; if so when korah fell it is called partitions exist, for only recognition is lacking; perforce in any case since the Sages said with three partitions we permit only when they have recognition beforehand — where recognition was removed — it is as partitions were nullified; likewise this lechi without recognition — partition is nullified from it rabbinically; and still requires study:
