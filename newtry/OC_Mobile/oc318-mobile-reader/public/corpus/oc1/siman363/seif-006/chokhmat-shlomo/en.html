See in Magen Avraham what is written — it is possible it is not called that the public penetrated it less than sixty myriads as is written in siman 345 seif 7; and it is difficult for me, for behold from Rashi and Magen Avraham below siman 365 s.k. 4 what is written that the public did not penetrate it — meaning such as where there is excrement or mud and filth that it is not the way of the public to pass there — it implies explicitly that specifically where the place is not fit to pass from itself this is not called the public penetrated it; but whatever the place is fit from itself to pass — even though the public did not actually pass there it is called the public penetrated it; and all the more if the public actually penetrated it — only they did not pass sixty myriads — it is called the public penetrated it since the hindrance is not from the place; and behold the main proof of Magen Avraham is from siman 345 that Rashi holds there is no reshut ha-rabbim less than sixty myriads and this is Rashi's view — thus; and from Rashi's words it is proved to the contrary that we do not require sixty myriads — for if so he should have explained plainly that the public did not penetrate it — that they did not pass sixty myriads; and perforce we do not require passing sixty myriads in it but whatever is fit to pass there is called the public penetrated it; and it is possible Magen Avraham distinguishes between this law and there — for there it deals with a mavo corrected with lechi and korah — since the concern there is lest they abandon the large opening and enter through the small opening and nullify the mavo correction — here whatever opening is fit for the public to pass there is this decree; therefore we require specifically excrement and mud, etc.; but where the city is enclosed by partitions like a wall and the like which is full reshut ha-yachid and the Sages did not permit through their decree to nullify the path from the category of reshut ha-yachid — it is not nullified until the public actually penetrated it — specifically sixty myriads; however this is not to examine Tur in this siman that wrote the mound rises in place of lechi — it implies that also with the mound it is from the reason of lechi and automatically whatever is fit to be the public penetrated it the lechi is nullified as is written above — and it is proved from the poskim mentioned that not like Magen Avraham, examine well; and behold the measure of the width of the path that the public penetrated it — we require that it be more than ten amot; but in less than ten amot there is no concern as is proved below in siman 365 and in the Talmud source of the law there; and it is true that from the language of the Shulchan Aruch that wrote there its measure implies the intent is ten tefachim everywhere — for regarding amot it always stated ten amot or simply ten feminine remembering always regarding tefachim; nevertheless perforce the Shulchan Aruch was not precise in language and there the intent is on ten amot as is proved from the Talmud that the matter depends on whether it is measure of an opening or not and measure of opening is always in ten amot — and perforce the Mechaber was not precise in his language; if so with the mound we require that the place where the public penetrated it be more than ten amot and in any case it appears not like Magen Avraham and with this we do not require sixty myriads and it is not similar to the law in siman 345 — for there it speaks regarding the law of full reshut ha-rabbim from the Torah therefore Rashi holds we require sixty myriads; but here for our law we do not require sixty myriads for it is less than sixty myriads it is not reshut ha-rabbim but at least karmelit; and rabbinically we do not require sixty myriads and even in less the partition is nullified; also one may say Rashi's intent there is specifically where there are not sixty myriads at all then it is not reshut ha-rabbim; but here where there are sixty myriads even though in that path they did not penetrate sixty myriads — as long as in the body of the city there are sixty myriads and in that mound the public penetrated it — the partition is nullified; also one may say here it is different for behold if there were no mound they would be forbidden to carry there and would be judged as the public — only through the mound they come to make it reshut ha-rabbim — whatever the public penetrates in the measure that if those public were in a place where there is no mound they would forbid each other — here too the partition is nullified and it has the law of the public penetrated it and the public's penetration operates at least to make the mound partitions as if they do not exist; and if there were no mound there they would certainly be forbidden — here too they are forbidden unlike Rashi's case where it is not from the reason of partition that he permits but that in less than sixty myriads it is not reshut ha-rabbim — here it is permitted in less than sixty myriads; but whatever without the partition would be forbidden — only through the partition they are permitted — whatever the public penetrates that would forbid if there were no mound or partition — also now they nullify the partition; and so it appears in my humble opinion and it is correct, examine well:
