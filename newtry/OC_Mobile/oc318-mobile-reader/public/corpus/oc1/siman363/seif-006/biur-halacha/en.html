Must be that there are not, etc., 3 tefachim — And see in Mishna Berurah what we wrote that if there are 4 tefachim between the tzurat hapetach and the wall — the view of Chayei Adam is that lechi must be placed at the wall; and see in Nishmat Adam that his reason is it is as if breached at its head; and behold he goes according to his view that he ruled in general seif 8 as Magen Avraham that if a breach of 4 tefachim at its head lechi is needed at the breach; but according to what many later authorities ruled there for the law not as Magen Avraham but that at its head no repair is needed, and like that of 34, and as we brought there, in the name of Rashba and Ritva and it is permitted by majority standing over the breach — the same here in our matter since he holds tzurat hapetach is a complete partition and for this reason we permit even when distant 3 tefachim from the wall — if so the same even when distant 4 tefachim and more from the wall — for what difference is there; and although in a breach from its side where 4 tefachim of pas did not remain we are strict that repair is needed there — that is different since they shorten their path; unlike here where they do not leave the large opening and enter the small opening as in the Gemara; and even if we conclude there as Magen Avraham, it is not so comparable to our matter — for there since Magen Avraham holds korah repair does not help there to permit the mavo because it is not placed on the walls, therefore lechi must be given adjacent to the wall; unlike in our matter where there is tzurat hapetach and tzurat hapetach is a complete partition — automatically the breach is permitted by majority standing over the breach; and much study is needed on this, but l'chatchila certainly one should be concerned to be strict even at a distance of 3 tefachim:
