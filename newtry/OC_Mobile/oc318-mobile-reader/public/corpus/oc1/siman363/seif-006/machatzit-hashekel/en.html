(s.k. 6) Even, etc. stones, etc. — it is a lechi; and the Taz below s.k. 19 wished to validate such for tzurat hapetach — see there; and in Tosafot Shabbos he disagrees and wrote that even adjacent to each other within three — nevertheless it is called doorways that people do not make, as stated daf 11 side 1; and therefore for lechi such is valid; but where tzurat hapetach is required it does not help — see there; nevertheless even for lechi, perforce the width of the stones must be a tefach, as stated in seif 4 in the name of Tosafos and the Tur (and the reason for pasin of a courtyard if nishyayir from the wall — we require specifically a tefach, as stated seif 1), although lechi suffices with a mashehu, for there is not as much recognition in a wall as in a complete pas, end of his words; and the same here for smeared plaster that the Rama wrote one makes for the sake of lechi — meaning the law of actual lechi, and all the more suffices; nevertheless the Taz wrote that we require some thickness in that plaster, and wrote not like one teacher who erred to validate by marking with white earth called kreid — see there:
