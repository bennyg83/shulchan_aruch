(א) It is required that it not be — some say the reason is because the air of this side and that side comes and nullifies it; and accordingly if the lechi were wider than three tefachim it is valid even if the lechi were distant three tefachim from the wall, for this is standing greater than the breach at its side, and in such a case we do not say the air comes and nullifies it; and some say that in every case the lechi must be within three tefachim of the wall, and one should be stringent:
(ב) The lechi — and when establishing tzurat hapetach for a mavo, the view of many poskim is that one need not be precise that it be within three tefachim of the wall, for tzurat hapetach is a complete partition; and some poskim hold there is no difference between tzurat hapetach and lechi for this matter, and in a time of need one may rely to be lenient. And if there are four tefachim between tzurat hapetach and the wall, the view of Chayei Adam is that one must place a lechi next to the wall; and for my part it requires further study also in this, as I clarified in Biur Halacha; and l'chatchila it is certainly proper to be concerned to be stringent even when distant three tefachim from the wall:
