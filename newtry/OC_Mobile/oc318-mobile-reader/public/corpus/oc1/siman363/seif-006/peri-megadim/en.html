Between — Taz in Eruvin 70b — and it appears "equal outside" — all agree it is lechi; and "appears outside and equal inside" — there is dispute; and the halacha is they are lechayim; and "between between" that the Mechaber set — for per the majority, between-between is this one, and a fortiori this one as what Semag wrote in Choshen Mishpat 72 letter 8; and if so it should have said here first "between appears outside" and "between appears inside" is judged because of lechi; but since in the Gemara this matter is set first "appears inside" etc. — and it is possible to impose this on Taz; and nevertheless it is difficult — it should have said the wording of Tur see there. (What Semag wrote there that the Mechaber explained "equal outside-inside" not to reduce, for it is not like asking — and therefore there letter 9; and in Klalim in the opening of the book we wrote from this.)
