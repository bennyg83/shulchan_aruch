Seif 36 in the gloss: and with this the mound counts as a partition — the language is garbled and explained for one who studies in Beit Yosef that Tur wrote it rises in place of lechi and Beit Yosef wrote that since lechi suffices he wrote thus; but in truth the slope rises in place of a complete partition; nevertheless there is no practical difference here whether it is like lechi or like a partition; if so why is he precise and with this the mound counts as a partition; and what Beit Yosef wrote but the mound — certainly that if it rose only in place of lechi it would not help at all since there are no partitions but the reason is because it rises in place of a partition for we say extend and raise, end of his words — his intent is not that here regarding mavo qualification it would not help at all, for from the Torah it would not help since there are no partitions; nevertheless the mound that is ten high is like lechi and why do I need extend and raise; only their intent is that there it discusses slope and mound and regarding mound we say that if one threw and it rested on it he is liable; and on this Beit Yosef says that if there were no other elevation in the mound but that it is like lechi it would not help at all — meaning for liability as he brought there before from the words of the Talmud and Rambam; and it is possible Beit Yosef is missing and must read it would not help at all to make him liable upon it; and on this he says well since there are no partitions — meaning and if so what of it that it rested on it, for behold there are no partitions around except that we say extend and raise — thus appears at first glance the explanation of Beit Yosef's words; and in any case Rama's language that wrote and with this etc. implies specifically in this we say it is like a partition — requires study; and also that here it deals only with slope and not with mound; and even though Tur stated in its language that the mound rises in place of lechi and Beit Yosef wrote that for slope too they call mound for behold it is like a kind of mound of earth — nevertheless Tur deals also with a complete mound, see there, and stated mound which includes both unlike here where it does not deal with mound at all; and why did he omit slope and state mound; and were it not that I fear I would say a printing error fell in Rama and must read and likewise a mound is like a partition and the scribe erred and wrote in place of likewise both and also separated the two heh of likewise and added by hand the word and with this the mound and this is the possible and common error; and the intent of his words revolves around what Beit Yosef wrote that in the Talmud mound was not mentioned regarding mavo only regarding throwing and Tur also wrote mound only regarding throwing and not regarding that lechi is not needed, see there; and Rama's intent is to bring this law of Tur for since the Mechaber wrote only the language of Rambam who did not mention except slope and not mound — on this he wrote that likewise mound is like a partition; and nevertheless in the long Maggid Mishneh it is stated on Tur's words that the mound rises in place of lechi and Beit Yosef wrote and not in place of lechi but rises in place of a complete partition and so is proved from the Gemara, end of his words — it appears he also intended this in the gloss since he did not mention in the Maggid this word that Beit Yosef wrote that even though mound was not mentioned only slope, Tur holds likewise mound;
