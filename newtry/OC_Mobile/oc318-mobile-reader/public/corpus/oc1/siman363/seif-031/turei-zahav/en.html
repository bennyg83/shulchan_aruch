(א) If there is a courtyard etc. — for then the people of that courtyard forbid into the mavo on those who wish to make eruv for themselves; and if the outer ones did not make lechi at the side of reshut ha-rabbim they were not fit to make eruv at all with the inner one since the mavo was not qualified from their side — they cannot forbid the others for one does not forbid one another unless all are one domain and they can make eruv together:

(ב) But if they made in the middle etc. — this is not regarding permission to carry inward; if so one lechi suffices as at the head of the mavo; rather they came here to divide domains so the outer ones separate from the inner and do not forbid them — tzurat hapetach or two pasin is required; meaning mashehu from here and mashehu from here and it is as if an actual partition between them; for specifically for permission to carry one suffices not for dividing domains — thus Ra'osh's reasoning here:
