(א) Until they join together — meaning that they participate together; and the reason is that behold the way of mavo'ot is that after correcting them with lechi or korah, all residents of the mavo participate together [for when they do not participate they will be forbidden to take out from the courtyards to the mavo]; and since the custom is thus, the mavo residents are again forbidden to divide into halves and each make participation by himself, for these forbid those, because it is like a mavo where one of the courtyards forgot and did not participate, which forbids the whole:
(ב) And they did not make, etc. — for since they did not make a lechi and cannot carry in the mavo, they are not fit to participate together; therefore they cannot forbid the inner one, even if they have habitual passage upon it [Peri Megadim]:
(ג) From it outward — forbidden — for we consider as if the mavo began from the lechi inward:
(ד) But if they made in the middle, etc. — in this the domains are divided from one another, and it is as if there is a partition between them, and they cannot forbid one another; and specifically when they do not have habitual passage one upon the other, such as if they have another opening; but otherwise even a door does not help:
(ה) Two pasin — of mashehuyin, something from here and something from here; and even a courtyard can be divided in such a manner. A city surrounded by a wall and the gates are locked at night and one gate is not locked — nevertheless through that gate it is like lechi; through that opening one may not divide it in halves; but if a breach was made, its qualification was nullified [and see siman 365 seif 2 through which breach the qualification was nullified] of the city, and they can join half and leave half:
