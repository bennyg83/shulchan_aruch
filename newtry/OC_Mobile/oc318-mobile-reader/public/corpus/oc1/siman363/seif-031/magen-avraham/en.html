(א) And they did not make. Since they did not make and cannot carry in the mavo they do not forbid the inner part:

(ב) Two pasin. For even one courtyard can divide thus (Rosh), and a city surrounded by a wall with doors fit to be locked except at one opening — that opening does not detract from lechi or korah and the entire city is like one mavo that has lechi at its head and one does not make eruv for half of it; and if there is a breach the qualification is nullified (and see siman 365 seif 2) and one may qualify half and leave half (Beit Yosef siman 364 in the name of Rivash):
