(א) (s.k. 31) Rises, etc. — and becomes ground — meaning he raises mud and clay there and thereby the partition is nullified, for the slope that was is gone and now its use is changed:

(ב) Lest they carry from the mavo, etc. — although it is explained at the end of this siman — a mavo level inside and slope to reshus ha-rabbim, etc. — no correction is required; it is different there, for from the start when they permitted carrying in this mavo they forbade them to carry to the slope place, and every coming Shabbat they remember this; unlike the sea where by itself it was not fit to use when the sandbar would rise — they would think it was also permitted to carry to the sea even now, only the sea is not fit to carry with the mavo and it was impossible to carry; and now when the sandbar rises they would carry, for they would think it is permitted; and even if they told them at first it is forbidden to carry outside the mavo to the sea — they would forget this, for much time passes until the sandbar rises; and until the sandbar rises there was nothing to remind them; unlike below end of this siman where every Shabbat they are forbidden to carry to the slope place — they will not forget:

(ג) And if so for us complete partitions are required, etc. — as Amemar did, etc.; and Tur holds like Rashi's view — if so correction suffices, etc. — meaning since we find in the Gemara that Amemar made complete partitions — if so per Rashi and Tur there is no other reason except that it lacks mavo correction when a sandbar rises; and if so when making correction that helps in a mavo — what is written "all mavo'ot" — if so perforce what Amemar made complete partitions was not from the law but as Maggid Mishneh wrote — it was not maintained there all week and nets were easy for them to spread there every erev Shabbat; and in what is written "they removed from there" — nevertheless he gave a new reason: what is not written in other mavo lest they err to carry to the sea place — therefore they were stringent; but it is better to explain Amemar's words literally — from the law complete partitions are required, even though in other mavo lechi or korah suffices since here there is another concern unlike other mavo — they were stringent; but in Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees and wrote that even per Maggid Mishneh lechi or korah suffices:

(ד) Constantinople, etc. — as if silent it is so. They rule like Rabbeinu Yosef Karo's view that we are not concerned lest the sea rise as a sandbar:

(ה) And in Rivash, etc. — and that one side is sea, etc. — meaning the sea itself is karmelit; but the implication, etc. — even open, etc. — and the reason as Magen Avraham wrote above siman 356 s.k. 2, etc. — that it itself becomes a partition, etc. — for behold it is ten deep, etc. — end; meaning the bank of the ditch in which the sea is — that is the partition, for behold ten deep and it separates between the water and the mavo, and water does not nullify a partition, as above siman 345 in Magen Avraham s.k. 16; and so Raavad himself in that place where it was breached:

(ו) And if so in our rivers whose way is to dry up, etc. — it is implied even in a time when they did not dry up — nevertheless forbidden, for we are concerned lest they dry up; as we are concerned regarding the sea lest a sandbar rise — and this is specifically per Maggid Mishneh's explanation; but Rashi's concern does not apply to rivers that dry up; for even when dried the partitions and slope remain, for mud and clay are not raised:

(ז) And thus the Yerushalmi, etc. — mavo'ot open to the sea, etc.; and the law of the ditch of R' Chiya, etc. — from this is somewhat proof for the poskim not like Raavad — for it is implied that the ditch of R' Chiya refers to the above that was also open to the sea, except the difference is that in the ditch of R' Chiya ten collects within four amot unlike other places where it does not collect so much — we learn that where ten collects within four amot there is partition even open to the sea — permitted:

(ח) And know that if they froze, etc. — the partition is nullified; for even though water does not nullify partitions as Magen Avraham siman 345 s.k. 14 — the reason in the name of Maggid Mishneh is because the partitions are visible; but when frozen the partitions are not visible; and likewise for another reason there freezing does not apply; however from Magen Avraham's words it is implied that nevertheless in summer months it is permitted to carry and we do not decree because of winter when frozen; not like Taz who wanted to forbid even in summer because of winter, and wrote the Gemara need not state the reason in the sea lest a sandbar rise because the sea does not freeze; but rivers that freeze — we decree because of cold season when frozen; and in responsum of Shav Yaakov and other Acharonim they agreed with Magen Avraham that we do not decree specifically on the sea which has no fixed time for sandbar rise, and sometimes it does not rise for seventy years or not at all in that place; and if so even if they tell them at the start when a sandbar rises they are forbidden — nevertheless one should be concerned over long days and years they will forget the prohibition, for they will have no reminder; unlike freezing which is every year — if they tell them at the start that in rainy season it is forbidden they will remember every year to forbid carrying in rainy season; and so it appears to me somewhat proof from end of this seif from mavo level inside and at its end slope, as written above in this s.k.
