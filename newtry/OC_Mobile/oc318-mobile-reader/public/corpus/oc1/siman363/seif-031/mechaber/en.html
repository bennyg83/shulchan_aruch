If one placed a lechi in the middle of the mavo — if there is a courtyard from its beginning outward and the outer ones also made a lechi at the head of the mavo — they forbid each other until they make an eruv together; and if there is no courtyard there, or there is a courtyard but the outer ones did not make a lechi at the head of the mavo — from the lechi inward they are permitted, outward they are forbidden; but if they made in the middle two pasin or a pas of four — if it is not wider than ten, or they made tzurat hapetach if it is wider than ten — even if there is a courtyard from the correction outward and the outer ones also made a correction at the head of the mavo — each may make an eruv and be permitted in his portion:
