**31.** If a lechi was placed in the middle of an alleyway, and there is a courtyard from the lechi outward, and those on the outside also placed a lechi at the head of the alleyway, they restrict one another and must make a joint eruv. If there is no courtyard, or there is a courtyard but the outsiders did not place a lechi at the alleyway head, those from the lechi inward are permitted and those outward are forbidden. But if two posts or a four-handbreadth post was placed in the middle — if the alleyway is no wider than ten cubits — or a doorframe shape was made — if wider than ten cubits — then even if there is a courtyard from the arrangement outward and the outsiders also made an arrangement at the alleyway head, each may make their own eruv and be permitted in their section.
