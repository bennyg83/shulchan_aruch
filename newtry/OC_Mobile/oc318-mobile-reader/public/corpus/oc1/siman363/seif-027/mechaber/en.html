A courtyard whose length is more than its width — its law is like a mavo and it is permitted by lechi or korah:Even though there are no courtyards and houses open into it, since it is not close to reshut ha-rabbim but open to a mavo and the mavo is open to reshut ha-rabbim: (Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Mahari Segal in the name of Semak):.
