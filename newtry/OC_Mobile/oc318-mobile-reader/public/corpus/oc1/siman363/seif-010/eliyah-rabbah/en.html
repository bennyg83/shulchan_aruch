[10] [Levush] If one kneaded it, etc. The Beit Yosef writes this. The Taz is astonished that the Mishnah at the end of Temurah and Yoreh Deah siman 144 teach that ashes of an asheirah tree are prohibited for benefit, so they must be scattered to the wind like the wood itself. See siman 586, seif 3. This requires study. It also appears to me that mitzvot were not given for personal benefit. The asheirah wood itself is prohibited because it stands for burning, while the ash stands to be scattered to the wind. Nevertheless, the matter requires consideration.
[11] [Levush] First form, second form, third form, fourth form. According to Chochmat Shlomo the third form should be deleted, because he writes in the text that according to everyone it does not exist, as stated by Malbushei Yom Tov. See the Taz and Nachalat Tzvi.
[12] [Levush] A tree that he planted, etc. The same applies if it grew by itself, as stated by Avodat HaKodesh.
