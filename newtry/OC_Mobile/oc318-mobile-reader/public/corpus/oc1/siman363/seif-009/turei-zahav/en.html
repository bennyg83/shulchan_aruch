Whether the protrusion is visible to those inside etc. — in the Gemara it is stated first this matter: visible to those inside and equal outside, and likewise the reverse — it is judged as lechi; and Rashi explained: visible inside — he placed the lechi its width toward the length of the mavo and did not draw somewhat of its width outward but equalized its outer edge to the thickness of the walls of the mavo and is like one who adds to the width of the walls and is not visible from outside as lechi but its inner edge is visible inside; visible outside and equal inside — meaning he drew all the lechi outward and the inner edge enters the walls of the mavo and is not visible inside but only appears like adding to the length of the wall but from outside it is recognizable that it is not from the wall of the mavo since it extends beyond the width of the wall etc.; and afterward a baraita of R' Chiyya is stated: a wall one of whose sides is indented from its fellow — whether visible outside and equal inside or the reverse — it is judged as lechi; and Rashi explained another matter: indented — the building receded and reduced its width — now it protrudes and extends like a kind of pillar called piler — whether its indentation is visible inside etc. that the wall recedes behind it and the inner part is visible inside; whether visible outside and equal inside such as the wall recedes from before it so the indentation is not visible to those behind it but relative to reshut ha-rabbim — end of his words; and Beit Yosef wrote regarding Rashi's second explanation: it must read that the side of the wall that was toward the mavo is called behind the wall and the side of the wall outside the mavo is called the face of the wall — end of his words; and the explanation is very astonishing: why should the side of the hollow of the mavo be called behind the wall; for Tosafos question on Rashi's first explanation that if visible outside is considered when the lechi is toward the inner side — how do they answer below that small walls enter etc.; and according to Beit Yosef's explanation in the second explanation it would also question on the second explanation; perforce it appears the difference is between the explanations in this: per the first explanation it would be visible inside like an inward step and visible outside like an outward bulge — it turns out in both the lechi is not at the edge of the wall on the side of the mavo for this does not help as Tosafos' question; rather they agree with the second explanation and it is in this manner: the face of the wall is called that side facing reshut ha-rabbim — namely the very head of the wall; and behind the wall are called the sides in the wall; perforce visible inside is when there is a flaw in behind the wall — namely the side toward the mavo like an inward step — and it is exactly like the first explanation and visible outside is when there is a flaw in the face of the wall that is the upper head like a gap at the top — this helps specifically since the lechi is not toward the hollow of the mavo for then there is not much recognition but appears like a long wall; and this is explained in Mordechai that wrote there are two explanations in Rashi on visible outside; and according to Beit Yosef there would also be two explanations in visible inside of Rashi's explanation;
