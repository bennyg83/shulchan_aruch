(s.k. 9) As if, etc. — it is implied that if explicitly, etc. — that they did not carry on the past Shabbat through this lechi — meaning they had no other lechi but this one and did not carry — behold we see they did not wish to rely on this lechi; but if they had another lechi and carried through this one it is not recognizable that they did not wish to rely on it; however if they placed on the past Shabbat (and so on this Shabbat) another lechi and that lechi was removed — forbidden to rely on the lechi standing by itself, for we see they did not wish to rely on the lechi standing by itself, for they placed another lechi on the past Shabbat:
