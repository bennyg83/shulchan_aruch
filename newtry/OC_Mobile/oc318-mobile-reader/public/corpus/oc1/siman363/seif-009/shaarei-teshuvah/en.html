The sea. Baer Heitev. And see in Chacham Tzvi siman 37 that wrote that England and Scotland and the like whose sea is their wall surrounding them and they are high from the ground of the sea much — they are liable for reshut ha-rabbim and bridges and planks in them require correction like other cities that are not enclosed, because the public comes and nullifies the partition since they are not made by human hands — see there; and see in responsum of Beit Efraim on this matter and in the words of Chacham Tzvi on this everything is explained well; and see in responsum of Ohel Yaakov siman 47 who also wrote regarding correcting a city for vows that there is empty space from the enclosure until the wall more than ten amot — opposite it there are stakes driven in the ground with three [tefachim] between one and its fellow, and on the heads of the stakes a korah or korot fixed on every side; and he wrote that since there is three [tefachim] space it does not help, and also this is not tzurat hapetach since they rely mainly on proof that it was not made for tzurat hapetach but for partition, and a partition with three [tefachim] space does not help — see there he wrote that even if one makes tzurat hapetach there is no way to make tzurat hapetach adjacent to each other, and specifically in a breach of less than thirty amot one places tzurat hapetach between them and a side of breach less than ten remains — it is nothing; and if the breach was more than thirty tzurat hapetach within ten does not help since on every side there remains measure of breach and a way to make many openings we say, but a way to make all openings or adjacent to each other we do not say — see there:
