[9] [Levush] Therefore, etc. And the Taz disagrees with Rama that from Terumat HaDeshen it appears specifically for tzurat hapetach where one need only one reed from here and one reed suffices in all that it is and not for lechi — and there is no difficulty, for Beit Yosef also holds that for every lechi all that it is suffices as above, and Levush Yom Tov wrote that Beit Yosef's view is that for every lechi all that it is suffices in any case; in smeared plaster he is uncertain, and it appears to me that for Rama it is obvious from broken fragments — until here, in She'arei Knesset HaGedolah it is explained that they practice even for lechi to be more lenient in smeared plaster for lechi than for tzurat hapetach, and they practice in both to be lenient, and it is obvious that the plaster should be somewhat thick like the cloak cord.
