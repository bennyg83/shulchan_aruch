(א) And a slope to reshus ha-rabbim — where the mavo ground was higher than reshus ha-rabbim ground and he had to slope at its opening toward reshus ha-rabbim:

(ב) Or level to reshus ha-rabbim, etc. — where reshus ha-rabbim was higher than mavo ground, and the mavo entry also from the opening inward was high like reshus ha-rabbim ground for the width of an amah or half an amah, and afterward it became a slope toward the middle side:

(ג) That raises ten, etc. — meaning that if the slope is four amot long, then the ground is already raised above ten tefachim and is like a partition; but if ten collect within five amot, it is as if it does not exist and is like other ground whose use is pleasant for walking:

(ד) And it requires no correction — for that height at the side of the opening is a partition, even though it slopes and continues; and this is what Rama concludes, "and with this mound," etc.:

(ה) And with this mound, etc. — and if the entire city is surrounded by a mound and afterward they built houses in it, it is forbidden, for it is not enclosed for dwelling, as written siman 358 seif 2; and see there seif 8, that even if he built partitions ten high on the mound it does not help; and if the mound was less than ten tefachim and he completed to ten tefachim, it helps, for this enclosure is enclosure for dwelling made after the city was built. Therefore one can correct even if the mound was around the entire city — meaning he diminishes from the mound in an area more than ten amot wide until it is not ten tefachim high, for then it is considered a breach, and afterward he completes this measure, and through this it helps, as above in siman 358 seif 2 see there:

(ו) Like a partition — and if around the city there is a ditch ten tefachim deep and four tefachim wide, which is like a partition, and the ditch was closed with earth and stones — if the closing does not rise more than ten amot, behold it is like an opening and does not forbid; but if it is more than ten amot, behold it is like a breach:
