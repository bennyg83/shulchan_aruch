[36] [Levush] And they will carry from the mavo, etc. This is the language of Levush Yom Tov — so Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Maggid Mishneh; but Rashi explained they will carry in the mavo itself, and Beit Yosef and Levush omitted Rashi's explanation; and it appears to me the Maggid does not explain that the entire side is sea and also not in width more than 10, therefore he was not concerned about carrying in the mavo itself — until here. And it appears to me the Maggid's view: even though dry land with a slope 10 deep remains as a partition as written at the end of this siman, nevertheless it is forbidden lest they carry from the mavo to karmelis; and so appears from Magen Avraham; but why he wrote that for Maggid Mishneh tzurat hapetach or lechi or korah does not help — only a complete partition — and in my humble opinion it helps since there is recognition they will not come to carry:
