(א) (s.k. 36) And some say, etc. — Beit Yosef challenged: behold we hold, etc. — even though in its bend there is not ten, etc. (what Rema wrote unless from the pas, etc. ten amot — is not precise but means more than ten; and so Tur and Taz) — Rema's law that he brought "some say," etc. is Tur's view; and they are Tosafos daf 10b s.v. one makes, etc. who wrote on that which Shmuel said: a mavo whose width is twenty amah — one makes a pas ten high along four amot and places it along the mavo's length; and Tosafos wrote: if from the head of the pas on the inner side until the middle wall of the mavo is more than ten amot — tzurat hapetach is required, for it is like a crooked mavo; and even though we hold like Shmuel and Shmuel holds crooked mavo law is like closed — nevertheless if in its bend there is more than ten we say above that Shmuel concedes for now:

(ב) If so Tosafos did not write that we require specifically more than ten but per Shmuel's view; but we who hold like Rav that crooked mavo even less than ten requires tzurat hapetach — if so why did Tur write specifically that there is more than ten; and it must be that Tur holds, etc. — they are Darkei Moshe's words — see there; and in my humble opinion the word "and wrote Darkei Moshe" is missing — it should read that Tur holds, etc., as Ritva wrote, etc.; but regarding more, etc. — meaning even though Ritva disagrees with Tosafos there why even with more than ten — Tur decided from his own reasoning to distinguish between ten and more than ten; however what Ritva wrote to distinguish between when the two openings to reshus ha-rabbim are one, etc. — end of his words. But in a crooked mavo there are two heads on each side to reshus ha-rabbim and it is customary to walk through the mavo from this side to that, etc. — until here his words; if so it appears per his view that a crooked mavo made like the letter ches like this* has no crooked-mavo law since both are open to one reshus ha-rabbim; and specifically made like the letter dalet; and per what we hold in siman 364 seif 3 that even made like ches is called crooked mavo — perforce we do not hold like Ritva; it must be as Maharsha wrote there — one resolution; and Rashi must be explained above, etc. — meaning like ches — at first glance both openings are also from one side to reshus ha-rabbim; and it is possible since the openings are distant from one another they are not as open to one reshus ha-rabbim as here regarding pas where the two openings are close to one another and only the pas interrupts between them — end:
