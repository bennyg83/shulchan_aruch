(16) There. And even if there is not in the breach 4 tefachim. And it is possible he speaks of a courtyard wide in its middle and at its end it is narrow so that in its full width there is not 4 tefachim; but if the entire courtyard is less than 4 tefachim no repair is needed as written in seif 28. Magen Avraham s.k. 1; and Shevut Shmuel wrote on it that not because it is light from what is written in seif 28 that certainly there is a difference between mavo and courtyard as explained in the Gemara, but rather he means it is possible what we say in the Gemara that in a courtyard even less than 4 — this is in width in the middle and in such a case Ritva wrote law 12 — see there; and see Eliyah Rabbah ot 3.
