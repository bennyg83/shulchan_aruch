(א) That it is twenty amot wide — and it deals with when he does not wish to make tzurat hapetach at the mavo opening, for this helps even however wide the mavo is, as at the end of siman 366:

(ב) Four amot wide — and in less than this it does not help, even though each side is less than ten, for the air of this side and that side comes and nullifies it; unlike when he makes it four amot wide as the measure along a mavo wall, as in siman 366 — air from here and there cannot nullify it, for air cannot nullify a mavo wall. And what is written "and he places it in the middle" means along the length of the mavo:

(ג) Since its length, etc. — and it is also required that the pas not be low less than ten tefachim, as the law of a mavo wall, as in siman 366:

(ד) At its head — meaning that it reaches from one mavo wall to the wall opposite it, to permit both sides; and the same, he can correct them by lechayim — lechi from here and lechi from here, or lechi on one and korah on one [meaning one head on the mavo wall and its other head on the pas], like other mavo'ot:

(ה) Placed on the pas, etc. — to exclude if the pas is low, even though the korah touches from this wall to the opposite wall, it does not help, for behold through the pas it is considered as two mavo'ot, for it is considered as a wall, and if so each mavo's korah is placed only on one wall:

(ו) Or within, etc. — for it is as lavud:

(ז) And every condition of a mavo — as explained above in siman 366; if not so, its law is like a courtyard, and korah does not help in it, but only two lechayim of mashehu on its two sides, as explained above in seif 2:

(ח) And what is from the pas, etc. — meaning, besides the korah correction made at the mavo opening, one must make another correction at the end of the pas length, as the law of a crooked mavo explained in siman 364 end of seif 3, even if there are not ten amot from the end of the pas length to the middle wall:

(ט) Bent in the middle and open — should read: bent in the middle and open:

(י) At their two heads to reshus ha-rabbim — and therefore one must make tzurat hapetach at the end of the pas length to the middle wall opposite it, and it is like one who made a pas along the entire mavo length:

(כ) For it does not have crooked mavo law, etc. — for although it is explained below in siman 364 regarding a crooked mavo made like a chet, that even if from the beginning of the bend to the middle wall is not ten amot, correction is still required there — there it is different, for it is actually crooked; but here one can say that through the pas they become as two mavo'ot completely divided to their ends; and although the pas does not reach the middle wall, nevertheless since from the end of the pas to the opposite wall is not more than ten amot, it is considered an opening and is like two mavo'ot completely divided, only that there is an opening from one to the other; unlike when more than ten amot, one cannot say it is like an opening, and therefore its law is like a crooked mavo; and for practical law Acharonim agreed the main view is like the first reasoning; and see Biur Halacha:
