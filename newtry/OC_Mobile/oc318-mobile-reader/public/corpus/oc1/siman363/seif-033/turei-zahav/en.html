(א) And he places it in the middle. — meaning in the middle of the opening:

(ב) And every condition of mavo etc. — thus it should read and not as some books wrote condition of courtyard:

(ג) And what is from the pas until the wall etc. — in Hagahot Ashiri and Tur it is written if there is from the pas to the middle wall of the mavo more than ten amot etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote I do not know why he wrote thus for even if there is not more than ten between them its law is like crooked mavo that its law is like open — and see siman 365 seif 2; and I wonder at Ra'osh how he was silent and at Rabbeinu and R' Yeruham who did not notice this — end of his words; and nevertheless Shulchan Aruch did not write here more than ten like Tur's wording but Rama wrote it in the wording of some say; and Mahari Chalavah resolved Tur's words with great strain and I wondered at Beit Yosef's wonder on this for certainly in actual crooked mavo as in siman 364 whose form is thus — there we say that even if there is not more than ten in its bends its law is like open — for Rav and such we hold; unlike here where we make one mavo going equally as two mavo'ot — namely through pas of four in length four amot — and we have to say these two mavo'ot go equally until the middle wall and here there is no crooked mavo at all for the opening open downward from the pas is an opening from one mavo to its fellow — if so its law is like two mavo'ot one to another and there is an opening from one to its fellow — perforce they wrote if there is more than ten from the pas to the middle wall as Tosafos wrote in Beit Yosef that wrote if from the head of the pas of the inner side until the middle wall there is more than ten — tzurat hapetach is required for it is like crooked mavo etc.; behold it is explained that thereby since there is no law of opening on it it becomes crooked mavo; unlike crooked mavo in siman 364 whose form and building was literally in the bend and need not make it crooked; and I wondered Beit Yosef did not notice this for it is a simple matter in my humble opinion; and if so here too it is fit to emend thus that there is more than ten from the pas to the wall like Tur:
