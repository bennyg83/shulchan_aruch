A mavo that is twenty amot wide — one makes a pas four amot wide and places it in the middle, and each side from the sides of the pas is considered a mavo by itself since the length of the pas is four amot; and one places a korah at its head; and one must beware that the korah be placed on the pas or within three tefachim; and it is required that in each of these two mavo'ot that the pas divides between them — houses and courtyards be open into it and every condition of (mavo); and what is from the pas until the middle wall of the mavo has the law of a crooked mavo, for behold these two mavo'ot bend and in the middle are open at both their heads to reshut ha-rabbim:(And some say it does not have the law of a crooked mavo unless from the pas until the middle wall there is more than ten amot (Rosh and R' Yeruham and Tur):) ומה שמן הפס עד כותל האמצעי של מבוי יש לו דין מבוי עקום שהרי אלו שני המבואות מתעקמים ובאמצע פתוחים בשני ראשיהן לרשות הרבים: הגה ויש אומרים דאין לו דין מבוי עקום אא"כ יש מן הפס עד כותל האמצעי יותר מעשר אמות (הרא"ש ור' ירוחם וטור.
