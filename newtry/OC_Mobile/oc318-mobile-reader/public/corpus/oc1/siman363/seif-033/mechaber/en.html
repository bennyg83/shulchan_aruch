**33.** An alleyway twenty cubits wide may be rectified by placing a four-cubit-wide post in the middle, making each side of the post a separate alleyway — since the post's length is four cubits — and a beam is placed at the head of each. Care must be taken that the beam rests on the post or within three handbreadths of it. Each of the two alleyways created by the post must have houses and courtyards opening into it and satisfy all alleyway conditions. The area between the post and the middle wall of the alleyway has the status of a bent alleyway (*mavoi akum*), since these two alleyways curve and are open at both ends to a public domain. {Rama: Some say it has the status of a bent alleyway only if the distance from the post to the middle wall exceeds ten cubits.}
