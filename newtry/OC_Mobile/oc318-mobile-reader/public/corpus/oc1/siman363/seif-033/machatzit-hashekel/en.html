(s.k. 33) And they did not make — for since not all, etc. — meaning this law follows from the law explained below siman 362 regarding dividing a city into mavo'ot — see there; and the same here, since they are one mavo and the way of all mavo residents is to join one eruv — if each half joins alone it forbids its portion and the other half like courtyard residents who cannot divide; but if the outer half of the mavo did not make a lechi — even if they wished to join with the inner it does not help to permit carrying; therefore when they did not join with them they do not forbid the inner:
