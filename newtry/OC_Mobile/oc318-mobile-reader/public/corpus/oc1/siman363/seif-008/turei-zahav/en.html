From asherah wood — valid. Beit Yosef wrote: and according to Maharam who holds that cloak cord specifically — do not question from that we validate asherah wood for cut down its measure; one answer: that which requires cloak cord is not because it requires measure but because it must be fit to place something on it — and asherah is also fit to place on it etc.; another answer: the reason of cut down measure is because it stands for burning and since it is possible that it will burn and become ash if one kneads and makes from it a pillar it helps as lechi — end of his words; and I am astonished that a whole mishnah at the end of Temurah was hidden from him: all that are burned — their ash is permitted except asherah wood; and as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 142 that ash of asherah is forbidden in benefit — if so one must scatter it to the wind and the ash is like the wood itself:
