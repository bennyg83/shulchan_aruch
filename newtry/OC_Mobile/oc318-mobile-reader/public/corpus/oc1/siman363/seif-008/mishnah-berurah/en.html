(א) A lechi that one made, etc. — in the Gemara it states that one may make a lechi from asherah wood, and it implies even l'chatchila. And one should not question: although for lechi, since it does not require measure, cut down its measure does not apply, as below — nevertheless it is forbidden in benefit; one may say that since the lechi is only for the sake of partition and one does not use anything in it itself, benefit from what one benefits through it for permitting carrying is not considered benefit [Beit Meir see there]:

(ב) Valid — the reason is that since a lechi does not require measure and any amount suffices, as above in seif 3, even from asherah wood it helps, although asherah wood stands for burning and is as if burned, and cut down its measure — nevertheless it does not require measure; and although here it still requires at least height measure of ten tefachim, since one can make it a very thin line of any amount, the Sages were not stringent about such a small measure; and they also answered in this that for lechi, even if the wood were burned to ash and kneaded with ash and glued to the wall, it is also considered a lechi, provided it is not removable by wind; therefore it is not disqualified because it stands for burning:
