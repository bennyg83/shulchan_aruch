(א) There. A courtyard breached in its full, etc. Explanation: in a square courtyard breached on the fourth side repair is needed different from what they repair for a mavo — its length exceeds its width and breached on the fourth side: in a courtyard a pas 4 on one side or two pasin of mashehu is needed; but in a mavo whose length exceeds its width and breached on the fourth side they permitted with lechi all that it is from one side as written below seif 3 Bach; and the same a courtyard whose length exceeds its width — its law is as a mavo as written below seif 27; and the reason for the difference between courtyard and mavo is that whatever is used discreetly requires more partition as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba; and so Razah ot 3 and see below ot 164.

(ב) There. In its full. Meaning the entire wall.
