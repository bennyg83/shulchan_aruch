Even if it is not breached across its full width, etc. This appears to mean that it is obvious that when it is breached across its full width and the breach exceeds ten amos, it must be repaired with a tzuras hapesach. Even when it is not breached across its full width, however, since the breach exceeds ten amos it must be repaired with a tzuras hapesach. Although according to Rambam a tzuras hapesach is ineffective for a breach exceeding ten amos unless the standing portion exceeds the breached portion, as stated above in siman 362, se'if 10, we already wrote there in subsection 89 that on the third side it is effective even according to Rambam.
