(א) (s.k. 19) And some invalidate — for they hold like the first view in seif 22 who hold that although within three we say lavud — nevertheless here it does not help, for it must be fit to place upon it; and if distant more than a tefach it is impossible to place a brick on them since there is in width only one tefach and a half. And here even if there is not a tefach between them — nevertheless since one is above and one below they are not fit to place a brick on them:
