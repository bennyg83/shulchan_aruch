[19] To receive a brick, etc. Meaning if the mavo is 10 amot wide it must receive 18 bricks which is 9 amot as written in seif 25 (Avodat HaKodesh). Beit Yosef and Bach wrote: if made of straw and reeds even if strong enough to receive a brick it is forbidden since most straw and reeds are not strong. A mavo open on two sides that was repaired on one side with tzurat hapetach and on one side the two sides of the mavo were not equal but one short and one long — lechi suffices (Ralbag 66):
