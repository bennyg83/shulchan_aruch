(א) By a korah — so that through it there will be recognition that they should not come to confuse and carry in a full reshus ha-rabbim, and also that they should not come to carry from it outward:

(ב) On the head of the mavo — meaning at the end of the mavo; and if he placed it in the middle of the mavo, see below in seif 362:

(ג) But if he drove, etc. — meaning that he inserted two pegs into the two walls on this side and that toward reshus ha-rabbim; and what is written "even close to it" means the pegs were very short, and automatically when he placed the korah on them it was close to the walls without any gap between them — nevertheless it is invalid; and the reason is that for a korah on walls we require that it not appear that it came to qualify the mavo, but only when it is within the hollow of the mavo, for it is not the way of any ceiling or korah to place it except on the walls and not from outside; and specifically for korah, but for lechi we do not require within the hollow of the mavo, but even outside the hollow of the mavo, and like that case of seif 13 above; and the reason of the matter is that a lechi, even standing outside, is recognizable that it came to qualify the mavo, unlike a korah, as explained:
