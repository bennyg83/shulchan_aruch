Another correction is possible for a mavo that is twenty amot wide — that he distanc two amot from the wall and make a pas three amot wide, and do so also on the second side, and an opening ten amot wide remains; or he distances one amah and makes a pas of one and a half amot and distances one amah and makes a pas of one and a half amot, and so on the second side; or he distances two amot and two tefachim and makes a pas of two amot and four tefachim, and so on the second side; and so all similar cases — provided the pas be greater than the airspace between it and the wall, for if not the airspace of this side and that side comes and nullifies it; and one must beware not to leave a large opening and enter through a small one, for if he did so the correction of the mavo is nullified unless he made tzurat hapetach for the small one:
