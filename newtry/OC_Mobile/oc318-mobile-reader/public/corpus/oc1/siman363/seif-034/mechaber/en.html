**34.** There is another way to rectify an alleyway twenty cubits wide: set back two cubits from the wall and place a three-cubit post, and do the same on the other side, leaving an opening of ten cubits. Alternatively, set back one cubit and place a post of one and a half cubits, then set back another cubit and place another post of one and a half cubits, doing the same on the other side. Or set back two cubits and two handbreadths and place a post of two cubits and four handbreadths, likewise on the other side. Similar arrangements work — provided the post is wider than the gap between it and the wall, for otherwise the air space on both sides would nullify it. Care must be taken not to leave a large opening while entering through a small one, for if this is done the alleyway's correction is nullified — unless a doorframe shape is made for the smaller opening.
