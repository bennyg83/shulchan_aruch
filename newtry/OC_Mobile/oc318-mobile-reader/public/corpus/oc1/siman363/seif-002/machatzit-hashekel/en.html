(s.k. 2) All the more even less than a fingerbreadth. He needed to write thus, for in Eruvin we learned that lechi suffices with "enough," and the Gemara asked how much is "enough" and concludes even like the thread of a cloak; and Mordechai wrote in the name of Maharam that the measure of the thread of a cloak is like the thickness of a finger, except that in Terumat HaDeshen siman 74 he wrote that we do not require the thickness of a finger but for lechi; but for tzurat hapetach the two reeds on the sides — even the slightest amount suffices (and from this came Rema's gloss "even lechi of plaster suffices" — see there); and Rabbeinu Yosef Karo wrote that since the poskim were silent it is implied they hold that the thread of a cloak is not precise but the slightest amount literally, and it has no measure at all; and so is the implication of Shulchan Aruch which was silent:
