A courtyard breached fully up to ten amot is permitted by a pas four tefachim wide that one places on one side in the place of the breach; and if he wishes to correct it on both sides of the breach, two pasin of two mashehuyin suffice; and the same applies if a fourth wall remained built to the measure of four tefachim in one place, or if from the two sides one tefach from here and one tefach from here remained — and all this at a height of ten tefachim; and even if the breach is not four tefachim, since it is fully breached correction is required until there remains in it less than' three; and if the breach is more than ten amot, even if it is not fully breached, it requires correction by tzurat hapetach:
