A courtyard that was breached across its full width up to ten cubits may be rectified with a post four handbreadths wide placed on one side at the breach. If one wishes to rectify it with posts on both sides of the breach, two posts of any minimal width suffice. The same applies if a fourth wall remains standing, measuring four handbreadths in one place, or if one handbreadth remains on each side. All of this must be at a height of ten handbreadths. Even if the breach is less than four handbreadths wide, since it spans the full width it still requires rectification until it is less than three handbreadths. If the breach exceeds ten cubits, even if it does not span the full width, it must be rectified with a doorframe shape (*tzurat hapetach*).
