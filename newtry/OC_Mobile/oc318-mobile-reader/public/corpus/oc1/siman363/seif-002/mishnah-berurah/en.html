(א) That was breached — meaning to a forbidden place for it:
(ב) Fully — meaning the entire wall:
(ג) Up to ten amot — and "up to" includes it; for although generally we hold that if a wall was breached ten amot the breach is considered an opening, here since the entire wall is missing we cannot consider this place an opening:
(ד) In one place — meaning at the top of the wall:
(ה) One tefach from here, etc. — not specifically one tefach but two mashehuyin suffice, as likewise for placing pasin l'chatchila, and as Beit Yosef wrote; and see Biur Halacha:
(ו) At a height of ten tefachim — and it need not be equal in height to the other walls; however below this it has no name of partition at all:
(ז) Since it is fully — this "fully" is not precise, for even if two or three tefachim remained from the wall, any minority relative to the breach requires correction; rather it comes to exclude where standing is greater, for there we hold the majority is as the whole and it is as closed, and even though it does not hold four tefachim, any slight amount more than the breach suffices:
(ח) Until there remains in it less than three — meaning that one corrects the breach in part and only less than three remains, for then it is as lavud; and all the more if the breach was only less than three, for then no correction is needed for this reason:
(ט) Even if it is not fully — this wording is not precise, for even when standing is greater than the breach it is also forbidden, as if the entire wall were breached. And tzurat hapetach helps even when it is fully:
