(There in seif 11) Even if he did not make it for the sake of lechi, etc., provided they relied on it on erev Shabbat, etc. Behold I was asked regarding the law of tzurat hapetach that they erected on Shabbat a reed with ropes — whether it is permitted to carry even though they did not rely on it on erev Shabbat; behold in my opinion the matter is simple and clear that it is permitted to carry and we do not require that they relied on it — only for lechi where there is no new recognition now and new making, and behold it is as it was yesterday, therefore we require that they relied on it on erev Shabbat; but for tzurat hapetach which is recognizable that it is an opening — not to mention if they erected the lechi and ropes on it now, certainly it is permitted, for it is not applicable to say that they did not rely on it on erev Shabbat since there is here great recognition that they erected it today; also even if the lechi stood from yesterday and now they tied the ropes on it — also permitted since we see that it is an opening, we do not require that they relied; and what the Taz wrote at the end of this siman — there it is different for the rope is not placed on the projections but only above and it is not true tzurat hapetach but only to combine it in our minds in place of lechi — here since it stood here from erev Shabbat and also is not placed on it but only above, here we require that they relied on it on erev Shabbat; but where they made it anew on Shabbat, even though the rope is above, or it was already there and they placed the rope on it in a manner that it is like a true opening — in this certainly we do not require that they relied on it on erev Shabbat, and so it appears correct in my humble opinion since it was made like a true opening we do not require that they relied on it on erev Shabbat, and so it appears and examine well:
