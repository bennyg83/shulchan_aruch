(there, in the Rama gloss) Two reeds ten [tefachim] high etc. — see responsum of Chatam Sofer Orach Chayim siman 91 [there he wrote at the end of the responsum that he found in his community in the community of Pressburg from the days of the Gaon R' Meshulam may his righteous memory be blessed that one of the reeds stands behind the wall because it is impossible to place it in reshut ha-rabbim in any manner in that place because of the people of the city, and the Gaon may his righteous memory be blessed commanded to wrap above it another reed outside the wall and it is much higher from the ground — only that it should appear from outside that there be recognition for the people of the mavo. And behold this second reed is invalid for behold it is much higher from the ground and this is only for recognition; nevertheless the community acted according to what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 362 s.k. 20 and I did not wish to change from the instructions of the Gaon who is fit to rely on even not in a time of pressing need. And in my humble opinion it would appear to place a thick korah and to groove half its thickness along the entire length of the wall in a manner that only from inside and above the wall its full thickness would remain, in a manner that it protrudes outside into the hollow of the mavo beyond the wall — and with this we satisfy all concerns]:
