(12) There. Two pasin of two mashehuin suffice. Shulchan Aruch distinguishes: if made by hand two mashehuin suffice, and if something remained specifically a tefach from here and a tefach from here as written afterward; and Bach wrote one should not be lenient, for even when made by hand one should make a tefach l'chatchila from here and from here — see there; however Eliyah Rabbah ot 2 challenged Bach and so is the view of the later authorities; and see below ot 14.
