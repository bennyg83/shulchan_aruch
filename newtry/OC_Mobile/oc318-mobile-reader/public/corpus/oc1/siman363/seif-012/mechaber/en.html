**12.** A lechi that stands on its own — even if one relied on it before Shabbat — if the alleyway is wider than eight cubits and the lechi protrudes four cubits into it, it is not treated as a lechi and another lechi is needed; it should be placed on the opposite side. If one wishes to place it alongside the first, one should make it slightly thicker or thinner than the protrusion, so that it is recognizable as a lechi.But if it was explicitly placed as a lechi — which is publicly known that a lechi was made for this alleyway — even if it protrudes four cubits it is valid as a lechi.
