In Magen Avraham s.k. 37: and what Magen Avraham wrote — see siman 364 that on the other side two pasin suffice; in Sefer Tosafot Shabbos he expanded his words that here two pasin are required like courtyard and the sea questioned Darkhei Moshe for it implies tzurat hapetach is required and wrote perhaps specifically in complete mavo Magen Avraham speaks that lechi suffices; but here tzurat hapetach is required — end of his words; and this is not Magen Avraham's intent; and in my humble opinion Magen Avraham holds that even here lechi suffices; for even if it has law of courtyard nevertheless its breach is in ten and then tzurat hapetach is required; and if not two pasin suffice if four remained in one place or a tefach from here as explained above seif 2; and in our towns where on the fourth side also there are houses from this and that side — two pasin suffice when breach is not in ten and only lechi or korah is required because it has law of mavo and also in mavo law lechi or korah does not help if breached more than ten; and what Darkhei Moshe wrote I do not know the meaning; but I saw Darkhei Moshe wondered at Rashba that Bach brought and wrote that in courtyard tzurat hapetach does not help from here and lechi or korah from here but two pasin are required from the other side; and in my humble opinion the question on Rashba is when there is no breach in full and it is not in ten only open from two sides to reshut ha-rabbim where it is — then Rashba speaks well that tzurat hapetach is a complete partition and is not less than mavo where the other side is permitted with lechi or korah; and what is in the name of Maharil in responsum requires study; for Maharil siman 262 and Bach brought that he doubts perhaps lechi alone does not suffice — this is when open to gentiles' mavo'ot and from reason of removing residents as explained in his wording explicitly there; but regarding mavo qualification he agrees lechi alone suffices — see there; see in Tosafot who wrote with lechi or korah from here and even though lechi does not help in width ten — one may say he makes a wide lechi that reduces the hollow to ten etc.;
