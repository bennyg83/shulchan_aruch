And with this Ba'er Heitev; and see in Emunat Shmuel siman 40 — if the ditch was blocked with earth and stones — if the blockage is not more than ten amot high it is like an opening and does not forbid; but if more than ten amot high it is like a breach; and see in responsum Chacham Tzvi siman 5 that the law of a city surrounded by a ditch ten deep and four wide is like a city enclosed by a high wall with doors and bar — therefore he permitted carrying in the city of Agh — see there; and so wrote also in responsum Ohel Yaakov siman 73 regarding permitting carrying in the city of Agh; however he wrote there are bridges over which people pass from side to side to karmelit — and he permitted in a manner that the bridges not be fixed with nails or building but movable and removable at any time when they need to raise them with chains — and it is like fit to remove; and through raising them the city would be locked and enclosed; and even if fixed nevertheless there is tzurat hapetach in the middle of the bridges; and since its breached head is to karmelit — tzurat hapetach suffices; and therefore through acquiring permission in the eruv and leaving a remainder not in the eruv for recognition — carrying is permitted; and see there that even if bridges not fixed are locked with a key — nevertheless fit to remove; and through raising the bridges and closing the key it is not fixed but temporary; and not similar to sunk in earth lacking action — see there; and see Noda B'Yehuda Mahadura Tinyana siman 42 who also wrote regarding a city breached entirely in the fourth direction only that a river runs along the city and city ground is ten tefachim higher than the water — only there is a bridge wider than ten — tzurat hapetach must be made on the bridge — see there;
