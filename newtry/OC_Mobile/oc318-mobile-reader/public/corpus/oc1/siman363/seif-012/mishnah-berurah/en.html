(א) A lechi standing, etc. — the measure of his words is thus: for that which is written in seif 11 that a lechi standing by itself and they relied on it helps — specifically if the lechi does not protrude into the width of the mavo four amot; but if it protrudes and extends from the house wall into the width of the mavo four amot, then it leaves the category of lechi [because it has the qualifying measure of mavo length, which is four amot, as below in siman 366], and there it is a mavo wall upon it, and another lechi is required at the mavo opening to permit it — unless the mavo does not hold its width except eight amot, for then it is permitted without another lechi, for it is standing as breached and the mavo is considered closed on all sides, as likewise in siman 362 seif 9:
(ב) By itself — for if he placed it for the sake of lechi, even four amot wide, it is a lechi, as written in the gloss:
(ג) Four amot — for four amot is the measure of a mavo wall, and there is no recognition at all that it is a lechi; but up to four amot we can count it as a lechi, and this is when they relied on it from erev Shabbat, as likewise in seif 11:
(ד) Even four amot wide — and the same for more than this; and the reason is that because he increased at the mavo opening in its closing, it is no worse than an ordinary lechi [Rashi]:
