(1) [Seif 1] A place that has three partitions, etc. The Tur wrote that from the Torah it is true reshut ha-yachid, and Beit Yosef wrote on this that he does not speak to make one who throws from reshus ha-rabbim into it liable, but to permit carrying in all of it, for such is the law of karmelit from the Torah, per Rambam chapters 14 and 17; but there are those who disagree and say that a place with three partitions is true reshut ha-yachid to make one who throws from reshus ha-rabbim into it liable; and the Maggid Mishneh already explained in chapter 17 that their dispute depends on the sugya in Eruvin — see there. But the Bach wrote that the Tur's view is not like the Rambam but to make liable also one who throws from reshus ha-rabbim into it — see there; and so the Taz s.k. 1, except he wrote to distinguish when open to reshus ha-rabbim and the public pushes into it — he is not liable for throwing into it from reshus ha-rabbim; but if the public does not push into it, it is true reshut ha-yachid — see there; and Eliyah Rabbah brought one opinion and wrote that from Bach and Magen Avraham it implies that in every case he is liable; and so Chayei Adam opinion 1; and so Nahar Shalom; and on the essence of this law — Rashi's view in Eruvin 12b and Bach in several places, Raavad in hasagot, Rosh first chapter of Sukkah, Rashba in chiddushim and Avodat HaKodesh, and Ritva — with three partitions or two partitions and lechi it is true reshut ha-yachid from the Torah; and see above siman 345 opinion 5 and siman 366 opinion 3, examine well:
