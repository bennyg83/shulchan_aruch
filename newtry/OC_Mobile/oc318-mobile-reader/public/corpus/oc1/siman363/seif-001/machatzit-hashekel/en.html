(א) (s.k. 1) It is not, etc. — it is possible that it deals, etc. — his intent is to write here a safek in the law, and it is that in Eruvin daf 12a Shmuel said that a courtyard breached fully is not permitted except if one makes most of the wall, etc., and it challenges — but aenv pas four tefachim suffices; and you should establish that Shmuel deals with a courtyard whose wall, meaning its width, is seven tefachim — on the contrary, in what Shmuel said "most of the wall" it is a leniency that we do not require pas four tefachim but in three and a half and mashehu suffices, for it is most of the wall; if so even in three and a half and mashehu suffices, since it closes three and a half and mashehu and four tefachim do not remain breached; and Rav Achlai said a mavo whose width is not four tefachim needs no correction at all; and it answers that there is a difference between courtyard and mavo — for in a courtyard even if wide less than four tefachim correction is required; and alternatively Rav Achlai is a tannaitic view and Shmuel holds like the Tanna who argues on Rav Achlai; and in this the poskim agreed that we hold like Shmuel, except that they disagree — for Rambam ruled like the second resolution in Shmuel, why need Rav Achlai — if so even a mavo whose width is not four tefachim needs correction, all the more a courtyard; and Rosh and Shulchan Aruch ruled like Shmuel's first resolution, not arguing with Rav Achlai, and there is a difference between courtyard and mavo — for a mavo whose width is not four tefachim needs no correction, but a courtyard whose width is not four tefachim needs correction; and both views were brought below seif 28. But in this both agree that whether per the one or per the other, a courtyard even if not four tefachim wide needs correction — except that Rabbeinu Yosef Karo wrote below in the name of Rashba: in a mavo whose width is not four tefachim that needs no correction — it is only if the entire mavo is not four tefachim wide, or even if very wide but at its end at the opening it is only four tefachim — no correction is needed. Magen Avraham brought it below s.k. 29. And in this Magen Avraham was uncertain — if we say the same for a courtyard there is no difference and in every case correction is required; or it is possible that it deals when the courtyard is wide in the middle, etc. — specifically in such a case correction is required, for per the questioner courtyard and mavo are equal (and so per the second resolution), and in both no correction is needed except that the first resolver so strained because of the necessity of distinguishing between courtyard and mavo; and if so what we were compelled by necessity suffices — meaning when the courtyard is wide in the middle, etc. — in such a case Shmuel deals, for he deals with a wall of seven and mashehu from three and a half does not suffice to close it, for then it would be nine wide in the middle and at the opening four tefachim less mashehu; therefore correction is required; but when the entire width is not four tefachim the questioner's reasoning remains that courtyard and mavo are equal and no correction is needed:
