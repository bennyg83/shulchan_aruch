In Tur it is written here that it is complete reshut ha-yachid from the Torah, and Beit Yosef questioned: behold in siman 345 Tur wrote that mavo'ot that have three partitions and no lechi and korah on the fourth side are like karmelit; and he answered that what he said here complete reshut ha-yachid is not to make one who throws into it from reshut ha-rabbim liable but to permit carrying within it etc., for such is the law of karmelit from the Torah etc., and this is strained; for behold he said here complete reshut ha-yachid; and in my humble opinion to answer: in siman 345 it deals that the fourth opening is to reshut ha-rabbim as he wrote there, and the corners adjacent to reshut ha-rabbim such as mavo'ot that have three partitions etc.; and this mavo sometimes the public press to enter there when there are many people in reshut ha-rabbim as stated in the Gemara chapter 1 of Shabbos daf 10 — therefore even though by itself it is complete reshut ha-yachid nevertheless reshut ha-rabbim nullifies it from being reshut ha-yachid but it is called side of reshut ha-rabbim and is like karmelit; unlike here where we do not deal with this matter that the public press there sometimes — it remains on it the name complete reshut ha-yachid from the Torah even though he said here wherever there are three partitions etc.; and this is in Mehadrin part 17; and the wording of Knesset HaMelekh that Tur mentioned implies even open to reshut ha-rabbim and this is where the public do not press there; and understand that wherever Tur stated it refers to courtyard and mavo:
