(ב) The common person writing on the law of lavud and tzurat hapetach — I have thoughts on the matter and I set them forth here there's help; know that lavud according to Maggid Mishneh is so, and even two lavud above and below, and even in length we say lavud; and in explaining lavud — not what people say as closed is considered, per Maggid Mishneh — lechi distant from the wall less than three tefachim, Magen Avraham 5; rather even where we say all air is as closed, like pas four in seif 35 and three ropes, etc., siman 362 — behold there is height ten, Rashi Sukkah 4 side 2 s.v. less, etc., that they extend, etc.; and behold in lavud Maggid Mishneh — it is a complete partition for all its matters and the conditions of lavud in Yoreh De'ah 100 side 1 — Raavad wrote in hasagot that lavud cannot be used to be stringent — see there in Magen Avraham siman 372 letter 9 he expanded on this and brought s.v. Eruvin 10 — see there; and in siman 632 letter 5 he explained what the Tur wrote that we do not say lavud to be stringent — it is as adjacent; do not err to explain his words that lavud as adjacent — for we rule lavud as extending and as closed per Avodat HaKodesh here; rather to say lavud entirely to be stringent as closed; and in invalid s'chach we do not say except as closed, not as extending; but with valid s'chach and invalid s'chach between them air less than three tefachim — I have doubt whether lavud — do we also say as adjacent; and invalid or only as extending and closed, and we say as closed in valid — see there; and see Levush; and behold if we say lavud as actual substance — to say as if it is not — the inquirer in Sukkah 4 side 2 and below in Hilchot Sukkah 633 seif 10 carved up to ten less than three tefachim to the wall we say lavud; and Rashi explained the carving as if reaching the wall — behold lavud as actual; and one may say this is a dispute in the Tur there — Magen Avraham there letter 8 — whether valid from carving and to the wall suffices; and see Beit Shmuel 2:
