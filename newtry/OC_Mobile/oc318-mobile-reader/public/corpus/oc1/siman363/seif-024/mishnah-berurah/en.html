(א) A mat — that he wished to strengthen the permission and spread a mat over it, and also tied it to the wall so wind would not move it, so that there would also be a partition name upon it:

(ב) Invalid — even if the mat itself is ten high:

(ג) And it is not visible — if so there is no recognition here:

(ד) A wide korah — meaning that it is ten tefachim wide; and one might have thought that since it is so wide, a partition name is upon it and not a korah name, and a partition is not valid when it is high from the ground greatly, like a mat — it informs us that it is valid, for because of its greatness the korah name was not nullified from it; and a mat is different, for it covered the korah and it is not recognizable:

(ה) And extends somewhat below ten — several Acharonim questioned his words, for it appears from his wording that he comes to inform us that even part of the wide korah enters within the lower ten tefachim of the mavo, it is also valid, for we say we view this portion as if it were not there and the korah stands only above ten, as the korah qualification explained in siman 366; but in truth this is not so, for if part of the korah width entered within the airspace of the lower ten, it is invalid, for we require that the hollow of the mavo up to the korah be precisely ten tefachim:
