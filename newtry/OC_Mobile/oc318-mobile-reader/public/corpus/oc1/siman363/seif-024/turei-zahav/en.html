Somewhat below ten. — in Tur likewise only the word somewhat is not written there; and Beit Yosef explained: it appears that when the korah is placed above ten it deals — but if placed below ten even if its width is not more than required — invalid as Mahari wrote etc.; and his words are not understood; for it is obvious that korah below ten is invalid; and when its width above ten goes until below ten — obviously it is valid for here one need not take anything like with bent — but all this is not the topic here that one makes a wide korah from above downward for korah qualification — this matter he is not dealing with; and nevertheless it is obvious that Beit Yosef's intent is not to tie the permission of this thing specifically to what is below ten; for certainly if above ten it is preferable; rather he said that the head of the korah must be above ten; and Mahari Chalavah disagrees with Beit Yosef and wrote that he understood that the concern is in what part of it is below ten and not above ten — and it is not so; rather one must erase the mem of above ten and it must read ten — meaning the partition is ten — and in the abbreviations he also wrote like here there is a printing error only it must read ten — that is the measure of partition — perforce his words; and in truth Beit Yosef did not intend this and heaven forbid to say it would be judged as korah more when reaching below ten than when reaching above ten; rather regarding prohibition Beit Yosef said thus that the head of the korah should not be below ten — and this is a matter that could not be written here; and the main point is there is no printing error neither in Tur nor in the abbreviations except to amplify they said thus for when the korah is above ten obviously it is valid as korah for behold korah is not nullified there because of partition for there is no partition above ten at all; and this even for Rashba is valid for he said korah that leans below nineteen is invalid — it teaches us that above nineteen is valid — perforce Ra'osh and Tur said it is not so but even if it reaches until below ten and one would say there is partition here and korah is nullified — it teaches us that korah is not nullified there because of its size since the korah is visible — all this is obvious and nothing to emend here;
