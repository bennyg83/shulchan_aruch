(24) There. They permitted with lechi, etc. There is no concern of transgression in repairing mavo'ot, Heaven forbid — on the contrary the diligent one is praiseworthy; and one may wonder on one who could repair and does not repair; and were it not for the burden of study it is the way of a Torah scholar to repair; and one whose heart strikes him on this is a complete layman or apostasy has been cast into him.
