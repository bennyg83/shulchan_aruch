24) There. They permitted it with a lechi, etc. There is, Heaven forbid, no concern of transgression in arranging rectifications for alleyways. On the contrary, one who acts promptly is praiseworthy, and there is reason to be astonished at one who can arrange a rectification but does not do so. Were it not for the demands of Torah study, it would be the way of a Torah scholar to arrange it. One whose conscience troubles him about this is either an utter ignoramus or has been infected by heresy. Tashbetz, responsa, volume 2, siman 37; Birkei Yosef, subsection 2.
