(א) They permitted by a lechi — and although in a courtyard we require specifically a pas four tefachim wide, here the Sages were lenient; and the reason is that the more something is made for dwelling and private uses, the more complete partitions are required; therefore courtyards, whose custom is that homeowners use them more for private uses and eating, require their partitions to be more complete so that there is reshut ha-yachid upon them, unlike mavo'ot where they do not use them for private uses; therefore a mavo suffices with a lechi; and see below siman 366 that this is specifically when its opening is not wider than ten amot, and further details see there:
(ב) Any amount — even less than a fingerbreadth (Acharonim):
(ג) At the opening of the mavo — see below siman 361:
(ד) Ten tefachim — spaced and not precise [Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger]; and ten tefachim help even for a mavo twenty amot high:
(ה) And even if he tied, etc. — and it deals with when they did not inform that they placed it for the sake of lechi; if so certainly it will go away and not stand there all day, therefore we require specifically tying; and some are stringent even when they informed, lest he forget and go away from there:
