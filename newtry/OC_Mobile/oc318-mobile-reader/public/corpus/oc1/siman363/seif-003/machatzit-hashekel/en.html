(א) (s.k. 3) Tied, etc. — that for a person tying is not required, etc. — and this is the way of "enough" (kol shehu); there it certainly deals when they did not inform, for otherwise it is forbidden for it is like one who makes a partition on Shabbat (or for another reason), as Magen Avraham wrote there s.k. 12; and nevertheless tying is not required, as Magen Avraham wrote there s.k. 8 — that a person does not leave his place except for need, and here presumably since they placed it on erev Shabbat for the sake of lechi he will stand all Shabbat and certainly they informed, since on erev Shabbat it is permitted to inform him; and if so certainly they informed so he would not move from there; and if so certainly he will not leave from there — why is tying required:

(ב) That it deals when they did not inform, etc. — meaning specifically above in siman 362 that person need not be there except per hour — tying is not required even if they did not inform, as above, for a person does not leave in a short time from his place except for need; unlike here where he must stand there all Shabbat, therefore tying is required:
