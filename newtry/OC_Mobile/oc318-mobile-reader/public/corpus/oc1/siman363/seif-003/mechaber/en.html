An alleyway that has three walls and is open on the fourth side may be permitted by a side-post (*lechi*) — meaning, as Targum translates, "one board" (*lucha chada*) — of any thickness and width, placed at the entrance of the alleyway, at least ten handbreadths high. It may be made of any material, even a living creature, provided it is tied there with ropes to the walls of the alleyway by pegs projecting from the walls, in such a way that it cannot lie down and thereby be reduced below ten handbreadths. {Rama: Even if a person is tied there, it also constitutes a lechi.}
