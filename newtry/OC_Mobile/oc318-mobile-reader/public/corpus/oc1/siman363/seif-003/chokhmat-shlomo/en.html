Behold this is a dispute in the first chapter of Eruvin — Abbaye holds that amot of a mavo and amot of a sukkah are in amot of a daughter of five, and Rava holds in amot of a daughter of six only precise; and behold the Ra'osh wrote there that the halacha follows Rava, for Abbaye's matter we establish like Rashbag and Rava's matter like all — and I wondered why he needed this proof, for behold it is explicit in the Talmud that everywhere the halacha follows Rava against Abbaye except yod ayin lamed kuf gimmel mem; and one cannot say that this means only in Torah matters and not in rabbinic matters — this is not so, for behold here Rava is lenient and if so all the more in rabbinic matters the halacha follows Rava to be lenient; and further behold in yod ayin lamed kog gimmel mem it deals with lechi standing by itself and thus it is proved that also in rabbinic matters this rule applies; also I saw there, in the Ra'osh letter dalet that he discusses whether the halacha follows Rav Yosef who is Abbaye's teacher or Abbaye, and it is implied that he holds that what we rule halacha follows the later ones from Abbaye and Rava onward — Abbaye and Rava themselves are not included; and see in my Chiburei Nedarim Zerizin siman 207 seif 18 where I brought there, in the name of the Bach to the contrary, and in my Chiburei Sefer HaChayim siman 258 — and it is astonishing that these words of the early ones brought in the Ra'osh here were hidden from them; requires study. Further I saw that the question I asked on the Ra'osh — why not per the reason that the halacha is not like Abbaye because Abbaye is only like Rashbag and Rava like all, and behold otherwise Abbaye and Rava — halacha follows Rava except yod ayin lamed kuf gimmel mem — the Knesset HaGedolah sensed this and strained in it; and in my humble opinion one may say more correctly: behold Rava said these are amot and Rashi explained amot precise, and accordingly one may say that this sign of yod ayin lamed kuf gimmel mem — the ayin hints at amot or the mem from kuf gimmel mem hints at precise — and thereby the halacha follows Abbaye; and one may say that certainly here the halacha follows Abbaye and it is yod ayin lamed kuf gimmel mem from what the Talmud ruled in ed zomem in Bava Kamma and mumar eating nevelot in Sanhedrin like Abbaye — one may say the Talmud was received that the halacha follows Abbaye in yod ayin lamed kuf gimmel mem and was itself uncertain whether the ayin hints at amot; but in ed zomem the halacha is not like Abbaye, or conversely the ayin hints at ed zomem and only the mem hints at precise, and in mumar the halacha is not like Abbaye; and since it is uncertain to him we rule according to both stringently like Abbaye who is stringent in both; but always one may say that amot here is one of the signs yod ayin lamed kuf gimmel mem and especially since it is to be stringent, therefore the Ra'osh wrote that here it is reasonable that the halacha is not like Abbaye since it is like a tannaitic dispute and therefore this is not yod ayin lamed kuf gimmel mem — and it comes out well thus it appears to speak on behalf of the Ra'osh, and this is easier than their strains — examine well; and behold when I was on this it was difficult for me in what they said both Abbaye and Rava that in sukkah where it is to be stringent it was a daughter of five or it is precise — and everything since it is to be stringent; and it is difficult, for behold we say in Sukkah chapter 3 regarding R' Yehuda who says a sukkah is enjoyed only with the four species of the lulav a fortiori, and they said there the Rabbis: any law whose beginning is to be stringent and its end to be lenient — there is no law; if he did not find four species he would sit idle, etc.; if so it is proved that this stringency is a leniency; if so here too it is a leniency if he has no sukkah except with a bent side of four amot precise or height twenty precise; if so if we say it is invalid and he has no other he would sit idle; if so this stringency is a leniency — and how did he say it is to be stringent, behold it is a stringency that leads to a leniency; and granted in a mavo it is only a stringency for otherwise he would not carry in it and he has no other; and one cannot say that granted there where it speaks from Torah law — if from Torah law it is not a sukkah except with four species, then even if he has no other he is exempt from sitting in this one, therefore it is considered a stringency that leads to a leniency; but here which is only rabbinic — if so in truth if he has no other he sits in this one and therefore it is not a stringency that leads to a leniency; but if so why does the Talmud challenge that it is a leniency within four amot, and for one answer they do not hold that it speaks of most amot — it should have challenged more, for behold in height and bent side there is also leniency if he has no other whether he is obligated to sit in this one or not — then we measure in an amah of a daughter of six and perforce we must say it speaks of most amot; if so it is difficult for the one who does not hold it speaks of most amot — requires study, examine well. And see in my Chiburei Laws of Orach Chayim from the year 5601 in the first chapter laws of Chanukah siman 471 what is written there regarding korah — whatever part of it is within twenty or near twenty amah, as long as the hollow is only twenty — even if you lean it upward the lower one does not suffice and it would fall, nevertheless it is valid since in the end now it stands, it becomes like iron skewers; but in s'chach it is not so — only here if you lean it upward the lower one's shade would not be greater from itself it is invalid — not like Ran whom the Taz brought in siman 633, see there, a matter in its reason for it is good there's help, examine well: (There) And if it was higher than twenty and he built a structure beneath the korah to reduce below twenty — a structure one tefach wide suffices in the width of the korah, etc. Behold what is written that it should be higher than twenty — the intent perforce is that it refers to the hollow, for the hollow is higher than twenty and not the korah, for behold the Shulchan Aruch ruled above that the hollow of a mavo we learned as is written explicitly in this seif, and if the hollow was higher than twenty, etc.; if so since here it deals that the hollow is more than twenty — why did he write afterward and he built a structure beneath the korah to reduce below twenty — it would be enough to reduce from more than twenty until twenty it is valid; from this it appears clearly that his intent is as the Maggid Mishneh wrote in s.k. 7 — it appears from the words of the Rif and R' Yonatan beginning of Eruvin that if initially it was above twenty and he comes to reduce it he places the korah so that it is also part of the twenty; but if it is not high above twenty only the korah — he need not reduce it for this purpose, perforce. And this that the Shulchan Aruch wrote precisely — first he wrote and if it was higher than twenty — meaning the hollow is more than twenty, for otherwise if the hollow was not more than twenty even though the korah is placed above twenty he would not need to reduce; but since the hollow is above twenty and if so he needs to reduce it — here he needs to reduce below twenty so that even twenty the hollow should not be only the korah should be within twenty; however it appears he need not reduce so much that the entire thickness of the korah should be within twenty — only enough to reduce somewhat from twenty so that somewhat of the korah is placed within twenty; and the reason appears: granted if the law were that the korah itself should be part of the twenty — then we require that the entire korah be within the twenty, for otherwise if you lean it — it would be a korah that was removed by wind and does not help; but according to us that we rule the hollow of a mavo we learned — then by law the entire korah may be above twenty, only therefore we require initially that the korah be within twenty lest he not be precise in measure and perhaps the hollow would be somewhat more than twenty and the korah invalid; therefore it is fit to be precise initially that the korah itself be within twenty and he not come to error in this — that if somewhat of the korah is placed within twenty there is no longer possibility of error that the hollow be more than twenty; and that the thickness of the korah is more than twenty we do not care, for in truth the entire korah is permitted above twenty for the hollow of a mavo we learned and it is not applicable to say it was removed; and therefore the Shulchan Aruch was precise and wrote that he built a structure to reduce below twenty and did not write how much to reduce per the measure of the korah's thickness — perforce that with a slight reduction it suffices, and this is as we wrote that with somewhat of the korah that is within twenty it is permitted even initially. And with this is resolved what my friend the sharp rabbi R' Yisrael Bik asked to challenge the Maggid Mishneh's view from the Talmud Eruvin daf 3 side 2 — what is stated there that it challenges per the one who says the hollow of a mavo we learned from what we learned how does he act — he places the korah from the edge of twenty and below, etc.; and why does it challenge — behold initially we require that the korah be within twenty and he did not say the hollow of a mavo we learned only for after the fact — and why does it challenge; and according to what we wrote it comes out very well, for behold Rashi explained there from the edge of twenty and below that the thickness of the korah should be within twenty — if so it challenges well: granted if the korah is part of the twenty — then we require that the entire thickness of the korah be part of the twenty; but if the hollow of a mavo we learned only initially it is fit that it be within twenty — here that if somewhat of the korah is within twenty and not the entire thickness of the korah — why require the entire thickness of the korah within the twenty; and what it answers there from the edge of twenty and above — the intent is not that until and not until is included and initially it is permitted to place the entire korah above twenty, only the intent is until and until is included that the korah should begin from the edge of twenty exactly and above so that part of the korah is placed at the edge of twenty and the length of its thickness goes above twenty — and this is exactly according to our view, examine well. However behold I examined again the language of Rabbeinu Yonatan beginning of Eruvin from which is the source of this law of the Maggid Mishneh — it is written there explicitly s.v. he places the korah, etc., that the entire thickness of the korah enters to complete twenty amah — and therefore the sharp rabbi's question remains in place; but the language of the Rif there does not bear even per the Maggid Mishneh's view — this is the language of the Rif there how does he reduce it — he places upon it a korah from twenty and below, for where the hollow is twenty, end of his words — and it is language that has no explanation at all; and even per the Maggid Mishneh's understanding there is no explanation that first it implies from his language that if he reduces he must reduce initially to be less than twenty and afterward he wrote for where the hollow is twenty — it implies he can make the reduction so its hollow is twenty — and his language contradicts itself; therefore it appeared clearly that a printing error fell in the Rif and it must read from twenty amah and above, for where the hollow is twenty; and also Rashi's view s.v. as much as he needs daf 4 side 2 is explicit there that enough initially to reduce so twenty hollow remains and not like the Maggid Mishneh's view. And behold to resolve Rabbeinu Yonatan's view it appeared — and also to resolve further what the sharp rabbi R' Yisrael Bik asked — why does it challenge on daf 4 it was high twenty amah and he came to reduce it how much does he reduce and it challenges as much as he needs — why does it challenge, let us say it challenges how much he reduces whether until twenty or more; but it appears certainly according to Rava himself who holds the hollow of a mavo we learned — even initially it is permitted to leave twenty of the hollow, only since Rabbah holds that the thickness of the korah is also part of the twenty — one may say this question how much does he reduce as much as he needs — Rav Yosef and Abbaye themselves who answer afterward are they themselves who asked thus, and like what Tosafos wrote in chapter 2 of Bava Metzia regarding and how much R' Yehuda in the first explanation, and in Beitzah daf 38 and in Nedarim likewise Rav Yosef and Abbaye themselves challenged thus — one may say they hold like Rabbah that with the korah it is twenty and do not hold like Rava, therefore since they and Rabbah hold thus and also for Rava the baraita that the Talmud challenges from it is difficult and must be emended — therefore the halacha follows Rava since he is later; and also since korah is rabbinic the halacha follows the lenient view — however this is specifically after the fact, but initially one should be concerned for Rabbah's view and his supporters and see that even the thickness of the korah be from the hollow of twenty in order to satisfy all views; but for Rava himself even initially we do not require that the korah be within twenty and it challenges well for Rava as in my humble opinion to explain Rabbeinu Yonatan's view; but since according to Rashi's view it is proved to the contrary and from the Rif also it is proved thus that there is a printing error in his words and it is proved he holds like Rashi — and this matter being rabbinic it appears we need not be concerned for this even initially as in my humble opinion here, examine well: Behold I said to copy here some questions necessary for practical law that I answered briefly. I was asked by the great rabbi R' Yoel Deutsch av beit din of Manistrishtsh regarding the eruv matter of the first chapter that was discussed — tzurat hapetach extended over several rooftops in a manner that the break between the middle houses — the lechi beneath the tzurat hapetach is not seen from this side and that, only the rope alone is seen — whether the eruv is valid; and this is because he saw in Tosafos Eruvin and the Ra'osh there, in the section of ten of Rav — we require that the people of the mavo see tzurat hapetach, and in this case the middle ones do not see the entire tzurat hapetach. And this is what I answered: behold here there is no room for concern, for certainly it suffices that they see the tzurat hapetach above, and since they do not see the place — everyone knows there is a lechi from here and a lechi from here; and what he brought as proof from responsum of Panim Me'irot siman 144 and Sefer Mikveh HaMayim that tzurat hapetach does not help if they stand inside the house — behold this is not because it is not visible but the reason that a house is as one filled with water as is written there in Mikveh HaMayim explicitly; and what he questioned from the Talmud I settled this already in my novellae previously; and also even if the reason were because it is not visible — that is there where it is not visible at all from outside; but here since at least it is visible to some people it is no worse than visible from inside and equal from outside or conversely — it is judged as lechi and is not similar to a crooked mavo where tzurat hapetach is not seen at all. Further behold we do not make any correction in crookedness and rely that our mavo'ot have the law of courtyards as the Magen Avraham wrote siman 363 — if so the same here, there is no room for concern at all; and what he asked in the case where one lechi does not stand at the wall but near the windowsill — behold if the windowsill is not ten high it appears it does not help, for behold the main reason of the Tur is that it is a partition through which goats penetrate, and if so in the end goats can leap through there and the partition is nullified; therefore it appears fit to place it on the windowsill at the wall — and with this there is no room for concern at all, so I answered. Further I was asked regarding the law of tzurat hapetach that they erected on Shabbat a reed with ropes — whether it is permitted to carry even though they did not rely on it on erev Shabbat; and I answered: behold in my opinion the matter is simple and clear that it is permitted to carry and we do not require that they relied — only for lechi where there is no new recognition now and new making, and behold it is as it was yesterday, therefore we require that they relied on it on erev Shabbat; but for tzurat hapetach which is recognizable that it is an opening — not to mention if they erected the lechi and ropes on it now, certainly it is permitted; and what the Taz wrote at the end of this siman — there it is different; but where they made it anew on Shabbat — in this certainly we do not require that they relied on it on erev Shabbat; and it is correct in my humble opinion even though they wrote that the Peri Megadim is uncertain in this — in my opinion there is no room for concern and no place for doubt at all, and it is clear in my humble opinion since it was made like a true opening we do not require that they relied on it on erev Shabbat, examine well. Further I was asked by the rabbinical judge R' Yosef Kastinbrahn of the holy community of Kalmya recently — they came and corrected the city's eruvin in this manner: they make at both ends of the rope a wooden ring tied at each end and each ring is placed around the head of the lechi and the head of the lechi protrudes above those rings and the protrusion is visible to all, and the head of each lechi is grooved around it toward the outside from the rope so the ring can rest there and not fall down, end of the questioner's words. And I answered: he spoke well, for this is tzurat hapetach made from the side — why in his case, for the groove is outside and on the side of the rope there is no groove — here the entire rope and ring stand at the side of the lechi and not on it; only also even if the groove were on the side of the rope or grooved all around it — since the rope and ring are not on the lechi but on the ring and the ring is placed on the lechi — if so in the end the rope begins only from the side of the lechi and if so it is tzurat hapetach made from the side — what difference whether this side faces the street or this side faces the lechi opposite it; and between the lechayim also it does not help if it is at its side and not on it — therefore this is not a correction but a defect, and whoever adds detracts; and the essence is as the custom of the early ones and our custom, so I answered, examine well:
