(א) A lechi that if wind blows, etc. — and the same when making tzurat hapetach, for it is not better than a partition that cannot stand in an ordinary wind, which is not a partition [Tosafot Shabbos and Shach]; and even if the wind does not knock it to the ground but only shakes it, as written siman 362 seif 1:
(ב) Is not considered a lechi — it appears to me that if the lechi below does not shake, such as if fixed with a nail or tied well to the wall, and for this reason it does not shake until along a height of ten tefachim, and only above it shakes by wind because of its great height — it is valid, for from what remains above there is still lechi measure:
