35) [Se'if 5] A lechi which, if the wind blows upon it, etc. The reason is that any partition unable to withstand an ordinary wind is not a partition, and the same applies to a lechi. Beis Yosef; Olas Shabbos, subsection 5. Even if the wind does not knock it down but only moves it, it is invalid, as stated at the beginning of siman 362. Magen Avraham, subsection 4. This means that it is invalid if the blowing wind nullifies the partition; otherwise, it may be valid. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 9; Eshel Avraham, subsection 4.
36) There. A lechi which, if the wind blows upon it, etc. The same applies to a tzuras hapesach. Tosefes Shabbos, there. See there regarding his challenge to Taz, subsection 4, who distinguishes between them. See above, siman 362, subsection 112.
