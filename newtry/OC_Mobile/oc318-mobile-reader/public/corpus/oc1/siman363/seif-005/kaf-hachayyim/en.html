Until one makes some form of rectification, etc. A tzuras hapesach is effective under Torah law to make it a complete private domain, as stated by Tosafos on Eruvin 6a, beginning with the words 'Rav,' and there on 22a, beginning with the words 'But did Rabbi Yochanan not say,' and by Rosh in chapter 1 of Sukkah, siman 34. A korah, however, is effective only by Rabbinic law, as stated by Tosafos on Eruvin 86a, beginning with the word 'Korah.' See also Tosafos there on 5b, beginning with the words 'Whose breach,' who write that it is a tradition from Sinai that the width of a lechi and korah is effective, while a courtyard requires a four-handbreadth strip. See the gloss of Chok Nosson there, who questions this, and Maharsha there regarding his explanation. See also Rosh there, chapter 8, siman 7, and Ritva on folio 86, who write that a korah four handbreadths wide constitutes a partition under Torah law, while one narrower than four handbreadths is effective only by Rabbinic law as a visual marker.
