(There in seif 40) A mavo whose one side is long and one short. Behold I was asked regarding a fence enclosed on four sides breached at a corner like the shape of a gimmel on this side and on that side in a manner that if the breach is measured equally — part of this side and part of that side would be more than ten — but if measured diagonally the breach would be only ten or less — how do we measure this, whether its law is as a breach more than ten or less; also whether we measure the diagonal or equally opposite the short side to the wall opposite it so it is less than the length of the diagonal; and this is what I answered: behold in my opinion the matter is clear that we measure the diagonal and it is proved from the Talmud Eruvin daf 8 from which is the source of seif 40 here, and thus it states: Rav Kahana said, etc., a mavo whose one side is long and one short — less than four amot he places the korah diagonally; four amot — he does not place the korah except opposite the short, etc.; Rav Kahana said since you heard the teaching of Kahana I will say a matter: that which you said he places the korah diagonally — we only said when there is not more than ten in its diagonal; but if there is more than ten in its diagonal — he does not place except opposite the short, etc.; and Rashi explained but if there is more than ten in its diagonal — it turns out its opening is wide more than ten for behold all beneath the korah is opening, see there — if so it is proved from this that with diagonal we measure; and even if equally opposite the short there would be only ten we measure specifically in the diagonal; and if so it is proved that with diagonal we measure and even if equally opposite the short there would be only ten; and one cannot say it is different there for it deals only regarding using beneath the korah — this is not so, for behold Taz wrote here that also regarding using within the mavo it is forbidden if it is more than ten in the diagonal — it is proved that also regarding using within the mavo we measure in the diagonal and if it is more than ten the korah does not help — if so it is proved that also regarding using within the mavo we measure in the diagonal; thus it appeared at first glance; however when we examine this it is not so, for this deals entirely when he wishes to place the korah diagonally — since he wishes to use diagonally and make the opening be diagonally the law of opening applies to it — then we measure the measure according to how it is in the diagonal since he wishes to use now the place of the long side and places the korah thus since it equals an opening diagonally — here we measure according to how it is wide in the diagonal; but if he wishes to place the korah opposite the short equally — we measure in truth the opening according to its width equally; if so regarding courtyard it appears since we hold its breach is in ten amot and in less than ten amot no correction is needed at all — it appears the same here; and as the law in mavo through korah — so the law in courtyard by itself to use opposite the long is forbidden if it is more than ten in the diagonal; but opposite the short it is permitted to use even though it is more in the diagonal since equally there are not ten amot — thus it appears to me correct and clear for practical law:
