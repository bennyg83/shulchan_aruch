(In Shulchan Aruch seif 26) The common practice etc. — and see in Bekhor Shor daf 113 that those reeds of tzurat hapetach must not be distant from the wall three [tefachim]. And also one should not raise the reeds from the ground three [tefachim]; and likewise one may permit not to place the reeds behind the wall when the reeds are not visible from inside within the mavo or opposite the airspace of the mavo as Shulchan Aruch wrote regarding lechi in seif 9 — see there; and see in responsum of Beit Efraim siman 28 explained at length that the words of Eshel Avraham on this are clarified like a new garment — see there, in the name of Sefer Mekor Chayim that specifically when the two reeds are distant; but when one side is adjacent to the house it is valid — and there I wrote that tzurat hapetach from a reed protruding from the projection of the wall like lechi — Tosafos Shabbos wrote to prove that it must be a width of a tefach like for lechi, and it is not required — see there; and also in seif 26 everything is explained well — and see what Peri Megadim Orach Chayim part 1 siman 28 wrote there:
