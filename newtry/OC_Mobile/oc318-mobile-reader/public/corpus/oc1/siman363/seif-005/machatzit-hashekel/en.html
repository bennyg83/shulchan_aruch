(א) (s.k. 5) Distant, etc. — since standing exceeds breach — meaning on the breach from one side which is only three tefachim and standing is three tefachim and mashehu — if so the air from the other side alone cannot nullify it:

(ב) But, etc. — see seif 34 and see there Magen Avraham s.k. 37 — they are Tosafos' words in Eruvin daf 10b s.v. and one makes a pas, etc. — behold even though standing is three amot and the breach is only two amot — nevertheless they wrote that besides the pas another lechi is required since the pas is distant from the wall; it is clear not like Shulchan Aruch; and the reason must be that although lechi is because of partition — nevertheless some recognition is required, as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 10; and if it is distant from the wall there is no recognition at all — somewhat like a korah not placed on the wall, for there is no recognition; although one should not compare them — nevertheless some similarity may be said:
