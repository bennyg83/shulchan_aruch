Seif 11: Such as if there was another lechi. I am in doubt if there was a partition and it fell on Shabbat and more than a tefach remained — whether we say it helps since that part was within the partition they relied on — it is called they relied on it; for on the entire partition they relied to permit carrying. And that part is included. And it appears reasonable that specifically they relied on it as lechi is required; for we hold lechi is for partition and partition standing by itself helps. And it implies also without relied such as if there was another partition and it fell on Shabbat and they now come to rely on this partition. And perforce this that with lechi it does not help requires recognition that we consider it a partition by relying on it initially. And if so relied as lechi is required. But from the sugya in Eruvin 93b it is difficult. And if there was a small courtyard breached to a large one — and the question: if so also the large to the thirteen — why was it dealing with breach on Shabbat; if so they did not rely from yesterday on the permission of the projections; perforce it is proven it is called relied since the projections were within the partition they relied on; unless we say pasin are preferable to lechi and do not require relied on it.
