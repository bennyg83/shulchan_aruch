(א) Inclines outside the mavo, etc. — for all these matters disqualify a korah:
(ב) We view whatever if one removed, etc. — and the reason is that we say lavud:
(ג) And if not — it is invalid; meaning specifically in this manner, where part of the bent korah is outside the qualifying measure of the mavo; but if it is entirely within the mavo as required, even if the bend is much more than three tefachim, it is valid, for we say we view it as if it were straight [thus it is evident from Rambam's conclusion in chapter 17 law 26 see there, and likewise from other Rishonim; however in Sefer Gaon Yaakov and in Chiddushei Even HaEzer they question this, see there]:
