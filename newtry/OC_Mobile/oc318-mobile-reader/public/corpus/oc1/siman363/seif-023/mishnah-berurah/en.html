(א) If it was, etc. — here he comes to add a leniency on the previous seif, that even if the two korot that cannot each receive a brick were not standing level but one was higher than its fellow up to three tefachim, it is also valid, for we say we view, etc.:

(ב) And the lower one, etc. — meaning, or the lower one [Rashi]:

(ג) Provided, etc. — twenty; for if it is above twenty, then even if the lower one is adjacent to it within three tefachim, we do not say as if it were below twenty, since now it stands in an invalid place; and likewise if the lower one is below ten and the upper is adjacent within three tefachim, we do not say we view the lower as if standing level with the upper, since now it has no korah qualification:

(ד) With intent — meaning aligned; and this is that even if we consider that this one already descended somewhat and the lower one ascended somewhat until they became opposite one another level, they would then be distant only less than three tefachim, for then lavud applies, as likewise in the previous seif in the first view:

(ה) And some disqualify — because they hold we do not say we view the upper, etc.; and although below in the laws of sukkah in siman 631 the Mechaber ruled plainly in seif 5 that if the s'chach is mixed and one above and one below, if there are not three tefachim between them we say lavud — here it is different, for we require that they be fit to receive a brick, and with one above and one below they are not fit to receive a brick [Or Zarua]; and this is that even if they are one tefach higher than one another it also does not help; and even if standing exactly level, this view holds like the second view above, that we require specifically that they be adjacent to one another, not more than one tefach; and Avraham Rabba wrote one should be stringent like this view:
