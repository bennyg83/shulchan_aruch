(ב) And it is possible that not all, etc. — Eshel Avraham questioned in what was Magen Avraham uncertain and also why proof is needed from Rambam's words — behold Mechaber wrote explicitly thus in seif 32: if he placed the lechi or korah in the middle of the mavo — that half of the mavo from the lechi and korah inward is permitted, and specifically when in that half of the mavo there are mavo conditions and one of them is that its length be four amot; and if not, since the length of half the mavo is not four amot — two pasin are required — see there; behold it is explicit thus; and Tosafot Shabbos explained: if from there one would say specifically there, since that inner half of the mavo is distant from reshus ha-rabbim — for there is interruption between it and reshus ha-rabbim by the outer half of the mavo — therefore they gave it courtyard law; unlike here where he placed the lechi and korah at the head of the mavo close to reshus ha-rabbim — they were stringent and require tzurat hapetach; this is the difference between courtyard and mavo, as written seif 27; and so Magen Avraham distinguished in seif 9 seif 27 — why in our mavo tzurat hapetach is required at its head and not in its bend; but in Rambam it is clear that even in such a case where he placed the lechi or korah at the head of the mavo adjacent to reshus ha-rabbim — less than four amot in two pasin suffices:
