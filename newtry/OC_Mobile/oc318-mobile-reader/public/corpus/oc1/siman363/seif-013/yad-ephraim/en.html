There s.k. 31 he ruled in nets — it appears at first glance to say that the mavo correction is recognition lest they carry from there to the place of the sandbar, and so too in Eliyah Rabbah; but below siman 365 seif 5 it is stated: one may not sit at the head of the mavo with an object in his hand, etc. — it appears there is no recognition lest they carry from reshut ha-rabbim to there; and that which we say korah is for recognition does not mean recognition lest they carry from the mavo to reshut ha-rabbim, etc., but recognition lest they come to carry in reshut ha-rabbim itself — see Rashi daf 3; and nevertheless there in siman 365 s.k. 11 Magen Avraham wrote it is possible tzurat hapetach is recognition regarding this concern lest they carry the object taken from his hand to reshut ha-yachid into the mavo; and if so here too tzurat hapetach would help, as Magen Avraham wrote from what Eimimar did — he made a complete partition; and one may say Eimimar could not make low tzurat hapetach because they needed to water camels through there, and similar to what Tosafos wrote on daf 105 regarding let him make tzurat hapetach at the mouth of the path — camels come, etc., see there; and therefore he ruled in nets that can easily be removed, as the Maggid wrote, see there; and high tzurat hapetach has no recognition as Magen Avraham wrote there s.k. 11 in siman 365; and according to this what Magen Avraham wrote and tzurat hapetach does not help — meaning high specifically; but low tzurat hapetach has recognition according to Magen Avraham himself whom I brought, and requires study:
