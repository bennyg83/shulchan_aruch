A lechi extending along the side of the mavo that one placed its end opposite the thickness of the wall so it is flush inside and visible outside — if the end of the lechi does not cover the entire thickness of the side, less than four amot — it is judged as a lechi and one may use up to its inner end; four amot — it is judged as a mavo and it is forbidden to use the entire mavo, for behold there is no lechi here:
