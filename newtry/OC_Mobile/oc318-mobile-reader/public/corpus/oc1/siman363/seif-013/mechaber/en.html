**13.** A lechi drawn along the wall of the alleyway with its edge placed against the wall's thickness — flush on the inside but visible from the outside, such that the lechi's edge does not cover the full thickness of the wall — if the alleyway is less than four cubits wide it is treated as a lechi, and one may use the wall with its inner edge. If four cubits or more, it is treated as part of the alleyway and it is forbidden to use the entire alleyway since there is effectively no lechi.
