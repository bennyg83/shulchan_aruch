(א) One who extends along its side, etc. — along the length of the mavo:
(ב) That the end of the lechi does not cover, etc. — rather it is shorter than it and extends beyond toward the rear of the mavo, as we illustrated above in seif 9 in Mishna Berurah:
(ג) Up to its inner end — meaning that through the lechi it is permitted to use the entire mavo up to the inner end of the lechi and no more; for although we hold in siman 365 seif 4 that between lechayim it is permitted to use, here it is different, for the other side is shorter than this, for there is no side opposite the lechi:
(ד) Four amot — it is judged as a mavo — for since it is four amot, which is generally the measure of mavo length, it leaves the category of lechi and is counted as a mavo wall; and likewise a mavo where one made along the length of the mavo inward many small lechayim arranged less than three tefachim from one another along four amot — the mavo is not permitted by this, for since they are less than three tefachim from one another, they are all considered as one lechi holding four amot in its width, and it leaves the category of lechi and is counted as a wall, and there is no lechi for this mavo. And nevertheless, if the first lechi standing at the end of the mavo does not stand flush with the end of the mavo but extends somewhat from the mavo inward, it is judged as lechi since it is visible from outside:
(ה) And it is forbidden to use — for this is forbidden even if it was placed for the sake of lechi, and it is not similar to what was stated above in seif 12, where they permit there when placed for lechi, for there he placed the projection across the width of the mavo and thereby its opening was closed, and therefore it is no worse than a lechi; unlike here, where he placed it along its length and it appears as adding further to the wall:
