(א) Seif 13 — that he placed. Meaning even if he placed for the sake of lechi — as Shulchan Aruch, and per Rashbag it should be as visible etc., and see Rashi there; and furthermore from what is written 'Shmuel — three' and 'lechi — four,' and Tosafos there 5b, 2 s.v. less etc.:

(ב) That its tip is not. Rashi:

(ג) And he uses etc. These are exactly the words of the Mechaber per the Gemara, and it is in Beit Yosef. And this requires further study, for we say there 'Shmuel — between etc.,' and we hold that between two lechayim is permitted; and already Bach wondered about this and explained Magen Avraham that here it deals when the walls are equal, and the Gemara deals when one side is long and the lechi extends opposite it — and these are the words of Ritva, and he said thus in his chiddushim: Rashi explained that the lechi etc., and the wording is difficult — why did they call it 'lechi extending with the side of the mavo,' and it appears that one wall of the mavo was long and this was short, and he added onto the short this lechi until it extends opposite the entire length of the long wall — and this is what we learn, that between two lechayim is forbidden, for if this lechi exceeds the wall opposite it — according to his words it is forbidden to use opposite it, for there is only one partition; and the wording 'lechi extending with the side of the mavo' means extending on this wall that was placed along the entire length of the other wall of the mavo opposite it that was long etc., see there. But Magen Avraham's words are strained and not settled, for Shulchan Aruch cites the Gemara's wording as it is, and also the plain meaning of the Gemara implies the walls are equal; and it would appear to me at first glance to say that the Mechaber explains that also the Gemara deals in this manner, except Rav Yosef holds the teaching as these — in a mavo whose one side is long, he places opposite the short and uses diagonally, as written in Yerushalmi there 'he places the korah diagonally and uses diagonally,' and as written in seif 30, and so Rashi explained; but for us, since we hold like Rava who follows his approach that concludes in Beit Yosef there that likewise lechi is forbidden — and so too in Rosh and Tur that they permitted only tzurat hapetach; except Rosh is not proof that he wrote according to Rava who holds lechi is also for recognition, as written 15a, 1; but we hold like Abbaye there, and Tur wrote because tzurat hapetach helps even in more than ten; but in the Gemara they said explicitly that even korah would be permitted if one holds korah is for partition — and so wrote Bach; and accordingly what Rif wrote is resolved: 'and if there is in the diagonal more than ten' — according to his words he does not place except opposite the short because of lechi; and resolves what R' Yehonatan and Rosh challenged on him — I could have numbered thus, but it is not so, for we say there that they dispute whether korah is for partition or recognition; but for lechi we hold it is for partition — indeed Beit Yosef follows his approach who holds that lechi on one long side does not help — and deals with seif 31 in Taz and Magen Avraham, as above. But still the sugya is not settled that Shmuel said between two lechayim is forbidden, as above. But it appears that even one lechi helps in this case and one uses diagonally — and this is what Shmuel said etc., and with this is resolved that Abbaye and Rava dispute between two lechayim, and Abbaye does not say 'from where do I know from this of Rav Yosef,' and he did not challenge Rava; but with this Rava is resolved who holds lechi is for recognition — it is not proven that he holds that also for lechi he does not place except etc.; unlike Abbaye; but we hold like Abbaye in this of one hundred thirty-five laws, as above, and therefore Rif and Rosh omitted stam Rama's gloss on this law, for the distinction between four amot or less is included in the general category of protruding lechi etc. However with this one may resolve the words of Shulchan Aruch in our time that are open to karmelit, and this is what Magen Avraham said, and see there seif 30, meaning that there it writes that lechi does not help on one long side etc. according to Beit Yosef. And siman 365 seif 4 means that there it writes 'and this is when open' etc.:
