And even if a person tied there, etc. — and it deals when they did not inform that they placed it for the sake of lechi; if so certainly he will go away and not stand there all day, therefore we require tying — Magen Avraham s.k. 3; and it implies that if they informed, tying is not required; and it is strained — if so why this trouble, better that he inform us; and it appears more that notification does not help here, lest he forget and go and tilt himself in a manner that it not be ten high — siman 362 seif 5 deals per the moment, for then we are not concerned for this. Tosafot Yeshanim opinion 7, and Eliyah Rabbah brought it opinion 3; however R' Zalman opinion 5 wrote that if they informed that they place it for the sake of lechi we are not concerned he will go away, except then they must place it from erev Shabbat, for behold lechi is judged as partition and it is forbidden to make a partition from a person on Shabbat except not with his knowledge, as written siman 362 seif 5, end of his words; and see there seif 3 and below seif 11:
