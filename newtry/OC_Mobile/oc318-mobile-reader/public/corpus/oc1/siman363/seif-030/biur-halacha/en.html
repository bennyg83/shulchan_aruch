(א) One side long and one side short even if there are not, etc., 4 amot — See Mishna Berurah what we wrote that the same for less than this measure; and proof from what they said in the Gemara in Rava's statement that in a diagonal there is no recognition — it implies even 2 and 3 amot; however from Yerushalmi chapter 1 halacha 16 it is stated there in Rav Kahana's statement that if the excess was less than 3 amot even if there was a diagonal more than 10 it is permitted — and therefore the reason is since the hollow of the mavo itself does not hold more than 10 amot we are not strict on the small diagonal added through the excess up to 3 amot and we consider it as standing equal; and if so at first glance one can say that even according to what we rule as Rava that korah is because of recognition and in a diagonal there is no recognition — it is possible specifically in the manner Rav Kahana speaks of excess of less than 4 amot; but in a diagonal coming from excess of less than 3 amot it is possible there is no diagonal concern at all and there is recognition; however from Rambam's conclusion where he was silent and wrote a mavo one side long and one side short — it appears in all cases he only places opposite the short; and nevertheless it appears clear that at least one should be strict only when visible to the eye that one side is longer than its fellow; but when not visible to the eye explicitly and only through measurement we know one side is long — we are not strict on this, for this reason does not apply what Rashi explained that people of reshut harabbim will come to err. And it further appears to say that even regarding two lechayim that Chayei Adam mentioned we require they be aligned specifically opposite each other and not in a diagonal — and as I copied in Mishna Berurah — even if we are strict that a small diagonal is also called diagonal, this is specifically when visible to the eye as a diagonal; but they were not so strict that they be aligned through measurement:

(ב) He only places the korah opposite the short — Machatzit HaShekel wrote: this is because even if he places it in a diagonal nevertheless he will not be permitted to carry even in the long place except opposite the short; but if he wants to place in a diagonal and not carry except opposite the short he is permitted, etc., see there; and see in Peri Megadim who disagrees and holds from the Gemara's language if he places opposite the long it does not help at all see there his reason, and his view is reasonable:
