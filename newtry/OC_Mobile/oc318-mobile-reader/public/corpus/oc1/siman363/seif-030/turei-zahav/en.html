Except opposite the short side. — meaning it is forbidden to place it diagonally and use the long side — Rava holds korah diagonally is not recognition; Rashi explained: those viewing the people of the mavo extend and use reshut ha-rabbim outside except opposite the short wall; or: permitted to use reshut ha-rabbim according to what is not like that part of the mavo — end of his words; and nevertheless the reason appears permitted diagonally within the mavo since there one will not err in permission in reshut ha-rabbim; and this is specifically when there is not more than ten in the diagonal for the Gemara said when more than ten all hold he does not place opposite the short but diagonally; but within ten we hold like Rava who said opposite the short; and accordingly within the mavo Rava also permits diagonally — this also within ten thus appeared initially; except Shulchan Aruch and Tur wrote but if he wishes he may make tzurat hapetach diagonally within the mavo — he may; it implies within the mavo equals tzurat hapetach and tzurat hapetach even in width more than ten; and Beit Yosef wrote on outside the mavo that tzurat hapetach helps and it appears initially likewise for lechayim for we hold lechi is for partition; and straightly; but two lechayim placed one opposite the other diagonally — permitted to use until opposite the lechayim diagonally provided there is not more than ten between the lechayim etc.; and it is possible that even though lechayim are for partition they do not help like tzurat hapetach etc. — end of his words; and Mahari Chalavah wrote plainly that when there is not more than ten lechayim help as explained shortly afterward; but if they made in the middle two pasin etc. — it teaches that similar to tzurat hapetach; and what Raavad wrote here that tzurat hapetach helps even in width ten — meaning to say even in width ten it helps unlike two lechayim; and not like Beit Yosef's conclusion that lechayim do not help — and it is an error regarding him for certainly they help even diagonally — end of his words; and in my humble opinion even doubt there is none as Beit Yosef thought; rather it is simple they do not help at all for whenever lechayim do not stand equally they are not this matter to each other; that one standing at the short side itself permits carrying in the mavo until opposite it straight and no more outward; and that lechi standing at the long side neither helps nor hurts for it does not permit opposite it for behold opposite it is only reshut ha-rabbim; if so it is only like an ordinary tree and what would the lechi opposite the short help him and it has no partnership with him and the short side does not need it; unlike korah placed diagonally from this to that — the partnership is visible to the eyes that one goes from this to that; unlike two lechayim where the one outside does not need its fellow and permits opposite itself well alone and it alone for partition — we view as if a partition spread equally opposite it and the partition has no connection with the second; and I would say regarding what is written shortly that two lechayim permit in the middle of the mavo — there they stand straight and each needs its fellow in order to divide domains and there specifically two are required as written shortly; unlike here diagonally and even within the mavo permitted here with korah diagonally — nevertheless if one made two pasin diagonally there is no partnership but that closer to outside itself permits opposite itself and the second is without need — all this appears clear and there is not even doubt for permission:
