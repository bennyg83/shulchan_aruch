(א) Meaning one tefach — meaning that together they should be one tefach wide:
(ב) And there are not three tefachim between them — we consider them as if adjacent to one another because of lavud:
(ג) And some say, etc. — they hold that lavud does not apply here to permit, for we require that the korah be fit to receive a brick in its width, and when they are far apart they cannot receive a brick upon them:
(ד) Within one tefach — for a brick in its width holds one tefach and a half, and there must be at least a quarter tefach leaning from it upon this korah and a quarter upon the other; if so, the gap between them will not remain except the measure of one tefach and no more:
