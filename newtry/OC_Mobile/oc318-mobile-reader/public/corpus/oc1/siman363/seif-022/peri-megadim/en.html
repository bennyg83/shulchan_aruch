If — Taz: behold he placed lechi in the middle of the mavo — namely in the middle of the wall and adjacent to the wall less than 3 tefachim as explained; and all that the outer ones also made lechi for the remaining mavo and they can make eruv together — then they forbid one another even though the outer ones have no treading of foot on the inner ones — for it is like a city of an individual that became of the many since they were accustomed to make eruv together; see Beit Yosef in the name of Ra'osh; and it appears to me even if they did so initially — each placed one lechi for itself and they were never accustomed to make eruv together — nevertheless since most mavo'ot are accustomed thus to make eruv together — all the mavo is likewise; and so too somewhat in Levush; and if the outer ones have no lechi — they do not forbid the inner ones since the outer ones are not fit to make eruv; and it appears even if the outer ones have treading of foot on the inner ones such a case where they did not make lechi — they do not forbid the inner ones; unlike when the residents divided with pasin or tzurat hapetach and the outer ones did not make eruv — if the outer ones have treading of foot they forbid the inner ones; and if they have no treading of foot they do not forbid; see Beit Yosef; and Tosafos Shabbos; and what the Mechaber wrote in 362 — and all this when there is qualification of mavo — namely he placed lechi in half the mavo and not outside; and nevertheless section 17 section 20 refers above — for they also made outer ones and made eruv — the mavo combines together whenever there are between them all two courtyards — permitted; but in tzurat hapetach two pasin — I am in doubt if in the outer part there are not two courtyards whether they are permitted — requires study:
