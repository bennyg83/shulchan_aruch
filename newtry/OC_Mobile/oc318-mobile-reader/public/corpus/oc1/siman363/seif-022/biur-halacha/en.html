(א) That they must be close, etc. — From the plain meaning of Shulchan Aruch's words it appears that even according to this view we also require that the two korot together between them have the measure of a tefach like the first view, and so is proven for one who examines Rabbenu Yehonatan see there, and also from Ritva's words there is no proof to the contrary — for what he wrote in the second answer and a finger on this and a finger on this, etc., means it suffices that this measure be placed on the korah, but the korot themselves each are half a tefach wide [however for the first answer brought in Ritva and this is the explanation Rashba took in his chiddushim and in Avodat HaKodesh it is stated that the two korot themselves need not have the measure of a tefach because the air between them joins to this; and Eliyah Rabbah understood to harmonize the view of all early authorities who stand on the second approach that we do not require the measure of a tefach in the two korot — but in truth this is only Rashba's view, but the view of the other early authorities is not proven they hold thus, and on the contrary from the plain meaning of their language it appears to the contrary that the measure of a tefach is always needed]; and Ritva's words are brought in Biur HaGra — if so Shulchan Aruch's intent is as plain, and as we wrote; and know that although the Mechaber wrote on the second view in the name of some — it is not a lone view, for such is the view of Tosafos and Rabbenu Yehonatan and Rashba and Ritva and Rashi and Hagahot Maimoniyot — they are stricter that even within a tefach it does not help, and specifically when they touch — see Bach:
