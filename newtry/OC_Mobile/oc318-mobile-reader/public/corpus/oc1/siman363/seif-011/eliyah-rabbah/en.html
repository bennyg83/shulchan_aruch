[13] As though they relied on it, etc. This implies that if they explicitly did not rely upon it, such as where they did not carry on the previous Shabbos by virtue of this lechi, it does not become a lechi, as stated by the Magen Avraham. It appears to me that this applies specifically where they did not carry at all on the previous Shabbos and there was no other lechi.
[14] [Levush] Because the breached portion exceeds, etc. If a wall is more than four handbreadths and there is uncertainty whether the standing part equals the breach or exceeds it, it is permitted if equal standing and breach would itself be valid. This is because a doubt in a rabbinic matter is lenient, and there are also two independent possibilities for leniency: perhaps the standing portion exceeds the breach, and if not, perhaps they are exactly equal. So writes Avodat HaKodesh, page 5.
