Seif 11: Such as if there was another lechi. I am in doubt if there was a partition and it fell on Shabbat and more than a tefach remained — whether we say it helps since that part was within the partition they relied on — it is called they relied on it; for on the entire partition they relied to permit carrying. And that part is included. And it appears reasonable that specifically they relied on it as lechi is required; for we hold lechi is for partition and partition standing by itself helps. And it implies also without relied such as if there was another partition and it fell on Shabbat and they now come to rely on this partition. And perforce this that with lechi it does not help requires recognition that we consider it a partition by relying on it initially. And if so relied as lechi is required. But from the sugya in Eruvin 93b it is difficult. And if there was a small courtyard breached to a large one — and the question: if so also the large to the thirteen — why was it dealing with breach on Shabbat; if so they did not rely from yesterday on the permission of the projections; perforce it is proven it is called relied since the projections were within the partition they relied on; unless we say pasin are preferable to lechi and do not require relied on it. If so one of these is proven — either in such a case it is called relied; or with pasin we do not require relied — requires study for the law:

Further it appears to me proof from the sugya there from what Abbaye said: I would say from R' Huna that lechi protruding etc. and R' Yosi to remove from the law of lechi; and if it were that these measures of spaced and metsumtsamot are from the essence of the law — if so for Abbaye who removes from lechi depends on mavo measure — if lechi is mavo measure it removes from lechi; if so since mavo measure is four spaced — likewise protruding lechi should not remove from lechi until it is four spaced; and behold what R' Huna said four requires another lechi — certainly four metsumtsamot as the transmitted rule that amot for stringency are metsumtsamot; and even if we strain since he said like mavo we know it is spaced which is mavo measure; nevertheless for R' Yosi who does not tie this to mavo measure — for mavo measure is four tefachim — until four it does not remove from lechi; if so automatically four is metsumtsamot; for it is in the category of what they said all measures for stringency are metsumtsamot; and this is not implied that Rabbah and R' Yosi explicitly disagree on R' Huna's explanation of four requires another lechi; for not for Abbaye is four spaced; and for R' Yosi four is metsumtsamot; perforce according to side one above that measures are in the manner of doubt and stringently; therefore also for Abbaye that four requires another lechi is metsumtsamot; for what mavo measure four spaced is only in the manner of doubt and stringency; and if so conversely for judging it as lechi that left the law of lechi — since it has mavo measure — we also go stringently that metsumtsamot is mavo measure and requires another lechi to permit it; and in truth that other lechi suffices in height nineteen metsumtsamot from the manner of necessity that if you judge amot spaced — automatically the first lechi of four metsumtsamot has law of lechi and the mavo is permitted with one of those lechayim — examine well:
