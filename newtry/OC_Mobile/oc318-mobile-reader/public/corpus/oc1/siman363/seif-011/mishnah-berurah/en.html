(א) That happened to be there by itself — such as if a projection came out from the mavo wall at its top in the measure of a length ten tefachim high, or it was placed there for some purpose, and even if they did not fix it — it itself has always been there — it is also valid, for lechi is because of partition, and a partition standing by itself is valid:

(ב) Such as if there was another lechi there, etc. — for it is as if he said on this I rely and not on this, and this one is as if rejected from the category of lechi [Or Zarua]:

(ג) As if they relied on it — meaning although they did not rely on it explicitly; however if they did not rely on it explicitly, such as if they did not carry on the past Shabbat through this lechi and there was no other lechi there, it does not help for the coming Shabbat until they rely on it explicitly from erev Shabbat:
