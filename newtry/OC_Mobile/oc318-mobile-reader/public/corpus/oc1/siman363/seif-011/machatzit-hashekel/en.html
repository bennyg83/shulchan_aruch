(s.k. 11) They placed, etc. — not like Rabbeinu Yosef Karo. Meaning what Rabbeinu Yosef Karo wrote in his long book; but in Shulchan Aruch on the contrary he wrote explicitly even if they relied on it, etc. — for in his long book he challenged the Tur who wrote in Shulchan Aruch's language "even if they relied on it, etc.," and wrote on this that it is not like Rashi and Tosafos who hold that when they relied on it, even if wide several amot — valid. And Bach wrote: although Rashi wrote, etc., "it follows from the law of lechi that we do not rely on it" even though if made for lechi it would be valid, for because he increased in its closing it is no worse than lechi of mashehu; here since it was not fixed for this purpose we cannot in thought remove it from the category of wall and make it lechi, etc. — end; it appears to Beit Yosef specifically in thought, but in speech when they relied on it one can remove and make it only what was not made by hand to fix for lechi — even in speech one cannot remove from wall category, even per Rashi's explanation at the beginning of his commentary, etc. — end Bach. If so Rabbeinu Yosef Karo who challenged the Tur who wrote "even if they relied on it" — perforce it appeared to him strained to explain that what the Tur wrote "even if they relied on it" means in thought, rather from his silence it is implied even in speech; and in speech Beit Yosef holds per his understanding of Rashi that it helps. And even so he cited here in Shulchan Aruch "even if they relied on it" — perforce Rabbeinu Yosef Karo retracted. Except it is not clear from what he retracted — whether he retracted for practical law and conceded to the Tur that even if they relied in speech it does not help and we require specifically placement by hand for lechi; or he retracted from his challenge and holds that to make it even if they relied one may explain in thought, and in speech the Tur concedes it helps, as Beit Yosef holds per Rashi per Rabbeinu Yosef Karo's view, and so is his intent here in Shulchan Aruch; even though this explanation is strained — nevertheless since it is not clear for practical law in Beit Yosef's words here in Shulchan Aruch how he holds — therefore Magen Avraham placed his words on Rema's words who wrote for practical law; and "if they placed" implies certainly he holds that placement by hand is required and even speech does not help:
