(117) That does not have three tefachim in its width — and commentators wrote that if its opening does not have width of three, no correction is required even if inside it is very wide; and likewise for the view that holds we require specifically width of four — it deals when there was width of four in all the walls, but where it narrows near its opening, no correction is required even if inside is very wide. And behold from the Mechaber it appears he holds primarily like the first view, that where it is three wide correction is required; and see Darkhei Moshe who disagrees, and his view is that we can be lenient like the other view, see there; and in my humble opinion it appears that even if we are stringent like the first view, that where it is three wide correction is required — this is specifically where inside it was at least four wide but narrows at its opening as explained; but where throughout it is not four wide, certainly there is room to be lenient:
