(א) (s.k. 28) Two reeds, etc. — as written siman 631 seif 3, in a sukkah that has two walls opposite each other one makes a wall four tefachim wide and mashehu and places it three tefachim less than mashehu near the wall and we say lavud; and there is qualification of sukkah that is seven tefachim — nevertheless some say tzurat hapetach requires placing a reed from the pas on the other wall; behold dedicated reeds for tzurat hapetach are not required; however it is more fit to emend in Magen Avraham siman 631 seif 2 — from there is the question — see there; and it is possible it is different there in sukkah where it is stringent, etc., for from Torah law two suffice as they are, and a third wall suffices with one tefach:
