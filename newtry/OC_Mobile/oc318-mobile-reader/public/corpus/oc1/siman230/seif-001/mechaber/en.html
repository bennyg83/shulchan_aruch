דין קצת ברכות פרטיות. ובו ה סעיפים:
The law of some private blessings. It contains 5 seifim: One who prays for something that already happened — for example, he entered a city and heard a cry in the city and said 'May it be Heaven's will that this cry not be in my house, or his wife was pregnant after forty days of pregnancy and he said 'May it be Heaven's will that my wife bear a male' — behold this is tefillat shav. Rather a person should pray for the future and give thanks for the past; for example, one who enters a walled city says 'May it be Your will, Heaven our Heaven, that You bring me into this city in peace.' When he enters in peace he says 'I give thanks before You, Heaven our Heaven, that You brought me into this city in peace.' When he wishes to leave he says 'May it be Your will, Heaven our Heaven, that You take me out from this city in peace.' When he leaves in peace he says 'I give thanks before You, Heaven my Heaven, that You took me out from this city in peace, and as You took me out in peace so may You lead me in peace' etc. until 'Blessed are You Heaven Who hears prayer.' And this is tefillat haderech — it and all its laws are written in siman 110.
