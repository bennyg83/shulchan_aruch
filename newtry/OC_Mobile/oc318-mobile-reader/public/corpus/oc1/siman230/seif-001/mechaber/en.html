דין קצת ברכות פרטיות. ובו ה סעיפים:
Dean some private greetings. And in it the sections: The one who prays for what has passed, such as one who entered a city and heard a loud voice in the city and said, "May it be that this voice may not be in my house, or that his wife was passing after M. a day for her transgression, and said, "May it be desired that my wife give birth, remember the 17th. You pray in vain, but a person prays for the future to come and gives confession of his past, for example, the one who enters the volume says, May it be your will, O God, that you bring me into this volume in peace. I enter in peace. I give thanks before you." O the Omnipresent our God, that you brought me into this volume in peace, ask to come out, saying, May it be your will, O God, that you bring me out of this volume into peace, I say, I thank you, O God, that you brought me out of this volume into peace, and just as you brought me out into peace, so will you bring me into peace, etc.
