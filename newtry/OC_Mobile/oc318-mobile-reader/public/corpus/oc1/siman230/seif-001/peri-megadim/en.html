And behold in what Taz wrote regarding one leaving a walled city — whether a guest lodging there or dwelling there — tefillat haderech changed that he should say tefillat haderech immediately upon leaving, unlike one dwelling in a small town leaving who need not pray for the walled city — then he waits for tefillat haderech until established on the road as siman 110 seif 7 and Magen Avraham 14; and it is difficult they did not mention this there. But it would seem tefillat haderech applies specifically to a guest who is not known and they will fabricate against him, unlike one dwelling there — if so why did he say one entering a walled city precedes entry? Bach — that now it is possible what they say 'established on the road' applies specifically to a townsman leaving — but a guest lodging in a city and leaving, even from a small town, can say tefillat haderech immediately since he was already established on the road from yesterday; and therefore they closed there in siman 110. And see Berachos 61 — and even in a walled city where they do not judge, dwelling there there is no danger; and it will be explained further in Magen Avraham note 3.
