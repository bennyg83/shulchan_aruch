(1) [Seif 1] And he heard a cry etc. — it is not fitting for a person to accustom himself to fear, as is said 'For the fear I feared came upon me' — and it says 'Tremble, O sinners in Zion' and did not say 'Fortunate is the man who always fears' except regarding words of Torah that he should fear and always beware of them lest he forget them. Riaz in Pesakim on tractate Berachos chapter HaRo'eh, Yad Ephraim note 1 — and it is from gemara Berachos 60a — see there.

(1) A person should not request something that is not done according to nature — for example if his wife miscarried at eight months that it live. And likewise it is forbidden to pray that Heaven perform for him a miracle changing the world — for example if he has a tree that it produce fruit before its time. Chayei Adam siman 694. And likewise in Kitzur Shulchan Aruch on tractate Shabbat 21b — see there.
