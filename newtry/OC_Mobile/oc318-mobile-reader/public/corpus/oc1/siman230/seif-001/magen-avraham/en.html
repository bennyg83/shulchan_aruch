To a walled city — for there bad governors are found who seek pretexts (Rashi).

And this is tefillat haderech — and if he already said tefillat haderech in the morning he says modah ani etc. until l'shalom; and if leaving in the morning from a small town he says yehi ratzon alone — and so Bach.

In siman 110 — it implies that even after leaving the walled city he should not say until established on the road — not like Olas Tamid.
