1) [Seif 1] And he heard the sound of a cry, etc. A person should not accustom himself to fear, as it is stated, 'For the thing I greatly feared came upon me,' and, 'Sinners in Tziyon were afraid.' The verse, 'Fortunate is the person who always fears,' refers only to words of Torah, meaning that he should always fear for them and take care not to forget them. Riaz in Piskei Halachos on the chapter HaRoeh in Berachos; Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 1. This is from Berachos 60a; see there.
1) A person should not request something contrary to nature, such as praying that his wife, who miscarried in the eighth month, should deliver a surviving child. Likewise, it is forbidden to pray that the Holy One, blessed is He, perform a miracle for him by changing the world's order, such as that his tree produce fruit before its proper time. Sefer Chassidim, siman 794. Kisei Shlomo on Shabbos 21b writes the same; see there.
