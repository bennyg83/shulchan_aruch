(Note 1) After etc. — for within forty days prayer is effective. And this is lest both man and woman emitted seed at once. But if the man emitted first certainly it will be male; and if the woman emitted first likewise female — Avnei Nezer. And it is gemara Berachos daf 60a; and he wrote in Sefer Avnei Nezer in the name of Sulat Belulah that even after forty days one can pray that the child be enduring seed engaged in Torah and good deeds.
