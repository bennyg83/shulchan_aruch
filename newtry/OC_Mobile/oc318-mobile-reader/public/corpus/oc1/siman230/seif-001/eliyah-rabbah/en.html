(1) [Levush] But within etc. — the explanation is lest both of them emitted seed at once; for if the man emitted first she bears a female and his prayer is not effective, but if she emitted first she bears a male without his prayer. He wrote in Sulat Belulah that even after forty days one can pray that this child be living seed, enduring, engaged in Torah and mitzvos and good deeds.
