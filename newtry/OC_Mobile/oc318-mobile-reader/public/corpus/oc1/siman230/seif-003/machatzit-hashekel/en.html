(Note 3) And this is tefillat haderech — and if he already said tefillat haderech in the morning — for example he was not in the walled city in the morning and said tefillat haderech, and in the middle of the day entered a walled city — when entering he says yehi ratzon etc. that You bring me in etc., and when entering in peace says modah ani etc.; when wishing to leave says yehi ratzon etc.; when leaving in peace says modah ani etc. that You took me out from this walled city in peace and no more — such is the implication of Magen Avraham in the name of Bach. For the extra 'as You took me out etc. lead me etc.' is tefillat haderech and he already said tefillat haderech in the morning. However Bach himself wrote he should also say 'as You took me out etc. so lead me etc.' until end of tefillat haderech only without signing it. And certainly Magen Avraham agrees that if he wishes to say tefillat haderech without closing he is permitted — only Magen Avraham holds there is no obligation for this prayer since he already said it in the morning, therefore he concluded etc.; and Bach likewise wrote and Bach did not write 'and as You took me out' — but regarding the text Magen Avraham did not write like Bach, and not like Sefer Avnei Nezer who wrote on Magen Avraham he was not precise — see there.
