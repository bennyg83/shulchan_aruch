One who enters a bathhouse etc. — now they are not accustomed to say so; and Mahari Veil gave the reason it was instituted only in their days when they heated fire from below in a pit and bathed in a tub above, as stated in chapter Eizehu Mekoman — Rav commanded his sons not to enter a new bathhouse until after twelve months lest it collapse from above; but nowadays the oven stands in a corner — there is no danger. And in Yerushalmi too R' Avahu distinguishes — in a bathhouse that is heated; but in a bathhouse that is not heated one says only for hot water damage; and Beit Yosef brings it. And now one need not concern himself with hot water damage since the eye sees and the heart understands — beware of hot water damage when standing exposed, until here.
