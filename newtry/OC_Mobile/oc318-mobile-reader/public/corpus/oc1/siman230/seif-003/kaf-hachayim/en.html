(3) And even after forty days one can pray that this fetus be living seed, enduring, engaged in Torah, mitzvos and good deeds — Avnei Nezer stated in the name of Sulat Belulah. And see Sefer HaGilgulim introduction 10 who wrote everything depends on the time of union in the thought of man and woman — from the man surrounding light is drawn and from the woman inner light; if both intended for good the child comes out completely righteous whether as inner light or surrounding light; if both intended for evil the child comes out wicked whether inner or surrounding light; if the father intended for good and the mother for evil then the surrounding will be good and inner evil, and in course of time the surrounding will overpower the inner and turn it to merit — see there. Therefore every man must also instruct his wife to have good thought at that hour — meaning both intend for a mitzvah matter so the child come out righteous in inner and surrounding light.
