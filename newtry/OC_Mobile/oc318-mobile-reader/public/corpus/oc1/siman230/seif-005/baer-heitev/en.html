To let blood. And the same applies for any medical treatment — one says this. (But in Rabbeinu Yerucham it implies specifically bloodletting which is dangerous lest he cut a vein and die Heaven forbid.) And it is obvious one needs shem u'malchus like all blessings. One who sneezes and his fellow says to him asuta — he should say to him 'May you be blessed' and afterward say 'For Your salvation I hope, Heaven' — for one who prays for his fellow is answered first. Yeshuos Yaakov Bava Kamma chapter 8 siman 64.
