(5) Blessing on the grain pile etc. — thus Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote, and I wonder — in the gemara Ta'anit daf 8 and chapter HaZahav daf 42 it states that blessing is sent on all our handiwork, and so Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Remazim, Semag, Abudraham, Tzeda Laderech, and Kol Bo; and perhaps according to us who do not practice tithes Tur holds one should not bless on all our handiwork, for Abudraham wrote the blessing is in the name of the verse in parashat Maaserot 'So that Heaven your Heaven will bless you in all your handiwork.'
