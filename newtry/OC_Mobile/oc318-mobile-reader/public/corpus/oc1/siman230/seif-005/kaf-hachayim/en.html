(5) And even though in the gemara we establish it per the first version — in a walled city where they do not judge and execute — but we say there per the latter version that it deals even in a walled city where they judge and execute, and the halachah is like the latter version; and all the more so regarding a blessing that does not contain mention of shem u'malchus and is only prayer and thanksgiving — and so Raavad in his hasagot on Ba'al HaMaor end of tractate Berachos, brought in Sefer Tamim De'im siman 200. Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Taz, Avnei Nezer note 2; and the reason they say stated in the gemara — sometimes no one who will plead merit for him happens to be present, therefore one must pray before the Omnipresent. And in Yerushalmi there it states even in Jewish dwellings. Avnei Nezer there; and in Chayei Adam general rule 64 note 4 he wrote the same law applies if there is concern of monetary loss one must pray.
