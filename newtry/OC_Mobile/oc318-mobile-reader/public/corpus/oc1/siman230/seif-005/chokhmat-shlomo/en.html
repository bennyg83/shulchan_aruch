Seif 5. A person should always accustom himself to say, “Whatever the Merciful One does is for the good.” Note: See my work on the Torah, Parashas Vayikra, year 5609, where I wrote that one should accustom himself to say, “This too is for the good,” rather than the wording of the Shulchan Aruch. See there, where I explained the reason and the practical difference between these two formulations. See there and examine carefully.
