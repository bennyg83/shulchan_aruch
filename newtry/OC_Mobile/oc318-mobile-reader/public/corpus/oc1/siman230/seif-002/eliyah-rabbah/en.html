A blessing in the grain pile, etc. This is the wording of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch. I am surprised, because in Taanit 8 and Bava Metzia 42 the Gemara says, "May You send blessing in all the work of our hands," and this is also the text of Rif, Rambam, Rosh, remazim, Semag, Abudarham, Tzeidah LaDerech, and Kol Bo. Perhaps the Tur holds that since we do not nowadays practice maaser in the same manner, one should not say "in all the work of our hands." Abudarham explains that the formula is based on the verse in the section of tithes, "so that Hashem your God will bless you in all the work of your hands."
