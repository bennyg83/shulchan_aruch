2) There: After forty days, etc. Within forty days, however, his prayer can help, because the fetus's formation is not complete until forty days. Levush. Prayer within forty days helps if both produced seed simultaneously. If the man produced seed first, she bears a female and his prayer is ineffective. If the woman produced seed first, she bears a male without his prayer. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1. This is from the Gemara there. The unqualified wording of the halachic authorities implies that throughout the first forty days he may pray, because perhaps they both produced seed simultaneously. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1.
