(Note 4) In siman 110 — it implies etc. until he is established etc. And in Sefer Avnei Nezer he disagrees — since modah ani etc. applies to him immediately when leaving the walled city even if he did not establish himself on the road; if so he should also say tefillat haderech then. And in my opinion it follows what is written in siman 110 — if he said tefillat haderech before establishing himself on the road, in any case he fulfills b'dieved — until here. And it is possible according to what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 110 that tefillat haderech one should not say within the passageway of the city which is like within the city — if so modah ani etc. also one should not say until exiting outside the passageway of the city since he has not yet left the city's danger — if so the time for both is equal.

And he says modah ani etc. and tefillat haderech after exiting outside the passageway of the city.
