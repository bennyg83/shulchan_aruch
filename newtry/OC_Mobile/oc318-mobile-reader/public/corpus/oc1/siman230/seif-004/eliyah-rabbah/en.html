(4) [Levush] And all prayer etc. — meaning when he dwells in a walled city and leaves from there at the beginning of his journey, or lodged there — then he says this prayer with the closing, since he is also obligated in its prayer included in this; but if he dwells in a city that is not a walled city and there is no fear in leaving, only that he said tefillat haderech as required — on a day when he walks through a walled city and does not lodge there, then he says yehi ratzon etc. and modah ani etc. until u'orvim baderech without a closing, as explained in Bach and Taz; and so wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham and Abudraham and poskim explicitly, and not like Magen Avraham who wrote in the name of Bach that one says until l'shalom — he was not precise. Also what he wrote there further he was not precise and one need not extend. Also what Magen Avraham wrote that even after leaving the walled city he should not say until he is established on the road — not like Olas Tamid, until here — is incorrect, for the prayer of leaving the walled city one says immediately when leaving the city as I explained in siman 110 note 16; and since he is obligated in this he concludes incidentally with the blessing and also fulfills tefillat haderech, for b'dieved he fulfills according to all, as I explained above and as implied in Bach for one who examines.
