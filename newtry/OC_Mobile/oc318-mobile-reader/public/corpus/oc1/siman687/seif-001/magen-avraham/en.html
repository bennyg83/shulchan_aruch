It is written in Hagahos Maimoniyos: a convert who converted before sunrise is exempt from the nighttime reading, for its main mitzvah is by day; end quote. It appears to me that this is the explanation: although regarding Shema it is called night and he is obligated, as written in siman 58, nevertheless he is exempt from megillah, since its main mitzvah is by day. It implies that another person who did not read it at night reads it before sunrise. But this does not appear to me; he does not read after dawn has risen. Granted, Shema depends on the time of lying down, and there are people who sleep at that time. But here it depends on night, and once dawn has risen it is called day, as stated in the Gemara. All night. Although Shema is only until midnight, here we do not decree lest he come to negligence, because it is beloved to them; end quote of the Re'em in Sefer Mitzvos Gadol. According to what I wrote above, that its main mitzvah is by day, there is no difficulty at all.
