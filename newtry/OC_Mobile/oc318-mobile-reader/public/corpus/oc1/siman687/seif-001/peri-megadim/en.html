And know. What is written in Shulchan Aruch, from sunrise, is the mishnah in Megillah 20a, and in the Rambam, chapter 1 of Megillah. I did not find it in the Tur; in the Gemara they marked Tur and Shulchan Aruch, but I did not find it in the Tur, only in Shulchan Aruch. See Lechem Mishneh, chapter 1 of Hilchos Megillah, who found it difficult why the mishnah did not say, "this is the rule: anything valid all night," to include megillah. If we say that megillah may be read until sunrise, as Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos, it works well, for the mishnah speaks after the fact, since burning fats is initially until midnight. However, even without this, one cannot say that it includes megillah in "this is the rule," for why did it not explicitly teach megillah, since it spoke of it previously? Since it did not teach it, this apparently indicates, as Hagahos Maimoniyos says, that after the fact one reads the night reading until sunrise. For this reason too it did not say "this is the rule" to include Shema, where after the fact, if one erred or was prevented by circumstances, its time is until sunrise. And to say that this supports Rabbeinu Tam at the beginning of Berachos, that the halachah follows Rabbi Yehudah from plag haminchah, is not correct, for Pesach too there is not according to halachah, since the halachah follows Rabbi Elazar ben Azaryah, as Tosafos wrote; see there.
