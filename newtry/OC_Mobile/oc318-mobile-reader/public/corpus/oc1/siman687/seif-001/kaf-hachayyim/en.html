1) [Seif 1] A person is obligated to read the Megillah at night, etc. For R' Yehoshua ben Levi said: A person is obligated to read the Megillah at night and repeat it by day, as it is stated, 'My God, I call by day and You do not answer, and at night,' etc. Megillah 4a. Rashi explains that this commemorates the miracle, for during the days of their distress they cried out day and night:
2) Ibid. A person is obligated to read the Megillah at night, etc. Know that the primary reading of the Megillah is by day, as Tosafos wrote on 4a, and so too wrote Meiri in his rulings. Noda B'Yehuda wrote in a responsum, Orach Chaim, siman 41, that the nighttime reading is rabbinic, whereas the daytime reading is from Divrei Kabbalah, see there. Machazik Berachah, letter 1.
