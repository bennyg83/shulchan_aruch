1) [Seif 1] A person is obligated to read the Megillah at night, etc. For R' Yehoshua ben Levi said: A person is obligated to read the Megillah at night and repeat it by day, as it is stated, 'My God, I call by day and You do not answer, and at night,' etc. Megillah 4a.
