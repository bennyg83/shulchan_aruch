Section 1 in Shulchan Aruch: A person is obligated, etc. See Noda BiYehudah, siman 41, whose opinion is that the night reading is rabbinic and the day reading is from the words of tradition; see there. See Birkei Yosef, that if one forgot or was prevented by circumstances at night, the day reading is enough, and he need not repeat it again as compensation for the night reading; see there.
