Taz seif 2 — surely it is void to say so etc. The difficulty is: that which is void means surely for sake of human dignity, and if so it is an argument on the derashah, and the body of the pilpul is whether human dignity postpones Megillah — indeed it is simple that initially Taz speaks when it is possible to bury afterward, and he holds like Magen Avraham seif 6 that delay of burial more than the state's law is nullification of human dignity; and since the gemara stated the reason is because of the great [principle of] human dignity, we learn it is completely postponed (and what one can question on this proof — there regarding "and you shall not ignore" is as if no time — see what I wrote in the discourse just before to explain Magen Avraham's first proof; this is understood also from Taz's proof, and delve). And afterward he added to bring proof from no time, meaning the dogs will eat him — this is certainly void; if so, it also implies that even when there is time the law is so, since advancing burial by state law is called human dignity. And it is clear.
