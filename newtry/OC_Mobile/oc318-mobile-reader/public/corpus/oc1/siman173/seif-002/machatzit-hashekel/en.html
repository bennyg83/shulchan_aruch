Seif katan 2: From distraction, etc. From distraction regarding prayer, but here he must recite Bircas HaMazon.
See siman 158, where it is stated that one who washes his hands for fruit is considered arrogant. It must therefore be that we are not concerned about distraction for the blessing over fruit. If so, Bircas HaMazon may have the same law as other blessings.
