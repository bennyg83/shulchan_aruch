After. See Baer Heitev. See Peri Chadash, Yoreh Deah, siman 89, regarding Rashba’s statement that a vessel is not required into which to wash. Chacham Tzvi, siman 55, disagrees with him. Maharshal likewise understood Rashba as Peri Chadash did. Eliyah Rabbah challenges this and explains that Rashba means only that one need not wash from a vessel. See there. Regarding the gloss, which states that specifically water is required, see Toras Chatas, principle 77, and Minchas Yaakov there. Eliyah Rabbah writes to reconcile the Acharonim.
