However, between a cooked dish and cheese, washing is obligatory, etc. Here the Tur writes that after meat, cheese is permitted through cleansing and rinsing the mouth, and Netilas Yadayim. Some say that one must rinse his mouth with water or wine twice. Maharshal writes that the text specifically reads “twice,” based on precise manuscripts, but I do not know his source, for this is not found in any text here in Orach Chayim or in Yoreh Deah, siman 89. The Tur further records the law of two guests, which is fully explained in Yoreh Deah, siman 88. I wrote it here because the Tur states that they should place a pitcher between them as a distinguishing sign, or each should eat on his own tablecloth. This implies that if a pitcher separates them, they do not each need a separate tablecloth, but may eat together on one tablecloth, one meat and the other cheese. Nevertheless, Or Zarua is stringent.
