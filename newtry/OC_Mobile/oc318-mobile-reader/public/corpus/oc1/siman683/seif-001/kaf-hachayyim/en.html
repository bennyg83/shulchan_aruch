1) [Seif 1] Throughout all 8 days of Chanukah, one completes Hallel. The reason is that its lamps differ, for each day differs from the preceding day, and it is therefore like Sukkos, whose offerings differ from day to day. Another reason is that the miracle was renewed each day. Furthermore, we read the section of the Nesi'im, and on each day its Nasi differs from the Nasi of the preceding day. Accordingly, for each Nasi one must complete Hallel over his offerings, and it is not proper to institute a new Torah reading without the recitation of Hallel. Beit Yosef. Levush:
