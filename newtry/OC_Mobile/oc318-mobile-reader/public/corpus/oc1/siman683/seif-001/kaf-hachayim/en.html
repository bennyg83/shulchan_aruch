A) [Section 1] Every eight days of Hanukkah they sing the Hallel. And the reason is because we differ in our candles, every day is different from its predecessors, and it is like a holiday that is different in its sacrifices. And another flavor according to which the miracle will be renewed every day. And furthermore, since the presidents are called and every day his president has a robe from his friend's, so every president should finish the praise for his sacrifices and it is not appropriate to resume the call without saying the praise.
