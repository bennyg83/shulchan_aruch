We complete Hallel, etc. Pri Chadash wrote that Hallel should not be said in a house of mourning. But in Eliyah Rabbah, siman 131, he wrote that they do say it, and so wrote Machazik Berachah, paragraph 21, that it is obvious that on Chanukah they should say Hallel in a house of mourning. Magen Avraham, siman 135, wrote that on Chanukah it is better that each person read it at home; Baer Heitev brought this there, see there.
