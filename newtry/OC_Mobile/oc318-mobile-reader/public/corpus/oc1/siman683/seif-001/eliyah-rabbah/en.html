Tziduk Hadin, etc. See the end of siman 670 and siman 420, paragraph 2. Kol Bo wrote: we do not say A song, a psalm, etc., because these days are like a Yom Tov after we say Hallel on them; and there are places that say in its place A song, a psalm for the dedication, etc. Until here. Here in Prague the practice is to say A song, a psalm, etc. It seems to me that this compares it to Purim, as I wrote in siman 693, that Kol Bo wrote not to say it, and nevertheless the practice is to say it, relying on what Tosafos wrote. Nevertheless, one may say that Purim is different, for it contains the downfall of Amalek, but the Greek wars are not mentioned.
