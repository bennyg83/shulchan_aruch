(ב) But only a kezayit — if they eat it at the first meal he will not have for afikoman; and if he wishes to fulfill his obligation with it also in place of afikoman, he may not eat after it, as above in siman 478; and therefore he must act as is explained and continues.

(ג) He blesses on eating maror, etc. — meaning, after he blesses at first only HaMotzi on matzah that is not guarded, he blesses on maror; and the blessing on eating matzah he blesses at the end at the time when he eats the guarded kezayit. And although l'chatchila he must eat the matzah of obligation and afterward maror, because it is stated "on matzot and marorim" — first matzah and afterward maror — where it is not possible it is different. The Mechaber omitted the law of korech, and it is implied that he holds we do not require korech for him, since he has guarded matzah only a kezayit; and with matzah that is not guarded, korech is not considered korech, for it is not matzah at all.

(ט) And two whole matzot for night 2 — because of lechem mishneh; there are among the later authorities who wrote that in this one should not break a slice for afikoman on the second night until after HaMotzi, before the blessing on eating matzah, in order that he bless HaMotzi on whole lechem mishneh and on eating matzah bless on the slice, for the slice is lechem oni. Magen Avraham wrote: if it is possible for him to leave on night 1 a small slice of a kezayit, he should leave it and place it on night 2 between the two whole matzot, so that he will have lechem mishneh besides the slice on which he blesses for eating matzah, as explained in siman 473. And likewise conversely: if it is not enough for him on the first night in one matzah for all his household, he takes a slice from the second and leaves one matzah and a half for the second night; since it is a time of pressing need he can rely on the poskim who hold that on Pesach night it suffices l'chatchila with a slice, and he blesses HaMotzi and on eating matzah on the slice that is on top of the whole matzah, and from the whole matzah he makes korech and afikoman.
