(א) Maror. That is, one recites kiddush first and blesses HaMotzi on matzah that is not guarded (shmurah), and afterward on maror; see Taz and Yad Ephraim. A sick person who can eat only a kezayit of matzah, or one who has only a kezayit of matzah, should eat his meal without HaMotzi, and after his meal he blesses HaMotzi and al achilas matzah and eats a kezayit of matzah. But if he also has no wine, he must recite kiddush on the matzah first and afterward eat his meal. Magen Avraham.

(ב) And nevertheless, if he can leave a small slice he should leave it and place it between the two whole matzot. If one matzah is not enough for him on the first night, from one he takes a slice and on the second night he breaks on one and a half. Magen Avraham see there and see Chayei Adam.
