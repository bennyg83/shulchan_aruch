(א) Who's called. A.J. and I will be there recently.

(ב) He blesses al achilas matzah u'maror and eats. This is the language of Shulchan Aruch, and see Magen Avraham; and not like the Rif, who wrote to bless and eat maror after that guarded kezayit, and likewise korech, as the Rosh questioned him from what is stated, "ein maftirin achar matzah afikoman," so that the taste of matzah not be nullified, as stated in the gemara there and as stated in Shulchan Aruch below, "and he does not taste," etc.; and in Shaarei Torah; and Shulchan Aruch omitted korech because he holds like what the Ran said in the name of the Razah that he does not wrap at all, since it is only a remembrance where possible, etc.; and likewise the Rosh, to the view of Rabbeinu Yonah.

(ג) And when he finishes, etc. All is the language of Rambam and as the Rif wrote and as written in the gemara 40a regarding the dough of Eretz Yisrael, etc., and provided, etc., 49, etc., and there 119, 120 we may say the sufganin support him, and although they rejected it we do not rule like this rejection.

(ד) And he does not taste. Rambam, and so R' Yonah, and as above.

(ה) And one who does not have. As written in Tosafos in Berachos in the name of R' Menachem, and as above.

(ו) And the two whole matzot. Because of lechem mishneh, and per the Rosh that a slice on Pesach is to add on other Yom Tov and not to reduce, and where it is impossible we act as on other Yom Tov.
