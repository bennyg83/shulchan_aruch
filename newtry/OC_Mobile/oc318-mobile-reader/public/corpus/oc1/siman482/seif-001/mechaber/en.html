The law of one who does not have guarded matzah. It contains 1 seif: One who has only a single kezayit of guarded matzah first recites the blessing over eating maror and eats it. When he finishes his meal, which he ate with matzah that was not guarded, he recites the blessing over eating matzah, eats that kezayit, and tastes nothing afterward. {Rama: One who has only 3 matzot for the 2 nights should, on the first night, recite HaMotzi and the blessing over eating matzah, and make the korech, all from the broken matzah, leaving the 2 whole matzot for the second night. [Abudarham in the name of Ibn Yarchi]}
