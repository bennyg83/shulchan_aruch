(א) (s"k 1) On, etc. — explanation: immediately, etc.; thus in the Rif. But in the Rambam, whom the Mechaber copied in his language, the Maggid Mishneh was uncertain whether his view too is like the view of the Rif, or he holds that one eats maror first.

(ב) And it leans somewhat more to the view of the Maggid Mishneh that for the Rambam one eats maror first. The reason of the Rif can be said in order to connect the matzah and the meal to kiddush.

(ג) Regarding korech: if he has only one kezayit, as is clear from the Mechaber's language — how should he act? It is brought in Beit Yosef: two views. One says the korech should be from matzah that is not guarded; and one says that since korech is only a remembrance, and since he has no guarded matzah, he should not wrap at all, see there.
