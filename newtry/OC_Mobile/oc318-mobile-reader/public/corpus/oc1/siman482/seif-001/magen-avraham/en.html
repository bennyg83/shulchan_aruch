On eating maror. Meaning: immediately after he recited hamotzi over matzah that is not guarded. And a sick person who cannot eat more than a kezayis of matzah, or who has only a kezayis of matzah: he should eat his cooked food without hamotzi, and after his meal he should recite hamotzi and al achilas matzah and eat a kezayis; and see what I wrote in siman 461, se'if 4. But if he also has no wine, then he must recite Kiddush over the matzah first, and afterward eat his cooked food.
And the two whole ones for the second night. Nevertheless, if he can leave over a small broken piece, he should leave it over and place it between the two whole ones; see siman 475. And if one does not suffice for him on the first night, he should take a broken piece from the second and break on the second night over one and a half, for the Rif and the Rambam permit l'chatchila to break over one and a half; and so wrote Beis Yosef at the beginning of siman 475 in the name of the Rashba; and see siman 483.
