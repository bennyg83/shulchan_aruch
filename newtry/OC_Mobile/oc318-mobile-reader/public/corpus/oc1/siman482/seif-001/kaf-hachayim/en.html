(1) [Seif 1] One who does not have guarded matzah, etc., from grinding or from kneading, as written above siman 453 seif 4; and in our words Orach Chayyim 60 and Orach Chayyim 75 that b'dieved guarding from kneading suffices and one fulfills Yom Tov with it, see there. And so R' Zalman Orach Chayyim 1: one who does not have matzah guarded from kneading onward except one kezayit alone, etc.; if so, it is implied that if he has matzah guarded from kneading as much as he needs, then he should act in the order written in Shulchan Aruch above siman 475 — namely, that he bless on it HaMotzi and al achilas matzah, and make from it also korech, and afterward eat afikoman from it, as written siman 477 see there.
