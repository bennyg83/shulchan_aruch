1) [Se'if 1] One who lacks guarded matzah, etc. This means matzah guarded from the time of milling or from the time of kneading, as stated above in siman 453, se'if 4, and our words there in subsections 60 and 75. After the fact, guarding from the time of kneading suffices and one fulfills the obligation with it. See there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, likewise writes, 'One who has only a single olive-sized quantity of matzah guarded from the time of kneading onward,' and so forth. This implies that if one has enough matzah guarded from the time of kneading, one follows the order stated above in Shulchan Aruch, siman 475: one recites HaMotzi and Al Achilas Matzah over it, uses it for korech, and afterward eats it for the afikoman, as stated at the beginning of siman 477. See there.
