And when he finishes — Taz raised: all that he has guarded matzah from kneading, which for most poskim is excellent matzah, and he is stringent on himself like those poskim who hold from harvesting, and he has only a kezayit guarded from harvesting — he should act like the Rosh, that he eat that kezayit immediately and bless on it al achilas matzah and maror with a blessing; and korech he makes from matzah guarded from kneading, and likewise afikoman guarded from kneading. But if he lacks besides a kezayit guarded from harvesting only gentile doughs that are not guarded in kneading but only in baking guarded — for most poskim siman 454 — from this he eats a kezayit of matzah guarded in kneading at the end, like R' Hillel; and I have many difficulties in what the Rosh z"l wrote, Pesachim 120a. Again I saw that Peri Chadash and Kaf HaChayim stood on this, siman 476. And if he has only one guarded kezayit, seemingly he needs no three matzot on the seder plate but only two for lechem mishneh; nevertheless, so as not to change three matzot; and Eliyah Rabbah, and see Magen Avraham, will explain further.
