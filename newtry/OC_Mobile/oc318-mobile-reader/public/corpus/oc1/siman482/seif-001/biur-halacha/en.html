(א) Guarded matzah — see above siman 453 seif 4 that from the outset of the law it suffices with guarding from grinding, and under pressure from kneading; but there are those who are stringent that guarding is required from harvesting. Therefore, if he has no matzah guarded from harvesting except a kezayit, and his other matzot are guarded only from kneading, in this he should conduct as is commonly practiced — namely, that he bless HaMotzi on the kezayit guarded from harvesting, and also on eating matzah; and korech he makes from matzah guarded from kneading, and likewise afikoman also from this matzah. Thus is explained from the words of Taz, and likewise the view of the Gra z"l and Chayei Adam, and likewise is sided in Peri Megadim.

(ב) One who does not have two nights but only three matzot, etc. — see in Eliyah Rabbah who sides that possibly the poskim were precise when he has only three guarded matzot alone; but our matzot are all called guarded [as they are guarded from kneading], so he may take on the second night from other matzot. Peri Megadim wrote on this and a great question, for it seems matzat mitzvah with which one fulfills yom tov requires that at the time of making he say, "I am making for the sake of the mitzvah to fulfill with it yom tov," and b'dieved thought suffices; and our matzot, even though made by an adult with intellect and guarded from leavening, do not become fit to fulfill yom tov unless he says at first, "All that I do shall be for the sake of the mitzvah to fulfill my obligation," and requires study, end of his words. And therefore it appears to me that in this it is better that he leave one matzah from the three for the second night in order to fulfill with it the mitzvah of eating matzah, and also for afikoman; and because of lechem mishneh he combines another matzah that is not guarded.
