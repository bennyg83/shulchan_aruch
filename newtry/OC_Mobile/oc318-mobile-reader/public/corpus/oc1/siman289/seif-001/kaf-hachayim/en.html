(1) [seif 1] His table should be set and the bed nicely spread. Explanation: because their custom was to sit on the bed. Perishah paragraph 1; and Be'er Sheva daf 119 wrote that it deals with spread out from minchah time or sleeping on it on Shabbat; but on a weekday it is forbidden — rather one spreads a sheet on the bed and does not move the pillows, see there. Magen Avraham siman 302 wrote that if the bed stands in a room one passes through and there is disgrace, it is permitted to spread it because it is for Shabbat need — therefore. And ostensibly since it is possible with spreading a sheet there is not so much disgrace; nevertheless Rokeach siman 55 wrote they spread ten sheets so that if he needs he may sit on one of them, see there. Eliyahu Rabbah paragraph 1; and likewise Maharam Tirol paragraph 6 that whatever one does for honor of the day is permitted and is forbidden only if done for weekday use, see there.
