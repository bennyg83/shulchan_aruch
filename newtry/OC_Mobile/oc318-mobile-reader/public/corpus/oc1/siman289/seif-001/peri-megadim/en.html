It is considered wine. See Taz, who was troubled by the apparently unnecessary blessing. With respect to precedence, HaMotzi ordinarily comes before Borei Peri HaGafen, as explained in siman 211, seif 5. Nevertheless, the wine of Kiddush is of special importance and precedes the bread because Kiddush must be recited over it. When one wishes to avoid an unnecessary blessing, the order must be arranged so that the blessing required for Kiddush is not displaced. The Mechaber’s ruling concerns a person whose principal intention is to fulfill Kiddush and then begin the meal. If the bread is already covered, the wine is blessed first, both to avoid embarrassing the bread and because Kiddush establishes the meal. Beer or another beverage can serve only where the local beverage has the status of chamar medinah and wine is unavailable or inappropriate. Even then, the laws of precedence and the blessing over bread remain distinct. Taz’s question is therefore resolved by distinguishing the obligation of Kiddush from the ordinary rules governing which food or drink is blessed first. The blessing is not unnecessary, since it fulfills an independent obligation. See siman 273. See siman 302. See siman 597.
