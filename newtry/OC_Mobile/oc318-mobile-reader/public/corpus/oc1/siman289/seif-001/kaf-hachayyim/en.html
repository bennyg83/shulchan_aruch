1) [Seif 1] His table should be set and his bed attractively arranged. This means that their custom was to sit upon the bed. Perishah, paragraph 1. Be'er Sheva, folio 119, writes that this discusses a bed arranged before Shabbos or one on which he will sleep on Shabbos. Otherwise, arranging it is forbidden. Rather, he should spread a sheet over the bed without moving the cushions; see there. Magen Avraham, siman 302, writes that if it stands in the room where he lives and leaving it disordered is undignified, he may arrange it, because this is for a Shabbos need. At first glance, since he can spread a sheet over it, the indignity is not so great. Rokeach, siman 55, however, writes that ten beds may be arranged so that if he needs one, he can sit upon it; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 6, similarly writes that anything done in honor of the day is permitted, and only preparation for after Shabbos is forbidden; see there. See further below, siman 302, paragraph 23.
