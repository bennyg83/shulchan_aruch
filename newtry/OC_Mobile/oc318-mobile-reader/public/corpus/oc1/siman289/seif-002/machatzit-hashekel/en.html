(א) (s.k. 2) This is reward — meaning in siman 272 Beit Yosef: Beit Yosef disputes whether at night Kiddush one sanctifies on beer if it is chamar medinah; but here for Kiddush of the day it is unspecified that they sanctify on it as if there is no dispute. Regarding morning Kiddush they rely, etc., so there will be a change and recognition — for if he sanctifies on bread and blesses Hamotzi it is not recognizable that it is because of Kiddush, for in every meal one blesses Hamotzi; unlike when he blesses on beer before the meal it is recognizable that it is because of Kiddush, for it is not his way to drink before Hamotzi; unlike at night where even on bread there is recognition because at night one says the Kiddush blessing — therefore it is better to decide to sanctify on bread than to sanctify on beer. Even if he did not bless on bread — meaning for example he did not eat bread — for certainly if he ate bread he fulfills Kiddush in any case, whether with beer or bread, as Magen Avraham wrote end of siman 272:
