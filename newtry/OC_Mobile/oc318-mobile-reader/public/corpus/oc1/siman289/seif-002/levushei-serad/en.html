Taz s.k. 1. And here there is a blessing that is not needed — meaning that per the law it is fitting for the one who brings forth food to bless first; and if one need not bless on beer because bread exempts all kinds of beverages except wine, as explained in siman 174 seif 1 and seif 7 — if so, when one blesses on beer first he causes a blessing that is not needed. Granted in the Temple on wine — even though by law the one who brings forth food precedes, as explained in siman 211 seif 4 — nevertheless because of kiddush they precede the wine; but here where there is no such reason, why precede the beer?
