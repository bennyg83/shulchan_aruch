And he recites the blessing, etc. Kol Bo, siman 39, writes that the early authorities practiced reciting after this Kiddush, “Blessed is He Who sanctifies Shabbos,” without the Divine Name or kingship. Some begin before Kiddush with “The children of Israel shall observe Shabbos.” After Kiddush they begin aloud with “A chapter of Tehillim of David, Hashem is my Shepherd,” followed by “A chapter of Tehillim of David, the earth and its fullness belong to Hashem,” and “Bless Hashem, my soul.” Some begin from “He causes grass to grow,” and afterward break bread. It appears that one who wishes to follow this practice should recite these Tehillim after HaMotzi, because our practice is to wash before Kiddush and their recitation would constitute an interruption. Rokeach, siman 54, writes: After they have eaten to satisfaction, some sing zemiros and praise the Holy One, blessed be He. It is therefore proper to study a small amount of Torah before eating. One should make the daytime meal somewhat more abundant than the nighttime meal. See siman 271, seif 3.
