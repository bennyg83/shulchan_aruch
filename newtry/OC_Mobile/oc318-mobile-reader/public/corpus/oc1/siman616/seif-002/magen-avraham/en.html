We train him. Rabbeinu Yitzchak explained that all training of a minor applies only to the father; see there in Tosafos (Hagahos Maimoniyos). Nevertheless, it seems to me that she should not give them food, because it is like directly feeding him neveilos. It is not comparable to sukkah, where one may give him food outside the sukkah, because there the eating itself contains no prohibition; she gives him food, and what is it to her whether he eats in the sukkah or outside the sukkah? She is not obligated to bring him into the sukkah. This is unlike here, where the eating itself is forbidden, and it is like giving him neveilah. Rather, when he eats, one need not separate him; see siman 343. Furthermore, there it is a positive commandment; see siman 269. The reason we are not exacting nowadays to make a child in his twelfth year fast is because everyone studies and they are like sick people, and furthermore, ordinarily they are considered sick [Bach].
