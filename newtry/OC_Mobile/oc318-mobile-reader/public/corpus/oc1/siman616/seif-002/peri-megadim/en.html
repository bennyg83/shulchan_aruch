We feed. See the Taz. Perhaps his version was 'in the fourth,' which implies during the fourth hour, and he said this is not so, because he holds that 'two hours' means the end of two, namely at three we train him and give him during the fourth; if he was accustomed at three, meaning the end of three, which is at four, we give him at four, meaning the end of four, which is in the fifth. This is from Berachos and siman 70. All the more so according to Rashi's explanation that a minor is exempt from Kerias Shema even by training, because he is not usually awake at the time of Kerias Shema, as he sleeps in the morning, meaning through three hours; this is unlike prayer at four, when he is awake, as in siman 106. If so, presumably here too this is the explanation, and the Taz of blessed memory would agree that we train only by one hour, as stated. He reads 'at four,' meaning the end of four, which is the fifth. See Eliyah Rabbah, and according to what I wrote it is somewhat resolved.
