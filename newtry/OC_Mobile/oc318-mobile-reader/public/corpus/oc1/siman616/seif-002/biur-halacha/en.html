Regarding a youth who is thin, etc. See the Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote: unless it is known that he is healthy, etc. In Responsa Magen Avraham, siman 111, he inclines to rule primarily like the 'some say' opinion, because several great early authorities hold this way.
And wherever we train him, etc. See the Bi'ur HaGra, who brought that the Ran disagrees with this. But I found the words of the Tur also in the Ittur, Shibbolei HaLeket, and Meiri, and certainly the halachah follows them.
