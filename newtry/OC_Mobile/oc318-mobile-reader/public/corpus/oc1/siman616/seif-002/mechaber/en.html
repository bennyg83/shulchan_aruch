A minor {who is healthy}, a full nine years old or a full ten years old, we train to [fast for] hours. How so? If he was accustomed to eat at the second hour of the day, we feed him at the third; [if] he was accustomed to eat at the third, we feed him at the fourth — according to the strength of the child, we add to afflict him by [delaying the] hours {and the same applies to a healthy [minor] girl (Tur)}. At eleven, whether male or female, they fast and complete [the fast] by Rabbinic law, in order to train them in mitzvos. {Rama: And some say that they need not complete [the fast] Rabbinically at all (Ran; Or Zarua; Hagahos Maimoni in the name of Halachos Gedolos and the Rokeach; Ra'em); and one may rely on them regarding a boy who is thin and not strong enough to fast (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 155). And wherever we train him in [abstaining from] eating, we train him in [abstaining from] washing and anointing (Tur).} A girl of twelve years and one day, and a boy of thirteen years and one day, who have brought forth two [pubic] hairs, are like adults for all mitzvos, and they complete [the fast] by Torah [law]; but if they have not brought forth two hairs, they are still minors, and they complete only by Rabbinic law. {Rama: And even if he is delicate and thin, he must complete [the fast], for we are concerned lest the hairs have fallen out (Terumas HaDeshen, siman 155).} A minor who is less than nine [years old], one does not afflict him on Yom Kippur, lest he come to danger. {Rama: Even if he wishes to be stringent upon himself, one protests against him (Kol Bo).}
