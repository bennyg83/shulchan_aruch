And permitted — Taz Yoma 78b and Magen Avraham 7 explained thus; Avodat Yisrael; Taz explains washing a woman, etc., for child unable to take himself — nevertheless forbidden to rinse her hand in water and give bread — he takes himself lest one enjoy her hand-washing — siman 273. Permitted to feed by hand even child able to take — siman 272 Hagahah in Terumat HaDeshen brought Beit Yosef here; for those who forbid Magen Avraham there — vegetable leaf and nuts from Minchah upward permitted due to distress of soul — nevertheless even touching forbidden. Mishnah Berurah Hagahah: training minor includes washing — Tur; though in siman 271 those are rabbinic, still must train as elsewhere; all the more for us where all are Torah law like siman 271. Training minor in four fasts — Tosafot Yom Kippur 2:1 and Avodat Yisrael Hilchot Tishah BeAv and Taanit explains training only on Yom Kippur.
