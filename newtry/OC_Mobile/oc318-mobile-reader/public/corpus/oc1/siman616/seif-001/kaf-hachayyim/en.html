1) [Seif 1] Children are permitted in all these matters. Eating, drinking, washing, and anointing. Rashi, Yoma 78b. See below, sec. 3.
2) Ibid. Except for wearing shoes, etc. The Gemara there explains the reason: regarding this matter, which is not necessary for their growth, the Sages decreed; regarding those matters that are necessary for their growth, the Sages did not decree. Beit Yosef. If a minor puts them on by himself, regarding whether one must object, see above siman 343 and our words there, with the help of Heaven.
