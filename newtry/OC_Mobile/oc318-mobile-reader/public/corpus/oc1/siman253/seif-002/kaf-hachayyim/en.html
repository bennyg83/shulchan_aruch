This implies that if it was fueled with sesame refuse, one may place a dish upon it and leave it until night even if continued cooking improves it. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1, writes the same and explains that the decree is lest one stoke the coals. This concern applies only to material that produces coals, whereas sesame refuse does not produce coals. He adds, however, that some dispute this and forbid sesame refuse as well; see there.
