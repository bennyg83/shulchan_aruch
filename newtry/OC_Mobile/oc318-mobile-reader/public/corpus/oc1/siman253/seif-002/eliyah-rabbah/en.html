(ב) Raw, etc. — meaning then it is permitted to put it close to actual nightfall; but if put from before the day it is forbidden, for it already cooked somewhat before Shabbat and must be removed lest it cook — Magen Avraham. From Rambam in mishnah commentary it appears whatever did not cook half its cooking is called raw; but in his composition it appears whatever cooked somewhat, even less than a third, is forbidden, etc.
