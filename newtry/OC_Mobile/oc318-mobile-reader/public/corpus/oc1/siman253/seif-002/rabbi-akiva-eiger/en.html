Ibid. 'Or when it is raw.' From Tosafos, Shabbos 39b, s.v. 'From the incident,' one may learn that Rabbeinu Yonah maintains that it is permitted to leave raw water upon the fire. It is only with respect to insulating that it is forbidden, because one may not insulate a raw pot. According to Rabbeinu Shmuel, however, in Tosafos at the beginning of the chapter Bameh Tomnin, it is evident that water does not have the status of a raw pot. See below, siman 254 and siman 257, se'if 7.
