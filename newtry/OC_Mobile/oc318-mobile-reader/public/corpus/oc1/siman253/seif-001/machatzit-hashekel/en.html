Know there is a matter of leaving food and a matter of returning. "Leaving" is when one puts food from Erev Shabbat on a kirah not in the manner of insulating, leaving it standing on the kirah, and on Shabbat takes it from the kirah. "Returning" is when he left it from Erev Shabbat on a kirah and on Shabbat takes it from the kirah and wishes to return it again on the kirah — this is the essence of the law of returning; but there is another matter of returning as explained, etc.
