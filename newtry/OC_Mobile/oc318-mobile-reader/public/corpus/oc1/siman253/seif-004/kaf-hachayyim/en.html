4) There: And it continues to contract, etc. This means that its volume diminishes, and is related to the expression 'shriveled breasts.' Perishah, paragraph 1. See Responsa Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, klal 3, end of siman 4, who writes that a coffee dish is harmed by continued contraction; see there.
