(ס"ק ג) That he covered, etc. — only in dimming coals; Sefer Tosefet Shabbat wrote one certainly must dim all coals, for dimming is not better than sweeping which requires sweeping and removing all coals — if so one must dim all coals. What Magen Avraham wrote that dimming coals suffices means one need not put much ash until the cover is thick, but a light thin cover suffices.
