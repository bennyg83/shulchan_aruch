Old. A new pot is forbidden because the heat hardens it. Although the Beit Yosef rules leniently at the end of siman 502, that applies when food is inside it. When it is empty, he may concede that it is forbidden.
Over the opening of the stove. The stove is thereby regarded as swept or covered with ashes, because the pot seals its opening. Hagahos Mordechai and Maggid Mishneh, chapter 22. This also appears from siman 318, se'if 18. Accordingly, one may perhaps place a pot on top of our ovens even while there is a fire inside, since a layer of masonry separates the pot from the fire. This also appears from siman 318, se'if 15. Maharil writes: If a piece of wood or a board is lying upon the oven, one may place a pot upon it. The Agudah likewise cites the Tashbetz: If boards are installed over the oven, as they are in Rothenburg and many other places, one may place a pot upon the boards. This implies that some object must be placed beneath it as a recognizable distinction. The same appears at the end of this siman in the gloss. All this applies only when the food is hot.
