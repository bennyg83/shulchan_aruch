Magen Avraham, s.k. 3. 'And even if it later flared up.' See Tosafos and Rashba. In their opinion, only regarding food cooked completely does it have the status of a fire covered with ashes; if it is less cooked than this, it does not have that status. If the coals merely became glowing, which is less than having flared up, they have the status of coals covered with ashes for all purposes.
