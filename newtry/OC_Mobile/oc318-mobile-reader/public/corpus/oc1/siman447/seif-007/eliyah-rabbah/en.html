(7) There are those who are lenient, etc. Shach in Yoreh De'ah siman 108 seized on Rama's "there are those who are lenient" and Darkei Moshe wrote so in Mordechai perek Kol Sha'ah; it is not in Mordechai — only reicha does not apply to chametz bread baked with matzah; but other dishes all agree there is prohibition — end; so Olat Shabbat; and he wondered on Rama — Mordechai wrote this in the name of Rabbenu Tam and he holds chametz on Pesach has no prohibition in any amount — end; in my humble opinion Darkei Moshe did not mean Rabbenu Tam's practice at all but the case Mordechai brought earlier — a gentile threw chametz cakes on Pesach into an oven and they ruled permitted because we hold reicha is not significant; if so proven he holds chametz equals other prohibitions — otherwise reicha would be significant and nevertheless permitted because there is no reicha in bread; and so explicitly in Rokeach siman 461 — otherwise what is the question from hadas of mitzvah forbidden to smell gentile incense; and Rabbenu Tam's case the distinction between bread seems clear he follows his view that in every prohibition reicha is significant as he wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 97; therefore he needed a distinction of bread. For halachah Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah there in loss rely on lenient; Beit Chadash is stringent here; what She'erit Kenesset HaGedolah wondered on Beit Chadash I already answered in Eliyahu Zuta; nevertheless since in responsum of Maharil siman 214 he wrote chametz equals other prohibitions and I also proved from Mordechai and Rokeach — be lenient in loss and Yom Tov joy; and it implies in Yoreh De'ah even in a small sealed oven; but large or small open permitted even without loss — unlike Olat Shabbat; and I also proved in my book on Yoreh De'ah Rama's view — what is lenient in loss is when slightly open where smoke exits; but completely sealed forbidden even in loss — so Magen Avraham; and I also wrote there Rashal forbids in other prohibitions even in loss when oven small and completely sealed — perhaps Maharil agrees. Rama wrote in Yoreh De'ah: if the prohibition is sharp and all the more if the permitted is sharp — forbidden even in loss; and implies even in open large oven as Torat Chatat wrote; although Rashal and Beit Chadash challenged — Taz and Shach wrote it is reasonable to forbid; in my book on Yoreh De'ah I wrote they did not analyze Mordechai perek Gid HaNasheh on roasted with spices — forbidden for it is sharp — end; seemingly wondrous why he did not write this general principle earlier that reicha is not significant — rather clear his explanation refers to what he wrote above that in large oven permitted; he wrote on it that in sharp forbidden; clear proof from responsum of Maharam Rotenburg siman 25 as I elaborated in my book. Sharp means most of the dish is sharp as Yoreh De'ah siman 122 seif 3. Rama wrote: if one is covered even in mere dough it is permitted — from Torat Chatat and Mordechai; in my book I proved from Mordechai and other Poskim dough covering does not help except for b'dieved permitted l'chatchila with dough; but in sharp forbidden unless bowl covering, etc.; all in roasting; in cooking permitted unless insulated for Shabbat all night sealed — so Magen Avraham; but implies he forbids chametz even in great loss — possibly lenient in great loss and Yom Tov joy. Incident before my ancestors the Gaon — they sealed the oven with food with a board used all year without rinsing and permitted.
