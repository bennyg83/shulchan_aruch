Unripe grapes that were pounded before Pesach, etc. - Mishna Berurah holds that unripe grapes are not considered davar charif; but according to what we rule in Yoreh De'ah siman 96 that even sour fruits are included in davar charif - unripe grapes are also included; and therefore through charif and pressure, chametz from the mortar is absorbed in the unripe grapes, and there are not sixty in the unripe grapes against the entire mortar to nullify it - therefore even before Pesach one should be stringent. See Acharonim who wrote that in a case of pressing need one may rely on Poskim who hold sour fruits are not davar charif.
