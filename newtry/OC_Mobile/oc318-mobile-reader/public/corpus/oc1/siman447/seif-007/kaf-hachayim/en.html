(7) There. In any amount etc., and even any amount of chametz nokshah which is forbidden rabbinically. Peri Chadash. And so Taz subsection 19: if chametz nokshah fell into food it is forbidden to eat on Pesach but permitted to retain after Pesach — see there. However Magen Avraham subsection 5 wrote in the name of Lekutim of Maharil that chametz nokshah even during Pesach is nullified in sixty since there is no karet, and he brought what Piskei Maharai siman 486 wrote that it forbids in any amount, and he challenged him and concluded that one who is lenient like Maharil does not lose — see there. And so agreed Nahar Shalom sign 2, Erech HaShulchan sign 9, Makor Chayim sign 7, Razah sign 8, Chok Yaakov. Chayei Adam general rule 121 sign 13. Magen Alef. However fruit juice with water, although it is nokshah, forbids in any amount. Makor Chayim there. And see below sign 268.
