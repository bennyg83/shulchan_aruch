Unripe grapes crushed before Pesach — from this wording it is implied that during Pesach one may not be lenient; and the reason is because of what Semak concluded at the end of his words "and even if it discharges" etc. [as brought by Taz]; therefore according to this it is permitted only before Pesach, and Rama decided for practical law like the first part of his words that it does not discharge when cold, and therefore even during Pesach it is permitted [Mordechai]; however it is difficult, for Rama should have written "and some say" or "it appears to me," not stam; and Mateh Yehudah already challenged this and it remains in difficulty; and in my humble opinion if we hold for practical law like Eshel Avraham and Netziv that before Pesach it is permitted when crushed even if the mortar was not clean — it is simple the Mechaber deals when the vessel was not clean, and therefore they permit only before Pesach; and Rama's language fits very well for one who examines and understands.
