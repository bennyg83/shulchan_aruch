(ז) Permitted — Taz; although in Tosefta 127 he decided leniently in stam as letter 6 — nevertheless here and nearby he brought Shulchan Aruch: he should not have been silent here or written above; halacha is like first view. And what he wrote here there is no proof from Tosefta as Magen Avraham letter 51 in the name of Bedicha; and in Tosefta in siman 91 s.v. it implies per those who hold it returns and awakens — and it was certainly chametz, even taste returns and awakens — not like Magen Avraham 7; Eliyah Rabba will explain there. And in Tosefta it implies even bal yera'eh — a skewer permits — Mechaber; therefore no return-and-awaken, or as s.v. says; and if he roasted fatty chametz on a skewer — even b'dieved forbidden for us on Pesach; see Pri Chadash and Chok Yaakov 4 there; Eliyah Rabba there; and in Magen Avraham here Magen Avraham 17.
