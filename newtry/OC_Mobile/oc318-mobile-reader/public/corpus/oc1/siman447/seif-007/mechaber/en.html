Unripe grapes that were crushed before Pesach in leavened mortars — it is permitted to eat them on Pesach. {Rama: Because [the vessel] does not discharge when cold; and even if it was done on Pesach, it is not forbidden if the vessel was clean. But if he cut it with a chametz knife during Pesach, one should be stringent, for an ordinary knife is not clean, and there is concern for chametz stuck upon it (Maharil). But if that item became mixed into a cooked dish, one need not be concerned, nor stringent to forbid it out of doubt — so it appears to me.}
