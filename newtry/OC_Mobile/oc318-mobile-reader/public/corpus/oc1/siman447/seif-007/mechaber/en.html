Unripe produce that was crushed before Passover in leavened mortars is permitted to be eaten on Passover. Gloss: because it does not release when cold; and even if it was prepared during Passover, it is not forbidden if the vessel was clean. However, if it was cut during Passover with a knife used for leaven, one should be stringent, since an ordinary knife is not presumed clean and there is concern for leaven adhering to it (Maharil). But if that item became mixed into a cooked dish, one need not be concerned or be stringent to forbid it out of doubt; כך נראה לי (so it appears to me). ) :
