Magen Avraham subsection 5: as proven in Tosafot beginning of Pesachim — only there; there one can say for the first reason regarding concern that one will come to eat from it they were stringent because they do not separate from it alone, as Tosafot wrote that one who vows taste permits for others and there is no karet in a vow, but in truth from nokshah it is proven because they do not separate from it alone; but regarding the law that in any amount, necessarily they were stringent only in combination with karet, for what he wrote that from chametz b'eyna there is no proof, for it is considered somewhat separating since it is the time of destruction — this is insufficient, for Rav who holds like R' Yehuda, chametz for afterward is d'oraisa, and nevertheless he said that in min b'mino in sixty yes, but in min b'mino in any amount like all prohibitions, for min b'mino in any amount; and if so for us also in min b'mino it is nullified like Rav in not its kind, and if so it is proven that without karet, even without separating alone, it is not in any amount.
