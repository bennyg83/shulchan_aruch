There therefore he challenged Pesachim — see there; perhaps it is permitted, for from what he challenged Pesachim there is proof: if we explain the Gemara's words plainly that what it said they roasted in two pots means pot-roasting, if so why does he simply challenge Pesachim, for according to Tanna Kama of Rabbi on daf 41 pot-roasting is permitted; if so the challenger holds that here when it answers they roasted in two pots it means cooking, for otherwise there is still reason to forbid because smell exits from above; therefore he challenges Pesachim that cooking is certainly impossible on Pesach. However according to Tosafot daf 41 that even for Tanna Kama pot-roasting is invalid, and also there is a positive commandment that only fire-roasting applies, if so even if it deals with roasting in two pots he challenges Pesachim properly.
