In any amount etc. — one explanation: rather less than sixty is called any amount; or in any amount that improved the mixture and it is sold for more than before — if so behold the Mechaber wrote below siman 467 seif 10 regarding a cooked chicken in which a split wheat was found on Pesach that it is permitted to sell it, and here he wrote that in any amount it forbids in benefit; rather certainly as we said. Peri Chadash. And Maamar Mordechai there sign 16 wrote that there he relied on Rosh that wheat even if split is not full chametz, therefore for benefit he relied to be lenient — see there; and see further in our words there in detail.
