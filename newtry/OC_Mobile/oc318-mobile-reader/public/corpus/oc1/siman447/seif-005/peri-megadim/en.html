This is obvious, for chametz is no less than other prohibitions. Even in the fifth hour one can say so. Regarding something that will become permitted and a different type too, I wrote in my Pri Megadim to Yoreh De'ah that the piece becomes like neveilah, even though it is not comparable to meat and milk, where there is permission to its prohibition. However, with liquid in liquid without cooking and the like, where there is a dispute whether to combine, one should forbid, for there are those who say it reawakens and the like. This requires examination, because it seems to me that in rabbinic law we do not combine stringencies to create a double doubt toward prohibition. In Yoreh De'ah 110 we wrote that even regarding a double doubt toward stringency in rabbinic law, some say not to forbid, and certainly not to combine. It sounds that adding is permitted during the time of permission, but mixing actual substance in sixty is forbidden during the time of permission. See Bach here, se'if 6, that mixing is forbidden for eating, but to keep it is permitted. The reason is that some say it reawakens.
