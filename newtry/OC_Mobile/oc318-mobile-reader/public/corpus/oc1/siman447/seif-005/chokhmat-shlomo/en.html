In the gloss: "But with melted fat in a chametz utensil, it is forbidden by law if they were not careful about chametz from the time it was made, etc." Note: But if they were careful, though for another intention and not for the sake of Pesach, see Chok Yaakov in the name of Tzemach Tzedek. See my work on Yoreh De'ah from the year 5596, siman 23, to the holy community of Korets, where I wrote a proof to this from the Mishnah in the third chapter of Parah; see there and examine.
