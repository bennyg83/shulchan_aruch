(ו) And likewise anything etc. — know that there are Poskim who hold specifically regarding fat, that the pan in which they melt it is customarily used constantly, and likewise similar cases regarding pots used frequently — presumably they are ben yomo until you know they are not ben yomo; but in other vessels we say presumably they are not ben yomo [and this is Mahari"t's view, agreed by Kenesset HaGedolah and Peri Chadash]; nevertheless from Shulchan Aruch and Acharonim it is implied that because of chametz stringency we are stringent in every case. And regarding mixtures, Magen Avraham's view in subsection 26 is to be lenient in wine and vinegar except fat because crumbs do not usually mix into them, even if they became mixed in less than sixty; and Chok Yaakov's view in subsection 32 implies in all things one is not lenient except in great loss; and it appears one may rely on Magen Avraham's view since Mahari"t and his supporters are lenient in stam vessels even without mixtures; and likewise in fat one may rely on Chok Yaakov to be lenient in great loss, for several Acharonim copied his words for halachah [Gra and Magen Alef and Makor Chayim]; and also implied in Gra's explanation there that the essential law is stringency — see there.
