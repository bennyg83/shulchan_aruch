Dry meat, etc. And in a wooden vessel — even though they salted in a chametz vessel, even ben yomo, it is permitted for we hold salting does not apply to wood. Salted meat and fish before Pesach — do not treat its prohibition lightly, meaning do not say stam vessels are not ben yomo and therefore when it is known the vessel is not ben yomo it is permitted even per Rosh; but from stam we are concerned the vessel was ben yomo — and so we practice ben yomo; and so Maharshal: we received from our ancestors not to soak salted meat — end of his words; however some practice leniency as written below siman 491 regarding dry cheese — some practice prohibition lest crumbs mixed in. And if already cooked those things — do not cast them to loss if impossible to keep until after Pesach; and all the more they do not forbid their mixture even in less than sixty, for it is only a rabbinic doubt of a minute amount; and if cut in a chametz knife Magen Avraham forbids even b'dieved — see there:
