(א) And revives. And it appears [this refers] to the wheat above in seif 3 — that if they heated while the wheat was still inside, for since the substance of the wheat is visible there on Pesach. Peri Chadash wrote: an incident occurred where one loaf of dough was found inside the basket in which they placed matzot as they came out of the oven; they emptied the basket and it was not known which matzah the dough touched — I ruled that one in two nullifies since the mixture was before Pesach, and it is permitted to heat the matzot on Pesach according to all, since there is only flavor; however, all matzot known to have touched the dough require peeling [see there]. And similarly in Sefer Ahavat Chesed siman 99:

(ב) Moist in moist. It appears to me that if moist became mixed in moist in less than sixty before Pesach, one may increase upon it a hundredfold until there are sixty so that it will be permitted on Pesach, and this is not [called] nullifying a prohibition l'chatchila — Taz; and see Beit Chadash siman 453 and in Sha'ar HaTziyun there. Flour in flour is called moist in moist, and if nullified a hundredfold it is permitted to bake matzot from it even on Pesach. Terumat HaDeshen, Beit Chadash:

(ג) Forbidden on Pesach. That is, to drink it; but it is permitted to benefit from it, and certainly it is permitted to keep it. And everything that is permitted to keep is permitted to benefit from — see Chok Yaakov. And if one took from that wine after removing the bread from it and mixed it with other wine, the entire mixture is forbidden — [so rule] the Rashba and the Magen Avraham. And specifically, this applies when it was not strained before Pesach; but if it was strained, according to all opinions it is permitted — Magen Avraham and Chok Yaakov; see there. And the Taz wrote that straining does not help, for perhaps [particles] exit through the holes of the strainer; but if one strained through a thick cloth, it appears to me to permit it. And the Bach wrote in a responsum, siman 107, that if one drew the sediment of honey-water with a sieve in which flour had been sifted, it is permitted to drink it on Pesach, for it is nullified in sixty, since it is liquid in liquid; see there.
