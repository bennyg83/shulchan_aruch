[4] That was cooked. See Baer Heitev. See Responsa Beis Ephraim, siman 35, regarding a copper utensil in which chametz was used on Pesach. If it has prior absorbed taste, it is permitted after Pesach. If it is a new utensil and actual chametz was used in it on Pesach, and it absorbed much or even little, and in estimation there is not a majority in the cooked food now being cooked in it against what it absorbed, one should be stringent. But if there is a majority in the cooked food, it is permitted. Perhaps even ab initio one may be lenient in such a case, where he now wants to cook a dish that will have a majority against the absorbed taste; see there.
