[4] "The custom follows the first view in every mixture, etc." This means that it does not reawaken on Pesach. Flour mixed with flour is considered liquid with liquid, because it mixes well (Beis Yosef, Terumas HaDeshen, and Semag). It appears that if it became mixed before Pesach in less than sixty, he may add to it before Pesach until there is enough to permit it on Pesach, and this does not involve nullifying a prohibition ab initio, since it is still permitted. See below in the laws of sukkah, siman 626, se'if 1. And this term "liquid with liquid" does not come to exclude this, for it too mixes well. See below, siman 453, se'if 3, where perhaps crumbs remained in it.
