[4] With a dry item. Two chametz loaves became mixed with several matzah loaves. Chok Yaakov and Pri Chadash concluded that on the eve of Pesach their law is like other prohibitions, and one in two is nullified. In Responsa Devar Moshe, siman 26, he concluded that this is if they are not yet baked; but not after they are baked, because then it is an important item. See there at length for the laws that branch out from this.
