(א) Not returning. Even the wise men were not allowed before the Passover, but rather, they were told that they would not be the house of Judah

(ב) In a dry thing. Two loaves of chametz mixed with several loaves of matzah — Chok Yaakov and Peri Chadash raised that b'eyna their law is like other prohibitions and one in two is nullified. And in responsa Devar Moshe siman 26 he raised that if they are not yet baked it is nullified, but not after baking, for it is a significant item — see there at length for laws branching from this.
