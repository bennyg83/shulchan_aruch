And know what the Mechaber wrote "and was nullified in sixty" — he gave the example of a moist thing nullified in sixty; and likewise a dry thing that became mixed, as Ateret Zekenim proved from responsum of Rashba siman 485. And even if nullified only one in two — so from Magen Avraham note 11 and it is plain; and those who disagree that it returns and revives — their view applies also to a moist thing that became mixed before Pesach and was nullified in sixty, as he wrote in siman 453 seif 3 in Mishna Berurah:
