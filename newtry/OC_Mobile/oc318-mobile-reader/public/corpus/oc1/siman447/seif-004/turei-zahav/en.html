(א) That it is moist in moist. It appears to me that if moist became mixed in moist in less than sixty before Pesach, one may increase upon it before Pesach until there are sixty so it will be permitted on Pesach, and this is not [called] nullifying a prohibition l'chatchila; and I wrote proof in Hilchot Sukkah siman 626 seif 1 [see there]. And this moist in moist does not exclude flour in flour, which also mixes well, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Semag; see siman 453 seif 3; and similarly ruled in Shulchan Aruch below siman 451 seif 27 regarding a spit — we do not say it returns and revives:
