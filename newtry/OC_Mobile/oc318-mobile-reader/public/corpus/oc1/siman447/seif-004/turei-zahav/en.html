It appears to me that if liquid became mixed with liquid in less than sixty before Pesach, he may add to it before Pesach until there is sixty so that it will be permitted on Pesach, and this does not involve the prohibition of nullifying a prohibition ab initio. I wrote proof to this in the laws of sukkah, siman 626, se'if 1; see there. This phrase "liquid with liquid" does not come to exclude flour with flour, for that too mixes well, as Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Semag. See what I wrote in siman 453, se'if 3. So too Shulchan Aruch decided below in siman 451, se'if 27, regarding a spit, that we do not say it reawakens.
[4] Crumbs remained for him. Straining does not help for this, because perhaps there are very fine particles that pass through the holes of the strainer.
