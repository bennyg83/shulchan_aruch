(10) One who cooked fish in a chametz pan that was not used for many days, and the spoon with which he stirred later stirred another dish, and from it they gave some to another dish — see in Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 21 who wrote one may not be lenient in this (and this is according to Rama's view below seif 10 that he forbids taste for detriment on Pesach — see there), unless there is otherwise basis to permit; and here there is additional basis to permit taste for detriment even on Pesach b'dieved after two months (and it is implied that also what was cooked in the pan is permitted b'dieved), on condition it is folded so that what is cooked in it is not a sharp thing (see Yoreh De'ah siman 93 in the gloss and siman 122 seif 3 in the gloss); and from this one learns to be stringent in covering a pot even though it is not ben yomo, nevertheless after two months there is room to permit; however l'chatchila in all prohibitions it is forbidden to use vessels that are not ben yomo without hagalah even if not used for many years, and all the more chametz — see there. And Beit Din Shaarei Tzedek sign 2; however in responsa Chacham Tzvi siman 75 he wrote to permit specifically if twelve hours passed after chametz was cooked in it, and Rav Naftali Cohen agreed with him as written there siman 76, and likewise siman 80 — see there. And this will be explained further below siman 451 sign 4 — see there.
