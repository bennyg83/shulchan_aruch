[8] Prohibitions. See Baer Heitev. See Responsa Noda BiYehudah, first edition, siman 21, that in the fifth hour, noksheh is permitted according to everyone, and according to the view that noksheh is rabbinic, it is permitted the entire eve of Pesach; see there. However, according to the many poskim who hold that noksheh is biblical, one must forbid noksheh from the fifth hour onward. See Responsa Beis Ephraim, siman 30. See Noda BiYehudah, second edition, that even though noksheh is rabbinic, one should not permit selling to a non-Jew when the cooked food does not have sixty against the noksheh chametz, just as it is forbidden with actual chametz even when there is sixty; see there.
