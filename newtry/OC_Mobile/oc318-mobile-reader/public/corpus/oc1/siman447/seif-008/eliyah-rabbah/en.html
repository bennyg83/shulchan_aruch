Levush: With sixty, etc. This implies that he holds chametz is not a davar sheyesh lo matirin, and therefore it is nullified in sixty even with its own type. So ruled Toras Chatas, klal 36, and Sefer Apei Rabravei, unlike the Taz and Shach in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 102; there I clarified my words. So too it appears from Shiltei Gibborim in Perek HaSocher.
