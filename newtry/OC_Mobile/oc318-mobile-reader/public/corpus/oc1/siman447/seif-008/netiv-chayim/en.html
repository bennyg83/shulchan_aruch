(There) And according to what was written above, therefore it appears to me — see there; his words are closed, and it appears this is the explanation: according to what was written that Mordechai forbids because of iruy, but all the more he permits even chametz b'eyna; if so the question returns to its place — why does Mordechai forbid even cold; on this he says therefore it appears that Mordechai deals with filaments. And to me, the small Shimon Hirsch, it appears this is what he means: it is proven from Hagahot Maimoniyot that the reason is iruy, for if from kli sheini, if so there is a difficulty in Hagahot Maimoniyot contradicting themselves, for in chapter 1 Hagahot wrote one need not be concerned for kli sheini (in truth in the Hagahah before us it is not in chapter 1; Beit Yosef brought one need not be concerned in kli sheini in the name of Hagahot — see there — but he did not explain which Hagahah he meant), and if so who are "there are those who are stringent in kli sheini" that Rama brought — Hagahot Maimoniyot in the last chapter; also Mordechai who forbids scalding in kli sheini, this is from iruy, and from where do we forbid kli sheini. And one cannot say that this iruy deals with discharging from a chametz vessel and here through iruy he holds it discharges; and from this is proof to be stringent in kli sheini when chametz is b'eyna — this is not so, for chametz b'eyna Mordechai forbids even in cold, and also Hagahot Maimoniyot forbids in cold when there is gravy. And it must be said that "there are those who are stringent" refers to the chicken, and when there is no gravy; if so from where does Mordechai forbid kli sheini — on this he answered that Mordechai who forbids cold deals when there are filaments, and now it can be said well that "there are those who are stringent" is Mordechai, and when chametz is b'eyna they forbid in kli sheini because iruy causes discharge from the vessel. And one could say: if so cold should also forbid when chametz is b'eyna — one can say cold does not forbid meat except when there are filaments, but this wheat found in the chicken, which has no filaments, cold does not forbid; and this requires clarification.
