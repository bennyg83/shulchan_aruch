[8] New. But with an old knife, even if it is clean and was not used that day, it is forbidden, since it is a sharp item. This is specifically when they were cut before he put them to pickle, when they are still sharp, or even after they were pickled and became sour, for then too it is called a sharp item. This is not so when they were cut after water was put on them, before their pickling was complete, for then it is not called a sharp item, since water nullifies their sharpness.
[8] Used that day. Although with a sharp item it forbids even if the utensil was not used that day, here, since they put water in them, their sharpness is nullified; Tur. If it was used that day, they are forbidden because soaked is like cooked. But it is permitted to keep them, and it does not forbid their mixture, as explained in se'if 5 regarding items cooked in their utensils; see there, and see Magen Avraham and Chok Yaakov.
