(א) New [knife]. But with an old knife, even if it is clean and is not ben yomo, it is forbidden, since it is a davar charif. And specifically [this applies] when they were cut before they were placed to be pickled, for then they are still in their sharp state; or even after they were pickled and became fermented, for then they are also considered a davar charif. This is not the case when they were cut after water was placed on them before the completion of their pickling, for then they are not considered a davar charif, since water nullifies their sharpness.

(ב) Beit Yosef. Even though with a davar charif it is forbidden even if [the knife] is not ben yomo, here, since water is placed on them, their sharpness is nullified — Tur. And if it is ben yomo, it is forbidden, for that which is pickled is like that which is cooked. But it is permitted to keep them, and it does not forbid their mixture, as is explained in seif 5 regarding things that are cooked in their vessels — see there, and [see] Magen Avraham and Chok Yaakov.
