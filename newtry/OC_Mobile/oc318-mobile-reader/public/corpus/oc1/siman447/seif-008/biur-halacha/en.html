(א) If it is not ben yomo etc. — see Mishna Berurah that if it is ben yomo it is forbidden, and so in Tur; and see Rabbi Akiva Eiger and other Acharonim who noted that through pickling it is never ben yomo, for the absorption of the forbidden substance or chametz is completed before the absorption of the pickled item placed afterward; and so Magen Avraham above in seif 5 wrote there that it cannot be conceived to forbid through pickling except when it was b'eyna — at the moment they removed the chametz at that moment they placed the pickled item, and this is forced, for then Tur and Mechaber should have explained and not been stam; and Chok Yaakov wrote that in our matter it is forbidden because of absorption, since there is some sharpness; and this too is forced, for Tur wrote stam that with water their sharpness is nullified; nevertheless for practical law it appears to be stringent, for Beit HaLevi in siman 69 and Peri Chadash resolved Taz's and Shach's difficulty that a vessel standing with gravy never loses its taste forever, only when empty it loses taste; and according to them Shulchan Aruch's words come for practical law per their plain meaning; and so in Sefer Maamar Mordechai; and even though several Acharonim copied Taz's and Shach's words for halachah, nevertheless it does not appear to be lenient l'chatchila, especially here where Chok Yaakov also wrote for prohibition and Makor Chayim copied; however in time of pressure it appears one may rely to be lenient also in this, for otherwise Chok Yaakov's view above in sign 19 is not to forbid through pickling except in a vessel used frequently for chametz, when one must estimate against the whole; if not, we say there are sixty against its discharge — see there.
