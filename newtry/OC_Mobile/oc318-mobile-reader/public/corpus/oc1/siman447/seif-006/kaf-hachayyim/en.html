6) There. Even the slightest amount, etc. Whether the substance of the chametz dissolved or whether it remains intact and only its flavor was imparted, the slightest amount prohibits the mixture. Responsa Rashba, simanim 30 and 53. This is also evident from the words of the Shulchan Aruch in this siman and in siman 467. Peri Chadash. Mateh Yehudah likewise agrees that even flavor alone prohibits in the slightest amount. See there. See above, siman 442, subsection 4.
