[6] His intent is that with a dry mixture in a dry mixture, the prohibition reawakens. According to this, what the Beis Yosef wrote in siman 447, that Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote in the name of Rashba that it reawakens on Pesach, must mean specifically a dry mixture in a dry mixture. But with a liquid mixture in a liquid mixture, Rashba certainly holds that it does not reawaken; for otherwise, why would he need to say that it is like nullifying a prohibition? This is easy to understand.
