There note 29 and so the law in the gloss: if so no proof to forbid kal vehomer — for Darkei Moshe wrote in Hagahot Maimoniyot etc. It seems to explain: granted if we say cold is fully permitted, there is proof from Mordechai regarding kli sheini — although he stirred water into the vessel, nevertheless he wrote it is kli sheini — plainly the hen was put in afterward, or even say the hen was there at plucking; nevertheless although the bowl discharges via irui, this discharge has no power to return and be absorbed in the hen except via heat inside — only kli sheini (and it seems this is the view of one who permits mentioned Shulchan Aruch 14 end siman 98 if they stirred to pluck a hen in a milk pan — see Shach in Levush: taste because spurting stopped; in my view not needed — reason is regurgitation of discharge returning to the hen is not due to irui but like kli sheini; Beit Yosef ruled there to permit in kal vehomer). If so, proof from Mordechai for kal vehomer — although irui works for vessel discharge, for forbidding the hen there is only kal vehomer; proven kal vehomer forbids. Unlike if we say cold forbidden — no proof for kal vehomer, since via irui the vessel discharged and the hen was forbidden though only cold — cold too would be forbidden; whence Rama to forbid kal vehomer without gravy? He wrote further Darkei Moshe etc. — must be another gloss: Darkei Moshe holds another gloss, therefore two views here. Per Lechem Mishneh we can say there the reason taste discharged from vessel via irui and hen forbidden though only cold; second gloss: in kli sheini no concern — without gravy; therefore Mordechai forbids cold due to plucking etc.; otherwise no prohibition in cold — proven from Mordechai kal vehomer forbids, for he forbids plucking the hen although in its feather without plucking, and irui only helps discharge from vessel as written.
