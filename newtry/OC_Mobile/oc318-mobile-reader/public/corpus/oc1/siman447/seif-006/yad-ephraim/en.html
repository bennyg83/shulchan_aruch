There subsection 29, and so appears the law in Be'er HaGolah: if so there is no proof to forbid kli sheini etc., for Be'er HaGolah wrote in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot etc., thus it should read — it appears to explain: granted if we say cold is entirely permitted, there is well proof from Mordechai regarding kli sheini, for although it deals with pouring water into the vessel and nevertheless it is kli sheini, it is implied it deals with placing the chicken afterward, or even if the chicken was there at scalding; nevertheless even though the bowl discharges because of iruy, this discharge has no power to return and be absorbed in the chicken, only because of the hot inside, and this is only kli sheini (and it appears this is the view of "there are those who permit" mentioned in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah end of siman 68: if they poured to scald a chicken in a milk pan — see there in Shach in the name of Levush who wrote the reason because the flow was interrupted; and in my humble opinion this is unnecessary, rather the reason is that the returned discharge that returns to absorb into the chicken again is not because of iruy but is like kli sheini, and Beit Yosef ruled there to permit in kli sheini); if so there is well proof from Mordechai regarding kli sheini, for although iruy acts regarding discharging the vessel, nevertheless regarding the chicken being forbidden there is only kli sheini, and it is proven that kli sheini forbids; not so if we say cold is forbidden, for since because of iruy the vessel discharged, automatically the chicken is forbidden even if only kli sheini — why would Rama forbid kli sheini when there is no gravy; and afterward he wrote "and furthermore Be'er HaGolah wrote" etc., and it should read that this is another gloss, meaning such is Darkei Moshe's view, which is another gloss, and therefore he wrote here two views in the gloss. And according to what was written above, for we can say Mordechai's reason there is discharge from the vessel through iruy and automatically the chicken is forbidden, not only cold; and what he wrote in the second gloss that in kli sheini one need not be concerned — meaning without gravy; and on this he wrote therefore it appears what Mordechai wrote to forbid in cold is because of filaments etc.; otherwise there is no prohibition in cold, if so there is well proof from Mordechai that even kli sheini is forbidden, for Mordechai forbids scalding the chicken even though the chicken is in its feathers and has no filaments, and iruy helps only to discharge from the vessel, as written; and see in my responsa Beit Efrayim Orach Chayim part siman 47, and there you will find this matter explained very much, and many were perplexed on this, and emended and switched the views, and the examiner will see that this explanation is correct and illuminates the eyes, with Hashem's help.
