[6] With cold. Meaning with dry food. This is not so with cold wet food and through soaking, where the Rav above in se'if 5 is concerned and stringent. To pound inside it, even if it was not used that day, if it is a sharp item it is forbidden, as explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 96. This is the agreement of the later authorities. Nevertheless, if it was pounded before Pesach in our mortar, most of whose use is with spices, perfumes, and the like, where there is only concern for the taste of the smallest amount and it was nullified before Pesach, even in a utensil used that day one may permit; Chok Yaakov. Magen Avraham is stringent if it was pounded in it; see there.
