In cold [contact]. Meaning, in dry [contact]. Which is not so in cold wet [contact] and through soaking — the author above in seif 5 is concerned to be stringent. And [regarding] pounding in it: even if it is not ben yomo, if it is a sharp item, it is forbidden, as explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 96. So is the consensus of the later authorities. Nevertheless, if chametz was pounded in our mortar whose primary use is for spices and aromatics and the like, where there is only a concern about a trace amount of flavor, and the chametz was nullified — even in a ben yomo vessel — one may permit; [this is the opinion of the] Chayei Adam. But the Magen Avraham is stringent if it was pounded in it. See there.
