The place of contact, etc. If it was cooked without first removing a peel's thickness, the later authorities in Yoreh Deah rule regarding other prohibitions that sixty is required against the amount of that peel. It therefore appears to me that on Pesach it prohibits in any amount. Magen Avraham writes likewise at the end of siman 464. Although I concluded at the beginning of this siman that a piece that absorbed an infinitesimal amount does not prohibit another cooked dish in any amount, this case is different, because the peel itself does not contain sixty against what it absorbed. This differs from Olas Shabbos, who understood the two laws to depend upon one another. Chok Yaakov writes that one may permit it for benefit, but this is not compelling.
