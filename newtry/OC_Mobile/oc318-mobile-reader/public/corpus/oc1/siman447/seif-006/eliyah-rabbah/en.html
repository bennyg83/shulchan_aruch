The place of its contact, etc. If it was cooked without peeling, the view of the later authorities in Yoreh Deah regarding other prohibitions is that sixty is required against the peel. If so, it appears to me that on Pesach it forbids by a mashehu, and so wrote Magen Avraham at the end of siman 464. Although I concluded at the beginning of this siman that a piece that absorbed a mashehu does not forbid another dish by a mashehu, here it is different, for the peel itself does not have sixty against what was absorbed, unlike Olas Shabbos, who understood that one depends on the other; examine it. Chok Yaakov wrote that it may be permitted for benefit, but this is not compelling.
