(Seif katan 6) And detrimental taste. The correct text is: “Shibbolei HaLeket writes.”
Chok Yaakov disagrees, maintaining that this is not Shibbolei HaLeket’s meaning. Eliyah Rabbah agrees with Magen Avraham and his interpretation.
The case of wheat in a well is considered detrimental taste. Chok Yaakov challenges this from siman 467:12, where Beit Yosef cites an opinion forbidding water in which a wheat kernel was found, even though Beit Yosef permits detrimental taste on Pesach.
Eliyah Rabbah answers that only a well, whose waters remain connected to their source, produces detrimental taste. In siman 467, however, the wheat was found in detached water, where its taste is not detrimental.
