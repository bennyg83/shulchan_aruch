The laws of a mixture of chametz during Pesach. It contains twelve se'ifim.
Chametz on Pesach forbids its mixture — whether of its own kind or not of its own kind — in any [minute] amount, even for benefit. {Rama: And one must burn it all; it does not suffice to redeem the value of the chametz and sell the rest (Mordechai, first chapter of Pesachim; Piskei Mahari, section 4; Responsa of Mahari Bruna). However, vessels in which [the mixture] was cooked are permitted after Pesach and do not require breaking or hag'alah (Tosafos, chapter "Kol Sha'ah").} And the law of its mixture is like the law of other mixtures, except that what forbids in other mixtures [when there is] less than sixty [times the volume] forbids in chametz in any amount; but if in other mixtures sixty were not required, but only peeling or removal of the [affected] place [sufficed], so too with chametz — for hot [touching] hot without sauce, peeling suffices, as with other prohibitions. And likewise, if a loaf of chametz touched a loaf of matzah, and both are hot and there is no fatty substance present, only the place of contact is forbidden, since it does not cause absorption further. {Rama: And regarding the law of "reicha milsa" [aroma is significant] with respect to a cooked dish that contains chametz together with other cooked dishes: some are lenient in a place where it would be permitted with other prohibitions (Mordechai, chapter "Kol Sha'ah"); and some are stringent, for at least a minute amount is present (Hagahos Semak; Tosafos). And this is specifically in a place where "reicha" is relevant; for with other prohibitions it is of no consequence, as will be explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 108, with the help of Heaven.}
