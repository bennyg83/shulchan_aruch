1) [Se'if 1] Chametz during Pesach renders a mixture prohibited, etc. Under Torah law, chametz is like other prohibited substances. The Sages were stringent regarding it because its consumption carries the penalty of kares and people are not accustomed to avoiding it throughout the year. Magen Avraham, at the beginning of the siman. Rambam, chapter 15 of Hilchos Ma'achalos Asuros, halachah 9, explains that chametz is a substance that will later become permitted. See there. Beis Yosef cites his words. Rema writes in Responsa Rema, siman 28, that when another basis for leniency exists, it may be possible to rely upon Mordechai, who writes that one may rely upon She'iltos and treat chametz as nullified in sixty like other prohibitions. Responsa Mas'as Binyamin, siman 69, writes likewise. This is also cited by Magen Avraham below in siman 467, subsection 33. In practice, however, their words indicate that this position is relied upon only when many additional grounds for leniency exist. Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 1; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1; Chayei Adam, rule 121, subsection 8.
