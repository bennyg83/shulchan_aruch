Know that the later authorities wrote that in a case of very great loss, one need not burn all of it, but may sell all of it to a non-Jew except for the value of the prohibition within it. Since he does not receive money for the prohibited substance mixed into it, he is not deriving benefit from it. This follows the view of the Mechaber in siman 467, seif 10. See there in seif katan 9 in the Mishnah Berurah regarding the case where a little of that pot which became forbidden in any amount became mixed into another pot.
