See Taz subsection 2: "and there is no permeating substance" etc. — meaning loaf to loaf certainly need not explain it is not permeating, for ordinarily such is the case; therefore he wrote at the beginning that he refers to what he said hot with hot without gravy, namely such as meat that touched chametz — a peel suffices; on this he says there is no permeating substance, meaning the meat is lean without milk fat, meaning fattiness; but if it has milk fat, meaning fat, then it permeates through all of it and infuses it with chametz and forbids all the meat, as explained above according to Taz's intent; and some explain that he refers to hot with hot in other prohibitions — this is not clear.
