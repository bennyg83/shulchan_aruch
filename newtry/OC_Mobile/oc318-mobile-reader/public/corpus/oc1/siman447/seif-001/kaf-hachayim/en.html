(1) [Seif 1] chametz on Pesach forbids etc. By d'oraisa law chametz is like other prohibitions, but the Sages were stringent regarding it since it carries karet and one is not accustomed to separating from it throughout the year. Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman, and Rambam chapter 15 of the Laws of chametz siman 9 wrote the reason because it is a davar sheyesh lo matirin — see there, and Beit Yosef brought it. And Rama wrote in responsa siman 28: where there is an additional basis for leniency one may rely on what Mordechai wrote that there is basis to rely on his rulings and one may be lenient and nullify chametz in sixty like other prohibitions; and so in responsa Mishna Berurah siman 69, and also cited in Magen Avraham below siman 467 subsection 33; nevertheless in practice it appears from their words one does not rely on this unless there are many additional bases for leniency.
