(א) In any amount. And see in responsa Yoreh De'ah siman 563 where there is a doubt of mixtures, all agree it is permitted, for it is a doubt in a rabbinic matter — perhaps it did not mix, and perhaps the law is according to those who hold it is nullified. And know what the Mechaber wrote "in any amount" — for in sixty he permits selling the chicken outside etc. Rather, less than sixty: because it is a davar sheyesh lo matirin. And see Yoreh De'ah siman 102 where he wrote in the name of Rashba that chametz is not in this category, which spoils while waiting. And for this reason Rambam was precise; but in bread that is not lost while waiting — see there. And according to Rosh who were stringent regarding it, whether because of the stringency of bal yera'eh or because it returns to prohibition next year — even in a dish that spoils it is forbidden even in any amount.
