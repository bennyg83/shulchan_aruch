[1] In the smallest amount. See in the responsum in Yoreh De'ah, siman 163, that where there is a doubtful mixture, everyone agrees it is permitted, for it is a doubt in a rabbinic matter: perhaps it was not mixed, and perhaps the halachah follows the view that it is nullified. Know that what the Mechaber wrote, the language "in the smallest amount," is not precise, for in se'if 6 he permits selling the chicken except for, etc.; rather, it means less than sixty. The reason is that it is something that will become permitted. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 102, where he wrote in the name of Rashba that chametz is not included in this category, since it spoils while waiting. For this reason the Rambam was precise: but with bread, which is not spoiled by waiting; see there. According to the Rosh, who was stringent because of the stringency of bal yera'eh, or because it returns to its prohibition the following year, even a cooked food that spoils is forbidden even in the smallest amount.
