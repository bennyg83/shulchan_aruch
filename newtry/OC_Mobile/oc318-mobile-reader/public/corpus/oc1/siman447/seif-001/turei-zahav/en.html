[1] In the smallest amount. The Rambam gave the reason that chametz is something that will become permitted. The Rosh in the final chapter of Avodah Zarah gave the reason that people are not separated from chametz all year, and therefore they were stringent about it even in the smallest amount. See what I wrote in se'if 2, that on the last Yom Tov it is permitted to keep it.
[1] And there is nothing there that spreads. This refers to what he first wrote, hot with hot without liquid, meaning lean meat that has no fat. But if it has fat, we say in Yoreh De'ah, siman 105, se'if 5, that it spreads throughout all of it.
[1] Specifically in a place, etc. This means, for example, where the oven is small and does not hold twelve esronim, and it is closed, and the chametz and matzah are both uncovered inside the oven. In this case we permit after the fact with other prohibitions, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 108, except that with matzah it is forbidden in this case. But if the oven is large, even if it is closed, or one of the cooked foods is covered, whether the chametz or the matzah, it is permitted, for in this case smell is not relevant at all. There in Yoreh De'ah, Rema decided that in a case of loss, one may rely on those who are lenient, and it is like other prohibitions. The poskim wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Tam that smell is not relevant to bread at all. Therefore chametz bread baked together with matzah bread does not forbid, even if these three conditions are present. So too he brought in Shulchan Aruch, siman 461, se'if 5.
