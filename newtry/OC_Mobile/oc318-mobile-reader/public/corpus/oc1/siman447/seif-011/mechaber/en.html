Whether leaven became mixed in before Pesach and Pesach passed over it, or it became mixed during Pesach and the entire Pesach passed over it, or Pesach passed over the leaven and it became mixed after Pesach,it is nullified in sixty; and if there is less than sixty, it suffices to discard the benefit of the prohibition into the Dead Sea (Hagahot Maimoniot, ch. 1).
