Whether leaven became mixed in before Passover and Passover passed over it, or it became mixed during Passover and the entire Passover passed over it, or Passover passed over the leaven and it became mixed after Passover—it is nullified in sixty; and if there is less than sixty, it suffices to discard the benefit of the prohibition into the Dead Sea (Hagahot Maimoniot, ch. 1).
