11) A householder's loaf of chametz that became mixed with loaves of matzah renders all of them prohibited for consumption and benefit, even if it became mixed with one thousand loaves, because it is an item sold by number, as stated at the beginning of Yoreh De'ah, siman 110. It appears to me that they remain prohibited even after Pesach. If the loaf was cut or broken, it is permitted after Pesach. Some maintain that it is permitted after Pesach even while still whole, because every chametz mixture was permitted after Pesach despite having been prohibited by the slightest amount; an item sold by number, which is likewise prohibited by the slightest amount, should be no more stringent. I am stringent in this case, because the Sages treated a mixture containing an item sold by number like the prohibited item itself. Sefer Beis Moed; Birkei Yosef, subsection 2. Peri Chadash in siman 448, se'if 3, likewise rules that it is prohibited even after Pesach. See there. See below, se'if 9, and our words there, with the help of Heaven.
