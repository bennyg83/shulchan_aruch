Whether chametz etc. — see Mishna Berurah where I wrote: if it became mixed in less than sixty etc. — according to his view, through chametz mixtures of a kezayit, so it is in the beginning of siman 442 in Magen Avraham — see there: if there is not sixty against a kezayit, according to all one does not transgress bal yera'eh; and so Gra brought in this siman; and I did not copy what Magen Avraham brought in the name of Bach, that if it became mixed in less than sixty, even if there was not a kezayit, one does not transgress bal yera'eh, nevertheless for eating it is forbidden after Pesach because chametz taste in pieces is forbidden without redemption, because Beit Meir questioned this since one did not transgress bal yera'eh on this mixture, why should it not be permitted in eating (and taste of chametz in pieces does not apply after Pesach); and so implied in Gra's explanation in subsection 39 that the Mechaber who requires sixty deals when there is at least a kezayit; and in subsection 41 it is implied that regarding mixtures on which one did not transgress bal yera'eh, it is permitted in eating and benefit, even without redemption — and this is even without redemption that the Mechaber does not deal with this; however Makor Chayim's view here is like Magen Avraham, that even less than a kezayit is forbidden without redemption, and his reason is because many Poskim hold one transgresses bal yera'eh also on less than a kezayit; and perhaps Gra also holds one transgresses bal yera'eh even on less than a kezayit, but this is specifically when it is b'eyna and not through mixtures — and this requires study for practical law.
