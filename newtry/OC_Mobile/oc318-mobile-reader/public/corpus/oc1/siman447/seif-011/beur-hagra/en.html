(א) Siman 461 'between,' etc. He holds what Rava said 'not at its time,' etc., is within sixty — and therefore he said 'between Passover passed over it,' etc. What he said 'between it became mixed during Pesach,' etc., he learned from what Rava said 'at its time,' etc.; and Rambam explained chapter 15 law 1 and likewise in Milchamot because it is davar sheyeish lo matirin and not in its kind — as if in its kind per Rav; therefore he said 'between it became mixed,' etc., and all the more if mixed before Pesach; and per those who say it returns and awakens as he wrote in seif 4 — nevertheless it does not detract: if mixed during Pesach. And he said 'between chametz,' etc. — but all poskim disagree on chametz over which Passover passed that became mixed after Pesach requiring sixty; he holds it is nullified in majority; Magen Avraham; and Rashi there s.v. not at its time and s.v. Rava said kedeirot, etc. But if mixed during or before Pesach in less than sixty it is certainly forbidden since he violated bal yera'eh — R' Shimon acquires as in its essence; Ran: 'R' Shimon acquires,' etc. — meaning between mixed before Pesach or after, except if mixed before or during in a manner they violate bal yera'eh — an olive-bulk in a kav (as chapter 3) forbidden after Pesach; but after Pesach possibly even more than that is permitted provided the permitted increases as terumat challah, etc. (see there) — per his view that only an olive-bulk in a kav violates bal yera'eh; see what he wrote siman 462 seif 1 mixtures of chametz violate bal yera'eh even without olive-bulk in a kav as Mishnah Lemelech in the name of R' Yehuda HaChasid and Rambam — only that there be an olive-bulk among them since permitted does not combine with forbidden as he wrote there 43 and 44; and for this reason too there is no essence since there is no olive-bulk in essence; see Magen Avraham siman 462 s.k. 1; and most poskim explain thus; and so from Rambam; and so from gemara: 'these violate on Pesach,' etc., and Rambam and Rashi explained bal yera'eh — and although there is no olive-bulk in a kav in them, we say regarding Median beer they put it in barley water and Rashi and Rosh there; and as gemara there 44:1 'leave the Babylonian to crush,' etc. — therefore likewise for others not in it at all one cannot say 'if in its essence' or 'if by estimate,' etc.; therefore no olive-bulk in a kav is needed; and nevertheless even Rabbanan violate bal yera'eh — Rabbanan only on eating because one does not eat an olive-bulk in a kav, unlike bal yera'eh since it is not nullified in mixtures; as Rambam wrote and Shulchan Aruch siman 462 seif 1 — although Rambam ruled like Rabbanan (see there); and gemara asks on these mountains with a warning 'who taught?' and not on the first 'these violate.'
