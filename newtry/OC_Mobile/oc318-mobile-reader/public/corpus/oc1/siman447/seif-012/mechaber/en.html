Inferior leaven (ḥametz nuksheh), even in its own right, is not forbidden for benefit after Pesach; but “periyosh” items are considered full leaven and are forbidden for benefit after Pesach. Gloss: some refrain from playing on the table during Pesach with cards called “kartin,” out of concern that crumbs of inferior leaven within them may fall into food (Piskei Mahari §167).
