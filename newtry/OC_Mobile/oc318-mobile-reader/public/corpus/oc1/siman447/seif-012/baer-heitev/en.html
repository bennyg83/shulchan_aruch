[12] For benefit. But for eating it is forbidden if it is in its own substance; Chok Yaakov. Magen Avraham wrote that even for eating it is permitted, and the reason he mentioned benefit is because noksheh chametz is not fit for eating; see there. Maharil wrote that one should not light with oils made from kitniyos because of chametz. Shlah wrote that the same applies to candles of fat used for looking into pots, since they were not careful about chametz utensils when they made the candles; rather, one should take a wax candle or an oil candle. See siman 453, se'if 8, and Responsa Sha'ar Ephraim, se'if 5, and see Chok Yaakov in this siman, se'if katan 27.
