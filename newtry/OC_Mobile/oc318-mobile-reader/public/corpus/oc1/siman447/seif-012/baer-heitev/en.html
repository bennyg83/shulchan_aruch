(ב) For benefit. But for consumption it is forbidden if it is in its original form — Chayei Adam. And the Magen Avraham wrote that even for consumption it is permitted, and the reason [the Shulchan Aruch] stated 'benefit' is because chametz nuksha is not fit for consumption; see there. The Maharil wrote that one may not light [lamps] with oils made from kitniyot on account of chametz. And the Shelah wrote that the same law applies to candles of fat (shuman) [used] to look into pots, since [the manufacturers] were not careful about chametz utensils when making the candles. Rather, one should take a wax candle or an oil [lamp]; see siman 453, seif 8, and responsa, shin-alef, siman 65. And see the Chayei Adam, this siman, sub-paragraph 27.
