Lest it fall, etc. And if kartin fell into food, the food is forbidden, as above regarding chametz nokshah such as tarika and the like in siman 442; but permitted to keep and after Pesach as above. Tur brought above in this siman and likewise Ba'al HaItur: specifically chametz b'eyna; but discharge from the walls of a vessel regarding honey and oil of a gentile on Pesach, etc., is nullified; and for several days our master practiced prohibition, and the stringent 572 — end of his words. It is difficult: how is it nullified, behold sixty is required, and what is inside the vessel is not sixty against the entire vessel, as Beit Yosef wrote; it appears Ba'al HaItur follows his reason that also in Yoreh De'ah siman 93 he holds there is sixty against vessel discharge, as Beit Yosef there according to him; and although Tur does not hold this view, he brought his words because some mention what Ba'al HaItur wrote, such as meat not careful in salting; and furthermore perhaps he holds as Mordechai Kol Sha'ah in the name of Or Zarua to permit a beer barrel — rinse with water, wipe, and put wine and honey for Pesach, because beer taste is nullified in sixty before Pesach and we do not measure against the entire vessel since the permitted absorbed — and Rama ruled thus in siman 451:
