2) The sacred Zohar states in parashas Ki Seitzei, 282b: 'Those who observe Pesach, when the night of guarding holds sway over them, must all be guarded and protected from even the slightest amount of chametz and leaven, and all their foods and drinks must be guarded. One who guards them from chametz and leaven has the body guarded from the evil inclination below and the soul guarded above. Of such a person it is stated, "Evil shall not dwell with You," for the person's body becomes holy and the person's soul becomes holy of holies. Of the evil inclination it is stated, "No stranger may eat of the holy," and "The stranger who approaches shall die."' These are its words. Ba'er Hetev writes this in the name of the Arizal. At present I have not found it written in the works of the Arizal available to us, except in Mishnas Chassidim, tractate Nissan, chapter 3, subsection 4.
