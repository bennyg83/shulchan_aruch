(א) Prohibited items. Since it does not carry karet. And the Maharil wrote, and for this reason chametz nuksheh, such as kartin, which does not carry karet, is nullified in sixty even during Pesach. And so the Magen Avraham agreed for the halacha; see there. But the Taz prohibits eating it, and it is permitted to keep it after Pesach. See subsection 19 in the Taz regarding kartin that fell into food; see there. And see Yad Aharon.
