Magen Avraham agreed to this as halachah; see there. But Taz forbids eating and permits keeping it until after Pesach; see Taz, se'if katan 19, regarding kartin that fell into food, and see there. See Yad Aharon.
[2] To impair. This refers to the eve of Pesach, even in less than sixty. Shibolei HaLeket wrote that if se'or or chametz was cooked with cooked or roasted meat, it imparts an impaired flavor and is permitted. In Yoreh De'ah, siman 323, it does not sound so; examine this carefully. Chok Yaakov also disagrees about this; see there.
