(There seif 12) chametz nokshah, even b'eyna, is not forbidden in benefit. — see there; see in Magen Avraham until "there is concern that each person will do so" etc. — it is implied that otherwise it would be permitted in benefit; and see in Shitah Mekubetzet in Bava Kamma chapter 9 daf 96b in the mishnah regarding one who stole chametz and Pesach passed over it, he says to him "here is yours before you," for he wrote in the name of Rambam Scarti that neither the robber nor the robbed transgress bal yera'eh — see there. And this is wondrous, for if so why is it forbidden in benefit, and if permitted what does it teach — it is obvious he can return it, and this requires study; and see in my compositions on Bava Kamma daf 98b where I settled this, with Hashem's help — see there and understand.
