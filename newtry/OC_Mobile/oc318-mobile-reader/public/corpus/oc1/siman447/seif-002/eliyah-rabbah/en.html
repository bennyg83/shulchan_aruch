(2) [Levush] For we are accustomed to burn, etc. So Rama wrote in the name of Mordechai and rulings of Maharai siman 164; my brother-in-law in Chok Yaakov challenged: Mordechai refers to wheat not a chicken in which wheat was found; Maharai explained at length: if about wheat — why burn on Yom Tov; therefore explained it refers to less than kezayit no destruction needed — see there at length; and the challenge: Agudah on this Mordechai case wrote when he nullified it — therefore did not rule to burn on Yom Tov; and anyway Maharai's explanation is strained; rather plainly Maharai's explanation of the questioner: if to burn or cast in public domain; answered custom is burning; explained Mordechai's words; wrote on it "it does not appear he refers to the wheat itself" — regarding law for this he wrote even wheat needs no destruction for less than kezayit; if so it refers to custom and the same for the chicken; and so Agudah perek 2 of Pesachim explicitly we burn the chicken on chol hamoed — certainly Mordechai so explained; although Mordechai wrote chicken law in perek 2 — known Mordechai style; here he wrote in the name of R' Yehuda and below in other Poskim; and so in Amar Kol on parchment; and so Shibolei Leket end siman 63 and baraita — therefore do not deviate from Rama and Levush on this in any matter:
