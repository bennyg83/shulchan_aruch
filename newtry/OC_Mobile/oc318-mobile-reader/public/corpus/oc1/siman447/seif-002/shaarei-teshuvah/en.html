Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 21, wrote regarding one who cooked fish in a chametz pan that had not been used for many days, and immediately after stirring with it he stirred another cooked food, and from that they put some into another cooked food. He wrote that in his book Chok Yaakov a printing error fell in what was written in the name of Taz and Shach to forbid, and in truth this is not so. However, Nekudas HaKesef forbids, and he wrote that this ruling has spread, and so wrote Olas Shabbos and Magen Avraham. Therefore one should not be lenient unless there is another side for permission after the fact, after two months. It sounds that even what was cooked in the pan is permitted after the fact. This is on the double condition that the item cooked in it not be a sharp food. From this one can learn that one who is stringent about a pot cover, even when it was not used that day, nevertheless after two months one may permit it. Ab initio, with all prohibitions it is forbidden to use utensils, even if they have not been used for many years, and all the more so with chametz; see there.
