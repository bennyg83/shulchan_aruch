From six hours and onward, etc. Although one receives lashes at that time for eating it, and it is also forbidden for benefit according to the Torah, nevertheless since there is no kareis liability at that time, the Sages did not become stringent with it to forbid in any amount, nor regarding the stringency of noten taam lifgam. Regarding chametz nuksheh during Pesach, some are stringent to forbid it in any amount, but the view of the Magen Avraham and many other later authorities is that since according to all opinions there is no kareis for it, and according to many poskim it is only a D’Rabbanan prohibition, it is nullified in sixty even during Pesach, and one may rely on them in pressing circumstances. Know that on the last day of Yom Tov, even though it too is D’Rabbanan, nevertheless since it was enacted because of doubt regarding the day, it is forbidden for eating through a mixture in any amount just like on Pesach, as is proven below in siman 467, seif 10.
