(2) And Zohar wrote in parashat Ki Teitzei daf 282b: and thus — on the night of guarding all need to be guarded from chametz and se'or in any amount and all foods and drinks are guarded; whoever guards them from chametz and se'or — he is guarded from the evil inclination below and the soul above; and it is said of him: evil shall not dwell with you, for his body became holy and his soul holy of holies; and it is said of the evil inclination and any stranger shall not eat holy, and the stranger who approaches shall die — end of his words.
