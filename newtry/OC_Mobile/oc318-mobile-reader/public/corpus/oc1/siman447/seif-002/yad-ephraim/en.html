In Magen Avraham there subsection 2, and so it appears in siman 467 etc., and he did not distinguish whether the chicken is fat etc. — it should read: although Magen Avraham in siman 467 establishes that case with a weak one, for otherwise there is difficulty on the first view that does not hold to forbid all of it — see there; nevertheless his intent is not that he holds it deals only with a weak one alone, for this is a great forced reading to be so stam; but Per the first view he took cutting the place of permeation according to estimation, and if so he gave a general rule that everything is Per the matter of permeation; and in a weak chicken the matter of permeation is slight and removal suffices, and in a fat one that permeates through all by infusion, indeed it forbids all of it since the place of permeation is throughout; and on this are the differences of views afterward, that in every case even in a weak one it is forbidden because of turning the spit.
