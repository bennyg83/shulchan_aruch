[9] Question: Does dry meat that is dried over smoke rising from chametz become forbidden even after the fact on Pesach? And can one distinguish between smoke, salty vapor, or clear liquid?
[9] Answer: In Yoreh De'ah 91:2, rising vapor forbids, for vapor is like actual substance; see there. In Responsa Mas'as Moshe, Yoreh De'ah, siman 4, around page 74, he innovated that this is specifically with liquids, not foods, and he brought the words of the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilchos Tum'ah, halachah 4, that melted fat is not connected by a stream. See Yoreh De'ah 105:3 in the gloss, regarding fat for a lamp, milk; perhaps it is a scribal error and should read oil. See Taz 6 there. This innovation is wondrous in my eyes, and in Seder Issur VeHeter I marked it. See Magen Avraham 451, note 30, where it is explained that even vapor from chametz foods is like chametz, and with Hashem's help it will be explained there.
