At first glance this is difficult, for Shmuel said: they taught this only where it was cooked, but if it was roasted with it, one peels it. But Shmuel holds that the same type mixed with the same type is not nullified. If so, it is proven that there is not even the smallest amount. Although one can say that he said this as an explanation of the Mishnah of the thigh, and that Mishnah holds that the same type mixed with the same type is nullified, nevertheless what does it ask on this from Rav Huna, who said that a kid roasted in its fat is forbidden, etc.? Perhaps Rav Huna holds like his teacher Rav, that the same type mixed with the same type is not nullified. Perhaps one can say that Hagahos Maimoniyos holds that milk and meat are called unlike types, unlike Rashba and Ran. One can also say that the minute amount that spread beyond the amount requiring removal spreads together with the flavor of the part requiring removal, and since the power of permission is mixed with it, it is called unlike types, as Ran wrote in chapter Gid HaNasheh regarding once it gives flavor in the piece. Only with chametz is it forbidden, since even unlike types forbid in the smallest amount. According to this, with something that has permitted status, Hagahos Maimoniyos also concedes that it is forbidden only to the depth requiring removal. Examine carefully.
