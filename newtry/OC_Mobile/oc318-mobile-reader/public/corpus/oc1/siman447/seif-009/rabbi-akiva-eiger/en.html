There in Darkei Moshe: but chametz that is in any amount is forbidden even in roasting; and at first glance this is difficult, for Shmuel said there is no difference except that it was cooked, but it was roasted and a peel was removed from it — and Shmuel holds min b'mino is not nullified, and if so it is proven even in any amount there is none; and even if one says he said so as explanation in the mishnah of the way, and this mishnah holds min b'mino is nullified, nevertheless why challenge on it from R' Hoshaya who said a kid roasted in its milk is forbidden etc. — perhaps R' Hoshaya holds like his teacher Rav that min b'mino is not nullified; and perhaps one can say Hagahot Maimoniyot holds milk and meat are called not its kind, not like Rashba and Ran; also one can say the any amount that spread outside of the removal spreads with the taste of the removal, and since the power of the permitted is mixed in it, it is called not its kind, as Ran wrote in perek Gid HaNasheh regarding once it gave taste in the piece; and only in chametz it is forbidden since also b'she'eino mino in any amount — according to this a dry thing, Hagahot Maimoniyot also agrees it is forbidden only as removal, and understand.
