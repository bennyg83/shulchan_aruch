(א) Even in a thousand not nullified. Beit Yosef wrote: its reason appears because of davar sheyesh lo matirin, or he holds dry in dry is nullified one in two in a prohibition dependent on noten ta'am; but where they were more stringent than noten ta'am, there is no difference between dry and moist, as Mordechai wrote in perek Kol Sha'ah — end of his words; and therefore if mixed before Pesach one in two in dry, when then its measure is noten ta'am, its law is like other prohibitions:

(ב) And some say chametz is equal, etc. Automatically, if the chametz is a piece fit for honor or other things not nullified in other prohibitions, plainly so on Pesach too; except in Rashi whom Beit Yosef brings, who distinguishes according to R' Akiva between a whole loaf and a slice of loaf; Beit Yosef wrote we do not rule like R' Akiva — therefore we do not distinguish here, for R' Akiva's reason is that a loaf is important in itself, and we hold a matter fit for honor depends on context:
