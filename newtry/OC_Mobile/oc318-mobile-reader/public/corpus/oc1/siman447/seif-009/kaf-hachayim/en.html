(9) Wheat found in a pot — the spoon with which they stirred the pot is forbidden; if they stirred other pots with it — all are forbidden. Tur siman 467. Chayei Adam there. And so Ateret Zekenim in this siman end of letter 1: the spoon that absorbed from a minute amount forbids in a minute amount. And although in a piece forbidden in a minute amount Ateret Zekenim holds it does not return and forbid in a minute amount as in the previous letter — he already wrote there the reason: specifically from a piece the minute amount cannot spread; but from a spoon that has no taste of its own it can spread — see there; meaning when the piece has taste, when it discharges, etc.:
