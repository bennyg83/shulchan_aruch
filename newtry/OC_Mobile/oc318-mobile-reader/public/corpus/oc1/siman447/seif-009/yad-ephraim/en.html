Magen Avraham subsection 28: therefore if it stood before chametz etc. — see Chok Yaakov that even not ben yomo one should forbid since it is a sharp thing; and some emend in Magen Avraham "even not ben yomo"; and perhaps since it is not ben yomo and ta'am bar ta'am also, nevertheless many Poskim hold that pickling so that it is placed on fire etc. does not discharge from a vessel, as written in subsection 17 — one need not be stringent.
