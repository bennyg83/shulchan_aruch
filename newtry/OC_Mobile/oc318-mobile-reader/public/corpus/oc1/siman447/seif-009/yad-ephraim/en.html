[9] Magen Avraham. Se'if katan 28: "Therefore if it stood before chametz, etc." See Chok Yaakov, who says that even if it was not used that day one should forbid, since it is a sharp item. Some emend Magen Avraham to read "even if it was not used that day." Perhaps since it was not used that day and is also secondary flavor, and even aside from this several poskim hold that soaking for the time needed to place on the fire, etc., does not cause emission from a utensil, as written in se'if katan 17, one should not be stringent.
