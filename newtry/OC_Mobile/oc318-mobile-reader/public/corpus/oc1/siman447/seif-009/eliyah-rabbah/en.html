(9) Taste for detriment, etc. Se'or or chametz cooked with cooked or roasted meat imparts taste for detriment (Magen Avraham in Shibolei Leket); Chok Yaakov wondered what detriment applies; see Yam Shel Shlomo — he wrote explicitly: se'or or chametz fell into a pot or cooked with roasted meat — one knows immediately it imparts taste for detriment in that food and permitted, etc. — end; I looked at what he wrote "one knows" not "and one knows" — so in Sefer Tanya siman 46 — implies we know from stam that it is so; and so at the end of his words. Magen Avraham wrote wheat in a well is taste for detriment; Chok Yaakov wrote this is not clear as he wrote siman 467 note 12 — end; in my opinion there it refers to wheat in water in a vessel not a well.
