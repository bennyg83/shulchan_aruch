Magen Avraham subsection 2: forbidden substance exits from piece to piece without gravy — in my humble opinion this is wondrous, since if the entire matzah were chametz it would require against all of it because of infusion, and one cannot say that sixty against the edge that touches suffices, for from the other side the plain way through that edge is absorbed, which does not exit without gravy; if so what difference does it make that this side was not folded — nevertheless the taste of this matzah spreads through all of it; is the matzah a prophet that if all of it is chametz all spreads, and if this side is not chametz not all spreads — this is impossible to say.
[7] Se'if 3. Regarding a cooked chicken, it is permitted to nullify it. If the wheat kernel was found in a cooked chicken on the eve of Pesach before the sixth hour and there was not sixty against it, one can say that afterward the chicken becomes like a piece of neveilah. If it was roasted with geese, sixty is required against all of it.
