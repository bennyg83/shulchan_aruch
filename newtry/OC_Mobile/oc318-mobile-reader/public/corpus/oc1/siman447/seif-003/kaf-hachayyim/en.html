Whether mixed with its own kind or with another kind, etc. This means both a mixture with another kind, where with other prohibitions the law generally follows whether flavor was imparted, and a mixture with its own kind, where with other prohibitions we measure whether there is sixty times the prohibited substance, as stated at the beginning of Yoreh De'ah, siman 98. See there. Regarding chametz, however, there is no distinction; even the slightest amount prohibits the mixture, including deriving benefit from it.
