(In Shulchan Aruch) Seif 3: but if they heated it etc. — and see in responsa Ge'ulat Binyamin part 1 siman 505 regarding wheat found in charmeselin made from fat, honey, and water — one should be stringent that it forbids in any amount; and if the honey in the charmesel was boiled before Pesach to float the wax, one can hang the reasoning that the wheat was in the honey and was already cooked there, being fruit juice without water — one may rely to be lenient if wheat was found in that honey from which they fried more grains and the wheat did not split — see there.
