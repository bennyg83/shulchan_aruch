[9] In Shulchan Aruch, se'if 3: But if they heated, etc. See Responsa Givas Binyamin, part 1, siman 25, regarding a wheat kernel found in chremslach made from fat, honey, and water. One should be stringent that it forbids in the smallest amount. But if the honey in the chremslach had been boiled before Pesach to float out the wax, and one can attribute that the wheat kernel was in the honey and had already been cooked there, so that it was fruit juice without water, one may rely on this leniently if further wheat kernels were found in that honey from which they fried, and the wheat kernel had not split; see there.
