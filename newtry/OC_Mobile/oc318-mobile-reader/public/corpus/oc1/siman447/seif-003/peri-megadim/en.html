(3) And precisely Taz Yoreh De'ah 108:1 in Hagahah brought Rabbenu Yerucham's dispute whether an oven does not hold twelve logs and is sealed and both openings uncovered — if chametz is forbidden or permitted, and in loss permitted (Eliyahu Zuta here — meaning, and Peri Megadim there) — when the oven is large or one opening covered, not asked when open — then in other prohibitions l'chatchila permitted, only custom to forbid l'chatchila as Rabbenu Yerucham there; if so we learn even in any amount there is none, for otherwise one would not nullify prohibition l'chatchila even without intent to nullify whenever there is no strain (and bee legs in honey 11a, 12a) — if so likewise chametz at least b'dieved permitted except 3 logs sealed where l'chatchila forbidden — Taz there 2; and possible small and open b'dieved permitted also in chametz. What is written in bread — smell — Magen Avraham siman 461:1 that smell without greasiness at all does not forbid; and Eliyah Rabbah general 68:3 the reason both are weak — meaning chametz bread with a dish forbids the dish because it imparts flavor, etc., and forbids afterward even matzah in the oven — see Shach siman 461 Hagahot Beit Yosef note 10 and Magen Avraham Laws of Ma'achalot Assurot 13:2 and Peri Chadash siman 461 and Levush siman 461:5 wrote dry smell does not travel far — meaning the reason is dry; even though there is fat in the oven there is no imparting of flavor to forbid, even per those who hold imparting by smell in prohibition — no; and Rabbenu Tam brought proof from thanksgiving loaves though there was oil in them, and Magen Avraham Eliyah Rabbah general 35 note 11 that oil is not fat and imparts — in Peri Megadim Yoreh De'ah we expanded: one may say oil imparts; per Levush no; and as the practice Maharik brought Beit Yosef copied in siman 461. Taz 104:10 that permitted flavor does not impart prohibition — nevertheless in strong smell it imparts in oven — see there 108:1; and so Yoreh De'ah 108:15 — difficulty; and see Magen Avraham note 4 explained further.
