Wheat which was found o the eve of Pesach in cooked chicken: it's permitted when it's less than 1/60. However, if one heated the chicken on Pesach when the wheat was still inside, it reverts to giving the innards taste and is forbidden in any amount. {Rama: Rema: however, if one heated it in a kli sheini, one need not be concerned. There are those who rule strictly regarding a kli sheini on Pesach and Yom tov, saying if the kli sheini is heated to yad soledes bo, this is not called a kli sheini.}
