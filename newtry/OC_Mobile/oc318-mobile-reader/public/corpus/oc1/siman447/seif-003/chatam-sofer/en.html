(There in Magen Avraham subsection 3) And so too in the responsum of Maharil — see there; the Maharil in the responsum there, and proof that one does not permit meat of a slaughtered animal with nevelah in any case, even when both are one species, even according to those who hold min b'mino in any amount; and so it is proven in the chapter Keitzad Tzolin, for they challenge according to those who hold because of mixed flavors: what difference to me between kid and kid, what difference between kid and lamb — let us say that not only kid and kid, which is min b'mino, etc.; and according to R' Yehuda and Rabbi who ordered the mishnah it is min b'mino in any amount, etc. And with this he corrected the words of Magen Avraham subsection 4, and this requires clarification.
