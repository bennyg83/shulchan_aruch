The laws of the sukkah on the seventh day. It contains 1 seif: Even if one finished eating on the morning of the seventh day, he should not dismantle his sukkah; but from Minchah onward he removes the utensils from it and prepares the house for the honor of the final Yom Tov. And if he has nowhere to clear his utensils and wishes to eat in it on Shemini Atzeret, he must reduce in it a place of 4 by 4, to make a recognition that he is sitting in it not for the mitzvah of sukkah, so that it should not appear as though he is adding. {Rama: And if he wishes to eat in the sukkah after the festival, he need not reduce it, because it appears as adding only on Shemini Atzeret. [Minhagim]} And if he still needs to eat during the rest of the day, he must eat in the sukkah, because its mitzvah applies all 7 days. And in the Diaspora, where one must sit in it on Shemini Atzeret, when he finishes eating on the eighth day he takes down his utensils and clears them from it. And if he has nowhere to take down his utensils and wishes to eat in it on the ninth, he cannot reduce it because it is Yom Tov. What should he do for recognition? If it was small, such that it was forbidden to place a lamp in it on the other days, he places a lamp in it; and if it is large, such that it is permitted to place a lamp in it, he brings into it pots and dishes and the like, in order to recognize that it is invalid and that its mitzvah has already ended.
