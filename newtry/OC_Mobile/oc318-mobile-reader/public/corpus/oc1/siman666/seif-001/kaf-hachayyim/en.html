1) [Seif 1] One should not dismantle his sukkah. For the obligation to sleep and study in it applies throughout the entire day, and if a meal presents itself to him, he eats it inside the sukkah. Rashi, Sukkah 48a. Therefore, one should not recite Kiddush at night until it is fully dark, for if he were to recite Kiddush while it is still day, he would be required to sit in the sukkah. If he were to recite the blessing over the sukkah, these would be two contradictory acts, since he has already prayed Maariv of Shemini Atzeres, as written below in siman 668, os 10; see there.
