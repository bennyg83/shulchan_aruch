On every fast that one eats at night, whether it is a communal or individual fast, one may eat and drink until dawn. This holds if one does not sleep but if one sleeps he may not go back and eat and drink unless he made a condition [prior to sleeping] to eat and drink. {Rama: (for a long time) (Tur), (for a long time), Rem"a: There are those who say that drinking requires no condition, as we assume that a person intends to drink after sleeping and it is as if he made the condition (Tur, Mordechai, Hagahot Maimoniot, and Hagahot Ashri).}
