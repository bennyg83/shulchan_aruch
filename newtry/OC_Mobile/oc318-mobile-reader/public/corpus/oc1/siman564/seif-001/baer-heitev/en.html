Eats. It appears to me that regarding a dream-fast it is forbidden, for from the moment he dreamed he must fast, as is written in siman 288, se'if 4; and so the Shalah wrote, that from the moment he dreamed he should not have marital relations.
Fixed. Even not on his bed. Taz.
Fixed. Even in the midst of his meal, Beis Yosef. And the Taz ruled like the Rif, that if he slept in the midst of the meal he is permitted until he finishes, and afterward sleeps a fixed sleep; see there.
Regarding drinking. The Beis Yosef wrote that even all beverages are permitted. And the Kol Bo wrote specifically water.
He stipulated. And the custom is to make a condition even regarding drinking, and this is proper (later authorities). And the Magen Avraham wrote: and it appears to me that it all depends on what kind of person he is: if he is accustomed to drink at night, it is permitted to drink without a condition. And see siman 581, se'if 2, in the gloss of the Rama; and so wrote the Radbaz, siman 247.
