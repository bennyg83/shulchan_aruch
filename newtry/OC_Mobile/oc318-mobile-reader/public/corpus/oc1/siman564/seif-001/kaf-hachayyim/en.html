1) [Seif 1] He may eat and drink until dawn. On a fast prompted by a dream, however, it is forbidden, because from the time he dreamed he must fast, as written above in siman 288, seif 4, in the Rama's gloss. So too wrote the Shelah, that from the time he dreamed he should not engage in marital relations. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1.
