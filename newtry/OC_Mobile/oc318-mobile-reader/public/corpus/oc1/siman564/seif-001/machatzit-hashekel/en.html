(s.k. 1) Eats, etc. As is written in siman 288, se'if 4, in the gloss of the Rama: that some say one who slept an afternoon nap and dreamed a bad dream should fast from midday until midnight, and it counts for him as a full day's fast even though he ate until midday. Thus it does not depend on the day, but only from the moment he dreamed. And the same applies conversely to be stringent: if he dreamed at night, it is forbidden immediately, even at night.
