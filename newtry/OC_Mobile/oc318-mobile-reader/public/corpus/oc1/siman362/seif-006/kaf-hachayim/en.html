And their base where attached to the tree, etc. — and the same if they are not ten high where attached to the tree but in the middle are ten high made like an arch; and it appears to me that where they are ten high they must be four wide — otherwise it is karmelit. Magen Avraham s.k. 2. Meaning that within their hollow there must be ten high and four wide as reshut ha-yachid law — then what is at the sides is called holes of reshut ha-yachid; but if there is no hollow ten high and four wide, only the four-wide area is lower than ten — it is karmelit. So Turei Zahav 2 explains Magen Avraham's intent; and so R' Zeira 2; thereby the Ma'amar Mordechai's wonder on Magen Avraham's words is resolved:
