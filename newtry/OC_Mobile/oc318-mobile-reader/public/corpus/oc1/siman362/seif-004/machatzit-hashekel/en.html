(s.k. 4) Partition, etc. — therefore there is need to appraise Tosafos and Ra'osh's words so Magen Avraham's words are understood well. It is in Shabbos 101:2 — taught: attached ships may join eruv and carry from one to another; if broken — forbidden; if re-tied — whether unintentionally or intentionally they return to original permission; and similarly mats spread toward reshus ha-rabbim — join eruv and carry; if rolled up — forbidden; if spread again — whether unintentionally or intentionally return to original permission — every partition made on Shabbat whether unintentionally or intentionally has partition status; it challenges — but Rav Nachman said we only taught for throwing but for carrying forbidden; answered when Rav Nachman was stated — intentionally; and when the Mishnah teaches returned to original permission — unintentionally; when it teaches intentionally — partition status; and for throwing per Rav Nachman — end; Ra'osh wrote: if so why does it teach intentionally, etc.; also what Rashi explained Rav Nachman refers to the end of this baraita — we do not think so; if so in Eruvin where this baraita is primarily stated — it should bring Rav Nachman on it (per our texts Eruvin 21:1 and 25:1 — on the contrary the Gemara concludes as Rashi); therefore it must be Rav Nachman's matter was stated generally regarding intentional partition and not on this baraita. Rabbeinu Tam explained Rav Nachman's words were stated generally on partition made intentionally and not on this baraita; and incidentally with this baraita Rav Nachman's words are challenged. The answer: Rav Nachman was stated regarding intentional partition and not on the baraita. And the baraita of mats spread to reshus ha-rabbim is like houses one beside the other — join eruv and carry — a wall between two courtyards with a window — if they wish two may join eruv, if one wish one may join; if rolled up — forbidden; if spread again — return to original permission — like two courtyards fully breached into each other; every partition made on Shabbat, etc. — gives reason for what we rule generally that partition made on Shabbat even for reshus ha-rabbim has partition status; all the more here where this partition only comes to permit the prohibition of dwellings of the two courtyards — therefore return to original permission even if intentional — not relevant to Rav Nachman at all, for Rav Nachman deals with one who makes partition in reshus ha-rabbim through which reshut ha-yachid is made; but here without this partition reshut ha-yachid exists and the partition only divides dwellings — end Ra'osh; Beit Yosef and Bach understood Ra'osh's end and what he answered — Rav Nachman intentionally means one who makes partition in reshus ha-rabbim; but in the end of the baraita even if partition fell on Shabbat and rebuilt intentionally — permitted; Ra'osh's beginning is also R' Tam's view as Ran wrote — end Beit Yosef:

For Ra'osh wrote, etc. — why did he not explain it refers to the end; Beit Yosef did not explain Ra'osh's reason — we do not think so; his way is to abbreviate what does not affect the ruling:

If so per plain understanding — why did Ra'osh need Rabbeinu Tam to say Rav Nachman was stated generally and not on the baraita — Rav Nachman has no relevance to the baraita; Rav Nachman deals when through the partition reshut ha-yachid is made; the baraita deals when it is already reshut ha-yachid and the partition only divides dwellings; therefore in the baraita's case even intentional is permitted; therefore Ra'osh had to say Rav Nachman was stated generally and not on the baraita; Magen Avraham properly questioned that Ra'osh should have said it refers to the baraita's end dealing when through partition reshut ha-yachid was made as Ra'osh wrote; however since the baraita is primarily in Eruvin it should be brought there; nevertheless Magen Avraham properly questioned — why didn't Rav Nachman say his statement on the baraita; in any case since Rav Nachman's words relate to the baraita the Gemara should have brought Rav Nachman on the baraita in Eruvin; but Magen Avraham — his way is to abbreviate — questioned as Ra'osh brought Beit Yosef; and wrote: and answered Rav Nachman intentionally was stated — how he makes partition in reshus ha-rabbim:
