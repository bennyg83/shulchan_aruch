It is a partition for carrying, etc. — and all these partitions, although they are complete partitions d'oraisa and one who throws into them from reshus ha-rabbim is liable even if there are many mil within, nevertheless from the Sages one may not carry within them except within four amot if there is more than beit se'atayim because they are not considered enclosed for dwelling; but up to beit se'ah and within that amount it is permitted to carry throughout the entire enclosure.
