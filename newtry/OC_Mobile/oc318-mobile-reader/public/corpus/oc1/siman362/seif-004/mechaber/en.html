One may carry throughout a boat, even if it is more than two se'ah, since it is considered enclosed for dwelling (see above Siman 366 and end of Siman 382). If it was turned upside down to dwell beneath it, it constitutes a private domain (reshut hayachid) even on top of it. If it was turned upside down to caulk it (l'zofta), one may only carry within four cubits if it is larger than two se'ah.
