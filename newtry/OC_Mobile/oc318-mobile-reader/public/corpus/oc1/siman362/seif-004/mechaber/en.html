A ship — it is permitted to carry throughout all of it even if it is more than beit se'atayim, for it is considered enclosed for dwelling (and see above siman 366 and end of siman 482); if its deck is for dwelling beneath it, it is reshut ha-yachid; but on its deck, if its deck is for tar — one may not carry in it except within four amot if it is more than se'atayim: () כפאה לדור תחתיה הוי רה"י אף עליה כפאה לזופתה אין מטלטלין בה אלא בד' אמות אם היא יותר מסאתים:
