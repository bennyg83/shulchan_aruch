With anything they make a partition — with vessels and with saddles, whether of warp alone such as of ropes, or of woof alone such as reeds stuck in the ground — and they are permitted up to se'atayim even for an individual in a settled area, provided there is not between one rope and its fellow nor between one reed and its fellow three tefachim; and even with animals, provided they are bound; and even with people standing one beside the other within less than three [tefachim], and even when they are walking — they' are considered a partition and between them is reshut ha-yachid; and this is when they did not know they were stood for the sake of a partition; and even if one of them knows — it is forbidden; and even if he did not inform them that he is making partitions now — if it is likely they will know, such as if he once made a partition from them — he should not make a partition from them again:
