One may make a partition with any material — with vessels, with saddles — whether using only horizontal elements (erev) such as ropes, or only vertical elements (shti) such as reeds stuck in the ground. These are permitted up to two se'ah, even for an individual in a settled area, provided there is not a gap of three handbreadths between one rope and another or between one reed and another. This applies even with animals, provided they are tied; and even with people standing next to each other less than three handbreadths apart — even when walking they are considered a partition and the space between them is a private domain — provided they do not know they were positioned to serve as a partition. If even one of them knows, it is forbidden. And even if one did not inform them that he is now making partitions, if it is likely they would find out — such as if he once already made a partition from them — he may not do so again.
