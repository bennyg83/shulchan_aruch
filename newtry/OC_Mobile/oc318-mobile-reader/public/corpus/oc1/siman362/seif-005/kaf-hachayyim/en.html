It is a partition with regard to carrying, etc. Although all these are complete partitions under Torah law, and one who throws an object into them from a public domain is liable even if the enclosure extends for several mil, nevertheless, by Rabbinic law one may carry within them only four amos if they contain more than beis sasayim, because they are not considered enclosed for residential use. If they contain no more than beis sasayim, including exactly beis sasayim, it is permitted to carry throughout the enclosed area. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there.
