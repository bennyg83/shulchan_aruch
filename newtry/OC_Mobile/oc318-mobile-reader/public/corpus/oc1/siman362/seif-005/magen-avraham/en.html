That they should not move (Rambam); Bach wrote should not move as above regarding tree branches — his intent: a partition wind moves is not a partition; requires study — if so a walking person is not a partition because he moves; further Sukkah chapter 2 is difficult — why invalid animal and not say because they move; further Tur siman 634 wrote provided it is tied — if tied what difference if they move; therefore it appears Rambam's should not move means should not flee as in the Gemara; so Bach siman 634; that it does not say tied — here it stands in a breach and has nothing to tie to — therefore wrote bound; for a person need not be bound — a person does not leave his place except for need and we are not concerned lest he flee as Maggid Mishneh wrote; and deals with siman 363; in my humble opinion for tree branches — there because of partition weakness wind moves it therefore not a partition — unlike animal and person who move themselves — that is not a weakness:
