And even with people, etc., within less than three — Bach questioned: behold a person is normally an amah wide — so even with much more than three between them it should be permitted because of standing greater than breach; Magen Avraham answered therefore we require less than three because of their feet which are like ordinary reeds — normally feet are not stuck together, especially when walking there is between foot and foot about half an amah as siman 301 — see there; and Turei Zahav wrote the law with Magen Avraham is when walking but not when standing — see there; and in my humble opinion it is difficult even when walking — the measure of three tefachim between them is only at the end of the foot, not higher; the gap shortens going up — so even if below there is hollow between feet of three tefachim, when measured somewhat higher within three tefachim in height there will not be three tefachim hollow width between feet — say lavud; requires study:
