Breach equal to standing permitted whether warp or woof — see Mishna Berurah what is written that for woof one does not find it; I will explain: even though Beit Yosef wrote to explain Tur's words that although in the Gemara they discuss woof regarding standing greater than breach — nevertheless according to what we rule that breach equal to standing is also permitted, the same law applies to woof; but necessarily one cannot say this — for in woof how do you find it? If it was breach equal to standing such as if he placed above a beam of three and a half tefachim and the hollow is also three and a half [and below a beam of three tefachim — otherwise there is no partition at all] — we cannot permit by breach equal to standing, since there is airspace above the upper beam to the sky we say the air of this side and that side [the hollow in the middle] comes and nullifies the beam; and only with standing greater than breach such as the beam was four and in the middle three — then we do not say the air comes and nullifies; but not when breach equals standing, as is explicit in the sugya 10:2 — even for one who says breach equal to standing is permitted, if there is airspace from both sides the air comes and nullifies; and Turei Zahav brought this for practical law regarding warp — see there; therefore Tur must not have cited breach equal to standing specifically for woof, as we wrote in Mishna Berurah; and Beit Yosef's words require study.
