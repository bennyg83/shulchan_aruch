(s.k. 9) Within less than three, etc. — standing greater than breach — even if more than three between one and another, breach exists only a tefach or slightly more — half amah — standing is the person an amah wide:

Feet are not stuck together; even if they intend no gap between feet except less than three — lavud — two feet would be more than three or four tefachim — thereby permitted between man and man more than three — permitted by standing greater than breach; nevertheless since normally there is not between feet except less than three he had to stipulate — whether he said between person and fellow three or between foot and foot three:

And even his width is not full, etc. — this does not answer Bach's question — even slightly less than amah per Bach even with more than three between people it is permitted by standing greater than breach; rather Magen Avraham's intent: Shulchan Aruch did not write precisely partition between legs — between animal's forelegs and legs:

And its body ten high — since feet are bound it lies on legs and feet do not combine for ten height:
