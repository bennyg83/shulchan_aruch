A tel five high, etc. — one may question whether the tel must specifically be five in vertical rise, and as we say generally that if ten tefachim collect within four amot he is liable — perhaps the same here if five tefachim collect within two amot they combine for the partition above; or perhaps since the tel does not rise in height to ten tefachim its use is still somewhat for walking and cannot combine for the partition — and here it deals when the tel was in vertical rise. Further one may question: if the tel was less than three tefachim and with the partition there are ten tefachim — does it become reshut ha-yachid? Perhaps since less than three tefachim is nullified relative to reshus ha-rabbim, he only made a partition of eight tefachim on reshus ha-rabbim:

That he made upon it — see Mishna Berurah who explained he made upon the edge of the tel [meaning when the tel goes in vertical rise]; and if he distanced from its edge three tefachim, and all the more four tefachim or more — requires study, for it appears it does not help; and an example from siman 358 seif 7 — if he distanced a new partition from the old three tefachim it no longer combines with it, and there it is to be lenient — here it would be to be stringent; or perhaps we consider as if the tel was made this way from the start — five first then distancing three or more and rising another five — it is reasonable that whatever collects to ten within four amot combines, and perhaps from Gra's words above siman 358 seif 2 in the gloss in his question there it is implied they combine; and perhaps Terumat HaDeshen distinguishes thus:
