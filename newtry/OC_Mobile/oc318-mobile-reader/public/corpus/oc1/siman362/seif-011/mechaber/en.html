What is tzurat ha-petach? A reed from here and a reed from there and a reed upon them — even if it does not touch them but there are several amot between them, provided the height of the reeds from here and from there is ten and they are aligned opposite the upper reed; and if he attached the upper reed to the two reeds or to one of them from the side — it does not help; and the reeds at the sides must be strong enough to receive a door of any kind, even of straw or of reeds; but the reed upon them suffices with any amount, and even a rope of palm branches helps:
