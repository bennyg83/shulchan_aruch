What constitutes a tzurat hapetach? A reed on this side and a reed on that side, and a reed on top of them — even if the top reed does not touch them but there are several cubits between them — provided the side reeds are ten handbreadths high and are aligned directly below the upper reed. If one attached the upper reed to both side reeds or to one of them from the side, it is not valid. The side reeds must be sturdy enough to hold even the smallest door, even one of straw or reeds; but the reed on top need only be any size at all, and even a rush (gemi) is valid.
