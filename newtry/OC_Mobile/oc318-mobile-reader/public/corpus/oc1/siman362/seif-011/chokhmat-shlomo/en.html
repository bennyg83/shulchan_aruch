There seif 11, what is tzurat ha-petach, etc. Per the reeds of tzurat ha-petach they stand diagonally with the rope and are not upright but lean backward or forward — it appears for mezuzah such an opening requires a mezuzah since for mezuzah even if seen from the side it is an opening — likewise if not upright. The reason: altogether we are not so strict that it must truly have the title of opening; but for eruvin since it is not truly an opening and we require full tzurat ha-petach — if made from the side it does not help — likewise if they lean — it is as if the upper rope is not placed opposite the lower reeds but from the side and has no door law — we require them truly upright and the rope upon them; this appears clear and correct for practical law; I have no time to elaborate now — perhaps God will grant another time to explain more — requires study well:
