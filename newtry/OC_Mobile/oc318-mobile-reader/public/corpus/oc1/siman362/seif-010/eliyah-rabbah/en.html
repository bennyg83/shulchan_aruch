(10) And this is when they did not know, etc. Intentionally — so Beit Yosef wrote in the name of R' Yonatan; Taz wondered — Rav Chisda who makes his hand is like intentional — see there where he elaborated; he did not examine R' Yonatan himself who answers: the matter turned through his hand — we are not harmed by his intent to consider a partition made intentionally, for Rav Chisda did not arrange the people by hand but they formed by themselves gathering to wonder at him or comfort him — end. Nevertheless the essence appears in the reason of prohibition when knowing, as Aruchat Chayim wrote in the name of Rashba — all the more when they know they will lighten and treat Shabbat prohibition lightly; and so appears in Avodat HaKodesh; and accordingly even in what is explained seif 3 that intentional is permitted — perhaps here it is forbidden:
