But in a field it does not help, etc. — sealed from Turei Zahav and from Ra'osh's brief rulings and from Tosafos s.v. if we say — it appears even with ten it does not help in a field; however from Yerushalmi brought in Ra'osh it appears explicitly that only more than ten does not help, but with ten it helps even in a field; why would Ra'osh disagree — see there; and also somewhat from the plainness of our sugya that says "if we say within ten," etc., and does not answer that R' Yochanan deals with a field — see there; and for practical law certainly one may be lenient and rely on Semag and Semak who wrote explicitly to be lenient; later I found Chayei Adam also wrote explicitly to be lenient. Know further that if he made four posts in reshus ha-rabbim with tzurat ha-petach — certainly it does not help even with ten, for it is explained below siman 364 that one does not make an eruv in reshus ha-rabbim except with doorways:

When all directions by tzurat ha-petach — Peri Megadim was uncertain in siman 363 regarding tzurat ha-petach of four posts whether it is a partition d'oraisa to render liable for throwing from reshus ha-rabbim, or tzurat ha-petach only helps to permit karmelit or breach more than ten — for from Torah it is a partition and therefore tzurat ha-petach helps — see there [and what he brought as proof from siman 634:66 regarding sukkah — I searched the Rishonim and did not find one who wrote it deals with placing reeds on the posts]. And from Tosafos Eruvin 11 s.v. if we say and Ra'osh and Rashba who questioned why they needed posts for cisterns to permit watering festival pilgrims' animals — let them make four posts and a reed upon them which is tzurat ha-petach; and behold there the posts are even in reshus ha-rabbim as in the Gemara 20 — nevertheless permitted in their view by tzurat ha-petach of four posts to carry from the pit to there — it follows d'oraisa it became reshut ha-yachid; however for Rashba's answer there in Tosafos the Gemara deals only when he has two partitions properly, and Ritva agreed there — see there; if so nothing is proved; and all the more per R' Chananel's explanation that the four posts had reeds between them less than three tefachim each — certainly proved explicitly that by posts alone there is no partition; and so Ritva brought this explanation in his teacher's name — see there; and so Rif in the name of Rav Hai Gaon explains thus [since he writes on tzurat ha-petach made from the side — meaning to make tzurat ha-petach in a corner; and because a doorway in a corner people do not make — and this statement is brought in the Gemara on the case of four posts — therefore it was closed like a partition until that place]. And as Rashba's novellae wrote — Rif follows R' Chananel; and Rambam who copied Rif's words regarding from the side — also proves he explains like R' Chananel; and for all these in any case there is no source that there is a partition d'oraisa by four posts alone; on the contrary it appears the opposite since they needed reeds between them [unless one can somewhat reject that it is rabbinic only]. And it is astonishing that Peri Megadim did not raise all this. Know that all this is specifically with four posts with no partition between them — only a reed above them; but if there are two complete partitions on two sides and on the other two sides or even one side he made a kind of tzurat ha-petach — it appears for all that it becomes reshut ha-yachid thereby; and proof — what we rule that an alley open to reshus ha-rabbim makes tzurat ha-petach on one side and lechi and korah on another, as Rambam wrote and brought in Magen Avraham s.k. 17:
