If one made a tzurat hapetach, even for a breach wider than ten cubits it is permitted, even if one only planted four posts in four directions and made a tzurat hapetach over them. This applies in a courtyard or alleyway that has residents; but in an open field it does not help in all directions through a tzurat hapetach. According to the Rambam, a tzurat hapetach does not help for a breach wider than ten cubits unless the standing space exceeds the open space.((In that case it helps even in an open field in all directions.) (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag and Semak).
