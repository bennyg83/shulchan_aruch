Some say that the person who wishes to use this partition should not position the people himself, but rather another person should position them without their knowledge.Rema: One should only make a partition of people in a time of need and pressing circumstance. If one forgot something in a public domain, it is preferable to send children to bring the object without a partition than to make a partition of people and have an adult bring it. (Beit Yosef in the name of Shibolei HaLeket, Semag, and Sefer HaIttur).
