(42) He should not stand them, etc. — as those standing must not know they are stood for partition, the beneficiary must not know while they are being stood; after stood — no concern what he knows as seif 6:

(43) Rather another should stand — Rashba disagrees — he himself may stand them; only concern is those standing should not know; see Eshel Avraham that other poskim imply the same — essential:

(44) Bring children there — child does not know item is father's — for own pleasure he brings what he thinks he found in reshus ha-rabbim; if knows it is father's — presumed intent for father; everyone must protest child not to bring; all the more not send to bring; same if another's item child knows — brings to him; others too; father must educate, protest, separate when seeing Shabbat violation even for own pleasure; all the more not send to bring even if not his [later authorities]:
