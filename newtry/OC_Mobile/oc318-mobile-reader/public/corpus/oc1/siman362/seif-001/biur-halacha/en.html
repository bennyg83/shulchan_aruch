Or to guard what they place within it — see Mishna Berurah. And therefore it appears that a building they make to bring grain or other merchandise there [called in foreign tongue "magazin"] — if it is more than beit se'atayim its law is like a karpef, since it is made only to guard and not to dwell in it, and it is similar to muktzeh behind houses that Ritva explained in the last chapter in Rashi's name — made to bring wood to storage; and nevertheless we say on daf 24 that in muktzeh the house must be open to it and also the doorway must precede the enclosure; but otherwise not. However one may argue: perhaps since it is roofed it is better than muktzeh. However in Rashi 22 s.v. and every dwelling explicitly states that if not made for dwelling, even if roofed it does not help. Yet Rashi's main precision is from what they said: every dwelling whose use is airspace, etc.; and one who examines Rambam beginning of chapter 9 who wrote such as gardens and orchards and one who encloses a place, etc. — it is implied he does not explain as Rashi; and according to him one may well say that when roofed there is no karpef name in any case. And one may also say that an important building like a magazin is different even per Rashi — no karpef name applies at all. Later I found in Peri Megadim beginning of siman 358 that he is uncertain regarding roofed — see there in Eshel Avraham and Machatzit HaShekel.
