(1) But for modesty — such as partition builders make to rest there briefly while hewing stone; builders eat and stand there briefly in privacy — not a dwelling partition but modesty partition:
(2) Or to guard what is within — made to place fruits and vessels there; see Biur Halacha:
(3) It is a partition for carrying, etc. — meaning through enclosing with the partition it is permitted to carry throughout the enclosed area if not more than beit se'atayim; what he wrote therefore tree, etc. — intent is on what he concluded at the end; but more than beit se'atayim, etc.:
(4) They are not three high, etc. — lavud to the ground:
(5) It is ten high, etc. — and the same if not ten high where attached but in middle ten like arch; in any case must also be four wide where ten high — if four wide only where lower than ten — karmelit:
(6) And permitted throughout — even where not ten high — holes of reshut ha-yachid and like reshut ha-yachid as siman 345:4:
(7) And this is when he fills, etc. — through filling the partition is strengthened against wind; nevertheless also tie branches in the ground for this:
(8) But more, etc. — such as very long branches or many adjacent trees:
(9) Even if planted for this — to rest in its shade:
(10) Not made for dwelling — if he filled straw actually for dwelling even if not planted for this — if most of partition from straw it is enclosed for dwelling [Magen Avraham and Turei Zahav — see Turei Zahav]:
