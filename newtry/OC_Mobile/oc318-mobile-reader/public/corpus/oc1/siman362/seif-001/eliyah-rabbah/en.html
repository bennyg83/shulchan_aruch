[1] Or to sit, etc. This is because the watchmen guard there only by day and sleep in their homes at night, as stated by the Bach.
[2] And its base, etc. The same applies if the branches are not ten high where attached to the tree but rise to ten in the middle in an arch. It appears to me that where they reach ten they must also be four handbreadths wide, because otherwise the space would be a karmelit.
[3] That it is not made for dwelling, etc. If he filled it with straw for residential use, as in siman 358, seif 2, then even if the tree was not originally planted for this purpose, if most of the wall consists of straw it is considered enclosed for dwelling, as stated by the Magen Avraham.
