Which partition is called a partition for carrying. And in it are 12 seifim: Every
partition that was not made for dwelling within it but for modesty or to guard what they place within it or to sit in it in order to guard the fields — it is a partition for carrying what is within it, but it is not a partition to make what is within it enclosed for dwelling if it is more than beit se'atayim; therefore a tree whose branches descend downward — if they are not three [tefachim] high from the ground and their base in the place where they are attached to the tree is ten high — it is considered a partition and it is permitted to carry throughout all of it; and this is when he fills the airspace between the branches with wood or straw and ties the branches so the wind does not move them, for every partition that cannot stand in a common wind is not a partition; and specifically up to beit se'ah, but more than beit se'ah — not, even if he planted it for this, since it is not made for dwelling within it but only to rest in its shade to guard the fields:
