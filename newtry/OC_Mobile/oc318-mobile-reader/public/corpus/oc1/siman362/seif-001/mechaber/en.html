Which partition is called a partition for carrying. 12 sections: Any partition that was
not made to dwell inside it, but rather for privacy, or to guard what is placed inside it, or to sit in it in order to guard fields — it is a valid partition for carrying within it, but it does not make what is inside it an enclosed dwelling (mukaf l'dirah) if it is more than two se'ah. Therefore, a tree whose branches hang down — if they are not three handbreadths above the ground, and their base where they connect to the tree is ten handbreadths high — it is considered a partition and one may carry throughout it, provided one fills the airspace between the branches with wood or straw and ties the branches so the wind does not move them, for any partition that cannot withstand an ordinary wind is not a valid partition. This applies only up to two se'ah, but more than two se'ah is not valid, even if the tree was planted for this purpose, since it is not made to dwell inside but to sit in its shade to guard the fields.
