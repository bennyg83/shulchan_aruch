(s.k. 3) That it is not, etc. — therefore if most of the partition is from straw, etc., as in Tosafos Eruvin 15:1:4 that a partition standing by itself is not called a partition; the Gemara challenged from what we learn in Sukkah 24:2 one who makes a sukkah between trees and the trees are its walls is valid — answered it deals when he planted the trees initially for a partition and they are not standing by themselves. Tosafos challenged in Sukkah and Eruvin — in Sukkah the Gemara challenged this Mishnah — they are partitions that cannot stand in common wind — not a partition; answered tied and branches filled with straw so wind does not move them; once filled with straw they are no longer standing by themselves — if so why does the Gemara challenge in Eruvin; Tosafos answered one resolution — the Gemara thought the Mishnah deals in all cases whether most of partition from tree or from straw — certainly if most from straw it works well there is no question of partition standing by itself; but the Gemara in Eruvin challenges from most from tree called standing by itself — end:

It is clear from Tosafos that if most from straw he still requires it was planted initially — it is as if planted entirely for this; and we do not say branches are nullified to the tree trunk; if so here too if he filled straw for dwelling it is as if he planted the whole tree for dwelling; nevertheless he specifically requires mostly from straw; but half and half does not suffice since breach equal to standing is permitted — if so even without filling straw it would have partition name; filling straw for dwelling does not help as above siman 328 seif 2:
