Partition made on Shabbat. Because Beit Yosef and Bach questioned Tur and Bach emended — see there; therefore I elaborate somewhat to show his words are true and correct; and Ra'osh and Ran should not be two contradicting witnesses on R' Tam — for Ra'osh wrote Rabbeinu Tam said Rav Nachman was stated generally — why not explain it refers to the baraita's end that teaches every partition made on Shabbat whether unintentionally or intentionally has partition status — deals with reshus ha-rabbim and presumably Rav Nachman only for throwing forbidden but carrying permitted — therefore as Ra'osh wrote the beginning resembles the end teaching even for carrying permitted; if so what does it answer that Rav Nachman intentionally was stated — in the baraita's end it also teaches intentional and still permitted; Ra'osh questioned this; wrote: and answered Rav Nachman intentionally was stated where he makes partition in reshus ha-rabbim — never was partition there and now makes it; but in the end it deals when partition was there, fell on Shabbat, returned — then permitted even intentional; as Ran in R' Tam's name — only Ra'osh questioned: in the beginning too it deals when it fell on a weekday — otherwise say Shabbat once permitted, permitted as siman 374:2; therefore it deals when fell weekday and still permitted even made intentionally; so Tosafos and Ra'osh first chapter Eruvin; Rav Nachman is difficult; Ra'osh answered beginning deals when it was not reshut ha-yachid through this — but Rav Nachman deals when reshut ha-yachid is made through it; thereby precise that baraita cites mats spread to reshus ha-rabbim because of the end — clear; thereby Tur, Ra'osh, Ran in R' Tam's name, and Shulchan Aruch agree: wherever through partition reshut ha-yachid was made — fell weekday, returned on Shabbat intentionally forbidden, unintentionally permitted (this is Rav Nachman who said only for throwing, etc., dealing with intentional);
