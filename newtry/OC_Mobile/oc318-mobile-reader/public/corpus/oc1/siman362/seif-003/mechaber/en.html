A partition standing by itself — that is, which was not made for the sake of a partition — is valid like a partition made on Shabbat is valid; and this applies when it was made on Shabbat unintentionally; but if intentionally it becomes a partition to be stringent — to render liable one who throws from reshus ha-rabbim into it, but not to permit carrying within it; and this applies when there was no partition there initially; but if there was and he removed it and it returned and was made, even intentionally it returns to its original permission — such as two or three who surrounded themselves with mats in reshus ha-rabbim and also separated between themselves with mats and joined in an eruv together — they are permitted to carry from one to the other; if the mats rolled up they became forbidden; if they returned and were spread out, even intentionally they return to their original permission:
