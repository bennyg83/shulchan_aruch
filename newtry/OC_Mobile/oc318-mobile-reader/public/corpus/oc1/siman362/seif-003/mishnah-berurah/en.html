(25) Mats rolled up — around them and partitions nullified:
(26) Even intentional, etc. — majority of poskim: partition through which reshut ha-yachid was made on Shabbat — we do not say return to original permission when made intentionally; only if already reshut ha-yachid with partitions and partition in middle divided dwellings and each joined eruv alone — partitions between prevent one forbidding the other — when fell they forbade each other — if returned even intentionally each may carry in his courtyard — other does not forbid him; but if even before Shabbat partition in reshus ha-rabbim made reshut ha-yachid here — fell and returned intentionally — does not help make reshut ha-yachid again to carry within — that touches d'oraisa reshut ha-yachid; unlike first case where original partitions only for rabbinic prohibition — one forbidding the other without partition — for that the partition helps:
