The laws of permitting vows on Shabbat. And in it are 3
seifim: They permit vows on Shabbat if they are for Shabbat need, such as if one vowed not to eat or not to drink — even though he had leisure to annul them before Shabbat. But the husband may annul his wife's vows even if they are not for Shabbat need, because if he does not annul them for her today he will no longer be able to annul them:
