1) [Seif 1] Vows may be released on Shabbos, etc. This applies specifically when they concern a Shabbos need, such as a vow not to eat or drink. Vows unrelated to Shabbos may not be released, because why should one perform today something unnecessary that can be done tomorrow? Levush. In Yoreh De'ah, siman 228, seif 3, the reason given is the prohibition of pursuing one's weekday affairs.
2) There: Vows may be released on Shabbos, etc. When releasing a vow for a Shabbos need, one says only 'It is permitted to you' three times and nothing more, because this is legally sufficient, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, siman 228, seif 3; see there. The additional language included in the weekday formula of release is not recited on Shabbos because it is unnecessary for Shabbos.
3) There: If they are needed for Shabbos. A vow may likewise be released for any mitzvah purpose even when it is not needed for Shabbos, because it concerns the affairs of Heaven. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1; Chayei Adam, klal 60, paragraph 16.
