1) [Seif 1] Vows may be released on Shabbos, etc. This applies specifically when they concern a Shabbos need, such as a vow not to eat or drink. Vows unrelated to Shabbos may not be released, because why should one perform today something unnecessary that can be done tomorrow? Levush. In Yoreh De'ah, siman 228, seif 3, the reason given is the prohibition of pursuing one's weekday affairs.
