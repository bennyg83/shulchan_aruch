[1] Not to eat, etc. This does not mean only refraining from food generally, but a vow not to eat a particular food or drink, or not to wear a particular garment needed that day, as stated by Maharshal on the Semag.
[2] His wife's vows, etc. The same applies to a father annulling his daughter's vow. See Yoreh Deah siman 234, seif 27. Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah writes that when someone was placed under a ban in a dream, they acted in practice and released him from the ban on Shabbos.
