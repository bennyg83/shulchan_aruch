2) There: Vows may be released on Shabbos, etc. When releasing a vow for a Shabbos need, one says only 'It is permitted to you' three times and nothing more, because this is legally sufficient, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, siman 228, seif 3; see there. The additional language included in the weekday formula of release is not recited on Shabbos because it is unnecessary for Shabbos.
