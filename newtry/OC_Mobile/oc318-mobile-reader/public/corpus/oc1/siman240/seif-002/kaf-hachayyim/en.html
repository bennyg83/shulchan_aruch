2) One who is not a Torah scholar may engage in marital relations on weekday nights, but not during the day, and even at night only after midnight. Torah scholars are forbidden to engage in marital relations on weekdays and are permitted only on Shabbos night, also specifically after midnight. Before midnight on Shabbos night has the same status as after midnight on weekday nights, except that it is now more fragrant on Shabbos night than during the week; nevertheless, it was permitted only for an am haaretz. Yom Tov and Rosh Chodesh nights have the same law as Shabbos nights, and even Torah scholars are permitted after midnight, although they do not possess as much holiness as Shabbos night. The same applies to the night of a mitzvah immersion even if it is a weekday. Shaar HaMitzvos there; see there for the mystical reason. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 73a; Pri Etz Chaim, gate 16, chapter 11, and gate 18, chapter 18. It is obvious that all this applies to one who secludes himself with his Creator and does not come to improper thoughts if he waits for the stated times. If his inclination overpowers him and he comes to improper thoughts, Heaven forbid, he may act when necessary, especially during pregnancy and nursing, when no soul is drawn to a child, as stated in the following paragraph. Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 4, writes the same.
