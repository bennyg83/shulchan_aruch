As stated above. This follows from Rashi’s words. See Taz and Magen Avraham, seif katan 24, and Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 19. If the lamp is in another room, it is certainly permitted when he darkens the area with his garment. Without doing so, if the light shines directly upon them it is forbidden; if not, it is permitted, like moonlight, as cited by Magen Avraham, seif katan 24. See Taz, Yoreh Deah, siman 282, seif katan 4, who forbids sitting on a chest containing sacred books even if it holds forty seah, because we rule like Rabbi in Eruvin 30a that a movable tent is not considered a tent. Accordingly, here too, for relations, a large chest does not help unless the books are covered by a vessel within a vessel. See Magen Avraham here, seif katan 19. One may distinguish between a movable tent not serving as a barrier against corpse impurity and its serving as a separate domain. This requires study. The remaining matters will, with Hashem’s help, be explained in siman 241.
