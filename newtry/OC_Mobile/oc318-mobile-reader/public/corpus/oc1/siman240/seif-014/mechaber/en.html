Keriat zera is the strength of the body and the light of the eyes; and whenever it comes out in excess the body is wasted and his life is lost; and everyone who is excessive in relations — old age springs upon him and his strength fails and his eyes dim and a bad odor wafts from his mouth and the hair of his head and eyebrows and eyelashes fall out and the hair of his beard and mustache and leg hair increase and his teeth fall out and many pains come upon him except for these that come upon him. The Sages, the physicians, said: one in a thousand dies from other illnesses and the thousand from excessive use; therefore a person must be careful:
