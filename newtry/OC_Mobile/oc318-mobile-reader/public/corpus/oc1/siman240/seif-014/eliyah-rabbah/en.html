A partition, etc. The Olas Tamid wrote that this is not specifically by means of a built wall; even a mere curtain permits. The Magen Avraham wrote that if there are holes and windows like latticework, it is forbidden until the books are covered. From what he wrote at the beginning of siman 315 it seems somewhat that it is permitted. Therefore he explained that what he wrote there is that only a partition ten handbreadths high is needed, and even if the books are standing exposed above the partition, it is permitted to urinate there, for it is considered as if he is in another domain, end quote. This requires study, for there is a distinction between excrement in glass and ervah in siman 75, seif 5; and in Teshuvas Shaar Ephraim, siman 119, he prohibits. However, the fact that relations are also permitted with a partition of ten handbreadths, as the Tur wrote, implies that this is so even if it is exposed above, and this requires study. Know that if they are exposed above, one must tie the partition so that the wind will not move it. But when the books are covered in these manners, this is not necessary, and even a partition of less than ten handbreadths suffices. It appears that the same law applies below regarding a partition for candlelight.
