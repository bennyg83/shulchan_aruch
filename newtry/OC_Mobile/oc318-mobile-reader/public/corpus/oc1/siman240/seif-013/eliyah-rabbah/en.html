The Olas Tamid wrote that one should be uncertain whether a deaf person is comparable to a minor who does not know how to speak, who is like an animal. All this is according to the law, but as a trait of piety one should be careful even in front of an animal, as we say that Abaye would drive the flies away from the bed. Many sages were also careful even before mice, end quote. It is obvious that all this discusses at night or in a dark place, and so Tosafot wrote in Niddah 17b.
