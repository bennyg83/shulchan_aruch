(49) A guest, etc.: This applies even when all members of the household are asleep.
(50) If they designated, etc.: Meaning, the homeowner has no use for that place and it is designated exclusively for the couple. The guest is then considered like the homeowner.
(51) A house: The same applies to a room.
(52) In the homeowner’s garment: Lest he experience a seminal emission upon it. Eruvin 63a states that one who sleeps in a room in which a man and his wife are lodging is addressed by the verse, “You drive the women of My people from their pleasant homes,” even when she is a niddah. Rabbi Akiva Eiger cites this.
