(א) And even with partition — he senses when another uses the bed nearby; no modesty. If situation where they do not sense at all — permitted with partition [Acharonim].

(ב) Made as scrolls — written on parchment like a Torah scroll but containing only a chumash; same for other holy writings in scroll form.

(ג) Until there is etc. — even placed in chest with cover on chest helps for tefillin and other books; for Sefer Torah forbidden until blocking partition; with partition permitted even not in chest.

(ד) Partition — curtain around bed is not a partition [even if tall and book not visible with Torah in chest with many covers still forbidden] since curtain moves. Magen Avraham: holes like lattice — forbidden until books covered from sight; Nehar Shalom doubts; in pressing need may rely; l'chatchila cover especially Sefer Torah.

(ה) Another house — another room.

(ו) Tefillin — same for mezuzah; see above siman 40 Mishna Berurah for details.

(ז) Or books — twenty-four holy books not in scroll form; if scroll form law like Sefer Torah as above.

(ח) Even Gemara — although Gemara was not written in early generations because oral matters forbidden in writing, since Amoraim permitted writing due to 'it is time to act for Heaven' it is like other holy books; today all written or printed books have holiness [Peri Megadim].

(ט) Until placed in vessel within vessel — even without partition like Sefer Torah still need double vessel. Curtain before bed not partition since not tied and moves — not interruption for Sefer Torah; if books not visible through cover may suffice as double cover.

(י) Designated vessel — even hundred count as one.

(כ) Tallit on chest — counts as vessel within vessel.

(ל) See siman 40 for details.
