A partition. It appears to me that if it has holes and openings like latticework, although for the laws of tumah it is considered a partition, as stated in Ohalos, chapter 10, here it is forbidden until the sacred books are concealed from view and cannot be seen. What is written at the beginning of siman 315 somewhat implies that it is permitted.
Another house. Meaning, another room.
Or sacred books. It is obvious that a mezuzah must likewise be covered with a vessel within a vessel. See Yoreh Deah, siman 286.
Even books of Talmud. Meaning, although the Oral Torah was not permitted to be written in their era, since in our time it was permitted to be written, such books have the same status as other sacred books.
A chest. If the chest is large enough to hold forty seah, measuring one amah by one amah and three amos high, it constitutes a separate domain. This requires study. Responsa Rama, siman 34. It appears to me that one may be lenient, since even regarding Torah-level tumah we are lenient, as I wrote in siman 314.
