(א) Partition — if holes and windows like lattice — for tumah counts as partition but here forbidden until books covered from sight — see there; responsum Sha'ar Efrayim 119; Even HaEzer 25 from Shach.

(ב) Other — another room.

(ג) Or books — obviously mezuzah too in vessel within vessel.

(ד) Chest — if holds forty seah and one by one by three cubits height divides domain; see siman 314; Chavat Yair 184 curtain not partition since it moves; if impossible otherwise permitted in room with books at onah time and immersion night and journey departure/return; see siman 315.

(ה) Or books — even Gemara forbidden until vessel within vessel not designated for them.
