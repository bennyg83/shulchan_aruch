(6) Shall be in niduy — Tosafot Kol HaYad not niduy to himself but fit for niduy; Rashba Ramban R' Yehuda; EH 340 Shulchan Aruch/Levush: in niduy means sitting in it — when emitted seed; here not emitting seed not niduy to self but fit; Lechem Chamudot Kol HaYad; see above siman 3 seif 16; Shelah 99 this responsum. Also warned two males not one bed; not near strange youth; Chalakhat Mechokek 24 marry sons young before urges; groom not with bride until night of relations.
