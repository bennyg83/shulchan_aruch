He shall be under nidui, etc. Tosafot in the chapter Kol HaYad explain that he is not automatically excommunicated, but is fit to be excommunicated. So wrote the Rashba, Ramban, and Ri. In Even HaEzer, where the Shulchan Aruch and Levush wrote that he sits in nidui, implying that he is automatically excommunicated, one can say that there it discusses where he emitted seed. But here, where he deliberately caused an erection, as long as he does not emit seed he is not automatically under nidui, but is fit to be excommunicated. See Lechem Chamudos on the chapter Kol HaYad, and above siman 3, seif katan 16, and see the Shelah, page 99, from this responsum. He further warned that two male children should not lie in one bed, nor next to a strange young man; and so ruled the Chelkas Mechokek in siman 24. The Shelah further warned to marry off sons in their youth before they come to sinful thoughts, and afterward they should learn in purity. He further warned that the groom should not sit next to the bride. He further wrote that the groom should not lie next to the bride until the night when he intends to consummate the marriage. He further wrote: I saw a proper person who was accustomed to immerse on the day that his wife immersed, in order to draw sanctity above at the time of union.
