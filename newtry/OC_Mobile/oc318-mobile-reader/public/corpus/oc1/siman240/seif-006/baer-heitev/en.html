(א) Partition. And if there are holes and windows like a lattice — although for tumah they count as a partition, here it is forbidden until the books are covered from sight so they are not visible — Magen Avraham there. And see responsum Sha'ar Efrayim siman 119. And see Even HaEzer siman 25 seif kaf heh from Shach.

(ב) Other. Meaning another room.

(ג) Or books. And it is obvious that the mezuzah too must be covered in a vessel within a vessel.

(ד) Chest. And if the chest is large, holding forty seah and is one cubit by one cubit in height three cubits, it divides a domain for itself — Magen Avraham there; and see siman 314. And see responsum Chavat Yair siman 184 that a curtain around the books or around the bed is not called a partition since it moves; and if impossible otherwise, it is permitted to have marital relations in the room where the books are at the time of onah and the night of her immersion and the night of his departure and return from a journey. And see Magen Avraham siman 315.
