It is forbidden for a person to use his bed before any adult person, even by means of breaking a partition of ten [tefachim]; and before a minor who does not know to speak — it is permitted. A house in which there is a Sefer Torah or Chumashim made as scrolls — it is forbidden to use his bed in it until there should be before him a partition [and regarding making it on Shabbos see above at the beginning of siman 315]. And if he has another room — it is forbidden until he removes it. And if there are tefillin or books in it, even of Gemara — it is forbidden until he puts them in a vessel within a vessel, provided the inner one is not designated for them [and see above siman 40 seif 2]; but if it is designated for them, even one hundred are considered as one.
