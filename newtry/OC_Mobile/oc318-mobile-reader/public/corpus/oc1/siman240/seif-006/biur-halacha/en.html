(א) Awake — and before one who is asleep it is permitted even without breaking a partition of ten; and nevertheless l'chatchila it is good where people sleep in the house to set a partition around the bed [called paraavn].

(ב) House with Sefer Torah — Gemara implies danger if one transgresses this.

(ג) Until partition before them — from Shulchan Aruch implication: with partition interrupting, even if Torah scroll stands high and visible from afar — permitted; Eliyah Rabbah questions; for Sefer Torah certainly stringent to cover [only when partition fixed not moving by wind like paravan; curtain around bed not partition unless tied below; covering helps for Torah; other books may be lenient when full partition — Chayei Adam 128].

(ד) Forbidden until he removes it — if door open and visible from afar requires study; even per Magen Avraham seif 96 stringent when partition like lattice because books visible; here stronger since another room entirely no disgrace; Nehar Shalom lenient; practically other books may rely on leniency; Sefer Torah cover there; Petach Einayim EH 282; one who relies on Nehar Shalom need not be protested.

(ה) Until placed in vessels — no difference books below or on shelf if same room without partition — Magen Avraham end seif 3 siman 315; Chavat Yair 184; Petach Einayim EH 282.
