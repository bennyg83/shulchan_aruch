(46) Except, etc.: If his inclination overcomes him and there is concern that he may waste seed, Eliyah Rabbah cites Sefer Divrei David, siman 574, as permitting relations. Beis Meir to Even HaEzer rules likewise.
(47) Who has no children: Meaning, he has not yet fulfilled the mitzvah of procreation. Therefore, even if he has sons, if he does not yet have a daughter, it is permitted.
(48) See below, siman 574: Meaning, the Mechaber permits it there also on the night of immersion. See the later authorities there.
