For the purpose of a mitzvah, etc. In Yevamos 62b the Gemara states the reason: because they are preoccupied. Rashi explains: he is preoccupied with the mitzvah; alternatively, he may become preoccupied with relations and neglect the mitzvah, end quote. See siman 248, the beginning of siman 415, and the beginning of siman 536. The Bach wrote that for commerce it is called optional activity, and the Olas Tamid inferred this from his words; but he did not examine properly, for the Bach wrote this explicitly.
