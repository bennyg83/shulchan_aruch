4) Shaar HaMitzvos writes there: I heard that my teacher, of blessed memory, would remain awake every night of a mitzvah immersion until midnight and engage in Torah. Afterward, he would engage in marital relations, then recite Krias Shema and sleep. His reason was to avoid entirely coming to improper thoughts in bed and perhaps to a seminal emission, Heaven forbid. It is obvious that the statement that he afterward recited Krias Shema and slept means that he wished to fall asleep amid words of Torah. He certainly recited Krias Shema al HaMitah at its proper time before midnight, in order to direct its intentions and effect at midnight the rectification produced by Krias Shema al HaMitah. The glosses to Shaar HaMitzvos there write the same. It further appears to me that after midnight he also recited Birkas HaShachar and Tikkun Chatzos, studied a little, afterward engaged in marital relations, and then recited Krias Shema and slept, so that he would not taste the taste of death, as explained in our words above in siman 1, paragraphs 7 and 10.
