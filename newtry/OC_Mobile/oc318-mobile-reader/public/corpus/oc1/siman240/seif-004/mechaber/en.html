It is forbidden to look at that place, for whoever looks there has no shame in his face and transgresses 'and walk modestly with your Heaven,' and he removes shame from his face — for whoever is ashamed does not sin, as it is written 'and in reverence for Him will be on your faces' — this is shame 'so that you not sin'; and further because he arouses the evil impulse in himself; and all the more so one who kisses there — he transgresses all these, and further he transgresses 'do not make yourselves abominable':
