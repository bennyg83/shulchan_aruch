One who has relations on a bed on which a baby sleeps — that baby is afflicted. And we stated this only when the baby is not a year old; but when the baby is a year old — we have no concern about it. And we stated this only when the baby sleeps at its feet [meaning sleeps at its feet]; but when it sleeps at its head — we have no concern about it.
