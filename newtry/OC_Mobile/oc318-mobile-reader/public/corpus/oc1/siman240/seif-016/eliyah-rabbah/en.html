(16) Male children — Tur; EH 25 Raavad; I checked Ba'alei Nefesh — wrote beautiful children not specifically male; from Nedarim end R' Eliezer uncover/cover tefach. R' David Abudraham: first emission like earth second seed; male if man emits first. Sefer HaToldot: seven openings three right three left one middle — position determines sex. Shelah 103: remember holy ancestors; Levi read from Shemot names; water vessel wash before/after; study this siman before union. Mechaber apron — Chasidim served naked; apron different less covered; both willing is modesty (Magen Avraham).
