[Levush] That she did so, etc. Although she said, 'come to me,' nevertheless this is modest language that can bear the interpretation, 'come to me, to my room.' For she should have said, 'lie with me,' as it is stated, 'therefore he shall lie with you tonight.' But an immodest woman is one who explicitly speaks of relations in her words, Perishah. In the Rambam, chapter 15 of Hilchos Ishus, this is his wording: she should not demand relations with her mouth, and she should not speak about this matter, end quote. If she desires him, he should first speak with her about relations, for if she demands it the children will not be good, Sefer Chasidim 516. Tosafot at the end of the first chapter of Niddah wrote that it is forbidden to have relations with one who is sleeping; the Beit Yosef brings this in Yoreh De'ah siman 184. So wrote the Rambam in chapter 5 of Hilchos Dei'os.
