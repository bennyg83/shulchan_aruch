(א) Anusah. Meaning she is not literally coerced but is unwilling; therefore he should appease her and afterward have relations.

(ב) Senuah. At the time of relations; but if she is willing at the time of relations, even though she is hated, it is permitted — Tur.

(ג) Temurah. Even if both are his wives — e.g. he intended for this one and the other happened to him — Tur. This requires further study, for did not Yaakov intend for Rachel and came upon Leah? See Magen Avraham.

(ד) Moredet. For she said 'I do not want you,' yet he has relations with her even though she is willing at the time of relations.

(ה) Shikorah. She or he.

(ו) Gerushat halev. That in his mind he will divorce her, even though he loves her — such as those whom they force to divorce her.

(ז) Erevuvia. E.g. he set his mind on another woman, as written in seif 2.

(ח) Chatzufah. That she demanded him with her mouth. But if she adorns herself and pleases him so he pays attention to her, these are proper children.
