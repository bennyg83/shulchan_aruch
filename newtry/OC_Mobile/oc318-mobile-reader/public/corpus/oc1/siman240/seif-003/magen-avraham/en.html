(א) Anusah — even if not literally coerced but unwilling; therefore appease and afterward have relations.

(ב) Senuah — at time of relations; if willing at that time even if hated — permitted — Tur.

(ג) Temurah — even if both are his wives, e.g. he intended for this one and the other occurred (Tur). This requires further study — did not Yaakov intend for Rachel and come upon Leah? And in siman 248 he wrote therefore Reuven's tribe did not take a portion in Eretz Yisrael only beyond the Jordan; nevertheless this requires further study that they should Heaven forbid be among rebels and transgressors, especially since she conceived from this union as Tosafot Yevamot 76 wrote; one must say specifically when he saw Rachel lying in the bed and intended for that body and then Leah happened beneath him; but when Yaakov entered the chuppah he saw Leah and intended for her body, only he thought her name was Rachel — we have no concern; and similar division in Sanhedrin regarding intending to kill one and killing another.

(ד) Moredet — she said 'I do not want you' yet he has relations when she is willing at the time.

(ה) Shikorah — she or he.

(ו) Gerushat halev — he intends to divorce though he loves her — such as those forced to divorce.

(ז) Erevuvia — e.g. set mind on another as seif 2.

(ח) Chatzufah — she demanded with her mouth; if she adorns and pleases so he pays attention — proper children.
