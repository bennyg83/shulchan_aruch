(א) Anusah — even if not literally coerced but unwilling because of anger she has toward him; therefore appease and afterward have relations.

(ב) Senuah — at time of relations; if then willing even if hated — permitted [Tur].

(ג) Temurah — even both his wives; e.g. intended for one and the other occurred.

(ד) Moredet — she said she does not want him to have relations yet he does when she is willing at the time.

(ה) Shikorah — he or she is drunk; appears specifically when mind is confused from drunkenness, not merely drinking a quarter-log [Peri Megadim].

(ו) Gerushat halev — he intends to divorce though he loves her — such as those forced to divorce.

(ז) Erevuvia — e.g. sets mind on another as seif 2.

(ח) Chatzufah — she demands with her mouth; but if she adorns and pleases so he pays attention — on the contrary then obligated in onah as above seif 1 and proper children. All these matters require care even when pregnant or old and not fit to bear [Peri Megadim]. Acharonim wrote to beware coming upon his wife while she sleeps.
