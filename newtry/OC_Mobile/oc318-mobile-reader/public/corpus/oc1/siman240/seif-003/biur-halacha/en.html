Sons of niduy — meaning when he or she were then in niduy; and likewise when he or she were in mourning [and what is stated in Yoreh De'ah that it is permitted to come upon his wife when she is a mourner and does not know — as long as she does not know it is not a prohibition]. And some have the girsa sons of niddah — that even though the child is not a mamzer from this, nevertheless there is blemish; and of them Scripture said 'and I will purge from you' etc. [Peri Megadim].
