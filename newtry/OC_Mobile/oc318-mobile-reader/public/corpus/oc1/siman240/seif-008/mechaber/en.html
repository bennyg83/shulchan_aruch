And he should have relations with awe and fear, as was said of R' Eliezer that he would reveal a handbreadth and cover a handbreadth — meaning with awe and fear as though a demon forced him; meaning as though a demon coerced him. And there are those who explain: he would reveal a handbreadth and cover a handbreadth — that he would not delay upon the organ during relations in order to minimize pleasure, and it is similar to as though a demon coerced him, that he does the act under coercion. And there are those who explain: he would reveal a handbreadth in the woman — meaning now he reveals it for the need of relations, and now he covers it — meaning he would not prolong in that act, and it is similar to when a demon comes and is frightened and leaves the act — so much would he shorten in relations. And there are those who explain: he would reveal a handbreadth on the sash that she would gird with it — that even during relations he would require her to gird it, and he would reveal only a handbreadth from it and cover immediately in order to minimize his pleasure.
