He should shorten relations. This is difficult, for the Sages said that as a reward for delaying themselves upon the abdomen they merit sons. Raavad answered that all actions performed for the sake of Heaven are good. One who knows that he can prolong relations without entertaining another thought, and delays so that his wife will derive pleasure and emit seed first, is rewarded by the Holy One, blessed is He, with sons. One who does not trust himself and hastens in order to escape sin likewise performs a mitzvah, and the Holy One, blessed is He, grants him sons.
He would require her to gird him. This requires study, for at the end of Even HaEzer, siman 76, it states that one who says, “I will have relations only while I am clothed and she is clothed,” must divorce her and pay the kesubah, because direct bodily contact is required. Sefer Chassidim, siman 570, records that one pious man had relations unclothed for this reason, and also so that semen should not adhere to the garment. One may answer that an apron is different, since the body is not so fully covered. Alternatively, if both consent, it is a measure of modesty. Tosafos, Niddah 17a, cite Vayikra Rabbah that the Holy One, blessed is He, hates one who has relations unclothed.
