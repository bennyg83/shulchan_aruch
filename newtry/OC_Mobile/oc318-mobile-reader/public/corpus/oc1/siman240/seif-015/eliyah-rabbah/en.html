The Sefer Chasidim wrote that it is forbidden to mention the Name or anything sacred while semen is still upon him; rather, he should rinse with water the places where he soiled himself. The Damesek Eliezer, page 326, wrote that one who has relations and does not wash his hands, his ways are corrupted, meaning that the seed is corrupted. The same applies to one who does not wash that place after relations. See above siman 76, seif 4.
