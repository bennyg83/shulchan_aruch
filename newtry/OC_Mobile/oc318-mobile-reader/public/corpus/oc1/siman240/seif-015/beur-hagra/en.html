(א) Siman 15 — he should not have relations when sated or hungry. So explained the commentators from what is written 'everyone who places his bed between north and south.'

(ב) And not on the day he enters the bathhouse, and not on the day of bloodletting. Gittin there.

(ג) And not on the day he departs on a journey or on the day he returns from a journey. There 'one who comes' etc.; and there 'in their order a dead person who is not' etc.; and even some of them harm — nevertheless even for a healthy person see there.
