(53) And not on the day of bloodletting: One who was ill and recovered should refrain from relations until his body regains strength, for relations may cause him pain and renewed illness, and he may not realize that he is endangering himself. Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Tzavaas HaYesharim.
(54) While traveling: Magen Avraham writes that here the concern is medical, whereas in seif 1 the discussion is a matter of law. This is evident because the Mechaber also says not to have relations on the day one enters the bathhouse, nor before or after these events. Yet it is known that it is a mitzvah to bathe every Friday, while the conjugal interval of Torah scholars is from Shabbos to Shabbos. Necessarily, all these cautions are only medical. One who leaves a fixed privy, meaning one with a seat, as opposed to a temporary privy, should not have relations until at least the time needed to walk half a mil has passed, because the spirit of the privy clings to him. Gittin 70a. Seder HaYom writes similarly in the name of the Zohar, though he says not to have relations for that entire hour. Yad Efrayim explains that the Zohar does not mean a literal hour, but merely some period of time. Bedek HaBayis cites the Zohar, and the later authorities bring it, that a nursing woman should have relations only while the child sleeps, and after relations should not nurse until the time needed to walk two mil has passed. If the child cries, she should wait at least the time needed to walk one mil. A woman who delays immersion in order to distress her husband commits a grave sin and causes many evils. Zohar. Sefer Chassidim writes that one may not mention Hashem’s name or recite sacred words while semen remains on his body; he should first wash the soiled areas. The works of mussar write that one should always keep water near the bed and wash the hands before and after relations. If one awakens from a dream with an erection, he should be careful not to have relations then, for the children may be spiritually blemished, Heaven forbid. Even if she is pregnant, she may give birth to harmful spirits. Arizal. One should be careful not to place a sacred book on a bed upon which one sleeps, especially with one’s wife, since semen may be present there.
