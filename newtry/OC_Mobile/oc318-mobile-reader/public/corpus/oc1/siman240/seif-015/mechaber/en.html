He should not have relations when he is sated or hungry except when the food in his intestines has been digested; and not standing or sitting; and not in the bathhouse; and not on the day he enters the bathhouse; and not on the day of bloodletting; and not on the day of departure on a journey or on the day of return from a journey — and not before them and not after them. [And that which is stated at the beginning of the siman that he is obligated to visit her — that speaks when he is riding or sitting in a carriage; and here it speaks when walking on foot] [Maharshal]:[ ]:
