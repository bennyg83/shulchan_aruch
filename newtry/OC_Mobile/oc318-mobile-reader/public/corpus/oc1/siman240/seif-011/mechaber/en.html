It is forbidden to have relations by candlelight, even if he darkens with his tallit;but if he makes a partition ten tefachim high before the candle (Hagahot Maimoniot ch. 3 Hilchot Yom Tov), even if the light is visible through the partition,] such as if interrupted by a sheet — it is permitted; so it appears to me from Rashi's words in chapter 2 of Beitzah 22a; they also say there it is permitted when one places a vessel over the candle; whether such a partition may be made on Shabbat see beginning of siman 315}.
