(א) Visible. And the same if the light is visible above the partition — permitted; nevertheless one needs darkening with a tallit, for after the room is full of light it is no worse than daytime — Yeshuot Yaakov Beitzah siman 6. And in Kavanot he also wrote that one should not see light, for it causes harm to children even if she is pregnant; and if the moon shines on them directly it is forbidden, but if it shines on the house and not on them there is not so much concern — Knesset HaGedolah forbids. But if they are under the open sky, even in moon shadow it is forbidden — see Magen Avraham.

(ב) In his tallit. One does not rely on this except for great need of a Torah scholar — meaning when his urge overcomes him; and in Ketubot 65a it implies that in a dark house it is permitted l'chatchila — Magen Avraham.
