Seif 11, in the gloss. A partition ten tefachim high. See Tiferes LeMoshe, Yoreh Deah, siman 341, who writes that a curtain hanging before the bed, since it does not withstand an ordinary wind, is ineffective as a partition for having relations in the presence of a Sefer Torah. It helps only with respect to a lamp and other sacred books, where it functions as a covering. See Magen Avraham below, siman 315, seif 3.
