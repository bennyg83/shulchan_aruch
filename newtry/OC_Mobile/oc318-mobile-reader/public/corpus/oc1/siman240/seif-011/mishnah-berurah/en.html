(39) By candlelight: Even if there is only a little light, such as when the candle burns inside a tube or lantern and only some light emerges, it is forbidden unless a partition blocks it. The same applies to moonlight. This is specifically when the moonlight shines directly upon them. If it does not shine upon them, even though it illuminates the house, it is permitted, though it is proper to darken the area with his garment. If they are under the open sky, it is forbidden even when the moonlight does not shine directly upon them and they are merely in its shadow. This applies even if partitions surround them, for in an exposed place without partitions relations are forbidden in any event, as explained in Even HaEzer, siman 25, seif 4.
(40) A partition ten tefachim high: This refers to one tied at the bottom so that it does not move in an ordinary wind. Otherwise it does not have the status of a partition.
(41) Although the light is visible, etc.: The same applies if the light is visible above the partition. It is permitted because the partition is complete. All this applies specifically when he also darkens the area with his garment. Without that, it is forbidden, for since the room is full of light it is no better than daytime. Some are stringent even when he darkens with his garment, since the light is nevertheless visible in the room.
(42) Through the partition: All the more so, if the lamp is in another room and illuminates this room, it is permitted. Chochmas Adam writes that he must nevertheless darken the area with his garment.
(43) When he covers it with a vessel: See below, at the end of siman 277, in Taz and Mishnah Berurah there.
(44) By day: For this is not a modest manner.
(45) And a Torah scholar: Since he is modest in his ways and will not come to look, it is permitted when he darkens the area with his garment. Nevertheless, one should be lenient only in a case of great need, namely when his inclination overcomes him.
