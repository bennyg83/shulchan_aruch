(א) By candlelight — and even if there is only a little light, such as when the candle burns inside a lamp-shade in which the candle is placed, or when it slightly illuminates through an opening [when there is no partition that blocks]; and the same applies to moonlight — it is also forbidden; and specifically when the moonlight shines directly upon them; but if it does not shine upon them, even though it illuminates the house — it is permitted [and nevertheless it is proper that he darken with his tallit]. And if they are under the open sky, even if the moonlight does not literally shine upon them but they are in its shadow — nevertheless it is forbidden. And this is even if there are partitions around them; for in an exposed place where there are no partitions it is forbidden to have relations, as is explained in Even HaEzer siman 25 seif 4:

(ב) A partition ten [tefachim] high — and it speaks when he tied it from below so it will not sway in an ordinary wind, for otherwise without this there is no partition law upon it:

(ג) Even though the light is visible etc. — and the same applies if the light is visible above the partition — it is permitted since it is a complete partition. And all this is specifically when he darkens with his tallit; but without this it is forbidden, for since the room is full of light it is no better than by day; and there are those who are stringent even through darkening with a tallit since nevertheless the light is visible in that room:

(ד) In the way of the partition — and all the more so if the candle is in another room and illuminates this room — it is permitted; and Chachmat Adam wrote that nevertheless darkening with a tallit is required:

(ה) When he covers with a vessel — see below at the end of siman 577 in Taz and Mishna Berurah there:

(ו) By day — for this is not the way of modesty:
