(א) And when — Peri Tzedek; so too below seif 9; Maharshal and Magen Avraham seif 29 see there; Levush wrote not before or after them — without vav one may explain as Mechaber: not on day of departure before them means before going, not after them on day of return; in Shulchan Aruch seif 16 'not before them' implies also at night; possibly near veset hour; see Levush here near veset and Yoreh Deah 184; Shach 27; Levush there: some explain his view like first anonymous opinion. See EH 25 and 76 for these laws. If not yet fulfilled procreation — fulfills two mitzvot procreation and Shabbat; if fulfilled procreation — Shabbat only; if she waives onah while procreation fulfilled — helps; without procreation fulfillment does not help — see EH 76; Beit Shmuel EH 1.

(ב) We have a doubt whether disqualifying lineage like Egyptian, Ammonite, mamzer etc. are commanded regarding procreation; Tosafot Gittin 41b s.v. lo that R' Yehuda bar Mordechai wrote procreation language is in context of freedom not slavery, and Shabbat applies to slave too (some in Yerushalmi question; Gaon explains per Tosafot; seemingly procreation is whether mitzvah and concludes yes). Half-slave is disqualifying yet procreation applies in freedom; one may say enactment at emancipation unlike mamzer and other disqualifications; see siman 546 cannot marry during festival — disqualifying not listed there for three reasons mixing joy etc. — see Taz and Magen Avraham; cannot expand here; see 339 and 524.
