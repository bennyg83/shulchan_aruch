(א) In the Torah. One must fulfill onah even during days of pregnancy and nursing. Regarding winter days they asked the Ari z"l whether one is exempt from onah when it is cold season because he must immerse for keri, and also when it is time of her pregnancy and nursing; he answered that if the woman waives, he is exempt, but nevertheless it is good to fulfill it; and they testified about him that he did not fulfill the mitzvah of onah on those days — Sefer HaKavanot. And see Shulchan Aruch Even HaEzer siman 1 seif kaf alef.

(ב) The idle. Healthy and indulgent men. Rambam. And it implies in the Gemara even if they are sons of Torah.

(ג) To Shabbat night. And the same applies on Rosh Chodesh and festivals. And a man should not say 'I will make myself like a Torah scholar,' for it is written 'and her onah he shall not diminish' — Sefer HaKaneh.

(ד) Her immersion. Even not at the time of her onah. It is good to say before union the psalm 'A psalm of David, there is my shepherd, I shall not want' etc. And all the warnings mentioned regarding union from Shabbat to Shabbat refer to one who has relations for the sake of conception — Ari z"l. And one should not have relations two nights of Rosh Hashanah; one who is careful on the ten days of repentance — Knesset HaGedolah. And the Ari z"l wrote that one should not have relations on erev Shabbat, the night of Shavuot, and the first night of Passover unless on the night of immersion. And the seventh night of Passover is permitted for union — Sefer HaKavanot.

(ה) On a journey. See seif 16.
