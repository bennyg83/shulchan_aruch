(א) He should not be too accustomed — see Ramban on the Chumash Parashat Kedoshim at the beginning:

(ב) Rather at the onah etc. — and one must fulfill the onah even when she is pregnant or nursing. And he should not nullify her onah except with her consent when she waives to him and he already fulfilled peru urevu:

(ג) That which is stated in the Torah — and all this is for one whose body is healthy; but one who is not healthy is not obligated except according to what they estimate he can fulfill [Even HaEzer siman 76 seif 3]:

(ד) The travelers — healthy and prosperous people [Rambam]; and even if they are sons of Torah — all this when he is not a Torah scholar [Eliyah Rabbah and so too Beit Shmuel in Even HaEzer siman 76 that a Torah scholar whose Torah diminishes his strength in every way — from erev Shabbat to erev Shabbat; and so in responsum Maharatz siman 51]; and see below in seif kaf vav:

(ה) The camel drivers — who bring bundles on camels from a distant place:

(ו) A Torah scholar — that on weekdays they must engage in Torah at night, for Torah is their craft, and a person should not say 'I will make myself like a Torah scholar,' for it is written 'and her onah he shall not diminish' [Magen Avraham in the name of Sefer HaKaneh] — meaning to nullify the onah not with her permission; but with her permission blessing will come upon him [Peri Megadim]; and see there that he is uncertain in our time whether one is called a Torah scholar for this; and nevertheless he sides that since per the generation he is a Torah scholar and he married accordingly — there is no onah obligation except on Shabbat night; and see in Biur Halacha:

(ז) From Friday night etc. — there are those who wrote that the same applies on Rosh Chodesh and a festival. And seemingly per the reason of Magen Avraham mentioned above in seif kaf vav that they must engage in Torah at night — it does not apply to obligate him on Rosh Chodesh; and see in Biur Halacha. One should not have relations on the first night of Passover and the night of Shavuot and the two days of Rosh Hashanah and the night of Shemini Atzeret unless on the night of immersion. And all this is only for a person who is full of fear and will not sin, Heaven forbid; but those whose impulse overcomes them and they think it is like a Torah prohibition and through this come, Heaven forbid, to many stumbling blocks — it is a mitzvah to have relations even on Rosh Hashanah and immerse the next day, for from the side of law there is prohibition only on Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av and in days of mourning of him or her [Chachmat Adam]:

(ח) On the night of her immersion — meaning even not at the time of her onah:

(ט) On a journey — see below in seif samech vav:

(י) Nursing — meaning even though she is nursing or pregnant, nevertheless he is obligated to visit her [Beit Yosef]:

(כ) To visit her — meaning even not at the time of her onah; and the same applies when he goes on a journey, except there her waiver helps [Peri Megadim]:

(ל) Like a man who pays his debt etc. — and he should intend this even during pregnancy and nursing:
