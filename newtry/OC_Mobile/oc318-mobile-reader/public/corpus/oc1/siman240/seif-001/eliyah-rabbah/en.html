That he should not be accustomed, etc. In the Shelah, page 102, he warned that one should study this siman, and Even HaEzer siman 25, and chapter 14 of Reishis Chochmah in Shaar HaKedushah, many times, so that they be fluent in his mouth by heart. The Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah wrote: we have practiced abstinence ourselves on the two nights of Rosh Hashanah, for perhaps the King is sitting on the throne of judgment, and a person will come to have relations with his wife and perhaps intend for his own pleasure, and it will become a snare for him. For this reason, one who is careful about this during the Ten Days of Repentance, may blessing come upon him; and on the night of Hoshana Rabbah, and the three days before Shavuos, and certainly the nights of Shavuos themselves; and from Rosh Chodesh Av until the night of the eleventh of Av. Even if Tishah B'Av was postponed until the tenth, it is proper to refrain on the night of the eleventh, as with the law of meat and wine, end quote. The Olas Tamid wrote about this that his words do not appear correct, to add onto the words of the Sages, except that on the seventh night of Pesach the masters of hidden wisdom wrote to be careful. The Magen Avraham wrote that one should not have relations on the first night of Pesach, the night of Shavuos, or the night of Shemini Atzeres, unless it is the night of immersion, from the writings. From the Baal HaTurim in Parshas Ki Seitzei it appears that everything is permitted, for this is his wording: 'and he shall gladden his wife', the numerical value of the days of 'and he shall gladden' indicates that he is obligated in marital relations all the days of the year except Yom Kippur. They further wrote that when one is aroused with an erection in a dream, he should be careful not to have relations then, Heaven forbid, for the children born through that erection will be blemished; and even if she is pregnant, he brings into being harmful spirits. A person should also be careful that when he leaves the bathroom, he should not have relations for that entire hour. In Seder HaYom he wrote: it appears to me that this is specifically in a fixed bathroom, which has a set place and seat, but not in another place used incidentally. Know that the conjugal obligation must be fulfilled even during pregnancy and nursing. But one who is accustomed to immerse for seminal impurity, and it is a cold season, if she waives it he is exempt from the conjugal obligation, since she is pregnant or nursing.
