And the onah of Torah scholars etc. The reason the Mechaber did not write briefly 'the onah of Torah scholars on Shabbat night' appears from what Ran wrote in chapter Eip — although unspecified Torah scholars have their onah thus, nevertheless a Torah scholar may reduce his onah when his heart is not free because of much study; and Rambam also wrote that only healthy Torah scholars, as will be written below siman 280. Therefore it comes to inform us that a Torah scholar may not add to marital relations except from Shabbat night to Shabbat night. If he had said 'the onah of Torah scholars on Shabbat night' it would imply that on that night alone there is obligatory onah and if he wishes he may add; therefore he wrote 'from Shabbat night to Shabbat night' to teach that only on Shabbat night is obligatory onah and he cannot add.
