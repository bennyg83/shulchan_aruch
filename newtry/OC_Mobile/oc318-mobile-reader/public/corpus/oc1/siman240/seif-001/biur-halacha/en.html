(א) Only at the onah etc. — see Mishnah Berurah that even when she is pregnant or nursing; and see in Even HaRav that even when she is barren or old and can no longer conceive — nevertheless the mitzvah of onah applies see there. And one must also then conduct with holiness, because the kabbalists wrote that even then holy souls are created thereby.

(ב) Torah scholars etc. — see Mishnah Berurah what he wrote in the name of Peri Megadim; and likewise in responsum Maharatz siman 51 that anyone who learns constantly — whether he is among those who teach the public or among students — is permitted not to fulfill his onah except once a week; and even though we do not have the law of Torah scholar in our time for several matters, nevertheless in this he may make himself a Torah scholar. And what Magen Avraham wrote that he is not permitted — that is when he is truly a worker or traveler and is not engaged in Torah; but for those engaged in Torah whose strength is weakened there is no distinction between a Torah scholar in their time and in our time see there. And nevertheless he wrote at the end that he would advise those around him to fulfill the onah of workers twice a week; and all this was brought in Petach Even HaEzer siman 76; and likewise brought in Chayei Adam in the name of the will of R' Yehuda of Prague; and the main thing is everything according to a person's strength, as above in seif kaf gimmel; and see below in siman 14.

(ג) From Friday night to Friday night etc. — see Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote that one should not have relations on the night of one Shavuot and the night of Shavuot etc. See Yeshuot Yaakov who sides that if he has not yet fulfilled peru urevu one need not be stringent in all this.
