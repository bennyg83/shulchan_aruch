(א) One must fulfill onah even during pregnancy and nursing. Regarding winter they asked the Ari z"l whether one is exempt when it is cold season (meaning he must immerse for keri), and also during pregnancy and nursing; he answered if the woman waives he is exempt, but nevertheless good to fulfill; they testified he did not fulfill onah on those days (HaKavanot). Even HaEzer siman 76 seif 6. Idle men — healthy indulgent people (Rambam); Gemara implies even sons of Torah.

(ב) And they stay every night — 100:100 in Even HaEzer siman 76 wrote workers etc.; only Rama wrote there some disagree.

(ג) Torah scholars — on weekdays must engage in Torah at night since Torah is their craft; same on Rosh Chodesh and festivals; a man should not say 'I will make myself a Torah scholar' for it is written 'and her onah he shall not diminish' (Sefer HaKaneh). One should not have relations on first night of Pesach, Shavuot night, and erev Shabbat unless immersion night (Kabbalists).

(ד) Obligated to visit her — even not at time of her onah.

(ה) In the last six months — in the first three it is hard for the woman and the fetus; one who has relations on the night she conceived sheds blood that can kill the fetus (Kabbalists, Zohar Shemot 3); Gemara concludes nowadays Heaven preserves the simple-minded; in middle three months good for child, hard for woman.
