1) [Seif 1] If he was married, etc. Regarding marital relations during pregnancy and nursing, a person is certainly obligated to fulfill the mitzvah of onah even during these periods. One should not say that the seed then appears, Heaven forbid, to be wasted. During these periods, however, if the husband wishes to abstain, he may do so, provided his wife does not object and forgives him. If she objects, he must fulfill her onah. Regarding relations intended to produce souls, however, he cannot exempt himself at all, even if his wife forgives him. Shaar HaMitzvos, parashas Bereishis. It implies there that a man whose wife is infertile or has ceased bearing children has the same law as during pregnancy and nursing; see there.
