And when he departs on a journey. This appears difficult in light of seif 15, which says not to have relations on the day of departure. It appears that seif 15 refers to one traveling on foot, who requires greater strength, whereas here it refers to one traveling in a wagon. I later found that Maharshal wrote this in Even HaEzer, siman 25. Levush writes that here it refers to the preceding night, but the phrase “when he departs” does not imply this. Moreover, in seif 11 he writes, “not before them.”
