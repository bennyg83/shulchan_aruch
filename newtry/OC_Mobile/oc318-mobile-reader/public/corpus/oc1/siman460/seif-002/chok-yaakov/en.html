And the Shelah wrote: and even Jewish women who have not learned the matters of Pesach, one does not trust them regarding baking and kneading on Pesach; therefore most Torah scholars and rabbis stand over the rolling and over its kneading and over its baking, for several things will arise in women's minds that they are permitted, or perhaps they forget and do something improper, and she does not know the reward of a mitzvah and the loss of a transgression; and if a person stands over them they are more careful; end quote. And so wrote Kenesses HaGedolah in the name of Maggid Mishneh.
To occupy himself with the mitzvah. And the Taz wrote in the name of the kavanos of the Arizal that one should toil over matzas mitzvah until he becomes warm and sweats, and through this the klipos born of wasteful emission will be broken.
