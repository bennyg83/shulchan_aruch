See under Taz; and Peri Chadash would occupy himself with all the matzos, for according to the majority they do not make [them] according to the law; see there; and see Chok Yaakov in the name of Maharil; and see in the Rosh regarding bread of poverty: the way of a poor person is that he kindles and his wife bakes; and see Beis Yosef and Levush; and see there. Certainly adults of understanding must stand by all the matzos to see whether they make [them] according to Torah law; and matzas mitzvah needs making for its sake, whether drawing water and measuring flour and kneading and rolling and baking, through an adult Jew of thirteen years and two hairs, for the sake of matzas mitzvah.
