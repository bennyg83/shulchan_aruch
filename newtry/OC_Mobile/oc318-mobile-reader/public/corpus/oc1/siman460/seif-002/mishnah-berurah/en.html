(6) Regarding matzas mitzvah: and Maharil ruled to be careful so l'chatchila regarding all the matzos; and so wrote Peri Chadash, that also regarding baking the other matzos made for all the days of Pesach he would practice to stand over them, for the stumbling is great; and so wrote Chok Yaakov in the name of Shibolei HaLeket, that one should not trust women regarding kneading and baking, for several things will arise in women's minds that they are permitted, or perhaps they forget and do something improper, and they do not know the reward of a mitzvah and the loss of a transgression; therefore most Torah scholars and rabbis stand over its rolling and over its kneading and over its baking.
(7) Himself: and he should toil over matzas mitzvah until he becomes warm and sweats, and this is a great repair for the severe sin [the Arizal].
