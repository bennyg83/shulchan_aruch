(s.k. 2) All, etc. For dough crumbs, etc.: that is to say, even though crumbs of themselves are nullified, as stated in siman 434, s.k. 5, and those are the words of the Talmud.
And siman 442, se'if 8, where one must also say this distinction; see there.
One must burn them. For all chametz, even though one nullified it, needs biur. And we hold that l'chatchila it requires burning, as written at the beginning of siman 445.
If one said, etc. And see Even HaEzer, siman 141, se'if 63: so it must be read; for it is explained there regarding one who sends a get and nullifies it: specifically if one says "let this get be void" it avails, but "this get is void" does not avail, for the reason Magen Avraham wrote; and further there are explained the remaining formulations that avail or do not.
