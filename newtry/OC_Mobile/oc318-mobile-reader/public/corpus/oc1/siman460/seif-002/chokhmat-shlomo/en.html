Se'if 2. The Rosh would occupy himself with matzas mitzvah, etc. Note: see in Magen Avraham what he wrote, that it is a greater mitzvah to do it himself than through his agent; and see what I wrote in a derashah for Sukkos of the year 5607, in section 94 of my written work in manuscript: there I wrote that this is not compelling (see there), and one may say that specifically regarding kiddushin and Shabbos the law is so, and not regarding other mitzvos (see there for its reason, for it is good), and I brought a proof for this from tractate Nedarim; and understand carefully. And behold, I was asked by the rav of Nizhyniv whether he did well to enact not to bake matzos at night: behold, he enacted a great enactment; and likewise when I came here to the holy community of Brod, for some years they were very careful about this. But what his honor wrote, to require for every matzah a lamp of its own, and to compare it to chametz: this is not so, and there are no words [for it], for what has a bed to do with Mount Sinai? Rather, the reason and support for this is from what Rashi wrote in tractate Niddah, folio 67, and in Tosafos there and in the poskim, that one should do the chafifah by day, for at night, because she is flustered about her household, she does not comb properly; so too here with matzah, for at night, since one is flustered about one's household, they do not do guarding properly; and also at night there is the compulsion of sleep, as is written in tractate Berachos: so there is concern that perhaps they will doze and not guard it properly; therefore one certainly should not bake matzah at night, and his Torah honor did well to enact so. Thus I answered briefly to the rav of Nizhyniv; and understand carefully.
