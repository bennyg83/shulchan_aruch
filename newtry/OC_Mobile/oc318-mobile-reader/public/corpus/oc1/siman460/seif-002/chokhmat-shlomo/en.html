Seif 2: the Rosh would strive for matzah of mitzvah, etc. Marginal note: see Magen Avraham what he wrote that the mitzvah is greater in it than in sending it; and see what he wrote in the derashah for Sukkot year 5637 in section 94 in my manuscript composition there I wrote that this is not necessary — see there; and one may say that specifically for kiddushin and Shabbat the law is so, but not for other mitzvot — see there in his reason, for it is good, and I brought proof from tractate Nedarim — study. Behold, I was asked by the rabbi of Nizhniv whether it was proper that he enacted not to bake matzot at night — behold, he enacted a great enactment; and likewise when I came here to the community of Brad, for several years they were very careful about this. But what His Honor wrote, to require a candle for each matzah and compare it to chametz — one does not say such things, and they are not comparable; what connection does a bed have with Mount Sinai? But the reason and support for this is from what Rashi wrote in tractate Niddah daf 67 and in Tosafot there and in the poskim that one should do the combing by day, for at night due to the tumult of the house she does not comb properly; likewise matzah, for at night due to the tumult of the house they do not guard properly; also at night there is oness of sleep, as stated in tractate Berachot — so one should be concerned lest they doze and not guard properly; therefore certainly one should not bake matzah at night, and he enacted well, as the rabbi. Thus I answered briefly to the rabbi of Nizhniv — study.
