[6] With a comb, etc. Meaning: even though it is like a decoration, since one does not intend to decorate; and some practiced on Chol HaMoed to make decorations, and it is not proper (Magen Avraham). And the Rokeach in siman 280 [wrote]: what they pressed concerning a mold is not specifically writing regarding a get, but on Yom Tov it is forbidden to mark with it; here too it is like writing or decorating; until here. And in Sefer Amarkal he concluded: and even on Chol HaMoed. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: one should not make pieces in the matzos in a manner that they fold over.
