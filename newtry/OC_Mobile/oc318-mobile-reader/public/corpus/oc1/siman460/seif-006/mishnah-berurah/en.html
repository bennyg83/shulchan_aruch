(18) Panadish, etc.: they are matzah doughs filled with meat or cheese, because at the time of baking moisture comes out of the meat and the cheese and moistens the dough and impedes its baking; and furthermore, this moisture has the status of fruit juice, which when mixed into dough kneaded with water hastens to leaven immediately, as will be explained in siman 462; and if so there is concern that when the moisture came out into the dough it leavened immediately before the heat of the fire managed to govern it to bake it. And even after the fact, if one forgot and put them into the matzah, one should forbid the matzah.
