Not to make pandish or flandish. These are types of food made with flour. Even when prepared with fruit juice, the Sages prohibited them. This differs from baked matzah that was ground, from which one may prepare anything desired, as explained at the end of siman 463. Peri Chadash writes similarly in Yoreh Deah 87, seif katan 6. See Knesses HaGedolah, siman 461, who writes that a mishap occurred and a decree was issued that anything ordinarily made throughout the year with flour may not be prepared from baked matzah. Our practice is to permit it.
