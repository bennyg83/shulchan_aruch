Eggs. See under Taz; and see Magen Avraham [letter] 5: regarding a small matter it does not impede baking, and it is possible that kitniyos too are similar to seeds after the fact; and see Chok Yaakov, letter 17, who brought from siman 467, letter 11, that a small matter does not impede baking; and one may say that we require the measure of netilah: if so there is no proof. And the Taz forbids the whole matzah once baked within Pesach; and God willing in [siman] 461, se'if 5, it will be explained; and see the Responsa Maharam of Lublin, of blessed memory, siman 105.
