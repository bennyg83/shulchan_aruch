All crumbs. For dough crumbs collect together and stick and are significant, which is not so of bread crumbs, which are not significant (Maharil) (Bach, not like Olas Shabbos); see siman 434, se'if 2, and siman 442, se'if 8. And one should tread them with his feet in the earth before they come to leavening, for if they come to leavening one must burn them (Shelah); see the beginning of siman 445. One should be careful to shake them from the table so that they not mix into the dough. If one said "crumbs are ownerless," it is nothing, for it implies that of themselves they are ownerless; rather one should say "let them be ownerless" (Darchei Moshe from Mahariv, Mateh Moshe, Shelah); and see Even HaEzer, siman 141, se'if 63, which formulations avail; and one can resolve the world's custom, since it is stated in the Gemara that crumbs do not need bitul, for of themselves they are nullified; but we distinguish between bread crumbs and dough crumbs, as above; therefore they say "crumbs are ownerless," meaning that these too, by them, are like other crumbs that of themselves are nullified; so it seemed to me to resolve the custom. And nevertheless one should say as written in the Shulchan Aruch; and in any case one must say it in a language one understands, as written in siman 434, se'if 2.
