I. See under Taz; and Eliyah Rabbah, letter 4, implies bread crumbs will gather together; and Mahari of Ronkel answered [regarding] dough as significant; see there; and Pesachim [says] crumbs are nullified, implying that we are not concerned that they gather together except regarding dough; and understand. And before the prohibition, nullification is as written in "all chametz"; and after one said "all chametz," one can nullify after the leavening; which is not so of one who kneads after the time of prohibition, namely at the sixth hour: one must nullify before it leavens; and God willing in Magen Avraham [letter] 2 it will be explained further; and see siman 459 in Magen Avraham, letter 13, that even on Yom Tov it is permitted there [with] a condition and bitul because of a mitzvah; and in letter 2 it will be explained.
