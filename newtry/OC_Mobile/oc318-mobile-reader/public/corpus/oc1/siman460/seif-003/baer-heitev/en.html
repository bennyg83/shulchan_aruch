Crumbs. And one must say it in a language one understands. And those who say "crumbs are ownerless" err, for it implies that of themselves they are ownerless; rather one should say "let it be ownerless." Taz, Magen Avraham; see there. (And see Even HaEzer, siman 141, se'if 63, which formulations avail.) And Chok Yaakov wrote that it is not an error; see there.
