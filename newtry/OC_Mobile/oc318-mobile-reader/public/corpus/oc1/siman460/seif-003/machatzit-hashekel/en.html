(s.k. 3) With a comb, etc. Since one does not intend, etc. For behold the prohibition of decorations is lest one delay and through this it leaven; and since one does not intend to decorate, one will not delay to beautify the decoration.
"Perhaps he will make it with a mold": that is to say, on what we say that one does not make decorations, he challenges: let him make it with a mold and stamp the decoration quickly and not need to delay; and it answers: since most people do not have molds, they forbade because of "we do not distinguish," or because of appearance, so that people not know it was made with a mold. And what is the difficulty? Behold, by means of a mold it is in any case forbidden because of dough that enters the mold.
Like the teeth of a comb, which does not stick and one can clean it immediately. And regarding tevilah of these combs and irons with which they pierce: see Yoreh Deah, siman 120: out of doubt one should immerse them without a blessing, or with another vessel that needs tevilah, and intend that with one blessing he also exempt the irons.
