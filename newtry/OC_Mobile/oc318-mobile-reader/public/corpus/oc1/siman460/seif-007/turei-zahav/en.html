Whole eggs. The reason in Beis Yosef is that moisture comes out of them and impedes the baking; and Orchos Chaim concluded with this: and some say that the same applies, that it is forbidden to knead the dough with seeds such as fennel and the like, for this reason; end quote. And from this I forbade a matzah in which after baking one kitniyos [seed] was found, even though kitniyos do not leaven, as written in siman 453; nevertheless, because of impeding baking, one should forbid.
