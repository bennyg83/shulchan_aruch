Serikin. Meaning: l'chatchila; and after the fact one fulfills with them.
With a comb. See Yoreh Deah, siman 120, se'if 5, in the gloss: the Rema ruled that the irons with which they prepare the matzos need no tevilah; see there. And the Taz wrote there that one should immerse them without a blessing; see there; and so ruled Peri Chadash and Chok Yaakov; or he should immerse them with other vessels.
Wafers. (And upon this they rely to make decorations on them; and the Bach protests against them greatly; see there.) The custom is to make them the thickness of a finger. Beis Hillel, Yoreh Deah, siman 96.
