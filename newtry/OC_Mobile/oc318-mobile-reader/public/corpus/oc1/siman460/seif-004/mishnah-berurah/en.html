(13) One does not make, etc.: meaning l'chatchila; and after the fact they are permitted for eating if one did not delay in decorating them the measure of leavening, and one also fulfills one's obligation with them.
(14) Decorated serikin, etc.: because one delays over them to decorate them, and sometimes one comes to leavening through this delay; and even if one wishes to make the decoration by means of a mold, with which one does not delay at all, even so the Sages did not distinguish and forbade everything, because one who sees that matzos were baked does not know that they were made with a mold and will come to permit even without a mold. And likewise there is no distinction between serikin of householders and those of bakers, even though bakers are established and practiced in this and do not delay in making them: even so it is forbidden; and even if the matzos are thin wafers that do not hasten to leaven, even so one should not make them decorated.
(15) With a comb: meaning, and even though something like a decoration is formed on the matzah, nevertheless it is permitted, for since one does not intend to decorate, one will not thereby come to delaying the measure of leavening.
(16) Wafers: that is, even though it is explained in se'if 5 that less than a tefach is permitted to make, nevertheless it is more proper l'chatchila to make thin wafers.
