One does not make serikin, etc. That is, l'chatchila; but after the fact one fulfills with them; so it is stated explicitly in the baraita: Our Rabbis taught: one fulfills with decorated serikin.
For the wafers, etc. So wrote Beis Yosef in the name of Semak in the name of Ritzba, that we hold like R' Yose, who said they make serikin like wafers. And upon this we rely to decorate our matzos, because we make them like wafers; end quote. It is implied from the Rama's language that this does not avail regarding decorations; and so wrote my teacher and father-in-law, of blessed memory, that nowadays they do not make decorations at all.
