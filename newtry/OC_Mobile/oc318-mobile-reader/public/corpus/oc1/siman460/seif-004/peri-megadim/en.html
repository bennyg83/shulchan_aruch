One does not. See under Taz: l'chatchila one does not make [them], whether of householders or of bakers, and even with a mold it is forbidden; and after the fact even in every respect it is permitted; and see Beis Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham: it is proven in the Gemara, and after the fact it is permitted; and it is explained there explicitly on folio 37b; and as the Taz, of blessed memory, wrote. And it is possible that without a doubt after the fact it is permitted, and not as the essence of the decree; therefore he brought in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham; see there. And wood with which one pierces one matzah one should not take for another matzah; see Beis Yosef and Peri Chadash in the name of the Rikash, for one occupies himself with dough; see there. And piercing is not complete occupation, and delays combine; see siman 459 in the Taz and Magen Avraham [letter] 4; and God willing at the end of siman 468 the laws of baking matzah will be explained further.
