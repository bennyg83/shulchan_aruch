With a comb. Meaning: even though it is like a decoration, it is permitted, since one does not intend to decorate; and some practiced on Chol HaMoed to make decorations, and it is not proper. And Maharil wrote that it is forbidden to make [them] with a mold, for the dough enters the mold and becomes chametz; end quote. And one must say that what the Gemara said, "perhaps he will make it with a mold," means a mold made like the teeth of a comb, and not a mold in which they make confectioneries.
