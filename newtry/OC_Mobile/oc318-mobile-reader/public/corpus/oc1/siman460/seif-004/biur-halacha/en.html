And one should make the matzot thin wafers, etc. — see Mishna Berurah; and although per the Rama's words it would be more correct to explain that he refers to the Mechaber's words regarding a comb that ties even if a form is made thereby, and he says that nevertheless it is good to hurry — and on this the Rama added another advice that one should make them thin, for in this way they do not leaven quickly.
