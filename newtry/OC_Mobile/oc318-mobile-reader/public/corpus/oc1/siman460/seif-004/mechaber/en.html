One should not make illustrated cakes — that is, to shape the bread like a kind of animal or bird; but whatever one does with a comb so that it not puff up, and whatever one perforates in the matzot — is permitted; nevertheless it is good to hurry to do them so as not to delay.And one should make the matzot thin wafers and not thick bread like other bread, for thin wafers do not leaven quickly.:
