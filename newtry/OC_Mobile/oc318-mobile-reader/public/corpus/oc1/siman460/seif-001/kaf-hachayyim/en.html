1) [Se'if 1] Mitzvah matzah may not be kneaded, etc. The verse states, 'You shall guard the matzos,' requiring guarding for the sake of matzah. Tur; Levush. Regarding whether guarding for the sake of matzah is required under Torah or rabbinic law, Bach implies that the requirement is rabbinic. Peri Chadash writes that it is required under Torah law. This also appears from Responsa Rashba, simanim 26 and 593, and Levush, siman 454, se'if 4. See there. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1.
