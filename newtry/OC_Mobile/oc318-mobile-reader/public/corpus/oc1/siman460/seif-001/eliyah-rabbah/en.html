[1] [Levush] Not to knead and not to roll, etc. (Mahariv.) And even to pierce the matzah it is proper to be careful (Taz). Milling depends on the dispute in the Talmud at the end of the chapter "All that is mekabel," and as the Rashba wrote in responsa, siman 27; and not like the Taz; and understand carefully, for I abbreviated. And all this is regarding matzas mitzvah; but regarding other matzos, even though it is also good to be careful with them, nevertheless one should not be stringent with minors and the like; and see Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah in siman 461.
