[A] [Clothing] not to be kneaded or edited, etc. (Mahari 16) and even to peck at the matzah it is appropriate to be careful (16). Tahina shall be hung in the Plugata Shas at the end of the chapter of every hour, and as the Rabbi wrote in the response to Siman 27 and Dela 26 and Dok because I shortened it. All this is due to a mitzvah, but in other mitzvos it is also good to be careful with them. Also, in any case, do not be strict with the small ones and the like.
