One should not knead, etc. — it appears to me specifically kneading; and even baking is forbidden via an akum, for it requires guarding for the sake of the mitzvah, and an akum does not act except according to his own intent, even if an Israelite stands over him and warns him to intend for the sake of the mitzvah, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of responsa of Rashba; but grinding the wheat — there is no prohibition
