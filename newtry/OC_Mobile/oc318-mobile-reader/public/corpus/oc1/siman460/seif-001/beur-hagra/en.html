Seif 1 — one should not knead, etc. As he wrote in ch. 21 38b and there 41a regarding matzot of Eretz Yisrael, etc.; why? Because they did not perform guarding for it from kneading onward it requires guarding, and via an akum they did not; and they explain even Eretz Yisrael — kneaded in our presence; and so Chacham Tzvi, as he wrote in Gittin.
