(17) A tafh thick pat - when it is so thick, one must feel that the heat of the fire does not control the inside of it and it sours inside it during baking, but with less than a tafah is allowed and refer to the Bahl that we have brought up how many rulings on even with less than a tafah one must be careful. And in retrospect, if it is already baked, there is methirin even in a taph thickness and there is oserin indeed in less than a taph, it should not be banned in retrospect.
