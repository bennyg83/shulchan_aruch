(17) Bread a tefach thick: for when it is so thick there is concern that perhaps the heat of the fire will not govern inside it and it will leaven inside during its baking; but with less than a tefach it is permitted; and see Biur Halacha, where we brought the view of several poskim that even with less than a tefach one should be careful. And after the fact, if one already baked, some permit even when thick a tefach, and some forbid; however, with less than a tefach one should not forbid after the fact. And nevertheless, whenever it is thick one should examine inside it whether it was baked well and did not become leavened.
