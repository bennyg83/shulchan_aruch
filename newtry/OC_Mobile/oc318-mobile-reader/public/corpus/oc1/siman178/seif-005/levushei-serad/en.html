There, in the gloss, “in one house.” This means from room to room, as opposed to seif 2, where “in one house” means in one room. With this you will understand Magen Avraham, seif katan 4, for if it were within one room, even if he had not intended it, this would merely be moving from one corner to another. Regarding the ruling of Magen Avraham where he can see his original place, it is uncertain whether this applies specifically from room to room, comparable to moving from a house to a courtyard in siman 273, or perhaps even between two houses, as below at the beginning of siman 195 concerning zimun.
