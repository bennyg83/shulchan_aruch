(A.C.) It is necessary to congratulate John R. R. D.C. from Panama to Panama, and to welcome:

Since enclosed, etc., per Beit Yosef—Rashba challenged this law of fig tree in Shulchan Aruch from Yerushalmi corner to corner; Taz: initially I turn corner to corner when enclosed by partitions, unlike fig tree standing without partitions—where no partitions any change is change; possible where no partitions, all who see first place is not change; east and west of fig tree differ for fig divides into two places—more correct, end quote; implies in last words added even without partitions if sees place not change; nevertheless agrees first answer: when enclosed by partitions need not see place; from Rashba's second words: where no partitions all who see, etc.—implies with partitions need not see; so Magen Avraham in name Raavad s.k. 2 seeing place even room to room permitted—what advantage of corner to corner? Therefore corner to corner even if does not see place no blessing needed—when enclosed by partitions like room to room.
