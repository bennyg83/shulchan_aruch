Seif 5: Some say, etc. This is Rashbam’s view there, s.v. aval, following the reading in Rav Chisda’s Tosefta: to drink wine, etc.
And some say, etc. Tosafos there, s.v. ella, etc.; see there. Their proof is unanswerable, and this is the principal ruling. Rif and Rosh rule likewise.
And some say, etc. Since the Gemara states the matter without qualification and does not explain otherwise, it implies that it relies on the Mishnah in Berachos, where only bread is mentioned. This is the first opinion in Tosafos: “only bread,” etc. Tosafos avoided the objection that, if so, why does the Gemara say “foods,” by writing “all kinds of bread.” The principal explanation, however, is as Tosafos later writes, for even then it should not have said “foods,” since everything is included in bread.
