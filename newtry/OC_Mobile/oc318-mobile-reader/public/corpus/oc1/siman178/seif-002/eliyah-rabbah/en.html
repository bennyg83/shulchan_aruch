(2) [Levush] In one house, etc.—requires much study, for Rama wrote both places in one house, and Rama ruled so at beginning of siman 473; there too Levush seif 2 needs study; there seif 5 explained he holds even house to house helps with intent—requires study. Forced to say what he wrote "other house" means other room as I explained above—and he should have written complete other house does not help. Here one can say he wrote per conclusion: seif 2 on things requiring blessing in their place no blessing needed; and although we hold here even without intent at establishment, l'chatchila forbidden is implied—since his intent for this, permitted even l'chatchila as Bach wrote—but all strained. Also we hold in this seif per poskim even bread needs blessing—perhaps Levush from reasoning that even per those who say no distinction between requiring blessing in place or not, when he has intent all agree there is distinction. Per this possibly his intent also below siman 473; he holds for this matter wine unlike bread; but as examined in Tosafot, Rosh, Ran Pesachim 100—it is proven and clear for kiddush one cannot say so and house to house does not help at all; on contrary kiddush is weaker since he ate nothing only made kiddush.
