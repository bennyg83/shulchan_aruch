(Seif katan 2) His intention, etc. The same applies when he can see, etc. See siman 273. Meaning, regarding Rama's ruling that prior intention is effective from room to room, Beit Yosef writes that Raavad derives this from the law of Kiddush in siman 273, where prior intention is effective. Accordingly, since Raavad also writes that visibility is effective, we likewise follow his view, for siman 273 explicitly rules that visibility is effective regarding Kiddush. Magen Avraham writes there that this applies even through a window and even if he sees only part of his original place.
