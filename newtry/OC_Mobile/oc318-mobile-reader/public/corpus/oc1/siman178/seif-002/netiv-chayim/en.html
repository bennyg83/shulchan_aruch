(Magen Avraham s.k. 7) And so in Gemara says, etc. In margin: his words are wondrous—how is it leaving some since guests did not establish at feast—what answer to Rambam who considers wine among things requiring blessing in their place and leaving some helps even when eating elsewhere? I can uphold his words not from his reason that exempting from blessing because of leaving is only after they returned to their place—so explicitly Rashi and Rambam on things requiring blessing in their place: meaning he went elsewhere to eat. Per first establishment he went to bless one blessing on both—for first establishment he returns, meaning per first meal he eats now to finish his meal—implies "establish" means finishing meal elsewhere; with leaving some: need not bless birkat acharonah since they will return; from reasoning: since because of leaving must return to friends' establishment place—requires study.
