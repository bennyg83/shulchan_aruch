And specifically if he did not leave, etc.—this is wondrous since we hold like Rabbenu Chananel now—no distinction here as said: fruits even with leaving liable, bread even without leaving no blessing needed. Truth this hagah from Semak Beit Yosef copied: group sitting reclining uprooted feet to go—when left some friends need no retroactive birkat hamazon nor hamotzi even for things not requiring blessing after in their place; for things requiring blessing even if left, change of place need not bless before them in things requiring, etc. His words are wondrous—for sugya implies for things not requiring, i.e. fruits, no distinction left or not as said; besides Beit Yosef wrote Semak's words are not precise, wrote "not retroactive birkat hamazon" implying bread, then established for things not requiring blessing in place. Further wondrous he first wrote even for things not requiring—implies at least also things requiring, only for emphasis said even for non-requiring—contradicts what follows. I say missing word "left"—suggestion: Semak until "even" is his language from inside and from "even" onward is Hagahot Semak Rabbenu Peretz—inside holds like Rav Sheshet, therefore wrote tanna "when left" regarding bread; in hagah disputes inside, wrote we do not hold so but even if left for things not requiring after in their place, whereas for requiring even without left no blessing needed—meaning we hold Rabbenu Chananel not Rav Sheshet; nevertheless for non-requiring even if left must bless, for requiring even without left no blessing. General conclusion: per Tosafot and Rosh we do not care at all about left or not; Semag also distinguishes left—explained below; therefore Rama has no place here to distinguish left or not but between requiring and non-requiring.
