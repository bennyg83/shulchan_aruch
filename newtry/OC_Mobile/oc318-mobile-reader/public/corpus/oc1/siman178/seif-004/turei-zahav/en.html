But in the second place, etc.—this discusses he had intent for this, like travelers mentioned later; question from Rama seif 1 who specifies one house—but two houses per first view require retroactive blessing—must say also first view which was like Rav Sheshet holds if he had intent to eat elsewhere no blessing needed, for not change of place, not like Rama's hagah; question from garden to garden—see below intent does not help for that. I say also first view distinguishes fruits and bread regarding intent to eat elsewhere: bread since establishment applies, intent to eat there helps so no blessing; fruits intent does not help for no joint connection—contradiction on Rama's hagah above but Mechaber's words seif 2 when they return need blessing—for there not that he eats in second place but returns to finish in first; if eats in second place no blessing if had intent—but Rama's words specifying one house are wondrous; proof from siman 473 not similar—for there he does not eat at all where he makes kiddush, only intent to eat in second place—therefore not called meal place unless one house; not so here where he eats here too; if intent to eat more elsewhere we do not require specifically one house as I find. Practically since we hold like Tosafot Rashbam and Rosh everywhere we follow them—law is l'chatchila one should not leave meal without birkat hamazon unless passing mitzvah e.g. prayer time arrived—even without prior intent; our custom at mitzvah meal e.g. wedding feast eat some at home go to wedding to finish, with intent from start to go there; travelers bless at place of finishing eating; if happened he went elsewhere not to eat there and returned to meal—if ate what needs blessing in its place as seif 5, no need bless again, certainly not retroactive birkat hamazon; if ate fruits or drank wine went and returned—bless first on what he will drink on return; if diverted mind from eating while gone in every case must return and bless again on what he will eat; garden fruits depends if one garden intent helps for all in garden; seems even one garden without partition around is like garden to garden as Beit Yosef on Rambam seif 2 ate east of fig tree; though later reason seeing first place—here from second tree wants to eat, law like garden to garden, seeing first place does not help as I find.
