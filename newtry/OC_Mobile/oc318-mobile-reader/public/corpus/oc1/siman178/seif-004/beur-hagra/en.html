Seif 4: If one ate, etc. So writes Rabbeinu Shimshon in his gloss to Semak, following Rashbam’s explanation there and Rashi, s.v. aval devarim, concerning an established meal, etc., and s.v. lekivei, etc. This is unlike Semak’s explanation that “to his original establishment” means he must return to the first place. The incident involving Rabbah bar bar Channah in Berachos, chapter 8, concerns a case where he had no bread there.
As, etc. At the end of chapter 7 there: one who eats while walking, etc. Semag explains that he sits in the final place.
