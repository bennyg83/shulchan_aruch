(4) There in gloss: if he had intent, etc.—the same when he sees his place is not called change of place as said siman 473. Magen Avraham s.k. 2; Magen Avraham s.k. 3 wrote even seeing through window or even seeing part of his place is not change of place; so Machatzit HaShekel s.k. 2, Ohr Zaru'a note 1, Chayei Adam there note 6; however in Even HaEzer he disagrees and wrote whoever goes to another room even if he sees first place is called change of place—whether to return to first place to bless birkat hamazon there or to return and bless on things not requiring blessing in their place, see there—and Zechor leAvraham brought, see there; appears l'chatchila one should be careful and b'dieved rely on Magen Avraham because of Rav Sheshet.
