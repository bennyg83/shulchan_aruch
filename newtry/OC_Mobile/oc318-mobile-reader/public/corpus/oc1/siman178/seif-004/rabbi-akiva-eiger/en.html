Magen Avraham, seif katan 12. “In cases of doubtful blessings, we are lenient.” In my opinion, the practical halachah here requires study. Although according to the authorities who hold that the seven species require an after-blessing in the place where they were eaten, a change of place does not require a new blessing, our present ruling that one need not recite the after-blessing in the original place is itself based upon the principle that in a Rabbinic doubt we are lenient. Consequently, he is uprooted from his original established place, since he is not expected to return there and the principle that he returns to his original establishment does not apply. It would therefore follow that this is a change of place and that he must recite a new blessing. This question cannot itself be resolved by saying that in a Rabbinic doubt we are lenient. This requires study.
