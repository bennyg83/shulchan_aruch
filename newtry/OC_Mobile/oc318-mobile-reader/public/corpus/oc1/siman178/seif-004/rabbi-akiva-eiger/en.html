(Magen Avraham s.k. 12) Doubtful blessings—to be lenient—in my humble opinion requires study here; for although per poskim the seven species require blessing after in their place, the law of change of place does not require blessing; nevertheless for us, since from doubt of d'rabbanan we are lenient, the law stands that no blessing from his place—and consequently uprooted from his establishment since he does not stand to return to his place and the first establishment does not return—consequently the law is change of place and must bless; and this is not a matter of d'rabbanan to be lenient; requires study.
