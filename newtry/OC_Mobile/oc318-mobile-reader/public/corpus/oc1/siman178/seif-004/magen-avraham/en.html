And he returned and ate. Meaning, ideally he may not eat in the second place until he has recited the after-blessing, as stated in seif 1. If he violated this and ate there, however, he recites the after-blessing in the second place, for that too is considered reciting it in the place of eating.
As they were accustomed. They may eat while traveling, since they never established a fixed place for their eating, as Rambam writes in chapter 4. See there.
