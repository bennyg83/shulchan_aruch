(Taz s.k. 10) And this is that of going for a groom, etc.—his words are wondrous—for the questioner brings that of going for a groom where it states without leaving it is uprooting—how does he answer that beraita for conclusion that R' Yosi too does not distinguish except uprooting for synagogue; especially what Taz wrote and even though Gemara does not answer so—it is wondrous why he could not answer, since beraita states even for groom where they can eat there we require specifically left, as Taz wrote Shulchan Aruch s.k. 1; with difficulty one can reconcile his intent that certainly also for groom sometimes found he cannot eat there, being in market etc.; accordingly can answer Rabbenu Chananel discusses matter where he can also eat there, beraita of groom where he cannot eat there and therefore depends on left. Therefore Taz says Gemara can answer simply—that of groom discusses where he can eat there as questioner thought; but per conclusion R' Yosi does not dispute Rabbanan and must distinguish place where he can eat. Then beraita of groom questioner brought is strained and establishes itself where he cannot eat there; what Taz wrote in matter where he can eat, etc., and this of groom is per thought of questioner that groom discusses even in such case—and means from questioner from that of groom plain meaning implies he can eat there yet must bless without left—must strain and explain his words though not in his language; requires study.
