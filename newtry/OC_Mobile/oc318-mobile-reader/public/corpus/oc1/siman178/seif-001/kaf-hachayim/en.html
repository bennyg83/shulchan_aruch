(1) [Seif 1] Since he changed his place he must bless retroactively, etc.—meaning l'chatchila must bless birkat hamazon before uprooting as seif 2; if he did not bless before uprooting must return to his place and bless birkat hamazon, then eat where he wishes—only bless hamotzi first. Magen Avraham s.k. 1; so Taz s.k. 2. This per Maran z"l; but per Rema below in gloss seif 2: if he ate things requiring blessing in their place, even what he will eat afterward—no blessing needed, see there; later poskim agreed with Rema because doubt—Rav Sheshet, as below note 14, see there. Nevertheless practice when going in meal to another place or to speak with friends: take piece of bread in hand so not divert mind from eating bread and not from netilah—need take again as siman 170 note 13, see there; see below note 20.
