1) [Seif 1] Since he changed his location, he must recite a blessing retroactively, etc. This means that initially he must recite Birkas HaMazon before leaving, as stated in seif 2. If he did not recite it before leaving, he must return to his place and recite Birkas HaMazon, and afterward he may eat wherever he wishes, provided that he first recites HaMotzi. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Taz, paragraph 2, writes the same. This follows Maran's words, of blessed memory. According to Rema, of blessed memory, however, in the gloss to seif 2 below, if he ate foods that require a blessing in the place where they were eaten, he need not recite a new blessing even over what he subsequently wishes to eat; see there. The later authorities agree with Rema, of blessed memory, because in cases of doubt concerning blessings we rule leniently, as stated below in paragraph 14; see there. Some nevertheless practice taking a piece of bread in their hand when they go elsewhere during the meal or go to speak with friends, so that they do not divert their attention from eating bread and also do not divert their attention from the washing, which would require them to wash again, as stated above in siman 170, paragraph 13; see there.
