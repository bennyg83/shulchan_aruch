Which things are considered an interruption during a meal. Containing 7 paragraphs: If one was
eating in one house and interrupted his meal and went to another house, or if he was eating and his friend called him to speak with him and he went out to the entrance of his house and returned — since he changed his location he must recite a blessing retroactively for what he already ate, and then recite HaMotzi again and complete his meal. But if his friend spoke with him inside the house, even if he changed his spot from corner to corner, he need not recite a blessing again. ((See below §273 — if he had in mind to eat in another place it is not called a change of location, provided both places are in the same house; and see above §184.).
