(Seif katan 1) One must, etc. Meaning, ideally, etc. He comes to resolve the wording of the Shulchan Aruch, which implies that after one returns to his place, he recites Bircas HaMazon there. Yet seif 2 explains that one may not leave his place until he first recites Bircas HaMazon. He therefore answers that although this is indeed the proper course initially, the ruling here is still understandable because it deals with one who already left without reciting Bircas HaMazon. Taz writes likewise in seif katan 2.
He must return. This applies if he left intentionally. If he left unintentionally, see above, siman 184.
