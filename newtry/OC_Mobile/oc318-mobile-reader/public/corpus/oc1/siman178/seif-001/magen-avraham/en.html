He must recite the blessing retroactively. Meaning, ideally he must recite Bircas HaMazon before leaving, as stated in seif 2. If he did not recite it before leaving, he must return to his place and recite Bircas HaMazon, and afterward he may eat wherever he wishes, provided that he first recites HaMotzi. See siman 184.
