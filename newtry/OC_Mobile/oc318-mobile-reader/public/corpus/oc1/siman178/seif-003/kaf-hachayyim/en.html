3) There: From one corner to another, etc. Even if it is a large hall, for this law we consider the entire building as the place where he ate, and he need not recite a blessing. Even if, when he initially recited the blessing, he did not intend to move to another corner, he nevertheless need not recite a new blessing. Chayei Adam, rule 59, paragraph 4; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1.
