(There R' Menachem and Ran) I did not know where this is Ran's view—for he does not hold so; only per Rashi that on change of place one needs birkat hamazon too, and even if he went out without birkat hamazon and returned to his place he must bless birkat hamazon first—this is Ramban in Milchamot; but Rabbenu Chananel certainly need not bless birkat hamazon, for he does not consider it change of place at all. The beraita is arranged: friends reclining, uprooted to go to synagogue—when leaving they need no retroactive blessing; so even l'chatchila unnecessary, and poskim like Rabbenu Chananel rule like this Tosefekhta. If we say the Tosefekhta discusses passing time for a mitzvah need—why does the first beraita teach when they went out for a groom, which is also for a mitzvah, that when leaving they require retroactive blessing? Rather that case is different—for that beraita makes it change of place; and since his meal already ended, what he eats later is another meal—therefore must bless birkat hamazon immediately when leaving lest he forget; for this reason even if he went out and returned he must bless birkat hamazon first, for he was already obligated in birkat hamazon. See Ramban and you will find. But per what we hold like Rabbenu Chananel that his meal has not ended—what he eats later finishes his meal—no need to bless birkat hamazon; understand well.
