Seif 3: Some say, etc., and this is, etc. This is Raavad’s view in his glosses, based on the Yerushalmi cited above concerning one who ate on the eastern side, etc. He writes that this applies only if he did not intend this from the outset, as stated in the gloss to seif 1. See above, etc., and this is, etc. Hence the ruling here, “but from garden,” etc., and “even if,” etc. See Tosafos 100b, s.v. yedei Kiddush, etc. Rambam and Shulchan Aruch in seif 2 hold that since he cannot see his original place and the two places are not enclosed by walls, prior intent does not help. See Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham.
