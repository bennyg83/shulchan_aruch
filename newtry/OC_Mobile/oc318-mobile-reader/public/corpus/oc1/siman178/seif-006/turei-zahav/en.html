With all this it is not interruption. Reason: although impossible to eat and pray together and should be interruption as we say regarding birkat hamazon—drink and bless together impossible—here too; for this reason R' Yitzchak truly must bless hamotzi again afterward and wash hands; nevertheless Tosafot and Rosh did not rule so—for birkat hamazon is finish and removal on what already ate, not so here. It appears this is specifically if left some friends or did not leave and pray in same room where meal is; but if all reclining uprooted to go to synagogue to pray there—it is interruption; so Semag explains: further they conclude there if uprooted feet to go to synagogue when returning need no blessing retroactively or l'chatchila if left friends there even though prayed there meanwhile. Question: Semag already ruled like Rabbenu Chananel and we proved Rabbenu Chananel no distinction left or not—how Semag conditions this for law? Further why Semag writes language "we conclude"—where found agreement? Rather Tosafot proved from beraita Gemara brings to answer Rabbenu Chananel who said uprooted feet to go to synagogue—implies prayer is not interruption. I explain per Beit Yosef everywhere to reject Rosh's proof who rules Rabbenu Chananel and strengthen Rif and Rambam who ruled Rav Sheshet: since we find in ch. Zayin Bet they argue R' Yehuda ben Levi and R' Yosi—for R' Yehuda ben Levi everything R' Yosi says "stated only" is only per words of sages and R' Yosi disputes; Tosafot ch. Bnei Ha'ir ruled like R' Yehuda ben Levi—so truly no dispute R' Yosi and Tosefekhta; difficulty on Rabbenu Chananel except Ran answers Rabbenu Chananel can say "stated only" divides—and since we hold "stated only" is per sages, we hold halacha like Rav Sheshet and Ran's views we do not rely on. Beit Yosef spoke well—yet Tosafot contradict themselves: here ruled Rabbenu Chananel so "stated only" divides, in ch. Birkat Hamazon ruled like R' Yehuda ben Levi so "stated only" is per sages—requires answer. I say Tosafot hold certainly in conclusion "stated only" here too is per sages, and Rabbanan also hold distinction left or not, and distinction in beraita above questioner cited—they went for groom; here went to synagogue. Practical difference: wherever uproot so they can also eat there—not called uprooting, no blessing—that is for groom; but uprooting where they cannot eat there, like going to synagogue to pray—all agree without leaving it is uprooting and removal; though Gemara per R' Tarfon does not answer so to distinguish this uprooting above, it fits to answer per R' Tarfon who holds R' Yosi disputes Rabbanan; we see Tosefekhta Rabbenu Chananel—halacha so—and laws contradict as said—we must distinguish uprooting for groom and uprooting for synagogue; beraita synagogue remains for law; Semag ruled well as beraita implies: if two matters—uprooting all without leaving some, went to pray—then interruption; if left—not interruption; therefore said "we conclude" per conclusion proves so though initially we would not grasp for Rabbenu Chananel. If meticulous not to rely on this pilpul to consider prayer interruption if did not leave some here—still I come yes from other side Beit Yosef wrote here: seems though Rif in ch. Ayin Peh appears to dispute Rosh who wrote rabbanan must bless on cup and supports—hear now as for birkat hamazon interruption, so since impossible drink and recite together must return bless after finish Hagadah and Hallel; Hagadah and Hallel are not removal and diversion from what ate and drank, yet must bless on cup in Pesach Haggadah—so too praying in meal per R' Yitzchak; nevertheless since world not practiced like Rif—for cannot bless on every cup of Hagadah—we do not hold like him in prayer in meal but like Rosh. Wonder on our custom we practice in our land like Rif as Rama siman 474 Ashkenaz custom and most geonim—so we hold halacha like Rif that though no removal and finish must bless if matter impossible, i.e. pray and eat; we should rule R' Yitzchak—yet cannot rule against settled law here that prayer is not interruption; nevertheless in this not explicit in Tosafot Tur Shulchan Aruch—if all uprooted went to pray in synagogue—in this considered finish and removal and interruption—practically should be careful not to say no interruption except praying in same room or left some friends as I find.
