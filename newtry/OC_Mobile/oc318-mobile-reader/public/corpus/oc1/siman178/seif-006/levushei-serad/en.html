Shulchan Aruch seif 2: ate in east. Difficulty—for this is corner to corner; one can say there walls of house surround both corners therefore considered one place; unlike fig tree standing where there are no partitions—therefore fig tree not precise but where no partitions any change of place is change. However possible where no partitions, all who see first place is not change of place; east and west of fig tree differ for fig tree divides—it is two places—and this appears more correct per Beit Yosef; thereby understand Magen Avraham s.k. 5 and 6 who hold what Beit Yosef wrote fig tree divides means he does not see his place; and holds further Beit Yosef requires two things: no partitions and also does not see his place—requires study; also implied from Bach's language who wrote where no partitions all who see, etc.—nevertheless where there are partitions even if he does not see, such as divider—no blessing needed.
