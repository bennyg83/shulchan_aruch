Seif 6. One who remembers during the meal that he has not prayed. Note: If he had no time whatsoever to wait, even for a moment or two, see Taz, siman 271, seif katan 4, whose words imply that in such a case the prayer constitutes an interruption and he must recite a new blessing. According to Magen Avraham there, seif katan 6, however, it is evident that even in such a case he need not recite a new blessing. See there and examine carefully.
