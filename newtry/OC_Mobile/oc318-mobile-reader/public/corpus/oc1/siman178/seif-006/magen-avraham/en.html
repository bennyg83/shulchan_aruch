It is not considered an interruption. Nevertheless, it appears to me that he must wash his hands again because there was a diversion of attention. This is also implied by the Gemara in the chapter Arvei Pesachim, and likewise by the Tur: If he washed his hands for the first dipping, he must wash them again for the second dipping. Why? Since he needed to recite the Haggadah and Hallel, he diverted his attention. See siman 92, seif 5. This applies unless he guarded his hands, in which case he is exempt.
