A discretionary matter. This is not precise. The same law applies even if he interrupted for a mitzvah, such as when he prayed in the middle of the meal, as explained above in seif 6. The wording “a discretionary matter” is used because we find that an obligatory and regularly mandated interruption, such as the Hallel and Haggadah recited between the cups, is considered an interruption and requires a separate blessing over each cup. Gra explains this in order to resolve the difficulty raised by Perishah.
For example, he needed to relieve himself, etc. It was explained above that this applies specifically during a bread meal. With fruit or beverages, leaving one's place itself constitutes an interruption, and when he returns he must recite a new blessing.
