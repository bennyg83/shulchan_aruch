(s.k. 7) And how, etc.—so Gemara Pesachim 101a Samuel: those who heard kiddush in synagogue do not fulfill kiddush—for need kiddush in meal place; do not fulfill wine—must bless on wine they drink at home though tasted kiddush cup in synagogue, for change of place is interruption; Gemara challenges Samuel why kiddush in synagogue—answers for guests eating in rooms adjoining synagogue, for them this is kiddush in meal place—must bless wine at home for change of place though left some guests in synagogue—leaving some does not help when eating elsewhere; though if ate things requiring blessing in place even elsewhere no blessing—wine alone per seif 5 requires blessing in place. Difficulty on Samuel: why not fulfill wine when going home—Rosh: for things requiring blessing in place no blessing needed—for if not eating here would return to bless—as if still in place, meal here, bless here birkat hamazon; therefore even bread if ate less than satiating amount for birkat hamazon, no return to first establishment, when eating elsewhere must bless; kiddush discusses listeners going home who tasted from kiddush wine; Tosafot: even per those disputing Samuel that change of place not interruption, since did not taste wine at time of blessing—interruption between blessing and tasting—brought Magen Avraham above siman 167 s.k. 19; or they tasted wine in synagogue—discusses they did not drink satiating amount for birkat acharonah—for we hold wine requires birkat acharonah in its place—would need birkat acharonah before leaving synagogue—plain they must bless again on wine they will drink even without change of place; discusses not drinking satiating amount; similarly bread must bless for no return to first establishment; unlike if left some friends—even without satiating amount would not need bless.
