7) There: If they left some of the group there, etc. This applies even if those remaining were not among the people seated to eat. R' Yaakov Castro. Even women are effective for this purpose. Maaseh Rokeach, chapter 4 of Hilchos Berachos; Zechor LeAvraham, under samech. Erekh HaShulchan, paragraph 3, however, writes that those remaining must specifically be members of the group who were sitting and eating with them; see there. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 2, appears to hold the same view; see there. Beis Menuchah, paragraph 4.
