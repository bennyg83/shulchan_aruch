(Note 1) In every [hour] that he remembers them, etc. (Bach). His proof: we learn from the tzitz, which has only one Name — the Torah said "and it shall be always upon his forehead" that he not be distracted from it; tefillin, which have several Names, all the more so. If so, from this inference we hear only that he not distract his mind from them, but that he handle them we do not hear. Moreover, shall he every hour that the tefillin are on him not remove his hand from the tefillin of the hand and shel rosh? I wonder. Perforce it must mean: at every hour that he remembers them, etc.

That he not forget them and distract his mind from them; and if he did not remember them, nevertheless this is not called distraction of mind, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 44 note 2 — see there.
