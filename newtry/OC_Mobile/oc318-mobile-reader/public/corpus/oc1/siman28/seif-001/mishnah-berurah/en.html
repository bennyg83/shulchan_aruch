(1) At every time. Meaning, whenever he remembers them, he is obligated to touch them. By doing so, he remembers them constantly and will not come to distraction. It also serves to adjust them so that they do not shift from their proper place.
(2) That he not divert his attention. During prayer it is unnecessary to touch them. Magen Avraham below in siman 44; see Peri Megadim.
(3) The tefillin of the arm. Because the tefillin of the arm are closer to his hand for touching, and one may not pass over a mitzvah.
(4) First. And afterward the tefillin of the head. If, while touching the tefillin of the head, he finds that they have shifted from their place, and he then realizes that the tefillin of the arm have likewise shifted, he must first return the tefillin of the arm to their place, based upon the verse, “You shall bind them as a sign upon your hand,” and afterward, “as totafos between your eyes,” as explained above in siman 25, seif 6.
