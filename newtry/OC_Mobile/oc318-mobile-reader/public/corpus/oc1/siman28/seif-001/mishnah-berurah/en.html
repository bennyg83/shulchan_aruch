(1) At every hour — meaning every hour he remembers them he must handle them, for thereby he remembers them frequently and does not come to distraction of mind; and also to adjust them so they not move from their place.

(2) That he not distract his mind — and during prayer one need not handle them [Magen Avraham below in siman 44; and see in Peri Megadim].

(3) On the shel yad — for the shel yad is near him to handle, and we do not pass over mitzvot.

(4) First — and then on the shel rosh. If while handling the shel rosh he finds it slipped from its place and he knows then that the shel yad also slipped, he must return the shel yad first to its place, from the verse "and you shall bind them for a sign upon your hand" and then "and for totafot between your eyes," as above in siman 25 section 6.
