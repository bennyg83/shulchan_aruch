Hour. Ba'er Hetev, and they wrote in the name of the disciples of the Ari that during Torah study and prayer one need not attend to the tefillin; see below siman 44. And see in responsa Shagat Aryeh siman 40, who forbids distraction in every matter; he wrote it may be said the measure of distraction is like walking a hundred amot, as they said regarding casual sleep — see there. In truth he is lenient in the measure, for it is only a fraction of 67 of an hour, and Torah was not given to ministering angels; see in siman 44 in the Taz there. It seems the world relies on Rabbeinu Yonah, the Rosh, and the Tur that distraction applies only when standing in levity and lightheadedness. What Shagat Aryeh challenged may be reconciled; see in Magen Avraham siman 44.
