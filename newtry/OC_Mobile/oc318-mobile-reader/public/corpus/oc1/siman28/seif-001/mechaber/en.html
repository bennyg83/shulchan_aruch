The laws of removing tefillin. Containing 3 sections. A person must handle the
tefillin at every hour so that he not distract his mind from them; and he should handle the shel yad first. When he says "and' you shall bind them for a sign upon your hand" he handles the shel yad, and when he says "and they shall be for totafot between your eyes" he handles the shel rosh.
