First. Ba'er Hetev; and above in siman 25 I wrote in the name of the Zohar that just as one must enter the synagogue crowned with tefillin, so one must leave the synagogue crowned with tefillin. In Shulchan Shelomo he wrote he did not find anywhere in the Zohar that one should be careful about this. Perhaps he means that for the same reason they said in the Zohar to enter with tefillin — how can he say "I bow" — so too when taking leave of one's rabbi; and this is not sufficient — see there.
