(5) He removes first. After removing the three windings from the finger.
(6) While standing. The same applies to removing the windings from the finger. According to our custom, in which the tefillin of the arm are also put on while standing, they must likewise be removed while standing, because the manner of removal should correspond to the manner of putting them on. Some Sages had the custom to remove the tefillin of the head with the left hand, the weaker hand, to show that removing them was difficult for them. If one is left-handed and performs all labor with his left hand, he should remove them with his right hand, so that the removal not be done quickly.
(7) And above them the tefillin of the arm. Bach and Taz above in siman 25, seif katan 10, write that one should make a long, narrow case so that the tefillin are placed one above the other. Magen Avraham writes that it is preferable to place them side by side, and this is the custom. When Shulchan Aruch writes “and above them the tefillin of the arm,” the meaning is that they should be positioned slightly higher so that one encounters them first. Shulchan Shlomo writes that it is also proper to place the tefillin of the arm on the right side of the case for this reason. Some have the custom to make two separate pouches, one for the tefillin of the arm and one for the tefillin of the head. Even with two pouches, it is preferable to place the pouch of the tefillin of the arm slightly higher so that one encounters it first and does not pass over a mitzvah. Taz above in siman 25 maintains that the prohibition against passing over a mitzvah applies even through a pouch. See Magen Avraham, siman 25, seif katan 1. Those who make separate cases for the tefillin should mark which belongs to the tefillin of the head and which to the tefillin of the arm, so that they do not mistakenly remove the tefillin of the head first. Also, since the tefillin of the head possess greater sanctity, one should thereby be careful not later to change its case and place the tefillin of the arm in it, unless he stipulated this initially, as explained below in siman 42.
(8) First. So that he will not need to pass over a mitzvah. It is proper to be careful not to remove the tefillin of the arm until he has placed the tefillin of the head inside the case, so that he not forget and place the tefillin of the arm into the case first.
