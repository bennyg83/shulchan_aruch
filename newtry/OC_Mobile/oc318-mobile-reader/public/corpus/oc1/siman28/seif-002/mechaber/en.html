The tefillin of the head are removed first, because it is written, “And they shall be as totafos between your eyes”: whenever they are between your eyes, there should be two upon you. The tefillin of the head must be removed while standing. He should place the tefillin of the head in the case, and the tefillin of the arm above them, so that when he comes to put them on, he will encounter the tefillin of the arm first.
