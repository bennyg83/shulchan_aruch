First. Before removing the strap from the finger. Galya Raza, Magen Avraham (the editor said: it appears to me there is a printing error in the Magen Avraham and it should read “and first he removes the strap,” etc.):

Standing. The same applies to the wrappings on the finger. And according to our custom, since we place the arm tefillin while standing, one must also remove them while standing, for as their placement is, so is the manner of their removal; and they accustomed to wrap them like wings — see there. Wings of a dove — Mateh Moshe:

In the bag. In the responsum of Binyamin Ze'ev, siman 289, he wrote that one should make the pouch or bag narrow so that they lie one upon the other; and likewise the Bach and Taz, siman 25, note 10; and the Magen Avraham wrote that it is better to place them side by side and make a mark which is shel rosh and which is shel yad, for since the sanctity of shel rosh is more stringent than shel yad, it is forbidden to place shel yad on shel rosh. One should not place the strap on the batim but on the sides. One should not grasp the straps and roll the tefillin, nor shake them out of the bag, but remove them with one's hand.
