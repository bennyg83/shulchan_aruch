First. Before removing the strap from the finger. Gallei Razaya; Magen Avraham. The glossator writes: It appears that there is a scribal error in Magen Avraham, and it should read, “First he should remove the strap, etc.”
While standing. The same applies to the windings around the finger. According to our custom, in which the tefillin of the arm are also put on while standing, they must likewise be removed while standing, because the manner of removal should correspond to the manner of putting them on. The custom is to wind the straps like wings, alluding to the wings of a dove. Mateh Moshe.
In the case. Responsa Binyamin Ze'ev, siman 189, writes that one should make the pouch or case narrow so that the tefillin are placed one above the other. Bach and Taz, siman 25, seif katan 10, write likewise. Magen Avraham writes that it is preferable to place them side by side and mark which is the tefillin of the head and which is the tefillin of the arm. Since the tefillin of the head possess greater sanctity, one may not place the tefillin of the arm upon them. One should not place the straps upon the boxes, but rather along their sides. One should not hold the straps and wind the tefillin around them, nor shake them out of the case. Rather, he should remove them with his hand. Sefer Chassidim; Magen Avraham.
