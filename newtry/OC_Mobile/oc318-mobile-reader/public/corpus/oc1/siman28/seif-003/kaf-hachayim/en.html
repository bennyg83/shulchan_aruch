C) There — that he not distract his mind from them. A Torah scribe who corrects a sefer Torah for Shabbat reading while still wearing tefillin did not act properly, for perforce he distracted his mind from the tefillin in order to give his eyes and heart to correcting the sefer Torah in its missing and extra letters, connected and separated forms, and sometimes he must write and correct and sew a sheet; and sometimes the weekly portion is long or there are several booklets — all this time he did not escape distraction. Petach HaDevir letter 1. And see there that he wrote: although there is no place to rebuke him, for Rabbi Yehuda and the Tur in siman 44 do not consider distraction except when standing in levity and lightheadedness, but when occupied with his work and craft and his mind is not really on them, this is not called distraction — so too plainly Chayei Adam general 14 letter 15; nevertheless one who fears God's word should be careful, for since nowadays we do not wear tefillin all day as in Talmudic times, at least for a brief hour one should be careful of distraction even without levity and lightheadedness. Moreover, Shagat Aryeh siman 34 forbids distraction entirely. All the more so Rabbeinu the Ari and Mitzvat Shemurim were very strict in this and explained that it causes a great flaw — see Eliyah Rabbah in this siman; certainly every person of spirit should be strict. And see there that he brought proof from the Bach siman 38 section 7 and Peri Megadim there in Mishbetzot Zahav letter 4 — see there. And Kaf HaChayyim brought this siman 10 letter 26 and wrote that the law is with him — see there.
