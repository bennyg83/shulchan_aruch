There: “And in Chiddushei Rashba.” He comes to explain the words of Galya Razya. One should not say that since the Gemara in Megillah concludes, “because it is impossible, it is permitted,” no further care is required. He therefore writes that Rashba rules that even two objects of equal sanctity may not be placed one upon the other when it is possible to avoid doing so. All the more does this apply here, where the straps possess less sanctity than the tefillin.
