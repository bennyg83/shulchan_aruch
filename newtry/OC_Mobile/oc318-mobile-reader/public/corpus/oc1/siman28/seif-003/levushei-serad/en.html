He comes to answer the words of Galia Raza: do not say that since the Gemara concludes in Megillah that when it is impossible it is permitted, therefore there is no stringency; rather the Rashba wrote that even two equal sanctities may not place one upon the other where it is possible — all the more so here, where the straps are of lesser sanctity than the tefillin.
