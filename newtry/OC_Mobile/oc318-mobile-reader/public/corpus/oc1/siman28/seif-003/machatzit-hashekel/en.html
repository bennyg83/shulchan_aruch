(Seif katan 3) While standing. The same applies to the windings around the finger. They must be removed while standing, just as one must stand when making those windings, as stated in siman 25, seif katan 18.
Therefore he removes them with the left hand, because it is impossible to remove them as quickly with the left hand as with the right. Accordingly, a left-handed person should remove them with the right hand, meaning the hand with which an ordinary person acts. This certainly depends upon the hand used for all ordinary labor. Even if he writes with his right hand, if he performs all other labor with his left, he is considered left-handed. If he removes them with his left hand, he would do so quickly.
See what Magen Avraham writes in siman 123 concerning the steps taken backward at the conclusion of the Amidah. For the same reason, one begins by stepping backward with the left foot.
