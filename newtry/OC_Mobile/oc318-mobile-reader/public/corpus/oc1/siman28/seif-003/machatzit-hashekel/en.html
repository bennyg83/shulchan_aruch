(Note 3) Standing — so too the wrappings of the finger must be while standing, as one must do at the time of wrappings, which require standing, as written in siman 25 note 18.

Therefore he disagrees regarding the left hand: on the left one cannot hurry the removal as on the right; if so, a left-handed person removes with the right — this depends for everyone on whether one who does all his work with the left is called left-handed even if he writes with the right; nevertheless, since he does all his work with the left, if he removes with the left he would do the removal quickly.

And see what I wrote in siman 123 regarding steps: when stepping back at the end of the Eighteen Blessings, the left foot steps first.
