(9) And when removing them — when folding the tefillin one should not wrap the straps on the batim but on the sides of the batim on the titura; some wrap them like wings, after the wings of a dove. When wrapping on the sides of the tefillin one must hold the tefillin in his hand and roll the strap upon them, not hold the strap and roll the tefillin into it. Also when taking tefillin from their pouch one should not shake them but take them by hand from within the pouch. A tefillin bag is forbidden if of shaatnez, like a mantle for books [Peri Megadim].
