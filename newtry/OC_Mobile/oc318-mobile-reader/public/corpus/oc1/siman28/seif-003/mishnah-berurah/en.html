(9) And when removing them - when folding the tefillin, do not wrap the strips on the houses, but on the sides of the houses on the titurah, and some have the custom of wrapping them like wings, after the wings of a dove. And when you fold them on the sides of the tefillin, one should hold the tefillin in his hand and roll the strip over them, not hold the strip in his hand and roll the tefillin into it.
