Magen Avraham, subparagraph 2. Second night, etc. His intention is as the Bayis Chadash wrote in responsum siman 132, where he was concerned about this law of the Rema because of a berachah in vain, since one can say that the Shehecheyanu he recited in his house exempts also the Shehecheyanu of sukkah. See there, where he elaborates on this reasoning. Afterward he concludes that even one who wants to practice like the Rema should do so specifically where he did not eat in the sukkah both nights; then he recites Shehecheyanu in the morning because of the sukkah. But if he did not eat on the first night, even though he eats in the sukkah in the morning, he should not recite Shehecheyanu, since on the second night he will recite Shehecheyanu in any case; why should he recite it in the morning? Although initially one should recite Shehecheyanu of sukkah immediately at the time of its mitzvah and not delay it until the second night, nevertheless, since because of the concern of a berachah in vain he is delaying it, he may delay it initially. See there at length. This is the Magen Avraham's intent, and it is surprising that he was brief and did not point to the responsum of the Bayis Chadash. What he wrote, 'see siman 661,' means that his words there are based on his words here: since he does not recite Shehecheyanu in the morning, therefore on the second night he says sukkah and afterward Shehecheyanu; see there.
