1) [Seif 1] One who makes a sukkah, etc. Although we learn in the Yerushalmi, first chapter of Sukkah and final chapter of Berachos, that one who makes a sukkah for himself recites, 'Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to make a sukkah,' and if he made it for another he recites, 'concerning the making of a sukkah,' we rely upon our Gemara, where we learn in the chapter HaTecheles, Menachos 42a: R' Chisda said, regarding every mitzvah whose performance may be carried out by a non-Jew, a Jew does not recite a blessing over its performance. A sukkah of the acronym categories Ganbach is valid. There is also an opinion that any mitzvah whose performance does not complete the mitzvah does not require a blessing; see there. Tur and Beit Yosef; Levush.
