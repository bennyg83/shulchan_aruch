For himself. But when one makes it for others, it would have been proper for the householder to recite Shehecheyanu; see siman 585, se'if 2. This applies specifically to something made from year to year, but a shofar and a Megillah, which are made for many years, one does not recite Shehecheyanu over their making. The Chanukah lamp too is not recognizable as something he makes for the sake of the mitzvah; this is from the Mordechai.
