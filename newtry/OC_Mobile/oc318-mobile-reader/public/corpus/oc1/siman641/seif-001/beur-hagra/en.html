Se'if 1: Whether, etc. This is unlike the Yerushalmi in the first chapter of Sukkah and the last chapter of Berachos, which obligates him to recite a berachah whether he makes it for himself or for others. But in the Gemara in Menachos, even for himself he does not recite the berachah.
When he makes, etc. The Gemara there says 'for himself.' See Tosafos there, s.v. one who makes, etc.
And if he did not, etc. The Gemara there: they said to him, when he enters to sit in it, he recites two berachos.
