Into the hollow of the heh. If it enters into a dalet and it appears like a heh, it is invalid, as the Rama wrote in Even HaEzer, siman 125, end of seif 18. See Magen Avraham, who concludes with these words: The Knesses HaGedolah wrote that some validate it entirely. The author of Yad Aharon protested against him loudly, saying that this is not so, and that we have not found anyone who is lenient in this matter; see there.
