Even. Taz: Shabbat 108a:1, Tosafot, s.v. eizeh nechorot — also permitted, for the Torah was particular only about "permitted in your mouth." One who bows to an animal — see siman 11, siman 68, Magen Avraham 12, and Turei Zahav on Pri Chadash, letter 6 — lekhatchilah forbidden for a mitzvah; bedi'eved it is fine; and it is not issurei hana'ah, for commoners are not forbidden. Here from tefillin he derives all of it — and for every mitzvah from "permitted in your mouth"; see Pri Chadash in Magen Avraham 3. It is difficult to me: if so, a garment of rabbit wool and camel, etc., should be invalid for tzitzit — we have not heard this; and as it appears, silk from a worm proves it seems from its body (see siman 9) — and you would say they were not qualified for "work of Heaven," meaning sanctity — to exclude tzitzit, which is a mitzvah; behold burka and shofar prove too — mitzvah, and nevertheless impure animal is invalid; see Megillah 26 and above, siman 21, in Beit Yosef. However one who examines Ran, beginning of chapter 2 of Rosh Hashanah — in the mishnah, shofar is "work of Heaven" — for remembrance inward it counts; and that of tachash (Shabbat 28b) implies this is not a full hekesh; if so, tzitzit — a mitzvah — should not derive from sanctity; and his words, Taz, letter 27, on straps halachah leMoshe miSinai — for the hekesh is not a full hekesh; see Eliyah Rabbah, Pri Chadash, letter 5. And Levush so wrote — straps halachah leMoshe miSinai.
