He should make all its parshiyos open, except for the last parsha written in the Torah, which is Vehayah im shamoa, which he should make closed; if he changed this, it is invalid. Rama: Some validate if they are all open. Maharam Padua, siman 77, in the name of Orchos Chaim, and Beis Yosef in the name of the Ittur. In these countries, the practice is that even the parsha of Vehayah im shamoa begins at the head of the line like the other parshiyos. Therefore the practice is that the parshiyos of Kadesh li, Vehayah ki yeviacha, and Shema begin at the head of the line. At the end of Kadesh li and at the end of Vehayah ki yeviacha they leave blank space sufficient to write nine letters. At the end of Shema they do not leave blank space, and if they leave any, it is less than enough to write nine letters. The parsha of Vehayah im shamoa begins in the middle of the upper line, leaving before it blank space sufficient to write nine letters. It emerges that the three parshiyos are open according to both the Rambam and the Rosh, and the final parsha is closed according to the Rambam.
