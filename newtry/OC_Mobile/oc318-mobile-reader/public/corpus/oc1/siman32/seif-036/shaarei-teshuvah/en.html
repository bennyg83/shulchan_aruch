The dragging. Be'er Heitev — see Dvar Shmuel siman 154 who wrote one should not hang one word between lines in writing tefillin and mezuzot even where writing is in order between earlier and later — one: this too is called out of order due to change; and also because their writing is thin without much space between line and line — therefore one does not hang, see there — he brought from responsum Ran siman 39; and see Ginat Veradim klal 2 siman 4 mid-responsum: if the line is too cramped for its words — one should not permit hanging one word above between lines and then descending to write in line order; and there he wrote per Rashi tefillin and mezuzot are stricter — even hanging one letter appears spotted, see there.
