Even the Taz extended and explained his intent: below it is literally one bayit, only above the four batim are separated — and b'dieved this suffices. He wrote them on one actual klaf, only above a large section is a margin; he cuts there and inserts each margin-scroll into part of the four-parshiyot bayit on one klaf. Below they stand upright in one bayit; below there is only one bayit and it is not rolled — they simply stand thus; in any case they are upright. And what he wrote that the halachah follows Rav Dimi and not Abbaye — apparently not like Abbaye; and per Bach's method he said the halachah follows Rav Dimi: l'chatchilah forbidden and b'dieved permitted, and not like Abbaye who is strict b'dieved, and not like Beit Yosef see there who wrote them on one klaf — b'dieved is the case. Therefore what he wrote in Semag, that one may run the shin until the seam — also not for invalidation but only l'chatchilah, for that is the explanation of Rashi on Rav Dimi in Menachot 35a. Beer HaGolah samekh: if so b'dieved it does not invalidate; and Rabbenu Yerucham who wrote that the yod too is required — is not so precise, for that is for invalidation and this for l'chatchilah.
