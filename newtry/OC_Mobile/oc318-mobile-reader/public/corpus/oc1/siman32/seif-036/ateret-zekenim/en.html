And some validate, etc. The gloss: as above; it seems the main view follows Rama, and several great ones of his generation agreed, especially the elder Maharam Padua in responsa of Rama siman 86; one should not make an uproar in the worlds and innovate halachah lest, God forbid, it lead to general disparagement of the early authorities (Lechem Chamudot). But in all our diasporas in the West we also place nine letters at the end of Shema and begin VeHayah at the head of the line even though that form is open and VeHayah should be closed — we are not strict, since it is not adjacent to Shema in the Torah. Yet it is very puzzling regarding our custom, for our tefillin are invalid per the Rambam; per the Rosh we must justify it, since they are not adjacent in the Torah there is no concern for openness; if we followed the custom of Ishmaelite lands we would satisfy everyone — for per the Rambam they are truly closed as required, and per the Rosh even if open we could say that since they are not adjacent in the Torah there is no concern. If I had strength I would practice as they do; they told me two pairs of tefillin rely on the Ittur, which validates all as open, for our tefillin are certainly valid (Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari, responsa of Maharam in the name of Rabbi Yehudah Shemesh of Lublin).
