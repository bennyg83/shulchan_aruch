(ibid.) In the author, se'if 36 — he shall make all its parashiyot open. See note: see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim, siman 4, where he was asked about tefillin whose arm [parashah] "and it shall be" was written closed, although the head tefillin had all its parashiyot open — whether it is proper to place them, or whether it is two contradictory matters; he answered to agree with the questioners that there is no stringency in the matter; see there.
