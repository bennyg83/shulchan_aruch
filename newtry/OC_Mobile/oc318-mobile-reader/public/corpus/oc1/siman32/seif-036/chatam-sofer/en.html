There, in the Mechaber, seif 36: 'He should make all its passages open.' Note: See Responsa Chatam Sofer, Orach Chayyim, siman 4. He was asked about tefillin whose hand-tefillin passage Vehayah Im Shamoa was written as a closed passage, while all the head-tefillin passages were open: may they be worn, or does this constitute two contradictory practices? He answered in agreement with the questioners that there is no concern; see there.
