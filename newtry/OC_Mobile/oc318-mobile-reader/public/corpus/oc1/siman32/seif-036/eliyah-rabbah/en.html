[36] And one may be lenient, etc. And if he did not intend — and even if he intended each time but did not express at the start of writing that he writes for the sanctity of the tefillin — it seems invalid. However if he said at the start of writing that he writes all the divine names in it for the sanctity of the Name, it seems somewhat from the Taz in Yoreh De'ah at the start of siman 274 that it helps ex post facto even though he did not intend. But in the responsum of Masat Binyamin siman 99 he wrote — his wording: if the scribe writes several names at once — such as when he was not pure and therefore skipped the Name in the Torah scroll and left a place, and when he purified himself came to write them at once, or there are two Names one after another — then it suffices for the scribe with one sanctification for all the names, since he does not interrupt between them — so far his wording. It implies when he interrupts between them it does not help; and presumably he interrupts from the start of writing to its end — and this requires investigation.
