And one may be lenient, etc. If he did not have intent, and even if he had intent each time but did not say at the beginning of the writing that he writes for the sanctity of tefillin, it appears invalid. However, if he said at the beginning of the writing that he writes all the Divine Names in it for the sanctity of the Name, it appears somewhat from the Taz in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 274 that this helps after the fact even if he did not have intent. But in Responsa Masas Binyamin, siman 99, he wrote as follows: if the scribe is writing several Names at once, such as where he was not pure and therefore skipped the Name in a Torah scroll and left space for it, and when he purified himself he came to write them all at once, or there are two Names one after the other, then one sanctification is sufficient for the scribe for all the Names, because he does not interrupt between them, end quote. This implies that when he interrupts between them it does not help, and presumably there is an interruption from the beginning of the writing until its end. This requires study.
