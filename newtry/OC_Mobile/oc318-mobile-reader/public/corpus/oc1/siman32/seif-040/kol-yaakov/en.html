40) Seif 11. A hide that one processed, etc. See Taz, seif katan 6, who wrote that from this we learn that they can return and prepare it. See Devar Shmuel, siman 161, who wondered at the Shulchan Aruch and Levush, for they referred there to Tur, Yoreh De'ah, siman 271, but when they reached that place they omitted it. He wrote: I do not know the reason. Perhaps it is because the reality is difficult, and it is uncommon to do this and to annul the processing of parchments used in our time, which remains firm and enduring. If they annul it through soaking or the moisture of water, they will not be able to restore it well through stretching and a new processing, for finishing an old item is harder than doing so with a new one. To add to processing that was already completed without nullifying and stretching it will not help to count as for its sake, as we rule in Gittin, chapter HaNizakin, that passing the pen over a divine Name written not for its sake does not help to validate it as for its sake, etc. End quote; see there. See Zera Avraham, part 2, siman 118.
