Seif katan 31. The legs of the tavs. Meaning, the entire left leg became straight and was not bent outward at the bottom. This is also the intent of the Rama regarding the yuds of the shin, etc., where it became a simple straight stroke. It requires study why he must scrape the whole leg. Is it so that if one writes the left leg of a tav very thickly it is invalid? We have not found this. If so, if the leg of the tav became thick, he should bend it further outward at the bottom. The same requires study regarding the yuds of the shin in the Rama. Perhaps when it is excessively thick, the form of the letter is not upon it, since there is no room to bend the leg because of the following letter; and likewise with a shin. This requires examination.
