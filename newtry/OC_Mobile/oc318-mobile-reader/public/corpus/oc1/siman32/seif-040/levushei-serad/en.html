(s"k 31) The legs of the tet. Meaning that the entire left leg is made straight with no curve downward outward; and so is the Rama's intent regarding the yods of shin — that a straight line stick be made. Requires investigation why one must drag the entire leg; if one writes the left leg of the tet very thick, we have not found that it is invalid — if so, the leg of the tet would still curve downward outward. Likewise requires investigation regarding the yods of shin in the Rama; and perhaps when it is excessively thick the letter forms are not on it, for there is no place to bend the leg for the letter after it, and likewise in shin — and this requires examination.
