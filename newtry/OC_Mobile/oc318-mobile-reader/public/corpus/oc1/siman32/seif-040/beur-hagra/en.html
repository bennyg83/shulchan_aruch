Groove, etc. Like Abbaye; and as Rashi there — Abbaye is greater than Rav Dimi, as in chapter 2 of Bava Batra. Mordechai; and even though in the second statement we rule like Rav Dimi — there because the sugya of chapter 14 of Shabbat and chapter 9 of Chullin is like him.

And if not, etc. Rambam explains Abbaye does not argue except lekhatchilah, and bedi'eved he agrees with Rav Dimi, as written 34b; "and if they have no groove," etc. — and this is what is written "and it," etc.
