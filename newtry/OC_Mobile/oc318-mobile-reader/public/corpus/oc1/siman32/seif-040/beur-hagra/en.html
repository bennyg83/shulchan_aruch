Seif 40, leather, etc. See Tosafot there, s.v. straps, etc. The Rosh wrote: nevertheless, it is a mitzvah, etc., because of this is my G-d and I will beautify Him. But the Mordechai wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yitzchak that by law we require them to be black, from the question in Shabbos 28b: but did Rabbi Yitzchak not say, straps, etc.? This is difficult: what was the initial assumption, and what connection does one matter have to the other? Rather, this is its explanation: just as it is a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai that they must be the same color as the batim, so too regarding being from a kosher species. The Gemara answers: although we have such a tradition regarding this, etc.
A groove, etc. Like Abaye, and according to Rashi's explanation there, for Abaye is greater than Rav Dimi, as stated in the second chapter of Bava Basra. Mordechai. And although in the second statement we rule like Rav Dimi, there it is because the discussion in chapter 14 of Shabbos and chapter 9 of Chullin follows his view.
And if not, etc. This is the Rambam, who explains that Abaye said this only initially, but after the fact he agrees with Rav Dimi, as stated there on 34b: and if their grooves are not, etc. This is what he wrote: and it is, etc.
