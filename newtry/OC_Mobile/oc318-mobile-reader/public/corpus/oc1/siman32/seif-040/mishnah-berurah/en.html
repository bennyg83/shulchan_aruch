It is a mitzvah, etc. This implies that after the fact it does not invalidate. Some authorities maintain that it is a halachah given to Moshe at Sinai, like the straps, and that it invalidates even after the fact. Biur HaGra implies that he inclines this way as practical halachah, and so too Yeshuos Yaakov. This also appears from Eliyah Rabbah and Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah, that one should be stringent unless it is a case where this is not possible, in which case one may rely on the lenient opinions so as not to be idle from the mitzvah of tefillin.
Black. It is better that the blackening be with black color that has no tangible substance at all, so that the tefillin are black only in appearance. Nevertheless, those who blacken with a kind of gloss that cannot be peeled off, even though it can be removed from the parchment through fine crumbs, should not be treated stringently after the fact. See what is written in seif 48 in Biur Halacha in the name of Pri Megadim, whose opinion is also not to be stringent in this. However, what the scribes of our time have innovated, blackening with a coating that can be peeled off whole from every side of the tefillin and whose form is like black paper, is invalid, from the book Nishmas Adam, with whom the Gaon author of Beis Meir agreed, unlike Noda BiYehuda; see there.

Provided that it be, etc. But if it is not recognizable from the outside, even though inside there are four housings and each passage is placed in its own separate housing, it does not help.
The groove is recognizable. But a mere scratch or mark does not help at all, for in any event there must be some slight separation between the housings so that the grooves are truly recognizable. Chayei Adam also objected to the practice of scribes who glue the housings together and coat the entire housing with plaster or gloss, merely making a mark in the gloss, especially since initially we require the groove to reach all the way down; see Biur Halacha. All the more so scribes who hang leather over the four housings and mark grooves in it with a knife so that it looks like four housings make invalid tefillin, for through this it becomes an arm tefillin.
