Seif 67: and that one wrap hide. In Pri Dishna he disputes this. And in his view, if one made a four-bayit [shel rosh] intending to have a shel yad to wrap hide upon them, it does not help, since his making was not according to law. And specifically that case in seif 42 where the initial making was valid for shel rosh tefillin — wrapping hide in that case helps. Also there one may say they were permitted to wrap hide only for a time, because of pressing need when one has no shel yad tefillin; but to leave them thus wrapped in hide one may say it is forbidden.
