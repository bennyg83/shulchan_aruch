They were written out of order. In the matter of seif 16; and there are those who say no division from above, for there two zayins without a roof, and here with a roof; and there are those who say most poskim did not write thus — they write in name of "there are those who say" even though no division, as his good way always. And in siman 36, 62: if not touching — invalid, meaning all the time he did not fix; but if he fixed — helps all the time a child reads thus; and it appears specifically that their separation is not evident — for if separation is great and the letter changed — child does not help; and similar to two zayins and a roof — even though child reads chet, if separation evident — child does not help and it is writing out of order, in my view. And the tip of yod can be fixed afterward as Magen Avraham letter 36; in the matter of seif 36.

An incident written in tefillin: "guard, guard" twice; he erased one vav and "guard" twice; and extended the reish until the second vav — and I validated, Magen Avraham letter 33; and even if the second word were another word unrelated to tefillin at all, and in it a vav at the end — and dragged as above — also kosher — what of it; and even though initially disqualified by a vav far from "to your fathers" — so too one who did not fix and extended the bet appears as two words. And the rule: whenever he does not write the letter's form anew — even though disqualified, dragging afterward helps.

And know: if the face of the alef was touched excessively — no remedy to extend and thicken the yod, as Orach Chayyim letter 33 (in alef of the Name in a Torah scroll, see Magen Avraham letter 28) — nevertheless out of order; and Eliyahu Rabbah on yods of shin that separation not evident — and here all know this is not alef form, and all fixing letter form is out of order. And the reference should be in seif 25 more than here — and it is not a scribal error, for the reference in letter 21 refers to seif 23 as explained. See letter 21.
