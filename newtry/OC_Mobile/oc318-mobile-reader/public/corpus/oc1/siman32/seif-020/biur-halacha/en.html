Or one extra letter. If the extra letter was not attached to any word, but merely stood between one word and another, or between the lines, whether the tefillin are invalidated because of this, see Noda BiYehuda, Yoreh Deah, part 1, siman 74, who is lenient. Shaar Ephraim, Shaar 5, in Pesach Sheini, disagrees with him and concurs with Maharshal, who is stringent about this.
