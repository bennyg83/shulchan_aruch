One must be very God-fearing (as above), and all the more so not to give young apprentices to write tefillin — one will surely give account; therefore it is fitting to appoint in every district and every city proper tefillin writers, as slaughterers and inspectors are appointed (Maharam). He also wrote: those who say "write tefillin for my name" commit an act of ignorance. These are the deficient and plene spellings of our Rabbi Moshe ben Maimon, of blessed memory: bechor plene with vav; zachor plene with vav; bechazak deficient vav; hotzi deficient mem-vav; yetzi'im deficient chet-vav; bechodesh deficient chet-vav; ha'aviv deficient mem-yod; yevi'acha plene mem-yod; etc. — so listed the Rambam. However, Raavad and Rokeach wrote some plene and deficient forms in another style, but we practice only as the Rambam ruled in deficiencies and excesses.
