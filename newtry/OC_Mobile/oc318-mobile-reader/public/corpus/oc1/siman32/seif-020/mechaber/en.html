One must be exact regarding deficient and plene spellings, for if even one letter is missing or extra, they are invalid. It follows that those who put them on recite a blessing in vain every day, and also remain every day without the mitzvah of tefillin, and the scribe's punishment is very great. Therefore, one who engages in writing tefillin and repairing them must be very God-fearing and tremble before the word of Hashem.
