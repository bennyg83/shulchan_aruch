And through this all the compartments will be filled, etc. The Levush wrote that their scribes used another correction: although they made all the parchments the same thickness, they made all the parchments of the passages the same length by leaving extra margin at the end of the shorter passages; see there. This was cited by Baer Heitev, seif katan 18; Meleches Shamayim, klal 17, letter 3, in Chochmah; Keset HaSofer, siman 20, letter 3. See Mishbetzos Zahav, letter 7. Keset HaSofer there wrote: In our time, however, it seems that they do not do even this, and no one is concerned about it. End quote. So too now in our time, we have not seen any scribes who are concerned about it. It appears that they are not particular about this because there is another correction: each passage must be wrapped with one piece of parchment and placed in its compartment, as Maran wrote in seif 44. The compartments can be filled in this way: the passage of Kadesh is wrapped with very thin parchment, the passage of VeHayah Ki is wrapped with thicker parchment, and likewise the passage of Shema is wrapped with thick parchment, and one also wraps more around it; through this all the compartments will be filled. The scribes rely on this when placing the passages into the compartments.
