[49] Lamed, etc. One could say he ruled leniently, not like Rabbenu Yonah who requires leaving space like a full etzbah above the height of the lamed; and every conscientious person should be stringent for himself to do so — Beit Yosef — and the Taz wondered likewise. In Baruch She'amar I found this is his wording: "To me, Avraham, it does not seem that the world's explanation — that those who explain 'a full etzbah' mean for gripping the fingernail — is correct; rather I interpreted their explanation: surely at minimum one must leave half a fingernail's width above the neck of the lamed and below the thigh of the kaf, and the like. And their explanation that they explained 'gripping a fingernail' means above the tagin of the lamed and below the feet of the kaf so that it should be mukaf gevil" — until here. And similarly Maharil wrote in responsum siman 149 briefly in his name, and concluded that Baruch She'amar's work relies on him.
