**Need only outline upper line** — Bedieved if no outline, even upper only: per author, or all sides per Rama — depends Rabbenu Tam vs. other poskim: Rabbenu Tam — Torah scroll needs outline only on upper line per author or all sides per Rama, bedieved invalid; forced from Masechet Soferim — unlined sheet invalid, likewise tefillin one law per his view. But as we rule Yoreh Deah 271:65 Torah scroll fully outlined like mezuzah — no proof to invalidate tefillin bedieved if not outlined at all, only Gra's brief commentary — fits author did not write bedieved invalid as he wrote explicitly for Torah scroll and mezuzah.

**Some say one must, etc.** — Study if all lines outlined whether sides too needed — safek for Torah scroll and mezuzah where whole outline is lechatchilah; bedieved do not be strict even for mezuzah, not mentioned except outline between lines; especially per Radbaz cited Pischei Teshuvah Yoreh Deah — reason of outline even for mezuzah is to straighten lines — certainly do not be strict. Know: tefillin outline for all opinions need not be leshemah, as Peri Megadim wrote.

**And so is the custom** — Today all outline all lines. See Beit Shmuel: it seems good to outline lines toward hair side above and below, and on sides toward flesh side.
