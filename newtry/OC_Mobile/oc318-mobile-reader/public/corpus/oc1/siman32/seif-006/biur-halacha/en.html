He need not score except the upper line. After the fact, if he did not score even the upper line according to the Mechaber, or on every side according to the Rama, this depends on the view of Rabbeinu Tam and the other poskim. According to Rabbeinu Tam, who wrote that a Sefer Torah needs scoring only on the upper line according to the Mechaber, or on every side according to the Rama, and after the fact it is invalid without this, one is forced to say so from Maseches Sofrim, which states that a sheet that is not ruled is invalid. If so, the same applies to tefillin, for according to his view they have one law. But according to what we rule in Yoreh Deah, siman 271, seif 5, that a Sefer Torah must be scored throughout like a mezuzah, if so we have no proof to invalidate tefillin after the fact even if he did not score at all, except from the concise words of the Gra. According to his words it is understandable that the Mechaber did not write to invalidate after the fact, as he wrote explicitly in Yoreh Deah regarding a Sefer Torah and mezuzah.
And some say that it requires, etc. One should examine somewhat whether, if he scored all the lines, he must also score the sides or not. This doubt may be raised regarding a Sefer Torah and mezuzah, where all the scoring is indispensable. It appears that after the fact one should not be stringent in this, even regarding a mezuzah, because only scoring between the lines is mentioned everywhere. All the more according to what appears from Radbaz, brought in Pis’chei Teshuvah in Yoreh Deah; see there, that the reason for scoring even in a mezuzah is to straighten the lines, one certainly should not be stringent in this. Know that the scoring of tefillin, according to all opinions, need not be done for their sake, as the Pri Megadim wrote.
And so is the custom. Nowadays everyone is accustomed to score all the lines. See the book Baruch She’amar, which wrote that it seems to him good to score the lines on the hair side, and on top, bottom, and sides to score on the flesh side.
