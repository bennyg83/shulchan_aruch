(20) Top line — because regarding tefillin there is no halachah leMoshe miSinai to rule lines as with a mezuzah, only because it is forbidden to write three words of a verse without ruling; therefore when one rules a top line it suffices, for ordinary people know how to train their hand to write all lines straight after writing a top line. And he says one need not, because if he wishes to rule all lines to write more straightly to beautify the lines, the permission is in his hand. If he did not rule even a top line, see Biur Halachah:

(21) He should rule — meaning lechatchilah because of "This is my God and I will glorify Him"; but post factum, even if he did not rule and wrote crooked lines, one should not invalidate the tefillin:
