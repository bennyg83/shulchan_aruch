[6] If he threw, etc. The Levush's wording "and he shall not throw..." — Lechem Chamudot page 79 wrote that the Levush's words contradict Yoreh Deah siman 276 seif 6, which wrote that throwing gold on the other letters of the Torah is kosher, until here. I looked in the Levush there, who wrote this is his wording: "if he throws gold on them it is kosher" — until here; and if so, perhaps it deals with scraping, analogous to Divine Names that are invalid even through scraping, since the Levush here deals with after the fact — he wrote: if he threw, let it dry and afterward scrape.
