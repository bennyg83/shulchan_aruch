If he sprinkled, etc. The language of the Levush is, “and he should not sprinkle, etc.” Lechem Chamudos, folio 79, wrote that the words of the Levush contradict one another, for in Yoreh Deah, siman 276, seif 6, he wrote that sprinkling gold over the other letters of a Torah scroll is valid, end quote. I looked in the Levush there, and this is what he wrote: if he sprinkles gold over them, it is valid, end quote. If so, perhaps he is speaking of scraping, similar to the Divine Names, which are invalid even with scraping; for he is speaking after the fact, as the Levush wrote: if he sprinkled, he should leave it to dry and afterward remove it.
