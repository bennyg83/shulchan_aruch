(Seif katan 6) One should not score, etc. In Maseches Sofrim there is written here a gloss: it is not necessary to score. Meaning, according to Magen Avraham the text in Shulchan Aruch read, 'one should not score,' language implying that scoring is improper, as stated in the Yerushalmi. The text before us reads, 'it is not necessary'; the word 'necessary' is the Rama's gloss. This wording implies that if one wants to score, even though he knows how to keep the line straight, he may still score. The Bach disagreed with this and wrote that this is not Beis Yosef's view.
