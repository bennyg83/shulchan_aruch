(Note 6) One should not rule lines, etc., and in some texts it is written here in the haga: "One need not rule lines." That is, according to Magen Avraham the version in the Shulchan Aruch was "One should not rule lines" — this wording implies that it is not proper to rule lines, as written in the Yerushalmi; and what is before us, "One need not" — the word "need" is a haga of the Rama, and from this word it implies that if one wants to rule lines, even if he knows how to straighten the line, nevertheless he may rule lines; and on this the Bach disagreed and wrote that the Rabbi's view is not so.
