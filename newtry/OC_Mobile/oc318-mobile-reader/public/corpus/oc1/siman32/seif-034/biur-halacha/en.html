One word. See the Mishnah Berurah. So wrote the Rosh in Hilchos Ketanos, and he ruled accordingly in Yoreh Deah. See Magen Avraham, s.k. 46, who wrote that even the Rambam agrees to be lenient in this case, while the Gra in his commentary wrote that according to the Rambam it is forbidden.
