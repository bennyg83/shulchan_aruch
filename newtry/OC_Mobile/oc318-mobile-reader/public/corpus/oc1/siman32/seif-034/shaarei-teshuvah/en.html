The writing. Be'er Heitev wrote in Ginat Veradim siman 10 and 11: in writing the holy Name per Sefer Matzat Shmurim one may rely, for they derive from the place of our Rabbi the Ari, may he rest in peace; and one need not stutter because the Name's letters must be written as separate parts and he tarries in writing — even though writing at once is preferable as implied in Yoma — nevertheless here intent in Name sanctity is needed, and also in writing one letter he is occupied — no concern; and no difference if he starts from end to beginning, etc., see there.
