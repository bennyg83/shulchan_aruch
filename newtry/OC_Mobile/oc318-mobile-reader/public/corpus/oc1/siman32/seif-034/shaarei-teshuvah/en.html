The writing. See Baer Heitev. Ginas Veradim writes that regarding writing the holy Name according to Sefer Matzas Shimurim one may rely on it, for it flows from the holy source of the Arizal. There is no concern that he initially writes the letters of the Name as separated parts and delays somewhat in writing, since there is a need for intent in the sanctity of the Name, and while occupied in writing a single letter there is no objection. There is also no distinction whether he begins from the end toward the beginning, etc.
