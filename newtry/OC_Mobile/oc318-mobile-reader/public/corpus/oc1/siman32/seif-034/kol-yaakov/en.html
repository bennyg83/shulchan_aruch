34) Seif 8. The parchment must be processed, etc. He should leave the hide in lime until the hair falls off by itself; if he removes it before then, he should not write on it, for it is diftera. Magen Avraham, seif katan 9; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 17; GraZ, letter 10; Keset HaSofer, siman 12, letter 6; Meleches Shamayim, klal 2, letter 2, in Chochmah.
