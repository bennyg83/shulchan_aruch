(ibid.) In Magen Avraham, se'if kaf-dalet — permitted to scrape. See note: see responsa Chatam Sofer Orach Chayyim, siman 178 [there he was asked about a Torah scroll in which an error was found — a kaf peshuta was written in place of a hei, and the scribe dragged from the foot of the kaf peshuta until it became a dalet and made in it a left leg, and it became a hei; afterward the place of the above correction was not found and it is not known now where it is. There is room to doubt whether this is disqualified because of engraving the hollow, and if it is disqualified, nevertheless, since it was lost, whether it is permitted to read it in public in this Torah scroll that lacks this letter. He concludes there on the first doubt, and these are his words: if so, in our case it is possible that according to all it is invalid because he did not correct it in writing but only the left leg of the hei, and it is not one golem with the hei; even if we speak of an open mem, when he corrected only the serif in writing it suffices for all mems, even though there are two letters in view — nevertheless they help each other because in any case they are one golem; not so hei and kuf, which are two golems — it is possible without correcting in writing in that very golem, and correcting the leg does not help for engraving the dalet — requires study. On the second doubt he extends whether there is nullification of this letter and concludes: but in any case, from the essential Torah law it was nullified and the invalid returned to be kosher, and only rabbinically something important is not nullified; if so, one may combine here the view of Rambam in his responsum and the act of a rabbi, that they read before the Geonim in a Torah scroll in which one letter was invalid, with the view of Rashba and Ran that he disqualifies only that pentateuch, etc.; and here, since from the body of the law the letter was already nullified and became kosher again, we can combine the two above views; and in order not to bring the scroll to burial, it seems to me to rely on the above to read it in public and bless on it. See the responsum inside well, and Chatam Sofer Yoreh De'ah, siman 277.
