So Maran ruled as halachah in Beis Yosef, unlike Hagahos Semak, who wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yechiel that regarding head tefillin, since they are four parchments, one need not be particular about the order of their writing. Although Rashbash, siman 632, upheld the words of Hagahos Semak, the halachah is not so. Machazik Berachah, letter 2; Chikrei Lev, siman 7; Zecher LeAvraham, letter tav; Shaarei Teshuvah, letter 3; and so wrote Meleches Shamayim, rule 8, letter 1, in Chochmah; Keset HaSofer, siman 9, letter 1; and Imrei Shefer, rule 20, letter 1, in plain terms, without distinguishing between hand tefillin and head tefillin.
