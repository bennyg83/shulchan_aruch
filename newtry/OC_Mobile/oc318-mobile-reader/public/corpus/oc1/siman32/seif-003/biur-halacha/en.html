They shall be written in black ink — See Rambam, chapter 1 of Laws of Tefillin: the law stated "they shall write in ink" excludes only other dyes such as red and green; anything producing black appearance like ink is kosher if it endures. So in Laws of a Torah scroll, one of twenty disqualifications: written without enduring black. If appearance resembles blue [French: bleu], we should be strict; proof from Chullin 87b — kuchla is kosher like ink, afatzim invalid; from Rambam, halachah means black appearance; we decided below, siman 33, on straps too. Here, since halachah requires ink, even if ink is not precise for Rambam, any black appearance suffices if like ink. All the more for Rosh and Shitah Mekubetzet requiring actual ink — blue certainly not ink. If ink resembled raven-black, also not ink — Niddah 20a: ink blacker than raven; "black like ink" — impure as raven, totally blackened, pure; Midrash "black as raven" — these letters; we do not learn halachah from aggadah; or strained: black as raven is black like ink, but wet ink is pure as Gemara shows. Even if nature blackens completely after writing — consider bedi'eved; halachah said write in ink (Yerushalmi), not that scroll must be written in ink; Shulchan Arukh implies ink black at writing; also Shulchan Arukh means especially black even from afatzim and kankantum, unlike Rabbenu Tam, as Beit Yosef explained — difficult.

Whether it contains astringent water — The author stated generally without explaining source material; even from kankantum and astringent water, or gum and astringent water — kosher, for two kinds together are called ink — unlike Rabbenu Tam who forbids, as Beit Yosef explained [so Gra, siman 691 below and here — Shulchan Arukh wrote only "contains," not "even from astringent alone," settling Peri Megadim's difficulty]. Rama added: lekhatchilah be strict not to mix kankantum or gum, being indelible writing, as Yoreh De'ah, siman 271, seif 6 — bedi'eved with astringent and kankantum kosher [gum too is indelible, Sotah 17a Mishnah]; Rambam, Laws of Tefillin 1:4. Rama does not mean lekhatchilah only from smoke not wood [see Magen Avraham and Eliyahu Rabbah] — in Yoreh De'ah, siman 271, Rama wrote lekhatchilah better from tree matter. Later in Az Nafshi, Olat Tamid explained so. Or: Rama limits Beit Yosef's general "black ink" implying especially black since enduring and visible on parchment as ink — Rabbeinu Simchah; Rama added lekhatchilah be strict per Rabbenu Tam — specifically from tree.

Whether not, etc. — See below, siman 691, and Yoreh De'ah, siman 271: Shulchan Arukh — written with astringent and kankantum kosher. Peri Megadim: from omitting gum, even without gum kosher; he is uncertain whether specifically both together without gum, or each alone since black. Gra rules astringent alone or kankantum alone invalid even bedi'eved — not called ink except two kinds together: astringent and gum, astringent and kankantum, gum and kankantum without astringent, or wood smoke and oils — Gra's view unclear; Maharam Benet on Mordechai is strict lekhatchilah; kankantum alone or gum alone strict even bedi'eved like Gra; Mishkenot Ya'akov, siman 37, strict even astringent with kankantum without gum. Bedi'eved certainly do not disqualify astringent with kankantum against Shulchan Arukh, siman 691 and Yoreh De'ah 271; lekhatchilah heed his words: if making ink from astringent, even mixed with kankantum, do not omit gum; same for astringent and gum alone. See Mishkenot Ya'akov for supporting poskim; he wrote lekhatchilah boil per Ramban and Ran when possible.

These are invalid — If first letter or all written so and later in Torah scroll wants to pass quill in order with ink — uncertain if helps; Peri Megadim in Magen Tzedek, letter 15; Chatam Sofer, Yoreh De'ah, siman 256 — safek invalid, general ink over sikra; Gittin 19a; law with Eliyahu Rabbah, not Peri Megadim — see there.

Gold dust — Same for other dyes on ink — invalid for writing; no fix by passing over; Orach Tzedek and Eliyahu Rabbah — ineffective; on a divine name letter no fix even covering with ink — Peri Megadim rejected this proof; consider safek invalid like ink over dye after lower and upper are ink — cannot elaborate.

He passes over the gold — Perishah: see Yoreh De'ah, siman 276 — Ribash on numbered writing if many letters on many pages; Lechem Chamudos and Eliyahu Rabbah challenged — Ribash does not refer to Nekudot Yosef throwing gold on ink where numbered writing irrelevant since lower writing stands; Ribash on main law: writing in gold or dyes themselves, scraping and rewriting in ink — numbered writing applies; he elaborated; Birkhei Yosef, siman 276, agrees with Eliyahu Rabbah; so Bnei Yonah.

As erasing the Name — Actual erasure is not so — lower writing remains; even gold not in divine Name context — forbidden to erase unlike Shach, Yoreh De'ah, siman 276, seif kaf-yod-bet. Peri Megadim.
