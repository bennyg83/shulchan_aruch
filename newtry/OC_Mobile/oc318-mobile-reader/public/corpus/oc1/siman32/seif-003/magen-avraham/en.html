Also, in Yoreh Deah he wrote that initially one should make ink from something that comes from a tree, which implies that they were not particular about smoke. The Rambam wrote that it is a mitzvah to write with ink that can be erased. In Responsa Maharil, Dapim Heh, siman 127, he wrote that many great authorities validated our ink, and you will find this in Hagahos Maimoniyos. Nevertheless, since Rabbeinu Tam invalidates gallnut ink, Rashi invalidates adramint, and our other rabbis invalidated glotshteyn, it is proper to be stringent in order to satisfy all opinions and to write with shtoftint, which is made from thorns, or with herbal ink, as the Maimonides wrote, end quote. See chapter 2 of Shabbos, where it implies that they made ink from the smoke of oils.
