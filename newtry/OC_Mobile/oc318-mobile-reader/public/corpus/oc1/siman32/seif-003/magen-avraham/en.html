From wood smoke. I have not seen any of the great authorities practice thus; likewise in Yoreh De'ah he wrote that ab initio one should make ink from something that comes from a tree — implying they are not particular about smoke. The Rambam wrote it is a mitzvah to write with ink that can be erased. In responsum of Maharil, s.v., siman 127, he wrote that our ink — many authorities validated it — and you will find this in Hagahot Maimoniyot. Nevertheless, since Rabbeinu Tam disqualifies oak-gall ink and Rashi disqualifies vitriol, and many of our teachers disqualified glue-based ink, it is proper to be strict to satisfy all of them and write with gallnut ink, which is of thorns, or with plant-based ink, as the Maimonidean authority wrote. See ch. 2 of Shabbos, which implies they made ink from oily smoke.
