"From wood smoke." I have not seen any of the great authorities practice this. Likewise, in Yoreh De'ah it is written that ideally the ink should be made from a substance derived from a tree, which implies that they were not particular that it specifically be made from smoke. Rambam writes that it is a mitzvah to write with ink that can be erased. Responsa Maharil, siman 127, writes that many great authorities validated our ink, as may be found in Hagahot Maimoniyot. Nevertheless, since Rabbeinu Tam invalidates gall-based ink, Rashi invalidates adramint, and other authorities invalidated glotshteyn, it is proper to be stringent in order to satisfy all opinions and to write with shtoftint, which is made from thorns, or with herbal ink, as Hagahot Maimoniyot writes. See also the second chapter of Shabbos, which implies that they made ink from the smoke of oils.
