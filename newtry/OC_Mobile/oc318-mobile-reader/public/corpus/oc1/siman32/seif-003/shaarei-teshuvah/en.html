Of the hand. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Sefer HaKavanos. One must be careful as much as possible to write all four parshiyos of the head or of the hand continuously, without any speech interruption at all, and certainly not in the middle. If he is old or ill and cannot write all four at once, he should write the first three and then the fourth. If this too is difficult, he should at least write the first two parshiyos continuously and afterward the other two together. But between the first and second parsha he should not interrupt at all. If he did interrupt, he should pass the pen over the opening words of the earlier parshiyos to create some connection. Before beginning, he should pray that the Shechinah rest upon his handiwork, that he succeed in writing the tefillin for the sanctity of the mitzvah of tefillin, and that he be saved from mistakes in writing and intent. It is proper to say such a shortened prayer.
