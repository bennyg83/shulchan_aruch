Black. See Beis Yosef. One should not ask that this baraisa in the chapter HaBoneh was established in the chapter HaKometz as referring to a Sefer Torah, and not to tefillin and mezuzos, regarding that which they said there: “They asked: what is the law concerning two and three and one, etc.?” and we answer, “When that baraisa was taught, it was taught regarding a Sefer Torah”; see there. Tosafos likewise wrote in the chapter Bameh Madlikin that a Sefer Torah requires ink from this sugya in the chapter HaKometz. See Yad Aharon, who resolves it. It is permitted to write tefillin and mezuzos with ink made from ordinary gentile wine: Devar Shmuel, simanim 162 and 164; Yad Eliyahu, siman 35; and see Be’er Esek, siman 109.
