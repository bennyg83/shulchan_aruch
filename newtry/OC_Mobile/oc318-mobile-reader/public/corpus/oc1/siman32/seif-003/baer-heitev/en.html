Black. See Beit Yosef; do not challenge this baraita of the chapter HaBoneh — we establish it in the chapter HaKometz regarding a Torah scroll, not tefillin and mezuzot, on that which is asked there "two and three and one, what is the law," etc., and we answer that when that baraita is taught it refers to a Torah scroll; see there. So too Tosafot in the chapter Bameh Madlikin — a Torah scroll requires ink from this sugya of HaKometz; see Yad Aharon who reconciles it. It is permitted to write Torah, tefillin, and mezuzot with ink made from ordinary wine — Devar Shmuel siman 262, 264; Yad Eliyahu siman 35; see Be'er Eshek siman 109.

Wood. Magen Avraham wrote: I have not seen any of the great authorities practice so; see there.
