Magen Avraham comment 4 — if he wrote with the left hand, it is kosher. Explanation for one who examines: Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Sefer HaTerumah that he must write with the right. Proof from the passage of the handful: we say, "and you shall bind them and write them" — just as writing is with the right, so binding is with the right. And even bedieved it is invalid if he wrote with the left, from the passage of the builder who writes whether with his right or his left; and we challenge: but there is no manner of writing with the left! And they answer: one who is ambidextrous. Thus there is no writing with the left — see there. And it seems puzzling, for the proofs contradict each other: from the first proof, even one ambidextrous must write with the right, for we rule that even one ambidextrous places tefillin on the left because of "writing with the right" — thus even in such a case writing is with the right. Whereas from the second proof, the builder passage, one who is ambidextrous — writing with the left also counts as writing. Therefore one must say that from the handful it proves only lekhatchilah; this is indeed so, that even for one ambidextrous writing is lekhatchilah with the right, and only regarding bedieved invalidity does he bring proof from the builder — and that is only for one who is skilled with the right alone. And the Magen Avraham's words on this are clear.
