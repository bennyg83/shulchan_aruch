They shall be written in black ink, whether it contains gallnut water or does not contain gallnut water. {Rama: Ideally, one should be stringent to write with ink made from the soot of wood or oils soaked in gallnut water. [Maharil, siman 119] This is as will be explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 271.} If even 1 letter was written with another type of coloring substance or with gold, they are invalid. If gold dust was cast upon the letters, one removes the gold and the writing underneath remains, and it is valid. But if gold was cast upon a letter of a Divine Name, there is no remedy, because it is forbidden to remove the gold, since doing so is considered erasing the Divine Name.
