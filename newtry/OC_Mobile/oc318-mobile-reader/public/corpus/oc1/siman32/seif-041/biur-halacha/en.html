The length and width of the housings, etc. — See Eruvin 93b in the Gemara, and see well in Rosh in Hilchot Ketanot, in the order of repairing tefillin, briefly what he wrote regarding the measure of tefillin, and in Biur HaGra on this se'if. In any case it seems to me that even according to Shulchan Aruch, who rules anonymously that they have no measure — nevertheless, as the Gemara proves regarding the head, and likewise the arm, there is room only to place two tefillin and not three; one should in any case be careful not to wear tefillin once very small and once so large as three times the first — for example, the first time they were a finger and a half by a finger and a half, and the second time four and a half fingers by four and a half fingers, and the like — for either way, either the first tefillin were smaller than the measure, or the second are placed not in the place of tefillin. See below, siman 233, se'if 1, that one should not make two contradictory matters even not on one day. Even though one could reject this — that in the time of the Gemara their tefillin were average, like two fingers and more, unlike here — but if so, the view of Rosh there and Shulchan Aruch, who ruled not like Rosh, is not mentioned among the acharonim that it should be because of this, but for another reason; see Beit Yosef and Biur HaGra. The correct thing for a person is that his tefillin always be average — that is, not less than the measure of two fingers by two fingers, and not more than four by four fingers — that is, with the titura and ma'avarta; in this way he will in any case satisfy everyone under the law.

And their height — See Magen Avraham.

It has no measure — See Shulchan Shlomo, who wrote that nevertheless one should not make tefillin very large, for thereby it will not be possible to place them on the place of hair growth; likewise on the arm, lest one come to place them partly on the half-bone near the armpit, or on a place that is not flesh of the biceps — so far. See what I wrote above, siman 27, se'if 9, in Biur Halachah.
