Measure. Nevertheless, one should be concerned for the words of the Geonim and not make them smaller than the width of two fingers, whether the hand-tefillin or the head-tefillin. Beit Yosef, Bach, Lechem Chamudos, Magen Avraham. This refers to the titura below. One should protest against travelers who have very small tefillin made for themselves, for it is clear from the Talmud that they must be wider than a finger; otherwise they are invalid.
