Measure. Nevertheless one should heed the Geonim not to make them smaller than the width of two fingers, whether arm or head — Beit Yosef, Beit Chadash, Lechem Chamudot, Magen Avraham — that is the titura below; one should protest passersby who make very small tefillin, for it is clear from the Talmud they must be wider than a finger, otherwise invalid — Orach Tzedek; see there.
