41 — Length, etc. But Tosafot disagree on this; and Tosafot Eruvin 95b, s.v. makom, etc.; so Shitah Mekubetzet, Sefer HaTerumah, Semag, Mordechai; and Tosafot Sukkah 5a, s.v. velo, etc.; Yoma 12b, 25a; their proof: the tzitz is in place of tefillin — from what the Torah called the High Priest's mitznefet and the ordinary priest's migba'ot; except the High Priest's was shorter because of tzitz and tefillin, unlike the ordinary priest who has only tefillin. See Tosafot Sukkah 5a. But Rambam, chapter 8 of Laws of Temple Vessels, wrote the turban mentioned regarding Aaron is the migba'ot mentioned regarding his sons — except the High Priest wraps it like one wrapping on a break, and his sons wrap it like a hat; therefore it is called migba'ot; so Ramban in Parashat Tetzaveh; he brought proof from what is written at the end of chapter 7 of Yoma; the ordinary priest with four, etc., adds, etc.; end of the chapter: the mitznefet of the High Priest or ordinary priest — its length sixteen cubits, etc.
