Length and width. Nevertheless, it is proper to be concerned for the words of the Geonim, who hold that initially one should not make them smaller than two fingerbreadths by two fingerbreadths, and this includes the titura. See Olas Tamid, who wrote that if, including the titura, it holds only about a fingerbreadth by a fingerbreadth, it is invalid even after the fact. So ruled the book Biur Mordechai. See the book Eliyah Rabbah, who defended them, but nevertheless concluded that every conscientious person should be careful to make the titura two fingerbreadths wide. Apart from all this, it is very common among these small tefillin that their passages are extremely poor because of the cramped space, as I have seen many of them with my own eyes; therefore one who guards his soul should guard himself from them.
