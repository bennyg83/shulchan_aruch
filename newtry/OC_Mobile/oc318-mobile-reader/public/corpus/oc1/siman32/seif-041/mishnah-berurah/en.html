(189) Length and width — and nevertheless it is correct to be concerned for the words of the geonim who hold that l'chatchila one should not make them smaller than two fingerbreadths by two fingerbreadths, and that is including the titura; see Aruch HaShulchan who wrote that if it does not include the titura but only a fingerbreadth on a fingerbreadth, even b'dieved it is invalid; and so ruled Sefer Biur Mordechai; and see Sefer Eliyah Rabbah who learned merit for them; nevertheless he concluded that every person of spirit should be careful to make the titura two fingerbreadths wide by itself. All these are very common in those small tefillin whose parshiyot are very poor because of the pressure of space — as I myself saw many of them with my eyes; therefore one who guards his soul will guard against them:
