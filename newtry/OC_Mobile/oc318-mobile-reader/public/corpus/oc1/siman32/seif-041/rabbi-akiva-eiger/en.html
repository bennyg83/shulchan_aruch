Magen Avraham, note 66: and in my humble opinion, a different reason. And one may say Beit Yosef gave a reason to forbid buying from a gentile even before twisting. Also the essential law — one may say twisting for its sake is not required, for twisting was not stated at Sinai; only that one sew them with sinews. And if one takes thin sinews, he can sew without twisting. Nazir Shimshon.
