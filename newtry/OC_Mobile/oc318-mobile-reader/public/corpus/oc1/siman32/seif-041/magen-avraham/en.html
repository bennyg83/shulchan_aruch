It has no measure. Nevertheless one should heed the words of the Geonim who say not to make them as small as the width of two fingers [Beit Yosef, Bach, siman 38]. So Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chayyim: the custom of sages and fathers of court is to make shel rosh taller than three fingers, since they place them exposed; but sages, disciples, and a disciple before his rabbi who do not customarily expose their tefillin place small ones like a finger and a half under the turban, because of derech eretz that they not compare themselves to their rabbis, etc., end quote — see siman 27.
