Nevertheless, one should be concerned for the words of the Geonim, who say not to make them as small as the width of two fingers, as cited by the Beis Yosef, Bach, and Lechem Chamudos. The Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Orchos Chaim that the custom of sages and heads of beis din was to make the shel rosh higher than three fingers, since they put them on visibly. But sages, students, and a student before his teacher, where it is not the way to reveal his tefillin before him, put on small ones, about a finger and a half, under the head-covering, out of proper respect, so that they not make themselves equal to their teachers. See the end of siman 27.
