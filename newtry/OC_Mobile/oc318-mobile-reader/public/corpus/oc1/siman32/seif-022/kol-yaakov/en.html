Se'if 5, he must write with his right hand, etc. And if he wrote with his left hand it is invalid, etc.; this refers to one whose dominant hand is the right. But one who controls both hands, if he wrote with his left hand, it is valid. Magen Avraham, s.k. 4; Eliyah Rabbah, s.k. 10; Halachah Berurah, letter 4; Yeshuos Yaakov, letter 6; GraZ, letter 7; Gra, letter 15; Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger; Keset HaSofer, siman 3, letter 7. Nevertheless, a person of spiritual sensitivity should be concerned for himself, since he lays tefillin on the left arm and therefore should specifically write with the right hand, as I wrote in the holy work Kaf HaChaim, siman 27, letter 31; see there.
