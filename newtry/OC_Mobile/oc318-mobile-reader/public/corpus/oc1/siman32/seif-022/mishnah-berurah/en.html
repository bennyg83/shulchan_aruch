(ka) before it begins - it is not RL at the beginning of the parasha but before he begins to write in the parasha:

(Ku) What he wrote - in the same episode and not the later one:

(Kaz) By his hand - when the quill is dipped to write the name in it, he will not start writing it immediately so that we don't lose too much ink or maybe there is a gate on it and the writing does not come out straight and also because the ink on the quill needs to be sanctified before the name is written. In the letters before the name that are still wet, the name will be finished but will not be dipped in the letters of the name Themselves, and one must dip in the letters of the name themselves Debizoi Licha since it is to complete the name with it. And if the letters in front of him are not composed, then he will dip again and look for a letter or tag that needs ink, etc.
