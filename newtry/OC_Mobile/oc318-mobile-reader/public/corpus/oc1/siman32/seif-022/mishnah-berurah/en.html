(Ku) What he wrote - in the same episode and not the later one:
By means of it. When he dips the quill in order to write the Divine Name, he should not begin writing it immediately, lest he spoil it with too much ink, or perhaps there is a hair on it and the writing will not come out straight. Also, because he must sanctify the ink on the quill before writing the Name, he should have in mind to leave one letter unwritten before the Name, and begin writing with that letter. If he did not do so, he should search for a letter or tag that needs ink, fill it in, and then write the Name. If he needs ink before finishing the Name, he should dip into the letters before the Name, if they are still wet, and finish the Name; but he should not dip into the letters of the Name itself. Some are lenient to dip into the letters of the Name itself, for there is no disgrace since it is for the purpose of completing the Name with it. If the preceding letters are not wet, he should dip anew and search for a letter or tag that needs ink, as above. All this is for the preferred performance of the mitzvah, but it does not prevent validity after the fact.
