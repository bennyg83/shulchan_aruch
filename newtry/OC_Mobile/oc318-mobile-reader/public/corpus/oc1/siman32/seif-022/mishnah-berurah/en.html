(105) Before he begins — not meaning only at the beginning of the parshah but before he begins to write in the parshah:

(106) What he wrote — in that parshah and not what he will write after:

(107) By his hand — when dipping the quill to write the Name in it, he should not begin writing it immediately lest he ruin it from excess ink or perhaps there is a hair on it and the writing will not come out straight; also because he must sanctify the ink on the quill before writing the Name. Therefore he should intend to leave one letter unwritten before the Name and begin writing with that letter. If he did not do so, he should seek another letter or tag needing ink and fill it and write the Name. If he needs ink before finishing the Name, he should dip in letters before the Name that are still wet and finish the Name, but not dip in the letters of the Name themselves; some are lenient to dip in the letters of the Name themselves, for there is no disgrace since it is to complete the Name with them.
