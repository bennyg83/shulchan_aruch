Until seif katan 31. See Yad Ephraim, where you will find an explanation of the matters that Magen Avraham brought from Responsa HaGedolim, from which many strong halachos may be learned. Ginas Veradim writes of a scribe who extended the roof of a final kaf to complete the line so that it resembled a large daled or reish; in pressing circumstances there is room to be lenient, since the thin writing proves it is not a large letter but was only extended to complete the line. If there are no other tefillin, one may bless on these. If it occurred in the kaf of 'your God,' he may not scrape it and rewrite it, for perhaps the Divine Name was already sanctified. In other letters and words he may scrape the whole letter and rewrite it. Correcting by scraping a little of the width is valid only in pressing circumstances. See also the cited responsa concerning the form of a final kaf and over-extended points of yuds.
