Up to seder katan 31. See Yad Ephraim — you will find explanation of matters Magen Avraham brought from great responsa, and how halachah is weighty — there is to infer; and he wrote in Ginat Veradim klal 20 siman 5: a scribe happened to have a simple kaf to complete the line and extended the kaf's roof until the end — and thereby it resembles dalet or large reish, invalid per several poskim; one may be lenient in pressing need, for the matter is evident by writing with a thin quill that is not large letters — only extended to complete the line. And if he has no other tefillin he may bless on these. If it happened in kaf of Elohekha — forbidden to drag and rewrite, for perhaps the Name was already sanctified; in other letters and words he may drag all and rewrite; but to fix by scraping some width to make simple kaf as correction — no kosher except in pressing need, as before correction, see there. And see Magen Avraham in name of responsum Mahari Halevi and Yad Ephraim on this. And he wrote in Birkhei Yosef on form of simple kaf: if written simply and with corners like dalet — Maharash Garboni disqualifies and Maharam Galanti responsum 124 validated; and so Maharik in a Torah scroll testified he saw a Torah scroll Maharal ben Chabib wrote for himself and the kaf on it in form of dalet; and so in Lekutei Peri Chadash Orach Chayyim. And see Zichron Yosef siman 2: if the scribe lengthened yod tips more than proper until equal to right legs — one should not erase the whole tip and rewrite, especially in yod of the Name; it suffices to drag the tip's length from its proper measure; one who wants to be strict may pass the quill over the remaining tip, etc., see there.
