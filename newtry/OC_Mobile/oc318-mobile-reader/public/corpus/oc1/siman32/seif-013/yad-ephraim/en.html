(in Magen Avraham note 35) The leg of the aleph. Meaning: regarding the leg one must scrape the entire leg, and before the aleph one must scrape as stated above in seif 18 — that is, if he writes the middle line first and then writes the lower yod or upper yod and touches it, he must scrape the yods; and if he wrote the upper yod first and then when writing the line touches it, he must scrape the line and the lower yod; and likewise in reverse if he wrote the lower one first:
