There But if after it was written. This is because a hole in the thickness of a letter is more lenient even than a hole inside the hollow, and certainly more lenient than a hole outside the letter, as is explained in the responsum of Maharlanch cited in the Beis Yosef. Consequently, if the hole is outside the letter, we validate it in seif 15 when it was made after writing; all the more so with a hole in the thickness of the letter.
