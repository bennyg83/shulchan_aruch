Into two. Meaning at the time of writing it became closed — the ink passes over it and the hole is not felt by the quill; even though a thin hole is visible against the sun it is valid — Beit Chadash, Magen Avraham, Shekhinat Hashem-Geshem, Orach Tzedek. But the Taz wrote we require a small hole such that ink covers it and it is not visible at all against the sun; see there. All this is before writing; but if pierced after writing, even if the letter was divided in two, it is valid if a child can read it — Beit Chadash, Shekhinat Hashem-Geshem, Orach Tzedek, Taz; see seif 16 and responsum Mateh Yosef chelek 1 siman 1 and responsum Torat Chaim chelek 3, Kuntras Olelot HaKerem.
