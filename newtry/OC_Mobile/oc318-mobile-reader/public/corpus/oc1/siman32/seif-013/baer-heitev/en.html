That is to say, if at the time of writing the hole was sealed, for the ink passed over it and the hole was not felt by the quill, even though a fine hole is visible against the sun, it is valid: Bach, Magen Avraham, Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah, Olas Tamid. But the Taz writes that there must be such a tiny hole there that the ink seals it and it is not visible at all against the sun; see there. All this is before the writing. But if it was punctured after the writing, even if the letter was divided into two, it is valid, provided that a child can read it: Bach, Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah, Olas Tamid, Taz. See seif 16, Responsa Mateh Yosef, part 1, siman 1, and Responsa Toras Chaim, part 3, in Kuntres Olelos HaKerem.
