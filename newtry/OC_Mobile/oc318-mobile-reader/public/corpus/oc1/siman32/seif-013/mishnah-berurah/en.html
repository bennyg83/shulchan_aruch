(32) Into two—[a hole] so small that when passing a quill over it the hole is blocked by ink and the hole is not felt by the quill; they write over it even if a little ink fell in that place and a fine hole appears against the sun it is valid; but if pierced so much that ink does not pass over it, it is invalid because the letter appears divided in two by it—even if the hole is in the middle of the letter's thickness on its roof or leg and ink surrounds on all sides it is invalid, even if up to the place of the hole there is letter shape. All this before writing; but if after writing the letter was divided in two by a hole, we see whether it has letter shape up to the place of the hole—it is valid, as explained below in seif 16; see Biur Halachah.
