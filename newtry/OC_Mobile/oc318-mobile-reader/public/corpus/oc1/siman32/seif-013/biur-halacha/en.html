The letter, etc., is divided in two. See the Mishnah Berurah at the end of s.k. 32, where he wrote that if after the writing, etc., we see whether it has the form of a letter, etc. If the hole that was made after the writing is only in the middle of the thickness of the letter, perhaps there is room to be lenient like the Taz even if there is no remaining letter until that place, since the form of the letter was not changed thereby. But from the Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger, s.v. but, who remains requiring study regarding the Taz, it appears that he questions this stringently. According to the explanation of the Levushei Serad in the Taz, we can say simply that the Taz too would agree here to be stringent, and the Taz discusses only where there remains a letter until that place. His proof is from seif 16, and what he brought from the case where its interior was punctured was not precise; see there. Therefore this law requires study.
