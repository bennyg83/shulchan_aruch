The letter, etc., split in two — See Mishnah Berurah end of seif kaf-bet: if after writing one sees whether letter form remains; if the hole after writing is only in the middle of the letter's thickness, one may lean lenient like Taz even without letter remnant to that point, since letter form did not change — a chiddush of Ra'avad, s.v. aval shenish'ar; on Taz it seems he doubts being strict. Per Eliyah Rabba on Taz we can say simply Taz would also be strict; Taz discusses only where letter remnant remains to that point; his proof from Taz and what he brought from piercing its interior is not precise; see there. Therefore this law is difficult.
