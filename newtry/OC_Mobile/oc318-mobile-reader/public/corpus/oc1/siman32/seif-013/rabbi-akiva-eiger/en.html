Taz, seif katan 10. That if it happened that the scribe wrote. In my humble opinion, the Bach also agrees to this; rather, he holds that if it was severed after writing, and there is the measure of a letter by joining the lower part with the upper part, it is valid. Regarding this the Bach wrote that if from the beginning of the writing it was severed, a child’s reading does not help. This, in my humble opinion, is clearly the view of the Bach, as I wrote above.
