The parchment must be whole, etc. (as above). But if the letter does not appear divided in two, even though a thin hole is visible against the sun, it is valid; but if it was pierced so much that the letter appears divided in two, it is invalid and cannot be fixed even if a child can read it — and all this is before writing. But if after writing the letter was divided in two by a hole, it is valid if a child can read it (Beit Yosef siman 19).
