(Seif katan 13) With an imprint, etc. For in Yoreh De'ah siman 118, etc., it is written that two letters are considered like two seals. Therefore, although meat sent through a non-Jew requires two seals, two letters suffice. He wrote that some say that in a place, etc., writing is not a sign, etc. In Beis Shmuel he wrote that one should write at the top on the inside, etc., because their practice then was not to process the head. If so, the Jew's writing would remain on it until after the processing. But on the outside one cannot write because of the hair, and it also lasts better on the inside than on the outside.
