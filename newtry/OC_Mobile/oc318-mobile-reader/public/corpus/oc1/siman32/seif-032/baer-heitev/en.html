Of a lamed. The Bach ruled that one must leave the measure of half a fingernail above the head of the lamed. See the Taz, who says the same applies below.
A letter. After the fact, this does not invalidate unless they appear as one letter according to a child who is neither especially wise nor especially foolish. Taz and Olas Tamid; so ruled the Knesses HaGedolah.
To mark a break. The Magen Avraham ruled that one should not leave any blank space at all, and likewise between one line and another it is not necessary; see there. See also Darkei Moshe and Olas Tamid. In the book Eliyah Rabbah he ruled like the Rama, that one must leave a blank space between one verse and another, and he brought proof from the book Baruch SheAmar, who explicitly wrote so, and likewise between one line and another; see there.
