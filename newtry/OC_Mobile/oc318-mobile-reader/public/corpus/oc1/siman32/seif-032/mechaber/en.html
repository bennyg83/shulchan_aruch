He must leave blank space above equal to the roof of a Lamed. Rama: so that they too will be surrounded by parchment. Responsa of Maharil, siman 134, and Beis Shmuel. Below, he must leave the measure of a final Kaf and final Nun. At their beginning and end he need not leave any space at all. Rama: Nevertheless, the scribes are accustomed to leave a little at the beginning and end. Agur, Rokeach. One must leave between each word and word the width of a letter, and likewise between the lines the space of a line, and between each letter the width of a hair, as in a Sefer Torah and as will be explained in the Tur, Yoreh Deah. One must also leave a little blank space between one verse and another.
