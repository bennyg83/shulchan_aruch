(s.k. 32) At first, etc. For when he dips the quill, he must sanctify the ink, etc., with one letter, etc. However, in Yoreh Deah, siman 276, seif 4, he wrote another reason in the name of the Rosh, as follows: when he dips, etc., he should not immediately begin writing the Name, lest there be too much ink on it and the writing not come out straight. Rather, he should intend to leave one letter unwritten before the Name, etc. If he did not do so, he should look for a letter that needs ink, end quote. The Shach there further wrote as follows: “he should look,” etc., implies that to write another ordinary letter first on some other matter is forbidden, since, etc. See there.
Then he should dip into the letters before the Name, etc. This implies that he should not dip into letters of the Name that he has already written. See Yoreh Deah, siman 274, seif 5 in the gloss, and the Shach there, from which it is somewhat implied that it is permitted if he wants to write a Divine Name with it.
