The measure of the roof of a lamed. This is the measure of a full “atba” of scribes, which they said in chapter HaKometz, namely about half a fingernail, as the Beit Yosef wrote, and not like the view of the Tur, who holds that there is a difference between the Rosh and the Semak in this. The Beit Yosef also brought in the name of Orchot Chaim in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah that there must be the measure of half a fingernail above the top of the lamed and likewise below the side of the tzadi. I do not know why the Beit Yosef did not take account of this at least initially, and he should have brought it in the Shulchan Aruch.
Like the fullness of a letter. In Yoreh Deah, siman 274, seif 4, I proved with the help of Heaven that this is initially, but after the fact it is not invalid unless it appears as one word according to a child who is not wise, etc. See there.
