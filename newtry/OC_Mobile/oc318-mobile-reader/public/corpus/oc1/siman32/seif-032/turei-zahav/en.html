[A space] like the roof of a lamed. This is the full measure of an atba of the Soferim in Hakometz—and that is about half a fingernail, as the Beit Yosef wrote—and not like the Tur, who held there is a difference between Ra'ash and Semak on this. The Beit Yosef also brought in the name of Orḥot Ḥayyim in the name of Ra'avyah that the measure of half a fingernail must be above the head of the lamed and likewise below from its sides; I do not know why the Beit Yosef did not consider this, at least for le-khatḥilah—and he should have brought it in the Shulchan Aruch.

Like a full letter. In Yoreh De'ah siman 274, section 4, I proved be-siyata di-Shemaya that this is le-khatḥilah; but bedi'eved it is not invalid unless it looks like one letter according to a child who is not astute—see there.
