Seif 32, one must, etc. 32a: the space of the full measure, etc. The Geonim explained this as half a fingernail, as written in Yoreh Deah at the beginning of siman 288. The Sefer HaTerumah wrote the reason: so that there is room to write the roof of a lamed, and a final kaf and final nun.
That they should be, etc. As written above in seif 16.
And at their beginning, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. and he makes, etc. The word initially is a scribal error, and it should read: at the beginning; meaning, as with scrolls. This is as Rabbeinu Yitzchak wrote in Tosafos to Bava Basra 13b, s.v. and he makes, and so all the poskim wrote there. The same applies to tefillin, as written below in seif 44: he should roll, etc. The Mordechai likewise wrote this in Hilchos Mezuzah. But in Hilchos Tefillin he wrote that Rabbeinu Tzemach says one need not leave, in a mezuzah or tefillin, space for surrounding, since they do not have a column at their end as scrolls do, and also because they are not read from like scrolls. Furthermore, regarding a mezuzah, the practice is to write Shaddai on the outside at its beginning, and if so there is necessarily no surrounding space there at all. So too wrote the Sefer HaTerumah. The words of the Rosh contradict one another: in Hilchos Tefillin he explained that one need not leave space, like the opinion of the Sefer HaTerumah, but in Hilchos Mezuzah he retracted and wrote that one must. So too is it in the Tur and Shulchan Aruch, for in Yoreh Deah, siman 288, he ruled that one must. The Beit Yosef himself already raised this difficulty. I do not know why he ruled plainly here, for certainly one cannot distinguish between tefillin and mezuzah.
And one must leave, etc. 30a.
Also one must, etc. His words are astonishing. In the Rivash, the questioner asked whether it should be invalidated if he left a small blank space, and he answered that although in the third chapter of Maseches Sofrim it invalidates, that is where he marked it with ink. But otherwise one should not invalidate it, since it does not have the measure of a parsha. Nor should it be invalidated because he made it like a song, for there that applies specifically in the form of brick over half-brick, and there too the measure of a parsha is required. The novelty taught is that although he made it like a song, even so it is invalid; see there. And as written there at the end of siman 274 in the gloss. He further proved this from what the Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch wrote in siman 273, seif 4: and if not, etc. But initially, certainly one should not leave such a space. See Magen Avraham.
