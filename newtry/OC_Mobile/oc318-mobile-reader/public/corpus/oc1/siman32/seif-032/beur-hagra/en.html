Se'if 32, "one must," etc. 32a: "full," etc.; and the Geonim explained like half a fingernail, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 288; and Sefer HaTerumah writes the reason — so that one may write the roof of lamed, kaf, and straight nun.

Se'if 32, "that they be," etc. As stated above in se'if 16.

Se'if 32, "and at their beginning," etc. Tosafos there, s.v. "and he makes," etc. — lekhatchilah it is a scribal error and should read "at the beginning"; and Ralbag: like Torah scrolls, as R' Yitzchak in Tosafos, Bava Batra 13b, s.v. "and he makes"; and all the poskim there agree. The same applies to tefillin, as stated above, Semag: "one rolls," etc.; and so Mordechai in Hilchot Mezuzah. But in Hilchot Tefillin, R' Tzvi says one need not leave space for mezuzah and tefillin as for surrounding, since they have no column at the end like scrolls; also because one does not read them like scrolls; and further, for mezuzah the custom is to write Shaddai outside at the beginning — so there is no surrounding at all. So too in Sefer HaTerumah. Rosh’s words contradict each other: in Hilchot Tefillin he writes one need not, per Sefer HaTerumah; in Hilchot Mezuzah he retracted and writes one must. So ruled Shulchan Aruch in Yoreh De'ah, siman 288. Beit Yosef himself already questioned this — I do not know why he was silent here; surely one should not distinguish between tefillin and mezuzah.

Se'if 32, "and one must leave," etc. 30a.

Se'if 32, "also one must," etc. His words are puzzling: Rivash was asked whether to invalidate if one left a small space; he answered that even though in chapter 3 of tractate Menachos we disqualify — that refers to a dot in ink; but in any case one should not disqualify, since there is no measure for a parashah; also not to disqualify because he made it valid — there specifically "brick upon brick," and there a parashah measure is required, and a novelty is taught: even if he made it valid, it is still invalid. See there, siman 274 in the gloss; he also proved from Rambam and Shulchan Aruch, siman 273, se'if 4: "and if not," etc. But lekhatchilah certainly one should not leave — Magen Avraham.
