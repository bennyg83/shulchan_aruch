Se'if 7, a halachah transmitted to Moshe from Sinai, etc., and if he changed it, it is invalid. This applies to all of them. Biurei HaGra, letter 24. So too in Shulchan Aruch of Rabbi Zalman, letter 9, and Meleches Shamayim, rule 2, letter 3, in Chochmah. What Ateres Zekeinim wrote, that some wrote that it is valid, is the opinion of Mordechai, and Beis Yosef and Bach already wrote that we do not maintain like him. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 14.
