One must leave space. Bach ruled that one must leave a full atba de-sifrei above the roof of the lamed.

A full line's width. In Shiltei Gibborim on Hilchot Sefer Torah and Maharil siman 149 he wrote one need leave space only in a Sefer Torah (thus); and scribes are accustomed not to be exacting about this.

Between verse and verse. It is unresolved, for this was taken from Rivash siman 286, and he did not write there except that if one did so he did not invalidate — but ab initio one should not leave any space at all between verse and verse (Sefer HaZichronot, thus). The main point, as above, is that we say in the first chapter of Kiddushin that we are not expert in [the boundaries of] verses; also "Arur" is among five things that have no decisive ruling, and for us it is the beginning of a verse — therefore it seems one should not leave any space at all; and in Yoreh De'ah, end of siman 274, in Beit Yosef.
