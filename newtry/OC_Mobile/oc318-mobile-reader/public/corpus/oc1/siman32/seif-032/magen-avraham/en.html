The measure of a line. In Shiltei Giborim, Hilchos Sefer Torah, and Maharil, siman 149, he wrote that one need leave this only in a Sefer Torah; Knesses HaGedolah. So the scribes have practiced, that they are not particular about this.
Between verse and verse. This requires study, for he derived this from the Rivash, siman 286, and he wrote there only that if one did so it does not invalidate, but initially one should not leave any blank space at all between verse and verse; Sefer HaZichronos, Knesses HaGedolah. This latter view appears to me primary, for we say in the first chapter of Kiddushin that we are not expert in verses. Also, the word arur is one of five things that have no determination, and for us it is the beginning of a verse. Therefore it seems to me that one should not leave blank space at all. See Yoreh Deah, at the end of siman 274, in Beis Yosef.
