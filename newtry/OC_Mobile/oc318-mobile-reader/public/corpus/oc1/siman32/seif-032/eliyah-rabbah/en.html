[32] And if he dragged, etc. In Menachem Azariah's responsum he ruled separation does not help where it adhered before the letter was finished. Olat Tamid wrote: specifically above; but if adhered below it is kosher — so far. And even above it seems to me one may rely on the authors of the Shulchan Aruch and Levush to validate. Olat Tamid wrote in the name of the Ramban's responsum siman 236: reish adhered to bet — one must drag the entire reish but need not drag the bet — so far. His words are puzzling, for this is lo kelachar yad. I looked into the Ramban's responsum itself and saw he refers to a Torah scroll where lo kelachar yad is kosher. And further it seems to me the Ramban according to his reasoning holds dragging does not fix adhesion; but for us, where dragging adhesion alone helps — however Radbaz wrote: if the letter's form changed through adhesion it is like a mem that was sealed shut.
