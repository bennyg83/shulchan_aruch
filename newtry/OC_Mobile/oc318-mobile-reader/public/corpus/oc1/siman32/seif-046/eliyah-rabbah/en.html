In the hollow of the heh, etc. Lechem Chamudos, folio 80, wrote: it seems to me that all the more so this applies in the hollow of a dalet, for it appears like a heh, and so is also explained in Hilchos Gittin, siman 125, se'if 18, end quote. Knesses HaGedolah wrote that the same applies to the leg of a final kaf and the like extending into the letter on the line below. A letter swallowed within another, such as where one letter enters into a gimel or a nun, is invalid according to everyone, end quote.
