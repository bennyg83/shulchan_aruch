(s.k. 46) Three letters, etc. It is possible that he came to teach, etc., meaning that he took a definite case: two letters are permitted in every circumstance, even if they are the majority of a word. Three letters, by contrast, are sometimes forbidden if they are the majority of a word. However, even with two letters, even from a large word, if those two letters appear as a word by themselves, such as heh mem from Avraham, one must also be careful. But Shulchan Aruch there disagrees with this and writes that only if the two letters are truly a complete word by themselves is it forbidden; but if they are from a larger word, even if they appear as writing by themselves, it is permitted.
