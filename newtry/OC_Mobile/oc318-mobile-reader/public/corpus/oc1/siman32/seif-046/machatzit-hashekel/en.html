(Note 46) Three letters, etc., and it is possible that he comes to teach, etc. — that is, he cites the word "stop" — that two letters in any matter are permitted, even if they are the majority of a word; not so three letters, which are sometimes forbidden if they are the majority of a word. However, even with two letters, even from a large word, if those two letters appear as a word on its own — such as heh-mem from Avraham — one must also be careful. But the Shulchan Aruch there disagrees and wrote that specifically if the two letters are truly a complete word on its own, then it is forbidden; but if it is a large word, even though it appears as a writing on its own, it is permitted.
