Toward the opening here. For otherwise the letters will stand reversed before the reader.
According to their law. For we find that the order in which they are placed in the housings is also arranged before the right of the reader, who stands opposite the wearer, so that he reads in order: first the passage of Kadesh on his right, then the passage of Ve-hayah Ki Yeviacha, and the remaining passages, as written in siman 34. Therefore the end of the roll, which is also the beginning of the passage, must be on the right side facing him.
