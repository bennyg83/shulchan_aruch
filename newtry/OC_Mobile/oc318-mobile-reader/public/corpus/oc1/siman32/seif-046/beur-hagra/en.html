Seif 46, and the beginnings of the parshiyos, etc. As written in the Yerushalmi, second chapter of Megillah: its Shema must face the doorway. According to Rashi's explanation, this means that the end of the rolling should face outward, so that the reader reads it in order, as the Rosh and other poskim wrote, and as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 289, seif 6. We also say regarding tefillin there on 34b: there is no difficulty, etc., and the reader reads them in order. This is what the Shulchan Aruch wrote: the margin should be, etc., in the manner of reading. All the more so according to Rabbeinu Tam's explanation of the above Yerushalmi, as written there in Yoreh Deah in the gloss: and he should intend, etc.
