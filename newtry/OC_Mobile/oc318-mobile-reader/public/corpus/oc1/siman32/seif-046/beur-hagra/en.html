46 — And the heads of the parashah, etc. As Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Megillah: its Shema must see the opening; for Rashi — meaning the end of the roll should face outward so the reader reads in order, as the Rosh and Shitah Mekubetzet; as Yoreh De'ah, siman 289, seif 6; we also say regarding tefillin there 34b — no difficulty, etc.; "and the reader reads in order" — this is what Shulchan Arukh wrote: the parchment, etc., in reading order; especially for Rabbenu Tam in that Yerushalmi, as Yoreh De'ah there in Hagahah; and one should intend, etc.
