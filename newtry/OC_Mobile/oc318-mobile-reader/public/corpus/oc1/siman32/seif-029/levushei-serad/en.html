(s.k. 23) And completing the letter by writing. Meaning: part of a letter was written, a drop fell and he erased the drop — that part is scratching out; completing the letter by writing is invalid, for we require the whole letter by writing and not by scratching — i.e. scratching out; here part of the letter — its first part — was by scratching. Later he brought a law that likewise in this manner — and how much more this case, where there it is worse: the first part of the letter was made only by ink that fell unintentionally and not at all deliberately.
