Seif katan 23. And he completed the letter by means of it. This means that part of a letter was written, a drop fell, and he erased the drop; that part is then considered produced by engraving the interior. If he then completed the letter by writing, it is invalid, because the entire letter must be made through writing and not through engraving, meaning not by engraving the interior. Here, part of the letter, namely its first portion, was made through engraving. Afterward he brings a ruling that proceeds in the form of 'not only this but even this.' There the case is worse, for the first part of the letter was not made even by engraving, but only by ink that fell without intention or act at all.
