(s.k. 29) Invalid. It appears to me that this is specifically where it touched, etc. In Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote: if something like this occurred in the Name, what should be done? He answered that it is comparable to what is written in Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 276 regarding the hei of the Name that touches by a hairbreadth, where it is forbidden to erase it, since people read it as a hei. The same applies here: since people read it as an alef, it is forbidden to erase it. He wrote that he found a remedy: one should add ink above, in the yud, until the lower part is considered the leg of the yud, for scribes say that sometimes the yud is made so thick, end quote.
