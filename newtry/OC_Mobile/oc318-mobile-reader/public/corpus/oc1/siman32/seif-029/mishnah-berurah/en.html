Fluent in his mouth. That is, when he begins to write, we assume that he is not yet sufficiently expert in reading them by heart, especially regarding defective and plene spellings.
From writing. Or from the mouth of one dictating, so that he not err. If part of the passage is fluent in his mouth, he may write that part by heart. See the Bach, who writes that nevertheless the choicest form of the mitzvah is in every case to write from written text.
