Except. Taz: he added the word "from Egypt" — invalid if he made VeHayah Im Shamoah open (in Beit Yosef, only if he changed [it] is it invalid), because for Shema closed all agree it is invalid even if not adjacent to VeHayah Im Shamoah, because "He will bring you" — for there is no parashah before it for the four [parashiyot] to make Shema open; only its beginning at the head of the line (according to Rambam, may he rest in peace, which is essential, as written in letter 26, there). If so, in tefillin too it is proper that it be open; and mezuzah too — its closed [parashah] is kosher — VeHayah Im Shamoah stands alone; see however chapter 5 of Laws of Tefillin, Alef-Bet, and Yoreh De'ah responsum 88, siman 13. And he wrote to reconcile from Menachot 32a — here you will see that the words of our master, may he rest in peace, are built on Adni Paz, as I will say. For there 72b he challenges: "They lower" — do they make? and mezuzah requires ruling lines, and he strains to change what is not the halachah; and a Torah scroll — we require ruling lines, as Rambam, may he rest in peace, wrote in Laws of Torah scroll, chapter 1, halachah 12 — even if halachah leMoshe miSinai; and let us say because of Torah scroll he holds "they do not lower" — equal reason for both (see Tosafot, s.v. ha); but the questioner who challenges from Torah scroll and tefillin [regarding] VeHayah Im Shamoah — here closed and here open — and Rashi, s.v. ha, explains: here in a Torah scroll and there in a mezuzah (to reject Taz's difficulty, which is difficult from Torah scroll); and how was it placed for him that Shema and VeHayah Im Shamoah are far — one in Va'etchanan and one in Eikev? See Tosafot Shabbat 89b, s.v. ha, and Menachot 32a, s.v. dilma; and one must say he thought VeHayah Im Shamoah was at the beginning of a column and he would write Shema — and necessarily he wrote the parashiyot out of order — kosher, only the words invalid (Taz, Yoreh De'ah responsum 88:3, and responsum 1). And he answered from two [sources] only to complete — meaning it does not refer except to Shema at the end of a column and VeHayah Im Shamoah completing it. Or as Tosafot wrote, like Rashi's explanation that he sews them together and it is kosher. And here for Rambam, may he rest in peace, both are absent — for in chapter 8, halachah 1, if he sews them or precedes parashah to parashah — invalid; and therefore the questioner thought that in a Torah scroll there is certainly in any case a reason they do not make — only he holds an equal reason for both; if so, according to his view it is necessary that the main difficulty is from tefillin — for in tefillin too we require VeHayah Im Shamoah closed specifically; and there 72b too he challenges ruling lines from tefillin — for in a Torah scroll in any case they do not make, for they are distant, and sewing is invalid; and to complete is strained in his view, since in truth even open [parashiyot] — see there; and therefore he challenges him and answers: it is a dispute of tannaim, and it is not as strained as completing. And see Yoreh De'ah responsum 88 in Shach, letter 1; understand.

Question. A scribe wrote VeHayah Ki Yad with "with a strong hand the Lord brought us out," and saw that if he writes "from Egypt" there will not be a space of nine letters; and Shema open — only at the head of the line — it will appear he hangs the word "from Egypt" above, and it will be a space of eleven letters; for that which one may not hang is because it is out of order — so concludes from tefillin, Taz; but in order it is permitted — see Perush (24 Megillah 6a) in Yerushalmi: he wrote Chullin in order; and the essence is like Rambam, may he rest in peace, that we require open at the head of the line, as written in letter 26; uncertain.
