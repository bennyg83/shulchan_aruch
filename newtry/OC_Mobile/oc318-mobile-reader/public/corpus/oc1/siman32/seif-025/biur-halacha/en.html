One letter split, etc. — Some disqualify imperceptible split until close inspection; source Peri Megadim — no fix if separation not evident. I hold: for law, do not be strict; will clarify. (1) Ria — source; Beit Yosef: split into two letters like tzadi written yod-nun; Rosh: hei/kuf stuck — no fix; Magen Avraham: even child reads — invalid if looks like two letters. Ria/Magen Avraham even if separation not evident — Ria: whole letter forbidden, partial outside column permitted; never whole letter outside. Kuf proof: invalid because not another letter by adhesion; tzadi yod-nun — child may read tzadi if separation not evident until scrutiny — permitted to fix. Ria: "even child" when visibly two letters. Shulchan Arukh: tzadi yod-nun — no break at junction mentioned. Olat Tamid: visibly two letters. Hagahah: Ria — before fix had that letter's name; tzadi yod-nun child won't read tzadi — form lost. Darkhei Moshe/Ria: yod/shin not touching — child reading helps; tzadi not. Taz seif 21: chet without crowns — repair helps; here without crown, presumably same for all listed cases. Taz difficult — author implies visible separation in opening, not in chet exception. Tehillah leDavid: Rosh on hei/kuf touching — not like Ria on yodin; Beit Yosef: Rosh also on yodin touching letter body — invalid, no erasure fix; Tehillah leDavid: possibly Rosh on "must touch" — not our invalidation. Rosh includes yod behind tzadi point on alef, yod in shin/ayin — if not touched, form changes; Tehillah leDavid doubts invalidating gittin; for tefillin/mezuzot only invalid; gittin require Sinai-connected letters except hei/kuf — thus fix yod/shin/ayin not touching; yod behind tzadi same. Imperceptible split — permitted to fix [show child lekhatchilah]. Acharonim lean lenient if child reads letter — against Peri Megadim. Very thin split visible only against sun — kosher even per Peri Megadim; no fix needed — not worse than ink-passing "hole"; Shabbat 108a bird skin — only ink-passing hole, not concern random hole in tzadi/shin. Want strict — require fix; do not disqualify tefillin.

And they recognize that, etc. — Mishnah Berurah: Shulchan Arukh even visible separation; Gra disagrees with Peri Megadim; Beit Yosef end siman 36 on chet — no proof; visible like two zayins — form lost, split letter; our case separation visible but form remains — child fixes. Opening and closing same — visible separation, child helps. Gra: large gap — form lost, not like tzadi yod-nun — no child help. Responsum Pri Megadim 2:16 — distant gap not reaching letter body — Beit Yosef distant four; Bach amah — no child; if far yet reads alef/shin when close — may rely.
