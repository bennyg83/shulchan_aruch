Yod, nun. The same if he did not make the right leg of yod; but if he did not make its left leg he may fix it, for the name is yod aleph — Beit Yosef, Magen Avraham.

Chet. Tefillin whose chet is like dalet and zayin thus, or the reverse — do not invalidate; drag from the dalet until made zayin — not out of order; see Magen Avraham siman 36, seif kaf-gimel 3. So I wrote in responsum on tefillin found with the word bneikhem in two kafs as bneikhkhem — drag the second kaf entirely and extend the first kaf until near mem, but do not drag mem and make the second kaf into mem, for that is out of order — Kenesset HaGedolah in gloss on Tosafot; see responsum Darchei Noam siman 1 and Avodat HaGershuni siman 82. If he made chet like Rashi's view thus, it is valid; even if he lengthened it at end of line until not square, still valid — Magen Avraham in siman 36, seif kaf-gimel 3.
