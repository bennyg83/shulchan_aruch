Of a ches. Tefillin whose ches is made like a dalet and zayin, or the reverse, should not be invalidated; he should scrape from the dalet until he makes it into a zayin, and this is not considered out of order. See Magen Avraham, siman 36, s.k. 3. So too I wrote in a responsum regarding tefillin in which the word “your children” was found with two kafs; he should scrape away the second kaf entirely and lengthen the first kaf until it reaches close to the mem, but he should not scrape the mem and make the second kaf into a mem, for that would be out of order: Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah in his glosses to the Tur. See Responsa Darchei Noam, siman 1, and Avodas HaGershuni, siman 82. If one made a ches according to Rashi’s view, it is valid, and even if he lengthened it at the end of a line until it is not square, it is nevertheless valid. Magen Avraham, siman 36, s.k. 3.
