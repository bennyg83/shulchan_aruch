(In the author) se'if 25. See note: included in this are three laws here — letters in the pardes that appear as two letters, such as shin appearing as ayin with yod and the like — invalid, and correction does not help because it is out of order; not so yod on aleph, nun-vav — invalid, nevertheless one may correct and it is not called out of order. But in other letters, if a child can read it, correction is not needed and it is kosher, as above, se'if 16, or as below, se'if 27, and no other case — as written, and Taz extended, se'if 9, and Magen Avraham, se'if 2.
