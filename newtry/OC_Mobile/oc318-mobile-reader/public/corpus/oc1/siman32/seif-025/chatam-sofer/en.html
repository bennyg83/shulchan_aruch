In the Mechaber, seif 25. Note: He includes three laws here. First, when separated components appear as two letters (for example, a shin appears as an ayin together with a yod, or the like) the letter is invalid and correction does not help because it would be written out of order. Second, although a yod above an alef is invalid when detached, it can nevertheless be corrected and this is not considered writing out of order. Third, regarding other letters, if a child can read the letter, it requires no correction and is valid, as above in seif 16 or below in seif 27. There are no other categories, as Taz, se'if katan 9, and Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2, write at length.
