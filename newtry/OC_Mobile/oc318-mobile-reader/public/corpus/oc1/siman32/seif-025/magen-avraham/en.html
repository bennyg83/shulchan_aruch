The leg of the alef — then one must drag the whole thing, as stated in seif 18.

Yud, nun, etc. In all these, even if a child can read it, it is invalid since it looks like two letters; and the same if he did not make the right leg of the yud. But if he did not make its left leg, he may fix it, for the name "yud" is upon it (Beit Yosef siman 36).

It is permitted to glue them together, for a child is not accustomed to a chet like this; see siman 18.
