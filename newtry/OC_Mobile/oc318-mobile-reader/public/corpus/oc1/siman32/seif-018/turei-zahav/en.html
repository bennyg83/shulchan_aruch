In the form of a bent nun. Explanation: for the open mem is written in two strokes—first they write like a bent nun, then hang from its side like a vav. If so, whatever became invalid in it—namely the vav—must be scraped entirely; but the nun that was written in a kosher manner need not be scraped. By contrast, in resh that he made like a dalet, where everything was written invalid at once, he must scrape it all. Accordingly, the same applies to any letter written in two strokes, such as gimmel and the like, where the invalidity occurred in the first stroke: he need scrape only that stroke.

Whether after it was completed, etc. Nevertheless, if it is found thus in a sefer Torah, one need not take out another, for the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba: even if he does not scrape it, as long as he could scrape it, it is kosher—as Turei Zahav said: anything fit to be kneaded, etc. And the Maharach wrote: even if it is found thus on Shabbat when one cannot scrape it, nevertheless one need not take out another, since from the basic law of a sefer Torah there is a correction—only a Shabbat prohibition is layered on it.
