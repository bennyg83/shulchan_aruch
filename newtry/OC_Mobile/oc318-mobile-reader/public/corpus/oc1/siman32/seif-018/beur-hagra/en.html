Se'if 18, "an open mem," etc. It is stated there in a baraisa, not like Rav Chisda; and all the poskim brought the baraisa alone. In the beginning of chapter 2 of Menachos: all doubled letters in the aleph-beis — one writes the first at the beginning of the word and in the middle of the word, and the last at the end; if one changed, it is invalid.

Se'if 18, "if it became attached," etc. Menachos 29a, 34a — and not like Yerushalmi, as stated above in se'if 16.

Se'if 18, "and if he dragged," etc. From what is stated in Shabbos 104b — such as if he lifted it to the roof, etc.; Mordechai, siman 553; and it requires study, for we also say there that he lifted it to its tag, etc. In the baraisa there, 103b; and the same for kuf, that they hung them — one reason for both, as stated in 128a and Menachos 29b, Rivash. It is also not necessary, for there it is because it resembles another, as stated above, siman 36, se'if 1, and in Yoreh De'ah, siman 274, se'if 5; see the Tur and Rivash. Even though in Menachos we say "we look at the scribes," etc. — implying the optimal mitzvah, as Mordechai writes, siman 554 — one may reject that, for there they separated greatly; and Rashi in Gittin, chapter 5, 52b, s.v. "and not to write touching," etc. But the plain sense of our sugya accords with Mordechai. Even though in Shabbos we say there for delay — the het of wheat, etc. — since he lifted it to the roof, the het would be evident in any case. This is what he meant by "he lifted," etc., and "he hung," etc.

Se'if 18, "if they touched," etc. — "if one touched," etc. — and likewise, etc. Even though it does not resemble another for this reason, one may say there was a change in its form, as stated above, siman 36, se'if 1 — like the tip of yud, according to Tosafos, 29a, s.v. "its tip."

Se'if 18, "and there is no," etc. Since the letter was touched.
