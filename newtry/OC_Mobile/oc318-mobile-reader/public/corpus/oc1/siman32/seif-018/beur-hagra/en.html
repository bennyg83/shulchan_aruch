Seif 18, an open mem, etc. There in the beraisa, unlike Rav Chisda; and so too all the poskim cited only the beraisa. At the end of the second chapter of Maseches Sofrim: all the doubled letters in the alphabet, one writes the first forms at the beginning of the word and in the middle of the word, and the latter forms at the end; and if he changed this, it is invalid.
If it became attached, etc. Menachos 29b and 34a, and not like the Yerushalmi, as written above in seif 16.
And if he scraped away, etc. From what is written in Shabbos 104b, such as where he removed its roof, etc. Mordechai, siman 952. This requires study, for we also say there that he removed its crown, etc., in the beraisa there, 103b. The same applies to the letter kuf, since the reason for their suspension is one and the same, as stated in Shabbos 120a and Menachos 29b. Rivash. This too is not necessarily conclusive, for there it is because it resembles another letter, as written above, siman 36, seif 1, and in Yoreh Deah, siman 274, seif 5; see there in the Tur. The Rivash wrote that although we say in Menachos there, we saw scribes, etc., which implies that it is the most preferred form of the mitzvah, as written in Mordechai, siman 953, this can be rejected, for there they separated them greatly. See Rashi in Gittin 85b, s.v. and one should not write, etc. Nevertheless, the plain sense of the sugya implies like the words of the Mordechai. And although we say in Shabbos there that the hehs and chets are indispensable, etc., since he had scored the roof of the chet, it was recognizable even without this; and this is what is meant by, they scored, etc., and suspended, etc.
If they touched, etc.; if it touched, etc.; and so too, etc. Although because of this it does not resemble another letter, one may say that it is a change in its form, as written above, siman 36, seif 1, and like the point of a yud according to Tosafos, 29a, s.v. kotzo.
