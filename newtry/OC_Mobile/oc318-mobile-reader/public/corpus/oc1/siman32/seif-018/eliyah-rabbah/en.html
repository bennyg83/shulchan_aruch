We are not concerned, etc. Nevertheless, those who do not want to trouble themselves do not mark the hides according to the full law; rather, they fill one or two hollows of a known count and say that this is sufficient. They are not acting properly, and it is forbidden to rely on this. See Sefer HaZichronos, folio 29. In Sefer Baruch SheAmar he wrote that one should write at the top on the inside and not mark with an awl, as cited by Magen Avraham.
