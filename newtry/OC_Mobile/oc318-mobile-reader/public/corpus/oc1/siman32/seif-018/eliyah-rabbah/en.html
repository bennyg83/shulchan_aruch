[18] One need not be concerned, etc. Nevertheless those who do not wish to trouble themselves do not mark the hides as the full law requires, but fill one or two furrows from a known number and say this suffices — they do not act well and it is forbidden to rely on this — Sefer HaZichronot page 29. And in Sefer Baruch he said he wrote that one should write on the head side inward and not mark with an awl (Magen Avraham).
