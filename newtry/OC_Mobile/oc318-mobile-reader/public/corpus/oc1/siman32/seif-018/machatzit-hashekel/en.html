(Seif katan 18) The leg of the first, etc. According to this, one must delete the word 'or.' The correct reading is: such as a vav or a zayin whose first leg was severed, meaning the leg of the vav or the leg of the zayin.
And it is a scribal error, as I wrote above in seif katan 20. So too the Taz wrote in the name of Maharik. See Responsa Pnei Moshe, part 2, siman 16, who is very lenient.
