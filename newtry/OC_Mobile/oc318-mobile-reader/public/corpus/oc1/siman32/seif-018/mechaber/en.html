An open Mem whose opening became attached and was closed, it is not effective to scrape away the attachment and open it, because that is like engraving from within. What is its remedy? He must scrape away the entire nose, so that it remains in the form of a bent Nun, and afterward write what he scraped away. A Reish that he made like a Dalet, one should be stringent and say that it is not enough to scrape away only the leg or only the roof and then rewrite it like a Reish, because both the roof and the leg were made invalidly; therefore he must scrape away both. If a letter became attached to another letter, whether before it was completed or after it was completed, it is invalid. If he scraped and separated it, it is valid, and this is not called engraving from within, since the letter itself had been written properly. If the legs of the Hei and Kuf touched the roof, he scrapes away the leg and writes it again, and he need not scrape away the entire letter, because the roof was written properly. If the leg of the Alef touched the roof of the Alef, or the inner face of the Alef touched the roof beneath it, it is invalid, and there is no remedy by scraping to separate it, for that is like engraving from within. Rather, he must scrape away everything that was made invalidly and then write it again. Rama: The same law applies to the Yuds of Shin, Tzadi, Ayin, and Pei, if they touched the body of the letter more than the place of their attachment. Beis Yosef.
