An open mem whose opening connected and became closed — it is not effective to scrape the adhesion to open it, because that is like hollowing of interiors. And what is its remedy? He should scrape the entire nose [of the mem] and leave what looks like a bent nun, and afterward write what he scraped away. And a reish that he made like a dalet — one should be strict and say that it does not suffice when he scrapes the thigh alone or the roof alone and rewrites it like a reish, because either the roof or the thigh was made in invalid fashion; therefore he must scrape both. If a letter stuck to a letter, whether before it was completed or after it was completed — invalid. And if he scraped and separated — kosher, and it is not called hollowing of interiors since the letter itself was written properly. If the feet of a heh or a kuf touched the roof, he should scrape the foot and rewrite it, and need not scrape the entire letter because the roof was written properly. If the foot of an alef touched the roof of the alef, or the face of the alef on the inside touched the roof beneath it — invalid, and there is no remedy by scraping to separate, because that is hollowing of interiors; rather he should scrape everything made in invalid fashion and rewrite it.And so too is the law with the yods of shin, and tzadi, and ayin, and peh if they touched the body of the letter more than the place of their attachment [Beit Yosef].]:
