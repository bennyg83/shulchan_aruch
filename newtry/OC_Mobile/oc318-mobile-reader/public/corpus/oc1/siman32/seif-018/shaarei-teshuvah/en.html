Be'er Heitev. And in Be'er Heitev he wrote in name of Yad Aharon that there is a scribal error in Shulchan Arukh — and it should read: he thinned from parashat VeHayah Ki Yevi'acha which is larger. And for parashah Kadesh and VeHayah Im Shamoah which are long — they make very thin parchment, see there. And in Hilchot Ketanot siman 259 he wrote one need not thin, see there — and his words are strained. And also because what he wrote "will push outward" — plainly this spoils beauty most; and here they want to equalize compartments for beauty of tefillin. And what matter to call it long because cramped in the middle — therefore clearly one should thin as Yad Aharon wrote, as above.
