See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Yad Aharon that there is a scribal error in Shulchan Aruch and the text should read that the thinner parchment is for the parsha of Vehayah Ki Yeviacha, which is larger, while for Kadesh and Vehayah Im Shamoa, which are long, they make very thin parchment. Halachos Ketanos wrote that one need not emend the text, but his words are forced. It is clear to emend as Yad Aharon wrote.
