(Taz s.k. 8) Therefore if its entire inside was pierced. There is a textual error here: at the end of the s.k. it implies specifically the entire inside, not part of it — these words belong here, and the continuation of his words: even if we are strict about surrounding gvil inside per Yerushalmi, nevertheless we have one leniency from what Tur wrote on Yerushalmi's words — therefore "its entire inside was pierced" is invalid; it implies if half the inside was pierced, then half the letter lacks surrounding gvil and it is valid. He wrote the reason seems: parchment by half a letter is as if the other half is also surrounded, since the whole is one part.
