Taz, seif katan 8. Therefore, if its entire hollow was pierced. There is a scribal error here. What he writes at the end of the seif katan, implying specifically its entire hollow and not part of it, belongs here. The flow of his words is that even if we are stringent regarding surrounding blank parchment inside the letter, like the Yerushalmi, nevertheless we have one lenient distinction in it. Since the Tur wrote regarding the words of the Yerushalmi, 'therefore, if its entire hollow was pierced it is invalid,' this implies that if half its hollow was pierced, so that half the letter lacks surrounding blank parchment, it remains valid. On this he writes, 'and the reason appears,' etc. Meaning, the blank parchment adjacent to half the letter is considered as though it also surrounds the other half, since the whole is one part.
