Between. In the matter of seif 16: ostensibly if we find a final nun stuck at the end — for Rashba in his explanation of Yerushalmi below, kosher without dragging as Riaz; even for us who require dragging, Rashba is fit to rely upon not to rule otherwise. Not so adhesion above — for Rashba invalid and dragging does not help, since he rules like Yerushalmi. If so, upon whom shall we rely? And see siman 143 — it will be explained. And know that the author regarding adhesion letter to letter wrote whether before it was finished or after; but regarding a drop of ink that fell in seif 16 he did not write "before finished" — for sometimes kosher; see Mahari AS in Magen Avraham letter 23.

And know. If the letters of the Divine Name are finished and stuck below, he may drag — for do we not say that since we rely on Rashba to be lenient, all the more to be strict; and perhaps no dragging is needed and he would erase in vain (for Eliyahu Rabbah in Yoreh De'ah there permitted only because it is invalid) — this is an error: if forbidden to drag, it is not fit to separate and it is invalid — properly one separates. And Eliyahu Rabbah [holds] Shabbat prohibition: because of time it is fit to separate.
