Open mem stuck — See Mishnah Berurah: law stuck like hair-thread depends on hei; we lean: even lenient on hei per Beit Yosef below, siman 36, agree here — at least closed mem; what difference thin or thick closure? Their proof: Gemara sees dukna scribes hang hei leg — only for mitzvah, not essential hei form need not hang leg; still unlike chet as Gra below, or Rashba on Menachot — see there; for mem no proof to read open mem because closure thin.

And it closed — See Taz, Even HaEzer, siman 125, seif 4: specifically mem closed after finishing; if closed mem before finish — may drag closure since still must add on slanted strap like yod — drag stuck part first then finish; see there. Per Radbaz we wrote in Mishnah Berurah, seif 38 — strict here too.

The whole nose — Plain: specifically stuck while writing nose — nose made invalidly; if stuck after writing — need not drag only nose — may drag from nun until letter form nullified. Uncertain if valid writing also helps dragging half nose — depends if whole nose always required for mem or only lekhatchilah — needs study.

Reish made like dalet, etc. — Wide roof on plain kuf until looks like reish — Magen Avraham: extend leg double like roof [if doubling for delay or lekhatchilah — siman 36, letter forms]. No room to extend — Magen Avraham in Ralbag's name; Peri Megadim, siman 36, unstated: fix is erase whole roof — dragging excess certainly invalid, actual chet; even in "Elohekha" may erase but beware leg. Unlike reish-as-dalet requiring full drag since written in first chapter; same here. Yad Ephraim divides somewhat; strained. Ra'avad: plainly like reish-as-dalet [Shulchan Aruch HaRav, seif 22, copied responsum Ginat Veradim — uncertain to rely after acharonim require erasing roof]. If occurred in hei of Elohekha made wide like dalet or reish — lean lenient erasing roof if no room to extend leg; Peri Chadash lenient on reish-as-dalet; Machatzit HaShekel: suffices to nullify form; Gra left Shulchan Arukh difficult from Peri Chadash's question (wonder they did not mention).

One should be strict and say, etc. — See Ra'avad's chiddushim; so Machatzit HaShekel in Peri Chadash's name.

Because between the roof and the leg, etc. — For the first time everything was written in the usual scribal manner; if he first wrote reish and later mistakenly made a tag like dalet, it suffices when he drags the roof or leg with the tag and writes it again as reish. Levush; Peri Megadim.

And if he dragged and separated, etc. — Even if adhesion occurred before the letter was finished; see Gra and Sha'arei Ephraim to Maharaz Margaliyot — this Shulchan Arukh law is doubtful; from Shulchan Arukh HaRav it may be lenient except for tefillin and mezuzot, where erasure would violate "in order"; in Torah scroll and tefillin, if later words not yet written, dragging adhesion alone does not help if stuck before finishing the invalid letter.

Kosher and not called, etc. — See Mishnah Berurah: explained from what we rule that dragging helps even if the letter stuck at the start.

Legs of hei and kuf, etc. — See Mishnah Berurah; Chayei Adam, Even HaEzer 125:16; Peri Chadash strict even at the knot; Magen Avraham, Radbaz; Yoreh De'ah 276 in Beit Yosef. If in a Torah scroll hei/kuf legs touch above and a child reads correctly — do not replace; safek; Rambam permits reading invalid scroll — double safek for tefillin. Gra agrees with Mordechai: hei hanging [and kuf] is mitzvah custom where scribes write chet with crowns — distinction clear; lenient only if touch is hair-thin and from afar reads as hei; if chet has crowns, recognize again — bedi'eved do not disqualify if leg sticks thickly forming real chet. Maharam Benet and Tashbetz agree with Mordechai; Beit Yosef, Even HaEzer 126; Ra'avad siman 36. Shulchan Arukh strict on separation — custom without crowns in gittin; kuf leg touch does not change form. Pressing case: thin touch after valid writing — combine Semak (Tur, fallen ink drop) leniency on dragging.

He shall drag the leg — See Mishnah Berurah: touching during writing; after writing, hei requires erasing whole leg if first hei remains (Rosh, Taz); kuf — nullify form suffices. Peri Megadim siman 36: drag roof so leg outside roof, then extend kuf roof — not "out of order" if touch after writing (invalid leg must be dragged).

Alef leg touched, etc. — No difference during or after writing, like ink drop (seif 17); during writing — erase whole leg; after proper completion — nullify form suffices (Levush, Magen Avraham end seif 25).

Or alef face, etc. — Magen Avraham: only if yod body stuck to alef; left tip touch — kosher, still yod without tip; no impediment to fixing (Peri Megadim); dragging suffices, need not erase whole yod.

He shall drag all done invalidly, etc. — Magen Avraham: all written in that letter after touch. Peri Megadim: upper yod on alef after completion — drag whole alef, not yod alone; Magen Avraham strained "all written" — Beit Yosef: alef leg on roof or faces touching — Agur: all invalid, no fix by erasure/scraping except scraping invalid part like hei/kuf above; both cases alike — yod/leg dragging suffices. Agur on upper yod: scrape stuck yod and fix lekhatchilah — Darkhei Moshe; Peri Megadim requires dragging whole letter for upper yod on alef. Beit Yosef siman 36 on shin/yod/ayin — same. Do not limit to after completion — partial drag nullifies form. Levush: upper and lower yod equal (Peri Megadim) [unless wrote letter body first then upper yod stuck to curve]. Without Peri Megadim: Magen Avraham on lower leg — drag whole leg written in one act after touch; upper yod — drag whole yod. Taz siman 36 letter forms: upper vs lower yod — each letter requires full writing, not automatic formation — does not apply here (two writings). Lechem Shlomo: no upper/lower split. Some acharonim like Peri Megadim — lekhatchilah drag all invalid after touch including lower leg; bedi'eved if lower not dragged — lenient; curved line (alef roof) — scrape if invalid adhesion; if wrote curve and lower leg first then completed upper yod — only drag yod (Mahari Halevi, Taz's brother).
