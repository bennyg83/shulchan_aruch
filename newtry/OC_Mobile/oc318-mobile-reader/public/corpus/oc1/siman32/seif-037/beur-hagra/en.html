Seif 37, and he may, etc. For it too is called leather, as stated there, 34b: the Sages taught: I might think, etc.; how so, etc.; the Sages taught: how, etc. Thus it calls the parshiyos leathers. So too regarding the straps, as stated there, 35a: tefillin may not be tied, etc. At first glance it implies that this is specifically from parchment, like the parshiyos, since they were stated together, as above. But from what is written in Eruvin 96b, and when he troubled himself, etc., it is clear that the leather of the batim is ordinarily like an amulet. In Shabbos 78b: leather, enough to make an amulet; parchment, enough to, etc. So too there, 79a: Rava asked Rav Nachman, etc.; come and hear, etc.: there are three kinds of hides, etc. And the same applies to the straps. See Rashi there, s.v. rather, of their own kind, etc.
Or, etc. Meaning, although regarding tumah it is considered like flesh, as written in Chullin 122a, and so ruled the Rambam in the first chapter of Hilchos Avos HaTumah and in the fourth chapter of Hilchos Maachalos Asuros, and he omitted only the skin of the head of a tender calf and the skin of the hoof-house, for what is said there that it is an individual opinion was said only regarding these two: the skin of the hooves, there 55b, and the skin of the head, there 122b. With this, the difficulty of Tosafos in Zevachim 28a, s.v. tenina, etc., is resolved. Nevertheless, regarding tefillin it is considered leather, for we say there, 76b, he said to him: a young dove, etc.; and so too every bird, as the poskim and the Shulchan Aruch wrote there. The same applies regarding tumah, as stated there: those soft sinews, etc.; here is different, for it hardens again, etc. Even so, one may write tefillin upon it, as written above in seif 12, and even from that of a young bird, as written in Shabbos 108a: with its wings, etc.; see there.
