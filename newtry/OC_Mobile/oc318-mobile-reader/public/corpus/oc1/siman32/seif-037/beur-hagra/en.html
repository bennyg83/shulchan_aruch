137 — And he may, etc. For it too is called hide, as written 34b — "the Sages taught: I might think," etc.; "how?," etc.; "the Sages taught: how," etc. — clearly he calls the parashiyot hides, and so the straps, as written 35a: "tefillin — one does not tie," etc. It would seem specifically from parchment like the parashiyot, since in one act they are scraped, as above; but from what is written in Eruvin 96b, "did he toil," etc., clearly the housings' hide is regularly like an amulet; and Shabbat 78b — hide to make an amulet, parchment to, etc.; and there 79a — Rava asked Rabbah, etc.; "come and hear: three hides," etc.; and the straps; and Rashi there, s.v. ella bemeynan, etc.

Or, etc. Meaning: even though for impurity it counts as flesh, as in Chullin 122a and Rambam, chapter 1 of Laws of Fathers of Impurity and chapter 4 of Laws of Impurity — he omitted only the head-skin of a tender calf and the split-hoof skin, which is unique; they spoke only of those two — hoof-skin, Menachot 35b; head-skin, Chullin 122b. With this the Tosafot's difficulty in Zevachim 28a, s.v. tenina, is resolved. Nevertheless for tefillin it is like hide, for we say there 76b, "Bar Guzla," etc.; and so every bird, as the poskim and Shulchan Arukh there; the same for impurity, as written "those soft sinews," etc. — here is different, for he retracted, etc.; nevertheless they write tefillin on it, as above, seif 12; and even a dove, as Shabbat 108a, "in its wings," etc., there.

And so, etc. As above.

He requires, etc. Dispute of the Rabbis and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel — Gittin 45, Sanhedrin 48, Menachot 42; Tosafot there explain Rava like RSBag, and so is the halachah; they brought proof from Gittin 54. But Rashi and the Rambam explain the Rabbis like the first tanna and distinguish between housing hide and parashah parchment, as Tosafot cite in the name of Yere'im, though they rejected it.
