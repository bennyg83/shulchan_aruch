And one may make from parchment. In Beit Shmuel he wrote that one should not make the batim from parchment unless one places the parchment in water from tree bark called in foreign tongue "loua" with which hides are processed; in the hagahah there he validates parchment, and it is good to be strict l'chatchila. Mateh Ephraim siman 37 wrote: hide of a nursing animal is most preferred, then bird hide, then wild animal, then domestic animal, then nevelah hide, end quote.

Where it is possible. If he has no other parchment he puts them on without a berachah [Bach]. It seems to me it is fitting to rely on the Rambam to recite a berachah, for Rashi also holds with him — he holds like Rava who said designation is not a matter — he holds like the first Tanna who does not require processing for its sake. Tosafot challenged: we say in Gittin that a Torah scroll requires processing for its sake, and they wrote one strains to distinguish between the hide of the batim and the parchment written upon; but Rashi does not hold one must strain, and so the Rambam — therefore he wrote that processing for its sake is not required. So too Netziv; see siman 42.
