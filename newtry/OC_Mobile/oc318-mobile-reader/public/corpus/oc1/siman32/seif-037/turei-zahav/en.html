The skin of the batim, etc. For it is a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai that only the skin of a kosher animal is valid for tefillin. This includes even the straps. Regarding the skin of the batim, it is obvious from “in your mouth,” meaning from what is permitted in your mouth; and since the letter shin is formed in the skin of the batim, it is obvious that it must be from what is permitted in your mouth.
Even from a neveilah. For this is called a species permitted in your mouth.
From parchment. For it too is called skin.
Or from the skin of a fetus. We do not say that since it is considered like flesh regarding impurity, it is not considered skin. The Beit Yosef brings in the name of some authorities that the skin of a fetus is preferable, because no transgression was done with it, as there was no use of male or female.
Where possible. For the Rambam holds that the skin of the batim does not require processing at all; on the contrary, it is stronger when it was not processed. Therefore there is no invalidation in processing it not for its required purpose. Other decisors, however, disagree with the Rambam, and the Shulchan Aruch rules like the Rambam only in pressing circumstances.
