The hide of the housings, etc. For it is halakha le-Moshe mi-Sinai that only the hide of a pure domestic animal is kosher for tefillin. Included in this are even the straps—for the housings' hide is straightforward from "in your mouth from that permitted in your mouth"; and since the letter shin wrinkles in the housings' hide, it is obvious that it must be from that permitted in your mouth.

Even from nevelah—for this is called from the species permitted in your mouth.

Skin that is peeled off — for it too is called hide.

Or from stillborn hide. We do not say that since it is considered like flesh for impurity it is not considered hide. Beit Yosef brings in the name of those who say that stillborn hide is preferable — hide of a stillborn in which no sin was performed, that it had no use as male or female.

Where possible — for Rambam holds that hide of the batim needs no processing at all; on the contrary it is stronger when not processed. Nevertheless processing not for its sake does not invalidate it; however many dispute Rambam, and Shulchan Aruch ruled like Rambam in a time of need.
