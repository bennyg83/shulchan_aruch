[37] With missing and extra letters, etc. Olat Tamid wrote: per the Rashba above that everything fit for soaking, etc. — the same law applies here with extras, since it is fit to correct — one may not recite a blessing in vain; however as soon as he knows there are extras he must correct — so far; and this requires investigation. The Levush extended here as they let youths write tefillin to accustom them to writing and exaggerated the punishment. He also ridiculed those who tell the scribe "write tefillin for my sake." Sefer Baruch wrote he said: write good and whole letters, not broken ones, with moderation and great intent; one should not hurry in writing them to profit much money, for that profit will go to loss and to Gehinnom and he will lose his soul, for he causes many to sin. And whoever writes good kosher tefillin to his ability — his reward is doubled and redoubled and he is saved from the judgment of Gehinnom — so far. Sefer Chassidim siman 85: "and his righteousness stands forever" — this refers to one who benefits many, such as one who teaches those who fear the Omnipresent the repair of tefillin to repair for others.
