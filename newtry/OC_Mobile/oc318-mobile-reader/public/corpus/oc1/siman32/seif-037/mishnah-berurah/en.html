(165) Hide of the batim — and the same applies to the hide of the titura and the ma'avarta, since they are attached and sewn to the batim:

(166) Pure — as it is written 'that the Torah of the Omnipresent may be in your mouth' (Ex. 13:9) — from what is permitted in your mouth; and since the shin letter that hints at the Name Shaddai is compressed in the hide of the tefillin, it is included in the Torah of the Omnipresent. There is no distinction between shel rosh or shel yad:

(167) From peeling — even though it is thin, it is properly called hide:

(168) Stillborn — for its hide too is called hide; and even though it is called flesh regarding impurity, nevertheless it is no worse than bird hide:

(169) To make them from peeled — only that one blacken them, for 'black straps' is halacha leMoshe miSinai:

(170) Processed for its sake — and if the processing was only for the straps it does not help for the batim, for their sanctity is more stringent:

(171) Where possible — for the Rambam holds that hide of the batim does not require processing at all; on the contrary, it is stronger when not processed, and therefore it is valid even hide that is not processed at all — and all the more that it does not require processing for its sake. Most poskim disagree with him and require processing and also for its sake; the Shulchan Aruch ruled that where possible it requires for its sake, but where it is not possible to find covering for the batim from hide processed for its sake, one may rely on the words of the Rambam so as not to be prevented from the mitzvah of tefillin; and when afterward he finds hide processed for its sake, he should store away these batim and make from hide processed for its sake.
