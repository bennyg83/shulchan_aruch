And he is the Taz. See Menachot 34b: their dispute — one who places four columns in one bayit: for R. Yehuda b'dieved one must glue; for R. Yosei one need not even l'chatchilah. But four batim of shel rosh — he has no shin; b'dieved only did R. Yosei say it is valid. See Levush: four columns in one bayit is valid and it is a mitzvah min hamuvchar to glue — he has no shin and has two straps hanging, etc. He holds R. Yosei said one need not b'dieved — implying that l'chatchilah even in one bayit one must glue; and this is Bach's and Taz's method in note 36 on Rav Dimi who said one need not reach the place of the seam — that is b'dieved; and the halachah follows Rav Dimi, not like Abbaye see there. But for Beit Yosef in his long book who explained the halachah follows Abbaye: for Rav Dimi one need not l'chatchilah, the same law applies — R. Yosei need not l'chatchilah; but four batim and hanging only b'dieved. Now there is no difficulty to teach us a novelty that this is l'chatchilah and this b'dieved; and Rabbenu Yerucham who emended and the custom — we mean in one bayit too it is valid b'dieved; if so l'chatchilah R. Yosei agrees one must glue — understand.
