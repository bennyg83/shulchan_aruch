The leather of the casings needs to be from the leather of a kosher domesticated animal, a wild animal or a bird, even from their carcasses or those unfit for eating. And it's permitted to make them from the skin or the leather of a fetus. . And it needs to be processed for the sake [of tefillin ] whenever possible. {Rama: Rema: and so too the straps are able to be made from the skin and leather of a fetus (Mordechai, page 94)}
