The hide of an unborn animal is superior, and the skin of a bird is better than either of them, even for writing tefillin upon it. This is stated in Taamei HaMitzvos. It is a halachah transmitted to Moshe at Sinai that both the housings and the straps should be black. Nevertheless, if the housings were made another color, they remain valid. It appears to me, however, that the straps must be black, and if they are not black they are invalid, unlike the housings. This also appears from the words of Lechem Chamudos. See there. The mystical reason for the color black is known. On the level of reason, it symbolizes Hashem’s true unity. Just as black does not receive another color in the same manner as other colors do, so Hashem says, “I, Hashem, have not changed.” So wrote my revered father, of blessed memory.
