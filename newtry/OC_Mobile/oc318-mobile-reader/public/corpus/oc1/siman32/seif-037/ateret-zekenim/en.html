Stillborn hide of a live animal is more praiseworthy, and bird hide is better than both even for writing tefillin (Taamei Mitzvot). Halachah from Moses at Sinai: black between the compartments and between the straps; however, if he made them another color they are valid. It seems the straps must be black, and if they are not black they are invalid, unlike the compartments as above; so appears from the words of Lechem Chamudot; the secret of black color is as known, and intellectually to liken through His true unity — as black does not receive another color as other colors do, so it is said of the Holy One, blessed be He: "I am the the Omnipresent, I have not changed" (so wrote Ateret Zekenim).
