37) Seif 9. If a non-Jew processed it, etc.; and according to the Rosh it is valid if a Jew stands there, etc. If the Jew places it into the lime for its sake, he may then leave the non-Jew alone to remove it and prepare it, and he need not assist him further. Magen Avraham, seif katan 13; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 17; Halachah Berurah, letter 11; GraZ, letter 12. See our words in Yoreh De'ah, siman 271, letter 10.
