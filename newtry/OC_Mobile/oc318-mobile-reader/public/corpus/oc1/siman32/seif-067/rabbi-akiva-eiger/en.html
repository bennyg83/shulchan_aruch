Seif 67. Provided that he covers it with a hide. The Derishah disagrees with this. He holds that if one made four batim intending them to serve as hand tefillin by covering them with a hide, this does not help, since their making was not in accordance with the law. Only the case in siman 42, where the initial making was valid for head tefillin, is helped by covering with a hide. Also there, one may say that they permitted covering with a hide only temporarily, due to pressing need when he has no hand tefillin. But to leave them permanently covered with a hide, one may say, is forbidden.
