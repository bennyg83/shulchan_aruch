He should make four batim. We derive this from “totafos”: “tat” in Katfi means two, and “pat” in Afriki means two. That is, in the places called Katfi and Afriki, when they want to say two, they say “tat” or “pat.” Since it is written “as a remembrance between your eyes,” “one remembrance I told you,” how is this done? He writes them on four pieces of skin and places them in four batim within one hide. But regarding hand tefillin it is written, “and it shall be for you as a sign,” just as there is one sign on the outside, so too there is one sign on the inside.
