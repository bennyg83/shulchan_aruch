He shall make four compartments. They derived it from totafot: tet on katef is two, and pat on afrak is two — meaning in places called katef and afrak when they want to say "two" they say tet or pat. From "for a remembrance between your eyes" — one remembrance I told you — how so? Write them on four hides and place them in four compartments in one hide. In Shir HaShirim it is written "and it shall be for you as a sign" — just as one sign is outside, so one sign is inside.
