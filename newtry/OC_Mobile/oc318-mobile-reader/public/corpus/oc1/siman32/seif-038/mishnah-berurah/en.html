(172) From one hide — and a piece of hide sewn together: the Magen Avraham holds they are considered as one hide; and so too in responsum Chasam Sofer Orach Chayyim siman 5 that one may be lenient with seams; and if they were only glued, he is strict there; but in Chayei Adam it is implied the same if they are glued; and so too it is implied in Or Zarua; and thus the custom has spread now in our countries. Nevertheless l'chatchila it is fitting and correct to make it from one hide literally, for there are those who are strict and hold that pieces sewn together or glued with glue do not help; and see Chayei Adam that in any case they require that the four batim be separate from one another and not glued — unlike what scribes do who glue with glue only above, making marks like a groove superficially; there is doubt whether to validate even b'dieved, for if we consider by glue as one hide, then we have only one bayit with four partitions inside — invalid; and if the glue does not make one hide and there are four batim with four grooves where the glue is considered separate, then the tefillin themselves are made from two hides — invalid.
