And he assisted. See Taz, and Yoreh Deah 271:3 there. Blackening the straps does not help for the processing of straps, since they require processing for their required purpose, as written in siman 33:3; see Magen Avraham here, letter 11. The same applies to preparing the parchment and scoring lines, which do not help, for this is not part of the work of processing. So wrote the Taz.
