Black. See Baer Heitev. What he wrote in the name of that authority regarding ordinary wine of non-Jews is in siman 164, where he concludes that nevertheless, a sacred matter and writing several Divine Names is more stringent. It is proper to sanctify ourselves with what is permitted to us and distance ourselves from ugliness and what resembles it. He also was uncertain whether it is permitted to write tefillin and mezuzos with a quill from an impure bird, perhaps since the substance of the prohibition is not recognizable after the writing, it is unlike skins and sinews of impure animals.
