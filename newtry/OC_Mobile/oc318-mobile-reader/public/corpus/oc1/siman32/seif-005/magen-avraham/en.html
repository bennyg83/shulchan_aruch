With the left hand it is invalid. This means one whose dominant hand is his right. But one who controls both hands, if he wrote with his left hand, it is valid.
And a left-handed person, etc. A left-handed person who wrote with his right hand is also invalid. Rema MiPano wrote in his responsa, siman 38, that there was an incident in Egypt where someone wrote with his mouth, holding the quill with his lips, and he invalidated it even where others cannot be found.
