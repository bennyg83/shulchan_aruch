On the left is invalid. That is when he is proficient with the right; but if he is proficient with both hands, if he writes with the left it is valid.

And a lefty, etc. A lefty who wrote with the right is also invalid. Rama wrote in responsum siman 38: there was an incident in Egypt where someone wrote with his mouth — he held the quill with his lips — and they disqualified it even though he could not find others.
