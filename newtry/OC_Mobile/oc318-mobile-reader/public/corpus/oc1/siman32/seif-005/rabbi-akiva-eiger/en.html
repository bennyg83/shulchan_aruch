Magen Avraham, note 8: and they did not say [this] in Yerushalmi. In the Machazit HaShekel — there is no relevance here. For since he cannot train his hand to align well without ruling lines, he is not permitted to write without ruling lines; if so he is not in the category of "exempt but does" — for he is not exempt from this. But Beit Yosef wrote thus on what the Tur wrote to rule the first line, and if he wishes he may rule them all — Beit Yosef wrote to give a reason why this is not in the category of "exempt but does."
