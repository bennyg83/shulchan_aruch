(17) That he write with his right hand. For writing with the left is not the normal manner of writing. The same applies to making any correction with the left hand in the writing, which is invalid. But separating adhesions between letters appears to be valid even with the left hand, similar to the fact that we validate this, at least after the fact, when done by people who are invalid for writing, as stated below in siman 39; see there.
(18) With the left. This refers to one who controls only his right hand. But if he controls both hands, even if he wrote with his left it is valid. Even if he controls only his right hand, if it is impossible to find others, one should put them on, but not recite a blessing over them. Regarding a scribe who writes with his right hand but does all his work with his left, or the reverse, the Pri Megadim wrote that initially it is proper not to accept him as a scribe, but after the fact one should not prohibit it; see there for the reason.
(19) It is considered his right. Therefore, if he wrote with his right hand it is invalid like an ordinary person writing with his left hand, as above, where others can be found. Rama of Pano wrote that there was an incident in Egypt where someone held the quill with his lips and wrote with it, and they invalidated it, because writing with the mouth is not the manner of writing according to all opinions. Magen Avraham wrote that it is invalid even if others cannot be found. See Sefer Mishnas Avraham in the name of Get Mekushar and other poskim, who equate this case, or writing with one’s foot, to writing with the left hand.
