(17) That he write with his right hand — for one does not write with the left; the same applies to making any correction with the left regarding writing, which is invalid. But to separate touches between letters appears valid even with the left, similar to what we validate regarding those disqualified for this, below in siman 39, at least post factum — see there:

(18) With the left — meaning when he is skilled only with his right; but if skilled with both hands, even if he wrote with his left it is valid. And even if skilled only with his right, if others cannot be found, he may give them tefillin but may not bless over them. A scribe who writes with his right and all his work with his left, or the reverse — Peri Megadim wrote that lechatchilah one should not accept him as a scribe, but post factum one should not forbid; see there the reason:

(19) It is the right hand — meaning if he wrote with his right it is invalid as with the left in general, and similarly if others can be found. Rama wrote: an incident occurred in Egypt where one held the quill in his lips and wrote with it, and they invalidated it, for writing with the mouth is not the manner of writing for anyone. Magen Avraham wrote it is invalid even if others cannot be found.
