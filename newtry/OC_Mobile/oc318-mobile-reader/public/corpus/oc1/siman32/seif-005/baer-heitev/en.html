Invalid. This refers to one who controls only his right hand, but if he controls both hands, and he wrote with his left, it is valid. Magen Avraham.
The right hand. If he wrote with his right hand, it is invalid. The Rama MiPano wrote in siman 38 that one person wrote with his mouth, and he invalidated it even where it is impossible to find others; Magen Avraham. The same applies to anything similar, for this is not the manner of writing. Even if there are no others, one should not recite a blessing on tefillin written in such a manner. Olas Tamid; see there.
