Invalid. This means one who is left-handed only; but if he is ambidextrous and wrote with the left, it is valid — Magen Avraham.

Right. If he wrote with the right it is invalid. Rama, siman 38, wrote that one who wrote with his mouth — they invalidated it even if no others are available — Magen Avraham; likewise anything that is not the manner of writing, even if no others exist, one does not recite a blessing on tefillin written thus; see Orach Tzedek, see there.
