(s"k 10) Or for the name of… but an order; and we hold like Rava that an order is not a matter — although they were written for the name of heaven, may it be blessed, meaning even if the writing is not for its sake, only the order; and that is in tefillin, for the essence of performing the mitzvah is placing them, unlike in a Sefer Torah where beginning to write is called an act — likewise in Sefer Ateret Rosh:

And know that what is stated in Shulchan Aruch seif 37, that our parchment which they drag much toward the flesh side is therefore called klaf — he wrote in Sefer Ateret Rosh that one hears the mark to know which side is toward the flesh; if one moistens it a little in water it shrinks toward the flesh side, and one must be careful that nothing remain on the hide before writing on it a membrane from the part of the flesh side, which is duchsustos; and needless to say in tefillin, where it is forbidden to write on duchsustos, but even in mezuzah, where the mitzvah is to write on duchsustos as explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 288 — nevertheless one must not leave on the hide a thin membrane, for on duchsustos one must write toward the hair side; and if he writes on that membrane he is writing on duchsustos toward the flesh side:
