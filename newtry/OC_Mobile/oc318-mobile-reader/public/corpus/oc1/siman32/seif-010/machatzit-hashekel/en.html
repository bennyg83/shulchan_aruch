(Seif katan 10) Or for the sake of, etc. This is only designation, and we rule like Rava that designation is not legally significant. Even though they were written for the sake of a Torah scroll, meaning that even the writing does not help and is only designation, this is because with tefillin the main performance of the mitzvah is wearing them. This differs from a Torah scroll, where the beginning of the writing is called an act. So too Eliyah Rabbah wrote.
Know that what is written in Shulchan Aruch seif 7, that our parchments are scraped extensively on the flesh side and therefore are called kelaf, Eliyah Rabbah wrote that he heard a sign by which to know which side is the flesh side: if one moistens it slightly with water, it contracts somewhat toward the flesh side. One must take care that before writing on the hide there remains no membrane from the flesh-side layer, which is duchsustus. This is not only regarding tefillin, where it is forbidden to write on duchsustus; even regarding a mezuzah, where the mitzvah is to write on duchsustus as explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 288, nevertheless no thin membrane may remain on the hide, for duchsustus must be written on the hair side. If he writes on that membrane, he is writing on duchsustus on the flesh side.
