Like the form of letters — So the gentile will not switch them for other hides. Specifically like letters; but holes as a mark do not help [Peri Megadim].

To forge — He places hide on hide and also punches with an awl like those letters. Machatzit HaShekel wrote: great care is needed when a gentile craftsman processes — he places cloths on holes in the parchment; those patches are almost certainly not processed leshemah, being cut pieces he takes from whatever comes to hand; only with difficulty are they recognized after processing, against the sun.

Because he enjoys — Otherwise this would not apply leniency [for on his view leshemah is rabbinic] — wherever the gentile regularly enjoys it, it is close to certain. Where he does not enjoy it through switching, we rule we are not concerned for switching [Peri Megadim].
