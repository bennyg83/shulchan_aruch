For it is easy for a non-Jew to counterfeit. For he places the hide on top of another hide and likewise makes holes in the form of letters.
By visual recognition. For he provided signs; Beis Yosef and Hagahos Maimoniyos. Or because these holes were made more recently than his own; Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 1. This requires study, for in Yoreh Deah, siman 118, se'if 3, he wrote that in a place where apostates who know how to write are common, the writing is not a sign except for one who recognizes the handwriting, and we do not say that the non-Jew is afraid lest the writing be recognized. One may say that there too he was stringent only when sending something to his fellow, where the non-Jew knows that he will not recognize the handwriting. Alternatively, one may say that in a food matter they were more stringent; see Yoreh Deah, siman 130, in Beis Yosef. Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote: if he gives it to him initially to process for the sake of sanctity, he may leave the non-Jew to take it out and fix it, and he need not assist him further. In the book Beis Shmuel he wrote that he should write at the head in front of him and not mark it with an awl.
