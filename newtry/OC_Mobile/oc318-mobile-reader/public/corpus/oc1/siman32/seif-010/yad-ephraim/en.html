The Name. For even according to Rosh, etc., "and he shall write in place of the Name" as it should be. Meaning: if some error befell a Divine Name, one may scrape it and rewrite in its place — meaning scrape the Divine Name written in error and write the Name again in that scraped place, and not leave the place completely bare, for lechatchilah one should not leave any bare spot even between verse and verse, as Magen Avraham wrote seif mem heh; how much more between word and word. Sometimes in the scraped place there may remain more than nine letters invalid, as explained seif lamed dalet — therefore he returns and writes the Name where scraped. If he does not wish to return and write God's Name there because where scraped the ink spreads and becomes blurred, he erases the word before it and writes the Name in its place — meaning he extends writing the word so it is written also where the Name was scraped. On this Magen Avraham wrote: in Tosafot Eruvin it says forbidden to write in place where he scraped the Name; I saw in Tosafot Shabbat they explain "in place of the Name" means in place of the word — the word is called shem, as Shach wrote Yoreh Deah siman 274 — not clear to me. See Beit Yosef dispute of Rambam and Sifrei Torah: Rambam permits writing even the Name in scraped place; Sifrei Torah — other words too not to write. Nevertheless it appears here he discusses wanting to be strict about the Name not to write in scraped place, regarding other words not so strict. Main point as above from responsum of Mahari Levi.
