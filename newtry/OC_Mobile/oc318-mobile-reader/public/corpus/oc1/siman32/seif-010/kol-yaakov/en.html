38) Seif 10. When they mark the holes with an awl in the form of letters. This implies that actual letters are required; holes merely made as a sign do not help. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 12. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 18, who wrote as follows: Nevertheless, those who do not wish to trouble themselves and do not mark the hides according to the strict law, but merely fill one or two grooves from a known number and say that this is sufficient, are not acting properly, and one may not rely on this. End quote.
39) There. We are not concerned, etc. Afterward, one must check for the sign, so that hides processed for their sake are not exchanged with hides that were not processed for their sake. Meleches Shamayim, klal 3, letter 7, in Chochmah. They must also be very careful after the processing, for sometimes the non-Jew places patches over holes that were in the hide, and those patches are presumably from hide not processed for its sake. After processing, they are hardly recognizable except against the sun. Machatzis HaShekel, seif katan 11; Meleches Shamayim there; Keset HaSofer, siman 2, letter 6.
