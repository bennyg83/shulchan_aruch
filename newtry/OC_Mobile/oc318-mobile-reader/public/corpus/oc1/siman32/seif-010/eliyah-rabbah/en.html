[10] If possible, etc. And if he has no others, he blesses on these he wears; it seems to me one should not bless, as Beit Yosef and Bach imply. Magen Avraham wrote that if he controls both hands and wrote with the left, it is kosher; a left-handed person who wrote with the right is invalid. Olat Tamid wrote in the name of responsum Menachem Azariah, siman 38, that he wrote: with the mouth it is invalid — even if he has no others one should not bless on them; the responsum itself there requires study.
