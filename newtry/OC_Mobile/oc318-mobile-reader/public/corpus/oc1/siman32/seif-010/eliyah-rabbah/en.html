If possible, etc. If he has no others, the Levush says that he recites the blessing over these. To me it seems that he should not recite the blessing, as is implied by Beis Yosef and Bach. Magen Avraham wrote that if one has equal control of both hands and wrote with his left hand, it is valid, while a left-handed person who wrote with his right hand is invalid. Olas Tamid wrote in the name of Responsa Menachem Azaryah, siman 38, that writing with the mouth is invalid, and even if there are no others one should not recite the blessing over them; this requires study in the responsum itself there.
