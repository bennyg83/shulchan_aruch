Taz, seif katan 7. But if after it was written it was pierced. The Bach wrote likewise, and he follows his own view, as I wrote in his name in the margin of the Taz nearby in seif 10, that if after writing the letter was severed and divided into two, one combines the lower part with the upper part for the measure of the letter. But according to the Taz there, the case is where the upper part alone has the required measure, and the lower part is not combined with it. If so, how can he bring proof from the nearby law of a letter whose inside was pierced? This requires study.
