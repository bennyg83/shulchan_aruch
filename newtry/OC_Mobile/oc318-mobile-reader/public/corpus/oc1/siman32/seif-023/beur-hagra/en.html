Seif 23, if, etc. As written in the Mechilta, as above in seif 1, and in the Yerushalmi, first chapter of Megillah: one may suspend insertions in scrolls, but one may not suspend insertions in tefillin and mezuzos, unlike Tosafos, 32a, s.v. perhaps, etc.
And they shall be, etc. As written in Berachos, Megillah, and Sotah.
But if, etc. As written at the beginning of the second chapter of Maseches Sofrim: if he mixed the letters or made an interruption in the middle of the Divine Name, one may not read from it.
