Se'if 23, "if," etc. As stated in Mechilta, as above, se'if 1; and Yerushalmi, first chapter of Megillah: one hangs Torah scrolls, but one does not hang tefillin and mezuzos — not like Tosafos, 32a, s.v. "perhaps," etc.

Se'if 23, "and they were," etc. As stated in Berachos, Megillah, and Sotah.

Se'if 23, "but if," etc. As stated at the beginning of chapter 2 of Menachos: if one mixed the letters or paused in the middle of the Name, one may not read from it.
