Words. Beit Yosef in the name of Riaz: sometimes correction helps for an extra letter — e.g. leavoteikha plene with vav: after dragging the vav he extends the bet before it to fill the dragged space; but in tzadi we are uncertain, e.g. hotzi'akha written with yod or mitzvot plene with vav — if he erases yod or vav he can extend the lower part not the upper — whether valid or not. Beit Yosef concludes: nevertheless whatever appears as two words is invalid; so Levush — be strict. Magen Avraham: if at the very start he extends the lower leg of nun and tzadi and writes the adjacent letter within it, such gimmel is permitted as one writing, unlike Erlau Nachal chelek 2 siman 78; see Kenesset HaGedolah and Yad Aharon. Extra word — fix. If part of nine letters — when dragged it becomes a portion where there is no portion, invalid; but permitted to extend heh even if no leg at end — Magen Avraham. Found extra vav or yod — drag it and thicken the letter before or after slightly — no change of letter; see Magen Avraham and Yad Aharon (see Eliyahu Rabbah on these matters in the name of Kenesset HaGedolah; responsum Radbaz).
