Words. The Beis Yosef writes in the name of Rias that sometimes correction helps for an extra letter, such as if “to your fathers” was written full with a vav, for after he scrapes away the vav he may extend the beis before it so that the empty place of the scraping is filled. But regarding a tzadi he was uncertain, such as in “He brought you out” where it was written with a yud, or “mitzvos” written full with a vav, and if he erases the yud or vav he can afterward extend the lower part but not the upper part, whether it is valid or not. The Beis Yosef concludes: In any event, whenever it appears like two words, it is invalid. So too the Levush writes that one should be stringent in this. The Magen Avraham writes that if at the beginning of the writing he extends the lower leg of the nun or tzadi and writes the adjacent letter inside it, this is permitted, for it is literally one act of writing, unlike the Ralnach, part 2, siman 78. See Knesses HaGedolah and Yad Aharon. If he wrote an extra word, he may correct it. But if there will be a space of nine letters, so that when he scrapes it away there will be an open section where there should be no section, it is invalid. However, it is permitted to extend the hei even though it will not have a leg at its end: Magen Avraham. If he found an extra vav or yud, he should scrape it away and thicken slightly the letter before it or after it, for this does not constitute changing a letter. See Magen Avraham and Yad Aharon. See Eliyah Rabbah on these matters in the name of Knesses HaGedolah and Responsa Radbaz.
