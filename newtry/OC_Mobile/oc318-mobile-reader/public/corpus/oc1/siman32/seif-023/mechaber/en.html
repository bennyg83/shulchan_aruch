If he finds that one letter is missing, there is no remedy, for then they were written out of order and are invalid, since it is written "and they shall be"— in their state they shall be. If one letter is extra, there is a remedy by scraping it away if it is at the end or beginning of a word; but if it is in the middle of a word, not — for when he scrapes it, it will appear like two words.':
