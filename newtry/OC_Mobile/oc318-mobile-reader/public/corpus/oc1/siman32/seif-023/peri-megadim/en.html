Measure. Taz (half a fingernail, whether in length or in width); and that [sugya] in Menachot 32a — atba of a Sefer scroll; in mezuzah, space for mem-dalet and kaf simple below; "the God-fearing person" he places above mem-dalet, like atba of a Sefer — Bach, Eliyah Rabbah, Magen Avraham. And Mahari Bruna wrote: scribes practiced leaving a little at the beginning and end — meaning a little more than gevil wrapping requires; end of commentary. Some are strict that at the beginning there should be parchment enough to roll the entire parashah, like mezuzah — see there.
