So that. See Taz. Regarding half a fingernail, I do not know whether this is in length or width. The case in Menachos 32a, “the scribes’ saddle,” regarding a mezuzah, requires space for a lamed above and a final kaf below. One who fears the word of Hashem should leave above a lamed as much as the scribes’ saddle, as the Bach and Eliyah Rabbah wrote. What the Rav wrote that scribes are accustomed to leave a little at the beginning and end means a little more than what is needed for surrounding parchment; see Beit Yosef. Some are stringent that at the beginning there should be enough parchment to roll the entire parsha, as with a mezuzah; see there.
