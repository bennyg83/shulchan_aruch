If a child — See Peri Megadim and Eliyahu Rabbah: if full ok of its type, e.g. vav or thin nun, clearly vav or nun — kosher, need not show child.

Knows to read it — Then even large hole or break clearly separated — kosher without any fix; not worse than writing such short letter lekhatchilah.

And if not — Even break afterward, e.g. ink skipped leaving rust-colored trace; small break not evident — showed child part below break, read as letter — invalid: do not combine below, per Taz.
