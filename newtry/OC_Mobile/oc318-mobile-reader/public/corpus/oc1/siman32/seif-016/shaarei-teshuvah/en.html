The pure ones. Be'er Heitev wrote in Bekhor Shor on Shabbat 108 — from where that one does not write tefillin, etc.; and it is per what we explained in one question whether it is permitted to write tefillin on hide of a pure animal that was worshiped and made for idolatry; and we raised per what is written here that we require "from what is permitted in your mouth" (and R. — in his responsum there he proved we require literally "in your mouth," unlike Tosafot who refer to the species; and his book of responsa was lost — a pity) — but even per Tosafot that the intent is the permitted species, nevertheless one must forbid, for idolatry is compared to a corpse, etc., see there.
