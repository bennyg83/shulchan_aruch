The kosher animals. See Baer Heitev. Bechor Shor on Shabbos 108 explains the teaching that tefillin may be written only on the skin of a kosher animal in connection with a question whether one may write tefillin on the skin of a kosher animal that had been worshipped as idolatry. He concluded that since we require something actually permitted in your mouth, it is forbidden. Even according to Tosafot, who interpret the requirement as referring to a permitted species, one should still prohibit it, for idolatry is compared to a corpse.
