Se'if 16, "the simple letters," etc. So too Mordechai — to distinguish between the case here, which is among the simple letters, as stated above, and the vav of "and he shall be put to death" in that of se'if 15.

Se'if 16, "and one need not," etc. So explained Rashi there, s.v. "for he is not wise," etc. — MK.

Se'if 16, "however," etc. For the benefit of a child’s reading for clarification is that the letter is in its proper form there.

Se'if 16, "that we validate," etc. From what is stated: "any letter that is not surrounded," etc.; and this is what he wrote in se'if 15: "if after it was written," etc.; and this is what he wrote: "without surrounding the parchment from its beginning," etc.

Se'if 16, "or if," etc. Even though in Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Berachos, we say: "he mixed the letters" — one version says valid and one says invalid: one says valid from below, one says invalid from above, as in "our land" and "our glory," but "your land" and "your glory" require it. And it was asked: Mordechai and Shiltei Gibborim write that in our Talmud we say "any letter that is not surrounded by parchment," etc., and do not distinguish between below and above — that always requires surrounding. See there.
