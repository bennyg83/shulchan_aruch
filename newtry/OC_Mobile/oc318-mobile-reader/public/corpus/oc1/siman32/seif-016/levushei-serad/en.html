His meaning: after it was written it is valid — as I wrote in letter 19; he brings proof for Bach that even if the ink fell from the hole when it dried, nevertheless since he wrote over the hole while wet, it was valid at the time of writing; so Bach's meaning (see there). Taz disqualifies in this, as in letter 11; I am inclined that this was not written in validity since it is destined to fall when it dries. But if even when dry it does not fall — we wrote Taz admits it is valid. It is possible that in Hagahot, even if later it fell, Taz would also admit it is valid in such a case since when it dried it was sealed — clear.
