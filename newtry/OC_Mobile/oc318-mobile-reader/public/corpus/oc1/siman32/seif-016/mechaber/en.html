If one of the letters was severed, Rama: a simple letter, such as Vav or Zayin, or the leg of the Alef, Nun, and the like was severed, Mordechai, Hilchos Ketanos, folio 92, if a child who is neither wise nor foolish knows how to read it, it is valid, and if not, it is invalid. One need not cover the other letters for him, as is commonly done. Rama: However, if we see that the form of the letter did not remain properly, it is invalid even though the child reads it correctly. Mordechai, Maharik, shoresh 69, and Rivash. That which we validate when a letter was severed is specifically when it was written validly and was severed afterward. But if from the outset, when it was written, there was a hole there and it was severed by it, or if the leg of the final Kaf or the like reaches the edge of the parchment without being surrounded by parchment from the outset, it is invalid.
