If he threw gold dust on the letters, he removes the gold, etc. But as long as he has not removed the gold, the tefillin are invalid, because the upper writing nullifies the lower writing. Beis Yosef; Olas Tamid, letter 5. Olas Tamid wrote there that gold was mentioned as a novelty, and all the more so other kinds of colors; see there. Although as long as he has not removed it the writing is invalid, this is not considered out of order, since he is not writing, but merely removing, and the lower writing remains on its own. Olas Tamid there. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 2, and Meleches Shamayim, rule 6, letter tav in Chochmah and letter 13 in Binah, wrote that it is invalid because of engraving out the inside, and he brought proof from Sefer Get Pashut, siman 125, s.k. 37; see there. But Keset HaSofer, siman 8, letter 13, in Lishkas HaSofer, rejected his proofs and reconciled the words of Get Pashut according to the view of Shulchan Aruch; see there. See Birkei Yosef, Yoreh De'ah, siman 276, letter 16, and Machazik Berachah here, letter 5.
