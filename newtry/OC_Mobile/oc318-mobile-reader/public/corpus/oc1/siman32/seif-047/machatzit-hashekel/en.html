(Note 47) And it did not extend, etc. — for several poskim validate when hanging part of the Name — that is, the entire Name: we rule that one may hang; but part of the Name the Shulchan Aruch wrote that one may not hang, and so too the Shach, that there are those who permit even to hang part of the Name.

And from where do we invalidate, etc., even per the one who holds that one may not hang part of it — but outside the line, which all agree is valid.

And in the Mishna Berurah, etc., since it is not from the Name itself — and even though the Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 276 subsection 7 disagrees and he holds that the yod of Elokeinu requires hanging — see there; nevertheless he admits that if it were not the root of the Name, it would be permitted to hang it; if so, all the more so if it extended, etc.

To erase all the upper lines, etc. — that is, in a sefer Torah; but in tefillin it is impossible to erase, for if so it would be lo k'sidran. Unless it is possible to extend letters written in upper lines without erasing, and also that the form of the letters is not ruined by that extension.
