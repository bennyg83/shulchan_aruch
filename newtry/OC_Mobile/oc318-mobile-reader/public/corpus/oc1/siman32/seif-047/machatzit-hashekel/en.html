(s.k. 47) And it did not emerge, etc. For some authorities validate when he suspended part of the Name. Meaning: regarding an entire Name, we rule that one may suspend it; but regarding part of the Name, Shulchan Aruch wrote that one may not suspend it. About this the Shach wrote that some permit even suspending part of the Name.
And from where do we know to invalidate, etc. Even according to the opinion that one may not suspend part of it, that is only within the line; but outside the line, where we rule that according to everyone it is valid.
And in Mishnas Binyamin, etc. Since it is not part of the Name itself. And although the Taz in Yoreh Deah, siman 277, s.k. 7, disagrees with him and holds that the yud is part of the root, see there; nevertheless, he agrees that if it were not part of the root, it would be permitted to suspend it. If so, all the more so if it emerged, etc.
To erase all the upper lines, etc. This refers to a Sefer Torah; but with tefillin one cannot erase, for if so it would be out of order, unless it is possible to extend the letters written on the upper lines without erasure and without ruining the shape of the letters through that extension.
