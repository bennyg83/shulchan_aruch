47) The requirement that the hole not be so large that the letter appears divided into two applies specifically when it was punctured before the writing. But if it was punctured after it was written, even if it was split in two, it is valid, provided that a child is able to read it. Bach; Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, letter 7; Olas Tamid, letter 21; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 18. This applies if the part extending until the hole has the valid measure of that letter without joining what is below the hole, or if the hole does not interrupt the full thickness of the letter, but a thin complete thread of its thickness remains at the place of the hole; see there.
