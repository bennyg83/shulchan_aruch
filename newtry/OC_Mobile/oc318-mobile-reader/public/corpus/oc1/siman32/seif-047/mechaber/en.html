If he writes all four parshihats on one piece of parchment, they are even kosher, and there is no space between them, provided that there is a thread or a connection between each stanza and the stanza of a hand. He writes the four parshiyats on one piece of parchment and rolls them from end to beginning, and wraps a piece of parchment over them and a calf's hair and puts them in their house like a head.
