If he wrote all four parshiyos on one piece of parchment, they are valid, even if there is no space between them, provided that there is a thread or cord between each bayis and bayis. For the hand tefillin, he writes the four parshiyos on one piece of parchment and rolls them from their end toward their beginning, and wraps parchment over them, with hair over the parchment, and places them in their bayis like a Sefer Torah. If he wrote them on four parchments and placed them in four batim, he fulfills the obligation, and that is when he covers the four batim with one leather, and they are considered like one bayis.
