If he wrote them, etc., and placed them in four compartments, etc. See Pri Megadim, who wrote that according to the Mechaber, if they are all in one compartment, even initially they need not be attached. To this the Rama added and wrote, “and the custom, etc.,” meaning that even in one compartment, initially it is a mitzvah to attach them.
Provided that he covers it with leather. See Pri Megadim, that he should cover with the leather also the place of the shin, so that it not appear outwardly; otherwise there is a concern of adding to the Name of Hashem, for the shin is not needed.
