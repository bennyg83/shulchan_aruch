If he wrote them, etc., and placed them in four housings, etc. — See Peri Megadim, who wrote that per the author, if they are all in one housing, even lekhatchilah he need not glue; on this Rama added and wrote "and the custom," etc. — meaning that even in one housing, lekhatchilah it is a mitzvah to glue them.

Provided he removed a hide — See Peri Megadim that he covers with the hide also over the place of the shin so it not show outside; otherwise there is concern of bal tosif on the Divine Name, for the shin is not needed.
