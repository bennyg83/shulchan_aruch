(Magen Avraham s.k. 2) Meaning: he was not particular about smoke either. So in the responsum of Maharil who wrote regarding ink of herbs — see that he was not particular about smoke either. Nevertheless Shabbat ch. 2 implies they made ink from smoke; if so it is well explained that what is written in Shas as "with the ink mentioned" means that made from this smoke — this continues his words.
