[1] And if he changed, etc. For it is written "and they shall be" in their being — and Malbushei Yom Tov wrote in the name of derashot of Maharash, this is his language: therefore, if the fourth parashah is found invalid, he can write another and not so in the others — rather he writes that one and also those after it — so far. If the first parashah was invalidated and he has an old one that he knows was written before these last three, it is permitted to attach it even though at first the last ones were not written for this combination; but from doubt it is forbidden — so Avodat HaGershuni wrote, siman 60. See the beginning of (siman) 34: I found written — a man who writes tefillin: ink from which he earns a trefeik, and it is istri, and it is an eighth of a Tyrian rock selah from a sheet, chapter HaSholeach — so far.
