Se'if 1, "the mitzvah of," etc. Mishnah 28a.

Se'if 1, "that they are," etc. Rashi there; and Mechilta at the end of parashas Bo: "and it shall be for a sign upon your hand" — for in four places Scripture mentions the section of tefillin: "Sanctify to Me every firstborn," "and it shall be when He brings you," "Hear O Israel," and "and it shall be if you heed." From here they said: the mitzvah of tefillin — the four sections of the arm are one scroll; the four sections of the head are four compartments; and these are: "Sanctify," "and it shall be when He brings you," "Hear," and "and it shall be if you heed." One writes them in order; if one wrote them out of order, they are to be buried. This is what he meant by "and one must," etc.

Se'if 1, "and lekhatchilah," etc. Since in the verse [the section of] "and it shall be" precedes [that of] "Hear," according to R' Yitzchak Abohav and Bach — but not as a matter of law.
