The order of writing tefillin. In it are 52
se'ifim. The mitzvah of tefillin is to write four parshiyot: Kadesh Li Kol Bechor until lemo'adah; VeHayah Ki Yevi'acha until ki bechozek yad hotzi'anu the Omnipresent miMitzrayim; parashat Shema until uvish'arekha; and parashat VeHayah Im Shamo'a until al ha'aretz.They must be written in this order — the order in the Torah; if one changed the order they are invalid. L'chatchila one should write shel yad before shel rosh.
