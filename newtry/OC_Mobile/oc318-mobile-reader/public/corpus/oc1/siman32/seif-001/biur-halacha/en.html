And they must be written, etc. — This Hagahah speaks of precedence of time and precedence of place; explained below, siman 34, in Tur and Beit Yosef; see there.

In this order — See Mishnah Berurah; it is from Orach Chaim and Peri Megadim and other acharonim. Taz in Yoreh De'ah, siman 280: according to Tosafot, Menachot 32a, s.v. dilma — kosher if there is no order in the Shema parashiyot; Eshel Avraham — after they are not adjacent in the Torah. See there in Nekudat HaKesef, who disagrees. I also saw in Kedushat Yom Tov that he too disagrees with Taz; there is no distinction in ordering between parashiyot and letters. He wrote Tosafot hold Mekhilta means order of place on the parchment — it was before sanctification; but if arranged after sanctification, even if written first it is kosher. We for halachah do not rule leniently even so, since it was written out of order, as proven below, seif 23, and Yoreh De'ah, siman 288; also from the Gra in all these places it is proven he holds like Shulchan Arukh, not Tosafot. See below, siman 64, in Biur Halachah, where we resolve Taz's difficulty, with God's help.

And if he changed — If he wrote the later parashah first — even if its writing was in place, e.g. he left part of one page and wrote on the second page "when He brings you," and afterward wrote the parashah "Sanctify" on the first page — it is invalid.
