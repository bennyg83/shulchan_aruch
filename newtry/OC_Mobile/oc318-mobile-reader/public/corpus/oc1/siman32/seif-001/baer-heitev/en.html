Tefillin whose passages were written in their proper order, and over time the first two passages became invalid, and the scribe has two other passages like those that became invalid which are valid, may be joined to the last two passages that remained valid. The hand-tefillin before the head-tefillin. In Ateres Zekeinim he writes, but it appears to me that one should write the head-tefillin first. So too the Ari, of blessed memory, wrote in Sefer HaKavanos that one should write the head-tefillin, repair it completely, and blacken it, and afterward write the hand-tefillin. One should be careful to write all four passages of the head-tefillin and hand-tefillin consecutively, and not interrupt between them with any speech at all; see there.
