And the portion of Shema. One must write the dalet of echad large, like the four dalets that are small. So write the Ari, Magen Avraham.

In this order. Tefillin that were written in order and over time the first two portions became invalid — the scribe may write two more portions like those that were invalidated, which are valid, and join them to the last two portions that remained valid. Responsum Avodat HaGershuni siman 60; see Shekhinat Hashem-Geshem and responsum Devar Shmuel siman 302.

Arm before head. In Ateret Zekenim he wrote, and in our view, that one should write head first; so too the Ari, of blessed memory, in Sefer HaKavanot — write the head portion, complete and blacken it, then write the arm and take care to write all four portions of head and arm consecutively without interrupting with any speech at all; see there.
