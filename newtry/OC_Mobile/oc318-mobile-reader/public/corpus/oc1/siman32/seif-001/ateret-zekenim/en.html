One who writes tefillin must be expert enough to earn a traffic (tariff) and is an istiri (expert scribe), and he is twenty years old in Tyrian selas (see 72b in the margin on the chapter HaSholeach).

He should write the arm tefillin; but it seems to me he should write the head tefillin first, because its sanctity is greater than that of the arm, since it contains shin and dalet.
