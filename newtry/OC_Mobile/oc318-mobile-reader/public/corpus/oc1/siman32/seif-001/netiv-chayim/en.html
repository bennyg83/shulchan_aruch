(Magen Avraham, se'if kaf zayin) Beit Chadash wrote in Even HaEzer — and if the entire length, etc. — and this does not seem so from Rosh, chapter 2 of Gittin, siman 19, who said: if letters are stuck together he may remove the ink between them, but if a drop of ink fell, etc.; since he does not distinguish between letters stuck in part and letters stuck for the full height of the letters, it implies that even in such a case he may separate them.
