Magen Avraham, note 37: that the child is not accustomed [to the form]. Even though in any event it is not a matter of tinok makiro alone — and regarding alef, ayin, and shin letters, if the yuds do not touch, tinok makiro is required — there it must be that we are dealing with a situation where their separation is clearly recognizable. However, I saw in Pri Megadim on this siman, at the end of the laws of the forms of the letters, who wrote that when they are widely separated and their separation is recognizable even in alef, ayin, and shin letters, tinok makiro does not help. And that which he cited alef, ayin, and shin regarding tinok makiro — that is not because clarification is required to validate through clarification of tinok, but rather to exclude lest there be the fault of tinok ein makiro; see there, and it is strained.
