There. Siman 125. The Beis Yosef there validates both of the above cases regarding a get, based on Terumas HaDeshen; see there. The same would apply to tefillin, that according to them it is valid. The Pri Megadim objects: in tefillin we require writing for its sake, so how can we validate the second case, where half the letter was made with no intention at all through ink falling? See there. This is not difficult at all, for a get also requires writing for its sake. Rather, one must say that the Beis Yosef there holds that whether the invalidation is lack of proper intent or engraving the interior, the law is the same: if the invalidation is not in the entire letter, it is valid.
