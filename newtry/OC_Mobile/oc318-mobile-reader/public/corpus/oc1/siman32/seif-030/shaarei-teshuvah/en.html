In invalidity. Be'er Heitev and what he wrote from responsum Mahari Halevi and Magen Avraham — see responsum Dvar Shmuel siman 288 who wrote that although Mahari Halevi clearly permitted dragging the dalet in Elohekha, it seems his advice: if possible, extend the leg at first until more than the roof, and afterward drag somewhat from the roof so the leg remains doubled as initial letter form — in this way one does not erase the letter like hollowing of interiors — end quote; it seems he holds one cannot extend double like the roof literally as Magen Avraham — one [reason]: since he extended in width until almost twice in the leg, presumably no room between lines; and when he extends the leg again double in width it will be very large, unlike large letters — therefore he wrote extend only a little longer than the roof and then drag; uncertain if in such case dragging is permitted in letters of the Name, for perhaps doubling is not for invalidation. See Yad Ephraim on Mahari Halevi and what is written in the seder katan before this.
