In invalidation. See Baer Heitev and what he wrote from Responsa Mahari HaLevi and Magen Avraham. Devar Shmuel wrote that although Mahari HaLevi clearly permitted scraping the daled in the word 'your God,' the best counsel, where possible, is first to extend the leg until it is longer than the roof and afterward scrape some of the roof, so that the leg remains double according to the proper initial form of the letter. In this way one is not erasing the letter in a manner of carving out the inside. It appears that he holds one cannot extend it fully to twice the roof, and this requires study whether such scraping is permitted in letters of the Divine Name.
