[30] That he drag the stylus, etc. The open mem is in two writings — i.e., initially like nun and afterward like vav; and the same applies to any letter written in two stages such as gimmel and the like — if it became invalid in the latter writing, he need only drag that part — Taz; and this requires investigation in Beit Yosef. It is plain all this deals when he did not write after, for otherwise it is lo kelachar yad. And this is the Levush's wording: reish written like dalet, and likewise kaf made like bet — it does not suffice to drag only the leg or roof; however specifically when he wrote the roof with a projection and tag behind it like dalet, and afterward made the leg from below as is the custom of some scribes. But if he wrote reish and afterward they erred and made a tag behind it, it suffices when he drags the roof or the leg with the tag.
