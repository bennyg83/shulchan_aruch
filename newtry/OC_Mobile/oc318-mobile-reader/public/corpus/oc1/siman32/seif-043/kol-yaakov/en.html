43) Seif 13. The parchment should be whole, etc. A small hole like the point of a needle, which closes when ink is passed over it so that the letter is not split in two, is not called a hole, and one may write over it. Beis Yosef; Emes LeYaakov in the laws of holes and tears; Ledevid Emes, siman 14, letter 1; Beis Menuchah in the laws of holes, letter 1.
