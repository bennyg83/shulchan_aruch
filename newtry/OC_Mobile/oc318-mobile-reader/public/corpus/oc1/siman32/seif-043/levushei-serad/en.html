(Taz s"k 21) To fill the line. Apparently meaning that since the lines must be equal as stated in Shulchan Aruch seif 33, therefore he lengthened the nun; it is difficult — what is the matter of a bent nun at the end of the line, and furthermore why did he write that there is doubt whether it is valid, for even if they are not equal it is not invalid bedieved; and it is possible that his intent is such as the word le-avoteinu at the end of a line, and he wished to align the lines — he lengthened the nun so that he would write the vav beside it and the lines would be equal, and we were uncertain lest since he does not extend upward it counts as two words. Nevertheless it is not precise why he did not state this doubt plainly, such as when an extra vav was written — as in Beit Yosef brought in Magen Avraham s"k 36, such as he wrote "noten" — and why was it necessary to invent that he wrote thus to align the lines; therefore it is more plausible that it is a textual error and should read "to fill the section," that is, as the words of Beit Yosef, and the word "line" is a textual error:
