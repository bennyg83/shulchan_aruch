Seif 17. Because it is. If one made a shin with four heads and then scraped away one of the heads, it is not called engraving from within, for from the outset it too was called a shin, and no new letter was made by erasure. So wrote Maharikash, Even HaEzer, siman 125, seif 8.
There. Like engraving from within. Passing the quill over it does not help. Sefer HaTerumah, laws of tefillin.
