[17] And thus they practice, etc. The Bach and latter authorities validate this ex post facto even without a Gentile assistant. The Taz wrote that even if a Gentile assists at the end of the processing it helps, but what a Jew assists in blackening the hides does not help, for that is not part of the crafts of processing; and anyone who is lenient in this causes many to sin — so far; and thus wrote Magen Avraham. But I found in the responsum of Be'er Sheva siman 10 for practical halacha that it is permitted, for thus they practice in Saloniki, and thus ruled the rabbi (Menachem Korkos) of Romemni — and this requires investigation. And in the Rosh at the end of the laws of tzitzit, correction, smoothing, and scraping done for its sake do not help. And see in the Tur in Yoreh De'ah there that if they have the ability to return the parchment to a stretched state and pass lime over it for its sake it helps; and in the responsum of Be'er Sheva there it implies that he argues with the Rosh — but in my humble opinion they do not argue; understand this well. It is written in Hagahot Maimuni that if a Jew gives it initially for processing for the sake of holiness he need not have an assistant. Be'er Sheva also wrote: leave the hide in lime until the hair falls off by itself; and if one removed it before then one may not write on it. Mahari Luria wrote in Shulchan Aruch: one must process two hides — one for the head strap and one for the hand strap; therefore when placing the hide in lime and water for the head straps one should intend the name of the Tetragrammaton, and for the hand straps the name Adonai — and this is for their sake — so far. And even though I have not seen people practice thus, I wrote it for the meticulous.
