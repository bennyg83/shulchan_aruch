If it fell, etc. — Know: wherever Shulchan Arukh says dragging alone does not help for invalid interior — poskim imply no difference during or after writing; invalid for lacking gevil wrap depends on Beit Yosef's two answers on "how do we validate," as Taz ruled; Peri Megadim copied below, seif 25; see Get Pashut, siman 125, Even HaEzer.

A drop of ink — See Mishnah Berurah, seif kaf-alef; Darkhei Chaim in laws of Torah reading, law 35: wax drop on letter on weekday — remove [on divine names not erased even weekday, fear peeling letter — warm gevil outside against drop, wax removes easily without adhering to writing — Shulchan Arukh, gate 5, law 7]. Shabbat: if letter visible through wax and readable — kosher; if covered in wax between readers — cannot read by heart what wax covers, no Chumash from honor of congregation, no scroll defect (two men two scrolls not defect) — take another; mid-reading — read covered by heart, then finish with remaining readers since wax-covered part already read — may continue in this scroll, wax cover not disqualifying. See Shulchan Arukh there and Ra'avad's chiddushim here. Gra: Jewish craftsman fixing worn siddur must place paper glued (with spit) on names not erased — concern erasure, cannot peel keeping all letters — worse than wax; he elaborated; craftsman should place paper on each name without glue or little wax — not erasure [Shabbat scraping wax from Torah scroll — different reason, see there]; then glue paper so second paper on first over name — not erasure if peel second, name whole.

And it is not recognizable — See Mishnah Berurah, seif kaf-samekh-bet: on ink lines of letter itself we wrote to be lenient; Peri Megadim, Magen Tzedek, seif 15, leans strict — writing over writing if not, at drop's fall letter already dry — challenged; Piskei Tosafot, Even HaEzer, order of Gittin, settles somewhat with great strain — do not be strict after majority lenient even on true writing-over-writing, all the more ink drop falling by itself. Another advice: even writing-over-writing, second not leshemah — pass quill leshemah; helps, not "out of order" since form from first writing, as Magen Avraham, seif 39.

And thus the letter will be recognizable — See Mishnah Berurah: for law even fix not needed, etc. Drop outside letter near letter during writing — even if letter still visible and did not touch another letter — must drag drop at contact point on letter, else letter not considered wrapped in gevil, as Mishnah Berurah, siman 75 — see there.

Invalid because we require "and he shall write," etc. — See Beit Meir, Even HaEzer, siman 125: holds like Rashba — not engraving violation since initially made by writing; mistaken dalet for reish — only rabbinic interior violation; Gra in chiddushim disagrees; Yeshuot Ya'akov, Gittin 20 — from Torah.

And he wrote dalet instead of reish — Same if dalet made after reish finished — do not validate by scraping per author's ruling on ink drop and letter touch even after finished, unlike Semak; later Ra'avad's chiddushim noted this.
