If a drop of ink fell into a letter and the letter is not recognizable, there is no remedy of scraping away the ink so that the letter becomes recognizable, for that is considered engraving from within, and it is invalid, because we require 'and he shall write,' and not 'and he shall engrave.' The same applies if he erred and wrote a Dalet in place of a Reish, or a Beis in place of a Kaf; there is no remedy of erasing the tag to correct the letter, because this is like engraving from within.
