The letter. If it lacks a tip of a letter or appears as the body of a letter though the letter is evident — invalid — Radak, Shekhinat Hashem-Geshem, Pri Chadash in Orach Chaim siman 125, seif kaf-alef 15 disagrees and wrote he sees no reason for this ruling; if the roof or thigh was thickened by a drop of ink on the letter body, he may fix it and rewrite with the quill — this is not chak tochot. Likewise a tiny protrusion outside the letter that does not change the form and the letter is evident to a neither-wise-nor-foolish child — he inclines to validate; see there. So Orach Tzedek; see there. If a drop fell, even erasing it completely and completing the letter by writing is invalid — the whole letter must be by writing — Radak. A drop made like half a letter — we find none who permit completing by writing — Rabbi Moshe Alshakar siman 5; but Beit Yosef in Orach Chaim siman 125 validates; so Rama there, seif dalet. Maharikash in Orach Chaim siman 125: if he made a hanging word appearing as two words and on the side of the parts made shin of three heads into four heads and lengthened to fill the gap — then dragged the middle heads of shin leaving shin of three heads made by itself — valid and not chak tochot; nevertheless better to erase the first head before writing the extended shin — Maharam ben Chaviv there, seif kaf-lamed 35; Pri Chadash there, seif kaf-alef 15 rejected and wrote in such a case it is chak tochot and invalid; see there; the second correction is primary. If he wrote two words as one by an extra letter between them — e.g. needed bechozek yad and added kuf between chozek and yad as bechozekk yad, or wrote bechozek adding bet at the start of bechozek — permitted to erase the bet and kuf between them; no invalidity of chak tochot — Get Pashut siman 125, seif kaf-lamed 36. If the leg of heh attached like a hair's breadth and a neither-wise-nor-foolish child knows it is heh — one who would separate the leg alone, I would not restrain him — Erlau Nachal siman 1 — examine. Radak, Bayit 1, chelek 31, and Pri Chadash in Orach Chaim siman 125, seif 10 disagreed — one should not rule leniently and should rebuke one who does so. So Orach Tzedek: since it appears no letter form remains though thin — whether thick or thin, if attached what difference thick or thin? If a drop fell inside the space of bet appearing as peh, or no bet on it, or fell into another letter with no name on it — no remedy by erasing — Beit Yosef. Two bet adjacent in one word stuck underneath so the form is not recognizable as bet or kaf — no remedy by dragging the adhesion, for it is chak tochot, only by erasing the second leg and rewriting — Devar Shmuel siman 89. If wax dripped and was removed — permitted even if the letter is not visible; one may drag off the wax — no chak tochot — Pri Chadash; so Magen Avraham.

To drag. Even passing the quill after chak tochot does not help — Orach Tzedek.

Kaf. The same applies in reverse.

Chak tochot. Only engraving invalidates; but if he wrote resh resembling dalet and wants to add ink to round it into resh, or made resh instead of dalet — there is remedy by adding ink to square it — permitted; likewise from bet to kaf or reverse — the law is so; all this where there is remedy before writing the next letter; afterward it is out of order and invalid in tefillin. Tefillin where the scribe happened to write a simple portion at the end and extended the roof of kaf to the line end so the roof width resembles large dalet or resh — what is the law?
