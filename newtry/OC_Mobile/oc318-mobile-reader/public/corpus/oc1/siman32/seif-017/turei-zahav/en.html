Ḥak tukhot [hollowing the interior]. Explanation: he hollows out the interior of the erasure, and thereby what is not erased remains in the form of a letter. It is obvious that even if he merely dipped ink on it, it is still invalid, since in any case the form of the letter was invalidated before the correction.

Dalet in place of resh, etc. There is no correction by erasing, etc. Explanation: through erasing the tag there is no correction; but if he wishes to add ink to it and make it round, that certainly is fine. So too if he made resh in place of dalet—there is correction by adding ink and making it square, for only ḥakika [incising] invalidates; and wherever there is correction, that is before he wrote the letter after it—but not afterward, for then it is written out of order and invalid in tefillin.
