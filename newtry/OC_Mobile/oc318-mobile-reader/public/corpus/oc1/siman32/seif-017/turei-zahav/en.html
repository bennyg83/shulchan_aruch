Engraving from within. This means that he engraves out the inside of the erasure, and automatically what he does not erase remains in the form of a letter. It is obvious that even if the drop of ink is still moist, it is nevertheless invalid, since in any event the form of the letter became invalid before the repair.
A dalet instead of a reish, etc.; there is no remedy to erase, etc. This means that there is no remedy through erasing the tag. But if he wants to add ink to it and make it round, that is certainly proper. Likewise, if he made a reish instead of a dalet, there is a remedy by adding ink and making it square. Only engraving invalidates. Whenever there is a remedy, this is only before he wrote the following letter, but not afterward, for then it is out of order and invalidates tefillin.
