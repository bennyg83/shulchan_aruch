Se'if 17, "if," etc. The same applies, as stated in Gittin 20a.

Se'if 17, "into the letter," etc. Not like Semak, who validates ink that fell after the letter was completed — from the aforementioned Yerushalmi, for we hold not like Yerushalmi. They also proved from chapter 5 of Menachos, halachah 7: but ink that dripped onto the writing may be erased, etc. — that is no proof, for there one erases the whole letter, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 276, se'if 1 in the gloss.

Se'if 17, "a lamed," etc. — bet in place of kaf. Shabbos 103b; and even bedieved, since he challenges Rav Chisda and R' Chananel that he speaks bedieved.
