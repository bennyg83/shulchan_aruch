And the letter is not cut off. If it lacks a bit of a letter or appears as if it is the body of a letter even though the letter is clearly recognizable, it is invalid (Radbaz, Orach Chayyim ch. 31, simanim 31–34) (thus). Even if he completely erased the drop and completed the letter by writing, it is invalid, for we require the entire letter to be formed by writing (Radbaz). See R' Moshe Alshakar siman 5; and thus he wrote: an ink drop fell and it is made like half a letter — we do not find anyone who permits completing the letter by writing. But Beit Yosef in Orach Chayyim siman 125 validates it, and so Rama there, seif 4; see there several rulings. If a drop fell inside the hollow of a bet and it looks like a pe, or there is no bet on it, or it fell into another letter and its name is not on it — there is no remedy by erasing (Beit Yosef in Beit HaTefillah). It seems to me that if wax dripped and he removed it, it is permitted; see there siman 340 seif 3.

Bet in place of kaf — and the same applies in reverse (R' Moshe Alshakar, thus). If he wrote a dalet resembling a resh, he may fix it with a quill as long as he did not write more (Beit Yosef, R' Yitzchak, and so Rivash wrote siman 7); and the same from bet to kaf.
