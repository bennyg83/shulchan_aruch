(Shulchan Aruch se'if 15) If after it was written — this wording too is Taz's. See (Taz s.k. 14) that he wrote Taz does not accept Beit Yosef's second answer, and the author in this se'if also follows Tur — so why did they write here "if after it was written"? There is no distinction, and it is valid for Bavli even if the hole was first before writing and he filled the entire hollow. Perhaps because poskim write only what is explicit in Shas: the case of piercing inside in Shas deals with piercing after writing, not that writing occurred at the hole — for regarding piercing the thigh it says if k'malei ot katanah remained, it is clear it deals with piercing after writing; therefore they wrote here too "after it was written." In truth it is not precise; likewise what they wrote here "pierced inside" is not precise, since we stand in Beit Yosef's first answer — we are not strict about surrounding gvil except for letter touching letter. Hence even a hole outside the letter, which is stricter, is also valid; this is the law of the next se'if. These two se'ifim are not "both this and that" — they deal with both before and after writing. The author wrote "we validate, etc." per Beit Yosef's second answer. Some dispute: what difference between inside the letter and outside? In truth his words contradict each other, as written (Taz s.k. 14).
