Shulchan Aruch, seif 15. If after it was written. This is also the language of the Tur. See Taz, seif katan 14, who writes that the Tur does not accept the Beis Yosef's second answer, and that the Mechaber in this seif also follows the Tur's approach. If so, it requires study why they wrote here, 'if after it was written,' since there is no distinction, and according to the Bavli it is valid even if the hole existed first before the writing and then he filled the entire hollow of the hole. Perhaps it is because the poskim tend to write only what is explicit in the Gemara, and the case there of a hole inside the letter deals with a hole made after it was written, not with writing occurring at the place of a hole. For regarding the case where its leg was pierced, the Gemara says that if enough remained to form a small letter, it is valid; this shows that it is speaking of a hole after writing. Therefore they also wrote here, 'after it was written,' but in truth this is not precise. Likewise, what they wrote here regarding a hole inside the letter is also not precise. Since they are operating with the Beis Yosef's first answer, we are not particular about surrounding blank parchment except regarding one letter's adhering to another. If so, even a hole outside the letter, which is more severe, would also be valid. This is the law of the following seif, and these two seifim are written in the form of 'not only this but even this.' Both cases concern even where the hole existed before it was written. Afterward the Mechaber wrote, 'that which we validate,' etc., in accordance with the Beis Yosef's second answer, and some disagree whether it is inside the letter or outside it. In truth, his words contradict each other, as stated in Taz, seif katan 14.
