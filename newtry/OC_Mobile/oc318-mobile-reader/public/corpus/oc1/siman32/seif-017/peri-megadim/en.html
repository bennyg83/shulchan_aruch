In the form. See Taz. Whenever the invalidity was made only in one letter, he scrapes that letter, and that suffices, for example gimel, vav, yud, and the like. If a heh was written instead of a dalet, scraping the leg and leaving a dalet is not enough. This resembles a samech or beis into which a drop of ink fell, where he does not perform an act in the body of the letter, and it is invalid. So too, where a heh was written in place of a dalet, he must scrape away a bit of the roof or the side and make an act in the body of the letter. See Magen Avraham, letter 25.
