Regarding form. In the matter of seif 16: whenever the invalidity was made only in one [letter], one drags that letter — gimel, vav, yod, and the like; and if he wrote heh instead of dalet, he drags the leg and a dalet remains. Similarly with samekh and bet when a drop of ink fell there that does not act upon the body of the letter — invalid. So too heh instead of dalet: one must drag somewhat from the roof or thigh and act upon the body of the letter. And Magen Avraham, letter 25.
