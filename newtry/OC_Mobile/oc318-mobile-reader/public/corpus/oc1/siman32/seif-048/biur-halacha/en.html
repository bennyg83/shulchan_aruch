If he plated the compartments, etc. See Mishnah Berurah in the name of Pri Megadim. He wrote further: if valid leather is placed over valid leather and the shin is visible, it is valid, even though the compartment does not see the air, since the shin is visible. According to this, all the more so with those who make a thick color that yellows the tefillin, there is no concern, and the shin is also clearly visible, end quote. This is what we wrote in Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 185, in his name.
