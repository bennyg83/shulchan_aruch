He coated the housings, etc. — See Mishnah Berurah in the name of Peri Megadim; he also wrote that hide is kosher on kosher hide, and the shin appears kosher even though the housing does not see air, since the shin is visible; therefore all the more those who apply thick paint that whitens tefillin — there is no concern, and the shin is also well visible — so far. This is what we wrote in Mishnah Berurah, se'if kuf-peh, in his name.
