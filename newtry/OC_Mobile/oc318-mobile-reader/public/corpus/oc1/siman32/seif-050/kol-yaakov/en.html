50) Seif 15. If after it was written it was punctured, etc.; but in the Yerushalmi it implies, etc. The Shulchan Gavoah, letter 34, wrote: Our master cited this Yerushalmi only as an initial concern, for where there is an anonymous ruling followed by a dispute, the halachah follows the anonymous ruling. Therefore, if there are no other tefillin except these, he puts them on with a blessing. End quote. So wrote Tosefes Shabbos in Sefer Pelaim; Keset HaSofer, siman 7, letter 7; Emes LeYaakov, klal 10, letter 2. See Levush on Taz, seif katan 14, and our words below, letter 69.
