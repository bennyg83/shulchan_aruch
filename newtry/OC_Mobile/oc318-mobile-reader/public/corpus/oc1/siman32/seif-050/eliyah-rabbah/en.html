[50] And one must leave, etc. Olat Tamid wrote, and it appears that after the fact it is not disqualifying. And this is Maharil's wording in siman 179: since in a Torah scroll the reason to leave between word and word like a full letter is only for ornament, and in tefillin which are covered there is no concern — and similarly wrote Sheltei Gibborim and Knesset HaGedolah, until here his wording. One could say: I looked at Maharil there, also Sheltei Gibborim and Knesset HaGedolah, and the law of between word and word is not mentioned in this context; rather they speak of the law between line and line — see there. In any case it appears for practical halacha that it is kosher after the fact even if he did not leave between letter and letter like a hair's breadth, and between word and word like a full letter; but if it appears as one writing from the closeness until a child would err in it, it is invalid, as explained in Yoreh Deah siman 274.
