One should not buy, etc. So Beit Yosef in the name of Agur: one should not buy sinews from a non-Jew, for they also take from an unclean animal — a Torah doubt — and every fixed case is like half on half, end quote. What Iyun Tzion wrote — why "fixed"? Derive for me that most animals are unclean. One may say: in any case most edible animals are pure, and from those not eaten one does not buy sinews, for certainly the non-Jew does not slaughter his animal because of sinews, and nevelot and tereifot are few. It seems to me specifically when it is known that some of them use impure animals — that is "fixed"; but stam we follow the majority, as in Yoreh De'ah end of siman 86: one buys eggs from a non-Jew and need not fear they are nevelah or tereifah — that is the Talmud in Chullin. Even when it is known that some use impure animals — specifically in their homes it is forbidden to buy; but in the market it is permitted, for whatever separates from the majority separates, as in Yoreh De'ah siman 114, see there — and it is in Ketubot chapter 1 and above siman 513 in Beit Yosef. It seems to me another reason it is forbidden to buy from a non-Jew: since for them it is a Torah law that spinning requires leshmah, as siman 33 se'if 4 — and a non-Jew is not subject to leshmah.
