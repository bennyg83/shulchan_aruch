Seif 50, one should not buy, etc. As stated in Chullin 63b: perhaps it is from a bird, etc.
The place, etc. There on 31b: but not with flax. The Baal HaItur wrote: but with flax, it has nothing in it; these are telidoras that are made from parchment. Since it is of its own kind, it is permitted, for they are like sinews themselves. The Beit Yosef wrote: the place, etc., one may rely on him so as not to be idle from the mitzvah of tefillin.
