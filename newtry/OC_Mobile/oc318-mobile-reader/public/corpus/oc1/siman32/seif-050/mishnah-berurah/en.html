(240) We are concerned, etc. — and specifically when he goes to gentiles' houses to buy sinews from them, and he also knows that some gentiles take sinews from an impure animal; therefore, even though the majority take from a pure animal, since he goes to a gentile at a time when they are fixed in their homes, one does not follow the majority, for anything fixed is considered half and half; but if the gentile brought them to the market, it is permitted to buy from him, since having left his fixed place we say "whatever separates from the majority separated" — unless they are sold in market shops, in which case they return to their fixed status; and likewise if it is not known that some take them from an impure animal, it is also permitted to buy from them in any case where the majority of sinews are from pure animals. But all this is with sinews that are not spun; with spun sinews, Magen Avraham holds it is forbidden to buy from them even if he knows they are from a pure animal, since the halachah leMoshe miSinai requires that their making be lishmah, and spinning the sinews is their making, and gentiles are not fit for lishmah; and we already wrote that Eliyah Rabbah and Peri Megadim question whether spinning requires lishmah at all:

(241) Tallitor — they are threads made from klaf; and in a pressing situation some hold that since their appearance is like sinews themselves, they are valid for sewing a Torah scroll:

(242) Until an opportunity arises — so as not to neglect the mitzvah of tefillin; and when sinews become available to him he should remove them and sew with sinews. Some of the latter authorities are concerned not to fulfill the mitzvah with them even in a pressing situation, for the halachah leMoshe miSinai is sinews, and tallitor is hide — that is, klaf — and we have not heard of hide for sewing; therefore, if he has no other sinews, at any rate he should not recite a blessing on them. Likewise thin, dry intestinal membranes like sinew threads — one should not sew with them, for the halachah leMoshe miSinai is specifically sinews.
