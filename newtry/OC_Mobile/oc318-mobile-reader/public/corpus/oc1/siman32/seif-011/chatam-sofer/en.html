There, in Magen Avraham, se'if katan 52. Note: See Responsa Chatam Sofer, Orach Chayyim, siman 5. He discusses at length a Sefer Torah, tefillin, mezuzah, or get written and fashioned from two pieces of hide: whether stitching or gluing joins them into one. He explains the entire relevant sugya and concludes: 'I have written what appears correct in my humble opinion. The ruling is that a stitched join invalidates a get and a mezuzah (even between columns) except according to Rambam and R' Yitzchak, the Tosafist. Regarding tefillin compartments, stitching is valid, while gluing is invalid, as with the sheets of a Sefer Torah.'
