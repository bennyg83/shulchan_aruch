[11] One need not rule lines, etc. Because they are covered and one does not read in them. It is somewhat difficult — if so, why do we require ruling lines in a mezuzah in Yoreh De'ah, siman 288, even though mezuzah is halachah leMoshe miSinai? In any case, what is the reason? However, in Sefer HaTerumot, siman 196, I found another reason: since in tefillin there are only a few lines — nevertheless, on this reason it is difficult. My master, my father the rabbi, may his light shine, answered that a mezuzah differs — whenever he wishes he can take it from the tube and read it; also in a mezuzah one must check it twice a week — so far. Afterward I found in Kessef Mishneh, chapter 1 of Laws of Tefillin, as his words.
