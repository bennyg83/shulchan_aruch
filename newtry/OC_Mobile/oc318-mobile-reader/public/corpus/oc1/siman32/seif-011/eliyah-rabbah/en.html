It need not be scored, etc. Because they are covered and are not read. This is somewhat difficult: if so, why do we require scoring for a mezuzah in Yoreh Deah, siman 281, even though a mezuzah is a law given to Moshe at Sinai? In any event, the reason is difficult. However, in Sefer HaTerumos, siman 196, I found another reason: because tefillin contain only a few lines. Still, this reason too is difficult. My father, the rabbi, may his lamp shine, answered that mezuzah is different, because whenever he wants he can easily take it from its tube and read it; moreover, a mezuzah must be checked twice every seven years, end quote. Afterward I found in Kesef Mishneh, chapter 1 of Hilchos Tefillin, in accordance with his words.
