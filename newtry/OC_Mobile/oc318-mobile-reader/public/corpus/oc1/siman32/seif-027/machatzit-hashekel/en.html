(s.k. 27) And separated it, etc. The Bach wrote, etc., that if the entire length of the letter was attached, etc. But Chok Moshe and Pri Chadash in Even HaEzer, siman 185, seif 6, disagree and are lenient to separate it, unless the form of the letter was changed by the attachment.
If it became attached, etc. The correct reading is: one who is lenient to separate it, I will not protest against him.
See Yoreh Deah at the end of siman 276, meaning if this occurred in the hei of the Name, how it is handled. And regarding what the Taz wrote in s.k. 18 about attached letters, with respect to taking out another Sefer Torah, that one may rely on the Rashba, see what Magen Avraham wrote about this in siman 143, s.k. 7.
