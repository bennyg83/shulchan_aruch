And afterward it became invalid, etc. This is difficult, in light of what the Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote, that the Yerushalmi invalidates when the entire inside is perforated, even after the writing. So too the Levush wrote in se'if 13. If so, all the more should we require a large surrounding blank area on the outside, and if so, even if it happened after the writing it should invalidate. A similar difficulty also arises for me on the Beis Yosef, s.v. 'and if you say, etc.,' regarding the explanations he gave there, and also regarding the responsum of Rabbi Levi that the Beis Yosef cited. This requires study, and I have been brief.
