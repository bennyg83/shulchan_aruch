Se'if 6, it is not necessary to score lines, etc. Bach wrote that Shulchan Aruch ruled like Tosafos and the Rosh in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, that if one knows how to write straight without scoring, he has no permission to score all the lines, for he is considered an ordinary person acting unnecessarily. Moreover, since Baal HaItur and Rabbeinu Simchah hold that scoring lines in tefillin invalidates them even after the fact, therefore one should score only the top line, which is required in any event because it is prohibited to write three words without scoring. But he should not score all four sides, lest according to them he has done an extra act and invalidated the tefillin. The rabbi in the gloss to Shulchan Aruch, however, followed the stringent authorities and wrote that one must score the four sides, and he inserted the word must, end quote of Bach; see there. This is also the explicit reading in Olas Tamid, that one should not score; see there, letters 12 and 13. Such was the reading before Magen Avraham; see s.k. 6, Machatzis HaShekel there, Levushei Serad there, Halachah Berurah, letter 7, Maamar Mordechai, letter 7, and Birkei Yosef, letter 1.
