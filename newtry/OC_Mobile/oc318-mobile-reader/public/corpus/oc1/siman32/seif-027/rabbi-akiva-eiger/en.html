Magen Avraham comment 33 — and it seems to me that if one extends the roof. See responsum of Radbaz there; two reasons are given. One: the letter is not kosher unless the leg of the body is at the head of its roof, for it requires the form of hei, kuf, etc., from the letters of the special Name — and thus there is no remedy that tefillin be invalid lekhatchilah. Two: since he corrects the letter, it is lekhatchilah valid; and what difference to me whether part of the letter is lekhatchilah or the whole letter is lekhatchilah? And you might say it is different here, for here the whole letter is complete and this is only an addition — thus this is only a mere smudge and not part of the letter's essence; better to leave part of what would be interrupted in this smudge — end quote. And Rabbi Shmuel wrote siman 168: we never found to invalidate for extending roofs.
