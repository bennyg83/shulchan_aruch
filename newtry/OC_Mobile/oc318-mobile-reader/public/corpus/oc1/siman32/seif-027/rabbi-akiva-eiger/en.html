Magen Avraham, seif katan 33. But it appears to me that this is what he means: if he extends the roof. See Responsa Radbaz there, where two reasons are given. First, the letter is not made valid unless the leg of the gimel is at the head of its roof, for it requires the form of the letter, etc., from the letters of the special Name; consequently there is no remedy, because in tefillin it would be out of order. Second, since he is repairing the letter, it is out of order, and what difference is there to me between part of the letter being out of order and the whole letter being out of order? And if you will say that here it is different, since the entire letter is complete and this is only an addition, if so, this is merely a blur and is not part of the essence of the letter, and it is better to leave it blank than to separate it with this blur. End quote. Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 168, wrote that we have never found that extending the roofs invalidates.
