Tefillin, whether of the head or of the hand, are halachah le-Moshe mi-Sinai that they be square at their seam and at their diagonal—that is, that their square be aligned so length equals width, in order that they have that diagonal of which the Sages said: for every amah in a square, an amah and two-fifths in the diagonal. And one must square the place of their resting, and also the compartments.But regarding the height of the compartments we are not particular if it is greater than their width and length (Beit Yosef in the name of Asheri, Semak, Mordechai, and Rambam chapter 3).} If one made them square and afterward their squareness was ruined, there are those who say one must square them again. {Rama: And one makes all four compartments equal so one is not taller than its fellow (Baruch She'amar). עשאן מרובעות ואחר זמן נתקלקל ריבוען יש מי שאומר שצריך לרבען: הגה ויעשה כל הד' בתים בשוה שלא יהא אחד גדול מחבירו: [ברוך שאמר]:
