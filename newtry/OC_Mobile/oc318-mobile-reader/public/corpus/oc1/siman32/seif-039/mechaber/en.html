Tefillin, whether of the head or of the hand, are a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai that they must be square in their stitching and in their diagonal, meaning that their square must be exact, their length like their width, so that they have the same diagonal of which the Sages said: every cubit in a square has a cubit and two fifths in the diagonal. One must square the place where they sit, and also the batim. Rama: But regarding the height of the batim, one need not be particular if it is greater than their width and length. Beis Yosef in the name of the Rosh, Semak, Mordechai, and Rambam, chapter 3. If he made them square and after some time their squareness became ruined, there is one who says that he must square them again. Rama: He should make all four batim equal, so that one is not larger than another. Baruch She'amar.
