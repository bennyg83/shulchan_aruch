The Taz wrote: for even if the thread broke one may tie it; but in Magen Avraham note 68, if broken it is evident it is detached and even tying does not help — all the more what he wrote in the name of Eliyah Rabbah that a short thread one ties, etc. — it is proved in Biur that if broken even tying does not help; and scribes practice thus for halachah. But if he has no other sinews, the Taz is fit to rely on him and tie a broken thread. "Kafufah" — bent: end of the word straight in the middle — Yerushalmi Megillah chapter 1 halachah 2:
