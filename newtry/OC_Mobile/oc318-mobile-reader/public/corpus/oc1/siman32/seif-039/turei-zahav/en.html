So that they should have that diagonal, etc. This means in a true square, whose length is exactly equal to its width. The Sages estimated that whatever measure it has along its side, its diagonal has two fifths more than that measure. Here too it must actually be greater. But if it is not truly square, its diagonal will not have an addition of two fifths, but a different measure.
Their squareness was ruined. Although regarding tzitzis we say in siman 11, seif 10, that if the woof threads were ruined after they had been made validly, one need not repair it afterward, tzitzis is different, for it is written, “and they shall make for themselves tzitzis,” and the verse is particular about the time of making. But tefillin being square is a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai, and at every moment we require that they be square.
