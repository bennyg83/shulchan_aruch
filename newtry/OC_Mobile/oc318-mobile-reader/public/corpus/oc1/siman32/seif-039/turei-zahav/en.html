So that they have that same diagonal, etc. Meaning: in a perfect square whose length equals its width exactly, the Sages estimated that whatever measure it has, its diagonal has two-fifths added to that measure. Here too it must be truly increased. But if it is not an exact square, its diagonal will not have the measure of two-fifths added but a different measure.
