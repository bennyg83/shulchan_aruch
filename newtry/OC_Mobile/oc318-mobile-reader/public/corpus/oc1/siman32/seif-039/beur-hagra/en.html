Seif 39, between, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. tefillin, etc., and other poskim, unlike the Halachos Gedolos, who wrote that for the hand tefillin their length is about half a cubit finger and their width is the width of a finger. Mordechai.
That is, etc. Rashi.
And one must square, etc. Although in the above Tosafot they were uncertain about this, the Rosh and Mordechai explained that what is stated that tefillin must be square refers to the batim. The Rif added that this applies even to their stitching, meaning that the stitches must be square, and the same applies to the titura itself. For if it referred specifically to their stitching, then what proof would there be from one who makes his tefillin, etc.? According to Rashi, it all refers to the batim, for he explains that with their stitching means that he should not ruin the squareness through his stitching. This follows his view there, s.v. titura, that it is of one piece of leather with the batim. But Rabbeinu Yitzchak explained as the Geonim wrote, that one takes another piece of leather for the titura together with the passageway for the strap, as written in the Sefer Mitzvos Gadol. The Rosh also wrote this; see Tosafot, Megillah 24b, s.v. with their stitching, and the Mordechai. Therefore they explained with their stitching as referring to the stitching itself. This is also the view of all the Geonim, that we require the stitching to be square, and also the titura.
