139 — Between, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. tefillin, etc.; Shitah Mekubetzet — not like one who wrote for arm tefillin length about half a fingerbreadth and width a fingerbreadth. Mordechai.

That is, etc. Rashi.

And one must square, etc. Even though those Tosafot were uncertain, the Rosh and Mordechai explain that "tefillin must be square" refers to the housings; Ra'avad added even in their stitching — meaning the stitches should be square; the titura itself too — for if only in stitching, what support from "his prayer," etc.; according to Rashi everything refers to the housings, that he explained "in their stitching" — not to ruin the square in sewing. In his view, titura is one hide with the housings; but Rabbi Yehudah explains as the Geonim: they take another hide for titura with the ma'avarta, as in Semak; so the Rosh; and Tosafot Megillah 24b, s.v. betafren, and Mordechai — therefore he explained "in their stitching" on the sewing itself; so all Geonim require square stitching and titura too.

But, etc. Squaring is said only of length and width.

He made them, etc. Orach Tzedek.
