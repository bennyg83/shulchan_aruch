The essence in Shulchan Arukh letter 1, Beit letter 1: even bedi'eved impedes; and if he left four compartments (case 42) — but the parashiyot are lekhatchilah on one parchment, as letter 1 outside so inside; and bedi'eved kosher — custom to glue as seif 47. And in Shulchan Arukh responsum: four compartments for totafot tot, etc. — will be explained there.

And what Shulchan Arukh wrote in seif 63: if he threw gold it transfers, and even though 27 that does not transfer is invalid — it is not "out of order," since he writes only by transferring and the lower remains anyway. And in Eliyahu Rabbah 5: he wrote in ink and threw gold and covered with ink — nevertheless invalid, for otherwise there is remedy for divine names, etc.; and one may say when he transfers ink over part of a letter and has not yet completed it — also like erasing. And from what Nekudat Yosef wrote, Beit Yosef brought: threw gold on divine names over ink; and Beit Yosef: from where does he take divine names? AL — from where? And divine names he takes that other letters transfer ink; and one may say Nekudat Yosef did not write from the difficulty — one may say "he wrote" refers to a Torah scroll, as Rashi Shabbat 103b s.v. ketavah — kosher for a Torah scroll, and there other letters he corrected to drag and write, and in divine names forbidden — and see here. See Levush 14, 276, 65, and Shach 6; and there is already to answer language compression there that other letters dragging helps. Also what he wrote there: if he drags and writes in ink — that is in gold script; not so throwing gold transfers and ink remains as written here. Again I saw in Eliyahu Rabbah here letter 6, 7 — he felt this, etc.; and therefore in tefillin throwing gold there is remedy, not writing. And here what is written — like erasing the Name that erases truly — not so, for lower writing remains; and it implies from here that even gold thrown not in holiness of the Name — forbidden to erase, unlike Shach 14, 276, 12, etc.; uncertain. See letter 15 from this and Magen Avraham letter 38. I will also return to what is written in letter 1 to join a parashah from other tefillin in doubt of Taz Yoreh De'ah responsum 281, 1, and Ritva 1 in name of Tosafot Menachot 32 s.v. delma — they validate out of order parashiyot not adjacent in Torah; and for them Kadesh after VeHayah Ki Yevi'acha invalid for adjacency, but after Shema and VeHayah Im Shamoah no adjacency and kosher; and so Shema after VeHayah Im Shamoah kosher, like reading backward in Kriat Shema (below siman 64). For this difference: Kadesh and VeHayah Ki Yevi'acha invalid in doubt; but Shema and VeHayah Im Shamoah — safek safek: perhaps written in order, and perhaps halachah like Tosafot that no adjacency and kosher; except that after Shulchan Arukh here and Yoreh De'ah responsum 288 did not mention Tosafot at all — plainly one should not consider safek like this; and as written in Yoreh De'ah in customs Orach Chayyim, etc.; and in siman 64 we wrote from this.
