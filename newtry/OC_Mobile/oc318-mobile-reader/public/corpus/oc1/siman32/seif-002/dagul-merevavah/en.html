According to this reason, one need be careful only regarding kaf; but in other straight letters this reason does not apply. But Beit Yosef in siman 36 wrote in the name of Rama that all straight letters must be long in measure so that if doubled they would bend. Indeed, there Beit Yosef himself wrote the reason — that it not resemble resh — and concludes that even bedi'eved it is invalid. One must say that in other straight letters it is only a lekhatchilah precaution, but in a straight kaf it is invalid even bedi'eved.
