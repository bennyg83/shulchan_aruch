[2] Of the hand first, etc. See in this siman seif 23 and siman 25 seif 6. The Bach wrote that the Tur's view is that ab initio one writes the head [tefillin] first, and thus ruled in Sefer Mitzvot Shomrim, because the head [tefillin] are holier, and thus practiced Mahari Luria. The Levush wrote: write in straight script, for it is written "This is my God and I will glorify Him" — adorn oneself before Him with mitzvot — so far. That is to say, when writing without ruling he should take care that the lines are straight and not crooked. However ex post facto if he wrote them crooked the Bach wrote we have no concern. Radbaz siman 120 wrote one should write in Ashurit script, not script like Printers'; and the Perishah wrote: write straight script — do not write one word large and one small; "straight" means beautiful.
