(s"k 2) From smoke… and also in Yoreh De'ah he wrote that ab initio… that comes from a tree — meaning the Rabbenu Yerucham wrote there siman 271, and so on: a Sefer Torah must be written with ink made from smoke of oils soaked in gallnut water; and on this Rama wrote there, and ab initio one should be careful not to make ink except from things that come from a tree — end quote. Yet smoke of oils and gallnuts are things that come from a tree; nevertheless the Rama comes to exclude being strict not to make from smoke of oils, and the main stringency is things that come from a tree — and this contradicts what Rama wrote here and the Gemara in Shabbat that implies they made ink from oil smoke. Thus the Gemara there folio 23a: Rabbi Shimon bar Zotrai taught: all oils are good for ink and olive oil is the best. Rabbi Shmuel bar Zotrai taught thus: all smokes are good for ink and smoke of olive oil is the best — end quote; nevertheless it seems oils and smokes are equal for ink:
