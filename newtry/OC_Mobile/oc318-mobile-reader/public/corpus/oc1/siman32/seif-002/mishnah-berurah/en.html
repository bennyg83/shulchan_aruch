(6) On one parchment — for it is written "and it shall be for a sign upon your hand," implying one sign, that is, one compartment; and just as it is one sign on the outside, so lekhatchilah there should be one sign on the inside, that each parshah should be on one parchment; but in shel rosh, which has four compartments, the parshiyot must be written on four parchments — and all this is only lekhatchilah, as everything will be explained below in seif 47. And know that the passage Shema must also be written specifically on four columns, each parshah on a separate column.
