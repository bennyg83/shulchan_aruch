In this order. Be'er Heitev and what he wrote in name of Avnei HaGedolah — meaning he had two parashiyot he knows he wrote before these last ones left; only he thought that since not written to join those they would be invalid to join — teaches we do not care; and even a single witness is believed to say he knows these were written first; but if he is uncertain whether before or after — such should not be in Israel to join together, God forbid, see there. And in Eliyahu Rabbah and what Be'er Heitev wrote — see Dvar Shmuel siman 382: in Leket HaKemach he wrote Darkei Shmuel is strict here — but it is not so, for Darkei Shmuel there discusses finding a parashah from the first ones invalid and writing another after it invalid; and also per Avnei HaGedolah he is strict even in his uncertainty, as written; and Machatzit HaShekel wrote Beit Yosef siman 33 brought Semag glosses that for head [tefillin] written on four parchments there is no concern for precedence — and halachah is not so. And see responsa Rashbatz who wants to hold Semak glosses and sugya delma that one does not divide between head and hand; therefore a scribe who wrote several parashiyot for tefillin shel yad in one hide and was careful to write in order, and afterward they got mixed — matter where he might take the first parashah written later than three earlier ones require genizah; and if he wrote shel rosh and the last was ruined — he writes another under it; not so if a parashah from the earlier was ruined — he must rewrite it with all parashiyot he wrote after it — so too the last ones, see there.
