At the beginning, etc. See the Mishnah Berurah, where he wrote that it is proper to say, "and all the Divine Names in it." So wrote the Taz in Yoreh Deah, siman 274, se'if 1, and the Pri Megadim cited it here. He wrote that initially even the Taz agrees that each Divine Name must be sanctified every time, even if he sanctified them at the beginning of the writing, and this helps only after the fact. It is surprising that he did not cite the Lechem Chamudos in Hilchos Sefer Torah, who disagrees and holds that even after the fact it does not help. See Birkei Yosef in Yoreh Deah, siman 274, who is stringent about this. In any event, it appears to me that if one has a doubt about a particular Name, whether he sanctified it or not, and he knows that at the beginning of the writing he said, "and all the Divine Names in it," one may be lenient. See Pri Megadim, who is uncertain whether this sanctification is biblical or rabbinic; and even according to the Devar Shmuel, cited there in Birkei Yosef, who holds that the requirement to sanctify the Divine Names for their sake is biblical, nevertheless the view of the Taz may be combined here to be lenient. So it appears to me.
