At the beginning, etc. — See Mishnah Berurah: say "and all divine names therein"; Taz Yoreh De'ah 274:1, Peri Megadim — lekhatchilah Taz agrees sanctify each time; bedi'eved suffices. Lechem Chamudos disagrees — invalid even bedi'eved; Birkhei Yosef strict. If doubt which name — knew he said at start — lenient; Peri Megadim: sanctification Torah or rabbinic — combine Taz to lenient.
