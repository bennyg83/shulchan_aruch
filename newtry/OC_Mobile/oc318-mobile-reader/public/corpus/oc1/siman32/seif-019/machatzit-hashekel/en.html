(Seif katan 19) And one need not cover. And Maharit, etc. The Taz in seif katan 10 wrote that this is specifically if it was completely severed and no lower part remained at all. But if the lower part remained and there is only a break between the upper and lower parts, then one must cover the lower part when showing it to a child, because the child will combine the lower part as well, and that does not help. See there. According to this, since we do not combine the lower part, the upper part alone must retain the measure of a letter. In Eliyah Rabbah he was uncertain about this, because it appears somewhat from the work of Rabbi Menachem Meiri that the lower part is joined if a child reads it as the letter. The Taz wrote that 'not wise' means that the child does not understand the matter, and 'not foolish' means that he at least knows and understands the letters properly.
