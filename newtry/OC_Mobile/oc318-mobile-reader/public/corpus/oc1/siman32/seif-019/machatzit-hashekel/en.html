(s"k 19) And one need not cover. And Maharit… and Taz in s"k 10 wrote specifically if it was severed entirely and no part of the lower portion remains; but if part of the lower portion remains, only with a break between upper and lower portions, then one must cover the lower portion when showing a child, for the child will join the lower portion too — and this is a dispute — see there. According to this, since we do not join the lower portion, the upper portion alone must remain the measure of a letter. In Sefer Ateret Rosh we were uncertain, for it seems somewhat from Sefer HaMeiri that we join the lower portion if a child reads it; and Taz wrote that neither-wise-nor-foolish is not called one who does not understand the matter, nor is a fool called one who in any case knows and understands letters well:
