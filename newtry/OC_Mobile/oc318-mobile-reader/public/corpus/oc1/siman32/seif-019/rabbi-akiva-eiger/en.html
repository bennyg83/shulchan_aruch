There. And so the Rama ruled. I am astonished, for if so, that everything follows the completion, even though part of the letter was not made by writing, nevertheless it is valid because he completes the letter by writing. If so, why do we invalidate nearby in seif 18, regarding a reish that he made like a dalet, where it does not suffice to scrape only the leg? Why should the result after scraping be worse than if the part of the letter that remained had come through ink spilling, which is made valid by completing the letter? This requires great study.
Seif 19. He should say verbally. This implies that it is essential, as also appears from the wording of the gloss. Above, in seif 9, however, he wrote, “and it is good to articulate with his lips.” See Yoreh Deah, beginning of simanim 271 and 274, and see Responsa Radbaz, part 2, siman 154.
