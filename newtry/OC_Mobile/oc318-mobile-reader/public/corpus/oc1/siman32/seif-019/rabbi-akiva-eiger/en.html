And I wonder: if so, that everything follows the completion of the letter, and even though part of the letter was not formed in writing, nevertheless it is kosher when one completes the letter in writing — then why did they invalidate nearby in seif 18, in the head of the reish, that he made it like a dalet, which does not suffice when one drags only the leg alone? And why should dragging be worse after dragging than if the partial letter that remained came from falling ink, which becomes kosher by completing the letter? Requires further study.

Seif 19 — "he shall say with his mouth" implies it is for impediment, as appears from the wording of the Hagahah. And above seif 9 it says, "and it is good to express with his lips." See Yoreh Deah responsa simanim 471 and 474, and responsum Radbaz part 2 siman 154.
