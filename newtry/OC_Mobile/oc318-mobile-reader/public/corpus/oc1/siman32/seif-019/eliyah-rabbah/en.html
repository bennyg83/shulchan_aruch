[19] Domestic animal, wild animal, and fowl, etc. It seems "the later is dearer," and thus wrote in the responsum of Menachem Azariah siman 37: fowl hide is preferable for tefillin over animal hide, because holy spirits dwell upon a pure fowl — therefore its blood requires covering — and for this reason wild animal hide is preferable to domestic, even though fowl is more preferable; and one may say lead's lightness [is a factor]. He also wrote that a fetus is best of all because it did not enjoy this world; and no creature goes into the air of the world that does not receive judgment, for this world was created through them — and the fetus is exempt from judgment because it did not go into the air of the world. There is no doubt that a wild animal's fetus is better than a domestic one's; and in both, if the mother was slaughtered, it is even better, for its mother's slaughter purifies it and it has no independent status relative to a fetus. But nevelah, whether by human hands or dying on its own — it implies this is harsh judgment even for a fetus; and domestic slaughtered is better than wild nevelah — so far. One may question whether hide from a slaughtered domestic animal precedes hide from a wild nevelah — perhaps it is only regarding a fetus, where slaughtered has no status, that there is a distinction between slaughtered and nevelah; and thus it seems in Baruch's statement — what is not so for an animal born into the air of the world, perhaps there is no distinction. However, from Olat Tamid it implies Menachem Azariah's intent is that slaughtered domestic precedes. Also requires investigation: hide from slaughtered domestic vs. a fetus whose mother became nevelah — which precedes? Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Tosafot that even other scrolls containing divine names may not be written on impure hide.
