Needs. In the matter of seif 16. Certainly the scribe's punishment is greater than one who feeds terefot from shechita — this is a negative commandment and causes stumbling in a positive commandment of not laying tefillin; and even if we say a negative commandment is graver than a positive act (Yevamot ch. 1) — nevertheless a blessing in vain, Responsa Tashbetz 11 — that it would be "you shall not carry God's Torah every day." And leshemah — for tefillin, not like gittin. If a minor writes — invalid certainly, as siman 39; and even an adult youth without intent, and divine names not for holiness of the Name — see Levush who extended; and see introduction to letter 32 I will explain.
