At the beginning of writing he should say with his mouth: "I am writing for the sake of the sanctity of tefillin." And besides this, every time he writes a Divine Name he must say that he writes it for the sake of the sanctity of the Name.And some say it suffices when he intends that he writes the mentions for their sake, since he expressed at the beginning of writing with his mouth — this suffices [Rosh Hilchot Tefillin and Sefer Torah, and Tur Yoreh De'ah and Orach Chayyim]. And one may be lenient b'dieved; and when he comes to doze he should not write, for then he is not writing with intention [Or Zaruah].]:
