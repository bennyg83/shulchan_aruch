At the beginning of the writing he should say with his mouth, 'I am writing for the sake of the sanctity of tefillin.' Besides this, each time he writes a Divine Name he must say that he is writing it for the sanctity of the Name. Rama: Some say it is enough that he thinks that he is writing the Divine Names for their required purpose, since he said it with his mouth at the beginning of the writing, and this is sufficient. Rosh, Hilchos Tefillin and Sefer Torah, Tur Yoreh Deah and Orach Chaim. One may be lenient after the fact. When he begins to doze, he should not write, for he is then not writing with intention. Or Zarua.
