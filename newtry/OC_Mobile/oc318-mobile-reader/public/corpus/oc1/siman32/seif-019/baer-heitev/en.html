Before he writes the name, he will look at the quill we will dip before he writes the last letter before the name and sanctify the ink with that letter, and if he needs ink before the name is finished, then he will dip the letters before the name that are wet and the name will be finished, and if he did not put a letter before the name, he will look to see if there is a letter or tag that needs ink so that he can sanctify the ink before he writes the name.
