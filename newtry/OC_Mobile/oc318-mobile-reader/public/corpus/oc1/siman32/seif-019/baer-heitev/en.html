The writing. Before he writes the Divine Name, he should examine the quill and dip it before writing the final letter preceding the Name, and sanctify the ink with that letter. If he needs ink before finishing the Name, he should dip into the letters before the Name while they are moist, and finish the Name. If he did not leave a letter before the Name, he should examine whether there is a letter or tag that needs ink so that he may sanctify the ink before writing the Name: Beis Shmuel; Magen Avraham. For the esoteric order of writing the Name, see Ateres Zekeinim and Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, klal 2, simanim 10 and 11.
