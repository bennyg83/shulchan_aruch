The writing. Before writing the Name he should examine the quill, dip before writing the last letter before the Name and sanctify the ink with that letter; if he needs ink before finishing the Name he dips in letters before the Name that are wet and finishes the Name; if he placed no letter before the Name he examines whether a letter or tag needs ink to sanctify ink before writing the Name — Beit Shmuel, Magen Avraham. Order of writing the Name follows secret — see Ateret Zekenim and Ginat Veradim chelek alef chet, klal 2, siman 10 and 11.
