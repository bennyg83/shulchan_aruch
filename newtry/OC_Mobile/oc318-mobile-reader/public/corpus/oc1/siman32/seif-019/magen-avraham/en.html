And at the beginning of writing. When he writes the Divine Name, he should examine the quill and dip it before writing the final letter before the Name. For when he dips the quill, he must sanctify the ink on the quill with one letter before writing the Name, and he should not begin immediately after dipping the quill, but must first sanctify it. If he needs ink before completing the Name, he should dip into the letters before the Name that are still moist and complete the Name. If he forgot and did not leave a letter before the Name, he should examine whether he can find a letter or crown that needs ink, in order to sanctify the ink before writing the Name; Beis Shmuel.
