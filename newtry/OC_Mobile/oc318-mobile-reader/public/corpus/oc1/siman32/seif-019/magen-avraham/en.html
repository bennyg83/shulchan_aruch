And at the beginning of writing. When he will write a Name he should examine the quill and dip the quill before writing the last letter before the Name, for when he dips the quill he must sanctify the ink on the quill with a letter alef before writing the Name; he should not begin immediately when he dipped but needs to sanctify. If he needs ink before finishing the Name, then he dips in letters before the Name that are still wet and completes the Name. If he forgot and did not leave a letter before the Name, he should see whether he finds a letter or tag that needs ink in order to sanctify with ink before writing the Name (Beit Shmuel).
