(23) With lime or with plaster — and it invalidates, for it is not called parchment without this, only diphtera. One places the hide in lime until the hair falls off by itself and not by scraping; if removed before then one may not write on it, for it is still diphtera and invalid. Some disagree and wrote that a tanner who removed hides after four days and hair has not yet gone — post factum there is no concern, for it does not depend on hair at all, since it was already placed in lime and fixed properly it is not called diphtera — see there:

(24) With his lips — and post factum verbal intent suffices:

(25) At the beginning — and moreover one need neither say it with his lips nor think they are being processed for their sake except at the beginning alone, even if processing lasts many days, for whatever one does follows his original intent. The beginning of processing is when the hides are placed into the lime, not when placed in water beforehand, for that is not yet called processing:

(26) For a Sefer Torah — for its sanctity is greater than tefillin, including two hundred maneh; nevertheless one may switch to tefillin and mezuzot although their sanctity is lighter than it. But for mundane matters it is forbidden to switch alone; if he stipulated explicitly at the beginning of their processing that switching would be permitted even for mundane matters, then it is permitted. The proper practice is that scribes lechatchilah, when placing hides in lime, say: I place these hides in lime for the sanctity of a Sefer Torah, and I stipulate that when I wish I may switch them for any purpose — and not as scribes customarily say "for a Sefer Torah or tefillin or mezuzot or other optional matter," for then the latter authorities are uncertain if it helps; see Biur Halachah:

(27) Invalid — and does not help except for a mezuzah alone, whose sanctity is lighter than tefillin. If processed for tefillin it helps even for a mezuzah, not for a Sefer Torah. If he processed the hide for straps, it does not help to write parshiyot on it if he later makes parchment from them, for their sanctity is greater; and even to make housings from them, some say also invalid, for their sanctity is greater than a strap [Peri Megadim; see there more of what he wrote on this]:
