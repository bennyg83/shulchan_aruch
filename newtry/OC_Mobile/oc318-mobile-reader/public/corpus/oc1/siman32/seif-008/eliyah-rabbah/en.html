[8] Mukaf gevil, etc. Specifically when there is only the form of the letter itself and when anything is missing from it its form is lost and there is no letter at all — that case is discussed; but if it has more than the form of a letter to the extent that if you scrape part of it the letter remains in its form — if it stuck to its head or middle it is invalid, but if stuck at its end it is kosher — Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Radak; and see seif 25. If connected from the tag of one letter to its neighbor and the bodies of the letters are each recognizable on their own, one should be stringent where possible; and Magen Avraham was stringent also in the first ruling.
