(Note 24) And likewise the reverse — Maharam Alshekar, "and if he wrote," etc. One who examines Maharam Alshekar's words will see that he addresses there adding to the form of the letter, such as making a resh like a dalet and a kaf like a bet; and one could say there is no distinction between this and scraping, for just as scraping does not help, so addition does not help. And according to his reason he holds that whenever the letter is in a form different from what is required, it is like a mere drop of ink and one must scrape the whole thing from the beginning; and therefore he wrote that this is similar to a drop of ink that fell like half a letter and he completed it through writing — invalid — and likewise here. And thus Keneset HaGedolah copied, and it seems Magen Avraham wrote thus per the phrasing with which Keneset HaGedolah concluded, namely: "Nevertheless he raised that we do not differ, and even if he made a dalet like a resh and a bet like a kaf it does not help by scraping" — end quote. The examiner will see there is a scribal error, for there he brings initially Maharam Alshekar's words that perhaps one may distinguish between scraping and adding, and therefore he wrote that he raised there is no distinction, and even if he made a dalet like a resh, etc., addition does not help like scraping (and the word "by scraping" is a scribal error and should read "like scraping"). And if so, what did Magen Avraham say on Maharam Alshekar's words "and if he wrote a dalet," etc., and likewise Rivash, etc.? The explanation is: Maharam Alshekar addresses these words of Rivash and wrote that he does not know a distinction between scraping and adding — see there. And what Magen Avraham stated, "and likewise from bet to kaf" — it seems it should read "and likewise regarding bet to kaf," meaning he wrote a bet, for why would kaf have a remedy to add more ink to make corners like a bet. But what he said above, "and likewise the reverse," implying that one makes from kaf a bet through scraping — that is not Maharam Alshekar's intent.
