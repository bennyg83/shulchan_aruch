34) Seif 8. The parchment must be processed, etc. He should leave the hide in lime until the hair falls off by itself; if he removes it before then, he should not write on it, for it is diftera. Magen Avraham, seif katan 9; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 17; GraZ, letter 10; Keset HaSofer, siman 12, letter 6; Meleches Shamayim, klal 2, letter 2, in Chochmah. See also what is written there, klal 3, inquiry 3.
35) There. It must be processed for its sake, etc. See our words in Yoreh De'ah, siman 271, where we explained all the matters that require processing.
36) There. Or for the sake of a Sefer Torah, etc. If one began writing it for the sake of a Sefer Torah and it became invalid there, it is forbidden to write tefillin or mezuzos on the remainder. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 16, inferred this from the Levush; Keset HaSofer, siman 25, letter 2. Even if, at the time of processing, he also mentioned tefillin and mezuzos, it is also forbidden, according to Lishkas HaSofer there. Some permit writing tefillin on it, but not a mezuzah; Noda BiYehuda, second edition, Yoreh De'ah, siman 174, cited by Meleches Shamayim, klal 21, letter 5, in Chochmah. The Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 10, permits even for a mezuzah; see there. Since this is a dispute, one should not do so initially. After the fact, however, if he already wrote it, one should not invalidate it. Even according to those who prohibit, it is possible to say that they prohibited only initially, but after the fact, if it was already written, they do not invalidate it.
