The hide must be processed for its sake, and he should declare this with his lips; if he intended it for its sake that also suffices — but only if a Jew himself processes it; if a non-Jew processes it, it does not help until a Jew declares with his lips that he processes it for its sake. When he places the hides in water he should say: For the sanctity of Israel, for the sake of tefillin, mezuzot, and amulets, and for hide for housings for tefillin — I place these hides in water; then it is fit to write on them whatever he wishes (see siman 9).
