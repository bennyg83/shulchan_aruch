It must be processed for its sake, and it is proper for him to articulate this with his lips. If he merely had in mind that it be processed for its sake, that is also effective, but only where a Jew himself processes it. If a non-Jew processes it, intent alone is ineffective unless the Jew expressly states that it is being processed for its sake. Thus, when placing the hides into the water, he should say: “For the sanctity of Israel, for tefillin, mezuzos, and amulets, and for leather for the housings of tefillin, I place these hides into the water.” It is then fit for him to write upon it whatever he wishes. See seif katan 9.
