(Seif katan 8) And if he does not know, etc. See Yam Shel Shlomo, etc., that according to the law of the Gemara it is permitted to eat meat after cheese, and one need not wait six hours. Rather, Maharam was stringent for himself because one time he found cheese between his teeth a long time after eating cheese; therefore he accepted this stringency upon himself. But another person may not be stringent to wait six hours after eating cheese. See what the Shach wrote about this in Yoreh De'ah siman 89 seif katan 17.
