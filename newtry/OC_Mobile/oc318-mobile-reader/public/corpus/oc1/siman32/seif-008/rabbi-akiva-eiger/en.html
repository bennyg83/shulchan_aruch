Seif 8. And it is good to articulate with his lips. This implies that initially one should be concerned for the doubt of Rabbeinu Baruch, perhaps he must say verbally that he is doing this for its required purpose, but after the fact not. This is difficult for me, for in seif 19 in the gloss it says, “and some say that it suffices,” etc., and one may be lenient after the fact, implying that after the fact the thought that he writes the Divine Names for their required purpose helps, since at the beginning of the writing he said that he writes for the sanctity of tefillin, but without that, thought does not help. Also in Yoreh Deah regarding a sefer Torah the Mechaber used the expression, “and he should say with his mouth,” and did not use the wording “and it is good,” implying that by law he must articulate it with his lips. This requires study.
