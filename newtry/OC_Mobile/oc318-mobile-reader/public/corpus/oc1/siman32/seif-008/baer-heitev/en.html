Sefer Torah. In the responsum of Avodat HaGerushoni, siman 65, he forbids writing tefillin on parchment that was processed for the sake of a Sefer Torah unless one stipulates at the outset that if he later wishes to change it to lesser holiness he has permission in his hand le-shemah. See Yad Aharon for what he challenged against him. See in the responsum of Tzemach Tzedek, siman 81, whether the condition helps for one who processes hide for tefillin straps to use what remains of the hide for mundane use—and his view is in that matter of straps it is permitted le-shemah.
