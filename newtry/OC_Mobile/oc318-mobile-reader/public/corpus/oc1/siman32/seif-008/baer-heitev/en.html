With gallnuts. He should leave the hide in lime until the hair falls out by itself, but if he removes it before then he should not write upon it, for it is considered diftera. Beis Shmuel; Magen Avraham.
A Sefer Torah. In Responsa Avodas HaGershuni, siman 65, he forbids writing tefillin on parchment that was processed for the sake of a Sefer Torah, unless he stipulated at the outset that if he later wished to change it to a lesser sanctity, he would have permission to do so; see there. See also Yad Aharon, who challenges him. See Responsa Tzemach Tzedek, siman 81, whether such a condition is effective when processing hide for tefillin straps, so that what remains of the hide may be used for ordinary use; his view there is that regarding straps it is permitted; see there. See also Yoreh Deah, siman 271, s.k. 3, where I wrote in the name of the Tur that preparing the parchment and scoring lines need not be done for their sake.
