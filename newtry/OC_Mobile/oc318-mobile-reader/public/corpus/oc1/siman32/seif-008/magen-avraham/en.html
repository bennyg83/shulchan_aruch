The parchment must, etc. One leaves the hide in lime until the hair falls by itself; if one removes it before then one should not write on it, for it is diphtera [Beit Shmuel].

Or for a Torah scroll. So Beit Yosef: if he processed for a Torah scroll it helps to write tefillin in it, for the sanctity of a Torah scroll is stricter than tefillin's sanctity, end quote. Levush wrote: although it is forbidden to change from stricter to lighter sanctity, as siman 42 — here it is different, for it is only designation, and one may change it to lighter sanctity; but for mundane matter it is forbidden to change it, end quote. So too in responsum siman 42, who wrote it is permitted to make new shel rosh tefillin for shel yad even though they were written for shel rosh.
