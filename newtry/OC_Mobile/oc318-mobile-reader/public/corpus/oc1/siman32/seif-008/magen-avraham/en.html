The parchment must, etc. He should leave the hide in lime until the hair falls off by itself. If he removes it before then, he should not write on it, for it is considered diftera; Beis Shmuel.
Or for the sake of a Sefer Torah. This is the language of Beis Yosef: If he processed it for the sake of a Sefer Torah, it is effective for writing tefillin on it, because the sanctity of a Sefer Torah is more severe than the sanctity of tefillin, end quote. The Levush wrote: although it is forbidden to change from a more severe sanctity to a lighter sanctity, as written in siman 42, here it is different, for it is only designation, and it is permitted to change it to a lighter sanctity. But it is forbidden to change it for mundane use, end quote. This is also implied at the beginning of siman 42, where he wrote that it is permitted to make new tefillin of the head into tefillin of the hand, even though they were written for the sake of the head.
