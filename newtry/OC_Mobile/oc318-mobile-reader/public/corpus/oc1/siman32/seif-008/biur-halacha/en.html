**Parchment is required, etc.** — Processing is a halachah leMoshe miSinai: without it there is only "hide," not gvil, klaf, or duchsustos. Peri Megadim. All sections here on processing apply to tefillin parshiyot and writing books and mezuzot [except Rambam on mezuzah as Yoreh Deah 288:65]; house leather explained below se'if 37.

**And it must be, etc., leshemah** — Without this they are invalid. If he cannot obtain other tefillin except these on skin processed not leshemah — for Torah scroll he might bless in public, but for tefillin he wears without blessing (see Peri Megadim). Peri Megadim decided plainly processing leshemah is rabbinic, proof from Rambam's responsum cited in many places Tefillin 1:11 — practical difference if skin exchanged not leshemah: safek derabbanan lekula; from Rabbi Akiva Eiger 2 it seems biblical; I saw one write on behalf of Magen Giborim that strict poskim on processing leshemah hold it biblical; nevertheless in pressure be lenient on their doubts, for many rishonim hold processing not required [Mishkanot Yaakov Orach Chaim 42; Perach MA ch. 2 siman 101 in Birchei Yosef Yoreh Deah 271]. Know: unstated processing is as not leshemah — Perach MA siman 45, as Tosafot Gitin 45b s.v. processing leshemah and Beit Aharon. New issue today: most writing on klaf is only Torah scroll — he holds biblical; I saw same in Magen Giborim; nevertheless in pressure lenient; unstated processing as not leshemah — requires study.

**At the beginning of processing** — If only the end was leshemah, see below se'if 59: Taz Yoreh Deah 271:63 holds it helps; Perach ch. 1 siman 45 disagrees — safek whether "its end proves its beginning"; Gra siman 11 wrote likewise. Still do not be strict bedieved with many lenient, including Maharam Benet on Mordechai; evidence Taz brings from geonim returning fully processed-not-leshemah klaf to stretching and lime leshemah — a fortiori here [hagahah: Chavot Yair explains geonim permit returning skin not fully processed — proves our case; see Gra's Ketzot to Shulchan Aruch; duka in geonim is actual klaf per Tur and Shach — cannot reject Tur deals with klaf not fixed by salting alone; Tur would not permit without ability even under pressure; Tur and Shulchan Aruch omitted geonim's first ruling on salting alone — they disagree; reason: halachah leMoshe miSinai per Rambam Tefillin 1 — do not lighten]. Perhaps Gra agrees on safek oral declaration only — they did not be strict lechatchilah; Tosafot wrote good to declare orally; Peri Megadim says same here, especially if processing leshemah is derabbanan. Conclusion: if he forgot to put hides in lime leshemah and remembered during processing while lime remains — remove and replace leshemah — clear in Az Nafshi. If he intends leshemah in the processing act itself; if he intends only when fixing klaf afterward (scraping with pim stone, etc.) or when ruling Torah scroll or blackening straps alone — certainly does not help [Taz Yoreh Deah there, Peri Megadim, later agreement]. Know: we hold no leshemah needed even for mezuzah outline which is halachah leMoshe miSinai; some later authorities strict on ruling [Beit Chadash on mezuzah, Bnei Yonah even Torah scroll] bedieved — heed lechatchilah; see Peri Megadim Mishbetzot Zahav s.k. dalet. Peri Megadim: per Beit Chadash ruling does not help Torah scroll for mezuzah; as we rule they are equal it helps; Beit Chadash certainly does not accept tefillin/mezuzah ruling even leshemah — we ruled not strict bedieved; explicit non-leshemah ruling may be worse.

**Or for Torah scroll** — See Mishnah Berurah: not as scribes practice (see there). Reason: we hold no bererah biblically; Peri Megadim holds processing leshemah derabbanan — lighten bedieved. Practically requires study.
