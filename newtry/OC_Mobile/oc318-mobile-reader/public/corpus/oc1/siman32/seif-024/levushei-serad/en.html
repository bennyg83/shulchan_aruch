There. A thin scratch. It is possible that he does not disagree with the Bach, for the Bach speaks where the break is recognizable and visible, as is explicit in the Bach. But regarding a thin scratch where the break is not visible, he agrees. Nevertheless, even with a thin scratch he required that it not pass through to the parchment, for if the parchment was cut in the middle of the letter, even if the break is not recognizable at all, it is invalid because it is not surrounded by blank parchment if that cut existed before the writing.
