See the holy work Kaf HaChaim there, where I wrote that even if one writes with his left hand and does all his work with his left hand, and he wrote tefillin with his left hand, which according to the law is permitted, nevertheless a person of spiritual sensitivity should be concerned for himself and should buy tefillin only from one who writes with the right hand and also does all his work with the right hand; see there.
