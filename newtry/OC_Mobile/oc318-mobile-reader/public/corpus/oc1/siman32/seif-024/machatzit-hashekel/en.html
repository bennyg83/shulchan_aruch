(s"k 24) In bet… one may fix them with a quill, for he makes the letter through writing. All this when he did not write more; but if he already wrote more and fixes the letter before it, they are out of order:

And likewise from bet to kaf, and likewise from kaf to bet, or from dalet to resh — he should make with a quill the leg of the dalet until its head is round like a resh:

On Shulchan Aruch se'if 18 — reish made like dalet, etc. — therefore one must drag both, wrote Peri Chadash siman 125 s.k. 5, per the author there to validate when an ink drop fell and he completed the letter by writing — valid; as Magen Avraham s.k. 13 above. Then one need not drag both — suffice to drag part of roof and part of leg and complete the letter by writing (see there).
