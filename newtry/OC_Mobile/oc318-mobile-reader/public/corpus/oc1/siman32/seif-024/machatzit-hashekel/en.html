(s.k. 24) Beis, etc. It is permitted to repair them with a quill, for he makes the letter through writing. This applies as long as he has not written further, for if he has already written further and then repairs the letter before it, it is considered out of order.
The same applies from a beis to a kaf, and the same applies from a kaf to a beis, or from a dalet to a reish, where he makes the leg of the dalet with a quill until its top becomes rounded like a reish.
However, regarding what is written in Shulchan Aruch, seif 18: a reish that he made like a dalet, etc. Therefore he must scrape both of them. The Pri Chadash, siman 125, s.k. 5, writes that according to what the Mechaber wrote there, that when a drop of ink fell and he completed the letter through writing it is valid, as stated above in Magen Avraham, s.k. 13, if so, what he wrote that one must scrape both is not precise. Rather, it is enough that he scrape part of the roof and part of the leg, and afterward complete the letter through writing. See there.
