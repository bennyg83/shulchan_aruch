Se'if 24, "it is permitted," etc. Per the one who says 2 [amudim]; and Rav said the halachah: one hangs, etc.; and Rabbah in Bach, etc.; and according to Tosafos there, hanging is inferior to erasing — Rav himself holds in Yerushalmi that erasing, etc.; if so, dragging and erasing are permitted for all the amoraim. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 276, se'if 6–7.

Se'if 24, "and one should not erase," etc. Tosafos there, s.v. "R' Yitzchak," etc. — it is not the same generation; but lekhatchilah certainly all agree one fulfills.
