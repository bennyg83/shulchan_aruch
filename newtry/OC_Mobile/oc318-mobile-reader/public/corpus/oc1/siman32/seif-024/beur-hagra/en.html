Seif 24, it is permitted, etc. 30b, and Rav said: the halachah is that one may suspend, etc.; and Rabbah bar bar Chanah, etc. This follows the explanation of Tosafos there, that suspension is worse than erasure, for Rav himself holds in the Yerushalmi that erasure, etc. If so, scraping and erasing are permitted according to all the amoraim. See Yoreh Deah, siman 276, seifim 6 and 7.
And he should not erase, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. Rabbi Yehudah, etc.; it is not so beautified. Initially, one should certainly fulfill the words of all opinions.
