Even if its whole interior was pierced, etc. In chapter HaKometz: pierced interior of heh — kosher; its thigh — invalid. Rashi: its interior — inner leg; its thigh — right leg. Another explanation: its interior — gevil of the part within — end quote; and behold for each language leniency and stringency: for the first language — leniency in heh's inner leg that even if something remains — kosher; and stringency — not from gevil within at all — we rely on Yerushalmi Tur brings that also from inside must be surrounded by gevil. Second language — stringency in inner leg equaling right leg — invalid if full small letter not left; and leniency — our Talmud contradicts Yerushalmi and holds from inside need not gevil surround — and Rosh agreed to first explanation as Shulchan Arukh wrote; and Rama in Shulchan Arukh HaRav brings Rama holds second explanation — therefore per Rama we do not heed Yerushalmi requiring gevil wrap inside; and perhaps Rama rules stringency of both explanations we mentioned — and so correct; except Tur concludes: therefore if whole interior pierced — invalid — meaning specifically whole interior, not part; reason: all gevil within the letter is one part — therefore even if gevil inside split on one side, what remains on the other side protects; similar if part split outside on one side and remainder on that side of the body — we do not find invalidity in this.
