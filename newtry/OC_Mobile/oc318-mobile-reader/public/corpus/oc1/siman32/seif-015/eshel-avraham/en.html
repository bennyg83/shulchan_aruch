Parchment. And see Shut Batei Kohen, Beit Din — she'elah 12, who concludes a split in a letter is not invalidating so long as its form is not lost, since the Ramban's language is so precise; and so is explicit in Tur; and Maharik's doubts do not remove us from the plain teaching of these great teachers. And see Yoreh Deah siman 280 seif 1. That which Maharik was concerned per Yerushalmi — our Talmud concludes we do not require mukaf gevil, only that letter not touch letter — Yoreh Deah siman 23: a hole in its interior is different, for its interior is considered more part of its body than its surroundings, which are from the body of the hide.

Invalid. Yods whose lower thorns are not found on the left — per Rashi kosher after the fact; per Rabbeinu Tam invalid; and similarly Mordechai; and R' Yitzchak bar Baruch wrote that Rashi concedes to Rabbeinu Tam here; in any case it is a Torah-level doubt. Nevertheless the gloss on Tur Yoreh Deah: do not invalidate them, but he still has a remedy; do not heed Agur that this is out of order — see Yad Aharon siman 36.
