If, after it was written, a hole was made inside the Hei or the closed Mem, it is valid, even if its entire inside was pierced so that the hole fills the whole hollow. But the Yerushalmi implies that even inside it must be surrounded by parchment. If the inner leg of the Hei was pierced, even if only the smallest amount of it remains, it is valid according to the Rosh. Rama: But the other poskim require the measure of a small letter, and this is the halacha. Beis Yosef. If the right leg was pierced, if the measure of a small letter remains of it, it is valid; if not, it is invalid.
