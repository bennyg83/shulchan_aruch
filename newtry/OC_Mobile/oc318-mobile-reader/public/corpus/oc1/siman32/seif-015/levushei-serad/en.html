(Magen Avraham s.k. 15) Against the sun. He disputes Taz s.k. 7; it seems if the hole was sealed by ink — even though against the sun the place of the hole is visible because the parchment is pierced there and the sun's rays shine through the ink — Taz would admit it is valid. Taz deals only where the ink also fell out of the hole, but because of the hole's smallness it is not visible except against the sun.
