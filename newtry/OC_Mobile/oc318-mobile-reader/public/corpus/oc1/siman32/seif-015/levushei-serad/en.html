Magen Avraham, seif katan 15. Against the sun. He disagrees with the Taz, seif katan 7. It appears to me that if the hole was sealed by the ink, then even though, when held against the sun, the place of the hole is visible because the parchment is pierced there and the rays of the sun shine through the ink, the Taz agrees that it is valid. The Taz speaks only where the ink also fell away from the hole, but because the hole is so small it is not recognizable except against the sun.
