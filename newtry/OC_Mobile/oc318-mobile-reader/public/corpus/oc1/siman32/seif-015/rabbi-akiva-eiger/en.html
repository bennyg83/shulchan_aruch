Magen Avraham, seif katan 21. Even without a hole, Bach. The Magen Avraham was brief here, for regarding what the Shulchan Aruch discusses here, the requirement of surrounding parchment, it is obvious that if it was severed and the upper part contains the measure of a letter, even if there was a break from the beginning of the writing, it is valid. The Bach also wrote explicitly that if the break is after the lower line, the upper part alone is valid. Rather, the Bach’s words address a case where there is no measure of a letter except by joining the lower part to the upper part through the reading of a child, who combines them, which is valid according to the Meiri cited in the Beit Yosef. In this the Bach distinguishes that nevertheless we require that it not have been severed from the beginning of the writing, for then the parts are not combined. See there.
