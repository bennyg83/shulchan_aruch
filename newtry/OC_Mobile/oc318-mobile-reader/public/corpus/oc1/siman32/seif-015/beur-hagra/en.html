Se'if 15, "if," etc. Rambam; and per the second wording in Rashi; and Sefer HaTerumah writes that this is the main point — that its interior is hollowed like the hollow of forms; and so in Yerushalmi, as written below. But the first wording is not clear to us, for it says "right leg" or "left."

Se'if 15, "that the hole," etc. So explained all the commentators: he speaks anonymously, and they disagree with Yerushalmi.

Se'if 15, "but in Yerushalmi," etc. Ashiyan said: if one pierced a hole in the middle of the letter, if parchment surrounded it on all sides it is valid; and they told him it is invalid.

Se'if 15, "pierced," etc. For Rosh writes that the first wording is primary; the second wording is difficult — why did he mention aleph more than other letters that have an interior? But Sefer HaTerumah writes that the second wording is primary; its difficulty may be answered that he mentioned it incidentally, meaning the right leg. This is what he meant by "but other," etc.
