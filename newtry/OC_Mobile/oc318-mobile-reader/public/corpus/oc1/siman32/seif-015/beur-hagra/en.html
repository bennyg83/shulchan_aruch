Seif 15, if, etc. The Rambam, and according to the second explanation in Rashi. The Sefer HaTerumah wrote that this is primary, for its interior means its hollow, like engraving out the interior. So too in the Yerushalmi, as written below. But the first explanation does not appear correct, for what difference is there between the right leg and the left leg?
That the hole, etc. So explained all the commentators, that he stated it without qualification, and they disagree with the Yerushalmi.
But in the Yerushalmi, etc. Ashyan said: If a hole was made in the middle of the bayis, if gevil surrounds it on all sides it is valid, and if not, it is invalid.
If it was pierced, etc. For the Rosh wrote that the first explanation is primary, since according to the second explanation it is difficult why the first letter was taken more than the other letters that have an interior. But the Sefer HaTerumah wrote that the second explanation is primary, and his difficulty can be answered that it mentioned it incidentally, because he means the right leg. This is what is meant by: but the other, etc.
