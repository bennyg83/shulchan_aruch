There, if he wrote even one letter with other kinds of colors, etc., they are invalid. This implies even if he covers it with ink. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 5; Erech HaShulchan, letter 1; Yeshuos Yaakov, letter 4. See below at the end of letter 141. The same applies if he wrote with ink but threw gold or other types of colors upon it; covering it with ink does not help. Eliyah Rabbah there; Yeshuos Yaakov there; and so it also appears from Olas Tamid, letter 5.
