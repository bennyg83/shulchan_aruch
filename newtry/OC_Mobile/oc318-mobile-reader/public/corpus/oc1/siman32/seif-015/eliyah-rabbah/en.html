[15] And it is good to express thus with his lips, etc. It implies that by law intent alone suffices, and thus wrote in the responsum of Menachem Azariah siman 94. However, when Samaritans process [parchment] with Jewish intent it does not suffice until a Jew expresses it with his lips. The Bach wrote in siman 271 that tanning the parchment and ruling do not require special intent.
