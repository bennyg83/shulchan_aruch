And it is good to express this with his lips, etc. This implies that by strict law thought alone suffices, and so wrote Responsa Menachem Azaryah, siman 94. However, when non-Jews process it with the intent of a Jew, it does not suffice unless the Jew expresses it with his lips. The Bach, siman 271, wrote that preparing the parchment and scoring do not require being done for their sake.
