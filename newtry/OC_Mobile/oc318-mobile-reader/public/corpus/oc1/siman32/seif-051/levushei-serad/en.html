But it is impossible to make a closed passage that will be valid according to everyone, for one cannot write at the end of the Shema column and at the beginning of VeHayah Ki, since each passage is written on its own separate column. If he leaves a blank line, it will be open according to Rambam. Nevertheless, it would have been possible to leave part of a line blank and begin in the middle of the second line, which would be closed according to everyone; however, this is not the custom.
