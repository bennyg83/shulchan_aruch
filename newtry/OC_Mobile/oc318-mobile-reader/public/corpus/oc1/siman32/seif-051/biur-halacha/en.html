He shall sew, etc. — I heard in the name of one of the great authorities that he warned not to glue the titura with glue before sewing, for glue is considered joining like sewing, as written in se'if 47 in the Hagahah and in Beit Yosef there; see there. If so, it is possible that sewing afterward is not considered anything, and the halachah leMoshe miSinai was stated only regarding sewing and not regarding gluing — requires study in this.
