[51] A little open space, etc. Knesset HaGedolah wrote: it appears the Rabbi z"l learned from Rivash's responsum siman 286 — and it is puzzling, for Rivash z"l wrote that after the fact it is not invalid, but not that one should leave open space ab initio; and similarly wondered Sefer Zichronot on Rama, until here. And so ruled Magen Avraham; and so I saw in Lechem Chamudot — this is his wording: "I do not know his source for this"; also in Hilchot Sefer Torah siman 274 Rama did not write thus, only that if he left such it is kosher, until here. I was privileged with Baruch She'amar and found he wrote explicitly like Rama's words — and there is no doubt Rama took it from him; and from Hilchot Sefer Torah there is no difficulty, for there he speaks of if he left much empty space — whatever did not leave the measure of a parsha is kosher, but a little certainly must be left between verse and verse, as Shach wrote there. Also on the law between line and line Knesset HaGedolah wrote there: "I did not see poskim who mention this." I saw in Baruch She'amar there, and there is no doubt that book was not in the hands of the great ones mentioned above; and although I wrote above in Maharil's name that one need not leave between line and line — he did not write there except for those who practice thus. This is his wording: "And if they do not practice between line and line, perhaps in a Torah scroll it is for ornament," etc. — until here. But for practical halacha it appears he also holds one must leave space; however after the fact one does not invalidate in any of this.
