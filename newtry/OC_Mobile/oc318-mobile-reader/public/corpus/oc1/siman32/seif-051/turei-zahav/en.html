And pass the sewing thread. It is difficult why this was written in seif 47 since in all tefillin we say so. There is indeed a dispute among poskim in what the Talmud says that thread or mashicha is required between them — whether it refers specifically to writing on one klaf or even on four klafim. It appears the Tur and Shulchan Aruch hold that in truth the Talmud refers to one klaf, and then it is invalid without thread between them — for the Talmud seems to speak there lechatchilah; nevertheless they were concerned to be strict also with four klafim lechatchilah. Therefore they wrote above "provided..." which teaches that even bedieved it is meakevet; here they used lechatchilah language. But it is difficult on Rama who wrote afterward "however if he made only ten stitches..." that it is kosher bedieved — he should have said the same regarding the sewing thread between the bays.

They shall be with one thread. In Beit Yosef in the name of R. Yitzchak ibn Ghayyat he concludes on this: the thread shall not break, and if broken they are invalid — his intent is clear: he does not come to exclude if one re-ties at the break, but that they be contiguous together without a break between them.
