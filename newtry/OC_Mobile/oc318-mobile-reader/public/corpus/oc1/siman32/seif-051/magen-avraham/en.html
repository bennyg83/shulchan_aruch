He shall sew. Mateh Ephraim siman 38 wrote: if the sewing is not inside the bay but only inside the ma'abarta it is invalid, end quote. But in Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh, Rashi, and Mordechai it implies sewing inside the bay is not required — only near the bay, see there.

With one thread. If the thread broke at the time of making, they are invalid, for it must be one thread (Beit Yosef in the name of Ri). Bach wrote: specifically if it broke in the thread it is invalid, for it is evident the thread will break again; but if the thread is short one may finish the sewing with another thread l'chatchila — and so is the custom, end quote.
