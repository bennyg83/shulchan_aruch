He should sew. Menahem Azaryah, siman 38, wrote: if the stitching is not within the housing but only within the passageway, it is invalid. But in the Beis Yosef, in the name of the Rosh, Rashi, and Mordechai, it implies that we do not require stitching within the housing, only close to the housing; see there.
With one thread. If the thread broke while making it, they are invalid, for we require one thread, as stated by the Beis Yosef in the name of the Riaz. The Bach wrote that this is only if it broke because of a defective thread, for then it is evident that the thread will break again; but if the thread is short, he may initially finish the stitching with another thread, and this is the custom.
