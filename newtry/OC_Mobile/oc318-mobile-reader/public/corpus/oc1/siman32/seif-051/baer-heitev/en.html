Side. Reason for twelve stitches corresponding to tribes of Israel; if he made fourteen corresponding to Menashe and Ephraim — good; see Orach Tzedek; Ari, of blessed memory, wrote per the secret specifically twelve. Rama from Pano siman 38 wrote: if stitching is not inside the compartment but only in the passage — invalid; but Beit Yosef in the name of Rosh, Rashi, and Mordechai implies stitching need not be inside the compartment, only near it — see there, Magen Avraham.

With one thread. If the thread broke during work they are invalid, for one thread is required — Beit Yosef in the name of Riaz. Taz wrote this does not exclude if he retied at the break, only that they must be joined and not broken between. Beit Chadash wrote: specifically if the thread broke it is invalid, evident the thread broke; but if the thread is short he may finish the stitch with another thread initially — so is the custom.
