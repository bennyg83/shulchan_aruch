Side. The reason for twelve stitches is corresponding to the tribes of Israel; and if one made fourteen corresponding to Menashe and Ephraim, it is good. See Olas Tamid. The Ari of blessed memory wrote, according to the secret teaching, specifically twelve. The Rama of Fano, siman 38, wrote that if the sewing is not within the compartment but only within the passageway, it is invalid. But in the Beit Yosef in the name of the Rosh, Rashi, and Mordechai, it implies that the sewing need not be within the compartment, only adjacent to the compartment. See there and Magen Avraham.
With one thread. If the thread snapped while it was being made, they are invalid, for we require that it be one thread. Beit Yosef in the name of the Re'em. The Taz wrote that this does not come to exclude a case where he went back and tied it at the place of the break, but only requires that it be joined together and not separated between them. The Bach wrote that specifically if the thread snapped it is invalid, because it is recognizable that the thread broke; but if the thread is short, he may complete the sewing with another thread even ab initio, and this is the practice.
