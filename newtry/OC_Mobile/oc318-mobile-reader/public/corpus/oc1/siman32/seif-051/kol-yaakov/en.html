If the hole does not fill the entire hollow, even though the hole is immediately adjacent to the letter and no blank parchment at all remains between the letter and the hole inside it, nevertheless it is considered surrounded by blank parchment even at the place of the hole, by means of the blank parchment inside it on the other side of the hole. Taz, end of seif katan 8; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 22; GraZ, letter 19; Noda BiYehuda, first edition, Yoreh De'ah, siman 75. Some say that blank parchment on one side does not help for the other side, and we require the letter to be surrounded by blank parchment on all its sides; whenever the hole reaches the thickness of the letter, it is invalid according to this view. Maamar Mordechai, letter 13. So wrote Nahar Shalom, letter 15; Keset HaSofer, inquiry 4, s.v. hen. See below, letter 69.
