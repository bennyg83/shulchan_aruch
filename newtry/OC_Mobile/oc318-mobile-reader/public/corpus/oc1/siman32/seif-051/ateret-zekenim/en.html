He should sew, etc. — twelve stitches are required; however, if he made only ten stitches it is valid, for when you remove the tribe of Judah, which is malchut, and the tribe of priesthood, ten remain (in the name of Rava). Some say if he made fourteen stitches corresponding to the addition of Menashe and Ephraim, that is good (Hagahot Maimoniyot). After making the knot like a yod on arm tefillin, one also cuts at the end of the straps by the knot so it appears like the form of a yod (Tur).
