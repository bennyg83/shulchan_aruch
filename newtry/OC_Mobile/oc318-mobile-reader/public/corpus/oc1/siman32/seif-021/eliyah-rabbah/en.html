[21] Pierced within it, etc. From what the Levush wrote — ab initio one should not write thus in place of a hole — it implies that if he transgressed and did so it is kosher. And from the conclusion where it says "but if after it was written it was pierced," etc., it implies that if it was pierced before it was written and he transgressed and wrote, it is invalid. The reason may also be as above, because at the time of writing it was not whole; also it compares to the end of seif 16, and thus it seems in the Bach seif 12.
