He must write complete script so that not even the tip of a yod is missing — in the chapter HaKometz, Rashi explains "the tip of a yod" as the right leg of the yod, and the reason is that the left head of the yod must bend downward as stated in HaKometz: why is the head of a yod bent? In Pesikta it is taught that the world to come was created with a yod, which has one point below — a hint to the dead who descend to Gehinnom — and one above — a hint that they will ascend in the future; so too here.
