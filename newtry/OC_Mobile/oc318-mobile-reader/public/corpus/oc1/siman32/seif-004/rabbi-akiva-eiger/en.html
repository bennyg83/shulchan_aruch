Magen Avraham, note 5: it is impossible to find other [tefillin]. I did not find it thus there. Rather Ram"a there explicitly holds the words of Sefer HaTerumah — if one wrote with the left hand it is invalid, since for purposes of Shabbat it is like the back of the hand — and he concluded there "and if they said parashat HaBoneh, there is no manner of writing with the left hand, kal va-chomer there is no manner of writing with the mouth," end quote Ram"a — so he learned this from the law of writing with the left hand. And since we rule that with writing on the left, if there are no other tefillin one places them without a berachah — so too with writing with the mouth [one may say similarly].
