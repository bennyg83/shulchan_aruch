[4] Like wood shavings, etc. And Olat Tamid wrote: I did not know what he wanted to teach us, for water with shavings does not cause [ink] not to erase — only kankantum causes this — so far; and he did not look at Beit Yosef who brought the view of Tosafot and Semak that when it has shavings it is not ink and is invalid; and the Tur in Yoreh De'ah siman 271 wrote so in their name. Therefore he wrote that even when it has shavings it is kosher — the halacha is not like them — rather any black color is kosher. And for practical law it seems in the Shulchan Aruch in this siman that ab initio it is kosher with any ink even if not made from smoke; and thus it seems in Beit Yosef; and see in Yoreh De'ah siman 271 in Shulchan Aruch and in the Rama's words — and this requires investigation.
