Gall water, etc. Olas Tamid wrote: I do not know what he seeks to teach us, for gall water does not cause it not to be erased; only copper sulfate causes that, end quote. But he did not look at Beis Yosef, who brought the view of Tosafos and Semak that when it contains gall-nuts it is not considered ink and is invalid. So too the Tur wrote in Yoreh Deah, siman 271, in their name. Therefore he wrote that even if it contains gall-nuts it is valid, for the halachah does not follow them; rather, any black color is valid. As a matter of law, it appears from Shulchan Aruch in this siman that initially any ink is valid, even if it is not made from smoke, and so it appears from Beis Yosef. See Yoreh Deah, siman 271, in Shulchan Aruch and in the words of the Rama; this requires study.
