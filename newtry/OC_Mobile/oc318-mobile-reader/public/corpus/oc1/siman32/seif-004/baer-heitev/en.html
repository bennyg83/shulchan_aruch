To another letter. If the letter is large and it became attached at its end in such a way that, even if what attached is scraped away, the form of the letter will nevertheless remain, it is valid. Radach, Bayis 1, Cheder 22. The Magen Avraham writes that one should be stringent. If the letter itself is not attached, but only one tag is attached to another, it is valid, as stated by Rabbi HaMeiri. The Pri Chadash, in Even HaEzer, siman 125, s.k. 11, writes that this is not clear, and that even where the attachment is through a tag one must scrape away the attachment; see there. The Ralnach also rules stringently; see Magen Avraham, and see below, seif 25, what is written there.
