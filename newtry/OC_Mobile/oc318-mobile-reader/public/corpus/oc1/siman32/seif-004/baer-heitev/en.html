Joined to its neighbor. If the letter is large and attached at its end such that if what is attached is dragged the letter's form would still remain valid — Radak, Bayit 1, Chelek 22; Magen Avraham wrote to be strict. If it is not attached to itself but only a tag is attached to its neighbor, it is valid — Rabbeinu Meir. Pri Chadash in Orach Chaim siman 125, seif kaf-alef 11, wrote it is not clear; even when attached by a tag one may drag the attachment; see there; also Erlau Nachal is strict — see Magen Avraham and below seif 25, as explained.
