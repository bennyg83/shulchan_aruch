(s.k. 4) "With the left — invalid" means one who is right-handed, etc., so you do not err: since the Rabbi equated ambidextrous-right with ambidextrous-both who must write right-handed l'chatchila — if so, "if he wrote with the left — invalid" applies to both, even ambidextrous-both. Magen Avraham wrote this is not found — b'dieved he is not equal to right-handed only. What the Rabbi wrote "invalid" if others can be found implies if impossible one may wear left-written tefillin; Beit Shmuel Even HaEzer 123 s.k. 4: nevertheless do not bless — most poskim hold invalid, though Rosh and Semag did not write explicitly; if no others, wear without blessing perhaps per Rosh/Semag, but do not bless — doubtful blessings are lenient; unlike Levush who wrote to bless — see there.
