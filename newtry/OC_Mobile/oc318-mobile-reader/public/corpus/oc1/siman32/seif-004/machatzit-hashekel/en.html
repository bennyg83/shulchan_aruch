(Seif katan 4) With the left hand it is invalid, that is, where he is dominant, etc. Do not err and say that since Beis Yosef equated one who is right-handed with one who controls both hands, that both must initially write with the right hand, therefore when he wrote afterward that if one wrote with the left hand it is invalid, this also refers to both of them, even to one who controls both hands. For this reason Magen Avraham wrote that this is not so, since after the fact he is not the same as one who is right-handed alone. Know that Beis Yosef's statement that it is invalid if others can be found implies that if others cannot be found, he should put on tefillin written with the left hand. Nevertheless, Beis Shmuel in Even HaEzer siman 123 seif katan 4 wrote that one still should not recite a blessing over them, for most decisors explicitly state that they are invalid. Only the Rosh and Semag did not state explicitly that they are invalid. Therefore, when he has no others, he should put these on, perhaps fulfilling the mitzvah according to the Rosh and Semag; but he may not bless, for in cases of doubtful blessings we are lenient. All the more so since the Rosh and Semag do not state explicitly that they are valid, unlike the Levush, who wrote to bless over them. These are his words; see there.
