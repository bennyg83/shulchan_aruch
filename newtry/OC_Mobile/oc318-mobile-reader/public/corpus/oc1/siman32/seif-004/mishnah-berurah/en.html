(12) To another letter. If the letter is large and became attached at its end in such a way that even if one scrapes away what attached, the form of the letter will nevertheless remain, some validate and some invalidate. The later authorities agreed to be stringent; therefore one must scrape the place of attachment. Even in tefillin and mezuzos this correction helps, since the form of the letter had not changed previously.
(13) Every letter. Even the last letter of the line must be surrounded by blank parchment on all four sides, and this is indispensable even after the fact, as stated below at the end of seif 16. Even if surrounding blank parchment is lacking for the tip of the yud, it is also invalid, as stated in Menachos 29. For all the details of this law, see below in seif 17 and siman 36.
(14) Perfect writing. This means that one should not write beiss like kafs, kafs like beiss, zayins like nuns, nuns like zayins, and the like.
(15) The tip of the yud. This means the left thorn of the yud, and all the more so, if the right leg is missing it is invalid. For the form of the yud, see below in the laws of the forms of the letters in siman 36, and what is written there in the name of the Pri Megadim.
(16) According to its halachah. This refers to the letters shin, ayin, tes, nun, zayin, gimel, and tzadi. This is only required initially, for after the fact, according to most poskim it is valid if one did not add the crowns, as stated below in siman 36, seif 3; see there, where all details of the laws of crowns are explained.
