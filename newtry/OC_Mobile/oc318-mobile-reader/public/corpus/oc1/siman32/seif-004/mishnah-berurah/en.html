(12) In its adhesion — and if the letter is large and adhered at its end such that if what adhered were dragged away the form of a letter would nevertheless remain — some validate it and some invalidate it, and the later authorities agreed to be strict; therefore one must scrape the place of the adhesion; and even in tefillin and mezuzot the correction helps, since the form of the letter was not changed from before.

(13) Every letter — and even the last letter on the line must be surrounded by parchment on all four sides, and this invalidates even post factum, as explained below at the end of se'if 16; and even if the surrounding by parchment is missing from the point of the yod, it is likewise invalid, as stated in Menachot 29a; for all details of this law see below in se'if 17 and in siman 36:

(14) Perfect writing — meaning he should not write bet-yud-nun as kaf-yud-nun kaf-yud-nun bet-yud-nun zayin-yud-nun nun-vav-nun nun-vav-nun zayin-yud-nun, and everything similar:

(15) The point of the yod — meaning the left tip of the yod; all the more so if the right leg is missing, it is invalid. For the form of the yod see below in the laws of the shape of the letters in siman 36, and what is written in the name of Peri Megadim:
