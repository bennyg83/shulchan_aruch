It requires further study, for there is mixing of matters here — for in Shulchan Aruch it refers even if there is the form of a letter on what precedes the hole alone, nevertheless it is invalid since at its beginning it was not mukaf gvil; and in this case of a hole specifically we require that if it broke without a hole there is no invalidation here, that there is letter form on what precedes the break; and Bach refers when it broke in the middle of the letter specifically and there is no letter form on what precedes the break at all — then he holds it is invalid when done at the time of writing, whether through a hole or not through a hole, and a child does not help, as written in Taz in his name. And here in Taz he does not refer to this matter, only to the matter of mukaf gvil; and if at the beginning when it was written there was a break in the middle of the letter and a child can read it, it is kosher for Shulchan Aruch, as stated in Taz. And it is possible that Magen Avraham means on what is implied in Shulchan Aruch that if at the beginning when it was written there was a hole and a break in it, it would be kosher on account of a child's reading, were it not for the reason that it is not mukaf gvil, as explained in Beit Yosef.
