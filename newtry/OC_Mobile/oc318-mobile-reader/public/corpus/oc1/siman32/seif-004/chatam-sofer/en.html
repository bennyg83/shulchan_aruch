See note: see responsa Chatam Sofer Yoreh De'ah, siman 263 [regarding a Torah scroll in which is written Elokeinu thus, and he extended the bottom of the nun and wrote a vav at its side — invalid according to Magen Avraham; how to fix it. He first attacked the words of responsum Avodat HaGershuni, siman 95, who plainly holds that what is attached behind the Name is only a rabbinic prohibition; and on what he wrote that to write a letter and thereby invalidate another letter is only caused erasure — he disputes him on these two matters and concludes there that if it appears clearly that it is writing split into two, which is invalid without doubt, then without hesitation it is permitted to erase the vav first and write another inside the nun — for because of this we erase, because it looks like two words; if so, it is not attached to the Name, etc. But if it does not appear as two words, one cannot erase the vav, lest it be kosher and we touch the Name; it is also forbidden to bore the Name, etc. Nevertheless, in order not to bury a whole sheet, one may permit boring the vav and burying it, for boring is not explicitly forbidden in the Torah, and a doubt is lenient; and he may write another vav inside the nun in the manner I wrote above.
