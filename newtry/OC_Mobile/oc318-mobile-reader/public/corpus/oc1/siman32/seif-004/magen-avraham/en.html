That it not adhere. If the letter is large and adheres at its end such that if dragged what adheres nevertheless the letter's form remains valid — Radbaz in Orach Chayyim 32. If it does not adhere to itself but only tagin of one letter adhere to its fellow — valid (Rabbi Meiri). Rabbi Lev Chayyim wrote it seems the Rambam disagrees; see Beit Yosef under the words "and a letter" and "how do they validate," etc. (27 in Kaf). It seems to me that even in the first case one should be strict; see siman 36 and siman 18, and in Beit Yosef there not like your honor; see s.k. 35.
