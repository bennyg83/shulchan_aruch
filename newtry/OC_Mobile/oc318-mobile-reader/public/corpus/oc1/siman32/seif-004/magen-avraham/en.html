If the letter is large and it became attached at its end in such a way that, even if the attached part were scraped away, the form of a valid letter would still remain, it is valid; Radach in Orach Chaim 32. If it is not the letter itself that became attached, but only one crown that became attached to another, it is valid; Rav HaMeiri. Ralbach wrote that it appears the Rambam disagrees. See Beis Yosef, s.v. Ve'im tomar veheichi machsherei, etc.; all this is in Knesses HaGedolah. It seems to me that even in the first ruling one should be stringent; see siman 36, se'if 18, and Beis Yosef there, unlike Olas Tamid. See what I wrote in s.k. 35.
