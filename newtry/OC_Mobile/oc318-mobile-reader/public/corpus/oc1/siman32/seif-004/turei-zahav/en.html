Complete writing. This means that every letter must be surrounded by parchment. So wrote the Tur. My teacher and father-in-law, of blessed memory, wrote: Regarding halacha, it appears that in tefillin, if one letter touches another, even in other words, whether above or below, it is invalid and has no remedy; but in a sefer Torah it has a remedy, to scrape it, etc. It is obvious that some mistaken student wrote this, for below in this siman, seif 25, it is stated explicitly that because of this it is not called out of order, and one may scrape where the letters adhere. He himself also wrote so below. Therefore one should not take account of this, for certainly it did not come from his mouth.
