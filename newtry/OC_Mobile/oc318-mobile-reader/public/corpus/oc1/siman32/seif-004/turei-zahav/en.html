Complete writing — meaning that every letter be surrounded by parchment; thus writes the Tur, and Mahari Cohen wrote likewise. Regarding the law, it appears that in tefillin it is invalid if one letter touches another, even in other words whether from above or below, and it has no remedy. But in a sefer Torah there is remedy by scraping, etc. It is obvious that some erring student wrote this, for explicitly below, siman 32 seif 22, that through this it is not called out of order and one may scrape where letters stick together — and he himself says so below. Therefore one should not heed this, for it certainly did not come from his mouth.
