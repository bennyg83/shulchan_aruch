Or. See Taz. His text read “the leg of the alef” (there are three readings: the leg of the nun, the leg of the alef, and the alef). He elaborates that in seif 25 the Mechaber himself rules that if the leg of the alef does not touch the roof, a child’s reading helps regarding the concern that it should not be considered out of order, but it still requires correction. This is the second opinion of the Maharik. According to the first opinion, even correction does not help. How then did the Rav combine it with a straight letter, where no correction at all is needed? See Eliyah Rabbah 24, who says that it may be speaking where it reaches the middle line and is severed only at the head of the yud; see there. A full small letter suffices for this.
