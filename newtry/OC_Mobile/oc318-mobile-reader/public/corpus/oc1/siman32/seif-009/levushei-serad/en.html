(There) And in the book Be'er Sheva. This follows what he first asked from Yoreh De'ah, where it is written that marking does not help. He answered two answers. About this he wrote that the Be'er Sheva indeed rules stringently, that marking in the middle does not help, as in Yoreh De'ah; rather, one should write on the hide stripped from the head on its inner side, and the non-Jew does not know of it at all, so it is an excellent sign.
