Magen Avraham note 10 and so Pri Megadim in siman 42 who wrote. And it is explicit in the sugya Menachot 34. However I find it difficult — if so, what is the challenge there "and per the view that hazmanah is a significant act," etc.; perhaps also to remove it to non-sacred status, but lowering to light sanctity is permitted, as for us with hazmanah in the body of the sanctified object. And I found in responsum Avbet Hillulim who proved from this that there is no distinction between secular use and lowering. And for this agreed also in responsum Pnei Moshe part 3 siman 11. And regarding Magen Avraham's proof — from what we answer in Chadadei — one may explain per the foundation of the Maor's words, that hazmanah in the body of sanctity is a significant act because they learn it from eglah arufah. And this is for practical halachah, since we rule like Rabbi Yehudah that before its descent to Nahal Eitan it is forbidden. But there in Menachot, where we answer in Chadadei — that is on the reading of the baraita there, which is Rabbi Yehudah who says one does not lower, etc.; and per Rabbi Yehudah in Keritut, its arufah forbids it. In truth, even regarding the body of sanctity they say hazmanah is not a significant act, and Chadadei answers well. And therefore the challenge "and per the view that hazmanah is a significant act" — that refers to Abaye. And so too for the one who holds its descent to Nahal Eitan forbids it — according to him, in the body of sanctity in any event it is a significant act; think on this. And in a responsum I elaborated on this.
