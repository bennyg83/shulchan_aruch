If a non-Jew processed them, etc., and a Jew assisted somewhat in the processing — that is, after the non-Jew takes them from the second water, then the Jew helps stretch the hide until it becomes parchment and stretches the hide to its full length with all his strength so the parchment is long enough to write the four portions of the arm on one piece, for it is a mitzvah to write arm tefillin initially from one piece; after stretching he takes the knife and draws out the water from both sides of the hide with all his ability, and afterward he leaves the non-Jew to do the rest and to dry (Barukh She'amar siman 12).
