That he place, etc. — See Mishnah Berurah, and what he wrote "to go out," etc. — that is Rashi's view; Rambam too holds like Rashi, as he wrote in Kessef Mishneh. Even though the Geonim disagree on this, and also Rabbenu Yitzchak, Rosh, Mordechai, and Tur hold like them, and the custom of the world is like them — nevertheless it is good to fulfill also their view to go out. Even though Rosh and Mordechai imply that it is better not to make one hide with the housings — see the reason in their words, and you will see that this defect does not apply in our time to one who knows the craft of housings. Gra exaggerated in copying the words of Rikanti, from whom it appears he holds so from the point of law and only the world did not act so; but in truth, in my humble opinion, this is as we wrote — many disagree with them.

He shall roll each parashah, etc. — It is a mitzvah and not an impediment [acharonim in se'if 47].

And wrap them in a small parchment — See Mishnah Berurah; the reason is that Rosh, Tur, and Shitah Mekubetzet hold it is not halachah leMoshe miSinai, and it is customary in France and Ashkenaz not to wrap anything on it except hair alone; but lekhatchilah Shulchan Aruch was concerned for Rambam's words, who wrote that it is halachah leMoshe miSinai. See Eshel Avraham, from whom it appears one may also bless if he has no others — unlike Machatzit HaShekel, who was uncertain about this.
