Of the tefillin. The Mechaber wrote simply that one places hide below, and he did not clarify whether the titura must be from the hide of the compartments, or whether it may even be from a separate piece of hide, as scribes now practice. Since he wrote without qualification, it implies that in every manner it is valid. So too wrote the Rosh and the Tur, that in every manner it is valid. See Olas Tamid.
Small. If he did not wrap them, it is valid after the fact if he has no others. Bach, Magen Avraham.
For the compartments. It is good to place it by the passage Vehayah Im Shamoa, on the side facing the passage Kadesh. Shlah, and see Magen Avraham.
