A bit of hair. It is written in Yesh Sachar in the name of the Zohar that hair should not extend the measure of an olive outside the batim; so Zohar Parashat Pikudei page 225: we require the secret of tefillin to be hidden — one hair of a calf should go out and be visible, for these threads of hair do not defile outside; but if that hair is attached and made like an olive — less than that does not defile, end quote. There is difficulty, for hairs do not impart impurity even when many; moreover less than an olive's bulk — even a bone does not impart impurity, all the more flesh or hair. It seems he referred to what we learned in chapter 3 of Ohalot: everything in a corpse is impure except hair, etc., and at the time of attachment all is impure — meaning when hair is attached to it. If so, his words mean: these threads of hair do not defile outside, but if attached, etc. — that is, when hair is attached to the bone as an olive's bulk it becomes like an olive's bulk — meaning he holds hair joins to the bone as an olive's bulk, as above; if so one may say we do not care if hair extends outside the batim more than an olive's bulk. Shelah page 116 wrote: some say hair must extend from parashat Kodesh; I received from parashat VeHayah Im Shamo'a — it seems good to place them near parashat VeHayah Im Shamo'a on the side facing parashat Kodesh, end quote.
