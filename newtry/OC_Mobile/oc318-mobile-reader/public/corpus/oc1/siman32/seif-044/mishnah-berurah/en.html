(201) That one place [hide], etc. — and even according to those who hold that the batim must all be from one actual hide, nevertheless we do not require that the titura and ma'avarta be from one hide with the batim; rather, even if it is hide on its own it is valid, and this is what the Mechaber wrote, "that one place," etc. Nevertheless, if possible it is good to be stringent in this too, to satisfy those who are strict about this; see Biur Halachah:

(202) It should be long — and if it tore, one may sew it:

(203) That it be recognizable — for the titura too must by law be square, as we wrote above in seif 39:

(204) From its end to its beginning — when one places it in its bayit, like a mezuzah that is rolled from the end toward the top (shin) and not the reverse, for the reason explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 288:

(205) And wrap them, etc. — that is, lechatchilah; but if he did not wrap them, it is valid b'dieved if he has no others; see Machatzit HaShekel and Biur Halachah:

(206) On a small piece of klaf — and the same applies to a piece of cloth if he has no klaf [Peri Megadim]; see Biur HaGra:

(207) On valid klaf — that is, not from an impure animal or beast; but with a cloth, even for this view it is fine. See Beit Yosef, who wrote the reason of the matter: since one brings klaf, it must specifically be from what is permitted in one's mouth, like for batim and retzuot; and in Biur HaGra he questions this ruling:

(208) And for this reason — therefore if he did not wrap the hair on it, it is invalid even if he wrapped klaf on it:

(209) And the custom is to wrap, etc. — for some say one wraps the hair on the body of the parasha and then wraps the klaf on top, and some say the reverse; therefore the custom fulfills both views. See Biur HaGra, who wrote that by law there is no insistence which comes first:

(210) Of a calf — so that he remember the incident of the calf and not sin, and also to atone for that sin; and Eliyah Rabbah wrote that for this reason it is good to make every tefillin repair from calf hide — as opposed to those who make the retzuot from goat hide:

(211) A little hair — see Magen Avraham; and see Chidushei Rabbeinu Avraham, who agreed that the hair protruding should be less than the length of a barley grain:

(212) Outside the batim — some say it should be at the bayit in which parashat Kadesh is placed, and some say at [the bayit of] vehaya im shamoa; and it is good that it protrude from parashat vehaya im shamoa on the side facing parashat Kadesh:
