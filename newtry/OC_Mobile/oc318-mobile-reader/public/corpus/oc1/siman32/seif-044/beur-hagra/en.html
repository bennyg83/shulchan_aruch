44 — And this is, etc. As the Geonim explain; one need not challenge that if so on Shabbat 28b we place titura and ma'avarta — one may say since it is sewn to him it is like him, unlike straps. Mordechai.

He shall wrap, etc. As written 31b; likewise above 132; and above siman 691, seif 2, as written; see Tosafot 35a, s.v. me'evereta — it does not seem to disqualify, etc., one, etc.

And he wraps them, etc. From Yerushalmi, chapter 1 of Megillah: the law that they write on hides, etc., wrap in hair, coat in cloth — therefore the Rambam wrote he wraps cloth over them; but in Shitah Mekubetzet — parchment and cloth, meaning from parchment; Sefer HaTerumah and Shitah Mekubetzet wrote there is no proof from Yerushalmi, for there it discusses tearing, as in Mishnat Sofrim; the Rambam disqualifies even bedi'eved, as he holds it is the law; they argued on him; nevertheless Mordechai wrote: go see, etc., that they practiced like Shitah Mekubetzet; this is what is written Shabbat 79b regarding mezuzah itself — "itself" seems superfluous.

And there are, etc. As Shabbat there — "they were not validated," etc.; and it is difficult why we do not establish it on the cloth.

And the custom, etc. For the Rambam wrote the cloth precedes wrapping the hair, as Shulchan Arukh; Semag challenged — in the Yerushalmi above it is the opposite; so in Shitah Mekubetzet; therefore Agur wrote "and the custom," etc.; but for law there is no insistence which comes first.

And they practiced, etc. As written "and with the arm of His strength," etc.; so Zohar, part 2, 127b.

And if, etc. Also for the above reason; but of a calf is preferable, as written "and the soles of their feet," etc.; Az Chayyim 32:4.
