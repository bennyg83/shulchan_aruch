In the air of the heh. See Be'er Heitev; and see Taz who challenged Shulchan Arukh who wrote one must beware — for even bedi'eved invalid; and see responsa Makom Shmuel who brought from Shulchan Arukh Even HaEzer siman 125 — there too implies lekhatchilah only; bedi'eved invalid only if letter form changed thereby; therefore his view to be lenient on lamed of "and serve Him" whose head entered into heh of "and love" and did not touch — only entered the air, see there siman 13; and one should not be lenient except in pressing need.
