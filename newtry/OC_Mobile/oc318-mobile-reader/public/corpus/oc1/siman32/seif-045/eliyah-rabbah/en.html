[45] One should be careful, etc. The Bach wrote that even ex post facto it is invalid — not as the Shulchan Aruch wrote one should be careful in this, which implies ex post facto it is not invalid — and this is not so — so far. But in Lechem Chamudot there he also understood the words of the Shulchan Aruch that it is invalid even ex post facto — not like the Taz.
