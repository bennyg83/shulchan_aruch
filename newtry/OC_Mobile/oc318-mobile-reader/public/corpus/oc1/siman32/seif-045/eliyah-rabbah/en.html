One must be careful, etc. The Bach wrote that even after the fact it is invalid, not as the Shulchan Aruch wrote, that one must be careful about this, which implies that after the fact it is not invalid; that is not so, end quote. But in Lechem Chamudos there, he also understood the words of the Shulchan Aruch to mean that it is invalid even after the fact, not like the Taz.
