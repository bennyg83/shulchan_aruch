(s.k. 45) Between verses… "cursed" is one of the five… if he stops before "cursed" like other verse-ends, it seems "cursed" belongs below — a Talmudic doubt; if he does not stop, somewhat implies above. We consider the heh verse-start; if he stops before every start and here does not — opposite of our view appears.

And see Yoreh De'ah end of siman 274 in the Beit Yosef and see in the Shach there who wrote, and this is his language: "But if he left space between the verses, etc." — kosher. From this it is implied that ab initio one should not leave much space, but a little must be left between verse and verse, and as written in Orach Chayyim siman 32, etc. — behold, he resolves the difficulty of Magen Avraham, that the words of the Shulchan Aruch do not contradict each other, nor the words of the Rivash. But the Rama has no proof for this from the Rivash, and there still remains the difficulty of Magen Avraham that we are not expert in [the division of] verses.
