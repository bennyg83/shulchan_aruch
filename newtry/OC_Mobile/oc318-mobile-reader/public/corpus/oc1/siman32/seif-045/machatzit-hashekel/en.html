(s.k. 45) Between verse, etc. Also, “Arur” is one of the five words, etc. If so, either way: if he pauses before the word “Arur” as at other verse endings, then from this it will appear that “Arur” belongs below, though it is a doubt among the Sages of the Talmud. And if he does not pause, it somewhat implies that it belongs above. Also, in truth, we consider it the beginning of a verse; if he pauses before every beginning of a verse, but here does not pause, it appears the opposite of what we hold.
See Yoreh Deah, end of siman 274, in Beis Yosef, and see there in the Shach, who wrote as follows: but if he left space between the verses, etc. It is valid. This implies that initially one should not leave much space, but a small space should be left between one verse and another, as written in Orach Chaim, siman 32, etc. Thus he resolves the Magen Avraham’s difficulty, that the words of Shulchan Aruch do not contradict each other, nor do the words of the Rivash. However, the Rama has no proof for this from the Rivash, and the Magen Avraham’s difficulty still remains, since we are not expert in the verses.
