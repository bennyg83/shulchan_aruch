Upright. In the manner it is read, and in the manner a Torah scroll is placed in the ark. After the fact, if he placed it there lying down, the tefillin should not be invalidated because of this; so wrote the Beis Yosef in the name of Ri ben Chaviv, and so wrote the Bach. See Magen Avraham. It appears that nevertheless, going forward, he must fix it, meaning that he should take them out and place them according to law.
