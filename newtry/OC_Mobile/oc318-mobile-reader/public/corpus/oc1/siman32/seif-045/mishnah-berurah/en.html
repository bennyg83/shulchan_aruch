(213) Upright — as it is read and as a Sefer Torah is stood in the Heichal; and b'dieved, if one placed it there lying down, one does not invalidate the tefillin for this. So wrote Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari ben Chabiv, and so Bach; see Magen Avraham.
