133 — He shall make, etc. Meaning: lekhatchilah he places this ("Eli") and "Anohu".

He shall beware, etc. Here is left unspecified, per the Rosh — there he happened upon a five-letter word; he says specifically a five-letter word, that more than two letters one may not write outside the column, for otherwise it should have said in general that one may not write most of the word outside the column. But the Rambam, chapter 7, writes that even a ten-letter word or fewer or more — one may write half outside the column; and he was even more lenient that even half is permitted, and he learned from what is written, "a two-letter word," etc. — specifically the whole word, but half is permitted. From his words it implies that even in a three-letter word, two letters are forbidden outside the column; but the Rosh writes that it is permitted — from what is written, "a word," etc., it implies specifically the whole word is forbidden; otherwise it is always permitted. And this is what is written here: three letters, whether from a large word or a small one. But in Yoreh De'ah, siman 273, seif 4, he wrote the Rambam's words, and in a three-letter word he ruled like the Rosh.
