Seif 33, he should make, etc. Meaning, initially, because of: This is my G-d and I will beautify Him.
He should be careful, etc. He ruled here without qualification according to the opinion of the Rosh. What the Rosh wrote there, if a word of, etc., came before him, refers specifically to a five-letter word, for more than two letters may not be written outside the column. Were this not so, he should have stated plainly: one may not write the majority of a word outside the column. But the Rambam wrote in chapter 7 that even a word of ten letters, or fewer, or more, may have half of it written outside the column; and he was more lenient, that even half is permitted. He learned this from what is stated there regarding a two-letter word, etc., that specifically all of it is prohibited, but half is permitted. It appears from his words that even in a three-letter word, two letters outside the column are prohibited. But the Rosh wrote that this is permitted, from what is stated: a word of, etc., implying that specifically all of it is prohibited, but otherwise it is always permitted. This is what is written here: three letters, whether from a large word or a small one. But in Yoreh Deah, siman 273, seif 4, he wrote the words of the Rambam, while regarding a three-letter word he ruled according to the opinion of the Rosh.
