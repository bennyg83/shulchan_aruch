(147) And one protrudes — even one letter, because of "Anokhi ve'Anvehu."

(148) Three letters — since he did not distinguish, it implies that even if these three letters are the minority of the word it is forbidden; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 273 he ruled like the Rambam that we are strict only that one not write the majority of the word outside the line; and if the word is eight letters long, it is permitted to write half of it outside the line. See in Shach there, who wrote that one should be strict as here; and see in Eliyah Rabbah, whose opinion is that in any case in tefillin where the sheets are short one should be strict.

(149) And if he wrote them — whether the protrusion was at the beginning of the line or at its end.

(150) It does not invalidate — even if he wrote an entire word outside the line, as long as it is evident that it was written with that same line and not with the other column beside it.
