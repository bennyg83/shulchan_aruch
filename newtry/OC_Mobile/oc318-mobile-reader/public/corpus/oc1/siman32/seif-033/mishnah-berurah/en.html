And one protrudes. Even one letter, because of the principle, “This is my God and I will beautify Him.”
Three letters. Since he did not distinguish, it implies that even if these three letters are the minority of the word, it is forbidden. In Yoreh Deah, siman 273, however, he ruled like the Rambam, that we are particular only that one not write most of the word outside the line; if the word has eight letters, it is permitted to write half of it outside the line. See the Shach there, who writes that one should be stringent like the ruling here. See Eliyah Rabbah, whose view is that at least in tefillin, where the margins are narrow, one should be stringent.
And if he wrote them. Whether the protrusion was at the beginning of the line or at its end.
It did not invalidate. Even if he wrote an entire word outside the line, provided it is recognizable that it is read with that line and not with the adjacent column beside it.
