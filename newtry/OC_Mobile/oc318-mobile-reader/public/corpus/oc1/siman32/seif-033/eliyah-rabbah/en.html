That he should not write three, etc. If he happened to have a word of six letters, Olas Tamid wrote that it is permitted to write three letters outside the column. But the Shach in Yoreh De'ah, siman 273, forbids this, and he wondered at the Shulchan Aruch and Levush, who forbid here while regarding a Torah scroll they are lenient. In my humble opinion it appears that the Shulchan Aruch holds that tefillin are different, since their lines are small and few and their margin is small, so it is more unsightly.
