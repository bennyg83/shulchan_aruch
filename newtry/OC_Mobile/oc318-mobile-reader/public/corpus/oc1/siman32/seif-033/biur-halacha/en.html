Three letters. See the Mishnah Berurah. Its source is the Shach, Yoreh Deah, siman 273, s.k. 4; see there. See Biur HaGra, which implies that he also agrees with the Shach, for the Mechaber here wrote anonymously like the Rosh. See Magen Avraham; I omitted his answer because of the difficulty raised by the Baer Heitev, see there. The Pri Megadim also raised this difficulty. However, so as not, Heaven forbid, to make our master the Magen Avraham mistaken, I said it may be resolved as follows: from se'if 34 there is no proof to be lenient here, for perhaps it excludes only that it is permitted to write one letter of a two-letter word outside the line, even though that is half the word, as the Gra wrote according to the Rambam, and not like the Levush, who is stringent; see his reason there. But two letters of a three-letter word, which are most of the word, one might have thought is forbidden, as the Gra indeed wrote according to the Rambam; therefore the Mechaber teaches that two letters are permitted in every case. This appears to be his intent.
