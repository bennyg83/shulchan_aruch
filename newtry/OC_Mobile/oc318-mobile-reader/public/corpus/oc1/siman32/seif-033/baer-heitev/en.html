To the line. In Yoreh Deah, siman 273, he ruled like the Rambam, that we are particular only that one not write most of a word outside the line. If the word has eight letters, one may write half of it outside the line. The Magen Avraham wrote: Perhaps he comes to teach here that two letters may be written outside the line even in a three-letter word, for in this the Rambam agrees; see there. And I say that this is already known from the following seif, since he writes, 'two letters that are a word,' and so on. This implies that two letters which are not one word are permitted even in a three-letter word. This is straightforward.
