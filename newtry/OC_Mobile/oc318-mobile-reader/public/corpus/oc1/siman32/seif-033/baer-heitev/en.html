To the line. In Yoreh Deah siman 273 he ruled like Rambam — we are not strict except not to write most of the word outside the line; if the word is eight letters, half may be written outside the line — Magen Avraham wrote, and possibly he comes to teach here that two letters may be written even in a three-letter word, which Rambam would admit; see there. I say from the next section we know this from "two letters that are a word" — implying two letters that are not one word are permitted even in a three-letter word — and examine.
