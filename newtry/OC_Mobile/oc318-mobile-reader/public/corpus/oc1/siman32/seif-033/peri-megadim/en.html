In order to be 17, the length must be the same as the actual width, and no matter what angles there are, as long as its length is greater than its width, there will not be an excess of pentagons on the diagonal. And it is necessary to measure in an existing line, that is, first, measure line A, the length and line A, width, which should be equal, and this is not enough, lest the middle of them be equal, and the sides decrease. Therefore, you need to measure another two lines on the diagonal, which will be equal. and sewing that will not pull the sewing thread And this is as it means that the skin of the house makes a Titorah and the Titorah does not come out except for the house and sews around the seam for the Didan which means that a Titorah knot goes outside the house and we take another skin and make a Titorah and sew in the middle of the Titorah.
