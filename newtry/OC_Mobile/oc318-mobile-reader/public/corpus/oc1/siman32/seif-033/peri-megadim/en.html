It must be measured in four lines: first one measures one line lengthwise and one line widthwise, that they be equal. This alone is insufficient, for perhaps in the middle they are equal but at the sides it narrows. Therefore he must also measure two diagonal lines so that the two diagonals are also equal. See Derishah in the name of Maharash. Now, in Menachos 35a, tefillin being square is a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai in their stitching and diagonal. Rashi explains: the compartment is square, as are the titura and the stitching, so that he should not pull the sewing thread. This is according to his explanation, that one makes the titura from the leather of the compartment, and the titura does not extend outside the compartment, and he sews around it. But according to our practice, where the titura means a projection outside the compartment and another piece of leather is taken to make the titura, and one sews in the middle of the titura, it would seem necessary to align the square of the stitching as an exact square as well. This requires study. See Rambam, laws of tefillin 3:3, who explains like Rashi; see there. See Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai, and Magen Avraham 68.
