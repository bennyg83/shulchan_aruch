Measure. Taz: length must equal width actually; and what has corners does not help — whatever its length exceeds its width should not be on a diagonal of two fifths excess. One must measure on four sides — meaning, first measure one line length and one line width that they be equal; and this is not enough, lest the middle be equal and the sides taper; therefore one must measure two more lines on the diagonal that they be equal — two diagonal lines too; see Derishah in the name of Maharash. In Menachot 35a: "tefillin must be square" — halachah leMoshe miSinai in their sewing and diagonals; Rashi explains: the housing squared, titura, and sewing so the sewing thread not pull. This is according to his explanation that from the housing's hide one makes titura, and titura does not go out beyond the housing and he sews around — unlike for us, whose explanation of titura is a knot going out beyond the housing; we take another hide and make titura and sew in the middle of titura. It would seem one must intend the square of the sewing also to be an actual square — uncertain in this. See Rambam, chapter 3 of Laws of Tefillin, halachah 3 — he explained like Rashi; see there. End of commentary in the name of Mordechai — Magen Avraham, siman 68.
