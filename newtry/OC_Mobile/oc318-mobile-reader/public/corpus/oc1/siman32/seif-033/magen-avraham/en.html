Three letters. This requires study, for here the ruling was stated without qualification in accordance with the Rosh, whereas in Yoreh Deah, siman 273, the ruling follows the Rambam, that we are particular only that he not write the majority of the word outside the line; if the word has eight letters, it is permitted to write half of it outside the line. Perhaps the intent here is to teach that two letters may be written outside the line even in a three-letter word, for in this the Rambam agrees, as stated there.
