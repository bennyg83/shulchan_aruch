Three letters. It is unresolved, for unstated it follows the Rosh; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 273 he ruled like the Rambam that they are not particular except that one not write the majority of the word outside the line; if the word is eight letters, one may write half of it outside the line. It is possible he came to teach here that two letters may be written even in a three-letter word — for in this the Rambam agrees, as written there.
