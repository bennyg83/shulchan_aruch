This is a scribal error, and it should say: for the passage of VeHayah Ki Yeviacha, which is larger; and for the passages of Kadesh and VeHayah Im Shamoa, which are long, etc. See Biur HaGra and Yad Aharon. Rabbi Mordechai Yaffe wrote that their scribes make a different adjustment: all the parchments are equal in one length and one thickness, but they leave margins in the shorter passages.
