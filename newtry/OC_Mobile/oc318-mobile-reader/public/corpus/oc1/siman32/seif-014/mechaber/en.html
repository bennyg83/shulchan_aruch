Careful scribes make three kinds of parchment: the thickest to write the parsha of Shema, which is small; thinner than it for the parsha of Vehayah im shamoa, which is larger; and for the parshiyos of Kadesh and Vehayah ki yeviacha, which are long, they make very thin parchment. In this way the batim will be filled evenly, and this is beautification for tefillin.
