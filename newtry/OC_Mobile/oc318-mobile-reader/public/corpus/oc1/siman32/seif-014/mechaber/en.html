Diligent scribes make three kinds of parchment: the thicker to write parshat Shema, which is the shorter; thinner than it for parshat V'haya im shamo'a, which is larger; and for parshiyot Kadesh and V'haya ki y'vi'acha, which are long, they make very thin parchment — and by this the compartments are filled evenly, and this is beauty for tefillin.
