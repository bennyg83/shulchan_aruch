Small. Rema MiFano writes that their scribes make a different arrangement, namely that all the parchments are equal in length and thickness, except that they leave margin in the shorter passages; see there. On this basis I heard an answer to what Tosafos Yom Tov asked in tractate Shabbos, chapter 8, mishnah 3, in the comment beginning “which is Shema Yisrael, etc.”; see there. One can say that the Tanna came to teach that for the laws of Shabbos the parchment need not be thick or include the margin as in tefillin. Rather, the measure is only enough to write upon it the passage of Shema Yisrael, and no margin is required, even though the parchment is thin; this is clear. See Sefer Yad Aharon and Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 259.
