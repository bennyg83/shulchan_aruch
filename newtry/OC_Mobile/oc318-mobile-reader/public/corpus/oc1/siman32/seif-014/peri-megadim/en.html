Taz: what explanation 1 of Beit Yosef says, like Rashi on Menachot 29a, s.v. she'ein — "not surrounded, attached" — he explained well in note 8; see there — one may reconcile Shulchan Aruch's words. However, what the Tur holds in explanation 1 — I did not merit to understand, for plainly it is difficult: even if pierced outside it does not invalidate except when a letter adheres to a letter — all the more if pierced inside. And apparently one may reconcile explanation 1: when a letter adheres to a letter there is no letter upon it — is it surrounded by gevil? A simple kuf reaching the end, or a broken heh and the like — whenever the letter form is recognizable. And in Yerushalmi it says "from inside too" — meaning, whatever is pierced through the entire hollow inside is not considered a form thus; and it is similar to a letter stuck to a letter where its form is not upon it, and inside is worse, etc., in filling the entire hollow; but therefore I do not understand what he wrote in note 8 that the Tur concludes "pierced the entire hollow" — for if so, for the Tur and explanation 1 one certainly requires the entire hollow; unlike us, for explanation 2, and as Shulchan Aruch ruled: whatever is pierced from the outset within the hollow, even on one side — invalid. And it is possible that likewise for us; understand.
