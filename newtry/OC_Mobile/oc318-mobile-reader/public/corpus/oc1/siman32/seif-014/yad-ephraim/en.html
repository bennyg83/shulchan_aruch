(Note 42) "demagas girsin," etc. And it requires further study — for if it refers to one who has the text fluent in his mouth, then another is not needed to read it to him, and he also does not need to write from a written text at all; perforce here it refers when he does not have it fluent — for then if another does not read it to him he must go back and read it with his mouth; and it implies that if he writes from a written text he does not need to go back and read even where he does not recite girsin; and it is possible he means that although not fluent in his mouth, nevertheless it is common in tefillin that there be something memorized somewhat in one's mouth, and therefore one may be lenient that he need not go back and read:
