[35] And some say it suffices, etc. And thus it seems from the conclusion of Orach Chaim, since he concludes that thus is the view of Sefer HaTerumot and the Rambam — and that is, Lechem Chamudot wrote the Rama wrote as Orach Chaim holds — so far. He meant: if per Beit Yosef he does not accept Sefer HaTerumot and Rambam thus, but per Orach Chaim he accepts thus — easy to understand; I only wrote to remove the wonder of the remnants of Keneset HaGedolah and Lechem Chamudot; see there, who expanded needlessly. And behold in Yoreh De'ah at the start of siman 276 regarding a Torah scroll they ruled with the first view that if he did not say it with his lips it is invalid; and Lechem Chamudot wrote page 90: "I wonder — what distinguishes a Torah scroll from tefillin?" The Bach and Shach also ruled leniently for a Torah scroll; but Sefer HaTerumot ruled there with the first view, and thus it seems to be stringent when possible. The Levush in Yoreh De'ah there wrote that even passing the quill does not help; and it seems from the Levush that even though he did not mention the divine name explicitly it suffices, for this is included in saying for the sanctity of the tefillin.
