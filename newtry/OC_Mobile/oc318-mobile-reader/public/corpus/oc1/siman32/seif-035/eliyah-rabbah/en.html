And some say that it is enough, etc. This also appears to be the conclusion of Orchos Chaim, since he concludes that this is the view of Sefer HaTerumah and the Rambam; that is what Lechem Chamudos meant when he wrote that the Rama wrote in accordance with Orchos Chaim, end quote. Meaning, according to the Beis Yosef, Sefer HaTerumah and the Rambam do not hold this way, but according to Orchos Chaim they do hold this way; this is easy to understand. I wrote this only to remove the wonder of Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah and Lechem Chamudos; see there, where he elaborated unnecessarily. Now in Yoreh De'ah, the beginning of siman 276, regarding a Torah scroll, they ruled like the first view, that if he did not say it with his lips, it is invalid. Lechem Chamudos, folio 90, wrote: I wonder, why should a Torah scroll differ from tefillin? The Bach and Shach also ruled leniently regarding a Torah scroll, but Sefer HaTerumah ruled there like the first view. It also appears proper to be stringent where possible. The Levush wrote there in Yoreh De'ah that even passing the quill over it again does not help. It implies from the Levush that even if he did not explicitly mention the Divine Names, it is enough, for this is included when he says that he writes for the sanctity of tefillin.
