Words. See Baer Heitev and Devar Shmuel, siman 354. Regarding a word with an extra vav, if erasing it would divide the word and make it appear as two words, he may first need to reshape the preceding letter so the vav enters into it, and only then erase the vav. If a word was extra and its removal would leave a blank space of nine letters, one should first extend a heh or kuf from the preceding word, or remove only part of the extra word and extend the letter before erasing the rest, so that the tefillin were never invalid.
