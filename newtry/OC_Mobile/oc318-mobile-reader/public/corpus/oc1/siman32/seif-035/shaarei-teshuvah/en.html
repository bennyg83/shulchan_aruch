Words. Be'er Heitev — see Dvar Shmuel siman 254 who brought Beit Yosef and Rambam; explained in his words on "to your fathers": if he comes to erase the extra vav — perhaps he must erase first the preceding bet, roof and bottom adjacent to the vav, and the vav enters within — then erase the vav; but if he erases first immediately invalid, for "to your fathers" will be divided and appear as two words, and extending afterward will not help since invalidity is evident — nothing in fix of writing and law of extension for out-of-order writing. And Be'er Heitev wrote: if divided — abbreviation in copying Magen Avraham; his intent: where one must drag an extra word — if when dragging there will be a space of nine letters and tefillin disqualified — to avoid disqualifying, if at end of the word before there is heh or kuf of a Name, extend the heh or kuf roof — and even though by extension the heh or kuf leg will be at its end, no concern bedi'eved, see there. And per Darkei Shmuel — ostensibly here too one should extend before dragging; if so, extend above the extra word while the word is written — corruption of the letter; and perhaps since invalidity is only nine-letter space — not needed; may extend afterward without out-of-order; and it seems one should drag first two or three letters from the extra word, extend the heh, then drag the rest — then never invalid. See responsa Machatzit HaShekel who wanted to learn from doubled words in tefillin; Sha'arei Ephraim gate 5 in Petchei She'arim: in tefillin if out of order — but certainly one must erase and remove the extra word, see there.
