Within the page. Nothing may protrude at all — except Rabbi Baal Be'er Sheva wrote at end of his book page 52b: from the doubling of language it implies even after the fact invalid, even in other names that are not erased, and even what hangs after the name is invalid if outside the line — so for halachah, not practice. Magen Avraham challenged him: Shakh in Yoreh Deah siman 276, seif vav wrote some validate when part of the name hangs — see there, seif heh 5, apparently all the more when what hangs after the name is outside the line it is valid; the Gaon Rabbi Yitzchak av beit din of Pozna ordered erasing upper lines in a Torah scroll and drawing them level with the name since it can be fixed — not merely after the fact; but if a name is in upper lines that may not be erased, valid after the fact — until here Magen Avraham; likewise in tefillin erasing is forbidden as out of order — valid after the fact. Author Halachah Berurah erred greatly in what he wrote — I wonder, for it is out of order; see there, seif mem-alef 41. See Beit Yosef in Yoreh Deah siman 276 who brought Rambam's responsum on a Torah scroll where heh was partly within the line and partly outside — proper to exchange that sheet and bury it, and after exchange read publicly ab initio — apparently Rambam holds invalid even after the fact; Yad Aharon elaborated that practically it is not a full prohibition in his hand. See responsum Ginat Veradim, Orach Chaim part, klal 2, siman 29, and Mishneh LaMelekh on Hilchot Tefillin chapter 5.
