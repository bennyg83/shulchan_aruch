135 — Letters of the Name, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. sheloshah, etc. — that outside the column is by the law of hanging, and one may not hang part of the Name, as written above. Orach Chaim: the halachah follows Rabbi Shimon Shezuri, and the Rif and Rosh brought it there, and all poskim agree, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 276, seif 6. And it is difficult — for we say in Chullin 87b, "I, Ana," etc., and see Tosafot there, s.v. Ana, etc.
