Seif 35, the letters of the Divine Name, etc. Tosafos there, s.v. three, etc., because writing outside the column falls under the law of suspension, and one may not suspend part of the Divine Name, as stated there: Rabbi Chisda said, the halachah is like Rabbi Shimon Shezuri. The Rif and Rosh brought this there, and so ruled all the poskim, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 276, seif 6. This requires study, for we say in Chullin 75b, he said to him: I, etc.; and see Tosafos there, s.v. I, etc.
