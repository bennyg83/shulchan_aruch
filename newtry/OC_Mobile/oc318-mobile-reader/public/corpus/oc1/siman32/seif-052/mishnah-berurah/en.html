(247) And he shall make a knot — and the knot of tefillin is a halachah leMoshe miSinai; and it appears that it must be made lishmah, and one should not make it small [Peri Megadim]:

(248) Like a dalet — and some make a knot like a closed mem, which appears as two dalets on two sides, one's leg beside the other's head; see Sefer Tiferet Aryeh, who cited in the name of a responsum of Me'ahavah and agreed with him for practical law that those who make a dalet knot are more aligned with the law. Also, one should not make a knot designed to slip back and forth, as I explained in Biur Halachah. Eliyah Rabbah wrote: an incident occurred — one saw after another removed his tefillin that the head-knot had come undone; I instructed him to put them back on and recite Shema without a blessing, for the mitzvah of tefillin applies all day — end quote; and his reason is that according to Rashi, who holds the yud is the halachah leMoshe miSinai, he had not yet fulfilled the mitzvah:

(249) To pass through — the reason is that head tefillin are placed under one's garments and sway and come loose and are damaged; therefore they used to pass this strap over them to strengthen them; but nowadays in these countries this is not practiced [Eliyah Rabbah, see there]:

(250) Except afterward, etc. — meaning: thus it is correct lechatchilah; and if it happened that the head-knot came undone, one need only repair it and not permit the hand tefillin:
