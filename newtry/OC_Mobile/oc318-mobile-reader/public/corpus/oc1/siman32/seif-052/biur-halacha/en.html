And he shall make a knot, etc. — See Petach HaDvir, who brought in the name of Ba'al HaIttur that it is a mitzvah that it be a knot and not a bow; see there. Recently some have come who make a knot that can be slipped off here and there in order to lessen trouble if they want to shrink or enlarge it — they do not act well, for such a knot is not better than a bow, as is plainly evident above.
