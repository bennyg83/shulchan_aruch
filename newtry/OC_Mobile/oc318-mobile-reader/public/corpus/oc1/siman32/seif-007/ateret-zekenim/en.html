Tefillin on the parchment, etc., and a sign for the matter: "the honor of the the Omnipresent is hidden" — meaning that tefillin and mezuzah, which are the honor of the the Omnipresent, are written on the hidden side of the parchment, and the dukhestos — that is, where they are glued together; if one reversed them they are invalid, as is the view of all the decisors; some wrote that they are valid.
