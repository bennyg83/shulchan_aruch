This is so even though at the outset those passages were not written in order to be joined to these passages, but to other passages. But if there is doubt whether they were written earlier or not, it is invalid. Avodas HaGershuni, siman 60; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos Tur; Pri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav, letter 1; Yeshuos Yaakov, letter 8; Shaarei Teshuvah, letter 2; Maamar Mordechai, letter 1; Meleches Shamayim, rule 8, letter 2, in Chochmah; Keset HaSofer, siman 9, letter 2, in Lishkas HaSofer; Imrei Shefer, rule 20, letter 1. Although Chikrei Lev in Orach Chaim, siman 7, wrote that there is room to challenge Avodas HaGershuni, nevertheless, since all the later authorities mentioned agree with him, this is how we rule.
