33) There, and on the flesh side one scrapes a great deal, etc. One must take care that before writing on the hide, no membrane from the flesh-side portion remains, for that is duchsustus. This is not only for tefillin, where it is forbidden to write on duchsustus, but even for a mezuzah, where the mitzvah is to write on duchsustus as explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 288. Even so, no thin membrane may remain on the hide, for with duchsustus one must write on the hair side; if he writes on that membrane, he is writing on the flesh side of the duchsustus. Machatzis HaShekel, letter 10. A sign for this is anything that can be peeled and separated with a needle, even a peel as thin as a hair; it is residue of duchsustus. Even if one letter is written on it, it is invalid. Keset HaSofer, siman 2, letter 9; Meleches Shamayim, klal 2, letter 2, in Chochmah.
