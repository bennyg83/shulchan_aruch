Divided. See Taz. He explains that perfect writing is required even in the thickness of the letter. Do not say that this applies only where the letter is split across its width or along its side, etc. Regarding what he wrote that it should not be visible against the sun, the Magen Avraham in the name of the Bach disagrees. This means that even if the ink fell in that place and there is an actual hole, if this occurred after the writing its law is as in seif 16; see there. However, if it is at the side or split across its width, perhaps the Bach would agree in this case. See what I wrote in seif 15.
What is written. The Shulchan Aruch states that careful scribes, etc. In the Levush it says that our scribes correct this by leaving margins at the end of the shorter parshiyos; see there. See seif 33, that he should make the lines equal. If he shortened the first line and the second line is long, and in the empty part of one line there is space for nine letters, it appears like a parsha break, and it seems invalid even after the fact. This is unlike when the lines are equal, where the margin does no harm even if it is more than seventy letters. This will be explained further below with the help of Heaven.
