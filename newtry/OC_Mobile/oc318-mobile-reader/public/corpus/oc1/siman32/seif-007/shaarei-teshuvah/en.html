To its fellow. See Baer Heitev. This is specifically when it adhered at its end, but if it adhered at its head or middle, Radach agrees that it is invalid even in such a case; see Knesses HaGedolah. What he wrote that only one tag adhered to its fellow means that the tag of one word touches the tag of the adjacent word and they adhered; in this the Meiri validates, since it is evident that the letter itself is not attached. If tags within one word touch from one letter to another, it appears to have the same law according to the Meiri. The Peri Chadash also agrees that one must certainly scrape the adhesion, but the writing below was not invalidated as out of order before the scraping; this requires study.
