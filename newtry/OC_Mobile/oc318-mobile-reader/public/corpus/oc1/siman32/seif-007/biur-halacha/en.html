**And not on the gvil** — Gvil is hide not split — only hair removed and prepared there; flesh side nothing removed.

**And if he changed, invalid** — Applies to all; even yud's tip not on parchment but on flesh, e.g. one place did not remove duchsustos — invalid; see below.

**And they write on them toward flesh** — Beware not to err writing toward hair — delays bedieved. Sign after processing: which side is near flesh — see which side parchment curls when moistened, that is flesh side. Still do not invalidate old tefillin from this sign — we assume expert scribe wrote on presumption of expertise; rely on Mordechai validating if written toward hair. Eliyahu Rabbah.

**To repair and smooth it** — If scraped more, see Neshamat Adam klal 13 — not called "piece of parchment"; Chatam Sofer ch. 3 seems lenient.

**And on the flesh side they scrape much** — Our sign to know that all duchsustos is removed is accepted among scribes: wherever it remains on parchment it is visible to all, for that spot is smooth while the rest is not; that smooth spot can be peeled with a knife or needle. Bedieved if not scraped so much, see Machatzit HaShekel — he is strict; likewise Mishkanot Yaakov and Neshamat Adam klal 13 — do not invalidate tefillin bedieved, since at least the thin peel near flesh was removed and duchsustos certainly applied there; likewise Chasam Sofer and Chesed LeAvraham are lenient. Though one need not protest one who is lenient bedieved, he has whom to rely on — it is very proper to warn scribes lechatchilah not to leave any membrane on the parchment before writing, even thin as a hair-thread, for all this is duchsustos concern.
