17 — And not on gevil. Shabbat there; and Tosafot Menachot 35a, s.v. shin, etc.

And if, etc. It refers to all of them, as written there and in Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Megillah: the law that they write in ink on ruled hide, wrap in hair, coat in cloth, glue with glue, sew with sinews, write on gevil where hair was and on klaf where the duchsustus was; if he changed — invalid; so in Mishnat Sofrim, chapter 1.

What if, etc. And klaf, etc.; Tosafot on Shabbat there.
