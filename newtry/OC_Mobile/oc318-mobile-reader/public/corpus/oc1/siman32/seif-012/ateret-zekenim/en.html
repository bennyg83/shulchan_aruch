The parchment must be from the hide of a pure animal (as above). It is a mitzvah of choice to write tefillin on the hide of a stillborn fetus, since no work of use was done with it, and it is properly called hide — it is no worse than the hide of a pure bird, which is valid; one may also make head tefillin from stillborn hide because of its thinness, more than thick hide (Sefer HaMitzvot siman 13).
