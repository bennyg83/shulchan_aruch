The parchment shall be from animal hide, etc. — Choice bird; after it, chayah; after it, beheimah; a fetus is better, not yet having entered the world [acharonim].

The pure ones — A pure animal born of an impure one: see responsum of Yad Eliyahu, siman 2, who extended to be strict; but an impure one born of a pure one — one may write a Torah scroll on it; see responsum on laws of tefillin, siman 36. Hide of issur hana'ah: see Peri Megadim, seif kaf-zayin.

Even from nevelah, etc. — Even if it became nevelah through others, e.g. strangulation or uprooting. Tosafot, Shabbat 108.

Torat Hashem — Even other books that are not Tanakh, if they contain names that are not erased — forbidden on tamei beheimah hide; if written only by allusion to the Divine Name, one may say it is permitted; so too Shas and poskim in such a case are permitted [Peri Megadim].
