The parchment must be from the hide of an animal, etc. Bird parchment is superior, after it wild animal parchment, and after it domesticated animal parchment. A fetus is better, for it has not yet emerged into the air of the world. Acharonim.

Even from a carcass, etc. Even if it became a carcass through others, such as animals whose throats were torn or whose signs were uprooted. Tosafos, Shabbos 108.
The Torah of Hashem. Even other books that are not Tanach, if they contain names that may not be erased, may not be written on the hide of a non-kosher animal. But if those names are written only by allusion as a sign for the Name, one may say it is permitted to write them. The same applies to books of the Talmud and poskim in such a case, that it is permitted. Pri Megadim.
