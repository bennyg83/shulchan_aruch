Torah scroll. Be'er Heitev in name of Avnei HaGedolah and in Panim Me'urot vol. 3 siman 11 wrote that the grandson of the Gaon Avnei HaGedolah challenged him from words of Shulchan Arukh and Beit Yosef here, and Panim Me'urot proved from Gemara Menachot that the law is with Avnei HaGedolah, see there. However in Sha'agat Aryeh siman 47 he extended that processing and writing leshemah helps from severe sanctity to light sanctity — and needless to say from Torah scroll to tefillin, for it seems one matter and helps both from Torah scroll to tefillin and vice versa; and if processed for Torah scroll — helps for tefillin and mezuzot, shekels from Torah scroll sanctity; and if processed for tefillin — helps for mezuzah and not for Torah scroll; and if for mezuzah — helps only for mezuzah alone, as Beit Yosef wrote siman 33, see there.
