A Torah scroll. See Baer Heitev in the name of Olas HaGolah. Panim Meiros, part 3, siman 11, reports that the grandson of Olas HaGolah challenged him from the Shulchan Aruch and Beit Yosef here, but Panim Meiros proved from the Gemara in Menachos that the law is with Olas HaGolah. Shaagas Aryeh, siman 47, explains at length that processing and writing for a more severe sanctity help for a lighter sanctity: from tefillin shel rosh to tefillin shel yad and the reverse, from a Torah scroll to tefillin and mezuzos, from tefillin to mezuzah, but if processed for a mezuzah it helps only for a mezuzah.
