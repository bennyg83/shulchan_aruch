The parchment must be from the hide of a domesticated animal, wild animal, or fowl that are pure — even from their nevelah or terefah; but not from the hide of impure domesticated animals, wild animals, or fowl, as it is written: that the Torah of Hashem be in your mouth — from a species permitted to your mouth. And not from fish hide, even if it is pure, because its foulness is great.
