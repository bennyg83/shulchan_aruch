The parchment must be from the skin of a kosher domestic animal, wild animal, or bird, even from their neveilah or tereifah. But it may not be from the skin of non-kosher domestic animals, wild animals, or birds, for it is written, 'so that the Torah of Hashem shall be in your mouth,' meaning from a species permitted to your mouth. Nor may it be from fish skin, even if the fish is kosher, because its filth is abundant.
