The pure. Tosafot Sotah 17 wrote in the name of the Yerushalmi that even other books containing divine names may not be written on the hide of an impure animal — Magen Avraham. In responsum Rama siman 37 he wrote there are advantages to stillborn hide as mitzvah of choice, and hide of a wild animal more than domesticated; slaughtered animal better than carcass — see there; so too in Shekhinat Hashem-Geshem and Orach Tzedek. If an impure animal was born from a pure one, whether its hides are valid for Torah, tefillin, and mezuzot, or hide of a pure animal born from impure — see responsum Yad Eliyahu siman 2 where he elaborated and inclined to be strict. Hide of a copulating or copulated animal — forbidden to write Torah, tefillin, and mezuzot — Yad Eliyahu siman 35; see there.
