The pure ones. Tosafos in Sotah, folio 17, wrote in the name of the Yerushalmi that even other books that contain Divine Names may not be written on the hide of a non-kosher animal; Magen Avraham. In Responsa Rama MiPano, siman 37, he writes that there are levels: parchment from a fetus is the choicest performance of the mitzvah; that of a wild animal is superior to that of a domesticated animal; and that of a slaughtered domesticated animal is preferable to that of a carcass of a wild animal; see there. So too wrote Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah and Olas Tamid. Whether the hides of a non-kosher animal born from a kosher animal are valid for tefillin and mezuzos, or whether the hide of a kosher animal born from a non-kosher animal is invalid for tefillin and mezuzos, see Responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 2, where he discusses it at length and concludes stringently. The hide of an animal that copulated with a person or with which a person copulated may not be used for writing tefillin and mezuzos; Yad Eliyahu, siman 35; see there.
