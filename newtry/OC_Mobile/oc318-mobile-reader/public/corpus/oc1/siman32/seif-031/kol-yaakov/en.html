If after the fact he scored with other kinds of colors, some validate: Lechem Chamudos, Laws of Torah Scrolls, letter 25; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 13; GraZ, letter 8. But Levush, letter 6, invalidated. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 9, wrote that according to Levush, even if he scored with ink it is invalid, and it is forbidden to score between the lines as well. Keset HaSofer, siman 3, letter 3, in Lishkas HaSofer, wrote about Lechem Chamudos that it is not compelling. Regarding what Lechem Chamudos wrote about Levush, that in the laws of tefillin, siman 32, se'if 6, he wrote that if one scored with lead it is invalid, while in the laws of a Torah scroll, siman 271, se'if 5, he did not mention any invalidation, Keset HaSofer wrote in Lishkas HaSofer there: One can say that Levush relied on what he wrote in the laws of tefillin, that it is invalid; see Beis Yosef, Even HaEzer, siman 125, end quote. Also Meleches Shamayim, rule 5, letter 5, in Binah, challenged Eliyah Rabbah and Bnei Yonah, cited in Keset HaSofer there, from Beis Yosef, Even HaEzer, siman 125, and wrote: From there we learn to here that, beyond the lack of the required scoring, there is also a concern of writing over writing, end quote. See Pis'chei Teshuvah, siman 271, letter 15.
