The writing. Implies one writing from a written text need not read every word; but Beit Din siman 274, seif 2 implies even so one must read — Magen Avraham wrote, and possibly his intent is to be lenient in tefillin; but Beit Chadash ruled even writing from text one must read each word before writing — so Orach Tzedek; so Shekhinat Hashem-Geshem.
