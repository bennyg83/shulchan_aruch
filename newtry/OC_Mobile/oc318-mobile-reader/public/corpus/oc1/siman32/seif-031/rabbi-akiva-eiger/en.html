Magen Avraham, seif katan 40. They validate entirely. I did not find this there. On the contrary, he wrote in the name of his teacher, the Maharit, Yoreh Deah, siman 37, that the one who validates speaks only where it enters the hollow by the smallest amount. But if it enters in a manner that ruins the form of the letter, it is invalid. Even if we would say that even in this case they validate, nevertheless, where the roof of the lamed enters the hollow of the reish and appears to be a kuf, according to all it is invalid. See there. In Responsa Meil Shmuel, siman 2, he concludes to invalidate where it enters the hollow. See also Responsa Ranach, part 2, siman 78, and Responsa Maggid MeReshit, siman 1.
