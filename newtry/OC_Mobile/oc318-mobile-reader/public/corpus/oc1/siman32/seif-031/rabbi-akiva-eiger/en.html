Magen Avraham, note 40: they validate it entirely. I did not find it thus there. Rather, he wrote in the name of his teacher Mahari"t, Yoreh De'ah siman 37, that mekasher applies only when it enters inside the hollow [of the letter], all the more so. But if it enters in a manner that destroys the form of the letter, it is invalid. And even if we say that in this case too they validate it — nevertheless regarding the roof of the lamed that entered inside the hollow of the resh and appears to a hundred [authorities] as invalid to all, see there. And in responsum Me'il Shmuel siman 2 he raised it to invalidate when entering inside the hollow. And see in responsum Ranach part 2 siman 78, and in responsum Maggid Meireishit siman 1.
