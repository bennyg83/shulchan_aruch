Where. Taz; and end of commentary — for in Sanhedrin 47b there is a dispute; and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel: processing leshemah we require for housing hide; and Rambam explains it on parchment of parashiyot or straps — the reason: straps require processing to be soft for tying and wrapping; and parchment requires processing, requires leshemah — unlike housing hide, which is better than matzah hide that is not processed at all, stronger; and how is it possible that when processed it requires leshemah? And Rosh holds the plain meaning of the baraita refers to housing hide, and we require processing and leshemah. See Levush; but in Beit HaLevi he wrote he ruled like the first tanna; and in Kesef Mishnah, chapter 3, Taz wrote only this version; and I say: if so, why do straps need processing leshemah? And Torah scroll and parashah parchment are different — extra sanctity requires processing leshemah; Magen Avraham, letter 21; uncertain. And know: housing hide — if he intended it alone, certainly it suffices; and it is like a doubtful Torah scroll; and also for parchment — only good; see seif 8 from this.
