Where. See Taz and Beit Yosef, for in Sanhedrin 47b there is a dispute, and Rabbi Shimon ben Gamliel requires processing for its required purpose for the leather of the compartments. The Rambam interprets that as referring to the parchment of the parshiyos or to the straps. The reason is that straps require processing so that they be soft for tying and wrapping, and whatever requires processing requires it for its required purpose. By contrast, the leather of the compartments is better when it is matzah leather, not processed at all, for it is stronger; how then could it be that if he processes them they require processing for their required purpose? The Rosh holds that the plain meaning of the baraisa refers to the leather of the compartments, and processing is required and must be for their required purpose. See the Levush. But in Bedek HaBayis he wrote that he ruled like the first Tanna, and in Kesef Mishneh chapter 3 the Taz wrote only this answer. I do not know, if so, why straps require processing for their required purpose. A Torah scroll and the parchment of parshiyos are different, because their greater sanctity requires processing for their required purpose. See Magen Avraham, letter 21. This requires study. Know that for the leather of the compartments, if he had only that in mind, it is certainly enough, and it is like a double doubt. Even for parchment it is only preferable; see seif 8 concerning this.
