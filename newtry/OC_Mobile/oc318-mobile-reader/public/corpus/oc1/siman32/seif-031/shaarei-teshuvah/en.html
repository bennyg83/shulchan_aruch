Birkei Yosef writes that Beis HaRo'eh greatly questioned Magen Avraham for permitting scraping a little of the leg of a final kaf that reached the end of the parchment. Devar Shmuel discusses two adjacent beis letters in tefillin that adhered at the bottom during writing so that their legs appeared as one straight leg and it was not recognizable whether it was a kaf or beis. He concludes that one must erase the entire second leg until the end of the first leg and rewrite it. If letters were written properly and afterward separated, and their form remains recognizable, one may repair them without concern of out of order; this appears primary.
