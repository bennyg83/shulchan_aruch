And separation. Be'er Heitev wrote in Be'er Heitev on Sefer HaRo'eh to Maharshaf — wondered greatly at Magen Avraham who wrote one may drag somewhat of the leg from kaf reaching the parchment end, see there; and Dvar Shmuel siman 89 wrote: two adjacent words in tefillin stuck while writing underneath each other as if their legs were one straight leg — not recognizable now if kaf or bet; and the scribe before writing another letter before it took care to fix and separate at the chisel head in their lower adhesion — he raised there that ostensibly one should distinguish Maharadakh's case of zayins made like chet by ink drop — here adhesion does not change to another letter by law but lost letter form so one cannot distinguish it from its companion; nevertheless since by pressure the difference is not evident to the eye — one must erase the whole second leg to the end of the first leg and rewrite, see there. And see responsum Dvar Shmuel siman 265 who wrote to distinguish adhesion letter to letter — safek safek lest disqualified before finishing, and lest fix by dragging is not "out of order"; but if known stuck first — safek dispute whether separation by dragging is like writing out of order; custom not to protest scribes; and in shin separated below — doubt even if separated by a hair's breadth, see there. And in Be'er Heitev he wrote: if written properly and afterward separated and letter form evident — may fix and it is not out of order; so from R' Yehiel Alexandri Beit Yosef brought; and so Yad Aharon; and although Darkei Shmuel wavers — the essence seems as I wrote. And he wrote further in Dvar Shmuel on two words "al ken" written in one word — and already wrote below that dragging some thickness of lamed of "al" and some thickness of kaf of "ken" does not help — nevertheless out of order; and in Torah scroll one may be concerned for hollowing of interiors, see there.
