The Shin of tefillin is a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai, that one should make in the leather of the batim of the head a kind of protruding Shin from the folds of the leather, one on his right and one on his left. The one on the right of the wearer has three heads, and the one on the left of the wearer has four heads. Rama: However, if he reversed them, it is not invalid. Mordechai, Semag, and Tosafot, chapter HaKometz.
