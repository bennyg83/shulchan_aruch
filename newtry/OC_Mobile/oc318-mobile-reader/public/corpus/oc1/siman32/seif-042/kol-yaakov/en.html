42) There, but not from the hide of a non-kosher animal, etc. Even other books that contain divine Names may not be written on the hide of a non-kosher animal. Magen Avraham, seif katan 14; Eliyah Rabbah, letter 19; Halachah Berurah, letter 12; GraZ, letter 14. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 14, wrote: Anything in which the Names are written explicitly certainly may not be written on it; but if they are only alluded to by a sign of the Name, one may say that it is permitted to write. The same applies to books of the Talmud and poskim in such a case; it is permitted. End quote.
