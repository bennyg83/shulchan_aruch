The shin of tefillin is halachah leMoshe miSinai, etc., like a shin — See Mishnah Berurah that even our script, etc. — rather it is an example from Bach; see there. Therefore one should examine what Nekudot Yosef ruled — if there is some gap between the yod and the shin, meaning its lower edge does not reach — it impedes bedi'eved, as in a Torah scroll; for it appears to Bach that possibly only lekhatchilah must one be careful, not for validity — requires study.
