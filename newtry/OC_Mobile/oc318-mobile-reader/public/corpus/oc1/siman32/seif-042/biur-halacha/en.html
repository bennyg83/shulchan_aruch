The shin of tefillin is a halachah leMoshe miSinai, etc.; like the form of a shin. See the Mishnah Berurah, that even our script, etc., is only a model from the Bach; see there. According to this, one must examine the ruling of the Nesivos Adam, that if there is a small break between the yud and the shin, meaning that it does not reach its lower edge, this is indispensable after the fact, as in a Sefer Torah. Seemingly, according to the Bach, perhaps only initially one must be careful about this, but it is not indispensable. This requires study.
