(190) Halacha leMoshe miSinai — and if the shin was ruined and is not recognizable, one must fix it anew, similar to when squaredness was ruined as explained nearby:

(191) Like the form of a shin — and it is implied among the later authorities that our image of a shin that we make on tefillin suffices in their view; indeed they do not require actual satam writing literally but a model; and the custom of scribes nowadays in some places is to be stringent and make them in actual Assyrian script:

(192) Protruding, etc. — and if he made the shin on another piece of klaf and glued it with adhesive onto the bayit, it is invalid:

(193) From creases in the hide — that is, one creases the hide with a plane by folding the hide until the branches of the shin are formed. To make them by press: a protruding shin is engraved on the mold and the mold is pressed into the hide of the bayit and a shin form is produced. See Taz and Magen Avraham, who imply that lechatchilah it is good to avoid this; and in Kenesset HaGedolah in the name of a responsum of Rama wrote that this is greater hiddur, and the simple custom today is to make them by press, because thereby the shin has proper form in its sections and is more beautiful than a shin creased from the hide:

(194) Of the right that he places — and there is no difference in this between a left-handed person and others, for after right and left we follow the ordinary rule:
