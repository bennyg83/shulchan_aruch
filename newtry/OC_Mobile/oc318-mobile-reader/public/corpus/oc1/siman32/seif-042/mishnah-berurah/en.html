A halachah given to Moshe at Sinai. If the shin became ruined and is no longer recognizable, one must repair it anew, similar to the case where the squareness discussed nearby was ruined.
Like a shin. It appears from the later authorities that the form of the shins that we make on tefillin suffices, and we do not specifically require actual scribal script, but only a likeness. The custom of scribes nowadays in some places is to beautify the mitzvah and make them in actual Ashuris script.
Raised, etc. If he made the shin on another piece of parchment and glued it onto the housing, it is invalid.
From folds of the leather. Meaning that he creases the leather with a tool by folding the leather until the branches of the shin are formed. Regarding making them with a mold, meaning that a raised shin is engraved in the mold and the mold is pressed into the leather of the housing, producing the form of a shin, see Taz and Magen Avraham, from whom it appears that initially it is good to avoid this. In Knesses HaGedolah, in the name of Responsa Rama MiPano, he wrote that this is a greater beautification. Today the simple custom is to make them with a mold, because through this the shin has the form of its segments and ornamentation more than a shin creased from the leather.
On the right of the wearer. There is no distinction here between a left-handed person and anyone else, for we follow the ordinary right and left.
