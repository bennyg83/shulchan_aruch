Seif 42. The shin of tefillin is a halacha given to Moshe at Sinai. Regarding what one scholar wished to innovate, to make a shin cut out in the shape of a shin and paste it onto the bayis, I wrote at length in a responsum to refute all his proofs, and to explain the words of the Beit Shmuel, Even HaEzer, siman 130, seif 7.
From ga sak sach the sewing with thread.
