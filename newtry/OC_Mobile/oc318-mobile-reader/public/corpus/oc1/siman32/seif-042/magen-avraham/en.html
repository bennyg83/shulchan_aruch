From wrinkles of the hide. So Darkhei Moshe in the name of the hagahah in Beit Shmuel: one should not change the custom of elders who made them in a wrinkle of hide; nevertheless if one made them with a press or wrote in ink on the white bay we have no authority to disqualify, end quote. In Gittin folio 20 it implies that when pressed on the other side with a press it is called engraved on the sides and is valid; so in Orach Chayyim siman 125 se'if 4. So they practice making the shin with a press. Nevertheless one may say specifically in a metal plate; but with hide that is soft, the surrounding area is also pushed — that is engraving on the inside. Mateh Ephraim wrote in responsum siman 93 that in a Torah scroll, tefillin, and mezuzot even engraving on the sides is invalid; from his words it seems one who prints with ink is valid, for that is called writing — only he did not wish to say so for practice, see there. It seems to me in tefillin and mezuzot it is invalid because one cannot limit precisely, and sometimes the second letter is printed before the first — that is out of order. Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 271 wrote that even a get should not be made with a press, for it resembles engraving; in Gittin Tosafot imply we learn from the tablets that engraving is valid in a Torah scroll, as we find in the tablets — but l'chatchila one should be careful, and so they practiced.

Of the right of the wearer. It seems to me: left-handers follow the world's right and left; the vowel-marker in Semag wrote that of four heads it counts — therefore they make it on the right of the reader; if so we do not care whether the wearer is left-handed.

Four heads. Meaning: the vowel-marker — the reason there should be in writing a sunken shin of three heads as on the tablets; therefore the yudin must not touch the shin below, unlike most scribes who are not careful in this, end quote Bach. It seems to me one should not deviate from our ancestors' custom, for the vowel-marker invented a reason from his heart. In the Zohar parashat Pinchas page 506 other reasons are found for four heads — if so, just as with three heads the yud must touch, so with four heads both must touch below. In Semag and in Shemusha Rabbah he wrote: one shall make within it two yudin, etc., see there; so Maran in se'if 43.
