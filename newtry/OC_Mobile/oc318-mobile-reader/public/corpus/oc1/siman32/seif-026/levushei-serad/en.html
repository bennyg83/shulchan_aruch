(there) For he holds like the first answer — meaning: he holds Tur holds like the first answer, that there is no rationale at all to distinguish between written in validity or not, but Bavli is not strict except for letter touching letter, not for piercing; therefore Tur made the dispute that Bavli disqualifies only for letters touching, not for piercing, even if the hole was before writing, since there is no rationale to distinguish validity or not. For Yerushalmi, who requires surrounding gvil even inside, it is invalid even after writing. Pri Megadim also erred here; Noda B'Yehudah vol. 1 siman 75 erred, understanding Taz holds like the first answer — i.e. Yerushalmi holds like the first answer; therefore he wondered: for the first answer it is invalid only for letters touching, and there is no place for Yerushalmi's law (see there). The error is that Taz holds like Beit Yosef's first answer — i.e. like Bavli, who validates a severed leg; therefore Tur made a dispute because Yerushalmi does not distinguish between letters touching and piercing, since it requires surrounding gvil even inside the letter — clear.
