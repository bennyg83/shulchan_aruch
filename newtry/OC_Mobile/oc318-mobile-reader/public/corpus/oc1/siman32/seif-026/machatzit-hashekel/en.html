(s.k. 26) Scratching, etc. — Rambam wrote… and although the Rabbi marked siman 36 in Riaz's name that invalid… and the Rabbi's language on kaf peshutah: its roof short… Rambam wrote all peshutot must be long so if bent they bend properly, end quote. Why not make this kaf with corners upward but round like reish — if bent it becomes bent kaf; no difference except one peshutah and one bent, end quote.

And Riel wrote there: even for Rosh who forbids lakdor, as in Yoreh De'ah siman 276 se'if 8 in the gloss.

And write in its place the Name — Tosafot Shabbat: "the Name" means the word, as Shakh Yoreh De'ah se'if 276 s.k. 4; and this is correct.

But Tosafot Eruvin… and Shakh Yoreh De'ah siman 276 s.k. 3 wrote in Maharalbach's name: all the more so forbidden to remove thin skin from parchment, for then it appears more disgraceful and close to erasure, end quote; Beit Yosef in Beit HaMikdash on end of 276 in Rashbatz's name — asked whether to peel the Name like duchsustos and store it; answered: l'chatchila it seems disqualified lest the peel not be intact and lead to erasure; also ink is absorbed in gvil and an impression remains — thus the upper Name is cut and half in the lower; even not at a Divine Name one could forbid because of writing on parchment place we do not find except peeling and erasing; he wrote at end scribes do not peel (meaning not at a Divine Name), see there.
