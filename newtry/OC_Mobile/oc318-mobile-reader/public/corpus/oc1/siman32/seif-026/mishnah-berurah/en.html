Adhering. This applies whether they adhere above or below. This is specifically where they became attached during the writing, but if they became attached after the writing was completed, one may not separate them; see Biur Halacha.
To separate them. There is no prohibition of erasing, since he is correcting thereby. All the more so, if the letters were attached to other letters, it is permitted to separate the other letters from them in any manner, provided he is careful that the knife not touch the final point that is attached to the Divine Name. This is from Baer Heitev in the name of Knesses HaGedolah; what he wrote there about pressing circumstances is not correct for one who examines the source.
