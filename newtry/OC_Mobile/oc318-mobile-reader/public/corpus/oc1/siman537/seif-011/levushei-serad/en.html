s.k. 7: It is possible that it is forbidden. That is to say, just as when it was two tefachim deep and he deepens it to seven, he is digging five, so too when it was three deep and he deepens it to eight; only that one may say that the eighth tefach is excessive bother, for he must bend to remove the dirt from the eighth tefach, and he bends more than enough.
