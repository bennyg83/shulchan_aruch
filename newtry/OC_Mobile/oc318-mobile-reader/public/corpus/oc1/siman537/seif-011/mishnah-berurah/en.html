(34) And if it is evident that he intends for the branches, etc. And in all these matters and the like, we require two things: that his intent be for what is permitted, and that his actions prove and make evident that he is doing for the need of the permit, as we have written. But if his intent is for a prohibition, even though his actions prove that he is doing for the need of the permit, and likewise if his intent is for a permitted matter and his actions do not prove regarding it, it is forbidden.
