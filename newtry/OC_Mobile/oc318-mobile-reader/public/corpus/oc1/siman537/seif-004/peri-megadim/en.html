And they became ruined. See Taz; see Gemara 4a-4b; and in the Rambam, chapters 7 and 8 of Hilchos Yom Tov; and the distinction of circular basins: any amount in which the water stand there is enough; therefore it is forbidden to set up more than there was; and specifically also where a slight groove is recognizable, for otherwise it is like something new. And an aqueduct is so called because it is deep and six tefachim wide so that the water course through and pass to the field; therefore, whenever there was a tefach, he may deepen until six; but if a tefach-mark did not remain, it is forbidden. And see what I wrote in the letter after this, with Hashem's help.
