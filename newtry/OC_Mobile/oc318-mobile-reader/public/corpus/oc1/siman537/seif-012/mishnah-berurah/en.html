(35) One may not remove worms, etc. That he removes the worms that fell into the trees: even though the tree is being ruined, it is forbidden, for it is excessive bother.
(36) Nor smear [them]. When some of its bark has peeled, they stick manure there so that it not die; and its prohibition is likewise because of excessive bother.
(37) With oil. That is to say, if they cut off its top before the moed so that it put forth many shoots around the place of the cutting, it is permitted to smear oil on the place of the cutting so that it not die; and likewise the fruits may be smeared with oil so that they not be ruined, for all this is not excessive bother and is permitted.
