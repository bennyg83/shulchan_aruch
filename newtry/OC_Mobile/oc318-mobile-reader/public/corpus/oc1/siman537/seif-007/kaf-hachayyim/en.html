This follows Rambam's view as presented by Beis Yosef. In the name of Raavad, however, Beis Yosef writes that if circumstances beyond one's control before the festival prevented watering, the field may be watered during the festival. It is forbidden only when one forgot or was negligent. See there. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2, writes that Shulchan Aruch somewhat implies that it rules according to Raavad: if circumstances beyond one's control prevented watering before the festival, the field may be watered during the festival when it will otherwise be ruined. This distinction applies when the produce is not needed for the festival. If it is needed for the festival, such as vegetables to be eaten during the festival, all authorities permit watering, as written below, se'if 4. See there.
