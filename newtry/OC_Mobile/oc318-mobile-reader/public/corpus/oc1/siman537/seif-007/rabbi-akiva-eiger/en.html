Seif 7: From tree to tree. Rashi and Tur: in the field of the owner. And Kesef Mishneh wrote in the name of R' Yitzchak Gi'at: in an irrigated field; see Tosafot. And in Beit Yosef he brought the words of Rabbeinu Yeruham that they are close, as is customary to plant them four to a beit se'ah, but not far; and likewise it is stated thus in Yerushalmi.
