(26) They draw the water, etc. Meaning: they make excavations around the tree to fill them with water, and then there is no longer much bother in making a channel so that the water is drawn in it from tree to tree.
(27) Provided that he not water, etc. For even if he does not water all of it, there is no loss to the trees; for even though by watering there would be benefit for the trees, we do not permit great bother for the sake of benefit.
(28) All of it. For since it was already moist with water beforehand, there is no additional bother through the watering.
