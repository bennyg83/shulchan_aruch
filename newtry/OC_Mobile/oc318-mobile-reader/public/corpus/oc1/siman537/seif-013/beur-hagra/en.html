Se'if 13: Moles, etc. The reading of Tosafos, the Rif, and the Rosh in the Mishnah: R' Yehudah says, in a place; and the Sages say; and in a beraisa it is taught: When they said [this] regarding a white field, [they meant] in its usual way, etc.; and it refers to the first Tanna. And all this is so according to our reading as well, and like the Sages.
And some say, etc. Who read in the Mishnah according to our reading, and in the beraisa: When they said regarding a white field, they said it only regarding a white field adjacent, etc.; and like the Rif's reading, except that he did not read the word "in its usual way" in the beraisa.
