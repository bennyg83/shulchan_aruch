Or from collected rainwater, etc. Even if he does not presently draw water, meaning that the place is full, but merely conducts it with his foot, it is forbidden because he will ultimately need to draw when the water diminishes. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. This follows Beis Yosef's conclusion. Mikraei Kodesh, siman 50, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 7, write likewise. Permission to water from a spring likewise applies only by conducting the water; drawing from it is forbidden. This is Beis Yosef's conclusion. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1, and Mishnah Berurah there write likewise. This implies that the prohibition applies specifically when one draws with a bucket or pitcher, which entails great exertion, as the source concludes. If one conducts the water with machinery that does not entail great exertion, it is permitted. See below, subsection 25.
