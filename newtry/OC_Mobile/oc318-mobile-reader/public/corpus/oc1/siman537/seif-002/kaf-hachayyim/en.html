10) [Se'if 2] Whether new. This means a spring that is now emerging for the first time. We are not concerned that its walls might collapse and that one will repair them during the festival, thereby involving excessive exertion. Rashi on Rif; Nimukei Yosef; Beis Yosef; Levush. Levushei Serad writes that the teaching is that since the spring has newly formed, we do not fear that it might become damaged and cause one to bring water from elsewhere, which would involve excessive exertion.
11) There. Whether old. This means that certainly an old spring, which is not now emerging for the first time and is not prone to collapse, is permitted. Rashi and Nimukei Yosef there.
12) There. But he may not draw and water from a pool. This is a deep pit containing collected rainwater, from which drawing water entails excessive exertion. Rashi on Rif; Nimukei Yosef; Tur and Beis Yosef; Levushei Serad.
13) There. Or from collected rainwater, etc. Even if he does not presently draw water, meaning that the place is full, but merely conducts it with his foot, it is forbidden because he will ultimately need to draw when the water diminishes. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. This follows Beis Yosef's conclusion. Mikraei Kodesh, siman 50, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 7, write likewise. Permission to water from a spring likewise applies only by conducting the water; drawing from it is forbidden. This is Beis Yosef's conclusion. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1, and Mishnah Berurah there write likewise. This implies that the prohibition applies specifically when one draws with a bucket or pitcher, which entails great exertion, as the source concludes. If one conducts the water with machinery that does not entail great exertion, it is permitted. See below, subsection 25.
14) There. Even one garden bed, half of which is high, etc. Certainly, when there are two garden beds, one above the other, one may not draw from the lower one to water the upper one. The Talmud there. If they contain vegetables that he wishes to eat during the festival, however, it is permitted, as stated below, se'if 4.
