Se'if 6: If it was, etc. The wording of the Rambam, and it is according to Rashi's second explanation; and Maggid Mishneh and Nimukei Yosef brought it. And this is the wording of Maggid Mishneh: Rashi explained the measure of an amah as six tefachim; and if it was deep, etc., it is permitted to deepen it to the original depth. Alternatively: even if he had not yet dug in it but [only] a tefach, he deepens until six. And this wording is missing from our Rashi, but in Rashi on the Rif it is there; and so is the view of Riva. And Riva follows his approach, for he holds that Chol HaMoed is rabbinic and ruled leniently; and so is the view of Tosafos and the Rosh and those who support them, that Chol HaMoed is rabbinic; and see siman 530 that it also fits well according to the Ramban's reasoning. But all this contradicts what he wrote in se'if 5; although one could distinguish, it does not appear [correct].
