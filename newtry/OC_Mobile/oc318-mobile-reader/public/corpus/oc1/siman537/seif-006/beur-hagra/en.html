(seif 6) If it was, etc. — the Rambam's wording, and it is Rashi's second explanation; and Maggid Mishneh and Nissim brought it, and Maggid Mishneh said: Rashi explained the measure of an amah and nine handbreadths; and if it was deep, etc., it is permitted to deepen it like the first depth. Or: even if he had not yet dug in it but only a handbreadth — deepen until six handbreadths minus one — this wording is in our Rashi and in Rashi on the Rif it is there; and so is Riva's view, and Riva follows his method that he holds Chol HaMoed is d'rabbanan and rules leniently; and so is the view of Tosafot and Rosh and their supporters that Chol HaMoed is d'rabbanan; and see siman 530 that it also fits Ramban's view, but all this contradicts what he wrote in seif 5 — although one could distinguish, it does not appear so.
