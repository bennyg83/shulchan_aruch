Se'if 9: And if it is evident. For we require two things: that his doing be for that [purpose], and that is what [the text] said, "It is forbidden," etc.; and also that it be evident. And so it appears from the Gemara: "This one who...," etc., "How so?," etc.; and since it said "this one," etc., and then said "How so?," etc., it appears that we require two things. And likewise regarding a convert's property we require two things; and not as Maggid Mishneh wrote at first. And so it is written in Tur Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat, siman 275.
For example, etc. Tur; and see Tosafos there, s.v. "mound," etc.; and so wrote Nimukei Yosef, and according to Rashi's reading and his explanation. But the reading of the Rif, Rambam, and Rosh is the opposite; and so it is written in Tur Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat there.
