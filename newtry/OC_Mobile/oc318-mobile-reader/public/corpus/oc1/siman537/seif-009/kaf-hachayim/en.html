9) A field that has not been sown and is constantly drenched in water in order to sow it is forbidden to drench at the time of the year if he loses the dredging he did before the dredging, since there are no seeds in it, it is not a loss, since he can return to dredging whatever he wants after the dredging, and it is only as late as its fruits and assumes the fragrance and praise that could have made it better.
