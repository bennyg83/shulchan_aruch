Se'if 14: And if he brought them in, etc. So explained Nimukei Yosef from what is written there, "And if they came," etc.
Even on Shabbos, even, etc. Tur; and he explained from what is written there, "R' Elazar on Shabbos," etc., as referring to the opening clause, where he was not a Shabbos hire, etc.
Provided, etc. For wherever it is forbidden to do, it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to do it, as written in chapter 2. Nimukei Yosef.
They assist, etc., on Chol HaMoed. According to the reading of se'if 1: If he was a hire, etc., he works on Shabbos and Yom Tov, and they assist him on the moed, and they hand over to them, etc.; and so it is written in the Tur. Except that the Tur explained that "they hand over" also refers to Shabbos and Yom Tov; but the Shulchan Aruch holds that it refers to Chol HaMoed alone, while on Shabbos and Yom Tov there is no distinction whether [he is hired] for a year or not. And that which [the text] said, "he works on Shabbos and Yom Tov," means of his own accord; and see siman 244, se'if 5. And there is another approach of the Rambam, who explains the words of the teacher as referring to the latter clause, "If he was a hire," etc., and he explained [it] of the wage for which they hire them, unlike Nimukei Yosef; but he omitted the law of Shabbos and Yom Tov, and Maggid Mishneh already wondered about this.
