[15] [Levush] for behold Samaritan workers, etc. Requires study — from where does he derive this? Perhaps since it was permitted because of the owner's loss, it was permitted through Israelites even when possible through a Samaritan; and sometimes the matter is delayed and will come to loss. Also regarding a worker who has nothing to eat — perhaps it is permitted for an Israelite to give him work even when possible through a Samaritan. And it appears he learned from what Shulchan Aruch wrote this law regarding wine — that if possible through Samaritans; however in Darkei Moshe they wrote for other melachot. And in any case one should not be lenient without proof, for Beit Yosef's words in the name of R' Yehudah imply so somewhat.
