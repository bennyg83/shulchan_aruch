And as to what he ruled above in siman 533, se'if 4, that one may trap fish because it is possible that he will eat of them on the moed, and here he wrote "to eat them on the moed": this case is different, for in the end the field also drinks from it, and therefore he requires that he specifically eat [of them], which is not so there; and see Magen Avraham.
