Se'if 8: In order to eat them on the moed. As written there, 11a, that initially it was with intent to eat, etc.; and even according to the latter version, nevertheless intent to eat is required. And furthermore, there trapping is otherwise permitted because it resembles a davar ha'aved; rather it is because of salting that he is particular, for that is melacha and bother, and as the Rosh wrote there in the name of the Ra'avad in the second answer; and so wrote Nimukei Yosef. But otherwise it is specifically for the need of the moed, as written regarding crushed [grain]: for the need of the moed; and they distinguished only regarding publicity.
