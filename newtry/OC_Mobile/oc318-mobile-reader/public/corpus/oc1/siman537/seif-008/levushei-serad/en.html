s.k. 2: And he may draw from the river [or] from a well of which they said that it does not fail: that is to say, that it never fails at any time, but rather he always draws from the large river, that is to say from the pools. But if they failed at some time, even though now there is an abundance of water in them such that throughout all of Chol HaMoed he will not need to draw with a bucket, it is forbidden, because [Chazal] did not distinguish: perhaps it will seem to him that there is enough water in it for all of Chol HaMoed, when in truth it is not so, and he will come to draw with a bucket.
