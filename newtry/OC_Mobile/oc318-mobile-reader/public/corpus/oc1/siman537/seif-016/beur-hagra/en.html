Se'if 16: One who, etc., may harvest it. From the incident of R' Yannai in the Gemara, and in the Yerushalmi there on the Mishnah: And likewise one whose wine is in the pit. Said R' Zeira: He said only "And likewise one whose wine is in the pit"; implying that initially it is forbidden. What case are we dealing with? If his time to harvest had arrived and he did not harvest, he has sinned against himself; if his time to harvest had not arrived, he could have waited. Rather, we are dealing where his time to harvest had arrived; he thought he could leave it standing, and it did not stand. Like this: R' Akiva of the house of R' Yannai harvested his vineyard on the moed, etc.
And to tread the wine. Even though in the aforementioned Yerushalmi they said that initially it is forbidden, that is when it can keep; and see the Rosh; and this requires further study.
And to make, etc. From what is written in the Gemara there: They pitch barrels, etc.; and see siman 538, se'if 1. And the Mordechai wrote: Even according to the one who forbids, that is specifically with a whole barrel, and they pitch from the inside so that the wine and the beer will be strengthened; but repairing it is certainly permitted, for it is a davar ha'aved; and all the more so according to the one who permits.
