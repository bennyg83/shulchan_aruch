An aqueduct. See Taz; and it implies that even if before the moed it was only a tefach deep, nevertheless he deepens it on the moed until six, for with less than this water do not course; and according to Rashi's second explanation in Moed Katan 4b; and so ruled the Rambam in the Mishnah commentary; and a "ruined" [channel] that was not made properly before the moed; and see Levush. And prima facie it implies from all these that specifically when it is a davar ha'aved, that the trees dried or the field [would be lost], is it permitted regarding a ruined [channel], and not [to make] something new, for that is excessive bother; and this requires further study in the Ran, where prima facie it implies there because it was not a davar ha'aved, for if so, how may one repair a ruined [channel]? And all the more so according to the one who holds Chol HaMoed is forbidden according to the Torah. And see Magen Avraham 7; and with Hashem's help there it will be explained further.
