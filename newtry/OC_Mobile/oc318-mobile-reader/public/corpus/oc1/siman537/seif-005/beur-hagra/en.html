Se'if 5: And they became ruined. Rambam and Tur; and Maggid Mishneh wrote that this means they were closed up completely, [or] not; and so wrote Nimukei Yosef in the name of the Ra'avad, that this is like something new, as with an aqueduct; and see what I wrote below.
But it is forbidden, etc. For they learned from the law of the aqueduct in se'if 6; and as Rashi explained there, 4b, s.v. "for if," etc.; and the same applies here. And Nimukei Yosef wrote in the name of the Ra'avad: and here one does not need it to be a tefach deep as with an aqueduct, for there we need a place for water to pass through in a torrent, but here its waters stand still, and anything [of depth] suffices, only that it not be closed up completely, as above; and see se'if 6.
