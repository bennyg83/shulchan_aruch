10) [Se'if 2] Whether new. This means a spring that is now emerging for the first time. We are not concerned that its walls might collapse and that one will repair them during the festival, thereby involving excessive exertion. Rashi on Rif; Nimukei Yosef; Beis Yosef; Levush. Levushei Serad writes that the teaching is that since the spring has newly formed, we do not fear that it might become damaged and cause one to bring water from elsewhere, which would involve excessive exertion.
