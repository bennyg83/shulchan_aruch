(seif 1) The mitzvah of Yom Tov to apportion it, etc. NB: It would seem by law that if one is obligated to immerse on the festival—see Responsa, part 1, that it is not practiced in this time. In my humble opinion it is an explicit Talmud in Berakhot 83b that Rav Chama would immerse every eve of Pesach, and the halacha is not like him; and it is difficult, for the halacha follows Rava, yet still without immersion one should immerse close to the festival from the power of the festival; see Chak Yosef regarding what he wrote that one must immerse himself on the festival, meaning close to the festival; and if so it is difficult, for without immersion one needs immersion close to the festival from the power of the festival; and according to Tzelach the question is from the blessing, for on festival immersion one is also obligated in a blessing, and thus it is proven that in our time this obligation does not apply. And if we say not like Haredbaz, only for a rabbinic mitzvah is intent required, it fits that one does not fulfill without intent and it is a blessing in vain, see there.
