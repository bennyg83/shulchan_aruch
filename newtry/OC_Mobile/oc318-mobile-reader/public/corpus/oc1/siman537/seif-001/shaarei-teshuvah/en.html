(•) In Shulchan Aruch (seif 1) davar ha'aved is permitted, and Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Maharam of Provence in a responsa responsum 21 to print books on Chol HaMoed even though he has workers whose wages were fixed for him whether he will have work for them or not — it is not called davar ha'aved because he loses the workers' wages, since the printing work itself is not davar ha'aved; and also if he hires workers who have nothing to eat it is forbidden, for a person is not permitted to do melacha on Chol HaMoed in order to give wages to a worker who has nothing to eat — see there; and Birkei Yosef wrote on this; and see below siman 542. And in what the Acharonim wrote — requires study — end quote; and he means that there it implies one may give work to a worker who has nothing to eat, and so in siman 542 that Maharam's words need an answer; and in my humble opinion it appears that specifically when the work has some aspect on Yom Tov — unlike printing work, which is only for profit; and see in Biur Halacha Moed Katan and Mishna Berurah that I brought in siman 542; and see below siman 545 what is written on this in the name of Mor uKetzia.
