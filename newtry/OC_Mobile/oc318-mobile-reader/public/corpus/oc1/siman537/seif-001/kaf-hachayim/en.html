A) [seif 1] One who must ride on the festival to travel, etc.—but from Eretz Yisrael to abroad it is forbidden to repair riding needs. Magen Avraham, end of seif. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 11, in Hagah Beit Yosef. And this is if not for any purpose at all. Chayei Adam, general rules, letter 11; but when going to profit or to see a friend's face it is permitted. Machatzit HaShekel, seif 62 letter 2; see above siman 531 letter 24 and in Acharonim.
