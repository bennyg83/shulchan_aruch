Se'if 1: It is permitted to do it on Chol HaMoed without change. In Darkei Moshe; and he wrote in Toras HaAdam 54, section 5: And we do not say that he should do melacha only to the extent that it will not be lost; rather he should do the entire melacha altogether and finish it, end quote. In Beis Yosef at the end of this siman, s.v. "It is forbidden to reap," it is explained that according to the Rambam, wherever it is possible to do it with a change and it will be truly as good, without loss, as without a change, he must change; and see Magen Avraham s.k. 16.
