The law of one who vows at a time of distress. It contains two se'ifim.
An individual who fasts on account of any distress that occurred, or on account of a sick person, and [the sick person] was healed or died, must complete all the fasts that he accepted upon himself. But a community that fasts on account of some matter and were answered before midday do not complete [the fast]; and if the Torah scholars and the majority of the community wish to complete [it], the individual is not permitted to separate himself from them.
