1) [Seif 1] An individual who fasts because of a distress, and it passed, etc. The distinction between an individual and the community is because of the burden upon the community. Alternatively, when beit din decrees a fast, their stipulation concerning it is effective, but with an individual, matters that remain only in his heart are not considered matters. Beit Yosef in the name of the Maggid Mishneh; Levush; Taz, s.k. 1; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. However, if he explicitly stipulated this, even an individual need not complete the fast. Levush. So too is the ruling in Yoreh De'ah, siman 220, seif 15, in the Rama's gloss, see there.
2) Ibid. An individual who fasts, etc. The same applies if he vowed to give charity or made other vows. Rama in his gloss in Yoreh De'ah, ibid.
