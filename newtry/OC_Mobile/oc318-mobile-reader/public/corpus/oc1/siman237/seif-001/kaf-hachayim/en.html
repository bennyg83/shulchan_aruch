(1) [Seif 1] The order of Maariv prayer — they say "and He is compassionate." They instituted to say it because at Shacharit and Minchah there are tamid offerings that atone, but Maariv, for which there is no tamid offering to atone, they instituted to say "and He is compassionate." And some say: because they were accustomed to give lashes at Maariv after they sinned all day, and through lashes they are atoned — therefore they were accustomed to say after the lashes "and He is compassionate." And also the reason: because they say it three times at the time of lashes, for it has 13 words and three times 39 words like the number of lashes; therefore in Maariv prayer, which is after the lashes, they instituted to say it. Tur and Beit Yosef. And Kolbo wrote the reason: because we say in Tanhuma — a person should never lodge in Jerusalem with a sin in his hand, for the morning tamid atones for sins of the night and the evening tamid atones for sins of the day; therefore they instituted Maariv and Shacharit to appease the Face of the Omnipresent that He atone our sins day and night — Shacharit in the kedushah order. And they also instituted [to say it] in pesukei dezimra for Shabbat night, for they do not say it — because the limbs of the evening tamid are not offered on Shabbat night, as it is written "the burnt-offering of Shabbat on its Shabbat" and not a weekday burnt-offering on Shabbat; and likewise on Yom Tov, etc. — see there. And Beit Yosef brought this. And furthermore see the reason above siman 235, note 5 and note 7 — see there.
