1) [Seif 1] The order of Maariv begins with VeHu Rachum. The Sages instituted its recitation because the daily offerings of Shacharis and Minchah provide atonement, but Maariv has no daily offering to atone, so they instituted VeHu Rachum. Some say that people customarily received lashes in the evening after sinning throughout the day, and the lashes atoned for them. Therefore, after receiving lashes they practiced reciting VeHu Rachum. Another reason is that they said it three times during the lashes. It contains thirteen words, and three times thirteen is thirty-nine, the number of lashes. Therefore, during Maariv, which follows the lashes, they instituted its recitation. Tur and Beis Yosef. Kol Bo explains that the Tanchuma states that no person ever spent a night in Yerushalayim with sin in his hand, because the morning daily offering atoned for the night's sins and the evening daily offering atoned for the day's sins. The Sages therefore instituted supplication before the Omnipresent during Maariv and Shacharis, so that He atone for our sins day and night, with the order of Kedushah at Shacharis. They also instituted it in Pesukei DeZimrah for Shabbos night, when it is not recited, because the limbs of the weekday evening daily offering were not brought on Shabbos night, as it is written, 'the Shabbos burnt offering on its Shabbos,' not a weekday burnt offering on Shabbos. The same applies on Yom Tov, etc.; see there. Beis Yosef cites this. See another reason above in siman 235, paragraphs 5 and 7; see there.
