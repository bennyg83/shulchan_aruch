"There is no [repetition]," etc. — Rambam; and from here we infer: if he prayed, he is expert and the shaliach tzibbur does not discharge an expert; and if he never prayed, it is optional — for whom does the shaliach tzibbur discharge? Rashba — Beit Yosef brought this, see there. And it appears certainly [regarding] a congregation that were compelled and did not pray Minchah and compensate with two [Amidahs] at Maariv — nevertheless the shaliach tzibbur does not repeat a second time; and even at Minchah in such a case there is no repetition, for nowadays all are expert and the enactment did not move; and in such a case [regarding] "compensation" enactment — it appears to me.

At the beginning of the seif — in name of Kol Bo; and it implies that the morning tamid atones for night sins and "between the evenings" for those of the day; and from Minchah onward until night there is no one to atone, for the limbs were already consumed — therefore they say "and He is compassionate." They also say "and He is compassionate" at Maariv, for night is a time of accusation; and before the morning tamid that will atone they ask that the the Master atone. And on Shabbat night and Yom Tov night it would be more fit to say it, for weekday limbs are not offered on Shabbat; nevertheless one does not say it at Shabbat Maariv. And Eliyah Rabbah [wrote] in name of Mateh Moshe: three administrators in Gehinnom — anger, destruction, and wrath — and on Shabbat the wicked have rest; and see siman 267, and there it will be explained further.
