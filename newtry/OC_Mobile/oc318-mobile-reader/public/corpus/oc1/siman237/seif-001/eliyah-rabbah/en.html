[1] [Levush] "For there is no tamid offering," etc. — according to this, on Shabbat nights and Yom Tov nights as well it would be fit to say it; but per the reason, some say it fits well because lashes are not given on Shabbat, and so wrote R' David Abudraham; and see below siman 267. And in Mateh Moshe he wrote per the sod that three administrators rule in Gehinnom — destruction, anger, and wrath — from the time of Maariv prayer, and they judge the wicked at night for having done their deeds in the dark; therefore they say "and He is compassionate," etc., — "and He will not destroy" — this is destruction; "and He will restrain much His anger" — this is anger; "and He will not stir all His wrath" — this is wrath; and therefore they do not say it on Shabbat, for the wicked have rest in Gehinnom on Shabbat and [the administrators] do not rule; see there daf 96 at length. R' David Abudraham wrote: "and He will not stir all His wrath" — meaning any matter of His wrath; and it is from the language "and to the poor there is nothing," until here. So wrote Kolbo page 33; but in Yalkut Tehillim siman 78 it derives: "all His wrath" — He does not stir entirely, but He stirs partially, until here; and so Rashi explained there.
