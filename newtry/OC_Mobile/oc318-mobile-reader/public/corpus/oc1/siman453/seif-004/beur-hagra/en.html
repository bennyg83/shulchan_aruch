(א) 4) The wheat. As he wrote; nevertheless Rava did not retract etc.

(ב) At least. Rosh wrote: Rava was stringent on himself; meaning: nevertheless although Rava's words were rejected, he did not retract and was stringent on himself; brings proof from there Mar bar Ravina etc., implies other sages did not do so, only he alone was stringent; wrote to guard from grinding, for then they approach water; we say there: perhaps it is different there, at time he goes to guard etc.; so Ran, except he wrote per Rif's view Rava states law; holds guarding for kneading is not guarding; nevertheless law is only from harvest, only before kneading, as Gemara there: guarding initially we require; so Rashi Chullin 7a s.v. for mitzvah.

(ג) And in duress. Primary guarding is from kneading, as there; likewise guarding for kneading is guarding; there in doughs of Eretz Yisrael etc., specifically doughs that did not have guarding for kneading — thus we do not establish prohibition lest water touched at grinding; and Acharonim there; so Ran in the name of a Gaon: we do not establish prohibition; Gemara: guarding for kneading is not guarding — l'chatchila; all this per Rif who holds guarding for kneeding is not guarding; Yerushalmi brought Rosh beginning chapter 3:19:40: guarding of verse from kneading onward, as there: from where we do not grind etc., it says "but you shall guard the matzot" — meaning from kneading onward etc.; see there chapter 1 halacha 10.
