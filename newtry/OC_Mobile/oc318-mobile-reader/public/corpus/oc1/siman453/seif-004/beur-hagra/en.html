Se'if 4. The wheat. As is written there; and even so, Rava did not retract, etc.
And at least. For the Rosh wrote that Rava was stringent upon himself; and this is what it means: and even so, though Rava's words were rejected, nevertheless he did not retract and was stringent upon himself. And he brings a proof from that which it says there, Mar the son of Ravina, etc., which implies that the other Sages did not do so; only he alone was stringent. And he wrote to guard them from the time of milling, for then they bring them close to the water; and we say there: and perhaps that case is different, for at the time that he enters into guarding, etc. And so ruled the Ran; only that he wrote so according to the view of the Rif, that Rava states it as the law, and he holds that the guarding of kneading is not [true] guarding; nevertheless it is not specifically from the time of harvesting, but only before kneading, as is written in the Gemara there: we require guarding from the outset. And so wrote Rashi in Chullin 7a s.v. "for regarding a mitzvah."
And under pressing circumstances. For the main guarding is from kneading, as is written there; here too, the guarding of kneading is guarding. And there, regarding the doughs of non-Jews, etc., specifically doughs that did not have the guarding of kneading: hence we do not presume a prohibition, lest water touched them at the milling; and see the Rosh there. And so wrote the Ran in the name of a Gaon, that we do not presume a prohibition. And what is written in the Gemara, that the guarding of kneading is not [true] guarding, that is l'chatchila. And all this he wrote according to the view of the Rif, that we rule that the guarding of kneading is not [true] guarding. And in the Yerushalmi which the Rosh brought at the beginning of chapter 3 of Beitzah, folio 40, that the guarding of the verse is from kneading and onward, as is written there: from where [do we know] that we do not mill, etc.? The verse says "but," etc., "and you shall guard the matzos," meaning from kneading and onward, etc. And see there, chapter 1, halacha 10.
