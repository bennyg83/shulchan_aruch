4) There. With wheat, barley, etc. Wheat is listed first because it precedes barley in the verse, 'A land of wheat and barley,' and so forth. Barley is listed before spelt, although spelt is a type of wheat, because barley is one of the seven species and therefore precedes spelt, as stated above in siman 168, se'if 4. Spelt precedes oats and rye because spelt is a type of wheat. In the Mishnah there, however, the version places rye before oats. We likewise wrote above in siman 168, subsection 19, that rye precedes oats. See there. See the following subsection.
