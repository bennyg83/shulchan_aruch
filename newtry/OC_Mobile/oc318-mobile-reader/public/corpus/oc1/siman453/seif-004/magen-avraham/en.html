From grinding. Because then they approach water (Rosh); it implies where they grind in donkey-mill or wind drives the mill, guarding is not required; however there too they regularly wash wheat.

Flour from the marketplace. We do not establish prohibition lest water touched it (Shiltei Giborim and baraita); the Gaon Rabbi Yitzchak zikhrono le-verachah of Gumbin, av beit din of community of Lisa: in our country where they regularly wash wheat and soak them in water, forbidden to take flour from marketplace, literally like sunk grain, as siman 467 seif 2; further proof from Beit Yosef siman 442 in the name of Terumat HaDeshen: they establish fine flour as full chametz because they moisten wheat (Eshel Avraham); and so ruled all sages of Prague; once a teacher erred and ruled to take flour from marketplace and decreed a fast on his error, end quote; I heard from my teacher Rabbi Chaim Katz zikhrono le-verachah, av beit din in community of Pozna, end quote; and so in Sefer Tzitz Eliezer — see there.
