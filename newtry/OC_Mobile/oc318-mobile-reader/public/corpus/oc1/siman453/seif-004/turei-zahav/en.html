(3) Flour from the market. The reason is that we do not presume a prohibition; and it appears that even in a place where they customarily soak the wheat, we say perhaps they did not soak it in a way that it became chametz, even though we are not expert [in this]. And in a place where they grind the wheat in mills that grind maltzin (malt), it is forbidden even under pressing circumstances, for it is absolute chametz.
