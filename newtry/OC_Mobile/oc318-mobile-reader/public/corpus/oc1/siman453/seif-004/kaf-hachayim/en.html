(4) There. In wheat and barley etc. — he listed wheat first because it is earlier in the verse "a land of wheat and barley" etc.; and he listed barley before spelt although it is a type of wheat, because barley is of the seven species and therefore precedes spelt, as written above siman 168 seif 4; and he preceded spelt to oats and rye because spelt is a type of wheat. However in the Mishnah there rye precedes oats; and so above siman 168 orot 19 that rye precedes oats — see there; and see in orot afterward.
