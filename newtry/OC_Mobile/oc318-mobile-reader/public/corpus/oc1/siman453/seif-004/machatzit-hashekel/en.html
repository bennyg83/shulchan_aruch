(Note 4) Taste etc. — olive-bulk within time of eating a meal etc. — this is the language of the Mishnah. Poskim disagree: some hold like taste is essence d'oraisa for prohibition; likewise for fulfilling matzah obligation taste suffices; some hold for matzah taste does not help, only specifically in wheat and rice, for wheat draws rice; also rice becomes like wheat and therefore one fulfills; accordingly only wheat and rice; but not wheat and another, such as kitniyot, or rice and one of the other five species such as rice and barley — one does not fulfill by taste; and some require specifically olive-bulk within time of eating a meal [and measure of eating a meal see siman 612 seif 4: some three eggs, some four eggs]; Magen Avraham is stringent like view 3; yet here too there are divided views: some require he eat the measure of a meal, and since an olive-bulk is mixed in, he ate an olive-bulk and fulfilled, although he stopped eating another species, for within eating a meal it joins even if he waited; some hold if olive-bulk within time of eating a meal suffices, if he ate an olive-bulk from it he fulfilled; Peri Chadash is stringent — see there; see Magen Avraham above siman 168 note 15 and laws of challah.
