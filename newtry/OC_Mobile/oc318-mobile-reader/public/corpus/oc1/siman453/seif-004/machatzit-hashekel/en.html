(s.k. 4) Taste, etc. A ke-zayis within the time of eating a pras, etc. This is the language of the Mishnah. And the poskim disagree: some say that just as taste is like the substance itself according to the Torah regarding a prohibition, the same law applies regarding fulfilling the obligation of matzah, that taste suffices. And there are poskim who say that regarding matzah, taste does not help, and it is only with wheat and rice, for the wheat draws the rice, and the rice also becomes like wheat, and therefore one fulfills. And according to this, it is specifically wheat and rice, but not wheat and something else, such as kitniyos, or rice and one of the other five species, such as rice and barley: one does not fulfill by taste. And some say that they require specifically a ke-zayis within the time of eating a pras [and the measure of eating a pras, see siman 612 se'if 4, for some say three eggs and some say four eggs]. And Magen Avraham is stringent like the third view. But even in this there are divided views: some say that we require that he eat [during] the eating of a pras, and since a ke-zayis is mixed into it, he has eaten a ke-zayis and fulfilled, even though he interrupted with eating another species, for within the eating of a pras it combines even if he delayed; and some say that if there is a ke-zayis within the time of eating a pras, it suffices if he ate a ke-zayis from it. And the Peri Chadash is stringent; see there. And see Magen Avraham above, siman 208 s.k. 15, and in the laws of challah.
