Permitted — see in responsum Chaim Sha'al part 2 siman 38: there is one species that looks like flour and is called in these cities saggi; it is made from tree moss of India and is permitted on Pesach; but in the city Londros they were clever to make like it from wheat flour and call it arineya di saggo; and Londros experts recognize it is made from this flour — I heard from a great scholar etc. — see there; and I wrote this so one should know to be careful, for sometimes physician-sages prescribe for one who has illness and pain to eat saggo, and one must be careful on Pesach that it is the one that comes from the Indian tree and not otherwise.
