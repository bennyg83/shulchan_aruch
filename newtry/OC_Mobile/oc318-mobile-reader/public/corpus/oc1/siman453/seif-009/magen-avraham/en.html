A day or two days. Meaning, l'chatchila one should mill two days before the kneading; and if that is impossible, at least mill one day before the kneading. And it appears to me that we do not require a full twenty-four hours, only overnight lodging (and see Choshen Mishpat siman 306 se'if 8, and in the Sema).
Some say it is forbidden to knead. And in our place they are not concerned for this; and it is possible that since they sift the flour, it cools (so it is).
And it heats the water. This implies that heat alone does not cause leavening without water; and so it is implied in the Gemara, that they were accustomed to make flour of avishuna, and as is written in siman 463. And if so, what is written in se'if 7, not to leave many sacks one upon another, that is near the time of kneading; but a day or two before that, it is permitted. And this requires further study, for in the Yerushalmi it does not imply so; see Beis Yosef.
