[Levush] Hard wheat etc. — therefore even if there is not sixty against them it is permitted b'dieved; and it is possible that specifically wheat, but barley quickly becomes chametz — see seif 5 (Magen Avraham); and in my humble opinion it is not specifically wheat that he said, for we do not find in the Gemara daf 40 a distinction between wheat and barley, only regarding moistening l'chatchila; but b'dieved there is no distinction, and in both they are permitted b'dieved as long as they did not split; and so is explained explicitly in responsum of Ramban siman 150 and responsum of Rashba 428 that he permits because we see they do not split — if so perforce barley too is permitted; and on the contrary we find a reason in the Gemara that wheat is inferior since it is not smooth like barley; and for this reason many poskim forbade wheat even if it did not split, as Beit Yosef wrote siman 467. Also it appears to me what the poskim wrote "hard wheat" means more than flour or dough, as Tur wrote explicitly siman 467 regarding wheat in dough.
