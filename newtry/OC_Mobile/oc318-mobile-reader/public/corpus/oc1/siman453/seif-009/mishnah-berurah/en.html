(42) A day or two days — and in any case at least overnight sleep; and even if ground by animals, must likewise wait, for here too flour is hot; and even if he shakes the flour, we do not say it cools thereby.
(43) That it is forbidden to knead — and if he transgressed and kneaded, not forbidden b'dieved; only guard it well from chametz, meaning engage in it with more alacrity than other doughs.
