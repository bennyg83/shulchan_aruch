And also they do not come to chametz — and even if one kneaded rice flour and the like while hot and covered it in garments until it swelled like dough that became chametz — Rashi permits eating it, for this is not chametz but spoilage; Rambam chapter 5 law 1, and so Kol Bo siman 48; Rashi orot 1; and so from words of Tur and Shulchan Aruch who wrote "and also they do not come to chametz" — it implies they do not come to chametz at all in any manner; and so from what is written above orot 2 in the name of Rashi and Levush; and so from words of Rivash 135 ch. 3 who wrote: and it is permitted to make from them every type of cooked dish — see there. And what is written there in Rambam — Raavad's hasagot: this matter is not simple and not all agree to it etc. — that is on law 2, if one kneaded in fruit juice; and as Mishnah Berurah wrote there, that he copied it in law 2; and so Yere'im in Hagahot; and so from words of our master, as he did not speak at all on Raavad's hasagot mentioned, because he relied on Mishnah Berurah; and also because it is true that not all agree regarding kneading in fruit juice, as Tur and Beit Yosef wrote siman 462 — see there; see Kaf HaChayyim orot 4.
