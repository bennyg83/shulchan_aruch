(34) On an animal — for flour warms from the animal's body which is warm and therefore rushes to chametz at time of kneading; also animals are accustomed to sweat under the weight of the load; possible animal sweat chametzes like water; and if he transgressed and placed on an animal, not forbidden b'dieved; in any case must wait overnight until kneading so the flour cools, as below seif 9.

(35) And likewise be careful etc. — also because flour warms; and if he placed [sacks one on another], must likewise wait overnight as above.

(36) One upon another — and likewise one should not sit on flour sacks, as above.
