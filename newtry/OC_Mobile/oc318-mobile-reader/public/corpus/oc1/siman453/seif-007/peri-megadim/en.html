Day — Magen Avraham letters 13, 14; Kaf HaChayyim note 26: l'chatchila we require; b'dieved no prohibition — see there; especially for what is written such in the name of one sage: when they shake flour, no concern; in Magen Avraham see further; siman 466 further laws related here explained.

(ב) I was asked whether it is permitted to make rice flour to sprinkle on a cloth for one who walks among the rows [of grain]. I answered: in time of duress, it may be said it is permitted and we are not concerned for confusion in this, although they make groifen from the five species nowadays (siman 113); and even if one would say otherwise, nevertheless time of duress is different; and there are places where I heard they do this from eggshells ground — and we are not so concerned for confusion — see Maharil regarding rice flour and wheat flour on a cloth after sleep.
