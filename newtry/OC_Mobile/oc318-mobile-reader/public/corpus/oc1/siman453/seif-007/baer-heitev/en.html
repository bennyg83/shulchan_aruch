(א) Animal. For the flour is warmed in the animal's sweat; b'dieved one may rule that animal sweat does not chametz — Magen Avraham; see Kaf HaChayyim.

(ב) Sacks. And if he transgressed and placed the sacks one upon another, there is still a remedy that he leave them a day or two before kneading — Kaf HaChayyim and Magen Avraham this siman; and if he transgressed and kneaded — there is no prohibition b'dieved — Kaf HaChayyim and so Netziv.
