An animal. For the flour becomes heated by the animal's sweat; and after the fact one should rule that the animal's sweat does not cause leavening (Magen Avraham); see Chok Yaakov.
Sacks. And if he transgressed and placed the sacks one upon another, there is still a remedy: that he leave them a day or two before kneading (Chok Yaakov); and see Magen Avraham at the end of this siman. And if he transgressed and kneaded, there is no prohibition after the fact (Chok Yaakov); and so wrote Natz.
