[Levush] And its majority etc. — and so he wrote in Shulchan Aruch YD siman 324 seif 9; and since he did not write more stringency — even if there is not an olive-bulk within the time of eating a meal — it implies he holds like Rashba and Raavad that we require an olive-bulk within the time of eating a meal; however it is possible to push in duress; and in Eliyahu Zuta I wrote that so it implies in Beit Yosef, and that is because Rashba agreed to Raavad; and this too appears to me the intent of Magen Avraham; and accordingly in other species we require a majority of grain. And Chok Yaakov ruled like Ramban that in rice, even if there is not an olive-bulk within the time of eating a meal, it suffices; and in Shach to Yoreh Deah there it implies he ruled so in all species; and so implies Rabbenu Yerucham page 42 in the name of the Rosh, because taste is like the essence d'oraisa; nevertheless regarding fulfilling the mitzvah it is certainly fit to be stringent; and in challah it is also a stringency.
