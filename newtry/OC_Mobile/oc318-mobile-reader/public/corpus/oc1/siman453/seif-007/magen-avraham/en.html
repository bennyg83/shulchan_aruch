On top of an animal. For it became heated from the animal; and further, it is not clear to us whether the sweat of an animal causes leavening. And even though according to reason it does not cause leavening, like the sweat of a person, nevertheless one should not be lenient in a matter that is not so clear (Terumat HaDeshen). And it appears to me that after the fact one should rule that the sweat of an animal does not cause leavening.
