Se'if 7. When they carry, etc. For it becomes heated from the animal, as is written in Niddah 14a. And that which he wrote that there is no saddle, and he said or a thick hide, as is written in chapter 9 of Kilayim regarding bolsters and pillows, etc. And the reason is because they are hard, as is written at the end of the first chapter of Beitzah, "but with hard ones," and as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 301 se'if 1: but regarding bolsters and pillows that are hard, etc. And the prohibition of placing [the sack on the animal] so that it not become heated is learned from what is written in the gloss, "and likewise," etc.
And likewise one should be careful, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 3, end of halacha 1: R' Avahu would charge the millers not to place sacks one upon another, so that they not heat up and become chametz. And the Mordechai wrote that it is possible that this is specifically for them, who soak, but for us there is no concern; nevertheless it is good to be careful if possible. And that is what he wrote: one should be careful l'chatchila, etc.
