Not with rice. For one does not fulfill the obligation of matzah on the first night except with something that can come to leavening; tartarki does not leaven (Maharam of Lublin).
It is permitted to kindle with oils. And the Maharil wrote not to use oil in a place where there is concern that it may fall into a cooked dish, for they pound it in a mortar in which they soak barley; and even olive oil one should not kindle in candlesticks that are used all year, for they become chametz from the force of the oil; end quote. And in Darchei Moshe he wrote that even other oils, after the fact one should not forbid them, even if it is known that they pounded them in a mortar in which they soak barley; for even if a minute amount became mixed into it, it is nullified in sixty before Pesach. And one should not say that it absorbed taste from the mortar, for specifically with a sharp food we say that through pressure it absorbs, as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 96; but with something that is not sharp, we do not say so. And it is implied from the Maharil that it is permitted to eat olive oil. And it is obvious that all types of groats made from the five species it is forbidden to keep in the house, for they moisten them with water before pounding.
It is permitted to keep. And the same law applies that it is permitted to derive benefit from them (Terumat HaDeshen). Beina wrote that it is good to be stringent not to eat anise and caraway until the last day, for it is impossible to clean them well.
