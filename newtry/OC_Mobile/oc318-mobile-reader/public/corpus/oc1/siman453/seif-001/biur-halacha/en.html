And some forbid — see in Mishna Berurah the reason. And we copied here the words of our teacher Menachem in his commentary to Rambam: after he brought several weak reasons for this custom he concludes thus: Afterwards I found written that there is one species called witzash and they are groats of wheat that change in their land in form and nature like zunin; and in a year that is not blessed the crop goes out of the normal way and returns zunin from the language "the land played the harlot"; and in a rainy year the crop returns witzash and they call it witzash parmantelsh — therefore they forbade all kitniyot, end quote there. And the explanation of his words is that there are types of wheat that in a year that is not as it should be change and appear like types of seeds — therefore they forbade all types of seeds.
