This is not explained in Maharil; and perhaps since the rabbi Maharil was so stringent regarding other oils, he was stringent regarding olive oil as well lest one come to exchange it. However according to Darkei Moshe it is certain one should not decree olive oil because of other oils; nevertheless I have not seen the custom to eat olive oil on Pesach. And further it appears to me a stringency in olive oil: olives are crushed in a mortar as stated in Menachot daf 86a in the Mishnah and Gemara, and olives are called a sharp item — see above siman 447 seif 8 and see siman 451 seif 16 regarding the mortar; therefore one should be concerned lest they were crushed in a mortar in which chametz was crushed. And Darkei Moshe regarding other oils did not be concerned about absorbing taste from the mortar, because other oils are not made from a sharp item; but olive is sharp — if so the matter is reversed and olive oil is worse than other oils; and even b'dieved it appears to me we do not forbid a mixture, but l'chatchila it is forbidden to put olive oil into a cooked dish.
