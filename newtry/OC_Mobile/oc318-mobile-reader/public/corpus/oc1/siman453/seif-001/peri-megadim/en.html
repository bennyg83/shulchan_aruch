And there are. See Taz, who brought two reasons; and in Toras Shabbos on the Taz, letter 6, he forbids when kitniyos are found in matzah; and in Magen Avraham, letter 5, he permits: such a small matter does not forbid; and God willing there it will be explained.
And what the Rav wrote that they do not forbid when they fell into a cooked dish, in Darchei Moshe and in Terumat HaDeshen 113, regarding a minute amount: however the Peri Chadash, Chok Yaakov, and Eliyah Rabbah letter 4 permit whenever there is a majority of permitted [matter]. And what the Peri Chadash wrote regarding challah of outside the Land, that one nullifies it in a majority, is not comparable, for there specifically they are lenient; which is not so here, that to nullify l'chatchila is forbidden like every rabbinic prohibition. And drinking coffee: they practice a permit, for there is no mixture in them, and they are not similar to grain for confusion. And it seems that in the sefer Chok Yosef he hesitated to forbid; and see Peri Chadash, who brought Pesachim 40b, Tosafos s.v. Rava; and see Taz siman 463 letter 3; see there. Nevertheless coffee is not confused at all, and one should not forbid where they practice a permit. And the same law applies to what they call erd-kaffee or erd-apfel, that it is permitted; unless among those who smell [suspect substitutes] who practiced a prohibition. However, what he wrote that the coffee is scorched and is fit for nothing: this is not so, for regarding chametz in such a case, like malt, since the drink from them is superior, one who drinks from them is liable to kares; see Responsa Chacham Tzvi of blessed memory, siman 20, that likewise yeast is so; see there. And types of kitniyos upon which water fell: even so, benefit from them is permitted; see Terumat HaDeshen there. And it is not comparable to hard [chametz] in siman 447, which is rabbinic for us, and forbidden in benefit, for there is a resemblance to chametz; which is not so regarding this. And see Magen Avraham letter 3; and God willing there it will be explained. See what I wrote at the end of the siman, God willing.
