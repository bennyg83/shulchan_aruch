And some forbid. Tur's reason: types of wheat mix in them; Semak wrote the reason: since it is fit to make dough from them, one comes to confuse with other chametz dough — all this is only a general stringency and one should not change because they practiced so. Anise and coriander require great examination that there not be one of the five species. Caraway called kimmel: I heard they distinguish: what is called krems kimmel brought from other countries is forbidden on Pesach, seeds resemble grain; but peled kimmel is permitted, for its form is not like grain. In siman 464 Rama wrote mustard — we do not eat on Pesach; I do not know why it differs from anise. It appears the reason: mustard growth is on stalks similar to kitniyot growth — therefore included in kitniyot prohibition; and what is written here that if they fell into a cooked dish they do not forbid — nevertheless if found in baked matzah it is forbidden; see reason siman 460 seif 7.
