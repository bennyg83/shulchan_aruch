They require etc. — mitzvah from the best to take new ones that had no flour, for through laundering concern flour sticks in the sack more, as above; also by law it appears forbidden; proof from Rosh chapter 21: Avi Ezri's practice: they soaked a little bran and rubbed geese on Pesach and forbade, for there are holes in the skin; we say in the skin and gravy; if there is a crack it is forbidden — all the more sacks whose weaving has holes; certainly flour sticks through laundering and does not leave. Also cloths on which they make matzot have this concern; better to make matzot on a smooth table without cloth; every Hashem-fearing person should not rely on laundering; fortunate is one who is careful.
