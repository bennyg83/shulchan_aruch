(29) Flour all year — likewise if he placed [flour] in them once, must permit the stitching (Acharonim).
(30) And they launder them well — and laundering for Pesach must specifically be with rubbing in hot water and not in cold, and also in ashes and beating.
(31) They must untie etc. — reason: flour in the stitching does not leave through laundering, and worse: water enters partially and moistens flour there and sticks in the folds and can later mix somewhat into flour; Magen Avraham siman 451 note 36: also must scrape chametz well with a knife before laundering where there was stitching.
(32) At the corners — and likewise the reinforcement called zoyem [Acharonim]; Acharonim wrote: l'chatchila better to buy new sacks, for even if the whole sack is hard to launder well, lest a little chametz remain in weave holes; b'dieved even if he did not untie stitching at all before laundering and put flour — do not forbid the flour; Chayei Adam wrote: if found inside stitching like dough that dried — even b'dieved forbidden even before Pesach, for crumbs are not called moist and return and awaken; whether shaking flour helps here — see below siman 466 seif 4 in Mishna Berurah and Beur Halacha there.
(33) Patches — and likewise a sack in which he once placed Pesach flour and wants to launder them and place flour — must likewise permit stitching and patches, for otherwise flour in the folds chametzes through laundering.
