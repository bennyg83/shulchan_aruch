(א) (Note 3) Permitted to keep [in the house] (and Kaf HaChayyim wrote: Shulchan Aruch needed to teach permitted to keep them, although their prohibition is only because these are considered grain, a decree lest one confuse with grain).

(ב) And that grain itself may be kept — all the more rice and the like; and one could think rice and the like, being light, are forbidden; it is only a stringency: if he keeps them in his house lest he come to eat, since the prohibition is light in his eyes it teaches it is permitted, for impossible to separate them. Taz wrote: krems kimmel resembling grain is forbidden and selection does not help; peled kimmel Taz permits, not resembling grain — see there; regarding mustard see below siman 464 and Taz here.
