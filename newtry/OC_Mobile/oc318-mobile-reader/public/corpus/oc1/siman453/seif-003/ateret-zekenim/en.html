However one must see that there is not so much [impurity] etc. My teacher ruled that mouse-bitten [grain] is not [forbidden] on account of chametz, as Rashba [holds], and also flour in flour mixes well — not as Maharam Isserles wrote that sprouted grain must be baked before Pesach, for that is not so. And his words contradict what he wrote in siman 464 that flour is like dough in dough; nevertheless every person of spirit should be concerned for himself, especially regarding sprouted grain, and one must guard the flour from the time of grinding until after baking — at least so [writes] Rashi in Chullin daf 7a; see there.
