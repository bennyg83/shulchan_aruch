If one did not separate [from] the wheat the mice-eaten [portions], it is of no concern. {Rama: Rema: And similarly if one did not separate from it the grain sprouts. However, it is necessary to ascertain that all of this [the forbidden portions] is not equal to one-sixtieth of the permitted portions. (Hagahot Maimoniot in the name of Sma"k and Terumas Hadeshen in siman 114)}
