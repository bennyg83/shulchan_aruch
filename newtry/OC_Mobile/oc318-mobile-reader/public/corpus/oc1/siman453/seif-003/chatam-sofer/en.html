Those [grains] on the water — see below siman 467 seif 3; and in any case Radbaz in responsa part 1, second [volume] siman 286 wrote: one who guards his soul should distance himself, because when the measure is lacking the sailors pour water to fill their lack; and in any case it appears to me that one should not take from them matzah for the mitzvah, for it requires guarding le-shemah from when it approaches the water, for it is no less than water-millstones; and so I taught the practical halacha in Chatam Sofer OC siman 134; and see in Magen Avraham siman 466 note 9; and see below siman 467 seif 6 what is written on the margin.
