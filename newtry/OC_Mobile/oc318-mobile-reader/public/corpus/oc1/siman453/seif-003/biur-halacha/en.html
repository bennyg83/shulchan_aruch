If they did not separate the wheat etc. — from this language it implies l'chatchila one must separate those bitten by mice; but in Tur and likewise in Levush it is found that one need not even l'chatchila; and so in Ma'amar Mordechai; and it is possible the Mechaber deals when there is not sixty against them, and therefore it is fit l'chatchila to separate them [Peri Megadim].
