(Note 2) Permitted etc. — a place where it is feared they may fall. Even if they fell into a cooked dish they do not forbid, as Rama wrote (and Kaf HaChayyim and Peri Chadash: that which Shulchan Aruch wrote they do not forbid does not require sixty; but a majority is required at least, for in a minority it is considered a mixture as if in essence) — nevertheless l'chatchila one should be careful.

(ב) Even olive oil — meaning in this there is no concern that they crushed in a mortar in which they crush barley, for it is known there are special vessels for olive oil; from this Magen Avraham proved it is permitted to eat olive oil.

(ג) They chametz — meaning the candles chametz from the force of other oils with which one lit them; and the rest of the oils are chametz that they crush in a mortar in which barley is crushed, and it absorbs olive oil from the candles and is forbidden.

(ד) And in Darkei Moshe he wrote that even the rest of the oils are permitted to hang even in a place where it is feared they may fall into a cooked dish, for they do not forbid b'dieved etc. — so too in Magen Avraham.
