2) There. With wheat, barley, etc. The verse states, 'You shall not eat chametz with it; for seven days you shall eat matzos with it.' One fulfills the obligation of matzah with substances that can become chametz. This excludes rice and millet, which do not become chametz but instead spoil. The Gemara, there. Rashi explains that rice and millet do not produce chametz; if one attempts to ferment them, they spoil. Levush writes likewise. See below, subsection 7.
