It is called dough in dough and is not noten taam. If it was ground also with bran, one could say it does not mix well and one must shake the flour well that it mix well — then it is dough in dough. If the cap was heated in a chametz vessel — Beit Yosef — and mixed with another cap: one could say grinding does not help, for it does not mix well — we do not judge it as dough in dough; so too in Mishbetzot Zahav siman 467 note 8.
