(2) There. In wheat and barley etc. — as the verse says: you shall not eat upon it chametz, seven days you shall eat upon it matzot — things that come to chametz, a person fulfills his obligation of matzah with them; rice and millet are excluded, for they do not come to chametz but only to spoilage. Gemara there; and Rashi explained: rice and millet do not make chametz, and if one makes them chametz they spoil — end quote; and so Levush; and see below orot 7.
