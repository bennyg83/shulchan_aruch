(14) If it has taste of grain — and it implies even if the dough does not have enough wheat that there be an olive-bulk within the time of eating a meal, one also fulfills when eating an olive-bulk from the dough, although the main part is rice; and rice alone does not fulfill matzah, as above — here is different, since joined together rice is drawn after wheat, and when wheat chametzes, rice too chametzes; therefore its law is like wheat; so in wheat and rice; but if he mixed wheat flour with millet flour etc., or mixed flour of other grain species with rice flour and made matzah from it — he does not fulfill his obligation by taste of grain unless there is grain flour such that in every meal-portion there is an olive-bulk of grain flour — then we consider the whole dough as grain and he fulfills eating an olive-bulk from the dough. Some say even in wheat and rice one fulfills matzah obligation only if there is wheat in rice for the measure of eating a meal; in other grain species, even for eating a meal, not — for we do not consider the whole dough as all grain unless majority grain and minority rice or other species, then rice is nullified in majority and it is as all grain; l'chatchila correct to be stringent like the latter view; in duress rely on the first view.
