If it has the taste of grain. Even if there is not an olive's volume within the time of eating a half-loaf. This follows the view of the Rambam, Ramban, Rosh, Tur, and Maggid Mishneh, unlike the Rashba and the Raavad, who hold that an olive's volume within the time of eating a half-loaf is specifically required. In the Magen Avraham, siman 208, seif katan 15, there is a printing error, for he wrote the opposite in the name of the Rashba. See the Beit Yosef. My brother-in-law the Rav wrote in Soles Belulah: Since the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Rashba that with rice there specifically must be an olive's volume within the time of eating a half-loaf, and in Yoreh Deah siman 324 he wrote in his name that all this is specifically with rice and wheat, according to this, with other species one would need a majority of grain specifically, unlike the Olas Shabbos, and this requires study. In truth, according to the Rashba his words are correct, and I likewise saw this explicitly in the words of the Rashba in his rulings on challah, where he derived this from the Yerushalmi. See there. However, according to the Rambam and those who support him, that with rice he fulfills the obligation even when there is not an olive's volume within the time of eating a half-loaf, then with other species it is sufficient to have that measure. This is explicit and clearly implied from the language of the Maggid Mishneh cited in the Beit Yosef, and this is the intent of the author of Olas Shabbos. See there. The Magen Avraham ruled that one should be stringent and require an olive's volume within the time of eating a half-loaf even with rice. This is an excessive stringency against the implication of the Rishonim and the agreement of the later authorities here and in Yoreh Deah, siman 324.
He fulfills the obligation with it. We do not say that the rice nullifies the taste of the grain, as we say regarding maror that it nullifies the taste of matzah. See below, siman 475, and Tosafot in Menachos 23b, s.v. kasavar.
