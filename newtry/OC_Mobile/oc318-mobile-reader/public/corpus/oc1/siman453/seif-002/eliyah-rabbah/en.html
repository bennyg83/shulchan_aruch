But not in rice etc. They call it reyz or hirez and titraki or heydin — so it implies in Yoreh Deah siman 84 and above siman 168; but groifen are forbidden to keep, for they are made from barley and placed in water to remove their shells, and that is moistening in seif 5; and I heard that my ancestor the Gaon zikhrono le-verachah permitted one who forgot and had groifen to sell them to a non-Jew; and it appears astonishing; and it is possible that by law of the Gemara moistening is permitted b'dieved even in barley, as long as it did not split, as Tur wrote siman 467 seif 5; and see Magen Avraham there who wrote that even if it rests calmly it is only a doubt of chametz — see note 9. Chok Yaakov wrote: I heard what they call in this country tyrchin and weitz — it is a type of kitniyot.
