(37) To pick millstones — also to cover all vessels in linen garment or wash vessels well that no flour from chametz of the whole year remain; correct to make a new sack to shake flour for Pesach need, not store year to year the mill sack; all the more not use an old sack even by rinsing, rubbing, and hagalah, as above siman 451 seif 18 in gloss; no distinction whether he grinds for Pesach after Purim or before Purim [so several Acharonim].

(38) Because sometimes etc. — likewise careful when they grind in millstones grain for moistening not to grind in that house for Pesach even in another millstone, for dust flies and mixes together; and even if we say there is sixty, nevertheless l'chatchila careful; therefore make a partition between them from the ground below to the ceiling.

(39) Fine flour — all the more in places where all year they grind in them sour barley (called maltz) and they are still somewhat moist and stick in millstones [Levush]; Acharonim wrote: in any case b'dieved or in duress when impossible to pick millstones because close to Pesach etc., permitted to grind without picking, only bake everything before Pesach [and it appears in any case must shake the flour lest some dry crumb mixed in the flour]; and likewise if they ground for Pesach and in that house they ground moistening grain in millstones — although dust mixed together, not forbidden b'dieved since it is before Pesach, then nullified.

(40) They guard it etc. — or eat it before Pesach; the reason in any case: lest a little of what stuck in the millstones mixed in.

(41) To go themselves — for the mitzvah is greater than sending; in any case fit to place at the mill a Heaven-fearing man expert somewhat in laws, not a minor, lest it come to chametz concern; all the more not seat a child to guard.
