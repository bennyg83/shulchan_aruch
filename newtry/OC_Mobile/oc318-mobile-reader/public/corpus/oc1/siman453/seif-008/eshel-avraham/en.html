The millstones. In Tzfas, may it be rebuilt and established speedily, they mill with water-mills, and before the milling they remove the [upper] stone and sweep it well so that nothing remains of the flour of the whole year. And it happened year after year that at the time of sifting small dry crumbs were found, and they were in doubt whether it was clay from the white earth of Tzfas. And they tested it, and it gave off smoke and its smell went forth like bread; after they burned them it became clear through this that it was chametz nuksheh, but there was not a ke-zayis within the time of eating a pras. See its law in a responsum in Yoreh De'ah siman 59 and siman 74; see there.
