[Levush] And specifically rice etc. — it implies rice with all five species fulfills as taste of grain; and examination is needed, for in Beit Yosef in the name of Ramban it implies specifically wheat in rice; and so implies Levush YD there. Also it is astonishing to me, for Rabbenu Yerucham page 42 wrote in his name: wheat and barley one rubs to come to matzah and chametz; the species mixed with them — but not spelt, oats, and rye — until here; behold explicitly there is no distinction between rice and other species, only between wheat and barley and between spelt, oats, and rye.
