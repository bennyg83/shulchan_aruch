To pick clean. He wrote in Teshuvos Nachalas Shivah siman 12 that thirty days before Pesach it is permitted to mill even without picking the millstones clean; see there. And in siman 13 he wrote that if they cannot easily pick the millstones clean, it is like a time of pressing need, and it is permitted to mill without picking them clean; see there. Nevertheless one should bake everything before Pesach (Chok Yaakov).
They guard. Or Zarua wrote that now they practice to cover the millstones with flax; and according to this, it is possible to permit even the first flour; see there. And the Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Matzas that when they mill soaked produce on the millstones, one should not mill in that building at that time even on other millstones, for the dust flies and mixes. And Chok Yaakov wrote that he has not seen people careful about this; see there. And see above, siman 447 s.k. 20, what is written there. A minor should not guard the millstones (Matzas).
