To soak. Meaning letiah: the Rambam wrote that they soak the wheat in water. And the Aruch explained [it as] placing them for soaking; and because we are not expert in soaking, therefore the Geonim forbade it. Nevertheless it appears that it is permitted to keep such wheat in their houses, since by the law of the Gemara the wheat is permitted with soaking so long as it is not close to splitting, as is written in siman 467 se'if 9; and that is specifically with a soaking or a slight washing, but with a heavy soaking it is absolute chametz, like grain that was submerged in a river (Chok Yaakov). And the Taz wrote that it is forbidden to buy the wheat from the miller, for they are accustomed to rinse the wheat first.
Barleys. The Mechaber's language is not precise, for it implies that wheat and barley are equal; but in truth, barleys, since they are soft, even by the law of the Gemara it is forbidden to soak them. And it is possible that nowadays it is forbidden to keep them in the house when they are soaked, even if they are not close to splitting; which is not so regarding wheat, as I wrote in s.k. 11 (Chok Yaakov). And rye is a type of barley for this matter (Magen Avraham).
