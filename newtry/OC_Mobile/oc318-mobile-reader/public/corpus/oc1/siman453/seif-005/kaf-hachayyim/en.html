5) There, gloss. The custom is initially to use wheat. Wheat is the choicest grain and takes precedence for the mitzvah. Levush. If one has no wheat, Chok Yaakov, subsection 2, writes that according to all opinions one should choose the species one prefers, in order to fulfill the mitzvah of eating matzah with greater appetite. See there. Chemed Moshe, subsection 1, and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2, write likewise. If all the species are equally preferred, one should follow the order stated in the preceding subsection.
