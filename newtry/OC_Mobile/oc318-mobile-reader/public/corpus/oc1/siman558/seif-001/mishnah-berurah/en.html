On the tenth day. And the reason they established it on the ninth is because the beginning of the calamity is preferable.
Not to eat meat. And it is obvious that at a seudas mitzvah all the invited guests are permitted to eat meat, even if one is not a relative of his, for it is obvious that this night is lighter than from Rosh Chodesh Av; for from Rosh Chodesh Av there is some mourning by the law of the Gemara, in that we reduce joy and business dealings, which is not so regarding this, where by law everything is permitted. However, the meal that they make on the night before the wedding, in a place where they do not send sevalonos (bridal gifts) at that time, is not called a seudas mitzvah; therefore one should not eat meat, and all the more so there should be no musical instruments there until after midday of the tenth day (according to what the Rama wrote later). And in the Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote in the name of his grandfather Maharash, of blessed memory, that this is only for other people, but for the chasan and kallah and their father and mother it is permitted to eat meat. Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that it is proper not to have marital relations on the night of the tenth, unless it is the night of immersion, or he is going out on the road, or he has come from the road.

But at night it is forbidden, etc. That is, only regarding eating meat and drinking wine; but regarding haircutting, it is permitted according to everyone.
Because of the mourning of the day. It implies that only regarding this, because of the mourning of Tisha B'Av, we are stringent also on the night after it, for it is like Bein HaMetzarim, when there are people who are stringent upon themselves regarding meat and wine; but regarding other fasts, one should not be stringent in this.
