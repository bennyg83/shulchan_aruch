In Magen Avraham s.k. 1: for by law of the Gemara it is permitted to cut hair and launder — "to cut and launder" is not precise, for regarding this there is no d'oraisa prohibition except in the week Tisha B'Av falls; nevertheless for a mitzvah meal it is forbidden from Rosh Chodesh only what pertains to the meal; it appears the main intent of Magen Avraham is that from Rosh Chodesh there is at least some mourning by law of the Gemara, diminishing joy — unlike here where all is permitted.
