(s.k. 1) Not to, etc. All the invited guests. That is to say, even if he is not a relative and also is not going because of friendship. Even though from Erev Rosh Chodesh until Tisha B'Av one is not permitted to go to a seudas mitzvah except one who goes because of kinship or friendship, each according to what is fitting for him, as above in siman 551, se'if 10.
This night is light. And see in Even HaEzer, in the Bach at the end of siman 62 (and so it is in Beis Shmuel there), who wrote specifically where he has already betrothed, etc. And according to this, even in a place where they send sevalonos it would not be a seudas mitzvah, since Magen Avraham there made a seudas mitzvah dependent on the blessings of "shehasimcha bimono." And not like what he wrote in siman 444. But in the Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote for the practical law that in a place where they send, it is a seudas mitzvah. And so he wrote in the Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 70. And he further wrote in the Sefer Eliyah Rabbah in the name of his grandfather Maharash, of blessed memory, that even in a place where they do not send sevalonos, nevertheless he permitted the chasan and kallah and their father and mother to eat meat, but not other people. And also regarding musical instruments, see there.
