Until midday. See Mishnah Berurah that the same law applies regarding bathing in a bathhouse and haircutting; so wrote the later authorities in the name of Maharshal. And in Maamar Mordechai he wrote that from the terse language of Maran and Morenu HaRama here, and likewise from what he wrote above in siman 551, se'if 4, it implies that even from the proper custom, only meat and wine are forbidden, but regarding bathing and haircutting they were not stringent; and I found that the Rav of Shiyarei also permitted as well; end of his words. Nevertheless, it is difficult to be lenient in this, for several of the later authorities of our time have copied this matter for practical halacha.
