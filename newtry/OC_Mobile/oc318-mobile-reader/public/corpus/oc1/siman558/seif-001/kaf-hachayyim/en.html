1) [Seif 1] On Tisha B’Av toward evening, etc. This is as R’ Yochanan said in Taanis 29a: Had I been in that generation, I would have established the fast only on the tenth, because most of the Sanctuary was burned on that day. It is stated in the Jerusalem Talmud that R’ Avin fasted on the ninth and tenth, and R’ Levi fasted on the ninth and the night of the tenth, because he lacked the strength to fast the entire tenth day. The Tur cited their words and wrote: In our time, our strength has weakened, and even regarding Yom Kippur, for which it would have been proper to observe two days because of doubt, we are unable to do so. End quote. Regarding why the Sages ordained the fast on the ninth rather than the tenth, they said there in the Gemara that they held the beginning of the calamity to be more significant. This means that the fire began on the ninth toward evening, as stated there in the Gemara, and therefore they ordained the fast on the ninth.
