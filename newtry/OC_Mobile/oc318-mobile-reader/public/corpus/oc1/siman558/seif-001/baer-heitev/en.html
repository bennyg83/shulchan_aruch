(א) Not. And it is obvious that at a seudat mitzvah all invited may eat meat — Taz and Magen Avraham. But the feast they make on the night before the wedding in a place where they do not send gifts at that time is not called a seudat mitzvah; therefore one may not eat meat, and all the more so there should not be musical instruments until after noon of the tenth — Magen Avraham — see there. And see in responsa Chavat Yair siman 70. And the Gaon Maharash of Prague ruled: when Tisha B'Av falls on Thursday and the wedding is on Shabbat, he permitted Thursday night for the groom and bride and their fathers to eat meat, but not the rest of the feast participants, and one may not enjoy even other foods. And Keneset haGedolah wrote it is good not to use the bed on the night of the tenth; but one who also fasts on the tenth need not be stringent in this, for some say the tenth itself is a stringency — later authorities.
