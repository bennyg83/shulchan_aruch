(א) Because of this etc. Yerushalmi there: on the ninth they kindled the fire and on the tenth R' Yehuda ben Bava was burned — they fast the ninth and tenth; R' Avin: ninth and tenth; R' Levi: ninth and the night of the tenth; and this is what "some are stringent" etc. — all this is stringency, but by law it is permitted immediately, even things forbidden on Shabbat when Tisha B'Av falls within it, as Maran wrote from Rosh Chodesh until the fast; and there how it falls on Sunday etc.; and in the Tosefta: on the tenth they are permitted in all of them; and in the Yerushalmi there R' Yosi ben Elazar: from when it was Tisha B'Av they would announce: open the study halls — whoever wants, a scroll.
