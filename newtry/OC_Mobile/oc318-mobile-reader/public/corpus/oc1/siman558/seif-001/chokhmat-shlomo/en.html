Seif 1: in the evening they lit a fire in the Temple, etc. N.b.: examine Rashi in Taanit 26b s.v. "he turned the bed face down" — from R' Yehuda's explanation that he had to turn the bed so as not to sleep on it, it follows that according to Rashi, since the sages did not agree with him, the sages hold it is permitted to sleep on a bed on Tisha B'Av; and since we rule like the sages, it is permitted to sleep on a bed on Tisha B'Av per the above Rashi — examine this carefully.
