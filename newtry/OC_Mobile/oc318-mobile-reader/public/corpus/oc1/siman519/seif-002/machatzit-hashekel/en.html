(s.k. 2) So that, etc. It is forbidden to put them back. Even if he opened them for the need of Yom Tov to sell to the public, nevertheless it is forbidden to put them back, since they stand in the house; there is no [permit] because of their beginning, for even if we do not permit the return, he will still not refrain from opening them, since in the house there is no concern of theft. But to open them is certainly permitted, even only for his own need and not for the need of the public; and see the Taz.
