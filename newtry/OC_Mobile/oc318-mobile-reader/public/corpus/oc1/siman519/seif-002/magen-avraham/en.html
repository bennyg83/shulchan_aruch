Provided that he does not wedge it in. Meaning forcefully. It is implied that we are not dealing with a loose [fit], and even so it is permitted, even though on Shabbos it is forbidden as is written in siman 279; on Yom Tov they did not decree, since there is no building and demolishing regarding vessels; and so appears to be the view of the Rambam, and so wrote the Tosafos in Beitzah; and for this reason the Maharil wrote: a non-Jew who brought a sealed letter, it is permitted to open it on Yom Tov, end quote. And it appears to me that if letters or designs are written upon it, it is forbidden, for it is [then] erasing; see siman 340, se'if 3.
