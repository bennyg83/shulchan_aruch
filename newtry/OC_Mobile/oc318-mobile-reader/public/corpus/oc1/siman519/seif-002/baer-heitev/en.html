That he not wedge. Meaning forcefully. It is implied that we are not dealing with a loose [fit], and even so it is permitted, even though on Shabbos it is forbidden as is written in siman 279; on Yom Tov they did not decree, since there is no building and demolishing regarding vessels; and for this reason the Maharil wrote: a non-Jew who brought a sealed letter, it is permitted to open it on Yom Tov; and the Magen Avraham wrote, and it appears to me that if letters or designs are written upon it, it is forbidden, for it is [then] erasing; see siman 340, se'if 3; and so wrote the Taz. And see Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah.
