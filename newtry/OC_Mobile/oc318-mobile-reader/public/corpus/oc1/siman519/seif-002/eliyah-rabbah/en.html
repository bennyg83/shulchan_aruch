[2] [Levush] In a house it is permitted, etc. And so wrote the Maharshal siman 33; and the Taz and Lechem Mishneh chapter 4 — and it is implied even for an individual's need it is permitted; and the Maharshal wrote that he explicitly wrote in Kolbo regarding spices needed for him. One can reject this: perhaps it is not precise, since afterward he wrote "needed for them"; and so wrote the Rambam and Smag. Nevertheless one can say that Kolbo is precise, and what he wrote afterward "for them" there refers to returning it — for it appears forbidden if not for the need of the public; also the Bach's view appears so — and requires study.
