To snip. See the Taz; see siman 322 regarding this. And what he wrote that it is permitted to break a seal when there are no letters upon it: see Magen Avraham here, letter 4, and in siman 340 regarding this; and in Magen Avraham letter 18, [regarding] one who tears paper it is forbidden: the discussion refers to when he breaks only the wax; but if he tears the paper, one may say that even on Yom Tov it is forbidden to open.
