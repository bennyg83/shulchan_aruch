(s.k. 5) Like their law, etc. And the Bach wrote, etc. For in siman 314 the Shulchan Aruch brings two views whether it is permitted to remove the hinges. And if so, according to what is written here in the Shulchan Aruch, "like their law on Shabbos," etc., it is implied that also on Yom Tov there are two views. And those two views stem from the words of the Mordechai. And regarding this the Bach wrote that on Yom Tov there is no dispute, and it is permitted according to everyone. For the view of the Bach is that those who forbid in the Mordechai [do so] because of lest one wedge, and on Yom Tov we do not decree.
And he compares it to vessels, etc. For example a menorah, etc., explained in se'if 2, that we do not decree lest one wedge on Yom Tov (and according to this a reason is needed why they forbade taking [away]). One cannot say that he decreed on account of putting back; even though putting back itself is forbidden only because of lest one wedge, and it would be a decree upon a decree, one must say that regarding building and demolishing we sometimes decree even when it is like a decree upon a decree. For regarding shutters, even if they have a hinge in the middle it would have been proper to forbid because of a decree on account of a hinge from the side, were it not that they permitted their end because of their beginning, even though even when they have a hinge from the side their prohibition is because of a decree lest one wedge. And see in the Gemara, Beitzah, folio 11b.
And he did not look carefully, etc. For he holds that removing hinges by nails is complete demolishing. And likewise if one sets the hinges by means of nails it is complete building. But those who disagree and permit even on Shabbos hold that this is only poor demolishing, since he does not break anything, and even without hinges it is a vessel; and if so he did not demolish a vessel; see there in the Magen Avraham, siman 314, s.k. 10. And since it is complete demolishing there is no distinction between Shabbos and Yom Tov, and whoever forbids on Shabbos forbids also on Yom Tov; and by means of a non-Jew the Beis Yosef permits in siman 314. But the Yam Shel Shlomo permitted only in a case of loss, or when one is very rushed for the need of a mitzvah. But with a knife or a needle it is permitted to open. And it is astonishing that the Bach himself in siman 314 brought that the view of those who forbid in the Mordechai is because it is complete building and demolishing.
