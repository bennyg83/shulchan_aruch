(s.k. 1) Shop shutters, etc. Of a chest, etc. Since we hold that there is no building and demolishing regarding vessels. But when they have a hinge from the side, putting back is forbidden because of a decree lest one wedge it in; and even if they have a hinge in the middle, where there is no concern lest one wedge it in, nevertheless it would have been proper to decree on account of those that have a hinge from the side (even though it is a decree upon a decree); nevertheless they permitted their end because of their beginning, since it involves only a rabbinic prohibition. And as is stated in Beitzah, folio 12b.
As is written in siman 313. And in siman 308, se'if 9.
It is forbidden to remove them and to put them back. That is to say, or to put them back; for whether mere removal alone or mere putting back alone, they are forbidden, since it is a Torah prohibition.
