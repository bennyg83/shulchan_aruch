Shutters. Meaning doors of shops made as a chest, box, or tower that are not attached to the ground; but regarding those that are attached, there is building and demolishing, as is written in siman 313, and it is forbidden to remove them and to put them back. (Acharonim)
And they put back. For if it were forbidden to put them back he would not want to open them, lest they steal; but when the shops stand in the house it is forbidden to put them back, for there he is not concerned about theft. (Magen Avraham)
The side. Meaning that it has a hinge above and below. And the Beis Yosef wrote that this refers specifically to when one must remove the board that has the hinge in order to open; for if one opens and closes while it is still fixed in place, it is certainly permitted, for if not so, it would be forbidden to open the house on Shabbos, end quote. And this is obvious, for if opening and closing alone [is involved], there is no prohibition even regarding [something] attached. (Taz)
