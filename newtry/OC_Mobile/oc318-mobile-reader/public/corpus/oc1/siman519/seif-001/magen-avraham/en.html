Shop shutters. Meaning doors of shops made as a chest, box, or tower that are not attached to the ground; but regarding those that are attached, there is building and demolishing, as is written in siman 313, and it is forbidden to remove them and to put them back.
So that he may take out. And putting them back is also permitted, for if it were forbidden to put them back he would not want to open them, lest they steal; but when the shops stand in the house it is forbidden to put them back, for there he is not concerned about theft.
A hinge in the middle. All hinges were such that one end of the door protruded above like a peg and one end opposite it below, and in the threshold there was a hole into which it was inserted, and above opposite it there was likewise a hole.
