The pivots of doors may be removed and replaced for the need of Yom Tov. It contains five se'ifim.
One may remove the shutters {i.e., the boards with which they close the shops} of shops and replace them on Yom Tov, so that he may take out spices that he needs and not be prevented from the joy of Yom Tov. To what does this apply? When they have a pivot in the middle; but if they have a pivot on the side, it is forbidden, as a decree lest he drive [it in firmly]; and those that have no pivot on the side at all, it is permitted to replace even in a house.
