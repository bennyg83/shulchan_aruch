(s.k. 3) A hinge, etc. All hinges, etc., and in such a case there is no concern lest one wedge it in. And the Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 313, s.k. 9, and this is his language: And prima facie it appears that our hinges made of iron, it is not relevant to decree lest one wedge, for the hole is wide and it is impossible to wedge further. But in the Mordechai, etc., and this is his language: It is forbidden to put back a chest door, as a decree lest one wedge it in. Meaning, lest he set the hinges (meaning that he will attach them with nails), end quote of the Magen Avraham.
