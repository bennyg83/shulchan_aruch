From the side. Meaning that it has a hinge above and below. And the Beis Yosef wrote that this refers specifically to when one must remove the board that has the hinge in order to open; for if one opens and closes while it is still fixed in place, it is certainly permitted, for if not so, it would be forbidden to open the house on Shabbos, end quote. And this is obvious, for if opening and closing alone [is involved], there is no prohibition even regarding [something] attached.
