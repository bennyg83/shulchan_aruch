Like their law on Shabbos. And the Bach wrote that it is permitted to remove the hinges on Yom Tov, for on Shabbos it is forbidden only because of a decree lest one wedge them in, as the Mordechai wrote in chapter 3 of Eruvin, and on Yom Tov they did not decree, as is written in se'if 2, end quote. But he did not look carefully in the Mordechai, for it is stated there that it is forbidden by law, for a matter that has complete building is forbidden even regarding vessels.
