(2) There — called kadosh. For on that day that he sits in fasting he is like an angel, as written in Or HaYashar siman 9, note 4; and furthermore what he wrote there note 8, that it shows he is humble, for arrogance comes from much eating, as written "and Yeshurun grew fat and kicked." And see in Avot HaRishonim chapter 2 note 1, that I wrote from there, in the name of the Rav in Mar'it HaAyin at the beginning of tractate Avot — one who is truly humble will find it easy to fulfill everything and be kadosh. And similarly in Sefer Lev David parashat 32: if a person is humble and lowly, "kadosh" is said of him — and the sign is miltha, for shafel (lowly) gematria kadosh — may the Omnipresent help. Pri Etz Chaim chapter 2 or 1.
