2) Ibid. He is called holy. For on that day when he sits in fasting, he is like an angel, as written in the sefer Or HaYashar, siman 9, os 4. Furthermore, it is written there, os 8, that he demonstrates that he is humble, for arrogance comes from excessive eating, as it is written, "Yeshurun grew fat and kicked." See the sefer Avos HaRosh, volume 2, 4a, where I wrote in the name of the author of Maris HaAyin at the beginning of tractate Avos that one who is truly humble will find it easy to fulfill everything and will be holy. So too is written in the sefer Lev David, chapter 32, that if a person is humble and lowly, he shall be called holy. There is an allusion to this, for the numerical value of "shafal, lowly" equals that of "kadosh, holy"; see there at length. Yafeh LaLev, volume 2, os 1.
