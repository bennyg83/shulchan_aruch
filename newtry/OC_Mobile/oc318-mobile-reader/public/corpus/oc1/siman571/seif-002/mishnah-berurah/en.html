(5) Their law is like a Torah scholar — for there are two matters in them: they diminish work of Heaven and rob the creatures; for even other workers are forbidden to fast, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 333 seif 5 — all the more so with them. A minor boy of thirteen years and one day, and a minor girl of twelve years and one day, who accepted a fast upon themselves — their vow stands [even if they do not know in whose name they vowed, for if they know their vow takes effect from the Torah one year prior — meaning from when the boy became thirteen years and one day and the girl eleven years and one day]; and the father cannot annul his daughter's vows except until she is a girl of twelve years and six months, for until that time she is in her father's domain [and from then she becomes a bogeret and is not in his domain. And even if the year she is thirteen is a pregnant year, once six months have arrived she is a bogeret]. And he must speak to her specifically in the language of annulment [Magen Avraham]. Furthermore he wrote the father alone is believed regarding their years even if he is very small per appearance, and another is not believed unless there are two witnesses. And it is implied somewhat in the Gemara that if their years are not known, his vow is not a vow; and it may be that this is when per appearance of his stature he is small and has not reached full years — otherwise it would be a safek d'oraisa.
