Se'if 2, unless, etc. For individuals fast even beforehand and afterward; and see above, siman 575, se'if 17.
And teachers [of children], etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 7 of Demai, halacha 4: Rabbi Yochanan went to a certain place and found a teacher [of children] looking faint. He said to them, "What is this?" They said to him, "He is fasting." He said to them, "It is forbidden for you; and if [neglect of] the work of flesh and blood is forbidden, [neglect of] the work of the Holy One, blessed be He, all the more so."
