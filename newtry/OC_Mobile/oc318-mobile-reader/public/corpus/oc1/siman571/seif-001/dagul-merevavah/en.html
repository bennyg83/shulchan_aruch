But if they also spoke in terms of months, an intercalated month does not count then; likewise in months of birth a month would not count toward the seven months. Therefore Magen Avraham did not bring proof from the sugya in Kiddushin 39a, which says there, “And in six months does she give birth?” — because one certainly should not distinguish regarding an intercalated month between counting months of birth and counting months of majority. Examine this; there is no distinction regarding an intercalated month between bodily events and other matters, as explained in Shach, Yoreh Deah siman 189, note 13, in the name of the Yerushalmi.
