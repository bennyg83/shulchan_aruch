(Magen Avraham, note 3.) Once six months have arrived. This is proven from the plain sense of the sugya in Ketubot 39, from their challenge there that she conceived while she was still a naarah, etc., and “in six months does she give birth?” — implying that one does not find seven months between naarus and bagrus even in an intercalated year. So too this proof in Levushei Chen, parashat Matot.
