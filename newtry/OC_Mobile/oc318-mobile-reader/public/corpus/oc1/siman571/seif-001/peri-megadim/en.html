Called kadosh — Taz and Magen Avraham note 1 in this siman in the name of Shelah; and see Levush what he wrote in Nazir; see Gemara Taanis 11a note 1, that they answer: one who afflicts himself in impure nazir — but one who afflicts in permitted matters is not so; and Rashi in Chumash parashat Nasso Bamidbar 7, verses 5, 8, 11: even though he brought a sin-offering for afflicting his soul — he did not explain kadosh as affliction, for the simple sense of the verse refers to the nazir; and kadosh in the second sense is that he not become impure for the dead, as the verse continues "and if he dies," etc. — see there; and see in the Gemara.
