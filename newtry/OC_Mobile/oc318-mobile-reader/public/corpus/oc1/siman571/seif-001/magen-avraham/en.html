Called kadosh — for through this his sins are refined because he fasts (Rashi); and it appears to me this is not discussing sins known to him, for even a Torah scholar is obligated to fast for them, as Bach wrote in the name of R' Chananel; and even if he cannot afflict himself — see Shelah parashat Nasso. However on Yom Kippur he wrote that a Torah scholar whose Torah is his occupation need not afflict himself so much over sins and should learn more than he was accustomed; and Tur siman 200 wrote in the name of Ra'avad: a person who can fast one day without great exertion, and nevertheless eats a little as people do — he is not caught by that eating and receives reward, for he shows his soul a little pleasure and does not wish to pain it — as it says "he who is kind to himself," etc.; and books of musar wrote that in the middle of eating, while he still desires to eat, he withdraws his hand — and this is considered a great affliction for him.
