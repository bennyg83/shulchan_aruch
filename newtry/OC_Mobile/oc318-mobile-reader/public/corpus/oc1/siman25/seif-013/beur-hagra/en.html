Se'if 13 — Three kedushot. Textual error — should be reversed (Shelah; Magen Avraham cites Sefer HaMusar 31b): one should not remove until Kaddish Titkabel after order of kedushah — answer with tefillin: three Kaddishim against shin of three heads in tefillin; answer four kedushot against shin of four heads on left — they are "Baruchu," "Kedushat beSafah Berurah," "Kedushat Ha'amidah," "Kedushat uMekabbelin din min haDin."
