The Kedushah of Keser. Meaning, in a place where we say Nekadesh in Mussaf, in some countries they say Keser yitnu lecha, etc. The Beit Yosef wrote the reason, that it is not proper at that time for the crown of tefillin to be upon him, as is found in the name of the Zohar, which the Beit Yosef brings regarding tefillin on Chol HaMoed in siman 31. I am astonished at what the Rama wrote, that the custom is to remove them in every place. Is not Chol HaMoed more stringent regarding tefillin, not to put them on, as the Beit Yosef elaborated on this in the name of the Zohar? Nevertheless, they put on tefillin in these countries, even with a blessing. Yet here, because of the Kedushah of Keser that exists in other countries, should we remove tefillin? On the contrary, in many places where one does not put on tefillin, nevertheless one does not remove them if they are already upon him, and here should we say the opposite? I heard in the name of one of the great men of the generation that he did not remove them during Mussaf, since we do not say Keser. Therefore it appears that one who practices this way and does not remove them has no complaint against him that he contradicts the custom, for he has upon whom to rely.
