(55) Custom — some don't delay beyond obligation — bodily cleanliness per person; if unclean and fears passing gas — remove immediately. Siman 37:2 — most don't wear all day; Ari removed after netilat yadayim; circumcision day — remove after milah (milah and tefillin are signs).

(56) "Three Kedushot" — error; three Kaddishim, four Kedushot: Barchu; clear-speech Kedusha; Amidah Kedusha; Uva leTzion. Three Kaddishim: chatzi Kaddish before Barchu, chatzi after Amidah, full after Uva leTzion. Where orphan Kaddish after Aleinu — don't remove until after it. Folding tefillin into bag during Kaddish improper — must answer Amen with kavanah (siman 56); higher than Kedusha (Magen Avraham) — no trivial use while blessing rabbinic berakhot (siman 191).

(57) Until they return — allusion: "your king passes before them" [Torah scroll], "the Lord at their head" [tefillin] (Beit Yosef).

(58) Place in sanctuary — one who removed earlier should not remove head tefillin before Torah lest bare head before scroll — step aside; arm has no exposure, or head under tallit gadol permitted [Pri Megadim].

(59) Musaf — after Torah returned in sanctuary; in our places after reading — remove after Kaddish before Musaf. Eliyahu Rabbah: Rosh Chodesh — after Uva leTzion before "Yehi ratzon… we keep Your statutes" — not wait until after Kaddish (avoid gap before prayer).

(60) Likewise Chol HaMoed — acharonim: some don't don at all (siman 31) — remove before Hallel, shaliach tzibbur after; Sukkot Chol HaMoed — shaliach tzibbur before Hallel while waiting for etrog. All-day wearers re-don Rosh Chodesh after Musaf without repeat if intended at removal (Rama 12); Chol HaMoed don't redon until evening. Rabbeinu Tam wearers don't don on Chol HaMoed; Rosh Chodesh — after Musaf or remove Rashi before Uva leTzion, don Rabbeinu Tam at Uva leTzion [Pri Megadim].

(61) Kedushat Keter — where we say Nekadesh in Musaf, some say Keter Yitnu Lekha — improper to wear tefillin "crown" then, even during silent prayer; if forgot and started — don't remove mid-prayer (mere custom).

(62) In every place — Taz: custom not to remove at Musaf — no complaint, since we don't say Keter Yitnu Lekha; heard great one didn't remove — communal prayer must follow congregation.
