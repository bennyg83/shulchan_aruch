"From the bag." See Bach; siman 28 (Magen Avraham). Rabbi Yonah (Mahari Molin): forgot, wore head first — need not remove (Beit Yosef 26; Ya'avetz 125), unlike Halachot Ketanot 53 — if only head because no arm, need not remove head when arm arrives. Shulchani Tzibbur (Maharach): teacher did not remove head from bag until arm on bayit and wound on arm; then head may be removed — perhaps another may not remove head until arm placed (Magen Avraham); Ya'avetz 26 (siman 40).
