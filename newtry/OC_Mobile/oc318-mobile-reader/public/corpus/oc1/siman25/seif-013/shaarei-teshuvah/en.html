From the case. See Baer Heitev, and see below, siman 28, what I wrote in the name of the Magen Avraham. The Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Responsa Mahari Molkhin that one who forgot and wore the tefillin of the head before the tefillin of the hand need not remove it, since he has fulfilled its mitzvah, and he proved this from the Beit Yosef in siman 26; see there. So too wrote She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 125, and this is not like the author of Halachos Ketanos, siman 53. Nevertheless, he too wrote that if one wore the tefillin of the head because he did not have the tefillin of the hand, and afterward the tefillin of the hand became available to him, he need not remove the tefillin of the head before putting on the tefillin of the hand; see there. See Shalmei Tzibbur in the name of Maharchu, who wrote: my teacher of blessed memory was careful not to remove the tefillin of the head from the pouch until he put on and tied the tefillin of the hand around the arm, on the biceps at the place of the knot. Afterward he was not concerned about removing the tefillin of the head, even though he had not yet wound around the arm itself. According to the reason written by the Magen Avraham, it is possible that even through another person one should not take out the tefillin of the head until he puts on the tefillin of the hand; see there. See Responsa Yaavetz, part 2, siman 26, which I brought below in siman 40; see there.
