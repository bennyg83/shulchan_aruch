The widespread custom is to refrain from removing one's Tefillin until after the recital of "Kedushat U'va Letzion." On a day where there is the reading of the Sefer Torah, the custom is to not remove them until after the Sefer Torah is returned to the Heichal. On Rosh Chodesh, we remove the Tefillin before the Mussaf prayers.[[There is an opinion based on Kabbala to not remove one's Tefillin until one has recited the three Kedushot and the four Kaddishim, meaning after Kaddish Yatom and this is practice of those who are scrupulous [with mitzvos].] Rem"a: This refers to a place where the Sefer Torah is returned after U'va Letzion. However, according to the custom of our province where we return the Sefer Torah right after the reading , one should not remove them [at that point], rather he should do it like on all other days (The Rem"a's own opinion). Rem"a: The same applies to Chol Hamoed and specifically in a place where they say Kedushat Keter in Mussaf.
