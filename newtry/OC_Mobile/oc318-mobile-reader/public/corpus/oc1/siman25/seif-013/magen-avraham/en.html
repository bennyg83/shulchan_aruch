They practiced. There are those who do not leave them on longer than the obligation, because they require a clean body, as stated by Maharam, Maharam, and Maharai. This is not clear, as stated by Mateh Moshe. Everything depends on the person; see siman 37, seif 2.
Three Kedushos. In the Shelah, page 116, he wrote that this is a textual error, and the opposite is found in Sefer HaMussar: three Kaddishes and four Kedushos, for Barchu is also counted as a Kedushah, see there. In the Kavvanos it is stated that the Arizal did not remove his tefillin until he had said, therefore we place our hope in You, etc. The Hagahos Minhagim wrote that on a day when there is a bris milah, one should not remove them until after the bris milah.
That they return the Sefer Torah. One who removes them before then should not remove them in front of the Sefer Torah, but should move aside. Mateh Moshe, Maharshal. See siman 38, seif 11.
Before Mussaf prayer. After the Sefer Torah has been returned to the ark, as stated in the Kavvanos and the writings. This is unlike the Levush, who wrote to remove them before the Torah reading, for on Rosh Chodesh removing them is only a custom.
The same applies on Chol HaMoed. See siman 31, where there are those who hold not to put them on at all on Chol HaMoed, and the Zohar is very stringent in this matter. Therefore the Rema wrote in a responsum to remove them before Hallel, and the prayer leader after Hallel. So too wrote the Levush in siman 31, and this is the proper practice. See siman 651, seif 7, in the gloss. It seems to me that on Chol HaMoed of Sukkos, even the prayer leader should remove them before Hallel while the esrog is being sold.
They will give You a crown. This is the custom of the Sefardim, and therefore it is not proper that the crown of tefillin should be upon him then.
