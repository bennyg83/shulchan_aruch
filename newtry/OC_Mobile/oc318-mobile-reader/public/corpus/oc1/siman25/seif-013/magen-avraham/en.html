"Custom." Some don't delay beyond obligation — bodily cleanliness (Maharshal, Maharam, Mahari); unclear (Mateh Moshe) — each according to his level (siman 37:2).

"Three Kedushot." Shelah 4:96 — text error; Sefer HaMusar: three Kaddishim, four Kedushot — Barchu counts as Kedushah. Kavanot: Ari removed after "netilat yadayim lekha"; Hagahot Maimoniyot: on circumcision day remove after circumcision.

"They return Torah scroll." One who removed earlier should not remove before Torah — step aside (Mateh Moshe, Maharshal) — siman 38:1.

"Before Musaf." After Torah reading in sanctuary (kavanot, writings) — unlike Levush who removes before Torah — on Rosh Chodesh only custom to remove.

"Likewise on Chol HaMoed." Siman 31:18 — some don't don at all on Chol HaMoed; Zohar very strict — Rama of Pano: remove before Hallel, shaliach tzibbur after; Levush siman 31; see 651:7 Hagahah — on Sukkot Chol HaMoed shaliach tzibbur removes before Hallel while etrogim sold.

"Keter Yitnu Lekha." Sephardic custom — improper to wear tefillin "crown" then.
