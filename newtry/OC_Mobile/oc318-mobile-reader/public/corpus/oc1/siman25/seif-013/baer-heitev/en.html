The world practiced. Some do not leave them on longer than the obligation, for they require a clean body, and everything depends on the person. Magen Avraham. The Ari of blessed memory did not remove them until after Al kein nekaveh. On a day of circumcision, one should not remove them until after the circumcision. Magen Avraham.
Three sanctifications. This is a scribal error, and it should say three Kaddishim and four matters of sanctity. The four matters of sanctity are: Barchu es Hashem, one; the Kedushah of Sefah Berurah, second; the Kedushah of the Amidah; and the Kedushah of Uva leTziyon. In places where they say mourner’s Kaddish after Aleinu, these are also four Kaddishim. See Mateh Moshe, Shelah, and Olas Tamid.
In the ark. A sign for the matter is: “Their king passed before them, and Hashem at their head.” One who removes them before then should not remove them in front of the Torah scroll, but should move to the side. Mateh Moshe, Maharshal, Magen Avraham.
Mussaf. After the Torah scroll is returned to the ark. On Chol HaMoed one should remove them before Hallel, and the shaliach tzibbur after Hallel; on Chol HaMoed of Sukkos, even the shaliach tzibbur removes them before Hallel. Magen Avraham. See Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah. Those who practice wearing them the whole day should put them on again on Rosh Chodesh after Mussaf, but on Chol HaMoed they should not put them on at all, even at Minchah. Rama of Fano.
The sanctification of Kesser. Meaning, in the place where we say Nakdish in Mussaf, in some countries they say Kesser yitenu lach; therefore it is not fitting that the crown of tefillin be upon him. The Taz wrote that one who practices not to remove them at Mussaf has no complaint against him, since we do not say the Kedushah of Kesser yitenu lach. I heard in the name of one great person that he did not remove them at Mussaf; see there.
