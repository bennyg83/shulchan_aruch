Custom of the world. Some do not delay beyond obligation — they require bodily cleanliness, each according to his level (Magen Avraham). The Ari did not remove until after netilat yadayim; on circumcision day do not remove until after circumcision (Magen Avraham).

Three Kedushot. Text error — should read three Kaddishim and four Kedushot. The four Kedushot: Barchu; Kedusha with clear speech; Kedusha of Amidah; Kedusha of Uva leTzion. Where orphan Kaddish follows Aleinu, those are also four Kaddishim — see Mateh Moshe. Shelah and Pri Megadim.

In the sanctuary. Siman 34 — "and your king shall pass before them and the Lord at their head." One who removed earlier should not remove before a Torah scroll but step aside (Mateh Moshe, Maharshal, Magen Avraham).

Musaf. After Torah reading in sanctuary and on Chol HaMoed remove before Hallel; shaliach tzibbur after Hallel; on Sukkot Chol HaMoed even shaliach tzibbur before Hallel (Magen Avraham). See Kenesset HaGedolah: those who wear all day re-don on Rosh Chodesh after Musaf, but on Chol HaMoed do not put on again even at Minchah (Rama of Pano).

Kedushat Keter. Where we say "Nekadesh" in Musaf, some countries say "Keter Yitnu Lekha" — therefore improper to wear the "crown" of tefillin then. Turei Zahav: one who customarily does not remove at Musaf has no complaint, since we do not say Keter Yitnu Lekha. I heard from a great one he did not remove at Musaf.
