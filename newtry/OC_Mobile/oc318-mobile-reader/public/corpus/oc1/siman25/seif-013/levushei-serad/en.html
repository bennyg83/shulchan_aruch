Taz seif katan 9 Like the words of the Rosh, and it is necessary. This is how the text should read. It appears from this that the Taz disagrees with the responsum of his brother, of blessed memory. I discussed this at length in a responsum, siman 40, and therefore concluded there that one should practice like the Magen Avraham, seif katan 9, to say “al mitzvas” with a patach.
