Taz — R' Akiva Eiger wrote first: knot broke before tightening — no blessing; after tightening — bless; then wrote likewise Raavad — tightening permitted before head, no blessing… Seems Raavad contradicts first — how write both halachot? Conclude: in long work uncertain if Raavad disputes above, or knot-making is greater hefsek (Magen Avraham 23) — therefore wrote nothing whether they contradict. Shulchan Aruch wrote only clear: broke before tightening — no blessing; after tightening — wrote nothing. Had he meant to distinguish knot vs. permitted tightening (Magen Avraham) he would explain explicitly — wrote Raavad's law — safek berakhot lehakel even knot after tightening; also if head not yet placed — no repeat blessing unlike Magen Avraham 23; safek berakhot lekula.
