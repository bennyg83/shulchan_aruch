In his house. It seems to me that even if the large tallis came to his hand before he put on tefillin, he need not wrap himself in it at home, since his intent is not to wear it here. Something similar is written in siman 168, end of seif 1.
And there he should wrap himself. So too I found written in the name of Minhagei Maharai. In a place where non-Jews are commonly found in the street, all agree that he should wrap himself in the courtyard of the synagogue, as written in siman 554, seif 17.
