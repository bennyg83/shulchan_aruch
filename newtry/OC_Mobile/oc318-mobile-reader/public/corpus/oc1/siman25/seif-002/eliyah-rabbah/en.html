[2] "He dons tefillin, etc." Chinuch (Va'etchanan 421): man of flesh needs guards — tefillin, tzitzis, mezuzah — Torah day and night lest we oppress. Kiddushin 37b: entry to Land adjoined to tefillin. Rosh Hashanah 17a: tefillin tip scales to guilt. Barukh She'amar: four parshiyot of tefillin lack gimmel and samech — haughty should not wear. Sefer Chassidim 273: don't shake bag — take by hand. Emek Berachah 15: wash where tefillin rest before donning. Berakhot 14b: Shema without tefillin — false testimony. Levush explains like Rashi on God's Name — implausible; verse says "mitzvotai"; rain not withheld for omitting tefillin. Ma'adanei Melech: Rashi means he would keep mitzvot if sincere. Bach: tying testifies to unity; without tefillin seems not one. Tosafot 14b s.v. umenach — testifies God did not command tefillin; Avudraham agrees with Rashi. Levush did not heed Tosafot/Avudraham's resolution. Perhaps Levush should say four-cornered garment without tzitzis is false testimony — "people don't examine" weak. My father: Levush's rain proof fails — Sanhedrin 113 on idolatry; Maharsha — rain tied to "you will perish quickly"; Ve'hayah im shamo'a to multitudes (Rashi Ekev) — if so rain only when public omit tefillin — people might not learn like Ahab — still appears false; truth is Rashi's plain sense.
