One who is careful, etc. This means that the Zohar states that it is a mitzvah to leave the entrance of one's home wearing tzitzis and tefillin. If one is not careful to wear a tallis katan, he must therefore don the tallis gadol at home before the tefillin. If he is careful to wear a tallis katan, he can fulfill this requirement through the tallis katan and don the tallis gadol in the synagogue. All the more so may he also wrap himself in the tallis gadol at home if he wishes. From Gra's explanation.
