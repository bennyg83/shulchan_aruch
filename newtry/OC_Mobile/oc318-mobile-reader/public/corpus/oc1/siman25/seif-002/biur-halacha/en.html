He who is careful, etc. - RL because it is written in the Zohar that he is commanded to go to the door of his house with a tzizit and tephalin, etc. If he is not careful in the tak, he is obliged by this to wear the takef in his house first, but if he is careful in the tak, he can go out on the 27th with a small tallit, and the tak can be worn in the school, and the takif if he wants to also wrap himself in the tak at home [from the explanation of the Graha]:
