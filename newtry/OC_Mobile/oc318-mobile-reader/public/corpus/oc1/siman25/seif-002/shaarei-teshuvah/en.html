With a large tallis. See Baer Heitev. In Birkei Yosef he wrote in the name of the manuscript Responsa of Mahari Molkhin regarding one who had in his pouch a weekday tallis, tefillin, and a Shabbos tallis, and the Shabbos tallis was above everything: whether he may pass over the Shabbos tallis and take the weekday tallis. He brought from Tosafot, Yoma 53, that the rule that one does not pass over mitzvos applies only when he wants to perform both, but afterward he rejected this. The Birkei Yosef wrote to learn from these words of the Magen Avraham, brought by the Baer Heitev, that the same applies here, that he may pass over the Shabbos tallis and take the weekday tallis; see there. There he wrote that according to the Arizal, even if one is wearing a tallis katan, he should also wear a large tallis before the tefillin; see there.
