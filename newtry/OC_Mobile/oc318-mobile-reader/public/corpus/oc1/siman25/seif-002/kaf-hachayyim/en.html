2) There: After donning a fringed tallis, he should put on tefillin. A mnemonic is: 'From a thread to a shoelace'; first the threads of tzitzis and afterward the straps of tefillin. Another mnemonic for wrapping in a tallis before putting on tefillin is: 'The wrapped ones were Lavan's, and the bound ones Yaakov's.' This means that first comes the wrapping in a white tallis, and afterward the bindings of the tefillin are Yaakov's; that is, they come last. Ateres Zekeinim 1; this also appears in Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef; Kaf HaChaim by Rav Chaim Palagi 10:1; and Ben Ish Chai, ibid.
