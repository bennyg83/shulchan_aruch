With tzitzis and crowned with tefillin. For the Zohar speaks very strongly about one who goes to the synagogue without tzitzis and tefillin.
And there he should wrap himself. Although the tallis gadol came to his hand before he put on tefillin, he need not wrap himself in it at home, since his intent is not to wear it here.
To wrap himself. In a place where non-Jews are common in the street, he should wrap himself in the courtyard of the synagogue if possible.
And bless over it. Meaning the blessing lehit’atef; he should intend with this blessing also to exempt the tallis katan, as above in siman 8, seif 10; see there in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 24.
