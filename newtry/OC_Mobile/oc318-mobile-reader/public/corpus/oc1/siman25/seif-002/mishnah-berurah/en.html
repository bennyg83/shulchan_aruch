(8) In his house — leave home with tzitzis and tefillin (Beit Yosef, Darkei Moshe, Zohar — great matter). Filthy alleys or gentiles in street — don in synagogue courtyard; if impossible, at home covered in hat or hand. Acharonim: rising before dawn, coming to synagogue — Zohar warning not yet applicable; when day breaks, better don in courtyard then enter.

(9) There he wraps — even if tallit gadol came first, need not wrap at home if not wearing it there.

(10) To wrap — where gentiles in street, wrap in synagogue courtyard if possible.

(11) And bless on it — blessing lehitateif, intending to exempt tallit katan too (siman 8:10; Mishnah Berurah there se'if kaf 24).
