And there he shall wrap himself, etc. Zohar: bless tzitzit before tefillin — first spread covering on head, then tie knot of unity that tefillin guard His unity. Nekudot Yosef: those who wrap in tallit gadol after all garments — tefillin mitzvah comes first and they bless it first because ein ma'avirin. Beit Yosef's meaning: skullcap-like garment already on head with tallit over it; donning tefillin requires removing tallit and cap — like passing over tzitzit already on head. Tur (siman 8:45): fulfill both by tallit katan and tefillin first, then wrap in tallit gadol in beit ha'keneset. Rama: world wraps in tallit gadol at home too — no difficulty; uncovering part of tallit from head is not passing over tzitzit on him — we ruled siman 8 even if head sometimes uncovered from tallit we do not care.
