And there he should wrap himself, etc. Meaning, the Zohar wrote that one must bless over tzitzis before tefillin, because first one must spread over his head a covering of mitzvah, and afterward he should bind himself with the bond of unity, which is tefillin, that point to the unity of His blessed Name. The Nimukei Yosef also wrote so for another reason, but afterward wrote: nevertheless, those who wrap themselves in a large tallis, since the way is not to wrap oneself except after all the garments, when they have placed their caps on their heads, the mitzvah of tefillin comes before him first, and they bless over them first, because one does not pass over mitzvos. End quote. It seems the explanation of his words is that since the cap, which is a garment like a hat, is already on his head, and the tallis is wrapped over it from above, when he afterward wants to put on tefillin he will need to remove the tallis together with the cap from his head. This is like passing over the mitzvos, namely the mitzvah of tzitzis that is already upon his head. He holds that the essential wrapping of the tallis is on the head, like the wrapping of the Ishmaelites mentioned by the Tur in siman 8. Therefore he concludes in the name of the great authorities that one should satisfy both: wear a tallis katan and put on tefillin, and afterward in the synagogue wrap himself in a large tallis. According to what the Rama wrote, that the world practiced to wrap in a large tallis also at home, one must be careful to take the tallis first, so as not to pass over the mitzvos.
