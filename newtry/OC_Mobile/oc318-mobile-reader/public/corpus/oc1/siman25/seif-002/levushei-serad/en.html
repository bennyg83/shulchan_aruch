Magen Avraham seif katan 1 And so it appears from seif 11. His intent is from the fact that there it mentions not removing the shel rosh from the case before placing the tefillin shel yad, because when he removes the shel rosh, prepares it, and then places the shel yad, he is passing over the mitzvos. It would have been more appropriate to write that before he finishes placing the shel yad he should not take into his hand at all the case containing the shel rosh, and then it would certainly follow that it is forbidden to take the shel rosh itself. Rather, this proves that it is permitted to take it together with the case, for then the rule of not passing over mitzvos does not apply. Therefore it prohibited only removing it from the case, and this is clear.
