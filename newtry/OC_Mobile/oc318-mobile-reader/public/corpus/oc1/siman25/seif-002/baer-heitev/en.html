In a large tallis. Then even if the tallis came to hand before donning tefillin, he need not wrap in it at home since he does not intend to wear it there (Magen Avraham). See Radbaz siman 36. Maharam of Lublin (responsum 34:1) wrote: one bound himself with a fine to go to Tehillim before dawn and now wishes to retract because he cannot go to synagogue with tzitzis and tefillin — he may retract, like erroneous vows. Similarly why the Ari was not among first yods — they rose before dawn and he would not go without tefillin; one could say he should leave when day breaks and don tefillin — perhaps meanwhile the congregation finishes birchot hashachar, so better cancel the former first; Magen Avraham wrote he likely needs release. See Nachalat Shiva 6 (Eliyahu Rabbah cites Kenesset HaGedolah: even with filthy alleys, don at home and cover tefillin — also responsum Menachem Azariah 59 and Zohar Va'etchanan and Shelach).
