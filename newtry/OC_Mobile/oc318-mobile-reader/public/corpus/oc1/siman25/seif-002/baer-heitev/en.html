With a tallis gadol. Then, even if the tallis came to his hand before he put on tefillin, he need not wrap himself in it at home, since his intention is not to wear it there. Magen Avraham. See Radbaz, siman 36. In Responsa Maharam Lublin, siman 34, he wrote regarding one who bound himself with a penalty to go to Tehillim before dawn and now wishes to retract because he cannot go to the synagogue with tzitzis and tefillin: he has permission to retract, for it is comparable to mistaken vows. So too it is in the kavanot, that for this reason the Ari was not among the first ten, because they would rise before dawn and he did not want to go to the synagogue without tefillin. One should not ask that he could leave the synagogue when day broke and put on tefillin, for meanwhile the congregation would finish the morning blessings; therefore it is better to set this aside before that. The Magen Avraham wrote: it seems to me that he requires release. See Responsa Nachalas Shivah, siman 6. In Eliyah Rabbah he brings in the name of Knesses HaGedolah that even when there are filthy alleys, he should put them on at home and cover the tefillin. This also implies from Responsa Mapa, siman 59, and from the Zohar, parshas Vaeschanan and parshas Shelach Lecha.
