(Se'if kaf 2) "Ready," and so in Yevamot. Even if waiting for tzitzis would allow blessing tzitzis before tefillin (opening of siman) — still need not wait — as Yevamot: mitzvah for elder to perform yibum; if no elder, minor performs; minor cannot delay until elder returns from overseas — minor must yibum or chalitzah immediately, though waiting would be "more choicest" — we do not delay mitzvah performance; performing sooner is preferable.

Menachot 72: though tamid limbs between evenings may be offered all night, one may burn Shabbat afternoon tamid on Shabbat though could wait until Shabbat night.

Still Shabbat may be violated — mitzvah in its time is dear; precede the mitzvah.
