(Seif katan 2) Available, and so it is in Yevamos. Meaning, although if he were to wait until tzitzis were brought to him, he would perform the preferred mitzvah by blessing on the tzitzis before the tefillin, as written at the beginning of the siman, nevertheless he need not wait. Regarding this he wrote that so it is in Yevamos, where we learned: the mitzvah is for the eldest to perform yibbum, as it is written, “and the firstborn,” etc.; if there is no eldest, the younger performs yibbum. And we learned: if the younger made the matter depend on the elder until he comes from overseas, meaning the elder is overseas and the younger says to wait until he comes, we do not listen to the younger. Rather, the younger must perform yibbum or chalitzah immediately. Although if we waited until the elder came the mitzvah would be done in the preferred way, for initially the mitzvah is with the elder, nevertheless it is preferable for the younger to perform yibbum or chalitzah immediately, for we do not delay a mitzvah; whenever one can bring the performance of the mitzvah closer, that is preferable.
And in Menachos, page 72, although the limbs and fats of the afternoon tamid have their mitzvah the entire night, nevertheless one may burn on Shabbos the limbs and fats of the afternoon tamid of Shabbos, even though it would be possible to burn them on Motzaei Shabbos.
Even so, it was permitted to desecrate Shabbos, because a mitzvah is beloved in its proper time, to advance the mitzvah.
