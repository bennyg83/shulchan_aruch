First. See Baer Heitev. One who practices putting on the tefillin of the hand while sitting and the tefillin of the head while standing, according to the view of the Arizal, the author of Darkei Noam wrote there that since according to the law as well there is no obligation to recite the blessing over tefillin while standing, he is not permitted to be stringent and recite the blessing standing, for according to the kabbalists the proper way is sitting.
