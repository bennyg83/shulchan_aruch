And then it was, etc. In order to give praise and thanks in this blessing also for tefillin, which are called glory, as it says, “bind your glory upon yourself,” referring to tefillin, which are glory for Israel, as it says, “and all the peoples of the earth shall see,” etc., “and they shall fear you,” and we expound that this refers to the tefillin of the head.
And he blesses Oter, etc. In Be’er Heitev Chaim he wrote: I have not seen people practice this way. Rather, they put on tefillin before the morning blessings or afterward, each according to his custom. I have only seen meticulous people touch the arm tefillin and the head tefillin when reciting this blessing.
