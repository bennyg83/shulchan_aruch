(12) "And it was," etc. — praise in this blessing also for tefillin called pe'er — "Your splendor is bound on you" — head tefillin; "all nations see… they fear you" — these are head tefillin.

(13) He blesses Oter, etc. — Aruch HaShulchan: not practiced — only don tefillin before or after birchot hashachar per custom; some touch arm and head tefillin when saying this blessing.
