Seif katan 3 He need not wait. The text should read: “to wait specifically for the tzitzis, so that he will go out,” etc. His intent is to explain what the Rama teaches and why one might have thought that he must wait. Therefore he wrote that one might have thought he should wait so that he will go out, etc. This is what people say, but I am not comfortable with it, for it would have been better to explain simply that one might have thought he should wait in order to wear tzitzis first, because we ascend in holiness. It appears more correct to me that this is a scribal error, and this seif katan should be marked on seif 2, “and he should put on tefillin at home,” specifically so that he should go out, etc.; these are the words of the Beit Yosef on that law, see there, s.v. “and the correct way.” So it appears to me.
