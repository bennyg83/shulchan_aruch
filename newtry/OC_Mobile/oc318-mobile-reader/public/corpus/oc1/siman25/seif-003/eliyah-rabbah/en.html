[3] For one ascends in matters of sanctity, etc. At the end of this se'if, Levush writes that some say tefillin precede because they possess greater sanctity. One must say that the two positions dispute this very rationale: whether the item of greater sanctity should be put on first, or later, because one ascends in sanctity. In truth, I do not know who the 'some say' whom he cites below are; Beis Yosef does not mention them. The rationale of ascending in sanctity appears in Likkutei Mahariv, Dinim VeHalachos 20. It appears to follow the mystical teaching that a person proceeds from a lesser sanctity to a greater sanctity, as stated in the Shulchan Aruch of Mahari Luria, Hilchos Tzitzis 20, as well as in Zohar and Makor Chaim 1. Megillah 21b similarly states that when the last reader reads four verses he is praiseworthy, because one ascends in sanctity and does not descend. Moreover, the principal reason for the 'some say' below is, as Levush concludes, that the mitzvah of tzitzis was deferred when he dressed because his hands were not clean. One cannot object that Levush himself writes in siman 8:11 that the garment can be donned without a berachah. Nevertheless, at the beginning of that siman he implies that, ideally, it is better to don it after washing. Levush asks why Shulchan Aruch needed to give a reason for tzitzis preceding tefillin: the obligation of tzitzis takes effect immediately when one dresses, while tefillin are not put on when naked; for this reason, he says, the authorities did not need to mention it. In my humble opinion, this poses no difficulty. We rule that tzitzis is not an obligation upon one's person; one may, if he wishes, make all his garments without four corners and tzitzis. Only during Krias Shema and prayer must even one who does not remain wrapped in tzitzis all day take care to wear them, as stated above in siman 24:1. Shulchan Aruch therefore needed to give these reasons. Regarding Levush's statement that the authorities did not need to mention it for this reason, see Eliyah Zuta 1.
