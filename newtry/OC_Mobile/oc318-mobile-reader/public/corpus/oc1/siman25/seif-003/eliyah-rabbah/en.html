For we ascend in holiness, etc. At the end of this seif the Levush wrote that some say tefillin come first, since they possess greater holiness. One must say that the dispute in this reasoning is whether something of greater holiness should come earlier, or later because we ascend in holiness. In truth, I do not know who these “some say” are that he cites later, and they are not mentioned in the Beit Yosef. The reason of ascending in holiness is in the collected rulings of Mahariv, laws and halachos, siman 20. It appears to be according to the hidden wisdom, that a person enters from a lighter holiness to a more severe holiness, as found in the Shulchan Aruch of Rabbi Yitzchak Luria, in the laws of tzitzis, seif 20, and likewise in the Zohar and Makor Chayim. So too in Megillah, the rule is that we ascend in holiness and do not descend.
