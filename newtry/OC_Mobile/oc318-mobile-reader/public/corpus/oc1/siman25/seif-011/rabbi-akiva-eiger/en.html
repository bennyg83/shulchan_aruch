Seif 13. 'On Rosh Chodesh he removes them.' Eliyah Zuta writes in the name of Responsa Rema MiPano that those who customarily wear tefillin throughout the day should put them on again on Rosh Chodesh after Musaf. On Chol HaMoed, however, they should not put them on afterward, even for the Minchah prayer.
