If they slipped. Taz; siman 8:12 — Beit HaKenesset: cannot insert into tefillin — hefsek; though siman 21:3 Beit Tzvi not for synagogue — not from law; therefore with tzitzit not hefsek (Magen Avraham 22 — our synagogue without permanent knot, no berachah; so practice). Mechaber brought first Rabbi (Sukkah 46), then Rabbanan deBei Rav Ashi (Be'er HaGolah 10 — Rashi/Rosh mark Gemara), then his explanation Rabbanan deBei R' Elazar — feel to return even if slipped unintentionally; Rabbenu Yonah agreed to Rosh — unintentionally like intent not to return immediately; disputed moving with intent to return immediately; even removed completely to return immediately — disputed; explained siman 8:14. Seemingly "slipped from place" unnecessary for Mechaber; moreover if unintentionally returned arm and blessed lehaniach then returned head — Mechaber too repeats if did not know at first blessing.
