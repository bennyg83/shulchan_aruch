(38) Before winding — lehaniach covers head too (se'if 31); Mechaber one blessing — minimize gap; arm windings not essential — after them place head. Ari kavanot: seven windings on arm first [not three on finger] — winding is mitzvah, not interruption; common custom; Mishkanot Yaakov 28 — wind first for strength. If Kaddish/Kedusha after arm blessing — place head before windings if can tighten arm slightly without moving.

(39) "Forbidden," etc. — even removing both equally from bag — no ein ma'avirin, still be careful (kabbalah, Beit Yosef); certainly forbidden to hold arm while removing head.

(40) "Placed" — fastened well on arm — permitted even before seven windings. Another may remove head while friend dons arm — Pri Megadim stringent, Aruch HaShulchan lenient.

(41) He should not adjust — even if during fixing head he still holds arm, when placing head afterward while holding arm — ein ma'avirin.

(42) Both standing — Magen Avraham: arm seated, blessing standing; Eliyahu Rabbah (Maharshal 98): we follow Rashi over kabbalist who prayed like infant — cannot attain secrets, might "cut saplings" — bless and don standing; Gra proved Zohar allows arm standing — don't change custom. Kenesset HaGedolah: Kabbalah vs Gemara — follow Gemara; may be stringent if Kabbalists are; unstated in Gemara — cannot compel; unstated law — follow Kabbalah; Kabbalah decides disputes.
