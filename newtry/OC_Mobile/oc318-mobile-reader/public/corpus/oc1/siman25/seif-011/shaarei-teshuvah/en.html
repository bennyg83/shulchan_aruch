With them. See Baer Heitev. In Panim Meiros, part 1, siman 54, his view is that it is permitted initially to answer amen after his fellow's blessing over tefillin. Although for Kaddish and Kedushah he should not interrupt, nevertheless amen is an acceptance of the matter, that he believes in Hashem who commanded us to put on tefillin, and it is not an interruption. Since according to Rabbeinu Tam one interrupts even for Kaddish and Kedushah, one may rely on him regarding answering amen; see there. What he wrote in the name of Darkei Noam regarding interrupting to answer Kaddish, Kedushah, and amen between the tefillin of the hand and the tefillin of the head with Rabbeinu Tam tefillin, so too wrote Bechor Shor in Sotah 44b; see there.
