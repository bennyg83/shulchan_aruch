The strap. In the kavanot it is stated that the Ari of blessed memory wrapped before he put on the head tefillin, and see above, siman 27, seif katan 15, what I wrote there.
From the case. He should place them in the case in such a way that it is impossible for the head tefillin to come first. Taz. The Knesses HaGedolah wrote in the name of his teacher that there is concern only when the one putting them on himself removes the head tefillin from the case before putting on the arm tefillin. But if another person, while his fellow is putting on the arm tefillin, removes and unties the head tefillin for him, there is no concern; see there. See the book Mishnas Chasidim, page 57, and Olas Tamid.
Standing. Knesses HaGedolah wrote in his rules of the poskim that whenever the masters of Kabbalah and the Zohar disagree with the Gemara, follow the Gemara and the poskim. However, if the masters of Kabbalah are stringent, one should also be stringent. If the matter is not mentioned in the Gemara and poskim, even though it is mentioned in Kabbalah, we cannot force people to practice that way; see there, and see Magen Avraham.
