The strap. In kavanot it says the Ari wound before placing head tefillin — see siman 27 se'if kaf 15.

From the bag. Place them in the bag so head tefillin cannot come out first (Turei Zahav). Kenesset HaGedolah cites his teacher: concern only when he himself removes from the bag before placing arm tefillin; but another person, while his friend places arm tefillin, may remove and hand him head tefillin — no concern. See Mishnat Chasidim p. 57 and Pri Megadim.

Standing. Kenesset HaGedolah wrote in general rules: when Kabbalists and Zohar disagree with Gemara, follow Gemara and poskim; but if Kabbalists are stringent, one may be stringent. If not in Gemara or poskim, even if in Kabbalah we cannot compel practice. Pri Megadim and Magen Avraham.
