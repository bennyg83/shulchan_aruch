The reason, in the Tur in the name of the Rosh, is that since the second blessing returns also upon the tefillin of the hand, and for this reason one must not interrupt between them, one should minimize interruption as much as possible. The wrapping around the arm is not part of the mitzvah, therefore it is good to delay it until he puts on the tefillin of the head. End quote. The Beit Yosef wrote in the name of a responsum of the Rosh in this wording: because the blessing over the tefillin of the hand also belongs to the tefillin of the head. End quote. Rabbi Eliezer of Prague explained that what the Tur wrote, also upon the tefillin of the hand, means upon the blessing of the tefillin of the hand, and thus the words of the Tur are like the words of the Rosh. This is forced, for otherwise you would have difficulty, since he afterward wrote that if one has only tefillin of the hand he recites lehaniach alone, which shows that for tefillin of the hand only lehaniach is required. So too it implies in the Rosh in Hilchos Ketanos. In the book of Kavanat HaArizal, in the name of Rabbi Yitzchak Luria, it says that he practiced to wrap seven wrappings first on the arm and afterward put on the tefillin of the head, and he wrote a secret regarding this; see there.
To take out the tefillin of the head, etc. Some practice to make a separate pouch for each tefillin and to make signs on the pouches indicating which is of the hand and which is of the head. This is not correct, for nevertheless, when the pouch containing the tefillin of the head happens to come before him first, opposite his right hand, he must turn the pouches so that the tefillin of the hand comes before him first, and he is found passing over the mitzvos. Rather, he should place both in one long, narrow pouch, and place the tefillin of the head below and the tefillin of the hand above, in such a way that in no manner can the tefillin of the head come first.
