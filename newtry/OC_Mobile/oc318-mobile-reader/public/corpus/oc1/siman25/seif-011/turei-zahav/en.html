Before winding. Rosh: second blessing covers arm too — minimize gap; arm windings not essential mitzvah — after them place head. Beit Yosef: lehaniach on arm covers head. Maharal Prague: "also on arm" means lehaniach on arm. If only arm — lehaniach alone (Halachot Ketanot; Ari kavanot — seven windings before head).

Removing head tefillin from bag, etc. Separate pockets with labels is wrong: when head bag faces you first, turning for arm causes ein ma'avirin. Better one narrow bag — head below, arm above so head cannot come out first.
