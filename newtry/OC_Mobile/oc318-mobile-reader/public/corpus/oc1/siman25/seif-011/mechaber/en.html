After tying the arm tefillin to the arm, one should put on the head tefillin before winding the strap around the arm. There is an opinion who forbids removing the head-tefillin from the bag before putting on the arm-tefillin.Even if both are before him outside the bag, one should not open the head tefillin until after the arm is on (Mahari"a, Mahari ben Chabib). Some say: arm tefillin seated, head standing (Agur, Zohar siman 84); in our lands we do not practice thus — both standing.
