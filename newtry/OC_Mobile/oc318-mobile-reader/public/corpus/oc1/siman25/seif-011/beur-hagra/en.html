And there is, etc., and even, etc. As written in Yoma 33b according to Rabbeinu Tam, as above in seif 6.
There is, etc. A sign for this is the blessing of Kerias Shema and prayer. They derived it from the Zohar at the end of parashas Bamidbar, 120b: “first, at the time,” etc., “and afterward tefillah shel yad, which is,” etc. However, this is not a proof that it must specifically be while seated. See Zohar, parashas Terumah 139b, regarding the dispute of Shammai and Hillel, etc.; and see Zohar at the end of parashas Chayei Sarah 132b. The Rav held that it must specifically be seated, but this is not so, for the prayer of Yotzer is not specifically recited while seated, as stated at the end of the first chapter of Berachos, in the Mishnah: “they stand and read, they sit,” etc. See parashas Terumah, as above.
And in these countries, etc. This follows what is written at the beginning of siman 8 in the name of the Yerushalmi, that all blessings over mitzvos are recited standing. But I have already written there that this is not so. There is no dispute about this except that tefillin shel rosh is put on standing according to the Zohar, while for tefillin shel yad there is no concern, as above. Likewise, the Semak and other poskim wrote “standing” only regarding the Omer, tzitzis, and tefillin, because the verse says “for yourselves.” He wrote that therefore the practice is to recite Havdalah standing, since it says “and you shall separate for yourselves.” Tosafot wrote to recite Havdalah seated, and Kiddush, according to all opinions, is seated, as written in siman 271.
