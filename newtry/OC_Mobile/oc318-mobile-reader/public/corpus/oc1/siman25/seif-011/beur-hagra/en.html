Se'if 11 — After, etc. Blessing applies to both per all views — therefore if he spoke, etc.; good to minimize interruption.

And some, and even, etc. As in Yoma 33b per Rabbeinu Tam, as above se'if 6.

Some, etc. Sign for this: Shema and prayer — from Zohar Bamidbar 120b (Kadmeita): after arm tefillin, etc. Not required specifically seated — Terumah 139b dispute Shammai/Hillel; Zohar Chayei Sarah 132b — Rav holds specifically seated; not so — Avodat HaCreator blessing not only seated (Berakhot ch. 1: standing for Shema, sitting for Keriat Shema); Terumah as above.

In these countries, etc. Per Rashba siman 8 citing Yerushalmi — all mitzvah blessings standing; I wrote there it is not found and no dispute — only head tefillin standing per Zohar; arm tefillin no stringency (above); Semak and Shulchan Aruch did not write "standing" but "omer tzitzit and tefillin" because "for you" — therefore custom to distinguish from standing of "veHibdaltenu"; Tosafot: distinguish from seated; Kiddush for all seated (siman 271).
