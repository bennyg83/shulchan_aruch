"After he tied." Kavanot: Ari wound before head tefillin — holding winding is mitzvah, done standing.

"He should not adjust." Taking in hand without placing is like passing over mitzvot.

"They did not practice thus." Placement seated; blessing and tying standing (R' Yehudah ibn Attar: all mitzvah berakhot standing). Kenesset HaGedolah rules: Kabbalah/Zohar vs Gemara — follow Gemara (Radbaz 1:49; Yuchasin; R' Yehudah Halevi 41; R' Yehudah ibn Attar here); if Kabbalists stringent, may be stringent; unstated in Gemara/poskim — cannot compel kabbalistic practice (Ra'ah 1:1).
