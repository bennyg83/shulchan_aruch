After he tied. In the Kavvanos it is stated that the Arizal wrapped before putting on the head tefillin, for he held that the wrapping is a mitzvah, and he did it standing.
He should not adjust. For when he takes it in his hand and does not put it on, it is considered passing over the mitzvos.
They did not practice so. It seems to me that the placing should be while seated, and the blessing and tying should be while standing, as the Beit Yosef wrote at the beginning of siman 8, that all blessings over mitzvos are recited standing. The Knesses HaGedolah wrote in the rules of the poskim: whenever the masters of Kabbalah and the Zohar dispute the Gemara, follow the Gemara and the poskim, as stated by the Radbaz, part 1, siman 49, Sefer Yuchasin, and Rabbi Yosef HaLevi, siman 41, and so wrote the Beit Yosef here. However, if the masters of Kabbalah are stringent, one should also be stringent. And anything not mentioned in the Gemara and poskim, even though it is mentioned in Kabbalah, we cannot compel people to practice that way, as stated by the Re'em, part 1, siman 1. End quote.
