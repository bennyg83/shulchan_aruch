Magen Avraham seif katan 2 Near. According to the simple meaning, that “over” means before, the language of the Mechaber, who wrote “therefore,” etc., “after placement,” does not fit, for this does not depend on being before its performance. Therefore he explained that the word “over” also includes the sense of near; then the language “therefore” fits both matters, after placement and before tying. The Taz in seif katan 7 answered that “therefore” refers only to before tying, while after placement is obvious without this; see there.
