(Seif katan 6) He should intend, etc. Also the two passages, etc. Meaning, four passages are written in tefillin: Shema, Vehayah im shamoa, Kadesh, and Vehayah ki yeviacha. Two of them, namely Shema and Vehayah im shamoa, all Israel read, and regarding this he wrote that the other two passages, Kadesh and Vehayah ki yeviacha, should also be read.
