"If he encountered, etc." — Mishnah Berurah: Taz (siman 484) rules not like Avudraham; Gra and Pri Megadim agree; Gra on Rama about Chanukah implies if one wrongly donned head first he need not remove [MA cites Avudraham]. Aruch HaShulchan sides with Avudraham — must fix "two between eyes"; but head does not hinder arm — he fulfills head mitzvah, only not ideally; removing active mitzvah to fulfill "better" later is wrong (Yevamot 39a — do yibum with minor, don't wait for adult; don't delay mitzvah). Magen Avraham 2 supports; counter-proof siman 426 Hagahah (Tehillim David) — Yevamot case is shev ve'al ta'aseh; here active removal — most agree. Gra rushed Kiddush Levanah — rule with Taz: don arm tefillin quickly so "two between eyes" — enough.
