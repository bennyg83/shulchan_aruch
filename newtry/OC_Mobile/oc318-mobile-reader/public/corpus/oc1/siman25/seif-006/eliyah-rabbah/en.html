[6] Donning tefillin and walking, etc. Olat Tamid: filthy alleys — don in synagogue vestibule. Kenesset HaGedolah: even filthy alleys — don at home, cover tefillin. Menachem Azariah 59; Magen Avraham 43. Zohar Va'etchanan (Levush): leave home with tzitzit and tefillin — angels accompany. Zohar Shelach: don at home. Mekor Chaim: one seeking first ten at dawn — reward for early minyan; still sanctify with tzitzit/tefillin before Musaf; Ari: better wrapped to synagogue (Mahari Luria 21; Maharam Lublin 34; Magen Avraham 3). Mekor Chaim stronger before zeman tzitzit. Nachalat Sheva 6; Tosafot Chayim — agrees.
