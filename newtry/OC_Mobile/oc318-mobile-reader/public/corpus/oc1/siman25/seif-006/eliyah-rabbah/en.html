He puts on tefillin and goes, etc. Olas Tamid wrote that in places where people live among Cutheans, or where the alleyways are filthy and one cannot wear a tallis and tefillin at home, he should put them on in the entryway of the beis knesses and not enter the beis knesses without tallis and tefillin. But in Kenesses HaGedolah it is clear that even where there are filthy alleyways he should put them on at home and cover the tefillin in the place of filth. So it appears to me as well from Responsa Menachem Azaryah, siman 59, and so it appears from Magen Avraham, siman 43, at the end of seif katan 11. In the Zohar, parashas Vaeschanan, which the Levush cites at the beginning of his words, it says that when a person leaves the doorway of his house with tzitzis and tefillin, four holy angels join him and accompany him to the beis knesses.
