Lehaniach. Taz — see Levush's challenge on Beit Yosef: truly lehaniach with kamatz — root nuach menachei ayin (no dagesh in nun); lehaniach with patach on hei (nun with dagesh) — root yanach, placement and abandonment. Meir Netiv (concordance): yanach — placement not abandonment; placement fits better for arm and head than nuach — rest in place, not donning. (Ezekiel 44:44 — that verse is lehaniach berachah.) Copy question there. Mechaber: when Shach blesses al mitzvot with patach — only on head. Rif end Rosh HaShanah — no speech between tekiyot; Rambam 4:4. "Do not bundle mitzvot" — I did not understand; perhaps Pesachim 102 — two kiddushot on one cup (Rashi Berakhot ein chotemin). He raised: lehaniach and mitzvot both — arm and head; not "bundles" — each blessing of tefillah has three mitzvot: (1) seeing tefillin, Exodus in them proves renewal of world, providence (commentators; Rama — "I am the Lord… who brought you out" not "who created you" — miracles like creation; fire in hail; Pharaoh "who is the Lord" — Elokim nature, after creation no change — therefore first plague and others "the Lord is righteous"); (2) placement on hand and head to subjugate mind and soul with body — therefore two blessings on arm: lehaniach two mitzvot of tefillin to remember what is in them… Even arm alone — two blessings, no break — both blessings refer to both. End: mitzvot in cholam — two mitzvot, mitzvot also on arm (Tur here Beit Yosef) — plural; head alone two when spoke — cholam for Riaz (see Riaz and Ran end RH — brought s"k 4): lehaniach only arm, mitzvot only head; touch needed; contiguity; like two on head but main lehaniach on arm while touching — all time touching he blesses (Magen Avraham 15). For us likewise. Only arm — two blessings; only head — two; mitzvot with patach — one mitzvah, seeing tefillin to remember. We follow Rav: head — two; arm — lehaniach and also al mitzvot. Cholam/patach — authority, Taz and brother, Magen Avraham 9; if erred al mitzvot on arm and lehaniach on head — seems fine for this teshuvah, uncertain. Magen Avraham 11; Sha'arei Tefillah 318 — Rav Zalman grammarian.
