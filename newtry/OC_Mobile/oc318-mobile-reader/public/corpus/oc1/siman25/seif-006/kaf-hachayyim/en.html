6) There: Those who place the tefillin and the tallis in a single bag must take care not to place the tefillin bag on top, etc. Magen Avraham 1 writes: It appears to me that there is no concern here, since the tefillin are still inside their bag; this is also implied by se'if 11. His proof from se'if 11, however, is no proof. Maran derived the law in se'if 11 from the responsum of Rabbi Yosef Gikatilla, based on Kabbalah, that the head-tefillin should not be removed from their case until the hand-tefillin have been put on, as explained in Beis Yosef. Shaar HaKavanos, Derushei Tefillin, derush 5, likewise states that the Arizal was careful not to remove the head-tefillin from their bag until he had put on the hand-tefillin and fastened it above the biceps. That prohibition is not based on the rule that one may not pass over mitzvos; rather, this is the prescribed kabbalistic order. A practical difference is that even if another person wishes to take out the head-tefillin for him before he has put on and fastened the hand-tefillin, doing so is forbidden. This is unlike Machatzis HaShekel, who suggests that the reason could have been the prohibition against passing over mitzvos, and that this reason was not given because, while the tefillin remain bound in their cases, that prohibition does not apply. Rather, the reason the prohibition against passing over mitzvos was not given is the practical difference just stated: even another person may not take out the head-tefillin for him before he fastens the hand-tefillin. Had the reason been passing over mitzvos, another person would have been permitted to remove them, as we shall explain further in se'if 11, with God's help. Maamar Mordechai 3 likewise writes regarding Magen Avraham's words that there is no proof at all from se'if 11, even by a strained inference. Nehar Shalom 1 also writes that se'if 11 affords no proof; see there. Nishmas Adam 13:2 rules likewise. Levush writes that even when the tefillin are inside a bag, passing them over entails the prohibition against passing over mitzvos. Taz 10, Eliyah Rabbah 4, and Chayei Adam 13:1 rule likewise. See Nishmas Adam 2, who adduces proofs, particularly because this is Maran's view. So too rule the later authorities: Shalmei Tzibbur 37a; Siddur Beis Yaakov, laws of the place where tefillin are put on, 1; Chesed LaAlafim 1; and Kitzur Shulchan Aruch 10:2, unlike Shulchan Aruch HaRav 3.
