He encountered, etc. Specifically if he encountered it. But if he already put on the head tefillin first, or found that the arm tefillin had slipped from its place, he should not remove the head tefillin, for what has happened has happened; rather, he should hurry to put the arm tefillin in its place. Some disagree; see Biur Halacha.
To pass over. Since the verse states explicitly that the arm comes first, first “and you shall bind them,” and afterward “and they shall be totafos,” therefore we do not take into account passing over the mitzvah. This differs from above in seif 1 regarding tzitzis and tefillin.
