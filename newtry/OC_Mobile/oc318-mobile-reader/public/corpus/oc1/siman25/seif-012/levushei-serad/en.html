Shulchan Aruch, seif 11 And there is one who says. This is not language of dispute against what preceded, and there is no disagreement at all. Rather, it is the continuation of the matter. Since he first wrote that one should place the shel rosh before wrapping the strap, that is in order to minimize the interruption between the shel yad and the shel rosh, because the blessings apply to both. Therefore there were those who wished to minimize the interruption still further, by first taking the shel rosh out of the case and preparing it, so that after he places the shel yad the shel rosh will be ready before him immediately. Therefore he wrote that this is forbidden because it is like passing over the mitzvah. So it appears from the Beit Yosef; see there.
