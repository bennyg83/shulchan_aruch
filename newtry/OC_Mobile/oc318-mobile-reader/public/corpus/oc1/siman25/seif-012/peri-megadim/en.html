He need not. See Taz. I do not know what he teaches us, see Magen Avraham letter 23. Initially, certainly one should not do so, for this is before the prior stage, and also a large interruption. After the fact, the blessing is effective. What he brought as proof from siman 8, seif 15, see Magen Avraham there, letter 20. What he wrote, that lehaniach applies to the head tefillin, etc., and therefore in the second law, where the tightening became loosened before the head tefillin, which is the law of the Raavad, one does not bless because the mitzvah was not completed, raises the question of what is written here. The Magen Avraham in letter 24 answered this, that making the knot is an interruption in such a case, but the Taz was not comfortable with that. As practical halachah he ruled in the next letter that indeed the Shulchan Aruch retracted from this and holds that even when making the knot, if it tore before placing the head tefillin, one does not bless.
