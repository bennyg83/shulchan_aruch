Shifted from place, etc. Clarified siman 8:14 — Rama there: if removed to enter bathroom — repeat; if not entering — no repeat.

Need not repeat blessing. Hanachah not yet complete — blessing not yet "on" mitzvah; no hesech hada'at — second hanachah valid (Beit Yosef). Learn: tefillin in hand without knot — bless, tie, don — blessing stands; tying is tikun not hefsek (like salt between haMotzi and eating, siman 167). If tightened then strap broke — Beit Yosef: must bless again since mitzvah done in tightening — proof from tallit that fell entirely (siman 8:96)? Difficulty: lehaniach before arm covers head too — still engaged until head placed; knot on arm is part of one mitzvah — Ra'avad: if permitted before head, blessing still continues (Shulchan Aruch follows Ra'avad).

If arm strap came loose, etc. Difficulty: Shulchan Aruch treats "came loose" and "broke" alike; Beit Yosef begins with "broke" then cites Ra'avad on "came loose." Perhaps Beit Yosef doubted Ra'avad's distinction — loose needs less repeat than break; while head not yet placed, arm still under lehaniach — no repeat even if broke after tightening (Ra'avad). Shulchan Aruch wrote only clear case: broke at start of tightening — no repeat; after tightening — learn from Ra'avad: no repeat until head not placed.

Not on stolen. Blessing through theft until ye'ush and change of ownership — Beit Yosef (Orach Chaim) citing Hagahot Or Zaru'a. Shulchan Aruch: invalid in all cases; Rambam siman 11 "invalid."
