The strap. See Baer Heitev in the name of the Arizal, and so wrote the Radbaz in Responsa, part 3, siman 623. In Birkei Yosef he wrote that such is the custom in every place. In the book Maamar Mordechai he wrote: however, it seems that if after he blessed he heard Kaddish or Kedushah, and if he does not wrap he will have time to put on the tefillin of the head and answer, he should do so, and this is preferable; and so I practiced. End quote.
