If one puts on tefillin several times in one day, he must recite the blessing over them each time. If they slipped from their proper position and he handles them to return them to their place, he must recite the blessing. {Rama: If he is returning only one of them, he recites the blessing appropriate to one who puts on only one tefillah, as explained below in siman 26. [Rama's own ruling]} If he removed them with the intention of putting them back immediately, he must nevertheless recite the blessing again. If he was putting on the arm tefillin and had recited the blessing, and while tightening it the knot broke so that he must make another knot, if he did not divert his attention from the tefillin he need not recite another blessing. If the arm tefillin loosened before he put on the head tefillin, he tightens it and need not recite another blessing. But if he had already put on the head tefillin and afterward the arm tefillin loosened, he tightens it and recites the blessing. {Rama: Some say that he should not recite a blessing in this case [Tur, siman 8], and this is the prevalent custom, as was explained above in siman 8, seif 14.} One may recite the blessing over borrowed tefillin, but not over stolen tefillin.
