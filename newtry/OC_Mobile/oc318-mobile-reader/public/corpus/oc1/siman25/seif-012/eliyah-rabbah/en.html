[12] "With kamatz, etc." Mitzvat Shimurim (Aruch s.v. tefillin): Mechaber erred — kamatz means rest; here patach with dagesh like Chanukah "lehaniach." Surprising to argue with Shulchan Aruch — he is Agur 37; Levush refuted from Torah verses; Talmud inconclusive; perhaps Chanukah also kamatz; Teshuvot Tashbetz agrees with Shulchan Aruch. Radak: nuach kamatz, yanach patach. Verses "hanach" — abandonment. Olat Tamid: Levush and Lechem Chamudos dispute Shulchan Aruch for patach — Levush held kamatz; Lechem Chamudos, Malbushei Yom Tov kamatz primary. Piskei Tosafot Menachot 131: bless lehaniach with sheva, not lehaniach with patach — i.e. kamatz. Lechem Chamudos: "al mitzvot" — cholam or patach under vav; I say patach — one mitzvah. Kenesset HaGedolah, Or Chadash — custom patach, unlike Taz. Only head — all agree patach.
