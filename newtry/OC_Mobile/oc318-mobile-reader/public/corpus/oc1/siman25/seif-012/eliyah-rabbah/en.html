With a kamatz, etc. In Sefer Mitzos Shemurim, in the name of the Ma’arich in the Aruch, it is written that the Shulchan Aruch, who ruled to say it with a kamatz, erred, for that is a term of resting; here one must say it with a patach and dagesh, meaning to place tefillin, like the expression regarding Chanukah light, that the mitzvah is to place it at the entrance of his house. This is astonishing, that his heart was so bold as to disagree with the Shulchan Aruch, which follows the Agur. He also did not see the words of the Levush, who rejected this from verses of the Torah. There is no proof from the wording of the Talmud, and perhaps regarding Chanukah as well the Talmud’s text should be with a kamatz. So too in Tashbetz Katan it is clear like the Shulchan Aruch. He also overlooked the words of Radak in Sefer HaShorashim, who brought them as two separate roots: the kamatz in the root meaning resting, and the patach in another root.
