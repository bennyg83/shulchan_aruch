(Seif katan 12) Before, etc. As written in siman 167 regarding the blessing of HaMotzi: although we require the blessing to be before its performance, nevertheless one should not bless before the before. Therefore one should not bless HaMotzi until he takes hold of the bread before cutting it. And in siman 651, for this reason he should not bless over the lulav until he takes hold of the lulav; see there.
