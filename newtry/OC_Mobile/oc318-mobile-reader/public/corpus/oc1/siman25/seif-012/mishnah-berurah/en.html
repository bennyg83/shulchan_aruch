(43) Every time — even if intended immediate return without moving — Rama disagrees nearby. If at blessing he intended to remove and replace — Magen Avraham siman 8:17 — all agree no repeat.

(44) Slipped — Shelah: we don't bless when slipped — during prayer not forgotten — like removal to return; Hagahah rules no repeat. Those wearing until chatzot — if slipped, should bless. Chayei Adam: even during prayer, blessers don't lose; minimizing berakhot preferable.

(45) From their place — all or most; partial shift — replace, no repeat [Taz siman 8:15; Pri Megadim].

(46) Must bless — shifting is like full removal.

(47) Not to bless — shifted intending return differs from slipping — mitzvah lapses when slipped, hence Mechaber silent. Full removal intending immediate return — Yesh Omrim disagree; acharonim agree. Not intending immediate return, or delayed despite intent, or distracted — all repeat. Removed to enter bathroom — repeat though intending immediate redonning — cannot go with them (siman 65); Chayei Adam: removing to blow nose — repeat.

(48) Arm — same for head tefillin.

(49) Did not forget — even spoke for need — not interruption bedi'eved (siman 167:6); see siman 8 Mishnah Berurah 28.

(50) And bless — placement not yet done — blessing not yet on mitzvah; tying not interruption — blessing applies to second placement. Learn: tefillin without knot, blessed, tied, placed — first blessing valid. Shulchan Aruch seems: knot breaks after arm tightened — repeat (mitzvah complete, like slipped). Acharonim dispute: lehaniach on arm covers head — until head placed still same berachah; like knot break before head — no repeat; knot breaks after head placed — repeat. No one to fix knot, must take other tefillin — repeat (like siman 46:6).

(51) Allowed — knot. Same if slipped; "placed head" means tightened — main placement.

(52) Tightens and blesses — like slipped; if arm removed after tightening — repeat when replacing on head; per Shelah above — no blessing if removed during prayer in any case.

(53) Borrowed — even unknowing borrower permitted — owner pleased mitzvah with his property; fold as originally, don't remove from first place (tallis siman 14) [acharonim].

(54) Stolen — mitzvah through transgression; even after despair, bedi'eved no fulfillment; if thief sold after despair — per Magen Avraham YD 649, Machatzit HaShekel siman 11 — buyer may bless (despair for him); Taz, Gra here — forbidden to bless always; Gra: may wear but not bless — blessing through theft removes from owner; second buyer may bless (Damascus Eliezer siman 11).
