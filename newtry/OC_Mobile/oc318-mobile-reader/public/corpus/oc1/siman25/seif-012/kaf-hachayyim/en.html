12) [Se'if 2] One who is careful to wear a tallis katan should don it and put on tefillin, etc. This follows the view of the Sephardic kabbalists cited by Beis Yosef. They said that one who has no tallis katan should certainly don the tallis and afterward the tefillin, but one who has donned a tallis katan should first put on tefillin and afterward wrap himself in a tallis gadol. Their reasoning is that, since he donned a tallis katan and then put on tefillin, he has already fulfilled the Zohar's instruction. The Arizal, however, rejected this view and wrote that this is not the Zohar's meaning. Rather, both the tallis katan and the tallis gadol must be donned before putting on tefillin, as stated above in paragraph 1. Mahari Tzemach likewise writes this in the Zohar's name, as cited by Birkei Yosef 4. See also the glosses to Zohar, Bamidbar 120b, note 3, in the names of Ramaz and Imrei Noam; and see above, paragraph 1, in the names of the later authorities.
