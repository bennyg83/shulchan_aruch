12) [Section 2] Whoever is careful with a small Talit will wear it and put on a tefillin, etc. This is according to the opinion of the Kabbalists of Sephardi, who says that the one who does not have a small tallit must put on a tallit and then a tefillin, but the one who wears a small tallit will put on a tefillin first and then wrap himself in a large tallit. And their opinion is that since they wore a small tallit and then put on a tefillin, they have already fulfilled the words of the Zohar. But the late Rabbi rejected this opinion and wrote that the meaning of the Zohar is not so, but between a small tallit and a large tallit one must wear a tefillin first, like the letter A.
