To bless. Shelah wrote we do not now bless when they slipped from place, since during prayer he presumably does not forget them — like removing to return (Magen Avraham; Eliyahu Rabbah ruled like Shulchan Aruch and brought proof).

To bless. But if removing to enter a bathroom — all agree he must bless, since he may not go to synagogue with them and they are set aside; likewise if he did not intend to return immediately (Bach, Pri Megadim, Turei Zahav). Magen Avraham: perhaps when the bathroom is not far, like their field bathroom, no blessing — then no distinction between bathroom and elsewhere, hence Rama's simple wording. Know Rama's "and some say" applies here too if removing completely — same whether shifted or fully removed. Pri Megadim.

The tightening. Meaning before the mitzvah is complete as required — therefore no repeat blessing, since tying is not interruption being part of the mitzvah (Turei Zahav: learn from one holding tefillin without knot, blesses, ties, and places — the first blessing stands; tying is not interruption. But after tightening on the arm the mitzvah is complete and then the knot breaks — seemingly requires blessing; Turei Zahav disagrees: as long as head tefillin not placed, still engaged in arm mitzvah, for head cannot precede arm; arm mitzvah not finished — no less than Shulchan Aruch: if arm removed before head, no blessing — likewise if knot breaks. Magen Avraham agrees; per Shelah in se'if kaf 15, even after tightening if knot breaks no blessing; Leket Yosher vol. 2 siman 120 agrees. See Pri Megadim, Nachalat Tzvi, Yad Aharon).

Stolen. It is a mitzvah through transgression. Turei Zahav: even despair and change of ownership do not help. Unknowing borrower is permitted for tefillin as for tallis, but fold as originally. Responsum Binyamin Ze'ev 299, cited in Kenesset HaGedolah, Bnei Chayya, Pri Megadim.
