Se'if 12 — Fell off. There; Rosh in Halachot Ketanot (R' Yosef): must bless when touching after placement — we rule like Rabanan against R' Eliezer; from R' Shmuel bar Sheniur: only when fell off — forced; all poskim like first view.

Shifted, and already, etc. As stated above.

One who places, etc. Even per view that shifted requires blessing — all the more "fell off"; here like fell off — at start of tightening break, first blessing not yet on mitzvah; now is first placement — Beit Yosef.

If strap came loose, but, etc. Like fell off — but before head placed, even if fell off, no repeat — first blessing still on head; if spoke, etc. Same for knot break after tightening before head — no repeat, unlike opening words and "at start of tightening." Taz and Magen Avraham 24 — knot-making is not hefsek — else start of tightening too (Berakhot 40a); bigger hefsek is between blessing and mitzvah start, between mitzvot, or mid-mitzvah (siman 8:14). Knot-making is not hefsek — only speech or hesech hada'at — like loose strap (Dagul Merevavah 8). Leshem and Magen Avraham 23 differ — end unclear; should read: here like fell off, not Leshem/Magen Avraham 23.

Permitted, etc. As sukkah and lulav from day 2, even in Temple, and other mitzvot.

Not on stolen, etc. Even after ye'ush and change of ownership — siman 649:1, unlike Beit Yosef (Or Zaru'a) — therefore omitted in Shulchan Aruch. Stolen invalid because of blessing in vain — Sukkah ref. 3; even after ye'ush; for "bedekah" even after change of act (649:1); all the more ye'ush and head — Tosafot there.
