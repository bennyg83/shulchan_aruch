They slipped. The Shelah wrote that the reason we do not practice nowadays to bless when they slipped from their place is that during prayer, presumably, one does not remove his mind from them, and it is like one who removes them with intent to put them back on.
And some say not to bless. But if he removes them in order to enter the bathroom, he must bless according to all opinions, since he is not permitted to go into the bathroom with them and they have been pushed away from use, as written at the beginning of siman 65. This also appears from the Gemara, Darkei Moshe, Bach, and siman 38. The same applies if he did not intend to put them back on immediately, that he must bless, as written at the end of siman 639. It is possible to say that when the bathroom is not far away, unlike their bathrooms which were in the field, he need not bless; if so, there is no difference between a bathroom and another place. Therefore the Rama wrote here without distinction.
And at the beginning of the tightening. This implies that if he tightened them and afterward the knot tore, he must bless, for the act of the mitzvah has already been completed, and it is like when they slipped from their place, where he must bless according to all opinions. This also appears to be the view of the Rama, who did not add any gloss here. Although in the Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe it implies that according to the opinion above, here too he need not bless, it seems to me that he retracted here in the Shulchan Aruch for the reason I wrote. However, according to what I wrote above in the name of the Shelah, even when they slipped, for us he need not bless.
Before placing the head tefillin. This implies even though he has already tightened them. It is not comparable to the case above, for there he interrupts through making the knot, unlike here.
He tightens and blesses. For it is like when they slipped from their place.
And not over stolen ones. For that would be a mitzvah that comes through a transgression. See siman 649, seif 5, unlike the Olas Tamid.
