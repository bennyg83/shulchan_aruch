In order (se'if 1). Taz — in my lay view the late master had one intent: Beit Yosef's reason for tzitzit before tefillin — weighty as all mitzvot; also frequent on Shabbat and Yom Tov. Shulchan Aruch wrote ma'alin baKodesh (apparently omitted tadir; and tadir is not always prior by Torah — Rambam siman 18: day garment at night no obligation; for us night is tefillin time but we do not instruct; a chassid need not remove at night — perhaps tefillin more frequent, see siman 44; unlike Rosh). Ein ma'avirin on mitzvot is deOraita (Levush; "and you shall keep the mitzvot"). If so, tadir is deOraita — why reject explicit tadir in Scripture except olat haBoker (Zevachim beginning Kol haTadir)? Because ein ma'avirin — so this reason too is only asmakhta; tefillin are more sanctified (mitzvah vs. kedushah). Rambam Hilchot Tefillin ch. 9 halachah 2: tadir and mekudash — whichever he wishes he precedes; ein ma'avirin defers to weight and ma'alin. Ma'alin means man ascends from mitzvah to kedushah. [Textual: siman 66 not 69.]
