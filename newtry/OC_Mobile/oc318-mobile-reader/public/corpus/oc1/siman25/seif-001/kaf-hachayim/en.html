(1) After wearing tzitzit, don tefillin — ma'alin baKodesh. Nimukey Yosef: tzitzit equals all mitzvot; more frequent — tadir kodem. Beit Yosef, Levush. Ari: wear tallit (katan then gadol) before tefillin — tzitzit are mochin of katnut before gedlut (Sha'ar HaKavanot, etc.) — opposite some kabbalists who don tefillin before tallit gadol after katan; Zohar supports our way (many sources cited).
