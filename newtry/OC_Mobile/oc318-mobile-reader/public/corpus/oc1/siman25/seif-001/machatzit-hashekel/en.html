(Se'if kaf 1) "That not, etc." As in se'if 11 — Rama wrote some forbid removing head tefillin from the bag until arm tefillin are placed; source: responsum R' Yehudah Gikatlia; kabbalistic reason in R' Yehudah ibn Attar's Aruch. Rama: even if both are outside the bag, do not open head until arm placed (Mahari Weil, Mahari Abohav — Beit Yosef). R' Yehudah ibn Attar (and Magen Avraham below): removing without placing is like passing over mitzvot — why then kabbalistic reason for first law? Because not yet open — even removed from bag no ein ma'avirin; all the more while still in bag.
