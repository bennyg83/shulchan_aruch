(Mechaber) The wording implies tefillin have greater holiness, therefore tzitzis is put on first because we ascend in holiness — this is puzzling: rather, because of its holiness it should come first! An explicit mishnah (chapter Kol HaTadir): whatever is holier than its fellow precedes its fellow. But Beit Yosef explains tzitzis first because tzitzis outweighs all mitzvot, or because it is more frequent.
