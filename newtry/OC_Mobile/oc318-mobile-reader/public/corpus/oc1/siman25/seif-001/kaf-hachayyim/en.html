1) [Se'if 1] After donning a fringed tallis, he should put on tefillin, for one ascends in matters of sanctity. Nimukei Yosef, in Hilchos Tzitzis, writes: Since the mitzvah of tzitzis is equivalent to all the mitzvos, it is fitting to give it precedence. Moreover, it is more frequent, for it applies both on weekdays and on Shabbos and Yom Tov, and when one matter is frequent and the other is not, the frequent one takes precedence. Beis Yosef and Levush cite him; see there. The Arizal gave a kabbalistic reason why one must specifically don the tallis before the tefillin (both the tallis katan and the tallis gadol) and the tallis katan is donned before the tallis gadol. Shaar HaKavanos, Derushei HaTzitzis, derush 2, states: Since tzitzis derives from the mochin of katnus, which precede the mochin of gadlus, one must therefore first don tzitzis and only afterward tefillin. This applies even to the tallis gadol used for wrapping, for the foregoing reason, contrary to the view of certain kabbalists who put on tefillin before the tallis gadol after having donned the tallis katan. All the more so should one not follow those who say that tefillin precede every form of tzitzis. Whoever examines the Zohar will find that the truth accords with our words. He writes similarly in Derushei HaTzitzis, derush 5, and Derushei Tefillas HaShachar, derush 3; see there for the mystical meaning. See also Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaTzitzis, chapter 3, and Shaar HaTefillah, chapter 7, s.v.
