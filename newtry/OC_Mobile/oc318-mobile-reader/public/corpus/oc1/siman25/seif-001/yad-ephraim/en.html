(Shulchan Aruch se'if 1) Ma'alin baKodesh. He did not come to explain delaying tefillin from tzitzit via ma'alin baKodesh — that applies only within one mitzvah (e.g. head tefillin before arm, Chanukah lights increasing). For precedence among mitzvot, Zevachim: "what is holier from its fellow precedes its fellow." Main reason tzitzit precedes tefillin: frequency (Beit Yosef) plus another reason there. Here he only says: do not think we precede because tzitzit is holier — in "kol haTadir" tadir vs. mekudash is debated; here there is some ma'alin baKodesh within the mitzvah itself, so we follow tadir. Mahariw at end of responsa has this wording — Mechaber copied it as a branch; Sha'agat Aryeh challenges Shulchan Aruch — see there.
