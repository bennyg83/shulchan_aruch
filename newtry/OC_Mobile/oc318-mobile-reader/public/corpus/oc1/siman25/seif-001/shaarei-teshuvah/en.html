He dons tefillin. Abudraham; Rabbi Yonah (acharonim): if he can borrow tefillin, tzitzis still precedes. See siman 38 se'if kaf 14 (tefillin before mezuzah). If he trusts he can borrow without returning until he buys — still tzitzis first per these poskim (Yad Ephraim; Sha'agat Aryeh 28; Shemesh Tzedakah 15; Taz). If he has Rashi tefillin but not Rabbeinu Tam and no tzitzis — tzitzis first, since most don't wear Rabbeinu Tam tefillin. Reverse not so; even with Rabbeinu Tam without Rashi — tefillin before tzitzis for berachah (siman 34). Rabbeinu Tam holiness greater, but for yotzei — Rashi first; most omit Rabbeinu Tam (Zohar Chadash, siman 34). One who cannot buy both: buy Rashi first, then Rabbeinu Tam — common practice; same for tzitzis. Shulchani Tzibbur (Devar Moshe): if cannot afford arm and head — buy head first.
