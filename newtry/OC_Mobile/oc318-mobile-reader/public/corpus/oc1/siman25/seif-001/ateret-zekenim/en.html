After wearing tzitzis one dons tefillin. A sign for this: from thread to shoelace — another sign is wrapping in a tallis before donning tefillin; "and those wrapped in white and tied to Jacob" — meaning: first wrap in a white tallis, and afterward tie tefillin last (Orach Chayim). It is a serious prohibition to don tefillin before wearing tzitzis (Beit Yosef in the name of Agur and Zohar, parashat Bamidbar). Some say a Torah scholar is exempt from tefillin; some do not don tefillin because they require bodily cleanliness — Magen Avraham wrote that in his day there were thousands and tens of thousands in Spain and other lands who did not wear tefillin for this reason.
