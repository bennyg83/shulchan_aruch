Tefillin. "Pelilah" — sign and testimony that Shechinah rests on us (Tur). Beit David 100: pelilah is wonder/distinction from nations, not only testimony. Tosafot Menachot 34 — wonder and dispute. Be'er Heitev 1 cites Kenesset HaGedolah on tzitzit frequency — Beit David 10 challenges: tzitzit is chovat manah; can exempt garments entirely unlike karkafeta. They precede as preparation for main mitzvah. Eliyahu Rabbah rules tefillin before tzitzit — see there.
