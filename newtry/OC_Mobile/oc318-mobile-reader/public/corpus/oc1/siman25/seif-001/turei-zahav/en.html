So as not to pass over mitzvot. For tefillin, if he encounters head first he removes it and dons arm first (se'if 6) — there the verse says "sign on your hand." Here tzitzit precedence is only a weak asmakhta; since tzitzis is frequent on Shabbat and weekday (Beit Yosef), better delay asmakhta than pass over mitzvot — Beit Yosef.
