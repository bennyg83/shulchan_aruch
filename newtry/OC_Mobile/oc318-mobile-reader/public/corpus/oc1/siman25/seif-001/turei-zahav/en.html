So that he should not pass over the mitzvos. Although regarding tefillin, if he encountered the tefillin of the head first, he should remove it and put on the tefillin of the hand first, as written in this siman, seif 6, that is different, for there it is written, and it shall be as a sign, etc., as written nearby. But here, where the precedence of tzitzis is only from a mere asmachta, since it is more frequent on Shabbos and weekday, as the Beit Yosef wrote at the beginning of this siman, it is better that this asmachta be pushed aside than that he pass over the mitzvah. So wrote the Beit Yosef.
