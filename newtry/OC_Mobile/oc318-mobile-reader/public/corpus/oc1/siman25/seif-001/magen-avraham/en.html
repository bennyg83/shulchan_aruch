"That they not place." Tzlaf — it seems no concern while tefillin remain in the bag, as se'if 11.

"Ready." Yevamot 39 — we do not delay mitzvah though later performance would be more choicest; Yalkut Vayikra end 129 — mitzvah in its time is dear; Menachot 72.

"Need not." Only to leave home with tzitzis and tefillin (Zohar, kavanot). Filthy alleys — see siman 43. Maharam Lublin 37: one bound himself to Tehillim before dawn, now cannot go to synagogue with tzitzis/tefillin — may retract like erroneous vows. Ari: not among first ten at synagogue — they rose before dawn; he wouldn't go without tefillin — could leave when day breaks and don tefillin; meanwhile congregation finishes birchot hashachar — better cancel former first; see YD 232:10 — likely needs release.
