Need not. This is specifically so that he should leave the entrance of his house with tzitzis and tefillin, as is stated in the Zohar and in the Kavvanos. If he must walk through filthy alleyways, see what I wrote in siman 43. Maharam of Lublin wrote in responsum 37 regarding one who bound himself with a penalty to go to Tehillim before dawn, and now wishes to retract because he cannot go to the synagogue with tzitzis and tefillin, that he has permission to retract, for it is comparable to mistaken vows. This is also in accordance with the kavvanah of the Arizal, for this reason the Arizal was not among the first ten in the synagogue, since they would rise early before dawn and he did not want to go to the synagogue without tefillin. One should not say that he should leave the synagogue when the day brightens and then put on tefillin, for in the meantime the congregation would have finished Birchot HaShachar and Pesukei DeZimra. Therefore it is better to set this aside before that. See Yoreh Deah siman 232, seif 10. It seems to me that he requires release from the vow.
