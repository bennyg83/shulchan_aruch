[1] After he wore, etc. Kenesset HaGedolah (Beit Yosef gloss) heard support from "wrapped in white and tied to Jacob." Levush and Beit Yosef: tzitzit equals all mitzvot; more frequent (Shabbat/Yom Tov) — tadir kodem. Therefore Kenesset HaGedolah (Tur gloss): if cannot afford both — tzitzis first. Olat Tamid disagrees — buy tefillin; Rosh Hashanah 17a: "poshei Yisrael in their bodies" without tefillin. I say this is only when omitting from contempt (Tosafot s.v. karkafeta; Beit Yosef 37; Mordechai; Ran; Rosh agrees one buying another mitzvah is not karkafeta). Still, karkafeta is said only of tefillin — tefillin is stricter. Beit Yosef: one explanation — tefillin not unique, same for all mitzvot. Practically buy tefillin — tzitzis not obligatory on person (siman 24:1); poskim call tefillin mitzvat haguf, not tzitzis (Avudraham; tefillin before mezuzah siman 38). Beit Hillel Yoreh Deah 285:2 — tefillin are tashmishei kedushah, tzitzis not. Yerushalmi Peah: not obligated to pawn doors for tefillin and tzitzis. Bach: some today lack tefillin and borrow after praying — great punishment.
