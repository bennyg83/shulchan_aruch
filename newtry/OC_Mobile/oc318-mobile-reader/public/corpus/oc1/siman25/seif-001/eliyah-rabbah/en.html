After he has worn, etc. Kenesses HaGedolah wrote: I heard a support from the verse, “the wrapped ones were for Lavan and the tied ones for Yaakov.” The Levush and Beit Yosef wrote the reason: the mitzvah of tzitzis is equivalent to all the mitzvos; furthermore, it is more frequent, for it applies also on Shabbos and Yom Tov, and the frequent takes precedence. For this reason the Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that one who cannot afford to buy both tzitzis and tefillin should buy tzitzis before tefillin. But Olas Tamid wrote against him that this is not correct, rather he should buy tefillin, for Chazal said: “The sinners of Israel with their bodies” refers to a skull that did not put on tefillin. I say that if this is the reason, it is not so, for Tosafot already wrote there that this refers specifically to one who refrains from putting them on out of contempt or rebellion, but not to one who is unable.
