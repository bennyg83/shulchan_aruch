The Laws of Tefillin in Detail. Containing 13 Se'ifim: After putting
on one's tallit with tzitzit [on it], one should put on tefillin because [we do mitzvot] in ascending order of holiness. And those who put their tefillin and tallit bags [together] into one bag must take care not to place the tefillin bag on top so that they won't encounter them first and [subsequently] have to put them on before the tallit in order not to skip mitzvot.': Rem"a: However, if one has the tefillin ready but does not have a tallit, he does not have to wait for a tallit, rather he puts on the tefillin. When they bring him a tallit, he puts it on [his (the Rem"a's) opinion].]:
