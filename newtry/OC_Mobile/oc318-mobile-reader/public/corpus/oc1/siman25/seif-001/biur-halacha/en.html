"We ascend in holiness" — refers to the person who must go step by step and rise in holiness: at first he only covers himself with a mitzvah garment; through tefillin he binds himself in unity and holiness [to resolve Shulchan Aruch's difficulty and Dagul MeRevavah].

"That they not place" — see Mishnah Berurah: Magen Avraham wrote it seems there is no concern since tefillin remain in the bag; Bach, Turei Zahav se'if kaf imply no distinction; Nishmat Adam disagrees (his proof from Tosafot Yoma 33b, R' Eliyahu — touch arm first though head box is mostly covered — weak: MA's reason is tying is not yet ready while still in bag; touching is the mitzvah even if shirt covers tefillin — still "ein ma'avirin"). Hard to be lenient like MA when many disagree. If embarrassed in synagogue — perhaps lenient after Nishmat Adam siman 8; Pri Megadim Eshel Avraham holds ein ma'avirin is Torah — Shulchan Aruch 13 end: Torah is not overridden for human dignity except great disgrace; Rama: sitting without tallit long-term is only small disgrace (Magen Avraham) — here only moments until donning tallit — doubtful.

"That he not encounter, etc." — see Mishnah Berurah on "even if"; source Yoma 33b. Even if tefillin are in tallit pocket before removal — more lenient; if he touched them he may withdraw hand and take tallis (Pri Megadim: MA allows both in bag equally; if head encountered first, remove and don arm first — no ein ma'avirin). This does not fit Mechaber and most acharonim [Levush, Bach, Taz, Nekudat HaKesef, Aruch HaShulchan] — ein ma'avirin even in bag. MA may only argue when tefillin untouched in separate pocket; here in hand via pocket they are ready — still in bag irrelevant (MA siman 28 se'if 4: arm tefillin slightly higher so he encounters them first; Pri Megadim: mark on straps suffices). We rule not like MA even in bag: if hand in pocket touches tefillin first — remove and don them first; ideally place head tefillin higher in bag so not to take head out first.

"He must don them" — if he removed tallit/tefillin briefly intending immediate return — we rule below no repeat blessing; if tefillin were ready when he re-dons — perhaps still engaged in mitzvah without distraction. Yoma 33b Tosafot R' Eliyahu on touching arm first — ein ma'avirin; for touching we don't bless (still in mitzvah) — a fortiori here. That passage may follow old custom of blessing on touch; Rava himself (Sukkah 46a) holds no blessing on touch; R' Eliyahu vs Rava on whether shifting place is "hanachah" — Tosafot's question on Rava remains; in our case no distinction.
