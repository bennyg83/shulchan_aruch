On the Mechaber, se'if 1: "We ascend in holiness" — I explain: take the tallis from the bag first to ascend in holiness; but if both are outside the bag, the holier comes first. If night is not time for tefillin (and Shabbat/Yom Tov), tzitzis is more frequent — "frequent precedes holy" — whichever one wishes may come first; here tzitzis is weighed against the whole Torah, so tzitzis precedes — this is Nimukey Yosef's intent in Beit Yosef. But if by Torah night is time for tefillin, and per one view even day-specific tzitzis is exempt at night, tzitzis is not more frequent — tefillin is holier; when both are outside the bag there is no proof to prioritize tzitzis. See Dagul MeRevavah; he did not examine well. See Magen Avraham siman 437 se'if kaf 2 and my gloss there; Pri Megadim ch. 9 from Tosafot Menachot in the name of R' Moshe Korcus (Tosafot Menachot 2, chapter 10).
