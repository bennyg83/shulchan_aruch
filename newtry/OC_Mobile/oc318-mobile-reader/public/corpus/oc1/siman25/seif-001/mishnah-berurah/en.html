After putting on the tallis. For tefillin have greater sanctity, and the rule is: we ascend in sanctity. This is specifically after he has put on his tallis, but if tefillin came to his hand first, although he has no tallis yet, he need not wait for the tallis; rather, he puts on the tefillin. When the tallis is then brought, he wraps himself in it.
For one who is careful. And if he has money to buy only one of them, the halachic authorities agreed that tefillin come first, because they are an obligation of the body. This applies specifically regarding tefillin, but one who does not have money to buy tzitzis and mezuzah, mezuzah comes first because it applies on Shabbos and weekdays, while tzitzis applies only on weekdays.
To put them on. The reason is that we do not pass over mitzvos, and since the tefillin came to his hand first, how can he leave them and wait for the mitzvah of tzitzis? Even if the tefillin are now before him and the tzitzis are also available but are not before him, he need not wait for the tzitzis; rather, he puts on tefillin immediately. The Acharonim wrote that one who has tefillin on and later receives tzitzis need not remove the tefillin and put them on again after wrapping in tzitzis.
