(1) After he wore, etc. — even if the tallis is rabbinic only, e.g. borrowed after thirty days (siman 14) — still precede it to tefillin [Aruch HaShulchan].

(2) He dons tefillin — if he cannot afford both, acharonim rule tefillin first: tzitzis only if he has a four-cornered garment; tefillin is obligatory — one who omits them is among "poshei Yisrael in their bodies" (below siman 37). All the more so for mehudar tefillin vs. nice tallis — tefillin first universally; people err here. One who cannot afford both need not pawn doorposts; one who can but borrows after praying — Bach: great punishment.

(3) That he not encounter — even without grasping, if by reaching in they lie before him first — ein ma'avirin; he must don them first. Only if donning now; if not donning until later — ein ma'avirin does not apply.

(4) He must don them — if praying at home with tallis in one room and tefillin in another before him — don tefillin first lest ein ma'avirin, even before taking them in hand [proof Yoma 33b — entering Temple, etc.].

(5) Before the tallis — if he took tefillin in hand then took tallis, forbidden to leave tallis and take tefillin.

(6) He lacks tzitzis — even going without four corners; certainly per our custom each is careful with tallit katan.

(7) Need not wait — do not delay mitzvah though later would be more choicest; mitzvah in its time is dear; tadir kodem when frequent not before us — need not wait.
