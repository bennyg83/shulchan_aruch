He should put on tefillin. The rabbi in Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah ruled that one whose means do not suffice to buy both tzitzis and tefillin, tzitzis comes first; see there. But the author of Bnei Chaya, Olas Tamid, Haleket part 1, siman 54, the responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 41, and Yad Aharon, all as one disagreed with him and ruled that tefillin comes first; see there. Whether one is obligated to go begging in order to buy tefillin and tzitzis, we learned in the Yerushalmi of Peah that he is not obligated; see there. Olas Tamid.
