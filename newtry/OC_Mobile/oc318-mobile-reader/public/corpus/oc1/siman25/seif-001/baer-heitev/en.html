He dons tefillin. The rabbi in Shiurei Kenesset HaGedolah ruled that one who cannot afford both buys tzitzis and tefillin — tzitzis first. The author of Bnei Chayya, Pri Megadim, Leket (vol. 1 siman 54), responsum Yad Eliyahu 41, and Yad Aharon all disagreed and ruled tefillin first. If one must pawn doorposts to buy tefillin and tzitzis — we learn in Yerushalmi Peah one is not obligated. Pri Megadim.
