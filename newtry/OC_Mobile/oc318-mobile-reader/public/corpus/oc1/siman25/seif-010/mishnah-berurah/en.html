(33) If he spoke — even between lehaniach and placing arm.

(34) "He need not," etc. — lechatchilah forbidden speech from lehaniach until head placed, even for tefillin needs unless unavoidable. Chol HaMoed without blessing — perhaps lenient lechatchilah for tefillin needs.

(35) Should not interrupt — even Amen to this blessing itself, e.g. another's tefillin blessing — forbidden unless mutual intent to fulfill [Darkhei Shmuel, Gra — unlike Peri Megadim].

(36) To answer with them — if interrupted and answered Kaddish, Kedusha, Barchu, Amen — repeat; see Biur Halachah. Chol HaMoed or Rabbeinu Tam without blessing — may answer; then shift arm before head for one continuity.

(37) Silent listening — silence counts as answering for Yotzei, not as interruption.
