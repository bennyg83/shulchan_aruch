[10] "Good to always say Baruch Shem…" Levush omitted — Beit Yosef dislikes inviting safek berachah relying on Baruch Shem. Lechem Chamudos: Rama did not heed Beit Yosef — we bless both; Baruch Shem for clarity, not safek (Malbushei Yom Tov, Maharash drashah 171 — we follow Rabbeinu Tam). Levush: don't say Baruch Shem lest one err into safek. Olat Tamid: better intend not to fulfill via first blessing — then head blessing valid (Menachem Azariah 102) — strange: if spoke or only head we bless twice; intending not to fulfill forces another lehaniach — should intend to cover head too (Levush). Menachem Azariah — one blessing for both; we bless two — follow Rama. Lechem Chamudos: "always" includes after speech — for us repeat lehaniach; Baruch Shem between blessings; if spoke, intend Baruch Shem on repeated lehaniach. So if only head alone.
