For the need of tefillin. Ab initio forbidden; but if unavoidable, speech is permitted (Magen Avraham).

While wearing them. Even to answer Amen to another's tefillin blessing he blessed for himself is forbidden (Darkhei Shmuel 142). If he interrupted and answered — dispute whether to repeat the blessing; Turei Zahav rules no repeat, and Darkhei Shmuel agrees; see Panim Me'irot, Orach Chayim 10. Magen Avraham: one who dons on Chol HaMoed without blessing may answer — prohibition is only lest it cause a blessing, and here there is none. Therefore one may answer Kaddish, Kedusha, and Amen between arm and head tefillin — Rabbeinu Tam, since we do not bless on them. Explicit in Darchei Noam, Orach Chayim 10. Also if a friend blesses and he intends to fulfill through the friend and the friend intends for him — he may answer Kaddish and Kedusha. Mundane talk is always forbidden (Darchei Noam; Pri Megadim se'if kaf 12). See siman 31 se'if kaf 3.
