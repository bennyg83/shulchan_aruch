"If he heard Kaddish, etc." — Mishnah Berurah 36: if interrupted and answered — repeat. Chayei Adam: we bless two on head; Mechaber — only "al mitzvot"; Aruch HaShulchan: even for us one blessing only. Beit Yosef: mutual intent to fulfill — may answer Kaddish; answering Amen to another's tefillin blessing when interrupting — dispute; causes unnecessary blessing; see Eliyahu Rabbah, siman 167. Even bedi'eved be stringent; lechatchilah forbidden to interrupt when friend blesses for him.
