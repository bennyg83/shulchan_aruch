(Seif katan 10) Baruch shem kevod malchuso, etc. As written in siman 206, it is a correction for one who recites a blessing in vain. Although initially one should not do this, to recite an unnecessary blessing and say Baruch shem kevod malchuso, nevertheless here the primary opinion of the poskim is to recite two blessings. In order additionally to satisfy the opinion of those who say that one blesses only one blessing, for according to them the second blessing is in vain, he should therefore say Baruch shem kevod malchuso.
