Rather, he is silent and listens. For silence is like answering regarding fulfilling his obligation, but not that it should be considered an interruption. It implies here that if he transgressed and answered yehei shemei rabbah and the like, it is not considered an interruption requiring him to bless a second time over the tefillin, like the law of one who interrupts with mundane speech. So too the Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Mordechai, because it is not an interruption since it is needed for him.
