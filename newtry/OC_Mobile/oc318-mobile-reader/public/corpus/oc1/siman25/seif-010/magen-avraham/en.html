If he spoke for the need. But initially it is forbidden to speak even for the need of the tefillin, as written in siman 167, seif 6. However, where it cannot be done otherwise, it is permitted to speak, as written in siman 592, seif 3.
He should not interrupt. If he interrupted and answered, he returns and blesses, as stated by the Rosh, Rashba, and Agur. Nevertheless, it appears that one who puts on tefillin on Chol HaMoed without a blessing may answer, for there is no prohibition here except because he causes an additional blessing.
