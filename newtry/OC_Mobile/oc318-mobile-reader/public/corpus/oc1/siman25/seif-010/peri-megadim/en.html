To take out. See Taz. Although they are in two pouches, nevertheless, when they are inside a large pouch in which the tallis is placed, it is impossible to see, and sometimes the pouch of the head tefillin will come to his hand. According to what the Magen Avraham wrote in letter 1, when it is in a pouch there is no issue of passing over. Nevertheless, he should make a narrow pouch. See Magen Avraham, siman 28, letter 4, that one should not do this, because the sanctity of the head tefillin is more severe than that of the arm tefillin, due to the letters on it, and it is forbidden to place the arm tefillin on the head tefillin. See what I wrote in Magen Avraham letter 1. When wrapping the straps he should make a sign, and then place his hand into the case and discern, or use a wide pouch and look into it beforehand to see which tefillin he is taking out.
