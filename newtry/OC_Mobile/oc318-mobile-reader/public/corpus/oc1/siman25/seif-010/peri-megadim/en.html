To remove. Taz — two pouches; nevertheless inside, in large pouch with tallit inside — cannot see; sometimes hand takes head pouch. Magen Avraham — one pouch, no ma'avirin. Still make narrow pouch (Magen Avraham siman 28:4) — do not do so: head holiness stricter than arm (shin-dalet); forbidden to place arm on head; Magen Avraham 1. When winding straps — mark, hand in bag, feel, or wide pouch — inspect first which tefillah he removes.
