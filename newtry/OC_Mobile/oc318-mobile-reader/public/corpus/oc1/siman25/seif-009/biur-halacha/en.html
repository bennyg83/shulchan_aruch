"If he interrupted" — interruption includes speech or distraction (Magen Avraham siman 8 se'if kaf 14); even one word (Rosh) — Amen between blessings requires repeat (Chayei Adam ch. 5 doubts one/two words between blessing and act). Beit Yosef: spoke before bayit touched flesh but after reaching head air — "held as placed"? (Leket Yosher 2:42). Aruch HaShulchan refutes — until tightened mitzvah incomplete (Ikkei Dinim, Tefillah l'David). Beit Yosef/Leket: three windings without blessing then spoke — two blessings; Aruch HaShulchan — like tallit katan then tallit gadol one blessing (OC 8:10) — one "lehaniach" covers both; Mechaber disagrees.

"And for us who practice, etc." — Mishnah Berurah se'if 32 on touching; Gra: if he spoke, bless first on head "al mitzvot," then touch arm, tighten, bless "lehaniach" — satisfies Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam.
