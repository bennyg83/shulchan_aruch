(28) Forbidden to interrupt — even mitzvah speech (greeting rabbi) — causes unnecessary blessing. Even Chol HaMoed without blessing, Rabbeinu Tam after removing Rashi, removal to return (Rama 12 — no repeat) — still transgression to speak between — lechatchilah contiguous: "sign on hand, remembrance between eyes" — one continuity.

(29) By speech — even Hebrew; lechatchilah forbidden long unnecessary silence. Winking between arm and head — be careful. Arm in one house, head in another — see siman 8:13 Mishnah Berurah (tallis) — same here.

(30) If he interrupted — even unintentionally.

(31) For us who practice, etc. — two blessings because originally they blessed lehaniach for both; when placing and tightening head — al mitzvot completes mitzvah; if spoke — repeat lehaniach on head too [Rosh].

(32) Repeat blessing — touch arm, strengthen knot — lehaniach applies to arm again as if donning now contiguously; if blessed without touching, touch after; see Biur Halachah, Gra.
