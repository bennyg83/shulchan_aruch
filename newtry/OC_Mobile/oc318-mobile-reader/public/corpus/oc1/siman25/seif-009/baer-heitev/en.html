By speech. Even in the Holy Tongue. Be’er Sheva, Knesses HaGedolah, and Olas Tamid. In the book Birkat Avraham he wrote that not only speech, but even if he put on the arm tefillin in one house and went to put on the head tefillin in another house, although at the time of the blessing his intention was for this, he must bless; so wrote Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah and Olas Tamid wrote regarding it that it requires study in practice; see there. If he hinted with his eyes or gestured with his fingers, it is an interruption between the arm tefillin and the head tefillin. Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 57. If he put on the arm tefillin and, when he came to put on the head tefillin, before the box reached the flesh but after it reached the airspace of the head, he interrupted with speech, whether we say here that something received in the airspace is like placed, see Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 42. One who put on the arm tefillin and wrapped the three upper wrappings but did not bless, and spoke in between, blesses two blessings, since blessings do not prevent fulfillment and it is properly called speaking between one tefillin and the other. Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 119.
