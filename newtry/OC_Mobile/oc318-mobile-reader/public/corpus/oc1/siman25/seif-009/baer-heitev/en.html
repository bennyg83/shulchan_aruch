By speech. Even in Hebrew (Be'er Sheva, Kenesset HaGedolah, Pri Megadim). Berakhat Avraham wrote "dibbur" — even if he placed arm tefillin in one house and went to place head tefillin in another, though at the blessing his mind was on the bayit — he must repeat the blessing; so Kenesset HaGedolah and Pri Megadim — doubtful in practice. If he signaled with eyes or winked fingers — interruption between arm and head tefillin (Leket Yosher vol. 1 siman 57). If he placed arm tefillin and before the bayit touched flesh but after reaching head air he spoke — do we say "held is as placed"? See Leket Yosher vol. 2 siman 42. One who placed arm tefillin, wound three upper windings without blessing, and spoke in between — blesses twice, since blessings do not hinder; properly he spoke between tefillin and blesses twice (Leket Yosher vol. 2 siman 119).

"Al mitzvot." He must touch the arm tefillin when saying a second time "lehaniach." Turei Zahav.
