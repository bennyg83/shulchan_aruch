It is forbidden to make an interruption with speaking between [putting on] the arm tefillin and the head tefillin, and if he spoke, he must recite the blessing on the head tefillin: "...on the Mitzvah of Tefillin". {Rama: Rem"a: And for us [Ashkenazim] that have the custom to make the two blessings even if one did not speak in between, he must go back and make both blessings on the head tefillin [his (ie. the Rema's) own opinion]}
