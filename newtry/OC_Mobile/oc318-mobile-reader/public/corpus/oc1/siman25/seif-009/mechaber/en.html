It is forbidden to make an interruption with speaking between [putting on] the arm tefillin and the head tefillin, and if he spoke, he must recite the blessing on the head tefillin: "...on the Mitzvah of Tefillin".For us, who bless twice even without speaking between, he must repeat both blessings on the head — *lehaniach* and *al mitzvot* (my own view).]:
