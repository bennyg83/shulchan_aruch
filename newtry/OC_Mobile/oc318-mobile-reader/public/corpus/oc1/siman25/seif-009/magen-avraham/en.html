It is forbidden to interrupt. For he thereby causes an additional blessing, see siman 215. Even for those who put on tefillin during Chol HaMoed without a blessing, it is nevertheless a transgression to be distracted. Beit Yosef in the name of Maharai.
To put on, and also al mitzvas. The Ran wrote: he touches the arm tefillin, strengthens the knot, and recites the blessing over the head tefillin, the two of them, etc. His reason is that since there are those who say that one must bless when touching the tefillin, and here there are those who say that one need not recite the blessing of lehaniach, therefore he should touch the arm tefillin. See seif 12.
