"Forbidden to interrupt." Causes berachah (siman 215); even those who don on Chol HaMoed without blessing — speech is transgression (Beit Yosef, Mahari Weil).

"Lehaniach and al mitzvot." Ran: touch arm, tighten knot, bless on head — some require blessing when touching; here some say no lehaniach on head — therefore touch head (siman 12).
