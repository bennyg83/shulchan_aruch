(Se'if kaf 9) "Al mitzvot" with patach. Patach — singular; cholam — plural (siman 26 Rama: two blessings for head and arm; only head — two blessings lehaniach and al mitzvot; both — lehaniach on arm covers both, al mitzvot on head suffices; only head — both needed; only arm — only lehaniach, for al mitzvot does not apply to arm alone).

Even per Tur, etc. See Taz se'if kaf 9 — if al mitzvot also on arm, two mitzvot — should say mitzvot with cholam.

For forbidden, etc. Blessing on two as one — "Bless the Lord day by day" — each day its blessing; so every mitzvah.

"Mitzvot Hashem tzivah" — patach on mitzvot for all mitzvot — all mitzvot interlinked, many mitzvot called singular (Ekev: "kol hamitzvah"). Here mitzvot patach: if only head — one mitzvah, singular fits; if also arm — two mitzvot, still singular as many mitzvot use singular.

Does not fit the other explanation — for those who hold al mitzvot is only head (one mitzvah) yet say cholam plural.
