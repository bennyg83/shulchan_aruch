Magen Avraham seif katan 15 And see siman 12. Perhaps the correct text is siman 11, for there, at the end of the siman, it is explained that through handling them the blessing properly applies to them. Or perhaps the correct text is: “and see seif 12,” for there the law of handling is explained. Nevertheless, this requires study, for it is not comparable to siman 11, since there he had not yet recited the blessing on the tzitzis, unlike here where he already recited the blessing on the shel yad; and it is not comparable to seif 12, for there it discusses where they slipped out of place.
