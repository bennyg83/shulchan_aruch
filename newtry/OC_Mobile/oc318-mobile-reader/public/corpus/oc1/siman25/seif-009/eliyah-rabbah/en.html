[9] Lehaniach tefillin, etc. We do not bless likshor — main mitzvah is wearing all day; likshor would imply momentary fulfillment. Therefore lehaniach — at least one hour (Bach). Mekor Chaim: lehaniach also exempts head when only arm. Lechem Chamudos: tefillin with dagesh mem — doubling / pelilah. If placed head first — siman 484.
