Se'if 9 — forbidden to interrupt in speech. Panim Me'orot 1:59 — if heard in between, one blesses on arm tefillin — may answer Amen; that Amen on hanachat tefillin is not hefsek. Devar Shmuel 242 — do not answer Amen; proof from Tur 61 (Rama): Amen on "haBocher beAmo Yisrael beAhavah" — no hefsek, not worse than greeting — implies otherwise Amen is hefsek even on same blessing. Mechaber 66:7 — no Amen after Ga'al Yisrael — hefsek though Amen is on Ga'al — per R' Ovadiah: Amen is within redemption / semichat ge'ullah, but between blessing and mitzvah it is hefsek.
