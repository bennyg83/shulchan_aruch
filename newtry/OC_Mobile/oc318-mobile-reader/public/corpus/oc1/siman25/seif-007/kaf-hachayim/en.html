(7) Before tallit — ein ma'avirin. Beit Yosef, Levush, Taz. Tefillin: pass head, remove — arm first (verse order). Here only asmakhta — better delay asmakhta than pass mitzvah. Ari: tzitzit before tefillin by sod — if encounter tefillin first, remove and don tzitzit (veShamartem et hamitzvah). Levush: ordered mitzvot may pass over; Eliyahu Zuta 1; Yafeh Lelev 6; Ben Ish Chai.
