To place with kamatz. Though no difference in meaning, bless in biblical wording "lehaniach" (Beit Yosef, Agur). Responsum of R' Yitzchak Halevi z"l on "al mitzvot" / plural. Question: custom like Hagahot Maimoniyot and R"T — on arm "lehaniach tefillin," on head "al mitzvot tefillin"; when donning both, holam or kamatz on "mitzvot"? Answer: Mishnah — arm does not block head; two mitzvot (Rif, Rosh, Rambam, Tur). Tosafot difficulty from slaughter — resolved: distinct bodies/places; each has remembrance and placement — adjoin blessings; ein ma'avirin; if spoke — bless twice on head. R' Ephraim vs. Mordechai on Torah reading — R' Yitzchak: first blessing not void retroactively. Conclusion: "al mitzvot" with holam when both; kamatz if only head; only arm — two blessings (Semak) — Tur on arm alone only lehaniach is against poskim.
