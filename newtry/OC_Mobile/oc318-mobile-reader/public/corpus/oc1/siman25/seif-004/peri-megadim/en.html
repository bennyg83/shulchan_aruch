And he places. Taz cites Riaz end of Rosh HaShanah; Beit Yosef via Ran; Magen Avraham s"k 15. Note: Riaz there concludes like blessing twice on head — third view: Rif — if spoke…; Rabbeinu Tam — two on head, lehaniach also on head; Riaz — two mitzvot (all agree); lehaniach only on arm unless spoke between — "and it shall be a sign… totafot" one continuity, no break (even long needless silence forbidden). If interrupted — repeat lehaniach on arm, touch arm, adjoin head with al mitzvot; Shach's two blessings are not two on head but appears so — truly lehaniach on arm. Even if blessed lehaniach anew without touching — still touch before al mitzvot for contiguity. We hold Shach blesses literally two on head (siman 26 — head alone, two like R"T, not Riaz) — seemingly no touch needed; Magen Avraham s"k 15 — touch to resolve doubt (better view: tefillin, touch to bless). Magen Avraham's proof in Taz: Beit Yosef brought Ran on Riaz without concluding two on head — like R"T; practice like R'T touches. Still: if spoke and blessed lehaniach without touch — touch so two mitzvot adjoin; silence too — no unnecessary pause. Understand Magen Avraham 15; see s"k 200, Orach Tov conditional intent, Avodat Rachamim 10 — do not practice; understand.
