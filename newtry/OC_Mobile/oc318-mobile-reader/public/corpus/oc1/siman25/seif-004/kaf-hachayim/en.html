(4) Rashi tefillin, wants R"T, no tzitzit. Tzitzit first — most don't wear R"T at all. If only R"T without Rashi — Rashi tefillin first (main for berachah, siman 34). R"T holier but for yotzei Rashi first; world omitted R"T (Zohar). Ari: Rashi mochin before R"T. If cannot buy both — Rashi first. When cannot buy both — tefillin before tzitzit (bittul tefillin worse, siman 37). R"T vs tzitzit — see Yafeh Lelev; Ari allows either order if won't cancel.
