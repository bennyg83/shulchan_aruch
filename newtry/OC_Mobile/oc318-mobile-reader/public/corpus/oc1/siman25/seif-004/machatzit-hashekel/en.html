(Seif katan 4) In his house, etc. Similar to this he wrote in siman 168, seif 1: if before him there is a piece of bread and a whole loaf, he must bless HaMotzi over the whole one. The Rama wrote there that this is specifically if his intent is to eat both; but if he wants to eat only from one of them, he should break that one, even though the second is more beloved and more significant.
