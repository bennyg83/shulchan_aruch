(14) At time of Shema and prayer — at least then (siman 37:2). Gemara: Shema without tefillin — false testimony (Tosafot: says "bind them" but does not bind; even if bedi'eved fulfilled Shema, shows unwillingness to fulfill God's will). Another explanation — Levush; Chareidim: from "you shall love the Lord" — enter love in heart, not false speech. Only when willfully lazy before Shema; without tefillin, traveling, cold preventing donning — no fault for late Shema (Levush siman 58 — cited there).
