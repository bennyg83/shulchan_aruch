At the time of Shema and prayer. Meaning, at least at the time of Shema and prayer, as below in siman 37, seif 2. The Gemara says that anyone who reads Shema without tefillin is as though he bears false testimony about himself, Heaven forbid. Tosafot explained that this is because he says “and you shall bind them as a sign,” etc., and does not bind. Although after the fact he fulfilled Shema, nevertheless he has another transgression, for he shows about himself that he does not want to fulfill the will of Hashem; this is the false testimony he bears about himself. There is another explanation; see the Levush. Sefer Chareidim wrote that from this we learn that when one says “and you shall love Hashem,” etc., he should see to it that he brings love of Hashem into his heart, so that he not be like one speaking falsely, Heaven forbid. Know that they said this only when he does so intentionally, being lazy about putting on tefillin before Shema. But one who has no tefillin, or is on the road and because of cold and chill cannot put on tefillin, and similar cases, certainly should not delay Shema past its time because of this.
