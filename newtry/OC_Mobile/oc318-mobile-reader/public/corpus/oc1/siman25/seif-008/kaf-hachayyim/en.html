8) There: So that he does not pass over the mitzvah. If one's Shabbos tallis was in the bag in which he keeps his weekday tallis and tefillin, and the Shabbos tallis was on top of everything, he may set aside the Shabbos tallis and take the weekday tallis. This does not violate the rule against passing over mitzvos, since he does not intend to wear it now, on a weekday, but only on Shabbos. Birkei Yosef 3; see there, where he adduces proof from Magen Avraham 4. See also Siddur Beis Yaakov, laws of the place where tefillin are put on, 2; Shalmei Tzibbur 37a; Zechirah LeChaim 9; Shaarei Teshuvah 2; and Chesed LaAlafim 2.
