Magen Avraham, seif katan 15. 'Therefore he should touch the arm tefillin, etc.' This is difficult for me, for he gains nothing through this. If we take Rashi's approach into account, according to which one who spoke recites two blessings, this means that he recites al mitzvat tefillin separately. It follows that when he did not speak and recites only one blessing, he does not recite any blessing over the head tefillin itself. Consequently, when he touches the arm tefillin and recites lehaniach, it is no longer appropriate for him to recite al mitzvat tefillin over the head tefillin. Perhaps he actually first recites al mitzvat tefillin over the head tefillin and afterward touches the arm tefillin and recites lehaniach, but the wording does not imply this. The matter requires examination.
