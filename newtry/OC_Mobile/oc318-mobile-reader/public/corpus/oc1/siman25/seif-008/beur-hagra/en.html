Seif 8, therefore, etc. The Gemara there.
And likewise with the tefillin shel rosh, etc. The Rosh explains that it refers to both of them, from the time, etc.; and as Rabbeinu Tam explains there, that “binding” means tightening, like the term binding in Chullin 77b, Niddah 26b, and Avodah Zarah 5a. It cannot mean the actual tying of the knot each day, as Rabbi Eliyahu explains, for if so it should have said the reverse, “from the time of tying until,” etc. And in Eruvin 94a it calls it a permanent knot; if so, were it as he says, it would not be a permanent knot, as stated at the beginning of chapter 15 of Shabbos and in the final chapter of Mikvaos. “Tefillin shel rosh,” etc., “and shel zeroa,” etc.; see Tosafot in Menachos there, s.v. “from the time,” and Chullin 9a.
