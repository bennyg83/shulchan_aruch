Se'if 8 — Therefore, etc. Gemara there.

And likewise for head tefillin, etc. Rosh — he speaks of both from the time of placement; Rabbeinu Tam there — "keshirah" means tightening, as binding in Chullin 17b, Niddah 26b, Avodah Zarah 5a — not literal knot (R' Eliyahu) — otherwise the order should be reversed. Eruvin 24a calls it a lasting knot; if not lasting, as in refu'ot on Shabbat and end of Mikvaot; head and arm tefillin — see Tosafot Menachot s.v. mishe'et and Chullin 9a.
