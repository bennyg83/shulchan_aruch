But. See Taz, who explained opposite to the words of the Magen Avraham in letter 17. Rather, he holds that he does not return and bless, like the Mordechai. Although in the Beit Yosef he wrote that it appears from the Mordechai that one must interrupt and answer Kaddish and Kedushah, in the Shulchan Aruch he ruled that initially one should not do so, just as speaking for a need is not permitted initially. I elaborated on this in Magen Avraham letter 17. See Levush, who ruled explicitly that if one interrupted to answer the response to Kaddish, he returns and blesses. See siman 21, Magen Avraham 3.
