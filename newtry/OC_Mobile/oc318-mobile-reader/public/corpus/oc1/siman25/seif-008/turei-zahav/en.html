Therefore one must bless, etc. Meaning, before placing it he certainly should not bless, for in any event we require it to be adjacent to the performance, and that would be before the before. Therefore he should bless after placing it and before the essential performance, which is the tying, meaning the tightening of the strap that is placed on the arm. So the Beit Yosef explained in his first explanation. He also gave a second explanation, that it comes only to exclude blessing after the tightening, but before it; if he wants to bless even before the placing, that is proper. The Beit Yosef agreed to the first explanation. It is difficult, for how is this different from the blessing al biur chametz, which is recited before the searching, because the searching is needed for the removal, as the Tur wrote at the beginning of Hilchos Pesach? Certainly we should say here that the placing is needed for the tying, which is the main performance. This is a proof for the second explanation. One can answer according to the first explanation that even by checking chametz it would have been proper to bless before the removal, but its time is not fixed during the day, when a person walks in the market, and there is concern that through his occupations the time will pass, unlike at night, when every person is found in his house. But here the tightening after the placement is certainly immediate, therefore he should do the choicest manner, as it appears to me.
