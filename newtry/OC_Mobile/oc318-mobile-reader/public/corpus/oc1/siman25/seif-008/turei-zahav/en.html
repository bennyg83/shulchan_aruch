Therefore one must bless, etc. First explanation: cannot bless long after act — still "before" main act (tightening strap on arm) — bless after placing, before tightening (Beit Yosef). Second: only not after tightening; may bless even before placing — Beit Yosef agrees. Chametz: bless before bedikah because bedikah serves bi'ur — a fortiori here placement serves tying (second view). First view: bedikah should be before bi'ur but daytime fear of missing time — not so here, tightening follows placement immediately.
