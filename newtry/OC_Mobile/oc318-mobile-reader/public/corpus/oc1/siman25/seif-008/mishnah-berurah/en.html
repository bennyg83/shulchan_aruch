After placement, etc. For before that, initially it is not proper to bless, since it is too early before the act, and one must bring the blessing as close as possible to the performance of the mitzvah.
Before tying them. After the fact he may bless even afterward, because it is a mitzvah whose duration extends throughout the whole day.
Before tightening them. For the tightening is the mitzvah of tying. One must also be careful that the blessing be after they are placed on the head, not like those who bless while they are still in their hands, for if so it is too early before the act. Also at the time of the blessing on the head tefillin, he should see that his head is covered with the tallis and not bless with an uncovered head.
