(25) After placement — before then lechatchilah improper to bless (too early); bring blessing close to mitzvah act.

(26) Before tying — bedi'eved may bless after, since mitzvah lasts all day.

(27) Before tightening — tightening is mitzvah of tying; bless after on head, not in hand (too early). When blessing on head, head covered with tallis — not bareheaded.
