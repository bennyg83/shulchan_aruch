(Se'if kaf 8) Tefillin — mem with dagesh: Tur — called tefillin from pelilah (pleading), sign Shechinah dwells on us ("all nations see God's Name called on you" — head tefillin). Root palal — should be two mems like palal; dagesh on mem as double mem. Rashi Vayishlach: "chanani" — no chanan without two nuns.
