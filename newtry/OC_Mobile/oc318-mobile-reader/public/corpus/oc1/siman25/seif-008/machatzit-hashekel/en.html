(Sek 8) Tefillin of the Lamad in the Dagash according to the column 20 and Zal and are called Tefillin L. Pilalah which are a sign and testimony to all our witnesses that the Shechinah has a line on us and all the peoples of the land have seen that the name of the Lord will be called upon you and they will see from you and my sermons. These are Tefillin that are at the head of Ekal and since the Detfillin that recited Pellal Ach had to write Tefillin on the second Madin like Pellal and that is why it is emphasized in the Lamad Faded like these, two midi'an were written.
