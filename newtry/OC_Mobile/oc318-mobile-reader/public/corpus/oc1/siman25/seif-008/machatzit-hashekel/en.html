(Seif katan 8) Tefillin, the lamed with a dagesh. Because the Tur wrote as follows: They are called tefillin, from the language of judgment or testimony, for they are a sign and testimony to all who see us that the Shechinah rests upon us, as it is written, “and all the peoples of the earth shall see that the Name of Hashem is called upon you and they shall fear you,” and we expound: these are the tefillin of the head. End quote. Since tefillin has its root in palal, it should have been written with two lameds, like palal; therefore a dagesh comes in the lamed, which is as though two lameds were written. So wrote Rashi in parshas Vayishlach on the verse “for God has favored me,” that there is no chanan without two nuns.
