[8] On the arm, etc. Another reason is that placement on the arm corresponds to the sense of touch, which depends upon the hand; it therefore has only one compartment. The four compartments upon the head correspond to the four senses located in the head: sight, hearing, smell, and taste. This is intended to arouse a person through all five senses to remember God's kindnesses and His unity, and that He watches over all His creatures. As an allusion to this, the right strap extends to the navel, indicating that the influence upon the lower world, by its Creator's command, extends to the navel of the world. This excludes the opinion of those who say that Divine providence reaches only as far as the sphere of the moon and no farther. We also find that the hand-tefillin commemorate the mighty hand, while the head-tefillin commemorate the Exodus from Egypt, which they saw with their eyes. See above, siman 8:9, regarding whether one who put on tefillin without intention fulfilled his obligation.
