On the arm, etc. A further reason: on the arm corresponds to the sense of touch, which depends on the hand, and therefore it has only one compartment; on the head there are four compartments corresponding to the four senses in the head, sight, hearing, smell, and taste. This is so that a person be aroused through the five senses and remember the kindnesses of G-d, His unity, and that He watches over all His creatures. To allude to this, the right strap reaches to the navel, hinting that the influence upon the lower world by the command of its Creator extends until the navel of the world, excluding the view of those who say that Divine supervision reaches only until the sphere of the moon and no further. We also find that tefillin shel yad are a remembrance of the strong hand, and tefillin shel rosh are a remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt that they saw with their eyes. See above, siman 8.
