[13] After placing it upon the biceps, etc. Bach 6 likewise writes that one should not begin the berachah until after placing the hand-tefillin upon the biceps, before fastening it. For the head-tefillin, he should not recite the berachah until after placing it around his head, before tightening it upon his head. This is also the tradition I received. When he recites 'to put on tefillin' a second time, he should touch the hand-tefillin. Taz 4; Matzas Shimurim, s.v. VeAchar Kach Kodem.
