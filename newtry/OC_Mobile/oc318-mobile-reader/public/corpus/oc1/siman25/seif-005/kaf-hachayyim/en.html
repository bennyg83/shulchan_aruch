5) One who cannot afford both head-tefillin and hand-tefillin should first buy head-tefillin. Divrei Moshe 3:1; see there; Shalmei Tzibbur 36a; and Shaarei Teshuvah at the end of paragraph 1. Yafeh LaLev 26:1, however, writes that he should first buy hand-tefillin; see there. If one cannot afford either tefillin or tzitzis, he is not obligated to go begging from door to door in order to buy them. Olas Tamid 1 in the name of Yerushalmi Peah; Eliyah Rabbah 1; and Shesilei Zeisim at the end of paragraph 1. Nevertheless, he should endeavor to borrow tzitzis and tefillin so that he can recite Krias Shema and pray while wearing them. Likewise, regarding what we wrote above; that one who has neither tzitzis nor tefillin and cannot afford both should first buy tefillin; he should endeavor to borrow tzitzis. Similarly, one who has only one of the two tefillin should endeavor to borrow tzitzis and the other tefillah, so as not to reverse the rectification and order prescribed by the Arizal: one must first don tzitzis, then tefillin, and afterward pray, as stated above in paragraph 1. He should at least endeavor to don the tzitzis and tefillin before reciting the passage of the daily offering, for that is the order according to the Arizal. See below, paragraph 27.
