[5] Even with a tallis, etc. The kabbalists write likewise, and conclude that when one puts on the hand-tefillin he should do so discreetly; that is, he should cover his arm with the tallis and put them on. Magen Avraham 5, however, writes that he found it recorded in the name of Minhagei Mahari that one wraps himself in the tallis gadol in the synagogue. In places where non-Jews are commonly found in the street, all agree that he should wrap himself in the courtyard of the synagogue. Magen Avraham 4 further writes that even if the tallis came into his hand before the tefillin, he need not wrap himself in it at home, since he does not intend to wear it there.
