Even with a tallis, etc. So wrote the kabbalists, and they concluded that when one puts on the tefillin shel yad he should place them discreetly, meaning he should cover the arm with the tallis and then put them on. But Magen Avraham wrote that he found in the name of the practices of Mahariv that in the beis knesses he wrapped himself in a large tallis, and in places where non-Jews are commonly found in the street, all agree that he should wrap himself in the courtyard of the beis knesses. He further wrote that even if the tallis came to his hand before the tefillin, he need not wrap himself in it at home, since he does not intend to wear it there.
