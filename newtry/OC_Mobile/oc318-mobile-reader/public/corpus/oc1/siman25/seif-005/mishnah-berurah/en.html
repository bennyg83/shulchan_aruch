(15) Intent when donning — Bach siman 8: from "sign on your hand… Torah in your mouth… strong hand" — essence depends on kavanah at fulfillment; Pri Megadim: bedi'eved intent for mitzvah alone suffices.

(16) Donning four parshiyot — some read Kadesh and Ve'hayah ki yeviacha after donning (Shema and Ve'hayah im shamo'a recited at Shema); Rabbeinu Tam tefillin — all four. Fine custom [Aruch HaShulchan].

(17) First — "you shall bind them" then "they shall be totafot."

(18) And he blesses — before tightening, as below.

(19) Tefillin — mem with dagesh.

(20) "Al mitzvot" — patach under vav (singular), not cholam — blessing fixed for head tefillin only (siman 26; most acharonim, unlike Taz).

(21) Good to say Baruch Shem — safek berachah le'vatalah (siman 46), not full safek. Ashkenazim follow Rabbeinu Tam; Baruch Shem for clarity. Be careful: Baruch Shem only after head tefillin properly tightened — else interruption between blessing and placement — berachah le'vatalah, must repeat; people err.
