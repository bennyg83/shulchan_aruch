There in hagahah — custom like Rashi. Tikkun for berakhah leVatalah: when blessing lehaniach, intend — if law like Rashi, not to exempt head; then bless "al mitzvot" on head. If like Tosafot (two on head), intend lehaniach also covers head. Avodat Yisrael (Or Zaru'a): intend lehaniach not to exempt head — then per Tosafot need two on head; after all, basis to intend not to exempt. Compare siman 174; conditional intent like siman 489 Magen Avraham 7.
