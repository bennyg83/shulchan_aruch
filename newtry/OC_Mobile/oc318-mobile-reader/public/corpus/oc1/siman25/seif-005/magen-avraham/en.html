"He should intend." Bach (Maharal Fivas): read Kadesh and Ve'hayah ki yeviacha too (Shelah). Sifrei Va'etchanan — only Shema and Ve'hayah im shamo'a recited; Yalkut: need not recite others; one who does does not lose — they considered adding Balak to Shema for exodus but feared public burden (Berakhot ch. 1); these also contain exodus.

"And he places, etc." Pri Megadim did not cite siman 28.

"Tefillin." Mem with dagesh (Lechem Chamudos).

"Al mitzvot." Patach under vav — only on head alone (siman 26); even if Tur includes arm, I say patach — both considered one (Levush). We rule Sukkah 46 like Ri — forbidden one blessing on two mitzvot — both one unit; verse "mitzvot Hashem tzivah" — all mitzvot singular; mitzvot cholam doesn't fit other views.

"Baruch Shem." Due to safek berachah le'vatalah — siman 46.
