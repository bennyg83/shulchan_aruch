"And good, etc." — Gra rules like Mechaber; R' Akiva Eiger suggests intending during "lehaniach" (if halacha follows Rashi) not to fulfill via that blessing for head — then bless head separately [not unnecessary blessing due to safek; see Aruch HaShulchan]. Olat Tamid and Pri Megadim explain; Aruch HaShulchan agrees in theory; Pri Megadim concludes not to practice this lest one doubt established custom.
