Rabbi Akiva Eiger's novellae suggest that when reciting 'to put on tefillin,' one should intend: If the law follows Rashi's position, I intend that this berachah not discharge the head-tefillin. This would be equivalent to having spoken between the tefillin, allowing him to recite a berachah over the head-tefillin. This is not considered causing an unnecessary berachah, because the uncertainty in the law compels him to do so; Artzos HaChaim gives additional reasons. Olas Tamid already preceded him with this suggestion, according to Pri Megadim's explanation of his words. Artzos HaChaim also appears to agree. Pri Megadim, however, concludes that one should not follow this practice. Perhaps his reason is that a person should not accustom himself to question the Jewish custom, which was established according to the views of many Rishonim.
