On… and on… As stated siman 27 se'ifim 1 and 9.

That they teach, etc. As in "that you may know," "against all the gods of Egypt," and similar verses.

Which is on the brain. Shabbat 61a.

Which is the essence. As in Avot chapter 2: "All his words are your words"; Yerushalmi Berakhot: "If you do not give Me your heart, I know you are entirely Mine"; Midrash: the heart thinks, the heart desires — therefore God commanded placing tefillin there to accept the yoke of heaven in heart and soul.

And he places. Menachot 36a.

And he blesses. There (ibid.).

And some say, etc. Per Tosafot there and depending on textual variants — our texts: he repeats and blesses; only Tosafot's explanation fits (what is asked and answered). Tosefta Berakhot ch. 6: when donning he says "asher kidshanu… lehaniach tefillin" — strong difficulty for Tosafot. Tosefta on terumah/maaser: one blessing if he separated all at once without interruption; Rambam Maaserot 1:16; Shulchan Aruch Yoreh Deah 331:78 — one may bless once for several distinct mitzvot if uninterrupted. How bless twice on head after arm is placed? If only head (siman 26:2) — say twice "veTzivanu" on one mitzvah? Several tallitot with tzitzit — one blessing if uninterrupted; here two blessings for head alone after blessing arm? Tosafot Berakhot 60b s.v. asher — forced reading "menach adrei'a." Gemara Menachot 36a asks and answers: spoke — bless twice; did not speak — one blessing — meaning they count how many berakhot apply to tefillin as with terumah when interrupted; thus our Gemara need not argue with that Tosefta, Yerushalmi, Zohar, Rav Hai Gaon, Rav Acha — this is primary.
