And some say, etc. This follows the explanation of Tosafot there, and depends on the variant readings. According to the reading in our books, “he returns and recites a blessing,” it can be explained only like Tosafot, for otherwise what is the question and what is the answer? So too in Tanchuma. But according to the reading of the Rif and Rambam, who do not read “he returns,” but rather “he recites a blessing,” it must be like Rashi. The view of Rashi, Rif, and Rambam is primary, and so the Rashba wrote in a responsum that their text is very correct, for it does not read “he returns.” There is decisive proof from what is stated in the Tosefta of Berachos, chapter 6: “When he puts them on, he says, Who sanctified us, etc., to put on tefillin.” This is a very strong difficulty against the explanation of Tosafot from the above Tosefta. There is a further difficulty from the same chapter of the Tosefta regarding the blessing over terumah and maaser: “If he was going to separate terumah and maaser, he recites one blessing,” and so the Rambam rules in the first chapter.
