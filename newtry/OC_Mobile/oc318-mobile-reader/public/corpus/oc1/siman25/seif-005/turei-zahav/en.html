And he shall place arm tefillin. "Bind them as a sign on your hand… and they shall be totafot" — Gemara: if he spoke, one blessing; if not, two — Rashi: one on arm and head together (lehaniach); if he spoke between them, bless again "al mitzvot" on head — two with lehaniach on arm. Rabbeinu Tam: if he did not speak, bless only "al mitzvot" on head besides lehaniach on arm; if he spoke, two on head. Shulchan Aruch like Rashi; Rama: custom like R"T; Rosh: blessings instituted so lehaniach begins donning and applies to head too; on head "al mitzvot" completes mitzvah — if distracted, repeat lehaniach; head alone — two blessings. Ra'avad (Beit Yosef): if spoke and blesses two on head, touch arm when saying lehaniach again.
