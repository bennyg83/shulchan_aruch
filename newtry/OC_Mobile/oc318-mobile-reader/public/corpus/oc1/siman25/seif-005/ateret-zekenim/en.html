Place the arm tefillin first and bless "to place" — not "to tie," though the Torah says "and you shall bind them" — because the essence of tefillin is wearing them all day; blessing "to tie" would imply fulfilling the mitzvah in the moment of tying; therefore they enacted "to place," implying at least an hour of wearing (Mahari Weil, Orach Chayim, Bach). It is valid to don tefillin at minchah (responsum Menachem Azariah 39). Do not remove them until "as long as the heavens are over the earth" — evening; "tefillin" relates to pleading, as Pinchas who pleaded with his Maker — testimony that God's Name is upon us and we fear Him (Tosafos, chapter HaKometz). Place the arm strap on the titura immediately after tying so it not shift — if it shifted and one does not know whether before head tefillin or after, there is doubt about repeating the blessing. Tie with three windings on the head and four on the arm, then place head tefillin (R' Yehuda Leon in Shulchan Aruch, according to truth) (Orach Chayim). In these countries both are donned standing, since we cannot attain the secret correctly and might "cut the saplings" — therefore stand like a servant before his master receiving his seal (Maharshal, responsum 98:13). One who prays Musaf with tefillin is liable to death (Zohar Pinchas) — perhaps also where they say "Keter" in Musaf Kedusha (Rama). Proper to remove them at Musaf when saying "May it be before You that we keep Your statutes in this world," etc. — because Eastern communities blessed on removing tefillin at the end of the Amidah to keep His statutes, though one should not bless on removal since there is no blessing in the Shulchan Aruch; some do not don on Chol HaMoed, and certainly not on Sukkot Chol HaMoed when the four species correspond to four parshiyot in tefillin — so it seems to me.
