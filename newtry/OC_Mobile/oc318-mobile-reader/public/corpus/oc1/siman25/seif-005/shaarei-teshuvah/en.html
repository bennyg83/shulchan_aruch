Two blessings. Abudraham; Machzik Berachah: mistaken — blessed arm tefillin with "al mitzvot" — fulfilled. Ashkenazim: if remembered before tightening, may bless lehaniach before tightening and al mitzvot on head (Maharshal, Weintraub). Michtam L'David disagrees: Ashkenazim — lehaniach before tightening, no blessing on head; if not remembered, lehaniach alone when placing head.
