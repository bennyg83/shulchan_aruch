Two. Blessings. See Baer Heitev. In Machazik Berachah he wrote that one who erred and blessed over the tefillin of the hand al mitzvas tefillin has fulfilled his obligation. If he is among Ashkenazim, then if he remembered before tightening, he should also recite lehaniach before tightening, and over the tefillin of the head he recites al mitzvas. So wrote Maharam Ventura. In Michtam LeDavid he disagreed with him and wrote that among Ashkenazim, if he remembered before tightening, he should recite lehaniach, and over the tefillin of the head he should not bless at all. If he did not remember, then when he puts on the tefillin of the head he should recite only lehaniach; see there.
