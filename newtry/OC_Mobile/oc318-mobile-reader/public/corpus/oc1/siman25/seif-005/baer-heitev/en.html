First. See Rama of Fano, siman 120, and Responsa Darkei Noam, Orach Chaim part 1, siman 3, who discusses at length whether the blessings over tefillin must be recited standing.
To put on tefillin. The lamed is with a dagesh. Lechem Chamudos. For al mitzvas, one should bless with the vav pronounced with a patach; Lechem Chamudos, letter 26, and so wrote the Magen Avraham. But the Taz wrote in the name of his brother, Rav Yitzchak HaLevi, that one should say the vav with a cholam, in the plural, for there are two mitzvos and it refers to both the head tefillin and the arm tefillin. For the Gemara states that the arm tefillin does not prevent fulfillment of the head tefillin, and the head tefillin does not prevent fulfillment of the arm tefillin. This proves that they are two mitzvos, for if they were one mitzvah they would prevent one another, as we learned regarding tzitzis that the four tzitzis prevent one another because they are one mitzvah. From this we learn that regarding tzitzis one blesses mitzvas tzitzis with a patach, because they are one mitzvah, but regarding tefillin one blesses mitzvos with a cholam, because they are two mitzvos. The same applies if he has only the head tefillin alone, that he should bless with a patach; see there.
