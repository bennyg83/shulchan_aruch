First. See Rama of Pano siman 120 and responsum Darchei Noam, Orach Chayim 3, which discusses whether tefillin blessings require standing.

To place tefillin. The mem has a dagesh (Levush). "Al mitzvot" — bless with patach (Levush, letter 26; Magen Avraham agrees). Turei Zahav cites his brother R' Yehudah Halevi: say cholam — plural, two mitzvos — both head and arm; in the Talmud arm tefillin do not hinder head and head does not hinder arm — proving two mitzvos; if one mitzvah they would hinder each other as four tzitzis hinder each other being one mitzvah. From tzitzis we learn: bless "mitzvat tzitzit" with patach (one mitzvah); for tefillin bless "mitzvot" with cholam (two). Likewise if he has only head tefillin, bless with patach. Pri Megadim.
