(5) Cannot afford head and arm. Buy head first (Davar Moshe 3:1; Shatz; Yafeh Lelev 26). Not obligated to pawn doors (Yerushalmi Peah; Orach Tov). Strive to borrow tzitzit and tefillin for Shema/tefillah; buy tefillin first, borrow tzitzit; one tefillah — borrow the other — preserve Ari's order (tzitzit then tefillin before prayer).
