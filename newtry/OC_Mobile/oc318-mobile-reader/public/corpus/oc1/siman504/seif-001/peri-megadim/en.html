Grinding. Taz Beitzah 14a and Tosafot there, and Rabbenu Chananel in hagahah like Tur and Tosafot — and both are stringent when he knew from yesterday, even though it nullifies taste, or when he did not know and it does not nullify — forbidden on Yom Tov only by way of a change. There is a printing defect and it should read that Rabbenu Chananel should have said: when he knows, they are accustomed to forbid; but an am ha'aretz and it nullifies — permitted, only because of the multitude of people, etc.; see Beit Yosef and Levush. In our novellae and in the introduction to Yom Tov laws we wrote for the view that grinding is forbidden — see Tosafot Beitzah 3a: it is difficult how they permitted grinding spices on Yom Tov in their usual manner; see there and it may be said. To grind — on Yom Tov in a pestle it is permitted because it nullifies; in a mill — weekday labor, forbidden even though it has no sieve-like vessel, and even by change in a mill is forbidden; responsa Panim Me'irot. And almonds on Yom Tov, etc. — Magen Avraham 495 note 3, Beit Yeshash first chapter siman 39, see there; and squeezing is forbidden on Yom Tov like Shabbat — meaning even though they nullify their taste; and siman 495 in Magen Avraham 4. And know: salt means even what is dug from earth or from the sea; but salt from water that became thick (called talpin and the like) — siman 321 seifim 58-59; and in Magen Avraham there 12: hard cheese in Reib Eizen is forbidden — but grinding may be said it is not gathered from the outset; and further there is no grinding except in a mill, see there; and if so what is forbidden on Yom Tov — grating cheese in Reib Eizen — the reason implies it is like weekday labor; and to grind baked matzah that is permitted on Pesach even though there is no grinder after grinder — nevertheless requires further study regarding Reib Eizen as said; and in my humble opinion those who permit from the reason there is no grinder after grinder — requires further study; and on the two first days of Yom Tov one may say impossible from Yom Tov needs, forbidden lest he come to eat, see there in my humble opinion; and in siman 471 he wrote there is no concern, see there; and in Magen Avraham there that on the 13th it is permitted to eat matzah — and if so he will still crumble on the 13th, see there. And see siman 506 note 3 in Taz it will be explained further. And Reib Eizen for grinding on Yom Tov — one may say forbidden because the way is to do it for many days; but if not so, permitted because it nullifies from Yom Tov needs, even though it is actual grinding — nevertheless we see specifically with grain grinding applies and not with pepper and the like; and siman 495 on this. And what he wrote on Rif regarding turmeric — Eshel Avraham 1 and 5; and in our novellae we wrote more on this. And Magen Avraham notes 7, 8, 9 — the law of Reib Eizen and baked matzah for grinding; and see there it will be explained further.
