(1) [Seif 1] One grinds the spices in their usual manner, etc. — this is the view of the Rif and the Rambam chapter 3; but according to Tosafos, pepper and spices too need grinding if he knows from erev Yom Tov how many pots he wishes to cook, and if he does not know it is permitted; and garlic, mustard, and cress even if he knows from erev Yom Tov it is permitted, because they are damaged more if one grinds them from erev Yom Tov. Tur. And see below in the gloss at the end of this seif and in our words there, with God's help. And although without this it is forbidden to eat peeled garlic, peeled onion, etc., over which the night passed, as stated in Niddah 17a 1a — one may say that when they are cooked or mixed with something else it does not harm if the night passed over them, as written in Zivchei Tzedek on Yoreh De'ah siman 116, section 61, see there.
