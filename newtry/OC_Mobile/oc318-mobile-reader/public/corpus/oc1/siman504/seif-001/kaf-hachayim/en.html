(1) [Seif 1] To prepare spices in their manner, etc. — this is the law of the Tur and Shulchan Aruch; see Magen Avraham and Mishna Berurah there. Pri Megadim wrote it is only through a change; see there. And the custom is to be lenient only in pressing need — Chayei Adam. And one should not crush more than needed for that day — Semag. And if the taste is nullified in the food, a change is not needed — Rivash, Beit Yosef, Yeshuos Shabbos; however some are stringent if he knew from yesterday — as in small seif 2 — Magen Avraham.
