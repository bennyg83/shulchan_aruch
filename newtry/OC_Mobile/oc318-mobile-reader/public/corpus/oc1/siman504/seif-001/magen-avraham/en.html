The spices. And the same law applies to all that are pounded, for example garlic and cress (Yam Shel Shlomo; Maggid Mishneh; Tosefta); and the same law applies to saffron (Beis Yosef; Maggid Mishneh). But the Yam Shel Shlomo, Bach, and Maharil forbid regarding saffron and peppers that do not dissipate their flavor; and by means of a change it is permitted.
And one does not grind the peppers. Even one by one (Hagahos Maimoniyos).
