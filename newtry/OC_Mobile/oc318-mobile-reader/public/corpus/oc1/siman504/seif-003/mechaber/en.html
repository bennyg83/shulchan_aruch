One may not pound groats in a large mortar, but one may pound them in a small mortar — that being the change applicable to them. In the Land of Israel even a small mortar is forbidden. Since we no longer know what is called large or small, everything should be forbidden.Rema: It is permitted to grate cheese on Yom Tov on a grater, however a slight change is required, like the pounding of salt. [Rivash §184] The same] applies to matzah without any change, since there is no grinding prohibition for foods that were already ground. [Maharil]]:
