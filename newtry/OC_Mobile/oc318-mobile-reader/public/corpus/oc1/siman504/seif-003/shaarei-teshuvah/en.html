To grate. Avodat HaTevodah; and see in Mayim Rabim siman 47 regarding grinding kakaz and kneading gikulaki on Yom Tov; and see in Panim Me'irot part 2 siman 44 that it is permitted to grind kavui in their small hand mills; and in Be'er Heitev he doubts this, but in Mishna Berurah he returned and wrote he saw in Moreh uKetziah that in everyone's home mills it is permitted, and a shopkeeper's mills are forbidden even on the second day of Yom Tov; and in Chesed David he also wrote it is permitted, but one who is stringent will be blessed, as Tosafot wrote regarding all spices — see there.
