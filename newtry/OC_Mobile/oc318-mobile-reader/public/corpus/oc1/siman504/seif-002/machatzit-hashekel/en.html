(S.k. 2) And one does not, etc. Even one by one, etc.: that is to say, for we hold that on Shabbos it is forbidden to pound; and we say on Shabbos, folio 141a, according to one opinion, that peppers with the handle of a knife are permitted to pound one by one. And Rava disagrees and says: since one changes by means of the knife handle, even a large amount is permitted; and so we hold in siman 321. Nevertheless we find according to one opinion that on Shabbos he forbids pounding peppers even by means of a change with the knife handle, and even so he permits one by one; one might have thought that the same applies on Yom Tov regarding grinding with a mill, which is forbidden: nevertheless one by one is permitted. It teaches us that this is not so, and even one by one is forbidden.
