[2] Salt, etc. In a place where there is coarse salt (Rashi and Ran). And see siman 321, se'if 8; and Tosafos there wrote that sea salt needs a change to pound that grain, but ours does not need a change since it is made by human hand; end quote. And Maharsha wrote: I do not know their intent; end quote. And it appears to me that it was difficult for them why Rav did not distinguish regarding salt between Shabbos and Yom Tov as with peppers; and they answered that with sea salt we need a complete change with the handle of a knife, specifically one by one, as with peppers; but with our salt one does not need so much of a change, and even a large amount is permitted with a wooden or stone pestle by tilting; and therefore Rav did not mention salt, since it is not applicable to our salt; and this too is only regarding coarse salt, as above. But in Piskei Tosafos this requires further study, and one can resolve it. He wrote in Sefer Yereim, Rokeach, and Semak that salt is actual ochel nefesh, and since it is possible to do it on the eve of Yom Tov they required a change; and see siman 495, se'if 1. And the Ran wrote that pounding spices too is food, in Kol Bo, folio 59 column 3; and with this he resolves what Lechem Mishneh challenged.
