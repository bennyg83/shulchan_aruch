Spices may be pounded in their usual manner, etc. This means that although all authorities agree that grinding is forbidden on Yom Tov, as written above, siman 495, se'if 2, pounding spices is nevertheless permitted because pounding them before Yom Tov would diminish their flavor. Certainly, garlic, onion, garden cress, and similar items may be pounded according to all authorities, as written in the preceding subsection.
