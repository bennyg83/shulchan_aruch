(ב) There. One grinds the spices in their usual manner, etc. — meaning: even though grinding is forbidden for everyone on Yom Tov, as written above siman 495 seif 2; nevertheless grinding spices is permitted because if one grinds them from the outset he will nullify their taste; and all the more so garlic, onion, and leeks and the like, which are permitted for everyone, as written in the previous section.
