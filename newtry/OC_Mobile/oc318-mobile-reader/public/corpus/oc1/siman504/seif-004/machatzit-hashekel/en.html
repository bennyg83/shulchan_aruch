(S.k. 4) To change, etc. And in the Yam Shel Shlomo he wrote, etc. And in the Responsa Panim Meirot, part 2, siman 44, he wrote regarding grinding coffee in a mill: it is certainly forbidden, a fortiori from garlic and cress, which dissipate flavor more than coffee, and even so it is forbidden to grind them in a mill; but to pound them with a pestle is permitted. And nevertheless one needs some change, for it does not dissipate flavor more than saffron, which requires some change by tilting; see there. And in my humble opinion, nowadays coffee is like [the case where] he knew what pot he needs to cook in, for people are accustomed to drink it every day; and even if it dissipates flavor a great deal, one needs some change.
In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 2: it is permitted to bring spices, etc. To exclude the view of Beis Shammai, who forbid, for he is concerned lest one reconsider and not pound, and it turns out to be unnecessary trouble; rather we hold like Beis Hillel, who are not concerned about reconsidering.
