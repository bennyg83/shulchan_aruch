[4] The mustard, etc. And the view of Maharshal, chapter 3, siman 1, is to permit per the Mordechai, that even peppers are only because of weekday labor; end quote. And in truth Tosafos, the Rosh, and Shibbolei HaLeket wrote this reason; and so wrote Beis Yosef in the name of the Maggid and the Ran, who wrote thus in the name of the Rashba. But it is surprising: I examined Avodat HaKodesh of the Rashba, folio 38, where he wrote the reason that this is full grinding, both regarding peppers and regarding mustard; and grinding he ruled on folio 37 is forbidden according to the Torah on Yom Tov; and it is forced to resolve. And likewise in the Maggid, chapter 3, he did not mention weekday labor. And even aside from this one should not be lenient at all against the Rashba, the Ran, and the Maggid; and so wrote the Rivash, siman 184.
