Regarding the Mechaber, seif 2. Together with the chazzan. This is likewise implied by the Mishnah in chapter 3 of tractate Bikkurim: “They prompt him,” and it does not say “they read before him,” as it says in chapter 1 of Yoma. Necessarily, he himself reads, as they said in chapter 2 of Rosh Hashanah: one person sounds the shofar and the other prompts him. Tosafos Yom Tov writes similarly there. In my humble opinion, this is a proof.
