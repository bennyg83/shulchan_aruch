(6) Two should not read — for two voices are not heard.

(7) And the prayer leader is silent — however, if the one called up errs in vowels or in trop, he assists him in a whisper.

(8) Or the prayer leader reads, etc. — and as is our custom now, that always the prayer leader reads, even when the one called up is expert, so as not to shame one who does not know how to read; and also many are found who do not know well to read the vowels and trop and wish to read, and the congregation does not fulfill through their reading, and when they prevent them from reading they come to quarrel.

(9) To read with the prayer leader — meaning from the script.

(10) In vain — for it is unreasonable that the one called up bless over the reading of the prayer leader.

(11) Quietly — for two voices are not heard; this applies only when reading in a loud voice.

(12) That he not make it audible to his ears — see Beit Yosef, who brought support for this from Sefer HaZohar.

(13) And even if he makes it audible, etc. — meaning that even though it is better not to make it audible to his ears, nevertheless if he makes it audible there is no concern, for it is no better than prayer, which was also enacted to be recited in a whisper, and nevertheless one makes it audible to his ears, as above siman 101 seif 2; here too is the same.
