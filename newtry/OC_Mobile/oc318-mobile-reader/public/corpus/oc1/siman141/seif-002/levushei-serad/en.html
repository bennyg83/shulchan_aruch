And if so, even to lean — his intent is to dispute the Levush, who wrote that slight leaning is entirely permitted, and leaning such that if it were removed he would fall is forbidden; and for a baal basar even this is permitted; and Magen Avraham holds that since the reason is awe, even slight leaning is forbidden; and consequently only for a baal basar did they permit slight leaning; but leaning such that if removed he would fall is forbidden even for a baal basar, like leaning while lying down — they did not permit for a baal basar except slight leaning on his side.
