One must read standing — Bach wrote in siman 429 that even b'dieved one did not fulfill and must return and read again, and so is the language of the Tur, and Hagahot Maimoniyot; but from what Rav Yaakov Beirav wrote in the name of Rashi it implies that specifically l'chatchila we are strict but b'dieved one fulfilled; see there; Magen Avraham s.k. 1; and so Peri Chadash section 1, that b'dieved reading while seated is valid; see there; and so Mateh Yehuda section 1; and so Avnei Nezer there; and he wrote that Bach spoke only according to those who say that in Megillah l'chatchila it is forbidden while seated, etc.; see there; and so Maamar Mordechai section 1; Avnei Nezer on Laws of Reading the Shema section 4, to the opposing view siman 7 section 4, Chayei Adam general law 41 section 6, Or HaChayyim section 1, Shaarei Ephraim Shulchan section 11.
