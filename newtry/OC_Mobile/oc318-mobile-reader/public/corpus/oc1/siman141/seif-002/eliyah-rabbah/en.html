(2) And even to lean — for standing by means of leaning is not standing, for thus we say in chapter 2 of Zevachim — so the Tur wrote; there on daf 19 it deals that if the leaning is removed he would fall, as Rashi explained there; and if so what the Levush concluded, nevertheless if baal basar, etc., implies they are also permitted in this, unlike Magen Avraham; however l'chatchila when there is no baal basar, even to lean slightly is forbidden, not to treat it lightly, as Moses wrote below siman 276; and with this it is resolved what all the later authorities wondered from Choshen Mishpat siman 17 that leaning is standing — here they were stringent due to awe and fear of the Torah.
