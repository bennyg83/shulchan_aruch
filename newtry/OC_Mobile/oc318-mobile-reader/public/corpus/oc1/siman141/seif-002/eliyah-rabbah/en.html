Two should not read, etc. This is because two voices are not heard distinctly, even if each person can hear his own voice. The Perishah's question that the Shulchan Aruch contradicts itself was already addressed in siman 101, seif katan 4: he did not see Bedek HaBayit, where the Shulchan Aruch retracted his earlier ruling regarding prayer as well. As a matter of practical law, it was explained there that one may make his own voice audible to his ears. The responsum of Rabbi Eliyahu HaLevi likewise implies this. See above siman 142.
Through an intermediary, etc. The Levush writes that the gabbai, as it were, stands in the place of the Holy One, blessed be He, since he has the mitzvah to call whomever he sees fit; the chazzan is the intermediary corresponding to Moshe; and the oleh receives the Torah on behalf of all Israel and as their agent. See Responsa Nachalat Shivah, siman 38, regarding a case where a gabbai left the Sefer Torah and walked away. He deserves punishment: if a person sinned, why should the Sefer Torah be humiliated, etc.
