2) There: One must read while standing. The Bach in siman 690 wrote that even after the fact he did not fulfill and must return and read; and so is implied [by] the language of the Tur and the Hagahos Maimoniyos. But from what the Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Rashi it is implied that we are particular only l'chatchilah, but after the fact he fulfilled; see there. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. And so wrote the Pri Chadash, ot 1, that after the fact the reading while sitting was valid; see there. And so wrote the Mateh Yehudah, ot 1; and so wrote the Eliyah Rabbah there, and wrote that even the Bach said [his ruling] only according to the one who holds that regarding the Megillah l'chatchilah it is forbidden [to read] while sitting, etc.; see there. And so wrote the Maamar Mordechai, ot 1; Emes LeYaakov in the law of Krias Sefer Torah for the shaliach tzibbur, ot 4; Ledovid Emes, siman 7, ot 4; Chayei Adam, klal 31, ot 6; Erech HaShulchan, ot 1; Sha'ar Ephraim, sha'ar 3, ot 11.
