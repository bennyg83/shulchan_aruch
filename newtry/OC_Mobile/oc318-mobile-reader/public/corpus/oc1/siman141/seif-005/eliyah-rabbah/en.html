(5) Through an intermediary, etc. — Levush wrote: the baal segan stands [as if] in the place of the Name blessed be He, for there is a mitzvah to call up whoever is pleasing in his eyes, and the prayer leader is an intermediary in place of Moses, and the one called up is the recipient in place of all Israel through his agency — until here. And see in the responsum of Nachalat Shiva siman 38: if the baal segan went and abandoned the Torah scroll, he is worthy of punishment; if a person sinned, why is the Torah scroll ashamed, etc.
