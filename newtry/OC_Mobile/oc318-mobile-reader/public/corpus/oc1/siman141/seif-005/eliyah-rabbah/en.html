Until it is completed, etc. Sometimes chazzanim hurry and begin reading almost an entire verse before the congregation can hear the reading, because the congregation is still answering Amen after the blessing. Maharam Mintz, siman 81, writes that nevertheless the custom is for the chazzan himself to answer Amen aloud so that the congregation understands and hears when he begins reading. At first glance this is surprising, because it is contrary to Sotah 39 and all the poskim. According to what I have written, however, his meaning is that after the listeners finish answering, the chazzan himself then answers Amen. He further writes that when someone recites Birkat HaGomel, the chazzan and the entire congregation answer after him: 'Amen. May He who has bestowed every good upon you continue to bestow every good upon you,' etc.
