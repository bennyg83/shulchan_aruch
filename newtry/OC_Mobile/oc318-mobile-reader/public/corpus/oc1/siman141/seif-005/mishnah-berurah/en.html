(17) Amen from the mouth of the congregation — and even if there are people who prolong Amen, one must wait for them, for all need to hear the reading; therefore the reader must wait even for the minority until they finish Amen, and afterward he begins to read. The later authorities wrote that the custom is that the reader answers Amen with the congregation in a loud voice and continues in it slightly more than the congregation so they understand that he is beginning to read and incline the ear to hear, and he begins to read immediately; and it is good to pause slightly between Amen and the beginning of the reading so it not appear that on account of the tune of Amen he is attached to what he reads.
