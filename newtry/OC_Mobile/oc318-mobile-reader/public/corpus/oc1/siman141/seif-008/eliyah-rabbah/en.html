(8) Because of the evil eye, etc. — in Beit Yosef he wrote one does not refrain on account of the evil eye, and so it is in Kolbo; and one must say there is a scribal error and the word "but" (ela) is missing; and so I saw in Tashbetz siman 190, and requires study. And it appears to me that Maharil implies even if one says he is not particular about the evil eye, one should not do so. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah wrote: there is no distinction between a brother from the father or the mother, and likewise a father with his son. And if they already read for him, he should not descend, since the reason is only on account of the evil eye.
