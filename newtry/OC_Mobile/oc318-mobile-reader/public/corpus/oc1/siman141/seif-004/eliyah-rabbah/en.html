(4) Two should not read, etc. — because two voices are not heard, even if one makes it audible to his own ears, etc.; and so Perishah, who questioned the words of Shulchan Aruch contradicting each other — I already wrote in siman 101 s.k. 4 that he did not see Badek HaBayit, where Shulchan Aruch returned also regarding the law of prayer. And for the law it was explained there that one may make it audible to his ears, and so is implied in the name of the responsum of R' Eliyahu HaLevi; and see above siman 142.
