And even to lean, etc. — and if he leaned so much that if that thing were removed he would fall, it is not standing; and if so, even a baal basar is forbidden, and it is permitted to a baal basar only slight leaning; Magen Avraham there; Mateh Yehuda there; Emunat Moshe there; Ledavid there; and if he cannot lean except in this matter and he needs support, it is also permitted in this, Shaarei Ephraim there. Mishna Berurah note 4 wrote there in Shaarei Ephraim note 12: if the Torah scroll is long and he cannot roll to see the top of the page except by bowing oneself near the Torah scroll, and thereby comes to lean on the table — there is room to permit; see there; and Mishna Berurah brought it in note ה.
