Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Testimony — the poskim proved that if they testified while seated it is valid b'dieved; for we taught regarding a Torah scholar they permitted him to testify while seated for his honor; and on this basis he wrote one should distinguish: because standing of witnesses is d'oraisa, as it is written "and two men shall stand" — therefore if it were invalid b'dieved they would not have permitted on account of the honor of a Torah scholar; but reading while standing is only d'rabbanan — therefore one can say that although it is forbidden, b'dieved they permitted for a king; or one should distinguish between the honor of a king and the honor of a Torah scholar.
