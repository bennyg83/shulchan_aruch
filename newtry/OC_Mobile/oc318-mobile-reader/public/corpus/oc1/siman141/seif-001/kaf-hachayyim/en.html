1) [Seif 1] One must read while standing. And it is implied [that this is] specifically if he reads in public, but for an individual it is permitted to read while sitting; and so wrote the Beis Yosef in the name of Rashi, of blessed memory. And so wrote the Olas Tamid, ot 1; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1. And it is implied [that] specifically the chazan and the oleh must stand, but the congregation need not stand, as stated in the Beis Yosef; and so it is ruled in the Shulchan Aruch below in siman 146, seif 4; see there, and see our words there, with the help of Heaven.
2) There: One must read while standing. The Bach in siman 690 wrote that even after the fact he did not fulfill and must return and read; and so is implied [by] the language of the Tur and the Hagahos Maimoniyos. But from what the Beis Yosef wrote in the name of Rashi it is implied that we are particular only l'chatchilah, but after the fact he fulfilled; see there. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. And so wrote the Pri Chadash, ot 1, that after the fact the reading while sitting was valid; see there. And so wrote the Mateh Yehudah, ot 1; and so wrote the Eliyah Rabbah there, and wrote that even the Bach said [his ruling] only according to the one who holds that regarding the Megillah l'chatchilah it is forbidden [to read] while sitting, etc.; see there. And so wrote the Maamar Mordechai, ot 1; Emes LeYaakov in the law of Krias Sefer Torah for the shaliach tzibbur, ot 4; Ledovid Emes, siman 7, ot 4; Chayei Adam, klal 31, ot 6; Erech HaShulchan, ot 1; Sha'ar Ephraim, sha'ar 3, ot 11.
3) There: And even to lean, etc. For just as it was given with awe, so must we conduct ourselves with it with awe. Beis Yosef in the name of the Yerushalmi; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2. And the Magen Avraham wrote there that even to lean a little is forbidden; and so wrote the Mateh Yehudah there; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 2; Emes LeYaakov there; Ledovid Emes there. And see our words above in siman 94, ot 30.
4) There: And even to lean, etc. And if he leaned so much that, were that thing removed, he would fall, it is not [considered] standing; and if so, even a heavy person is forbidden, and a heavy person is permitted only a slight leaning. Magen Avraham there; Mateh Yehudah there; Emes LeYaakov there; Ledovid Emes there. And if it is impossible for him [to stand] except by leaning in this manner, and he needs support to hold him up, it is permitted even in this. Sha'ar Ephraim there; Mishnah Berurah, ot 4. And the Sha'ar Ephraim wrote there, ot 12, that if the Sefer Torah is tall and it is impossible to see at the top of the column except by bending oneself close to the Sefer Torah, and through this one comes to lean on the table, one may permit it; see there, and the Mishnah Berurah cited him, ot 5.
5) There: Or to stand is forbidden. And the same applies to his fellow, as above in siman 94, seif 8. Pri Chadash, ot 1.
6) There: Unless he is a heavy person. And the same applies to a sick person or one very old, for whom standing without leaning is difficult. Sha'ar Ephraim there, ot 11; Mishnah Berurah there. And one who is old and needs to lean -- it is good that he remove the mantle a little and lean on the wooden rollers or on the [reading-]stone, and not on the mantle, for it is an accessory of holiness. Maharam Mintz, siman 81; Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur; Olas Tamid, ot 3; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 3; Ledovid Emes, siman 5, ot 48; Sha'ar Ephraim there; Mishnah Berurah there.
7) There: Unless he is a heavy person. And it appears that the same applies if he cannot see the writing of the Sefer Torah in order to read with the shaliach tzibbur, so that his blessing not be in vain, as [the author] wrote nearby in seif 2 -- unless he leans himself and supports himself on the teivah on which the Sefer Torah rests, which is permitted; for although we might say that the verse 'and you, stand here with Me' [teaches] a complete derivation, as the Olas Tamid wrote, ot 2, nonetheless the [prohibition] derived from 'you shall not take the Name of Hashem your God in vain,' from which we derive not to make a blessing in vain, is more stringent, as stated in the Yosef L'Chok, parashas Shemini, from the Shaarei Kedushah, part 2, sha'ar 8; see there. Yafeh LaLev, ot 2.
8) There, gloss: And likewise the chazan, etc. So it appears, since we derive at the beginning of the chapter 'HaKorei omed' from the verse 'and you, stand here with Me,' [that] as it were, even the Holy One, blessed be He, is [described as] standing. Pri Chadash there.
