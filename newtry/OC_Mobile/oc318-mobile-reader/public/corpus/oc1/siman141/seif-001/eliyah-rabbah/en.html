(1) One must read, etc., standing — in Bach siman 429, that even b'dieved one has not fulfilled and must return and read again; and so is implied in the Tur; but from Rashi it appears that b'dieved one has fulfilled (Magen Avraham). And in my humble opinion, Bach too did not write thus except according to those who say that in Megillah l'chatchila it is forbidden while seated; but according to those who say that in Megillah even l'chatchila it is permitted, as Bach ruled there — if so, the Gemara says what is not so regarding Torah, that it is forbidden l'chatchila but b'dieved one has fulfilled; for the verse "stand here with me" is an asmakhta; and it is clear from Rashi's explanation there that all this is specifically in congregation; and so Rav Yerucham wrote; and see Yerushalmi, and requires study.
