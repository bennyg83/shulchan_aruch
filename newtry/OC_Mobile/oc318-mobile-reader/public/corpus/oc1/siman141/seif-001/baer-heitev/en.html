Standing. After the fact, if he read while seated, he has fulfilled his obligation. Peri Chadash. See Magen Avraham and Yad Aharon.
To lean. For one must stand with awe. Beit Yosef. Accordingly, even leaning slightly is forbidden unless he is corpulent. If he leans so heavily that he would fall were the support removed, this is not considered standing, and is forbidden even for a corpulent person. A corpulent person is permitted only to lean slightly. See Magen Avraham. Maharam Mintz, siman 81, writes that a corpulent person who needs to lean should not lean on the covering, since it is used for a mitzvah. Rather, he should remove the covering and lean on the wood.
