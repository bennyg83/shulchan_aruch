(6) Until the blessing is finished, etc. — for sometimes the chazzanim hurry and begin to read almost a verse before the congregation hears his reading on account of answering Amen at the end after the congregation answered Amen; Maharam Mintz siman 81, and these are his words: nevertheless the custom is that the chazzan answers Amen in a loud voice so the congregation understands and hears when he begins to read — until here. And at first glance it is astonishing that this is against the gemara in Sotah daf 39 and all the poskim; but according to what I wrote it is plausible to him that this is the explanation: after the listeners finish their answering, he answers. He also wrote: one who needs to confess — the chazzan and the entire congregation answer after him Amen Mi sheGamlakh kol tov, etc.
