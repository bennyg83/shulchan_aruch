Seif 6: They may, etc. This is unlike Orchos Chayim, who writes that they may not read because it is stated, “The testimony of Hashem is faithful,” while they are disqualified from testimony. Yet women and minors are disqualified from testimony and may nevertheless receive an aliyah, as stated in chapter 3 of Megillah. The same applies to a slave, as stated in the Yerushalmi at the end of chapter 2 of Kesubos, from the Baraisa cited in the Gemara there: “He read three verses,” etc.
