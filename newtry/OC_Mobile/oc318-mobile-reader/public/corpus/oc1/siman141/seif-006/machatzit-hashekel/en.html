(s.k. 6) Amen — they commonly err, thinking it refers to what is written at the end of the seif, until Amen is finished from the mouth of the congregation.

As written siman 128 s.y. 11, who wrote that the kohanim may not begin the priestly blessing until Amen is finished from the mouth of the entire congregation; and Tosafot inferred from saying "from the mouth of the entire congregation" that even for those who prolong, one must wait; for regarding the priestly blessing, one who prolongs does not act properly, nevertheless the kohanim must wait for him, for the entire congregation must hear the priestly blessing; and if so, the same applies here, although it does not explicitly teach "the entire congregation," and there is no place to infer one must wait for one who prolongs — nevertheless by reasoning we say one must wait; and he wrote it is not comparable to what is written siman 167 seif 16, that the one who cuts may not cut until Amen is finished from the mouths of the responders, and nevertheless one need not wait for those who prolong, as with the kohanim — for the reason that exists regarding kohanim exists here too, for the entire congregation must hear the Torah reading.
