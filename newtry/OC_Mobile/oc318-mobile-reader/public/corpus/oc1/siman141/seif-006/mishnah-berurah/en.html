(18) They may call up two [siblings], etc. — meaning that by law there is no prohibition at all; and therefore if they already called him up and he has already ascended, he should not descend; but l'chatchila they do not allow calling up on account of the evil eye.

(19) On account of the evil eye — and there is no difference between brothers from the father or from the mother; and even if they say they are not particular about the evil eye, there are those who are stringent even for a father with his son on account of evil eye; and in a case of need one may permit with a grandson.

(20) And even if one, etc. — meaning that even though they interrupt with Kaddish between them, nevertheless it is called one after the other since they do not interrupt with something else between them. And specifically on Shabbat when they do not take out another Torah scroll for the maftir; but if they take out another Torah scroll, it is permitted; and likewise if the maftir is not yet a bar mitzvah lad, it is still permitted.

(21) With his name — unlike a place where the practice is not to call the maftir by name but only to stand a generic maftir; since they do not specify his name, it is permitted even for two brothers one after the other, or father and son; and specifically in a place where they practice so; but to do so in a place where they practice calling by name — that itself creates greater recognition; and likewise if the custom of not calling by name is only on the seventh [aliyah], it is permitted to call them on the sixth and seventh.
