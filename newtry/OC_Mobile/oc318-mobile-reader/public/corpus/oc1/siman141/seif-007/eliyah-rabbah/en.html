(7) The maftir, etc. — specifically when the maftir is a bar mitzvah lad, and on Shabbat when they do not read the haftarah maftir from another Torah scroll — that is forbidden, shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah. And what Rama and Levush wrote, one should not call them by name, etc., implies that when reading maftir generically it is permitted; and it appears to me that specifically in places where sometimes they call up a generic maftir is what is dealt with, but when the practice is not so — then it is an astonishing matter that people ask about it, and there is more evil eye, and it is somewhat like the case of the closeted foundling in Yoreh De'ah siman 14, and requires study. Emunat Shmuel siman 47 wrote: per the Ashkenaz custom where they call generically for the seventh and do not specify by name — it is permitted to call the sixth and seventh to two brothers.
