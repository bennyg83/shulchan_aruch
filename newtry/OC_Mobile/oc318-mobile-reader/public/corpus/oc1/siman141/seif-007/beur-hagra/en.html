Seif 7: The one who receives the aliyah, etc. As stated, “One who enters the courtyard enters by the shortest route and exits by the longer route.” This does not appear correct, however, because nowhere in the Tosefta or Gemara does it state that one who enters the courtyard enters, etc. Moreover, it appears more appropriate to derive this from the altar, around which they would first circle and then leave by the shortest route.
And if, etc. As stated in many places, “All turns,” etc. At the end of Megillah it is stated: “One who enters a synagogue to pray,” etc. The Rosh’s text reads, “It is a mitzvah,” etc., as stated in the verse there, “He shall not return,” etc. See also Zevachim 63a, “All who ascend,” etc., and chapter 2 of Middos, “All who enter,” etc.
And he should not descend, etc. As stated in chapter 5 of Pesachim, “He receives the full one,” etc. Maharil in the name of Mordechai. See above, end of siman 149.
