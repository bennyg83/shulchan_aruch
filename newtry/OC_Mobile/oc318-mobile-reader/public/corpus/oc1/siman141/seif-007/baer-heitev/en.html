The one fit to read after him. The custom is for the first person to wait until the second begins the blessing. Those meticulous in observance are concerned that while walking he will be unable to hear the reading properly. They therefore wait until the second person finishes the blessing and descend between one person and the next. Magen Avraham. Responsa Maaseh Aharon, siman 43, writes that when two people contend with one another over who will precede the other, they should be admonished in terms suited to what they are prepared to accept, for the sanctity of the place cannot tolerate pride and contempt in any manner; see there. Eliyah Rabbah writes that the practice of some people on Sukkos is improper: they stand aside with their lulavim and do not participate in the circuits because of the honor accorded to the person at the head; see there.
