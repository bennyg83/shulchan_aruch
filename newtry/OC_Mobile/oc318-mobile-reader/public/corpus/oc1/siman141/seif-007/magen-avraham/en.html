By the shorter path. At first glance, the same applies to the chazzan when he brings the Torah scroll to the reading desk — the same law applies that he should go by the shorter path; and see Beit Yosef s.k. 88.

Until he has gone up. The custom is to wait until the second one begins the blessing; see end of siman 147. And the meticulous are concerned that on the way he will not be able to hear the reading properly; therefore they wait until the second finishes and descend between one reader and the next.
