The one who ascends to the raised platform [i.e. the bimah] should go up from the opening that is the shortest way from one's place [i.e. the place where one is praying] and descend from the platform on a different path which is the longest path back to one's place. And if two paths are equal [in distance from his place], one ascends by the opening that is on the right-hand path and descends by the opening on the opposite side.[(And one should not descend until the person who is designated to read after one has already come up. (Mordekhai Hagadol))]:
