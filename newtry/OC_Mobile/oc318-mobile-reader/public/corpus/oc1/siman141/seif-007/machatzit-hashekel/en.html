(s.k. 7) By the path, etc. — and in Avodah Berurah siman 128 — meaning Beit Yosef wrote: when the chazzan brings the Torah scroll to the bimah, then the chazzan's face is to the west; therefore he must lead it by way of the north, for then his turning will be to the right; and when he returns it to the heikhal, then his face is toward the heikhal, which is in the east — he leads the Torah scroll by way of the south, for then this is facing right; and this is ostensibly unlike Magen Avraham; but one can say that for Rav Yosef Karo it deals when the two paths are equal in length.
