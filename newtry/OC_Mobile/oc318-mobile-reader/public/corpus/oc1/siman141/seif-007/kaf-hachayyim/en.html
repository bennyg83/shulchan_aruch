And it appears that the same applies if he cannot see the writing of the Sefer Torah in order to read with the shaliach tzibbur, so that his blessing not be in vain, as [the author] wrote nearby in seif 2 -- unless he leans himself and supports himself on the teivah on which the Sefer Torah rests, which is permitted; for although we might say that the verse 'and you, stand here with Me' [teaches] a complete derivation, as the Olas Tamid wrote, ot 2, nonetheless the [prohibition] derived from 'you shall not take the Name of Hashem your God in vain,' from which we derive not to make a blessing in vain, is more stringent, as stated in the Yosef L'Chok, parashas Shemini, from the Shaarei Kedushah, part 2, sha'ar 8; see there.
