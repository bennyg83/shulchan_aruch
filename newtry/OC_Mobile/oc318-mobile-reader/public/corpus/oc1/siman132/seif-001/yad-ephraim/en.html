In Magen Avraham, note 2: parallel to a minor who goes within seven [days] to the synagogue and has all the Kaddishim — nevertheless he must give the yahrzeit one Kaddish. And that on Shabbat the yahrzeit has nothing — meaning: a great mourner who goes on Shabbat to the synagogue completely pushes off the yahrzeit, because the yahrzeit can say on Motza'ei Shabbat, when then the mourner will not be within seven in the synagogue; and although the time of the yahrzeit has already passed, nevertheless completion applies on the night following it — unlike one whose mourning was annulled on a festival, who also goes the night after to the synagogue; if so, he is like a minor.
