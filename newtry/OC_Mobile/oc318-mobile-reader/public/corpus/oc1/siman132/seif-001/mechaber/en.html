The Laws of K'dushat Uva le-Tzion. Containing 2 seifim: We translate [i.e.,
recite the Aramaic Targum of] the kedusha of Uva le-Tzion, and one must be very careful to say it with intention.And regarding whether an individual says it — its law is like the kedusha in Yotzer; see above siman 59. And regarding the kedusha in the translation, an individual says it and not two; and one should not say it in a loud voice. (Beit Yosef siman 59)]:
