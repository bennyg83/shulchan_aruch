(1) [Seif 1] They translate kedushah and Uva Letziyon, and when they announce something one must announce before the chazzan begins Ashrei and not between Ashrei and Lamnatzeiach, and all the more so at the time when they say Ashrei so that their intention not be nullified — six pillars and Semag in Choshen Mishpat siman 267, and so too in Beit Yosef siman 559, Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman end of seif 2 letter 1, Chasam Sofer letter 8, Kaf HaChayyim siman 109 letter 2; and Orach Chayyim Acharon wrote that what they go around for a donation and the like is not proper, and all the more so at the time of repetition of the prayer, for they confuse the intention — end of his words. However, Tosafos Chayyim letter 1 wrote to resolve the custom that it is not similar to an announcement that announces news, and thereby intention is nullified; not so with something practiced always, whose intention is not nullified thereby.
