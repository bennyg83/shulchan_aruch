(א) And Uva Letziyon. When they announce something, one must announce before the chazzan begins Ashrei and not between Ashrei and Lamnatzeiach, and all the more so at the time when they say Ashrei, so that their intention not be nullified — Semag in Choshen Mishpat siman 267 [printed 262]. If an individual comes to the synagogue at the time when they say the order of kedushah, he should say it with them even before his prayer, for there are those who say that an individual does not say it; and likewise he may say it before he says the second verses, namely Uva Letziyon etc. and "And I, this is My covenant" etc., and afterward he says the second verses. And all the more so that he may say Ashrei and Lamnatzeiach afterward in order to say it with the congregation. Magen Avraham.

(ב) Two. Since anything that is in the language of the Targum may not be said in the plural, and the sign for this is "two read and one translate." Zohar.
