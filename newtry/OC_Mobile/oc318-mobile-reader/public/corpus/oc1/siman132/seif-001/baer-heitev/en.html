U'Va LeTzion. When an announcement is to be made, it should be announced before the chazzan begins Ashrei, not between Ashrei and Lamnatzeiach, and certainly not while Ashrei is being recited, lest the congregation's concentration be disturbed. Sema, Choshen Mishpat, siman (262) [267]. If an individual enters the synagogue while the congregation is reciting Seder Kedushah, he should recite it with them even before his own prayer, since some authorities hold that an individual may not recite it alone. Likewise, he may recite it before saying the two verses, namely, "U'Va LeTzion," etc., and "And as for Me, this is My covenant," etc., and afterward recite those two verses. Certainly, he may recite Ashrei and Lamnatzeiach afterward in order to recite Seder Kedushah with the congregation. Magen Avraham.
Two. Anything written in the language of Targum should not be recited communally. A mnemonic is: "Two readings of Scripture and one of Targum." Zohar.
