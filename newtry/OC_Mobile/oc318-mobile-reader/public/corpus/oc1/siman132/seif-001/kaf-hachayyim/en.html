1) [Seif 1] They translate the Kedushah of 'Uva LeTzion.' And when they announce something, one should announce it before the chazan begins 'Ashrei,' and not between 'Ashrei' and 'Lamenatze'ach'; and all the more so at the time that they say 'Ashrei' [one should not announce], for their concentration would be nullified. Amudei Shesh; and the Sma in Choshen Mishpat, siman 267; and so is implied in the Beis Yosef, siman 550; Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman; Soles Belulah, ot 1; Chesed LaAlafim, ot 18; Kaf HaChaim, siman 17, ot 2. And the Eshel Avraham wrote that it is not proper that they go around collecting for charity and the like [during this], and all the more so during the repetition of the tefillah, for they confuse the concentration. End of his language. However, the Tosafos Chaim, ot 1, wrote to justify the custom, that it is not comparable to an announcement that announces news, through which the concentration is nullified, unlike a matter that is done regularly, through which the concentration is not nullified.
2) There: They translate the Kedushah of 'Uva LeTzion,' and one says 'Uva LeTzion' and 'Va'ani zos berisi' and afterward 'V'Atah Kadosh.' Tur, end of siman 131, in the name of the Rambam and Rav Amram, of blessed memory. And see above siman 131, seif 1, in the gloss, which wrote that one should say 'Ashrei' and 'Lamenatze'ach' before 'Uva LeTzion.' And so it is written in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 49b, and see there for a reason rooted in the hidden teachings. And he wrote further there in Shaar HaKavanos that one should say 'Ashrei' three times before 'Tehillah l'Dovid,' and they are: 'Ashrei yoshvei veisecha,' etc., 'Ashrei ha'am shekachah lo,' 'Ashrei ha'am,' etc.; and see there for a reason rooted in the hidden teachings.
