In Magen Avraham note 2, end of his words. And I saw in Noda B'Yehuda, and it is explained in Levush and Minhagim, that prayer kaddishim specifically belong to yahrzeit, namely two on weekdays, Maariv and Shacharit, and three on Shabbat, Maariv, Musaf, and Minchah; and if there is at Shacharit Shir HaShirim or Ruth, that belongs to him as well. And what Magen Avraham wrote of Perakim, Knesset Yechezkel added also of Barchi Nafshi; nevertheless this does not include the song of the day nor any chapter of Tehillim kaddish, for otherwise you would find three on weekdays and four on Shabbat, for the chapter of Tehillim of the day is by law to say every day and presumably they say kaddish after it, as Magen Avraham wrote in this siman note 4. And what is the reason he wrote two on weekdays? Rather perforce this is not prayer kaddish. And in responsa of Knesset Yechezkel he wrote that the song of glory kaddish belongs to yahrzeit, and one must say that for this reason he did not account for three on weekdays and four on Shabbat, because they did not practice saying the song of glory in all places; nevertheless I do not know from where he has this, and so I found written in the margin that he wondered at him on this; nevertheless one does not deviate from the custom.
