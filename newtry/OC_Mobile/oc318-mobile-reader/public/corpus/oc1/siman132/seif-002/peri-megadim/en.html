If so, Minchah corresponds to the limbs and is obligatory, and certainly l'chatchila it is a mitzvah; and proof from Tur, for in Rambam, Hilchot Temidim u-Musafim, halacha 6, Maariv corresponds to limbs and fats that are consumed, etc.; but Tur explicitly says so , from which it follows that Minchah corresponds to limbs, etc.; and likewise Shacharit for us, who say the parashah of the tamid; and on the other hand, in Rambam, Hilchot Temidim, one can say the halacha is like Rabbenu Chananel, and nevertheless there it is difficult somewhat; see there. And the evening [tamid] has no fixed time and not until midnight, for it was enacted corresponding to limbs that were not consumed; but Minchah was enacted corresponding to limbs that one is commanded to raise at their time while it is still day, for Shabbat is overridden, as written in chapter 4 of Hilchot Ma'aseh ha-Korbanot, halacha 3; see there.
