(2) The individual says it etc. And a hint to what Beit Yosef wrote in siman 59 in the name of the Zohar that language of translation may not be said in the plural, and he gave the sign "two read and one translate." Perishah wished to say in the holy language, which is Scripture, one should say specifically two and not specifically two above, but rather he wished to say in the plural and at least ten; but in translation one says individually. However, study is required, for we do not hold like the Zohar, for we say even the holy language individually; and if so, since this is not so, this too is not so — until here his language. And perhaps for this reason Levush wrote another reason, and it is explained in his words that in the kedushah of Yotzer two do not suffice but we require ten for the Zohar; and so it appears in Beit Yosef siman 59 that he agreed with Rashba that we require ten per the Zohar — see there; and to exclude olat tamid, which he wrote that for the Zohar they say two and more. And see siman 59 that Levush wrote: in the Zohar I saw that translation is in a whisper; and it is not so, but in Beit Yosef, since an individual says it he says it in a whisper; and in Abudraham I found what they practiced to say this order in a whisper because of the verse "Heaven shall reign forever," which Moshe said and it was fitting to say first before "Kadosh" and his name last, in the manner of the order of kedushot where malchut is at the end; and know that it is so, for the first verses the shaliach tzibbur says in a loud voice with translations, and the verse "Heaven shall reign" and its translation he says in a whisper.
