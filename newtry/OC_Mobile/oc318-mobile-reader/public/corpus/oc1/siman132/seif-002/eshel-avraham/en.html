Orphan. Regarding that minors who have not reached education say kaddish and the congregation answers after them; and likewise what they say after learning mishnah, beraita of Rosh Chodesh — Heaven wished to give merit to the Land of Israel etc. — see Tosafos in Yoreh De'ah siman 52 and siman 31. And customs of kaddish see in Ba'er Heitev note 5. A group that studies every day with ten — their custom is that if one of them has a yahrzeit or mourning, heaven forbid, he says all the kaddishim; and since there is no mourner among them they hired all the kaddishim to another who is not from the members of the group, and they did not stipulate that if, heaven forbid, mourning befell one of them the hire would be cancelled; and afterward mourning befell one of them — Eliyah Rabbah raised in responsa of Shev Yaakov question 7 that they should divide the kaddishim equally.
