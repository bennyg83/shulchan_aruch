After the conclusion of the prayer they say Aleinu, and there are those who wrote that one should say Aleinu after every prayer, even after Minchah (Lechem HaShamayim). And one should say "and the seat of His glory is in the heavens" and not say "and the throne of His glory" (Tur in siman 133 and responsa of Maharshal siman 68). And they say the song that the Levites would say in the Temple only at Shacharit, and so too Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chayyim, and Rashi explained that in the morning the song would hold up the offering, and in the evening it would not hold it up; but one should not explain that they would not say the song in the Temple except in the morning — if so, there is a difficulty, for is it not a complete mishnah on Rosh Hashana that the Levites became confused in the song, and that means the song of Maariv? So it appears to resolve it. And see Sefer Be'er Sheva, who resolves this difficulty in a different matter far from the view and it is strained. And see in responsa of R' Moshe Alshich.
