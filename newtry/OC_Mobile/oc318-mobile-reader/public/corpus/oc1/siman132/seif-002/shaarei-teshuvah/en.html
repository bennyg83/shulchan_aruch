Two [people], per Beit Yosef. And see in Sefer ha-Kavanot that the Ari was not concerned about this and would say the word of translation in a loud voice; but according to what is implied in Beit Yosef siman 59, the main reason not to read aloud is so the congregation should not say it together — and the language of translation is specifically for an individual, as written in the Zohar; but to read aloud by himself, not together with other people of the congregation, he may say even in a loud voice. And likewise in Shulchan Aruch he wrote that the Zohar speaks regarding not participating to read two together; see there. He also wrote that from Abudraham it appears that the verse "Heaven yimlokh" should also be said quietly; see there.
