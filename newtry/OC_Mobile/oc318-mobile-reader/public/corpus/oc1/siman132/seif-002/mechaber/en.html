It is forbidden for a person to leave the synagogue before kedusha de-sidra.And after the conclusion of the prayer they say Aleinu le-Shabe'ach standing (Kol Bo), and one should be careful to say it with intention; and when he reaches "Lo yoshia," he should pause a little before saying "Va-anachnu kor'im," etc.(Tur). And they say Kaddish Yatom after Aleinu; and even if there is no orphan in the synagogue, one who has no father and mother should say it; and even one who has a father and mother may say it if his father and mother are not particular [Agur, Tashbetz, and responsum of Maharil siman 64]. And one should say Pitum ha-Ketoret] in the evening and morning after the prayer; and they say first "Ein kelokeinu," etc. And they say the song that the Levites'. would say in the Temple only at Shacharit (Tur). And some wrote that one should be careful to say Pitum ha-Ketoret from a text and not by heart, because the recitation is in place of the burning, and we are concerned lest he skip [Beit Yosef in the name of Mahari and Orchot Chaim] one of the spices; and we say that one is liable to death if one of the spices is missing. Therefore the custom is not to say it on weekdays when people hurry to their work, lest one skip. And when one leaves the synagogue he says "Heaven, nechani," etc. [Kol] Bo], and he bows and leaves.
