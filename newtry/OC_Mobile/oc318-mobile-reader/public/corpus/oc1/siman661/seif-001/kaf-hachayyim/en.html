1) [Seif 1] On the second night of Yom Tov, he recites Kiddush and Shehecheyanu immediately afterward, and then the blessing of sukkah. For Shehecheyanu applies only to Kiddush and not to the sukkah, since even if the first day was a weekday he already fulfilled the Shehecheyanu for the sukkah. Therefore, one should not interrupt between Kiddush and Shehecheyanu with the blessing of sukkah. Tur. Levush. Taz, s.k. 1. See above, siman 643, seif 1.
