And afterward the berachah of sukkah. For Shehecheyanu refers only to Kiddush and not to the sukkah, since even if the first day was weekday, he fulfilled the Shehecheyanu of sukkah. Therefore one must not interrupt with sukkah between Kiddush and Shehecheyanu. The Tur and the Mordechai wrote in the name of the Raaviah that even on the second night one should say Shehecheyanu last, like Yaknehaz, even though Shehecheyanu does not refer back to Havdalah. The Rosh agreed with the first reasoning. In Terumas HaDeshen he wrote that we practice like the Raaviah. In the responsum of Rashal, siman 64, he wrote that he had a tradition to practice like the Raaviah, for the two days are equal. So too my father-in-law, of blessed memory, concluded in the name of the Acharonim.
