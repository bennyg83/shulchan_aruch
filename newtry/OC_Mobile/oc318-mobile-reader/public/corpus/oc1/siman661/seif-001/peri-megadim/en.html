And afterward. The berachah of sukkah. See the Taz. In Terumas HaDeshen, siman 95, he wrote that the custom is like the Raaviah; Yaknehaz proves this, for Shehecheyanu comes afterward. The Bach also concludes so, that the obligation of the day, meaning sukkah, is proper to precede even on the second night; see there. See siman 639 in the Taz, letter 17, and siman 641.
