After it. One who did not eat in the sukkah on the first night must, on the second night, recite the berachah of sukkah and afterward Shehecheyanu; Magen Avraham. See siman 641, seif katan 2, and what is written there. The Bach wrote in the name of most Acharonim that one should always recite sukkah and afterward Shehecheyanu, and so the Hagahos Minhagim wrote in the name of many poskim, and so too in Matzeis Asirah. See Magen Avraham. In Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah he wrote that one who says the berachah of sukkah on a day when there is Kiddush together with the berachah of HaMotzi is mistaken; rather, he must say it after Kiddush. See there, and see Birkei Yosef.
