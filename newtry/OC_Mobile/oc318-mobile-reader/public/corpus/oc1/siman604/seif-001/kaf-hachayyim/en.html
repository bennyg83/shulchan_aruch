1) [Seif 1] It is a mitzvah to eat on erev Yom Kippur, etc. For R' Chiya bar Rav of Difti taught: It is written, "You shall afflict your souls on the ninth day of the month in the evening." Does one fast on the ninth? Surely one fasts on the tenth. Rather, this teaches you that anyone who eats and drinks on the ninth is regarded by Scripture as though he fasted on the ninth and the tenth. This is stated in Berachos 8b and in several places. The Tur and Beit Yosef cited this. One may ask: Why was it necessary to say that it is as though he fasted on the tenth? It should have said only that it is as though he fasted on the ninth, for the tenth stands independently, whether he eats or does not eat. Perhaps one can say that this teaches us that since the eating and drinking are regarded as affliction, he is given reward as though he fasted for two consecutive days. Alternatively, one can say that for the eating alone he receives reward as though he fasted for two days, aside from the reward for fasting on the tenth day. Pri Chadash.
