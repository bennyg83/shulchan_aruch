Translation) In Maali Yoma Dakhpuri Give me, Chai' Bar Rav from Dipat Katib and you answered your souls on Tisha to the new 20. All the food on the Tisha is lifted up on it the scripture is like the Tishita and Tenth of Ekal Hagam And without a doubt, Ei would also have completed years of reading and translation of the whole year. And without a doubt, my opinion,' said Bibi Talha, who would like to complete on the eve of Yohak and not every parsha from every Shabbat on his Shabbat, as we do. Therefore, the Omnipresent has allowed me to cancel the first day of the week, that is to say, not every week. And give me, my life, all the food on the ninth day of the week, because it is the person who will have to cancel every week from his study in order to increase the feast of the week, and the the Master commanded him not to complete the portion in the week. 27 which must be canceled from his teaching in the same way from the 9th. who never said the time has come to stand up from BH'M except for the A.R.P.
