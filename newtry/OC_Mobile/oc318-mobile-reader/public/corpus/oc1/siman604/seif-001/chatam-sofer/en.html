In my opinion: for one who holds it is d'oraisa from Scriptural decree or that he will have strength to fast on Yom Kippur as Rashi explained Yoma daf 81b — if so even a Torah scholar, although fasting is joy and pleasure for him to save him from harm, nevertheless on Erev Yom Kippur it is permitted on Shavuot and Purim in any case; Erev Yom Kippur is not a day of joy and Yom Tov but a Scriptural decree to eat on it — therefore forbidden to fast in any case. However for one who holds it is only Jewish custom with verse support, and the reason of the custom is to rejoice against the arrival of the holy day and like the incident of the tailor and as Rabbeinu Yehuda wrote — then permitted for a Torah scholar to fast since it is joy and pleasure for him. And it appears to me this also depends on the customs of the countries that Magen Avraham wrote below at end of his words — requires study.
