[Levush] The verse credits him, etc. In the Gemara: Scripture credits him as though he fasted on the ninth and the tenth. The Beis Yosef and later authorities wrote at length about this. The Kenesses HaGedolah wrote, by way of wit: I heard that it is as though he fasted on the ninth, which is Tishah B'Av, and on the tenth, which is Yom Kippur. End quote. In the Sefer Amarkal I found: the ninth is Tishah B'Av, and the tenth is Asarah B'Teves. End quote; this requires analysis. In Shibolei HaLeket he wrote that because one eats well on Erev Yom Kippur and fasts on Yom Kippur, the affliction is harder for him; and see siman 608.
