Even. See Turei Zahav: on Purim and Shavuos, although eating is a mitzvah, one may fast a fast for a dream. This is unlike Erev Yom Kippur, where eating is as though he fasted, and such a fast is beneficial for a dream; why should he fast? See siman 288, in Turei Zahav, os 3, that afterward Yom Kippur, if he fasted on Yom Kippur, he fulfilled his obligation; and even if he did not fast, Yom Kippur atones for a positive command. See there. And see Magen Avraham, os 1; with Hashem's help it will be explained further there.
