They do not fall on their faces on Erev Yom Kippur.Also they do not say Lamenatzeach and Mizmor leTodah (Minhagim); also they do not say many selichot before dawn, and some places are accustomed to increase selichot, and all is according to custom. Regarding saying Avinu Malkeinu on Erev Yom Kippur there is dispute among acharonim; my city's custom is not to say it except when Yom Kippur falls on Shabbat when Avinu Malkeinu is not said — then they say it on Erev Yom Kippur at shacharit.:
