(s.k. 2) Erev Yom Kippur, etc. See what I wrote in siman 584, where the Magen Avraham wrote that even if the second day of Rosh Hashanah falls on Erev Shabbos, Avinu Malkeinu is not said at Minchah.
And so it is implied in siman 606, seif 5, where the Rema states without qualification and writes that on Erev Yom Kippur at Minchah Avinu Malkeinu is not said. He did not distinguish that if Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos they would say it at Minchah; all the more so, even in such a case they do not say it at Minchah.
