It is a mitzvah to eat on the night of Yashuk, etc. and the reason for the secret is explained in Shear Hakav, page 44, because on each day of Yahusha we repair our external lives by eating and drinking and their blessings, and on Yushka it is forbidden to eat, and therefore we need to increase our eating and drinking during Yushka in order to repair our external lives on the day and tomorrow of Yashuk. And it is explained in Pe'ah Sha'ar 27 PA that one should eat at the same time per the value of B. days of Yash, therefore at least he should make B. feasts on the feast so that he would have to bless the one who spends it and in B.H. correct the external life by eating and drinking and the blessing and at the feast of the cattle they will correct the external life of today and at the feast of the break they will correct the external life of tomorrow.
