And even a fast for a dream, etc. This implies that even if he wants to be stringent and fast until the concluding meal, it is forbidden, for this is not comparable to Erev Tishah B'Av, siman 552, seif 11, since here the eating itself stands in place of affliction. So the Shelah ruled, unlike the Magen Avraham; and in siman 529 I wrote much about this. The laws of mourning, such as sitting on the ground, apply on Erev Yom Kippur until nightfall, for it has no special elevation except that there is a mitzvah to eat on it; Magen Avraham at the end of siman 606. The Shelah wrote: Those accustomed not to eat meat, fruit, and wine except on Yom Tov may do so on Erev Yom Kippur, even on the night before it. However, in a place where they are accustomed to increase Selichos, as on Erev Rosh Hashanah, it is forbidden at night. End quote. And where it is permitted, that is specifically after tzeis hakochavim; but before then, even if they prayed Maariv, it is forbidden, since Tachanun is recited at the Minchah before it. Magen Avraham. And one who vowed is forbidden even at night; see there.
