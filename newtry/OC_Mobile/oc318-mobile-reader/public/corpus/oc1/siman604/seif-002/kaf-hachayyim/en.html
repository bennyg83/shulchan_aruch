2) Ibid.: It is a mitzvah to eat on erev Yom Kippur, etc. The reason according to the secret teachings is explained in Shaar HaKavanos, 100d. On each day of the Aseres Yemei Teshuvah, the external aspect is rectified through eating, drinking, and their blessings. On Yom Kippur it is forbidden to eat; therefore, one must increase his eating and drinking on erev Yom Kippur in order to rectify the external aspect of that day and of the following day, see there. It is explained in Pri Etz Chaim, Gate 27, chapter 1, that one must eat on erev Yom Kippur an amount corresponding to two days, see there. Therefore, at minimum, one should eat two bread meals, so that he will be required to recite HaMotzi and Birkas HaMazon, in order to rectify the external aspect through eating, drinking, and the blessing. At the morning meal he should intend to rectify the external aspect of the present day, and at the concluding meal he should intend to rectify the external aspect of the following day. Some are accustomed to break bread over two loaves, as on Yom Tov, as the Yafeh LaLev, volume 2, os 3, wrote in the name of the Shelah and Me'il Shmuel, see there. Before the meal, he should sit in his place and recite the chapter of Tehillim, "A chapter of Tehillim of David, Hashem is my Shepherd, etc." He should then say: "For the sake of the unification of the Holy One, blessed is He, and His Shechinah, etc., and in the name of every nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, and yechidah associated with the roots of our nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, and yechidah, in their general and particular aspects in Atzilus, Beriah, Yetzirah, and Asiyah, I hereby come to fulfill the mitzvah of the meal of erev Yom Kippur, as our Sages, of blessed memory, expounded: Anyone who eats on the ninth is regarded as though he fasted on the ninth and the tenth, and to rectify the root of this mitzvah and all the mitzvos dependent upon it in the supernal realm. May the pleasantness, etc. May it be Your will before You, Hashem, our God and God of our fathers, that all the sparks of holiness within the food and drink that I eat and drink be refined to the ultimate degree of their rectification and refinement; protect me so that they not cause me to sin and not harm me, and purify our souls. May the pleasantness, etc." See citations 40.
