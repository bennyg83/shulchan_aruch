Even a fast for a dream. The reason appears to be that since eating on this day is considered an actual fast, it is properly included in the rule that a fast is beneficial for a dream, etc. This is unlike other Yom Tov days and Shabbos, where although eating is a mitzvah and there is reward for it, nevertheless it is not considered an actual fast in all respects. And if someone erred and fasted, he must observe a fast for his fast; so wrote the Beis Yosef, siman 568. See what I wrote in siman 288, that in this case he need not fast after Yom Kippur.
