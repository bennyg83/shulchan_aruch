On the way. The Yerushalmi: this was stated only regarding one who is passing by; but regarding one who is standing, there is a chazakah that he intended. And this requires further study: is he not one who knows whether he intended or not? And perhaps it refers to where he forgot whether he intended or not (Magen Avraham. One who comes to the synagogue to fulfill his obligation with the congregation) even though at the time he heard he did not direct his heart, but only in a general way; has fulfilled; but one who comes to the synagogue in a general way has not fulfilled (Radbaz, part 1, siman 160). A blind person is obligated in tekiah; and if he was established to blow every year and they removed him, if they do not find one as expert as he, they should restore him to his appointment. And l'chatchila one should not remove him because he is blind, even though another as expert as he is found (Radbaz, part 1, siman 59; and Kenesses HaGedolah). And see Responsa Shaar Ephraim, siman 26. A blower who died and another entered in his place, and when his son grew up he wished to inherit his father's right (see Halachot Ketanot, siman 39). And see the Sefer Kol Ben Levi, folio 28c.
