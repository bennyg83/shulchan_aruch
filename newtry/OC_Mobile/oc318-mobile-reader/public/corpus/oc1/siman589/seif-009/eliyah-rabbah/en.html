[9] [Levush] One who passes before the synagogue, etc. But one who is standing; there is a chazakah that he intended (Yerushalmi). And it appears to me that this is specifically when he was passing and [then] stood; but if he was standing inside his house, there is no such chazakah. One who comes to the synagogue to fulfill his obligation with the congregation (even though at the time he heard he did not direct his heart, but only in a general way) has fulfilled; but one who comes to the synagogue in a general way has not fulfilled (Radbaz; Kenesses HaGedolah; Magen Avraham). And regarding distance from the synagogue: if it is clear to him that he heard a shofar sound, he has fulfilled (Taz, siman 587).
