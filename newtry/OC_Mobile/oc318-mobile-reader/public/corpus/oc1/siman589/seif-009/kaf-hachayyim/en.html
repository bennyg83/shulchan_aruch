9) On the first day of Rosh Hashanah, if the shofar blower lacked the strength to sound the blasts arranged during the blessings, and a minor who had reached 13 years sounded them, but it was not known whether he had developed the signs of maturity, the congregation fulfills its obligation through him. This is because they had already fulfilled the Torah obligation through the blasts sounded while seated, and for a rabbinic obligation we rely on the presumption that he developed two pubic hairs. Responsa Shoel U'Meishiv, vol. 3, siman 177, see there; Pitchei Teshuvah; Pischei Olam, os 2. See above, siman 55, seif 5, in the Rama's gloss, and our words there, with God's help.
