A minor who cannot discharge an adult's obligation does not discharge it even after becoming a full 13 years old, until it is known that he developed two pubic hairs after age 13. For an obligation according to the Torah, we do not rely on the presumption that once he has reached the requisite age he has developed the signs of maturity. He cannot discharge even the obligation of one like himself, who is already 13 but whose development of two pubic hairs is uncertain. One should be stringent in this even on the second day of Rosh Hashanah, where another person is available. All this concerns discharging the obligation of an individual. However, for appointment as the shofar blower for the congregation, to discharge their obligation, one should seek a shofar blower who is 30 years old and possesses the other qualities that we wrote in siman 581 regarding prayer. At the very least, his beard must have filled out, in accordance with the law of a shaliach tzibbur explained in siman 53. Mateh Ephraim, os 7.
