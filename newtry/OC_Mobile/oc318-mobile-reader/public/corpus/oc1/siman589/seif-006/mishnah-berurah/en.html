(8) They may blow, and we do not say that since they are exempt there is a desecration of Yom Tov in their blowing; for we hold "greater is one who is commanded and does than one who is not commanded and does" hence one who is not commanded and does also has reward. And likewise a minor may blow even l'chatchila in order to train him, as is found below in siman 596 in the gloss.
(9) And likewise another who has already fulfilled, that is to say, even if he has already fulfilled; and we do not say that since he himself does not now need to blow, he then transgresses a shevus when he blows for them, for nevertheless they have some mitzvah in their blowing, as above.
(10) But they do not recite the blessing, etc., for what "and commanded us" is there regarding a matter in which she is not obligated, neither by Torah law nor by the words of the Scribes; for a woman regarding time-bound positive mitzvos is exempt even by rabbinic law.
(11) If they have already fulfilled, and therefore there are those who say he should blow for them before he hears the tekios in the synagogue. But the Magen Avraham concludes in the name of the Maharil that one should not blow during the first three hours, because then judgment is appointed, and it is not worthwhile to blow as an individual; rather he should blow for them after the synagogue tekios, and they should recite the blessing for themselves, or he should intend in his heart not to fulfill his obligation with the synagogue tekios, and then afterward he can also recite the blessing on their behalf.
