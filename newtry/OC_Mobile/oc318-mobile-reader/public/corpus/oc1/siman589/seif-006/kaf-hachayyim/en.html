6) [Seif 2] A deaf-mute, a mentally incompetent person, and a minor are exempt. A blind person is obligated in all the mitzvos of the Torah and in sounding the shofar according to the Torah, and he may discharge the obligation of others. Rabbeinu Yerucham, Nesiv 13, part 1, disagrees. The Radbaz wrote in vol. 1, siman 59, that one need not be concerned for his view. Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos Tur. So too wrote the Pri Chadash in seif 4, that a blind person is obligated according to the Torah and may discharge the obligation of others, and one need not be concerned for the view of Rabbeinu Yerucham, who wrote that he is obligated only rabbinically, see there.
