Even though women are exempt, etc. And therefore one should not blow for women until after they have left the synagogue, after they have blown for the congregation, and not beforehand (Maharil). However, if she is a woman who has given birth or is ill and cannot wait, one should blow for her immediately.
But others should not recite the blessing, etc. And if they cannot recite the blessing, the blower may recite the blessing on their behalf (Rosh). And there are those who disagree: for they said, all the blessings (even though one has already fulfilled them, he may discharge others) except for the blessing over bread and wine; the same applies to all blessings over enjoyment: if one has already fulfilled, he may not discharge another. Since he is not obligated in the matter, it is forbidden for his fellow to recite the blessing on his behalf; all the more so regarding these blessings, where it is preferable that the women themselves not recite the blessing (only that we do not protest against them) so that another should not recite the blessing on their behalf (Beis Yosef; Darkei Moshe).
