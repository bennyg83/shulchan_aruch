[6] [Levush] Before they blow in the synagogue, etc. But in Maaglei Tzedek he wrote in the name of the Maharil that one should not blow during the first three [hours], because judgment is then appointed, as he wrote at the end of siman 591; therefore he should blow for them after the synagogue tekiah (end quote). And if so, he cannot recite the blessing for them. Therefore it appears to me that if he has another shofar he should blow at the time of the synagogue tekiah, for then even if it is within the first three there is no concern; and if not, he should intend in his heart not to fulfill his obligation with the synagogue tekiah, and then he can recite the blessing for them. Magen Avraham. And in my humble opinion it is also possible that Maaglei Tzedek's view is thus, for he wrote this wording: "they should blow immediately after they blow in the synagogue" end of his wording; and he changed from the Maharil's wording who wrote "after they blow," but rather he means immediately after they know that they have begun (and see siman 585).
