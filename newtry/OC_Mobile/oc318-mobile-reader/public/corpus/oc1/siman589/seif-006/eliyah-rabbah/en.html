[6] [Levush] Before they blow in the synagogue, etc. But in Maaglei Tzedek he wrote in the name of Maharil that one should not blow during the first three [hours] because judgment is then appointed, as he wrote at the end of siman 591; therefore he blows for them after the synagogue tekios — end of his words. If so he cannot bless for them. Therefore it appears to me that if he has another shofar he should blow at the time of the synagogue tekios, for then even if it is within the first three there is no concern; and if not, he should intend in his heart not to fulfill his obligation with the synagogue tekios and then he can bless for them — Magen Avraham. In my humble opinion it is also possible that Maaglei Tzedek's view is thus, for he wrote this wording: "they shall blow immediately after they blow in the synagogue" — end of his wording; and he changed from Maharil's wording who wrote "after they blow," but he means immediately after they know they began — see siman 585.
