(s.k. 1) But one who hears. And even l'chatchila another may recite the blessing, etc., so the text should read; and so it is in the Bach. And it teaches us: one might have thought that since he does not speak and cannot recite the blessing, [he may] not [blow] l'chatchila; it teaches us [otherwise].
A blind person is obligated, etc. For we do not hold like R' Yehudah in Bava Kamma, folio 88, who holds that a blind person is exempt from all the mitzvos; [a discussion] brought in Beis Yosef, siman 473, where it is implied that he ruled like R' Yehudah that a blind person's obligation is only rabbinic, and therefore he cannot discharge one who is obligated according to the Torah.
