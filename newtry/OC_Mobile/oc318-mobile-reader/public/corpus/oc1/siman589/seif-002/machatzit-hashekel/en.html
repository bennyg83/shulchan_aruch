(Seif katan 2) His own kind, etc. And the Rif explained, etc. I did not find this anywhere. The matter itself is also astonishing, that it should be male for some time and female for some time. Does it not have both male and female organs? I also saw in Shulchan Aruch Baer Heitev that he wrote: if so, why do we need a verse to exclude that circumcision of an androginos does not override Shabbat? Either way: if he was born at a time when he is female, it is obvious; and if then he is male, do we know that after some time he will be female? End quote.
And to this one may say that certainly every time is equal for him, for male and female organs are recognizable in him. Rather, sometimes the male organ is more recognizable and he appears primarily male, and afterward, for some time, the female organ is more recognizable and he appears primarily female. But I have not found this. On the contrary, according to my limited knowledge, it is implied in all the poskim that all androginos cases are alike.
