(s.k. 2) His own kind, etc. And the Rif explained, etc. (I did not find this anywhere. And the matter itself is also astonishing: that for some time he should be male and for some time female) for does he not have both male and female organs? I also saw in the Shulchan Aruch Baer Heitev that he wrote: if so, why do we need a verse to exclude that circumcision of an androginos does not override Shabbos? Either way: if he was born at a time when he is female, it is obvious; and if then he is male, who knows that after some time he will be female? End quote.
And to this one may say: certainly at every time it is equal for him, for male and female organs are recognizable in him. Rather, sometimes the male is more recognizable and he appears primarily male, and afterward for some time the female is more recognizable and he appears primarily female; but I have not found this. On the contrary, according to my limited knowledge it is implied in all the poskim that all androginos cases are alike.
