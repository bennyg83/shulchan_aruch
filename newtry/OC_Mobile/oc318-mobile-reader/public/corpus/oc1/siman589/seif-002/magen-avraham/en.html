The Maharil calls out a challenge to those who bring little things to the Bakhch that confuse the minds of those who hear, but that the women should take them with them, they are exempted: But he hears. And if another person can bless and he is impaled (28), a soma must be impaled and if he was held to be impaled every year and they removed him because they were afraid of being judged, if he is not found to be as good as him, they will restore him to his appointment.
