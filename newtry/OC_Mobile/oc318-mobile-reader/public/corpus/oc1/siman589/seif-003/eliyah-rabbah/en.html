[3] [Levush] There are those who say it is a complete obligation, etc. This requires further study: for even according to the one who says at the end of chapter HaArel that he is a distinct creature, nevertheless it is a doubt; and so it is implied in the Rambam, chapter 2 of Hilchos Berachos, and the Ran. There is also some proof from the rule that one does not discharge one who is not his kind, and that is from the Gemara. However, in Issur VeHeter, siman 44, there are those who say he is definitely male; and see the Bach; and this requires further study.
