A woman is exempt, etc. And even though women are exempt, they may blow and recite the blessing, as is explained nearby in se'if 6: since they have understanding, they may take the obligation upon themselves; and the same law applies to a minor. But a shoteh and a deaf person, who never come to obligation in any way; we protest against them. This is the view of Rabbeinu Tam, who holds that regarding every positive mitzvah women may recite the blessing; and so wrote the Rosh, the Ran, and the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 2 of Hilchos Shofar.
