On the way. See Baer Heitev; and he wrote in Shemesh Tzedakah, siman 29, that they raised a difficulty against what the Maggid Mishneh wrote in chapter 2 of Hilchos Shofar; that therefore intent is required, since the essence of the mitzvah is the hearing, and even the blower [needs intent], which is not so regarding eating matzah, etc., for it is difficult against this from the law of chalitzah, which is an act; and see there, where he wrote at length to resolve this. And in the responsum of the author's son he clarifies the matter of whether mitzvos require intent (see there. And that which the Radbaz wrote regarding a blind person) see Devar Shmuel, siman 12: a blind person discharges others with shofar-blowing and with all Torah mitzvos (see there).
