(12) One who vowed not to benefit, etc., for example, Reuven who vowed upon himself not to benefit from Shimon: even so, Shimon is permitted to blow for him, for mitzvos were not given for the sake of benefit, and mitzvah tekios are not included in the benefit-prohibition he imposed upon himself, which is not so when he forbade [Shimon's] tekios themselves: he forbade even this, which is not benefit.
(13) Of his own accord; even when he says, "Whoever wishes to discharge me of my obligation should blow," it is permitted; for in such a case he is not performing his agency.
(14) "And discharge me" is forbidden, for since he acts as his agent, he has benefit in that his agency is performed; and after the fact he has fulfilled [Peri Megadim in the name of the Peri Chadash].
