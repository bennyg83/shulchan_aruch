7) If he had an established right to the mitzvah of sounding the shofar and they removed him because of Rabbeinu Yerucham's view, then if they cannot find a proficient shofar blower equal to him, they should restore him to his appointment. If they find someone equally proficient, the second person retains his established position, and the first, once displaced, remains displaced, since the view was issued by Rabbeinu Yerucham. Radbaz, ibid.; Knesset HaGedolah, ibid.; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2. We have already written further concerning this above, siman 53, os 65; take it from there.
