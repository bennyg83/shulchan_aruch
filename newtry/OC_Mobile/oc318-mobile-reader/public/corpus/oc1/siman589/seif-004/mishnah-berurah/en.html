(5) Discharges his own kind, for all androginos cases are alike, having male and female organs; and if he is in the category of male, his fellow too is in the category of male. But he cannot discharge one who is not his kind, for perhaps he is in the category of female.
(6) Even his own kind, etc., for perhaps if it is torn open it will be found that this tumtum is female and his fellow is male. And even on the second day, which is rabbinic, we adopt all the stringencies of the first day. However, even on the first day, if a male heard from a tumtum, he blows again without a blessing [Peri Megadim]. A tumtum and an androginos, even though they are obligated to blow, nevertheless should not recite the blessing, for the blessing is rabbinic and a doubt is resolved leniently [and as above in siman 17, se'if 2].
