In the Shulchan Aruch: Even though women are exempt, etc. And he wrote in Shaagat Aryeh, siman 24, that it is more proper for a man to blow for a woman than for her to blow for herself, or a woman for a woman. And see there, siman 26, that it is forbidden to carry a lulav or a shofar out to the public domain for the needs of a woman so that she take the lulav or hear shofar-blowing. And see there, siman 27, that it is permitted to carry out to the public domain for the needs of a man who is in doubt whether he blew or did not blow, and likewise for a tumtum; and the same law applies to a lulav (end quote). And the Machazik Berakhah wrote that he adopted the view of safek de'oraita lechumra, whereas we hold that it is only rabbinic; if so, here it is forbidden. And see there, siman 108, that it is forbidden to carry the shofar out to the public domain for the needs of a minor; and also for a man who has already heard the seated tekios it is forbidden to carry out for the needs of the Musaf tekios; and likewise it is forbidden to carry out the lulav for the needs of the Hallel wavings, since he has already fulfilled his Torah obligation (see there).
