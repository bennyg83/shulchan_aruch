His own kind. For perhaps this tumtum is female and that one is male; but androginos cases are all alike, for they have both male and female organs (so it is in the Rambam in Hilchos Ishus; and so wrote the Rama in siman 17, se'if 3) see there. And the Magen Avraham wrote: and the Rif explained that even though his fellow is also an androginos, at the time that he is female and his fellow is male, he does not discharge him (end quote) see there. But in the Rif before us I did not find this. And further, according to this it would imply that at times he is male and at times female, as I have heard people say that an androginos is male one month and female one month, and that is not implied in the Gemara or the poskim. And further, if so, why do we need a verse to exclude an androginos such that his circumcision does not override Shabbos: if he was born in a month when he is female, it is obvious; and if he is male, it is also difficult how we know that the coming month he will be female, and it is easily understood.
