(1) After he finished, etc.—meaning: when he blesses birkat hamazon on a cup as explained above siman 182, he blesses Borei peri hagafen after he finishes birkat hamazon; and this is even if he already blessed on wine during the meal, for all that one eats and drinks after birkat hamazon is a separate meal, because birkat hamazon is departure and diversion of attention from what preceded.
(2) See siman 271 seif 15—that he returns and blesses; and in a matter pertaining to the meal he does not return and bless b'dieved.
(3) And afterward the others taste—for since he blessed on this cup it is proper that he drink first.
(4) If all need—the wording is somewhat garbled; the intent is as if he wrote: and likewise if all need; meaning: it is unnecessary to state if there is only one cup and all drink from this cup—it is certainly l'chatchila that he taste first; and even if each has an empty cup before him and he pours after the berachah before his tasting from his cup to their cups—likewise they should not taste first, since in any case they all need his cup.
(5) But if they are not, etc.—meaning: if there is wine before each and he discharges them with his berachah and it is as if they bless for themselves—they need not wait for him.
(6) Need not, etc.—this refers to the above that they did not need the blesser's cup; and the reason he need not pour is that since they fulfilled through his berachah, each one's cup is called a cup of berachah.
(7) Defective—that then he must pour a little from his cup to their cups before tasting from it in order to repair their defect; and in this they must be careful not to taste before he tastes first, since they need his cup.
