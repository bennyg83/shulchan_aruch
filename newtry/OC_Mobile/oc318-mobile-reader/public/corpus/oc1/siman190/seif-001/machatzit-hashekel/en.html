(s.k. 1) After he finished, etc. And what they do not bless at a wedding—meaning regarding birkat hamazon: they bless after birkat hamazon first six berachot and afterward Borei peri hagafen; it would seem at least Borei peri hagafen should precede the six berachot as in kiddush—we do not dispute. Meaning: in birkat hamazon each time Borei peri hagafen is last. But Bach there siman 62 s.k. 2 wrote as follows: and for our custom one can say the reason that it should not appear we bless Borei peri hagafen for the sake of birkat hamazon and not on account of the seven berachot—therefore we delay Borei peri hagafen; so too Bach; end of his words.
