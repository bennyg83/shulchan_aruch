(1) [Seif 1] After he finished birkat hamazon he blesses, etc.—and even if he already blessed on wine during the meal. Beit Yosef. Levush. Razah letter 1. And that which one does not bless Borei peri hagafen before birkat hamazon as in kiddush and havdalah—for birkat hamazon is different, for it is departure and diversion of attention, and there will be interruption between berachah and drinking—Tur. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. And that which they are accustomed in birkat hamazon at a wedding to wait to bless Borei peri hagafen after six berachot—that is the reason: since it was enacted after birkat hamazon with Borei peri hagafen adjacent to drinking, the same was enacted also at a wedding to say it after they say six berachot. Binyamin Zev siman 379. Kitzur Nachmanides in Hagahot Taz, Magen Avraham there. However there are places accustomed to say also after birkat hamazon Borei peri hagafen before six berachot; and see Ma'amar Mordechai letter 11 who made support for this custom from words of the poskim; but the view of Maran z"l is not so, but rather in birkat hamazon one blesses six berachot before Borei peri hagafen, as written in Even haEzer siman 62 seif 9—see there. And nevertheless one who fears—custom is custom; and the custom of our holy city Jerusalem is as the first version that Maran wrote there: they say berachat chatanim on another cup—meaning they give one cup in the hand of the one who blesses birkat hamazon who says zimmun, and another cup before another person; and when the one who blesses birkat hamazon finishes and says Oseh shalom, etc., the other takes the cup before him in his hand and says upon it six berachot; and the one who blesses birkat hamazon returns to say Borei peri hagafen on the cup in his hand on which he blessed in birkat hamazon; and afterward they mix the cups one with another and drink—the one who blesses birkat hamazon and the one who blesses berachat chatanim—each from the cup in his hand; and from the cup of birkat chatanim they give to groom and bride, and from the cup of birkat hamazon they give to the fellow diners.
