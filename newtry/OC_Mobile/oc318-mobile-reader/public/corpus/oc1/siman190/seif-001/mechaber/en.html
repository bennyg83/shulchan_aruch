Drinking wine after the berachah and its laws. And in it are 5 seifim. After he finishes birkat hamazon he blesses Borei peri hagafen and the blesser tastes [and if he interrupted between the berachah and tasting see siman 271 seif 15]; and afterward the others taste if all need
one cup, and the blesser gives from his cup to an empty cup in their hands—they should not taste until he tastes. But if they do not need his cup they may taste before he tastes.
