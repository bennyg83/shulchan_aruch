And what we do not bless Borei peri hagafen before birkat hamazon as in kiddush and havdalah—for birkat hamazon is departure and diversion of attention and there will be interruption between berachah and drinking (Tur). And what we do not bless at a wedding Borei peri hagafen before the seven berachot—because of not dividing (Beit Yosef siman 49).

And the blesser tastes. It is a mitzvah to swallow a coarse gulp because of affection for the mitzvah (Sukkah 49; see there siman 184 seif 4).
