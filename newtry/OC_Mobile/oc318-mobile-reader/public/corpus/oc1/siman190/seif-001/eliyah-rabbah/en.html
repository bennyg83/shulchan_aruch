(1) The berachah is an interruption, etc. It is not correct—what is this interruption to me between birkat hamazon and eating? For immediately upon washing hands they said a berachah, and not upon eating; also this berachah belongs to birkat hamazon; and furthermore he blesses Borei peri hagafen and afterward takes and blesses. And it appears he understood so in Ra'ash's words that Beit Yosef brought, and he did not examine with due honor—for this is his explanation: since birkat hamazon is diversion of attention and interruption from eating, there is also diversion of attention from the wine berachah, and there is interruption between the wine berachah and drinking—which is not so in kiddush and havdalah, for it is not for diversion of attention, therefore it is not an interruption, even though drinking and blessing together are not possible.
