He blesses Borei peri hagafen, etc. See Tosafot chapter Eruvin and in Ra'ash the reason why birkat hamazon was changed from all other matters that require a cup—for all of them one blesses Borei peri hagafen first, and here Borei peri hagafen is last; see there. And we say savri maranan even though the house of swallowing is empty, so as not to divide the blessing over the wine; and the same applies in kiddush and havdalah. And there are several reasons why we say savri over wine and not over beer; and I found one reason: because wine brought a curse to the world in the days of Noah when he became drunk and the sons of Canaan were cursed; therefore we say savri—meaning, understand that I intend to drink with your consent I will drink so that no harm come—and therefore they are accustomed to answer after him lechaim.
