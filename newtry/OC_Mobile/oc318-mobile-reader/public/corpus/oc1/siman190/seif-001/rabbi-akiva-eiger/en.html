(Magen Avraham s.k. 3) That if he erred—meaning when he knows at the time of the error that his intent is to eat more, but he erred in the law and thought he must bless another berachah; but if inadvertently he blessed berachah acharonah—not like below siman 487 seif 2. Kiryat Melech Rav in his responsa siman 9.
