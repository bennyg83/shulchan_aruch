(Seif 4) One of the fellow diners tastes. And the blesser in birkat hamazon should not bless Borei peri hagafen, and the taster is the one who blesses—for perhaps birkat hamazon does not require a cup. And in particular since we rule thus for the essential law—if so it is not a berachah on mitzvot and one may not bless on behalf of another if he does not also bless for himself.
