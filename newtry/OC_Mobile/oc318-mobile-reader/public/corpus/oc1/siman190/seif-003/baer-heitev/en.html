Wine—and the same applies to other beverages. The doubt is whether we compare it to eating and it suffices with a kezayit, or we say that since it is drinking it requires drinking enough for satiation, which is a revi'it. And some wish to say that for aged wine one blesses with less than a kezayit, for with a little one's mind is settled; and it appears to me that one needs the full measure, for the Sages did not divide measures.
