The measure of drinking wine to become obligated in berachah acharonah—there is a doubt whether a kezayit suffices or a revi'it; therefore one should be careful to drink either less than a kezayit or a revi'it in order to remove doubt. And here it is impossible to drink less than a kezayit, for anything requiring a cup requires drinking from it melo lugmav, which is rov revi'it; therefore one should drink a full revi'it.
