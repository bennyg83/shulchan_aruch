There: Beit HaGolah concedes that one is obligated in that of Rav Ashi—that one is exempt with the cup of birkat hamazon; and also in Nemeilik; and we do not rule like him—even in Nemeilik, and all the more in the cup of birkat hamazon; but rather like Amemar, as explained in siman 174 seif 5; and how is it possible that from this Beit HaGolah took his law?
