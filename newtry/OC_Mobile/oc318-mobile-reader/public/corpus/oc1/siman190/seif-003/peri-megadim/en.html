That it is. See Taz and Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 5, regarding what Taz wrote. We do not find the exposition that “you shall bless” refers to the cup of Birkas HaMazon. Rather, “you shall bless” refers to the blessing of HaZan. Regarding the fourth cup on Pesach, one should drink a full reviis so that he can recite the concluding blessing; for the first three cups, most of a reviis suffices.
Regarding the proof from Shabbos 77a, Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 4, writes that Rambam in Hilchos Shabbos, chapter 18, halachah 2, rules like Rabbi Nasan that a dried substance has the measure of a kezayis. This shows that wine and other liquids have the measure of a reviis while liquid, but a kezayis when congealed. See there. Rav Yosef said that Rabbi Nasan and Rabbi Yose son of Rabbi Yehudah expressed the same view, which implies that this is a rejected school of opinion and the halachah does not follow them. Rambam nevertheless rules like Rabbi Nasan, because he follows Abaye, who holds that they disagree. Rabbi Yose son of Rabbi Yehudah speaks of blood, which is thick, so that a reviis congeals to a kezayis. Wine is thinner, and therefore more than a reviis is required to congeal to a kezayis. Even so, Rambam rules like Rabbi Nasan regarding Shabbos because any congealed substance containing a kezayis is classified as food, not because it retains the law of a liquid. See Hilchos Tumas Ochlin 1:19 and Hilchos She'ar Avos HaTumah 1:4. This presently requires study.
