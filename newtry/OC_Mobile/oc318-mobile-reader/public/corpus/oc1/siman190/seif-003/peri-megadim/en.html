That he is—Taz, first note letter 5; see Taz. And we do not find this teaching regarding the cup of birkat hamazon except from the berachah of Hamotzi; and this berachah is Birkat haZan. And the fourth cup on Passover one drinks a full revi'it so he may bless berachah acharonah, and the first three cups rov revi'it suffices. And what he wrote from Shabbat 97a—Avraham David letter 4 wrote that Rambam in Hilchot Shabbat ruled (chapter 18 halachah 2) like Rabbi Natan: dry in a kezayit—thus wine; and other beverages a revi'it in liquid; in the sea in dry a kezayit; see there. And behold Rav Yosef said Ramban and Rashi in Beit Yosef said one thing—it appears this is one shitah and halachah is not like them; and Rambam z"l ruled like Ramban who holds like Abaye, for Rashi in Beit Yosef disputes regarding blood that is thick—a revi'it stands on a kezayit; from this wine that is thin—more than a revi'it is a kezayit; and nevertheless Rambam ruled like Ramban on Shabbat that anything that has a kezayit of food taste is food; when dried it is not beverage taste. And see Rambam Tum'at Ochlin chapter 1 halachah 19 and chapter 1 from halachah 5 halachah 4; and it requires study at this time.
