Drinking wine. And the same applies to other beverages—there is a doubt (Ba'al Halachot Gedolot chapter 3 and Mordechai chapter Keitzad Mevarkhin s.v. mayim); and the doubt is whether we compare it to eating and a kezayit suffices, or we say that since we expound "vesavata" is drinking we require drinking enough for satiation which is a revi'it, as Shulchan Aruch wrote. And some wish to say that for aged wine one blesses with less than a kezayit, for with a little one's mind is settled; and it appears to me one needs the full measure, for the Sages did not divide measures.

That he is rov revi'it—meaning for an average person; but for a large person we require more, for all drinking is according to what the person is (Tosafot Yoma daf 80); and nevertheless we do not require more than a revi'it (Ran, Bach siman 472, and so Aggudah chapter Erev Pesach).
