Drinking wine. The same doubt applies to other beverages. Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 3, and Mordechai, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin, cited in Darchei Moshe. The doubt is whether drinking should be compared to eating, in which case the measure is a kezayis, or whether, since we interpret “and you shall be satisfied” as referring to drinking, one must drink enough to reach satisfaction, namely a reviis, as written in seif 10. Some wish to say that for liquor one recites an after-blessing after less than a kezayis because even a small amount settles the mind. This does not appear correct to me. A fixed measure is required. There are certainly very strong wines and rich foods, such as preserves, of which even a small quantity settles the mind, yet the required measure still applies, for Chazal did not differentiate in their measures.
Which is most of a reviis. This refers to an average-sized person. A large person requires more, because regarding drinking the measure depends on the individual. Tosafos, Yoma 80. Nevertheless, no more than a full reviis is required. Ran; Bach, siman 272; and Agudah, chapter Arvei Pesachim.
