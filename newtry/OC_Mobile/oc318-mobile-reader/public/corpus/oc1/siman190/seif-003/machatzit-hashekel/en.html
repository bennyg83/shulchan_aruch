(s.k. 3) After, etc. And it appears to me if he erred, etc.—nevertheless, since his intent was to drink more. And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote as follows: and I am uncertain, for it is not preferable to the cup of birkat hamazon, and even so it is an interruption (meaning: therefore one must bless Borei peri hagafen on the cup of birkat hamazon after birkat hamazon because birkat hamazon is departure, etc., as Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 1)—and perhaps he erred; so I; end of his words.

And specifically when his intent, etc.—this refers to Tur's words that he brought: if his intent is to drink more he should not bless after it, etc., as he wrote; and specifically when his intent, etc., for otherwise there is concern, etc., and if so it must be immediately after.
