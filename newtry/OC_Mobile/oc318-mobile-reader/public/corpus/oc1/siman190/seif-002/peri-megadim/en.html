Me'ein three—Taz and Bach and Perishah on this; and first note letter 5 that Ba'al Halachot Gedolot also holds thus regarding the cup of birkat hamazon—no berachah acharonah is needed for drinking melo lugmav rov revi'it, while berachah acharonah specifically requires a revi'it; even aged wine specifically requires a revi'it; so Magen Avraham letter 4 and Taz siman 271 letter 1; and doubt in berachot to be lenient; one who cannot drink a revi'it of aged wine should drink only within the meal; and more bread to dip in it; and birkat hamazon exempts him; nevertheless even if he did not dip in it, birkat hamazon exempts him from berachah acharonah.
