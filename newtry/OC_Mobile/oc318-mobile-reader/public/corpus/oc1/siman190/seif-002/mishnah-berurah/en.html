(8) After he drank, etc.—and if his intent is to drink more he should not bless after it except at the end after he finishes his drinking; and specifically when his intent is to drink immediately, for otherwise there is concern lest it be digested, for digestion of drinking is not a large measure; see above siman 184 seif 5 Magen Avraham and see in Biur Halacha.
