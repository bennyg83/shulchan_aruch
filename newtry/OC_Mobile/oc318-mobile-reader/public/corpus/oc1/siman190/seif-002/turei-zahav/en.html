Me'ein three. In Tur—to exclude Ba'al Halachot Gedolot who wrote that one should not bless after the cup of Birkat haMazon; and Beit Yosef wrote Ba'al Halachot Gedolot's reason: since we say in the Gemara Rav Ashi blessed the first cup and did not bless again—and this is not with precision, for this refers to the berachah of Borei peri hagafen; and in this Ba'al Halachot Gedolot concedes he is obligated.
