5) There: Afterward, the others should taste, etc. This applies if they did not interrupt with speech. If someone interrupted between the leader's Borei Pri HaGafen and tasting, the person who interrupted must recite a blessing and then drink, as stated above in siman 167, paragraph 58; see there. Nevertheless, the more correct course is for the person who interrupted neither to recite a blessing nor drink. See the cited paragraph and understand this. Chesed LaAlafim there writes the same. If the leader recited the concluding blessing over the wine before all the diners finished tasting, they may still taste without reciting an initial blessing, relying upon their intent to be covered by the leader. Nevertheless, refraining from action is preferable, since there is no obligation to taste. Zechor LeAvraham, under kaf, paragraph 2.
