(5) A complete revi'it, etc. In Lechem Chamudot there he emended in Ashrei that one must say rov revi'it—so wrote Mordechai and Aggudah chapter Keitzad Mevarkhin and chapter Sheloshah sheAchlu in name of R' Yosi that if he drinks an egg's volume he blesses; but he concludes at the end in name of Rambam and Semag and Rosh that a complete revi'it is required; and so wrote Shibolei haLeket daf 20 in name of Rabbeinu Simchah and Rabbeinu Avigdor. Also one can resolve Ra'ash's words according to what Tosafot wrote in Eruvin daf 4—see there and understand, for I shortened; and so agreed Bach; and so ruled Rabbeinu Yerucham and Tur and Agur and Mahariu siman 153. And in responsum of Rashba siman 245 he wrote this language: one who drinks the cup of birkat hamazon must drink a revi'it—until here; explanation: because of final berachah; and it is possible he holds as a matter of law that the cup of birkat hamazon itself is so—that one must drink a revi'it—and requires study. And in this matter it appears to me to reconcile all the difficulties of early and later authorities on Ba'al Halachot Gedolot in what they wrote that one need not bless on the cup of birkat hamazon me'ein three—for it appears to me that in truth if he drinks a revi'it he concedes one must bless; only he says there is no obligation to drink so much—and understand. I saw in Taz that he extended to prove and rule that even with less than a revi'it one blesses me'ein three; and his main proof: that final berachah is preferable to the cup of birkat hamazon, which is also learned from "uvarekhu" and it suffices as a full lugmav—see there. And I did not know, and I heard from this study that uverakhu teaches regarding the cup of birkat hamazon; and in Berakhot daf 48b this berachah is berachat haZan. Also in any case nothing is difficult—for the cup of birkat hamazon is like kiddush and havdalah, for one must bless upon it a first berachah Borei peri hagafen; but in final berachah we require a revi'it, as there is a difference between first berachah of eating—even less than a kezayit—and in final berachah we require a kezayit. Also what he wondered on Tur in siman 480 who ruled on a revi'it cup that one blesses after it—nothing is difficult, for it deals with one who drank a revi'it and relies on what Tur wrote in this siman; and there it teaches us that specifically on a revi'it cup one blesses and not on a third—see there. End of the matter: in my place I stand that one does not bless a final berachah except with a complete revi'it; and even for aged wine we require a revi'it, for we do not distinguish.
