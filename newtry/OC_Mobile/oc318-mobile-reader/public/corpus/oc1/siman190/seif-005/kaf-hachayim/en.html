(5) There, and afterward the others taste, etc.—meaning if they did not interrupt with speech; but if they interrupted with speech between Borei peri hagafen of the blesser and tasting, the one who interrupted must bless and afterward drink, as written above siman 167 letter 58—see there; and nevertheless the more correct way is not to bless and not to drink for one who interrupted—see in the noted letter and examine; and so too Chesed leAvraham there. And if the blesser blessed a final berachah on the wine before the fellow diners finished tasting, they may taste without a first berachah relying on what they intended regarding the blesser; and nevertheless return and examine—for it is preferable since there is no obligation to taste—Zekan Levi letter 2 from Ma'arekhet haKodesh.
