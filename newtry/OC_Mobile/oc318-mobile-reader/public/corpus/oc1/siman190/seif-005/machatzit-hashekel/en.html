(s.k. 5) That he is a person (Tosafot Pesachim daf 107a s.v. im ta'am, etc., and he brings proof from Yoma daf 80 as should be read).

And nevertheless we do not require more than a revi'it, since the cup measure is a revi'it for every person; certainly a large person does not need more, but for an average person they were lenient and a rov revi'it suffices; so Ran. And what Bach wrote at the end of Beit Yosef in the indexes siman 49 that the groom under the canopy when drinking wine does not fulfill except when drinking a revi'it—see there.

And I saw in Even haEzer Be'er Heitev Chadashim siman 34 he brought Bach's words above. And he concluded: and similarly wrote on him, and I did not see in our times that they are careful about this, but all who taste one tasting suffices; end of his words. And Sefer Kanahag is not translated and I do not know his reason.
