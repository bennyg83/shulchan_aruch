(19) Must bless—for presumably he does not intend to fulfill through the blesser's berachah since he does not know whether it will reach him. And Magen Avraham wrote: if the fellow diners explicitly intended that if the cup reaches them they fulfill through his berachah, they fulfill through this if the blesser too intended to discharge each of the fellow diners with his berachah; and many later authorities agreed. And all who wish to taste from the cup relying on the blesser's berachah should be careful not to speak until they taste from the cup, so there not be interruption between berachah and tasting.
