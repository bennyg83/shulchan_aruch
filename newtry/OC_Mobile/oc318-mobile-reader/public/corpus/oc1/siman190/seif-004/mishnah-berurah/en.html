(15) One of the fellow diners tastes—for since the fellow diners heard the berachah from the start and he intended regarding them to discharge them, their tasting helps all; and see in Biur Halacha what we wrote in name of Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger.
(16) And drinking of two, etc.—for we require that he or one of the fellow diners taste the measure of benefit so his mind is settled on it—and that is melo lugmav.
(17) That all taste—meaning a mere tasting, and melo lugmav is needed only for one. And behold here the Mechaber speaks only regarding the mitzvah of drinking the cup; and according to what the Mechaber explained above in seif 3 that to fulfill doubt l'chatchila one should drink a full revi'it—it cannot be what he wrote that mitzvah min hamuvchar is that all taste from a cup holding a revi'it unless it holds more than a revi'it.
(18) Siman 271—that some say drinking of all combines for melo lugmav; and see below siman 271 s.k. 69 in Mishna Berurah.
