(4) [Levush] In the chapter HaMotzi, etc. Taz wrote: I do not know what he thought to bring proof from a contradictory place—for there it says perhaps regarding blood that is thick it is specifically so; but regarding wine that is thin, a kezayit has more than a revi'it—if so he should have been uncertain that perhaps it requires more than a revi'it—until here. And in my humble opinion nothing is difficult—for Abaye speaks only perhaps Rabbi Yosi holds so; but R' Nathan certainly holds so even regarding wine; and furthermore R' Yosef said that Rabbi Yosi also said like R' Nathan; and so I saw in Rambam chapter 18 of Laws of Shabbat that he ruled like R' Nathan; and see Lechem Chamudot daf 62.
