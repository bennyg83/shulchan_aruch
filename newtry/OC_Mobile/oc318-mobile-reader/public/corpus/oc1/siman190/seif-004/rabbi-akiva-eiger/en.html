Seif 4. One of those who recited the blessing should taste it. The person who recited Bircas HaMazon should not recite Borei Peri HaGafen; rather, the person who tastes should recite it. Perhaps Bircas HaMazon does not require a cup, and this is especially so because we rule that this is the primary halachah. It is therefore not a blessing over a mitzvah, and one cannot recite it on behalf of another unless he is also reciting it for himself.
