He must give from the cup of blessing. This means before the one reciting the blessing drinks, so that everyone drinks from a cup that is not pagum. This is likewise implied in siman 190 and at the end of siman 271. The present practice, however, whereby the one reciting the blessing drinks first and afterward pours into the cups of those dining, which are then pagum, has no rationale. One might ask: if there is only one cup for the one reciting the blessing, the others will necessarily drink from a pagum cup, since he has already tasted from it. The answer is that wherever repair is possible, we repair it; where it is impossible, it suffices that the one reciting the blessing drinks from a cup that is not pagum. Therefore, it is preferable, where possible, for each person to have a separate cup.
