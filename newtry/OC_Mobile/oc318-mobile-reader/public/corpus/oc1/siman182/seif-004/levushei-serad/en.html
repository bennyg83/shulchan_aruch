Seif katan 11 The words should be emended to read: ‘See what I wrote in siman 296, seif 2.’ His intent is that Rama writes there that it is preferable to recite Kiddush over a cup of pagum wine than over beer. This is difficult: why not pour some beer into the wine, thereby removing the defect? It therefore appears that specifically water repairs it; alternatively, beer may spoil the wine.
