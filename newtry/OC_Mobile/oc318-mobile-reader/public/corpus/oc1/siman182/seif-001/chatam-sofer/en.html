In the Maga SKI dumya the defect of the altar is the defect of the altar because of its deficiency and indeed its deficiency is filled, but this is not because of the deficiency of the cup, for even the cup is full to the brim if it is filled from a vessel whose taste is wrong, and also if it is poured out of it in his hand and does not taste in his mouth, there is nothing wrong. Wine and M.M. have already mentioned yes in Yerushalmi, but to be filled with water is something that is not appropriate to be blessed on its own in Pah 3, and it is not his kind, nor blood for the altar at all.
