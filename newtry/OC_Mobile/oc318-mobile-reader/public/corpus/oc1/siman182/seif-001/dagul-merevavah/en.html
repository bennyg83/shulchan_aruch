Magen Avraham likewise wrote in siman 483, seif katan 3, regarding a beverage made from honey, called mead in Poland, distinguishing between Pesach and the rest of the year. See there in Eliyah Rabbah, who explains that they were lenient regarding the four cups. In my opinion, that explanation is unnecessary. Rather, since at that time this is what most people drink, it is considered chamar medinah during those days.
