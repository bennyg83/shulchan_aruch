[1] Even when alone, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov writes that this is not explicit in the Ran, and it likewise does not appear so from the words of those who analyze the matter carefully. Maharshal, however, in responsum 23, and Bach agree that a cup is required. Bach and Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah also agree that even when alone one should hold the cup in his hand, unlike those who infer otherwise. So too write Responsa Maharam of Rothenburg, siman 507; Tashbetz, siman 303; and Mahari Weil, siman 193. Knesses HaGedolah writes that perhaps the case is one in which he takes the cup and afterward puts it down, and Magen Avraham writes likewise. They were not precise, however, for Mahari Weil concludes by citing Tashbetz, and Tashbetz explicitly states that he should not put it down, because Bircas HaMazon requires a cup even for an individual. It is also explained there that one must observe all the laws stated in siman 183 even when alone. Bach further writes that even those who analyze the matter carefully spoke only specifically regarding wine.
