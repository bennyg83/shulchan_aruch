Laws of the cup for birkat hamazon and that it not
be defective. It has seven seifim: Some say birkat hamazon requires a cup even when alone, and one must wait and not eat if he has no cup to bless on when he anticipates it is possible he will have one even if must pass time of one eating period. Per this if two eat together each needs cup for birkat hamazon. Some say only with three; some say no cup at all even with three:nevertheless mitzvah min hamuvchar to bless on cup (Ran ch. Arvei Pesachim).]:
