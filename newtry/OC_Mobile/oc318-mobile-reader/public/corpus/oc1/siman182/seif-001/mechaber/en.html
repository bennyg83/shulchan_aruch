Laws of the cup for Birkat Hamazon and that it should not be defective.
It contains 7 paragraphs: Some say that Birkat Hamazon requires a cup even when one is alone, and one must wait for it and not eat if he does not have a cup to bless over, if he anticipates and it is possible that he will have one—even if this means passing the time of one eating period. According to this, if two people eat together, each must take a cup for Birkat Hamazon. And some say it is only required when there are three, and some say it is not required at all, even with three:Rema: nevertheless, it is an optimal mitzvah to bless over a cup (Ran, chapter Arvei Pesaḥim).]:
