[2] And he should not eat, etc. The Tur derives this from Havdalah. Magen Avraham objects that Havdalah is different, since tasting before Havdalah is prohibited because of askarah. It appears to me, however, that Pesachim 105 states that one may eat before Havdalah in order to recite the blessing over the cup, as Rashba writes in responsum 342 and Tosafos on 102b. His other questions may likewise be resolved. Nevertheless, one may be lenient because some authorities hold that Bircas HaMazon does not require a cup.
