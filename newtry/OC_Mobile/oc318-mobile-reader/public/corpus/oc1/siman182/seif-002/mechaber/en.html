The cup for Birkat Hamazon must be of wine and not of other beverages, even if he established his meal over them. And if wine is not available in that place, and beer or other drinks are considered ḥemar medina (important local beverage), one may bless over them, except for water:Rema: regarding the custom in these countries to bless over beer, one should not protest, since there are opinions that a cup is not required at all, and furthermore, the primary ḥemar medina is beer, and people establish their meals upon it. And even though wine is found in the city, it is still not considered readily available for this purpose, as it is expensive and not feasible to purchase wine for every meal to bless over. Nonetheless, it is an optimal mitzvah to bless over] wine (Darkhei Moshe). Some are meticulous, when blessing alone over wine, not to hold the cup in their hand but to place it on the table before them, and this practice is proper according to the Kabbalah (Beit Yosef).
