Cup for birkat hamazon is only wine and not other beverages even if established meal on them. If wine not available in that place and beer or other drinks are chamar medinah one blesses on them except water:what is practiced in these lands to bless on beer—do not protest for some say no cup required at all; also primary chamar medinah is beer and they establish meal on it; even though wine in city not called available for this being expensive and impossible buy wine every meal; nevertheless mitzvah min hamuvchar on wine] (Darkhei Moshe). Some meticulous when bless alone on wine not hold cup in hand but place on table before them—practice proper per Kabbalah (Beit Yosef).
