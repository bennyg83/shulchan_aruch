(Seif katan 5) And it is impossible to purchase, etc. ‘For this is not called seeking it out,’ Bach. Meaning, the Rosh implies that where beer is chamar medinah, one need not seek out wine. But one who already has wine in his house is not seeking it out, since it is already available to him. Therefore, even if beer is chamar medinah, he must recite Kiddush over the wine. So writes Bach.
