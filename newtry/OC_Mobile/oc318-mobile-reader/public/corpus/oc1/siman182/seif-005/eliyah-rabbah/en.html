Taz writes this, and the same applies to licorice beverage commonly drunk on Pesach. The later authorities write that where people drink mead and beer and wine is unavailable, one should recite the blessing over mead, which is more beloved, and not over beer. This applies specifically where mead is considered the local beverage. In a place where it is drunk only occasionally, it is not considered the local beverage, just like other drinks such as apple wine and pomegranate wine.
