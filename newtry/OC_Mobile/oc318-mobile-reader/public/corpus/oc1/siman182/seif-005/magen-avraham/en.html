Therefore, the practice is to add a little wine from the pitcher to the cup, thereby repairing it, and afterward pour the cup back into the pitcher. He then pours wine from the pitcher for the blessing, as explained in siman 183. Bach implies that only when one pours it in little by little do we say that each first portion becomes nullified as it enters, but not when he pours it all at once. It appears to me that one need not be stringent, for even regarding yayin nesech some authorities are lenient; see Yoreh Deah, siman 134. Here there is no reason for stringency at all, provided that the wine in the pitcher exceeds the wine in the cup.
