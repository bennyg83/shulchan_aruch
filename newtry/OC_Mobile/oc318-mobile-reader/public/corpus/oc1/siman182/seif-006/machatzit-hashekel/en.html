(Seif katan 6) Not to hold, etc. ‘And Mahari Weil,’ etc.; ‘and perhaps,’ etc. Meaning, Mahari Weil's statement that one must raise the cup a tefach even when alone is no proof that he agrees with Bach that an individual must hold it in his hand throughout. Mahari Weil may mean only that he should raise it initially, when taking it before Bircas HaMazon, but during Bircas HaMazon he should not hold it in his hand; rather, he should place it on the table.
