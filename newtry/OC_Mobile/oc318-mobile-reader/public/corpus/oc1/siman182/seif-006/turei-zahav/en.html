For each first portion becomes nullified. It appears that this means when he pours in a thin stream, analogous to yayin nesech in Yoreh Deah, siman 134. It is difficult, however, for he afterward writes that the cup can be repaired with a little wine or water. Why should each first portion not become nullified as it enters, leaving no repair? It appears to me that since there is a dispute in Yoreh Deah whether we say that each first portion becomes nullified, and here the requirement that the cup not be pagum is only an initial preference, for in pressing circumstances a pagum cup is valid, we therefore adopt the lenient position in every respect.
