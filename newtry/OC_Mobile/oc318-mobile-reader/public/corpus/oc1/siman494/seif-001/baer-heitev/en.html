(א) Shavuot. See Magen Avraham in what he wrote that according to all opinions the Torah was given on the 51st day of counting — why did the Holy One, blessed be He, establish the festival of Shavuot for generations on the 50th day of counting before and after noon. And Maharsha in ch. 1 ch. 1 of Avodah Zarah in the sugya "Heaven and earth testify regarding their testimony" etc. also raised this question and answered there that the Holy One, blessed be He, intended thereby to teach that fear of sin precedes wisdom in rank and in time before noon. And according to this I said that this is the intention of Rav Yosef, that on Yom Tov of Shavuot they made him a three-year-old calf; he said: if not for this day that causes it, how many Josephs are in the market — end of his words; see Pesachim 68a. It is fitting to examine it well. First, what is this language that he says "if not for this day that causes" — it implies the day causes it; he should have said "if not for the giving of the Torah." And further, if not for the giving of the Torah the world would return to void and formlessness, as we say in the baraita: the Holy One, blessed be He, with the act of Creation — if Israel accept the Torah, good; if not, I will return them to void and formlessness. And further, after Rav Yosef was the most humble of any person as we say at the end of Sotah — when Rabbi died humility ceased, Rav Yosef said to him: do not teach humility for I am here before noon — and here he boasts that because of the giving of the Torah he is superior to other people; this is not within the bounds of humility. And according to what I wrote, his intention was thus in what he said "if not for this day" etc., for it is known what Chazal said: humility brings to fear of sin. If so, Rav Yosef was on a level in that he was humble, he was also on a level that his fear of sin preceded his wisdom. And this is what he said on Yom Tov of Shavuot — meaning on the 50th day they made him a three-year-old calf and he said: if not for this day that causes — meaning if the day of Shavuot were not on this day but on the 51st day of counting, it would not be proven that fear of sin precedes wisdom in rank and in time, and my superiority would be nothing, and how many Josephs are in the market; but now that the day of Shavuot is on the 50th day and the giving of the Torah was on the 51st day, the question is difficult and must be answered as Maharsha wrote, and understand well. And the later authorities wrote that they delay Ma'ariv at the entry of Shavuot so that the days of counting shall be complete.
