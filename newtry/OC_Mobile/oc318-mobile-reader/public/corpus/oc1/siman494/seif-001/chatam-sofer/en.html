On the Taz, s.k. 1, regarding the Torah reading. Note in the margin: in the sefer Eliyah Rabbah and Chok Yaakov they cite the responsa Shaarei Ephraim, siman 10, to uphold the custom, and so too the view of the Magen Avraham above at the beginning of siman 146 appears; see there, and it is easy. And where the custom is practiced, practice it, and it is lengthy; but l'chatchilah one should not practice so, and so wrote in the Siddur of Moreinu HaRav Yaavetz, may the memory of the righteous be for a blessing, for anyone who studies Shaarei Ephraim at the end of the responsum will see that all his proofs are thin and weak, and also that he only answered why one does not say it between the blessing and the reading, or after two or three verses, and specifically between the first and the second; but the more proper way is to say it before the blessing at all.
