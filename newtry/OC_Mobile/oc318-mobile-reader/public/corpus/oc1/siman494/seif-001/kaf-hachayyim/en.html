1) [Se'if 1] On the fiftieth day of the counting of the omer, etc. The beginning of Maariv at the onset of Shavuos is delayed so that the days of the counting will be complete. Taz, at the beginning of the siman; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1. Some later authorities write likewise. Shelah, page 179b, however, writes that one need be careful only regarding Kiddush. Reciting the prayer while it is still daytime is of no consequence, for Rabbi Oshaya recited the Motzaei Shabbos prayer on Shabbos even though it was still fully Shabbos. See there. Therefore, one who cannot delay the prayer should nevertheless not recite Kiddush until nightfall, as written in the following subsection.
