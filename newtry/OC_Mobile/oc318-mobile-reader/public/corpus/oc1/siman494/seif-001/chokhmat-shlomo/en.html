Seif 1. “The fiftieth day of the counting of the Omer is the festival of Shavuos,” etc. Note: See Magen Avraham, seif katan 1, who asks why we say on the sixth of Sivan, “the time of the giving of our Torah.” See my resolution in my work Maaseh Yedei Yotzer on the Pesach Haggadah, section 8, page 76, on the passage “Blessed is He who keeps His promise to Israel,” where I explained the matter correctly, with Hashem’s help. See also my work Kehillas Yaakov, in the discourse for Shavuos of the year 5611, where I resolved Magen Avraham’s question. I wrote another resolution to that question, and also explained why the Torah itself does not state the date of the giving of the Torah although we say it in prayer. See my work on Megillas Rus and Shavuos from the year 5618. Examine carefully.
