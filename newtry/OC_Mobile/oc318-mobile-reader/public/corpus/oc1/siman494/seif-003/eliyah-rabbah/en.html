[3] [Levush] And they make Kiddush, etc. In the house they wait to make Kiddush until nightfall, for we require seven complete weeks (Mas'at Binyamin and Taz); and the Taz wrote that they delay beginning Ma'ariv, etc., and this requires further study. He wrote in the Shulchan Aruch of Moreinu HaRav Rabbi Yitzchak Luria: one must immerse on Erev Shavuot; and know that anyone who on that night did not sleep at all whatsoever and was occupied in Torah is assured that he will complete his year and that no harm will befall him, until here; and the reason the Magen Avraham wrote according to its plain sense is that Yisrael were sleeping all night and the Holy One, blessed be He, had to awaken them, therefore we must correct this; and the order of study, see there in the Shenei Luchos HaBeris, folio 160; and all of this is only from traits of abstinence and caution. The law of Birkas HaTorah was explained in siman 47. The Rokeach wrote that they seat the children to learn on Shavuot, and he discussed at length what they do for him; and so too in the Kol Bo, siman 74, he discussed at length, only that it implies that they do so on other days as well, and he concludes that on the day a person enters his son into Talmud Torah, it is before Hashem like the day Yisrael stood at Chorev.
