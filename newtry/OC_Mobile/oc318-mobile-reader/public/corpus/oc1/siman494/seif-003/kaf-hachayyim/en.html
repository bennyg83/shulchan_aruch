3) There. On the fiftieth day of the counting of the omer, etc. Rivash, siman 96, writes that Scripture did not make Shavuos dependent upon the day on which the Torah was given, but upon the fiftieth day of the omer. It is not necessary that this be precisely the day on which the Torah was given, neither earlier nor later. Now, however, according to our calculation, in which Nissan is full and Iyar is deficient, it will always occur in this manner, because the fifty days conclude on the sixth of Sivan. For this reason, we refer in the prayer to the season of the giving of our Torah. See there. Pri Chadash; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 3; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.
