Se'if 3: It is forbidden to fast on the night after the festival of Shavuot. And in the Agudah, chapter 1 of Chagigah: that for all thirty [days] eulogy and fasting are forbidden. And see in the Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 7 of Hilchos Klei HaMikdash, halacha 9.
Magen Avraham, s.k. 3: that women are permitted in melacha on Isru Chag. See responsa Maharchash, siman 8.
