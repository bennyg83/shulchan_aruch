On the night after. Because when Atzeres fell on Shabbos, the day of slaughtering the offerings was after Shabbos. And women are permitted in melacha on Isru Chag. Sheyarei Kenesses HaGedolah; Magen Avraham; and see Yad Aharon.
Tachanun. And they also do not fast. Later authorities.
Grasses. And if it falls on Sunday, they spread them at the entry of Shabbos (Maharil). But the custom is that they spread them on Yom Tov; see Magen Avraham; and they also practice to set up trees in the synagogue as a memorial that on Atzeres they are judged regarding the fruits of the tree, and they should pray for them. And the later authorities wrote in the name of the Shenei Luchos HaBeris: regarding what they practice to distribute fragrant grasses in the synagogue, they should not distribute until after the Shemoneh Esreh prayer, so that one can recite a blessing, for it is forbidden to interrupt from Baruch She'amar until after Shemoneh Esreh, end quote; and the Chok Yaakov wrote that it is permitted to recite a blessing over fragrance between the chapters; see there. One must immerse on Erev Shavuot. Anyone who did not sleep at all at night and occupies himself in Torah is assured that he will complete his year and that no harm will befall him. And in the morning one should not recite the blessing on washing the hands, nor over a tallis katan. And Birkas HaTorah one must recite; but one who wishes to fulfill his obligation amid doubt should hear Birkas HaTorah from another and intend to fulfill his obligation (Magen Avraham); see there. And see siman 235, that one must read Krias Shema after nightfall when the stars appear, since he does not read it upon his bed. The night of Shavuot is forbidden for marital relations; and if it is the night of her immersion, he should have relations. Sefer HaKavanos.
Milk. There are many reasons; and he wrote the reason in the Zohar, that those seven weeks were for Yisrael seven clean days, like a woman from her niddah, and it is known that blood curdles and becomes milk; and that is from judgment to mercy; and the custom of our fathers is Torah. But one must be careful not to come to a prohibition; and see in Yoreh De'ah, siman 89, that one need not interrupt with Birkas HaMazon if he is not eating hard cheese; and he should be careful to take another tablecloth, end quote of the Magen Avraham. And in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah he wrote: I practice to eat honey and milk and recite Birkas HaMazon and eat meat; and so wrote the Shenei Luchos HaBeris. And see above in siman 505, that milk milked on Yom Tov by a non-Jew should be treated as forbidden, and one should not change; but on the second day of Yom Tov there is room to permit it; and so is the agreement of the Taz and the Magen Avraham there, not like the Maharshal who permitted; and so wrote in responsa Chut HaShani, siman 31, and in Nachalas Shivah, siman 36; and he concludes: nevertheless, if I were in a place where they practice permission, I would not protest against them. Or Zarua wrote in the name of the Kol Bo that on Shavuot some practice to eat cheese at the afternoon meal even though six hours have not passed from the morning meal, provided that he rinses his mouth and cleans between his teeth to remove the meat; and see Chok Yaakov. One should not fast on Shavuot even a fast for a dream (Maharil and the later authorities). And the Magen Avraham wrote: when one bakes bread with butter, he should be careful to heat the oven well, and also the peel needs to be new, not like some women who flip it to the other side; see there. And I heard that they eat dairy and afterward meat, not like what the angels did by Avraham, who ate meat with milk, and for that reason the Torah was given to Yisrael; and examine it carefully.
