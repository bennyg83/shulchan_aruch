(א) And there are those who say he should not mix, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah what is written; and specifically when he put the beverage on Shabbat, etc. — it is from Magen Avraham in the name of Mordechai; and Biur HaGra wrote there is a textual error in Mordechai and it should read "if he put," etc. — meaning specifically if he put erev Shabbat — permitted by hand; but if he did not put beverage erev Shabbat — also by hand forbidden, for all Mordechai's words are according to one who holds putting water is its kneading, see there; and for him certainly even by hand forbidden due to putting water from the beginning; and for this reason Biur HaGra emended Mordechai "if he put the beverage," etc.; nevertheless I did not wish to deviate from Magen Avraham's words that are in Beit Yosef; and Darkei Moshe at length also appears like Magen Avraham; and in truth this difficulty was already raised in Beit Yosef on Mordechai and remains in requires study — and it is a very serious difficulty; and see in Damasek Eliezer, who was greatly troubled even according to Biur HaGra's emendation. And behold on Or Zarua, who also brought this language written in Mordechai — there is no wonder at all, for he himself holds there that putting water is not its kneading — therefore permitted to put beverage on Shabbat; but on Mordechai it is difficult; and perhaps one can say it should read in Mordechai and Or Zarua "if not," etc.; and with this Beit Yosef's difficulty is resolved; but nevertheless Magen Avraham's words are still not well resolved, for certainly according to Or Zarua, even if he put water erev Shabbat — also forbidden on Shabbat to beat with the palm; and that which he stated "if he did not put" — for amplification, that even in this permitted to mix water and wine on Shabbat when he mixes with his finger:
