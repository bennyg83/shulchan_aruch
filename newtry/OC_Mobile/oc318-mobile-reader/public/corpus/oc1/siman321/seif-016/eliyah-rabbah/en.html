(16) If detached from erev Shabbat or crushed, etc. It implies regarding detached they do not require crushing; but in the Gemara beginning of chapter 22 it states detached requires crushing like written siman 320 seif 2 regarding olives and grapes; therefore it appears to me thus is the explanation: when detached — he may remove the honeycombs from the hive in order to eat the honey stuck around them; or to squeeze into food; or if crushed, etc. (Magen Avraham) — and this is strained; but it appears to me in what he challenged — he himself afterward: since in the Gemara it says honey is originally food and ultimately food — if so why should it be inferior to fruits standing for eating that are permitted to squeeze since there is no liquid on them; and he concluded requires study to resolve; and in my humble opinion it is well resolved: the first difficulty resolves its counterpart — in truth if detached from erev Shabbat they do not require crushing and permitted to squeeze in every manner since there is no liquid on it; and that crushing is required for honeycombs — when not detached, for it resembles detaching; but regarding olives and grapes — originally food and now beverage; therefore even when detached forbidden lest he squeeze; and this is clearly correct:
