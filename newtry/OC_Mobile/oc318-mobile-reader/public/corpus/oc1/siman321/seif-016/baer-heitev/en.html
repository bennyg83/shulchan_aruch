(א) By hand. And if he put the beverage erev Shabbat — he mixes it in the palm. Mordechai. Magen Avraham:

(ב) In mustard. And the same applies to roasted flour; see siman 324 seif 3 — Magen Avraham. And it is plain that if he put some beverage into it erev Shabbat such that some kneading is visible in it — permitted to add to it on Shabbat as he wishes. Taz, see there. And Magen Avraham forbids even if he put a little beverage erev Shabbat, for it is not considered as kneaded thereby, see there; and see siman 473 seif 5:

(ג) A change. And specifically in a thin batter; but in a thick one a change does not help — therefore Rama wrote like in a beverage; and he also wrote with his finger — meaning and not in the palm. And all this, even for the need of that meal — forbidden — Magen Avraham, see there. And see Taz, who raised that one who forgot to pour vinegar erev Shabbat into the cream must do on Shabbat a thin batter and put vinegar first and afterward the cream, not in the manner they do on weekdays, and he should not mix with force or hold the vessel itself and shake it; and requires study according to Rama's view, who requires a change in food in what they practice to do on Shabbat — very fine radish with cucumbers that they call ogorkes very fine and afterward pour vinegar into it, and they make no change at all; and it appears here they were not stringent like regarding cream, since it is impossible to do this erev Shabbat, for the taste of the radish would spoil — therefore they did not require a change, since the main making is on Shabbat that they abandoned the main halacha; and if so, he must at least not mix it with force or by means of the vessel, as is written; and preferable that he put vinegar first in the vessel, as appears correct to me in these matters. And Maharach z"l brought in the name of Rashal that he heard to be lenient regarding mustard and cream that we make, for in their time they made it like dough, unlike in our country where its soaking is not its kneading; and Maharach wrote on this that one should not be lenient from estimation of the mind; and regarding the vegetable called salad one need not be precise in all this, since it does not enter this category, since they do not cut it very fine, as appears correct to me in these matters, end of Taz; and see s.k. 15 what is written there and s.k. 20 what is written there:
