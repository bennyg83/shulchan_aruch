(s.k. 16) That were detached, etc. — it implies when detached there is no crushing; meaning no crushing erev Shabbat; for at first glance it appears "detached and crushed" are one law since he grouped them together; therefore one must say the explanation is, etc.; and the law of detached and crushed is not the same: when crushed — permitted the honey that came out by itself; but to squeeze is certainly forbidden except into food; and since they were not detached and forbidden to take the loaves — impossible to squeeze into food; and when detached but not crushed — where there is no skimming prohibition, permitted to squeeze into food as above siman 322 seif 4; but honey that comes out by itself is forbidden as above beginning of siman 322, and permitted only honey stuck on the loaves. And see Tosafos daf 43, etc.; he brings proof that even when not crushed — honey that comes out by itself is forbidden; nevertheless honey stuck on the loaves is permitted, for there the Gemara challenges R' Yosi who holds it is only for the need of something detachable: from that which is taught one spreads a mat over a beehive on Shabbat, whether in summer or winter; and behold the bees are not detachable; and it answers there is honey in it; and in rainy season there are also two loaves placed in the hive so the bees are sustained from them in winter; and it challenges: those two loaves are muktzeh, for he placed them for the bees' need; and it answers it deals where he thought about them erev Shabbat to eat them — not muktzeh; and Tosafos challenged: what of it — behold forbidden to skim on Shabbat; and it answered it deals where they are already skimmed; or where it is customary that there is honey floating on the loaves and no skimming is needed, end quote; behold the second explanation deals where they are not skimmed, and nevertheless floating honey on them is permitted; and we say in the Gemara daf 19b and daf 145b: it wants to compare — just as honeycombs that one crushed and honey came out — permitted; so olives and grapes that one crushed erev Shabbat — likewise the liquids that come from them are permitted; and it rejects: granted honey — before it comes out it is food and when it comes out there is still food upon them and not the name of beverage; and Rashi explained: why need "lest he squeeze" — there is no squeezing in it; but olives and grapes — before it comes out it has the status of food and after it comes out the status of beverage, and squeezing applies to them.
