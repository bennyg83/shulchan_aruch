Garlic, etc., and he should not beat it. In the Rambam it says: garlic that he crushed, etc., and he should not grind it. The Maggid Mishneh in chapter 3 wrote that this refers either to something that still lacks pounding, or that grinding it is forbidden because of the mixture of the groats; see seif 19 and siman 252.
And some say that he may not, etc. If he put in the liquid from before Shabbos, he may mix it with a spoon. So wrote the Mordechai, and so it appears in the Or Zarua. It is possible that the Terumas HaDeshen also agrees to this; see there.
With mustard. The same applies to roasted flour; see siman 324, seif 3.
It is forbidden to put liquid. The Rashal wrote: in our countries kneading does not apply to garlic and mustard, but only in foreign lands, where they make it thick like dough. So too they make horseradish, called chrein, by pressing it together like a ball; therefore, when they soak it, that is its kneading. But it does not appear to me at all to be lenient, as the Bach writes. The Terumas HaDeshen also wrote that the same applies to salatin and other dips, that it is forbidden. Even if he put in a little liquid before Shabbos, that accomplishes nothing, for by doing so it is not considered kneaded. So wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham. See what I wrote in siman 473, seif 5.
For it is a change, like with drinking mixtures. This applies specifically to a thin mixture, but with a thick mixture a change does not help, as the Terumas HaDeshen and Bach write. Therefore the Rama wrote, 'like with a drinking mixture,' and he also wrote, 'with his finger,' meaning not with a spoon. The Olas Shabbos was not precise in the Rama's wording. All of this is forbidden even for the need of that same meal. It is forbidden to beat soft-boiled eggs in a bowl, for it looks like one who wants to put them into a pot, as Rashi writes on Shabbos 140a. In Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 22, he wrote that one may not beat wine vinegar on Shabbos, etc. This implies that it is forbidden to beat anything, as written in seif 17; see seif 19.
