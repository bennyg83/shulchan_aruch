(ב) And some say not, etc. And if he gave the liquid erev Shabbat — he mixes it with a spoon, so Mordechai; and so in Avodah Zarah; and possible Terumat HaDeshen also concedes to this, see there:

(ג) In mustard. And the same with roasted flour; see siman 324 seif 3:

(ד) Forbidden to give liquid. And Rashal wrote: in our country kneading does not apply to garlic and mustard, only in a foreign land where they make it thick like dough; and likewise they make tamcha, called krein, that they stick it like a ball; therefore when they soak it — that is its kneading, end quote; and it appears to me one should generally be lenient (Bach); and also Terumat HaDeshen wrote the same regarding lettuce and other types of dipping — forbidden; and even if he gave a little liquid erev Shabbat — not considered as kneading through this; and so Ri'aw; and see siman 473 seif 5:

(ה) That it is a change like for drinking. And specifically thin; but thick — no change (Terumat HaDeshen and Bach); and therefore Rama wrote "like for drinking," and also wrote "with his finger," meaning and not in the palm; and Beit Yosef was not precise in Rama's language; and all this is forbidden even for that meal: forbidden to beat eggs in a bowl, for it appears like one who wants to cook them in a pot (Rashi daf 140a); and in Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 22 he wrote, end quote: and wine ziggera one may not beat on Shabbat, etc.; it implies every thing is forbidden to beat, as siman 19:
