(א) He puts wine, etc. and he should not pound — also meaning with force; and the reason all as above; and likewise regarding garlic; and in Rambam it is written garlic that he crushed, etc. and he should not grind; and it deals with something lacking grinding or who forbids grinding because of mixture of grains — and see seif 19 and what we wrote there:
(ב) Tomorrow he puts beans, etc. — and in Rambam it states he puts into beans and groats on Shabbat. And if they are hot see above in siman 318 seif 10 and 19 and in Mishna Berurah there:
(ג) And some say, etc. — it refers also to seif 15; and specifically when he puts the liquid on Shabbat; but if he put the liquid from erev Shabbat — permitted to mix in the palm little by little even according to this view:
(ד) Only by hand — or he shakes the vessel itself [R' Avraham]:
(ה) And some say that which is permitted, etc. — it refers also to eggs and garlic and roasted flour mentioned above in seif 14; and the reason they hold putting liquid itself is included in kneading; and therefore specifically when he puts it from erev, and because through this it is already as kneaded — therefore permitted to knead on Shabbat and also to add more liquid on Shabbat and mix; but he should do with a change, meaning in roasted flour he kneads little by little, and in these things he should not pound with force or mix by hand per the gloss view, as above:
(ו) Specifically that he put it — and if he put some liquid from erev Shabbat — permitted to add on Shabbat according to all; but in a few drops — no, for it is not considered kneaded thereby:
(ז) On account of kneading — and specifically in a thick batter; but in a thin batter kneading does not apply here; nevertheless he must make the change explained in the gloss, meaning he puts the food first and afterward the liquid; and so Acharonim wrote the gloss deals specifically with a thin batter; and for this he concluded "like in a beverage" where they permitted specifically blessing, as above; and in all this there is no distinction between that meal or another meal [Magen Avraham]:
(ח) And so they practice, etc. — meaning in mustard and garlic; and the same in other types of seasoning when they make them fine and mix them together:
(ט) And a place whose manner, etc. — and therefore if he forgot to pour vinegar on erev Shabbat into the greens — he must do on Shabbat in a thin batter and put the vinegar and afterward the greens not as they do on a weekday, and hold the vessel itself and shake it; and in the vegetable called salad one need not be particular in all this, since they do not cut it fine; but what they cut radish and cucumbers called cucumbers fine and pour vinegar or another wet thing into them and mix them together — they must be careful not to mix in the palm with force, or shake the vessel itself; and good to be stringent also that he put vinegar and afterward the food. And when Pesach falls on Shabbat and he forgot from erev to put liquid into the charoset — per the first view permitted to put liquid on Shabbat and he should not pound in the palm but with a finger; and per the second view also this is forbidden unless he also makes it thin.
