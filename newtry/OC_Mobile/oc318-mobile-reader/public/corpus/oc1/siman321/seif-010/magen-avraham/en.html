Which is called a grater. In German, a grating iron. Since the utensil is designated for this purpose, it is like grinding spices in a mortar, which is forbidden, as the Rivash writes. If so, it is forbidden even in order to eat immediately. It appears to me that the same applies to a very small utensil made in this fashion, for in any case it is a utensil. But it is permitted to cut it very finely with a knife, as written in seif 9. It appears to me that even if it is very hard, it is permitted, since if he wished he could chew it with difficulty. Likewise, it is permitted to cut it with an axe or a saw, even though it is somewhat hard, as stated in the Mishnah in chapter 17. Something that one does not wish to eat may not be chewed with one's teeth, because of grinding; so it appears from Rashi on Shabbos 133. See siman 331, seif 7.
