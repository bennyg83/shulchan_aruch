That they call "raleiv." In German "reib eisen," for since the vessel is designated for that — it is like grinding spices in a mortar, which is forbidden [Rivash]; and if so, even to eat immediately — forbidden; and it appears the same in a very small vessel made thus — forbidden, for in any case it is a vessel; but permitted to cut with a thin thin knife as siman 9; it appears even if it is very hard — permitted, since if he wants he chews it with difficulty; and likewise permitted to cut with an axe or a plane even though it is somewhat hard, as is stated in ch. 17 of the Mishnah.
