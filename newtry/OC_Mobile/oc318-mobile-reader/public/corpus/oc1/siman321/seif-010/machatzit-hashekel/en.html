(א) (s.k. 10) Permitted, etc.; for behold Tosafos and Rosh, etc., on daf 74b R' Pappa said: one who slices (meaning cuts fine fine) celery is liable on account of grinding; and there are divided opinions: Rosh wrote in name of R' Chananel that the explanation of "slice" is not cutting fine fine but that in palm trees there are fibers, and when one separates the fibers and beats them a flour-like substance comes out — in this one is liable specifically on account of grinding; and Tosafos wrote specifically one who cuts celery fine fine is liable; and some hold that in every vegetable one is liable on account of grinding; however he holds specifically plants of the ground; but meat, which is not a plant — permitted; and so we rule. But raw meat, since it is not fit for eating thus but only to tear it, not in the usual manner — as stated. And meaning in such a case one can say: those poskim who hold there is no grinding except in plants nevertheless agree that in such a case where it is not fit for eating except through grinding — there is grinding even if it is not a plant; so there in Terumat HaDeshen:

(ב) And further one may touch it, etc.; for on Shabbat daf 155 there is a dispute: Rav Huna holds excessive trouble with animal food — meaning that without that trouble it would be fit for animal food but through that trouble it desires food more — permitted to trouble; but making food — without that trouble it would not be fit at all for animal food, and through the trouble he makes it food — forbidden; and Rav Yehuda holds the opposite: making food — permitted since their sustenance is upon him and impossible to eat otherwise; but excessive trouble with food — forbidden since possible to eat without it; and we rule like R' Yehuda later siman 324. And the author of Terumat HaDeshen did not decide the dispute and brought two views; and his intent is that it depends on that dispute; and if so per the one who holds making food — here too one should forbid. And in Shibolei HaLeket he wrote, etc., as Tosafos daf 73b s.v. milking wrote, end quote: Rashi explained milking is a toldah of threshing; and R' Tam challenged, for on daf 95 we say there is no threshing except in plants, etc.; but in milking one is liable, for an animal is considered a plant as is proven ch. 2 of Eruvin, etc., end quote:
