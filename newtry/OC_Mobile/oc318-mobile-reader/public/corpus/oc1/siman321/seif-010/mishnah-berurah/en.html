(36) That they call a grater — in German "rib eizen"; since the vessel is designated for this, it is a weekday manner and similar to grinding spices in a mortar; and the same in another vessel designated for this; and if so, even to eat immediately forbidden; but permitted to cut it fine with a knife, like above in seif 9 regarding meat, for this too is not something that grows from the ground; and even if very hard permitted, since they can chew it with difficulty and place food on it; and likewise permitted to cut it with an axe or saw, for even though they are vessels whose craft is for prohibition, permitted to move them for their own need as was explained in siman 308 [Ma'alot Shabbos and Magen Avraham].
