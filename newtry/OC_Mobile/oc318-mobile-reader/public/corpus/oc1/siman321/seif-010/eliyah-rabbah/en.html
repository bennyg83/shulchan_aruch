[14] With a grater, etc. Meaning the implement called a grater. Since it is designated for that purpose, it is like grinding spices in a mortar (Rivash, siman 147). Olat Shabbat asks that cheese, like meat, is not a growth from the ground. There is no difficulty, because it is prohibited due to resemblance to a mortar. In truth, cheese may be cut finely with a knife, and Magen Avraham permits this even when it is hard. Likewise it may be cut with an axe or saw. Something one does not wish to eat may not be chewed with the teeth because of grinding (Magen Avraham). See siman 331, seif 7.
