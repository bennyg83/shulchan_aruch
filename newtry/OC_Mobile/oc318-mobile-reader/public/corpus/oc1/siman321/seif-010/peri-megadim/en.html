(א) He turns food (sign 5): in siman 324 sign 7 letter 4 (scribal error sign 9) and Magen Avraham there sign "and here" 10 — it appears the essence of the reason in "he turns food" now is food and not before — is grinding thus; and not from the reason of equating food alone; and cooked meat fit for most of the world. And see there sign 13 where through duress permitted and where impossible entirely forbidden because he turns food; and in Magen Avraham there sign 4: even if not eaten at all permitted to equate food; and here because of tochen; and Eliyah Rabbah 13; and if not so in letter 10 it will be explained at length:

(ב) If (sign 5) said the lay writer: I have here thoughts of matters; and I will set them forth there's help. Behold in Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishnah chapter 21 law 18 he brought Rashba's responsum: everything immediately even in a vessel — this is not the way of grinding; and likewise for chickens to crumble bread to eat immediately (even if there is grinder after grinder) permitted like borer; and borer in a vessel forbidden there because its way is thus; and he learned from there even by hand after time liable for chatas immediately — likewise here immediately in a vessel, for there is no way of grinding immediately — from reasoning; and therefore Rambam: in order to cook after time even that same day; and (sign 5) in siman 324 and Kesef Mishnah it appears the version in chapter 8 law 15 "to cook" and the Mechaber in seif 12 omitted "in order to cook" and Mishna Berurah emended. Behold salt and spices to crush in a stone and wood mortar to eat immediately forbidden because of weekday labor; however chatas liability or to cut with a knife in salt and pepper — one may say since we permitted immediately — meaning food matter; behold whatever is not food except to sweeten food like pepper — one may say liable for tochen even to put immediately in stew; also for birds permitted immediately as Magen Avraham 15 in the name of Ran — and this is from Rashba's responsum: if specifically a thing fit for a person; but a thing not fit for a person — liable for tochen in any case rabbinically forbidden; and this is what Rambam wrote chapter 21 law 18: chaff and carob that appear like grinding; however in siman 324 sign 8 the reason in spreading — there not fit permitted — meaning to eat immediately or large pieces; and now what the Mechaber wrote in seif 8 pepper and salt and implies with a knife — forbidden to put coarse salt — only ours permitted (Magen Avraham sign 9): there is doubt if to cut with a knife to put in stew immediately if permitted because it is food or not considered food; and likewise what he wrote in seif 9 raw meat for birds forbidden if not after time — behold immediately permitted, for there is no grinding immediately; or one may say whatever a person does not eat — forbidden for birds immediately; and siman 324 sign 8 deals with large pieces; and what Mishna Berurah wrote in seif 12 to crumble bread — there is no grinding even after time:

(ג) See Mishnah Lechem part 1 Choshen Mishpat law 3: the emendation that one who grinds matzah to return to fine flour — there is grinder after grinder; and he brings Tosafos Shabbos 114b s.v. ella; and one may say there regarding vegetables they distinguish between large and small pieces; unlike grinder after grinder — one may say even crumbling fine to flour there is no grinding (Mishna Berurah) and rabbinically forbidden on a grater like weekday labor (Magen Avraham 12); and if not so there it will be explained:

(ד) And know that tochen applies only in something that grows from the ground and was in the toldah itself and he crumbles it. For behold in Sefer HaTerumah siman 56 he wrote there is no winnowing, etc. — see there; and see chapter 9 law 5 in Mishnah Berurah and so there; and what Magen Avraham 15 wrote: nevertheless rabbinically there is winnowing and threshing even though not something that grows from the ground; and borer appears in siman 319 sign 2 — two types of fish, species from species (Mishna Berurah) — see there in siman 57. And in the Mishkan one may not derive one from its fellow — only winnowing and threshing in something that grows from the ground and not the others (borer — jugs that have wine and have beer and the like — it appears it is full borer); and if so to crumble live fish for birds forbidden at least rabbinically; and in Magen Avraham 10 if not so it will be explained; and one who grinds a brick is not liable for tochen because it is burned in a kiln and is not in the toldah; but one who grinds a clod of earth is tochen, for in the Mishkan spices also were thus — some of them are like stones in the ground; and what Tosafos wrote that spreading is forbidden in dust there — "your threshing and your harvest" is written — we require specifically something that grows from the ground; unlike tochen whose essence of grinding applies in such things.
