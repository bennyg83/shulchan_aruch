With a grater. In a foreign language they call it reib eizen; since the vessel is designated for this — it is like grinding spices in a mortar, which is forbidden — Rivash. And if so, even to eat immediately — forbidden. And it appears to me the same applies to a very small vessel made for this — forbidden, for in any case it is a vessel; but permitted to cut it with a fine knife, as is written in seif 9. And it appears to me that even if it is very hard — permitted, since if he wishes he can chew it with difficulty; and the same applies that permitted to cut it with an axe or saw, as is stated in Mishnah chapter 17.
