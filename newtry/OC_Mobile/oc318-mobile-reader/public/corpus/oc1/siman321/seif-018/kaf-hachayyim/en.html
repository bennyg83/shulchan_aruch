18) There: Four or five together, etc. This is Tur's wording. The statement in the following seif that some write that one may dip several pieces is from Terumas HaDeshen, cited in Beis Yosef. Beis Yosef implies that Terumas HaDeshen does not disagree with Tur but explains him. Why, then, does Shulchan Aruch introduce Terumas HaDeshen's ruling as 'some write'? Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, paragraph 1, explains that the author was uncertain how to understand Tur. Tur may mean, as Terumas HaDeshen explains, that only salting them together is forbidden, while salting each piece separately is permitted even for four or five pieces. Alternatively, Tur may hold that once several pieces are salted, it is forbidden even when each was salted separately. The author therefore cites Tur's exact words, which can bear both interpretations, and afterward introduces Terumas HaDeshen's interpretation as 'some write' to indicate that Terumas HaDeshen chose this explanation; see there. It appears to me that the Shulchan Aruch says 'some write' because Terumas HaDeshen himself presents the ruling tentatively, as one can see from his words in Beis Yosef. Examine carefully.
