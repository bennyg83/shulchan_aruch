And what is written in seif 6 "there is one who wrote" that permitted to dip several pieces, etc. — it is from Sefer HaTerumah that Beit Yosef brought; and although it appears from Beit Yosef that Sefer HaTerumah does not dispute Tur but explains his words — if so why did Shulchan Aruch write Sefer HaTerumah's words in the name of "there is one who wrote." Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef or 1 wrote: it appears our teacher the Mechaber was uncertain in understanding Tur's intent — what his intent is; possibly Tur's intent is like Sefer HaTerumah said: only together forbidden, but each one alone permitted even 4 or 5 pieces; or possibly his view is that whatever was salted once — even if each one was salted alone — forbidden; and therefore he brought Tur's words as written and in their language to say his words bear both explanations; and afterward he wrote Sefer HaTerumah's reasoning in the name of "there is one who wrote" to say Sefer HaTerumah agreed to explain thus — see there.
