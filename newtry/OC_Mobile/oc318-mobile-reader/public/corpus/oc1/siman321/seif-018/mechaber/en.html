One does not soak chalaitit neither in lukewarm nor in cold water, for its manner is to soak it for medicine; but he puts it in vinegar and dips his bread in it; if it was soaked from yesterday — permitted to drink it on Shabbos; and if he drank from it on the fifth and sixth days and needs to drink it also on Shabbos — permitted, for such is its manner of healing to drink it seven days one after another; therefore permitted to soak it in cold and place it in the sun because it is dangerous if he does not drink from it:
