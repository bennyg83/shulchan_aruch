(א) Seif 67 "permitted." As Tosafos, Ri'f, and Semak explain — it refers to Shabbos; on Yom Tov it is permitted in its usual manner, and as stated in the Tosefta "pepper as a spice," etc.; and therefore Rashbag and the Sages in chapter 2 of Yom Tov 19 explain only in their mill because of weekday work; and furthermore, behold even salt is crushed in a wooden mortar — all the more pepper that spoils; and he did not know, etc.; and what they strained regarding R' Chananel and Sha'alotos for the reading "what shall he thresh" — how difficult to explain to permit threshing on Shabbos; and it is not so, for thus it is also taught regarding salt in the Tosefta, and as stated in seif 8; and see Rashba:

(ב) "And in a bowl." See Taz. And Rambam learned from what is stated at the end of chapter 2 of Yom Tov 19 "one does not grind," etc., and Tosafos explain there 14 and Shabbos 141a — because of weekday work; and Aruch in Shabbos there s.v. "for the gemara answers" — and even though with salt he did not require a bowl, pepper is more severe on Shabbos because of R' Yosi who said one by one specifically, unlike with salt; and on Shabbos it is also crushing — weekday work:
