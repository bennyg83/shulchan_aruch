Mishnah Lechem (sign 1): likewise he was challenged — why do I need a verse to offer in salt on Shabbat, behold there is no me'abed in foods; and one may say practical difference: to disqualify it so it not descend — does not need plain meaning and there is me'abed. In my humble opinion on Shabbat one may say we do not offer to disqualify to profane Shabbat and wait until after Shabbat; also Alexandrian salt has cooking in it and our salt has no cooking as in siman 318. And at present milk — there is me'abed in siman 305 or 19; and if not so it will be explained there; and here not to elaborate:
