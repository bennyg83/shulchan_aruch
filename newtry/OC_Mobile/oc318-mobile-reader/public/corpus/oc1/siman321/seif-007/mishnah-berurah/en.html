(א) Permitted, etc. — and if he grinds pepper and other spices in a mortar as is his manner, even one grain — there is d'oraisa prohibition on account of grinding:

(ב) Even many together — and specifically what he needs for that Shabbat:

(ג) And in a bowl — meaning but he should not grind in a knife handle in a mortar; even though he changed in what he grinds in a knife handle, nevertheless in a mortar it appears like a weekday act; and likewise grinding a knife handle on a table or on the bottom of a vessel on a table — for this is two changes; but in a pestle designated for pounding, even on a table or inside the bowl — forbidden. Taz wrote in this siman one should not put pepper inside a garment and grind with a knife on it, for he creates a scent; however in siman 471 letter 8 Taz implies he retracted this and agreed to Maharshal that it is permitted because he does not intend for this — see there.
