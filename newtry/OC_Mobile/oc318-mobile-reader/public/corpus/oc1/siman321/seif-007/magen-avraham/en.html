And in a bowl. Meaning, or in a bowl, by crushing them against the bottom of the bowl, for that is a complete change. This appears to be the Rambam's explanation. One should not say that we require two changes, for that is difficult, since in the Gemara only one change is mentioned, namely with a knife. So too it appears from the Tosefta, as brought by the Beit Yosef in the name of the Agur. It implies that cutting it with a knife is forbidden, and the same applies to all spices; see seif 12.
