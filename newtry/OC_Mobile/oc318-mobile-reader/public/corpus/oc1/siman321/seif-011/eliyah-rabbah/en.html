(11) Permitted, etc. Rambam's language chapter 21: one who needs to crush pepper and the like to put into food on Shabbat — crushes with the knife handle and in a bowl; but not in a mortar — until here; and Magen Avraham explained: or in a bowl — they crush on the rim of the bowl; and from Rambam's implication it does not appear thus, rather two things are required: crushing with the knife handle and within the bowl; and so Beit Yosef explained; and in the Gemara that mentions with the knife handle alone — one must say that stam he is not crushing in a mortar; and so it appears in Semak law of grinding; and in Ran chapter Tolim; and it appears to me proof from what Kol Bo wrote daf 33: coarse salt — permitted to scatter on the table and to press with the knife handle — until here; and it implies specifically on the table; and he concludes there: salt and spices are the same; and so it further appears there — meaning he concludes Rambam: but not in a mortar; and in truth when crushing on the rim of the bowl it is a change like with the knife handle, only crushing in another bowl — unlike in a mortar; and this is Agur's intent — requires study.
