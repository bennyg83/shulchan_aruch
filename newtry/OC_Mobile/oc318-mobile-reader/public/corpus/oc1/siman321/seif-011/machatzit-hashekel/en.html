(א) (s.k. 11) And see siman 324 seif 7; and in what is written there it proves, etc., not like Ohel Yesharim; for behold Ohel Yesharim understood that Rama forbade here to cut meat fine fine because he makes it food, namely he ruled like Rav Huna who forbids making food; and therefore he challenged: behold later siman 324, on the contrary he ruled like R' Yehuda who permits making food and forbids excessive trouble; and Taz challenged similarly in siman 324 and left it in difficulty; and so understood in Sefer Avodah Zarah and Sefer Teshuvah; and Ohel Yesharim explained that what he permitted in siman 324 to make food — that is specifically something fit to eat somewhat through pressure; but here where it is not fit for eating at all without fine cutting — therefore even for us who rule like R' Yehuda it is forbidden; and this Ohel Yesharim rejected — Magen Avraham, on the contrary, proves that whatever is very hard and impossible to eat at all without repair — more permitted for R' Yehuda, since their sustenance is upon it and they cannot eat except through that trouble; therefore Magen Avraham wrote in this s.k. see siman 324 seif 7 and what is written there; meaning through what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 324 the difficulty of Ohel Yesharim and Taz above is settled; for behold in Terumat HaDeshen he gave two reasons for prohibition: one, since it is not fit for eating without this — in such a case all agree there is grinding concern even for those who hold grinding applies only to plants — and for this reason there is Torah prohibition; he added a second reason on account of making food — and for this reason there is only rabbinic prohibition; and why did Rama omit reason one that has Torah grinding prohibition and mention reason two that has only rabbinic prohibition:

(ב) Also from Terumat HaDeshen's words it appears the second reason was only ancillary and Rama omitted the main reason; therefore one can say that in truth such is Rama's intent in what he wrote "since," etc., he makes it food — meaning through this there is grinding concern like the first reason Terumat HaDeshen wrote; but if there were no grinding prohibition, on account of making food it would be permitted for us; and with this Ohel Yesharim's difficulty is answered — that which in siman 324 deals with cutting fine fine and there is no grinding concern but only making food — that is permitted for us who rule like R' Yehuda; and so explicitly from Magen Avraham's words in siman 324 s.k. 5, that he understood Rama's reason here is grinding, for he wrote, end quote: fine fine, etc., for it is a matter of melacha such as grinding, etc.:
