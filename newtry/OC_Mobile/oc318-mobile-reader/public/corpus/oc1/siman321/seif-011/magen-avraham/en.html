Permitted to give water. Since these vegetables are fit to eat that day — permitted to give them water as it is permitted to move them; unlike something not fit to eat; and see end of siman 324; and one might say that in the Gemara daf 141b it states: a knife — and they wash the dead; and it challenges: but behold it is forbidden to wash the ground [and Rashi explains: apparently something forbidden to move is forbidden to give water]; and it answers there on account of leveling holes; apparently something forbidden to move is permitted to give water and to wash; and one must say this is specifically when it is needed that day, similar to ground and a dead person on account of his honor; but vegetables have no need that day — forbidden:
