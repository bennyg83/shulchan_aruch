It is permitted to water. Since these vegetables are fit to eat that day, it is permitted to water them just as it is permitted to move them, unlike something that is not fit to eat; see siman 654. This is surprising, for in the Gemara, Shabbos 151b, it says that one may anoint and rinse a corpse, and it asks: but is it not forbidden to rinse the ground? Rashi explains that since it is forbidden to move, it should be forbidden to anoint it. The Gemara answers that there the concern is making furrows. It follows that something forbidden to move may still be anointed and rinsed. One must say that this applies specifically when it is needed for that day, like the ground and like a corpse, because of its honor. But with these vegetables, since there is no need for that day, it is forbidden.
