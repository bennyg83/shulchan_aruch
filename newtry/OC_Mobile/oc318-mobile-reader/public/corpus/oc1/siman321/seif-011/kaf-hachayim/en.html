(k) There. But he can make a little from it, etc. Meaning: for the need of dipping that meal. Ran. It implies for another meal even a little forbidden. Magen Avraham s.k. 4. And likewise Olat Shabbat there. Rabbi Zalman there. Ben Ish Chai there. But Eliyah Rabbah or 3 wrote: permitted to make whatever he needs for this Shabbat even if not for this meal; and Ran who mentions "for this meal" — manner of speech he mentions, for since he only does for that meal he does a little; and he wrote that so is everyone in Rambam chapter 22 and Rabbeinu Yeruham daf 87; and so explained in Shibbolei HaLeket siman 4; and so in Sefer Tanya and not like Olat Shabbat and Magen Avraham — see there. And so Nechmadot Yisrael leans; and likewise Piskei HaDin or 2: whatever is a little does not enter the category of melacha — therefore permitted even for another meal that same day; unlike borer which is in its prohibition even for that same day — see there.
