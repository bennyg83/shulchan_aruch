(14) In a grater, etc. Which they call reib eizen (grater) — since it is designated for this it is like grinding spices in a mortar (Rivash responsum 147); and Olat Shabbat who challenged: behold cheese is not something that grows from the ground like meat — no difficulty, for forbidden because it resembles a mortar; but in truth permitted to cut it very fine with a knife; and even if it is very hard — Magen Avraham permits; and the same law: permitted to cut with an axe or file.
