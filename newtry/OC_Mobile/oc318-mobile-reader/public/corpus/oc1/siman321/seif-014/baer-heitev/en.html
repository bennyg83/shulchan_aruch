(ב) Kneading. And it appears to me forbidden to urinate on mud due to kneading — Magen Avraham; see siman 328 seif 14. And Taz wrote: it appears to me to learn one new law — namely, the flour they make from grinding baked matzah that they call matzah meal, and they are accustomed to mix it with wine or honey water that they call mead — that it is forbidden to make this on Shabbat even in a thin batter, for it appears the law of this flour is like the flour mentioned here — coarse grinding, etc.; and here, where thin batter is permitted, it is through a change that on weekdays the manner is to put vinegar first and on Shabbat he puts the flour first — the change is evident to all; but with matzah flour there is no known manner what one puts first on weekdays in order to know what the change is — therefore forbidden on Shabbat in every manner he wishes to mix in a knife or in the palm the matzah flour with the beverage, end of his words.
