(א) Flour that is not roasted. And in this, even little by little — forbidden:

(ב) And permitted to knead, etc. For he holds that one who gives water to flour is not liable until he kneads; and therefore here, when he changes in kneading — permitted, since it is not fit for kneading:

(ג) For behold it is like sand. Rambam follows his view, for he wrote ch. 8: one who kneads in ashes — this is a toldah of kneading; and there is no kneading in ashes, nor in coarse sand, nor in morayis, nor in the like; and Ra'avad disagrees and wrote it is not so, for they did not say ashes is not fit for kneading but that it is not lacking kneading, and with giving water alone he is liable in any case; and so the later authorities [nevertheless] siman 507 and siman 320 seif 12; and it appears forbidden to urinate on clay on account of kneading; see siman 322 seif 14:
