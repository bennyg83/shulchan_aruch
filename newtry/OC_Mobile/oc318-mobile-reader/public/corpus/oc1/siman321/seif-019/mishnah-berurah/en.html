(א) That he crushed them, etc. — for there are three matters in things that are crushed to extract their oil: crushing, grinding, and pounding; and pounding is more than grinding; therefore he says that if they were crushed well from erev and lack only pounding — it is considered as his melacha was completed from erev and permitted to pound on Shabbat:

(ב) Therefore permitted, etc. — nevertheless one should be careful not to pound with force [Bach and Magen Avraham and R' Avraham, unlike Taz]:

(ג) Groats — a porridge dish; and the reason: since they already ground them in a mill and cooked them fully and lack only a little pounding:

(ד) After they remove it — and while it is still on the fire, some say there is concern of stirring, for it is like cooking; and see above in siman 318 seif 8 and in what we wrote there:

(ה) On account of smoothing — meaning wax, pitch, and the like of things that spread; one who spreads and smooths their surfaces — there is concern of melacha of smoothing, as we wrote above in siman 314:

(ו) Without this — therefore he does nothing; but if impossible to eat without this — forbidden, for there is tanning of foods d'rabbanan as we wrote in seif 5; and likewise there is smoothing d'rabbanan in them; and possible even for that meal forbidden [Peri Megadim]:

(ז) Blessing will come upon him — and to spread cooked apples on bread, and likewise fat and butter — certainly permitted:

(ח) To peel, etc. — on account of borer; and just as in borer we are lenient above when he selects food from refuse and it is adjacent to the meal, so too here:

(ט) Garlic and onions — and likewise apples, nuts, almonds, and the like; and if he opens plums and throws the hard pits to leave for later time — it is borer; and in apples likewise one may say also forbidden; but immediately to the mouth permitted to put; and possible even for that meal permitted [Peri Megadim]:
