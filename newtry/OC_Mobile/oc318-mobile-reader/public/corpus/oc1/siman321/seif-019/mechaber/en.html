Green garlic, melilot, and that he crushed them erev Shabbos lacking pounding — forbidden to finish their pounding on Shabbos; and if they lack only grinding — permitted to finish on Shabbos; therefore permitted to finish pounding licorice powder on a wooden chip in a pot on Shabbos after they remove it from the fire:And permitted to smooth food on Shabbos, and this is not considered smoothing (memachek), since it is possible to eat without this; nevertheless one who is stringent with apple food and the like whose custom is thus — good to practice [Mordechai in chapter Kol] HaGadol].
