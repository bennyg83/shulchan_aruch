(א) (s.k. 19) For behold, etc.; Rambam follows his view — meaning in what he relies upon for the permission that it resembles weekday activity and will not be confused with flour; it appears that on weekdays too it is permitted, namely according to his view that he holds even in something not fit for kneading at all R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda disputes, as above:

(ב) One who kneads in dust (so it should read) — this is a toldah of kneading; and there is no kneading in ashes:

(ג) And Ra'avad disagrees, etc.; for he holds something not fit for kneading at all, like ashes — R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda concedes, as above; siman 507 where they brought proof for Rambam's view from what we say in Beitzah, brought siman 507: katma — permitted; Rashi explained: permitted to put water in ashes and knead and smear the mouth of an oven so heat not escape; and this is like R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda that giving water is not his kneading, and since ashes are not fit for kneading — permitted to knead; and for Ra'avad's view, that in something not fit for kneading R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda concedes — why is katma permitted; and see Tosafos Shabbos daf 18 why they explained this katma differently; and see Magen Avraham siman 506 to reconcile this katma for Ra'avad; and it appears forbidden to urinate water on clay — clay is fit for kneading; and for R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda certainly permitted for all; except he was concerned for the poskim' view like R' Yehuda that even in something fit for kneading, giving water is kneading; Rabbenu Yonah brought their view in seif 16; and therefore the same with ashes — forbidden, for also dust is fit for kneading; and so Sefer Avodah Zarah translated Magen Avraham's language; and it appears forbidden to urinate on clay or dust:

(ד) See siman 322 seif 14 that Maharam permitted urinating on snow even though through the urine the snow melts, since impossible otherwise, as permitted to tread on snow; and Rosh was careful there because of their dispute; and possible here too Rosh concedes, since here there is further leniency to combine the poskim' view like R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda that giving water is not his kneading:
