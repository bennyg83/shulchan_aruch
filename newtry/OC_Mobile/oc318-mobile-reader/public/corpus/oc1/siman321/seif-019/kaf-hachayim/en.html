(א) (s) There. Four or five together, etc. And requires study regarding our custom where they cut radish very fine on Shabbat and put it in a bowl and salt it and pour vinegar on it and eat it — behold this resembles many pieces that are forbidden even if he eats immediately; and the permission is only through dipping each one by itself. And it appears the reason: since they do not leave it at all to sweat but pour vinegar there; and also other types do not resemble me'abed — except I saw some do a vegetable called salad: they salt the vegetable by itself first and soak it thus and strain the water that comes out and afterward mix it with olive oil and vinegar — and this is a complete prohibition and resembles me'abed more, since he waits until the salt is well absorbed. Taz end of s.k. 1. And in Shevut Yaakov chapter 8 Sheratzim he wrote: permitted to make salted vegetables on Shabbat — they put salt on the vegetables and afterward oil and afterward vinegar, and so all similar. And Knesset HaGedolah brought it in Hagahot Tur and Magen Avraham s.k. 6. And one must say it deals when he puts oil and vinegar on the vegetables immediately after putting salt; for if he soaks it in salt until it sweats and afterward puts oil and vinegar — forbidden as above in the name of Taz; and likewise Nechmadot Yisrael or 3, Rabbi Zalman or 4, Turei Even general 32 or 11, Ben Ish Chai chapter 21 or 17. And see below or 67:

(ב) (t) And therefore those who make salad on Shabbat from cucumbers or vegetables and the like — they cut them into small pieces and put salt on them — must immediately when he puts salt on them put vinegar; and also forbidden to squeeze them in salt to soften them; and although above in siman 320 seif 7 he permitted to squeeze cabbage to soften — that is different, for since they were salted from before Shabbat the squeezing does not help them; but here where he now comes to salt them — if he squeezes them in salt the salt acts on them on Shabbat through squeezing and it is like pickling pickled vegetables; rather if he wants to squeeze to soften — he should squeeze without salt until they soften and afterward put salt on them and immediately put vinegar.
