(19) But hard, etc. Shilta Gibborim challenged: why forever forbidden in hard, behold shatit is light from roasted and permitted in hard; and to me it appears that in roasted also hard is forbidden; and refer above regarding this in any case (Dress of Yom Tov); and it is strained to investigate Rambam himself; and Bach explained plainly: Rambam permits roasted a little even thick; also in the Gemara specifically regarding shatit distinguishes between soft and thick, not regarding roasted; and further for other decisors plainly permitted; and it is known that whatever we can minimize in dispute we minimize; and so it appears to me conversely: Rambam permits roasted a little, for it appears to him that even in shatit thick a little is permitted; and that he forbids in hard — is in large quantity; and with this the question of Lechem Mishnah is resolved — his language on Rambam: shatit and roasted are two things; where did the Gemara write regarding apinktin of Levi, etc. — R' Yosei bar Yehuda only deals with roasted and they are a thick mixture of shatit; until here; and according to what I wrote it is resolved: it deals with thick shatit and a little, for it is not inferior to roasted as above:
