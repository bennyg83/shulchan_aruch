The Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Rambam that they stir it greatly, mix it, and beat it with the stirring stick against the sides of the pot or in the bowl, until the meat, groats, and water become mixed and thickened, and they all become one body; sometimes they add hot water to it. It appears to me that the Beit Yosef intentionally omitted these details, because it is forbidden to beat, as written in seif 16. See siman 253, seif 4, and siman 318 at the end of seif 18.
Since it is possible. This implies that if it is impossible to eat it without this, it is forbidden, for there is processing of foods D'Rabbanan, as written in seif 5. Smoothing is like memachek. It is permitted to spread apples back into the empty place, as Hagahos Semak writes, and the same applies on bread.
It is forbidden to peel. This is because of selecting. If so, it is also forbidden to peel apples in order to leave them for later, as written in siman 319, seif 3.
