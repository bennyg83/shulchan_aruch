(א) Permitted to finish. Beit Yosef wrote in name of Rambam: they stir it much and mix it and fix it with a broken piece of wood with the sides of the pot or inside the bowl until the meat, vegetables, and water are mixed and thickened and become one body; and sometimes they add hot water, end quote; and it appears he intentionally omitted Rabbenu Yonah, for it is forbidden to beat, as siman 16; and see siman 453 seif 4 and siman 318 seif 88:

(ב) Since it is possible. If one cannot eat without this — forbidden, for there is food processing rabbinically, as siman 5; and smoothing is like erasing; and permitted to spread apples so they return also to the empty place (Hagahot Semak); and the same on bread:

(ג) Forbidden to peel. On account of borer; and if so, apples too are forbidden to peel to leave, as siman 319 seif 3:
