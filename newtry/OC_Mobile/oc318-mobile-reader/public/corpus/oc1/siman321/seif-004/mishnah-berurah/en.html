(א) One by one alone — for in this it does not appear like pickling pickled vegetables; and what he wrote to place them together before him means that they should be ready before him to eat, but he should beware that they not touch one another:

(ב) Immediately one after another — meaning he should not leave them a long time, even within that meal itself, such as from the beginning of the meal until its end so they sweat much — for this is similar to tanning; but if they stand a short time and sweat a little — there is nothing in this [Taz]; and all this is according to Shulchan Aruch's view; but the practice is not to leave at all in salt but to dip and eat in salt [Acharonim]:
