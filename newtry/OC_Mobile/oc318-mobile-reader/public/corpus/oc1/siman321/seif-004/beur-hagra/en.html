Seif 64 "there is one who [permits]." From that it says one may salt, etc. And Rashi wrote at first "four or five" implies that dipping is permitted even in such a case; and in Hagahos Maimoniot he wrote it is permitted to leave them until a little hour as stated in Yerushalmi chapter 7 of Shabbos — R' Ze'eira said: a radish in salt, provided he does not leave it; and this is what he said "in order to eat them," etc.:
