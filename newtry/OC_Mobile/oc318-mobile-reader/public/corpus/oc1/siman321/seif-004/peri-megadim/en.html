And know that forbidden to put raw vegetable into strong vinegar in the measure that one puts on fire and it boils — for it is like pickling (YD 105); and it appears like cooking on Shabbat for Rambam's reason; and for Tur one may say me'abed applies in salt. And possibly even b'dieved thus forbidden, for it is like a kind of cooking; and for all the more so — requires study. Magen Avraham sign 3:
