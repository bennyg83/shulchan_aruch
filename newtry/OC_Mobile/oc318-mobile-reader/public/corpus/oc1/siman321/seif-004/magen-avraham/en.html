There is one who wrote. And one of the great ones ruled there is prohibition only to give many pieces and scatter salt upon them and throw and shake as they do for fish in their salting, which resembles tanning; but one-by-one — permitted. But in Semag and Semak it implies one may not be lenient except to dip, etc. (Terumat HaDeshen); and Bach wrote that according to Shulchan Aruch seif 3 the reason it appears like pickling kavush — when each piece rests alone there is no kavush concern; but all poskim hold like Rashi that the reason is salt processes them and it is repair; if so forbidden to salt more than two and even with two one should be stringent; and so the world practices not to salt them at all but to dip each one in salt and eat.
