[15] But if it is for immediate eating, etc. Or if chickens will eat it immediately (Ran). See seif 9. Magen Avraham writes that Shiltei Gibborim questions this leniency, and Beit Yosef says one should take care to cut somewhat larger pieces. In our lands the custom is to cut radish extremely fine, but this should not be done until people leave the synagogue because it must be close to the meal, as at the beginning of siman 319. There I explained that one need not wait so long.
