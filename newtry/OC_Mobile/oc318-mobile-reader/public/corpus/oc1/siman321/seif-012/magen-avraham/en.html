(א) That it is forbidden to cut. What is written here in name of the Tosefta — this is not so, for the Tosefta teaches: one does not crush dried figs, etc.; but one crushes with the handle of a knife; and if we say that "crush" means to cut something hard — how is it possible to cut with the handle of a knife; therefore it appears "crush" means to pound with a beam, as siman 322 seif 2 — forbidden except to crush with the handle of a knife; and so the Tosefta teaches at the beginning regarding salt erev Shabbat; and as siman 8; and nevertheless it appears the law is true that it is forbidden to cut a dried fig, since it is a plant; and as siman 9; and it implies that before one who can chew it thus — permitted:

(ב) To eat immediately. Or that the chickens eat immediately — permitted to cut [Ran], for they did not forbid a person to eat his food in large or small pieces; if so this is his manner of eating [Beit Yosef]; and in Shibolei HaLeket he doubts this permission; and also Rabbenu Yonah wrote to be careful to cut somewhat large pieces, see there; but in our country they practice cutting radish very fine fine, and they have whom to rely upon; and in any case forbidden to do so until the departure of the synagogue, for we require adjacent to the meal literally, as is written at the beginning of siman 319; and so Ran ch. 15:
