(ב) To eat it. Or that the chickens eat immediately — permitted to cut, for they did not forbid a person to eat his food in large or small pieces — if so, it is the manner of his eating thus — Ran, Beit Yosef. And from this permission to make mizrei'ah — namely that they cut radish very fine.
