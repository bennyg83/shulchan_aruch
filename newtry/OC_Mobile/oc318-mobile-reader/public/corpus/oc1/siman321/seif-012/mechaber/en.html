One who cuts vegetables fine is liable for grinding.. And similarly forbidden to cut dried figs and carobs before elders [Beit Yosef in the name of Tosefta]; and specifically fruits and the like are forbidden; but permitted to crumble bread before chickens, since it was already ground — no concern, for there is no grinding after grinding [Hagahot Maimoniot chapter 21, Mordechai, and Ran in chapter Kol HaGadol, and Semag]; and all] this speaks only when he cuts and leaves it; but if to eat immediately — everything is permitted, like sorting to eat immediately which is permitted [responsa of Rashba and Ran in chapter Kol HaGadol] as above siman 319:
