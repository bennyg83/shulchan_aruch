That were detached erev Shabbat or were crushed, etc. It implies that when detached one does not require crushing; but in the Gemara ch. 22 it states that crushing is required, as siman 322 seif 2 regarding olives and grapes; and so Rambam ch. 21 seif 16, see there; and therefore it appears the explanation is: when the honeycombs were detached erev Shabbat one may skim the honeycombs from the hive in order to eat the honey stuck on them around, or squeeze it into food; or when they were crushed, etc. — permitted to skim it; meaning: permitted to take the floating honey; but forbidden to take the honeycombs; and see Tosafos daf 43 at the beginning; and we say in the Gemara that honey is initially food and finally food; and requires study: if so why should it be less than fruits standing for eating, that it is permitted to squeeze them since there is no beverage upon them; and one must say: since the wax is not fit for eating — it is like borer, food from refuse, as siman 322 seif 5; but per the words of those who permit there — requires study to reconcile:
