(א) Honey from the hive — meaning even if he does not want to separate honey from the wax — for this is included in extracting; rather he takes the honeycombs themselves from the hive:
(ב) That is similar to detaching — for the place of the honey's growth is in the hive and the Sages considered it as attached:
(ג) If they were detached — meaning the honeycombs were detached from their place of attachment in the hive and lie thus within it and the name of attached no longer applies to them; therefore he may remove them from the hive in order to eat the honey stuck on them around; but nevertheless forbidden to crush them and extract honey from the wax, for this is included in extracting [for it is similar to extracting grain from its husks]; and even if the honey came out by itself — forbidden until evening, a decree lest he crush by hand:
(ד) That were crushed from erev — meaning he crushed the honeycombs and they are still attached in the hive but the honey flowed from them on Shabbat through their crushing — permitted to take this floating honey, for the name of hive no longer applies; but forbidden to remove the honeycombs themselves, since they are still attached in the hive:
