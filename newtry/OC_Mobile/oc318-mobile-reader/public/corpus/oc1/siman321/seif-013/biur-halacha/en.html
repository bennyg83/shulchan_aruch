(ב) That were crushed erev Shabbat — Meaning, since they were already crushed, there is no longer reason to decree regarding liquids that emerge lest he crush; and they did not decree crushed on account of not crushed [Rashi in the Mishnah]; and see above in siman 320 seif 2 in Biur Halacha regarding olives and grapes that were crushed — that according to Tur and his supporters they require specifically that it be crushed well so it lacks beating; and then permitted the liquids that emerge on Shabbat; and it is reasonable the same applies here, even though one could distinguish somewhat because initially it was food and now it is food; see Shabbat 19b.
