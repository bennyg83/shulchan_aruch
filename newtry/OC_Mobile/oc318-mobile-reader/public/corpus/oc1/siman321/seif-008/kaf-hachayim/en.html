One does not make much salt water, etc. It implies specifically to put into pickled vegetables forbidden; but if he wants to make much salt water to eat immediately and to put into the stew — permitted; but from what he wrote at the end "but he can make," etc. — one must infer the opposite: much — even to put into the stew forbidden. And it appears to me Shulchan Aruch mentions manner of speech, for the world's way is to make much for pickled vegetables and a little for the stew; but in truth much is forbidden even if he makes to put into the stew, for Rabbanan do not dispute. See there or 1; and likewise Eliyah Rabbah or 3: when making much — forbidden to put even into the stew and even for this meal, for it appears like doing melacha from the melacha of the stew — end of his words.
