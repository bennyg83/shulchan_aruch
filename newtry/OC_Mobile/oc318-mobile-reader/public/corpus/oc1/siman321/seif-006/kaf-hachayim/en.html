And see above siman 319 seif 7 that Mordechai z"l wrote in the gloss that there are those who are stringent; and there Bach wrote: certainly Maharam's view is also for halacha like those who permit through a change, only he holds it is good to be stringent — and so we rule; and likewise in this siman — see there; and in our words to there. But Tosafos Shabbos or 3 in this siman wrote that Rashba's view is to distinguish between crumbling of grain and hyssop and hyssop of the mortar; and he holds that in hyssop and hyssop of the mortar it is permitted even to crumble the food; and he wrote: and it appears this is Maharam's view, that he ruled stam here like Shulchan Aruch's words — therefore one may be lenient in this; see there.
