6) There: With his fingertips. This is provided that he does not rub a large quantity with his hand as he does during the week. Gemara. Rashi writes that using his entire hand is forbidden. See above, siman 319, seif 7, where Rema writes in the gloss that some are stringent. Bach writes there that Rema certainly rules in accordance with those who permit when it is done unusually, but he holds that it is proper to be stringent. This is our accepted ruling, and he writes the same in this siman; see there and our words there. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 3, however, writes here that Baal HaMaor distinguishes between rubbing grain and rubbing pe'ah and thyme, holding that with pe'ah and thyme one may even crumble the food. He writes that this appears to be Rema's view, since here he states the Shulchan Aruch's ruling without qualification, and therefore one may be lenient; see there. This also appears to be the view of the later authorities, who do not cite here the stringent opinion stated there.
