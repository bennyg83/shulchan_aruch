(א) Forbidden (sign 5) for Rashi and Tur — radish forbidden whenever he salts one piece and leaves it until end of meal; or two immediately — for it resembles me'abed — this in radish where salt labors it; unlike egg where at end of meal salt does not labor it and two together also there is no way of me'abed; but to leave egg and meat for another meal — meaning a very long time — there is some me'abed and forbidden (Magen Avraham sign 7); unlike Rambam whose reason appears like pickling — and egg has no way to pickle — difficult even to leave meat and egg; and the Mechaber gave each side its due — this appears Taz's intent. And requires study whether the Mechaber holds like Tur and Rashi that me'abed rabbinically applies in foods, only salting radish to eat immediately was difficult for him — why forbid; and he does not hold it appears like melacha of me'abed; therefore he explained it appears like pickling — and therefore boiled eggs (and on this some dispute: some say egg forbidden because of me'abed and egg is superior since radish spoils; some say pickling — egg has no way (Magen Avraham sign 5)); unlike leaving for a long time from meal to meal — the Mechaber agrees forbidden because of me'abed (Magen Avraham sign 7):
