Now let us come to Taz's words. He copied Sefer HaTerumah's words at the beginning of the responsum with the question — see there. And there is a missing of two words "and this that Rashi says but," etc. And one who examines there in Sefer HaTerumah — it appears he agrees with Semag and Semak and everyone: one piece through throwing and scattering permitted to leave until end of meal, for they do not labor in one meal (and he wrote on this: do not wonder that one of the great ones explained "I do not salt two" through scattering — behold dipping two until end of meal also permitted; and one should not be so lenient since Semag did not explain thus, only one permitted through scattering until end of meal — behold two appear like laboring until end of meal even through dipping; and immediately: two through dipping permitted; and through scattering immediately — behold to eat them in a vessel also forbidden). But Taz's view is not so; and so the Mechaber's view is possible. And he began with Rashi above who explained four or five — he does not distinguish between salting and dipping; and even dipping forbidden — meaning until end of meal; and even one forbidden thus — only manner of speech he mentions four or five stam for end of meal. And indeed this that he distinguishes between salting and dipping — necessarily for immediately two he mentions two — whatever through scattering two together appears like melacha of me'abed — behold dipping thus two one by one, for in this dipping there is not one and the second in dipping two permitted; behold four or five dipping forbidden because it is until end of meal. And through scattering one permitted; and two — impossible to narrow so the salt not touch two — forbidden except to dip one by one as said. However what he wrote Eliyah Rabbah disputes Semak and wrote in Eliyah Rabbah's language: permitted to salt radish in a place where dipping is permitted — I did not understand; and requires study.
