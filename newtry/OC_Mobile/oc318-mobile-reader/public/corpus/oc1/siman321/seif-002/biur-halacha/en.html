But he may make a little from it — See in Mishna Berurah; and behold Magen Avraham brought proof for his words that for another meal even a little is forbidden from seif 5, where he forbade there to salt an egg to leave it for another meal; and he follows his view in s.k. 7. And Eliyah Rabbah challenged him; and see in Sha'arei Teshuvah. And the case is not so comparable, for there it is due to concern of tanning, as Biur HaGra wrote, unlike here; and clear proof for my humble opinion — behold the law that to leave it forbidden to salt is from Shibolei HaLeket, and he himself copied from Sefer HaTerumah that one who salts a little for the need of his day — permitted to do erev Shabbat:
