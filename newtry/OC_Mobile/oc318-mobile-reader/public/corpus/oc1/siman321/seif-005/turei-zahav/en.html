Forbidden to salt meat, etc. It appears in these the reason is not because it is similar to tanning, for salt does not change the nature of these as it changes radish and other things that are sharp or bitter; but in these it is only to give taste — therefore the prohibition is only when he leaves it, as stated according to Rashi that I mentioned nearby; but according to Rambam, who hangs it on what is customary to pickle — I do not know why there is prohibition in these that are not customary to pickle; therefore it appears to me the master of this law does not hold like Rambam; and if so, difficult on Shulchan Aruch that he copied Rambam and forbids in this. And Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashal the reason that salting is a principal melacha in the Mishnah regarding a deer; and even though that case is not salting meat, and further one could say "I will salt a piece and eat it" and afterward change his mind, etc. — nevertheless one should not do so in cooked meat, since the meat is not lost without salt, end of his words; it appears according to his conclusion that there is no clear prohibition in this even according to their words, but from the aspect that he does not need this and why should he trouble himself for nothing on Shabbat — behold he can salt at another meal that he will eat on a weekday; and so Beit Yosef in the name of responsum of Rashba siman 65: every thing customary to make very fine at the time of its cooking and leave it for an hour — forbidden; and the same here with salting according to this view. It follows that if there is some aspect whereby it is better for him to salt now than later, such as that it is now somewhat warm and will accept the salt more — there is no prohibition in the matter, as appears correct to me:
