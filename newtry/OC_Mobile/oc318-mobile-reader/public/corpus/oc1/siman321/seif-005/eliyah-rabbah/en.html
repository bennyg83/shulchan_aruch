(5) Four or five, etc. And Bach wrote that the whole world is accustomed not to salt them at all, rather each one dips in salt when he wants to eat — until here; and so Maharil wrote in the laws of Yom Tov; and so it appears to me the explanation of the Gemara: to salt even one is forbidden because it resembles me'abed; and so it appears from Rambam; and so I found in Ra'avad siman 252. Shilta Gibborim wrote: permitted to put salt on the salad and afterward oil and vinegar — until here.
