(q) [Seif 3] Forbidden to salt pieces of radish, etc. And in Rambam chapter 22 law 10 he wrote: radish and the like — because it appears like pickling pickled vegetables, etc. — meaning every thing whose way is to make pickled vegetables from it — forbidden to salt on Shabbat because it appears like pickling pickled vegetables; and not specifically a thing they regularly make pickled vegetables from, rather likewise every thing that salting helps — to sweeten it from its bitterness — forbidden. And so Taz s.k. 2: not specifically radish — likewise every thing that needs salting (meaning salting helps it to soften or harden or remove its bitterness), such as onions and garlic. And likewise Eliyah Rabbah or 5, Rabbi Zalman or 4; but a thing whose way is not to pickle and salting also does not help it — permitted as written below or 24.
