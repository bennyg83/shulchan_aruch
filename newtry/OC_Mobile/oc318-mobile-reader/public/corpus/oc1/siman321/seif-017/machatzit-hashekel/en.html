(s.k. 17) Flour, etc.; and in this even little by little — forbidden; know that on daf 155b we taught: one gives water to morayis but one does not knead it; and Abbaye said: I said before master: whose mishnah is this; and he said: R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda; for it is taught: one gives flour and one gives water — the latter is liable; R' Yehuda's words, for he holds giving water is his kneading and he is liable for kneading; R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda says until he kneads; but giving water alone — exempt, for giving water is not kneading; and it challenges: whence; perhaps R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda said "until he kneads" only regarding flour, which is fit for kneading; but morayis, which is not fit for kneading — he concedes giving water is kneading; and it answers: it should not enter your mind, for it is taught explicitly: one does not give water to morayis — R' Yehuda's words; R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda says one gives water to morayis; and roasted flour has the law of morayis, for there it is taught: one does not knead kali; and some say one kneads; and we say: who is "some say"; and Rav Chisda said: R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda; and Rashi explained: for R' Yehuda, although kali is not fit for kneading — nevertheless liable for giving water; but for R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda, since he is not liable for giving water and also it is not fit for kneading — therefore it teaches "one kneads"; and there is dispute among poskim: some rule like R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda, and so Rambam and Shulchan Aruch; but some rule like R' Yehuda. Further dispute between Rambam and Ra'avad and Tosafos on daf 18: we taught in Beit Shammai one does not soak ink and spices except so they soak by themselves; and we say: who is the tanna; giving water to ink — this is its soaking; and Rashi explained: even though he did not knead, etc., and liable for kneading, for this mishnah has no kneading and they divided it erev Shabbat — from which on Shabbat one is liable for a chatas; R' Yosef said: it is R' Yehuda, for it is taught: one gives flour, etc.; R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda says until he kneads; Abbaye said to him: perhaps R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda said only regarding flour, which is fit for kneading; but ink, which is not fit for kneading — R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda concedes giving water is kneading; and it answered: behold it is taught that also regarding ashes they dispute — and ashes are not fit for kneading; and it rejected: perhaps it means dust fit for kneading; and Abbaye's view remained that something not fit for kneading — R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda concedes; however since on daf 155b the Gemara rejected this view, for we find even morayis and kali, which are not fit for kneading, that R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda disputes; and it appears to Rambam that the sugya on daf 155b is primary and for R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda there is no distinction even in something not fit for kneading; but Tosafos hold both sugyot are true and there are three matters: flour is certainly fit for kneading; morayis and kali compared to flour are not fit for kneading but nevertheless are fit for kneading; and with this the Gemara on daf 155b proved that also in morayis and kali R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda disputes; but ink and ashes that are not fit for kneading at all — the sugya on daf 18 remains that R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda concedes; and so Ra'avad; and we say there on daf 155b that also for R' Yosi son of R' Yehuda who permits kneading kali — nevertheless requires a change; and with this Shulchan Aruch's and Magen Avraham's words are explained, and Rabbenu Yonah's words in his long work Marpesan Igra; and Sefer Teshuvah already addressed them.
