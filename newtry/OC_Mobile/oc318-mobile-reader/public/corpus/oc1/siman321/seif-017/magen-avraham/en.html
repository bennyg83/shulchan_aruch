Yaynomelin. The Maza permitted even straining it through a strainer, but it does not appear to me so. It was permitted only to mix them and eat them as they are, but to beat them well and strain them is forbidden, for this is greater exertion, a significant craft, and a significant fixing. Indeed, it was forbidden to beat even mustard that had already been kneaded. Thus wrote the Shiltei Gibborim. So too the Beit Yosef wrote at the end of siman 319 in the name of Shibolei HaLeket. Maharil wrote that it is forbidden to put cassia into vinegar to make a dip, and likewise it is forbidden to make hard-boiled eggs with parsley in vinegar, for this resembles brine. This requires study, for brine is forbidden because it is like pickling vegetables, whereas this resembles yaynomelin, which is permitted. A small amount is permitted according to all opinions, as written in seif 2.
