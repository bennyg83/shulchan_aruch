Yainomelin. And it was permitted only to mix them and eat them as they are; but to beat them well and strain them — forbidden, for this is more trouble and a great craft and a great repair — Beit Yosef siman 319, Rashal. Maharil wrote: forbidden to put saffron into vinegar to make a dip; and likewise to make hard eggs with parsley in vinegar — forbidden, for it is similar to yainomelin, end of his words. And Magen Avraham wrote: requires study, for yainomelin is forbidden because it is like pickling pickles; but this is similar to yainomelin that is permitted. And a little is permitted according to his words, as is written in seif 2. Forbidden to beat rolled eggs in a bowl, for it appears like one who wishes to cook them in a pot — Rashi daf 109.
