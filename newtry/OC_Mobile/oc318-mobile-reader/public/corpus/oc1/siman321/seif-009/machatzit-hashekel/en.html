(א) (s.k. 9) And in a bowl. Meaning: or in a bowl that one grinds them on the rim, etc. — to exclude the view of Rabbenu Yonah in his work that holds we require two changes: in the knife handle and also grinding inside a bowl instead of a mortar; but Magen Avraham holds one change suffices. And "in a bowl" means: or grind on the rim of the bowl instead of the knife handle:

(ב) That it is a complete change. Meaning Rabbenu Yonah wrote in name of the Agur that some challenge: why was it permitted through a change — behold there is no Torah prohibition; nevertheless there is rabbinic prohibition; and one can answer that where there is a complete change — even rabbinic prohibition does not apply; and it implies to cut them, etc.; and see siman 12 that it is forbidden on account of grinding:
