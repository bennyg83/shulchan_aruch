(א) Since, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah what is written; and it is from the words of Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah; and their intent here is to resolve Taz's difficulty below in siman 324 seif 7, where the Mechaber ruled that making food permitted on Shabbat; and therefore they wrote that the prohibition here is due to grinding, as we explained; and the language "he makes it food" that Rama wrote is not so precise according to their explanation:
