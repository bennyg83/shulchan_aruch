(א) Permitted to cut. For behold Tosafos and Rosh wrote that there is no grinding in foods; and even though some hold there is grinding, as siman 12, specifically in plants; but meat, which does not grow from the ground — grinding does not apply to it; and specifically roasted meat, for if he wants he eats it whole and chews it between his teeth; therefore even one who cannot chew — permitted to cut it, for we follow the majority of the world. But raw meat, since it is fit only to tear it, not in the usual manner — forbidden to cut it fine fine before birds; and even though it is fit for dogs and wild animals — they are not maintained for that but for birds on account of its importance; and therefore one may not permit; and further one may touch it on account of the prohibition of making food for an animal; and on account of excessive trouble with food [Terumat HaDeshen]; and in Shibolei HaLeket he wrote it is not necessary, for meat is considered a plant, as Tosafos and Ran wrote regarding milking, a toldah of threshing:
