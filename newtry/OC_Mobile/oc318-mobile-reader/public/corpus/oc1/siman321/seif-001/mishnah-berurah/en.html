(ג) From them — for he designated his intent away from them and they become muktzeh:
(ד) He cuts off and eats — for some are also fit for human food:
(ה) But not in a vessel — like the weekday manner:
(ו) And he winnows in his fingers — meaning he breaks off the stalks to eat the seed within them:
