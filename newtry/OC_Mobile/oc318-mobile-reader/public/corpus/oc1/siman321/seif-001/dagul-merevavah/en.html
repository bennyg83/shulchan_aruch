And according to this, in fowl — and it is a species of duck — permitted on erev Shabbos in Magen Avraham siman 308 s.k. 57; and perhaps since in any case there is no manner to eat it thus it is not similar to vegetables; and therefore in stuffed geese that if they roast them all the fat is lost — permitted via a non-Jew; and if there is no non-Jew and it is great loss — permitted for Israel to pour water on them.
