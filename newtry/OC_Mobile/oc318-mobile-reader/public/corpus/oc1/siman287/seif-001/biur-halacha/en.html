And likewise one may visit the sick, etc. — see Mishna Berurah s.k. 1 that it is not proper, etc., unless on weekdays he was busy and on Shabbat when he has leisure he goes to a sick person he loves, knowing that the sick person derives satisfaction from his visit — he performs a mitzvah and one should not prevent him from going on Shabbat and Yom Tov [Shaarei Teshuvah]. And it appears that likewise if he went to visit him on a weekday too, there is no prohibition to visit on Shabbat, for the Sages said there is no fixed measure for visiting the sick, and the poskim are lenient only if on weekdays he never goes at all and intends to go only on Shabbat; and especially if he knows that his going to the sick person is beneficial to advise him how to conduct himself regarding his illness, and also to strengthen him so his heart does not fall — he certainly performs a great mitzvah in this:
