(א) One may, etc. — We say in the Gemara 'with difficulty they permitted comforting mourners and visiting the sick on Shabbat'; therefore those who all week do not go except on Shabbat do not act properly:

(ב) (b) As he says in the sand, he cries out and cries out on the Shabbat:

(ג) One says to him, etc. — this refers to visiting the sick; and in comforting mourners one says to him 'It is Shabbat, not a time to comfort; consolation will soon come,' and there are those who are lenient that one may say 'May Heaven comfort you.' Peri Megadim wrote: if the mourner came to the synagogue after reciting Mizmor Shir leYom haShabbat, they no longer call out 'The sun will set' against the mourner, for one should not mention mourning in public; nevertheless he may go himself to tell him 'It is Shabbat, not a time to comfort,' etc., and he is permitted:
