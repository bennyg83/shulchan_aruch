And in Eretz Yisrael — per Be'er Heitev; and Rabbi Yosef, son of Eretz Yisrael, wrote that he was in Eretz Yisrael on 7 Cheshvan and asked per the Eretz Yisrael custom for a few days, and again returned to chutz la'aretz — since he had begun, he does not stop. And see in Halakhot Ketanot part 1 siman 73: if a son of Eretz Yisrael forgot to ask for rain, they do not return him; and Mahari Molcho wrote that they do return him; and he wrote in Shatz that to resolve doubt he should return and pray as a nedavah, and need not innovate anything new.
