If he asked for rain, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah that he must return to the beginning of Birkas HaShanim; and if he had not begun, only from the middle of the berachah, etc., it goes without saying that if he remembered within kedei dibbur he certainly fulfilled bedieved; but even if after kedei dibbur he began "Vesein berachah," "Baruch shenasanu," etc., and finished the berachah, he fulfilled bedieved [Chayei Adam on the name of Noda B'Yehuda].
