And so Eliyah Rabba letter 3 in the name of Mateh Moshe, and he wrote the incident of "and he went up," etc. And one who forgot and did not pray Minchah on the day before beginning to ask, or forgot and asked in Minchah before the day of asking — whose law is to return and he did not remember until Maariv — was explained above in siman 108, letter 56, see there.
