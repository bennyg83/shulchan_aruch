(15) If he did not request — see in Magen Avraham and Sha'arei Teshuvah, who wrote that if he remembered before he began to blow the shofar, he says it there; and in siman 114 in Biur Halacha, under without a closing, we expanded on this and clarified that it is better to say it in Shomea Tefilla, for thereby he satisfies all opinions. Now I found in Kitzur Shulchan Aruch that he ruled so too. And if he remembered before he finished the Blessing of the Years, he says it in the place where he remembered; but if he remembered after for good years, he returns and says and bless our years as good years, so it is like a closing adjacent to a closing; and it is better that he begin and give dew and rain and finish in order.
