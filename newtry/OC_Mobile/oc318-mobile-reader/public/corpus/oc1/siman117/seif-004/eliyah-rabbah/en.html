[4] Until the Minchah prayer, etc. In Tur he wrote until Musaf prayer, and they challenged: but on Yom Tov prayer asking is not relevant. Bach answered he comes to teach that if one prayed Shacharis of Pesach and erred and began weekday [prayer] and remembered in the middle of Birkas HaShanim, he finishes that berachah and mentions in it "Vesein tal u'matar," for they do not cease until Musaf — thus far. And so Tur's wording implies: all the time they mention rains, behold whenever they mention rains they ask when possible. And one need not challenge from Taanis 4a, which asks "asking on Yom Tov — does such a thing exist?" — for one may say it is different there, for it speaks of the prayer of the shaliach tzibbur, where it is not relevant that he err and pray weekday until Birkas HaShanim, for certainly the congregation will mention before Birkas HaShanim, as above siman 114, seif 2. I again saw in Magen Avraham who rejected Bach's words from the Gemara mentioned above; and to my humble opinion there is no difficulty, as I explained.
