(א) (Endnote 4) "Those who need," etc. — and it needs investigation, etc. — meaning: for the reason for this law is according to responsa of the Rosh, who wrote that he wondered why we do not ask on the seventh of Cheshvan like the people of the Land of Israel. Meaning: we follow the people of Babylonia — that is, among the laws in which the people of the West disagree with the Babylonian Talmud, or the people of chutz la'aretz with the people of the Land of Israel; unlike this, which depends on the nature of lands — for in the Land of Israel they needed rain immediately on the seventh of Cheshvan and therefore ask on the seventh of Cheshvon, and the people of Babylonia did not need rains until sixty days after the tekufah, therefore they did not ask until sixty days after the tekufah. And since in these lands they need rains in Cheshvan, they would have been worthy to practice like the people of the Land of Israel to ask on the seventh of Cheshvan. And the Rosh wrote that his words were not accepted. And based on this Mahari wrote that ab initio we do not rule like him, since he himself wrote that his words were not accepted; nevertheless, if an individual erred and mentioned on the seventh of Cheshvan, he need not return and pray unless as a voluntary prayer. And based on this, even if there is withholding of rains — if an individual erred and asked from the seventh of Cheshvan onward, it would have been worthy that he need not return and pray; and from the words of Shulchan Aruch it implies when there is specifically withholding of rains.

(ב) "At harvest time," etc. — for then it is certainly a sign of curse, as Shmuel I 12 says: "Is it not wheat harvest today? I will call to Heaven and He will give thunder and rain; know and see that your evil is great that you have done in the eyes of Heaven, to ask for yourselves a king."
