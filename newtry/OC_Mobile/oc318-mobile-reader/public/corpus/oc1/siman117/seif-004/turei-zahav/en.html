Even though he asked for dew. — Above, regarding mere mention in siman 114, dew suffices. In my view the reason: in mere mention, the main reason they fixed it there is because rains are life of the world, as written there; and dew also is life of the world, for through it produce is blessed — and this is known in many places that called dew revival; therefore this mention suffices, unlike here, where we request everything that brings to blessing, whether dew or rain.
