[3] And the day of tekufah, etc., is included, etc. Even if the tekufah falls after midday. Mateh Moshe wrote siman for asking: on whichever day the tekufah of Tishrei fell, from it count four days, and on that fourth day the asking will be in Kislev — for example, if the tekufah fell on Sunday, then the asking will be on Wednesday, and so always. And the sign: "And enough dew will go up," etc. He also wrote that bedieved, if one erred and said "Vesein tal u'matar" from the night of 7 Marcheshvan onward, one need not return even in our lands, since there is no reason to distinguish between our lands and Eretz Yisrael — for Babylonia was swampy, but these lands need water like Eretz Yisrael. And see Eliyahu Zuta.
