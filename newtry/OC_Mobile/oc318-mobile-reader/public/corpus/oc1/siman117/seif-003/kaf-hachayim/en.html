3) There — when they begin to ask for rain, etc. The Tashbetz wrote in volume 3, siman 123 in the name of his teacher z"l who received from the Gaonim z"l that the sixty [days] in the tekufah — we count according to Shmuel's tekufah and not according to Rav Ada's tekufah, see there. And so Maamar Mordechai letter 3 — that we count according to Shmuel's tekufah, and so in all books of our later authorities who wrote on the matter of moladot — they count according to Shmuel's tekufah; and so is practiced in the entire world, see there. And see in Yeshuot Yaakov, responsa from his grandson the Rashatz ASH z"l, who elaborated why we count according to Shmuel's tekufah and for festivals according to Rav Ada's tekufah, see there, Orach Torah letter 2.
