(13) In the hot season — see above in seif 1, and see in Biur Halacha that if he requested rain after the festival, even before sixty days of the equinox, in lands that need rain at the beginning of winter, he must return and pray only as voluntary.
(14) We make him return — meaning if he moved his feet, he returns to the beginning of the prayer; and if he did not move his feet, he returns to the beginning of the Blessing of the Years; and even if he remembered before he finished the blessing, he also returns to the beginning of the Blessing of the Years — see in Biur Halacha.
