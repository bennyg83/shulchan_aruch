Even though — on this matter; and see in siman 114 ot 11 that in mere mention dew helps, not wind; see there. And behold, in Yemei HaChamah, if they asked for dew and rain and remembered within the blessing, he returns to the beginning of the blessing, as in mere mention, so there should be recognition; and similarly if he finished and said other blessings afterward, he returns to Birkat HaShanim, for intermediate blessings have an order. And in Beit Yosef Hagahot: if he did not ask for dew and rain within the blessing, he says dew and rain at the conclusion of and satisfy us; in mere mention he says it in its place, not here; and if he finished he says it in its place; and if he began other blessings he says it in Shemoneh Esrei; and if not, he returns to Birkat HaShanim; and if he stepped back he returns to the beginning of the Amidah.
