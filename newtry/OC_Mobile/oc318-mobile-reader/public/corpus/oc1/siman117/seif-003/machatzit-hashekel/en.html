Seif katan 3, in Shome'a Tefillah, etc. Rain that causes harm, etc. Although a person may add requests for his personal needs even when others do not need that matter, those requests do not harm them. Rain is different: even if some people need it, because it harms others it is improper to establish the request in its regular communal place, namely Barech Aleinu. It may be requested only in Shome'a Tefillah, as an individual's prayer.
Because they burdened, etc. The Taz disagrees, for where something is needed by people, it is not considered burdening Heaven. Rather, he infers from the Gemara's wording that although rain may be mentioned in Shome'a Tefillah, this applies only in the silent Amidah. When the chazzan repeats the Amidah aloud, he should not request it even in Shome'a Tefillah. Those authorities, however, requested it even in the repetition. Eliyah Rabbah writes that the custom nevertheless is to recite in Shome'a Tefillah the prayer composed for a drought. He distinguishes that perhaps it is permitted because it is not phrased as “Grant dew and rain.” He concludes that one should nevertheless be careful not to request rain in prayer unless there is a great need.
