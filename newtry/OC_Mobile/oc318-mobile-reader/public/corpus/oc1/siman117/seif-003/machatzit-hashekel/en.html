(א) (Endnote 3) "On Shabbat and Yom Tov," etc. — rain that harms, etc. — meaning: one is permitted to add a request for one's needs even though others do not need that thing; nevertheless it does not harm them. Not so rain — meaning: they need it nevertheless; but since it harms others, it is not proper to fix it in its place — that is, in the blessing "Barekh aleinu" as communal prayer — but on Shabbat and Yom Tov as individual prayer.

(ב) "For having troubled," etc. — and Taz disagrees, for in something that people need, this is not applicable to troubling Heaven; rather he is precise in the language of the Gemara — that even though they mention on Shabbat and Yom Tov, that is when praying silently; but when the prayer leader prays aloud, even on Shabbat and Yom Tov he should not ask, and they asked even in prayer aloud — see there. And he wrote in the book Eliyah Rabba that nevertheless the custom is to read the prayer established for withholding rains on Shabbat and Yom Tov, and he disagreed and wrote: and it is possible that since they do not say in language "v'ten tal u'matar" it is permitted. And he concluded: nevertheless one should be careful not to ask in prayer when there is no great need, end quote.
