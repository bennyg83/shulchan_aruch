3) There: And we begin to request rain, etc. The Tashbetz, volume 3, siman 123, wrote in the name of his teacher, of blessed memory, that it is a tradition in the hands of the Geonim, of blessed memory, that the 'sixtieth [day] of the season' (tekufah) is reckoned according to the season of Shmuel and not according to the season of Rav Adda; see there. So too wrote the Maamar Mordechai, ot 3, that we reckon according to the season of Rav Shmuel, and so it is in all the sefarim of the authorities of our times who composed [works] on the matter of the molados, that they reckon according to the season of Rav Shmuel, and such is the practice throughout the world; see there. And see in the Yeshuos Yaakov, in a responsum from his grandson, R' Tzvi Hirsch, of blessed memory, who elaborated on why we reckon [the request for rain] according to the season of Rav Shmuel, but the festivals according to the season of Rav Adda; see there.
