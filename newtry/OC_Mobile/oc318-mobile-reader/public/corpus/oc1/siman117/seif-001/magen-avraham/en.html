Until the Minchah prayer. In the Tur he wrote until the Musaf prayer, and as Mahari it is not precise, for in the Yom Tov prayer requesting is not applicable. And the Bach wrote for example: one who erred and began weekday prayer on Yom Tov and remembered in the blessing "Alenu" — he finishes the entire blessing as written in siman 268, and he should say "and give dew and rain," end quote. And it is questionable, for by law immediately in Maariv he should cease even from mentioning; rather, because one should not form bundles of neter until Musaf, as written in siman 114. And in requesting, where this reason does not apply as said above, therefore even an individual does not request in Maariv; and so explicitly in Taanit 4b — requesting on Yom Tov, who is there? etc. See there, as said above, that the festivals have passed and rain is nullified, for rains are not good. And it seems the same when requesting falls on Shabbat night and an individual erred and prayed Maariv without mentioning dew and rain, since the congregation had not begun; and so in the Tur, see there. And in Darkei Moshe he wrote another answer to the Beit Yosef's difficulty and said that our custom is that they say at Musaf of Passover piyyutim of rain to inform them that we cease rain and pray for dew, as the custom of Spain. One should say in the Blessing of the Years "and satisfy us from Your goodness" and not "from Your good," for that is the formula of the prayer; and I have not heard nor seen anyone who said "from Your good," and one should not change from the coin that the Sages minted, end quote from responsa of the Rosh, general 4, siman 20; and so too Maharshal.
