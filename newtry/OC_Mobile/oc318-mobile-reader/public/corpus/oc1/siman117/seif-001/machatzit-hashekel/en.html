(א) (Endnote 1) "Of," etc. — for example, if etc. — meaning: if one knows the week in which they begin to ask but does not know which day, if one knows the day of the tekufah one need not calculate the days but rather [proceed] in general: if the tekufah is on day one, the requesting is etc.

(ב) "Always two days." — meaning the ninth or tenth week after the tekufah. And in Beit Yosef he brought in the name of Avudraham that they always begin on the 22nd of November — and that is on condition that the month of February is sometimes 28 days and sometimes 29 days; therefore if the February before November had 28 days, then the requesting is on the 22nd of November, but if February had 29 days then the requesting is on the 23rd of November. And the matter depends on their months, for we count by the sun and the tekufah too follows the course of the sun.
