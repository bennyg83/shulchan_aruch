(א) The years. It should read "and satisfy us from Your bounty," not "from its bounty." The Rosh and Rashal. Magen Avraham. And Taz wrote that when one is on the road he says "from Your bounty," and when in a city he says "from its bounty," for the reason explained there; and in the siddur of the Ari, of blessed memory, it is written "from its bounty."

(ב) Sixty. That is to say, in the evening prayer when the sixtieth day begins.

(ג) The sixty. That is to say, the day on which the tekufah fell — we count it among the sixty, even if the tekufah falls at midday or later, provided there is still some [of the day] before night. And always [there are] two days between the tekufah and beginning to ask: if the tekufah is on day one, the asking is in the evening prayer pertaining to day four.

(ד) And in Eretz Yisrael. Because there one needs rain more, since it is higher than all lands. One who left Eretz Yisrael for chutz la'aretz — if he has a wife and children in Eretz Yisrael, even if he does not intend to return during the rainy season — asks as one of the children of Eretz Yisrael (Maharikash and Levush, part 1, siman 74). And I heard from the Rav Rabbeinu Avraham Yitzchaki, of blessed memory, who heard from Mahari Molcho, of blessed memory, that Maharam Gota, of blessed memory, and several other great authorities disagreed with Maharikash on this and wrote that one asks as one of chutz la'aretz; and so ruled in the responsum Devar Shmuel, siman 223; and Pri Chadash ruled that children of Eretz Yisrael who came to chutz la'aretz — all the time their intention is to return that same year to Eretz Yisrael, they ask as children of Eretz Yisrael; and if their intention is not to return except after a year or two, like emissaries of Eretz Yisrael, even if they have wife and children in Eretz Yisrael, they ask as children of chutz la'aretz.
