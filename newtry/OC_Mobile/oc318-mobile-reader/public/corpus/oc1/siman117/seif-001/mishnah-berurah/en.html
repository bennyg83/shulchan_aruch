(1) The years — the formula is "and satisfy us from Your goodness" [Magen Avraham in the name of the Rosh and Maharshal]; and in the Siddur of the Ari it is written "from Your good."

(2) In the evening prayer — see in Biur Halacha.

(3) Of the sixtieth day — meaning in the evening prayer when the sixtieth day begins; and then the shamash announces after Kaddish, before the prayer, "dew and rain," so they know to say "and give dew and rain"; and if he did not announce, they nevertheless say it.

(4) The sixty — meaning the day on which the equinox fell is counted among the sixty, even if the equinox falls at midday or later, as long as there is a bit before night; and there are always two days between the equinox and the request, for if the equinox is on Sunday, the request is in the evening prayer that belongs to Wednesday.

(5) And in Eretz Yisrael, etc. — because there they need rain, as it is higher than all lands, unlike in exile; and we in all Diaspora follow the children of exile of Babylonia. A son of Eretz Yisrael in Diaspora, or the reverse — if he intends within a year to return, he requests as his place; and if after a year, he requests as the place where he is, even if he has wife and children at home — so Peri Chadash, and the Pri Megadim brought it; and in Brachi Yosef he agreed in the name of many great ones per Maharaz Gut and Mahari Molcho that each asks as the sons of the city where he is found, unlike Peri Chadash — see there. And apparently from the words of Ba'er Heitev it implies they speak specifically when he does not intend to return; and one needs to examine responsa Devar Shmuel and Yad Aharon, for there is their source.

(6) From the night of the seventh, etc. — and post factum, if he did not request, we make him return; and it is not similar to Rosh Chodesh evening, where the reason is because they do not sanctify the month at night [so wrote Brachi Yosef and Zachor L'Avraham, siding not to return post factum, and per Levush HaOrah, vol. 1, siman 73, and as in mentioning — see there; but from the words of several Acharonim I saw, it does not imply so].

(7) And they ask until, etc. — meaning "and" is included, and there is no difference in cessation between Eretz Yisrael and Diaspora.
