Seif 2. Individuals who need rain, etc. — they do not ask for it in Birkas HaShanim but only in Shomea Tefillah, etc. NB: See in Taz what he wrote as proof from Rabbi's wording, who answered "they are considered like individuals" in Shomea Tefillah — and apparently the intent is that even in Shomea Tefillah they are like individuals; see there. And I say: even if we parse it thus, it would be proven from the Gemara there to the contrary, for behold the Gemara concludes "what is the halachic outcome?" and resolves there "in Shomea Tefillah," and did not conclude there the wording "they are considered like individuals in Shomea Tefillah." And why did it change from Rabbi's wording? Necessarily, since the Gemara concludes there is a dispute in the matter and Rabbi is a Tanna and argues — and therefore, since there is one view that they have true congregation status and mention in Birkas HaShanim, and Rabbi holds to the contrary that even in Shomea Tefillah they are like individuals, as the Taz wrote — therefore for practical halacha the Gemara decides both sides: regarding this matter the halacha is like Rabbi that they mention in Shomea Tefillah; but regarding mention in a loud voice, the law is like the other side that they mention in a loud voice and they have congregation status. Therefore it omitted the wording "they are considered like individuals" and said only that for this matter the halacha is like Rabbi in Shomea Tefillah, but not in what it says "like individuals." And this fits well and is correct; examine it carefully.
