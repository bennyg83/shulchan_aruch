In Eretz Yisrael — on this matter, and this is an explanation of what the Mechaber wrote in seif 2 that one prays as a voluntary prayer. And behold, for the Mechaber one must return and pray with doubt and give, as in siman 117; and Darkei Moshe disagrees and wrote that the essence follows Mahari"i, and nevertheless he wrote here that if one wishes he returns and prays as a nedavah and need not stipulate that the first prayer was accepted for him. And see Nitzotzim, who expanded, and it appears better to stipulate — a son of Eretz Yisrael in chutz la'aretz, or the reverse, per Pri Chadash: if his intention is within a year to return he asks as his place; and if his intention is after a year he asks as the place where he is there, even though he has a wife and children at home.
