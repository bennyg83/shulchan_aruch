(א) Paragraph 1, in chutz la'aretz, etc. Rashi there: that all our customs follow the sons of Babylonia; and likewise Rashi in the third chapter of Chullin regarding milk of the stomach [kutzah milk].

(ב) In prayer, etc. Unlike the Yerushalmi, which says we count from the evening; and likewise Rabeinu Efraim — and they did not practice thus, the Rosh; and as is plain from the gemara, as in most places that begin from the evening (Magen Avraham).

(ג) And the day, etc. For the halacha is established that the day of the tekufah begins according to Rabbi Yosi in Sanhedrin 13a, and as written in Rosh Hashana 28a — see Rashi and Tosafos there on the passage, "because you have not yet known."

(ד) And in Eretz Yisrael, etc. Although there, folio 2, we say — and the Ari began, etc. — the Rif and Rambam hold that they omitted this sugya because it was rejected, this sugya having been pushed aside since it was not stated in that place [Taanis] 10a and in the exile (golah), etc. — and this is not according to Shinyana; for us, etc. And also from what is written above, the anonymous [statement] is halacha like Rabbi Gamliel 40 even in this era. And Arna elaborated on this.

(ה) And they ask, etc. Folio 2 rules thus, and in this there is no amora who argues on him.
