For even if he did not mention rain, per the Yerushalmi which says that in summer He brings down dew, and if he said dew in place of rain he has fulfilled his obligation; but during the interruption at Pesach one could say that in any case if some of them mention it their prayer is not proper and they must return and pray. And the reason they were not concerned about factions in the request that begins at Maariv is because there it is not the same, for even when they begin at Shacharit we do not exempt ourselves from this, for not everyone comes to the synagogue; unlike on Yom Tov, which is a time of assembly for all. And Magen Avraham's words have no substance.
