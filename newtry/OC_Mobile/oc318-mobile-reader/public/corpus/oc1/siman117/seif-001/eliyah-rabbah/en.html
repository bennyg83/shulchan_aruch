[1] Birkas HaShanim. In Eliyahu Zuta I wrote: "meTovkha" refers to the Holy One, blessed be He, and not "meTovah" that refers to the year; and so wrote Magen Avraham in the name of the Rosh's responsum, and so wrote Rashal, Mateh Moshe, and Kitzur Shlah — and not as found in the siddur in the name of Shlah to say "meTovah."
