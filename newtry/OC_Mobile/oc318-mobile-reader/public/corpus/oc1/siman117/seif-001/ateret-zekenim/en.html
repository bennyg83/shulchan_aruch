We insert in the ninth word of the second blessing, "Bless us... this year," etc., "[and] satisfy us from Your bounty (mituvkha)" as it should be read, not "from its bounty (mituva)" — for satiety, may the food be blessed in one's bowels, does not depend on the land's produce but on the blessing over food, that He pour satiety from Himself, as written, "He sets your border in peace; with the fat of wheat He satisfies you"; and also in "the good treasure" Heaven applies, as written, "Heaven will open for you His good treasure" (Maharshal). And it appears when one is on the road one should say "mituvkha," for then there is a hidden [spelling out of the] name of yud-hei-vav-hei from the verse, "For He will command His angels for you...," [by] hidden [letters], and likewise there is a hidden [name] in "causes the grass... and satisfy us from Your bounty"; unlike when one is in a city, then one should say "mituva," because in the hidden [letters] there is the name of Heaven in "causes the grass... and satisfy us from its bounty."
