The years — per Be'er Heitev. And see in Shatz: and bless shenatinu without a vav, with the nun in kamatz; and what Avudraham wrote, that shenotenu is a scribal error — for immediately afterward one says and let its end be, in singular language; see there. And it appears that also in the Ashkenazic nusach, where they do not say and let its end be, nevertheless one should not change to the wording shenotenu, only shenatinu with kamatz on the nun — and so is the custom.
