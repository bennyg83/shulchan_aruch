1) [Seif 1] Birkat HaShanim, etc. In Ashkenaz they are accustomed to say both in summer and winter "Barekh aleinu," except that they change the request by season: in the rainy season they say "v'ten tal u'matar livrachah," and in the summer months "v'ten berachah." The Sephardim are accustomed to say in summer "barekhenu" and in winter "barekh aleinu," as written in Tur and Beit Yosef. In Sha'ar HaKavanot he wrote in the name of the Ari z"l that one should say in summer "barekhenu" and in winter "barekh aleinu," and we wrote above in siman 48, see there. So too Birkei Yosef letter 1 in the name of disciples of the Ari z"l, and they cited Shulchan Aruch page 129 side b and Yeshuot Yaakov letter 1. It is astonishing that he wrote in the name of disciples and not in the name of the Ari, for in Kaf HaChayim he brings from Sha'ar HaKavanot that Maharash Wake z"l arranged. And see in our words above siman 114, letter 60.
