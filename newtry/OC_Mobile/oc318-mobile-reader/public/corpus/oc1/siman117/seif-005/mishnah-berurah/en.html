(16) And requests in Shomea Tefilla — meaning in the blessing "Hear our voice" before "for You hear," which is ordained for all requests, as below in siman 119. But mention of "Who causes the wind to blow" — see above in siman 114, that if he forgot, he returns, because mention is praise and has no place in this blessing, which is ordained for request.
(17) Before Aneinu — for requesting is stricter than Aneinu, for if he did not say it we make him return — unlike Aneinu.
(18) As if moved — and the same if he is accustomed to say supplications and finished his supplications and said after them the verse "May the words of my mouth be acceptable," etc.; for by saying this verse he diverted his attention from saying more supplications, and his prayer is considered complete even though he has not yet begun "He who makes peace."
(19) After he concluded — and if he remembered after he said "in the land," he finishes "teach me Your statutes" and says "and give dew and rain," and afterward "for You hear," etc. [Biur Halacha above in siman 114].
