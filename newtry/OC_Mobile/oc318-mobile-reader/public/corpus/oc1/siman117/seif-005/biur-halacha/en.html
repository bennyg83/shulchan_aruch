If he did not ask for rain, etc. — It would seem plainly that the same applies if he omitted in another berachah something that is essential to the berachah [such as omitting the matter of ingathering of exiles in Tekias Shofar, which is called in Megillah 13a by this name; see there] — he likewise may complete it in Shomea Tefillah. And proof from Tosafos Berachos 29a on the passage beginning "Mipnei," etc., that they did not exclude except something that is a matter of praise, such as mention and havdalah and other praises that are not matters of prayer and request — unlike this. And also even regarding havdalah itself, Magen Avraham ruled in siman 294, se'if katan 3, that if one knows he will not have a cup for havdalah and forgot to say havdalah in "Atah Chonen," he should say it in Shomea Tefillah so as not to need to return. If so, the same here. And what is brought below in siman 119, seif 3, that if one erred in one of the middle berachos one returns to the beginning of the berachah — possibly there it speaks of one who remembered after Shomea Tefillah. And you may know, for in his words there he also included if one erred and forgot to say tal u'matar in Birkas HaShanim; and in this certainly he need not return to the beginning of the berachah but asks in Shomea Tefillah. But it is certain that regarding tal u'matar the Mechaber's law cannot apply except if one remembered after Shomea Tefillah; if so, possibly the same here. And why the Mechaber did not explain this there explicitly — because he wished there to include his law also regarding one who skipped; and regarding skipping there is no distinction whether he remembered before Shomea Tefillah or after, for he is not permitted to include in Shomea Tefillah. And although Peri Chadash is lenient also in this, Chayei Adam already challenged him. Also the matter of a comprehensive error: if he added something that spoils the berachah, such as saying tal u'matar in summer days and the like — in this certainly he has no correction in Shomea Tefillah; but regarding omission, possibly he may complete it in Shomea Tefillah, as stated. And know that per the Gra above in siman 59, seif 1, he holds the essential matter depends on the closing — if he finished [properly] he fulfilled, and even completion is not required, for such is required according to Shulchan Aruch there. Afterwards I found in Peri Megadim in siman 119, ot gimmel in Mishbetzos Zahav, that his view is also not like the Peri Chadash above; and also regarding our doubt he wrote that if he skipped some words that are essential to the berachah, he cannot complete it in Shomea Tefillah. And his reason: it does not resemble asking [for rain] and havdalah, for these are not a fixed formula always; therefore they enacted a regulation for this — unlike [rain in Shomea Tefillah, where] Peri Megadim did not bring proof for his view in this.

Like uprooted [feet] — See Mishna Berurah; so too Magen Avraham in siman 422, and so all the Acharonim, and not like Chayei Adam who wrote we require actual uprooting of the feet. And what he wrote in Nishmas Adam about the Yerushalmi that Magen Avraham brought — it is not found in chapter 5 of Berachos after the mishnah "Modim Modim"; and Rashba also brought it in his chiddushim in the chapter "Tefillas HaShachar." And so we found in Ezrat HaShulchan in Terumat HaDeshen, chapter "Tefillas HaShachar," on the passage "Lo amarnu ela beshe-regil lomar techinunim," etc., explained like Magen Avraham; see there. And you see that the Nishmas Adam from Rashi in Berachos may be rejected, for possibly Rashi holds like Rav Amram brought in Tur siman 422, who rules it depends only on uprooting the feet, or as the first wording in the Gemara.
