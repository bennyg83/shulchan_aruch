(א) Paragraph 65, if not, etc. In Berachos there.

(ב) And if he had [fasted], etc. Magen Avraham.

(ג) If not so. In Tosafos in Berachos there, in the name of the Yerushalmi, they wrote that one returns to Shomea Tefillah; and likewise Terumat HaDeshen and Rashba in the name of Rav Hai Gaon. But Rambam, Raavad, and Rosh hold that one returns to Birkas HaShanim, and as written in siman 33, paragraph 1 — one who erred in this and in this returns to the beginning.

(ד) And if he uprooted [his feet], and if [he had completed his prayer], etc. There, 29b, and in Tosafos there on the passage beginning with "Amar Rabeinu," etc.

(ה) And if he remembered, etc. Beit Yosef, and as written above, siman 114, paragraph 6.
