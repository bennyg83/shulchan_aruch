5) There — in the Maariv prayer of the sixtieth day, etc. When the time for requesting arrives in Birkat HaShanim, it is good that the shamash proclaim on the day before in all the prayers in this language: "On this night is the request," as they proclaim the tekufah, because the time for requesting is not a limited time as with "Morid haGeshem" or "Morid haTal," which is always on Passover and Sukkot — Orchot Yosher ch. 2; and so in Moed LeKol Chai siman 27 letter 4 — that every teacher of righteousness should issue a proclamation about saying "Barekh aleinu" standing one day or two days before the night when the sixtieth day of the tekufah occurs.
