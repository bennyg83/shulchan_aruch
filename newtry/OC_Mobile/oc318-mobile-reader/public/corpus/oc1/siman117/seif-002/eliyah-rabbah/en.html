[2] In the Maariv prayer, etc. It is found: fifty-eight days; and the sign: "Your dew is like dew" (the gematria of tal = 39), meaning after the count of dew they say "Vesein tal." And if the sixtieth day comes out on Shabbat, they begin at night (first) [Motzei Shabbat], for on Shabbat they do not say "Vesein tal u'matar"; so wrote Mateh Moshe. And it seems somewhat also regarding one who erred and began in the berachah "Barech Aleinu," etc., on Shabbat — he does not say "Vesein tal u'matar," since the congregation has not yet said it; and so wrote Magen Avraham, but it is not required. And to me it seems that in our lands one may say it, for bedieved one does not return from the night of 7 Cheshvan, as below in se'if katan 3.
