Individuals who require rain, or even an entire country such as all of Spain or all of Ashkenaz, are regarded as individuals and should request it in the blessing of Shomea Tefillah. It appears that according to this, all the more so if, Heaven forbid, rain is needed in MarCheshvan, it should be requested in Shomea Tefillah when necessary. Even during the season of Tammuz, when rain is ordinarily a sign of curse, it may be requested in Shomea Tefillah when needed, so certainly this applies during the rainy season. Nevertheless, I received a tradition that one must take care not to request rain at all outside the time established by the Sages, even in Shomea Tefillah. Rather, one should seek favor before Hashem through fasting, Selichos, the Thirteen Attributes of Mercy, and the recitation of verses and Tehillim concerning rain, but should not say “Grant dew and rain.” This is from my teacher, Bach. I heard that one of the great authorities once ruled, during a summer drought, that the congregation should say “Grant dew and rain” in Shomea Tefillah. He died that same year, and people attributed this to his having imposed an excessive burden upon Heaven.
