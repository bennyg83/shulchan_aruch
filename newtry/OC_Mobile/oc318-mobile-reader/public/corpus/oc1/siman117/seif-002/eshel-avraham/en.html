Like individuals. A place far from the equator toward the south, where the southern pole is elevated there about twenty degrees, and the northern pole is hidden from them below the horizon twenty degrees and more — in a manner that the seasons and summer days for them are from Tishrei until Nissan, and rainy days from Nissan until Tishrei; and from Nissan until Tishrei they do not need rain. And greater than this: if rain passes over them at that time it is very bad for them, for the air becomes humid and turns to bad illnesses, may God have mercy. And because of these reasons they wish to change the order of prayer regarding asking and mention, opposite our country. And the author of Chokhmat Adam, chapter 3, se'if 63, wrote: certainly in summer days one need not mention and ask in Birkas HaShanim except in Shomea Tefillah; however from the festival of Pesach onward, since rain is bad for them, certainly one should not mention and not ask for rain at all, as Rambam wrote in explanation of the mishnah. And so in the Gemara: R' Elazar said, R' Yehoshua said: the halacha is like R' Yehoshua; and it challenges: did R' Yehoshua say thus? And we learned, etc.; and it answers: this for us, this for them. And it challenges: what difference to us, who have fruit in the field (meaning the sons of Babylonia have fruit in the field)? To them also they have pilgrim festivals and two [days of Yom Tov]; for R' Yehoshua spoke when the Temple was standing — now that you have come to this, etc.; see there. From all the above it appears: wherever there is harm from rain, one does not ask; therefore they have no [permission] to ask except in Shomea Tefillah.
