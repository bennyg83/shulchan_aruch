On Shabbat and Yom Tov. And even though one is permitted to add a matter from the blessing, as written in siman 119, rain is different, for it harms most of the world. The Ran wrote; the Bach — however, I received that one should be careful not to request rain at all except in the time the Sages ordained, even on Shabbat and Yom Tov; rather, we appease Him in fasting and selichot and say verses and psalms of rain, but one does not request "and give dew and rain," etc. And I heard that two great ones ruled to request in the congregation "and give dew and rain" on Shabbat and Yom Tov at the time of withholding of rains, and pestilence gathered on their nation that year, and they attributed the matter to having troubled Heaven, on that account — incident in 1575. And in responsa of the Rosh, general 4, siman 10, he wrote that even on Shabbat, when one does not fast, they mention the thirteen attributes and say verses of rain; see there and see there, siman 62.
