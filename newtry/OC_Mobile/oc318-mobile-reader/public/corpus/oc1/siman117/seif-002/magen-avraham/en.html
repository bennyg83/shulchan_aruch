In Shomea Tefillah. Although one may add a request that relates to the subject of a blessing, as stated in siman 119, rain is different because it is harmful in most of the world. Ran. Bach writes: I received a tradition that one must be careful not to request rain at all outside the period established by the Sages, even in Shomea Tefillah. Rather, people should seek favor before Hashem through fasting and Selichos and should recite verses and psalms concerning rain, but they should not say, “Grant dew and rain.” I heard that two great authorities once ruled that the congregation should request “Grant dew and rain” in Shomea Tefillah during a drought. Both passed away that year, and people attributed this to their having imposed an excessive burden upon Heaven. Thus far his words. See siman 575, seif 9. Responsa Rosh, klal 4, siman 10, writes that even on Shabbos, when people do not fast, they mention the Thirteen Attributes of Mercy and recite verses concerning rain. See there and seif 2 there. Those who require rain. The wording implies that this applies specifically where rain has been withheld. Otherwise, one who requested rain must repeat the prayer, even if rain is not a sign of curse in that location. This requires study. During the harvest season, however, all agree that he must repeat the prayer.
