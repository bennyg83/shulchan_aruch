In Shome'a Tefillah. Although one may add a request related to the blessing, as stated in siman 119, rain is different because it harms most of the world. Ran. The Bach wrote: “I received a tradition that one must be careful not to request rain at all outside the time established by the Sages, even in Shome'a Tefillah. Rather, we appease Hashem through fasting and Selichos and recite verses and psalms concerning rain, but do not request ‘Grant dew and rain,’ etc. I heard that two great authorities instructed a congregation to request ‘Grant dew and rain’ in Shome'a Tefillah during a drought, and both passed away that year. This was attributed to their having burdened Heaven.” See siman 575, seif 9. Responsa Rosh, principle 4, siman 10, states that even on Shabbos, when fasting is prohibited, the Thirteen Attributes are recited together with verses concerning rain; see there, and see there seif 2.
