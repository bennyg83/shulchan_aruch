In Shomea Tefillah. It appears that according to this, all the more so we may request rain after the seventh of MarCheshvan but before sixty days from the autumnal equinox, in Shomea Tefillah, when it is needed. After all, even during Tammuz, when rain is a sign of curse, it may be requested in Shomea Tefillah. Taz writes that the permission for a congregation to pray for rain in Shomea Tefillah applies when they pray silently. The chazzan, however, should not say it aloud, even in Shomea Tefillah. Bach writes: I received a tradition not to request rain at all outside the time established by the Sages, even in Shomea Tefillah. Rather, they should seek favor before Hashem through fasting and Selichos and recite verses and Tehillim concerning rain, but they should not say “Grant dew and rain.” I heard that two great authorities once ruled that the congregation should request “Grant dew and rain” in Shomea Tefillah during a drought, and both passed away that year. People attributed this to their having imposed an excessive burden upon Heaven. Thus far his words. Responsa Rosh, klal 4, siman 10, writes that even on Shabbos, when fasting is not observed, the Thirteen Attributes of Mercy are mentioned and verses concerning rain are recited. Taz writes concerning Bach’s tradition that if it is authoritative, it plainly refers only to the chazzan’s public repetition. Each individual may request rain silently in Shomea Tefillah, as stated there.
At all. According to this, it appears that a resident of outside Eretz Yisrael who mistakenly requested rain after the seventh of MarCheshvan but before sixty days from the equinox need not repeat the prayer, even when rain is not needed. This is the view of Knesses HaGedolah, Mateh Moshe, and Radbaz, siman 58, part 6, siman 2055. Olas Tamid rules similarly. Pri Chadash disagrees and requires repetition when rain is not needed. The Shulchan Aruch’s reference to those who need rain applies specifically when rain has been withheld. Otherwise, the person must repeat the prayer, even if rain is not a sign of curse in that location. This requires study. During the harvest season, however, all agree that he must repeat the prayer. Magen Avraham.
