In Shome'a Tefillah. It appears that, according to this, we certainly may request rain after the seventh of Marcheshvan but before sixty days after the tekufah, in Shome'a Tefillah, when it is needed. After all, even during the tekufah of Tammuz, when rain is an adverse sign, they may request it in Shome'a Tefillah. The Taz wrote that a congregation may pray for rain in Shome'a Tefillah only in the silent Amidah, but the chazzan should not say it aloud even in Shome'a Tefillah. The Bach wrote: “I received a tradition not to request rain at all outside the time established by Chazal, even in Shome'a Tefillah. Rather, we appease Hashem through fasting and Selichos and recite verses and psalms concerning rain, but do not say ‘Grant dew and rain.’ I heard that two great authorities instructed a congregation to request ‘Grant dew and rain’ in Shome'a Tefillah during a drought, and both passed away that year; this was attributed to their having burdened Heaven.” In Responsa Rosh, principle 4, siman 10, it is written that even on Shabbos, when fasting is prohibited, the Thirteen Attributes are recited together with verses concerning rain; see there. The Taz wrote concerning this that, if this tradition is authoritative, it plainly refers only to the chazzan's repetition, but each individual may request rain silently in Shome'a Tefillah, as stated there.
At all. According to this, it appears that a resident outside Eretz Yisrael who mistakenly requested rain after the seventh of Marcheshvan but before sixty days after the tekufah need not repeat the Amidah, even when rain is not needed. This is stated by Knesses HaGedolah, Mateh Moshe, Radbaz, siman 58, and Olas Tamid. Peri Chadash disagrees and rules that he must repeat the Amidah if rain is not needed; see there. The wording of the Shulchan Aruch, “those who require rain,” refers specifically to a drought. Otherwise, he must repeat the Amidah, even if rain is not an adverse sign in that place. This requires study. In any event, during harvest time all agree that he must repeat the Amidah. Magen Avraham.
