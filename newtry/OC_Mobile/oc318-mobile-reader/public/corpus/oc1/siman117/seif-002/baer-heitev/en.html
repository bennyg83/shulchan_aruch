(א) On Shabbat. And it appears that according to this reasoning, all the more so we should ask for rain after the seventh of Cheshvan within the sixty [days] until the tekufah on Shabbat when we need to — for even at the tekufah of Tammuz, which are a bad omen, they ask on Shabbat. And Taz wrote: that which the congregation may pray on Shabbat — that is when they pray silently; but the prayer leader (shaliach tzibur) should not say it aloud, even on Shabbat. And Beit Chadash wrote: I received not to ask for rain at all outside the time Chazal established, even on Shabbat, but rather to appease before Him, blessed be He, through fasting and selichot, and they say verses and psalms of rain — but one does not ask "and give dew and rain." And I heard that two great authorities instructed to ask in the congregation "and give dew and rain" on Shabbat at a time of withholding of rain, and they were gathered to their people in that year; they attributed the matter to having troubled Heaven, end quote. And in the responsum of the Rosh, general principle 4, siman 10, he wrote that even on Shabbat, when they do not fast, they mention the thirteen attributes and say verses of rain — see there. And Taz wrote on him: if this tradition has substance, it seems straightforward that he speaks of the prayer of the prayer leader; but each individual may ask for rain on Shabbat silently, as explained there.

(ב) General rule. And it appears that from this reasoning, a son of chutz la'aretz who erred and asked for rain in prayer after the seventh of Cheshvan within sixty days [until] the tekufah — when [the place] does not need rain — need not repeat [the prayer], even when rain is not needed, as the halacha decides, from Taz, Maharadbaz, siman 58 [chalev 2255], and likewise in Or Torah. And Pri Chadash disagrees, that one must go back if rain is not needed — see there. And that which is written in Shulchan Aruch, "those who need rain," etc. — that is specifically when the rain has been withheld; but otherwise they make him go back, even though rain in that place is not a bad omen — uncertain. However, at harvest time, all agree they make him go back. Magen Avraham.
