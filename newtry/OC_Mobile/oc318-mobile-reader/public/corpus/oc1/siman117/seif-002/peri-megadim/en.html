In Shemoneh Esrei — on this matter: he brought in repetition that one should not say even in Shemoneh Esrei and give dew and rain, except in a whisper one may say it; and our custom is to say Yehi Ratzon and not mention explicitly these words and give dew and rain, except in verses and other expressions, per Arizal. And it appears from what the Mechaber wrote: an individual, if he erred in Birkat HaShanim, does not return — for he was deliberate and said it in Birkat HaShanim — he returns; and possibly to the beginning of the prayer, as in siman 114 seif 7; see what Magen Avraham wrote there.
