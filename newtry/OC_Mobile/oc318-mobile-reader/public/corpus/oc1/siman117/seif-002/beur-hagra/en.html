(א) Paragraph 62: and even one city, etc. There.

(ב) Or one land, etc. Unlike the Rosh, who wrote to distinguish between one city and one land, and wrote that which Rashi explained, "we follow the sons of Babylonia" — that is a matter that depends on the essence of the law; but in what depends on the need of the hour, it is fit to follow the places, as stated in the gemara there. But the poskim disagreed with him, and Maharia wrote: since the words of the Rosh appear [correct], except that one should not form factions, since they did not practice thus; but an individual who erred and asked — one does not make him go back. But Beit Yosef wrote: since the sugya of the world is not like the Rosh, it seems one must go back, as written in the first chapter of Sanhedrin. However, since Maharia ruled thus, it is fit to pray voluntarily (nedavah), as in a doubt in law, as written above, siman 108, seif 11 — and Rama wrote thus. However, in Darkei Moshe he agreed with the words of Maharia, and Rama wrote in the gloss: but he is not required, etc.
