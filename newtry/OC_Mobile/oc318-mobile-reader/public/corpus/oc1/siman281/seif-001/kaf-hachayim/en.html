(1) [seif 1] One may not chat in "U'lecha anachnu modim," etc. It appears specifically in Modim which is not language of conversation. And the same applies to "B'chol komah lefanecha tishtachaveh" which is only a manner of narrative; but in "Va'anachnu korim u'mishtachavim," etc., in "Bau lanu l'shabeach" one may chat, for since it says "va'anachnu korim u'mishtachavim," etc., if he does not bow and prostrate, then it appears as if he is lying when he reads. Derishah. And similarly one should not bow on Motza'ei Shabbat when saying "Hishtachavu laHashem b'hadrat kodesh." Orach Chayyim or Aleph:
