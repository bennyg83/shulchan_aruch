That one should not bow at "U'lecha anachnu modim." And in it one
seif: One may not chat at "U'lecha anachnu modim" — one may not chat except in places where the Sages said:And the custom is that on Shabbat they come later to the synagogue than on weekdays, because regarding the weekday tamid it says "baboker" and regarding Shabbat it says "uv'yom haShabbat" which implies delay (Mordechai end of chapter 22). And the custom is to increase the Shabbat zemirot, each place according to its custom. In any matter, if they did not say them, they do not return him, except if he did not say "L'Kel asher shavat,"' etc. — they return him. And one may extend and make the zemirot pleasant, and one may not protest against one who prolongs them even though the protester intends because of bitul Torah (Or Zarua). Nevertheless, on Shabbat and Yom Tov one should not extend too much so they eat before the sixth hour, as below siman 288.
