That one should not bow at 'U'lecha anachnu modim.' It contains 1 seif: One should not bow at 'U'lecha anachnu modim,' because one may bow only in the places established by the Sages. {Rama: The custom on Shabbos is to come to the synagogue later than on weekdays, because regarding the weekday tamid it says 'in the morning,' while regarding Shabbos it says 'and on the Shabbos day,' which implies delay. [Mordechai, end of chapter 22] The custom is also to increase the Shabbos zemirot, each place according to its custom. If any of them were omitted, one does not return, except that if he omitted 'LaKel asher shavat,' etc., he returns. One may prolong and beautify the zemirot, and one should not protest against a person who prolongs them even if the protest is motivated by concern for bitul Torah. [Or Zarua] Nevertheless, on Shabbos and Yom Tov one should not prolong them excessively, so that they eat before the sixth hour, as stated below in siman 288.}
