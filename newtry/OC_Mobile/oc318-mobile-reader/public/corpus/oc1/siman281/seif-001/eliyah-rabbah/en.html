[Levush] And so is the practice, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov writes that the people customarily rise later on Yom Tov as well, including sleeping longer in the morning, because this is pleasurable to them. Bach's principal explanation is that morning sleep is itself oneg Shabbos and the verse is only a support. If so, the same should apply on Yom Tov, where one is obligated in enjoyment as on Shabbos; see siman 529. This requires study. Since on Yom Tov people rise early because of the work of preparing the meal, as below in siman 529, those who do not need to prepare may sleep later for oneg Yom Tov.
