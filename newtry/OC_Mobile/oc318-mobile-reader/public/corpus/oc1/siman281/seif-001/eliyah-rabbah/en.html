(1) [Levush] And so is the custom, etc. But the people are accustomed to extend on Yom Tov also in the morning sleep and it is pleasure for them (Malbush Yom Tov). And in truth, what Bach wrote — that the main reason is because of morning sleep which is oneg Shabbat, but they relied on a verse — if so, the same law applies to Yom Tov, for one is obligated to enjoy it on Yom Tov as on Shabbat, see siman 529; and it requires study.
