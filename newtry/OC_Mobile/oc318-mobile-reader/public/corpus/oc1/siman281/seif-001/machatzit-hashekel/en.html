(s.k. 1) From weekday. But nevertheless, etc., from Yom Tov, etc., see siman 529 where he wrote there that on Yom Tov they delay coming to the synagogue so they have time to prepare their food for the morning meal, and afterward they read Shema — even though it is at its time, nevertheless the time is compressed; unlike Shabbat when even melacha of ochel nefesh is forbidden, and therefore everything is prepared from erev Shabbat and one need not toil again in anything; therefore it is good to hurry to come to the synagogue so they read Shema and the time for Shema will be ample:
