1) [Seif 1] One should not bow during VeLach Anachnu Modim, etc. It appears that this applies specifically to Modim, which is not an expression of bowing. The same applies to the words, 'Every upright being shall bow before You,' which are merely a narrative statement. During 'But we bow, prostrate ourselves,' etc., in Aleinu, however, one should bow. If one says, 'But we bow and prostrate ourselves,' etc., without bowing or prostrating, his conduct appears false. Derishah. Similarly, one should not bow on Shabbos night while saying, 'Bow before Hashem in the splendor of holiness.' Orach Chaim, paragraph 1.
