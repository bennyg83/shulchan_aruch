(א) They return him. That is, that he should say it after the prayer; unlike the other zemirot which are only optional — if he wishes, he need not say them at all. Magen Avraham:

(ב) And to make them pleasant. Specifically with a melody that has sweetness, as is done in the Pesukei deZimra; and the ancients already spoke at length in condemnation of cantors who prolong and separate a letter from its companion. Nevertheless, they should be careful to read and pray at the proper time — Bach.
