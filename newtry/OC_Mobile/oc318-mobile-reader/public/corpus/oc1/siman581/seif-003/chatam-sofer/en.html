There, s.k. 7: Even the entire day. Note: Kol Bo and Darkei Moshe state ‘the evening service preceding it,’ etc. Accordingly, Magen Avraham’s wording implies the evening service following it, and he disagrees with them. In my humble opinion, however, one can say that the evening service following it properly relates to the preceding Mincha, because the evening service corresponds to the limbs and sacrificial fats left over from the afternoon Tamid offering. This is easy to understand.
