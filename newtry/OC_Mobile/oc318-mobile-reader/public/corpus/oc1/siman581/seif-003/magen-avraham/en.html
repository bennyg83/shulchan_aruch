In the watch [of the night]. Because they are accustomed to finish before dawn (Maharil); that is to say, since most of the time they are accustomed to finish before dawn, they enacted to fall [in Tachanun] even if on one occasion it continues into the day; they still fall. And so is implied in the Levush; and also regarding prayer it is not called day until sunrise.
And they do not sound [the shofar]. To make a break between the discretionary soundings and the obligatory soundings; therefore they stop on the twenty-eighth day (Levush). It is implied that also on the night beforehand one should not sound; and nevertheless in his house it is permitted to sound in order to practice, for it is enough that he stops in the synagogue. But in the Minhagim the reason is written: to confound the Satan; if so, one should not sound at all.
