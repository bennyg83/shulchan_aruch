Accepted by the congregation; see Taz; and not to lead the prayer by force; and see Eliyah Rabbah. And he must intend to fulfill the obligation of each one. And if he has an enemy and intends not to fulfill that one's obligation, nothing avails. And a mourner, if there is none as proper as he, it would also seem that a mourner leads the prayer; but regarding a father or mother within twelve months, one may say that regarding other relatives they are equal in this, and one has no advantage over his fellow; and see siman 660, letter 2: one may say he grows the hair of his head thirty days, and the measure of rebuke in Yoreh Deah; see there.
