(22) They do not fall upon their faces, etc.: for it is like other eves of Yom Tov.
(23) Even though they fall upon their faces: that is, even if it continues into the day; because most of the time they are accustomed to finish before dawn, they enacted to fall [in Tachanun] even if on one occasion it continues into the day.
(24) And they do not sound [the shofar], etc.: to make a break between the discretionary soundings and the obligatory soundings [and even when the first day of Rosh Hashana falls on Shabbos, they also do not sound on erev Shabbos]. And nevertheless it is permitted to sound in order to practice, for it is enough that he stops in the synagogue; but in the Minhagim the reason is written: in order to confound the Satan; if so, one should not sound at all. And in Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Sefer Amarkol it is implied that to practice is permitted in a closed room.
