They wash [clothes]. And in any case one should not wear on Rosh Hashana embroidered garments and silk as on other Yamim Tovim, so that the fear of judgment be upon him (Taz).
To immerse. The Maharil immersed himself three times on erev Rosh Hashana, and see there. The Shelah wrote: one should not have marital relations on the night of Rosh Hashana, but if it is the night of immersion he is obligated to fulfill his marital duty, and he should immerse again in the morning. And in Kenesses HaGedolah he wrote: one should not have marital relations on both nights unless it was a mitzvah immersion that she could not perform beforehand; then it is permitted, and he should immerse again in the morning; and see what Yad Aharon wrote. And likewise he should not conduct himself with his wife in jest and frivolity.
The graves. For the cemetery is a place of rest for the righteous, and therefore it is a holy and pure place, and prayer is accepted more there; and one who prays at the graves of the righteous should not set his aim toward the dead, but should ask of Hashem, may He be blessed, that He grant him mercy by the merit of the righteous who dwell in the dust (Maharil). And he should circle the graves and give charity before saying the Tachanunim (see Shelah). And one should not go to one grave twice in one day (Arizal). And see what I wrote at the end of siman 559.
