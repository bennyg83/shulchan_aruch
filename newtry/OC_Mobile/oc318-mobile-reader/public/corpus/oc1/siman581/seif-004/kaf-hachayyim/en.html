4) One who rises during the final watch of the night and finds that the congregation is already reciting Selichos, and there is not enough time to recite both Tikkun Chatzos and Selichos, so that he must omit one of them, should preferably recite Tikkun Chatzos according to the order of the Arizal. It is a far greater and more essential matter than Selichos. Ramaz in a manuscript responsum; Birkei Yosef, os 3; Shaarei Teshuvah, ibid. It has now been printed in Igros HaRamaz, siman 77; Machazik Berachah, os 1. We already wrote this above as well, in siman 1, os 16, in the name of several later authorities, see there.
