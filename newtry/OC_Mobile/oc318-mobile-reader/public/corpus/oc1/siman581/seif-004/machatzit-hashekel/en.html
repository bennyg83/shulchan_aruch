(s.k. 4) And a mourner, etc. It is forbidden to lead the prayers on the High Holy Days. And he wrote in the sefer Eliyah Rabbah at the end of siman 582 that specifically on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur it is forbidden; but on the Selichos days, even "Zechor Bris" of erev Rosh Hashana, he may lead the prayer. And likewise on days when Tachanun is not said it is permitted to lead the prayer even at Shacharis if another will say Hallel; but Musaf he should not lead. And on Chanukah he should not lead on the first night because they say Shehecheyanu over the Chanukah lights, and for this extra joy is needed; see there further (and perhaps if another kindles, he is permitted to lead the prayer).
However, where there is no other it is permitted. So it is in the Responsa of the Maharil, siman 128. But in the Darkei Moshe he wrote: if there is none better than he, it is permitted; and this was also cited in the Taz.
