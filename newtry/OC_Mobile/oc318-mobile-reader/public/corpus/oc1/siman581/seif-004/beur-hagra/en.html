Se'if 4. They wash [clothes] and cut hair. Yerushalmi, first chapter of Rosh Hashana; and Midrash Rabbah: Rabbi Simon said, it is written "For what great nation is there that has righteous statutes and ordinances," etc. Rabbi Chanina bar Chanina and Rabbi Hoshaya: one said, which nation is like this nation? The way of the world is that a person who knows he has a judgment wears black and wraps himself in black and grows his beard, for he does not know how his judgment will come out; but Israel are not so; rather they wear white and wrap themselves in white and eat and drink and rejoice, and they know that the Holy One, Blessed be He, performs miracles for them. And the Rosh cited it there, and the Mordechai there, and Hagahos Maimoniyos and the Tur.
