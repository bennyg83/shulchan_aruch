And therefore one should not have marital relations on the night of Rosh Hashana; but if it is the night of immersion he is obligated to fulfill his marital duty, and he should immerse again in the morning (Shelah). And in Kenesses HaGedolah he wrote: one should not have marital relations on both nights unless it was a mitzvah immersion that she could not perform beforehand; then it is permitted, and he should immerse again in the morning.
