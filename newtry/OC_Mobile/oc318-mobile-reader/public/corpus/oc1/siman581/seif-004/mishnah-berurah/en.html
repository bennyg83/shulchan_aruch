(25) They wash [clothes] and cut hair on erev Rosh Hashana: to show that we are confident in His kindness, may He be blessed, that He will bring forth our judgment to righteousness. And nevertheless one should not wear on Rosh Hashana embroidered garments and silk as on other Yamim Tovim, so that there be fear of the judgment; rather he should wear nice white garments (Taz in the name of the Maharshal). And see in the Magen Avraham, siman 597, that in a place where they are not accustomed to wear white, in any case he should not wear garments so important.
(26) They are accustomed to immerse, etc.: and if he cannot immerse because of cold, he should see to pouring nine kavim of water upon his body. And he should not advance to immerse until an hour before midday (Chayei Adam). Some later authorities wrote that it is proper to refrain from marital relations on both nights of Rosh Hashana unless it was a night of immersion, for then he is obligated to fulfill his marital duty, and he should immerse again in the morning. However, those people who eat to satiety of their belly, or Heaven forfend if thought of a woman enters his mind, it is better that he have marital relations rather than, Heaven forfend, come to sin, and he should immerse again in the morning (Chayei Adam).
(27) The graves: for the cemetery is a place of rest for the righteous, and prayer is accepted there more; but he should not set his aim toward the dead; rather he should ask of Hashem, may He be blessed, that He grant him mercy by the merit of the righteous who dwell in the dust; and he should circle the graves and give charity before saying the Tachanunim. And one should not go to one grave twice in one day.
