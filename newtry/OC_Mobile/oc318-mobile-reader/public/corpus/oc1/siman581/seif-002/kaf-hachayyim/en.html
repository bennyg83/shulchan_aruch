It is customary to rise during the final watch of the night, etc. Places where it is customary to recite Selichos during Maariv follow a bad and bitter custom; it should be forgotten and not mentioned. It is not proper to mention the 13 Attributes except at an auspicious time, and those who recite them during Maariv come close to causing a severance, God forbid.
