They may eat. See the Responsa, Yoreh Deah, siman 344, where he concluded that circumcision does not override an obligatory fast, and even the Fast of Esther and erev Rosh Hashana; but [it does override] Behab after Pesach and after Sukkos, and the Ten Days of Repentance. And it goes without saying regarding the firstborns' fast on erev Pesach; and likewise a yahrzeit for a baal bris and a sandak are pushed aside. And he further concludes that since it is a mitzvah upon him to eat, it is forbidden for him to fast, and especially a yahrzeit, which has no mention at all in the Talmud, except that annulment is needed since it is only because of custom. And likewise he concludes that a baal bris should not say piyutim and Tachanunim with the congregation; and see in the Responsa of the Maharshal.
