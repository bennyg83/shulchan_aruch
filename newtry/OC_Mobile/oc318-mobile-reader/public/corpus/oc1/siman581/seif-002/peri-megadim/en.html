And if; see Taz. The Ten Days will be short two [days] of Rosh Hashana on which they do not fast; and if the first day falls on Shabbos there are also two Shabbosos among the Ten Days. And for a tallis he should take another's; see siman 14 in the Taz, [s.k.] 4. And regarding the blessing over the Torah, see siman 6, letter 5, where he somewhat resolves; and in the Magen Avraham [s.k.] 7 there, that it is proper not to say the blessings twice.
