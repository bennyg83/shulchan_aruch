1) [Seif 1] It is customary to rise during the final watch of the night, etc. This is because the Holy One, blessed is He, traverses 18,000 worlds, and at the end of the night He traverses this world, and it is an auspicious time. Hagahos Minhagim. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. See above, siman 1, end of os 10, and siman 238, os 3, and examine carefully.
2) Ibid. It is customary to rise during the final watch of the night, etc. Places where it is customary to recite Selichos during Maariv follow a bad and bitter custom; it should be forgotten and not mentioned. It is not proper to mention the 13 Attributes except at an auspicious time, and those who recite them during Maariv come close to causing a severance, G-d forbid. Responsa of Ramaz, siman 30; Birkei Yosef, os 1; Shaarei Teshuvah on seif 1. So too was written above, siman 131, os 26, see there. See further above, siman 237, os 8.
3) One who sits in a synagogue where it is customary to recite Selichos during Maariv should sit silently, and there is no concern of "you shall not form separate factions." He may, if he wishes, recite chapters of Tehillim and the like. He may also recite the confession, except on Motzei Shabbos, when it is forbidden to recite it until after midnight, since the sanctity of Shabbos still remains until then; this is what we received in the name of the Arizal. Ramaz in the aforementioned responsum; Birkei Yosef, os 2; Shaarei Teshuvah, ibid. So too was written above, siman 131, os 14, see there. However, regarding what he wrote that one may recite chapters of Tehillim if he wishes, see above, siman 237, os 9, where we wrote in the name of the Arizal that it is not proper to read Scripture at night, see there. Therefore, one should study the Oral Torah.
4) One who rises during the final watch of the night and finds that the congregation is already reciting Selichos, and there is not enough time to recite both Tikkun Chatzos and Selichos, so that he must omit one of them, should preferably recite Tikkun Chatzos according to the order of the Arizal. It is a far greater and more essential matter than Selichos. Ramaz in a manuscript responsum; Birkei Yosef, os 3; Shaarei Teshuvah, ibid. It has now been printed in Igros HaRamaz, siman 77; Machazik Berachah, os 1. We already wrote this above as well, in siman 1, os 16, in the name of several later authorities, see there.
