From Rosh Chodesh Elul it appears to me [to begin] the day after Rosh Chodesh, for on Rosh Chodesh one should not say Selichos and Tachanunim, but rather the day after Rosh Chodesh (Responsa of Maharam of Padua, siman 79). And repentance with a whole heart avails for all transgressions in the Torah, even idolatry, forbidden relations, and bloodshed. And the reason that they stand, in any case, for four days before Rosh Hashana each time is because regarding all the offerings it is said "and you shall offer," and regarding Rosh Hashana it is said "and you shall make," that a person should make himself an offering on Rosh Hashana; and therefore four days of inspection are needed because of blemishes, and through fasting he examines and inspects, similar to an offering (Zichron Moshe).
And they should be particular to seek out a prayer leader, etc. And they were accustomed that on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur the trustees, the faithful leaders who shield their generation, pass before the ark (Mordechai and Rokeach). And in any case he must be acceptable and welcomed by the entire congregation. And if through this they come to quarrel and strife, it is proper to repair this breach so that no one pray except the fixed prayer leader who is accustomed to lead the prayers all year; and especially in small communities it is proper to abolish this custom entirely, for from this they come to quarrels and strife (my teacher in the Bach, siman 619). And the prayer leader, or whoever was selected by the congregation to lead the prayer or to sound [the shofar], must separate himself for three days before Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur from all things that bring to impurity, and they need purity all the more so the one who sounds [the shofar]; and likewise it is proper during those three days to check whether he is fit to lead the prayer, whether because of some flaw in him, or because he does not know the intent of the prayer properly, in which case one must teach him, or because of the purity of his body regarding immersion and the like (Zohar, Parashas Vayikra, folio 25). One who engaged in illicit relations is not fit to be the prayer leader on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur unless he has done repentance (Toras Emes).
