[1] [Levush] Rosh Chodesh Elul, etc. Once Elul has entered, when a person writes a letter to his fellow, he must hint at the beginning that he seeks for him a good year (Maharil). When one fasts from Rosh Chodesh Elul onward, he should ask of Hashem, may He be blessed, that He grant him authority, and life, and children for His service; when the Sefer Torah is taken out he should say the Thirteen Attributes three times, and also "And as for me, my prayer is to You, Hashem, at a favorable time" three times (Mahari of Ria). Those who fast forty days should not fast on Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh, and to complete forty days he should begin from the fifteenth of Av (there); and the Shelah wrote to begin from the seventeenth of Tammuz to fast on scattered days, and days when he already fasts anyway, such as Tishah B'Av and erev Rosh Chodesh and a yahrzeit and a fast for a dream, do not count toward the total, but within Elul itself everything counts, until here. But in the Magen Avraham at the end of siman 568 he wrote simply that everything counts, and this requires further study, that he did not mention the words of the Shelah cited above. The Magen Avraham wrote in siman 562 that those forty fasts need not be completed, for they fast because of repentance. "Aryeh" is an acronym: Elul, Rosh Hashana, Yom Kippur, Hoshanah Rabbah; "Shall a lion roar and not fear?" At these times, for this month is a time prepared for those who repent; therefore one who fears the word of Hashem throughout this entire month should sit before his meal and before he sleeps and be astonished and examine his deeds and confess them, and he shall return and be healed (Mateh Moshe). "A man to his fellow and gifts to the poor" hints at gifts to the poor in Elul (Sefer Amarkol).
