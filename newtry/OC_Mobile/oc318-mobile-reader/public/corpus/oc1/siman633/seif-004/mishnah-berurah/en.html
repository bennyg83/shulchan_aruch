(10) He reduced it with straw. But if he spread grain over the ground, it does not help even if he nullifies it, for his opinion is nullified, as with cushions and pillows.
(11) And he nullified it. See above, siman 358:2, where there is a dispute about this: some say specifically when he nullifies it forever, and some are lenient that it is enough when he nullifies it for that Shabbos. Here too, regarding sukkah, according to this it is enough if he nullifies it for all the days of the festival, not to move it from its place; the later authorities inclined to be lenient in this.
(12) This is a reduction. And he need not shake the s'chach anew, even though it was invalid before he reduced its height, as is proven above in siman 626:2 in the gloss; see there.
(13) Until he nullifies it verbally. See above, siman 372:16, in Mishnah Berurah, small paragraph 21, that some say nullification in the heart is also sufficient. The later authorities wrote that it is forbidden to reduce it on Yom Tov, for this is like building, as above in siman 343:10; and furthermore, he repairs the sukkah through this and makes it valid, and it is forbidden like the case of the myrtle in siman 646; see there.
