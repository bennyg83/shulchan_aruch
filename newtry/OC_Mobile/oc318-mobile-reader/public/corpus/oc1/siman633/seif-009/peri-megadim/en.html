Even. See Turei Zahav and Sukkah 4b, like Rava there. Regarding what Rashi wrote there, seven by seven and something more, see Maharsha, of blessed memory, and Pnei Yehoshua: specifically seven and something more, for it is fit for dwelling even according to Abaye; it is not reasonable to say that we require an interior space of seven by seven; see there, and see siman 345 in Turei Zahav, letter 2. Pnei Yehoshua there inclined to say that by Torah law one can say "extend upward" in sukkah, and it is rabbinically that we require recognizable partitions; see there, for he discussed it at length. From the Levush it implies that by Torah law it must be like a dwelling; see there. The practical difference is if one has no other sukkah except one like this: he must sit in it. See siman 629 in Magen Avraham, letter 22, that regarding all rabbinic disqualifications, when he has no other, see there. As for what he wrote, that if "extend upward" is a law given to Moshe at Sinai only for Shabbos, why does the beginning of HaZorek ask where carrying out is written, since it is a law given to Moshe at Sinai? See the Ran in chapter Klal Gadol, that one cannot punish based on a law given to Moshe at Sinai unless it is attached to the written Torah; and we would have said that the law given to Moshe at Sinai is only for prohibition. Or perhaps passing from a private domain to a private domain through the public domain is a primary category and is included in carrying out, as the Ran, of blessed memory, wrote there and in the Yerushalmi. And perhaps carrying out is an inferior labor, but passing is not an inferior labor, and the law given to Moshe at Sinai concerns passing. Understand this; this is not its place.
