(26) And small branches, etc. One might have thought it valid because the principal sechach is not below ten tefachim; only its insignificant ends, which do not contain the required amount of sechach. The ruling teaches that it is nevertheless invalid, because ultimately it is difficult to live in such a sukkah and it is a degraded dwelling.
(27) The decorations, etc., do not invalidate it. Since they decorate the sukkah, they are subordinate to it and do not make it a degraded dwelling. The poskim explain that the same applies if he suspended utensils and the like.
