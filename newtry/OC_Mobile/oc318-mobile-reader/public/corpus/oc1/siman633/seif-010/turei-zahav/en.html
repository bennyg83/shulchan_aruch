If there is not between the dug-out area, etc. For then the dug-out area has the sukkah's walls through lavud. Our master the Tur wrote about this that the validation applies specifically inside the dug-out area and not outside it. This is difficult: why is it different from a sukkah that was twenty high and he built a platform, where in se'if 5 we validate the entire platform? Why should we not say the same here, that the section outside it is like s'chach extending out from the sukkah, as above? So the Beis Yosef asked, and for this reason he did not mention here that the validation is specifically inside the dug-out area. But in truth this is not difficult at all, for here it is different, since the invalidity is because it is a foul dwelling. We say that even if he wants to sit there in that section, he will certainly leave there immediately, as we say in siman 629:14 regarding s'chach whose smell is bad. So too here, since the space is narrow for him there, the validation of the dug-out area therefore does not help for the area outside it.
And there is between them, etc. We do not say here "bent wall," for we say "bent wall" only when there is already a wall, but it has a disqualification. This is unlike here, where it is not ten high and does not have the name of a wall; and through the bending you wish to make it into a wall. That we do not say.
