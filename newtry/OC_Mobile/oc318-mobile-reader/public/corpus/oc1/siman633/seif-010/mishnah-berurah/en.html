(28) And he hollowed it out to complete ten. The same applies if the sechach was thick and he removed some from below until the sukkah became ten tefachim high [Magen Avraham].
(29) Three tefachim; it is valid. We apply lavud, treating the walls as though they reach the hollow. Nevertheless, some poskim are stringent against eating or sleeping outside the hollow, whether toward the entrance or toward the walls; they do not validate it through the hollow as above with a central platform, because that area ultimately remains a degraded dwelling, being less than ten tefachim high.
(30) If there are three tefachim between them, it is invalid. It is not validated through a bent wall; that is, by treating the wall as though it bends above to the point opposite the hollow; because anything less than ten tefachim high is not a wall, and the laws of walls do not apply to it.
