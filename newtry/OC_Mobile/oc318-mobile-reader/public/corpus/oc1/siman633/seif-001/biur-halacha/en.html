Higher than twenty amos. See Mishnah Berurah and Sha'ar HaTziyun. I found it proper to copy what the book Ma'aseh Roke'ach wrote in chapter 4 of Hilchos Sukkah in the name of Rabbi Avraham, the son of the Rambam, and this is his language: "The finger mentioned is the thumb of an average man in build, not one dwarfed in his limbs and not one large in his limbs, and not young in years, but one of average build who has reached full growth, and that is at thirty-five years old or more." These are his words. This has practical relevance for several matters.
Even if all its s'chach is above twenty. See Darkei Moshe in siman 363, letter 7, who wrote: It appears from the words of the Rif and Rabbi Yehonasan at the beginning of Eruvin that if it was originally above twenty and he came to reduce it, he should place the beam so that it is included within twenty, etc. This is practically relevant to our matter as well. However, all the poskim did not distinguish in this way. Also, from what he himself concluded, "so that its interior space should be twenty," it does not imply this. In the book Gaon Yaakov he wrote that it is preferable to say that the books became corrupted, and in the Rif it should read: from the edge of twenty and upward.
