In Taz, s.k. 2: It follows according to what we rule, etc., even if that portion has more sun than shade, etc. Certainly there is a dispute about this, for Tosafos explicitly wrote that if it had more sun than shade, those other Amoraim would not have validated it. See Tosafos in Eruvin and Tosafos in Sukkah 9, s.v. ‘for behold.’
