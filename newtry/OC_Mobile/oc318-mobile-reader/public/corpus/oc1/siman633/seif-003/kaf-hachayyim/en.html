8) [Seif 3] And he suspended decorative items in it, etc. They do not reduce the height, because they are not a type of s'chach. Rashi; Magen Avraham, s.k. 3. This refers to illustrated sheets. Sukkah decorations made from grasses and flowers, however, which are fit for s'chach, do reduce the height. Mishbetzos Zahav, os 3. The Levush, however, implies that such items are not regarded as s'chach, and even a type of valid s'chach does not reduce the height when placed there for decoration. Eshel Avraham, os 3; Mishna Berurah, os 7.
9) Ibid. They do not reduce the height. This applies even if their shade is greater than their sunlight. Levush.
10) Ibid. Similarly, if he reduced its height with cushions, etc. This means that he placed cushions and bedding on the ground below to reduce its interior height. Even if he nullified them there permanently, they do not reduce the height, because people do not ordinarily nullify such items. His intention is therefore disregarded in comparison with the conduct of all people. Levush.
