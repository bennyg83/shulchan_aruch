(7) It is not considered a reduction. Even if, through them, their shade is greater than their sunlight. The reason is that they are not a type of s'chach, for example if he adorned it with decorated cloths. Some say that even if he reduced it with grasses and flowers that are valid for s'chach, it is also not considered a reduction as long as he hung them only to beautify the sukkah and not for shade; the name of s'chach is not upon them, as below in siman 635.
(8) He reduced it with cushions, etc. Meaning that he placed them on its floor to raise it.
(9) It is not considered a reduction. For his opinion is nullified in relation to everyone, since no person ruins his property and leaves his cushions in earth and stones and nullifies them.
