(א) And he hung pleasant things on it, etc., this is not a reduction — see Mishna Berurah that I copied that it is not a species of sechach, and this is from Magen Avraham in the name of Rashi; and see in Peri Megadim that from Rashi it appears if he decorated with grasses and flowers they reduce, and he wrote that from Levush the opposite appears; but in truth as Rashi's words are explained in several rishonim in Shulchan Aruch and Or Zarua, and so explicitly in Yerushalmi chapter 1 halachah 1: if it was above twenty amot and he hung decorations that are fit for sechach on it, they reduce it to kosher size; see there, in the commentators. However it is possible Rashi also admits if he hung them only for decoration and not for shade they cannot reduce since there is no sechach over them in that manner, as proven below in siman 635, and Yerushalmi does not deal with that case.
