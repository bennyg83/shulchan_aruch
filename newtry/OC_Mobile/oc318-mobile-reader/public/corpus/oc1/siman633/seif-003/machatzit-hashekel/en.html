(Small paragraph 3) It is not considered a reduction. "For they are not a type, etc." This is marked on cushions and pillows, and it is a mistake, for there the reason is that his opinion is nullified relative to everyone, since no person nullifies cushions and pillows. Rather, Rashi wrote this reason regarding sukkah decorations, where he nullifies them, and therefore he had to say that it is because they are not a type of s'chach. Therefore the words of the Magen Avraham must refer to the beginning of the se'if, where it says that he hung decorative items in it, etc.
