Decorative items. See Turei Zahav, siman 627, letter 5. This refers to decorated sheets, and here too they do not reduce the measure. But sukkah decorations made of grasses and roses, which are fit for s'chach, do reduce the measure in a sukkah; see Rashi, Sukkah 10b, regarding sukkah decorations. In Magen Avraham, letter 3, it will be explained, with Hashem's help. What the Levush wrote, that decorative items are considered as s'chach, requires analysis.
