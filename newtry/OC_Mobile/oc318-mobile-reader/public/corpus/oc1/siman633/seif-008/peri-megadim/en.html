More than ten. See Turei Zahav. The language is unclear, and from the Levush it implies two reasons. One is that with sukkah we do not say that another domain creates "extend upward," as I wrote below in letter 9, with Hashem's help. Another is: what difference does it make that it is another domain? Nevertheless we say "bent wall" and they join. See the Rosh and Beis Yosef in the name of the Ran, who holds that there is room to be uncertain like Riatz Geyus, who invalidates when it is ten handbreadths high, because we do not say "bent wall"; see there. [And see Rashi, Sukkah 4b, s.v. u-vanah, "far away," which is not a proof. See Pnei Yehoshua there; one can say that according to Rava we do not say "extend upward" rabbinically, and there is some support from the Ran, of blessed memory. With Hashem's help, in letter 9 this will be explained.]
