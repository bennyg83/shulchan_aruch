From what was higher than twenty and built in it a platform that validates — Rashi even from the platform and above — end of his words. Therefore Shulchan Aruch wrote plainly implying even outside the dug area is valid unlike Tur; Bach settled Tur's view and agreed for law. He distinguished this case from the law outside the platform: invalid material protruding from the wall is not validated unless there are complete partitions and valid sechach, only there is a defect as explained siman 531 seif 6-7; therefore outside the platform one could say complete partitions and valid sechach even though it is above twenty — nevertheless the law of valid sechach applies and as Magen Avraham wrote above small seif 5 in the name of Rosh therefore we judge it as invalid material protruding from the wall. But here outside the dug area since it is not ten high there are no partitions at all — we do not judge invalid material protruding from the wall.
