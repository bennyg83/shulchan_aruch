(22) It is valid upon the platform. As above, through the principle of a bent wall. This teaches that in our case the principle is applied simultaneously on several sides.
(23) Even if it is high, etc. We do not say that at such a height it constitutes an independent domain and the walls have no relation to it.
(24) Even if the platform is ten tefachim high. This excludes the Gemara's initial thought that at such a height it does not need the sukkah's walls at all, since it has its own walls through the principle of gud asik mechitzta; we extend the platform's edge upward, making its sides the sukkah's walls. The ruling teaches that we do not say this, because a sukkah requires recognizable walls.
