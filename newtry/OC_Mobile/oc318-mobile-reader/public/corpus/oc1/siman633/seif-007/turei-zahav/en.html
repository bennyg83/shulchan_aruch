The platform in the middle. He teaches us that even from two sides we say "bent wall."
More than ten. We do not say that then the platform is a different domain.
Even if the platform is ten high. Meaning that Abaye holds: since there is a valid sukkah on the top of that platform, which has seven by seven and a little more, extend the partition upward over the platform, around the heights surrounding its top, and it is considered a sukkah on its top; the sides of the platform are its walls through the principle of extending upward, and the s'chach above is valid for it and is not above twenty from the top of the platform. Just as we say regarding Shabbos, extend upward, Rava teaches us that we do not say this regarding sukkah, for we require recognizable partitions like a dwelling, and they are not present. Even though in siman 630:8 we say that the edge of a roof descends and seals, the edge of a roof is indeed considered recognizable partitions; so wrote Tosafos.
