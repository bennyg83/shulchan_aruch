Less than four amos. For if there are four amos from the edge of the platform to the second wall, from which he distanced it, it is invalid, for this platform has only two walls: the one beside which it was placed, and the second wall which the head of the platform reaches. But less than four amos is valid, for it too is validated by a bent wall, and we view the upper s'chach connected to the distant wall as though it is part of the wall itself and bends until opposite the edge of the platform. Here we measure its height from the edge of the platform upward, and even though beneath it the space continues outward, since its top does not end before this point, this is the height of its interior space. Thus its interior space has been reduced beneath it. But the entire sukkah is not valid here, for it has no s'chach, since we view the s'chach as though it is a bent wall.
