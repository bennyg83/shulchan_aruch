Seif katan 6. “At the end of siman 632,” etc. This refers to Magen Avraham there, seif katan 4, and to what we wrote there in the second discussion. Although we apply the principle of a bent wall, the inner area is nevertheless valid. To this he writes that there, regarding the inner area, we do not apply a bent wall at all. Here, however, you must necessarily invoke the principle of a bent wall.
