Upon the platform specifically. For what is outside the platform has no s'chach, since we view the s'chach as though it were a bent wall [the Rosh]. This is not comparable to what is written in siman 632:2, for there the s'chach is valid and rests below twenty; therefore, even though we say "bent wall," it is permitted to sit beneath it, for the one sitting beneath it considers it s'chach. But here, you are coming to validate it by force of "s'chach that extends from the sukkah," and if so, you must say that it is like a wall. Examine this carefully. As for the fact that at the end of siman 631 he wrote that it is permitted to sleep under the walls because it is like s'chach extending from the sukkah, one must say that since there is a tent in one place, it is as though all of it is a tent, as the Rosh wrote; if so, it is as though all of it is ten high.
