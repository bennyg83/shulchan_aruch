(14) Platform. This is a structure of stones or wood, and the same applies to a floor laid over the ground. When measuring from the top of the platform and upward, there is not more than twenty amos until the s'chach.
(15) Opposite, etc. This means adjacent to the wall itself.
(16) The middle. This is the wall opposite the opening, and it is called this because a standard sukkah has only three walls, and the fourth is entirely open.
(17) Across its entire face. It is therefore also touching the two walls on either side, so it has three walls.
(18) Even from the platform and beyond. One may eat and sleep there. The reason is that we view that platform as though it extends throughout the entire sukkah, as we say similarly above in siman 631:7 regarding poles that protrude toward the open side; see there.
