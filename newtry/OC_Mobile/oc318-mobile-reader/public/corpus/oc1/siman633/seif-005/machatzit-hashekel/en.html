(Small paragraph 5) From the platform, etc. "Since in truth, etc." He wrote this to answer the difficulty of Rabbi Yeshayah of Trani, who asked: if so, when there is, in the place of valid s'chach, the measure of the extension of a sukkah, and from there onward it is covered with invalid s'chach, will it be valid under the invalid s'chach because of "s'chach that extends from the sukkah"? Rather, perforce one must say that "s'chach that extends from the sukkah" applies only when it has walls and valid s'chach, except that there is some deficiency in them; in that case it is validated through "s'chach that extends from the sukkah," as explained above in siman 631, se'ifim 6 and 7. To this the Rosh answered that here too, above four, it is intrinsically valid s'chach, only the location causes it disqualification. This is the reasoning of Rabbeinu Tam, brought above in Magen Avraham, siman 628, small paragraph 3.
