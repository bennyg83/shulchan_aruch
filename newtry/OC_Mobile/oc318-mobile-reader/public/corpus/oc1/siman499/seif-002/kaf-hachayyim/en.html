2) [Se'if 2] It is forbidden to scald a kid, etc. This means placing it in boiling water to remove its hair. See Yoreh Deah, siman 68, se'if 10, which states that kids, lambs, and birds may not be scalded in a kli rishon unless their forbidden fats were removed and they were salted. See there.
