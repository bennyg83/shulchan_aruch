Unless he scalds it. He wrote this according to a corrupted reading that happened to be before him in the Tur; but the correct reading is that even if he wishes to eat the skin, it is forbidden to scald; so wrote the Bach and Yam Shel Shlomo and Hagahot Maimoniyot; and so wrote Tosafos, the Rosh, Rabbeinu Yeruham, and Mordechai; and so is implied in the Tur and in Shulchan Aruch, siman 500, se'if 4, where he wrote that they scald the head and the legs, which implies that [for] the whole animal it is forbidden to scald; and the Bach wrote in the name of Semak that a chicken may be entirely scalded, for it is impossible to flay it.
