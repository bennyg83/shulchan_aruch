(א) [Seif 1] One may not flay by habit on Yom Tov etc. — Rambam chapter 3 law 6 wording. Rashi explained Bechoros 33a to flay by legs — whole hide for bellows or honey/oil sellers who put in whole flayed sheep hides. Beit Yosef: from Rambam seems whatever not flaying pulling all meat from one leg permitted though whole hide front and back; Rashi implies on Yom Tov forbidden whole hide however started — only habit forbidden, if from mouth toward tail permitted not habit. Bach: per Beit Yosef explanation even flaying from legs toward head forbidden on Yom Tov, especially head to legs; conclude practice should be stringent all cases whole hide as Tur. Bach, Knesset HaGedolah Hagahot Beit Yosef, Magen Avraham sk 1, Taz sk 1, Atzei Arazim, R' Zalman, Machatzit HaShekel siman 14 in Lekutei Rima sk 1 — however Peri Chadash wrote to lighten as Rambam — only habit forbidden; tearing hide between two legs leg to leg then cutting all sides knife or hand until whole hide comes off in circuit permitted, not Bach; Shulchan Gavoah end sk 1 on Peri Chadash for practice — truth we accepted Shulchan Aruch rulings especially since Bach stringent all cases. Dispute — prohibition is exertion; if not habitual no exertion, lighten Rambam and followers; forbid only pulling meat from one leg where all agree extra exertion; but unhabitual great exertion forbid all cases whole hide.
