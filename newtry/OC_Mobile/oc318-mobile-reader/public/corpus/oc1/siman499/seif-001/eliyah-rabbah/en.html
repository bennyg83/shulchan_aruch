[1] [Levush] And its legs, etc. The language of the Shulchan Aruch is "from one leg"; and see Rambam, chapter 3 of Hilchos Yom Tov, and the Beis Yosef (and he himself in the Kesef Mishnah) inferred from this that in another manner it is permitted, for specifically by way of one leg there is much toil; and the Levush, who used "its legs" in the plural, is the language of Rashi; but Rashi is not particular about taking flesh out by way of one leg; and in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 360 he wrote Rashi's language alone; and this requires further study also regarding the Shulchan Aruch, who here wrote in the language of the Rambam, and there wrote the language of Rashi. The Taz wrote: It is astonishing regarding the Rama that he did not bring the Tur's view, who wrote plainly that one should not flay a whole hide; end quote. And in Avodas HaKodesh of the Rashba and in Kol Bo I saw [it] in the language of the Rambam; and it appears to me that for this reason the Rambam ruled not like the Bach, who is stringent in every case.
