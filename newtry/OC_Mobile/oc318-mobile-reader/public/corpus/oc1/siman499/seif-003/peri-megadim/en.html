It is permitted. See Taz: that which concerns a dangerous animal is not like in siman 498, se'if 6. And for me, the poor one, there is some intent here, for at first glance one can say two reasons are needed: that it is somewhat fit to recline upon under pressure when moist, and also "their end for the sake of their beginning," regarding what he wrote in siman 498, se'if 4, that that [teaching] about a hide before those who tread is swapped; and therefore the reason is that it is fit to recline under pressure, together with "their end"; see Peri Chadash; see Beitzah 11a-b. But the Taz, according to his own view in siman 498 [s.k.] 12, after the fact one can rely on Rabbeinu Chananel that [the teaching about] one who slaughters is swapped; and if so, a hide, even though it is not fit to recline upon at all, is permitted at least to lift in handling from sun to shade and the like; but for treaders, one can say we need the reason of reclining together with "their end," as in the Gemara; and for this reason, bird feathers: only handling alone was permitted because of "their end" alone; and see Peri Chadash and Eliyah Rabbah letter 5, and you will understand. And with Hashem's help, in letter 4 this will be further explained.
