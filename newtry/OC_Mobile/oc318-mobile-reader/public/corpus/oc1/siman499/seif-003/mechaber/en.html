Regarding an animal that was slaughtered on Yom Tov, it is permissible to remove its skin and put it in an area of foot traffic even though there is no meat on it, and it is permissible to salt it lightly like the way we salt for roasting, and it is permissible to trick (by leaving bits of meat) and salt here and there until you salt the whole thing, but if it was slaughtered before Yom Tov it is forbidden.
