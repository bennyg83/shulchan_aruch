(18) To pound groats with it: for that is forbidden on Yom Tov, as below in siman 504, se'if 3.
(19) It is permitted to handle it: by the law of any utensil whose work is for a prohibited use, above in siman 308, se'if 3, that it is permitted to handle it for the need of its body (that is, to do with it a permitted labor) or for the need of its place; and the view of Magen Avraham is that even if the pestle was muktzeh due to monetary loss, for example that he is particular not to use the pestle for something else lest it be ruined, which in such a case is forbidden everywhere, here it is permitted because of the joy of Yom Tov; and see Biur Halacha.
(20) From sun to shade: or so that it not be stolen, as above in siman 308.
(21) But for the need of, etc.: by the law of any utensil whose work is for a prohibited use. And a thick chopping-block upon which they chop meat is permitted to be handled after he chopped, even from sun to shade and the like, for behold it is a utensil whose work is for a permitted use.
