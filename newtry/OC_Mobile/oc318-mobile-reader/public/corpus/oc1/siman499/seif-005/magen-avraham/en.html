Whose work is for a prohibited use. It appears to me that even a utensil that is muktzeh due to monetary loss is permitted to be handled to chop upon it because of the joy of Yom Tov, as Tosafos wrote in chapter 17 of Shabbos; and so ruled Yam Shel Shlomo; and the Yerushalmi too supports him; even the Rif did not disagree with him for practical law.
And its place is permitted. Even according to the one who forbids muktzeh, it is permitted to handle a utensil, for behold Rava holds like R' Yehudah and even so permits handling a utensil; see siman 308.
