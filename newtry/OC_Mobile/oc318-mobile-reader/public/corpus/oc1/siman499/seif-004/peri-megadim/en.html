One does not. See Taz, Beitzah 11b, and in Rashi there: "one would say," meaning certainly the reason is not that it is fit to recline upon, for moist [hides] or of a small animal are not fit to recline upon; see siman 308, se'if 25; rather, one who sees [it], just as he could err in this, would err toward leniency and say that the Sages permitted handling because it is fit to recline upon even when moist; and nevertheless there is no concern of a Torah matter; which is not so regarding fats, where one can say salting them involves a Torah matter or something close to it; and feathers are fine according to what I wrote in letter 3 about this.
