(א) (s.k. 1) To another courtyard etc., but in Rashi it is implied etc.; and what they said in the Gemara that the reason to another courtyard is forbidden is because his intent was fulfilled even though his intent was not for this courtyard — nevertheless through this his intent was fulfilled that he had to extend it from this courtyard where he was initially; therefore it was permitted only to return it to that courtyard where he was initially:
