[1] [Levush] For if it were above, etc. This permission applies specifically to returning it to the same courtyard. Returning it to another courtyard is prohibited so that his original intention not be fulfilled, as stated in Avodat HaKodesh, page 16. It appears to me that this resolves Tosafot's question in Shabbos 4a, s.v. u'mai shna, by explaining that the case is above ten handbreadths. Examine carefully. Tosafot on page 5a, s.v. kan, implies that initially one may carry above ten unless the route passes below ten, and even then it may be returned above ten. Avodat HaKodesh there implies that even above ten is prohibited initially. This requires study. See siman 347, seif katan 4.
