And some say, when he forgot in Birkat Hamazon, etc. All the more so in prayer: if he forgot to say it, he must say it before May the words be acceptable, and so it also implies in Turei Zahav. Responsa Maharam of Rothenburg, siman 70, wrote that one need not mention the subject of the day for Chanukah and Purim in Me'ein Shalosh, since there is no thanksgiving in the blessing of Me'ein Shalosh, and the event of Chanukah and Purim is mentioned only where there is thanksgiving in the prayer. Until here. Meaning: even though we say And we thank You, etc., that is because it is similar to the conclusion, and it is not considered thanksgiving in prayer. Since there is no thanksgiving in the prayer, it was never instituted at all. This is the explanation of what Levush wrote in siman 208, section 12; examine it. Lechem Chamudos, who wondered about him, missed the aforementioned responsum.
