We do not. See Turei Zahav and Beis Yosef. The reason is that Shabbos and Yom Tov have a Torah-level mussaf offering, and Rosh Chodesh also has one; therefore they instituted mentioning them in prayer and make him go back. By contrast, Chanukah is entirely rabbinic, and for that reason we do not make him go back. Kiddush over bread on Shabbos is Torah law; on Yom Tov some say it is from the words of the Sages and some say it is rabbinic. See siman 271 and what I wrote there.
