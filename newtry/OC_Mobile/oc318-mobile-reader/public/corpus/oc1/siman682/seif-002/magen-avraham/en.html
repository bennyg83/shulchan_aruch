The word wicked is read with a hataf. Beis Yosef. One says, to make them forget Your Torah and to make them pass from the statutes, etc., without mems. Shiltei Giborim in Mordechai. So too in Knesses HaGedolah in the name of Abudarham. Some say, these, to give thanks, etc. But it seems to me that one should say, these days of Chanukah, just as we say, And You gave us this day of such-and-such Festival. Even though there is no mussaf. For it is a day on which one became obligated in four prayers.
