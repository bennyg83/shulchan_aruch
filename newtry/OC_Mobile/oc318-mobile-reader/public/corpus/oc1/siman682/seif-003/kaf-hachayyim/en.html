One recites Al HaNissim in Bircas HaMazon, etc. If one ate something that requires the recitation of the abbreviated form of the 3 blessings, he should say in it, "and for the miracles and wonders that You performed for us during these days, for You, Hashem, are good, etc., and we shall thank You for the Land, etc. Blessed are You, Hashem, for the Land, etc." Yad Ne'eman, folio 5a; Yafeh LaLev, vol. 2, letter 1. So too Yafeh LaLev, vol. 5, at the end of letter 1, wrote that although the Shulchan Aruch, siman 208, seif 12, ruled that one does not mention the theme of the day in the abbreviated form of the 3 blessings on Chanukah and Purim, it appears proper to mention the kindnesses of Hashem. There is no concern of adding to the blessing or of an interruption, since the matter pertains to the day; see there. However, Responsa Maharam of Rothenburg, siman 70, wrote that one need not mention the theme of the day on Chanukah and Purim in the abbreviated form of the 3 blessings, since there is no thanksgiving in that blessing, and the theme of Chanukah and Purim is mentioned only where there is thanksgiving, as in prayer; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 2, cited his words and wrote that although we say, "and we shall thank You, etc.," that is only because it reflects the conclusion of the blessing and is not thanksgiving like the thanksgiving in prayer. Since there is no thanksgiving like that of prayer, no enactment was made at all. This follows the explanation of the Levush in siman 208, seif 12; see there. So too wrote Eshel Avraham, letter 1; Mishna Berurah, letter 2. See above, siman 208, letter 68.
