As You did. It appears per this wording it should be "so You will do for us" — for per this it is prayer; this is the reason of the 11th who cannot say it because one does not request needs in the last three [within prayer]; since Beit Yosef concludes permission is in the pray-er's hand, if so what Rama wrote in seif 1 that in synagogue one says as You did, etc., and in synagogue in Nodeh Lecha it is thanks — nevertheless one may say so; all the more after prayer one may say as request "do for us miracles as You did," etc. In the wording al hanissim Levush wrote one may say bayamim hahem uvezman hazeh with vav to include all miracles for ancestors and to specify that miracle — now like the event — each day He does for us open and hidden miracles as for ancestors open and hidden — end; I do not think so, for then why bayamim hahem — certainly it was in those days when ancestors lived; granted for bazman without vav one must say so, otherwise it implies in bazman hazeh while we exist a miracle was done for ancestors — therefore must say bayamim hahem bazman hazeh as if saying in those days on such-and-such of the month; also one passing where a miracle occurred says Blessed is He who performed a miracle for me in this place — siman 218 — and did not say uvemakom hazeh to include other hidden miracles — that is not in the wording at all and erroneous to say uvezman with vav as I wrote.
