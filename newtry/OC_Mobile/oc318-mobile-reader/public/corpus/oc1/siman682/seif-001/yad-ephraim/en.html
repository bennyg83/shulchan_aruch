In the gloss. “Some say,” etc. Some maintain that on Rosh Chodesh one should say this after HaRachaman Hu Yechadesh, because the more frequent obligation comes first, as explained below in siman 684. Abudraham writes that Barchi Nafshi should be recited before Mizmor Shir Chanukas HaBayis. This is unlike Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah. See the selections of Pri Chadash, who writes that only Mizmor Shir Chanukas HaBayis should be recited, citing Yerushalmi Shekalim concerning the song of Shabbos and the song of Rosh Chodesh. See there. See also Sukkah 54b and Rashi, beginning “to know that Rosh Chodesh was established in its proper time.” From there it appears that his conclusion is not certain.
