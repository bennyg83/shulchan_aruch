He is also obligated to shake during Hallel, if he is able to buy a lulav specifically in order to shake during Hallel with the congregation. Now, in Sukkah 42a, the baraisa regarding tzitzis and tefillin says 'to buy for him,' and did not say simply 'he is obligated.' One may say the opposite: this teaches that regarding lulav he is obligated only where he knows how to shake; if he does not know how to shake, even if he is already six years old, there is no obligation of chinuch, for chinuch is not equal in all mitzvos. If it had said 'he is obligated to buy,' I would have thought that even if he does not know how to shake, he is at least obligated to train him to take a lulav. It therefore teaches otherwise. For this reason, regarding chinuch it taught 'to buy,' while regarding lulav it says 'obligated,' as stated. In siman 658:6, regarding a minor before the age of pe'utos, a six-year-old, see Magen Avraham 9, etc. This is missing in print in siman 658. It implies that even one below the age of chinuch, if he wants, he may say the berachah with the child, 'Who commanded us,' for although he is not of chinuch age, one may say that for learning it is permitted.
