Recites Kiddush. The Mateh Yehudah wrote: the common practice is for two or three householders, or more, to sit in one sukkah and wait for one another for Kiddush out of honor. They do not act properly, for on the contrary, they should not recite Kiddush together, since two voices are not heard distinctly; see siman 284, se'if 5. In siman 488, subparagraph 3, I wrote in the name of the Chok Yaakov that it is proper to do this; see there. It is proper to say the berachah of sukkah aloud and drawn out in melody, like the other berachos of Kiddush. The same applies to all other berachos: one should accustom himself to say them in a loud voice, because the voice arouses intent. This is from the Shlah.
