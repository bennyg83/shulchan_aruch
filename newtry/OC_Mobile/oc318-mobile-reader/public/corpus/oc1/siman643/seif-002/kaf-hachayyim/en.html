2) Minchas Yom Tov wrote: It is common for two or more householders to sit in one sukkah and wait for one another before Kiddush out of respect. They are not acting properly, for on the contrary, they should not recite Kiddush together, since two voices cannot be heard distinctly. Eliyah Rabbah, sec. 1. See above, siman 488, sec. 17, and siman 271, secs. 11 and 12.
