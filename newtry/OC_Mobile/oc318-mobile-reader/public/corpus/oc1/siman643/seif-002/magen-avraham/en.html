If the sukkah was already made and standing with its sechach, he recites Shehecheyanu and afterward leishev ba-sukkah, as written in siman 661; this is from the Bayis Chadash. If he did not recite Shehecheyanu on the first night, he recites it all seven days; this is from the Gemara. Standing. In order that it be before performing the mitzvah. The custom is not so, for since we recite the berachah only over the eating, as written at the end of siman 639, it is considered before performing the mitzvah.
