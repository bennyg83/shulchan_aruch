[2] Levush: For it is impossible to recite the berachah, etc. The Turei Zahav wrote as follows: the reason we say it after Kiddush and not at the time of entering is that Shehecheyanu also relates to Kiddush. These are his words. This requires analysis, for if so, on the second night, when there is no need for Shehecheyanu for the sukkah, one should recite the berachah at the time of entering. According to the Levush's reason this is understandable. What they said in the Gemara, 'when he enters to sit in it,' means after he has sat and established himself to eat, as will be explained.
