Magen Avraham: and if it was, etc. As written in siman 661, from the Bayis Chadash. This means that the Bayis Chadash wrote so for us, according to what the Beis Yosef ruled below in siman 661, that specifically on the first night Shehecheyanu also relates to making the sukkah. Therefore he first recites the berachah of sukkah and afterward Shehecheyanu, so that the Shehecheyanu will refer back to both: to making the sukkah and to the festival. This is not so on the second night, when he needs Shehecheyanu only because of the festival, for with regard to making the sukkah he fulfilled through yesterday's Shehecheyanu, even if you say the first day was a weekday, as written above in siman 641. Therefore one should attach the Shehecheyanu immediately to Kiddush, to which it relates, and afterward recite the berachah of sukkah. Therefore, if the sukkah was already made and there is no need for Shehecheyanu because of making the sukkah, but only because of the festival, then the first night has the law of the second night, and one should attach Shehecheyanu immediately after Kiddush and afterward recite the berachah of sukkah. But the Bayis Chadash himself ruled below in siman 661 that even on the second night one should first say the berachah of sukkah and afterward Shehecheyanu; see there. Perhaps one can distinguish that regarding making he agrees, and this is easy to understand, but the language of the Bayis Chadash does not imply so.
