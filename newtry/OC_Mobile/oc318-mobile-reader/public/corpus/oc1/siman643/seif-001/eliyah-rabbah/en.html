[1] The order of Kiddush, etc. The Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: the common practice is for two householders or more to sit in one sukkah and wait for one another for Kiddush out of honor. They do not act properly; on the contrary, they should not recite Kiddush together, because two voices are not heard distinctly. These are his words. In siman 271 more of this was explained.
