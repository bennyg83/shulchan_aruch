Magen Avraham. s.v. And if it was made, etc. This means that in the Rema it is explained that on the second night one says Shehecheyanu first, because Shehecheyanu does not relate to the sukkah. Therefore, even on the first night, if it was already made and standing, Shehecheyanu does not apply to the sukkah, and he says Shehecheyanu first. But this is astonishing, for the opposite is true. We say in Sukkah 46 that when one makes a sukkah, he recites Shehecheyanu and then need not recite it when he enters it; if it is already made and standing, he recites Shehecheyanu when he enters it. See there. In Perek HaChalil, where Rav Yehudah and Rabbah bar bar Chanah disagree, brought by the Turei Zahav in subparagraph 1, Rashi explains that they disagree when the sukkah was already made and standing, for then at the time of entering he recites Shehecheyanu, and they also dispute whether leishev ba-sukkah comes first. These words show the opposite. From the Bayis Chadash and Magen Avraham it emerges that when it is made and standing, one says sukkah and afterward Shehecheyanu, because Shehecheyanu relates to the fulfillment of the mitzvah of sukkah that he fulfills by entering it. But when it is not made and standing, he recited Shehecheyanu at the time of making and need not recite Shehecheyanu for the sukkah; he says Shehecheyanu only for Kiddush, as on other festivals, and then Shehecheyanu comes before sukkah. The Eliyah Rabbah emends it to read, 'if the sukkah was not made and standing,' for then he recited Shehecheyanu at the time of making, as we said. Still, it is astonishing why they wrote this law, for it is relevant only in the time of the Tannaim, when one recited Shehecheyanu at the time of making the sukkah. But for us, the halachah has already been decided like the Rema, that one does not recite a berachah at the time of making, but relies on Shehecheyanu of Kiddush. If so, there is no difference whether it was made and standing or not. This requires further analysis. There is more lengthy discussion about this in Responsa Bayis Chadash, siman 152, which I brought in se'if 10, and this is not its place.
