Recites Kiddush. See Be'er Heitev. Those who recite Kiddush together have the custom that when they reach the berachah of Borei Peri HaGafen, each waits until the first recites it, and they answer Amen after him; afterward the second recites it and they answer after him. They act improperly, because the Amen answered by one who has already recited Borei Peri HaGafen is an interruption between the berachah and drinking. I already wrote above in siman 261, se'if 16, in the name of Panim Meiros, part 2, that it is not proper to do this; see there. See the Magen Avraham regarding if the sukkah was already made and standing, etc. Eliyah Rabbah emends the words of the Bayis Chadash. See Yad Ephraim, where I wrote about this. Now I have seen that Birkei Yosef discusses this at length and concludes that if one recited Shehecheyanu at the time of making, he should not recite Shehecheyanu for the festival over the cup, since according to Tosafos and the Rosh he does not recite it; see there.
