1) [Seif 1] The order of Kiddush is wine, Kiddush, and sukkah, etc. For one cannot recite the blessing over the sukkah before reciting the blessing over the sanctity of the day, since necessarily the day must first become sanctified before he becomes obligated to dwell in the sukkah. Levush; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 1; Mishna Berurah, sec. 1. This is only ab initio, however, and is not indispensable after the fact. Mishna Berurah, Shaar HaTziyun, sec. 1.
