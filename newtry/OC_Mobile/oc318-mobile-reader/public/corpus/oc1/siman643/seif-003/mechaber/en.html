On the remaining days, he recites the blessing over the sukkah before HaMotzi. The custom is to recite the blessing over the sukkah after HaMotzi, before tasting. {Rama: This is the practice on weekdays. On Shabbos and Yom Tov, when Kiddush is recited, he recites the blessing over the sukkah after Kiddush.
