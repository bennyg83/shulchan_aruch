In Shulchan Aruch: but on Shabbos and Yom Tov, etc. See Be'er Heitev. See below, siman 661, what I wrote in the name of Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, that on a day when there is Kiddush, one who says the berachah of sukkah together with Hamotzi is mistaken; rather, he should say it during Kiddush. In Devar Shmuel, siman 215, he wrote that there is reason to question whether one should interrupt the Kiddush of the day with the berachah of sukkah between the berachah over the wine and drinking, since this was not established by the early authorities, is not mentioned in the Talmud, and there is no prohibition in drinking wine outside the sukkah. These are his words. Therefore my view is that since Kiddush has no arrangement of other berachos in it, one recites the berachah over the sukkah either before Hamotzi or after it, etc., meaning according to each opinion: according to Maharam he recites it before Hamotzi, and according to the Rosh, whose practice we follow, he recites it after Hamotzi. However, one can say that this refers to one who recites the berachah over wine and immediately washes for the meal. But one who recites the berachah over wine and does not immediately wash for a meal, but merely eats pas haba'ah bekisnin so that it is considered Kiddush in the place of a meal, one can say that even according to Devar Shmuel, in such a case one should first recite Borei Minei Mezonos and afterward the berachah of sukkah. Since in truth the view of Machazik Berachah is that one should not recite the berachah of sukkah over pas haba'ah bekisnin even if there is more than an egg-volume, unlike the Ginas Veradim, as I wrote above in siman 639, according to this there would be no place to recite the berachah of sukkah at all. Yet in this case, because he establishes it through Kiddush, one can say that even Machazik Berachah agrees that it is considered fixed. According to this, since the whole basis for the berachah is the fixed character of Kiddush, it is proper that after Kiddush, which is the berachah over the wine, he recite the berachah of sukkah and not wait until after Borei Minei Mezonos over the pas haba'ah bekisnin. This is the common practice, to recite the berachah of sukkah after the berachah over wine. But one who recites Kiddush over wine and enters immediately into the meal, if he wants to do as Devar Shmuel wrote, to recite the berachah over wine, wash his hands, recite Hamotzi and then the berachah of sukkah, we do not protest against him.
