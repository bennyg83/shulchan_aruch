Se'if 3: On the other, etc. As was written: when he enters to sit, immediately, from forty. This is from the Rosh.
And the custom is, etc. As above, siman 639, se'if 8. Therefore it is proper that he begin the meal. The proof is from the fact that they disputed, regarding sukkah and Shehecheyanu, which comes first, implying that this is after the berachah over wine or bread. This is why he wrote: but on Shabbos and Yom Tov, etc.
