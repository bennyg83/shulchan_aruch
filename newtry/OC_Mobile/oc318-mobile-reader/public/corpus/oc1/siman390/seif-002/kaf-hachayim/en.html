(ב) There. An alleyway where one side is gentile etc. — and if there is a gentile courtyard between Jewish courtyards (meaning adjacent to two Jewish courtyards): if the gentile courtyard is not open to this alleyway and also has no opening or window except above ten handbreadths, it is permitted to carry from the gentile courtyard to the Jewish courtyard, since he does not use it directly, rental is not required (for he does not prohibit them); but if the gentile courtyard is open to the alleyway or to the Jewish courtyard below ten handbreadths, it is forbidden to use it with them (whether in the alleyway or in the courtyard, for he prohibits them) — Bach siman 382 in the name of Mordechai. But the opinion of the Ravyah there is that specifically via the alleyway he prohibits, but from courtyard to courtyard (meaning even if the window is within ten) he does not prohibit. And see there seif 3; and even two Jewish courtyards with a gentile courtyard between them (meaning separating them) may eruv together through openings to carry via the gentile courtyard, for the separation is not considered since he does not prohibit; nevertheless this is not so strictly required. Rashi. Magen Avraham s.k. 1.
