1) [Se'if 1] An alleyway with a non-Jew on one side, etc. Although this alleyway contains only one courtyard belonging to a Jewish person, it can be rendered permitted by a lechi or korah because another courtyard opens onto it. Although a non-Jew resides there, the non-Jew's residence is considered a residence for purposes of leniency. Beis Yosef in this siman, citing the Tur in siman 363. The Shulchan Aruch already records this there in se'if 26. See there, and see our words there in subsection 175.
