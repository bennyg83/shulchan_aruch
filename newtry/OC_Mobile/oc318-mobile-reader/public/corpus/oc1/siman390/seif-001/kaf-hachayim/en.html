(א) [seif 1] An alleyway where one side is gentile etc. — and this alleyway, even though there is only one Jewish courtyard in it, is permitted with a lechi or korah since another courtyard is open to it; and even though a Samaritan lives in it, the Samaritan's dwelling is considered a dwelling for leniency. Beit Yosef this siman in the name of the Tur siman 363; and they already wrote in Shulchan Aruch there seif 26 — see there; and see in our words there siman 375.
