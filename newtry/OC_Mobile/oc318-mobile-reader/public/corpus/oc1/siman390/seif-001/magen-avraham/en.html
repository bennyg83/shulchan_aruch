An alleyway where one side etc. And if there is a gentile courtyard between the Jewish courtyards: if the gentile courtyard is not open to this alleyway and also has no opening or window except above ten handbreadths, it is permitted to carry from the gentile courtyard to the Jewish courtyard, since he does not use it directly, rental is not required; but if the gentile courtyard is open to the alleyway or to the Jewish courtyard below ten handbreadths, it is forbidden to use with him entirely (Bach, Mordechai).
