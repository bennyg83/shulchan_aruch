(א) (s.k. 1) Alleyway etc. — since he does not use it directly, meaning above ten handbreadths, its use is not satisfactory and it is as if the gentile does not dwell there:

(ב) Forbidden to use with him entirely — meaning whether in the alleyway or in the gentile courtyard or a courtyard of one Jew, for the gentile prohibits them until they rent from the gentile (Bach, Mordechai brought in Bach siman 382); but the opinion of the Ravyah in siman 382 in his work on the Tur, and likewise above in Shulchan Aruch siman 382 seif 3:

(ג) And even two courtyards etc. with a courtyard between them etc. — for initially he did not write to permit carrying from courtyard to courtyard except when no gentile courtyard separates between two Jewish courtyards — namely when two Jewish courtyards are adjacent to each other and from the side of one courtyard there is a gentile courtyard and the gentile has an opening to the alleyway but has no regular foot traffic on the Jews, therefore he does not prohibit them in their courtyards except in the alleyway; but when a gentile courtyard is between two Jewish courtyards, then also from one Jewish courtyard to his fellow's Jewish courtyard it is fit to forbid even if they made an eruv, for the gentile separates between them; and regarding this he wrote that one may say that even in such a case it is permitted for the reason Magen Avraham wrote, but he concluded that the latter ruling is not so strictly required:
