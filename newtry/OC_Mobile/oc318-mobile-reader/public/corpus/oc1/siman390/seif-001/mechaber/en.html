An alleyway where one side is a non-Jews and one side is a Jews. Containing 2 paragraphs. An alleyway where
one side is owned by a non-Jew and one side by a Jew, and another Jewish house adjacent opens to the public domain but not to the alleyway, and there is a window between him and the Jew living in the alleyway—[the one whose house opens to the public domain] cannot make an eruv through the window between them to carry his utensils into the alleyway via his Jewish neighbor's house which opens to the alleyway. But if there is a doorway between them, it is permitted. And there is one who prohibits even when there is a doorway open between them.
