An alleyway where one side is gentile and one side is Jew. Containing 2 seifim. An alleyway where
one side is gentile and one side is Jew, and another Jewish house adjacent to him opens to reshut ha-rabbim and not to the alleyway, and there is a window between him and the Jew living in the alleyway — he cannot make an eruv through the window between them to take out his utensils to the alleyway via his Jewish neighbor's house that opens to the alleyway; but if there is a doorway between them, it is permitted. And there is one who prohibits even when there is a doorway open between them.
