**"And afterward tehillah, etc."** As written in chapter 1 of Berachos.

**"And afterward hallelu, etc."** As Rashi explained in chapter 16 of Shabbos—they are pesukei dezimra. Hagahos Maimoniy.

**"And if there is, etc."** That is, after he said all the halleluyahs that precede, as Rif, Rambam, and Rosh wrote—that they are pesukei dezimra—as they said there; and likewise Shibolei HaLeket in the name of Machzor Vitry; and likewise Machzor Chamudos.

**"And if, etc."** So too Semag and Tur in the name of the Geonim, as explained.

**"If still, etc."** As written in chapter 1 of Berachos.

**"And if already, etc."** For prayer with the congregation is desirable, as written in chapter 1 of Berachos and chapter 1 of Rosh Hashanah and Shavuos.

**"And afterward, etc."** The Geonim wrote that he should not read them at all, for they were established only before prayer, as written in chapter 5 and in chapter 1 of Avodah Zarah—not like R' Yehuda; rather that he may say them like one reading Torah. Beit Yosef.

**"And nevertheless, etc."** That is, after prayer; and that is, that he is obligated to say them from what is written "he arose early to repeat," etc.; and specifically the blessing on the Torah that he fulfilled in Ahavah Rabbah when he learned on the spot—unlike the rest.
