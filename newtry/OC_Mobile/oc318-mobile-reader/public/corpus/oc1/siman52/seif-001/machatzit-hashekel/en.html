Thus wrote the Semag, etc., until le-shem tifarteikha — for until here the verses are from Divrei Hayamim, and afterward verses from Nechemyah begin.

But the Semag, who wrote to say vayevarech David and to skip all Halleluyah until Hallelu El bekodsho — it is implied that he holds vayevarech David precedes all Halleluyah except Hallelu El, etc.; but from other poskim the opposite appears.

(SA note 1) He shall say, etc., before the rest of Halleluyah — since he did not mention that he should say the rest of Halleluyah, it is implied that it deals with one who does not have so much time; and nevertheless he wrote that he should say Hodu and not the rest of Halleluyah. For he did not mention afterward any of the rest of Halleluyah — that is, it is impossible that he holds Hodu precedes the rest of Halleluyah. And it deals with one who does not have time to say the rest of Halleluyah, and he said he should say Hodu — it would have been fitting to write afterward, "and if he has more time he should also say the rest of Halleluyah." Or he holds the rest of Halleluyah is obvious, that it precedes Hodu, and because of its great obviousness in his eyes he did not need to mention this. And if so, what the Rama wrote, "and if he has more time" — means after he said all Halleluyah. Since the Semag placed it before all Halleluyah, even though we do not rule like him, nevertheless in this one should rule like him to place it before Hodu.

For the main concern, etc., even if he does not recite Keriat Shema with the congregation. And know that what Rama concluded — "he shall say all the blessings" — means except for birchas haTorah, as written in siman 47 seif 7 letter 8.
