These are the words of the Semag, etc. Until "Your glorious Name," because up to that point the verses are from Divrei HaYamim, whereas afterward the verses from Nechemiah begin.
However, etc. From the Semag, who wrote that one should recite "David blessed" and skip all the Hallelujah passages until "Praise God in His Sanctuary," it appears that he holds that "David blessed" precedes all the Hallelujah passages except "Praise God, etc." From the other poskim, however, the opposite appears.
(Seif katan 1) He should recite, etc. Before the other Hallelujah passages. Since he did not mention that one should recite the other Hallelujah passages, it appears that he is speaking where there is not so much time, yet he nevertheless wrote that one should recite "Give thanks" and not the other Hallelujah passages. For afterward he did not mention the other Hallelujah passages at all. Meaning, were it possible that he holds that "Give thanks" precedes the other Hallelujah passages, and that he is speaking where there is insufficient time to recite them, then after stating that one should recite "Give thanks" he should have written: If he has more time, he should also recite the other Hallelujah passages. Rather, it is evident that he holds that the other Hallelujah passages plainly precede "Give thanks," and because this was so obvious to him he did not need to mention it. Accordingly, when the Rama writes, "If there is more time," he means after one has recited all the Hallelujah passages. Since the Semag placed "David blessed" before all the Hallelujah passages, although we do not rule like him in that respect, at least regarding this point we should rule like him and place it before "Give thanks."
For the primary concern, etc. Even though he will not recite Krias Shema together with the congregation. Know that when the Rama concludes that he should recite all the blessings, he means except for Bircas HaTorah, as written in siman 47, seifim 7 and 8.
