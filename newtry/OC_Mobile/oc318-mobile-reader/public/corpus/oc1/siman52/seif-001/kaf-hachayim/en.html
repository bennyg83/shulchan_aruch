(1) [Seif 1] If he comes to the synagogue and finds the congregation at the end of Pesukei Dezimra, etc. — and it is plain that all this applies only in places where the congregation prays only one prayer service. But in places where the congregation prays the first and second services, etc., he must go to pray with the congregation so as not to reverse the order that the Sages enacted. And likewise Shaarei Teshuvah on siman 57 end of 4a. Pithei Teshuvah letter 1.
