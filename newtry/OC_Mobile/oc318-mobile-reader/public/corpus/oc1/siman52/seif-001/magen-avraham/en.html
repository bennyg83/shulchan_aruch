This is Semag's wording: He recites Baruch She'amar and skips to Ashrei, etc.; skips to 'Praise God in His sanctuary'; continues through 'Your glorious Name'; and skips to Yishtabach. End quote. The other poskim, however, imply that all the psalms beginning and ending with Halleluyah precede Vayevarech David. He should recite 'Give thanks to Hashem, etc.' This implies that 'Give thanks to Hashem; proclaim, etc.' precedes the other Halleluyah psalms, and Semak writes this explicitly. Rif, however, writes that the essential Pesukei D'Zimrah extend from Ashrei through 'Let every soul praise Hashem,' implying that all the Halleluyah psalms precede 'Give thanks to Hashem.' Tur also implies this. Rama's words, 'If there is more time,' can be explained as referring to time remaining after he has recited all the Halleluyah psalms, since Rama subsequently mentions none of the other Halleluyah psalms. It also appears to me that Vayevarech David precedes 'Give thanks to Hashem,' since Semag placed it first. If the congregation has begun Yishtabach, he should listen to Yishtabach from the prayer leader. Mateh Moshe. If he arrives close to Krias Shema and can begin with Yotzer so as to reach Shemoneh Esrei with the congregation, he should do so, for the principal concern is to pray Shemoneh Esrei with the congregation. Hagahos Yesh Nochalin.
