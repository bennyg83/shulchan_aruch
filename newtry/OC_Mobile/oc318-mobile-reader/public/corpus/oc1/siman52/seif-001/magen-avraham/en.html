Thus wrote the Semag: he says Baruch She'amar and skips until Tehillah leDavid, etc., and skips until Hallelu El bekodsho until le-shem tifarteikha, and skips until Yishtabach — so far. But in other poskim it is implied that all the Halleluyah precede vayevarech David. He shall say Hodu laHashem, etc. — it is implied that Hodu laHashem kiru, etc., precedes the rest of Halleluyah; and so the Semak explicitly. But the Rif wrote that the essence of Pesukei Dezimra is from Tehillah leDavid until Ketz Hayeshuas — it is implied that all the Halleluyah precede Hodu laHashem; and so in the Tur. And so one may explain what the Rama wrote, "and if he has more time" — meaning after he said all the Halleluyah, for he did not mention afterward any of the rest of Halleluyah at all. And further it appears to me that vayevarech David precedes Hodu laHashem, since the Semag placed it first. If the congregation began Yishtabach, he should hear Yishtabach from the prayer leader (Mateh Moshe). And if he came near Keriat Shema — if he can begin at Yotzer so that he will reach praying the Shemoneh Esreh with the congregation, he should do so; for the main concern is that he pray the Shemoneh Esreh with the congregation (Hagahot Yesh Nof).
