And the second one did not make an eruv at all, etc. If the second courtyard made an eruv for itself, it does not prohibit the first courtyard, and the courtyard that made an eruv may pour directly into the pit. But when the second did not make an eruv, it is a foot that is prohibited in its own place and therefore prohibits the courtyard. See the beginning of siman 375, as stated by Magen Avraham.
