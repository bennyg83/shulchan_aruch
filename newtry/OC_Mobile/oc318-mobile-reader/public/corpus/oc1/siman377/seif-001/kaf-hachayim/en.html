(1) [Seif 1] And one made a depression in the courtyard, etc. — already explained Rashi siman 357: a courtyard adjacent to reshus ha-rabbim lacking four amot fit to absorb se'ah of water a person needs daily — then need a depression in the courtyard for water to descend; then even if they go outside he is not pleased thus and it is not direct pouring to reshus ha-rabbim as written there; therefore if one made a depression and they joined together both are permitted.
