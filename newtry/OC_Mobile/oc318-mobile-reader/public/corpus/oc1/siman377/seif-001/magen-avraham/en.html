(א) That did not join — forbidden. Even if she made a depression — forbidden to carry onto the balcony; thus even to pour in her house forbidden — we fear she will spare house spoilage and carry utensils to the balcony.

(ב) Permitted. Provided she made a depression; but forbidden to pour on the balcony lest water descend from courtyard to the depression her neighbor made — we fear she will fear courtyard spoilage since the depression is far and bring house utensils to the courtyard to pour near the depression — so Bach; in briefs it does not imply thus — see there; and it appears to me he wrote "the second did not join at all" — if the second joined for itself it does not forbid the courtyard and the one that made a depression may pour directly into the depression; but whoever did not join at all has forbidden foot in its place and forbids the courtyard — Eruvin 98 and doubt; see siman 364 seif 3.
