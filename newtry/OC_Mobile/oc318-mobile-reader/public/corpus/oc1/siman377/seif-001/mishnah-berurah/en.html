(א) And one made a depression, etc. — adjacent below opposite her upper story.
(ב) In order to pour into a courtyard that lacks, etc. — in such a courtyard forbidden to pour water without a depression for it, as siman 357 seif 1; see there. If the courtyard has four amot — see end of seif in gloss and what we wrote there.
(ג) To pour their water — even directly into the depression.
(ד) And one joined, etc. — the one that made the depression with all neighbors dwelling at her upper story; deals when each upper story has a balcony before it, all enter the balcony via the upper story opening, and from it descend to the courtyard.
(ה) Forbidden — even if she too made a depression [and all the more so per the Mechaber when she did not make a depression]; not only forbidden to carry utensils with wastewater through the courtyard to her depression or to the balcony since she joined only for the upper story — even to pour in her house so water goes by itself to the balcony and descends — forbidden, lest he spare spoilage of his house from wastewater and carry utensils with wastewater directly to the shared balcony, since he did not join with other upper story residents.
(ו) And she who joined is permitted — provided she made a depression as above; without a depression forbidden to pour in her house or to a balcony lest water descend to the courtyard and certainly to reshus ha-rabbim as siman 357; even if her neighbor made a depression and water would descend from the courtyard to that depression — forbidden, lest he fear courtyard spoilage since the depression is far and he carries house utensils to the courtyard to pour directly into the depression — they did not join together.
(ז) That she not pour directly into the depression — for the courtyard is shared by both and they did not join together.
(ח) As in an unjoined courtyard, etc. — meaning: even though the courtyard with the depression belongs to both and water descends there to the depression by his force.
(ט) Both are permitted, etc. — meaning permitted to pour also onto the balcony and descend to the courtyard; deals when each joined for itself; we do not fear he will carry utensils with water to the courtyard to pour into the depression his neighbor made, since the courtyard has four amot of such width that water is absorbed in place and the courtyard is not ruined.
(י) And need no depression — meaning therefore even if one made a depression on his upper story side we do not fear the second will carry utensils with wastewater there.
