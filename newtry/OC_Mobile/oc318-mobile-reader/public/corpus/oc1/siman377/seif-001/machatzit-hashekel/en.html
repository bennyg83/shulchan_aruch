(Siman 1) Even if she made, etc. — Bach questioned: why teach "did not join for itself" — even if she joined for itself, forbidden to pour on the balcony lest water descend to neighbor's depression — as in the Talmud: since neighbor's depression is far from her balcony she fears if water descends from balcony to courtyard then to depression the whole courtyard will be muddied — therefore she lowers utensils with water to the courtyard to pour into the depression — forbidden since they did not join together; therefore Bach wrote why teach "did not join for itself" — to forbid in every case even if she too made a depression — forbidden to carry onto the balcony since upper story residents did not join with one another.
