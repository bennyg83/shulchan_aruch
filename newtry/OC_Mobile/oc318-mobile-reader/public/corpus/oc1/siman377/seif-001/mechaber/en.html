The law of two upper stories opposite one another open to a courtyard. And in it one seif:
Two upper stories open to a courtyard opposite one another, and one made a depression in the courtyard in order to pour into a courtyard that lacks four amot of water, and the second did not make one; if they joined together both are permitted to pour' their water; and if they did not join together and one joined for itself and the second did not join at all — the one that did not' join is' forbidden and the one that joined is permitted, provided she does not pour directly into the depression but pours in the upper story and they descend to the depression.For force in an unjoined courtyard is permitted (Tur); and likewise if the courtyard is more than four amot both are permitted in this manner and need no depression (Beit Yosef).
