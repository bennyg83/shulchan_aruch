And one made a depression, etc. — Beit Yosef brought Tosafos' explanation on this passage; Rambam also has another explanation of this mishnah in commentary on the mishnah and likewise in his laws; but per Tosafos' explanation Rashba and Ritva also wrote, and they wrote that most commentators wrote thus.

And one joined for itself and the second did not join — per the Mechaber's initial depiction that one did not make a depression, this law applies even when both joined each for itself: the one that made a depression is permitted and the one that did not is forbidden [later authorities].

(ג) More than four amot — not precise; likewise even four amot, as above siman 357.

And they need no depression — see Mishna Berurah; Taz questions Beit Yosef — his view: since there is a depression in the courtyard we fear one will carry utensils with wastewater there; he brought proof from Rambam and many later authorities rejected his proof [Tosafos Yeshanim, Nehar Shalom, Avkat Rochel, Atzei Almogim — Rambam refers only to the first part of Rambam's words, not the latter]; nevertheless many rishonim wrote like Rashi — it deals with a courtyard less than four amot — Rashba, Ritva, Or Zarua; see Atzei Almogim — likewise in rainy season no depression needed, as siman 357 even under four amot both are permitted.
