And this — Taz siman 366 part 7 — see there. And behold in some editions of Shulchan Aruch here the word 'if so' is written 'it is fit that one should say,' etc.; and in some 'or'; see Tosafot; and I saw in Avodat HaKodesh siman 366 part 17 that he understood thus in Taz — that the word 'or' should be 'and' — meaning if residents were added to the city it is fit that one should say explicitly at the beginning of the blessing thus, and there is no concern even if it diminished afterward from the measure; and one who examines Beit Yosef implies they are not dependent on one another — for even according to the first view in siman 366 seif 9 that one should say explicitly thus 'and for everyone who will be added' — nevertheless measure is needed that it not diminish from the measure, for for them it is the beginning of the eruv; and see Levush there; requires study; and it would appear superficially the word 'then' with a zayin — meaning in a city accustomed that residents are added, then it is fit not to rely on what diminished less than the measure — for for them it is the beginning of the eruv, and even if they stipulated explicitly on them it does not help; and if in such a city it is fit to rule like the first view anonymously in siman 366 that one should say explicitly 'for everyone who will be added' — then there is no concern — meaning since we always see it does not diminish from the measure, and like our matzah that remained whole and whatever has six eggs or eight according to every view suffices even for many courtyard residents — there is no concern at all; and nevertheless we practice with matzah mouth to mouth and it should be whole all Shabbats of the year until the coming Friday we remove the first and place the second there, and we are lenient on the wall and there is no bizuy ochelin that it become moldy; and see the explanation regarding afikoman.
