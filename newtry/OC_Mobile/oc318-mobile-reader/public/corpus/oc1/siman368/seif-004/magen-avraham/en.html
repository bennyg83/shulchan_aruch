Diminished. And if the eruv broke, even before the first Shabbat entered it is permitted since at the time of placement it was whole (Maharil); and in responsum of Rambam he wrote that if it broke even after the first Shabbat it is good to make another, and his reason is that which the mishnah taught 'if something remained, any amount is valid' — meaning whole; or alternatively that anything that broke before Shabbat and there is time to make another is called the beginning of the eruv, end of his words; and behold Rabbenu Yonah ruled explicitly that it is valid even for the remaining Shabbats; also what he wrote that 'any amount' means whole is forced in my eyes; therefore it appears to me like Maharil that since at the time of placement it was whole, we no longer decree lest the matter return to its corruption; and nevertheless it appears to me that if nothing remained in it the required measure it is invalid — for specifically when they make partnership then the eruv is only so the children not forget, therefore it is valid in any amount; but for us it is invalid; and it is possible that this is Rambam's reason in siman 258, where he concludes that all his teachers practiced to make another, for he holds that also with a slice one should not be lenient even if it has the required measure; however there it implies that his reason is that we decree lest the matter return to its corruption as written siman 366 — specifically when one person enables for all we are lenient; but now when each one gives flour we are not lenient.
