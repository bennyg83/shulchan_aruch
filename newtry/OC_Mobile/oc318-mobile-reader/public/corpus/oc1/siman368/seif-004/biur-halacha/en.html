Even for the remaining Shabbats — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of Magen Avraham, and it is not his own words, for in truth it is explained thus in R' Yehonatan's words in this sugya, and likewise Rashi's view in the mishnah there — that this 'and they did not say' refers to R' Yossi's words; and that which is written that some disagree is Tur's view, who ruled in siman 387 that we rely on partnership in place of eruv; and here he rules like R' Yossi that its remainder in any amount — therefore he holds that in all cases it is valid if something remained, and one cannot say it deals when there was partnership of alleyways — for if so even if nothing remained from eruv chatzerot it is also valid, for we rely on partnership in place of eruv; and likewise Bach and Maharalnach in his glosses in Tur's view; and likewise Rashba's view in Avodat HaKodesh appears, who cited anonymously 'its remainder in any amount' and did not distinguish that it deals when partnership existed; and likewise Or Zaru'a HaGadol brings many explanations that explain this 'and they did not say' unlike Rashi's explanation — and according to their explanations this law is not necessary; and likewise in Tosafot 46b, 2 — see there; and already Chemed Moshe was aroused on this, and see in Machatzit HaShekel who settled it somewhat; and see in Or Zaru'a there, and it appears according to him it is a matter in its own right; and therefore it appears one may be lenient in this in a time of need; and likewise in Sefer Shulchan Atzei Shittim; and l'chatchila certainly it is proper to be careful in this; and Taz wrote another reason not to rely l'chatchila on an eruv that diminished — lest residents be added afterward to that city and then certainly one must add on their account as written siman 366 seif 9 (however what Taz concluded as further advice for this — that one should say at the time of the blessing 'for every person who will be added to this city' — and this helps even when it already diminished from its measure — many Acharonim challenged this).
