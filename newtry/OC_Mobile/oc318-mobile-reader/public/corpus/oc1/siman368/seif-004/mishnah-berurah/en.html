(13) From its measure — a dried fig for each one, or two meals for the many.
(14) After it entered — for if it diminished before the day was sanctified upon it, it is for them like the beginning of an eruv even though they already placed it for the sake of eruv, as at the beginning of the siman.
(15) Valid — for measure is needed only initially, both for eruv chatzerot and for partnership of alleyways.
(16) Even for the remaining, etc. — for for this Shabbat it is valid even if nothing remained at all, since the eruv existed at bein ha-shemashot; and see in Magen Avraham who wrote that the law that if something remained it is valid applies only in the time of the Gemara when they made eruv chatzerot and partnership of alleyways each separately; unlike for us who practice to make partnership and eruv together, as explained in siman 387 — invalid for the second Shabbat if it does not have eruv measure; and see in Biur Halacha that some disagree on this; and l'chatchila certainly one must be careful to add until the measure.
(17) Shabbats — and even though we hold above in siman 366 seif 6 that one does not make an eruv with a slice — that is when it was a slice at the hour it was placed for the sake of eruv; unlike here where it was sliced afterward we have no concern; nevertheless some are stringent when it was sliced before bein ha-shemashot since there is still time to place another whole loaf.
