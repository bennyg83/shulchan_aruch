He must inform them. Even if he wishes to make an eruv from his own, lest it not be agreeable to them; and if they are in one alleyway and he wishes to make an eruv from their own he must inform them, for it is for them like the beginning of an eruv; and such is implied in Tur; and R' Yosef Karo's words require study, for he wrote that if he makes an eruv from his own he need not inform — and such is implied in Ri of Lunel — see there; requires study why.
