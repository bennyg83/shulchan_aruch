(2) [Levush] Six eggs, etc. He omitted what Shulchan Aruch wrote — and some say eight eggs — because he holds they were lenient in eruv; and so it is written in siman 409 seif 7; and so Maharil wrote; but Bach there wrote that the practice is with eight, and it is proper to do so l'chatchila; and regarding what Olat Shabbat wondered — see Bach there that there is no difficulty; and Riaz in Shiltei HaGiborim is lenient with five medium eggs and a third.
