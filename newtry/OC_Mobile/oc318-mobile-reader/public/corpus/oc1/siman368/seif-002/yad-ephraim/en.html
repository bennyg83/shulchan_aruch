In Magen Avraham s.k. 1 'and if they are in one alleyway,' etc. — note: he cited alleyway because the sugya says 'in a courtyard between two alleyways,' etc.; see in Beit Yosef; and according to what deals here in Shulchan Aruch regarding eruv chatzerot it must read 'and if the house opens to one courtyard,' etc.; and that which he wrote 'and likewise it appears in Tur' — Magen Avraham's intent in this is to resolve Bach's and Taz's difficulty in siman 367 on Tur, who contradicts there what he wrote here that one makes an eruv without knowledge — and Magen Avraham resolves that Tur's intent is that he can make an eruv without his knowledge if he wishes to make an eruv specifically from his own, unlike in siman 367 that deals when they wish to make an eruv from his own to take bread from him — he holds one does not make an eruv without his knowledge; and that which he wrote 'and R' Yosef Karo's words require study' — meaning from the anonymity of his words it appears it deals even when open to two courtyards the law is thus; and on this he wrote 'requires study why'; and in my humble opinion it is possible he holds like the 'some say' in the name of the first view — that for us who hold he is permitted with both of them there is no concern if they also make an eruv with these, and he does not hold like Rambam that it is not agreeable to him thus — lest people be disturbed — see there.
