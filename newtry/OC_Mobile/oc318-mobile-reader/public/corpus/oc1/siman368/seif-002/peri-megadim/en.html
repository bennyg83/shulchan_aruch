And even — Taz; it appears this refers to what Tur wrote here that one makes an eruv without knowledge — nevertheless taking the bread requires knowledge of members of the household as written in siman 367; and this is at the beginning of the eruv or when residents were added or from a different type and entirely consumed; but from the original type and it was consumed before the second Shabbat, or from a different type and diminished from the measure before the first Shabbat — one need not inform a member of the household at all, since they already made an eruv; and therefore such is the reading: if with knowledge of members of the household it is obvious he is permitted — see Tosafot; and see Magen Avraham.
