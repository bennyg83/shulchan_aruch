[2] [Levush] Six eggs, etc. The Levush omitted the Shulchan Aruch's opinion requiring eight eggs because he holds the Sages were lenient regarding eruv, as stated in siman 409, seif 7. Maharil likewise writes six. The Bach there, however, writes that the custom is eight, and it is proper to do so initially. Olat Shabbos's question is answered by the Bach there. Riaz in Shiltei Giborim is lenient with five and one-third average eggs.
