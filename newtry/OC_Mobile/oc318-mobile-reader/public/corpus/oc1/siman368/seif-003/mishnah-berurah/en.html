(10) And if they are more, etc. — meaning that the measure of eighteen dried figs is the largest measure, which is the measure of two meals; and with this measure it suffices even for a thousand; and that which he wrote 'its measure is two meals' means its measure is also two meals.

(11) Which are six eggs — meaning with their shells.

(12) And some say eight eggs — and l'chatchila it is proper to practice thus; and b'dieved six eggs suffice.
