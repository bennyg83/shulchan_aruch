(1) To restore and repair it — this deals with the first Shabbat, and it was invalidated before Shabbat entered; for if it was invalidated after Shabbat already entered, no repair is needed at all for this Shabbat even if it was already entirely consumed and lost completely; and regarding the second Shabbat — even though if it was already entirely consumed one must make an eruv anew, nevertheless if something remained even any amount no repair is needed since there was eruv measure at bein ha-shemashot of the first Shabbat, as written in seif 4; and what does he say at the end of the seif 'and if it was not entirely consumed,' etc., even though he makes an eruv from their own — certainly as we wrote that it was invalidated before the first Shabbat entered.
(2) He need not inform — to ask them if they will agree to this, for we establish their presumption that certainly all will agree to this type as they agreed initially [Levush].
(3) From a different type — such as if initially it was wheat bread and now he wishes to make an eruv from barley bread.
(4) He must inform them — to ask them, for since the first type was already entirely consumed it is for them like making an eruv l'chatchila; and Mordechai wrote that specifically in a house open to two courtyards we do not know which is agreeable to him; but if open to one courtyard it is a benefit for him and his intent is not needed, and so other Acharonim wrote.
(5) And if from his own he need not inform them — because certainly they will agree that he make an eruv for them in whatever types he wishes, since they give nothing and they already agreed to make an eruv together.
(6) But only diminished — from the measure specified below in seif 3; and it deals when it diminished before Shabbat entered, as written in s.k. 1.
(7) He need not inform them — for certainly they will agree to complete the eruv.
