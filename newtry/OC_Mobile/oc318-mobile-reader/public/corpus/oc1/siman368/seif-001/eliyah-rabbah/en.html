[1] He must inform them, etc. The Tur says this even if he wishes to contribute the eruv from his own food. Rabbenu Yonah writes that if he supplies his own food, he need not inform them, and Rabbenu Yerucham appears likewise. The Magen Avraham requires study as to why. The Mordechai also somewhat implies this. It appears that all this concerns newly added residents. But in seif 1, where a homeowner had already made an eruv, there is no distinction, because we presume his present intent remains as before unless he now makes the eruv in a different direction, in which case the law is like newly added residents.
