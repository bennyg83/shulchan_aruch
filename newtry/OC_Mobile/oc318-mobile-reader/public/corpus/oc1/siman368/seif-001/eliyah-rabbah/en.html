(1) He must inform them, etc. Even if he wishes to make an eruv from his own — so Tur wrote; and R' Yosef Karo wrote that if he makes an eruv from his own he need not inform — and likewise it appears in Ri of Lunel; requires study why (Magen Avraham); and likewise it appears somewhat in Mordechai; and it appears that all this is when residents were added, but in seif 1 regarding the homeowner who already made an eruv there is no distinction — for we say presumably his intent is as at first, unless he now makes an eruv toward another side — then his law is like residents who were added.
