And even if he makes an eruv for them from their own — see in Taz, and I did not cite it in Mishna Berurah because many Acharonim challenged him, and also Peri Megadim interpreted his words toward another intent — see there.

And if from his own he need not inform them at all — behold Mechaber cited Tur's wording, and such is also the view of Rashi and R' Yehonatan as Beit Yosef wrote; but R' Chananel's view is not so as he wrote explicitly, and likewise Rambam's view — that in a house open to two courtyards one must inform him in all cases; and such is implied in Avodat HaKodesh of Rashba — that the entire sugya deals when one makes an eruv from his own; and nevertheless we say in the sugya that with two types, when the first was consumed one must inform them; and nevertheless for halacha one should not deviate from Shulchan Aruch's words, since many poskim are lenient on the fundamental law of this even when making an eruv from their own — for us who rule that a house open to two courtyards that made an eruv with both of them is permitted in this and that — see Rashba and Ritva; if so, at least when making an eruv from his own one should not be stringent.
