דין אם אחר שעירבו נתקלקל העירוב. ובו ה סעיפים:
The law if after they made an eruv the eruv was invalidated. And it contains five seifim: They made an eruv and afterward the eruv was invalidated, and one of the courtyard residents came to restore and repair it — if he comes to make an eruv from the original type, even if it was entirely consumed he need not inform them, and even if he makes an eruv for them from their own; but if he comes to make an eruv from a different type — if the first was entirely consumed he must inform them if he makes an eruv from their own, and if from his own he need not inform them at all; and if the first was not entirely consumed but only diminished — even though he makes an eruv from their own, he need not inform them.
