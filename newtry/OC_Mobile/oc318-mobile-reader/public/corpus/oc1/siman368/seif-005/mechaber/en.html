If the eruv bread became moldy and was disqualified from being eaten — behold it is as if it was entirely consumed, and one must make an eruv anew.And therefore the practice is to make the eruv from matzah challah, which does not quickly become moldy [Kol Bo; Beit Yosef siman 254]; and furthermore one can preserve it during the days of Passover and one can preserve the eruv all year [his own view]; and this is better than] making an eruv every Shabbat so they not forget to make an eruv; however if they wish to make an eruv every erev Shabbat and eat the eruv every Shabbat — the permission is in their hands; and see below siman 394.
