If the eruv bread became moldy and unfit to eat, it is as if it were completely gone and the eruv must be remade.Rema: Therefore the custom developed to make the eruv from matzah, which does not mold quickly, and furthermore it can be kept through Passover and used as the eruv all year long. This is better than making a new eruv] each Shabbat, lest people forget to make one. However, if one wishes to make a new eruv each Shabbat eve and eat the eruv each Shabbat, one may do so. See below Siman 394.
