(5) And this is better, etc. And Taz wrote that it is better to make an eruv every Friday, for most of the time the matzah becomes invalidated and moldy from the length of time; and one who wishes to do on the better side should make an eruv from his own every Friday and enable through another and not recite the blessing since there is eruv matzah in the synagogue and blessings are not indispensable. And that which is practiced in some communities that on the Great Friday the shamash goes from house to house and gives each one from the eruv matzah is not correct unless he makes an eruv that Shabbat from his own and enables them.
