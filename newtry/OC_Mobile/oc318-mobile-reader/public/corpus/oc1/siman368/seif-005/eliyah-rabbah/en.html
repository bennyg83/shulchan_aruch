[4] And furthermore, etc. Another reason is that there is no poor Jew who does not possess flour, unlike other times of the year, as stated in Tanya.
[5] And it is preferable, etc. The Taz writes that it is preferable to make an eruv every Friday because often the matzah spoils and molds over a long period. One who wishes to act in the best manner should contribute his own eruv each Friday and grant it through another person, but should not recite a blessing because the synagogue's eruv matzah already exists and blessings do not invalidate the eruv. The practice in some communities that before Shabbos HaGadol the attendant goes from house to house and gives each person some of the eruv matzah is improper unless he is making that Shabbos's eruv from his own food and formally grants them a share.
