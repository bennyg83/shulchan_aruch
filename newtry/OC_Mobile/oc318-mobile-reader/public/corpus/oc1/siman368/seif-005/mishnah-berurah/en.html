(18) If it became moldy, etc. — and the same applies when it became wormy with small worms (called mealybugs) [Acharonim].
(19) And was disqualified from being eaten — meaning when all of it became moldy.
(20) Anew — and if he saw this on Shabbat and it is unknown to him when it became moldy — whether before Shabbat or on Shabbat — we lean to leniency and say it probably became moldy recently and at bein ha-shemashot it was fit to be eaten, as in siman 394.
(21) This is better, etc. — and the Acharonim wrote that it is better to make an eruv every Friday, for most of the time matzah of the eruv is invalidated and becomes moldy from the length of time, and worms commonly fall in it from the length of time and it is not fit for eating — behold it is as if entirely consumed as above; and one who wishes to make a good strategy should make an eruv from his own every Friday and enable the entire congregation through another — and with this there is no concern if the eruv in the synagogue is invalidated and he should not recite a blessing on his own since there is eruv in the synagogue and perhaps it is valid, and blessings are not indispensable.
(22) And to eat the eruv every Shabbat — because since one mitzvah was done with it, let another mitzvah be done with it.
