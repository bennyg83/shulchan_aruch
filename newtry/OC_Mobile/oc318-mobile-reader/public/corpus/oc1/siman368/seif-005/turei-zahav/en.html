And this is better, etc. And in my humble opinion it is better to make an eruv every Friday, for most of the time the matzah of the eruv is invalidated and becomes moldy from the length of time and is not fit for eating at all and is as if entirely consumed; and furthermore there is a custom in some communities that on the Great Friday the shamash goes from house to house and gives each one a bit from the eruv matzah — and this is forbidden, for behold on that Shabbat there is no eruv at all; and perhaps the custom spread because the shamash would leave in the synagogue a small piece from the eruv which is the end of the eruv, and they err in this by distributing all of it; therefore it is good at least to make an eruv that Shabbat from his own and enable the entire congregation — namely in a place where they bake bread for the honor of Shabbat; and on Friday he should add so there will be measure in what he makes an eruv and enables — see siman 394 regarding eating the eruv; and one who wishes to make a good strategy should make an eruv from his own every Friday and enable through another — and with this we are not concerned for invalidation of the eruv in the synagogue and he should not recite a blessing since there is eruv matzah in the synagogue and blessings are not indispensable, as above; and it appears to me one should be careful not to rely on an eruv that diminished, lest it diminish from its measure and residents be added afterward to that city and then one must add on their account as written siman 366 seif 9; or it is fit that one should say at the time of the blessing 'for every person who will be added to this city' as the first view there — and if so there is no concern if residents are added afterward, as above.
