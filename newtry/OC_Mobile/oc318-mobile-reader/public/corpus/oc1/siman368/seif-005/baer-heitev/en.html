And Taz wrote: and in my humble opinion it is better to make an eruv every Friday, for most of the time the matzah of the eruv is invalidated and becomes moldy from the length of time and is not fit for eating at all and is as if entirely consumed; and furthermore there is a custom in some communities that on the Great Friday the shamash goes from house to house and gives each one a bit from the eruv matzah — and this is forbidden, for behold on that Shabbat there is no eruv at all; and perhaps the custom spread because the shamash would leave in the synagogue a small piece from the eruv which is the end of the eruv, and they err in this by distributing all of it. Therefore it is good at least to make an eruv that Shabbat from his own and enable the entire congregation; and one who wishes to make a good strategy should make an eruv from his own every Friday and enable through another — and with this we are not concerned if the eruv in the synagogue is invalidated and he should not recite the blessing on his own since there is eruv matzah in the synagogue and perhaps it is valid, and blessings are not indispensable, end of his words; see there.
