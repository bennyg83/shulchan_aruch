Se'if 4, A woman after childbirth, etc. End of chapter 18 of Shabbos. See above, siman 330, se'if 4. This requires analysis.
If she says, 'I need,' etc. But the Ran wrote that even without a statement we feed her, as the Rif wrote in chapter 18 of Shabbos, and so wrote the rest of the authorities.
And these days, etc. Like the version in chapter Hadar: let them tell a non-Jew to warm water for the child on account of his mother. See Tosafos in Gittin there, who did not write this so plainly. In the Rosh, Bava Kamma chapter 7, se'if 15, he wrote explicitly the opposite; although one can say that he wrote that language according to the Ba'al HaIttur, the wording does not imply so.
