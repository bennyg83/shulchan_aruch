1) [Seif 1] Pregnant and nursing women fast, etc. Because it is a Torah obligation. Levush.
2) Ibid. Pregnant and nursing women fast, etc. A nursing woman whose child is ill and in danger, and the child refuses to eat or nurse from anyone except her, and if she fasts the child will be endangered, does not fast on Yom Kippur. Divrei Shmuel, siman 107; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 133, sec. 12. If possible, she should be fed small amounts at a time, as written below in siman 618, seifim 7 and 8, see there.
