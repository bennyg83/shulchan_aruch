Seif 2: pregnant woman who craved, etc. NB: see Magen Avraham who wrote if she says "I need" even without changed complexion she is believed. Proof from Mishnah Avot ch. 5 ten miracles in Temple: no woman miscarried from smell of sacrificial meat — Tosefot Yom Tov in Rashi's name: if she smelled they would not give her sacrificial meat. Tosafot Chadashim wondered from Yoma that explicitly for pregnant craving sacrificial meat they insert even gravy — left difficulty on Rashi. Gilyon there in name of Rav Saul: intent when pregnancy not visible she is not believed to say she smelled, etc. — in my view incorrect: one witness believed in prohibitions; whether established pregnant or she says pregnant — believed she smelled, not suspected lying. Saying pregnancy is common, smelling common — therefore his ruling wrong, certainly believed pregnant and smelled. Rashi's intent: Tosafot Chadashim's precision why Mishnah listed not miscarrying not not smelling is true but not as they thought for outward reason — Tanna lists main benefit/miracle not not smelling — what if she smelled? Therefore miracle of not miscarrying. Still hard for Rashi why counted among Temple miracles — world benefit, why Temple miracle? Must say benefit/miracle to Temple itself not to profane kodosh for her — if miscarried would need to profane kodosh for her; if smelled, not given — not profaned — counted Temple miracle. If listed not miscarrying not not smelling — for world benefit; if miracle relates to kodosh — miracle she not smell; therefore should say not smelling — forced Rashi: miracle not that she never smelled but if smelled never miscarried — benefit to kodosh even if smelled not given — counted Temple miracle. Rashi: only after miracle not given; implies without miracle would give on word alone — perforce Mishnah when complexion changed; supports Magen Avraham. Not contradicted by Tosefot Yom Tov Yoma re vinegar — no miracle there; where clear prohibition rely on established miracle; where prohibition would occur, rely on miracle — general rule: do not rely on miracle without prohibition; established miracle believed where prohibition — see there. Yoma source for this law — Rashi: Yom Kippur — Sefer Shmot Arets wondered why Rashi needed to teach — explicit in Gemara — strained there; intent to exclude Torah additions to Yom Kippur — might think whisper if smelled — women eating until dark not stopped — might think only if she eats herself, if smelled not to feed, especially for fetus whisper in ear — Rashi: not so, only Yom Kippur itself; Torah additions — women eating not stopped if take themselves — no whisper needed. Perhaps Rashi implies Torah additions not called "wicked sprout from womb" since not explicit; if women eat not stopped — not wicked for this — only Yom Kippur itself — examine well.
