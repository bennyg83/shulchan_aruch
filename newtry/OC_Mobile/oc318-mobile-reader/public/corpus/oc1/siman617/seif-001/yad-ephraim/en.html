In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1: And her statement is not effective unless, etc. In the Ran it is explained that a healthy person who smelled and whose soul was disturbed is fed on his own statement and on the basis of an expert who recognizes him, etc. Perhaps the Rosh also agrees, and 'changed' is not precise. So too the Ran concludes that once he turns pale, we are concerned for danger; nevertheless, at first he wrote that we feed him on his own statement.
