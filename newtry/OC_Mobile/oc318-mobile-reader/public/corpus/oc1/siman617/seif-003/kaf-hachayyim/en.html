3) [Seif 2] A pregnant woman who smelled food, etc. It is written in the Or Zarua that a pregnant woman must be warned to guard herself to the greatest extent possible not to eat pork, neveilos, or tereifos, for this causes the child to be God-fearing. He brings many proofs for this from the Gemara and Yerushalmi. Darkei Moshe, sec. 1. Likewise, a nursing woman who requires food because of illness should not eat forbidden foods, nor should the child himself, for all this harms him in his old age, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 81, seif 7 in the gloss, see there.
