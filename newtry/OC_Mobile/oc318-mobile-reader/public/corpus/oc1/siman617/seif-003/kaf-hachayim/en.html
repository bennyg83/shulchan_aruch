(3) [Seif 2] Pregnant woman who craved, etc. Avodat Yisrael: warn pregnant woman guard herself as much as possible not to eat pig, nevelah, treif — causes child to be rebellious — many proofs from Gemara and Yerushalmi; see there. Likewise nursing woman (if needed for illness) forbidden foods, likewise child — harms in old age as Yoreh Deah 81:7 Hagahah — see there.
