(1) Even though she didn't say. It means that she said she needs me, even though her face does not change, etc. in siman 10 he wrote, she will just pass away, that she smelled, and when he said, ``I need,'' I am here to prevent them from knowing that she smelled, and that she is more beautiful.

(ב) Feed her. Meaning: insert piece in gravy into her mouth; if mind not settled give gravy; if not give food itself [Beit Yosef Bach Levush]. Specifically pregnant who sometimes settles with little; ill — per doctor, less than shiur as siman 618:7 [Rosh].
