So it is in the Tur and Rosh here in Yoma. But the matter is astonishing, for at the end of chapter Mefanin it is explained in the Rif and Rosh that only if she says, 'I do not need,' we do not desecrate; and so ruled the Shulchan Aruch above, siman 330, se'if 4. Why should we be more stringent on Yom Kippur than on Shabbos? Therefore in my humble opinion, here it speaks where her companions say that she does not need. See the Bach, siman 330.
