3) [Seif 2] A pregnant woman who smelled food, etc. It is written in the Or Zarua that a pregnant woman must be warned to guard herself to the greatest extent possible not to eat pork, neveilos, or tereifos, for this causes the child to be G-d-fearing. He brings many proofs for this from the Gemara and Yerushalmi. Darkei Moshe, sec. 1. Likewise, a nursing woman who requires food because of illness should not eat forbidden foods, nor should the child himself, for all this harms him in his old age, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 81, seif 7 in the gloss, see there.
4) Ibid. A pregnant woman who smelled food, etc. There is no practical difference whether this occurs at the beginning of her pregnancy or at its end. It is possible that this applies only after 40 days. Mishna Berurah, sec. 1, and Shaar HaTziyun, sec. 1. See above siman 550, sec. 5.
