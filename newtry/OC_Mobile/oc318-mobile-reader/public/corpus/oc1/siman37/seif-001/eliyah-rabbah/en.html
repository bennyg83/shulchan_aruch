[1] Among those who sin against Israel in their bodies, etc. Beit Chadash wrote: even if one does not lay tefillin because he does not have a clean body, and not because of illness beyond his control but he sins with his body through excessive eating and drinking — he is among those who sin against Israel regarding his body. And when he does not lay them because he does not wish to be idle from his work — he is among those who sin against Israel in their bodies, not literally in their bodies, but he has some punishment of Gehenom within twelve months; so far. And see in Nachalat Tzvi.
