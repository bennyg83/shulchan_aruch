Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. Assi 38. It is explained there that tefillin precede mezuzah because they are an obligation of the body; and in any case they precede so as not to be among those who sin against Israel in their body — nevertheless one who refrains for some reason is not among those who sin against Israel in their body. And there too he refrains because he lacks [means] for both; and one might think mezuzah precedes if not for the reason of bodily obligation.
