Seif 3 in the gloss: and one day. I wonder — for this is by law obligated in tefillin, for regarding Torah law we are concerned lest the hairs fell out, as stated in Niddah 46a. And according to what Beit Chadash wrote in siman 255, that if he brought two hairs before thirteen and they are still on him and did not fall out, we are not concerned that he brought other hairs and they fell — it works well here to say that it deals with such a case.
