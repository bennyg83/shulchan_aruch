(1) [Seif 1] Great is the reward of the mitzvah of tefillin. In the Tur he concludes that all who lay them prolong days, as it is said, "the Lord upon them shall live" — and he explains the tekhelet at the end of Menachot 44a; and Rashi explained: those who bear the name of God upon them in tefillin shall live. The reason: Yafeh Lelev wrote in the name of Sefer Chaim, beginning of folio 18a — because the essence of a person's life is in the heart and brain, as our Sages said; therefore one who lays tefillin on the place of his life and sanctifies his mind and heart — behold, he prolongs days. And see what he wrote in the name of the Tikunim, tikun 21; and what the Ari wrote in Shaar HaKavanot, derush 2 on tefillin on the verse "and the matter is," etc., who gave a reason in secret; see there. And further see the reason in the holy book Chafetz Chaim in the derush on tefillin; see there.

(1) Further — the Tur wrote. And Rava said: whoever lays tefillin and wraps in tzitzit and recites Shema and prays is assured that he is a son of the World to Come. Abaye said: I can vouch for him from evening that the fire of Gehenom does not rule over him. Rav Papa said: I can vouch for him from evening that all his sins are forgiven; so far. This is from the Gaon called Shmusha Rabbah, as Beit Yosef wrote; and all the laws of tefillin found in the name of that Gaon were brought by the Rosh at the end of his Leket on the laws of tefillin; see there. Perishah wrote: Rava came to add and said — it is not only that he prolongs days in this world, but he is assured also to be a son of the World to Come, and that is the essence of prolonging days and life. Abaye came and added that not only will he be a son of the World to Come, but Gehenom does not rule over him at all, because no sin will befall him, as written above regarding tzitzit siman 24 — "the angel of the Lord encamps," etc., and delivers them from sin. Rav Papa came and added that even sins he already committed are forgiven if he lays tefillin; so far. And see Chassidim letter 1. All this is if he is careful with them not to treat them lightly through idle chatter or vanity — and how much more so not to do work in them; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch folio 55d. And Kaf HaChaim brought this for the Chafetz Chaim, siman 10, letter 35.
