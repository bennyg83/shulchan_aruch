(Seif katan 2) They practiced, etc. As Beit Yosef writes according to one explanation. The Yerushalmi asks: Why did people not establish a presumption of reliability through wearing tefillin? It answers: because of deceivers. Tosafos in chapter Bameh Ishah explains the question as asking why one who wears tefillin is not presumed trustworthy. The answer is that deceivers would wear tefillin in order to mislead people. Rabbeinu Yerucham, however, explains the question differently: why did upright people not establish the practice of wearing tefillin all day? The answer is: because of deceivers. Meaning, they did not wish to assist deceivers by creating an opportunity for them to mislead people. The deceivers would likewise wear tefillin all day so that others would regard them as righteous and trustworthy.
