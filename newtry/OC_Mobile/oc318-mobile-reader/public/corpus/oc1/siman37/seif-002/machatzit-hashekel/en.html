(S.k. 2) They practiced, etc. As Beit Yosef wrote for one explanation — that we say in Yerushalmi: why did they not keep it? Because of swindlers. Tosafot, chapter 2 of Berachot, explain: why did they not keep trustworthiness in a person who lays tefillin? And it says: because of swindlers who laid tefillin to deceive people; so far. But Rivan explains: why did they not keep the worthy ones laying them all day? And on this it says: because of swindlers — meaning, not to give opportunity to swindlers who would thereby have a place to deceive people, since they too would lay tefillin all day so people would consider them righteous and worthy.
