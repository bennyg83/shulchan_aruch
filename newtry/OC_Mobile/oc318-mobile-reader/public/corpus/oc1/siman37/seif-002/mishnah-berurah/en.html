(5) For them to be on him — for in such a short time one can easily be careful about passing gas and distraction.

(6) At the time of Keriat Shema — for anyone who recites Shema without tefillin is like one who bears false witness against himself. If it did not happen for him then, or he had intestinal illness at the time — all day is his time and he is obligated to lay them at least so as not to nullify one day of the mitzvah of tefillin.

(7) And prayer — and see above siman 25, seif 13. This is for every person; but working people usually study after prayer while wearing tefillin — yet one must be careful not to speak idle words in them, for besides the prohibition of idle words, one thereby comes to distraction. And see above siman 28, s.k. 3, and in Maaseh Rav.
