(5) To have them upon him. During such a short period one can easily guard against passing wind and distraction.
(6) During Krias Shema. Whoever recites Krias Shema without tefillin is considered as though he bears false witness against himself. If tefillin were unavailable at that time, or he suffered from intestinal illness then, the entire day remains the time for the mitzvah. He is obligated to put them on at least at some point so that he does not allow a full day to pass without fulfilling the mitzvah of tefillin.
(7) And prayer. See above, siman 25, seif 13. This applies to every person. Men of exceptional conduct have the custom to study after prayer while still wearing tefillin. One must be careful not to speak idle words while wearing them. Apart from the prohibition against idle speech itself, this leads to distraction from the tefillin. See above, siman 28, seif katan 3, and Maaseh Rav.
