Seif 2: Their mitzvah, etc. We say, “And according to the inhabitants of the West, etc.” Tosafos explains that this refers specifically to one who removed them at night. Rabbi Chiya and Rabbi Huna prayed in them in the evening. See Menachos at the end of chapter 3, the final chapter of Yoma, chapter 16 of Shabbos, chapter 2 of Sukkah, chapter 4 of Megillah, and elsewhere.
But, etc. Shabbos 49a.
And that he not, etc. As stated in several places: a person is obligated to touch his tefillin, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. “that he not, etc.” Tur and Shulchan Aruch therefore omitted it here, because it is included in these requirements.
They practiced, etc. As stated there on 130a. See Tosafos on 49a, s.v. “like Elisha, etc.”: “This is not difficult, etc.”
Nevertheless, etc. As stated in chapter 2 of Berachos. During this limited period he can easily be careful. Rosh.
