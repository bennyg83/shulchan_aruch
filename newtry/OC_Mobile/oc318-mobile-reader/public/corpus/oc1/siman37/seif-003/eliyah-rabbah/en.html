[3] And so they practiced, etc. — But Beit Chadash extended greatly to wonder, and concludes that any child who studies Shas, even if he is only twelve years old, who knows to guard tefillin and not pass gas in them — his father must buy him tefillin, and so they practiced; so far. And Kenesset HaGedolah likewise wrote in the name of Maharash. Magen Avraham wrote: now they practice laying them two or three months before the time; so far.
