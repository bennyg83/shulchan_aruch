Years. Beit Chadash disagrees and wrote that a minor who studies Talmud and knows to guard himself — his father is obligated to buy him tefillin; also Rash HaLevi, part 1, OC siman 1, ruled that from age ten he must educate him in the mitzvah of tefillin. Now the custom is to lay them two or three months before the thirteenth year. Magen Avraham.
