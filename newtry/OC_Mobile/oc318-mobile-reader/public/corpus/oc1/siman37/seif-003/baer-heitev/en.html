Years. Bach disagrees and writes that if a child studies Gemara and knows how to guard himself, his father is obligated to purchase tefillin for him. Rabbi Shmuel HaLevi, part 1, Orach Chayim, siman 1, likewise rules that once he reaches ten years of age, one is obligated to train him in the mitzvah of tefillin. Nowadays the custom is to begin putting them on two or three months before the age of thirteen. Magen Avraham.
