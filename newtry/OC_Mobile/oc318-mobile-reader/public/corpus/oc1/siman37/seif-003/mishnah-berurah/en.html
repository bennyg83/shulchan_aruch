(8) Who knows — for before that he is not permitted to lay tefillin, and he is not yet subject to chinuch.

(9) To educate him — in the mitzvah of laying them, and likewise to teach him the laws needed for this.

(10) Specifically — and before that one does not let him wear tefillin even if he wants, for certainly he does not know to guard tefillin.

(11) Thirteen years — meaning even if he did not bring two hairs [Pri Megadim].

(12) And so they practiced — and now they practice laying them two or three months before the time [Magen Avraham]; and see in Biur Halacha. A deaf-mute who speaks and does not hear, or hears and does not speak, must lay tefillin; but one who neither hears nor speaks — they do not stop him from laying them if he wants [Ba'er Heitev].
