Today. See Baer Heitev and what I wrote above at the end of siman 34. 'Erev Shabbos' appears not to be specific; the same applies on erev Yom Tov. One cannot explain that the sanctity of Shabbos has already begun to shine because adding to Shabbos is required by Torah law, for adding to Yom Tov is likewise required by Torah law, as Beit Yosef writes in siman 261; see there. Responsa Shemesh Tzedakah, Orach Chayyim 7-8, proves that strictly speaking there is no prohibition. One should not protest against a person who puts on tefillin while it is still daytime, before the sanctity of Shabbos takes effect. In siman 7, however, he cites the kabbalist R' Binyamin Kohen, of blessed memory, who asked several leading authorities in Eretz Yisrael, and they told him not to put on tefillin on erev Shabbos.
