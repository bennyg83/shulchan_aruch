The day. Avraham ben HaRambam; and I already wrote on this above in siman 34; see there. And it seems on erev Shabbat it is not specifically so, and the same applies to erev Yom Tov — that per the reason already explained, the sanctity of Shabbat has already begun shining, namely because of the addition of Shabbat that is Torah law; for if so, the addition of Yom Tov is also Torah law, as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 261; see there. And see in responsa Shemesh Tzedakah, part 8, simanim 7 and 8, who proved there is no prohibition by law; and the custom to lay them while it is still day before Shabbat sanctity begins — they do not stop him. But there in siman 7 he wrote in the name of the kabbalist Rabbi Benjamin Cohen, who asked several great ones in the land of Israel, and they told him not to lay tefillin on erev Shabbat.
