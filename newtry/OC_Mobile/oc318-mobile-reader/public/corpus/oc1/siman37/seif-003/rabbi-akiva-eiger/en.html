Seif 3, in Rama: And one day. This is difficult for me. By law he is already obligated in tefillin, because regarding a Torah obligation we are concerned that the two hairs may have fallen out, as stated in Niddah 46a. According to Bach in siman 155, however, if he developed two hairs before the age of thirteen and they remain present and did not fall out, we do not suspect that he developed other hairs that later fell out. Our case may be explained as referring to such circumstances.
