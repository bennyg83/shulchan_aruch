A minor who knows to guard tefillin in purity — that he not sleep in them and not pass gas in them;and not to enter a bathroom with them [Rashi, chapter Lulav HaGazul]} — his father is obligated to buy him tefillin to educate him. {Rama: And some say this "minor" is specifically when he is thirteen years and one day [Ba'al HaItur]; and so is the custom, and one should not change [his own words].] חייב אביו לקנות לו תפילין לחנכו: הגה וי"א דהאי קטן דוקא שהוא בן י"ג שנים ויום א' [בעל העיטור] וכן נהגו ואין לשנות [דברי עצמו]:
