A minor who knows to guard tefillin in purity, such that he will not sleep or flatulate while wearing them his father is obligated to acquire tefillin for him in order to train him [in the commandment]. {Rama: Rema: and not to enter the bathroom with them [based on Rashi in the chapter of "The Stolen Lulav"] Rema: "And some say that this 'minor' is only if he is 13 years and one day (Ba’al HaIitur). And so is the custom and one should not change from it.}
