(s.k. 3) Good, etc. And therefore even if he bakes before the time of its prohibition, nevertheless because of "we do not distinguish" he should heat a second time. And as written in siman 445, that he must make a separate fire when he burns the chametz so as not to benefit from it; and even if he burns it before the time of its prohibition, because of "we do not distinguish." And according to this he must do, etc., that is to say according to the second reason: for if he does not wait, all the more so he would spare burning the bread, since he is heating the oven two times one after another.
And as for what they do with meat and milk, etc. That is according to the second reason; but according to the first reason there is no difficulty. And see in Chok Yaakov who wrote in the name of the Gaon Mahari Horowitz: if he forgot to kasher the oven before Yom Tov and needs the oven on Yom Tov, he permitted heating it on Yom Tov and baking with that same heating, for such a case is permitted on Yom Tov later in siman 509. And even though Rama wrote that it is good and upright to return and heat, etc., nevertheless this language implies it is only a stringency, and in such a case where it is not possible it is permitted; and the author of Chok Yaakov agreed with him. And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wondered: until here he only permitted in siman 509 where light libun suffices, so that it is not recognizable that his intent is to kasher; but an oven that requires kashering so that sparks fly, where it is recognizable that his intent is to kasher, is explained later as forbidden on Yom Tov.
