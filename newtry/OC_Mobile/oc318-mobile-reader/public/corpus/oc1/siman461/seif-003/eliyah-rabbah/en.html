And when he forgot to purge it until Yom Tov, he should do thus: namely heat coals over its entire surface and bake at once without another heating, as stated in siman 509; so wrote Chok Yaakov in the name of Mahari Horowitz; and this requires further study, since we hold here that sparks must fly from it, so it is like something that absorbed from neveilah, which is forbidden in siman 509; and see siman 451 s.k. 13.
