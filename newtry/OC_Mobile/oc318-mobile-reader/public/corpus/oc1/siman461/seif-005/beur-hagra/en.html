If they baked, etc. As written in Zevachim: a wafer that touched a wafer, etc.
The measure of removing. As written in chapter "How do they roast": if gravy dripped from its roast juice, etc., he should remove its place; and as Tosafos wrote in chapter "Gid HaNasheh."
And the rest is permitted. For aroma is nothing according to all opinions regarding something to which greasiness does not apply, as written concerning fatty meat that he roasted, etc.; and as written in siman 447 se'if 1; and the proof is from the loaves of the todah, which were baked as chametz with matzah in one oven, for he did not reduce in Menachos 94a that they be baked one by one, only the two loaves; Mordechai, chapter "Every hour."
A matzah that folded over. From what is written: a person should not blanch two, etc.
They forbid it, etc. That is to say, if the baking was during Pesach, for [then it is] by a morsel.
But other. As written in Chullin 108a-b, that they do not pass from piece to piece without sauce; and see there in Tosafos.
Only the place. As above, according to what is written at the beginning of siman 447 se'if 1: even during Pesach one need forbid only the place of their adhesion; and see Beis Yosef.
A puffed matzah. As written: if it puffed, scrape it, etc.; and see the Aruch.
