And stuck. But when it is not truly stuck one need not forbid; and nevertheless if its lower surface at the place of the fold did not crust, it is forbidden. Later authorities.
It. But other matzos one need not forbid because of a puffed or folded matzah, even if they scraped them with one oven peel, since that itself is a mere stringency, as written above in siman 451 s.k. 46; see there.
The adhesion. Even if found during Pesach, since it was baked before Pesach. And from Rama's silence it implies he holds that even though there is not sixty opposite the place of the fold it is permitted; and so wrote Taz and most poskim; but Rashal and Bach and Maharai require sixty opposite the adhesion; and nevertheless it appears to me that we do not require sixty except opposite the inner side where they are stuck to each other, for on the outer side the fire reaches well; Magen Avraham; and Chok Yaakov concluded that the primary view is that we do not require sixty opposite the place of adhesion; see there; and so wrote in Or Zarua. And if a folded and puffed matzah is found after Pesach, Chok Yaakov concluded that one should not be stringent, since that itself is a mere stringency, and one should permit it when found after Pesach; see there; and not like Or Zarua; and see above siman 447 s.k. 43 what is written there. And see Magen Avraham.
Puffed. There are two interpretations in this: one interpretation, that the thickness of the matzah split and the upper part rose upward; and another interpretation: puffed in its middle, that it did not split at all, but rather the body of the matzah puffed as it is in chametz bread; so wrote Bach. And Maharam of Lublin wrote according to the first interpretation. And Taz lengthened to permit according to the first interpretation and concluded that there is no prohibition except according to the second interpretation; see there. And in Magen Avraham he wrote in the name of Taz that he permitted a puffed [matzah] entirely, and this is its language: I testify upon heaven and earth that I never once saw from my father-in-law the Bach that he forbade any matzah that puffed, etc., and on the contrary he laughed at those who forbid; therefore one should not cause loss of Jewish money for this; end quote of Taz; and he exaggerated his measures to take lightly a matter that our fathers and fathers' fathers practiced as forbidden; end quote of Magen Avraham. Behold, what he wrote in the name of Taz is not found thus in the Taz book before us; rather he makes a compromise between the opinions as I wrote. However in Responsa Taz he wrote the language exactly as Magen Avraham copied in his name. And Chok Yaakov wrote: certainly one should not be lenient in a matter that our fathers practiced as forbidden; nevertheless it is enough to forbid that matzah itself, not as I have seen some who rule and forbid even mixtures even in a morsel as if it were complete chametz, and likewise forbid the Riv Eisen and other vessels and what was used with them, causing loss of Jewish money and withholding Yom Tov joy; for this stringency suffices regarding the matzah itself. And see Shaarei Teshuvah, Devar Shmuel siman 214, and Shevus Yaakov who is also lenient in this; see there. And if after it was baked they smeared it with fat and returned it to the oven and it became puffed, it is permitted according to all opinions. Magen Avraham.
Forbidden. The whole matzah, and even sixty does not avail, even before Pesach, for all of it became chametz; and so wrote Maharam of Lublin siman 105. And so is the agreement of the later authorities; and so wrote in Sefer Or Zarua; and he wrote there in the name of his grandfather the Gaon that one should not forbid unless it is recognizable that the hole rose upward, and the hole must have in it a cavity that holds like a hazelnut that they call a large Hazel-Nuss, or a middle thumb; and so wrote Magen Avraham; and Magen Avraham further wrote: and even with a hole that did not rise upward I have seen the great ones of our generation rule to forbid only the place of the hole, and they permit the rest of the matzah even during Pesach; and so it is written in the name of the Gaon Mahari Horowitz. And if there are two holes, a hole within a hole, they are concerned to forbid all of it. And I found written: if the hole is smooth it is permitted, and if there is belazlik inside it, it is forbidden; end quote.
Touch. And after the fact, if they bit one another and both touch the oven below, then the heat of the fire reaches well below and it was baked properly and is permitted; Rashal; Magen Avraham; and even on Pesach one need not forbid; see there. And see in Teshuvos Beis Yaakov siman 68, who permits a puffed matzah if the puffing is not visible from outside; but if the puffing is visible from outside one should forbid; and he wrote there that he has a tradition from the Gaon Moreinu HaRav Heshel; see there.
