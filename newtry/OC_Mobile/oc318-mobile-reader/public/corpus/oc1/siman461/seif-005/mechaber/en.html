If chametz and matzah were baked together, the matzah is not forbidden unless it touched the chametz — one removes from the place of contact the amount required for removal, and the rest is permitted.Matzah that folded over in the oven and is stuck together such that the fire does not penetrate there — it is forbidden during Pesach, but the other matzot in the oven are permitted. Before Pesach one need only forbid the place of the fold (Terumat HaDeshen siman 157 and his rulings siman 145). Matzah that puffed up) in its middle is forbidden; but if a crust formed over it in the way a crust normally forms over bread during baking, it is permitted (Maharil). If) two matzot lie one on top of the other in the oven before they are baked, they are forbidden as it is like folded matzah (Mahariv). Similarly one must take care that they not touch each other in the oven while still moist, for the fire does not penetrate the place of contact and they come to chametzing (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chayyim}.):
