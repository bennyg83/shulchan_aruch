[1] Several times, etc. And after the fact one may be lenient; but if they heated it only once and did not intend to permit it, and it is doubtful whether the coals went over its entire surface, one should forbid after the fact; also on Shavuos one should be careful about this (Magen Avraham; and this requires further study as to the law). I found written in a gloss that he ruled one should not bake matzah in an oven on a spot where its fellow was already baked, unless one heats the oven a second time; and the proof is from chalitah, which in their days took the place of salting and boiling, for salting is like roasting that is boiling; and we say in Chullin that this vinegar in which he blanched once, one should not blanch in it again, etc., for the strength of the vinegar has weakened; and the same applies here, for the heat of the oven has already cooled at the place where the first matzah sat; end quote. And see Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 67; and this requires further study.
