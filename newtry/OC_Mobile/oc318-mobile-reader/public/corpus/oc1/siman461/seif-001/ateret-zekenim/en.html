Some follow the practice, etc. One must remove a layer of earth at least the thickness of a finger. Less than this is ineffective through wiping alone, and the oven must also be heated. If one removes a matzah from the oven with a chametz peel, even during Pesach, there is no need to be stringent beyond removing a thin outer layer. This is from Maharam Yaffe, and so I received from my teachers, of blessed memory. Regarding a matzah that folded over, see the reasons given in the responsum of Maharam of Lublin. One must take care not to place matzah where flour is present, lest the flour adhere to the matzah and the matzah later be placed into soup or water together with the flour upon it, thereby leading to fermentation. One should not bake a matzah in an oven upon the exact place where another matzah was already baked unless the oven is heated again. The heat of the oven at the place where the first matzah rested has already cooled, and the second matzah will therefore not bake properly. I likewise heard that this was the ruling of Maharshal.
