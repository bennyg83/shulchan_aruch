And after the fact it appears to me that one may be lenient; but if they heated it only once and did not intend to permit it, and it is doubtful whether the coals went over its entire surface, one should forbid after the fact, for an oven has the law of an earthenware vessel and requires thorough heating, as written in siman 451 se'if 2; but there is room to doubt, since people are not careful on Shavuos when baking dairy without kashering the oven and rely on the heating, and one must say they hold that even though [the coals] do not go over its entire surface it is permitted since it absorbed permitted substance, and as written at the end of se'if 4; if so, also with chametz we should be lenient after the fact, as Rama wrote there. And nevertheless it appears to me to forbid; and also on Shavuos one should be careful about this.
