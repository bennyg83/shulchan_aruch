The laws of baking matzah. And in it are 5
seifim: An oven in which chametz was baked — when firing it to bake matzah one must take care that the coals spread across its entire surface, and a flame alone is not sufficient, since just as it absorbs through coals so it releases through coals, and sparks must fly from it. Therefore if it was fired several times before Pesach it is not sufficient unless one specifically intended to kasher it for Pesach, since perhaps the coals did not spread across the entire surface as long as one did not intend this. Some have the custom to apply new earth to it so that it does not require firing, and this is a fine custom.And when kashering through firing, it is good and proper to fire it again for the purpose of baking and not to do everything in one firing (responsum of Mahariv}.):
