And there is on Taz: he forbids after the fact whenever one removed it from the oven and returned it at once before the measure of a mil, since it is hot and becomes chametz at once; and see Magen Avraham letter 6 and "some say," and what he wrote there on the words of Levush; see there; and see Chok Yaakov letter 10 and "some say": when in doubt one should not be stringent; and whenever one inserts a finger and dough does not stick, it is a sign that threads are not trailing; and in Eliyah Rabbah letter 8 he wrote that this is while it is still hot, but when cold this is no longer a sign; and those matzos that they grind on a Riv Eisen and it appears they were baked like half-cooked food of a dog: at any rate one should not forbid on Motzei Pesach a morsel mixture of them; and regarding benefit in such a case one may also tend [to be lenient]. And everything is according to the eyes of the teacher in this.
