(s.k. 1) They heated it, etc. On Shavuos, etc., without kashering. For it is forbidden to kasher on Yom Tov, as stated later in siman 509. Rather, in such a case where he bakes with that same heating, so that it is not recognizable that he did it in order to kasher, and as written in siman 509, it is permitted.
And as written in se'if 7. That is to say, there in siman 451.
And as Rama wrote there in siman 451, that also chametz regarding this is called absorbed permitted substance, and does not need kashering until sparks fly. And even though Magen Avraham wondered there at him, that chametz is not called absorbed permitted substance, nevertheless his difficulty is on Rama, who holds that chametz regarding this is called absorbed permitted substance.
