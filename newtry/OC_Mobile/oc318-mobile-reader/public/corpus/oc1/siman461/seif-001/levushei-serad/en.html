And absorbed issur does not pass from piece to piece without sauce, etc. Some of the later authorities cast doubt, saying that this is not absorbed issur even not at the place of the fold; see Chok Yaakov 447 s.k. 8, and see Shach, Yoreh De'ah siman 66 s.k. 13, and examine carefully; and see Taz, Yoreh De'ah siman 105 s.k. 1 and 22, and Mishbetzos Zahav there likewise implies not like Chok Yaakov; and I explained it in a responsum.
