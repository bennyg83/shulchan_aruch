Since Beis Yosef was silent, it implies that even with wine it is permitted, as he wrote in the name of Rokeach; but not with boiling liquids and not with broth (Maharil); but from the language of the Rif and Rambam and Rosh it implies specifically with water but not with wine, which nullifies the taste of matzah; and so wrote Mahariv, that even though it is permitted to dip the Pesach offering in liquids, that is because its taste is more substantial than matzah, as stated in the Gemara folio 119; therefore one should not be lenient except for a sick person; and even with water the Rif wrote that we permit only for an elderly person or a sick person; and so it appears from the language of the Shulchan Aruch; and in the Gemara that wrote "a person fulfills," etc., it implies after the fact; and all this is whether for the blessing "al achilas matzah" or for afikoman.
