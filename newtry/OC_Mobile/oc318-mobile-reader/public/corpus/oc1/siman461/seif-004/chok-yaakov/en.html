A person fulfills with soaked matzah. And Magen Avraham wrote: and specifically after the fact, or for an elderly person or a sick person even ideally; and if it is impossible for him to eat what is soaked in water, we permit him even what is soaked in wine and other liquids; and for any other person, even after the fact he does not fulfill with what is soaked in wine or in liquids; and in all this there is no distinction between what one blesses upon "al achilas matzah" and regarding afikoman; and see in Bach later in siman 475 what my brother-in-law the Rav wrote in Seif Alef Zayin.
But if he cooked it one does not fulfill with it. And not specifically that he cooked it; rather the same applies when soaked into boiling liquid; so wrote the poskim in the name of Rokeach; and Magen Avraham wrote that even in a kli sheni one should be stringent; and it appears that the same applies to pickling for a full day-cycle even without dissolving, for pickled is like cooked; and see above in siman 473 what is written regarding the law of pickled maror.
