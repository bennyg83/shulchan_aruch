If he cooked it, etc. Because the taste of matzah was nullified; and as for what we say in siman 475 se'if 2, if he swallowed matzah he fulfilled, for we do not require that he taste the taste of matzah: that means he need not have the taste of matzah in the mouth as with maror, but the matzah itself must have the taste of matzah; and see siman 475. And Rokeach wrote that soaked matzah that is permitted means specifically in cold water or wine, and not in boiling liquids.
