(s.k. 4) Some say: before, etc. He wrote in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah: I found written that in a place where matzah stood in the oven and they removed it, or they pushed it with a peel to another place, one should not put another matzah into the oven at that place, for that place has already cooled because of the first matzah, and the second will become chametz before it bakes; and he brought proof from vinegar that one blanched in once: one should not blanch in it a second time, for the strength of the vinegar has already weakened; and as written in Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 67.
