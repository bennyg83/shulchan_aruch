Soaked in water. And Magen Avraham wrote: and specifically after the fact, or for an elderly person or a sick person even ideally. And if it is impossible for him to eat what is soaked in water, we permit him even what is soaked in wine and other liquids. And for any other person, even after the fact he does not fulfill with what is soaked in wine or in liquids. And in all this there is no distinction between what one blesses upon "al achilas matzah" and regarding afikoman; and see Bach.
He cooked it. Not specifically that he cooked it; rather the same applies when soaked into boiling liquid; and Magen Avraham wrote that even in a kli sheni one should be stringent; and it appears that the same applies to pickling for a full day-cycle even without dissolving, for pickled is like cooked; Chok Yaakov; and see above siman 473 regarding the law of pickled maror.
