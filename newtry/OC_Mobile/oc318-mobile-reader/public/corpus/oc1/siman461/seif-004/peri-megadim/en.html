They forbid on Taz: behold it implies that absorbed matter does not pass without sauce, and even a peel is not required; and in Responsa Rashal siman 57 he wrote that whatever with other issurim is a peel, the same applies, etc.; if so one can say it refers to another matter; and see siman 447 se'if 1; however there he concluded that even so a peel is required, namely when it bites and sticks to it; and therefore we require a peel from a kosher matzah; but mere contact does not need a peel; and see Yoreh De'ah 105 in Taz letter 14.
And behold a matzah that became chametz in the oven is complete chametz: that place in the oven needs kashering by libun; see Chok Yaakov letter 14; and the rest does not need; and if it is doubtful that he does not know where its place is, at first glance even with a dry floor, dry into dry, one in two is nullified; and see Yoreh De'ah 110 regarding a fixed shop, and the words of Sefer HaKerisos on this of Nachal Eitan, and the Mishnah in Terumos of a bed among beds; and in Peri there we lengthened; and if they baked thus and the matzos mixed, one can say one in two is nullified, for its issur is not from itself; see siman 447. And there it is explained whether to distinguish between before Pesach or during Pesach; and with a puffed and folded matzah the oven does not need kashering, and the oven peel likewise after the fact; see Chok Yaakov letter 17 and 451 letter 54; and see Magen Avraham 451 letter 37 on what is written there.
Further regarding kashering the oven: ideally one should kasher it before Yom Tov, for the reason that it is fit to heat twice; and after the fact, or erev Pesach that is Shabbos, and he cannot easily bake challah and tzalingt elsewhere, it is like after the fact, and he kashers it on Yom Tov with one heating, as written in Chok Yaakov, which I brought in Magen Avraham. And know that in siman 509 se'if 5 one does not purge and do hagalah on Yom Tov; and an oven does not appear as one who kashers, as written in Magen Avraham; and even a utensil that absorbed a spoiled taste of rabbinic [law]: one does not do hagalah and kasher on Yom Tov; a spit is different, for it is only custom; see there in Magen Avraham 12; and not like one born of impurity who is fit during the days of his impurity; see siman 324. However a vessel forbidden by aroma, or that had in it a dish forbidden by aroma, or absorbed and a morsel, or a kli sheni and absorbed and a morsel: see Magen Avraham 447:41, that even in a place where they practiced to forbid absorbed [taste], when further leniencies join, do not add, and it is permitted; see there; and according to this we found a solution to do hagalah of a vessel on Yom Tov of Pesach itself; see siman 452 se'if 1 in the gloss; and even on Yom Tov it is not a proper repair of absorbed spoiled taste, only custom; and likewise aroma: some are lenient, and it resembles a spit in 509; and absorbed spoiled taste means from this pot it received a spoiled taste from a chametz vessel, absorbed in a kli sheni or the like; understand.
