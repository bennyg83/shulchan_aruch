A person fulfills, etc.: see in Mishnah Berurah that this is for an elderly person and a sick person; and there is another advice for one who finds it hard to eat dry matzah: that he eat crumbled matzah even though it is like flour, and one recites over this haMotzi and "al achilas matzah," provided he eats a ke-zayit (later authorities).
Provided it was not dissolved: behold, from Rashi's commentary it implies that even if it became mushy, so long as it was not completely dissolved one fulfills with it; and see in Bach, which implies that he tends thus as the law; and so it implies in Beur HaGra, who showed a source for this law from what they said: if he dissolved it and gulped it, if it is matzah a person does not fulfill his obligation on Pesach (and that is to say: certainly through soaking, for if through cooking, even if it was not dissolved one does not fulfill, for we hold like R' Yose; but the need for the distinction there in Chullin does not come out so well; see there on folio 120); thus "dissolved" is called where it is fit for gulping. Indeed, from the words of Beis Yosef it implies that even if it was not completely dissolved one also does not fulfill the obligation of matzah; and as for why he said "and gulped it," perhaps he said it for a novel point regarding chametz, that even in this case where he made it like drinking he is also liable, because it is written "nefesh" to include one who drinks, as stated there in the Gemara; and this requires further study.
