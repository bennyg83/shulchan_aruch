Se'if 2. A tpaka. It is the buchya; and they said there: a buchya that they heated from outside, etc.; and if he fills them, etc., that is to say to bake in it by itself; and even though he heated it from outside, it is permitted to bake in it, and not like Resh Lakish, as written at the beginning of chapter 4 of Yevamos; and from this we learn the law is like Resh Lakish in these three, etc.; and even though there is in one of these beraisos of Toras Kohanim that likewise the law is not like R' Yehuda, even though Rava explains his reason from what is written in chapter 4 of Yevamos; and this blanched, etc.; and see Rif and Ran.
Whether, etc., a kubya. As above: the oven and the pan. There, folio 37a.
But to stick it, etc. And so wrote Raavad in the Hasagos, and he brought proof from what is written: one should not scorch two sheaves, etc.; and as written there: while he is cooking it, it becomes chametz; and Rambam rules even if he boiled it and afterward stuck [the dough]; and as written there in the Gemara: come and hear: one fulfills, etc.; so too here, that he boiled, etc.; but according to R' Yehuda, with whom we hold as the Rif wrote there, even if he stuck and afterward boiled; and regarding chametz, according to all opinions, no; and Raavad must hold that in the Gemara they said after the fact, and he says ideally; and so wrote Peri Chadash.
And there is one who says, etc. So that nothing stick and delay the baking, as above.
