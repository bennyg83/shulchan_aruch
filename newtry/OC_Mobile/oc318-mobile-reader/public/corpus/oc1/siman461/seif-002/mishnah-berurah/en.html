(5) But he must heat first, etc.: for if not there is reason to be concerned that it will become chametz before it begins to bake.
(6) Whether a kubya: that is the tpaka mentioned above.
(7) In a pan: wide bowls, and sometimes they bake in them.
(8) Without water: for if there is water there, even if he puts the dough there after the water is boiling, it is also forbidden, for this is chalitah and is forbidden as he wrote above in siman 454 se'if 3.
(9) Whether on the ground: and that is trkinin explained above in siman 68 se'if 15; and see there that one may not bless haMotzi upon it unless he fixed his meal upon it; and it is stated in the Gemara that a person fulfills with this the obligation of matzah on Pesach, for it is significant as bread of poverty.
(10) Some forbid: namely ideally (Gra); and specifically if it did not wait the measure of a mil until the start of baking, for if not according to all opinions it is forbidden even after the fact.
(11) And it is good to be careful: and even after the fact it is proper to be stringent not to eat it if possible for him. If they put matzah on paper inside the oven to bake it with the paper, one need not forbid after the fact, for certainly the heat of the fire reached through the paper and it was baked quickly before it began to become chametz; but ideally one should not do so (later authorities).
(12) In hot ash: namely not to bake them inside the hot ash, for there is reason to be concerned lest they become chametz before they begin to bake; and if he transgressed and baked, one need not forbid.
