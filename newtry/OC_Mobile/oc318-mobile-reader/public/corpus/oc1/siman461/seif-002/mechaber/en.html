A new ceramic roof tile (meaning: one of the tiles with which they cover the roof — kupi in Old French) — whether it was fired from inside or outside, it is permitted, since the heat dominates beneath it; even though the flame does not rise above it, it boils vigorously and the bread bakes immediately and does not come to leavening. However one must first fire — whether an oven, a kubiyah, a dry frying pan, or on the ground — before baking. But attaching the dough first and then firing — some forbid it, and it is good to be careful.
