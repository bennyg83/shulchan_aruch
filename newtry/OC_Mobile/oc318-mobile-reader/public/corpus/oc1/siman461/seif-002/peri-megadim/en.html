If on Taz: see Rif and Rambam of blessed memory, chapter 6 halacha 6 of Chametz uMatzah: the reason is that cooking nullifies the taste of matzah; and Pesachim 41a says according to R' Yose the reason is that cooking nullifies the baking and it is not bread; see there Tosafos s.v. "But"; this is according to what one might have thought; but according to us, who hold that cooked foods retain their status and one blesses Borei Pri HaAdamah, therefore the reason is that there is no taste of matzah, as concluded in Berachos 38b; and see Peri Chadash here. And what Taz wrote to distinguish regarding swallowing matzah: so too Levush wrote, and fitness for mixing is required; which is not so with maror, where we require a remembrance that "they embittered their lives," that he taste a bitter taste in his mouth. And I have reflections on what Rashba of blessed memory wrote in Berachos 38b, that R' Meir and R' Yehuda agree that we require the taste of matzah, for R' Meir admits regarding pickled and thoroughly cooked maror that one does not fulfill; rather R' Meir holds that cooking does not nullify the taste of matzah; and it is somewhat difficult that they should disagree about a factual matter; and according to what Bach and Taz of blessed memory wrote, one can say maror is different, as aforesaid. Also what he wrote regarding wrapping, that he is concerned lest maror nullify the matzah: one can say it is like something not fit for mixing; and see Penei Yehoshua there; and see Lechem Mishneh chapter 8 halacha 8 of Hilchos Korban Pesach; and according to what was clarified at the conclusion of Berachos, that R' Yose holds cooking does not nullify baking, only we require the taste of matzah; and if so the Rabbis who disagree with Rabbi hold like R' Yose; and regarding thoroughly cooked it was necessary [to teach] for one who roasted and afterward cooked; and see Bach regarding the Pesach offering, whose taste is substantial, whether cooking nullifies the roasting; and with wine it is permitted implies that it does not nullify the taste of matzah; and see Magen Avraham letter 7.
If they ground matzah: one fulfills with crumbs on the first night; see siman 168 se'if 10; and crumbs that have no ke-zayit, which lack the appearance of bread, when the water blanched or with a bubbling liquid: one does not fulfill; see there in Magen Avraham to this view, and Taz 15; and the rule is: whatever one does not bless haMotzi and Birkas HaMazon upon, one does not fulfill; see Rambam of blessed memory, chapter 6 halacha 7 of Chametz uMatzah; and with God's help in 475 and 482 this will be explained further.
A question. Flour roasted in the oven and afterward kneaded with water and baked in the oven: does one fulfill on Pesach? See Magen Avraham 471 letter 5; and see Maggid Mishneh chapter 6 of Chametz uMatzah halacha 5; and in Lechem Mishneh there: fruit juice alone, whether it is called coming to leavening; and according to Magen Avraham he said with water; see there; and if so, flour roasted beforehand in the oven does not afterward come to leavening, and it does not avail that it is of a type that comes to leavening; and see siman 454. However, bread of poverty: its taste was not changed by this. And see Beis Yosef here: roasted flour is permitted to cook, and Rabbeinu Yeruham forbids; see there; and see Peri Chadash here; and with God's help in 463 se'if 3 this will be explained; and here there is no need to lengthen.
