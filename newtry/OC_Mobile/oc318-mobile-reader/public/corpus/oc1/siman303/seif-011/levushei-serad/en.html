There since woman fastens with it — on mishnah she may not go out with unperforated needle, if went out exempt — challenges for what fit: not ornament, burden, why exempt; answers deals fastening hair, challenges would be like birit permitted l'chatchila, therefore answers not fastening hair but parting hair, challenges for what fit — not garment nor ornament, burden though needed to part hair; answers unperforated needle with gold plate on head, weekday parts hair, Shabbat places against forehead and adorns, ornament therefore exempt but forbidden lest remove; Tosafot challenged need not return from first answer fastening hair to establish parting — could establish fastening and still forbidden since gold plate ornament concern remove; answered if fastening hair never places against forehead since must hide hair — not her way with such needle — Gemara answers for parting hair and adorning Shabbat.
