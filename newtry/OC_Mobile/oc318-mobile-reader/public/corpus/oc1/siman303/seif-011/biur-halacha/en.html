(א) She may not go out with kulyar — see Mishnah Berurah conclusion: where she closes — permitted l'chatchila; per Magen Avraham and Taz s.k. 8 agreeing Tosafot on unperforated needle with ornament name even fastening knots forbidden — kulyar also forbidden; nevertheless possible leniency per Tosafot themselves Bava Batra 176b permit l'chatchila, no remove/show lest expose flesh — kulyar better; Gaon Tosafot Rishonim noted; Mechaber stam stringent even when closing, retracted Beit Yosef — unclear; correct as Mishnah Berurah and Shaarei Tzedek in name Perishah and many Rishonim.

(ב) Spice tied in it — only if contains spice; if none at all liable on knot itself — not called ornament because absorbed scent [Gemara].
