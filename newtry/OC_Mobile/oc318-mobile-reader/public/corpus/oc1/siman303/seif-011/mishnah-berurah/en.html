(א) She may not go out with kulyar — mishnah: liable chatat on kulyar; see below.

(ב) With which she fastens etc. — kulyar's way is for this; but now she does not close cloak with it but loads thus — therefore liable chatat, not considered ornament but burden only to show wealth; if still closes cloak openings — permitted l'chatchila as ornament, no remove/show concern, for then she would expose flesh; see Biur Halacha.

(ג) It is a knot etc. — woman with bad odor loads it, not liable since ornament; but l'chatchila forbidden lest remove and carry.

(ד) Spice tied in it — likewise may not go out with vial of perfumed oil fixed on arm.
