Whose point was removed. Mordechai ch. 4 Parshat Rishonim: they may not go out with needle whose point or eye removed — no designation for tumah — end. At start Kelim we say if point or eye removed forbidden to move — if so "they may not go out" R' Yosef said is not precise, for moving also forbidden — end Rabbenu Yosef; likewise here in Shulchan Aruch — he did not analyze well, for Gemara deals with perforated needle therefore suffices point or eye; Mordechai deals with unperforated — when point removed, i.e. her point or eye meaning the knot, still fit for its matter until both removed — explicit in Sefer HaTerumah siman 254.
