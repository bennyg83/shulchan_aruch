(א) She is liable. Even if fastening her knots with it — there is no way to place it in perforated needle and it is a burden (Beit Yosef); Bach wrote exempt — not its way of carrying — he did not analyze well, for Gemara challenges "for what is it fit" and otherwise she would be liable chatat and it is not answered that carrying is not its way; moreover Tosafot daf 11 wrote if inserted in garment she is liable — what difference garment or kerchief; Beit Yosef's words are primary.

(ב) Permitted to go out. Implies even when not ornament — not so; only when she has gold plate on head and places against forehead — prohibition only lest remove and show; but here since done for modesty not remove and show; but when not ornament she may not go out, for she cannot go out with all needed things since neither garment nor ornament — burden; not like Beit Yosef; likewise Tosafot and Rosh explicitly — end Bach; he did not analyze that Tosafot and Rosh likewise per Rashi see there: deals when this needle not needed at all for dressing but only parts hair with it — they wrote it is burden; but when fastening knots with it conversely Gemara challenges and permits, answers like gold plate on head on weekday parts hair, Shabbat places against forehead and we decree lest remove and show; Tosafot wrote if she fastens hair with it she never places against forehead — not considered ornament and not show — permitted — end; therefore poskim ruled if fastening knots permitted, but when placed against forehead and has plate — forbidden lest show; however if made like ornament on head even fastening knots forbidden lest show as Beit Yosef in name Semag and Terumah who hold like Tosafot that even for modesty we decree lest show see there — not like Beit Yosef; possibly with our needles this does not apply and they practiced leniency — see siman 18 and likewise Raavan and Sefer HaTerumah and see siman 9.
