(א) She may not go out etc. — even when inserted in garment, way of carrying for woman all agree; for man see above siman 301 seif 8.

(ב) She is liable — it is burden even when fastening knots, inserting needle in kerchief or closing cloak openings — no way to fasten and close with perforated; therefore burden; her way of carrying thus on weekdays to give needles inserted in kerchief or garment.

(ג) Her knots — kerchief knots or closing cloak openings etc.

(ד) Permitted — some Acharonim wrote only when needle not made to adorn; then permitted because used for dressing need — like handle of garments; if made to adorn, e.g. thick head like ornament — rabbinically forbidden to fasten knots lest remove and show. Some permit all cases since for modesty not remove and show lest hair uncovered — Gra sides thus in Beur; main permit reason when fastening knots is modesty; therefore do not protest those lenient, especially our unperforated needle [called shpilke] even if thick head not so much like ornament — possibly all permit; certainly rely to be lenient fastening knots.

(ה) Forbidden — to go out inserted in kerchief or garment even if adorning, e.g. thick head like ornament — no Torah liability, still rabbinically forbidden lest remove and show; all the more if not like ornament — considered burden and liable chatat. Know: when not like ornament, even if needed for some purpose e.g. parting hair — still full burden, person cannot go out with all needed items that are neither garment nor ornament.
