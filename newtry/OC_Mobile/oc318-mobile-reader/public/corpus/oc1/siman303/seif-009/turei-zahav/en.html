(א) She fastens her knots with it. Meaning she inserts the needle into the hairnet and fastens the hairnet with it so hairs not emerge outside her knots — clear in the Gemara that when she fastens her knots, i.e. what the Gemara calls at first "since a woman fastens her hair with it," they challenged: the mishnah teaches and not with an unperforated needle — one cannot fasten with it, for then we should say it is permitted as they challenged, and it would be like a birit; Tosafot explain the permit: not like Rashi who explained because for modesty she will not remove — for regarding koveles it is literally like a birit and we are concerned lest she remove; rather here it is not an ornament and permitted because it is like a ring of utensils and a way of dress since it is for ankle houses need; therefore they establish the mishnah that forbids parting hair when she has a gold plate on Shabbat placed against her forehead where there is an ornament on it — therefore forbidden lest she remove etc. Tosafot and Rosh: even in what a woman needs, whatever is neither garment nor ornament is a burden and forbidden; thus for parting hair she needs a gold plate that is an ornament, forbidden lest she remove. But when fastening knots it is full permit like birit as a handle of a utensil — specifically when not an ornament; if ornament, forbidden lest she remove; not like Rashi that for modesty she will not remove — proof from koveles. The rule per Tosafot and Rosh: parting hair when not ornament — chatat liability since not a way of dress; if ornament — rabbinically forbidden lest she remove. When fastening knots it is a way of dress like a handle of her veil — permitted even rabbinically when not ornament; if ornament, also rabbinically forbidden — permit only when not ornament. Sefer Mitzvot Gadol wrote: unperforated needle when not ornament but only parting hair, even if fastening knots with it — forbidden to go out since neither ornament nor garment — a burden; likewise Tosafot and Rosh, not as Shulchan Aruch permits stam when fastening knots; he cited Rashi that for modesty she will not remove — all this without precision; when fastening knots full permit as utensil handle even if not ornament — likewise Tosafot, Rosh, Tur, Shulchan Aruch: this is like dress needed for clothing; but parting hair has no clothing need only bodily need — burden when not ornament; we do not say here "since for modesty" even when fastening knots — simple in Gemara per Tosafot except his mishnah troubled him. Maharach challenged Beit Yosef on Tur "she may not go out with perforated needle" even if fastening knots — liable chatat since carrying where she fastens knots is not its way of carrying — not a difficulty, we explained this in siman 301 "she may not go out with needle"; likewise for a man inserting in garment as Tur wrote explicitly above — for a man certainly not a way of carrying unless in hand; but for a woman her way is to go with needle in kerchief where she fastens knots or for parting hair; since perforated it is a burden for woman and she is liable chatat.

(ב) And if she does not fasten her knots with it — forbidden. Difficult: if here it is not ornament, even when fastening it should be forbidden lest she remove per Tosafot as above; if when not fastening there is even chatat liability as burden — the Gemara's difficulty "for what is it fit" — say "forbidden" here is the opposite of the first clause that teaches permitted, but in truth liable chatat. Tur wrote: she may not go out with needle with which she parts hair even if made to adorn; but needle with which she fastens knots may go out; she may not go out with perforated needle — if she went out liable; unperforated — should not go out, if did exempt. Must explain: parting hair — chatat only if ornament with gold plate as Gemara — then rabbinically forbidden lest remove; needle that fastens and is not for adorning may go out as garment; may not go out perforated — liable even if ornament since perforated is burden for woman. Unperforated and ornament — forbidden l'chatchila lest remove; if not ornament explained above distinction between parting or fastening — simple whatever has no need at all is all the more liable when not ornament; Tur and Shulchan Aruch abbreviated here — clear per Tosafot and Rosh; Beit Yosef brought Semag on unperforated contradicting each other. Beit Yosef explained permit when inserted in place of veil, forbid when not — wonder at his holy mouth: even in place of veil rabbinic prohibition per Tosafot s.v. vetihavei ke-birit — they imply even when fastening knots rabbinically forbidden if ornament; Semag considers thick head ornament. It appears Semag held Tosafot's challenge on Rashi — Rashi's reason fastening hair for modesty she won't remove, Tosafot disagree and compare to koveles: per their explanation she inserts needle in hairnet and presses hairnet so hairs not fall — properly similar to birit holding ankle houses; Tosafot hold even for modesty she may remove since not immediately revealed — likewise Rosh explicitly: like birit — if removed by hand ankles not immediately revealed until houses fall; but per Rashi hair outside knots wraps around needle and inserts in hairnet underneath so hairs not seen — Tosafot concede for modesty she won't remove, for if she removed hairs immediately revealed — better than birit and koveles above ankles — very correct. I learned from Semak: silver and gold ornaments under married women's veil permitted, those on veil forbidden in public domain — under veil is Rashi, on veil is Tosafot; Semag likewise. "Permitted unperforated" — under veil; "forbidden" — on veil, concern lest remove since ornament per Semag because head thick. Difficult per Semag why Gemara returns from establishing "fastens hair" — could establish thick head ornament; similarly Tosafot challenged gold plate — they answered if gold plate while fastening hair she could not place on forehead; but when ornament from thick head we can say so — requires study.
