Whether made etc. — see Beit Yosef proved Rif holds thus from stam permitting without distinction of aniska; Rambam likewise; therefore ruled halacha though Rosh, Ran, Tur explicitly forbid aniska; in our Peri Chadash explicitly forbid — see reason there; likewise Rashi on kelilah; examining Rif and Razah shows no proof he permits — as Kenesset HaGedolah expanded; therefore doubt if may be lenient with beads on thread against all who forbid.
