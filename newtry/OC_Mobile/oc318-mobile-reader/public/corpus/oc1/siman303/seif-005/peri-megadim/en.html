(א) On katlah — Taz seif 7 (Shabbat 64), Tosafot Shabbat 57b s.v. here; rule: all she tightens to appear fleshy even if not gilded forbidden for chatzitzah per Rashi 57a s.v. chavekin; if loose — if important forbidden lest show as seif 7; if not important and loose permitted per Bach there.

(ב) Mechaber wrote not totafot and serabitin when not sewn — lest remove and show; sewn in braid permitted per Tur; Rema in Pithei Shabbat likewise; Magen Avraham there; however totafot and serabitin reason lest fall and carry four amot — all sewn to each other permitted; Rashi and Rif hold lest show ornament — only in braid not uncovering hair; nevertheless why did Mechaber change and write sewn in braid — tied in braid also permitted as Tur; Levush seif 2 wrote tied permitted; seif 5 kelilah permitted since tied; seif 26 s.k. 1 tied and has trouble etc. holds all tied no concern lest untie and show — remembers Shabbat; totafot only in braid — their custom tie in braid; likewise all tied permitted; Mechaber holds even tied if permitted to untie on Shabbat and ornament concern lest remove and show unless only in braid; possibly tied in braid means sewn; seif 26 not important ornament to remove and show therefore all tied not thrown off — no concern lest fall; Magen Avraham note 6; cannot expand — expanded in novellae. Beit Yosef wrote Rema's words — slight difficulty — Tur and Rambam seem to dispute totafot explanation as I wrote; see Shabbat 57a Rav Huna poverty makes colors and wealth silver gold — appears answer mishnah when not sewn means each other; here fixed in braid for colors mishnah colors not considered, sewn each other suffices; braita silver gold only in braid; Rema cannot say thus wrote both weekday and Shabbat 65 colors too — needs study all this.

(ג) Further: all Sages forbade woman for chatzitzah — possibly androgynous frequent immersion like woman; tumtum safeik — rabbinic lenient; male not decreed even chatzitzah items — uncommon obligatory immersion even Talmud times pure food; woman even old virgin may not go out Taz note 2 — all the more for chullin and kodshim immersion as here.
