In Magen Avraham s.k. 15: he did not analyze that Gemara deals with perforated needle and Mordechai with unperforated etc. — so it should read; nevertheless requires study, for it appears Beit Yosef only meant not specifically going out on Shabbat but moving in general forbidden; since Mordechai wrote point and eye — meaning both removed, since wrote no designation for tumah — if so also for moving forbidden — this is Beit Yosef's intent; as with perforated when one of point or eye removed forbidden to move since not fit for its matter; likewise unperforated both removed forbidden to move for this reason — not specifically R' Yosef on going out; and it appears Magen Avraham explains Mordechai's words that one suffices, whereas Shulchan Aruch here wrote point or eye implying unperforated as appears from his words on Mordechai in Beit Yosef — on this Magen Avraham says Mordechai deals with unperforated and requires both — not as Shulchan Aruch here that one suffices; see below siman 308 seif 11 where perforated is dealt with.
