Because of dyeing — see Mishnah Berurah only rabbinic per Magen Avraham; see Avodah Zarah brought Rishonim ruled like R' Eliezer pokeset [likewise files nails, kohl] liable chatat; for another all agree liable; cited Gemara chapter HaMitznien; Rashba there like Rabbanan; appears he erred — there aliba de-R' Eliezer as end of his words; Tosefta explicit R' Eliezer; reason Rashi: for herself exempt cannot build well; proof from R' Eliezer not proof — possibly R' Eliezer like those Rishonim; Rabbanan certainly exempt for another; Rambam ruled Rabbanan, stam in these matters filing and pokeset, kohl forbidden, did not distinguish liable chatat for another.
