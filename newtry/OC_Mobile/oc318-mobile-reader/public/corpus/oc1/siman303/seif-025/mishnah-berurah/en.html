(א) Serak — red dye; Tosefta: she not wipe face with cloth containing serak — implies even without dye intent, psik reisha; reason: woman accustomed to beautify by face dyeing — appears like dyer; man not accustomed — not called dyer as end siman 320 permits eating staining fruits though colors face and hands while eating; nevertheless woman passing serak on face only rabbinic — no Torah dyeing on human skin; see Biur Halacha.

(ב) To apply kohl — on eyes.

(ג) To smear dough on face — some practice forbidden smoothing hair with milk curd mixed with spices called pomade — I fear chatat, appears memareiach; warn household [Maamar Mordechai].
