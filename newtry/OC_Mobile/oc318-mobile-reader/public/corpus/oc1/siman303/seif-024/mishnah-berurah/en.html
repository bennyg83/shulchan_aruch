(א) With standing stone — what we call stone pregnant women carry [Taz]; Beur Maharshal on Semag: copyist found hollow flint with small stone inside like bell clapper — created thus; brought in Bereishit Rabbah; custom weigh something against stone — also helps; this is "weight weighed against it"; Gemara: if intended and scale found object naturally matches weight without subtracting/adding — has segulah; reason: permit as expert amulet.
(ב) Even not yet pregnant — but fears lest become pregnant and miscarry [Gemara].
