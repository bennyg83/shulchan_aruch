A woman may fasten [her garment] {i.e., tie it} on Shabbos over a nut or over a stone that she designated for this purpose, and go out with it. But over a coin it is forbidden to fasten on Shabbos, for it is not subject to handling, and designation does not help for it; and if she fastened over it before Shabbos, it is permitted to go out with it on Shabbos.
