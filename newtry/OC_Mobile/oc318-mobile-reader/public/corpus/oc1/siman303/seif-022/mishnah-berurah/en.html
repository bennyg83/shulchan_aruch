(א) She ties etc. — wraps in tallit with strap on one end, ties nut or stone on other, wraps strap so tallit not fall; does not appear intending to take nut/stone, all know she needs — unlike salt grain seif 15 not all know, therefore some forbid there.

(ב) She designated for this — from before day; otherwise stone muktzeh like coin; Acharonim: round stone their way to tie — designation one Shabbat helps as Magen Avraham siman 308 s.k. 47; non-round — one Shabbat designation insufficient unless forever — helps even coin as below.

(ג) Designation does not help — one Shabbat; if designated from erev Shabbat forever — helps even coin, muktzeh prohibition removed [Tosafot Rosh; Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger].

(ד) Tied before Shabbat — action effective to remove muktzeh name.
