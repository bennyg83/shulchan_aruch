22) [Seif 5] A kelila, which is an ornament, etc. Only a distinguished woman ordinarily goes out with it, and she will not remove and display it. Regarding ornaments commonly worn by all women, however, even a distinguished woman is forbidden, because the Sages made no distinction. Shiltei Gibborim; Magen Avraham, paragraph 6; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 10.
