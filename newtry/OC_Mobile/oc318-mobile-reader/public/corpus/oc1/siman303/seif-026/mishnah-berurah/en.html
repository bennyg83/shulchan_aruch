(א) To braid — rabbinically like building [homiletical: Heaven built Eve's side]; but braiding detached strands liable weaving per Rambam — here hair attached, not weaving; also will not endure, stands to undo [Magen Avraham; Shulchan Aruch in Tosafot]. Detached foreign braid [paruk] — though no building, forbidden weaving; if not enduring still rabbinic; nevertheless not worth protesting where they won't listen.
(ב) Not to untie braid — resembles demolishing.
(ג) Some forbid parting hair — concern uprooting; first lenient view: no intent, not psik reisha.
