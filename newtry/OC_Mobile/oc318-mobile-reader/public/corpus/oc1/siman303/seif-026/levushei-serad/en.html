Shulchan Aruch seif 16 she may untie — things usually removed, Sages forbade going out wearing lest when removing forget Shabbat and carry; permitted to go out wearing — not usual to remove — permitted tie and untie public domain: if remembers Shabbat when untying won't carry; if forgets, forbidding untie won't help — Tosafot start Bameh Ishah.
