20) [Seif 4] Which is a round ornament, etc. Although only a distinguished woman ordinarily wears it, we are nevertheless concerned that she might carry it, because it is more valuable than the kelila discussed below. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 9.
21) There: Which they attach, etc. It is not fully attached and can be removed. Since it is an ornament, we are concerned that she might remove and display it. This reason also applies according to Rabbeinu Tam's explanation. Levush Serad.
