(א) Provided she not put them initially. Some say likewise shoe forbidden; some permit; doubtful to him if same applies (Hagahot Semak).

(ב) She does not return. Tosafot Eruvin 102 wrote specifically when fell on ground, but on utensil permitted as siman 328 s.k. 25 — these words omitted from latter authors — Taz; in Shulchan Aruch he did not know what Taz wrote, likewise in his name siman 321; in truth per them it was omitted what Tosafot wrote above, i.e. specifically per R' Peretz; but per Rashi and Rosh who wrote here forbidden because it appears he intends to take pepper outside — per them even on utensil forbidden; likewise Tosafot Shabbat ch. 6 — so is primary.

(ג) And with ankle houses. Because not ornament and not remove and show; and see seif 9 in name Tosafot; but per Rashi it is ornament only she won't remove since for modesty — therefore forbidden on arm.
