(א) For healing eye-stroke etc. — appears because in their time this was proven remedy, like expert amulet; no remove/show as not adornment.

(ב) Tied and tightened — otherwise concern lest fall and carry.

(ג) In her ear — made to absorb ear discharge.

(ד) In sandal — for comfort; also need tie lest fall and carry; unlike menstruation cotton — even if falls will not take at all since repulsive.

(ה) Tied in sandal — Shulchan Aruch wording implies here not required tightened; likewise Tosafot Rosh.

(ו) On every side — excluding our pantofle — concern when not tied lest fall and carry.

(ז) And distress her — but if only to prepare soiling to save garments from soiling — forbidden as siman 301 seif 13.

(ח) Handle — for her cotton she can hold by handle; teaches even so repulsive to take.

(ט) And pepper and salt grain — pepper for mouth odor, salt for tooth ailment.

(י) And provided etc. — this "provided" refers only to what she puts in mouth; but regarding cotton above even l'chatchila permitted Shabbat; many Acharonim likewise permit l'chatchila putting straw in our fully closed shoes; good to be stringent unless need.

(כ) She not put initially etc. — all healing they decreed lest crush spices, or appears scheming to carry; unlike above cotton where need evident and not scheming [Ateret Zekenim].

(ל) If fell she not return — appears scheming to carry; likewise some say main reason like initial placement, decree crushing spices; per this reason forbid return only on ground, not on utensil as siman 328 s.k. 25 regarding rag; Gra Beur implies likewise.

(מ) They tie etc. — even if salve is adornment, not ornament lest remove and show — need for garment, like garment handle; certainly won't remove lest ankle houses fall.

(נ) With salve — in Shibolei Leket and Rishonim: salve or strap; fits ending "not concerned lest slip" — salve and strap referred; clear in Semak.

(ס) With etzba'ah on arm — see Biur Halacha: placed and tightened on flesh not on shirt above — permitted, no concern remove and show lest arm uncovered; same reason on leg; some say only leg lenient not arm.

(ע) That adheres etc. — if loose, concern fall and carry.
