(א) And she may go out with etzba'ah etc. — see Magen Avraham s.k. 12 tied this to Tosafot and Rashi seif 9; etzba'ah refers as Acharonim wrote; per this Shulchan Aruch stam first view like Tosafot; Beit Yosef implies like Rashi — Tosafot wondered; Magen Avraham words very strange: he tied to Tosafot birit not ornament, likewise etzba'ah — not comparable: birit is ring of utensils for ankle houses, pure, permitted Shabbat; etzba'ah on arm is tame per verse — ornament, why permitted Shabbat? Peri Megadim raised; truth: permit as Rambam on etzba'ah arm and leg — ornaments not shown to people; etzba'ah on flesh under shirt [as ends "adheres"]; Rambam no concern remove/show; some say only leg not arm.

(ב) And some forbid on arm — Rav HaMagid wrote clear from Rashi only permitted birit on ankles lest fall; not arm; per above Rambam etzba'ah on flesh — possibly Rashi agrees; Gra disagrees: per Rashi even on leg not permitted except birit on ankles; etzba'ah on leg itself — may remove not before people then show — his intent.
