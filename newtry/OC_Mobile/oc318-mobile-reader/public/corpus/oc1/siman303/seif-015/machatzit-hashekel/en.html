(א) (s.k. 15) Point removed in Mordechai etc. — this law is Mordechai's words; Beit Yosef brought to explain his words not as Rabbenu Yonatan understood.

(ב) If in it etc. — meaning matter that designation helps tumah like vessel designated for olives if pierced olive-size nullified vessel tumah; if designated for pomegranates larger receives tumah since hole not pomegranate-size; while not designated no vessel tumah; for Shabbat even not designated is vessel — mishnah all broken vessels if still fit melacha permitted move; below siman 308 seif 6; for tumah not vessel as Menachot; needle point removed and end even by designation not vessel tumah unless work done; also Shabbat not vessel — Tosafot start Kelim; Rabbenu Yonatan innovated two things: R' Yehuda words not as Rabbenu Yonatan understood forbidden even moving; Rabbenu Yonatan agrees with Rabbenu Yonatan on moving; further innovated R' Yehuda "point removed and end" vav of v'end is dividing vav as if wrote or end; one suffices to forbid — Shulchan Aruch likewise; Magen Avraham disputes needs both.

(ג) Didn't examine well — Gemara deals perforated needle then if one removed no longer fit; Mordechai certainly unperforated; meaning Mordechai certainly unperforated; if point removed etc. as girsa; Mordechai certainly unperforated — if went out not as Rabbenu Yonatan thought only moving; nevertheless teaches going out; perforated whole forbidden even fastening as above.

(ד) (And Shulchan Aruch his words are Mordechai — also certainly unperforated; unperforated needs both removed; if only one removed still fit and permitted move.)
