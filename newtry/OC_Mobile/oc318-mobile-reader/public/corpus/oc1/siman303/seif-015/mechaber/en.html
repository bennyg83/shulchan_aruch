A woman may go out with a knot made as a remedy against the evil eye, so that it should not prevail; with absorbent material tied and secured in her ear; with absorbent material in her sandal when it is tied to the sandal; and with a sandal or shoe that is closed on all sides, even if the material is not tied. She may also go out with absorbent material that she prepared for her menstrual flow. {Rama: This is so that blood should not fall upon her and cause her distress. [Beit Yosef in the name of the authorities]} Even if it is not tied, it is permitted, since it is repulsive and there is no concern that she will remove it and carry it. She may likewise go out with pepper or a grain of salt, or anything placed in her mouth as a remedy, provided that she did not place it in her mouth initially on Shabbos. If it fell out, she may not return it to her mouth. A false tooth or a gold tooth may be worn outside according to some authorities, while others prohibit it.
