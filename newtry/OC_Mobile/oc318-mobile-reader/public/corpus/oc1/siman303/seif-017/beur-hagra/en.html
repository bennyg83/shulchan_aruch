Seif 17 some forbid — Yerushalmi brought Rosh 10 story R' Gamliel etc. — meaning in hand on finger — if literally in hand why say this etc. — all ornaments liable chatat if carry in hand as lest remove etc. see Gemara 62b may not go out key in hand etc. even rabbis agree there even gold forbidden as Yerushalmi all permit there and Rosh — all the more iron is burden as siman 301; said even in courtyard as Yerushalmi and second version seif 18 who are forbidders here Ramban and Rashba; in hand permitted as Magen Avraham two songs etc. likewise all vessels etc.
