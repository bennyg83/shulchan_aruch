(א) In house courtyard — even joined courtyard; this is second view seif 18 below: whatever forbidden to go out wearing in public domain forbidden to walk even in house and joined courtyard.

(ב) Only in hand etc. — where permitted in hand to courtyard, no forget and take to public domain concern; otherwise no utensil could move in house lest forget; but permitted wearing on belt — common to forget and go everywhere even public domain without removing — this is reason of second view seif 18: ornament forbidden public domain as wearing — forbidden adorn at home and courtyard.
