(א) Forbidden to comb — uprooting body hair, tolada of shearing.
(ב) That impossible etc. — therefore even without uproot intent forbidden psik reisha; Gemara: all who comb to remove dangling hairs [partially detached] intend to separate hairs well; from this even soft comb forbidden if not manner without removing those hairs. Yeshuot Yaakov: saw people lenient combing Shabbat nearly permitted — woe to eyes; scholar should warn; they answer need fix hair for dignity — can do permitted way above; also seif 87; besides can smooth with hand — why violate serious prohibition.
(ג) Lest uproot hairs — nevertheless permitted fix little head hair with pig-hair utensil — only combing forbidden as removes hair; fix little permitted; comb forbidden even for little; practice designate pig-hair utensil for Shabbat lest appear weekday work.
(ד) And to arrange with hand — separate hairs one from another; Rambam: smooth hair with hand, scrape nails; even if hair falls not concerned, no intent to remove; possibly won't remove; see siman 326 seif 9 and Yoreh Deah and Mishnah Berurah there — not smooth with something that certainly removes hair.
