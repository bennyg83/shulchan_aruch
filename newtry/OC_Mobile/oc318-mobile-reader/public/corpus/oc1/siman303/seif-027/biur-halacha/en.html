To smooth and arrange with hand — Gemara pokeset forbidden shevut like building; Rashi in teachers' name: fixes hair with comb or hand; Ribash: Rashi not combing — forbidden shearing; other fixing with comb; possibly girls after combed wash comb in spiced oil water to stick hairs — like building; implies otherwise permitted this fix after combed [Magen Avraham; see s.k. 22 Magen Avraham Ribash forbids even little fix with comb unless already combed].
