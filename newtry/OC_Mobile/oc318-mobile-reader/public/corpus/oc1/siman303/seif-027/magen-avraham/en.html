(א) That hairs will be uprooted. So Rashi Shabbat 81a — psik reisha; Ribash siman 394 challenged: in Nazir 42b explicitly reason forbidden combing is to remove dangling unconnected hairs — he intends thus — end; possibly Rashi comes to teach even without intending to remove hairs forbidden; Gemara there deals with soft comb — Ribash: girls fix a little head hair with pig-hair utensil — known no uprooting concern; but comb forbidden; permitted scratching animal for hairs are thick [see siman 323 s.k. 2]; even per view psik reisha not an issue permitted as siman 320 seif 18 — nevertheless since he wants separation and impossible without removal — liable like one who cuts nails liable for shearing though nails not needed in Mishkan where they sheared tachash skins though wool not needed [see siman 340] — unlike flaying skin as Shabbat 94b — end; see siman 498.

(ב) To smooth. Rashi 51a: smooths hair with neter and sand and arranges — separates hairs one from another; in Nazir Rashi and Tosafot arrange — separates hairs; simple forbidden to smooth with something that certainly removes hair as Nazir "pokeset" forbidden — Rashi fixes hair with comb and hand; Ribash: possibly washing comb in spiced oil water and passing on head to stick hairs together and lay on head — similar to building — end; implies otherwise permitted.
