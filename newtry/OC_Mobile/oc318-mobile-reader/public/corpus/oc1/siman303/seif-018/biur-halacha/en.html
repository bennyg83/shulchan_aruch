(א) Whatever they forbade etc. — brief explanation of views: mishnah — all ornaments forbidden even courtyard except koveles and foreign braid permitted lest repulsive to husband; R' Chanina bar Shila from R' Yishmael disagrees — all permitted in courtyard; Rambam and many like Rav; within this: Rambam et al. — only unjoined courtyard resembles public domain; joined courtyard and house — adorn permitted — first view in Shulchan Aruch; Ramban et al. — even joined courtyard forbidden, likewise house — first Yesh Omrim; Tosafot — halacha like R' Chanina — all permitted even unjoined — second Yesh Omrim; expanded in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 62ff.

(ב) And now we have no full public domain — Mechaber siman 345 seif 7 stam like first view public domain exists; here as Yesh Omrim wrote; Magen Avraham siman 13 and 345 wrote in name Mechaber no public domain now — not so [Tosafot Shabbat]; see siman 345 Biur Halacha. Question on this view: why not blow shofar Rosh Hashanah Shabbat nowadays since no public domain and no carry four amos concern.

(ג) Because in what has no use they listen — implies not fully relying on permissions, only not protesting — better unwitting [Acharonim].
