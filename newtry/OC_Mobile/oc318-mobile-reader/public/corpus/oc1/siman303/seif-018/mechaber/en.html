(41) Whatever the Sages forbade to go out with in the public domain is forbidden to go out with in an unjoined courtyard (58)(59), except a koveles and a foreign braid, meaning a hair braid she braided within her hair (42)(60). Some say whatever they forbade to go out with, even to adorn with at home is forbidden, and all the more so to go out to a joined courtyard (12)(61), except koveles and foreign braid (43). Some say everything is permitted to go out with in a courtyard even if unjoined (44). And today our women are accustomed to go out with all ornaments (45)(62), and some said by law they are forbidden but since they will not listen it is better they be unwitting than intentional (46), and some learned merit for them saying they practice per the latter view I wrote that they did not forbid ornaments in an unjoined courtyard — and now that we have no full public domain, all our public domains are karmelit and their law is like an unjoined courtyard and permitted.And some give another reason to permit: now ornaments are common and they go out with them even on weekdays, so no concern lest she remove and show as in their days when they only went out with them on Shabbat and they were not common (Tosafot chapter Kol Kitvei and Hagahot Alfasi chapter Bameh Ishah).} (47)(63) However a ring with a seal for a woman and without a seal for a man — we learned she is liable for a chatat (64) — even in a karmelit is forbidden even for us, and likewise all where chatat liability was stated (48). Some say that nowadays men are accustomed to go out with a ring without a seal — it is an ornament for them and permitted; and therefore possibly since women now go out with a ring that has a seal it is an ornament for them and permitted (49)(65)(66).
