Seif 18: And some say that nowadays the custom — its source is the Ran who wrote thus: per Rabbeinu Tam for men there is no concern of remove and show — for us permitted to go out, also without seal on it is now ornament; but per ruling of Shulchan Aruch siman 301 seif 9 ornament for man and woman — also for man forbidden — if so what helps that now no seal — it is ornament; nevertheless it is also ornament for woman and forbidden rabbinically, and even with seal since now also ornament for women it is worse; if so this leniency is only per those who permit that we have no public domain and they did not decree remove and show; but per what Mechaber wrote "nevertheless must warn women" etc. "they will listen to us" — implies we do not rely on these permissions, only we do not protest to women — better unwitting; if so apparently now men who heed sages should not go out with ring whether with or without seal — analyze; and it appears Rama intended above siman 301 seif 9 as wrote, and see siman 303 seif 18; possibly Mechaber's intent since there is merit to say our karmelit is like unjoined courtyard one may rely on Ran that for man there is no remove/show concern at all; nevertheless baal nefesh should be concerned for himself not to go out with ring at all.
