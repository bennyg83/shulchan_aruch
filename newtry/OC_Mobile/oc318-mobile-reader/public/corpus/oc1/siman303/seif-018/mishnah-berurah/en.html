(א) To go out to courtyard etc. — house to courtyard; likewise not adorn in unjoined courtyard as resembles public domain; but to joined courtyard permitted, all the more in house.
(ב) Except koveles etc. — wool cap seif 3, likewise itztima there; reason: permitted these ornaments so she not repulsive to husband if not adorn at all.
(ג) Hair braid — detached hair she piles on hair above to appear hairy.
(ד) Except koveles etc. — permitted all courtyards so not repulsive to husband as above; see Beur HaGra sides with first view.
(ה) Accustomed to go out — even our public domain; then Mechaber explains custom: some say by law forbidden, for even if permit unjoined courtyard, our public domain even if not full public domain per "banners in desert" — at least karmelit, no Gemara view permitting karmelit; some merit: now no public domain per many poskim, no decree lest resemble public domain — our karmelit like unjoined courtyard and permitted — Shulchan Aruch explanation for Beit Yosef inquirers though wording slightly strained.
(ו) However etc. — when wearing, no burden, only rabbinic even full public domain lest remove and show — therefore lenient in our areas; unlike this case.
(ז) Even in karmelit — likewise even courtyard forbidden [Gra].
(ח) It is for them etc. — in Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger: though need not protest those with whom to rely, baal nefesh should concern not go out with ring at all — besides eruv.
(ט) It is for them etc. — per what wrote initially per latter view ornaments permitted.
(י) Nevertheless etc. — even per all lenient views only ornament not non-ornament; therefore what permitted seif 9 needle for knots — no more needles in kerchief than needed for knots; needle not fastening knots not ornament at all as explained seif 9.
