(א) (s.k. 3) And some say concern lest she loosen them.

(ב) And to loosen permitted on Shabbat; but since even if loosens still forbidden to immerse in them as Yoreh Deah 198 seif 2; but after loosening can slip straps from braid without undoing — therefore concern immersion occurs, loosens braid, slips straps, immerses, afterward carries straps four amot in public domain.
