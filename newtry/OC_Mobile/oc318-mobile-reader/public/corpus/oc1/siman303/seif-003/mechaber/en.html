(9)(11) She may not go out with a woolen cap, meaning wool threads that are cut and made like a basket (meaning like vessels made from palm lulav leaves that are woven — unlike an itztima made like a type of felt as in the Gemara there), two fingerbreadths wide (12) like the measure of a tzitz, nor with an itztima, meaning a cloth from which colored threads hang that they hang before a bride to drive flies from her..
