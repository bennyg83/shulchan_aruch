14) There: But a katla around her neck is forbidden, etc. Here the reason is that it interposes. A katla is explained in seif 7. There, however, its importance creates concern that she might remove and display it. Why does the present ruling give interposition as the reason? Tosafos explains that since she tightens it so that she will appear fleshy, she will not remove it, because she would then no longer appear fleshy. Below, however, the case involves a katla lying loosely around her neck, which creates no interposition. It is forbidden only because she might remove and display it. Taz, paragraph 5.
