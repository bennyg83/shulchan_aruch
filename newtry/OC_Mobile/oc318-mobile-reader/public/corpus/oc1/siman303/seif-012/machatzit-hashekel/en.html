(א) (s.k. 12) And in houses etc. see seif 9 Magen Avraham s.k. 8 — birit for ankle houses; therefore forbidden on arm — "some forbid" Mechaber.

(ב) Know Rama gloss seif 18 reason permit ornaments nowadays since ornaments common — nevertheless chatzitzah items or lest disgrace like torn sandal or gold tooth Rama reason doesn't apply — likewise Sefer Teshuvot.

(ג) Know Shulchan Aruch seif 16 in all she may go out may untie erev Shabbat etc. — Tosafot start Bameh Ishah reason: if concern forgets today Shabbat comes carry four amot — what use forbidding untie Shabbat erev Shabbat; if won't forget Shabbat even permit untie won't carry four amot; if concern remembers Shabbat — use forbidding permit — immediately after untying forgets Shabbat carries four amot erev Shabbat remote concern forgets Shabbat shortly — unlike not adorning ornaments decreed concern while still home remembers Shabbat if forbidden won't adorn; if permit adorn now knows Shabbat nevertheless going out long time meets friend meanwhile forgets Shabbat removes and smooths carries four amot — Acharonim brought not like garment.

(ד) Know Shulchan Aruch may go out with eben tekuma — Taz is what called shtarins in Yiddish; Sefer Aleph Rabbenu wrote in name copyist Biyurei Rashal on Semag stone hollow small stone inside like grape in cluster; scale weighed against eben tekuma found stone by itself equal weight without cutting diminishing.
