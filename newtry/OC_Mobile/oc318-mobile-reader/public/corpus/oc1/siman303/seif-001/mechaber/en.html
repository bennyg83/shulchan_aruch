The laws of a woman's ornaments, and it contains 27 seifim:
A woman may not go out with woolen threads, nor with flax threads, nor with strips on her head, because (1) she must remove them at the time of immersion — we are concerned (2)(3) lest she carry them four amos in the public domain. (2) And if they are woven into her hair it is permitted (3)(4), though some forbid (4). And if they are (5) woven work it is permitted (6) because she need not remove them at immersion.Provided (2)(6) they are not soiled or gilded (3), for then she removes them (5) so they not become soiled in the water (Tur, Rosh, and Semag).
