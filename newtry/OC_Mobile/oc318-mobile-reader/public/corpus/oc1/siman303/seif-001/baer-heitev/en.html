(א) Immersion. The Ba'er Hetev before me wrote: if a virgin is forbidden because of this decree — requires study, for virgins are forbidden to immerse as written in Yoreh Deah siman 183 see there. I do not know why he is more doubtful about a virgin than an older woman, for immersion does not apply to her either; in my view there is no doubt the Sages did not distinguish in their enactment, and likewise the Taz in s.k. (4) [2] — see there.
