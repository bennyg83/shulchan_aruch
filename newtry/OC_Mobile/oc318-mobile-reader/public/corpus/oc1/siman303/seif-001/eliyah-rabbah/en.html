[3] An act of weaving, etc. This requires study. In Yoreh Deah siman 198 it is ruled that it constitutes a barrier for immersion, and the matter will be explained there fully.
[4] [Levush] Because it interposes, etc. This requires study, for in Yoreh Deah there he cites Rashi's wording that she removes it so that it will not become soiled.
