(א) Lest she carry them. Specifically with small things like these we are concerned she may carry, for even on weekdays sometimes she carries them in hand — unlike main garments (Tosafot).

(ב) Woven into her hair — permitted. For it is forbidden to undo hair braids on Shabbat, all the more so on Yom Kippur.

(ג) And some forbid. We are concerned she may loosen them (Bach).

(ד) She need not remove them. Requires study Yoreh Deah siman 198 seif 3 stam like Raavad — specifically when hollow she need not remove them.

(ה) So they not become soiled. And they are chatzitzot as written in Yoreh Deah there.
