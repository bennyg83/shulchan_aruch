(א) On her head — all these refer to this, excluding threads on her neck as in seif 2.

(ב) That she must remove them etc. — lest obligatory immersion occur and she be forced to remove them because of chatzitzah and carry four amos. See Acharonim who wrote the Sages did not distinguish in their enactment — even old woman and virgin may not go out.

(ג) Lest she carry them — specifically with small things like these we are concerned, since even on weekdays she sometimes carries them in hand; but main garments we are not concerned, for her way is not to walk without them and she will certainly wear them after immersion.

(ד) Woven into her hair — permitted, for no concern lest she carry, since forbidden to undo hair braid as in s.k. 26; reason of "some forbid": lest when obligatory immersion occurs she undo braid via gentile and take threads and then carry [Taz]; other reasons see Bach and Beur HaGra.

(ה) Woven work — these do not tighten well and do not interpose; see Yoreh Deah siman 198 where stam in s.k. 3 only when woven work they are hollow need not remove.

(ו) That they not be soiled — when threads soiled in mud, water dissolves mud and soils flesh on ascending from immersion, therefore her way to remove initially; since she removes for soiling she may carry; likewise gilded threads she removes before immersion so threads not soiled in water and then carry. Some add she must remove before immersion for chatzitzah since she is particular about them.
