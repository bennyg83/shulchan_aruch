(א) Woven threads — Taz: Tosafot 57a s.v. bameh see there and below seif 26 forbidden to braid and undo hair on Shabbat, Magen Avraham note 2; what he wrote via gentile as siman 307 seif 5 shevut di-shevut for immersion mitzvah they did not decree — therefore Tosafot per their view forbidding even shevut di-shevut for mitzvah, see Tur 307 there challenged well and answered Magen Avraham and Mishna Berurah note 11 gedilah forbidden d'oraisa likewise undoing — see there seif 26. Rashi two voices per mishnah on woven: if loosens somewhat permitted to immerse, not chatzitzah, may go out Shabbat; fixed hair threads need not loosen — explicitly 64a, Rosh wrote inside braid loosening does not help until removes entirely — from fixed threads inside braid also forbidden, only on hair not chatzitzah; Bach explained loosen means pulls thread out entirely — Rosh not placed there, per Rashi water enters between threads to hair (Bach may have different girsa not "to hair"); plainly leaves threads and Rosh forbids immerse until removes threads at least — loosening threads useless; Yoreh Deah 198 seif 2 inside braid loosening useless — if loosens braid and removes all thread permitted though hairs still braided; from combing law 199 seif 1 one may say must undo braid entirely erev Shabbat — needs study. Rashi "braids hair with them" — wraps around hair like tzitzit gedilah — Mishna Berurah in name Raavan; Tosafot did not challenge Rashi etc. explains not inside braid — Rosh likewise — needs study.
