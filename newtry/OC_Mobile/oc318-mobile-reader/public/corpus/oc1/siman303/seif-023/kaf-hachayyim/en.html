23) There: Strung upon a thread. According to Rosh, however, if they are strung upon a thread it is forbidden. Tur cites this. The Shulchan Aruch rules like Rif and Rambam, who permit it even when strung upon a thread, as explained in Beis Yosef. This follows Maran's known method when the three pillars of halachic instruction, Rif, Rambam, and Rosh, disagree: he follows the majority. The later authorities likewise permit it in accordance with the Shulchan Aruch.
