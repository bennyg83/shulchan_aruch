(א) (s.k. 23) To comb etc. plain forbidden etc. as Nazir taught R' Yishmael may not comb in dirt because removes hair — likewise Tosafot Shabbat 57b s.v. R' Shimon etc.

(ב) Pokess etc. is separate matter and law — mishnah Shabbat 94b.

(ג) Implies without etc. — meaning dry comb without oil and water.
