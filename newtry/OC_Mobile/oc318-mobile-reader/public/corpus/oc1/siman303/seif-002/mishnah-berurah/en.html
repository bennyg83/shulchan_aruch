(א) They are loose — she does not tighten strongly, does not choke herself, unlike katlah which is wide strap as below seif 7 and does not choke; threads not forbidden lest remove and show — that applies only to ornament.

(ב) Because she tightens — therefore chatzitzah concern; though below seif 7 implies reason is important item and concern lest remove and show — there deals with loose around neck, still forbidden lest remove and show; here when she tightens to appear fleshy she will not remove, for then not appear fleshy, yet forbidden because must remove for chatzitzah.

(ג) We are concerned etc. — they are types of ornaments.

(ד) No such concern — she does not remove hairnet on Shabbat lest hair be uncovered.
