(5) She may go out with threads on her neck (4)(7) that are loose and she need not remove them at immersion (6). But with a katlah on her neck it is forbidden because she must remove it at immersion (8), since she tightens it to appear fleshy — and we are concerned lest she come to carry four amos in the public domain (7). She may not go out with a totefes, which is like a forehead plate reaching from ear to ear, nor with sharvitin, which is also a plate reaching only to the cheeks that she wraps on her head and hangs on her cheeks from here and from there — when they are not sewn into her hairnet (9), for we are concerned lest she remove them to show her friend and come to carry four amos in the public domain (8). But if sewn (10) there is no such concern and it is permitted.Some say regarding a virgin who is not concerned about uncovering her head, even if sewn into the hairnet it is forbidden, for we are concerned lest she remove it with the hairnet and carry (Or Zarua).
