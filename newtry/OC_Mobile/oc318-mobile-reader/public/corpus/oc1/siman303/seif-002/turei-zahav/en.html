(א) They are loose etc. It appears they did not forbid here lest she remove and show, for that applies only to an ornament that is a novelty.

(ב) With a katlah on her neck. Here the reason is because of immersion — it is a chatzitzah; and katlah is explained in seif 7, but there there is prohibition lest she remove and show her friend since it is important; if so why need a chatzitzah reason here? Tosafot explain: here since she tightens to appear fleshy she will not remove it, for then she would not appear fleshy; but below it deals with a loose katlah around her neck with no chatzitzah, only forbidden lest she remove etc. The distinction between threads and katlah: the Gemara says threads are not tightened strongly because she does not choke herself, unlike katlah which is wide and does tighten.
