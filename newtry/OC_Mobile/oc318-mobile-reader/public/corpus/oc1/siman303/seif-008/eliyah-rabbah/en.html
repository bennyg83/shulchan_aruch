(8) [Dress] and not comparable to totefes etc. therefore etc. — Levush took Beit Yosef's words: not comparable to totefes — totefes not tied, if she comes to tie she ties in hairnet; but kelilah always ties, not in hairnet but hanging straps she ties with them — end his words. Requires study: Gemara 59a — reason whose way to go out with kelilah is important woman, important woman does not remove and show — if so not comparable to totefes whose way is all women — then even important forbidden as Tosafot wrote; therefore Levush misaimed Beit Yosef — Beit Yosef intended reverse difficulty: totefes permitted tied, here if not important woman forbidden even tied; answers for totefes clear recognition in tying since not her way and won't remove; tied in hairnet won't uncover head — unlike kelilah whose way is to tie, not in hairnet — concern she unties tying to show unless important woman — this is clear.
