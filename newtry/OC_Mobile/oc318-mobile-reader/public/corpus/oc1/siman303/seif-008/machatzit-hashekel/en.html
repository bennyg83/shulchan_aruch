(א) (s.k. 8) Permitted etc. not precise — Tosafot challenged Bach three times: refuted Bach's proofs from Tosafot and Rosh where no plate even fastening hair nevertheless liable — Tosafot only on answer parts hair; Tosafot wrote in such case is burden liable as above s.k. before.

(ב) But etc. on contrary challenges v'tishteri etc. — meaning as answers first fastens hair challenges would be like birit permitted l'chatchila though not yet gold plate; second Magen Avraham challenge; answers like plate etc.; Tosafot third challenge per their view v'tehei k'birit permitted because birit and needle fastening not ornament only for garment need like ring of vessels no concern lest remove — should answer deals with gold plate is ornament therefore forbidden lest remove etc.; answer since fastens hair doesn't place on forehead — implies if fastens and gold plate places on forehead forbidden lest remove; opposite Bach who wrote with gold plate permitted when fastening.

(ג) Nevertheless if made like ornament etc. — learned from Semag per Magen Avraham: Semag unperforated one end thick closes coat key forbidden lest remove; Rabbenu Yonatan appears unperforated customary to be inserted in garment not place that closes — if place that closes even l'chatchila permitted; Magen Avraham disputes explains Semag thick head is ornament therefore even closes still forbidden lest remove though modesty — Tosafot view on birit unlike Rashi.

(ד) What Magen Avraham wrote therefore poskim stam — stam deals without plate.
