In Magen Avraham s.k. 22: and impossible without removal. Meaning removal causes separation of hairs tied by dangling tangled strands; when he removes these hairs his intent in separating hairs is fulfilled — it pleases him to remove these hairs even though he does not want these hairs that tangled and tied — not called for this psik reisha de-nicha leih; like one who cuts nails liable for shearing though in Mishkan they sheared tachash skins though wool not needed — unlike flaying skin where we say if he takes with small tongs no liability for flaying because Mishkan work in flaying was mainly for skin, and since he ruins skin no melacha in flaying — as Ribash explains see there.
