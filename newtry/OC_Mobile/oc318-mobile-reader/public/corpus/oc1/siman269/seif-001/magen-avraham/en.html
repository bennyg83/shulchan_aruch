(א) They give a taste to a minor. Although it is forbidden to feed a minor a prohibition by hand as written siman 343 — that is regarding something that has a negative commandment; but kiddush is only a positive commandment (Hagah Maimoniyot in the name of Maharam). And per what is written in siman 343 that even a d'rabbanan prohibition does not apply to feeding by hand — one must say since some say even an adult may drink and fulfills through this kiddush, although we do not rule thus, nevertheless for minors it is permitted; or alternatively since it is a public need it is permitted [Beit Yosef]. And Bach wrote they give specifically to minors who have not reached education; but in responsum of Ran siman 82 in the name of Rashba that if one gives to such a minor it is a blessing in vain, for he has not reached education in blessing; and even in a place where they practice giving to the circumcised infant on the day of milah — that is because the blessing is for his sake; but in kiddush not — rather one must give to a minor who reached education. And per what Rabbi Yosef Karo wrote in siman 343 in the name of Rashba and Ran that in everything that is for the need of an infant such as eating and drinking, one may feed him by hand, for the Sages did not decree on it — for even in the synagogue there is a mitzvah to feed him and they do not educate him even for hours as written siman 326, all the more here. And there is a distinction between eating a prohibition and eating something permitted at a forbidden time, as Tosafot wrote Pesachim daf 106b; and all the more permitted to feed them on Shabbat morning before kiddush, for some say even an adult may eat as Raavad wrote — see there. And it appears to me it is forbidden to answer the minors, as written siman 326; and we are not concerned perhaps he will continue, for regarding something that is for the infant's correction they did not decree (Rashba in responsum siman 82 / 92). And that they give them a taste in kiddush and did not decree — because it is not a fixed matter, for sometimes there are guests and they do not give taste to the infant (Rashba siman 303): Hagah Maimoniyot wrote if there is no infant in the synagogue they give an adult to drink for kiddush and havdalah — end of his words. And it appears to me for kiddush he should drink the required measure as written siman 273 s.h. 5; and so Bach; and it appears to me the one who makes kiddush should have in mind to fulfill at his home through this kiddush, for some say one fulfills through this; and nevertheless he may sanctify again at home as written siman 273 s.d.
