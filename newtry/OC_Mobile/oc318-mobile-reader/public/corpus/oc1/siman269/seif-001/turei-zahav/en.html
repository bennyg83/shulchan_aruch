They are accustomed to make kiddush in the synagogue. Tur brings the words of Rav Natronai Gaon and wonders at them from the law; but in truth they are wondrous from the explanation of his language that is not correct; and a wide explanation is found on them: Beit Yosef explained they would make the first kiddush for healing and that was in the synagogue, and the second at home on account of kiddush in the place of a meal — and that is wondrous, for if so there is a blessing in vain, Heaven forbid; also regarding tasting that Rav Natronai wrote is for healing without specification and afterward wrote to place on their eyes; and furthermore at the start he did not bring proof that tasting is healing and afterward regarding eyes he brought proof from what we say one coarse step, etc. And in my humble opinion thus is the explanation of Rav Natronai Gaon's words: he explains that which we say kiddush is only in the place of a meal — the reason is not as Rashi brings in Beit Yosef that the Rabbis relied on the verse "call Shabbat delight" in a place of delight shall be the reading; rather on account of healing, for perhaps this was a tradition in his hand that the meal he makes after kiddush is healing for him, and therefore they agreed to kiddush for tasting — and that is the meal, as Tur wrote end of siman 273 in the name of the Geonim that drinking a cup is called in the place of a meal; found that there is no obligation of tasting on account of kiddush itself, for kiddush is without it and even without wine at all suffices by Torah law for kiddush as Tosafot chapter Ayin Peh daf 106 wrote — rather they relied together for healing. And the matter of kiddush in the synagogue of Rav Natronai Gaon was certainly that not all the congregation drank from the kiddush cup — that is a strange thing; and what he wrote that all the congregation taste — the explanation is not in the synagogue but rather all the congregation taste in their homes after kiddush on account of healing; and in the synagogue they did not drink, but perhaps one drank or they gave to an infant and the rest of the congregation placed on their eyes on account of healing as we say one coarse step, etc. And therefore here specifically proof is needed from above that he mentioned healing regarding tasting — he did not need proof that this is the essence of the explanation of kiddush in the place of a meal as written; rather here with placing on eyes and not drinking proof is needed that this too is healing; and the explanation of the matters is thus: although kiddush is only in the place of a meal, nevertheless they make kiddush in the synagogue, for the tasting of kiddush is not on account of the mitzvah of kiddush; and what all the congregation taste in their homes is on account of healing, not on account of the obligation of kiddush — for kiddush is fine without it, so it is in their homes; and this that he makes kiddush in the synagogue and gives to the congregation and they did not drink but only placed on their eyes — that too is for healing as stated on account of one coarse step, etc. And one may say: why place on the eye for healing — behold better the healing should be in their homes afterward through tasting; to this he answered: because there is one who has no wine and will not have this healing; therefore they enacted to make kiddush in the synagogue on wine and to place on their eyes — found both are in their hands: the mitzvah of kiddush although they did not taste, for the tasting is on account of healing; and here there is healing in what they place on the eyes as above. Correct is the explanation of Rav Natronai Gaon's words, and the difficulty of our teacher Tur is also resolved on them, with Heaven's help.
