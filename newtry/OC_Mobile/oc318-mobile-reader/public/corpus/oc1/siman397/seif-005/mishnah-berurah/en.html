(1) Handing over to a shepherd, etc. - and affi' to a shepherd, Akoum Dahari is like the objects of Akoum DKIL Dakuna Shbita and is not drawn after Alim Yisrael [MA]:
(2) She even gave him a kiot - Dabar from the kiot his opinion was not to buy a strike from him but from the shepherd who handed it over to him the next day and it means that the author's language ends, even if there are several shepherds in the city since he only gave it to one, he is like the shepherd's feet, but some of the rulings of Holkin 17 and Soberin said that if there are two shepherds in the city and he handed over to one of the kiot, then he is like the owner's feet since it was not clarified from the Iott when buying a strike to whom he would give Tomorrow remains in the possession of the owner anyway, if not that he is accustomed to handing it over to one of them at that time, then he is like the feet of that shepherd. And I know that the OT does not belong to the Shabbat as well as the law of the OT and the OT and the SIA and the rest of the dinin belongs only to the OT and for that reason he applied it to all of them here [BAH]:
(3) Because he did not buy one of them - RL Davg such that if there are several shepherds, there is no way to hand over what they have in their possession for the purpose of hunting property. And the author's closing means that there are no two, whether he handed over to two shepherds on Shabbat, or whether he handed over to them on Shabbat. The shepherds can indeed go if at the time it was given to them he did not find out to which of them he gave custody, and the next day on Shabbat he found out that one of them was given custody, then he is like the owner since he did not inquire about the tax return and also if he handed over to the two shepherds on Shabbat in person the opinion of the majority of the judges is therefore like the owner's feet:
