(1) (SK 9) After all, siman 19 is like min b'mino and is not nullified in the dough — but regarding other prohibitions the law is not so; see Peri Chadash Yoreh De'ah siman 102. This matter is discussed siman 113 below. Rama wrote Magen Avraham there: if not apparent in appearance but only in taste it is nullified even if it came to fix the pot, such as water and salt in dough — end of his words. But this case is different, for these are Magen Avraham's words per Tosafos Beitzah 39a s.v. mishum — they answered first: regarding techumim it is different since they are stringent as min b'mino because it is monetary; water and salt have other owners like any money and one must nullify the owner's name — difficult; therefore they were stringent in doubt. And why did the author mention water and salt — one may say he cited mishnah language in Beitzah; nevertheless since we have two answers of Tosafos the Mechaber should not mislead — truth is only when it comes to fix is it not nullified; with Tosafos' first answer, since it is monetary, but in general not — if so the Mechaber holds only when it comes to fix; with both Tosafos answers together when monetary and comes to fix.
