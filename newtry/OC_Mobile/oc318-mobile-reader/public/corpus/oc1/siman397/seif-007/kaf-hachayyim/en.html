7) There. Medium steps. A person's ordinary stride is one amah. Rashi. This equals the length of two shoes: one shoe corresponds to the place occupied by the foot, and one shoe to the space between one foot and the other. Mahari Weil, siman 45; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, there; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2; Chayei Adam, rule 76, subsection 1. Ritva writes in his novellae that all this applies when one needs to walk on Shabbos for a mitzvah purpose. One may count the steps because this is comparable to measuring for a mitzvah purpose, which is permitted, as stated in siman 306, se'if 7. See there. Tosefes Shabbos, there.
