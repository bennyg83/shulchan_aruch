A designated corner. The Tur and Rosh imply that when he designated a corner for the owner's objects, he did not accept responsibility for guarding them, but merely told him, “The house is before you.” They are therefore considered as remaining under the owner's control. This also appears from the Gemara, which states that in the other case he accepted responsibility for guarding them. See Choshen Mishpat, siman 398.
