17) [Se'if 4] One who entrusts his animal to his son, etc. People commonly entrust their utensils to their children without intending to transfer them into the children's authority. Maggid Mishneh, chapter 5 of Hilchos Yom Tov, halachah 11; Magen Avraham, subsection 3; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 4; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 4.
