1) [Se'if 1] Every person has two thousand amos, etc. This is derived from the verse, 'Let no man go out from his place,' which refers to two thousand amos. Eruvin 51a. This prohibition is only Rabbinic, and the verse is merely a Scriptural support. Rif and Rosh write this at the end of chapter 1 of Eruvin, as does the Tur. Regarding traveling beyond twelve mil, however, Rif writes there that the Yerushalmi states that the prohibition is of Torah origin. See there. Rambam likewise writes this at the beginning of chapter 27. Maggid Mishneh writes there that this is the consensus of the Geonim. Rosh and Mordechai, however, write that according to the Babylonian Gemara, even traveling beyond twelve mil is prohibited only by Rabbinic law. Maggid Mishneh writes that Ramban and Rashba agree with this opinion. See there. Beis Yosef cites their words. Know that even according to the authorities who maintain that traveling beyond twelve mil is prohibited by Torah law, the violation does not incur kareis but only transgresses a negative commandment, since it is not counted among the thirty-nine melachos. Rambam there accordingly mentions only liability for lashes. Sefer HaChinuch and Sefer Mitzvos Hashem, parashas Beshalach, write likewise. See there. Mateh Yehudah challenges Levush's statement that one is liable to bring a sin offering, and Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1, cites the challenge. See there. See above, siman 334, subsection 137, and below, siman 404, subsection 14.
2) The ruling that traveling beyond twelve mil is prohibited by Torah law applies specifically to the person himself. The techum of his utensils and animals is only Rabbinic. Maharlanch, siman 28. Maharam Alshakar, siman 41, appears to disagree. Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef. See above at the end of siman 305 in the gloss, and below, subsection 12.
3) There. He has two thousand amos, etc. If a false accusation was brought against him, he may travel beyond the techum because of danger to life. Maharil's Derashos states that he does not even require atonement. Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, subsection 1.
4) There. Beyond his four amos. Every person has four amos at the place where he established his Shabbos residence, as stated in the preceding siman. From there, two thousand amos are measured in every direction.
5) There. Or from the place where he established his Shabbos residence. If he established his Shabbos residence in a place surrounded by partitions, the entire enclosed area is regarded as four amos, as stated in the preceding siman, se'if 2. Two thousand amos are then measured in every direction from around those partitions.
