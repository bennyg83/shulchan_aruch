6) [Se'if 2] He walks two thousand steps. This is in addition to his four amos, as stated in the preceding se'if. See siman 399, se'if 10, where he is granted the diagonal measurement. This is also implied by Beis Yosef. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 4. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, writes likewise. See our words there, with the help of Heaven.
