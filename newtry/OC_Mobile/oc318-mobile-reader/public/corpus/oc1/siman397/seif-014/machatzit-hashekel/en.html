(א) (note 14) As the feet of, etc. — according to what we hold, etc. — this law is a dispute between Rav Nachman and Rav Sheshet in Beitzah 39. Rav Nachman holds it follows the feet of the one for whom it was filled; Rav Sheshet holds it follows the feet of the filler. It concludes they dispute regarding one who lifts a found object for his fellow: Rav Nachman according to his reasoning that he holds the fellow did not acquire. Nevertheless the filler did not intend to acquire, and even the one for whom it was filled did not acquire — likewise ownerless property — therefore when they came into the hands of the one for whom they were filled he acquired from ownerlessness. Rav Sheshet holds one who lifts a found object for his fellow — his fellow acquires; nevertheless since the one for whom it was filled did not acquire except through the filler — since he could have acquired for himself, etc. — therefore it follows the feet of the filler. We hold like Rav Sheshet.
