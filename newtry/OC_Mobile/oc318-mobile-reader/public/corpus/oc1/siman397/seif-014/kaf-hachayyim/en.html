14) There. So too his utensils and animal, etc. The same applies to his produce and anything else belonging to him. Beitzah 39b and Rashi there. Avodas HaKodesh, folio 35, writes: When does this apply? When the items are within his techum. Even if they are stored in warehouses outside the city, whenever they are within his techum they have the same techum as he does. If, however, they are situated in another city, then although the residents of that city established an eruv in his direction and can reach the place where he established his Shabbos residence, his produce has the status of that city and may not be moved from its place.
