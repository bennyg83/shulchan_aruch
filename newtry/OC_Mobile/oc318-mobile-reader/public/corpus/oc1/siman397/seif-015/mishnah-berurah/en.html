(א) (36) Rivers that draw — even if they do not flow; and those that flow — even if they do not exit from their place of spring to continue further.

(ב) (37) Like the feet of the one who fills — even if they are not hefker, such as belonging to some city or individual; the reason is since they move they did not acquire residence with their owners, and behold they are like the feet of the one who fills them, who then acquired residence at his rest when they were then cut off from their place of spring or from their place of flow.

(ג) (38) Of them on Shabbat, etc. — since they did not acquire residence at all outside the techum because they move. Rains that fall from eve of Yom Tov near the city — all the more when they fell in the city itself — are like the feet of the people of that city; the reason is because the people of the city relied on them; but when they fell on Yom Tov they are like the feet of the one who fills.
