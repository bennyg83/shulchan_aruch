(א) Those who draw — it is implied in the gemara: even if they do not flow, since they move they do not acquire residence.

(ב) Those that flow — and even though they do not exit outside their place of spring for a distance further, rains that fall on Yom Tov near the city are like the feet of the people of that city; all the more when they have fallen within the city itself, for they did not acquire residence in the ocean or clouds which constantly move (Yeshuas Shnei, gemara). And it is implied in Tosafot daf 47 that within the techum is called near the city. And see siman 402 and siman 497 seif 8 and siman 498 seif 3 and siman 558 s.k. 61.
