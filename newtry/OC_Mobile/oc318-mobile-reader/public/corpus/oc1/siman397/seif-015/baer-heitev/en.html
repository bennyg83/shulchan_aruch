(א) Those who draw — even if they do not flow, since they move they do not acquire residence.

(ב) Those that flow — even if they do not exit except from their place of spring. Magen Avraham.

(ג) Outside — rains that fall on Yom Tov near the city are like the feet of the people of that city; all the more when they have fallen within the city itself, for they did not acquire residence in the ocean or clouds which constantly move — see Yeshuas Shnei, gemara. And it is implied in Tosafot daf 46 that within the techum is called near the city. Magen Avraham see there.
