For the need of his friend, behold they are as the feet of the filler — see Mishna Berurah, which we wrote that there are disputants on this; and we shall explain that in truth the Mechaber's method is per the words of Rabbeinu Tam and the Rosh and the Ran [also in the chiddushim of the Ra'ah to Beitzah he holds the method of Rabbeinu Tam, except that he is inclined there to say that even as the feet of the filler he speaks, but also as the feet of the one for whom it was filled must be, and the second cannot carry except to a place where both of them made eruv — see there; and his reason is understood according to what he wrote in the chiddushim of the Rashba to challenge the view of Rabbeinu Tam — see in the chiddushim of the Rashba and examine]. But in truth most rishonim disagree on this, and their opinion is that behold they are as the feet of the one for whom it was filled, according to what is established everywhere: one who lifts a found object for his friend — his friend acquires it; and they are Rashi and Rashbam brought in Tosafos, and so ruled R' Chananel in his commentary [and what is brought in the hagahah of Maimonides in the name of R' Chananel is garbled and perhaps there is a textual error there], and so is the opinion of the author of the Ma'or and the Ra'avad there in his hasagot, and so is the opinion of the Rashba in his chiddushim — see there — who extended and challenged the view of Rabbeinu Tam; and so is the Meiri inclined — see there; and so is the opinion of the Maggid, who wrote there that such is the opinion of the Rambam and the Rif, who omitted this halachah because they relied on the known rule that one who lifts a found object for his friend — his friend acquires it [and this reason for the omission of the Rif and the Rambam is more comfortable than the reason of the Ran that they ruled like R' Shimon and relied on the anonymous mishnah that an ox of the Babylonian exiles is as the feet of the filler, and since they did not distinguish in the anonymous case that it deals whether one fills for his own need or for his friend's need — this is a great strain]. And so is the opinion of the Riaz, except that he copied only when we gave it to him and for the reason that he acquires from hefker, and that is only as brought in the sugya of Beitzah; but according to what is established everywhere that one who lifts a found object for his friend — his friend acquires it — we do not need this, and perhaps his intention is also thus, except that he copied as stated in the sugya of Beitzah to Rav Nachman; and so ruled in the method of Rivav in the Alfasi as the feet of the one for whom it was filled, and in Avodat HaKodesh and in Nahar Shalom they commented on almost all the matters, except that they wrote and framed this law as already having come into the hand of the one for whom it was filled; and in truth from all these rishonim it is proven that even while still in the hand of the filler it is likewise forbidden to carry them as his own feet, but as the feet of the one for whom it was filled on his behalf, for behold they are not his but are connected to the one for whom it was filled on his behalf, for one who lifts a found object, etc. — his friend acquires it; and what is written in Avodat HaKodesh that he gave them to his friend is the language of the Gemara, and the same applies even if he did not give, and we did not find this distinction except in the Riaz, and as above he cited for the reason that he acquires from hefker; but for all the rishonim we do not need this but for the reason that one who lifts, etc. — his friend acquires it; and therefore it is plausible to rule not per the words of the Mechaber but only as the feet of the one for whom it was filled on his behalf, and in particular if they have already reached the hand of the one for whom it was filled — in this it is explicit also in the Riaz; and so too is the opinion of the Bach in such a case that they are as the feet of the one for whom it was filled; and in truth there is more reason to say as the feet of the one for whom it was filled while they are in his hand than while they are still in the hand of the filler — see in the chiddushim of the Rashba in his challenges on the method of Rabbeinu Tam and examine.
