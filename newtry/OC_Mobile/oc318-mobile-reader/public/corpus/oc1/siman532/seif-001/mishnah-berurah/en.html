(1) Even with scissors: and all the more so with a knife, or by hand and with his teeth, that it is permitted.
(2) There are those who are stringent, etc.: for they hold that in this too the reason applies, so that he not enter the moed looking unkempt and cut them on the eve of the festival. And all those who are permitted to cut hair, as above in siman 531, are permitted to cut nails. And if he cut them from erev Yom Tov, it is permitted to cut them on Chol HaMoed, for behold there are those who permit always when he cut hair from erev Yom Tov; and even though we do not hold so, as above in siman 531, se'if 2, nevertheless regarding this matter, since there are in any case many opinions to be lenient, one should not be stringent.
(3) Whether with a knife, etc.: but with his hands or with his teeth it is permitted. One who cuts his nails with his teeth has no [issue of] disgust in them [Gemara].
(4) That she cuts them in their weekday manner: that is to say, even by herself, and she need not [do so] through a non-Jewish woman, as regarding a woman who is a mourner [in Yoreh De'ah, siman 390, se'if 6], since there are those who permit in every case [Gra].
