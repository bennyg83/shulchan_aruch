And they forbid. And it is obvious that all those who are permitted to cut hair are permitted to cut nails (Magen Avraham). And it appears to me that one should forbid, for one can cut nails in any place; and examine carefully the Baer Heitev before you. And in Shaarei Teshuvah, Nachalas Shivah, siman 56, permits one who is careful to cut them every erev Shabbos to cut them on Chol HaMoed on erev Shabbos. Magen Avraham wrote: and it appears to me that if he cut them on erev Yom Tov, it is permitted to cut them on Chol HaMoed, for behold Rabbeinu Tam permits in this even to cut hair; see there.
With a knife. This implies that with his hand or with his teeth it is permitted. And Riaz wrote: one who cuts his nail with his teeth has no [issue of] disgust.
