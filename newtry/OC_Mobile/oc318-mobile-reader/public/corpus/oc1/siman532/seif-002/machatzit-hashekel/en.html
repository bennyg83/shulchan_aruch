(s.k. 2) All medical treatment, etc. This implies that on Yom Tov it is forbidden. That is to say, even on the second day of Yom Tov. And above in siman 496 it is clarified that every rabbinic shevus is permitted on the second day of Yom Tov for the need of medical treatment, even if it is not an illness that encompasses the entire body; and drinking a beverage for medical purposes likewise involves only a shevus.
