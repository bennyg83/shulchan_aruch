(13) He may adjust it, etc. Even if most of it has fallen off.
(14) Entirely. Even if the wrapping still remains in his hand, it constitutes an interruption.
(15) He cannot, etc. If he transgressed and wrapped himself in it, he need not return to the beginning of the blessing. Rather, he completes the prayer and continues, and when he finishes his prayer he should adjust it and recite the blessing. See above, siman 8, seif 15, and the Mishnah Berurah there. An elderly person who wears spectacles, called bril, if they are loose on his nose and liable to fall during bowing and prostration, should preferably be stringent and tie them beforehand with a cord. Later authorities.
(16) For it constitutes an interruption. Unless he is distracted by it and cannot concentrate, in which case he should put it on when he completes the blessing.
