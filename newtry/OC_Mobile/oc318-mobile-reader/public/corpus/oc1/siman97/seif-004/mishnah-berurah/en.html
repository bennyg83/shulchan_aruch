(13) He may adjust it, etc. — even if most of it fell.

(14) Entirely — even if the wrapping still remains in his hand, it is an interruption.

(15) He cannot, etc. — and if he transgressed and wrapped in it, he need not begin from the beginning of the blessing but completes the prayer and goes; and when he finishes his prayer he adjusts it and blesses; see above in siman 8 siman 15 and Mishna Berurah there. An elder who places on his eyes what they call spectacles — if they are propped on his nose so they are apt to fall through bowing and prostrations — it is proper to be stringent that he tie them at the outset with a string [later authorities].

(16) For it is an interruption — unless he is distracted by this and cannot concentrate; then he should put it on when he finishes the blessing.
