Seif 62, "forbidden," etc., and "if," etc. — there and in Yerushalmi, but he does not spit, etc. R' Yehuda Abuhav: even spitting, etc., for all agree that spitting into his mantle is forbidden.

"He conceals it," etc. — Gemara there.

"And if it is impossible to throw it," etc. — Yerushalmi there: before him forbidden, behind him permitted, to his right forbidden, to his left permitted. Terumat HaDeshen wrote we learn from here that if it is impossible behind him, it is permitted to his left; and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch omitted this, since in our Gemara they only said behind him.
