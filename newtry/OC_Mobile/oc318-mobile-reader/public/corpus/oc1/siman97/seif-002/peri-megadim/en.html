He casts to his left (Taz). I did not understand — for the Beit Yosef resolved in his father's name as the Rashba wrote here in his name; but he ruled like Rabbeinu Meir, peace upon him, who wrote only behind him, as our Talmud, and not to his left and not to his right — see there in Beit Yosef. And what is written likewise in Darkei Moshe — the Magen Avraham brought it, letter vav — see there. However, if it is impossible, it is permitted even to his right or before him, if he will be distracted on account of this in his prayer; and they forbade only when it is possible toward his left, then that is preferable.
