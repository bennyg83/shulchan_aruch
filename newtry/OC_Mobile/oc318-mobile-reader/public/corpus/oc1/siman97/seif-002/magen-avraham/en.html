Not to his right. The Beit Yosef asks that, on the contrary, his own right is the left of HaKadosh Baruch Hu, as stated in siman 123. See there for his answer. The Darchei Moshe answers that when one spits, he did not wish to indicate that the Shechinah is opposite him. On the contrary, through his spitting the Shechinah withdraws from him. End of quotation. Shibolei HaLeket writes in the name of the Yerushalmi: To his right is forbidden, and to his left is permitted, as it is stated, “Hashem is your shadow at your right hand.” Accordingly, this is well understood, for the Shechinah stands at his right side and one may not spit toward the Shechinah. By contrast, when he gives peace to HaKadosh Baruch Hu on both sides, he first gives it toward the right of HaKadosh Baruch Hu.
