That it should not be visible — specifically in an undergarment; but in an outer garment, since the saliva is visible, it is forbidden (Terumat HaDeshen). And there is a question in our countries, where they wear on the belt a piece of cloth called a handkerchief, whether it is permitted to absorb the saliva therein, since the saliva is visible; and it is possible that since it is made for this purpose, there is no issue of disgust.

Not to his right. And the Beit Yosef asked: on the contrary, his right is the left of the Holy One Blessed Be He, as stated in siman 123; see there what is written. And in Darkei Moshe it is written that when spitting, he did not wish to teach that the Shechinah is opposite him; on the contrary, by his spitting the Shechinah withdraws from him — so far his words. And in Shlah it is written in the name of the Yerushalmi: to his right is forbidden, to his left is permitted, as it is said, "the Omnipresent is your shade at your right hand" — so far. And if so, one should not say that the Shechinah stands at his right side and it is forbidden to spit toward the Shechinah; unlike when he gives peace from both sides, for to the Holy One Blessed Be He he gives to the right first.
