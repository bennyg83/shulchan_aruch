5) [Seif 2] It is forbidden for him to spit, etc. The Gemara states there: "One who spits during his prayer is as though he spat in the king's presence." Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2, cites this teaching. It is forbidden even during the supplications following the prayer, for as long as one has not stepped backward, he is regarded as standing before the King. Magen Avraham, seif katan 2; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 5; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTur; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 110a; Kesher Gudal, siman 12, paragraph 26; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 18, paragraph 9.
