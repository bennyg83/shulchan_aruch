2) There: If he must yawn because he cannot prevent it, etc. Tur writes, "If he must do them," etc., as stated in the preceding paragraph. This implies that whether he must belch or yawn, he must place his hand over his mouth. The wording of Shulchan Aruch, however ("If he must yawn," etc.) implies that one must place his hand there specifically when yawning, because he opens his mouth widely, but not when belching. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1; Nehar Shalom, paragraph 1; and Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1, write accordingly. Levush, however, writes, "If he must do them," etc., following Tur. So too Solet Belulah in Mekor Chaim, paragraph 1. This also appears from the words of Chida in Kesher Gudal, siman 12, paragraph 27; Siddur Beis Yaakov, paragraph 43; and Kitzur Shulchan Aruch there. It appears that they explain that Shulchan Aruch's word "yawn" is not precise, and that the same rule applies to belching; Shulchan Aruch mentions only one of the two. This follows from the fact that Beis Yosef quotes Tur's wording in Beis Yosef and writes that this too is stated in the Gemara, without commenting upon it at all, implying that he agrees. Moreover, see Beis Yosef there, who adduces proof for Tur from the Yerushalmi in the chapter Mi Shemeiso. Rabbi Chanina said: "I saw Rebbi yawn and sneeze, and he placed his hands over his mouth." This implies that he placed his hands over his mouth both when yawning and when sneezing. See also Rosh, chapter Mi Shemeiso, siman 39, whose version of the Gemara does not read, "When he yawned, he would place," etc., but merely, "and he would place," etc. Maharsha there likewise writes that this implies that he would place his hand over his mouth for all of them; belching, yawning, and sneezing. One should therefore be stringent.
