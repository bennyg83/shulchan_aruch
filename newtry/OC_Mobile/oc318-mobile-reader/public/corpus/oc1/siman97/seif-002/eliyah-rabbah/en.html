(2) "That it should not be visible," etc. — Ateret Tzvi explained: that he should not spit on an outer garment where the saliva would be visible; Olas Tamid explained that he conceals it little by little until the saliva is not visible on his garment. It appears that if he has a separate garment, as is practiced in those countries, he may place the saliva there even if it is visible on the garment, but he must wrap the garment so the saliva is not visible from outside.
