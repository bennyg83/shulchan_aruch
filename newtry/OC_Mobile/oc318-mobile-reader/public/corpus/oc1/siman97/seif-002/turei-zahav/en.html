In Beit Yosef he asked in the name of his father Rav Ovadiah: here he gives precedence to man's right, and below in the law of three steps he apportions honor more to his left because it is the right of the Shechinah; and he left it in doubt — therefore Beit Yosef did not write it in Shulchan Aruch here. And in my humble opinion, here at the time of spitting the honor of the Shechinah withdraws from him, as written regarding "honor the honored," etc.; therefore we follow the right and left of the person, unlike in three steps, for there he bows toward the Shechinah — therefore we follow the right of the Shechinah. And it is astonishing on our great master Rav Ovadiah and the Beit Yosef that they did not notice this, for it is correct and clear.
