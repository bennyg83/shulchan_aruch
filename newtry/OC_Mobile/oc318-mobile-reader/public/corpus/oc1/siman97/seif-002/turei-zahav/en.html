The Beit Yosef asks in the name of Rabbi Abohav that here preference is given to a person’s right side, whereas below, regarding the three steps, greater honor is accorded to his left because it corresponds to the right side of the Shechinah, and he leaves the matter requiring study. Therefore the Beit Yosef did not record this ruling here in the Shulchan Aruch. In my humble opinion, here, when he spits, he removes from himself the honor of the Shechinah, as written concerning “Honor yourselves, honored ones,” and therefore we follow the person’s own right and left. This is unlike the three steps, when he bows to the Shechinah, and therefore we follow the right side of the Shechinah. It is surprising that the great Rabbi Abohav and the Beit Yosef did not notice this, for it is correct and clear.
