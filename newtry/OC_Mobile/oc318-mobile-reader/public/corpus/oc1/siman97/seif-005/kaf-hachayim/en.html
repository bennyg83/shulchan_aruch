(5) [Seif 2] It is forbidden for him to spit, etc. — for they said in the Gemara: spitting during his prayer is like one who spits before the king. Rabbeinu Zalman brought this, letter bet. And even in supplications after prayer it is forbidden, for as long as he has not stepped away it is considered as standing before the King — Magen Avraham seif kaf-bet, Eliyah Rabbah letter hei, Yad Eitan in Hagahot HaTaz, Rabbeinu Zalman there, She'eris Yosef, Da'as Kedoshim amud alef, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 12 letter kaf-vav, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 18 letter tet.
