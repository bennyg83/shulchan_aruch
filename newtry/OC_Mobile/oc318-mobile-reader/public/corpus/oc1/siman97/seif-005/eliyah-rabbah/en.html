However, the Tur and the other poskim imply that behind him and to his left are equal, and this is also implied by the Kol Bo. Rokeach, siman 324, cites the Midrash Vayechulu as follows: “See, I have begun to deliver before you.” If one is standing in prayer and saliva comes to him, he should spit to the sides and conduct himself with awe and fear, and HaKadosh Baruch Hu humbles his enemies. End of quotation. This implies that, ideally, he should spit to the sides. The Midrash cited above is difficult, and in my commentary on the Torah I expounded upon it. Magen Avraham writes that supplications recited after the prayer have the same law as during the prayer, as long as one has not stepped back.
