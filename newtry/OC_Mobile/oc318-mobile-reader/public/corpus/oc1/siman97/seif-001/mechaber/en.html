One Should Not Belch or Yawn During the Prayers, containing 5 s'ifim One should not belch nor yawn . And if one needs to yawn due to a compulsion, one should place one's hand over one's mouth so
that its opening will not be seen. ((i.e. one brings out from his body to his mouth, a blowing, as a result of one's satiation - "ritiur" in Old French) (i.e. the palate of one's jaws opens to bring forth a breath from the mouth) And one should be careful to not place one's hand on one's chin during prayer, for that is the way of haughtiness (Tur and the Ri"f).) ולא יפהק (חיך מלקוחיו פותח להוציא רוח הפה) ואם צריך לפהק מתוך אונס יניח ידו על פיו שלא תראה פתיחתו ויזהר שלא יניח ידו על סנטרו בשעת התפל' דהוי דרך גסות הרוח (טור והרי"ף.
