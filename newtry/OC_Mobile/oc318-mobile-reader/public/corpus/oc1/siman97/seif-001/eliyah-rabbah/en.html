(1) "And he shall not gape," etc. — Rashi explains: yawning (gihuk) is when a person brings from his body to his mouth a puff of air and its smell is like the smell of the food he ate; gaping (pihuk) means he opens his mouth wide to expel the breath of the mouth, full, like a person who wishes to sleep or who has risen from sleep. Rabbeinu Menachem explained in Kesef Mishnah: yawning is when he straightens and extends his body upward. What is written, "and if he needs to do them," etc. — such is the wording of the Tur, and it implies that it applies also to yawning; but the wording of the Shulchan Aruch is "and if he needs to gape."
