And he should beware not to place his hand on his chin, and likewise everything that appears to involve arrogance one must beware of when standing in prayer before the King of kings, the Holy One blessed be He. As for what cantors do when they raise their voices — they place their hands on their cheek — there is nothing wrong with this, since they are not doing so for display or arrogance; but when not at the time of singing one should protest to them. And also if they can sing without needing to place their hand on the cheek, that is very good (Lechem Chumudot).
