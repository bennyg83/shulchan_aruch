One should not belch (Taz). As I explained in Berachos 24a — and explanation 2: belching — he straightens his body and arms. And so in ch. 4 Maharit 11 he explained yawning expels a voice like drunkards; and Rashi before us on belch explained thus. And what the Shulchan Aruch wrote, "and if one yawns from compulsion" — likewise belching too, if impossible, is permitted so he is not distracted in prayer; only he need not place his hand on his mouth except when yawning, which opens his mouth greatly. And see Acharonim and Berachos 24a: Rabbi [said regarding] belching and yawning — see there. And according to explanation 2 there, one should not say regarding yawning he places his hand on his chin; and according to explanation 1, straightening the body — possibly it is forbidden; see R' Meir ch. 4 Maharit 11, he should examine himself beforehand — see there.
