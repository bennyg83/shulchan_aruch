(1) To yawn from compulsion — the same applies to belching from compulsion: there is no prohibition; and that they did not mention it, possibly because regarding it there is no obligation to place his hand, since his mouth does not open as much, and also it is for a very brief time, unlike yawning.

(2) His chin — this is the lower cheek; and cantors who do so at the time of singing are permitted, since they do not intend arrogance but to sweeten the voice; but not at the time of singing it is forbidden.
