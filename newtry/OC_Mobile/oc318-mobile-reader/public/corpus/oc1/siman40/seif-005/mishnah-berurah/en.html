(13) Their law, etc. Since a person sometimes turns over in bed, there is concern that he may lie upon them. Regarding synagogue benches with a compartment beneath them for storing a tallis and tefillin, some authorities forbid sitting upon the benches and others permit it. If there is an empty space of one tefach inside the compartment, it is certainly permitted to sit upon the bench. A conscientious person should nevertheless be stringent even in this case when not pressed, because one must be especially careful with the sanctity of tefillin. If the compartment is fixed to the wall with nails, it is permitted to sit upon the bench.
