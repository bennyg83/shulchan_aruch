(5) Kenesset HaGedolah wrote in Hagahot Tur: customary to fast when one's tefillin fall; in Mishpetei Shmuel siman 12 you will find support — end quote. Beit David, Magen Avraham in siman 44 s"k 5, Yad Aharon in Hagahot Tur, Machatzit HaBerurah in siman 44 note 2, Razal siman 44 note 2, Shitilei Zetim there note 5. Likewise one whose Torah scroll fell from his hand — Mishpetei Shmuel; Kenesset HaGedolah in Yoreh De'ah siman 282, Berach Kehilot there in Sha'ar Yosef note 1, Magen Avraham in the note cited, who wrote: it seems to me this deals without their cases; but for a Torah scroll, even in its case — end quote: when tefillin fell without cases one fasts; if in cases, no fast; Torah scroll — even in case one fasts. So Razal there, Shitilei Zetim, Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur siman 564 note 3 in Mahari Beruna's name: my teachers were stringent to require fasting even if they fell to the ground with their pouch; I am lenient to give a perutah to charity until the bayit fell — even if straps were in hand, but straps — no concern — end quote. So Eliyah Rabbah in this siman note 5 in his name: if fell in pouch, give perutah; straps — no concern if fell — see there. So Avnei Nezer in siman 571 s"k 1 in his name. Machatzit HaBerurah siman 44 s"k 5, Chayei Adam note 5, Yafeh Lelev siman 44 note 2, Ben Ish Chai Parashat Chayyei Sarah note 18; see Chida in Chaim Sha'al part 1 siman 12: even one whose tefillin themselves fell, if fasting is hard, especially a Torah scholar who diminishes study — give charity to atone for falling tefillin; charity atones even for intentional sin, kal vachomer unintentional or compelled — see there; so Chayei Adam, Yafeh Lelev, Ben Ish Chai. Rechovot HaNahar in Kaf HaChayyim Yoreh De'ah note 38 in Ohrchot Yosher chapter 15. However if a Torah scroll fell, heaven forbid, do not be lenient — Chaim Sha'al there. Beit David Rav Pe'alim part 1 siman 33.
