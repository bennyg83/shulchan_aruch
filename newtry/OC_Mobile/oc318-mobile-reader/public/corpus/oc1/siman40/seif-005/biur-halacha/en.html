To place them in the bed opposite his side, etc. See Mishnah Berurah for the reason, taken from Rabbeinu Yonah. This requires study from Tamid 27a. Rav Pappa says there: “We learn from this that tefillin placed at one's side are permitted,” and the Gemara does not fear that he may roll over upon them. Even if we explain, like the commentator there, that the reference is to placement between the pillow and cushion away from directly opposite the head, we still see that the Gemara does not fear that he may roll over. One cannot answer that this inference was rejected because Rambam rules that priestly garments were permitted for personal benefit. The sugya nevertheless proves that if we fear rolling over, placement away from directly opposite the head would also be forbidden. Since Shulchan Aruch rules that placement away from directly opposite the head is permitted, it follows that we do not fear that he may roll over. If so, placement at his side should likewise be permitted. This requires study.
