To place them on the bed at his side, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah the reason, from Terumat HaDeshen; it requires investigation from Temidim 27a: "tefillin from the side are permitted" and we are not concerned lest one roll, etc.; and even if we explain per the commentator there that the intent is between pillow and bolster not opposite his head — nevertheless we see we are not concerned lest one roll; and one cannot say that per the Rambam priestly garments may be used for benefit, etc. — nevertheless it is proven from that sugya that if we say lest one roll, even not opposite his head is forbidden; if so, since we rule in Shulchan Aruch that not opposite his head is permitted, we do not concern ourselves lest one roll; if so, from the side too should be permitted — requires investigation.
