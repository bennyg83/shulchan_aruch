[5] And saw keri, etc. Sefer Chassidim siman 257: one should not place books on a bed where there is a drop of semen or a person passing wind in bed; and one should not hang words of Torah between one's legs or behind one — end quote. Likutei Maharil: when he saw a book upside down he turned it and kissed it; if books fell, heaven forbid, he waited kissing them until he lifted all; when carrying a book he does not enter or exit a doorway before it. And see below siman 564: I wrote in Kenesset HaGedolah and R' Yehuda Beruna that one whose tefillin fall fasts that day; if on Shabbat he waits until the next day; specifically the bayit, but straps — no concern. If tefillin fell with their pouch he gives a perutah to charity; in a pouch or bag without tefillin — no concern — end quote; Magen Avraham end of siman 44 omitted this. When a Torah scroll fell, even in its case, one should fast.
