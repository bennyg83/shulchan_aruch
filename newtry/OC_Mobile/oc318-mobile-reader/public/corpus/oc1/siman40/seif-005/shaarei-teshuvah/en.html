Permitted. Beit Yosef; see Ohel Yaakov siman 1 on synagogue benches with box under seat for tallit and tefillin — some forbid sitting; some permit if hollow handbreadth between sitter and tefillin; he wrote to forbid; later responsum leaned to permit when no height or hollow at all between sitter and feet — degradation by pressing with feet or weight, etc.; concluded: if not concerned, better; all the more remove from house to synagogue in holy place with some height — seems no doubt; see there; nevertheless pious should be stringent with handbreadth height per Yerushalmi dispute on Torah scroll, for tefillin require great holiness as above.
