And he does not wish to have relations. Beit Yosef proved from braita: house with Torah or tefillin — forbidden to use bed; it did not teach forbidden wife on bed — only if he does not wish relations it is as if wife not with him. I wonder at holy mouth going lenient for weak proof — certainly the tanna stated main prohibition is relations, and we know that whenever wife with him we must warn against forbidden relations lest he forget — clear proof from Talmud Yerushalmi chapter HaYashan: children always remove tefillin lest come to keri — Abaye establishes children and wives with them lest come to keri — when sleeping in tefillin lest come to relations; kal vachomer here: there tefillin on him with fear of Heaven as R' Zeira pushed away mats because tefillin on him and he did not fear lightheadedness — yet concern lest come to relations, for relations are habitual; kal vachomer here under pillow lest forget and come to relations. He told me: there on body stricter; here under pillow less — specifically there more concern. Further proof from chapter where practiced: Yom Kippur whether to light — intent is relations lest desire and forget Yom Kippur — we do not concern intentional; certainly concern lest desire and forget tefillin. So Semag: vessel within vessel permitted between pillow and bolster even wife on bed — where forbidden, forbidden if wife on bed even without intent. Further from siman 44 "no woman was with him" — in my humble opinion should forbid even without intent for reasons stated, though unworthy to dispute our Rabbi the author — nevertheless clear as stated.
