His wife, etc., and he does not wish to have relations, etc. See Mishnah Berurah in the name of Taz. I saw in Mateh Yehudah that even according to Shulchan Aruch, this leniency applies only where he has firmly decided not to have relations, for then we do not fear that he may change his mind. If no such decision was made, everyone agrees that this is considered “his wife is with him.” See there.
