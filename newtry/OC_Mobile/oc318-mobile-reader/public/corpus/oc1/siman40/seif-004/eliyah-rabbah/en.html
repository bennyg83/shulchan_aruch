[4] He needs a vessel, etc. Meaning: he should place tefillin with their pouch in something not designated for them; but between pillow and bolster is not called a vessel — and so Beit Yosef that such is the halacha, and so Nachalat Tzvi and Semag and Kolbo — not like Magen Avraham. And see Taz who attacked Shulchan Aruch: even if he does not intend relations, since his wife is with him he needs vessel within vessel; he elaborated proofs and they may be dismissed easily; also from Semag there is no proof, for so is Rambam and they used Talmudic wording; one may explain as when his wife is with him and he intends relations, as Beit Yosef explained the Talmud's words — and so it seems to me he relied on proof in Berachot 24 — see there and investigate.
