(s"k 4) Vessel, etc. From the change in wording: Rambam chapter 4 Hilchot Tefillin: if he placed them in vessel within vessel he may place them under his head between pillow and bolster — end quote.

And Rabbenu Yerucham wrote: from this it implies the pillow is not considered a vessel.

And so in Tosafot 24 end s.v. "and R' Chiyya taught," etc. After they wrote on their challenge that perhaps it deals not opposite his head — if so why need a hat, as stated — they wrote: nevertheless it is difficult to answer that it deals not opposite his head and his wife is with him requiring vessel within vessel, therefore he needs a hat — end quote. Impossible that pillow is not a vessel — what does a hat help? Still only one vessel. Rather certainly Tosafot hold pillow is a vessel; and Magen Avraham could bring proof from the beginning of Tosafot's words: impossible it deals not opposite his head — if so why need a hat? We need one vessel in any case; impossible pillow is not a vessel — he needed the hat. Rather certainly pillow is a vessel; but from there no proof pillows are vessels where vessel within vessel is required — for if both are designated it is not vessel within vessel; therefore Magen Avraham's proof from end of Tosafot is clear: even for vessel within vessel the pillow is called a vessel.
