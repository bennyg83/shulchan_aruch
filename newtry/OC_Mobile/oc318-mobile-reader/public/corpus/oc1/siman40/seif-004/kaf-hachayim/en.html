(4) Prayer books fixed on boards with a silver chain — forbidden to hang on them; not comparable to tefillin permitted in their pouch, for there the pouch is a vessel by itself, unlike here where boards are attached to the book and it is like the book itself. Magen Avraham there, Beit David there, Eliyah Rabbah there, Yad Aharon in Hagahot Tur, Razal note 2, Shitilei Zetim there.
