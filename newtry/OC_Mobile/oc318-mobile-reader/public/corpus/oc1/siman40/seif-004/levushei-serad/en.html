Ibid., siman 75, seif 5. There it is forbidden to recite Krias Shema opposite nakedness visible through glass. This is no contradiction. There, the prohibition is based upon the verse, “No naked matter shall be seen among you,” and the Torah made the matter dependent upon sight. Nakedness remains visible through glass. Here, however, once a cloth is spread over the glass there is no issue of sight because it is no longer visible. The only remaining requirement is one container within another. For that purpose glass is effective, because it is at least a container.
