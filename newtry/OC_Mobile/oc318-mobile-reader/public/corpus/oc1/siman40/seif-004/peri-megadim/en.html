And he does not — Taz elaborated; Semag seems Rambam chapter 4 Hilchot Tefillin 24: if placed vessel within vessel between pillow and bolster — permitted even wife on bed; "on bed" teaches without vessel within vessel, even wife on bed without relations forbidden lest he forget. In Chiddushei Berachot we wrote on this; here abbreviated, for there 23b challenges Shmuel who permits with wife: braita forbids with wife lest he have relations — they were stricter at head more than in house; in house, if not having relations — permitted, no vessel within vessel at head — more degradation with wife on bed, needs vessel within vessel. Shmuel deals necessarily vessel within vessel — Beit Yosef: wife with him implies having relations; if so what challenge from braita? Understand; do not elaborate; see Netziv.
