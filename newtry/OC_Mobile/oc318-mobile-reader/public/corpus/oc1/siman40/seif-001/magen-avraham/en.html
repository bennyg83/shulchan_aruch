Forbidden to hang. But incidentally when one holds tefillin and straps hang — we have no concern; but if tefillin hang — forbidden, as stated. Forbidden to hang a Torah scroll even in a pouch [Gemara, Rashba's novelties]; and it seems prayer books fixed on boards with silver chain are forbidden to hang; unlike tefillin permitted in their pouch, for there the pouch is a vessel by itself, unlike here where boards are attached to the book. R' Yom Tov wrote it is permitted to write verses on paper and hang at the synagogue entrance (25).
