(1) [Seif 1] It is forbidden to hang tefillin, etc. But incidentally when one holds tefillin in his hand and straps hang — we have no concern. But if tefillin hang — forbidden — Magen Avraham s"k 1. Meaning: specifically if he holds the batim in his hand and straps hang — incidentally permitted; but if he holds the straps and batim hang, even incidentally forbidden. However Taz s"k 1 wrote this is not included in head tefillin hanging in air, holding straps and placing on head, for that is need of placement and no degradation — end quote. It appears they do not disagree: Magen Avraham who forbade holding straps with tefillin hanging, even incidentally, is when not at time of placement; but at time of placement he agrees with Taz there is no degradation. So Machatzit HaShekel note 1, and so from Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav note 1, Razal note 1, Shemen HaMaor note 1. However Mateh Yehudah note 2 wrote per his view one should grasp the knot with straps hanging, since it is possible thus; he wrote he saw scribes careful in this; he wrote this seems from Magen Avraham who forbade hanging tefillin even incidentally, even at time of donning — only straps hanging is permitted as need of placement — see there. So Chayei Adam note 1: even at placement one should be stringent not to hold the knot with bayit hanging until placing on head; rather take knot with bayit attached and place bayit in place, then knot — end quote; and Shitilei Zetim note 1 likewise. Taz deals when impossible — therefore ab initio one should be careful per Mateh Yehudah, Chayei Adam, and Shitilei Zetim; if he cannot easily do so he may rely on Taz who permits for need of placement; Beit David 42:1, Maamar Mordechai 1:1 — it seems they hold like him, especially since Peri Megadim, Machatzit HaShekel, Razal, and Shemen HaMaor imply Magen Avraham too agrees with Taz at time of placement. Yafeh Lelev 1:1: if impossible easily, rely on those who permit — see there.
