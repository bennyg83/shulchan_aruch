(Taz s"k 3) And he answers: he takes brim outside. His intent: explaining that answer "brim outside" — tefillin protrude outside head; otherwise forbidden; but whole pouch with tefillin under pillow — challenger thought opposite head without pouch permitted; answerer: opposite head permitted in every case; not opposite head also needs pouch; brim outside not opposite head; nevertheless under pillow — vessel within vessel. Taz: thus contrary proof we do not need vessel within vessel not opposite head — challenger surely thought so; answerer need not dispute challenger that we always need hat; also answerer agrees challenger: R' Yitzchak without hat; R' Chiyya's hat does not mean tefillin under pillow and not opposite head — only brim outside, not under pillow at all — as Magen Avraham: not under pillow but pouch end only; Taz: tefillin in hat under head — not placement under pillow but in hat alone, hat under head means outside head where pouch end is because of under pillow; brim of tefillin hangs outside pillow; when outside head called under head. Possibly: only thus called not opposite head when outside pillow; if under pillow not opposite head, concern lest he turn habitually and place head on tefillin — unlike when protruding outside pillow no concern.
