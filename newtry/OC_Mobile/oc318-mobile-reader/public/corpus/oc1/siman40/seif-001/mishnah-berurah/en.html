(1) Forbidden to hang — on a peg because it is a degrading manner.

(2) Whether by batim — batim hanging below; or straps hanging below. Nevertheless incidentally when holding tefillin in hand with straps hanging — no concern; but if tefillin hang — forbidden in every case; not included in shel rosh hanging in air, holding straps and placing on head, for that is need of placement. Some place shel rosh on the bench while donning, and the like.

(3) In their pouch — and a Torah scroll forbidden to hang in every case, even in holy ark hanging the cover; see Chayei Adam general 41 that even when cover attached with nails to wall — forbidden if nothing stands beneath. Books such as Talmud are like tefillin. Nevertheless prayer books fixed on boards with silver chain — forbidden, for boards are not like a pouch since attached to the book and are like the book itself. Permitted to write verses on paper and hang at synagogue entrance. Custom to fast when tefillin fall from hand to ground without case; likewise Torah scroll even in case. See Eliyah Rabbah: even tefillin in case — give perutah to charity.
