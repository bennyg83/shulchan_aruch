[A] Bin in the strip etc. The meaning of placing the strip on the stake and the house hanging down, between the houses where the house is placed on the stake and the strip hanging down. And he wrote Magen Avraham at random in Alma while holding a tefillin in his hand and the straps are hanging from the leaves, but if the tefillin are hanging it is forbidden. And the 16th wrote a pretense that putting a tefillin on one's head and holding a strap and the house hangs is not an act of blasphemy that needs to be put down. He wrote in the Great Knesset a pretense that writing verses on paper and chanting them in the synagogue is not prohibited.
