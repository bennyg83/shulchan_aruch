(1) By the strap, etc. Meaning, whether one hangs the strap upon a peg while the bayis hangs below, or places the bayis upon the peg while the strap hangs below. Magen Avraham writes that if one temporarily holds the tefillin in his hand while the straps hang down, it is permitted. If the tefillin themselves hang, it is forbidden. Taz writes that when one puts on the head-tefillin by holding the straps while the bayis hangs, this is not degrading because it is required for putting them on. Knesses HaGedolah writes that there is no prohibition in writing verses upon paper and hanging them in the synagogue. Magen Avraham writes that siddurim fitted with a silver chain may not be hung by it, and a Sefer Torah may not be suspended even in its pouch. Beis Hillel, siman 282, writes likewise.
