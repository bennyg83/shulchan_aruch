[1] Whether by the strap, etc. Explanation: to hang the strap on a peg and the bayit hangs below; whether with the batim — placing the bayit on the peg and the strap hangs below. Magen Avraham wrote: incidentally, when one holds tefillin in his hand and the straps hang — permitted; but if the tefillin hang — forbidden. Taz wrote that what people do with head tefillin hanging in the air, holding by the strap and placing on the head — this is not degradation, for it is the need of placement. Kenesset HaGedolah wrote that writing verses on paper and hanging them in the synagogue is not forbidden at all. Magen Avraham wrote: prayer books fixed with a silver chain are forbidden to hang on them; and it is forbidden to hang a Torah scroll even in a pouch — and so Beit Hillel siman 282. See there.
