Tefillin. If one merely holds the tefillin in his hand temporarily while the straps hang down, there is no concern. If, however, the tefillin themselves are hanging, it is forbidden. Magen Avraham. Taz writes that this does not include the common act of holding the head-tefillin by their straps while the batim hang in the air as one places them upon the head, because this is necessary for putting them on and is not degrading. In their pouch. A Sefer Torah may not be suspended even when enclosed in a pouch. Chiddushei Rashba. Siddurim fitted with a silver chain may not be hung by that chain. Magen Avraham. It is permitted to write verses upon paper and hang them in a synagogue. Knesses HaGedolah in the name of Maharit. The custom is for one whose tefillin fall to fast. The same applies if a Sefer Torah falls. Responsa Mishpetei Shmuel, siman 12, gives support for this custom. Magen Avraham writes in siman 44, seif katan 5, that this appears to refer to tefillin falling without their case. Regarding a Sefer Torah, however, one fasts even if it fell while in its case. See Knesses HaGedolah and Shemos BaAretz, Kuntres Kapos Temarim, page 13.
