Tefillin. But incidentally, when one holds the tefillin in his hand and the straps hang down — we have no concern. But if the tefillin themselves are hanging, it is forbidden. Magen Avraham and Taz wrote that this is not included in what people do when the head tefillin hang in the air and one holds them by the straps and places them on the head, for that is the need of their placement and there is no degradation — see there.

In their pouch. But a Torah scroll is forbidden to hang even in a pouch — novelties of the Rashba. Prayer books in which a silver chain is fixed are forbidden to hang on them. Magen Avraham. And it is permitted to write verses on paper to hang them in the synagogue — Kenesset HaGedolah in the name of Mahari'at. It is customary to fast for one whose tefillin fell, and likewise when a Torah scroll fell; and in responsa Mishpetei Shmuel siman 12 you will find support for the custom; and Magen Avraham wrote in siman 44 s"k 5 — it seems to me this deals when they are without their cases; but for a Torah scroll, even in its case — see there. And see Kenesset HaGedolah and Sefer Shemot Ba'aretz in the pamphlet Kefot Tamarim folio 13.
