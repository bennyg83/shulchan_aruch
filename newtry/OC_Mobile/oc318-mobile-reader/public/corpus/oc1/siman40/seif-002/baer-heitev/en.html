Tefillin. If one spreads a tallis over the tefillin pouch, it is permitted even if the tallis is not wrapped beneath it, because the tallis is not considered the tefillin's designated container. Those who place both the tefillin pouch and the tallis inside a larger bag should know that the larger bag is also considered their designated container, and another covering is required over it. If, however, the tefillin pouch is attached above the tallis compartment, the other side of the large bag is not considered the tefillin's designated container. Magen Avraham. Responsa Maharit Tzahalon, siman 131, writes that the bag in which we place a tallis and tefillin is considered a non-designated container, and marital relations are permitted in the room. This applies only if the bag was originally designated for the tallis alone and the tefillin were placed there afterward. If it was designated from the outset for both tallis and tefillin, it is considered their designated container and is forbidden. See there. In pressing circumstances, wrapping the pouch in a cloth counts as another container. Maharit Tzahalon there. If a mezuzah is fixed inside the room, it also requires a double covering. If glass covers the Divine Name, spreading a cloth over it is sufficient. Magen Avraham. Taz in Yoreh Deah, siman 286, writes that a glass covering does not help. His wording implies that even spreading a cloth over the glass remains forbidden. Magen Avraham disagrees.
For them. Meaning, when requiring one vessel within another, it is unnecessary for both vessels to be non-designated. It is enough that one of them is non-designated, while the designated vessel combines with it. Rama comes only to explain the Mechaber's words. One should not infer from the phrase “provided that the second is not designated for them” that it is forbidden when both are non-designated, or when the inner one is non-designated and the outer one designated. Rama teaches that these arrangements are permitted. One vessel alone, however, does not suffice even if it is non-designated.
