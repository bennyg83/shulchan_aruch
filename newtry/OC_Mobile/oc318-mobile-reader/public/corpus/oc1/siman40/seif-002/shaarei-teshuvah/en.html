In their pouch. Beit Yosef and Eliyah Rabbah in Mahari Beruna's name: if tefillin fell in their pouch give perutah to charity — see there; and in responsa Chaim Sha'al (author of R' Yosef and Machatzit HaBerurah): some wished to fast for seeing tefillin fall and their words were rejected — it fell from his hand — therefore brought briefly in Machatzit HaBerurah.
