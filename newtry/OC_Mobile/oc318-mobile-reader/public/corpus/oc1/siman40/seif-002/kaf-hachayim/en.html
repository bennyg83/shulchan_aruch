A Torah scroll is forbidden to hang even in a pouch — Gemara Berachot 24 end 1a, novelties of Rashba; Magen Avraham s"k 1, Beit David note 1, Eliyah Rabbah note 1, Peri Megadim A.A. note 1, Razal note 2, Petach HaDevir note 1, Shitilei Zetim note 2. Even if the Torah scroll stands in the holy ark and one hangs the ark cover — forbidden — Chayei Adam general 41 note 43; and even when the ark cover is attached with nails to the wall — forbidden — see there. In Neshmat Adam note 5 and Beit David Petchei Olam note 1 he wrote, in Petchei Olam in Mishna Berurah's name, that Talmud books and the like are like tefillin.
