[2] Both are not, etc. Explanation: do not think that since both are not designated it is worse and is like one vessel — he teaches that it is permitted; but in truth the same applies when the outer is not designated even if the inner is designated — and so Beit Yosef and Lechem Chamudos. And see below siman 140 seif 6 that holiness of books, even of Talmud, is like holiness of tefillin; and in truth so wrote the Tur; but Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot and Ra'avad implies that covering alone suffices and vessel within vessel is unnecessary — and so in Kolbo folio 204; requires study. And in responsa Masat Binyamin siman 99: printed books have holiness like written books — and so Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 271: whoever is lenient about holiness of printed books will give judgment — see there. But one may hesitate: when printing they do not sanctify the parchment and the Divine Names; and so I found in responsa Chut HaMeshulash siman 287 leaning to leniency in a time of need — and see above siman 32 seif 42 and siman 44 s"k 5. Further in responsa Masat Binyamin siman 100: even books not written in full script, such as gimshonat writing, have minor holiness and require honor — see there regarding margins of books. Magen Avraham wrote: a mezuzah fixed inside a room requires vessel within vessel; and if there is glass over the Name, spreading a cloth suffices. Riaz wrote: he placed them inside his tallit and placed the tallit inside a chest of books — behold vessel within vessel, for the tallit is not kelim — end quote. Piskei Rekanati explained spreading a tallit on the tefillin pouch even if not wrapped below — permitted — end quote. This fits responsa of R' Yom Tov Tzahalon siman 131, where he was uncertain if wrapping the pouch well in a handkerchief is called one vessel or we need an actual vessel — see there: since the tallit spread on it is an actual vessel, like wrapping well in a handkerchief. Magen Avraham wrote: those who place tefillin pouch and tallit in a large pouch — the large pouch too is kelim; however if the tefillin pouch is attached above to the tallit pouch, the other side of the large pouch is not kelim — end quote. And I saw in responsa of R' Yom Tov there that he elaborated on the first law and concludes to be lenient in a time of need when he has no other vessel, since it is not designated only for tefillin it is not kelim.
