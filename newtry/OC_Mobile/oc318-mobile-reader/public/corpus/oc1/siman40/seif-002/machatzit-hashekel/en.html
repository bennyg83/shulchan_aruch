(s"k 2) A house, etc. Even if not wrapped below — permitted, etc.; and so in Gemara 25a: Rava said a cloak on a saddle is like vessel within vessel; and since he said "on a saddle" it implies the cloak is not spread except on the saddle and not underneath — and nevertheless permitted.

He wrote in Sha'ar HaGolah, etc. Also the large pouch is kelim, etc.; and if the tallit is in the large pouch and one places tallit above tefillin — he already wrote it is permitted, for the tallit is not kelim — we have no concern, as stated.

As stated siman 75: although where there is excrement by lamplight one may read Shema opposite it, nakedness by lamplight is forbidden to read opposite, for "indecent matter" depends on seeing, and it is seen through glass. And know that what Rama wrote in seif 2 in the gloss "or the inner is not designated and the outer is designated — permitted" — in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah in Beit Yosef's name; and Lechem Chamudos: all the more the reverse, inner designated and outer not.
