Berachos 24a at the end of the page and Chiddushei HaRashba; Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1; Soferim U'Sefarim, ot 1; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, ot 1; Erekh HaShulchan, ot 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 2; Petach HaDevir, ot 1; and Shitilei Zeitim, ot 2. Even if the Sefer Torah stands inside the Aron Kodesh, it is forbidden to suspend the Aron Kodesh. Chayei Adam, rule 31, ot 43, writes that it is forbidden even when the Aron Kodesh is attached to the wall with pegs; see there and Nishmat Adam, ot 5. Pischei Olam, ot 1, cites this and writes there in the name of Mishna Berurah that volumes of Talmud and the like have the same status as tefillin.
