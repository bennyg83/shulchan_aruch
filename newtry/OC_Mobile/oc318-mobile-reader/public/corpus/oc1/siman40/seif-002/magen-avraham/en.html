A house that has tefillin. And if one spread tallit on the tefillin pouch placed there, even if not wrapped below — permitted (novelties of Rashba, Piskei Rekanati siman 305, and so in Gemara). Sha'ar HaGolah in Riaz's name: tallit is not kelim; however it seems those who place tefillin pouch and tallit in a large pouch — the large pouch too is kelim and requires another covering; however if tefillin pouch is attached above to tallit pouch, the other side of the large pouch is not kelim. It seems if mezuzah is fixed inside a room he also needs vessel within vessel; and if glass over the Name, spreading cloth suffices; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 286 seif 2 where Taz wrote glass covering does not avail, as siman 75 seif 5; and it should deal even if cloth spread on top — with one vessel obviously forbidden; and it seems we should permit spreading cloth, for it is not visible; only the Sages required vessel within vessel, and glass suffices.
