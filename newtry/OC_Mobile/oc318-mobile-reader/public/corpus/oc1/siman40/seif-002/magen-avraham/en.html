A house containing tefillin. If one spreads a tallis over the tefillin pouch, it is permitted even if the tallis is not wrapped beneath it. Chiddushei Rashba and Piskei Rekanti, siman 305; this is also implied by the Gemara. Shiltei HaGibborim writes in the name of Ri'az that a tallis is not considered the tefillin's designated container. Nevertheless, it appears to me that those who place the tefillin pouch and tallis inside a large bag should know that the large bag is likewise considered their designated container, and another covering is required over it. If, however, the tefillin pouch is attached above the tallis compartment, the other side of the large bag is not considered their designated container. It further appears to me that a mezuzah fixed inside the room also requires one vessel within another. If glass covers the Divine Name, spreading a cloth over it is sufficient. See Yoreh Deah, siman 286, seif 2, where Taz writes that a glass covering is ineffective, as stated in siman 75, seif 5. He must mean that even spreading a cloth over it remains forbidden, because with only one vessel the prohibition is obvious. This does not appear correct to me. There is room to permit it by spreading a cloth over the glass, because the Divine Name is not visible. The Sages merely required one vessel within another, and the glass should suffice as one of them.
