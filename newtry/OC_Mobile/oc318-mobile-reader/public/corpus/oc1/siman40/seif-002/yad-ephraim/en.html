s"k 4 And so learn from Rambam and Semag — so should read; nevertheless in my humble opinion his proof requires investigation: if intent from "wife on bed" implying not wishing relations, yet Rambam and Semag require vessel within vessel — no proof against R' Yitzchak; Rambam does not distinguish — even without wife requires vessel within vessel opposite head; so Beit Yosef in his name. If so no proof at all. Even per Beit Yosef, since dealing with wife he mentions vessel within vessel; nevertheless to me Rambam and Semag who forbid even wife on bed deal with stam even wishing relations — Beit Yosef inferred from "forbidden to use" — if vessel within vessel suffices even without relations, he should teach stam wife on bed, not "forbidden to use"; nevertheless Rambam's "even wife on bed" certainly deals wishing relations — know from not stating "even to have relations"; possibly wishes relations — permitted with vessel within vessel; must say so, else whence vessel within vessel required — not mentioned pillow and bolster in Talmud; therefore because dealing with relations Rambam requires vessel within vessel between pillow and bolster as Rava's case; possibly inferred from "even wife on bed" — language implies even without relations; in truth main law is with relations; if so should state main leniency that even wishing relations permitted with vessel within vessel; rather whenever wife with him considered as wishing relations.
