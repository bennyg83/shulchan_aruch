A room containing, etc., one vessel within another. See Mishnah Berurah concerning spreading a tallis, etc. I omitted what Baer Heitev cites in the name of Maharit Tzahalon, who was uncertain about a tefillin pouch wrapped in a cloth, because all the later authorities challenge him. It is certainly permitted, as shown by Rava's ruling in Berachos 26 concerning a cloak spread over a chest, even though the upper covering does not cover every side. All the more so here. If, however, both coverings cover only the top and not the bottom, the matter requires some study. The poskim did not mention any distinction. Regarding a tefillin pouch placed inside the large tallis bag, Mishnah Berurah records Magen Avraham's view that the large bag is their designated container. Derech HaChayim copies his ruling. Eliyah Rabbah, however, cites Maharit Tzahalon as permitting it in pressing circumstances. I omitted this because one can cover the tefillin with the tallis, as written in Mishnah Berurah. Maharit Tzahalon's ruling does appear relevant to placing money in the tallis bag in pressing circumstances. Since the bag is intended for both the tallis and tefillin, it is not considered designated exclusively for tefillin. Chayei Adam, general rule 14, writes likewise. In truth, it appears to me that everyone permits this, as will be explained, with Hashem's help, below in the general rules of Mishnah Berurah in Biur Halachah. See there.
It is forbidden to have marital relations. See Mishnah Berurah, which states that the same applies to relieving oneself in the room. This comes from Derech HaChayim. The source requires study. No proof can be brought from siman 43, seif 7. Certainly a covering is required, as may be seen in siman 315, seif 2, but where is the source that one vessel within another is required?
