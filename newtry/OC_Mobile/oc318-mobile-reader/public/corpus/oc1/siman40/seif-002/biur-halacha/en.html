A house that has, etc. — vessel within vessel — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote: "and if one spread a tallit," etc.; and I omitted what Beit Yosef brought in the name of Maharitatz, who was uncertain about a pouch that was wrapped with a handkerchief, for all the later authorities attacked him — certainly it is permitted from what Rava permitted a cloak on a saddle in Berachot 26, even though the upper covering does not cover all sides; and all the more so here. But if both coverings were such that they covered only above and not below — requires investigation; and the poskim did not mention a distinction in this. Regarding the tefillin pouch placed inside the large tallit pouch, see in Mishna Berurah what we wrote — the large pouch is kelim; this is Magen Avraham's view and Darchei Halacha copied his words. But Eliyah Rabbah brought in the name of Maharitatz to be lenient in a time of need, and I omitted this because one can cover the tefillin with the tallit as stated inside; nevertheless there is a practical difference from Maharitatz regarding permitting forming coins in a time of need in the tallit pouch, for it is not designated only for tefillin since it is for both — and Chayei Adam likewise in general 14. But in truth it seems to me this is permitted to all, as we will explain below in general 42 in Biur Halacha — see there.

Forbidden to use his bed — see in Mishna Berurah that the same applies to doing one's needs, and this is from Darchei Halacha; it requires investigation whence he has this, and from below siman 43 seif 7 there is no proof, and certainly he requires covering [and see siman 315 seif 2]; but whence vessel within vessel?
