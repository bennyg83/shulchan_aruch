A room containing tefillin may not be used for marital relations until the tefillin are removed or placed inside one container within another, provided that the outer container is not designated for them. If it is designated for them, even one hundred such containers are considered like a single container. Rama: If neither container is designated for them, or if the inner container is not designated for them while the outer one is designated for them, it is permitted. See below, siman 240, seif 6.
