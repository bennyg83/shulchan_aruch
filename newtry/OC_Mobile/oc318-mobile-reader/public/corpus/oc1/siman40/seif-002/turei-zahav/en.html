And if both, etc. In Beit Yosef from R' Yitzchak's words it seems not to avail; thus: R' Yitzchak — but if placed in vessel that is not kelim, even ten count as one vessel — do not say ten that are kelim count as one; rather even ten that are not kelim count as one; with kelim vessel they join — permitted — end quote. Per this, two vessels that are not kelim do not avail — impossible to say. Rashal emended: do not say two that are kelim count as one; rather even ten kelim count as one; I would emend: do not say two kelim count as one; rather even ten kelim count as one — end quote. Intent: Abaye did not err initially; from "placed in vessel not kelim" we learn two vessels required not kelim; one kelim vessel does not join non-kelim; he says: placed in kelim vessel, even ten count as one; as one non-kelim does not join kelim, so ten non-kelim — not so; rather: two kelim do not avail; even ten kelim do not avail; nevertheless kelim joins with non-kelim.
