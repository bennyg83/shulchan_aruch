6) One who fasts because his tefillin fell should fast that same day, even though he did not accept the fast on the preceding day, just as with a fast for a dream; and he recites Aneinu as on a fast for a dream. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah there, ot 7, in the name of the aforementioned authority; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 5; Baer Heitev there; Chesed LaAlafim there; and Ben Ish Chai there. R' Chayyim Palachi, in Kaf HaChayyim, siman 10, ot 38, writes that he should fast on the following day and accept the fast the preceding evening. If his tefillin fell on Shabbos, however, he should not fast on Shabbos but wait until Motzei Shabbos. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah there, ot 5, in the name of the aforementioned authority; Eliyah Rabbah there; and Baer Heitev there.
