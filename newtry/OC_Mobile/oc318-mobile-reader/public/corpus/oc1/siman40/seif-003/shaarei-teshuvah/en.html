Tefillin. See Ba'er Heitev and Sha'arei Teshuvah: placing tallit on tefillin — he does not know what world relies on to permit; all agree tefillin holiness is stricter than tzitzit; tallit should be in one pouch, tefillin in another — placing tefillin on tallit is improper, lest one transgress mitzvot — see there. It seems if he has no tefillin pouch and places tallit on them for protection lest soil — tallit becomes kelim and permitted. Responsa Tashbetz part 3 siman 26: tefillin pouch should be somewhat narrow so shel yad is reached first and not pass over mitzvot; "tefillin pouch handbreadth" is for placing on ground — in kelim-designated vessel less than handbreadth is degradation; but no hollow handbreadth needed after inserting tefillin to separate from nakedness or child in house — any covering avails for "and your camp shall be holy"; laws of marital relations explained below siman 140.
