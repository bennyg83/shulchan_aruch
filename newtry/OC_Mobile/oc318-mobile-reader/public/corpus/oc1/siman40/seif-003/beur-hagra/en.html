Seif 3: Even to place, etc. From what is stated "even if his wife is with him" and there is no difficulty — even under his feet, for in any case with his wife he needs vessel within vessel.

Even in a vessel, etc.; and even. Tosafot there 24a s.v. "and behold," etc., and the Rav says, etc., and so wrote R' Yitzchak; and therefore when his wife is not with him, as Tosafot challenge and nevertheless, etc. — from this one may also infer that when his wife is not with him and not opposite his head he does not need vessel within vessel; and this is what is meant "but," etc.; "and if," etc. — meaning that the pillow is a second vessel as Tosafot say there; and nevertheless, etc. — not like the Rambam.
