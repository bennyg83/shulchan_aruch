Even, etc., directly opposite his head, even inside one vessel within another. This applies even if they were already inside one vessel within another before he placed them beneath the pillow. All the more so, it applies where the pillow itself is used to complete the second covering. This is clear from Rambam. In this particular ruling, the later authorities do not disagree with Rambam. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 4.
But when not, etc., it is permitted. See Mishnah Berurah, which explains that this means between the pillow and cushion. This is explicit in the Gemara and all the poskim. I do not understand Yad Ephraim's statement, “Perhaps he holds that specifically, etc.” It contradicts both Taz and the Gemara. Perhaps his text contains a scribal error.
If his wife is with him, one vessel within another is required. See Mishnah Berurah, which states that the pillow may combine to create one vessel within another. Magen Avraham writes this. Eliyah Rabbah rules stringently like Rambam and requires one vessel within another apart from the pillow. Biur HaGra, however, implies like Magen Avraham. Derech HaChayim and the other later authorities rule likewise.
