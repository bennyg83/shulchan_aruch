Even, etc. opposite his head, even in vessel within vessel — and even if they were like vessel within vessel before he placed them under the pillow, and all the more if through combining the pillow it became vessel within vessel — so is proven from the Rambam. And see Magen Avraham s"k 4; in any case in this law the later authorities do not disagree with the Rambam.

But not opposite, etc. — permitted — see in Mishna Berurah that he means between pillow and bolster, as in the Gemara and all poskim; and Yad Ephraim wrote: perhaps he holds that specifically, etc. — I do not understand his intent, for he contradicts Taz and the Gemara; perhaps there is a textual error in his words.

And if his wife is with him he needs vessel within vessel — see in Mishna Berurah that he wrote that through combining the pillow it becomes vessel within vessel — so too Magen Avraham; and in Eliyah Rabbah he is stringent like the Rambam that we require vessel within vessel besides the pillow alone; but in Beur HaGra it seems like Magen Avraham, and so Darchei Halacha and other later authorities.
