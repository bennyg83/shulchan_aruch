(4) A vessel is required, etc. Meaning, one should place the tefillin together with their pouch inside an item not designated for them. A pillow and cushion, however, are not considered an additional vessel. Beit Yosef writes that this is the practical halachah, and Nachalas Tzvi explains likewise. Semag and Kol Bo also imply this, unlike Magen Avraham. See Taz, who challenges Shulchan Aruch and argues that whenever one's wife is with him, one vessel within another is required even if he does not intend marital relations. He gives several proofs, but they are readily rejected. Semag proves nothing, because he follows Rambam and uses the Gemara's language. His words may be understood as referring to one's wife being with him together with an intention for relations, as Beit Yosef explains the Gemara. This appears correct, with support from Berachos 24a, where it says, “I knew it was her immersion night,” etc. See Rashi there and examine carefully.
