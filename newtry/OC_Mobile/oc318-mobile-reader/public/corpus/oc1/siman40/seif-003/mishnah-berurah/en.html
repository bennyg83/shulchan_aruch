(10) Permitted — even without any vessel, only placed so they will not roll to the sides, e.g. between lower pillow and pillow under head, not opposite head.

(11) Within a vessel — in their pouch under pillow, pillow called second vessel; or without vessel under two pillows. The Sages did not permit under head when wife with him even vessel within vessel except to guard from thieves and mice; if guarded elsewhere he should not place on bed. However if above head a place protruding three handbreadths or three low — permitted.
