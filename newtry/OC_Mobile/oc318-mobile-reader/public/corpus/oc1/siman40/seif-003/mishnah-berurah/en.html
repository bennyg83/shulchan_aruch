(9) Directly opposite his head. This too is a degrading placement.
(10) It is permitted. This applies even without any vessel, provided they are arranged so that they will not roll to the sides. For example, one may place them between the lower cushion and the pillow beneath his head, but not directly opposite his head.
(11) Inside a vessel. Meaning, he places them inside their designated vessel or pouch beneath the pillow, with the pillow serving as the second vessel. Alternatively, he may place them without any vessel beneath two pillows. The Sages permitted placing tefillin beneath one's pillow while his wife is with him, even inside one vessel within another, only so that they would be protected from thieves and mice. Therefore, if another secure place is available, he should not place them in the bed at all. If there is a projection above the head of the bed that is three tefachim higher, or a place three tefachim lower than the head of the bed, he may place them there.
