Permitted — Taz; see Magen Avraham note 2 on this. The questioner thought Taz added here as the challenger asks from "hat" — therefore even opposite head permitted; what challenge — R' Yirmiyah requires one vessel not opposite head (unlike R' Yitzchak in Rambam who requires vessel within vessel even not opposite head); therefore he says the challenge is proper from not finding permission except not opposite head — opposite head with vessel within vessel forbidden; otherwise say not opposite head with vessel within vessel permitted, and challenge R' Chiyya who mentions hat — therefore necessarily permits opposite head with vessel within vessel at least via hat; he answers on the contrary: from hat we prove permission is only not opposite head; otherwise say placing vessel within vessel under head (even many pillows or under bolster permitted) — only hat can be imagined where permission is only not opposite head, via long hat taking brim outside — not opposite head; but opposite head forbidden even vessel within vessel; not opposite head, even hat alone permitted, brim outside is one vessel — as Magen Avraham 3.
