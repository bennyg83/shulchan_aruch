And if his wife is with him he requires a vessel within a vessel. And some say that in every case he requires a vessel within a vessel (Rabbeinu Yonah in the chapter One Who Died (Berachot 4:17) in the name of the Rambam) — meaning that he should place the tefillin with their pouch in something not designated for them; but between a pillow and a bolster is not called a vessel (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rambam chapter 4 of Hilchot Tefillin).
