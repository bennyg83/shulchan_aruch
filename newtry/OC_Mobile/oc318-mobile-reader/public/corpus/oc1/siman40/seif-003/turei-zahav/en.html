Permitted. Meaning even without vessel within vessel — so Beit Yosef; seemingly difficult from Gemara: how place them? R' Yirmiyah between pillow and bolster not opposite head; R' Chiyya taught in hat under head; answers: brim outside — Tosafot challenged: why ask? Perhaps under head means not opposite; answered: if so why need hat? Even without hat; rather we learn even opposite head. If challenger's proof was from hat, why answer not opposite head? Hat unnecessary; rather necessarily even opposite head requires vessel within vessel — contradicts Shulchan Aruch per R' Yitzchak. Rather from this proof for Shulchan Aruch: R' Yirmiyah said between pillow and bolster not opposite head without mentioning hat — when wife not with him no distinction not opposite head in one or two vessels; opposite head forbidden in every case. Challenge: we find permission only not opposite head — opposite forbidden; R' Chiyya teaches even opposite head permitted via vessel within vessel, hat; answers: not as you think hat shows we need vessel within vessel to permit opposite head; in that hat he informs not opposite head — hat at head end with tefillin protruding outside head — in my humble opinion correct. Zecher tzaddik livracha explained answer too requires vessel within vessel even not opposite head — question from R' Yitzchak who said only between pillow and bolster — challenger's proof from R' Chiyya's hat implies stam without hat.
