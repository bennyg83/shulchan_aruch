Ibid. “Even without their pouch.” One might ask: perhaps Rabbi Yirmiyah refers to a case where they are not in their pouch, and therefore requires that they not be directly opposite the head. The answer is as Taz writes afterward: since the Gemara found no permitted arrangement except by extending the pouch outward, etc. Know that Tosafos's words also fit Beit Yosef's ruling like Rambam, cited above. Rambam's view applies specifically where one container within another is required: the pillow and cushion do not combine, and the tefillin must first be placed in one container within another before being placed between them. Where the tefillin are not directly opposite the head and no double covering is required, however, placing the bare tefillin between the pillow and cushion is sufficient, since this counts as one container. Examine this carefully.
