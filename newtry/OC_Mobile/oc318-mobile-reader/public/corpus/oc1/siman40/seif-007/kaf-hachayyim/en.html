7) If a person's tefillin fell and he must fast, or if they were inside their pouch and he must give a perutah to charity, the rule is not as people say; that it applies only if they fell from more than ten tefachim. The concern is degradation of the tefillin, and degradation exists in every case, even if they fell less than ten tefachim. So writes Ben Ish Chai, of blessed memory, Parashat Chayei Sarah, ot 18. Nevertheless, it appears to me that if they fell from a height of less than three tefachim he need not fast. Shabbos 97a and many other passages state that anything less than three tefachim is treated as joined. Shabbos 5a similarly discusses one who lowered his hand to within three tefachim, and Rashi explains that it is considered as the ground. Tosafos, Ketubos 31b, s.v. 'Rav Ashi,' likewise write that below three tefachim is treated as joined and as though the object rested upon the ground; see there. This principle appears in many places. Accordingly, since anything within three tefachim is regarded as resting upon the ground, there is no degradation when tefillin fall from a height of less than three tefachim. Nevertheless, one should give a perutah to charity, as when they fall inside their pouch.
