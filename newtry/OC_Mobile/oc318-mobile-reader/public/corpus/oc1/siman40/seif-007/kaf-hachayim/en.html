(7) One whose tefillin fell and must fast, or if in the pouch must give perutah to charity — not as people say until they fall more than ten handbreadths, for that is due to degradation of tefillin; in every case there is degradation even if less than ten. So Ben Ish Chai Parashat Chayyei Sarah note 18. However it seems if they fell less than three handbreadths high one need not fast, since we say in Shabbat 97a and many places: anything less than three is like lavud; and Shabbat 5a: e.g. one extended his hand below three — Rashi: it is like the ground; so Tosafot Kesubot 31b s.v. Rav Ashi; and everywhere. If so, less than three is as placed on the ground — no degradation if fell less than three handbreadths; nevertheless give perutah as when fell in the pouch.
