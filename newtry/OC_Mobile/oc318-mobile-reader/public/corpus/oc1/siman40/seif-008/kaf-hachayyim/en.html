8) One who sees tefillin fall is not obligated to fast. Chayyim Sha'al, part 1, siman 12, and Machazik Berachah, ot 3. He writes that this is evident from Mishpetei Shmuel, siman 12, and Mahari Bruna, who wrote only that the person whose tefillin fall fasts, and this is the ancient custom; see there. Shaarei Teshuvah, ot 1, and Petach HaDevir, ot 2, cite this. It is unlike Sedei HaAretz, part 3, siman 31, who writes that even one who witnesses the fall must fast, as cited by Zechor LeAvraham, part 1, ot 400, and Petach HaDevir there. See Birkei Yosef, Yoreh De'ah 282, Shiyurei Berachah, ot 4, who writes that even one who sees a Sefer Torah fall is not strictly obligated to fast; see there. He writes the same in Chayyim Sha'al there and Ledavid Emet, siman 3, ot 11; see there.
