(8) One who sees tefillin fall is not obligated to fast — Chaim Sha'al part 1 siman 12 and in his Machatzit HaBerurah note 3; he wrote this is proven from Mishpetei Shmuel siman 12 and Mahari Beruna, who wrote only one whose tefillin fall fasts — ancient custom — see there; Beit David Sha'ar HaTefillah note 1, Petach HaDevir note 2 — not like Sedei HaAretz part 3 siman 31 that even a witness must fast; he brought Zekenim Chayei Adam note 9 and Petach HaDevir there. See Berach Kehilot in Yoreh De'ah siman 282 in Sha'ar Yosef note 4: even one who sees a Torah scroll fall is not obligated by law to fast — see there; and so Chaim Sha'al there leDavid emet siman 3 note 11 — see there.
