One who enters for a fixed meal, etc. until time of blessing — in the Rambam it states that he dons them after washing mayim acharonim, and presumably his reason is so as not to don them with soiled hands; and the later authorities did not bring his words, and perhaps their reason is that per our ruling at the beginning of siman 179, even casual speech is forbidden to interrupt between mayim acharonim and Birkat HaMazon — see there in Beit Yosef; all the more so here — see at the beginning of siman 179 in Magen Avraham. And see what we will write below regarding blessing.

And he returns and dons them — see in Yerushalmi in the chapter He Was Reading, which proves in any case for all versions [see Peri Megadim and Sefer Chareidim] that for a fixed meal when he removes them he must afterward bless when donning. And for the law: for the author above in siman 25 seif 12 he certainly must bless again; and even per Rama's view there that when he removes them with intent to return immediately he need not bless — here too he removed them and placed them on the table with intent to return and don them at Birkat HaMazon — nevertheless they wrote there that when he removes them with intent to enter the synagogue in them, all agree he must bless again, for since he may not walk in them to the synagogue this hour is pushed off from the mitzvah; if so here too, since the Sages decreed not to go in tefillin during the meal, the hour is pushed off from the mitzvah — requires investigation. And from Yerushalmi there is no proof, for one may say per Penei Moshe's version and so Gra's gloss on Yerushalmi that it deals when he removed the tefillin that Yerushalmi discusses per what is in the braita (in the Bavli Berachot 23b) that one must relieve himself before entering a fixed meal — if so necessarily he must bless now on the tefillin; and our doubt is when he did not relieve himself beforehand.
