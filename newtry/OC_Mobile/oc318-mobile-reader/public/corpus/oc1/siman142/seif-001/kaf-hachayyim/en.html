1) [Seif 1] If he read and erred, even in the grammar, etc. So wrote the Rambam in chapter 12 of Hilchos Tefillah; and the Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote that it seems [right] to bring a proof from that which we learn in the Yerushalmi: from where [do we know about] Targum? For it is written, 'and they read in the sefer,' etc. -- 'and they read in the sefer,' this is the Scripture; 'clearly,' this is the Targum; 'and gave the sense,' these are the cantillation notes; 'and caused [them] to understand,' this is the Masorah. And Rabbi Yonah said: even though it was stated that the Targum is not indispensable, if he erred [in it] they make him return; and all the more so for grammar, which we also derive from this verse. And the Rabbi Manoach brought a proof from that which we say in the chapter 'Chelek' (Sanhedrin, folio 99a): anyone who says the entire Torah is from Heaven except for one grammatical detail -- this one is included in 'he has spurned the word of Hashem'; and since one grammatical detail has a great root, one must not [treat] the moving [sheva] as resting, nor the resting as moving, nor weaken the strong [dagesh], nor strengthen the weak. End of his language. And the Beis Yosef cited it, and the Olas Tamid, ot 1. And so it is written in the holy Zohar, parashas Vayakhel, folio 205b, and this is its language: We learned, it is written, 'and they read in the sefer, the Torah of God, clearly and giving the sense, and they understood in the Scripture' -- and they established it [as] the secret that those verses, cantillation notes, and Masorah, and all those precise details and supernal secrets, all were transmitted to Moshe from Sinai. End of its language. And so it is written in the sefer Mevo She'arim, sha'ar 2, part 2, chapter 7, and in the new edition, folio 9c, and this is its language: And the fact that the Sefer Torah is without vowel-points and cantillation notes indicates the kings who died, which are the letters and the crowns upon them; and they are rectified through the Yesod of Abba, for in thought all is clarified; and through the reading that a person reads, he draws into it -- into Zeir Anpin, which is the Written Torah -- the cantillation notes and the vowel-points through his reading. And therefore you will see that the cantillation notes and the vowel-points have feeling and movement in their reading, since they indicate the revival of the kings who returned to life through the new cantillation notes that came, and also through the vowel-points that were sown into them; but the crowns have no movement at all, for they are the aspect of the lights while they hover over the bodies, etc. End of its language. And further see in the Etz Chaim, sha'ar 8, chapter 6. And now you have been shown to know that the vowel-points and cantillation notes contain supernal secrets; and if so, how much must the shaliach tzibbur strive to learn the parashah well in order to read the vowel-points and cantillation notes properly; and likewise the oleh who reads with the shaliach tzibbur in an undertone must also take care to read properly. And see our words above in siman 139, ot 1, and below in ot 4.
