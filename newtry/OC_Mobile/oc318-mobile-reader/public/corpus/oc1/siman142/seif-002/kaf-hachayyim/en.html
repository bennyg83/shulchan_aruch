2) There: Even in the grammar of one letter, etc. And even if he erred between 'im' and 'im' [different vocalizations], Beis Yosef in the name of the Yerushalmi; and the same applies that if in place of 'Avraham' he read 'Avram,' or the reverse, in place of 'Avram' 'Avraham,' or he read in place of 'Aharon' 'Haran,' skipping the alef of 'Aharon' in the reading, or in place of 'Mitzrayim' he read 'Mitzrayim' [with a different vocalization], and the like -- they make him return. And this is unlike the words of the Rama, of blessed memory, who wrote that [we make him return] specifically for a change through which the meaning changes, which is not [so]; and so wrote the Rav, of blessed memory, in the Beis Yosef plainly, that even for an error through which the meaning does not change they make the reader return, and that so it appears from the words of the Orchos Chaim. However, for grammar of cantillation notes or vowel-points in a matter that does not change any meaning, they do not make him return, but they rebuke him. Pri Chadash, ot 1; Emes LeYaakov in the law of Krias Sefer Torah for the shaliach tzibbur, ot 1; Ledovid Emes, siman 7, ot 1; Chayei Adam, klal 31, ot 30. And likewise one should be stringent like the words of the aforementioned poskim, because according to the hidden teachings it is stringent, as stated in the previous ot, and not like the Mateh Yehudah, siman 141, ot 8, who wrote not like the Pri Chadash; see there.
