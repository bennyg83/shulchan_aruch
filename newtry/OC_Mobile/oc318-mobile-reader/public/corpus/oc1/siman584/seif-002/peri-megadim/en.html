Malin et al. 17 in the name of clothing, when Sha'l R. on Shabbat says a dry day between redemption and prayer and sand not AMA letter 7. And before the shofar from the day the word to confirm Abraham's covenant, the word for the binding of Yitzchak, the shofar alludes to Yitzchak's Ilo. And on Shabbat he mourns after reading the Torah and says, "Blessed are the inhabitants of your house." And after that he says, "Hallelujah, and look at clothing and crowns of old men, and the poor mourn on Shabbat after leaving the Dezerizin Synagogue from the middle of the day and not after midnight, look at clothing and at 17:00." There, in the Hebrew the Gemara, the king says that the king sits, not the one who sits, as usual, and now there is no one sitting, but he is sitting now. M. S. Yom Livishe AA B.T.
