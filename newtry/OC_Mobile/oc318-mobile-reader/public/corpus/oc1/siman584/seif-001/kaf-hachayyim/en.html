1) [Seif 1] Hallel is not recited on Rosh Hashanah, etc. For R. Abahu said: The ministering angels said before the Holy One, blessed is He, "Master of the universe, why do the Jewish people not recite song before You on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur?" He said to them, "Is it possible that the King sits upon the throne of judgment, with the books of the living and the books of the dead open before Him, and the Jewish people recite song?" Rosh Hashanah 32b; Tur. Although we are confident that we will emerge meritorious in judgment, one must nevertheless be fearful and trembling from the dread of judgment, and through this we will merit to be found meritorious. Taz, s.k. 1; Mishna Berurah, os 1.
