Midnight. At the very least. Unless when it falls on Shabbos: Yam Shel Shlomo on Beitzah. And specifically with piyutim and prayers, and not with melodies. Magen Avraham. The Arizal would weep on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, and said that whoever does not weep on these days, his soul is not good and whole. He also said that whoever then weeps of himself with great weeping spontaneously: at that hour they judge him in the heavenly beis din, and his soul senses it, and the weeping comes to him of itself.
