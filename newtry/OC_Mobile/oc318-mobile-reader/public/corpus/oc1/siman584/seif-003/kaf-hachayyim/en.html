3) Ibid., gloss. It is customary to recite Avinu Malkeinu, etc. The reason is that we learn in the third chapter of Taanis, 25b, that on one occasion a fast was decreed and they were not answered. R. Akiva descended before the lectern and said, "Our Father, our King, we have sinned before You," etc., and he was immediately answered.
