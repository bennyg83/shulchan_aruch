Even. The synagogue burned, and no place remained in it for the Sifrei Torah to be kept, and they were forced to hide them in a place and in one dwelling. And each time on the day of bringing them in, they would bring the Sefer Torah in the morning before the prayer to the house in which they pray. And a young scholar arose and ruled that they should no longer bring it in the morning; rather, specifically at the time of the reading, leading members of the community should go there to bring it. And in a responsum in Birkhei Yosef, siman 44, he wished to defend him, and his foundation is built on the words of Rabbeinu Yerucham, which Maran brought in Beis Yosef, siman 144; see there. But the author attacked that view and brought many proofs that one should bring it in the morning; and so agreed Moreinu HaRav Rabbi Chaim Shabtai.
