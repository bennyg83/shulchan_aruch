(א) (יא) Between Kaddish etc. — order covenant of Avraham [milah] and binding of Yitzchak; Gemara Rosh Hashanah why blow ram horn to remember Yitzchak's ram; milah more common than tekiot and similar to tekiah and precedes [Gra]; where circumcise at home — circumcise after leaving synagogue lest congregation trouble; if house not far and little trouble — better circumcise before shofar for merit of milah at tekiot [Mateh Ephraim].
(2) For the blowing of a shofar - and on Shabbat there is no blowing of a shofar for mourning after Ashari. And after the word and its blessings, they say praise him, and there are those who wrote that even on Shabbat, they mourn first, so that there would not be a long pause to dedicate. When the word of the the Master is uttered on Shabbat, they say a day for dry land before the end of the blessing of salvation, which is a praise for the word, but when the the Master said on the day of Hol 11, it is a day for dry land. One great man used to be a mohel of the the Master, he did not blow his mouth after the word, but with his mouth soiled with the blood of the word, he blew a word into the evening with the shofar, and it seems that he would still blow his mouth from the outside and also wash his hands out of respect for the blessing [Meta Ephraim]:
