4) Ibid., gloss. It is customary to recite Avinu Malkeinu in its prescribed order. The Beit Yosef writes that the other requests recited in Avinu Malkeinu are said on Rosh Hashanah, but "We have sinned before You" and anything similar containing a confession of sin are not recited, for the reason that confession is not recited on Rosh Hashanah, end quote. Darkei Moshe, os 1, writes regarding this that the custom does not follow his words. This is what the Rama writes here, "It is customary to recite Avinu Malkeinu in its prescribed order," meaning the entire order without omission. However, the opinion of the Arizal in Shaar HaKavanos accords with the Beit Yosef, as written above, siman 582, os 16, see there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 2, explains that those who omit "Our Father, our King, we have sinned before You" on Rosh Hashanah do so because confession is not recited on Rosh Hashanah, so as not to give the Accuser an opening. So too writes Mishna Berurah, os 3. The aforementioned words of the Beit Yosef are cited by Magen Avraham, s.k. 2, who writes that there is no concern with reciting verses that mention sin, since they are not recited by way of confession. So too writes Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ibid.; Mishna Berurah, ibid.
