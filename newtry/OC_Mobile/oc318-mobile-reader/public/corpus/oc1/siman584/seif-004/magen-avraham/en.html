They circumcise. And now they have the custom to circumcise after leaving the synagogue (Kenesses HaGedolah); see there for the reason. And we are not accustomed thus: on Shabbos one does not say the pizmon "Zechor Beris," and they circumcise after Ashrei, and they say "Yom LiYabasha"; and on a weekday they say the pizmon and do not say "Yom LiYabasha"; and the same law applies on every Yom Tov. And it appears to me that the reason is that on Shabbos one need not be so concerned about juxtaposing Geulah to Tefillah, as I wrote in siman 111; and see the Levush.
