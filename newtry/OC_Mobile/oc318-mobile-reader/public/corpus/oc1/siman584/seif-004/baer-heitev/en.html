"They perform brit milah." Taz writes: I heard that the Gaon Moreinu HaRav Rabbi Feivish of Krakow performed a brit milah on Rosh Hashanah and did not wipe his mouth after the procedure; rather, while his mouth was still soiled with the blood of the brit milah, he blew the shofar, in order to combine the mitzvah of brit milah with the mitzvah of shofar. When Rosh Hashanah falls on Shabbos, the piyyut Zechor Brit is not recited; the brit milah is performed after Ashrei, and Yom LaYabashah is recited. On a weekday the piyyut is recited and Yom LaYabashah is not recited. The same applies on every Yom Tov. Magen Avraham. See Levush, and see siman 490, seif 7, regarding what is written there.
