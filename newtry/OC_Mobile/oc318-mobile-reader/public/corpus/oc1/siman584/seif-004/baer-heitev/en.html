They circumcise. Taz wrote: I heard that the gaon Moreinu HaRav Rabbi Feivish of Krakow circumcised on Rosh Hashanah and did not wipe his mouth after the milah; rather, with a mouth soiled with the blood of milah, he blew the shofar, to combine the mitzvah of milah with the shofar. When it falls on Shabbos, one does not say the pizmon "Zechor Beris," and they circumcise after Ashrei, and they say "Yom LiYabasha"; and on a weekday they say the pizmon and do not say "Yom LiYabasha"; and the same law applies on every Yom Tov (Magen Avraham); and see the Levush. And see siman 490, se'if 7, what is written there.
