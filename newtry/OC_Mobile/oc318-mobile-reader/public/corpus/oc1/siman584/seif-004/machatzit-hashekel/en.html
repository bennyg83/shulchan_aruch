(s.k. 4) Until midday, at the very least. And so wrote Yam Shel Shlomo, etc., one may lengthen, etc. From the wording of the Maharil, from whom the Rama's words flow, it is implied that he does not hold like Yam Shel Shlomo. For according to the wording Magen Avraham brought in the name of the Maharil, it is implied that it is proper to lengthen. But from the wording of Yam Shel Shlomo it is implied that one is permitted to lengthen, but it is not stated that it is proper to do so. If so, even though Magen Avraham wrote "and so wrote Yam Shel Shlomo," that is not exact.
See siman 529. And it is explained there how one estimates and calculates midday.
