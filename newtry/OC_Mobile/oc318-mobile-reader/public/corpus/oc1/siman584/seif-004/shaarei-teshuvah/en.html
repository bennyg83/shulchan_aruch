Milah — Ba'er Heitev; see Dagul Merevavah who wrote one should not do so, lest blood remain after drying in the mouth of the shofar; and coating with gold is not better, etc. And similarly if a little blood enters the shofar and dries, for dry blood has substance and interposes — see there. In my humble opinion, since there is no blood in the mouth but only smearing of the mouth alone, there is not enough to flow from it into the shofar; and also what smears the mouth of the shofar with blood smear that he applied — there is no interposition even if it reddens somewhat from the blood smear, for appearance is not a matter; and even this smear does not dry because it smears the mouth of the blower, and at the end of the tekiot he wipes well and that suffices. See Yoreh Deah siman 265 that the mohel washes hands and mouth to bless in cleanliness; nevertheless Mori permitted this and blessed the blessing on the shofar with mouth soiled with blood in order to combine the mitzva of milah with the shofar; see Ohr LaTziyon beginning siman 591 that his view is it is better to circumcise before Ashrei so milah is the end of Torah as Mateh Moshe and Mordechai in name of responsa of Geonim; so too Rokeach. And further that the interruption in Kaddish standing on Ashrei, etc. And he wrote in Shulchan Aruch HaRav ch. 1 siman 244: a small city had an emergency that they had no shofar on Rosh Hashanah until after Musaf; there was a child to circumcise — they circumcised him after Ashrei to be among the zealous as on Rosh Hashanah falling on Shabbat; and what Mateh Moshe wrote to fulfill "remember the covenant of Avraham and the binding of Isaac" — meaning to precede milah; but they need not be immediately one after the other, and also it is only a hint; therefore to be zealous not to delay milah is preferable.
