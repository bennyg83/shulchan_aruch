They circumcise. See Baer Heitev; and see Dagul Merevavah, who wrote that one should not do so, for one must be concerned that blood may remain after it dries in the mouth of the shofar, and it is no better than coating it with gold, etc. And likewise if a little blood enters inside the shofar and dries, for dry blood has substance and interposes; see there. And in my humble opinion: since there is no blood in the mouth but only smearing of the mouth alone, there is not enough to drip from it into the shofar; and also what soils the mouth of the shofar with the smear of blood that he pressed: there is no interposition even if it reddens somewhat from the smear of blood, for appearance is not a matter; and even this smear does not dry, for it soils the mouth of the blower, and when the tekios are finished he wipes well, and that suffices. And see Yoreh Deah, siman 265, that the mohel should wash his hands and mouth so that he blesses in cleanliness; nevertheless Moreinu Rabbi Feivish was not particular about this and recited the berachah of the shofar with a mouth soiled with bloods, in order to combine the mitzvah of milah with the shofar. And see Eliyah Rabbah at the beginning of siman 591, whose view is that it is better to circumcise before Ashrei so that the milah is the end of the Torah, as Mateh Moshe and the Mordechai wrote in the name of a responsum of the Geonim; and so it is implied in the Rokeach. And furthermore, so that there not be so much of an interruption in the Kaddish that stands upon Ashrei, etc. And Shevus Yaakov, part 1, siman 74, wrote: a certain small city had an emergency that they had no shofar on Rosh Hashanah until after Musaf, and there was a child to circumcise: they circumcise him after Ashrei to be among the zealous, as when Rosh Hashanah falls on Shabbos. And what Terumas HaDeshen wrote to fulfill "remember the covenant of Avraham and the binding of Yitzchak" means to advance the milah, but they need not be immediately adjacent one to the other; and also it is only a hint; therefore being zealous not to delay the mitzvah of milah is preferable.
