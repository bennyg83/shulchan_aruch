(1) [Seif 1] Just as they fast and sound for rains, etc. Rambam wrote beginning of chapter 1 of Laws of Fasts: a mitzvah from the Torah to cry out and sound trumpets for every trouble that comes on the congregation, as it says regarding the enemy that oppresses you — and you shall sound trumpets, meaning every matter that troubles you such as drought, plague, etc. — cry out over them and sound. This is from ways of repentance that when trouble comes and they cry out and sound, all know that because of their evil deeds harm came to them, as written “your iniquities have turned,” etc., and this causes removing the trouble from them, end of his words. What Rambam wrote “and sound trumpets” — Maggid Mishneh wrote there it is clear like Rashba that in the Temple there are two trumpets and shofar, but in the borders there is only one of them — trumpet or shofar, see there. Magen Avraham in this siman wondered why we do not practice sounding in time of trouble; Nachalat Hashulchan note 1 answered they rely on Semag who did not bring this mitzvah to sound in trouble even by hint, end of his words. And Machatzit HaShekel siman 575 note 2 wrote in Scripture it is written “in your land” — one might say not in chutz laAretz, and in Eretz Yisrael itself when not in our possession one might say not, see there; see also what later authorities answered.
