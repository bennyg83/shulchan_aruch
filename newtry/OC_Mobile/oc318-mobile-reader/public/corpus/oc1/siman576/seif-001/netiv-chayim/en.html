(Magen Avraham at the beginning) And I am puzzled why. A gloss note: and to me there is no difficulty, for there is a full verse: "And when you go to war in your land" etc., "and you shall sound with the trumpets"; it emerges that it is a positive commandment from the Torah in the Land of Israel. And this is the intent of the Rambam: "and they sound with trumpets alone in the provinces," meaning outside the Temple, but nevertheless specifically in the Land of Israel.
