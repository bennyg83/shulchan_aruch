y) The incense compound — a wonderful custom to say it at the time of the matter; and good to fulfill also and meditate on it day and night; and the secret is that taking good ketoret compound in the third gate. Also one should beware of falsehood; and this is a great principle: for whoever speaks falsehood at the time of the epidemic is endangered, may Heaven protect us. Beit David ch. 4 siman 57 from there Kaf HaChayyim from the Ari z"l. See in Zohar parashat Vayera daf 101 in the Midrash HaNe'elam that it is written they should take forty worthy men from the city and divide them to the four directions of the city, ten in each corner, and say the incense compound with great intent; and afterward verses; and Moshe said to Aharon take the firepan, etc.; and Aharon took as Moshe spoke, etc.; and he stood between the dead, etc.; and they did thus three times and the plague ceased.
