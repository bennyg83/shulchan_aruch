10) Pitum HaKetores is a wondrous practice to recite during a plague. It is also good to fulfill 'You shall meditate upon it day and night'; mystically, the numerical value of 'lekach tov' equals that of 'mageifah.' One must also avoid falsehood; this is a great principle, for one who speaks falsely during a plague is in danger, may the Merciful One protect us. Beis David IV:57 in the name of a manuscript of the Arizal. Zohar, Vayeira 101, Midrash HaNe'elam, says that forty worthy men from the city should be divided among its four corners, ten at each, recite Pitum HaKetores with great concentration, and then the verses 'Moshe said to Aharon: Take the firepan, etc.,' 'Aharon took it as Moshe had spoken, etc.,' and 'He stood between the dead, etc.' They did this three times and the plague ceased. See also Shaar HaKavanos, discourse on Pitum HaKetores, for additional passages to recite during a plague, may the Merciful One protect us.
