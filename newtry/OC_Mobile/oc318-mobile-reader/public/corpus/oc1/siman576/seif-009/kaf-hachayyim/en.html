9) When there is a plague in the city, the deceased are examined, lest one of them have swallowed the shroud, which is dangerous, or have hands not extended, as it says, 'After him all men will be drawn.' Anything that involves possible danger to life may be done to a corpse. Sefer Chasidim 451. Zivchei Tzedek there, paragraph 42, records that in Baghdad in 5533, during an earlier plague, people mistakenly opened the grave of a woman who died at its outset and saw her consuming her shrouds, may the Merciful One protect us. The av beis din ordered that she be slaughtered; when this was done, the plague immediately ceased. See there.
