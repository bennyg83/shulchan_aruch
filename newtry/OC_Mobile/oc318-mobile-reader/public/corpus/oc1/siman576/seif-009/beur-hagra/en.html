Se'if 9: Even if it was not seen, etc. Beraisa there, folio 22a.
And even if it is not, etc. Gemara there, folio 21b.
They Fast and sound the alarm. As above in se'if 1, that "sound the alarm" in the Mishnah means they Fast and sound the alarm; and so wherever they said "sound the alarm," except regarding Shabbos, where they do not Fast and do not sound [with instruments], but only cry out verbally; and see se'if 13.
And over locusts, etc. Beraisa there, folio 19b.
But over, etc. Not like R' Shimon ben Elazar there, as written there folio 14 and Bava Kamma, chapter 2.
And nowadays, etc. The Raavad in the Hasagos.
