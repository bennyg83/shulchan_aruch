Se'if 14: These Fasts, etc. Tosefta at the end of chapter 2: pregnant women and nursing mothers Fast on Tishah B'Av and on Yom Kippur and on the three communal Fasts, meaning the middle ones for rain; and other Fasts they would not Fast.
And not minors. All the more so, for even on Yom Kippur they do not Fast, as written in the last chapter of Yoma.
And they are permitted, etc. As written on folio 11b: There is no communal Fast in Babylonia etc. And he explains as Tosafos wrote there: There is no etc.; and as written there on folio 11a: R' Oshaya etc.; R' Atira etc.; and that is what [is meant by] "there is no communal Fast" etc.: that they have Fasts only for other matters, and in those their law is not like a communal Fast.
