(32) And not minors: Not specifically absolute minors; rather, anyone younger than eighteen for a male and fifteen for a female: the community does not decree these Fasts upon them, since they are not fixed Fasts (Magen Avraham). And some say that what they said "and not minors" means that there is no mitzvah of education upon them as on Yom Kippur, below in siman 616, se'if 2; and nevertheless it is proper that they eat only enough to sustain the body and not indulge in their eating. And Magen Avraham wrote that the custom is that they do not decree these Fasts upon those younger than eighteen for a male and fifteen for a female.
(33) Except for the rain Fasts: See above, siman 575, se'if 5.
