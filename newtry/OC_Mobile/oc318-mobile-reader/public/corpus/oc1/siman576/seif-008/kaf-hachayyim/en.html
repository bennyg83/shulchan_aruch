8) During a plague, one should not walk in the middle of the roads, because the Angel of Death has permission and walks openly. When there is peace in the city, one should not walk along the sides of the roads, because the Angel lacks permission and hides himself. Gemara, Bava Kamma, chapter HaKones. The rule during peace applies specifically at night, when harmful forces are common; by day they are not. The plague rule applies both day and night. Taz on Yoreh De'ah 116:5 in the name of Maharshal; Knesses HaGedolah there, Hagahos Beis Yosef 35; Beis Lechem Yehudah 12; Zivchei Tzedek 40.
