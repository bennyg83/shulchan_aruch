(27) Yellow blight and blasting: Yellow blight is grain whose face has become blanched, and blasting is a wind that empties the seed from the grain. And its measure is as much as fills the mouth of the oven: meaning grain fit to make bread as much as fills the mouth of an oven; and the Gemara is in doubt whether that means like the cover of the oven, or like the rows of loaves stuck at the mouth of the oven one beside another, or alternatively one above the other.
(28) In that province: It is a kingdom. And specifically in this case, when it is a spreading affliction; but when it is not spreading, it does not sound the alarm, but only Fasts, as written in se'if 12 (Magen Avraham).
