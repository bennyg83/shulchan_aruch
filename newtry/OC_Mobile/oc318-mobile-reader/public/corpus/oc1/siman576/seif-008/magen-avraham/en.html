Yellow blight. Grain whose face has become blanched. Blasting: a wind that empties the seed from the grain.
The mouth of the oven. Meaning grain fit to make bread as much as fills the mouth of an oven; and in the Gemara they asked whether that means like the cover of the oven, or like the rows of loaves stuck at the mouth of the oven one beside another, or alternatively one above the other.
In that province. And specifically with a spreading affliction; but when it is not spreading, they Fast but do not sound the alarm, as is written in se'if 12.
