Se'if 5: And they were, etc. There: and askara at a time when, etc.
And likewise chafing, etc. Bava Kamma, chapter 2: they sound the alarm over chafing, etc.; this is not difficult, etc. And the Rambam explains that wet [boils] are severe; and that is what [follows from] R' Yehoshua ben Levi etc.: wet on the outside, as it is said, "and it became boils breaking forth with blisters," meaning from the word "breaking forth" we learn that it was wet; and one need not force the reading as Rashi wrote there; and that is what [is meant by] "likewise when boils break forth."
And if it spread, etc. From what it says "and they come upon the community," meaning upon most of them; and according to Moed Katan folio 26a.
But dry chafing, etc. Bava Kamma there.
And in these, etc. See what I wrote above in se'if 1, that the Rambam's reading is "they sound the alarm immediately," and so is the Rosh's reading.
