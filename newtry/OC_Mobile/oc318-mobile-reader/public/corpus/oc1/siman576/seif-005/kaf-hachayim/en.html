(5) See in Maggid Meisharim to Maran z"l parashat Emor p. 35d who wrote that since they surrounded R' Elazar with the four species that come to appease for water, water was aroused and came; had they surrounded again, most rains would come to the world as in the days of Choni haMeagel; therefore continuous rains of blessing came so they would not surround again. This principle shall be in your hands all the time the world is in your hands — all the time the world needs much rain, go surround the mentioned righteous and fast; for every trouble that should not come on the congregation surround them four times and fast, end of his words; it implies specifically for rain one must surround with four species because they come to appease for water; but for other troubles that should not come, one need only surround seven times. What is written “the mentioned righteous” — it seems other righteous too whom the incident was so.
