(14) The illnesses: And the same applies when smallpox breaks out among children and they die; one should decree a Fast; and already a Selichah for children's diseases has been printed; and in the Shelah he wrote that each one should take his children out of the city at that time, for it is a contagious disease.
(15) And they were dying, etc.: See below in the gloss.
(16) Chafing: A boil from which a person scratches himself.
(17) And if it spread among most of the community, etc.: See the Bach, whose view is that in this case, even if they do not die from it, they decree a Fast, for wet boils are like those in Egypt and are as severe as death; but for this we require specifically that it spread among most of the community, which is not so for other illnesses.
(18) Immediately: See the novellae of the Ritva, whose view is that at any rate we require that three people die from that illness, and then the illness is established as a killer disease; only that for this it need not be specifically within three days as with plague, but rather whether in one day or more they Fast and sound the alarm over it. But according to what is explained in Beis Yosef in the name of the Yerushalmi, it is proved that regarding askara, even if only one died from it they Fast over it; and it appears that nevertheless this is only when this illness has come upon many people, even though only one of them has yet died, one must be stringent, as the wording of the Shulchan Aruch implies; but if not so, then not. And see Lechem Mishneh, whose view is that we require that two people die from it (and the words of Beis Yosef in the name of the Yerushalmi, and likewise the Ritva, escaped his notice).
