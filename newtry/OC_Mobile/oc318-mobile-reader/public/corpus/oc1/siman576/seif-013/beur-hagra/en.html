Se'if 13: Even for an individual, etc. According to the reading of the Rif and the Rosh in the Gemara there: Our Rabbis taught: a city etc., and an individual who is pursued etc., they sound the alarm for them on Shabbos; and over all of them they are permitted etc.
Or a sick person, etc. So wrote the Ran, and according to his explanation: because of an evil spirit, because he is sick with dangerous illnesses; and he wrote that from here [we learn] for every sick person whose life is in danger; and see siman 288, se'if 10; and so it is in Bava Kamma, chapter 2: they sound the alarm over chafing on Shabbos; and see above, se'if 5.
They cry out etc.; if he ate, etc. There, folio 14a.
Unless, etc. For he explains what is written there, "it is a dispute of Tannaim," as the first Tanna and R' Yosi: according to R' Yosi they necessarily sound the alarm even with a shofar, since it is saving a life; and the first Tanna does not dispute R' Yosi, but rather R' Yosi disputes the first Tanna, and the law follows the first Tanna.
