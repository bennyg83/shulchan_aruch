13) There: And three deaths occurred in it, etc. Maharikash writes in his glosses that since it says simply 'three deaths' rather than 'three of them,' plague status applies even if the dead were women, children, or elders; they need not be counted among the footmen. This is common practice. The Shulchan Aruch's later statement that women and children are not included among the city's people, however, implies they are also excluded in counting the dead. Levush explicitly writes that women, children, and elders who ceased working are not included among the deaths because they are physically weak and may have died without plague. Birkei Yosef 3 challenges Maharikash and writes that Maran holds the opposite view; Maamar Mordechai 2 agrees.
