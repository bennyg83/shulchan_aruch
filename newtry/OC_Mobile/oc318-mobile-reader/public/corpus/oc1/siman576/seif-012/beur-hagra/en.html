(א) “Every city is called.” In the words of the Holy Qur’an and the the spirit:

(2) But they ask, etc. As for all of them, there is no evidence except in their hypharachia and RL in the mouth as a mashal, and the hypharachia is its surroundings for its interpretation:

(ג) And in every place, etc. — meaning what is said regarding Shabbat: they do not fast and not with shofar but cry out orally, as written 14a: shofarot on Shabbat — who permits? If it were that they fast, it should say “fast on Shabbat — who permits?” as written chapter 2: we do not decree a fast on the congregation on Rosh Chodesh, etc., and if they began, etc.; from not mentioning Shabbat and Yom Tov it implies even if they began they interrupt; as Tosefta: communal fast we do not interrupt for written Yom Tov in Megillat Taanit — implying only for those; so Rambam and for those mentioned in the Mishnah, city that was surrounded, etc., and those in the baraita, and likewise individual, etc., and provisions and anointing; but for others even crying out is not done as written “on these,” etc.; therefore “and they do not cry out,” etc., except city surrounded, etc.
