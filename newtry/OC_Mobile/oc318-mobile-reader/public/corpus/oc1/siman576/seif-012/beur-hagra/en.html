Se'if 12: Every city, etc. The wording of the Rambam, and according to the reading of the Shiltei Giborim in Kesef Mishneh, and as I wrote above in se'if 1; and see there.
But they request, etc. As written there, folio 19b: and over all of them they sound the alarm only in their own province; meaning verbally, as I wrote above; and "province" is its surroundings according to his explanation.
And everywhere, etc. Meaning that "sound the alarm" stated regarding Shabbos does not mean they Fast, and not with a shofar, but they cry out verbally; and as written on folio 14a: Are shofaros permitted on Shabbos? And if it were that they Fast, it should have said, Is a Fast permitted on Shabbos?, as written in chapter 2: They do not decree a Fast upon the community on Rosh Chodesh etc., and if they had begun etc.; and since it did not mention Shabbos and Yom Tov, the explanation is that even if they had begun, they interrupt; and as written in the Tosefta: a communal Fast they do not interrupt for Yom Tov festivals written in Megillas Taanis, which implies specifically for those; and so it is in the Rambam. And all this is for those mentioned in the Mishnah: a city that was surrounded etc., and those mentioned in the beraisa, and likewise an individual etc., and food, and chafing; but for other [troubles], they do not even cry out, as written "Over these" etc.; and that is what [is meant by] "and they do not cry out" etc., except etc. a city that was surrounded etc.
