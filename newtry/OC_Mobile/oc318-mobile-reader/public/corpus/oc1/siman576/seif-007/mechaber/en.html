Houses constructed in a settled and populated area, if they are located in a wilderness or rural region where wild animals are common, and a wild animal climbs onto the roof and takes a child from its crib (meaning a small bed made of iron), then it is considered sent. However, if it did not reach that specific crib, it is not considered sent. This applies to humans who endanger themselves by coming to a wildlife habitat.
