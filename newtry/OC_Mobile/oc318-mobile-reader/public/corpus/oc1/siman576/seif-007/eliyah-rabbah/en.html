And not minors, etc. This requires study, for minors never Fast; and it is possible that it refers to thirteen-year-olds, and even so they are called minors; and so is the practice, that they do not decree upon those younger than eighteen for a male and fifteen for a female (Magen Avraham). And in my humble opinion, it is possible that it refers to education, that they should eat bread and water; and see siman 549, s.k. 3, and Magen Avraham, siman 500.
