6) [Se'if 2] Likewise over plague. Nowadays one does not fast at all during a plague, for experience shows that lack of food and drink makes one vulnerable, God forbid, to the altered air. Magen Avraham 2; Eliyah Rabbah 1. This was also transmitted in the name of the Arizal, and has support in Ta'anis 22. Maharam ibn Chaviv, manuscript responsum 16; Birkei Yosef 4; Shaarei Teshuvah 2.
