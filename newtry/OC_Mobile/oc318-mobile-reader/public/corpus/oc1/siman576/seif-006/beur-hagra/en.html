(ג) In every place even the distant ones. So Tur wrote and as the Mishnah reads “in every place”; as the incident, etc.; explained they fast and sound like all who sound in the Mishnah; explained in every place even the distant, as Rashi on the Mishnah there s.v. sound in every place: if they see in Spain they sound in Babylonia, in Babylonia in Spain; what the Gemara says on all of them we sound only in their province — explained like Rashi there s.v. in their province in that kingdom where all wells are there, meaning it refers only to the place stated earlier in the baraita there; but Tosefta is difficult according to Rashi's explanation who said from that baraita, etc., and repeated after the other things the Mishnah said “on these we sound in every place,” and said on all of them we sound on them only in that province, etc. — according to Ran it fits; see the gloss. There is doubt on Rama why he did not emend here and why he did not argue on Shulchan Aruch in seif 61 and 62; on Shulchan Aruch it is very difficult.
