Se'if 6: That was sent forth. Gemara there, folio 22a, and as written there.
But [only] appeared. Like R' Yosi, for the entire beraisa and Gemara there follow him; and as written there, "Which..." etc.
Everywhere, even the distant ones. So wrote the Tur, and according to the reading in the Mishnah "everywhere," and as written "An incident..." etc.; and he explains that they Fast and sound the alarm like every "sound the alarm" in the Mishnah; and he explains "everywhere" as even the distant ones, as Rashi wrote on the Mishnah there, s.v. "they sound the alarm everywhere": if they are seen in Aspamya they sound the alarm in Babylonia; in Babylonia they sound the alarm in Aspamya. And what the Gemara said, "and over all of them they sound the alarm only in their own province," he explains like Rashi there, s.v. "in the province": in that kingdom where the cistern waters were finished, etc.; meaning it refers only to what precedes this in the beraisa there. But the Tosefta is difficult according to that reading, for from that beraisa it says "and over all of them they do not..." and then it repeats there after the other matters stated in the Mishnah, "Over these they sound the alarm everywhere," and said "and over all of them they sound the alarm only in that province" etc.; and according to the Ran it is fine; and see se'if 8 in the gloss. And this requires study regarding the Rama, why he did not gloss here; and also why he did not dispute the Shulchan Aruch in se'if 1 and se'if 12; and against the Shulchan Aruch the difficulty is greatest.
