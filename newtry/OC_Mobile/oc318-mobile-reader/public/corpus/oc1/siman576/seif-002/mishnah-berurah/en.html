(2) For the plague: And nowadays they do not Fast at all at the time of plague, for it is dangerous: when one does not eat and drink, he absorbs a change in the air, Heaven forbid. And regarding Tishah B'Av, see above in siman 554, se'if 6, in the Biur Halacha.
(3) Three dead: One died on each day.
(4) This is not a plague: For this is not fixed, but mere happenstance.
(5) Included in the count of the people of the land: Because they are of weak constitution; and see the Levush, that neither the count of the five hundred nor the count of the dead should be from these; and so it implies in Shiltei Giborim.
(6) In the Land of Israel: For since when the mistress is stricken, how much more so the maidservant. And Syria is not included in the Land of Israel, for conquest by an individual is not called conquest (Magen Avraham).
(7) And not in part of it: For there is also a mistress who is saved, and she will be saved among those who are saved.
(8) If there was a plague in the country: Even outside the Land.
(9) Even though, etc.: Because the plague accompanies them and comes with them.
(10) That are distant from one another: But if caravans travel, the distant ones do not Fast; but all its surroundings Fast but do not sound the alarm, in order to share in their distress; and so too regarding collapse, its surroundings Fast for this reason.
