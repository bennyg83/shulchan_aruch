The Rambam wrote: It is a positive commandment from the Torah to cry out and sound with trumpets at any time of trouble so that it not come, as it is said, "and you shall sound with the trumpets" etc.; and by rabbinic law, to Fast etc.; and they sound with trumpets alone in the provinces. And the Maggid Mishneh wrote: and the clearer [view] is like the words of the Rashba: either a shofar or trumpets; end quote. And I am puzzled why we do not practice sounding [the alarm] in a time of trouble; and even though there is no communal Fast in Babylonia, nevertheless according to the Torah there is a mitzvah to sound without a Fast. A matter in the Land of Israel. For since when the mistress is stricken, how much more so the maidservant; and Syria is not included in the Land of Israel, for conquest by an individual is not called conquest, as is written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 151, se'if 8.
And not in part of it. For there is also a mistress who is not stricken. Nowadays they do not Fast at all at the time of plague, for it is dangerous: when one does not eat and drink, he absorbs, Heaven forbid, the change in the air; and see Yoreh De'ah, siman 374.
