(Magen Avraham s.k. 12) I am puzzled, etc. A gloss note: and it seems to me that [the requirement of] an assembly we learn from what is written, "Gather the elders," and afterward it is written, "Sanctify a Fast, call a solemn assembly"; and a Fast and abstention from labor do not apply in Babylonia, since there is no Nasi, as I wrote in my works on tractate Taanis, first chapter, siman 9; see there. From this there is no gathering of elders; alternatively, as the Ritva wrote the reason regarding the Fast of Vayechal, and not the blessings and curses as we learned in Megillah, in order to make a distinguishing mark so that we know it is not a communal Fast; so too one may say regarding this matter, not to make an assembly as a distinguishing mark.
