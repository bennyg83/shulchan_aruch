4) There. And he may rely upon whichever of them, etc. He may choose even after nightfall, as stated in siman 413. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2. Consequently, if he does not wish to choose until after nightfall, when placing each eruv he should say only: 'Through this eruv, I shall be permitted to walk two thousand amos in every direction from this location.' He makes the same declaration over the eruv placed on the other side. He then says: 'Tomorrow I will travel toward whichever of these eruvei techumin I choose, and the other shall take effect for the second day.' After nightfall, or the following day when he chooses one, he says: 'Today I will travel toward my eruv in the east,' or, 'toward my eruv in the west.' The second eruv then automatically takes effect during the bein hashmashos preceding the second day. On the second day he may not travel in the same direction in which he traveled on the first day by relying upon the first day's eruv.
