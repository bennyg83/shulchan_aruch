He may establish two eruvei techumin, etc. If he needs to travel in one direction on the first day and in the other direction on the second day, he may place one eruv to the east and one to the west on the eve of the first day of Yom Tov. He says: 'My eruv to the east shall take effect today for tomorrow, and my eruv to the west shall take effect during tomorrow's bein hashmashos for the second day.' Rashi on Eruvin 38a.
