1) [Se'if 1] When Yom Tov occurs adjacent to Shabbos, etc. Shabbos and Yom Tov are two separate sanctities, and one does not depend upon the other. Tur; Levush.
2) There. He may establish an eruv, etc. This means that he may establish two eruvei techumin, etc. Tur; Levush. See below, se'if 3, regarding the procedure for placing eruvei techumin in two directions. Regarding the blessing, he should recite one blessing over both eruvei techumin on the eve of Yom Tov, when he places them.
3) There. He may establish two eruvei techumin, etc. If he needs to travel in one direction on the first day and in the other direction on the second day, he may place one eruv to the east and one to the west on the eve of the first day of Yom Tov. He says: 'My eruv to the east shall take effect today for tomorrow, and my eruv to the west shall take effect during tomorrow's bein hashmashos for the second day.' Rashi on Eruvin 38a.
4) There. And he may rely upon whichever of them, etc. He may choose even after nightfall, as stated in siman 413. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2. Consequently, if he does not wish to choose until after nightfall, when placing each eruv he should say only: 'Through this eruv, I shall be permitted to walk two thousand amos in every direction from this location.' He makes the same declaration over the eruv placed on the other side. He then says: 'Tomorrow I will travel toward whichever of these eruvei techumin I choose, and the other shall take effect for the second day.' After nightfall, or the following day when he chooses one, he says: 'Today I will travel toward my eruv in the east,' or, 'toward my eruv in the west.' The second eruv then automatically takes effect during the bein hashmashos preceding the second day. On the second day he may not travel in the same direction in which he traveled on the first day by relying upon the first day's eruv.
5) There. And he relies upon it for one of them, etc. Here too, when placing the eruv he stipulates that it shall take effect during the bein hashmashos preceding whichever of the two days he chooses, while on the other day he will have the status of his city's residents.
