42) That he reads a verse in the megillah, etc. This means even if in this manner the reading of the entire megillah was valid, since he intended thereby to fulfill his obligation. But if he did not intend to fulfill, even only in the first verse, although from there onward he intended to fulfill, he has not fulfilled.
43) If he directed his heart to fulfill his obligation. He states this anonymously like the poskim who hold that mitzvos require intent in order to fulfill the mitzvah. See above, siman 60, section 4.
44) That it be written in its entirety. See above, section 3.
45) And likewise if he was correcting. That is to say, while correcting it he reads it and intends to fulfill his obligation. This is specifically if he read with the proper vocalization; but if he read in order to correct according to the tradition and not with the proper vocalization, he does not fulfill his obligation.
46) For it is forbidden to interrupt, etc. See above, section 5 in the gloss.
47) With other matters. This means matters that are not of the subject of the day.
