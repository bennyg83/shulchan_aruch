If he was writing it, meaning that he reads a verse in the megillah from which he is copying and writes it, if he directed his heart to fulfill his obligation, he has fulfilled, provided that it was all written before him in the megillah from which he was copying. The same applies if he was correcting it, and likewise if he was expounding it, meaning that he reads a verse in a complete megillah and expounds it: if he directed his heart to fulfill his obligation, he has fulfilled. He should not interrupt in it with other matters when he expounds, for it is forbidden to interrupt in it with other matters.
