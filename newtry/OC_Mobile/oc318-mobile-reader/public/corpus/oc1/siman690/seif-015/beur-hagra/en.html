Section 15: And specifically. The Rif's text is: he must say them.
And initially, etc. In Hagahos Maimoniyos he wondered about this, for in the Gemara we say, what is the reason? All of them, etc., and this does not apply to "five hundred men." It is possible that this custom was established based on what Rabbi Yitzchak bar Rabbi Yehudah wrote, to read the end of the verse and the beginning of the verse after it in one breath; Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote that so was the custom. And although even so one would need to begin only from "men," nevertheless "five hundred men" is one subject. But Hagahos Maimoniyos refuted this from the Gemara mentioned above, and wrote that Rav Tzemach Gaon ruled so. See Taz.
