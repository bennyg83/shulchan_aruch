13) He should not read, etc. For perhaps the listener will set his heart on this reader and not on the prayer leader. According to this reason, if he is reading only for one person, he may read together with the reader from an invalid megillah or from a Chumash. But some are stringent in every case, because he sets his mind on his own reading and does not pay attention to what the prayer leader says, since he is occupied with reading it.
14) And likewise he should not assist, etc. This too is because of the above reason. See Eliyah Rabbah, who inclines here as well to be stringent even when he is reading to discharge an individual, lest he set his mind on what he himself reads by heart, and meanwhile several words that the prayer leader reads will slip away from him.
15) Therefore, those verses, etc. That is to say, just as there we are stringent that one should not assist by heart, because through this several people will set their minds to hear from this assister who reads by heart, while in truth it must initially be read entirely from the writing, so too here the cantor must go back and read them from a valid megillah, and they should intend to fulfill with his reading, so that all of it will be from the writing.
16) The cantor must, etc. This means initially.
