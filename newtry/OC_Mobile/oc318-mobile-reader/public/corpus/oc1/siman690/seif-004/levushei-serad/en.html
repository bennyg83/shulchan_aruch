Ibid. Regarding that which we say, “If he was writing it,” etc. He was writing it and thereby reading it; if he directed his heart, he fulfilled his obligation. This means that he writes one verse and reads that verse from the writing, and so on through the entire Megillah, so that he is not reading by heart. The Gemara asks that the entire Megillah must already be written before him. This shows that even after the fact it is invalid, for otherwise the question has no force. How, then, do we validate after the fact where he omitted up to half and read it by heart? See what I shall write regarding Magen Avraham, seif katan 5.
