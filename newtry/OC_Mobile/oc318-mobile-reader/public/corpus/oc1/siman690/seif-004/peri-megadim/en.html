Rather. See Taz, and see Magen Avraham 6. There we wrote that Beis Yosef in the name of the Rashba and Shibbolei HaLeket implies that the reason is that one who reads from a Chumash gives his attention to the reading in an invalid megillah, and he himself does not fulfill his obligation. Therefore he wrote that one should listen and be silent; even in a whisper and a thin silent voice that does not confuse others, it is forbidden, for he does not fulfill his obligation, as stated.
