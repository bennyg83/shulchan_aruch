25) And in the sacred script. It should read, "in the sacred script," and so is the text in the Rambam. It means in Ashuris script.
26) He has fulfilled his obligation. For even we do not know what "the couriers, sons of the royal steeds" means; rather, we require only the mitzvah of reading and publicizing the miracle, and this is so even initially. For this reason, women and unlearned people also fulfill. Peri Megadim wrote that it is proper for every God-fearing person to have a valid megillah in his hand and to read word by word quietly, because it is impossible to hear everything from the prayer leader, especially when the children confuse matters.
