(1) (Thus) and in the Holy Scriptures - Tsal in the Holy Scriptures and thus the version in Rambam and Ral in Assyrian writing:

(כו) He fulfilled — for the Achashteranim sons of the Ramachim — even we do not know what they are; we require only the mitzvah of reading and publicizing the miracle; and even l'chatchila likewise; and therefore women and amei ha'aretz also fulfill. Peri Megadim wrote: every yirei shamayim should have a kosher Megillah in hand and read word by word in a whisper, for impossible to hear from the shaliach tzibbur because of the noise and confusion when they strike wood and make sound; fitting for all Israel who have ability [Peri Megadim]; and see below siman 690 seif 4.
