Levush: he should not read it, etc. Regarding after the fact, there are two opinions in the Shulchan Aruch. The Olas Shabbos brought a proof and asked: since the Gemara in Megillah 18 does not answer that what the Mishnah teaches, that he has not fulfilled the obligation, refers to one who understands the Holy Tongue, therefore it teaches that he has fulfilled it. In my humble opinion, he holds that since the end teaches, 'but one may read for foreign-speaking women in a foreign language,' and it did not teach that one may read in a foreign language for one who does not understand the Holy Tongue, it is evident that the Mishnah is not speaking of one who understands the Holy Tongue. As for the halacha, it is proper to go back and read without a blessing.
