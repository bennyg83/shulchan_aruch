Levush: provided that he does not interrupt with matters, etc. In Responsa Rashba, siman 444, which is the source of this law, it is explicit that even if he spoke about other matters, he fulfills the obligation after the fact. This also seems to be the view of the Rama. From the Shulchan Aruch, seif 13, it does not seem that it is forbidden even after the fact. Perhaps his intent is where he interrupts with complete distraction of the mind, as in siman 178 regarding interruption in a meal, and this requires study. See siman 692, seif 2.
