If the scribe omitted, etc. — and it appears to me not required to read them orally except letters that ruin reading such as "and these days of Purim shall not pass" — if he omitted the word "not" he did not ruin reading; likewise mem of "and gifts for the poor" and similar; but if he omitted other letters or other words that do not ruin reading — he fulfilled even though he did not read them at all.
