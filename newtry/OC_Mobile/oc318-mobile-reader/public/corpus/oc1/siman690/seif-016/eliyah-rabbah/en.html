You shall surely erase, etc. Even from upon wood and stones. 'I will surely erase' has the numerical value of 'this is Haman,' as written by Mateh Moshe. When they strike, the chazzan must be silent so that everyone will hear his reading, and each individual should read one or two verses from the Chumash while they are striking Haman, for then even if he does not hear from the chazzan, he has fulfilled the obligation.
