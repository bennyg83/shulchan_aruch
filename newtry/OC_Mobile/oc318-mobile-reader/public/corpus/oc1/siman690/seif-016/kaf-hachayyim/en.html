16) Ibid. If the scribe omitted, etc. It appears to me that one need read them by heart only when the omitted letters change the meaning of the reading. For example, in the verse, 'And these days of Purim shall not pass,' if the word 'not' was omitted, the meaning of the reading is impaired; likewise the mem of 'and gifts to the poor,' and similar cases. But if he omitted other letters or other words that do not impair the meaning of the reading, he has fulfilled his obligation even though he did not read them at all. Riaz, cited by Shiltei HaGibborim in the first chapter of Megillah; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos HaTur; Mishbetzos Zahav, s.k. 4. However, the Magen Avraham wrote there regarding the words of the Riaz mentioned above that below, seif 14, it does not appear so.
