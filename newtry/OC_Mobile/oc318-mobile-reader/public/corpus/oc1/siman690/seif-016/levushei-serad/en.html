All mitzvos, etc. Meaning, because of “with a multitude of people.” Even when a minyan is present, Torah study and the Temple service are suspended in order to read publicly because of this enhancement, except for public Torah study, as I explained in siman 687 on Magen Avraham, seif katan 3, second paragraph.
