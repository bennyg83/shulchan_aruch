39) He read it while dozing. Dozing is defined as where they call to him and he answers, but he does not know how to respond with reasoning, and when they remind him, he remembers. One must say that nevertheless he can intend to fulfill, for mitzvos require intent, Peri Megadim.
40) He has fulfilled. The same applies that one who hears from him has also fulfilled, provided only that the words are heard well. So wrote the Meiri. And although he must intend to discharge others, it is possible that one who is dozing can also intend. It appears more correct to say that it speaks where he thought at the beginning of the reading, before he began dozing, to discharge the listeners.
41) But if he heard it, etc. For with reading, since he reads properly, it is evident that he put effort upon himself to say it properly; but with hearing, he certainly missed several words.
