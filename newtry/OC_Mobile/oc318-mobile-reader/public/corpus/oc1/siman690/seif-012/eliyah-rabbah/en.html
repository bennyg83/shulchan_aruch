Levush: before he prays, etc. Meaning that he spreads out the whole Megillah, sheet over sheet, before beginning, and he should be careful that it not drag on the ground, as written by Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah. I also found this in Sefer Imarkal. The Bach wrote that this applies specifically to the shaliach tzibbur, but one who hears with a Megillah need not spread it out.
