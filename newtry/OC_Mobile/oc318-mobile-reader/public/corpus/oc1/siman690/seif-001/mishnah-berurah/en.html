1) Whether sitting. However, he should recite the blessing while standing.
2) But he should not read, etc. This means that if he reads publicly, he must read standing. See Biur Halacha, where we inclined that standing by means of leaning is permitted regarding the megillah.
3) And it is forbidden for the cantor, etc. For discharging the congregation is a matter of honor, and it is not proper for a person to apportion honor to himself as long as they have not honored him. All this is where he is not fixed for this role, but with a fixed prayer leader whom they appointed for this, he need not wait.
