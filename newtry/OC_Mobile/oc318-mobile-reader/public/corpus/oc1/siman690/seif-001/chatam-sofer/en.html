On the Mechaber, section 7, in the gloss, "and one should not abolish it." Note: See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chaim siman 51, and Chasam Sofer, Yoreh Deah siman 191. It appears to me that the reason is to make noise and confuse the listeners' ears so that they will not hear the name of this blotted-out Agagite.
