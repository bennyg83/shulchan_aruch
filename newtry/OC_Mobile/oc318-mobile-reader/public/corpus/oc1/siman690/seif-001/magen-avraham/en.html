Whether seated. See the beginning of seif 8, which implies that the blessing should be recited standing, and see the end of siman 146 and what I wrote there.
And it is forbidden for the chazzan. This applies where the custom is that the chazzan does not ordinarily read the Megillah. In our countries, where the chazzan always reads it, they need not expressly appoint him, because they appointed him for this from the outset, as stated in siman 139, seif 3. This is also implied by Mordechai; see there.
