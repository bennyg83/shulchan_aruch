Seif katan 1: Whether sitting, etc. See the end of siman 146. The listener must also stand at the time of the blessing, and there too he wrote this regarding the blessing over the Torah; the reason there is only because it is a matter of sanctity, as Magen Avraham wrote there. But here the listener must also bless, only that the one who blesses discharges the whole congregation with his blessing, as Magen Avraham wrote above, siman 284, and below, siman 692, seif katan 2. The listener is like one who answers and as though he himself is blessing; therefore, according to the law, the listener must stand just like the one who blesses.
