A megillah on the fourteenth and on the fifteenth requires one to seek out ten people. If ten are impossible, they read it individually. Gloss: There is room to be uncertain whether women join to make ten, Hagahos Ashiri, first chapter, Tur, siman 684. If they read it in the congregation and some individual did not hear it, he may read it even initially as an individual, since they read it in that city with ten, Beis Yosef in the name of Orchos Chaim. And when an individual reads it in its proper time, he must bless over it, Beis Yosef.
