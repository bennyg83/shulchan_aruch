Ten. Even if he has one hundred people with him, it is a mitzvah to read it in the congregation, because "in the multitude of the people is the King's glory"; Ran, Bach. And see Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 287, that even if his house is near the synagogue and its windows open into the synagogue, nevertheless he should go to the synagogue because of publicizing the miracle; see there.
