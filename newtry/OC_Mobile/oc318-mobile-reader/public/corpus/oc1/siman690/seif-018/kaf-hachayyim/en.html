Such is the view of the Rashba, Ran, Meiri, and Tur; see there. It is possible that they hold as the Tur wrote in siman 678 in the name of his brother, R' Yehudah, regarding an esrog; see there. However, the Rambam's view is that if half is missing it is invalid, for he holds like the Ra'avad there. Erekh HaShulchan, os 3. Such is also the view of Orchos Chaim and the Ra'ah, in accordance with the Rambam, that if half is missing it is invalid, as Birkei Yosef wrote in os 5; see there. Therefore, one should be stringent.
