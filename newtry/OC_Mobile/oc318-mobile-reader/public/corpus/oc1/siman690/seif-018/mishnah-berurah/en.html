61) On the fourteenth and on the fifteenth. This means on the fourteenth for unwalled towns and on the fifteenth for walled towns. That is to say, although it is in its proper time and there is publicizing of the miracle in any case, since everyone reads at that time, nevertheless one must make the effort to gather ten who will be present at the reading, because of publicizing the miracle. All the more so if it is not in its proper time, such as one who sets out to sea or goes out with a caravan and cannot find a megillah to take with him, where he is permitted to advance the reading before he leaves, as above in siman 688, section 7; or if the fifteenth falls on Shabbos, where those in walled towns must advance the reading and read it on Friday, as stated there in section 6. He certainly must gather ten for his reading. In this case it is even more stringent, for if there are not ten, those in walled towns should not bless over it.
62) To seek out ten. If at that time they are reading it publicly in the synagogue, then even if he has one hundred people in his house, it is a mitzvah to go there, because "in the multitude of the people is the King's glory." So too, one whose house was near the synagogue and whose windows opened into the synagogue must nevertheless go to the synagogue according to everyone, because of the multitude of the people. See Biur Halacha.
63) If women, etc. For it is possible that since it is only because of publicizing the miracle, women also suffice. See Peri Megadim: since the Rav did not mention minors, it implies that he holds that minors certainly do not join to make ten, for they are not obligated, and their obligation is only because of education.
64) Initially as an individual. For the miracle has already been publicized, and there is only the issue of "in the multitude of the people," so we do not trouble him to gather ten. And that which we hold, that even if he has a minyan in his house, nevertheless we interrupt Torah study and service to go and read publicly because of "in the multitude of the people," is because there the congregation is already prepared before him. But here he is not obligated to create a congregation. See Yad Ephraim, who wrote that this law which Rama brought in the name of Orchos Chaim is not agreed to by the other poskim, and therefore even in such a case he should seek out ten people.
65) And when the individual reads it. This means in a place where it is impossible for him to gather ten, or in the case that Rama described previously.
66) In its proper time. This excludes where the individual reads it not in its proper time, such as walled cities whose fifteenth falls on Shabbos, so they read it on Friday, and he reads it individually; he should not bless over it in any case, as above in seif katan 61.
