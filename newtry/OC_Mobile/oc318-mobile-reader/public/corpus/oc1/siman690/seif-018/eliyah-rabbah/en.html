With ten, etc. There is room to doubt whether women combine toward the ten, as noted by the Rama and Hagahos Maimoniyos from Hagahos Ashiri. It seems to me that the reason the Levush omitted this is that in the Tur, in the name of the Ittur in siman 689, it is written that they do not combine, implying even according to the opinion that women can discharge men. Nevertheless, they do not combine. Even according to the Ran who disagrees, that is because he holds that they can discharge men, as appears there in the Beit Yosef, but according to the opinion that they cannot discharge, they do not combine. The Levush follows his own reasoning, for he holds this way. The Rama and Hagahos Maimoniyos require study, for they wrote the words of Hagahos Ashiri without qualification and did not note the poskim mentioned above. Certainly we do not set aside the clarity of the poskim mentioned above before the doubts of Hagahos Ashiri. It also seems to me that Hagahos Ashiri speaks of minors, and so it seems from the Magen Avraham, but regarding women he may agree, because of concern for impropriety.
