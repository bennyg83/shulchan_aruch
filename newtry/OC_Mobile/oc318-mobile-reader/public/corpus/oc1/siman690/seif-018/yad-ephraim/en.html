In the gloss, section 18. "And when an individual reads, etc." See Beis Yosef, where it appears that according to Orchos Chaim in the name of the Raavad, even after the fact an individual may not read whenever there are not ten present. If so, the Rama, who wrote afterward that when an individual reads he must bless, implying even when there is no congregation there, and also from the fact that he did not write to disagree with the Shulchan Aruch, appears to have ruled like the Raavad regarding the initial law. This requires analysis: from where does he know this? It appears that the Raavad wrote this to reject the proof of Tosafos and the other poskim from that which Rabbi Yochanan said, "a congregation is different." This also answers the questions of the Ran and the poskim from the case of one who was passing behind the synagogue. If so, it is clear that the other poskim disagree with the Raavad and do not distinguish this way. Therefore, according to them, initially one requires ten even where there is no congregation.
