In Hagahah seif 18: and when individual reads, etc. — see Beit Yosef who appears early commentator in name of Raavad: even b'dieved individual should not read whenever there are not ten; and if so Rama who wrote afterward when individual reads must bless implies even without congregation; and from that he did not write to disagree with Shulchan Aruch — appears he ruled like Raavad l'chatchila; requires study from where it appears Raavad likewise to reject Tosafot and other poskim' proof from R' Yochanan: in congregation it is different; and also answered thus Ran's and poskim's difficulties from one who passed behind synagogue, etc.; and explained other poskim argue on Raavad and do not divide thus; and if so per them l'chatchila ten required even without congregation:
