37) One should protest against, etc. The reason is that the men who read understand the Holy Tongue, and there is concern for the opinion of the poskim who hold that where he understands the Holy Tongue, he does not fulfill by reading in a foreign language, and automatically he cannot discharge through this reading even one who does not understand the Holy Tongue, as above. Furthermore, there is the matter of "the couriers, sons of the royal steeds," whose foreign-language equivalent is not known. And if you will say that he should read "the couriers, sons of the royal steeds" in the Holy Tongue, one can say that initially it is forbidden to read it in two languages.
38) Even though it is written in a foreign language. For if it were not written in a foreign language, and he merely looks at it and translates it in a foreign language, he would be reading by heart, as above, and in any event he would not discharge them. The later authorities wrote that even those who read on both days as a stringency should also not read in a foreign language.
