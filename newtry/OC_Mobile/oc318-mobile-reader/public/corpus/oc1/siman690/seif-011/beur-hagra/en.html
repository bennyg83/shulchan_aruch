Section 11: One should protest, etc. Because according to the first view he does not discharge them, as written in the Yerushalmi mentioned above: one who knows Ashuris, etc.; like this rule, etc. This is what it means by "even though it is written, etc." For if not so, even according to the second view he does not discharge them, for that is how it explains the Yerushalmi mentioned above. And further, because of "the couriers, sons of the royal steeds," whose foreign-language equivalent he does not know, as written in the Gemara: do we know "the couriers, etc."? See Rivash, siman 388 and 391; see there.
