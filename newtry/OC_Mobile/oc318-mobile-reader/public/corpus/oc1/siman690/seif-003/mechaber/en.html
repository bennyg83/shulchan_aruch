The Megillah must be read in its entirety and from the written text. If one read it by heart, he has not fulfilled his obligation. Ideally, the entire Megillah must be written before him. After the fact, however, if the scribe omitted words in its middle, even up to half of it, and the reader recited those words by heart, he has fulfilled his obligation. {Rama: But if the scribe omitted even a minority at its beginning or end, he has not fulfilled his obligation. [Ran] Even in the middle, this allowance applies only if an entire subject was not omitted. [Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim]} If more than half is missing, or even if it is written but blurred so that the writing is not discernible, the Megillah is invalid.
