And the reader read them by heart. This applies specifically to letters whose omission ruins the reading, such as in the phrase, 'these days of Purim shall not pass'; if he omitted the word 'not,' he ruined the reading. The same applies to the mem of 'and gifts to the poor' and similar cases. But other letters or words that do not ruin the reading, he has fulfilled the obligation even if he did not read them at all. So writes Riaz in Shiltei Giborim. The Magen Avraham wrote that below, seif 14, this does not seem so. See seif katan 8.
