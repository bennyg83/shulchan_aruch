Section 3: But if. From what is written, "and even according to the one who says from 'There was a Jewish man,' etc." It implies that this is even indispensable, since we challenge from it there: rather, where he wrote verse by verse, etc. The Rashba answered that this is specifically in the middle, where it appears like a scroll that has an error; but when the beginning or end is missing, it is like a deficient scroll.
And even in its middle. This too follows from the above difficulty.
But if more than, etc. From the fact that we say, "part of it."
