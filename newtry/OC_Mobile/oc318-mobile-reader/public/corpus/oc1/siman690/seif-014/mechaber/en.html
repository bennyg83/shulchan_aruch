One who reads the megillah must intend to discharge the listener, and the listener must intend to fulfill. If the reader is the prayer leader, presumably his intent is for all the listeners, even if they are behind the synagogue. We are not exacting regarding its errors. Some say that this is specifically an error where the language and the meaning are the same, like that incident of two students who were sitting before Rav, where one read one form of "Jews" and the other read another form of "Jews," and he did not make either of them go back; but another error is not included.
