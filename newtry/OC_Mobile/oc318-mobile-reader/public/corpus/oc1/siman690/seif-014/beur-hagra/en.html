Section 14: The reader, etc., and if. Rosh Hashanah 29a.
We are not, etc., and some say. The Yerushalmi there says: we are not exacting regarding its errors. Rabbi Yitzchak and Rabbi Chananel were sitting before Rav, etc. It explains that this is specifically in such a case, for we hold that one must read all of it, and an error that changes the meaning is as though he did not read it. And we say, "Do we know what 'the couriers' means, etc.?" Learn from this that only regarding some words is it so, because there is still reading, etc. And there: if the scribe omitted letters, etc., and he read them, etc.; but if he did not read them, he has not fulfilled.
