He must concentrate. Rashba, siman 467, was asked whether a listener must focus on every word. The questioner had heard of a sage who held a Megillah in his hand, and wondered whether he could not fulfill his obligation from the reader, especially since women and unlearned people fulfill their obligation from an Ashuris text. Rashba answered that the questioner was correct, as is proved from speakers of foreign languages and unlearned people, and from the rule that one who passes behind a synagogue and hears the Megillah is presumed to have intended if he stopped. As to whether the reader must repeat when he omitted one word, even one omitted word requires repetition. It follows that if the listener failed to hear one word, he likewise did not fulfill his obligation, for the listener cannot have greater force than the reader. Tur, siman 59, and Beit Yosef, siman 692, write similarly. If he heard the words but did not consciously focus upon each one, however, he fulfilled his obligation. I have elaborated because Bach in siman 691 appears otherwise.
