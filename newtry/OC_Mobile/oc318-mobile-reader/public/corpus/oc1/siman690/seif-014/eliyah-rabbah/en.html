And he rolls it, etc. He places it in its place and then recites the blessing, as written in Ma'aglei Tzedek, Mateh Moshe, and Maharil. This is not comparable to what is written in siman 284, seif 6, that one should not remove the haftara until after reciting the blessing, because here it is different, since the blessing does not refer to the Megillah. These are the words of the Magen Avraham. I am very astonished at such a great authority, for I examined the words of the poskim mentioned above, and they did not write at all that he places it in its place. On the contrary, in Maharil it is written to place it before him, and in Mateh Moshe this is his language: they placed it near the reader on the platform. Thus its law is explicit like the haftara. Also, what he wrote that the blessing does not refer to it is not correct, for even if it was not instituted over it, nevertheless it refers to it. See siman 692.
