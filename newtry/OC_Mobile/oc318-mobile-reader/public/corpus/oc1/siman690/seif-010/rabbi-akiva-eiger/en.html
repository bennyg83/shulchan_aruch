Section 10. "He does not fulfill in a foreign language." And he cannot discharge, in a foreign language, the obligation of one who understands that foreign language but does not know Ashuris. Since the reader cannot fulfill his own obligation through this, he is, with respect to this, like one who is not obligated in the matter. This is from the Yerushalmi, brought in the Ran. There is also a practical difference according to the first opinion if the reader does not know the foreign language.
