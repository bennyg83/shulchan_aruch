Seif katan 15. You asked whether the listener must concentrate, etc. Although Magen Avraham cites Rashba on the words “he must intend to fulfill his obligation,” Rashba is not discussing that question, but whether the listener must attend to every word. He proves that such exact concentration is unnecessary, since unlearned people who do not understand the language cannot concentrate so precisely. Rather, if he inclines his ear to the reader and does not think of other matters, we presume he heard everything, and he need not consciously focus upon each word. If, however, he knows that he did not hear even one word, he has not fulfilled his obligation. In seif 3, omission is validated only because he may supply the missing words by reading them by heart. This, in my humble opinion, is the full intent of Magen Avraham. See Taz in the following siman, seif katan 2.
