33) He does not fulfill in a foreign language. The Mechaber's opinion appears to state the law anonymously like this view. Nevertheless, he does not go back and bless, for we are lenient in cases of doubt regarding blessings. It is explained in the poskim that according to this view, he cannot discharge even one who does not understand the Holy Tongue, for since his reading does not work for himself, he is like one who is not obligated in the matter, who cannot discharge others.
34) And some say that he fulfills. Know that wherever we say that one fulfills in a foreign language, this means that in any case he should read the words "the couriers, sons of the royal steeds" before them in the Holy Tongue.
35) In two languages. For example, part of the megillah in one language and part in another language.
36) They have fulfilled. After the fact; but initially it is not proper for it to be written in two languages.
