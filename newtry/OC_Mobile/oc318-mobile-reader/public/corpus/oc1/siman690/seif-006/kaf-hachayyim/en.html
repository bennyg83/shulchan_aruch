6) Ibid., Rama's gloss. It is forbidden for the chazzan to read, etc. This refers to a place where the custom is that the chazzan does not regularly read the Megillah. In our countries, however, where the chazzan always reads the Megillah, there is no need for them to tell him to do so, for he was appointed for this purpose from the outset, as written in siman 139, seif 3. This also appears from the Mordechai; see there. Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Eliyah Rabbah, os 2; Eshel Avraham, os 2; Mishna Berurah, os 3.
