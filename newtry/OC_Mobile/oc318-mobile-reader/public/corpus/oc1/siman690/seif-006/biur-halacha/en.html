One who reads, etc., out of order has not fulfilled his obligation. It appears that he must also go back and bless where he began reading immediately after the blessing from somewhere other than the beginning of the megillah, for his blessing was nullified, like the law of any interruption between the blessing and the beginning of the mitzvah. See above, at the end of siman 422, section 6, in Mishnah Berurah and Biur Halacha.
