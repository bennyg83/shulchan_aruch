Shulchan Aruch, seif 5. Even if he paused. Rama did not gloss here that if the interruption was caused by compulsion he returns to the beginning, as he did in siman 65 regarding Krias Shema according to Rosh, because Megillah is D’Rabbanan and regarding a D’Rabbanan obligation we do not rule like Rosh. Magen Avraham writes similarly in siman 422 in the name of Darkei Moshe regarding Hallel, and Darkei Moshe explicitly writes the same regarding Megillah. See there.
