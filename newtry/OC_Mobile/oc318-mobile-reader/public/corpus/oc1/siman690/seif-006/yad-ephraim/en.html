Shulchan Aruch, section 10. "And some say that he has fulfilled, etc." It appears that this is when the words "the couriers, etc." are written as they are written in the Holy Tongue, as written in Magen Avraham, seif katan 12. According to Magen Avraham, he has not fulfilled even for one who knows only the foreign language alone, and he does not discharge his obligation. See Rivash.
