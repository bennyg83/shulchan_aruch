21) Out of order. This means that he read later verses before he read earlier verses, even only one verse, as below. The same law applies where he read the second section before the first.
22) And skipped the second and read the third. The same law applies if, after the skipping, he read the whole megillah or one entire subject, and afterward read the skipped verse; he has not fulfilled. The same applies if he read one word out of order; all this is included in out of order, and he must go back and read in order.
23) And afterward went back and read the second, he has not fulfilled. This means where he then reads the fourth verse. Rather, after he went back and read the second verse, he must again read the third verse. This is what he concludes: rather, what should he do, etc.
