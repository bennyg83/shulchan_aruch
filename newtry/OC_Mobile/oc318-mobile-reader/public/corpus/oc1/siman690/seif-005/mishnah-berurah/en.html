17) Even if he waited long enough to finish all of it. That is to say, he could have finished the whole megillah from its beginning to its end during this time.
18) He has fulfilled. If he paused because of circumstances beyond his control, then according to what we hold above in siman 65 regarding Shema, that he returns to the beginning, the same applies here, that he goes back and reads. However, he should not bless a second time. See what I wrote there in Biur Halacha.
19) And even if he spoke, etc. That is to say, it is not only where he paused in silence; even if they stopped in the middle of the megillah with conversation and waited because of this, he also need not go back, but finishes from the place where he stopped. All this is when the reader spoke. But a listener who spoke and because of this did not hear the reading, even if he missed only one word, the opinion of several poskim is that he has not fulfilled. Therefore there is great need for each person to have a Chumash, because while the children strike Haman and the like, it is impossible to hear several words from the prayer leader; he fulfills through what he reads by heart from the Chumash, at least after the fact, Peri Megadim.
20) However, we rebuke, etc. Since he blessed over it, he may not interrupt in the meantime until after the entire mitzvah.
