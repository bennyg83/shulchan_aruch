Levush: lest they direct, etc. This implies that he himself may listen to the shaliach tzibbur even though he is reading. It also implies that this is specifically by heart, but from an invalid Megillah he should not read, as the Levush wrote at the beginning of this seif, because he directs his attention, etc. This is from the Beit Yosef in the name of Shibolei HaLeket, unlike the Magen Avraham, who wrote the reason of lest he direct, etc., regarding one reading from an invalid Megillah. This also requires study, for in Tanchya it is written that one may not read along with the reader by heart, for they fulfill the obligation only if they hear from the one reading from writing. So too it is written in Sefer Imarkal, implying that even by heart he himself does not fulfill the obligation. One may also say that when they wrote the reason lest they direct, etc., this means that even if he himself is not concerned, we rebuke him because of others. In Mateh Moshe, siman 1002, he wrote that when we read from Chumashim along with the reading of the shaliach tzibbur, we rely on the shaliach tzibbur's reading, because then one is better able to hear every word, and he should read in a very low voice, as with Torah reading. In my humble opinion, one should be stringent with the Megillah to listen from a Chumash and not read, for Megillah reading is more stringent than Torah reading in this respect, as we have said. See siman 692, seif katan 6. Also regarding Torah reading there are those who are stringent, as written in siman 141.
