7) [Seif 2] Even two, and even ten, may read it, etc. Although with the Torah only one person reads, the Megillah is different, since it is beloved to them and they concentrate, so all these voices can be heard distinctly. Beit Yosef; Taz, s.k. 2; Magen Avraham, s.k. 3.
8) Ibid. They may read it, etc. This implies even l'chatchilah, and such is the view of all the poskim. Erekh HaShulchan, os 1.
9) Ibid. And even ten may read it, etc. It appears that when there are many people in the synagogue, as a result even ten may read for the congregation. However, when there is only a minyan, it is preferable that one person read and the others listen, in fulfillment of the principle of greater honor to the King through a multitude of people. Eshel Avraham, os 3.
10) Ibid. They and those listening to them fulfill their obligation. This applies specifically when one can hear every single word. But if there are so many readers that even when the listener concentrates he cannot hear, he has not fulfilled his obligation. Likkutei Pri Chadash; Mishna Berurah, os 4.
