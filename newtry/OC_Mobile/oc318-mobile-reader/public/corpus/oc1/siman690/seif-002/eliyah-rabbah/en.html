He should not read, etc. See siman 53, seif 15, that a fixed shaliach tzibbur descends before the ark on his own. It is possible that here it speaks where he is not fixed. In Mordechai, in the chapter HaKorei, which is the source of this law, he holds that the shaliach tzibbur should not read, etc. Although there it refers to Torah reading, the same law applies to the Megillah. See siman 139, seif 6. The Magen Avraham wrote that there are countries where the chazzan always reads the Megillah and they need not tell him to do so.
