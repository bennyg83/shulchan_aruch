If it was written in translation or in another language of the nations, one does not fulfill his obligation by reading it unless he knows that language alone. But if it was written in Hebrew script and he read it in Aramaic for an Aramaic-speaker, he has not fulfilled, for this is found to be reading by heart. And since the reader has not fulfilled his own obligation, the one who hears from him has not fulfilled. Gloss: But one need not be concerned about which script it is written in, Beis Yosef.
