And one from the Torah. If he completed with a verse from the Prophets, he fulfilled his obligation.
And in Your Torah. One must also say, 'And so it is written in the words of Your holiness,' and likewise, 'And it was said through Your servants the Prophets' [Shiltei Giborim; Knesses HaGedolah].
He fulfilled his obligation. Even ab initio one is permitted not to say any verse. But if he began one verse, he must say all of them; so it is in the Gemara, Tur, and Bach. Nevertheless, it seems to me that nowadays they have made it obligatory upon themselves, and one must say all of them, as the Rif wrote regarding the Maariv prayer. Therefore the Rema wrote in language of after the fact.
