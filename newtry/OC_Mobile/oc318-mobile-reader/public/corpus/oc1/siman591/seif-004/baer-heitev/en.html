He fulfilled his obligation. Even ab initio one is permitted not to say any verse. But if he began with one verse, he must say all of them. Nevertheless, it seems to me that nowadays they have accepted it upon themselves as an obligation and must say all of them, as written regarding the Maariv prayer; therefore the Rema wrote in language of after the fact. Magen Avraham. One must also say, 'And so it is written in the words of Your holiness,' and, 'And so it was said through Your servants the Prophets.' Shiltei Giborim; Knesses HaGedolah.
