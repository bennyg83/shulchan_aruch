(7) We say ten, etc. Corresponding to the ten verses of 'Praise God in His sanctuary,' corresponding to the Ten Commandments, and corresponding to the ten utterances through which the world was created [Gemara].
(8) Malchiyos. To accept upon himself the kingship of Heaven; and Zichronos, through which our remembrance should rise before Him for good. And with what? With the shofar [Gemara].
(9) And one from the Torah. If he completed with a verse from the Prophets, he fulfilled his obligation [there].
(10) He only said, 'and in Your Torah,' etc. This language is not precise. It means that he says, 'as is written in Your Torah,' and through this it is considered as though he mentioned those verses with his mouth.
(11) He fulfilled his obligation. See Magen Avraham, that this is specifically after the fact, but ab initio one must be careful to say all of them. He further wrote that one must also say, 'And so it is written in the words of Your holiness,' and likewise, 'And it was said through Your servants the Prophets.'
