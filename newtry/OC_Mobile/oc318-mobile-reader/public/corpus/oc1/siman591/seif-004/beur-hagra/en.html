Three of them. There, 1b; see Rashi there, s.v. 'and he begins with Torah,' until the Mishnah; and see there in the Rosh in the name of the Maggid Mishneh.
And if he wanted. The Rosh there. So too it is implied from the statement, 'one may not have fewer,' etc. The Rosh wrote: although they said what these correspond to, likewise in chapter 3 of Megillah regarding the number of those called to the Torah, they nevertheless said that one may add to them.
However, if. Gemara 35a. See Tosafos 8b, s.v. 'if you say,' and Rabbeinu Tam explained, etc. The Shulchan Aruch omitted it, as written in Beis Yosef, because the Rambam holds like the explanation of Rashi. But the Rosh and the Tur, although they disagree with Rabbeinu Tam in what he wrote that it does not refer to the Mussaf verses, and see siman 268, seif 4 in the gloss, nevertheless agree with him in this, in that he holds like the explanation of Rashi's teachers; and this is the view of the Rav.
