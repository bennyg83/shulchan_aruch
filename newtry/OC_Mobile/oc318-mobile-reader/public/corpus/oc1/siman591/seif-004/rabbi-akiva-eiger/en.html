Seif 4. And ten verses of Shofaros in every blessing. He concludes and says, 'Who hears the sound of the teruos of His people Yisrael today with mercy.' On Shabbos he should not say 'today.' So wrote Maharikash. In the book Gan HaMelech by the author of Ginas Veradim, seif 5, he wrote that on Shabbos one should say, 'Who hears the remembrance of the teruah of His people Yisrael today with mercy.'
