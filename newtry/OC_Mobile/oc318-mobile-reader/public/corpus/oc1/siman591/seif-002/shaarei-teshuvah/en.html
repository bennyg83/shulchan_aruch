Mussaf offerings. See Baer Heitev, and see Halachos Ketanos there, that even if he did not finish the blessing he does not go back on the second day. On the first day, if he finished the blessing, he goes back to the beginning of the blessing.

They say 'besides,' etc. See Dagul Mervavah, who says not to say 'the continual burnt-offering,' etc., but only 'besides the burnt-offering of the month and its meal-offering,' etc. See also below, siman 621. The custom follows the Rema, and in the kuntres Mateh Ephraim I wrote about this.
