(2) For we do not mention, etc. For one should not say the verses of the Mussaf offering except on Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh, where people are accustomed to them and will not come to err. But on other festivals, where people are not accustomed to them, they may come to err; therefore we do not mention them, but rather say, 'as is written in Your Torah,' etc.
(3) And we do not say the verses of the Mussaf of Rosh Chodesh. Even in the morning near the sections of the offerings we do not say it. The reason for all this is so that people should not say that the second day of Rosh Chodesh is the primary one, as with other months of Rosh Chodesh; in truth this is not so, for the month of Elul is never intercalated. Another reason is not to give this day an advantage because it is Rosh Chodesh, since it has a greater advantage as Rosh Hashanah. Rosh Chodesh is included in what we say, 'remembrance,' which also refers to Rosh Chodesh; and it is written, 'on the day of concealment,' which is Rosh Hashanah, when the new moon is concealed.
(4) And two goats for atonement. This means the goat that is for a sin-offering and the goat of Rosh Chodesh.
(5) And we also say 'the Mussaf offerings of the Day of Remembrance.' This is to include Rosh Chodesh as well. Although the Mechaber wrote this himself in seif 3, the Rema wrote it here to inform us that even according to our custom, where we say the verses, one should say 'Mussaf offerings.'
