[2] [Levush] But on Shabbos. I say that it is better to perform the circumcision before Ashrei, so that the circumcision will be joined to the Torah reading, as Mateh Moshe and Shiltei Giborim in Mordechai on Shabbos wrote in the name of a responsum of the Geonim. This is also implied in Rokeach, siman 217, regarding Yom Kippur. Another reason is so that there should not be so great an interruption in the Kaddish that relates to Ashrei, as they do on other Shabbosos, when they say Yekum Purkan, Mi Sheberach, the memorial prayer, and Av Harachamim between the Torah reading and Ashrei. See siman 621, seif 2.
