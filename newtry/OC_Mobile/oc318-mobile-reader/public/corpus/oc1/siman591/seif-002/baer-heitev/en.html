Mussaf offerings. Although the Beis Yosef wrote this nearby in seif 3, the Rema wrote it here to inform us that even according to our custom, where we say the verses, one should say 'Mussaf offerings.' See Magen Avraham. If on the second day he said 'Mussaf' in the singular, he need not go back; Mabit, part 1, siman 46, and Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 222. See Yad Aharon.
