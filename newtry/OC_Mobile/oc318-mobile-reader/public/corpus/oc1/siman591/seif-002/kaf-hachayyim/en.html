2) One who is under duress and cannot come to the synagogue, such as the people in the fields, an elderly person, or a sick person, is discharged by the shaliach tzibbur even though he did not come to the synagogue. Tur; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, os 2. This applies if they do not know how to pray or are unable to pray. If they are able to pray, they should pray in their own locations even though they cannot come to the synagogue, for the reason we wrote in the preceding os.
