That the verses are not recited, etc. The verses of the Mussaf offering are recited only on Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh, for people are familiar with them and will not come to err. Regarding the other festivals, however, people are not familiar with them and may come to err. Therefore, those verses are not recited; instead, one says, ‘As is written in Your Torah,’ etc., in a place where this suffices to fulfill the obligation of Malchuyos, Zichronos, and Shofaros.
And two goats to atone. This means the goat brought as the sin offering and the goat of Rosh Chodesh. Although Rosh Chodesh is not mentioned, this is so that people should not say that the second day of Rosh Chodesh is primary, as it is on other Rashei Chodashim. There is a further reason not to confer distinction upon this day because it is Rosh Chodesh: it already possesses a greater distinction because it is Rosh Hashanah. Rosh Chodesh is included in the phrase ‘a remembrance,’ which also refers to Rosh Chodesh, and the verse states, ‘On the day when the moon is concealed’; this is Rosh Hashanah, when the new moon is concealed.
