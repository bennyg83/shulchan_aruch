The common custom, etc. Even on other festivals we do not mention the verses of the Mussaf offering, and we fulfill the Mussaf requirement with what we say, 'as is written in Your Torah through Moshe Your servant.' Only on Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh, where people are accustomed to them and will not come to err, do we do so. We also say 'the Mussaf offerings of this Day of Remembrance,' and after 'besides the burnt-offering of Rosh Chodesh' we say 'and their meal-offering' [Terumas HaDeshen, siman 143]. There is one who wrote that on the second day one should not say 'Mussaf offerings' [Kol Bo].
