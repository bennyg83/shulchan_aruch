In the gloss, seif 2. Rather, they say 'besides,' etc. See Darkei Moshe in the name of Terumas HaDeshen, that one who says 'besides the burnt-offering of the month' should afterward say 'and their meal-offering,' etc. Although he already said 'and the two continual offerings according to their law,' he may properly say 'and the continual burnt-offering,' for he is mentioning the verse as it is written and it is not proper to skip part of it; this is unlike if he says it in the opposite order. See there in Pesakim, siman 143, and in Kesavim, siman 117. See Ateres Zekeinim, who brought the words of Terumas HaDeshen. In my humble opinion, this is how the words of the Rema should read, but it was omitted in print, for in Darkei Moshe he also wrote the order as written here, and on this he brought the words of Terumas HaDeshen, that one should say afterward 'and their meal-offering'; see there.
In Taz s.k. 2. That the month is covered on it. In Tosafos on Rosh Hashanah, folio 8, he brought the Yerushalmi and wrote that the liable one should not come and atone for the innocent one, for that of Rosh Chodesh is called liable, etc.
