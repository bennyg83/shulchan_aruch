"And the Binding of Yitzchak — may You today remember it mercifully for his descendants" — this is the well-known text; and those who are precise to say "may You remember it for the descendants of Yaakov" deviate from the formula that the Sages coined in the blessings, and this is nothing but an error. {Rama: And we say "Aleinu" and "Meloch," and the chazzan adds "Ochilah" and the other piyyutim, each place according to its custom (Tur).}
