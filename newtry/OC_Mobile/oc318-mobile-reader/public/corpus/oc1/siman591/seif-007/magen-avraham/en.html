For the seed of Yaakov. For if one says 'for his seed,' Esav would be included. One who errs in this should know that we learned: one who says, 'Konam from the seed of Avraham,' is permitted to benefit from the nations, as it says, 'for through Yitzchak seed will be called yours,' end quote of the Rivash. So too wrote the commentator in Nedarim, meaning that we say 'through Yitzchak,' but not all of Yitzchak. This requires analysis, for the Holy One, blessed is He, said that statement to Avraham, that Esav would not be called by Avraham's name; but he is called by Yitzchak's name, and one who vows not to benefit from the seed of Yitzchak is forbidden to benefit from Esav. One may answer that since the Merciful One said to Yitzchak, 'To your seed I will give all these lands,' and He did not give them to Esav, we learn that Esav is not included; so it is written in the Yerushalmi in Nedarim. But this is difficult, for He gave Esav the Keini, Kenizi, and Kadmoni, as Rashi wrote in Parashas Devarim. One may answer that this was not an actual inheritance, as with the descendants of Lot; see there.
He changes the formula. Nevertheless, after the fact he has fulfilled his obligation, for this is not an actual change. Maharil wrote 'for the seed of Yaakov,' etc., as above, but in Yefeh Mareh on Eichah Rabbah, part 2, siman 32, he wrote to say 'for his seed'; see there.
