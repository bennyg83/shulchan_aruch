(12) He changes, etc. For Esav too is not included in 'the seed of Yitzchak.' Nevertheless, after the fact he has fulfilled his obligation, for this is not an actual change. Some say that on the contrary, it is better to say 'for the seed of Yaakov,' because in prayer we need to explain as much as possible. Therefore each person should follow the custom of his place, for each has an authority on whom to rely.
