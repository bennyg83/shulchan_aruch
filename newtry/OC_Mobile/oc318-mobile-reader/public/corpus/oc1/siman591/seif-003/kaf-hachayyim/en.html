3) Ibid. Nine blessings. The Tur cites a dispute whether the congregation should pray seven or nine blessings. He wrote in the name of his father, the Rosh, of blessed memory, that one who prays seven recites a blessing in vain, because he omits some of the blessings. The custom of our forefathers is Torah and should not be changed, see there. So too wrote the Beit Yosef: "Since this does not arise as a practical matter, because the widespread custom throughout the world is to pray nine, I did not see fit to elaborate," end quote. He therefore ruled accordingly in the Shulchan Aruch. This is also the consensus of the Acharonim, and this is now the established custom in all places, that the congregation also prays nine blessings. This is likewise required according to the mystical meaning, as written above, siman 585, os 28, see there.
