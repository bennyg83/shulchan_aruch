(6) And also on the second day, etc. That is to say, although in the month of Tishrei the first day is the primary day by which the festivals are counted, nevertheless we say 'Mussaf offerings' so that people not treat the second day lightly. After the fact, if he said 'Mussaf' in the singular, he need not go back; but on the first day, if he said 'Mussaf' in the singular, he goes back.
