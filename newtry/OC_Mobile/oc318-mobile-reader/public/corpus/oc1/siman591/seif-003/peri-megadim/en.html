Mishnah — Taz in Nedarim 41a and Magen Avraham note 7. And what he wrote that seed also means disqualified seed — Yevamot 70 and 22b in Tosafot s.v. ken implies there seed fit/unfit needs amplification — see there; nevertheless from disqualified Rabbi learns: although usually unspecified seed is fit, minority is also disqualified, and in prayer we need more clarity — see Raavad z"l parashat Mishpatim verse "and if it is evil in the eyes of her master to the foreign people" — although regarding Yishmael it is written "for he is your seed" the land was not given except to Yaakov and his sons — see there Magen Avraham note 7 and Rambam z"l explaining the mishnah Nedarim "a convert shall be your seed" — only Yaakov; requires study, for in the gemara it says "for in Isaac," etc.; and Yishmael is also called "your seed" — nevertheless unspecified is only seed of Yaakov and his sons as said; see Tosafot Yom Tov Nedarim and Rambam z"l chapter 8 law 21 from chapter Nedarim; and see there Magen Avraham note 8 will be explained further.
