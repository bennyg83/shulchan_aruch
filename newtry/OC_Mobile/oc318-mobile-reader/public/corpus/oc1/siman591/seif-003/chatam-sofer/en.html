(Name: and not all Yitzchak). It appears to me: in all this in what Ramban wrote on parashat Devarim on the verse "your brothers the children of Esau" see there, that the children of Ishmael and Keturah are not called brothers because it is written "for in Yitzchak shall seed be called to you" — not so Esau; and one must say Ramban holds as Magen Avraham wrote that he is called at least seed of Yitzchak and there is a side of brotherhood from Yitzchak's force — not so children of Ishmael and Keturah. And in responsa Yaavetz part 1 siman 144 he wrote "to your seed" refers to Avraham whom he mentioned at the beginning that Avraham our father bound Yitzchak his son; and on this he said to the seed of Avraham remember — and Esau is not included; and this is correct. However what he also wrote that there is no loss if they pray also for nations of the world on this day and he brings proof from Noah similarly — requires study.
