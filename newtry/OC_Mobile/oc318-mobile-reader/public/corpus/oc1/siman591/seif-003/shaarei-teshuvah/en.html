In the Shulchan Aruch: and also on the second day, etc. See Magen Avraham and Beer HaGolah. Birkei Yosef wrote that even in Eretz Yisrael, at the time when they sanctified the month by sighting, they would observe two days because of doubt, as the Rambam wrote. Therefore now in Eretz Yisrael we observe Rosh Hashanah as they did then. It follows that for residents of Eretz Yisrael, Rosh Hashanah is like the festivals of the Diaspora, and it is proper to say 'the Mussaf offerings'; see there.
