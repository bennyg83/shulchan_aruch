Seif 6 — some call out against the custom to complete the Shofarot verses with the verse "and on the day of your joy" which did not mention shofar but chatzotzrot; and Rosh and Ran wrote to uphold the custom. It appears to me: Rosh's reason is as he wrote: I say one should not change the coin of blessings practiced in early days; and just as teruah without shofar is called shofar, so tekiah — and because chatzotzrot are written in the verse they are not inferior — end of his words. I wonder from gemara Rosh Hashanah 29 that asks there: Kohanim, Leviim, and Israelites are obligated in Shema — obvious; and answers Kohanim were needed lest one think since it is written "a day of teruah shall be for you" — perhaps only the tekiah of one day obligates and these Kohanim since they exist in tekiyot all year as written "and you shall blow with chatzotzrot" — say they are not obligated; it teaches. It asks: are they comparable — there chatzotzrot and here shofar, etc.; and why does it ask — after all they exist in tekiyot which are also with shofar — thus they exist in tekiyot all year; strained to distinguish action from mention — for mention too we need shofar not chatzotzrot; even so since tekiah is written it includes shofar — if so in action too let us say since they exist in tekiah and tekiah is shofar we do not care that in truth they are chatzotzrot — requires study. When I was here it was difficult: why does the gemara ask obvious — let us say so per above 27 that all shofarot are valid except the cow's horn because it is a keren; Ulla said the reason of Rabbanan is because a prosecutor cannot become an advocate; it asks there but because it is a keren it teaches; it answers one reason and Rabbanan say another — keren and not shofar. If so why ask here — perhaps the tanna's intent is all are obligated in shofar tekiyot specifically and do not fulfill with the cow's horn which is keren — the supposition that the main reason we need shofar is because a prosecutor cannot become an advocate — if so Kohanim and Leviim who did not sin with it would fulfill even with the cow; therefore it teaches the main reason is because it is keren — therefore they do not fulfill with the cow, and likewise for other amoraim who resolve there alternatively — one could say per truth it is possible to say thus per Rabbanan's reason because it asked there and there is the spoon and coal-pan and golden garments and it was not pleasing to say thus in Rabbanan's reason — nevertheless per the supposition it works to say Kohanim and Leviim would be permitted even with keren — therefore it teaches in truth that reason does not exist, only because it is called keren — requires study. I wonder on Ran and Rosh and those who call out that they did not see Sifrei brought in Yalkut on parashat Beha'alotekha on "and you shall blow with chatzotzrot" — R' Nasan says: "and you shall blow with chatzotzrot" — unnecessary, behold shofarot — see there. This is explicit that these are shofarot; the explanation of Sifrei's intent we wrote in derush for Rosh Hashanah years 584, 595, and 561 — see there and examine well.
