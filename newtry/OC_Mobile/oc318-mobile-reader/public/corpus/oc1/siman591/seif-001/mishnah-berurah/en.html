(1) The congregation should pray, etc., nine blessings. This excludes some poskim who hold that in the silent prayer they should pray only seven blessings, as at Shacharis. In a place where the custom is that the congregation prays together with the shaliach tzibbur word for word, and the shaliach tzibbur blows and errs, it is forbidden to speak in the middle and rebuke him, for they have already fulfilled their obligation with the sitting blasts. Even if they did not hear the sitting blasts, they should go back after the prayer and blow the set in which he erred, and that is sufficient.
