(א) Seif 61 — the congregation should pray in a whisper, etc. Gemara Rosh Hashanah 34b: he said to him per your words why does the congregation, etc.; and even though we say in chapter 4 of Berakhot these nine of Rosh Hashanah, etc., which implies at first glance in all prayers — and so Razah; but in Yerushalmi chapter 1 of Shevuot we learn R' Yaakov in the name of R' Yosi: one who passes before the ark on Rosh Hashanah morning — Beit Shammai say eight and Beit Hillel say seven; in musaf Beit Shammai say ten and Beit Hillel say nine — meaning if it falls on Shabbat. And what is written in chapter 4 of Rosh Hashanah the second day the blower blows — why, etc., and we do on the first, etc. — this means: let it be said in the morning nine and blow there; see Ran and all poskim; and so explained Rosh and Ritz Gi'at. But the view of the Geonim is the congregation does not pray in a whisper but seven like the morning; and Ramban agreed; and he wrote that what is written halakha follows Rabbenu Gamliel in Berakhot of Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur — that is Malchuyot, Zichronot, and Shofarot alone that they cry out — and so in Yerushalmi R' Huna Tzipporin in the name of R' Yochanan: halakha follows Rabbenu Gamliel in these tekiyot; R' Zeira and R' Chisda were sitting for these tekiyot — whoever prayed came with his prayer; Rav Chisda stood; R' Zeira said: did we not already pray? He said: I pray and pray again, for easterners came here and said in the name of R' Yochanan: halakha follows Rabbenu Gamliel in these tekiyot — meaning they prayed the morning prayer and Rosh Chodesh returned and prayed musaf in a whisper. R' Zeira challenged him: granted in the morning which are seven the prayer leader does not discharge others; but in musaf which are forty the prayer leader discharges — why should he pray? He answered him that the prayer leader discharges only in these tekiyot in Malchuyot, Zichronot, and Shofarot alone as above. Ran — and not like Razah above; see there where he elaborated; see in Rosh there; see in Milchamot the Ramban's claims and their refutation — see there where he elaborated greatly; but now there is no practical difference in all this, for they cannot intend to fulfill with the prayer leader's blessing.
