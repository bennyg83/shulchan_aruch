Shevus Yaakov, part 3, siman 43, wrote regarding what is printed in the Sulzbach machzorim, which the Jews of Ashkenaz follow, 'and the binding of Yitzchak for the seed of Yisrael' - another text: 'for the seed of Yaakov may You remember' - that one should not say this, but rather as written in the Shulchan Aruch, 'for his seed may You remember,' and as the words of the Rivash. So too wrote Mabit, part 1, siman 270, and Maharit, Orach Chaim section, siman 9; see there. See She'eilas Yaavetz, part 1, siman 144, who also wrote that the primary text is 'for the seed may You remember.' See there, that the intention of 'for the seed may You remember' means the seed of Avraham, for that is what is being discussed, since he says, 'and remember for us the binding, when Avraham our father bound,' etc.; see there. See Responsa Mahari Bassan, siman 1, and Parashas Derachim, folio 11; see there.
