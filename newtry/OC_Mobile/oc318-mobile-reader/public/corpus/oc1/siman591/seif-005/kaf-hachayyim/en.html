So wrote the Beit Yosef in the name of responsa Rivash, siman 37, that there is no doubt that the proper custom is for the congregation to pray first and for the shaliach tzibbur to pray silently with them, in order to arrange the prayer, and afterward for the shaliach tzibbur to repeat the prayer aloud, as stated at the end of Rosh Hashanah. This is the custom in Catalonia and in every place whose inhabitants are Torah scholars, see there. Sefer Chassidim, siman 784, wrote that the prayer should be fluent in his mouth, like R' Chanina ben Dosa, for when his prayer was fluent in his mouth it was accepted, end quote. We have already written above, siman 585, os 28, that according to the mystical meaning, both the silent prayer and the repetition are necessary, see there.
