(5) The musaf additions, etc. The Bach challenged the Rama: since they mention verses of Rosh Hashanah one should say only "the musaf" — see there at length. I challenged in Eliyahu Zuta: behold Maran who brought Beit Yosef that one does not mention verses of Rosh Chodesh because he holds one mentions those of Rosh Hashanah — even so he ruled to say musaf additions. Again Magen Avraham was printed and challenged this too. And it appears to me the Bach holds per Ran one also does not say verses of Rosh Hashanah — only the gemara teaches us that even regarding Rosh Chodesh verses said every Rosh Chodesh — even so one does not say them; however in Rabbeinu Yerucham page 47 column 48 it implies that even though they say the offerings of Rosh Hashanah, even so they say musaf additions.
