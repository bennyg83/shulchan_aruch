[Levush:] And there are those who say, etc. In Shulchan Aruch, siman 564, he ruled according to the first opinion, and so wrote Beis Yosef; however, the Bach and Taz agreed with "there are those who say," and so ruled Ri'az in Shiyurei Kenesses HaGedolah. The Bach wrote: and concerning one who is stringent, it shall be said of him that he is holy, until here. I found that one should not eat at night more than his usual amount, for then the fast will not benefit him, since that excess which he eats is stored up for him for the day of his fast.
