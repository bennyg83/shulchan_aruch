(א) Seif 1 — even enough. Rosh and Ran; and as explained per their words; and these, etc.

(ב) That we presume he ate it and these, etc. Rambam's language; and so it appears; Rif — see there.

(ג) And some say if, etc. Tosafot there: otherwise perhaps he left over; and as written there regarding a mouse in bread one leaves over; and the first version holds: since crumbs fell, we presume he ate — per his view that he holds like Rava who said "this — what," etc.; but here they certainly ate since there are crumbs, and Rava does not hold one leaves over; and Tosafot and Tur per their view hold like R. Yehudah: even if he certainly ate we say he left over — as written siman 439.

(ד) Laws of mouse, etc. Rambam ruled per his view to rule accordingly; but Tur ruled all are doubts and everything depends whether he nullified or not; and see Beit Yosef who was uncertain in Tur's words whether "if he nullified" means specifically that he already nullified, or that he can still nullify — and we follow leniently that he can still nullify; and so Bach, unlike Darkei Moshe; and so Rosh wrote explicitly in chapter 1 seif 2 "it appears," etc.; and so Shulchan Aruch in seif 2; and nullifies — siman 442 seif 8; and see there.
