(א) (s.k. 1) To return, etc. Meaning the rooms, etc.; for Bach holds when it says "the whole house" it means literally the whole house — all rooms; and therefore it speaks where they did not know which room it entered.

(ב) And in my humble opinion, etc. One need not check except that room, etc.; unlike Bach who holds other rooms need no checking in any case; and Magen Avraham's view is like the Gemara's question when one enters a courtyard, etc.; if so, the same here — specifically with nullification, which is a rabbinic doubt, or a small loaf where we tell him "you ate it" and it is a doubt — as written siman 439; but otherwise one must check all rooms.

(ג) Meaning two houses, etc. As explained siman 439 — it is also a question; and with nullification one need not check — see there.
