(Seif 1) After the loaf. Meaning: in this case it is required that he know clearly he found the loaf that entered; but if he found a loaf — even per Rambam who rules we say a lost loaf is a found loaf; and so Tur who holds thus with nullification — nevertheless here it is worse since he found crumbs and it is recognizable another loaf came to the house; one may say perhaps this found one is another loaf (so Magen Avraham Leff).
