(א) To return and check the house. Meaning the open rooms; but locked ones need no bedikah, and we are not concerned it entered through a hole; and if we saw which room it entered, the other rooms need no bedikah (Bach). And in my humble opinion "house" means a room — one need not check except that room we saw entered; but other rooms — this is the Gemara's question when one enters a courtyard and it is unknown whether he entered the house; and we conclude: even though very close to the house we rely leniently and need not check if he nullified or the loaf is not large — as siman 439; the same here. And if we did not know which room it entered — we are in two houses and we do not know which it entered.

(ב) That it is not its way. And the same for a dog or chicken — they do not crumble (Bach, Rokeach).

(ג) A toddler entered, etc. And it speaks where he lacks understanding; if he has understanding he is believed to say he ate it all — as siman 437.
