(1) [Seif 1] A mouse that entered a checked house, etc. — even with a small loaf a mouse could eat, and even if he nullified — we do not rely leniently because of a rabbinic doubt, since doubt of eating does not remove from a presumption of definite prohibition; for since we know certainly a mouse dragged the chametz before us, doubt does not remove certainty. Bach in this siman and siman 439. Kaf HaChayim note 1; Chok Yosef note 1; Makor Chayim note 1; Birkei Yosef note 1; Otzar HaChayim note 1; Rabbi Zalman note 1; and see above siman 434 note 6.
