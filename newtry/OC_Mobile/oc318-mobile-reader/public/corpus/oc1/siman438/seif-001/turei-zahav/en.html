(א) A mouse that entered, etc. Rosh wrote: where he checked and afterward saw a mouse enter the house with bread in its mouth — even though he went up to that house and finds crumbs the measure of that bread — we do not say "perhaps it ate that bread and these crumbs are its"; end. Difficulty: initially he said "and finds crumbs the measure of that bread" and then says we do not say "perhaps it ate"; and in Rif he did not write the words "the measure of that bread"; and in my humble opinion emend: "and finds crumbs — we do not say perhaps it ate that bread and these crumbs are its"; and even if he finds crumbs the measure of that bread.

(ב) But a toddler entered there, etc. Difficulty: we say in the Gemara 10 regarding a weasel dragged — we do not say eating doubt removes definite chametz; why do we say here we presume it ate? One may say: here there are indicia to the matter since there are crumbs here; therefore if there were no crumbs here we would not remove from definite chametz; and the second view holds doubt does not remove definite chametz unless there is enough for the whole loaf.

(ג) Laws of mouse, etc. The Rabbi omitted, etc. And I say all are needed to inform us similarly regarding a gentile, which is common.
