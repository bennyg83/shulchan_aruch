1) [Se'if 1] A mouse entered a house that had been searched, etc. This applies even when the loaf is small enough for a mouse to eat, and even if one nullified the chametz. We do not rule leniently on the ground that a doubt concerning a rabbinic law is resolved leniently, because the possibility that the mouse ate it cannot displace the definite presumption of a prohibition. Since we know with certainty that the mouse dragged the chametz before us, a possibility cannot displace a certainty. Bach in this siman and in siman 439; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 1; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1; Birkei Yosef, subsection 1; Erech HaShulchan, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1.
