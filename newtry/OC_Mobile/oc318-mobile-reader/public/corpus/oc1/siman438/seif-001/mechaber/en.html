A mouse that entered a checked place with a loaf in its mouth. Contains 2 seifim.
If a mouse entered a checked house with a loaf in its mouth, and another entered after it and found crumbs — even enough for the whole loaf — he must return and check the house after the loaf the mouse brought in, because it is not a mouse's way to crumble, and these crumbs came from elsewhere and not from that loaf. But if a toddler entered a checked house with a loaf in his hand, and another entered after it and found crumbs — he need not return and check, for we presume he ate it and these crumbs fell at the time of eating, for a toddler's way is to crumble while eating. Some say: if the crumbs are not enough for the whole loaf, he must return and check.The laws of a mouse entering and leaving — the Rabbi omitted since they are uncommon.:
