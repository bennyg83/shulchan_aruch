(א) An olive's bulk of chametz — it implies; but less than an olive's bulk — he need not bring a ladder to lower it; nullification suffices (Kaf HaChayim); and some question this.

(ב) We obligate him — even if he knew before its prohibition time — then nullification helps that he not transgress bal yeira'eh; nevertheless the rabbis required him to lower and destroy lest it fall from the beam during Pesach and he come to eat; and all the more if he knew after its prohibition time and did not nullify before — then nullification no longer helps and he certainly must lower and destroy.

(ג) In a pit — even if it has no water; and if one's way is to use there all year — he is obligated to bring it up and destroy.

(ד) He nullifies in his heart, etc. — specifically where it fell there by itself, for we consider it chametz that fell on him from a collapse; but to hide with one's hands so it remain until after Pesach — forbidden; and even nullification does not help — he must remove and destroy on the 14th.

(ה) And that suffices — and if he knew after its prohibition time when nullification does not help — he must remove and destroy.
