A kezayis of chametz. This implies that for less than a kezayis one need not bring a ladder to remove it, and nullification suffices. Olas Shabbos writes likewise.
Hot water in a pit. Rokeach, siman 271, writes in the name of Tosefta that chametz that fell into a well is considered removed. One must distinguish between a well containing water and a dry pit. See below, siman 467, regarding using during Pesach a well into which chametz fell.
