(Seif kaf heh) The impurity etc. — and it appears to me they can resolve that regarding spikenard oil even if made to remove impurity one blesses on it — from that which is stated daf 43 one blesses on oil. For the rabbis taught: they brought before him oil and hadas — in the house of Shammai he blesses on both, and in the house of Hillel he blesses on the hadas and afterward blesses on the oil (and so we rule in siman 296 seif 1); and Rashi wrote: oil and hadas at the end of the meal — oil to anoint hands to remove impurity of the food and hadas to smell, end of his words. And it is difficult for Rashi — for to remove impurity one does not bless at all; therefore Rashi proved that spikenard oil for its excellence — even though it comes to remove impurity, nevertheless one blesses on it. And therefore one may say this oil and hadas deals with spikenard oil, as is clear from what Rashi wrote there on the second baraita: they brought before him oil and wine etc. and he returns and blesses on the oil — Rashi: Borei shemen arev, end. (And what Rashi explained there per Rabbenu Gershom who said I will decide to bless on the oil first because with oil we merited its smell and anointing, but with hadas we merited its smell but not its anointing — Rashi: we merited its smell and anointing such as anointing oil — therefore oil is preferable, end; behold he cited anointing oil and omitted spikenard of the two that stand — and one must say he cited anointing oil because it is more common that they anoint with it than with spikenard oil due to its cost and importance.) However requires study — for Tosafot there wrote etc., meaning Rashi's words are not clear; but Tosafot on daf 53 who also agreed with Rashi's explanation — for them, whence to distinguish that on spikenard oil even when made to remove impurity one blesses? For from daf 43 they have no proof, for there it is written: this oil is for smell, for if to remove impurity — we say in chapter Arvei Pesachim one does not bless on it, end.

And one must say it deals with oil of herbs of perfumes — for if of wood of perfumes, its blessing is atzei besamim, and then its blessing equals hadas; and why does it teach they bless on hadas and afterward bless on oil — one blessing suffices? Granted for Rashi's explanation it deals with spikenard oil — its blessing is not equal to hadas, for its blessing is Borei shemen arev; and although if so it would be fit to precede the oil blessing since spikenard's blessing is clear and specific — nevertheless, since it comes to remove impurity, that without spikenard's importance we would not bless on it at all — it is that due to its importance they bless on it with its blessing; nevertheless hadas that comes for smell — its blessing is more important to precede. But Tosafot who wrote the oil also comes for smell — impossible it deals with spikenard oil, for then it should be preceded; and although Magen Avraham wrote above siman 216 seif kaf tet that nevertheless hadas is preferable and more important — behold they left it requiring study because it is not visible to the eye; therefore it is more reasonable to say per Tosafot it deals with oil of herbs of perfumes, for then its blessing is not equal to hadas's blessing.
