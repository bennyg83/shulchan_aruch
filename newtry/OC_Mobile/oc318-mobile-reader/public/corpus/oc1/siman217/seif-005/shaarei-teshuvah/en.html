Of a forbidden woman. See Baer Heitev and Eliyah Rabbah, who writes that one should prohibit this even regarding an unmarried woman. Machazik Berachah writes likewise in the name of Mor U'Ketziah; see there. See also Birkei Yosef, who rules that even regarding the spices of one's wife while she is a niddah, it is forbidden. Although the Torah testified, “fenced in with roses,” one must nevertheless distance himself. The phrase itself provides a mnemonic: even roses require a fence.
