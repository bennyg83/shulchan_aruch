(5) There he does not bless. And that in siman 178 all agree that regarding things that do not require a concluding blessing, if he changed his place he must bless even if his intention was to return — one must say this case is different, for in the end he smells those perfumes he smelled before and therefore need not bless, for it is like leaving his meal with intention to return; not so in eating that he eats something else and therefore must bless. And therefore if when he returns he smells other perfumes he must bless. And further one may say that here he lacks no action but when he enters he smells of itself and therefore his intention helps.
