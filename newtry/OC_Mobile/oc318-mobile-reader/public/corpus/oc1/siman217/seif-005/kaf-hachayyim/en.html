5) There: He should not recite a blessing. In siman 178, all agree that regarding foods that do not require a concluding blessing in the place where they were eaten, if one changes location he must recite another blessing even if he intended to return. One must say that this case is different because when he returns he ultimately smells the same spices he smelled previously, and therefore he need not recite another blessing. It resembles one who leaves his dining group intending to return. Eating is different because he eats another item and must therefore recite another blessing. Accordingly, if upon returning he smells different spices, he must recite a blessing. Alternatively, here no action is lacking: when he enters, he smells automatically, and therefore his intention is effective. Magen Avraham, paragraph 3. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 1, answers that the case concerns one who went only to the shop entrance and can see his original place, which is effective as explained above in siman 178 and below in siman 273. He challenges Derishah, who writes that the case concerns one who left some members of the group behind, because that is ineffective here; see there.
