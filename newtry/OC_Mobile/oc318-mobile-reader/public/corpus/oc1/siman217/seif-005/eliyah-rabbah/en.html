[5] Which was not made, etc. It is uncertain what the law is if he also made it for fragrance. Perhaps it is comparable to taking fruit both to eat and to smell, in which case he must recite a blessing, as stated above in siman 216, seif 2. The Rosh in the chapter Eilu Devarim and Abudraham likewise appear to me to indicate that the two cases have the same law. See there and examine carefully. Regarding the esrog and bread mentioned above, it appears that the concept of having been made for fragrance does not apply to them.
