(Seif kaf vav) Lest he come etc. — for Rashba wondered etc. who wrote on Rambam that he is mistaken in this law and said: I do not know whence Rav has this, that I do not know a place for him; and perhaps of actual ervah where it is made to remove impurity; or (meaning even elsewhere on her neck and the like) that they are made for sin to cast poison of the yetzer, end; and therefore for the second reason because of yetzer hara it suffices for us to forbid actual ervah and not a single woman. And know that Taz wrote that even if she removed from her neck and it is placed elsewhere and one knows and recognizes her — forbidden to bless on them, for etc.; and further through this he may come to improper thoughts — implies blessing is forbidden even if he does not know and recognize her; and for this reason one should be careful not to smell tobacco from a vessel special to a woman who carries with her the tobacco herb; and likewise bundles of perfumes tied together special for smell — if special to a woman, its law is so.
