See Responsa Radbaz HaChadashos, siman 44, that one may smell blossoms of orlah trees even though they contain the beginning of fruit, but one may not smell the fruit once it has developed somewhat. Machazik Berachah writes in the name of Zera Yaakov that one may smell fruit belonging to hekdesh or prohibited by a konam, but should not recite a blessing. See Shach and Peri Chadash, Yoreh Deah 105, and see there in the name of Zera Yaakov. A rose or myrtle plucked on Yom Tov may be smelled without moving it. See Responsa Radbaz, part 3, siman 621, regarding smelling roses belonging to hekdesh, where he rules that it is prohibited; see there.
