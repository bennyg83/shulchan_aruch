(2) There he blesses Borei minei etc. And there is no obligation in this blessing except for one who enters and intends to smell; but if the smell comes of itself he is not obligated, for so is my view regarding one who does not intend, like a thing not made for smell. And therefore it is resolved that the seller of perfumes is not obligated to bless every time he deals with them but certainly is exempt since he did not intend to smell. Taz seif kaf alef. And it is implied from his words that we require two things: made for smell and also intended to smell; but from Bach's words it is implied that since it is made for smell, even if he does not intend to smell he is obligated to bless, see there. And likewise R' Akiva Eiger letter alef, Nahar Shalom letter alef, Mishnat Zahav letter alef; and therefore since there is a dispute in this one should intend in his mind that he wishes to smell and bless.
