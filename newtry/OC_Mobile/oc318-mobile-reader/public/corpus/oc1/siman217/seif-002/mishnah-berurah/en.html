(8) Placed below — meaning before his bed.

(9) For honor of the living — so they smell and enjoy.

(10) On perfumes of the Temple — that one brings istenis to the Temple to remove bad smell; if a person walks and smells from afar fragrance of these perfumes he need not bless, since the owners did not make them except to remove bad smell; and even if intending to smell he also does not bless.

(11) Made to remove etc. — such as a person whose body sweats much and must anoint his body with perfumed oils to remove sweat's impurity whose smell is bad; and likewise when eating and his hands are impure from touching foods and he anoints his hands with oil — he also does not bless, for here too the primary purpose is to remove impurity and not to smell; and there is no difference between plain oil and spikenard oil that is important; in every case one does not bless any blessing on them since they come only to remove impurity.
