And likewise — Taz and what he wrote that Tur in what Magen Avraham wrote did not distinguish so much; but Taz was not satisfied, for on daf 43 there he did not mention Rosh's filtering at all, and on the contrary brought Raavad that if he removed the wood in Mishna Berurah meaning he did not really filter and filtering was not mentioned at all — necessarily Tur and Magen Avraham did not distinguish meaning they did not explain here, and why here it was difficult for him on Rambam; and Taz and Rashi explained there it is not made for smell per Bach, and it is implied if one takes oil only to smell he blesses; and Perishah wrote in his name smell without substance and not made for smell is nullified — if so even when taking only to smell he does not bless; but the author left in doubt above and here agreed he does not bless — Taz and Magen Avraham siman 216 letter yud gimel. And what he wrote regarding some householders etc. is difficult to me — since he takes in hand and intends to smell, even though not made for smell he blesses as Taz wrote siman 297 letter zayin, see letter alef; and perfumed vessels smelling in them — thus Tur that they are made for smell; and even per Maharshal who wrote on filtered oil that it is nullified relative to oil — here one may say it is not nullified relative to the vessel; also Maharshal wrote plainly similarly; requires study. And regarding perfumes — what he wrote if he has a sealed vessel he smells to restore the soul without blessing; but filtered oil or perfumed vessels per Taz do not smell, lest he require a blessing and enjoy above the burden; not so Bach and Magen Avraham siman 216 letter yud gimel, see there.
