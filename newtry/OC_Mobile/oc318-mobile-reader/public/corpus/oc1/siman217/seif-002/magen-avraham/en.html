Of the Temple. That one brings istenis to the Temple to remove the smell.

The impurity. Such as to anoint hands with it after eating; and if it is spikenard oil he blesses Borei shemen arev [Rashi and Tosafot daf 53]; and it appears to me they derive this from that which is stated daf 43 one blesses on oil etc., as written siman 216 seif 1; however requires study — for Tosafot wrote there it deals with smell etc., and one must say it deals with oil of herbs of perfumes — for if their blessings are equal as in the Gemara, whence that on spikenard oil one blesses when made to remove impurity; and so appears the view of poskim who are silent.
