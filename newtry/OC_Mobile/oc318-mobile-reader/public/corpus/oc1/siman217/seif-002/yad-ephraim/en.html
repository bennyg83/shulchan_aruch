In Magen Avraham seif kaf heh — it deals with smell etc.; and one must say it deals with smell etc. as cited; and if so, whence do they etc. as cited; his intent is the law that one blesses on spikenard Borei shemen arev even when it comes to remove impurity — they derived from that one blesses on oil, for stam oil after eating is to remove impurity, and to remove impurity one does not bless; therefore one must say specifically plain oil, but on arev oil one blesses; and on this Magen Avraham wrote this matter is not compelled per what Tosafot wrote there that it deals when they brought for smell, and from this reason to remove impurity one does not bless; and per them therefore it does not please them to establish spikenard oil, for then their blessings would not be equal; but per them one must say oil of herbs of perfumes, as explained in Magen Avraham's words above siman 217 seif kaf tet per Rosh; and if so whence on oil etc. — see above siman 216 seif kaf tet at the end where he showed a place for this.
