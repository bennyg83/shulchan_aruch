On perfumes etc. — see in Mishna Berurah what I wrote if a person walks etc. And see in Peri Megadim who doubts perhaps the matter is similar to mugmar that has no substance when smelling from afar; and perhaps a partition interruption helps in his view — one cannot say so, and proof from that which we say one who walks outside the city and smells a fragrance — if the majority are Israel he blesses, see there; perhaps with one person the perfumes were in his house and his door was open; and also the Gemara does not distinguish whether he stands near the city or far.

Of the Temple — see in Mishna Berurah that even if he intends to smell he does not bless; and see in Peri Megadim who sides that even if he takes it in order to smell he also cannot bless; and see in Shaarei Tzion that we brought proof for this and we sweeten the explanation in this matter — that although regarding etrog it is also not for smell and nevertheless if he smells he blesses as in Rosh siman 2 there — since he takes it to smell it indeed stands for smell [except that when he does not take it to smell, presumably most of its use is for eating as evident in the baraita and on hadas and in Rashba's chiddushim there] — not so here, for although he takes it to smell and smells, nevertheless it does not stand even now for smell but to remove the foul odor.
