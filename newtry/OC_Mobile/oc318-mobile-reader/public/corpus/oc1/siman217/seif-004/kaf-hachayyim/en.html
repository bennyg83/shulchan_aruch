4) There: If, however, he intended to return, etc. This applies specifically if he intended to return immediately. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. See, however, Taz, siman 639, paragraph 20, who writes that whenever he intends to return and has not diverted his attention, even if he remained away a long time, he need not recite another blessing; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, however, writes in the name of Tashbetz and Birchas Avraham, folio 78, like Magen Avraham, that he must return immediately and must also not have diverted his attention; see there. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1, holds the same view.
