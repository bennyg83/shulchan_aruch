Of a forbidden woman, etc. Rambam, chapter 9, says fragrance belonging to a woman forbidden as one of the arayot. Semag and Semak say fragrance of forbidden women, implying even a woman who is not technically an ervah, perhaps even an unmarried woman. Abudarham brings proof from the incident at the end of chapter Ben Sorer UMoreh, which concerns unmarried women. It is difficult to distinguish. Magen Avraham permits fragrance of unmarried women and did not note this source.
Hanging from her neck, etc. It appears to me that even if she removed it and it is now lying separately, one may not smell it, because knowing and recognizing its owner can lead to improper thoughts, as Taz writes. Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, however, writes that if the spices give off fragrance without one bringing himself near the woman, he recites a blessing over the fragrance. This would imply all the more so where they have been removed and are lying separately. The matter requires study. See siman 336 seif katan 6.
