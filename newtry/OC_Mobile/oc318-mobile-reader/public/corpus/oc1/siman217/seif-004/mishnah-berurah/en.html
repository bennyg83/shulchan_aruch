(16) Of ervah — meaning of a woman who is ervah to him; but regarding a single woman it is possible it is permitted [Magen Avraham]; but many acharonim are stringent that even on a single woman it is forbidden lest he come to improper thoughts; and likewise even when she is not a niddah. And our virgins who are presumed niddot because they do not immerse for their niddah — forbidden to all, for they are in the category of forbidden relations. And Shaarei Teshuvah wrote in the name of Birkei Yosef that even on his wife's perfumes when she is a niddah it is forbidden to smell.

(17) Hanging on her neck — some acharonim wrote it is not specifically when hanging on her neck, but he cited the custom that regarding perfumes she is accustomed to carry on her neck — even if they are now placed on the table one also does not bless on them, lest he come to improper thoughts if he recognizes and knows her.
