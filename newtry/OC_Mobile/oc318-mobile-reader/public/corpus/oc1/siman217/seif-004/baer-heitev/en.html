Ervah. Specifically of an ervah; but of a single woman they bless. Shaarei Teshuvah.

On her neck. The same law if she removed it from there and it is placed — it is forbidden. Taz Shaarei Teshuvah. And in Shulchan Aruch HaGadol he wrote that even if the perfumes are on the woman, as long as he himself did not reach the woman and the perfumes gave off smell — he blesses on the smell. Shaarei Teshuvah.
