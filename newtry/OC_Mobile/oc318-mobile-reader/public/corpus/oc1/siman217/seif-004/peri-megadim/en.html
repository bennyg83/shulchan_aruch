Was walking — Taz strengthened what he wrote letter alef that we require he intend to smell, and automatically even if he smells he does not bless; and one may challenge: one walking outside the city who smells — he blesses, thus even without intent? He explained: necessarily on what he will still smell, for otherwise there is no blessing, see there (for smell already passed and is lesser than eating as written siman 216 alef); if so it deals when he intends afterward. And what he wrote proof from 'all the more' — one may say there it does not please him and it is distress; not so here; and Magen Avraham alef and siman 216 letter dalet and Bach hold whoever smells even without intent blesses.
