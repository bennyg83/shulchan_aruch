To a perfume shop. Made for smell that they smell and come to buy (Gemara) siman 216 seif 1; and if so, when placed in a room and not in the shop he does not bless even if his intention is to smell, since the perfumes were not made there for smell.

His intention to return. And it appears to me specifically when his intention was to return immediately, as written end of siman 239.

He does not bless. Requires study — for in siman 178 all agree that regarding things that do not require a concluding blessing, if he changed his place he must bless even if his intention was to return; and one must say this case is different, for in the end he smells those perfumes he smelled before and therefore need not bless, for it is like leaving his meal with intention to return as written end of siman 239; and in siman 218 seif 3 and siman 8 seif 14; not so in eating that he eats something else and therefore must bless; and therefore if when he returns he smells other perfumes he must bless. Further there is to resolve that here he lacks no action but when he enters he smells of itself and therefore his intention helps.
