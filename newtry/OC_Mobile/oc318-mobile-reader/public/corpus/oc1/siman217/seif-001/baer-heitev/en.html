Specifically if he intended to smell; but if he did not intend to smell he is exempt — and therefore it is resolved that the seller of perfumes is not obligated to bless every time he deals with them, but certainly he is exempt since he did not intend to smell and the smell comes of itself. Taz. If they are placed in a room and not in the shop he does not bless even if his intention is to smell, since the perfumes were not made there for smelling. Magen Avraham.
