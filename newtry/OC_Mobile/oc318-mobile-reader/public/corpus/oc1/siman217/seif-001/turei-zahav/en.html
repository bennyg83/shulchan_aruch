Enters and leaves etc. — siman 239 seif 8, what Magen Avraham wrote from this; and in the Gemara it challenges: but it is not made for smell, so why bless? And it answers: it is also made for smell so people smell and come and buy species. It appears from this there is no obligation in this blessing except for one who enters and intends to smell; but otherwise, only that smell comes of itself — he is not obligated, for so is my view regarding one who does not intend, like something not made for smell. And therefore it is resolved that the seller of perfumes is not obligated to bless every time he deals with them but certainly exempt since he did not intend to smell; and so in the Gemara one who entered and smelled fragrance etc.
