Enters — Taz in siman 239 letter kaf explains Magen Avraham's difficulties here letter gimel: his intent is that in siman 178 changing domain is like diversion of attention and departure; and here the language is departure — one who sits all day in the shop, for smell comes to him of itself (and although without intent he does not bless, nevertheless the language is departure in this); and anything lacking departure in one domain means walking from domain to domain does not interrupt his intention to return, and it is implied there that intention the same day helps; and Magen Avraham here requires specifically immediately — and so appears for practical law. And therefore if perfumes are placed on the table in his house and he took and smelled and blessed and replaced them — as long as he did not divert attention he does not bless when leaving, for it is a change of domain and he blesses; since he does not smell of itself but only when taking them to his hand, diversion of attention helps. Still requires study — for one may say anything requiring action to remove does not bless, also per Magen Avraham letter gimel for explanation alef, explained there. And Taz further wrote we require two things: perfumes made for smell there and he intends to smell — otherwise he does not bless even if he smells and enjoys. And one may challenge from siman 216 seif 1 — fruit he took and did not intend to smell he does not bless, for we require two things; but Bach here and Magen Avraham siman 216 letter dalet — fruits are not made for smell, this is made for smell even without intent, since he smells and enjoys he blesses; however anything not made for smell, even if intending to smell, as long as he does not take in his hand he does not bless, even per Bach and Magen Avraham; and difference from the challenge in Berakhot 53a on his shop that is not for smell — perhaps it deals with intent; when he takes in hand and intends to smell, even though not made for smell like fruits whose primary purpose is eating — since he takes in hand and intends to smell, now it is made for smell; and Taz siman 297 letter zayin on this (and there per his view here what he wrote that etrog is also made for smell; however regarding perfumes of the dead and Temple and the like, if he takes in hand and intends to smell them it appears he does not bless, for explicitly made only to remove impurity and not to smell in them, as Rashi there on perfumes one brings istenis to Temple etc. even though in hand etc. — unless from his shop we learn one law for them. And what Rashi wrote that one brings etc. to Temple — impossible to bless even if taking in hand, nevertheless this taking is not to smell but to carry and smell of itself etc. Eliyah Rabbah letter heh — one may say there it is made for both impurity and smell; requires study in all this.
