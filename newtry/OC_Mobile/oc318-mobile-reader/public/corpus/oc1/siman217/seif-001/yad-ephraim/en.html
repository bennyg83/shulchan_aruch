In Taz seif kaf bet — I saw some householders etc.; and in my humble opinion there is some support for Rambam's words from Shabbat daf 62b regarding a pouch and amulets that have no perfume, even though we rule one who removes food less than the requisite amount in a vessel is liable — here exempt, as Rav Ashi's resolution there, because it has nothing to feel at all; and although one may distinguish and say that is specifically regarding that the vessel is not subsidiary to smell — nevertheless if one holds one blesses on smell the vessel absorbed, behold that too is less than the requisite amount; and one may also say the reverse — if one holds one does not bless on smell, what is Rav Adda's view to compare it to less than requisite amount, see there; and I shortened — it appears the main point is in any event doubt in blessings is lenient; and Taz wrote well not to take a vessel that has only absorbed smell alone.
