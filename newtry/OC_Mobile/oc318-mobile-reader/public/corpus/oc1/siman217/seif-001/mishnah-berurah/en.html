(1) One who enters his shop etc. — and even though in seif 2 it is explained that we require the perfume be made for smell, and this one only gathered his merchandise in his shop to sell — in truth also here it is called made for smell, for he is pleased that people smell the perfume's fragrance and come to buy from him [Gemara]; and therefore if perfumes are placed in the perfumer's room and not in his shop [meaning they are not placed publicly where people constantly enter and leave] — there it is not made for smell; then even if the entrant intended to smell them he need not bless unless he took them in his hand to smell that taking makes it made for smell and he must bless.

(2) He blesses — some say specifically when intending to smell; but when he does not intend and the smell comes of itself and he enjoys — he does not bless; but many disagree and hold that even if he does not smell intentionally, since perfumes are made to be smelled in them and he enjoys — he blesses.

(3) Borei minei besamim — because in a perfume shop there are wood and herbs of perfumes, therefore he blesses minei besamim; and some disagree with this from above siman 296 seif 10 — that if trees and herbs were before him he blesses on each the appropriate blessing; there it deals when they bring before him two or three kinds and he takes in his hand from each kind separately to smell it, therefore he blesses each kind separately; not so here that he does not take any perfume in his hand but when he enters smell rises from several kinds of perfumes at once, therefore he blesses one inclusive blessing [acharonim].

(4) Except once — and it makes no difference whether he diverted attention or not, since smell rises to him constantly as long as he sits in the shop. And the shop owner himself too — the first time each day he enters his shop he must bless on it since he enjoys the smell and no more; and even entering and leaving — for his intention is to return to his shop, as below; and some acharonim hold that even the first time he enters his shop he need not bless; and therefore it is good he intend to smell and then all agree he must bless the first time.

(5) Enters and leaves etc. — all the more when he went to another perfume shop he certainly must bless there another time.

(6) His intention was to return — specifically when his intention was to return immediately. Bundles of perfumes placed on the table to smell — if he took them once to smell and blessed on them and returns and places them on the table, if his intention was to return and smell them he need not bless when he returns and smells them; and even if he left his house and his intention is to return immediately he need not bless on them since from the start his intention was to smell them every hour; but if he went to his business or to pray in synagogue — that is an interruption and he must bless again.

(7) He does not bless — and even when he returns and smells other perfumes he also need not bless.
