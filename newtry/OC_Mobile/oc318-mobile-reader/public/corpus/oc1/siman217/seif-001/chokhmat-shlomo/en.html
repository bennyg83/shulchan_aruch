Seif 7 — one who was walking outside the city and smelled a good fragrance — if the majority of the city are idolaters he does not bless etc. — behold Taz did not copy here the law of half and half — what is its law, whether he blesses; and see Tzelach and Sefer Ma'ayan HaBerakhot; and the view of Magen Avraham is that he blesses here as in siman 298; and see in my writings to the acharonim in 570 siman 17 third edition year 5599 in my responsum to the community of Dinkowitz there I drew from matter to matter and raised there that here one does not bless in half and half — Shaarei Teshuvah with his reason, also in explaining the sugya there; requires careful study.
