(1) [Seif 1] One who enters a perfume shop etc. — for smell too it is made so that people smell and come and buy from it. Berakhot 53a. And if so, when placed in a room and not in the shop he does not bless even if his intention is to smell, since the perfumes were not made there for smell. Magen Avraham seif kaf alef. And therefore the same for one who enters an orchard from which fragrance of perfumes emerges — one does not bless even if his intention is to smell, since it is not made there for smell. Nevertheless it appears to me better to take in his hand from the perfumes that are in the room or orchard at the beginning of his entry there and bless on them and intend to exempt the smell that rises of itself so as not to enjoy this world without a blessing.
