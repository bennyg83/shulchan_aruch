One who enters his shop etc. — see in Eliyah Rabbah that his view is that likewise apothecary; and in Kaf HaChayim in the margin he wrote it is astonishing regarding a house of medicines that a healthy person does not buy and a sick person without choice buys medicines — perhaps this reasoning does not apply, that they are pleased that people smell and come and buy from him, see there; and in truth his reasoning is not compelled, for likewise they are pleased that people smell from outside and know there is an apothecary; but on another side there is room to doubt, see below s.v. but smell etc.; and also combine with this the view of Taz where one does not intend to smell.
