(12) Mugmar — that they burn perfumes and pass vessels or garments over the perfume smoke and smell is absorbed in vessels or garments.
(13) One does not bless on it — whether the perfumer himself or another person who goes and smells.
(14) For it was not made etc. — however if he intends to perfume his vessels and also to smell he must bless on it, for it also stands for smell.
(15) But smell without substance etc. — and some say that they do not have perfumes to smell on Shabbat and they take from a mortar in which perfumes were ground and smell was absorbed and they smell and bless their blessing in vain; and as written here that it is smell without substance.
