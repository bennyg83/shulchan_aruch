(Seif kaf gimel) He does not bless etc. — for it is like one who leaves his meal etc., for when he returns he returns to his first meal.

Further one may say that here he lacks no action. And therefore through his intention to return it is not considered departure, since everything depends on his intention alone and he needs nothing else.

And therefore explain this — even if he smells other perfumes whose blessing equals the first perfumes, nevertheless he need not bless.
