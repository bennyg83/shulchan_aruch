But smell without substance — it appears to me that therefore also in a perfumery one does not bless unless the perfumes are exposed and give off smell; but if the perfumes are placed in flasks and they are sealed so that smell does not break forth from them but one smells in the house because they open them every hour and smell breaks forth and rises and eventually during the day the house fills with smell — in such a case one does not bless, for this is smell that has no substance, like here regarding smell absorbed in vessels. And greater than this Ra'ah wrote in his laws that also regarding mugmar itself if the mugmar is consumed in fire one no longer blesses; and although we wrote above to disagree with him, that is specifically regarding mugmar where the coals of perfume remain at least; but in our case where the flasks are sealed and no smell comes from them but only smell was absorbed at first in the air — it is reasonable that one does not bless on this.
