[3] His intention to return etc. And that above siman 8, one who spreads his tallit with intention to return must bless — Perishah resolves that perhaps that case is different, for the act he did in removing the tallit is considered departure; also one must distinguish between that and siman 178 regarding diversion of attention, see there. Magen Avraham wrote and it appears to me specifically when his intention was to return immediately, end. And I found in Sefer Birkat Avraham daf 78 this language: one may explain without delay and without diversion of attention in the interim, end of his words. And likewise I found explicitly in Tashbetz daf 29 this language: it appears his intention is not to return immediately is what is dealt with; but if his intention is to return immediately he did not divert his attention, end. Further he wrote in Sefer Birkat Avraham there that if he went to another perfume shop, even if he did not divert his attention he must bless another blessing; and see above siman 178.
