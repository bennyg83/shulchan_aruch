And if the majority — see in Mishna Berurah what I wrote that even half and half he also blesses; thus agreed Magen Giborim and as Ra'avad's view that we did not find anyone who disagrees with him. And in my humble opinion it is difficult — since it is half and half it is a Torah doubt, for smell of idolatry fully adheres to your hand from anything of the ban [as written in Rambam's Mishnah commentary] and we rule it strictly; if so, since it is forbidden to smell from doubt how can one bless on it — unless we say that the entire essence of smell is only d'rabbanan; and see Peri Megadim; requires further study.
