(Seif kaf zayin) He does not bless etc. — refers to seif 7 'one who was walking' etc. if majority are gentiles etc. — thus Terumat HaDeshen; and even though etc. in tractate Avodah Zarah; and Chokhmat Shlomo wrote one should not learn from this how when smell of wood of perfumes mixed with smell of herbs of perfumes that the minority is nullified and one blesses on the majority — for since it is fit to bless on the minority as on the majority it is not nullified (and therefore one may say Borei minei besamim); but here regarding that of a gentile it is not fit to bless — it is nullified; and in my humble opinion requires study in this.
