(22) If the majority, etc. We attribute the fragrance to the majority. It is therefore assumed that the non-Jews prepared it at their gathering, and he smells it from a distance. Magen Avraham.
(23) And if the majority are Jewish, etc. The same law applies when Jews and non-Jews are evenly divided, as explained below in siman 298 regarding the light used for Havdalah. See Biur Halachah.
(24) He recites a blessing. We wrote at the beginning of the siman that some authorities require a blessing only when one intends to smell, and otherwise no blessing is needed. Most poskim disagree and hold that he recites a blessing in all cases, since he nevertheless enjoys the fragrance that reaches him.
