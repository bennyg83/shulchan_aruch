(22) If the majority etc. — for we attribute smell to the majority; and certainly they are of idolaters who made them at a feast, and therefore one smells from afar [Magen Avraham].

(23) And if majority Israel etc. — and the same law for half and half Israel, as explained below siman 298 regarding light of havdalah; and see Biur Halacha.

(24) He blesses — we already wrote at the head of the siman letter yud alef that specifically when he intends to smell, and without that he need not bless; but most poskim disagree and hold he blesses in every case since in any event he enjoys the smell that rises.
