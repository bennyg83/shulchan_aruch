Was walking outside the city etc. It appears the blessing is certainly on what he will still smell willingly and enjoy; for on what he already smelled there is no blessing, for smell is like eating — as Tur wrote at the beginning of the siman, in eating one must bless specifically before eating; if so if he walks through that city and did not intend to enjoy the smell he need not bless, as written at the beginning of the siman regarding a shop of perfume; and proof further from chapter Kol Sha'ah (Pesachim daf 25) regarding benefit from prohibition that comes to a person unintentionally — there is no prohibition; all the more here one blesses only on a thing made for smell, as above.
