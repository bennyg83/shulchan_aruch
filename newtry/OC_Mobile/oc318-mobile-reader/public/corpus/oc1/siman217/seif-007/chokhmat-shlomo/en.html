Seif 7. If one was walking outside a city and smelled a pleasant fragrance, if most of the city consists of non-Jews he does not recite a blessing, etc. Note: The Tur and Shulchan Aruch did not state here what the law is when the population is evenly divided, whether one recites a blessing. See Tzelach and Maayan HaBerachos. Maadanei Melech holds that one does recite a blessing here, as in siman 298. See also my work on Even HaEzer, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 17, third edition, year 5599, in my responsum to the community of Dinkovitz. There I was led from one matter to another and concluded that here, when the population is evenly divided, one does not recite a blessing. See there for the reason and for my explanation of the sugya, and examine it carefully.
