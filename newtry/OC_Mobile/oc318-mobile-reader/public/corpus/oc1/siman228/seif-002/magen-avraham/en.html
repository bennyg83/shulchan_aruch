Like Chidekel and Peras. In Mordechai it is written: like Gichon and Pishon, Chidekel and Peras; and it requires further study — what is meant by 'like', for there are only these; and furthermore why not bless on other great rivers created on the six days of Creation; and also all poskim wrote generically 'rivers' — whence to distinguish; therefore it appears simple that their intent is as Bach wrote in the name of Radbaz and these are his words: specifically distinctive rivers such as Peras and Chidekel one blesses this blessing — until here; meaning on all rivers that are great like these one blesses; and so all poskim who wrote generically rivers — it implies all of them.

That was not changed — meaning in a place where they dug and changed their course one does not bless.
