(4) Like Chidekel and Peras — not specifically these alone; rather the same applies to all rivers that are as great as these four rivers, and that are known to be from the days of Creation like these and were not formed afterward — one blesses.
(5) In a place that was not changed etc. — meaning in a place where they dug and changed the course of the river to another path one does not bless from that place onward, for there it is not an act of Creation; and see in Abudraham who tends to say that if he is in doubt whether they were changed also — he does not bless.
(6) By man — and the same applies regarding seas: if people extended from one to another and made them one — one does not bless on that place; but regarding seas, in general we need not be concerned for this.
