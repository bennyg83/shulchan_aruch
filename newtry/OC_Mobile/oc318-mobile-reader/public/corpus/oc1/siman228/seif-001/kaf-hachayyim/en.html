1) [Seif 1] Over seas and rivers, etc. One must recite this blessing not only the first time, but every thirty days. Tur; Ateres Zekeinim; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. Regarding why the Shulchan Aruch does not write this, Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, explains that it relies upon siman 218 and the beginning of siman 224; see there. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 3, writes the same.
2) There: Over seas and rivers, etc. The term 'seas' means other seas that are not the ocean, or inlets separated from the ocean called the Great Sea. Olas Tamid there.
3) There: Who makes the work of Creation. This is a sign: since He established them from the beginning, it is praise of the Omnipresent when we now recognize something we know He created during the six days of Creation and which still exists. All the more so, its Maker continues to exist. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
