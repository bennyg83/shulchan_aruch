The Great Sea. And Lechem Chamudos proved that one does not bless on this except on Oceanus sea that surrounds the world; and what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of responsa of Rosh — see there — means that the questioner asked whether one blesses on the sea crossed to Eretz Yisrael; but Rosh's responsum was that one blesses only on Oceanus, and that is the one surrounding the world; and he brought proof for this, unlike Peri Megadim who did not [explain thus]; and so Raavad in commentary on Mishnayos; and so in responsa of Maharash Yonah; and in this vein he extended to defend Beit Yosef and did not succeed — see there; and in Sefer HaTishbi siman 242 he wrote that it is the sea surrounding the land of England as is found at the beginning of Sefer Yosippon.

Blessed are You Heaven our Heaven Who made the Great Sea. (Thus in Gemara and Rambam.)
