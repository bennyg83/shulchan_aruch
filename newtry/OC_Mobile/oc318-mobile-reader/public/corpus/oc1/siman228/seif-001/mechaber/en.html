Blessing on seas, rivers, mountains and hills. It contains 3 seifim: On seas, rivers,
mountains, hills and deserts one says Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who makes the work of Creation. And on the Great Sea — and that is the sea crossed to Eretz Yisrael and to Egypt — one says Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who makes the Great Sea.
