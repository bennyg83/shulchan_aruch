And on the Great Sea etc. — the reason is that because of its importance they established a separate blessing for it [Rashi]; and it appears to me that if he blessed on it Who makes the work of Creation he fulfills his obligation b'dieved, for it is similar to the blessing shehakol; and furthermore even regarding wine — if he blessed on it with shehakol, many early and later authorities hold that he fulfills b'dieved; for although l'chatchila because of its importance they established a separate blessing for it [as Rashi explained at the beginning of chapter 24 of Berachos], nevertheless regarding b'dieved it is not excluded from the category of other shehakol blessings; and as I noted in Shaarei Tzion on Rosh Chodesh note 67 — see there; and all the more so here where they are included among other acts of Creation.
