He says: Blessed are You, Hashem, etc. The meaning is that since Hashem established these features at creation, recognizing today something we know He created during the six days of creation and that still endures is praise of the Creator, all the more so that He Himself endures. Lechem Chamudot, page 71, wonders why the Levush and Shulchan Aruch omitted that one blesses only upon seeing them after thirty days. In my humble opinion there is no difficulty. Must they list every rule repeatedly? They already wrote it in siman 218 and at the beginning of siman 224. Rambam, chapter 10, repeats it here only because he did not state it in the earlier laws, following the order of the Gemara. Tosafot, the Rosh, and the poskim make clear that the same thirty-day rule applies here.
[Levush] And here there is no, etc. Rabbenu Yonah writes that because these things do not change but remain as they were from the creation of the world, one recites only Oseh Maaseh Bereishit.
And over the sea, etc. Rambam writes this in chapter 10. It is surprising that in his Commentary to the Mishnah he rules that the halachah does not follow Rabbi Yehudah. See Knesset HaGedolah.
