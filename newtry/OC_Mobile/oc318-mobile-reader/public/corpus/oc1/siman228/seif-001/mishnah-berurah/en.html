(1) Who makes the work of Creation — meaning: since He established them from then, the praise of the Omnipresent is when we recognize today something that we know the Omnipresent created from the six days of Creation and it still exists; and it is not appropriate to bless on them Whose strength and power fill the universe as on winds and thunder — for there they are seen and heard from afar; but seas and rivers each in its place.
(2) And it is the sea that is crossed etc. — and it is called the Great Sea because of the importance of Eretz Yisrael; but many later authorities disagree with the Mechaber and hold that specifically on Oceanus sea — which is the Great Sea among all seas that surrounds the entire world — they established a separate blessing because of its greatness; but on the sea crossed to Eretz Yisrael it is not called the Great Sea for this purpose, and one blesses on it like on other seas. And know that all these blessings are only when one sees them from thirty days to thirty days [later authorities].
(3) Who makes the Great Sea — thus is the language of Tur; but several poskim brought this blessing formula in past tense: Who made the Great Sea.
