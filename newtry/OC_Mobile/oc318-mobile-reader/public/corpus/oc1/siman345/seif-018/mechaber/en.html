The boundary of karmelit — that it not be less than four by four, and it extends only until ten; and above ten it is makom patur; and seas and rivers we measure from the water surface — therefore one who takes from them above the water surface until ten in the air is karmelit, and above ten in the air is makom patur.A pit standing in karmelit — even if it is a hundred amot deep it is karmelit unless it is four by four wide, for there is no makom patur in karmelit, as will be explained shortly:
