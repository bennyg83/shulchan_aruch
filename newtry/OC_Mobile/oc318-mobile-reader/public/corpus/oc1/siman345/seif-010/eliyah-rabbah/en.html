[16] And many people shoulder burdens there, etc. The Beit Yosef, Raavad, and Magen Avraham imply that if people do not actually shoulder burdens there, it is not a public domain even if suitable for that use. This is incorrect, because the Beit Yosef concludes in the name of the Maggid Mishneh that the Rashba says one should take the Rambam's view into account. I likewise saw in Avodat HaKodesh, page 9, that he concludes one may not carry from it into the public domain or even into a karmelit, nor from them onto it. Shiltei Giborim, page 99, also holds this way.
[17] And there is one who says, etc. It appears to me that the primary ruling follows the Rambam, the first opinion, as I saw in Avodat HaKodesh there, the Ran, and Kol Bo, unlike the Bach at the end of siman 352.
[18] It is a private domain, etc. This applies even if it is full of water or other transparent material through which the walls can still be seen. If it is filled with fruit, however, even if one intends to remove the fruit, it is not a private domain because the walls are not visible.
