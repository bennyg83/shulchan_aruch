(א) Even thorns, etc. — less than three we hold does not divide domain for itself and is nullified to reshut ha-rabbim:
(ב) And if three high, etc. — then public cannot tread from height removed from reshut ha-rabbim category; not reshut ha-yachid until ten high; Sages gave name karmelit — domain for itself forbidden hotza'ah to reshut ha-yachid and reshut ha-rabbim:
(ג) And if not four by four — even if length more than four tefachim we do not combine to width — not domain for itself unless four length and four width:
(ד) Nine tefachim — at this measure people in reshut ha-rabbim rearrange loads — not too high not too low; below nine not fit to rearrange — not reshut ha-rabbim even if public rearranges (Rashba):
(ה) Exactly — but more than nine not reshut ha-rabbim even if four by four:
(ו) And public rearranges — but when not rearranging, even fit to rearrange, not reshut ha-rabbim even if four wide:
(ז) Even if not four wide — since public custom to rearrange even below four — makes it reshut ha-rabbim:
(ח) The same for nine until ten, etc. — Tur's view — Gemara "post nine" comes only to exclude below nine not reshut ha-rabbim; but above nine likewise reshut ha-rabbim when public rearranges:
(ט) It is reshut ha-rabbim — for halacha we hold first view — many poskim disagree:
(י) If four wide — four by four:
(כ) To hollow to complete — such as post adjacent to wall — nevertheless no hollow to complete — makom patur — permitted hotza'ah from it to reshut ha-yachid and reshut ha-rabbim:
