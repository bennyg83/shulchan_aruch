[10] Up to the sky, etc. If one plants a pole, even one hundred amot high, its top is a private domain.
[11] And even a vessel, etc. This means even a pillar in the public domain. Likewise, a pillar in the public domain ten handbreadths high and four wide is a private domain extending upward to the sky even though it has no visible walls above, as stated in Avodat HaKodesh. Even if one places one or more pegs on top, whether three handbreadths high or less, it remains a private domain. So rules the Bach, and the authorities cited by the Maggid Mishneh agree, unlike Olat Shabbos at the end of this siman. A vessel less than ten handbreadths high has the status of the public domain, because a vessel does not create a karmelit unless attached to the ground, as stated by Tosafot on page 11.
