Place enclosed by partitions, etc. — implies from poskim when there are two partitions and lechi it is reshus hayachid from Torah as Eruvin 12a lechi is because of partition; Rosh chapter 1 Sukkah likewise. Four posts at corners like passei bira'os (each ten high, two amot this side and two that, not more than thirteen and a third between) — reshus hayachid from Torah Eruvin 80a. Mound sloping ten within four amot is reshus hayachid; if ten within five amot walking is comfortable and reshus harabim if standing there. Reed ten in reshus harabim with tray four wide — we do not say gud achit (Tosafos Shabbos 99, Machatzit HaShekel). Tray itself ten and four is reshus hayachid even beneath reshus harabim.
