(א) A place enclosed by partitions. In siman 363 it will be explained how many partitions are required there:

(ב) And it has four tefachim, etc. Implies partition thickness is measured with the hollow — and so Rosh explicitly in the first chapter of Shabbat regarding a beehive that the walls combine with the hollow of the beehive for four width, etc.; and so Tosafos in the first chapter daf 8 unlike R' Meir who explained four within the beehive — rather the thickness also combines as in a ditch-chain where it combines to the hollow of the ditch since it is fit to place something upon it, etc.; and they conclude as Rashi explained; and what Rashi wrote Beit Yosef brought: and likewise a fence — we say from four sides gud asik the partition faces to its top and its head is enclosed from four sides and its hollow is four — end of his words; do not err — specifically in the hollow there must be four tefachim without the partitions; therefore Rashi wrote "partition faces" to indicate the outer edge rises and gud asik partitions — the partition thickness is included in the hollow between this outer edge and the opposite edge; this is proven — if you wish to explain Rashi's words on the hollow itself, the explanation would accord with R' Chiya and Tosafos conclude Rashi accords with their explanation; and Rambam wrote in chapter 14 "enclosed by four high partitions and between them four" — his intent is also with wall thickness; and "between them" means between all as above; however a question — from what is written in chapter HaZorek (Shabbos daf 99): R' Yehudah asked — a wall in reshut ha-rabbim ten high and not four wide enclosing karmelit and he made it reshut ha-yachid and threw and it rested on top — what is the law? Do we say since it is not four wide it is makom patur, or since he made it reshut ha-yachid it is as one who filled it? Rashi explained: and its head is like adjacent ground — the whole hollow; Ulla said a fortiori for others he makes a partition — for himself all the more so; Rashi: the wall head is before us — since he made it reshut ha-yachid we say it is as one who filled — reshut ha-yachid rises until the firmament; unlike here where without thickness it is not reshut ha-yachid it does not rise until the firmament and is not like adjacent ground; and likewise in what he concluded "for others he makes reshut ha-yachid" — this is not clear here and requires study:
