Partitions. See seif 19 that wrote there must be between the partitions four — unlike Olat Shabbat; implies from poskim' words that when there are two partitions and a lechi it is reshut ha-yachid d'oraisa as in Eruvin daf 12 lechi is because of partition, and so Rosh first chapter of Sukkah; and see siman 363 seif 11; and Rambam's view that three partitions are karmelit — see there; and see siman 354 in the gloss and siman 356; and if one makes four posts at the four corners like pasei bira'os it is reshut ha-yachid d'oraisa as in Eruvin daf 20; see siman 394 seif 7 and siman 363; a mound that slopes and ten accumulate within four amot of walking is reshut ha-yachid, and if ten accumulate within five amot its use is comfortable and it is reshut ha-rabbim if one stands there — see siman 399 seif 4 as written; if one planted a reed ten high in reshut ha-rabbim and on its head a tray four wide we do not say gud achit partition for it is a partition goats breach; but the tray itself ten and four — also beneath it is reshut ha-yachid (Tosafos); a post ten and four without four in its body — even without goat-breach beneath is not reshut ha-yachid only on top (Tosafos Shabbat daf 99) — see siman 266:
