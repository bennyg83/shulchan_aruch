What is reshut ha-yachid? A place enclosed by partitions ten tefachim high and having four tefachim by four tefachim or more; and likewise a ditch ten deep and four by four wide; and likewise a mound ten high and four by four wide.Some say that in these we require four by four including their diagonal, as will be explained below siman 349 (Hagahot Ashiri first chapter of Shabbat and Tosafos Eruvin daf 51).
