2) There: There are four domains regarding Shabbos, etc. Why does the Mishnah not list five domains? A karpef larger than beis sasayim that was not enclosed for residence is a full private domain, so one who throws from a public domain into it or the reverse is liable, yet one may carry within it only four amos. One can answer that it is a full private domain, and only regarding the restriction against carrying more than four amos did the Sages treat it like a karmelis. Since the Mishnah already lists a private domain and karmelis, it need not list this case. For the same reason, it does not list a courtyard whose residents did not establish an eruv. Tosafos, Shabbos 6a.
