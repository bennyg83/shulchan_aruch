(א) Enclosed by partitions — in siman 363 it will be explained how many partitions are required and several more details on this matter:

(ב) And it has four, etc. — from seif 19 it is proven we require between partitions a hollow four by four, for partition thickness does not combine — view of most poskim; and some Rishonim wrote partition thickness combines to this measure; and in Be'er HaGolah brought in Rashba's name (and likewise in Milchamot) to distinguish: thick partitions fit to place a board above and use there — thickness combines with hollow for this measure since fit to use there; if not, does not combine; and see Acharonim who agreed all this regarding calling reshut ha-yachid on partition thickness from above — but regarding hollow within partitions, all agree not called reshut ha-yachid unless hollow has four by four:

(ג) And likewise a ditch, etc. and likewise a mound, etc. — and the same for a post ten tefachim high and four by four; and all this even standing in reshut ha-rabbim as below seif 10:

(ד) Ten high, etc. — but if the mound or partitions are less than ten tefachim, even if very wide, it is not reshut ha-yachid; and the mound need not stand upright in height — even if sloped, if ten tefachim accumulate in height within four amot of slope it is as if entirely upright and is reshut ha-yachid at its height (Gemara daf 100):

(ה) Including their diagonal as will be explained, etc. — meaning as there regarding carrying four amot in reshut ha-rabbim all agree one is not liable until carrying measure of four amot with diagonal totaling five amot and three-fifths of an amah — so here for reshut ha-yachid not called reshut ha-yachid until width measure of four tefachim with diagonal totaling five tefachim and three-fifths of a tefach; and Levush omitted "some say" from his work; and see Be'er HaGolah who wrote he did well to omit for it is only an individual view and all Rishonim disagree and hold if only four by four square it counts reshut ha-yachid and does not compare to below siman 349 regarding carrying four amot in reshut ha-rabbim — see there; and Gra rejected this "some say" from halacha and wrote it also does not compare to siman 349 — see there:
