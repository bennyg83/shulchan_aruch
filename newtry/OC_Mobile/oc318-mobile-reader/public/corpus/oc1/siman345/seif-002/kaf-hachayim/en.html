B) There. Four domains for Shabbat, etc. — why not teach five domains, for karpef more than beit se'ah not enclosed for dwelling — one who throws from reshus harabim into it or vice versa is liable and one may not move therein except four amot? One can say that case is complete except for moving only four amot they made it like karmelit, and already taught reshus hayachid and karmelit — therefore also did not teach a courtyard without eruv.
