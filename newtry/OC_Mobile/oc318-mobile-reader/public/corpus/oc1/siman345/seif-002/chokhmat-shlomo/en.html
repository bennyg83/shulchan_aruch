Seif 2. And it has four tefachim by four tefachim. In my humble opinion see Taz what he discusses whether partitions are included in the four — and I already wrote proof for his words from "between them" — they themselves are also included, from what we say "ten days between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur," etc. However what Taz challenged from the Talmud appears to resolve well; and the question is — this is similar to what we say in Gittin that the bill of divorce and the courtyard come together and we do not say the reverse — if she is not divorced the courtyard is also not hers — rather we follow the main element and both take effect; so too here we say they made reshut ha-yachid through them and it combines automatically; accordingly one can say since in bill and courtyard they come together it is a dispute and R' Eliezer holds we do not say bill and courtyard come together — one can say Ulla holds like R' Eliezer and likewise here we do not say reshut ha-yachid below and above come together; therefore he said the reason "for others he makes — for himself all the more so"; but for us who hold bill and courtyard come together, likewise here reshut ha-yachid below and above come together. Or one can say Ulla also holds so — only per the first view that we say even though below is reshut ha-yachid above is another domain and reshut ha-yachid above is not applicable — then certainly a fortiori if there were not four tefachim between them the walls would not combine to make them reshut ha-yachid; but since Ulla concludes since for others he makes for himself all the more so — there is applicability of reshut ha-yachid above even though not equal to the ground below — automatically even though there is not four between them the walls combine to four and reshut ha-yachid below and above come together; and per the Talmud's conclusion Rashi explained well — examine this and it is correct:
