(א) (s.k. 2) Holes and even, etc. — deals with covered holes open to reshut ha-rabbim side but pass through also inward to reshut ha-yachid; explained Shabbat 7b Abaye and Rava dispute — all hold holes of reshut ha-yachid like reshut ha-yachid; in holes of reshut ha-rabbim Abaye like reshut ha-rabbim and Rava not like reshut ha-rabbim; Gemara challenges Abaye from mishnah one who throws four amot in reshut ha-rabbim and rests on wall — sides above ten like throwing in air which is makom patur as below seif 12 — exempt below ten like throwing to ground and liable; we ask why like ground — did it not rest; Rashi: when it returned it fell within four — perforce leaned on wall bouncing to another — end; R' Yehudah in a leaf eight taught remainder stuck to wall side; R' Yehudah locates with pebble or object resting in hole and answers mishnah in wall without hole — from beginning above like air and if there were a hole it rested in hole; R' Chayim: threw above ten and rested in any hole — dispute R' Meir and Rabbanan; R' Meir for mezuzah if opening not four wide and side wall can be hollowed to four — we say hollow to complete and as hollowed is considered and liable for mezuzah; here too hollow and considered resting on four place and Rabbanan no hollow for mezuzah likewise no hollow for Shabbat and not resting on four place — exempt; stam mishnah R' Meir (Rashi); if so above ten liable for resting on four place ten high is reshut ha-yachid — throwing from reshut ha-rabbim to reshut ha-yachid; or deals where no hole and Tosafos challenged we say there for mezuzah R' Meir only hollows if feet three high four wide — otherwise no hollow; here in hole nowhere four wide; R' Yehudah deals with holes normally made at construction passing through:

(ב) And on reshut ha-yachid side four wide but not on reshut ha-rabbim side — all hold reshut ha-yachid for holes of reshut ha-yachid; dispute only whether we hollow — four place or not; and wrote further specifically holes above ten are holes of reshut ha-yachid but below ten residents of reshut ha-yachid do not use because residents of reshut ha-rabbim use there — not holes of reshut ha-yachid; Olat Shabbat wrote holes below ten if open through do not have holes of reshut ha-yachid law; Magen Avraham disagrees — we find no posek dividing holes of reshut ha-yachid thus; Tosafos too from where derive new law from logic without necessity — perforce Tosafos did not write thus except per the challenge then necessary; but per conclusion all agree even below ten holes of reshut ha-yachid law; Tosafos' challenge per view maybe hole — establishes without hole all the more no four anywhere even R' Meir no hollow; answer stam holes are thus (and second answer wrote necessary); if so why need prove mishnah deals without hole — otherwise challenge from beginning should prove from mishnah itself whether hole; perforce holes of reshut ha-yachid and reshut ha-yachid law for all — why teach throwing four amot liable — even less than four amot liable for throwing reshut ha-rabbim to reshut ha-yachid; perforce below ten not called holes of reshut ha-yachid since reshut ha-rabbim residents use there — therefore needed to teach specifically throwing four amot and cannot infer from end — needed to infer from beginning; one can say law not clear to him below not holes of reshut ha-yachid but prefers inferring from beginning since from end ba'al din could reject saying below ten not holes of reshut ha-yachid — therefore better infer from beginning with no rejection; but truly even below ten holes of reshut ha-yachid — we find nowhere in Talmud or poskim dividing above or below ten:
