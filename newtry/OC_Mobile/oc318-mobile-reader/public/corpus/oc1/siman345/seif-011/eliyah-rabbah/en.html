(11) And even a vessel, etc. — meaning even a post in reshus harabim; and a post in reshus harabim ten high and four wide is like reshus hayachid and rises until the firmament even without recognizable partitions above (Avodat HaKodesh); even if he planted on its head a peg or pegs whether three high or not — always reshus hayachid; Bach ruled so and poskim agreed in Maggid there, unlike Olat Shabbat end of this siman; a vessel not ten high is like reshus harabim for there is no karmelit in vessels unless attached to ground (Tosafos 11a):
