Reshut ha-yachid. Even if it is full of water; and the same for things a person looks into and sees within and the partitions are visible; but full of fruit is not reshut ha-yachid for the partitions are not recognizable even if he intends to remove them; and Rashba wrote specifically when full of tevel that one cannot move — nevertheless; and see siman 372 seif 16; and see siman 356 that specifically a pit is reshut ha-yachid but the sea is karmelit as written in seif 14:
