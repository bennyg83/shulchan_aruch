(א) Not three deep — even if many tefachim wide:
(ב) Until ten — even if public uses that pit — use by constraint not called use (Gemara):
(ג) Ten deep — even full of water or things one looks into and sees partitions within; but full of fruit not reshut ha-yachid — partitions not recognizable even if intends to remove — and one who throws into from reshut ha-rabbim exempt (nevertheless prohibition since fruit movable on Shabbat — not fully nullified); Rashba specifically tevel or thing forbidden to move nullifies partitions:
(ד) Must be four wide — if not makom patur even twenty amot deep (Acharonim):
