A) [Seif 1] Four domains for Shabbat, etc. — hotza'ah and hachnasah from reshus to reshus (meaning from reshus harabim to reshus hayachid or from reshus hayachid to reshus harabim) is a melacha of the avot. And although this matter with all bodies of Torah was stated orally to Moses at Sinai, Scripture says "man and woman shall do no more melacha for the sacred contribution" and the people ceased bringing — we learn bringing is called melacha. Likewise one who carries in reshus harabim from the start of four to the end of four is as one who carries from reshus to reshus and is liable. Rambam chapter 12 law 8; distinction which is d'oraisa and which d'rabbanan Shulchan Aruch wrote later siman 346 — see there; the reason in sod: one who carries from reshus hayachid to reshus harabim draws holy sparks already clarified to kelipot which are reshus harabim, and one who brings from reshus harabim to reshus hayachid draws the Shechinah on Shabbat to kelipot to clarify and elevate — great blemish as explained in Orach Chayim 11 end ch. 7 — see there:
