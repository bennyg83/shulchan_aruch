(א) (s.k. 1) Partitions. See seif 19, etc. — unlike Olat Shabbat who inferred from Rambam's wording "and between them four by four" that the hollow must be four by four; and Rabbenu Yosef Karo wrote in Shulchan Aruch Tur's wording "and it has four by four" — implies he holds like poskim who disagree with Rambam that partition thickness combines to width and length of four even if the hollow alone is not four; therefore Magen Avraham wrote perforce this is not so — behold the Mechaber himself in seif 19 wrote makom patur is called partitions higher than three between which there is not four — implies it is not called reshut ha-yachid until there is four between them — namely per Olat Shabbat the hollow alone must be four; and although Taz wrote from "between them" there is no decisive inference — one can say "between them" means including partitions; nevertheless per Olat Shabbat who infers from Rambam's "between them" that Rambam holds partitions do not combine — he properly refutes Magen Avraham from seif 19:

(ב) From poskim' words it implies he holds three partitions suffice from the Torah for d'oraisa reshut ha-yachid:

(ג) And one who throws into it from reshut ha-rabbim is liable; but nevertheless forbidden to move within d'rabbanan. So Rosh first chapter of Sukkah seif 9: Rabba said schach on an alley with a lechi on one side — the sukkah is valid, for since this partition helps for Shabbat it helps for sukkah; Rosh wrote perforce it deals with an open alley — two partitions opposite each other and open on both sides opposite each other except a lechi on one side; for in a closed alley from three sides with lechi on the fourth — why need the reason "since it is a partition for Shabbat" — behold in sukkah two partitions like a gamma and a third even a tefach suffices; rather than deals with open alley and holds lechi is because of partition and from the Torah one who throws from reshut ha-rabbim is liable — therefore it helps for sukkah too; and although in general Shabbat forbidden to move in an alley — nevertheless for sukkah mitzvah they permitted moving as they permitted for pasei bira'os — end Rosh's words; Rosh ruled so and as below siman 394 he ruled open alley with lechi is d'oraisa reshut ha-yachid:

(ד) See siman 363 seif 11 — meaning even though in the Gemara there is a dispute whether lechi is because of partition or recognition and not a partition; nevertheless below siman 363 it is explained we hold like those who say lechi is because of partition (see there in Magen Avraham s.k. 8):

(ה) And Rambam's view that three is karmelit — meaning from the Torah it is karmelit not reshut ha-yachid; but d'rabbanan also poskim agree it has karmelit law and forbidden to move within; but the word karmelit he wrote is not precise — from the Torah there is no karmelit, only three domains — reshut ha-rabbim, reshut ha-yachid, or makom patur — rather meaning from the Torah it is not reshut ha-yachid; nevertheless d'rabbanan it is also not called reshut ha-rabbim from the Torah:

(ו) And see seif 14 in the gloss Rama wrote: karmelit such as alleys with three partitions without lechi or korah in the fourth — end of his words; but this wording is unclear — per whom; in any case whether per poskim or Rambam with three partitions forbidden to move until fixing the fourth wind with lechi or korah d'rabbanan; their dispute is only regarding Torah law whether three partitions is reshut ha-yachid; but d'rabbanan certainly karmelit law applies:

(ז) Like pasei bira'os — four posts at four corners *thus, each post extending each direction one amah and between posts air not more than thirteen amot and a third; the pasei bira'os permitter is for festival pilgrims to water their animals from the well which is reshut ha-yachid and above outside the well is reshut ha-rabbim — impossible to make a partition at every well — they permitted via posts as above with space between posts thirteen amot and a third so two teams of four cattle can enter — one enters one exits — comfortable use like reshut ha-rabbim; each head of cattle is an amah and two-thirds as in chapter Osin Pasin; and they said Eruvin 20b perforce such is d'oraisa reshut ha-yachid — otherwise how did the Sages permit for pilgrims — perforce only d'rabbanan prohibition; therefore Magen Avraham wrote pasei bira'os is d'oraisa reshut ha-yachid even where there is no well; see siman 394 why schach on pasei bira'os is valid — see there:

(ח) A mound that accumulates — Gemara Shabbat 100a — is reshut ha-yachid for its use is not comfortable since standing upright it is hard to walk bottom to top; therefore if above it is four by four it is reshut ha-yachid above as Shulchan Aruch wrote a mound ten high and four wide is reshut ha-yachid even for moving there — we say gud asik partitions:

(ט) Planted a reed, etc. — goats breach it — dispute of R' Yosi b'R' Yehudah and Rabbanan: R' Yosi b'R' Yehudah also in such a case we say gud achit partitions; Rabbanan since goats breach we do not say gud achit — we hold like Rabbanan Shabbat 101a; but tray (basket) — also beneath it is reshut ha-yachid — perforce scribal error here; Tosafos Shabbat 99a s.v. "two projections," etc. — and likewise wagons of the Tabernacle were reshut ha-yachid even though beneath them reshut ha-rabbim since from the wagons until above the boards more than ten — end Tosafos — even though wagons ten high above, beneath is reshut ha-rabbim as Gemara there; and although Tosafos 101a s.v. "and threw and rested," etc. on what we say Rabbanan agree with R' Yosi b'R' Yehudah here regarding tray that above is reshut ha-yachid and bring proof from deep high, etc. without four in body is reshut ha-yachid as Magen Avraham wrote immediately after and refuted — a post is different for no goat-breach; but in tray there is goat-breach — Rabbanan hold we do not say gud achit and likewise fish breach in water is not called breach and does not nullify partition; Tosafos challenged per the view Rabbanan agree — then challenge from mishnah Eruvin 87 projection above water — one may not draw from it on Shabbat unless he made a partition ten high (explained below siman 355) — without ten suffices like tray; answer one answer specifically on top is reshut ha-yachid per R' Yosi b'R' Yehudah but below beneath tray not reshut ha-yachid unless tray has ten partition — end Tosafos — appears like Magen Avraham's words; but this is not so — perforce Tosafos wrote thus only for R' Yosi b'R' Yehudah and per the view Rabbanan agree and we are not concerned for goat-breach; but per conclusion we hold like them where goat-breach nullifies partition even tray ten high — nevertheless below not reshut ha-yachid; water is different for no goat-breach as Tosafos wrote — perforce scribal error and should read; but the tray itself ten and four wide is reshut ha-yachid even though beneath is reshut ha-rabbim — these are Tosafos Shabbat 99 as above:
