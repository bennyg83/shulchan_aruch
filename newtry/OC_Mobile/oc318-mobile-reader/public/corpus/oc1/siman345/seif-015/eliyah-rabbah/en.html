(15) If their length, etc. — this is Beit Yosef's language from Maggid: shortened alleyways open to reshus harabim — if length open to width of reshus harabim it is not reshus harabim — until here; it appears he means if width is sixteen only shortened it is reshus harabim even if length is not along reshus harabim; but in alleyways not sixteen throughout there is a distinction — requires study on Shulchan Aruch too:
