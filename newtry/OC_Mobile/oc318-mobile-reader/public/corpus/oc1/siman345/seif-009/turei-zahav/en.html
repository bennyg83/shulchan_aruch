Thirteen amot and a third. Even though reshut ha-rabbim width is sixteen amot as between the wagons in the desert, nevertheless here since both ends are to reshut ha-rabbim this is also reshut ha-rabbim; and the reason for this measure is as we find in pasei bira'os — the space between each post was this measure, namely so two teams of four cattle can pass when they meet — one enters and one exits — then its use there is comfortable like reshut ha-rabbim; and every head of cattle is an amah and two-thirds; as explained in chapter Osin Pasin:
