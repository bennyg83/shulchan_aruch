[15] If their length, etc. The Beit Yosef cites the Maggid Mishneh that passages which narrow in places are still a public domain. Therefore alleyways open into a public domain whose length runs along the length of the public domain are themselves a public domain even if they are not sixteen amot wide. But if their length opens across the width of the public domain, they are not a public domain. It appears to me that he means that if they are generally sixteen wide but narrow in places, they are a public domain even when their length does not run along the public domain. Only alleyways that nowhere have a width of sixteen require the distinction concerning their direction. This requires study, including in the Shulchan Aruch.
