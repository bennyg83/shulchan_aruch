(8) Holes, etc. — and even below ten; what Tosafos wrote below ten applies to reshus harabim is per their approach above unlike Olat Shabbat (Magen Avraham); and I found in Avodat HaKodesh in Rashba's name: even below ten and not four by four wide — until here.
