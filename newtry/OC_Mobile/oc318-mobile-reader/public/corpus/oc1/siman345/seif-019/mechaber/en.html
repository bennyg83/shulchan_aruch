Makom patur is a place that does not have four' by four and is from three high and above until the firmament; or a ditch that' does not have four by four and is more than three deep; and likewise partitions higher than three and above between which there is not four by four.And all this is specifically when standing in reshut ha-rabbim; but in karmelit we say "the kind found its kind and was aroused" and its law is like karmelit (Ran chapter Keitzad Mishtatfin and Hagahot Mordechai chapter 15 of Shabbat and Tur and Beit Yosef in the name of Rambam); and there are those who disagree and say there is no distinction between reshut ha-rabbim and karmelit (Rashi and Maggid chapter 14 in the name of Rashba); but if standing in reshut ha-yachid, all agree its law is like reshut ha-yachid (Beit Yosef):.
