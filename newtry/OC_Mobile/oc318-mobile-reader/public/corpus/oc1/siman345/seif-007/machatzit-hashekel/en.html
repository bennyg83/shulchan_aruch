(s.k. 7) And some say, etc. And so Shulchan Aruch siman 303 seif 18 — meaning certainly Rabbenu Yosef Karo holds primarily like first view to be stringent as general tradition — wherever Shulchan Aruch wrote first view stam and second as "some say" the first view is primary for Rabbenu Yosef Karo; but siman 303 explains in Rabbenu Yosef Karo himself that custom is like "some say" — behold he wrote regarding our custom women go out on Shabbat with jewelry the Sages forbade — no one protests — better they err, etc.; one can justify they hold like those who forbade only reshut ha-rabbim but to unjoined courtyard permitted; since our reshut ha-rabbim is karmelit law like unjoined courtyard and permitted — behold he wrote first reason better they err, etc.; and further wrote reason for that permission implies for this reason they act according to law — perforce custom is like "some say" here that we have no reshut ha-rabbim requiring 60 myriads though for Rabbenu Yosef Karo primary is first view — custom is not so; and custom equates karmelit to unjoined courtyard as poskim who hold so — see siman 334 on this:
