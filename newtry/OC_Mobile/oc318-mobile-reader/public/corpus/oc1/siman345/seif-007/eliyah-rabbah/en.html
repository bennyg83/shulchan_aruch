(7) And likewise a mound, etc. — a mound that slopes and ten handbreadths accumulate within four amot of walking is reshus hayachid; if ten accumulate within five amot it is reshus harabim. If one planted a reed ten high in reshus harabim and on its head a tray four wide we do not say gud achit partition for it is a partition goats breach; but if the tray itself is ten high and four wide, also what is beneath it is reshus hayachid (Magen Avraham), and this is Avodat HaKodesh 9a: if its short side is three wide it is judged as reshus hayachid for whatever its short side is thick in this we say gud achit partitions — until here — and Maggid chapter 14 likewise:
