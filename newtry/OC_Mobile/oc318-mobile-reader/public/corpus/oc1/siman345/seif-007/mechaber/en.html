What is reshut ha-rabbim? Streets and markets that are sixteen amot wide and are not roofed and do not have a wall — and even if they have a wall, if they are open from gate to gate (and their doors are not locked at night) (Tur) — it is reshut ha-rabbim; and some say that whenever there are not 600,000 passing through it each day it is not reshut ha-rabbim: ( ) הוי רשות הרבים ויש אומרים שכל שאין ששים רבוא עוברים בו בכל יום אינו רשות הרבים:
