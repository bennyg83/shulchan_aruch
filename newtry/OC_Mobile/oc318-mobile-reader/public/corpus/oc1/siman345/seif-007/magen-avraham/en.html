(א) Streets. Roads passing from city to city are reshut ha-rabbim:

(ב) Open from gate. Meaning the gates face each other and that road aligned from gate to gate has all the law of reshut ha-rabbim; and one should examine whether there is a distinction between a city of an individual and of the public (Rivash part 3 siman 74) — and see siman 362:
