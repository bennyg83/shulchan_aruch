(א) Streets — and roads passing city to city are also reshut ha-rabbim since many frequent them:

(ב) Sixteen amot wide — we derive from banners of the desert — place of wagon passage was sixteen amot; if less than this measure not reshut ha-rabbim from Torah but karmelit:

(ג) And not roofed — banners of desert were not roofed:

(ד) Open from gate to gate — gates face each other and that road aligned gate to gate has all reshut ha-rabbim law (Magen Avraham); see Biur Halacha:

(ה) And their doors, etc. — and if one door locked at night see below siman 364 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 5:

(ו) Locked — from this wording implies if not actually locked, even if fit to lock, not nullified — like first view below siman 364 seif 2 — see there:

(ז) And some say whenever not 60 myriads, etc. — they hold we also require similarity to banners of desert which had 60 myriads; even though mixed multitude women and children were there we count only explicit in Scripture; for halacha from Mechaber writing this view only as "some say" implies for halacha he does not hold so; nevertheless we cannot protest those relying on this view — not individual view as written in Biur Halacha; several Acharonim sided; every person of spirit should be stringent for himself; see siman 364 Mishna Berurah s.k. 7 and Biur Halacha here and there:

(ח) Each day — I searched all Rishonim on this view — this condition not mentioned — only that 60 myriads frequent there; see what we wrote Biur Halacha in name of Ramban and Ritva:
