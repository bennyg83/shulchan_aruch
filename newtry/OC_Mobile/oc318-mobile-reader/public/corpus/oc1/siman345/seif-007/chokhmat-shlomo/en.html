There, seif 7: What is reshut harabbim? Streets and marketplaces sixteen amot wide, etc. NB: Whether we require precisely sixteen amot, or even close to sixteen amot, such as more than fifteen, is also considered reshut harabbim, is not clarified in the poskim. But one who examines the Rashbam in Bava Batra, chapter 5, fol. 83b, s.v. ba'i R. Elazar, "and if you say that reshut harabbim does not interrupt, such as from four amot until sixteen, but not including sixteen, what is the law, does it interrupt," and so too Tosafot there, s.v. reshut harabbim mahu shetifsok, wrote that it is not speaking of a width of sixteen even though its ordinary measure is such, will see that they hold that even if it is not a full sixteen it is still reshut harabbim, and we do not require complete exactness. This requires study for now.
