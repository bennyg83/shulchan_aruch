(א) And their doors are not locked. It appears that if one door is locked it also nullifies reshut ha-rabbim since it is not open:

(ב) And some say that whenever there are not 60 myriads, etc. Beit Yosef wrote the latter view is Rashi and Tosafos and Rosh and Tur and Semag and Semak and Sefer HaTerumah (and Mordechai chapter Kol Kitvei) and Rokeach and the last Mahari Weil in a responsum; and the first view is Rambam and Ramban and Rashbam and Maggid Mishneh and Ran and Rivash; and I wonder — here Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Maggid Mishneh and Rambam and Rashba that 60 myriads passing are not required, but above siman 334 Tur wrote to challenge Sefer HaTerumah from this reason that there is no d'oraisa prohibition for us since we have no reshut ha-rabbim, etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote this challenge Maggid Mishneh brought in the name of Rashba — if so it implies he holds there was no reshut ha-rabbim until 60 myriads; and Rashal in chapter Ein Tzrichim seif 18 and likewise Mahar R' Binyamin in responsum siman 92 that one should be stringent like the first view; and Rama wrote in siman 346 that all our domains are karmelit — implies like the latter view; and among the public it is customary now that we have no reshut ha-rabbim, which is like the view of "some say" and they are many — especially since in the view of Maggid Mishneh and Rashba we also find the reverse as written — therefore one who is stringent should be stringent for himself and cannot protest what is practiced now like those many who are lenient as above:
