(4) Four wide, etc. — I do not know why Levush omitted what Rama wrote that some say we require four by four with length and diagonal like siman 349 (Malbushei Yom Tov). In my humble opinion Levush did well to omit it, for it is clear from Rashi's explanation in Eruvin 51a that specifically in siman 349 regarding carrying four amot in reshus harabim where corners cannot be placed we require length and diagonal, but here four by four suffices; and so Avodat HaKodesh 14b and Rabbeinu Yerucham 71a and Maggid chapter 14 halacha 6 and Rambam chapter 12 and Tur and the consensus of poskim imply:
