In Magen Avraham s.k. 13 "the ceiling" — meaning what Rosh wrote outer partitions — when they are ten high with the ceiling, then outwardly they appear ten high and inwardly since the hollow of the partitions is not ten high it is not reshut ha-yachid; and we follow the outer side that is ten high outward including the ceiling since in any case the domain within is preserved by the partitions and they are ten high outward — if so if not ten high with the ceiling, even though through the hollow there is ten high it does not help since far from the partitions three tefachim; but in my humble opinion this does not appear so — Tosafos Shabbat daf 7b s.v. "and if" wrote in the name of Rivba that sukkah is different for we require adjacent to schach, etc.; and in the margin there Tosafos Yeshanim wrote and further one can say it is different here for there are partitions high outward — see there; and see Tosafos Sukkah daf 4 that they chose the first answer — requires study:
