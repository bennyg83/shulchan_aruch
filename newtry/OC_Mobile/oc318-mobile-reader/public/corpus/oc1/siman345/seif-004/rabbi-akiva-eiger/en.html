Seif 4. That face reshut ha-yachid are reshut ha-yachid. A tree ten high and four wide with a basket hanging on it — relative to the tree the basket is holes of reshut ha-yachid; even at the basket lip deeper than three it is reshut ha-yachid; and even though goats breach beneath the basket — in holes of reshut ha-yachid there is no concern if goats breach — Gemara Eruvin 33 and Tosafos there:
