4) [Seif 2] What is a private domain? A place enclosed by partitions, etc. This means four partitions, or three partitions with a sidepost or crossbeam on the fourth side. It is then a complete private domain within which carrying is permitted even rabbinically. If it has only three partitions and no sidepost or crossbeam on the fourth, Rambam, chapter 14, halachah 4, considers it a karmelis. Other authorities consider it a private domain, and one who throws from a public domain into it is liable, as explained in Beis Yosef; see there.
