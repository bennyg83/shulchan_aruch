Seif 4. And if they are. He learned this from the words of the Maggid Mishneh in the name of Rashba chapter 14 halacha 10; and this is the language of Rashba in Sefer HaEretz 10:2: What are chorei reshus hayachid? A hole in walls etc. — they had holes open outward and people do not pass toward etc.; and Beit Yosef brought it. But Rashba deals only above ten [tefachim] and the hole is not four [tefachim] wide — for even so it is reshus hayachid on account of chorei reshus hayachid; and even for the Rabbanan, for we rule like Rav Chisda regarding reshus hayachid, one does not need placement of four [tefachim]; but below ten it is chorei reshus harabim, as Tosafos wrote 7b s.v. "and the halacha" etc.; and what Magen Avraham wrote here is hard to fit; and further proof, for we say in Eruvin 98b "and one may use all" etc.; and see Tosafos there s.v. "and one may use all" etc.; and the aforementioned Tosafos s.v. "and the halacha."
