(א) That face reshut ha-yachid — meaning even if also facing reshut ha-rabbim, since the hole passes through and enters also toward reshut ha-yachid their law is like reshut ha-yachid; perforce forbidden to take anything resting on the hole to reshut ha-rabbim or adjacent karmelit; and if one brings from reshut ha-rabbim into it one is liable; Tosafos' view liability only if hole four by four; Rashba and Meiri hold in holes of reshut ha-yachid need not be four wide — they have reshut ha-yachid law in all respects:
(ב) They are reshut ha-yachid — from Mechaber's conclusion implies even if holes below ten, since open inward they are like reshut ha-yachid; and so Rama's wording if not passing inward judged by height and width — implies if holes pass inward we do not look at height and width — always like reshut ha-yachid; so Magen Avraham and Turei Zahav; Be'er HaGolah wrote this is a dispute among Rishonim — some hold below ten residents of reshut ha-yachid do not use because reshut ha-rabbim residents use there — therefore like holes of reshut ha-rabbim explained below seif 12; Gra likewise in his Biur this siman and siman 353 — see there:
(ג) According to height and width — if ten tefachim high and four wide it is reshut ha-yachid; if not ten high, if four wide it is karmelit, and if not makom patur (and although Rama's law explained explicitly in seif 13 — cited here to explain even holes of reshut ha-rabbim when open to reshut ha-yachid are like holes of reshut ha-yachid):
