14) [Seif 3] Their upper surfaces are a private domain, etc. Since through their enclosure they render the enclosed ground a private domain when it was previously a karmelis, it follows all the more that the partitions themselves are a private domain. Taz, paragraph 3. This is from the Gemara.
15) There: Their upper surfaces are a private domain, etc. From here one may learn that carrying is permitted upon a city wall. This also follows from the Gemara, Pesachim 85b, which states that windows and the thickness of the wall are considered inside. Kisei Eliyahu, paragraph 2.
