And karmelit — Yerushalmi beginning Shabbos Rabbi Chiya taught: Carmel soft and ground — not moist and not dry but intermediate; so karmelit is neither like reshus hayachid nor reshus harabim. Tosafos there. Rashi explained (Shabbos 3b) karmelit from "forest and Carmel" (Isaiah 16) — not constant public passage nor reshus hayachid use.
