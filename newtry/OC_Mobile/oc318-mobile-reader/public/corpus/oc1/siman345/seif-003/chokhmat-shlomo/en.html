In my humble opinion it appears as a proof for this from what is written in Berakhot daf 58a that Ulla said there is no population in Bavel — we learn there is no population in less than 60 myriads. One can infer — what does Ulla teach us? Either way if you see 60 myriads there what does his rule help since we see 60 myriads; and if we do not know there are 60 myriads without doubt we do not bless for doubt in blessings is lenient; and also why the Talmud adjoins to Ulla's words that braita "there is no population in less than 60 myriads" — therefore it appears this matters for reshut ha-rabbim — if it were reshut ha-rabbim it would be d'oraisa prohibition and there would be doubt d'oraisa and stringency — therefore he teaches there is no population in Bavel and no Torah doubt; but in Eretz Yisrael there is Torah doubt lest there are 60 myriads; and Rambam who holds there is reshut ha-rabbim without 60 myriads therefore the Talmud explains otherwise — see there; and the inclination favors Rashi's view and the sugya will be explained elsewhere as above — examine this:
