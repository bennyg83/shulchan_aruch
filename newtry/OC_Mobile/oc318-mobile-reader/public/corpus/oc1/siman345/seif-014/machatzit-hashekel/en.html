(א) (s.k. 14) From water, etc. And if you ask behold water does not nullify partition and sea banks certainly ten high:

(ב) Ten accumulating within four amot not reshut ha-yachid — not called partition for use comfortable and easy to ascend mound and bank of water curve; now filled with water use not comfortable; since bank itself comfortable use only another thing — water causes uncomfortable use — therefore neither reshut ha-yachid nor reshut ha-rabbim but intermediate — karmelit:

(ג) And if there is a pit. Meaning pit has banks accumulating ten within four amot — all the more straight partitions — those pits full reshut ha-yachid law for water does not nullify partition:

(ד) And it appears to me even, etc. d'rabbanan — meaning in pits in sea where reshut ha-yachid law applies — nevertheless stam those pits large more than beit se'ah — d'oraisa reshut ha-yachid and throwing from reshut ha-rabbim liable; nevertheless d'rabbanan forbidden to move from reshut ha-yachid; and in them more than four amot like karpef and mound not enclosed for dwelling more than beit se'ah:
