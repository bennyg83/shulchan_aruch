(s.k. 12) Before, etc. even not thirteen and a third, etc. Shabbat 7a Tosafos s.v. "but between pillars" reshut ha-rabbim even if not sixteen amot between pillar and pillar since reshut ha-rabbim residents break through and outside pillars sixteen amot wide — end; Olat Shabbat inferred since Tosafos wrote not sixteen between them and concluded reason outside pillars sixteen — intent should be open alley law since outside between pillars is reshut ha-rabbim sixteen wide — then like open alley to reshut ha-rabbim not reshut ha-rabbim law except width thirteen and a third as seif 9 — likewise here between pillars; Magen Avraham disagrees — not comparable to open alley — Rashba chiddushim wrote: but before pillars like reshut ha-rabbim even if not sixteen wide between pillars, etc. — here unreasonable pillar diminishes domain measure — if so even planting reed in reshut ha-rabbim diminishes — end; wrote reason insignificant thing does not diminish sixteen measure — then not subject of open alley for they themselves are sixteen wide since pillars do not diminish sixteen unlike alley:
