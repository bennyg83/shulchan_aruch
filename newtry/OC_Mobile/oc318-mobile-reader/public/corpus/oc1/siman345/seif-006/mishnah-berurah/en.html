(ב) Box, etc. or beehive — cited two types of vessels square or round:

(ג) If it has in its quarter — perforce in round beehive not reshut ha-yachid until hollow holds five tefachim and three-fifths tefach — then hollow quarter is four by four:

(ד) It is reshut ha-yachid — within hollow and vessel wall from above; and likewise seif 363; vessel not ten high standing in reshut ha-rabbim has no karmelit law — no karmelit in vessels — like reshut ha-rabbim nullified to domain it stands in (Magen Avraham); therefore standing in karmelit karmelit law from domain it stands in (Turei Zahav; Beit Meir per Magen Avraham); vessel attached to ground called karmelit even in reshut ha-rabbim:
