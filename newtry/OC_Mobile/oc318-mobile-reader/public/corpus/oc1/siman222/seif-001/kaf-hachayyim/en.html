1) [Seif 1] Over reports, etc. This applies specifically if he heard the report from a trustworthy person who saw the event. Sefer Chassidim, siman 841; Mateh Moshe, siman 369; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Even if he heard it from a trustworthy person, such as Reuven who was robbed and heard that the robber was caught and that everything would be returned, he should not recite the blessing until it is actually returned to him. Sefer Chassidim there; Mateh Moshe there; Olas Tamid at the end of siman 221; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1.
