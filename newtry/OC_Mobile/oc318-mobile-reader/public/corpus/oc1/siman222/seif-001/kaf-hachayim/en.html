(א) [Seif 1] On reports, etc. — and specifically that he heard from a trustworthy person who saw the matter. Shulchan Chayim siman 494. Mateh Moshe siman 369. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. And even if he heard from a trustworthy person — such as Reuven from whom they robbed and he heard the robber was caught and will return everything — he should not bless until it is returned to him. Shulchan Chayim there, Mateh Moshe there. Or Torah siman 221. Eliyah Rabbah ot 1.
