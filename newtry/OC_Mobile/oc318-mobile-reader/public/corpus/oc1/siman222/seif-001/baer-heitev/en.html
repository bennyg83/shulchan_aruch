Reports. He used the plural "reports" to inform us that if many came to him at once, one blessing suffices. Sefer Leket part 1 siman 199. If he heard a report that requires blessing haTov vehaMetiv or shehecheyanu or Dayan HaEmet, and the informant is not trustworthy, or he did not see the matter — he does not bless; and likewise if he heard the robber was caught he does not bless until the robbery is returned to him — Shulchan Chayim, Mateh Moshe. One who was forced due to poverty to take a rich wife against his will blesses haTov vehaMetiv and Dayan HaEmet — Shulchan Chayim, Magen Avraham.
