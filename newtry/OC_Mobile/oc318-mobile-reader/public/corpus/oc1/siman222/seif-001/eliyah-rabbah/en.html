[א] [Levush] A person is obligated, etc. — this is not the reason for blessing on bad reports — why specifically on evils one sees; but I found in R' David Abudraham a reason for good reports: since it is said "You will make me hear joy and gladness, bones You crushed will exult," and the verse praises it — we have to give thanks for them there; and for bad reports, since it is said "and a bad report dries the bones" and the verse condemns it — we have to justify the judgment on it. He wrote in Sefer Chasidim siman 382: if due to poverty he was forced to take a rich wife against his will he blesses haTov vehaMetiv and baruch Dayan HaEmet. He wrote in Mateh Moshe: in all reports, if the informant is not trustworthy or the informant did not see the matter — he does not bless at all; and likewise Reuven from whom they robbed and he heard from a trustworthy person the robber was caught and will return everything — he should not bless until the robbery is returned to him; and the incident of Avraham's servant — he relied on Abraham's prophecy. R' David Abudraham wrote: on grass in standing grain and female among sons he does not bless Dayan HaEmet, for the wheat did not turn to grass and the son did not turn to female — for if the Creator did not wish to give him all wheat and all males, he does not bless on what the Creator did not give him, but only on what He gave him and it spoiled or was lost.
