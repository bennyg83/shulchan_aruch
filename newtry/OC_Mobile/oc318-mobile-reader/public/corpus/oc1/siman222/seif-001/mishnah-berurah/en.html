(א) On, etc. — and specifically when he heard from a trustworthy person and that person saw it himself; but if he heard a report [whether a good report requiring haTov vehaMetiv or shehecheyanu, or a bad report requiring Dayan HaEmet] and the informant is not trustworthy or the informant did not see it himself — he does not bless; and likewise if he heard the robber who robbed him was caught — he does not bless until the robbery is returned. One who was forced due to poverty to take a rich wife against his will blesses haTov vehaMetiv and Dayan HaEmet [Magen Avraham in name of Shulchan Chayim]; and it seems that today they are sparing with these blessings [Peri Megadim]; also in Sefer Mor VeKetziah he questions this.
(ב) Reports — he used plural language to teach that if he heard several reports at once, one blessing suffices for him.
(ג) He blesses shehecheyanu — meaning when he has joy from the report; and likewise the reverse for Dayan HaEmet when he has pain from the report.
