He blesses on the good haTov vehaMetiv even though he fears lest evil come from it — for example he found a lost object and fears lest it become known to the king and the king take all he has; and likewise he blesses on the bad baruch Dayan HaEmet even though good will come from it — for example a flood came on his field, even though when the flood passes it is good for him that it watered his field.
