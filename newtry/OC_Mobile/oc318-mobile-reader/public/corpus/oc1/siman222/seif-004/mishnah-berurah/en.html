(א) (ה) Even though he fears, etc. — for we do not look to the future that it may not be so [Rambam in explanation of the Mishnah].
(ב) (ו) For example he found a lost object — and fears they will accuse him and take what is his; and it deals with one who has household members for whom there is good in this too, and the blessing haTov vehaMetiv applies; for otherwise he would bless only shehecheyanu.
