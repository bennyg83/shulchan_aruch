(ב) One who was forced due to poverty to take a rich wife against his will blesses haTov vehaMetiv and Dayan HaEmet. Shulchan Chayim siman 382. Magen Avraham there. Eliyah Rabbah there. However see Rabbi Akiva Eiger ot 1 who wrote that today they are sparing with these blessings as written siman 223 in the gloss seif 1 — see there; and therefore it appears one should bless without Heaven's name and kingship.
