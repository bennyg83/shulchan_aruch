(א) On bad reports — whether they are bad for him alone or also for others:

(ב) Dayan HaEmet — if they told him much grass was found in his standing grain, or his wife gave birth to a female and his desire was only for a male son — it is not relevant to bless Dayan HaEmet on this even if he has pain from it, for this blessing was instituted only on something given him at first and afterward spoiled or lost; unlike here, the wheat did not turn to grass nor the son to a daughter, but from the outset he was not given a son [and likewise with wheat — from the outset he was not given all wheat], and one does not bless Dayan HaEmet on what was not given him [Avudraham in name of Abudraham]. If it became known to him that his wine soured he blesses Dayan HaEmet [Tosafot Yom Tov in name of Yerushalmi]; and it is obvious that the same applies if his property burned or his animal died, and everything of the kind that people are accustomed to grieve over.
