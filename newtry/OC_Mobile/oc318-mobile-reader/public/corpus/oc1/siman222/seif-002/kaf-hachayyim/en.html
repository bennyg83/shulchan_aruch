2) One who was compelled by poverty to marry a wealthy woman against his desire recites HaTov VeHaMeitiv and Dayan HaEmes. Sefer Chassidim, siman 382; Magen Avraham there; Eliyah Rabbah there. See, however, Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1, who writes that nowadays these blessings are recited less frequently, as stated in the gloss to siman 223, seif 1; see there. Therefore, it appears that he should recite them without the Name and kingship.
