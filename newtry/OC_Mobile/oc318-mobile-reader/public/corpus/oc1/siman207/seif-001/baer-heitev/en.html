Chay ha'olamim. The text of the blessing: "Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who creates many souls and their needs, for everything that You created to sustain in them the soul of every living being — blessed is the Life of the worlds." "Chay" is pointed with tzeirei because it is connected [to "ha'olamim"], and so wrote Tosafot at the end of Tamid. (And in Eliyahu Rabbah he testified that he heard that he retracted, and so he wrote in siman (57) [54].)
