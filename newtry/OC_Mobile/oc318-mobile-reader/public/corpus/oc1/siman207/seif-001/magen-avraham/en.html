The text of the blessing: "Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who creates many souls and their needs, for everything that You created to sustain in them the soul of every living being — blessed is the Life of the worlds." And so in the codices to say "shebarata" in the second person. "Chay ha'olamim" — the chet is pointed with tzeirei because it is connected, and so in Tosafot at the end of Tamid. One blessing. The same law applies if he ate and drank.
