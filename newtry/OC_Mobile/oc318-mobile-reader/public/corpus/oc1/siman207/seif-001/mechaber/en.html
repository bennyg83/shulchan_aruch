<b>The law of the after-blessing on fruits. And it contains one seif:</b>
(1) Tree fruits except the five species, and all ground fruits and vegetables (2) and everything whose growth is not from the earth — their after-blessing (3) is Borei Nefashot Rabot. And if he ate from all these species, from these he blesses (4) after all of them one blessing (5). And this blessing he concludes without the Name — that he conclude thus: Baruch chay ha'olamim.
