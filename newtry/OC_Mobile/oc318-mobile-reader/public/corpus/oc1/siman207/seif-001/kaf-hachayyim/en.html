1) [Seif 1] Tree fruits other than the five, etc., require Borei Nefashos, etc. If one ate apples or similar fruits and afterward recited Al HaEtz, after the fact he fulfilled his obligation. Proof may be brought from the ruling below in siman 208, seif 13, that if one ate fruit of the seven species and apples, he need not recite Borei Nefashos, because the apples too are included in the phrase, 'the fruit of the tree'; see there. Birkei Yosef in Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 1, and in Machazik Berachah, paragraph 1; Maamar Mordechai, siman 208, paragraph 28; Shaarei Teshuvah in this siman, paragraph 1.
