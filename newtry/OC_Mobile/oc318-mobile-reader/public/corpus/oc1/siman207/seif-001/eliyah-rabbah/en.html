[Levush] For everything, etc. The wording is "that You created," addressing Hashem directly, and not "that He created," as stated in the writings and by Magen Avraham. Beit Yosef cites the same from Talmidei Rabbenu Yonah, and so write Kol Bo, Tzeidah LaDerech, and Raavan, siman 190. Regarding the Levush's wording "for everything," Maharshal and Bach agree that the word "for" should be without an initial vav. Maadanei Melech, page 46, however, cites Tosafot as requiring "and for everything" with a vav, and says that this appears correct and is also the wording of the Tur and Talmidei Rabbenu Yonah. In my humble opinion, he was not precise, for in the text of the blessing they themselves copy "for" without a vav. Their later phrase "and for" is merely their own prose, meaning "and this phrase, for everything." I found this explicit in Kol Bo, page 19, citing Tosafot and Rabbenu Yonah, and Bach explains Tosafot the same way. This is the primary ruling. See siman 210 seif 2. Lechem Chamudot and Malbushei Yom Tov write that one should pronounce "Chai ha'olamim" with a tzerei under the chet. I already wrote in siman 57 that I heard he later retracted this.
