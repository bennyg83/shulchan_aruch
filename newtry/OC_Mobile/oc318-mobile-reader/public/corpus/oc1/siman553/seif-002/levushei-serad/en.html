(Magen Avraham, s.k. 1) Siman 608; see there, se'if 3: that whoever accepted upon himself the fast of Yom Kippur, even though he did not say 'today,' is still forbidden, because a portion of this day until evening is also accounted as acceptance of a fast for part of the day, even though he ate at the beginning, as written in siman 502, se'if 18.
