(s.k. 2) It is not, etc. And therefore it is good to stipulate; and that is, by mouth or in the heart. And the reason one must stipulate, and it does not suffice merely that he does not accept the fast upon himself, is because the Magen Avraham wrote below in siman 608, s.k. 4, in the name of the Maharil and Maharshal and Bach, as follows: the people have already adopted the custom to accept the fast with their interruption [of eating], and it is as if they accepted it by mouth, etc.; and he concluded, and one should not be lenient.
He must stipulate to the contrary, that he does not accept it by means of the interruption [of the meal].
