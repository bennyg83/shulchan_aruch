On Shabbos. That is to say, erev Tisha B'Av. If so, all the more so if Tisha B'Av itself falls on Shabbos, that it is forbidden to learn the whole day. And the Taz wrote: and if there is no prohibition of eating meat and wine, why should we be stringent not to learn? And furthermore I find it puzzling, for they read [the Torah] at Minchah, and even on Tisha B'Av things that belong to the day, such as the portion of the Tamid and the Mishnayos of the korbanos; so why should the Mishnayos of Pirkei Avos be different? And I found that the Maharshal learned after midday on every erev Tisha B'Av and permitted others to learn; and it appears that one who learns on Shabbos, even after midday, has not lost his reward, end quote of the Taz. And the Knesses HaGedolah permits learning on erev Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos, and forbids it on Tisha B'Av itself that falls on Shabbos; see there. And the Magen Avraham wrote: it appears that one should be stringent in both, for one can learn the things that are permitted on Tisha B'Av; see there.
