(There, in Magen Avraham at the end of s.k. 7) In Megillas Eichah. Note: see Responsa Chatam Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 156 [there he was asked whether it is proper to assemble public congregations on Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos and is postponed, in the manner of Israel every Shabbos, or not. And after he wrote to explain the words of the midrash that Rebbi read Megillas Eichah on Tisha B'Av that fell on Shabbos, etc., he concluded as follows: and according to this, one should permit at least before midday to speak in public, such as the haftarah and the like, and so is the practice in every place I have seen, end quote. And the reason for the custom not to learn on erev Tisha B'Av after midday he wrote there, because whatever one learns from midday onward, his thought is still upon it and he contemplates it at night, and he enters mourning while he is joyful. And if so, on Shabbos after midday too it is forbidden, for it is not because of mourning of that day itself, but because of the night that follows it; and for this reason it applies on Shabbos as well].
