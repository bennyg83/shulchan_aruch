(2) Ibid. Not to eat any more today. This implies that if he said "I am fasting tomorrow," he is permitted to eat today. But from the Tur and the authorities it is implied that as soon as he accepted the fast he is forbidden to eat any more. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. The Ma'amar Mordechai os 1 wrote that this is also the view of the Shulchan Aruch. So too wrote Mishna Berurah os 1. This applies specifically when he has already eaten the seudah ha'mafsekes, and therefore we say that his intention was to accept the fast. But if he has not yet eaten and intends to eat the seudah ha'mafsekes and he said this, he is permitted to eat, for in the laws of vows we follow the intent of the one who vowed, as stated in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 218, see there. Accordingly, what he said is merely a statement of fact.
