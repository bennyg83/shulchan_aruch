It falls on Shabbos, etc. If so, all the more so if Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos, that it is forbidden to learn the whole day, for by law it is forbidden to learn on Tisha B'Av; and furthermore, private matters apply, as written in siman 554, se'if 19. And in the responsa of Maharam of Lublin he wrote: if Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos, it is permitted to learn, for marital relations themselves are an excessive stringency, and we should not add to it, end quote; and this requires further study, that he did not cite the words of the Rama. However, in the responsa of the Maharil he wrote as follows: Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos, it is forbidden to learn after midday as on a weekday, end quote; implying that before midday it is permitted. And it appears to me the reason is that since it was postponed, it is postponed, and it has only the law of erev Tisha B'Av, not the law of Tisha B'Av; and nevertheless regarding marital relations we are stringent. But in Darkei Moshe he cited in the responsa of the Maharil erev Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos, and so it is in the indices of the responsa of the Maharil; and if so there is no proof from there. And in Kenesses HaGedolah he permits learning on erev Tisha B'Av that falls on Shabbos, and forbids it on Tisha B'Av itself that falls on Shabbos. And it appears to me to be stringent in both, for one can learn the things that are permitted on Tisha B'Av; and so we find in the Gemara that Rebbi read Megillas Eichah on Tisha B'Av that fell on Shabbos; see what I wrote in siman 554, se'if 4.
