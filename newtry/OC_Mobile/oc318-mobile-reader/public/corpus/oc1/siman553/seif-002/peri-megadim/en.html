And they adopted the custom. See the Taz, and see Magen Avraham letter zayin. And the view of the Rama may be explained: that Pirkei Avos is not like the order of the day said all year, for Pirkei Avos is said only in the summer, words of mussar, and there are places that do not say Pirkei Avos; therefore it is like Torah learning and is forbidden. And for this reason one does not display mourning on Shabbos. And see the Levush: regarding what the Rabbi wrote that the chazzan first says Hamavdil, there is room to doubt whether he concludes with a beracha or not; and it appears that he should say Hamavdil without a beracha of Shem and malchus, and not conclude with a beracha; see siman 299 in the Magen Avraham, letter yud-daled. And regarding strolling on erev Tisha B'Av, it implies that one should not stroll the whole day; and this requires further study.
