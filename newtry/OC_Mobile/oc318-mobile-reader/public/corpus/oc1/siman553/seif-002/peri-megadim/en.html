The custom — Taz and Magen Avraham letter 7; and Rama's view may be said that chapters are not like the daily order that they say all year, for chapters are not said except in summer — words of mussar; and there are places that do not say chapters; therefore it is like Torah study and forbidden; and for this reason one does not show mourning on Shabbat. See Levush what Mishnah Berurah wrote that the prayer leader first makes havdalah — there is doubt whether he concludes with a blessing or not; it appears he should say havdalah without a blessing of name and kingdom and not conclude with a blessing; see siman 559 in Magen Avraham letter 14. And to stroll on erev Tisha B'Av implies all day one may not stroll — doubt on this.
