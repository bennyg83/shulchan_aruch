And they adopted the custom not to learn, etc. I have much to wonder about this: for if there is no prohibition of eating meat and wine, and on the contrary it is an obligation upon him to eat meat for the honor of Shabbos, as the Tur wrote at the end of siman 552, why should we be stringent upon him not to learn because of mourning, especially one who has a set study that he regularly does at that time? And furthermore I find it puzzling: for they read the Torah at Minchah, and even on Tisha B'Av things that belong to its day, such as the portion of the Tamid and the Mishnayos of the korbanos, since they are accustomed to say them every day; so why should the Mishnayos of Pirkei Avos that belong to that Shabbos be different? And if so, even without the honor of Shabbos he ought to say Pirkei Avos. And I found written in the name of the Maharshal that he learned after midday on every erev Tisha B'Av and permitted some to learn. And our custom that the Rama wrote according to the minhagim requires further study. And it appears that one who learns on Shabbos after midday has not lost his reward. So it appears to my humble opinion.
