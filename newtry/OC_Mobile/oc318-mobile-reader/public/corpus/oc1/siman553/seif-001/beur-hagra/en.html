(1) Even though, etc. — as stated in Eichah Rabbati, R' Yehoshua ben Beseira, etc., brought by the Rosh and Tosafot beginning of chapter 3 of Eruvin and end of Taanit and Shulchan Aruch.

(2) Unless he accepted, etc. — as written in chapter 2 of Eruvin, and he says explicitly to exclude if his intent is not to eat, for that is not acceptance; and acceptance, etc. — and likewise in siman 568 seif 3 in the gloss; his words are very puzzling and it is an error, for the poskim do not discuss except one whose intent is not to eat — Maharam and Ravyah wrote that even so it is forbidden, and there is no proof from the midrash that it was morning, and Hagahot Maimoniyot, and so from the words of the Rambam that it is forbidden; see chapter 3 of Laws of Fast Days and Hagahot Maimoniyot there; the poskim disagree on this unless he accepted — but acceptance in the heart is not discussed if it is acceptance and its law is like any fast; and even though the Maggid Mishneh wrote that when he accepted explicitly and uttered with his lips, etc., he holds every acceptance requires utterance — but for us who hold that even acceptance in the heart suffices, as stated above siman 562 seif 6 and Yoreh De'ah siman 258 and Choshen Mishpat siman 212, it is no less than any fast acceptance, as Rabbeinu Tam wrote that any vow in the heart suffices.
