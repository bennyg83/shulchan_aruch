(1) [Seif 1] Unless he explicitly accepted upon himself, etc. Even if he explicitly accepted the fast upon himself, he is not forbidden in washing or anointing until nightfall. Maharshal in his responsa siman 92. So too wrote the Levush. However, the Bach wrote that once he has accepted the fast upon himself such that he is forbidden to eat, he is also forbidden to wash and anoint, and he wrote regarding the words of the Maharshal that they are not correct, see there. The Knesset HaGedolah cited his words in the glosses. So too wrote the Taz s.k. 1. Magen Avraham s.k. 4. Eliyah Rabbah os 2, Mateh Yehuda, who wrote that this is unlike the Levush. Ma'amar Mordechai os 1, Erech HaShulchan os 2, Mish'meres Zahav os 1, Chayei Adam rule 134 os 8, Derech HaChayyim os 4, Mishna Berurah os 2. However, wearing shoes is permitted, since we can see that they are on his feet and he did not accept [to refrain from] this. Eliyah Rabbah ibid. Erech HaShulchan ibid. Mish'meres Zahav ibid. Chayei Adam ibid. Derech HaChayyim ibid. Mishna Berurah ibid. The prohibition of washing and anointing from when he accepted the fast applies specifically if it is from plag ha'minchah onward, but before that time, what he accepted, he accepted, and no more. Mish'meres Zahav ibid, and Eishel Avraham os 4, Mishna Berurah os 4.
(2) Ibid. Not to eat any more today. This implies that if he said "I am fasting tomorrow," he is permitted to eat today. But from the Tur and the authorities it is implied that as soon as he accepted the fast he is forbidden to eat any more. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. The Ma'amar Mordechai os 1 wrote that this is also the view of the Shulchan Aruch. So too wrote Mishna Berurah os 1. This applies specifically when he has already eaten the seudah ha'mafsekes, and therefore we say that his intention was to accept the fast. But if he has not yet eaten and intends to eat the seudah ha'mafsekes and he said this, he is permitted to eat, for in the laws of vows we follow the intent of the one who vowed, as stated in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 218, see there. Accordingly, what he said is merely a statement of fact.
