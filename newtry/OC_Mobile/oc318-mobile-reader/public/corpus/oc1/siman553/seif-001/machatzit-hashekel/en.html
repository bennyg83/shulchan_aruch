(s.k. 1) Not, etc. And so it is in siman 608 on erev Yom Kippur: that if he said, 'Behold, I accept upon myself the fast,' even though he did not say that he accepts upon himself not to eat further today, he is nevertheless forbidden immediately.
And see siman 562, se'if 11. That is to say, do not wonder: what does his acceptance achieve, that he not eat further today? For that time from the moment of acceptance until dark cannot be counted as an addition to Tisha B'Av, since Tisha B'Av does not require adding [to the fast]; only its twilight is forbidden. And if it is because he accepted upon himself today to fast until dark, and it is like a fast of hours: since he already ate that day, he has no law of a fast of hours. And for this he alluded to see siman 562, se'if 11: although in such a case a fast of hours does not avail regarding praying Aneinu, nevertheless he must fulfill his vow not to eat until night. And so it is in the Beis Yosef.
