And it appears to me that the Rama in Darkei Moshe emended 'erev Tisha B'Av' to say that even on erev Tisha B'Av it is forbidden; but on Tisha B'Av itself the Rama agrees that it is forbidden only after midday. For otherwise, why did the Rama never write anywhere that if Tisha B'Av falls on Shabbos one should not say on Friday night the Mishnah chapter Bameh Madlikin, and say that chapter Bameh Madlikin is more regarded as the order of the day than chapter Avos at Minchah? Consider this carefully. And therefore it appears to my humble opinion as I wrote.
