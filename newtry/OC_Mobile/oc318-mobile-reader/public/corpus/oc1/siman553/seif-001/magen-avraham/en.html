Not to eat further. This implies that if he said, 'Behold, I am fasting tomorrow,' it is permitted to eat today; but in the Tur and the poskim it implies that once he accepted a fast, it is immediately forbidden to eat further; and so it is in siman 608; and see siman 562, se'if 11.
It is not an acceptance. And the Bach wrote that it is an acceptance; therefore it is good to stipulate at the time of the seudah hamafsekes.
