The common practice, however, is to recite the blessing without Hashem’s name and kingship, and this is incorrect. Needless to say, upon the death of a Torah scholar or distinguished person one must recite the blessing with Hashem’s name and kingship. Shelah, page 96, likewise writes that those who say Baruch Dayan HaEmes, Baruch Shekocho Ugevuraso, or Baruch Oseh Maaseh Bereishis without Hashem’s name and kingship have not fulfilled their obligation to recite the blessing.
