2) There: He recites HaTov, etc. He should bow toward the east and recite the blessing. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Eliyah Rabbah there cites this and writes that the same applies to all these blessings. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2; Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 2. It appears that this applies to those who pray facing east. Those who pray facing west should bow toward the west, and this is obvious. While bowing, he should say, 'Give thanks to Hashem, for He is good,' etc., and then straighten and recite the blessing. See above, siman 222, paragraph 9.
