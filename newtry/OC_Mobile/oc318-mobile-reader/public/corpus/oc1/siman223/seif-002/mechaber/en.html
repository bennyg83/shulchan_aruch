If one's father died, he recites Dayan HaEmet. If the father left money that he inherits and he has no brothers, he also recites Shehecheyanu. If he has brothers, in place of Shehecheyanu he recites HaTov veHaMeitiv. {Rama: Rema: One only recites HaTov veHaMeitiv when others share in the benefit. (Tur)}
