His father died. The same applies to any other upright person, and all the more to a Torah scholar, for these are included among bad tidings. Magen Avraham.
The true Judge. One must bless with the Divine Name and kingship: "Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe, the true Judge." This is specifically for his father or another upright person over whom he is distressed; but for another person he need not bless. Therefore the common practice is to say "Blessed is the true Judge" without the Divine Name and kingship. In any case, for his father, relatives, or an important person, and certainly upon the death of a Torah scholar, he is obligated to bless specifically with the Divine Name and kingship. Bach and Taz.
That they inherited. From here Halachot Ketanot, part 2 siman 162, wanted to prove that if he knew from the beginning that his father had property, he does not bless hatov vehametiv; see there. But this is not compelling.
Hatov vehametiv. He first blesses over the news that was told to him first. If they told him, "Your father died and left money," he should say "the true Judge" and afterward hatov vehametiv, and likewise the reverse. If two people told him at once, he should give precedence to the good, for we do not advance calamity. Halachot Ketanot, part 1 siman 205.
