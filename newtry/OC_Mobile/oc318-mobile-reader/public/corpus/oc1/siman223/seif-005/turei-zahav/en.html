He purchased utensils, etc.; he recites HaTov, etc. The Tur writes that he also recites HaTov. If so, he must first recite Shehecheyanu, as I wrote in seif 1. All the blessings recorded in this siman plainly require the Divine Name and kingship, except that Dayan HaEmes should certainly be said even over the death of another person. The Mechaber mentioned one’s father only because of the other blessings that then apply. The common practice upon hearing that someone died is to say Baruch Dayan Emes without the Divine Name and kingship, except where one is personally distressed by the death. All the more, upon the death of an important person it must certainly be recited with the Divine Name and kingship, for this is included among bad tidings, Heaven forbid, as the Tur writes in siman 225 in the name of Raavad regarding the blessing Meshaneh HaBeriyos.
