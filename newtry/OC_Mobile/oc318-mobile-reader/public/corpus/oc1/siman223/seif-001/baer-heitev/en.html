HaTov VeHaMeitiv. He should bow toward the east and recite the blessing. So I found written. This applies even if he already had sons. Taz.
The Rashba. Taz writes that the ruling follows the Mechaber, that even if his wife died he recites HaTov VeHaMeitiv. What the Rashba wrote, that he recites Shehecheyanu, means in addition to HaTov VeHaMeitiv. See there. So also write Knesses HaGedolah and Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 237. See there, and see Yad Aharon.
