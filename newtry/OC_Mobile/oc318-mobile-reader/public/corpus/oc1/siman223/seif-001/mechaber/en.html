One whose wife gave birth, whose benefactor died, who built a new house, and who acquired new vessels — and what blessing he recites. 6 sections:
If one's wife gave birth to a male, he recites HaTov veHaMeitiv, and she too must recite it.Rema: If his wife died in childbirth he recites Shehecheyanu, since there is no benefit to others. Similarly if the father died before she gave birth, she recites Shehecheyanu. (This also seems to me to reconcile the Rashba §245.) Some have written that the custom is to be lenient with this blessing since it is optional rather than obligatory, and from this it has spread that many are lenient with these blessings.
