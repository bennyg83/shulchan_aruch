In Magen Avraham, seif katan 5. In Even HaEzer, siman 27, at the end of seif 9, it states “specifically,” etc. This is the correct text. Alternatively, the abbreviation samech-samech may stand for “the end of the siman.” See Beis Shmuel there. It implies that ordinary people are considered distinguished. Responsa Rashba, however, states the opposite. See there and what I wrote in the name of Derishah concerning the words of Chelkas Mechokek, siman 190. Regarding a blessing, one may say that in cases of doubt concerning blessings we are lenient. It is possible, however, that this matter need not be analyzed so precisely at all. If, according to the circumstances, it appears that the giver derives benefit from the recipient’s acceptance, the blessing may be recited.
