Regarding Magen Avraham, end of seif katan 5: Concerning books. Note: See my Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 52, regarding why no blessing is recited over writing a Sefer Torah. I wrote that Chazal were not expert in the deficient and plene spellings, as explained in Kiddushin 71a, where it states that they were not even expert in the verses. Moreover, there are several instances where the Masorah differs from the Gemara, and we write according to the Masorah, whereas according to the Gemara the Sefer Torah would be invalid. Since we rule that a Sefer Torah missing even one letter is not called a Sefer Torah, no blessing can be recited. See also Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 53, regarding whether HaTov VeHaMeitiv is recited over the gift of a Sefer Torah. At the end of that responsum I wrote that even if it is only doubtful, the rule is that in cases of doubtful blessings we are lenient, and no blessing should be recited. Regarding reciting a blessing upon the dedication of a synagogue, see Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 156, s.v. es shenis. Examine it carefully. At the end of the responsum I wrote that one who recites the blessing has not lost, etc.
