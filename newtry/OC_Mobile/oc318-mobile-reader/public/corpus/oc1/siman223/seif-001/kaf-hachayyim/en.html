1) [Seif 1] If his wife gave birth to a son, he recites HaTov, etc. The reason is that his wife shares the good with him, for she too is pleased with a son. Beis Yosef; Olas Tamid, paragraph 1. There is no distinction between seeing for himself and hearing that his wife bore a son. Olas Tamid there; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. This applies even if the husband is in another city. Beis Yosef; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1. When he arrives and sees the child, he recites Shehecheyanu. Halachos Ketanos, volume 1, siman 201; Yad Aharon, Hagahos Beis Yosef. See below, paragraph 6.
