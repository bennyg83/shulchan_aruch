A new house. If a community built or purchased a synagogue, or if it was demolished and they rebuilt it, the chazzan should stand and recite HaTov VeHaMeitiv aloud. Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 179.
New utensils. One should not be lenient regarding this blessing. It applies specifically to functional utensils, but one does not recite a blessing over new sefarim. Magen Avraham. Bircas Rema MiPano, however, writes that one should recite a blessing over sefarim, while Yad Aharon rules that one should not. See there. See also the end of Responsa Mahari Bei Rav, who writes that one who purchases a Sefer Torah should recite Shehecheyanu, and several great authorities agreed with him. See there. Sefer Neeman Shmuel, siman 7, writes that to avoid doubt, one should recite the blessing over a new fruit the first time he reads from the Sefer Torah. See there, and see above, siman 22, regarding what is written there.
For old ones. When two people exchange utensils, both recite the blessing. Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 207.
