New utensils. I have not seen people careful about this. Perhaps they rely on Tosafos in the fourth chapter of Sukkah in the name of Rav Sherira Gaon, that one does not bless because it does not occur at fixed intervals. See page 46. Nevertheless, one should not be lenient. It appears to me that this applies specifically to functional utensils, but one does not bless over new sefarim, because mitzvos were not given for physical enjoyment. One blesses only over mitzvos that occur at fixed intervals, as written in siman 22. Bircas Rema MiPano, however, writes that one should bless over sefarim.
