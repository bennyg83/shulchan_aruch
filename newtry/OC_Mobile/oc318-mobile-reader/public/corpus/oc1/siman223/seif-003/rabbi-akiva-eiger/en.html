Article B welcomes that we live. And if the Son died, and he had no mercy, the Father would bless us The daughter of his son should be named to the P.S., and he will be so blessed with him:
Seif 3. Or bought new vessels. Regarding vessels that require immersion — there is room to argue that one does not bless at the time of acquisition, since he may still not use them before immersion; likewise regarding building a new house — behold he may not dwell in it before affixing a mezuzah; see Magen Avraham sk 22; and it requires study for the law.
