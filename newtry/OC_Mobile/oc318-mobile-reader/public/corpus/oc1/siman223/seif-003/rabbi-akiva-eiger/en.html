Seif 2. He also recites Shehecheyanu. Conversely, if the son died and possessed property that his father inherits, the father does not recite Shehecheyanu, since it is an absolute calamity for a father to witness the death of his son, as stated in the chapter Yesh Nochalin. So writes Sefer Livyas Chen on Parashas Bo.
Seif 3. Or he purchased new utensils. Regarding utensils that require immersion, one may question whether the blessing should be recited at the time of purchase, since he cannot yet use them before immersion. Likewise, regarding one who built a new house, he cannot live in it before affixing a mezuzah. See Magen Avraham, siman 22. This requires practical study.
