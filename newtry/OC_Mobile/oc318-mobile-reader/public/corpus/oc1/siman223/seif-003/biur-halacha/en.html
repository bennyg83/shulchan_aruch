He built a new house, etc. See Lechem Chamudos and Eliyah Rabbah, who write that if he has a wife and children, it is like having partners in the house and he recites HaTov VeHaMeitiv, as stated below in seif 4 concerning utensils. Chayei Adam distinguishes that when one purchases important utensils for himself and his household to use, this is not compelled by an obligation and is therefore considered a benefit, whereas here he is obligated to provide a home for his wife and children. This is unconvincing. Once his sons reach age six, the support he provides them is only within the category of charity, and a house purchased for them is certainly a complete benefit. Even regarding his wife, he could have rented a dwelling for her and is not obligated to purchase a house for this purpose. When he buys a house, they certainly experience heartfelt joy, it is a complete benefit to them, and HaTov VeHaMeitiv applies.
