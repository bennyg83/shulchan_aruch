"Some say even a poor person." Specifically a woolen garment and similar items resemble something that comes from time to time, because ordinary people acquire new ones only at intervals. This differs from inexpensive articles that ordinary people commonly replace frequently. "And this is the custom." Radbaz writes that Tosafos were discussing a wealthy person, but a poor person recites the blessing. Conversely, an extremely wealthy person who purchases important utensils but derives no joy from them and does not regard them as significant should not recite the blessing. "From animal hides." The law depends upon the joy and significance of the new article, subject to the distinctions stated by the authorities regarding garments made from animal hides.
