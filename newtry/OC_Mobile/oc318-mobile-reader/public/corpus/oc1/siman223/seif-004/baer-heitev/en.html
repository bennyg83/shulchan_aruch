The acquisition. Meaning, from the time he purchases the garment, even though he must give it to a tailor to fashion it into clothing. Taz. Magen Avraham writes that this applies only when he purchases a finished utensil as it is. If he purchases material and gives it to an artisan to make a utensil, he recites the blessing only when he uses it. See there.
Who clothes the naked. One who purchases a hat should recite Oter Yisrael, and over a belt, Ozer Yisrael. Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 5. Meni’ach writes there that these should be recited without the Divine Name and kingship.
