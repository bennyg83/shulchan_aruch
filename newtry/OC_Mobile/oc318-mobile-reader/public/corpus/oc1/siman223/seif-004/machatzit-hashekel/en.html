Seif katan 4: His father died, etc. See Yoreh Deah, siman 340, seif 6, regarding who is considered an upright person and the law of tearing one’s garment for an upright person. See Taz, who writes that the custom is not to mention Hashem’s name and kingship over another person unless one was distressed by his death. Regarding a Torah scholar, however, Heaven forbid, one must certainly mention Hashem’s name and kingship.
