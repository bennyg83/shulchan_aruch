[6] At the time of acquisition, etc. It appears to me that this means from the time he purchases the cloth, even though he must still give it to a tailor. Taz. Magen Avraham, however, writes that in such a case he recites the blessing when he wears it. Only when he purchases a completed garment does he recite the blessing at the time of purchase. He writes similarly below in siman 551 and siman 600. This is also implied by the Remazim. See siman 673, seif katan 15.
