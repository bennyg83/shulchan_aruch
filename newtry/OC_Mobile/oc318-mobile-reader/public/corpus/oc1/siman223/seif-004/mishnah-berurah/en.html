(17) At the time of acquisition, etc. This applies specifically if he purchases a garment that is fit to wear immediately. If he purchases something that cannot be worn or used as it is and must first be given to an artisan for repair, he does not recite Shehecheyanu until he wears the garment or uses the utensil.
(18) Who clothes the naked. This is recited before Shehecheyanu. If he puts on the garment in the morning, however, he may fulfill this obligation through the blessing Malbish Arumim recited among the morning blessings. Later authorities.
