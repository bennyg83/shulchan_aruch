[ד] And one does not kindle, etc. Bach wondered and ruled that even if one kindles in his house he must bless whoever does not hear the blessing — so Mordechai wrote — and Taz disagrees that Mordechai is alone against Tur and Ran, as Knesset HaGedolah and Magen Avraham ruled. I wonder: Bach wrote it follows Rif, Rambam, Rosh, Semag, and Agudah, and Rashal ruled so; I also found Keli Yakar and Shiltot Giborim daf 106 in the name of Riaz, and in Shiltot Giborim around Mordechai in the name of Ittur and Rashba, and in Rama's responsum siman 99 — no doubt if the great ones above had seen the poskim I brought they would not disagree with Bach. I also say Tur's view is likewise: he wrote one does not kindle for him and did not write "in his house" as in siman 677 — his intent excludes when he hears the blessings, for even if they do not kindle and he blesses himself he need not bless on seeing; what he wrote in siman 677 "if they kindle for him in his house" — no more is needed. It appears to me he need not kindle and need not participate as it speaks there, but he does require a blessing on seeing. Shulchan Aruch, Rama, and all the greats understood Tur that no blessing is needed and strained his words — in my humble opinion correct as I interpreted.
