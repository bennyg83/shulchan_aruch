With the rightmost lamp. There is no distinction whether he kindles on the right of the entrance or on the left of the entrance. Beis Yosef. But Maharshal wrote that when they are placed to the right of the entrance, he must begin with the rightmost lamp, which is adjacent to the entrance. One who is precise should place them lengthwise like a spit, so that all of them are equally within a handbreadth adjacent to the entrance, and he should begin on the left and turn to the right. So wrote Bach. Mateh Moshe wrote in the name of Maharshal that he should place them in the open space of the entrance like a mezuzah. Bach wrote: I found written that a person who already kindled may kindle for a woman and bless, provided that she stands near him at the time of the blessing; but otherwise he should not bless, since the mitzvah is placed upon the person's body. Until here. See siman 8, where I wrote in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos, which implies that another may bless and she may kindle, and see what I wrote in siman 432, section 2. Bach wrote: when another blesses, he should bless over the kindling of the Chanukah lamp. Until here. In Ran, first chapter of Pesachim, regarding separating terumos, it does not imply this.
