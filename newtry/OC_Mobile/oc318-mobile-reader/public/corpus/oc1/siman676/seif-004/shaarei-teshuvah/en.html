To the right. See Bach; and see in Pri Megadim part 1 section 98 who wrote on the proof of Magen Avraham from Yoma daf 59 that there is no proof — one may say it is fine as Levush to kindle from right to left as the way of writing; there too regarding gifts from the inner altar one may say that what draws his hands toward him as the way of writing is the way of the right, and what draws his hand away from his body and onward is the way of the left — see there.
