Rightmost. See Baer Heitev. See Pri Me'ah, part 1, end of siman 98, who wrote regarding Magen Avraham's proof from Yoma 59 that there is no proof. One may say that Levush is correct, to kindle from right to left in the manner of writing, for there too, regarding the applications of blood on the inner altar, one may say that when he draws his hand toward himself in the manner of writing, that is the way of the right, and when he draws his hand away from his body and onward, that is the way of the left.
