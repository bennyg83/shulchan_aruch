Paragraph 3: He says Haneros, etc. And there should be thirty-six words, etc., as a hint to the number of lamps, meaning the lamps kindled during all eight days of Chanukah, which are thirty-six lamps. This is the text that Maharshal wrote: These lamps we kindle for the salvations and for the miracles and for the wonders that You performed for our fathers through Your holy kohanim. During all eight days of Chanukah these lamps are holy, and we have no permission to use them, only to look at them, in order to give thanks to Your name for Your wonders and for Your miracles and for Your salvations. Maharshal concluded: do not change, do not add, and do not subtract. The reason is explained: it contains thirty-six words, aside from Haneros Halalu, as if he said, these lamps are thirty-six. Until here. In the sefer Eliyah Rabbah, in the name of Mateh Moshe, he wrote that there is one extra word. Eliyah Rabbah wrote that the words holy they are are an error, and one should say holy; the word they is extra, for immediately after the word they he says, and we have no permission to use them. He wrote that this is the text in Abudarham, where it says only holy and not they. He wrote that so was the practice of Rambam, Re'em, and Rosh. Until here are his words. One must say that also the words Haneros Halalu in the phrase Haneros Halalu are holy, etc., those two words are also not included in the count, and then the count is exact.
