And he does not. See Turei Zahav. Rashi, Shabbos 23a, says this is one who has not yet kindled in his home, or one sitting on a ship. See Bach. Turei Zahav explained that the word yet is not precise. Perhaps yet means all night, even though he will kindle on the second night. One should not say that he should not bless Shehecheyanu on the first night and should bless Shehecheyanu on the second night; it therefore teaches that he immediately blesses Shehecheyanu, and on the second night he does not bless Shehecheyanu. This requires examination, for if so, what is the meaning of or one sitting on a ship?
