To kindle, etc. Even when one kindles for another and he already kindled for himself, the custom is to bless to kindle, unlike Bach, who wrote that he blesses over the kindling. Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah. The fact that he blesses for another applies specifically when he stands near him at the time of the blessing; otherwise one should not bless, since the mitzvah is placed upon the person's body. Bach and Hagahos Maimoniyos. A convert from among the nations, if he wants to say for our fathers, may say it; and if he says, that You performed miracles for Israel, he may say that. Iggeres HaRambam. See siman 8, paragraph 8, that another may bless and he may kindle.
