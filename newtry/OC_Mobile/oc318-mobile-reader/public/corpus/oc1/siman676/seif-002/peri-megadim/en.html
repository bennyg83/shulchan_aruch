Before ATZ — if he kindled all the candles he sees he should not bless afterward, for in all matters of a mitzvah without duration one does not bless afterward; the time of saying it is on the second night — not after kindling on the first night; when he remembers is on the third night onward, for these blessings were instituted only from kindling. Some say like festival that one says it even in the market — Pri Chadash here; Magen Avraham 673:1. However one may say she'asah nissim and shehecheyanu — the viewer also blesses them as in siman 15 here, even if he kindled all without blessing at kindling he blesses on seeing; but blessing to kindle — no, for it already passed. However if he kindled one candle one may say he blesses on the rest on night two onward even though the first is only the mitzvah and the rest are mehaderin — as siman 671; nevertheless Magen Avraham letter 1 in the name of Beit Yosef 673 wrote the blessing refers to all of them — it implies if he did not intend to bless he blesses on the rest; Eliyah Rabbah — it will be explained there. Nevertheless requires study, since after the mitzvah there is only mehaderin.
