However, on Friday night the wording of the blessing is, "to kindle the light of Shabbos." The reason is that it is forbidden to make use of the Chanukah light. He therefore says, "a Chanukah light," to indicate that the light is only for the mitzvah of Chanukah. This is not so with the Shabbos light, whose purpose is a light that we use, and whose illumination benefits us. This illuminating light is "of Shabbos." Birkei Yosef, letter 1. In Machazik Berachah, letter 1, he wrote another reason: On Chanukah there is no act other than the lights, and no other practical matter at all. It is therefore called a "Chanukah light," for Chanukah is specifically the light. This is not so with Shabbos, which has several practical mitzvos, such as the meal, Kiddush, fine garments, and the like, with the light being one of the mitzvos. This is the meaning of "the light of Shabbos," for Shabbos has several matters and this is one of them, namely, the familiar light of Shabbos among its many matters. This is not so with Chanukah, which has no practical act other than the light, end quote. Shaarei Teshuvah, letter 1, cited his words. See also below, letter 9.
