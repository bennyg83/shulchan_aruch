To kindle. See Baer Heitev, that on Shabbos we say lamp of Shabbos because the Chanukah lamp is forbidden to use by its light. Therefore he says Chanukah lamp, to indicate that the lamp exists only for the mitzvah of Chanukah. This is unlike the Shabbos lamp, where the intent is that we use it and its light shines for our benefit, and this illuminating lamp is of Shabbos; see there. See Eliyah Rabbah. In Machazik Berachah he wrote another reason: on Chanukah, the practical mitzvah is only with the lamp itself, unlike Shabbos, which has practical mitzvos such as kiddush and the meal, etc.; the lamp is also one of them, so it is appropriate to say lamp of Shabbos, which is known in several matters aside from the lamp. See there. See Knesses HaGedolah in the name of Iggeres HaRambam, that a convert may say, Who performed miracles for our fathers, and if he wants to say, Who performed miracles for Israel, he may say it. See there. See above, siman 473, in the name of Ohr HaChayim on Parashas Behaaloscha, that a convert may say the whole order of the Haggadah, We were slaves, etc.; see there.
