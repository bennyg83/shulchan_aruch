(1) [Seif 1] To kindle a Hanukkah light. Thus is the main precise formula; the formula in Shas and Rif and Rambam in which it is written to kindle a ner shel Chanukah is not primary. Peri Chadash. Shaarei Teshuvah letter 2; so too Halakhot Ketanot part 1 siman 3 that it is better to bless lehadlik ner Chanukah and not shel; per the secret acronym nachal. Beit David letter 11 in Hagahot Taz.
