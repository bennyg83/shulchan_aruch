Levush: Over kindling, etc. A mnemonic for the matter is: Make for yourself a fiery serpent, and place it on a pole, and anyone who sees it shall live. Maharshal and Shnei Luchos HaBris wrote that Shelchanukah is one word, not two words, meaning that it is only for Chanukah and not for anything else. This is unlike a Shabbos lamp, which is to give light for oneself, only the mitzvah of Shabbos rests upon it. One says lazman hazeh, not uvazman hazeh. The lamed of lazman has a chirik.
