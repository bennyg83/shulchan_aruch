1) [Seif 1] To kindle a Chanukah light. This is the principal, precise wording, and the wording of the Gemara, the Rif, and the Rambam, in which it is written, "to kindle a light of Chanukah," is not the principal wording. Pri Chadash; Shulchan Gavohah, letter 2. So too wrote Halachos Ketanos, vol. 1, siman 3, that it is better to recite, "to kindle a Chanukah light," and not "of," because according to the hidden meaning its initial letters spell nachal. Yad Aharon cited his words in Hagahos HaTur. So too wrote Yafeh LaLev, vol. 2, letter 2; Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Vayeishev, letter 2, and see there, where he wrote several reasons for this.
