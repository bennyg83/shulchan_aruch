(ב) In order to turn to the right — see Mishna Berurah and the view of Levush and Taz that the Gemara's intent is the opposite of this, namely that one goes from right to left [and you saw Magen Avraham against this view was already rejected by the Acharonim — see Shaarei Teshuvah] — and per this Taz ruled thus: where there is a mezuzah and one must arrange them on the left of the entrance at the wall, arrange them in the tefach next to the door; on the first night place the candle at the door, on the second night place the additional candle toward the room's interior — the additional candle is on his right and the first on his left; he begins kindling from the additional candle and lights from right to left; on the third night when he adds another candle it is on his right and the first candles on his left — he begins from the additional candle and goes right to left. Where he arranges them in the door's opening or window or synagogue — on the first night at the row's end the candle is on his left; on the second night place the additional candle next to it on his right and the first remains on his left; he begins from the additional candle on his right and goes right to left; each night when he adds the additional candle he lights from it right to left until on the eighth night the additional candle is at the row's end on his right and he lights it first too and goes right to left — he must stand with his back to north and face south. See Peri Megadim who wrote he practiced like Taz — we have three views: Mechaber, Rashal, Gra, and Taz; each does per custom; even if Chayei Adam mentions only Shulchan Aruch and Maharshal, do not reject Taz after Peri Megadim and Dagul MeRevavah copied it to halacha; in any case for the mitzvah itself all agree l'chatchila only differs where to begin kindling.
