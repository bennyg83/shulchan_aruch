(ה) In the wording of the blessing she'asah nissim one says bayamim hahem bazman hazeh — so explained: He performed miracles in days that actually passed at this time, not as some say bayamim hahem uvezman hazeh. Rashal in responsum siman 64. Knesset HaGedolah there. Magen Avraham beginning of the section Farach.
