Nevertheless, this requires examination, for between the blessing and kindling the first lamp it is forbidden to interrupt and say Haneros Halalu. If so, one may say that the blessing applies to all the lamps, as appears from what Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of the siman in the name of Beis Yosef in siman 672, that if he kindled afterward he does not bless. This implies that if he had intended that he must bless again, the blessing applies to all the lamps. Why then should he interrupt between the blessing and kindling the other lamps? Nevertheless, certainly after completing all three blessings and kindling the first lamp, that is when he should say Haneros. I, the poor one, have not seen the practice to be so. Heaven forbid to speak against the light of Israel, Maharshal, of blessed memory. Nevertheless, one who practices and says Haneros Halalu afterward, after he kindled everything, also acts properly, etc.; and this requires examination. For the count of thirty-six words, see Magen Avraham, and there it will be explained with Hashem's help. See Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 8, about this.
