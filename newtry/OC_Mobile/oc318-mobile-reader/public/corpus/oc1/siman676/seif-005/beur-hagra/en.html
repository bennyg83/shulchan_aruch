Section 5: He begins. In Mordechai it is written that he should kindle from left to right, as written in Zevachim: all turns, etc.
And he blesses over the added one. Maharik. This has neither taste nor scent, for this is only for the mehadrin min hamehadrin. Shall he leave the main mitzvah and bless over the optional beautification? See siman 674, section 1, in the gloss. Also, because of turning to the right, shall he nullify the main mitzvah, which is within a handbreadth adjacent to the entrance? Rather, the main point is that he begins with the lamp adjacent to the entrance, whether he kindles on the right of the entrance or on the left, and he blesses every night over it. In this way he always kindles and blesses over the lamp adjacent to the entrance and over the main mitzvah. See Magen Avraham in the name of Maharshal. But what he wrote, that he should place it like a spit, does not seem correct.
