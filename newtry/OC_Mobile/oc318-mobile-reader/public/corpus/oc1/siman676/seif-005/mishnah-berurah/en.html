(א) (i) With the rightmost candle, etc. - he will begin and bless the other one, etc. There is no difference between the person who places it on the right of the opening and the person who places it on the left of the opening, the blessing will always begin with the additional candle [II] and the 11th. And it is true that if he can arrange everyone inside the space of the opening that they should all be in the tap next to the opening, he will indeed be careful that the opening is not opened for half an hour while the candles are burning so that the wind does not extinguish them and refer to what we wrote in Bahl Da'at 16 on this matter:

(ב) (j) To turn to the right - as the Sage said, every corner you turn will be nothing but a right-hand path:

(3) Likewise, every night, etc., he blesses the extra one, etc. - And the Omnipresent, when he lights a candle in the window or in the opening space or in the bachan on the first night, he will light the rightmost candle at the beginning, and on the second night, the leftmost candle, as well as on the night of the third and fourth until the eighth day, he will light everything with the extra candle, and then he turns and goes to the right side, and in all of this, he will move Ae to the left side at the time of lighting, so that he will touch it first and will not have to pass on the unleavened bread. And in the opinion of the above-mentioned HA, even in this, he will always bless the first candle, which is the main part of the mitzvah. And it will be seen that the first candle will be placed on the left side and then every day it will be added and goes to the right side and the lighting will always be with a candle that stands on the left side as we wrote and turns and goes to the right side:
