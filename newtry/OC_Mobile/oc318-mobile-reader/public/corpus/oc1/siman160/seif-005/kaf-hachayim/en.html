However, Taz note 1 and Magen Avraham note 2 are lenient regarding change because of themselves, since it is not in the mishnah and Rambam — see there. But Bach wrote that one should be stringent as Shulchan Aruch ruled, and they brought this in Shakh Ne'eman HaGadol in Hagahot Taz, note 1, and in Yad Efraim; see Birkei Yosef note 2, who elaborated on this and concluded: "And for halacha, although the later authorities are lenient regarding change because of themselves, one should be stringent per the Tur, Maran, and Maharshal," end quote. And such is the opinion of Ma'amar Mordechai, note 1; see there what he wrote to resolve the difficulty of Taz and Magen Avraham — see there. And likewise Chida, note 1, and Ben Ish Chai, Parashat Kedoshim, note 1; and the essential point is as we already wrote in the previous siman, that although netilat yadayim is d'rabbanan, nevertheless because of doubt regarding berachot one should be stringent; all the more so here, for such is also Maran's opinion, whose rulings we accepted; and such is our teacher's opinion, since he does not argue.
