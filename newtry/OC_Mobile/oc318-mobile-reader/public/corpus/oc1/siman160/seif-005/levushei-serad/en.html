Taz, seif katan 3 There is a dispute. Rambam does not prove the matter, for one may say that although he explained the Mishnah as referring to one who scooped the water, he nevertheless agrees that in this case it is valid. Taz therefore brings proof that Rashba, because he interprets the Mishnah like Rambam, invalidates water used for rinsing. It follows that Rambam presumably invalidates it as well. He then proves that Rosh also invalidates water used for rinsing.
