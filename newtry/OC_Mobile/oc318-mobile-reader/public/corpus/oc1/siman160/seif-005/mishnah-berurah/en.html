Or in the ground. Meaning, even if at the time of the labor they were resting in the ground, since they were drawn water from the beginning, labor invalidates them. But when one performed labor with them while attached, they are valid for netilas yadayim once detached.
Not with mikveh water. All this is with respect to performing labor. But regarding water that became invalid for animal drinking, there is no distinction between drawn water and mikveh water, and it is invalid; Taz. All this concerns drawing water from the mikveh with a vessel for netilas yadayim. But to immerse his hands in the mikveh itself is permitted even in this case. Regarding change of appearance, see above in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 4.
