On their own. Rambam does not have the reading “whether because of themselves.” Therefore, one may be lenient when they changed on their own. Taz; Magen Avraham.
They are invalid. Bach writes that a change in appearance invalidates specifically for washing, but water of forty seah is not invalidated for immersion because of a change in appearance. Since it is valid for immersion of the entire body, all the more so for netilas yadayim.
