Between whether because of themselves, etc. The Tur wrote likewise and brings proof from Avodah Zarah as we learned: one placed komos and kankantum into them and their appearance changed — they are invalid. This is puzzling, for the mishnah did not mention "because of themselves." One could say they share one law: regarding mikveh it is stated in Yoreh De'ah siman 201 that change of appearance because of something that fell in invalidates, but if it changed because of themselves it is valid, as stated in seifim 27–28 there; if so, a fortiori here for netilat yadayim, which is d'rabbanan, it should not invalidate because of themselves; at minimum the tanna should have written a novelty here. In Rambam it is not found — truly this matter of "between because of themselves." Maharshal wrote that in some Tur texts it is also missing; it requires study, for as it appears there is no place to uphold this.**** END BLOCK ****
