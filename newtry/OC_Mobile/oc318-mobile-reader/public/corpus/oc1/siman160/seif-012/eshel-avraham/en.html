And salt. In a responsum in Yoreh Deah, siman 77, he rules more leniently regarding salt than regarding snow and hail, because salt is fit to season meat and foods. See there, where he discusses the matter at length and ultimately reconciles the words of the Shulchan Aruch by explaining that its clauses address separate cases: snow, hail, frost, and the like are effective for washing when crushed, whereas salt is effective only through immersion, to complete a measure of forty seah.
