Rabbi Eliezer. There it is explained that even if the snow was not crushed, it is valid for a mikveh, and all the more so for netilas yadayim. From this it appears permissible for travelers who have no water to go to a place with deep snow and insert their hands into it. However, he should ensure that there is enough snow there for the measure of a mikveh. Olas Tamid. Taz likewise writes in siman 159, seif katan 20, that if the snow is gathered in a depression and there is a measure of forty seah, one may rely on it for netilas yadayim even initially; see there. Magen Avraham permits even snow spread over the surface of the earth, provided that forty seah is connected together, even if it is very shallow; see there. Lechem Chamudos writes, however, that according to all opinions it is forbidden to take snow in one hand and rub the other hand with it. See Gan HaMelech, siman 95.
