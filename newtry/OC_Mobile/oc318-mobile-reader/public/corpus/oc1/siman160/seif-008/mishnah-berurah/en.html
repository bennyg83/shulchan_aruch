Because they are bitter. They are not fit, as below in seif 9. But if it is because of their heat, they should not be invalidated, as the Mechaber wrote in seif 6.
The dog. Even though their taste differs from other water and they are not so fit for a person to drink, since in any event they are fit for a dog to drink, they are valid for washing. Know that according to the stringent opinion we wrote above in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 27, regarding water heated by fire, all the more so one should be stringent here regarding hot spring water, which never had a time of fitness.
