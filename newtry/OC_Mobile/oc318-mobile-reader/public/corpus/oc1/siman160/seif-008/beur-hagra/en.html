Seif 8: The reason, etc. As stated in seif 9, they are not invalid merely because of their heat, as stated in seif 6 there. See Rashi there, s.v. dekhulei alma, who gives another reason, and the Rif writes likewise. However, from the Gemara’s statement there, “this is a dispute among Tannaim,” etc., the explanation of Rabbeinu Yonah appears correct. See Rashi there, s.v. shenifsalu, etc.
