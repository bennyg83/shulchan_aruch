14) There: If one cooled wine in the water, etc. The same applies if one cooled water in water, such as during the summer; the outer water is invalid. Maaseh Rokeach on the Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchos Berachos. He writes that this is unlike R' Arama, who was uncertain and concluded that it is permitted; see there. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 5, likewise rejects that view and concludes that it is forbidden; see there. Zechor L'Avraham, paragraph 50, writes that this is evident from Maran's ruling concerning cooling wine in water and from Beit David, siman 530; see there. Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 3, cites him.
