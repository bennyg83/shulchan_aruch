13) There: If one cooled wine in the water, etc. This applies specifically when he cooled the wine. If the wine stood in the water only so that it would not become warm, and the wine was already cold, the water is not invalid for netilas yadayim. Olas Tamid, paragraph 3; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 6; Siddur Beis Oved, paragraph 3; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 6; Ben Ish Chai there, paragraph 7, unlike Beis Menuchah, paragraph 4. The same applies if one put live fish into the water; it is not invalid. Olas Tamid there; Magen Avraham, paragraph 5; Yad Aharon in Hagahot HaTur; Siddur Beis Oved there; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3; Derech HaChaim, paragraph 2. Eliyah Rabbah writes there that it is permitted even if the fish died in the water; see there. Chayei Adam, klal 39, paragraph 3, and Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 9, cite him. The words of the aforementioned poskim, however, appear to apply specifically while the fish remain alive in the water. If they died, the water becomes invalid. Even according to Eliyah Rabbah, it appears that he refers only to a case where the dead fish did not remain in the water. Otherwise, the water putrefies and becomes wastewater. If the fish was removed immediately upon dying, it appears that even according to Olas Tamid and Magen Avraham the water is not invalid.
