Seif 13: It requires, etc.; and this applies, etc. The Gemara on 107a states, and Rabbi Elazar said, etc., “I do not know,” etc. The view of Raavad and Rabbeinu Yonah is that a reviis is always required. However, when the water comes from remnants of a valid washing, it need not be a full reviis collected in one place, for if he later completes it from another eighth of a log, that suffices in such a case. Rambam’s view is that the ruling stated here also concerns the second waters, such as for terumah according to his opinion. For the first waters, however, a reviis is always required.
Provided that, etc. Rashba asked: what advantage is there in the water having come from remnants of a valid washing? It still lacks a reviis. He answered that the stream does not cease, as stated regarding a mikveh in chapter 2 of Chagigah: if his feet were, etc. See there. Netilas yadayim is among the Rabbinic gradations of purity. It cannot mean literally all at once, for if so, how can the Gemara say that it comes from remnants of purity? Which of them is last?
And some permit, etc. This is stated there in Toras HaBayis, following the plain meaning of the Gemara, that they came, etc.
