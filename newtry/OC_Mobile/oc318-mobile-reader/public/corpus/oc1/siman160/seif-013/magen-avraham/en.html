Who came to wash together. This implies specifically when both intended from the outset to wash together. But if one intended to wash alone and the second afterward extended his hands beneath the first person’s hands, the second person’s hands remain impure. He must dry them and wash them again. Terumas HaDeshen writes similarly in the name of Tosafos. See siman 162, seifim 5-6.
From half a log for three people. One may ask: Since a reviis suffices for two people, a reviis and a half should suffice for three. The answer is that the Sages were concerned that when many people wash, they will not be sufficiently careful to wash until the joint, since each person will restrict his use of the water so that enough remains for the next. They therefore prohibited three people from washing from only a reviis and a half. This applies specifically if a full reviis did not remain for the last two. But if the first person washed with half a reviis, it is obvious that the last two may wash with the remaining reviis. Rash.
