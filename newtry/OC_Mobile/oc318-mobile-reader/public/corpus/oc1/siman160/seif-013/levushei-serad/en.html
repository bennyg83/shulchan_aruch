Taz, seif katan 10 “This is Rashi’s opinion.” In truth, it is Raavad’s opinion, for according to Rashi fruit juice is permitted, whereas this view in Shulchan Aruch prohibits even fruit juice; the permissive view regarding fruit juice is not brought until afterward. Taz’s primary discussion here concerns wine, and he was therefore not precise in his wording on this point. Regarding wine, Rashi also holds that one may never wash with wine according to the Sages, and presumably the halachah follows them. Taz’s statement that this is Rashi’s opinion is intended only to exclude Rashba, who disagrees even regarding wine. This is clear.
