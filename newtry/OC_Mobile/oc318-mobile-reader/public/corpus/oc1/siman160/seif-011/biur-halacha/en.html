Water concerning which he is uncertain whether work was performed with it, etc. Peri Megadim implies that he may also recite the blessing over it, since Chazal permitted him to wash with such water. I wrote similarly below, in siman 261, seif 1, concerning an eruv chatzeiros made during bein hashemashos; see Biur Halachah there for proof from Magen Avraham. In the latter clause, however, if he has other water, then even according to the dissenting opinion he need not recite a blessing, as Olas Tamid writes.
