Seif katan 11: One may not immerse, etc. Rabbeinu Yonah holds that this applies specifically, etc. Meaning, the Gemara’s decree refers specifically to, etc.
We decree that if he immerses, etc. He may not realize that the hands became valid through immersion. He may mistakenly reason that just as this water is invalid for immersion of the entire body, it is also invalid for immersion of the hands. He will therefore assume that the hands were rendered valid through washing, and will then also permit taking water from it with a vessel and washing his hands with that water.
All the more so his hands. He will realize that the validity results from immersion of the hands and not from washing.
