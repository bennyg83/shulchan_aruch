(11) There, "even if he intended to soak," etc. If Reuven soaked his bread in Shimon's water: per the view that a person does not forbid what is not his, the water is valid; but per the view that a person forbids regarding what is not his, one should be stringent. Mahar"a Nachum in a responsum in manuscript. Birkei Yosef note 4, Sha'arei Teshuvah note 4; and Sha'arei Teshuvah wrote there that what he hung on the dispute whether a person forbids what is not his is puzzling, for Mordechai in Chullin wrote to distinguish between the case of a vineyard partition, etc., and one who throws milk into his fellow's pot — see there. And he means that this is comparable to throwing milk into a fellow's pot, and all agree it should be forbidden; and so he raised in Yad Yosef on Bava Metzia 34 — see there. And they brought Eshel Avraham note 3.
