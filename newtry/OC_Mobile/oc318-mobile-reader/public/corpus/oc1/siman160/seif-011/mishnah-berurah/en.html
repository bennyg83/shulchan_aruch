A doubt whether labor was done, etc. This means whether he became uncertain after washing, or even before washing; he may wash his hands with them if he has no other water.
If they have the measure. In this case, specifically if he became uncertain after he washed his hands. But beforehand, no, for that which he does not know how to clarify whether it has the required measure is not considered a doubt; Magen Avraham and other authorities. A doubt in a dispute among the poskim is like any other doubt, for since they did not resolve it, it is a doubt in a D'Rabbanan matter, and we rule leniently.
Impure or pure. Meaning, invalid water or valid water; Beit Yosef. For regarding washing for ordinary food, the water is not invalidated because of its impurity, as above in siman 159, seif 11.
Pure. The reason is that washing is D'Rabbanan, and they were not stringent regarding a doubt. Even if all the doubts were together, it is valid.
And every doubt of purity, etc. For example, if there was a doubt whether the vessel was whole or not, and the like.
And there is one who says, etc. This refers to the entire seif. The reason is that it is possible to leave the doubt easily.
He should wash his hands. In the Beit Yosef it implies that it is proper to seek out water, for if one does not fulfill netilas yadayim properly, it is harmful to poverty.
And remove himself, etc. If he knows that he washed according to the halacha and afterward a doubt arose about them, such as if he was unsure whether he diverted his attention or not, or he knows that he did not divert his attention but extended his hands near a filthy place and is in doubt whether he touched it or did not touch it, he need not go back and wash them even to avoid the doubt, because we maintain them in their established status.
They did not become invalid. As in seif 2 regarding a baker. Although when one washed one hand and rubbed it against the other, the water on his hand became impure, as below in siman 162, seif 4, the water in the vessel did not become impure through his touching it. Therefore, one who leaves the bathroom and comes to wash his hands may draw water with his handfuls from the bucket and pour the water outside, and the water remaining in the bucket did not become impure through his touching it and is valid for netilas yadayim for a meal. But if he shook his hands in the bucket itself in order to purify them through this, labor was thereby performed with all the water in the bucket, and it is invalid for washing. Even if his hands were not actually dirty, nevertheless, since he needs to clean them in the water because they touched a filthy place, the water became like waste water; later authorities.
From water with which one washed, etc. Because the water has already become impure, and also because labor was done with it. Therefore, even if he washed from a revi'is all at once, it is also forbidden.
