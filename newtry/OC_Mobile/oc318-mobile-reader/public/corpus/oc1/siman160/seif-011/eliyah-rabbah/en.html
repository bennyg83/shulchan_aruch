Levush: exposed water, etc. The Olas Tamid wrote that it is implied from the Levush that if we saw that a snake drank from it, it is invalid, and he was not precise, for the Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 4 that with any domestic animal, wild animal, or bird one should validate. Perhaps because of danger it is invalid. I say that it is obvious that it is forbidden because of danger, for in Levush, Yoreh Deah, siman 116, he wrote that even now, if one sees that a snake or creeping creature drank from it, it is invalid for drinking. Furthermore, the Levush does not hold like the Shulchan Aruch, as I explained above.
