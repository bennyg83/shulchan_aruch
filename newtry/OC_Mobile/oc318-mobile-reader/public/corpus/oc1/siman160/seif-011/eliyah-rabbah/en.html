[11] [Levush] Exposed water, etc. Olat Tamid wrote: it is implied from the Levush that if we saw a snake drink from it, it is invalid. He was not precise, for the Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 4 that with any animal, beast, or bird one should validate it; perhaps it is invalid because of danger, end quote. I say that it is obvious that it is forbidden because of danger, for in Levush, Yoreh De'ah siman 116, he wrote that even now, if one sees that a snake or creeping creature drank from it, it is unfit for drinking. Furthermore, the Levush does not hold like the Shulchan Aruch, as I explained above.
