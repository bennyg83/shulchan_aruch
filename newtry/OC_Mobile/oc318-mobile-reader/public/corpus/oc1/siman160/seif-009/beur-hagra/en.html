Seif 9: Salty water, etc. There, in the dispute among Tannaim, etc. We rule like Rabbi Shimon ben Elazar, who follows Chizkiyah, as stated in Toras HaBayis. Their law is the same as that of the hot waters of Tiberias in seif 7, as stated in seif 8, “the reason,” etc. However, from the wording of the Rif and Rosh, who omit the entire discussion, it appears that their text of the Gemara did not contain, “this is a dispute among Tannaim,” etc. They therefore explain that the reason concerning the hot waters of Tiberias is that they never had a state of fitness, and for this reason they rule like Chizkiyah. This resolves Rashba’s difficulty in Toras HaBayis that the ruling should follow Rabbi Yochanan, who agrees with the first Tanna. Nevertheless, the Tur and Shulchan Aruch require study for omitting that one may immerse his hands in them.
And if, etc. See Tosafos in Chullin there, s.v. mayim, and in Zevachim there, and at the end of the first chapter of Sukkah.
Whether for netilas yadayim, etc. This requires study, for there it appears that it is valid only to complete the required measure. Regarding mosquito larvae, the Gemara asks, “even by themselves,” etc. Likewise, at the end of the first chapter of Sukkah, it says that liquid mud proves the matter, since it combines, etc. It is possible to answer that the phrase “cloudy because of mud” implies that water is also present, as stated in the Mishnah at the end of chapter 2 of Mikvaos: Rabbi Yehoshua says, regarding water and mud, in which kind of mud may one immerse, etc.
