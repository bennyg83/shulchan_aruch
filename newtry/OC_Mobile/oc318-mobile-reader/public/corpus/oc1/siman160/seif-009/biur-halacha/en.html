For immersion in mikveh water. Many poskim, including the Rash, Rashba, and Ritva, write that this case is also comparable to the hot springs of Tiberias. If the water was diverted into a hollow that does not contain forty seah, it is invalid for immersion according to all opinions, because it is likewise unfit for a dog's drinking, as explained above. The later authorities have similarly demonstrated that, according to the Shulchan Aruch, the two laws are identical. Nevertheless, if it remains connected to the water of the spring or to a mikveh containing forty seah, one may rely leniently on the view of Rabbeinu Yonah cited above, since according to the Rif and Rambam it appears to be permitted in every circumstance, as the Gra explains from the Rif's text. Eliyah Rabbah likewise rules leniently.
If a dog can, etc. The Gemara in Zevachim 22 states, 'if a cow bends and drinks from it,' which would appear to imply that even if a dog can drink, so long as a cow cannot drink it, the water is invalid for netilas yadayim. This requires study. Peri Megadim.
