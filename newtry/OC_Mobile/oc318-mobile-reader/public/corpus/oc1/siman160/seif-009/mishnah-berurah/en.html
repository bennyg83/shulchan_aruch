Salty water. Sea water, if one boiled it, returns to being fit for a dog to drink and is valid for washing; Magen Avraham and other authorities in the name of the Radbaz. To immerse in it is always permitted, as with all salty water.
That the dog cannot, etc. Since it is so spoiled that even a dog cannot drink from it, it is as if it has been nullified from the status of water, and the Sages invalidated it for netilas yadayim; later authorities.
For mikveh water for immersion. The same applies to immersion of hands, for it is no better than immersion; Gemara.
If the dog can, etc. Meaning, that it is not so thick. See Chullin, chapter Kol HaBasar, in Divrei Chamudos, letter 38.
They are valid. They should not be invalidated because of change of appearance, for this is the way of water, as above in letter 3 in the Mishnah Berurah.
