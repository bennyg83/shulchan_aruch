Some invalidate. From the reason that they derive from mei chatat: whatever bird drank from them except the dove is invalid, because they raise their mouths and what they absorbed from the water falls from their mouths — behold, they are like water in which melacha was done; such is Rashi's opinion. But Tosafot hold melacha is not applicable to drinking. Terumat HaDeshen wrote that even per Rashi he only invalidated mei chatat; but for netilat yadayim they are nullified in the minority. Beit Yosef further challenged: mei chatat was stringent in many matters; therefore he wrote here that it does not seem to invalidate; and likewise Rashal, chapter 25.**** END BLOCK ****
