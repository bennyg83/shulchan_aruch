The chickens or lapped, etc. According to what is explained in the Beit Yosef, these are not specific; the same applies to every domestic animal, wild animal, and bird, except a dove.
There is one who invalidates. For we compare it to the water of the Parah Adumah, which becomes invalid when a domestic animal, wild animal, or bird drinks from it, except a dove. Some explain the reason there as because of labor, for when they raise their heads, water drips from their mouths, and this water is considered like water with which labor was performed, since it was used for drinking, and it invalidates all the water through its mixture. The same applies to netilas yadayim, where labor invalidates water.
And his words do not appear correct. For specifically with the Parah Adumah, any amount of labor invalidates; also, all the early authorities hold that it is not considered labor even regarding the Parah Adumah, and they explained other reasons there that do not apply here. Besides this, regarding netilas yadayim, all the water is not invalidated through this, for it is nullified in the majority mixture, as below.
Rather, whether, etc., one should validate. The later authorities distinguish in this matter: regarding chickens, and likewise other wild animals and birds, they are valid, like the view of the Mechaber, as above. But if a dog or pig drank from it, it is invalid because of disgust, for the water becomes disgusting for drinking and becomes like waste water. The Chayei Adam wrote that in pressing circumstances one may rely on the view of the Mechaber to permit even these. If a snake drank from it, one may not wash the hands with it. But regarding merely exposed water, nowadays we are not concerned that perhaps a snake drank from it, since they are not common. Even one who is careful about this for drinking need not be stringent regarding washing; Bach. Invalid water that became mixed with valid water is nullified by a minority and completes even a revi'is, if its appearance did not change; later authorities.
