Which a dog licked from. Bach writes that one should be stringent if a dog or pig drank from the water, because the water becomes repugnant and is like waste water. Terumas HaDeshen writes likewise in the name of Or Zarua and Rabbeinu Chananel, and Mordechai writes likewise in the name of the Tosefta. Had Beit Yosef seen these sources, he would not have disagreed. Rambam writes at the end of Hilchos Mikvaos: If a small amount of water invalid for netilas yadayim was added to valid water, the mixture remains valid. If one then removed from the vessel the amount that had been added, leaving only the original reviis, it is invalid, because the invalid water had contributed to completing the required measure. His reason is the statement in the second chapter of Zevachim, page 22: “Everything that completes,” etc., excluding the case in which one added a seah and removed a seah, etc. Bedek HaBayis. Rashi appears to hold that this applies specifically where there was exactly a reviis, since the small measure of a reviis is not significant enough to nullify the invalid water. Rambam appears to hold likewise. From Beit Yosef’s citation of this ruling here in Bedek HaBayis, it appears that he holds that it was stated regarding ordinary food. In my view, however, since the poskim omitted it, they apparently hold that it was stated only regarding terumah. Rambam likewise did not record it in Hilchos Berachos. Terumas HaDeshen also writes that the invalid water is nullified in the minority.
