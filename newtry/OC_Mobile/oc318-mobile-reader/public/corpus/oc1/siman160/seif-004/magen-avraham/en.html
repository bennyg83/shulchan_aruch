A dog licked from them. Bach wrote that one should be stringent if a dog or pig drank from them, for they are repugnant and are like pourings; and likewise Terumat HaDeshen in the name of Or Zaru'a and R' Chananel; and likewise Mordechai in the name of the Tosefta. And if Ra'avy saw this, he did not argue on it. Rambam wrote at the end of Mikvaot: valid water into which a small amount of water invalid for netilat yadayim was placed — behold, they are valid for netilat yadayim. One took from the vessels the measure that was placed, and only a revi'it remained as it was — this is invalid, because the invalid water completed the measure. The reason: as we say in chapter 2 of Zevachim 22, "all who complete," etc., to exclude "one gave a seah and took a seah," etc. — it appears from Rashi, s.v. there, specifically when there was a revi'it in concentration, for the small measure of a revi'it is not considered to nullify them; and such is Rambam's opinion. And from what Ra'avy wrote in Beit Yosef, peh — it appears he holds it was stated regarding chullin; but in my humble opinion, since the poskim omitted it, it appears he holds it was stated specifically regarding terumah; and such is Rambam, who did not write them in Hilchot Berachot; and likewise Terumat HaDeshen, that they are nullified in the minority.**** END BLOCK ****
