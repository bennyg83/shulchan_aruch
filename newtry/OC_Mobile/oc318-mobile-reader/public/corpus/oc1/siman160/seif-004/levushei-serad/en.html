Note 5. To invalidate because of melacha. It seems per Rashi's opinion that he holds that water from which chickens drank is considered melacha; this is brought in Taz note 5, and the poskim disagree with him and say that such a minor act is not considered melacha for netilat yadayim; only for mei chatat they were stringent regarding a minor act, as brought in Beit Yosef. If so, per Rashi who holds that even for netilat yadayim even a minor act is considered melacha, it fits well with what Shlah wrote — that it is in any case considered a minor act; not so for us, who hold that a minor act does not invalidate netilat yadayim.
