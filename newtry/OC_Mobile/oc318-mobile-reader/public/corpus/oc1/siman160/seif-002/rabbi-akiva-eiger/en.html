Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. It became wastewater. If one performed work using another person’s water, one might say that although the established law is that a person can prohibit something belonging to another through an act, nevertheless the reason here is that he has rendered it wastewater. He has no authority to designate another person’s water as wastewater, and it does not thereby become wastewater for the owner. This requires practical study.
