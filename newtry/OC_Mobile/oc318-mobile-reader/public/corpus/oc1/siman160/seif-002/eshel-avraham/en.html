This is obvious according to Levush’s reason that water used for work is invalid because it is impossible that its appearance did not change, for here its appearance did not change. Even according to Rashi and Rambam, who explain that work removes the designation of water and renders it wastewater, that applies only when an actual operation was performed with the water.
