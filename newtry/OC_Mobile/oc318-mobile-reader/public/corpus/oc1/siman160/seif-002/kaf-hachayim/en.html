What the Rambam wrote there, that uncovered water is disqualified for netilat yadayim — the Tur wrote in this siman that nowadays, since we are not concerned for exposure, it is valid even for netilah, and Maran agreed in Beit Yosef — see there; and likewise Levush, seif 5. It appears that where possible one should be careful as with drinking l'chatchila; see Yoreh De'ah at the start of siman 116 and the later authorities there.
