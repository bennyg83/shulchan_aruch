Levush: And there are those who validate, etc. In the Shulchan Aruch it is written that they validate, even if they were not nullified, because since it is not fit for drinking, it is like labor was done with it, as the Beit Yosef wrote. But the Levush holds that the main view is like the Terumas HaDeshen, siman 260, cited by the Beit Yosef, who distinguishes between whether they were nullified, etc.; and according to the first view, even if they were nullified it is forbidden, as brought in the Terumas HaDeshen there. The Olas Tamid challenged the Levush for no reason. However, as a matter of law, it appears to me that with a dog, pig, and the like, which are disgusting to most people, it is like waste water and is invalid, and they are not nullified by a minority. So too it is implied in the Bach and Magen Avraham. But if chickens drank from them, or if he immersed a sponge in them, it is valid even if they were not nullified by a minority. The Rambam wrote at the end of Mikvaos that water invalid for netilas yadayim does not complete the measure of a revi'is. The Magen Avraham wrote that this is specifically with respect to terumah. So too the Terumas HaDeshen wrote that they are nullified by a minority. One can reject this, for the Terumas HaDeshen speaks where it fell into a revi'is of valid water. However, in Lechem Mishneh, chapter 6 of Hilchos Berachos, he also wrote that they complete the measure for ordinary food.
While attached, etc. The reason is that labor invalidates only because it becomes disgusting for drinking and is like waste water, and while attached it did not become disgusting for drinking; Bach. According to this, based on the reasons I mentioned in seif katan 3, even when attached it should apparently be forbidden. It also requires study, for the mishnah taught simply that if one performed labor it is forbidden, and in that same mishnah it taught simply the law of changed appearance and the law of becoming invalid for animal drinking, and in those cases even when attached it is invalid, as the Taz wrote; this requires study.
Levush: exposed water, etc. The Olas Tamid wrote that it is implied from the Levush that if we saw that a snake drank from it, it is invalid, and he was not precise, for the Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 4 that with any domestic animal, wild animal, or bird one should validate. Perhaps because of danger it is invalid. I say that it is obvious that it is forbidden because of danger, for in Levush, Yoreh Deah, siman 116, he wrote that even now, if one sees that a snake or creeping creature drank from it, it is invalid for drinking. Furthermore, the Levush does not hold like the Shulchan Aruch, as I explained above.
Levush: their form, etc. I have already written that this does not depend on its form changing; rather, the reason is that he nullified it from the status of water.
