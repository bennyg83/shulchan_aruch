Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah there; Yad Aharon in Hagahot HaTur; Peri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1; Chayei Adam, klal 39, paragraph 1; Derech HaChaim, paragraph 1; Ben Ish Chai, parashas Kedoshim, paragraph 1. Machatzis HaShekel at the end of paragraph 1, however, writes that if valid water later becomes available, one should wash again without a blessing; see there. Since all the aforementioned later authorities agree with Magen Avraham, there is no need to be concerned for Machatzis HaShekel's position except initially, if other water can easily be found. If no other water is available, one should wash and recite the blessing. If he already washed, he need not wash again.
