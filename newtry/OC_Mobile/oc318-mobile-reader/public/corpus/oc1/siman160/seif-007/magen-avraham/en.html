A trench. This means a trench in the ground, whose water is not considered drawn. See siman 159, seif 7.
It does not contain the required measure. Even according to those who validate such water in siman 159, seif 14, here it is invalid, because we decree concerning a trench on account of a vessel. So write Levush and the Gemara, contrary to Olas Tamid.
One may not immerse. We decree because of a vessel. Rabbeinu Yonah holds that the decree applies specifically where the water is disconnected from the ground and therefore invalid for immersion of the body. If one were permitted to immerse his hands there, he might also come to wash his hands from it. But if the water is connected to the ground, so that one may immerse his entire body in it, all the more so he may immerse his hands, and there is no basis for a decree.
