Seif 7, hot waters. There, and as the Rif and Rosh emend: Chizkiya said one may not take from them for the hands, and R' Yehudah said the entire body may immerse in them — all the more so the hands. And the Rosh wrote in the first chapter of Yadayim that thus emended R' Chananel and R' Gershom, and he was forced to this, for otherwise R' Yehudah would contradict the stam mishnah in the first chapter of Yadayim, "The waters…," and R' Yehudah said the halacha follows the stam mishnah everywhere. And the Rif explained like Chizkiya, for R' Yehudah was his teacher; and even though he holds like a lone opinion, we follow the amoraim. See Terumat HaDeshen; and it requires study regarding the Rambam, who explained in refuah 6 of Shabbat like Rav and Shmuel — see in the Kaf HaChayim there (and see in seif 9). And Rashi there, s.v. "for we ruled them," etc. — and there is no [need for what the Tur wrote in the Shulchan Aruch]. But [the Tur wrote] "And if one drew," etc., "if there is," etc., "and if," etc., "and if," etc. — and likewise in Terumat HaDeshen: if they are not connected, nevertheless one may not take, since there is no measure of immersion — we require netilat yadayim, for we require a vessel. And likewise Tosafot there, s.v. "for we ruled them," etc. Alternatively… And Terumat Yisrael wrote that it was necessary to explain when there are not forty seah in them, for otherwise the question would arise: "The entire body may immerse in them," etc., as stated in its place, etc. And it was necessary to explain when they are connected, for otherwise how could R' Yehudah say "the entire body," etc.? Rather, in this case they disagree: Chizkiya decreed — and there is no need now for "his entire body," etc. — for granted regarding the body there is no need; but is a vessel not [different]? Not so here, that he would see that it is permitted to immerse his hands — he would not say it is because of mixture; rather, that there is no prohibition in Tiberias waters at all. But R' Yehudah wrote that we ruled them completely — meaning, as the first explanation in Tosafot there. And now Chizkiya decreed lest he see that there are not forty seah in them and no mixture, and nevertheless it is permitted in a vessel; and R' Yehudah said "the entire body," etc. — not in them literally, but in those waters when there are forty seah. And see in siman 159, seif 4.
