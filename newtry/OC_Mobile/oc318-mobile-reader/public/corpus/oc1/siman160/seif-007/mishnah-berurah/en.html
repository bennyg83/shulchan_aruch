To immerse in them. Like all springs, for the hot springs of Tiberias are a spring. This is so even if they do not contain forty se'ah, as above in siman 159, seif 14.
But to wash from them, etc. Even from a large spring that has forty se'ah.
With a vessel, no. Even if they cooled, it is forbidden, because they are not fit for a dog to drink, as below.
Through a trench. In the ground. The same applies if it traveled through a pipe, if it does not have the status of a vessel; Peri Megadim.
And he separated them. From the spring, for then the status of a spring is removed from it, and it is only a mikveh.
One may immerse in them. For now that his whole body can immerse in them, all the more so his hands.
And if they do not contain, etc. Even according to those who validate above in siman 159, seif 14, with less than forty se'ah even in a mikveh, here regarding the hot springs of Tiberias they agree that we decree: if we come to immerse hands where he separated them from the spring, people will say that the waters of the hot springs of Tiberias are like other water and will come to wash from them even with a vessel.
One may not immerse. For here too they decreed lest one come to wash with them also from a vessel, since he separated them from their place and they do not themselves contain forty se'ah. The opinion of Rabbeinu Yonah is that since they are connected to the spring water and are valid even for immersion of his body, the decree lest one come to wash with them from a vessel no longer applies.
