Note 10. And even for those who are lenient — meaning, they are lenient even in a mikveh that does not have forty seah; here, since the waters are hot, we decree lest [one confuse] a vessel; or it is possible that because they are hot it applies to decree lest a vessel, even when they have cooled, since at the beginning of their creation they were hot. However, the author in the next seif wrote the reason that he emends per Rashba and Rabbenu Yonah; and wonder at what Taz brought in note 8 from Rashal. And one could say his intent is to be stringent, unlike the author in seif 8 who explains that in every dispute regarding netilat yadayim they are lenient; and perhaps his intent is to rely on Rashi to be lenient regarding cold and hot springs like Tiberias hot waters, for Rashi did not decree lest a vessel. One need not elaborate here; understand well.
