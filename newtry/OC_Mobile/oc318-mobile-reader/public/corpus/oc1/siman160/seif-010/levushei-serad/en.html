Seif katan 10 “Even according to those who validate” means those who validate even a mikveh that does not contain forty seah. Here, however, since the water is bitter, we decree because of a vessel. Alternatively, because the water is hot, the decree because of a vessel applies even after it cooled, since it was naturally hot at its source. The Mechaber, however, in the following seif gives the reason according to the reading of Rashba and Rabbeinu Yonah. It is somewhat difficult why Taz, in seif katan 8, cited the words of Maharshal; examine this. One cannot say that Taz intends to rule stringently against the Mechaber in seif 8, for in every dispute concerning netilas yadayim we rule leniently. Perhaps his intent is to rely on Rashi leniently regarding cold and bitter springs resembling the hot springs of Tiberias, because according to Rashi we do not decree in such a case because of a vessel. This is not the place to elaborate. Examine carefully.
