Whose beginning is from water. Meaning, the beginning of its creation is from water.
Red larvae. That were formed from the water, and there is no issue here of change of appearance, for the essence of the beginning of their growth was this way.
If he crushed them, etc. The Magen Avraham in siman 162 wrote that in this case it is more stringent than snow, for even to immerse the hands in them one should be stringent if he did not crush them, although with snow it is permitted to immerse in it in pressing circumstances, as below in seif 12 in the Mishnah Berurah; see there.
