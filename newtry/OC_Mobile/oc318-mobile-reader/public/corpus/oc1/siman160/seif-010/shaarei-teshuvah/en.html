Radbaz writes that if he lacks a perforated vessel with which to proceed as explained in siman 159, seif katan 10, and if he can boil the seawater, seawater is also preferable to earth because it cleanses and may be used for washing. We are not concerned that observers will err and conclude that seawater is generally fit for washing the hands. See there. Nevertheless, it appears that he should also wrap his hands in a cloth, as stated in siman 158, seif katan 15.
