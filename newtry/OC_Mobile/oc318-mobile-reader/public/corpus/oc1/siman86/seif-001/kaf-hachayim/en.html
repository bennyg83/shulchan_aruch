(1) [Section 1] Foul water, etc. It is written in Sefer Tosafot Yerushalayim in the name of the Yerushalmi of Dabravsky: It is forbidden to pray and mention anything sacred when the work has begun that has a bad smell—even the craftsman himself, if he is accustomed to it, whenever people are distressed by it. And they cited it in Piskei Teshuvos in siman 85, letter gimel.
