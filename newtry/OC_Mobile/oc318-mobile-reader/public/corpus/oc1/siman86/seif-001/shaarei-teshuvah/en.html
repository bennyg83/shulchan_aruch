In the Shulchan Aruch: Foul water, etc. See Beit Yosef's discussion of Rambam's Commentary on the Mishnah. Knesset HaGedolah writes in his responsa, siman 26, that the inference is not conclusive; see there and in Shiyurei Berachah. That responsum rules that if foul, putrid water is channeled into a garden adjoining a synagogue, the garden owner must distance it even if the garden preceded the synagogue, because this is a matter of religious prohibition; see there.
