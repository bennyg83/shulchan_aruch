In the Shulchan Aruch, foul water, etc. See in the Beit Yosef what the commentators on Rambam wrote in his commentary on the Mishnah, and the Knesset HaGedolah in his responsa, siman 26, wrote that there is no obligation—see there and in Shiyurei Berachah. And he wrote in the aforementioned responsum: A garden into which foul and bad water flows, and it is adjacent to a beit kenesset—even if the garden owner preceded [in establishing the garden], one must distance, for it is a matter of prohibition—see there.
