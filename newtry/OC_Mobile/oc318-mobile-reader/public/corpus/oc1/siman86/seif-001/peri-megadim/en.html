What the Mechaber wrote in seif 6 concerning foul water, etc., is from the Mishnah in Berachos 22b. One must distance himself from it in front as far as he can see, and behind him four amos, as with excrement, and it is therefore prohibited on a Torah level. Urine has the same law; see what I wrote in siman 87, with Hashem’s help. See Eliyah Rabbah on seif 6, where the correct reading is that in wine cellars having a foul odor, such as a musty smell, if one is repulsed by it, it is prohibited to recite Krias Shema there; see there. Regarding mikvaos whose water becomes foul in the summer, not only is it prohibited to recite a blessing inside them, but one must even distance himself from them as from excrement, as explained in Yoreh Deah 282:19. Thinking Torah thoughts in filthy alleyways is prohibited because of the honor of the Torah. This requires study, for Shabbos 150a derives this from “your camp shall be holy,” which is a Torah law. This presently requires study.
