What the author wrote in siman 86, foul water, etc.—the mishnah in Berachos 22b; and that is, before him as far as his eyes can see, and behind him four amos, like excrement; and therefore it is forbidden. And urine—their law is thus; and see above in siman 87, with God's help. And see Eliyah Rabbah in siman 86, and there he wrote in wine cellars that smell bad (moldy and the like) and disgust one, it is forbidden to read Kerias Shema there—see there. Those mikvaos whose water becomes foul in the summer months—it seems to me that it is forbidden to recite a berachah within them; even distancing as from excrement we require, as explained in Yoreh De'ah, responsa 282, siman 69. Thought in filthy alleyways is forbidden because of honor of the Torah; and it requires study, for in Shabbos 150a, "educate him in holiness" is from the Torah—and it requires study now.
