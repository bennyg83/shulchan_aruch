To add. Because the water is not improved thereby. And it appears to me that if he does so so that the pot not crack, it is permitted (Magen Avraham); see siman 507, se'if 5, and siman 514, se'if 1. And Levush wrote: And as for candles, the custom is to kindle from dark onward, even before Barkhu; and Or Zarua wrote that women should recite the blessing before they go to the synagogue; and so wrote the Shelah, that it is a greater mitzvah than the custom of kindling when they come home, when they are sitters in darkness; end quote. And in the synagogue the custom is to kindle even while it is still day, for in the synagogue it is always a mitzvah, even by day (Levush).
