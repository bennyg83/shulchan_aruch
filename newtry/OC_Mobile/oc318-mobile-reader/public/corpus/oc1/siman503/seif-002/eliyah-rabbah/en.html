[2] [Levush] He improves more, etc. — specifically in cooking but not in roasting (Maharil), and it appears specifically meat and not other dishes like water at the end of this siman, and Semak wondered — Beit Yosef: it appears to me it refers to other dishes, and Beit Yosef and all the great ones strained over his words; for what I explained, his words fit their plain sense — somewhat to align with Shaarei of Knesset HaGedolah that they have no explanation to examine; also what he wrote that Maharil likewise divided is wondrous, and I abbreviated. And in responsa R' Yom Tov Tzahlin he expanded to wonder on Rivash and Ran chapter 2 of Beitzah who wrote the reason of improving more — behold anyway it is permitted because of one effort as Rashi explained [Beitzah] daf 17, and he answered he found in Shelah that for the reason of improving more, even if he said explicitly for tomorrow's need — end his words briefly. And one may wonder on him that he did not see in Tur from which Shelah copied, and further that truly it is explained in Rokeach that Beit Yaakov brought that even for the reason of improving more [it is forbidden] explicitly, and so Rashal and Bach and Acharonim forbid; however b'dieved it appears that for everyone it is permitted, and mainly his question should have been answered that therefore they wrote the reason of improving more — for even after he set the pot on the fire there is another effort, permitted to add meat as Levush wrote at the end of this siman, and it is plain that also Rashi who explained the reason of effort is for the first tanna but he agrees to Rabbi that, etc. [text continues per source]:
