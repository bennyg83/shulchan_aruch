1) [Se'if 1] It is forbidden to bake or cook, etc. It is forbidden to prepare on one day of Yom Tov for the next even something that does not involve a melachah. This is certainly so nowadays, when we are expert in determining the new month, so that the second day of Yom Tov is a weekday relative to the first and preparation is forbidden. Magen Avraham, at the beginning of the siman. Nevertheless, regarding something that entails no melachah, it appears that having a non-Jew perform it is permitted. Eshel Avraham, at the beginning of the siman; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 13, Likutei Rima, subsection 3.
