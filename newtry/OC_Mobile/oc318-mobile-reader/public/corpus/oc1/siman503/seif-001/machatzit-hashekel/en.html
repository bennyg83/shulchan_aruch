Magen Avraham wrote: It is forbidden, etc. Even something that is not a melacha, etc.: That is to say, even though in the Shulchan Aruch prohibition was mentioned only for something that is a melacha, baking and cooking and the like, Magen Avraham teaches us that even something that is not a melacha but mere effort is likewise forbidden, as he wrote in siman 667 that it is forbidden to bring wine from one Yom Tov to another.
And Maharil forbids searching the Sefer Torah and rolling it to the place that tomorrow they will read; it is forbidden from the first day of Yom Tov to the second. And see siman 447, that even speech for the need of the second day of Yom Tov is forbidden; for it is stated there regarding one who mixes with his feet, namely during twilight of Shabbos he stands in the place where he wants to acquire residence, and says, "Here shall be my residence."
And if he mixes with his feet on the two days of Yom Tov of the diaspora in one direction. Even though he stood during twilight of the first [day] at the place of residence, nevertheless also during twilight of the second day he must go to the place of residence; but he must say, "Here shall be my residence," and we hold that it is forbidden for him to say during the second twilight, "Here shall be my residence."
Even though he does not need that speech. Nevertheless it appears as preparing from the first day of Yom Tov for the second.
