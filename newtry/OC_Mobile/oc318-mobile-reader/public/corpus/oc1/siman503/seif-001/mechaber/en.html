Not to prepare on one Yom Tov for another. It contains 2 seifim: It is forbidden to bake, cook, or slaughter on Yom Tov for the needs of the next day, even if it is Shabbos or Yom Tov, and even on the 2 days of Rosh Hashana. But a woman may fill a pot with meat even though she needs only one piece. {Rama: And all the more so may one slaughter even though he needs only a kezayit. [Semak] And likewise she may cook many pots and eat a little from each. [Beit Yosef; Rabbeinu Yeruham; and a responsum of Rashba]} And this is specifically before eating; but after eating she may not cook and say that she will eat a kezayit from it, because that is a ruse. Nevertheless, if she transgressed and cooked [or slaughtered], it is permitted to eat it.
