In Shulchan Aruch, seif 1: all the more so. All the more so, he may slaughter, etc. Machazik Berachah writes concerning Shavuos that fell on Wednesday and Thursday. After most of the community had already eaten, but before the shochet himself had eaten his Yom Tov meal, the shochet slaughtered two calves in a slaughterhouse belonging to non-Jews where animals were slaughtered for Jewish needs. The shochet and his household ate a kezayis from each calf during their Yom Tov meal. He had seen earlier shochatim act in this manner, and he did so in order that meat would be available for Shabbos, because otherwise the non-Jews would have killed the calves without shechitah. Responsa Zera Emes writes that he did not act properly. He disputes Be’er Eisek, who permits slaughtering on the second day of Pesach after eating for the needs of Chol HaMoed whenever the shochet eats a kezayis from the animal. Zera Emes calls this ruling astonishing. Nevertheless, he concludes that after the fact the meat should not be prohibited. See there.
