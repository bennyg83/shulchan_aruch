Seif 62: If in the middle [of prayer], etc. As stated there, Berachos 22b: The Tanna taught, “There was [a case], etc., and water [dripping on his knees], etc.” — thus he does not interrupt to go to another place but only up to [four cubits ahead], and he prays in his place; and all the more so if he can restrain himself until he finishes. And the same applies to adults:

And if at the time [of Kriat Shema], etc. — that is, to include [the case] even during Kriat Shema; except that here, if he wished to go farther to urinate, it is permitted there [as the Gemara states]:

And specifically [when the urge is not so strong], etc. — for in the Tosefta, chapter Dalet, it is forbidden to restrain oneself [from urinating]; and he distinguished in this that they should not contradict one another; see Magen Avraham:
