Seif 2, If in the middle, etc. As stated there, 22b: The Rabbis taught, if one was, etc., and water, etc. Thus, he does not interrupt to go elsewhere, but rather waits until, etc., and prays in his place. All the more so when he can restrain himself until he finishes. The same applies regarding relieving oneself.
And if at the time of, etc. Meaning, as a greater novelty, even during Krias Shema. Here, however, if he wishes to distance himself in order to urinate, it is permitted, as stated there.
And specifically, etc. For Terumas HaDeshen ruled that it is forbidden to restrain oneself, and distinguished in this manner so that the rulings should not contradict one another. See Magen Avraham.
