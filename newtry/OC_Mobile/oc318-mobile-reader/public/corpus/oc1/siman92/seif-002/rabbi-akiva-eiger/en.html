Magen Avraham note 2: that one must distance oneself all the more. It is difficult to me — why "all the more"? Is not prayer in a place of urine lighter than when one needs his orifices? For in the Gemara: if he prayed, his prayer is prayer, as below siman 85; but when he needs his orifices, his prayer is an abomination. One may also say that in the Gemara, since if he prayed he has fulfilled, therefore when he has already begun it is bedi'eaved and he need not interrupt — not so when he needs his orifices.
