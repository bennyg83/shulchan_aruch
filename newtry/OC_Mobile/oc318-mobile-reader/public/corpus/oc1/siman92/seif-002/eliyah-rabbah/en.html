[2] The measure of walking distance, etc. — This is the measure of one hour (Bach). See above siman 58, note 5, and it requires study. And know that regarding one who needed to relieve himself — the Talmud and poskim included all his orifices in the meaning, both large and small, as implied in seif 3; and this is also explained in the responsum of Rashba in siman 131, and likewise wrote in the responsum of Rama, siman 98, and Ma'adanei Melech page 24. And one might wonder about Magen Avraham, who wrote plainly that one need not return for small [needs], and he omitted the large ones mentioned above. Also the proof from the Talmud [Berachos] 23b that he cited is not precise — for there one did not need to relieve himself before prayer but rather during prayer.
