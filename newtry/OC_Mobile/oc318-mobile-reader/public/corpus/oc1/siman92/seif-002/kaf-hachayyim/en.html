2) There: If he needed to relieve himself, he should not pray, etc. Sefer Chasidim 821 states: 'Prepare to meet your God, O Israel' means that one should not come to need the privy while the congregation is going to the synagogue, so that he may pray with them. If circumstances left him no opportunity to relieve himself beforehand, it is improper to go to the synagogue while needing to do so, for it says, 'Bless Hashem, my soul, and all my innards, His holy Name.' Eliyah Rabbah 1 cites this.
