[2] There: If he needed to relieve himself he should not pray, etc. — And thus it is written in Shulchan Chassidim siman 821: "Prepare to meet your God, O Israel" — that you should not need to attend to your needs at the hour when the congregation goes to synagogue so that you pray with them. And if it happened that he could not do so beforehand because he had no leisure to attend to his needs, it is not proper that he go to synagogue — and he needs to relieve himself, as it says "Bless Hashem, O my soul, and all my inward parts, His holy name" — up to here.
