One who spits, etc.; and specifically, etc. See Chayei Adam, whose words imply that the qualification “and specifically” also refers to the first clause. This is not convincing. Rabbeinu Yonah likewise mentions the leniency of an incidental occurrence only regarding one who spits after prayer.
See siman 97. See Mishnah Berurah. Eliyah Rabbah is uncertain whether immediately after prayer the law may be more stringent, so that it is forbidden in all circumstances until one waits, since the reason of distraction does not apply then. The plain language of the Shulchan Aruch implies leniency in this matter, following Rabbeinu Yonah.
