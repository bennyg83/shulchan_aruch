9) There: if he wishes to distance himself and urinate, he may do so. But for defecation he must restrain himself if he can, and he does not interrupt even during Keriat Shema and its blessings, or even during Pesukei deZimra from Baruch She'amar onward — when he does not feel the urge so strongly that there would be a concern of bal teshaktzu if he restrains himself, even though he could not restrain himself for the measure of a parasang. But if he is in a situation where there would be a concern of bal teshaktzu, he interrupts — whether for urination or defecation — in the middle of Keriat Shema and its blessings. Raz (there). And similarly Mishbetzot Zahav at the beginning of this siman: regarding Keriat Shema, certainly even Magen Avraham agrees that if he feels the urge so strongly that there would be bal teshaktzu, he interrupts in the middle of Keriat Shema — and this is obvious.
