For defecation, however, he must restrain himself if able and should not interrupt even Krias Shema and its blessings, or Pesukei D'Zimrah from Baruch She'amar onward, provided the urge is not so strong that restraining himself violates bal teshaktzu, even though he cannot last a parsah. If bal teshaktzu applies, he interrupts for either urination or defecation during Krias Shema and its blessings. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. Mishbetzos Zahav at the beginning of the siman likewise writes that Magen Avraham certainly agrees that one interrupts Krias Shema for an urge involving bal teshaktzu.
