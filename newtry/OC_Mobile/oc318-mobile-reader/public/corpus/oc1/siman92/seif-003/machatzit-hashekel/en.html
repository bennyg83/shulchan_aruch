(Seif katan 3) He should clean, etc. What relevance does cleaning with a pebble have to hands whose status became uncertain through lapse of attention? Only water purifies, whereas cleaning serves merely to remove dirt. One might therefore have thought that when attention was diverted, cleaning was required as a form of purification.
It must be that perhaps something adhered, etc. Meaning, even when attention was diverted, the reason is not purification. Rather, we are concerned that through the lapse of attention the hands may have become slightly soiled. Cleaning is therefore effective.
