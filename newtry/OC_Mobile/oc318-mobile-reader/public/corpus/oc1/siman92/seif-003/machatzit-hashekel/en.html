(Comment 3) He should clean [his hands], etc. — what relevance does cleaning have to cleaning with a pebble in the hands of one who was distracted, etc.? For only water purifies, but cleaning is not effective for purification, only to remove filth. And regarding distraction, one might have thought the reason for cleaning is on account of purity.

And it should be said: perhaps something is stuck, etc. — meaning that even regarding distraction the reason is not on account of purity, but rather we are concerned that through distraction he became slightly soiled; if so, cleaning is indeed effective.
