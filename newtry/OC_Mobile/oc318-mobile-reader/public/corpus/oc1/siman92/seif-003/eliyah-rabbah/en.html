[3] But ab initio, etc. Olas Tamid wrote in the name of Bach that if he examines himself before prayer, the time of prayer will pass — it is permitted even ab initio, since he can restrain himself for the measure of a parasang — up to here. And he was not precise — for specifically per Rif's text, which the Talmud reads as permitted ab initio, Bach ruled like him; Bach wrote that it deals with when the time of prayer passes. But per the text of Rosh and Rabbeinu Yonah, who forbid — and such we hold — Bach forbids ab initio even if the time of prayer will pass, and this is clear. However, for practical halacha, Magen Avraham wrote that one may rely on Rif in this, and thus it seems to me that I saw in Agudah who ruled like Rif.
