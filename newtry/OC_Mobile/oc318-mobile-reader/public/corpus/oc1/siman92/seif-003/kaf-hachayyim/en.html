3) There: He must repeat the prayer. Pri Chadash 1 rules likewise and writes that this is the view of all the poskim, rejecting Tosafos in chapter Mi SheMeiso, who describe it as a distortion that cannot be corrected. See Maamar Mordechai 2; Levush 1; Soles Belulah in Mekor Chaim 1; Beis Oved 34; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 1; Chayei Adam 3:5. Chayei Adam specifies that only the Shemoneh Esrei must be repeated; for other blessings he has fulfilled his obligation after the fact. Chesed LaAlafim 1, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch 12:3, and Ben Ish Chai, Mishpatim 2, agree. See our words above in siman 76, paragraph 37.
