10) [Se'if 3] Before prayer he must remove his phlegm and mucus, etc. Rashi on Eruvin 99a explains phlegm as moisture detached from the body through illness, called tous, and on Bava Kamma 3b explains mucus as nasal discharge. Tosafos there disagree: phlegm is expelled forcefully from the throat, while mucus leaves the mouth through movement. See Kesef Mishneh, chapter 4 of Hilchos Tefillah, law 10, in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach. Neveh Shalom 2 favors Rashi: phlegm is thick saliva caused by illness or cold, and mucus is thick nasal discharge. See Aruch, entries koach and noa, cited by Yafeh LaLev 5. Yafeh LaLev writes in Yosher Levav 3 that this applies not only before prayer but also before study, as the warnings of Mahari Saruk, disciple of the Arizal, paragraph 54, say: before every study session and prayer one must remove phlegm, mucus, and anything distracting him.
