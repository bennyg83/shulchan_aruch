Because of the droplets, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: This is obvious, and did it need to be stated? Also, why four cubits? Is it worse than flowing water in siman 78, where he waits until it ceases? Rashi explained that the concern is that his garments may become soiled by droplets from his organ. It appears to me that this teaches that after he urinates in streams, there are droplets for the time it takes to walk four cubits. And what was said above, 'until they cease,' is also explained to mean that they cease completely, meaning that he waits from the beginning of his urination until the end. See more on this in Eliyahu Zuta.
