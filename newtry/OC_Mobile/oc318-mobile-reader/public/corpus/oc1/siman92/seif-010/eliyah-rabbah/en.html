[10] Because of [urine] droplets, etc. Malbush Yom Tov wrote: it is obvious and needs to be stated; and further, for the distance of four cubits — and it is less than [the case of] water dripping in siman 78, where one waits until it finishes; and Rashi explained that [one waits] so that clothes not be soiled by the droplets in his urine — up to here. And it seems to me that this teaches us that after he urinated, [one waits for] droplets for the distance of four cubits, and until they finish; and above too they explained that they finish entirely — that is, he waits from the beginning of his urination until the end.
