(36) Prayer — as it is written, "And I, in righteousness, shall behold Your face" (Psalms 17:15). And in some holy congregations the custom was established to give charity when reaching [the verse] "and the strength..." etc., "and You rule over all." And there are places that collect charity during Kerias HaTorah — and this is improper, for they thereby cause people not to hear Kerias HaTorah and the response to Barchu, "Bless the Omnipresent..." etc.
