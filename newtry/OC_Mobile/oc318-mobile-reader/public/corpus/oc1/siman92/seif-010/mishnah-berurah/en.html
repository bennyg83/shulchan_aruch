Prayer. For it is written, “As for me, through righteousness I will behold Your face.” In some holy communities the custom was established to give tzedakah when one reaches the verse, “And wealth,” etc., “and You rule over all.” There are places that customarily collect tzedakah during the Torah reading, and this is improper, for by doing so they prevent people from hearing the Torah reading and from answering “Bless Hashem,” etc.
