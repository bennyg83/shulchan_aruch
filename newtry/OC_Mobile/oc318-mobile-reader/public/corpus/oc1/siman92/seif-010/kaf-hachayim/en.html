[10] [Seif 3] One must before prayer remove his phlegm and mucus, etc. Keicho — Rashi explained in Eruvin 99a: mucus that detaches from the body through illness, which they call "toshe." Nieo — Rashi explained in Bava Kamma 3b: mucus of the spleen. However Tosafos there disagreed with him and wrote that keicho is what comes forcefully from the throat, and nieo is what comes from the mouth through shaking; see there. See what Kesef Mishneh wrote, chapter 4 of Hilchos Tefillah, law 10, in the name of R' Menachem. And Nahar Shalom note 2 wrote that the primary view is like Rashi's explanation — that keicho is thick saliva of the mouth that comes through illness or a cold, and nieo is thick mucus of the nose; see there. See in Aruch, entry "koach" and entry "na," which they cited — Yafeh Lalev note 5; see there. And Yafeh Lalev wrote in Kovetz Yosher Levav note 3 that it is not specifically before prayer, but likewise before study — as so in warnings of Mahari Saruk, disciple of the Ari, may he rest in Eden, note 54: before all study and prayer one must remove keicho, nieo, and anything that disturbs him — up to here:
