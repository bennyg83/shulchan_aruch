Good — Ateret Zekenim. And when one gives a perutah to the ruler — in everything good and beautiful it is fitting; and all the more a poor person should give, for he knows that everything is from God, that he troubles himself and engages in labor and merchandise and has nothing; not so a rich person, Heaven forbid, who sometimes says it is his strength and the might of his hand. And in Sefer HaMagid we elaborated on this.
