[4] There: and one must return and pray — and specifically for large [needs], but for small ones one need not return — Magen Avraham, note 1, subsection 2, in Zochor; Halakhot Ketanot vol. 2 siman 79; Yeshuot Yaakov in Hagahot Tosephta; Habraham, note 1; Alef HaRokeach, note 1; Machatzit HaShekel, note 1. However, Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1-2, wrote that regarding one who needed to relieve himself — the Talmud and poskim included all his orifices in the meaning, both large and small; and he wrote as the Mechaber in seif 3; and similarly explained in the responsum of Rashba siman 131 and in the responsum of Rama siman 98 and Ma'adanei Melech page 24; see there. And similarly Mahari Ayash in Sefer Lechem Yehuda, and they cited it in Nahar Shalom, note 1; and so it appears from the words of the Mechaber, note 1, who wrote "if he needed to relieve himself," etc., both large and small — and it does not resemble what is written below siman 405; see there. However, Ma'amar Mordechai letter 1 wrote to uphold the words of Magen Avraham and challenged Rashba, Rama, and Ma'adanei Melech; see there. And what the Mechaber wrote about it, and note 2 — see the rabbi mentioned in his book Devar Mordechai, vol. 1 page 54a, who upheld his statement and cited the words of Menachem in Hagahot Tosephta, letter 1; and Yafeh Lalev, note 2; see there. And similarly Erech Lechem, letter 1 — even though he cited the words of the responsum of Rashba, Rama, and Ma'adanei Melech, afterward he concluded that one need not return for small ones, and wrote as in Behag; see there.
