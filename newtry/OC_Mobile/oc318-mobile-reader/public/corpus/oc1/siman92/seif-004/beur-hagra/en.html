Seif 4, And one must, etc. This follows the reading of Rashi, Rabbeinu Chananel, Rif, and Rambam, unlike Tosafos.
More than a mil, etc. Beit Yosef wrote that this is Rambam’s reading in the Gemara, and inferred it from his wording, ‘up to a mil,’ but beyond that he need not return. This inference is not compelling, and in Kesef Mishneh he retracted it.
And if he fears, etc. In that case one may rely upon the reading of Tosafos. See there, s.v. Upon whom, etc. The Tur wrote in siman 233 that this applies specifically when he would otherwise miss the time for prayer. According to the reading of the Geonim cited above, however, he must even allow the time for prayer to pass until the stated measure, in the name of Rav Amram. This also appears from Rashi’s explanation.
