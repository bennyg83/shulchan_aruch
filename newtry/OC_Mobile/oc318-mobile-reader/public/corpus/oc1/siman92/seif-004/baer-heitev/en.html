With water. Drying the hands is not indispensable, unlike washing before eating, where drying is indispensable. Bircas Avraham.
A parsah. This is so provided that he will not need to travel alone, in which case he need not be concerned. The measure of a parsah is one hour and one fifth; see Taz. Bach writes: Since, according to all opinions, one need not seek water for Krias Shema, and now one must juxtapose redemption to prayer, one should likewise not seek water for Shacharis. This is our ruling, contrary to Shulchan Aruch. Olas Tamid disagrees and writes that one can wait at the words 'Shirah Chadashah,' as stated in siman 66; see there.
A mil. A full mil is included. Olas Tamid. Peri Chadash writes that only when the water is less than a mil behind him must he return, but for a full mil he need not return; see there.
The prayer. Taz challenges this ruling, because according to the Rif one must wait for water even if the time for prayer will pass; see there. Yad Aharon resolves the difficulty; see there.
With a pebble. This applies whether his hands are soiled or merely because he diverted his attention from guarding them. Tur. See Magen Avraham.
