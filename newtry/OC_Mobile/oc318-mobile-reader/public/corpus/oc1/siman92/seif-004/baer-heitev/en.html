In water. And drying is not an impediment, as it impedes for netilas yadayim for eating. Birchas Avraham.

Parsa. If he will not need to walk alone — 38 [ammos]. And the measure of a parsa is one hour and a fifth; see Taz. And Bach wrote: since one need not return for water for Kerias Shema, for everyone now, since one must adjoin redemption to prayer, one should not return for water for Shacharis prayer either; and thus we rule, unlike Shulchan Aruch, end quote. And Taz disagrees with him and wrote that it is possible to wait with a new song, as written siman 66, see there.

Mil. And a mil is included. Taz. And Peri Chadash wrote: specifically less than a mil one returns, but for a mil one does not return, see there.

The prayer. Taz challenged him, for per the Rif one must wait for water even if the time for prayer passes, see there. And Yad Aharon reconciles it, see there.

In a bundle. It makes no difference whether they are soiled — the language of “that he diverted his attention.” Tur. And see Magen Avraham.
