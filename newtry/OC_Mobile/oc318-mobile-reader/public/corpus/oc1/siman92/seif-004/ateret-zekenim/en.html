One must wash his hands with water, etc. And one must return for water even if the time for prayer passes (Rif, Rambam, and Rashba in the name of Rabbenu Hai); and some say one need not return for water between Kerias Shema and prayer (Tosafos and R' Yitzchak and the Rosh); and some write that even if he knows the time for prayer will not pass, nevertheless he need not return for water — not so at Maariv, since one need not adjoin redemption to prayer; then it is fine if he is stringent and returns for water both before and after (my teacher, in Bach).
