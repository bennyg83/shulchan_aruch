To wash. For the reason for this, see above at the beginning of siman 4 in the Mishnah Berurah. Also for Minchah and Maariv one needs washing, as below in siman 233, seif 2. The Rambam’s opinion is that where one must wash his hands and did not wash, and also did not clean them with a pebble, even after the fact his prayer is not a prayer. This requires study in practice (Peri Megadim). See Biur Halacha, where we explained that he does not repeat the prayer.
With water. Drying does not prevent validity here as it prevents validity in washing for eating (Beis Avraham).
He must. Specifically when he knows they are dirty, but with ordinary hands the Mechaber is lenient below in seif 5; see there.
To go back, etc. If because of this he will later need to travel alone, or because of this he will be unable to reach his destination while it is still day, he need not go back at all.
Traveling on the road. One who rides has the same law in all this as one who walks on foot. There is also no distinction between a bad road and a good road. All this is specifically when he knows with certainty that he will find water there, but not in a case of doubt.
Behind him. All the more so when he is sitting in his house, where he certainly is initially obligated to go back after water if he knows that his hands are dirty.
Up to a mil. Many poskim maintain that a mil itself has the law of more than a mil.
The time for prayer. Entirely. If he is in a place where there are ten people praying, and if he goes back after water the time for prayer with the congregation will pass, the Bach wrote that even if he has time, he need not go back, and he should clean with something that cleans.
He should clean his hands. Meaning, he should not then go back after water, lest the time pass. All the more so regarding Krias Shema, where it is certainly forbidden for him to wait for water if there is concern that the time may pass; rather, he should clean and read. There are many poskim who maintain that for Krias Shema, immediately when the beginning of its time arrives, if he has no water he should clean and read, and not wait for water.
