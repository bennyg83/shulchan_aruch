Need to wash, etc. — It is astonishing, for the Rif ruled, and as Beit Yosef wrote, there is a distinction between before him and after him regarding water; and according to him one must wait for water even if the time of prayer passes, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Ra'avad. And what the Gemara said — that anything that cleans helps — is specifically for Kerias Shema, but not for prayer. So why did he conclude here that if he fears time will pass, etc., he should clean, etc.? This is the opinion of Tosafos and Tur. And one could say he adopts the stringency of both — this is not so; on the contrary, for the Rif there is a stringency: that it is forbidden to pray until he has water.
