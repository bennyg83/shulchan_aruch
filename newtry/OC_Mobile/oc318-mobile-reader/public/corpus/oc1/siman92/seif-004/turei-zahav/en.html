He must wash, etc. This is difficult, for the Rif rules, as cited by Beit Yosef, that there is a distinction between water ahead of him and water behind him. According to the Rif, one must wait for water even if the time for prayer will pass, as Beit Yosef writes in the name of Ra'avad. The Gemara's statement that any cleansing substance is effective applies specifically to Krias Shema, but not to prayer. Why, then, does the Mechaber conclude here that if he fears the time will pass, he should clean his hands with any cleansing substance? That is the view of Tosafos and the Tur. One cannot answer that he adopts the stringencies of both views, because the opposite is true: according to the Rif there is a stringency that one may not pray until water is available.
