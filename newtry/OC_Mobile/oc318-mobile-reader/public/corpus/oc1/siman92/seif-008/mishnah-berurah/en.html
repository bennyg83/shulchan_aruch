One who urinates. Meaning, into a vessel, and the vessel was removed, and similar cases where it is not forbidden to pray because of the urine itself.
That he should wait the time, etc. After four amos, the Sages estimated that the drops in his organ are finished within his garment.
The time of walking. Meaning, the time it takes to walk four amos. But after he waited this measure, it is permitted to urinate even in the place where he prayed. Nevertheless, as a measure of piety one should not urinate within four amos of prayer. Sefer Chasidim, siman 828, wrote that one should not urinate or spit in a place where he covered the blood of a wild animal or bird with a blessing while the blood remains covered.
