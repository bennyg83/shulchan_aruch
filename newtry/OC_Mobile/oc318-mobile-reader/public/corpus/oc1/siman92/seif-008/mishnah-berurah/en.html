(31) One who urinates — that is, into a vessel and the vessel is removed; and all such cases where it is not forbidden to pray on account of the urine itself.

(32) That he wait the time, etc. — and afterward (another matter): the Sages estimated that the sparks in his urine are absorbed in his clothing.

(33) The time of walking — that is, the time of a four-mil walk; but after he waited this measure, it is permitted to urinate even in the place where he prayed. Nevertheless, it is the practice of piety not to urinate within four mil of prayer. Sha'arei Chayyim wrote in siman 428 that one should not urinate or spit in a place where one covered the blood of a living creature or fowl in a blessing while the blood is still covered.
