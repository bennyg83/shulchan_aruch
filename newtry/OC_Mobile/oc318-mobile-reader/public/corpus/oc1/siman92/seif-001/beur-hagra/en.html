Seif 1: And if he prayed, etc. — there.

And he must, etc. — Rambam and Tur and Tosafos 22b, s.v. afa'al, and R' Yitzchak explained, etc.; and so it is apparent from what is stated “his prayer is prayer” — from inference at the beginning, not so it appears.

He fulfilled after the fact — as the textual reading in our books: Rabbeinu Yonah, Rosh, and Rambam.

But at the outset, etc. — as the Rashba stated, as R' Yitzchak who needs [to relieve himself], etc.; and one hundred ammos; and even though he can rely on himself — Rabbeinu Yonah; and also per the reading “his prayer is prayer” — inference that at the outset it is not so; but in Yerushalmi chapter 2, folio 40, that at the outset it is permitted.
