[1] [Seif 1] If he needed to relieve himself he should not pray, etc. — And if he guards his orifices that he not pray until he relieves himself first — it goes without saying that he receives reward for having been careful not to stand before Him, blessed be He, in prayer in a body full of feces and filth of urine; rather further he receives reward for having adorned himself to stand before Him in a clean and dignified body without any filth — as a person who adorns himself to wear dignified garments when standing before the king; so too he adorns himself with a clean body, which is also a garment for the soul when standing in prayer before Him, blessed be He. And this too is included in "Prepare to meet your God, O Israel" — which means adorning oneself before Him, blessed be He. Bach:
