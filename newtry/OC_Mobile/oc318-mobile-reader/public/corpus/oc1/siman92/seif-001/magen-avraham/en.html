And he must repeat. This applies specifically to relieving oneself of feces, but after urinating he need not repeat the prayer. This appears from the Gemara on page 23 and from the Rosh; see siman 78. The Bach writes that even if the time for prayer will pass while he relieves himself, he may not pray until he does so, even if he can restrain himself for the distance of a parsah. In my view, however, it is possible that in such a case one may rely on the Rif, who permits prayer initially when one can restrain himself for a parsah. This also appears from siman 80.
