And he must return. And this is specifically for defecation; but for urination he need not return, as our master taught in the Gemara on page 23 and in Rosh on siman 78. The Bach wrote: even though when he relieves himself the time for prayer will pass, it is forbidden to pray until he relieves himself, even if he can restrain himself for a parasang, etc. And it seems to me that perhaps in this one may rely on the Rif, who permits ab initio if one can restrain oneself for a parasang — and similarly in siman 80.
