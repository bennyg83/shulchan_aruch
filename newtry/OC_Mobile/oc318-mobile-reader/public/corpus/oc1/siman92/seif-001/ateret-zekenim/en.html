If he needed to relieve himself through his orifices, etc.: the measure of walking a parsa, and the measure of a parsa is one hour and a fifth of an hour. The reason is that the time for prayer is an hour slightly before it and slightly after it, for every person must dwell there — behold, the fifth [of an hour] is for the two “slightly before” and “slightly after”; and even if the time for prayer passes, nevertheless it is forbidden to pray until he cleans his body first (Bach, per the words of the Rambam, the Rosh, and R' Yitzchak).
