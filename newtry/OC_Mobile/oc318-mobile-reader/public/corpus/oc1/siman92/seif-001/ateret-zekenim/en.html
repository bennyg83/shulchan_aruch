If he needed to relieve himself, etc. The relevant measure is the time required to walk a parsah. The measure of a parsah is one and one-fifth hours. The reason is that the time for prayer is one hour, with the brief periods that every person must wait before and after it; the additional fifth of an hour accounts for those two periods of waiting. Even if the time for prayer will pass, it is nevertheless forbidden for him to pray until he first cleans his body. Bach, based on the words of Rambam, Rosh, and Rabbeinu Yonah.
