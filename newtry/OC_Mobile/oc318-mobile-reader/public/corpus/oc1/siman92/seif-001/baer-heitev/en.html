And to pray — specifically for adults; but for minors one need not return. Magen Avraham — from the implication of the Gemara. And thus wrote LaKer Tov, part 2, siman 79, see there.

He prays — and even if he sees that the time for prayer will pass if he cleans himself first, nevertheless it is forbidden at the outset; but if he can stand until a parsa (Bach, see there). And Magen Avraham is lenient — if he can stand until a parsa even if the time for prayer will pass, see there. And Taz wrote in the name of Bach to the contrary, see there. And he did not analyze carefully.
