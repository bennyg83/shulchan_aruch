And to pray. This applies specifically after relieving oneself of solid waste, but after urinating one need not seek water. Magen Avraham, based on the implication of the Gemara. So too writes Halachos Ketanos, part II, siman 79; see there.
He should pray. Even if he sees that the time for prayer will pass should he first relieve himself, it is nevertheless initially forbidden to pray when he can restrain himself for the time needed to walk a parsah. Bach; see there. Magen Avraham is lenient when he can restrain himself for the time needed to walk a parsah if the time for prayer will pass; see there. Olas Tamid cites Bach as ruling the opposite; see there. He was not precise.
