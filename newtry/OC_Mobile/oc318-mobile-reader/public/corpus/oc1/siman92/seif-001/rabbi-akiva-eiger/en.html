Seif 1: The measure of walking a parsa. In Gan HaMelekh to the author of Gevuros Ohr siman 48 he wrote that since they did not specify the measure of an hour and a fifth, but only brought it to clarify that even though by sitting in one place one can restrain oneself a little more, they estimate as though he were walking — for he cannot restrain himself and walk a parsa.
