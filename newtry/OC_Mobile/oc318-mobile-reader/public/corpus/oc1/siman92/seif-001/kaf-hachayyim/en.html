1) [Se'if 1] If he needed to relieve himself, he should not pray, etc. If he guards himself and waits to pray until he first relieves himself, not only is he rewarded for taking care not to stand in prayer before God with a body full of excrement and urinary filth, but he is additionally rewarded for adorning himself to stand before Him with a clean and dignified body, without any filth. Just as one adorns himself with dignified garments before a king, he adorns himself with a clean body (which is also the soul's garment) when standing in prayer before God. This too is included in 'Prepare to meet your God, O Israel,' interpreted as: adorn yourself before God. Bach.
