And similarly [this applies] to the prayer leader — if he needed to relieve himself before the return repetition of the prayer leader, it is permitted on account of human dignity; and similarly when they called him to ascend to the Torah it is permitted. [6] There, gloss: and anyone who needs to relieve himself is forbidden even in words of Torah, etc. — And according to this, prayer that was cited is not precise — for likewise Keriat Shema and its blessings; Avodat HaTumah note 4. And there are times when a person delays himself on account of human dignity when standing in the congregation, so as not to trouble the many to stand before him, or so as not to pass before the heads of the holy people, and the like — and fortunate is he whose ways are thus, and who considers the loss of a mitzvah; and always he should precede [and] examine his orifices so that he need not forfeit communal prayer and need not trouble the many.
