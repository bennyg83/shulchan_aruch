[7] And if there is no prepared water, etc. — It implies that even if there is dirt on them, if he has no prepared water he cleans, etc.; and so seems the view of Lechem Chamudos page 14, that even with dirt one need not return four mil, since he already took [water] in the morning — we rely on the poskim that one need not return four mil. But in Olas Tamid he did not see his words and understood the words of Shulchan Aruch as dealing specifically when there is no dirt. Also he challenged Shulchan Aruch that we do not find any one of the poskim who distinguished thus. And in truth for practical halacha one should not be lenient regarding dirt, for it seems from Shulchan Aruch that dirt is stricter than distraction of the mind, as some say in Tur; and if so, specifically for distraction of the mind Shulchan Aruch relied on the poskim that one need not return four mil.
