(29) Or engaged in Torah — and bedi'eavad ordinary cleaning suffices for Torah and for blessings, even if he has water. See above at the end of siman 4 in Mishna Berurah. And if he touched mud and mire, perhaps even lekhatchilah it is permitted thus; but above at the end of siman 4 the Peri Megadim wrote that one must clean from the outset even for words of Torah, and so it seems proper to be stringent lekhatchilah regarding mire.

(30) The ear and the nose — and the Gra is lenient in this, and likewise in Sha'ar HaTziyun in the name of Moreh veKitziah.
