If one washed his hands in the morning, and he got distracted, they need a [re-]washing for prayer if he has water, even if one does not know whether they [i.e. his hands] have any dirt [on them], and he should not bless [for the washing of their hands]. And if he does not have readily available water, he do not need to go back and wash.Rem"a: And even if his hands were dirty and he washes them for prayer, he should not bless - and see above Orach Chayyim 6':
