[5] More than a mil, etc. — It implies that for the mil itself one returns. They wondered about this in Lechem Chamudos and Nachalas Tzvi page 14 — that he abandoned the text of Rif and Rosh that one does not return a mil itself, and ruled that it appears from Rambam, chapter 4 of Hilchos Tefillah. And further, in Rambam himself one can explain like Rif, as Kesef Mishneh himself wrote; and if so, no one ruled that one returns a mil itself, and therefore he ruled thus for halacha against Shulchan Aruch — see there. But I found in Kolbo page 7 and in Sefer Tzida LaDerech page 43 that they ruled that one returns a mil itself; and so it seems in Sefer Chassidim siman 18, and such is the primary view; and so Lechem Mishneh explained Rambam's words. But great study is required in his words — for he reads there like the text of Rif. And know that in Kolbo it deals with when one can reach the place he desires, etc., as above siman 90 seif 17. He wrote in the responsum of Beit Yaakov siman 35 that a rider's law in this is like one walking on foot, and there is no distinction between a bad road and a good one. See siman 249, note 3:
