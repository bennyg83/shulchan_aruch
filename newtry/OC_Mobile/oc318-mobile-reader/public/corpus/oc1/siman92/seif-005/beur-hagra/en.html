Seif 65: He washed [his hands in the morning], etc. — as for netilat yadayim for eating, as stated in Pesachim 115b; therefore he did not touch it literally, for if so even for a berachah it would be invalid since his hands are not clean, and as stated in chapter 6 of Berachos regarding the order of grace, one takes one hand, etc., and as stated in Nazir 59a; except that the Ran in Pesachim wrote that for prayer too they are fit — only for ritual impurity they decreed that they must be made secondary; therefore there for eating netilah is required. And this is what is meant by "and if there is no [water]," etc. — that in such a case one may rely on him. See Bava Metzia 85b:
