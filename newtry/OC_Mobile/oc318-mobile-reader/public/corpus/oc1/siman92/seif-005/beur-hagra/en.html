Seif 5, He washed, etc. This is like washing one’s hands for eating, as stated in Pesachim 115b. It must therefore be that he did not actually touch, for if he had, his hands would be unfit even for a blessing because they are not clean. This is as stated in chapter 6 of Berachos regarding the order of reclining, that he washes one hand, etc., and as stated in Nazir 59a. However, Ran in Pesachim wrote that even for prayer the hands remain fit, and that the decree making them second-degree impure applies only for ritual impurity. Therefore, for eating there, washing is required. This is the meaning of, ‘and if there is no, etc.,’ for in such a case one may rely upon this view. See Bava Metzia 85b.
