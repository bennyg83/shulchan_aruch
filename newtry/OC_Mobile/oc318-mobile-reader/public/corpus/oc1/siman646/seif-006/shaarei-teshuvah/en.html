That he picked; see Be'er Heitev. See my Responsa Beis Ephraim, siman 55 and siman 59. There the Rambam's words are explained very well. I also wrote there to resolve why it is not difficult that we say, 'if he reduced them it is valid,' even though we rule that anything the Merciful One said not to do, if one did it, it does not take effect. This is based on the words of Tosafos in Temurah regarding one who blemishes the ear of a firstborn animal, etc.; see there. See the Mordechai on Sukkah, brought below in siman 647, that if a non-Jew cut willows on Shabbos, he brings proof from this law of one who transgressed and picked them that there is no issue of a mitzvah that comes through a transgression. In Birkei Yosef here and in Machazik Berachah, siman 667, he discusses this at length; see there.
