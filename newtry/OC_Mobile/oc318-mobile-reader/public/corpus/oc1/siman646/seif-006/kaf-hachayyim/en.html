6) It is the manner of hadas branches that shoots emerge from their sides, and these interrupt the whorls of the hadas so that there are not three leaves in one whorl. They must be cut off. Ran. This applies when they interrupt between one leaf and another. Even if those emerging shoots themselves have three leaves in one whorl, they nevertheless interrupt between the leaves of the main stem and the bases of the shoots. One cannot fulfill the mitzvah with the shoots emerging from the side because they lack the required length of a hadas. If, however, those shoots emerge between one whorl and another, they do not need to be cut off. Yet if the shoots emerging from the sides do not have three leaves in one whorl, I am uncertain whether they must be cut off. Machatzis HaShekel, s.k. 1. Nevertheless, the common practice is to cut them off in every case. One should take care to cut them off before Yom Tov.
