That he picked them. This version is not correct in the Ramban, for in the Gemara we say: Rabbi Shimon bar Elazar says, one may reduce them on Yom Tov. It asks: but he is repairing a vessel. Rav Ashi answers: such as where he picked them for eating and not to make it valid. It asks: but it is an inevitable consequence. It answers: where he has another hosha'ana. End quote. It is difficult regarding the Rambam, for it implies that he wants to permit ab initio to reduce them when he picks them for eating, but he should have mentioned that he has another hosha'ana. The Maggid Mishneh reads: or another picked them for eating, meaning another person who does not need to repair this. Taz and Magen Avraham. Nevertheless, for the need of a mitzvah it is permitted. Tosafot, daf 33a.
