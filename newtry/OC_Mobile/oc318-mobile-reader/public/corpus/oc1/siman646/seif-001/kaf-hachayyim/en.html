1) [Seif 1] A hadas whose tip was severed is valid. Below, in seif 10, he cites a dispute. The reason is that these first two seifim are the language of the Rambam, while from seif 3 onward it is the language of the Tur. Here he followed the Rambam's wording incidentally together with the other laws. Biur HaGra; Mishna Berurah, sec. 2. See Machatzis HaShekel.
