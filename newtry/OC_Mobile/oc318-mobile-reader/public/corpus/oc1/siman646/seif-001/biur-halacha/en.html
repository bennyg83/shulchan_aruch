A myrtle, etc. The Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Tamim De'im that invalidations because of avos apply all seven days. This implies that only what is invalid because of avos is meant, but other invalidations, such as its leaves dried or its berries are more numerous than its leaves, are invalid only on the first day. In truth, however, the Ra'avad follows his own view there in Tamim De'im, that defects because of hadar are invalid only on the first day. But according to what the Rema ruled in siman 649, se'if 5, regarding chazazis, and as the Magen Avraham himself ruled regarding berries more numerous than leaves, all myrtle invalidations mentioned in this siman apply all seven days, for all of them are either because of avos or because of hadar. These are the words of Bikkurei Yaakov. However, this matter, that defects because of hadar are invalid all seven days, is not clear-cut; see below in siman 649 regarding chazazis, where we wrote about this.
