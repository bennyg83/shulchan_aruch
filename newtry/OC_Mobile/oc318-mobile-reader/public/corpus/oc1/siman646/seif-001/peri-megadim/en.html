A myrtle. See Turei Zahav. It appears that he explains here as he wrote in se'if 10. See Be'er HaGolah, letter 1. It is difficult: for what purpose did the Mechaber repeat his words? The Levush omitted this here and brought the dispute only in se'if 9. If one says that here he is speaking of the upper leaves being cut off, for with a lulav, when its top is cut off in such a case, it is invalid, see siman 665, se'if 6, whereas here it is valid, this works well. In siman 645, letter 6, the Turei Zahav wrote similarly; see there. See also the Turei Zahav in siman 647, subparagraph 4, regarding a willow.
