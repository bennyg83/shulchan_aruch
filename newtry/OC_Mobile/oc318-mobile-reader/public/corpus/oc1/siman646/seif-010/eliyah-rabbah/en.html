[10] Levushei Malchus: From the first day, etc. This does not seem correct to me. Rather, whenever he has not reduced them now, it is invalid; this is from the Bedek HaBayis. So also ruled the Magen Avraham. It is unlike the spotted myrtle in siman 649, for there it is not in his power to repair it. Even according to those who validate a spotted myrtle, perhaps they would invalidate here, since on Chol HaMoed he can repair it. The Orchot Chaim perhaps spoke of a case where they blackened on Yom Tov and it was fit and then rejected, so reducing them on Chol HaMoed does not help. In practice, I wrote above that it is valid.
