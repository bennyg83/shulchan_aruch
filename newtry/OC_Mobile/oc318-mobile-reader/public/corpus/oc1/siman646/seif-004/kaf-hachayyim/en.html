4) The invalidations of avos apply equally on the first day of Yom Tov and on the remaining days. Terumas HaDeshen, siman 226, in the name of the Raavad; Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur; Olas Shabbos, sec. 1; Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman. See Responsa Penei Yehoshua, siman 3, regarding a hadas whose majority of leaves fell off, that since it is invalid, it remains invalid even on the remaining days according to the opinion that beauty is required all seven days. Even a lulav and aravah are invalid on the remaining days just as on the first day, and likewise all invalidations except for something deficient and a borrowed species. It goes without saying that a hadas whose leaves fell off is invalid, since the Torah explicitly calls it avos, thickly leaved, etc.; see there. Shaarei Teshuvah. See above, siman 645, sec. 26.
