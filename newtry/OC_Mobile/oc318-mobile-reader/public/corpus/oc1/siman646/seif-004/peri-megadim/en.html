And one may not reduce, etc. See Turei Zahav. At first glance this requires analysis, for regarding 'a new shoot grew on it' in Sukkah 33a, Rashi explains this as not referring to myrtle berries that became red or black while detached; rather, they grew while attached. Perhaps some words are missing. And regarding a new shoot that grew when its top was cut off, even if it occurred on Yom Tov, where it was fit and then rejected, it does not return and become fit. See letter 8 and letter 12, and with Hashem's help it will be explained there. This requires analysis.
