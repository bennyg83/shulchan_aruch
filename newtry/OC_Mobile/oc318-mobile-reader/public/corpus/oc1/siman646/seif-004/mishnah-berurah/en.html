And they fell off from them until, etc. This applies even if it was so along the entire length of the hadas.
Even if most of them fell off. Since three remain, we still read it as 'avot.' But if only two remained, it is invalid. Even though regarding a hadas that has three in one nest, where one fell from each nest and only two remained, several later authorities agreed to validate there, that case is different, for at least most of the nest remained, and the majority is like the whole. This is not so here, where most fell off; we require at least that the measure of 'avot' remain, for through this we can validate it and say that even now we read it as 'avot.'
