[9] Levushei Malchus: Melachah, etc. At first glance this applies specifically when he picks them for eating, but if he picks them in order to make the myrtle valid, it is forbidden even when he has another one. However, Rashi and the Beis Yosef imply that when he has another one, even if he picks them to make this one valid, it is not called repairing, because he does not need it. One can reject this proof; see Tosafos.
