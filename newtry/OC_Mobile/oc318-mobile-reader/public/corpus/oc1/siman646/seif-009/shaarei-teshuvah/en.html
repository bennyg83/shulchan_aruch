(Se'if 10) In Shulchan Aruch: and some invalidate, etc. See Halachos Ketanos, part 1, siman 220, regarding one branch of myrtle that has many offshoots and is considered one. If the middle one was cut off and one offshoot remained whole, there is a remedy: he should cut the middle one so that it remains like a handle for the upper offshoot, and it will not appear that he is reducing it. The same applies here. According to this, it is also forbidden here to do this on Yom Tov. It is obvious that in any event it needs a length of three tefachim. Although one must measure its bend and the slope in the middle of the reed, we have no issue with this as long as its leaves cover its wood as required.
