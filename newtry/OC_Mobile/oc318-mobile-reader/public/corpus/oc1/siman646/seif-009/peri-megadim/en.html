And for indispensability. See Turei Zahav, for in this respect dried is preferable, as in se'if 8. It implies that the majority of the myrtle's required measure, mentioned in siman 650, is three tefachim. It is possible that it need not have the required avos measure in one place. For example, if the myrtle is six tefachim long and along its whole length there are two tefachim and a little more of valid avos, perhaps it is valid even if scattered. Or perhaps, wherever the majority is not avos, it is not hadar and is invalid. This requires analysis.
