Green. See Turei Zahav, Sukkah 33b, according to Rashi's explanation. Now, for red berries, why not derive the invalidation from the fact that they differ from the myrtle, whose wood and leaves are green, while this is red? Even without saying that red is black, red should be invalid. If we say that the invalidation is because it is fruit, and we require a branch of a tree and not fruit, this is understandable: red is already fruit, and when it dries a little the completion of its fruit as black will be recognizable. See the Yerushalmi: what practical difference is there between them, etc. Now, 'green' includes both green and yellow in the vernacular; see Yoreh De'ah about this. If so, yellow in the vernacular also differs in appearance, except that since its fruit is not completed it is valid, as stated. For now I have not found this explicit. See the Gaon who explains there.
