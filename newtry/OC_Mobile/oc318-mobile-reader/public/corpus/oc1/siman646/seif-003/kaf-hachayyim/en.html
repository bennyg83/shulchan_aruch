3) Ibid. If the majority of its leaves fell off, if three remained, etc. This too is the language of the Rambam, as stated above. The Maggid Mishneh explains that he means that three leaves remained in each and every whorl, as written in seif 4. This is contrary to the Tur's understanding of the Rambam, namely that according to the Rambam even one whorl suffices, regarding which the Beit Yosef expressed astonishment; see there. Biur HaGra. So too wrote Bigdei Yesha, that the Shulchan Aruch's statement, 'If the majority of its leaves fell off,' refers to what is written in seif 4, as Taz, s.k. 2, wrote. So too wrote Mishna Berurah, sec. 3.
