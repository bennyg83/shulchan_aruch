Seif 8: They dried, etc. According to Rashi's explanation there, which refers to all three myrtle branches.
And some explain, etc. According to Rosh's explanation, which refers to a single myrtle branch. This requires study, because according to his interpretation three clusters must remain, with one moist leaf in each cluster, and they must be at the top. See Rashi on Bava Metzia 24a, s.v. “a leaf,” etc.; however, his words are difficult.
