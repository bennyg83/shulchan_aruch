See Responsa Chacham Tzvi 161: the hadasim found in Amsterdam and Hamburg, three by three in one nest, are valid, and it is the choicest mitzvah to buy them for expensive money, unlike Shevut Yaakov, who wrote that they are grafted and that those that come from Italy are invalid; see there. See Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 28. So too he ruled in Responsa Panim Me'irot, siman 9, and he wrote that one who takes, alongside the moist hadasim that stand three in one nest, another dry hadas brought from Italy in order to fulfill according to the view of the book Shevut Yaakov, violates adding to the mitzvah; see there.
