[8] Levushei Malchus: And here too it is in his power, etc. So wrote the Beis Yosef, that even if he has no other myrtle, since if he finds another he will be able to repair the matter, it is not considered rejected. If he intends to pick them for eating, it is permitted. The Bayis Chadash wrote that rejection in mitzvos is only a rabbinic doubt.
