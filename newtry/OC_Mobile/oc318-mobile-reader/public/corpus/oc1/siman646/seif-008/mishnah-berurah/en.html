Valid. See the Rosh, who holds that three nests must remain, and in each nest one moist leaf at its head. The later authorities wondered why the Mechaber did not mention this.
And it, etc. Meaning that what we validate with one moist leaf at its head is specifically in this manner, where it is set over the other two, for then the beauty is more visible. But if all three leaves were moist, even if all were equal, it is valid.
That it is set over the two of them. Even though in se'if 3 the Mechaber ruled that this is included in wild hadas, perhaps there he speaks where the entire required length of the hadas was in this manner, and it is not included in the hadas stated in the Torah, but is a wild hadas. But here, he is speaking where the entire required measure of the hadas had nests of three equal leaves according to law; only because they were dry it is not called beautiful. Therefore we say that if three nests are found, and in each one a leaf that is set over the other two is moist, the beauty is visible and recognizable in it, and it is valid. The halachah follows the first view.
