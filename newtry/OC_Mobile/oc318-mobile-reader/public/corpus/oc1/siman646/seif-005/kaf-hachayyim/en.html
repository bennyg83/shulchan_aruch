If three leaves remained, etc. Regarding our hadasim, which have two leaves above two, in accordance with what is written below in seif 3 in the Rama's gloss, it is obvious that even if only one leaf from each whorl was severed throughout the majority of the required length of the hadas, it is invalid, since initially there were only two leaves in each whorl and the majority of the whorl did not remain. Bach; Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos Beit Yosef, sec. 2; Olas Shabbos, sec. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, sec. 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 7. This implies that if one leaf from each whorl was severed only throughout the minority of the length, while the majority remained complete, it is valid. Chayei Adam, klal 150, sec. 3, wrote that even if one leaf fell from one whorl within the majority of the required length of the hadas, it is invalid; see there. This will be further explained below, sec. 39; see there.
