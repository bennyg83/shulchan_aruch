One by one for eating. See Turei Zahav and Magen Avraham, letter 2. See Levush in the gloss, who refers to Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, se'if 6. It appears that this is where he has no other hosha'ana, and he transgressed and picked them on Yom Tov. It is forbidden to eat them, for they were muktzeh at twilight. Even with a rabbinic prohibition we say that since it was muktzeh then, it remains muktzeh, as I wrote in Hilchos Shabbos.
