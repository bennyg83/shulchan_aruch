Seif 5: For the preferred fulfillment, etc. This follows the statement, “Now, a three-leaf arrangement,” etc. A completely non-leafy myrtle is commonly found.
And indispensably, etc. This is comparable to a case in which some of the leaves fell, which remains valid, as with a willow. So too is stated in the Tosefta concerning lulav, and as explained above in siman 645, seif 3.
Even if it is not, etc. Meaning, although regarding dryness the defect must specifically be at the top, as stated in seif 8, because the issue there is beauty, here the defect concerns the leafy structure. The Gemara states without qualification that if some of its leaves fell it remains valid, even if the leaves fell from its top.
