7) [Seif 2] If its berries were more numerous, etc. It is the nature of the hadas tree to grow fruit resembling grapes. If within the required length of the hadas, which is three tefachim, these berries are more numerous than the leaves growing within that length, it is invalid even from the second day onward. This is because the natural color of these berries is black or red while the hadas is green, and it therefore appears speckled and is not beautiful. Accordingly, if the color of these berries is green like the hadas, it is valid even on the first day, although they are more numerous than the leaves. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 12. However, regarding what he wrote, that it is invalid even from the second day onward, this will be explained below, sec. 21; see there.
