In Shulchan Aruch: most of its leaves fell, etc. See Responsa Pnei Yehoshua, siman 3, regarding a case where most of its leaves fell. It is invalid even on the other days according to the one who holds that we require hadar all seven days. Even a lulav and willow are invalid on the other days as on the first day, and so are all invalidations except for missing and borrowed. Needless to say, this applies to fallen myrtle leaves, for regarding the myrtle the Torah writes avos, etc.; see there.
