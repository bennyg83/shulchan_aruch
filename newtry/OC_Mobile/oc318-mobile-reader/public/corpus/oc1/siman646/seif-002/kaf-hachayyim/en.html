7) [Seif 2] If its berries were more numerous, etc. It is the nature of the hadas tree to grow fruit resembling grapes. If within the required length of the hadas, which is three tefachim, these berries are more numerous than the leaves growing within that length, it is invalid even from the second day onward. This is because the natural color of these berries is black or red while the hadas is green, and it therefore appears speckled and is not beautiful. Accordingly, if the color of these berries is green like the hadas, it is valid even on the first day, although they are more numerous than the leaves. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, sec. 12. However, regarding what he wrote, that it is invalid even from the second day onward, this will be explained below, sec. 21; see there.
8) Ibid. If they are green, it is valid. For they are of the same kind as the hadas. Sukkah 33b. Rashi explains that their appearance is the same as the hadas and it does not appear speckled.
9) Ibid. If they are red or black, it is invalid. For red is considered like black, as R' Chanina said: This black blood was originally red, but it deteriorated. Gemara there.
10) Ibid. If they are red or black, it is invalid. There is no distinction whether they were that way initially or became black afterward; it is invalid because this is not beautiful. Levush, seif 10; Eshel Avraham, sec. 1.
11) Ibid. If they are red, etc. If they were other colors, such as yellow or blue, Kapos Temarim inclines toward stringency, while Mishbetzos Zahav, sec. 3, remains uncertain regarding this. Mishna Berurah, sec. 6.
