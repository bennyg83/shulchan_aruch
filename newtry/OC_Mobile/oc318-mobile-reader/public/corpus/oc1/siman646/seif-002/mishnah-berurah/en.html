Its berries. For sometimes a fruit like a berry grows on the hadas.
If they are green, it is valid. For the hadas too is green, and there is no change of appearance because of the berries. This is unlike black ones. Red ones are also considered black, for we say that this black is really red that has deteriorated. But red or black ones are invalid, for it is not beautiful. If they were other colors, such as yellow or white, Kapot Temarim inclines to be stringent, and the Peri Megadim is uncertain about this.
And if he reduced them. This means before Yom Tov. It is permitted to reduce them ab initio so that its leaves will outnumber its berries, even to reduce them after the lulav was bound.
Or one picked them one by one for eating. See the Taz and the Gra, who agreed that the text should read: or another picked them for eating. So the Magen Avraham agreed in practice: specifically another person who picked them for eating and did not intend to make it valid is permitted, even though it is an inevitable consequence that it becomes valid automatically, for he is not pleased with that validation. Since in the end there will be a benefit in this for another person, who will be able to fulfill the mitzvah with them, we are not stringent in this. But that man himself, even if he intends for eating and not to make it valid, is nevertheless prohibited, for it is an inevitable consequence, unless he has other hadasim with which to fulfill the obligation and does not need these.
Valid in any manner. Meaning even if he did not reduce them at all. His reason is that he holds that all invalidations, even those invalid because of beauty, invalidate only on the first day. According to this, for us, who rule below in siman 649 that invalidations because of beauty invalidate all seven days, in this case too they invalidate all seven days.
