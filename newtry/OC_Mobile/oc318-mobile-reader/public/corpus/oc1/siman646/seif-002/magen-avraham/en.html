Invalidities of avot invalidate throughout all seven days; Terumat HaDeshen 226. Green ones are kosher. For it is a species of myrtle.
One by one for eating. In the Maimonides edition of Maharam, what he wrote, "or that he picked them one by one for eating," means that after another person, who does not need this myrtle, it is permitted for him initially to pick them for eating; and even though he thereby repairs the myrtle, it is a davar she-eino mitkaven. But one who needs the myrtle, even if he picks them for eating, it is a psik reisha and forbidden. So it is in the Gemara, and so wrote Beit Yosef. Even according to the poskim at the end of siman 320 who do not hold like the Aruch, see there in Beit Yosef, nevertheless for a mitzvah need it is permitted through another person; Tosafot.
And onward it is kosher. Because it is in his power to repair it.
In every case. In Bedek HaBayit he wrote: "This does not seem so to me; rather, as long as he did not reduce them, it is always invalid." End quote. So it appears to me, for Orchot Chaim holds like the Yerushalmi that he brings in siman 648, that a chazazit on an etrog is kosher on the other days; but we hold that it is invalid on all days, as Rama himself wrote in siman 649 seif 4, that a speckled one is invalid throughout all seven days. If so, this too is invalid, for the Gemara states explicitly that berries are like speckling. Perhaps he holds that according to the conclusion the reason is not speckling except when it is in two or three places, but when it is in one place the reason is not hadar. Yet truth points its way: even in one place the reason is hadar, for otherwise on what ground would it be invalid? The words of Rama require study; therefore one should not be lenient.
