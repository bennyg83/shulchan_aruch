Withered. See Turei Zahav in se'if 6. Since withered is valid, where the majority is so it is different from dried; when the majority is dried, withered leaves do not save it according to those who invalidate. The Mechaber did not decide the matter. At first glance, on the first day it is a Torah doubt, and on the remaining days it is a rabbinic doubt and we are lenient. And if you say: how can one recite a berachah according to the 'some say' view, which is a Torah matter, as in siman 276? One can answer that whenever, because of rabbinic doubt, it is valid, he can then properly recite a berachah. In the Beis Yosef he brought in the name of the Ran, of blessed memory, that it is proper to be concerned for Rabbi Aharon, of blessed memory. See what I wrote in Kelalim Ketanim about places where the Mechaber brings 'some explain' and afterward 'some say'; see there. If one withered leaf remained at its top, one might say that it is a double doubt and invalid.
