He takes it but does not recite a berachah over it. See the Beis Yosef, who brought in the name of Orchot Chaim, who brought in the name of the Baal HaHashlamah, that it is valid on the other days. In Bedek HaBayis he wrote about this: it does not appear correct to me; rather, it is invalid on all the days. In my humble opinion, the Baal HaHashlamah is not alone in this matter, for this is also the view of the Maggid Mishneh in explaining the Rambam. In chapter 8 of Hilchos Lulav, halachah 9, he wrote: know that all the invalidations mentioned by our teacher in chapter 7 are invalid on the second day just as on the first. This implies that what he mentioned in chapter 8 is valid on the other days, and this law of its berries being more numerous than its leaves appears in chapter 8. However, the words of the Pri Megadim on this matter require analysis, for from his words in siman 649, Mishbetzos Zahav subparagraph 12, it is proven that he thought this was also the view of the Mechaber, to validate berries more numerous than leaves on the other days. He overlooked what the Mechaber wrote explicitly in Bedek HaBayis, that it is invalid on the other days as well.
