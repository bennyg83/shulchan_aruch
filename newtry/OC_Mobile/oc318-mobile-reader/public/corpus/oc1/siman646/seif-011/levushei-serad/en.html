Magen Avraham, subparagraph 3. For it is in his power to repair it, etc. The Magen Avraham explains the words of the Rema, the Beis Yosef, and the Orchot Chaim as follows: on the first day it is invalid because it is not in his power to reduce the berries, since that may not be done on Yom Tov. On Chol HaMoed it is in his power to repair it, and therefore it is like anything fit for mixing, where actual mixing is not indispensable. But the Magen Avraham disagrees with this ruling in practice.
