(Seif katan 4) And if he does not, etc. — know that what was glossed in the Shulchan Aruch, "and he shall remove them immediately," and likewise afterward, "and there are those who say if," etc., is from the glosses of the Taz; but the Magen Avraham had before him the old version and these words that were added were not written, therefore it was difficult for him — meaning the middle case. For the Magen Avraham understood by the plain meaning that even in the middle case his intent is to place the others, etc., meaning after the prayer as at the end; and further he was difficult with what he wrote "upon reliance on the first blessing" — behold, also at the beginning the law is so.

And in our opinion the first, etc., and afterward he places as the words of, etc.; and in any case both pairs shall be upon him at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer; therefore he was not particular in the middle case which he places, Rashi tefillin or Rabbenu Tam, since either way both pairs will be upon him at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer. And the Mordechai wrote: although one interrupts with blessings, etc., that if one places the two hand tefillin one after the other and afterward the two head tefillin one after the other, it is worse to interrupt between the hand and head tefillin at their placement; for even on a weekday when one does not bless, nevertheless it is forbidden to interrupt between the hand and head tefillin, as above in siman 25 in the Magen Avraham, seif katan 14.
