(Seif katan 4) And if he does not know, etc. Magen Avraham's text apparently lacked the words “and he should immediately remove them.” He therefore found the wording difficult. According to the old text, Shulchan Aruch seems to mean that after putting on one pair he should remove it immediately and then put on the other pair, which is difficult because the latter is then not covered by the original blessing.
And accordingly, etc. He should put on according to each opinion in such a way that both pairs are upon him during Krias Shema and prayer. Therefore, it makes little difference whether he first puts on Rashi or Rabbeinu Tam tefillin, since both will be present during Krias Shema and prayer. Nevertheless, placing both arm tefillin first would create an interruption between the arm and head tefillin. Even where no blessing is recited over the second pair, one may not interrupt between the arm and head tefillin, as explained above in siman 25.
