**He should not place** — See in Mishna Berurah; and it is from Magen Avraham. And see below in siman 42 s.e. 3 that all their laws apply here. And it seems that even if making the pouch was with one person's intent, if before he placed them the one time he uprooted explicitly his first designation, it avails; and this is not at all within "speech follows action" and nullifies the act — this making is considered only a generic designation; see Sanhedrin 48, and so forth to elaborate.

**Two pairs** — And it deals with a pair to which the pouch was designated from the start without a pouch; and even if it is in a pouch and sometimes on the way incidentally they are placed in it without a pouch, it becomes sanctified thereby, as in Megillah 26b in the Gemara at the beginning "it was thought," etc. — see there. But if it is always in a pouch, behold the pouch is not made by it anything but an accessory of an accessory, and there is no sanctity in it. And apparently it seems specifically if the pouch covers also the ma'barta and titura, for they too are part of the body of the sanctity — it is not considered in Megillah p. 26 for items of sanctity in tefillin except the pouch and strap; and also from Shabbat 28b, "and Abaye said: the shin of tefillin," etc. And see — afterward I found something like this in Mordechai in Laws of Tefillin.

**It is profane** — See in Mishna Berurah; and I copied all this from Beit HaTefilah in the name of responsum of Damesek Nahar; and it requires study for practical law in what he wrote that if he did not place them, etc., he may exchange them — for it is straightforward his source is from below in siman 42 in Chadashim that he may change them; yet per what is explained among the Acharonim, it is permitted only to lower from stringent to lenient, but not to profane, for designation for the body of sanctity is a matter; and here this author himself holds below that this is included in lowering to profane. Also what he wrote afterward "and even if he made a condition on them," etc., profane to profane, etc. — see responsum of Damesek Eliezer, who concludes that with retzuot a condition avails; and it is straightforward they are only accessories of sanctity, and a condition avails in this to lower them to profane, as below in siman 42 s.e. 3. And even with batim and parshiyot — in my humble opinion one should examine this, for behold on parchment worked for the sake of parshiyot, which is designation for the body of sanctity, it is forbidden to lower it to profane — I proved below in siman 42 that he may write on it other words of Torah because there is thereby in any event some sanctity; and the same in our matter — behold even now, although there is no sanctity of tefillin on the parshiyot, nevertheless there is sanctity in them; and the batim are accessories for sanctity; if so, a condition should avail, and the answer is not in my power to examine it. And perhaps one may be lenient through a condition with head tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam for Rashi if he has no other parshiyot — and it requires study. I also wrote in the name of Peri Megadim that if he found a strap, etc.; and I omitted responsum of Yad Eliyahu cited in Beit HaTefilah that in our time the majority is Rashi; and see further in Sefer Petach Einayim what he wrote on this. And because Peri Megadim's words are difficult to understand, I said to copy them here, as follows: if he found of the hand [printing error: should read also or of the head] and a strap without parshiyot, and there is doubt whether they are Rabbeinu Tam's — it is proper to place Rashi's parshiyot, perhaps perhaps, and the majority is Rashi, and we raise in holiness; and we are expert, and Rashi is primary. And working requires study — also with retzuot from profane to sanctity — understand, end quote. Behold what he wrote "perhaps" plainly — perhaps they are Rashi's, and perhaps Rashi is primary; yet for this reason alone would it be permitted to place them also for Rabbeinu Tam's, or perhaps of both is forbidden, for doubts contradict each other; therefore he concluded "majority" and "we are expert, and Rashi is primary." But the end of his words are wondrous — that in working there is no relevance of "perhaps he worked them for Rashi tefillin or Rabbeinu Tam tefillin," for working is generic for its sake; and in my humble opinion his intention is: below in siman 39 s.e. 9 it states that if one bought tefillin not from an expert, he checks three compartments for missing and extra, and that suffices; and we are not concerned perhaps the seller worked them not for their sake, for all are expert in this; therefore Peri Megadim is uncertain in our matter — let it be that all are expert that parshiyot require working for their sake; behold with working of batim and retzuot we cannot say all are expert now in this, for there is dispute among poskim, as above in siman 33; if so, perhaps that which was lost from him — he made the batim or retzuot without working for their sake; if so, he now raises from profane to sanctity without superior designation of working. And what the Gemara permits to buy tefillin — which implies even with retzuot — is specifically in their time, when it was known whether working was needed or not; and so in our time, if one buys from one who is not expert, he must inquire of the seller whether there was working for its sake in the batim and retzuot; unlike our matter, which deals with this find — this is my humble opinion of his intention. And in truth it requires study in our time regarding a found strap — perhaps it was only blackened for its sake and not worked for its sake, for many are lenient in this, and it is not according to law, as Damesek Eliezer explained above in siman 33 — see there what we wrote. And what he wrote "if it happened," etc. — Peri Megadim.
