In one pouch. It is forbidden to exchange the pouches, and all the more so the housings, straps, and parashiyot from Rashi to Rabbeinu Tam or vice versa — Darchei Noam ch. 1 siman 50; and likewise Rabbeinu Bezalel and Rama siman 37. If a person never put them on for the sake of the mitzvah of tefillin, he may exchange them; and if he made a condition from the outset to exchange them when he wishes, the condition is ineffective once he arranged them according to one view and put them on even once for the sake of the mitzvah of tefillin according to Rabbeinu Tam or Rashi — it is forbidden to exchange them afterward, even if he conditioned from the beginning that from holy to mundane the condition does not help — Darchei Noam ch. 1 siman 2. If he made the tefillin pouch from the outset to put both in it, it is permitted, for it is as if he made a condition — Magen Avraham. Straps that were found and it is not known whether they are Rashi's or Rabbeinu Tam's: if in that place they are accustomed to put on two pairs, he puts them on whichever he wishes; and if he is in a place where the majority put on Rashi's, he puts them on as Rashi's — responsum of Yad Eliyahu siman 5.
