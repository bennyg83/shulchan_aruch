In one pouch. It is forbidden to exchange the pouches, and all the more so the batim, straps, or passages, from Rashi to Rabbeinu Tam or the reverse. Darkei Noam, Orach Chayim, siman 50. Rav David ibn Zimra and Responsa Rema MiPano, siman 37, write likewise. If no person ever wore them for the mitzvah of tefillin, they may be exchanged. If one initially stipulated that he could exchange them whenever he wished, the condition is ineffective once they were arranged according to one opinion and worn even once for the mitzvah of tefillin according to Rabbeinu Tam or Rashi. Afterward they may not be exchanged, even if he stipulated initially, because a condition does not permit transferring an item from sanctity to non-sanctity. Darkei Noam, Orach Chayim, siman 2. If he originally made the tefillin pouch to hold both pairs, it is permitted, because it is as though he stipulated this. Magen Avraham. If straps are found and it is unknown whether they belong to Rashi or Rabbeinu Tam tefillin, then in a place where people commonly wear both pairs he may use them for whichever pair he wishes. In a place where the majority wear Rashi tefillin, he should use them for Rashi tefillin. Responsa Yad Eliyahu, siman 5.
