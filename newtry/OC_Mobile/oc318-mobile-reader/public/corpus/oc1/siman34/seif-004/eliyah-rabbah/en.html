[4] **One who is established, etc.** For they sent a letter from the Land of Israel that fell in the sea on the grave of the prophet Ezekiel, and they found old tefillin in Rashi's order. And some reject: if they are invalid, they were buried for a name; and it is not that they needed burial except to change their placement from this bayit to that bayit — so Beit Chadash wrote. He wrote in Sefer Baruch that he said all God-fearing people should be careful to preserve and bury all tefillin matters — retzuot, ma'bartot, titurot, batim, patches, and all the more so the parshiyot themselves; and how to bury them — place them in an earthenware vessel at the grave of a Torah scholar, for in another matter they will eventually be thrown away, even when buried in the Ark of the Covenant, for there is no burial there for many days, end quote. And know that even for Rabbeinu Tam one must write them in order, as written in siman 32 s.e. 1; therefore he must place part of parashat V'hayah im shamoa and write parashat Shema at the edge of the parchment, and afterward write parashat V'hayah im shamoa in the middle.
