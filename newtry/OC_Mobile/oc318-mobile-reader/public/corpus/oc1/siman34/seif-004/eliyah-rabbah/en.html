[d] He who is held, etc. They sent a letter from the Land of Israel that fell on the grave of the prophet Ezekiel and they found old tefillin according to the order of Rashi. And there is a delphi dapsolin hoi ganzum there and it is not that there was no need for a ganiza but to change the nachan from one house to another, so the Bah wrote. He wrote in Sefer Baruch that he said that all the God-fearers should be careful to keep and store away all tefillin items, both strips and bands and Titorah and boxes and patches, and even more so the parshiyots themselves, and how they should be stored in an earthenware vessel at the burial of a scholar, because in another matter They will eventually be thrown away even when they are stored in the Ark of the Covenant because there is no geniza for many days, so far.
