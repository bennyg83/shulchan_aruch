Avraham ben Natan Halevi — and see in Matzat Shmura that his view is to bless on tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam, and it is not so; and per what the Ari z"l wrote that one should not bless (on those of Rashi whose holiness is stricter, and a fortiori) on those of Rabbeinu Tam — see in Iggeret HaRamaz siman 5; and see above siman 25 in Beit Yosef note 11 in the name of Darchei Noam, and similarly in Bechor Shor; and see in Bechor Shor folio 115 that although one does not bless on those of Rabbeinu Tam, nevertheless one should not interrupt between tefillin shel yad and tefillin shel rosh — see there; and see in Pnei Meirut part 1 siman 67 that one who places tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam without a blessing and reads Keriat Shema in them may interrupt in the middle of the chapter for honor, but nevertheless should not engage in idle speech — see there; and it seems one should be stringent not to interrupt even for honor; and see below siman 66. And it seems ab initio one should not interrupt while wearing them; and see siman 65. They practiced to wear tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam at Minchah, as the Ari practiced before he made tefillin of Shimusha Rabba; and so practiced Maharachav; and although per the sod of the matter, since they did so it is certainly as they said — we do not know so much in sod. And see below siman 38 that one should not place tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam on days of mourning; and on Tisha BeAv at Minchah — Magen Avraham wrote in siman 555 that one who places Rabbeinu Tam's should place them at Minchah as well; and see there in Sheyurei Kenesset HaGedolah in the name of responsum of Rama and what Azriel wrote — see there; and what he wrote in the name of Shvus Yaakov that if shel rosh was written per Rashi's view, shel rosh should be placed first — see there, for it is explained that he blesses two blessings on shel rosh as written in the gloss at the end of siman 26; and shel yad of Rabbeinu Tam he places without a blessing; and if shel yad is per Rashi's view he blesses on it to place alone, and on shel rosh which is of Rabbeinu Tam he does not bless at all.
