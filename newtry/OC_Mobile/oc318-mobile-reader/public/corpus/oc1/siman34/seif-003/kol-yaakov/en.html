3. There: And according to Rabbeinu Tam, in the third compartment, etc. Nevertheless, the passages must be written in the order in which they appear in the Torah. If he changed that order, they are invalid, both for the arm and for the head. Therefore, in the arm tefillin, whose passages are written on one parchment, he writes Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha, leaves blank the place for Vehayah Im Shamoa, writes Shema, and afterward writes Vehayah Im Shamoa. Tur; Beit Yosef; Magen Avraham, seif katan 2; Ateres Zekeinim; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 3; and the later authorities. See what we wrote above in siman 32, letter 169.
