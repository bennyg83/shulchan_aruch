(Seif katan 3) Like ordinary straps — for from necessity one of them is invalid; but if, etc., the Rosh wrote in Hilchot Tefillin, folio 91, and the rest are like an ordinary strap and there is no bal tosif here except for one who makes five batim, end quote. Therefore the Levush was uncertain: from his mentioning five batim there, meaning specifically five batim; but if one places two pairs of tefillin of one order, both of Rashi or both of Rabbenu Tam, there is no bal tosif, comparable to tzitzit, for one can wear several garments that have tzitzit; or perhaps even with two pairs of one order there is bal tosif, and it is not comparable to tzitzit, for every garment with four corners is obligated in tzitzit, etc. But the Magen Avraham is clear that two pairs of one order involve bal tosif, and his proof is from the beginning of chapter 10 of Eruvin: we learned, one who finds tefillin on Rosh Hashanah that falls on Shabbat in a place where they would be lost brings them in by way of clothing, pair by pair; and Rabban Gamliel says he brings them in two by two. And it needs to say at the outset that everyone holds Shabbat is not the time for tefillin; nevertheless, because of the rain the Sages permitted bringing them in by way of clothing, and it is an ornament — and only in the place of tefillin is it an ornament. In this they disagree: the Tanna Kamma holds there is room on the head and arm only to place one tefillin there; therefore he permitted only pair by pair. Rabban Gamliel holds there is room on the head and arm to place two tefillin there; therefore it is permitted to bring them in two by two. And according to another explanation, everyone holds there is room on the head and arm to place two tefillin, and in this they disagree: the Tanna Kamma holds Shabbat is the time for tefillin, and therefore if one wears more than one pair there is bal tosif; and Rabban Gamliel holds Shabbat is not the time for tefillin and there is no bal tosif, for outside the time of the mitzvah one does not transgress until he intends to add. And there are further several resolutions; see there. In any case it is proven from the Tanna Kamma for this resolution — and likewise from other resolutions that he concludes afterward — that one order of two pairs involves bal tosif; and so he brought in siman 38 this proof. And likewise the Beit Yosef in the name of the Maggid Mishneh in siman 651 in the laws of lulav, chapter 7, and he learned from tefillin that if one took two lulavim he transgresses bal tosif; but if he added another species that is not among the four species of the lulav we say this one stands alone and that one stands alone, as stated in the chapter HaNichnakin, and he did not transgress bal tosif, end quote — and these are the words of the Magen Avraham. I omitted the words of the above Maggid Mishneh and the Gemara — meaning the words of the Maggid Mishneh were omitted from both, but the words of the Gemara were omitted only regarding clothing; but the Levush HaChadash brought it, as explained. And what he wrote from that of HaNichnakin — meaning what we say in Sanhedrin, folio 88 side b: R. Eliezer said in the name of R. Oshaya, a rebellious elder is liable only when he rules on a matter uprooted from the Torah and its explanation is from the words of the Scribes and it may be added to, and if he adds he detracts; and we have only tefillin, whose root is from the Torah, as it is written "and you shall bind them for a sign," etc., but it is not explicit what it is, and its explanation is from the words of the Scribes, for thus the Oral Law received the law of tefillin, and it may be added to beyond four batim, and if he adds he detracts. And he asks: if one made four batim and brought another bayit and placed it beside them, this one stands alone and that one stands alone (and meaning: there is no bal tosif, for the fifth bayit has no connection with the four batim); and if he made five batim (meaning glued together) it is defective and stands (Rashi explained: from the outset he did not do properly, etc., and we require "he detracts" — for now that it was kosher from the outset he invalidates it with his addition, end quote). And he answers: R. Zeira said, the outer bayit that does not see the air is invalid (Rashi explained: after he made four batim and it was kosher from the outset he added and glued to it a fifth bayit, and so too the Rambam, chapter 7 of Hilchot Mamrim). Thus if one places four batim and places another fifth bayit beside them and they are not glued, there is no bal tosif, for we say this one stands alone, etc.; and if so, all the more so two pairs of one order, which is somewhat comparable to tzitzit per the above Levush — all the more so there is no bal tosif.

For there it writes a different parasha, etc., and this is the view of the above Maggid Mishneh. And that the Gemara does not answer that it deals with one who added one parasha from the four parshiyot of tefillin — one can say he holds as the Rashba wrote in responsum 731, brought in Kafot Temarim on Sukkah, folio 31 side b, that in such a case although he transgressed bal tosif, nevertheless he fulfilled the mitzvah; and if so, even if it deals with adding one of four parshiyot, still it is difficult, for a rebellious elder requires that if he adds he detracts, and since he fulfilled the mitzvah there is no "he detracts" — only that the beginning of the difficulty from Sanhedrin is from the words of the questioner who said this one stands alone, etc., which implies there is no bal tosif; and for this the Magen Avraham had to answer that he thought he added a different parasha (and meaning: the questioner took such a case, for thus is the way of the matter more that a rebellious elder would err to add a fifth parasha and not to rule to write one parasha twice); but the answerer need not answer that he added a parasha from the four parshiyot, for the difficulty still stands as above (only that for the Maggid Mishneh one must say that he too holds as the Magen Avraham, as above, and from the words of Kafot Temarim there it appears he holds that even if he added he does not fulfill the mitzvah). And that the Rosh had to explain that the same applies to straps because they are invalid — one must say it is like a different parasha, because the Maggid Mishneh wrote there regarding lulav: if one brought another species, although there is no bal tosif, nevertheless there is a rabbinic prohibition, see there — only that there in Sanhedrin it deals with Torah law, for it deals with the death penalty of a rebellious elder; therefore to fulfill even rabbinic prohibition the Rosh had to explain that one should intend that they are like straps. And see the Magen Avraham, siman 651, seif katan 27.
