And if — Taz in Shulchan Aruch: the gloss now has the words "and remove them immediately" and the words "and some say" — but it is a gloss of Beur HaGra note 5: for the middle view is the view of Semag that if he does not know how to intend two pairs equally he places one pair and removes them immediately and places two with one blessing, even though he interrupts with the shel rosh of the first before the second shel yad — it is fine (for two blessings refer to both). And some say the view of the Ashkenazi responsum (Maharil), and so is the view of Taz, except that he explained well that Semag likewise speaks of one for whom the two pairs of tefillin are equal; if so, if Rabbeinu Tam's happened to him first, "do not pass over mitzvot" does not apply, etc., and he blesses on shel yad and shel rosh and removes them and places those of Rashi afterward, or the reverse. And some say that if he cannot — meaning he cannot intend — he must bless specifically on those of Rashi for they are primary, and Rabbeinu Tam's he places afterward after prayer without a blessing, and he need not remove Rashi's immediately and place Rabbeinu Tam with one blessing, for Rashi is primary, like maror where one blesses first and wraps without a blessing afterward. But per the old text that did not write "and remove them immediately" and also "and some say" is absent — only "and some say he cannot intend" — it was difficult for him that it contradicts what he wrote in Semag and afterward as Ashkenazi responsum; therefore he emended "and some say," etc.; but the words "and remove them immediately" are necessary, for it follows that with one blessing, meaning without interruption he removes them — and that is for one for whom both are equal; and this seems the view of Levush — see there. And what he wrote for the middle view to place Rabbeinu Tam first is an addition from Levush, since we hold for one for whom both are equal; if so he blesses and places Rabbeinu Tam's and removes them and places Rashi's and reads Keriat Shema in Rashi's so as not to separate from the congregation. And a third view: Ashkenazi responsum — Rashi is primary; but Netziv understood they are one, and the explanation: if he cannot intend he places one and afterward others with one blessing; and he explains that is "and if he cannot place them," meaning he cannot intend — he places Rashi's first as Ashkenazi responsum and Semag and Maharil explain; if so what he wrote "and reads Keriat Shema" is Beit Yosef's view, for per Semag he places both with one blessing — it follows there is no interruption of speech and prayer for a long time, and necessarily he removes them immediately, as Taz wrote "and I saw," etc., intending what Netziv wrote; and per what is unnecessary, the words of Levush — and Netziv challenged Levush, for Taz wrote explicitly that Semag wrote his words for one for whom both are equal, etc.; and see in Magen Avraham note 4 on this — see there. And if he found shel yad and a strap without parshiyot and doubts whether of Rabbeinu Tam — it is fine to place parshiyot of Rashi, for the majority are of Rashi and we elevate; and we are expert and Rashi is primary. Processing requires study; also straps from mundane to holy — understand.
