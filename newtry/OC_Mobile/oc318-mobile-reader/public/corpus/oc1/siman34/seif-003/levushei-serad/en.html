Taz, seif katan 2. “They did not accept all the answers, etc., even D'Rabbanan.” Meaning, according to that answer, the first Tanna's reason is not that bal tosif applies without intent, but that Shabbos is a time for tefillin, and therefore bal tosif applies within the proper time. Thus, although with two pairs there is only a D'Rabbanan form of bal tosif, that is sufficient to forbid bringing in two pairs. Taz asks: does this answer assume that he has intent? Certainly he does not, for he intends only rescue. This answer therefore adds that even Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel agrees that bal tosif does not require intent, and permits two pairs only because Shabbos is not a time for tefillin. The original difficulty thus returns. Since he has no intent, why does the first Tanna forbid it? Although Shabbos is a time for tefillin and it is therefore within the proper time, D'Rabbanan bal tosif should still require intent. Taz answers that there the prohibition of carrying exists independently. Although according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who holds Shabbos is not a time for tefillin and there is no bal tosif, carrying them in this manner is permitted, the addition of an appearance of bal tosif, even without intent, combines with the carrying concern and makes it forbidden. One cannot ask that according to the Gemara's first answer the matter is likewise resolved by saying that the first Tanna forbids it because bal tosif applies without intent and joins with carrying. If so, why would Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel permit it? Even if he maintains that Torah-level bal tosif requires intent, it should still join with the carrying concern. Furthermore, according to the first Tanna, why did the Gemara need to say that Torah-level bal tosif does not require intent? It must be that according to that answer, the Gemara maintains that there is actual bal tosif here. According to the later answer, however, everyone agrees that there is no actual bal tosif here, because it is only D'Rabbanan and there is no intent. The first Tanna forbids it because Shabbos is within the proper time and the act resembles bal tosif, which joins with the carrying concern. Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel permits it because Shabbos is outside the proper time and it does not resemble bal tosif at all.
