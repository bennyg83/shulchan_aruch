Taz s.k. 2: they did not uphold all the resolutions, etc., even rabbinically. Meaning that for this resolution it fits that the reason of the first tanna is not because there is Torah bal tosif even without intention, but because Shabbos is the time for tefillin, and in his time there is Torah bal tosif; therefore even with two pairs, where there is no Torah bal tosif, rabbinically it suffices to forbid placing two pairs. And on this Taz challenged: is it that this change holds there is intention? Behold certainly there is no intention, for he intends for rescue alone; and this change adds to say that even Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel holds bal tosif does not require intention, only he permits because it is not the time for tefillin; if so, the difficulty returns to its place — since there is no intention, why did the first tanna forbid, for although Shabbos is the time for tefillin and there is Torah bal tosif in his time, nevertheless for rabbinic bal tosif we require intention. And on this he answered: it is different there, for without bal tosif there is a prohibition of burden — for according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who holds it is not the time for tefillin and there is no Torah bal tosif, it is permitted; nevertheless, to repair through joining Torah bal tosif without intention, we forbade him. And one should not challenge: if so, also for the first change of the Talmud it fits that the reason of the first tanna is because there is Torah bal tosif without intention; and although for rabbinic bal tosif intention is required, nevertheless it joins to burden — for if so, what is Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel's reason? Although he holds that even for Torah bal tosif intention is required, nevertheless it should join to burden; and also as to the first tanna, why was it necessary at all to say that for Torah bal tosif intention is not required — unless in that change the Talmud says that for the first tanna there is complete Torah bal tosif here, unlike in that change where in truth according to all there is no Torah bal tosif here, since it is rabbinic without intention; only for the first tanna, who is in his time and it appears like Torah bal tosif, it joins to burden; and for Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, who is not in his time, it does not appear at all like Torah bal tosif and does not join.
