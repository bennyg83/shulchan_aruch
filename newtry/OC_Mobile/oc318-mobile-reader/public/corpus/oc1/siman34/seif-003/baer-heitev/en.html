As an act of piety. I was asked about a man who had the practice of putting on Rabbeinu Tam tefillin after prayer openly before the congregation, whether this appeared arrogant. Responsa Maharshal HaLevi rules that it does appear arrogant and that he must discontinue the practice. Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 44, likewise writes that even if some people do so, there remains a concern of arrogance. If he puts them on in the presence of a great scholar who does not have the practice of putting them on except privately in his home, it certainly appears arrogant. See there.
