In piety. I was asked about a man who was accustomed to put on Rabbeinu Tam's tefillin after prayer publicly before the congregation — whether it appears as arrogance. Maharash Levi ruled in his responsum that it appears as arrogance and he must abolish his custom. And likewise in the responsum of Shevut Yaakov vol. 2 siman 44: even if some do so, there is concern of arrogance; and if he puts them on before a great person who is not accustomed to put them on except within his home — it certainly appears as arrogance — Peri Megadim.
