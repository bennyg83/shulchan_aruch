(16) So — meaning even if he wishes to place them only after prayer; and Beit Yosef wrote of one man who used to place tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam after prayer in public before the congregation — if it appears as yohara; Maharash Halevi ruled in a responsum that it appears as yohara and he must annul his custom; and similarly in responsa of Shvus Yaakov part 2 siman 44 that even if some do so there is yohara; and if he places them before a great person who is not accustomed to place them except within his house, it certainly appears as yohara — see there.

(17) In piety — since the world practices according to Rashi, one who is stringent upon himself in this appears as yohara if he is not established as one who is stringent upon himself also in other matters.
