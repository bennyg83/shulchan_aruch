One puts on Rashi's tefillin, etc. And some say: one puts on Rabbeinu Tam's first and blesses on them and removes them immediately, and afterward puts on Rashi's relying on the first blessing so that he recites Kerias Shema and prayer in Rashi's tefillin (Maharam Isserles understood thus from the words of Semag and Sefer HaTerumah). But this did not occur to them as he understood; and it may be said that they also hold like the responsum of the Ashkenazim — therefore it is correct to put on Rashi's first and bless, and afterward put on Rabbeinu Tam's as above, and read in them Shema and VeHayah Im Shamo'a, and all the more so VeYomer, since it is not only for that purpose — so that he not testify false testimony against himself.
