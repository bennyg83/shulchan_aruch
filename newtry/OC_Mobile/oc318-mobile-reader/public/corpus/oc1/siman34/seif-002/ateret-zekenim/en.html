And for Rabbeinu Tam in any case one must write them in the order they are written in the Torah, and if one changed the order they are invalid — whether for the arm tefillin or the head tefillin. Therefore for the arm tefillin, which are on one parchment, one writes Kadesh and VeHayah Ki Yevi'acha and leaves space for part of VeHayah, and writes Shema, and afterward writes VeHayah Im Shamo'a (Tur 32).
