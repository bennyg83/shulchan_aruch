4. Seif 2: A God-fearing person, etc.; and the others are merely like straps, etc. According to the Arizal, both pairs are sacred and both must be worn together. Machazik Berachah, letter 1, and Kuntres Acharon, letter 2. Shaar HaKavanos, discourse 6 concerning Rabbeinu Tam tefillin, states: Initially, my master the Arizal would put on Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam tefillin together during Shacharis and pray while wearing them, in accordance with the statement of the Sages in HaKometz Rabbah that there is room on the head to put on two pairs of tefillin, meaning simultaneously, for one after another there is room for a thousand pairs. At Minchah he would wear only Rabbeinu Tam tefillin. Later he made a pair of Rashi tefillin according to the dimensions of Shimusha Rabba, two fingerbreadths by two fingerbreadths. Their grooves were recessed down to the ma'abarta so that every compartment was separate from the next, and during stitching the sinew threads passed through the grooves themselves from side to side. In the morning he would wear both Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam tefillin, and at Minchah he would wear the Shimusha Rabba pair. He said that according to Shimusha Rabba these tefillin combine both the Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam arrangements, because the mochin of Abba, corresponding to Rabbeinu Tam tefillin, and the mochin of Imma, corresponding to Rashi tefillin, become intermingled and form one pair. This is why they measure two fingerbreadths by two fingerbreadths, corresponding to Abba and Imma. The manner of putting them on is as follows. First he inserts the Rashi arm tefillin, and afterward the Rabbeinu Tam arm tefillin. Both rest upon the biceps of the upper left arm. The Rashi tefillin should be higher, closer to the shoulder, and the Rabbeinu Tam tefillin lower, closer to the middle joint. Similarly, for the head, he first puts on the Rashi tefillin and afterward the Rabbeinu Tam tefillin above them. The knot of the Rashi tefillin should likewise be lower, upon the nape itself. The reason Rashi tefillin are put on first is that the mochin of Imma enter first, followed by the mochin of Abba, corresponding to Rabbeinu Tam tefillin. When wrapping the straps around the arm, place the Rabbeinu Tam strap beneath the Rashi strap, so that it is covered and only the Rashi strap is visible. This is because the lights of Abba are covered within the lights of Imma. Machazik Berachah, letter 8, cites Rabbi Moshe Zacuto that the Shimusha Rabba tefillin replace both pairs specifically at Minchah, but not at Shacharis. See there, and see also what is written in Kaf HaChayim, siman 27, letter 41, and siman 37, letter 10.
