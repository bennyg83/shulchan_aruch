Both of them. And one who is accustomed to put on two pairs together puts Rashi's below and Rabbeinu Tam's above — Taz. And likewise the Ari: that he inserts Rashi's first and positions them at the side of the shoulder, and afterward Rabbeinu Tam's at the side of the arm; and both on the skull, and he places on his head Rashi's lower and Rabbeinu Tam's higher; and both in the place fit for tefillin. And the straps of Rabbeinu Tam are beneath the straps of Rashi, covered by them so they are not exposed — only the straps of Rashi are visible; and Rabbeinu Tam's tefillin are smaller than Rashi's tefillin. See Sefer HaKavanot, tefillin chapter 10, 12 (and see in Eliyah Rabbah who rejected the words of the Taz and his proof). One who puts on arm tefillin according to Rabbeinu Tam and head tefillin according to Rashi and the Rambam — if he fulfills the obligation: Shevut Yaakov vol. 1 siman 2 ruled that he does not fulfill according to all opinions; but in a time of need it is permitted to put them on thus, for in a time of need one fulfills if he puts on one alone. Nevertheless he should bless only on the one written according to our custom, the view of Rashi and the Rambam; and if the head one was written according to Rashi's view, he should put on the head tefillin first, and when removing he removes the arm tefillin first — Peri Megadim.

In general. For necessarily one of them is invalid; but if he put on two pairs of valid tefillin he violates bal tosif. Magen Avraham. And see the Taz who elaborated, and Yad Aharon challenged him.

The place, etc. And some say that if he cannot, etc. — uncertain; that is, the intermediate view. And furthermore, even in the beginning of the first clause — where he knows how to intend the place — he blesses only on one of them; and it seems to me that this is what it means: in the first clause it speaks of one who puts both together on the arm and blesses and tightens them at once, and the same applies to the head tefillin. And afterward it says: and if he does not know how to intend the place and to put both together — meaning, when he tightens them at once he cannot intend the place and must tie them one opposite the other: he puts on according to one view the arm and head tefillin, and puts the others relying on the blessing; and we do not read "and remove them immediately." And afterward it says: and some say that if he cannot, etc. — meaning, even one opposite the other he cannot put them on at once, such as if he has a wound or is ashamed before people. Magen Avraham. And see Darchei Noam part 1 siman 2 and in Sefer Bnei Chayya.
