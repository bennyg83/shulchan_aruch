One who fears Heaven should fulfill both and make two pairs of tefillin and place both and intend at their placement: with those that follow the halacha I fulfill my obligation, and the rest are like ordinary straps, for there is room on the head to place two tefillin and likewise on the arm. And if he does not know to direct the place and to place both together, he places according to one view, hand and head, and places the others upon reliance on the first blessing. And there are those who say: if he cannot place them in one) compartment, he places Rashi tefillin and blesses on them and they remain upon him at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer; and after the prayer he places Rabbenu Tam tefillin without a blessing and reads in them Shema and Vehayah im shamoa.
