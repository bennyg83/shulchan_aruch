Seif 2: A God-fearing person, etc. This is the language of Rosh, Tur, and the other poskim.
And he should intend, etc. Because one of the two pairs is certainly invalid, as stated there concerning one who exchanged the passages, etc. Therefore, there is no prohibition of bal tosif. With two valid pairs, however, there is a prohibition of bal tosif, as stated at the beginning of chapter 10 of Eruvin. Even with invalid additions, bal tosif may apply if they are tied together with the valid mitzvah, as stated in the third chapter of Sukkah and in the chapter HaNechenakin. See Magen Avraham. This also resolves the wording of Rosh and Tur.
Because, etc., and likewise on the arm. Eruvin there.
Some say that if, etc.; he should not do so, etc. This refers to the “some say,” as written in the responsum cited above, because the principal halachah follows Rashi. He should act as is done with the additional matzah wrapping at the Pesach seder, without reciting a blessing.
