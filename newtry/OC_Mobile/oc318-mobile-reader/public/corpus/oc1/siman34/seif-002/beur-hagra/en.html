Siman 32: "There is room," etc. — the language of the Rosh and Tur and Shitah Mekubetzet.

And he should intend, etc. For one of them is certainly invalid, as stated: he exchanged their parashiyot, etc.; therefore there is no concern of bal tosif, even though with valid ones there is concern of bal tosif, as stated in the first chapter of Eruvin; and even with invalid ones, if one is tied with the valid one, as stated in Sukkah chapter 3 and chapter HaNichnakin and in Magen Avraham — and through this the words of the Rosh and Tur are settled.

For, etc., and likewise on the arm. Eruvin there.

And some say that if, etc., "he should not do," etc. — this refers to "some say," as stated in the above responsum, for the essential law is like Rashi; and he wrote: as they do in wrapping in the chapter "al peh" without a blessing.
