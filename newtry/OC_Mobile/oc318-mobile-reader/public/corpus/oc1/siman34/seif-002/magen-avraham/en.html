For from necessity one of them is invalid; but if one placed two pairs of kosher tefillin he transgresses bal tosif, as stated at the beginning of chapter 10 of Eruvin; and so too the Beit Yosef in the name of the Maggid Mishneh, and the Levush and Levush HaChadash were uncertain about this; and it is plain that the words of the above Maggid Mishneh and the above Gemara were omitted; and what he wrote from that of HaNichnakin is no proof, for there it writes a different parasha — if so, it is not relevant to tefillin at all; but with two kosher pairs one transgresses bal tosif. And see siman 10, seif 6.

And if he does not know, etc., and if he cannot, etc. — it needs investigation; meaning the middle case; and further, also at the beginning of the beginning, that one who knows to direct the place does not bless except on one of them — and the Levush HaChadash forced this; and in our opinion the first case in the beginning speaks of placing both together on the arm and blessing and fastening them in one compartment, and the same for the head; and afterward he speaks: and if he does not know to direct the place and to place both together — meaning when he fastens them in one compartment he cannot direct the place, and he must tie them one after the other; he places according to one view, hand and head, etc. And the Mordechai wrote: even though one interrupts with the blessing on the head tefillin between the first blessing of the hand and the second, it is permitted.

That if he cannot, etc. — even one after the other he cannot place them in one compartment, such as if he has a wound as written in siman 27, seif 7, or he is ashamed before people who mock him.
