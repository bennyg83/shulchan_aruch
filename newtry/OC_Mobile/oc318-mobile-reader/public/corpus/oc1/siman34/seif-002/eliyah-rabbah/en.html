[2] **He should place as the words of the one say, etc.** And then he should place first Rabbeinu Tam's and afterward Rashi's, and pray in Rashi's — Levush. And it seems he saw duplicated language in Shulchan Arukh; Levush learned to make two divisions in the matter, and wrote he should place Rabbeinu Tam's first and afterward Rashi's; and then wrote it is better he place Rashi's first, etc., as Shulchan Arukh concludes. But in Lechem Chamudot p. 91 he wrote this in his language, and it seems to me Shulchan Arukh initially wrote that if he does not know, etc., he should place one after the other and bless only on one; and then explains which he places first — and there are not here two divisions, end quote. And so Nachalat Tzvi disagrees and wrote that according to all, one should not bless first on Rabbeinu Tam's — see there. However, it seems in my humble opinion from Sefer HaTerumot siman 250 that one may bless first on Rabbeinu Tam's; and Taz and Rema, proofreaders of Shulchan Arukh; and some say that if he cannot place, etc.; and likewise in Shulchan Arukh it is printed with Ba'al Olat Tamid — it also seems like Levush's words. And I saw in Magen Avraham's commentary: at first he says he should place both and bless and fasten them in one bayit; and then he says "and if he does not know how to intend," etc. — meaning when they fasten in one bayit and need to tie them one after the other, he should place as the words of the one say, etc., so far. And one might say: if so, why did Shulchan Arukh write "and he should remove them from his hand"?
