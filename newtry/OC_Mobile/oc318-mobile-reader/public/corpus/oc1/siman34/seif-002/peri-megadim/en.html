And he shall place — Taz: Bach and Taz extended — they hold that two pairs constitute bal tosif; and in Sanhedrin 88b there is no difficulty that it is not a zaken mamre, and Bach holds like Taz; but Taz holds that according to Torah law with two pairs there is no Torah bal tosif, but rabbinically there is; and wherever there is Torah bal tosif, even without intention it is forbidden, but rabbinically without intention it is permitted if it is for a need. And Eruvin 96b — they say they disagree whether bal tosif requires intention: for the first tanna one pair and no more, for Torah bal tosif does not require intention, and the halacha is like the first tanna; if so we see that even in a rabbinic matter one violates Torah bal tosif without intention — and we wrote that rabbinically it certainly requires intention (and apparently a textual error "not" should read "requires intention"), and he answered that we do not hold this version but they disagree for the first tanna whether Shabbat is time for tefillin; and he asked: nevertheless, since Shabbat is time for tefillin, the reason is that he violates bal tosif in its time, and that is rabbinic, and rabbinically without intention is permitted — and he answered that there is also a prohibition of burden with two pairs, for the first pair is a mitzvah, and even though it is not a complete burden, with the addition of rabbinic bal tosif without intention they did not permit; and see in Raavad's explanation there. And I do not know why not plainly that the first tanna holds Shabbat is time for tefillin and they permitted only because of mitzvah, not because of clothing and ornament — therefore especially the first pair is a burden. And for what he wrote afterward in a question: according to the conclusion for the first tanna there is no room on the head to place two tefillin — it is unnecessary; it is not because of bal tosif but only because of burden alone.

However, so he wrote plainly that by Torah law even without intention it is bal tosif — it requires study: for although without intention one violates in its time, that is in general; but if he specifies that he does so because of doubt there is no bal tosif, as is proven in many places; and the case of four measures that became mixed in one measure of giving, where he gives in the measure of four what remains for water — there is no bal tosif; see Eruvin 100a. Also one who examines Sanhedrin 88b may say he made a fifth bay and placed it with them — although there is bal tosif, there is no "subtracts," for four batim on him stand alone and the person's skull stands alone — it is not bal tosif, and "adding subtracts" we require that all be disqualified; and see Rashi there s.v. "and this is not subtracting" — here there is bal tosif, here when he joins them together it subtracts and disqualifies all; and "the hands of tefillin" entirely — and they answer there: thus when they were on his head he joined a fifth bay and one does not see — and I do not know if so it is valid; nevertheless it subtracts and requires study; and Tosafot there s.v. "I do not know," and see in Magen Avraham note 3 on this; and here is not the place to elaborate; and see in the general introduction on this.

What he wrote in responsa of Rama part 1 letter 54 to settle his difficulty and the difficulty of Taz — see there. And we practice that afterward they place tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam after removing those of Rashi, and that is as the law explained in siman 97, paragraph 13; and one reads in Rabbeinu Tam two portions Shema and Vehayah im shamo'a, but the portion of tzitzit is unnecessary, because the concern lest he testify false testimony in reciting Keriat Shema without tefillin was remedied with two portions. And recently some remove tefillin of Rashi and place tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam after Kedushah of the Shemoneh Esreh; and in truth, besides doing contrary to the law explained in siman 27, paragraph 13, one must also have intention during the Shemoneh Esreh toward the Shatz not to engage in another matter — and I did not wish to ask them at all, and I do not want their answer.
