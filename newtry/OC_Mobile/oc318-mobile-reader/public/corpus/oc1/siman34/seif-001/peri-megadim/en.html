According to Rashi. And Rambam. See Ateres Zekeinim and Menachos 34b. Three views exist, though the poskim mention only two. Rashi and Rambam maintain that Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha are on the wearer's left, while Shema and Vehayah Im Shamoa follow them in Torah order. Rabbeinu Tam also places Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha on the wearer's left, but places Shema in the outer compartment on the wearer's right and Vehayah Im Shamoa beside it. The reader then reads them in the order of their compartments. A third view is Raavad's. He explains that Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha are on the wearer's right, while Shema and Vehayah Im Shamoa are on his left, so that the reader standing opposite reads Shema and Vehayah Im Shamoa in their Torah order. This also gives special meaning to the baraisa's phrase “one who reads reads them in order.” A support is the verse, “The Name of Hashem is called upon you, and they shall fear you,” since Shema and Vehayah Im Shamoa contain acceptance of the yoke of Heaven. Nevertheless, we follow only Rabbeinu Tam's alternative to Rashi. It is surprising that Beit Yosef did not mention Raavad, perhaps because he regarded the view as rejected. Therefore one should make only two pairs, as Shulchan Aruch states, not three.
