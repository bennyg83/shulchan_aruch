(In Taz note 2) time for tefillin — even rabbinically: for per the view that Shabbat is time for tefillin, one pair alone is forbidden because of bal tosif; but per the view that Shabbat is not time for tefillin he brings in two, for there is no bal tosif here even rabbinically — such is Taz's intent; and there is a slight omission of language here. And what he wrote "and one should not say the reason," etc. — meaning even for this answer it is difficult for the first tanna why he holds he brings in one pair alone; therefore he answered because without Torah bal tosif there is prohibition because of burden, etc., unlike the other answers there where they disagree whether transgressing Torah bal tosif requires intention, etc. — one cannot say so, for it implies in the Gemara in the first tanna's reason because he transgressed bal tosif even without intention; and in truth it is not so. And further, if so what is Rashbag's reason — although he requires intention, nevertheless it would be a burden; and if we say Rashbag does not hold this reasoning — if so their dispute is not whether intention is required or not, but only whether he considers it a burden or not; rather they must disagree on bal tosif alone: for the first tanna he transgressed bal tosif without intention; if so Torah bal tosif here is only rabbinic; and therefore he had to answer that those answers do not stand.
