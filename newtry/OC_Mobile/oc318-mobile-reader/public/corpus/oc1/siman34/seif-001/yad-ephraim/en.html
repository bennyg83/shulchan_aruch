In Taz, seif katan 2: "a time for tefillin," even rabbinically. According to the view that Shabbos is a time for tefillin, only 1 pair may be brought in, because wearing an additional pair is prohibited because of Bal Tosif. According to the view that Shabbos is not a time for tefillin, 2 pairs may be brought in, because there is no Bal Tosif here even rabbinically. This is Taz's intent, although a few words are missing from the text. It cannot be explained that the underlying distinction is whether violation of Bal Tosif requires intent, or anything similar, because the Gemara implies that the first Tanna's reason is that he violates Bal Tosif even without intent, and that is not actually so. Moreover, if that were the point, what would be the reasoning of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel? Even if intent were required, the extra pair should still be considered a burden. If one were to say that Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel rejects that consideration, then their dispute would not concern whether intent is required for Bal Tosif, but whether the additional pair is considered a burden. Rather, they dispute Bal Tosif alone: according to the first Tanna, one violates Bal Tosif here even without intent. In this setting, however, the Bal Tosif concern is only rabbinic. Taz therefore had to explain that the other proposed answers do not stand.
