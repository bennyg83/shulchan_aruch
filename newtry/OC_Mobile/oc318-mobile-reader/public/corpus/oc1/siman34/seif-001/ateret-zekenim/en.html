I found in the writings of the Ari that both interpretations are true, and through each of the two pairs one effects a special action by putting them on, as explained there regarding their secret meaning. And thus the Gemara says: there is room on the head to put two tefillin, and tefillin of Rabbeinu Tam are on a higher level than tefillin of Rashi; and because they are greater in their level, most of the world cannot effect that action through putting them on — therefore they refrained from putting them on, end quote. These are the words of Zohar Chadash folio 11b, and thus: since those of the later academy are not expert, they equate the two pairs of tefillin out of doubt, not knowing the secret that both are required. Similarly the words of the Zohar in Pinchas regarding the tekufot, and thus: therefore these Babylonians did not know the secret of the dibbur and the wailing, and did not know that both are required. And in Sefer Matzat Shmura he warns very greatly to put both at one time and brings a great secret about not separating them. In any case one should conduct himself as written in the siddur of kavanot, and thus: the arranger — and the Ari did not put them on at one time because of arrogance, but only after prayer, as mentioned in the Shulchan Aruch.
