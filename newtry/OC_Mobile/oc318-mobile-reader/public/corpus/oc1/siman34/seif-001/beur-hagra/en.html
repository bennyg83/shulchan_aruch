(1) For Rashi, etc. As stated above, "and the reader," etc.; and in the Mekhilta they write them in order, etc. — as stated above, siman 32.

And for Rabbeinu Tam, etc. Since he separates them in this order — Kadesh, etc., Shema, etc. — and the above proofs rejected in Tosafos there, s.v. vehakore, etc., and the Rosh, and Shitah Mekubetzet that the Mekhilta speaks of writing; and what it says "and the reader," etc., is the end of the baraisa of "how are they arranged," which explains that it refers to the reader, in the order they are explained in the baraisa.

And the custom, etc. And likewise the Ramban elaborated in his responsum siman 234 to prove like Rashi; also what Rabbeinu Tam challenged on Rashi from what is stated: Kadesh and VeHayah Ki Yevi'acha from the right, Shema, etc., from the left, and VeHayah Im Shamo'a there — and his words there; and in conclusion our view is like Rashi and the Rambam, and on them we rely and act; and so practiced Rabbenu Yitzchak and the Ramban, and they acted themselves; and such is the essential law.
