Seif 1: According to Rashi, etc. As stated there, “and one who reads, etc.” The Mechilta likewise says that they are written in their order, as explained above at the beginning of siman 32.
And according to Rabbeinu Tam, etc. This is derived from the fact that the passages are divided in this order: Kadesh, etc., Shema, etc. Tosafos there, s.v. “and one who reads,” Rosh, and the other poskim reject the proofs cited above. They explain that the Mechilta refers to the order of writing. The phrase “and one who reads, etc.” is the conclusion of the baraisa explaining how they are arranged, and refers to the reader, in the order in which the passages are listed in the baraisa.
And the custom, etc. Ramban, in responsum 234, likewise discusses the matter at length and proves that the halachah follows Rashi. He also addresses Rabbeinu Tam's challenge to Rashi from the statement there that Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha are on the right, and Shema, etc., on the left, and resolves it. He concludes: “The final matter is that our view follows Rashi and Rambam, of blessed memory. Upon them we rely and act in practice. Rabbeinu Yonah and Ramban likewise followed this practice themselves. This is the principal ruling.”
