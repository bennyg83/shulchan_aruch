The order of placing the parshiyot in tefillin, and those who are meticulous who have two pairs of tefillin. And in it are four seifim: The order
of placing them in the batim: per Rashi and the Rambam, Kadesh on the left of the wearer in the outermost bayit; after it Ki Yeviacha in the second bayit, and Shema in the third bayit, and Vehayah im shamoa in the fourth bayit, which is the outer bayit to his right. Per Rabbenu Tam, in the third bayit Vehayah im shamoa, and in the fourth bayit, which is the outer one, Shema. And the custom of the world follows Rashi and the Rambam.
