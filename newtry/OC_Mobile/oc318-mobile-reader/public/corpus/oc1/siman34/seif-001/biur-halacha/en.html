**The order of placing them** — See in Mishna Berurah; and it is straightforward that even in hand tefillin — such as where he does not have head tefillin but has two arm tefillin, as explained above that he wraps leather on one of them — only if they are placed in the required order, as explained in Beit Yosef [see in Beit Chadash, from which the words of Beit Yosef are clarified], who agreed that this law that if one exchanged their parshiyot they are invalid applies also to hand tefillin. And apparently the same applies if he wrote them on four parchments and placed them in one bayit, that one fulfills in this way even if they are not glued, as explained in siman 32 s.e. 17 in Beit Yosef — that one must likewise be careful that they be placed specifically in order, Kadesh on the right, etc. Or perhaps here their interchange is not as noticeable as in one parchment in head tefillin where he writes V'hayah after Shema. And this doubt also applies to Semak — perhaps Semak would also concede here that it is invalid since they are four parchments; or perhaps for Semak the law of fixed placement of parshiyot in head tefillin was never stated at all, since it is possible to write them on one parchment — and it requires further study.
