For example, where one lacks arm tefillin but has two pairs of head tefillin, and, as explained above, covers one of them with leather to make arm tefillin, this is valid only if the passages are positioned there in their proper order, as explained by Beit Yosef. Bach's discussion clarifies Beit Yosef's words and agrees that the rule invalidating exchanged passages applies also to arm tefillin. It would appear that the same applies if the passages were written on four parchments and placed in a single bayis, which is valid even if the parchments were not glued together, as explained in siman 32, seif 47. One must then ensure that they are placed specifically in the order Kadesh on the right, etc. Alternatively, perhaps an exchange is less noticeable here than where arm tefillin are written on one parchment and Vehayah Im Shamoa is written after Shema. According to Semak as well, this matter remains uncertain. Perhaps Semak would agree that it is invalid when the passages are on four parchments, or perhaps Semak holds that there is no fixed placement rule at all for the passages of arm tefillin, since they may be written on a single parchment. This requires study.
