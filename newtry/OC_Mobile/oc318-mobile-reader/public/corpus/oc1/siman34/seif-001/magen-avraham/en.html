On the left of the wearer. It seems to me that a left-handed person follows the world's right and left; for we say in the Gemara the reason that Kadesh should be on the right of the reader is so that he read in the order written in the Torah.

And for Rabbenu Tam, etc. — nevertheless one must write them as written in siman 32, seif 1 in the gloss; therefore one must leave a blank space in parashat Vehayah im shamoa and write parashat Shema at the end of the klaf, and afterward write parashat Vehayah im shamoa in the middle.
