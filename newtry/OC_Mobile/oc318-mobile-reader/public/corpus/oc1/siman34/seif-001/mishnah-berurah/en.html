(1) The order of their placement — know that according to everyone, if he switched any parasha from the bayit designated for it they are invalid, even though there are four parshiyot in four batim; and all the more if he placed two parshiyot in one bayit.

(2) The wearer — and even if the wearer is left-handed we follow the world's right and left.

(3) And for Rabbenu Tam — the poskim wrote that Rabbenu Tam too concedes that we require their writing in the order they are written in the Torah, which is Kadesh, Vehayah ki yeviacha, Shema, Vehayah im shamoa; only he disagrees with Rashi regarding placing them in the batim. Therefore one who wishes to write tefillin according to Rabbenu Tam should write in the arm tefillin, which are on one klaf, Kadesh and Vehayah ki yeviacha, and leave a blank space for Vehayah im shamoa and write Shema, and afterward write Vehayah im shamoa; and in the head tefillin he writes them on four klafim in proper order and arranges them in the batim: Kadesh, Vehayah ki, Vehayah im, Shema. And regarding the laws of open and closed parshiyot in tefillin, how to conduct oneself according to his view — we already explained above at length in siman 32; see there.

(4) Like Rashi and the Rambam — and the Beit Yosef and other Acharonim wrote that this is the essential view, and the Gra agreed in his commentary.
