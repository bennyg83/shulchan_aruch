(1) According to Rashi and Rambam. Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha are placed to the wearer's left, and Shema and Vehayah Im Shamoa to his right, all in the order in which they appear in the Torah.
(2) And according to Rabbeinu Tam. Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha are likewise to the wearer's left, but on the right side Shema is in the outer compartment and Vehayah Im Shamoa beside it toward the center. Thus the two passages beginning Vehayah are adjacent.
(3) In the third compartment. This applies only to their placement in the compartments. They must still be written in the order in which they appear in the Torah. For arm tefillin, written on one parchment, the scribe writes Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha, leaves space for Vehayah Im Shamoa, writes Shema, and afterward returns to write Vehayah Im Shamoa. For head tefillin, written on four parchments, he writes them in Torah order. The rules of open and closed passages were explained above in siman 32.
(4) Like Rashi and Rambam. Beit Yosef and the other later authorities rule that this is the principal halachah. Gra agrees.
