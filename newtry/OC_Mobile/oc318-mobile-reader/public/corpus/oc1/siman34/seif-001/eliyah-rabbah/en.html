[1] **Two tefillin, etc.** One might say: if so, why did they rule in siman 301 regarding one who finds tefillin on Shabbat that he should take out only one pair? And one might say, as concluded at the beginning of "one who finds tefillin" — that Shabbat is time for tefillin, and the prohibition of bal tosif comes, and it makes it like a burden. If so, it is difficult — behold we maintain in siman 31 that Shabbat is not time for tefillin. And further I found in Tosafot Yom Tov, chapter "Bameh ishah," that he wondered at Tur's words in siman 31 mentioned above from siman 301, that Tur wrote the reason he should not fulfill with tefillin — lest he come to carry in the public domain, taking it from the outset if he comes at evening — Alma that it seems to him Shabbat is time for tefillin. And until my wonder increased, I examined, and it seems in my humble opinion there are three disputes in the matter. What we say "Shabbat is time for tefillin" — we mean there is also a mitzvah to place them on Shabbat; and we say in the baraita there according to this tanna that women are obligated in tefillin, for it is a positive time-bound commandment; and it seems to the Talmud that the tanna of the Mishnah holds like the tanna of the baraita — and examine. And according to one who says "and you shall observe the statute" — not on Shabbat and Yom Tov — there is a prohibition when placing tefillin on Shabbat, as explained in siman 29. And for Rabbi Akiva, who learned from "it shall be for you a sign" — Shabbat and Yom Tov, which are themselves a sign, came out — there is no prohibition when placing tefillin on Shabbat, only there is no obligation of blessing. And if so, we maintain like Rabbi Akiva, as explained in siman 31; therefore we maintain he should take out only one pair — since there is no prohibition to place tefillin, although it is only optional, nevertheless it is forbidden to add and he makes it like a burden; therefore Tur in siman 301 needed the reason "lest he come to carry in the public domain" — one may not say the reason is because of burden, for behold there is no prohibition to place them on Shabbat — and examine; in this everything is resolved satisfactorily. And now what is printed in Taz, who elaborated in responsum on the question of one who places Rashi and Rabbeinu Tam tefillin together — he should place Rashi's below toward the face and Rabbeinu Tam's above it — and he elaborated to analyze the Talmud sugyot; see there. And in what I explained, all his words are rejected for one who examines — and examine; and in any event his words are strained.
