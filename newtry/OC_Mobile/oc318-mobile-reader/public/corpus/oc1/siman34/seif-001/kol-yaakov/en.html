Seif 1: Their order, etc.; Kadesh is to the left of the wearer, etc. Even if the wearer is left-handed, we do not follow his personal right and left, but the right and left of the reader, meaning the ordinary right and left of all people. Magen Avraham, seif katan 1; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 1; and the later authorities. The same applies to the arm tefillin. The beginning of Kadesh should be positioned toward the ordinary left, outward, so that the passages are read in order. For a left-handed person, the beginning of Kadesh is still toward the ordinary left and toward the wearer's inner side, not toward his right and outer side. Peri Megadim there.
