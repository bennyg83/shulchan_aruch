1. Seif 1: Their order, etc.; Kadesh is to the left of the wearer, etc. Even if the wearer is left-handed, we do not follow his personal right and left, but the right and left of the reader, meaning the ordinary right and left of all people. Magen Avraham, seif katan 1; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 1; and the later authorities. The same applies to the arm tefillin. The beginning of Kadesh should be positioned toward the ordinary left, outward, so that the passages are read in order. For a left-handed person, the beginning of Kadesh is still toward the ordinary left and toward the wearer's inner side, not toward his right and outer side. Peri Megadim there.
2. There: And after it Vehayah Ki Yeviacha in the second compartment, etc. If he exchanged any passage and placed it in a compartment other than the one designated for it, the tefillin are invalid until corrected. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 2, and the later authorities.
3. There: And according to Rabbeinu Tam, in the third compartment, etc. Nevertheless, the passages must be written in the order in which they appear in the Torah. If he changed that order, they are invalid, both for the arm and for the head. Therefore, in the arm tefillin, whose passages are written on one parchment, he writes Kadesh and Vehayah Ki Yeviacha, leaves blank the place for Vehayah Im Shamoa, writes Shema, and afterward writes Vehayah Im Shamoa. Tur; Beit Yosef; Magen Avraham, seif katan 2; Ateres Zekeinim; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, letter 3; and the later authorities. See what we wrote above in siman 32, letter 169.
