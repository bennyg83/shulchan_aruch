It is permitted to place before nightfall, etc. If the lower pot is resting on the fire, this has the status of something that adds heat by means of the fire, as stated above in siman 257, se'if 8. It is therefore forbidden even before nightfall to cover the pots from above with garments, because of hatmanah. If he does not cover them with garments, the case is governed by the laws of shehiyah, and the cold item must have been cooked to the stage of ma'achal ben Derusai before nightfall according to the Rama above in siman 253. So wrote the Magen Avraham and Peri Megadim. See below, siman 318, in Biur Halacha to se'if 6.
For this is not considered insulating, etc. See the Mishnah Berurah, where we wrote that if one arranged it in the manner of hatmanah on Shabbos, it is forbidden even when it will not reach yad soledes bo heat. This is evident from the Ran in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, and it is cited by the Gra. It appears there that the Gra agrees with this view, for he brings support for it; see there. The words of the Peri Megadim in Mishbetzos Zahav imply that in our case, where the item is cold, if it cannot reach yad soledes bo heat, at which point one would become liable for cooking, it is permitted even in the manner of hatmanah. For according to his understanding, the Beit Yosef maintains that when the upper vessel touches the urn and is partially insulated within it, this is included in hatmanah, and nevertheless he permits it if it will not thereby reach yad soledes bo heat. This is contrary to Rabbeinu Yonah and the Gra. His interpretation of the Beit Yosef, however, is not compelling. One who examines the Beit Yosef throughout this discussion will see that he is not speaking of a case arranged as hatmanah, but of one in which the vessel is placed above the pot while remaining entirely uncovered. It is therefore permitted on Shabbos when it will not reach yad soledes bo heat. If it were arranged as hatmanah, it is possible that he too would agree with the Ran in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. The Beit Yosef's statement that the lower pot is cooling was intended to explain the Tur's words, that this is not considered insulating in a substance that adds heat, which imply that before nightfall it is permitted even in the manner of hatmanah. Machatzis HaShekel explained that according to the Magen Avraham it is unnecessary to interpret the case as one of hatmanah, and the end of the Taz's words likewise implies this. Nevertheless, as a matter of practical law, it is not necessary to say that they disagree with the Beit Yosef, whose reasoning implies that even in the manner of hatmanah it is permitted, especially since Tosefes Shabbos and the Gra wrote this explicitly. I therefore cited their words. Know that here the Beit Yosef gives the reason that the lower pot is cooling, as we wrote in the Mishnah Berurah. Above, however, in siman 257, the Beit Yosef himself ruled regarding placing a pot upon a stove whose coals were covered with ash that it is considered something that adds heat, even though the pot naturally cools, contrary to Rashi. Ma'amar Mordechai has already raised this difficulty. It must be that the case there is more stringent because the heat of the stove is very great.
