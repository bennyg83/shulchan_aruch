As one who insulates. Those who, during the winter, take a vessel containing a beverage on Shabbos and insert it into a vessel filled with hot water so that its chill will dissipate, and then drink the beverage, do not act properly. This is literally the manner of hatmanah, since the entire vessel is insulated within it, and hatmanah is forbidden even for a short time. The principal basis for permitting the case here is that it is not actually insulated within the lower pot, but merely rests upon it. Taz; see there. Eliyah Rabbah disagrees with him and permits even when it is entirely insulated within the hot water, since it is a kli sheni and therefore does not add heat. In siman 257 it is explained that on Shabbos one may insulate something in a material that does not add heat so that its chill will dissipate; see there.
