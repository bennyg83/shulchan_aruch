Seif 1 — permitted, etc., because it is not. See Taz in the name of Beit Yosef, and likewise Tosafot there, in the name of R' Baruch; but on Shabbat it is forbidden even though it is permitted to insulate something cold specifically so that it not become cold, etc., as written siman 257 seif 6; and for that which is written chaziyah, etc., danach koza, etc., and as Ran wrote in the name of R' Yonah that it speaks of insulation since it teaches in this chapter, and Rashi ZL holds it is permitted since it does not add heat, and it is permitted to insulate something cold — and he forbade him as stated.
