Seif 11. In the gloss: “Wax or a candle donated to the synagogue by an apostate or a gentile may not be lit.” Note: Regarding whether a donation from a gentile may be accepted for building a synagogue, see what I wrote in my responsa on Yoreh Deah, Orach Chayim edition, siman 113, in the laws of tzedakah, siman 254, in my responsum to the holy community of Dynów, with Hashem’s help. Examine it carefully.
