I am astonished, for Maharam there permits even their paroches, upon which they place the Torah scroll, because they also place the Torah scroll on the cloth on the table. But a paroches that is not so, it appears that even making it into a cloth for the lectern is permitted; so it also implies in Responsa Bach, siman 17. What he wrote there, that it is forbidden to make from it a canopy for a groom and bride, is not the custom, and Magen Avraham also wrote that the heart of beis din stipulates regarding it. Maharil wrote: a cloth spread over the table may not be spread over the lectern before the chazzan. The same applies that it is forbidden to make from it a paroches. However, if they sometimes place a bare Torah scroll upon it, it is permitted, as implied in Responsa Maharam there. Although the cloth lies there more constantly than a paroches, nevertheless, since it does not always lie upon it, it is no better, and it is not comparable to seif 6, as mentioned above. It is obvious that it is forbidden to wrap the cloth of a Torah scroll around a haftarah book.
