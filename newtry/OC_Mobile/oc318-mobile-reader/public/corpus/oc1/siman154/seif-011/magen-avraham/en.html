It is forbidden to light them. The same applies to every mitzvah candle, and the same applies to all mitzvah matters: one may not make them from something made for idolatry. See siman 11, seif 8, and siman 586, seif 3. In Knesses HaGedolah he wrote in the name of Re'em, part 1, siman 79, that even if idolatry was worshipped in a house on a fixed basis, it is permitted to pray in it. Perhaps he holds that something attached is different; and although its building is considered detached, nevertheless it resembles something attached. This requires study in Avodah Zarah 46 and 47. Here it is different, for they did not worship the house itself. See Yoreh Deah, siman 145, seif 3. One should not bring proof from what is stated in Avodah Zarah 43, that they set up an image in a synagogue, and nevertheless they prayed in it, for there the image came into its domain.
