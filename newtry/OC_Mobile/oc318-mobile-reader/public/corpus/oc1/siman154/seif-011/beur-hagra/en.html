Seif 11: Candles, etc. As stated in Avodah Zarah 47a regarding an asheirah that was nullified, etc.
An apostate, etc. Although donations are accepted from a non-Jew, as stated at the beginning of Arachin, because sacrifices are accepted from them, as Tosafos write in Bava Basra 8a, s.v. yasiv, concerning “and he accepted,” etc. This does not apply to an apostate, as stated at the beginning of Chullin from “from among you,” etc., and as written at the end of Yoreh Deah 254.
