Holy — Av Bet Din; and see in Sheyarei Berachah what he wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yehudah ben the Rash in his book Chukkat HaTorah: they asked one who had a Torah scroll placed in an ark and made another ark and placed it in it — whether he may use the first ark for placing on it gemarot and commentaries and other books; and one sage wrote it is permitted, with proof from the first chapter of Shabbat regarding not hiding terumah near books because of loss — if not for that it would be permitted because this is holy and this is holy. And this is not proof, for there the Torah scroll stood in its place and its holiness remained; and further, perhaps the holiness of terumah is like the holiness of a Torah scroll, but here, since the Torah scroll is not there, it is forbidden to place gemarot and commentaries, for one lowers the ark from its holiness, as we say regarding the box of arpita that it is forbidden to make from it a mini-seat, end of his words. And see responsa Noda BiYehudah siman 9, who permits placing invalid Torah scrolls in the Land of Israel by virtue of the holiness of the ark, but it is forbidden lest they come to a stumbling block of reading from it; understand well.
