Seif katan 11: He raises an objection, etc. Mordechai implies that even an ark built into a wall, as in seif 1, may not be converted into a chair. Mordechai writes in the chapter Bnei HaIr, concerning such an ark, that it is not an article serving sacred objects, because only a chest-shaped ark made for honor rather than protection is called an aron kodesh. He proves this from Rava’s term “box” regarding a damaged ark, from which one may make a smaller box but not a chair. Although such an item is not called an aron kodesh, it still may not be lowered into a chair. The same therefore applies to an ark built into a wall. Although Magen Avraham, seif katan 4, writes that it has only the sanctity of a synagogue, this cannot be exact. He means only that it lacks the full sanctity of an aron kodesh; it nevertheless has greater sanctity than an ordinary synagogue area. See seif katan 14.
