Seif katan 4: It is permitted, etc. And it has the law of, etc. The phrase “it is permitted for everyone” is certainly not literal, for what prohibition exists in removing the Sefer Torah from this ark and placing it in another? Rather, it means that the first ark has no sanctity. Magen Avraham explains that specifically the sanctity of an aron kodesh does not apply, but the sanctity of a synagogue does, since it is no worse than the other areas of the synagogue.
