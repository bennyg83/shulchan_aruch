12) The Radbaz writes in his responsa, volume 3, siman 530, that throughout all Jewish lands, from the rising of the sun to its setting, the custom is to place the Sefer Torah upright, and the custom of our ancestors is Torah. Although Rabbeinu Tam holds that it is preferable for it to lie horizontally, he follows his own view that a mezuzah too should be horizontal, like a Sefer Torah and the Luchos, as stated in Tosafos on Menachos 33a, s.v. ha. The Rambam and most of the commentators, of blessed memory, disagree with him. Just as they disagree with him regarding a mezuzah, they disagree with him regarding placement of a Sefer Torah, etc. Although the Sefer Torah in the Aron was horizontal, this is not difficult, because it was not positioned for reading, unlike our sefarim. See his full discussion. Petach HaDevir, paragraph 2, cites him and provides several additional supports. He writes that this is also Maran's view in siman 32, in the Beit Yosef and in the Shulchan Aruch there, seif 45, regarding the passages of tefillin, and in Yoreh De'ah, siman 289, seif 6, regarding a mezuzah. Maran rules like Rashi, the Rambam, and the Tur that it must stand upright, unlike Rabbeinu Tam. He questions the words of Adnei Paz, who writes that one must make a chest with the Sefer Torah lying inside it, because that is the respectful manner, etc.; see there.
