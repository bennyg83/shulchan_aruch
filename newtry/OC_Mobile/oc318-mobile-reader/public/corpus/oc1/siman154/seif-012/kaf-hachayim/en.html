(12) Radbaz wrote in responsa volume 3 siman 370 that in all the provinces of Israel from the rising of the sun until its setting they practice placing the Torah scroll standing, and the custom of our fathers is Torah. And although Rabbeinu Tam holds that lying is preferable — that is, he goes according to his view, who said regarding a mezuzah too that it lies like a Torah scroll and the tablets (as is found in Tosafot Menachot 33a s.v. ha) — but the Rambam and most commentators disagree with him; and just as they argue with him regarding a mezuzah, they argue with him regarding placing a Torah scroll, etc. And although the Torah scroll in the ark was lying — there is no question, because it was not standing for reading, unlike our scrolls — see Pischei Teshuvah. And Rav Betzalel Ashkenazi also wrote several supports for this, and wrote that this is also the view of Maran z"l in siman 32 in Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch there, as Magen HaElef regarding the parshiyot of tefillin; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 289 seif 6 regarding a mezuzah, that he ruled like Rashi and the Rambam and the Tur that it must be standing, and not like Rabbeinu Tam. And he wondered at the words of Rav Adni Paz, who wrote that one must make an ark-chest and the Torah scroll is placed inside it, which is a way of honor, etc.; see there.
