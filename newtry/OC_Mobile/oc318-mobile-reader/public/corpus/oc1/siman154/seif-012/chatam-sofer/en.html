There, in Magen Avraham, seif katan 20. Even to light from it. Note: this is not a kal vachomer, for lighting from it involves diminishing it and degrading the mitzvah, unlike reading by its light, which is merely sound, appearance, and smell, and these are not subject to me'ilah except D'Rabbanan. Perhaps in a synagogue it is even initially permitted. See what Tosafot wrote in Shabbos 21a, s.v. simchos, etc., and similarly in Pesachim 26a, s.v. me'ilah, etc.
