If it is repulsive: Even if there is sixty times as much against it, so that it is permitted for eating, since it is repulsive it is forbidden because of, Offer it now to your governor. Some are lenient where there is sixty times as much.
It is forbidden to light it in the synagogue: The Peri Megadim suggests that the same applies to other mitzvah lighting, such as Shabbos and Chanukah lights; it is forbidden if it is repulsive to him.
