Rather — Taz what he wrote to distinguish between this case and other partners, that possession is like them; and see Levush here, and there is a slight omission in the wording — for the second reason, even if they cheapened; and Magen Avraham note 23, see there on Levush — cloths (and maps) one does not make from sha'atnez, but a parochet is permitted — see there; and these are the words of Mordechai in the name of Or Zaru'a brought in Beit Yosef here; and Taz 14 Orach Chayim note 11, and parochet is explained there that it is permitted. And Beit HaRoeh note 22 sided to permit from ma'anteli and kilayim cloth; and in Mordechai here it is explained that it is forbidden; and per this, if one made from kilayim and placed it on a sefer Torah it is not holy — hekdesh in error; and likewise Beit HaRoeh — make a parochet from it; and one could say perhaps one must make entirely from it hekdesh and a vow in error, and one needs nothing.
