13) [Seif 2] Those who rent a house, etc. The Beit Yosef writes in the name of Mahari ben Chaviv that even if there is a residence above the house in which they pray, one should not protest, provided that cleanliness is maintained in the rooms above the prayer hall. The Taz, paragraph 1, cites him and writes: 'Therefore, one should not rent a room for prayer if the area above it is not clean.' Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, writes the same. See what we wrote above in siman 151, paragraphs 74 and 75, that one who guards his soul should distance himself from this and not live above a prayer hall; see there.
