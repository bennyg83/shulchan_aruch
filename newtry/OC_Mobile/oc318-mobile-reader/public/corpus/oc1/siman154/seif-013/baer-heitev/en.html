By its light. This refers specifically to a matter of Torah study, as is also implied by the prohibition in seif 14 against even lighting another lamp from it. This applies specifically to a person who donated a lamp to the synagogue, which is the subject under discussion. The lamps made by the treasurers, however, which people customarily take home after Motzaei Shabbos and use for mundane purposes, are subject to an implicit stipulation of the beis din. Nevertheless, from lamps that remain standing upon the menorah, one may not read mundane material. Magen Avraham.
