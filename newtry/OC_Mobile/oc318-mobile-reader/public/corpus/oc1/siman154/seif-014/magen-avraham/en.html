We do not light, etc. A havdalah candle, after one has already recited havdalah over it, has already completed its mitzvah, and it is permitted to light from it. Bach at the end of siman 674 in the name of Maharshal in a responsum. I do not know what sanctity there is in a havdalah candle, for one recites the blessing over the light of a furnace. Furthermore, even a Shabbos candle has no sanctity, and it is permitted to light from it.
For a great need. This requires study, for it implies that for a great need it is permitted as a matter of law, as written in seif 12 in the name of Nimukei Yosef, that for a woman in childbirth or for a sick person it is permitted, for this is called a mitzvah matter. One can say that Nimukei Yosef speaks where there is danger, as written there, whereas here he speaks where there is no danger.
