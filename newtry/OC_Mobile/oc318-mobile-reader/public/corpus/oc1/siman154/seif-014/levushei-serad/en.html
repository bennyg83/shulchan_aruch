Shulchan Aruch, seif 9: “To place it in a sefer.” This is because any change from one sanctity to another is forbidden unless it is to a higher sanctity; changing between equal levels is likewise forbidden, as explained above in siman 153, seif 4. The “some permit” here apparently refers to those who permit an equal-level change there in siman 153, for Beit Yosef cites no separate lenient opinion here. See there.
