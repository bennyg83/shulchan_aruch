We may not light an everyday candle from a synagogue candle. But there is one who says that this is [only] applicable when they are lit for a mitzvah; but when he needs to extinguish it, it is permissible. {Rama: Ram"a: However [we] are not accustomed to be careful about this, and do light a candle from them for a great need. And it is possible that the court stipulated about this also. And likewise with all of the things that the practice is to be lenient about things like these, it is perhaps from this reason (Piskei Mahara"i - siman 225).}
