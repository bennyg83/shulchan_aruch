For in everyone's understanding they were consecrated: All the more so coverings that the treasurers purchase for the needs of the books in the synagogue were certainly consecrated initially with this understanding, that it should be permitted to move them from one scroll to another.
It is forbidden to place it, etc.: Because every change from one sanctity to another sanctity is forbidden unless it is to a greater sanctity.
And some permit: Since in any event he is not lowering it from its sanctity.
Because of the condition of beis din, etc.: Meaning, although by strict law it would be proper to rule like the first opinion, which is the view of most poskim, that changing from one sanctity to an equal sanctity is forbidden, nevertheless here it is permitted because of the condition of beis din, etc.
