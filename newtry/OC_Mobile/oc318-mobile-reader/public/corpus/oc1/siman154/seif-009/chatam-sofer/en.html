There, in the gloss to seif 9. The heart of beis din. Note: see Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 4 of the laws of Shekalim, halacha 12, who raises a difficulty with this, for the principle that the heart of beis din stipulates does not apply to an individual's donation. This is not difficult at all, for here he merely uses a common expression. Certainly the first donors stipulated this explicitly, and afterward one who donates without specification donates according to the established practice. This is simple.
