It is permitted. See Taz and Magen Avraham. A donation is likewise not accepted from an apostate who publicly desecrates Shabbos. The same applies to an apostate who sins defiantly regarding any one prohibition, just as a sacrifice is not accepted from him. Levush writes that perhaps his intention is directed toward idolatry. According to this, a donation could be accepted from one who desecrates Shabbos, for although he is not trusted regarding the entire Torah, he is not necessarily an idolater. Perhaps Levush was troubled by the ruling of Mabit cited by Shach in Yoreh Deah 254:5. Yet in siman 259, seif 4, Levush writes that the word “from you” excludes an apostate from bringing a sacrifice. This requires study.
