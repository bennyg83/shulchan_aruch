And so they practice now on account of lev beit din matana, etc. — at first glance, per what we wrote in Mishna Berurah note 135 in the name of Turei Zahav, that in an individual's Torah scroll that he has in his home, lev beit din matana does not apply; if so, here, since in his home the wrapper was already consecrated for this Torah scroll before he consecrated the Torah scroll to the synagogue — what relevance does lev beit din have on this; and perhaps we say it deals when his intent from the outset at the time he acquired it was to consecrate it ultimately to the synagogue, and automatically he did not designate it for this one specifically; but if so, Rama should have explained this; and from the anonymous ruling it implies that in all cases it is permitted; and requires study.
