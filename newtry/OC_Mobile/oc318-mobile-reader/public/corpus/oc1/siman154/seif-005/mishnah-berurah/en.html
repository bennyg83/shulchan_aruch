A Torah scroll that became worn out: The same applies to Nevi'im and Kesuvim when they are written on hide, in scroll form, and with ink. Peri Megadim.
In an earthenware vessel: So that it will endure.
And they place it in genizah: The same applies to other books that became worn out, which must be placed in genizah, and it is forbidden to burn them. Although his intention is that they not come to disgrace, nevertheless he acts in a destructive manner. The Rambam wrote in negative commandment 65 that one who destroys sacred writings transgresses the prohibition of, You shall not do so to Hashem your G-d. The same applies to all accessories of sanctity; one must be careful about this. Acharonim.
One who studies halachos: Meaning Mishnayos and Beraisos, but did not serve Torah scholars in Gemara, which is the explanation of the reasons of the Mishnah.
