A Torah scroll that became worn out. The same applies to books, and it is forbidden to burn them. Be'er Sheva. It appears to me that the same applies to all accessories of sanctity, for this is included in 'you shall not do so to Hashem your God,' etc., as written at the end of siman 152. So the Rambam wrote in negative commandment 65, that one who destroys sacred writings transgresses a negative commandment.
