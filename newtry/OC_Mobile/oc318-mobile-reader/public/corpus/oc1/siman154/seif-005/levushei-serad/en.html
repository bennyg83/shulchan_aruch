Seif katan 8: “That is sacred.” Certainly, if it rests directly upon a sacred object without any intervening layer, it is considered an accessory even if it was not made entirely for that purpose, such as a chest and the like. If it was made entirely for honor, it is considered an accessory even when it rests upon the sacred object without an intervening layer, such as the covering placed upon the boards. A curtain, however, does not rest upon the sacred object at all but hangs as a partition. Therefore, even though it was made for honor, it is not considered an accessory, as Rama writes in seif 6.
