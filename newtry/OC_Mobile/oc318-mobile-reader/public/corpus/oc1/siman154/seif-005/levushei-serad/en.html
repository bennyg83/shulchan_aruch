Note 8 — in holiness. It is certain that if placed on a holy object without interruption it is called an article, even if not made completely for that purpose, such as a box and the like; but if made completely for honor it is called an article even if placed on the holy object without interruption, like the cover on the boards; unlike a curtain that is not placed at all on the holy object but hangs on a partition — therefore even though made for honor it is not called an article, as Rama wrote in seif 6.
