In Shulchan Aruch — one who rents a house; and see in responsa Keneset HaGedolah siman 26 that even though they have no holiness for the matter of sale, nevertheless they may not treat them with a custom of contempt; and see Maharash Halevi siman 46 who wrote that if it was done with royal permission, even though they have in it only a chazakah, it has the law of holiness; and in Beit Menachem, even without this there is holiness because of it; so in Beit Rabbi in the name of Keneset HaGedolah; understand well.
