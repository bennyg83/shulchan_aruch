A case for scrolls: This means Torah, Nevi'im, and Kesuvim, which have sanctity, and the case is an accessory to sanctity. The same applies to other sacred books. What he wrote about tefillin straps applies likewise to their case, both for the hand tefillin and for the head tefillin.
And a chest, etc.: This speaks where it was prepared for this purpose, even if he placed something in it only once. Or, even if it was not initially prepared for this purpose, but he regularly places it there on a fixed basis, it also becomes sanctified through this, as above in siman 42 in the Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 9; see there.
