Magen Avraham, seif katan 17. He writes that they erected an idolatrous image in the synagogue. I did not find this stated in the Gemara. It states only that there was an andarta in the synagogue, meaning a human image, and the Gemara discusses why suspicion was not a concern. It does not state that the image was worshipped as idolatry. Likewise, his answer that “it came into its own domain” is stated in the Mishnah in Avodah Zarah concerning the bathhouse of Aphrodite, but not regarding this incident. See there. As for the questioner’s objection that Rashi there writes, “to bring an image upon the andarta,” this is no objection. The term tzelem does not necessarily mean an idol that is worshipped; it may simply denote a sculpted image. Regarding the statement that had Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah been flogged they would have worshipped the image, Tosafos in Pesachim 53b write that this image was made by Nevuchadnetzar in his own honor. Thus, they explain that the tzelem was an andarta and not an object of idolatry.
