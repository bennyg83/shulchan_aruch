And the straps. See Taz and Tosafos, Megillah 26b, s.v. tashmishei. See Bach. According to Rashi, one may say that the knot itself possesses sanctity, while the remaining straps are accessories, etc. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 2, letter 3. According to Rashi, converting them constitutes lowering from sanctity to an accessory. What the Rosh writes is difficult, for that which previously possessed sanctity is thereby made into an accessory. Regarding the compartment of the head tefillin, one may say that it itself possesses sanctity because the letter shin is upon it, whereas the compartment of the arm tefillin is an accessory of sanctity, like the straps. According to this, the straps could afterward be made into an accessory for a Sefer Torah, or perhaps for Chumashim. They could not, however, be made into an accessory for Neviim, Kesuvim, or Gemaras, because that would still constitute lowering from a more stringent sanctity to a lesser one. The compartment of the head tefillin, however, may not be converted from sanctity into an accessory. The prohibition against using a tefillin case as an ordinary accessory of sanctity would then refer either to the head tefillin, or, according to Rashi, to the knot of the arm tefillin. Accordingly, where each of the head and arm tefillin has its own outer case made of silver plate or the like, the outer case of the head tefillin is an accessory of sanctity, while that of the arm tefillin is an accessory of an accessory, even according to Rashi, because it is designated only for the compartment. This requires study. See siman 43, Magen Avraham, seif katan 1, and what he writes in the name of the Even HaEzer of the Rav, of blessed memory, from siman 301, seif 7. See Tosafos, Shabbos 61a, s.v. dilma, and Menachos 35b, s.v. elu, where Tosafos is uncertain whether the arm tefillin must be removed before entering a privy; in Shabbos they write that it need not be removed. We rule that the arm tefillin must be removed, which implies accordance with Rashi. See Shabbos 62a, Tosafos s.v. shin. This is not the place to elaborate.
