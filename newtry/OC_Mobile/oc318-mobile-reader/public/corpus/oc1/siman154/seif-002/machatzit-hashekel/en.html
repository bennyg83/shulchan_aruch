Seif katan 2: Which was made, etc. From a wooden wall, etc. Shulchan Aruch writes, “built into the wall.” Magen Avraham explains that one should not think this means specifically a stone wall, since the second reason, that the books become damaged, would not apply to wood. In fact, Shulchan Aruch’s wording indicates that the first reason alone suffices, and that reason applies even to wood. As Magen Avraham concludes, it is merely like a room, meaning that it was made for protection and not for honor.
