Seif katan 8: Made for honor and placed, etc. Meaning that it is permanently attached to the Sefer Torah. This refers to leather or silk covering the wooden boards of the scroll. A curtain, however, is not attached to the scroll. This differs from Olas Tamid, who holds that even though the board covering serves an article that itself serves the Sefer Torah, it acquires sanctity because it was made for honor. According to him, a curtain made for honor should likewise be sacred, and Beit Yosef’s distinction is difficult: he rules stringently concerning the board covering, yet says that a curtain is sacred only because the Sefer Torah is placed upon it, while our paroches is not sacred despite being made for honor. According to Magen Avraham, the law does not depend on whether it was made for honor.
