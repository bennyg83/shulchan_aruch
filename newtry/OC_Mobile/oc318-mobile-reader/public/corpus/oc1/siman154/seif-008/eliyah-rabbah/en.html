(8) An ark as a chair, etc. — for in a box the scroll is always placed, and on a chair only temporarily; and Magen Avraham wrote, and it is implied from Mordechai that even one built in a wall is forbidden to make from it a chair, end of his words. And seemingly this contradicts somewhat what he wrote above that it is permitted to place books in it; and further that which we say it is not an article of holiness requires study. And further, Mordechai brings proof from this box of arpita, etc., that an ark built in a wall is not called a box — hence it is permitted to make from it a seat; and this is implied more in the Mordechai's greatness.
