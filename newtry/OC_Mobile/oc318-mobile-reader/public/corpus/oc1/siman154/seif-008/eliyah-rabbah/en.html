[13] Even though they did not stipulate, etc. The poskim strained to find this reason in order to somewhat justify why people are not careful about this. It appears to me that this applies specifically to something where a practice to be lenient is clear; but in something that is not a practice, we have no basis to be lenient. Perhaps it is not considered as though they stipulated, and not for this. Magen Avraham wrote: it is proper for every person, when he donates an item, or for the treasurers when they purchase, to stipulate that they may use them.
