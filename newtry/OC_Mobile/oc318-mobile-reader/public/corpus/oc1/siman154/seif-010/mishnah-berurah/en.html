We do not protest against them: For I say that the condition of beis din applies to this in a place where such is the custom, since they use them for the honor of the Torah. But this does not apply to the heads of ordinary bridegrooms, for a person does not make a condition regarding crowns that are accessories of sanctity for the honor of the Torah scroll, that a layperson may use them for his own honor. But if they explicitly stipulated at the time the crown was made that they would place it on the heads of ordinary bridegrooms, it is permitted, as written in Sefer Chasidim. Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah.
