Sanctity. Reuven donated a paroches bearing images before which it is forbidden to pray, and then died. May his son seek annulment of the donation, or does it possess no sanctity at all because it is comparable to consecrating a blemished animal for the altar? Also, the reason it is forbidden to pray facing it applies even in a public setting, where suspicion is not present. See Responsa Shaagas Aryeh, siman 177.
