And now they hang them on a sefer Torah for ornament: cloths spread on a sefer Torah may be hung on a synagogue wall; and likewise silver vessels placed on a sefer Torah may be placed on the pillars in the synagogue [Beit Yosef siman 174]. Bach wrote in siman 153 / 153 — Rashal wrote: it does not seem called lowering from kedushah if one places other books in the ark with a sefer Torah, for its kedushah still remains; know that we always place invalid curtains in the ark, and likewise chumashim and other books. However it requires study: where they removed the sefer Torah from it, whether they may place other books inside — for since there is no sefer Torah in it, is there lowering from kedushah temporarily or not — end of his words. And in my humble opinion it is obvious that it is forbidden even temporarily, and even to place them next to a sefer Torah also does not seem to be lenient — who will tell us it is established custom — end of his words. And in Shulchan Aruch HaRav siman 438 / 434 it states that it is forbidden to place invalid curtains inside, and it is possible it is permitted because beit din minds; and in an ark built like a room it is obviously permitted, as written above. And in Megillah we say it is permitted to place the box inside the window to seal it so tumah does not enter the synagogue — even though it is tashmishei chol — and it must be said: since one does not use it for anything, what difference if it is placed here or there; and Tosafot Bava Batra 20 wrote: since he does not nullify it being there forever — it is permitted. And Taz in Yoreh De'ah wrote it is forbidden to take a book and place it under another book in order to raise it so it is comfortable to see and study inside it, unless the book is already placed — then it is permitted to place the second on top of it — end of his words. And from what we wrote above it implies it is permitted, and all the more when one does so in order to learn from it, as siman 315 seif 7. And in Shulchan Aruch HaRav siman 498 — one should not rule lines on the book, because notebooks have no kedushah until something is written in them — implies that when written in, it is permitted to place on it. And afterward he wrote that if the sun shines and one cannot learn, it is permitted to place one book to be for shade; but not for need — forbidden to benefit from it — see there. One should not place notebooks on a book, nor write one's accounts in a book, nor test a quill on it — end of his words. They said in Shabbat chapter 2: women die for the sin of calling the holy ark "arna" — one must say "holy ark." Nevertheless it is found many times in the gemara and poskim "aron" — and it must be said that when engaged in study it is different, for everything is a matter of kedushah. Rashi wrote "arna" — thus they called their towers; but "aron" is a special name also for the holy ark.
