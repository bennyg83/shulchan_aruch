The books. Concerning decorative finials for the rollers of a Sefer Torah, made from white bone for adornment, whether one may use them as rollers for Megillas Esther: Rabbi Raphael Trivish ruled that it is forbidden, bringing proof from sukkah decorations, whose law is like that of the sukkah itself. See siman 638, Magen Avraham seif katan 13, and Taz seif katan 4. Rabbi Yom Tov Algazi disagreed, arguing that sukkah decorations are different because degradation of the mitzvah is involved, whereas here the issue is only that one ascends in sanctity and does not descend, for these are merely decorations of articles that serve sanctity. The author of Responsa Panim Meiros, part 2, siman 3, discusses the matter at great length and concludes: “I have found no basis or avenue whatsoever to permit it.” See there.
