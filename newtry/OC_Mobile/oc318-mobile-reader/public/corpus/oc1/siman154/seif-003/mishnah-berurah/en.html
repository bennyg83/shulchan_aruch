(10) And a chair, etc. — this is the table standing on the bimah; and even though a cloth is placed on it and on the cloth the Torah scroll is placed — nevertheless the table is only an article for an article; still, it has the law of an article of holiness because sometimes they place the Torah scroll on it without a cloth [gemara].

(11) And a curtain that they hang — this is the parochet; and this is according to Talmudic custom, when they sometimes placed a Torah scroll on it or covered the Torah scroll with it [and it appears this was common in their time, for otherwise it would be a mere occasional article and would not have the name of an article of holiness]; but nowadays, since we do not practice thus, it is only an article of an article; and this is what Rama emended below in seif 6 in the gloss [later authorities].
