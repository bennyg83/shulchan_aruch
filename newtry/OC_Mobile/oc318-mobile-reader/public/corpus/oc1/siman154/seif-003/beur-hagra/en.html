Seif 3: And a chest, etc. There: “the basket for Chumashim and the chest for sefarim,” etc.
And a chair, etc.; and a curtain, etc. There, and Rav said from the beginning, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. me’reish, etc. This is the meaning of “before,” etc.
Specifically, etc. There.
Or, etc. There: “and the chest for sefarim,” etc. Although the Sefer Torah is not placed inside it without its wrapping, as Rambam and the Shulchan Aruch write in Yoreh Deah 282:12.
But, etc. This is the meaning of the statement there, “for protection,” etc., in such a case.
It is forbidden, etc. As stated in chapter 4 of Rosh Hashanah regarding a shofar.
