(א) Tashmishei kedushah, etc. — and regarding whether in this the seven good men of the city help to remove the cloth-cover and the like to mundane use, as in a synagogue explained above siman 153 seif 7 — Magen Avraham wrote there in that siman s.k. 34 that no counsel helps to remove tashmishei kedushah from their kedushah — see there; and similarly there Beit HaRoeh; and we already wrote there in Sha'arei Tziyon that thus is proven from Ramban, RaShba, Ritva, and Ran in chapter "sons of the city," and they strained greatly there. Also in the law of synagogue — what is the reason ZT"H b'mao"h helps? Rather Ramban and RaShba explained to her that its kedushah is not more severe than all tashmishei mitzvah that are cast aside after they cease doing mitzvah with them — see there — and all this is not relevant to tashmishei kedushah. However Peri Megadim in siman 153 note 14 in Orach Chayim wrote that from Rambam's words, chapter 10 of Hilchot Sefer Torah, it implies that also in tashmishei kedushah the city's good men help to take it to mundane use at least if he intends to upgrade with its money; and Tur in Yoreh De'ah 282 adds further that they may take it to mundane use even to drink date beer with its money where there are good men in ZT"H b'mao"h — literally like synagogue where they may sell it and the buyer uses it for mundane purposes, and also the money they may take out to drink date beer; and Beit Yosef wrote there that Rambam also agrees where there are good men of city in ZT"H b'mao"h, only he was brief — see there in Beit Yosef; and similarly stam in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah there; but the later authorities wondered what to answer Ramban's difficulty and his supporters — see responsa Chatam Sofer siman 38 who strained on this [and what he wrote there to wonder on Rambam himself that his words contradict his own words in beginning of law 4 where he wrote stam regarding tashmishei kedushah they are buried and did not distinguish that through sale one may take them to mundane — no difficulty, for his words there at end of law 4 refer to all his words there in law 4]. And in my humble opinion the reason of Rambam and Tur is simple [for in truth on Ramban's reason Ran already challenged — see there what he innovated, and his words are somewhat strained] — he holds the power of city's good men that helps in synagogue is because all who vow vow per their intent, and it is like hekdesh on condition that helps, and like that of Yerushalmi chapter sons of the city [that the Rishonim brought] — R. Yirmiyah went into the bathhouse, they gave a key in the ark, he came and asked R. Ami; he said: I say they stipulated on this initially; and per this reason there is no difference between tashmishei mitzvah like synagogue and tashmishei kedushah — in all ZT"H b'mao"h helps; and similarly implied in Meiri in Megillah that this is the reason good men help — he wrote: but if they sold it ZT"H b'mao"h in assembly, etc., it went to mundane use even if they sold it to lower its value to lighter kedushah and even to take them to mundane matters; and similarly kedushah of the house went to mundane use in buyer's hand for all kinds of use that they sanctified with this intent and relied on them — end of his words [and nevertheless even per this view what Tur adjoined in Yoreh De'ah there the law of his father the RaSh that an individual on his own is like good men of city in ZT"H b'mao"h — and implied from him explicitly that therefore also an individual may sell tashmishei kedushah he sanctified to take them to mundane — is very difficult; granted one can say when he sanctified he sanctified per his intent and made the matter depend on his will and no less than if he depended on intent of a mere individual, and as I truly found in Mordechai siman 424 in name of Ra'aviah — see there; nevertheless it is reasonable he did not depend on his intent except if he wishes to sell and take money to mundane he may, but to say when he sanctified he depended on his intent that if he wishes to take the object itself to mundane he is permitted — this is not reasonable at all; on the contrary, all who did not stipulate explicitly — stam he sanctified forever]. And nevertheless for the law, although Rambam and Tosafot there to be lenient, and also from Ra'aviah brought in Mordechai chapter sons of the city siman 426 implies also sale of city sons helps to remove to mundane even kedushah of tashmishei kedushah [though in Bigdei Yesha he explains differently but his words are strained] — nevertheless difficult to be lenient against all these great ones Ramban, RaShba, Ritva, Ran mentioned, and also from Gra's wording in seif 9 implies likewise one cannot lower tashmishei kedushah from kedushah in any manner; and see Peri Megadim in 153 in Mishbetzot Zahav note 15 that he also remained in this law — requires study.
