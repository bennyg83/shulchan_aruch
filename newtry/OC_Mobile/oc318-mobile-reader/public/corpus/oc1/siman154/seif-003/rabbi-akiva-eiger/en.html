Magen Avraham, seif katan 23. “And hekdesh has no hand.” The source of this law is Tosafos to Me’ilah 13a, s.v. aval. See Tosafos there. See also Ramban to the chapter HaSefinah, who gives a different answer to Tosafos’ difficulty there. According to his answer, there is no necessary proof for this ruling.
