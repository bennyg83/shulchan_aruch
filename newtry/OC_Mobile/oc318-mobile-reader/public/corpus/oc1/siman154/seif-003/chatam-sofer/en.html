Note: and he raised in Keneset Yechezkel that one may not destroy them by hand even where there is concern they will come to contempt through wicked people. And he wrote: who compelled us in the world — and we bury them fifty handbreadths deep; and if impossible in a synagogue, one buries them elsewhere; and not like Baal Shevut Yaakov there.
