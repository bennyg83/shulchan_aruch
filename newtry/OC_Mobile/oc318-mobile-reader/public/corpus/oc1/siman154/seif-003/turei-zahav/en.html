And tefillin straps. Tosafos write that this implies that the dalet and yud formed by the knots of the straps are not complete letters and were not transmitted as a halachah to Moshe at Sinai. Only the shin on the tefillin boxes has that status, since the Gemara calls the straps merely articles serving sacred objects.
And a curtain that is hung, etc. This refers to the Talmudic practice of sometimes folding the curtain and placing a Sefer Torah upon it, as Rava states. It appears that the curtain was regularly designated for this purpose; otherwise it would be merely incidental use and would not acquire the sanctity of an ark. Nowadays, when this is not practiced, the curtain is not an article serving a sacred object but an article serving another article. So write Beit Yosef and Rama in seif 6.
