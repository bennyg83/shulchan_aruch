15) If it was established with royal permission, it has sanctity even if the congregation possesses only a right of occupancy. Responsa of R' Shlomo HaLevi, siman 46; Yad Aharon, second edition, in Hagahot Beit Yosef, paragraph 1. Responsa Chasam Sofer, Yoreh De'ah, siman 225, writes similarly; see there. R' Shlomo HaLevi, of blessed memory, distinguished between a place established with permission and one established without permission only regarding a synagogue, because the government objects to the construction of a synagogue without its permission. With regard to a beis midrash, where the government does not object if it is built without permission, it has the law of a beis midrash even if it was not established with royal permission. They may not sell it unless the seven trustees of the town act in the presence of the townspeople. Responsa Knesset HaGedolah cited above; Yad Aharon there, paragraph 2; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 2; Shaarei Teshuvah there.
