Seif katan 7: A curtain, etc. And the Sefer Torah is placed upon it. The curtain then directly serves a sacred object, namely the Sefer Torah itself. A curtain hanging before the aron kodesh is merely an article serving another article. Magen Avraham therefore concludes, “See the end of seif 6,” where Rama writes that our paroches does not have the sanctity of an ark, as Magen Avraham explains in seif katan 13.
