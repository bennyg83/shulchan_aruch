They are accustomed — Taz, Magen Avraham note 14, and Beit Yosef in Yerushalmi that they place a key in the ark — implies that even when there is a sefer Torah it is forbidden to place other things there unless by stipulation; but a stipulation helps for all tashmishei kedushah to take them to mundane use literally afterward — only books remain in their kedushah. And what is written that to lower is forbidden specifically while it is still fit, etc. — requires a little study, for this "teivah" (ark-box) that is damaged and the like implies that even when it is not fit at all as a box it is forbidden to make a chair; and one could say if fit for a small one — even if not fit, forbidden — requires study.
