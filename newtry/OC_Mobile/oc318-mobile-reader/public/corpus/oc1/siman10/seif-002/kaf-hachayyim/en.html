2) There. “A tallis that does not have four corners is exempt.” If the threads at the corner's edge came loose until the corner became rounded, although the Sifrei states that it must be square, this is of no concern, because we consider its condition at the time the tzitzis are made, just as with the requirement that the hole be at least a thumb-joint from the edge; see siman 11, seif 10.
