The distant ones. See the Taz. These are the words of the Kesef Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Tzitzis, that it is based on logic. See Levush, who corrected the word therefore in the Rambam; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1, in the name of the Lechem Chamudos, wrote that it is according to the Torah that it must be distant, and after the fact it is invalid if he did not make it on the distant corner; see there.
