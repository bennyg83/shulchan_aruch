Seif 8: Because of marit ayin. Regarding a silk tallis, one may be lenient in private according to the Mechaber's approach in siman 9, under which silk is obligated only rabbinically. See siman 301, Magen Avraham, ot 56. Even with a woolen garment, if it is beneath one's clothing in such a manner that it remains concealed when he walks in the public domain, one may permit it. See Shabbos 62a, Tosafos, s.v. 'and the tanna.'
