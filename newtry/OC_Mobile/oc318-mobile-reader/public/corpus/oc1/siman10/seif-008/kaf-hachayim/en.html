(8) [Seif 3] He doubled the corners of his tallis, etc. Magen Avraham, s.k. 4, implies in Tosafos, chapter HaKometz, that if he doubled the corners and sewed them such that the corner remained and he attached tzitzis on the fold—it is permitted, as stated seif 6; and likewise Razah, letter 6, and so in Levush. But in Mordechai, laws of minors, he wrote that here it is not comparable to that of seif 6, and these are his words: that of seif 6 deals with one who doubled it entirely for this purpose it is obligated, for such is the manner of dress—end quote—and Machatzit HaShekel, s.k. 4, brought this and wrote, and these are his words: "If so the matter is in dispute stated"—end quote—and the meaning is: according to Magen Avraham he places tzitzis on the fold, and according to Mordechai he places tzitzis on the main corner that was before the fold. And see Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, s.k. 4, who wrote that apparently Maran's view, of blessed memory, equated sewing to tying as tying—even if there is a corner tied diagonally, one certainly does not attach on the fold if it is more than three fingerbreadths; likewise sewing—see there. Therefore one must be careful not to double the corners of the tallis even in a manner where he would make a corner at the place of sewing, to escape the dispute; but if he folds a little, as they do an 'imra on the hem of the garment, there is nothing in it. But one must be careful that the hole where tzitzis are placed be within three fingerbreadths of the garment hem from the start when it is now folded, and above tie the thumb-knot from the place of the fold—see siman 11, seif 9.
