And in Terumat HaDeshen siman 166 he wrote that hooks are not considered a connection unless they are bent very much so that they cannot be untied except by an action. And it is possible that nevertheless it is forbidden because of marit ayin (Magen Avraham). And Beit Yosef wrote: one should examine our garments that have two corners near the ground — why do we not place tzitzit on them? And one can answer: the reason is that after the mitzvah of tzitzit is that two [corners] should be before him and two behind him, and our garments have all four corners before him and it is impossible to wear them in another manner — they are not obligated in tzitzit; therefore they were accustomed to be lenient. And a garment called roquelaure, which has attached above a square piece of cloth placed on his shoulder from behind, called kolner — it is obvious that it is exempt, for on the upper side it has no corners, only the corners of the kolner placed somewhat behind him, and these are not [valid] corners since the two corners behind him are not aligned opposite the corners before him — thus wrote Darkei Moshe. And Magen Avraham wrote: nevertheless one who fears Heaven should make one corner rounded, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the elders.
