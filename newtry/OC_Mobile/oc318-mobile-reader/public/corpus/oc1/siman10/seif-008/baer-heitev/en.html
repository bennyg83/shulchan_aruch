Perhaps nevertheless it is forbidden because of maris ayin. Magen Avraham. The Beis Yosef wrote: One must examine our garments, which have two corners near the ground, why we do not place tzitzis on them. The Darchei Moshe answered that since the mitzvah of tzitzis is that two should return in front of him and two behind him, and in our garments all four corners are in front of him, and it is impossible to wear them in another manner, they are not obligated in tzitzis; therefore the practice was to be lenient. As for a garment called a rok, to which a square piece of fabric is attached above and rests on his shoulders behind him, called a collar, it is obvious that it is exempt, for on the upper side it has no corners, only the corners of the collar that rest somewhat behind him; these are not corners, since the two corners behind him are not aligned opposite the corners in front of him. End quote from Darchei Moshe. The Magen Avraham wrote: Nevertheless, a God-fearing person should make one corner round, as the Beis Yosef wrote in the name of the elders.
