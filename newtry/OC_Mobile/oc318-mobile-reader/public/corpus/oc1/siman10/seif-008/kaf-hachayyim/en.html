8) [Seif 3] He folded the corners of his tallis, etc. Magen Avraham, se'if katan 4, writes that Tosafos in the chapter HaKometz implies that if one folded the corners and sewed them in a manner that left a corner, he may place the tzitzis on the fold, as stated in seif 6. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, entry 6, rules likewise, as is also implied by Levush. Mordechai in Hilkhot Ketanot, however, writes that this case is not comparable to seif 6: there the entire garment was folded, and it is therefore obligated because garments are commonly worn in that manner; this concludes his words. Machatzit HaShekel, se'if katan 4, cites him and writes, “If so, the matter is subject to dispute”; this concludes his words. That is, according to Magen Avraham he places the tzitzis on the fold, whereas according to Mordechai he places them on the original corner that existed before it was folded. See Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, se'if katan 4, who writes that Maran appears to equate tying the corners with sewing them. Thus, even if a corner remains when one ties it diagonally, he certainly may not place the tzitzis on the fold if it is more than three fingerbreadths from the original edge; the same applies when he sews it. See there. One should therefore take care not to fold the corners of a tallis, even in a manner that forms a corner at the seam, in order to avoid the dispute. There is no concern, however, if one folds over only a small amount, as is done to make a hem at the garment's edge. He must nevertheless ensure that the hole in which the tzitzis are placed is within three fingerbreadths of the garment's original edge, which is now folded, and at least a thumb-joint above the fold. See siman 11, seif 9.
