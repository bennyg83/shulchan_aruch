(א) "Astringah" — it is something that joins the sides of the garment, one to the other.

(ב) To make it as closed — therefore one should not make clasps on a tallit katan below halfway, for that is considered closed. This is when the heads of the clasps are bent over greatly toward the other side so that one cannot undo them except by action; then they are considered fully joined both for kilayim and for tzitzis. But otherwise they are not considered joined thereby.

(ג) Marit ha'ayin — and one who always goes with a tallit katan under his garments, and even when he walks in the public domain it is concealed, one may say marit ha'ayin does not apply here [Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger].
