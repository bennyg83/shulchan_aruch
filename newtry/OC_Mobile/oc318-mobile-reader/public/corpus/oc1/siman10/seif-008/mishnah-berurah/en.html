"Astringah" - It is something which connects the ends of the garment, one to another.

To make it closed - Therefore, one should not make clasps on his טלית a minor / child lower than halfway down for that would be considered as closed. And this is if the heads of the clasps were bent over a lot towards the other side so that you're not able to undo them except through a physical action... for then it is considered completely attached in regards to kelayim (forbidden mixtures of certain materials) and in regards to ציצית. But without that (ie. if the clasps were loose), it is not considered an attachment because of that.

Appears to the eye (ie. how it looks to others) - And one who always goes with his טלית a minor / child under his clothes, and even when he walks in public, it is hidden [under his clothes]... we can say that מַרְאִית הָעַיִן does not apply there [Chiddushei R. Akiva Eiger]
