Astringa. It is something that joins the sides of the garment to one another.
To make it as closed. Therefore one should not make clasps on a small tallis below its halfway point, for that is like closed. This is where the heads of the clasps are bent very much to the other side, so that they cannot be undone except by an act; in that case they are considered a full connection, both regarding kilayim and regarding tzitzis. Otherwise, they are not considered a connection by means of this.
Appearance to the eye. One who always wears a small tallis under his clothing, and even when he walks in the public domain it is concealed, one may say that maris ayin does not apply in this case. Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger.
