“As if closed.” Therefore one should not put buttons on a small tallis below the belt, for it is as if closed (Levush). Mishnah Berurah siman 297 writes that buttons are not considered attached for kilayim unless they are bent so tightly that they cannot be undone without a deliberate act—see there—and Yoreh Deah 300:3; perhaps even so it is forbidden because of maris ayin. Maharam responsum 287: tallises made like “captain’s coats”—these are the tallises of Ashkenazi pious men; they have four corners, and when he removes it he spreads it and covers himself with it; but those “kutlash” garments that have a sleeve-opening are not a concern, for their “wing” is not a corner.
