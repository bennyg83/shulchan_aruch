Two before them — On the Taz: objected against those who cover the head and place two tzitzis before them, throw part of the tallit over their shoulders, and roll it behind them until it becomes thin and covers only like a thread down the neck. I would say the same applies to the body; but as ordinary people do, they cover their whole body and it is called two before them and two behind them — like a tallit katan, two on the right and two on the left, and it is called two before them and two behind them.
