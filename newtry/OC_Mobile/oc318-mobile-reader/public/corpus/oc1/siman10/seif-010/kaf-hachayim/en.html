(10) [Seif 4] A tallis of cloth, etc. This is Rava's view in chapter HaTekhelet (Menachot page 40b)—that we require the main cloth; and so ruled the Rif, Rambam, Rosh, and Maran agrees with them. However, Rav Achai disagrees and holds we follow the corner; and some geonim were concerned for his words—see Beit Yosef. And even though Maran, of blessed memory, wrote in Beit Yosef that we need not heed those who are concerned to be stringent—for legal ruling—nevertheless every God-fearing person should be stringent for himself ab initio: if the corner tore or wore out, not to make a corner except from the cloth's kind, to fulfill according to all; but what they place on the corner a piece of leather or cloth to strengthen is permitted even for one who says we follow the corner—see Beit Yosef in this seif and in siman 11. And although there are those who are stringent, nevertheless they already practiced leniently per the permissive view, as Maran wrote in Shulchan Arukh.
