{Rama: There, in note 4} Nevertheless it is forbidden because of marit ha'ayin. I ask: if so, in Yoreh De'ah, siman 303, it should likewise be forbidden because of marit ha'ayin; and all the more so Levush wrote one should not make clasps below the belt even if the tallit is long and most of it remains open, because of marit ha'ayin lest they say the majority is closed since it is below the belt. And if so one should be concerned that perhaps on this pair they will err in two ways — they will think the clasps bend over and think the majority is closed in them. If so, all the more so it is fitting to prohibit clasps for kilayim, for there is only one marit ha'ayin — lest they say the clasps bend over. And it requires study.
