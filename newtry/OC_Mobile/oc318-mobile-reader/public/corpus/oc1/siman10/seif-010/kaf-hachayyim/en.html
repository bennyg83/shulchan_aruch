10) [Seif 4] A tallis made of cloth, etc. This is Rava's view in the chapter HaTecheiles, Menachos 40b: we follow the material of the principal garment. The Rif, Rambam, and Rosh rule accordingly, and Maran follows their view. Rav Achai, however, disagrees and holds that we follow the material of the corner, and some of the Geonim were concerned for his view; see Beit Yosef. Although Maran, of blessed memory, writes in Beit Yosef that we need not be concerned for those who rule stringently, that applies as a matter of law. A God-fearing person should nevertheless be stringent initially if the corner tore or wore out and replace it only with the same material as the garment, thereby satisfying all opinions. A piece of leather or cloth placed on the corner merely to reinforce it, however, is permitted even according to the opinion that follows the corner's material; see Beit Yosef in this siman and at the end of siman 11. Although some authorities are stringent, the custom is already to be lenient in accordance with those who permit it, as Maran writes there in the Shulchan Aruch.
