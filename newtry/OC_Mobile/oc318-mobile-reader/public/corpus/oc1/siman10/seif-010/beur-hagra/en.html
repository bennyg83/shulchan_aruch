Seif 10: "A turban" etc. In Sifrei: "with it" excludes a ma'afeles with which one does not cover his head and most [of his body].

"Since [the mitpachat is thin]" etc. As stated regarding clothing sellers etc. — thus since it is not made to protect from the sun, we do not call it a garment (talbash); likewise here we do not call it body covering.
