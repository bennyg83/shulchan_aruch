Since its primary purpose — It was made for him from the outset only for covering the head.

And not head-covering — And what people make as a crown on the tallit is not because its primary purpose is to cover the head, but for identification so the upper tzitzit are not switched to the bottom. And see above in Siman 8, seif katan 9, where I wrote that the Ari was not particular about this; and so too Eliyah Rabbah rules to be lenient if it tore above, and the like.
