[10] And one does not go out, etc. into the public domain—Levush. It implies it is permitted in a karmelit, for a rabbinic uncertainty is resolved leniently—and so Olas Tamid wrote; but Magen Avraham disagrees and wrote it is forbidden to create a rabbinic prohibition uncertainty ab initio—end quote. To me it appears they did not decree on an uncertainty. Levush wrote one does not bless. Magen Avraham wrote: if most of the tallis is cloth and the rest leather, it is obligated—and the same law applies conversely, that we follow its majority.
