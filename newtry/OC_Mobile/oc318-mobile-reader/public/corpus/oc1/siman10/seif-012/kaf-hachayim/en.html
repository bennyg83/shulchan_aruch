(12) There — a tallis of cloth, etc.; and if the tallis is leather and underneath it is sewn to cloth, it is exempt—and the same conversely it is obligated, for what is underneath is secondary to the main garment—Magen Avraham, there; Eliyah Rabbah, there; Razah, letter 9; Chayei Adam, there. However, Moreh U'Ketzia is uncertain—see there—and wrote: "And if the matter came before me in both cases, I would attach tzitzis whether in cloth sewn underneath to leather or conversely, and would not bless on them together"—end quote. And see Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, s.k. 5.
