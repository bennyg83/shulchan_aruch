Two before him. And some people fold one part of the tallit very much until it becomes thin and does not cover at all except the neck, and their intention is that two tzitziyot should be behind them — this is not optimal, for then there is no body covering here but only head covering. And it is better, as the custom of the Togarmians, that the hand separates at half the tallit so that on both sides one of the tzitziyot is before him and part behind him (Taz). And in the responsum of Beit Yaakov siman 92, not so — see there (Be'er Heitev).
