Two before them. Some people fold one part of the tallis very much until it becomes thin and does not cover at all, only the neck, and their intent is that two tzitzis should be behind them. This is not the choicest practice, for if so there is no covering of the body here, only a covering of the head. It is better to follow the practice of the Turks, where the hand separates the tallis in half, in such a way that on each of the two sides one of the tzitzis is in front of him and part is behind him. Taz. In Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 92, he did not write this way; see there.
