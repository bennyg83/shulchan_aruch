“Two before him,” etc. Darkhei Moshe cited on this a midrash “The waters were a wall” — Gabriel proclaimed to the waters behind “beware those who throw a tzitzis corner behind them,” etc. It appears to me proof from this one should wear the tallis so one throws the corner with two tzitzis behind — perhaps from this some people fold part of the tallis greatly until after folding it is very thin and does not cover to the neck — their intent two tzitzis behind. It appears to me this is not best — then there is no body covering only head covering — better like Turkish custom wrapping the tallis as is around head and body — hand divides middle of tallis this side and that — so from both sides one tzitzis before him and part behind — likewise Tosafos end Arakhin: “I found in a seder R. Yosei made reason he places one tzitzis before and one behind, right and left.”
