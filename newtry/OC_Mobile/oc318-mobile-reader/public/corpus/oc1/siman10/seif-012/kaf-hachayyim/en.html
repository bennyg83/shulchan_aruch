12) There. “A tallis made of cloth,” etc. If the tallis is made of leather and cloth is sewn beneath it, it is exempt. The same applies in reverse: it is obligated, because the material beneath is subordinate to the garment's principal material. See Magen Avraham there; Eliyah Rabbah there; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, entry 9; and Chayei Adam there. Mor U'Ketziah, however, is uncertain about this; see there. He writes: “If such a case came before me, I would attach tzitzis in either configuration (whether cloth was sewn beneath leather or the reverse) but I would not recite a blessing over them, even when both materials were present together”; this concludes his words. See Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, se'if katan 5.
