Since their corners are not made, etc. See the Mishnah Berurah's citation of Darkei Moshe. Pri Megadim says that according to Darkei Moshe a sardak cut entirely in front and also cut on the left is not obligated. It is improper to exempt it completely on that basis, since requiring two tzitzis in front and two behind is only preferable, not essential law. To ensure obligation, one should make two corners on the right and two on the left, and leave visibly more than half open; otherwise the blessing and tzitzis create an appearance concern.
