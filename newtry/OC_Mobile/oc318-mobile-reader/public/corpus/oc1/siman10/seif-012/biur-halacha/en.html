"Since their corners are not made," etc. See in Mishna Berurah what we cited from Darkei Moshe's words; and Pri Megadim wrote: if so, in a serapelak where they make a cut in front completely and on the left side too a cut—according to Darkei Moshe this is not obligated in tzitzis and is completely exempt for this reason—it is not correct, for what we require, that there be two tzitzis in front and two behind, is not from the essence of the law but only for the mitzvah in general; therefore, so that it be obligated in tzitzis, he should see to make two corners on the right and two on the left, and most of it open—most of what is visible to the eyes—for otherwise there is marit ayin in the blessing and tzitzis:
