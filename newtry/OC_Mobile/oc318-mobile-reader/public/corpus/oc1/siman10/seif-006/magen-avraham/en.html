“And it is good.” This requires study: if the Rambam held it were exempt he could say “it is good,” etc., and fulfill the second [corner] as well; but the Rambam holds one ties on the unfolded garment and the Rosh holds one ties on the folds—how then shall we act? In truth Darkei Moshe disagreed with Beis Yosef and wrote that since Rabban Shimon exempts it implies completely exempt; Levush omitted the first gloss; Mordechai wrote to tie on the unfolded corners—therefore one should be careful not to make a doubled tallis at all unless it is sewn. And I explained: a doubled tallis is one twice a person’s height that he can wear only by folding it—therefore it is exempt, for it is not a garment.

“The first.” Perhaps he specified only that case, but if he cut the secondaries it is invalid since he has already nullified the first ones.

“The first ones.” He specified “the first” because Rabbeinu Yerucham discusses cutting the first ones—hence he wrote “before cutting the first ones,” etc. But in truth for the Rosh even if he cuts the secondaries it is valid like the first; Beis Yosef states this explicitly—see there—and Bach’s words require study—see there—and note well.
