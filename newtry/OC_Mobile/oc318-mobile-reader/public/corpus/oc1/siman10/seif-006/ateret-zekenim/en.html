If one placed tzitzit upon tzitzit: if he intended to nullify the first [set], he cuts off the first [set] and it is valid; and all the more so if he cut off the latter ones, for they are valid as stated above.

{Rama: See Shulchan Aruch there. And some validate in every case, etc. And some say whether the first were cut or the last were cut, whether he intended to invalidate them or intended to add [to them], or whether he placed them without intention — if he separates the latter ones the first are valid, and likewise the reverse. But if he does not separate them at all they are invalid. But if he places many threads, we have no concern for bal tosif, and it is valid even if he did not separate them at all, as we find regarding arevei nachal, where we expound arevei shnayim from it, and the custom is to tie many hadassim on the lulav, as Rashba and Raikanati wrote; and the reason is that since they are in one bundle there is no concern of bal tosif, as explained.}
