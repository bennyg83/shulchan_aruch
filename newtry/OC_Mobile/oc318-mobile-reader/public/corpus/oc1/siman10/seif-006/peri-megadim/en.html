His intent is: not only according to the Rav there, who cites those who validate in every case, all the more so here; even according to the Mechaber, five tzitzis are sufficient when he cuts off one of them. At first glance it implies that even if he made the fifth one last and cut off one of the four, it is valid. I do not know why, for it implies that specifically the fifth one that he added must be cut, since the other four were made validly. But if so, sometimes he would need to cut two, especially according to the Yom Tov, that according to the Torah we require the distant corner, as written in letter 2. This requires study.
