And it appears to me that he holds one need not attach tzitzis on the unfolded portions, since it is a garment unfit to wear, as Taz wrote in the name of Nimukei Yosef; and Taz distinguishes in law that Nimukei Yosef deals with a very long tallis, whereas Rashi deals with one who folds it voluntarily—therefore one must attach tzitzis on the unfolded portions.
