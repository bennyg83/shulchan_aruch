Not to recite a blessing. All the later authorities were astonished: what did he repair by not reciting a blessing? Perhaps the halacha is still like the Mechaber, and he must place them on the extended corners; how may he wear this garment, since according to all opinions it is forbidden to add more than four tzitzis? See Pri Megadim. They conclude as the practical law that one should be careful initially not to make a folded tallis at all unless he sewed it, even with wide stitches, for once he sewed it, it is no longer made to be spread out. Then, whether it is a square tallis or a tallis that was originally too long, he places tzitzis on the folds and recites a blessing.
