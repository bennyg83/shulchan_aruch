On tzitzis. See what the Ateres Zekeinim wrote about this. What the Mechaber wrote in siman 11, seif 12, escaped his notice; see there and in the Beis Yosef.
The first ones. According to the Rosh, even if he cut the second ones. See Magen Avraham, Taz, and Olas Tamid.
