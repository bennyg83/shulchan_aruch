(siman katan 6) Three tzitzis, etc. And I do not know why — for behold the others were made in validity; that is, at the time of making the last ones the tallis was already subject to obligation, for it had four corners, except that it had one invalid tzitzis because of ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy. And on this Magen Avraham wrote: is it because one tzitzis is invalid, etc.? And if so, if one invalid tzitzis on a tallis by its strings being severed would also invalidate the three remaining valid tzitzis — I am astonished. What difference is there whether one tzitzis was invalidated because its strings were cut or one tzitzis was invalidated because of ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy?

And similarly siman 15, seif 4, as should be written there: and some say that according to R. Akiva it is not invalidated (when the tallis is torn within three fingerbreadths near the edge of the corner) except the tzitzis that were on it when it was sewn; and the reason is that the tzitzis that were in the piece that was torn was invalid before it was sewn, for it was not called a garment; and when they sewed it to the garment with the tzitzis, then the torn piece there became a garment — hence ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy — see there in Magen Avraham.

And from this wording it appears that it was not invalidated in such a case except that tzitzis alone and not the rest of the tzitzis; admittedly, this is not completely precise.
