(1) [Seif 1] A tallit that does not have four corners is exempt; if it has more than four it is obligated. Baraisa in chapter HaTekhelet; and so ruled the Rif, Rambam, and Rosh—but R' Eliezer of Metz and Rabbeinu Simcha ruled that one of five is also exempt, per the baraisa in Sifri and chapter 2 of Zevachim, and Beit Yosef brought them—see there. Bach wrote: therefore one who fears Heaven should wear only a tallis with four corners. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1, wrote that in Berachot, chapter 2 of Zevachim, he too inclines to this view. Eliyah Rabbah, s.k. 1; Etz Yosef; Razah, letter 5; and likewise Penei Yitzchak, may his light shine, from letter 226 of HaTayit, letter 126, in the name of Lev Chayyim, siman 78. And likewise Rabbi Ben Ish Chai, may his light shine, Parashat Noah, letter 6, wrote there Penei Yitzchak, may his light shine, that one should not bless on a tallis of five corners on account of safek berachah lehakel—and see below, letter 7.
