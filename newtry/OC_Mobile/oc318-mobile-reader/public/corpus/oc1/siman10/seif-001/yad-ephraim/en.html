Taz note 1 — do not say: certainly if there is no extension [of the verse], nevertheless from the side of reasoning we say that even a five-cornered [garment], because five includes four, nevertheless it is impossible to obligate more than four tzitzis — for it says "twisted cords you shall make for yourself on the four corners of your garment." Rather we say that although the tallis has more [corners], it is obligated since in any case there are four here; but nevertheless one may not make more than four tzitzis even on a five-cornered [garment]. And if so, one might have thought: since in any case one may not make more than four tzitzis, therefore one may also not make more corners — for we do not follow the reasoning that five includes four, since in any case one may not make more than four tzitzis because the Torah mentioned only making tzitzis on the four corners of the garment. And if so, there is also reasoning to make corners matching the number of tzitzis. Therefore Scripture wrote the extension "with which you cover yourself" to teach us that a five-cornered [garment] is obligated — namely to say we follow the reasoning that five includes four; and automatically regarding tzitzis it remains as it was, that no more than four tzitzis are made, per the plain meaning of the verse that one makes only on four corners — and there is no difference whether the corners are four or more:
