“That it does not have four corners.” It was taught: “on your four-cornered garment” — four and not three; or only four and not five? When it says “with which you cover yourself” — to include a five-cornered [garment]; “what did you see to include a five-cornered? I include a five-cornered because five includes four; I exclude a three-cornered because three does not include four.” One could ask: perhaps when you include five-cornered you include five tzitzis — and why “with which you cover yourself”? It teaches from this that five includes four — one resolves the other: therefore Scripture wrote “with which you cover yourself” — do not [make more than four tzitzis] — since one may not make more than four tzitzis, so too one may not make more than four corners — tzitzis and corners are equal — “with which you cover” teaches extension for the garment beyond what tzitzis has — hence invalid tzitzis beyond four — as above.

“The more distant.” It is reasonable to make [corners] on those most distinct from the rest.
