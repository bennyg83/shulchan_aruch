That there is no — On the Taz. And indeed there are difficulties in understanding (see Chemed Moshe). See Zevachim 18b and Kometz 37. Beit Yosef brought it explained: if not for "asher" being superfluous, a five-cornered [garment] is exempt; and according to R. Yishmael they are four separate mitzvot — one places five tzitzis. Here at first it seems this is not the difficulty being asked — why do I need "asher"? Rather he wishes to answer a second difficulty raised against Beit Yosef and Levush: why do we not include for the Rabbis a five-cornered [garment] for five tzitzis? And what of it that all four are one mitzvah — therefore he explains that the Rabbis hold that logically five includes four and "asher" is superfluous, and one must necessarily exclude five so that he place only four tzitzis. Nevertheless from Kometz it is difficult: if R. Yishmael has difficulty with "asher," and if it is not needed then it does not depend on whether they are four mitzvot, etc. Also whence do we know this — shall we say according to all he places five tzitzis? And at first it would seem in Zevachim it concludes that according to all anything extra is considered as if it exists, and the questioner [asks that] as if it does not exist it is not considered — superfluous language is not needed; therefore Samuel validates priestly garments from Jerusalem that excess is like deficiency. And we hold in Chullin 58b and Beitzah 95 that anything extra is considered as removed; if so, a verse is not needed, and only to teach that one does not place tzitzis on it. And in Kometz likewise Tosafos in Zevachim, s.v. ve'idakh, on "asher": if there is a simple derashah we expound it, and if not we do not — see there well. And thus for the first tanna, four mitzvot are one — we expound "asher" and include a five-cornered [garment], and only four tzitzis; unlike R. Yishmael, for whom they are four mitzvot — if we include from "asher" the reasoning to include that he place five tzitzis, since each is a separate mitzvah, this is impossible, for then let "asher" be silent — then he does not expound "asher" at all in such a case and truly places on five corners (see Gimatriya, chapter 3 of tzitzis, letter 2, and understand there). And therefore if one cuts a blemish in two in one corner, one may say anything extra is considered removed, and only a three-cornered [garment] — see siman 25 regarding when they are far apart; see there. And all this is not comparable in my view, for Rambam, chapter 8 of Keilim, halachah 4, ruled garments from Jerusalem are invalid, yet ruled in terefot that extra shade is nullified — whence we learn they are not comparable, and it requires study.
