Seif 1: "If it has [more than four]" etc. Not like R. Simcha who exempts, for that baraita which exempts in chapter 2 of Zevachim; and as stated in Menachos 37b regarding "one with five" — the difference between them is that the Rabbis hold it is exempt and R' Yehuda holds it is obligated. And there the halacha is fixed per the Rabbis. And there, "he who shortened his cloak" etc. — meaning that he made it to place tzitzit in it — he has done nothing, for it is exempt. Why? Because he made it into one with five [corners]. And see Hagahos Maimoniyos chapter 3. And although in Sifrei it is taught "four corners" came out regarding those with three, five, six, seven, eight — and likewise in Zevachim 18b — Rosh explained this means one does not place [tzitzit] on all its corners but only on four; and one with three is exempt since it does not have four. And likewise the sugya at the beginning of chapter 3 of Menachos 37b that obligates — and as Rashi explains there "he who shortened his cloak" — unlike the explanation above. And the sugya there 43b that all hold not like the baraita that exempts.

"And makes [it into five]" etc. Like the first Tanna there 37b, as stated above that the halacha is not according to him.

"The distant [corners]" etc. Rambam. And it is proven there that it says "he who shortened his cloak" etc. and "nothing" — and since it says in this language "it is not exempt," it implies he does not add or remove; and if so, behold he made it [into one with five] so that he can place tzitzit on these and not on the others.
