[1] The distant ones, etc. Levush wrote it is reasonable, etc.; and in Lechem Chamudos page 104 he learned this from the verse itself, for it is written "on four corners" and that which are distant from one another—the name "corners" applies to them—end quote. So too it appears in the early poskim, and it appears that even after the fact it is a hindrance and invalid, that one does not make them on distant ones. So too it is implied in the responsum of Maharam Mintz siman 13. Bach wrote: one who fears Heaven should wear only a tallis that has four corners, for R' Meir exempts even one that has five:
