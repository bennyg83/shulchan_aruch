[A] The distances, etc. In the clothing it is written that it turns out, etc. and in the bread of chamudot, page KD, it is learned that the reading of a shirt has an inscription on four wings, and the ones that are far from each other, the name of wings falls on them, so far. Likewise, it appears in the rulings that there are wings in the first place, and it is seen in retrospect that it is hindering and invalid that one does not do things at a distance. And it also means in the answer of Rabbi Mintz, Seaman 13.
