He placed — On the Taz: he brought the Gemara, Menachot 42, and the Rosh brought version one of Rashi and another version: placing on a suspended [garment] is valid on the latter ones; and even though when he does the latter ones he does not annul the first ones, it is found that those three are not tzitzis that he needs; and when he does a fourth and annuls the first ones, we do not call those three in the latter ones "tassel of blue," as explained, for there was no act of [proper] making. Maharal and Beit Yosef were puzzled: what is the difference between version one and two? The Taz explained the difference: version one specifically when he cut the first ones; and version two, even if both remain, it is valid. (One who examines Beit Yosef wrote that to add is certainly not logical to validate while both remain, only if he intended to cook — and Rashi explained thus; for if so, he is not puzzled at all that placing on a suspended [garment] is valid while both remain if he intended to annul — and on this he explained; and even though he cut the first ones, and likewise when he did not cut at all; and for Rava, who does not heed R. Papa's challenge, it is so for Rashi.) Afterward the Taz explained the words of some in the gloss, who are Tur and R. Yishmael; and likewise one may explain the Rosh's words, that they ruled like Rava, and even for annulment there is no initial act of making; and he gave a spice to this, since he formed in his mind that he would transgress a positive commandment if he intends or stam (but if he intends to annul, it does not involve a positive commandment, as Tosafos wrote, Menachot 40b, s.v. mimai, in that case in Eruvin 8 — and Bach does not seem so). And the Taz holds that what the other view wrote, "when he cuts the first ones" specifically, is not like Magen Avraham, letter 9; and Magen Avraham already wondered at Bach (and Taz) on this, for in Beit Yosef it does not seem so, as I wrote in Magen Avraham, letter 9, on this. And the Taz explained the words of the Rambam, that this is the author's view: here he challenges Rava from R. Zeira — placing on a suspended [garment] is valid, thus "tassel of blue" is not invalid; and Rava said "tassel of blue" is invalid; and R. Zeira's [view], that it is a halachah of a positive commandment, deals with "there was no act of making" in invalid — and if so R. Zeira's words are rejected; and R. Papa supports R. Zeira's words, that R. Zeira deals with when he intended to annul the first ones, for then two acts of valid making existed; and the Rambam ruled thus like R. Papa, and Rava agrees to this in truth. And there is a slight omission in the wording, and it should read: and from this they challenged Rava from R. Zeira, who permitted on a suspended [garment].
