(siman katan 9) The first ones, etc. But in truth per the Rosh (and this is the view of the Maggid Mishneh that Rama brought), even if he cut the second ones it is valid like the first ones — even not precisely as explained in Rabbeinu Yonah; on the contrary, if he cuts the second ones it is more reasonable to validate by the first ones, since the beginning of their making was in validity, to validate if he cuts the first ones by the second ones whose beginning of making was in invalidity; nevertheless for the practical ruling he wrote that whether one cuts the first or second it is valid. And Bach's words require study — that he understood Rama thus; and so too Rabbeinu Yonah in his long work, that for the Rosh only when cutting the first ones is it valid and not when cutting the second ones — Rabbeinu Yonah explicitly explains to the contrary.
