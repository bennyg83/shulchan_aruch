(9) There — nevertheless it is not exempt; therefore one must be careful with some garments that have four corners to cut one corner specifically round—for if he sews one corner to make it round it does not help, for as long as he has not cut it is still classified as garment. And likewise Kitzur Shulchan Arukh, siman 9, letter 21. And therefore here in Jerusalem, the holy city, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days, where there are people who place a square folded garment on their shoulders and body—one must cut one corner round on both sides—that is, after folding the garment in two, cut one corner together with the fold beneath it, to escape the dispute; see above letter 8 and below letter 15.
