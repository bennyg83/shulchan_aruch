"And that they not be round." Requires study: to what extent are they called by the name of roundness? For it is not reasonable to say that through something that rounds the tip of the corner the garment would again be exempt from tzitzis—for why should the garment's square be more important than the altar's square? As we rule in Zevachim 62 regarding the altar's square and its corners hindering; nevertheless we rule in Chullin 18 that if the altar was damaged less than a handbreadth or an olive's volume, it is valid, and no practical difference is mentioned there between whether it was damaged at the tip of a corner or elsewhere on the altar—it implies that in every manner it is valid.
