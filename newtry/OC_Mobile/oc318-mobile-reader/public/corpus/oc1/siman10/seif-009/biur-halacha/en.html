And not rounded. How much rounding gives that name requires consideration. It is unreasonable that slightly rounding a corner's point should exempt the garment. Squareness of a garment should not exceed that of the altar, whose square form and corners are indispensable [Zevachim 62], yet Chullin 18 validates an altar chipped by less than a tefach or kezayis. No distinction is made between damage at a corner point or elsewhere, implying validity in all cases. Tosafos, Sukkah 49b, explains that it retains the name square. Perhaps the altar's large dimensions distinguish it from our case. The matter requires consideration.
