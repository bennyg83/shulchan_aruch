9) There. “Nevertheless, it is not exempt.” Accordingly, with certain garments that have four corners, care must be taken to cut away one corner specifically so that it becomes rounded. Sewing one corner in order to make it round is ineffective, because as long as it has not been cut away it remains part of the garment. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 9, entry 21, rules likewise. Therefore, here in the holy city of Jerusalem, may it be built and established speedily in our days, where some people place a square garment folded over their shoulders and body, one corner must be cut round through both layers. That is, after folding the garment in two, one should cut one corner together with the fold beneath it, in order to avoid the dispute. See above, entry 8, and below, entry 15.
