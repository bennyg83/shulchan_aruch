Of cloth, etc. For it is written, "with which you cover yourself," and a person covers himself only with the main body of the garment. Therefore we follow it. Even if the place of the garment itself is entirely round and has no corner, but leather corners are attached to it, it is obligated, because we follow the main body of the garment.
And its corners are of leather. The same law applies if most of the tallis is cloth and the remainder is leather; it is obligated.
And its corners are of cloth. The same law applies if only most of the tallis is leather: even if the minority cloth portion has the minimum measure of a tallis, it is exempt from tzitzis, for since the main garment is not cloth, we do not apply to it the verse "on the corners of their garments." Leather is exempt even D'Rabbanan, for it is not included in the category of a garment at all. The same law applies to a tallis whose underside is sewn with leather: it is obligated. But if the upper side is leather, it is exempt, for the lower side is secondary to the upper side, which is the main garment.
