“It is obligated.” We follow the main fabric of the garment—as implied in seif 7: if most of the tallis is cloth and the rest leather it is obligated, and likewise the reverse, for we follow the majority. It appears to me: if the tallis is leather and cloth is sewn underneath it is exempt, and the reverse is obligated, for what is underneath is secondary to the main garment—and see Beis Yosef on this siman and siman 11: if he sews leather onto the corner it is permitted; see there, which supports this view.
