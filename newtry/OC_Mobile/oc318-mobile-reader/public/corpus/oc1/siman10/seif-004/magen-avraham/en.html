“It is obligated.” We follow the main fabric of the garment—seif 7 implies: if most of the tallis is cloth and the rest leather it is obligated, and vice versa we follow the majority. It appears to me: if the tallis is leather and cloth is sewn underneath it is exempt, and the reverse is obligated, for what is underneath is secondary to the main garment—and Beiur Yosef on this siman and siman 11: if he sews leather onto the corner it is permitted—see there, which supports this.
