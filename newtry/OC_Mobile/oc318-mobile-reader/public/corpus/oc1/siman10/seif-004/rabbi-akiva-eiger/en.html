Magen Avraham, se'if katan 6: 'But one knot was made invalidly.' This means a knot together with a winding, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 11, se'if katan 18. It implies that even if he made the remaining windings and knots after cutting the invalid material, they do not help. I do not know the source for this. Perhaps in such a case it is valid: disregard the first winding and knot, which were ta'aseh v'lo min ha'asui (made through a preexisting condition rather than proper action) and let the later winding and knot, which were made validly, render it fit.
