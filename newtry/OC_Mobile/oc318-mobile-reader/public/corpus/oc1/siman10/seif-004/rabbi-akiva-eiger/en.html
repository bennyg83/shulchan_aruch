{Rama: Magen Avraham, note 6} Rather one knot is invalid — that is, knot and hulyot. Magen Avraham, siman 11, note 18. It implies that even if he made the rest of the hulyot and knots after he cut them, it does not help. And I did not know the source for this — perhaps in such a case it is valid, for the hulyah and first knot are from he'asuy; nevertheless they may be validated by making hulyot and a knot afterward that are made properly.
