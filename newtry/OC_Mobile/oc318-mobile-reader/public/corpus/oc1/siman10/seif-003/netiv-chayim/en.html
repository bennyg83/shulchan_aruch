{Rama: (Taz, note 7)} For he did not commit a transgression. Note: It implies that if he himself bowed to the linen, it is forbidden to spin it. And I omitted Tosafos in Sukkah 50b, s.v. mitzvah, and the Rosh there, siman 15, that a mitzvah that comes through a transgression is not considered [invalid], since at the time the transgression committed in its name does not take effect.
