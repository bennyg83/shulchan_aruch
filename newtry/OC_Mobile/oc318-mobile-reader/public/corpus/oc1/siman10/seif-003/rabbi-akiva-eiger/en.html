{Rama: There, in note 4} See there — it implies as I said. I did not know any meaning to this. The Mordechai there only divides: even per the view that corners of leather are exempt — that is there, where the whole corner is leather; unlike if the corner itself: the upper part is garment and the lower part leather. And one may say the reverse is also possible: if the upper part is leather and the lower part garment, it is likewise obligated, for both are essential and wherever there is one thread of wool it suffices to obligate.
