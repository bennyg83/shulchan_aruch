(3) There — and one makes for it four tzitzis, etc. And if he made five, he transgresses bal tosif—Magen Avraham, s.k. 2, in the name of Rashi, Parashat Va'etchanan. And likewise Razah, letter 2; Nehar Shalom, letter 1; Ikkevei HaDat, siman 2, letter 2, in the name of Sedei Ha'aretz, volume 3, siman 1; Ben Ish Chai, there. And what Levush wrote: "but one need not make five corners"—implies that if he wishes, he may; Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 2—and see Yeshuot Yaakov, letter 2.

(3) Further — Magen Avraham wrote in that passage, and in seif 6, if he cut them afterward. And Machatzit HaShekel explained: that is, if he made five tzitzis and afterward cut one of the five tzitzis and only four remained—it depends on the dispute of Rabbeinu Yitzchak and Rama in seif 6, regarding attaching tzitzis on tzitzis and cutting them—end quote. And likewise Ma'amar Mordechai, letter 4, that according to Maran it is like adding and invalid whichever one he cuts, whether first or last. But Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham wrote on the above words of Magen Avraham, and these are his words: "And what compares this to seif 6 regarding attaching tzitzis on tzitzis? One can say that here—even for one who disqualifies there when attaching on one corner two—unlike here, for all agree the four first ones that he made valid become valid when he afterward cuts tzitzis of one corner"; and Etz Yosef 6 implies thus—end quote. And likewise Yeshuot Yaakov, in that passage; Eliyah Rabbah, s.k. 2. Nevertheless, to escape the dispute one should untie all the tzitzis and re-tie them anew.
