"And it has no corners," etc. See in Biur Halacha, who is uncertain: if the garment was round and he folded part of its hem onto it and sewed until it became square, do we say the sewing does not help even to be lenient? He concludes that this matter depends on a dispute—therefore one places tzitzis on it and does not bless; see below what we wrote on this:

"It is not exempt." See in Mishna Berurah: if he made the corner square now too, there are opinions among the later authorities whether the sewing helps. And if he inserted the edge of the garment inward and sewed it until the fold above is not recognizable at all, as tailors do—it appears that according to all the sewing helps, and the reason "if it were not needed," etc. does not apply here, for such is craftsmen's practice; if so, also in that case of Biur Halacha that we wrote—if he did it in this manner he must place tzitzis on it and bless; and it is possible furthermore that even to be lenient like that of Shulchan Aruch, sewing such as this helps:
