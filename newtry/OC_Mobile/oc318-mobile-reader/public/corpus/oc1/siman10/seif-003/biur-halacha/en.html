And it has no corners, etc. Artzos HaChaim questions a round garment whose edge was folded and sewn until square: perhaps sewing is ineffective even to create leniency. He concludes that this depends on a dispute, so tzitzis should be attached without a blessing. See what we write immediately below.
It did not become exempt. The Mishnah Berurah cites differing later opinions where the sewn corner remains square. If he folded the garment's edge inward and sewed it so the fold is entirely invisible above, as tailors ordinarily do, all apparently agree the sewing is effective; the rationale 'if he did not need it' does not apply because this is craftsmen's normal method. Accordingly, in Artzos HaChaim's case, if fashioned this way he must attach tzitzis with a blessing. Such sewing may perhaps even create the leniency in Shulchan Aruch's case.
