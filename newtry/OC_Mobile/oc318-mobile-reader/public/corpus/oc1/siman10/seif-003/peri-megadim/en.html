One diagonally — On the Taz: here is Rashi, Menachot 37a, s.v. ba'alat chamesh: he cut from the corner diagonally; and s.v. davtzir: this is a blemish — see there and in Beit Yosef. And thus there is a difference if he cut a little piece from the garment and made three [corners] look like four, it is valid; but if he cut nothing, only a blemish with a knife tore the corner — a diagonal cut does not make a four-cornered [garment] until the majority is open, as below in seif 7. From here our tallitot are seen, where the warp threads emerge from cuts only at the corners, and one need not cut the entire length, for he cut a little piece from the garment and it has four corners that look valid, even if the corner is more than three fingers relative to the rest, since there is no garment of four [corners] there; only if there is a garment do we say the majority closed we follow it — unlike here. See also below, siman 11 in Magen Avraham, letter 7, on this.
