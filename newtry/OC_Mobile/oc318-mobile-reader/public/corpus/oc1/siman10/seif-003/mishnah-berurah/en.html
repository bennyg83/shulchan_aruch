It is not exempt — For if he does not need the corners, let him cut them off and discard them; rather it is certain he will eventually undo the stitching. Therefore it is considered as if he already undid the stitching and it is obligated in tzitzit. For this reason he may now affix tzitzit on the fold even below the thumb-knuckle measurement, if when it is opened it will be above the thumb-knuckle. And all this is when he folded and stitched it rounded; but if he folded and stitched it square, since now too it has a corner, Levush and Magen Avraham hold he may affix tzitzit there on the fold even though it is more than three fingerbreadths from the edge of the corner, since once stitched it is permitted, as in seif 6 regarding the tallit fold where stitching avails. Many disagree and hold that here too stitching does not avail; and that case in seif 6 differs, for there he folded the entire edge, which is the normal way of a garment, unlike here where he folded only part of the garment.
