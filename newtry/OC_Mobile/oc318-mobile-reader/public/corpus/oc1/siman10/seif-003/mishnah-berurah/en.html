It was not exempted. For if he did not need the corners, he should have cut them off and thrown them away. Rather, he certainly intends ultimately to undo the stitching; therefore it is considered as though he had already undone the stitches, and the garment is obligated in tzitzis. For this reason, he may now place the tzitzis on the fold, even below the measure of a thumb-joint, if when it is spread out it will be above the measure of a thumb-joint. All this is where he folded it and sewed it in a rounded manner. But if he folded and sewed it square, since even now there is a corner, the Levush and the Magen Avraham hold that he may place the tzitzis there on the fold, even though it is more than three fingerbreadths from the edge of the corner, since he sewed it. This is like seif 6 regarding a folded tallis, where sewing is effective. Many disagree with this and hold that here too sewing is not effective. That case in seif 6 is different, for it discusses where he folded the whole garment, and that is the manner of wearing it; here, by contrast, he folded only part of the garment.
