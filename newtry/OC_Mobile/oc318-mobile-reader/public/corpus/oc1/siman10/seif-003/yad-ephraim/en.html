Taz note 9 — to validate them by cutting the others, etc. I did not understand — for at the time of making the latter ones they were invalid, since he had not yet cut off the first ones; and how can they return to validity after he cuts the first ones? Rather necessarily, since he stands in bal tosif it is not called an act at all, and the main act is when he cuts the first ones. If so, all the more so: if he cuts the latter ones, they are valid in the first ones — for the act of the latter ones is not an act at all; and all the more so here, where the latter were made invalid and nevertheless they return and become valid — all the more so the first ones that were made in valid form. And likewise is the Magen Avraham's view in that note. And one can say: when cutting the latter ones they do not become valid in the first ones, for that is visible-then-rejected, which does not return to visibility — unlike cutting the first ones, which validates the latter ones, for that is deferral from the outset, which is preferable to visible-then-rejected; deferral from the outset returns to visibility. And although deferral from the outset is also questionable — nevertheless the reason is because it is in his hand, as explained in siman 466 and siman 485 and in Magen Avraham; and if so, visible-then-rejected also, as explained there — one can say that here, where he rejected by hand, it detracts more in visible-then-rejected — it requires study:
