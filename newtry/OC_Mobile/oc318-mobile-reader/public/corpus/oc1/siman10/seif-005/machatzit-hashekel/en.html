(siman katan 5) It is obligated, etc. — and it appears in seif 7, etc., that we follow the majority. In truth Rabbeinu Yonah wrote in the name of Mahariik that it is simple that it is not exempt unless the majority is leather. And below in seif 7, that it is not exempt except when the majority is sealed — one may derive from here that just as for leather the majority must be exempt, so too for sealing: the sealed [portion] must be the majority exempt; except that in Shulchan Aruch the matter is explained for sealing that the majority is required in seif 7, and for leather requiring majority it is not explained explicitly; therefore Magen Avraham had to derive further from the sealed [portion] of this seif.

And see Beit Yosef on this siman. This refers to that [law] of sealing and leather being equal regarding requiring majority; and there is doubt: some say that if he sews leather on the corner it is permitted, etc. — for Rabbeinu Yonah wrote there are those who validate placing pieces of leather on the corner to strengthen the corner, and so too in Shulchan Aruch there; and Rabbeinu Yonah wrote there that even per the view in the Gemara that we follow the corner — and if the corner is leather even though the tallis is wool it is exempt — but we do not rule thus; nevertheless in such a case he is obligated, since it is made only to strengthen; and it is reasonable that underneath it is wool, for Rabbeinu Yonah wrote they place leather on the corner, which implies the leather pieces are on top and underneath is cloth — even so we follow the upper [layer].
