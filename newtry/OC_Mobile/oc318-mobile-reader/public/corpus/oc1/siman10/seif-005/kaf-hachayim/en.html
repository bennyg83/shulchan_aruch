(5) [Seif 2] It has four and he cut one diagonally, etc. The same law applies if it initially had three and he cut one into two—it becomes one with four and is obligated—Darkei Moshe, letter 1; Taz, s.k. 3; Eliyah Rabbah, s.k. 2, in Mishbetzos Zahav, s.k. 3. And specifically if he cut a piece and made from three four; but if he did not cut at all—only notched with a knife, tore at the corner, cut diagonally—it does not become one with four until the majority is less, as below, seif 7, in Mishbetzos Zahav, in that passage. He also wrote in Mishbetzos Zahav: from this we see that our tallisos whose warp strings emerge from the cuts only at the corners need not cut the entire length—see there.
