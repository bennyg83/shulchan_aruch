[5] Because of "you shall make," etc. For it is written: "twisted cords you shall make for yourself on four corners"—it implies the corners must be made before the tzitzis are made; and not specifically that three tzitzis were made, but even if he tied only one knot in invalid fashion and afterward completed the four corners—it is also invalid, and he must untie the knot or the tzitzis made invalidly before making the rest of the tzitzis; but if he completed the tallis and tzitzis before he untied the knot or tzitzis, and afterward he untied the invalid and retied it—it is invalid—Levush.
