“Three tzitzis.” Not specifically that he made three tzitzis invalidly—i.e., before it became a four-cornered garment the majority was invalid—but even if he tied only one knot invalidly and then completed the four corners it is still invalid, and he must untie the knot or the tzitzis made invalidly before making the rest. But if he completed the tallis and tzitzis before untying the invalid knot or tzitzis, and afterward untied the invalid part and retied it, it does not become valid thereby, for all the tzitzis were made while invalid and in violation of a positive command. Levush—and I do not know why—for the others were made validly; is it because one tzitzis is invalid that all are invalidated? So too Sefer Lechem Rav—see siman 15:4.
