Three tzitziyot. There are those who say that one who made three tzitziyot in invalid fashion — that is, before it became a [garment with] four [corners] — for the majority was made in invalid fashion. But even if he tied only one knot and afterward completed the four corners, it is likewise invalid, and he must untie the knot or the tzitzit made in invalid fashion before making the rest of the tzitzit. And if he completed the tallit and tzitzit before untying the knot or tzitzit made in invalid fashion, and afterward untied the invalid [knot or tzitzit] and went back and tied it — it is valid (Magen Avraham), unlike the Levush — see there. If he forgot and made all five tzitziyot on a [garment with] five [corners], he can nullify one afterward and cut [one corner]; for the others were not made in invalid fashion (Taz).
