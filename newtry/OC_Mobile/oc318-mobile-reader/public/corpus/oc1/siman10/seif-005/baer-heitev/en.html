Three tzitzis. This is not specifically when he made three tzitzis in invalidity, meaning before it became a four-cornered garment, so that the majority was made in invalidity. Rather, even if he tied only one knot and afterward completed the four corners, it is also invalid, and he must untie the knot or the tzitzis that was made in invalidity before he makes the rest of the tzitzis. If he completed the tallis and the tzitzis before untying the knot or the tzitzis that had been made in invalidity, and afterward he removed the invalid part and tied it again, it is valid. Magen Avraham, unlike the Levush; see there. If he forgot and made five tzitzis on a five-cornered garment, he can nullify afterward and cut off one, for in any event the others were not made in invalidity. Taz.
