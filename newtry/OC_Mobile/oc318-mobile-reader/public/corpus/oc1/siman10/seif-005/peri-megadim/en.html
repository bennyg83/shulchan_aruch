And its corners — On the Taz: Menachot 40b, 49: the main garment is in view; and one might explain the verse "on the corners of their garments" — on the corners of their garments — the corner may be of whatever kind, provided the corner is attached to the garment. And Levush explained that even if the garment has no corners at all, if he wove rounded corners and placed leather and square corners were made, he is obligated in tzitzis; and one should not say that only when the garment has a square corner do we follow the majority — for then Rava should have said in a short garment we follow the majority. And even though it is also written "on four corners of your garment," learn the unspecified from the specified; and even though the other view in siman 9 [holds] all garments are obligated, leather is not a garment — we require weaving in view.
