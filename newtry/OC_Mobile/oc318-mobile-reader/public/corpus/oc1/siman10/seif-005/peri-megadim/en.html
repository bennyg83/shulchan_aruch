And its corners. See the Taz. Menachos 40b: What is the reason? We require the main part of the garment. I would have thought the verse, on the corners of their garments, means that on the corners of their garments, the corner may be of whatever material it is, provided the corner is attached to the garment. The Levush explains for us that even if the garment has no corners at all, for he wove it with round corners and attached leather so that they became square corners, it is obligated according to the Torah in tzitzis. Do not say that this is only where the garment has a square corner and we follow the majority, for if so Rava should have said briefly: we follow the majority. Although it is also written, on the four corners of your garment, the unspecified verse is learned from the explicit one. Although according to the Rav in siman 9 all garments are obligated, leather is not a garment, for woven material is required as clothing. See what I wrote in Magen Avraham, letter 5. In Beer HaGolah there is a textual error, and it should read 40b.
