If it had three corners, etc. — the same law applies: even if he tied only one knot and afterward completed [the garment to] four corners and completed [placing] the tzitzit, it falls under ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy, and he must untie that knot made in invalid fashion before making the rest of the tzitzit. But if he completed the tallit and the tzitzit, and afterward untied the first knot and went back and tied it, it is not made valid thereby (Maharam of Padua). And some say it is valid in every case, provided he corrects the irregularity (Lechem Rav). And so it appears to me to rule.
