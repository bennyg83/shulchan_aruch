Three corners — Even if it originally had four corners with tzitzit and afterward one corner was cut and rounded, even if that corner was later repaired, nevertheless those three tzitzit are automatically nullified, as shown later in Siman 15, seif 3; and one must undo them and retie them.

Three tzitzit — Not that he made three tzitzit such that the majority was invalid, meaning before it became four-cornered; rather even if he tied only one knot with its chulya and afterward completed the four corners, that knot too is invalid and must be undone and retied.

Because of "you shall make" — That is, as it is written "You shall make fringes for yourself on the four corners," implying the corners must be four before the tzitzit are made, not that tzitzit be made before the corners, which would be "you shall make" and not from what was already made, and invalid. Therefore one must undo and retie them. Specifically the first three tzitzit made invalidly; but the tzitzit on the fourth corner need not be undone, since it was made validly, and the invalidation of the first does not invalidate it.
