Three corners. Even if it originally had four corners with tzitzis, and afterward one corner was cut and became round, then even if that same corner was later repaired, nevertheless those three tzitzis were automatically invalidated, as is proven below in siman 15, seif 3. He must untie them and then tie them again.
Three tzitzis. This does not mean specifically that he made three tzitzis, so that the majority were made invalidly, meaning before it became a four-cornered garment. Rather, even if he tied only one knot with its winding, and afterward completed the four corners, that knot too is invalid, and he must untie it and then tie it again.
Because of "you shall make." That is to say, since it is written, "You shall make yourself twisted threads upon the four corners, etc.," this implies that the corners must first be made into four before the tzitzis are made, and not that the tzitzis are made before the corners. That would be "you shall make," and not from what is already made, and they are invalid. Therefore, he must untie them and then tie them again. This applies specifically to the first three tzitzis, whose making was invalid, but the tzitzis that he made on the fourth corner need not be untied, since it was made validly, and the invalidity of the first ones does not invalidate it.
