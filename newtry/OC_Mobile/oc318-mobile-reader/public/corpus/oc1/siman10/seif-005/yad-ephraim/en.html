Magen Avraham note 13 — if it was, etc. From this it appears, as the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 10 note 13, that one should not tuck the tzitzis into the corners — see there. And in truth, what Darkei Moshe wrote to reject [the proof] from ben tzitzit ha-keset — in my humble opinion the main concern is because of the tzitzis in front of him, since the upper garment is long and the tzitzis at his feet get trampled and torn constantly — unlike the tzitzis of ben tzitzit ha-keset that dragged behind him: there is concern only from people coming behind him who might trample them with their feet; in that there is no concern, for their eyes see the tzitzis and they will not step on them. And also as explained there, that they dragged along the pillows — per Rashi's explanation that he would walk only on cloth items, and presumably he would not walk on them alone — if so it is obvious there is no concern. And in any case I did not understand his difficulty from ben tzitzit ha-keset, for he did so; and if he needed to fix it he would fix it — but in any case his tallis was made in the manner of our tallisot, that if he wishes he raises it on his shoulder and they do not drag at all. But in the upper garment that is long for honor — one can say the Torah did not obligate [tzitzis that] tear constantly, and his ways are ways of pleasantness, as the Beit Yosef wrote — thus in my humble opinion to settle the Beit Yosef's view on this. And in Eliyah Zuta he wrote that ben tzitzit ha-keset had countless garments and each time wore a different one — in my humble opinion this is not so, for the Rama holds: since per the Beit Yosef the Torah exempted a garment whose tzitzis drag, the Torah did not distinguish between a rich person who can change garments and one who cannot; and if so he tied tzitzis in vain in an exempt place. With this is also rejected what was said that they dragged along pillows — for nevertheless, if the Torah exempted, it is strained to say he was lenient with tzitzis on an exempt garment.
