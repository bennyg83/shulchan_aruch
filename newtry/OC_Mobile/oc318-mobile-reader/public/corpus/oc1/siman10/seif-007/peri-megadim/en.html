Like — On the Taz: see later authorities. He explained for Rashi that he gave the reason for tzitzis in the middle — that is not a corner; rather in the unfolded portion he places them. And R. Shimon exempts, for one need not double tzitzis as long as it is not sewn (Tosafos 37b, s.v. hai) — it deals with [a garment] fit to wear thus without doubling. And what Rashi wrote there, s.v. tallit kefulah: long, twice — that is like the way of a tallit to cover most of his body, and it was about twice [the body length]; nevertheless over his whole body from covering the head he can wear without doubling. And Nissim Gaon exempts a doubled tallit even when unfolded, for it deals with one so long that it cannot be worn thus at all without doubling; and R. Shimon exempts entirely — and he raised both interpretations to halachah. And here you will see a special intent, as I shall say: Rashi there (Menachot 41a), s.v. ifshat, that it was twice as wide as his height — surely he cannot wear it thus; and he explained so with intent, for it is difficult why he did not tell him lechatchilah immediately when he saw a doubled tallit with two tzitzis in the folds and three in the unfolded portion — for R. Shimon, the halachah follows him, and thus he transgressed a positive commandment, for he needs four in the unfolded portion, since he told him "go throw them," meaning in the unfolded portion; whence we learn the fold was twice his height. And Nissim Gaon exempts entirely biblically; only rabbinically, because of marit ha'ayin, it is obligated in the unfolded portion — therefore he did not tell him at first, because human dignity was there (see siman 13); only afterward he told him thus, "go throw them," rabbinically because of marit ha'ayin. See letter 8 on this and Rabbi Akiva Eiger, letter 7; and from there, long as two — without this, it requires study, as above.
