[7] And it is good, etc. Olas Tamid wrote: in my humble opinion I do not know a reason for this compromise—for even if he does not bless, what remedy is there if he does not fulfill [the obligation] with these tzitzis? After all, the garment is without tzitzis and it is forbidden to wear it on account of doubt—end quote. And in this vein Magen Avraham wondered; and I already resolved in Eliyah Zuta that Rama holds: if he wears it when folded, certainly he need not attach tzitzis on the unfolded portions since the unfolded portions are not in the fold; only if he wears them when unfolded must he attach tzitzis on the unfolded portions—and we are taught that we should not say that since it is so long he need not attach tzitzis at all. And if so, Rama wrote well that if he wears it folded he should make tzitzis on his folds and not bless; and in Eliyah Zuta I elaborated—see there—and still requires study.
