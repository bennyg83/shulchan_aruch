(siman katan 7) And it is good, and requires study, etc. — for on folio 41 it is taught: a doubled tallis is obligated in tzitzis, and Rabbi Shimon exempts; and the Rambam ruled like Rabbi Shimon, and this is also Rabbeinu Yonah's view here in Shulchan Aruch — except that Rabbeinu Yonah wrote that Rabbi Shimon's exemption is [only] for tying tzitzis on the fold, but in any case one must tie tzitzis on the spread [corners], as Rama wrote in the gloss. But the Rosh ruled like the first tanna that it is obligated — that is, to tie tzitzis on the fold — and these are the views Rama brought. And therefore Magen Avraham challenged what Rama wrote, "and it is good," etc. — it appears that thereby one fulfills both views, and this is not so. And in truth Darkei Moshe disagreed entirely; and even on the spread [corners] one need not tie. And according to Darkei Moshe's view, the Rambam's ruling like Rabbi Shimon is likewise that he exempts entirely. And therefore one should not [rely on] what Rama wrote, "and it is good to do," etc. — that is, on the fold. And thereby, in Rama's view, in Darkei Moshe's understanding of the Rambam's intent, one fulfills both views — that is, the Rambam and the Rosh: for the Rambam exempts entirely and for the Rosh one is obligated to tie tzitzis on the fold; and therefore if one ties tzitzis on the fold and does not bless on it, one fulfills both views, for blessings do not impede. And according to this, what Rama wrote, "but one must tie on the four spread corners" — not that Rama holds thus per the Rambam, but he comes to explain Rabbeinu Yonah's view, who rules like the Rambam himself; and Rabbeinu Yonah holds thus per the Rambam, as he wrote in his long work, that in any case one must tie on the four spread corners; and therefore Rama explains Rabbeinu Yonah's view in Shulchan Aruch likewise. And what Rama wrote here, "and it is good," etc., is according to Rama's understanding of the Rambam's words; and it appears to Rama that the main part of his intent is to exempt entirely — as above.

But Mordechai wrote, etc. — that is, even according to Rama's understanding of the Rambam's words that he exempts entirely, nevertheless one does not fulfill according to all views by tying on the four folded corners; for in any case one does not fulfill Mordechai's view, who wrote that one must tie on the spread [corners].

And Nekudas Yosef explained, etc. — Parashas Shelach, "a man's measure," etc., that it is not a garment. And in such a case the Taz wrote that certainly one is entirely exempt from tying tzitzis on it, since it is not fit for wearing.
