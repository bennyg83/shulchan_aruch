“Most of it closed.” We follow the main garment, as stated in seif 4; if he put tzitzis in it and afterward opened the majority, they are invalid, as stated in seif 5.

“And one does not fulfill with it.” It appears to me the same applies: one does not bless on it. It appears to me that even for those who follow the lenient custom it is forbidden ab initio to create a rabbinic doubt of prohibition, as I wrote—not like Eliyah Rabbah.
