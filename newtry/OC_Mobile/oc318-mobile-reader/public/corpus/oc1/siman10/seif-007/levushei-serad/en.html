(Magen Avraham, siman katan 9) The second [set] like the first — not like the Taz in siman katan 9; and even though when cutting the second there is no reason to validate — and for this reason the Taz disqualifies, as I explained in Taz there — nevertheless Magen Avraham holds that ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy does not disqualify except when its making began in invalid fashion; but if at first it was valid and became invalid, it returns to validity; and therefore in truth it is necessarily valid: if he cut the first it is valid because the second is not an act, since he transgressed bal tosif; and if he cut the second it is valid since the first had the beginning of its making in validity. And the Taz apparently holds that even at the beginning of its making in validity, ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy applies — see siman 15 and in what follows.
