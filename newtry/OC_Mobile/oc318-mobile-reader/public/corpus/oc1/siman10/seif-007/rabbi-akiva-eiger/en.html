See Maggid Mishneh, chapter 7 of Hilchot Lulav. He appears to reconcile Sanhedrin with Sukkah by distinguishing an addition of the same species, which diminishes the mitzvah, from taking another species, where we say that each stands independently; see there. At first glance this is astonishing, because Sanhedrin appears to apply 'each stands independently' even to additional tzitzis threads of the same type. This requires further study, as Lechem Mishneh writes there: when the addition does not contain the minimum measure of a mitzvah, it is treated as a different species; see there. Yet the difficulty remains, for a rebellious elder can still be found in a case of eight threads or one who added tzitzis to existing tzitzis. This requires further study.
