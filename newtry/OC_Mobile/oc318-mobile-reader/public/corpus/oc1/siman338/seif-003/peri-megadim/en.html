And not with apples — Taz; and these are Bach's words; also in Tur it is written one does not play with nuts and the like — for R' Chaim on account of making sound, and for Rif on account of leveling bumps; and for R' Chaim with apples on ground where one touches another, making sound also applies, see there in Bach; nevertheless for us we rule there is no prohibition of making sound when not intending for song except on account of leveling bumps; and on paved ground it is possible to permit, see siman 337 and Magen Avraham here letter 7; and what Mishna Berurah wrote in the gloss on "tashich" permitted — meaning they separated from yesterday and designated them for that; if they separated on Shabbat — one can say it is muktzeh, see siman 308 in Magen Avraham letter 50
