To repair. See Magen Avraham who raised that it is forbidden to say to repair the musical instrument, rather to say to the gentile that he play, see there (and in Sefer Orach Chayyim) he sided to permit, and it is in Maharil that once the ruler decreed they should not play with musical instruments and he commanded to bring bridegroom and bride to another city to make the wedding there in order to play with musical instruments. And Radbaz part 1 siman 332 wrote that in his land they were accustomed to forbid telling a gentile to play, nevertheless if the gentile came and played on his own they do not protest him, and by law it is permitted to tell him on erev Shabbat that he play on Shabbat since the Israelite himself is forbidden only on account of a decree, and if there is concern for evasion one should make a fence also in this.
