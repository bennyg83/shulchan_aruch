(א) Clock pair that jingles for hours — meaning it shows the hours [called "clock"] made to make sound to announce the hours
(ב) Permitted to set it — meaning we are not concerned when its sound is heard that they will suspect he set it on Shabbat, for all know it is his way to set it from yesterday
(ג) And to prepare from erev — but on Shabbat not to mention that if it stopped it is forbidden to prepare it that it go; and even only to oscillate the iron rod that oscillates and makes sound — for several poskim this is Torah prohibition of repairing a utensil; but even while it is going it is also forbidden to pull the weights [or in foreign tongue the chain of the wheels] so its motion not stop; unless for a sick person who needs this there is room to permit setting it while going if difficult to find a Jew for this, and through gentile one may permit setting even when motion stopped [Acharonim]. And know further regarding those clocks that sometimes when standing and motion stopped he partially oscillates it and motion returns — he must be careful not to oscillate on Shabbat
