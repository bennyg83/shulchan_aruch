(s.k. 6) And throw, etc. — for it is a decree for a decree. And so Magen Avraham wrote there in siman 392 s.k. 23 that it is a dispute of Abaye and Rava in tractate Shabbos 11b, and likewise in Eruvin — Abaye holds that whatever is forbidden in reshus harabim, even if forbidden only by decree, nevertheless also in karmelis it is forbidden, a decree lest reshus harabim; and even though generally we do not decree a decree for a decree, nevertheless regarding carrying they decree; and Rava holds that also regarding carrying they do not decree a decree for a decree; and we rule like Rava.
