6) Leather boots worn during the rainy season, called gezmes, have iron nails called nalges fixed in their heels and make a loud sound while walking. Similarly, walking sticks used by the elderly have a large piece of iron fixed at their lower end that makes a sound when it strikes stones on the ground. All opinions permit these.
