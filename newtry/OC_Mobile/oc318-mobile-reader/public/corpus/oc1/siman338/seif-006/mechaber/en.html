Forbidden to draw water with a wheel — a decree lest he fill his garden and ruin or the soaking pool of flax. Therefore if there was neither garden nor ruin nor pool to soak flax there — permitted. And some say they forbade only a large wheel that draws much water together without exertion, which are wheels fixed with many buckets around, but our wheels that do not fill except a little — permitted for there is nothing to be concerned about
