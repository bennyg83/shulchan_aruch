4) There: When it is not musical, it is permitted. Certainly, it is permitted when one does not need the sound, such as wearing a hollow ring containing a stone that makes noise. Since one does not desire that sound, all opinions permit it, even the authority who rules that producing any sound is forbidden. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1. Tur writes the same in the name of Maharam. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1. See above, siman 301, seif 23, and our words there in paragraph 133.
