(א) (s.k. 4) To repair, etc. Requires study, for in Mordechai, etc. — and Rama's law derives from Mordechai, as is written siman 307 seif 5 that for need of mitzva they permitted only shvut di-shvut; and to repair the instrument is Torah prohibition, as he said afterward in the name of Rashi in Eruvin; therefore Mordechai permitted only telling a gentile to play, for it is shvut di-shvut.

(ב) It is in Maharil, etc. — and he commanded to bring, etc. — and this action was on a weekday; nevertheless it is heard from this incident that the essence of joy of bridegroom and bride depends on musical instrument; therefore Rama permitted from his own view more than Mordechai, even telling a gentile to do Torah melacha.
