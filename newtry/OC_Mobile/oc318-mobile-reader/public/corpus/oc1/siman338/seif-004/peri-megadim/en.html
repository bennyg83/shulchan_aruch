The drip — Taz expanded; and Magen Avraham letter 12 — it is implied he explained like Yeshuot Shmuel that nullifying from its primary use depends on one does not make graf shel re'i l'chatchila; see Tur who challenged Rambam who wrote chapter 25 halacha 24 that with unfit drip it is forbidden — this is for R' Yehuda Shabbos 43, for a utensil is moved only for something moved, etc.; and the difficulty is also for R' Chaim, for he too establishes only with fit drip, otherwise nullifying a vessel from its primary use; therefore Taz explained that for Rambam he holds like Abaye there that even in a place of loss one does not make graf shel re'i l'chatchila; if so it is nullifying a vessel from its primary use; and Tur holds like Rabba that one makes l'chatchila in a place of loss like Rabba there; and on Shabbat 43 they establish with fit for R' Chaim — it is for Abaye who challenged there thus, but for law Tur holds like Rabba and wherever there is drip one makes l'chatchila and there is no nullifying from its primary use when pouring.
