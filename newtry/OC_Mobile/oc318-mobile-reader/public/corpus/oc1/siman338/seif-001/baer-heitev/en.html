Song. And the same applies to anything that is in the manner of song, as is written siman 339 seif 3. And so Yeshuot Shmuel: any sound-maker not intended for joy, such as a sun-clock that jingles for the synagogue and the like — permitted, end of his words. And if they place water in a holed vessel and drip into a metal vessel so that a pleasant sound is heard — forbidden, for this is like a musical instrument. And for a sick person it is permitted. And if he drips forcefully so as to awaken the sleeping — even for a healthy person it is permitted, for it is not like a musical instrument that acts pleasantly and gently; if so, all the more so these bells on the curtain have no prohibition, for his intent is not that they sound pleasantly and gently. And furthermore, since for a sick person it is permitted, all the more so for a matter of mitzva. And furthermore, nowadays there is room to be lenient as is written siman 339 seif 3 in the gloss, and so Knesset HaGedolah is lenient because it is for the need of a mitzva that the people hear and stand, for likewise for dancing it is also permitted for the need of a mitzva as is written siman 339. And Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 282 s.k. 2 forbids here too and wrote one should protest them, see there. But they already were accustomed to be lenient as is written.
