Things forbidden on Shabbat on account of making sound. And in it are 8 seifim: Making sound
with a musical instrument is forbidden, but to knock on the door and the like when it is not in the manner of song is permitted:And likewise if he does not perform an action it is permitted, therefore those who call their friends and whistle in their mouths like a bird are permitted to do so on Shabbat (Hagahot Alfasi). And forbidden to strike on Shabbat on the door with the ring fixed in the door even though he does not intend for song, nevertheless since the utensil is designated for that it is forbidden, therefore forbidden for the servant to strike the door to call to the synagogue by means of the utensil designated for that, rather he strikes with his hand on the door. (Agur and Beit Yosef in the name of Peskei Tosafos of Eruvin).
