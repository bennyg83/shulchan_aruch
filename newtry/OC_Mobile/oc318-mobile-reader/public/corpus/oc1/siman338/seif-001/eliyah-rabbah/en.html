[1] To knock, etc. All the more so when he does not need sound, such as a hollow ring with a stone making sound — permitted to place it; and if they place water in a holed vessel and drip into a metal vessel so a pleasant sound is heard like the sound of cymbals — forbidden, for this is like a musical instrument; and for a sick person permitted; and if he strikes forcefully to awaken the sleeping — even for a healthy person permitted, for it is not like a musical instrument (Tur and Acharonim). And bells on a Torah scroll or curtain — Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 282 and here forbids; Nachalat Tzvi and Magen Avraham permit as is written siman 339 seif 3; and per this one may rely to be lenient regarding a clock pair fixed on house doors to make sound when a person enters; nevertheless below it will be explained that specifically regarding clapping and dancing they are lenient; and furthermore the curtain is a mitzva; but for the reason Magen Avraham wrote that the one who opens the curtain does not intend for sound — if so the same applies to the door clock; nevertheless one should not be lenient for this reason alone, rather we say leave them alone like dancing not in a place of mitzva
