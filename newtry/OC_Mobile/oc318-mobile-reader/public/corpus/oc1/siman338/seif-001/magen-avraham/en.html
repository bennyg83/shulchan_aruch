(א) With musical instrument. And the same applies to anything in the manner of song, as is written siman 339 seif 3; and so Yeshuot Shmuel: any sound-maker not intended for joy, such as a sun-clock that jingles for the synagogue and the like — permitted, end of his words. And Maggid Mishneh wrote it is permitted to place a ring that has lead making sound, see there siman 301 s.k. 23. And if they place water in a holed vessel and drip into a metal vessel so a pleasant sound is heard like the sound of cymbals — forbidden, for this is like a musical instrument; and for a sick person permitted; and if he drips forcefully to awaken the sleeping — even for a healthy person permitted, for it is not like a musical instrument that acts pleasantly and gently; if so all the more so these bells on the curtain have no prohibition, for his intent is not that they sound pleasantly and gently. And furthermore since for a sick person permitted, all the more so for a matter of mitzva. And furthermore nowadays there is room to be lenient as is written siman 339 seif 3 in the gloss, and so Knesset HaGedolah is lenient because it is for need of mitzva that the people hear and stand, for likewise for dancing it is also permitted for need of mitzva as is written siman 339. And even though Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 282 forbids, they already were accustomed to be lenient as is written; and furthermore that one who opens the curtain does not intend for sound at all, as is written siman 301 s.k. 23 see there

(ב) Like a bird. That they call "tsufil" in foreign tongue; even though in those whistles they are also accustomed to sweeten with song — here since he intends to call his friend he does not sweeten in the call, etc.; and also he does not perform an action, end of his words — it is implied that if he sweetens with song it is forbidden even though permitted to whistle with the mouth here, for this case is different for it resembles a musical instrument; nevertheless it appears to me it is permitted since he does not perform an action

(ג) Forbidden for servant, etc. And there are places where they take another utensil to strike on Shabbat
