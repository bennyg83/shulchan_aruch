(Magen Avraham s.k. 11) Yeshuot Shmuel ruled; Mishna Berurah — and it does not appear so, for it is clear explicitly that even on Shabbat they cover fruits and even wine on account of monetary loss, for according to R' Yehuda the Mishnah deals with fit fruits; and also for Ula the Mishnah also deals with fit fruits, for he said "and even in the airspace of the bricks." And the braita teaches spreading a mat over bricks on Shabbat even though the Talmud establishes if extra remains from the building that they are fit for plastering on them — nevertheless it is not better than fit fruits and more than this the braita teaches above daf 3: an egg born on Shabbat, etc., but one may place a utensil over it on account of monetary loss; and the same applies that they cover even tevel fruits; and even though R' Yehuda establishes it requires its place — but for Ula we do not establish it requires its place, as Rashi explained there; therefore one should not budge from Shulchan Aruch's ruling
