(א) (s.k. 1) With instrument, etc. And as is written siman 339 seif 3 that even to strike with a finger in the manner of singers it is forbidden, and likewise to rattle a nut since it is in the manner of song; and so Yeshuot Shmuel, etc., brings also proof for his words from Yeshuot Shmuel that he does not intend for joy, etc.; and specifically if he does not intend for joy, for if he intends for joy then certainly it is in the manner of song and forbidden — it is implied even through a utensil.

(ב) Such as a sun-clock that jingles, etc. And it should be said that he does not hold like Rama who forbids since it is designated for that; or it deals when he takes another utensil on Shabbat as Magen Avraham s.k. 3 wrote. And Maggid Mishneh wrote it is permitted to place a ring, etc.; and so Tur in the name of Rambam. And he wrote this is permitted — in my humble opinion for us who require specifically sound of song; rather even for poskim who are strict and forbid even sound that is not of song — namely specifically when intending to make sound; but a ring with lead — it is made l'chatchila for ornament to make sound, but the wearer's intent is not to make sound, rather it makes sound automatically.

(ג) And if they place water in a vessel, etc. — all this is Gemara Eruvin 104a, namely according to Rabba whom we rule like, that it requires specifically sound of song, see there.

(ד) Sound of cymbals. There — a type of musical instrument.

(ה) And for a sick person permitted. He wanted to say through hearing pleasant sound sleep comes to the sick; if so all the more so these bells, etc.; and furthermore since for sick permitted, all the more so for matter of mitzva. And he challenged in Sefer Teshuvat Shavet that what a fortiori is this — behold we see they were more lenient for sick than for matter of mitzva, etc.; and in my humble opinion one can say thus is Magen Avraham's intent: one, they do not intend to make pleasant sound like song, and if so permitted for us who require specifically sound of song; and he also wrote: meaning even for those who are strict and forbid even sound that is not of song — nevertheless in place of mitzva he agrees it is permitted, since for sick permitted even sound of song, all the more so for matter of mitzva that is permitted in any case when not intending for pleasant sound of song.

(ו) Siman 339 seif 3 — for us who are not expert in making musical instruments there is no lest he make a musical instrument; and even though it is implied Rama did not bring Tosafos' words who are lenient nowadays — not for this reason but to settle the custom; nevertheless their words do not appear as essence in my eyes, Magen Avraham — a matter forbidden by vote one may not permit even though the reason ceased; nevertheless for matter of mitzva one may be lenient and rely on Tosafos' words.

(ז) And even though Taz in Yoreh De'ah siman 282, so it should read, and so too Taz here.

(ח) And furthermore that one who opens, etc. — and it resembles a ring with lead, for the curtain too is made l'chatchila with bells to make sound when opening the ark of holiness so the people hear and stand; nevertheless the opener does not intend except to take the Torah scroll and does not intend to make sound at all — therefore permitted for all.
