(א) (s.k. 2) And it is implied that if he sweetens, etc. — for we require specifically two for certainty: that he does not sweeten; and also he does not perform an action.

(ב) Nevertheless it appears to me permitted. And in Sefer Shulchan Gavoah, etc., he says one suffices since he does not sweeten in the call; and also for another reason that he does not perform an action — in one of these it suffices.
