(א) To repair the instrument. Requires study, for in Mordechai there he permitted only rabbinic melacha, as is written siman 307 seif 5 see there; and Rashi in Eruvin daf 103 wrote when the string broke and he rolls the upper peg until the string reaches the lower peg and wraps it — this is a primary melacha of repairing a utensil, end of his words; if so forbidden to tell a gentile to do it; and it is possible Rama decided from his own view to permit for honor of bridegroom and bride, for it is in Maharil that once the ruler decreed they should not play with musical instruments and commanded to bring bridegroom and bride to another city to make the wedding there in order to play with musical instruments; and Radbaz part 1 siman 332 wrote that in his land they were accustomed to forbid telling a gentile to play, nevertheless if the gentile came and played on his own they do not protest; and by law permitted to tell him on erev Shabbat that he play on Shabbat since the Israelite himself is forbidden only on account of a decree, and if there is concern for evasion one should make a fence also in this, end of his words, so it is
