One does not play with nuts nor with apples and the like on account of leveling bumps:And specifically on top of the ground, but on top of a table it is permitted for there is nothing to decree there on account of bumps (Rabbenu Yerucham). Permitted to play with bones called "tashich" even though they make sound, since they do not intend for song (Hagahot Alfasi end of Eruvin). And all this is when playing in a manner of mere jest (Beit Yosef). But when playing in order to profit it is forbidden even when playing fairly and unfairly for it is like buying and selling (Agur and Beit Yosef and Rambam chapter 23). Nevertheless one should not protest women and children for it is better they be inadvertent and not intentional [Tosafos and Semag and Hagahat Maimoniot chapter 21]. And regarding playing with a ball see above siman 308 seif 45
