5) If water is placed in a perforated utensil and drips into a metal vessel to produce a pleasant humming sound, it is forbidden because it is like a musical instrument. It is permitted for an ill person to help him sleep. If the water drips forcefully to awaken a sleeping person, it is permitted even for a healthy person, because it does not resemble a musical instrument, since it does not produce a gentle, pleasant sound.
