(א) One does not play, etc. — meaning they roll them and strike one against another in the manner girls play

(ב) Leveling bumps — that he levels the way to roll the nut

(ג) On a table, etc. — and the same on a mat or garment or board; and we do not decree in all this lest ground, for decree for a decree; but on ground, even if paved where leveling bumps does not apply — also forbidden, for ground exchanges with ground; and we decree lest unpaved ground

(ד) With bones, etc. — and they practice making them of silver so they are designated for Shabbat, lest otherwise it resemble weekday activity; and Mahar"e Sasson disagrees and holds since there is no benefit in knowing that play wisdom it is forbidden; and the same all types of play; and see in Birkei Yosef who brought more in the name of several great ones who were strict in this

(ה) Fairly and unfairly — meaning "even" or "odd" in foreign language
