(א) Table permitted. And requires study, for at end of siman 324 they decreed a feed-trough of a vessel lest it be a feed-trough of ground; and it is not difficult, for there it is psik reisha on ground like Ran wrote end of chapter "they suspend" and Tosafos there wrote they are accustomed to intend; and this is Rabbenu Yerucham's language: on a mat or garment or board permitted

(ב) With bones. With dice; and they are accustomed to make the dice of silver lest otherwise it resemble weekday activity, as is written siman 327 seif 3 (Shulchan Gavoah); and R' Eliezer Sasson disagrees and holds that since there is no benefit in knowing the wisdom of dice it is forbidden, as is written siman 307 seif 17, end of his words; if so the same applies to all types of play; and in a place where they were accustomed to be lenient one should not protest them

(ג) Fairly and unfairly. Meaning "even" or "odd" in German
