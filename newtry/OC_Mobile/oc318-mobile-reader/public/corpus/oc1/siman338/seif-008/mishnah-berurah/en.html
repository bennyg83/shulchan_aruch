(א) Under the drip — that drips through the ceiling and the like

(ב) For washing — for there is no muktzeh or nolad on the rain falling there, and he can drink it or wash from it; and the same if fit only for animal drinking

(ג) Forbidden on account that one does not, etc. — meaning one does not make something repulsive on its account so that afterward it will be permitted to take out like graf shel re'i; and if even if he resigns that it remain so and he not move it from its place — also forbidden as poskim wrote; see in Biur Halacha

(ד) Permitted, etc. — meaning even before it filled [Eshel Avraham]

(ה) To move it, etc. — and specifically when placed in a place of his permanent sitting there that it is repulsive to him, and it is like graf shel re'i that they permitted moving to take to the refuse heap for his honor if it stands in his permanent sitting place, as above siman 308 seif 134 see there
