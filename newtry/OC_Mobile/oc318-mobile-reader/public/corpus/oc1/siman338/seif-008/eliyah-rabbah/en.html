[8] Permitted to cover them. Yam Shel Shlomo chapter 5 of Beitzah forbids; Nachalat Tzvi is lenient on account of loss; and so it appears to me as the essence, for so I found in Avodat HaKodesh daf 44; and furthermore Rashal wrote Ran forbids; and in my humble opinion so is implied from what Ran wrote chapter "they lower" — this is his language: it teaches us that even though it is not moved on Shabbat they move a utensil on its account to cover it, until here — it is somewhat implied permitted on Shabbat; and what he wrote in the Mishnah "and we do not say exertion not for the need of Yom Tov" — the rabbis permitted it on account of monetary loss, until here his language — not specifically Yom Tov; furthermore one can say for precision he said Yom Tov, for there is no permission even on Yom Tov except on account of loss; and per this one can say also in Rashi's explanation thus; see siman 324
