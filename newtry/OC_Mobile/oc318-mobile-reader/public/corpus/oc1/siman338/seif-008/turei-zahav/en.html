Drip fit for washing. Thus Rambam and Tur wrote on it and it is not clear, etc., and certainly requires study to resolve Tur's words, for in chapter Kirah (Shabbos daf 43) they establish it only with fit drip so that it not be nullifying a vessel from its primary use; and it appears to resolve that Rambam who wrote here that one does not make a graf shel re'i l'chatchila holds like Abaye there, that even though he said "let my sin come upon me" for transgressing Rabba's words in a manner of mourning, he said so but for law he did not retract; and so Beit Yosef in siman 308 in the name of the Maggid; therefore he said if the water is not fit it is forbidden, for it is a graf shel re'i, even though in chapter Kirah it says it requires fit drip because otherwise it is nullifying a vessel from its primary use — all one reason, for the reason of prohibition of nullifying a vessel from its primary use is that it is like building, since it is forbidden to move it, it is like building in this, and this is on account that it is forbidden to move it, and graf shel re'i does not help here like Abaye, for it is l'chatchila; and this is Rambam's view who gave the reason on account of one does not make graf, etc., l'chatchila. And that which they did not mention in chapter Kirah only the reason of nullifying a vessel from its primary use — because there they do not deal with pouring the drip outside, only with placing the vessel under the drip, and they forbade it on account of nullifying the vessel from its primary use since afterward it is forbidden to pour out because the water is not fit, and how would there be permission on account of graf shel re'i for this — therefore Rambam wrote here that it is forbidden l'chatchila, for Rambam deals with pouring outside. But Tur holds like Rabba that there is no prohibition of graf l'chatchila, since rain already drips in the house, as Tosafos wrote in chapter "they lower" there, and the sugya in chapter Kirah is according to Abaye as is written there that Abaye challenged R' Yosef; therefore there was nullifying a vessel from its primary use as Rambam wrote; and we rule like Rabba as is implied in chapter "they lower" that Abaye retracted; if so there is no nullifying a vessel from its primary use, for it is permitted to pour out on account of graf shel re'i; therefore he ruled here that the water need not be fit for washing, as was explained in settling their words; and for law he ruled here like Rambam
