(א) That one does not make. Explanation: the essence of the reason is one does not nullify a vessel from its primary use; but if it were permitted to make a graf shel re'i l'chatchila it would be permitted to place a vessel under it, for behold afterward it is permitted to pour out; rather on account that one does not make graf — if so when he places the vessel he should not do so in order that it be a graf and take it out, rather it should stand for that purpose and it is nullifying a vessel from its primary use (Yeshuot Shmuel)

(ב) Permitted to move it. And specifically when it is placed in a place where it is repulsive to him, as is written siman 308 seifim 136–137
