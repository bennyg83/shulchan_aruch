(א) Forbidden to lower them — for exertion not for the need of Shabbat; behold fruits for today's need are permitted lest they spoil in rain [Peri Megadim]
(ב) Permitted to cover them — behold Yeshuot Shmuel was strict here and Magen Avraham brought him; but Eshel Avraham and other Acharonim proved from the Gemara it is permitted
(ג) That they are muktzeh — such as they are arranged standing for building, as above siman 308 seif 17
(ד) Permitted to cover them — but forbidden to move them from their place even if he has nothing with which to cover [Magen Avraham in siman 334 s.k. 3]
