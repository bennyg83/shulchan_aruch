[8] It is permitted to cover them. Yam Shel Shlomo in chapter 5 of Beitzah prohibits this, while Nachalat Tzvi is lenient because of financial loss. This appears to me primary, for I found the same in Avodat HaKodesh, page 44. Moreover, Maharshal writes that the Ran prohibits, but in my humble opinion the Ran in Perek Mashilin implies the opposite. He writes that the Mishnah teaches that even though the object itself may not be moved on Shabbos, a vessel may be moved for its sake in order to cover it. This somewhat implies permission on Shabbos. His statement on the Mishnah that we do not regard this as exertion unnecessary for Yom Tov because the Sages permitted it for financial loss does not mean specifically Yom Tov. Alternatively, the mention of Yom Tov is deliberate only to teach that even on Yom Tov the permission exists solely because of financial loss. Rashi can likewise be explained this way. See siman 521.
