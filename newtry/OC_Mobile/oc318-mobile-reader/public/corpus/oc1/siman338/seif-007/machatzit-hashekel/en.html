(א) (s.k. 7) Table, etc. — feed-trough of vessel lest feed-trough of ground; and also in feed-trough of ground it is only on account of a decree lest he come to level bumps, and it is a decree for a decree.

(ב) And as Rabbenu Sasson wrote in siman 337 — they forbid paved lest unpaved, and in unpaved itself it is forbidden only lest he come to level bumps; and in truth one should not compare, etc., for feed-trough is different, for both feed-trough of vessel and feed-trough of ground are called feed-trough.

(ג) Therefore feed-trough exchanges with feed-trough; and in siman 337 both are ground and one exchanges with the other; unlike table with ground that does not exchange; and so we say in Shabbos 151a: ground exchanges with ground; a corpse in ground does not exchange.
