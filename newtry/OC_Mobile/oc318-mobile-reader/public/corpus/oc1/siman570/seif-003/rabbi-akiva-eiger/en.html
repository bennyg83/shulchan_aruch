Seif 3: On one who swore in vain and ate. In responsa Rashba siman 214: possible to say Rambam holds it is d'oraisa forbidden to fast on Shabbat, as we say in Berachot; but from now on Shabbat and Yom Tov — since one certainly eats — likewise on Shabbat one must eat d'oraisa, similar to Yom Tov which is one day of Pesach and Sukkot — see there. If so, it implies one who swears not to eat bread on Shabbat also makes a vain oath. And it requires study also from Rambam's wording in chapter 1 of laws of oaths that he wrote one should not eat matzah on Passover night or fast on Shabbat and Yom Tov — since he did not also write not to eat bread on Shabbat, it implies he speaks only of fasting. Also what appears from the above words of Rashba that for Rambam fasting on Yom Tov is not d'oraisa, for chapter 1 of Berachot deals with the first night of Pesach and Yom Tov — yet Rambam wrote Shabbat and Yom Tov — requires study.
