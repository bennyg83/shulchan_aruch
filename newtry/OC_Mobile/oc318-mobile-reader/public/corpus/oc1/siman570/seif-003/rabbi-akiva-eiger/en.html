Se'if 3. "For he swore in vain and eats." In the responsa of the Rashba, siman 214, he wrote: it is possible to say that the Rambam holds that it is forbidden according to the Torah to fast on Shabbos, from that which we say in Berachos: "If so, then Shabbos and Yom Tov, where he must eat," implies likewise that on Shabbos one must eat according to the Torah, similar to Yom Tov, namely the first day of Pesach and of Sukkos; see there. If so, it implies that one who swore not to eat bread on Shabbos is likewise [under] a vain oath. And this requires further study, also from the language of the Rambam in chapter 1 of Hilchos Shevuos, where he wrote "[if he swore] that he will not eat matzah on the night of Pesach, or that he will fast on Shabbos and Yom Tov"; since he did not also cite "[or] that he will not eat bread on Shabbos," it implies that he said specifically "to fast." Also, from what appears from the words of the Rashba above, that according to the Rambam a fast of Yom Tov is not according to the Torah, for that which is in chapter 1 of Berachos deals with the first night of Pesach, and [calling it] "Yom Tov" is difficult, since the Rambam cites Shabbos and Yom Tov; and this requires further study.
