An individual, etc. — it appears to me in siman 248 and in Yoreh Deah siman 215, and in siman 239: sometimes a person damages the letters of the Tetragrammaton — once in this letter and once in that letter — and repair is needed per the damage. All deeds a person does while still wicked — the Torah he learned does not give power to holiness; on the contrary it adds power to the kelipah, and of him it is said, “But to the wicked the Omnipresent said,” etc., and by this he adds sin to transgression. When he returns in repentance he removes that power from the kelipah and brings it into holiness. The matter of repentance is written at length in its place, and the gates of tears are not locked, for tears are supernal matters (Lechem min haShamayim).
