1) [Seif 1] An individual who accepted a fast upon himself, etc. For if they decreed a fast upon the community and began fasting, and one of these days occurred during the series, their decree takes effect after the fact, as stated above in siman 418, seif 2, and below in siman 572, seif 2, see there.
2) Ibid. If he accepted it using the language of accepting a fast, etc., namely, he said, "I hereby undertake a fast for such and such a number of days." Levush; Shulchan Gavoah, os 1. The same applies when he said, "It is incumbent upon me to fast," or, "I hereby vow to fast." As for that which was written afterward, that "it is incumbent upon me" is the language of a vow, the Magen Avraham explained that he said, "The food of such and such a day is konam upon me." Eliyah Rabbah, os 1. However, the unqualified wording of the other poskim implies that specifically when he said, "I hereby undertake a fast for such and such a number of days," it is language of acceptance, but if he said, "It is incumbent upon me to fast," and the like, this too is language of a vow and requires annulment, as written below in os 8, see there.
3) Ibid. If he accepted it using the language of accepting a fast, etc. It must be said that he accepted it at Minchah. Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. Meaning, if he did not accept it at Minchah close to the day of the fast, there are in any event those who say that he need not fast at all, as written in siman 563 and in the Magen Avraham there. See our words on siman 562, os 50.
