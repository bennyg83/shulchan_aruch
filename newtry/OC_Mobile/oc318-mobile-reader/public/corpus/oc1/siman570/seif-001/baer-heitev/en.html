Acceptance. That is, that they accepted it at Mincha; see siman 562 and 563. One who vowed to fast four consecutive days, and within them dreamed a dream on Shabbos and needed to sit a [follow-up] fast for his fast: the Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 251, was in doubt whether it counts for him toward the tally; and see what I wrote above in siman 288. And see siman 568, s.k. 21, what is written there.
Behold. Meaning that he says, "Behold, eating on such-and-such a day is konam upon me"; but if he says, "Behold I vow to fast one day," it is not called a neder. Therefore they practiced that one who says, "Behold I vow to fast Mondays and Thursdays all the days of the year," need not fast on Rosh Chodesh and needs no annulment (Magen Avraham); and see Yoreh De'ah, siman 215.
He needs. And if they did not annul it for him, he must fast according to all opinions; and it appears to me that he must sit a [follow-up] fast for his fast, as is written in siman 568, se'if 5 (Magen Avraham).
And Purim. For these days are not called Yom Tov. And the same applies to Rosh Chodesh. But Chol HaMoed is called Yom Tov (Magen Avraham).
