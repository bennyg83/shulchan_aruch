The language of a neder requires annulment by a sage. For a neder takes effect upon a matter of mitzvah, for he forbids the object upon himself, and there is no commandment upon the object; but with a shevuah, where he forbids himself regarding the object, and he is already commanded from Mount Sinai not to do so. And see in our words in Yoreh De'ah, siman 215, what was raised from this against what is there, and how we hold the halachah; and that entire siman was elucidated there, with Heaven's help.
