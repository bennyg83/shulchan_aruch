Language. See the Taz; and see Yoreh De'ah 215 in the Taz, letter 3, where he dealt with this at length; and see here siman 418. And days on which tachanun is not said, such as the 15th of Av and of Shevat, and Isru Chag, and [the period] between Rosh Chodesh Sivan and Shavuos, and between Yom Kippur and Sukkos: see what he wrote in Yoreh De'ah 215, letter 11, that their law in this matter is like the days forbidden by their [rabbinic] words, and not regarding sitting a [follow-up] fast for his fast; see Magen Avraham 429. However, if he erred and fasted not as a dream-fast, it requires further study whether he must sit a [follow-up] fast for his fast on days when tachanun is not said. And regarding the 15th of Av, whether it is included in "Yom Tov," see Eliyah Rabbah here, and the end of siman 520 and siman 573 in the Magen Avraham, letter 1; it is implied that the same applies to the 15th of Shevat; and see the Magen Avraham here; and with Hashem's help it will be elucidated further there.
