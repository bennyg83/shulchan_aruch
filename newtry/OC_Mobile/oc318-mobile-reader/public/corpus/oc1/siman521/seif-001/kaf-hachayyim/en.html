1) [Se'if 1] Produce may be lowered through a skylight, etc. This refers to one who has wheat and barley spread upon his roof to dry and sees rain approaching. The Sages permitted him to exert himself and throw the produce through a skylight in the roof so that it falls to the ground, because this does not entail excessive exertion. Rashi, Beitzah 35b; Beis Yosef; Levush; Taz, subsection 1. This applies to produce fit to be eaten that day, even though he does not need it. Regarding something unfit for eating, even if it is not muktzeh, such as dry wood on the roof upon which rain is falling, it appears that one may not lower it through a skylight. Eshel Avraham, siman 495, subsection 1. The later authorities cite him. Nevertheless, if he needs it that day and cannot obtain other wood, it appears that this is permitted.
2) There. Produce may be lowered, etc. This is permitted specifically when rain is falling upon it, because permission was granted to prevent a loss. Otherwise, it resembles clearing a storehouse and is forbidden unless done for a mitzvah, as written in siman 333. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. Rambam's unqualified wording, however, implies that it is permitted for any need. It is possible that he relied upon the laws of Shabbos, where he permits it only for Torah study or guests, and the same applies to preventing a loss. Bigdei Yesha. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1, rules in accordance with Magen Avraham that it is permitted only to prevent a loss. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1, rule likewise.
3) There. Produce may be lowered, etc. It may be lowered by a rope. Tur; Levush; Taz, subsection 1. Sefer HaBatim, cited at the beginning of Maaseh Rokeach, page 4d, however, writes that Rambam's words appear to forbid lowering it by a rope through a skylight. See there. Erech HaShulchan, subsection 2, cites this. Chemed Moshe, subsection 2, however, rules according to Tur that it is permitted even with a rope. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1, likewise writes that one may either throw the produce through the skylight or lower it by a rope. Mikraei Kodesh, siman 17, subsection 13; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1, write likewise.
