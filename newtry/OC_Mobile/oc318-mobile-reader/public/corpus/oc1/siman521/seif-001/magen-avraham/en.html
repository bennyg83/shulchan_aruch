One may lower [produce]. From the Gemara it is implied specifically when rain is falling upon them, and because of the loss it is permitted; and so wrote the Tur, for if not so, it would be like one who clears a storehouse; see siman 333. And the Yam Shel Shlomo wrote that even a large amount, and even finishing the storehouse, is permitted; and such is the view of the Rif and the Rambam, since they left their words unqualified; and so appears to be the view of the Shulchan Aruch, not as written in the Beis Yosef.
