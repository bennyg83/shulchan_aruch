(1) [Seif 1] One may lower fruits through a skylight, etc. — meaning: one who has wheat and barley spread on his roof to dry and sees rain approaching — they permitted him to exert himself and throw them through the skylight on the roof and they fall to the ground, for there is no extra exertion. Rashi Beitzah 35b. Beit Yosef. Levush. Taz s.k. 1. And this is regarding fruits fit for human food that day, even if he does not need them that day they permitted; but something not fit for food, even if not muktzeh, such as dry wood on the roof and rain falls on them — one could say it is forbidden to lower through a skylight.
