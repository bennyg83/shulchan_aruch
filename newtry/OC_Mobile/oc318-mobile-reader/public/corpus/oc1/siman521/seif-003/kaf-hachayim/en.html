(3) There. One may lower fruits, etc. — that he lower them below with a rope. Tur. Levush. Taz s.k. 1. However, in Sefer HaBatim at the beginning of Ma'aseh Rokeach daf 4a he wrote that it appears from Rambam's words that it is forbidden to lower with a rope through a skylight — see there and the later authorities. Aruch HaShulchan note 2. However, Chacham Tzvi note 2 ruled like Tur's words that even with a rope it is permitted. And likewise R' Zalman note 1 that whether to throw the fruits through a skylight or lower them with a rope it is permitted. And likewise Mekor Chaim siman 17 note 13. Mishna Berurah note 1:
