2) There. Produce may be lowered, etc. This is permitted specifically when rain is falling upon it, because permission was granted to prevent a loss. Otherwise, it resembles clearing a storehouse and is forbidden unless done for a mitzvah, as written in siman 333. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. Rambam's unqualified wording, however, implies that it is permitted for any need. It is possible that he relied upon the laws of Shabbos, where he permits it only for Torah study or guests, and the same applies to preventing a loss. Bigdei Yesha. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1, rules in accordance with Magen Avraham that it is permitted only to prevent a loss. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1, rule likewise.
