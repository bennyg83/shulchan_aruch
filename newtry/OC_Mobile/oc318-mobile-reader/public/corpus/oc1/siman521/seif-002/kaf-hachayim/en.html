One may lower fruits, etc. — and specifically when rain is falling on them and because of loss it is permitted, for otherwise it would be like emptying a storehouse which is forbidden unless for a mitzvah matter as written in siman 533. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. However, from Rambam's wording who states it stam implies that for any need it is permitted; and it is possible he relied on the laws of Shabbat where he permits there only for Torah study need or for guests' need, and the same applies because of loss. Bigdei Yesha. And likewise Machatzit HaShekel note 1 per Magen Avraham's words that it is permitted only because of loss. And likewise R' Zalman note 1 or 1 Mishna Berurah note 1:
