[5] Bent, etc. Nowadays, when we do not have a true public domain, no correction is needed at the bend, as stated by Mahariv. The Taz asks that a bent alleyway has the law of an alleyway open through at both ends, and such an alleyway is prohibited even when opening only to a karmelit, as stated at the beginning of this siman. It appears to me that a truly open-ended alleyway is worse. Proof is that Shmuel on page 6 says a bent alleyway is treated as closed. See siman 363, seif katan 15.
