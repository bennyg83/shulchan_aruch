A bent alleyway, etc. Rashi explained: its two openings to a public domain — implying that if open to a karmelit its law is not so, but rather as closed at its bend; and for this reason he cited in Sefer Mishna Berurah siman 92 in the name of Mahariv that nowadays, since we have no public domain, one does not require an arrangement at its bend; and there he disagreed with Mahariv and said: where there are sixteen amot, we too have a public domain; and I already wrote in siman 345 that we do not heed this, but rely on most poskim that there is no public domain unless 600,000 pass through it. However I wonder: behold we hold regarding a bent alleyway that its law is like a pass-through alleyway, and in a pass-through alleyway there is no distinction between to a public domain and to a karmelit, as this siman wrote; if so, also for us in a bent alleyway one requires an arrangement as in a pass-through alleyway; and wherever they make an eruv in alleyways, one also requires an arrangement at the bend, as above in the essential law.
