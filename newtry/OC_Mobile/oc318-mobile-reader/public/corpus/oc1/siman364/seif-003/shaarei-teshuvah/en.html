Pass-through — Avraham ben HaRav Tzvi; and see in responsa Bach Chadashot siman 3, responsum of the Gaon R' Yehoshua, that his view is that arrangement is required; and there he wrote that R' Zalman Schor wrote that arrangement is not required; and likewise the view of his brother, author of Torat Chayim, in his chiddushim on Eruvin; and see in Kaf HaChayyim there support for those who say arrangement is not required for us — the matter depends on reasoning, see there; and likewise Magen Avraham siman 363, see there.
