A bent alleyway. Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: even though each of these alleyway residents has permission to tread on his fellow's [property] and they cannot stop one another, nevertheless if these made an eruv for themselves and those for themselves, they are permitted, for each has an opening to a public domain; and since they made an eruv for themselves, their intent is revealed that they withdraw from treading on one another; and such a case — we compel according to the measure of Sodom that they not pass over one another on Shabbos, as written siman 386 — end of his words. And according to what is written in siman 378, a permitted foot in its place does not forbid when not in its place — they do not forbid one another even if they pass there on Shabbos — end of his words, Beit Yosef; and it appears to me that Rabbeinu Yonah wrote well, for we hold in siman 392 seif 6 that an alleyway is different, for specifically a courtyard — since the essence of a courtyard belongs to the outer residents — the inner one does not forbid it even though one passes through there; which is not so in a pass-through alleyway, where for these it is like for those — we require that they remove themselves from there; and likewise Tosafos daf 99, see there, and in Rashi; and it requires study on Rabbeinu Yerucham who ruled there that even withdrawal does not help, and here he wrote thus; and see siman 363 seif 31 that a doorframe shape clearly helps.
