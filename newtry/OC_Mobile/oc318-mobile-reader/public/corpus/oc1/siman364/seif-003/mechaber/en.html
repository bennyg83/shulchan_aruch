A bent alleyway shaped like the letter dalet is treated as if it were a pass-through alleyway at the bend, and requires a doorframe shape at the bend. At each of its two ends it needs either a lechi, beam, or doorframe shape — and a lechi or beam at the bend. If it is shaped like the letter chet, it requires a doorframe shape at each of its two bends, and a lechi or beam at each of its two ends.
