(s.k. 3) An alleyway, etc. If they made an eruv, etc., they are permitted — meaning, they do not forbid one another, as written siman 386 seif 9: a courtyard open to two alleyways. If one was accustomed with one and partnered with one of them, the other is permitted, for behold he removed himself from it. And if the alleyway to which he was accustomed — they shared among themselves, and that one to which he was not accustomed they did not share, and he is not a partner neither with this one nor with that one — they reject him toward the one he is not accustomed to, and he withdraws from the one he is accustomed to so that he be permitted, since he loses nothing, etc., and there is benefit for others, etc.; they compel him according to the measure of Sodom.

And according to what is written in siman 378, etc. — meaning, according to Rabbeinu Yonah, there is no permission except when they do not pass over one another on Shabbos, for then they reject him; but according to Rabbeinu Yerucham, they forbid; but Rabbeinu Yerucham's view is that they do not forbid even if they pass over one another.

Permitted in its place — meaning, two courtyards one before the other, and the inner one has foot traffic on the outer one: if the outer one alone made an eruv and the inner one did not make an eruv with it and also not for itself, behold the inner one is forbidden in its place — meaning in the inner one — then it forbids also the outer one; but if the inner one also made an eruv for itself, or a single person lives in the inner one who does not need an eruv, since it is permitted in its place — meaning in the inner one — even though it did not make an eruv with the outer one, it does not forbid the outer one even if it passes through the outer one also on Shabbos; for we hold in siman 392, etc., that one may not divide a city that has many alleyways to make an eruv for each alleyway alone, even though each has its own way to a public domain — for they forbid one another even though they are permitted in their place — until they withdraw, meaning until they make between one alleyway and its fellow a lechi or beam.

For even withdrawal does not help — meaning, a lechi if not a beam.

That a doorframe shape clearly helps — meaning, when wider than ten; but at ten, two posts suffice, or one post four amot wide; as explained below siman 392, and above siman 363 seif 31.
