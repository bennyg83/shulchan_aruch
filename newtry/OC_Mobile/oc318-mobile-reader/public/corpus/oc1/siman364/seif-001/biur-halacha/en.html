Pass-through alleyway, etc. — see in Mishna Berurah that it deals with one that was not the width of a public domain, meaning less than sixteen amot, as most poskim hold; and that which helps on the other side with a lechi deals with one who makes a wide lechi so that the airspace is reduced to less than ten amot [Tosafos].

And its other side to a courtyard, etc. — see Mishna Berurah; and nevertheless, when it is pass-through on one side to a karpef larger than beit se'atayim that was not enclosed for residence, its law is as less than a karmelit [Peri Megadim].

That is not combined — see Taz who wrote that in a combined courtyard one requires no arrangement at all on the courtyard side [meaning even if they did not participate with the alleyway residents; for when they did participate, even when the courtyard is not combined one does not need even a lechi]; and in Biur HaGra he proved from siman 363 seif 31 to the contrary — that specifically when it is not combined, since they cannot carry by themselves due to lack of eruv, they cannot forbid the alleyway residents when they made a lechi; but if it is combined and they are permitted to carry by themselves, they forbid one another until they participate together with the alleyway residents, and then they make a courtyard arrangement as required — meaning two posts from both sides or one post four amot wide; and likewise on the side — also in Peri Megadim, see there.

One needs only a lechi or beam, etc. — see in the article of Mordechai who wrote that from a responsum of Ran brought in Beit Yosef it appears that he disagrees with this law.
