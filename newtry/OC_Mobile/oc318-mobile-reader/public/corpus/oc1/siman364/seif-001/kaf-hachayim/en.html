(1) [Seif 1] A pass-through alleyway, etc. — and he explained: pass-through to a public domain from here and from here, when it is not sixteen amot wide; but if sixteen amot wide, this is a complete public domain. Beit Yosef in the name of Maggid Mishneh chapter 17 — meaning, that which a pass-through alleyway to a public domain is permitted with a doorframe shape from here and a lechi or beam from here — when it is not sixteen wide; for if sixteen wide, doors are required, as written in the following seif; and likewise Tur — a public domain itself, meaning sixteen amot wide, pass-through from gate to gate, and 600,000 pass through it, is permitted only with doors — end of his words; however, we already wrote above siman 345 seif 7 that according to Shulchan Aruch, even if 600,000 do not pass through it, see there; except that in the name of R' Yitzchak, Tur wrote that an alleyway thirteen amot and a third wide whose two heads are pass-through to a public domain sixteen amot wide — its law is like a public domain and requires doors — end of his words; and likewise Eliyahu Rabba, part 1, and Tosafos Shabbos 1, 2; and we already wrote on this dispute above siman 345 seifim 8 and 9, see there; and therefore l'chatchila one should be concerned for the view of those who forbid, and one should not permit an alleyway thirteen amot and a third wide pass-through to a public domain except with doors, as written in the following seif; and in a time of need, whatever is not sixteen amot wide can be permitted with a doorframe shape; and that which they are accustomed to permit with a doorframe shape even for an alleyway wider than sixteen amot — they rely on Tur's view and those who agree with him, who hold it is not a public domain until it is sixteen amot wide and 600,000 pass through it every day; and anything less than this is not a public domain and is permitted with a doorframe shape, as explained above siman 345 seif 7; but according to the plain Shulchan Aruch there, that whatever is sixteen amot wide, even if 600,000 do not pass through it, is a public domain — it is not permitted with a doorframe shape but only with doors, as written in the following seif; and therefore, in a place where they are accustomed to permit with a doorframe shape even for an alleyway wider than sixteen amot because 600,000 do not pass through it, one need not protest, for many great ones hold thus, as explained there; and only every God-fearing person should be stringent for himself.
