(s.k. 1) Or a beam, etc. Bach wrote, etc., it appears closed, etc. — meaning, the Sages did not permit through a lechi or beam except for an alleyway closed on three sides; and therefore in an alleyway pass-through on both sides, they required on one side a doorframe shape, and through this it is called closed on that side and is closed on three sides, and a lechi or beam suffices on the fourth side; except for pass-through to a courtyard that is not combined, where they were lenient and we require only a light closing — meaning a lechi, for we hold it is because of partition, unlike a beam, for we hold it is because of recognition, as above siman 363 seif 11 and seif 15; and see there in Magen Avraham.

Likewise in glosses of Semak who wrote, and these are his words: it suffices with a lechi from that side of the inner area, and it is as closed from that side through the lechi; and on the other side, toward the public domain, a lechi or beam suffices — end of his words.
