Seif 1 — or one side or two sides, etc. There 7a.

But if, etc. There: if the alleyway ended in a wide area, etc.; and there 8a, when they had not made an eruv they disagree regarding nireh, etc.; from which we learn that even without an eruv one needs only a lechi. And even though one could say that there two lechayim and a courtyard arrangement were already explained above siman 363 seif 31 — to separate the dwellings so they do not forbid one another — and the outer residents did not make a lechi at the head of the alleyway, one needs only a lechi or beam; and Rosh wrote that the outer residents cannot carry and therefore do not forbid the inner ones; and the same law applies here. This is what he means by a courtyard that is not combined in an eruv. But for a combined courtyard that did not participate in the alleyway, one requires a courtyard arrangement on the courtyard side, as he wrote there.
