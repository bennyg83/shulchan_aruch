One requires a doorframe shape from here. So that it become as closed in the partition from that side and be like an alleyway closed on three sides, and it suffices on the other side with a lechi or beam; but if one side is open to a courtyard that is not combined in an eruv, that side is as closed without arrangement, and the arrangement is only because of recognition d'rabbanan — therefore a beam suffices also from here and from here, and all the more so a lechi, which is because of partition; and if one side is open to a combined courtyard, that side requires no arrangement at all, for it is like an alleyway closed on three sides literally, even d'rabbanan — only the other side as above; and in vain did Maharach wonder at this law that a beam suffices on the courtyard side.
