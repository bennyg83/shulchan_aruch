Laws of a Pass-Through Alleyway and One Shaped Like the Letter Nun — 5 Sections A pass-through alleyway open at both ends to a public domain, or with one side opening to a public domain and the other to a karmelitrequires a doorframe shape on one side and a lechi or beam on the other. But if one side opens to a public domain and the other to an uneruvated courtyard, only a lechi or beam is needed at each of the two ends. {Rama: — or with both ends opening to a karmelit All of this is subject to the conditions explained above in Siman 363, section 27, regarding the laws of an alleyway. But if it is entirely within a courtyard, it requires the courtyard arrangement on both sides.}
