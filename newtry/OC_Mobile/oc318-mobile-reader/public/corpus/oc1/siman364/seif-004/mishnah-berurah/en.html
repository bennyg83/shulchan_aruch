(14) That open into one another — meaning: even though the law of a bent alleyway is with two alleyways whose heads open to a public domain specifically, and here there are many small alleyways entering one into another without an opening to a public domain — nevertheless, since the end of the last alleyway opens to a public domain, and likewise the beginning of the first alleyway, all are considered as pass-through and require arrangement at each bend of each one.
