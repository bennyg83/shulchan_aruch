An alleyway shaped like the letter nun meaning a large alleyway into which many small alleyways open, with the other ends of the small ones opening to the public domain — even if the openings of the small alleyways on each side do not directly face one another: one makes a doorframe shape for each small alleyway at the point' where it opens into the large alleyway, and a lechi or beam at its other end which opens to the public domain. As for the large alleyway itself: if it is a pass-through, one makes a doorframe shape on one side and a lechi or beam on the other; if it is closed at one end, one makes a lechi or beam at its open end.
