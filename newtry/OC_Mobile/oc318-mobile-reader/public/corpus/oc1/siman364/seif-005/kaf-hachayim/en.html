(5) There. To a courtyard that is not combined, etc. — for that side is as closed without arrangement, and the arrangement is only because of recognition d'rabbanan; and therefore a beam suffices from here and a beam from here, and all the more so a lechi, which is because of partition — Taz there. And all this is when the courtyard is not accustomed to this alleyway — such as when it has an opening to another alleyway; but if it is accustomed to it, even a doorframe shape and doors do not help to permit there unless this alleyway participated with the other courtyards open to it; and if it participated in it, one need not make an eruv at all, as written in siman 387, part 1; and see our words there, with God's help.
