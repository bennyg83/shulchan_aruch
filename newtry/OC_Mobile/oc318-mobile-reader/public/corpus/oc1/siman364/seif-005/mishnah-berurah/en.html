(15) Shaped like a nun — it is a creeping creature that has many legs on both its sides that are not aligned one opposite the other; so too this alleyway, that many small alleyways open into it from both sides, not aligned one opposite the other.
(16) And the other ends — it should read: and their other end; meaning, that each of the small alleyways, whose one end opens to the large alleyway, has its other end open to a public domain; and the same applies to a karmelit.
(17) Even if the openings are not, etc. — from the Mechaber's wording it implies that even when the openings of the small alleyways are aligned one opposite the other, one also requires arrangement where they open into the large alleyway; and one should not consider it as one long pass-through alleyway in a straight line, for nevertheless each one is like a bent alleyway with the large one.
(18) One makes for each, etc. — meaning, for all the small alleyways on this side, and likewise on the other side; and the same if there are no small alleyways except on one side of the large alleyway — this arrangement is also required.
(19) At the place of its opening, etc. — he follows his reasoning that he ruled above that a bent alleyway requires arrangement at its bend; and here each of the small alleyways is included in the category of a bent alleyway, since one enters from it into the large alleyway and from it goes out to a public domain.
