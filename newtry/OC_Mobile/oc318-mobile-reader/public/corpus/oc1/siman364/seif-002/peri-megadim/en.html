An alleyway — Taz and Eliyah Rabbah letter 5: one can say a bent alleyway is lighter than a complete pass-through, for Shmuel is lenient — Eruvin 6a — and said its law is like closed; if so, one can say for us it is lighter, and in a karmelit one does not require arrangement in a bent alleyway; and one can say: if so, also at its two heads it would suffice with a lechi and beam, lighter than a complete pass-through; and open to a karmelit let us say thus; and we hold that one requires arrangement — a doorframe shape at one of its heads — the same in a bent alleyway.

One requires arrangement. And in Magen Avraham 363 letter kaf-zayin — in a walled city it is obvious one does not require; and even without a wall, our alleyways have the law of a courtyard and one does not require arrangement in the bend, only at its head; and it is found that a courtyard is lighter than an alleyway, for one requires no arrangement in the bend at all; see there, and Tosafos Shabbos here letter 8.
