A pass-through alleyway, etc. In responsum of Rashba part 3, printed siman 401, he was asked if there is in an alleyway an arch called 'volta' whether the law of the arch is like a doorframe shape; and he answered that if it has ten [amot] in its base, according to all it has a doorframe shape to obligate a mezuzah and to permit an alleyway by reason of doorframe shape. Mechaber part 1.
