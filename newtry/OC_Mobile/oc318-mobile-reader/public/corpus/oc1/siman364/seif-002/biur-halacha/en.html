And they are locked at night — and if they were not locked, and all the more so a doorframe shape alone, one is liable for a chatas according to this view, for the many come and nullify the partition; and this is the view of Rif, Rosh, Semag, and other poskim who brought R' Yochanan's words as halacha. The second view is Rambam's view; and according to Rambam, even if there were no doors at all but only a doorframe shape, one is not liable for a chatas, for he ruled like R' Elazar that the many do not come and nullify the partition — thus wrote the Acharonim; and nevertheless, rabbinically a door is required even according to him.

And after he made for it, etc. — behold it is explained in this seif that a public domain requires doors according to all, and per the first view one must lock them, and if not it is forbidden to carry d'oraisa; and per the second view it suffices that they be fit to lock; and if so, it is difficult regarding the custom of the world who rely on a doorframe shape in a public domain that is seventeen amot wide and pass-through from gate to gate, and rely on the view that for us there is no public domain; it is also very difficult, for in truth most Rishonim hold that even for us there is a public domain — see in Mishkenot Yaakov who elaborated on this; and if so it is difficult to rely on this regarding a Torah prohibition of stoning. The correct approach is to rely on Rambam's view, who ruled like R' Elazar that the many do not come and nullify the partition; and according to him certainly from the Torah a doorframe shape suffices, and we are left only with a rabbinic prohibition, for R' Elazar too [requires doors] without doors, as is evident from Rambam; and with this one can rely on the view that there is no public domain unless there are 600,000, and it is only a karmelit, and therefore a doorframe shape suffices; and nevertheless it is not the proper way, for most poskim cited R' Yochanan that without locked doors there is chatas liability; and also this leniency that there is no public domain without 600,000 — many Rishonim disagree on it; and therefore, although one need not protest against the world who are accustomed to be lenient, nevertheless a ba'al nefesh should be stringent for himself, for there is here a trace of chatas liability; and likewise in Sefer Beit Me'ir, see there.
