2) There. An alleyway open at both ends, etc. Rashba was asked in newly printed responsa, volume 3, siman 271, whether an alleyway containing an arch called a volta has the law of a tzuras hapesach. He answered that if the vertical sides of the arch are ten handbreadths high, all opinions agree that it constitutes a tzuras hapesach, both to obligate affixing a mezuzah and to render the alleyway permitted by virtue of a tzuras hapesach.
