(5) A public domain itself — meaning streets and markets sixteen amot wide and pass-through from gate to gate; and see there seif 8 and Taz, that there are other types of public domain.

(6) Only with doors — from here and from here; through this it becomes like a courtyard of the many; but a doorframe shape does not help [even if one makes it on both sides]; and some poskim hold that it suffices with a door on one side when locked at night, for this is not a public domain since it prevents the many from passing from gate to gate, and it suffices on the other side with a lechi or beam, like generally where there are three complete partitions.

(7) That they are locked at night — for through this the name of public domain is nullified from it, since it is not similar to the banners of the desert that were open at all times.

(8) And some say, etc. — since the Mechaber cited the first view anonymously and the second view in the name of 'some say,' it implies that his view is like the first view. And behold from the words of Shulchan Aruch it is proven that one does not make an eruv in a public domain except with doors. And in our towns, where the custom of the world is to arrange through a doorframe shape even though their streets are very wide and pass-through from gate to gate, and also often the king's road passes through the city and by law it is a complete public domain through this, as above in siman 345 — therefore they rely on the 'some say' in siman 345 that a public domain does not exist unless 600,000 pass through it, and this is not common [Peri Megadim]; nevertheless, in truth many Rishonim disagree with that 'some say,' as we wrote there in siman 345; and therefore, although one need not protest against others who are accustomed to be lenient through a doorframe shape, as they have always acted according to the poskim who are lenient in this, nevertheless every ba'al nefesh should be stringent for himself not to carry through a doorframe shape alone; and see in Biur Halacha.
