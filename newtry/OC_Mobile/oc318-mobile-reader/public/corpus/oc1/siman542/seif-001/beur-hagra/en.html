Se'if 1: And davar ha'aved, etc. Just as they permitted for the householder because of the laborer, so too for the laborer because of the householder; and so it appears at the beginning of chapter 2, "These are the things that one does," etc.; and it implies even for wages; see there; and all the more so on Chol HaMoed, which is lighter, as is written there.
Se'if 2: Any melacha, etc. From what is written there: "and if it should enter your mind [regarding] wages," etc.: not like the Ramah. Nimukei Yosef.
