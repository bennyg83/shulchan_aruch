(2) And know that in the explanation of Avi Nigeri there are three interpretations: (1) Rashi z"l that they were Israelite workers, table-arrangers etc. as stated. But there is a second explanation that they were gentile workers who ruled over workers to do the work fast and well and assist them somewhat, and the explanation Avi Nigeri from 'av' and 'patron' that they were heads of the workers; and the labor was not for festival needs but only gentile workers in detached or attached tenancy — even on Shabbat permitted — and Rav Chama permitted heads of craftsmen who were in the house of the Exilarch to assist gentile workers, since they did not take wages for this in the Exilarch's house but ate at his table — we have no concern; and the explanation helpers who speed profit in work done quickly — and one should not rely on this statement alone; it was stated about contractors and helping in a winepress — see there; and per Biur Yosef and Magen Avraham 543 letter 3 meant this — see there. And there is a third explanation: Avi Nigeri etc. and the workers were all Israelites, and for festival needs the labor was only that other workers did for wages because they had nothing to eat — permitted as in seif 2; and Avi Nigeri etc. assist the workers, and helpers profit those workers who took more wages for this — nevertheless permitted, for one working gratuitously and workers permitted for wages so they eat on the festival — and this is Darkei Moshe's words on this — see there, for seemingly it has no explanation and he omitted here — possibly it is simple, understand this:
