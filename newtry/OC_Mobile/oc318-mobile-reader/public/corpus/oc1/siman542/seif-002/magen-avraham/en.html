Who has nothing to eat. This implies that if he has something to eat, even bread and water, it is forbidden. And so it appears in Beis Yosef, siman 545, who wrote in the name of Nimukei Yosef that if he has bread and water it is permitted to write tefillin; this implies that other melachos are forbidden. And so it appears in the Rosh; and see there in the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 3, what he wrote in the name of the Maggid Mishneh. And as for what is written in siman 539 in Beis Yosef, that according to the Rambam the case is where he has something to eat and wants to sell in order to profit more generously: that is specifically regarding commerce; but regarding melacha, he wrote explicitly in siman 545 that he has nothing at all to eat.
