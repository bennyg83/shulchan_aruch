To eat. See Baer Heitev; and see in Noda BiYehuda, Mahadura Tinyana, siman 104, regarding one who gives a garment to a tailor to sew: if besides this garment he has other garments, it is obvious that it is forbidden; but even if it is a garment needed for the festival, it is permitted only as amateur work, and it is permitted even for wages to sew with "dog's teeth" stitches; but if it is not urgent for the festival, or to sew in the usual manner, it is forbidden even for free, and all the more so for wages, unless through a poor laborer who has nothing to eat; see there.
