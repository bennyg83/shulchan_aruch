(6) To a gentile — even if he is a merchant, for he is also suspected of bloodshed and illicit relations. That which we said above in seif 1 — the presumption that he acquired it from a Jew, etc. — means that a Jew transgressed and sold it to him.

(7) “Lest he accompany him” — and there is another reason in the Gemara: because of a prostitute. Rashi explained that he give it to a prostitute as her fee and she will say Jews gave it to her; if so, all the more it is forbidden to sell to a gentile woman for this reason. See the Chayei Adam, who wrote that for the reason “lest he accompany him” there is no concern now, since they are not suspected of bloodshed; but for the reason of a prostitute there is concern.

(8) “Temporarily” — but if he removed the tzitzis, even though the holes remain, it is permitted even to sell the tallis to a gentile.
