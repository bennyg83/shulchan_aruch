(Section 2) They do not sell — the Bach wrote, etc.; and Rashi, etc.: on the plain meaning of the baraita one could say as the Bach wrote — one who buys from a merchant is valid even though they said one may not sell to a gentile; if so, apparently the merchant would be lying. One could say that specifically for one who is not a merchant they may not sell, but to a merchant it is permitted to sell, for he buys it certainly to resell and not to keep for himself — and there is no concern lest he kill him, nor because of a prostitute, as the Magen Avraham wrote in the following section. But from Rashi it is proven that Rashi does not hold like the Bach.
