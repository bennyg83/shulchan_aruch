{Rama: In Magen Avraham, se'if kaf alef} we say in Avodah Zarah. See note: meaning that Rav should have listed white tzitzit thread and also tekhelet for dyeing with two types of pieces, as where he listed two types of pieces; but for Shmuel there in Avodah Zarah, who also listed tekhelet—for his view there is no difficulty, even though he requires spinning for its sake, nevertheless he did not list two types of pieces, nor should he have listed two types of tzitzit; and it suffices with what he listed, tekhelet alone—and understand well, as above, to explain the Magen Avraham's words in se'if kaf alef.
