Laws of buying and selling a tallis — it contains two clauses.
One who buys a tallis with tzitzis from a Jew or from a gentile merchant — [if he says he acquired it from a Jew he is believed — Noda BiYehudah Hilchos Tzitzis] — it is valid, since he is a merchant we presume he acquired it from a Jew and does not harm himself; but if one bought from a gentile who is not a merchant it is invalid.
