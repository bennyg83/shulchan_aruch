One who buys a tallit, etc., from a Gentile merchant, etc.—See in Chayei Adam, klal 11, who wrote; and it seems to me that the buyer unties the tzitzit and ties them anew, and I do not know any reason for this. And if it refers to one who is not a merchant, what would it help if he unties the tzitzit—perhaps they were not spun for their sake? It goes without saying for the Magen Avraham, who is stringent regarding buying tzitzit from a non-Jew; even per the Taz one should be stringent here, for it is possible that because he wants many buyers to purchase the tallit, therefore he spun the tzitzit himself and attached them to the garment. And it would not have been fitting for Chayei Adam to conceal this but to explain; and it seems there is a scribal error in Chayei Adam, and this "it seems to me" must be after what he wrote at the beginning regarding stolen tallitot—see there.
