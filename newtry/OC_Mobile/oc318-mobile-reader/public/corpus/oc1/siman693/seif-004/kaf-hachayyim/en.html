4) Ibid. He says, 'But You are holy.' Kol Bo, siman 45, wrote the reason: The obligation to read the Megillah by day and by night is derived from the verse, 'I call by day, but You do not answer; and at night, but there is no silence for me.' That verse appears in the chapter of Tehillim, 'My God, my God, why have You forsaken me,' which was said regarding Esther. Therefore, the custom is to begin after the reading with the verse, 'But You are holy,' which is in that same chapter of Tehillim. Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, os 2.
