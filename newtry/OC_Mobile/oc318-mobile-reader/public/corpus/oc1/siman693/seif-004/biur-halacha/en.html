They take out a Torah scroll etc. — Tosafot Megillah (daf 4) wrote that one does not return the Torah scroll to its place but sits holding it until they finish reading the megillah; and in Levush it implies he returns the Torah scroll to its place and then reads the megillah; and likewise in Darkhei Moshe; afterward I found in Beit Meir who wrote: most poskim omitted Tosafot's words — implying they do not hold thus; and it appears the reason is that the holder too must intend to fulfill hearing megillah reading as stated siman 690 s.k. 14; and if he holds a Torah scroll his heart should be on the Torah scroll as stated siman 96 regarding prayer — end of his words.
