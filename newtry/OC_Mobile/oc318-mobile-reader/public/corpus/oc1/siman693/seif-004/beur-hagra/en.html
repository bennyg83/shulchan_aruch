Seif 4: And even though there is no. Yerushalmi, chapter 4, halachah 2, and the Rif brought it: Regarding Parashas Amalek, he said to him, it is different, for it is the order of the day. This is as written regarding the haftarah in the Gemara, 23b, Tosafos and the Rosh.
And the custom is not. As it appears to me above.
When there is a bris, and so on. As written there, Taz seif katan 2, “and joy,” this refers to a bris, and so on. This requires analysis, for even the public Avodah we delay, though its time is in the morning; all the more so a bris, whose priority is only because the zealous act early, and so on. So too wrote the Pri Chadash.
