(ד) There — one says VeAtah Kadosh. The reason — Kol Bo wrote siman 45: because megillah reading is by day and night, a verse emerged as written Ekra yomam velo ta'aneh velailah velo dumiah li; and this verse is in the psalm Eli Eli lamah azavtani said about Esther; therefore they are accustomed to open after reading with VeAtah Kadosh which is in that psalm. Shulchan Gavoah letter 2.
