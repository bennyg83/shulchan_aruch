[3] Levush: Rather, half Kaddish, and so on. In truth, this is how it appears in the Kol Bo and Avudraham. But Tosafos in Megillah, page 4, the Rokeach, Hagahos Maimoniyos, and Responsa Maharil, siman 52, wrote that a full Kaddish is said, and so ruled the Malbushei Yom Tov. I later saw in Sefer Amarkal that he brings two opinions, and that customs vary by place. Therefore, since the custom in these places is to say a full Kaddish, Heaven forbid changing it.
