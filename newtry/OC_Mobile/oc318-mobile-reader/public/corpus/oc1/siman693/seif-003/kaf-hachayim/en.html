(3) There. They say "Ata Kadosh." Rav Amram wrote: on Purim night after reading the Megillah one says "Uva leTzion," and some say one does not say it because the essence of redemption is not at night; rather they begin with "Ata Kadosh" — Tur. And this is what is written here in Shulchan Aruch: one says "Ata Kadosh," meaning but not "Uva leTzion" as some say the Tur wrote. So too Shulchan Gavoah letter 1 in the name of several poskim that one does not say it, and wrote that such is the custom — not to say it except by day.
