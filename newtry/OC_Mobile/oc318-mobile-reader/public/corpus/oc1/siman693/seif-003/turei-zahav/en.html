But not on the fifteenth. So wrote the Beis Yosef in the name of Orchos Chayim. He wrote about this: I do not know what prohibition there is in mentioning Al HaNissim. I wondered at this, for certainly, since it does not belong to that day, it is not relevant to the event and is an interruption in davening. So too wrote my father-in-law, of blessed memory, but he went too far by saying that even after the fact, if he said it, he repeats, because he changed the formula coined by Chazal. This is not so, for it is stated in siman 108, seif 12, that one who errs and mentions an event of other days in davening outside its time has not made an interruption. Even according to what we distinguished there between Havdalah and other matters, nevertheless here there is some connection to these days. Therefore, certainly he need not repeat if he said it. So it appears to my humble opinion, clearly.
