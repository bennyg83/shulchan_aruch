(Seif 3 in Shulchan Aruch) one does not read Hallel in it. Eliyah Rabbah wrote in name of Meiri: for this reason they said in Berachot because its reading is hallelah — if he has no megillah to read he should say Hallel etc.; and Eliyah Rabbah wrote: since Rava said the reason is we are still servants of Achashverosh etc. — he should not say Hallel at all; and halacha is like Rava who is later; and also per Rif, Rosh, and Ran Rava challenges etc. — implies halacha is like him since they did not answer him; therefore poskim ruled he never says Hallel — see there; but in Mishna Berurah he brought Beit Yosef and Rambam also wrote reading is hallelah as Meiri implies and gives R' Nachman's reason etc. — see there; and per this the law returns: one without megillah says Hallel; but in my view the Talmud's main intent is the core enactment; but after they did not enact Hallel on Purim because reading is hallelah — automatically no place to read Hallel what Chazal did not enact for that day; and in my view nevertheless when he has no megillah he may read Hallel without blessing before and after like reading Tehillim — and this is fine; and even if he has a chumash and reads megillah for himself — one who wants also to read Hallel without blessing did not lose; possible saying Hallel they did not enact is better with some kedusha like a Torah scroll unlike megillah reading whose core enactment is to be written properly — therefore he fulfills both.
