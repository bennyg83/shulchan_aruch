(א) You are holy. Saying an entire devotee will not be accepted after reading the scroll. See the name:

Vayehi Noam. After reading the megillah — Minhagim; and likewise Mahari Abuhav who wrote that they say Vayehi Noam — to exclude Kol Bo who ruled one does not say it at all; and what he wrote "and they read the megillah" refers after it, and afterward they make havdalah; and see in Shoneh Halachot in Hagahot Beit Yosef letter 4; and see in responsum Devar Shmuel siman 128.
