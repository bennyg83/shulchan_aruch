One may ask: since he cast a pur and it fell in the month of Adar, why did he wait until the thirteenth of it, and not write in the letters that they should be ready on Rosh Chodesh Adar, or on the seventh of it, when Moshe died? One can answer that since the strength of mourning is on the seventh day, he therefore said on the thirteenth of Adar, which is the seventh day after Moshe's death. This is the language of the Avudraham. In Sefer Amarkal I found that Haman thought: In Adar there were three days of darkness, when the wicked of Yisrael died; if so, I can destroy them. But he did not consider that “for all Bnei Yisrael there was light in their dwellings.” Therefore this caused that “for the Jews there was light,” and so on.
