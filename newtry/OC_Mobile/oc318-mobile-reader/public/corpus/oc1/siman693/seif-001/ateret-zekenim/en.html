After reading the megillah etc. — Maharil wrote: after they prayed Maariv they wait until night and read the megillah; and see above siman 687 whether one may read before night — Minhagim wrote; and one does not say Lamenatzeach and not E-lo E-lo in the two days of Purim; and likewise in the name of Tosafot that one does not say Lamenatzeach; Kol Bo wrote; and one does not say Mizmor L'todah. We are not accustomed thus and say Mizmor L'todah. Our custom is to read the megillah and Asher He'enia and say Vayehi Noam and make havdalah and say V'titen Lecha — and so in Minhagim.
