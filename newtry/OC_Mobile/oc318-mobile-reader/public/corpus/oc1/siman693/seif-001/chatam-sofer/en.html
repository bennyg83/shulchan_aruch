In Magen Avraham beginning of the siman — view of Minhagim. NB it requires study: above siman 500 s.k. 59 he wrote Rama in name of Minhagim: all Kaddishim said after Eichah do not say Titkabel; and if you would say after megillah of Eichah they do not say Titkabel — then there is no Kaddish here that should have Titkabel except Shacharit, since that of Maariv they already said before Eichah; and what does "all the Kaddishim" mean — rather certainly after megillah of Eichah one should say Titkabel, if not because of Tisha B'Av "You have heard my prayer" — and so on Purim it would be fit to say it after megillah reading; but in truth we have only custom; and so fit to attach Titkabel to Maariv prayer as much as possible; but Magen Avraham's words always require study. Again I found in Hagahot Asheri end of Masechet Taanit like our custom — see there; requires study.
