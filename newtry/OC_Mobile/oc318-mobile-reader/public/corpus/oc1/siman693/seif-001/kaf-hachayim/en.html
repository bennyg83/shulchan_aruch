(1) [Seif 1] After reading the Megillah and after the prayer, the shaliach tzibur says Kaddish until "le'eilah" and omits "titkabeil," because Kaddish belongs after the Megillah reading and then one says "titkabeil" (Reda; Kolbo 45; Levush; Taz s.k. 1). Minhagim records full Kaddish after Mincha before the Megillah (Maharil responsum 56/Rokeach; Tosafot 4a; Hagahot Maimoniyot). Be'er Heitev and Magen Avraham note that where the custom is full Kaddish before the Megillah, after the reading they say full Kaddish without "titkabeil." Eshel Avraham (letter 5) adds full Kaddish with "titkabeil" afterward so it applies to the prayer in kedushah. Some places follow Magen Avraham for both Mincha and Megillah (Bigdei Yeshua). Where customs differ, each community follows its own practice.
