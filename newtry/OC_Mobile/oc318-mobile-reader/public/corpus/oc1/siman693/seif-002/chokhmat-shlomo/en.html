Seif 2. In the gloss: “And we say Al HaNissim only on the fourteenth day, but not on the fifteenth,” and so on. Note: see the Taz, seif katan 3, and see proof for this in Rashi, chapter 2 of Shabbos, s.v. “we do not mention,” where he explains that they did not obligate mentioning it; and afterward, s.v. “he does not mention,” he explains that he does not need to mention it. From this it implies that even according to the view that it was not instituted to mention it in Birkas HaMazon, nevertheless if he did mention it, it is not an interruption, and he is also permitted to mention it initially. It is proven that whenever there is some connection to the event of these days, this is not an interruption. If so, the same applies on the fifteenth. This is clear and simple; examine it carefully.
