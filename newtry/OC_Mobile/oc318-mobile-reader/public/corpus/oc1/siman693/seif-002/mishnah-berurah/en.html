(4) At night. Even if he davened Maariv early, before the emergence of the stars.
(5) We do not make him repeat. See above in siman 682, seif 1, and the same applies in our matter. Regarding Al HaNissim in Birkas HaMazon, see below in siman 695, seif 3.
(6) But not on the fifteenth. Since this does not belong to that day, it is not relevant to the event and is an interruption in davening. However, if he said it, we do not make him repeat, for nevertheless these days have some connection. Doubtful walled cities, where there is uncertainty whether they were surrounded by a wall, say Al HaNissim in davening on both days, and this is not an interruption, since they say it because of doubt. At Shacharis they rise early to the beis midrash. One should not remove the tefillin, even if they already davened, until after Megillah reading, for we expound that “honor” refers to tefillin, as in Minhagim.
