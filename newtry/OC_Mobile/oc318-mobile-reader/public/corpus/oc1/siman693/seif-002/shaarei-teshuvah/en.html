Vayehi Noam. And see Be'er HaGolah; and see there in Devar Shmuel who wrote most of the world are accustomed to precede megillah reading to havdalah on the cup in synagogue — meaning also after finishing order of Kedushah and Kaddish Titkabel as custom all year; but since there is no ruling from Talmud and rishonim one should not compel a community to change custom; each community is a city unto itself; and this is not lo titgodedu etc.; and see in Maharshal Yoreh Deah siman 170; and see Magen Avraham siman 693; and see Eliyah Rabbah that Peri Chadash wrote one should make havdalah before — not fit to benefit from the candle before havdalah — end; and per this it suffices if he blesses Borei Meorei HaEsh; and Beit Din was perplexed how to discharge his wife with this blessing etc.; if so he should bless meein sheva in synagogue before havdalah since many rabbanan wrote to bless meein sheva first so as not to benefit without blessing etc.; and see in Shoneh Halachot in name of Maharash Halevi he need not bless meein sheva first since they order afterward on the cup and candle benefit was already discharged in Yotzer HaMeorot and it is only remembrance of what happened to Adam — see there; and see Tosefta Pesachim daf 91; and see Shiryei Shoneh Halachot: even on Tisha B'Av night they bless before — there he will not order on the cup that night etc.; one who blessed meein sheva before megillah did not lose — see there. Shaarei Chaim brought from Torat Chesed also Shoneh Halachot in name of Maharash Halevi etc. and my words above — see there. Regarding moving megillah on Shabbat to bring to synagogue mibaal yad for Purim night on Motzei Shabbat — see above siman 688 on 15 falling on Shabbat where Peri Chadash wrote forbidden to move; and in Shoneh Halachot siman 308 somewhat doubts; Shaarei Chaim brought in name of Matteh Yehudah: even for Peri Chadash prohibition only if falls on 15 since reading obligation exists; but other Shabbatot Rabba's decree does not apply — why forbid reading like Torah reading; and since reading permitted, carrying permitted; and Shoneh Halachot seems to lean thus; and world custom is lenient — end; and see above siman 308 in my words I brought from Eliyah Rabbah megillah law is like ketavim kodesh and Peri Chadash forbids; per Matteh Yehudah one can say they do not argue — Eliyah Rabbah other Shabbatot of the year and Peri Chadash agrees; but bringing to synagogue before night I would prevent because of preparation from Shabbat to weekday as explained siman 696. Now we found thus in omissions end of Moed Katan — see there. One may be strict not to bring via a minor — see responsa Beit Ephraim Yoreh Deah siman 62 and 63; rather wait until dark or bring discreetly mibaal yad to synagogue and study it somewhat as learning — then it does not look so much like preparation; and see Magen Avraham siman 334 s.k. 18 regarding megillah not written properly — nevertheless we save etc. — see there.
