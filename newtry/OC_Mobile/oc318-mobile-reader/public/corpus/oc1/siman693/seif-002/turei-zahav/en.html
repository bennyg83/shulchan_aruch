If they did not say etc. — Mordekhai siman 200 wrote: days without etc. — it became obligatory on him etc. — end; and his words contradict regarding Chanukah and Purim: first he said they do not return, at end wrote they return — besides opposing Talmud in explaining they do not return; many great ones erase the words on Chanukah and Purim at the end; and Rashal in responsum siman 48 wrote a great one of his generation Mahari Pollak erased veChanukah at the end; and from his custom thus — both are true: Purim evening and morning they do not return him — not better than prayer; but Purim meal which prophets and sages imposed on all Israel as days of feasting and joy more than Shabbatot and Yom Tov etc. — if so not less than Shabbat meal etc. — end; found his conclusion: on Purim there is a distinction — they return only in Purim meal, not evening and morning and Minchah — not in prayer nor Birkat Hamazon except meal time alone. I question this gloss from what Mordekhai wrote in name of Rabbenu Yonah etc. — even though he concludes here Chanukah mention is optional in Birkat Hamazon etc. — implies Yerushalmi had veChanukah; and all the more the first gloss I mentioned that they erased veChanukah and Purim entirely; and in my humble opinion the opposite is in Mordekhai regarding Chanukah — and so it should read: this would say Rosh Chodesh and Chanukah but not Shabbat Yom Tov and Purim which cannot be without eating etc. — Purim has obligation like Shabbat and Yom Tov as written yemei mishteh vesimchah; and Rava said Purim meal eaten at night did not fulfill — implying the meal is obligatory; if so certainly one must eat; and Tur's view seems thus who wrote in 695 they say on miracles in Hodah and did not mention if they did not say they do not return as in 682 Chanukah — rather certainly as he explained; and so for halacha as Rashal in name of Mahari Pollak: in Birkat Hamazon of the meal one must return for on miracles in Purim; but it seems not specifically meal after Minchah but first meal one eats on Purim — for the core reason is meal obligation that day as yemei mishteh vesimchah and insufficient without eating; if so first meal eaten that day fulfills Purim meal thereby; and we need not hang the law on what we call meal after Minchah Purim meal which is added joy — and on this insufficient without eating — so for practical halacha.
