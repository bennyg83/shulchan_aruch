Written in Minhagim that they say full Kaddish after Maariv prayer before megillah reading; and likewise Maharil in responsum siman 56 Rokeach; and likewise Tosafot daf 4 and Hagahot Maimoniot unlike Levush; and in Agudah wrote in name of Rokeach to say half-Kaddish; and in Masechet Taanit wrote regarding Tisha B'Av they say Shema before Eichah; but Rokeach wrote to say half-Kaddish — end; and Abudraham also wrote half-Kaddish. It appears to me: in these countries where they say full Kaddish, then after megillah reading they say Shema without Titkabel; and possible this is Minhagim's view — siman 559; but not on 15. However if they said it they do not return him as written s.k. 108 unlike Bach.
