s.k. 2 — and his words: days that do not have etc. that do not have Musaf — if he did not say on the miracles in Chanukah and Purim they do not return him — so in Yerushalmi; and further our version there: he erred and did not mention Rosh Chodesh in Birkat Hamazon — they do not return him since it is possible without eating bread; this would say on Rosh Chodesh; but Shabbat and Yom Tov, Chanukah and Purim which cannot be without bread — they return. And it appears to Rabbenu Yonah: even though he mentions here that Chanukah mention is optional in Birkat Hamazon — since all are accustomed to mention and he also blessed intending to mention — we treat it as obligatory to mention — end Mordekhai.
