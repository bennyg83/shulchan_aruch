(א) On the mitzva of eruv etc. — one who places eruvin of courtyards and eruv techumin, or an eruv of courtyards that includes partnership of alleyways, includes them in one blessing and blesses on the mitzva of eruvin in plural language; but when he has many eruvin of courtyards in one city, or eruvin of alleyways, and such as when it was sent from many people, he blesses on the mitzva of eruv, for they are all one type (Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah):
