Laws of the blessing over eruv. Containing 1 seif. It is a mitzva to check after partnerships of
alleyways, and one blesses over it on the mitzva of eruv and says: Through this partnership all the residents of the alleyway shall be permitted to carry in and out from their courtyards to the alleyway (and from them to the houses) (Beit Yosef in the name of Shibbolei Leket) on Shabbat. When does one bless over it? At the time he collects it from the residents of the alleyway, or at the time he transfers ownership to them. (And if they collected it for the purpose of eruv and forgot and did not bless over it, it is a valid eruv since the blessing does not invalidate it (Agur and Shibbolei Leket) See siman 366.) בשבת אימתי מברך עליו בשעה שמקבץ אותו מבני המבוי או בשעה שמזכה להם: הגה ואם גבו אותו לשם עירוב ושכחו ולא ברכו עליו הוי עירוב כי אין הברכה מעכבת (אגור ושבולי לקט) עססי' שס"ו:
