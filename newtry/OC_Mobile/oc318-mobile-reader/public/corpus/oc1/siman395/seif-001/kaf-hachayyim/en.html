1) [Se'if 1] It is a mitzvah to seek, etc. This means that although eruv chatzeiros and shitufei mevo'os may be established even for an optional purpose, it is nevertheless a mitzvah to seek to establish them so that people do not come to violate the prohibition against carrying in a courtyard or alleyway. This is unlike an eruv techumin, which there is no mitzvah to seek to establish. On the contrary, an eruv techumin could lead one to violate the prohibition against walking beyond the techum in the opposite direction. It is permitted to establish such an eruv only in order to fulfill a mitzvah. Bach. See below, at the beginning of siman 415.
