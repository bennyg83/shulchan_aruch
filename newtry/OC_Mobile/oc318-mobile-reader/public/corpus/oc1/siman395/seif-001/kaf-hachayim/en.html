(א) [seif 1] It is a mitzva to check etc. — meaning that although in eruvin of courtyards and partnerships of alleyways one may eruv even for a permitted matter, nevertheless it is a mitzva to check after them so they not come to a prohibition of carrying in the courtyard and alleyway; unlike eruv techumin where there is no mitzva to check after it, for on the contrary it is possible that through eruv techumin one will come to a prohibition of walking outside the techum; but in order to fulfill the mitzva it is permitted to eruv on Shabbat; and see below siman 415.
