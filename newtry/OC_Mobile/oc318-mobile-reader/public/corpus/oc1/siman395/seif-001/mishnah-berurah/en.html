(א) It is a mitzva to check etc. — lest they forget and come to a prohibition of carrying:

(ב) On the mitzva of eruv — for also on partnership the name eruv applies, that all the courtyards in the alleyway are mingled and partnered together:

(ג) And from them to houses — meaning from the alleyways and courtyards to houses; and even though here we deal with partnerships of alleyways alone, nevertheless it is explained in siman 387 that they rely on partnership in place of eruv, and the Rama deals with this manner; and see above at the end of siman 366 in Mishna Berurah where we copied the formula commonly said:

(ד) At the time he collects etc. — see above in siman 366 in Mishna Berurah s.k. 80, 81, 82, and it applies here too:

(ה) And they did not bless over it etc. — the same applies even if they did not say over it also the formula of "through this partnership" mentioned above:
