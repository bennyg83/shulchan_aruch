(1) [Seif 1] As long as tefillin are on his head or on his arm it is forbidden to sleep in them even temporary sleep, etc. The Taz wrote in note 1, and thus: "Not like the Tur and Rosh who permit temporary sleep" — end of his words. And it requires study — when did the Tur permit temporary sleep more than the Shulchan Aruch? For behold, the Tur wrote: "He spreads a cloth over them and sleeps in them temporary sleep," and such are also the words of the Shulchan Aruch; and likewise the Bach — that in the Shulchan Aruch he ruled like the Rambam, and this is the view of our rabbi who is the Tur — see there. Afterwards I saw in Ma'amar Mordechai at the end of letter 1, that he raised this difficulty on the Taz's words above — see there. However, one can somewhat resolve the Taz's words above and say that he holds that what the Tur wrote — "he spreads a cloth over them and sleeps in them" — he mentioned as a general stringency, so that one not be with head uncovered, as written in the Chiddushei Hagahot on the margin of Beit Yosef in the name of Maharalach in answer 1 — see there. Not so the words of the Shulchan Aruch, which are taken from the Rambam's words, for he states law. And see the words of Lehem Chamudot on chapter Mi she'Meiso, letter tzadi-dalet.
