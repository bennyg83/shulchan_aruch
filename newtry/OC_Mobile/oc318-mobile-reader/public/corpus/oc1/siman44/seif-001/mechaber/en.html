Prohibition of sleeping in tefillin. And it contains one seif: As long
as tefillin are on his head or on his arm, it is forbidden to sleep in them even temporary sleep, except if he placed a cloth over them and no woman was with him — he may nap in them. And how does he do this? He places his head between his knees while sitting and sleeps. If the tefillin were wrapped in his hand, it is permitted to sleep in them even deep sleep. And if he holds them in his hand and they are not wrapped in his hand, it is forbidden to sleep in them even temporary sleep. And this is specifically when he holds them without their case; but when they are in their case, it is permitted in every case (Beit Yosef in the name of the Ittur).
