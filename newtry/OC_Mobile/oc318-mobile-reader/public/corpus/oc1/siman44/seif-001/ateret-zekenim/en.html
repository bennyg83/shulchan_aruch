And how does he do this? He places his head between his knees, etc. Even if he spreads a cloth over them, nevertheless we must be concerned lest he not remember and come to deep sleep; therefore he must also do this — place his head between his knees (Lehem Chamudot, folio 24b). And some wrote that even spreading a cloth alone suffices (Maharam of Italy). Temporary sleep is for the time of walking a hundred amot, or [if] his fellow need not wake him because he has not yet slept even temporary sleep — and even more than a hundred amot is called only temporary sleep when he has not fallen asleep well and sunk into sleep; therefore one need not be stringent when he has not sunk into sleep (my teacher in the Bach).
