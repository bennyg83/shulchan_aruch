Even temporary sleep — not like the Rosh and R' Yehuda who permit temporary sleep; and in the Gra's commentary at the beginning of the siman it appears that his opinion is to rule like them to be lenient; but in his opinion temporary sleep is not called such except for the time of walking a hundred amot — and even in this, specifically if he placed his head between his knees, as he wrote later in that siman:

He sleeps in them, etc. — see in the Mishnah Berurah, note 3, what he wrote: except during prayer and study, etc. Even though in the Peri Megadim he wrote that it is a mitzvah of the preferred way not to distract his mind at all from tefillin even during Kerias Shema and prayer — I did not conclude thus, for the Be'er Heitev in the name of the Ari, of blessed memory, wrote the opposite. And especially since I found in the Or Zarua, and thus: in siman 485 — "A person when wearing tefillin must remember that tefillin are upon him and not distract his mind from them, neither to sleep in them nor to blow on them; for because he remembers that he bears the Exalted Name upon him, he does not come to lightheadedness and grasps fear of Heaven in his heart, as we say in chapter Eilu Overin," etc. Therefore, when a person directs his heart in his prayer or in his halachah — this is not distraction, and all the more so there is fear of Heaven — end of his words.
