Even — the Taz should see in his Prisha that the Tur holds like the Rosh, his actual father, in explaining the baraita, Sukkah 27a: in his hand forbidden in every case. And thus Rabbenu Asher, of blessed memory — not so; and on his head temporary sleep is permitted, and he spreads a cloth between pillow and bolster as Rashi explained — except that nevertheless on his head we require a cloth, for it is disgusting to sleep in tefillin without a cloth, as the Levush wrote in this explanation. But for lest he fall asleep we are not concerned. And that which he brought the Beit Yosef's difficulty at length, and what he wrote in the name of Beit Yosef in the name of R' Yitzchak and the Rosh — I do not [agree], for it is clear in the Tur also this. And Magen Avraham, letter 2, and see in the Magen Avraham further on this:

The author said: the sugya and the words of the poskim here are deep; I extended in study with students, and here I will hint briefly. Bach — in what he wrote for Rabbeinu Yonah and the Rosh, temporary sleep is permitted. And one can explain — is deep sleep then a decree upon a decree? Perhaps deep sleep and perhaps he will blow? If so, what does Abaye challenge: what is Sukkah compared to tefillin? Behold Sukkah is one concern and tefillin is a decree upon a decree! And even if we say deep sleep — certainly this is difficult: this is a Torah scroll and this is tefillin which is rabbinic.

Also in Tosafos there, entry "but" — they challenged from Shabbos 49b on Abaye himself: a clean body lest he blow — behold, for temporary sleep there is no concern, and here Abaye forbids. And they answered; and for Rava it is difficult — for there he said "a clean body" that he not sleep, and that means even temporary sleep; and here for lest he fall asleep he is not concerned and temporary sleep is permitted. And one can say that they hold like R' Meir, for Rava does not disagree regarding placing his head, etc. — rather regarding sukkah he disagrees. And for Rashi, who explained "lest he fall asleep he is not concerned" — he explains the text of Shabbos 49a, entry "that he not sleep during the day" because of distraction; and Rava holds that even one who forgets the vanities of the world is distraction. And according to this, the baraita that one who sleeps temporary sleep and not deep sleep — certainly for Rava [this applies] in the hands when wrapped; and that which not temporary sleep when holding them is like the explanation of the Rambam, of blessed memory; and deep sleep is permitted when spreading a cloth on the tefillin, as the Ittur that I brought in Magen Avraham 5. Or Rashi will say: he holds we require a cloth in every case, or like the Bach, or like the Prisha that I brought in Magen Avraham; and therefore "a clean body" — if he is accustomed to temporary sleep he should not leave them on, lest he sleep temporary sleep and forget without a cloth, as stated. But if so, Rashi should have explained spreading a cloth on the head — and it is not so, for nevertheless deep sleep is forbidden. And what the Kesef Mishnah wrote in chapter 4 of Hilchot Tefillin — that therefore the Rambam ruled like Abaye on Shabbos 49 and not like Rava from the baraita of one who sleeps temporary sleep — I did not understand; for the Rambam holds that Rava is concerned lest he fall asleep, and without [placing his head] between his knees and with a cloth it is forbidden. And it requires study.
