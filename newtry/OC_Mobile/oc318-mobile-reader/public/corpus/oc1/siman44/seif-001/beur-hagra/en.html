Seif 1: Whenever, etc. This is the language of Rambam, following his explanation of the Gemara there. According to Rashi's explanation, the clause concerning holding them in his hand should have appeared at the end. Nevertheless, all the poskim follow Rashi's explanation, including Rabbeinu Yonah and Tur.
And not, etc. There, 2, following Rabbi Yose, for the halachah follows Rabbi Yose against his colleagues, as stated in chapter 4 of Eruvin. He holds that the reference to young men is not literal.
And how does he, etc. This follows Rabbi Yochanan, because Rava does not disagree with him regarding tefillin, as Rashi explains there. He understands Rami bar Yechezkel to disagree with Rabbi Yochanan, and the halachah follows Rabbi Yochanan. This requires study. First, it appears that Rami bar Yechezkel does not disagree, but merely defines the measure, while Rabbi Yochanan teaches that even within that measure one must specifically place his head between his knees and avoid falling fully asleep. Second, Rami bar Yechezkel's teaching is a baraisa.
And if he holds them, etc. Berachos 23b. Rambam and Ramach were challenged from this baraisa. Beit Yosef and Kesef Mishneh answer that there the tefillin must be wrapped, and therefore Rambam writes that they are wrapped, because then there is no concern that they may fall.
And specifically, etc. See Rashi on Berachos there, 23a, s.v. “he does, etc.” According to this, the pouch must specifically be a tefach. See below, siman 43, seif 6.
