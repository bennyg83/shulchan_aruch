(1) As long as, etc. — the Rambam's wording; and as the Gemara explains there: according to Rashi's explanation it should read "that which he holds them in his hand at the end"; but all the poskim hold like Rashi's explanation, and so too R' Yehuda and the Tur:

And not, etc. — there, section 2; and like R' Yossi, for the halachah follows R' Yossi against his colleagues, as written in chapter 4 of Eruvin; and he holds that for children there is no concern:

And how does he do this, etc. — like R' Yochanan, for Rava does not disagree with him regarding tefillin, as Rashi wrote there; and he holds that Rami bar Yechezkel argues with him, but the halachah follows R' Yochanan. And it requires study: one [difficulty] — for who holds that he does not disagree? Rather, he explains the measure, and R' Yochanan says that even with this measure one needs specifically his head between his knees so that he not fall asleep. And furthermore — behold, our Rabbis taught [regarding] Rami bar Yechezkel:

And if he holds them, etc. — Berachos 23b; and likewise they challenged the Rambam and Ra'avad from this baraita; and the answer of Beit Yosef — and thus it appears that there it requires them wrapped; therefore the Rambam wrote "wrapped," for we are not concerned lest they fall:
