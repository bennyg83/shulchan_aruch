And thus said Rabbeinu Yonah: distraction is not considered except when he is lightheaded and in jest; but when he is occupied with his needs, even if he is occupied with his trade and craft and his mind is not really on them — this is not called distraction. At first glance it is difficult — for we say in Shabbos that a person may go out wearing tefillin on erev Shabbos with approaching darkness, etc., see there. And perhaps since it is a mitzvah of the preferred way not to distract his mind from them at all, we are not concerned that he go out with them on Shabbos to the public domain — see Yad Aharon.
