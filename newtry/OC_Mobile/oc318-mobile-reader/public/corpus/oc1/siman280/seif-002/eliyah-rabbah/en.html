To have relations, etc. In our lands the custom has spread to prohibit this, Bach writes. Taz responds that Heaven forbid establishing such a prohibitory custom, and says he heard that saintly great sages themselves practiced leniency. What people avoid is often because of embarrassment, and even after Shabbos they frequently do not have relations. Shelah, page 100, cautions that the groom should not lie beside the bride until the night on which he intends to consummate the marriage.
