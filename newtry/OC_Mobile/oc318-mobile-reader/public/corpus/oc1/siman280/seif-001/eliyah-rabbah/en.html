[Levush] And he should be careful, etc. One who neglects the marital obligation even once is punished, even if he was drawn into prolonged Torah study, according to Taz; Perishah adds even if he borrowed money and repaid it. It is a mitzvah to eat garlic on Erev Shabbos or Friday night, and likewise other foods that increase seed, as Magen Avraham writes. The initial letters of "the Children of Israel shall guard Shabbos" allude to marital relations, teaching that women too are included in the command of the Shabbos marital obligation because they too are included in guarding Shabbos. See the end of siman 608. I found, however, in Sefer Chasidim siman 390 that ordinary garlic diminishes desire, while roasted garlic increases seed. It further says that all salty foods diminish it, while legumes and lentils cooked without salt increase it. See siman 724.
