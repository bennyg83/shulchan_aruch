The conjugal interval, etc. One should be careful to fulfill the mitzvah of the marital obligation. It is a mitzvah to eat garlic on Friday or on Shabbos night, and likewise other foods that increase fertility. See siman 608. Sefer Chassidim, siman 390, writes that raw garlic suppresses desire, whereas roasted garlic increases fertility. It further writes that salty foods and legumes diminish fertility, while unsalted cooked lentils increase it, as cited by Eliyah Rabbah.
Healthy people. This excludes those of weak constitution, who are obligated only according to their strength.
From Shabbos night, etc. This is apart from the night of immersion. Before nightfall, one must be careful to display added affection and love toward his wife, and certainly must not accustom himself to quarrelling on Friday, as the Bach writes.
