Tachanun is not recited on the 14th and 15th of the first Adar, and the law of fasting and eulogizing on those days. It contains 1 seif: On the 14th and 15th of the first Adar one does not fall on his face in Tachanun and does not recite the psalm "May Hashem answer you on the day of distress." Eulogizing and fasting are forbidden, but the other Purim practices are not observed. Some say that even eulogizing and fasting are permitted. {Rama: The custom follows the first view. Some say that one is obligated to increase feasting and rejoicing on the 14th of the first Adar. [Tur in the name of Rif] This is not the custom. Nevertheless, one should add somewhat to the meal in order to satisfy the stringent opinions. [Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Semak] "A cheerful heart has a continual feast."}
