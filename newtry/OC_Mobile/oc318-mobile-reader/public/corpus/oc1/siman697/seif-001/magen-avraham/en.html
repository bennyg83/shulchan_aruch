On the fourteenth of the first Adar. According to all opinions, Al HaNissim is not recited, since the Megillah is not read. For there are those who say that it is not recited even during Maariv on Purim; although we do not rule that way, here all agree that it is not recited.
And one who is cheerful of heart has a continual feast. May our God sustain us amid the joys of mitzvos; then our mouths will be filled with laughter, speedily in our days, amen.
