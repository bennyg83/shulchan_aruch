1) [Seif 1] The 14th and 15th days of the first Adar, etc. Purim is observed only in the Adar adjacent to Nissan, in order to connect one redemption to another, the redemption of Mordechai to the redemption of Moshe. Lest you say that it should be observed in both months, the verse teaches, 'each and every year.' Just as each year it is observed once, so too in a leap year it is observed once. And just as each year it is observed in the Adar adjacent to Nissan, so too in a leap year it is observed in the Adar adjacent to Nissan. Levush. See above, siman 685, os 1.
