On the 14th of first Adar — not the 15th, even though in second Adar they practice feast and joy on both (Beit Yosef and Hagahot Maimoniot in the name of Semak); however regarding prohibition of melacha there is no prohibition on the first since on the second too there is no prohibition except where they practiced (Beit Yosef in the name of Semak). And some say they practice not to do melacha on Purim Katan both days (Abudraham) and we do not practice so. May the Almighty send a redeemer to Judah and Israel and the days of Purim not be annulled even after the Temple is rebuilt, amen amen.
