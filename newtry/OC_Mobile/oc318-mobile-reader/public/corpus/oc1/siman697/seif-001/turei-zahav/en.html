And they are forbidden in eulogizing and fasting. For we learned: there is no difference between the first Adar and the second Adar except regarding reading the Megillah and gifts to the poor. We infer: but regarding eulogizing and fasting, this one and that one are equal. The some say hold that this refers to what was stated at the beginning there: if they read the Megillah and then the year was intercalated, etc. In that case specifically they are equal regarding eulogizing and fasting. But in other leap years, when they did not read the Megillah on the fourteenth of the first Adar, they differ also regarding eulogizing and fasting, for in the first Adar they are permitted.
Nevertheless, he should increase somewhat in the meal, etc. In the glosses to Tashbetz, siman 178, he wrote that one should increase, and Rabbeinu Yechiel of Paris was accustomed to increase and invite people, as implied by the language of the Mishnah, There is no difference between Adar, etc. The Rama therefore concludes well here, and one who is good-hearted feasts constantly.
