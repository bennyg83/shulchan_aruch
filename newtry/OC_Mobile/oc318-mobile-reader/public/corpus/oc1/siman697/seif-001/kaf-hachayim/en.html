(1) [Seif 1] One does not say Tachanun on the 14th and 15th of Adar I, and the law of fasting and eulogizing on those days. Containing one paragraph. On the 14th and 15th of Adar I we do not fall on our faces and we do not say the psalm "Ya'ancha Heaven beyom tzarah," and on those days it is forbidden to eulogize and to fast; but other matters are not practiced on them. And some say that even eulogy and fasting are permitted. {Rama: The custom follows the first view. Some say one is obligated to increase in feasting and joy on the 14th of Adar I (Tur in the name of the Rif); however we do not practice thus. Nevertheless one should increase somewhat at the meal to satisfy those who are stringent (Hagahot Maimuni in the name of SeMaK). "But the good-hearted has a continual feast" (Proverbs 15:15).}
