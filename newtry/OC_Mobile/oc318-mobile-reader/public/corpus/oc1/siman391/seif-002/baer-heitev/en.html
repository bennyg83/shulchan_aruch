R' Shlomo Luria siman 18 wrote he received from elders to prohibit carrying at Lublin fair — perhaps because accustomed to establish coming at every fair and regular guest prohibits immediately between Israel and gentile see siman 384 end quote. Mordechai wrote in any case guest forbidden take from his house to courtyard or city; see Maharik 346; appears law with him for courtyard belongs to homeowner and house to guest for daily rental is sale; if so transfers reshut to reshut but homeowner permitted take out for guest has no share in alleyway or courtyard until thirty days; see siman 370 Magen Avraham.
