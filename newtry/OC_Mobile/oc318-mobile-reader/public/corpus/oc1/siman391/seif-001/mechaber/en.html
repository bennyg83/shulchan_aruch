Laws of nullifying reshut for one who forgot to eruv.
Containing 2 seifim. As all nullification laws for one who forgot and did not eruv in his courtyard — so in alleyway if one courtyard forgot and did not join — nullifies reshut to other alleyway residents' or they to her even though many — courtyard residents regarding alleyway considered as individual in courtyard.
