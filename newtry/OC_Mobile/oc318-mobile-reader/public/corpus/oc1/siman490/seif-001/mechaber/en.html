Order of Prayers for the Second Day and Chol HaMoed (Intermediate Days) of Passover (Nine Sections): On the second day of Yom Tov
the Torah reading is from Parashat Emor, starting from "שור או כשב" (An ox or a sheep) until the end of the section and the Maftir reading is the same as on the first day. The Haftarah is from Melachim Bet (Kings II), from "וישלח המלך" (And the king sent) until "ואחריו לא קם כמוהו" (And after him, none arose like him), recounting the Passover of King Josiah.
