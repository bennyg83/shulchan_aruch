1) [Se'if 1] On the second day, etc. At the meal on the second day, it is proper to do something in remembrance of Esther's feast, because Haman was hanged on that day. Shelah; Magen Avraham, at the beginning of the siman; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1. The allusion is in the verse, 'The manna ceased on the day after Pesach.' Eshel Avraham. The statement that Haman was hanged on that day appears in Rashi's commentary to Megillah 15a and 16a, and in Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer, chapter 3, and Midrash Esther. Rashi writes there that the fast occurred on the fourteenth and fifteenth, whereas Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer and Midrash Rabbah state that it occurred on the thirteenth, fourteenth, and fifteenth. Accordingly, the phrase, 'It happened on the third day,' has its straightforward meaning, the third day of the fast, and need not be interpreted as the third day after the couriers were sent, as Rashi explains there. See there and analyze carefully.
2) There. On the second day, the reading is, etc. The prayer order is the same as on the first day. Tur; Levush. See above, siman 487, for the order. This applies to those outside Eretz Yisrael who observe a second day of Yom Tov. For residents of Eretz Yisrael, who observe one day, the second day's order is as stated in the following se'if.
3) There. Until the end of the passage. This means through the verse, 'Moshe declared the appointed festivals of Havayah.' Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 2.
4) There. The maftir reads, etc. See above, siman 188, se'if 3. In Eretz Yisrael, the reading begins VeHikravtem, as stated below in se'if 6.
5) It is very proper to study the passage of the omer on the sixteenth of Nissan. It appears in parashas Emor, from, 'Havayah spoke,' and, 'Speak to the Children of Israel,' through, 'And bread,' and so forth; and in parashas Vayikra, from, 'If you bring a meal-offering of first produce,' through, 'A fire-offering to Havayah.' One should afterward say the prayer beginning, 'Master,' and so forth, asking that it be regarded as though the omer was offered at its proper time. Hagahos Yafeh Nof; Pis'chei Olam, subsection 1. Before saying, 'Master of the worlds,' one may also recite chapter 10 of Menachos, beginning, 'Rabbi Yishmael says: The omer,' and so forth. See above, siman 429, subsection 6.
