1) [Se'if 1] On the second day, etc. At the meal on the second day, it is proper to do something in remembrance of Esther's feast, because Haman was hanged on that day. Shelah; Magen Avraham, at the beginning of the siman; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1. The allusion is in the verse, 'The manna ceased on the day after Pesach.' Eshel Avraham. The statement that Haman was hanged on that day appears in Rashi's commentary to Megillah 15a and 16a, and in Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer, chapter 3, and Midrash Esther. Rashi writes there that the fast occurred on the fourteenth and fifteenth, whereas Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer and Midrash Rabbah state that it occurred on the thirteenth, fourteenth, and fifteenth. Accordingly, the phrase, 'It happened on the third day,' has its straightforward meaning, the third day of the fast, and need not be interpreted as the third day after the couriers were sent, as Rashi explains there. See there and analyze carefully.
