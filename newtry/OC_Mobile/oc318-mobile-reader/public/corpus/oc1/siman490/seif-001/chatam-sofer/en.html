In the Mechaber seif 9 in the gloss "and the people practice." Note: see Tractate Soferim ch. 14 halacha 33. And in my humble opinion it appears that there it refers to the haftarah in them, which is discussed above from it; and in halacha 4 there it also deals with the haftarah in the Scriptures, as Tosafot wrote at the beginning of the chapter HaKorei Omed — see there. But there is no mention there of reading these megillot at these times and blessing on them; and as Rama wrote in responsa siman 35, and in Tractate Soferim ch. 18 halacha 4: four — there is no mention of a blessing on kinot; Eichah — from which it is implied that one should not bless, as appears to me — source.
