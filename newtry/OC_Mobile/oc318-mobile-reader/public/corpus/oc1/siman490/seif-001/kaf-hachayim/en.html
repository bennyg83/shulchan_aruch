(א) [Seif 1] On the second day etc. — and one should do on the second day at the meal something as a remembrance of Esther's feast, for on that day Haman was hanged — Shelah. Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman; Chayei Adam note 1. And what he wrote "on that day Haman was hanged" — thus in Rashi's explanation to Megillah daf 15 and 16a — see there; and thus in Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer ch. 3 and in Midrash Esther; except that Rashi wrote there that the fast was on 14, 15, Adar 7; it is explained there in PDR"E and Midrash that the fast was on 13, 14, 15 — see there. And accordingly "and it was on the third day" comes according to its plain sense — the third of the fast — and one need not strain to explain "three" as the messengers, as Rashi wrote there — see there and examine.
