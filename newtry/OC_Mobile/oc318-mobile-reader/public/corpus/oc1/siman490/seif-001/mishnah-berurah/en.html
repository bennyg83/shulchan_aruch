(1) “An ox or a sheep.” That passage discusses the laws of all the festivals, including Pesach.
(2) Until the end of the passage. Meaning, until “Moshe declared the appointed times of Hashem,” etc. The sefarim write that it is proper to do something during the meal on the second day of Pesach in remembrance of Esther's feast, since Haman was hanged on that day.
