One should do etc. that on that day Haman was hanged, as Rashi wrote in ch. 1 of Megillah daf 15 on what is written there "and Mordechai passed" — Rav said that he transferred the first day of Yom Tov of Passover, and Rashi explained: the first day of Yom Tov of Passover was on a fast, for on the 13th of Nisan the decree was written and the edict was given in Shushan, and on the 14th and 15th they fasted, and on the 16th Haman was hanged in the evening — end of his words.
