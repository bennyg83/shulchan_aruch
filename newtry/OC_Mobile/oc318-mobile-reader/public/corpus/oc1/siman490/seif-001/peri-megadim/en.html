We do not. See the Taz. Regarding prayer, they make him return even for Maariv, for nowadays they have accepted it as obligatory (Pri Chadash). Which is not so regarding Rosh Chodesh at night: they do not make him return for the prayer, for they do not sanctify [the new moon] at night; see siman 422, and see siman 188. Magen Avraham [note] 10. And tefillin on Chol HaMoed: see siman 31, Taz note 2, and siman 25 in the Magen Avraham, letter 31: to remove them before Hallel, and the sheliach tzibbur after Hallel; and on Chol HaMoed of Sukkos the sheliach tzibbur also before Hallel; and see the Levush, which implies before Musaf; and examine Eliyah Rabbah there. And the Levush here wrote to remove them before the reading of the musaf parsha; and on Sukkos before Hallel, for the four species allude to the four parshiyos. And if so, an individual who has no lulav should remove [them] after Hallel according to the opinion of the Rama and the Levush in siman 25 and 31. Nevertheless the world practices to remove them, both on Pesach and on Sukkos, before Hallel, for most hold that Chol HaMoed is forbidden for tefillin; and see the responsa of Moreinu HaRav Menachem Azariah of Fano, of blessed memory. And see the Levush here: the sheliach tzibbur should not recite the blessing in a loud voice, meaning in a place where the sheliach tzibbur blesses over the tefillin aloud to discharge one who is not expert; and on Chol HaMoed he should not do so. And perhaps this is the intent of the Rav [Rama] in a slightly different wording; see there.
