There is no Taz — in prayer they return him even at Maariv, for now they have accepted it as obligatory — Pri Chadash; unlike Rosh Chodesh at night, where they do not return in prayer, for they do not sanctify at night — see siman 422 and siman 488; Magen Avraham note 10. Tefillin on chol hamoed — see siman 31; Taz note 2; and siman 25 in Magen Avraham note 31: to remove them before Hallel and the chazan after Hallel; and on Sukkot chol hamoed the chazan also before Hallel; and see Levush, which implies before musaf; and examine there. And Levush here wrote to remove them before reading the parsha of the musaf offerings; and on Sukkot before Hallel, for the four species allude to four parshiyot; and therefore a lone person without a lulav should remove after Hallel per Rama and Levush in siman 25 and 31; nevertheless the world practices to remove both on Passover and Sukkot before Hallel, for most hold that chol hamoed is forbidden in tefillin; and see responsa of Maharam of Padua.
