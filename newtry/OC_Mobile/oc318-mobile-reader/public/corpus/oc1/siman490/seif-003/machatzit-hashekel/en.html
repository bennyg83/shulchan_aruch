(s.k. 3) Mashach etc.: what Shulchan Aruch wrote: this order does not change etc., that when it is Shabbat they read Re'eh etc.: Levush wrote the reason: both on Shabbat chol hamoed Pesach and on Shabbat chol hamoed Sukkot they read Re'eh, because in the parsha there is "Observe the festival of Matzot" and "pilgrimage festivals" and chol hamoed, as it is written therein "you shall observe the festival of Matzot"; and we derive the prohibition of melacha on chol hamoed from this verse in tractate Chagigah; except that on Shabbat they need to read seven men, therefore they begin from Re'eh: Magen Avraham:
