The Hallel. See the Taz; see Beis Yosef: this is the reason of the Midrash; and in Arakhin 10b the reason is that on Pesach there is no distinction in the offerings, which is not so of Sukkos; and examine Eliyah Rabbah, letter 6, for a further reason; see there. And regarding interruption, see siman 488, se'if 1, and siman 493, se'if 4: from the third day onward its law is like Rosh Chodesh in this; and see siman 422 in the Magen Avraham, note 7: the same applies on Pesach; and two who have reached the age of chinuch suffice; see there.
