[3] [Levush] Tefillin, etc. It was explained at the end of siman 25 in another wording that we have, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: this "sign" applies all day, for the entire day is fit for taking the lulav; but since the Rosh and the Tur did not mention it, we should not invent it from our own hearts. And even though the entire day is fit, nevertheless with one taking one fulfills, which is not so of chametz on Pesach and of Sukkah on Sukkos, whose obligation is constant all day; and this alone is what the Rosh was concerned about, that it is a "sign."
