He says, “this day.” Magen Avraham writes that this proves one should not say in Birkas HaMazon, “May the Merciful One grant us the day that is entirely good.” I have heard that when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos, some say, “May the Merciful One grant us the day that is entirely Shabbos, goodness, and rest, for eternal life.”
We do not customarily say it. The present custom is to say, “this day of holy convocation,” and this is also the conclusion of the later authorities.
