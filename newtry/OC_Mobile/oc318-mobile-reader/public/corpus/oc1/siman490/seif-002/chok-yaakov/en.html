He prays in the usual manner. Mizmor LeSodah is not recited, because the thanksgiving offering itself was not brought then, since it required two loaves of chametz. For the same reason it is not recited on Erev Pesach. Regarding putting on and removing tefillin, see above at the beginning of simanim 25 and 31. The later authorities appear to agree that they should be removed before Hallel and the Torah reading, while the shaliach tzibbur removes them after Hallel. The widespread custom, however, follows the Rama at the end of siman 25, to remove them only before the silent Mussaf Amidah, and their custom should not be changed. The Taz himself writes at the end of siman 25 that one should not protest even against one who does not remove them at all. This is certainly true of the present custom, which was established according to the Rama in the name of the Beit Yosef, whose rulings we follow everywhere, particularly on Chol HaMoed Pesach. The Rosh writes in Hilchos Tefillin in the name of Rabbi Yochanan that, because he suffered from his head, he would put on tefillin only from Pesach to Pesach. It appears to me that he put them on during Pesach because the verse, “You shall observe this statute,” was written concerning Pesach. If so, how can one remove the tefillin before reading that passage? Therefore, to maintain a uniform practice, they should always be removed after the Torah reading. So it appears to me.
