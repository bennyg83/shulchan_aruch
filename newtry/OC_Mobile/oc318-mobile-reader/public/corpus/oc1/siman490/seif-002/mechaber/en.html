Chol HaMoed (Intermediate Days): The Ma’ariv, Shacharit, and Mincha prayers follow the usual weekday format. Ya’aleh V’Yavo is added in the Avodah section of the Amidah. If one forgets to say Ya’aleh V’Yavo, they must repeat the Amidah. It is also recited in Birkat HaMazon, but if omitted, one does not need to repeat it. Mussaf follows the format of Yom Tov Mussaf.Gloss: When reaching "על ידי משה עבדך כאמור" (Through Moses, Your servant, as it is stated...), one recites "והקרבתם עולה וגו’" (And you shall offer a burnt offering...) until "ושני תמידים כהלכתם" (And two daily offerings as prescribed) (Tur).):
