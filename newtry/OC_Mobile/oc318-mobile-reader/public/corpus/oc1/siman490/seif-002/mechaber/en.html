On the third day, which is Chol HaMoed, at Maariv, Shacharis, and Minchah he prays in the usual manner and says Yaaleh VeYavo in Retzei. If he omitted it, he must repeat the Amidah. He likewise mentions it in Birkas HaMazon, but if he omitted it, he does not repeat Birkas HaMazon. At Mussaf he prays as he does for Mussaf on Yom Tov. Rama: When he reaches “through Moshe Your servant, as stated,” he says, “You shall offer an elevation offering,” through “and the two daily offerings according to their law.” Tur.
