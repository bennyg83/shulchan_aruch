They do not return him. For specifically in prayer it does not suffice that he not pray; and since he did not pray properly, he returns; but in Birkat HaMazon on chol hamoed, if he wishes he does not eat anything that obligates him in Birkat HaMazon that he should eat meat and fruits — therefore they do not return him.
