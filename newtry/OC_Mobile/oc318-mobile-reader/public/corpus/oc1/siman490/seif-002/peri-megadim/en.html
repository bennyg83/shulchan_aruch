(1) There is no Taz — in Sephard they say on Yom Tov "the festival of Matzot, this day of festival, holy convocation" as siman 487; in Ashkenaz Eshel Avraham: on Yom Tov not "day of festival, holy convocation" at all; our custom not to say "day of festival" at all, only mikra kodesh on Yom Tov and on chol hamoed also mikra kodesh; in Avodah Yaaleh VeYavo — not mikra kodesh; and behold Rambam wrote not mikra kodesh on Yom Tov and chol hamoed — one may say Yaaleh VeYavo in Avodah speaks, but in musaf they say mikra kodesh as Bach wrote: mitzvah to assemble in synagogue for musaf korban — see there; nevertheless why did he not write not to say "day of festival, holy convocation" on Yom Tov and chol hamoed? In Levush seif 3 he wrote not to say "this day of festival, holy convocation" on Yom Tov or chol hamoed; in seif 9 he wrote why on days 7–8 of Passover in musaf after "And you shall offer" and on the seventh day mikra kodesh — for they practice to mention mikra kodesh — see there, namely in musaf — see there; our custom to say "festival of such-and-such, season... holy convocation" and skip "day of festival" between Shacharit and musaf and chol hamoed in musaf, but in Yaaleh VeYavo in "You chose us" or in Avodah only "festival of such-and-such, remember us" etc. and skip mikra kodesh (Pri Megadim):

(ב) And behold the reason for mikra kodesh — Eshel Avraham siman 487 in the name of Ramban z"l in parshat Emor: prayer on a weekday is d'rabbanan (not like Rambam z"l, see siman 106); and on Yom Tov one may say prayer is d'oraisa, to gather in the house of prayer to sanctify the day in prayer publicly — see there; it implies we require ten elders thirteen years, and Beit Shammai: the matter of publicity is regarding a woman who comes upon a non-Jew, and likewise "And Boaz took ten men" — all publicity [requires] ten (many become three); and therefore it is fitting to be concerned for this and to seek after elders known to have brought Beit Shammai — see Magen Avraham siman 55 note 7 and 199: in words of Torah we require known [experts] who brought two [hairs] — see there.
