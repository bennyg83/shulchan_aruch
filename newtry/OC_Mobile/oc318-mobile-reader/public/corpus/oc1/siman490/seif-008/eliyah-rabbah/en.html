(ח) [Levush] and Minchah etc. Hagahot Maimoniyot wrote: at Minchah they say "And I, my prayer" and read three in the weekly parsha, and they do not say Tzidkatcha nor Mish'al, and say "Elohim, chonanenu"; at Maariv "You have favored us" and they do not say Vayehi Noam and say "And grant you," until here; but in Kol Bo siman 57 he wrote that they also do not say "And grant you"; and it is reasonable, for it would be like Yom Tov when melacha is forbidden. Maharil wrote: one does not enjoy it because of Shabbat, like the Shabbat of chol hamoed that has Yom Tov before it and Yom Tov after it, and it itself is moed. Mateh Moshe wrote in the name of Shibolei HaLeket: if he forgot in Shacharit "Moses rejoiced" and said "You chose us" and mentioned that of Shabbat, he need not return and pray — until here; and in Shibolei HaLeket itself and in a tannaic teaching it is explained that the same law applies in Maariv and Minchah of Shabbat and chol hamoed:
