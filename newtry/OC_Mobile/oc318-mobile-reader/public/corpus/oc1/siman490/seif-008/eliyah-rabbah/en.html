[8] [Levush] And Minchah, etc. The Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote: at Minchah they say "And I, my prayer," and they read three in the weekly parsha, and they do not say Tzidkatcha nor Maskil, and they say "Elokim yechanenu"; at Maariv, "You have favored us," and they do not say Vayehi Noam, and they say Vayiten Lecha; until here. But in the Kol Bo, siman 57, he wrote that they also do not say Vayiten Lecha; and that is reasonable, for this would be like Yom Tov, when melacha is forbidden. The Maharil wrote: one does not derive [that Motza'ei Shabbos benefit] by reason of Shabbos as from a Shabbos of Chol HaMoed, for [that Shabbos] has Yom Tov before it and Yom Tov after it, and it itself is a festival. Mateh Moshe wrote in the name of Shibolei HaLeket: if he forgot in the Shacharis prayer "Moshe rejoiced" and said "You chose us" and mentioned that of Shabbos, he need not return and pray; until here. And in Shibolei HaLeket itself, and in a baraisa, it is explained that the same law applies in Maariv and Minchah of Shabbos and Chol HaMoed.
