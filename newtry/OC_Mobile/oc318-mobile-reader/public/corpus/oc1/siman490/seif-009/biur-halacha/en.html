He prays as usual — and Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Mahari Molcho: if he concluded in Shacharit of Shabbat chol hamoed "Who sanctifies Shabbat, Israel, and the seasons," he need not return; and if he concluded "Israel and the seasons" and did not mention Shabbat, he did not fulfill; and if within kedei dibbur he returned and said "Who sanctifies Shabbat," he fulfilled. And Beit Yosef wrote that if in the order of musaf he prayed musaf of Shabbat alone, he did not fulfill — source.
