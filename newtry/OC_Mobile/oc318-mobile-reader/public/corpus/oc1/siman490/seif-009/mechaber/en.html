Shabbat that Falls on Chol HaMoed: The Ma’ariv, Shacharit, and Mincha prayers follow the usual Shabbat format, with Ya’aleh V’Yavo added in the' Avodah section. In Mussaf, one follows the order for Chol HaMoed but includes Shabbat: "ותתן לנו את יום המנוח הזה ויום חג המצות הזה" (And You have given us this day of rest and this festival of Matzot). The conclusion is: "מקדש השבת וישראל והזמנים" (Who sanctifies the Shabbat, Israel, and the festivals). The Haftarah is "היתה עלי".Gloss: The blessing on the Haftarah does not mention Pesach, neither in the middle nor at the end. It is customary to read Shir HaShirim on Shabbat Chol HaMoed. If Shabbat falls on the last day of Yom Tov, Shir HaShirim is read then instead. Similarly, on Sukkot, Kohelet is read, and on Shavuot, Ruth is read (Abudraham). The common custom is not) to recite a blessing on the reading of these Megillot, neither "על מקרא Megillah" nor "על מקרא כתובים".:
