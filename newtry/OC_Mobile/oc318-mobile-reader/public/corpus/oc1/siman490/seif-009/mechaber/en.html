When Shabbos falls on Chol HaMoed, at Maariv, Shacharis, and Minchah he prays the usual Shabbos Amidah and says Yaaleh VeYavo in Retzei. At Mussaf he follows the order of the Chol HaMoed Mussaf, but mentions Shabbos, saying, “You have given us this day of rest and this day of the Festival of Matzos.” He likewise says, “the Mussaf offerings of this day of rest and this day of the Festival of Matzos,” and concludes, “Who sanctifies Shabbos, Yisrael, and the festivals.” The Haftarah is “The hand of Hashem was upon me.” Rama: Pesach is not mentioned in the blessings after the Haftarah, neither in the middle blessing nor in the conclusion. The custom is to read Shir HaShirim on Shabbos Chol HaMoed. If Shabbos falls on the final Yom Tov, it is read on that Shabbos. The same applies on Sukkos with Koheles, and the custom is to read Rus on Shavuos. Avudraham. The people customarily do not recite a blessing over these readings, neither “concerning the reading of the Megillah” nor “concerning the reading of the Writings.”
