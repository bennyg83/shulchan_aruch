Shabbat that falls on chol hamoed — evening, morning, and minchah he prays as on Shabbat and says Yaaleh VeYavo in Avodah; in' musaf he says the order of musaf for chol hamoed, but mentions Shabbat and says "And grant us this day of rest and this festival of Passover," and likewise says "and the musaf offerings of this day of rest and this festival of Passover," and concludes "Who sanctifies Shabbat, Israel, and the seasons"; the haftarah is "It was upon me."And they do not mention Passover in the haftarah berachah, neither in the middle nor in the conclusion; and it is customary to say Shir HaShirim on the Shabbat of chol hamoed; if Shabbat is on the last Yom Tov they say it that Shabbat; and likewise on Sukkot with Kohelet; and Ruth on Shavuot (Avudraham); and the people practice) not to bless on them neither "on the reading of a megillah" nor "on the reading of Ketuvim.":
