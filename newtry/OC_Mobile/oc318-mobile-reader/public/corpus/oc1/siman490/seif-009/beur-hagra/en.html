(א) Seif 9 — and they read haftarah. Megillah there — source.

(ב) And they do not. As Tosafot wrote in Shabbat 24b s.v. velo etc., for the halacha follows Rav Gidal — source.

(ג) Not etc. For even according to Rashi and Rav Alfasi, who hold not like Rav Gidal, nevertheless they do not conclude with that of Passover, as Rosh wrote there; and this is what he wrote "not in the middle"; and see Rosh there — source.

(ד) And they practice. Because it speaks of the exodus from Egypt. And thus Darkei Moshe wrote: Avudraham wrote that the world practiced to read on the festival of Matzot Shir etc. that speaks etc.; and on Shavuot Ruth, for it is written in it the beginning of harvest, and also that our ancestors who received the Torah converted, and Ruth converted; and on Sukkot Kohelet, because it is written in it "Give a portion to seven and also to eight" etc.; and they say in the end of Megillah: one who reads the five megillot blesses "on the reading of a megillah," even if it is written among the Scriptures — end of his words; and Maharil likewise wrote to bless thus even if it is not written on a scroll; and so too in Hagahot Maimoniot, Laws of 9 Av; and thus is the custom, not like Mordechai, who wrote in ch. 1 of Megillah to bless "on the reading of Ketuvim" — end of Darkei Moshe. And so too Bach and Levush and Minhagim and Mateh Moshe: to bless even if they are not written on a scroll, and all the more when they are written on a scroll like a Torah scroll; and thus is the custom of all the early authorities; and so too Magen Avraham. And what he wrote "except Kohelet" — it is not so, for in Seder Yadaim the conclusion of the mishnah there is that they disagreed thus, and they concluded that Shir HaShirim and Kohelet are equal to render the hands impure; and so Rambam in ch. 9 of Laws of Hands ch. 8: all five megillot render the hands impure like a Torah scroll; and so the Gemara in ch. 1 of Megillah: all are equal for this blessing; and thus is the essential ruling — source.

(ה) On Shabbat. For it is a day of assembly, for even on Yom Tov they were in the fields, as written at the end of Rosh HaShanah 55a — source.
