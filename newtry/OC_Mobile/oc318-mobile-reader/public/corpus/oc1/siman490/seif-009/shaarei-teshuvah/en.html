(In the Shulchan Aruch, se'if 9) He prays in his usual manner. And the Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Maharay Molcho: if in Shacharis of Shabbos and Chol HaMoed he sealed "Who sanctifies the Shabbos and Israel and the seasons," he need not return; and if he sealed "Israel and the seasons" and did not mention Shabbos, he did not fulfill his obligation; and if within toch kedei dibbur he went back and said "Who sanctifies the Shabbos," he fulfilled. And the Birkei Yosef wrote that if in the order of Musaf he prayed only the Musaf of Shabbos alone, he did not fulfill; and see Mayim Rabbim, siman 42, regarding if on Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh he prayed only that of Shabbos alone; see there. And if he prayed the Musaf of Chol HaMoed and did not mention Shabbos in the middle, but only in the seal he sealed "Who sanctifies the Shabbos and Israel and the seasons," he did not fulfill and must return. And likewise if on Shabbos Chol HaMoed he prayed only the Musaf of Shabbos and said Yaaleh VeYavo in Avodah, it does not help, since he did not mention the Musaf of Chol HaMoed; so too in the Birkei Yosef. But his colleagues cut against him in the Machatzit Hashekel, siman 238, and he brought the words of the Rama in Alfassi Zuta and elaborated, and concludes that in these two cases (where on Shabbos he sealed even though he did not mention [Shabbos] in the middle, and likewise where he did not mention the Musaf of Chol HaMoed but said Yaaleh VeYavo) he does not return; see there. And see Ginas Veradim, klal 4, siman 19, and Perach Shoshan, siman 3 and siman 4, from these laws.
Not to recite a blessing. See the Baer Heitev; and see the Responsa of the Rama, siman 35, who elaborated on the reason for the matter; and the Magen Avraham brought it in brief; see there.
