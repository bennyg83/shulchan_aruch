Not to bless etc. — so it should read, not as the second Krakow edition wrote: Rama in responsa siman 34/35 — Maharil and Avudraham and Hagahot Maimoniyot p. 5 laws of Tefillin wrote to bless, and so in Darkhei Moshe; therefore I was precise and wrote "and the people practice" and did not write stam, and so we practice as in other places; and so it appears to me from several reasons that specifically when the chazan reads to make heard to the congregation he blesses etc., and so is custom of Minhagim; and see above that when they are written properly in rolled scrolls one blesses, but in our chumashim it is obvious one does not bless etc. — end quote; but Levush and Bach and Minhagim and Mateh Moshe wrote to bless on all except Kohelet, and so is primary as our early custom.
