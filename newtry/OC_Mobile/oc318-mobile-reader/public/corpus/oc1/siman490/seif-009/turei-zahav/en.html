(א) And Morphein was on me. In the column, he said, “I heard from the wise that the resurrection of the dead will be in San and the triumph of Gogo and Gothic in the chains of the dry bones and the chains of the day of Gogo:

(ב) And they do not mention Passover in the haftarah blessing etc. For were it not Shabbat, there is no haftarah on chol hamoed:

(ג) And the people practiced not to bless on them etc. Levush wondered at this and said, "I do not know why they should not bless on them"; and furthermore, who told him the custom? Perhaps they make the blessing in a whisper, or perhaps they did not know from what is written in Minhagim that one should bless on these two megillot. And in the new edition of Shulchan Aruch the editor emended here "and the people practiced to bless" — and such are empty words; for in responsa of Rama siman 35 he expanded on this and explained his words that are here; and what appears to me is not to bless on any megillah except Megillat Esther, because we do not find in the words of the Talmud that they enacted to read them that one should say "and He commanded us to read" — he expanded on this; and the essential reason is that even on Megillat Esther, which one is fully obligated to read, nevertheless one blesses only on a fit megillah; but if one reads it in a chumash, as written siman 691, one does not bless on it — a fortiori on these megillot, whose obligation is not clear, only custom, and they are printed in chumashim on which one may not bless; and if blessing were applicable to them, they would have been careful to write them in the manner of a megillah on parchment like Megillat Esther — and this was never heard; and so Beit Yosef siman 559 — end quote: and the people did not practice to bless on any megillah except Megillat Esther; therefore whoever blesses on these megillot — it is a blessing in vain:
