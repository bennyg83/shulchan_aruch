(s.k. 9) Not, etc. Therefore I was precise and wrote "and the people practiced," which implies that the people practiced thus of their own accord. And so it appears to me that the custom of the people, which they instituted of themselves, is as proper practice.
And one may further say, etc. But regarding our Chumashim, it is obvious that one should not recite a blessing.
And according to this reason, one should also not recite a blessing over Eichah; but this reason is not primary, as the Magen Avraham wrote above in siman 284, that print is proper writing; and even though it is on paper, nevertheless one should recite a blessing; see there.
Except for Koheles: also in Masseches Sofrim, chapter 14, he mentioned blessing only over four megillos (Esther, Eichah, Shir HaShirim, and Ruth), and Koheles was not mentioned. But I do not know and have not found a reason to distinguish, except that in the Taz he wrote in the name of Sefer Lechem Rav: since they say in Shabbos, chapter Bameh Madlikin, that at first the Sages sought to sequester Sefer Koheles.
And so is the main [ruling]. And in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah he wrote to recite a blessing without the Divine Name and kingship.
