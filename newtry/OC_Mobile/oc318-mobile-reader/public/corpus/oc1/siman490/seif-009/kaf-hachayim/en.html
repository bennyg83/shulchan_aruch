(ט) And regarding Mizmor LeTodah — see above siman 51 in the gloss and in our words there in brief. And regarding putting on tefillin on chol hamoed — see above siman 31 seif 2 and in our words there in brief. And regarding removing them for those who practice putting on tefillin on chol hamoed — see above siman 25 in the gloss; and Chayei Adam wrote note 2 that thus the custom spread based on our teacher Rama z"l to remove them only before musaf prayer, and one should not change — see there. And so Chok Yosef note 7; even though on Sukkot chol hamoed they practice removing tefillin before Hallel, on Passover they practice as Rama ruled in siman 25 to remove them before musaf prayer; and Sukkot is different because of taking the lulav and etrog, so tefillin should not be an interruption — end of his words. Nevertheless Mishnat Hasidim wrote note 1 that the world practices to remove them both on Passover and on Sukkot before Hallel — see there.
