(א) (d) And he was on me, which is said to be the case for the resurrection of the dead. And the taste that the resurrection of the dead shall be in the midst of the bones of the land, and shall be evaporated by the people of the land, and shall be evaporated by thee:
(ב) And there is no mention of it, and so on, that there is no mercy on the land of the earth, but only for the Sabbaths of the year:
(ג) (טז) And not in the conclusion — that is, he does not conclude except "Who sanctifies Shabbat"; and the later authorities wrote that on Shabbat chol hamoed of Sukkot he concludes "Who sanctifies Shabbat, Israel, and the seasons," and likewise mentions that of Sukkot in the middle of the blessing, like on the first Yom Tov of Sukkot; and the reason is that each day of Sukkot chol hamoed is like a moed in its own right, because they differ in the musaf offerings:
(4) (17) And it is customary to say Shir HaShirim etc. — because it explicitly discusses the exodus from Egypt; and on Sukkot Kohelet because they are days of joy, and it is written in Kohelet "and of joy, what does it accomplish"; and Ruth on Shavuot, for it is stated in Yalkut Ruth: what is Ruth doing with Atzeret? To teach that Torah was given only through suffering and poverty etc., and they practiced to read it on the second day.
(ה) (יח) On the Shabbat of chol hamoed — because the piyyutim of that Shabbat are founded on Shir HaShirim, as above:
(ו) (יט) And the people practice etc. — and Taz agreed, and Magen Avraham agreed with those poskim who require blessing on all of them except Kohelet; and the Gra in his Biur wrote that even on Kohelet one should bless; therefore one who practices to bless at least when they are written on parchment — certainly one should not protest him.
