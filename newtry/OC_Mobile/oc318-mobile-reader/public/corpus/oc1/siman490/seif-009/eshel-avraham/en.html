And in Musaf he says: regarding one who prayed the Musaf of Shabbos on Shabbos Chol HaMoed and mentioned the substance of the occasion in Avodah, he ruled in Responsa Eretz Yisrael, siman 13, that he fulfills with this; and so it is in the Maggid Mishneh, siman 679: if in Shacharis of Shabbos Chol HaMoed he prayed "You chose us" and mentioned that of Shabbos, he need not be made to return. And this certainly refers to a case where he did not seal with that of Shabbos; for otherwise, what novelty is there? And see Shulchan Aruch, siman 268. And do not say: did he not mention the musaf offerings, on the principle of "and we shall complete [the offerings of] bulls," etc.? For he already fulfilled through what they read in the Torah; rather it was enacted because it is a day obligated in four prayers, and when this prayer was exchanged for that, he fulfilled.
