And in musaf one says: whoever prayed musaf of Shabbat on Shabbat chol hamoed and mentioned the matter of the event in Avodah — he fulfilled, as in responsa Avnei Nezer siman 13; and likewise in Magen Mishneh siman 479: if in Shacharit of Shabbat chol hamoed he prayed "You chose us" and mentioned that of Shabbat, he need not return. And this is certain, that he did not conclude with that of Shabbat — for otherwise what is the novelty? And see Shulchan Aruch siman 268: and one should not say that he did not mention the musaf offerings — "and bulls of our lips" etc. — for he already fulfilled through what they read in the Torah; rather it was enacted because it is a day obligated in four prayers, and when this prayer was exchanged for that, he fulfilled — source.
