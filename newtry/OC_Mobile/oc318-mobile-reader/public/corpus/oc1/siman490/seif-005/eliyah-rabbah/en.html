[5] [Levush] Why is it different to mention, etc. And now they practice to say "a holy convocation" (later authorities); and the reason is explained in Beis Yosef, siman 487: "a holy convocation" teaches that all should be called and gathered in the house of God in prayer. Furthermore one may say that in prayer one mentions only what is mentioned in the Torah, and melacha is also included in "a holy convocation."
