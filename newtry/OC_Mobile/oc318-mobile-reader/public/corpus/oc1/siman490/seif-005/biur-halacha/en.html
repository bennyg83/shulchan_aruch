The order of the parshiyos, etc. In the Peri Megadim in the name of the Pri Chadash: if one erred and changed the order on the four weekdays of Chol HaMoed, on the following day they read the parsha that was skipped. And the Peri Megadim was also in doubt regarding Yom Tov: if on the first day he read the parsha of Shor, why should he not read on the second day the parsha of Mishchu that he skipped?
