(5) How good to engage in the omer parsha — on the 16th of Nisan, which is in parshat Emor "He spoke... Speak to the children of Israel" until "and bread"; and in parshat Vayikra "And if you offer a meal-offering of bikkurim — first fruits" until "a fire-offering to the the Master"; then say "Master of the worlds" etc. as if the omer were offered in its time. Hagahot Y"N. Patach Eliyahu seif 1; and one may also say before "Master of the worlds" etc. ch. 10 Menachot — R. Yishmael says the omer etc.; and see above siman 429 seif 6.
