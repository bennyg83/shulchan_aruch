The siman of the parshiyot of the eight days of Passover: Mashach, Torah, Kodesh, Bekaspa, Pasol, Bamidbara, Shalach, Bachra — this order does not change except when Passover falls on Thursday, that on the third day which is Shabbat they read "See, you" which is Pasol, and on days 1, 2, 3 they read Kodesh, Bekaspa, Bamidbara.
