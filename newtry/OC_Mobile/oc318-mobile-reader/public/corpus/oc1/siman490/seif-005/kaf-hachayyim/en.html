5) It is very proper to study the passage of the omer on the sixteenth of Nissan. It appears in parashas Emor, from, 'Havayah spoke,' and, 'Speak to the Children of Israel,' through, 'And bread,' and so forth; and in parashas Vayikra, from, 'If you bring a meal-offering of first produce,' through, 'A fire-offering to Havayah.' One should afterward say the prayer beginning, 'Master,' and so forth, asking that it be regarded as though the omer was offered at its proper time. Hagahos Yafeh Nof; Pis'chei Olam, subsection 1. Before saying, 'Master of the worlds,' one may also recite chapter 10 of Menachos, beginning, 'Rabbi Yishmael says: The omer,' and so forth. See above, siman 429, subsection 6.
