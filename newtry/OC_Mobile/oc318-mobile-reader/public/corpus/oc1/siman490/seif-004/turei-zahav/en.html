Hallel with omissions. Since the Egyptians drowned on the seventh day of Pesach, the Holy One, blessed be He, said, 'My handiwork is drowning in the sea, and you recite song?' Since complete Hallel is not recited on the seventh day, it is likewise not completed on Chol HaMoed, so that Chol HaMoed should not be treated as superior to the last Yom Tov.
