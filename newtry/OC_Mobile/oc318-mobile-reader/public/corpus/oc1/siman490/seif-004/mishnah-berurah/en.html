(7) Hallel is recited with omissions, etc. Since the Egyptians drowned on the seventh day of Pesach, the Holy One, blessed is He, said, “My handiwork is drowning in the sea, and you are reciting song before Me?” Since the complete Hallel is not recited on the seventh day, it is likewise not recited during Chol HaMoed, so that Chol HaMoed should not be treated as more distinguished than the final Yom Tov.
