(ז) They read Hallel with omissions etc. — because on the seventh day of Passover the Egyptians drowned, the Holy One, blessed be He, said, "My handiwork is drowning in the sea, and you say song before Me"; and since on the seventh they do not say it, therefore on chol hamoed they also do not say it, so that it should not be superior to the last Yom Tov:
