[4] This Festival day, etc. We learn from this that Chol HaMoed is not called Yom Tov, and one should not say on Chol HaMoed "May the Merciful One grant us inheritance of a day that is entirely good" (Magen Avraham). I heard that when Yom Tov falls on Shabbos they say "entirely Shabbos, good and rest for eternal life" (Chok Yaakov); and we do not practice thus, but rather say each on its own, like Retzeh and Yaaleh VeYavo.
