(ד) "This Yom Tov" etc. — we learn from this that chol hamoed is not called Yom Tov, and one should not say on chol hamoed in Birkat HaMazon "May the Merciful One grant us a day that is entirely good" (Magen Avraham). I heard that on Yom Tov that falls on Shabbat they say "entirely Shabbat, good and rest for eternal life" (Chok Yaakov); and we do not practice thus, but each says separately, like "Retzeh" and "Yaaleh VeYavo":
