The last. Meaning, on the seventh. For a circumcision on the eighth they say "Yom LeYabashah," which is not so on other Yomim Tovim, where they do not say "Yom LeYabashah" unless they fall on Shabbos (Levush siman 584 and Minhagim). And if the seventh day falls on Shabbos, they say in the Yotzer the piyyutim of Shabbos Chol HaMoed, because they say Shir HaShirim on that day; and "Yom LeYabashah" they say before "Shirah Chadashah." And if the last day falls on Shabbos, they say the Yotzer of Shabbos Chol HaMoed and reverse in the Eighteen Blessings to say the piyyut of the seventh day; and if there is a circumcision on it, they say "Yom LeYabashah" before "Shirah Chadashah"; see Magen Avraham.
