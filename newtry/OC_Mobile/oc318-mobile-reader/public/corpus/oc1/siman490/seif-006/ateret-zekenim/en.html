On all days of chol hamoed they read the fourth in the Torah scroll and say Kaddish on the second Torah scroll, for then the day's obligation is completed — Rabbenu Yerucham; and it is good to remove tefillin on Passover chol hamoed before the Torah reading in the scroll, for reading is in place of offering the sacrifice.
