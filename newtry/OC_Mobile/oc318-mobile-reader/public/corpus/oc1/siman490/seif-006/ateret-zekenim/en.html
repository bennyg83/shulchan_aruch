On every day of Chol HaMoed, four people are called to the Torah. Kaddish is recited after the reading from the second Torah scroll, because the obligation of the day is completed at that point. This is from Rabbeinu Yerucham. It is proper to remove one’s tefillin on Chol HaMoed Pesach before the Torah reading, because the reading corresponds to the offering of the day. Some authorities hold that before reading Shir HaShirim one recites the blessing “concerning the reading of a scroll,” whereas no blessing is recited before Koheles because the Sages considered concealing it. This is from Lechem Rav. It appears that even before Shir HaShirim no blessing should be recited unless it is written properly upon parchment, like Megillas Esther. So writes Rama in responsum 35.
