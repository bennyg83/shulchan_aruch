The last — Ba'er Heitev; and see in responsa Chavat Yair siman 225 regarding what Ashkenazim practice, not saying "opan" on the seventh of Passover, and saying in the manner of a metaphor "and he removed the chariots of his riders"; and he wrote that one should not speculate after the custom, and there is support for the matter from what they said in Sanhedrin: on the night of the seventh of Passover they asked to say song; the Holy One, blessed be He, said, "My handiwork is drowning" etc. — end quote; and see what he wrote on the words of Tosafot in Eruvin daf 10 regarding "and the living beings ran and returned" etc. — see there; and one should be careful in the piyyut of the song on the last [day] of Passover in the rhyme "nerdam ve'avad" etc. — the masses regularly pause at the word "chasidekha" and wait at the word "nachit bechasdekha" until the chazan finishes, as is their custom with other rhymes; and it is a mistake, for here one must say the entire rhyme in one utterance — namely, also the words "nachit bechasdekha" that are linked above to "and the children of Your pious ones," as is understood:
