And the people — Taz's conclusion: one should not bless on any megillah, even Eichah, except Esther that is written properly and in its required form; and the rest, even if written on parchment, one does not bless. And in responsa of Rama siman 35 (it should be 34) he wrote: initially, on Eichah one blesses per the custom of Mahar"a Tirna z"l, since it is mentioned in the Talmud at the end of Taanit; and also because the chazan makes heard to the many; and in the end he concludes not to bless, since it is not written on parchment — meaning, even Eichah one does not bless; for if not, we would write it on parchment — from which it is implied that even written properly one does not bless except Esther; and so Chayei Adam note 11; and thus they practice, as it seems.
