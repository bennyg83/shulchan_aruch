(In Shulchan Aruch seif 9) He prays as usual; and Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Mahari Molcho: if he concluded in Shacharit of Shabbat chol hamoed "Who sanctifies Shabbat, Israel, and the seasons," he need not return; and if he concluded "Israel and the seasons" and did not mention Shabbat, he did not fulfill; and if within kedei dibbur he returned and said "Who sanctifies Shabbat," he fulfilled. And Beit Yosef wrote that if in the order of musaf he prayed musaf of Shabbat alone, he did not fulfill; and see in Mayim Rabim siman 42 regarding if on Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh he prayed that of Shabbat alone — see there; and if he prayed musaf of chol hamoed and did not mention Shabbat in the middle, only in the conclusion he concluded "Who sanctifies Shabbat, Israel, and the seasons," he did not fulfill and must return; and likewise if he prayed on Shabbat chol hamoed musaf of Shabbat alone and said Yaaleh VeYavo in Avodah, it does not help, since he did not mention musaf of chol hamoed — 22 in Beit Yosef; but examine Leket Yosher in Machzik Berachah siman 238, who brought the words of Rama in Alfasi Zuta and expanded and concluded that in these two cases where he concluded on Shabbat even though he did not mention in the middle, and likewise where he did not mention musaf of chol hamoed and said Yaaleh VeYavo — he does not return — see there; and see in Gevurat Anashim kelal 4 siman 19 and in Perach Shoshan siman 3 and siman 4 on these laws:
