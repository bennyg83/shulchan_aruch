(12) Kiddush is recited over wine. Meaning, in the synagogue. His point is that although Kiddush is not recited in the synagogue on the first two nights, because there is no poor Jew who lacks the four cups, that reason does not apply here.
(13) And the blessing of the season is not recited. Meaning, Shehecheyanu, since the final Yom Tov of Pesach is not a separate festival as Shemini Atzeres is. When a bris milah occurs on the seventh or eighth day of Pesach, the piyyut “Yom LaYabashah” is recited before Ga'al Yisrael. If it occurs on Shabbos, it is recited before “Shirah Chadashah,” because the custom then is to recite the piyyut “Berach Dodi” before Ga'al Yisrael. On other festivals, “Yom LaYabashah” is not recited because of a bris unless the festival falls on Shabbos. This is stated by the Magen Avraham in the name of the Levush. He further writes that when the seventh day falls on Shabbos, the Yotzer piyyutim of Shabbos Chol HaMoed are recited, because Shir HaShirim is read on that day and those piyyutim are based upon Shir HaShirim. When the final day falls on Shabbos, the Yotzer piyyutim of Shabbos Chol HaMoed are recited, while in the Amidah the order is reversed and the piyyut of the seventh day is recited.
