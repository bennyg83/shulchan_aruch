1) [Seif 1] Its leaf is elongated like a brook. It is not round, which excludes the tzafzafah. Rashi, Sukkah 33b. One may further say that this means it extends evenly, and that the top of the leaf is not broad and then narrows, as is found in certain trees.
2) Ibid. And its edge is smooth. This means that the edge of the leaves is smooth and is not made with repeated notches. Rashi, Sukkah 34a.
