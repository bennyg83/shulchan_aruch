In Magen Avraham se'if katan 2 And the Maggid Mishneh explained, etc. It appears that this was written in the margin and belongs to the end of se'if katan 1. He means that the Shulchan Aruch adopts the Rosh's explanation, that 'were broken apart' means they fell off; and about this he wrote that the Maggid Mishneh explained that it means they were loosened from the stem, which is not the same as falling off, as explained in the Taz above in siman 645, or that they split, etc. The scribe who inserted this into the text erred and wrote it here in se'if katan 2. The reference mark was omitted from above the word 'ba,' and it is an abbreviation for Ba'al Halachot.
