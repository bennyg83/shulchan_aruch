It implies that we require all three signs, and if one is missing it is invalid; see Bach and Levush. A sickle-like edge is considered like smooth. One can ask: if he has no aravah, should he take a tzaftzafah? According to the 'some say' that a rain-fed aravah or mountain aravah is invalid after the fact, see the Tur and Tosafot 34a, if so one can say that a tzaftzafah that grew in water is valid, for most grow in the mountains. But perhaps there is tzaftzafah that grows by a river, and so wrote Kapot Temarim; see Bach, and this requires investigation. If so, in any case he should take it without a blessing; see siman 649 in Magen Avraham 24 and 28. However, from the verse in Yechezkel it implies that tzaftzafah is invalid; see Kapot Temarim, and one can discuss this. And when the mishnah says invalid, one can say this means ab initio; see siman 645 in Taz 7. In Chiddushei HaRan it implies like the Bach, that tzaftzafah does not grow by a river; and this requires investigation.
