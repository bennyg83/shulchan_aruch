[1] [Levush] And from the fact that it is written 'arvei' in plural language, etc. This is difficult, for the Beit Yosef wrote that the Rosh inferred from the Rambam that he does not expound 'arvei' this way, for he holds that we require two aravot, and if so, we need 'arvei' for the two aravot; see there. If so, it is difficult, for the Levush also ruled in siman 651 that we require two aravot. I then saw that the Ran concludes from the fact that Abaye includes also chilfei gili from 'arvei,' that he holds the entire word 'arvei' is expounded. The Beit Yosef must be saying this according to the Rosh, who holds that we do not expound all of 'arvei,' as Tosafot wrote, Sukkah 34. But the Levush holds like the Ran, and this is primary, for otherwise the anonymous mishnah that validates a rain-fed aravah would be difficult from the later mishnah that requires two aravot; and it is forced to say that this is an anonymous mishnah followed by a dispute. This also appears to me to be the reason of the Rosh, who explained that without the derivation of 'arvei' we validate rain-fed aravah and rely on it even ab initio. However, according to the Rosh the baraitot disagree, while according to me they do not disagree; examine this. Regarding the question Tosafot asked from the Gemara's question, 'and according to the Rabbis, from where do we know aravah in the Temple?' it appears to me that specifically a new matter, aravah in the Temple, is not also learned from it; but all the other laws of the aravah in the lulav itself can be said to be learned entirely from it.
