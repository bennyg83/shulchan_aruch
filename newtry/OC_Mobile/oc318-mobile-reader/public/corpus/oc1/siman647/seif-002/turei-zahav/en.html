Or its head was cut off. Even according to that opinion which validates a hadas whose head was cut off in the preceding siman, hadas is different, for all its beauty is in its thickness, since its leaves cover its head, and the cutting of the head is not recognizable in it. This is not so with the other species, where the defect in beauty is recognizable in the cutting of the head. The Rambam compares aravah to hadas in this. I am astonished: from where did the Rambam know to equate aravah with hadas in this? Does he have the power to say so from reasoning, to contradict an anonymous mishnah regarding aravah where there is no dispute, unlike hadas? Furthermore, if so, regarding lulav as well let us say this, not to rule like the anonymous mishnah; what strength does lulav have over aravah in this? It appears to me that he learned it from the order of the mishnayot: first it arranged the law of lulav, then hadas and aravah, and afterward it returned to explain the dispute of Rabbi Yishmael, Rabbi Tarfon, and Rabbi Akiva regarding hadas that was cut off, and afterward arranged the law of esrog. This certainly requires explanation. Rabbeinu HaKadosh should have arranged the dispute of Rabbi Yishmael, Rabbi Tarfon, and Rabbi Akiva after the mishnah that speaks of hadas, or should have arranged the dispute after the laws of esrog; that would have been more fitting, to arrange how much each species must be after arranging every detail of each species. Rather, the correct explanation is that the tanna's intent in the dispute of hadas, where Rabbi Tarfon and Rabbi Yishmael validate cut ones, is that the same applies to aravah. Therefore he inserted the law of aravah among the laws of hadas. This is unlike lulav, where there is no dispute and all agree that cutting invalidates. From this the Rif, Rambam, and Rosh were supported in what they hold above regarding a hadas whose head was cut off, that we do not rule like the anonymous mishnah that cut off is invalid, unlike the Ra'avad. This appears very correct, with Heaven's help.
