Therefore it appears that one must be careful not to reduce the leaves from the aravot, even though one could distinguish between where they fell off by themselves and where a person plucked them; but it is not reasonable to distinguish this way. This is regarding their required measure, namely two and a half handbreadths. Above, siman 645, it appears from the language of the Tur in the name of Rashi that even if most of the lulav's leaves were plucked off it is invalid, and he did not use specifically the language that they fell off by themselves. The same applies to aravah. Therefore one must be careful not to pluck off most of the leaves of the aravah that is in the lulav. However, regarding the aravah of Hosha'ana Rabbah, see below, siman 664:7.
