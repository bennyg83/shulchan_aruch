Seif 2: A willow that dried, or most of its leaves fell, or its head was cut off — it is invalid, etc. We read in the Gemara Sukkah: its head was cut off on Yom Tov and a leaf grew on Yom Tov, etc. — there is rejection regarding mitzvot, etc. The examiner in the Gemara will see explicitly that if a leaf grew, etc. — see there.
