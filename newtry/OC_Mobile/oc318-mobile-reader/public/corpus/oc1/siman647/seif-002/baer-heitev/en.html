That fell off. Therefore one must be careful not to pluck off most of the leaves of the aravah that is in the lulav. See siman 664:4. One must be precise about this, for sometimes they fall off by being inserted into the lulav, and also through shaking.
That was cut off. Meaning that its wood was cut off. Terumat HaDeshen, siman 231. The Maggid Mishneh explained: if its leaves were loosened from the stem, or if they split and divided, its leaves are invalid. One should be stringent in all of these, for aravot are common. Magen Avraham. See Taz.
