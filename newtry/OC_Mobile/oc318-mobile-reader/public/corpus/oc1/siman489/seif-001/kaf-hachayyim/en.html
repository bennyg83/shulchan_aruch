1) [Se'if 1] On the second night, etc. The Ma'arivos associated with the second night are recited even if the first day fell on Shabbos. On other festivals, if the first day falls on Shabbos, the Ma'arivos for the first day are recited on the second night and those of the second day are displaced. On the second day of Pesach, however, the Ma'arivos associated with the second day are always recited because of the first counting of the omer. Levush; Magen Avraham, at the beginning of the siman; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Chok Yosef, subsection 1. The same applies when Simchas Torah falls on the night after Shabbos: the Ma'arivos associated with Shemini Atzeres are recited, although rain is mentioned in them. Ma'arivos are not recited on Shabbos lest an unskilled reader tilt a lamp. Eshel Avraham, at the beginning of the siman. All this follows the Ashkenazic custom. Sephardim do not have this practice.
