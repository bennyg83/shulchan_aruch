(In Taz, s.k. 7) And to this the Rosh there refers. This requires further study, for the Rosh there refers to what the Rif wrote, "and they recite Birkas HaMazon," namely when he first makes [the meal] in his own house; see above, siman 284. And even if you say that it also refers to when he first makes [the meal] in others' house, which the Rif wrote afterward there, nevertheless it is clarified there that he does not taste anything with them; so how can one say that he fulfilled Birkas HaMazon? And also what he brought as a proof from a lulav for a minor requires further study, for there, when the lulav is in the minor's hand, one cannot say that he [the adult] fulfills with it. And I found in Eliyah Rabbah that he sensed [a difficulty] regarding the proof from a lulav for a minor; see there.
