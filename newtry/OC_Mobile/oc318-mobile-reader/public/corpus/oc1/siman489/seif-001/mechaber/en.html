The order of prayer on the second night of Passover and counting the Omer. It has 10 seifim: On the second night after ma'ariv one begins to count the
Omer; if he forgot at the beginning of the night he continues and may count the entire night. It is a mitzvah for each person to count for himself. One must count standing and recite a berachah first, and counts the days and weeks. How? On the first day one says "Today is one day (of the Omer)" until he reaches seven days, then he says "Today is seven days, which are one week (of the Omer)." On the eighth day he says "Today is eight days, which are' one week and one day (of the Omer)," and likewise until fourteen days he says "Today is fourteen days, which are two weeks (of the Omer)," and in this manner he counts until 49 days.
