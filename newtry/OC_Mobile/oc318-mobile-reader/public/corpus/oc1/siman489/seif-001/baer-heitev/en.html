(א) At night. They say the ma'arivim belonging to the second night even if the first day falls on Shabbat — for other festivals, if the first day falls on Shabbat they say on the second night the ma'arivim of the first day and the second is postponed; nevertheless on the second day of Passover they always say the ma'arivim belonging to that day because of prioritizing the omer count, and so is custom.

(ב) To count. See Magen Avraham and Kaf HaChayyim — women are exempt from counting, for it is a positive time-bound mitzvah — Rambam; however they have already made it obligatory upon them. Magen Avraham.

(ג) On. If he wishes he hears the berachah from the shaliach tzibbur and counts for himself, as it is written "you shall count" — the count shall be for each one — see Magen Avraham and Kaf HaChayyim; if he transgressed and heard the count from another he has fulfilled if listener and reciter intended — Peri Chadash, and see Yad Aharon on Beit Yosef's hagahah. It is simple one may count in any language, specifically one he understands; if he does not understand Holy Tongue and counted in Holy Tongue he did not fulfill, for he does not know what he says and this is not counting. Magen Avraham.

(ד) Standing. For it is written "when you rise" — do not read "when grain rises" but "when you rise"; b'dieved if he counted seated he fulfilled. Rambam.

(ה) And to bless. That we bless on this count and not on counting of niddah — see Radbaz part 1 siman 27 and see Kaf HaChayyim; that we do not bless shehecheyanu on counting because there is no joy and pleasure. Acharonim — see Kaf HaChayyim.

(ו) And the weeks. If he counted days alone and did not count weeks, or counted weeks alone and not days — he must return and count with berachah — Peri Chadash and so Maharshal Levi O.C. siman 5; but Kesef Mishnah, Magen Avraham, and Kaf HaChayyim agreed b'dieved he fulfilled; therefore it appears he counts without berachah, for berachot are not me'akev. Keset HaSofer wrote in Maharshal's name: if he counted in acronym e.g. "today 4 days" or said "8 days which are one week and one day" he fulfilled — Yoreh De'ah; Peri Chadash wrote he did not fulfill Yoreh De'ah; if he said "forty lacking one of the omer" he fulfilled — Mahari Mulcho, Yad Aharon.

(ז) Today. He should not say "that today" is last. Kaf HaChayyim wrote one who says "that today" was not mistaken — see there.

(ח) In the Omer. So too Taz and the Ari's text "la'omer," and so Shelah.

(ט) One. He should not say "one" in feminine, for "week" is masculine. He always puts the smaller number first, e.g. one and twenty days; until ten he says "days," from eleven upward says "day" — elegance in Holy Tongue; custom to say Psalm "Elohim yichaneinu" after counting — see Kaf HaChayyim.

(י) 49. See what Rosh wrote in chapter Acharonim — some question: since the verse says count fifty days, Scripture's way at a round sum is to count in tens and not notice the lacking one, etc. — see there. Therefore question what the gemara wants in Kiddushin 38a: "Bnei Yisrael ate man forty years" — forty years lack thirty days — see there. Per Rosh there is no difficulty — such is Scripture's way; see Yad Aharon.
