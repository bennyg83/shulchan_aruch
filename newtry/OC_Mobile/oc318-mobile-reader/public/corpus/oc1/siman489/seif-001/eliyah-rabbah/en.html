[א] [Levush] He does not fulfill, etc. So Agudah in chapter R' Yishmael: "you shall count for yourselves" — for each one, not agent of congregation for all; so Rashba 126: if he intends to fulfill he counts without berachah if he does not interrupt — see siman 8 Talit. In Eliyah Zuta I questioned: "you shall count for yourselves" excludes yovel count entrusted to court as Kelbo and Abudraham wrote; whence that if one intends to fulfill for another and other intends to fulfill, he does not fulfill sefirah, since we hold shome'a ke'oneh for kiddush and mitzvot dependent on speech — unlike lulav "you shall take" — taking for each one, not speech-dependent; siman 658 I wrote perhaps learned gezeirah shavah from "count" to "take" — requires study.
