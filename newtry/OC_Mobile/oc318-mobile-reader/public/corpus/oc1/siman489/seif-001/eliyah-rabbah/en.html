[1] [Levush] He does not fulfill, etc. So wrote in Agudah in the chapter of R' Yishmael: "and you shall count for yourselves," for each and every one, and not for the chazzan on behalf of all; and so it implies in the Rashba, siman 126, that if he intends to fulfill, he counts without a blessing if he does not interrupt; see siman 8 regarding a tallis. And in Eliyahu Zuta I challenged: that which it says in the chapter of R' Yishmael, "and you shall count for yourselves," for each and every one, that is to exclude the counting of shemittah, which was given over to the court, as they wrote in Kol Bo and Avudraham; but whence does he know that if one intends to discharge his fellow and his fellow intends to fulfill, he does not fulfill the counting, since we hold regarding kiddush and mitzvos that depend on speech that one who hears is as one who answers? And it is not similar to lulav, where we say "and you shall take" so that there be a taking in each one, for there it does not depend on speech. And in siman 651 there I wrote that perhaps he learned it from a gezeirah shavah from "and you shall count" from "and you shall take"; and this requires study.
