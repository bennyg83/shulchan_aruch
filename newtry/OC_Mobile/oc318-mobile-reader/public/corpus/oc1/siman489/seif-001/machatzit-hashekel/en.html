1. Even if, etc.: belonging to the seventh:

2. And ma'ariv of the last day is postponed. End Sukkah 55a lists Tehillim of chol hamoed Sukkot; on sixth day they said chapter of Tehillim 82; when one day of chol hamoed fell on Shabbat the special Shabbat song was postponed: e.g. if second chol hamoed was Shabbat, sixth day's song postponed and on third day they said second day's song, etc., until sixth day they said fifth day's; always the last "they totter" of sixth day is postponed: unlike Pozna custom.
