(א) Even if, etc. — belonging to the seventh:

(ב) And ma'ariv of the last day is postponed. End Sukkah 55a lists psalms of chol hamoed Sukkot; on sixth day they said Psalm 82; when one day of chol hamoed fell on Shabbat the special Shabbat song was postponed — e.g. if second chol hamoed was Shabbat, sixth day's song postponed and on third day they said second day's song, etc., until sixth day they said fifth day's; always the last "they totter" of sixth day is postponed — unlike Pozna custom.
