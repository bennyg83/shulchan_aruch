[2] Standing, etc. And if he counted while sitting, he has fulfilled (Rambam and Rokeach). Further there: women and slaves are exempt from counting, for it is a positive time-bound mitzvah; and Magen Avraham wrote: however, they have already assumed it upon themselves as an obligation. And some are accustomed to say the chapter of Tehillim "May God be gracious to us" immediately after the counting, as is written in Ma'agalei Tzedek.
