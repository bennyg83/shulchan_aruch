They count again after nightfall. If he counted during bein hashemashos, he has fulfilled his obligation after the fact. According to some poskim, one may even initially count during bein hashemashos. See Beit Yosef. Olas Shabbos writes that from plag haminchah onward one counts again without a blessing. Bach writes that the universal custom today is to recite the blessing during bein hashemashos, but before bein hashemashos it is obvious that one may not recite the blessing, even on Erev Shabbos, contrary to Hagahos Minhagim.
