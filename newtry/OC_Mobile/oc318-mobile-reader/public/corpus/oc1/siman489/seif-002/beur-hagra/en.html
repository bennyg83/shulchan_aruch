(ב) If they erred etc. — as written in Berachot 27b: the community is different etc., unlike here where the community is not required

And the punctilious etc. — as written at the beginning of Berachot and at the end of chapter 2 of Megillah; and we rule until stars emerge etc.; and to exclude the view of Tosafot in Menachot 66a s.v. zachar: that which is written since it is a rabbinic uncertainty, nevertheless it is not proper to place oneself in rabbinic uncertainty; and that which is written because of temimot — in Temple times per its law at night, and one should not be stringent in our time more.
