(יד) And the punctilious — meaning by law it was possible to be lenient to count from darkening even before stars since twilight is rabbinic uncertainty and we follow leniency in rabbinic uncertainty in counting nowadays d'rabbanan for most poskim; nevertheless not proper to place oneself in uncertainty l'chatchila — therefore punctilious wait until stars which is certainly night

(טו) And so is proper — meaning l'chatchila; nevertheless b'dieved if he blessed in twilight he fulfilled as above; Avodat Yom Tov questions and sides proper he return and count at stars without blessing
