(12) They go back, etc.: This is from the words of the Rashba in a responsum, siman 154, and it is clarified there that the congregation also prayed Ma'ariv beforehand; and regarding Ma'ariv they need not go back and pray because of burden on the congregation (as above at the beginning of siman 263, se'if 14); only regarding the counting we do not attend to their burden, for how can he say, for example, ten days when they are only nine, for that day is the ninth and not the tenth until the stars appear, end of his words there.
(13) To count when it becomes dark: And with a blessing, according to law.
(14) And the punctilious: That is to say, according to the law one could be lenient to count when it becomes dark even before the stars appear, for twilight is a doubt of night, and we follow leniency in a rabbinic doubt regarding counting nowadays, which is rabbinic according to most poskim; but nevertheless it is not proper to enter oneself into a doubt l'chatchila, and therefore the punctilious wait until the stars appear, which is definitely night.
(15) And so it is proper to do: That is to say, l'chatchila; and nevertheless after the fact, if he blessed during twilight, he has fulfilled, as above. But Eliyah Rabbah questions this and tends that it is proper that he go back and count at the appearance of the stars without a blessing.
