2) There. The counting of the omer begins. Kesef Mishneh, chapter 6 of Hilchos Sukkah, asks how a blessing can be recited over counting on the second day of Yom Tov without concern for diminishing the sanctity of Yom Tov. For this reason, no blessing is recited over sitting in the sukkah on Shemini Atzeres. He answers that there one says over the cup, 'This day of Shemini Atzeres,' and reciting a blessing over the sukkah would be self-contradictory, unlike counting. Alternatively, since people outside Eretz Yisrael are not expert in determining the new month, by strict law they need not sit in the sukkah on the eighth day, and therefore no blessing is recited. Here the reverse is true: by strict law, that day must be counted. See there. Magen Avraham, subsection 1, cites this. Ran at the end of Sukkah writes likewise. See Chok Yaakov, subsection 2, who gives additional resolutions.
