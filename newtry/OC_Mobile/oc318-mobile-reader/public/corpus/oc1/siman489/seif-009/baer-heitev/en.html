Tiskabel. Such is the custom.
Of Pesach. On the second day of Pesach, however, Kiddush is not recited in the synagogue at all, as explained above in siman 487. Taz writes: It appears to me that on the final night of Yom Tov one should always count before Kiddush. It is preferable to prolong the sanctity of the seventh day, which is from the Torah, rather than the sanctity of the final Yom Tov, which is Rabbinic. Chok Yaakov writes that this is contrary to Rama’s implication, which indicates that one always counts after Kiddush unless the night falls on Motzaei Shabbos. Such is the accepted custom.
