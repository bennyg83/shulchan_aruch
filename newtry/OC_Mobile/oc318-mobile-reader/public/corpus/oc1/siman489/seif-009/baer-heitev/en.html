(א) Kiddush. Because entry of Shabbat pleases us more; but at home he counts before kiddush, for forbidden to eat before counting — Acharonim.

(ב) Titkabel. And so is custom.

(ג) Of Passover. But the second day of Passover there is no kiddush at all in the synagogue as siman 487; Taz wrote it appears on the last Yom Tov night one always counts before kiddush of the last Yom Tov, for better to draw sanctity of the seventh day which is from Torah, and sanctity of the last Yom Tov is rabbinic — end quote; Kaf HaChayyim wrote this is against the Rav's implication that one always counts after kiddush unless Motzei Shabbat, and so is custom.
