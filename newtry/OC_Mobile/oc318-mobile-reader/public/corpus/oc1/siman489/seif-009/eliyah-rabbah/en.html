[ט] [Levush] To the day they left, etc. If you say count from day one — it appears day one was entirely for remembering the great miracle of Exodus, proof of creation and Divine providence — we should not mix other matters in our joy. Do not say "today such-and-such" on second Passover night lest the count be unfit to say "second day"; therefore fixed ba'omer as remembrance we believe the Omnipresent renews grain yearly (Sefer HaChinuch).
