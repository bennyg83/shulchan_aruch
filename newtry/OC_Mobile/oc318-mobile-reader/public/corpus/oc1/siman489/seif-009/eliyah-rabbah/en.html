Levush: Corresponding to the day on which they left, etc. One might ask why the count does not begin on the first day of Pesach. It appears to me that the entire first day was designated exclusively for recalling the great miracle of the Exodus from Egypt, which demonstrates the creation of the world and the providence of Hashem. We should not mix anything else into that joy by mentioning another matter together with it. One also cannot say, “Today is such and such a day of the second day of Pesach,” because this would not be an appropriate count. It should instead say, “the second day.” The formulation “of the Omer” was therefore instituted, because the Omer commemorates our belief that the Holy One, blessed be He, renews the grain each year to sustain us. Sefer HaChinuch.
