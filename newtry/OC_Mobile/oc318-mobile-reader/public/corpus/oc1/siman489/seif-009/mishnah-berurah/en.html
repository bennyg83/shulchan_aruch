(39) After kiddush in the synagogue: For whatever we can advance of the sanctity of the day, we should advance; and if he counts in his house he should count before kiddush, for it is forbidden to eat before the counting, as above.
(40) Before havdalah: For taking the day out, we defer it as much as possible.
(41) After Kaddish Tiskabel: That is to say, what they say after "Vehi Noam" and "Ve'atah Kadosh," for then is the end of the prayer; for the first Kaddish is not a conclusion, since it is not a complete Kaddish. And "Yiten Lecha" they say after the counting.
(42) And when the last Yom Tov falls, etc.: Therefore he specified the last Yom Tov, for the seventh of Pesach does not fall on Motza'ei Shabbos; and on the second night of Pesach they do not make kiddush in the synagogue, as is written in siman 487 [Gra and Chok Yaakov].
(43) One should count before: As above, for taking the day out we defer as much as possible; even though here he accepts the sanctity of Yom Tov in his kiddush, nevertheless the sanctity of Shabbos is greater than the sanctity of Yom Tov. And the view of the Taz is that it is not specifically when the last Yom Tov falls on Motza'ei Shabbos; the same applies even on a weekday, that one should put counting before kiddush, for it is more preferable to defer the sanctity of the seventh day, which is according to the Torah, than to accept the sanctity of the last Yom Tov, which is only rabbinic; but the later authorities disagreed with him.
