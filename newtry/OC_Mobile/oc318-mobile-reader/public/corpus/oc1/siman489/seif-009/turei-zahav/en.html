After kiddush. Entry of Shabbat pleases us more; what is written "better afterward" is only on last Yom Tov of Passover on Motzei Shabbat when kiddush and havdalah combine — count before kiddush. It appears on the night between seventh day of Passover and eighth day of Passover, even on chol hamoed, one counts before kiddush of last Yom Tov, for better to draw sanctity of seventh day of Passover which is Torah, and last Yom Tov is only rabbinic. Appears simple: one who erred in the day's count and was not known until the next day — like one who forgot entirely; but if he erred in days but not weeks, the weeks still help and afterward he counts days in his manner.
