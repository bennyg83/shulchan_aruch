After Kiddush. We prefer to bring Shabbos in earlier, whereas on Motzei Shabbos we prefer to delay its departure. The exception is the last Yom Tov of Pesach when it falls on Motzei Shabbos, when Kiddush and Havdalah are combined, and one counts before Kiddush. It appears to me that on the night between the seventh day of Pesach and the eighth day of Pesach, even when it is a weekday, one should count before Kiddush for the last Yom Tov, because we prefer to extend the sanctity of the seventh day of Pesach, which is according to the Torah, whereas the sanctity of the last Yom Tov is only rabbinic. It appears obvious that one who erred in counting the day and did not discover this until the following day has the same law as one who entirely forgot to count. However, if he erred in the number of days but did not err in the number of weeks, this at least helps so that afterward he may continue counting on the other days in the usual manner.
