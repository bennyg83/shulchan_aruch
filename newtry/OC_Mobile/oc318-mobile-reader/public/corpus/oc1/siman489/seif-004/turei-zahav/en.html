If he tells him today such-and-such. It appears simple: if a friend asks how many days of sefirah and he answers "such-and-such days" without mentioning "today such-and-such," he certainly did not fulfill, for the essence of the mitzvah is saying "today such-and-such"; therefore this deals specifically with saying "today such-and-such." Why not answer "today such-and-such" — it is only a reply to one who asks, unlike stam counting that Beit Yosef in Rambam's name says fulfills without intent, except when intent is not to fulfill as Rama wrote shortly — not so, for we find in siman 484 Tur in Rosh's name: if they do not know to bless he reads word by word — proven there he did not fulfill; Rif there: if he wishes he does the whole seder elsewhere first — Rosh relies on this; further proof from siman 658: on first day do not give lulav to a minor before he fulfills — implies on other days permitted, yet when giving to minor he reads berachah word by word and the minor fulfills afterward — unless like intent not to fulfill; so too one answering a questioner — therefore here he warns l'chatchila to avoid this when possible; but if he happened to answer the count of this day without "today" he did not fulfill and must return to count with berachah; therefore better not to tell the count of this day, only "yesterday was such-and-such."
