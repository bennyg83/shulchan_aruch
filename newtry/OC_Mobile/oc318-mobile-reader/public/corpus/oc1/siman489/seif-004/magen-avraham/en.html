He cannot go back and count. Even though he did not say "of the omer," he has fulfilled after the fact; and even though he did not intend to fulfill, as is written in se'if 3.
And when the time arrives. This requires further study, for he should have said that a half hour before the time is also forbidden, as is written in siman 232 and 235. And it appears to me that this was the reason of the Maharshal, that he did not eat until after the counting, because they leave the synagogue a half hour close to nightfall, and he holds it is forbidden to eat; see in Matteh Moshe and the Shelah. And the Rama's view must be explained: since he has time to count the whole night and the whole following day, we are not so stringent; and nevertheless it requires further study whence this [leniency], therefore one should be stringent. But in a place where the custom is that the shamash calls to count the counting, one should not be stringent before its time, as is written in siman 232. And perhaps the Rama speaks of this case, and therefore he wrote "and when the time arrives," etc.
