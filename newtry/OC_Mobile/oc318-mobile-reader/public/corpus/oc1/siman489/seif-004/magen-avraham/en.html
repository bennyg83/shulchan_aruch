(א) Today such-and-such. If he said such-and-such without "today" — we have no concern (Taz).

(ב) Cannot return to count. Even though he did not say ba'omer he fulfilled b'dieved; even though he did not intend to fulfill as sk 3.

(ג) And when time arrives. Requires study — should half hour before also be forbidden as siman 232 and 235? Perhaps Rashi Lublin did not eat until after counting because they leave synagogue half hour before night and he holds eating forbidden; see Mateh Moshe, Shelah; Rama's view: since time to count all night and next day we are not so stringent; nevertheless question the source; perhaps where sun calls to count one need not be stringent before time as siman 232 — perhaps Rama deals with that.
