(א) Today. If he said such-and-such and did not say "today" — we have no concern. Taz and Magen Avraham.

(ב) With berachah. Even though he did not say "ba'omer" he fulfilled b'dieved. Magen Avraham.

(ג) Time. Requires study — should half hour before time also be forbidden as siman 232? It appears where custom is the sun calls to count, one need not be stringent before time as siman 232 — Magen Avraham, see Kaf HaChayyim.
