[ד] Days and weeks, etc. In Eliyah Zuta I brought Rabbeinu Yerucham p. 44: why not two berachot — days and weeks are two mitzvot like tefillin of hand and head; why say "they are one week" — say "today one week"; appears because "seven weeks" is written — week counting written only by omer, day counting not by omer — days are Torah even now, week counting without omer is only Temple remembrance, and for remembrance alone one does not bless as Passover wrapping; therefore "they are one week" — not real counting; if truly counting as in omer times berachah would return for weeks — end quote; Beit Yosef and Acharonim distinguished this month; fit to rule if he forgot days though counted weeks he must return and bless; specifically end of each week; mid-week some hold no need even l'chatchila except "today one week and one day" — though we do not hold so, do not return and bless — below sk 14.
