Seif 9: one should count before blessing. Source in Terumat HaDeshen; in my opinion one should discuss for halacha that Terumat HaDeshen may hold like poskim that havdalah is rabbinic, but per Rama's rulings above siman 471 seif 5 he interrupts a meal for havdalah — therefore he holds havdalah is d'oraisa, as Magen Avraham siman 239 wrote (and Rama siman 296 that two should not make havdalah for themselves is l'chatchila to be concerned for stringency of those who hold havdalah rabbinic, and women are exempt, but from essence of law men make havdalah for themselves, havdalah d'oraisa of "remember" at entry and exit, and women are obligated, for all included in guarding are included). One may say havdalah comes first; and though in all places counting comes first because of yoma adif over the view of precedence of d'oraisa, and proof from Kiddushin Nazir — even though Kiddush of Yom Tov is rabbinic as Magen Avraham siman 471, nevertheless one may say in Musaf of Yom Tov regarding counting there is yu'lei yoma in Kiddush and yoma adif in havdalah stands in law to precede d'oraisa; and truly what is written in Musaf of weekday also has proof to precede counting to havdalah from Kiddushin Nazir — there are two elevations: one yu'lei yoma of Kiddush and one yoma adif of havdalah — therefore they precede rabbinic Kiddush before d'oraisa havdalah; and though Rama did not emend thus and Mechaber appears to hold havdalah d'oraisa from his words siman 296 that women are obligated in havdalah — since Rama ruled here seif 4 the Posek from eating to counting and is concerned for those who hold counting today d'oraisa, therefore he holds here like Terumat HaDeshen they count first; but per Magen Avraham here sk 26 if he erred and counted in Maariv he fulfilled since we are not concerned for poskim who say he did not fulfill, and it is proper to count from darkening without berachah since not great effort, for he holds essence of counting today is rabbinic — then great proof is needed to precede rabbinic counting before d'oraisa havdalah, since against yoma adif there is also yu'lei yoma — uncertain.
