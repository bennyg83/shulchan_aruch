(There in Magen Avraham, s.k. 2) He counted himself. Marginal note: And it is not similar to taking a lulav, for a mitzvah done through a person's body, such as putting on tefillin and taking a lulav, one person does not discharge another; which is not so for a mitzvah done through speech and spoken words, where one who hears is as one who answers; see there Kapos Temarim on Sukkah, folio 41b. And that which we say "and you shall count" so that there be a counting for each and every one, that is to exclude the counting of the Yovel, which is only for the Great Sanhedrin in Jerusalem, as Tosafos wrote in Menachos, folio 65b, s.v. "And you shall count," etc.; see there. And see Chatam Sofer, Orach Chaim, siman 201.
