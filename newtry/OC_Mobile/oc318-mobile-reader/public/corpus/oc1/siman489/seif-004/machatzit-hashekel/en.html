(s.k. 4) And the weeks, etc. For we hold, etc., that it is rabbinic. And that which the Rama wrote in se'if 4, "and if he began to eat he stops": and if it is rabbinic he need not stop, as above in siman 235, se'if 2. Chok Yaakov answered: since there are poskim that it is according to the Torah, although we do not hold like them, nevertheless since there is no loss in pausing a little to count, one should also fulfill the view of the poskim that it is according to the Torah.
And if he counted weeks alone he has also fulfilled, as the Tur wrote in the name of Ravyah, that one should count thus l'chatchila. That is, until seven days he should count days; and when the seventh day arrives he should say "today is one week"; and on the eighth day he should say "today is one week and one day"; and he need not say "today is eight days, which are one week and one day"; and similarly from there onward in this manner. Although we do not hold like them, nevertheless after the fact one may rely on him.
