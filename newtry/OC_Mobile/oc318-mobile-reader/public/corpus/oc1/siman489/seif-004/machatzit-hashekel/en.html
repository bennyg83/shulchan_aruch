(א) (ס"ק ד) And weeks, etc. — we hold rabbinic. Rama seif 4: if he began eating he stops — if rabbinic need not stop, as siman 235:2 — Kaf HaChayyim: since some hold d'oraisa, no loss in stopping briefly to count — satisfy those who hold d'oraisa.

(ב) If he counted weeks alone he also fulfilled — Tur in Ravyah: may count so l'chatchila — until seven days count days; day seven say one week; day eight say one week and one day; not "eight days which are one week and one day" — not our practice; b'dieved rely on Ravyah.
