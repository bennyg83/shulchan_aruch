If a friend asks him during twilight how many days of the count are on this night, he tells him "yesterday were such-and-such," for if he tells him "today is such-and-such" he cannot return and' count with a berachah; but before twilight, since it is not time for counting, it does not matter.And when the time arrives it is forbidden to eat until he counts; even if he began eating he stops and counts. However, if he began eating before the time arrived he need not interrupt but finishes his meal and counts afterward (this is his own view for those who hold counting in our time is d'oraisa}.):
