(א) One who prays with congregation, etc. Rashba siman 235: one who said he counts with congregation while still day relies on nonsense of our Rabbi — all who count while still day did nothing; rather he testifies falsely; if he fears forgetting and relies on this count like Shema of ma'ariv — he already fulfilled sefirah and when he returns to bless it is berachah levatalah — end quote; so Abudraham concluded. But Rambam: counted and did not bless — fulfilled; Beit Yosef: nevertheless his repair ruined — perhaps he forgot first time and forbidden to bless again; why did Rabbis write this? It appears per his view mitzvot need intent — he intends: if I remember at night I do not intend to fulfill in this count, as Rama wrote intent not to fulfill; Hagah Maimoni: some permit counting twilight after ma'ariv; Bach wrote we do not hold so. It appears permitted to count after stars even before ma'ariv, even Motzei Shabbat — night is for all matters, even kiddush and havdalah before prayer; one fulfills Birkat Hamazon as siman 271, 293, 267; Sefer Chanukat HaBayit wondered why custom counts before havdalah while still Shabbat — no concern, Shabbat has passed, only melacha prohibitions until havdalah.

(ב) Intent not to fulfill. Question: even stam he did not fulfill, for he needs intent to fulfill as siman 60:4; perhaps since forbidden berachah levatalah, if stam forbidden to bless again since some hold mitzvot need no intent.
