[3] To bless, etc. The Ran in the chapter of Lulav and Aravah asked: how do they recite a blessing over the counting on the second day of Yom Tov, and we are not concerned about degrading Yom Tov, as they do not recite a blessing to sit in the sukkah on Shemini Atzeres? And they answered that we are expert in the fixing of the month, and according to the law one must count, therefore they bless; see there. And one should not ask: since he has not made havdalah, one should not degrade; for if so, on Shemini Atzeres, since he makes kiddush, one likewise should not degrade.
