Counts with them without berachah. Meaning his intent is not to fulfill thereby until he blesses at night at home, as Rama wrote — and I do not know what benefit he has counting with the congregation; for Abudraham it fits that he says at the outset: if I remember at night I will return and bless properly, so he did not bless in vain, and if he forgets he already counted; but Rambam himself wrote if he counted without blessing he fulfilled, and that is primary — so this condition is incorrect, for one cannot stipulate a condition contingent on remembering initially, for one does not count a mitzvah from safeik. Why count with the congregation at all? It appears so they not suspect he refuses to count; why does the congregation count while still day — are they wicked? It appears not precise "while still day" but in safeik of darkness as written shortly, and he intends to return and bless at night.
