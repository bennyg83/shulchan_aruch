(16) While it is still day: Some later authorities explained that this means twilight, which is a doubt of night, and they fulfill the counting then according to the poskim who hold that counting nowadays is rabbinic; however he wants to be punctilious and count from the appearance of the stars, as above. And the Mechaber says that he should count with them lest he forget afterward, and think in his mind: if I remember afterward at night to count, I do not wish to fulfill with this counting, as below in the gloss. And therefore when the time arrives afterward he should bless and count; and see Biur Halacha.
(17) Not to fulfill: And specifically if he intends not to fulfill; but with unspecified [intent] he has fulfilled according to the view that mitzvos do not require intent. And even though we ruled above in siman 60 that mitzvos require intent, perhaps regarding a blessing in which one must mention the Name of Heaven one must be concerned for that view.
(18) And he should bless and count at night: And even on erev Shabbos, when he has already accepted Shabbos and also prayed with the congregation, he should nevertheless bless and count at night, since he did not wish to fulfill with what he counted with them during twilight. The later authorities wrote that if the stars have come out it is permitted for him to count even if he has not yet prayed Ma'ariv, and even if it is Motza'ei Shabbos, for it is night for all matters, and even to make kiddush and havdalah is permitted before the prayer; only that he is forbidden in melachah until he makes havdalah.
