[י] They return to count, etc. With berachah; if from plag haMinchah onward return and count without berachah — see there, requires study; appears specifically after ma'ariv; before ma'ariv count with berachah; if counted twilight fulfilled — counting rabbinic, safeik rabbinic lenient; however per above Rabbeinu Yerucham days counting d'oraisa — fit to return count without berachah; Ran end Pesachim cites many who hold d'oraisa — therefore our custom not to count even publicly until stars.
