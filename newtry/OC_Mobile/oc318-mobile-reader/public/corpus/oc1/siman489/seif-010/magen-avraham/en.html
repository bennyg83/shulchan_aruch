Forbidden to eat chadash. Avi Ezri 293: Rama permitted chadash; Shelah: not applicable in Poland where most grain is sown after Passover; precise for barley and oats; wheat and spelt mostly Cheshvan — no chadash — see Shach on beer from chadash; Rama responsa: even careful about chadash need not worry absorbed taste, for all chadash today is rabbinic safeik; even if certain prohibition nullified in majority d'oraisa, all the more chadash which is only general stringency — Maharai: do not derive from majority to forbid, we follow majority of old grain; therefore little concern for absorbed taste. Nullified in majority d'oraisa — chadash reason nullified in majority d'oraisa only min b'mino; meat cooking is not min b'mino — needs sixty d'oraisa — Avi Ezri 98. Majority of grain — when safeik which grain; if clear from new grain, forbidden even absorbed taste.
