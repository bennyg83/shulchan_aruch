After — Taz: behold in Shulchan Aruch specifically when last Yom Tov on Motzei Shabbat — on weekday counting after kiddush; Taz by his reasoning disagrees and is stringent even on weekday; see Terumat HaDeshen 4 that on weekday one counts after kiddush — see there; Chayei Adam sign 21; and so is practiced when it falls on weekday on the eighth day they count after kiddush. What Taz wrote if he erred in counting it is as if he did not count at all and counts other days without blessing — unlike if he erred in days and not weeks he counts other days with blessing — Magen Avraham 4; and implies all the more erred in weeks and not days; implies even if it was day 14 and he erred and said 17 or 15 and concluded they are two weeks — he fulfilled; requires slight study — one may say specifically when it was 14 and he erred and said 13 or 15. What Mechaber wrote in seif 8 if he forgot to bless on one of the days — meaning he did not count — if he counted without blessing it does not prevent and he counts other days with blessing; see Levush and Tosafot Menachot 66a s.v. zachar
