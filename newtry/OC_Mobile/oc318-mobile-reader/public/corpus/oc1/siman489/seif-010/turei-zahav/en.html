It is forbidden to eat, etc. Regarding the law of chadash and its permissibility nowadays, see Yoreh De'ah, siman 294, where, with God's help, the matter is properly explained. This refers to outside Eretz Yisrael. The Beit Yosef writes in siman 481 in the name of the Agur that the meticulous among the Jewish people conduct the second night of Pesach exactly like the first night in every respect. One who wishes to eat and drink on the second night after completing Hallel has committed no prohibition, but it is improper for him to remove himself from among the meticulous, and he violates the words of the Sages.
