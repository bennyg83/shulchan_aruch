The counting begins, etc. Although regarding the Jubilee the verse likewise states, 'You shall count for yourself,' and regarding a zavah it states, 'She shall count for herself,' the Sages received a tradition that 'you shall count' regarding the omer requires an actual numerical count, unlike those other countings. Ran at the end of Pesachim writes this, as does Taz, subsection 1. See Tosafos, Menachos 65b, s.v. usefartem, and below, subsection 18, and examine the matter carefully.
