[7] [Levush] That he not say "that today," etc. In Eliyahu Zuta I brought several poskim with the wording "that today"; therefore those who practice thus, one should not protest against them. And likewise those who say "in the omer" or "to the omer," both have support from great poskim, and one should not lengthen [on this]. And see Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 23.
