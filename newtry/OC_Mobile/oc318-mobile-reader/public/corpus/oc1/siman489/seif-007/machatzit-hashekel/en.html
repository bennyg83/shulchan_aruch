(ב) Beit Yosef implies he holds as corrected — question, for Abudraham agrees with Rambam.

(ג) I do not intend to fulfill — stam he did not fulfill; Magen Avraham truth: even if Rabbis hold no intent needed, all agree if explicitly intent not to fulfill he did not fulfill; Rabbis in 489 hold mitzvot need intent; Beit Yosef 475.

(ד) Twilight after ma'ariv — since night for Shabbat, so for counting — Kaf HaChayyim in Pesakkei Tosafot Menachot: even on chol if prayed ma'ariv may count; congregation relies on Magen Avraham, Rashba, Abudraham — not Taz who deals with twilight; we do not hold like Pesakkei Tosafot.
