(33) He should count by day: According to the view of many poskim that after the fact daytime counting counts as a counting.
(34) Without a blessing: For one should be concerned for the view of the poskim that the time for counting is only at night, and when he blesses by day it is in vain. However, from here onward he counts every night with a blessing, and it is not as if he skipped one day entirely so that he no longer counts with a blessing, as below.
