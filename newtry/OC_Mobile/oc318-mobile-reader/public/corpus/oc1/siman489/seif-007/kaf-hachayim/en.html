(א) ז) There. Before counting good to say parashat haomer in Emor and Vayedaber... until "new meal offering to the Omnipresent," then Yoma R' Yishmael omer, etc. — see above 429:6, below 494:5. Then say formula in Rashash siddur — chasidim of Beit El follow Ari.

(ב) ז) For unification... I come to fulfill positive mitzvah of sefirat haomer days and weeks (night 49 skip until "repair its root") [as written count for yourselves from morrow of rest day... seven complete weeks until morrow of seventh week count fifty days] to repair root above. May it be will before You, Master of the universe... that now be time of mercy to raise this omer count as if attaining all sealed secrets therein with intentions of Your beloved knowing holy names in this count... strengthen in this count's segulah and two great luminaries with small, fill chambers with understanding, increase knowledge for the warriors of Heaven. Also give good and our land yield.

(ג) ז) And may it be will... repair what we damaged in supernal attributes... one unity (night 49 skip) [seven complete weeks] on one stone seven eyes (Shabbat and Yom Tov night skip "and forgive") forgive all sins from youth until today. As You purified Israel at Exodus from Egyptian impurity by omer count power, purify our souls from all impurity and illness, sanctify with supernal holiness. As omer stood for Israel shield in Gideon's days, Chizkiyahu, Yechezkel, Mordechai and Esther to subdue enemies — waving fought them — so stand for us against soul enemies and seekers of evil. Wondrous mercies, save the oppressed today. Arouse might for salvation...
