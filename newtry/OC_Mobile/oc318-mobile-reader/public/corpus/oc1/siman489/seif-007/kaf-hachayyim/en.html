The counting begins, etc. Before counting, it is proper to recite the passage of the omer in parashas Emor, from 'Havayah spoke, etc. When you come into the land, etc.' through 'a new meal-offering to Havayah.' Then one should recite chapter 10 of Menachos, 'Rabbi Yishmael says: The omer, etc.' See above, siman 429, subsection 6, and below, siman 490, subsection 5, and examine the matter carefully. One should then recite the following formula, which is written in the siddur of Rashash. This is the practice of the pious members of Beis El, may it be rebuilt and established speedily in our days, who follow the teachings of Arizal.
7) For the sake of the unification of the Holy One, blessed is He, and His Shechinah, with fear and love, and love and fear, to unite the Name yud-hei with vav-hei in a complete unity, in the name of all Israel, we now come to fulfill the positive commandment of counting the omer, days and weeks. (On the forty-ninth night, one should not say from 'as it is written' onward, but should skip to 'to rectify its root.') [As it is written: 'You shall count for yourselves from the day after the Shabbos, from the day on which you bring the omer of the waving; seven complete weeks shall they be. Until the day after the seventh week you shall count fifty days.'] This is to rectify its root in the supernal realm. May it be Your will, Havayah, our Elokim and Elokim of our ancestors, that this now be a time of mercy and favor before You, and that this mitzvah of counting the omer ascend as though we had attained all the wondrous and awesome secrets sealed within it. May it ascend before You together with all the intentions of Your beloved children, who know and direct all the intentions of Your holy Names appropriate to this count. 'The prudent will crown themselves with knowledge.' And now, may the power of Adnus be magnified through the spiritual potency of this counting, through the two great luminaries together with the lesser ones. 'Through knowledge the chambers will be filled.' May they contemplate the kindnesses of Havayah, and may knowledge increase, 'to the aid of Havayah, to the aid of Havayah among the mighty.' 'Havayah will also grant the good, and our land will yield its produce.'
7) May it be Your will, Havayah, our Elokim and Elokim of our ancestors, that through this counting of ours there be rectified whatever we have damaged in the supernal attributes: greatness, might, beauty, eternity, splendor, 'for all that is in heaven and on earth,' and 'Yours, Havayah, is the kingdom,' each one individually. (This too is not said on the forty-ninth night.) [During these seven complete weeks,] 'upon one stone are seven eyes.' (On the night of Shabbos and Yom Tov, one should not say from 'Pardon' onward.) Pardon and forgive all our iniquities and sins through which we have caused damage from our youth until this very day. Just as You purified the souls of Your people Israel from the defilement of the serpent, the impurity of Egypt, when Israel left Egypt through the power of the mitzvah of counting the omer, so too, in Your abundant mercy and kindness, purify our souls of every impurity and contamination that has attached itself to them, and may we be purified and sanctified with the supernal holiness. Just as the mitzvah of the omer served Israel as a shield and buckler in the days of Gideon (see Midrash Rabbah, parashas Emor, section 28), in the days of Hezekiah, in the days of Ezekiel, and in the days of Mordechai and Esther, to subdue all their enemies, and the waving fought against them, so may it stand for us to subdue all the enemies of our souls and those who seek our harm. 'Display Your kindness wondrously, You Who save those who take refuge from adversaries, with Your right hand.' 'Awaken Your might, and come to our salvation.' 'May the pleasantness, etc.' 'Havayah will complete it for me; Havayah, Your kindness is everlasting; do not forsake the works of Your hands.' 'I will call to Elokim Most High, to El Who completes matters for me.' 'I passed by you and saw you wallowing in your blood, and I said to you: Through your blood, live! And I said to you: Through your blood, live!' 'Bless Havayah, my soul. Havayah, my Elokim, You are very great; You have donned majesty and splendor.' 'He wraps Himself in light like a garment; He spreads the heavens like a curtain.' He then recites the blessing.
