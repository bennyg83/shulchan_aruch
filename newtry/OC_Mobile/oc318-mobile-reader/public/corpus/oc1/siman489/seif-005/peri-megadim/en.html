Until — Avodat Yom Tov and Ran; language is shortened here and it should read: most commentators wrote that nowadays d'rabbanan — therefore better to count in twilight that temimot be certain; and rabbinic uncertainty to leniency, unlike Temple times required certainly uncertainty of Torah; Ran wrote it is not proper to make rabbinic uncertainty l'chatchila (and see 466 Magen Avraham 2); and if temimot one should not be stringent nowadays more than d'oraisa that by necessity one counts at stars. Tosafot Menachot 67a s.v. zachar and Tosafot Kerisot 17b s.v. from the latter that in twilight it is established somewhat; and in Lechem Mishneh chapter 8 inadvertent 2. One may distinguish between them; and for halacha l'chatchila he should not count until stars; if he counted in twilight he counts afterward without blessing — so appears chapter 7 matzah 4 cups 4 — rabbinic uncertainty since no trouble — so too here; and in chapter Nishaley — see in Magen Avraham 6:7 I will explain further. On erev Shabbat in congregation those who delay is better than those who precede and say already night and a moment before say great day to accept Shabbat — I call: heavens darkness to light and that light in a brief moment to darkness — therefore proper to prevent this; holy Israel hear and accept rebuke and reproof — therefore for accepting Shabbat they precede and for counting they delay
