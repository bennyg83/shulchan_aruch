They begin to count, etc. Most poskim: sefirat haomer today only rabbinic — remembrance of Temple when they offered omer on 16 Nisan and counted from bringing omer — as Amemar Menachot R' Yishmael: remembrance of Temple alone, therefore count days not weeks; other amoraim there count days and weeks — not arguing on Amemar fundamentally but mitzvah in general Israel counts days and weeks — Rishonim. However Rambam ch. 7 Temidim, Chinuch: mitzvah of sefirat haomer even today d'oraisa; Rabbis of Rav Ashi's academy count days and weeks — argue on Amemar, not mere remembrance but d'oraisa — rule like them, infer from gemara language "Amemar said count days not weeks — it is remembrance of Temple" — not fixed halachah. Kaf HaChayyim 1. R. Zera 2: primary like those who say today only rabbinic. Chayei Adam klal 131 1.
