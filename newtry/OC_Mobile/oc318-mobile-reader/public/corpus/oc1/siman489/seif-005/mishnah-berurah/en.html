(28) And he opened: the blessing.
(29) And he concluded like it, he has fulfilled: That is to say, even regarding the blessing, if with that intent he opened the blessing and concluded, there is a conclusion and an opening; and nevertheless l'chatchila he should not bless unless he knows from the outset which day today is, for it is forbidden to interrupt even with silence between the blessing and the mitzvah more than the span of speech.
