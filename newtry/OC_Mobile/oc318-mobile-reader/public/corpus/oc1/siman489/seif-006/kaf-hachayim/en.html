ו) There. They begin to count, etc. I have seen meticulous ones on second Yom Tov night do the seder, and after entire seder bless on omer count — reasonable. However by law forbidden to eat until counting, and throughout seder likely to forget. Kaf HaChayyim 5. However Sefer Nehora Hashaleish p. 25b in Nefesh Hashaleish: diaspora must bless omer berachah second Yom Tov night immediately after ma'ariv, for sefirah is d'oraisa mitzvah and seder is rabbinic — each has its aspect — do not delay after seder lest give foothold to externals — see chaverim 2.
