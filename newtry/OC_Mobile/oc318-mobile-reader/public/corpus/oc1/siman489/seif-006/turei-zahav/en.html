Opened and said, etc. Tur wrote these distinctions in Abba Eli'ezer's name as a way of safeik; since there is safeik there is safeik for us; Abba Eli'ezer is stringent to bless again since counting is d'oraisa and brings proof from "emet veyatziv" for those who hold it d'oraisa — question: there he did not say emet veyatziv for d'oraisa; but here even if counting d'oraisa, berachot are not me'akev — unless since counting d'oraisa the berachah is also d'oraisa, proven there otherwise why return to bless if berachot are not me'akev. Beit Yosef: from Ran and most poskim counting today is rabbinic — we are lenient; therefore ruled here no second berachah; what is written "and concluded with four" means he counted four, not that he only blessed — questions on both divisions; first: he concluded properly and did not bless at all and counted — fulfilled, berachot not me'akev; Levush challenged this division. Second: concluded improperly — obvious for Abba Eli'ezer he did not fulfill, all the more Shulchan Aruch ruled fulfilled — Maharshal challenged. It appears they were not precise in plain meaning: Abba Eli'ezer and Shulchan Aruch "concluded with five" and second case "concluded four" refer to conclusion of the berachah, not having counted yet — dissonance in berachah words; safeik whether mistaken at start of "Baruch Atah..." or at end — safeik whether berachah is berachah b'dieved and he counts again without berachah, or berachah void as if never blessed and must bless again — like opening intent for wine and concluding for beer — Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch ruled for us (counting rabbinic) no second berachah. When intent to conclude as he hears from friend and concluded like him — conclusion is actual counting, berachah was proper at opening and closing and safeik is the counting conclusion; unlike beginning where safeik is in berachah itself and conclusion is in berachah — appears clear if entire berachah was mistaken but he remembered at time of proper counting he must bless again on what he will count — proof from teruma in gemara Tur and Shulchan Aruch siman 206: blessed on fruit, it fell — must bless again on what he will eat though before him, since intent was only for that he blessed on; so one who blessed on Torah shown wrong place must return — siman 140; not applicable here — safeik is in berachah itself like opening for wine ending for beer — not like teruma.
