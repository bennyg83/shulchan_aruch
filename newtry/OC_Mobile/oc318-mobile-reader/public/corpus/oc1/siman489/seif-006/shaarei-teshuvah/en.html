And the weeks. Avi Ezra HaTov: if he counted in initials like yud-alef, lamed-gimmel ba'omer — view of Gi'at siman 526 he fulfilled; Peri Chadash and some Acharonim, and in She'elat Yaavetz siman 239 agreed he did not fulfill; in Birkei Yosef he wrote his view leans this way and good to return and count without berachah; if he counted in initials with full letters like yud-alef etc., She'elat Yaavetz wrote he returns and counts without berachah — see there; see Birkei Yosef if he wrote a letter to his friend and wrote yom-tet-bet for counting — obvious he must return and count orally with berachah; see there laws of writing whether it is like speech he brought from siman 46 regarding Birkat HaTorah and Shav Yaakov part 10 siman 49 and Knesset HaGedolah siman 210 — see there; in my opinion clear that even if he wrote "seventeen days that are" etc. he did not fulfill at all unless he intended to fulfill through this writing — slight room to examine, and it appears here too he should return and count with berachah, and it is not like other places where even if we say writing is like speech, it is where speech itself is only to reveal thought of the heart, unlike here; and as it does not help if one writes Shema and prayer, and Megillah clearly requires oral reading as we say "if he was writing" etc.; see below siman 498; see Eshel Avraham who showed place for responsum on what was written on this matter; see Shirurei Berachah who wrote one who counted in initials tet-vav-bet that afterward he will be shaliach tzibbur and bless to fulfill others — see there; in our country we did not practice fulfilling through shaliach tzibbur berachah at all, only each blesses and counts for himself — therefore he should not be shaliach tzibbur and bless since he is only for himself and must count without berachah.
