[ו] Fourteen, etc. Smaller number first; where they count larger first — per local custom (Acharonim). Rest of Knesset HaGedolah lengthy; in our lands even secular tongue — smaller first. Permitted any language understood; if not Holy Tongue — do not count in Holy Tongue (Magen Avraham). Shibulei Leket: until ten "days," from eleven "day" — elegance in Holy Tongue (Amek Berachah). Some say Psalm after count. Maharil: at 49 say they are seven weeks.
