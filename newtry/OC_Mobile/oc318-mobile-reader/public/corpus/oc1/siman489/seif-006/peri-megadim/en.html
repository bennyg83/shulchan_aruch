Counts Taz and Magen Avraham 7. Taz holds Avudraham wrote one may say if he counts without berachah he fulfills from Torah, but from rabbis he does not fulfill in counting unless he blessed first, for thus they acquired to bless on mitzvot; and one who reads with congregation in Maariv without berachah lest he forget at night — at least fulfills from Torah, and if he remembers he counts at night with berachah; and for what he wrote afterward that for Rambam he fulfills even from rabbis without berachah, and so halacha in all mitzvot berachot are not me'akev — necessarily he counts in congregation and intends not to fulfill at all in this counting — what benefit counting with congregation and it is not pleasing to him to count conditionally "if I remember this is not for the mitzvah" etc. — such a view holds perhaps he does not fulfill at all; Magen Avraham and what Taz wrote congregation reads in safeik of darkness in Maariv — see letter 5; regarding him b'dieved it is because of amei ha'aretz who are also in synagogue and may forget at night to count; but in general one should not make rabbinic safeik for leniency as in letter 5 and see Magen Avraham 7 will explain further — see there.
