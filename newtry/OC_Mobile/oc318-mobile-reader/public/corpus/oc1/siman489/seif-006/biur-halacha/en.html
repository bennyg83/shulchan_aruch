Or the reverse that they are four and he opened etc. and erred and concluded with five etc. — see Mishna Berurah Acharonim wondered on this; Taz resolved Mechaber does not mean he concluded on counting itself but on blessing — meaning at start of blessing when he said "Blessed are You, our God, King of the world" he thought he would count afterward four and only at conclusion of blessing it seemed to him the day is fifth of the omer and he concluded the blessing intending to count five of the omer — he does not return and bless, for we follow opening; and in first case Mechaber depicted similarly — there reason we follow conclusion and because it is rabbinic matter we follow both to leniency and he does not return and bless — Acharonim codified this law (see Darkhei Halacha and Avodat Yom Tov); but Taz derived new law: where opening and conclusion were with intent to count day 5 and after finishing blessing he learned the day is day 4 — he returns and blesses; in this law several Acharonim did not agree — see Mishna Berurah and Sha'arei Teshuvah
