(ו) And erred and concluded with five. Bach ruled he returns and blesses, for he spoke falsely; so many poskim siman 209, Levush — see there; however it appears he does not return and bless, only counts again, for in one berachah he fulfilled afterward unless he paused greatly; if he remembered within twenty-four hours and said four he fulfilled even if he did not know initially they are four, and all the more if he knew they are four but erred in speech and remembered within twenty-four hours — siman 209, 487.
