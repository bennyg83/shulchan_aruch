And he erred and concluded with five. And the Bach ruled in this that he goes back and blesses, for he is saying a falsehood; and so is the view of the poskim in siman 209; and so wrote the Levush; and see what I wrote there. However, it appears to me that he does not go back and bless, only that he count a second time, for with one blessing he has fulfilled afterward, unless he paused a great deal. And if he remembered within the span of speech and said four, he has fulfilled, even though he did not know at first that they were four; and all the more so when he knew they were four but erred in his speech and remembered within the span of speech, that he has fulfilled, as is written in siman 209 and siman 487.
