(א) (L) And he said five days:

(ב) (No) and he was wrong and wrong, and so on

(לב) He does not return and bless — Acharonim wondered on this law; granted initially concluding with five when it is five is fine he counted properly although at time of blessing he thought four — nothing since even without blessing at all he fulfilled; but in latter case what helps us that at time of blessing he knew which day — nevertheless in his mouth he counted wrong day; they agreed in this he must count anew and bless for first blessing was in vain since he concluded after it different day unless he remembered within kedei dibbur of his counting then he returns and counts properly and blessing counts; know further although Shulchan Aruch deals when at time of blessing he knew day of counting and blessed intending to count properly but afterward erred and concluded improperly; several Acharonim agreed even if at time of blessing his mind was also on different day and he erred in counting — nevertheless if within kedei dibbur he remembered he erred he immediately concludes properly and fulfills thus; further they wrote if he erred and said today day four of omer and within twenty-four he remembered it is day five — suffices he conclude fifth in omer and fulfills thus even if he did not say today day five since still within kedei dibbur
