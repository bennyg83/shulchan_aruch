8) After the blessing, one should say, 'May the Merciful One restore the service of the Beis HaMikdash to its place, speedily in our days.' Tosafos, Megillah 20b, s.v. kol. One should intend that through the blessing he draws down a surrounding light, and through saying 'May the Merciful One, etc.,' which corresponds to the actual service performed in the Beis HaMikdash, he draws down an inner light. He should then recite the entire Ana BeKoach prayer each night, followed by the entire chapter of Tehillim beginning 'May Elokim be gracious to us and bless us,' while standing each night. When reciting the chapter, he should direct his intention each night to one word of the chapter: on the first night to the word 'Elokim,' on the second night to 'be gracious to us,' and so forth through the completion of forty-nine days. Likewise, each night he should direct his intention to one letter of the verse 'Nations will rejoice and sing joyously, etc.': on the first night to the yud of 'will rejoice,' on the second night to the shin of 'will rejoice,' and so forth through the completion of forty-nine days. Similarly, when reciting Ana BeKoach, etc., each night he should direct his intention to the initial of one word: on the first night to the alef of Ana, on the second night to the beis of BeKoach, and so forth through the completion of six days. On the seventh night, he should direct his intention to the initials of the entire verse, namely Abag Yatatz. The same pattern applies in the second week: on the first night of the second week, he should direct his intention to the kuf, the initial of Kabel; on the second night, to the reish of Rinas; and so forth. On the seventh night, he should direct his intention to the initials of the entire verse, namely Kera Satan. This pattern continues through the completion of the seven weeks. All this is explained in Shaar HaKavanos, discourse 11 on counting the omer, and Pri Etz Chaim, gate 22, chapters 3 and 5. See there.
