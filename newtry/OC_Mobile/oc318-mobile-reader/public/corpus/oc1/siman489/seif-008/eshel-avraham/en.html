Blessing. And if on that day he wrote the days of the counting, writing "today such-and-such days and weeks to the omer," he does not fulfill his obligation, for writing is not like speech; see Responsa Maharash, siman 20. And if he was an onen at night, he counts after the burial without a blessing, and the following night it is obvious that he recites the blessing; Responsa Devar Moshe, siman 33; and Yoreh De'ah, siman 228; and Yad Ephraim, siman 531.
