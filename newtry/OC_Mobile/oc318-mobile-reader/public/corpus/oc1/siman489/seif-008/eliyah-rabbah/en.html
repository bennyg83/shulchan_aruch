[ח] [Levush] We do not bless time, etc. Abudraham: world in distress from Passover until Shavuot over grain and trees — therefore commanded to count these days to remember world's distress and return with whole heart to plead mercy on grain. Another reason: each Israelite busy in harvest days — commanded to count to know when Shavuot; also allusion to seventh millennium all Shabbat; therefore seventh day of Passover is Atzeret, Shavuot after seven weeks, Shemini Atzeret alludes to world to come and great yovel — Rabbeinu Yerucham; beside the way I found ancients had seven planetary deities and Balaam offered seven altars — each has seven dominions totaling forty-nine — Torah given in fifty and commanded not to serve them — the Holy One tore seven heavens and opened seven lands to show no deity besides Him.
