ח) After berachah say "HaRachaman may He return Temple service to its place speedily in our days." Tosafot Megillah 20b s.v. kol. Intend berachah draws surrounding light; "HaRachaman" action aspect as in Temple draws inner light. Then Ana bechoach... each night standing; when saying psalm intend each night letter alef of Elohim night one, yechaneinu night two, etc. until 49; likewise letter of "yismachu veyranenu"; Ana bekoach first night alef, second beit, etc. six days; night seven acronym of whole verse abg ytz... second week kuf resh... until seven weeks — see Shaar HaKavanot drush 11, Pokeach 22:3,5. See below 106, above 1:22, below 494:8.
