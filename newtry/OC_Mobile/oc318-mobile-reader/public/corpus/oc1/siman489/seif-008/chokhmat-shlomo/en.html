Se'if 8. But if he is in doubt whether he skipped one day and did not count, he should count on the remaining days with a blessing. Marginal note: See the source of this law in Terumat HaDeshen and his reason and rationale there; and see my novellae on the laws of blessings, siman 215, and it is in my work on Orach Chaim from the year 5607, in a responsum to the community of Michanovka near Berditchev, on whether one recites a blessing over a double doubt; see there and examine carefully.
