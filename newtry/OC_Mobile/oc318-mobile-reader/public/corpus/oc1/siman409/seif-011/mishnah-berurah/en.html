(א) Tree or fence — the same for any defined place such as a rock and the like. If he specified the shevitah place on a mound ten handbreadths high or in a valley ten handbreadths deep up to a beit se'atayim, all of it is considered four cubits and he has from that place and beyond two thousand cubits [Gemara].

(ב) My shevitah beneath it at its base — of the tree; as opposed to if he said stam "my shevitah beneath it," which includes also beneath the branches; even if the entire tree is within two thousand cubits from where he stands he does not have from the tree and beyond full two thousand but less, as below.

(ג) Shevitah with declaration — they were lenient for one coming on the road, as below seif 13.

(ד) Permitted to go there at a normal pace — the same: permitted to sit in his place and go there tomorrow [two thousand cubits to the place of the tree and two thousand from the base of the tree and beyond], for since he could reach there by running from before day his words help to acquire shevitah there even though he does not go there now.

(ה) And specifically when he specified, etc. — meaning even where he could reach there from before day it does not help except when he specified, etc. The Mechaber did not innovate here regarding what he wrote at the beginning of the seif; his intent is as if he said: and specifically in the manner we described, that he said "my shevitah at its base" — he specified this place for shevitah and this place was within two thousand; but if he did not specify the place beneath it and the entire tree is not within two thousand he did not acquire shevitah there, for perhaps he had in mind, etc.

(ו) No more than two thousand — not specifically; even if from his place to its base is two thousand and four cubits he acquires shevitah there, for they give him four cubits for the place of shevitah apart from the two thousand; also the Mechaber's intent is two thousand alone apart from the four cubits of the shevitah place; and what he wrote afterward "but, etc., within two thousand" — the intent is within two thousand and four cubits.

(ז) If the entire tree is not, etc. — meaning even though its base is within the techum only part of its branches extend outside the techum, outside two thousand and four cubits, and he did not specify his shevitah to be at the side of its base within the techum but said stam that he acquires shevitah beneath the tree — therefore he did not acquire shevitah at all, for perhaps he intended to acquire shevitah beneath the branches extending outside the techum.

(ח) And he may not move from his place — meaning from within his four cubits.

(ט) Four thousand from his place toward the side of the tree — there is abbreviation here; meaning from this side of the tree and from the side of the tree and beyond toward his house.

(י) He has there two thousand minus twenty cubits — meaning on each side of the tree they give him only this measure [and it turns out that together with the width of the tree it is four thousand minus twenty cubits in total]; the reason is: in all the width of the tree, which is twenty cubits, we are in doubt regarding the place of shevitah and we rule stringently — when measuring the two thousands from the tree and beyond we measure from the beginning of the tree at his feet, for perhaps he acquired shevitah there; and even though from there to his feet is two thousand minus twenty cubits, nevertheless he may not walk behind him at all from where he stands, for we say perhaps he acquired his shevitah in the four cubits at the end of the tree from which to his feet is full two thousand.

(כ) At a distant place and did not specify, etc. — and even though it is within the techum from where his feet are, per the first view he acquired shevitah there and loses only the width of the tree as above; per Rambam, since he did not specify his place he does not acquire shevitah there at all but acquires shevitah where his feet are.

(ל) And it is distant, etc. — he acquires shevitah at his place. Here too he argues with the first view, for per the first view here he may not move from his place because he did not acquire there, and from here is the core of his view; per Rambam he acquires shevitah in any event at his place and has two thousand in every direction from where he stands.

(מ) Eight cubits or more — here too per Rambam even if all eight cubits were within two thousand from his feet — not so per the first view.

(נ) At its north, etc. — and if it was wider than eight cubits it is also possible to specify the place, such as the first four cubits beneath the tree or the second.

(ס) Part of his place specified — in the middle cubit, for necessarily he did not clarify it: if in the middle of the tree he clarified for himself the four cubits, behold they are from them; if from the sides he clarified on whichever side, behold it is from them.

(ע) And so is the view of the Rif on this — see Avodat HaRishonim who wrote the main view is like the first view; see Biur Halacha where we wrote his words are not compelling; in any event l'chatchila certainly one should be careful to specify the shevitah place precisely per Rambam's view.
