(א) Terumah and it became impure — for it is fit neither for a kohen nor for an Israelite.

(ב) Before day — applies to all of them.

(ג) Such as doubt whether it was placed — the same if he placed l'chatchila doubtfully impure terumah or doubtfully terefah — it is not an eruv; for apart from requiring presumption of validity we require a meal fit for eating at bein hashemashot, and here where there is doubt of prohibition it is not fit.
