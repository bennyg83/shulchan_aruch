At the end of the techum and it rolled, etc. — in Tur this wording is not mentioned, that he placed at the end of the techum; only stam it rolled outside the techum; so implies Rashi on "one who places," and more so Rabbeinu Yonatan on "it is not" that it is not specifically when placed at the end of the techum — the same if placed in the middle of the techum and it rolled, as long as it is still within his four cubits of techum it is a valid eruv, for it is considered as if he has not yet gone outside; so implies the conclusion of acharonim that there is no difference, and accordingly I wrote in Mishna Berurah; the Mechaber's wording flows from Rambam's wording, and we may say he mentioned the common case that it rolls from the end of the techum outside the techum; but otherwise if he placed it within the techum far from the end and it rolled until outside the techum the law is the same; however from Hagahot Maimoniot [there chapter 6 halachah 11] it is proven explicitly that Rambam speaks specifically when he placed it at the end of the techum: because his eruv acquires four cubits where it is placed, therefore when it rolled at the end of the four cubits it is not considered that he went outside the techum; but if he placed his eruv within the techum some cubits and it rolled outside even one cubit — per Rambam it is not an eruv, for he went outside the techum [like a person who went outside the techum] and we do not say his eruv also has outside the techum except where he placed his eruv there, for it acquires four cubits for him. And Rabbeinu Yonatan's question brought in Taz may be resolved — requires study.
