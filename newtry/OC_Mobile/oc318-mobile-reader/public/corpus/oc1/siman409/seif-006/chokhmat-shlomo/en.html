Seif 6: eruv in a cemetery. Marginal note: I will write here some comments. Behold, in chapter 3 of Eruvin daf 35, in the Mishnah: it rolled outside the techum, etc., and in Tosafot s.v. that R' Yirmiyah said — in our Mishnah too we should say uphold the presumption of a person's techum of his house, etc. Behold, I was asked by someone: what is the difficulty — perhaps here the presumption of techum of his house was impaired, since at minimum he placed an eruv, similar to what the poskim say regarding doubt whether close to him or close to her that the presumption of her availability was impaired since at minimum he threw kiddushin to her; if so, here too we could say so. And he said that he later found in Ritva likewise. To my mind I do not understand: for there, if it fell close to her she is already consecrated and it is not in his or her power to return; therefore the presumption of her availability was impaired. But here, even though he placed the eruv, behold at the time of placement he does not acquire an eruv in any manner and it is still in his power to retract at every hour before the acquisition of the eruv takes effect — it is possible it is not an eruv at all in the world yet; therefore here the presumption of techum of his house was not impaired, and this is not similar to that at all — study well. Behold, what is said in this seif — doubtful eruv kosher — see in my composition Mei Niddah in the haskamot to laws of mikvaot siman 140 what pertains to this topic, the source of this law; and likewise what is difficult on this seif about what is in Hilchot Mikvaot seif 71, and likewise what is difficult on the source of this law stated in this seif in the Gemara in Eruvin daf 36; and see what I wrote in my composition in the haskamot to Mei Niddah, still in manuscript — may Heaven grant I publish it — see there and study. And incidentally I will mention what was somewhat difficult for me in the words of Tosafot in the sugya daf 36 s.v. here with two groups of witnesses, etc., that they wrote: even though in chapter 2 of Ketubot we say establish two against two even in d'oraisa matters, here is different since now she is certainly impure, end quote — and it was difficult for me, why did they need to challenge specifically for the one who says two and two is rabbinic doubt; behold they could have challenged more — even for the one who says two and two is d'oraisa doubt, nevertheless it is difficult, for behold it appears there the reason is we do not establish against presumption; it is difficult, why in all doubt do we follow presumption and with two and two we do not follow presumption — ostensibly the reason witnesses are different, presumption is not better than witnesses; if there were another thousand witnesses it would not help, for two are as a hundred; if so, it fits where we specifically need two witnesses, for then since here two are as a hundred, presumption does not help; but in a matter where one witness suffices, such as in prohibitions, behold we say in Yevamot that R' Nachman said: wherever the Torah believed, follow the majority of opinions; if so, since we do not say here two are as a hundred, and if there are more witnesses we follow the majority, presumption too why should it not help — what is different from every doubt that we follow presumption — and it would have been a challenge to all, also their answer is not relevant to this question since when this doubt arises, when was she defiled, and there were not two groups of witnesses, we follow presumption of initially, for even though now certainly impure, from force of presumption of initially presumption of initially is preferable to presumption of now per R' Meir, as he holds regarding one who touched another at night; if so, also when there are two groups of witnesses, why is it worse than other doubt and we do not follow presumption — behold if there were one more witness we would follow him, for we follow majority of opinions; if so, here too it is fitting to follow presumption, for there is reason presumption is preferable to one witness and one witness is not believed against presumption — study and requires further analysis.
