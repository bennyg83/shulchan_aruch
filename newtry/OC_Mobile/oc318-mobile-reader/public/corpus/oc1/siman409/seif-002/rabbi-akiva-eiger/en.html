Magen Avraham s.k. 1 that permanent ground is not forbidden. Specifically for an Israelite; but a kohen may not make eruv, for he cannot enter with a box and tower, for we hold a thrown tent is not called a tent; but in an individual grave if it is permanent ground permitted in benefit, even a kohen may make eruv, for he can enter until the lip of the grave, for although a corpse seizes four cubits, since it is only d'rabbanan for recognition it is permitted via box and tower; therefore Magen Avraham s.k. 5 was precise.
