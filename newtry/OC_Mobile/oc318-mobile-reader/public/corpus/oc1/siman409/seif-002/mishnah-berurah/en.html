(א) So that it is possible, etc. — for the reason of eruv is that he is considered as if he established his dwelling there.
(ב) Bein hashemashot — which is the time of acquiring eruv at the beginning of Shabbat.
(ג) Anything d'rabbanan, etc. — and it is explained in the Gemara that even if he places his eruv on a tree below ten handbreadths and he intended to spend Shabbat below on the ground which is a public domain, even though there are two shevut matters here if he takes the eruv from the tree — one from karmelit to public domain [for the tree is a karmelit], and also that he uses a tree which is attached — nevertheless it is a valid eruv because they did not decree upon shevut during bein hashemashot; nevertheless there are shevut matters they decreed even for this, as below in the next seif.
(ד) In a case of mitzva — and eruv techumin is considered a case of mitzva, for one may make eruv only for a matter of mitzva, as below siman 415.
