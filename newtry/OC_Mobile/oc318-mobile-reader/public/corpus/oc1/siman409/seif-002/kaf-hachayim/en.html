Avodat HaRishonim, opinion 1, Turei Zahav, opinion 1 — what is called a built grave depends on the dispute of the city and R' Yeshaya there: per R' Yehuda even earth taken from the grave and afterward placed on it to cover the coffin has the law of a built grave — detached and later attached and is not called permanent ground; only one who digs a niche in rock over which the rock that was not detached is called permanent ground, and certainly the sides of the grave; but per Tur even detached and later attached, since it is grave earth, it is permanent ground and forbidden only for an actual built grave — see there.
