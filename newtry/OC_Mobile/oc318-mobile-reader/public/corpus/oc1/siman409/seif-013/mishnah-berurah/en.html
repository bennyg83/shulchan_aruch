(א) For one coming on the road, etc. — because he is weary on the road they were lenient that he acquires shevitah by mere declaration of what he said "my shevitah in such-and-such a place," unlike one who was in his house who acquires shevitah only when he goes with his feet and sits there until dark or places there food for two meals.

(ב) Shevitah of his house — in this even the first view above seif 11 [that holds he may not move from his place] agrees he has shevitah in his house, for since he stands in his house it is certainly pleasing to him to acquire shevitah in his house when his declaration will not help to acquire shevitah elsewhere, as written above end siman 408 in Mishna Berurah.
