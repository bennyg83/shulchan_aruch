3) There. After it takes effect, etc. At the moment the eruv takes effect, one establishes it for a mitzvah purpose, as stated at the beginning of siman 415, and mitzvos were not given for personal benefit. Magen Avraham, subsection 2; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2. Since one is pleased that it remains guarded there after it takes effect, however, one derives benefit unrelated to the mitzvah. Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 2.
