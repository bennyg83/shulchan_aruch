If he placed it on top of a reed or giant reed growing from the ground — it is not a valid eruv, since being soft and easy to snap would make one liable for a sin-offering, and they decreed regarding them even during twilight and even in a case of mitzva. If they were detached and stuck into the ground, it is a valid eruv. {Rama: (and if they are as soft as a vegetable, see above at the beginning of Siman 336)}
