(א) Since afterward, etc. — meaning: even though when he placed his eruv on a tree there is also shevut from using the tree, nevertheless it is a valid eruv because they did not decree during bein hashemashot in a case of mitzva, as above; nevertheless regarding a reed that is soft and thin it is close that he will come to snapping when he comes to take his eruv [and snapping from attached is the melacha of reaping], and they decreed even during bein hashemashot; the same law if he placed his eruv in a public domain distant eight cubits from where he intended to spend Shabbat — it is not an eruv, for they do not permit bringing it to him during bein hashemashot less than four cubits, since it is easy to come to liability for a sin-offering that he will carry four cubits of full measure in a public domain.
(ב) Like a vegetable — meaning they are very soft, so one may say the law of a vegetable applies to them and they did not decree regarding them because of using what is attached nor because of perhaps snapping; this was explained siman 336 — see there in Mishna Berurah s.k. 14 and 15.
(ג) And if they were detached — and deals even when higher than ten and standing in a public domain, only that their width should not be four handbreadths, for then they do not have the name of private domain; the same if four wide and only less than ten high in height, for they are considered only a karmelit; but if he placed on a reed that is nineteen high and four wide, which is a full private domain, it is not an eruv if he intended to spend Shabbat below on the ground which is a public domain, for there is a d'oraisa melacha in taking it.
(ד) It is a valid eruv — for in this case even if he snaps there is no d'oraisa prohibition; and some say there is no d'rabbanan prohibition either in detached — see Avodat HaKodesh.
