(א) (s.k. 3) In a beit hapras, etc., that was plowed, etc., and its measure is one hundred by one hundred cubits — the Rabbis establish one who plows does not move bones.

(ב) Bone the size of a barley grain. Even though it does not impart tent impurity, nevertheless it imparts impurity by contact and carrying, and a priest may not walk there lest he move a bone with his feet — which is carrying.
