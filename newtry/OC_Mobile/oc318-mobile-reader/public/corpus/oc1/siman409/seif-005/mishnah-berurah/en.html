(א) Whoever places, etc. — the place of his eruv acquires for him four cubits always around it, apart from the two thousand they give him in every direction.

(ב) In a place — meaning in any place not enclosed by partitions, for in an enclosed place we consider it all four cubits.

(ג) At the end of the techum — the same if he placed it in the middle of the techum and it rolled within four cubits; the same law even outside the techum.

(ד) And it rolled — from before day; the same even if he rolled it himself intentionally [acharonim].

(ה) It is a valid eruv — for those four cubits belong to his techum, as above; and if he can place the eruv l'chatchila within four cubits outside the two thousand it will be explained below seif 11.

(ו) It is not an eruv — for it must be placed in a place where he can reach it at bein hashemashot; since it is outside the techum from where he stands at bein hashemashot and he cannot reach it, it is not an eruv and he remains on the techum of his house, as above.
