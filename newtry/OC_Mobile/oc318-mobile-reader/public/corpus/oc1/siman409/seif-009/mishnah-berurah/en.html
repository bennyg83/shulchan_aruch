(52) For they did not specify a direction for him — therefore we say presumably it pleases them in whichever direction he makes eruv; and even if they were not informed until it grew dark it is a valid eruv, for the matter is revealed that from the outset they acquired shevitah. However it appears that if the agent knew the sender's wish to make eruv in this direction even though he did not specify explicitly, it does not help if he made eruv in another direction even if now he wishes to go there.
