And if — Eshel Avraham note 15: in pressing circumstances it implies whoever reached him — presumption he accepted from him; in Gemara 41b "perhaps he did not take from him" — presumption is clear even though he did not see that they gave it to him it is presumption; in Rabbeinu Yerucham chapter 6 halachah 22 — see there; requires study. In Laws of Terumah responsum Pappada I expanded: specifically when he informed the agent the two who send via unfit person — presumption, etc.; otherwise there is no presumption, for the sender will mock him and think he will send via a trustworthy person and he will not take from him and will return and tell, as Tosafot on "here" wrote; all the more if one said "this one and that one" — specifically when there is a stumbling block for a trustworthy person; and Amud HaMelach chapter 4 Laws of Firstborns.
