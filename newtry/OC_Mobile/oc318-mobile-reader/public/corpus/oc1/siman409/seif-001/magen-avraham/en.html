(א) Forbidden in benefit. It should read: it discusses a built grave; but if he placed it in ground of a cemetery it is permitted, for eternal ground is not forbidden as written Yoreh Deah siman 364.

(ב) After acquisition. For at the time of acquisition he makes eruv for a mitzvah matter, and mitzvot were not given for benefit.

(ג) In a beit hapras. This is a field in which a grave was plowed and we are concerned perhaps a bone the size of a barley grain lies there.

(ד) Priest. And even though the eruv becomes impure and he is forbidden to eat it, nevertheless it is fit for an Israelite.

(ה) In a tower. This is a large box one cubit by one cubit, three cubits high, and they carry it on animals; he can take the eruv in plain wooden vessels without a handbreadth width and bring it to himself — see Tosafot there.

(ו) He blows with his mouth. And if there is a bone the size of a barley grain there, he must see it.
