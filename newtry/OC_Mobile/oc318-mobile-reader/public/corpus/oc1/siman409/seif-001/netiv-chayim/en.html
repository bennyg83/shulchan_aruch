(Magen Avraham s.k. 17) Requires study in Yoreh De'ah. One may say Tur deals where the sender can come to a stumbling block of transgression, such as when he gave benefit to the agent — but the pile is known to the sender and thereby he knows if he separated terumah; in such a case there is concern lest another heard and separated terumah, yet we rely presumption an agent carries out his mission; but Tosafot, Raosh, and the hints deal where he did not give benefit to the agent, then he cannot come to stumbling block — from where would it come? Another would not separate terumah without the owner's knowledge; and even if you say because of benefit to give his friend the kohen — he would separate terumah; nevertheless they still do not come to transgression, for if he does not find the terumah by the pile the sender thinks he did not separate terumah.
