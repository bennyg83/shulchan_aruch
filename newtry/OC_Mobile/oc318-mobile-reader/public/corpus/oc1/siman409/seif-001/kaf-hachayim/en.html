(1) [Seif 1] One who places his eruv in a cemetery, etc. — deals even with an Israelite, as explained in Beit Yosef; however Raosh and Tur hold specifically for a kohen because he cannot reach it at bein hashemashot, but an Israelite acquires eruv in a cemetery, and so Bach and Darkei Moshe — see there; so Korban Netanel on Raosh chapter 3, and Avodat HaKodesh brought. Many Poskim hold so and concluded; therefore one who is lenient did not lose, for it is fitting to rely on these Poskim even not in pressing circumstances, for we Ashkenazim follow them — see there. However it appears for Sephardim who follow Shulchan Aruch one should not permit except for need and pressing circumstances.
