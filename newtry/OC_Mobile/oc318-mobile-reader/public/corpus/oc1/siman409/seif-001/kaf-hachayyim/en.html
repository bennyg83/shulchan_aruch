1) [Se'if 1] One who places his eruv in a cemetery, etc. The discussion applies even to an ordinary Jewish person, as explained in Beis Yosef. Rosh and Tur, however, maintain that it applies specifically to a kohen because he cannot reach the eruv during bein hashmashos, whereas an ordinary Jewish person can acquire an eruv in a cemetery. Bach and Derishah also hold this opinion. See there. Korban Nesanel on Rosh, chapter 3, likewise holds this view. Olas Shabbos cites several authorities who agree and concludes that one who is lenient does not lose, for these authorities are worthy of reliance even outside pressing circumstances, since Ashkenazim follow their rulings. See there. It nevertheless appears that Sephardim, who follow the Shulchan Aruch, should permit this only when there is a need and pressing circumstances.
