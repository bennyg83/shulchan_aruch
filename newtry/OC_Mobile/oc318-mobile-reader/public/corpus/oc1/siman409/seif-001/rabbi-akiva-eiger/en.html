Seif 1 — it is not an eruv. I wonder where we find that b'dieved it is not a valid eruv; in the Gemara they say only that he is not permitted to make eruv in this case since he wants the permanence of the eruv after acquisition he benefits; but regarding b'dieved from Torah law we should say it is not an eruv. The prohibition of the transgression does not depend on fulfillment of the mitzva, for even if we say it is not an eruv he still transgressed benefit since he wants the eruv to remain. These are separate matters and the transgression is not in fulfillment of the mitzva. Requires study.
