(א) Cemetery. Meaning in a built grave; but if in ground of a cemetery it is permitted, for eternal ground is not forbidden — see Yoreh Deah siman 364.

(ב) In a tower. This is a large box one cubit by one cubit, three cubits high, and they carry it on animals; he can take the eruv in plain wooden vessels without a handbreadth width and bring it to himself — Magen Avraham.
