Know what the Mechaber wrote "whether one coming on the road" — Levush wrote there is a difference: one coming on the road needs no intent, and one who went out from his house needs intent as siman 401; so Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham 10. There "if he uprooted a reed" — one may say intentionally — requires study. In Rabbeinu Yerucham chapter 6 Laws of Eruvin 24 — to bless "who has sanctified us," namely with bread; but one who makes eruv with feet on his words in his heart alone — we do not bless even if he says explicitly, as siman 431 regarding annulment we do not bless — requires study; one may say the primary enactment was eruv with feet and afterward they were lenient for the wealthy; if so one may say we do not bless, like R' Eliezer there; in opening Hagahot Berachot we wrote thus — see there; matter 395 in Avodat HaRishonim in such a case to bless — end; requires study; matter 415 Magen Avraham 4.
