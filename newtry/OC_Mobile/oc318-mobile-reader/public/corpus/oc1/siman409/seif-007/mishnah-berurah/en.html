(א) And to let nightfall find him there — meaning he should sit there the entire bein hashemashot until it is certainly dark [Chayei Adam; and proof from below siman 415 seif 2].
(ב) This is the primary, etc. — meaning he acquires shevitah with his feet by standing there when Shabbat enters.
(ג) Its mitzva — the same: he may make eruv with bread even l'chatchila; only the primary enactment is with feet, and in order to be lenient for him that he need not go there himself specifically and sit until dark they permitted eruv with bread that he need not sit until dark, and he may also send via an agent, as below.
(ד) Whether, etc. — meaning in both he need not declare and silence suffices; nevertheless there is a difference: when he goes out from his house it is specifically when going out intending to let nightfall find him at the techum in order to acquire shevitah there; but stam — meaning he left the city and nightfall came upon him within his techum without intent to acquire shevitah there — he does not acquire shevitah there but at his house since he is near his house; but one who comes on the road and nightfall came upon him — even if he did not intend to acquire shevitah there he automatically acquires shevitah there and has two thousand in every direction, even if he sleeps, as explained above siman 401 [acharonim].
(ה) Per his food, and if he is ill — so it should read. Meaning: for an ill person we estimate with his meals per what he eats and we do not require even six eggs; the same for an elder we estimate per what he eats; but for a healthy person we always estimate with average meals, as below.
(ו) Or a large eater — it should read "and a large eater," for it does not refer to the above but teaches another law: even though for an ill person we estimate per him, nevertheless for a large eater we estimate with an average meal like another person; this is because in both we go to be lenient.
(ז) Six eggs — with their shells.
(ח) Siman 368 — for there the Mechaber brought the view of one authority that they are eight eggs, and here he ruled stam like the first view; see what is written there that l'chatchila it is proper to conduct like the second view.
(ט) Bread or any item, etc. — as explained siman 386 — see there; not like eruv chatzerot where one may make eruv only with bread.
(י) That they partner in — and one who swore not to benefit from bread — even though it is not fit for partnership, as explained above siman 386 end of seif 8 — per the second view for eruv techumin it is fit according to all, for one may make eruv techumin only for a matter of mitzva and benefit of a mitzva is not considered benefit, for mitzvot were not given for benefit.
(כ) And if it is a relish — what is included in the category of relish is all explained siman 386. For the measure of two meals here one needs food for two meals of food or relish for each and every one separately; even master of house with master of house must leave for each the measure of eruv, apart from a minor son who needs his mother, as below simanim 413 and 414; this is not similar to partnership of alleyways where they make eruv with food for two meals for the whole city [see Beit Yosef who wrote in name of Hagahot: advice to make eruv with vinegar, for a little vinegar suffices for many people to dip vegetables for food of two meals — that is the measure in vinegar as explained siman 386 seif 6; however per what Avodat HaRishonim wrote there in name of Poskim that to dip two meals requires a revi'it of vinegar — therefore per the number of people for whom he places the eruv he must give accordingly in revi'iot].
(ל) And he says, etc. — but if he placed in silence, even if he intended in his heart to acquire shevitah there, it is not an eruv when he made eruv with bread; however even if he did not say this entire formula and only said "this shall be for eruv" it suffices [Chayei Adam].
(מ) As if he dwells there — for we are witnesses that if he had a place to dwell there he would also sleep there [Gemara].
(נ) Which is primary for them — and plainly: even if they were all bein hashemashot at the householder's house, nevertheless they do not acquire shevitah there but at their teacher's house unless they expressly intended to acquire their shevitah there.
(ס) Where they would want — so too shepherds who sleep in the field, even though they eat in a house, follow their sleeping place where they would want to eat as well if they could; so too harvesters and fruit guards.
