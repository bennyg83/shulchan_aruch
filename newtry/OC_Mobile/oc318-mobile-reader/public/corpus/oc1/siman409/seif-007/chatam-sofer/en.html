In Mechaber seif 7 each one per his food. Note: Rambam omitted this matter of ill and elder per their food. It appears he holds like Tosafot Eruvin 30b on "Targema," etc., that per the Rabbis they estimate for ill and elder too with an average meal — see there. Accordingly Shulchan Aruch's words come without any Hagah unlike Magen Avraham and Taz; see what Tosafot Shabbat wrote on this and his inclination favors what we wrote — see there and understand; see well my responsum siman 80 what relates further to the measure of a small handful — see there.
