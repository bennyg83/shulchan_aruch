(א) And if he does not wish to trouble himself to let nightfall find him there, etc. — eruv with feet cannot be done via an agent but only by himself [acharonim].

(ב) Per his food if he is ill — meaning we do not require for him even six eggs since his food is less. Some acharonim lean to be stringent that even ill and elder — their measure is two average meals, namely six eggs like all people [see Turei Zahav and responsum Chatam Sofer siman 98]; know that even per Shulchan Aruch we require at least that the food be fit for the ill person to eat, and if not fit for him we require at least two meals, for then it is eruv because it is fit for others [Machatzit HaShekel].
