And if they placed it in a beit hapras, etc. — it is a field in which a grave was plowed and its impurity is d'rabbanan, for we are concerned perhaps a bone the size of a barley grain lies there. Taz s.k. 1, Magen Avraham s.k. 3, Turei Zahav opinion 3. Measure of beit hapras is one hundred by one hundred cubits — the Rabbis establish that beyond this the plow does not move bones. A bone the size of a barley grain — even though it does not impart tent impurity, nevertheless it imparts impurity by contact and carrying, and a priest may not walk there lest he move a bone with his feet, which is carrying. Machatzit HaShekel.
