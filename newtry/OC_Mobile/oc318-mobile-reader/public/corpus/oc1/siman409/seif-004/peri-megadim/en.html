Within — Eshel Avraham; and so from Rashi Eruvin 35a on the matter of Rava, etc.; and even if it rolled intentionally it is a valid eruv; and if per Bach's reason specifically unknowingly — halacha is like Beit Yosef. One may say: if so why mention rolled — even l'chatchila he may place at the end of two thousand and four cubits the bread and it will be eruv — see seif 11. In Beit Yosef there he placed his eruv in private domain and he is in public domain within four cubits — it is a valid eruv, for he and his eruv are in one place even though forbidden to take the eruv; and it implies full four cubits between him and the eruv — fine here too; requires study for siman 396 "he went out and he is in the middle" — see there.
