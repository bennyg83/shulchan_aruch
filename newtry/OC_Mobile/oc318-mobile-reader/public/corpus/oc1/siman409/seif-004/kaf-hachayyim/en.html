4) There. If he placed it in a beis haperas, etc. This is a field in which a grave was plowed. Its tumah is Rabbinic because of concern that a bone the size of a barley kernel lies there. Taz, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, subsection 3; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 3. A beis haperas measures one hundred by one hundred amos, for the Sages determined that a plow does not carry bones farther than this. Although a bone the size of a barley kernel does not convey tumah through a tent, it conveys tumah through touch and carrying. A kohen may not walk there lest his foot move a bone, which would constitute carrying it. Machatzis HaShekel.
