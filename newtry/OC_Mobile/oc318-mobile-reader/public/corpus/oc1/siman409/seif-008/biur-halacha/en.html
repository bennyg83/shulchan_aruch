(א) Via an agent — even via a slave or maidservant [Gemara, brought in Avodat HaRishonim; meaning when he immersed them for the sake of servitude] — otherwise behold they are in the category of non-Jews.

(ב) And he shall say through this eruv so-and-so, etc. — and declaration is essential [acharonim]; and if the master of house said "through this bread that my agent places for eruv I shall be permitted to walk from such-and-such a place two thousand in every direction" — it also helps [Peri Megadim].

(ג) And they gave it to him — the Mechaber copied this from Tur's wording which implies he must specifically see that they gave it to him; but from other Poskim [Rambam, Rashi, Rabbeinu Yonatan, Riaz, Avodat HaKodesh] it is explained that in this case where he saw it reached him it suffices because of the presumption an agent carries out his mission — the questioner and answer "perhaps he did not take from him" — answer: presumption an agent carries out his mission, and it is proven we say he certainly took from him; I already found this raised in Avodat HaRishonim and Nehar Shalom and Gaon Yaakov; however requires study on Tur from where he took this wording, and in his own hints he wrote like all the above Poskim.
