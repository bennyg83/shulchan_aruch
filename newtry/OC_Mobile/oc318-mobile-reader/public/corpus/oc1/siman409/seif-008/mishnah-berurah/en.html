(א) Deaf-mute, imbecile, and minor — because they are not people of understanding and are not capable of acquiring shevitah; and even if he stands and sees from afar that they place it in the place he designated — unlike eruv chatzerot where the main matter is gathering from everyone and thereby they join, therefore a minor also helps, as siman 366 seif 3.

(ב) One who does not believe in eruv — such as a non-Jew and Sadducees and the like, lest they not place it there; and even if he stands from afar and sees — also does not help, lest they did not place it for the sake of eruv.

(ג) Via a deaf-mute — and even if that deaf-mute hears and does not speak, who is generally like a competent person for all his matters — here it appears it does not help, for here we require that he say expressly that through this eruv so-and-so will be permitted to walk, and declaration is essential, as written in note 37, and he cannot speak.

(ד) It is not an eruv — and he remains on the techum of his house, as above siman 408 seif 2.

(ה) To receive it from him — and to place it for the sake of eruv.

(ו) And he sent it, etc. — meaning he told them to bring it to that person.

(ז) And he saw from afar — for otherwise we say perhaps it did not reach him.

(ח) And they gave it to him — see Biur Halacha.

(ט) It is a valid eruv — specifically when the other promised him expressly that they will receive it and place it for the sake of eruv on his behalf; but if he was silent to him we do not say in this case the presumption that he will carry out his mission.

(י) For we presume, etc. — specifically in this case which is a d'rabbanan prohibition we rely on this presumption; but for a d'oraisa prohibition we do not rely on this presumption to be lenient, only to be stringent; and some say even for d'oraisa where there is a stumbling block for the sender if he does not carry out his mission and he will think he carried out his mission — as here — we rely on the presumption that he carried out his mission; and when we know he carried out his mission and there is only doubt lest he changed, according to all we say he certainly did not change.

(כ) Behold these may send — and the agent must say at the time of placing the eruv that through this eruv those people who participated in the eruv will be permitted to walk.
