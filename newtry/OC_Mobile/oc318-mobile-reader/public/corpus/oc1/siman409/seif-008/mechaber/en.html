If he wishes, he may send the eruv through an agent and say "through this eruv so-and-so shall be permitted to walk" — provided the agent is not deaf-mute, imbecile, or minor, or one who does not believe in the eruv. If he sent it through a deaf-mute, imbecile, minor, non-Jew, or one who does not believe in the eruv — it is not a valid eruv. But if he told another to receive it from the agent and sent it through one of these — even if he sent it by elephant or monkey and saw from a distance that it arrived and was given to the receiver — it is a valid eruv, even if he did not see the other person place it, since we presume an agent carries out his mission. Similarly, many who made a partnership eruv of boundaries and wished to send their eruv through another may do so.
