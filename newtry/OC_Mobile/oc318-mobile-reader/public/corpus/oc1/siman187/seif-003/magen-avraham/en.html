If he did not mention. And if he did not say therein thanks at beginning and end—they return him [so Tur]; it can be said bekhol et uvekhol sha'ah—for etim are cold and heat; and one who changes is mistaken [Darkei Moshe; so Matat Moshe]; one does not answer amen peh for it is not end of blessing—only accustomed to pause baal hanissim siman 284 what is written.

And women, etc.—requires study—for he opened with two and concluded in one; in Tosafot beginning what is written it is explicit they do not say covenant and Torah; so Tosafot and Rashi in Erchin; so Berakhot 48b: covenant because not in women; Torah and kingdom not in women nor slaves—end; requires study—not like Early Authorities and Levush; requires study on what they relied that now women say; Levushei Serad strained to give reason; in s.v. wrote women are like men not called adam except when has wife—if so both one body—end—strained to settle custom; in my view difficult—for we say siman 47 women bless blessing of Torah because obligated to learn their mitzvot; so Yoreh Deah siman 246 s.6; we say koh tomar leveit Yaakov—these are the women; if so why not say al Toratecha she'limadtanu; also in Avodah Zarah 27a per those who say hamol yimol—woman is like one who bleeds; per those who say ve'atah et briti—women are not members of covenant; if so possible on britcha shechatamta bevasareinu they can say as one who bleeds; that Rav Chisda [did not say covenant and Torah because R' Chiya] [girsa Nehar Shalom] was zimmun and discharged women—therefore did not say covenant and Torah for not equal to women as men—for at least men are circumcised actually and learn entire Torah—therefore hold if they did not say fulfilled—see there; can say do not lack us with chirik under chet and do not cause us to lack with shva under yod and patach under chet and tzere under samekh; she'atah zan kamatz under shin; befikh kol chai for adjacent; see siman 51; Shelah wrote say chasar yechsar—for vowels ascend like bread.
