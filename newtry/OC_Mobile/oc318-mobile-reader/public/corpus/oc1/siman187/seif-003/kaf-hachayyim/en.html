(3) This is the opening text in the Rashash's Siddur HaKavanos: 'Blessed are You, Hashem, our God, King of the universe, the God Who nourishes us ... in His goodness.' The Divine Name has the numerical value associated with chesed; 'nosen' has the value of the Name Avag Yatatz. At 'for He is God Who nourishes,' intend that the Divine Name Shakkai of Beriah influences 'zan,' whose value is Kel Havayah of Yetzirah. At 'Pose'ach es yadecha,' intend the initials, whose value is the combined Name Y-ah-Adonai, and the final letters chasakh; through Atbash, sa'al, likewise has that value. 'Masbia' has the value chasakh. At 'every living thing with favor,' intend to draw abundant flow and blessing to all worlds. See there at length and Shaar HaMitzvos, Eikev. When intending to bring this flow to all worlds, one should apparently include his own nefesh, ruach, neshamah, and body.
