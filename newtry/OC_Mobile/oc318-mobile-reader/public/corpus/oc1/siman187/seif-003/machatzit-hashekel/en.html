(s.k. 3) And women, etc.—for he opened with two—that women and slaves do not say covenant and Torah; implies women do not say neither covenant nor Torah; and slaves do not say neither covenant nor Torah; and concluded in one that women are not members of covenant, etc.—implies they say Torah; and slaves implies they say covenant.

And in Tosafot beginning ch. 1 what is written daf 20b who explained the question whether women in birkat hamazon d'rabbanan because they are not in covenant and Torah—see there; brought above siman 185.

Daf 49a R' Zeira to R' Chiya: let my master come and teach; he said I did not learn birkat mazon and I teach traditions; he said what is this; he said I happened to house of R' Gamliel and blessed birkat mazon and R' Shimon stood over me like a snake; why did you not say covenant and Torah and kingdom; he said why did you not say; he said as Rav Chananel—Rav said: if he did not say covenant and Torah and kingdom he fulfilled; covenant because it is not in women; Torah and kingdom because not in women and slaves; he said you abandoned all these tannaim and amoraim and acted like Rav—end Gemara.

Per those who say circumcised, etc.—for we say there: whence that a gentile is unfit to circumcise Israel; Bar Papa expounded in name R' Eliezer who said Heaven to Abraham and you shall keep My covenant, your seed after you; R' Yochanan said it is written hamol yimol hamel yimol and said there is between them a woman; and that which Gemara says R' Chiya would zimmun, etc.—this is not explicit in Gemara and as I wrote language of Talmud; rather it is difficult to Magen Avraham what was R' Chiya's excuse why he did not say covenant and Torah and kingdom—for R' Chiya in name Rav said b'dieved he fulfilled; nevertheless difficult why he did so l'chatchila; therefore Magen Avraham explains R' Chiya was zimmun in house of R' Gamliel and they reclined also women and needed to discharge women too; since women cannot say covenant, etc., and also for a man b'dieved he fulfilled—therefore he did so l'chatchila to discharge women; and even per Magen Avraham who also in a woman covenant and Torah applies—for this Magen Avraham wrote nevertheless a woman is not equal to a man regarding covenant and Torah.

In mouth of, etc.—it is adjacent—meaning word befikh is adjacent to word kol; therefore do not say befah with heh at end for then it is recognized from word kol; rather befikhi with yod at end; and what I wrote siman 51 s.k.1 in blessing Baruch she'amar say befah with heh.

That the vowels ascend like bread count rising 72; kamatz is like yod vav and counts as 16; patach under samekh counts as vav—behold 22; segol under yod behold three points which are thirty—behold 52; shva under chet two points behold 20—and it is 72; patach under samekh behold another six—72.
