(3) There some say Blessed satisfies, etc.—meaning some add to say Blessed satisfies the hungry Blessed gives drink to the thirsty and afterward say Baruch Atah Heaven haZan et haOlam, etc.; on this Tur wrote one should not say it—for it is not the coin of the blessing; one who adds detracts—end; meaning only begin birkat hamazon Baruch Atah Heaven haZan, etc.; so here Shulchan Aruch; so Levush; Beit Yosef wrote Tur's view one should not say it at all whether blessing alone or in zimmun see there; so Darkei Moshe letter 1 custom not to say at all per Tur.

And this is opening formula in siddur HaKavanot of Rashash Baruch Atah Heaven haEl haZan otanu, etc., in His goodness—kavanot for names; because He is Heaven nourishes—intend He is Heaven Shaddai in Beriah and influences Heaven nourishes gematria El Havayah in Yetzirah; petach et yadecha—intend rashei teivot pa"y gematria Y-H-W-H; sofei teivot chatach; be'alef beit samekh alef gematria Y-H-W-H; misba'im gematria chatach; lechol chai ratzon—intend to draw abundance and great blessing to all worlds—end; see there long kavanah of this verse in Shem Mishmuel parashat Ekev see there; seems when intending to draw abundance to all worlds—intend also to his Nefesh Ruach Neshamah and body.
