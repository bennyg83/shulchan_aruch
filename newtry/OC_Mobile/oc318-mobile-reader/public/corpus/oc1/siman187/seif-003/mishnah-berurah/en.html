(6) If he did not mention, etc.—and that Mechaber did not write if he did not say desirable good land they return him as in Talmud—for that word is obvious as main of blessing of land; but even regarding covenant and Torah they return him [Avnei Nezer].
(7) In blessing of land, etc.—for through covenant land was given to Abraham in parashat milah as said And I gave you to your seed after you the land of your sojourning; and through merit of Torah and mitzvot they inherited land as said so you may live and multiply and come and inherit the land; and it says And He gave them lands of nations, etc., in order that they keep His statutes and His teachings.
(8) They return him—meaning to beginning of birkat hamazon—for all three first blessings are considered as one—so Magen Avraham below siman 188 s.k.8; see what we wrote there in name other Acharonim.
(9) For women are not, etc.—truly also not members of Torah; we split into two because slaves are in covenant; nevertheless today women practice to say also For Your covenant sealed in our flesh and For Your Torah You taught us—intent on covenant of males sealed in our flesh; and Your Torah You taught us on learning of males—for through Torah and covenant Israel inherited land; also women must learn their mitzvot to know how to perform as siman 47.
