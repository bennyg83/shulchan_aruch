(4) Blessing one. Some begin Baruch Hu uBaruch Shemo—should not begin so even with ten—see below siman 192 letter 7; some begin haZanenu, etc., hamafir tovu aleinu—this addition detracts; Netzach gave that no peh in birkat hamazon—to teach all who bless birkat hamazon with intent nothing controls him not af nor katzef; some Hagahot Tur; Chasam Sofer letter 8; already wrote previous letter in name siddur Rashash must begin Baruch Atah Heaven haEl haZan otanu; so Rav Ben Aharon parashat Chukat letter 14.

Do not lack us and do not cause us to lack. Word chasar chet kamatz samekh patach; yechsar yod shva chet patach samekh tzere—for then points ascend like bread count—for each point is yod each line vav kamatz is yod vav—Shelah Shaar HaOtiot ch. 3 1a; Magen Avraham end siman; Avnei Nezer letter 1 end letter 1.

Blessing two—do not begin on artzeinu but begin modim lecha; and on life You graced us and on food, etc.; on hakol without Baruch Atah; Chasam Sofer there; Ben Aharon there letter 15; wrote there Avnei Nezer must say anachnu modim lecha not ano which is curse like we are judges; so Hagahot Tur 67b there; Chasam Sofer; Ben Aharon there.

Blessing three. Upon us and upon all Israel Your people. Ra'enu zonenu Magen Avraham siman 188 s.k.3. Do not bring us to hand of flesh and blood nor loan but Your hand, etc. May it be will we not be ashamed in this world, etc., in formula ratzeh. Nishbot bo venanuach bo venis'aneg bo (at night nishbot bah venanuach bah, etc., as Shaar HaKavanot). After ratzeh or after yaaleh veyavo say vetibenah Yerushalayim ir haKodesh bimheirah beyameinu Baruch Atah Heaven Boneh Yerushalayim Chasam Sofer; Ben Aharon letter 16; see below siman 188 seif 4. Also Shabbat say ra'enu zonenu like weekday—no concern asking needs forbidden Shabbat—for formula of blessings and permitted; Yerushalmi Tur siman 188; Magen Avraham s.k.3; Beit Yosef Beit Meir letter 5.

Blessing four. Baruch Atah Heaven Elokeinu Avinu Malkeinu, etc.; see below siman 189 letters 4–5; after end of blessing veruach vehatzalah vechol tov listener answers amen Chasam Sofer siman 215 letter 3—Spanish custom; Ashkenazim end umikol tov tamid al yechsar lanu—and listener when blesser ends so must answer amen as Chayei Adam klal 47 letter 1 see there; see below siman 189 letter 1. haRachaman yefarnesenu, etc., with ease not restriction (do not say beheter velo be'issur). haRachaman yatzliach darkenu. haRachaman yatzliachenu belimudenu. haRachaman yerapeinu refuah sheleimah refuat hanefesh urefuat haguf. If idolater in house say haRachaman yevarech kol echad ve'echad mimenu bnei brit (see below siman 189) as our holy fathers Abraham bekhol Yitzchak mikol Yaakov kol. haRachaman yita Torato ve'ahavato, etc., veyihyu kol ma'aseinu lishmah Chasam Sofer; Ben Aharon letter 18; say haRachaman Shabbat like weekday—no concern she'eilat tzrachav for formula of blessings so is; Beit Yosef siman 188 in name Shelah; Mor uKetzia Darkei Moshe letter 4 in name Raz see there; though Beit Yosef there wrote to reject—nevertheless custom to say per Shelah and Raz; so Raz there letter 4; guest blesses baal habayit Shabbat—for also this is included in formula of blessings Birkei Yosef siman 188 in Shiyurei Berachah letter 1; answer amen after haRachaman in birkat hamazon though no mention of name; and after every supplication though no blessing and mention of name—Shelah Shaar HaOtiot 2b; Magen Avraham siman 189 s.k.1; Chasam Sofer siman 215 letter 4; Ben Aharon there.

On Shabbat Yom Tov Rosh Chodesh circumcision wedding Purim siyum masechet say magdol as below siman 189 letter 11 see there.

At end of birkat hamazon say verse oseh shalom bimromav, etc.; intend rashei teivot oseh bimromav for Havayah de'atzilut ba delet yudin Shem Mishmuel parashat Ekev; Ben Aharon there; see Avnei Nezer letter 1 what he expanded on birkat hamazon formula—we wrote briefly here what Acharonim agree; each explained in its place with God's help.
