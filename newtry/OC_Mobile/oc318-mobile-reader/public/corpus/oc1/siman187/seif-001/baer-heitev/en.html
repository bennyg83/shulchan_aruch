He fulfilled—b'dieved and not l'chatchila at all, except for minors under eight years. Bach. Matters that are fit to practice in birkat hamazon: that you nourish—kamatz under the shin. We thank You and not ano—for ano is language of mourning. In every living mouth and not in mouth. Our Shepherd—sheva under resh and not cholam. Sustain us with melafumon. And El—every day only on every day. Taz siman 189. He was good to us; He benefits us; He will benefit us. Maharshal wrote he should say He was good, benefits, will benefit us—see Magen Avraham siman 189. And all good and from all good with melafumum. And say from Heaven and be precise in the word from so as not to swallow the alef. Say and found favor and not venimtza favor. On Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh say magdol; on weekday magdil—for cholam is great king and chirik without yod is small king. Sign of month and Shabbat read mikra—meaning as written in prophets; see Ateret Zekenim siman 189. Do not lack us—chirik under chet. Do not cause us to lack—shva under yod and patach under chet and tzere under samekh. Shelah wrote to say chasar lanu and al yechsar—for the vowels ascend like bread count. In birkat habayit if alone say venomar amen; if others venomru amen. Say bekhol bekhol kol—all kaf degusha. If two actual tables and on each baalei batim sit separately—say al hashulchanot eleh; but if arranging pages on many tables like large feasts—it is like one table. Avi mori and not avi. One answers amen after haRachaman—see Acharonim (and see Sefer Eliyah Rabbah who corrected all birkat hamazon formulas).
