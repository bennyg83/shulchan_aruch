(1) [Levush] Clear and clean, etc. And since I saw many variations in formulas I exerted myself and studied in Rishonim to fix the birkat hamazon formula properly—and this came out first: and in mercy gives bread—so is R' David Abudraham and not in formula gives. Do not lack us and do not cause us to lack sustenance—some practice saying chasar with chet kamatz and samekh patach; some chet chirik and samekh patach—they must also say yechsar with yod shva and chet patach and samekh tzere so both are one decree—as Matat Moshe wrote; Shelah wrote I chose the first way. For He nourishes and sustains all—so Mordechai and R' David Abudraham and Matat Moshe and not like Orchot Chaim who wrote He is Heaven nourishes—Mordechai explained nourishes in eating and sustains in other needs. For all and benefits for all—second for all with kaf cholam for they are recognized and not adjacent; but for all His creatures which is adjacent and near—with kamatz on shin of she'atah zan—point kamatz gadol to broaden alef; and so in Shemoneh Esreh modim anachnu she'atah—so Radak and Matat Moshe wrote. At every time and every hour—for et is six hours and four times change daily morning to midday and midday to night, etc.—so Maharal and Matat Moshe. We thank You and not ano which is curse language like we are fishermen; in mouth of every living with yod and not peh—for peh word is recognized—so Radak and Matat Moshe. Also wrote ra'enu with shva and missing vav and not ro'enu with cholam; zonenu with melafumon—meaning praying before Him that He sustain us and not cholam; and so can explain parnesenu vechalkelenu. Acharonim conclude to say both Shabbat and weekday Boneh Yerushalayim; Yerushalayim first patach and second kamatz—for end of verse; and wait a little then answer amen quietly. Beit Yosef in name R' David Abudraham one should not say haEl for already said Elokeinu; Rav Amram in blessing after Megillah; but in Rambam's formula is haEl Avinu Malkeinu—end; and so Tur and Mordechai end Berakhot and responsum Maharshal siman 64 and Matat Moshe—therefore not fit to change custom but say haEl Avinu; still needs reason. Maharshal and Matat Moshe brought Mahari did not say boreinu but adireinu go'aleinu yetzireinu—and expanded reasons see there. I wonder—for in Rambam and Abudraham formulas I saw they wrote boreinu and not yetzireinu—therefore seems we should not move from our custom saying both boreinu yetzireinu. The good—the heh patach; do not say only every day, etc., implying chas veshalom two powers; rather say El every day without shin—as Bach siman 189 in name Kolbo and Shelah; Taz siman 189 wrote one should not say haEl at all; in my humble opinion do not change custom since Kolbo and other poskim wrote. Tur wrote Rosh said hu heitiv lanu hu metiv lanu hu yeitiv lanu hu gemalanu hu gomelinu hu yigmel lanu—so ruled Shulchan Aruch and Bach siman 189 and so is custom and so I practiced and it is correct—unlike those who say hu heitiv metiv yeitiv; rather as order of benefits I found now in Kolbo: hu metiv hu heitiv lanu hu yeitiv lanu hu gomelinu hu gemalanu hu yigmelenu—first present tense then future like Heaven malach Heaven melech Heaven yimloch—end his words; and so Mordechai in name Pardes and Rabbenu Yonah; and Abudraham in name Rosh; perhaps scribal error in Tur and must say also this order. Also heard from aforementioned poskim one should not say hu yigmel be'adenu but hu yigmelenu le'ad—as Matat Moshe wrote. Or Chadash wrote vechol tov umikol tov correct to say both melafumon—end; but I saw Tashbetz siman 317: kol tov with cholam. On this table—even if arranging pages table to table as in large feasts—not called two tables only when two actual tables and on each baal habayit sits separately (requires study Beit Meir Maharal on this)—only when zimmun together bless al hashulchanot eleh sheachalnu aleihem; so wrote in name Beit Meir; and apparently first and last contradict—perhaps he cited custom; same when making two actual tables at large feast or one baal habayit eating with household on actual tables—bless al hashulchanot eleh. Also wrote haRachaman yevarech avi mori not avi; guest does not bless baal habayit unless eating at his table and his bread—Yerushalmi and bless to include baal habayit. As our fathers Abraham Isaac Jacob were blessed bekhol mikol kol—all degusha in birkat hamazon not adjacent to Heaven nach. So bless us all together bnei brit when Samaritans or apostate Samaritans in house. Magen Avraham siman 189 in name Taz should say specifically otanu bnei brit kulanu yachad—no stringency since within time of speech and easy to understand; on the contrary per his words it is like repetition—as Kolbo wrote. Or Chadash implies no distinction woman to woman unlike those who err at wedding saying chatan kallah achinu raboteinu—all sitting here Beit Meir—end; I saw Kolbo page 24 they say haRachaman yismoch yes'od veyechalkel veyerachem me'alah me'alah hachatan vehaKallah hane'imah vechol raboteinu hamesubin kem shenitbarchu, etc.; Matat Moshe wrote ve'amru amen and not as end birkat hamazon. Magen Avraham siman 189 wrote cannot decree ve'amru amen unlike oseh shalom which is HQBH's and did not mention Matat Moshe's words. Some say me'am Heaven not me'et—correct not to change verse language and say me'et Heaven and be precise not to swallow alef—tzedakah me'Elohei yeshu'enu, etc. haRachaman yizkenenu leyemot haMashiach ulechayei olam haba—so Matat Moshe and Orchot Chaim; Abudraham haRachaman yizkenenu veyechayenu veyekarevenu, etc.—I do not know where they find to change Abudraham's formula for he brings verse for all. Abudraham on Shabbat when great king against weekday say magdol with vav cholam—vav cholam is great king; weekday magdil missing vav chirik without yod is small king; magdil is in Tehillim before David was king; magdol in prophets when already king—end. Acharonim: Yom Tov and Rosh Chodesh law like Shabbat; sign month and Shabbat read mikra. Kolbo: answer amen after haRachaman and after every supplication even without name and blessing—so Shelah. Matat Moshe: say hu ya'aseh not vehu. Acharonim: after ki le'olam chasdo verse na'ar hayiti gam zakanti velo ra'iti tzaddik ne'ezav vezaro mevakesh lachem, etc. When blessing on cup say before blessing kos yeshu'ot esa uveshem Heaven ekra—esa gematria Eheyeh asher Eheyeh.
