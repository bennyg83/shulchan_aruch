(1) [Seif 1] Some say Blessed satisfies, etc.—so is in Berakhot 48b. Tannaim taught order birkat hamazon thus: first blessing blessing haZan; second blessing of land; third Boneh Yerushalayim; fourth haTov vehaMetiv. Also there Gemara R' Nachman: Moshe enacted for Israel blessing haZan when man fell; Yehoshua enacted blessing of land when they entered land; David and Shlomo enacted Boneh Yerushalayim—David on Israel Your people and Yerushalayim Your city; Shlomo on the great holy House; haTov vehaMetiv in Yavneh enacted corresponding to slain of Beitar—the good that they did not putrefy and the beneficent that they were given burial—end. Rashba wrote that which we say Moshe enacted blessing of land, etc.—is difficult to me; behold objection nearby these blessings are d'oraisa; he answered their nature is that He accustomed them—for if d'oraisa if he wanted he could say any coin he wanted; Moshe Yehoshua David came and enacted a coin for each in its time; and certainly before conquest of land and building Yerushalayim they did not say as coin said after conquest and building; and as we do not say that coin David and Shlomo enacted—for we seek to restore kingdom and rebuild House and they sought to establish kingdom and building and continue land's tranquility—end. So Rosh and Tur brought siman 188. Ramban in Sefer HaHashagot: no expression in birkat hamazon d'oraisa; but commanded d'oraisa to bless after our eating each according to his understanding like Minyamin the shepherd (Berakhot 40b) said berikh Rachmana marei dehai pita; prophets came and fixed polished sweet language; we learned therein malchut beit David meshichecha, etc.—end; Beit Din Beit Yosef this siman; Perishah 188. Implies from Rashba Rosh Tur three blessings d'oraisa as Gemara there and brought proof from scripture; so Perishah there; Bach siman 191 see there. So Tosafot Bereshit 16a; Levush this siman; Magen Avraham siman 194 s.k.3. However maran in Beit Yosef siman 191 wrote number of blessings after meal not d'oraisa; wrote so from Rambam in Sefer HaMitzvot mitzvah 19 commanded to bless after eating and did not mention number of blessings; per him though drash in Gemara from verses—they are mere support. So in Kessef Mishneh ch. 2 Laws Berakhot halacha 2; wrote so from Ramban too see there. So in Midrash Rabbah parashat Masei no number three blessings d'oraisa see there; practical difference: per those who say three blessings d'oraisa—if blessed kind of three instead of birkat hamazon or omitted one—did not fulfill; what they were lenient siman 191 for workers to bless two is specifically workers for reason Tosafot wrote there see there. So Avnei Nezer siman 268 letter 18 if blessed kind of three instead of birkat hamazon did not fulfill see there. So Chayei Adam klal 47 letter 1 did not fulfill see there. But per those who say no number three d'oraisa—if omitted one or blessed kind of three—fulfilled; so Taz siman 168 s.k.6 if blessed kind of three instead of birkat hamazon fulfilled see there; so Sefer Meshivat Nefesh siman 4 formula three blessings d'rabbanan and d'oraisa fulfill with kind of three and his doubt lenient see there; so Raz siman 191. Therefore since dispute exists—for halacha if erred and blessed kind of three instead of birkat hamazon—if possible eat more and bless haMotzi and birkat hamazon and intend in birkat hamazon to exempt one eating; or hear birkat hamazon from others and intend to fulfill; if not possible—think blessing in heart as above siman 184 letter 22 see there. All when ate to satiation obligated birkat hamazon d'oraisa; but if did not eat to satiation obligation d'rabbanan—appears b'dieved can be lenient and can say fulfilled; see below letter 18.
