(1) Some say Blessed, etc.—meaning they were accustomed to say before beginning birkat hamazon.

(2) And one who adds detracts—for one may not add to coin Sages accustomed; do not say ki le'olam chasdo imanu ki chasadav hem im kol chai. she'atah zan kamatz under shin. anachnu modim not ano—for ano is mourning language. befikh kol chai not befah. ra'enu with shva not ro'enu with cholam—for it is request language; therefore zonenu melafumon. do not lack us and do not cause us to lack [Avnei Nezer in name Shelah].

(3) Malka marei, etc.—per what Mechaber wrote below siman 214—refers that he said malka de'alma; see what is written there.

(4) Fulfilled—hands of first blessing which is blessing haZan; even though greatly shortened blessing and did not say as formula Sages enacted in holy tongue—nevertheless b'dieved fulfilled since mentioned Name [they called HQBH Rachmana] and malchut and matter of blessing; also implies per first opinion even if did not conclude also helps—for though we hold where they said to conclude cannot refrain from concluding—nevertheless b'dieved fulfilled; second opinion holds it impedes even b'dieved; see Bach who wrote it appears primary this opinion and brought in Avnei Nezer. All specifically b'dieved; l'chatchila all agree not to change formula Sages enacted as Rambam ch. 1 Shema and ch. 1 Laws Berakhot; Acharonim imply small children can educate l'chatchila with this formula from blessing haZan and a little from each blessing until they know to bless each as fixed.
