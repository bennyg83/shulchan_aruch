In Magen Avraham s.k.3 and in my humble opinion difficult, etc.—meaning difficult why they strained—for plainly can say this is reason of custom that they are obligated to learn their mitzvot; also in Avodah Zarah we say gentile unfit per one opinion because of ve'atah et briti tishmor lezar'ach acharecha but gentile not; and one opinion holds from hamol yimol; and we say difference between them is woman—for per ve'atah, etc., woman is not subject to milah; meaning if so not for her he said you shall keep covenant—therefore also cannot circumcise others; per hamol yimol she can circumcise well as one who bleeds; since at least as one who bleeds she can say For Your covenant sealed in our flesh though no actual holy sign—nevertheless includes herself with men signed in flesh as one who bleeds; and what Magen Avraham wrote and this that he says, etc.—some words missing; correct from Gemara Rav Chisda did not say covenant and Torah because not in women; nevertheless can say Rav Chisda was zimmun, etc., as corrected. Meaning seemingly difficult—for though brit because not in women means not actually in women—still difficult how R' Chiya did not say l'chatchila since also in women themselves covenant and Torah apply and they can say l'chatchila; if not applicable to women at all and they have nothing to say—R' Chiya comes to teach even for men if wanted not to say permission is theirs since not in women from outset so enacted; unlike if women can say l'chatchila—why did R' Chiya do so l'chatchila; therefore Magen Avraham wrote to answer R' Chiya was zimmun to discharge women; though women can say—since covenant and Torah not equal in women as men and he discharged both together and held this is worse than women blessing separately; therefore did not include; since even for men if did not say fulfilled—because not actually in women as Rav said; on this Rav Sheshet challenged you abandoned tannaim and acted like Rav—meaning since per these tannaim for men even b'dieved did not fulfill—you should have said even if you were zimmun to discharge men as I explained in Magen Avraham's words.
