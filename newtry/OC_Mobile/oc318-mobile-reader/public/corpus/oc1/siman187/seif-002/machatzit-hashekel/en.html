(s.k. 2) If, etc.—thanks at beginning and end: beginning is modim lecha; end is ve'al hakol anachnu modim lecha.

So Tur brought in name Yerushalmi must say thanks beginning and end; afterward wrote other things that must be said; wrote afterward: all who change or do not mention what Sages required to mention did not fulfill—end; implies on all said before.

It can be said ki et, etc.—do not think et and sha'ah are one matter; rather et are six seasons mentioned in parashat Noach and sha'ah is literal hour.

Only that accustomed, etc.—meaning do not err from what appears pause in other siddurim after word uvekhol sha'ah—for here it is end of blessing; so Tur siman 284 in Berakhot after haftarah—siddurim have pause after uvetzedek and it is not end of blessing.
