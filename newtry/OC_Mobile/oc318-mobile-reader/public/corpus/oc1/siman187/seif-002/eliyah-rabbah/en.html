I wonder—for certainly he fulfills as explained in Talmud Berakhot 40 and all poskim. Also what he wrote to teach minors under eight—in my opinion since he fulfills with this—teach them this blessing. Also I wonder changing Rachmana mara malka to precede name before malchut—for Rachmana is the name as in Talmud there; if so fine to say Rachmana malka mara; and so all poskim; and so above siman 167 seif 10. Bach siman 192 brought short formula in examining house—entire first blessing as fixed then say modim lecha Heaven Elokeinu al shehinchalta, etc.; and implies there when ones and cannot extend—say it; also seems to me teach minors under eight; instead of first blessing teach formula berikh Rachmana that we mentioned.
