Seif 2 for Rosh, etc. Tosafot 48b s.v. brit, etc., and Rashi s.v. vetzarich, etc., and Rambam ruled plain meaning of Gemara.

Even, etc. In Tosefta R' Yosi says even one who did not mention brit in land—they return him; in Yerushalmi end ch. 1 R' Yehoshua ben Levi said one who did not mention Torah in land—they return him—why? And He gave them lands of nations—because of what? In order that they keep His statutes and His Torahs they will be preserved; Rabbah bar R' Chiya in name Rav: if he did not mention brit in land or did not mention in Boneh Yerushalayim kingdom of house of David—they return him; and this is what is written they return and not like R' Yechiel who wrote he did not fulfill—meaning for the mitzvah; see Tur.
