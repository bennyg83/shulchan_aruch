(1) For in sukkos I caused them to dwell, etc.; they are the clouds of glory, etc. - Corresponding to this, He commanded us to make sukkos so that we remember His awesome acts and wonders. Although we left Egypt in the month of Nissan, He did not command us to make sukkos at that time, because it is the summer season and everyone normally makes huts for shade, so our making them would not be recognizable as being for the command of Hashem. Therefore He commanded us to make them in the seventh month, which is the rainy season, when everyone normally leaves his sukkah for his house, and we leave the house to sit in the sukkah. This shows that he does it for the sake of Hashem's command, as the Tur writes. The later authorities wrote that when sitting in it, one should have in mind that the Holy One, blessed be He, commanded us to sit in the sukkah as a remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt and also as a remembrance of the clouds of glory that then surrounded us to protect us from heat and sun. All this is to fulfill the mitzvah properly, but after the fact one fulfills as long as he intended only to fulfill the mitzvah, as the Peri Megadim writes.
(2) And it is a mitzvah to prepare the sukkah immediately, etc. - Although this was already written above at the end of siman 624, there it refers to the night, as a mere beginning, and on the following day he prepares all of it if possible. Also, there it speaks about one who is meticulous in his deeds, that he should begin something with the sukkah immediately that night. But one who is not meticulous should at least begin and complete all of it, if possible, on the next day after leaving the synagogue, even if it is erev Shabbos, until midday. After that, one can say it is forbidden, as the Peri Megadim writes.
