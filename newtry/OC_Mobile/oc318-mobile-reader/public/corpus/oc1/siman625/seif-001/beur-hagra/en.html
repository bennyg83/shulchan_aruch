(א) Siman 61 in Sukkah etc. — like R' Eliezer there 11b, and although R' Akiva disagrees with him and apparently R' Yehuda there holds like him since he needed to learn from another verse, and so all the amoraim there — but in Yerushalmi there 40, R' Yehuda also holds like R' Eliezer, for they teach there: R' Yochanan said, it is written, "From your threshing floor and your winepress" — from the refuse in the threshing floor and winepress you shall make for yourself sekhakh; R' Shimon ben Lakish says, "And I will bring them up from the land," etc. R' Tanchuma said, the law is according to his view and the law is according to his view — R' Yochanan, both said the clouds were from above, both learned from "from your threshing"; R' Shimon ben Lakish said the clouds were from below, both learned from "clouds" etc. see there. And similarly in Bereishit Rabbah they disagree there in chapter 13 — R' Yehuda and R' Shimon ben Lakish — R' Yochanan said clouds are only from above, as it says, "And I saw with the clouds of heaven"; R' Shimon ben Lakish said clouds are only from below, as it says, "I will raise up leaders from the ends of the earth" etc. see there. And see Tosafot there s.v. bapsuleta etc., and similarly in Targum Onkelos we translate "ari" as "like a cloud canopy" etc., and so in Midrash Rabbah in many places, and from them there the Holy One blessed be He said to Avraham our father, you said "and shelter us under the tree" — by your life I will reward your children, as it says, "He spread a cloud for a covering" — in the wilderness in the land, from where is it written in Sukkot "you shall dwell seven days" — for the world to come, from where? as it says, "and a sukkah shall be for shade by day" etc., and similarly in Mekhilta parashat Beshalach see there, and see Rashi at the beginning of Sukkah 2b s.v. ha-hu.

(ב) A mitzvah that comes etc. — Mekhilta parashat Bo: "and you shall guard the matzot" — R' Yoshiyah says, do not read it thus but "and you shall guard the mitzvot" — just as one does not let matzot become leavened, so one should not let mitzvot become leavened; rather if a mitzvah comes to your hand, do it immediately. And they brought Rashi in Megillah 6b and Yoma 33a, and see Nazir chapter 4 23b and Bava Kamma 38b and Horayot 11, and other places.
