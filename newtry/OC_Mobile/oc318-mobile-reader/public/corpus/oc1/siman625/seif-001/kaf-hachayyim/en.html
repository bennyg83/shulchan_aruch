1) [Seif 1] You shall dwell in sukkos for seven days, etc. These are the Clouds of Glory, etc. As a remembrance of this, He commanded us to make sukkos so that we remember His wonders and awesome deeds. Although we left Egypt in the month of Nissan, He did not command us to make a sukkah at that time, because those are the summer days, when it is the way of every person to make a sukkah for shade, and it would not be evident that our making them was in fulfillment of the commandment of the Creator, blessed is He. Therefore, He commanded us to make them in the seventh month, which is the rainy season, when it is the way of every person to leave his sukkah and sit in his house, whereas we leave the house to sit in the sukkah. Through this it is evident to all that it is the King's commandment upon us to do so. Tur; Levush. The reason according to the mystical teachings is that from Rosh Hashanah until Yom Kippur there were aspects of judgment and strength, while from Yom Kippur onward there are aspects of kindness that are drawn down through the sukkah, the lulav, and its species, as explained in its place regarding the mystical meaning of, His left hand is beneath my head, which refers to the period from Rosh Hashanah until Yom Kippur, and His right hand embraces me, which refers to the period from Yom Kippur onward. The sukkah is an aspect of surrounding light that is drawn from Binah to the aspect of Malchus. These are seven surrounding lights, from Chesed through Malchus, and each day one kindness is drawn down: on the first night, the surrounding light of Chesed; on the second night, the surrounding light of Gevurah, and so forth. This is the mystical meaning of what our Sages, of blessed memory, said, that the sukkah corresponds to the Clouds of Glory in the Wilderness, for every cloud is an aspect of kindness, as it is written, By day Hashem will command His kindness. Therefore, the Clouds of Glory were in the merit of Aharon HaKohen, the man of kindness. They were seven clouds, corresponding to the seven surrounding lights of kindness in the seven lower sefirot of the feminine aspect, from Chesed through its Malchus. Corresponding to them are the seven days of Sukkos, as written in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 102, see there. That which was stated, that there were seven clouds, is taught in Sifrei, Parashas Behaaloscha, on the verse, And the cloud of Hashem was over them, etc.; in Mechilta, Parashas Beshalach; and in Rashi's commentary on the Torah, Parashas Behaaloscha, on the verse, And the cloud of Hashem was over them by day, see there. This is why the Torah commanded, You shall dwell in sukkos for seven days, corresponding to the seven clouds. See below, at the end of siman 639, sec. 8, s.v. And know.
