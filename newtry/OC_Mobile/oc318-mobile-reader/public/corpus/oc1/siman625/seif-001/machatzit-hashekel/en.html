(א) Magen Avraham wrote that one should have intent etc. as a remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt, and he infers this from the language of the Tur.

(ב) And the reason: although for all mitzvot, even according to those who hold mitzvot require intent, it suffices to intend to fulfill the mitzvah commanded from the Omnipresent through Moshe. But for the mitzvah of sukkah it is written regarding it, "so that your generations may know" etc. when I took them out of the land of Egypt — it appears from the language of the verse that the Torah was particular that at the time of fulfilling the mitzvah we know and intend the reason for the mitzvah, which is a remembrance of the Exodus from Egypt.

(ג) And he wrote there that the same law applies to tzitzit and tefillin — see there.
