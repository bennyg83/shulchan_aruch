(Sek 10) But first, etc. — even per the explanation, etc., it comes to settle the difficulty of Bach and Taz. And that which we say in Gittin, Abbaye said we hold: if there is no Kohen there, the bundle is separated; and Rashi explained the connection is severed, the Levi lost his honor, etc., and he does not read at all, etc.; but, etc., I heard there is no order of the matter to precede Levi to Israel, and whoever wishes may precede — end of his words. And Kesef Mishneh wrote that Rambam who wrote as the language in Shulchan Aruch "and a Levi shall not ascend after him" implies he holds per Rashi's first explanation, and so in his book Beit Yosef; and if so how did Rama write on the Mechaber's language "but first" — one can explain per Rashi's second explanation. But the Mechaber holds per the first explanation, and Rama should have written language of dispute; therefore he brought Magen Avraham, words of Darkei Moshe that for this that a Levi may ascend first, also Rashi's first explanation agrees, except he argues with the second explanation when Levi and Israel are equal in rank. Per the first explanation that wrote the Levi lost his honor — if so it is like two Israelites who are equal, therefore whoever wishes is called first; unlike per the second explanation that the Levi did not lose all his honor but only part — for if there is a Kohen here, after him Levi and after him Israel, even if the Israelite is greater than the Levi; unlike when there is no Kohen here, the Levi lost this right and whoever wishes may ascend, for each has a rank: the Israelite has rank that he is greater than the Levi, and the Levi has rank because he is a Levi; therefore they are equal; unlike when the Levi was equal in wisdom to the Israelite, then it is fit to precede the Levi to the Israelite, for even though there is no Kohen here, nevertheless some right remains to the Levi against Israel. And so Rosh explicitly per the second explanation, brought in Beit Yosef — at any rate when equal; also per the first explanation a Levi can ascend first; and Rama's words — specifically when the Israelite is not great, etc., and this is per Rashi's first explanation that Rambam and Beit Yosef ruled like him, as above.
