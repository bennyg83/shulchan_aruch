The order of Torah reading on Monday and Thursday. It contains 14 seifim: On Monday, Thursday, and Shabbos at Minchah, 3 people read. We do not reduce that number, we do not add to it, and there is no haftarah from the Prophets. {Rama: If there are 2 bridegrooms in the synagogue and both are Yisraelim, it is permitted to add a fourth reader, because for them it is like Yom Tov, when additions are permitted. [Mordechai, chapter HaKoreh Omed; Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 12 of the Laws of Sefer Torah] It appears that the same applies to 2 ba'alei brit, since it is their Yom Tov, as stated below in siman 559. Regarding adding readers on Shabbos and Yom Tov, see above at the beginning of siman 282.}
