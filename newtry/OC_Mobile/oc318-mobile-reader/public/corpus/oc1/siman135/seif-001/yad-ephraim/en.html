Taz seif 6 — this is the language of the Rambam, etc., and there it states "and a Levi shall not ascend after him" at all; therefore Kesef Mishneh explained this is per the first explanation. Nevertheless Taz holds that even though the Shulchan Aruch did not mention the word "at all," nevertheless its explanation is per the language of the Rambam — for if per the second explanation, at any rate the Levi ascends for the third or fourth [aliyah]; and also from that it writes "an Israelite is called" in place of a Kohen, it implies specifically an Israelite but not a Levi. And if so, what it wrote "a Levi shall not ascend after him" is for amplification — that even after an Israelite he shall not ascend, and how much more so he shall not ascend first, lest one err more regarding a Levi to say he has priestly sanctity.
