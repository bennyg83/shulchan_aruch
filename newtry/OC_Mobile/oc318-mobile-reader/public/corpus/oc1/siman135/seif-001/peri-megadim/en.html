Behold in Gittin 59b R' Chiya bar Aba derives from "you shall sanctify him" (Vayikra 21:8), and so in the poskim; and in Orach Mishpat R' Elazar letter 4 he expanded that it is from the Torah; and the Rambam z"l in Sefer HaMitzvot 32 — to apportion honor to the seed of Aharon and to precede him to all who are in sanctity, as it is said "and you shall sanctify him"; and Rashi in the Chumash holds "he shall be holy to you" — sanctify him by force — see there; Ra'avad and Perishah here hold he takes the beginning and from sanctity it follows. And Rav Matna derives "and he gave it to the Kohanim, sons of Levi, and to all the elders of Israel" (Devarim 39:9), and R' Yehudah, etc. And behold the difference between these derivations can be explained in four aspects: (1) Or "Kohanim sons of Levi" is only for Torah reading, but for other matters not; and "you shall sanctify him" — to all who are in sanctity (and sanctity includes taking terumat maaser ani and charity — Tosafos there s.v. velitol; and what is written regarding a meal — what is sanctity; and perhaps a seudat mitzvah of friends, etc.). (2) From the verse "and he gave" one can explain Kohen before Levi and Levi before Israel, for so the verse is written; but in the Yerushalmi on this, R' Shimon bar Yochai derives from "Kohanim sons of Levi" and afterward "elders of Israel," and does not derive from the extra "sons of Levi" — if so there is no precedence of Levi over Israel; and see Mefaresh there in Tosafos (second version). (3) One can explain a blemished Kohen from the verse "Kohanim sons of Levi" — these too are so, as "speak to the Kohanim" — even blemished ones are included; unlike "your bread of your God he offers" — one can explain a blemished one has no positive command; and one can explain "offers" — eating is also included (Rambam ch. 4 Hilchot Klei HaMikdash letter 2; and in Sefer HaMitzvot 32 positive commandment to apportion honor to the seed of Aharon). (4) If it is said "the Kohanim sons of Levi" and afterward "to the elders of Israel," we hear that a Kohen precedes even an elder Torah scholar from the Sanhedrin, for it is written implicitly; unlike "you shall sanctify him for he is holy" — one can explain sanctity of Torah is more severe than sanctity of the Kohen, and specifically when they are equal in mitzvah — see Mefaresh there and see Rambam's explanation of the mishnah in Gittin: "you shall sanctify him" when equal — behold a Torah scholar, sanctity of Torah is preferable; and from what is written in ch. 4 Hilchot Klei Mikdash letter 2 "every person needs" — implies even a Torah scholar more than him — and one can explain. And behold Magen Avraham left in an established presumption in R' Elazar letter 4 s.v. veha desiman 128 seif 40 — one who marries women in sin does not read first and an Israelite goes up; and there is force in a question (and it is said this question exists), or since he profaned his sanctity and a wicked person is not holy — therefore a wicked Kohen from other sins, certainly if there is another Kohen he precedes; and if not, he precedes an Israelite from the verse "you shall sanctify him." And it is difficult to me: since a Torah scholar is more than a Kohen he precedes, for sanctity of Torah is preferable — how much more so a wicked Kohen and a fit Israelite is more holy; and so too one can answer me that matter of 128 seif 40 implies specifically sins of kehunah — and it requires investigation.

(3) And here in Gittin there Abbaye answers: it is not needed — necessarily per the opinion from "and he gave it to the kohanim" — for if from "you shall sanctify him" one could say the mishnah speaks when they are not equal, that a kohen is not like a Torah scholar, an ordinary Israelite precedes, and for derachay shalom, etc.; or a blemished kohen, etc. Still Abbaye said we have a tradition "the bundle separated" — meaning he should not go up in the middle; but there is a mitzvah that Levi precedes Israel. And if so, in Taz and Magen Avraham orach 6 and 10 I will explain.
