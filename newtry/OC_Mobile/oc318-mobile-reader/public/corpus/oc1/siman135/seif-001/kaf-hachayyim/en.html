1) [Seif 1] On Monday and Thursday, etc. Moshe Rabbeinu instituted for Israel that they should read from the Torah publicly on Shabbos and on Monday and Thursday at Shacharis, so that they should not tarry three days without hearing Torah. And Ezra instituted that they should read likewise at Minchah every Shabbos, on account of the idlers in the marketplace; and he also instituted that on Monday and Thursday three men should read, and that they should not read fewer than ten verses. Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilchos Tefillah, law 1; and it is from the Gemara Bava Kama, folio 82a; see there. And see the Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman. And in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 48d, he wrote the reason that they take out a Sefer Torah on Monday and Thursday: because on Monday and Thursday the World of Yetzirah, which is the aspect of the Torah, holds sway; see there. And so wrote Pri Etz Chaim, chapter 1 of Shaar Krias Sefer Torah, and chapter 1 of Shaar HaShabbos. And the reason for the Sefer Torah that they take out on Shabbos: for in the morning on Shabbos day we read from the Sefer Torah corresponding to the attribute of Chesed, which is aroused at the beginning of the day, and at Minchah corresponding to Gevurah, which holds sway then; and the intent is to include this within that, fear together with lovingkindness, because the Torah was given from these two attributes, as the verse says, 'from His right hand, a fiery law unto them.' Shaar HaKavanos, folio 73d.
2) There: And one does not add to them, etc. On account of interruption of labor for the people; and one also does not read a Haftarah from the Prophets for this reason. Levush; Taz, s.k. 1; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1. And [as for] Shabbos at Minchah, where there is no interruption of labor: nonetheless, since they would expound until close to nightfall, the Sages did not wish to burden the congregation with an addition. So Rashi explained there in Megillah. Machatzis HaShekel, s.k. 1. However, in Shaar HaKavanos there, folio 48, he wrote the reason according to the hidden teachings: because there are revealed now, on Monday and Thursday, only the Netzach-Hod-Yesod of Abba alone, for the Netzach-Hod-Yesod of Abba split open and their illumination goes forth outward; therefore three ascend: a Kohen corresponding to Netzach, the right line; a Levi corresponding to Hod, the left line; a Yisrael corresponding to Yesod, the middle line. And behold, this illumination does not remain thus, but only for a moment, and afterward they return and are concealed within as at first; therefore, immediately after the reading they close the Sefer Torah and return it to its place within the Heichal. End of his words. And such is the custom of Beis El, may it be built and established, that immediately after the reading, before 'Ashrei' and 'Uva LeTzion,' they return the Sefer Torah to its place, whether on a weekday, on Rosh Chodesh, or on Chol HaMoed; only on Shabbos and Yom Tov it is after 'Ashrei.' And the three who ascend at Shabbos Minchah correspond to the first three sefiros of Abba which are revealed now, one corresponding to Chochmah, one corresponding to Binah, and one corresponding to Daas, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 75a and 75b; see there. Therefore one does not reduce from them and one does not add to them. And see below, ot 19.
3) There, gloss: And if there were two bridegrooms, etc., it is permitted to add, etc. Maran, of blessed memory, wrote in the Bedek HaBayis that we did not have the custom to add on Monday and Thursday on account of bridegrooms, and we have also not heard of anyone who practiced so. End of his language. And the Knesses HaGedolah cited him in the Hagahos Beis Yosef, and wrote: and so it is written in the Tikkun Yissachar, folio 55a; and so the Pri Chadash concurred, ot 1. And in the Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef, ot 2, he wrote that so he ruled in practice. And so wrote the Levush, that one does not practice so; and the Magen Avraham cited him, s.k. 2, and the Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1; and the Eliyah Rabbah wrote there on the words of the Levush that whether for two men of a bris or for two bridegrooms one does not practice to add. And so wrote the Eshel Avraham, ot 2; and so wrote the Maamar Mordechai, ot 1, that one does not practice to add on Monday and Thursday beyond three; see there. And so it appears from what was stated above in the previous ot in the name of the Arizal, of blessed memory, that according to the inner meaning of the matters there is no room for an addition on Monday and Thursday; see there, and examine it well.
4) There, in the gloss: And it appears that the same applies to two men of a bris, etc. But in the Tikkun Yissachar he wrote that the law of the Ritzva was stated only regarding a bridegroom, not regarding a man of a bris; and the Knesses HaGedolah cited him there. Magen Avraham, s.k. 3. And so wrote the Pri Chadash there, on the aforementioned words of the Rama, that it is not so; see there. And such appears the view of the Taz, s.k. 2. And we already wrote in the previous note that even regarding a bridegroom one does not practice to add on Monday and Thursday.
