They read, etc. See Baer Heitev, who cites Ateres Zekeinim. However, Responsa Masas Moshe concludes that there is no remedy for this. He brings proof from Or Zarua and Maharam Mintz, who write that if the regular reading was omitted on a particular Shabbos, it is read on the following Shabbos together with that week’s parashah. This is difficult, for they should rather have said that it be read on Sunday, so that three days should not pass without Torah reading. Evidently, it is preferable to leave it for the appropriate day. See further there for another proof of this.
It is permitted. Sefer Gan HaMelech writes that a chassan takes precedence over the father of a child at a bris. If the father of the child transgressed and went up, the chassan should be called after him, for in relation to the chassan this is his festival, on which four people may be called. If he is not called, the matter is degrading to him. See the responsum in Yoreh Deah, siman 50. The implication of the Gemara is that adding aliyos depends upon the day having a Mussaf offering. Examine this carefully.
