(In Magen Avraham at the beginning) Moshe instituted for Israel that they should read from the Torah on Shabbos, Yom Tov, Rosh Chodesh, and Chol HaMoed. Ezra instituted for Israel that three people should be called. This is the correct text, and so it appears in the Rosh, tractate Megillah, siman 22. See there.
