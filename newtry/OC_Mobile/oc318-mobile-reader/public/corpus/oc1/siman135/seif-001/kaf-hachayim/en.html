(1) [Seif 1] On Monday and Thursday, etc. — Moshe our teacher enacted for Israel that they read Torah publicly on Shabbat and on Monday and Thursday in the morning, so they would not go three days without hearing Torah; and Ezra enacted that they read thus at Minchah every Shabbat because of those dwelling in villages; and he also enacted that on Monday and Thursday three people read and they do not read fewer than ten verses — Rambam ch. 12 of Tefillah law 1, and this is from the Gemara Bava Kama daf 82a — see there; and see Magen Avraham at the beginning of this siman and in Shaar HaKavanot that the Maggid wrote the reason they take out a Torah scroll on Monday and Thursday: on Monday and Thursday the world of Yetzirah rules, which is in the aspect of Torah — see there; and likewise Peri Etz Chaim ch. 1 from the gate of Torah reading and ch. 1 from the gate of Shabbat; and the reason for the Torah scroll they take out on Shabbat: in the morning on Shabbat day we read with a Torah scroll corresponding to the attribute of Chesed that awakens at the beginning of the day, and at Minchah corresponding to Gevurah that rules then; and the intention is to combine fear with kindness, because the Torah was given from these two attributes, as it is said "from His right hand a fire of law to them" — Shaar HaKavanot ch. 73 letter 4.
