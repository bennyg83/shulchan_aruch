On Monday and Thursday and on Shabbat at Mincha they read three (aliyot). It appears that if they cancelled on Monday the reading from the Torah scroll under coercion (ones), he can read Monday's reading on Tuesday so that Ezra's enactment is not nullified and they do not go three days without Torah — which is not so if they cancelled on Thursday, for then they have already gone three days without Torah and its time has passed, its sacrifice is voided. Specifically when they cancelled on Monday inadvertently; but if deliberately it appears to me that it is similar to prayer mentioned in siman 108 — "That which is crooked cannot be made straight" — so too here; nevertheless this requires further study, for perhaps that case is different, where the day has passed its sacrifice is voided, whereas here in our case it is similar to one who ate garlic and his odor emanates — let him eat more garlic, etc. — so too this matter; and you are not free from Ezra's enactment on account of this that the day passed, and this requires further study.
