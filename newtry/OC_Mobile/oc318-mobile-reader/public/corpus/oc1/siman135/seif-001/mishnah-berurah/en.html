Rambam wrote chapter 12 of Laws of Prayer: Moses our teacher instituted for Israel that they read Torah publicly on Shabbat and on Monday and Thursday in the morning, so they would not pass three days without hearing Torah; and Ezra instituted that they also read at Minchah every Shabbat because of those sitting at corners; and he also instituted that on Monday and Thursday three people read, and they do not read fewer than ten verses [because in the time of Moses son of Amram the institution was not specifically that three read and specifically ten verses — he came and instituted this]; and in Yerushalmi it states: Moses instituted for Israel that they read Torah on Sabbaths, festivals, Rosh Chodesh, and Monday and Thursday; Rif brings it in chapter HaKorei Omed, and so Rambam chapter 13 halachah 8. (1) On Monday and Thursday — in the morning; and although b'dieved the whole day is its time, nevertheless l'chatchila it is a mitzvah to precede.

(2) And we do not add to them — because of nullification of work for the people; and also we do not read haftarah, for this reason.

(3) It is permitted to add — so that both can ascend to the Torah; and for practical law the later authorities wrote that they do not practice thus; and all the more so regarding baalei berit one certainly should not practice thus.
