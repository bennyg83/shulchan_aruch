It is stated in the Rif, chapter 4 of Megillah, citing the Yerushalmi: Moshe instituted for Israel that three people should read from the Torah on Monday and Thursday and at Minchah on Shabbos. This requires study, for in the chapter Merubah it is stated that Israel traveled three days without Torah in the days of Moshe, and the prophets among them arose and instituted that they should read on Monday, Thursday, and Shabbos, while Ezra instituted the reading at Minchah. Tosafos at the beginning of chapter 2 of Berachos likewise write that Ezra instituted Torah reading. Bach asks against them in siman 685 from the passage in Merubah. It may be said that Tosafos were not concerned there with this precision. Their primary difficulty is that, since there was already an institution of the prophets, it is inappropriate to say that the verse “and they shall be” came to teach this. What the Rif writes, that Ezra instituted the reading on Monday and Thursday, refers to the number of readers, as stated in the Gemara. Accordingly, Moshe instituted the number of readers on Shabbos, Yom Tov, Rosh Chodesh, and Chol HaMoed. This is also implied by Rambam, chapter 12 of Hilchos Tefillah, and Semak, siman 149. And they may not add. Because of the loss of work for the people.
