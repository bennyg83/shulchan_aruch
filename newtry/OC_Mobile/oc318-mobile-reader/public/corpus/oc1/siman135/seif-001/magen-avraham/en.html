See Rif ch. 4 of Megillah, Yerushalmi: Moshe enacted for Israel that they read three [aliyot] on Monday and Thursday and at Minchah — end of his words. And it requires investigation, for in Megillah it states that Israel went three days without Torah in the days of Moshe; the prophets among them stood and enacted that they read on Monday and Thursday and Shabbat, and Ezra enacted at Minchah; and Tosafos Berachot 2b also wrote that Ezra enacted Torah reading; and Bach challenged them in siman 285 from what is in Megillah, etc.; and it is possible to say Tosafos did not descend to precision on this, and the essence of their difficulty: since the prophets' enactment existed, there is no need to say the verse "and they shall be" came to teach this; and that which the Rif wrote that Ezra enacted on Monday and Thursday — this is from where are the readers, as stated in the Gemara; and if so Moshe enacted from where are the readers on Shabbat, festivals, Rosh Chodesh, and Chol HaMoed, as is clear in Rambam ch. 12 of Tefillah and in Semak siman 149: {Rama: And one does not add [aliyot]} because of nullification of melacha for the people.
