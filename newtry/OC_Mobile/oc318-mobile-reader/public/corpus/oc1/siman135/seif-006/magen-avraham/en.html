Reciting Barchu is not considered a beginning. This implies that if he began saying “Blessed are You, Hashem,” it is considered a beginning. Although he could conclude, “Blessed are You, Hashem, teach me Your statutes,” this is nevertheless improper, as stated in siman 206. See there.
And the Yisrael remains standing. So that he should not be embarrassed.
But he may be called first. Darchei Moshe writes in the name of Maharil that even according to Rashi’s first explanation, the levi may be called first. This also appears from Rambam, Ran, and Rabbeinu Yerucham. See there. It appears that this applies specifically when the Yisrael is not greater than the levi. See what I wrote at the end of siman 11.
