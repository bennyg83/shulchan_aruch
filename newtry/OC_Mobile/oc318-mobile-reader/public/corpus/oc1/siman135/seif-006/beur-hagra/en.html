(6) If he entered, etc. — for if because of honor of a Torah scholar he does not read, all the more so because of a blessing that is not needed — that they permitted to read by heart for the High Priest on Yom Kippur, as written in chapter 7 of Yoma; and he transgresses because of "you shall not bear," as written in chapter 5 of Berachot 33a.

But if, etc. — since they already said the blessing with the Divine Name.

And he stands, etc. and then, etc. — that he must read, as written in the last chapter of Berachot 55a "three," etc.

And he should not ascend, etc. — Rambam and per the first explanation in Rashi there, and proof from what is written in chapter 2 of Ketubot 25b "I am established," etc. — that one read after him Levi; and see Tosafot there and there, and Rambam wrote after him Levi in general, and Shulchan Aruch omitted the word "in general" that Shenei Luchot HaBrit wrote: they practice to read, etc.

But the first, etc. — as Rambam and Shulchan Aruch wrote after him, and as Rosh wrote there: as R. Avin said, "In my days I never blessed before a priest, nor did an Israelite bless before me."

And when they read, etc. — as written "I am established," etc., and because of those leaving — it is a general concern.
