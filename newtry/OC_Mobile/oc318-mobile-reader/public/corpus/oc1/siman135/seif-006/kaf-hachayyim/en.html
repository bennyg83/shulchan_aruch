6) If on one Shabbos they began to read the parashah and some of the olim read in it but did not finish the sidra on account of a quarrel, they finish it on the coming Shabbos together with the parashah belonging to that Shabbos; and if they finished the sidra, whether in another Sefer Torah or in another synagogue, one need not read it again on the coming Shabbos. Maharam Mintz there; Knesses HaGedolah there; Olas Tamid there; Magen Avraham there; Yad Aharon there; Soles Belulah there; Emes LeYaakov there, ot 3. And this is specifically when the congregation of this synagogue read it, only that it was in another synagogue; but if this congregation did not read it at all, one must complete it. Eliyah Rabbah there; Ledovid Emes there, ot 3. And this one that they did not finish on account of a quarrel: one must begin on the coming Shabbos from the head of the parashah of the previous Shabbos and read it with one person, and he should also begin to read from the parashah of the day's obligation, as mentioned in the previous ot.
