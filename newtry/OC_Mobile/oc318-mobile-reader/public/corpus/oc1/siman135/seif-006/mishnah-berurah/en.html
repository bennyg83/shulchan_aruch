(19) After he began, etc. Meaning, after he said, “Baruch Atah Hashem.”
(20) He does not interrupt. This is so that his blessing not be in vain. Nor does this cast doubt on the kohen, for people will assume that he was not present when the Yisrael was called, or that they mistakenly thought he was absent. It is reasonable that the Yisrael counts toward the seven aliyos and that the kohen need not be called after him. Even if the kohen was present when the Yisrael was called, but they mistakenly thought he was absent, the Yisrael still counts toward the seven, and the kohen should not be called after him, lest people question his kehunah because he read after a Yisrael.
(21) It is not considered a beginning. Saying Barchu alone is not a blessing in vain, since the congregation answered Baruch Hashem Hamevorach Leolam Vaed. Biur HaGra. Nevertheless, the kohen repeats Barchu. Eliyah Rabbah.
(22) The Yisrael remains standing. So that he not be embarrassed.
(23) A levi should not be called after him. Since a Yisrael read before him, if a levi were called afterward people might say that the first person was a kohen. This concern remains even if, when the Yisrael was called, it was announced that he was being called in place of a kohen, because people who enter later did not hear the announcement. Perishah.
(24) He may receive the aliyah. A levi is no worse than a Yisrael. This applies specifically when the Yisrael is not greater than the levi; otherwise, the Yisrael should be called first and the levi should not be called at all. Later authorities. Whether a levi may be called for the third or fourth aliyah where a Yisrael was called in place of a kohen is disputed. According to the ruling below at the end of seif 10, cited by Magen Avraham in the name of Levush, that in these countries a kohen or levi is called only for the final aliyah or maftir, this question has no practical consequence.
(25) And when he is called. This applies both when a Yisrael is called in place of a kohen and when he is called in place of a levi.
