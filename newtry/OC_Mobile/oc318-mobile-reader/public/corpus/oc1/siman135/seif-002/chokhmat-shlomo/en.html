Seif 2. If the public omitted the weekly parashah on one Shabbos, etc. I was asked about a city in which Parashas Pinchas was not read publicly on Shabbos. What should they do? I answered that there is no remedy, because on the following Shabbos Matos and Masei will be joined, and three parshiyos may not be read. There is no partial remedy, as Magen Avraham writes. Although Magen Avraham speaks of a case where the first Shabbos had two joined parshiyos, the same law certainly applies in the reverse case. There is no logical distinction, since his reason is that three parshiyos may not be read. What difference is there whether the double parashah was first or afterward? Even if one were to say that his wording is deliberate and applies only when there were two parshiyos at the outset, this would be because when the two joined parshiyos occur afterward there is a remedy: on the following Shabbos they may read two parshiyos, and on the Shabbos after that they may read the third, so that they are read two at a time. Even so, in the present case there is no remedy, because afterward begins a different book, Devarim, and they cannot be joined together, as Magen Avraham writes. Therefore, there is indeed no remedy here. Examine this carefully, for it is clear and straightforward.
