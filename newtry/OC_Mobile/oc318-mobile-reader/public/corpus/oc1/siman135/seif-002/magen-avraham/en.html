They failed to read on Shabbos. The same applies if they began the reading but did not complete it because of a quarrel. If, however, two parashiyos were joined on the Shabbos when they failed to read, they do not read three parashiyos on the following Shabbos, for we never find that three parashiyos are read. One should not say that they should at least read two of them, because an institution cannot be fulfilled partially. Hagahos Minhagim writes concerning this: “His words do not appear correct to me.” If they failed to read Parashas Vayechi, they should not read it together with Parashas Shemos, because when two parashiyos are read, one person must read from the end of the first parashah into the beginning of the second so that they are joined, and it is improper to do this from two separate Sifrei Torah. Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 85.
