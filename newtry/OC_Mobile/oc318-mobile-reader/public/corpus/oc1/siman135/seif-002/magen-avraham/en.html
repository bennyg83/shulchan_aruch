They annulled Shabbat. And similarly if they began and did not finish because of a fight; but if two portions were connected on that Shabbat that was annulled, they do not read on the coming Shabbat a third — for we never find reading three; and I say they should read two, at the very least, for there is no institution for halves (and in Hagahot Maimoniyot it is written on this, and his words do not appear correct in my eyes). And if they annulled Parashat Vayechi, one does not read it with Parashat Shemot — for when reading two portions one must read one man from the end of the first portion to the beginning of the second so they are connected, and this is not proper to do in two scrolls (Rama miGeña siman 85).
