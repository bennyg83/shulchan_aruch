And if — the Taz in siman 131 orach 10 — that all seven days of the festival are like a moed, and the same applies to a widower and widow for three days, and one who remarries a divorcee — it is also possible that it is his moed even from a marriage; and even though in siman 546 seif 2 it is permitted to remarry a divorcee from marriage — Magen Avraham there — requires further study on this. And what he wrote in the name of Ritzba, etc. — Even HaEzer in the name of Mordechai: three bridegrooms and one kohen and two Israelites — the kohen reads twice; and in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot: two bridegrooms (Israelites) or three, etc. — it appears he disagrees with Mordechai, for there another may not go up and the kohen reads twice; and if so, among Israelites only one goes up. Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Ritzba: two Israelites read — kohen and Levi; and the two bridegrooms — since kohen and Levi go up permissibly — but one bridegroom one may not add and call a fourth bridegroom, for it is not required, and let it be the third; and further that a bridegroom precedes other Israelites, as Peri Chadash wrote — proof from Moed Katan: minyan for a bridegroom who reclines at the head, as it is said "as a bridegroom he shall wear splendor" — therefore when there is no kohen the bridegroom reads first, see there. A. One married a divorcee and one a chalutzah — both are equal, not like Shakh — since they are Israelites. And if one is a virgin and the other not a virgin, it is also possible they are equal per Beit Yosef. And what Levush wrote that they did not practice thus — it appears regarding covenant partners, as Peri Chadash wrote; but Magen Avraham understood that also for a bridegroom they do not practice thus, and so in Bedek HaBayit that also for a bridegroom they do not practice. Even HaEzer siman 54 seif 2 — requires further study if a widower married a non-virgin whether work is forbidden and whether it is a moed for mourning, for a covenant partner observes seven days of mourning — see Orach Chaim 132 orach 4 and Peri Chadash here — requires further study for several matters.
