(2) There: and one does not add upon them, etc. — because of nullification of melacha for the people; and also they do not read haftarah from the prophets for this reason — Levush, Taz seif 1, Magen Avraham seif 1. And Shabbat at Minchah, where there is no nullification of melacha, nevertheless since they expound until close to dark, the Sages did not wish to trouble the congregation with addition — so Rashi explained there in Megillah, Maharsha seif 1; and however in Shaar HaKavanot there the Maggid wrote the reason in secret: because now on Monday and Thursday only Netzach of Abba is revealed, for the Netzachot of Abba break forth and their illumination goes outward; therefore they read three who ascend: Kohen corresponding to Netzach right line, Levi corresponding to Hod left line, Israel corresponding to Yesod middle line; and this illumination does not remain thus except temporarily, and afterward they return and are concealed inward as at first; therefore immediately after reading they close the Torah scroll and return it to its place inside the heichal — end of his words; and likewise the custom of Beit El Yakbatz that immediately after reading, before Ashrei and "Uva leTzion," they return the Torah scroll to its place, whether on weekday, Rosh Chodesh, or Chol HaMoed — only on Shabbat and Yom Tov after Ashrei, and the three who ascend at Shabbat Minchah are corresponding to the three upper ones of Abba revealed now: one corresponding to Chochmah, one to Binah, one to Daat, as written in Shaar HaKavanot p. 75a and 72 — see there; and therefore they do not diminish from them and do not add upon them; and see below section 19.
