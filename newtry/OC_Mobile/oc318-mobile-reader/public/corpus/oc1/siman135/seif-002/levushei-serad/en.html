From the verse “and these words shall be.” The Gemara derives there from “and these words shall be upon your heart” that they must remain in their original form, meaning specifically in Lashon HaKodesh. It seeks to say there that the entire Torah was given in Lashon HaKodesh. Rashi explains that this refers to reading from the Torah. Tosafos objects that Ezra instituted this, so before Ezra came, what need was there for the verse “and these words shall be”? See there, for the words of Tosafos in their place require clarification.
