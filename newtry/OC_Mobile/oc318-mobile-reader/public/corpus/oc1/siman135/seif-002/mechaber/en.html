The place that we stop [reading from the Torah] on Shabbat morning, from there we [start to] read on [Shabbat] mincha [at the afternoon service], and on Monday, Thursday, and on the following Shabbat [morning].Gloss: If they missed the public reading of the parasha on one Shabbat, then the following Shabbat they read that parasha [i.e., the missed one] along with the parasha belonging to that [i.e., the following] Shabbat (Or Zarua); and see below, chapter 282 regarding adding [aliyot].
