If they canceled it. The same applies if they began but did not finish because of a quarrel. If, however, two parshiyos were joined on the Shabbos whose reading they canceled, they do not read three parshiyos on the following Shabbos, for we never find three parshiyos read together. One cannot argue that they should at least read two, because there is no partial remedy. If they failed to read Parashas Vayechi, they should not read it together with Parashas Shemos, because one person must read from the end of the first parashah into the beginning of the second so that they are joined, and this is improper when using two Sifrei Torah. Maharam Mintz, siman 85. See Ateres Zekeinim.
