(14) People of stature, etc. — In Yerushalmi chapter 7 of Yoma: everywhere you say "we go after the Torah," and here you would say "we bring the Torah to him" — because they are people great in Torah, the Torah was elevated in them; and this is what he wrote "and if he is," etc. — and specifically one important in Torah, as written; and behold there they bring the Torah to the Exilarch — R. Yudan bar Bon said there: because the seed of David is sunk there, they act (and not) like their fathers' custom; see Rema Panim siman 78.
