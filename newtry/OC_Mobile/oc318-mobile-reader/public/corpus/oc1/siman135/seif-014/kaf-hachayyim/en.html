14) And the Maharik wrote in shoresh 9 concerning a congregation that had the custom that on Shabbos Bereishis one of the congregation would donate money for the needs of the synagogue in order to read first at the beginning of the Torah; and they also had the custom that if there is a Kohen there, he purchases that mitzvah or forgoes his honor and leaves the synagogue; see there. And the Beis Yosef cited him, and the Magen Avraham, s.k. 7. However, the Beis Yosef there disagreed with him, and wrote that if the Kohen forgoes his honor in order that the congregation fulfill their custom, he need not leave; and see there, where he brought supports for this from the Gemara, and wrote that we do not find that the Kohen must leave outside the synagogue except specifically regarding nesias kapayim, because it contains three positive commandments, and even that only if they said to him to ascend, as stated in siman 128; see there. And the Pri Chadash concurred with his words, ot 3. And so wrote the Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos Beis Yosef, that he found written in manuscript by the Maharar Levi Trivush the Frenchman, of blessed memory, that even if the Kohen does not leave the synagogue, the Yisrael who donated may ascend to read first; and the Emes LeYaakov cited him in the law of the ascent to the Sefer Torah, ot 46; Ledovid Emes, siman 5, ot 46 (and see the responsa Chasam Sofer, siman 24). And so wrote the Birkei Yosef, ot 5, in the name of the Mahar"sh Sha'ar Aryeh in manuscript, that there are those who have the custom that on the day of Shavuos the rav of the place reads in place of the Kohen, for the honor of the Torah, and the Kohen need not leave, and the shaliach tzibbur says explicitly, 'Let the rav stand,' etc. End of his language. And so wrote the Gaon Mahar"sh Kluger at the end of the sefer StaM, in the kuntres 'Prat V'Olelos,' in the addenda to the laws of Sefer Torah, folio 86a, that the Ashkenazim in Eretz Yisrael have the custom that a Yisrael reads in place of a Kohen, and sometimes the chazan says it even though there is a Kohen here, because guests who need to be given an aliyah to the Sefer Torah are frequently present among them, and presumably the Kohanim forgo to them, since it is known that it is specifically for the honor of the guests; there is no concern of coming to quarrel, for at a time when there are no guests they do not forgo, etc.; and further, great is human dignity, etc., since the way is to give aliyos to the guests; see there well. And our friend the Rav Eretz Chaim, may God protect and preserve him, cited him in this siman.
