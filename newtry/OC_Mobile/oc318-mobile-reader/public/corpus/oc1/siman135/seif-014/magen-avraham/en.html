They do not bring it. Meaning, when they wish to read from the Sefer Torah with a minyan.
For a day or two. Maharam of Padua writes: If they prepare an aron or a chest for it for a day or two, it is permitted. Darchei Moshe, siman 149. This implies that the essential factor is whether they establish a fixed place there for the Sefer Torah.
An important person. Hagahos Ashri, citing Or Zarua, implies that both conditions are required, namely that he be ill and an important person. Or Zarua itself, however, implies that if he is ill it is permitted even if he is not important, and if he is important it is permitted even if he is not ill. Darchei Moshe. It appears to me that for Parashas Zachor it is permitted to bring a Sefer Torah to him. See the end of siman 685.
