Magen Avraham seif katan 10 wrote in Darkei Moshe to resolve the Taz's difficulties, for the Taz holds that per the first explanation even before an Israelite he may not go up; and therefore he explained like the Rambam that the word "after him" is not precise. Magen Avraham explains that what Rashi said in the first explanation — that the Levi should not read at all — means after Israelites if they are equal, and even three or four; and this contradicts explanation 2 that three or four are permitted. If so, the Rambam's words and Shulchan Aruch and Rama are all per the first explanation that he may not go up after him specifically (and R"L when equal), but the first [aliyah] he goes up — and this is what Magen Avraham wrote, and so too the Rambam — meaning that for the Taz one must push that the word "after him" is not precise. And he holds that this is also proven from what the Rama wrote "the first may go up" — and if per the Taz, which is explanation 2, the word "may" is not applicable, for in explanation 2 it is explicit that specifically the Levi precedes; but per Magen Avraham it is not difficult, for this is explanation 1, and that explanation one can hold that as he wishes he may do.
