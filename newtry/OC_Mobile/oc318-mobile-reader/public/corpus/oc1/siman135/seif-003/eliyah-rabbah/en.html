However, if he is able to read, etc. The later authorities wrote in the name of the responsum of Re'em that one who does not know how to read at all, and also does not know how to recognize the letters, should not be appointed among those called up. Perhaps because of this the practice is to add beyond seven, for nowadays they do not customarily examine him at all. See Magen Avraham, and as below in siman 141.
