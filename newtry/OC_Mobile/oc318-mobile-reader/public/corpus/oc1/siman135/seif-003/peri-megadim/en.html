Kohen — Taz; and see Mefaresh in Yerushalmi (ch. 2 Gittin ch. 5) that R' Yehudah per his view derives from "and the Kohanim sons of Levi approached" (Devarim 21) — evidently before the elders of Israel; unlike "and you shall sanctify him" one can say sanctity of a Torah scholar and Torah is preferable — see there; and see Levush who brought Rav Matna's derivation together with "and you shall sanctify him," and Netsiv holds only "and he gave it to the Kohanim" (Devarim 31), and R' Yehudah brought all of them with "and you shall sanctify him," and there is much difference as written at the beginning of this siman; and Even HaEzer and Perishah answered that they all yield before him because of the abundance of his wisdom — we require this, and it is not found now except yielding not from abundance of wisdom; and if he is outstanding in his generation the younger says he is great — see there on this; and therefore the custom that a Kohen reads first. The Mechaber in Shulchan Aruch omitted the word "today" that Rambam goes per his view that this is settled in Beit Yosef. And even if the Kohen waives, R' Yehudah [holds] even on Monday and Thursday nowadays.
