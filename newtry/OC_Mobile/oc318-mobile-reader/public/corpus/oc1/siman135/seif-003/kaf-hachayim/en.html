(3) There in gloss: and if there were two bridegrooms, etc., it is permitted to add, etc. — Maran z"l wrote in Beit Yosef s.v.: we did not practice to add on Monday and Thursday for bridegrooms, and also we did not hear anyone who practiced thus — end of his words; and they brought it in Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Beit Yosef and wrote likewise in Tikkun Yissachar p. 55a; and likewise Peri Chadash agreed, section 1; and in Shach Knesset HaGedolah Hagahot Beit Yosef section 2 wrote that thus he ruled in practical law; and likewise Levush that they do not practice thus; and they brought Magen Avraham seif 2 and Eliyah Rabbah section 1, and wrote there Eliyah Rabbah: per Levush's words, whether between two covenant partners or two bridegrooms they do not practice to add; and likewise Even HaEzer section 2; and likewise from Maamar Mordechai section 1 that they do not practice to add on Monday and Thursday beyond three — see there; and so explicitly in the previous section — see there the Ari z"l that per the secret of the matter there is no place for addition on Monday and Thursday — see there and understand.
