And even for the one who says in siman 66 seif 4 that one interrupts — that is when they called him; but l'chatchila one should not read for him, for there is no concern of flaw since he is occupied with Shema; and they do not wait until he finishes, for we are concerned for congregational trouble; and not specifically that he heard — likewise when occupied in any place he may not interrupt, such as in the blessing before Shema [Beit Yosef Rashba Maharik]; and possibly verses of song it is permitted to read for him; see siman 139 seif 8.

And Israelites read. And they do not wait for him because of honor of the congregation [Rashba Maharik]. If there is no priest, they follow the greatest in wisdom; and from where — even though the other is greater in years (Beit Zahav Rama miGeña R. Nissim Gaon) — if the custom is to buy with money whoever will read first at the beginning of the Torah in Bereishit, and if an Israelite bought it, the priest waives his honor and leaves the synagogue; and once a priest did not want to leave the synagogue — it is permitted to compel him through the government to leave the synagogue so the custom will not be nullified and honor of Torah [Beit Yosef Maharik]; and even if he does not leave, the Israelite may read [so is the law]; however from Maharik's language it does not imply so — for otherwise there is no reason to compel through the government.
