(18) And it is good that the Kohen should leave — meaning that by law even if he does not leave he may still have an Israelite called in his place, since he cannot ascend; nevertheless l'chatchila it is better that he leave the synagogue, lest there be concern for a defect because not everyone knows he is not fasting. And if there is another Kohen in the synagogue who is fasting, he need not leave per all opinions, only inform the shaliach tzibbur not to call him. A congregation that practiced formerly that on Shabbat Bereishit one of the congregation would volunteer money for synagogue needs so he would be called first at the beginning of the Torah reading, and the Kohen does not wish to waive — they are not concerned for his words and the congregation does as their custom, since it is an ancient custom enacted for honor of the Torah [Maharik shoresh 9 — see there, he praises this custom]; and see Kenahag and Peri Chadash who agreed that even if he does not wish to leave the synagogue, nevertheless they are not concerned and call the volunteering Israelite. And this is specifically in such a case where there was an ancient agreed custom enacted also with permission of the Kohanim, whose custom was always to leave when Israelites are called, for elevation and honor of the Torah when they donate large sums for reading its opening; unlike a city that wanted to establish a fixed custom to sell matzot without permission of the Kohanim and that Kohanim leave when Torah is read when authority is not in their hands [Chatham Sofer siman 24; likewise in teshuvah Shivat Tzion].
