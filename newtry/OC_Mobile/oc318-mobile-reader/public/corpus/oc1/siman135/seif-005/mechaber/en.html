If the Torah scroll is opened and the Kohen is reciting Shema, he is not permitted to interrupt, and they call up a Yisrael in place of him.Gloss: And similarly, on a fast day that follow Pesach and Sukkot, where we read "Vay'chal" - if the Kohen is not fasting, we call a Yisrael [instead]; and it's preferable for the Kohen to go out from the synagogue (Maharik - shoresh 9).'.
