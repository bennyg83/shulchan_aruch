To interrupt. Even according to the opinion in siman 66, seif 4, that one interrupts, that applies when he has already been called; initially, however, he should not be called. This is not limited to Krias Shema. The same applies whenever he is engaged in a place where he may not interrupt, such as the blessings of Krias Shema. It is possible that during Pesukei DeZimrah it is permitted to call him. Magen Avraham.
In his place. They do not wait for him because of the honor of the congregation. Maharik. If the custom is to purchase with money the privilege of being called first at the beginning of the Torah in Bereishis, and a Yisrael purchases it, the kohen waives his honor and leaves the synagogue. (On one occasion the kohen refused to leave the synagogue.) It is permitted to compel him through the authorities to leave the synagogue so that the honor of the Torah not be diminished. Maharik there. Knesses HaGedolah and Peri Chadash rule that even if the kohen does not leave, the Yisrael may be called. See there. See Responsa Knesses Yechezkel, question 7, who expresses doubt about this.
