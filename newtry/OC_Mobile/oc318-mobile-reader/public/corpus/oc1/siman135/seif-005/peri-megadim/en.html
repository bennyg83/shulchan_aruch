And it is proper. See Taz and Beit Yosef regarding Rav and Rav Huna, who received the first aliyah in place of kohanim, while the kohanim did not leave the synagogue. There, they were known and renowned as the leading scholars of the generation, so there was no defect to the kohanim. On a fast day, however, there may be concern for a defect. See siman 566, seif katan 7, regarding one who is not fasting and is called at Minchah. See there, and Magen Avraham there, seif katan 8: at Shacharis on Monday and Thursday, he may receive an aliyah even though they set aside the weekly portion, but not on other weekdays.
