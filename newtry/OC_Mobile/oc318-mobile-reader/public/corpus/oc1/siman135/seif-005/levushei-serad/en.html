Seif katan 2 “But only one chassan.” Meaning, one might have thought that he should be the fourth person called, since he is permitted to be added. The author therefore says this is not so, because the person who would otherwise be called third would also acquire the status of an addition. This differs from a case of two chassanim, where it is proper to call four, since both are permitted to be added. A kohen and levi certainly cannot be described as additions. This is the intent of Ritzba. The Taz therefore asks that for this very reason, namely that the occasion belongs only to the chassanim, one should not be permitted to add even for two chassanim, since the rest of the congregation would suffer loss of work. This is readily understood.
