(5) He is not, etc. — although we say in Berachot 14b "Rav Meshi," etc. — specifically regarding tefillin, as Tosafot wrote there s.v. and rests, etc.

And they read, etc. — and there is no concern here for profaning the priesthood since he cannot read; and also they do not wait for him because of honor of the congregation — for even in place of a Torah scholar greater than him they set aside his sanctity, all the more so in a place of congregational trouble that they permitted the High Priest to read by heart, all the more so in such a case. And it is good, etc. — because of his flaw, as written there regarding priest after priest, etc. and regarding raising of hands; and see above siman 128 seif 4. Maharik — but Beit Yosef objected to him: for Rav and R. Huna who read among priests — we do not find they required the priests to leave; and further, if they left, he should not have said "this case is different," etc. that subjugated — without this, since they left, it is permitted; rather necessarily they were reading before them and were not concerned for flaw, for it is recognizable; likewise here it is recognizable; therefore he wrote "and it is good," etc.
