And the portion Olah — From the beginning of Vayikra through “Or if from the flock …” The portion Minchah—from “And when a soul offers …” through “you shall offer.” [If he has time he may also read in Parashas Tzav from “This is the law of the Minchah offering …” through “it is most holy.”] (Siman 48.) A kohen adds the verse “Every meal-offering of a kohen shall be wholly burnt.” The portion Shelamim—from “And if a sacrifice of peace-offering …” through “Or if of the flock …”; in Parashas Tzav from “This is the law of the Shelamim …” through “in the Sinai desert”—this is also Parashas Todah; afterward Parashas Nesachim in Parashas Shelach from “Speak to the children of Israel … through ‘I am the Lord your God.’” Parashas Chatas in Vayikra—from “If a soul sin …” through “and it shall be forgiven him” (if he has time also Parashas Tzav “This is the law of the Chatas … through shall be burned with fire”) (Siman 48). Parashas Asham in Parashas Tzav—from “This is the law of the Asham … through it is an asham” (if time, also in Vayikra) (there). Parashas Oleh ve’Yored as well—for a vain oath—from “If a soul sin …” through “it shall be to the kohen like a meal-offering” (Magen Avraham). See Pri Megadim—there is doubt whether one must say Parashas Nesachim three times for Olah, Todah, and Shelamim, since each requires libations, or once suffices for reading; Eshel Avraham inclines thus; it is good to say all these portions before prayer in synagogue (Eshel HaChayim citing Zohar)—provided one does not thereby delay Kerias Shema or even communal prayer. One must confess before reading Parashas HaKorbanos (Eshel HaChayim citing Eliyah Rabbah and Pri Megadim); confession should be standing. Today Sefer Avodah Tamah is printed explaining every detail of saying the sacrifices and quoting Rambam’s order of offering needed for this; books elaborate the mitzvah’s greatness and how good it is for one who practices thus to learn these matters first from Gemara or Rambam so he later understands what he says—it is then considered as if he actually offered a sacrifice; likewise what we say daily “Abbaye would arrange …”; regarding preparing incense one should study to understand what one says—see Shelah’s glosses on Sefer Yesh Nohalin at length.
