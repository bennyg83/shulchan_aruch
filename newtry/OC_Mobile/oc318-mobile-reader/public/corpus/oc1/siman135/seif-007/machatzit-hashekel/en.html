Seif katan 7: And they call, etc. The honor of the congregation, etc., as written in the preceding seif katan. The Shulchan Aruch writes, “It is proper that he leave,” and so forth, because of concern for discrediting him. Although in truth there is no concern for discredit, as written in the preceding seif katan, nevertheless, as an added precaution he should leave the synagogue. Taz, however, disagrees; see there. Regarding the Monday and Thursday fasts, Magen Avraham concludes below in siman 566, seif katan 8, that at Shacharis, when they would in any event have read from the Torah, although the weekly portion is displaced by the fast and they read Vayechal, a kohen who is not fasting may still receive an aliyah. At Minchah, however, when there would have been no Torah reading absent the fast, he should not receive an aliyah. Nevertheless, if he did receive one, he committed no prohibition. See there.
