They do not interrupt him. Otherwise the blessing would have been recited in vain. Furthermore, the concern for discredit applies only when a levi is called after another levi. When a levi is present but was not called, people may assume that he was not present when the kohen completed the first aliyah, or that they mistakenly thought he was absent. There is therefore no concern for discredit. Rashba, siman 229. The same reasoning should likewise apply to a kohen.
