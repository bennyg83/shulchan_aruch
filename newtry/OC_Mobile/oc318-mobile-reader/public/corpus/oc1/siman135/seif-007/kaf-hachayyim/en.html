7) One congregation who thought that parashas Matos and Masei were joined, as in most years, and read the two parshiyos on one Shabbos, and after they departed it became known to them that they were not joined: one need not return and read a second time in parashas Matos, seven men, even though in truth they did not read seven in the matter of the day, for it is not an obligation to read the weekly parashah but a custom; and on the Shabbos following it they return and read parashas Masei. Emes LeYaakov there, ot 4, in the name of the Pri Ha'aretz; and he wrote that the same applies on Monday and Thursday, if the congregation erred and read in a different sidra, three men, that one need not return a second time to read the weekly parashah, for in a matter of enactment we do not repeat the act; see there. And so wrote Ledovid Emes there, ot 4.
