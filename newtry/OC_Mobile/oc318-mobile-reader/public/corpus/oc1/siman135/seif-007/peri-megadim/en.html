And he began. See Taz. Here too, reciting Barchu is not considered the beginning of the aliyah. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 6. According to the text before us, the matter is clear. Here the kohen need not remain standing, because he suffers no embarrassment, having already received an aliyah. By contrast, when a Yisrael is called and then a kohen arrives, the Yisrael should remain standing because of the embarrassment. See Levush, seif 7, and Nechalas Tzvi, seif katan 11.
