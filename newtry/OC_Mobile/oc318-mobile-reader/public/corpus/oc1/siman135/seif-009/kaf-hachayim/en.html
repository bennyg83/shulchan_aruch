(9) There in gloss: if they cancelled one Shabbat, etc. — and if they cancelled Shabbat in the morning of the reading and at Minchah they found respite, it is written in the Gemara: if there is leisure to read the entire parasha they read it at Minchah, the entire parasha, and call seven [readers], for every day of Shabbat is time for reading; but if also at Minchah there was no respite, they do not read on Monday and Thursday the entire parasha from the past Shabbat because of nullification of melacha for the people, but read from the parasha of the coming Shabbat — see there; and they brought in Bar Yochai on Likutei Torah on this seif and wrote that although in Zohar parashat Vayakhel of R' Elazar end 72b it implies one should not read at all at Minchah seven [readers], and also not read the entire parasha, nevertheless per the Gemara it is explained — see there; however Chida in Sefer Chaim Shaal ch. 1 siman 71 orach 5 and ch. 2 siman 16 wrote plainly that one should not read even at Shabbat Minchah the parasha that was cancelled, and even without additional readers, only on the coming Shabbat read two parshiyot, as he wrote in his Birkei Yosef — see there; and they brought Ikarei HaDat siman 6 orach 60, Beit Yosef Meir in laws of error 168 orach 5; and see below siman 137 orach 18; and however on Monday and Thursday they read the parasha of the coming Shabbat like the rest of the congregation, because of "do not divide yourselves" — Pischei Teshuvah orach 2 — and understand.
