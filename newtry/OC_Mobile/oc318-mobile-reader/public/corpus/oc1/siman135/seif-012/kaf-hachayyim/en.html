12) [Seif 3] A Kohen reads from the Torah first, etc. For so the Sages of blessed memory instituted, on account of the ways of peace, so that they not come to quarrel and each one say, 'I will read first'; and they also supported it with a verse, as it is written, 'And Moshe wrote this Torah and gave it to the Kohanim, the sons of Levi' -- and we ask: is it not so that I already know that the Kohanim are the sons of Levi? And we answer: rather, a Kohen first; and as it is also written regarding a Kohen, 'and you shall sanctify him,' for every matter of holiness, to be first, etc., and afterward the Levi, and afterward all the people, etc. And that which they needed to say 'on account of the ways of peace,' since they supported it with a verse: this is to say that even if the Kohen wishes to forgo his honor and give honor to his teacher or to one greater than him, he is not permitted. Levush; and it is from the Gemara Gittin, folio 59b. And it is implied from the words of the Levush that it is rabbinic and the verse is a support. However, see the Magen Avraham, siman 201, s.k. 4, who brought a dispute in this, and there are those who say that it is from the Torah; see there.
