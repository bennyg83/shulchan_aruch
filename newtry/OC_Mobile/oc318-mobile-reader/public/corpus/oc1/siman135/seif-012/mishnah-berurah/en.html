(43) "Where all are kohanim," etc. This applies specifically when there is no Levi there at all, so that the order established by the Sages cannot be followed, and therefore it is preferable to alter the order for the sake of peace. But if there is even 1 Levi there, they read in the order stated in the Mishnah: Kohen, Levi, Yisrael, and afterward the remaining aliyot are given to kohanim. (44) "One Yisrael among them." If there are 2 Yisraelim, and all the more so if there are more, this falls under what the Shulchan Aruch writes afterward, that there are not enough Yisraelim to fill all the aliyot. Therefore a kohen reads first, a kohen also reads in place of the Levi, then the Yisraelim read, and the remaining aliyot are completed by kohanim. If there are 2 Levites and 2 Yisraelim, the order is Kohen, Levi, Yisrael, then another Kohen, Levi, Yisrael, and a kohen reads seventh, and certainly the same applies to additional aliyot. (45) "For the sake of peace." So that each one should not say, "Why are you called first rather than I?" The same applies when there is only 1 Levi in a city consisting entirely of kohanim: that Levi reads first for this reason. If there are only kohanim and Levites, a kohen may read after a kohen when a Levi intervenes between them. A kohen who is a mourner, when there is no other kohen in the synagogue on Shabbos, may be called as Kohen so that public mourning will not be apparent. On a weekday he should not receive the aliyah, because a mourner is forbidden Torah study. Nevertheless, both on a weekday and on Shabbos it is preferable that he leave the synagogue before the reading begins, and they should call a Yisrael and a Levi in place of a kohen. [Eliyah Rabbah and other authorities]
