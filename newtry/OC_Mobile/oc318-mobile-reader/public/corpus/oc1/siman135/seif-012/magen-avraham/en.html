A congregation consisting entirely of kohanim, etc. Maharik, shoresh 9, writes that this follows the opinion that giving precedence to a kohen is D'Rabbanan. According to the opinion that it is D'Oraisa, a kohen is always given precedence. See there, and see what I wrote in siman 201. It may be said that since there are many kohanim and it is unknown which one should precede the others, it is preferable to call a Yisrael first. If a levi is also present, the order should be Yisrael, levi, and then the kohanim. If there are two Yisraelim and two leviim, the order should be kohen, levi, Yisrael, kohen, levi, Yisrael, and the seventh should be a kohen. If there are two groups, such as kohanim and leviim, kohanim and Yisraelim, or leviim and Yisraelim, and neither group has seven members, I have found no solution that avoids quarrels. If one group has seven members, that group should receive the aliyos. If each group has seven members, they should cast lots to decide which group receives them. Levush. I do not understand why the Yisrael should precede the levi. On the contrary, the levi has precedence, as stated in seif 6. As for what he writes, that he found no solution when there are fewer than seven, it appears to me that the kohen should read twice, once in place of the levi, followed by all the Yisraelim, and then the remaining kohanim. This is the meaning of the Shulchan Aruch’s statement, “Whenever there are insufficient Yisraelim,” etc. There is no need to elaborate further.
