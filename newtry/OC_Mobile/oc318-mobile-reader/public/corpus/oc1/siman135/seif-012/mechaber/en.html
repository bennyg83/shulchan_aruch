[Regarding] a city of Kohanim - if there is one "Yisrael" among them, that person should read first for the sake of peace. And if there are not enough Yisraelim or if there are no Yisraelim at all, it is permitted to call a Kohen after a Kohen, because there is no cause for suspicion that any [of the Kohanim] are invalid, since everyone knows that there are only Kohanim there. And the same applies to a city of Levi'im.
