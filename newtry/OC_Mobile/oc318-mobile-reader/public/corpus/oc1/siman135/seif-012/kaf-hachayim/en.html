(12) [Seif 3] The kohen reads from the Torah first, etc. — for thus the Sages enacted for the sake of derachay shalom (ways of peace), so they do not come to quarrel and each say "I read first." They also relied on a verse, as it is written: "And Moshe wrote this Torah and gave it to the kohanim, sons of Levi" — and we challenge: Do I not know that kohanim are sons of Levi? — and we answer: Rather, the kohen first; and as it is also written regarding the kohen "And you shall sanctify him" — for every matter of sanctity, to read him first, etc.; and afterward the Levi, and afterward all the people, etc. And that they needed to say "for the sake of derachay shalom" — since they relied on a verse, this is to say that even if the kohen wishes to waive his honor and share honor with his rabbi or one greater than him, he is not permitted — Levush; and this is from the Gemara Gittin daf 59b. It is implied from the Levush's words that it is d'rabbanan and the verse is an asmakhta; however see Magen Avraham siman 201 seif katan 4 who brought a dispute in this, and some say it is d'oraisa — see there.
