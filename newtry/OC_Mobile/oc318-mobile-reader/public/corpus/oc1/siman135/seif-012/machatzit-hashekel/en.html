Seif katan 12: That the first, etc. The case here is, etc. Meaning, why in the case of one levi after another does he mention concern that both are disqualified, whereas with one kohen after another he mentions only concern that the first is disqualified? He answers that the ruling states the universally applicable concern. Suspicion that the first is disqualified includes even a case where it is established that their father is a kohen.
