(11) A congregation that agreed not to pray in a minyan unless they pay their dues — they cannot read from the Torah scroll and say Kaddish after him, for in people's language anything requiring a minyan is called prayer — Rosh in teshuvot klal 3 siman 9, as Kenahag in Hagahot Tur, Peri Chadash orach chaim siman 1 orach 4, Lechem Dvira siman 1 orach 4. Similarly if they agreed not to pray with Reuven — they do not read Torah if Reuven is in the congregation — Rosh's teshuvah there, Lechem Dvira there orach 9, Lechem Dvira there orach 9.
