Seif katan 11: One may not interrupt, lest it be a blessing in vain. This applies specifically if he said “Blessed are You, Hashem,” as stated above. So Taz writes.
If so, the same should apply to a kohen, etc. In seif 10, the Shulchan Aruch discusses only a case where the kohen entered after they had called a levi. From here it appears that even if the kohen had been present and they mistakenly called the levi, the law is the same as in the case of a levi here.
