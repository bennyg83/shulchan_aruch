Because of his blemish, etc. It is permitted to call his son in his place, for if he were blemished, his son too would be blemished; but his father is forbidden. This concludes the words of the Magen Avraham. At first glance, even regarding his son one should be concerned for his blemish, lest his father married a divorcee after his birth, as above in siman 128, seif 40. However, in Gittin 59 it implies that we are not concerned for this, and likewise above in seifim 8 and 9. Nevertheless, perhaps since whoever comes first acquires the right, the Sages did not distinguish, for another will come immediately before they call his son, since he does not know of his son, and he will be embarrassed.
