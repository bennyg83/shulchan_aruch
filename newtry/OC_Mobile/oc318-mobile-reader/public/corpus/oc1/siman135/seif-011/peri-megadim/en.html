Because — the Taz: when there are two Leviim and two Israelites, there is an enactment that they read kohen, Levi, Israel, kohen, Levi, Israel, and the seventh is kohen. And if there is only one Levi and one Israelite — Levush's opinion is they reverse the order: Israel, Levi, kohen; but the Taz holds they read in mishnah order, and they did not say derachay shalom for kohanim except in a matter where the mishnah order cannot be fulfilled; and so Netziv like Taz; and all the more so per the opinion that "you shall sanctify him" is Torah — Peri Chadash here; Magen Avraham 24; and what he wrote how many Leviim — Levi reads first, and afterward kohen after kohen with Levi in between — Magen Avraham orach 14; and here where all are kohanim it is especially preferable that they read thus; and see Levush further on this.
