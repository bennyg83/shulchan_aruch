Because. See Taz. If there are two leviim and two Yisraelim, there is a solution: call kohen, levi, Yisrael, kohen, levi, Yisrael, and a kohen for the seventh aliyah. If there is only one levi and one Yisrael, Levush holds that they should reverse the order and call Yisrael, levi, kohen. Taz, however, holds that they should follow the order of the Mishnah. The rule of peaceful relations among kohanim was stated only where the Mishnah’s order cannot be maintained. Nechalas Tzvi agrees with Taz, and this is certainly so according to the opinion that “and you shall sanctify him” is a Torah law. See Peri Chadash here and Magen Avraham, seif katan 20. Regarding what he writes about several leviim, a levi should be called first, followed by kohen after kohen with a levi intervening. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 14. Here, where everyone is a kohen, it is even more preferable to arrange the aliyos in this manner. See Levush further on this matter.
