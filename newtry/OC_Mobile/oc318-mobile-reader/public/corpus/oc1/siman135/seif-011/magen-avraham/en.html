A chazzan who is a kohen. Everyone knows that a kohen is permitted to serve as chazzan. Mordechai. Meaning, in their times the chazzan would call whomever he wished. If he himself could never receive an aliyah, they would not appoint him as chazzan. Nowadays, however, when the gabbai calls whomever he wishes, if the gabbai is a kohen he may not call another kohen, unless it is his brother, because everyone understands that he did so to honor him. Mateh Moshe and Maharshal. The accepted practice is not in accordance with this. See what I wrote in seif 7, and what I wrote in siman 138 in the name of Orchos Chaim.
