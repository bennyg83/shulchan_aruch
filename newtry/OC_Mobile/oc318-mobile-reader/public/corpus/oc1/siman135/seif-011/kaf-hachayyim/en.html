11) A congregation that agreed not to pray with a minyan unless their money is paid to them: they cannot read the Sefer Torah and say Kaddish after it, for in the language of people every matter that requires a minyan is called 'tefillah.' The Rosh in a responsum, klal 3, siman 9; Knesses HaGedolah in the Hagahos HaTur; Pri Chadash, ot 1; Emes LeYaakov, siman 1, ot 4; Ledovid Emes, siman 1, ot 4. And likewise, if they agreed not to pray with Reuven, they do not read from the Torah if Reuven is in the congregation. Responsum of the Rosh there; Emes LeYaakov there, ot 9; Ledovid Emes there, ot 9.
