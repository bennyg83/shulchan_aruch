(11) The common practice - That is to say, although many Rishonim hold that this enactment was not made to give precedence to an am ha'aretz before a scholar, nevertheless the practice follows the poskim who hold that, regarding Torah reading in the synagogue, because of the ways of peace, no distinction was made in this matter.
(12) Before a great scholar - There is no disgrace here to the honor of Torah, for since this is the practice, there is no degradation.
(13) Knows how to read - From the written text, as below in siman 139 seif 2.
(14) This suffices - This is specifically when there is no other kohen there besides him who can read by himself, as explained there.
(15) For if he does not know, etc. - Nowadays the practice is to call him even though he does not know how to read with the shaliach tzibbur from the written text, but only by heart. Even in this they were not accustomed to be very exact, and we assume that presumably he can at least read with the shaliach tzibbur by heart. See below, siman 139 seif 4, what we will write there.
