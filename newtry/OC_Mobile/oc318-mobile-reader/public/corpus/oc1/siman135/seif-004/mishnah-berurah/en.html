(11) The common practice. Although many Rishonim hold that this enactment was not made to give an unlearned person precedence over a Torah scholar, the practice follows the poskim who hold that, regarding Krias HaTorah in the synagogue, no distinction was made because of the ways of peace.
(12) Before a great scholar. This does not disgrace the honor of Torah, because once this is the accepted practice there is no degradation.
(13) Knows how to read. From the written text, as explained below in siman 139, seif 2.
(14) This suffices. This applies specifically when no other kohen is present who can read by himself, as explained there.
(15) For if he does not know, etc. Nowadays the practice is to call him even if he cannot read together with the chazzan from the written text, but only by heart. Even in this respect the practice is not to examine carefully, and we assume that he can at least read together with the chazzan by heart. See below, siman 139, seif 4, where this will be discussed.
