Taz, seif katan 1 “For this reason no haftarah is read” refers to Monday and Thursday, because of loss of work. On Rosh Chodesh too there is some loss of work, since men perform work, and on Chol HaMoed activity needed to prevent a loss is permitted. Therefore, no haftarah is read. Nevertheless, four are called to the Torah, because there is not as much loss of work as on an ordinary weekday.
