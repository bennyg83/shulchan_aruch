If we say that he explicitly disagrees with Magen Avraham and interprets the case like Levush, namely that they had already called him, then regarding the substance of the law he does not disagree with Magen Avraham, seif katan 6. But if he interprets it as Magen Avraham does, then he disagrees with him in the law itself, as explained. One should know that this is not limited to the blessings of Krias Shema, which are D’Rabbanan. The same applies to the paragraph of Vayomer, which is D’Rabbanan, although the first two paragraphs are Torah-level. See siman 66, seif katan 5, and siman 67, where I explained this. What he writes here concerning a defect means that it is known that the person prays at length, as stated regarding a fast day.
