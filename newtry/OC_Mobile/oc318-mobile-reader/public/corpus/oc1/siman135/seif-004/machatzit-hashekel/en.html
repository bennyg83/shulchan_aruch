Seif katan 4: They omitted, etc. This does not need to be done with two Torah scrolls. Accordingly, the same applies to Pekudei and Vayikra, and similar cases.
In Hagahos Minhagim he wrote concerning this: “The author’s words do not appear correct to me,” meaning that three sedarim should be read, unlike the Magen Avraham’s initial statement. See Eliyah Rabbah regarding what should be done if they did not read on Monday and Thursday.
