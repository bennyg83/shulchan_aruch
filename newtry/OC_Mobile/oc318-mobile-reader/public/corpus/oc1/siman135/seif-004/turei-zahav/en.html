A little supplication: “Our teacher: one who increases and one who decreases—provided he directs his heart in supplication—for a little with intention is better than much without.” Difficulty: if at first he increases with intention why compare to one who decreases; if without intention why later “better”—initially equates them; Gemara Menachos end “one who increases and one who decreases… directs heart to Heaven.” Explanation: Shabbat 10a Rava saw Rav Menuna lengthen prayer—“they abandon eternal life for temporal”—he held Torah time separate… Similarly one who lengthens prayer like Rav Menuna holds prayer time separate; one who shortens like Rava intends Torah—therefore concludes “provided he directs heart to Heaven”—meaning when shortening prayer it is for Torah’s sake; therefore our teacher said sometimes lengthening prevents concentration—better shorten with intention—therefore “one who increases and one who decreases” so one has intention—therefore ends “provided…” meaning the decrease is for intention—better little with intention than much without—that is why he decreases—to enable intention.
