That the first is disqualified. This case concerns two people known to have fathers who were kohanim. Regarding the first, people may say that his father married a divorcee and thereby disqualified his descendants. Regarding the second, this cannot be said, for if so he should not have been called even in place of a levi. If they are not established as kohanim, there is concern that both may be disqualified. If it is established that the first person’s mother was not a divorcee, it may be permitted. This also appears from Bach at the end of this siman. Yet this requires study, because there may be concern for another disqualification, such as that she committed adultery in secret.
