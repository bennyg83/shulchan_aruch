(28) But not another Kohen — and the same law: an Israelite should not be called in place of a Levi so they do not say about the first one that he is not a Kohen or that he is defective, since they did not call a Levi after him; and also lest they err about this Israelite that he is a Levi.

(29) That the first is defective — and that which it does not say "one of them is defective" as below in seif 9 is because here it deals even where we are established regarding the father of both that they are Kohanim; nevertheless regarding the first there is room to attribute that afterward it became known after they called him that his father married a divorcee, or perhaps she committed adultery in secret and afterward his offspring was profaned from the law of priestly sanctity; therefore they called again to another Kohen. But regarding the second there is no room to err thus — for if he were a challal they would not have given him importance to ascend in place of a Levi. But with two Levites one after the other there is concern regarding both that perhaps one is unfit from Levite status and is only an Israelite because he is a mamzer; nevertheless they wrote afterward that one of them is defective. And the later poskim wrote that even on a fast day when the Levi does not fast, the Kohen blesses in place of the Levi and they do not call an Israelite.
