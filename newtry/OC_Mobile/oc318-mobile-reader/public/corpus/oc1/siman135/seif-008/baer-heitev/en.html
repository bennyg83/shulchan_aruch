Disqualified. The second kohen is not subject to this suspicion, for the Gemara establishes the case where his father is known to us as a kohen. Regarding the first, there remains a concern that perhaps his father married a divorcee and fathered this son, making him a chalal, and this became known; therefore they called another kohen. Regarding the second, however, this cannot be said, because if he were a chalal they should not have called him even in place of a levi. By contrast, when two leviim are called one after the other, there is suspicion regarding both of them, that perhaps one is disqualified from levi status and is merely a Yisrael because he is a mamzer. Therefore the Mechaber writes afterward that one of them is disqualified. Bach writes that this proves that a mamzer may be called to the Torah.
