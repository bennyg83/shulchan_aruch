And know — Rashi Gittin 59b s.v. "but one who is not a priest" (so other Levites or Israelites are not provoked) — and Levi does not yield to Israel; and this is true in the Mishnah "these because of darchei shalom: priest, etc., and after him Levi and Israel" — implying also Levi and Israel are because of darchei shalom; and if there is a priest, nevertheless an Israelite after a priest is forbidden lest they say the priest is flawed and reads twice (see Levush on this). Rather it refers when there is no priest, and Levi and Israel are equals — then Levi precedes by law, as written in note 6; then he cannot waive to Israel because of darchei shalom; and this teaches as in note 6 that Levi reads first for all explanations. This requires further study why they did not mention any of this; see Rashi "we maintain: the bundle separated" — tradition from our ancestors; and at first glance "we maintain" from this sugya implies challenging "because of darchei shalom" of Torah — and this is the tanna of Beit R. Yishmael and "you shall sanctify him" (other studies are by way of derash and asmakhta, unlike "you shall sanctify him"; and Rambam counted it among the 613; Magen Avraham, etc.). If so, "you shall sanctify him" is no proof for Levi's importance; therefore "the bundle separated" well — he lost his honor, unlike other derashot proving Levi precedes Israel; when there is no priest he did not lose honor, and on the contrary reads first — but Rashi did not explain thus; understand.
