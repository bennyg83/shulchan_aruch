But the second is not under this suspicion, for we establish it in the Gemara that we are established regarding his father that he is a Kohen; and regarding the first there is still concern perhaps his father married a divorcee and begot this one who is a challal and the matter became known; therefore they called another Kohen. But regarding the second it is impossible to say so about him — for if he were a challal they would not have given him importance to ascend in place of a Levi. But with two Levites one after the other there is concern regarding both that perhaps one is unfit from Levite status and is only an Israelite because he is a mamzer; nevertheless they wrote afterward that one of them is defective. And Mateh Yehonatan z"l wrote it is proven from here that a mamzer is permitted to ascend to the Torah.
