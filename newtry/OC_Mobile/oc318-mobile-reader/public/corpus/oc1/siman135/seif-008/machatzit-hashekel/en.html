(Sek 8) "Barekhu," etc. — it implies specifically when he said Barekhu and the congregation answered after him "Blessed is the Omnipresent, blessed forever and ever" — there is no annulment, for the congregation answered as he said they should bless and the congregation blessed; but when he begins "Blessed are You" he causes it to be annulled if he does not finish the blessing and read.

As written siman 206: when he blessed in error it is annulled, he should say "Blessed are You" with the conclusion; and if he only said "Blessed are You" and remembered, he should finish "teach me Your statutes," for it appears like reading a verse in Tehillim 119; however since the Rosh wrote there so in the name of some — therefore wherever possible it is preferable to fix it.
