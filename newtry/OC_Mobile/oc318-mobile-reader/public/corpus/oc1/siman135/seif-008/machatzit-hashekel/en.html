Seif katan 8: Barechu, etc. It implies, etc. This applies specifically when he said Barechu and the congregation answered, “Blessed is Hashem Who is blessed forever and ever.” There is then no blessing in vain, since the congregation responded to his call that they bless Hashem, and they indeed blessed Him. But if he began “Blessed are You, Hashem,” he would utter Hashem’s name in vain unless he completes the blessing and reads.
As stated in siman 206: if one mistakenly recited a blessing in vain, he should say “Blessed is the name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever.” If he said only “Blessed are You, Hashem” and then remembered, he should conclude, “Teach me Your statutes,” so that it appears as though he is reciting a verse from Tehillim 119. Nevertheless, since the Rosh wrote there in the name of some authorities that this is effective, where the matter can be corrected properly, that is preferable.
