And a levi should not go up after him, etc. This means even as third or fourth, because of the blemish to him, as the Levush explains. Meaning, since the usual practice is to call Yisraelim for all the aliyos, people will say that this one is not a levi but a Yisrael. The Bach wrote that the reason is that it is a lowering of honor for the levi.
