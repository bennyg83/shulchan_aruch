8) There, in the gloss: If they omitted one Shabbos, etc. The Ateres Zekeinim, ot 1, wrote that if they omitted on Monday the reading in the Sefer Torah due to circumstances beyond their control, they may read the reading of Monday on Tuesday, so that Ezra's enactment not be nullified and so that they not go three days without Torah; which is not the case if they omitted on Thursday, for then they have already gone three days without Torah; see there. But the Rav Mas'as Moshe in his responsa, part 2, siman 1, concluded from the responsum of the Maharik, shoresh 177, that every matter that is on account of an enactment, what was, was, and we do not repeat the act; see there. And the Emes LeYaakov cited him there, ot 1. And likewise the Eliyah Rabbah, ot 2, contested the aforementioned words of the Ateres Zekeinim; see there. And likewise the Birkei Yosef, ot 2, although he wrote to refute the proofs of the aforementioned Rav Mas'as Moshe, nonetheless concurred with him regarding the law; see there. And so he ruled in his sefer Ledovid Emes there, ot 1. And so is implied from what was stated above in ot 1 in the name of the Arizal, of blessed memory, that there is no place for reading the Sefer Torah on a weekday except on Monday and Thursday alone; see there. However, it appears that they may read at Minchah if they omitted at Shacharis on Monday and Thursday; and so it is written in the responsa Yehudah Ya'aleh, siman 51, and the Tosafos Chaim cited him, ot 1, and wrote that possibly all agree in this; see there.
