2) There. Of a wedding. The same applies to a betrothal celebration. Avodas HaKodesh; Rabbeinu Yerucham; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 3; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1. Gra, however, writes that only going to perform the betrothal itself is considered a mitzvah purpose, as stated below in siman 444, se'if 7. See there. Nevertheless, one may rule leniently according to the first opinion because many authorities hold it.
