That one may only make an eruv of boundaries for a matter of mitzva. Contains 4 sections One may only make
an eruv of boundaries for a matter of mitzva — such as wishing to go to a house of mourning, or to a wedding feast, or to greet his teacher or friend who came from a journey, and similar cases. And if he made an eruv for none of these but for an optional matter, it is nonetheless a valid eruv.Rema: Or if he wishes to go for a stroll on Yom Tov or Shabbat in an orchard, which brings joy — this is considered a matter of mitzva. Or out of fear, such as wishing to flee from non-Jews or bandits and the like, in which case he is permitted to go even for an optional matter.) או מפני היראה כגון שהיה רוצה לברוח מן העכו"ם או מן הלסטים וכיוצא בזה ואז מותר לו לילך אפי' לדבר הרשות (טור והג"א פרק בכל מערבין) ואם עירב שלא לא' מכל אלו אלא לדברי הרשות הרי זה עירוב:
