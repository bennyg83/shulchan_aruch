That an eruv techumin is made only for a mitzvah purpose. It contains 4 seifim: One may make an eruv techumin only for a mitzvah purpose, such as when he wishes to go to a house of mourning, to a wedding feast, to greet his rabbi, or to greet his friend who has returned from a journey, and similar purposes. {Rama: The same applies if he wishes to go for pleasure on Yom Tov or Shabbos to an orchard where this will bring him joy; this is considered a mitzvah purpose. [Terumat HaDeshen, siman 77]} The same applies because of fear, such as when he wishes to flee from non-Jews, robbers, or the like; in such a case he may then go even for an optional purpose. [Tur and Hagahot Ashiri, chapter Bechol Me'arvin] If he made an eruv for none of these purposes, but rather for an optional purpose, the eruv is nevertheless valid.
