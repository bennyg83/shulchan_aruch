(א) Two people said to him, etc. — this law is mentioned above in siman 393 seif 3, except there it deals with eruv chatzerot, and here it teaches us that for eruv techumin too the law is thus. And there are those who disagree; they hold that specifically for eruv chatzerot they were lenient in this, but not for eruv techumin — and he does not acquire eruv except specifically the first one, because we establish the eruv on its presumption, and certainly it stood until the time it needed to stand; but not the second who placed during bein hashemashot, for presumption does not apply here. And see in Biur Halacha what we wrote that most Rishonim hold this view.

(ב) Both acquired eruv — for regarding each one we take the lenient view because it is a d'rabbanan matter; and see above in siman 393 seif 3 in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha in what we wrote there.
