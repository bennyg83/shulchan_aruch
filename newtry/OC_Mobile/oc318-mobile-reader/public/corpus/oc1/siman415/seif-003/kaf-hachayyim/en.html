3) There. Of a wedding. A wedding between the daughter of a kohen and an am ha'aretz, or between the daughter of a Torah scholar and an am ha'aretz, is not considered a mitzvah celebration, as stated in Pesachim, chapter 3. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 2. See Even HaEzer, siman 2, se'if 8.
