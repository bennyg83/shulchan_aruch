Machatzit HaShekel. And see Yoreh De'ah siman 200 letter 1 regarding the measure of forty se'ah — see there. And even though it is not made in this form — meaning one amah by one amah in height of three — whatever holds forty se'ah is a tent; and it appears nevertheless we require that its roof be a tefach — otherwise even if long and short it is not a tent.
