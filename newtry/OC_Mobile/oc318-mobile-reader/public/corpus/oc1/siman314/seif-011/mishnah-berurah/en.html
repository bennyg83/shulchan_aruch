(א) Wax or thick oil — even though there is no smearing in oil that is forbidden, for they decreed because of wax — and specifically thick oil, since smearing somewhat applies to it, lest one confuse them; and this wax must be that he prepared it for use from yesterday, for otherwise in any case it is forbidden because of muktzeh, for it is not a vessel; and when he prepared it for this it is permitted to move it, and we do not decree lest he stick it to a wall or to a vessel — for even if he sticks it is only rabbinically, lest he stick at the time of smearing. Fat and milk — their law is like wax:
(ב) He smears — and since it is because of concern of smearing it is forbidden even if he does not insert the oil inside the hole but only mere placement on top; and there is also no distinction in this between a Torah scholar and a common person [later authorities]:
(ג) Permitted — meaning even to insert it inside the hole; since the wine does not exit even without fixing it does not appear like fixing; and the later authorities wrote that the primary view is like the Rambam, who disagrees on this and holds that even if the wine does not exit through there it is also forbidden to place anything inside the hole to seal it, because it is like building; except on top of the hole from above, where there is no concern of building — it is forbidden only with a thing that smears:
(ד) Forbidden to seal it — rabbinically because it appears like fixing; and specifically when he seals it with a thing whose way is not to seal with it, such as a small pebble or splinter; but it is permitted according to all to seal with wood, such as in a faucet, as is always the way to seal with it:
(ה) To say that he does not intend — since in truth he does intend to seal it:
(ו) And if he is a Torah scholar — for since he is a Torah scholar there is no concern in him that if we permit through evasion he will come to transgress this rabbinic prohibition even without evasion; and Magen Avraham wrote that even though we rule that nowadays we have no law of Torah scholar — nevertheless regarding this matter one should not be stringent on erev Shabbat; and there are those who disagree with him:
