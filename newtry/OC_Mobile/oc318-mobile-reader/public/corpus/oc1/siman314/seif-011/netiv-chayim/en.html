(Taz s.k. 4) "and it appears to me that certainly the essence." It appears to me: his words are puzzling, for behold it is still difficult regarding a bed why four partitions are needed — does he not need the air beneath it for sandals, as Beit Yosef himself [wrote] in the name of Rashba; and it appears that regarding a bed, since the partitions were already made on chol, four partitions are required since it resembles building — he is liable since there are four partitions and now he makes an ohel atop them; but an egg and a barrel — even though they have only two partitions, nevertheless he makes on Shabbat the roof and two partitions and intends for the air beneath it; unlike if each has two partitions and they were already made from erev Shabbat — in this one cannot decree because of complete building, for it would be a decree upon a decree, for even if he makes such an ohel on Shabbat he is not liable, since at the time of the act he does not intend for the air beneath it.
