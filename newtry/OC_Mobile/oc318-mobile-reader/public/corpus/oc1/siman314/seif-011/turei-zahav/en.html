Since the wine does not come out, etc. So is the view of Beit Yosef who challenged — since they forbade specifically with wax and something that spreads — it implies other sealings are permitted; and afterward he wrote even with a garlic clove forbidden unless it is a stratagem for the sake of stratagem; and he answered that the conclusion deals when there is wine in it — for it appears like fixing; and it is not settled for me — for certainly even when there is no wine in it it appears like fixing in that he seals the hole; and no proof is needed for this; and furthermore it is difficult in what they permitted specifically for a young rabbinic student and not for others — what distinction is there? Certainly any person who seals and says he does so to store it there — what prohibition is in the matter? Granted in what is further written in the Gemara regarding passing through to guard his fruits and he said he does so to sleep — there is a distinction — for Rashi explained: They did not come to do this l'chatchila before everyone without stratagem to pass there in public — see there — his intent is clear: The young rabbinic student who does so acts discreetly not before the public and reveals to them he does so because he wants to sleep there — unlike another person who passes before everyone; but in a wine barrel in his house this does not apply; and it appears to me the main point is as the Maggid wrote according to Rambam that there is a distinction between sealing inserted inside the hole and if he places on top from outside; and were it not for Rambam's words I would have explained Tur's words — he first forbade with wax or oil where it deals with something glued outside and there is no wine in the barrel — for it is customary to fix it thus by spreading outside and afterward pouring wine inside; and this is a complete d'oraisa prohibition as it is a tolada of spreading; but when there is wine in it and it exits through the hole — impossible to fix except by inserting something into the hole — and in this there is d'rabbanan prohibition due to a decree lest he afterward spread outside with wax as we find they decreed on oil due to wax; and they permitted this for a young rabbinic student for we are not concerned lest he transgress and spread with wax — unlike others — as stated above according to Tur — there is no prohibition in sealing inside the hole when not spreading except due to the decree of wax; but Rambam wrote there is prohibition due to building — for he wrote just as it is forbidden to open any hole so it is forbidden to seal any hole, etc. — it implies they did not forbid in sealing not inside the hole due to a decree; and perforce we must say there are two prohibitions here — not one proof from the other: In wax spread outside on the hole there is prohibition due to spreading and not due to building; and in sealing inside the hole there is prohibition due to building and not spreading — perforce Rambam and Tur made from this two distinctions — namely the first distinction in sealing on the hole which is forbidden due to spreading and he did not mention there is wine in it — only an empty utensil he fixes thus — and stratagem does not help since there is concern of d'oraisa prohibition; and in the second distinction it is sealing inside the hole and he mentioned there is wine in it — for then he must fix — and the prohibition there is due to building — and then stratagem helps for a young rabbinic student since there is no complete d'oraisa building; and Maharachal said the opposite — that they permitted specifically due to loss of wine with stratagem; and it does not imply thus in Rif and hints — for he did not mention wine at all in this stratagem — rather the main permission as explicit in the Gemara is because it is d'rabbanan;
