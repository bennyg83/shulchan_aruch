(א) The wine exits, etc. For it appears like fixing [Beit Yosef]; and it appears that specifically with a thing whose way is not to seal; but it is permitted to seal with wood as is the way to seal with it [in s.v.]; and in Av Beit Chayyim and in Maggid Mishnah, who holds that even if the wine does not exit it is forbidden to seal the hole, just as it is forbidden to open it; and so too the Rambam; but with wax it is not called sealing, since one does not place it inside the hole, and therefore he states the reason is because of smearing:

(ב) A Torah scholar is permitted. It appears to me that even nowadays, when we have no Torah scholar, as is written at the end of siman 338 — nevertheless in this law it is permitted, since the Rambam holds that this evasion is permitted for every person; and it is obvious with wax that if he smears he is liable for a chatas; or with oil, for they decreed because of wax; even for a Torah scholar it is forbidden to evade even with mere placement; and this wax must be that he prepared it for use from yesterday, for otherwise it is forbidden in any case because of muktzeh, for it is not a vessel:
