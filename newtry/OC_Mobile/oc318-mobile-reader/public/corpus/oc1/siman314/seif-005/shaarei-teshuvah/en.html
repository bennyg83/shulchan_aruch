(In Shulchan Aruch seif 7) "but a lid," etc. — and see in responsa Mahari Halevi brother of the Taz that it is permitted to tell a gentile to open the door lock in a room with a craftsman utensil, for we do not decree lest he break the vessel or the teeth of the lock since it is not common, and especially when he is a craftsman, see there; and in Mishna Berurah he brought in the name of responsa Zera Emet who disagreed with him and forbade, see there; and an incident occurred when the ark was closed and they could not open it to remove the Torah scroll, and he ruled to bring a gentile craftsman if he can open without breaking anything; and if the craftsman says it cannot be done without breaking the teeth of the lock, he ruled to bring the Torah scroll from another synagogue for the honor of the congregation, see there.
