Such as a barrel that broke, etc. R' Korban Netanel (on Rosh chapter 22 of Shabbos letter 5) wondered at Beit Yosef who did not bring Rif and Rambam's view to permit even l'chatchila without glued fragments — and agreed to permit thus — see there. And likewise Rashba and Ran chapter Chavit; and likewise Ritva in his commentary Eruvin daf 35 — see there. And likewise Rashbatz in his commentary Rosh Hashana that if the shofar is inside a barrel he breaks it like the law of dried figs. And likewise Rashbash siman 165 — see there. And likewise Radbaz in Rambam's language siman 130 permitted to break a box to remove from it what is needed for Shabbat when the key was lost — see there. Eliyah Rabbah letter 1. And likewise Eliyah Rabbah siman 307 end of letter 31 — see there. And it appears that although it is difficult according to Shulchan Aruch's ruling that specifically in a barrel that broke, etc. it is permitted — but whole forbidden — nevertheless it is possible in a time of pressure when he suffers that he has nothing to eat — one may rely on the lenient authorities — provided it does not hold forty se'ah as written above letter 2 — see there; and also if it is a matter where he does not make it a utensil through this breaking — for otherwise it would be makeh b'patish and liable for everyone:
