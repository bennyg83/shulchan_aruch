(א) Permitted to place, etc. — for behold it stands and is designated for this and he does not fix a vessel; and nevertheless it appears forbidden to stick firmly with force with pegs, for in this there is concern of gmar melacha:
(ב) Even though, etc. — and even though he does not know if it will reach its measure — nevertheless it is permitted; and see in Beit Yosef, who wrote that it appears from the Rambam's words that even though the branch designated itself, it is not fully prepared and lacks some fixing — nevertheless it is permitted to insert it, and we are not concerned lest he fix it:
(ג) In a place where he has — know that the Mechaber wrote the reason for the prohibition because it appears like making a gutter; and Rama concludes: and one need not be concerned lest he trim — it is a dispute of Amoraim in the Gemara: one says the reason for the prohibition is lest he make a gutter; and one says a decree lest he trim a branch from the tree to place it in a barrel; and we say: what is the difference? — that they trim and place [meaning that they had many trimmed ones prepared from erev Shabbat for this, and therefore there is no concern lest he come to trim]; per the one who says because of a gutter it is forbidden even in such a case; and per the one who says lest he trim — permitted; and the Rosh and Tur ruled like the one who says lest he trim:
