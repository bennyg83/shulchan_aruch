(5) [Levushei Serad] And some say, etc. Shabbos daf 146 — this palm branch of myrtle is forbidden — what is the reason? Rav Yeimar said due to a drainpipe; Rav Ashi said due to lest he cut off. What is between them? "He cuts and places." And Perishah challenged — according to Rav Ashi where do we find prohibition? One cannot say where he did not cut and needs to cut on Shabbat — for this is obvious that it is forbidden and Shmuel did not need to say it — until here. And to me it appears we find prohibition when he places it in barrels connected with the branch — from a decree lest he cut from the branch; and this appears to me the view of Remazim who wrote on this cut myrtle from a branch is permitted to place, etc.; and likewise Rabbeinu Yerucham Netiv 13 part 8 wrote; except from this it appears even one is permitted since he cuts; contrary to what Tur wrote that this is the view of "some say" — requires study. And behold Perishah wrote Tur answered forbidden in one and he reads "he cuts and places" in plural; and from Beit Yosef it appears that what is forbidden even though already cut — this is when it is not prepared; and since one must seek it out they decreed lest he cut; but where he places — permitted. And behold Rashi explained lest he cut the leaf from the branch — and since it is difficult it would be fixing a utensil — until here. And in Chokhmat Shlomo he emended "since he cuts," etc.; and it appears he had difficulty — for behold myrtle leaves are soft as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Ran chapter HaMeivi; and in truth it is wondrous — for in Shabbos there Ran explained like the Rashi mentioned above; and it is strained to emend also in Ran. And I found in Perishah who explained Rashi's view is that Rav Ashi holds even though the leaf is soft — nevertheless since it was cut from the branch it is considered hard; and in chapter HaMeivi Ran explained for Rav Yeimar he is not concerned for lest he cut since the leaf is soft; also it appears to me in the explanation of the Talmud "he cuts and places" means it was already done before the day like a drainpipe; and if so for Rav Yeimar permitted and for Rav Ashi forbidden — end of discussion; and more it appears to me as stated in Shabbos daf 125 — a vine shoot tied to a palm branch — one fills with it on Shabbat; tied yes, not tied no — what is the reason? R' Ashi said a decree lest he cut; Rashi explained lest tomorrow it will be long and he will cut it and he will be making a utensil — until here; and if so we also say thus lest on Shabbat it will be long and he will cut — with this I say "cut and placed" means he did so on erev Shabbat like the tied case mentioned above — except all the poskim did not understand thus:
