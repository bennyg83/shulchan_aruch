(א) Permitted to untie, etc. — for cutting the thread is permitted in detached matter, as above in s.k. 37; and there is no concern of the melacha of cutting, since he is not particular about the measure. The poskim wrote: one who splits wood into large pieces — its prohibition is only rabbinically as a weekday act; one who splits into small pieces is liable for tochen; and if he is particular about the measure he is liable for cutting even in large pieces:
