(א) But. Taz — I have here thoughts on the matter and I will enumerate them:

(ב) Question — this in the ground he does not untie — if permitted through a non-Jew for a mitzvah matter to take a lulav and similar; also an opening not made for inserting and removing in the ground — is it d'oraisa or not; also a utensil holding forty se'ah that is like a tent — do we require specifically one amah by one amah in height of three in this form; or even ab initio whatever holds forty se'ah:

(ג) Answer briefly — behold Rashi Eruvin 35a s.v. uv'ei regarding a seal in the ground — d'rabbanan since it is merely opening a door — see there — it appears d'rabbanan; and through a non-Jew for a mitzvah matter shevut deshevut permitted; and in Tosafos there s.v. b'ei one can say in such a case it is d'oraisa; however it appears they agree with Rashi on the fundamental law; and in Beit Yosef he brought Terumat HaDeshen's words siman 65; and there he wrote regarding the oven mentioned here: A seal in the ground is not d'oraisa demolishing where he only makes an opening — one can say like Rashi; but to actually make an opening to pierce a hole for an opening — even if not made except for inserting alone or removing alone — one can say in the ground it is d'oraisa as Magen Avraham wrote letter 3; and here since the opening was already made and opening the board is inferior building or untying the rope — this is d'rabbanan; and regarding a tent — even if not made in this form — one can say one amah by one amah in height of three — whatever holds forty se'ah is a tent; and it appears nevertheless we require its roof be a tefach — otherwise even if long and short it is not a tent; and see siman 316.
