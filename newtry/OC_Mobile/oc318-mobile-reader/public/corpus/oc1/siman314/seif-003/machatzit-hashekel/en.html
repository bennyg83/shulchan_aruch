(א) (s.k. 3) With pitch, etc., because of inferiority, etc. — Tosafos and the Rosh; it is true that the Rosh likewise wrote in Shabbos daf 146 on the Mishnah of "a person breaks barrels," etc.: the Rosh wrote: in tractate 19, etc., he establishes it in moustaki — Rashi: broken barrels, etc., and therefore it is permitted; and because of inferiority they do not care about it and there is no need for "lest he intend to make a vessel," end of his words; and so Tosafos in Beitza daf 33b s.v. ki tani, etc. (except they gave another reason): and Rashi explained that it is not for fixing a vessel, since it is very shaky — they attribute to it lest he ruin the barrels and make a hole at least as much as he can, end of his words; and all this is according to Rashi's view, who holds that also in the broken barrels mentioned, if he fixes an opening for it there is a Torah prohibition and it is relevant to decree lest he intend to fix; therefore they needed the above explanation; but according to Tosafos and the Rosh and as is ruled in Shulchan Aruch, that even if he intends to make an opening for it there is no Torah prohibition — only because of metaken mana — and as Magen Avraham wrote in s.k. 1 — if so, in any case there is no need for "lest he intend to fix," for it is like a decree upon a decree; and Magen Avraham did not need to bring the Rosh's words in the name of Rashi for us; and so too from Tosafos' words in Beitza in the name of R' Yehuda — see there; and it must be that Magen Avraham is more precise than him, as Rashi stated according to his view:

(ב) The Rambam wrote, etc., and the Kesef Mishnah challenged in chapter 10 — in chapter 10 it should read — and it is a dispute in Shabbos daf 102b: Rav holds liable for building and Shmuel holds liable for makeh b'patish:

(ג) And he wrote further in chapter 10: One who makes a hole, etc., whether in building, etc. — from this too the Kesef Mishnah challenged there: behold, regarding a coop he wrote liable for building in chapter 23, and here he wrote: One who makes a hole is liable for makeh b'patish; and with this Magen Avraham's explanation does not apply, for here he wrote whether in building, etc. — behold, even when attached he is liable for makeh b'patish; and see in Sefer Teshuvah what he wrote on this. However, from Magen Avraham's words it appears that he holds as the Lechem Mishneh wrote in chapter 10 regarding the Rambam, who holds that Rav, who said liable for building, means also for building, but he agrees that he is also liable for makeh b'patish, for that is completion of the melacha; except that Shmuel holds he is not liable for building, as Tosafos wrote there s.v. lul, etc.; and therefore when it is attached he states the liability for building and cites Rav's language; but when it is not attached, where there is no liability for building, he writes liable for makeh b'patish. However, in chapter 10 what he wrote: One who makes a hole, etc., whether in wood, in building, etc., liable for makeh b'patish — this is because he included there many detached and attached matters; therefore he cited the liability that includes them all, namely makeh b'patish; unlike the liability of building, which exists only when attached:
