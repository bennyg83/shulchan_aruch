(א) In the place of an old hole — whether that hole was large or small, provided he does not widen it more than it was initially:

(ב) One may pierce even with an awl — for it is not considered sealing at all, since it is above the sediment; and so too; and therefore it is permitted to pierce in the stopper with which they seal a glass vessel [called stopper] even in a vessel prepared for this:

(ג) The faucet — he cited a common thing; and the same if the hole was in another place that was sealed:

(ד) And some say it is not permitted, etc. — this is everyone's view on it; and see in Eliyah Rabbah, who wrote that other poskim disagree on this and ruled like them; and so ruled in Nachalat Tzvi; but in Peri Megadim and Chayei Adam it is implied that there is room to be concerned to be stringent like the view of "some say":
