Magen Avraham section 1 — and Ran disagrees with Rashi. For Rashi in Shabbos 146 explains the reason of this law of fragments glued with pitch that it is permitted to break it is because of destructive ruining — even though destructive ruining is d'rabbanan forbidden they permitted for Shabbat need; and according to his words also in a large utensil permitted — for even though it is like a tent — nevertheless he demolishes and is liable only in order to rebuild — then it is not destructive ruining; therefore Magen Avraham wrote Ran and Tosafos and Rosh disagree with Rashi that they did not permit the prohibition of destructive ruining for Shabbat need; and here it is permitted because it is not complete demolishing — and such demolishing is permitted in utensils; therefore in a large one that is like a tent — forbidden; and afterward Magen Avraham concluded complete building also applies in a small utensil to explain the reason that in a whole one forbidden to break it — as I wrote letter 1; and in new editions they emended Magen Avraham's words here — and there is no need:
