(In Rama gloss seif 1) "that does not hold." Requires study. And it is difficult, for per the first view in Tosafos that deals with plugged barrels we need not distinguish between a large vessel and a small one; and the Rama erected two merchants — those two answers contradict each other. And it must be said that the Rama holds that even per the second view that deals with plugged barrels, from the perspective of reasoning we distinguish between a large vessel that is like an ohel but shakes — unlike the first view regarding inserting a basket, etc. — and investigate.
