(א) Fixing a utensil. Taz wrote that it appears this opening here of fixing a utensil is only d'rabbanan. And I did not understand — Beitza 33b Rashi s.v. vel'eit lei l'Rabbi Eliezer, etc. — that when he intends to make a utensil there is d'oraisa liability; and they answer with glued fragments that a shaky one they did not decree lest he intend to make a utensil; and likewise Tosafos Shabbos 146a s.v. shover wrote they are not concerned for it, etc.; and likewise Rosh there Magen Avraham brought letter 3. If so it is clear that if he intends to make a utensil with a knife or sword he is liable for a chatas even for the rabbis — R' Eliezer in Beitza specifically with the hand holds shevut; and a hole however small when he intends only for an opening — all that is not made for inserting and removing — only for inserting alone or removing alone — there is no Torah prohibition; unlike when he breaks with a knife and sword and narrows so it becomes a utensil thereby — there is chatas liability; however Tosafos Beitza there s.v. ki tania explain we are not concerned for fixing a utensil lest he ruin — and all one matter; but they further explained in glued fragments even if he intends to make a utensil thereby — this is not fixing a utensil and there is no Torah prohibition since it is glued in fragments — there are no Torah prohibitions; and nevertheless one may not derive from that case of any hole — for there he intends only for an opening as stated — and requires further study:

(ב) And incidentally I saw to mention briefly — behold Ran explained that breaking a barrel deals with a whole small one that does not hold forty se'ah — there is no building and demolishing in it; a large one forbidden. And I did not understand — for if the reason is destructive ruining exempt and permitted due to Shabbat need — the same for a large one permitted; one is liable only in order to rebuild in any case — see Shabbos 31b and likewise Rambam chapter 10 of Hilchot Shabbat. Taz. If so a large one too — any manner of destruction exempt; and due to Shabbat need they permitted; and if we say destructive ruining in order to fix — to eat from it — is not considered destructive ruining like one who tears in his anger and similar — if so a small one truly permitted due to destructive ruining as Ran wrote there. Possibly at first he wrote according to Rashi and afterward wrote however his explanation is not due to destructive ruining but the reason in a small one there is no building in utensils — but a large one, etc. — requires further study. And one can explain in a large one it is an av melacha of building and demolishing — they did not permit due to Shabbat need even destructive ruining; unlike in a small one which is only a tolada of making a utensil and breaking it — they were not stringent regarding destructive ruining for Shabbat need — this too is strained:

(ג) And behold Levush explained for us that in a small utensil there is no building and demolishing — only we call it fixing and making a utensil or breaking it; therefore in a barrel that does not hold forty se'ah — if whole and he breaks it — behold breaking a utensil and a tolada of building and demolishing; but gluing with pitch — this is not breaking a utensil; and if he joins it with pitch — this is not making a utensil and no craftsmanship is needed for this — therefore permitted for Shabbat need; but a large barrel holding forty se'ah — building applies since it is a tent — and even without craftsmanship he is liable due to building and demolishing; and possibly these are Ran's words even though he did not mention glued fragments in a small one; and on my own what is written in the name of Ran regarding a shofar — what Ran wrote at the beginning of HaBoneh "and all utensils" — if so in a small one necessarily in glued fragments; and requires further study. And with this what Rabbenu wrote in the gloss "and does not hold forty se'ah" from the Mechaber's words — even though this is one topic and this is one topic — for the examiner in Beit Yosef — both are true for halachic ruling and for stringency — see Netiv Tzafon. And at first glance it appears from Levush glued fragments permitted to break that adhesion of pitch — and one cannot distinguish — for it implies even if he breaks where the earthenware is whole — also permitted — see there. And what Levush further wrote in a whole utensil even a hole all the more breaking it — namely perforce if he intends to ruin — this is breaking it; and if for an opening — he makes an opening and a decree lest coop, etc. as stated in the Gemara 146a; and to widen it also due to opening; and see Tosafos Eruvin 34b s.v. ve'amai they challenged them this that Rav Ashi asks Shabbos 146a what about piercing a barrel with an awl — does he intend an opening and forbidden; or for a nice appearance and permitted in glued fragments — deals with this; Shulchan Aruch seif 16 implies thus in a whole small one even not for an opening forbidden — this is breaking it; and see Rosh Eruvin 34 — appears like Tosafos there s.v. ve'mai; and Tosafos Shabbos 146a s.v. shover — for the rabbis in glued fragments — otherwise it would be forbidden since there is building in utensils; and in Shabbos 146 one authority wrote; and as Chokhmat Shlomo wrote there — requires further study now.
