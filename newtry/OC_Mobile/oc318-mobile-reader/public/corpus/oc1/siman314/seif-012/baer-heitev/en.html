A Torah scholar. It appears to me that even nowadays when we do not have a Torah scholar — and as written at the end of siman 339 — nevertheless this law is permitted since Rambam holds that such a stratagem is permitted for any person. And it is simple regarding wax that if one spreads he is liable for a chatas; or with oil that we decree lest one use wax — even for a Torah scholar it is forbidden to use a stratagem even with mere placement.
