(א) Of wood — he cited of wood, for in a brick wall it often happens several times that their rows are not attached to one another, and then it is permitted to remove even if it was not "that was pulled" from erev Shabbat:
(ב) Forbidden to remove it — for through removing it, since it was initially stuck somewhat forcefully, it is impossible that the crack there not widen; and making any hole in an attached wall has concern of chisul, which is a toldah of building; and even though he does not intend for this it is almost psik reisha; and see above in s.k. 11 in Mishna Berurah what we wrote of the later authorities' view on this:
(ג) Permitted — to remove it and even to return and stick it in the wall; for since he already drove the knife into the wall from erev Shabbat and removed it and again drove it in — its seat was already widened and thereafter it is not psik reisha; but if he did not return and drive it in from erev Shabbat — it appears forbidden to stick it in the wall, for it has not yet widened from what he removed once from the wall:
