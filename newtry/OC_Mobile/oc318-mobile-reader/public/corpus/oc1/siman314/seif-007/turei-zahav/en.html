(א) Forbidden to remove the hinges. This is the view of R' Peretz who explains the Gemara in R' Peretz "all utensils" — the door of a chest, box, and tower — one removes and does not return — lest he drive in — meaning lest he put the hinges — and if so it is complete building in what he puts the hinges — perforce in removing them there is complete demolishing; and if so that which "one removes" deals when there are no hinges — for wherever there is d'oraisa prohibition in building there is also d'oraisa prohibition in demolishing; but without hinges where there is no d'oraisa prohibition in returning — only a decree lest he put hinges — there is no prohibition in demolishing:

(ב) And some permit. This is the view of R' Yehuda Mikkunon who wrote — the Mordechai in the name of R' Peretz: R' Yehuda Mikkunon says it is permitted to remove the hinges behind the box if the key was lost — for it is inferior demolishing since he need not break anything; but it does not appear to me according to R' Peretz, etc. — and as he stated his reason nearby; and if so it appears according to R' Yehuda Mikkunon who permits removing hinges — he also permits putting hinges — for it is one reason; and the decree lest he drive in does not apply to putting hinges but only that he drive in with very great force as Rashi explained R' Peretz "all utensils." And I did not understand what Shulchan Aruch wrote afterward "and breaking the lid of boxes — some permit" — for he already wrote this regarding a wooden and metal lid where there is a dispute; and this is the dispute of R' Yehuda Mikkunon and Rabbenu regarding removing hinges — one law with breaking a lid; and if you want to distinguish between removing hinges and breaking a lid — it is difficult — for first he ruled without qualification to forbid regarding a lid; and afterward he wrote a dispute — some permit and some forbid. Granted for Tur who first wrote the Rosh's view to forbid and wrote on this that Semak brought a dispute on this — but here he already ruled without qualification to forbid; and it seems there is a textual error here "and breaking a lid," etc.; and in Levush he copied as here and did not notice this:
