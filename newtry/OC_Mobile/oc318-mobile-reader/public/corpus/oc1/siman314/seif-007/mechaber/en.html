Seals on vessels such as a chest, box, and tower whose cover is tied to them with rope — he may untie it or cut it with a knife or untie its braid; and specifically [when tied] like rope tying and the like; but a wooden or metal lid — it is forbidden to pry open and break, for in vessels too complete building and demolishing apply; and for this reason it is forbidden to remove the hinges called gozgez behind the chest if the key was lost; and some permit this; and regarding breaking lids of boxes — some permit and some forbid; and it may be permitted through a gentile.
