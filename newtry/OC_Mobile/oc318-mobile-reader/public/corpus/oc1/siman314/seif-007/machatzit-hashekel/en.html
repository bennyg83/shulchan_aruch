(א) (s.k. 7) The head, etc., and it deals when he glued, etc. — for otherwise it is forbidden, lest he intend to make an opening, as is written seif 1:

(ב) But the Tur, etc. — with this he comes to resolve Beit Yosef's difficulty on the Tur: why did he write the head of the stopper — behold in the Mishnah it permits even the head of the barrels; therefore Magen Avraham wrote that the Tur cited a term that is certain — the head of the stopper in every case is permitted, even in a whole barrel; unlike the head of the barrel, which was not permitted except when the broken pieces were glued:
