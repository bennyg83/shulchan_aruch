(א) The head of the barrel. It is implied that even to throw off the body of the barrel with the stopper is permitted, as Tosafos stated daf 48; and it deals when he glued the broken pieces with pitch, as is written seif 1 (Shach); but the Tur wrote that it is permitted to throw off the head of the stopper, and this is permitted even in a whole barrel, for it is not a connection:

(ב) Above is permitted. Even though in a barrel it is forbidden, as is written seif 1, from the stopper it is different from the barrel, for even if the hole in the stopper were fit for inserting and removing he would not be liable; and here, since it is not fit for inserting and removing, it is permitted [Tosafos there]; and if so, even in a whole barrel it is permitted:
