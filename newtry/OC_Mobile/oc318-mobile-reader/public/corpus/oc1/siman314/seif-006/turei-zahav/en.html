(א) Permitted to slice the head of the barrel, etc. In the Mishnah we say: And one does not pierce from the body of the barrel — R' Yehuda and the sages permit; and one should not pierce it from the side — R' Huna said the dispute is above (Rashi: at the head of the stopper the sages permit since it is not customary to make an opening there but rather one takes the entire stopper) — but from the side (Rashi: sometimes they make it for an opening and he does not want to open it above lest earth fall in the wine) — therefore forbidden; and this is what the Mishnah teaches "and one should not pierce them from the side" (for it refers to from the body and "therefore" is the reason) — and R' Chiya said the dispute is from the side (there specifically R' Yehuda forbade) — but above therefore permitted; and that which the Mishnah teaches "one should not pierce it from the side" — from the body of the barrel. And one asks — why did Rashi first explain according to R' Huna that above is permitted for the sages because if he intended an opening he would take it entirely — and he should have explained the reason because lest earth fall there; and it appears we can ask — if the sages' reason were thus it is obvious that from the side it is forbidden for "therefore" since that reason of lest earth fall is not there — why would the tanna need to repeat "one should not pierce from the side"? Perforce Rashi explained the sages' reason because he would take it entirely — and one could say that from the side is also permitted for the sages due to this reason; the tanna teaches us that sometimes he does not want to take it entirely for some reason he has and intends to make an opening there; and lest you say therefore why is above permitted — perhaps he also intends this — Rashi explains he does so due to falling earth — it turns out above is permitted due to two reasons for its benefit; but from the side there is only one reason for its benefit — namely that he should have taken it entirely; with this we say that sometimes he has a reason for this that he does not want to take the entire stopper but only make a small opening — as stated above — correct is Rashi's view:

(ב) The head of the barrel with a sword. In Tur he wrote: the head of the stopper. And Beit Yosef wrote that in some texts it is as here; and he expanded somewhat on this; and it appears simple that the text here is correct — for Tosafos wrote in chapter Beitza daf 48 that it is proven from Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel who said "he slices its head" — meaning even in the body of the barrel; and they proved thus from Rav Sheshes who forbade to pierce with an awl into the barrel — and "barrel" implies the body of the barrel; and furthermore if in the stopper why forbidden — for we learned that the sages permit piercing in the stopper; rather certainly Rav Sheshes refers to the barrel itself; and if so what do we challenge on Rav Sheshes from Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel who breaks with a sword — for that deals with the stopper; rather perforce Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel who permits also permits in the body of the barrel; and Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham that Tosafos hold like R' Chiya — and therefore they wrote that in the stopper the sages permit in every manner; and if so Rav Sheshes forbade only in the body of the barrel; but one who rules like R' Huna — even in the stopper forbidden for "therefore" since it is from the side — perforce. And in my humble opinion — Tosafos did not intend this — for certainly we hold everywhere that what is forbidden to pierce is also forbidden with a spit — both in the body of the barrel and in the stopper from the side; and this is the main proof they proved — for if you say there is a distinction between barrel and stopper — whence do we challenge Rav Sheshes — for one can distinguish that R' Shimon deals with the body of the barrel and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel permits only in the stopper; rather certainly there is no distinction between them — Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel who permits to slice with a sword permits even in the barrel itself; and R' Shimon who forbids with a spit — namely in every manner that piercing is forbidden, spit is also forbidden — namely even in the stopper from the side according to R' Huna; and according to R' Chiya specifically in the body of the barrel there is prohibition — namely even with a spit; and what Tosafos wrote further — behold in the stopper piercing is permitted in the Mishnah — one cannot prove from this that they hold like R' Chiya — for if like R' Huna one could find prohibition also in the stopper from the side; and perhaps on this R' Shimon said he forbids also with a spit — this is not so — for then he should have challenged saying "it works well for R' Huna — but for R' Chiya what can be said?" as the Gemara does in several places; rather it is simple that both for R' Huna and R' Chiya this challenge goes on R' Shimon; and if so Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel also permits even in the body of the barrel — as stated above — clear is Tosafos' view; and for halacha we properly hold like R' Huna as Rif, Rosh, and Tur wrote — and not like R' Yehuda Ish Bilbao who holds Tosafos follow only R' Chiya — for if so they could not prove anything — for one could say it does not come there like R' Chiya but as I explained and there is no distinction at all between barrel and stopper — both for prohibition and for permission — as written nearby there's's help:

(ג) And piercing the stopper above is permitted. It implies that in the body of the barrel there is no permission above — and I do not know why — since there is no way to make an opening above because of falling earth — what difference between barrel and stopper; and in Tur he wrote permitted to slice, etc. — but to pierce it from the side even with a spear, etc.; and Beit Yosef wrote what our master wrote "this applies from the side," etc. — specifically in the stopper as stated above — end of his words. And in my humble opinion I did not find an explanation — for according to what appears he intends what he copied afterward from Rashi's explanation that "this applies above" — namely above at the head of the stopper he says the sages permit, etc.; and in my humble opinion Rashi did not write for precision that above in the barrel is forbidden — for there is no reason in this — for one could also say here if he intended an opening he would take the entire stopper and not make a hole above in the barrel; rather Rashi's intent is due to the prohibition from the side — forbidden also in the stopper; and in Tur's language one can infer a proper and correct precision in his wording — at first he wrote but to pierce it from the side forbidden; and on this he repeated and said "and this applies from the side," etc.; and he should not have said "but above," etc. and repeated a third time "but from the side forbidden because he intends an opening" as I explained — it is superfluous language and not our master's way; and it appears to me this is our master's intent: At first he said the body of the barrel itself is permitted to slice with a sword; and on this he said to pierce it from the side — namely the body of the barrel; and on this he said above permitted even with piercing since there is no opening there; and lest we err to say specifically in the body of the barrel forbidden on its side but on the side of the stopper permitted — perforce he repeated the matter; but from the side forbidden — namely everywhere even in the stopper; and perforce he wrote as I explained — meaning the reason I explained in the body of the barrel applies also in the stopper — forbidden from the side; it turns out forbidden even in the stopper on the side — and what is permitted is permitted even in the barrel — as stated above:
