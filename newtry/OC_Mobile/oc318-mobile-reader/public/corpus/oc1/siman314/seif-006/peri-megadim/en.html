And piercing. Taz wrote one can say also in the barrel above permitted and there is no distinction in piercing between the body of the barrel and the stopper from the side forbidden when he intends for an opening according to R' Huna in 146; and above even the body of the barrel permitted; and Eliyah Rabbah letter 8 and Magen Avraham letter 8. And behold in some texts in glued fragments permitted to break provided he does not intend to make a utensil — but whole forbidden; and even a hole forbidden; and even to widen it; and it is difficult — why even in a hole from the side even glued fragments forbidden like Rav Ashi 146a regarding piercing a barrel with an awl — even glued fragments in any case forbidden as Tosafos Eruvin 34b wrote — see there — breaking he intends only for a nice appearance and in piercing he intends for an opening and forbidden lest coop even in glued fragments; and even if we say above in the barrel he does not intend for an opening and in glued fragments permitted and in whole this is breaking it; and to widen it also possibly this is breaking it; and see Levush seif 1 — all this requires much study — for the Mechaber here ruled specifically in the stopper above permitted — not in the barrel itself.
