It is permitted to strike off the head of the barrel with a sword, for he does not intend an opening, since he removes its head; but to pierce it in its side, whether of a barrel or of a pitcher, is forbidden, even with a spear that makes a large hole and is not similar to an opening, for since it is from its side he certainly intends an opening; and to pierce the pitcher above is permitted, for he does not intend an opening, since it is not the way to make an opening above, rather he takes the entire pitcher.':
