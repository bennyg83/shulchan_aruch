(א) (s.k. 6) Lest he trim. And even though animal food, etc. — know that the Mechaber wrote the reason for the prohibition that it appears like making a gutter; and Rama concludes: and one need not be concerned lest he trim; and this is a dispute of the Amoraim: one says the reason for the prohibition is lest he make a gutter; and one says a decree lest he trim a branch to place it in a barrel; and we say: what is the difference between them? — that they trim and place; per the one who says because of a gutter it is forbidden; and per the one who says lest he trim — in such a case it is permitted; for specifically when he has no trimmed one prepared from erev Shabbat but must search for it on Shabbat — they are concerned lest he not find a trimmed one and will trim on Shabbat; but when he has a trimmed one prepared from erev Shabbat — there is no further need. The Rambam ruled like the one who says because of a gutter; and so ruled Beit Yosef, and therefore he forbids in every case; but the Rosh and Tur ruled like the one who says lest he trim; and so Rama. And therefore he permits when he has many trimmed ones; and that which he requires that he have many and one does not suffice — Beit Yosef wrote that he inferred thus from the language of the Gemara, that it says: one opinion — that they trim and place; and "and place" is plural language; and the reason: if he has only one, there is still concern lest it spoil and he will come to trim another; and Rama holds that even if he has two it does not suffice for this reason — rather we require that he have many trimmed ones; and see in Beit Yosef and in Bach:

(ב) And even though animal food, etc. — for behold it is permitted to trim a splinter from animal food to wedge between his teeth, and there is no concern of metaken mana:

(ג) And one can say that it is customary, etc. — meaning: specifically below siman 322, where one makes the entire vessel from animal food — and this is not the way of the world to fix the entire vessel from animal food; and the Sages did not forbid it because of metaken mana; but here, where the essence of the vessel is the barrels — only the top of the myrtle branch, which is animal food, he fixes for the barrels; and in this matter it is customary to do so, and therefore the Sages forbade it because of metaken mana:
