Taz expanded and the content of his intent — the Mechaber certainly permits even in psik reisha and Rabbenu disagrees and emended — and specifically like Terumat HaDeshen R' Dovid; and in Beit Yosef he challenged Terumat HaDeshen — since afterward in a detached bench permitted at first glance likewise in a barrel permitted — see there; and in Darkei Moshe letter 5 he answered in a barrel forbidden since there is concern he fixes an opening — and these are Taz's words below — and he did not see Darkei Moshe's words since Darkei Moshe was not before him in some places; and in Levush he wrote explicitly thus for the Mechaber — stuck from erev Shabbat permitted even if he did not return and remove; and some say specifically returned and removed; and Eliyah Rabbah letter 2 in the name of Tosafos Yom Tov. And likewise Levush in seif 1 in the gloss to distinguish between a barrel where he makes an opening, etc. And behold for the Mechaber there are here four distinctions: (1) Small barrel — even whole — if stuck from erev Shabbat — even without removed and returned — permitted; or psik reisha in d'rabbanan permitted — Magen Avraham letter 5; or this opening is d'rabbanan lest coop as written in letter 1; and this applies when he intends for an opening — but when he intends through breaking — there is no temporary building and demolishing in utensils as Levush wrote seif 1 and seif 11 — therefore permitted when not intending in any case. (2) In a small barrel. (3) Even glued fragments — the Mechaber agrees there is temporary building — if he did not return and remove from erev Shabbat it is psik reisha and forbidden. (4) And in a wall — explained in seif 11 — in something attached we require returned and removed — otherwise forbidden; and if so why did he mention a wooden wall — perhaps it is a novelty. However between boards or in a stone wall between gaps — also if it is with force likewise psik reisha — although drilling does not apply nevertheless he makes a cavity — and Terumat HaDeshen's words with Hashem's help require explanation; and Netiv Tzafon explains between gaps it is not with force and even without returned and removed it is not psik reisha — see there — requires further study. And behold according to Taz's emendations regarding one who digs and needs only earth — d'rabbanan — nevertheless psik reisha in d'rabbanan forbidden — so is his conclusion Terumat HaDeshen not like what arose according to Beit Yosef; but Magen Avraham letter 5 did not emend but that one who digs and needs earth is liable from the Torah — therefore perforce it is not psik reisha; behold in d'rabbanan he holds thus in Terumat HaDeshen's conclusion that psik reisha is permitted in d'rabbanan; and with Hashem's help in Magen Avraham it will be explained; and what Taz wrote at the beginning of his words that the Mordechai should have brought proof from the Mishnah — this is according to what he concludes there with a refutation; and see there; and for what Eliyah Rabbah wrote. And Beit Yosef expanded to explain Terumat HaDeshen's responsum — see there at length; and see Netiv Tzafon — and there was not sufficient time to study properly:
