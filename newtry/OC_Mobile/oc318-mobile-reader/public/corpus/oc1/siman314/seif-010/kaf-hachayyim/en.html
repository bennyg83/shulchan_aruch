10) There: It is considered fashioning a utensil. In the chapter Chavis, Shabbos 146, the Gemara states that any opening not made both to bring substances in and take them out is not an opening. The Sages nevertheless prohibited it because of a chicken coop, where an opening is made to bring in air and remove foul heat so that the chickens will not die. This implies that fashioning a utensil here is not forbidden by the Torah except in the case of a chicken coop. Taz, paragraph 1. Rambam writes the same at the beginning of chapter 23, and Magen Avraham cites him there. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 3, writes similarly. The rule that liability for a hole applies only when it is made both to bring in and take out refers to a detached object not connected to the ground. For something attached to the ground, liability for building applies even when the hole is not made both to bring in and take out. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 3.
