(s.k. 10) And there are those who permit, etc., and it appears to me that according to all it is forbidden to fix, etc. — lest you say: just as demolition is permitted, for it is inferior demolition — so fixing hinges is inferior building and permitted; and so is the view on this; therefore Magen Avraham wrote that nevertheless it is forbidden, for it is fixing; for in building there is none — nevertheless there is fixing; unlike in demolition, which is inferior demolition and also he does not fix — on the contrary, he medakdeiks:
