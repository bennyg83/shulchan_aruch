(10) There. It would be fixing a utensil. In chapter Chavit (Shabbos daf 146) we say: Any opening not made for inserting and removing is not an opening; and it is the rabbis who decreed due to a chicken coop — that one makes it for letting in air and letting out heat — meaning the foul odor so the chickens do not die. From this it appears this fixing a utensil is not forbidden from the Torah except for a chicken coop. Taz section 1. And likewise Rambam beginning of chapter 23 and Magen Avraham brought it there. And likewise Tosafos Shabbos letter 3. And that which we say he is not liable for piercing unless it is made for inserting and removing — this is in something detached not attached to the ground; but in something attached to the ground liability due to building applies even if not made for inserting and removing.
