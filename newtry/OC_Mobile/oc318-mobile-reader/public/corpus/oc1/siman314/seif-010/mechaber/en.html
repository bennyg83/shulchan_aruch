Seals that are in the ground — such as the door of a pit that has a rope tied to it — one may untie it, for it is not a permanent knot, since it stands to be untied; but one may not tear or cut [it], because of [the prohibition of] demolishing. And this is specifically where it was made to endure, [with the intent] not to remove it on Shabbos; but if it was not made to endure at all, it is permitted. And for this reason it is permitted to remove the board that is placed before the oven and sealed with clay, since it is not made to endure {and see above, siman 259}.
