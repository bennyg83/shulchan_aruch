Seals on the ground such as a cellar door to which a rope is tied — he may untie it, for it is not a permanent knot, since behold he stands to untie it; but he may not pry open and cut because of demolishing; and specifically when it is made to last, on condition not to remove it on Shabbat; but if it is not made to last at all, it is permitted; and for this reason it is permitted to remove a sheet that they place before the oven and coat with mud that is not made to last (and see above siman 259).(.
