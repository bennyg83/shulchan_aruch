Palm-frond bundles, etc. and undoing the rope chains — so is the language of Tur and it is the explanation of what was said there, in the Gemara "he unties"; and so Rashi explained there; and see in the novellae of R' Akiva Eiger who left on this requires further study from what Magen Avraham brought at the end of siman 317 in the name of Rambam that one who braids ropes is liable due to tying and one who undoes is liable due to untying; and even though he undoes not in order to rebuild — nevertheless forbidden d'rabbanan since he undoes a permanent knot. And behold according to Rashi and Tur there is no difficulty — for they would not reason thus like Rambam; but on Rambam it is difficult — why did they permit here to untie? And in my humble opinion Rambam will explain what the Gemara said "he unties" — namely he disconnects and tears; and perforce permitted — for it is no worse than what they permitted to cut even with a utensil [and it is not applicable to forbid due to tearing itself which is d'rabbanan forbidden even when not in order to sew — for this applies only to tearing a garment where tearing in order to sew applies — and therefore the rabbis decreed even when not in order to sew — unlike here]; and similarly as stated in Shabbos 21 regarding worn-out priestly trousers — from them they would kindle and from them they would untie — whose meaning there is that they would tear — and as Rashi explained there; but to undo the braiding of the rope — I can tell you it is forbidden since it is included in untying a permanent knot:
