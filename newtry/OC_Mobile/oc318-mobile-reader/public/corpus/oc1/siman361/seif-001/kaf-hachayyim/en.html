1) [Se'if 1] A roof adjacent to a public domain. It is not surrounded by a courtyard on all its sides; rather, a public domain lies along one side. Rashi on Eruvin 84b; Beis Yosef; Olas Shabbos, subsection 1.
2) There. A roof adjacent to a public domain, within ten handbreadths of the public domain and more than ten handbreadths above the courtyard. This means that on the side facing the public domain it is not ten handbreadths high, whereas on the side facing the courtyard it is ten handbreadths high on every side, and the people of the public domain use it.
