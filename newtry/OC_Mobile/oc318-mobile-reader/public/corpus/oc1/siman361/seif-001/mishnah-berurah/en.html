(א) Within ten tefachim — meaning that toward reshut ha-rabbim it is not ten tefachim high and toward the courtyard it is ten tefachim high; for if it were not ten high even toward the courtyard a ladder would not help at all, for it is a full public domain when the public leans on it, and when they do not lean on it it is in any case karmelit; but now since from the courtyard side it is ten high, the roof has the status of a private domain and it is permitted to make use of it when he has a fixed ladder to ascend from his courtyard to his roof, for by this he reveals his intent that he ascends to his roof from the people of reshut ha-rabbim and it applies only to him; and if from the side of reshut ha-rabbim it is ten high, a ladder is not needed. And if the roof is low less than ten tefachim on every side and its interior is sunken ten tefachim — such as if he dug the ground inside and completed it to ten — the view of Magen Avraham is that a ladder is not needed, for there is no karmelit upon it at all, for it is a full private domain; and many later authorities disagree with him, and their view is that d'rabbanan there is karmelit above from above since it is low less than ten on every side, and a ladder is needed to permit it; and especially when the public leans on it, it is possible that thereby it is a full public domain and even a ladder does not help to permit it:
(ב) Make use of it — that they place hats and small utensils on the roof:
(ג) This is like a public domain — not an actual public domain, for if so a ladder would not help; rather meaning like a public domain:
(ד) From his courtyard — but on the roof it is permitted to carry throughout all of it:
(ה) Any that is fixed there, etc. — meaning that the ladder stands there always; but if sometimes he removes it, it is like one who does not exist:
