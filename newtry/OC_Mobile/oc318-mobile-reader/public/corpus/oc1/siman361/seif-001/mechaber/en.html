דין גג הסמוך לרשות הרבים. ובו ב סעיפים:
The law of a roof adjacent to a public domain. And in it are 2 seifim: A roof adjacent to a public domain — within ten tefachim and above ten relative to the courtyard — since the public makes use of it, it is like a public domain, and it is forbidden for the owner of the roof to make use of it from his courtyard until he makes for it a fixed ladder from his courtyard. What is a fixed ladder? Any that is fixed there both for weekdays and for Shabbat.
