A courtyard that was breached fully or more than ten amot into a public domain — the place of the partition is judged like the sides of a public domain, which is karmelit,(as is the courtyard itself. If it was breached at a corner, even less than ten amot is' forbidden. Similarly a house that was breached at a corner even less than ten amot, and the roofing was also breached until only a diagonal strip remains, is forbidden. But a breach that is not at a corner — we apply the principle that the edge of the roof descends and seals, even if more than ten amot, provided the' edge of the roof is not slanted, since then there is no effective edge.And some say we also require the width of the roofing to be four tefachim (Tosafos and Rosh, chapter Keitzad Mishtatfim, Semag, and Tur). We apply the principle that the edge of the roof descends and seals even with two partitions, if there are two complete partitions joined together — but two facing each other, no. (Tur))
