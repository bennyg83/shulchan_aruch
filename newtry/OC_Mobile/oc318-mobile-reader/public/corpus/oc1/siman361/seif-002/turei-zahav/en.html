And it is provided the edge of the roof is not slanted. So too Tur wrote, and these are his words: such as that the edge of the roofing is level and four tefachim wide; but if it is slanted and does not have four tefachim we do not say descends and seals, end of his words; and Beit Yosef wrote that he learned this from what we say in chapter 2 of Eruvin daf 25 in the dispute of Rabba and Rabbi Zeira whether the airspace of the roofing exceeds or does not exceed — Rabba holds it exceeds and it is more than two se'ah, and Rabbi Zeira holds it does not exceed, for we say the edge of the roof descends and seals and it is as if there is a partition until below; let us say in the dispute of Rav and Shmuel they argue — as stated regarding a portico in a field: Rav said permitted to carry throughout all of it, we say the edge of the roof descends and seals; and Shmuel said one may carry only within four amot, we do not apply the edge of the roof, etc.; if made like the form of a portico where the roof is level — so too Rabba agrees we apply the edge of the roof, etc.; and what Rabba argues here is where it is made like an arzila — Rashi explained a protruding slanted roof with no edge — and it appears to our teacher that since there is no edge, all agree the principle the edge of the roof does not apply; and Rabbi Zeira who permits there — not that the edge of the roof is permitted, but because since it is roofed it is not considered karpef; and even though Tosafos and Rosh disagree in chapter Kol Gagot on Rashi who explained roofing and in diagonal in slope — not to say they hold that even when slanted we apply the edge of the roof, for that which is made like arzila where we say in Osin Pasin we do not apply the edge of the roof for all — meaning a slanted roof — they argue only in explaining diagonal; and "and there are not four tefachim" means that if the beam does not have width of four tefachim even though it is not slanted we do not apply the edge of the roof; and our teacher wrote vav in place of or, end of his words; and their words are very very astonishing — for if in slanted we would not apply the edge of the roof for all, why did Tur write in siman 358 at its end karpef of three se'ah and a beam from it beit se'ah permitted even if the roofing is slanted, for we consider as if a partition at the end of the roofing, etc., end of his words — it implies explicitly that we apply the edge of the roof; and this is not like the words of Beit Yosef who said that here all hold to forbid and we do not apply the edge of the roof; and further, according to Beit Yosef, what is the reason of Rabbi Zeira who permits — since we do not apply the edge of the roof; and further it is difficult — our teacher Tur wrote explicitly in hints chapter Osin Pasin in a karpef of three se'ah and roofing in it beit se'ah even if the roofing is slanted we apply the edge of the roof descends and seals — behold it is clear not like the words of Beit Yosef; therefore it appears our teacher Beit Yosef did not fulfill all his obligation of examination in this, even though Peri Megadim agreed with him; rather the main point is that the words of Tur are like the view of Rosh in chapter Osin Pasin regarding roofing — even for Rabbi Zeira the permission is because of the edge of the roof, but the Gemara says there that the dispute of Rabba and Rabbi Zeira is in the dispute of Rav and Shmuel whether we say the principle the edge of the roof at all, namely where made like a portico, and Rabba and Rabbi Zeira hold we certainly rule we apply the edge of the roof, etc., where made level — only that if made like arzila, namely slanted as Rashi explained as Rosh brings this Rashi there — in this Rabba and Rabbi Zeira argue, for Rabba we do not apply the edge of the roof if it is slanted and Rabbi Zeira we apply even when slanted, and we rule like Rabbi Zeira as Rosh wrote; but still one condition is needed, namely that the width of the beam whose edge descends and seals must be four tefachim, not like Rashi who holds one tefach suffices as Tosafos wrote in chapter Keitzad Mishtatfim (Eruvin daf 86) s.v. a beam of four is permitted, etc. — Tur did not need to mention there that there should be width of four, for there it deals with roofing that is a covering for a portico and certainly there are four tefachim; but here it deals with roofing that was breached and sometimes there remains one beam less than four tefachim — therefore he mentioned that there should specifically be four tefachim; and the explanation of his words is: such as that the edge of the roofing is level and four tefachim wide — meaning the edge is level and four tefachim wide in that width; but if it is slanted and in that slope that is toward the edge of the roofing there are not four tefachim we do not apply the edge of the roof; and therefore he did not say in the latter section and there is not in its width four tefachim as he mentioned in the beginning width of four tefachim — for here it does not refer to width in its simple sense but in the slope he said there are four tefachim in the place that is the edge of the roofing, then it is permitted; and if so we need not strain to explain that vav was in place of or as Beit Yosef wrote, but it is as its simple meaning; and that which we say here in chapter Kol Gagot that they say in the house of Rav in the name of Rav such as when breached at a corner in diagonal — Tur holds like Rosh here that the reason is not because it is in diagonal that the edge of the roof cannot apply, but because it was at a corner and the reason of the edge of the roof descends and seals is because it is like an opening and here at a corner people do not make an opening — it turns out according to this that even in slanted, all that there are in the slope before the edge four tefachim, we properly say the edge of the roof; and everything is resolved after examination, with Hashem's help; and Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch did not descend to this at all; and further Peri Megadim questioned the words of Tur against each other, for here he ruled like Tosafos that we require width of four tefachim for the edge of the roof, etc., and not like Rashi; and in chapter 5 of Sukkah siman 634 he wrote even if there is in the edge of the roofing of a portico one tefach we do not apply descends and seals, etc. — it appears the matter depends on the measure of one tefach and he distinguished between Shabbat and Sukkah — and this is a great strain; and to resolve it initially there he did not rule for halacha that one tefach suffices but mentioned there the measure that helps for the edge of the roof, namely for Rashi as he holds one tefach automatically for Tosafos four tefachim; and in hints he wrote there at the beginning of chapter Sukkah explicitly four tefachim; and on this he said nevertheless we do not apply there the edge of the roof for the reason mentioned there — all this is plain and clear:
