[2] [Levush] If they can, it does not require, etc. The Magen Avraham writes in the name of the Beit Yosef and Hagahot Ashri that if the hole is ten handbreadths high, even though from the courtyard side it is less than ten, no ladder is required. This requires study, because the sources appear to require a ladder, except where it is ten high specifically on the side adjacent to the public domain.
