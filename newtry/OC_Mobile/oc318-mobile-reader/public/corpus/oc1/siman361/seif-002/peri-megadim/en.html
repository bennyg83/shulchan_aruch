and Rashi explained roofing roof even all beit se'ah; and Taz explained the words of Tur that even in slanted we also say descends and seals like Rabbi Zeira Eruvin 25:1 and as written in siman 358 and in Shulchan Aruch seif 12 in slanted the edge of the roof, etc., and here is the connecting vav; and in my view the explanation is: when the roofing is level not slanted we require width of four tefachim in the beam and automatically width of four tefachim also on the walls; but even slanted that there are in that slope four tefachim in the roof and beam and in width below on the walls there are not four tefachim as is known — nevertheless we apply the edge of the roof descends and seals since there are in the beam and roof four tefachim at least; and therefore he did not conclude in the latter section width of four tefachim, for below on the walls there are not four tefachim but only in the slope of the roof there are four tefachim as stated, and it is a leniency. And the explanation in diagonal is not in slope but at a corner we do not apply the edge of the roof, for it is like an opening and at a corner people do not make an opening; see Levush and where the roofing remained above level and was not breached in diagonal we apply on every side the edge of the roof descends and as if there is an opening in every partition at its end as above; and according to this for the Mechaber here and in siman 358 seif 12 there is prima facie a leniency — roofed karpef more than two se'ah permitted to carry; and for what Taz wrote one can say forbidden and say roofing at the end of se'ah is lighter and recognition exists unlike roofing the entire karpef forbidden; even for the Mechaber this is strained; and it deals with siman 358 in Magen Avraham 1 and see Tosafos there; and with Hashem's help in siman 362 in Magen David 1 it will be explained further:
