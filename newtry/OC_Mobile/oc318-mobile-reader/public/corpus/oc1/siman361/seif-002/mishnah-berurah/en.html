(א) The place of the partition is judged like the sides of reshut ha-rabbim — the sides of reshut ha-rabbim ordinarily are called when they place large stones or insert stakes adjacent to the wall so wagons should not damage it; and the airspace between them is called the sides of reshut ha-rabbim and its law is like karmelit, and forbidden to carry from within it to reshut ha-rabbim; and it teaches us that even in our matter, even though there is no separation at all between the courtyard and reshut ha-rabbim and the public enters it directly and tramples it — nevertheless its law is only like the sides of reshut ha-rabbim and there is karmelit upon it, and forbidden to carry from this place outside which is reshut ha-rabbim; and if karmelit adjacent to a courtyard it is permitted, for from karmelit to karmelit one may carry; and in our reshut ha-rabbim one should be stringent:

(ב) As is the courtyard — meaning as the courtyard itself there is karmelit upon it because it is breached on a fourth side, so too is the place of the partition; and we learn from this language two matters: first, that in this courtyard it is forbidden to carry except within four amot; second, that from the place of the partition into the courtyard it is permitted to carry within four amot, for both have karmelit upon them, as above in siman 346 seifim 2 and 3:

(ג) And if breached at a corner — meaning that the breach consumed both walls of the courtyard at their ends together; and it appears in the Gemara that if the breach was made in other places of the walls, even if in two walls, as long as in each breach there is not more than ten amot it is permitted; and see there Tosafos that specifically if what stands exceeds what is breached, but if not it is forbidden when the breach is in two walls; and so Rashba in Avodat HaKodesh:

(ד) Less than ten, etc. — for we cannot call that breach an opening, for people do not make an opening at a corner; and we say this specifically regarding a breach; but if he made the form of an opening, and all the more if he made two doorposts and a lintel properly, certainly there is an opening upon it and it is permitted:

(ה) Forbidden — and it is reasonable that if the breach was less than one amah one should not be stringent [Peri Megadim]; however in Avodat HaKodesh I found for Rashba that from three tefachim until four it needs two lechi posts, and if it was four it has no validity until he reduces it from four, etc., see there:

(ו) And similarly a house, etc. — meaning that you should not say a house is different, for even though we cannot permit it because of an opening since it is at a corner, nevertheless there is because of the edge of the roof that upon it descends and seals; it teaches us that sometimes there is not because of the edge of the roof and explains how:

(ז) Until only a diagonal strip remains forbidden — for in this that the roofing is not level we do not apply the edge of the roof descends and seals:

(ח) Even more than ten — and even if this too was in the full entire wall so that no projections remained at all; and in the Mechaber's words it was not explained whether we apply the edge of the roof descends and seals specifically when one wall is breached, or even when two walls are breached; as appears in Beit Yosef; however Rama in his gloss copied for practical ruling the words of Tur who ruled plainly like the poskim who hold that even breached two partitions we apply the edge of the roof descends and seals, as below:

(ט) Provided, etc., slanted — meaning we do not apply that it descends and seals only when the roofing is level, but not when it is slanted like our roofs that stand on a slope if there were no roofing beneath it:

(י) And some say, etc. — and so is the halacha as I wrote in Biur Halacha:

(כ) That we also require, etc., four tefachim — meaning that then we apply the edge of the roof descends and seals, and it is not comparable to an ordinary mavo beam that suffices with one tefach — for a mavo beam is only for recognition, but wherever we need a partition then there must specifically be four tefachim in it, and then we apply the edge of the roof descends and seals:

(ל) If there are two complete partitions — meaning and the hidden partitions remain with roofing upon them that was not breached with them:
