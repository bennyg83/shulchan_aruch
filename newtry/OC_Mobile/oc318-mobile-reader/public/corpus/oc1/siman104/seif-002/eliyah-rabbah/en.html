(2) And if impossible, etc. — meaning he should ask after their welfare because of danger, end of his words. And Levush wrote: and responds "peace"; and so wrote Rosh and Remazim; and perhaps they hold when there is danger, if he does not ask it is permitted; but it appears to him that presumably there is no danger when he does not ask, since he stands in prayer. And in the incident of the pious man brought in the gemara, where the danger was for not responding — Bach wrote the same law when ones (coercion) faces him. Magen Avraham wrote that because of monetary loss he may not interrupt.
