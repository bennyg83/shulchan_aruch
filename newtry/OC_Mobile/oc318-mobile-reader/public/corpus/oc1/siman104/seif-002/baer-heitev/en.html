With supplications. For no need, however, it is forbidden to move from one's place until he has taken three steps, as stated in siman 123, Magen Avraham. Regarding chazzanim who leave their places on Yom Kippur when they reach 'and we bow,' see siman 621. After he has concluded the prayer, when Avinu Malkeinu is recited, there is no prohibition if he moves his feet for some mitzvah, such as when he is honored with opening the Aron Kodesh, which people regard to some extent as a mitzvah. Nevertheless, he must first say, 'May the words of my mouth be acceptable,' etc. Olas Tamid.
