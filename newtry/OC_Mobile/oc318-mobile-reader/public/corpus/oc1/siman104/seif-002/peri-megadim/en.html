Returns — Taz: the view of Ri'f and Rosh is known — Ri'f does not distinguish between ones or not, only between prayer and Kriat Shema; and Rosh does not distinguish between Kriat Shema and prayer, only between ones or not; and Rabbeinu Yonah: silence here is ones even not due to himself, he returns to the beginning; and so in siman 103 seif 2 Rabbeinu Yonah showed — prayer is like Kriat Shema; and so in Levush siman 85 seif 1 prayer equals Kriat Shema, and without ones he does not return; and Bach brought from Tosafot 33a s.v. "but scorpion" that it deals with delay — for without delay there is no view on daf 23a; and it is astonishing, for Rav Ashi concludes without delay, etc. (Tosafot's intent: since it states anonymously "he interrupts" and did not mention "returns to beginning" without delay — do not err that he also does not return to beginning of blessing — that is not so); and one can say there the man is "pushed away" per Rashi there, and also for Rosh at the time of drinking; but here from where to say he returns to beginning without delay — perforce when he delayed; and the reason is it is not ones due to himself; and Taz rejected this, for without delay it deals, per Rosh's explanation there; and behold the Perishah here wrote that the Sar MiKutzi disagrees with Rashba and Rama ruled like Sar MiKutzi; and see Beit Yosef here in the name of Chiddushei HaRash on Berachot 33a: per Ra'avad we require "pushed away," and with other ones he does not return to beginning — Sar MiKutzi's words are like Rosh 23a, which Bach brought in seif 5 — it implies whoever is anus returns to beginning; and so Rama here ruled that anus too returns to beginning; and thus Bach's words are Chiddushei HaRash's words as said; and Magen Avraham 65 note 2 regarding the halacha.
