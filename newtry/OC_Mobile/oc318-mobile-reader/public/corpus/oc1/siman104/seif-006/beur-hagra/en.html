Seif 6, “That which we say, etc.” This is from the Yerushalmi, as explained above in siman 65.
“If he spoke, etc.” This is unlike Rabbeinu David Abudraham and Orchos Chaim, who write that he must always return to the beginning. The Tur and Shulchan Aruch, however, in siman 114, seif 7, write in the name of the Raavyah that when it was intentional he always returns to the beginning, although the Bach already expressed surprise at this. According to the Rashba, he always returns to the place where he stopped, even in the middle of a blessing, as explained above.
