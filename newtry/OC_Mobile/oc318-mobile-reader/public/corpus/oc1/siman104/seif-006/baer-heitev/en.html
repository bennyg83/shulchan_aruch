During prayer. See siman 114, seif 7, that if one spoke intentionally he returns to the beginning. See Bach and Taz. It requires study what the law is if one spoke intentionally during Krias Shema. Nowadays, when most people are not careful to refrain from speaking during the blessings of Krias Shema, perhaps they are judged as inadvertent, since one who mistakenly believes an act is permitted is considered inadvertent. See Magen Avraham. If one spoke between one blessing and another, no corrective action is required; immediately after speaking he continues his prayer. If, however, he paused long enough to complete the entire prayer, he returns to the beginning. Taz; see there.
