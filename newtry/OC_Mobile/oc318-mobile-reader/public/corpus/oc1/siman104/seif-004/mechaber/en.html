If one saw an ox approaching one, one interrupts [one's prayer]. For we distance from a regular ox (i.e. one that is not accustomed to do harm) 50 cubits, and from a forewarned ox (i.e., that is accustomed to do harm] as far as one can see. And if oxen in that place are known not to do harm, one does not interrupt.[] נ' אמות וממועד [פי' שרגיל להזיק] מלא עיניו ואם שוורים שבמקום ההוא מוחזקים שאינם מזיקים אינו פוסק:
