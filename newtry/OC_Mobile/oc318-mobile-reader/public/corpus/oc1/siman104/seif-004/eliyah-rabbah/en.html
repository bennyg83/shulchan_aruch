(4) He should not interrupt, etc. — such as to speak after removing it; but he may walk, etc., for walking is not interruption (Beit Yosef); even though we say in the preceding seif he may not leave his place — nevertheless it is not called interruption regarding needing to start from the beginning of the blessing, but from where he stopped; but when he interrupted with speech it is a complete interruption and he must start from the beginning of the blessing; and so is implied in Lechem Chamudot page 40. Through this Taz's difficulty is resolved; see there; and see responsa Beit Yaakov siman 16.
