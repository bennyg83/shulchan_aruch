But it is allowed to hint with his hands so that the baby shuts up. And so Maharam ben Habib in his response to the 10th and the Maharayi wrote further that if he has already made a way for the baby to be silent and is still grinding and harassing him, he should distance himself from him and not speak. A dignitary who is standing in prayer and the Shatz is waiting for him to recite the Kaddish and he is not upset that they will wait for him because of a gathering and this thought bothers him in his prayer, he is allowed to imply to the Shatz that he prays as he should and will not wait for him like a baby who grinds and bothers his blood:
