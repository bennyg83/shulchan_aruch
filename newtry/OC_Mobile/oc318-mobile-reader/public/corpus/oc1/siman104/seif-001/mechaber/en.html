To Not Interrupt In The [Middle Of The Amidah] Prayer. Containing 8 S'ifim: One may not
interrupt during one's prayer [i.e. Amidah]. And even if a Jewish king is inquiring about one's well-being, one may not respond to him. But [regarding responding to] a king of the nations of the world, if one is able to shorten [one's prayer], meaning that one would say the beginning of the blessing and its end before the [king] reaches one, one should shorten it. Or if [one's on the road and] one is able to veer off the road, [then] one should veer off, but one may not interrupt by talking. And if it's impossible for one [to do so], one may interrupt.
