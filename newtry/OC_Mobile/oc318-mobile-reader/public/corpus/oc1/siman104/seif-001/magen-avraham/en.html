A non-Jewish king. He fears that the king may kill him. Because of monetary loss, however, he may not interrupt. See seif 3, siman 108 at the end of seif katan 8, and what I wrote in siman 92, seif 2. Whether one must interrupt in a place of danger, see Yoreh Deah, beginning of siman 157, and the Tur. Regarding the incident of the pious man and the official, it must be that he was confident that the official would accept his explanation.
