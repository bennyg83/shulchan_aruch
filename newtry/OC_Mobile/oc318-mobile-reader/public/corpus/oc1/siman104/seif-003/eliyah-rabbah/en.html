Olat Tamid wrote: if they honor the chazan by opening the ark, which the public considers somewhat a mitzvah, there is no prohibition to uproot; and proof from that incident of R' Akiva; and in any event it appears he should say beforehand "may they be acceptable," etc., end of his words. And I did not know what proof is needed for this and what prohibition applies — for every day we see the chazan uproots and says Tachanun; and on Monday and Thursday says "and He is merciful" and goes to the bimah and reads Torah before Kaddish "may it be accepted" that he says "Who makes peace"; only specifically for an individual praying does this prohibition apply; but the chazan, since he already prayed quietly, need not return three steps immediately, as written below siman 123; and see below end of siman 281 regarding uprooting by the chazan within prayer.
