(10) He should not interrupt. Meaning, verbally, such as by telling another person to remove it. Likewise, throughout this seif and the following seif, every reference to interruption means verbal interruption. Walking, however, is permitted, because walking is not called an interruption. This applies specifically here, where walking is needed to remove himself from the snake, even though we assume that ordinarily there is no danger. Without need, however, walking is also considered a minor interruption, as the Mechaber ruled in seif 2.
(11) And poised to injure. Even if it is not coiled around his heel, but he sees it approaching him, he interrupts. If he can avoid it by moving aside from the road before it reaches him, he should move aside and not interrupt verbally, as stated above in seif 1.
