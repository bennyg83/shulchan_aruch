(Seif katan 5) To the beginning, etc. Prayer is more stringent than Krias Shema. The Beit Yosef likewise distinguishes between Krias Shema and prayer when one paused long enough to complete the whole prayer, as explained above in seif 5. Even according to the Rama, who maintains that when one paused long enough to complete the whole prayer there is no distinction between Krias Shema and prayer, but only whether the interruption was caused by duress, nevertheless, where one did not pause long enough to complete the whole prayer, he agrees with the Beit Yosef.
And see Beit Yosef, siman 59, as the text should read. This was also codified above in siman 59, seif 5, that if one erred or became unable to continue during a blessing of Krias Shema and another person took his place, the second person must return to the beginning of the blessing. It is explained there what is considered the beginning of the blessing. See there.
