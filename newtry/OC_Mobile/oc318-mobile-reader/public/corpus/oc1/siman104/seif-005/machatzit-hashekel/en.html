(s.k. 5) To the beginning, etc. — for prayer is stricter than Keriat Shema; and so the Mahari Abuhav holds to distinguish between Keriat Shema and prayer: if he waited in order to finish it entirely, as explained above in siman 65; and even per the Rama, who holds that when one waits in order to finish it entirely there is no distinction between Keriat Shema and prayer except whether it was due to duress or not — nevertheless, when he did not wait in order to finish it entirely, the Mahari Abuhav admits.

And Shulchan Aruch brought it in siman 59, as stated — and it was also brought in Shulchan Aruch above in siman 59, seif 5: if one erred or became mute in the blessing of Keriat Shema and another stood in his place, he must return to the beginning of the blessing; and it is explained there what is called the beginning of the blessing — see there.
