To the beginning of the blessing. This is unlike Krias Shema, where one who inquires after another’s welfare returns to the place where he stopped, as stated in siman 66, because prayer is more stringent than Krias Shema. See Beit Yosef, siman 59, that if one became unable to continue or erred, he returns to the beginning of the blessing. See there, and see Responsa Rashba, end of siman 35.
