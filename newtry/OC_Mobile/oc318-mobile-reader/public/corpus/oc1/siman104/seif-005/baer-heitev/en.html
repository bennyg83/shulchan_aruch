To the beginning. Although in siman 65 he ruled regarding Krias Shema that one need return only to the place where he stopped, prayer is more stringent in this respect. So wrote the Rif. Tosafos, the Rosh, and the Tur, however, do not distinguish between Krias Shema and prayer, but rather distinguish whether the delay resulted from circumstances beyond his control, which is more severe. The Rama likewise ruled in siman 65. See Taz. If he did not return to the beginning, but only to the place where he stopped, and then completed the prayer, he must return to the beginning. Peri Chadash. (Eliyah Rabbah concludes that initially one should be stringent in every case of unavoidable interruption, even where the person himself was not unfit. See there at length.)
