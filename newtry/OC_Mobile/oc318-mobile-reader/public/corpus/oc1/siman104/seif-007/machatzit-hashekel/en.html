(Seif katan 7) He should remain silent, etc. See the end of siman 66, where Magen Avraham writes that at a point where one may interrupt for “Yehei Shemei Rabbah,” he should recite through “l’almei almaya” and then remain silent, because there is a dispute whether one is obligated to recite the entire passage beginning “Yisbarach.” Here, however, since he remains silent and listens, which is considered as though he responds, it is proper for him to remain silent through “da’amiran b’alma,” thereby satisfying all opinions.
