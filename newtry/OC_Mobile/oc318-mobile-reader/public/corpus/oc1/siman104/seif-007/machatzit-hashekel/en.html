(s.k. 7) He should be silent, etc. — and see siman 66, where Magen Avraham wrote: in a place where one stops for amen one should say "until the world" and be silent, for there is a dispute whether one must say the entire formula — until here. But here, where he is silent and listens, it is like answering; it is fitting to be silent until it is said in the congregation, to fulfill everyone's obligation.
