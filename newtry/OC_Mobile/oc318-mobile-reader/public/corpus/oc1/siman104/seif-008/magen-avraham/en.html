He does not interrupt. At first glance this requires study, since it was already ruled that even during Krias Shema he does not interrupt, as stated in siman 66, seif 4, and siman 135, seif 5. Why then must this be taught regarding prayer? Furthermore, the responsum of the Rashba there concerns Krias Shema. Perhaps during Krias Shema, since some authorities maintain that one does interrupt, a person who follows their view is not considered deliberate. During prayer, however, he is considered deliberate and must return to the beginning.
