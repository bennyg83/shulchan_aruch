[Levush] And women, since they did not receive, etc. Kohanim and Leviim are different because they received the forty eight cities, so it is appropriate for them to say 'which You gave to us.' The later authorities write this. Tosafot Berachot 20, however, leaves this point as a difficulty and therefore explains that the uncertainty concerning women is because of brit and Torah. According to the Levush's reasoning there would also be a difficulty why kohanim bring bikkurim and recite the declaration.
[Levush] By virtue of mutual responsibility, etc. The Levush writes the same at the end of siman 193, and so it appears in Tosafot, the Rosh, and the poskim. Above in siman 184, however, he gives the reason that the Sages attached it to a verse. The two explanations can be reconciled.
