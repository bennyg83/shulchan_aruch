1) [Seif 1] It is uncertain, etc. According to Rashba and Raavad, women are obligated by Torah law, while according to Rabbeinu Yonah they are obligated rabbinically. Rosh writes that this is an unresolved question. Therefore, women cannot discharge others from their obligation. The Rambam in chapter 5 of Hilchos Berachos holds the same view, as explained in Beis Yosef, and the Shulchan Aruch rules accordingly here. Levush rules the same, and this is the view of the later authorities. Nevertheless, after the fact, if a woman recited the blessing for men, they fulfilled their obligation. Rishon LeTziyon on Berachos 5b; Zechor LeAvraham, paragraph 2.
2) There: It is uncertain, etc. The same uncertainty applies to servants, since by default their status is equivalent to that of women. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1. An uncircumcised man whose brothers died because of circumcision is likewise subject to uncertainty. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1. See below, siman 199, seif 6.
