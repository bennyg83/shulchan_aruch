And also he, etc. Perishah wrote that even before they took possession it is permitted, and it is not similar to seif 2 when he also renounced his house — for he cannot carry out from it before they took possession, for there presumably he carries out his vessels and it appears as returning from renunciation; but here when he carries out from their houses, presumably they are their vessels and it does not appear as returning from his renunciation — up to here; and it appears to me there is proof from the mishnah daf 69 that teaches: and theirs are permitted to him and to them, since he preceded them before them — it implies that he is permitted immediately:
