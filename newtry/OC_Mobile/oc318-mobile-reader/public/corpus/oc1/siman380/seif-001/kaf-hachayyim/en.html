1) [Se'if 1] One of the courtyard's residents who forgot, etc. The Shulchan Aruch states that he forgot in order to teach that even in such a case he prohibits carrying if he does not renounce his rights. The same law applies even if he intentionally failed to establish an eruv: renunciation is effective, as stated in the Gemara on folio 70a. Rambam likewise writes this at the beginning of chapter 2.
