Laws of renouncing domain. And it contains 4 seifim: One of the
members of the courtyard who forgot and did not make an eruv with the others forbids them. What is their remedy? He renounces his domain to them — that he say 'my domain is renounced to you' or 'acquired by you' — and he need not acquire it with a formal acquisition; and he can renounce even after nightfall; and if he lives with four or five he must renounce to each and every one — that he say 'my domain is renounced to you and to you'; and one says that it suffices that he say 'my domain is renounced to all of you'; if he renounced his domain without specification, he did not renounce except his domain that he has in the courtyard — therefore they are permitted to carry out from their houses to the courtyard, and also he who is a mere guest; but they are forbidden to carry out from his house to the courtyard, and also he; and one says that he must lock his house so that he not come to carry out in a forbidden manner, and he should not open it except when he wishes to go out and come in, and he should lock it immediately after his going out and coming in:
