To you — Taz, Rambam z"l chapter 2 of Eruvin halacha 2, and Beit Yosef and nevertheless and in Rambam there: to you, to you, and to you three times when three dwell together — it can be explained as a majority; not so one to two, for all agree it can be explained that he says to all of you; and in Shulchan Aruch he wrote to you and to you twice. However, this is the language of the Tur in seifim 4 and 5, etc., as if he said to you and to you, etc. And it can be explained that the Mechaber holds the halacha is like one opinion, for he brought only the language of the Tur and wrote: and one says it is not explicit except in the Tur — see there; I will record it in the general principles. And what he wrote in the Rosh version: all or all of it — and the version stands for Rambam z"l as he wrote three times to you and to you and to you, that we do not explain the majority as the whole. And words fitting for one who spoke them. And know: if only part of the members of the courtyard stand and he says 'I renounce to all of you,' it can be explained that all agree he is not renounced except to those standing there; nevertheless, since he wrote if all were standing, etc., to all of you — it can be explained whether between all or to all of you:
