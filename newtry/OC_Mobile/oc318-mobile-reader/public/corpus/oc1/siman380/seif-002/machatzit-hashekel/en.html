(s"k 2) For all, etc. — even one who renounced is forbidden (nevertheless), and these are the words of the Gemara daf 96, 2, and this is Rashi there s.v. hag, etc. — per the one who does not explain that he did not renounce, all are forbidden; for even though this one renounced and made an eruv with his fellow, since he made the eruv from yesterday with the intent of this partner he did not make an eruv, for at this moment it is not his; and similarly it teaches: one returns from the market, etc., and left his domain to one of the members of the courtyard, etc.; and this is explained above siman 371 seif 3:
