(4) Seven by seven. With a cord surrounding twenty-nine handbreadths and two fifths, we can square within it seven by seven. The same applies when one makes it five-sided or more: we require that it have enough space to square seven by seven within it.
(5) Garments to beautify it, etc. Although regarding height it is stated above in siman 633:9 that they do not reduce it from its height by this, here, regarding the side, it does not help, for in any event it thereby does not contain his head, most of him, and his table.
