Se'if 3 And they reduce it. See Bach below, siman 639:3, who wrote the reason: since it is forbidden to remove it throughout all seven days. If so, where one stipulated that he does not separate himself from it for the entire twilight period, so that it is permitted to remove it as below, it does not reduce the measure.
