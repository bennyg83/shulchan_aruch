Garments to beautify it, etc. See Pri Megadim, who is uncertain: perhaps the fact that they reduce the measure from the side is only rabbinic.
It from seven, etc. See Bach below, siman 639:3, who wrote the reason: since it is forbidden to remove it throughout all seven days. According to this, where one stipulated that he is not separating himself from it for the entire twilight period, so that it is permitted to remove it, as explained below, it does not reduce the measure. See Mishbetzos Zahav below, siman 639:3 [for he wrote there that this requires analysis]. Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger.
Is invalid. See Shiltei Gibborim in the name of Riaz, that utensils used for service do not reduce the measure even from the side. Now, the Magen Avraham copied the words of Shiltei Gibborim above in siman 633, small paragraph 7, regarding when they are hanging and descend into the space of ten, that they are considered like decorations. Seemingly, it would have been a greater novelty had he copied it regarding reduction from the side: that even though decorations reduce the measure, utensils of service do not reduce it. It follows from this that the Magen Avraham did not regard this matter as definite, and therefore he did not copy it. This requires analysis. See siman 630:13, and below siman 639, Turei Zahav, small paragraph 3, and Bach there.
