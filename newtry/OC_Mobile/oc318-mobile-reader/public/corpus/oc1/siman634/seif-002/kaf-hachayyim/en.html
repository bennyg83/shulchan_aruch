2) Ibid. A sukkah that does not measure seven by seven tefachim is invalid. This applies specifically to this exact form, namely, a square measuring seven by seven tefachim. If it is not square, but is less than seven tefachim wide and more than seven tefachim long, it is invalid. Taz, s.k. 1; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 142; Responsa Divrei Shmuel, siman 203; Maamar Mordechai, os 1; Beit Me'ir. See Beit HaShoevah, os 6, who discusses this at length and also cites the words of Birkei Yosef, os 1, who writes that Tosafos to Sukkah came into his possession, etc. Beit HaShoevah challenges him and concludes that the Bach's ruling, which validates such a sukkah as a matter of halachah but is concerned to be stringent in practice, is incorrect. Rather, even as a matter of halachah, whenever it does not contain a square of seven by seven tefachim, it is invalid even after the fact according to all opinions, including Tosafos; see there.
