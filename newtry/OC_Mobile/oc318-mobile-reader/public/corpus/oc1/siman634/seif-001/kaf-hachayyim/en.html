1) [Seif 1] A sukkah that does not measure seven by seven tefachim is invalid. For so it is stated in the Gemara, Sukkah 3a: The halachah requires that it contain his head, the majority of his body, and his table. The Rosh writes that this means seven tefachim by seven tefachim, because a person sits within one amah and his table occupies one tefach, as stated in the Yerushalmi: How much space is needed for a table? One tefach. His words were cited by the Beit Yosef, who writes that this is agreed upon by all the poskim; see there. So too wrote the Bach.
