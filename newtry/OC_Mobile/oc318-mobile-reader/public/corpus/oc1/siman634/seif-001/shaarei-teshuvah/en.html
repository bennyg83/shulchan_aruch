Seven. See Ba'er Heitev. See Pnei Yehoshua, who wrote to uphold the view of Tosafos according to our text and in accordance with the approach of the Bach. The Birkei Yosef wrote that he found in Tosafos on old parchment a text like our version; see there. Also the Bach himself wrote to be stringent in practice; see there. See Devar Shmuel, siman 345, who also wrote that so we rule stringently in these countries, and therefore he was stringent with a sukkah longer than thirty and wider than fifteen; but above, its abundant amount reduces and invalidates the air of the s'chach until there remains uncovered air of only five handbreadths. See there.
