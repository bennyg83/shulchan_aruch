Se'if 1. A sukkah that does not have seven by seven, etc. Note: See the Turei Zahav and Magen Avraham, who discuss at length whether we require it to be square, and the Chacham Tzvi in a responsum also holds this way. See my novellae to Orach Chayim, siman 631, in my novellae to the Talmud there, where I wrote a proof for their words in this matter. Examine this carefully.
