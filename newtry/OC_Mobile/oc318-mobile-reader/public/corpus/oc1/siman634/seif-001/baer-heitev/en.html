Seven. See Turei Zahav and Magen Avraham, who emended Tosafos, page 3a, s.v. lo nitzrechah: "It is not a refutation, etc., for perhaps it was very long, but it was not seven handbreadths wide, etc." See there, for it must read: "and it was only seven wide." See there. This is straightforward. See Yad Aharon and Responsa Chacham Tzvi, siman 142. The Magen Avraham wrote: It appears to me that even in a very large sukkah, if in one place there is one corner drawn inward that does not have seven, it is forbidden to sit there, since the place is too narrow for him to sit there. See there.
