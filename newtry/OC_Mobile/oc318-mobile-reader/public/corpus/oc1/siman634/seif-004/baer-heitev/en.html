And his table. It appears to me that if part of his table stands in the sukkah and part of it is inside the house, it is permitted, for we do not decree lest he be drawn after his table, since part of it is standing in the sukkah. Furthermore, a table of one handbreadth is sufficient. Magen Avraham; see there. See Responsa Devar Shmuel, siman 203, and see Yad Aharon.
