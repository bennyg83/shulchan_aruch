4) It appears that even if the sukkah is very large, but in one place there is a projecting corner that does not measure seven by seven tefachim, it is forbidden to sit there, because that place is too narrow for him to sit in. Magen Avraham, ibid.; Eliyah Rabbah, ibid.; Beit HaShoevah, os 7; Mishna Berurah, os 1. See there in Biur Halachah, where he challenges those who question the aforementioned words of Magen Avraham. He therefore writes that one should not depart from Magen Avraham's ruling. In one particular respect, however, one may be lenient: One need not be careful not to place the table in that projecting corner. We are not concerned that he may be drawn after his table, because many poskim hold that even when one's table is inside the house we do not issue this decree. They are sufficient authorities upon whom to rely in this respect; see there.
