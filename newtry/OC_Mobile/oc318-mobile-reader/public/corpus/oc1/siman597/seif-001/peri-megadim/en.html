One does not. Fast on Rosh Hashanah; see Turei Zahav and Eliyah Rabbah. And she urged him to eat bread; one can say he did not eat. Also in the Zohar at the beginning of parashas Beshalach it expounds, and it was the day, and he passed there, that it was Rosh Hashanah, not on and it was the day, and he passed to Shunem. So too is the intent of the gloss of our teacher Rabbi Tzvi Hirsch, of blessed memory, on the margin of the Shulchan Aruch. But in the Midrash there is an exposition on and it was the day at the beginning of Melachim II, siman 44: she pressed him to eat, which implies there is no mitzvah to fast, for she was a great woman. So too, from, and whenever he passed by, he would turn there to eat bread, it implies that the first time also he ate bread. See what I will write, with Hashem's help, in the next siman.
