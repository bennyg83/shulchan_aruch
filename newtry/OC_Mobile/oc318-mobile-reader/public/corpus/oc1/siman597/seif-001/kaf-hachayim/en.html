A) [Section A] Eat and drink and be merry. R. L. Even though it is the Day of Judgement, it is appropriate in the Rabbinical Law (16 11) from the mitzva of and rejoice in your holiday that it is also a holiday in general, as it is written, blow a shofar in the new moon on the day of our holiday, and it is also said in Nehemiah 8, eat oils and drink sweets, etc., because this day is holy to our the Master, and do not grieve, for the wisdom of the Omnipresent is your strength.
