Seif 1 — they eat and drink and rejoice and do not fast on Rosh Hashanah, etc. NB: see Beit Yosef and Taz what they brought — two views on this. It appears to me there is proof from gemara that there is a tannaitic dispute — from what is taught in Taanit chapter 3: that city fasts and sounds teruah and all surroundings fast and do not sound; R' Akiva says they sound and do not fast. Tosefot brought in the name of Yerushalmi explaining their dispute: Tanna Kamma compares to Yom Kippur where they fast and do not sound; R' Akiva compares to Rosh Hashanah where they sound and do not fast — end. If so Yerushalmi is explicit on Rosh Hashanah there is no mitzva to fast, for this is R' Akiva's reason deriving from Rosh Hashanah. But one must understand why Tanna Kamma compares to Yom Kippur and why the other compares to Rosh Hashanah — in what reasoning do they argue? Therefore it appears in truth all agree it was more fitting to compare to Rosh Hashanah than Yom Kippur; only Tanna Kamma holds on Rosh Hashanah there is mitzva to fast — if so on Rosh Hashanah they fast and sound — teruah without fasting we do not find; conversely fasting without teruah we find on Yom Kippur — therefore he holds fast and do not sound. R' Akiva holds no mitzva to fast on Rosh Hashanah — if so you find teruah without fasting — therefore sound and do not fast. One could also say: for Tanna Kamma kal va-chomer to fast on Rosh Hashanah — per him teruah is inferior to fasting — he holds fast and do not sound — kal va-chomer for Rosh Hashanah: where they do not sound they fast — where they sound, all the more they should fast. Conversely for R' Akiva one cannot learn kal va-chomer — for him fasting is inferior to teruah — if so: where they do not fast they sound — where they fast, all the more they should sound — we would sound on Yom Kippur! This is not so — on Yom Kippur since Torah is explicit that only on Yom Kippur of Jubilee they blow, ordinary Yom Kippur has no blowing; for Rabbanan no explicit verse that one does not fast on Rosh Hashanah — therefore properly learn kal va-chomer. Since we hold like Tanna Kamma there, it is more reasonable like those who hold mitzva to fast on Rosh Hashanah — requires study. Some bring proof from Ezra and Elisha — in my opinion no proof, as known fasting is in place of sacrifice; in Temple times with sacrifices certainly no obligation to fast by Torah law; therefore by reasoning fitting to eat and rejoice as Ezra said. For us without Temple — one could say mitzva to fast in place of sacrifice; reject above proofs. With this explain Kohelet "rejoice in youth" — "know that for all these Heaven will bring you to judgment" — refers to Rosh Hashanah: only through joy and confidence they become similar to Him may He be blessed and fit to come with Him in judgment. Midrash: when Heaven ascends on throne of judgment — sometimes when Israel unworthy He lowers Himself and judges below — "I dwell with the crushed"; if worthy He elevates them — "I am with the crushed." This explains Rosh Hashanah 26a: one says on Rosh Hashanah as mind is settled is better; on Yom Kippur as depressed is better; other reverses. Rashi: depressed better — "My eyes and heart there all days"; settled better — "lift our hearts to palms." Yevamot: one says eyes downward; other heart upward — verses support each. Without confidence unfit for judgment with Him; if He comes — only kindness as Baal HaAkedah — He lowers Himself; with confidence they become like Him — fit for judgment together — He elevates. Action needed, not thought alone — bring potential to actual; likewise Abraham's trials. On Rosh Hashanah eating shows confidence as Ezra "eat fat foods" "joy of Heaven is your strength"; fasting shows no confidence. Yom Kippur of Jubilee: though they fast, fields return and slaves freed — greatest monetary loss — if no confidence they would fear loss — perforce confident — also hints confidence. Explains sugya: depressed better on RH means fasting, no confidence memory — unfit except kindness — Omnipresent lowers — "I dwell with crushed." Yom Kippur Jubilee: great confidence in freeing slaves — fit for judgment elevated — "I am with crushed" — settled mind better — "lift hearts." Other: mitzva eat RH — actual confidence deed — fit for judgment — "I am with crushed" — settled better. Other fasts: no slave release, no joy — no confidence — judgment only kindness — depressed better.
