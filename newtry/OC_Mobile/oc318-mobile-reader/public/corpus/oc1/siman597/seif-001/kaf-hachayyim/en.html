1) [Seif 1] They eat, drink, and rejoice. Meaning, although it is a day of judgment, as stated in Rosh Hashanah 16a, nevertheless, the mitzvah of "You shall rejoice on your festival" applies to it, for it too is included among the festivals, as it is written, "Sound the shofar at the new moon, at the appointed time for the day of our festival." It is likewise stated in Nechemiah 8: "Eat rich foods and drink sweet beverages, etc., for this day is holy to our Master. Do not be saddened, for the joy of Hashem is your strength." That day was Rosh Hashanah, as explained there.
