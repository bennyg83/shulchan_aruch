The Bach wrote that Maharshal did not eat fish on Rosh Hashanah because they were beloved to him; see what I wrote in siman 583. He wanted to reduce his desire in a specific matter. So too the Shelah wrote. At the end of siman 581 the Bach wrote in his name that one should not wear important garments, only white ones. It appears to me that in a place where they are not accustomed to wear white, he should not wear such important garments. In Hagahos Maimoniyos it is written, in Parashas Nitzavim, not to eat meat or drink wine on Rosh Hashanah. There are places, etc. One who fasts need not learn the entire day, for since he fasts it is called entirely for Hashem. Further, the reason that it is permitted to fast a fast for a dream is that the fast is pleasure for him; if so, it is called for yourselves. End quote, Darkei Moshe, siman 529. This second reason does not apply to one who fasts on Rosh Hashanah not because of a dream; therefore it is good to be stringent. See what I wrote in siman 527, at the end of seif 20, that according to everyone it is forbidden to cook; see there. This is unlike the Levush, who made the two matters dependent on one another, and because of this wrote, and there are those who permit a maidservant, etc.; this is not so, and he did not examine Mahariv. The wording of Hagahos Minhagim misled him; see there, for the woman he mentioned is not specifically a woman.
He need not fast. So did Rabbi Menachem Lonzano in practice, in Sefer Shtei Yados.
