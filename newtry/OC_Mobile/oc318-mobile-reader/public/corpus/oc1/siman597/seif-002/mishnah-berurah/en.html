There are places, etc. One who fasts on Rosh Hashanah should learn the entire day or engage in supplications and requests, so that it be entirely for Hashem. The later authorities wrote that although it is explained above in siman 527, seif 20, that one who fasts is forbidden to cook for others, nevertheless a woman serving in a household who is obligated to him, and the same applies to a wife who is obligated to her husband to bake and cook for him and has no one else to do it, may cook for him.
For anyone who is accustomed, etc. This means three times.
And changes his habit, etc. This refers to the day; but on the night of Rosh Hashanah it is certainly forbidden to fast according to everyone, as on other Yamim Tovim.
He only requires release, etc. See Yoreh Deah, siman 214, where it implies that a mitzvah custom that becomes like a vow is only when he became accustomed to it, or even once if at the time of doing it he intended to practice so forever. Otherwise, it does not become like a vow through doing it once.
