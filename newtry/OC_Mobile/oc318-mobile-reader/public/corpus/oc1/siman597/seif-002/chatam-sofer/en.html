(There in the Mechaber, seif 2, one who is accustomed to fast.) Marginal note: See Teshuvos Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 168, regarding one who for several years had been accustomed to fast on the second day of Rosh Hashanah because of a dream he once saw on it, and now merited old age and his eyes also hurt, whether he may borrow his fast and repay it on another day. At the end of the responsum he wrote as follows: Nevertheless it appears to me that since he did not see it on that day so strongly, and perhaps so, it is no better than what the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 288, s.k. 7, in the name of the Shelah, that on each day of Rosh Hashanah on which he was accustomed to fast, on that same day he should accept upon himself to fast two other days corresponding to it after Tishrei. First he should request release from his vow through hataras nedarim before three, with an opening of regret because he has reached old age and has pain in his eyes, etc.; therefore they may release his vow. And because of the danger of the dream, he should accept on that day to fast two weekday days in its place.
