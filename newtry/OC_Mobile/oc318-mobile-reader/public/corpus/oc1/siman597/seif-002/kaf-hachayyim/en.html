2) It is stated in the Midrash on Megillas Esther, regarding the verse, "To bring Vashti, etc.": R' Aivu said, "The atonement of the Jewish people is when the Jewish people eat, drink, and rejoice, etc.," see there. Furthermore, by eating and drinking on the 2 days of Rosh Hashanah, one will be able to fast on Tzom Gedaliah, which is an obligatory fast, as written above, siman 549, seif 1, and below, siman 602, see there. Yafeh LaLev, vol. 3, os 1. However, what was stated, "The atonement of the Jewish people, etc.," applies when they direct their intention in that eating and drinking for the sake of the mitzvah, as is known.
