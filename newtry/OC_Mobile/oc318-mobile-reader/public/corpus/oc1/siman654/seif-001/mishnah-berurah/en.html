(1) A woman may receive, etc. - Although she is not obligated in lulav, nevertheless she may move it, for she too may recite a berachah over it.
(2) And add water to it - We do not decree because of watering seeds.
(3) But she should not replace - For it is exertion to repair a utensil. Whether it is permitted to place them in water ab initio, see above, siman 336:11 and the Mishnah Berurah there. On Shabbos, it is prohibited to remove it from water, for it is muktzeh, as below in siman 658; and if he erred and removed it, it is prohibited to return it to the water [Beis Yosef in the name of Orchos Chaim].
(4) Moist and beautiful - It is a proper and good custom to place the lulav and aravah in water so they do not dry out [Beis Yosef in the name of the Mordechai].
(5) And tie it to the lulav - Bikkurei Yaakov wrote that this means he should tie it on the outside with the lulav, not like those who insert it into the binding. The Eliyah Rabbah already protested this custom, for because one constantly inserts and removes it, most of the leaves sometimes fall off. Even aside from this, one may say that it is not called a binding according to halachah, since it is not an actual knot, as he inserts and removes it. Therefore he should untie the knot completely and retie it. This is specifically on Chol HaMoed, but on Yom Tov it is prohibited to do so, for it is prohibited to tie.
