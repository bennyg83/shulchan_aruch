New. The Or Zarua wrote in siman 651: I am astonished at the common custom, that they take a new aravah every day and insert it into the binding, and because they pull it downward so that it be lower than the hadas, the aravah leaves fall off. Some tear them intentionally and do not notice that sometimes these were most of the leaves. In my opinion, it is better to bind before Yom Tov the complete aravah together with the lulav, place the lulav in water, and not take a new one every day; see there.
