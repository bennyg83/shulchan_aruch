1) [Seif 1] A woman may receive the lulav, etc. We do not say that she is handling something unfit for her use, because since it is fit for men to take, it has the status of a utensil and may be handled by everyone. Rashi, Sukkah 42a, on the Mishnah and the Gemara. This explanation follows his view, for he agrees with the Rambam that women do not recite a blessing over a time-bound positive commandment. According to Rabbeinu Tam, however, who permits them to recite a blessing, the passage is explained according to its plain meaning: It teaches that since it is fit for her personally to take and recite a blessing over, there is no prohibition of handling it.
