If he is not careful from bread etc.—we see here source is Yerushalmi Bach brings regarding tamei and pure bread and learned from this for five species and Israel—for cutter has choice; Tosafot chapter 22 end; but R' Shimon Shar commanded remove five species etc.—certainly Shar has no power to dispute Yerushalmi; simply he did not compare five species to tamei Yerushalmi mentioned and thought for five species Israel better and no choice; nevertheless not clear to Shar therefore commanded remove to extricate from doubt. Mordekhai chapter 22 wrote like Tosafot but added first view choice in five species when not careful; Bach implies if careful must bless Israel even coarse—Shar's command even when not careful for Mordekhai wrote but Shar etc.; must say first view choice Yerushalmi tamei pure also when not careful from tamei but when careful bless specifically pure; therefore Shulchan Aruch difficult wrote when not careful choice when careful remove—not like first view when careful bless Israel; and Shar in chapter 22 remove; and Bach brought in name Rabbenu Yehudah understood Rabbenu Yehudah in Mordekhai even when careful etc.
