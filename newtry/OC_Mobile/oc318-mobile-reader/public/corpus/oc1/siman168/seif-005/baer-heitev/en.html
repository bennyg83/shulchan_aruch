Whichever he wants. See Taz and Magen Avraham. If the non-Jew's bread is a clean slice and the Jew's bread is whole and coarse, one blesses on the Jew's bread, for it has two advantages. Likewise, if the Jew's bread is a clean slice and the non-Jew's bread is whole and coarse, he also blesses on the Jew's bread, for it too has two advantages. See Ateres Zekeinim.
