(Seif katan 5) From the place, etc. And also if it browns from cooking. Darkei Moshe. Meaning, the Darkei Moshe added a reason to what Maharil wrote, that there it appears like a slice.
But with a piece, etc. Nevertheless it is a slice. This is unlike here, where it is truly whole, except that on the side where it had been attached it appeared like a slice; therefore it is better to break from the other side.
