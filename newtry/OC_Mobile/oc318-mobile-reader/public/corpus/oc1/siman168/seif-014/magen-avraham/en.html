Scalded. Even if its batter is soft, if he scalded it in boiling water and afterward baked it in an oven, he recites HaMotzi. Tur, siman 454. From Rabbeinu Yerucham it implies that even if he fried it in oil and afterward baked it, he recites HaMotzi. If it was kneaded with oil, see seif 7 and what is written in seif 10.
