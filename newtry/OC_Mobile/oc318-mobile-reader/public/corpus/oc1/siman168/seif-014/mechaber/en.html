Chalut, meaning a kind of bread that is scalded in boiling water, which was afterward baked in an oven, is complete bread, and one recites HaMotzi over it. {Rama: Likewise, something made from a soft batter that was baked in an oven without liquid has the law of bread, and one recites HaMotzi and the 3 blessings of Birkat HaMazon over it. The same applies if it was baked in a pot without liquid. [Tosafos; Rosh; Ran, chapter Kol Sha'ah; Rambam, chapter 6 of Hilchot Challah; She'iltot; Tur] A small amount of liquid used merely to grease the pot so that the dough will not burn is not considered liquid. [Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 3 of Hilchot Berachos] But something made from a soft batter and fried in liquid is, according to all opinions, not bread. [Tosafos; Rosh; Ran; Rabbeinu Yonah, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin]}
