14) There: Even on Shabbos, etc. When joining the pieces on Shabbos, one must take care not to use a piece of wood that is muktzeh. Magen Avraham there; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 5; Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. He may use a utensil whose primary use is prohibited, such as a tool used for knitting stockings, or a muktzeh needle, because a utensil whose primary use is prohibited may be moved when its body or place is needed. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 4.
