(Seif katan 14) He blesses, etc. Although he reconsidered, etc., he need not recite HaMotzi. If this means actual reconsideration, then although he need not recite HaMotzi, he must at least recite Borei Minei Mezonos, since he reconsidered; see above, siman 206, in the Magen Avraham at the end of seif katan 7.
He must wash, recite al netilas yadayim, and recite HaMotzi. So wrote the Magen Avraham above, siman 158, seif katan 2, unlike the Shelah; see there.
