[14] [Levush] And so is practiced etc.—so Rama wrote; Nachalat Tzvi wrote world is lenient like first view; in Omek Berachah and Shelah wrote there-fearing person fulfills all and does not eat l'chatchila bread baked from dough mixed with honey and oil or milk or spices; if taste of mixture of fruit juice or spices is recognizable in dough—except within meal meaning he blesses motzi first on full bread or eats measure others usually establish—then he blesses motzi and birkat hamazon; and same law for matzalot kukin (so Bach wrote one does not take matzalot kukin for lechem mishneh even if water mixed in). But bread kneaded in milk with no water at all only actual milk and milk taste recognizable well—for all it is pat haba'ah b'kisnin; does not bless motzi and birkat hamazon only borei minei mezonot when eating them not within meal—unless he eats measure people usually establish on it; and all the more kreplech of Purim—until here; and so proper to practice. But what he wrote with no water at all is hard—for Hagahah wrote Rama and Shulchan Aruch dispute only when majority water; but when majority fruit juice even if minority water—for all kisnin applies. Again I saw Bach ruled even with no water at all such as plain lekuchin sold in market—there-fearing should etc.
