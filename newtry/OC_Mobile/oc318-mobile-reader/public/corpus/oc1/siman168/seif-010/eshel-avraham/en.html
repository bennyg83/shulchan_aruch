He recites HaMotzi. If one thickened fresh grape juice, mixed flour into it, then dried it in the sun and it is eaten as it is, Responsa Zera Avraham, siman 1, concludes: We are witnesses that the honey is not fit to be eaten except as an accompaniment to bread, and the flour gives it taste. One therefore recites Borei Minei Mezonos, for this is not flour added merely to bind the mixture. If he did not eat enough of it to contain a kezayis of flour within the time of kedei achilas pras, he recites afterward only Borei Nefashos. See there.
