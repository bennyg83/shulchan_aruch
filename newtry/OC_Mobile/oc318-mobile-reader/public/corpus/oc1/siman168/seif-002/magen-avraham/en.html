He may join them. The Rokeach wrote in this language: its law is like that of a whole loaf, even on Shabbos, as we say in Eruvin, etc. That is, even though on Shabbos one requires whole lechem mishneh, this is called whole. It is possible that on a weekday one need not join them at all, and so is the practice. When joining them on Shabbos, one should be careful not to take wood that is muktzeh.
