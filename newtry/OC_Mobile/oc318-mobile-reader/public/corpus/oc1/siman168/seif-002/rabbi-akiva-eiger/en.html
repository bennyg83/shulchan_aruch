In Even HaEzer s.v. “This provokes a further difficulty,” at the end of the passage: “Therefore, he recites afterward the three blessings and HaMotzi, and this is clear.” This still does not suffice. If we determine that genuine bread obligates Bircas HaMazon by Torah law whenever one is satiated from it, then even regarding pas haba’ah bekisnin, why does he not recite Bircas HaMazon in such a case? After all, it is genuine bread regarding matzah and challah. Necessarily, one must say either that even regarding genuine bread, the Torah’s obligation of “you shall eat and be satisfied” refers to a quantity from which people ordinarily become satisfied, or that by Torah law one fulfills the obligation with the abridged threefold blessing, since the number of blessings is not of Torah origin. See Beit Yosef, siman 10, and Magen Avraham there. Accordingly, in a case of doubt concerning pas haba’ah bekisnin, the uncertainty is only Rabbinic. Examine this.
