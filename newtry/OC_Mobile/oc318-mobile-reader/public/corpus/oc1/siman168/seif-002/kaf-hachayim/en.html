(2) There he blesses on the whole etc.—one who took a slice of bread to bless on it and meanwhile they brought him a whole loaf—he places the slice inside the whole and blesses; and if he already blessed on the slice his hands cut the slice—Mahari Molcho responsum 10 siman 84 Berachot Yosef note 1 Shaarei Teshuvah note 1; and it appears what he wrote place slice inside whole and bless means if he already began to bless on the slice; but if he had not yet begun to bless and they brought him a whole loaf—he blesses on the whole alone.
