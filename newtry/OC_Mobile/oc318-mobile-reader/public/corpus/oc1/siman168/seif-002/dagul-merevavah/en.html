In my humble opinion, one should not recite a blessing during the meal over the items mentioned in seif 7. Regarding each one there is a dispute whether it is pas haba b'kisnin. Because this is a doubt concerning a Rabbinic law, we rule leniently. When one eats them outside a meal, the leniency is that he need not recite HaMotzi and Bircas HaMazon, but may suffice with Borei Minei Mezonos and the abbreviated three-faceted blessing. During a meal, however, the leniency works in the opposite direction: perhaps this is not pas haba b'kisnin but ordinary bread, and it is covered by the HaMotzi he recited over the bread. One should therefore recite a separate blessing during the meal only over something that all opinions regard as pas haba b'kisnin. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 18.
