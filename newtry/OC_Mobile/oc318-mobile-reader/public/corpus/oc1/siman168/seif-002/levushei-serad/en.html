Magen Avraham, seif katan 1 For he concludes, “however.” After the dispute of Rav Huna and Rabbi Yochanan, it concludes: “However, regarding a piece of wheat bread and a whole loaf of barley bread, all agree that he recites the blessing over the piece,” etc. Accordingly, the case concerns large pieces, and ordinary wheat bread is cleaner than barley bread. Nevertheless, wheat takes precedence specifically because of, etc.
