Although he makes it his fixed meal, etc. This is the view of the Rosh, who disagrees with the Raavad. According to the Raavad, even a small amount suffices once he makes it his fixed meal, for we follow his personal practice. See the Rosh there. Nevertheless, it appears clear that an elderly person or a child, whose eating is naturally limited, must recite HaMotzi and Bircas HaMazon if he ate the amount upon which people of his type ordinarily establish a meal, since all people of that class are satisfied by such an amount. This is similar to what I wrote below in siman 184 in Biur Halachah in the name of Radbaz; see there.
