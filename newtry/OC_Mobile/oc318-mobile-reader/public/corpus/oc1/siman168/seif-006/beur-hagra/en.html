Seif 6: And afterward, etc. As stated there, 44a: “Whoever,” etc. See Tosafos 41b, s.v. ella, etc.; and as written in the Yerushalmi and cited by Tosafos in Sukkah 26b, s.v. velo, etc., and by the Rosh in Berachos, chapter 6, siman 16. See there carefully.
And if he ate, etc. There: “That case is different,” etc.
That others, etc. There. See Rashi there, s.v. amar Rav Nachman, “while,” etc.
And if initially, etc. One matter does not depend on the other, as stated in the Yerushalmi and cited by Tosafos in chapter 3 of Sukkah and by the Rosh in Berachos, chapter 6, siman 16: “They challenged: But less than a kezayis,” etc.; “It was needed for other species,” etc.
And if he ate, etc. So too in the Rif: “Rav Huna ate bread that came with pockets,” etc.; “Rather, Rav Nachman said: Anything over which others establish a meal,” etc.; and anything over which others do not establish a meal because of the meal does not require a blessing afterward.
