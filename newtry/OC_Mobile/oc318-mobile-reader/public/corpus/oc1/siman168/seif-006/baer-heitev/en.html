On it. It appears to me that if he fixed his meal on it, even though he ate meat and other foods with it, and had he eaten it alone he would not have been satiated from it, nevertheless he recites HaMotzi and the three blessings. If he ate it alone, we require that he eat so much that others are accustomed to be satiated from it alone. If he is satiated from it, and others are also accustomed to be satiated from it when they eat something with it, then even if he ate it alone he recites HaMotzi, since he was satiated from it. Magen Avraham. See what is written on this in Sefer Perach Shushan, Orach Chaim, klal 1, siman 3, and see Yad Aharon.
Initially. This implies that even if he reconsidered in the middle and decided to eat more, he need not recite HaMotzi, since the remaining amount does not have the required measure. So wrote Maharshal, part 1, siman 163. It is obvious that if, after eating a little, he reconsidered and decided to eat an amount that constitutes a fixed meal, he must wash and recite HaMotzi. Magen Avraham.
