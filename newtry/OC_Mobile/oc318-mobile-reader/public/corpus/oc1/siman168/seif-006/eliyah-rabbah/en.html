On the barley bread, etc. If the barley bread is a piece, even if it is smaller than a whole spelt loaf, its law is also like a piece of wheat bread, that he places the piece beneath the whole loaf, according to Nachalas Tzvi. This implies that if the whole loaf is rye, he blesses on the piece of barley bread; this requires study. See above, s.k. 2.
