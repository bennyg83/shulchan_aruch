And afterward he ate, etc. Responsa Maharshal, siman 173, writes that if he subsequently decides to eat an amount that independently constitutes the requisite measure, without combining the first eating, he recites HaMotzi. Or Chadash writes that the amount upon which people ordinarily establish a meal is three or four eggs. This requires study whether he means when eating the bread alone or together with other foods. I saw that Magen Avraham, seif katan 13, writes: if one eats it alone, he must eat the amount from which people ordinarily become satiated when eating it alone. If he eats it with meat and other foods, it suffices to eat the amount from which people ordinarily become satiated when eating it with accompaniments. If he eats that amount alone and becomes satiated, he recites HaMotzi, because others ordinarily become satiated from that quantity when eating it with accompaniments.
