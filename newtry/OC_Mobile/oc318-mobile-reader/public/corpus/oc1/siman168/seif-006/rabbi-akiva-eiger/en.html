Seif 6. And if he ate from it the quantity that others. Maharam ben Chaviv, in his work Tosafos Yom HaKippurim, 79b, Tosafos s.v. minei targima, writes that this is the quantity of a peras, which is the measure of a meal regarding an eruv, namely three or four egg-volumes. See below, siman 368, seif 6. Responsa Perach Shoshan, siman 4, writes likewise. Gan HaMelech, siman 36, does not agree.
Magen Avraham, seif katan 31. Therefore, so long as it has. His words require study, for the wording of the Shulchan Aruch and his own words do not make the law depend at all upon the appearance of bread, etc.
