Seif katan 5 As written at the beginning of siman 167, from the top and the bottom, which are also crust. There is no obligation to break bread specifically from a place surrounded entirely by crust. Accordingly, here too he could break it from the place where it had been attached. Maharil therefore gives the reason that it appears like a broken loaf, teaching that one should not break it even from the side. It follows that from an actually broken loaf, one may properly cut from the side.
