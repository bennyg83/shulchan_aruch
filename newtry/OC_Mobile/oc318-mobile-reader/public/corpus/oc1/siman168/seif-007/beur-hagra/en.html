Seif 7: Some explain, etc. Rabbeinu Yonah in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel. See Beer HaGolah.
And some say, etc. This is how Rambam explains it.
And it is, etc. This is how Beit Yosef explains his words, since he says that spices were mixed into it. Therefore, in the Shulchan Aruch he likewise writes regarding honey, oil, and milk that they were mixed in, even though Rambam writes that it was kneaded with them. Rama in the gloss disagrees with him. Rashi likewise explains on 41b: “many spices,” etc. See Magen Avraham. This is the primary view.
And some explain that it is, etc. As it is written, “And all their provisions of bread were dry; it was moldy,” whose Targum is, “it was dry, it was kisnin.” However, the Tosefta in chapter 6 implies the explanation of the first authorities, for it teaches: If they brought before him varieties of dessert, he recites over them “Who creates varieties of kisnin.” It is cited in the Aruch there. The Rosh, however, in chapter 2 of Sukkah has the reading, “Who creates varieties of nourishment.”
