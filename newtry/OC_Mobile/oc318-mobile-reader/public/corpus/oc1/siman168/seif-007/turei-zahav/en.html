(1) Y.M. Fat 20, which fills K. F. Sharshi interprets the following Fat in Kisnin, we were from the side of the filling because the dough itself is soaked in water, but it is filled with sugar or honey or nuts. Including this, what we make on Purim is a wide, double dough and fills it with sesame seeds, and there is no difference between filling with fruit or meat and fish, and Amash in the C. is SI, and some say that Rambam DSL Dakhisinin is from the pulp itself that has been soaked in honey or spice and we will be satisfied with I Nima, rather, that has been soaked in a lot of honey and a small amount of water. But if there is a small amount of honey, it is void for the majority of water, and it is a complete mouthful or a dilemma, even with a mixture of a little honey, as long as the taste in the paste is evident in cases of Kisnin. And he wrote that this seems to be the main thing. And he wrote that Nanin Halachh since Damidi d'rabbanan is to be lightened according to everyone's words, as well as pockets that fill them with honey from almonds between a paste in which fruit water or spices have been mixed. This is why he said that she is a voice, isn't it if he doesn't bless her with a blessing that she deserves either way, it is a bad thing because he violates the law of not carrying. And it seems to me that it is not too difficult to bless the creator of all kinds of food, even the mass of the food is exempt, except that due to the importance of the mouthful, the one who spends it has decreed three blessings on it, because it is not clear that there is any importance there, and it is not at all a sinin.
