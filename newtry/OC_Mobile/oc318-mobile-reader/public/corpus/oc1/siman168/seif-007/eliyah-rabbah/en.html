On the white bread, etc. So wrote the Beis Yosef from his own reasoning, and I found the same in the Agudah, chapter Keitzad Mevarchin. It seems to me that this applies even if the second is larger than the white one. The Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah wrote that if there is a little oil in the white bread, the practice is to remove it from the table and bless on other complete bread, even if it is not as nice as it, end quote. This implies that this is so even if they are equal in size.
