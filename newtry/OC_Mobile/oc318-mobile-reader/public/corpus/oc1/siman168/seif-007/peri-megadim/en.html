Bach was stringent that one should not put even a small amount of fat into the loaves used for lechem mishneh, because one should take into account the Mechaber's view that HaMotzi and Bircas HaMazon are not recited over such bread. Bread to which saffron is added, however, is not included in this concern, and according to all opinions it is full-fledged bread. Likewise, people commonly add a small amount of raisins. In any event, each raisin remains a separate substance and is not blended into the dough, so the loaf is valid for lechem mishneh.
Know that if full-fledged bread and bread kneaded with fat are both on the table, even if he intends to establish his meal upon the latter, he should nevertheless recite HaMotzi over the full-fledged bread. See Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 2, in the name of Knesses HaGedolah.
