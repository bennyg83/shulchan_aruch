The clean bread. Even if the coarse bread is larger than the clean bread. The Beit Yosef wrote that even if the one breaking bread says that clean bread is not beloved to him, his view is disregarded in relation to all people. The Magen Avraham challenged this from siman 211, see there, and the Yad Aharon resolves it.
More. If the second loaf is larger than this one, the matter requires doubt. Magen Avraham.
