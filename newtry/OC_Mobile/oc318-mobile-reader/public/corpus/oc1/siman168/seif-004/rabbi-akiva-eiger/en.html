Magen Avraham, seif katan 16. Regarding matzah, the same applies regarding. This is not difficult at all. Specifically something that is not considered bread at all regarding HaMotzi, such as rice, is not bread regarding matzah. Pas haba’ah bekisnin, however, over which one recites HaMotzi when establishing a meal upon it, is genuine bread, and one fulfills the mitzvah of matzah with it, as stated in Berachos 35a. I later saw that this is included in the words of Even HaEzer, s.v. dehashta.
