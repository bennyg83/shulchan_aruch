From one of the seven species. Although spelt is also considered among the seven species, for it is a type of wheat, as written in the Tur, siman 208, nevertheless it is not stated explicitly in the verse of the seven species. Therefore barley is considered more significant than it.
Even though the spelt is finer. And it is dear to him, for the advantage of one of the seven species is greater, as below in siman 211, and there it is explained that some say that what is dear takes precedence. Magen Avraham. According to this, this law is found only when both are equal in his eyes. Regarding spelt and rye, since neither is explicit in the verse, spelt takes precedence, for it is a type of wheat. Regarding rye and oats, since both are types of barley, rye takes precedence, for it is dearer.
Clean bread and coarse bread. And both are of one species. Tur. For if the coarse bread is more significant in its species than the clean one, such as wheat relative to barley, or barley relative to spelt, it takes precedence.
Over the clean one. Even though the coarse one is larger than it, for the advantage of cleanliness is greater. This speaks where both are equal, either both whole or both sliced, for if only the coarse one is whole, it precedes the clean sliced one, as above in seif 1. This is the rule from these seifim: the advantage of the seven species precedes everything; after it comes the advantage of whole; after it the advantage of clean; and after it the advantage of large. Chayei Adam. Regarding the advantage of what is dear, see siman 211.
Over the white one. Even if the second is larger than it, unless it is dearer to him.
