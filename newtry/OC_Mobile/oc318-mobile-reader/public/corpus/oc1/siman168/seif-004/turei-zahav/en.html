Since it is one of the seven species. This appears at first to imply that spelt is not one of the seven species. This is difficult, for in siman 208 the Tur explicitly writes that spelt is included within wheat. It appears to me that at the beginning of Kilayim it states: “Wheat and zunin,” and so forth. Rash writes there: “This is difficult, for here it appears that spelt and wheat are kilayim with one another, whereas in Menachos it states that spelt is a species of wheat.” He answers that the latter statement concerns fulfilling the mitzvah of matzah, and so forth. This shows that distinctions exist in this regard. It is therefore correct to say here that spelt is not included among the seven species for all purposes in the same way as barley. Nevertheless, because for some matters it is included with wheat, the Tur writes in siman 208 that it is significant regarding Hamotzi. See also the end of siman 211.
