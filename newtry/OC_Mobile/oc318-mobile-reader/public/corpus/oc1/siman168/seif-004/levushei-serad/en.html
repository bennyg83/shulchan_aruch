There One of them is rye. If the whole loaf is rye, he need not join it to the piece, but recites the blessing over the piece alone. All the more so, if the piece is rye, he need not join it to the whole loaf, but recites the blessing over the whole loaf alone. Rav Shalman’s ruling applies specifically to barley, which is important because it is explicitly mentioned in Scripture, as Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 6.
