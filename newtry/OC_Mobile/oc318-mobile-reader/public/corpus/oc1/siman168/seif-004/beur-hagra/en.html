Seif 4: Bread, etc. In the Yerushalmi there, Rabbi Yosi said in the name of Rabbi Zeira that this follows Rabbi Yehudah, who says that if one of them is from the seven species, he recites the blessing over it. See below, siman 211, seifim 1 and 2.
Fine bread, etc. The Tosefta, cited by Tosafos and the other poskim, states: One recites the blessing over grain that is of the superior kind. How so? If there is a whole loaf of fine bread and a whole loaf belonging to the homeowner, he recites the blessing over the whole fine loaf.
And if, etc. As stated above, because it is of the superior kind. All this is likewise stated in the Yerushalmi.
