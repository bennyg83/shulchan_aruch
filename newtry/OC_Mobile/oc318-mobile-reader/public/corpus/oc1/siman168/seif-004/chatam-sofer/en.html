There, seif katan 4. Note: See Responsa Chasam Sofer, Orach Chayim, siman 46. There he was asked about a Shabbos cake that broke into several pieces, whether it may be sewn together with several splinters in order to fulfill breaking bread over two loaves on Shabbos, and whether there is a distinction between one slice having broken off and several pieces requiring several joins. In his responsum he concludes: “In any event, sewing one piece to another, and another piece to that one, until an entire loaf is completed, is something we have not found. It resembles the rule in Shabbos 96b that we do not say, ‘Bring one potsherd and another potsherd.’ Since the basic law itself is doubtful in my humble opinion, apply the principle that we should not add to it. One should therefore not join many pieces, but should endeavor to obtain another whole loaf.”
