Since it is one of the seven species. It appears to me that according to the Rambam, beloved takes precedence, as written in siman 211. Although the Tur in siman 208 wrote that spelt is one of the seven species because it is included in wheat, and grain is included in barley, nevertheless barley is preferable because it is written explicitly in the verse. Agudah.
The clean bread. It appears to me that this applies even if the coarse bread is larger than the clean bread, for the advantage of cleanness is mentioned explicitly in the Yerushalmi, unlike the advantage of size. The Beit Yosef wrote that even if the one breaking bread says that clean bread is not beloved to him, his view is disregarded. This requires study, for in siman 211 it does not imply so, but rather that we follow his own preference. Perhaps clean bread is preferable for blessing even when it is not beloved to him, and this requires study.
