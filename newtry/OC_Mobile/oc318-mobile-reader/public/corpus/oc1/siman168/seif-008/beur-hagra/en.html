Seif 8: Small rolls, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. lachmaniyos, etc.
And if he ate, etc. There, 41b. See Tosafos there, s.v. ella, etc. What they wrote, that it is because it is a small amount, applies according to Rav Sheshes. According to Rav Huna and Rav Nachman, however, this is unnecessary. We also learn that they require a blessing beforehand, for on this they do not disagree.
