(א) Obliyash—I did not know what it is but appears baked without rolling—mixes dough with water thick kneading; Rashi refers to this what Gemara says haminiyot bless borei minei mezonot and if establishes blesses hamotzi; Tosafot reject this is full bread rather refers to soft thin kneading—this is what Shulchan Aruch mentions niblach; wrote in Amek Berachah this is nalysinke—mix flour water in pot like porridge pour on wide vegetable leaves bake in oven with leaves; so Maharshal; challenged Mordekhai contradicts himself beginning chapter 22 R' Yehuda practiced nilesh even on Purim establishing meal—not eat except within meal lest establishment not help; later same chapter R' Yehuda haminiyot means nilesh and Gemara says in haminiyot establishment helps; answered two types nilesh; and it appears so—nilesh is exactly that type R' Yehuda bakes in two layers; heard from one who knows language; nalysinke not in this category but in trita seif 15—for that type not thin at all—even if establishes meal borei minei mezonot as below trita; Tur wrote soft and very thin—that is R' Yehuda type also very thin.

(ב) And if he eats them within the meal etc.—this law applies also to pat haba'ah b'kisnin mentioned before and as Bach wrote explicitly in name Shelah; Tur and Shulchan Aruch wrote here regarding haminiyot—for stringency also haminiyot more reasonable to be due to meal than pat kisnin—for there is bread on them nevertheless bread blessing does not exempt first blessing before them—a fortiori pat kisnin; in Mordekhai chapter 22 we find opposite explanation wrote in name R' Meir in haminiyot derives a fortiori from kisnin requires blessing before them within meal; wrote there a fortiori is from kisnin that if establishes blesses hamotzi nevertheless bread does not exempt etc.—appears wonder for also regarding haminiyot which are nilesh Gemara says if establishes meal blesses hamotzi; appears explanation regarding nilesh was doubt if that is meaning of haminiyot or not—therefore derives a fortiori from kisnin that per his view establishment helps; and what resolved in kisnin more than haminiyot—since Rif mentioned borei minei mezonot law for kisnin and derives haminiyot from them; I will quote Rif: conclusion where eats them as kisnin initially blesses borei minei mezonot and at end nothing; where eats as establishment blesses hamotzi and three blessings—for R' Yehuda taught haminiyot bless hamotzi and we establish when establishes on them; explanation: if ate little not way of establishing meal—only borei minei mezonot; if established—hamotzi; and Mordekhai derives a fortiori from kisnin to haminiyot within meal requires blessing before them as above.
