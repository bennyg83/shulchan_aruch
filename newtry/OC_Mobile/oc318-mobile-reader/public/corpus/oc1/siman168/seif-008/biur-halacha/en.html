Require berachah before them—see Mishnah Berurah who wrote in name of Magen Avraham that same for all pat haba'ah b'kisnin explained in seif 7; and truly as his words are explained in many early authorities; and all this for essence of law—but for us who are doubtful in pat haba'ah b'kisnin what it is—even though Mechaber ruled to be lenient in all, that is because rabbinic doubt is lenient and we do not require hamotzi and birkat hamazon like other bread; unlike in our matter to require berachah within meal—for we hold it as kisnin bread, there is no reason that on contrary rabbinic doubt is lenient and perhaps this species is full bread and one fulfills with hamotzi—therefore one does not bless on kisnin bread within meal unless it is kisnin bread for all (meaning filled and also kneaded in honey and the like and it is thin and dry)—so Gemara and Gra; and similarly wrote in chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger and Chayei Adam; and per what we wrote above in name of Maamar Mordekhai that perhaps they concede to each other—no great difficulty; and for practical law it seems one who eats matter kneaded in honey and egg water and the like within meal such as lekach and kikhlach or eats dry k'eivin—he should not bless within meal even if eating for dessert; and if he eats matters filled with fruits (and as Mechaber depicted in first form)—one who blesses on them within meal did not lose, for it implies majority of poskim hold so that this is pat haba'ah b'kisnin. And in Chayei Adam it implies even for lekach and kikhlach l'chatchila one should intend at time of hamotzi etc.
