Oblias. It appears that it is baked without rolling; rather, flour is mixed with water into a thick dough. Taz.
Niblash. This is what they call nalesniki, where one mixes flour with water in a pot like porridge, pours the batter onto broad vegetable leaves, and they are baked in the oven together with the leaves.
His meal. The measure of a fixed meal is three or four eggs. See Sefer Perach Shushan, klal 1, siman 4, and see Yad Aharon. See above seif katan 8, what he wrote there in the name of the Magen Avraham. The Taz concluded that one should not establish a meal and recite HaMotzi on all types whose batter is soft and that are thin; but for the type called nalesniki, fixing a meal is effective to recite HaMotzi, since they are not thin. See there.
The meal. Meaning, they are not eaten for satiation but for pleasure or dessert, to ease the heaviness of the food.
Before them. Accordingly, all types of bread mentioned in seif 7 require a blessing before them within the meal when eaten as dessert. What we rule, that if one blessed on bread he exempted the parperes, refers to when they come to stimulate the appetite. The general principle is: anything eaten for nourishment and as a cooked dish, such as apple fladen and povidel fladen, always comes because of the meal and does not require a blessing within the meal, for the fruit has become a cooked dish inside the filling and the flour is primary. One should not be stringent to take some apples from inside and bless Borei Pri HaEtz on them, for that would be a blessing in vain, since the flour is primary; see siman 177. But something that comes for dessert or pleasure, such as thin cakes or sweets mentioned in seif 7, requires a blessing and the bread does not exempt it, unless they come to stimulate the appetite. Magen Avraham. On aflatkes and hoizen blazen called fritlach, one must recite Borei Minei Mezonos within the meal. Taz.
The confection. This means, for example, what they fry on Purim, nougat on doughs. One should not bless on that dough as long as he eats it with the nougat, but if he eats the dough by itself he recites Borei Minei Mezonos, for at the time of baking it was not baked as bread. See above seif katan 11. Taz.
