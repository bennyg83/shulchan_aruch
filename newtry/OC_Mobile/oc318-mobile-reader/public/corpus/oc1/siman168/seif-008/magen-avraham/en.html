Complete bread. For it is not made like those cakes written in seif 7, which are only for dessert.
And very thin. Such as those called in Russia nalesniki. Shelah. But if they are not so soft, one recites HaMotzi, as written in seif 14 in the gloss.
Not because of the meal. Meaning, they are not eaten for satiation but for pleasure, to ease the heaviness of the food. Birchos Maharam.
Require a blessing. So wrote Rabbeinu Yerucham, the Tur, and the Rosh. It appears to me they derived this from what the Gemara states: there is nothing that requires a blessing before it and not after it except pas haba'ah b'kisnin alone. They hold that this refers to within a meal for dessert, and small loaves are included. Accordingly, all types of bread mentioned in seif 7 require a blessing within the meal when eaten as dessert. What Rabbeinu Yonah wrote on the Mishnah, that one who blessed on bread exempted parperes, meaning niblash and fruit and the like, and small loaves are niblash, showing that bread exempts them, refers to when they come to stimulate the appetite, for fruit must be in that case, since otherwise bread does not exempt them, as written in siman 177. The general rule is: anything eaten for nourishment and as a cooked dish, like cooked foods called apple fladen and povidel fladen, always comes because of the meal and one need not bless on them within the meal, for the fruit has become a cooked dish inside the filling and the flour is primary. One should not be stringent to take some apples from inside and bless Borei Pri HaEtz, for that is a blessing in vain, since the flour is primary. See what I wrote in siman 177. Shelah. But something that comes as dessert, such as thin cakes, dry cakes, or sweets mentioned in seif 7, requires a blessing and the bread does not exempt it, similar to small loaves. See the gloss in seif 13 and see Beit Yosef, siman 177, that the Rashba has another approach, but we do not rule like him.
Thin wafers. They do not intend to eat the wafers; rather, they make the wafers so that the confection will not soil the hands.
