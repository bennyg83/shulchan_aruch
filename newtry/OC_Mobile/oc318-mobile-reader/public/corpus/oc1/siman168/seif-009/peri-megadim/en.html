Wafers. See Taz and Berachos 42a regarding small rolls. Rashi explains these as wafers, whereas dough kneaded with oil or the like does not become subject to HaMotzi and Bircas HaMazon even if one establishes a meal upon it; one recites only Borei Minei Mezonos and the abridged blessing afterward. According to Tosafos there, s.v. lachmaniyos, wafers are full-fledged bread, while dough kneaded with oil requires establishment of a meal. The Shulchan Aruch rules in accordance with Tosafos. This is why Rabbi Yochanan did not wish to rely upon a lenient ruling, for perhaps the halachah follows Rashi, according to whom establishment of a meal does not help for kneaded dough. Consequently, regarding wafers too he was stringent that one should eat them only when establishing a meal, and should not eat them without such establishment. Regarding thin pancakes, Taz first writes that their law resembles terisa in seif 15, for which establishment of a meal does not help, but at the end he appears to retract and hold that establishment does help. This may be reconciled, but the matter requires study.
