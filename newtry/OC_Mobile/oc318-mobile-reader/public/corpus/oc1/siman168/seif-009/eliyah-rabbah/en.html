[9] He blesses on whichever etc.—Bach here and in Yoreh De'ah siman 112 challenged Shulchan Aruch and Levush, for it is clear in Mordekhai chapter Keitzad Mevarchin that one who is not careful—Sherira commanded to remove white bread; but one who is careful need not remove; and in Nachalat Tzvi he added wonder that he saw explicitly in Terumat HaDeshen siman 32. And to my humble view: explain Mordekhai's words—for always Sherira refers to one who is careful, and so it appears—for it implies Sherira commanded for himself and presumably he was careful from pat shel kutim; and what Mordekhai brought on one who is not careful means he brings proof from careful to not careful—for since when careful he commanded to remove, if so same law for not careful he blesses on whichever he wishes; if he wishes to bless on kosher bread he removes white bread from table—but truly he may bless on white bread since he is not careful; and so I explain Tosafot there 39 s.v. aval etc.; and clear proof—for Tosafot and all poskim bring proof for this law from Tosefta on whichever he wishes he blesses—and if so how does Sherira dispute Tosefta; and if he disputes the proof he should have Tosafot or some posek explain his reason—rather perforce they do not dispute and for all one blesses on whichever when not careful; and so in Agudah it implies Sherira refers to one who is careful, see there; and see what I wrote end of note 13. For law: one who is careful always removes etc.
