First. See Baer Heitev. See Birkei Yosef, who writes that his grandfather cited an early manuscript work holding that regarding pas haba b'kisnin, if one establishes his meal upon it he recites HaMotzi and the three blessings of Bircas HaMazon, and that a Shabbos meal establishes eating just as Shabbos establishes produce for tithing. Maharai Chagiz, in Etz Chayim on Mishnah, chapter 4 of Maasros, was uncertain. Ginass Veradim, principle 2, siman 11, however, writes plainly that there is no distinction between Shabbos and a weekday. Birkei Yosef agrees, and writes that this is the accepted practice.
