Flour and water mixed in it—see Magen Avraham and we copied it in Mishnah Berurah. And know that per explained reason of this law in Rambam, ostensibly we need not Magen Avraham's distinction; and even if not very soft, nevertheless whoever did not establish does not bless hamotzi on it—for Rambam wrote chapter 3: dough baked on ground as Arabs of deserts make (this explains kova de'ara stated in Talmud; and Shulchan Aruch followed language of Rosh and Tur—and it is the same)—since it has no form of bread he blesses borei minei mezonot; and if he established etc.—behold main reason is because it has no form of bread; and in Levush he wrote reason because this is not way of kneading nor way of baking bread (meaning no way to knead and bake in kova de'ara); and per this we also need not Magen Avraham's distinction—and this requires study for law.
