(Seif katan 15) Some explain bread, etc. Nevertheless, a grain species, etc., is how it should read, as written in siman 208, seif 2 and seif 3.
And if he eats only the fruit and not the dough, etc. As written in siman 177, one who eats fruit within a meal without bread must recite a blessing before it. If he eats the dough alone without the fruit, the Taz wrote that he recites Borei Minei Mezonos, for we follow the time of baking, and at the time of baking it was filled with fruit. The same applies to pas haba'ah b'kisnin.
