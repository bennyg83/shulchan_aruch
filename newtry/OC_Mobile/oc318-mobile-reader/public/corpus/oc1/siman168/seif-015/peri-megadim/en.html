In wine. See Taz. The reason is not that the bread became red and is therefore regarded as pas haba'ah b'kisnin, for if so the presence of a kezayis would not help. Rather, where there is no kezayis, the wine changes the appearance of bread. Taz mentions pas haba'ah b'kisnin only as an example: just as there, although the food is bread, one recites Borei Minei Mezonos, so too here.
