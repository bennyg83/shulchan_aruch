Seif 16: and so bread etc.—this requires study why he did not distinguish between k'eiva and limudin as Tur and Rif, and as Gemara teaches regarding challah—and from there we learn regarding hamotzi; for all the above regarding challah was stated there; and so Rosh distinguishes in laws of challah sections 2 and 3; and so Rosh and Shulchan Aruch HaRav; and so Yoreh De'ah siman 329 section 7; and it seems Shulchan Aruch holds distinction applies only to challah because it is confused with other bread or a decree lest one change his mind—not so for hamotzi.
