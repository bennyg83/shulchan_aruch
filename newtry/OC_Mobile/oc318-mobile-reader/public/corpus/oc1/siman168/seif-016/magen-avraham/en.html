And it is bread. Meaning, dough. So too in Yoreh Deah, siman 329, seif 5, for bread baked on a spit is complete bread, as written in seif 13 in the gloss.
Bread made for kutach. Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote it together with trisa in one clause; see what I wrote in seif 15. That which he ruled in Yoreh Deah, siman 329, that if he made it like cakes it is obligated in challah, is a stringency there; but here he ruled leniently. See what I wrote in seif 7. Nevertheless, it requires study, for if he is satiated from it, the matter is according to the Torah; see what I wrote in seif 13.
