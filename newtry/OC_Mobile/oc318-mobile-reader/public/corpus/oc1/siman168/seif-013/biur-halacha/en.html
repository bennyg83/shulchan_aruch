(ד) And all this etc.—see Mishnah Berurah; and I omitted Taz's view who holds even for Yesh Cholkim fried is included in pat haba'ah b'kisnin and does not bless hamotzi when not established—for many achronim dispute him (see Chemdat Moshe, Nehar Shalom, Maamar Mordekhai, Even HaEzer); and they proved for Yesh Cholkim one blesses hamotzi even when not established. And this is only for Yesh Cholkim themselves; but for law many achronim agreed to Taz (see Even HaEzer, Beit Meir, Gra) that through frying finally in oil it becomes pat haba'ah b'kisnin as if kneaded in oil; and in our matter when not established on them one blesses for all borei minei mezonot and al hamichyah; and when established depends on two views—for first view borei minei mezonot and al hamichyah—for frying nullified from kisnin bread category; for Yesh Cholkim hamotzi and birkat hamazon. Practical difference from Taz's words also generally—even for first view such as kneaded thick at night and afterward fried in oil and baked in oven—for it has name pat haba'ah b'kisnin and does not bless hamotzi and birkat hamazon only when established on them. And to my view it seems clear that for first view even Rama holds frying in honey is considered like kneaded in honey; practical difference from what we wrote where he baked finally—for there kisnin bread applies; but per Yesh Cholkim who follow beginning we cannot consider it kisnin bread to bless borei minei mezonot when not established etc.
