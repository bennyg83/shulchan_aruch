Of bread. Meaning, for example, he baked it in a pan. Even so, it is not bread. This is not comparable to what is written in seif 10, for there it was bread initially; therefore, as long as it has the form of bread, it does not leave the category of bread. See seif 14 in the gloss, that a small amount of liquid is not called liquid.

To be lenient. Meaning that one does not recite HaMotzi, but only Borei Minei Mezonos. Nevertheless, it appears to me that if he was satiated from it, it is a doubt according to the Torah, and he must recite Birkas HaMazon out of doubt, as written in siman 184, seif 4. Magen Avraham.
And kreplach. Meaning, they are filled with meat, for if they are filled with types of fruit they are kisnin, as written in seif 7, even if baked in an oven. Magen Avraham. The Taz wrote that they are always kisnin, even when filled with meat; see there, and see what I wrote in seif katan 4.
