Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. He need not set it aside. Meaning, although rye is a species of barley, nevertheless, since it is not written explicitly in the verse, it is not considered one of the seven species, as below in seif 4 regarding spelt and barley and as I write below in seif katan 6. This is all the more so regarding rye, which is a species of barley, in comparison with actual wheat.
