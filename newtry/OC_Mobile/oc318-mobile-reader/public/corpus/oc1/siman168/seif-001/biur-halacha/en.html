He sets aside, etc. — see Magen Avraham who brought in the name of Rashal that if another reclines with him he blesses one for completion and one for the slice, and Magen Avraham found this compromise very difficult, see there; and some later authorities resolve Magen Avraham's difficulty with difficulty; and in Bigdei Yesha I saw that even Rashal did not say he blesses only for himself but the host blesses on the wheat slice and gives to the one reclining with him with his blessing; and in this way it is better that the guest hold the completion at the time of the berachah so it is more apparent, and this also has an advantage over placing both together where importance of each is not so apparent, see there — and this is correct.
