Seif 6—and if he ate from it measure others establish. Maharam ibn Chaviv in his Tosafot Yom Kippur on 9a s.v. minei targema wrote this is measure peras which is meal measure for eruv three or four eggs see below siman 368:66; and so Responsa Perach Shoshan siman 4 and Gan HaMelech 36 did not hold so.
