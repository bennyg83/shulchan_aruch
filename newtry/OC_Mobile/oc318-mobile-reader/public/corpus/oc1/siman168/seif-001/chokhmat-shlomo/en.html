Seif 6—pat haba'ah b'kisnin one blesses borei minei mezonot etc. Footnote: I saw in Peri Megadim he was in doubt in law of pat haba'ah b'kisnin if one ate satiety measure whether liable to bless from Torah or only rabbinically. Ostensibly one can resolve this doubt from Talmud first chapter of Arakhin daf 4 where it says all are liable in zimmun—priests, Levites, Israelites—and challenges obvious, it is needed when they ate kodashim, lest one say Merciful One said and they shall eat those through whom atonement was made and behold atonement was made—teaches us and you shall eat and be satisfied Merciful One said and behold they are there—end Talmud; and it is hard: bread we do not find in sacrifice except in menachot—for in meat birkat hamazon does not apply and necessarily you find this novelty only in menachot—and in menachot there is oil and one does not fulfill with matzah and automatically no obligation of birkat hamazon at all—how does it say they are liable in zimmun; necessarily it refers to eating satiety measure through meal satiety and on this it says and you shall eat and be satisfied Merciful One said and behold they are there—if so it implies birkat hamazon applies from Torah. And do not say it refers to challot of thanksgiving that one fulfills with matzah and is not rich matzah and automatically liable in birkat hamazon—for in thanksgiving it is not relevant to say and they shall eat those through whom atonement was made for it is not for atonement. But per this our proof is not—for then you do not find it in minchah that it is for atonement except minchat choteh which is for atonement—and in minchat choteh there is no oil and it is not pat haba'ah b'kisnin; nevertheless one can prove reverse that all birkat hamazon on pat haba'ah b'kisnin is only rabbinic—for otherwise it is hard who compelled Talmud to explain reason that it was thought they are exempt from power of behold atonement was made—for then novelty would be only in minchat choteh alone; we also need to invent new reasoning that what is atonement does not have berachah—which is against reasoning [though true one can say from this proof for view of those who hold where one eats under compulsion he does not bless—and therefore it was thought since kohanim are compelled to eat it and Merciful One compelled them and he did not wish to eat only Torah forced him to eat—therefore it was thought berachah does not apply—therefore teaches since written and you shall eat and be satisfied and behold they are there; and per Talmud's conclusion one can say reverse even one who eats under compulsion blesses since written and you shall eat and be satisfied and they are there—and so on at length]. In any case it is hard why Talmud needed this extra—it should have answered it teaches in other menachot even though they did not eat in way of measure others establish on it—and if so it was thought they are exempt since it is pat haba'ah b'kisnin—therefore teaches since mitzvah in eating them mitzvah establishes him as kavua—therefore teaches kohanim are liable in zimmun even less than establishment measure since mitzvah in eating them mitzvah makes kavua as written in Talmud chapter 5 of Beitzah eating of Shabbat establishes for tithe and so many places; and proven from there eating of matzah is not considered casual only kavua and necessarily mitzvah established it—and if so teaches here too with kohanim mitzvah makes kavua and even less than measure others establish they are liable in zimmun—and therefore proven even real establishment is only rabbinic—and here itself is rabbinic enactment—cannot say regarding kohanim they enacted to bless also on less from power mitzvah considered it kavua for that is enactment on enactment which we do not do—and if so we do not say mitzvah makes kavua except in matter that is from Torah or at least establishment itself is from Torah—therefore it helps also to say mitzvah established it; but for birkat hamazon whose establishment measure itself is only rabbinic and from Torah does not depend on establishment only satiety—and what they enacted for pat haba'ah b'kisnin the measure as way others establish is only rabbinic—cannot say mitzvah established it for enactment on enactment; and there is proof from this it is only rabbinic; and I had place to extend but time did not allow—perhaps Heaven will grant another time to explain more—examine well.
