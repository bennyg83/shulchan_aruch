Subordinate. See Taz, seif katan 8. Regarding pas haba'ah b'kisnin, if one removes the filling, he nevertheless recites Borei Minei Mezonos over the pastry itself, and even together with the filling the blessing is Borei Minei Mezonos. Although the filling is primary, as Magen Avraham seif katan 15 writes, the pastry is not entirely subordinate to it. This differs from thin wafers eaten with nougat, where the wafers are completely subordinate. When eaten with nougat, no separate blessing is recited over the wafers at all.
What the Mechaber writes in seif 9, that one who eats half a kezayis of bread recites HaMotzi, is not a full a fortiori argument. Kesef Mishneh, chapter 3 of Hilchos Berachos, halachah 12, explains that the initial blessing is required lest he continue and eat the requisite amount. Eliyah Rabbah, seif katan 21, cites this. Regarding pas haba'ah b'kisnin, however, we are not concerned that he may change his mind and establish a meal upon it, because people do not ordinarily establish a meal upon pas haba'ah b'kisnin.
