(Seif katan 11) Reclining with him. For then he cannot remove it because of the guest. The Levush wrote that the other person wants to eat it, and the blessing must apply to it as well; therefore it is permitted for the host, etc. According to this, if the guest is a distinguished person, regarding whom the Magen Avraham wrote in seif katan 32 in the name of Lechem Chamudos that nowadays one may give him to break bread, then this law would not apply. Perhaps nowadays, even if the guest is not distinguished, since the custom is that each person recites HaMotzi for himself and they do not fulfill their obligation through the host's blessing, the Levush's reason and this law would not apply, though I do not know the reason for the custom.
