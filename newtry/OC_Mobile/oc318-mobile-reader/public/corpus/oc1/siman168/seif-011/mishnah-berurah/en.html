That crumbs which are placed, etc. This is when they do not contain a kezayis, for if they contain a kezayis, they do not require the form of bread, as written in the previous seif.
In water. Those who place a piece of bread that they dry over coals into beer, called fenitz, and it does not contain a kezayis, and the beer becomes whitened through the soaking, have the same law, that one blesses Borei Minei Mezonos over them. If he placed only a little very fine bread into hot beer in order to give it flavor, and not for eating, he blesses only Shehakol over the beer, for it is primary, and the bread has no substance and no significance.
It goes away, etc. Although it is explained at the end of the previous seif that crumbs that are not cooked and not joined, even if they do not have the form of bread, require HaMotzi, even if they were soaked in water, as written there, that is specifically there, where the shape of the bread itself did not change at all, for it was soaked in water only a short time, and the reason the form of bread is not recognizable in it is only because of its smallness. This is unlike here, where it soaked in water for a long time until the water became whitened through it; this is a sign that its form was spoiled by the soaking, and the name bread was lost from it.
