[12] To cut on the beautiful etc.—Bach disputes this for since he is careful he must bless on Israel's bread; Nachalat Tzvi explains host cuts on his bread and guest at his table cuts on kutim bread; and see Shach and Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 112 who upheld and agreed to ruling of Shulchan Aruch and Levush; and so is essential—for I wrote there in Eliyah Rabbah that view of Agudah and Maharai and Maharil laws of kutim cooking that even when careful eating alone it is good to cut and eat from beautiful of kutim bread; and even though we do not hold like them, nevertheless they are worthy to rely on when another who is not careful reclines with him.
