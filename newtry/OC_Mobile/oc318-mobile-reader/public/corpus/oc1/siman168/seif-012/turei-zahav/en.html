In red wine. Mordechai writes there: “Likewise, one who soaks his bread in wine does not recite Hamotzi, for it has lost the status of bread because it became red. It resembles pas haba'ah bekisnin, and he recites Borei Minei Mezonos because it provides nourishment.” This is because its appearance has changed. With white wine, however, the law depends on whether the wine becomes whitened by the bread, as stated immediately above regarding water.
