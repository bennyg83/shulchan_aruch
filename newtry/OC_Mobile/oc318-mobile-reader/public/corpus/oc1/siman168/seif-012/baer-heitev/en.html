Regarding whether one must bless on the wine, it appears that we follow the primary element: if the wine is primary and eating the bread inside it is to sweeten the drink, he blesses on the wine and exempts the bread; if the bread is primary and the wine is to sweeten the eating, he blesses on the bread and exempts the wine; and if both are primary, he blesses on both, on the bread Borei Minei Mezonos and on the wine Borei Pri HaGafen.
