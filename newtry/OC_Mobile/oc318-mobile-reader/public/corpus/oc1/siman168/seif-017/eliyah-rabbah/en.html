[17] And afterward he ate, etc. Responsa Maharsach siman 173 wrote: if he changed his mind to eat the required amount without combining the first eating, he recites ha-motzi; end quote. Or Chadash wrote: the amount for people to establish a meal upon it is three or four eggs; end quote. It requires study whether his intent is when one eats bread alone or with other things. I saw in Magen Avraham seif katan 13 that he wrote as follows: if he ate it alone, he must eat as much as others are accustomed to be satisfied from it alone; and if he ate it with meat and other things, it suffices when he eats the amount from which others are accustomed to be satisfied together with other things. If he ate this amount alone and is satisfied from it, he recites ha-motzi, since others are accustomed to be satisfied from this amount when they accompany it with other foods.
