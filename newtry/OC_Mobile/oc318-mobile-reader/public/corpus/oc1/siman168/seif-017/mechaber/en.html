17 A pie which is baked in an oven with meat or with fish or with cheese – one recites on it the blessing "...Who takes out..." and grace after the meal. {Rama: Gloss: And only provided that one baked it in an oven without liquid. But if he baked it in a frying pan with liquid, he should not recite a blessing on it, and one should not eat it, except during the meal, as was explained [his own opinion, and likewise wrote Shibbolei Leket].}
