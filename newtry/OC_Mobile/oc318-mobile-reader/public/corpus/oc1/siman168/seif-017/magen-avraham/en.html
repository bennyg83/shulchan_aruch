With meat or fish—and similarly even if he did not establish on them—for they do not nullify Torah category of bread for they themselves are food—see siman 177; unlike fruits which are dessert seif 7; and so Shulchan Aruch HaGadol; Shelah; requires study for Tosefta writes on it and specifically when he established meal on it see there—and if so law is like pat kisnin requires study as implied by poskim; nevertheless in Shulchan Shelomo writes plainly five species are essence like act of pot see there; and possible all filled with fruits also same law; and it appears same dough kneaded in egg water without fat and baked to full bread—for eggs too are food and do not nullify bread category as Beitzah 16 Tosafot s.v. kemcha ikker—from here practiced to take idolater bread called kanavit and obliash that flour is essence and thick oven-baked etc.; and those kneaded—since do not bless hamotzi on them there is concern of kutim cooking etc.—end quote Shulchan Shelomo; that dough kneaded in eggs bless hamotzi and birkat hamazon Yoreh De'ah siman 113; however Shelah wrote when kneaded in eggs it is like kneaded in honey. Rules of this siman: (a) dough kneaded in fruit juice and baked later this seif; (b) if fried in fruit juice or boiled in water seif 13; (c) if filled with fruits seif 7; (d) if filled with meat and fish seif 16; all when batter thick; (e) soft batter seif 8, 14, 15; (f) full bread soaked and cooked afterward seif 10.
