The Shulchan Aruch here discusses where he fixed his meal on it, and teaches that one might have thought this pastida would not require HaMotzi even if he fixed his meal on it, because the meat is primary; therefore it teaches that this is not so. See there. The Magen Avraham distinguishes between a meat filling and a filling of fruit and spices, for a filling of fruit and spices is dessert and is kisnin. See there. Know that pas haba'ah b'kisnin, on which one recites Borei Minei Mezonos, is so even if it was baked in an oven. Principles of this siman: 1 dough kneaded with fruit juice and afterward baked, in seif 7; 2 if it was fried in fruit juice or in water, in seif 13; 3 if it was filled with fruit, in seif 7; 4 if it was filled with meat and fish, in seif 16, and all this is when its kneading is thick; 5 if its batter is soft, in seif 8, seif 14, and seif 15; 6 and complete bread that one soaked and afterward cooked, in seif 10. This is according to the Mechaber and the Magen Avraham, who distinguish between a meat filling and a fruit filling. But according to the Mechaber as understood by the Taz, there is no distinction between a meat filling and other things, and it is always kisnin. Seif 7 discusses both a meat filling and other fillings, while seif (16) [17] discusses where he fixed a meal on it. Analyze carefully, and see Bnei Chiya, Perach Shushan klal 1 siman 5 and siman 3, and Yad Aharon.
