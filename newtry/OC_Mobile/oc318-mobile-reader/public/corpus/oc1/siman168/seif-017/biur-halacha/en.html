Pastida baked etc.—see Mishnah Berurah that even when not established Mechaber holds one blesses hamotzi on it; and truly so is proven in Riaz—and so pat haba'ah b'kisnin is bread made in honey or spices that one eats like types of sweets for meal dessert; if he establishes etc.; and if he does not establish etc.; and my teacher my ancestor Rav explained that pastries baked in oven filled with meat or fish or cheese or vegetables—one blesses hamotzi on them; and so appears to me essentially they are made bread and liften together and for establishing a meal they are made—end of his words there in Piskei Halachot on Berachot and brought also in Shulchan Aruch HaGadol; and Magen Avraham hinted this; and not like Taz in understanding this law who holds intent is specifically when established; and I saw many achronim agree to law per Taz that filling with meat and fish is like filling with fruits that one does not bless hamotzi and birkat hamazon except when established; and they bring proof from Sefer HaTanya who wrote so explicitly; and truly when we examine source well it appears even Mechaber concedes to Taz that meat filling is like fruit filling; only Mechaber refers when made like plain bread made for satiety and therefore not in category of kisnin bread made only for dessert and mere sweetening (as poskim wrote kisnin bread is made only for meal dessert); and as Riaz's language teaches they are made bread and liften together and for establishing meal—meaning he considers meat and fish filled inside like eating bread with meat and fish since this bread is made for establishing meal not dessert; indeed if made thin small wafers mixed with bits of meat and recognizable they are made only for dessert after meal—law is exactly like bread mixed with fruits and other sweets—does not bless hamotzi and birkat hamazon when not established; and close to my eyes Tanya refers to such case and rules it is kisnin bread; and so proven from Chemdat Moshe for law as we wrote; and so proven in Derech Chaim; and per these words I wrote what is in Mishnah Berurah.
