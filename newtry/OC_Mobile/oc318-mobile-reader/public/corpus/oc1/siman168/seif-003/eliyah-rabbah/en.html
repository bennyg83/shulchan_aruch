He places the piece, etc. The Magen Avraham, the Shelah, the Bach, and all the later authorities wrote in the name of the Maharshal that this is specifically when one eats alone; but if two are eating, one should bless on the piece of wheat bread and one on the whole barley bread, end quote. The Magen Avraham wondered: all agree that the piece of wheat bread is preferable, but we say that a God-fearing person should fulfill both views. If so, how can one bless on the barley and leave the wheat? end quote. In my humble opinion, it is implied from the Ashri that according to R. Zalman there is a dispute, and this is what the Levush wrote, that they are equivalent; so it also implies in the Bach. What he challenged from siman 298, seif 4, that even a beis midrash is cancelled so that one person should bless for all, is not difficult at all, for HaMotzi is different, since it requires joining together, as above in siman 166. Even regarding a guest, it seems to me that the law is the same, and so it implies in the Bach, unlike the Magen Avraham. However, when both are of one species, the Bach wrote that one should not do this, since we hold that whole takes precedence.
