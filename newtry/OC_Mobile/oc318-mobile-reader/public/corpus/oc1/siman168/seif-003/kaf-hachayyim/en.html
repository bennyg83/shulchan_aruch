16) [Seif 3] In the gloss: He should break it only at its whole place. This is also because that place baked first. Darkei Moshe, paragraph 1. Magen Avraham, paragraph 5, cites this and writes that it requires study. If so, even from a piece one should break at its whole place, because it baked first, yet we have never seen this practice. Rather, it suffices to cut at the side of the bread; see there. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 5, responds that absence of observation is not proof. He fulfilled this ruling based on reason when he happened to break bread from a piece, and it is proper to practice this way and break at the whole place; see there. We similarly wrote above in siman 167, paragraph 5, that this is the proper practice in order to satisfy all opinions; see there.
