Magen Avraham, seif katan 2 It appears to me that in any event. Meaning, even Maharshal, who devised this solution, referred only to two householders or two guests. But in the case of a householder and a guest, since the mitzvah rests upon the householder, Maharshal agrees that he should recite the blessing over both. The proof is from seif 5, where we do not say that the guest should recite the blessing over the non-Jew’s bread. This is because the mitzvah rests upon the householder.
