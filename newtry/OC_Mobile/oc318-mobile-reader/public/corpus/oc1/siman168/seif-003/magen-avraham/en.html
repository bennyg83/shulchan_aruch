From the place that is whole. There too it browns from cooking. Darkei Moshe. This requires study, for if so, even with a sliced loaf he should break from the whole side, since there too it browns from cooking, and we have never seen this. Rather, it is enough to cut from the side of the bread, as written at the beginning of siman 267. Therefore Maharil needed to give the reason that there it appears like a slice, so one should not cut from the side; but with pieces of bread, even if he cuts from the opposite side, it is still a slice, so it is enough to cut from the side.
