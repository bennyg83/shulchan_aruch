If one erred in the Yotzer blessing in a matter that requires another to stand in his place — if he erred from Kedushah onward, the second need not begin except from the place where [the first] stopped.That is, he begins from Kedushah onward; but if he erred before Kedushah he must begin at the beginning (Ri and Rosh in Ein Om'din, Hagahot Maimoni chapter 10 of Laws of Prayer, and Tur).
