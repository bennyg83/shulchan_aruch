5) Alone, from then. When saying the word "me'az," one should intend the union represented by the combined Divine Name Yahadonai, for the numerical value of "az" is eight, corresponding to the number of letters in that Name. Shaar HaKavanos, in the discourse on the intentions of Yotzer, page 19a; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 90d; Kaf HaChaim by Rabbi Chaim Palagi, siman 14, paragraph 4.
