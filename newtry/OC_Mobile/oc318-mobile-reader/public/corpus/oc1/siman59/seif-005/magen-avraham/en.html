The second [leader] need not [return to the beginning]. It implies that if he wished to begin from the start he may [Beit Yosef]; but Darkei Moshe wrote that he may not, since the latter authorities ruled like the Yerushalmi — if so, if he begins from the start it is a blessing in vain.

From Kedushah onward. Thus wrote Terumat HaDeshen, chapter 5 of Berakhot: if he erred in "ofanim" or from "ofanim" until the end of the blessing, he need not return except from "El barukh" onward, for from there onward is like the beginning of a blessing — thus far. It implies that if he erred in "ofanim" he begins from "ofanim," and so too is the language of the Yerushalmi: since he finished Kaddish he finished the blessing; and so too the Levush; and so too is the language of Shulchan Aruch — if he erred from Kedushah onward, etc., not like Beit Chadash who disagrees.
