This applies even if the second person has not yet fulfilled his own obligation in the first half of the blessing, so that afterward he will have to repeat the beginning and conclusion of the blessing for himself. Nevertheless, this is permitted because he thereby discharges the obligation of the congregation, analogous to the law below in siman 126, seif 2, which applies in every case. See there. It appears that this applies only when the custom is to discharge others through the blessings. Today, however, it is forbidden for him to begin in the middle of the blessing if he has not yet recited it himself.
