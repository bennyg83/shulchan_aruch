Siman 61 — Be'er Heitev. And see in Rabbi Yehuda who explained in Beit Yosef that also after Yotzer ha-me'orot one should not answer amen but finish with the shaliach tzibbur, and not as some practice to answer amen after Yotzer ha-me'orot and not after HaBocher, see there. And indeed thus is implied from the language of Beit Yosef. But from the plain meaning of Shulchan Aruch it appears that specifically after HaBocher, since it is adjacent to Keriat Shema, it is like an interruption between the berakhah and the matter for which he blesses — and it is in doubt. And it is explained in Beit Yosef that if he finished before the shaliach tzibbur he answers amen, except that he should not hurry to finish before but rather finish together and then he will not answer amen, for it is like answering after his berakhot; and see later siman 81 in Darkei Moshe that in amen one intends "melekh ne'eman." And nevertheless, in completing the three words he should rely on what he hears from the mouth of the shaliach tzibbur when he returns "Hashem Elokeikhem emet." But individually he may say "melekh ne'eman," since in the Zohar it calls him "ma'uvet," etc., see there. And Shelah wrote that it appeared to him the custom of Sephardim not to answer amen and to finish with the shaliach tzibbur in order not to interrupt between Ahavah and accepting the yoke of the kingdom of heaven. Therefore both in Shacharit and Ma'ariv he concludes at Ahavah and again after the verse of Keriat Shema returns and begins "Ve'ahavta," see there. And nevertheless, if he finished before the shaliach tzibbur he should answer amen and intend "melekh ne'eman" as written in siman 61; and see in Pirchei Shoshan klal 1 siman 15 the reason not to answer amen even though it is permitted to interrupt for fear and honor as Tur wrote siman 66 — since he is engaged in praising the Omnipresent he should not interrupt for another praise; and see there what he wrote regarding one who needs to remove talit and tefillin and is after Barukh She'amar — lekhatchilah he should adjoin some psalms of zimrah and place them between mizmor and mizmor; and bedieved if he placed them thus, all the more so he did not lose the berakhah, see there; and see above siman 53 in Be'er Heitev s"k 3 in the name of GUR and what he wrote there; and later siman 66.
