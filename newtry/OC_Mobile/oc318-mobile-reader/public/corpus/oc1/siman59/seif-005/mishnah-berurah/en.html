In his place. Meaning that the first chazzan does not know how to return to his place.
From Kedushah onward. Meaning from ‘and the Ofanim,’ or from ‘To the blessed God they offer pleasant melodies.’
And onward. If he erred in ‘and the Ofanim,’ the replacement must begin from ‘and the Ofanim.’ If he erred in ‘To the blessed God they offer pleasant melodies,’ the replacement must return to the beginning of that passage. Although below in siman 126 we rule that the replacement must begin from the start of the blessing in which the first chazzan erred, this case differs. Once the congregation answered ‘Holy, holy,’ what follows is considered like the beginning of a new blessing.
At the beginning. One blessing may not be divided into two. This applies specifically in the period when the chazzan's blessings of Krias Shema were used to discharge the congregation. Today, when the custom is for everyone to recite the blessings independently and no one fulfills his obligation through the chazzan, if the chazzan becomes unable to continue during Yotzer Or, or similarly during any blessing other than the Amidah, the replacement is forbidden to repeat and begin from a point that he himself already recited.
