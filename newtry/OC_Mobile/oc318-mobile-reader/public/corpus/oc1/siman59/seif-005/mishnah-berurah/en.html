(26) "Under him" — meaning that he does not know to return to his place.

(27) "From kedushah and onward" — meaning in ha'ofanim or in "le'El barukh ne'imah."

(28) "And onward" — if he erred in ha'ofanim he must begin from ha'ofanim; and if in "le'El barukh ne'imah" he must return to the beginning of "le'El barukh ne'imah." And even though the halakhah is established later in siman 126 that the second [person who erred] must begin from the beginning of the berakhah in which the first erred — here it is different, for since they answered "Kadosh Kadosh" from there and onward is like the beginning of a berakhah.

(29) "At the head" — that one does not divide one berakhah into two. This applies specifically at a time when they were accustomed to fulfill through the Keriat Shema berakhah; but as today, when the custom is that each one blesses for himself and no one fulfills through the shaliach tzibbur's berakhah — if the shaliach tzibbur stopped in the berakhah of Yotzer Or (and similarly in all berakhot [except tefillah]), the second one standing "under him" is forbidden to return and begin from a place that he already said from the beginning by himself.
