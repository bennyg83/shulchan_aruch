Immediately. Specifically immediately, meaning within the time required to utter a brief phrase. If more time elapsed, he did not fulfill his obligation, because the opening words, ‘Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe,’ do not apply to his later correction, ‘Who forms light,’ since he did not say it immediately. Shulchan Aruch's later phrase, ‘and he did not say “Who forms light,”’ likewise includes a case where he said it, but not immediately. Peri Megadim writes this in the name of Bach and Perishah. Derech HaChaim, however, rules that even if he did not remember until near the end of the blessing, so long as he had not completed it he need not begin Yotzer Or again from the beginning. Rather, he says, ‘Who forms light and creates darkness,’ and continues through the conclusion, ‘Who forms the luminaries,’ thereby fulfilling his obligation. This is how one should act in practice, as we wrote in Biur Halachah.
