Siman 62—if one erred, etc., and likewise, etc. Thus he explained in the words of the Tur that two conditions are required: (1) that in the opening of the berakhah one must mention Yotzer Or either at the beginning or in the middle; (2) that the conclusion must be as required—and if one of these is lacking he has not fulfilled. And there is doubt, for the words of the Rosh do not say so, and also in the Gemara it does not appear so, for they answer there "it is different there," etc.—apparently conclusion alone suffices; and also per the conclusion that they answer "rather, since," etc., it also appears that conclusion alone suffices, but his words are according to what we establish that the question is lenient and the beraita comes per the plain meaning because he returned and said Yotzer Or per the explanation of Rif—but from what the beraita states in general "that," etc., it implies after the conclusion of the berakhah; therefore the main point is that if he finished "yotzer hama'orot," even though he said at the beginning "hakol m'ariv aravim," he has fulfilled according to all the explanations in the Gemara.
