Seif katan 2, ‘some say,’ and so forth. ‘They all revere and sanctify,’ with a final mem. It appears to me that the reason is that a final mem denotes the masculine form, whereas a final nun denotes the feminine form. Midrash Yalkut on Mishlei 21, on the verse ‘A wise man ascended a city of mighty men,’ states in the name of Rabbi Yehoshua and Rabbi Acha that ‘mighty men’ is written in a manner indicating that all are males and there are no females among them, and that ‘a wise man ascended’ refers to Moshe. Since we describe how the angels praise the Holy One, blessed is He, the masculine form should be used. I do not possess the work of Mahariv.
This applies specifically while seated. Eliyah Rabbah cites a responsum of Rabbi Menachem Azariah of Fano, siman 102: ‘Regarding the Kedushah recited while seated, if one was seated and stood, he is mistaken; if he was standing and sat down, it is evident that he is not wise.’
