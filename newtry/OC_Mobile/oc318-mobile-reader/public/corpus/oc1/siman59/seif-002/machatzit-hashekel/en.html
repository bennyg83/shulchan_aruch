(א) (Sk 2) There are those who say etc. "Me'aritzim u'mekadishim" — all with mem at the end of the word; and in my humble opinion the reason: mem at the end of the word is masculine language, but nun at the end of the word is feminine language; and it is in Midrash Yalkut Mishlei 21 "Ir giborim alah chacham" — R. Yehoshua in the name of R. Acha: "giborim" is written — all of them are males and there are no females among them. "Alah chacham" — this is Moshe etc.; and since we recount how the angels praise Hashem, therefore it must be in masculine language. And the book of Mahari"v is not in my possession:
