(ב) There — "yotzer or" etc. When one begins this blessing he should touch the tefillin of the hand, which is in the world of Beriah in the secret of action, corresponding to the prayer of the Creator who is in Beriah in the secret of speech; Sha'ar HaKavanot in derush Yotzer and Keriat Shema page 18d and in the prayer formula page 51b and Beit Yosef above siman 48 — see there; and further see above siman 25 ot 27 and ot 32 and siman 48 ot 1 s.v. "ve'od da" etc. — see there. And when he touches the tefillin of the head in the blessing "yotzer or" he should touch when he says the word "u'vorei" and "u'vorei choshech," which alludes to the world of Beriah, as written in Sha'ar HaKavanot there 19a — see there. But in the tefillin of the head he should not touch now at the beginning of the blessing "yotzer or," only when he reaches the Shemoneh Esrei prayer he touches the tefillin of the head, for both are in Atzilut — tefillin of the head in the aspect of action and prayer in the aspect of speech, as explained in Sha'ar HaKavanot derush 1 and derush 3 of Shacharit prayer and derush 1 of standing; and in our words above — see there. And I also saw written in the name of Maharashu z"l in a manuscript gloss on Sefer Peri Chadash on Sha'ar Keriat Shema chapter 1 on what the Rav z"l wrote there at the beginning of the ascent of the world of Beriah, which is "yotzer or" — one must hold the tefillin and touch the tefillin of the hand etc.; and it is written thus: I heard that "yotzer" and tefillin of the hand are both in the world of Beriah etc. as explained among us; therefore one touches in "yotzer or" specifically the tefillin of the hand and not tefillin of the head until the beginning of standing; therefore the Rav z"l omitted "u'vorei choshech" for touching tefillin of the head — until here in the manuscript. And so Shulchan Aruch siman 59; and so Chida ot 1, and this is its language: when he says "yotzer or" he touches tefillin of the hand and does not touch tefillin of the head except before the Shemoneh Esrei prayer in a whisper as required per the secret; and so they practice in the holy city Jerusalem in the congregation of Chasidim in the Beit El yeshiva — until here; and so Kaf HaChayyim siman 14 ot 2, Ben Ish Chai parashat Shemot and parashat Beshalach ot 1, and unlike what Machatzit HaShekel wrote in the prayer of Beriah chapter 1 ot 7 and ot 8; and Yad Aharon brought it in Hagahat Tashbetz and in Etz Chaim sk 1, that when he says "yotzer or" he touches tefillin of the hand and when he says "u'vorei choshech" he touches tefillin of the head — see there; except he should not touch tefillin of the head until he says the Shemoneh Esrei prayer, as above; and one should touch after he says the verse "the Omnipresent sefatai tiftach" etc. and before he begins the prayer, for there is the place of the beginning of the world of Atzilut, as explained in Sha'ar HaKavanot derush 1 of standing 28d — see there:
