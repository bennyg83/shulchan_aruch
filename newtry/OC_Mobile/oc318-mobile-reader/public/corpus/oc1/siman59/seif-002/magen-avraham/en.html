And he did not say "Yotzer Or." Explanation: even though he concluded properly, since his beginning and middle were not proper he has not fulfilled his obligation. And some disagree with what is written in siman 259, that if he concluded properly he has fulfilled his obligation — there the formula is short, but here they enacted a long formula, and he changed from the formula that the Sages instituted and has not fulfilled his obligation. And it seems to me that if he did not remember until he finished Ahavah Rabbah, he should not interrupt but should say Yotzer Or after [the eighteen blessings], for the order of blessings is not indispensable, as written in siman 60.
