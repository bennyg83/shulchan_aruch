And he did not say ‘Who forms light.’ Meaning, although he concluded properly, since the beginning and middle were improper, he did not fulfill his obligation. This is unlike what is written in siman 209, that if one concluded properly he fulfilled his obligation. There the formula is brief, whereas here the Sages instituted a lengthy formula, and he altered the formula established by the Sages, so he did not fulfill his obligation. It appears to me that if he did not remember until after completing Ahavah Rabbah, he should not interrupt. Rather, he should recite Yotzer Or after the Amidah, since the order of the blessings is not indispensable, as stated in siman 60.
