The blessing Yotzer and the evening blessing should be recited quietly together with the prayer leader. {Rama: One should hasten to finish before the prayer leader finishes and answer Amen after the prayer leader. [Hagahot Maimoniyot, chapter 1 of the Laws of Blessings, and Rokeach, siman 218] Nevertheless, if he did not recite the blessing himself but only heard it from the prayer leader, he has fulfilled his obligation, because for these blessings the prayer leader can discharge the obligation of an individual even if that person is proficient. The prayer leader cannot, however, discharge an individual's obligation with fewer than 10 present. [Rabbeinu Yonah, chapter Mi SheMeito]} One should not answer Amen after the conclusion 'who chooses His people Israel with love,' because that would constitute an interruption. See below, siman 61.
