The blessings of Yotzer and Aravit one recites with the shaliach tzibbur at a leisurely pace. One should not answer Amen after the conclusion of "...who chooses His people Israel with love," because it constitutes an interruption (see below, siman 61).And one should hasten to finish before the shaliach tzibbur finishes, in order to answer Amen after the shaliach tzibbur (Hagahot Maimoni, chapter 1 of Laws of Blessings, and Rokeach siman 218). Nevertheless, if one did not recite it but heard it from the shaliach tzibbur, he has fulfilled his obligation, for with these blessings the shaliach tzibbur fulfills the obligation of an individual even if he is expert.
