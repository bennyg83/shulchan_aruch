With fewer than ten. Thus also in Darkei Moshe in the name of Terumat HaDeshen, chapter 3 of Berakhot, and this is his language there: although "with those in the fields" who are under duress, the shaliach tzibbur fulfills them from tefillah, nevertheless from Keriat Shema they are not exempt except through hearing and answering Amen, and he does not exempt except with ten — thus far. Beit Yosef brings this in siman 69. It implies that it deals with one who is not expert — for otherwise, even in tefillah he would not fulfill his obligation, as written in siman 124; rather it deals when one is not expert, and nevertheless in Keriat Shema ten are required. So too Abudraham and Rabbi Moshe Alshakar, siman 10; and so wrote Beit Yosef in chapter 8 of the five [orders of] tefillah. And that which we say "the one who knows how to bless and the ignorant one fulfills" — it must be said that blessings of enjoyment are different — thus far. And in Ran, chapter Ha-Korei Omed, he did not understand thus, and therefore he challenged the Geonim on what they wrote; nevertheless, in other blessings all agree that one who is not expert does not require ten. And so is clear in Hagahot on chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah. See section 6 and siman 594.
