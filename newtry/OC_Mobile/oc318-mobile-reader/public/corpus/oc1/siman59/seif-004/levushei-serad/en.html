Shulchan Aruch, seif 4: ‘He should not answer Amen.’ Rama's statement at the beginning of the gloss, ‘He should hasten to finish,’ refers to the blessing of Yotzer. Regarding the blessing of Ahavah, however, Rama agrees with the ruling stated in Beit Yosef. The reason is that since this blessing relates to Krias Shema, it is improper to interrupt it. See there in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. Nevertheless, Darkei Moshe, letter 6, makes clear that Rama disagrees with Rabbeinu Yonah and instead follows the Rosh, who maintains that one answers Amen even after the blessing of Ahavah. This is why Rama added the gloss, ‘See below, siman 61.’ His intention is to the gloss there at the end of seif 3, where he writes that one should answer Amen.
