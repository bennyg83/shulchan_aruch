Shulchan Aruch seif 4 And he should not answer Amen. And what Rama wrote at the beginning of Hagahah "and he should hurry to finish" etc. — that is in the blessing "yotzer"; but in the blessing Ahavah Rabbah Rama agrees as Beit Yosef wrote the reason, since this blessing is on Keriat Shema it is not fit to interrupt it — in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. However in Darkei Moshe ot 6 it is explained he disagrees with Rabbeinu Yonah, rather like the Rosh holds one answers Amen also after the blessing Ahavah Rabbah; and this is what Rama emended and wrote "and see below siman 61" — his intent is there in Hagahah at the end of seif 3 he wrote one says Amen:
