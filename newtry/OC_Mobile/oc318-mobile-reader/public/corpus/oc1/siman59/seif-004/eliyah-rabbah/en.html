[4] And when they answer etc. Meaning in congregation; and so it implies in Levush that for an individual it is explained in siman 132 that it is in a whisper — and see above siman 61. He meant to say that there Rama wrote the practice is to answer; and so Tur wrote it is no worse than asking for peace that we interrupt. And in Shelah page 253 he wrote it did not seem right to him except the Sephardic custom not to answer Amen and to interrupt; and he added in their reason that even if one recites Keriat Shema with awe and fear and trembling and sweating in all 248 limbs, nevertheless one will enter from fear to love, to be the joy of faith in unity in his heart to deliver his soul and his wealth; therefore one concludes the blessing both in Shacharit and in Ma'ariv with love and acceptance of the yoke of heaven in the middle, and afterward "ve'ahavta" etc.; therefore one should not interrupt, rather it seems one should wait to finish the blessing with the shaliach tzibur; therefore if he finished before the shaliach tzibur it is fit to answer Amen as Tur's view:
