[1] To discharge etc. And Mahari Abohav wrote another reason — that if we were not to mention the attribute of night by day it would seem darkness is bad, which is not so, for everything He made well in its time — until here. And Shulchan Aruch omitted it because Beit Yosef challenged it; and according to his words we would not need to mention the attribute of day at night — until here. And in my humble opinion nothing is difficult: Mahari's intent is that whenever we bless on one thing, if we do not mention its opposite it implies the opposite is bad; and the same if we were not to mention day at night — it would seem light is bad; easy to understand:
