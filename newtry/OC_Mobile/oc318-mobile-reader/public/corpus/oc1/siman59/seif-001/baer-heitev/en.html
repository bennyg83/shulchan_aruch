Who forms light. One must pause between ‘forms’ and ‘light,’ so that it not sound like a single word. The initial letters of ‘mesharsim va'asher mesharsav’ spell mum, which is the final Name among the seventy-two-letter Name; one must therefore take care not to omit the vav of ‘va'asher.’ When saying ‘Who forms light,’ one should touch the hand-tefillin, and when saying ‘and creates darkness,’ one should touch the head-tefillin. They reverence and sanctify, all with a final mem. Some read them with a final nun. Those who say them with a mem must pause between ‘mamlichim’ and ‘es Shem haKel haMelech.’ One should say ‘retzon Koneihem,’ not ‘retzon Konam.’ See Lechem Rav, siman 1. The Arizal, Olas Tamid, and most authorities write likewise. Some say ‘retzon Konam’; see Ateres Zekeinim. The initial letters of ‘ahuvim, berurim, gibborim’ spell Abag, one of the Names within the forty-two-letter Name. They perform with awe, whose initial letters have the numerical value seventy-two. Whoever concentrates upon these Names will not be harmed throughout that day, as stated by Ateres Zekeinim. In ‘laKel Baruch ne'imos,’ the lamed has a kamatz. In ‘u'leMelech Kel chai,’ the lamed has a patach. The Arizal writes that both have a kamatz. See Yad Aharon.
