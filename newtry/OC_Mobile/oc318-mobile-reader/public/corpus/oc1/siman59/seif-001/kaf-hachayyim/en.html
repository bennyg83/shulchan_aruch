1) [Seif 1] Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe, Who forms light, etc. The reason we recite a blessing every day over the light of the sun, unlike the blessing over fire, which is recited only on Motzaei Shabbos, is that the sunlight is renewed each day. Tosafos write likewise in the chapter Makom Shenahagu. Pri Chadash, paragraph 1; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 90c.
