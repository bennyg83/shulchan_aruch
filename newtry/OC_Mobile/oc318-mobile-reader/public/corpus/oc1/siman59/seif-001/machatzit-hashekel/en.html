(א) Sk 1 — and he did not say etc. And some say like what Shulchan Aruch wrote in siman 209, that if he concluded properly he fulfills — meaning in the case of holding a cup of beer and erring and saying in the first three blessings "Borei" and within the time of speech recalled and also concluded "shehakol," and such was his saying in the first three blessings "shehakol" — he fulfills:

(ב) For there the coin is short. Meaning there is in the blessing only the conclusion and they did not fix anything in the middle, and he spoiled his conclusion in the first three blessings; and since he again said "shehakol" behold he fixed the conclusion he spoiled. But here they fixed a long coin, meaning one adds words in the blessing between the opening and conclusion; and he spoiled the coin in the middle of the blessing — even though he concluded and signed properly, he did not fix the middle of the blessing:

(ג) And it seems to me if he did not recall. Meaning as much as one needs to return and say the blessing "yotzer or" according to what is explained here in Shulchan Aruch; and he did not recall that one needs to return until after he finishes the blessing Ahavah Rabbah — he should not interrupt between Ahavah Rabbah and Keriat Shema to say the blessing "yotzer or," as explained in seif 4:
