N.B.: The custom is to wait for the shaliach tzibur; and in any case also in all the verses one waits, such as "Mi chamocha ba'elim" and "Hashem yimloch," for the reason written above in Magen Avraham siman 59. However, also at "Atah Hu" one waits, and I have not heard a reason for this. And it seems to me because Tur wrote below siman 61 to interrupt between "mamlikhim" and "Atah Hu" so it should not appear like mentioning the Name of the dead, heaven forbid; and one could say here there is greater scruple because of the heretics who worship the dead; therefore they make a break between "mamlikhim" and "Atah Hu":
