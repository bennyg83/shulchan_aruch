(Magen Avraham sk 1) If he concluded — meaning as Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 2: he took a cup of water and said "Baruch Atah Hashem Elokeinu Melech ha'olam" in the first three blessings and within the time of speech recalled he erred and concluded "shehakol" — he fulfills even though there was only the conclusion alone properly; and one could say here if there was only the conclusion alone properly, behold he changed from the coin, for the coin is long, i.e. opening and conclusion, and he shortened by saying only the conclusion alone:
