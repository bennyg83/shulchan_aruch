(10) Skipping. He only says "ve-omrim be-yirah kadosh," and "ve-ha-ofanim," etc., and "ve-omrim baruch." Pri Chadash wrote: even if he could have prayed alone because he was late in coming, since there is a congregation he says them even in a whisper, according to all opinions.

(11) The custom spread, etc. — and in Biur ha-Gra he agreed that the halakhah follows the last opinion; and since one should not depart from custom, it is fitting to be stringent if saying them when praying alone — to say them with tune and cantillation like one reading verses. And so too Peri Megadim in the name of Levush wrote that it is good to be stringent about this.

(12) And when they answer, etc. — this kedushah, if possible, it is good to say seated.

(13) In a loud voice — specifically in a congregation; but when alone one says in a whisper; so too in Eliyah Rabbah. But in Sha'ar ha-Tziyun he wrote there is no objection even when alone if he says it in a loud voice.
