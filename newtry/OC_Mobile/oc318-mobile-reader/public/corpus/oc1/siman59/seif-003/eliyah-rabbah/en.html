[3] There are those who say the Kedushah etc. Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Sefer HaKaneh to say it specifically while sitting. Mateh Moshe wrote one should say "me'aritzim u'mekadishim" all with mem at the end of the word, not nun. And some practice to stand when the shaliach tzibur says "u'mamlikhim et shem HaKel HaGadol HaNora," because "HaKel HaMelech HaGadol HaGibor VeHanora" — its initials in gematria equal the number of the Name Havayah; and this custom has no basis or principle — until here. And now everyone practices thus in Ma'or la'Aretz; and if you say what use is light to the earth — one should say the sun grows plants and sweetens fruits; and one says "be'rachamav" that He brought the light little by little, for one rising from his bed if he sees great light cannot open his eyes. Shulchan Aruch — Maharal wrote "Kel Baruch" with kamatz under the lamed instead of patach; and likewise "Melech Kel Chai" with kamatz under the lamed. R. David Abudraham wrote: one should wonder why they fixed in the blessing of the luminaries "be'rachamecha harabbim rachem aleinu" — one should say it should be "me'orot" missing vav because they cause miscarriage in infants; therefore they fixed to say "be'rachamecha harabbim" etc. in the blessing of the luminaries — until here. "Kel Baruch gadol" etc. is alef-bet on which heaven and earth stand; and because of the light they say "yetzirat hamalachim" who are also called or, and also are created every day as the light renews. Knesset HaGedolah wrote one should say "u'vne'imah kedushah" — dalet with cholam not shuruk; and one should say "u'vne'imot kol kedushah u'teharah" — until here. "Ratzon konam" without yud. Magen Avraham's gloss wrote: in the blessing "yotzer or" and in the Shemoneh Esrei prayer and in Birkat HaMazon there is every letter of the alef-bet except peh — to say that all who intend in these three, no demon rules over him; and one must pause between "yotzer" and "or" so it should not sound like "yotzror"; "meshartim" and "asher meshartav" — initials spell "mum," another name from the 72 names; therefore one must be careful not to skip the vav of "ve'asher." One should not say "u'melech ha'olam" only "melech" without vav. "Ahuvim berurim gibborim" — initials alef-bet-gimel, and it is a name of 42 names; doing with awe — initials ayin-bet; and all who intend in these names are not harmed all that day. But R. David Abudraham wrote to say "u'melech ha'olam." Shibolei HaLeket wrote: what we say "kulam ke'echad onim" — we do not say "kulam ke'achat." The Geonim wrote in Sandalfon, who is called "echad," as it says "va'eretz echad" — R.
