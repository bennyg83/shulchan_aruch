Ram (aloud) — Be'er Heitev. That Magen Avraham wrote: specifically from sitting. And likewise in Sefer HaKavanot. And what he wrote "u'vene'imah kedushah" with cholam — he wrote in Sha'ar HaTziyun that in Sefer HaKavanot from a manuscript of Maharachav — kedushah with shuruk and it refers to all of them as one, etc. And likewise in old books and in Shiray Keneged he wrote with cholam, for so it is in Sefer Heikhalot, see there. And he wrote in Sha'ar HaTziyun that it must be "l'umat haSerafim" "le'umatam meshabechim," as the Rav z"l wrote. And he further wrote that he heard from grammarians: when one says in tefillah "ba'al milchamot" one must emphasize the ayin well so it not be read "bal" heaven forbid, etc. And in the word "mahar" that it not be read with alef — and it is the language of "maharhav." And likewise in the verse "yisa berakhah me'et the Omnipresent" — expand reading the alef well so it not be understood heaven forbid [as] "mot." And he wrote in Levush the reason that in the congregation they answer in a loud voice — this kedushah that the congregation should say together like the angels, see there. And specifically in the congregation; but individually it is explained in siman 132 that one says quietly, likewise in Eliyahu Rabba, see there, and there I wrote in the name of Sha'ar HaTziyun regarding kedushah of the order for an individual — there is no requirement to say in a loud voice, see there. And the same applies here.
