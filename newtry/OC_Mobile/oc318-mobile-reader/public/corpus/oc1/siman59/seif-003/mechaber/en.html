There are those who say that the Kedushah in Yotzer may be recited by an individual, since it is only a recitation of a narrative. And there are those who say that an individual should skip it, and it is said only in public. One should be concerned for their words and take care that if an individual recites it, he does so with melody and cantillation, as one who reads the Torah.And the custom has already become widespread like the first opinion, that an individual recites it. And when they answer this Kedushah, they say it aloud (Hagahot Maimoni in the formula of prayer).
