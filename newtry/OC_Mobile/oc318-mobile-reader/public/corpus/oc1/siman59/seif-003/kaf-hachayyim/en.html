3) There: Who forms light. We find that the blessing Yotzer Or, Shemoneh Esrei, and Birkas HaMazon each contain every letter of the alphabet except the final form of the letter peh. This teaches that for one who concentrates during these three, neither Satan, an evil occurrence, nor any prosecutor (even when wrath surges, etc.) has power over his prayer or his meal. Ateres Zekeinim; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 90b.
