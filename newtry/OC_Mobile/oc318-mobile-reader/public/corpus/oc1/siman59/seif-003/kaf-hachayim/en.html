(ג) There — "yotzer or." Behold we find in the blessing "yotzer or," in the Shemoneh Esrei, and in Birkat HaMazon, every letter of the alef-bet except peh — to tell you that all who intend in these three, no Satan and no bad mishap and no accuser, even if he is angry etc., rules over his prayer and not even over his meal — end of quote.
