Ram (aloud). Ram"a wrote in siman 102: in this kedushah, if he was sitting and then stood—this is an error; but if he was standing and then sat, it is evident he is not wise—see there. Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Kanah: specifically from sitting. "B'safah berurah u'vene'imah"—the dalet with cholam, per Shakh, Pri Chadash, Olat Tamid, and Yad Aharon. When reaching "l'arba kanfot ha'aretz," one takes the four tzitzit in his left hand and places them on his heart until the section of vayomer; then he takes them in his right hand until he reaches "chayim vekayamim," kisses them, and places them.
