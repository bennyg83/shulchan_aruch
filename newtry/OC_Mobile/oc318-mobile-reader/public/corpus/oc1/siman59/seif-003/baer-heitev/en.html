Exalted. Rama MiPano writes in siman 102 that during this Kedushah, if one was sitting and then stood, he is mistaken; but if he was standing and then sat, it is evident that he is not learned. See there. Magen Avraham writes in the name of Sefer HaKanah that it should specifically be recited while sitting. In ‘with clear speech and sacred pleasantness,’ the dalet of ‘kedushah’ has a cholam, as stated by Knesses HaGedolah, Peri Chadash, Olas Tamid, and Yad Aharon. When one reaches ‘the four corners of the earth,’ he should take the four tzitzis in his left hand and place them over his heart until the paragraph of Vayomer. He should then take them in his right hand until reaching ‘living and enduring,’ kiss them, and release them.
