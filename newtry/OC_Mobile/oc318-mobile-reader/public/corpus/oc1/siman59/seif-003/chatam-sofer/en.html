Seif 3, in the gloss: In accordance with the first opinion. Note: The custom is to wait for the chazzan. Even aside from this, one also waits for the chazzan at all the verses, such as ‘Who is like You among the mighty’ and ‘Hashem shall reign,’ for the reason stated above by Magen Avraham in siman 49. Nevertheless, they also wait at the words ‘es Shem haKel,’ and I have not heard a reason for this. It appears to me that this is because the Tur writes below, in siman 61, that one must pause between ‘mamlichim’ and ‘es Shem,’ so that it not sound, Heaven forbid, as though one were saying ‘a dead one is there.’ It may be added that there is particular concern here because of the sectarians who worship the dead. They therefore make a pause between ‘mamlichim’ and ‘es Shem.’
