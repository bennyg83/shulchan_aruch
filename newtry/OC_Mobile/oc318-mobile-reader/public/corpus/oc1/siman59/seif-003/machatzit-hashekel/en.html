Seif katan 3, ‘he omits it.’ Rather, he should say, ‘and they say with awe: Holy,’ meaning only the first word of the Kedushah. It cannot mean that he should say only ‘and they say with awe,’ for that has no meaning. We are describing that the angels speak, but would not be stating what they say. For the same reason, he must say, ‘and the Ofanim,’ and so forth, ‘say: Blessed.’
