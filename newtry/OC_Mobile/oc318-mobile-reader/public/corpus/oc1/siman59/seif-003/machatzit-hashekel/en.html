(Note 3) When skipping [Kedushah], he only says "ve-omrim be-yirah kadosh" — meaning only the first word of the Kedushah. But he need not say only "ve-omrim be-yirah" alone, for that carries no meaning; we are relating that the angels say [Kedushah], not relating what they say. Similarly, from what is correct one should say "ve-ha-ofanim..." and "ve-omrim baruch."
