“For if he recited them out of order, etc.” See Mishnah Berurah, se’if katan 6, where I wrote, “If he recited the blessing,” etc. It is unnecessary to say that according to the Shulchan Aruch, who rules in se’if 2 that the blessings are not indispensable to Keriat Shema, unlike Rav Hai, the blessings are certainly not indispensable to one another either, as the Beit Yosef writes. Even according to Rav Hai, who holds that the blessings are indispensable to one another, this applies specifically to a congregation and not to an individual, as the Beit Yosef writes. Although the Bach and the Perishah disagree with the Beit Yosef’s understanding of the Tur’s presentation of Rav Hai and hold that an individual and a congregation have the same law, the Darchei Moshe HaArukh, Lechem Chamudot, Taz, and Gra have already agreed with the Beit Yosef’s explanation. See Mishbetzot Zahav, se’if katan 1.
