If he preceded, etc.—see in Mishnah Berurah s.k. 6 what he wrote: and if he blessed, etc.—that it is not necessary per the view of Shulchan Aruch, who ruled in seif 2 that berakhot do not impede Keriat Shema—not like Rav Hai; certainly they do not impede one another either, as Beit Yosef wrote; and even according to Rav Hai, who holds that berakhot impede one another—that is specifically in a congregation and not for an individual, as Beit Yosef wrote; and even though Bach and Perishah argue on Beit Yosef regarding the view of the Tur in the words of Rav Hai, and hold that individual equals congregation—but already Darkei Moshe, Levushei Serad, Taz, and the Gra agreed with the explanation of Beit Yosef; and see Machatzit HaShekel s.k. 1.
