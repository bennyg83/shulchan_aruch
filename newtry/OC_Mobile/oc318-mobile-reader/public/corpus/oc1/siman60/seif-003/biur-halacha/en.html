That if he recited them out of order, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 6, where I wrote, ‘If he recited the blessing,’ etc. According to the Shulchan Aruch, who rules in seif 2 that the blessings are not indispensable to Krias Shema, unlike Rav Hai Gaon, it is certainly clear that the blessings are also not indispensable to one another, as the Beit Yosef writes. Even according to Rav Hai, who maintains that the blessings are indispensable to one another, this applies only to a congregation and not to an individual, as the Beit Yosef explains. Although the Bach and Perishah disagree with the Beit Yosef's understanding of the Tur's presentation of Rav Hai, and hold that an individual and a congregation are governed alike, the Darchei Moshe HaAruch, Lechem Chamudos, Taz, and Biur HaGra have already agreed with the Beit Yosef's explanation. See Mishbetzos Zahav, seif katan 1.
