Although the Shulchan Aruch writes here that the order of the blessings is not indispensable, do not infer from this that the blessings themselves are nevertheless indispensable. That is incorrect, for the Mechaber concludes that he has fulfilled the obligation of the blessings, but he fulfills the obligation of Krias Shema even if he recited no blessings at all, as explained in seif 2.
