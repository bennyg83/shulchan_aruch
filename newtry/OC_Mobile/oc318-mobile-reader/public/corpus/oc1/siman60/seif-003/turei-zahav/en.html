Order of the berakhot, etc. This is written in Tur in the name of Rav Hai, and he did not distinguish between individual and congregation; Beit Yosef wondered—for behold Rosh and Rashba wrote in the name of Rav Hai to distinguish between individual and congregation: that specifically in a congregation order does not impede, but berakhot in any case impede; and for an individual, even if he did not say them at all, it does not impede. It appears there is a scribal error in Tur, and it should read: only for this matter the Gemara says they do not impede—that if he changed the order to say the first at the end and the second at the beginning it does not impede; and the Yerushalmi deals specifically with an individual, but in a congregation they impede. For the halachah, Shulchan Aruch ruled not like Rav Hai in distinguishing individual and congregation, as is Rashba's conclusion.
