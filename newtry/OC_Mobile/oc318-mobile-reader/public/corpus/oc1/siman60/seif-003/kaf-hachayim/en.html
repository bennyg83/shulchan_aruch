(א) And when one says "and to fulfill all the words of Your Torah in love," one should intend the general mitzvah of supporting the hands of Torah students, since they said in the Yerushalmi in Sotah ch. "Alef Nun" and in Midrash Rabbah Vayikra ch. 25: "he learned and taught, kept and did"—and it was in his power to support and he did not support, this one is included in "cursed is one who does not uphold the words of this Torah," see there. If so, after we pray "and place in our hearts to understand and to be wise, to hear, to learn, to teach, to keep and to do"—behold, if it is in one's power to support and one did not support, this one is included in "cursed is one who does not uphold all the words of this Torah"—therefore one must pray "and to fulfill all the words of Your Torah in love." Ra'ah note 3: "and enlighten our eyes in Your Torah"—one should intend that God enlighten his eyes in the secrets of Torah, as the pious Rav Mahari Yabetz wrote, and Rav R. Chayyim in his introduction folio 4 side b, see there, and Ra'ah brought it note 4.

(ב) "And we shall not be ashamed nor humiliated"—so it should read, and not as written in the siddurim "so that we not be ashamed." And one should pour out his soul in saying these words over his offspring, that they not cause him shame and disgrace in this world and the next, God forbid.

(ג) Also when saying "and we shall not be ashamed nor humiliated," one should intend to request that he merit the wisdom of Kabbalah and the secrets of Torah, and that he merit to complete the repair of nefesh, ruach, and neshamah. Chida note 3.
