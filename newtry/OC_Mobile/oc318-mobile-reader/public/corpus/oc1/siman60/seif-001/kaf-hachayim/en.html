(1) [Se'if 1] The second blessing Ahavat Olam, both in the morning and in the evening. Beit Yosef in the name of the Rif and Rambam, and he wrote that such is the custom of the people of Spain. And likewise the Rav in Sha'ar HaKavanot, discourse on the intention of Yotzer, folio 19 side a, and his language: it is not fitting to say Ahavah Rabbah, for Ahavah Rabbah is in the chesed of the world of Atzilut, and now we are in the heichalot of Beriah; therefore one must say Ahavat Olam, which is the minor love of the Malchut called Olam, which is still below in the world of Beriah. And behold it is explained that Ahavat Olam is the secret of the heichal of love, which is the chesed in Beriah, end quote. And likewise in Pri Etz Chaim, gate of Kriat Shema ch. 3; and there is no distinction between Shabbat and Yom Kippur and the other days of the year, for they are all in the heichalot of Beriah, as explained in Etz Chaim gate 46 ch. 4, and known to those who understand; and not like what the Shlah wrote, folio 92 side a, and see there at length and examine. And likewise is the custom of the chasidim of Beit El, may they be preserved for a good life, within our holy city, may it be built and established speedily in our days, that they say only Ahavat Olam, whether on a weekday, Shabbat, or Yom Kippur. And so the common people.
