(Note 1) "And he returns," etc.—he fulfilled Shema, etc. In truth Rabbeinu Yonah wrote, and his language: and it appears from their words that per the opinion that blessings are indispensable one from another—meaning that if one said only one blessing, etc., and even for this one that he blessed he did not fulfill—likewise if one recites Shema without its blessings, even for Shema as required he did not fulfill. And per the opinion that blessings are not indispensable one from another, if one recited Shema without its blessings, for Shema nevertheless he fulfilled, end quote.

And since we hold that blessings are indispensable one from another, only that the order of blessings does not hold one back, as is proven in se'if 3—if so, for Shema as required he also did not fulfill. And perforce one must say that what Magen Avraham wrote that he fulfilled Shema means from the Torah; but for Shema as required in truth he did not fulfill.
