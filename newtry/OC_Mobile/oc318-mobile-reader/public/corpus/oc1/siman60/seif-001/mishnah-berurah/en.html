(1) Ahavat Olam—the explanation is that the beginning of the berakhah begins with Ahavat Olam, but the entire wording of the berakhah and its closing are as we say in the berakhah Ahavah Rabbah, even according to this view.

(2) And so is the custom—in the morning; but in the evening the custom is to say Ahavat Olam. The reason they enacted to say in the morning Ahavah Rabbah is because it is written "Renewed each morning—great is Your faithfulness." All this is lekhatchilah; but bedi'eved, even if he said the entire wording of Ahavat Olam in the morning, he has fulfilled his obligation.
