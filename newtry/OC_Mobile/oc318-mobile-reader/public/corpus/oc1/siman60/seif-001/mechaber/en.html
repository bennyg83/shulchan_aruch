Laws of the blessings for the recitation of the Shema and whether it needs intention - containing 5 sections: The second blessing is 'Ahavat Olam'. And it does not start with the word 'Baruch', since it immediately follows the 'Yotzer Ohr' blessing. As to whether it counts for the [obligation of] the blessing for [studying] Torah, see above, Siman 47. {Rama: Rem"a: There are those who say 'Ahavah Rabbah' (and this is in fact the custom in all [lands of] Ashkenaz).}
