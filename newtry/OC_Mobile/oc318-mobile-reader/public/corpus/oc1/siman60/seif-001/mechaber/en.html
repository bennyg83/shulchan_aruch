The law of the blessings for the recitation of Shema and whether they require intention. It contains 5 seifim: The second blessing is Ahavat Olam. {Rama: Some say Ahavah Rabbah, and this is the custom throughout Ashkenaz.} It does not begin with Baruch because it follows directly after the blessing Yotzer Or. Regarding whether it exempts one from Birkat HaTorah, see above, siman 47.
