And it does not begin with Baruch, etc. The correct text is, 'So, our Father, merciful Father, etc.' One need not say, 'So favor us and teach us,' because only four expressions of teaching are required, as I wrote in my commentary on the siddur. If 'So favor us and teach us' were recited, there would be five expressions of teaching. One should not say 'the mighty,' but only 'the great and awesome.' Upon reaching 'from the four corners of the earth,' he should take all four tzitzis and intend to bring illumination upon Malchus through the four tzitzis, corresponding to the four corners of the earth, for the tzitzis relate to Malchus, which is called earth. He should gather them while they are spread apart, symbolizing how the four tzitzis together illuminate Malchus; hold them in his left hand; and place them on the left side opposite his heart until the words 'and desirable forever.' Lechem Min HaShamayim.
