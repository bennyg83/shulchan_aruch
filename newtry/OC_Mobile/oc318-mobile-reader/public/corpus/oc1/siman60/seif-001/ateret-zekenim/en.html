And it does not open with "baruch," etc. It should read "ken" and "Avinu Av HaRachaman," etc., and one should not read "ken chanenu ve'talmadenu," for one needs only once the language of teaching, as written in the explanation of the siddur; and if the text read "ken chanenu ve'talmadenu," there would be five times the language of teaching. One should not read "HaGibbor" but "HaGadol" and "HaNora." When he reaches "ume'arba kanfot ha'aretz," he takes four tzitzit and intends that the four tzitzit shine upon him—which are the four corners of the earth, the tzitzit of malchut called "aretz"—and he receives them in his hand while they are spread out, as the four tzitziyot shine together the malchut; and he receives them in his left hand and places them on the left side against the heart until "unechmadim le'ad" (Lechem min haShamayim).
