Siman 61—berakhah, etc. The girsa of the Rif and Rosh and the rabbis say Ahavat Olam; the Tanna—and A"A—[says] Ahavah Rabbah, but Ahavat Olam, and KH"A says so in our books, and he ruled like the rabbis of the Tanna Kamma. And that which is stated there "hashkima lishnot," etc., and there "what is one berakhah," etc.—all is a statement of Shmuel, and he follows his reasoning; but we rule like the rabbis.

And some say, etc. From all the above it does not apply at Ma'ariv, for there it is not discussed—Tosafot and Rosh.

And it does not, etc. As written in Pesachim 104b and Berakhot 46a—and he only needed to mention there the final berakhah because Keriat Shema interrupts between them, and in the Mishnah there "one long," etc.—Rambam explained "short" that it does not begin with baruch, and so Rashba explained, and he wrote that one cannot interpret "long" and "short" according to their plain meaning, for we say Minyan Binyamin is proof, etc., and we say "he opened with deshekra," etc., even though he lengthened per the explanation of the Rif; and he further proved from the Yerushalmi not like Rashi and Tosafot, and as I wrote above siman 69.
