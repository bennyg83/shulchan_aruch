Seif 1, ‘the blessing,’ etc. The reading of the Rif and the Rosh is: ‘And the Sages say: Ahavas Olam. It was likewise taught: One may not say Ahavah Rabbah, but rather Ahavas Olam, and so Scripture states,’ etc. The text in our books contains a scribal error. He wrote that we rule like the Sages, for a baraisa supports their view. The statements there, ‘If one arose early to study,’ etc., and ‘What is the one blessing?’ etc., are all statements of Shmuel, who follows his own position. We, however, rule like the Sages.
And some say, etc. This follows from everything stated above. The evening service is different, since Tosafos and the Rosh do not discuss it there.
And it does not, etc. As stated in Pesachim 104b and Berachos 46a. There, this was necessary only regarding the concluding blessing, because Krias Shema interrupts between the blessings. In the Mishnah there, ‘one is long,’ etc., the Rambam explains that ‘short’ means a blessing that does not begin with ‘Blessed.’ The Rashba explains likewise, and writes that ‘to lengthen and to shorten’ cannot be understood literally. After all, the Gemara discusses Binyamin the shepherd, etc., and one who began with the formula for beer, etc., even though, according to the Rif's explanation, he lengthened the blessing. He further proves from the Yerushalmi against Rashi and Tosafos. See what I wrote above in siman 69.
