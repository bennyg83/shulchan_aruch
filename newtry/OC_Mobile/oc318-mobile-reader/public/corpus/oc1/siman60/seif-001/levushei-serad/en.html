Magen Avraham, seif katan 1: See siman 58. It is explained there that after four hours one recites Krias Shema without its blessings. If it were true that one does not fulfill the obligation of Krias Shema without the blessings, it would be as though he had not recited it at all. Why, then, should he recite Krias Shema after four hours?
