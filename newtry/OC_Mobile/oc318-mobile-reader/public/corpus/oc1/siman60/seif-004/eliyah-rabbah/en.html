(4) "Or proofreading," etc. So Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Rambam without qualification. It requires investigation, for in Rashba's novellae he concludes folio 12 that nevertheless intention for reading is required in every reading of Shema; and so it appears from Raavad; and likewise R. David Abudraham ruled in the name of Baal HaShlamah without qualification that one does not fulfill by reading for proofreading; and so it appears from the Gemara's discussion in the chapter He Was Reading.
