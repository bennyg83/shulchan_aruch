[4] Or proofreads, etc. Beis Yosef writes this without qualification in the name of Rambam. This requires study, for Rashba concludes in his novellae on page 12 that intent to read is at least required throughout Krias Shema, and that this also appears to be Raavad's view. Rabbi David Abudraham likewise rules without qualification, in the name of Baal HaHashlamah, that one who reads as a proofreader does not fulfill his obligation. This also appears from the Gemara's discussion in the chapter Hayah Korei.
