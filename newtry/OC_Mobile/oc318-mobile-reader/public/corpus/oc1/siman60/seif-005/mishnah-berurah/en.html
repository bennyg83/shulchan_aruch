(11) Directed his heart. The intent discussed here is not the intent discussed in seif 4. There it means intent to fulfill the obligation of the mitzvah, which is required for all the sections of Krias Shema, as the Ritva in Rosh Hashanah and the Shitah Mekubetzes in Berachos write. Here, however, it means contemplating and paying attention to what he is saying. This is indispensable only in the first verse, which contains the essential acceptance of the yoke of Heaven and the declaration of Hashem's unity. Some say that even intent to fulfill the obligation is indispensable only in the first verse.
(12) Or proofreading. Provided that he reads it according to its proper form.
