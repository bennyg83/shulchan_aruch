(11) He had kavanah in his heart—the kavanah mentioned here is not the kavanah mentioned in seif 4; for there it is kavanah to fulfill the obligation of this mitzvah for all the sections [as Ritva in Rosh Hashanah and the accepted view in Berakhot hold]—whereas this kavanah is to reflect and put upon his heart what he is saying; therefore it impedes only in the first verse, which contains the essence of accepting the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven and His unity; and some say that even kavanah to fulfill impedes only in the first verse.

(12) Or correcting [the text]—and this is when he reads as prescribed.
