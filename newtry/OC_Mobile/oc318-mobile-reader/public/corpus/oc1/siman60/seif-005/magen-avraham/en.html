He has fulfilled [his obligation]. Even though in seif 4 he ruled that mitzvot require kavanah—that is, that he intend to fulfill [the mitzvah]—nevertheless kavanah of the heart, to put upon his heart what he is saying, is not required except in the first paragraph. Beit Yosef siman 63 seif 4 in the name of Rashba. And even though they say in the Gemara: one who reads to correct [the text] has not fulfilled—this is when he does not read as prescribed, as Tur wrote; but one who reads as prescribed has fulfilled, as above. And in Rambam chapter 2 it implies that he holds that even if he did not intend to fulfill [the mitzvah], only in the first paragraph he has fulfilled—and so too is the language of Rambam; see Mishnah Berurah siman 63 seif 5.
