He has fulfilled the obligation. Although in seif 4 the Mechaber rules that mitzvos require intent, that means one must intend to fulfill the obligation. Concentration of the heart on the meaning of what one is saying is required only for the first section. See the Beit Yosef in siman 63, seif 4, in the name of the Rashba. Although the Gemara states that one reading to proofread does not fulfill the obligation, that refers to one who is not reading according to the proper rules, as Rabbeinu Yonah writes. If he reads according to the proper rules, he fulfills the obligation, as explained above. The Kesef Mishneh in chapter 2 appears to hold that even if he intended to fulfill the obligation only during the first section, he has fulfilled it. This is also implied by the Rambam's wording. See what I wrote in siman 63, seif 5.
