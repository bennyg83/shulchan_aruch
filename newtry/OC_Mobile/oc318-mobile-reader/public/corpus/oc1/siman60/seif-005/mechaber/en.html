One who recites the Shema but did not have kavanah in his heart in the first verse, which is "Shema Yisrael"—one has not fulfilled one's obligation. As for the rest, if one did not have kavanah in one's heart—even if one was reading from the Torah or correcting these sections during the time of Keriat Shema—one has fulfilled, provided one had kavanah in one's heart in the first verse.
