Magen Avraham, seif katan 4: ‘See what I wrote in siman 63, seif 5.’ His intention is to what he cited there in seif katan 8 in the name of the Rashba, that only the first verse is D'Oraisa. It is therefore understandable that even if he intended to fulfill the mitzvah only during the first verse, he has fulfilled it, because mitzvos D'Rabbanan do not require intent, as the Magen Avraham writes here in seif katan 3.
