(Magen Avraham note 4) And see what I wrote in siman 63 se'if 5—his intent is what he brought there in note 8 in the name of Rashba, that the first verse alone is from the Torah; therefore he wrote that even if one did not intend to fulfill except in the first verse one has fulfilled, for in rabbinic mitzvot intention is not required, as Magen Avraham wrote here in note 3.
