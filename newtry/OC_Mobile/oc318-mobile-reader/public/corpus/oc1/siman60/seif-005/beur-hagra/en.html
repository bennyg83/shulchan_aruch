Seif 5, ‘One who reads,’ etc. The Mechaber holds that, since the Gemara placed the entire discussion there after stating, ‘Conclude from this that mitzvos,’ etc., intent is necessarily required. The Gemara's qualification, ‘when one reads,’ etc., implies that in other cases one fulfills even when reading merely to proofread. See the Rashba in his novellae and in responsum 344, where he expresses surprise at this ruling.
