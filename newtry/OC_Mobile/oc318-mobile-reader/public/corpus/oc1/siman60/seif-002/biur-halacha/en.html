And it seems to me that it is good, etc.—see in Pri Megadim that he wrote that the intent of Shulchan Aruch here is because he is concerned for the view of Rav Hai, who rules that one does not fulfill properly without berakhot. And it is astonishing, for even according to Rav Hai the order does not impede at all, and since he returns after Keriat Shema, the berakhot fulfill the obligation of Keriat Shema properly; see Beit Yosef—and it is strained to say that this applies only if at that time he had in mind immediately to bless the berakhot, and this is discussed when he did not have it in mind. And further, Beit Yosef holds that even according to Rabbi Hai an individual fulfills, and Shulchan Aruch does not distinguish in his words. And the truth is as I brought inside in the name of the Gra. And accordingly, even if he read Keriat Shema within three hours and the berakhot he blesses after three hours, nevertheless he must read Keriat Shema again, as the Gra brought this matter from the Yerushalmi—see there—not like Pri Megadim in Magen Avraham s.k. 1.
