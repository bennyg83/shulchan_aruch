(3) And he goes back and recites, etc. Meaning, although the blessings are not indispensable to Krias Shema, and he fulfills his obligation of Krias Shema even if he did not recite any blessing at all, he nevertheless has not fulfilled his obligation regarding the blessings. He may recite them without Krias Shema, because they were not instituted specifically as blessings over Krias Shema, since one does not say in them, ‘Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us.’
(4) With the blessings. This is so that he will stand in prayer after words of Torah, and this Krias Shema is regarded as reading from the Torah.
