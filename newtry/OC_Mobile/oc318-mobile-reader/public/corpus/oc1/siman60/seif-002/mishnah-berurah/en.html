(3) And he returns and recites, etc.—meaning: even though the berakhot do not impede Keriat Shema and he fulfills the obligation of Keriat Shema even if he did not bless at all, nevertheless he has not fulfilled the obligation of the berakhot; and he may bless without Keriat Shema, for they were not instituted specifically upon Keriat Shema—for one who does not bless there is no "Who commanded us" and "venu tzivanu."

(4) With the berakhot—in order to stand in prayer from words of Torah; and this Keriat Shema is considered like one reading from the Torah.
