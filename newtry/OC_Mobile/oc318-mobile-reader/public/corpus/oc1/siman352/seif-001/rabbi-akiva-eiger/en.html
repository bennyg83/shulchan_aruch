Magen Avraham siman kaf bet: and likewise if he threw an object to reshus harabbim. The source of the law is in Rambam chapter 13 halachah 14 of Hilchot Shabbat; thus: one who throws an object from domain to domain and it was tied with his rope and bound in his hand — if he can pull the object to him he is exempt, for there is no complete placing here, and it turns out he uprooted but did not place — end of his words; and Maggid Mishneh wrote on it explained in the sugya there chapter HaZorek 102 l'chatchila; and the tanna: bound in his hand; and I ask: this is proof for bound in hand that he is exempt — nevertheless from where do we learn specifically when he can pull to him; perhaps even when he cannot pull he is exempt, for it is like part of the vessel in his hand is reshus hayachid and it is like taking out an object part of which is in reshus harabbim and part in reshus hayachid — requires study; nevertheless even for Rambam it appears specifically when the entire object is outside except the rope in his hand; but part of the object in his hand — certainly even if he cannot pull the entire object to him he is exempt, for part of the object is not in reshus harabbim; and Magen Avraham's wording is not precise:
