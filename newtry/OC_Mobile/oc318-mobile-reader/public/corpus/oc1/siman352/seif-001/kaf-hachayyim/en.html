1) [Se'if 1] One who reads from a scroll, etc. All their books were made as scrolls, similar to our Torah scrolls. Rashi; Beis Yosef; Taz, subsection 1. Know that sacred writings include even volumes of Gemara and other sacred works, and even printed works possess sanctity. Mishbetzos Zahav, end of the siman.
2) There. And one end unrolled, etc. This means that it came to rest on the ground. If it is raised even slightly above the ground, it is not at all comparable to falling, and there is no basis for a decree on account of its falling. It is permitted even in a place where no degradation would result. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1.
