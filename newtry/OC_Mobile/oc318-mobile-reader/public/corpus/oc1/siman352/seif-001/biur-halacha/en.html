because many tread — the Mechaber only mentioned Rabbah's first resolution that the Mishnah discusses a reshus hayachid threshold when it is trodden; therefore he follows Beit Yosef who agreed to Razah's view that he rejects Abaye's resolution from halachah and holds that even within 4 amot it is forbidden on a threshold that is not trodden, even on a long karmelit threshold; their reason: Rosh appears to hold like Razah from how he cited his words, and also from Rif's wording it implies somewhat so; however I searched and found many early authorities who follow Rambam's approach who brought Abaye's resolution to halachah — establishing the Mishnah on a karmelit threshold, that is, not 19 high and people do not tread on it [and there is a practical difference between rolled within 4 amot and rolled outside 4 amot]; behold Tosafos and Rashba in his novellae on Shabbos and Meiri there, and so in Or Zarua; and I found that also Rabeinu Chananel in Eruvin wrote Abaye's resolution as primary, and Gra agreed in his Biur — see there; and for Abaye's resolution the Gemara establishes specifically on a long threshold, for then we are not concerned he will bring from reshus harabbim to reshus hayachid via the threshold without intermediate placing; but otherwise it is forbidden; Taz wondered on Rambam why he did not mention this condition; see in Bach and Biur HaGra and Beit Me'ir who resolved that Rambam discusses when there was no reshus hayachid from another side of the karmelit, as somewhat implied from his wording that he did not mention threshold at all [nevertheless still difficult why he omitted from his laws the Gemara's resolution of a karmelit threshold adjacent to reshus hayachid — it is permitted only on a long one; according to what we merited now in Rabeinu Chananel's explanation all is fine, for he has a reading there of another resolution that even on one that is not long it is permitted within 4 amot; it is very possible Rambam had this reading and ruled so because the Mishnah teaches anonymously];
