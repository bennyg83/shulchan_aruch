So too Maggid Mishneh there, that it refers to karmelit etc.; it appears his intent is that Maggid Mishneh there brought Abaye's resolution on a karmelit threshold and it fell to reshus harabbim within 4 amot — if it fell and he brings it there is no chatat liability etc.; and from his words it is proven that in other matters we are concerned it fell and he brings from reshus harabbim to the threshold which is karmelit; from this Rama learned there in gloss end of seif 1 "even if it only fell to karmelit" — that it refers to Shulchan Aruch discussing a reshus hayachid threshold and it fell to reshus harabbim; he holds to permit even in this like Rabbah's resolution there; therefore he wrote that in other matters it is forbidden even if it fell from a reshus hayachid threshold to karmelit, for we are concerned he may bring from karmelit to reshus harabbim; and likewise we forbid because of the decree he may bring from reshus harabbim to karmelit; likewise we forbid lest he bring from karmelit to reshus harabbim; this is what Magen Avraham wrote there, that it refers to karmelit — that is, there it discusses a karmelit threshold; therefore specifically when it rested in reshus harabbim it is forbidden even though there are two leniencies: one that it is bound in his hand, and we must decree lest it fell and he comes to bring — which is like a decree upon a decree, for even if he brings it to karmelit; unlike when it did not rest at all in reshus harabbim where there is another leniency and we learn that the same applies if it fell to karmelit and did not rest but hangs in the air — it is permitted; and in the explanation of the Gaon R' Eliyahu of Vilna he wrote: and in Maggid Mishneh it was not mentioned that it fell to karmelit; on the contrary, there it implies it fell to reshus harabbim since he says even within 4 amot, and in karmelit there is no distinction — only Beit Yosef and Darkei Moshe think it refers to Rambam's words and it is not so — end of his words; and I wonder, for also Rambam discusses that it fell to reshus harabbim only that he was reading in karmelit; and they properly learned from this that the same applies conversely: standing in reshus hayachid and it fell to karmelit — in other objects it is forbidden as he wrote; and what he wrote there that Rambam did not mention threshold at all but "one reading a scroll in karmelit" — that is, all domains are karmelit and for him there is no distinction between scroll and other things; and even for Rashba specifically in Abaye's case, but in the prior case of karmelit there is no concern and every thing is permitted — end of his words. I wonder, for certainly Rambam wrote karmelit to say the domain where he stands is karmelit so it should not be difficult lest he brings in reshus harabbim and brings to reshus hayachid since he did not rule like Ben Azai as he wrote; nevertheless the domain where it fell is reshus harabbim as Rambam wrote explicitly — part of the scroll rolled to reshus harabbim; this is literally Abaye's resolution; and what he wrote that in Rashba's novellae on Shabbos he wrote not specifically scroll — see in Sefer Machzik Berachah who brought from Rashba's words on Eruvin manuscript that initially he also wrote perhaps the same applies to other things, only from the authors' words it implies scroll specifically; and it may be said other things are forbidden lest all of it fell in reshus harabbim and he brings to reshus hayachid; and for scroll alone there is no decree because unspecified sacred writings one examines them and leaves them — end of his words; study, for according to what we rule not like Ben Azai there is concern lest one examine them there etc.; however in his explanation he wrote Rashba ruled like Ben Azai since it is a rabbinic matter — see there; accordingly also in other things there is no concern lest one examine (and it is possible in other things there is concern lest one throw — specifically sacred writings that one does not throw as explained there); and what Magen Avraham wrote "and likewise in gloss end of seif 1 that it refers to reshus harabbim" is puzzling; it is possible he means therefore Rama omitted in his words Maggid Mishneh's distinction between rested or not because it refers to reshus harabbim — that is, the threshold is reshus hayachid and it fell to reshus harabbim; in such a case even if it did not rest there is concern lest he bring from reshus harabbim to reshus hayachid; and although he concludes "even if it only fell to karmelit" this wording by itself implies fell to karmelit and rested there; and in Peri Megadim emend that it refers to reshus hayachid; accordingly it may be said as I wrote that therefore he does not distinguish because it discusses a reshus hayachid threshold; unlike when it refers to a karmelit threshold; it is more correct to emend this wording; and likewise in gloss seif 2 it implies it refers to reshus harabbim and he means Bach cited the words "likewise" in seif 2 of one reading on the roof discussing standing in reshus hayachid and it rolled to reshus harabbim as explained — in my humble opinion:
