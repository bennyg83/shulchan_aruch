(א) One who reads in a scroll etc. All books made in early days were made in a scroll like our Torah scroll:

(ב) he rolls it back to him. In the Gemara last chapter of Eruvin daf 98 we ask: that which is permitted to roll it back to him although it rests on the ground — we do not decree lest all of it fall to the ground and he come to bring 4 amot in reshus harabbim because of disgrace to sacred writings; and why is it forbidden in seif 2 at the end regarding one reading on the roof if the wall is sloped etc. — forbidden to roll it back because of this decree; and Rabbah answers: here in the beginning we discuss a trodden threshold, and because of disgrace to sacred writings — Rashi: the threshold is always reshus hayachid but trodden — the foot of the many passes through there; and where people's feet tread there is disgrace to sacred writings; therefore they permitted rolling it back to him; it is not comparable to reading on the roof where there is not such disgrace; but in this case since it is bound in his hand and it is mere shevut they did not decree — end of his words; and this is what Rama wrote: many tread etc.; and if so it implies that also on a threshold there is prohibition to roll it back if it is not trodden by the many; and why did Tur permit anonymously on a threshold — it implies every threshold is trodden; the Shulchan Aruch should have mentioned it discusses trodden, in the Gemara's wording; and so in Asheri and Remez; also on Rif it is difficult that he did not mention trodden at all; also on Rambam who Beit Yosef brought rules like Abaye who holds specifically on a karmelit threshold it is permitted; and in the Gemara we say that also in karmelit there is concern lest he take out from reshus hayachid to reshus harabbim and it must be established in the Gemara specifically on a long threshold; and Rambam did not mention this but permitted anonymously on a karmelit threshold; and although in the Gemara there is another resolution — that is, anonymous sacred writings one examines them — we do not rule like that resolution since it follows Ben Azai and we do not rule like Ben Azai, as Razah wrote — Rosh brought there;
