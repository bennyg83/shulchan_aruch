[1] The degradation of sacred writings, etc. Nevertheless, if the sacred writing fell entirely into a karmelit, it is prohibited to roll it toward oneself, because a rabbinic prohibition remains in force even for sacred writings, as stated by the Bach.
[2] He rolls it toward himself, etc. With other objects this is prohibited. However, when the object is suspended in the air of a karmelit, one end remains in his hand, and three separate leniencies are present, even other objects are permitted, as stated by the Magen Avraham.
