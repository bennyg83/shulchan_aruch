(א) he rolls it — Taz; Eruvin 98a asks: threshold reshus hayachid and stands reshus harabbim etc., and not specifically in reshus harabbim; rather that Rabbi Yehuda is not established there, and Rabbah (or Rava as the reading appears — see Rosh) in karmelit because it appeared to them the Mishnah anonymously even rolled outside 4 amot unlike the braita; therefore it must be on a trodden threshold; however it may be said that therefore it is not established so, for if so why specifically sacred writings — for Rava there would not be a decree upon a decree; but according to what Mishna Berurah wrote in gloss on other matters it is forbidden even to karmelit — Beit Yosef brought; if so why not establish so? It may be said we do not establish karmelit threshold because it is still difficult lest he brings from reshus harabbim to reshus hayachid; and why does the tanna hold teaching 1 long threshold — for Rava they decreed even two stories; teaching 2 Ben Azai we do not rule so — see in Rosh; and that it is not established standing karmelit — if so he should distinguish between threshold and roof; it may be said anonymous threshold is karmelit or trodden; Taz questioned Tur why he did not conclude specifically trodden as Mishna Berurah wrote; and on Rambam chapter 15 what he established like Abaye — therefore teaching 1 long threshold; and why did he not mention this. It is possible Rambam ruled like the braita: outside 4 amot no; this is not like Rabbah (or Rava), and so that the Mishnah's anonymous wording should be halachah necessarily we are not concerned for the difficulty "if so" and we are not concerned lest he brings from reshus harabbim to reshus hayachid, only concerned lest he brings to the place where he stands — for we rule like Rava that even two stories it is not liable as Rosh wrote in the name of Razah, and a long threshold does not help; nevertheless see there:
