If he was reading in it on the roof and one end rolled from his hand — until it did not reach the lower 10 tefachim adjacent to the ground he rolls it back to him; if it reached the lower 10 tefachim — if the wall is sloped such that it rests on it, it is forbidden to roll it back to him; and so it will not stand in disgrace he turns it onto the writing; if it is not sloped, as long as it has not reached the ground he rolls it back to him:
