(א) he rolls it back to him. Even if it rested on a ziz — if 4 wide it is reshus hayachid, and if not 4 wide it is a makom patur — see siman 345 siman 10: he rolls it /to him/, but other matters are forbidden, for even in karmelit when bound in his hand it is forbidden as written seif 1, all the more so in the air of reshus harabbim there is concern lest they fall and he bring them — as Ran wrote, and so siman 354 seif 1; however in the air of karmelit and bound in his hand — there are three leniencies; even in other matters it is permitted — so Maggid Mishneh wrote there, that it refers to karmelit; and likewise in gloss seif 1; and what Bach wrote in siman 354 that even in the air of reshus harabbim it is permitted if bound in his hand — see there: his words contradict themselves, for he wrote afterward that l'chatchila it is forbidden — see there; otherwise why need a pole 10 high; therefore the Tur also discusses a window 10 high, otherwise it is impossible to fill since the pole is 10 high — then water will come from above downward and pour; therefore the window must be high above 10 — see there and it is via makom patur; but via the air of reshus harabbim it is forbidden; and Ritva there is inclined like Hagahot Ashiri's view that scroll and other things are equal; therefore he questioned there; but already seif 1 ruled like Rashba and his camp that it is forbidden; and so the Tur; and what he omitted in siman 354 is because it is evident it discusses a window above 10 — as written see siman 353:
