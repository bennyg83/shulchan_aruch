(א) on the roof — discusses a roof that is reshus hayachid with reshus harabbim before it; however as long as it did not reach the lower 10 tefachim the air is a makom patur and therefore he rolls it back to him:

(ב) he rolls it back to him — and even if the house wall was sloped and it rested on it, or rested there on a ziz whether the ziz was 4 wide or not — in all this it is permitted according to all, even in other matters that are not sacred writings, such as when he had a sheet in hand and one end rolled from his hand; for above 10 tefachim there is no concern because of reshus harabbim:

(ג) if the wall is sloped — that is, then it is like one end resting on the ground; therefore if we are lenient here there is concern lest all of it fall from his hand there and he also come to bring from reshus harabbim to reshus hayachid:

(ד) that rested on it — and specifically when many lean on it; if not so it is karmelit, and since it is bound in his hand it is permitted to bring it to him in sacred writings according to all; see in Biur Halachah:

(ה) that did not reach the ground — and even if there is only a needle's fullness between it and the ground, since it did not actually rest on the ground and hangs in the air it is not called placing and he rolls it back to him; and specifically in sacred writings we are lenient, but in other matters whenever it reached the air of the 10 tefachim which is the air of reshus harabbim — even if it did not rest — it is forbidden to bring it to him, for we decree lest all of it fall to the ground and he also come to bring it to him; and some are lenient in this; and all this in the air of reshus harabbim; but if it fell to the air of karmelit it is permitted according to all to bring it to him since it is still bound in his hand:

(ו) he rolls it back to him — and in Biur HaGra he disagrees on this that whatever reached within 3 adjacent to the ground is considered as placed on the ground; see in Biur Halachah:
