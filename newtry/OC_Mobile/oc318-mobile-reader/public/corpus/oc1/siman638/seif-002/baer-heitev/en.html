Foods. If he knows that children will eat from them, it is better not to hang them. Sefer Chasidim, siman 263. It seems to me that he should make a condition about them. Magen Avraham. But only if they will not detach the strings.
For they are set aside. Regarding something set aside for its mitzvah, even R. Shimon agrees that it is forbidden.
The first. For otherwise, since the prohibition takes effect during the first twilight, it automatically takes effect during all of them, for in this respect it is like one long day, and a condition no longer helps.
To stipulate. Because people are not properly expert in conditions.
To stipulate. The Taz wrote: it seems to me to be lenient with painted sheets when there is concern because of thieves; we are witnesses that he did not hang them there from the outset to remain there permanently, and therefore they were not set aside at all, even if he did not stipulate; see there. Maharil wrote that it is permitted to hang shatnez curtains, provided they hang above human use. The Darchei Moshe wrote: one should not tie the decorations, because it is forbidden to untie them on Yom Tov; rather, he should make a bow. See siman 317. Those who engrave the verse "in sukkot you shall dwell" on a gourd do not act properly, as written in Yoreh De'ah siman 283 in the Beit Yosef and Taz. Even after Sukkot one should not step on sukkah wood, for it is an article used for a mitzvah, like tzitzit and lulav. Magen Avraham. See siman 21, small paragraph 2, where I wrote in the name of the Taz that with sukkah roofing it is good not to use it afterward for a purpose that is not respectful to the mitzvah that has passed; see there.
