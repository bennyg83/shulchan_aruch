A condition. Since it is impossible to remove it during twilight because he would be dismantling a tent, it is set aside for all seven days. This is unlike sukkah decorations in seif 2, where a condition is effective; see Magen Avraham.
Adjacent. If he leaned wood against the sukkah in order to thicken it, even after it was completed, it is forbidden, for it is no worse than sukkah decorations, unless he stipulated. Yam Shel Shlomo; see Magen Avraham.
It is legally effective. This is difficult, for in siman 42, seif 3, he wrote that designation for the body of sanctity is legally effective. One can answer that articles of a mitzvah are more lenient. It seems to me that one who has a sukkah built from year to year, nevertheless it is not forbidden until he sits in it during the festival. Although he already sat in it the previous year, when the festival passed its sanctity lapsed, and another act is needed for sanctity to take effect. Magen Avraham.
