[1] The wood of the sukkah etc. — wood adjacent to the sukkah is permitted; see siman 518 seif 8 (Rama), implying even for a mitzvah sukkah, for that is what is discussed here, and the same applies to bundles on the sekhakh; except Taz distinguishes there by his own reasoning and it is unclear; but Levush forbids in all cases, and that is from Beit Yosef; however Yafeh Shel Shlomo and Magen Avraham explain like Rama.
