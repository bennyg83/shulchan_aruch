In the Shulchan Aruch: the wood of the sukkah, etc. See Birkei Yosef in the name of Mahari Molcho: one who exchanged one sukkah for another has not removed the sanctity of the first. The proof is from siman 668, regarding one who designated seven etrogim, which implies specifically when he designated them, for that is like stipulating. The second sukkah, if he used it for the sake of sukkah, has sanctity take effect upon it as well; but if he did not use it, and only said, "this is in exchange for that," sanctity does not take effect upon it, for designation is not legally effective.
