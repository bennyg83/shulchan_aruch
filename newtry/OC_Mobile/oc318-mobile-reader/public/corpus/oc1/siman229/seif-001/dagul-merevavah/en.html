And in my opinion the entire day is the time, and 'morning' that Rambam said is because of zeal; and even if we say 'morning' specifically, perhaps to exclude evening — meaning from the hour the sun inclines westward; and if so, in any case until noon one blesses there's name and kingship; and so I practiced in the year 5545 when the sun did not shine until an hour before noon — and I blessed there's name and kingship.
