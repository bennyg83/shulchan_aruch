(1) One who sees the rainbow etc. — and it is not worthwhile to tell one's fellow that there is a rainbow because of motzi shem ra [Chayei Adam].

(2) One says etc. — and here if he saw it again, even within 30 days, he returns and blesses; and it is not similar to those where we hold once a month suffices to bless, for here the rainbow on which he blessed has passed and gone — and it is similar to blessing on thunder [Shaarei Teshuvah in the name of Birkei Yosef].

(3) Faithful etc. — Rambam and Tur have the text 'and faithful'.

(4) To His covenant — meaning that He will not destroy us even though the wicked have multiplied; and fulfills His word — even though there was no covenant, since He spoke plainly and said there will no longer be a flood to destroy the earth — it is clear He will fulfill His word [Abudraham].

(5) And it is forbidden to gaze etc. — and one who gazes excessively — his eyes grow dim [Chagigah 16]; rather he sees and blesses.
