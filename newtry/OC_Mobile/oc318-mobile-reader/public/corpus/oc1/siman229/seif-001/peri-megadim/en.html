Faithful — Taz, Berachos 59a; and there is no closing here as Bach wrote that all agree there is no Baruch in the closing; but in the opening there must be, for it is not adjacent; and the world makes two errors in the opening: Baruch and in the closing they say v'ne'eman etc. In Gemara and Tur 'and faithful' with vav; and so Rambam chapter 10 of Laws of Blessings, Taz, and in Shulchan Aruch 'faithful' without vav. See Levush who brought: when one did not see for three days in rainy days — sun, moon and stars — one blesses; and they do not practice thus; and even without Heaven's name and kingship — not at all. One who examines Berachos 59b and Rambam chapter 10 halacha 18 and Radbaz halacha 2 and responsa Mishnah Berurah 101 — it appears we rule like R' Yehoshua regarding this matter: in Nisan the world was created; and Machzor Gadol end of 28 years concludes in Nisan and immediately begins the start of the next Machzor Gadol on the night of Wednesday of the days of the week; and always the tekufah is on the night of Wednesday, in the morning on Wednesday in the first year of Machzor Gadol in Nisan one blesses Blessed are You Heaven our Heaven Who makes the work of Creation; and like R' Eliezer we rule for years of the world from Tishrei — see there; and Taz here.
