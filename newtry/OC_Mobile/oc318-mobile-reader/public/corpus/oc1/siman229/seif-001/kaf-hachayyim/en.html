1) [Seif 1] One who sees a rainbow, etc. One should not tell another person that a rainbow is present, because this spreads a negative report. Chayei Adam, rule 63; Pischei Teshuvah; Orach Chaim, paragraph 1.
2) There: One who sees a rainbow, etc. One who recited the blessing over a rainbow and saw another within thirty days recites the blessing again. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 1, proves this and rejects the view that one does not recite it within thirty days; see there. Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1.
