Seif katan 1, "faithful." Those who say "faithful," etc., beforehand, meaning that they say, "Faithful to His covenant and steadfast in His word, Blessed are You, Hashem, who remembers the covenant," are mistaken, because it is explicit in the Gemara that this blessing does not conclude with a separate blessing formula. Here the text should read Shelah, because Shelah wrote this. What was printed as the word Shelah at the end of seif katan 2 is an error, for the Gemara explicitly states in Chagigah 16a: "Whoever does not have concern for the honor of his Creator, it would have been better for him not to have come into existence." The Gemara asks what this refers to, and R' Abba says: one who gazes at a rainbow. It further says there that R' Yehudah bar Nachmani, the interpreter of Reish Lakish, expounded: anyone who gazes at 3 things has his eyesight dimmed, including a rainbow, etc.
