Note 1 — faithful. And those who say faithful etc. first — meaning they say faithful to His covenant and steadfast in His word, Blessed are You Who remembers the covenant — err, for it is clear in the Gemara that one does not close this blessing. And here must be Shelah — for Shelah wrote thus. And what is printed 'Shelah' in note 2 is an error — for the Gemara is arranged in Chagigah page 16a: we learned whoever is not concerned for his Creator's honor — it is fitting he should not have come to the world; and they said what is it — R' Abba said: this is one who gazes at the rainbow; and they said further there R' Yehuda bar Nachmani, interpreter of Resh Lakish: all who gaze at three things — their eyes grow dim: the rainbow etc.
