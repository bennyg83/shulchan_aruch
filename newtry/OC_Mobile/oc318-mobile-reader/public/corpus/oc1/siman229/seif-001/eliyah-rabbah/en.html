(1) In his statement etc. In Raavan siman 200 he wrote the formula: Blessed are You Heaven our Heaven King of the universe, there faithful in His word and steadfast in His covenant, Blessed are You Heaven Who remembers the covenant — until here. And from this it is difficult regarding what Perishah and Bach wrote in the name of Rashal that according to all poskim there is no closing in this blessing, and therefore they wondered why he was accustomed to close; and he strongly criticized them; and according to what I wrote they have whom to rely on; and Beit Yosef who wrote according to all there is no closing etc. — meaning it refers to Rif and Tosafos mentioned earlier. And in any case for practical halacha the essence is our formula as Levush wrote. In the Talmud chapter Ein Dorshin: one who gazes at the rainbow — his eyes grow dim. One left the synagogue after the prayer leader said HaEl HaKadosh and blessed on the rainbow — even though engaged in a mitzvah of answering amen after the prayer leader — nevertheless the blessing on the rainbow takes precedence because it passes (Sefer Chasidim 964).
