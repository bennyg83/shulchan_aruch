Faithful to His covenant etc. In the Gemara: what does one bless — Blessed Who remembers the covenant; in the Mishnah it taught faithful to His covenant etc.; Rashi explained therefore we teach both etc.; and those who ascend err to say first faithful to His covenant and afterward Blessed are You Who remembers the covenant — and this is not halacha, for there is no closing here at all; rather one must begin Blessed are You Heaven our Heaven Who remembers the covenant, and afterward faithful to His covenant etc.
