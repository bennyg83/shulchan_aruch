In the morning — meaning immediately at sunrise; and so Maharil commanded to announce in the evening that tomorrow everyone should be careful — when he sees sunrise he should bless; and Mishnah Berurah wrote that at Shacharit when leaving the synagogue the congregation gathers together and blesses this blessing — thus practiced Maharash; and so is my opinion to practice, there's help — until here; and Magen Avraham wrote and it appears to me that if he did not bless until after three hours he does not bless, for the sun has already passed from this place.

The remaining — they are Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury.
