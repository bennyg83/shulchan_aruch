(6) At its tekufah — the place to which it returns at the beginning of the cycle is the time of hanging the luminaries at the time of creation; and since then it began to circle and serve, and this continues 28 years until it comes to that same place precisely at the beginning of the night of Wednesday as at the time of creation.
(7) And the tekufah — meaning the tekufah of Nisan.
(8) In the morning — meaning l'chatchila its mitzvah is immediately to precede as much as possible; and it is good to bless with a large congregation; and b'dieved until three hours of the day [Levush and Magen Avraham]; but many later authorities agreed that one may bless at time of pressure until noon — and this means even there's name and kingship; and if the sun was covered in clouds and it is not seen — see in Shaarei Teshuvah. And in responsa Chatam Sofer siman 56 he wrote that if it was covered in clouds but its outline is seen between the clouds — one blesses; but when it is not seen at all — it does not seem to bless. And see there that he wrote his custom regarding this blessing: before the blessing they said Hallelu et Heaven min haShamayim; and after the blessing Who makes the work of Creation they said the piyyut El Adon al kol haMaasim until v'Chayot HaKodesh; and afterward Mizmor haShamayim mesaprim kevod Kel; and afterward Aleinu leShabeach and Kaddish.
(9) And similarly one blesses etc. — and nowadays the world does not practice this.
(10) The remaining — they are Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury.
