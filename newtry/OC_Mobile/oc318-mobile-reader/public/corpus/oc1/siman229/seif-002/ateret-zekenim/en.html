One who sees the sun etc. and it is every 28 years etc.; and so ruled in responsa Masat Binyamin siman 101. However there is another explanation for the sun at its tekufah and stars and constellations in their order — in rainy days when the three luminaries are overcast and the sun, moon and stars are not seen in them, one must bless on them afterward when they are seen Blessed are You Who makes the work of Creation (Beit Yosef in the name of Hagahot Maimon); and so in Aruch under the entry 'chamah' — two explanations: meaning what Shulchan Aruch wrote every 28 years to 28 years, and also another explanation in rainy days at the time of rain when the three luminaries were overcast; but I saw in Krakow community that they practiced thus at the beginning of the 28-year cycle — the entire congregation blesses Blessed are You Who makes the work of Creation per the command of the geonim rabbis; and so I heard they practiced in all communities of Israel.
