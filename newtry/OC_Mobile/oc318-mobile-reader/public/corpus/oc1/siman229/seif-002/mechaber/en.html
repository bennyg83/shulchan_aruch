One who sees the sun at its tekufah — and that is every 28 years to 28 years, and the tekufah is at the beginning of the night' of Wednesday — when he sees it on Wednesday morning says Blessed [are You Heaven...] Who makes the work of Creation; and one blesses similarly when seeing the moon in its purity, the stars in their watches, and the constellations in their times — meaning when the moon returns at the beginning of the constellation of Aries at the beginning of the month and is not inclined neither northward nor southward; and similarly when each of the five remaining [planets] returns to the beginning of the constellation of Aries and is not inclined neither northward nor southward; and similarly whenever one sees the constellation of Aries rising from the eastern edge.
