(2) [Levush] Three hours etc. Lechem Chamudos wrote: I do not know whence he has this — until here. And see below in laws of Rosh Chodesh in calculation of tekufot he wrote that on Wednesday three hours of the day the luminaries were hung — see there; and Meil Tzvi Yom Tov wrote: and in any case it appears to me that one can bless thus all day and at least until noon — until here. And this is unlike Magen Avraham who wrote not to bless after three; and so in Sefer Tzeda Laderech page 61 implies like Meil Tzvi Yom Tov; and in any case l'chatchila they should bless when leaving the synagogue at Shacharit when the congregation gathers together.
