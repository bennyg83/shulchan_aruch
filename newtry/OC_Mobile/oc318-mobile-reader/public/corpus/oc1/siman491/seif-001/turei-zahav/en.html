For on Yom Tov it is permitted to kindle a candle; and not over the spices, for on Motza'ei Shabbos they recite a blessing over them because of the additional soul that has departed, and on Yom Tov there is no additional soul; and on Yom Tov that falls on Motza'ei Shabbos they do not recite a blessing, because there are good foods that settle his mind like spices.
