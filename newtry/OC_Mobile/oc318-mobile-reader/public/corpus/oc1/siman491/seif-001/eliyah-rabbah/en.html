On Motzaei Yom Tov, etc. One who began eating on Motzaei Pesach while it was still daytime and continued until night, but had not yet prayed Maariv or recited Havdalah, may eat chametz. Once darkness has fallen, it is night for all purposes. The Sages prohibited only performing labor until Havdalah is recited. Magen Avraham.
