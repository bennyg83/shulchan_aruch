And he did not write here that on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into Yom Tov one does not recite a blessing over spices; and so he wrote in siman 473 regarding YaKNeHaZ, and [regarding] spices, no; and in the Beis Yosef one can explain this somewhat. And from Yom Tov to weekday they say the verses of salvation, Hinei El Yeshuasi; and so it is implied in the Levush, that he does not change [anything] except that one does not say [the blessings over] candle and spices; and what of it, for there is no blessing in vain here; and see siman 188 in the Magen Avraham, letter 11. One who observes a dream-fast on Shabbos: on Motza'ei Shabbos he smells spices, even according to the Mechaber in siman 624; and see the Magen Avraham and the Taz there, [note] 1, that he does not bless because they were fasting; nevertheless regarding an ordinary Shabbos they did not distinguish, and the Mechaber agrees. And one who fasts on Rosh Hashanah, and the first day falls on Motza'ei Shabbos: one can say that he also recites a blessing over the spices; and see the Magen Avraham, siman 597, that one should not fast two consecutive days and nights on Rosh Hashanah; see there.
And know. Regarding one with a blocked nose who does not smell, [the question is] whether one who recites a blessing over spices intending to exempt others [may do so]; see siman 294 in the Taz and the Magen Avraham. On Motza'ei Shabbos of Yom Kippur and of Shabbos, even according to our practice of smelling, there is no blessing in vain, as the Taz wrote in siman 624; which is not so for one with a blocked nose who does not smell: he should not recite the blessing, lest the halacha follow the Mechaber that on Motza'ei Shabbos and on Motza'ei Yom Kippur one has no obligation of spices at all; and this is obvious; and see Eliyah Rabbah, siman 624, and with God's help it will be explained there. Vayiten Lecha they say on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into weekday, or on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into Chol HaMoed, but not on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into Yom Tov, and not from Yom Tov leading into weekday. And Tehillim 67, Elokim Yechoneinu, and the like that they say in the synagogue on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into weekday: even on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into Chol HaMoed they say them; but on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into Yom Tov they do not say them, for it is a supplication; see the Levush at the end of siman 490 and siman 494; see there; and so it is proper to practice in this.
What [the Mechaber] wrote. The Mechaber [writes that] they say Vetodi'einu; see siman 599; on Rosh Hashanah they say Vetodi'einu and Chagigas HaRegel, for such is the wording; see there in the Taz; and in my book of customs we have written about this.
