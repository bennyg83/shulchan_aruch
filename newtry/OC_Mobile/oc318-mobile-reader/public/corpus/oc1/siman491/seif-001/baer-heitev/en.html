If one began eating on Motzaei Pesach while it was still daytime and continued until night, but had not yet prayed or recited Havdalah, it requires study whether he may eat chametz. It appears that he may eat it, because once night has fallen it is a weekday in every respect. Magen Avraham. Chok Yaakov also rules this way without qualification. Minhagim writes that on Motzaei Shabbos during Chol HaMoed, Vihi Noam and VeAtah Kadosh are not recited. Veyiten Lecha is recited. See Eliyah Zuta.
