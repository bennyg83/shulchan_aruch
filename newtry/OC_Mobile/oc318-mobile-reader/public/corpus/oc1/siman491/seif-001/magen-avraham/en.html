And not over the spices. For there is no additional soul (neshamah yeseirah) on Yom Tov; and as for the fact that they do not recite a blessing over spices on Motza'ei Shabbos leading into Yom Tov, it is because the joy of Yom Tov restores the soul. And one who began to eat on Motza'ei Pesach while it was still day, and continued until night, and has not yet prayed and made havdalah: this requires further study whether he is permitted to eat chametz; for at first glance it appears that he is permitted to eat, for once it has grown dark, night is weekday for all matters; only that the Sages said that it is forbidden to do melacha until he makes havdalah in the prayer; but the prohibition of chametz does not depend on havdalah, for even on Chol HaMoed it is forbidden to eat chametz; and therefore once it has grown dark, it is permitted to eat chametz; and see also the end of siman 263.
