1) [Se'if 1] He recites Havdalah in the prayer as on Motzaei Shabbos, etc. There is a scribal error here. The Tur states: 'Maariv is recited as on Motzaei Shabbos, and Havdalah is recited over a cup in the manner in which it is recited on Motzaei Shabbos, except, etc.' Biurei HaGra. This is also the text in the old edition of Shulchan Aruch printed in the year 5327.
