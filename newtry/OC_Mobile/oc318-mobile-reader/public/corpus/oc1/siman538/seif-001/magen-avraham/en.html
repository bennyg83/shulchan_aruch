The Nimukei Yosef wrote regarding seeds that had not soaked, in the name of the Raavad, that if there was ones, even mere profit is permitted on the moed; but when he forgot or was lazy, profit is forbidden; and this requires further study; see Beis Yosef at the beginning of siman 537. Between the small ones. And in the Gemara there is a dispute, and he ruled like the words of both of them to be lenient (Beis Yosef; and this requires further study, for in siman 504 he wrote in the name of the Rosh that we do not say regarding rabbinic matters follow the lenient view, since each one is lenient on one side; and so wrote Tosafos; and this requires further study to distinguish between them).
