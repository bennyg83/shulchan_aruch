The writing of N. Gabi Zeraim that they did not drink, etc. is a law mentioned in Sha'ar Ras 1177. Indeed, if he started to water it before the time, then it is permissible to water it at a time that fades, the lost thing if it is not watered at the time. But if he didn't start watering it before the deadline, watering it at the deadline is nothing more than a forbidden spirit. AZK HNI in the name of the Rabbid to divide.
