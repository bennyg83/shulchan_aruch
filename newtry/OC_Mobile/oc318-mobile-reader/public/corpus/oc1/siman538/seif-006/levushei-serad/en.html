end of seif 548. His intention is to explain the words of the Mechaber in siman 548 seif 1, that when the seventh day falls on erev Yom Tov, since part of the day is like all of it, one may cut hair on erev Yom Tov; and if so, if he did not shave on erev Yom Tov it is forbidden to shave on chol ha-moed—this teaches here that if it was Shabbat and he could not shave on erev Yom Tov, he may shave on chol ha-moed.
