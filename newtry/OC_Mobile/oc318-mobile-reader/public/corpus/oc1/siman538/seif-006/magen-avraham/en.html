A davar haaved. But regarding something that would not be lost, it is obvious that it is forbidden to do on Chol HaMoed; and if he did something that would not be lost on Chol HaMoed, even for his son the Sages imposed a fine (Nimukei Yosef), and they cause it to be lost from him; and this is specifically according to the one who holds that melacha of Chol HaMoed is according to the Torah (Rabbeinu Yeruham). And it appears to me that it is similar to one who does melacha on Shabbos, to whom it is forever forbidden, and the same applies to his son; and see siman 318 and siman 530; and this requires further study in Gittin chapter 5 and in Yoreh Deah siman 99.
