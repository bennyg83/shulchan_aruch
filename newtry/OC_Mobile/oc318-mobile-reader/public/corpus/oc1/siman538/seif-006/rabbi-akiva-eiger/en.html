Seif 6: We do not fine him after. It is possible if his father did not do the melacha — the law is he should not do so to "levar bevaro" — but if he already did, the law is dempeirotim — it is possible if he died, the son does not inherit and is not better than another, since it already became hefker. And for the Mechaber's intent if it is a lost item he need not fine if his father did the melacha — if so, even if his father did, the son is not liable after him.
