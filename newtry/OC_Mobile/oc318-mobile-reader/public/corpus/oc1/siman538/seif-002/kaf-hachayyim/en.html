2) There. One who turned over his olives, etc. Rambam, chapter 7, holds that even initially one may turn them over. His reason is that once they were placed in the storage vat and the time for turning them arrived, failure to turn them would create a matter subject to loss. Raavad disputes this, and Maggid Mishneh writes a resolution of Rambam's position. See there. Beis Yosef cites them. Ritva writes in his novellae that perhaps Rambam speaks of the second turning. Alternatively, in Rambam's locality, the turning itself was part of preventing a loss. Everything depends upon the place and time. See there. Erech HaShulchan, subsection 1; Pischei Olam, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah in Biur Halachah, cite this. See below, se'if 5.
