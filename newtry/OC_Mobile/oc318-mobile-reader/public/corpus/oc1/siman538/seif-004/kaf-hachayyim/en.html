4) There. Or his wine was in the pit, etc. The vessel into which the wine falls from the winepress is called the pit. Rashi, Moed Katan 12a. Nimukei Yosef on Rif writes that the pit is an excavation beneath the winepress, plastered inside with lime so that not a drop is lost, into which the wine flows from the press. Rashi there gives a similar explanation. It is possible that some people use one arrangement and others the other. Alternatively, one who ordinarily makes much wine constructs an excavation and structure in the ground, while one who does not ordinarily make much wine places a vessel into which the wine falls.
