(Magen Avraham s.k. 14) And what he wonders about the Tur. Note: to me this is not difficult. The Tur forbids it in eating because he considered it significant. Even according to one who does not hold of that rationale, nevertheless it is forbidden in eating because perhaps they were not well charred. But regarding the mixtures he follows his own approach, for he concludes at the end of siman 447 that it is permitted initially to mix chametz with intent to keep it, and so he wrote in siman 442 according to the Rif's approach. This is the view of the latter opinion that the Rosh brought. However, the Magen Avraham's explanation requires study: how is it proven that it is forbidden in benefit from the fact that it did not teach that it is permitted in eating? It seems that this is the explanation: the first view holds that morais is permitted in eating for this reason, because actual chametz through a mixture is permitted in benefit, and charred chametz before its time, even when intact, is permitted in benefit; therefore charred chametz through a mixture is raised one level and is permitted even in eating. The other view disagrees and proves that it is forbidden in eating, for if so it should have stated the greater novelty, that charred chametz before its time is permitted in eating. According to this, the Tur's words are correct and precise.
