(Magen Avraham s.k. 14) And wonder on Tur. Note: to me the key: Tur forbids eating because he designates it even for one who does not hold that view — nevertheless forbidden in eating lest not charred well; but regarding mixture he follows his conclusion 447 end permitting to mix l'chatchila to retain — likewise 442 in Rif's method; this is the "some say" Rosh brought — however Magen Avraham strained to learn how forbidden in benefit is proven from not writing permitted in eating — appears first view: morayis permitted in eating because full chametz through mixture permitted in benefit after Pesach and charring before its time even b'eyna permitted in benefit and charring through mixture adds one lenient level permitted even in eating — disagrees — if so greater novelty charring before its time permitted in eating; therefore Tur's words are correct.
