(א) Seif 3 — clothes, etc. He learned from kilyorot that one may retain them even though absorbed — this is the view of those who are stringent in the gloss; but the first view in the gloss holds as Maggid Mishneh who learned from the kneading-trough to which he put hides; likewise Rambam's language that there is no form, etc., and as in seif 1 regarding kilyor for it is spoiled, etc., and there the chametz is b'eyna.

(ב) And likewise. Like kilyor, etc.; and it appears for kolan shel sofrim there it deals before the papers were glued. Nevertheless.

(ג) And therefore. From Maggid Mishneh's words that he learned from the tanners' kneading-trough, etc.

(ד) And there are those who are stringent. And so it implies from Tur in the name of Ravyah who wrote the reason because then it is recognizable; therefore he explains Rambam's words regarding what is not visible — see Terumat HaDeshen; see Beit Yosef; see Magen Avraham.
