This resolves my brother-in-law's wonder about the Maggid Mishneh's explanation of the Raavad's words: if the Raavad were speaking of this, he should have written his gloss at the end of chapter 4, where the case is that it was nullified in sixty, which is what the Levush wrote in se'if 4, and not here; see there and examine carefully.
