(3) And Maggid Mishneh wrote there Rambam ruled chapter 1 no lav in eating chametz mixture unless he ate a kezayit of chametz within k'dei achilat peras (three eggs as Rambam chapter 1; Shulchan Aruch — some say four eggs as above 468:3 and 472:4 — see there); certainly per his words here too one transgresses only in mixture with kezayit within k'dei achilat peras — this is primary. Some Geonim hold even with less one transgresses; I saw one who explains Rambam so — though no lav in eating obligated in destruction for kezayit of chametz is there though mixed in many olives — see there; likewise our Rabbi in Kesef Mishneh: for this no distinction whether kezayit within k'dei achilat peras or not — see there. Olat Shabbat letter 1: as Ran and primary. Magen Avraham s.k. 1; Rabbi Akiva Eiger letter 1; Chayei Adam letter 1: agreement of Acharonim like Poskim even without kezayit within k'dei achilat peras transgresses bal yera'eh; Makor Chayim letter 1; Gra 7 letter 1; Chayei Adam klal 121 letter 1; only min b'she'eino mino not nullified d'oraisa in majority per ta'am ke'ikar d'oraisa; min b'mino (e.g. flour of sour produce in flour not leavened) nullified d'oraisa in majority permitted to eat — all the more to retain d'oraisa; d'rabbanan forbidden to retain lest eat on Pesach — destroy; if transgressed permitted after Pesach (even in eating — Gra 13) since no ben yomo (as below 447 and 466 Acharonim). Where sixty — even min b'she'eino mino likewise. Chayei Adam there; Chok Yosef letter 1; see below 447 letter 244. Wheat flour with barley flour etc. is min b'she'eino mino. Rabbi Akiva Eiger letter 1.
