And below. See the Taz and the Eliyah Rabbah, notes 6 and 14. A doubt whether something is a setting agent may be treated as a rabbinic doubt, since the law of a setting agent may itself be rabbinic. But perhaps it is not a true doubt, only a lack of knowledge. Regarding keeping it, it appears to be a rabbinic doubt and therefore lenient; regarding eating it, there is a double doubt toward prohibition, lest it is a setting agent and lest the halachah follows the view that it reawakens on Pesach.
