(א) That were laundered, etc. — meaning even close to Pesach.
(ב) With wheat starch — even if some chametz substance is visible on them.
(ג) And likewise papers, etc. — likewise even if chametz substance is somewhat visible outside; the reason in all these: the form is already lost, as he explains.
(ד) Permitted to keep them — nevertheless forbidden to spread garments laundered with wheat starch on the table, for sometimes chametz substance is found on them and we fear lest some crumble into food; and all the more forbidden to place Passover flour in them; and women's scarves regularly fixed with flour until somewhat thick, which tend to crumble — do not wear while eating; meticulous people do not launder garments and scarves within thirty days with wheat starch, only with other types not of the five grains.
(ה) And some are stringent, etc. — they hold when recognizable outside it is still called form of chametz remains, even though dried; and for them there is no permission to paste papers with chametz except so chametz substance is not visible outside. For pasting papers on a window, they require making the dough very soft at night and inserting all dough between the adhesives so nothing protrudes outside at all. However if made thirty days before, according to all no concern even if visible outside, for certainly already hardened and is repulsive, like bread that molded until disqualified from a dog; and if all the chametz paste on the paper was less than a kezayit, even within thirty days, no need to destroy according to all.
