(א) With wheat starch. Magen Avraham wrote: nevertheless forbidden to spread those garments on the table, for we fear lest some crumble into the food — sometimes there is some substance from wheat-starch flour in them. And all the more forbidden to put Passover flour into garments laundered with wheat starch; and also women's scarves that they regularly fix with flour — remove them at mealtime lest some crumble into the food — end of his words. And in winter days I saw geniuses stringent also to make women's scarves thirty days beforehand. And Chayei Adam wrote there is no concern, for there is no way to crumble anything from them — see there.

(ב) Visible from outside. And it was not made thirty days beforehand. Taz wrote the dough should be thin so there is no substance at all when it dries — then even if visible from outside we have no concern. And if what is in the window is less than a kezayit, one need not destroy in any case, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 446. And one who is stringent plasters it outside with clay — see there.
