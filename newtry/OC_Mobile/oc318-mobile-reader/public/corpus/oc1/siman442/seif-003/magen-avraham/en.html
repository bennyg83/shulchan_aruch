(א) Garments. It appears to me: nevertheless forbidden to spread those garments on the table, for we fear lest some crumble into the food — sometimes there is some substance from wheat-starch flour; and all the more forbidden to put Passover flour into garments laundered with wheat starch; and women's scarves they regularly fix with flour — remove at mealtime lest some crumble into the food. Proof from what is written end of siman 447.

(ב) And some are stringent, etc. For then the form of chametz remains; but if made thirty days before, it already hardened and is permitted, as Semak wrote regarding a barrel (Terumat HaDeshen). Therefore according to what Rama wrote siman 447 end, like Mordechai, that two months are required — why did he rule here thirty days? One may say it is different there, for we fear it will impart taste to wine and he will drink; not so here, which is only regarding prohibition of keeping in his house. And in winter days I saw geniuses stringent also to make women's scarves thirty days before; and it must be said they hold it is called visible from outside. It appears to me: if there is less than a kezayit in the window, one need not destroy according to all, as Chokhmat Shlomo and siman 447 seif 5.
