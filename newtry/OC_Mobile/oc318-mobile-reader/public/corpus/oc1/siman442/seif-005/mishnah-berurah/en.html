(א) Must destroy it — and transgresses bal yera'eh if not destroyed; also karet if drinking it when there is a kezayit within the time of eating a meal; and even if there is not that measure, nevertheless taste of chametz is in it, and we hold taste is like the essence d'oraisa. If the beer was mixed into something else, that mixture is forbidden on Pesach — see Peri Megadim what he wrote.
(ב) If he set — even if there are sixty in the cheeses against the vinegar, the vinegar is not nullified, for something that sets is not nullified even in a thousand; and moreover whatever sets is considered as in its essence; therefore honey water (called mead) leavened in honey yeast cooked all year, and the first honey cooked was from vinegar of beer yeast, and what he cooks now is fourth or fifth — always must destroy, even the last honey water, for each through the setting agent becomes all chametz; in this no leniency to drink even on the last Yom Tov of Pesach, only that mead with no such concern cooked in a chametz vessel — some are lenient on the last Yom Tov to drink it because an unspecified vessel is not same-day [but in places where they cook mead all year in cauldrons where they make burnt wine — sharp, strong, imparts good taste to mead, as known — what is not same-day does not help; see siman 447 seif 5 in the gloss and Mishna Berurah]. All this when the first mead was set with beer yeast alone [and likewise second mead in yeast of first mead alone, etc.]; but if beer yeast alone was insufficient to set it and another setting agent that is not chametz was mixed, and together they set — permitted even to drink on Pesach if there are sixty against the beer yeast, for this-and-that cause it (meaning forbidden and permitted) to set — permitted when the forbidden alone cannot set, as explained Yoreh De'ah siman 87.
(ג) With vinegar, etc. — or yayin nesech made from grain set with beer yeast. If cheese was set through a stomach first soaked in a chametz vessel from the outset — forbidden to eat and permitted to keep [Radbaz]; see Acharonim who side: if set before Pesach, forbidden only if there were not sixty in the cheese against the stomach, and on Pesach where prohibition is in any amount — forbidden in every case.
(ד) Must destroy them — on the eve; and if they poured on cheeses after they already became yayin nesech to strengthen them, must destroy even if there are sixty in the cheeses against them, for done for taste and not nullified; nevertheless all this is only d'rabbanan; b'dieved if he forgot and did not destroy, whether regarding setting agent or something done for taste, and Pesach passed — there is room to permit in great loss for benefit by casting the benefit of the forbidden into the Dead Sea; likewise dough leavened in beer yeast that must be destroyed by law even if sixty in the dough against it, and because of setting agent not nullified even in a thousand; if he forgot and did not destroy and Pesach passed — permit in great loss by casting benefit of the setting agent into the Dead Sea.
