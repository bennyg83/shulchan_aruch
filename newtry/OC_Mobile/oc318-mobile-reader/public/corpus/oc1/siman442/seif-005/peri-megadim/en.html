And if. See the Taz. What appears from his words, that chametz nukshah has no bal yera'eh, is a matter of dispute; see Pri Chadash note 7. In the introduction we wrote that regarding eating one may say the amounts are fit to combine, for perhaps he will eat a kezayis within the time of eating a peras and think that more time has passed. In my view this is not clear. See Magen Avraham 447, s.k. 2 and 4, where it will be explained. What the Taz wrote regarding lashes seems to be because it is a negative commandment without an action, according to the Rambam, chapter 1; this requires investigation.
