(א) Seif 5 — and beer they make. For even Median beer into which barley is thrown one must destroy as in seif 1 — all the more this whose essence is barley; and even the Rabbis concede here he is liable as written mishnah 1:1 that the Rabbis concede regarding a kezayit within k'dei achilat peras; and even per Rabbenu Tam who explains the mishnah "they transgress from the table" but is not obligated to destroy — he concedes here. Rosh.

(ב) And likewise if. Meaning even per Rabbenu Tam as above because here it is b'eyna and not a mixture as in Avodah Zarah 35a — here since he establishes it, etc. And all this the Gemara teaches per Rabbenu Tam's view.
