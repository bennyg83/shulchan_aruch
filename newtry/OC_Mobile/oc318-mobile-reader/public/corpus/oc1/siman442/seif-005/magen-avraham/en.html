(א) Beer that they make, etc. For taste is like the essence d'oraisa — see seif 1; and even if the beer was mixed into something else, that mixture is forbidden on Pesach (Mahari, siman 97).

(ב) If he set. Even if there are sixty against the vinegar it is not nullified, for something that sets is not nullified even in a thousand; and it is written in Orach Chayim: a general rule — d'oraisa it is nullified, only d'rabbanan forbidden; but Tur wrote it is considered as if in its essence — it appears he holds forbidden d'oraisa, for otherwise he would not need to destroy it in his view, as seif 1; and Yeshuot Yaakov chapter 8 of Chullin siman 106 disagrees and rules something that sets is nullified; and with chametz in any case permitted to keep — see there; and his words are void against all these great ones. And also in Rashba's responsum siman 459 he brings in the name of Maharam: vinegar that leavened in beer yeast — forbidden to keep; and Ramban wrote siman 8: an incident with one who cooked honey and leavened it in honey yeast he cooked all year, and the first honey he cooked was from vinegar of beer yeast, and what he cooked now was fourth or fifth for him; and R' Yaakov permitted to leave it until after Pesach and sell it, for R' Shimon did not penalize chametz that passed Pesach except where it exists in its essence; and I wondered at his words, for here since it sets it is considered as in its essence; and further R' Shimon permits only when the chametz remained and after Pesach was mixed — but when placed before Pesach, forbidden to keep, as we learn: and these transgress, etc. — end of his words; and see seif 1. And it must be said there is no other setting agent except the chametz; if there were also permitted honey yeast, it would be this-and-that cause and permitted, as Yoreh De'ah end of siman 87. And Radbaz wrote chelek 2 siman 487: cheese set in a stomach that was in a chametz vessel — forbidden to eat and permitted to keep (so he wrote). And it appears to me its law is like something cooked in a chametz vessel — see siman 447 seif 5; and examine that the stomach was above all in a chametz vessel, as Yoreh De'ah end of siman 87.
