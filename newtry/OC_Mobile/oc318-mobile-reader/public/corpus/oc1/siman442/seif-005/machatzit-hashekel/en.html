(s.k. 6) That chametz was mixed into it. The Magen Avraham explains that this means actual, complete chametz, while it still improves the mixture.
If before it became mixed it was not fit for eating, why is it forbidden to eat even when only a small amount is present? The Magen Avraham concludes in siman 447, se'if 5, that chametz nukshah is nullified in sixty even on Pesach, and all the more so here, where it was mixed before Pesach and we do not say that it reawakens on Pesach.
(s.k. 7) Forbidden, etc. It is no better than molded bread. Therefore the theriac must have been made specifically before Pesach, as the Magen Avraham wrote regarding molded bread in s.k. 3.
(s.k. 8) Already, etc. The point is that since taste is like substance according to the Torah, one must destroy it where he has a kezayis in the house, even if there is not a kezayis within the time of eating a peras.
