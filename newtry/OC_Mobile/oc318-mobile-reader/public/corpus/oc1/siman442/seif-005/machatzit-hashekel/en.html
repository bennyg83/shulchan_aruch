(א) (s.k. 5) And some say, etc. — regarding barrels whose boards stuck in dough and filled with wine: per view of one day, forbidden to drink on Pesach; but before one day already dried.

(ב) And some say Shach wrote we fear, etc. — stricter regarding wine; Terumat HaDeshen wrote here one day suffices, proof from what Semag holds regarding barrels — a fortiori from Semak one day in barrel, all the more here; but for us we hold barrel requires two months; nevertheless here lighter, one day suffices.

(ג) And in days, etc. — before, etc., not within one day; called visible, etc. — otherwise Rama wrote even stringent only within one day for visible outside.

(ד) Less than kezayit — all the more made to strengthen and no kezayit — Chokhmat Shlomo plain: no need to destroy.
