The Taz raised a difficulty from Yoreh De'ah siman 87, where we say that when it was curdled with the stomach lining of a kosher animal it is nullified in sixty. So too here, the 180 should be nullified. This is different, because the curdling agent has the power to give taste on Pesach. Rashal holds that an item that causes setting is at least permitted to keep, but the Rashba and the other great poskim forbid. For this reason the Ramban forbids cooking mead-water that was cooked from the whole year's cooking, where the first batch was fermented with beer-yeast, and that was its fourth or fifth batch.
