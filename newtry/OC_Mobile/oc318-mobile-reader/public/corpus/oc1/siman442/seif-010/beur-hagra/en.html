Seif 10 — ink, etc. Like charco before its time permitted in benefit after its time; and it appears regarding ink from mei einayim forbidden in benefit — there like charco after its time; and like melogma within Pesach that became chametz and afterward putrefied forbidden to retain as above; and even though he wrote in the name of Tosafos Avodah Zarah chapter 2 — not only per those who forbid stam yeinam in benefit is ink forbidden, but even per Rabbenu Tam who permits stam yeinam in benefit ink is forbidden lest he put the quill in his mouth; likewise here — nevertheless forbidden in eating as in seif 4; nevertheless the prohibition is only since he intends to eat it he designates it for eating, and for him it is not disqualified even from a person; and Rosh beginning chapter 21 — all the more not so here without intent; see Yoreh De'ah siman 133 seif 13.
