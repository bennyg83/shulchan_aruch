(א) Ink that was cooked, etc. — meaning before biur time; the reason: bitter things and other bitter items mixed in certainly impaired the beer until unfit even for a dog to drink — therefore permitted to write with it in season; but if a non-Jew cooked this ink on Pesach, forbidden to write with it, since the beer was not disqualified from a dog's eating before its time.

(ב) Permitted to write with it — and we do not fear lest he forget and put the quill in his mouth as scribes do; and chametz disqualified from a dog's eating is forbidden to eat, as above — this is specifically when eaten intentionally and he considered it significant; not so here, without intent there is no concern [Magen Avraham in the name of Terumat HaDeshen].
