It is permitted to write with it. We are not concerned that he might put his pen into his mouth. Chametz that became unfit for a dog's eating is forbidden in eating, because when he puts it in his mouth unintentionally there is no concern; it is forbidden specifically to eat it intentionally, because by doing so he considers it significant, as Terumas HaDeshen writes. See Yoreh De'ah at the end of siman 134, se'if 3.
