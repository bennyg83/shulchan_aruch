If there are two half-kezayit amounts in two places with a string of dough between them, we consider: if removing the string would cause them to be taken together, one must remove them; otherwise, one need not. This applies to a trough; however, in a house, even if removing the string would not take them together, one must remove them, because sometimes one gathers them. If there is half a kezayit in the house and half a kezayit in the upper story, or half in the house and half in a portico, or half in one house and half in an inner house—since these halves are attached to walls, beams, or floors, one is not obligated to remove them, but nullifies them in his heart and that suffices.
