[8] [Levush] And some permit where it was mixed, etc. At first glance this is difficult: with theriac it is obvious that he is speaking of a chametz mixture. One must say that this is what he means: specifically theriac, which is something that becomes mixed, but where the item is not mixed and the form of the chametz remains, as he mentioned earlier, it is forbidden according to everyone. Nevertheless, the wording is not precise, for why does he need to say 'it was mixed'? This is already heard from the fact that he says it was nullified, and with the other cases it is not relevant to say that it was nullified in sixty. It seems to me that this comes to exclude where one mixed it intentionally in order to keep it, which is forbidden, as written in siman 447 se'if 2, and there it will be explained.
