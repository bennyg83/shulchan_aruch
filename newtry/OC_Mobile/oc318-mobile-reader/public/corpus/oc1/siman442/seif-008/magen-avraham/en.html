(א) Need not destroy. Specifically in a place designated to strengthen — see there.

(ב) Since these, etc. Rosh wrote: some say even if not stuck together, no need to destroy, since it is less than kezayit; Maggid Mishneh, Ramach, and Kesef Mishneh wrote: specifically when stuck, for it is like placed to strengthen — and it is plain that even less than kezayit requires nullification, and if he did not nullify, destruction is required — see siman 454 seif 3.

(ג) He nullifies in his heart. Since this was an unresolved question in the gemara whether they combine; therefore once he nullified, it is afterward a rabbinic doubt and one need not search — see siman 439.
