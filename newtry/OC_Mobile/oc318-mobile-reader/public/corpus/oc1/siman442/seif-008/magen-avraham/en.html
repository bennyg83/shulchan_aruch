Since these, etc. The Rosh writes that even if it is not attached, less than a kezayis need not be destroyed. The Maggid Mishneh, Ramach, and Kesef Mishneh say that this applies specifically when it is attached, for then it is like something made to strengthen. Even less than a kezayis requires nullification, and if he did not nullify it, it must be destroyed; see siman 460, se'if 3.
He nullifies it in his heart. For the Gemara's question is whether they combine; after he has nullified it, the question is only rabbinic, and he need not check. See siman 439.
