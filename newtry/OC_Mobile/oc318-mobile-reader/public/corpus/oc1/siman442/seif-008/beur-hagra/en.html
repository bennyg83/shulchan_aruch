(א) Seif 8 — in what case in a kneading-trough. Magen Avraham s.k. 1.

(ב) Even though if. It appears even without any thread at all as Rashi wrote there; likewise in the Gemara since they asked regarding a house, attic, and two houses, etc.

(ג) Since, etc. He wrote thus to resolve Tosafos' difficulties s.v. kan — per Tosafos not in a kneading place there is no concern of mixing even if not asuy lechazek — less than a kezayit is nullified; likewise a house — not so Rambam; therefore he wrote since, etc., and it is as asuy lechazek; see Milchamot who wrote since it is stuck he is not particular, etc. — see there.

(ד) One nullifies it. For a rabbinic doubt is lenient.
