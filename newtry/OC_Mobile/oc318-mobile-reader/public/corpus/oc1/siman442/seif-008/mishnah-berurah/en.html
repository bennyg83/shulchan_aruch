(א) Whenever if the string were removed, etc. — for the string was thick and therefore combines them, and it is a kezayit in one place; therefore it does not help even if placed to strengthen.
(ב) Need not destroy — specifically when in a place designated to strengthen the trough; otherwise the vessel combines, as above.
(ג) For sometimes he gathers them — when cleaning the house, and they are in one place.
(ד) That are stuck, etc. — meaning in grooves that can be leveled, and it is like placed to strengthen, explained seif 7: in this not obligated to destroy unless kezayit in one place. And some say: even if not stuck, no need to destroy, since each is less than kezayit in its place; we do not fear they will gather in one place. Nevertheless nullification is required according to all; if he did not nullify before prohibition time, must destroy afterward.
(ה) He nullifies in his heart and that suffices — for this is an unresolved question in the gemara whether they combine; therefore once he nullified, it is a rabbinic doubt and one need not search.
