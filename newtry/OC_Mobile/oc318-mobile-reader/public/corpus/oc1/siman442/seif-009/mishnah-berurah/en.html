(א) Before its prohibition time and disqualified, etc. — meaning disqualified from a dog's eating before prohibition time; if before Pesach it did not spoil, only from human eating, and during Pesach spoiled more until unfit for a dog — was obligated to destroy, since at biur obligation time it was still fit for a dog.

(ב) Before its time — to exclude after its time, prohibition is not removed until he burns it completely.

(ג) Or that he designated for sitting, etc. — he had a thick lump of chametz between hard dough and bread, designated for sitting before Pesach; even though in his mind he removed it from the category of food and nullified it only for this — nevertheless without plastering with clay, nullification does not help. And plastering with clay alone does not help without designating for sitting.

(ד) And plastered it with clay — and through this it helps even if the chametz was not disqualified from eating; the reason: he thereby nullified it from the name of food. From the Mechaber's language it appears he must plaster from every side; but in the gemara: he plastered its face with clay — perhaps face means the exposed side from every direction.

(ה) Permitted to keep on Pesach — and likewise permitted for benefit; but forbidden to eat d'rabbanan until after Pesach; even though eating that is unfit is considered eating — since he wants to eat, forbidden because he considered it significant; specifically in this where he considered it; but if this chametz disqualified from a dog's eating fell by itself into food, even only in majority, no prohibition to eat that food, for this chametz is mere dust. And almudani made from fish fat with roasted bread before Pesach: if known the bread was roasted so much it is unfit for a dog — permitted to keep and sell to a non-Jew, but forbidden to eat; if unknown, forbidden even to keep and benefit.
