(א) Seif 6 — they practiced. He relied on this in Yerushalmi chapter 2 halachah 2: Rav said one who coats his house with dough is obligated to destroy. Ra'avan seif 7.

(ב) And if there is chametz. As in the Gemara 45b: if he coated, etc. — see there, Mordechai, Tur; likewise Yerushalmi there on Rav's words: obligated to destroy. Tanna: Rashba — dough he made as a patch is nullified — explain it thus, or they disagree with Rashba, or the patch is repulsive and meaning he spread in mud as in the Gemara — likewise Ra'avan; see Mordechai.
