6) The Tur writes that anything whose consumption is not prohibited under Torah law may initially be mixed and retained. Sefer Mitzvos Katan writes likewise. Tosafos at the beginning of Pesachim, however, write that it is forbidden, because the Sages made no distinction. Magen Avraham, subsection 1, writes that this is the opinion of the Shulchan Aruch.
