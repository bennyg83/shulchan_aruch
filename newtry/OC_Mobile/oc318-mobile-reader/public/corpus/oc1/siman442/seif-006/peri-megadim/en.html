Since. See the Taz and Magen Avraham s.k. 12. In several places the pieces are attached in cracks and are considered made to strengthen. But if a half-kezayis alone is lying unattached, one may be obligated to destroy it. The doubt in siman 461 concerns attached pieces in a kneading trough: even if not made to strengthen, they are attached and are nullified along with the trough when there is not a kezayis among all of them. This differs from a half-kezayis lying unattached on the ground. What the Taz wrote, that perhaps they will roll together, is difficult, because the question applies even to attached pieces, which sometimes gather through sweeping; therefore if they are in one house one must destroy them.
