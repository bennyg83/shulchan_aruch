Since Taz — Magen Avraham note 12; and in several places part 2 Choshen Mishpat 17 they were stuck in cracks — it is asuy lechazek — see there; behold even half a kezayit alone one is obligated to destroy; and there is a doubt of 461:1 that in any case stuck in a kneading-trough even though not asuy lechazek — nevertheless it is stuck and nullified along with it when altogether there is not a kezayit — not so when half a kezayit lies without sticking on the ground. And see Magen Avraham note 12 and what he wrote there "and plainly" etc. And what Taz wrote: perhaps they will roll to each other — I did not understand, for the question is also with stuck ones — sometimes they gather through scrubbing, and therefore in one place one must destroy.
