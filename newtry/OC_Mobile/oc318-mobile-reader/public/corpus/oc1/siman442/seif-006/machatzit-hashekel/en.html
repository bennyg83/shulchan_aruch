(s.k. 6) Was mixed, etc. — full chametz, etc. — if before mixtures, not fit for eating; why forbid eating, even only slight amount? Magen Avraham concludes end siman 447 seif 5 small: hard chametz even on Pesach nullified in sixty, all the more here mixed before Pesach where we do not say reawakens on Pesach.
