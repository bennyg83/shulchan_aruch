(א) If there is a kezayit, etc. — meaning even though certainly nullified when placed to strengthen broken parts of the trough, nevertheless since considered significant, not nullified.

(ב) Placed to strengthen, etc., or to seal, etc. — no difference whether at the bottom of the trough or on its lip.

(ג) Nullified in its minority — meaning even though there are many half-olive-volumes over the whole trough surface, since not in one place, each is nullified in its minority relative to the vessel.

(ד) And if not, must destroy — specifically when there is a kezayit among them all, for then the vessel combines; but when less than a kezayit remains among all, no need to destroy, even if not placed to strengthen — as proved seif 11; but truly there is dispute among poskim: many hold even less than kezayit requires destruction — specifically when fit for eating somewhat; but if slightly soiled and less than kezayit, no need to destroy according to all.
