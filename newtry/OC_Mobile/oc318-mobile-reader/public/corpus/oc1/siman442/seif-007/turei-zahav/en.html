And if not, if placed to strengthen, etc. So read in Shulchan Aruch — there is a printing error here. I wonder at all these in this siman whose law is no obligation to destroy unless there is a kezayit — it fits the reason of bedikah that chametz because of stringency of bal yera'eh is only kezayit; but per Tosafot beginning of the chapter, lest one eat it — chametz is d'oraisa forbidden even without lashes; if so even less than kezayit in every case must destroy for this reason. Therefore all these obligations here are only for lashes — is it not so one is exempt from lashes for less than kezayit? Why did Tur and Shulchan Aruch bring these laws — we have no practical outcome? Also on Rosh whom Tur brings: why tie it to Israel's holiness and scrape all chametz found even any amount stuck to a wall — derive from law that one must destroy lest one eat, d'oraisa forbidden even half measure? And one may say: it deals with something where eating concern does not apply — difficult that bedikah is unnecessary per this reason. And Beit Yosef in Hagahot in the name of Riaz wrote: it deals with something fit to eat somewhat, etc.; one may say: it deals with something placed where it would be trampled — nevertheless for kezayit one must destroy because of bal yera'eh even one moment before trampling one transgresses.
