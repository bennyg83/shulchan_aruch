(א) Seif 7 — dough, etc. Like Rav Huna there who said: remove my kilyorot, etc. — Abaye's explanation is not the halachah for he establishes like Rashba and they said there the halachah is not like Rashba; Rav Ashi comes to explain Abaye's matter and not to fix halachah — see Razah and Sefer HaMilchamot.

(ב) Or to seal. Per R' Chananel and Rishonim who explained asuy lechazek means dough that filled up; R' Chananel explains like Abaye; likewise Rashi and Tosafos; see Tur; see Sefer HaMilchamot — no distinction between inside and outside, threshold, or below; only Abaye outside we rule like the first version and inside where there is concern it will stick to kneading we are stringent like the latter version; likewise Tosafos s.v. kan, etc. Razah's method like Rashi: three distinctions — ledges, walls, and upper lip; per Rashi not in a kneading place is stricter — the upper lip; likewise walls outside as in the Gemara there — except Razah and Sefer HaMilchamot: even though he explains like Abaye, nevertheless regarding even a kezayit not obligated to destroy we do not rule like Rashba as in patch — the halachah is not like Rashba; four views emerge: Rashi, Tosafos, Razah, Ramban; Rif and Rambam like R' Hillel — no distinction at all between places; and a sixth view — R' Yechiel of Paris explains Rashi as in seif 11.
