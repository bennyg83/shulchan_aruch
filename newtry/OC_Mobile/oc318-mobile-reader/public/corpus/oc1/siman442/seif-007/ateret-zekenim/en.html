Dough in cracks, etc. — only when one does not knead in that kneading-trough all year is it nullified in its minority; but if one kneads there all year, even washing in hot water and scraping do not help — one must give it as a gift to a gentile or coat it with clay — end of his words. {Rama: in the name of Maharansh.} He wondered: what of it if the stomach fell from above into a chametz vessel — wine is not kavush until after above, and after above the vessel becomes damaged; see Yoreh De'ah siman 105 seif 1 and Shach s.k. 2 who elaborated to permit — end of his words. I was awestruck at the above Rav's conduct — it was not proper or lawful to reply to the Ari z"l alive after his death and overturn Maharach's words, for he was great in wisdom and pilpul and all later Poskim were fluent from his mouth — did he not also see the Acharonim in siman 105 cited? On the contrary, in waiving his honor he erred in proof — Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 437 seif 1 regarding yayin nesech, see there it is explicit like Maharach; also the practical halachah escaped him that Baal Beit Hilul ruled siman 69 and siman 105 not like those Acharonim — see there; also he did not see Chok Yosef in Chelek Chokhmat Da'at letter 20 in the name of the Gaon R' Yaakov of Lublin (Maharach) that he heard in his name there is a distinction if the vessel stands empty then the vessel becomes damaged — and now he struck Maharach on the head in vain in his astonishment — end of his words. In this siman Magen David s.k. 5: therefore all those obligations are only for lashes, etc. — if so why did Tur and Shulchan Aruch bring these laws when we have no practical nafka mina from this? One must say there is a great nafka mina even now regarding kiddushin of a woman — if forbidden d'oraisa and liable to lashes for them she is not betrothed — and other distinctions — end of his words.
