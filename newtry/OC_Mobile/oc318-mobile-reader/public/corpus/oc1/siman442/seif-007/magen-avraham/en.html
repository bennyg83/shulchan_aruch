And if not, he must destroy. Specifically when there is a kezayit among them all, for then the vessel combines it; but when less than a kezayit remains among them all, one need not destroy even if not placed to strengthen — as seif 1, so Semak proves; but many poskim hold: if not placed to strengthen, even less than kezayit requires destruction — see Chokhmat Shlomo; and what is written siman 454 seif 3; and Hagahot Maimoniot in the name of Ra'avan wrote: specifically when fit for eating somewhat; but slightly soiled and less than kezayit — no need to destroy.
