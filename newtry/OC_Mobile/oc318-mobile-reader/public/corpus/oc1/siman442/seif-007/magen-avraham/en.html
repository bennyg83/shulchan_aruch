If not, he must destroy it. That is only when, between all the pieces, there is a kezayis, for the vessel combines them. But if there is not a kezayis in all of them together, he need not destroy them, even if they were not made to strengthen, as is implied in se'if 1 and in the Semak. However, according to many poskim, if they were not made to strengthen, even less than a kezayis must be destroyed; see se'if 8 and siman 460, se'if 3. The Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote in the name of Re'em that this is specifically where it is somewhat fit to eat, but if it is somewhat soiled and less than a kezayis, it need not be destroyed.
