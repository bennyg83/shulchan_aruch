See Baer Heitev, and see Ginas Veradim, klal 4 siman 11, regarding the practice with hats: the inner covering cloth is smeared with a soft batter close to the amount of two eggs, afterward the outer cloth is attached over it and sewn, and it is placed by the heat until it dries and becomes a hat. There is no concern of a chametz prohibition, even though the dough reached the category of se'or, since an action was done to it, it was nullified, and it was covered with stitches; see there. See Responsa Rabbi Zuskind Rothenberg, where the questioner copied the language of the Taz and the Maharshal in siman 432. He wrote to resolve that there is no difficulty from hidden chametz: here, where it is not visible from outside, nullification helps. Nevertheless, from Terumas HaDeshen it is clear that the reason 'not visible from outside' is that the form of the chametz was nullified; see there.
