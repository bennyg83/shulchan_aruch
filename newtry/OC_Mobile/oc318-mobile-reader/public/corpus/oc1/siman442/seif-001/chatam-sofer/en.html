In Magen Avraham s.k. 1 s.v. ve'it appears if it became mixed, etc., and Beit Yosef's words require study. Note: in what he wrote for Tur according to Rabbanan he forbids in any case d'oraisa; Tur himself wrote for Rif there is only a rabbinic prohibition; also Tur wrote the reason for bedikah is because one transgresses bal yera'eh, and in siman 431 Tur himself wrote the reason lest one come to eat from it. Likewise Eliyah Zuta challenged.
