(א) Seif 1 — chametz mixture, etc. All four opening seifim are the language of Rambam chapter 4.

(ב) They transgress regarding it. Per Rashi's explanation and likewise the Geonim; Maggid Mishneh wrote only when there is a kezayit within k'dei achilat peras — not so, since there is no lav in its eating as he wrote chapter 1 — likewise bal yera'eh. Some Geonim hold even with less than this one transgresses; likewise Ramach explained our Rabbi's words but disagrees with him — see Maggid Mishneh chapter 4. Only chametz in which there is a kezayit within k'dei achilat peras, etc.; but Ramach's words are primary since he cited Babylonian paste and Median beer of which the Gemara says they lack k'dei achilat peras. Also since he distinguished in chapter 1 regarding eating and here did not distinguish — learn that in all cases one is liable; likewise Kesef Mishneh. What Maggid Mishneh compared to eating — Rambam does not hold so, for also regarding chametz nokshah he wrote chapter 4 clothes they laundered, etc., and papers glued — one transgresses bal yera'eh and bal yimatzei because the form of chametz does not stand — implying if not so one transgresses even though they are chametz nokshah as the mishnah says kolan shel sofrim, etc. — see Maggid Mishneh there and Ra'avad there in hasagot.

(ג) Such as morayis. See Rashi Avodah Zarah 29b s.v. hamorayis; see Rosh Kol Sha'ah seif 1 and chapter 2 daf 19 16b — it implies they put flour into it.

(ד) And paste, etc. The mishnah.

(ה) And all the like. There.

(ו) And theriac. Likewise R' Hillel the Gaon learned from the above and Rosh brought it. He wrote all the more so from melogma.
