(1) [Levush] And there are those who say one is obligated, etc. Shulchan Aruch ruled stam like this view; Kesef Mishneh chapter 4 agreed even without kezayit within k'dei achilat peras since there is a kezayit of chametz one transgresses bal yera'eh. Magen Avraham: if min b'mino such as flour in flour nullified in majority d'oraisa permitted even to retain d'oraisa — end of his words; if transgressed and retained — if remembered within Pesach destroy d'rabbanan; if not see end siman 447 after Pesach. Olat Shabbat: fat from a dish with only taste not substance transgresses bal yera'eh — ta'am ke'ikar d'oraisa — end of his words; my brother-in-law in Chok Yaakov: ta'am ke'ikar only for eating not retaining; d'rabbanan forbidden to retain; if transgressed permitted in benefit after Pesach.
