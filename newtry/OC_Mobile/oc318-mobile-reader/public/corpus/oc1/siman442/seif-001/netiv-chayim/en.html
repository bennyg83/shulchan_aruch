(Magen Avraham s.k. 1) Since it has begun to become chametz, therefore it should be burned. Note: this means that he should burn it afterward, when it becomes chametz, as the Bach ruled that actual chametz that came into being on Yom Tov is destroyed on Yom Tov; see below, siman 446, what I wrote at the end of s.k. 1. The Rashba holds like the other poskim who forbid destroying on Yom Tov; therefore he must say that the Sages did not differentiate.
