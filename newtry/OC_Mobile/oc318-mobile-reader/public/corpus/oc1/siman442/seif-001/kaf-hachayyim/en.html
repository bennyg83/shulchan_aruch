1) [Se'if 1] One transgresses regarding a mixture containing chametz, etc. One is not flogged for violating the prohibitions against chametz being seen or found unless one acquired chametz during Pesach or caused a substance to become chametz, so that one performed an action. If one possessed chametz before Pesach and Pesach arrived without one eliminating it, but one merely left it in one's domain, then although one violated two prohibitions, one is not flogged under Torah law because one performed no action. One is instead given lashes for rebellious conduct. Rambam, chapter 1, halachah 3. Regarding the rectification for one who transgressed, see above, siman 334.
