And he holds: since burning was permitted for a need, it was also permitted not for a need; but to bake is forbidden, for even for a need we do not say since it is permitted for a need, etc. — see Rambam chapter 1 of Laws of Yom Tov; and Tur holds like the plain meaning of Rambam's words there that specifically for carrying out and burning we say "from the need of" and not for other melachot even for ochel nefesh itself — unlike the Rav Maggid there; and further even per Maggid Mishneh other melachot were not permitted — only one does not receive lashes from the reason "since guests happen to arrive," as explained in Rambam there law 15; unlike carrying out and burning which were permitted from the Torah entirely even not for need at all. And what Taz wrote siman 446 note 3 in the name of Tur that even if they did not nullify it is forbidden to burn on Yom Tov — Taz cited first Tur's words who wrote thus in the name of "some say," but in the conclusion Tur concludes in the name of his brother R' Yechiel that one may not burn on Yom Tov if they did not nullify; and likewise nokshah — this cannot be nullified even while still nokshah, for it is forbidden in benefit d'rabbanan and not in his domain to nullify it.
