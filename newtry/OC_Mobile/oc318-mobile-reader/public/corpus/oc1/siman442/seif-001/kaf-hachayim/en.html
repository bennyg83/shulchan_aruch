A) [Section 1] A mixture of chametz is transgressed on him, etc. It is not defective because he will not see it and will not find it, unless he bought chametz on Pesach or left it in order to make an act with it. But if he had chametz before Passover and Passover came and was not in the forest, but left in his possession, an afi that passed on two to the enemy is not defective in the OT because he did not do an act with it and prepares it as a plague of sedition.
