The law of a mixture of chametz. It contains eleven se'ifim.
For a mixture of chametz one transgresses "bal yeira'eh" and "bal yimatzei" — such as muryas [fish brine], Babylonian kutach, Median beer, and all similar edible items. But something that contains a mixture of chametz and is not fit for eating, it is permitted to keep on Pesach — such as a tanners' trough into which he put flour and hides: even if he put them in one hour before the [time of] removal, it is permitted to keep it. And if he did not put in the hides but put in the flour three days before the time of removal, it is permitted to keep it, for it has already spoiled and become foul within three days; [but if it did not spoil] within three days, he is obligated to remove it. And likewise a collyrium, a bandage, a plaster, and theriac into which he put chametz, it is permitted to keep on Pesach, for the form of the chametz has spoiled.
