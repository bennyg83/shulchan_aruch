(א) They transgress. The agreement of the Acharonim: even if there is not a kezayit within the time of eating a meal (that is four eggs — Maggid Mishneh — and so is primary), one transgresses bal yera'eh; and specifically a species in a different species that is not nullified d'oraisa by majority. But a species in its own species is nullified d'oraisa by majority and permitted to eat; all the more so permitted to keep it d'oraisa — however d'rabbanan it is forbidden to keep it lest one come to eat from it on Pesach, and one must destroy it. Nevertheless if he transgressed and kept it, it is permitted after Pesach, since he did not transgress bal yera'eh, as explained below siman 447 and 467 — see there, in the words of the Acharonim. And likewise where there are sixty, even a species in a different species — the law is thus. And because of absorption of vessels — meaning that it was cooked in a chametz vessel — one need not destroy it and it is permitted to keep it, for in absorption of vessels it is only called a slight prohibition and it was nullified before Pesach. And where there is no substance of chametz at all, only taste of chametz alone — forbidden to keep it d'rabbanan (and even for one who holds taste is like the essence d'oraisa — that is for eating we learn from the verse, not to transgress bal yera'eh — Chayei Adam), and destruction is required if found during Pesach; but if he transgressed and kept it, there is no prohibition. And the same law for all these that are permitted d'oraisa to keep — only d'rabbanan forbidden to keep l'chatchila, and one burns it during Pesach d'rabbanan; if he transgressed and kept it, it is permitted as explained below siman 447. And Olat Shabbat wrote regarding taste and not substance that one transgresses bal yera'eh; and in absorption of vessels that it is forbidden to keep them l'chatchila — see there. And Chayei Adam disagrees and ruled as written. And hard chametz — see siman 446 seif 12 for its law. And see Yad Ephraim — lengthy pilpul, and Peri Chadash.

(ב) And Median beer. And all of them through mixture of water — for otherwise fruit juice does not cause leavening.

(ג) And everything similar. The Rav, author of Mishna Berurah siman 52, and the Rav, author of Shaarei Adam Orach Chayim siman 12, and in Chayei Adam this siman — all agree to the lenient side that burnt wine made from barley steeped in water is obligated in bal yera'eh; and so most Acharonim, not like the responsum of Yad Eliyahu siman 26 — see there; and see Yad Ephraim.

(ד) Permitted to keep on Pesach. And we do not decree lest one come to eat it, since it is not fit for eating at all. Not so with hard chametz, which is forbidden to keep l'chatchila — even though it is not human food, nevertheless it is fit for eating under duress. Acharonim.
