(א) Seif 11 — and the vessel combines them. R' Yitzchak's language distinguishes, per his method in Hagahot Semak following Rashi: not in a kneading place such as the upper lip outside — even less than a kezayit one must destroy, meaning there are many olive-fractions and the vessel combines them even without joining with a thread; the requirement of a thread is in the walls for the vessel does not combine on its outside walls as below — thereby Tosafos' difficulty s.v. kan is resolved: a house, although similar to the upper lip, nevertheless a house does not combine like a vessel and one needs the reason sometimes it compresses — end of his words. Likewise in the mishnah: if there is a kezayit in one place, less than this, etc. — implies not in one place; on this they said "if not," etc.; likewise Yerushalmi there — R' Yirmiyah in the name of R' Zeira: two half-olives in a house — the house does not combine; in a vessel the vessel combines — only inside, not outside on walls as above — it follows the Gemara's sugya except the Gemara concerned compression; one may resolve Yerushalmi also concerns compression but compression applies only to moist dough that sticks together and becomes one kezayit — in that case two half-olives of the Gemara; if one removed the thread it is removed with it — only when moist as Yerushalmi; Yerushalmi does not deal with moist dough where scraping does not help, as on the mishnah kezayit in one place, etc., peeled all at once; and even though they said there while moist, when dry — since if moist it would peel all at once, there it is already stuck as one except if peeled it would scatter — then what is written in chapter 1 "if we say because of crumbs," etc., fits; per Rambam whatever is not stuck to walls, etc. — even less one must destroy and not nullified; per this view it fits that only because of combination the house does not combine, and the gezeirah of compression does not apply to something dry — not like Rambam who requires sticking; likewise Yerushalmi there challenged on "within the vessel the vessel combines" — but we taught less than this is nullified in minority — proof for R' Yitzchak's explanation that the mishnah deals where there are many olive-fractions as above; they answer: here detached, here attached — mishnah attached, Yerushalmi detached — thus even detached in a house no need to destroy without compression; Yerushalmi did not descend to distinguish asuy lechazek or not, kneading place or not — they mentioned nothing of this; nevertheless one may resolve Yerushalmi's sugya per our sugya: what they distinguished between detached and attached per Rashi is in the walls, like two half-olives and thread, etc., in the walls as above; detached in all cases the vessel combines but the house never combines. Likewise Tosafos' method which all Rishonim accepted — one may explain per R' Yitzchak even though Tosefta does not imply so; for halachah one explains their method per Yerushalmi: in a kneading place and not asuy lechazek even less than a kezayit one must destroy — many olive-fractions; likewise Rif everywhere not asuy lechazek also many olive-fractions; no need to force that two half-olives in a house deals with stuck ones as asuy lechazek; also crumbs cannot be resolved except per R' Yitzchak — primary per Rif; Rashba is lone against Rif and R' Yitzchak.

(ב) And one must give them. As in siman 468 seif 3 from Tosefta and Magen Avraham.

(ג) Or coat with clay. As above from patch as in seif 10 and seif 9.

(ד) And likewise for dough. Per Rashi only on the upper lip outside; per Rif everywhere since not asuy lechazek; likewise Tosafos since in a kneading place and not asuy lechazek.

(ה) And from a cloth that was, etc. See end Pesachim 7a 7b: these leniencies, etc.; see Tosafos there s.v. dekisna, etc.; Magen Avraham s.k. 19 wrote: it appears the dough stuck, etc.; and even though Tosafos did not write to distinguish stuck except in something hot — see Rosh and Hagahot Ashri; nevertheless from Zevachim 79b 40 it applies even in cold use; see Rashi s.v. ve'afilu and s.v. she'ani.
