In Peri Megadim and Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah they copied 'the Ramban,' but that is a scribal error. The difficulty is that even the fifth honey batch from that which became chametz through beer yeast is forbidden, whereas in Yoreh De'ah simanim 112 and 115, in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham, they permit after three times. So too Panim Me'iros, part 2, siman 75, wrote about the Magen Avraham's words; see there. Therefore Knesses HaGedolah wrote to permit it for one who is careful regarding the prohibition of chadash, though I did not have the courage to permit keeping it on Pesach; nevertheless, keeping it seems permitted; see there. In Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah, part 2, siman 77, he rejected this, because they were lenient only regarding their bread and milk, whose prohibition is light, unlike the severe prohibition of chametz; therefore one should be stringent even about keeping it. However, if one transgressed and kept it, one may permit benefit from it after Pesach, together with the reason written by some poskim that nowadays, when everyone nullifies, one need not be so stringent; see there.
