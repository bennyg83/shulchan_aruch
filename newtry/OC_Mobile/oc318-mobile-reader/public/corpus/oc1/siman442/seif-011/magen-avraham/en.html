(א) Impossible to scrape them. And Radbaz chelek 2 siman 488 wrote: this is excessive stringency and our fathers did not practice it — these women of ours scrape them well — end of his words. And it appears to me: here we deal with troughs made from large boards in which bread is kneaded, with grooves between board and board where chametz is found; but small troughs made from one piece with no crack — plainly one can scrape them — see siman 451 seif 17.

(ב) Until after Pesach. And he should not tell the non-Jew thus; rather give them as an absolute gift, and after Pesach he may take them back — see siman 448.

(ג) That they place bread in them. That is wicker baskets; and Mahariv wrote: it appears to me to hide them in a room one does not regularly enter.

(ד) Laundering. It appears mere rinsing does not help — on the contrary the dough sticks and requires laundering in hot water, ash, and beating — see siman 438 seif 8 and Shaarei Knesset there.
