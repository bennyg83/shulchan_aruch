It is impossible to scrape them out. Radbaz, chelek 2 siman 488, wrote: this is an excessive stringency, and our fathers did not practice it, for our women scrape them well, end quote. It seems to me that here he is speaking of troughs made of large boards in which bread is kneaded, which have grooves between board and board. But small troughs made of one piece, with no crack in them, obviously can be scraped out. See siman 451 se'if 17.
Until after Pesach. He should not say this to the non-Jew; rather, he should give them to him as an absolute gift, and after Pesach he may take them back from him. See siman 448.
In which they place bread. This means wicker baskets. Mahari Weil wrote: it seems to me that one should hide them in a room that one does not regularly use.
Laundered. It seems to me that mere rinsing does not help; on the contrary, the dough sticks to it, and it requires laundering with hot water and ash and beating. See Yoreh De'ah siman 138 se'if 8 and the Shach there.
