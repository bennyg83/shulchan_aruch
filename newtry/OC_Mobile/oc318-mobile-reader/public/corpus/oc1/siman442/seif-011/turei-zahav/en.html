Impossible to scrape them, etc. I say: this means impossible to scrape and kasher them to use on Pesach — for then why need vessel combination to kezayit, for even less than kezayit is forbidden, chametz in any amount; rather certainly: impossible to scrape and store them, for there is still concern lest there is a kezayit and one transgresses bal yimatzei. Regarding using on Pesach it does not deal at all — with this I resolve what was asked: here Tur brought Abba Ezri first, who wrote all this regarding a trough in which one does not knead all year, but if one kneads all year, scrape and kasher; afterward he brings R' Yitzchak of Paris who wrote the words Shulchan Aruch copied imply for R' Yitzchak kashering does not help — and in siman 451 Tur wrote: a trough in which one kneads all year requires kashering — apparently R' Yitzchak of Paris also holds kashering possible, i.e., whoever wants to use on Pesach certainly exerts himself and scrapes as much as possible since chametz-eating time is upon him — there is possibility to scrape thin and kasher; but one who only wants to store does not exert himself and relies on scraping — regarding this he said impossible to scrape, etc.; and impossible is not precise — also Semak wrote in this language because usually impossible to scrape properly — implying not impossible that they can be scraped properly; and Tur did not bring R' Yitzchak to disagree with Abba Ezri, only the stringency of each — Abba Ezri stringent regarding kashering, R' Yitzchak of Paris stringent regarding storing — so it appears to me to reconcile Tur's words even though halachah does not follow this — end of my words.
