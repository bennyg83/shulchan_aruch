In the Mechaber seif 11 impossible to scrape. Note: if they kneaded matzah therein without scraping, hot crumbs of dough become se'or and there is concern the dough became chametz; even if they scrape, since they did not hag'alah the vessel's power leavens the dough — thus Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham siman 451 s.k. 40; likewise Chayei Adam klal 125 letter 3.
