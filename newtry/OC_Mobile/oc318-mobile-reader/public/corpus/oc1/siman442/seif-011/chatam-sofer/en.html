There. In the Mechaber, se'if 11: it is impossible to scrape them out. Note: if they kneaded matzah with them without scraping them out, the crumbs of the warm dough became leaven, and there is concern that the dough became chametz. Even if they are scraped out, nevertheless, since they did not kasher them, the power of the utensil causes the dough to become chametz. So wrote the Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham, siman 451 s.k. 40, and so wrote the Chayei Adam, klal 125, letter 3.
