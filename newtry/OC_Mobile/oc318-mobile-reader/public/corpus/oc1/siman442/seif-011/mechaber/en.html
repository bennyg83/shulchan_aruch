Kneading troughs in which ḥametz dough is kneaded—one should not rely on washing them with hot water and scraping out the ḥametz, because it is impossible to clean them so that a total of a kezayit does not remain, and the vessel combines it; therefore, one must give them as a gift to a non-Jew until after Pesaḥ or plaster them with clay. The same applies to dough in wooden vessels that cannot be removed. Rema: It is good to do this for all vessels in which flour is kept throughout the year (Mahariv), and likewise for vessels in which bread is kept all year; and a cloth that was placed on a sack of flour—shaking it is not sufficient, and it requires washing in order to use it during Pesaḥ (Maharil).
