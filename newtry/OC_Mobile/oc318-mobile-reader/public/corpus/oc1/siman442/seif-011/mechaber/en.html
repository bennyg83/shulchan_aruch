Kneading troughs in which chametz is kneaded — one may not rely on washing them with hot water and scraping the chametz from them, for it is impossible to scrape them so that less than a kezayit total remains among them all, and the vessel combines them; therefore one must give them as a gift to a non-Jew until after Pesach, or plaster them with clay. And the same applies to dough in wooden vessels that cannot be removed.And it is good to do so for all vessels in which flour is kept all year (Mahariv), and likewise for vessels in which bread is kept all year; and a cloth that lay on a sack of flour — shaking is not effective, and it requires laundering in order to use it on Pesach (Maharil).)
