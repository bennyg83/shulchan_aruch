(א) Seif 4 — a thing, etc. Since it says there and there he is not obligated to destroy — it implies forbidden in eating there, etc., and permitted in benefit — plainly no need that it was charred, etc.; but in eating forbidden.

(ב) And even though there is not. He holds that even though it was nullified before Pesach in sixty, on Pesach it revives in any amount; likewise many Geonim. Nevertheless see siman 447 seif 4.
