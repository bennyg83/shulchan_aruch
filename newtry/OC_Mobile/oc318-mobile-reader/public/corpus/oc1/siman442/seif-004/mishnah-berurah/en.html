(א) Was mixed into it — meaning before Pesach; nevertheless forbidden to eat, for it reawakens on Pesach.

(2) chametz - we used chametz from Alia and only because it was intermingled it is not suitable for eating. It was a stiff chametz before it went moldy. Many of the last ones are allowed to eat it and even when it is intermingled during Passover:

(ג) Every person — only for the ill.

(ד) Even though permitted to keep, forbidden to eat — and even though unfit for eating, since it is his food he considered it significant.

(ה) Behold it is forbidden to eat — nevertheless for benefit permitted when the mixture was made before Pesach, like molded bread above seif 3. For healing see siman 466 among Acharonim.

(ו) That some disagree and permit, etc. — he holds since already nullified before Pesach we do not say it reawakens; but what he concluded and so we hold is wondrous, for he wrote there the custom to be lenient and say it was nullified applies only when mixed moist with moist, not dry with dry; afterward I found Chemed Moshe already raised this.
