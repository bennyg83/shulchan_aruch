And some are stringent, etc. The reason is that within thirty days the form of chametz may still remain. If it was done earlier, it has already hardened and is not considered recognizable chametz. The distinction between thirty days here and two months in siman 447 is that there the concern is taste entering wine, while here the issue is keeping chametz in the house.
And in winter days. The scholars who were stringent about women's scarves apparently considered the starch visible from outside; nevertheless, where there is less than a kezayis, all agree that it need not be destroyed.
