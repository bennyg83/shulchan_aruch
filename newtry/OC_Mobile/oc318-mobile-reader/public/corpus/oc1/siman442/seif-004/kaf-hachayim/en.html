(4) And if no substance of chametz only taste (e.g. dish cooked in one vessel with chametz absorbed taste without substance — Gra) — Olat Shabbat: forbidden to retain on Pesach and transgresses ben yomo; Chok Yosef agrees; Chayei Adam: Poskim' implication not so; even ta'am ke'ikar d'oraisa only for eating from mesharet not bal yera'eh; concludes in any case forbidden to retain d'rabbanan — destroy if found within Pesach; if transgressed no prohibition; permitted in benefit; eating prohibition only general stringency; likewise all permitted d'oraisa to retain — d'rabbanan forbidden l'chatchila — burn within Pesach d'rabbanan; if transgressed permitted. Makor Chayim letter 1; Gra 7; Chayei Adam klal 121 letter 1.
