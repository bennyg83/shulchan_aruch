Anything into which ḥametz was mixed but is not food for humans at all, or not food for everyone—such as theriaca and the like—even though it may be kept, is forbidden to eat until after Pesaḥ; even if it contains only the slightest amount of ḥametz, it is forbidden to eat. Rema: Later (Siman 447:4) it will be explained that some disagree and permit it if it was nullified before Pesaḥ, and this is the accepted ruling. :
