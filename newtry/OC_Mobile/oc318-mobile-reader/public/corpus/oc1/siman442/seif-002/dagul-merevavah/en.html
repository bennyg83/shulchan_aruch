Rav Ashi at the end of the discussion: otherwise how did they prove from "from the time of chametz" that to destroy them one does not transgress — behold there is me'ilah on the value of a perutah and bal yera'eh on a kezayit; and perhaps it deals where there is not a kezayit but there is the value of a perutah — rather certainly that also for bal yera'eh half a measure is forbidden from the Torah; and see Tractate Shevuot 16b in Tosafos s.v. mah tamei and examine, for it implies we do not say regarding what is not a prohibition of eating that half a measure is forbidden from the Torah; and perhaps for bal yera'eh, since it is a food matter, we say as for eating half a measure is forbidden from the Torah, so too for bal yera'eh; and what appears more correct to me: in a matter that is not a prohibition of eating, half a measure is not forbidden from the Torah but forbidden d'rabbanan — if so Tosafos' words in Shevuot 16b fit properly, and also Tosafos' words in Pesachim 29b are correct that also what is forbidden d'rabbanan again has no me'ilah, for they wrote there that this which one does not redeem is only d'rabbanan and he does not commit me'ilah.
