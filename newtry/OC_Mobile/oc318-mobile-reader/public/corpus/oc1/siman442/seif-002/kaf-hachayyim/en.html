2) There. One transgresses regarding a mixture containing chametz, etc. The Shulchan Aruch uses the language of Rambam in chapter 4, who rules according to Rabbi Eliezer that a chametz mixture is subject to a prohibition, while hardened, inedible chametz is not subject to any prohibition. It therefore does not also write that one transgresses regarding hardened chametz, despite the unattributed Mishnah in the chapter Eilu Ovrin. This is also explained below in siman 447, se'if 12. See there. Nevertheless, initially it is forbidden to retain it during Pesach. Chok Yaakov, subsection 2. If one retained it, it is not prohibited after Pesach, as stated below in siman 447, se'if 12. See there.
