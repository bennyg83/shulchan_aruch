(Taz s.k. 1) And a proof for this from what is written. Note: it appears that the Tur's language should be corrected. Certainly regarding mixtures he wrote specifically that there is no negative prohibition in mixtures, which implies that there is nevertheless a Torah-level prohibition. What he wrote, 'but only a rabbinic prohibition,' continues on 'and not chametz nukshah.' If you say that since we include it from 'all,' then nukshah too should be included in the prohibition, one can answer that it is reasonable to establish the inclusion as referring to mixtures for prohibition, since even without this, when it is intact, it is forbidden according to the Torah as a half-measure.
