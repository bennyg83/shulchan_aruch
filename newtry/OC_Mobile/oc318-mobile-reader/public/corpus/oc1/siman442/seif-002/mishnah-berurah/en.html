(א) That molded, etc. — specifically that it molded before its prohibition time; if it molded after its prohibition time, even if molded so much it is unfit for a dog, nevertheless must destroy, since it was obligated before it molded.
(2) (j) From eating the dog - then he is not required to be discolored like an ash in the alma, but if he is not disqualified from eating a dog, even if a person has a bite and it is not suitable, he must still burn it like finished vinegar because it is appropriate to mix other pulps with it:
(ג) And poultice — a compress made from flour and figs and the like that a person chews and places on a wound.
(ד) So that he not forget, etc. — and although for other eating and benefit prohibitions we do not require a partition, here it is different because they do not avoid it all year — we are concerned lest he come to eat it; therefore a partition is required for recognition; and covering a vessel on top of the chametz does not suffice, lest he remove the vessel for his use and the chametz be exposed and they did not permit covering a vessel [in siman 446 seif 1] except for chametz found in his house on Yom Tov when a partition cannot be made, and also only for a short time, for at night he will burn it.
