(א) Seif 2 — bread, etc., and it was disqualified — there 45b: Tanna: bread, etc.; Rif wrote: if it was disqualified from being eaten by a dog one need not burn it, as written "like whom," etc. — they stated a general rule, etc. — see Tosafos 21b s.v. charco.

(ב) And melogma. Tosefta chapter 3; and even though in Yerushalmi chapter 2 halachah 6 only when it putrefied and afterward became chametz — there it deals with putrefaction within Pesach. Nevertheless one must say so, for the kneading-trough of the tanners to which he did not put hides and bread that molded — all became chametz before, only within Pesach; and as written in seif 9 we require specifically before its time — see there. But Ra'avad, Rosh, and Tur hold even before Pesach only when it putrefied and afterward became chametz. Tur wrote: what they did not distinguish in Tosefta regarding kilyor, ritya, and aspalnith — also because they immediately putrefy.
