And a plaster. This means a dressing made of flour, figs, and the like, which a person chews and places on a wound. The Rambam, in his commentary to the Mishnah in Shevi'is, chapter 8, mishnah 1, and in Keilim, chapter 24, mishnah 3, explained melugma as a mouthful, that one chews wheat for his wound.
