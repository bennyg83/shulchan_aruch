6) There: A handful. It says 'the hand full,' rather than merely 'a hand full,' meaning the left hand, with which one cleans himself. Bach; Eliyah Rabbah there. This makes a practical difference if the left hand is smaller than the right, or regarding whether a left-handed person may take only a left-handful. Mishbetzos Zahav there. See above, siman 3, paragraph 20. See also Eshel Avraham from Hagahos Kodesh, who inclines to permit cleaning oneself with a finger because smoothing does not apply to those hairs, nor does squeezing, and rabbinic prohibitions were permitted because of human dignity; see there. Orach Chaim, paragraph 2. One must nevertheless take care not to wipe forcefully, lest hairs be detached.
