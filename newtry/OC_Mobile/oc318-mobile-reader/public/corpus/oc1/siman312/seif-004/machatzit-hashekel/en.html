(s.k. 4) He wipes, etc. — nevertheless it is preferable to wipe with it than with a clod — meaning — specifically to wipe with shards of earthenware where there is danger, for it is common that they are sharp lest the lower teeth of the intestines break; therefore permitted to wipe with a clod; but rims of earthenware vessels that are not sharp and there is no concern of sorcery that is not common — it was not permitted to wipe with a clod:
