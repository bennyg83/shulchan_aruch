I question what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 3 s.k. 12, that fire dominates and lower teeth fall out — this is from sorcery. If so, how did they challenge in the sugya of Shabbos 1 on that of wool and grass — one wipes with grass? Behold one who wipes with something fire dominates over — lower teeth fall out from it. Behold this is only from sorcery, and a clod is preferable like pottery; and even though according to law a clod is preferable to dry grass, which are sharp and cut the flesh, nevertheless they should have challenged from this reason. But not from the reason that fire dominates over it, and requires study.
