[6] If their imprint is recognizable. Rabbenu Yerucham explains that they have not sunk into the dirt, but their imprint remains visible above the ground.
[7] It is permitted, etc. Even if the clod lies on the ground it may be taken, because lifting it from the ground is only rabbinically prohibited and the Sages did not impose the decree in a matter of human dignity. But pulling grass from a clod is biblically liable even when the clod is not resting on the ground. One may not take a clod to scrape himself, whether thick or thin, because this appears like demolition (Magen Avraham).
