(10) And there is no concern of danger, etc. Meaning due to sorcery as above, for he holds there is no concern of sorcery except in earthenware that is not fit for handling; and this supports what I explained in siman 3 section 10; but Bach understood that also in handles of utensils there is concern of sorcery — only on Shabbat they permitted, for preferable to a clod that is muktzeh; therefore he wondered in siman 3 on Rabbeinu Yerucham who wrote that even on a weekday one wipes with handles of earthenware vessels — against the Gemara; and according to what I explained it is fine; but I found in Yerushalmi end of chapter HaMotsi'i — it is not only earthenware but even handles of barrels — until here — implying there is also concern of sorcery in handles; and forbidden on a weekday — only on Shabbat permitted against a clod; and requires study:
