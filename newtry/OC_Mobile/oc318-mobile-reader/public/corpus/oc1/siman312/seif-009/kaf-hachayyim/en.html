9) There: The volume of a small pestle. This means the size of the foot of a small pestle used for spices. Beis Yosef, according to Rabbeinu Tam's understanding of Rabbeinu Chananel's version, which is also explicit in Yerushalmi; see there. This is an amount fit for cleaning oneself once. Levush. Rif and Rosh, however, read 'the size of the head of a small pestle,' and Tur likewise has this reading.
