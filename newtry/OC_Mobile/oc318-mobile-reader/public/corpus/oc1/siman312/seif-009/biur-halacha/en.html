(א) In a ploughed field, etc. — see Taz who wrote it is first ploughing and stands for sowing, and he explains Rashi there, in the Gemara; and it implies that in an ordinary field not yet ploughed or in second ploughing after sowing we are not concerned about leveling furrows; and although the essence of the prohibition of leveling furrows applies in all cases [and it is not mentioned anywhere in Gemara and poskim what difference this makes] nevertheless they only decreed lest he come to level furrows in a ploughed field; and the reason as Mishna Berurah wrote — in first ploughing furrows are common; and it appears to me this is the intent of Peri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav section 3, for otherwise his words are very puzzling:
