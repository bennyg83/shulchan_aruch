(9) There. The measure of a small clod. Meaning the measure of a small foot of a mortar of spices. Beit Yosef per the view of Rabbeinu Tam that appears to him like the reading of R' Chananel; and so is explained in Yerushalmi — examine. And this is that it is fit to wipe with once. Levush.
