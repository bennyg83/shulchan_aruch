(s.k. 8) Even when fully inserted — since he inserts it regularly, etc. — like one who takes out in his mouth, etc.; it is implied that a thing fully inserted in the body, etc. — and if so permitted to swallow, etc.; and in Sefer Teshuvah he brought Magen Avraham's words, etc.: and he wrote: Magen Avraham wrote that a thing fully inserted in the body that one does not insert in order to return and remove it regularly — permitted; and therefore permitted, end of his words; he understood that he permitted swallowing gold and pearls because one does not insert them in order to remove them regularly, only by chance; and in my humble opinion Magen Avraham's language does not imply so, for Magen Avraham did not write except "and it is implied that a thing fully inserted in the body is permitted"; and Teshuvah added "and one does not insert it in order to return," etc.; if so, according to Magen Avraham's language the essence of the permission is missing; and what is written "a thing fully inserted in the body," etc. — this does not cause the permission, for also the key is fully inserted in the body; also in the Tur's own words it is difficult: since his intent is to return and remove it — why would this thing cause prohibition; and more difficult according to Rambam whom the Tur brought: this litigant says so, etc.: and if fully inserted in the body it would appear permitted to go out with it, for it is not similar to that which we learned in the Mishnah in the chapter HaMotzi: one who takes out in his mouth and in his armpit is exempt; and all exemptions of Shabbat — exempt but forbidden; for there when he opens his mouth and extends his armpit the load is visible; but here when it is fully swallowed in the body it would appear permitted; however he does not permit it because he inserts it regularly in order to return and remove it; even though it is not actually similar to those that are exempt but forbidden, etc., end of his words; it is implied from the language: since he inserts it regularly in order to return and remove it — it is somewhat similar;
