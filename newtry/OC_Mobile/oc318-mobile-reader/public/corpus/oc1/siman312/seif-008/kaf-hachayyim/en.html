8) If he has a privy near his home that no other person enters, he may not bring stones there on Shabbos, even the volume of a small pestle, because he should have designated them the previous day. Magen Avraham there in the name of Hagahos Ashri, Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 26, and Tosafos; Tosefes Shabbos there; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 3. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, however, inclines to be lenient and writes that Netzach Yosef rules this way; see there. This also appears to be Beis Yosef's view at the end of this siman.
