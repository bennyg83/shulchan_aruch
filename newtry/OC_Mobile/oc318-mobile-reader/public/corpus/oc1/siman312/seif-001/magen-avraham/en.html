A designated place. For if some remain for him for the evening he will wipe with them in the morning [Rashi and Ran], and it appears to me that this applies if he always enters one place; if so, even if some remain he may wipe with them in the morning, even though others also enter there, and one need not be concerned lest another take them to wipe; nevertheless it is permitted to bring a full hand, for it is not moving not for a need, for what difference to me whether it is he or another; and so is clear in R' Shimon of Shantz in the gloss, and some say not like the view of Levush that here it is one trouble, and it is also possible that he himself will need them; therefore it is permitted. And what is written "and if he has no designated place" — meaning that he goes sometimes to this place and sometimes to another place; if so, if he afterward goes to another place, it turns out that he moved first not for a need; therefore he brings it only as much as a small spoonful, etc., for this reason he was precise and wrote "and if he has no designated place," etc., and so too in the rulings of the Rosh, and so is clear in Tosafos at the end of s.v. "what about bringing them up" there, and in Sukkah daf 36; and if he has a bathroom adjacent to his house where no other person enters — forbidden to bring in stones on Shabbat, for this is preparing from erev Shabbat [Hagahot Alfasi and Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 26], and so too Tosafos as stated, not like Bach there:
