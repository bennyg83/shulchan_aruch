Magen Avraham section 1 — to one place. For Bach explained designated place means for him alone; but if others also enter there — forbidden lest he trouble on Shabbat for others as in siman 313 where each must trouble for himself — therefore Magen Avraham wrote thus; rather designated means fixed; and for this reason he was precise in Shulchan Aruch and wrote "and if he has no fixed place" and not designated place; and the word "for him" is not precise and does not come to exclude if it is also for others:
