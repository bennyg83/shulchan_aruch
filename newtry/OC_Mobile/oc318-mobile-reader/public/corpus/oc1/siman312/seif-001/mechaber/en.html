What one who needs to relieve himself may use to wipe on Shabbat. 10
sections: Out of concern for human dignity, the Sages permitted handling stones for wiping — even carrying them up to a roof, which is extra effort, is permitted. One who has a designated place for a bathroom may bring a handful of stones with him. If he has no fixed place he may bring with him the size of a small pestle. If it is evident that a stone was used for wiping, it is permitted to bring it even if it is very large, or even if there are many more than a handful — since they were used for wiping they have been designated for that purpose.Rema: Some say it is only permitted to carry stones in a courtyard. (Rashi, end of ch. HaMotzi Yayin, R. Yerucham 12:16, and) Ran) Some say even from a karmelit to a private domain is permitted, since that too is only a Rabbinic prohibition and the Sages permitted it out of concern for human dignity. (Tosafot, ch. Lulav HaGazul, and Hagahot Mordechai, ch. HaZorek).
