One who needs to relieve himself — with what may one wipe on Shabbat. And in
it 10 seifim: Because of human dignity they permitted moving stones for wiping, even to bring them up to his roof with him, which is extra effort — permitted. And one who has a special place for a bathroom may bring in with him stones for wiping — a full handful. And if he has no fixed place he may bring in with him the measure of a small pestle. And if it is recognizable on a stone that he wiped with it, he is permitted to bring it in even if it is very large, or even if there are many more than a full handful — he is permitted to take all of them, for since he wiped with them they have been prepared for that purpose.Some say it is permitted to move stones only in a courtyard (Rashi end of chapter HaMotzi Yayin, Rabbeinu Yeruham part 2 chapter) 16, and Ran). And some say even from a karmelis to a private domain is also permitted, for this too is only a d'rabbanan prohibition, and because of human dignity they permitted it (Tosafos chapter Lulav HaGazul and Hagahot Mordechai chapter HaZorek).
