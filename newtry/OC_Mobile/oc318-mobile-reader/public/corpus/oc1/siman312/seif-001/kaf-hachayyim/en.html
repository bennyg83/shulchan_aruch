1) [Seif 1] Because of human dignity, etc. Human dignity overrides a negative commandment in the Torah, namely the prohibition of 'you may not hide yourself.' The verse states, 'and you shall hide yourself from them,' from which the Sages expounded that there are occasions when one may hide himself, such as when he is an elder, and so forth. Here too, rabbinic moving, which the Sages linked to the prohibition of 'do not deviate,' is overridden in the interest of human dignity. Levush; Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1.
