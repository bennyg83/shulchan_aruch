(א) Stones — and the same applies to clods or any other such thing that is not fit for moving:

(ב) A designated place — meaning the place is designated for him for the bathroom; since it is designated for him to enter there always — if so, even if some stones remain for him in the evening he can wipe with them in the morning; therefore they permitted him to bring in a full hand, and it is not moving not for a need; and even though that place is not designated for him alone and it is possible others will take the stones to wipe — we are not concerned; what difference to me whether it is he or another; and it is also possible that he himself will need them afterward; therefore permitted. And what is written afterward "and if he has no designated place" — meaning that he goes sometimes to this place and sometimes goes to another place; therefore forbidden to bring in more than what he needs for one time certainly — namely the measure of a small spoonful; for if he brings in more and some remain, it turns out that he moved first not for a need. And Magen Avraham wrote in the name of the poskim: that which they permit in a designated bathroom to bring stones there on Shabbat — deals specifically when it is not designated for him alone but others also enter there, for he could not prepare from erev Shabbat from concern lest others take them; and especially if it is a bathroom in a field that is trouble to prepare from erev Shabbat because it is distant; but if it was adjacent to his house and also designated for him alone, that others do not enter there, and even from his household — one should not permit to bring stones there at all, for he should have prepared from erev Shabbat. And Ateret Zekenim sides to be lenient in this in every manner, like the view of Levush; and the reason is because a person does not have in mind to prepare stones there from erev Shabbat:

(ג) A full hand — and even four or five stones [Rashi]:

(ד) The measure of a small spoonful — in Rif and Rosh it states: the measure of the head of a small spoon with which one grinds spices; and so is implied in the Tur:

(ה) And if recognizable, etc. — it is implied from this language that if it is not recognizable, even though it is known that he wiped with it — forbidden to take from it a full hand if he has no designated place; and some are lenient in this, for since it is known that he wiped with it — it is as if recognizable:

(ו) That he wiped with it — meaning from erev Shabbat; and it deals when he did not designate them explicitly from erev Shabbat for this; for if he designated them — even if he never wiped with them — permitted to bring in as many as he wishes; and the same applies to smooth paper that he is particular about — designation from erev Shabbat also helps, for thereby the name muktzeh is removed from it, and permitted to bring into the bathroom as many as he wishes; and if he did not designate them — he is not permitted to bring into a bathroom that is not designated, only according to his need for this time alone; and forbidden to tear the paper on Shabbat, as is explained later in siman 340 seif 13 there in Mishna Berurah:

(ז) Permitted to bring it in — see above in siman 3 seif 11 in Mishna Berurah there:
