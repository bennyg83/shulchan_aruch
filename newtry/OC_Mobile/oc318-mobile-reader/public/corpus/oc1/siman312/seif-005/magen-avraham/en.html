Because they are sharp. Requires further study, for in siman 3 it is written the reason is because one who wipes with a thing over which fire dominates — his lower teeth fall out, and so in the gemara; and the question is asked in Chullin daf 16: one may not wipe with reed peel, and they challenge: let it be derived that one who wipes with a thing over which fire dominates, etc., and they answer: wiping the mouth of a wound we are speaking of — implying that other grasses one does not cut the flesh; but nevertheless in chapter 26 he wrote that some have the reverse version: with moist ones one may not wipe because they loosen the teeth, and with dry ones one may wipe, there; and requires further study. Permitted to take a clod from a thick fence that is not recognizable and appears so; but from a thin fence it is forbidden because it makes a hole and is like demolishing (Yeshuot Malko Bava Kamma chapter 7 seif 40, and so in the gemara and Rosh); but in Tur Choshen Mishpat siman 374 he wrote to take a clod from it — implying from the field; but from the fence — forbidden, and so in Rif and Rambam who omitted even on Shabbat — we infer that he holds that from a fence it is forbidden to take, only from a field permitted; and this they already wrote in the laws of Shabbat; and see in the gemara in the incident of Mar Zutra the pious:
