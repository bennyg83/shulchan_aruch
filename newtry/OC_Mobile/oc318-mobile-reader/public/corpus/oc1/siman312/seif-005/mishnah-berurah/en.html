(א) That they are smooth — for when they are not smooth there is concern of danger in the matter; better to wipe with a clod even though it is muktzeh:

(ב) There is no concern of danger, etc. — and even though still in this there is concern of sorcery, as above in siman 3; nevertheless they did not permit because of this to wipe with a clod that is muktzeh, since he has earthenware to wipe with that has no muktzeh concern, as is explained; and especially according to what Rama wrote there that nowadays we are not concerned about sorcery in this:

(ג) Wipe with grasses — for it is not muktzeh:

(ד) But with dry ones, etc. — rather wipe with a clod even though it is muktzeh. It is in the gemara: permitted to relieve behind his fellow's field fence, and permitted to take a clod to wipe, even on Shabbat — meaning from the field; but one may not take from the fence, even from a thick fence that does not make a hole, for it appears like demolishing:
