Shulchan Aruch seif 2 — and they were embedded. Meaning they were embedded in the ground from rain; and the same applies if he takes them from there — it is like demolishing or grinding; the Gemara teaches that if their imprint is recognizable above the ground — permitted and it is not called demolishing or grinding; and it appears it deals when part of them is exposed so he can grasp them — as with a turnip in the previous siman seif 8 — requires study:
