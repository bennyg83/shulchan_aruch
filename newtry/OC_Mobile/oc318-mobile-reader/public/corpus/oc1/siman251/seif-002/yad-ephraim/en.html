Seif kaf — and it appears the reason for the leniency, etc. And if so, all melachot permitted on chol hamoed, etc. It appears to explain his words: Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Kol Bo that it is forbidden to cut hair from a Jewish book on Erev Shabbat and Yom Tov from minchah and above; it is possible to permit haircutting as an act of a layman, for it is no worse than chol hamoed where we say a layman sews in his usual way — until here. The world practiced leniency in the matter; and wherever, etc., until here. It appears from his words that initially he held even a layman's act is forbidden, and on this he wrote it is possible to permit, etc. The reason appears that here there are two matters: one from the side of melacha since Shabbat holiness already took effect, etc.
