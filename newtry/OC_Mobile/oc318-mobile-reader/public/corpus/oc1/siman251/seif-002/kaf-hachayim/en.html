(ב) There. One who does melacha on Erev Shabbat, etc. — but prakmatia is permitted. Magen Avraham seif kaf. However, the Arizal, letter 1, wondered at Magen Avraham's words cited and his intent is that melacha and prakmatia are equal for this matter, see there. And so is the Mechaber's intent and Nefesh HaChaim letter 1, see there. But Tosafot and Razah chapter 3 agreed with Magen Avraham that prakmatia is permitted, see there. And so Shoneh Halachot siman 496 in Hagah Beit Yosef note 5: actions done every day of carrying and giving in merchandise on Erev Shabbat and Erev Yom Tov until sunset without opening mouth and without whispering, see there. Nevertheless it appears that even for those who permit, one should be careful, etc.
