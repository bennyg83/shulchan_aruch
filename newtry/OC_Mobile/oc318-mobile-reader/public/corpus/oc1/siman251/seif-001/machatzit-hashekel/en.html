(א) (ס"ק א) One who does, etc. — Bnei Bishan. In Pesachim page 50b they asked R' Yehudah if it is permitted to travel on Erev Shabbat from Tyre to Sidon; Rashi there explained there was market day in Sidon on Erev Shabbat; since their fathers were wealthy and did not need that market day, they were strict on themselves — but their sons needed it because they were not wealthy: whether they must be strict like their fathers' custom. In Shabbat page 19a it teaches: one does not set sail on a ship less than three days before Shabbat, etc.; from Tyre to Sidon even on Erev Shabbat is permitted; Rashi wrote: from Tyre to Sidon is not, etc.

(ב) And so in chapter 2 of Shabbat, etc. — to stop the city and shops. Per the world's understanding, Magen Avraham brings proof from what was taught there that six tekiyot are blown on Erev Shabbat: tekiyah 1 to stop the people from field melacha; 2 to stop the city and shops — thus from tekiyah 1 melacha was already forbidden yet shops remained open until tekiyah 2; therefore they attacked him in Sefer Arizal and Sefer Tosefet Shabbat and Choshen Mishpat that on the contrary he brings proof against himself, for Rashi explained the second is to stop the city from melacha, as Magen Avraham wrote seif 4; and on the contrary melacha and closing shops share the same time from tekiyah 2 onward, and tekiyah 1 was, etc.
