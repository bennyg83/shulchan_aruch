Regarding Magen Avraham, seif katan 1, in the incident of the people of Beishan: This requires study, for perhaps it occurred before minchah. Nechalas Shivah raised the same objection. The Magen Avraham also appears to contradict what he wrote in seif katan 4, as Eliyah Rabbah likewise noted. See Tosafos Shabbos.
