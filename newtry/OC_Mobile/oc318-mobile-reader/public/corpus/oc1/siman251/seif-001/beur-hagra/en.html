Seif 1, Some explain. Since the Gemara there says “close to Minchah,” it does not imply that there is no distinction between Friday and the eve of Pesach except with respect to the period close to Minchah. Moreover, what was the premise of the question? The interval between midday and Minchah Ketanah is very great.
And some explain. Since the Gemara there says here “from midday,” and does not likewise say “close to Minchah,” as it says there on 99b. There is also proof from the six blasts sounded to cause cessation from labor; see Magen Avraham.
And specifically when he performs. For even on the eve of Pesach one may set hens upon eggs, clear away refuse, and place eggs under hens; all the more so on Friday.
Therefore. For this is permitted even on Chol HaMoed, Moed Katan 18b; and Friday is more lenient even than the eve of Pesach, as stated there in Pesachim, and certainly more lenient than Chol HaMoed, which is more stringent even than the eve of Pesach, as stated there on 55b at the end: “This presents no difficulty,” etc.
