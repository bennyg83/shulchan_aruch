(א) If he had dough — meaning: even after the time of its prohibition, nullification suffices, since at that hour it had not yet become chametz — its law is like before the time of its prohibition.
(ב) And he is occupied elsewhere — meaning: because of this it is impossible for him to come to his house immediately to bake it, and he fears lest it become chametz before he comes home.
(ג) But if it became chametz — and if the dough stood the measure of mil it certainly became chametz and nullification does not help.
(ד) Nullification does not help — and he transgresses every hour on bal yera'eh, and he must go immediately to his house and burn.
