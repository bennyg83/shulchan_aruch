"Meal" — Taz Pesachim 49:1; and the Beit Yosef holds that R' Yehuda and R' Yosi disagree also regarding the second of betrothal — also according to R' Yosi it is a mitzvah; and even the meal of gifts — according to R' Yehuda the third is a mitzvah; that third without gifts R' Yosi agrees is not a mitzvah (Bach; and one may say first and second are dear to him that his heart be coarse in it, and from here onward not). And this is what is stated in the Gemara: and now that Erev Pesach — this is explained thus in the baraita — except the second of betrothal without gifts; and regarding both they disagree. And it said "and now that Erev Pesach" to say that it is more reasonable to say that the Mishnah is R' Yehuda, that an unspecified betrothal meal implies the first — and the halachah is so. And this is what the Tur stated: first meal of betrothal or of gifts; and he ruled regarding the second of betrothal like R' Yehuda per the plain meaning of the Mishnah; and gifts like R' Yosi, that both are included — see there. For if like Rashi's explanation, it should have said to eat betrothal meal or gifts or first meal betrothal and second of gifts — and Taz explained like Bach and like Rashi, may he rest in Eden, that it is one dispute; and likewise Magen Avraham 9 like Bach; and if so, there it will be explained. And a meal at the time of match that they call knas — Eliyah Rabbah: see responsa Chavot Yair siman 70 that certainly it is not a meal of mitzvah. And what Eliyah Rabbah 7 and Chayei Yisrael 10 wrote regarding match with purchase — one may say that they send gifts then, as Magen Avraham 9 stated; but match without gifts — not; and see Ba'er Heitev. And for what he stated that specifically with gifts — and see Chok Yisrael, who expanded; see there. And Magen Avraham 9; and if so, there it will be explained.
