(8) [Levush] "Where nullification, mitzvah" etc. There it is explained that after the sixth hour, when it is impossible to nullify, one returns — for the positive commandment of "tashbitu" is more stringent, for every hour one transgresses upon it, which is not so with circumcision and Pesach; nevertheless it appears to me that saving a life is preferable to "tashbitu" of chametz, even on Pesach — for behold it overrides even Pesach, which has karet (Magen Avraham). And it is somewhat difficult, for he contradicts himself — he wrote that chametz is more stringent than circumcision and Pesach, since every hour one transgresses upon it, even though circumcision and Pesach have karet.
