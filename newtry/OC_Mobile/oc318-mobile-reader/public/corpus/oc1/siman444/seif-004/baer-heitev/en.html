On condition. Meaning: that one gives it to him unspecifiedly and does not tell him to take them out to the public domain. And for us, who have no public domain, it is permitted to command him to take them out. And to give him much at once — Magen Avraham and Taz. Mahari"v wrote that if there is no non-Jew to clean the house, an Israelite may clean by changing his garment. And Bach wrote that the custom is to give to dogs and not to a pig.
