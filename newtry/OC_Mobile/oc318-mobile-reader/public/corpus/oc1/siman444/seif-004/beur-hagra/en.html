(א) Seif 4 — "on condition that he not." Magen Avraham; and as stated in the Yerushalmi — one does not give to a non-Jew on condition that he go out, as stated in siman 325; see there.

(ב) "To the public domain." Taz; and likewise in siman 325 it is an issur of shevut shevut in a place of mitzvah as stated in chapter 6 of Eruvin.

(ג) "And a small amount." Magen Avraham; and as stated in Shabbos — one gives food; and Tosafos wrote specifically food — as Rambam wrote "to eat them."
