(א) (s.k. 4) One does not etc. — in Bach and Peri Chadash he added, and these are his words: I practice in my house to cook in new pots prepared for Pesach, and with a new spoon they remove the dish from the pot and place it in a chametz bowl etc.; end of his words. And the reason appears to me: that which one does not cook porridge is because it sticks to the pot. And a swallowed pot is permitted to leave on Pesach, as Rashi wrote siman 461; but what sticks and is visible requires scraping and burning. And so that one not need to scrape on Shabbat, one does not cook porridge but meat and the like that do not stick.

(ב) But Mahari"v was concerned that sometimes from meat and the like too a little remains stuck; and granted if it was cooked in a new pot — what of it? For there is no complete leavening in what sticks; not so if they cooked in chametz vessels — then the meat that cooks in it receives from the chametz absorbed in the pot and the meat too becomes chametz. And if meat remained stuck he would need scraping again; therefore one cooks in a new vessel. And therefore Peri Chadash added: since the way is to eat the meat in a chametz bowl — for they eat chametz bread with it — if so, when one pours from a new pot prepared for Pesach while the dish is still warm into a chametz bowl, b'dieved it is not needed; but l'chatchila one should not do so, as is explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 105 seif 3 in the gloss, because of vapor that rises from one to the other. And vapor of chametz rises to the Pesach pot (and see there in my gloss); therefore one removes with a new spoon, and all the more so one cannot remove with a chametz spoon.
