In Magen Avraham seif kaf 13 — and to this the Tur intended. NB: for in the first passage they wrote "one who goes to make an eruv" — meaning with his legs to the domain; but in the second passage they wrote "went to the evening" and they did not write to make an eruv, but to the evening — and that is with bread, since he goes to his rabbi; and it is simple.
