(א) He shall shake out the tablecloth. For the crumbs are trampled underfoot and also have no measure and are nullified (Rosh); see what is stated siman 434 seif 2 and siman 442 seif 7.

(ב) On condition that he not go out. Meaning: that one gives it to him unspecifiedly and does not tell him to take them out to the public domain; and siman 325 seif 2 — for us, who have no public domain, it is permitted to command him to take them out; and likewise Rashal in Biurei Semag; see there. And in a walled city it is permitted according to all; see what is stated siman 252 seif 61 and siman 446.

(ג) And a small amount. Meaning: each time one gives him a little; but much at once is forbidden, for it is evident that one gives him to take them out.
