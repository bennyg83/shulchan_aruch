"And covers" — Taz; and Magen Avraham 446:3 that at the fifth hour it is permitted to move and to give the chametz to a non-Jew — and this appears to be the opinion of Taz here, therefore he explained where there is no non-Jew or many and it is impossible to give a little etc.; see there. And to scatter and to crumble — there is a dispute whether it is permitted; but to a river or to a bathroom it is permitted — siman 446; and at the sixth hour to tell a non-Jew to throw to the river — one may say shevut shevut in a place of mitzvah is permitted — siman 446; and if so, there it will be further explained. And it also deals with where there is no dog to give; and therefore he covers the vessel; and see what is stated there in seif 7 from this.
