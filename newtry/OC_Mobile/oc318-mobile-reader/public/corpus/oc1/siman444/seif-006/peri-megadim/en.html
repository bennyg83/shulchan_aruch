And he covers. See the Taz and Magen Avraham siman 446, s.k. 3, that in the fifth hour it is permitted to move and give the chametz to a non-Jew. This appears to be the view of the Taz here; therefore he explained it as where there is no non-Jew, or where there is a lot and it is impossible to give a little, etc.; see there. Regarding scattering and crumbling, there is a dispute whether it is permitted, but to a river or to the privy is permitted; see siman 446. In the sixth hour, telling a non-Jew to throw it into the river, one may say that a shevus of a shevus in a place of mitzvah is permitted; see siman 446, and with Hashem's help it will be explained further there. It also speaks where there is no dog to give it to, and therefore he covers it with a utensil. See what I will write with Hashem's help in letter 7 regarding this.
