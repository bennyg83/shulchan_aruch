(א) Seif 1 — "We search..." As is stated there in siman 1 seif 1; and now that we rule as stated; Ramban regarding the time... and as stated in the Yerushalmi there; and I brought it in siman 453 seif 11.

(ב) "And we burn..." In the Rambam there — R' Elazar of Bartuta there 13:1; he holds accordingly and the halachah was decided according to him — Rambam. Not like the Rif, who wrote that R' Elazar bar Tzadok holds like R' Elazar b. Zadok and holds that what is written "and we burn everything..." refers to the end regarding terumah; and see in the hasagot there.

(ג) "And we leave over." There 13:1.

(ד) "For the meal." See what is stated siman 291 seif 2.

(ה) "Not with matzah." As stated in the Yerushalmi chapter 7 of Pesach, and the Rif and Rosh brought it in chapter 3 of Pesachim there — one who eats matzah on Erev Pesach...

(ו) "Rather... and one must." As Rashi stated chapter Erev Pesachim — one shall not eat... and they said there 107:2 that close to minchah ketanah... and there 100:2 here before the ninth hour etc. And Tosafos wrote there 99:2 s.v. "lo ye'achel"... and if you ask... one may answer that it deals with matzah ashirah... and the Rosh wrote that thus was the practice of Rabbenu Tam on Erev Pesach when it fell on Shabbat...

(ז) "And in lands... one fulfills..." For there is a dispute among poskim whether specifically bread or types of delicacies, and they decided according to those who say specifically bread; but where it is impossible one may rely etc., and as stated siman 291 seif 6; and see what is stated there. And one may do this even after the tenth hour as stated there 107:2 — and not like Tosafos, for Tosafos' proof that matzah ashirah is permitted on Erev Pesach from their question on what is stated on Erev Pesach close to minchah etc.; see there Tosafos on Erev Pesach 99:2 s.v. "samuch" — but Rambam explained there that it is stated and it is impossible to say that eating is forbidden in them; and as stated in siman 471 a little in seif 6. And as stated, therefore we do not practice with cooked matzah either; and so too in Zohar parashat Emor that this meal is entirely deferred — from which we learn that there is no remedy for the matter at all. And as the Rashba wrote that one does not fulfill the third meal except with bread, and it is impossible at all as stated above; and see Magen Avraham what he wrote in the name of the Mordechai and Bach and Kol Bo — and such is the main point.

(ח) "Or." Magen Avraham.
