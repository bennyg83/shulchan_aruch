(א) Food. The Acharonim wrote that nevertheless also at the morning meal he should stop his meal into two, for some say that one fulfills with this siman 291 seif 3. And if he forgot to separate challah on this Erev Shabbat from chametz bread that he baked for Shabbat — it is forbidden to eat from it — Magen Avraham in siman 456 seif 3, end of his words. And Ba'er Heitev which precedes me wrote, and it appears to me that he gives the remainder to a minor kohen in this matter; and understand well. And I did not understand his words — for what does this help? After all, in any event how did he separate challah? And understand well.

(ב) Fruits. And Shelah in the name of the Zohar wrote that R' Shimon bar Yochai would engage in Torah in place of the third meal. And Maharil wrote, and it would be fitting to make it with cooked matzah, but I have not seen people practice thus.
