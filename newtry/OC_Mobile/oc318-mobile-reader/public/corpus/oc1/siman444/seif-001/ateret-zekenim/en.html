"Rather with matzah ashirah" etc. And there are those who forbid matzah ashirah on the 14th day, for it is written "in the evening you shall eat matzot" — and matzah ashirah is also called matzah, for behold all the meal-offerings come as matzah and they were kneaded in oil. And it is possible to distinguish between matzah ashirah that has a mixture of water, through which a person fulfills under duress and it is forbidden on the 14th, and between matzah ashirah kneaded in fruit juice without water — then it does not come to leavening and one does not fulfill with it even under duress — and that matzah one should permit on the 14th before the tenth hour.
