(א) We search etc. — and one must bless on the bedika and also nullify as in other years on the night of the 14th [Acharonim].
(ב) The night of the 13th — for tomorrow on Erev Shabbat one cannot search, for one does not search by sunlight; and likewise above.
(ג) And we leave over etc. — and one must be careful to place it in a concealed place, as above siman 434 seif 1.
(ד) For the sake of Shabbat — and on this Shabbat they rise early to pray and do not prolong much so that they have time and not come to stumble [Acharonim].
(ה) Not with matzah — for it is forbidden to eat matzah on Erev Pesach, as below in siman 471.
(ו) Matzah ashirah — that is, that it is kneaded in fruit juice alone.
(ז) Before the tenth hour — for from then onward even matzah ashirah is forbidden to eat, so that one eat matzah with appetite, as below in siman 471 seif 2.
(ח) One fulfills etc. with kinds of fruit — see above in siman 291 seif 5 regarding views among poskim whether one must make it specifically with bread or it suffices with other things; therefore in such pressing need one may rely on the lenient; and see there that meat and fish are better than fruit. And likewise one may fulfill with a dish (such as knaidelach) — but there is a difference: this is permitted only before the tenth hour; whereas where one fulfills with fruit or meat and fish one may fulfill even after the tenth hour — but in any case be careful to eat only a little and not fill one's stomach so that one eat matzah with appetite. And see among Acharonim who wrote that it is also good to divide the morning bread meal into two, for some say one fulfills thus with the third meal; and likewise in Biur HaGra that it is proper to do so — but all this if one has time between to bless birkat hamazon and interrupt awhile so that it not be birkat ha'mazon she'eina tzricha.
