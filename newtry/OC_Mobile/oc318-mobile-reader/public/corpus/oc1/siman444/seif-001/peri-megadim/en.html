"And we leave over" — Taz. What he stated: the Tannaim who said "we burn everything" — leave food for two meals — so too Rashi Pesachim 49:1. However he explained thus, so that there not be four disputes — and R' Elazar of Bartuta and Pesachim 13:1. But it is not necessary that one must fulfill two meals with matzah ashirah — Magen Avraham, beginning of chapter, 1; or per the one who says there is no prohibition of matzah on Erev Pesach except after the prohibition of chametz — and see Magen Avraham, the Tur thus practiced Rashi to burn everything before Shabbat and not eat matzah; Beit Yosef wrote that one eats matzah for pleasure — and this is like the Rosh; Beit Yosef siman 471 that before the fifth hour matzah is permitted; also he did not burden him with matzah ashirah; see there. And what Taz stated "this burning" etc., as is proven in siman 446 seif 2, that before the fifth hour one must give to a non-Jew, for afterward it is forbidden in tilul; however telling a non-Jew regarding muktzeh — siman 476 in Magen Avraham 11; and also shevut shevut in a place of mitzvah, as in siman 307 seif 5. And what he stated "not the rest of the Shabbatot" — it is not proven in 446; Magen Avraham there 2 — to scatter to the wind is forbidden, as in siman 319 siman 17; see there. Magen Avraham chapter 1 heh 3 — fine crumbs very thin — one may say it is grinding; see there. And Bach seif 3 in the name of Ba'al HaMaor that it is permitted to crumble on Shabbat; and one who forbids, that it is weekday labor — not so much. And it is possible in 446 it is proven that it is forbidden to crumble; and if so, there it will be explained. And Magen Avraham 446 seif 3 and 5 — when there is a non-Jew it is permitted in tilul; and the deed of feeding in Pesachim 49:1 — one may say in the fourth or fifth hour, as Magen Avraham stated in 446. And see seif 5 here; and if so, there it will be explained.
