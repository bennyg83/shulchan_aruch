And we leave over. See the Taz. What he wrote, that the first tanna who says we destroy everything still leaves food for two meals, so wrote Rashi in Pesachim folio 49a. However, he explained it this way so that there should not be four disputes, and like Rabbi Elazar of Bartosa, and Pesachim folio 13a. But this is not compelled, for one can fulfill the two meals with egg matzah; see Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman. Or according to the one who says that the prohibition of matzah on the eve of Pesach applies only after the prohibition of chametz. See the Beis Yosef on what the Tur wrote, that Rashi practiced this way, to destroy everything before Shabbos and not eat matzah. The Beis Yosef wrote that he should eat matzah with appetite. This follows the Rosh, as in Beis Yosef siman 471, that before the fifth hour matzah is permitted; and they also did not trouble him with egg matzah; see there. What the Taz wrote, this destruction, etc., is as is proven in siman 446, letter 2, that before the fifth hour one must give it to a non-Jew, because afterward it is forbidden to move. However, regarding telling a non-Jew to move muktzeh, see siman 276, Magen Avraham s.k. 11; and also a shevus of a shevus in a place of mitzvah, as in siman 307 se'if 5. What he wrote, not on other Shabbosos, is not proven in siman 446; see Magen Avraham there, s.k. 2. Scattering to the wind is forbidden, as in siman 319 se'if 17; see there. See Mishneh LaMelech, chapter 1, law 3, that crumbling it very finely may be considered grinding; see there. See the Bach, se'if 3, in the name of the Baal HaMaor, that it is permitted to crumble on Shabbos; and the one who forbids because it is weekday activity did not forbid according to Torah law. It is possible that in siman 446 it is proven that crumbling is forbidden, and with Hashem's help it will be explained there. See Magen Avraham siman 446, letters 3 and 5, that where there is a non-Jew it is permitted with moving. The incident of Zunin in Pesachim folio 49a can be said to have been in the fourth or fifth hour, as the Magen Avraham wrote in siman 446. See letter 5 here, and with Hashem's help it will be explained.
