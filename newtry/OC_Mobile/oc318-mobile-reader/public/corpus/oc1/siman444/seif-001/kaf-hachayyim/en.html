1) [Se'if 1] One searches on the night of the thirteenth, etc. On Friday night one cannot carry a candle in order to search. Beis Yosef; Levush. One cannot search during the day of the thirteenth because initially it is forbidden to search during the day, as stated above in siman 433, subsection 3. See there.
2) There. One searches on the night of the thirteenth, etc. One recites the blessing over eliminating chametz and nullifies all chametz remaining in one's possession that was not found during this search. One must carefully guard both the chametz found during the search and the chametz retained for eating, placing it in a concealed location so that children or crawling creatures will not drag any away, as stated in siman 434. See there. Chemed Moshe, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1; Chayei Adam, rule 129, subsection 9; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 115, subsection 1.
3) There. And one retains food, etc. One must be careful to place it in a concealed location, as stated above in siman 434, se'if 1. See our words there in subsection 3.
4) There. And one retains food, etc. One should not suggest eliminating everything before Shabbos, retaining nothing, and eating matzah ashirah on Shabbos. Since not everyone is able to prepare enough matzah ashirah for all three meals, the Sages did not impose this burden. Beis Yosef.
5) There. And one retains food, etc. If one forgot on Friday to separate challah from chametz bread baked for this Shabbos, it is forbidden to eat from it and there is no remedy. Magen Avraham, siman 506, subsection 8; Chok Yaakov, siman 457, subsection 9; Eliyah Rabbah in this siman, subsection 1; Mekor Chaim, subsection 1; Chemed Moshe, subsection 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 12; Chayei Adam, rule 129, subsection 11. Nesiv Chaim in siman 506, however, challenges this ruling of Magen Avraham. He writes that the lenient authorities, Rabbeinu Chananel, Rosh, Baal HaTerumos, Raavad, Ran, and Rabbeinu Avigdor, are sufficiently authoritative to permit separating challah and giving it to a minor kohen or to an adult kohen who immersed after a seminal emission. It may even be given to a ritually impure kohen, who can nullify it in a majority and eat it, in accordance with the established ruling in Yoreh De'ah, siman 323. If no kohen is available, one may continue eating and leave less than an olive-sized quantity, covering it with a utensil until after Yom Tov, like other chametz remaining when no non-Jew is available, as stated in siman 444, se'if 5. See there. Responsa She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 133, and Responsa Panim Meiros, volume 2, siman 96, write likewise, as cited by Sha'arei Teshuvah in this siman, subsection 1. Bigdei Yesha, siman 506, and the novellae of Rabbi Akiva Eiger write likewise. Therefore, when no other bread can be found, it appears that one may rely upon the lenient authorities so that the Shabbos meals will not be lost. See below, at the end of siman 457 in the gloss, and our words there in subsection 16.
6) There. Food for two meals, for Friday night and Shabbos morning until one third of the day. Chemed Moshe, subsection 2. See above, siman 443, subsections 20 and 21.
7) There. For Shabbos. On this Shabbos one should not prolong the praises, lest the time when chametz becomes prohibited arrive. Sefer Chasidim, siman 314; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1. It is proper not to prolong the Shacharis and Mussaf prayers on this Shabbos, and certainly not to extend the singing. One should shorten them as much as possible so as not, Heaven forbid, to stumble by eating chametz after the time it becomes prohibited. Chemed Moshe, subsection 3. See above, siman 429, subsection 42.
8) There. Its time is after Minchah. Nevertheless, one should also divide the morning meal into two meals by reciting Birkas HaMazon between them, as explained by Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 2, because some authorities maintain that one fulfills the obligation this way. Shiltei HaGibborim and Mordechai, chapter 1 of Shabbos; Bach; Kol Bo; Magen Avraham, subsection 1. However, see above in Shulchan Aruch, siman 291, se'if 2, which rules that even after the fact one has not fulfilled the third meal if it was eaten before the time of Minchah. See there and our words there in subsection 12.
