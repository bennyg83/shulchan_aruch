"Food." Ba'er Heitev; and see She'eilat Ya'avetz siman 133 — he also challenged Ba'er Heitev; and see there that he wrote: that which implies from Magen Avraham that even to nullify oneg Shabbat, where he has no other bread — the law is not so; except in such a case it fits well to leave the vessels of separation in the mouth and hands without a blessing — and this is the separation, and a minor eats it, or an adult who immersed, or throw it in the pouch of the kohen; and where there is none, one should permit through nullification in a majority even for a non-Jew on Erev Shabbat. And in responsa Pnei Me'orot part 2 siman 36 he also wrote not like Magen Avraham; and he wrote that that which is forbidden to separate on Yom Tov — there, since he has another remedy to eat a little and leave a little; but on Erev Pesach that falls on Shabbat he does not have this remedy — one should permit through separation according to what we hold, challah outside the Land: eat and go etc., for the separation is not a d'oraisa remedy; and therefore it is well permitted to separate on Shabbat and give to a minor kohen and eat on Erev Shabbat.
