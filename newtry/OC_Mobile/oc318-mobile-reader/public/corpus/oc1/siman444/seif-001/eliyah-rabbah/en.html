(1) "The fourteenth when it falls" etc. They should bring water to knead on the night of the fourteenth, and not on Erev Shabbat, lest one precede or delay and desecrate Shabbat (Mateh Moshe). And it appears to me that there is a scribal error and it must say the night of the thirteenth — that is, Thursday night — as Rama wrote siman 455. And Beit Yosef wrote that even during bein hashemashot it is forbidden because of preparation; and the same law applies to those who bake on the second day of Yom Tov — they are forbidden to draw during bein hashemashot on Yom Tov, but rather on the night of Erev Yom Tov. And if one forgot to draw on Thursday night, he draws on Erev Shabbat while it is still day, or commands gentiles to draw during bein hashemashot, or takes drawn water in his house, since it is impossible through another matter (Magen Avraham). And if the cistern is in his courtyard, one may permit through pressing need to fill by many people one after another in small vessels. On Shabbat they should not prolong in prayer, but say it calmly, lest the time of the prohibition of chametz arrive — Sefer Chassidim siman 514. If one forgot to separate challah from chametz dough on Erev Shabbat, it is forbidden to eat from the bread and a remedy does not help to eat and separate tomorrow, as on other Shabbatot — for one must burn it (Magen Avraham siman 486).
