The fourteenth that falls, etc. They should bring water for kneading on the night of the fourteenth, and not on Friday, lest one do it too early or too late and desecrate Shabbos. Mateh Moshe. It appears to me that there is a scribal error, and it should say the night of the thirteenth, namely Thursday night, as the Rema wrote in siman 455. The Beis Yosef wrote that even bein hashmashos is forbidden because of preparation. The same law applies to those who bake on the second day of Yom Tov: they are forbidden to draw water during bein hashmashos on Yom Tov, but rather on the night of the eve of Yom Tov. If he forgot to draw on Thursday night, he should draw on Friday while it is still day, or command non-Jews to draw during bein hashmashos, or take drawn water in his house, since it is impossible in another manner. Magen Avraham. If the cistern is in his courtyard, one may permit, in a pressing situation, filling through many people one after another with small vessels. On Shabbos they should not prolong the prayer, but should say it calmly, lest the time of chametz prohibition arrive. Sefer Chassidim, siman 314. If he forgot to separate challah from chametz dough on Friday, it is forbidden to eat from the bread, and the remedy of eating and separating tomorrow does not help as on other Shabbosos, for he must destroy it. Magen Avraham, siman 506.
