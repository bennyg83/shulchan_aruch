And we leave over food for two meals. He stated two because according to the law one is not obligated to destroy it until close to Shabbos; if so, at the time of destruction he leaves only two meals for the need of Shabbos. But according to our custom in se'if 2, that one destroys everything before midday as in other years, one may leave more than two meals if he wants to eat more before night. So too the Rambam wrote without qualification: and he leaves from the chametz enough to eat from it until four hours of the Shabbos day. So wrote Ma'amar Mordechai.
For the need of Shabbos. See Shaarei Teshuvah regarding one who forgot to separate challah from chametz bread on this Friday. The view of Magen Avraham below in siman 506 is that this bread has no remedy for eating; see there in s.k. 8. He brought in the name of Responsa Pnei Moshe to be lenient; see there. I found like his words in Sefer Bigdei Yesha in siman 506, in Nesiv Chaim there, and in the chiddushim of Rabbi Akiva Eiger; all agree that in a pressing situation one may separate even on Shabbos with challah of the Diaspora, and give the challah to a minor kohen less than nine years old, or to an adult kohen who immersed from his seminal emission. See further there in Nesiv Chaim and Yad Ephraim regarding what they wrote about this. See Pri Megadim there. Although many later authorities copied the words of the Magen Avraham as practical law, see Eliyah Rabbah, Chok Yaakov, Chemed Moshe, the Rav Shulchan Aruch, Derech HaChaim, and Chayei Adam, nevertheless one may rely on the great authorities above to be lenient when he has no other bread to eat and so as not to nullify oneg Shabbos, whose obligation is with bread.
