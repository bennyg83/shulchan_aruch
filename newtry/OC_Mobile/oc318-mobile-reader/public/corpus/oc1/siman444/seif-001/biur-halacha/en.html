(א) "And we leave over food for two meals" — he stated two because by law one is not obligated to burn it until close to Shabbat; and if so, at the time of burning he leaves only two meals for the sake of Shabbat. And according to our custom in seif 2, that one burns everything before noon as in other years, one may leave more than two meals if he wishes to eat more before the night; and likewise Rambam wrote unspecifiedly: and one leaves from the chametz in order to eat from it until the fourth hour on Shabbat day; and likewise the Mordechai in the ma'amar.

(ב) "For the sake of Shabbat" — see in responsa regarding one who forgot to separate challah from chametz bread on this Erev Shabbat — for according to Magen Avraham below in siman 486, that bread has no remedy to eat on Erev Shabbat in seif kaf 8; and he brought in the name of responsum of Peri Megadim to be lenient on Erev Shabbat; and according to his words I found in Sefer Bigdei Yesha siman 486 and in Netiv Chayim there and in Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger — and all agree that in a place of pressing need one may separate even on Shabbat challah outside the Land, and the challah he gives to a minor kohen less than nine years, or to an adult who immersed for his keri; and see there further in Noda B'Yehuda and Yad Efrayim what they further wrote on this; and see in Peri Megadim there. And even though many later authorities copied Magen Avraham's words for the law [see Eliyah Rabbah and Chayei Yisrael and Chayei Adam and Gra and Derech HaChaim and Chayei Adam] — nevertheless one may rely on the great ones mentioned to be lenient when he has no other bread to eat and so as not to nullify oneg Shabbat, whose obligation is with bread.
