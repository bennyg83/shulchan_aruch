(א) And we leave over etc. And nevertheless also at the morning meal he should stop his meal into two, for some say that one fulfills with this (Shulchan Aruch and Mordechai chapter 1 of Shabbos and Bach and Kol Bo).

(ב) Or with meat and fish. Meaning: if he has meat and fish it is preferable to kinds of fruit, as is stated siman 291; and in Shelah he wrote in the name of the Zohar that R' Shimon bar Yochai would engage in Torah in place of the third meal; and Maharil wrote, and it would be fitting to make it with cooked matzah, but I have not seen people practice thus; end of his words.
