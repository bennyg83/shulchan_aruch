In Magen Avraham seif kaf 9 — and it is from the Yerushalmi; and it is etc. — meaning that the Yerushalmi in these laws disagrees with our Talmud, for behold the Yerushalmi holds that it is forbidden to carry on Erev Shabbat; and in our Gemara in Moed Katan it is proven that even on Erev Regel it is permitted; see above siman 249. And if so, the same applies regarding a betrothal meal; and the Yerushalmi disagrees in this with our Shas — and if so, one should not rule like the Yerushalmi.
