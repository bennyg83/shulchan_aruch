(א) "For the meal" — Taz. Seemingly after the time of minchah — that is, six and a half, as in siman 291; and Magen Avraham here 1 and 291 in this matter 4 — that one divides also the morning meal into two, for some say one fulfills; and that is, he blesses birkat hamazon.

(ב) "If one forgot to separate challah from Erev Shabbat" — Magen Avraham 486 seif 8; see there; and see Ba'er Heitev; and to declare it ownerless too is impossible. And per the one who says bein hashemashot there is no prohibition to leave the challah of the oven — it has no measure; but we hold that bein hashemashot is forbidden — siman 457 in Magen Avraham. And if he separated challah on Erev Shabbat and forgot to burn and Shabbat arrived — at the fourth hour to a non-Jew it is forbidden to give, except to a minor kohen; but to an adult and to nullify in a majority one does not nullify on Shabbat — Magen Avraham 323:14 and 14:99 and in Peri there and 324. However one may say he declares it ownerless, or challah that is not his — one does not transgress upon it; see Pesachim how one separates challah in impurity; and to give to his dog one may say it is permitted; and see Beitzah 27:2 — one may say we do not burn kodshim on Yom Tov and Shabbat. And challah is distinct from it — Or Zarua chapter 1 of Terumot: challah of Israel is forbidden in benefit of adornment; and likewise tevel is forbidden in benefit of adornment — Tosafos Pesachim 9:1; and this requires further study — if so, one must throw everything into the sea and not to a non-Jew; and it is possible challah outside the Land is likewise and permitted in benefit. And if he separated challah intentionally he shall not eat; and in unwitting he eats — Or Zarua, may he rest in Eden, chapter 5 of Shabbat; and see what is stated in 323 in Magen Avraham 11; and in Taz chapter 5 he concluded there that d'rabbanan is permitted — if so, challah of the Land is different, for it is d'oraisa; not so challah of the oven d'rabbanan — one may say it is not so much of a remedy, even though it is impossible to eat thus, as Maggid Mishneh stated; and for the law, where he has no other bread except this that he forgot to take challah — one may side with what Taz stated there; if so, there in 486 it will be further explained.

(ג) See in responsa Maharil siman 99 regarding the third meal — there is no positive commandment to override etc.; one may say it is a positive commandment of negligence — Tosafos Eruvin 100:1 s.v. "matan"; and see Magen Avraham in this matter in 291 regarding this; and siman 446 in Magen Avraham 2. In my book HaMagid I wrote the laws of Pesach that falls on Erev Pesach on Shabbat, and I noted them in Magen Avraham; and also here I will mention charoset and other things that must be prepared on Erev Shabbat, for on Yom Tov to crush in its normal way is somewhat forbidden; and b'dieved siman 484 — to roast meat and an egg in memory of the chagigah and Pesach; and one leaves for the second night of Shabbat on Erev Pesach — he roasts on Erev Shabbat, for on the first night of Yom Tov it is forbidden from Yom Tov to its fellow; and through cooking some say we do not say; and to fill a pot to cook many eggs — "for specifically meat that is improved thus" — Taz 473:4; see there. If it is permitted to put maror in water on Shabbat so that it not wither — Eliyah Rabbah 3 and 473:14 that it is permitted; and this requires further study regarding tilul and giving to drink, since it is not for the need of Shabbat it should be forbidden — siman 321 in Magen Avraham 13 and 454; and this requires further study if one inserts maror in the ground so that it not wither and not take root — see Mahariv chapter 5; and if so, there in 473 it will be explained; and Magen Avraham there seif 8 — if one forgot to roast the egg and meat, how shall he do on the night of Yom Tov; see there.

(ד) "Water" — our drawn water one draws on the night of the fifth when Pesach falls on Sunday; and siman 455 for the law; and if so, there it will be explained. If one forgot to filter wine from raisins on Erev Shabbat — it is good to leave for the night of Yom Tov, for on Shabbat there are several concerns of bereirah and the like — siman 319 and siman 471. And siman 462 in Magen Avraham 8 — and a general stringency that one does not eat raisins on Pesach and filters beforehand; and here it is impossible — it is good to leave it to filter on the night of Yom Tov per the ways explained in 471; and if so, there it will be explained.
