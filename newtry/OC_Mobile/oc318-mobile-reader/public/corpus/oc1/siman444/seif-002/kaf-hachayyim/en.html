One searches on the night of the thirteenth, etc. One recites the blessing over eliminating chametz and nullifies all chametz remaining in one's possession that was not found during this search. One must carefully guard both the chametz found during the search and the chametz retained for eating, placing it in a concealed location so that children or crawling creatures will not drag any away, as stated in siman 434. See there. Chemed Moshe, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1; Chayei Adam, rule 129, subsection 9; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 115, subsection 1.
