In the gloss: in these countries, etc. See Responsa Noda BiYehuda, siman 21, regarding what he objected to this, that there is no proof from Pesach itself to the eve of Pesach. See there, where he wrote that until midday it is certainly permitted to eat egg matzah on the eve of Pesach; and one who rules leniently the whole day, even without the need of a sick or elderly person, has not lost, if there is at least some need. But on Pesach itself we are stringent; see there.
In Shulchan Aruch, se'if 2: it is good to destroy, etc. See Responsa Chaim Sha'al, part 2, siman 38, letter 84, in the name of Sefer Nachal Eshkol, that it requires examination regarding labor on this Friday, whether its law is like any other eve of Pesach or whether one may be lenient. He wrote that in Derashos Maharil it says that one who wants to be stringent should be stringent; but the author of Kenesses HaGedolah, in his seder Haggadah called Pesach Me'ubin, wrote that he did not see people being careful even as a stringency. So too Machazik Berachah wrote in the name of Zera Emes that it is permitted; see there.
