There seif kaf 11 — and it implies that if it is from the beginning of the sixth hour and above etc. — this requires further study; for what is stated "from the beginning of the sixth hour" — even though he cannot nullify then, since d'rabbanan it is forbidden in benefit, nevertheless the positive commandment of "tashbitu" is not present except after the tenth hour; and if so, at the beginning of the sixth hour he need not return immediately, for the language "the positive commandment of tashbitu is more stringent" applies only from the sixth hour and above — if not where he would need to remain in performing the mitzvah and in the interim the sixth hour would pass and he would transgress the positive commandment of tashbitu. And also this requires further study — for it is possible that he should not transgress the mitzvah that is now before us, even though through this he will come to nullify the positive commandment of tashbitu — nullification automatically; and for this reason he should not leave the mitzvah before us. And that which he stated "he shall nullify it in his heart" — that is where it is possible through nullification to do so; but from the sixth hour and above, since the positive commandment of tashbitu has already taken effect, one may well say it is more stringent.
