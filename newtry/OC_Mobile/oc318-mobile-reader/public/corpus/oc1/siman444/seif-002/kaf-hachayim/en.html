"We search on the night of the 13th" etc. And one blesses on burning chametz and nullifies all chametz that remains in his possession that he did not find in this search; and one is careful with chametz found in the search and with chametz that one leaves for eating to place it in a hidden place, so that small creatures or rodents not drag from it, as is stated in siman 434; see there.
