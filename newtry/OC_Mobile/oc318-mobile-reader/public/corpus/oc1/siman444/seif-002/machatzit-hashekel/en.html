(s.k. 2) Or with meat etc. Meaning: if you say etc. — his intent is lest one err in the language of Shulchan Aruch, that fruit and meat and fish are equal to fulfill; on the contrary Rama preceded fruit to meat and fish to teach that the law is not specifically that if possible with meat or fish it is preferable to fruit. And in Chok Yaakov he wrote that Rama's language specifically means that on Erev Pesach it is preferable to make with fruit, for they do not satiate so much as meat and fish, and it is not necessary specifically that he eat only a little. Cooked matzah is permitted to eat on Erev Pesach since one does not fulfill with it at night, as is stated siman 461 seif 4. And Peri Chadash wrote: that which is written that it must read before the tenth hour — that is regarding eating matzah ashirah, because it is forbidden to eat bread from the tenth hour onward, as below siman 471; but fruit and meat and fish are permitted even after the tenth hour. Nevertheless, whether eating matzah ashirah or fruit and meat and fish — one should not eat more than a k'zayit, since this suffices to fulfill the third meal. And see above siman 291 in Shulchan Aruch and Magen Avraham, that it implies l'chatchila one needs more than a k'beitzah.
