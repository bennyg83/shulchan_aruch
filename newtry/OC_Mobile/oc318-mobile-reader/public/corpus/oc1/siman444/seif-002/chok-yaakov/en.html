It is good to destroy on Friday. According to the law of destruction that will be explained nearby, siman 445 se'if 1 and the gloss there; see there.
Before midday. As explained above in siman 494. Regarding the nullification he did not write that it is good to nullify on Friday before the sixth hour so that one should not come to err in other years, because the nullification can be done at its proper time on Shabbos, on the eve of Pesach, as on every eve of Pesach. This is unlike destruction, which cannot be done on this Shabbos. This appears to be the Rema's intent in the gloss, which at first glance is repetitive, since the Mechaber wrote this explicitly in se'if 6; rather, his intent is as I wrote. See the Magen Avraham, who also wants to settle the Rema's gloss here. In any event, there is no need at all to nullify on Friday, only at night at the time of checking and on Shabbos; this is simple. See above, siman 434.
