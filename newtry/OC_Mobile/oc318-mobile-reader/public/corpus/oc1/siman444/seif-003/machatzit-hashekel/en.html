(א) (s.k. 3) And on the day etc. — and it appears to me that on the sixth day etc. one need not; but on the night of the 13th at the time of bedika one certainly must nullify; and likewise Taz s.k. 7.

(ב) And so is implied from the words of Rama — that is, since he did not write "and also on Shabbat day one nullifies it"; and what Magen Avraham wrote, for otherwise there is a difficulty etc. — that is a different proof. And what did the sixth innovate? — it comes for precision specifically on Shabbat day, but not on the sixth day at the time of burning.
