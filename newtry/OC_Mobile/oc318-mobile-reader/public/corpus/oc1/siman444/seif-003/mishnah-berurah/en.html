(א) Porridge and the like — a thing whose way is to stick to a vessel, for he will need to rinse them after the meal because of the prohibition of chametz; and it is forbidden since one does not need them again that day, as in siman 323 seif 6.

(ב) Shrunken — dry.

(ג) And it is impossible to scrape it — for if it is possible through scraping alone it is forbidden to rinse when not for need.

(ד) One may rinse it a little — meaning only so that the chametz pass; and even though it is not for the need of Shabbat, we permit b'dieved because of the prohibition of chametz. And nevertheless if it is possible to rinse through a gentile it is better if impossible through scraping. The poskim wrote that it is good to cook in new vessels prepared for Pesach. And today in many places in our country they practice that after prayer they eat cold chametz foods, and after midday they eat warm dishes that stood in the oven and were cooked in Pesach vessels — and with this one fulfills the third meal.
