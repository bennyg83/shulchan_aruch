NB: and he asked in Sefer Even HaEzer — behold, one who goes to slaughter his Pesach — there is karet if he does not make Pesach; and he asked in Sefer Ba'er Heitev in the name of the Gaon R' Shaul, may he rest in Eden — behold, in any event it is impossible to make Pesach because of "you shall not slaughter upon chametz the blood of My sacrifice." And in my humble opinion it appears that this is specifically with his chametz; but with a deposit and he accepted responsibility — for on Pesach he transgresses with his own because of what is written "it shall not be found in your domain"; but because of "you shall not slaughter" he does not transgress — if so, in this case he slaughters his Pesach and does not return to burn chametz that was deposited with him; and with this nullification does not help even before the sixth hour.
