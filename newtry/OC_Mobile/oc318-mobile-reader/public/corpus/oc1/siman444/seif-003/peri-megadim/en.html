Before. See the Taz, and Magen Avraham siman 471, s.k. 1, regarding a quarter of the day, and letter 5 regarding what is egg matzah; see the end of siman 481. See Responsa Kenesses Yechezkel at the end of Orach Chayim, where he writes that when the first day falls on Motzaei Shabbos one should say, from the offerings and Pesach offerings, because it refers to the coming year. Mahariv, Hilchos Pesach, explains that when Pesach will come on Motzaei Shabbos means the following year; see there. See siman 473 in the Taz and Magen Avraham, letter 39. But one who examines Mahariv, Hilchos Pesach, where he writes, when it comes on Shabbos one should not read the Haggadah alone, and when it comes on Motzaei Shabbos, etc., sees that it implies this year; with Hashem's help it will be explained there.
