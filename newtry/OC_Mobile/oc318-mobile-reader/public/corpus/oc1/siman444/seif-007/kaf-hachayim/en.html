(7) There. "For the sake of Shabbat." And on this Shabbat they should not prolong in praises, lest the time of the prohibition of chametz arrive. Sefer Chassidim siman 514. Eliyah Rabbah seif 1. And it is good not to prolong on this Shabbat in the morning prayer and musaf; and it is obvious not to sing — only shorten as much as possible, so that one not stumble, Heaven forbid, in the prohibition of eating chametz after its time of prohibition. Maggid Mishneh seif 3; and see above in Shulchan Aruch siman 429 seif mem 2.
