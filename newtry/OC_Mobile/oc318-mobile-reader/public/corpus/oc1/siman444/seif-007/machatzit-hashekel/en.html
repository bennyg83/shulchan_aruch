(א) (s.k. 7) On condition etc. — meaning that one gives it to him unspecifiedly etc.; so is explained siman 325 that if one is not commanded to a gentile to take out it is permitted.

(ב) For we have no public domain and it is only like a karmelit where there is only rabbinic shevut — permitted through a gentile, for it is shevut shevut in a place of need of mitzvah to burn chametz. And Magen Avraham wrote above siman 325 s.k. 16 that nevertheless it is forbidden for an Israelite to give to a gentile by hand, since it is possible otherwise that he tell the gentile to take it — what does the Israelite need to do half a melachah of shevut, that is, the uprooting of the taking out?

(ג) See what is stated siman 252 that in a walled city specifically in sale it is forbidden; Chok Yaakov wrote: if one forgot to sell the chametz, to give the gentile the room with the chametz as a gift on Shabbat; and if he does not trust the gentile and perhaps he will seize it and not return it after Pesach — it is possible that even sale is permitted, since his intent is only to remove the prohibition of chametz.

(ד) And see siman 446 — meaning if it is possible to burn on Shabbat through another matter.
