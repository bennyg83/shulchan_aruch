7) There. For Shabbos. On this Shabbos one should not prolong the praises, lest the time when chametz becomes prohibited arrive. Sefer Chasidim, siman 314; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1. It is proper not to prolong the Shacharis and Mussaf prayers on this Shabbos, and certainly not to extend the singing. One should shorten them as much as possible so as not, Heaven forbid, to stumble by eating chametz after the time it becomes prohibited. Chemed Moshe, subsection 3.
