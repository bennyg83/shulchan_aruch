What is written in se'if 5, if it remains he nullifies it: in any case nullification is required. Rather, this is the explanation: if giving it to a non-Jew is impossible, he covers it with a utensil and includes it in the nullification, etc. Regarding drawing water, see siman 455 and Eliyah Zuta here, s.k. 1; with Hashem's help it will be explained there. They wrote, of blessed memory, that the shaliach tzibbur should not prolong the morning prayer. The Rav should expound on the Shabbos before it. In Responsa Kenesses Yechezkel, at the end of Orach Chayim, he wrote that on Shabbos that is the eve of Pesach one should warn members of the household on Friday about separating challah, for the reason we do not mention now is that with challah of the Diaspora one may continue eating and afterward separate; this does not apply on Shabbos that is the eve of Pesach.
