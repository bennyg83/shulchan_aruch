"One must" — Taz. And on Erev Shabbat one need not nullify — Magen Avraham 3; and Chayei Yisrael 4; and likewise in Levush in the gloss. And what is stated in seif 5 "if remains he nullifies it" — in any event nullification is required; only here if it is impossible through giving to a non-Jew he covers the vessel and includes within the nullification etc.; in drawing water siman 455; and Or Zarua here 1; and if so, there it will be explained. They wrote, may they rest in Eden, that the shaliach tzibbur should not prolong in the morning prayer. The rabbi expounds on the Shabbat before it. In responsa Knesset Yechezkel end of part 1 chalef chet — on Shabbat on Erev Pesach he warns the householder regarding parashat challah, for the reason that we do not mention now challah outside the Land — eat and go and afterward separate — and this language on Shabbat on Erev Pesach; see there siman 200 and 486 in Magen Avraham 8.
