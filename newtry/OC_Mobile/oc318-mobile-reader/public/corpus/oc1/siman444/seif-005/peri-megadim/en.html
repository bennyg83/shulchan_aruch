To the public domain. See the Taz and Magen Avraham s.k. 7. See Eliyah Rabbah, who answers what is written in the gloss in siman 325 se'if 2; see there. Outside the techum, which is rabbinic, for the need of a mitzvah, is a shevus of a shevus and is permitted; one can say this. What Chok Yaakov s.k. 1 and Eliyah Rabbah s.k. 5 wrote, that if he forgot to sell he may sell on Shabbos through meshichah, with proof from Tosafos, Eruvin folio 66a, s.v. well, requires some examination. Renting in Eruvin is merely a recognition to permit carrying, unlike here. And some say that a transaction done in a prohibited manner is not a transaction; see Choshen Mishpat siman 208 and Yoreh Deah, and see siman 383. This requires examination.
