Remains. Meaning: and there is no non-Jew to receive it from him. If one forgot to sell on Erev Shabbat chametz that he has in a special room, he must give on Shabbat as a gift to a non-Jew the room with transfer of the key; and it is possible that even a complete sale is also permitted, since one is doing only to escape the prohibition of chametz.
