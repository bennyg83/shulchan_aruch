(5) "On condition" etc. Not specifically; rather the same law applies in an unspecified case. And so ruled Mateh Moshe and the remainder of Knesset HaGedolah and Magen Avraham. And now it is fine that which he permits in siman 325 seif 2 — for there it deals with one who gives to him in an unspecified manner; and with much chametz it is as if one says to him explicitly to take it out — and not like Olat Shabbat. And see in Chok Yaakov; and I shortened, since we have no public domain — the later authorities wrote that it is permitted to command explicitly. And if there are no gentiles to clean the house, Mahariv wrote that the Jew may clean by garment. And Bach wrote that the custom is to give it to dogs whose food is upon them, and not to a pig. And if it is permitted to scatter to the wind on Shabbat — see siman 446; and in Magen Avraham it is forbidden. And see siman 457 seif 2: if one forgot to sell his chametz that he has in a special room on Erev Shabbat, he gives on Shabbat to gentiles the room with transfer of the key; and if it is impossible as a gift, he sells and it is not like buying and selling, since one does only to escape the prohibition of chametz.
