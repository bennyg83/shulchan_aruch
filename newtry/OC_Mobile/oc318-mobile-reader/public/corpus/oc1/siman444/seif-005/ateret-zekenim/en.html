"And he covers a vessel over it." And it appears that here it means: if he can give it to a non-Jew it is better; and if it is impossible to give it to a non-Jew, then he covers a vessel over it. And what is stated "on condition that he not go out to the public domain" — the same law applies in an unspecified case too, it is permitted; only one does not give to him on condition. And such is the agreement of all the poskim. And the Rav's wording of the gloss of Me'ironi took, who wrote in chapter 3 of Hilchot Chametz uMatzah; and with this is resolved that there is no difficulty from here on siman 325 seif 3 in the gloss (gloss in the name of Maharatz Schiff in Magen Avraham seif kaf 5). However if there was a pledge in his hand etc. — likewise with a pledge; and the borrower must pay him; end of his words; and this requires further study. And it appears to me that certainly he need not pay him — for what we say in Bava Kamma that one says to him "behold yours is before you" — because if you say that the depositor shall pay, then the depositor would benefit from what he receives payment; and if so, the depositor would say to the depositor: behold, you too are commanded not to benefit from chametz that passed over Pesach and are obligated in the mitzvah of burning chametz — therefore he says to him "behold yours is before you" and fulfill with your money the mitzvah of burning chametz; and he does not wish to pay him money. Not so here regarding a pledge — you wish to take the money from the borrower; if so, the borrower too would say to the lender: behold, you too are commanded not to benefit from chametz that passed over Pesach, and you can fulfill the mitzvah of burning chametz — why then do you wish to receive payment? And if so, certainly one may not take by force from the borrower; and it is simple. And if so, what Ba'al Magen David seif kaf 5 end of seif 4 stated, who also ruled that the borrower is obligated to pay — it is not so; and it is simple. Also what Ba'al Magen David seif kaf 5 end of the seif stated, and these are his words: and according to this he should not have written here "if they acquired all of it"; end of his words — it appears to me there is no difficulty at all, for since he does not say "if he acquired" but says "if they acquired" — it implies that it refers to above, that it was deposited with them; and if so, it is not a chametz-owner of transgressors; and it is simple (Maharar Nata Shapiro).
