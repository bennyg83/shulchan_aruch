(21) He nullifies it and covers etc. — and it deals with where there is no gentile or dog to give it to him; for otherwise, even though four hours have already passed and he stands in the fifth hour, he moves it and gives it to them; and likewise it is permitted to move it and throw it to the synagogue if he has a synagogue in his courtyard; and if he delayed until the sixth hour when it is forbidden in benefit and forbidden in tilul — nevertheless it is permitted through a gentile that they throw it in the river or to the synagogue.
