[2] Regarding washing, etc. And nowadays the custom regarding washing is to forbid (Bach); because of the honor of Shabbos it is permitted; and an individual who feels in himself that he can stand by this, it is good for him to cease eating while it is still day on all four fasts (Shelah, folio 139). And regarding the other matters the Shelah wrote that a baal nefesh should be stringent as on Tishah B'Av, except for removing shoes, so that it not be mockery and jest, until here; and nevertheless on the night of immersion he should not separate from his wife. The Taz raised a difficulty from Tosafos, Megillah folio 5a, s.v. "And he washed" etc., that perhaps they did not accept etc., and nevertheless washing is permitted according to the law; and see there his answer. And in my humble opinion, if so we would learn from Rabbi that one is obligated to fast, since he only washed and did not eat; therefore Tosafos answered that presumably they accepted it.
