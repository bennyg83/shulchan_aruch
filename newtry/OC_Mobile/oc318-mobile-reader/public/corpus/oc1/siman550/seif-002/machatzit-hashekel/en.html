(s.k. 2) Who are accustomed, etc. For in siman 575, regarding fasts that the beis din decrees because of the rains: on those fasts from which pregnant and nursing women are exempt, they are forbidden to be stringent, because of the welfare of the child.
Specifically individuals; that is to say, regarding a rain-fast those pregnant and nursing women are only individuals, and even if all the people of that city wish to be stringent, they are not permitted; possibly.
But here, originally, etc., all of them. That is to say, all of Israel.
