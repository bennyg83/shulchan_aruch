Taz, s.k. 1. What do Tosafos ask? That is to say: since the Gemara brings this incident to teach a permission of washing, the difficulty of Tosafos is not difficult at all, for it teaches us that he wished to fast and even so he washed; from this we infer that washing is permitted. Fair enough if washing were truly forbidden: then Rabbi, who washed, would teach us that he did not wish any fast at all; then Tosafos would rightly ask, why specifically mention washing? And this is easy.
