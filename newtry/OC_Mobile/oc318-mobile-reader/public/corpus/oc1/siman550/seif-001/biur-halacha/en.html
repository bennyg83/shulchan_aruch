All are obligated, etc. A girl of twelve years and one day, and a boy of thirteen years and one day, are obligated to fast, and not before that. And even if they have not brought two pubic hairs, they are obligated (Peri Megadim in the name of Eliyah Rabbah).
However, pregnant and nursing women, etc. See below in siman 686 in the gloss regarding Taanis Esther, that the same applies regarding those with eye pain if they suffer greatly. And for our matter, it is possible that it is more severe, for since all of Israel already willingly accepted upon themselves to fast on all four fasts, it then became an obligatory fast, and permission was given only regarding pregnant and nursing women, for in this they were lenient ab initio, as is evident from the Gemara, which mentioned completion only regarding Tishah B'Av, on which they fast and complete; however, in the Kovetz on the Rambam he wrote: it seems to me that I saw in the holy handwriting of the Gaon Maharaz Emrich, of blessed memory, to be lenient in this even on the other fasts, and everything is according to what the eyes see, end quote.
One should not fast. See Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Malbushei Yom Tov, that they should pay it back afterward, and his support is from what is below in siman 686. And one who examines the Beur HaGra there will see that there is no proof from there for our matter. And I have also not yet found any later authority who copies the words of Eliyah Rabbah on this.
