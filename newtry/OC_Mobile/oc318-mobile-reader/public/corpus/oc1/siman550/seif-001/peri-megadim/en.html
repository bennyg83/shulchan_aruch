(א) He asks in the Gemara Rosh Hashana daf 18b, see there; and see in Ein Yaakov in Maharsha: "Love truth and peace" — it shall be joy. The Second Temple was destroyed on 9 Av; afterward there shall be fasting. And in the braita it should have said "the day of the fast," which implies that the fast itself shall be joy. And one may say that for the days of Mashiach it prophesied that the fasts and afflictions shall receive reward for this, according to "make us glad as the days You afflicted us."

(ב) Permitted — Taz and Eliyah Rabbah and Bach on this; and Magen Avraham in the name of Shlah: a conscientious person should be stringent only regarding removing the shoe and bathing, meaning hot water; but cold one may say is permitted on the three fasts. See siman 575 seif 3 regarding washing on erev Shabbat before erev Shabbat in Eretz Yisrael; and likewise his face, hands, and feet in hot water one may say is permitted. And see siman 551 seif 6 in the Hagahah from Rosh Chodesh and onward it is forbidden in cold — on 17 Tammuz and likewise Tzom Gedalia and 10 Teves it is permitted in cold; and if you wish, it will be explained further there.
