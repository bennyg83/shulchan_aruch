1) [Se'if 1] Everyone is obligated to fast, etc. A girl twelve years and one day old and a boy thirteen years and one day old are obligated to fast, but not before then. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 549, subsection 7. They are obligated even if they have not developed two pubic hairs. Eshel Avraham, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah in Biur Halachah.
