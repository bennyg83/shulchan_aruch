And it is forbidden to breach the fence. Meaning: even though according to the law, when there is no danger, they are obligated to fast only on Tishah B'Av, nevertheless since they are accustomed that all of them fast, it is forbidden to breach the fence.
Who are accustomed to be stringent. This requires further study, for in siman 575, se'if 5, he wrote that it is forbidden for them to be stringent; one must say that specifically individuals are forbidden, but here originally all of them accepted upon themselves to be stringent to fast like Tishah B'Av. Mateh Moshe, siman 111, wrote: minors are fed bread and water so that they mourn with the congregation, end quote; and it appears to me that this is specifically a minor who has understanding to mourn.
