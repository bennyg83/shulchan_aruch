In Taz at the beginning of the prior siman regarding redemption. It appears to me that this prophecy of Zechariah was said after the Second Temple was built, when they asked about weeping in the fifth month, etc.; if so, he should not have said "they shall be" in future tense but "they are" for joy, or another present tense — and since he used future tense, we expound that he prophesied about after the destruction of the Temple; the word "they shall be" is expounded before it and after it: "and the fast of the fourth month... they shall be" — meaning they shall be fasts when there is no Temple; and it is also expounded after it: "the fast of the fourth month... they shall be for joy" — when the Temple exists.
