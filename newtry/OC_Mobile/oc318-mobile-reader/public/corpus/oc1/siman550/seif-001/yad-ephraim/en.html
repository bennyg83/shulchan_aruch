In the Taz. At the beginning of the prior siman, before "Geulah." And in my humble opinion it appears that this prophecy of Zechariah was said after the Second Temple was built, when they asked, "Shall I weep in the fifth month" etc.; and if so he should not have said "they shall be," which is future tense, but rather he should have said etc. "they are for joy and gladness," or another wording that indicates the present; and since he used future tense, we expound that he prophesied concerning after the destruction of the Temple; and his intent is that the word "they shall be" is expounded both before it and after it, and he holds "and the fast of the fourth" etc. "they shall be," that is to say, that they shall be a fast, namely when there is no Temple; and it is also expounded after it: "and the fast of the fourth shall be for joy" etc., and that is when the Temple stands.
