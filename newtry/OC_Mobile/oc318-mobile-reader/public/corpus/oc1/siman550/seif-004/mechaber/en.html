On Shabbos before a fast, the shaliach tzibur announces the fast except for Tisha B'Av, Yom Kippur, and Taanis Esther. Your mnemonic is ach"af — aleph for Tisha B'Av, kof for Yom Kippur, gimmel for Purim — "upon him his mouth" (see Proverbs 16:26). (The Ashkenaz custom is not to announce even one of them.)() :
