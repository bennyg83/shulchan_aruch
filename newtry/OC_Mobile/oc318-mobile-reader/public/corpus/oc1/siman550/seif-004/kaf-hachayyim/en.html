Nevertheless, pregnant and nursing women, etc. When the community wished and accepted the fasts, they were lenient in these respects: eating was permitted during the preceding nights, and pregnant and nursing women were exempt. Chayei Adam, rule 133, subsection 6; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1, writes in the name of Maadanei Yom Tov that they should make up the fast on another day, as written below, siman 686, se'if 2, in the gloss, but this is not the custom.
