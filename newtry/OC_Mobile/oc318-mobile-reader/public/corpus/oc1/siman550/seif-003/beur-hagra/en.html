Se'if 3: All, etc. See Beer HaGolah; and in the Mishnah he mentioned only Tishah B'Av, because the others depend on communal willingness.
And if they fell, etc. Since we conclude that he fasts and completes, its law is like that of other fasts, as is written in siman 566, se'if 1; see there; not like the Agur; and it is not precise to say this applies only to Anshei Maamad, for they lightened the rule for them that they do not fast at all on the sixth day.
And if a chuppah, etc. See above, siman 559, se'if 9, and Magen Avraham there, s.k. 11; and R' Shmuel HaLevi wrote etc.; and see what I wrote in siman 686, se'if 2.
