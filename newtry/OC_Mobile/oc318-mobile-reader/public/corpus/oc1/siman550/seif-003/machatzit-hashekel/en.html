Baran 20: Dodai, the regulations of the prophets, when they also have to fast, the third fasts, i.e., while there is a decree, they should practice all the strictures of the 12th Damash, and all the four shall fast, as the prophet once called. Except, since now, the prophet had a decree, and they did not use to fast, because of accepting them upon themselves. No. They initially imposed upon themselves these severities in the 3rd Tzumot Ekt'd. Rather, the Dhamma wanted to bury that dead body, why did they initially accept a half-fasting for them and not accept all the strictures as in the Gemara.
