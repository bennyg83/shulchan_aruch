(In Magen Avraham s.k. 6) Viduy and confession at minchah, etc. The language of Abudraham: if the 10th of Teves falls on Friday they pray shacharit like other fasts and also at minchah, but they do not say viduy and nefilat apayim because it is erev Shabbat — end quote. This implies the shaliach tzibur says "Ansenu" between "Go'el" and "Rofeh," and likewise in siman 554 Magen Avraham — thus he does not disagree with Knesset HaGedolah except that in any case there is no Taanis Esther; and greater is the view of Knesset HaGedolah siman 288: if there are ten fasting Torah scholars on Shabbat they say Ansenu, and even though in Be'er Heitev they brought Magen Avraham there that it is not right to do so — that is because there is no Taanis Esther, and also because on Shabbat itself there is no distress, as in siman 111; therefore there is somewhat to distinguish between that case and siman 554, which implies ab initio they would say Ansenu if it were Taanis Esther, because on Yom Tov "Ansenu b'et tzarah" applies properly as Magen Avraham brought there in name of Darkhei Moshe, because the following day is a day of trouble, or all the more in the gloss there because those are days of judgment on erev; nevertheless from Abudraham it appears properly they say Ansenu even on erev, because they properly say "Ansenu b'et tzarah" on this day which is Taanis Esther and Shabbat has not yet entered. And what Magen Avraham brought as proof for reading at minchah from mishnah chapter 4 of Taanit — see Shulchan Lechem who wrote this proof in the name of Mahar Avigdor, that it is a correct proof.
