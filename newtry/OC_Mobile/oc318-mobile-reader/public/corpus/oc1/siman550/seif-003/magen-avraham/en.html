All four fasts. This is not precise, for Asarah B'Teves never falls on Shabbos, as is written in siman 428, se'if 2.
They fell on erev Shabbos. This requires further study, for it is impossible for it to fall on erev Shabbos except for Asarah B'Teves.
They read, etc. And Beis Yosef wrote that they do not read at Minchah; and so it is in the Mishnah, chapter 4 of Taanis, that Anshei Maamad would not read at Minchah on erev Shabbos because of the honor of Shabbos; and one must say that nowadays everyone prepares while it is still day, and therefore they read; see what I wrote in siman 417. One does not say viduy at Minchah (Abudraham; Beis Yosef; Darkei Moshe). A chuppah on a fast: the groom is forbidden to drink from the cup of blessing while it is still day, and they give it to children; see siman 562, se'if 2.
