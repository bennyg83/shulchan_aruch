(א) (יא) Enclosed for dwelling — such as he rested in a city or other place enclosed for dwelling:

(ב) (יב) Even if there are no inhabitants now — such as it was ruined from habitation but its wall remains:

(ג) (יג) All of it is considered as four amot — even that place is large like Antioch and measures thousands of amot outside that place:

(ד) (יד) Up to two se'ah — and if the enclosure is more than two se'ah he only has two thousand amot in each direction from the place of four amot where he stood when Shabbat was sanctified:

(ה) (טו) On a high mound — ten handbreadths; and the same applies to stalks:

(ו) (טז) And stalks surround it — that he set boundaries around for harvesting [Rashi]; and that he bound the stalks together so no common wind moves them, for any partition the wind moves is not a partition [Sukkah 25]:

(ז) (יז) They are considered as four amot — for even though it formed by itself as a partition and mound, even though it has no enclosure above around it, nevertheless it is considered in the category of partition since it is ten handbreadths high, as above in siman 345 seif 2:
