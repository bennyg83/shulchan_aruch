In any place where Shabbat was sanctified upon him: if it is enclosed for dwelling, even if there are no inhabitants now, all of it is considered as four amot; and if' it is not enclosed for dwelling, up to two se'ah all of it is considered as four amot; and even if he rested on a high mound, on harvested standing grain, and stalks surround it — they are considered as four amot up to two se'ah:
