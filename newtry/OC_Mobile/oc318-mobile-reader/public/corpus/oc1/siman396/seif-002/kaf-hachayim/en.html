(ב) There. Even if he went outside the techum — for Moses said thus to those who went outside the techum to gather. Tur. And he read the verse "in which a man is under him" — those you found outside; and to warn "do not let a man go out from his place" to others who did not go out, and for generations forever. Malbush Yom Tov. Eliyah Rabbah siman 1; and it appears from Rashi that we measure for a person apart from the place he stands on; but Rambam did not write so. Darkei Moshe letter 1 in the name of Or Zaru'a.
