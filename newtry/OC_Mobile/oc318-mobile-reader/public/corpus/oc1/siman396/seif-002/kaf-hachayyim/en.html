2) There. Even if he went beyond the techum. Moshe said this to those who went beyond the techum to gather. Tur. The verse is interpreted as follows: 'Remain, each man, in his place' addresses those whom you found outside, while 'Let no man go out from his place' warns the others not to go out and applies for all generations. Mateh Yehudah; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1. Rashi implies that the four amos measured for a person are in addition to the place on which the person stands, but Rambam does not write this. Darkei Moshe, subsection 1, in the name of Or Zarua.
