He has four amot. In Tur it is written and Rif ruled he only has four amot and he is in the middle of them; and Raza"h ruled he has four amot in every direction, which are eight by eight; and it requires study: for therefore Rif ruled like R' Eliezer who holds in the mishnah thus on daf 45, and we do not find Rif ruled thus; on the contrary Rosh wrote that which Rif did not bring what we say in gemara R' Yehuda is the first tanna etc. because he holds halacha is like first tanna of the chapter "one who brought out" that he only has four amot; and Beit Yosef proved the Tur does not hold like R' Eliezer since he wrote siman 405 one who goes outside techum even one amah may not enter to be like city residents and he only has his four amot — end of his words; and I did not understand this proof, for certainly if he went outside techum he will have from that place two amot to each side, and in any case he may not enter the techum to be like city residents; and in Ashiri he wrote as Raza"h's words eight by eight and he is in the middle, and he did not mention this in Rif's rulings; also in glosses he did not mention in Rif's words only four amot and did not mention "he is in the middle" — therefore it appears this "and he is in the middle" is not in its place except after Raza"h's words as Ashiri wrote, as it appears to me:
