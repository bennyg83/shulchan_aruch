(א) (s.k. 1) And he is in the middle of them — so the Tur per Rif; requires further study, for in siman 349 etc. Magen Avraham's intent here is not to challenge the Tur; as Tosafot understood that the Tur in siman 349 wrote this law "they were two" etc. — nevertheless there is no hold on him, for he brought Rif's view here; nevertheless he also brought Razaah's view, which is "there is one who says" that Rama brought, and did not decide; on the contrary Bach proved that the Tur holds there is one who says "and he is in the middle" — see there; rather Magen Avraham's intent is to analyze Rama's gloss on the Mechaber's words "and these say etc. and he is in the middle" as if his words explain the Mechaber; on this Magen Avraham wrote that the Tur likewise per Rif — even Bach therefore does not hold so, as Bach wrote in Shulchan Aruch above siman 349 seif 4 "they were two" etc.; and even if strained, since Rama's gloss words may be loaded on the Mechaber's words, though the Mechaber does not hold so, Rama did not write in the language of "there is one who says" — and such is Rama's way many times; nevertheless Rama should have glossed above in siman 349 as Magen Avraham wrote there — see there:

(ב) And also in Rosh etc. — he follows Rif's method etc.; this is another challenge on the Tur for Rama: from where to explain Rif's words thus? For Rosh does not imply Rif holds "he is in the middle"; and Rambam's words, who follows his method, imply he chooses whichever direction he wishes — from where to invent a dispute here?
