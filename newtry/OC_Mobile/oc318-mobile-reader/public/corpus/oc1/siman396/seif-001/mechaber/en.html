דין ארבע אמו' שיש לכל אדם בשבת. ובו ב' סעיפים:
The law of four amot that each person has on Shabbat. Containing 2 seifim. In which a man is under him — from here [we learn] that each person has four amot in any place, even if he went outside the techum; and we measure for each person with his amah; and if he was a dwarf in his limbs, we give him four average amot of every person, each of which is six handbreadths.': And these four amot are measured wide for him, and he is in the middle of them (Tur).) And there are those who say he is permitted to walk four amot in each direction, but for carrying he only has four amot with the diagonal, as explained above in siman 349 (Tur in the name of the Razaah chapter 12 in the name of Rashba).
