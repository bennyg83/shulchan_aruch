(א) [seif 1] In which a man is under him etc. — as under him: how much is under him? His body three amot and an amah in order to extend his hands and legs. Eruvin 48a. And R' Yehonatan explained on Rif: his body three amot without his head, and when extending hands the head enters. Perishah siman 1; and see above siman 349.
