Seif 1, and specifically, etc. As written there: from where do we know that if they did not go out and do, etc. And in the Tosefta, first chapter: a city wall that was breached, they repair it; if it was repaired and breached again, they do not repair it. If it was near the border, he demolishes and builds in the usual manner. Thus they permitted only because of danger. This is what is written there, "even to correct," etc., and "these may be written," etc., for all these are public needs for their bodies, as Rambam wrote. This is unlike a Sefer Torah, which is not a bodily need, not like what is written in siman 545 seif 2. What he inferred from Rambam there is not compelling; this is what he means: all writing of a Sefer Torah and correcting it is not public need, as appears to me. This is what he wrote, "even a scroll of Ezra," implying that he excludes all of it, and then says "these may be written," specifically these but not a Sefer Torah.
The same applies to other, etc. From what is written there 6a, that the reason they go out regarding kilayim is because of the wage for labor, etc.
