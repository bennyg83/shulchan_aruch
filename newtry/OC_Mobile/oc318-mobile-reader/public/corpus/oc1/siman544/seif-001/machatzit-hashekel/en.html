(s.k. 1) For the body etc. For it is impossible that one of them will not need the bathhouse. That is to say, he will need it on the moed. And this is called communal needs even though only one person needs it, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 545, s.k. 17.
Even craftsman's work is permitted. For he holds that these are communal needs and they are a need of the moed, and in such a case even craftsman's work is permitted, as is written in seif 2.
