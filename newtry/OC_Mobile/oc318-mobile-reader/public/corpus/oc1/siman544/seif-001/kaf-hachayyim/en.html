1) [Se'if 1] Public needs may be attended to on Chol HaMoed, etc. This is permitted even when the work entails great exertion, is performed publicly, and was deliberately scheduled for the festival. Public needs are completed only when people are free. When they are free and assemble together, they perform the work; if it is not performed then, it will never be performed. Rosh, first chapter of Moed Katan, based on Raavad's explanation and the Yerushalmi. Beis Yosef and Bach cite him. Levush; Taz, subsection 2; Magen Avraham, subsection 3. Two distinctions apply. Skilled workmanship is permitted only when it is needed during the festival. Unskilled workmanship is permitted even when it is not needed during the festival, as explained in Rosh there, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch below.
