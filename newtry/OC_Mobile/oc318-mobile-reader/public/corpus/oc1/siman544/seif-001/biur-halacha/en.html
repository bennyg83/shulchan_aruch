Communal needs etc. See siman 542, seif 1, that regarding permitted melachos we require specifically that they be without pay; and see Magen Avraham, Maamar Mordechai, and Nahar Shalom in this siman, who all agreed that if they are for the need of a mitzvah it is permitted to take wages for them. And see Machatzit HaShekel, who wrote that regarding communal needs without a mitzvah it is forbidden to take wages; but from Peri Megadim it implies that regarding communal needs alone it is also permitted to take wages, and his reason is that it too is like the need of a mitzvah; and so it appears from the plain wording of the Mishnah, which included all communal needs in one category with the law that they go out [to clear] kilayim, and there it is stated explicitly in the Gemara that they take wages; and also, who would wish to do all communal needs without pay?
