Pits etc. And the same applies that it is permitted to make a ditch as layman's work so that the water will come to the city and they will drink in plenty; and even though it is great exertion and public, and they intended the melacha for the moed, even so it is permitted, because communal needs are completed only at a time when they are idle and all gather together, and if they do not do it now they will never do it (the Rosh). This implies that if there is a leader who has authority to do it alone, it is forbidden to do it on the moed.
And even if he needs etc. And even if he has nothing to drink, nevertheless because of great difficulty it is forbidden (Maggid Mishneh).
To channel water. Meaning that they collect rainwater from the roof and bring it to the pit, because there is no great exertion in this (Nimukei Yosef); but to bring water by way of a ditch, it implies in the Rosh that it is forbidden. And Beis Yosef wrote: any need of a mitzvah that is permitted on the moed, it is permitted to take wages for it; see siman 542.
