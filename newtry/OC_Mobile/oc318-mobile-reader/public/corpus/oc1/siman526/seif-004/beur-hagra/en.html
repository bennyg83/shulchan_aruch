Se'if 4: And to hew, etc. All the more so from a cloak and myrtle, which are not as necessary; and not like those who forbid this from what is written in chapter 3 of Moed Katan, "they do not dig," etc., and all the more so on the second day of Yom Tov. But the Rif, Rosh, Tosafos, and Ran already explained there that there [the prohibition] is for after the festival, for the need of dead who will die; and as written above, siman 547, se'if 11.
And if there is none, etc. And they hire, etc. As written above, that the second day of Yom Tov, etc.; and it is all the more so from myrtle, [and] all the more so for Jewish graves; and likewise to hire [workers] so that he not putrefy.
And even if it is possible, etc. As written: "they made it like a weekday," etc. Ran. And further, if it were only where it is impossible, then why do they permit even when he has not lingered?
But in Ashkenaz, etc. For Or Zarua ruled like Mar Zutra, and Rabbeinu Tam was stringent because Ravina said there "and nowadays," etc.; and so we have among us also regarding their melachah. And further, that on Shabbos there they forbade because they are not, etc.; and where it is possible we act according to them. And Tosafos rejected their reasoning that they are not called people of Torah, as with [an animal] eaten while still alive; and also what they wrote that Jews, etc., they rejected, because few Jews enter of their own accord, [so] we have nothing [from that].
