On the second day of Yom Tov, etc., until to heat water for him - Incidentally to the flow he mentioned all the melachos; but in truth, to heat is permitted through a Jew even on the first day of Yom Tov, as above in se'if 1; and already Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger noted this.
But if they do not wish to bury him that same day - See Mishnah Berurah: and that is to bring him a coffin and shrouds and the like; but not to leave him overnight so that he be buried tomorrow through a Jew, for that is not honor for the dead [Eliyah Rabbah]. Nevertheless, if he is a Torah scholar, one should look into this; and it is not comparable to those cases where the Mechaber ruled in se'if 2 that it is forbidden, for there the reason is as we wrote in Mishnah Berurah and in Shaar HaTziyun, which is not so here.
