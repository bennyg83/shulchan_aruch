Shrouds. And if one can find ready-made shrouds, one should not make new ones. If the shrouds are soiled, it is permitted to wash them. And if he has no boards, they saw boards for the coffin.
A grave. And specifically the members of the burial society; but others, whose way is not to dig, are forbidden. All the more so other people should not throw earth to seal the grave, or move the digging tools, unless it is near nightfall; see there, for then one who assists is praiseworthy. And Maharshdam, Orach Chaim, siman 17, wrote that it is permitted for any person to merit covering the grave, and Magen Avraham agreed with him; see there. But Mishpetei Shmuel, siman 13, disputes this; see there. And see Kenesses HaGedolah. And Or Zarua wrote: I saw in the pinkas of the Chevrah Kaddisha here in Prague, when the gaon Moreinu HaRav R' Ephraim, of blessed memory, passed away, and his students, our masters and rabbis, came and wished to purify him, for a Torah scholar is more fitting that his students purify him and occupy themselves with him, and they sent to the gaon Moreinu HaRav R' Yeshayah, of blessed memory, the head of the yeshiva, and he ruled that since the burial attendants occupy themselves all year and set aside their livelihoods, they should also occupy themselves with the tzaddik; only if the burial attendants of their own good will wish to yield to certain important people for the honor of the tzaddik, they have permission.
Like a weekday. Chayei Adam, part 4, wrote that the second day of Yom Tov of the first days is like Chol HaMoed. And Tosafos wrote that one should not distinguish in such a case. And Maharshal wrote, in the first chapter of Beitzah: if he commanded to take him to the graves of his ancestors, we do not heed him; but if he is not putrefying, they wait for him until the next day and take him. And if he is putrefying, they bury him that day in his place, and they do not take him on Yom Tov at all.
A non-Jew. And if one must wait many hours, and thereby the dead will come to disgrace or the like, a Jew should occupy himself with him.
Jews. And it is permitted to purify him with a sheet on the second day of Yom Tov, since it is only a decree lest one wring, provided they are careful not to wring by hand. Yam Shel Shlomo; Magen Avraham.
With earth. This is on the second day of Yom Tov; but on the first it is forbidden through a Jew. Taz; and see s.k. 5 for what is written there.
Carrying. But it is forbidden to take him out even to a karmelis; see Magen Avraham.
