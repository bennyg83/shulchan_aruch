Non-Jews; see Baer Heitev. And see Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 26, who permits purifying the dead when it is impossible to bury him, where there is concern that he will putrefy, for placing him in a coffin is like partial burial; see there. And see Radbaz, part 2, siman 506, who ruled that the purification should be that a Jew pour the water and the non-Jew wash, and at the end, after the non-Jew removes his hand, the Jew should pour water on his body and not move any limb at all, and this is the conclusion of his purification through a Jew, and we have not desecrated even a custom for him; see there. And see Responsa Bach HaChadashos, who permits bringing him to the cemetery within twelve mil even on the first day of Yom Tov, and beyond twelve mil it is forbidden; and see Kuntres Acharon there for what I wrote on this.
