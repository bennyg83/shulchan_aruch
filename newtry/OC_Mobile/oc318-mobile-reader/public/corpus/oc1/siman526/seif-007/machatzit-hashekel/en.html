(א) (sk 7) On Yom Tov, etc. — many Jews live there, etc. — as we learned in Moed Katan 9b: an aron with the dead in the courtyard — meaning on chol hamoed it is forbidden to make an aron for the dead except in that courtyard where the dead is; but not in another courtyard where they do not know there is a dead person and they will suspect he makes it for another need. Tur wrote in the name of R' Yehudah: what they do for the need of the dead on the second day of Yom Tov should be done discreetly; for the second day of Yom Tov is certainly not more lenient than chol hamoed. However, Yerushalmi: for a well-known person who died, even in another courtyard — since his death was publicized, they know they do it for his need. Tur: in our time, in every city only a few Jews live — if so, even every person is like the law of well-known people, for his death is known to all; and so we hold below siman 547 seif 10. On this Machatzit HaShekel wrote: in the land of Hungary where many Jews live in one city, the law returned to the law of the Talmud, as R' Yehudah wrote, and as above. And although it was publicized, etc. — meaning he was not a well-known person, and nevertheless by chance his death was publicized — nevertheless it does not help because of lo plug.
