{37} They are forbidden to ride, etc.: and the same law applies to going by boat; and even within the techum it is also forbidden. And even though they permit escorting outside the techum, riding is different, for there is concern of cutting a twig, which is a melachah according to the Torah, and it was not permitted for escorting, which is not among the primary needs of burial; and further, in riding there is more degradation of Yom Tov than walking on foot.
{38} And even the mourners: and it is implied that even if it is impossible in this place to walk on foot and he would remain without an escort, it is also forbidden.
{39} They permit them: however, they should not ride in the city because of the nations, so that they not say of them that they are desecrating Yom Tov.
{40} To ride upon an animal: and the same law applies that the buryers are permitted to go by boat.
