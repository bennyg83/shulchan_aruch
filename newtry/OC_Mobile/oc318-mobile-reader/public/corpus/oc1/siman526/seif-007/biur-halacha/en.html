If it is impossible, etc. — see Taz, meaning they have some exertion to walk on foot and it is permitted; and this follows his view that he wrote initially that the Mechaber's main ruling is unclear to him — see there. But according to what Magen Avraham and Erekh HaShulchan explained — that because of the degradation of Yom Tov one should not permit riding unless there is great exertion to walk on foot — and so wrote in Sefer Nehar Shalom and the Gra.
