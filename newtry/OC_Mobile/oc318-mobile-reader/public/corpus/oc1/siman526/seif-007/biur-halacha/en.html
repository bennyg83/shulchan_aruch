If it is impossible, etc. - See Taz, meaning that they have some exertion to walk on foot and it is permitted; and he goes according to his view, for he wrote initially that the Mechaber's main ruling is unclear to him; see there. But according to what Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah explained, that it is because of the degradation of Yom Tov, one should not permit riding unless there is great exertion to walk on foot; and so wrote Sefer Nehar Shalom and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav.
