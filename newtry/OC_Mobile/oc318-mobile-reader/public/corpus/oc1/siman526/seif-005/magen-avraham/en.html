It is permitted to take wages. For perhaps he will not find someone who will do it for free, as Rabbeinu Yerucham writes. In Yam Shel Shlomo he forbids taking wages, and if he does not want to do it for free they trick him, as with chalitzah, end quote. See the end of siman 585, where some say that for the need of a mitzvah it is permitted to take Shabbos and Yom Tov wages.
