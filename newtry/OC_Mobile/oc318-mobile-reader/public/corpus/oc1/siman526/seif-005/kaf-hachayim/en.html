And even if he will putrefy, etc. — one died on the first day of Yom Tov and they did not allow burial on Yom Tov because of their festival day until motzaei Shabbat, which was the third day from death, because Yom Tov fell on Thursday–Friday; it is permitted to purify him on the second day of Yom Tov because of concern for putrefaction and to place him in an aron; and although they cannot bury him, nevertheless since they place him inside the aron it is like partial burial. Shulchan Aruch haRav ch. 2 siman 26 Sha'arei Teshuvah note 2; Makor Chaim there in Likutei Rima note 6.
