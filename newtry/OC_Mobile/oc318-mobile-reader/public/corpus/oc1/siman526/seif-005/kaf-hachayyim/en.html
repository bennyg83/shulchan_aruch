5) There, in the gloss. Even if the body will decompose, etc. A person died on the first day of Yom Tov, and the authorities did not permit his burial on Yom Tov because of their festival day. Burial would therefore be delayed until after Shabbos, the third day after his death, because Yom Tov fell on Thursday and Friday. Because of concern that the body would decompose, it was permitted to perform taharah on the second day of Yom Tov and place him in a coffin. Although burial could not yet take place, placing him in the coffin constitutes partial burial. Shevus Yaakov, volume 2, siman 26; Shaarei Teshuvah, subsection 2; Mikraei Kodesh there, Likutei Rima, subsection 6.
