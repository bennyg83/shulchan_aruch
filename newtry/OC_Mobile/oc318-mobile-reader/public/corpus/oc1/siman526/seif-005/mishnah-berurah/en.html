{31} One who digs, etc.: and the same law applies to one who gives cloth for shrouds and the like.
{32} It is permitted for him to take a wage: provided they do not mention the sum of the price, and all the more so not to take money, but after Yom Tov they will come to an accounting. And even regarding this there is an opinion that forbids, that one should not take Shabbos wages; but some permit in a place of mitzvah, as explained above in siman 306, se'if 5. However, if the digger does not want to dig unless they set a money amount with him, or give him cash on Yom Tov: even though there is a transgression in his hand, nevertheless the relatives of the dead should give him, for the second day of Yom Tov regarding the dead they made like a weekday.
