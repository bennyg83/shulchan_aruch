[5] Lest he come, etc. One might ask that it should in any event be forbidden because of laundering, as Shulchan Aruch writes in siman 302. Olas Shabbos. This is no difficulty, for there are several cases in which laundering does not apply, such as where there is no dirt upon it, where it is washed with other liquids, or where it is washed in a manner of soiling.
