Se'if 10: Nefalim, etc. For it is not a custom of the Torah; and as written in Bereishis Rabbah, that the Holy One, blessed be He, removes their orlah and places it upon the wicked of Israel; and for that reason they practiced [thus].
And it is forbidden, etc. As written in the first chapter of Pesachim and in Niddah, that they would cast [them] into a pit; hence there is no mitzvah to bury him.
