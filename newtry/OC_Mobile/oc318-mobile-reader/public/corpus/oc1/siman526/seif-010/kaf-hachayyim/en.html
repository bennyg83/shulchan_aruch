10) Accordingly, on a weekday the son of the deceased or another close relative customarily places earth over the eyes of the deceased, and each person accompanying the deceased to the cemetery places a clod of earth into the grave. On the first day of Yom Tov, doing so is forbidden under Torah law because it resembles building. Shulchan Gavoah there; Zechor LeAvraham, volume 1, Yoreh Deah, Hilchos Aveilus, subsection 10; Mikraei Kodesh there, Likutei Rima, subsection 3.
