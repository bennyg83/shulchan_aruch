{44} A child who died, etc.: meaning that for nefalim they do not bury them on Yom Tov, and even through non-Jews, for they are like a stone and are forbidden even to be moved.
{45} If his hair was completed, etc.: and without completion, even if he died on day thirty itself, he is not in the presumption of a viable child, and his law is like a nefel.
{46} And his law is like an adult dead person: and he is buried on the first day through a non-Jew, and on the second day through a Jew.
{47} For most women give birth to a viable child: and we attribute his death to some cause. And some dispute this: since he died, the majority is weakened, for most viable children do not die within thirty [days], and presumably he was a nefel, and one should be stringent to bury him through a non-Jew. However, through a non-Jew it is possible to permit even without completion of his hair, etc., for in any case he is a doubtful viable child.
