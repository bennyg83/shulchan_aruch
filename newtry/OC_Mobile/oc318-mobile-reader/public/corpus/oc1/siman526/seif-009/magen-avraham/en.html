Presumed to be viable. For we circumcise him on Shabbos and presume him to be viable, as in Bedek HaBayis. It seems to me that there is no proof from there, for there he is alive, unlike here, where he died within thirty days and is a doubtful stillborn. So it also appears in Even HaEzer, siman 156, seif 4, regarding yibbum; see there. Furthermore, on Shabbos, folio 135a, Tosafot, s.v. ben shemonah, wrote that we circumcise him by reason of a whichever-way analysis, etc., and so it is in the Gemara; see there. In Yam Shel Shlomo he ruled that one does not bury him within thirty days; see what I wrote in seif 1.
