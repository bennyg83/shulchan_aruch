[9] And to hew, etc. This is permitted specifically to members of the burial society. Others, whose ordinary practice is not to dig, are forbidden, and all the more so may they not cast earth or move it without digging, unless it is Erev Shabbos close to nightfall. Olas Shabbos. Rashdam writes that every Jew may merit participation in covering the grave. Knesses HaGedolah. Magen Avraham maintains that even digging is permitted to everyone, comparable to milah on Shabbos, where two mohalim may perform the circumcision. Nevertheless, it is proper to be stringent.
