All this concerns making a grave, etc. Even if there is a certain excavation in the cemetery in which the deceased could be placed until after Yom Tov, or even a complete grave that is not his and from which he would later have to be removed, non-Jews may dig a grave for him on Yom Tov. Moving his bones and removing him from the temporary grave would not honor the deceased. Rosh, first chapter of Moed Katan. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2, agrees, unlike Olas Shabbos. Mishnah Berurah there rules likewise. See below, subsection 34.
