Se'if 3: On Shabbos and Yom Kippur, etc., even, etc. So it is implied in Rosh Hashanah there, that they asked: why did they say [to bury] through non-Jews, when they permitted a shevus because of an act of carrying via a loaf and a child, and to take him out to a karmelis is [permitted] even according to R' Yehudah, as written in chapter HaMatzni'a? And they answered that here it is for his honor, so that they not say Shabbos or Yom Kippur was desecrated for him; and as written in chapter 23: they made him a coffin and shrouds, etc., when he is standing in the street, etc.; and therefore even to take him out, etc. And see Tosafos of Bava Kamma, chapter 1, folio 1, s.v. "omer," etc. And see chapter 19 of Shabbos in the Rif, what he wrote in the name of Bahag explaining the distinction between a shevus that involves an act, etc., and the Rif agreed with him; and they asked against him that we find the opposite: that corpse that was in Darukra, whom R' Nachman permitted to take out to a karmelis, whereas to bury him through non-Jews is forbidden on Shabbos and Yom Kippur; and they answered that there it is for his honor, for he too is with him, etc.; and as written at the end of chapter 23 of Shabbos: "this one shall never be buried in it," etc.; see there.
Even, etc. See Magen Avraham: and even regarding David they permitted only carrying via a loaf and a child.
