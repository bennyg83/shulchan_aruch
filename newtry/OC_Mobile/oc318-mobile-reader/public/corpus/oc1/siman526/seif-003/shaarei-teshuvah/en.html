Therefore see in Avodat HaGershuni siman 16 regarding a matter where they buried a certain scholar on the second day of Yom Tov and did not leave him overnight until Orach Chayim so his wife, children, colleagues, and students could escort him — they did well, for leaving overnight has many concerns, hidden and revealed; as it is forbidden on the first day of Yom Tov to wait until the second day of Yom Tov so Jews will deal with him, as explained in Shulchan Aruch; and although the deceased joined his society, he did not join for this — that their bond would be fulfilled in a place of the prohibition of leaving overnight; and similarly Maharam of Lublin siman 34 (I brought this above siman 25 in laws of tefillin) — see there; and see in Rav Meshulam in the anthology of the Gaon ch. 18 daf 93 to permit digging a grave with covering even if they are not designated for this, and he brought proof from cutting the omer in three baskets, etc.; and all the more here to increase his honor as stated in Sotah: his honor is in multitudes, etc.; and so I acted regarding one of the town residents buried on the second day of Rosh Hashanah — I covered him with burial earth although I was not of the burial society, for it is no less than shearing his hair — end of his words.
