3) There, in the gloss. Jews should not attend to him, etc. This applies even if the person died on the first day of Yom Tov when it fell on Friday and burial by non-Jews is impossible. For example, there is no cemetery in their location, the non-Jews will not agree to transport the body if burial is delayed until Sunday because that is their festival day, and if burial is delayed until Monday the body will decompose. There is then no remedy except to have non-Jews bury him in his house that day, after which he should be removed following Shabbos and buried with his ancestors. Alternatively, non-Jews should prepare a shroud and coffin that day, place him in it, and seal it with clay and pitch so that no odor escapes, and he should be buried after Shabbos.
