Magen Avraham, s.k. 7. And going outside the techum is comparable. And that here it is permitted is because those who go with the dead are seen to be doing it for the dead; but there in the responsum the case is that the dead is in another place and they go there to bury him. And that below, se'if 8, must be explained as: since the dead is lying on Shabbos in disgrace, they were more lenient. However, nevertheless it is difficult from that of se'if 6, that they return to their place that same day, which is that "their end [is permitted] because of their beginning"; and this return, [of people] who are not going with the dead: in such a matter they did not permit even the beginning; so how may we permit that whose end is because of its beginning, when even at the beginning itself he is not permitted? And examine carefully.
