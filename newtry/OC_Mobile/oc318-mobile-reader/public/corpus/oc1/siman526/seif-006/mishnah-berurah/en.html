{33} Within the techum: meaning up to two thousand amos and no more; even though up to three parasangs according to all is only rabbinic, they did not permit [beyond two thousand] for escorting alone.
{34} Outside the techum: even outside the techum three parasangs; and even though they have no need for the dead, but escort them only for honor, nevertheless it is permitted, for the Rabbis made it like a weekday [Acharonim].
{35} And they return to their place that same day: and even if they went out beyond several miles, they are also permitted to return to their homes; for if they forbid them, they will be caused to stumble in the future, that they will not want to go and occupy themselves with the needs of the dead and with escorting him. And it stands to reason that all this is if there is no Jewish settlement there at the place of the graves within the techum; but if there is, and they can eat and sit there, we should not permit them to return, for merely because they cannot return to eat and go back to their homes this once, they will not refrain from escorting the dead; and see Biur Halacha. And it is explained in the posekim that nevertheless, if he can shorten his route, we should not permit him to return by a long route [see Magen Avraham and Machatzis HaShekel].
{36} And likewise it is permitted to return the burial tools: and they are not muktzeh, for they are vessels whose melachah is for a permitted [use], lest one need them for another dead person that same day; and if so, it is permitted to move them even if one will need them only to guard them so they not be stolen, as above in siman 308, se'if 4. And since he is already moving them, even if he finds near the city wall a secured place to leave them, he also need not; rather it is permitted to carry them to his house, as above in siman 308, se'if 3.
