And they return to their place. Since the Beis Yosef wrote without qualification, it is evident that he holds that even if they went outside four thousand amos, they may return, so that you not cause them to stumble in the future. So ruled the Shiltei Gibborim and Yam Shel Shlomo. A city shaped like a bow, in a case where one measures from the bow, they are forbidden to go by way of the permitted route, for they can go by way of the bow, as in Radbaz, and so it is, siman 398.
The burial implements. Since their primary labor is for permitted use, for perhaps they will be needed for another corpse that same day, they may therefore be moved so that they will not be stolen, as in Ramban and Beis Yosef. It implies that with a circumcision knife it is forbidden to move it after the circumcision when he knows that he will no longer need it; see what I wrote in siman 331, seif 6.
