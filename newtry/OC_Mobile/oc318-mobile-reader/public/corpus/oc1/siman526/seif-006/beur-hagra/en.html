Se'if 6: And on the second day of Yom Tov, etc. As above, that [it is] like a weekday, etc.
And they return, etc. As written in the final chapter of Yoma: "If so, you," etc.
And likewise, etc. For their melachah is for a permitted [use], and it is permitted [to move them] so they not be stolen, as written in chapter 17 of Shabbos; and even if there is a house near the wall that is secured [for them], so that [keeping them] would not be needed at all, and it is forbidden as written above, siman 308, se'if 4: nevertheless, since he took [them] so they not be stolen, he may move [them] wherever he wishes; and see there, se'if 3.
