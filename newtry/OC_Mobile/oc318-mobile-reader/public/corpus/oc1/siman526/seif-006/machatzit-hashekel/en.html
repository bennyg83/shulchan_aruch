(Seif katan 6) Even, etc. Because of the honor of the deceased, etc. Meaning, although it is not performed through a non-Jew, it is only a double shevus, since the burial chamber was dug before Shabbos. One might therefore think it should certainly be permitted for the sake of honor. He responds that, on the contrary, this itself is the deceased's honor, that Shabbos not be desecrated on his account.
See siman 311 and Magen Avraham there, seif katan 13.
