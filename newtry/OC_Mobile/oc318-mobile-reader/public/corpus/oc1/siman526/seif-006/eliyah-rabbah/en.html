[6] [Levush] Except for moving, etc. This requires study, for the Rosh wrote this only regarding the second day, as Shulchan Aruch writes in seif 4. On the first day, however, it appears forbidden. The words of Terumas HaDeshen, siman 82, may likewise be understood this way. Examine this carefully. Even on the second day, Maharshal was stringent; see seif katan 14.
