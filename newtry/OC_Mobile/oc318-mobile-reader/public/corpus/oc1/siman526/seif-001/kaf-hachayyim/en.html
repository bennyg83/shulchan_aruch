1) [Se'if 1] If it is the first day of Yom Tov, etc. Since its prohibition is under Torah law, it is not set aside for the honor of the deceased. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1. The positive commandment of burial does not override the prohibition and positive commandment of Yom Tov. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1. See below, subsection 22.
