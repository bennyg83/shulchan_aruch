(1) Who is lying, etc. Rashi there, s.v. "Rava said," etc.; that is to say, do not say that "Yom Tov" refers to the dead.
They should not occupy themselves, etc., and even, etc., and if, etc. So wrote Bahag; and so [it is] in Kesef Mishneh, in Rosh Hashanah chapter 1; rather, [only] if [on] the second day of Yom Tov, etc., [then] it is possible through non-Jews.
Even if he died, etc., and even. For even Mar Zutra, who requires that he have lingered, poses no difficulty except regarding the second day of Yom Tov, since Rav Ashi stated it without specification; and even though he did not, etc.; and there is no difficulty on the second day of Yom Tov. And even though in chapter Tolín he said "and I do not know," etc., the sugya here does not hold so, and all the more so according to Rav Ashi.
And all this, etc., but, etc. For it is permitted through "mitoch," both carrying out and kindling, as written in chapter 1; and even according to Tosafos, for the need of a mitzvah it is permitted, like a Sefer Torah and burning chametz. But many dispute this; and so the Ramban in Sefer Toras HaAdam, and Rashba siman 22, forbid even the carrying out, for it is like stones, concerning which one cannot say "mitoch"; and even though it is a mitzvah, the burning of kodashim proves [otherwise]. And so wrote the Ran, that what is said "they occupy themselves," etc., [means] every occupation.
And it is good, etc. For even for the need of a living person it is forbidden, as written there, folio 1a.
