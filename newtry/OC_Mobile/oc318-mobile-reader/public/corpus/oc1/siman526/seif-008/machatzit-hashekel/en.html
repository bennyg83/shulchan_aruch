(Seif katan 8) Like a weekday, etc. But, etc. The cited works are apparently Teshuvos Yeshanos and Terumas HaDeshen. Tosafos write, etc. At first glance, Magen Avraham appears to mean that Tosafos disagree and are lenient, holding that even the second day among the first festival days is more lenient than Chol HaMoed. This is incorrect. I have not seen the cited works, but Tosafos in Moed Katan 8b discuss the Mishnah, “One may not dig burial chambers and graves on Chol HaMoed.” Rashi explains that this means for those who will die later. Tosafos write that Rashi is correct, for regarding one who has already died we say that the Sages treated the second day of Yom Tov as a weekday for a corpse, even to cut myrtle and a shroud for him, and all the more so on Chol HaMoed. One might have argued that the second day is treated as a weekday only on Shavuos, or on the final festival days of Pesach and Sukkos, which are D'Rabbanan, whereas Chol HaMoed is according to the Torah and should be treated more stringently. Tosafos reject this because the Gemara states broadly that the second day is like a weekday, implying every second day, even among the first days of Pesach and Sukkos. Moreover, even the second day among the final festival days is more stringent regarding melachah than Chol HaMoed. Therefore Rashi's interpretation is primary. Accordingly, the authorities cited by Magen Avraham, who equate the second day among the first festival days with Chol HaMoed, must accept Tosafos' initial possibility: on such a second day, even when the person has already died, one may not dig his grave. They disagree with Rashi and follow Raavad, cited by the Rosh, who maintains that even on Chol HaMoed one may not dig a grave for one who has already died. Tosafos conclude that no distinction is made, and that even the second day among the first festival days was treated as a weekday, so one may dig a grave for one who has already died. They therefore agree with Rashi that the Mishnah's prohibition concerns those who will die later. This is how we rule below in siman 547.
