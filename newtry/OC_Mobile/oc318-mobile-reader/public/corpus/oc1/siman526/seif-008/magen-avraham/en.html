(1) To hurry his burial — meaning even on the back of the 24th hour if he does not want to bury him that day it is forbidden to do for him there what can be done after Yom Tov; not so here where he must go with them to hurry burial lying in disgrace in the field. Rivash siman 518 wrote it is forbidden to go there, for even on chol hamoed it is forbidden to make boards in another's courtyard as siman 542 seif 10 — all the more here where an observer will say he goes for his need; so too in Knesset HaGedolah in the name of several great ones; so too Yeshuot Shani: since he does not want to bury him that day, forbidden to go there; and even though there is concern dogs will eat him — for such concern one should not be lenient, especially concern dogs will eat before they arrive and thus Yom Tov would be violated for nothing; but where they see dogs will eat, they may certainly save and do melacha; even if not fit to bury today, since they do melacha today to bury tomorrow it is permitted — up to here. It appears to me to be lenient, for in siman 542 it is written if the matter is publicized it is permitted; also no concern Jews will harm them on the way, for on Yom Tov Jewish travelers are not common; however if not lying in a field but in a house, one should be stringent:

(2) and it is allowed to bring Afi is laid in a city, if there is no cemetery there, it is permissible to take him to a city where there is a cemetery, it does not detract from the malimiz liya asa that he is his honor, and if there is a grave there, and he is ordered to be taken to the graves of his ancestors, even on the back of a demzoa to fulfill the words of the dead, and in particular, that he is his honor, that he be buried in the place of his ancestors.
