{41} And he was not given for burial: and he was lying among the non-Jews.
{42} To hasten his burial: and even though the Mechaber did not permit above in se'if 4 to do any melachah when it is not his intention to bury him that same day: that case is different, for he had already been lying several days among the non-Jews, and he is a met mitzvah, and one must hasten as much as possible so that he not become disgraced. And Magen Avraham wrote: however, if he is lying there in a house, one should not be lenient; and see Biur Halacha for what we wrote on this.
{43} And it is permitted to bring, etc., from outside the techum: the Mechaber already wrote so in se'if 4, and the Rama repeated and wrote it only because of its novelty: that even if the dead is not here, but it became known to them that there is a dead person outside the techum, they are permitted to go outside the techum to bring him from there to their city to bury him that same day in Jewish graves. And see Biur Halacha that one should not be lenient in this unless he has already lingered there.
