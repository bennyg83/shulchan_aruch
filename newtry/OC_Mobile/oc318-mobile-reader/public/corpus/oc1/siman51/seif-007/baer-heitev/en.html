Forever. Sefer Nagid U'Metzaveh writes in the name of the Arizal that one should also recite the Aramaic translation of this verse, corresponding to twice Scripture and once translation. One should also recite the verse, “For Pharaoh's horse came,” because this verse too is part of the essential Song at the Sea. See there and Responsa Ginas Veradim, Orach Chayim, general rule 2, siman 8. Beit Yosef cites Orach Chayim that in the second phrase Mi Chamocha the kaf is strong, while in the first Mi Chamocha, and likewise in “All my bones shall say: Hashem, who is like You,” the kaf is soft. In “This people You redeemed,” the gimel is strong. In “They became still as stone,” the kaf is strong. One should pause between bamayim and adirim. The Song should be recited with concentration and joy. Zohar, Beshalach.

During Vayevarech David one should stand until and including the words, “You are Hashem, the God.” When reaching “And You rule over all,” one should give three coins to charity: two to the collector and one to the charity box. Mishnas Chassidim, page 64b. Magen Avraham questions the custom on the day of a bris that the mohel recites “And You made” and divides several verses. The Gemara at the end of the first chapter of Berachos states that any verse not divided by Moshe may not be divided. See his resolution of the custom.
