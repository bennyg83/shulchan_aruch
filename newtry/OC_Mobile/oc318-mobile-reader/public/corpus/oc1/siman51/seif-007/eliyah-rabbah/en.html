(7) Because it is [the end], etc. — Maharil wrote the reason: from the verse "Vayehi ba'ashmuras haboker" until "Hashem yimloch" there are eighteen mentions of the four-letter Name, and four times eighteen equals seventy-two letters corresponding to the seventy-two-letter Name. In pesukei dezimra they begin from "Vayoshea," and four Names are missing from before "Vayoshea"; therefore they repeat "Hashem yimloch" and add another verse, "Ki laHashem hameluchah," etc., to complete the count. The fifth is not part of the count but explains "Bayom hahu yihyeh Hashem echad." The writings of the Ari [say] it is good to say with the song the verse "Ki va sus Paroh," etc. The Levush wrote: "Mi kamocha" — the kaf of "kamocha" is emphasized, for otherwise it appears as blasphemy, as if saying "Hashem Micah" — he is the man who stole the Name and made the calf, as it says "Va'avar bayam tzarah," until here his wording. If you ask why he mentions the kaf of "Mi kamocha na'ar" and not "Mi kamocha ba'elim" — one must say this is not adjacent to the Name. Likewise "Kol atzmosai tomarnah Hashem mi kamocha" — the kaf is rafeh, for this was not said at the sea. "Yidmu ke'even" — the kaf is emphasized so it not be heard as "yedmucha even," Heaven forbid. Likewise "Im zu ga'alt" — the gimel is emphasized, etc.; for also "Ga'ah ga'ah" — the gimel is emphasized. Those excluded from the rule that letters after aleph-heh-vav-yud are rafeh without a mappiq — so wrote Radak in Sefer HaDikduk. Abudraham wrote: "Yehalelucha selah" — the samech with dagesh, for so is its lamed with dagesh; "Hodu al eretz ve'shamayim" — cholam between the dalet and final vav; "HaKena'ani, haChitti," etc. — the first three without vav, the last three with vav. In the siddur of R' Shabbasi Sofer: "Ya'alzu" — the first shva is nach and the second na, as is the rule for two shvas one after another; "Pifiyot" — the peh rafeh; "Malkeihem" — the kaf rafeh, and likewise all "malkeihem." Similarly "Gadlu" — the gimel with shuruk. "Betzelatzelei shema" — kamatz because of the etnachta; and they explained "Shema" with segol. "Yisrael avinu" — "avinu" refers to Yisrael; he meant to say Yaakov avinu.
