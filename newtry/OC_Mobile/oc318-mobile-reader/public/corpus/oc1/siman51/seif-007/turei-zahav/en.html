And they say the passage "And we," etc. And R' Akiva wrote to chain Halleluyah psalms after Halleluyah psalms—for all the sections until the Tikkunei [Zohar] are found at the end of the sections of Halleluyah; and the Halleluyah after "Tehillat the Omnipresent"—"yedaber pi" is the opening of the section; therefore they add "And we will bless," etc. Our master wrote in Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chayyim: one should be careful to say "Mi kamokha na'ader bakodesh" with hard kaf, for otherwise it appears as blasphemy, as if saying "the Omnipresent Micah"—and Micah is he who stole the Name and made the calf, as it says "Va'avor bayam tzarah"; and likewise in the passage "All my bones shall say, 'the Omnipresent, who is like You?'"—the kaf is soft, for that was not said about the sea, and one should not mention Micah regarding it; and likewise "Yikra yidmu ke-even"—the kaf is hard so it should not appear as saying "yidmokh even," and likewise "Am zu ga'alta"—the gimel is hard, for if it were soft it would appear as the language of "megua'al," and it would be blasphemous, until here.
