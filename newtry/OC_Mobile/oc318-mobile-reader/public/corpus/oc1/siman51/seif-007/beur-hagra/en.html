Seif 7: One must concentrate, etc. The principal reason for reciting Tehillah LeDavid is the verse “You open Your hand,” as stated in Berachos 4b.
And they say, etc. This is in order to conclude with Hallelujah and connect one Hallelujah to another, as in the following psalms. It is also recited at Minchah because no distinction was made. Kol Bo gives another reason.
And they repeat, etc.; and likewise, etc. It appears that this is the meaning of the statement in Sukkah 39a that Rabbi would repeat, etc.
When he reaches, etc. Berachos 34a-b. See Tosafos there.
And they practiced, etc. This is merely an added stringency.
