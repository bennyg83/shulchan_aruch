Seif 7: One must concentrate, etc. The principal reason for reciting Tehillah LeDavid is the verse “You open Your hand,” as stated in Berachos 4b.
And they say, etc. This is in order to conclude with Halleluyah. Rosh explains that the purpose is to connect Halleluyah to Halleluyah, as in all the following psalms, because the Halleluyah here belongs to the beginning of the next chapter, as stated in the final chapter of Pesachim, by Tur in the name of Rav Amram, and by Kol Bo in the name of Baal HaEshkol. It is also recited at Minchah because no distinction was made. Beit Yosef writes this. Kol Bo gives another reason; see Magen Avraham.
And they repeat, etc.; and likewise, etc. It appears that this is the meaning of the statement in Sukkah 39a that Rabbi would repeat in it, etc.
When he reaches, etc. Berachos 34a and 34b. See Tosafos there in the passage beginning “we teach, etc.”
And they practiced, etc. This is merely an added stringency. See above, siman 53.
