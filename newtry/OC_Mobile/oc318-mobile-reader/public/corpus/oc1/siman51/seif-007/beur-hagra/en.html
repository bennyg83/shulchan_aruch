Siman 67: one must intend etc.—for the essence of its recitation is because of this verse, as stated above seif 4.

And they say etc.—in order to conclude Halleluyah—Rosh; and he means in order to extend Halleluyah after Halleluyah, as in all the psalms that follow it, for the Halleluyah here is the beginning of the chapter, as written in chapter "Ein Omdin," Tur in the name of R' Akiva and Kol Bo in the name of Baal HaAshkol. They say it also at Minchah because we do not divide—Beit Yosef. And Kol Bo wrote further: on the Ninth of Av, page—Magen Avraham.

And they repeat etc., and so etc.—and it seems this is what is written in chapter 3 of Sukkah 39a: R' [Yehuda] repeats in it etc.

When he reaches etc.—Berachot 34a side 1–2; and Tosafot on the gloss "they teach" etc.

And they practiced etc.—for stringency in general; and see R' Akiva, Nagid uMetzavah.
