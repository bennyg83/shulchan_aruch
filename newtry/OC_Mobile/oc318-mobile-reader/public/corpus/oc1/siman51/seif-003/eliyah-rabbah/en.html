(3) In speech, etc. — But per Piskei Tosafos' wording — not to speak until he finishes the eighteen [blessings]; and when there is nefilat apayim, he should not speak until after nefilat apayim; and when praying with the congregation, he should not speak until he finishes "Aleinu." And specifically in a temporary synagogue; but in a permanent synagogue it is forbidden to speak idle chatter forever, until here. Shelah and Rishit Chochmah expanded on the severity of the punishment for speaking in synagogue.
