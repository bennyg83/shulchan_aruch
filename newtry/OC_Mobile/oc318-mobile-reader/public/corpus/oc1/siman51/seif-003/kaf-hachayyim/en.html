3) [Seif 2] If he completed Baruch SheAmar before the chazzan completed it, etc. This implies that if he completed it simultaneously with the chazzan, he should not answer Amen, because he would be answering Amen after his own blessing. So write Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 2; Solet Belulah, Mekor Chayyim, ot 1; Halacha Berurah, ot 1; Birkei Yosef, ot 2; Kesher Gudal, siman 7, ot 26; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 67a; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 3; Siddur Beit Yaakov, ot 11; and Ben Ish Chai, Parashat Vayigash, ot 8.
