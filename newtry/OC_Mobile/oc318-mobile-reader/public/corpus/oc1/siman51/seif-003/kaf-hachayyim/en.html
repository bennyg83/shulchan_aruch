6) [Seif 3] After Yishtabach one may answer Amen after his own blessing. Maran, of blessed memory, writes likewise below in siman 215, seif 1. So too in Shaar HaKavanot, the text of the prayer, page 51b; this was cited above in siman 48, see there. So too in the siddur of Rashash, of blessed memory. See what Rama writes there in his gloss to siman 215, that Amen is answered only after the blessing Boneh Yerushalayim, etc.; see there. I recorded this although in any event we rule like Maran, of blessed memory, because some Ashkenazim follow Arizal's rulings. Understand this carefully.
