(3) [Se'if 2] If he finished Baruch she'amar before the chazan finished, etc. — it implies; but if he finished with the chazan at once, he does not answer Amen, for it is like answering Amen after his own blessings. And so Magen Avraham, se'if bet; Eliyah Rabbah, ot bet; Shulchan Aruch HaRav in Mekor Chayyim, ot alef; Chayei Adam, ot alef; Birchas Yitzchak, ot bet; Kaf HaChayyim, siman 7, ot kaf-vav; Sha'arei Teshuvah on siman 67, amud alef; Razah, ot gimel; Siddur Beit Yosef, ot yud-alef; Ben Ish Chai, parashat Vayigash, ot chet.
