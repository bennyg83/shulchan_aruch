One must be careful, etc. Answering amen is permitted only at the end of a subject. Although this is not entirely clear even in the blessings of Krias Shema, it is proper here because the permission to answer amen during Pesukei DeZimrah is itself uncertain. Nevertheless, for Kaddish, Kedushah, Barechu, Modim, amen after HaKel HaKadosh, and amen after Shomea Tefillah, one interrupts anywhere in Pesukei DeZimrah. One may also recite Asher Yatzar. It should not be delayed until after prayer, lest he need to relieve himself again and lose the earlier blessing. It is better to postpone it only until between sections.
And even for a mitzvah. One should not initially call a person to the Torah during Pesukei DeZimrah, except where no other kohen or levi is present. He may read quietly together with the reader. Although some are stringent, the more correct view is lenient, lest the Torah blessings be in vain. If the chazzan asks his name, he may answer.
