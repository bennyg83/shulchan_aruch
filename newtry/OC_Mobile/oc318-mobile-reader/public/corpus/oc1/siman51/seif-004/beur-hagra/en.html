Siman 64: one must etc.—Rif and Rosh; and their reason: between Baruch she'amar and Yishtabach one may not interrupt, for they are adjacent—therefore it does not begin with Baruch, as Tosafot wrote in Berachot 46a and Pesachim 104b; and as stated above siman 54; and between Yishtabach and Yotzer likewise, as written in Yerushalmi and there siman 63; and in Berachot regarding Keriat Shema as in the Mishnah chapter 2; and between redemption and prayer as stated there page 2 and Rashi there and there 9b, 31a—and all the more so in prayer as in the Mishnah chapter 5; there 21b.

And even etc.—meaning: even though between Yishtabach and Yotzer it is permitted, as written in siman 54 seif 3, because it is a different matter—unlike here, as above.
