Seif 4: One must, etc. Rif and Rosh rule that one may not interrupt between Baruch SheAmar and Yishtabach because they are adjacent blessings, which is why Yishtabach does not begin with Baruch. One likewise may not interrupt between Yishtabach and Yotzer, within the blessings of Krias Shema, between redemption and prayer, and certainly during the Amidah.
And even, etc. Although between Yishtabach and Yotzer one may sometimes interrupt because it is a different subject, here the prohibition is stronger, as explained above.
