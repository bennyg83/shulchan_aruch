And see siman 66, note 9, Sha'arei Shem: he wrote in responsum Mekor Baruch siman 1 that it is fitting to abolish the custom of some who interrupt in order to bring the bridegroom to the synagogue between Baruch she'amar and Yotzer, and the power of this custom is not strong enough to follow it. And see responsum Penei Yehoshua part 1 siman 5. When one must interrupt between Baruch she'amar and Yishtabach, before speaking he says these verses: "Blessed be the Lord forever, Amen and Amen" until "And David blessed"; and when he returns to begin from where he stopped he says these verses as well, for it is like a blessing before and after them—Beit Yosef, Abudraham, Bach, and the later authorities. It is forbidden to say standing prayers and supplications in the verses of zimrah or while the shaliach tzibbur repeats the prayer.
