Responsa Mekor Baruch, siman 1, writes that the custom practiced by some to interrupt between Baruch SheAmar and Yotzer in order to escort a bridegroom into the synagogue should be abolished. This custom has no valid force to justify following it. See Responsa Penei Yehoshua, part 1, siman 5. When one must interrupt between Baruch SheAmar and Yishtabach, before speaking he should say the verses beginning “Blessed is Hashem forever, amen and amen” through “And David blessed.” When he resumes from where he stopped, he should say these verses again. They function like a blessing before and after the praises. Beit Yosef, Abudraham, Bach, and later authorities. It is forbidden to recite maamados or supplications during Pesukei DeZimrah or while the chazzan repeats the Amidah.
