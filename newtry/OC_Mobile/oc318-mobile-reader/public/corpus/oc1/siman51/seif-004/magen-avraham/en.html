One must be careful: Baruch She'amar is a blessing before Pesukei Dezimra, and Yishtabach is a concluding blessing; and between Yishtabach and Yotzer it is forbidden to interrupt, as stated in section 54; and in Yotzer it is the blessing of Keriat Shema — section 66. He wrote in the kavanot that it is permitted to answer Amen after Baruch She'amar; and it seems to me that even in the middle of the blessing it is permitted to answer Amen, since this blessing was not mentioned in the Gemara — section 60 in Tur; and Rashal wrote in section 64 that it is forbidden to say the passage "Who can recount, etc." after Baruch She'amar; and in Beit Yosef he wrote: some have the custom to say Kaddish before Baruch She'amar, and some have the custom to say it before Hodu, etc.
