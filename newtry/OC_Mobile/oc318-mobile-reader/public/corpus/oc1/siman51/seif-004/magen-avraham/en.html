One must be careful. Baruch SheAmar is the blessing before Pesukei DeZimrah, and Yishtabach is the concluding blessing. Between Yishtabach and Yotzer one may not interrupt, as explained in siman 54. Yotzer begins the blessings of Krias Shema; see siman 66. The kabbalistic writings state that one may answer amen after Baruch SheAmar. It appears to me that one may answer amen even in the middle of the blessing, because this blessing is not mentioned in the Gemara. See Tur in siman 60. Maharshal, responsum 64, writes that one may not say the passage “Who can recount” after Baruch SheAmar. Knesses HaGedolah writes that some recite Kaddish before Baruch SheAmar and others before Hodu.
