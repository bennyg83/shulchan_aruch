(3) Through speech, etc. Piskey Tosafos states that one should not speak until completing the Amidah. Where Tachanun is recited, one should not speak until after Tachanun. When praying with the congregation, one should not speak until completing Aleinu. This applies in a temporary synagogue. In a permanent synagogue, idle conversation is always forbidden. Shelah and Reishis Chochmah discuss at length the severity of speaking in a synagogue.
