Baruch she'amar. Standing, even when praying alone—Toldot Yaakov in Bach. The men of the Great Assembly instituted this praise through a note that fell from heaven, and they found written in it that it contains 87 words. Tur in the name of Sefer Heichalot. Therefore one should not subtract or add from the 87 words—Nagid uMetzavah; Mishnat Chassidim page 63 side 1. One holds the tzitzit of the tallit before him at the time of saying Baruch she'amar—Sefer Kavanot—with his mouth together with the segol in "betasbachot" with chirik.
