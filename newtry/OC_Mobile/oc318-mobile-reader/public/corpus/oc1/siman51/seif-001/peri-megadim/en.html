(Regarding) "They say" — Taz: Pri Chadash wondered, since it is not found in the Gemara one should not innovate blessings after the Talmud was sealed; and behold in Kabbalah that the men of the Great Assembly enacted it, for they were the arrangers of prayers; and Taz, siman 46, letter 7 — and every blessing they introduced presumedly they received that our ancestors established it — see there.
