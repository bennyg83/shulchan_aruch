(1) Baruch She'amar — this praise was enacted by the men of the Great Assembly through a note that fell from heaven; they found it written therein, and it has eighty-seven words; and they marked its beginning with the letters kaf-tav-mem pei-zayin, that is, the head of prayer is a blessing of eighty-seven words; therefore one must not diminish or add to the eighty-seven words. And it is proper to say it standing, even when alone. And one holds the two tzitzit before him at the time of saying Baruch She'amar, and after finishing Baruch She'amar one kisses them. With his mouth together with him in segol; in the praises with chirik.
