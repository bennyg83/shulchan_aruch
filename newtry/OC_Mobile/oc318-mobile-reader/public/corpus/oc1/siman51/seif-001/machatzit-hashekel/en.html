(Se'if kaf-alef) They say Baruch she'amar standing, even when praying alone. The essence of the law that Baruch she'amar is said standing — Rama wrote in his explanation in se'if 7 in the gloss; but Magen Avraham added that even when alone one must stand. However, from Magen Avraham's words in siman 53, se'if kaf-alef — where he proved from the above Rama gloss that he holds Yishtabach must be said standing even when alone — it follows likewise for Baruch she'amar, for Rama wrote them together in one clause.
