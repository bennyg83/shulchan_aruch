(Se'if katan 1) Baruch SheAmar is recited standing, even by an individual. Rama explicitly states the basic law that Baruch SheAmar is recited standing in his gloss to seif 7. Magen Avraham adds only that even an individual must stand. Yet Magen Avraham's words in siman 53, se'if katan 1, prove from the foregoing gloss of Rama that he holds that Yishtabach must be recited standing even by an individual. If so, the same applies to Baruch SheAmar, since Rama wrote both in a single clause.
