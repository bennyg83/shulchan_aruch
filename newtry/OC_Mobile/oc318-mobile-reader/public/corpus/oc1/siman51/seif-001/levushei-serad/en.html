Magen Avraham, se'if bet: "Finished with the chazan" — in one utterance. He did not want to explain that it comes to exclude [the case where] he has not yet finished — that he may not answer Amen in the middle of Baruch she'amar — because the Magen Avraham holds in se'if gimel that one answers Amen even in the middle of Baruch she'amar.
