Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. “He concluded together with the chazzan, Blessed are You.” He did not explain that this excludes a case in which the person has not yet finished and therefore should not answer amen in the middle of Baruch SheAmar. Magen Avraham holds in seif katan 3 that one may answer amen even in the middle of Baruch SheAmar.
