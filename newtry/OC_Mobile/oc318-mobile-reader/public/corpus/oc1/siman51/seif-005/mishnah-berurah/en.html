(12) Because of honor — to greet a distinguished person who deserves to be greeted with peace. In the verse "Hashem shall reign, Hashem shall reign, Hashem shall reign forever and ever" one should not interrupt in the middle as between "your God Hashem" and "truth" [Eliyah Rabbah]. He wrote in Darkei Chayyim: when it is necessary to interrupt and he is standing near a chapter, he should be careful and hurry to finish until the end of the chapter, for it is better to interrupt between chapters than in the middle of a chapter; and see below in section 66 in Magen Avraham, SA note 1, that today since we are not accustomed to ask for peace in the synagogue during prayer, God forbid to ask or answer, neither between the chapters of Keriat Shema nor in Pesukei Dezimra:
