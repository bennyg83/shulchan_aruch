(12) Because of honor. This means greeting a distinguished person whom it would be proper to greet first. During “Hashem reigns, Hashem reigned, Hashem shall reign forever and ever,” one should not interrupt, just as one may not interrupt between “Hashem your God” and “true.” Nowadays, when people do not ordinarily exchange greetings in synagogue during prayer, one should not initiate or return a greeting during Pesukei DeZimrah.
(13) In the middle of a psalm. Meaning, in the middle of a section. The sections include: Hodu through “amen and praise Hashem”; “Ascribe strength” through “Blessed is God”; “God of vengeance” through “upon the arrogant”; “Our soul waited” through “as we hoped in You”; “May the Name of Hashem be” through “His glory above the heavens”; between one Hallelujah and the next; “Blessed is Hashem forever” through “amen and amen”; Vayevarech David through “the Name of Your splendor”; “You alone are Hashem” through “in mighty waters”; and Vayosha through “Hashem shall reign forever and ever.”
