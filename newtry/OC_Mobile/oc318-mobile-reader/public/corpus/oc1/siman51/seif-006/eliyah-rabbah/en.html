(6) One must return, etc. — It somewhat implies that only an opening verse requires returning and repeating it — not as the Levush implies. Also the language of Rabbeinu Yonah that Beit Yosef cited, who is the source of this law — one may interpret thus; it requires examination. And this is not comparable to the first verse of Kerias Shema, for there it is written "ve'hayu," and it is invalid retroactively, as will be explained in siman 63, se'if 4, be'ezras the Omnipresent; and see below at the beginning of siman 101; it requires examination.
