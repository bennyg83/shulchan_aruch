(Se'if katan 6) He must repeat. The reason is that for these two features Chazal accorded special importance to the recitation of Tehillah LeDavid, as stated in the following se'if katan: its verses are arranged alphabetically, and it also contains the verse “You open Your hand,” which is a great praise of Hashem for feeding and sustaining all the worlds, whether great or small.
As stated in siman 101, although strictly one who failed to concentrate during the first blessing of the Amidah must repeat the prayer, nowadays he does not repeat it because he is likely to fail again.
See siman 63, seif 4. There it is written that one who failed to concentrate during the first verse of Krias Shema must repeat “Shema Yisrael,” since it is only one verse.
