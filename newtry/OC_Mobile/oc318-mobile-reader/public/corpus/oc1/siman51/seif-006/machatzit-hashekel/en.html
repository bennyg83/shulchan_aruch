(Seif katan 6) He must repeat. Chazal gave Tehillah LeDavid special importance because its verses follow the alphabet and because it contains “You open Your hand,” which praises Hashem for sustaining all worlds.
As stated in siman 101, although strictly one who failed to concentrate during the first blessing of the Amidah must repeat the prayer, nowadays he does not repeat it because he is likely to fail again.
See siman 63, seif 4. There it is written that one who failed to concentrate during the first verse of Krias Shema must repeat “Shema Yisrael,” since it is only one verse.
