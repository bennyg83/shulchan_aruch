(6) [Se'if 3] After Yishtabach one may answer Amen after one's own blessing. And so Maran z"l below, siman 215, se'if 1; and so in Sha'ar HaKavanos in the prayer text, 51 amud bet; and Bach above, siman 48; see there. And so in the siddur of Rashash z"l; and see what Moreinu Mordechai wrote there, siman 215, in the gloss that one answers Amen only after the blessing "Boneh Yerushalayim," etc.; see there. Therefore I wrote this even though in Biur Halacha we hold like Maran z"l, because there are Ashkenazim who follow the Ari z"l; understand and examine.
