(9) There — one must be careful not to interrupt, etc. Shulchan Aruch HaRav wrote there, citing the midrash: Rabbi Yosi said — once I was walking on the road and I met Eliyahu z"l, and with him were four thousand camels laden. I said to him: what do these carry? He said to me: wrath and anger to take vengeance with wrath and anger upon one who converses between "Kadosh" and "Baruch," between blessing and blessing, between chapter and chapter, between "Amen yehei shemei rabba" and "le'olam," between geulah and tefillah. And moreover, his prayer is not heard, as it says, "And you did not call Me, Yaakov," until here. And so Sha'arei Teshuvah on siman 67, amud bet. See Beit Yosef, siman 56, and later authorities there, who also cited this midrash; see there.
