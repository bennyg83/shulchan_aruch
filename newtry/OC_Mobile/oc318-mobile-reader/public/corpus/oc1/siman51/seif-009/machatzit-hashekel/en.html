(SA note 9) "And David blessed, etc." — the passage "For Pharaoh's horse came," which also belongs to the song; and as Rambam wrote in the laws of a Torah scroll, this verse too must be written in the form of a song. Those who do not say the passage "For Pharaoh's horse came, etc." perhaps rely on Ramban in the Torah portion Beshalach, who explained the words of Rabbenu Avraham, who also held that the passage "For Pharaoh's horse came, etc." is from the words of the song; and Ramban disagreed with him that it is not from the words of the song. And likewise many Torah scrolls are found in which this verse is not written in the form of a song. Nevertheless, we should conduct ourselves regarding a Torah scroll according to Rambam and write that passage too in the form of a song, for we rely on him in all matters of the laws of writing a Torah scroll, as explained by the decisors. However, those who say the passage "For Pharaoh's horse came, etc." should not double the passage "The Lord will reign, etc.," for according to them the passage "The Lord will reign, etc." is not the end of the song, and it is fit for them to double the verse "For Pharaoh's horse came, etc." In all such matters, modesty before God is good.
