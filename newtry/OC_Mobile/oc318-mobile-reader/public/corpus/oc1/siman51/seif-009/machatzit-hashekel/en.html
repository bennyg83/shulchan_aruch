(Se'if katan 9) And regarding Vayevarech David, etc. The verse “For Pharaoh's horse came,” etc., also belongs to the Song, as Rambam writes in Hilkhot Sefer Torah: this verse too must be written in the form of the Song. The common practice not to recite the verse “For Pharaoh's horse came,” etc., may rely upon Ramban in his commentary on the Chumash, parashat Beshalach. He cites R' Avraham, who likewise holds that the verse “For Pharaoh's horse came,” etc., is part of the Song, but Ramban disagrees with him and holds that it is not part of the Song. Many Torah scrolls are likewise found in which this verse is not written in the form of the Song. Nevertheless, in our Torah scrolls it is proper to follow Rambam and write that verse in the form of the Song as well, because we rely upon him throughout the laws of writing a Sefer Torah, as explained by the poskim. Those who recite the verse “For Pharaoh's horse came,” etc., however, should not repeat the verse “Hashem shall reign,” etc. According to them, “Hashem shall reign,” etc., is not the end of the Song, and they should instead repeat the verse “For Pharaoh's horse came,” etc. In all similar matters, it is good in the eyes of Hashem to walk modestly.
