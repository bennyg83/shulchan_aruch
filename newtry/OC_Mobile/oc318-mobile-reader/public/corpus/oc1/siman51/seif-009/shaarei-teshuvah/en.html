"And it is good"—Ba'er Heitev. And he wrote in Sha'arei Teshuvah that Yad Aharon wrote like Mahari Saruk to say Mizmor leTodah standing. And he wrote that according to what he wrote in Parashat HaTamid one should say it standing; but in truth there is no such thing, as written in section 48; also it is explained in Sefer HaKavanot that after saying Baruch she'amar standing he sits and says Mizmor leTodah seated—see above.
