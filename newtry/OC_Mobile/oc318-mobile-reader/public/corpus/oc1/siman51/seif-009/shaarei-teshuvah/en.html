And Yom Tov. See Baer Heitev. Shalmei Tzibbur writes that Yad Aharon cites Mahari Saruk that Mizmor LeSodah should be recited while standing. He writes that this would follow the view above concerning the passage of the daily offering, that it should be recited standing. In truth, however, that ruling is not correct, as explained in siman 48. Sefer HaKavanos explicitly states that after reciting Baruch SheAmar while standing, one should sit and recite Mizmor LeSodah while seated.
