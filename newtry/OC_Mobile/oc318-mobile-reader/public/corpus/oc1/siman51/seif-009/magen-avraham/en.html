A psalm of thanksgiving. There are places that say a psalm of thanksgiving on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur because it is written therein "Make a joyful noise to the Lord, all the earth" [Kol Bo].

And not on the eve of Yom Kippur — lest they not be able to eat it and will need to burn it; and for this reason one does not say it on the eve of Yom Kippur; and in Beit Yosef he wrote: some have the custom to say it on the eve of Yom Kippur; and in Hagahot Maimoniyot he wrote that some do not say it on the ninth of Tishrei nor on the tenth of Tishrei; and Rashal wrote in his responsum, section 64, that it is a great error, for behold when the Temple stood, Yom Tov was on the tenth of Tishrei and they would offer the thanksgiving offering.
