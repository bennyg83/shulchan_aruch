Mizmor LeSodah. Some places recite Mizmor LeSodah on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur because it contains “Call out to Hashem, all the earth.” Knesses HaGedolah.
And not on the eve of Pesach. Perhaps the thanksgiving offering could not be eaten and would have to be burned. For the same reason it is not recited on the eve of Yom Kippur. Knesses HaGedolah writes that some recite it on the eve of Yom Kippur. Hagahos Minhagim writes that some omit it on the eve of Tisha B'Av and on Tisha B'Av itself. Maharshal, responsum 64, writes that this is a great error, because in the time of the Beis HaMikdash Tisha B'Av would have been a Yom Tov and thanksgiving offerings would have been brought.
