Siman 69: that all songs etc.—Midrash Rabbah parashat Tzav paragraph 9 and parashat Emor chapter 27: R' Pinchas and R' Levi and R' Yochanan in the name of R' Menachem: in the future all sacrifices will be void and the thanksgiving offering will not be void; all prayers will be void and confession will not be void. This is what is written: "The voice of joy and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the voice of the bride, the voice of those who say, Give thanks to the the Omnipresent of hosts" etc.—this is confession. "They bring thanksgiving to the house of the the Omnipresent"—this is the thanksgiving offering. And likewise David says: "Upon me, O the Omnipresent, are Your vows; I will pay thanksgivings to You"—here it does not write "thanksgiving" but "thanksgivings"—confession and the thanksgiving offering.

And one may not etc.—for it is not offered on any of them, as written in chapter 2 of Yoma 19b.
