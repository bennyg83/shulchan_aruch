Seif 9: For all songs, etc. Midrash Rabbah, parashas Tzav, chapter 9, and parashas Emor, chapter 27: Rabbi Pinchas, Rabbi Levi, and Rabbi Yochanan said in the name of Rabbi Menachem of Gallia: In the future, all offerings will be abolished, but the thanksgiving offering will not be abolished. All prayers will be abolished, but thanksgiving will not be abolished. This is the meaning of the verse, “The sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the sound of bridegroom and the sound of bride, the sound of those saying: Give thanks to Hashem, Master of Legions,” meaning thanksgiving; “bringing a thanksgiving offering to the House of Hashem,” meaning the thanksgiving offering. David likewise says, “Your vows are upon me, O God; I shall pay thanksgivings to You.” It does not say “a thanksgiving,” but “thanksgivings,” referring to both verbal thanksgiving and the thanksgiving offering.
And one does not recite, etc. Because the thanksgiving offering was not brought on any of those days, as stated in chapter 2 of Beitzah, 19b.
