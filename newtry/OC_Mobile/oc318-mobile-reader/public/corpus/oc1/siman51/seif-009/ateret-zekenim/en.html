They do not say Mizmor leTodah on Shabbat and Yom Tov; and they add here nine psalms. They do not say "The heavens recount" on weekdays—and so it appears from the words of the Zohar, Beit Yosef in siman 50.

In such a case one does not say Mizmor leTodah. Some say that one does not say it either on the Ninth of Av or on the Seventeenth of Tammuz (Hagahot minhagim, Laws of the Ninth of Av, section 5). Some say this is an error—for if God will build [the Temple], they will then offer korbanot and the Seventeenth of Tammuz will be for joy and gladness—and then one will not fast (Maharshal in responsum siman 4).
