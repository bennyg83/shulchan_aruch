One does not recite Mizmor LeSodah on Shabbos or Yom Tov. Nine additional psalms are added here. One should not recite HaShamayim Mesaprim on weekdays. This also appears from the words of the Zohar. See Beit Yosef, siman 50. On the eve of Yom Kippur one does not recite Mizmor LeSodah. Some say that it is likewise omitted on the eve of Tisha B’Av and on Tisha B’Av itself. See Hagahos Minhagim, Hilchos Tisha B’Av, seif 5. Others say that this is an error. Were the Beis HaMikdash rebuilt, offerings would be brought and Tisha B’Av would become a day of joy and celebration, and there would then be no fast. This is stated by Maharshal in responsum 4.
