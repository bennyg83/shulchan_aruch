One does not recite Mizmor LeSodah on Shabbos and Yom Tov. Nine additional psalms are added here. One should not say HaShamayim Mesaprim on weekdays. This also appears from the words of the Zohar. Beit Yosef, siman 50.
On the eve of Yom Kippur one does not recite Mizmor LeSodah. Some say it is likewise omitted on the eve of Tisha B'Av and on Tisha B'Av itself. Hagahos Minhagim, Hilchos Tisha B'Av, seif 5. Others say this is an error. If the Beis HaMikdash were rebuilt, offerings would be brought and Tisha B'Av would become a day of joy and celebration, and there would be no fast. Maharshal, responsum 4.
