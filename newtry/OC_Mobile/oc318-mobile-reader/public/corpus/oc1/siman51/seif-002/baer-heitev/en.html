The cantor. But if he finished with the cantor at the same instant he should not answer Amen, for this is answering Amen after his own blessings; but if he finished Yishtabach with the shaliach tzibbur he may answer Amen, for many poskim hold that one answers Amen after one's own blessing—and even though we do not practice thus, nevertheless in such a case it is permitted. The same applies if the cantor finished another blessing and he said another blessing—he may answer Amen. Magen Avraham—and Yad Aharon disagrees and writes that one should not distinguish between blessings that are equal and when they are not equal; rather it all appears as answering Amen after his blessings—Orach Chayyim.
