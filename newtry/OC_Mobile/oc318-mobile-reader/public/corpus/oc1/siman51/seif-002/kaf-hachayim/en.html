(2) There — they say Baruch she'amar, etc. The text of eighty-seven words of Baruch she'amar that we wrote above in siman 48 in the name of the Ari z"l — this does not change at all at any time, whether on a weekday, Shabbat, Yom Tov, Rosh Hashanah, or Yom Kippur, because it contains hidden secrets, as written in Sha'ar HaKavanos, derush Tefillas Hashachar, 17 amud gimel. And so Mateh Moshe in Tefillas HaYetzirah, chapter 1, ot dalet; Yad Aharon in Hagahos HaTaz; and so Peri Chadash, ot alef; and so Chida in Tzeforas Shamir, siman 2, ot yud-tet; and so the words of Menachem in Hagahos Beit Yosef, ot vav, in the name of Mahara Nachum; see there. And so Chasam Sofer, ot bet. And not as Peri Megadim wrote in Hagahos HaTaz in the name of Radak, that on Shabbat they say "Baruch hamenacheil menuchah," etc., and on Yom Tov "Baruch shenasan mo'adim," etc. Chida wrote in Mechaber in Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 51, ot bet: even if the congregation says a different text, he alone says the fixed text mentioned, for its content is full of awesome secrets, until here — and Bach in Sha'arei Teshuvah, folio 66 amud bet; and so in Tzeforas Shamir there and in Kaf HaChayyim, siman 7, ot kaf-heh; Siddur Beit Yosef, ot bet.
