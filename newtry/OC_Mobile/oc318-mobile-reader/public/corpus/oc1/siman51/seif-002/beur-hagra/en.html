Seif 2: If he finished, etc. As stated in Berachos 45b: one who answers, etc. Amen expresses agreement and acceptance. Accordingly, after a person himself recites a blessing, no further acceptance is needed. The same applies here, unlike Magen Avraham. Rabbeinu Yonah explains that one who fulfills his obligation through the chazzan's blessing by answering amen should not conclude the blessing together with the chazzan, for he would then be answering amen after his own blessing. Initially, one should fulfill the obligation through answering amen; only after the fact does one fulfill it without answering amen.
