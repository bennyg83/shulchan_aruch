Siman 62: if he finished etc.—as written in Berachot 45b side 2: "the one who answers" etc.; and the reason is that Amen is agreement, as written in Shevuot "Amen—acceptance of words," and as written in chapter 8: one who blesses and one who answers Amen etc.; if so, after he blesses himself what further acceptance does he need? The same applies here—not like Magen Avraham, and as written in chapter 7 of Berachot: "and he shall not throw" etc.; and Rashi explained one explanation: if he takes leave of the shaliach tzibbur's blessing by answering Amen, he should not say the conclusion of the blessing with the shaliach tzibbur—for then he is answering Amen after his own blessings. And Rashi means that lekhatchilah he must take leave by answering Amen; only bedi'eved may he take leave without answering Amen, as written in chapter 3 of Sukkah: "he heard" etc.—he fulfilled in retrospect.
