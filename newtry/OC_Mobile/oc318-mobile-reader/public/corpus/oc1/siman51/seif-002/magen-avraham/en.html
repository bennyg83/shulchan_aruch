But if he finished with the cantor in one breath, he should not answer Amen, for it is considered answering Amen after his own blessings; and it would seem that this is not called answering Amen after his own blessings, since in any case he is answering the blessing of the prayer leader — and what can I do, for the elder already ruled? Nevertheless it seems to me that if he finished Yishtabach and the like with the prayer leader, he answers Amen, for many decisors hold that one answers Amen after them even after his own blessing, as stated in section 215; and even though we do not practice thus, nevertheless in such a case it is permitted; and likewise if the cantor finished one blessing and he said another blessing, it is permitted to answer Amen.
