Before he finishes. If he finished at the same moment as the chazzan, he should not answer amen, because it would be answering amen after his own blessing. At first glance, this should not be considered answering amen after one's own blessing, since he is also answering the chazzan's blessing. Nevertheless, an elder has already ruled otherwise. It appears to me, however, that if he finished Yishtabach or a similar blessing together with the chazzan, he does answer amen. Many poskim hold that one answers amen after such blessings even after one's own blessing, as explained in siman 215. Although that is not our ordinary practice, in this case it is permitted. The same applies if the chazzan concluded a different blessing while he himself was reciting another blessing.
