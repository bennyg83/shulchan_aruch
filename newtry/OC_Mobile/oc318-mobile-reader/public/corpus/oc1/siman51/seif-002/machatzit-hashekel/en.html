(Se'if kaf-bet) Before [the chazan finishes], etc. — Amen after his blessings is unseemly, as below, siman 215.

Yishtabach and the like — that is, "Yehalelucha" at the end of Hallel, and after the blessing "Shomer amo Yisrael la'ad" in Maariv — for they are in any case two adjacent blessings and constitute the conclusion of blessings.

We do not practice thus. Rather, only after the blessing "Boneh Yerushalayim" in Birkas Hamazon may one interrupt between Torah-ordained and rabbinic blessings, as written in siman 215.
