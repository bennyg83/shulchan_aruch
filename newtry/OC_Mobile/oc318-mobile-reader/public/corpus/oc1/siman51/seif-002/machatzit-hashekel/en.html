(Seif katan 2) Before, etc. This is answering amen after one's own blessing, which is improper, as explained below in siman 215.
Yishtabach and similar blessings, such as Yehalelucha at the end of Hallel and Shomer Amo Yisrael La'ad at Maariv, conclude at least two adjacent blessings and therefore close a series of blessings.
We do not practice this. We answer amen after our own blessing only after Boneh Yerushalayim in Bircas HaMazon, to separate the Torah-level blessings from the Rabbinic blessing.
