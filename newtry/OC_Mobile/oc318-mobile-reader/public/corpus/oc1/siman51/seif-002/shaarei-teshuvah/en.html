The cantor. Ba'er Heitev wrote that the Shulchan Arukh disagrees, and Birkei Yosef agreed with Magen Avraham, and likewise Sha'arei Teshuvah—that one should answer Amen, for there is greater severity in annulling the response of Amen than in what appears as answering after his blessings; as Pri Chadash wrote, it is unseemly but we do not silence him. If so, due to the doubt one should answer Amen, and there is not even disgrace.
