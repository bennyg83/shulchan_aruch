Magen Avraham, SA note 4 — Therefore he wrote that he said it in Minchah. One who examines the responsum of Maharshal siman 64 will find it is not so; rather one should not say the verse "Ashrei yoshvei" only in Minchah, for this verse was enacted only so that one wait one hour and then pray; but in Shacharit he does not say it and begins Tehillat Ledavid, and we conclude with the verse "and we will bless" in order to chain Halleluyah after Halleluyah. And in Minchah, although we do not say thus, nevertheless since he begins with the verse "Ashrei yoshvei" "they will praise You, Selah," it is fitting to conclude with Halleluyah; except in the order of Kedushah and "Uva leTziyon" where he begins Tehillat Ledavid and ends "the praise of the Omnipresent my mouth will speak" — he does not say "and we will bless" — see there. And likewise in Sefer Maamar Mordekhai.
