(8) When he reaches "Ve'atah Hu Elokeinu." — So wrote Rama in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah at the end of the chapter "Ein Omdin"; and I examined there — this is not mentioned, only "Malkecha levadecha anachnu modim" in Nishmas; and so wrote Beit Yosef, Shulchan Aruch, and Levush below, siman 113. One must say that he holds that presumably the law is one for them. Lechem Chamudot, folio 43, did not see these words of Rama; see there. The Shelah wrote, folio 254: I received from my teacher the Maharash to bow at "Aleinu" when they say "Ve'anachnu kor'im u'mishtachavim" — for otherwise he is presumed a heretic. Likewise in Ashmuros when the custom is to say "Bo'u nishtachaveh venichra'ah," etc. — "Nishtachaveh el heichal kodshecha." One should not challenge from "Kol komah lefanecha tishtachaveh," for there it does not say of itself [that we bow]; whereas here it says "nishtachaveh." And that we do not bow at "Anachnu modim lach" — it seems to me because of the language of "modim" [thanksgiving]; and see in the Shelah there and in the Taz, siman 113.
