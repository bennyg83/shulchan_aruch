(SA note 8) One must not prostrate extensively. See section 113 — that is, the reason one must not add to the bows enacted by the Sages, as stated in section 113; and there Magen Avraham wrote, and these are his words: and it is possible to prostrate slightly, as stated in section 51. If so, that is, specifically from standing the default is to prostrate as required until all the vertebrae of the spine are loosened; unlike when seated — one cannot prostrate to that extent, and it is only slight prostration and permitted.
