(Seif katan 8) One should not bow, etc. See siman 113. The reason is that one should not add bows beyond those instituted by Chazal, as explained in siman 113. There Magen Avraham writes: “Perhaps one may bow slightly, as stated at the end of siman 51.” His intention is that when standing, an ordinary bow is performed fully, until all the vertebrae of the spine protrude. When seated, however, one cannot bow to that extent. It is therefore only a slight bow and is permitted.
