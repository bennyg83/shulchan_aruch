(s.k. 3) The drip, etc., and it speaks when it did not break completely (that is, only cracked, as Rama wrote in the gloss in seif 1) — for otherwise even if fit it is forbidden, as is written in seif 1; and the Acharonim challenged: on the contrary, in seif 1 he permits saving in one vessel as much as he wants, and in two vessels at least food for three meals; and it may be Magen Avraham's intent is that they permit saving fit food at least food for three meals even if one will say what will he eat — nevertheless Magen Avraham wrote in siman 334 s.k. 4 in the name of Rabbenu Yerucham that it is permitted to save food for three meals; therefore they permitted him; however if he says explicitly that what he will save he will not eat on Shabbat — certainly in my humble opinion it is forbidden, for even in the morning, after the night meal has passed, one saves only food for two meals, as above; if so, regarding fit food when he says he is not saving for Shabbat need — certainly forbidden; and the drip here certainly he will not eat on Shabbat, for it is forbidden on account of liquids that flowed; and if so, it should be difficult why it needed "forbidden" — since the drip is not fit it is nullifying the vessel from its primary use; it must be taught: even if it were fit, but he is not saving for Shabbat need — even though there is no nullifying the vessel from its primary use, it would still be forbidden; and if so, why was the reason of nullifying the vessel from its primary use needed; perforce it speaks when cracked, not panicked; and if there were no reason of nullifying the vessel from its primary use it would be permitted even if we know he will not eat on Shabbat:
