(א) A barrel that broke — and there is no distinction between whether it broke and has not yet spilled entirely onto the ground, and whether it has already spilled onto the ground [Beit Yosef] — for in all of this the Sages were concerned that if we come to permit saving in many vessels more than three meals, out of his being panicked to return for many vessels he will also bring them via the public domain, or lest he come to repair the barrel that broke; and there are those who hold that when it has spilled entirely from the barrel onto the ground — then it is not relevant that he is panicked to save, since it is already done [Terumat HaDeshen]; and it is possible that there is room to be lenient thus in our time, since we do not find public domain among us — and see there in Terumat HaDeshen that he permits only to draw and pour as much as he needs, only into small cups — for such is not weekday practice, since it is not customary thus on a weekday; and when one deviates from weekday practice we are not concerned lest he come to a Torah melacha:

(ב) Food for three meals — that is, before eating the evening meal, as Beit Yosef wrote at the beginning of siman 334 — see there; and see in Tosafos Shabbos who wrote that where it is common and customary to drink it all day — and all the more so other beverages that are not so expensive and that one drinks all day — one may save more, for there is no fixed drinking:

(ג) And one may say to others, etc. — that is also food for three meals, as above in siman 334 seif 9:

(ד) And he should not dab with oil — that is also for this reason:

(ה) To collect and to combine — this law applies below in seif 2, when it broke on the top of the roof; and it was cited here to inform us that one may save in vessels — that is, even in many vessels:

(ו) To save from courtyard to courtyard — that is, even to another courtyard that made eruv with him; and because since it is not roofed it is somewhat like the public domain — they decreed that if we permit in many vessels, out of his being panicked he will forget that it is Shabbat and bring them via the public domain:
