(ב) And know that from "a barrel that broke on the top of the roof" it implies one may save in one vessel even a great amount, and in many vessels one may save at least food for three meals — that is, to place on the ground one by one; but to bring another vessel — forbidden even for three meals, all that collects from the air; and even to attach to the roof itself — nevertheless the saving appears excessive and is forbidden; for one vessel alone, even more than three meals, and even to collect far from the roof — permitted; for since they permitted only in one vessel he remembers; and incidentally in the name of Semag and Terumat HaDeshen — their proof from that which it said he should not bring another vessel and collect and combine — behold one vessel, even collecting, is permitted; from one vessel it is fine even more than three meals, and even in collecting it is permitted; and so too in Rambam chapter 22 laws of Shabbat 16: a barrel that broke — one saves what he and his guests need (that is, three meals);
