Food for three meals. That is, before eating the evening meal, as is written at the beginning of siman 334; and so too Rambam; and see in Maharai who wrote to infer from Rambam: where one deviates from weekday practice we are not concerned lest he come to a Torah melacha — and it is not necessary; and also Rabbenu Yerucham disagrees with him and holds that even if it spilled onto the ground it is also forbidden to save more than three meals, for we are concerned that out of his being panicked in saving he will bring vessels via the public domain; and it is possible that nowadays, since we have no public domain, there is room to be lenient; and it is possible that for this reason Rama wrote lest he repair it, etc.:
