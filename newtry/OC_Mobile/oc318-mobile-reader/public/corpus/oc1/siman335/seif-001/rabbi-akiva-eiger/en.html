Magen Avraham s.k. 1: we have no public domain — there is room to be lenient. In my humble opinion, nevertheless one should question — for we are concerned lest he sponge, as Tosafos wrote — and this applies also for us; however at first glance what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Beit Yosef — that also for spilling forbidden lest he bring vessels via public domain — behold Beit Yosef disagrees with Maharai from Rabbenu Yerucham's words in Tosafos; if so the reason is lest he sponge; and therefore it appears to me that Magen Avraham holds Beit Yosef argued only that also when spilled, panic applies — which is forced to say that per the conclusion it is the reverse — that when spilled it is permitted; but nevertheless, since it is evident from this that they decreed lest he bring vessels — we must say for new wine a decree lest he sponge; and all one reason — lest he bring vessels:
