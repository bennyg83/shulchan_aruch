דין חבית שנשברה. ובו ה סעיפים:
The law of a barrel that broke. And in it 5 seifim: A barrel that broke — one may save from it food for three meals, even in many vessels; for if in one vessel, even if it holds one hundred meals — one may save; and one may say to others: come and save for yourselves; provided that he does not sponge — that is, he should not place the sponge in the place of the wine to return and drip it — a decree lest he come to squeeze; and even if it has a handle such that there is no concern of squeezing — forbidden, that he should not do as he does on a weekday; and he should not dab with oil to insert his hand and wipe it on the lip of the vessel.And some say that specifically a barrel that broke that he is panicked — if he may save we are concerned lest he repair it; but if it cracked and drips drop by drop such that he is not so panicked — permitted to save in vessels to collect and to combine [Hagahot Mordechai and Tosafos chapter Kol Kitvei and Hagahot Maimoni chapter 24]; and some say that all this speaks only of saving from courtyard to courtyard, but to another house that made eruv with him — one may save in every manner, as explained above regarding fire (Piskei Maharai 196).
