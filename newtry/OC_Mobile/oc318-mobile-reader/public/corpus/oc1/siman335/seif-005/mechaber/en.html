If fruits scattered for him in the courtyard — one here and one there — he gathers little by little and eats, and he should not place them into the basket or into the tub; but if they fell in one place — he may place them even into the basket; unless they fell into pebbles and dust in the courtyard — then he gathers one by one and eats, and he should not place them into the basket or into the tub (and see above siman 319):(.
