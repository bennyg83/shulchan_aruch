If fruit are spread around his yard, one here and one there, he can gather them slowly and eat them, but he cannot put them in a basket or a pail. If they all fell in one place, he can even put them in a basket, unless they fell into the stones or the dirt in the yard, in which case he gathers them individually and eats them and does not place them in a basket or pail {Rama: (See above Chapter 319).}
