One here and one there — he gathers little by little and eats — behold according to what Beit Yosef brought in the name of Tosafos and Rosh — this "and eats" is not specifically; likewise permitted to place them in his bosom and garment; forbidden only into basket and tub, for this appears like weekday practice; but from Beur HaGra it appears that according to many early authorities one may not be lenient in this; and one should not say that Beur HaGra refers only when mixed with pebbles — for behold he brought the version of all the early authorities in the braita is "and eats," not like Tosafos and Rosh; and automatically for Rabbeinu Yonah's explanation of the braita "scattered" — one here — likewise one may not be lenient; and it is possible Beit Yosef also retracted his words, since he cited in Shulchan Aruch the word "and eats"; however in the conclusion regarding mixed with pebbles or dust — certainly one may not be lenient to gather them into bosom and garment; and so Peri Megadim: there is prohibition on account of borer; however according to his words, if he wishes to gather them in order to eat immediately — it is reasonable that it should be permitted according to Beit Yosef's words that this "and eats" is not specifically; but according to Beur HaGra it appears one may not be lenient in every case:
