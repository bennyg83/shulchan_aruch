(א) Seif 5. One here and one there, etc. unless, etc. — for they challenged from that which we say 142a:1: and let him throw them, etc., and we gather them, etc.; and Rabbeinu Yonah explained: fruits scattered — one here and one there; and this is what it says "scattered"; and Ramban explained: scattered into pebbles and dust; and so in Tosefta chapter 17: fruits that scattered — he gathers one by one and eats, etc.; or that he gathered from pebbles and dust — he is liable; therefore it says "in the courtyard"; and do not challenge in a house, for a house is stam made for honor.
