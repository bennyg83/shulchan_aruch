(א) He brings a vessel, etc. — and since it is one vessel, even if it holds one hundred meals — one may save, as stated:
(ב) Provided that he does not bring, etc. — for out of his going after more vessels we are concerned lest he forget that it is Shabbat and also bring them via the public domain; and all this speaks of more than three meals; but for three meals one may save even in many vessels, and even to collect and to combine; and in truth for more than three meals it is forbidden to save in another vessel even without collecting and combining, as stated in seif 1 — only that the manner of speech cited that the customary way to save is with collecting and combining; so too in Sefer Teshuvah and in Peri Megadim he leans to be stringent regarding collecting and combining even up to three meals — for although we hold above in seif 1 that up to three meals one may save even in many vessels, that is when they are placed on the ground; but via collecting and combining it is forbidden in every case whenever it is more than one vessel:
(ג) But if he saves in one vessel, etc. — and it is permitted even for more than three meals, for since they permitted only in one vessel he remembers that it is Shabbat [Peri Megadim]:
