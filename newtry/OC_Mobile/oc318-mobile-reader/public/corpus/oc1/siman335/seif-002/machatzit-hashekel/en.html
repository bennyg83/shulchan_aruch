(א) (s.k. 2) However if, etc. It requires study, etc. — know that on daf 117b it brings a braita: a barrel broke for him on the top of the roof — explained here in seif 2 — and concludes: if guests happened to him — he brings another vessel and collects, another vessel and combines; and he should not collect and afterward invite; rather he should invite and afterward collect; and one does not employ stratagems thus, on account of R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda who said: they employ stratagems — Rashi explained: and one does not employ stratagems to invite guests who do not need to eat and will leave over; and so Tur explained and ruled for prohibition like the first tanna; and so Mechaber cited; if so, the dispute of the first tanna and R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda is whether l'chatchila it is permitted to employ stratagems thus; and Rabbenu Yerucham wrote: however from Rambam's words — and similarly chapter 22 — it appears they explain that employing stratagems means that he employed stratagem and collected before he invited guests — permitted; and he ruled like R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda to be lenient in their words — end of Rabbenu Yerucham; and these are Rama's words here; and he did not write them in the language of "some say," for it may be that Tur also agrees to Rambam's law to be lenient when he collected and afterward invited, since according to Tur this law was not mentioned in the Gemara, and it is reasonable to be lenient, as Magen Avraham wrote; and Rabbenu Yerucham's proof that Rambam does not explain like Rashi — from Rambam's wording: if guests happened to him, etc., and he should not collect and afterward invite, etc., and if he employed stratagem in this matter it is permitted — end; and since the plain meaning of the braita implies they dispute regarding l'chatchila, and since Maggid Mishneh wrote he ruled like R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda to be lenient — Rambam should permit employing stratagem l'chatchila; and from Rambam's wording "if he employed stratagem, etc." it implies he permits only b'dieved; therefore Rabbenu Yerucham proved Rambam explains like Rabbenu Yerucham; and if so, for b'dieved they dispute — for the braita states plainly according to all: and he should not collect and afterward invite, etc.; and therefore Magen Avraham wrote it requires study why to forbid b'dieved; and if so, why does the first tanna forbid b'dieved:

(ב) Therefore it appears to me, etc.; and also Rambam explains like Rashi's explanation; and in truth Rambam permits even l'chatchila; and in truth one must force Rambam's wording that wrote "if he employed stratagem in this matter it is permitted" — for it implies somewhat that it speaks of b'dieved, as above; therefore Magen Avraham brought proof from Maggid Mishneh's wording: they employ stratagems in their words — implying l'chatchila; and that is that Rambam and Maggid Mishneh also explain like Rashi — to invite guests who do not need to eat:

(ג) And as is written at the beginning of siman 533 that he ruled it is permitted, etc. — that is, Rambam in the law brought at the beginning of siman 533 permits employing stratagem l'chatchila; but Rabbenu Yerucham's view at the beginning of siman 533 is not so — see there; and in the Gemara it is found that both are one law; and if so, since Rambam wrote explicitly in that siman 533 that they employ stratagems l'chatchila — therefore here too it is permitted to employ stratagem l'chatchila; and he intended what is written in tractate Moed Katan daf 12b: our Rabbis taught, etc. — they place beer on the festival for festival need, etc.; and if he placed and left over — it is permitted, provided he does not employ stratagem; and Rashi explained: that he should not make much and say for festival need I do this, intending that some remain after the festival; and it asks: but it was taught: they place beer on the festival for festival need, etc., and even though he has old — they employ stratagems and drink from the new; behold it is explained that it is permitted to employ stratagems; and it answers: it is a dispute between tannaim — as was taught: one does not employ stratagems thus; R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda says, etc.; and Magen Avraham understood the dispute of the first tanna and R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda that the Gemara brought is one braita with the braita brought in tractate Shabbos; and the first tanna and R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda dispute only regarding Shabbat and we do not find their dispute regarding stratagem on the festival — rather the Gemara compared the two stratagems of Shabbat and festival to each other; and whoever permits on Shabbat — likewise on the festival; and whoever forbids on the festival forbids also on Shabbat; and since Rambam ruled on the festival that they employ stratagems, and Maggid Mishneh gave the reason also because it is a rabbinic matter — we follow leniency like R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda; nevertheless explanation is needed: from where did Magen Avraham learn that the dispute the Gemara brought in Moed Katan is the braita where they dispute regarding Shabbat; and from this he proved that the Gemara holds both are one law; but from where is this necessary; however also from Tosafos tractate Shabbos daf 139b it appears they hold the braita brought in Moed Katan is what they dispute regarding Shabbat; for there Rabah bar R' Huna said: a person employs stratagem regarding the watch on Yom Tov to hang pomegranates on it and hangs watchmen on it; and R' Elazar said: and he is one who hangs pomegranates on it (and as is written in siman 501); and it asks: why not from that which was taught: they place beer on the festival, etc., even though he has old — they employ stratagems and drink from the new; and it answers — see there; and Tosafos wrote there: and they hold like R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda who said above in chapter Kol Kitvei they employ stratagems — end; and why did they need to bring from R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda who permits in chapter Kol Kitvei regarding a barrel that broke, and not write that it comes from R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda who permits employing stratagem on the festival itself — which is the dispute brought in Moed Katan; or that also Tosafos hold that the first tanna and R' Yosi in the name of R' Yehuda never disputed on the festival, only on Shabbat — but the Gemara in Moed Katan compared festival stratagem to Shabbat stratagem as Magen Avraham holds; however on Tosafos themselves a little study is needed — from where; and perhaps Tosafos also hold from reason that Shabbat and festival stratagem are the same; and if so, why need to dispute in two — Shabbat and festival;
