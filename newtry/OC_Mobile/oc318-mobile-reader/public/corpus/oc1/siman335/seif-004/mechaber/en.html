If wine drips from a vat of grapes that have not yet been trodden — that drip that is found is not fit, for the liquids that flowed are forbidden; and he is not permitted to place a vessel beneath it because he nullifies it from its primary use. How should he act? He places his bed or table there, and then that drip is before him like a chamber pot; and he may place a vessel there to receive the drip so that he does not make mud there; and when the vessel fills — he should not pour it into another vessel lest he nullify it from its primary use, but rather he pours it back into the vat from which the liquid flowed, for it is nullified from its primary use by the liquids in it on account of liquids that flowed.
