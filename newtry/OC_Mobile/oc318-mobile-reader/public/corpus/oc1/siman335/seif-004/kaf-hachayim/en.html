(4) There. Food for three meals. He wrote in Piskei Maharai siman 196 that if the barrel broke and the wine spilled onto the ground — permitted to draw as much as he wants in small cups and within troughs, for such weekday practice is certainly permitted, for once it spilled entirely onto the ground — in what would he be panicked; see there. But Beit Yosef challenged him and wrote one may not rely on this responsum — whether spilled or not spilled, forbidden to save more than three meals; see there. And so Magen Avraham s.k. 1 on Maharai's words above — it is not necessary; see there. And so Sefer Teshuvah note 1; however Eshel Avraham note 1 leans to be lenient like Maharai.
