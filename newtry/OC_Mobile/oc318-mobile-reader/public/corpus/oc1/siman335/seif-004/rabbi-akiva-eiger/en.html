Seif 4. Because he nullifies it from its primary use. I question why he ruled in Shulchan Aruch siman 266 seif 7 here to permit placing a vessel beneath it — according to when it fills to return and pour it into the vat, since there was no eruv techumin on it and also he does not intend to leave it there all Shabbat; and it requires study:

Magen Avraham s.k. 4: and he should not move muktzeh by hand. For nullifying a vessel from its primary use is lighter than the prohibition of moving muktzeh — see Rashi in Shabbos 154 on "and there is pain for the son"; and see Magen Avraham siman 266 seif 14 and siman 394 seif 4:
