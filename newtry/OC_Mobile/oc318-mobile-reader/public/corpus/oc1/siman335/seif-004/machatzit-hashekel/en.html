(א) (s.k. 4) He should not pour it, etc. — he should place that vessel — that is, if that other vessel is large and holds more than the first, he should place the large vessel immediately; and he would not need to move muktzeh; and even if the other vessel also does not hold more than the first — nevertheless it is difficult why he needed to move muktzeh; he should bring another vessel above on the vessel that already stands and is full, or place it beside the first full vessel:

(ב) And it is possible that it speaks, etc.; and similarly there are other cases where it is impossible to place that other vessel there; and see in the Acharonim; only Rama cited one of these cases:

(ג) And even though at the end of siman 338, etc. — and so too above in siman 308 seif 36; and he cited at the beginning of siman 338 that here is the very case discussed; and so too somewhat in Shabbos daf 43, where it asks per the view that one may not nullify a vessel from its primary use — from that which was taught: one places a vessel under the drip; and it answers: regarding fit drip; but regarding unfit drip — forbidden on account of nullifying the vessel from its primary use, for it is forbidden to move it when the drip falls into the vessel; and even though it would be permitted to move it because it will be like a chamber pot — if so it is not nullifying the vessel from its primary use; perforce one does not make a chamber pot l'chatchila; and see above in siman 338 in Magen Avraham and what Magen Avraham wrote, etc.; even though there is solid proof — nevertheless because Rabbenu Yerucham at the end of siman 338 wrote to resolve this, that it does not say it speaks even in unfit drip, and even so it is not nullifying, as is written — that it is permitted to move it because it is a chamber pot; and explain: one may say that the Maggid Mishneh came to change, etc.; or on account of the mishnah that states plainly "one places a vessel," etc. — implying even where it is forbidden to move a chamber pot, such as where a person does not place, etc. — end; and for Magen Avraham Rabbenu Yerucham's resolutions do not appear [convincing]; nevertheless it is not solid proof; and Magen Avraham wrote: and so it appears somewhat, etc. That the wine will be nullified, etc. — if so he would be permitted to move it and it would not be nullifying, etc.:

(ד) One does not nullify a prohibition, etc. — and similarly Maggid Mishneh: one does not nullify a prohibition on Shabbat, which is fixing, as is written in siman 323 s.k. 14; only one of them was cited; and in the glosses of Magen Avraham this too is resolved; so too in Chayei Adam:

(ה) And it appears to me that even if it increased. As is explained in Yoreh De'ah siman 134 — and these are the words of the Gemara in Avodah Zarah: if a gentile poured libation — he forbids in any case; specifically when it fell into the forbidden; but when forbidden fell into the permitted — we say: first by first it is nullified; and if the prohibition increased at the end so that there is not sixty of permitted against the prohibition — we have no concern in a type in a non-type, for the taste of the prohibition is discernible and certainly it returns and is stirred and the prohibition forbids; but even in a type in its type, where the taste of the prohibition is not discernible — nevertheless when the prohibition increased at the end so that there is not sixty in the permitted against it — it returns and is stirred; since in a non-type the taste is discernible — nevertheless for nullifying a vessel from its primary use we are lenient also in this case, since there are poskim regarding prohibition that even when it increased at the end it does not return and stir to forbid — that we do not hold like them; nevertheless for nullifying a vessel from its primary use we rely on those poskim:
