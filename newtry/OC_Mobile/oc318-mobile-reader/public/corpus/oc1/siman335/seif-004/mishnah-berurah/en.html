(א) If wine drips from a vat, etc. — and it speaks when the vat did not crack completely but drips drop by drop, as above in the gloss; and therefore if not on account of nullifying the vessel from its primary use it would be permitted to place a vessel beneath it to save; but if it broke completely, even if it were permitted to nullify the vessel from its primary use, it would also be forbidden to place a vessel beneath it, out of panic:
(ב) That have not yet been trodden — for if they were trodden, the liquid that drips would not be included in liquids that flowed, as above in siman 320 seif 2:
(ג) And he is not permitted, etc., because he nullifies it from its primary use — see in Darkei Moshe who agreed to make another stratagem — namely that he place the vessel with water beneath the drip so the wine will be nullified in the water first by first; and Magen Avraham wrote that even if the prohibition increased at the end so that they are more than the water — we have no concern, for one may rely on those who are lenient in Yoreh De'ah siman 134:
(ד) And he may place a vessel there, etc. — and even though at the end of siman 338 it is written that it is forbidden l'chatchila to place a vessel under a drip, for one does not make a chamber pot l'chatchila — here it is permitted on account of loss, as is written in siman 308 seif 37:
