(א) It is not fit. And it speaks when it did not break completely — for otherwise even if fit it is forbidden, as is written in seif 1.

(ב) And when it fills. And Darkei Moshe wrote: according to what is written in siman 513 — that in a type in a non-type it is nullified even in something that has a permitted form; if so, permitted to place a vessel with water beneath it so the wine will be nullified in the water first by first. And it requires study — for one does not nullify a prohibition l'chatchila, as is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 99; and it is possible that here it is different, for his intent is not to eat the prohibition but only to save the wine; and it appears to me that even if the prohibition increased at the end — we have no concern, for one may rely on those who are lenient in Yoreh De'ah siman 134.
