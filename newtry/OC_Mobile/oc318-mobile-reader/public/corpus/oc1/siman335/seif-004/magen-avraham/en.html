(א) The drip is not fit. It speaks when it did not break completely — for otherwise even if fit it is forbidden, as is written in seif 1.

(ב) He should not pour it into another vessel. And it requires study — for if he has another vessel he should place that vessel beneath it and not move the muktzeh by hand; and it is possible that it speaks when it is impossible to stand it there, such as when it is attached in its place; and even though in siman 308 seif 37 it is written that it is forbidden l'chatchila to place a vessel under a drip, for one does not make a chamber pot l'chatchila — see there; and so too somewhat in the Gemara Shabbos daf 43 — see there; here it is permitted on account of loss, as is written in siman 308 seif 37; and so too in Darkei Moshe; and all agree according to what is written in siman 572 — that in a type in a non-type it is nullified; if so, permitted to place a vessel with water beneath it so the wine will be nullified in the water first by first. End of his words.
