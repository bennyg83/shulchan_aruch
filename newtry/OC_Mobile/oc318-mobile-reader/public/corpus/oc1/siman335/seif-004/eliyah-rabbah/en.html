[4] He should not throw it away, etc. This requires study. If he has another vessel, he should place it beneath the leak rather than move muktzeh directly. The Magen Avraham suggests that perhaps the vessel cannot be positioned there because it is fixed in its place, but this is strained. One must say that something contained in a vessel is not treated as a chamber pot removable because of disgust; that rule applies when the repulsive matter is on the ground. Otherwise, what is in the vessel would not be muktzeh. It appears to me that perhaps direct movement of the muktzeh is permitted because otherwise he would nullify a vessel from its permitted use, and placing another vessel there would nullify two vessels. The Darkei Moshe writes that according to siman 510, seif 13, one species mixed with another becomes nullified even when the prohibition will later become permitted. One may therefore place a vessel containing water beneath the leak so the wine becomes nullified in the water immediately. According to this, it is permitted even if he does not place his bed there.
