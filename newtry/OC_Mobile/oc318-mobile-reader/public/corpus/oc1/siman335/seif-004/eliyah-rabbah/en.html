[4] He should not pour it, etc. And study is needed — for if he has another vessel he should place that vessel beneath it and not move muktzeh by hand; and it is possible it speaks when it is impossible to stand it there, such as when it is attached in its place (Magen Avraham) — and forced; and one must say that only when it is on the ground is it called a chamber pot, not when in a vessel — for otherwise what is in a vessel is not muktzeh. And it appears to me that it is possible it is permitted to move muktzeh by hand because he nullifies the vessel from its primary use; and if he placed another vessel here he would nullify two vessels from their primary use.
