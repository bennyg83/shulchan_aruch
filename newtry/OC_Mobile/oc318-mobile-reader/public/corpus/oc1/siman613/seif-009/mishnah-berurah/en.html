(23) Moist - This is not specifically with the hands, but even to sit on it is forbidden. The reason is that since it has enough moisture to wet something else, the moisture transfers and he derives pleasure; therefore it is forbidden. Gemara.
(24) They are lacking - And all the more so if they are full, for there is concern that the water will splash onto him.
(25) And he will come to wringing - But it is permitted to wipe his hands and feet on the eve of Yom Kippur with a cloth, and the next day pass it over his eyes, for it is still a little moist from yesterday's wiping of the hands, and in this case we are not concerned for wringing.
