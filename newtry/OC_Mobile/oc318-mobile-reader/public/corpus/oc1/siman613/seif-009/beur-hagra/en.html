Seif 9: But, etc. There.
And it is forbidden, etc. That is to say, although according to the strict law of the Gemara it is permitted in such a case, nevertheless it should be forbidden lest, etc., as stated there: “You stated the reverse,” etc. See Rashi and Rif, who have other readings; see there carefully.
One who is ill, etc. This is derived from the law of anointing there, and from what I wrote above.
