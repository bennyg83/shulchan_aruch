Whether - See Taz. At first glance the reasons differ: with metal, it is smooth, and with earthenware, it is not smooth; even so it is forbidden because it emits moisture and brings it outward, and this is washing. However, one can say that even if it is coated with lead inside, nevertheless one may say that it emits moisture. See the laws of Pesach. And see the Levush, who brought some who say that empty earthenware vessels are forbidden. This is in the Tur in the name of some authorities. See Pri Chadash. The Mechaber and the Rav omitted it.
