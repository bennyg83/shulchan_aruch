It will not dry well - Although when it is not dry there is also a prohibition of washing, nevertheless that is not as much of a prohibition as wringing. See siman 301, se'if 46, and siman 302, se'if 10. The Rif wrote that nevertheless it is permitted to wipe his hands and feet on the eve of Yom Kippur with a cloth, and the next day pass it over his eyes, for in this case we are not concerned for wringing.
