It is forbidden to cool oneself with moist clay, if it is wet enough to moisten [something else]; and it is forbidden to cool oneself with vessels that have water in them — even if they are lacking [i.e., not full] — whether of earthenware or of metal; but if they are empty, it is permitted. And likewise with fruits and with an infant. {Rama: And it is forbidden to soak a cloth while it is still day and make it like dry vessels and cool oneself with it on Yom Kippur, for we are concerned lest it not dry well and he come to wring it out (Hagahos Maimoni, chapter 2; Mordechai; Semak; Minhagim). A sick person washes in his usual manner, even though he is not in danger (Rambam).}
