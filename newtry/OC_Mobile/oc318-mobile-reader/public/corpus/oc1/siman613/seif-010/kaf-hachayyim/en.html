Regarding the blessing "Who has provided me with all my needs," some wrote that it should be recited and others disagree, as stated in the Beit Yosef. Wherever the poskim disagree, Kabbalah decides the matter, as the Radbaz wrote, and here Kabbalah decides that it should not be recited. Nehar Shalom, sec. 2.
