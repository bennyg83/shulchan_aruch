[10] It is permitted to pass, etc. The Bach was stringent. This is difficult, for in siman 301 he wrote regarding these words of the Rema, "an elder has already ruled." One can say that regarding Shabbos he is lenient, while regarding Yom Kippur, where there is also a prohibition of washing, he was stringent. So it appears. And so the Taz and Magen Avraham ruled, that one should not pass through.
