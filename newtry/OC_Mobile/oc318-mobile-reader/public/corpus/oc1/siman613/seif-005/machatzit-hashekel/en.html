(Seif katan 5) One greater than he, implying that an equal is forbidden. Yet seif 7 states that a teacher may not go to his student, although even one equal may not go to another. One may answer that seif 7 teaches a greater novelty: even a teacher may not go to his student. One might have thought it was permitted so that the teacher could study with his student; therefore it teaches that it is forbidden, because the student can go to the teacher. For this reason Taz, seif katan 6, writes that if the student cannot go to the teacher, the teacher may go to the student; see there.
