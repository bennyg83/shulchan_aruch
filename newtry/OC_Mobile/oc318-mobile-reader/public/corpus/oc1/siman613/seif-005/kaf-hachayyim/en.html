5) And if his body is soiled in several places and it is burdensome for him to wash each soiled place separately, he may enter the water and wash his entire body at one time in order to remove the dirt upon him. When he washes because of dirt, he should intend that it is not for the pleasure of washing, but only to remove the dirt. Mateh Ephraim, ibid.; Mishna Berurah, ibid.; Tosafos Chaim on Chayei Adam there, secs. 11 and 12.
