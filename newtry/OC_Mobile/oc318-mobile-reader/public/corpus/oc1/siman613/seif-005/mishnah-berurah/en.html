(12) To greet his father or his teacher, etc. - This is specifically when he has no other way to reach the place he desires. See below at the end of se'if 8 and in the Mishnah Berurah there.
(13) One greater than him - But if they are equals, it is forbidden.
(14) He may pass, etc. - And he need not be concerned for the prohibition of washing on Yom Kippur, since it is for a mitzvah matter and he does not intend at all the pleasure of washing.
(15) Both when returning - For otherwise you would cause him to stumble in the future, as he would not want to go again (Gemara).
(16) On his arm - For through this it does not appear like a garment, but like he is carrying it on his shoulder. And the master said: one who goes out on Shabbos with a folded tallis is liable to bring a sin-offering (Rashi).
(17) Until the loins - This is not precise; rather, even a little above them.
