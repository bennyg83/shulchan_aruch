The teacher - See Taz. Even if we say that all of them are Torah law, as in siman 611, or that they are supported on a verse, we do not say that a doubt is treated leniently; see Pri Chadash and the general introduction. Nevertheless, wherever washing is not for pleasure there is no prohibition, for only pleasure is forbidden. It would therefore properly be a rabbinic doubt and treated leniently. To this he answered that since the student can pass to the teacher, they did not permit it. See Pri Chadash: equals also are forbidden.
