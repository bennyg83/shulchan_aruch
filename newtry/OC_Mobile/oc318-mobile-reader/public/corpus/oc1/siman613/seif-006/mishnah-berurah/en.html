(18) But to pass through, etc. — washing that is forbidden only on Yom Kippur they permitted; but swimming, which is forbidden all Sabbaths of the year, they did not permit. And furthermore washing that is different, for one can do it through a change — meaning one who does not remove his hand from under his garment and it is recognizable, as above; not so with swimming where one cannot make it recognizable, and there is a decree lest he make a barrel of swimmers to swim on it. And even if the gentile leading does not do melacha on behalf of Israel, likewise one should not be lenient.
