Seif 4: One who, etc. As above. In the Yerushalmi there and there, shoes are permitted for one who is delicate. See also the end of siman 614.
And the custom is, etc. See the end of chapter 2 of Berachos, 16b, and Tosafos there, s.v. “Mourning,” etc., “One may answer,” etc. The same applies here, even according to what the Rosh writes here in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah, that the other afflictions are D’Rabbanan. Bach writes that even the Gaon permitted washing for a delicate person only when there is dirt upon his face.
