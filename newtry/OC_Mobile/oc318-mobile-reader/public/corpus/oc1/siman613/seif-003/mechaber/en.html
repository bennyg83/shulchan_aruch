If he urinated and rubbed with his hand, or relieved himself and wiped, it is permitted to wash, for his hands are dirty {and he washes up to the ends of the finger-joints}. And if he wishes to pray, even if he did not wipe, it is likewise permitted to wash up to the ends of the finger-joints. {Rama: And likewise a kohen who goes up to the duchan washes his hands, even though they are pure, for any washing that is not intended for pleasure is permitted (Hagahos Maimoni, chapter 2 of Hilchos Yom Kippur; Maharil); and therefore, even if he came from the road and his feet ache, it is permitted to wash them (Beis Yosef in the name of Hagahos Maimoni; Semag; Tur, Hilchos Tishah B'Av).}
