This is difficult: the Tur wrote in siman 554 that both on Tisha B'Av and on Yom Kippur, and likewise the rest of the day when one relieves himself and must recite Asher Yatzar, he may wash his hands. Why did he make it depend here on prayer, which is not all day? According to what is written here, there is no need to write the law of rubbing at all, since in every case he must recite Asher Yatzar. This requires study.
