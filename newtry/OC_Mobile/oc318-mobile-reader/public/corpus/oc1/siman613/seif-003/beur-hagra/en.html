Seif 3: If he urinated, etc., or relieved himself, etc. All of this follows the principle stated above, that they prohibited only, etc. This is as stated in chapter 3 of Yoma: “This is the rule: anyone who anoints, etc.,” even on Yom Kippur, for it refers back to the opening clause. It is stated there: Granted, regarding his feet, etc., it is a mitzvah to rub them. See the old Tosafos in Yoma there, who explain that this applies even if he urinated and did not rub, because of “prepare,” see there at length, s.v. “Because,” etc.
Therefore, etc. Yerushalmi there and there.
