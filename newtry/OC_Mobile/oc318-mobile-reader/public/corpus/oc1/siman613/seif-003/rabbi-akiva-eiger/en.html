Se'if 3 Or relieved himself and wiped. One who leaves a privy, even if he did not relieve himself and did not wipe, since he needs to wash because of the evil spirit, should also be permitted on Yom Kippur. This requires study.
There His hands are dirty. In Responsa Shev ha-Kohen, siman 3, he concluded that if he does not want to pray but only to say sacred words, he washes only the actual place of dirt. But if he wants to pray, even if he did not wipe, or if he urinated and rubbed, he washes until the end of the finger joints. And to ascend to the platform, he washes until the connection of the hand to the arm; see there.
