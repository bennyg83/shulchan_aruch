With mud - See Taz. So wrote the Bach, bringing proof from 614:1, and washing is like anointing, for all of them are from the Torah, though there are no lashes by Torah law; see Magen Avraham 611. But the Magen Avraham in 614 and the Levush there wrote simply that anointing is more severe, and it is forbidden to remove filth with it, but washing is permitted to remove filth. With the help of Heaven, in letter 2 and in the Magen Avraham this will be explained further.
