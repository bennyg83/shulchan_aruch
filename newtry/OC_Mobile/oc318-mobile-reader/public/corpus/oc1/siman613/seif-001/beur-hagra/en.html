Seif 1: And even, etc. Pesachim 54b.
His hands. According to the reading of the Rif.
Or his feet. Tosefta and Yerushalmi there, and the end of the first chapter of Taanis: If his feet became soiled, he immerses them in water and need not be concerned.
Or the rest of his body. Tosefta: If his feet were soiled with mud or excrement, he washes them with water so that they will not soil his garments. For the same reason, the law applies to his entire body. Hagahos Maimoniyos. In our text of the Tosefta it says “his hands,” but this is a scribal error.
Or, etc., that is not, etc. This is derived from the foregoing.
