Seif 2: He washes, etc. See Tosafos there, and above, siman 554, seif 10, and what I wrote there. In the Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Berachos, however, it is stated that on Yom Kippur and Tishah B’Av washing is forbidden unless one is dangerously ill. Its wording is: “He said to him: If not for [a corrupt phrase], if he wishes, even on Tishah B’Av, etc., even on Yom Kippur.” Rambam likewise writes in chapter 7 of Hilchos Taaniyos, halachah 8, and the Maggid Mishneh there likewise writes in the name of Rabbeinu Peretz, that there is no washing at all on Yom Kippur or Tishah B’Av except as stated there: If he was soiled, etc., with excrement, he washes normally, etc. This is the primary ruling.
