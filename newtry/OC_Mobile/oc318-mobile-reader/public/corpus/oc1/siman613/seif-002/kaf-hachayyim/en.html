8) [Seif 2] A person washes his hands in the morning, etc. In his usual manner throughout the year, three times, because otherwise he cannot touch his mouth, eyes, or ear with his hands due to danger, as stated in the Beit Yosef, see there. See above siman 554, sec. 48.
9) Ibid. And he recites the blessing al netilas yadayim. See what I wrote regarding this above, siman 554, secs. 49 and 50, see there.
10) Ibid. And he recites the blessing al netilas yadayim. Regarding the blessing "Who has provided me with all my needs," some wrote that it should be recited and others disagree, as stated in the Beit Yosef. Wherever the poskim disagree, Kabbalah decides the matter, as the Radbaz wrote, and here Kabbalah decides that it should not be recited. Nehar Shalom, sec. 2. So too I wrote above siman 46, sec. 17, see there.
11) Ibid. He should take care to wash only until the end of the joints, etc. This is because some say that this suffices, as stated above siman 161, seif 4, and on Yom Kippur and Tisha B'Av, when washing is forbidden, we rely upon them. Regarding the reason, see above siman 4, sec. 14, see there.
