A man washes his hands in the morning and makes the blessing over the washing of hands. 97 When hands are washed the following blessing is recited "Blessed art Thou, Lord our God, King of the Universe, who hast has sanctified us with Thy commandments, and commanded us concerning the washing of the hands". He should be careful not to wash his hands beyond the joints of his fingers. {Rama: Hagah : He must not intend enjoyment of the washing, only to get rid of the evil spirits 98 The washing of one's hands in the morning was not necessary because they were dirty and had to be cleaned, but rather the washing of one's hands in the morning was of a symbolic nature to remove the evil spirits which were believed to cling to the fingers at night while one was asleep.
