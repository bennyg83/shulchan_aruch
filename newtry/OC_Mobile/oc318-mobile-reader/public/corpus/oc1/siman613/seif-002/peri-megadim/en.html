To rub - See Taz, and see Bach and Magen Avraham letter 2. See siman 7: if he urinated and did not rub, see there. And in siman 6, one who leaves a privy needs washing even if he did not relieve himself. If so, one who leaves a fixed privy may wash his hands on Yom Kippur even if he did not relieve himself. See Pri Chadash and Ran. Now, on a public fast cold water is permitted; Yom Kippur is different, for it is Torah law, and this is proved from Sefer ha-Mitzvos; see Pri Chadash. And according to the one who says that on Yom Kippur all of them are rabbinic except eating and drinking, even so they were stringent with them and supported them on a verse.
