One who saw a seminal emission, even if during the rest of the year it is not his practice to wash when he sees a seminal emission, also does so (Maharil). And some say that even washing it is forbidden, even if during the rest of the year it is also his practice to wash when he sees a seminal emission (Hagahos Maimoniyos, chapter 2; and see above siman 556). And he must be anxious the entire year.
