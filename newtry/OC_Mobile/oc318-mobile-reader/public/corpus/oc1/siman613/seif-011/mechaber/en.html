One who had a seminal emission [keri] in the present time on Yom Kippur — if it is moist, he wipes it with a cloth, and that suffices; and if it is dry, or he became soiled by it, he washes only the soiled places and prays; and it is forbidden to wash his body or immerse, even though on the other days of the year he is accustomed to immerse for prayer.
