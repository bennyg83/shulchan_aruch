(27) Or it became soiled - That is, his flesh became soiled from it in several places.
(28) He washes the places, etc. - This means he washes his flesh by hand and not with a garment, so that he not come to wringing (Acharonim).
(29) And it is forbidden to wash his body - This means the other places. It is also forbidden to pour nine kav of water on himself, even for one who ordinarily conducts himself this way during the rest of the year.
(30) Even though during the rest of the year, etc. - For nowadays immersion for one who had a seminal emission is not required by law, neither for words of Torah nor for prayer, as above in siman 88. One may not permit the prohibition of washing on this basis.
