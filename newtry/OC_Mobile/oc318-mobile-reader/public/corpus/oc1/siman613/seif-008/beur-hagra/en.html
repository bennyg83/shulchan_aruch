Seif 8: It likewise appears that it is permitted, etc. This was derived from the statement in chapter 15 of Shabbos: “What should he do? If he goes around, he increases his walking; if he crosses, his garments may become soaked,” etc. Here they were not concerned for this. The consideration therefore remains that he should cross rather than go around, but this is not conclusive. See Magen Avraham.
