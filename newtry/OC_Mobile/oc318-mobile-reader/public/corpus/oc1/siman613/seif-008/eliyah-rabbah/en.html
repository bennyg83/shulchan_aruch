[8] [Levush] But to cross by swimming, etc. The language of the Shulchan Aruch is "to pass in a boat." In truth, the Tur is like the Levush, and it implies that specifically actual swimming is forbidden. See siman 248, subparagraph 4, where I explained all this. He adds further that the Tur asked, in these words: if they permitted severe washing, for they supported it on a verse, all the more so swimming, until here. The meaning is that washing on Yom Kippur is intrinsically severe, since it is forbidden because of affliction, unlike on Shabbos, when there is no prohibition of washing except by rabbinic decree. This kal vachomer is not relevant there, and this is clear. Now one who examines will see that the Taz was not precise in what he wondered about the Beis Yosef and Bach. Also what he answered for the Tur's words is not correct; see there, for I was brief.
