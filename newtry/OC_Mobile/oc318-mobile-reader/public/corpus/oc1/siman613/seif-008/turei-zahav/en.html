For minimizing walking is preferable. This is difficult to me. Although we find this reasoning in the chapter Elu Kesharim, that one should not increase walking where it is possible to jump over the water, that is different from here, where he will pass through water. Why should we act against the law because of a longer route? My teacher and father-in-law wrote that for practical law it seems to him to be stringent. So too it appears in the Maharshal in his gloss to the Semag, that the permission applies only where there is no other route.
