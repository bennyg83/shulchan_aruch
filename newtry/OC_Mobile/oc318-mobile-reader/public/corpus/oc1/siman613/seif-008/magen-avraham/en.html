It is permitted to pass - Because of financial loss (Ran).
For minimizing walking - For practical law it seems to me to be stringent. So wrote the Maharshal in his glosses to the Semag, that regarding one's teacher it speaks where he has no other route, and therefore it is permitted to pass. Until here is the language of the Bach. I do not know what stringency this is, for according to Rabbi Moshe Isserles it is forbidden to go around. See siman 498, se'if 7, and siman 510, se'if 8, where it is forbidden to carry a large load all at once, even though he thereby increases walking, so that it not appear like weekday conduct. All the more so here, where there is a prohibition of washing and a decree lest his garments become soaked in water and he come to wring them out, as stated in the Gemara, we are not concerned that he increases walking. So it seems to me.
