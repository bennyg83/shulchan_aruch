If he wants to enter a permanent bathroom to defecate, he must remove his תפילין from a distance of 4 cubits: He should wrap [his תפילין] in their straps and hold them in his right hand [while they are wrapped] in his garment and opposite his heart. And he should be careful that no strap should not stick out from his hand, a tefach [length or more]. And when he exits [from the bathroom], [he must] go a distance of 4 cubits and then don them. {Rama: Rema: And there are those who say that this is even [when he enters] without [needing to] defecate (Beit Yosef in the name if Rav Hai), and it's better to be stringent.}
