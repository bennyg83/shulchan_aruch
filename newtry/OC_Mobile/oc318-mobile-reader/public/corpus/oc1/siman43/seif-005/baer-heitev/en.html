His needs. And that which the Tur and the Shulchan Aruch in seif 1 wrote, "it is forbidden to enter to urinate," etc. — for emphasis he specified urinating, even though it is a person's vital need; nevertheless it is forbidden to enter, and all the more so when not for a need. Magen Avraham.

In his right hand. But not in his left hand, because one must wipe with it, as written in siman 3; and there are no sparks here, since it deals with a permanent privy, where one generally defecates while seated. Rama wrote in siman 59 that it is permitted to enter with tefillin into filthy alleys even if there is an explicitly filthy place, and it is good to cover it with a hat; and if he wishes to remove them and return and don them — see there. Magen Avraham.
