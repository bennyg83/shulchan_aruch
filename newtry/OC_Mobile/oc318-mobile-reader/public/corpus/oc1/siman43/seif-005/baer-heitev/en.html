In his right hand. He should not hold them in his left hand, because he must use that hand to wipe himself, as stated in siman 3. There is no concern about droplets here, since the discussion concerns a permanent privy, where a person ordinarily relieves himself while seated. Rema MiFano writes in responsum 59 that one may enter filthy alleyways while wearing tefillin even when there is an expressly filthy place there, although it is preferable to cover them with one's hat. If he wishes to remove them and afterward put them on again, that is also permissible. See Magen Avraham.
