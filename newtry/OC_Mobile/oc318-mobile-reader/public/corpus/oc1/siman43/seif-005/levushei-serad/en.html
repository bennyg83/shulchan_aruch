There, in the Gemara, “from the above”—meaning, because Beit Yosef brought Rashi’s words on the question posed to them whether it is permitted to enter a permanent beit hakisei with tefillin on his head to urinate; and in truth Rashi did not explain so on the question, but on the above, on what our Rabbis taught: “one who enters a permanent beit hakisei removes them at four cubits’ distance.”
