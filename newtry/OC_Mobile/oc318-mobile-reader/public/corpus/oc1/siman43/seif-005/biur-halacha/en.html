And in Sefer Or Zarua, which we merited to see recently in print, it seems like the Ram"a, who wrote in siman 485 that one may pass through a beit hakisei on his usual path when he does not need to relieve himself, with tefillin on his head; and from his wording it seems he distinguishes whether he is merely passing or settles himself to be there; and with this one may resolve somewhat the Magen Avraham’s difficulty in note 9 from the bathhouse that one removes [tefillin], as written in siman 45, section 2; and although for Torah matters we do not distinguish between walking and standing in Berachot 24b, for this matter we do distinguish.
