Or Zarua, whose light we have recently merited, implies the view of Rema MiPano. He writes in siman 586 that one may pass through a bathroom in the course of his route, when he does not need to relieve himself, while the tefillin are on his head. His wording implies a distinction between passing through and establishing oneself there. This somewhat resolves Magen Avraham's question in seif katan 9 from a bathhouse, where one removes the tefillin, as stated in siman 45, seif 2. Although regarding words of Torah we do not distinguish between walking and standing, as stated in Berakhot 24b, for this matter we do distinguish.
