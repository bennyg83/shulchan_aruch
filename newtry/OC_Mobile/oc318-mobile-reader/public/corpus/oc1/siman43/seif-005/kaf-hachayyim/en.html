5) There, in the gloss: In a permanent privy, a person ordinarily relieves himself while seated. This means that when the tefillin are upon his head, he is therefore forbidden even to urinate, as a decree lest he defecate. Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. It is forbidden even if he urinates while standing, and the same prohibition applies when the tefillin are in his hand. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, paragraph 2.
