And even to urinate. Meaning even to urinate while seated, as written in se'if 1.
If it contained a tefach. If it does not contain a tefach, it is subordinate to the tefillin because it is their designated container. Chiddushei Rashba asks: chapter 7 of Shabbos implies that but for the shin on the tefillin, their leather housing would protect them despite being attached to them; certainly then a pouch less than a tefach should protect. So too in Yoreh De'ah 282:6. Rashi explains that the case is where he removes them at home near nightfall: if the container is a tefach, it is considered a tent separating them from the ground, permitting them to be placed on the ground.
