"And even to urinate" — meaning, even to urinate while seated, as stated in seif 1.

"If it had a tefach" — but if it does not have a tefach it is nullified relative to the tefillin, since it is their container. And the Rashba's gloss raised a difficulty: in chapter 7 of Shabbat it implies that were it not for the shin of tefillin, the hide of tefillin saves even though attached to them — and all the more so a pouch less than a tefach. And so too Yoreh Deah siman 282 seif 6. And Rashi explained: it deals when he removes them near nightfall in his house — for when the vessel is a tefach it is considered a tent to separate between them and the ground, and it is permitted to place them on the ground.
