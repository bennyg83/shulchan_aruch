(21) Wrapped in his garment. The preceding se'if permits holding them in one's right hand wrapped in a garment only when there will still be time to put them on afterward, in which case he was not burdened to place them in a vessel. Where no time remains, this does not apply.
(22) If it contained a tefach. If it does not contain a tefach, it is subordinate to the tefillin because it is their designated container, and they may not be brought into a privy.
(23) In his hand, and enters. So that passersby will not take them [Gemara; see there].
