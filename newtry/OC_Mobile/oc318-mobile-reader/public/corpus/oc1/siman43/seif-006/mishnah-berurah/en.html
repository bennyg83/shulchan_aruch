(21) Rolled in his garment — meaning, what was permitted in the previous seif to hold them in his right hand in his garment applies specifically when he has time to don them again afterward; then they did not burden him to place them in a vessel — unlike when he has no time.

(22) If it had a handbreadth — but when it does not have a handbreadth, it is batel regarding the tefillin, since it is their designated vessel, and it is forbidden to bring them into a privy.

(23) In his hand and enters — lest passers-by take them [Gemara, see there].
