And even to urinate water—Rabbenu Yerucham: when seated, or on soft dirt where there is no concern for sparks, as above in section 1.

In a permanent beit hakisei—and the same applies regarding a temporary beit hakisei and tefillin on his head. [Biur of the Maharam on the Mordechai]

And places them in a vessel if it had a tefach, etc.—see Mishnah Berurah; and Magen Avraham brought further in the name of Rashi that if the vessel was a tefach, the vessel is considered a tent to interpose between them and the ground, and it is permitted to place them in one’s house on the ground; and in a vessel that is not a proper vessel, even less than a tefach [is sufficient].
