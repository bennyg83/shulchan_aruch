In a permanent bathroom. The same applies to a temporary bathroom when the tefillin are on his head. [The commentary of Maharam Banet on Mordechai.]
He places them in a vessel if it contained a handbreadth, etc. See Mishnah Berurah. Magen Avraham further cites Rashi that if the vessel was a handbreadth high, it is considered a tent that separates the tefillin from the ground. It is then permitted to place them in his house upon the ground. If the vessel is not one designated for the tefillin, it is effective even if it is less than a handbreadth high.
