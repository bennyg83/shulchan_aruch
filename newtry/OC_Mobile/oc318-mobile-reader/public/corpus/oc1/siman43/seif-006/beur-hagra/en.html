Section 6: “If he was clothed,” etc.—all this is the language of the Rambam; and as the Tur explains in the name of Rav Hai Gaon from what is written “on the day he rolls them up,” etc.—this also refers to “one who enters the beit hakisei.”

And “even to urinate,” etc.—he holds it refers to everything stated above.

And “he,” etc.—as explained, “and he shall not leave them exposed,” etc.
