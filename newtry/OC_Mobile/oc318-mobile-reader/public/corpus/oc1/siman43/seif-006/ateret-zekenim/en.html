“He holds the vessel in his hand and enters.” Some say this applies specifically during the day, when the tefillin are fit to be worn; only then did the Sages permit him to hold them in his garment in his hand and enter. Toward evening, however, when they are no longer fit to be worn again, and certainly at night, he may not enter the lavatory with tefillin even when he does so to guard them. Raavad.
