If he was wearing tefillin and needed a bathroom at night or near nightfall when there is not time to don them again after he exits — he should not enter with them wrapped in his garment, even to urinate in a permanent bathroom. Rather, what should he do? He removes them and places them in a vessel if it has a tefach, or in a vessel that is not their designated container even if it does not have a tefach; and he holds the vessel in his hand and enters.
