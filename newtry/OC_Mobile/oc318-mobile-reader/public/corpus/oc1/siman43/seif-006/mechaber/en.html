If he was wearing tefillin and needed the bathroom at night, or close to dark when there is no time to put them on again after he exits, he may not enter with them rolled in his garment, even to urinate in a fixed bathroom. Rather, what should he do? He removes them and places them in a vessel that has a tefach, or in a vessel that is not their usual vessel even if it does not have a tefach, and he holds the vessel in his hand and enters.
