In Magen Avraham s"k 6 and according to what he wrote above, etc. — meaning: according to what seemed in s"k 4 that even in a temporary privy one must remove them four amot — if so, it is impossible to explain what he inferred in "without a ditch": that if in a ditch he need not distance four amot — for even in temporary one needs distancing of four amot; and if so, necessarily he came to infer that specifically without a ditch it is forbidden to urinate when they are on his head, unlike in a ditch. And in truth this is correct — for there is room to decree lest he relieve himself with them; unlike according to what Magen Avraham concludes, that in a temporary privy one need not distance four amot to urinate — if so, it may well be said that he inferred "without a ditch": that if in a ditch one need not distance four amot, as in a temporary privy; but nevertheless it is obvious that he must remove them from his head.
