דין איך להתנהג בתפילין בהכנסו לבית הכסא. ובו ט סעיפים:
The law of how one must conduct himself with tefillin upon entering a bathroom. And it contains 9 seifim: It is forbidden to enter a permanent bathroom to urinate while wearing tefillin on his head — a decree lest he come to relieve himself in them. And if he holds them in his hand, it is permitted to urinate with them in a permanent bathroom.And this is specifically when urinating while seated, for then there is no concern for splashing droplets; but urinating while standing is obviously forbidden, for it is no better than a temporary bathroom. And the same applies if he urinates while seated or into soft earth where there are no splashing droplets — in a temporary bathroom this too is permitted. And there is no distinction between permanent and temporary for this matter, except that in a permanent bathroom one generally relieves oneself while seated, and in a temporary bathroom one generally does so while standing, since he does not intend to defecate (Beit Yosef in the name of Nimukei Yosef and Migdol Oz).} And in a temporary bathroom it is permitted to urinate with them when they are on his head; but if he holds them in his hand it is forbidden to urinate with them while standing, even if he holds them in his garment, because he must rub with his hand the droplets on his legs. Rather, he removes them at a distance of four amot and gives them to his fellow. And from the Rambam's words it appears that it is forbidden to urinate with them on his head, whether in a permanent or temporary bathroom; and one should be concerned for his words. ובב"ה עראי מותר להשתין בהם כשהם בראשו אבל אם אוחזן בידו אסור להשתין בהם מעומד אפי' אם תופס אותם בבגדו מפני שצריך לשפשף בידו ניצוצות שברגליו אלא חולצן ברחוק ד' אמות ונותנם לחבירו ומדברי הרמב"ם נראה שאסור להשתין כשהם בראשו בין בבית הכסא קבוע בין בבית הכסא עראי ויש לחוש לדבריו:
