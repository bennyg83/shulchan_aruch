1) [Section 1] It is forbidden to enter a permanent beit hakisei to urinate with tefillin on his head, etc.—the same applies on his arm, forbidden for this reason; and so too Rabbenu Yonah and the poskim. And that he specified “on his head” comes to exclude if he holds them in his hand—Magen Avraham, note 1. And so Nachalat Tzvi; Yad Aharon brought it in his Hagahot Taz and agreed; and he wondered at the rabbi of Beit Yaakov, siman 170, who wrote to permit arm tefillin because they are covered—see there. And so from the words of the author of Mekor Chokhmah, letter 1; and so Bnei Chayya brought in his Hagahot Taz, note 1—Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 2; Razav, letter 1. And so from the words of Chayei Adam, klal 14, paragraph 26; and so Shetiliei Zeitim, paragraph 2. And in particular per the Ari z”l in Shaar HaKavanot, derush 5, that even the batim of tefillin contain an allusion to supernal lights and names—as explained there—therefore one should be stringent; and not as Eshel Avraham, letter 1, and Erech HaTzelach, letter 1. And see Etz Chaim.
