(siman katan 1) "In tefillin" etc. — the same applies on his arm etc.; meaning, that arm tefillin placed on the arm in their proper place are also forbidden, even though the Rosh in Hilchot Tefillin siman 12 was uncertain in the name of Rabbeinu Yitzchak of Orleans regarding hand tefillin — perhaps only on the head, where there is a shin, which is a halachah leMoshe miSinai. See the responsum of Beit Yaakov.
