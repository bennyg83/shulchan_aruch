Se'if katan 1: 'With tefillin, etc.; the same applies upon his arm, etc.' This means that it is likewise forbidden when the hand tefillin rests upon the arm in its proper place. This is so although the Rosh, Hilchot Tefillin, siman 12, cites R' Yehuda of Orleans as uncertain regarding the hand tefillin: perhaps the rule applies specifically to the head tefillin, which bears the letter shin, a law given to Moshe at Sinai. See Responsa Beit Yaakov.
