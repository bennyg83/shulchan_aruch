(13) Without a pit. Then he must remove them four amos away. If the privy is in a pit, the pit is a separate domain from the privy above and has no status of a privy, as explained in siman 83; he may enter it wearing tefillin. Before urinating, however, everyone agrees that he must remove them, as at any permanent privy. Magen Avraham concludes that this applies to urination. For defecation, Beis Yosef implies one should be stringent and remove them four amos from the place where he intends to relieve himself, even where it is not a privy at all but his courtyard or the open field.
