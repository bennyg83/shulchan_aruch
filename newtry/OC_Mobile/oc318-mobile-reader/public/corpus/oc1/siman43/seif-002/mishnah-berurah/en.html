(13) Without a ditch — meaning, then one must remove them at a distance of four amot; but if he is in a ditch, the ditch is a private domain for him and the privy above is a private domain for him, and it has no status of a privy at all, as will be explained in siman 13; and it is permitted to enter inside it with the tefillin on him. Nevertheless, before he urinates he must remove them — for this reason, as in other permanent privies; so concludes Magen Avraham regarding urinating. But for defecation, it appears from the words of the Beit Yosef that one must be stringent to remove them at a distance of four amot from the place where he wishes to defecate, even if he relieves himself in a place that is not a privy at all but in his courtyard or at the edge of the field.
