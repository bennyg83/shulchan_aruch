[4] Permitted, etc.—there is no concern for sparks, and for “lest he wipe” there is none, for it is forbidden to wipe with one’s right hand (Levush)—meaning, since one urinates there seated, there are no sparks; see section 1, explained further. And the Taz wrote that even in a temporary beit hakisei there is no concern that he will take his hand to wipe—for here it deals when the tefillin are in his bosom and a belt interposes so they do not fall, or he holds them outside by his garment so they do not fall; and Magen Avraham wrote that it deals when they are tied in his garment.
