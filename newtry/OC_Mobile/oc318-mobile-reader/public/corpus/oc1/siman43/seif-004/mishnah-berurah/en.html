(16) If they are in his lap, etc. — for there is no longer concern about sparks, nor lest they fall from him; and it is permitted both in a permanent privy and a temporary one.

(17) Or in his garment in his hand — it is questionable, for in the first version it is written that even if he grasps them in his garment it is forbidden; see Taz and Magen Avraham, who pressed greatly on this. In the Gra's exposition it is written that this seif, which is from Baal HaTerumot, is a different view that disagrees with that of the first version, and holds that in his garment and hand we do not further concern ourselves about rubbing sparks; and likewise he compelled in Sefer Maamar Mordechai. And tied in his garment — all agree it is permitted.
