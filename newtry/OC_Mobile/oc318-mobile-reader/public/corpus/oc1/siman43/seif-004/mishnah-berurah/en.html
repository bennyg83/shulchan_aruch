(16) If they are in his lap, etc. There is then no concern about droplets or that they might fall. This is permitted both at a permanent and temporary privy.
(17) Or wrapped in his garment and held in his hand. This requires examination, since se'if 1 forbids it even when he holds them in his garment. Taz and Magen Avraham struggle greatly with this. Biur HaGra explains that this se'if, originating with Sefer HaTerumos, represents a different view that disputes se'if 1 and holds that wrapping and holding them removes the concern that he will wipe droplets. Maamar Mordechai also establishes this. If they are tied into his garment, everyone permits it.
