Explanation: in his right hand specifically, for one need not be concerned that he might wipe himself, for it is forbidden to wipe with the right hand; and above in seif 1 they said even with his garment in his hand. And if you wish to settle that which is written here — meaning in a permanent privy as Rashi explained — this is not so, for then he should have explained that here it deals with a permanent privy; moreover, in a permanent privy he permitted even in his hand without a garment. And it is written in Likutei Halachot that the Rav was not precise, for what I wrote in seif 1 is per the Tur, and in seif 4 is the view of the Shulchan Aruch, and they disagree with each other, as above.
