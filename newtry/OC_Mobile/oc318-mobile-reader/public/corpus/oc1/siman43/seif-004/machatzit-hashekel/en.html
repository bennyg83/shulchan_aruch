(siman katan 4) "He removes them" etc. — refers to a permanent bathroom; meaning, since at the beginning it deals with both temporary and permanent, one may well say that what is stated afterwards, "he removes them," refers to permanent. To be sure, if at the beginning it did not deal with permanent at all, there would be no need for "he removes them" afterwards to refer to permanent, for it did not deal with permanent at all. And this is clear from what is taught, "he removes them": if it referred to a temporary bathroom, then necessarily, if they are on his head, he is permitted to urinate in them. Rather, it refers when they are in his hand — then "he removes them" is not applicable, but rather he sets them aside, for he already removed them and they are in his hand. Rather, it refers to permanent and deals when they are on his head — and this is what "he removes them" means.
