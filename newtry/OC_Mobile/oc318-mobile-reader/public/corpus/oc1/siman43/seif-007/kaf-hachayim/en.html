7) There: “And in a temporary beit hakisei it is permitted to urinate in them when on his head”—whether standing or seated; and we do not decree lest he defecate in them, since it is not permanent; and not lest he wipe sparks on them, since they are on his head—Levush; Shetiliei Zeitim, letter 6. But with arm tefillin it is forbidden to urinate while standing because of sparks, for we are concerned lest he rub with his left hand the wrapped straps—for rubbing even with the right is forbidden, and all the more so the left—Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, letter 3.
