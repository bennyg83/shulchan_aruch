That is guarded. It implies that even in his pocket he may not bring them in. But to urinate in a temporary privy, if they are in his pocket, it is permitted — even per the Rambam — Magen Avraham. And it is implied in the Gemara that all these leniencies apply only to tefillin that one wears all day, and one cannot burden oneself [to remove them each time]; but other books are forbidden to bring in, even into a field privy; however, if they are in a pouch, it is permitted to bring them in, except for a Torah scroll, which is forbidden. Magen Avraham.
