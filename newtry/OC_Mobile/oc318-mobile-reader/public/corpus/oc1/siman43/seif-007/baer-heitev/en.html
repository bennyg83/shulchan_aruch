A protected place. This implies that even when the tefillin are in their pouch, he should not bring them in. To urinate in a temporary privy, however, it is permitted when they are in their pouch, even according to Rambam. See Magen Avraham. The Gemara implies that all these leniencies apply only to tefillin, which were worn throughout the day and whose removal would impose a burden. Other sacred books may not be brought even into a privy in a field. If they are in a pouch, however, they may be brought in, except for a Torah scroll, which remains forbidden. Magen Avraham.
