That it not. See Taz, who cites Rashi, Berachos 23a, s.v. 'that it not,' and Tosafos, Shabbos 61a, s.v. 'and perhaps,' and 63a, s.v. 'shin.' Their words imply that one may defecate in a privy while wearing the hand tefillin. The straps do not possess inherent sanctity but are merely accessories of a sacred article. See Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1. One can say that they did not burden him to trouble himself to remove the tefillin, but they were stringent that a handbreadth of strap not protrude; see there.
