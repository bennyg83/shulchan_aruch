That not — the Taz brought Rashi, Berachot 23a, on "that not," and Tosafot Shabbat 61a on "and perhaps," and seif 3 on "shin" — it appears in Shita Mekubetzet that it is permitted to relieve oneself in a privy. And the straps are not holy but articles of holiness; see Eliyah Rabbah, letter 1. One may say: to trouble him to remove — they did not trouble him; but that a strap extend a handbreadth — they were stringent; see there.
