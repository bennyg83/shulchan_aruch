(24) At all. This implies he may not bring them in even in a pouch. Machatzis HaShekel writes that if they are in their own pouch and he places that pouch into a pocket sewn into his garment, the sewn pocket is not their designated container; it is therefore a vessel within a vessel and permitted.
(25) A guarded place. Other books and writings containing Divine Names may be brought in if enclosed in a pouch. Some require specifically one covering within another when he must enter a privy, as above regarding tefillin; a Torah scroll is forbidden in every manner. Shaarei Teshuvah writes that babies who wear amulets containing Divine Names written on parchment must have them enclosed in two coverings, since the babies relieve themselves while wearing them.
