(24) General rule — it implies that even in a pouch he may not bring them in; see Machatzit HaShekel, who wrote that if they are in their pouch and he places them in a pouch sewn into his garment, that pouch is not their designated vessel and it is a vessel within a vessel, and it is permitted.

(25) A safe place — and other books and scrolls that contain Divine Names: if they are in a pouch, it is permitted to bring them in; and some say that specifically a case within a case is required if one must enter a privy; and as above regarding tefillin and a Torah scroll, which is forbidden in every manner. And it is written in responsa that those who dress infants in amulets with names written on parchment must take care that there be a vessel within a vessel, since infants relieve themselves in them while they are still on them.
