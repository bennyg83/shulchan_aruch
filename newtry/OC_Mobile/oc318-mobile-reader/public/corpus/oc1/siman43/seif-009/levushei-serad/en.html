First, Rashi distinguishes a trench only according to his own position that the requirement to remove the tefillin four amos away is due to entering the privy, not due to relieving oneself. According to Rosh and Beis Yosef, however, the four amos are required because the person wishes to relieve himself, even in a temporary privy; this should therefore apply all the more so to a trench. Magen Avraham then raises a second difficulty: Beis Yosef understood Rashi to permit urinating with the head tefillin in a trench, but this is entirely incorrect even according to Rashi. Since it is ultimately a permanent privy, there remains a concern that he may defecate while wearing them. This is the principal reason for the prohibition in a permanent privy, as stated at the beginning of seif 1.
