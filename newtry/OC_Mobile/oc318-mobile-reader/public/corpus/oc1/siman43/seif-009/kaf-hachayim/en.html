9) There: “Rather he removes them at four cubits’ distance,” etc.—this is in a permanent beit hakisei, where it is forbidden to urinate when on his head; but in a temporary beit hakisei it is permitted to urinate when on his head—only if they are in his hand, even in temporary beit hakisei he must give them to his fellow, but he need not [remove them] at four cubits’ distance—Taz, note 3; Magen Avraham, note 4; Shetiliei Zeitim, letter 9. However, Meirat Einayim, letter 2, wrote that if they are in his hand, even in temporary beit hakisei he must give them to his fellow at four cubits’ distance—see there; and see Pri Megadim in Mishbetzot Zahav, letter 3, and in our words below, letter 18.
