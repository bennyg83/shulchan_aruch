Temporary beit hakisei, etc.—“beit hakisei at first,” etc.—see Mishnah Berurah in the name of the Pri Megadim. And if it is a place that stinks because of urine that is always found there, apparently this place has the law of a permanent beit hakisei, for it is no less than a urine receptacle that stinks, whose law is like excrement by Torah law, as the Tur wrote; and it is no less than foul water or soaking water, etc., explained in siman 86, whose law is like excrement—see there. And it is possible that for this matter we do not distinguish between them, and in every case it is permitted to urinate there with tefillin on him, and we are not concerned lest he defecate there, since it is designated only for urination; and we do not forbid on account of the urine itself, since it is designated there—its law is like excrement; that is, if so, urine against a post also has the law of excrement, and nevertheless in a temporary beit hakisei it is permitted to urinate with tefillin on one’s head, and even in a permanent one it would be permitted were it not that we are concerned lest he defecate in them; and if so, it is possible that in this we are not concerned lest he defecate, and it is permitted.
