Seif katan 4. In a temporary privy he must likewise remove them. This means that if he wishes to remove them, he must do so at a distance of four amos. Magen Avraham is not presently troubled by the Mechaber's expression "he removes them," because the discussion can refer to one who wishes to remove them. His only question is why four amos are required, since the prohibition there exists only because of rubbing away droplets. From this he proves that the text refers to a permanent privy. At the end of his comments he writes that the expression "he removes them" itself likewise proves this, because it implies that he is obligated to remove them. Alternatively, at first he understood that "he removes them" could mean that he removes them from his hand and gives them to another person, but at the end he writes that the expression can only mean removing them from the place where they are worn. This is clear.
