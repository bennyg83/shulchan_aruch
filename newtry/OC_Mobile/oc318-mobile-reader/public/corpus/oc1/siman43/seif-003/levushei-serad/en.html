Note 4: “Temporary too requires removing them”—meaning, if he wishes to remove them, he must remove them at four cubits’ distance; and this is not difficult for Magen Avraham now on the wording “removes them” that the author wrote, since it deals when he wishes to remove them, and he only questioned why four cubits, since the prohibition is only because of rubbing—and he proved from this that it refers to permanent. And at the end of his words he wrote that from the wording “removes them” too it is proven, since “removes them” implies he must remove them; or one may say that initially it seemed to him that the wording “removes them” can be interpreted as taking them from his hand and giving them to his fellow’s hand, and at the end he wrote that “removes them” means only removing them from the place where they rest—and understand this.
