(14) Temporary privy, etc. — this explains what we said above, that it is permitted to urinate there with tefillin on his head: in a place where there is no excrement, and also a place where people are not accustomed to relieve themselves; therefore we did not decree lest he relieve himself with the tefillin on, even if it is a secluded place.

(15) Privy for the first time — see Peri Megadim, who holds that this wording is not precise, for even if designated for some time for urinating, nevertheless it is considered temporary.
