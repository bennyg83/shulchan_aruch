1) [Seif 1] One who sees his friend, etc. All the more so, if he sees someone greater than himself, his father, or his teacher, he recites the blessing. An allusion may be found in the verse, 'sending portions, each person to his friend,' while the Sages say in the first chapter of Megillah, 'Our teacher has fulfilled it for us,' etc. This is obvious, for the reason is simply that he derives enjoyment and joy from seeing him. Therefore, there is no distinction whether the person is greater or lesser than he. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1.
