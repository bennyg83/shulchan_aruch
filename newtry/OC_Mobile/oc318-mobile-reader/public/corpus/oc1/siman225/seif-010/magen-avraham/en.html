Even a non-Jew. Although the Sages said that it is forbidden to gaze at the likeness of a wicked person, here the case is that he merely sees him. It nevertheless appears that only gazing at him at length and contemplating his form and likeness is forbidden, while an incidental sight is permitted, as written at the beginning of siman 229. See there and in the Beit Yosef. It is forbidden to say, How beautiful this non-Jew is.
