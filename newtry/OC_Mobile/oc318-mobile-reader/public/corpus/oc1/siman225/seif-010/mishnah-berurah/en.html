(32) One who sees, etc., and beautiful creatures - Whether male or female. Nowadays they are not accustomed at all to bless this blessing, as Chayei Adam writes, see his reason there; nevertheless it is proper to bless without the Divine Name and kingship.
(33) An idolater - This means mere seeing, which is permitted; but to gaze at him excessively and contemplate his appearance is forbidden, as Magen Avraham writes. It is forbidden to say, "How handsome this gentile is," because of lo techanem, as stated in the Gemara.
(34) And not over others - Even after thirty days.
