S.k. 10: If he recited the blessing, etc. For example, red and black varieties. Since they differ in appearance, they are considered two species. All the more so when they differ in taste, even though they share one name. Abaye and Rava dispute such a case. According to Abaye it is considered one species mixed with another, because classification follows taste. According to Rava it is considered one species with its own kind, because classification follows the name, and we rule like Rava. Regarding Shehecheyanu, however, classification follows taste, for even regarding terumah they are called two species. See Shach, Yoreh De'ah, siman 98, s.k. 6, who explains that even regarding prohibitions we do not follow the name for every matter.
