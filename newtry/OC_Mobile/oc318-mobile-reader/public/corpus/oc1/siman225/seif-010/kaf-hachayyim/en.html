10) There, gloss: Some say that one whose son became bar mitzvah, etc. This means when he becomes thirteen years and one day old. Magen Avraham, paragraph 4. This is the beginning of his fourteenth year on the date of his birth, as Magen Avraham writes above in siman 53, paragraph 12. Machatzis HaShekel. See our words there, paragraph 53. The present practice is to recite the blessing when the youth leads prayer or reads on the first Shabbos, when it becomes publicly known that he is bar mitzvah. Lechem Chamudos; Magen Avraham there. It is clear that he may not read before completing thirteen years. This is likewise explained in siman 53. Although a minor may count among the seven aliyos, as stated in siman 282, he may not read to discharge the congregation. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 5.
