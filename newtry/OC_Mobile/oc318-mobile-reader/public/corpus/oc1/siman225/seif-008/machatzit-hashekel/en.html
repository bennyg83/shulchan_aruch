S.k. 8: Until, etc. The Rashba implies that if he recited the blessing, etc. He disagrees with the Radbaz, for the Rashba follows his own view that one does not recite the blessing until the fruit is fully developed, as the Rama writes. Therefore, if he recited it before the fruit was fully developed, it is ineffective. See the Beit Yosef, who cites the Rashba's words.
