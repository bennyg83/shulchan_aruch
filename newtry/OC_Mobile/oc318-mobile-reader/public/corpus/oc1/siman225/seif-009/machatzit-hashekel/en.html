S.k. 9: And if, etc. All the more so he should not recite the blessing, etc. This means that even if he eats the fruit a second time, he does not recite Shehecheyanu, although he eats and enjoys it. All the more so, if he forgot to recite the blessing before the first eating, he should not recite it afterward over that first eating while the fruit has not yet been digested.
