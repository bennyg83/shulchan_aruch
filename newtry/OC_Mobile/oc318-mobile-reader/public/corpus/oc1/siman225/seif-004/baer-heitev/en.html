And Kirsch cherries. Even if they do not differ in name, if they differ in taste, such as white figs and black figs, they are two species. Levush. Responsa Maharil, siman 135, likewise writes that one recites the blessing even over two types of pears, apples, or nuts. Mateh Moshe writes in the name of Sefer Chasidim that this applies even when they have the same taste, such as red and black varieties. Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 37, likewise rules that Shehecheyanu should be recited separately over black Kirsch cherries and red Kirsch cherries. See there. Responsa Panim Me'iros, siman 6, challenges this ruling and leaves the matter requiring further study. See there. See also Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 231.
