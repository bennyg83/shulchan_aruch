And cherries, etc. The language of Terumas HaDeshen, siman 33, is 'or amarellen.' In Mahari Weil, Hilchos Tisha B'Av, it says as follows: Even though they all have one name, for there are many varieties of pears, and likewise apples and cherries, and likewise hazelnuts and walnuts, each is its own species for a blessing. The Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that this is not the practice; rather, since one blessed over one species, one does not bless over the other species. The Malbushei Yom Tov also wrote that Maharash did not rule this way, and therefore one should not bless twice over krichen and shpitzachen. Our practice is like the Rama and Levush, based on the words of Sefer Chasidim, siman 855, Terumas HaDeshen, Responsa Mahari Weil, siman 135, and Agur.
Levush: in their taste, etc. The language of Sefer Chasidim there is: even though their taste is the same.
