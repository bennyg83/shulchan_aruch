4) There: He recites Mechayeh HaMeisim. Since he recites Mechayeh HaMeisim, he should not also recite Shehecheyanu, because Shehecheyanu is included within Mechayeh HaMeisim. Bach writes this in the name of Maharshal; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 3. One recites Mechayeh HaMeisim specifically if he received no letter from the person and did not know about his welfare. If he received a letter or knew about his welfare, he recites only Shehecheyanu. Halachos Ketanos, volume 1, siman 220. Birkei Yosef there writes the same in the name of R' Avraham Azulai.
