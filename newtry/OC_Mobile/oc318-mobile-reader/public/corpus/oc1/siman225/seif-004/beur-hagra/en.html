Seif 4, if he blessed, etc. This is from Sefer Chasidim, siman 848, and he brought proof there from the fourth chapter of Terumos, from black figs and white figs, where they disagree there. This is difficult. First, Rabbi Yehudah disagrees, and Rabbi Akiva speaks specifically where it is known, since it is recognizable; even with the same species, if it is recognizable, it is not nullified. Furthermore, if so, round cakes and rectangular cakes should also be the same. The words of the Terumas HaDeshen there are also difficult, for he wrote regarding wine and grapes that we follow the name; but this is difficult, for if so, black figs and white figs certainly have one name with respect to any amount. There is also no proof from the beginning of Perek Reishis HaGez, where it says there, two species, dark and white, for there it speaks according to the initial assumption, that with males and females there is certainly no uncertainty. Nor is there proof from the fourth chapter of Kerisus; see Rashi there, 20a, s.v. 'and Rabbi Yehudah holds,' etc., since they are, etc.
