He recites the blessing again. If two species were before him, he must recite a blessing over each one, because every fruit has its own taste and there is a distinct joy in eating each. Maharik in the name of an Ashkenazic responsum, siman 295. However, Responsa Menachem Azaryah, siman 45, concludes that this reasoning is incorrect and one blessing suffices. His proof is that regarding the construction of a sukkah, one does not recite a blessing upon constructing it, but incorporates that blessing over the cup. See siman 641. The same applies here.
