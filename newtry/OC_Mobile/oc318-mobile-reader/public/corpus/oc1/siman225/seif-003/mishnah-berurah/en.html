He recites Shehecheyanu. It is only optional, meaning that if he does not recite it he is not punished. Nevertheless, one should be careful not to neglect it. Eliyah Rabbah; see there.
In his friend's hand. The blessing was principally instituted for the joy of the heart upon seeing a new fruit that has grown.
Until the time of eating. One whose heart is not gladdened by seeing the fruit, but only by eating it, according to all opinions recites the blessing only when eating. Therefore, the universal custom is to do so, so as not to distinguish between cases. See Peri Megadim, who writes that ideally it is proper first to recite Shehecheyanu and then the appropriate fruit blessing. The same applies if he tastes a small amount and afterward recites Shehecheyanu, for perhaps this is also proper. After the fact, if he recited Shehecheyanu after reciting the fruit blessing but before beginning to taste, it is also not considered an interruption. See there.
Until it is fully developed, etc. Although regarding the blessing over fruit we rule in siman 202, seif 2, that one recites Borei Pri HaEitz over unripe grapes once they reach the size of a white bean, and over other tree fruits once they have produced fruit fit to be eaten with difficulty, regarding Shehecheyanu one does not recite the blessing until the fruit has completed its growth and is good to eat. Even after the fact, if he recited it before the fruit was fully developed, his blessing was in vain. See Magen Avraham, who cites the Radbaz's view that once grapes reach the size of a white bean, and presumably the same applies to other trees once they produce fruit, since one then recites the appropriate fruit blessing when eating it, one may also recite Shehecheyanu. After the fact, one may rely on this view and not recite the blessing again.
Upon a second sighting. Our practice is not to recite the blessing until eating, even though one may have seen the fruit several times beforehand. If he did not recite it at the first eating, he should not recite it at a second eating. All the more so, he should not recite it over that same eating even if the fruit has not yet been digested.
