Shehecheyanu. It is only optional. Gemara. Shiltei Giborim likewise writes this, and so appears in siman 223, unlike the strained explanation of Knesset HaGedolah.
Until the time of eating. One who derives no enjoyment from seeing the fruit, but only from eating it, recites the blessing when eating. Semak.
Until it is fully developed. The Radbaz writes that when grapes have reached the size of a white bean, one recites the blessing over them. The Rashba implies that if one recited the blessing before the fruit was fully developed, he recites it again when the fruit is fully developed. See the Beit Yosef.
And if he did not recite the blessing. Maharam and the Semak write that he does not recite it later. The same applies according to our practice if he did not recite it when eating. All the more so, he should not recite it over that same eating while the fruit has not yet been digested. Radbaz, part 1, siman 130.
