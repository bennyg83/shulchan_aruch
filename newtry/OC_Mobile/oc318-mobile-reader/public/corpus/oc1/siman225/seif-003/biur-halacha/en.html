A new fruit. Whether it is a tree fruit or a fruit of the ground, such as a gourd and the like. Regarding the ground fruit called earth apple, see Mishna Berurah, s.k. 18. The later authorities write that this blessing requires no minimum quantity, because it is recited over the heart's joy upon seeing that the fruit has ripened. Baer Heitev writes in the name of Halachos Ketanos that one does not recite the blessing over fruits produced by grafting one species with another, because their origin involved an act contrary to the Creator's command. Responsa She'eilas Yaavetz, siman 63, disagrees, for regarding beautiful creatures one even recites the blessing over a mamzer, and there is no more invalid mixture than that. See there.
Or on the tree. It appears that even if he sees it on Shabbos, when he cannot detach and eat it, he nevertheless recites the blessing. The fruit has in fact already ripened, but an external prohibition rests upon it. See siman 223, in the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger concerning one who built a new house but has not yet affixed a mezuzah, and what we wrote there.
