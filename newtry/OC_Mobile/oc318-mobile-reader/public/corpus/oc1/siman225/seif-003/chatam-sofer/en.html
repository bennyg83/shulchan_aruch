There. In seif 3 of the Mechaber, until the time of eating. Note: See Rambam, chapter 11 of Hilchos Bikkurim, halacha 4, where it is clear that even at a pidyon haben one first recites the blessing over the redemption and afterward Shehecheyanu. All the more so regarding fruit, since the fruit blessing is more frequent. Nevertheless, we do not follow this practice, based on the ruling of our teacher, the Gaon of blessed memory. This requires further study. See Responsa Ksav Sofer, Orach Chayyim, siman 25.
