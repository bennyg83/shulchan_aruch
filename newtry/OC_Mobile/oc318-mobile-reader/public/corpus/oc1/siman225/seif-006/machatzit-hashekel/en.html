S.k. 6: Shehecheyanu, and it is only optional, Gemara Eruvin, folio 40b. See also siman 223, seif 1, in the gloss. This is unlike Knesset HaGedolah, who strained to explain optional as meaning that one is not obligated to eat a new fruit in order to recite Shehecheyanu. As stated in the Yerushalmi cited at the end of s.k. 14, if he eats a new fruit he is obligated to recite Shehecheyanu. That interpretation is therefore incorrect.
