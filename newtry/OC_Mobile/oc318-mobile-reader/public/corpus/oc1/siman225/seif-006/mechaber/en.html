One does not recite Shehecheyanu over a fruit that does not renew itself from year to year, even if many days have passed since he ate it. {Rama: One recites Shehecheyanu over a fruit that renews itself twice a year. [Beit Yosef] But over a fruit that has no fixed season for its growth one does not recite Shehecheyanu. [Beit Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Manoach] Therefore one does not recite Shehecheyanu over a new vegetable that remains in the ground throughout the year. [Maharil]}
