Bar mitzvah. This means when he becomes thirteen years and one day old. The present custom is to recite the blessing when the youth leads the prayers or reads from the Torah on his first Shabbos, for then it becomes publicly known that he is bar mitzvah. Lechem Chamudos. Yalkut Chadash, Likkutim, siman 29, writes that it is a mitzvah for a person to make a meal on the day his son becomes bar mitzvah, like the day he enters the wedding canopy. This implies the day he enters his fourteenth year. Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 7 of Bava Kamma, writes that if the youth delivers a Torah discourse, it is a seudas mitzvah even if it is not held on that day.
From the punishment of this one. This means that until now, when the son sinned, the father was punished because he had failed to educate him. The Levush explains the reverse, that until now the son was punished for the father's sin. See there.
