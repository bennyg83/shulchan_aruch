Who has exempted me. Until now, the father was punished when the son sinned, because he had failed to educate him. The Levush explains the reverse, that until now the son was punished for the father's sin. It is a mitzvah for a person to make a meal on the day his son becomes bar mitzvah, like the day he enters the wedding canopy. Yalkut Chadash, Likkutim, siman 29. The Magen Avraham writes that this implies the day he enters his fourteenth year. Yam Shel Shlomo, chapter 7 of Bava Kamma, writes that if the youth delivers a Torah discourse, it is a seudas mitzvah even when it is not held on that day. See siman 53, seif 10.
