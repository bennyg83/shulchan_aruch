Levush: that it does not fall, etc. It seems to me that one need not bless with another formula, for the blessing of Shehecheyanu also includes others with him. So wrote Lechem Chamudos. This requires study from the beginning of siman 223, where there is a distinction between Shehecheyanu and HaTov VeHaMeitiv when another person is with him, and it is from the Gemara, Berachos 59. Regarding the essence of this law, that he does not bless HaTov VeHaMeitiv, it seems to me that there is proof from the Gemara, page 58, which says that Rav Pappa and Rav Huna were traveling on the road and encountered Rav Chanina. They said to him: Since we have seen you, we bless two blessings over you, 'Blessed is He Who apportioned of His wisdom to those who fear Him,' and Shehecheyanu. Thus, even though there were two of them, they blessed Shehecheyanu.
