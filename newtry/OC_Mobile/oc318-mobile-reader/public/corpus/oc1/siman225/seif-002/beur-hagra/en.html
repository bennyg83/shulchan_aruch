Seif 2, one who did not, etc. See Magen Avraham, and as written in seif 1, and he, etc.
Some say that one, etc. Bereishis Rabbah, beginning of Parshas Toldos, parsha 63. So too in Mahari Weil in Hilchos Krias HaTorah, and this is his language there: When Mahari Segal's son became bar mitzvah and read from the Torah, he blessed over him, 'Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe, Who has exempted me,' etc. So too it is brought in the great Mordechai, that this blessing is with the Name and kingship. This is the primary ruling.
