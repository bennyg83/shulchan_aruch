The one who has not seen his friend for a long time and sends him letters, even though he enjoys seeing him, does not bless upon seeing him. {Rama: Some say that one who has a son become bar mitzvah should bless "Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the Universe, who has exempted me from his punishment" (Maharil in the name of Mordechai and Baruch Rosh Parshat Toldot), and it is good to bless without the name and kingdom (De'at Atzmo).}
