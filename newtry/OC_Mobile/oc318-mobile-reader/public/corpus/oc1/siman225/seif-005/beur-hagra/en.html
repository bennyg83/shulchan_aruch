Seif 5, if, etc. Although we rule like Rava in the fifth chapter of Avodah Zarah, that we follow the name, since it is one taste we do not bless. For if one blessed over a lulav at the time of making it, he does not bless at the time of taking it. See the Rosh in the first chapter of Pesachim regarding what he wrote about Shehecheyanu, that we do not bless at the time of searching. This requires study; see there in Mahari Weil, siman 135 and siman 213.
