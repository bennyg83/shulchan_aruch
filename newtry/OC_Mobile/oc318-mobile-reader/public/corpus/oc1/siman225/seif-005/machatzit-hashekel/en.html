S.k. 5: From his punishment, etc. The Levush explains the reverse, that until now the son was punished for the father's sin. According to this, the phrase Who has exempted me from the punishment of this one must be explained based on the statement that anyone through whom his fellow is punished is not admitted into the partition of the Holy One, blessed is He. Hashem likewise said to David, etc., that Doeg was driven away through him, etc.
