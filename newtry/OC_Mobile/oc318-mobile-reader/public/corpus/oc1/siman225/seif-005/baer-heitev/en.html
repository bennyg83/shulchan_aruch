And he recites the blessing. The Radbaz and Terumas HaDeshen write that one should recite Shehecheyanu over new wine. Maharil writes: Out of doubt, I refrain from eating grapes until I drink grape juice. It is proper to bring oneself into an obligation to recite Shehecheyanu for another reason, such as over a new fruit, a garment, or the like, place the new wine beside him, and when reciting the blessing intend to exempt it as well. Emek Berachah and Shelah. Nevertheless, this appears to apply specifically when he drinks grape juice. If it has already become wine, even though he knows it is new, he need not recite the blessing, because new wine is not visibly distinguishable from old wine. If he recited Shehecheyanu over new wine, all agree that he does not again recite Shehecheyanu over the grapes. Olas Tamid.
