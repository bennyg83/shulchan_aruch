**62: every letter, etc.** Since the Gemara counts only hei and kuf and asks why its leg, etc.

**Therefore, etc.** And here a child's reading does not help, as stated above siman 32 s.k. 96; and the reason is that anything severed is as if it does not exist, as above — Ma'aseh Rav — "its leg was severed," etc.; and if so, the form of the letter changed.

**And in one, etc.** As stated above 29b: "its leg was severed," etc.
