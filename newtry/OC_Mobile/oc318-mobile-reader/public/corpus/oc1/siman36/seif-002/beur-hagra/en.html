Seif 2: Every letter, etc. This follows from the fact that the Gemara lists only the heh and kof, and asks, “Why is its leg, etc.?”
Therefore, etc. Here the reading of a child is ineffective, unlike what is stated above in siman 32, seif 16. The reason is that anything severed is regarded as nonexistent, as explained above from the incident on Erev Shabbos where the leg was severed. The form of the letter has therefore changed.
And in one, etc. As stated there on 29b: the leg was severed, etc.
