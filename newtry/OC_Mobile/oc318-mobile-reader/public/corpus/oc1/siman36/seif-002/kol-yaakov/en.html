2. The differences in letter forms between Sephardic and Ashkenazic writing all have support in the mysteries of Torah. Rabbi Chaim Vital writes this in the name of the Arizal in Shaar HaKavanos there. LeDavid Emes there. See also my sacred work Yagel Yaakov, in the discourse on the Torah, where, with Hashem's help, I gave an allusion to this in parashas Bamidbar on the verse, “Each man by his banner, according to the signs,” etc. See there. The letter forms that differ specifically in tefillin writing, as recorded in Shaar HaKavanos in the cited discourse, are uniform for all customs, whether Sephardic or Ashkenazic. See Mishnas Chassidim, Tikkun Tefillin, chapter 1, letter 8.
