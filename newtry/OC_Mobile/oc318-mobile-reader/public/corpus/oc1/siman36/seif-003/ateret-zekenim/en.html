Wherever the letters known by the mnemonic Shaatnez Gatz are written, even in a Sefer Torah, they require small crowns. This follows the law stated in the Talmud, chapter HaBoneh, Shabbos 104. The letters of Shaatnez Gatz without their crowns are not regarded as complete letters for this purpose, and one is not liable for writing them on Shabbos, as explained there.
