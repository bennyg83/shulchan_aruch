**63: and the scribes, etc.** As stated in Rambam and in poskim in the name of Shemusha Rabba.

**And if, etc.** So too from the words of Rambam; and so too Rabbenu Yonah in the name of Rav Hai Gaon. But Rabbenu Chananel disqualifies, and so wrote many poskim from what is stated in Shabbat 105a: "This requires ziyunin," etc.; and see Tosafot there s.v. vehatanya, etc., and there 104b s.v. nitkaven, etc.; and Mordechai at the beginning of Gittin in the name of Riv"a that one requires ziyunin also in a get, for otherwise it is not called a letter from those mentioned; and Sefer Torah disagreed and explained that which requires ziyunin in a Torah scroll, or one intended to write as in a Torah scroll — that which requires ziyunin means he wants to add ziyunin; and that which does not require ziyunin in a get and the like — both cases deal where he did not make ziyunin. And his proof is as stated below; however, their view is that in a Torah scroll it is invalid without ziyunin, but Hagahot Maimoniyot in the name of Ra'avan explains ziyunin: that he should not make the head round but elongated, so that each head has three faces. And I saw some scribes who make tagin from above — that is a mistake in their hands, etc.; and so too in Mordechai, and his proof from what is stated in Shabbat 104b, such as "he removed to its roof," etc.; and in chet there are no ziyunin, but rather what requires ziyunin refers to correcting the body of the letter, as above — that is the view of those who validate. But Sefer Torah explained in Gittin there, such as "he removed," etc., in a place that does not require ziyunin, as above. And behold Rambam did not write even lekhatchila that ziyunin are required — he explains from "seven letters," etc., that it refers only to mezuzah, as stated in the Gemara regarding a mezuzah parchment, and not all letters in it but some of them, and he gave a sign; and see there chapter 1 halachot 2–3: one must be careful with the tagin in it, etc., on each letter three zayins, and these are, etc., and the second paragraph, etc.; and so in Hagahot Maimoniyot there and in chapter 1; and even regarding this he wrote: if he did not make tagin or added or diminished, he did not invalidate. And that which they did not say in the Gemara how many letters in tefillin require tagging — it seems because those in tefillin are also tagged in a Torah scroll, as written in chapter 2 halachah 8: one must be careful with tagin, etc., as written in Ketuvot in a Torah scroll — and these are; and if he did not make the tagin or added or diminished, he did not invalidate; and see there chapter 7 halachah 8 — not so in a mezuzah.
