Ibid. “According to Rashi's view,” in the illustrated form. Menachos states that the roof of the ches is formed with a choter. Rashi explains that on the left side of the ches there is a line projecting upward like a staff. Rabbeinu Tam explains that the ches consists of two zayins with a hump over them.
