**SHATNEZ GETZ.** The tagin must touch the body of the letters, and each one separate from its fellow. Magen Avraham siman 35; also one should not attach them at the end of the letter but only in its middle. And if they do not touch the body of the letters, or they touch one another, they are invalid. However, if we know that an expert scribe wrote them, we may assume they separated after being written and validate them. Rama and Magen Avraham — the latter brought that if one made a chet like this it is valid even if he extended it, even though it is not square, it is valid; and see above siman 32 s.k. 46.

**Did not invalidate.** But Beit Chadash disqualifies if one did not tag SHATNEZ GETZ. And it seems to me that it is good to correct them afterward, and even in tefillin correction is effective, and there is no issue of "not in order" here; and so too in Or Zarua and in Shekhinah Negev. And see Taz. {Rama: And it is written in Baruch SheAmar that he brings many poskim to disqualify if one did not tag them. And it is written in responsa Radach that the same applies if one added to the three zayins mentioned in the Gemara — invalid. It is written in Sefer HaTiyul that SHATNEZ are the letters of Satan, idolatry, and they are two great accusers; and this is also the secret of SHATNEZ GETZ, for GETZ is also the name of one accuser, and the tagin upon them are like a sword and spear to be saved from them.}
