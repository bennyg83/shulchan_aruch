See Beit Yosef, who discusses the forms of the letters at length. See what I wrote at the beginning of siman 32. The Zohar, parashas Tetzaveh, page 316, states that the letter vav has one point above and five points below, and this is likewise its required measure in Sefer Baruch She'amar. The head of the lamed should be formed like a vav and not like a yod, because it must be lengthened. That it should not touch. The distance should be such that an average person can clearly distinguish it while reading from a Sefer Torah lying upon the bimah. Beit Yosef's statement of four amos is not exact. Responsa Rema MiPano, siman 36. Bach writes in the name of Baruch She'amar that the distance is one amah. It should also not be separated by more than the thickness of the roof of the letter.
