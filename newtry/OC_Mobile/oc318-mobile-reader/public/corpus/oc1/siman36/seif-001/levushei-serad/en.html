Magen Avraham, seif katan 3. “If the taggin touch one another, the writing is invalid.” Even according to the authorities who hold that omitting taggin entirely does not invalidate the writing, improperly formed taggin appear like additional small letters between the lines and therefore invalidate it. Thus writes Rema MiPano there. At first glance this is a refutation of Rav Yechezkel Landau in Noda BiYehudah, Yoreh Deah, part 1, siman 74, who writes that an extra letter does not invalidate. See there. This is not the place to elaborate.
