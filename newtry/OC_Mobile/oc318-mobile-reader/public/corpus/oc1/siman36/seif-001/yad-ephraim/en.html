In Magen Avraham, se'if katan 3: In the body of the letters. When the taggin do not touch the letters, they appear like small independent letters between the lines. Although complete omission of the taggin does not invalidate the writing, detached taggin are worse. Responsa Ramach writes this there. If the taggin touch one another, the reason for invalidity is that they then appear to form a different letter.
