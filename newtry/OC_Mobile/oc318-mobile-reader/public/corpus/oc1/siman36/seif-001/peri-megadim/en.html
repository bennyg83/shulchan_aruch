What the rabbi wrote in hagahah: if he changed writing style — meaning not writing forms in books; nevertheless letter-form must be upon it. All the more if he wrote resh-dalet-lamed, bet-lamed-yod, kaf-peh-yod — invalid. What Taz wrote that child does not help — without correction.

One problem I record: Shabbat 104a concludes Dantchin — wrote zeinin must be on left head of zayin; explained: GETZ without zeinin are not letters at all (commentators on this). Rabbi Ashi there 104b: one letter taken, removed chatoteret, made two zayins — zein (missing = no writing at all). Rabbi Shimshon Ashiveti in Tziyunim and Atot there 104:2 — Netchuin and Kof 1 Dalet — from Rav. Shabbat baraita not divided; we must write also. Who writes Nami must — see Shabbat 103:2; Rashi 11a yes, even from Shita must two letters Alef-Kuf 24 without zayin; Shabbat Yoma: in STaM begins with zayin; we do not fulfill his thought. And PZ if wrote broken letters on Shabbat — letters, if so ruled, liable like scribe; Ram from S. 17; R. exempt as minister in STaM; and I.A. Yevamot also.
