(א) And this is what Taz explained in the previous letter; and in Or Zarua he wrote that Rabbenu Chananel conceded to this that it is forbidden to mention measure explicitly; only this is permitted — that he say "fill this vessel for me" ten times and the like; but to total — forbidden to say "such vessels you gave me — give more such and they will be such and such:"

(ב) That which the Mechaber wrote in seif 3: permitted to say to one's fellow "give me eggs by count" and permitted to the shopkeeper, etc. — Or Zarua explained: at the beginning he informs us even though the buyer does not mention the count how much, only says "give me" and counts — nevertheless permitted; and at the end he teaches us even the buyer says so — permitted; and somewhat difficult, for the end is obvious that it is permitted; and in Levush it implies he teaches us for "to his fellow" one might say the loan returns in its form, or his exchange; and a shopkeeper — buying and selling — nevertheless permitted:

(ג) We cite in Yerushalmi chapter Ein Tzedim halakhah 9: the tanna taught "he says to his fellow give me a kallah of spices" — for such is an ordinary person's way, etc.; and Abaye; and Shulchan Aruch 324: in a baker's vessel; and in Yerushalmi it is explicit even for an ordinary person that he should not burn his cooked dish — permitted to measure on Yom Tov spices; and all the more permitted to say "give me a kallah measure," etc.; but on Shabbat one may say forbidden to measure and to say "give me a measure of spices" — for on Yom Tov it was permitted for ochel nefesh, not on Shabbat, as we say:
