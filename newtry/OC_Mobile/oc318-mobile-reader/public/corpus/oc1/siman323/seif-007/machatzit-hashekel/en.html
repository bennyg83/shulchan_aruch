(א) (s.k. 7) And some say to diminish. It requires further study, for it implies all agree to this. In tractate Beitzah beginning of chapter HaMeivi we learned: one who brings wine vessels from place to place shall not bring them in a basket and box; but he brings on his shoulder, etc.; and Tosafos wrote thus: one might say we say on Shabbat in chapter Mefanin — one clears four and five boxes (and this law was mentioned in siman 333 seif 2) on account of guests and on account of bitul beit hamidrash. And one may say there it discusses when he cannot take out outside (for it discusses Shabbat) and they do not see him — therefore permitted. And here it discusses Yom Tov when he can take it out and they see him — therefore forbidden, for the viewer says for weekday need he brings them; and they concluded. And with this one answers that there on Shabbat they say to diminish in carrying is preferable — meaning to bring in basket and box rather than bring little by little, for he diminishes in trouble and increases in carrying; and here they say the opposite — the reason on Shabbat where there is no concern of viewers, preferable to diminish in carrying; but on Yom Tov where there is concern of viewers and there is like weekday activity — therefore preferable to diminish in load through a change. Behold it is proven that on Shabbat on the contrary to diminish in carrying is preferable from the necessity of Tosafos' wonder; and if so it is difficult why the Mechaber wrote in the language "some say," and also on the first opinion that wrote one must increase in carrying it is difficult. And what Tur wrote, etc. — from this Rabbeinu Yerucham ruled Tur disagrees with Tosafos, and there is no necessity from Tosafos' words that Tosafos discusses a mixed alley where it is permitted to take out, and Shabbat and Yom Tov are equal:

(ב) However in Ran it implies, etc. — he resolves the above wonder of Tosafos, thus: beginning of chapter HaMeivi — he shall not bring them in basket and box to give three and four barrels within one box on account that it appears like weekday activity to give loads, etc.; and even though we conclude in chapter Mefanin that increasing in load is preferable, etc. — here it is different for he does weekday activity, end of his words. It appears from his words he does not distinguish between Yom Tov and Shabbat except when giving three or four within one box, which resembles weekday activity — forbidden also on Shabbat; and through the box itself when he gives nothing within the box — also on Yom Tov permitted. And see there in gloss below siman 501 Rama ruled even on Yom Tov from corner to corner in the same courtyard, if he does not take it outside — permitted to bring in a box, as Tosafos' words and the above "some say"; and he also did not mention Ran's distinction whether he gives three or four within one box or not:
