(א) Permitted, etc. — even if he had the vessel before Shabbat and Yom Tov; and according to those who forbid, even if the vessel reached him on Shabbat that the non-Jew gave him as a gift — nevertheless forbidden, because it appears like fixing the vessel through immersion, for initially it was forbidden to use it; therefore forbidden rabbinically:

(ב) New vessel — acquired from the non-Jew. The Mechaber was silent and did not mention which vessel he discusses; and it implies even a metal vessel, for many poskim hold its immersion is d'oraisa, and the Mechaber was likewise silent in Yoreh De'ah siman 120 seif 14 — also permitted to immerse; and see in Biur Halacha:

(ג) And some forbid — meaning even in a glass vessel, and even if it is for Shabbat need [Eshel Avraham and others]:

(ד) And some say, etc. — it implies from the law he agrees to the first opinion; and see in Darkei Moshe who wrote that for practical halacha one should rule like those who forbid; and likewise ruled in responsum Sha'agat Aryeh siman 56; nevertheless if he transgressed and immersed, perhaps permitted to use it since some permit even l'chatchila. And if he has doubt on the vessel whether it needs immersion — it appears to me he should rely on the first opinion and immerse it l'chatchila when he cannot easily do the advice explained in Shulchan Aruch:

(ה) As a gift, etc. — and even though forbidden to give a gift on Shabbat, as in siman 306 — here permitted on account of Shabbat need:

(ו) And he shall return and ask him — nevertheless after Shabbat he must immerse it, since ultimately the vessel will come under his hand it is his; so too Taz, and he brought proof for this; nevertheless he shall immerse it without a blessing, or immerse another vessel with it and bless on both of them:

(ז) He shall fill it with water — and he does not bless, for then it is not evident he does so for immersion; and it is obvious this is specifically if he has no other vessel for Shabbat need, for otherwise forbidden because he wastes the blessing in vain:
