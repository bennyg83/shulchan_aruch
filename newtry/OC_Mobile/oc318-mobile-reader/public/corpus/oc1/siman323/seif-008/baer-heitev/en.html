To rinse it — for the vessel itself is permitted and the wine forbids it, and when he removes it he is not fixing it but only like removing a refuse pan from it. And it is implied on Shabbos daf 47 in Tosafos that even if there are crumbs of bread in the cup that an idolater soaked in — permitted to move it and it is not a base for something forbidden, for they are nullified relative to the cup; see siman 308 seif 27. Forbidden to nullify prohibition on Shabbat or Yom Tov, for there is no greater repair than this, as Yoreh De'ah siman 99 seif 6 wrote; and where the prohibition is slight it is permitted, as siman 507 seif 2 wrote. But permitted to estimate sixty even if it became mixed on Erev Shabbat and he did not know until Shabbat — Terumat HaDeshen siman 54.
