And some are lenient to say, etc. Rosh wrote in responsum klal 22 what they are accustomed in your place — they measure wine on Shabbat and write from erev Shabbat so-and-so left such and such money and so-and-so such and such; and tomorrow when they measure the wine the wine owner takes a needle and makes holes corresponding to the number of measures he delivers — know that two evils they do: one, the measuring as the mishnah teaches a man may say to his fellow "fill this vessel for me" but not by measure; and the gemara explains but not in a vessel designated for measure; and further forbidden to look at that writing as the mishnah teaches a person counts his guests and desserts from his mouth but not from writing; and the gemara explains what is the reason — Rav Bibi said a decree lest he erase; Abaye said lest he read gentiles' documents — end of his words; and Beit Yosef brought it and Or Zarua letter 2; and Magen Avraham s.k. 5 wrote there Or Zarua that likewise one must forbid the zeichin in Ashkenaz language who make a writing of what he owes him from the beverage; and likewise zeichin they make from ivory also forbidden according to what the Mechaber wrote above siman 307 seif 12 that on a tablet and in a ledger even if engraved forbidden to read it — end of his words; and Magen Avraham there wrote that it implies from this responsum of Rosh that it is also forbidden to write on notes a sum of money; and when he takes the wine he places the note where the man's name is written — forbidden to read in these notes for they are gentiles' documents; and therefore they were accustomed to take a kernel for a sign or something else; and charity collectors perhaps permitted in notes as written in siman 307; and nevertheless better to be stringent where possible with a kernel; and so the ancients practiced — end of his words; and R' Levi: he gives a kernel where the man's name is written — one kernel for each measure.
