Permitted to rinse it. Rosh gave the reason for this that he holds that immersion there the prohibition is in the body of the vessel — it appears like fixing; and here the body of the vessel is permitted and the wine forbids it, and when he rinses he is not fixing it but only like removing a refuse pan from it, end of his words. And at first glance difficult: why did Shulchan Aruch not write a cup from which a gentile drank wine?
