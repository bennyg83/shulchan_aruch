10) There, gloss: This is the widespread custom, etc. This refers to Ashkenazim. Sephardim, who accepted the rulings of the Shulchan Aruch, must be stringent not to measure with a utensil designated for measuring and pour it into the buyer's vessels, even when giving slightly less or more. Where the lenient custom following Rema is already established, however, one should not protest. It is clear that those who follow the lenient view stated by Rema may say, 'Fill it for me another time,' meaning as on the first occasion when he gave slightly less or more. He may repeat this formulation as many times as necessary. Birkei Yosef, Shiyurei Berachah, paragraph 1.
