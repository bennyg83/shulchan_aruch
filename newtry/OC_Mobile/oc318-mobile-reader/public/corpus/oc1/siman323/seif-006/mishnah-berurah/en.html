(א) They rinse — even ten cups, and he needs only one — permitted to rinse all of them, since each one is fit for him they are all permitted; and likewise spreading ten beds:

(ב) For the need of the day — meaning even on Shabbat night permitted to rinse for tomorrow's need or for the need of a third meal:

(ג) Such as remains — and if he knows he will not need those vessels anymore — forbidden to rinse them on account of exertion; and conversely if he knows he will need them, such as he wants to eat more fruit even after the third meal — likewise permitted; only here we discuss stam:

(ד) All day — and if it is clear to him he will not drink again — forbidden to rinse the cup:
