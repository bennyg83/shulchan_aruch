(4) Sheep sellers who are called in Arabic arushi — custom that everyone who comes to buy from him on Shabbat arushi he does not give him except if he leaves a pledge such as rizah or mandil; and the owner of the arushi ties knots on it corresponding to the number of arushim he took — not two knots one upon the other, rather each knot separately in a special place so that tomorrow he collect per the number of arushim as the number of knots in the pledge; it appears there is no prohibition at all, for if on account of the pledge there is no prohibition, as Moredkei wrote above siman 307 seif 11; and if because of the knots — behold this is tying like it is not permanent and is not an artisan's act and permitted to tie initially as written above siman 317 seif 1; Pischei Teshuvah letter 2, see there:
