(א) Seif 4 "there — money." per the textual reading of the Ri'f in [the view of] the first Tanna: sum of purchase; and as the Ran wrote, the wording is not "sum"; and as is stated, neither of these two may one stipulate — one may not fix a price etc.; and as Rashi wrote s.v. "sum of purchase," money; and Tosafos wrote there s.v. "that not." And it is difficult etc.:

(ב) And not the sum of a measure. per the textual reading of Rashi in the words of the first Tanna: sum of a number:

(ג) And not the sum of a number. per the textual reading of R' Chananel in [the view of] the first Tanna: sum of a number — to say "behold" etc. Tosafos there, and the Tur cited all the explanations to be stringent, and so too the Rosh; and it appears to me there is a printing error in the Shulchan Aruch and it should read there "measure," and as Rashi wrote s.v. "sum of measure" etc.; and so too the Rambam that he should not mention to him the name of a measure nor the sum of a number; and so too the Tur above: it is forbidden to mention to him the name of a measure etc.; and as Rava stated in the gemara; and so it appears from his concluding to say "behold" etc., and not so regarding a measure:

(ד) And likewise regarding the sum of money. Beer HaGolah — they explain "sum of purchase" as is stated regarding sum of a number:

(ה) And likewise regarding the sum of a measure. So too Darkei Moshe, and he learned from the words of Beer HaGolah that the same applies to sum of a measure:

(ו) And even though. As is stated, one may not fix [a price] etc., as above:
