4) Sellers of pickled vegetables called turshi in Arabic customarily refuse to give any to a person who comes to take turshi on Shabbos unless he leaves collateral, such as a cord or scarf. The seller ties upon it one knot for each portion taken, not two knots upon one another, but each knot separately in its own place, so that the next day he can collect for as many portions as there are knots in the collateral. It appears that this involves no prohibition. There is no prohibition in taking collateral, as Rema writes above in siman 307, seif 11. The knots are nonpermanent and not craftsman's knots, so they may initially be tied, as stated above in siman 317, seif 1. Pischei HaDevir, paragraph 2; see there.
