It is permitted to say to the shopkeeper, "Give me four eggs and five pomegranates," provided he does not mention to him the name of a sum of money, nor a sum of measure, nor a sum of count, to say, "Here, you have fifty nuts in your hand — give me fifty others, and here you have a hundred":And similarly with a sum of money — it is forbidden only in such a case where he says, "Give me for such-and-such a sum of money, and you will have such-and-such in your hand"; but otherwise permitted; and similarly a sum of measure — forbidden specifically in such a case, but otherwise permitted; and even though it is forbidden to mention money at all, that is regarding a matter whose purchase price is not known; but regarding a matter that is known, only that he says to him how much he needs — permitted, if he does not mention to him a sum [Or Zarua and Hagahot Ashiri chapter Ein Tzedin]; and even though some are stringent in this matter, already the custom has spread in these lands to be lenient, in accordance with the reasoning mentioned:
