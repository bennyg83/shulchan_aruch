It is permitted to say to a shopkeeper, "Give me 4 eggs and 5 pomegranates," provided that he does not mention a price, a total measure, or a total count, such as saying, "You already owe me 50 nuts; give me another 50, so you will owe me 100." {Rama: Likewise, regarding a total price, the prohibition applies specifically when he says, "Give me merchandise for such and such an amount of money, so that you will owe me such and such an amount," but otherwise it is permitted. The same applies to a total measure: it is forbidden specifically in such a case, but otherwise it is permitted. Although it is generally forbidden to mention prices at all, that is regarding an item whose price is not fixed. Regarding an item whose price is known, if he merely tells the shopkeeper how much he needs, it is permitted, provided that he does not mention the total amount. [Or Zarua and Hagahot Ashiri, chapter Ein Tzedin] Although some are stringent regarding this matter, the custom has spread in these lands to be lenient in accordance with the view stated above.}
