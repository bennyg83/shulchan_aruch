(א) To the shopkeeper give me, etc. — this is that of seif 3; and it is possible it mentioned shopkeeper for emphasis even though his intent is certainly sale — nevertheless permitted since he did not mention explicitly; and so it appears in Levush and in the gemara it states a shopkeeper accustomed with him, and Rashi explained because he is accustomed with him he trusts him without fixing prices:

(ב) And likewise with sum of money, etc. — the language is not so precise and the intent is that what is written in the Mechaber's words there "money" means specifically sum of money, but money generally is permitted:
