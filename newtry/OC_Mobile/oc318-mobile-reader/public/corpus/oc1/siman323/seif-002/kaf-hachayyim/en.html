2) There: Even if it is designated for measuring. This means that it is ordinarily used to measure merchandise for sale. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1. A government standard measure or the like, however, which is not ordinarily used to measure merchandise for sale except when a barrel of liquor or similar item is purchased, or when someone specifically requests that it be used because it is an accurate measure, may not be requested when other measuring utensils are available. Saying specifically, 'Fill this utensil,' then effectively mentions a fixed quantity and appears to be a stratagem. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1.
