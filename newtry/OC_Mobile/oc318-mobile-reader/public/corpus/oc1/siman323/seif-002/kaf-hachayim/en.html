(2) There. Even if it is designated for a measure — meaning they regularly measure to sell with it. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Eliyah Rabbah letter 1; Turei Zahav letter 1; nevertheless the king's measure and the like that they do not regularly measure to sell with it except by happenstance that one buys, etc., or when one comes and says he should measure with it specifically because it is a true measure, etc., and he has other measuring vessels — forbidden to say "fill this vessel for me" specifically, for it mentions an amount of measure and appears like a trick.
