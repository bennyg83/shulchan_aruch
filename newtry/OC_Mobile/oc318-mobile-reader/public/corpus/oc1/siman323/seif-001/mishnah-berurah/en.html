(א) Permitted, etc. — meaning even though commerce and sale are forbidden on Shabbat and Yom Tov, and likewise one who sells by word or by delivery and pulling, and likewise measuring that is not for a mitzvah is also forbidden, as explained in siman 306; nevertheless permitted to say in this language that he does not say in the language of sale "sell to me," and he does not mention the name of a measure — only this vessel stam:

(ב) Dedicated to measure — meaning he is accustomed to measure and sell with it:

(ג) And he carries it to his house — for this is not the way of commerce and sale, and also does not appear like weekday activity, for even one who gives drink to his fellow as a gift sometimes also gives to drink in a vessel dedicated to measure:

(ד) But to measure, etc. — because this is the way of weekday commerce and sale:

(ה) And some are lenient, etc. — meaning through this there is no prohibition on the seller to measure in his measure-vessel and pour into the buyer's vessels:

(ו) That he diminishes, etc. — and there is no concern of theft, for it is known to the owner that everyone is accustomed to this; and so they were accustomed to cast a ring in the measure — and this is the permit for the seller [Magen Avraham]:
