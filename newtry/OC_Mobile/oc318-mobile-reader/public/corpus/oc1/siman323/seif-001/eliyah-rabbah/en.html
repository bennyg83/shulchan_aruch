[1] With a designated measuring utensil, etc. Meaning a utensil used for measuring and selling.
[2] [Levush] But I have not seen, etc. Hagahot Ashiri writes that the custom is to place a ring or another non-muktzeh item into the measuring vessel.
[3] [Levush] The name of a measure, etc. Yam Shel Shlomo, Shelah, and Taz warn that the public should be instructed about this. I say there are several grounds for leniency, as in seif 7. A God fearing person should be stringent, but the public has authorities on whom to rely. One should not say, "Sell me," however (Shiltei Gibborim).
