The laws of borrowing and acquiring Shabbos needs, rinsing utensils, repairing them, and immersing them on Shabbos. It contains 10 seifim: One may say to another person, 'Fill this vessel for me,' even if the vessel is specifically designated as a measure. This applies when the purchaser takes the seller's measuring vessel and carries it home, and certainly when the purchaser brings a vessel from his own home and says, 'Fill this vessel for me.' But it is forbidden to measure with a vessel specifically designated for measuring and then pour the contents into the purchaser's vessels. {Rama: Some are lenient whenever he does not intend an exact measure, but reduces or adds a little. [Hagahot Ashiri and Mordechai, chapter Ein Tzedin] The widespread custom therefore is to measure with a vessel designated for measuring and pour it into the purchaser's vessels. The reason for those who are lenient will be explained immediately below. The law of a Shabbos loan is the same as on Yom Tov; see above, siman 525, and above, siman 307, seif 11.}
