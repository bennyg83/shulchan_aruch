1) [Seif 1] One may say to another person, 'Fill this utensil for me,' etc. One may specifically say, 'Fill this utensil,' but all opinions forbid mentioning a unit of measure. The author writes the same shortly below in seif 4. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1. One may, however, say, 'Fill this utensil for me with a particular beverage and I will pay you after Shabbos.' Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1. See above, siman 307, seif 11.
