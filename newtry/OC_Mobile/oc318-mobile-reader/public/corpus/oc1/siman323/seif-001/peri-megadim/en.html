(ב) And know: every measuring vessel — if it is fit to receive and he is not particular about them, permitted to move it from sun to shade; and if they do not receive, such as ammah measures and the like — muktzeh on account of melacha; and the measure of wet goods — it appears to me and the like, if he is particular about them — it is muktzeh on account of monetary loss. And a nursing bottle designated for measure, as is now customary that they call hyber and hold a quart — permitted to say "fill this vessel for me" and pour into the buyer's vessel, for it is impossible to take the nursing bottle to bring to the buyer's house and it does not appear like mentioning explicitly the sum of the measure:
