Behold we learned in the mishnah above: a person may say to his fellow, "Fill this vessel for me," but he may not mention to him the name of a measure — there it speaks where his measure is not known, and here his measure is known, and therefore Rashba argues; and as Magen Avraham brought this distinction in the name of Darkei Moshe; and one can ask: why do Tosafos need to emend in the Tosefta "sum of count" — let them emend the reading of Rashi "sum of measure," and specifically a sum of measure is forbidden, but the name of a measure, since its purchase is known, it is permitted to mention; rather we learn that Rabbeinu Asher does not distinguish thus, but regarding the name of money and not regarding the name of measure; with all this it is fit to be stringent not to mention on Shabbat and Yom Tov the name of a measure, but only "fill this vessel for me"; but that which Taz challenged here — that Tosafos should have left the reading of Rashi "measure" and according to Rabbeinu Asher's reasoning — there is no challenge at all, for according to his view, as per the emendation of Mahadura Batra, one can say that Tosafos do not hold this reasoning of Rabbeinu Asher even regarding the name of money; but one who holds this reasoning can also say regarding measure; but according to Maharsha, Tosafos hold the reasoning of Rabbeinu Asher is proven — therefore it is well explained that Tosafos do not hold this law of Rama, and examine:
