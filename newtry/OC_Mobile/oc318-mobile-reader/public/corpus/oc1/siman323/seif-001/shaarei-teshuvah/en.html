(Shulchan Aruch seif 7.) Permitted to immerse a new utensil etc., and see in Sha'agat Aryeh simanim 56 and 57, who extended on this and concluded that on Shabbat and Yom Tov it is forbidden; but during bein hashmashos of erev Shabbat and Yom Tov one does not immerse metal utensils that require immersion d'oraisa; but glass utensils, which are rabbinic — permitted; but during bein hashmashos of Shabbat and Yom Tov themselves, even glass utensils — according to him one may not immerse them, because this immersion is not for the need of that day but for a weekday need, and it is forbidden; and see in responsa Beit Ephraim what he wrote on this law regarding Or Zarua, Maharsha, and Shach, who challenged the Rambam on this:
