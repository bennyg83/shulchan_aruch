(s.k. 1) Dedicated to measure, meaning he is accustomed, etc. — do not think "dedicated to measure." Meaning that he has not yet measured with it, but it was prepared and designated that if the vessel he now measures with breaks, he will take this one he designated in its place to measure; and the Talmud called this "standing for measure." And one might think if he already measured with it — forbidden; he teaches there is not — rather "dedicated to measure" means when he already measures with it and nevertheless it is permitted; and the Talmud called this "dedicated to measure":
