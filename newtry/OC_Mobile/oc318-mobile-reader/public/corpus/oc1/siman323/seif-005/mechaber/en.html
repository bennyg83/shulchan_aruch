One who brings wine vessels from place to place may not bring them in a basket and box in the manner of weekday, but rather he brings them before him or on his shoulder and the like, that he changes from the weekday manner; even if there is no lightness in the burden of carrying; and if it is impossible for him to change — such as when he invited many guests and needs to hurry to bring before them — permitted; and some say that to diminish in walking is preferable, and it is better to bring them in a basket and box once than to bring each one alone before himself:
