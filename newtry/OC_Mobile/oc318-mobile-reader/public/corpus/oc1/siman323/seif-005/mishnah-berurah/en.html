(א) He shall not bring them — to give three and four barrels within a box to carry them, on account that it appears like weekday activity to carry loads:

(ב) Before him — in his hand:

(ג) Or on his shoulder — one or two, for it is evident it is for Shabbat need:

(ד) And the like that he changes, etc. — in Tur siman 501 it states thus: and likewise every change from weekday way, etc.; and this wording is more corrected:

(ה) And some say to diminish, etc. — see siman 501 seif 8 in the gloss Rama ruled like these "some say"; and all this in a place where they are not seen; but when carrying them through a mixed alley where many pass and the matter is very public and they will say for weekday need he brings them — for everyone to diminish in load is preferable:
