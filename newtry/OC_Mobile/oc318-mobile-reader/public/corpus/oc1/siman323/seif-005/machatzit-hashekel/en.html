(א) (s.k. 5) That its purchase value, etc. — Darkei Moshe wrote and it appears to me likewise, etc.; and Eliyah Rabbah did not look at Darkei Moshe, for he holds mentioning the name of a measure is forbidden in every case; therefore he explained what Rama wrote "and likewise with sum of measure" — specifically in such a case it is forbidden, but otherwise permitted. Thus he explained he does not mention the name of a measure but said stam fill this vessel ten times and the like; but to mention the name of a measure is certainly forbidden, end of his words; see there:

(ב) But behold in Darkei Moshe it is explained that also regarding measure is permitted what is permitted regarding money, and there is no distinction between money and measure; however even though the truth is with Magen Avraham in explaining Rama's words that he permits also regarding measure — nevertheless Taz disagrees with Rama and holds for practical halacha that regarding measure in every case it is forbidden, as the view of Eliyah Rabbah. And the permit for the seller that he not stumble in the prohibition of measuring is explained in seif 1. Until the handles I did not find in Shulchan Gadol; but I saw also in Sefer Kehillat Yaakov he brings it in name of Shulchan Gadol:

(ג) And it is reasonable there is no distinction between until the handles and fill the vessel — both regarding the seller who needs to diminish or add a little, and likewise the buyer needs specifically a thing whose amount is known and he does not mention a sum and accounting with what he already took; if so what is the novelty of until the handles. And perhaps he teaches that even when he is precise in his speech until the handles (and they are made for measure) it appears more that his intent is toward measure — nevertheless it is permitted:
