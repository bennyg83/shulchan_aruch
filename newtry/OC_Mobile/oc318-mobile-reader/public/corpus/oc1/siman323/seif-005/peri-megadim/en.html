(ב) And with this one may say that which Rambam wrote in chapter 4 of Yom Tov the reason of Rav Bibi alone; and on Shabbat the reason of Rava, for he holds that immersion of a new vessel is forbidden lest he defer or repair; and we said in chapter 17 from Magen Avraham two reasons, such as kiddushin of silver; and nevertheless d'oraisa in metals see Maggid Mishneh there, or stringent that it is not fit for anything as Maggid Mishneh wrote; and therefore on Yom Tov there is no distinction between the reason of Rav Bibi and Rava — he wrote the reason of Rav Bibi in its place and it is more fitting to forbid a new vessel from Rava's reason that one may err if one says a new vessel is rabbinic, or glass in any case; and there is reasoning here and there he wrote the reason of Rav Bibi that it is forbidden even rabbinically lest he defer and it is common even rabbinically; and the offspring of impurity is not common in a vessel; nevertheless Maggid Mishneh did not write so there, only rabbinically for Rav Bibi; nevertheless he wrote the reason of Rav Bibi that there is no distinction between Rava's reason and Rav Bibi's reason; unlike on Shabbat there is a distinction, for carrying on Yom Tov itself is permitted for all; and on Shabbat if it became impure on Shabbat — according to Rav Bibi nowadays lest he defer in such a case; and according to Rava it is repair, for on Yom Tov ochel nefesh and its preparatory acts that cannot be done from erev Yom Tov are permitted on Yom Tov, not on Shabbat. And so it appears from what Maggid Mishneh wrote according to Rambam in chapter 5 of Magen Avraham that immersion of vessels is rabbinic — derive for him lest he defer even d'oraisa — learn that lest he defer applies also to a new vessel; and so is written in chapter 23 of Shabbos; and know that immersion of new vessels is forbidden on Shabbat that it appears like repair even though on Yom Tov it is doubtful — nevertheless from what is written here "repair" — learn that thus it is; and even if it became impure on Shabbat as said here.
