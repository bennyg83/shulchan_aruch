[5] [Levush] In the measure of wet goods, etc. Rama did not mention salt and dry goods; and in truth now also in liquids their measure and sale-price are known, and Rambam wrote likewise, and on this the world relied to be lenient; and Taz expanded to wonder from the gemara that one should not mention the name of a measure — implying that as soon as one mentions the name of a measure it is forbidden; and it appears to me there is no difficulty, for one may say it deals when one's measure and sale-price are not known; also other proofs can be pushed aside; and nevertheless one who fears Heaven should be stringent as above; and see siman 504 s.k. 31:
