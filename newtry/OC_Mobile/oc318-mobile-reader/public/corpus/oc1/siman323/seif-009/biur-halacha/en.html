Permitted to scrub vessels with every thing — see in Mishna Berurah that it is permitted with natron and salt, and all the more permitted to scrub with mortar; see in Tosefta chapter 4 of Beitzah and it is brought in Beit Yosef that mortar is lighter than natron and sand; and since we rule that one scrubs vessels even with natron and sand — all the more with mortar.
