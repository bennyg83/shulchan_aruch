The custom is likewise to place a ring inside the measuring vessel. Mordechai; Yam Shel Shlomo; Darkei Moshe. This permission applies to the seller. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 3; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1. The statement that this permission applies to the seller means that when he does not measure precisely, he does not transgress by measuring on Shabbos or Yom Tov.
