[1] [Levush] And they wish to bless, etc. — language of Raavan siman 183: three who ate and one of them asked to leave — one interrupts the meal if the two already finished and invited on behalf of that one who must leave, until here. It appears to me that what he wrote "and one of them asked" means one of the two who finished asked to leave — even though the second does not wish to leave; nevertheless, since he finished, the third who did not finish must interrupt — not as Levush implies.
