[Levush] And they wish to recite Birkat HaMazon, etc. Raavan writes in siman 183: If three ate together and one of them wishes to leave, one interrupts his meal, if the other two have already finished, and they recite zimun for the sake of the one who must leave. It appears to me that by "one of them wishes to leave" he means that one of the two who finished wishes to leave. Even if the second does not wish to leave, since he has finished, the third, who has not finished, must interrupt, unlike what appears from the Levush.
[2] And he answers with them, etc. Even one eating alone who is not reciting Bircas HaMazon with them answers, “Blessed is He of Whose food we have eaten.” Derashos Maharash. Sefer Chassidim, siman 900, records that one person was eating at a table where three others had eaten. They said to him, “Eat with us and hear Bircas HaMazon.” He replied, “You speak idle matters, while I eat quickly and immediately engage in Torah study.”
