If they did not want, etc. — so Rashba; and afterward Rashba wrote on this, but Rav Hai Gaon wrote that the one [who must leave] may bless for himself without zimmun and fulfill, and they finish their meal, and this requires further study, end of Rashba's words; and it appears that even Rav Hai Gaon said only when the matter is necessary for him to leave and he cannot delay; but where it is possible, he must wait for them until the two invite him; and he must interrupt until "Who feeds all," since the invitation is not a blessing containing God's name and kingship — therefore he interrupts his eating until "Who feeds all," so he interrupts eating for one blessing; and therefore the three who interrupted for one need not interrupt except until the blesser said "Blessed are our God Who we have eaten from His bounty," since they already heard mention of the Name and fulfilled their obligation.
