He interrupts. And if he did not interrupt, he does not fulfill zimmun.

He is not permitted. Bach ruled like Rav Hai: he is permitted to leave, for the obligation of zimmun does not apply until they finish their meal; and what is written in siman 193 seif 2 that they may not divide refers when they finished their meal — here they merely turned their faces this way and that, and so is the practice, end of his words; and so in Rashi on "flew" zimmun who wrote the leavers needed to go to the market before the groups finished their meal, end of his words.
