1) [Seif 1] Three who ate together: one interrupts, etc. If he did not interrupt, he does not fulfill his obligation of Zimun. Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1.
2) There: He responds with them, etc. Even one who eats alone and does not recite the blessing with them responds, 'Blessed is the One from Whose food we have eaten.' Derashos Maharshal; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2.
