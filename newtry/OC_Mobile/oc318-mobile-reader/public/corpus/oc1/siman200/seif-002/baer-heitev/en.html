Bread. And if he did not intend to eat bread, only parperet (fruit), he need not return except to the place he stopped — Mishna Medura, Bach. And if he ate parperet, he must bless on them the final blessing, since he did not say "Who feeds" in Birkat Hamazon; nevertheless he should be careful l'chatchila not to talk in between — in Shulchan Arukh. Magen Avraham.
