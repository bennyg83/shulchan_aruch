He only needs to interrupt until they say: "Blessed is He from Whose bounty we have eaten," (baroukh cheakhalnou mitouvo) etc. Rema: And some say that he must remain until they say: "Who nourishes all," (hazon et hakol) and this is the common custom (Rosh, Tosafot, R’ Yitzḥak in the name of the Baal HaHalakhot Gedolot, and the Tur). And if his intention was to return and eat bread, even if in the end he did not eat again — when he wishes to recite Birkat Hamazon, he begins from the blessing HaZan ("Who nourishes"), and all the more so if he actually did return and eat (R’ Yitzḥak, chapter "Three Who Ate") (Tur), and he may resume and finish his meal without repeating the opening blessing.': ) וחוזר וגומר סעודתו בלא ברכה בתחילה:
