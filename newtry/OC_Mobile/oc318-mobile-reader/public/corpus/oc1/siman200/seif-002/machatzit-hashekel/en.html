Or there is full permission.

And what is written in siman 193 seif 4 as should be read: even if they only blessed "Who brings forth" and have not yet eaten an olive's worth, they are not permitted to separate. And so appears from Rashi on "flew," etc., on 50a — for R. Huna said: three who came from three groups may not separate (and this is brought in siman 193 seif 5) — Rava said: we said only when these remaining ones preceded — those three separated from them and invited over them (Rashi explained: e.g. if there were four in each group and enough remained in each for zimmun, and these leavers needed to leave before the companions finished their meal); but inviting over them in their place — zimmun flew from them (Rashi: e.g. they joined them for zimmun as said above "they call him and invite over him and the group blessed") and there were not in each group only three with those who separated (and if not, there was only enough to bless and he blessed, etc., end of his words). And since Rashi wrote on "we did not say," etc., that there were four in each group, etc., and the leavers needed to leave before they finished, etc. — why did Rashi need to inform us the reason for their leaving, and also that their leaving was before finishing the meal? Therefore he holds like Rav Hai Gaon, and Rashi wanted to explain that their leaving was permitted; therefore he wrote they left before finishing the meal, which is permitted per Rav Hai Gaon. But Taz disagrees: one may not leave even before finishing unless the third did not establish himself at the start of eating with the two, as written above in siman 193; and now he leaves before finishing, see there.
