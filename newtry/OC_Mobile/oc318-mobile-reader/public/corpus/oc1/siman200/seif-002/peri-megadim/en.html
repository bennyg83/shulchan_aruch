Until — Taz: perhaps his intent is what Tur wrote, Rashi's explanation Berachot 45b: he interrupts for two until "Who feeds," which is the zimmun blessing per R. Shimon 46a; and behold Tur rules like his father Rosh that "Who feeds" is not a zimmun blessing and the individual says it, see there; and Taz corrected this — Rashi 46a explained thus for Rav Nachman: until where is it called zimmun blessing — the individual does not say it, then "let us bless"; but R. Shimon holds it is called zimmun and even though the individual says "Who feeds," nevertheless one must wait until they finish "Who feeds," since he does not mention the Name there; nevertheless it is evident he interrupted because of a blessing (unlike ten who mention the Name — even per R. Shimon until "let us bless" suffices, as we need to say below in Taz and Magen Avraham, note 5). And if so, what Tur wrote that it is a zimmun blessing is not literal — for the individual says it — rather one must wait; and so is Rashi's intent per Tosafot, see Darkei Moshe and Bach and other later authorities.
