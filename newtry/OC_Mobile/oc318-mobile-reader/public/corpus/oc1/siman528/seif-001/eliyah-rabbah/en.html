One need not do so on every Yom Tov, etc. Nevertheless, one who establishes an eruv on Erev Pesach should include Yom Tov as well and say, “for all the Shabbosos and Yamim Tovim of this year.” One who is able to establish it every Erev Shabbos should say, “for this Shabbos and for the coming Yom Tov, which should come upon us for good.” Maharshal. If he established an eruv chatzeiros, he may rely upon it for Shabbos as well.
