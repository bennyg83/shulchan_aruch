Yom Tov does not require eruvei chatzeiros, etc. For all of these were instituted as a fence for [the prohibition of] carrying out; and on Yom Tov we hold that since carrying out was permitted for the sake of food preparation (ochel nefesh), it was also permitted when not for [that] need, as above in siman 518. And from the plain wording of his language it implies that they did not institute eruvei chatzeiros for Yom Tov at all; and so he ruled plainly above in siman 416, se'if 5. However, from the words of the Rama above in siman 518, se'if 1, in the gloss, it is proven that he holds that things that have no need at all, it is forbidden to carry out from the house to the courtyard without eruvei chatzeiros. And therefore the Acharonim wrote that when one makes eruvei chatzeiros on Erev Pesach for the Shabbosos of the year, one should also include Yom Tov [so that thereby one can carry out even things that are not needed for the day at all], and one should say "for all the Shabbosos and Yom Tov of this year"; and one who places [an eruv] every Erev Shabbos, and Yom Tov happens to fall adjacent to it, should say "for this Shabbos and the Yom Tov that is coming upon us for good" [Acharonim].
