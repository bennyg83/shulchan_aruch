1) [Se'if 1] Yom Tov does not require an eruv chatzeros, etc. This is because we rule that since carrying was permitted for food preparation, it was also permitted when not needed for food preparation, as stated above at the beginning of siman 518. The unqualified wording of Shulchan Aruch implies that eruv chatzeros was not instituted at all for Yom Tov, and Shulchan Aruch likewise rules without qualification above, siman 416, se'if 5. Rama's words above, siman 518, se'if 1, in the gloss, however, demonstrate that he holds that an object for which there is no need at all may not be carried without an eruv chatzeros. The later authorities therefore write that when one prepares an eruv chatzeros on erev Pesach for all the Shabbosos of the year, he should include Yom Tov as well. Likewise, one who prepares it every Friday, when Yom Tov occurs adjacent to that Shabbos, should say, 'for this Shabbos and the approaching Yom Tov,' in order to satisfy all opinions. This is explained in our words above, siman 518, subsection 14. See there.
