(1) [seif 1] Yom Tov does not require eruv chatzerot, etc., because we hold that since carrying for ochel nefesh was permitted, it was also permitted not for that need; and as above siman 518. And it implies from the Mechaber's wording that they did not establish eruv chatzerot for Yom Tov at all; and so anonymously above siman 496 seif 5. However from Morenu Rama z"l above siman 518 seif 1 in the hagahah it is evident he holds that matters with no need at all are forbidden to carry without eruv chatzerot; therefore Acharonim wrote that when one makes eruv chatzerot on erev Pesach for the year's Shabbosot it includes Yom Tov too; and one who places it every erev Shabbat and Yom Tov occurs near it should say "this Shabbat and the upcoming Yom Tov" to exit per all opinions, as explained in our words above siman 518 letter 14, see there:
