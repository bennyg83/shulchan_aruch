If a holiday falls on Friday, we do not make either yard or techum eruvs . However, if one realized on the first day of a two day holiday in the Diaspora [that he didn't make an eruv], he can make an yard eruv on condition [see previous chapter paragraph 22]. However, we do not make techum eruvs on a holiday (under any circumstances). {Rama: (even if one made an eruv tavshilin) (Ra"n chapter 2 of Beitzah)}
