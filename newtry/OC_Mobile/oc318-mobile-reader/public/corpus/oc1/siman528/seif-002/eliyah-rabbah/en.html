[Levush:] For an eruv of techumin acquires, etc. It appears to me that this is [only] l'chatchilah; but b'dieved, when one has already made an eruv, it is effective on the two days of Yom Tov of the Diaspora, for if the second day is a weekday, the acquisition was not complete. And see siman 416, s.k. 6; and it appears to me that there [the restriction] applies specifically to Yom Tov after Shabbos or vice versa, but on the two days of Yom Tov of the Diaspora it is permitted b'dieved. And so it appears to me that one may carefully infer from Sefer Avodas HaKodesh, folio 34; and furthermore, in Ba'al Halachos Gedolos, folio 35, he ruled that one places [eruvei] techumin just as [one places] an eruv tavshilin. And even though from all the other poskim this does not appear [to be so], nevertheless in this matter we rely upon him; and see siman 339, se'if 4.
