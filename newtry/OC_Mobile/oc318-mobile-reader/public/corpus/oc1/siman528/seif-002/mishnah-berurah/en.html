One does not make an eruv, etc. For it appears as if one is preparing for the needs of tomorrow. And if one placed eruvei chatzeiros on Erev Yom Tov for the sake of Yom Tov, and the eruv is still extant, one may rely upon it also for Shabbos.
Even, etc., an eruv tavshilin. For an eruv tavshilin permits only preparing the needs of the meal for the sake of tomorrow.
Of the Diaspora. To exclude the two days of Rosh Hashanah, which are considered like one long day.
On condition. Namely, that one should say: "If today is a weekday, I am placing it for the sake of eruvei chatzeiros for Shabbos; and if today is sacred, I say nothing"; and on the morrow one should say: "If today is sacred, then I made an eruv yesterday; and if today is a weekday, I am placing it for the sake of eruvei chatzeiros." And see in siman 393 in the Mishnah Berurah.
But eruvei techumin one does not place, etc. For acquisition of shevisah is like acquisition of a house, and one does not acquire a house on Yom Tov even on condition. And the Eliyah Rabbah wrote that this is specifically l'chatchilah; but b'dieved, when one has already made an eruv, it is effective on the two days of Yom Tov of the Diaspora; and the Peri Megadim also cited this above in siman 416; and see there in the Biur Halacha, s.v. "because."
