2) If one placed an eruv chatzeros for Yom Tov, he may rely upon it for Shabbos as well. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 2. Likewise, if one placed an eruv techumin for Yom Tov, he may rely upon it for Shabbos as well if he intended this, and certainly if he stated it explicitly. Chemed Moshe, subsection 2. See above, siman 416, se'if 2, and our words there, with Heaven's help.
