Even those who fast on all of them except one day, so that the practice should not take effect upon them as a vow, require neither acceptance nor completion. Terumas HaDeshen, siman 146. Accordingly, those who always fast on the first day of Selichos, as written in our words below in siman 581, seif 2, require neither acceptance nor completion, since it stands accepted by force of their custom. Nevertheless, the common practice is to complete it, and perhaps it is forbidden to release them from this practice, as written in Yoreh De'ah, siman 214. It is also possible that the practice spread due to an error, because people saw that the Shulchan Aruch, in the Rama's gloss, wrote that one who does not fast all of the Ten Days, etc., requires acceptance and completion. They erred in this, for it refers to one who is not accustomed to fast every year. Such a person must accept the fast and must therefore complete it. One who fasts every year, however, even for only one day, requires neither acceptance nor completion, and certainly this is so for one who stipulates at the time of acceptance that he need not complete it. Magen Avraham, s.k. 4. Eliyah Rabbah, siman 581, os 20.
