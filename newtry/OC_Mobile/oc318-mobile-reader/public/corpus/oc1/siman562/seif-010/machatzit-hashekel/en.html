(s.k. 10) To conduct himself, etc. As is written in se'if 1. Meaning, like in se'if 1 regarding completion: since there is the view of the Rambam that even if he did not complete today it is called a fast, even though most poskim disagree, nevertheless regarding an individual's fast we rely on him, since an individual does not fix a blessing for Aneinu but includes it in Shomea Tefillah. The same applies here regarding acceptance from yesterday: since there is the view of R' Yehudah that the Mordechai brought, who holds that what we say in the Gemara that he must accept it from yesterday is specifically ideally, but after the fact even if they did not accept it from yesterday it is called a fast: regarding an individual's fast we rely on him, as above.
That even if they never accepted, etc. For in any case it is a fast of hours; so wrote Darkhei Moshe: according to the Rambam, even if he ate until midday and afterward reconsidered to fast, it is called a fast of hours; therefore in any case regarding an individual's fast we rely on him, as above; all the more so here, for in any case they accepted, only that the acceptance was not while it was still day.
