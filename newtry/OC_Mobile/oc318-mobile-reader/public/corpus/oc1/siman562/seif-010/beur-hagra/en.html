Se'if 10: How, etc., for behold, etc. The language of the Rambam; and so Rashi explained.
And some say that likewise this, etc. So wrote the Rosh and Tosafos in Avodah Zarah, folio 34a, s.v. "They fast," etc.; and one must say that it deals, etc.; and it is simple that according to the first rationale, specifically when he is obligated for a full day he must accept before the night, for the night too is included in the fast even though he eats and drinks, as the Ran wrote; which is not so when he is not so obligated: whenever he accepted beforehand it is a fast; and if so, why did they say there regarding one who lodged in a fast, "and this case is different," etc.?
For example, etc., or, etc. In the Rosh there are two interpretations of "they fast for hours," and both require acceptance while it is still day.
