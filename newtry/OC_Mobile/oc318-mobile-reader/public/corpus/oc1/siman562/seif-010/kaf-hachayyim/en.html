10) [Seif 2] Some say that one who is accustomed, etc. This was written in the name of "some say" because according to this opinion, although he does not complete his fast, he recites Aneinu, as written in the Beit Yosef, Bach, and Darkei Moshe, os 6, and in the Rama's gloss in this seif. According, however, to those who explain that any fast upon which the sun did not set is not a fast for the purpose of reciting Aneinu, as the Shulchan Aruch ruled above in seif 1, the same applies here.
