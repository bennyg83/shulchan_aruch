See the gloss of the Hagahos Maimoniyos; and Magen Avraham wrote that it implies specifically after Ma'ariv, which is after eleven hours less a quarter, for that is called completion after the next day has entered; however, it appears to me that whenever he prayed Minchah at the aforementioned time it suffices, since it is the beginning of shkiyah and is called completion of a fast; see there.
To complete. The reason is that they fast for the past, for the churban, and we do not say he intends only to afflict himself; which is not so during the Ten Days of Repentance, when they fast for the future, that their decree be torn up, [and] we say that they accepted only to afflict themselves (Terumat HaDeshen). And this is the reason for those who fast the forty days before Yom Kippur: that they need not complete [the fast], for they likewise fast because of teshuvah.
He stipulated. Meaning, at the time of accepting the fast; and in the Minhagim he wrote that one who is stringent to complete [the fast] during the Ten Days of Repentance, may a blessing come upon him.
