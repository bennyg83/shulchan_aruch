Ibid. The words ‘the fast of our fasting’ are correctly omitted. He may recite the entire remaining text of Aneinu, since it does not speak of a fast at all and is not an interruption, for we rule that a person may request all his needs in Shomea Tefillah. By contrast, if he mentions a fast when it is not truly considered a fast, he speaks falsely in his prayer, and it consequently also constitutes an interruption in the prayer.
