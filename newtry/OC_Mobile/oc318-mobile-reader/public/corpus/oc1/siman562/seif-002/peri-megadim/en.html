That they do not. See Taz. His view appears to be that nowadays, even those who fast only two or three days during the Aseres Yemei Teshuvah should not accept those fasts at the preceding Minchah, because this has always been the practice. Therefore, a communal fast and its completion likewise apply in such a case, for once this is the established practice, it is as though they fast throughout the Aseres Yemei Teshuvah. His words, ‘as though he expressly stated that he does not wish to complete it,’ arise from the question he later asks from seif 12: if these fasts are already accepted and standing, they should require completion. Rather, the Tur’s expression that ‘it is not customary’ means that they intentionally refrain from accepting them and intend only discomfort. If so, there is no distinction between fasting all ten days of repentance and fasting only two or three days. Terumas HaDeshen, siman 146, however, explains that both reasons are required: they do not formally accept the fast, and their purpose is merely discomfort.
