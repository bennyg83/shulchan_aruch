(17) Who accepted upon himself a plain fast, etc.: that is, he did not spell out at the time of acceptance his intent: we say that presumably his intent was like on other weekdays, whose law is until tzeit hakochavim so long as he did not spell out.
(18) If he did not, etc., until the congregation finish their prayer: and the same helps when he stipulates that he will fast only until the time of Minchah Gedolah: it is called a fast and he may pray Aneinu.
