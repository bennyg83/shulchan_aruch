(7) And so is the opinion of some rabbanan — their reason is that they hold that what the Gemara said: any fast over which the sun did not set is not a fast — and this is also for Anenu: only if he accepted upon himself to fast a full day and did not complete, it is not a fast and he does not pray Anenu; but if he accepted only until mincha, which is pain in general, it is a fast for this matter that he prays Anenu within his prayer even though he does not complete. However, most poskim disagree on this; and therefore Rema ruled not to rely on this except for an individual, that he should say it in Shemoneh Esreh and conclude "for You hear" etc., and not say "Blessed are You Who answers in time of distress"; and see Taz, who supports that even in this he should skip the words "fast our fast" so he should not appear as speaking falsely, since it is not a fast; and in Sefer Bigdei Yeshu'a he rules he need not skip, since he fasts until after chatzot, for then his eating is like throwing a stone at a wall — it does not appear when he will call — see there; and so Machatzit haShekel supports — see there.
