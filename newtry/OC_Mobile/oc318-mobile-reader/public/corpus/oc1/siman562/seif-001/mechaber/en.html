The law of accepting a fast, containing 13 seifim. Any fast upon which the sun did not set, meaning that he did not complete it until tzeit hakochavim, is not considered a fast. Tzeit hakochavim here means that 3 medium-sized stars are visible, or that the moon shines strongly enough to illuminate the earth. If he intends to eat before then, he does not recite Aneinu. {Rama: Nevertheless, some have the custom to recite Aneinu even when they do not complete the fast until tzeit hakochavim, in accordance with some authorities. [Terumat HaDeshen, siman 157] It appears to me that this applies specifically to an individual, who says Aneinu in Shomea Tefillah, since he may in any event add personal supplications there, as explained above in siman 119. A shaliach tzibbur, however, should not recite Aneinu unless the fast is completed, and this is the custom.}
