Taz, seif katan 1. A partial-day fast, etc. According to him, all the more so where he has not yet eaten but will eat before nightfall, this is called a partial-day fast. According to our Gemara, however, which states, ‘provided that he did not eat that entire day,’ a partial-day fast is called a fast only if he does not eat the entire day. For example, he was occupied until midday and did not eat, and afterward decided to fast until evening, as explained in seif 10. This is called a partial-day fast. Although the first half of the day was not observed for the sake of a fast, since he did not eat, that half-day is counted as a partial-day fast. But if he ate, whether at the beginning of the day or at its end before nightfall, it is not called even a partial-day fast for purposes of reciting Aneinu, as explained in seif 11.
