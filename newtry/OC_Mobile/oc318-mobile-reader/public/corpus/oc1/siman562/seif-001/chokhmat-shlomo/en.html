Seif 1. Any condition that was not fulfilled upon him is invalid, etc. Prophets — see in the Gemara s.k. 1 as the Gemara says, etc. And see to explain in its glosses the law of a bill of divorce, siman 143, from what is written there that this judgment is agreed, and it will be explained in the Gemara as a sign; and see above in the Gemara. And in Hagahot Taz it is explained that one may annul through a messenger; and in Hagahot Taz on the side of the Gemara it is explained that if he was in a congregation or in mourning, etc. — and Rashi and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch hold one may annul through a messenger. And I questioned what Perishah wrote in the name of Rashi that it is forbidden to annul through a messenger; and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch hold thus. And again I questioned: if there is prohibition of annulment after obligation — should be forbidden even to carry after obligation, similar to roasted spit on Yom Tov after roasting as explained in laws of Yom Tov — this is certainly not so, for they wrote prohibition of carrying only on Shabbat as Rama wrote siman 588 in my opinion.
