Intermediate [people]. But [estimates of] great ones do not help; and see siman 235, s.k. 1. (Says the glossator: even though in old books it was written "small ones," that is a mistake in Magen Avraham; see siman 235.)
They complete. That is, if he spelled out at the time of acceptance that he would not complete [the fast], even if he fasted until Minchah Gedolah he prays Aneinu; but if he accepted a fast without specification, he must complete it; see se'if 2. And if he did not complete it and ate, see siman 568; and if he ate before he prayed, he does not pray Aneinu (Terumat HaDeshen; Magen Avraham). And the Taz wrote: one who does not pray Aneinu on a fast that he does not complete gains [avoidance of] a doubtful blessing according to most poskim; and even though for this there is no separate blessing for an individual, but rather they include it in Shomea Tefillah, nevertheless the wording "the fast of our affliction" is not fitting, for a plain "fast" means a full day; and one who skips these two words, "the fast of our affliction," does well. End of his words.
