Magen Avraham wrote, and this is his language: the Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote, etc., the stringency of Tisha B'Av, etc., and the same applies to other communal fasts in Eretz Yisrael, [that] if not for [that stringency] its bein hashemashot would be permitted. Meaning, bein hashemashot of entering the fast. "Its doubt is permitted": meaning, since specifically on Tisha B'Av it is severe to forbid bein hashemashot of entering the fast, but other fasts, even though they are forbidden in eating on the night before the fast day, nevertheless bein hashemashot, which is a doubt of night, is permitted. If so, the same applies at the exit of the fast day: we should permit bein hashemashot even though it is a doubt of day; for example, erev Tisha B'Av. Meaning, by the force of the law, if not for the stringency of Tisha B'Av, as above.
And it is in the Gemara of Pesachim, folio 54b.
And see siman 387, se'if 9. And in Magen Avraham there, s.k. 12 and 13, this rationale is mentioned to distinguish between entering the day and exiting the day; and it is stated there (in Pesachim 54) regarding a communal fast that they cease [eating] while it is still day: meaning, that the night before the fast is also forbidden to eat.
See siman 575. Meaning, that even the night before the fast is permitted in eating; all the more so its bein hashemashot.
And if so, an individual, etc. This refers to the words of the Hagahos Maimoniyos who wrote that it appears in the Gemara that on other fasts (even though they are forbidden at night, nevertheless) their doubt is permitted: that is, to permit bein hashemashot of entering the fast.
But if he accepted upon himself a communal fast, etc. Since on a communal fast its bein hashemashot is forbidden; except that there is no communal fast in Bavel, for the reason explained in siman 575, s.k. 10. And since the individual accepted upon himself a fast with the stringency of a communal fast, bein hashemashot too is forbidden.
And see siman 261, where the measure of bein hashemashot is explained.
Nevertheless it is good, etc. Meaning, not to eat until bein hashemashot; rather he should cease beforehand, before bein hashemashot; and see Magen Avraham, siman 261, s.k. 9; see what I wrote in siman 299 regarding one who is fasting, how he should do Havdalah, as is written.
