Se'if 7: And likewise if, etc. According to the version of Rabbeinu Chananel and the Rif: an individual who accepted upon himself two days one after the other, even if he ate and drank the whole night, on the morrow he prays the fast prayer, etc. According to the Rosh's interpretation there.
And if he accepted, etc. According to the Mordechai's interpretation in the name of R' Yehudah on the aforementioned version of Rabbeinu Chananel: that this teaches us that even though he ate, etc., he has not lost [the fast], and we do not say that he accepted consecutive [days without a break]; and that is what [is meant by] "if he did not," etc.
And then, etc. It is a scribal error, and it should read "and even so," etc.; meaning, that we do not say it is like an extended day and like one day, as the Rokeach explained: an individual who accepted upon himself two days, etc., then he prays Aneinu both days; if he lodged in his fast he does not pray Aneinu; meaning, if he fasts the night between the two days he does not pray on the second day if he prayed on the first day. But the interpretation is not like his words, as above; for even if we say it is an extended day, nevertheless he is obligated in all his prayers, as is written in the second chapter of Shabbos; and even according to the Geonim who wrote that we are concerned about a ravenously severe craving, nevertheless in such a case he does not appear a liar on the first day because of what he ate on the night after it; as the Mordechai wrote: what is written "an individual who accepted, etc., on the morrow, etc." teaches us that we do not say he lost the fast; meaning, specifically what he prays on the morrow; but on the first day, even if he eats in between, he prays even in such a case.
