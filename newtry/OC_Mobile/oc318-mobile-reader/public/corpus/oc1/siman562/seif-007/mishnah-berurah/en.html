(35) He has lost nothing: for this is not considered an interruption, because the essential obligation of the fast is only for the entire day, except where they were explicitly stringent also for the night, for example regarding Tisha B'Av or those explained below in siman 575, se'if 3.
(36) To fast by day and eat by night: the intent is not that he spelled out that he would eat at night; rather, without specification it is likewise permitted, as below.
(37) And then he prays: it is a scribal error, and it should read "and even so he prays"; meaning, that even if he fasts also at night, one should not say that it is like an extended day and he should pray Aneinu only at Minchah of the second day; rather each day is a separate fast regarding Aneinu; and even if he fasts six consecutive days he likewise prays Aneinu every day at Minchah.
