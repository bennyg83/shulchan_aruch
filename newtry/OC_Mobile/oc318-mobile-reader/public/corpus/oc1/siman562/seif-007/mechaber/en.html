One who accepted a fast at the mincha prayer, even though they eat and drink at night, has not lost anything. And if they accept upon themself to fast three or four days or more, one after the other, to fast in the days and eat in the nights, even though they eat on each of the nights, has not lost anything. For this is an acceptance for all of them, even though they didn't accept for each individually in the preceding mincha. {Rama: Rema: and if they accepted to fast two consecutive days, it's permitted to eat in the night between them, if they didn't specify explicitly the night as well (Mordechai, ch1 of Ta'anit) and then they pray each day Aneinu in mincha.}
