What he previously wrote in the name of Rosh, that it is ineffective, means that he does not fulfill his vow and it is not counted as the fast he undertook. Nevertheless, it appears that according to all opinions he must fast, because it has the status of a vow. Even if he said a long time beforehand, ‘I shall fast on such-and-such a day,’ he must fulfill his vow. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 13, who writes that it is proper to contemplate the acceptance in Shomea Tefillah and afterward, in Elokai Netzor, state verbally that he accepts an individual fast for the following day. See Ateres Zekeinim, paragraph 5. The phrase ‘an individual fast’ is used so that it not be treated as a communal fast. In any event, there is no communal fast in Bavel except Tishah B’Av. Nevertheless, initially it is proper to specify ‘an individual fast.’ Even without specification, only eating and drinking are prohibited. See Eliyah Rabbah and Magen Avraham, siman 563, and Taz there, regarding one who accepted it before Minchah and whether he must fast. With Hashem’s help, this will be explained further there.
