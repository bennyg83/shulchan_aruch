Ibid. ‘And if he ate beforehand.’ This refers to the preceding case, where at the time of accepting the fast he expressly stated that he would not complete it. The practice of reciting Aneinu applies when he prays Minchah before eating. But if he already ate and then prays, he should not recite Aneinu, because it is no longer ‘the fast of our fasting,’ and he would be speaking falsely in his prayer. Terumas HaDeshen writes this there.
