And then he prays, etc. The wording is not precise, for it is obvious that when he eats the night in between, he prays Aneinu every day, since each day is a separate fast; rather, even if he fasted also through the night, he prays Aneinu every day; for by the law of the Gemara it is proper to pray Aneinu in every prayer, except that we decree lest a ravenously severe craving seize him and he be found a liar in his prayer; therefore in every Minchah, when the day has already turned [to evening], he prays Aneinu, even if he is fasting six consecutive days. And so it is in Maharei and in Hagahos Maimoniyos, laws of Rosh Chodesh, and in the Bach.
