And then he prays Aneinu every day. I do not know how to explain the wording "and then," for whether he ate in between or did not eat, he prays Aneinu every day; and it is possible that he said it for an additional novelty: that even then, when he fasts also at night, each day is separate regarding Aneinu, as is explained in the Piskei Maharia, siman 101.
