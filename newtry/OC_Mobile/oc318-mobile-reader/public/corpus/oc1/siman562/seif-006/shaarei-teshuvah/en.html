(In Shulchan Aruch se'if 6) If he contemplated, etc. And see the Pri Chadash, that hirhur helps only at the time of Minchah, and not beforehand or afterward at night. And see Responsa Veshav HaKohen, siman 21, who discusses this matter at length and writes to resolve what the Bach asked on the Rosh, whose words on fasting contradict [each other], and what he wrote in Shevuos; and it is well explained in his conclusion that even ideally one should be concerned to utter it with his mouth, but after the fact one should rule that acceptance in the heart helps; see there. And see Eliyah Rabbah, whose view is that hirhur helps even not at the time of Minchah, and who also wrote to resolve the Bach's difficulty.
