Se'if 6: In Shomea Tefillah. So explained Rabbeinu Chananel, and so wrote Tosafos, folio 12a, s.v. "Any," etc.; and so it appears in the Gemara "during the Minchah prayer"; and as is written in the fifth chapter of Berachos and the first chapter of Avodah Zarah: a person asks for his needs in Shomea Tefillah.
Or after, etc. Rashi there and in Avodah Zarah, chapter 2, who hold that this is not similar to asking for one's needs, and it is an interruption in the prayer.
Behold I am, etc. Gemara there, folio 12b: even though we hold like Shmuel that there is no communal fast in Bavel, nevertheless if he had explicitly accepted upon himself a communal fast he would need to practice [its stringencies]; therefore it is good to specify so that one not come to err; and so wrote the aforementioned Tosafos, s.v. "Any"; and see Beis Yosef in siman 563; and see above in the Shulchan Aruch, siman 568, se'if 6.
May it be [Your] will, etc. For otherwise it is not asking for one's needs, and it is forbidden to interrupt; and even according to those who explain [to say it] after the prayer, nevertheless it is an interruption.
And if he did not, etc. The Rosh there and Tosafos in the second chapter of Avodah Zarah; and as is written in Choshen Mishpat, siman 212, and Yoreh De'ah, siman 258; and specifically regarding tzedakah, which resembles hekdesh, and likewise acceptance of a fast; but other nedarim, no; as is written in Yoreh De'ah at the beginning of siman 210; and as is written in Shevuos there: we do not learn chullin from kodashim; and at the beginning of the second chapter of Kiddushin: what of these, etc. And even regarding this many poskim disagree and hold that it is only regarding actual kodashim.
And that is provided he contemplates, etc. As is written in siman 563: that it is no better, etc.; and not like what Tosafos in Avodah Zarah wrote in the name of Rabbeinu Tam, who wrote that what is written "any fast that they did not accept," etc., means ideally; see there. And it is good, etc.: since there is a dispute among the poskim regarding its interpretation, as above (Tur).
