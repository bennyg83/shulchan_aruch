Acceptance. And he is obligated to fast. And nevertheless, specifically when he accepted in his heart to fast; but if it was only in his mind to fast, it is not called acceptance (Bach; Magen Avraham).
Minchah. And the Bach wrote that it is permitted to accept it the entire time that it is day; and so wrote Magen Avraham; but before Minchah it does not help (Taz); see there.
Prayer. And nevertheless, also at the time of the prayer, when he reaches Shomea Tefillah, he should contemplate in his heart that he is accepting the fast (Bach; Magen Avraham).
