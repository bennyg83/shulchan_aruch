It is acceptance, and he is obligated to fast. See Yoreh De'ah, end of siman 258; and nevertheless, specifically when he accepted in his heart to fast; but if it was only in his mind to fast, it is not called acceptance; and so it appears in Yoreh De'ah, siman 214, that specifically when he began to fast he needs a release (Bach); and so it appears in siman 597 in the gloss; see there.
At the time of the Minchah prayer. This implies that it is forbidden to accept it after Minchah; but the Bach wrote that it is permitted to accept it the entire time that it is day; and so it appears in the Gemara; and see se'if 1.
And it is good, etc. And nevertheless, also at the time of the prayer, when he reaches Shomea Tefillah, he should contemplate in his heart that he is accepting the fast (Bach).
