Regarding a dream fast, it is as though he accepted it, etc., and in Shomea Tefillah he should say, ‘on the day of the fast of our fasting.’ I was uncertain whether, if ten people in the synagogue are fasting because of a disturbing dream, Heaven forbid, they should recite Aneinu as a separate blessing in the repetition. See siman 470 regarding the fast of the firstborn, where this is improper because it would publicize a fast on Rosh Chodesh Nissan. That concern does not apply here. The matter requires study.
