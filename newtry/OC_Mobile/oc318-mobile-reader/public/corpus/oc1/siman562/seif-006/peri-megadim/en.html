Praying — Eshel Avraham in Taanit Chayim: it is like acceptance etc., and he should say in Shemoneh Esreh on the day of our fast our fast. I was uncertain: if there are ten in the synagogue fasting a dream fast, God forbid — whether they should say Anenu in the repetition of the prayer as a separate blessing; and see siman 570 regarding fast of first fruits: it is not proper, for it would be public on Rosh Chodesh Nisan; not so here — and it requires study.
