Seif katan 2. See seif 2 and the end of siman 249. It is evident from there that specifically during the Aseres Yemei Teshuvah, when one does not accept the fast during the preceding prayer, he need not fast until nightfall. But where he accepted the fast, he must complete it. This is also evident from the end of siman 249 concerning one who accepted a fast; see there. The poskim dispute whether he must complete it until nightfall or only until the congregation leaves the synagogue. On a weekday, however, all agree that he must complete it until nightfall. Magen Avraham there, seif katan 8, writes the same; see there.
