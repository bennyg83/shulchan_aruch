If one accepted upon himself at the Minchah prayer three or four fasts that are not consecutive, one after another — such as one who accepted upon himself to fast Monday, Thursday, Monday, and the like — there is one who is in doubt whether a single acceptance suffices for all of them, or whether an acceptance is required for each and every one. {Rama: However, the people are accustomed to accept them with a single acceptance, and this suffices; and so is the opinion of some of the [early] authorities (Mordechai; Hagahos Maimoni).}
