If he accepted upon himself during the mincha prayer three or four fasts that are not consecutive one after another — such as if he accepted upon himself to fast Monday and Thursday and Monday, or the like — some are unsure whether one acceptance suffices for all of them or whether he needs acceptance for each one individually.However, the people are accustomed to accept them with one acceptance, and that suffices; and so is the opinion of some rabbanan (Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoni).):
