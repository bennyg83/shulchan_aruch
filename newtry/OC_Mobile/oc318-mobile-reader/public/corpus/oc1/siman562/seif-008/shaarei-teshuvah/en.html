An individual. See Baer Heitev; and he wrote in Mor UKetzi'ah that even during Minchah of Shabbos one may accept [a fast], but he should not say the tachanun; rather he should say only, "Behold I am on a voluntary fast tomorrow." And he wrote in Machatzit HaShekel that this is obvious, and so is the custom. And see Yad Eliyahu, siman 6 (and also): one who fasts every day, and sometimes wishes to make some interruptions and wants to stop [the fast] while it is still day and eat on that day on which he had been fasting beforehand: then if yesterday he accepted a fast without specification, he must complete it; if he stipulated until when he would fast, he may eat before he prays, for he is not permitted to push off communal prayer; even if he wishes to hear Kedushah and Barkhu in the synagogue and pray privately, that is not proper. And if he can interrupt after Ma'ariv that they pray while it is still day, and there is time to eat and to stop [the fast] while it is still day, that is fine. And therefore regarding a bridegroom below, that ideally he should pray with the congregation, see there.
