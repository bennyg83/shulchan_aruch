Taz, seif katan 2. ‘Those who fast in this manner, etc.’ Meaning, nowadays even those who fast only on some of the days have the practice not to accept the fast formally. The word ‘in this manner’ means ‘some days.’ Nevertheless, it is considered a fast, because once this became the established practice, their intent follows the practice. Consequently, they need not complete the fast, just like one who fasts throughout the Aseres Yemei Teshuvah, for it is as though he expressly stipulated this.
