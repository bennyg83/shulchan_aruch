(38) Whether one acceptance suffices for all of them: because he accepted them at once.
(39) However, the people are accustomed, etc.: and that is what they bless on the Shabbos after Pesach and Sukkos: "whoever will accept upon himself to fast Bahab," and the people answer Amen, and that is the acceptance, and he need not accept it again at the Minchah before it. And nevertheless, if afterward he wishes not to fast, he has license, since he did not bring it out with his mouth. But according to what we wrote below in siman 563, that there are those who are stringent that even with hirhur alone it is acceptance, according to their words one must be careful: if at the time he answered Amen it was in his heart to fast, not to retract from this.
