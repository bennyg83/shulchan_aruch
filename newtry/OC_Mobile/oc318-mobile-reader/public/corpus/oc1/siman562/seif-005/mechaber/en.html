Every fast which an individual did not accept upon themself whilst it was still daytime is not a fast. {Rama: Rema: [not a real fast] to pray Aneinu, and not regarding if the person is obligated in the fast at all; and the person who fasts thusly has not completed their vow (Mordechai and Hagahot Maimoni ch1). There are those that say that one prays Aneinu (Mordechai in the name of the R"i), and so it seems to me to act for the fast of an individual, and universally the one who fasts for a dream prays Aneinu even if they had not accepted it upon themself from yesterday.}
