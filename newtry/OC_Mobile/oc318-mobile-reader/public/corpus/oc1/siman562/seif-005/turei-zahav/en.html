And so it appears to me to practice regarding an individual's fast. We have already written nearby regarding [a fast] upon which the sun did not set, concerning the Aneinu prayer; and so it is also here, for these two matters are equivalent: namely, completion of the fast and acceptance of the fast. And in a place where the Rama required an individual to say Aneinu, he should skip two words, namely "the fast of our affliction," for they are only mere affliction; and with this he fulfills according to all views, as it appears to my humble opinion.
He prays Aneinu. It appears the reason is that he may properly say "on the fast of our affliction" even though they did not accept it, since Heaven imposed it upon him, and that stands in place of accepting it himself.
