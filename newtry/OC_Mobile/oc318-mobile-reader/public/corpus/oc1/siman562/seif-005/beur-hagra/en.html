Se'if 5: To pray, etc., and not, etc. The Rosh there. But he must fulfill his vow, unlike Rashi and the Mordechai there, and they follow their views; but according to the Rosh, who explains the Yerushalmi that he must fulfill his vow, as I wrote above in se'if 2, the same applies here; and see the gloss of Beis Yosef.
However, some say, etc. And a proof from a dream-fast, for in the Gemara it called it a fast; hence he prays Aneinu. And what is written "it is not a fast" is to fulfill his vow if he previously accepted upon himself several fasts. And the first view holds that it is not comparable to a dream-fast, for there Heaven imposed it upon him, as I wrote above in se'if 12 from the Yerushalmi there: that necessarily he is drawn after the nasi or the congregation even though he did not accept; and all the more so in such a case; and that is what [is meant by] "and according to everyone," etc. And see Tosafos there, s.v. "He lodged," and R' Meir wrote, etc.
