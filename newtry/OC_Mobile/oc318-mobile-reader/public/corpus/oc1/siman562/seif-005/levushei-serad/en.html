Seif katan 1. ‘But small stars.’ In siman 293 it is explained that small stars are certainly effective, because they are visible only when it is truly night. There is a scribal error here, and it should read: ‘But large stars are not effective,’ because large stars are visible even by day when one looks upward. Small stars, however, are not required. That is to say, although siman 293 states that one should not recite Maariv after Shabbos until he sees three small stars, because we are not expert in distinguishing large stars from medium-sized stars, that stringency applies specifically to Shabbos. Regarding a fast, we are not stringent here and do not require small stars; medium-sized stars suffice.
