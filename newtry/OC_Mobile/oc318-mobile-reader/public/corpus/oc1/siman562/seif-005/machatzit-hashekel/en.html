(s.k. 5) Until, etc. And as Beis Yosef wrote in siman 249 in the name of the Mordechai: even though we do not hold so except regarding erev Shabbos, but not on a weekday, as Magen Avraham wrote in siman 249, s.k. 8, nevertheless during the Ten Days of Repentance it suffices until after Ma'ariv, for the reasons explained.
And so it appears in se'if 3. Meaning, that he also wrote the wording "until the congregation finish their prayer," implying that the matter depends on the prayer and the time of Ma'ariv.
And the end of siman 559, se'if 9: meaning, what Magen Avraham wrote there in the name of the Hagahos Asheri in s.k. 13, who disagrees there with what the Shulchan Aruch wrote there regarding a deferred Tisha B'Av, that it suffices for baalei bris to fast until after they prayed Minchah Gedolah; and the Hagahos Asheri holds that we specifically require eleven hours. And according to what Magen Avraham wrote here, one must say that [eleven hours] is not precise; rather that then he had already prayed Minchah Ketanah and waited a little until the time of Ma'ariv arrived, that is, near eleven and a quarter hours. But in Sefer Eliyah Rabbah above he wrote that it is a scribal error in the Hagahos Asheri, and it should read that he waited six hours and prayed Minchah Gedolah; see there in the Hagahos Asheri, chapter "These may shave."
And so wrote the Bach (at the end of this siman) regarding a bridegroom: that he must in any case fast until near eleven and a quarter hours; and it appears there that it suffices even if he has not yet prayed Ma'ariv.
