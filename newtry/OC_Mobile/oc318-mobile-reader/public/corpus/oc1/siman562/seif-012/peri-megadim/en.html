Monday and Thursday. See Taz in the name of Maharam Mintz. One who accepted without specification to observe a Monday-Thursday-Monday fast on certain days, but did not specify which days, may fulfill his obligation by fasting Thursday-Monday-Thursday three times as well. The same applies if he observes them intermittently, meaning that over the course of several weeks he completes the Monday-Thursday-Monday sequence; this suffices and he fulfills his obligation. The Mechaber’s wording should read, ‘There is one who is uncertain.’ See seif 12, Taz, paragraph 16; see there. With Hashem’s help, this will be explained there.
