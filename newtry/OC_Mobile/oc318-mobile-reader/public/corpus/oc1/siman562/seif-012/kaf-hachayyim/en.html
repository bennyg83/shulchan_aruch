This applies specifically in such a case, where both factors are present: it is merely for affliction as part of repentance, and he did not formally accept it. If he accepted it without qualification, it is a complete fast and he must complete it. Biur HaGra. Mishna Berurah in Biur Halacha. This follows from what is written below in seif 3, that if he accepted a fast without qualification and did not specify otherwise, he must fast until the emergence of the stars; see there. Only here, where the practice is not to complete it and he fasted in accordance with the custom, it is as though he explicitly stated that he would not complete it. If, however, he accepted a fast without qualification, he must complete it.
