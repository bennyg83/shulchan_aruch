Se'if 12: A fast, etc. Gemara there, folio 24a: the house of the nasi, etc.
And some say, etc. For they hold specifically a nasi, as in se'if 6; and as is written in the Yerushalmi, chapter 2, law 1: R' Chelbo said to R' Yehudah the Nasi, Go out with us and afflict yourself; said R' Yosa: this says that these fasts that we do are not fasts; why? Because the nasi is not with us. And that is what [is meant by] "there is no communal fast in Bavel"; and not only Bavel, but all of chutz la'aretz, as is written "we do not judge laws of fines in Bavel."
