And likewise is our custom regarding the Bahab fasts after Pesach and after Sukkos: that one who answers Amen after the Mi Shebeirach that the chazzan says concerning the fast, that constitutes acceptance (Taz). And Magen Avraham wrote in siman 563, s.k. 1, that even if he then resolved in his heart to fast, he is not obligated to fast, since it is not at the time of Minchah; but if he then uttered it with his lips, it appears to me that one should be stringent; see there.
