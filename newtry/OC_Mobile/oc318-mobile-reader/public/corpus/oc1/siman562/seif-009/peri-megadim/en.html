At the time — see letter 7 and letter 8; and although a fast is like a vow of consecrated property, nevertheless we require specifically at the time of prayer, for otherwise the thought does not impose obligation upon him, for it is usual to reconsider; and see Eshel Avraham letter 5, who strengthens this; and see siman 563, which implies like Taz — namely if he expressed with his lips even before chatzot or much time before, in any case he is obligated to fulfill the vow and fast that day; not so for thought alone, that he did not think at its set time — it is not like speech, and he may retract afterward and eat the next day; and Eshel Avraham will explain further below.
