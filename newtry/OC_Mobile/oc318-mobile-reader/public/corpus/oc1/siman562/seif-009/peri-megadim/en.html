At the time. See Taz, and see paragraphs 7 and 8. Although a fast is like a vow concerning sacred matters, it nevertheless must be undertaken specifically during prayer. Otherwise, the thought does not establish an obligation upon him, since a person is prone to reconsider. See Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 5, who is uncertain about this. See siman 563, which appears to agree with Taz: if he expressed it verbally, even before midday or a long time beforehand, he is in any event obligated to fulfill the vow and fast that day. By contrast, where there was only thought, so long as he did not contemplate it at the prescribed time, thought is not equivalent to speech, and he may later retract and eat on the following day. With Hashem’s help, this will be explained further below.
