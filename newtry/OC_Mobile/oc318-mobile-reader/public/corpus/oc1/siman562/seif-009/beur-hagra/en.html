Se'if 9: And he fasted, etc. For there it is called a fast of hours; and we say there "and that is provided he did not taste," etc., as is written in se'if 10.
He reconsidered. Gemara there: it is needed only where he reconsidered.
Because he did not, etc. Gemara there: Abaye said to him, etc.; and there: this case is different, etc.
