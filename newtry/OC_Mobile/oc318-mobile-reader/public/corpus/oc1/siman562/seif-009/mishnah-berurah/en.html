(א) (b) "I will hear from him, and so on, I will hear him," he said

(ב) It is not an answer to the Omnipresent’s people, nor is it for us to pray for our sins

(ג) He said, “I am not afraid of the Omnipresent’s people, and I will not be able to do it, and I will be able to do it, but if you have been given it to him, you will not be able to do it before.” Roni them, who is a believer, and who believes in the fulfillment of a whole day, does not have a gift of hours, and does not receive a blessing from the Omnipresent’s people, even if he does not have an answer to the Omnipresent. And he said, “What the the Omnipresent has written about us is to do with a single matter that we have answered in the Gemara, and that we have not received any of the things we have done here, and so is the the Omnipresent.”
