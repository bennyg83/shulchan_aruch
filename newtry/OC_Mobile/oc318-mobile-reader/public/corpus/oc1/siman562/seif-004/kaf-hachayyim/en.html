Although above, in siman 263, regarding Motzei Shabbos, the Shulchan Aruch ruled that one may not perform melacha until three small stars are visible, that is because they were stringent regarding Shabbos, since not everyone is expert in determining which stars are considered medium-sized, as explained there in the Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham. Regarding a fast, however, they maintained the basic law, and medium-sized stars suffice. Machatzis HaShekel. So too wrote Levushei Serad. What is written there, that the stars must also be consecutive, is because of the added time of holiness, whereas a fast, even Tishah B'Av, does not require an added period. Regarding Yom Kippur, one can say that medium-sized consecutive stars constitute the added period. Alternatively, one can say that they are stringent regarding a Torah law, whereas regarding a rabbinic fast they did not impose this burden. Eshel Avraham, os 1. Meaning, specifically regarding a rabbinic fast they did not impose this burden, and medium-sized stars that are not consecutive suffice.
