(s.k. 4) That there is no, etc. However, the world is accustomed to complete. Meaning, those who fast only the first day of Selichos.
And it is possible that it is forbidden to permit it for them; see Yoreh De'ah, siman 214. Meaning, it is forbidden to permit it for them: to say to them "you are permitted"; rather they need a release through three people through an opening of regret; for these are permitted matters and others who know they are permitted treat them as forbidden: the Rama ruled there in Yoreh De'ah. And so appears there the Beis Yosef's view: that in such a case a release is needed like the law of a neder, and a release helps like a neder.
And it is possible that the custom spread from a mistake: meaning, and if so they need no release through three people like a neder; rather one may say to them "you are permitted," since the custom was in error; and so it is explained there in Yoreh De'ah; see there: that it deals with one who is not accustomed, etc. Meaning, not like the world's error, who think it refers to anyone who does not fast all Ten Days, even if those few days that he fasts he fasts every year, even so he needs acceptance and completion; but the truth is not so; rather it deals with one who is not accustomed, etc.
And all the more so one who stipulates at the time [of acceptance], as it should read.
