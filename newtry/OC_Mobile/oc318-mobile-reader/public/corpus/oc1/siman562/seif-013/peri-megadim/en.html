The practical relevance of this entire siman regarding a fast is whether one fulfills his vow and whether, in Aneinu, he may say, ‘on the day of the fast of our fasting,’ etc. But regarding his obligation to fulfill the vow, see Taz, paragraph 15. This means that even if he ate for half the day and afterward accepted upon himself to fast until night, he must in any event complete his vow. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 9.
