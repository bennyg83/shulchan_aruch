It is an asmachta. Rabbeinu Yeruham equated a fast and tzedakah to these matters; and this requires further study regarding tzedakah, where he ruled that even for an optional matter it is not an asmachta, as Beis Yosef wrote in Yoreh De'ah, siman 258, se'if 10, and in Choshen Mishpat, siman 207; and it is forced to distinguish, for in siman 563 he also wrote in the name of the Rosh that a fast resembles tzedakah. And it is possible that for this reason he wrote "there is one who says," to make known that many dispute the matter, and therefore one should be stringent.
