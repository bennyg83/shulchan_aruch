Se'if 13: There is one who, etc. And so it appears in the third chapter of Sanhedrin (Rabbeinu Yeruham). Meaning there, folio 55a: that [case] of R' Yehudah in the name of R' Tarfon, etc.; hence even regarding nezirus it is an asmachta, because he conditioned on an optional matter; and see Tosafos there, s.v. "That," etc. And this is unlike the view of Maharam that the Mordechai cited in his name in the fourth chapter of Bava Kamma, and the Hagahos Maimoniyos in Sefer Hafla'ah: that even regarding an optional matter it is not an asmachta; and he brought a proof from what is written: this cow said, Behold I am a nezira, etc.; and so [Beis Yosef] explained in Yoreh De'ah, siman 258, se'if 10, and in Choshen Mishpat, siman 207, se'if 19; and Magen Avraham was in doubt whether they disagree. And he did not examine their root: they certainly disagree; and Maharam's proof may be rejected, for there it is according to the first Tanna against R' Tarfon, who holds that whoever hangs [a condition] not in his control, it is not an asmachta, as is written in Sanhedrin there; and see the end of the fifth chapter of Nazir.
But if, etc. For he did it to spur himself, and he resolved in his mind; as is written at the end of Nedarim: that man who forbade benefit from the world, etc.
And likewise if, etc. As is written: "And Yisrael vowed a vow: If You will surely give," etc.
Or, etc. As is written: "And Yaakov vowed a vow," etc., "If God will be," etc.; and it is written "that you vowed to Me," etc.
