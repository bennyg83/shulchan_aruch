It implies specifically after the maariv prayer, which is after eleven hours less a quarter, as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 249 in the name of Mordechai, that this is called completion after the next day has entered. And so in seif 3 and seif 11 and siman 559 seif 9. However it appears to me that whenever he prayed mincha at that time (meaning after eleven hours less a quarter) it suffices, since it is the beginning of sunset it is called completion of the fast. Magen Avraham s.k. 5. However what he wrote to bring proof from siman 559 — see in our words there paragraph 14, for several acharonim challenged Magen Avraham's words there, and hold that even after mincha gedola it is permitted to break — see there. And so Machatzit haShekel siman 581 s.k. 10: now many practice not to fast until the time of mincha gedola, and first pray mincha gedola — see there. And so Eshel Avraham paragraph 5 and Be'er Heitev and Mishna Berurah paragraph 10 wrote: for a weak person he may certainly rely to be lenient to fast only until the time of mincha gedola, and pray mincha and the prayer of Anenu and eat — end of his words. And it appears better to stipulate from the outset that he fasts tomorrow only until mincha gedola, and also he may pray Anenu in Elohei Netzor as written above paragraph 8, in order to satisfy all opinions.
