There are those who say that the person who vows and says "If I do not go to such and such a place, I will sit in fasting from after there", that the condition is a matter of choice, is a mere support for the statement (asmachta) [?]. But if they said "If I do not do such and such a mitzvah, I will sit in fasting", and so if they say "If God saves me from trouble" or "If my path succeeds", this is a support. {Rama: (Meaning, a person relies in their mind on being able to do something, and on this assumption they make the vow, or they are obliged in that thing)}
