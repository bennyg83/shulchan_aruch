(4) With the left hand — written in the Ari's writings: hold them between ring finger and pinky; when reaching the portion of tzitzis, take them also in the right hand, look at them, keep them until "the faithful One… forever," then kiss the tzitzis and remove them from his hand.

(5) "Upon your heart" — the heart is on the left. This mitzvah saves a person from sin, as written: "Do not explore… so that you remember… and you shall be holy." Although other mitzvos lack this virtue to save from the evil inclination, tzitzis is superior — as the incident in Menachos (44a): there was an incident with a certain man, etc.
