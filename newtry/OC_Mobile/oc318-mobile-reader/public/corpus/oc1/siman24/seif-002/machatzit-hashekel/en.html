(Se'if katan 2) To the havayos — meaning the sefiros. There are ten havayos — one Tetragrammaton without vowels at all; the other nine havayos are each pointed with one of the nine known vowels — first, entirely kamatz; second, entirely patach; third, entirely tzere, etc. See Sefer Shelah. "Sefiros" — in Sefer Asarah Ma'amaros, from the word sapir (sapphire); a metaphor, as "under His feet like the work of sapphire brick."
