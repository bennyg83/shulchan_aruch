One should be careful to make a fine tallis because of "This is my God and I will adorn Him" (Gemara). To hold the tzitzis — between ring finger and pinky (Berachos, Rambam). When reaching the portion of tzitzis, take them also in the right hand, look at them, keep them until "the faithful One… forever," then kiss the tzitzis and remove them (Ari's writings).
