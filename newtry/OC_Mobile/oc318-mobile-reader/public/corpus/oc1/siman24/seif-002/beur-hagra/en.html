Seif 2: It is a mitzvah, etc. This appears in the Pesikta, cited in Yalkut Tehillim, section 723, and in Midrash Shocher Tov: “All my bones shall say, etc.” David said before HaKadosh Baruch Hu: “I shall praise You with all my limbs and fulfill mitzvos with them. Upon my chest I place the tzitzis opposite my heart whenever I recite Krias Shema, as it says, ‘And these words, etc., shall be upon your heart.’ With my left hand I bind the tefillin of the hand, and with it I hold the tzitzis during Krias Shema.” Mordechai at the end of chapter 3 of Sukkah cites the Yerushalmi: “Not only, etc.,” as explained above in siman 8, seif 4, and at the end of siman 17.
