Se'if 2, "it is a mitzvah," etc. Pesikta, brought in Yalkut Tehillim 5733 and Midrash Shir HaShirim: "All my bones shall say" — David said before the Holy One: I praise You with all my limbs and fulfill mitzvos with them — on the chest I place tzitzis opposite the heart while reciting Shema, as "these words… upon your heart"; left hand — I bind tefillin of God's Name and hold tzitzis during Shema. Mordechai, beginning of chapter 3 of Sukkah, in the name of Yerushalmi: "not only," etc. — as siman 8, se'if 4 and siman 17.
