(6) There — at minimum tzitzis at prayer. Levush: at Shema and prayer; likewise Raz, letter 2; Perishah — se'if 1 reads Shema and prayer. If the text is "at prayer," he emphasizes even at prayer one should have tzitzis — all the more at Shema, which mentions tzitzis explicitly; without them one bears false testimony — so far. Likewise Magen Avraham, se'if katan 2, Zohar Shelach Lekha; Pischei Olam, letter 1, R' Chaim, Sha'ar HaKedushah ch. 10; Machazik Berachah.
