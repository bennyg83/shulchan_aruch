(9) Careful in the mitzvah of tzitzis — a mitzvah to make a fine tallis and fine tzitzis; likewise all mitzvos should be done with hiddur as much as possible — "This is my God and I will adorn Him," beautifying mitzvos before Him. But it is forbidden to embroider Torah verses on a tallis; nevertheless, if already embroidered, one may bless on it. Even on napkins one should not instruct permission lechatchilah to embroider Torah on them.
