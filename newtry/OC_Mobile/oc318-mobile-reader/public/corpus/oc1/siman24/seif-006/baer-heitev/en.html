One who is careful with the mitzvah of tzitzis. He should take care to make a beautiful tallis because of “This is my God, and I will beautify Him.” See the Zohar, parashas Shelach Lecha, which states that one who recites Krias Shema without tzitzis bears false testimony against himself. Magen Avraham. Responsa Radbaz, part 4, siman 45, forbids embroidering verses of Torah upon a tallis. The author of Leket HaKemach writes that there is room to permit it; see there.
