Careful in the mitzvah of tzitzis. One should be careful to make a fine tallis because of "This is my God and I will adorn Him." See Zohar Shelach Lekha: one who recites Shema without tzitzis bears false testimony against himself. Magen Avraham — in responsum Radbaz 4:45 forbids embroidering Torah verses on a tallis; Leket HaKemach wrote he found grounds to permit — see there.
