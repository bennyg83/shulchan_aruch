[4] Allusion to the havayos, etc. Perishah: allusion to "and you shall see them" — through seeing tzitzis you see and examine the Divine Name. Chinuch, Shelach Lekha: my heart tells me there is hint and remembrance that body and soul are all to God — white hints body from earth created from white snow; threads hint body as they say the body's beginning is like threads, as Chazal: "his two thighs like two threads of whiteness," etc.; tekhelet like the sky hints soul from above — tekhelet like sky, sky like Throne, as "they saw the God of Israel," "like sapphire stone, likeness of a throne," under the throne souls of righteous are stored — therefore wrap tekhelet on white, soul above, body below. Bach: because Chazal said God passed wrapped in His tallis, "His garment like white snow" — tallis should be white. Gemara: whoever wears tefillin, mezuzah at his door, tzitzis on his garment will not sin — "triple cord," "God's angel encamps around those who fear Him." Why two verses? From "triple cord" one might say only three mitzvos — therefore bring "God's angel encamps" — Rashi: mitzvos are also called God's angels, emissaries to Israel. From "encamps" one might say tzitzis alone saves from sin by surrounding — Levush implies. This is difficult against Makor Chayim siman 16: mitzvos do not save from the evil inclination, yet tzitzis saves since equivalent to all mitzvos, and because God tied this mitzvah opposite heart and eye. However it is explained there: specifically from licentiousness it saves, as the incident of the student.
