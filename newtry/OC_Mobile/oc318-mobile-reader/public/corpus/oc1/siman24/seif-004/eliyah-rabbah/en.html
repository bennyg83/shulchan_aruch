[3] And to place them upon the eyes, etc. It is found in the name of the early authorities that anyone who passes the tzitzis over his eyes while reciting the passage of tzitzis is assured that he will not come to blindness. This explains the verse, “And it shall be for you as tzitzis.” It is seemingly obvious that the strings are tzitzis. Rather, the verse means that they should be for you as an object of gazing, for the word tzitzis connotes looking. Matzas Shimurim writes that this practice is also beneficial for attaining lofty perception and brings a person to fear of Heaven, for the letters of “seeing” can be rearranged to form “fear.” Looking at the corner bearing tzitzis is also beneficial for removing anger, for the numerical value of “corner” equals that of “anger.”
