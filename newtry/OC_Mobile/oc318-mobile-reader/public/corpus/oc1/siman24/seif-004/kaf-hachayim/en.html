(4) There — proper to wear over garments; likewise above siman 8, se'if 11 — the essence of tallit katan is over one's clothes. I already wrote there letter 43: Ari z"l and later authorities hold tallit katan should be worn below other garments, on one's shirt; wearing it over all garments is a great error, opposite the truth — see there. So is widespread custom.

(4) What may be asked on Maran z"l — here and siman 8 he wrote to wear the tallis over garments, and siman 23 he brought Beit Yosef's wording that they wear tallit katan under clothes. Ma'amar Mordechai answered here, letter 1: siman 23 speaks of world custom under clothes; here and siman 8 he says it is proper over garments — see there. We already wrote above that this is not the Ari's view.
