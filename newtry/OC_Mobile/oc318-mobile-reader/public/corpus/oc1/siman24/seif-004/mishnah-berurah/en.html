(g) On the eyes - it is found in the name of the ancients that anyone who passes a tassel over his eyes when reciting Parshat Tzishit will be guaranteed that he will not come into the hands of a blindfold. He has the soma to hold the tassel in his hand at the time of Kash Afi as it was said and you saw it since he slept in the sight of others, but to pass the tassel over his eyes is not Damhazi as Hokha and Italula:
