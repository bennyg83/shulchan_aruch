Ba'er Heitev — regarding embroidering verses, see Yoreh De'ah siman 283 that it is improper; Ginat Veradim, rule 1, siman 25: if already done and the portion of tzitzis embroidered, one may bless. Be careful when sitting on a bench to lift the corners and not sit on the tallis body where Torah is embroidered. See Shach there — they practiced permitting embroidery — see there. Ginat Veradim siman 110: one who asked about napkins — do not instruct permission lechatchilah, all the more on a tallis; he cited Radbaz. Leket HaKemach also cited Radbaz, agreeing bedieved to bless. What Yoreh De'ah cites in the Rambam's name does not apply to our tallisos, which we treat with sanctity not to enter a bathroom, etc. Or Zarua in the name of Makor Chayim: although mitzvos do not save from the evil inclination, tzitzis saves — seemingly from Menachos' incident of a certain man. "And you shall see them and remember all God's mitzvos… that you go after… so you remember… and be holy" — our words emerge in holiness.
