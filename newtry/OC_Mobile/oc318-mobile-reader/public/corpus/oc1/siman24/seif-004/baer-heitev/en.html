On the eyes. It is found in the name of the ancients: whoever passes tzitzis over his eyes when reading the portion of tzitzis is assured he will not become blind. A blind person may hold the tzitzis in his hand when saying "and you shall see them," and may kiss them out of love of the mitzvah; but passing tzitzis over his eyes appears like mockery — Shabbos Yaakov 72:368 — see there.
