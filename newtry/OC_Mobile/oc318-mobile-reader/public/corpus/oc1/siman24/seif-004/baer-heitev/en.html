Upon the eyes. It is found in the name of the early authorities that anyone who passes the tzitzis over his eyes while reciting the passage of tzitzis is assured that he will not come to blindness. A blind person should hold the tzitzis in his hand when saying “and you shall see it,” and should likewise kiss them out of affection for the mitzvah. He should not, however, pass them over his eyes, because that appears ridiculous. Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 38; see there.
