(1) So that he remember, etc. — An example: like one who warns his friend about a matter by tying a knot in his belt so he will remember it (Tur).

(2) Over the garments — see above siman 8 in Mishnah Berurah what we wrote there.

(3) At the time of prayer — and Shema. It is stated in Zohar Shelach Lekha: one who recites Shema without tzitzis bears false testimony against himself — he reads the portion of tzitzis and does not fulfill the verse. Chayei Adam wrote: it is not good that many people pray on the road without a tallis gadol; and most tallit katan garments are not made according to law in all details to be fit for blessing, and he also sleeps in it at night. See above siman 8, se'if 96 in Mishnah Berurah.
