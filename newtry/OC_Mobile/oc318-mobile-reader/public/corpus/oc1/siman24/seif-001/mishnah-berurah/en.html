(1) In order that he remember, etc. This may be compared to a person who warns his fellow about a certain matter and ties a knot in his belt so that he will remember it. Tur.
(2) Upon the garments. See above, siman 8, in Mishnah Berurah, where we discussed this.
(3) During prayer. And during Krias Shema. The Zohar in parashas Shelach Lecha states that one who recites Krias Shema without tzitzis bears false testimony against himself, for he recites the passage of tzitzis but does not fulfill the verse. Chayei Adam writes that the general public acts improperly when they pray while traveling without a tallis gadol. In most cases the tallis katan is not made in accordance with all the details of the law so that it would be fit for a blessing, and he also sleeps in it at night. See above, siman 8, seif 16, in Mishnah Berurah.
