Seif 1: In order that, etc. There, the Gemara says: “He seeks devices, etc.” At the end of the first chapter of Sotah, 11a, it says: “Nevertheless, he desired, etc.” See Tosafos in Arvei Pesachim 113b, s.v. “And there is no,” beginning “Alternatively.” Rabbi Yonah likewise writes in Sefer HaYirah: “He should have a tallis katan and wear it beneath his garments, for the principal mitzvah of tzitzis is to wear it constantly, since they were given as a reminder of the mitzvos and so that a person should not stray after the stubbornness of his heart and the sight of his eyes.” Meaning, as the verse states: “And you shall see it,” followed by, “and you shall not stray.”
In order that, etc. As stated in Menachos 43b: “And you shall see it,” for seeing leads to remembering, etc.
Therefore one should, etc. Tur.
And it is proper, etc. As explained above, siman 8, seif 11.
And at least, etc. Rambam and Tur. Although according to the strict law this is unnecessary, see Tosafos on Berachos 14b, s.v. “And he puts on,” and see Zohar, volume 3, page 122.
