Se'if 1, "so that," etc. There it is written "the righteous shall live by his faith," etc.; end of Sotah 14a — "nevertheless he desired," etc. See Tosafos parashat Ekev 113b, s.v. ve'ein, or alternatively, etc.; likewise Rabbeinu Yonah in Sefer HaYirah — his wording: he should have a tallit katan and wear it under his garments, for the essence of tzitzis is to wear it always, for remembrance of mitzvos they were given, and so a person not explore after the whims of his heart and after what his eyes see — meaning, as "and you shall see them" and "do not explore."

"So that," etc. As Menachos 43b: "and you shall see them" — seeing leads to remembrance, etc.

Therefore there are, etc. Tur.

And proper, etc. As stated siman 8, se'if 11.

At minimum, etc. Rambam and Tur — although by law it is not required. See Tosafos Berachos 14b, s.v. umanich; Zohar 3:122.
