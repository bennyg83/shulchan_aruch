(1) [Se'if 1] If one does not wear a four-cornered garment, one is not obligated in tzitzis — but one loses that reward in the World to Come that one would receive if wearing tzitzis, which has five expressions of positive commandment: "make for them tzitzis," "place on the tzitzis," "and you shall see them," "it shall be for you for tzitzis," "twisted threads you shall make for yourself" (Beit Yosef, Levush, Shetili Zeitim, letter 1).
