The conduct of wearing tzitzis, its reward and punishment. Containing 6 se'ifim: If a person
does not wear a four-cornered garment, he is not obligated in tzitzis; but it is good and proper that every person be careful to wear a tallit katan all day, so that he remember the mitzvos at every moment — therefore it has five knots corresponding to the five books of the Torah, and four corners so that whichever direction he turns he will remember. It is proper to wear it over one's garments; at minimum, one should be careful to wear tzitzis at the time of prayer.':
