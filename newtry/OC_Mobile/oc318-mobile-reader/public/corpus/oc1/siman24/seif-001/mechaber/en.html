[Various] Practices of Wearing ציצית and it's Rewards and Punishments. Containing 6 Se'ifim: If a person does not
wear a garment with four corners then he is not obligated in [the מצוה of] ציצית. [However,] it is good and proper that a person should be cautious to wear a small טלית every day, in order that he should remember the מצות at every moment. Therefore, there are five knots [in each corner] which correspond to the five books of the Torah, and there are four corners, so that in whichever direction he faces, he will remember. It is proper to wear ציצית over his garments and at the very least, he should be careful to wear ציצית at the time of prayer.':
