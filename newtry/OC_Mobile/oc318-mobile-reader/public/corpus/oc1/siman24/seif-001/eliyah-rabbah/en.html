[1] At every moment, etc. I already wrote in siman 21 that meticulous individuals sleep wearing tzitzis even at night. Tashbetz, siman 261, writes that the four tzitzis contain thirty-two strings. The Torah begins with the letter beis in Bereishis and ends with the letter lamed, together spelling thirty-two. Abudraham writes that the thirty-two strings are twisted, making sixty-four, corresponding to the sixty-four faces upon the Throne of Glory. Likewise, there are sixty-four words from “Speak to the children of Israel” until “I am Hashem your God,” hinting that it is as though the Shechinah stands opposite him. Sefer Tzeidah LaDerech writes that one who is accustomed to tzitzis merits both physical and intellectual attainments. See below, at the beginning of siman 60.
