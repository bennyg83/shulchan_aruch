[1] At every moment, etc. I already wrote siman 21 that the punctilious sleep in tzitzis even at night. Tashbetz siman 261: four tzitzis are thirty-two threads — Torah begins with bet in bereshit and ends with lamed. Avudraham: thirty-two threads are twisted, sixty-four against sixty-four faces of the Throne of Glory; likewise sixty-four words from "Speak to the children of Israel" until "I am the Lord your God" — hinting the Shechinah is before him. Sefer Tzeda LaDerech: one accustomed in tzitzis merits physical and intellectual elevations. See below at the start of siman 60.
