And the four corners, so that whichever direction he turns, he will remember. The gloss there explains that the four corners correspond to the four directions of the world. Techeiles resembles the sea, the sea resembles the sky, and the sky resembles the Throne of Glory. This is the seat of judgment upon which capital cases are judged, and this is the strap with which the Father disciplines the son. So writes the Zohar in parashas Shelach Lecha. Accordingly, the blessing should be pronounced “lehisatef betzitzis,” with a patach under the beis, indicating that the essential mitzvah is the thread of techeiles. “At all times let your garments be white” refers to the mitzvah of tzitzis. One who is careful with it merits to behold the countenance of the Shechinah. See there.
