And four corners — whichever direction he turns he will remember. Rama gloss on this: four corners correspond to the corners of the world. Tekhelet resembles the sea, sea to the sky, sky to the Throne of Glory — this is the throne of judgment, ten laws of souls; this is the strap of Abba, lashes ten times (so Zohar, parashat Shelach Lekha). Accordingly one should wrap in tzitzis at the doorway under the letter bet, to indicate the essence of the mitzvah — the tekhelet thread. "At all times your garments shall be white" — this is the mitzvah of tzitzis; one careful in it merits to see the Divine Presence — see there.
