(Se'if katan 3) "Great," etc. — bears false testimony against himself. Berachos 14: they said this of one who recites Shema without tefillin; Zohar adds likewise without tzitzis. "Bears false testimony against himself" — Rashi: an elevated expression. Terumat HaDeshen: literally the reader — he reads "you shall bind them as a sign," etc., and does not fulfill the verse. The same may be said for tzitzis.
