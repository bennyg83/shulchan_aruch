(3) There — good to wear a tallis, etc.; also careful to make a fine tallis — "This is my God and I will adorn Him," as Shabbat 133b: beautify before Him mitzvos — fine sukkah, fine tzitzis, etc. (Magen Avraham at the start of this siman; Machatzit HaShekel, letter 3). It should be laundered and clean (Chayei Adam, letter 5). Orchot Yosher was very careful about washing it frequently so it stays white — see there; Rechovot HaPeninim in Kaf HaChayim Yoreh De'ah, letter 1. Chayei Adam wrote: how much shame for one who dresses in fine clothes when able, yet wears a worn, torn tallis — shame and gloom, worrying about repair — meaning, etc. Mishnat Chachamim proved this, letter 218 — see there; Rechovot HaPeninim cited there.
