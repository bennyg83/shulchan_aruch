[3] And place them on the eyes, etc. Found in the ancients: whoever passes tzitzis over his eyes when reading the portion of tzitzis is assured he will not become blind — so far. This explains the verse "it shall be for you for tzitzis" — why needed? Strings are obviously for tzitzis; rather it means for looking, for tzitzis is from "looking." Preserved matzah is also good for great attainment and brings awe — "you saw letters of fear." Looking at the corner of tzitzis is good to remove anger — kanaf (corner) equals ka'as (anger) in gematria.
