c) It is good and right for every person to be careful to wear a tallit, etc. and also be careful to make a nice tallit. Because of this Eli and Anuho Kmash in tractate Shabbat page 13 12b it is Eli and Anuho he had fun before him with matzoh made before him a nice sukkah etc. a nice tassel etc. etc. etc., etc. And Rabbi Urhot Osher warned a lot about the washing that he should wash it at least once in a while so that it would be white. And the shame is for the one who dresses in fine clothes when his hand reaches and the tallit is worn and torn, he will wear shame and wrap himself in a garment and his soul will be sad and he will worry about revenge Rabbi Akal.
