He returns and prays. See the reason in the Gemara, “Would that a person pray all day,” contrary to Olas Tamid. He stipulates: If I have not prayed, let this count as my obligation; and if I have prayed, let it count as my voluntary prayer. Beit Yosef and Rashba.
Except for Mussaf. Because it includes a sin offering, which cannot be brought voluntarily. Although the Mussaf of Shabbos consisted of olos, we nevertheless follow the majority of Mussaf offerings. This is the Rosh’s explanation. I do not know why he had to force this answer, for the reason appears clear to me: Shacharis can be prayed in place of another offering, whereas in Mussaf one says, “and the Mussaf offerings according to their law,” etc. I wrote similarly in siman 108, seif 6.
