Seif 1 — If he is in doubt, etc.: According to R. Yochanan; and the Rif explains that R. Yitzchak does not argue with Shmuel, who said there he was standing [ready to pray], etc. He stops, etc. For R. Yitzchak only argues regarding voluntary prayer, to exclude that which Shmuel [said, namely] that one who stood to pray as an obligation is like one who offers two tamid offerings. And this is specifically for an individual, but not for the congregation, for the congregation does not offer a voluntary burnt offering. And similarly Musaf—even for an individual—not because it does not come as a voluntary offering, but Tur and Rosh in the name of R. Hai Gaon wrote that specifically through innovation [is it permitted]; and proof from "one prayed and entered," etc. If, etc., and "only with the congregation"—if so, what is he saying, and it is necessary, etc. Rather that even for an individual it is through innovation. And furthermore we say—one might think one could pray all day, etc.; and certainly it is impossible to say it is obligatory to pray all day, but rather optional. And furthermore regarding obligation, what is he saying, and three times, etc., evening, etc.—behold we hold Maariv is optional. And similarly the verse is difficult. And specifically when certain; but in doubt one does not need innovation, for you have no greater innovation than this, that he fulfills his doubt; and this is what is meant by "would that," etc. And that which they challenge in chapter 4 of Pesachim—it is certainly because there it is about innovation. And similarly Tosafot on "and R. Yitzchak," etc. And similarly is Rashi's view, except that he explains they argue.

Except, etc. And Tur and Rosh wrote because of the sin-offering in it; and they challenged from Shabbat Musaf, and they answered that Shabbat is different, for on Shabbat one does not offer vows and voluntary offerings; and see chapter 2, siman 19, and chapter 15 of Shabbat. And this is what is meant by "and on Shabbat," etc. And similarly wrote the Rambam in the name of some Geonim.

And if, etc. Gemara there, and as above.
