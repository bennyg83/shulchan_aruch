Taz, siman kaf alef. All hold according to R' Yochanan: for that which R' Eliezer said (Berachos 21a)—"If one is in doubt whether one prayed, one should not return and pray"—R' Yochanan said about it: Would that a person might pray all day. And that which the Taz wrote regarding the statement of one who remembered in the middle—this is the ruling in Shulchan Aruch in this siman. And that which he wrote regarding the statement "if he knows how to innovate"—this is what he said: If one prayed and entered the synagogue and found the congregation praying—if he can innovate something in it he should return and pray; if not, he should not pray.
