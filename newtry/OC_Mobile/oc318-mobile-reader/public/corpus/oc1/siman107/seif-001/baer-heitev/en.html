And prays. And he makes a condition and says: if I did not pray, let this be for my obligation, and if I prayed, let this be for my voluntary offering (Beit Yosef, Magen Avraham).

Musaf. Because it contains [the aspect of] a sin-offering and does not come as a voluntary offering; and even though the Shabbat Musaf was brought as a burnt offering, nevertheless on Shabbat one cannot in any case bring a voluntary offering, as will be explained; and see in the Rosh.

Blessing. For since he began to pray with the intent that he had not prayed, he rendered it as an obligation; and even in Maariv, which is optional, he stops (Magen Avraham).
