If he is in doubt, he must make a condition: if he did not pray, let this prayer be for his obligation, and if he already prayed, let this one be for his voluntary offering; and then he prays whether alone or with the congregation. And if he remembered in the middle of the prayer that he had already prayed—if he stipulated this at the outset, he completes it as a voluntary prayer and innovates something in a blessing from that point onward, and the whole [remainder] is voluntary (Tur, Beit Yosef, and the Rashba, R. Yitzchak). Except for the Musaf prayer, for one does not pray Musaf voluntarily because they mention the Musaf offering. Therefore, if he wishes to pray voluntarily and not mention the Musaf offering, he is permitted. And therefore, if he wishes to mention in Shacharit the tamid offering as well, it is forbidden to pray voluntarily (responsa of Maharam of Padua, siman 19). The Rav inclined in the Taz, may his memory be blessed, to the view of the Rav Magen Avraham, may his memory be blessed.
