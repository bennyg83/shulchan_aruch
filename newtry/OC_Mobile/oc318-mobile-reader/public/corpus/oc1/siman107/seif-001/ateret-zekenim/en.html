If he is uncertain, he must stipulate: If I have not prayed, let this prayer count as my obligation; and if I have already prayed, let it be a voluntary prayer. He may then pray either privately or with the congregation. If he remembers during the prayer that he already prayed, then, if he made this stipulation at the outset, he completes it as a voluntary prayer and introduces a new element from that point onward, so that the entire prayer is voluntary. This excludes the Mussaf prayer, which may not be offered voluntarily because the Mussaf offering is mentioned in it. According to this, if he wishes to offer a voluntary prayer without mentioning the Mussaf offering, he may do so. It would likewise follow that if he wishes to mention the daily offering in Shacharis, he may not pray it voluntarily. This is stated in the responsa of Maharam of Padua, siman 19. The author of Ateres Zekeinim intended to accord with the view of the Magen Avraham.
