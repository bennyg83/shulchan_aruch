(Siman kaf gimmel.) "He stops, as implied..."—for the Rambam (Hilchos Tefillah ch. 10) holds that for Maariv one does not stop, for the reason one must stop is that since he began to pray with intent of obligation, how can he finish with intent of nedavah and half the prayer be as obligation when he is not obligated? Not so the evening prayer, which is optional—even initially, when he thought he had not yet prayed, it was only as nedavah, for it is optional. But the Ra'avad disagrees, since now it has been established as obligatory—it has the law of another prayer.
