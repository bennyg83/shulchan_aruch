The Baal HaMaor and Rabbeinu Yonah already challenged this from what is stated in Shekalim, “six were for voluntary offerings,” and in Zevachim 103a and Temurah 20a and 20b, that surplus funds go toward communal voluntary offerings, and in Toras Kohanim at the beginning of Vayikra, “your offering” teaches that a communal voluntary offering may be brought.
