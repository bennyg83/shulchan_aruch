2) There, he returns and prays, and makes a condition: if he did not pray, let this be for his obligation, and if he prayed, let this be for his voluntary offering (Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba; Levush; Etz Yosef there; Magen Avraham, note 1; Atzei Etz, paragraph 1; Razah, paragraph 1). And that which Aruch HaShulchan wrote in paragraph 2—that if he is in doubt whether he prayed and the time passed that is completed in the prayer adjacent to it and he makes a condition—it appears that for us, who do not pray voluntarily, as will be explained below in seif 4, he should also not do this; for it suffices for us if the one in doubt prays conditionally within the time and not after the time has passed. And they said, do not add to this; and see in the paragraphs afterward and below, paragraph 20. And furthermore, he himself in siman 108, paragraph 2, in Aruch HaShulchan, returned and was in doubt about this and did not decide the matter, as cited there. Therefore what we wrote is the main point.
