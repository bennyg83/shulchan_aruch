And if he innovated even in one blessing, etc. — From this wording it implies that even in the blessing "Who hears prayer," and not as it would seem from what is brought in the Tur; see there. And nevertheless it appears that even per the Shulchan Aruch we require that this request not be habitual for him every day at the time of obligatory prayer; for behold the reason that through innovation one knows it is voluntary and not obligatory.
