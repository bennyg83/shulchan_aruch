Seif 4 — One who wishes, etc.: As was written, "why do I need," etc.; and as is written for the one who says in chapter 2, "one should always estimate," etc.; and even though he explained in Avot that this refers to obligatory prayer, and as is written in chapter 4, seif 2, "one must have intention," etc.—so that one not be nullified from acts of kindness; not so regarding voluntary prayer.
