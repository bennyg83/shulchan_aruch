Taz, seif katan 1 Rav Hai maintains that even a voluntary prayer requires a new element. Until now the Taz explained two approaches, that of Rabbeinu Yonah and that of the Rif. He now begins to explain a third approach, that of Rav Hai. His statement that even a voluntary prayer requires a new element refers to Rabbi Yochanan’s dictum and means specifically with a new element. His statement that a new element does not help for an obligatory prayer refers to the ruling that one stops even in the middle of a blessing, which applies even when he is able to introduce a new element.
