(In Taz, siman kaf alef.) And Rabbeinu Hai holds even for nedavah. Until now the Taz explained two views—the view of R' Yitzchak and the view of Rif—and now he begins to explain a third view, that of Rav Hai. And that which he wrote that even for nedavah one needs innovation—this is the statement of R' Yochanan, and it deals specifically with innovation. And that which he wrote but for obligation innovation does not help—this is the statement of one who stops even in the middle of a blessing, even if he could innovate.
