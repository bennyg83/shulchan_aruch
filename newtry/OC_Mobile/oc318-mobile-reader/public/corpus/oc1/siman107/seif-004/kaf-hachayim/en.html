There, and one need not innovate anything—for you have no greater innovation than this, that one prays out of doubt; and it may be that the entire prayer is obligatory, lest perhaps he had not yet prayed. Beit Yosef and Levush, seif 3, and Rosh there. However, some say that even if he returns and prays because of doubt he must innovate something in it—Bach, according to Rav Hai Gaon—and it is proper to heed his words, for there is no concern at all if one adds; for even in an obligatory prayer, if one wishes to add one may add, as is stated below at the beginning of siman 119—see there; and so too Chasdei David, ot 1.
