8) “They are prohibited from putting on, etc.” The Shach in Yoreh De’ah, siman 334, se’if katan 11, rules that an excommunicated person is permitted to wear tefillin. It is surprising that he does not mention the words of the Shulchan Aruch and Levush here, who rule that it is prohibited. As a matter of practical law, however, Lechem Chamudot, page 100, also indicates that one should permit tefillin both to an excommunicated person and to a metzora. The Beit Yosef there and Knesset HaGedolah likewise imply this.
