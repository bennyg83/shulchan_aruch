(38) One who is under a ban and a metzora, etc.: See Lechem Chamudos and Shach in Yoreh Deah siman 334, seif 2, and the other later authorities, who rule the opposite. Pri Megadim wrote that they should put them on without a blessing, but from the Biur of the Gra it appears at first glance that they must also recite the blessing.
