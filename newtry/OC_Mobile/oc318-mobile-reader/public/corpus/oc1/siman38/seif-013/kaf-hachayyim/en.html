14) Knesses HaGedolah 2 calls this opinion astonishing: should the mitzvah of tefillin be canceled because of thoughts? The Yerushalmi's definition of a clean body as clean thoughts may concern wearing tefillin all day like Elisha Baal Kenafayim, not exemption from tefillin during Krias Shema. Shaliach Tzibbur, page 40d; Yafeh LaLev 3. Yafeh LaLev questions why Beis Oved omitted this dissent. There is no difficulty: the dissent contradicts Maran and Rema and therefore was omitted. Beis Yosef's practical counsel is to persuade such people, draw their hearts toward fear of Heaven, remove vanity from them, and have them accept the heavenly yoke. See paragraphs 12–13.
