(22) And all members of the wedding party, etc.: Specifically in the place of the chuppah, for there drunkenness and levity are common.
(23) Are exempt: In Responsa Rama, siman 132, he wrote that nowadays, since even the chasan is obligated in Krias Shema and prayer, as written in siman 70, automatically the chasan and all members of the wedding party are also obligated in tefillin; see there. See Magen Avraham, who questions his ruling and inclines to rule like the Shulchan Aruch, that they are exempt from tefillin at the time of the feast. Also regarding prayer, one who is lenient like Rashi to exempt them at the time of the feast has not lost, if the chasan is reclining with them, for then it is a mitzvah according to Rashi. But from the words of the Gra in his Biur it appears that one should not be lenient regarding prayer, as written in Beis Yosef in siman 232. See Olas Tamid and Birkei Yosef, who wrote that the widespread custom is like the Rama, that the chasan and his entire group recite, put on tefillin, and pray from the first day until the seventh day.
