A groom, etc. In Responsa Rama, siman 132, he wrote that nowadays, since they are obligated in Krias Shema and prayer, they are also obligated in tefillin, for otherwise it is as though one testifies falsely about himself. This requires study, for according to the Rosh the teaching that the attendants are obligated concerns Krias Shema, while in tefillin they are exempt, and we do not say that he testifies falsely about himself since he is exempt, just as on Shabbosos, Yamim Tovim, and nights. It must be said that in the Gemara they were obligated only in the first verse of Shema, so it is not like false testimony, unlike our practice.
