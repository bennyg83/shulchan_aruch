A groom and his attendants are exempt from tefillin—specifically in the house of the chuppah where they drink and become intoxicated; but if they go in another house they are obligated. And this is when it is not the time of Keriat Shema; but at the time of Keriat Shema, since the groom is obligated in Keriat Shema, he is also obligated in tefillin so that he should not be like one who testifies false testimony about himself (responsum of Maharar Rama, siman 132).
