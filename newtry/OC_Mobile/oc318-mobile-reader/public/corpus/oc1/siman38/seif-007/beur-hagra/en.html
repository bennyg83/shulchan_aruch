Seif 7, a chasan, etc. See Tosafot there, end of s.v. chasan, and it is only regarding Krias Shema, etc. So explained Rashi and the other poskim, and he ruled like Rabbi Sheila, as I wrote above in siman 70, seif 3, for an anonymous Mishnah follows him. In Shulchan Aruch he omitted the law of prayer, and his reason is as Tosafot and the Rosh wrote, that their exemption is not because one engaged in a mitzvah is exempt from another mitzvah, for if so they would be exempt even from Krias Shema. Rather, it is because they cannot concentrate, unlike nowadays, as I wrote above in siman 70, seif 3.
