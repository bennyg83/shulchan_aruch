(7) [Seif 3] Women and slaves are exempt from tefillin because it is a positive time-bound commandment; and although we rule like R. Akiva who said night is the time for tefillin, nevertheless Shabbat and Yom Tov are not time for tefillin, and it is a positive time-bound commandment—Rosh in Hilchot Ketanot, Laws of Tefillin, siman 29; Beit Yosef; Levush; Taz, note 1; and see the Rosh there, who wrote further other reasons.
