A bridegroom and his attendants. Responsa Rama, siman 132, writes that nowadays a bridegroom is obligated in prayer and tefillin. This has become the widespread practice in these countries: the bridegroom and all members of the wedding party recite Krias Shema and pray. Magen Avraham writes: "It appears to me that one who is lenient has not lost out, if the bridegroom is reclining with them." See there and Knesses HaGedolah.
