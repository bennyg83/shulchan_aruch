Groom and attendants. In responsum Rama, siman 132, he wrote that a groom nowadays is obligated in prayer and tefillin, and so the custom has spread in our lands that the groom and all members of the wedding party read and pray; and the Magen Avraham wrote, and in our humble opinion one who is lenient has not lost if the groom reclines among them—end of quote, and in Kitzur Shulchan Aruch.
