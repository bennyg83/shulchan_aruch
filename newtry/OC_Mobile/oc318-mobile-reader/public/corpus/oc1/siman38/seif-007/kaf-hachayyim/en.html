7) [Se'if 3] Women and slaves are exempt from tefillin because it is a time-bound positive commandment. Although we rule like Rabbi Akiva that nighttime is a time for tefillin, Shabbos and Yom Tov are not times for tefillin, so it remains time-bound. Rosh, Halachos Ketanos, Hilchos Tefillin 29; Beis Yosef; Levush; Taz 1. See Rosh there for additional reasons.
