(37) Tefillin come first: Because it is a mitzvah of one's body, and furthermore the holiness of tefillin is higher than the holiness of a mezuzah, as above in siman 32, seif 8. However, for us, who put them on only at the time of Krias Shema and prayer, if borrowing is possible, a mezuzah comes first, for it is impossible through borrowing, Magen Avraham.
