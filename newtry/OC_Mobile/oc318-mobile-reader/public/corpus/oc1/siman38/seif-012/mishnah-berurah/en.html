(37) Tefillin take precedence—for it is a mitzvah of the body, and also the sanctity of tefillin is higher than that of a mezuzah, as above in siman 32, seif 8. However, for us who wear them only at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer, if possible to ask, the mezuzah precedes, for it cannot be asked—[Magen Avraham].
