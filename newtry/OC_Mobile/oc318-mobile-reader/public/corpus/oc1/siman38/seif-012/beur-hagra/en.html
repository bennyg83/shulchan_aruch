Seif 12, if he needed, etc. Yerushalmi at the end of Megillah: tefillin and mezuzah, which comes first? Shmuel said: mezuzah comes first. Rav Huna said: tefillin come first. What is Rav Huna's reason? For they are practiced by those who travel the seas and those who go in deserts. The Rosh wrote that it is logical that the halacha follows Rav Huna, for a mitzvah of one's body is preferable, and he also explains his reason there. Although we say there that the Mishnah supports Shmuel, since worn-out tefillin may be made into a mezuzah, but a worn-out mezuzah may not be made into tefillin, because one ascends in holiness, etc., that passage is not according to the halacha, for in the Gemara, Menachos chapter 3, we say the opposite, and it is support for the opposite view.
