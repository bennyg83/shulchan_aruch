Seif 12—he needed, etc. Yerushalmi at the end of Megillah: tefillin and mezuzah—which takes precedence? Shmuel said mezuzah takes precedence; Rabbi Huna said tefillin take precedence. What is Rabbi Huna's reason? For thus they practice among those who interpret days and those who walk in deserts. And the Rosh wrote, and it is reasonable that the halachah follows Rabbi Huna, for a mitzvah of the body takes precedence; and he also explains the reason there. And although we say there the mishnah supports Shmuel—worn-out tefillin they make into a mezuzah; a worn-out mezuzah they do not make into tefillin, because we ascend in holiness, etc.—that is not the halachah, for in the Gemara, chapter 3 of Menachot, they say the opposite, and it is a support for the opposite.
