On tefillin—Av Beit Din. And see in Chavat Yair who wrote that even if he never wore tefillin he does not recite Shehecheyanu, since it is regular and frequent (see Yoreh De'ah, siman 28, and Taz there); and likewise at the conclusion of a book, and at a son's wedding and a first grandson's birth, one does not recite Shehecheyanu; and at the birth of a male child some recite Shehecheyanu, and therefore one does not bless at circumcision, and even at the birth of a firstborn son, as Shakh wrote in Yoreh De'ah, siman 265—end of quote; and see above in Taz at the end of siman 22.
