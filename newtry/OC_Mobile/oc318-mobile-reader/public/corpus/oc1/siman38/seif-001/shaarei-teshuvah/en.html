From tefillin. See Be'er Heitev. See Chavot Yair, who writes that even one who has never put on tefillin should not recite Shehecheyanu, because the mitzvah is regular and frequent. See Yoreh De'ah, siman 28, and Tevuot Shor there. Likewise, one should not recite Shehecheyanu upon completing a book, at his son's wedding, or upon the birth of his first grandson. Upon the birth of a male child, some recite Shehecheyanu; it is therefore not recited at the circumcision, even for a firstborn son, as Shach writes in Yoreh De'ah, siman 265. These are his words. See above in Turei Zahav at the end of siman 22.
