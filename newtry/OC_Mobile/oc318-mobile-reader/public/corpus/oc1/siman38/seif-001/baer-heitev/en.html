On tefillin. One who canceled the mitzvah of tefillin due to illness and several days passed during which he did not put them on—when he returns and puts them on, must he recite Shehecheyanu or not? He ruled in responsum Chavat Yair, siman 237, that he need not recite Shehecheyanu, since it is not a mitzvah that comes from time to time—end of quote.

Distress. Even if he walks in markets and streets he is exempt. And the Magen Avraham wrote that if he wishes to be strict at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer he is permitted, as written in seif 2.
