From tefillin. Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 237, rules that if illness prevented a person from fulfilling the mitzvah of tefillin and thirty days passed without his putting them on, he need not recite Shehecheyanu when he resumes wearing them, because this is not a mitzvah that recurs only at intervals.
