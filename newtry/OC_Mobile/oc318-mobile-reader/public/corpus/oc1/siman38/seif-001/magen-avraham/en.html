Even if he has no pain. Even if he walks in markets and streets he is exempt. It appears from this that he may not be stringent upon himself, for if it were permitted Rami bar Tamri would not have exempted himself from it. So too it appears from Tosafot in Kesubos, who wrote that Rebbe conducted himself with excessive sanctity and knew well how to be careful; this implies that for other people it is forbidden. Nevertheless, it seems to me that during Krias Shema and prayer it is permitted, as written in seif 2. One whose heart is exposed should not put on tefillin; see siman 91 and the end of siman 74.
