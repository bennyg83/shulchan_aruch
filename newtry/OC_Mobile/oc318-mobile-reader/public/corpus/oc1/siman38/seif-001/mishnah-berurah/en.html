(1) Intestinal illness: The same applies to one who drank a laxative beverage.
(2) Is exempt: Because tefillin require a clean body.
(3) From tefillin: One who neglected the mitzvah of tefillin because of illness, and thirty days passed without his putting them on, when he returns and puts them on he need not recite Shehecheyanu.
(4) Distress: Even if he is walking in markets and streets, he is exempt, and it is forbidden for him to be stringent upon himself in this, except at the time of Krias Shema and prayer if he knows that he can maintain himself with a clean body, as written below in seif 2.
(5) Is exempt: Lest, because of the distress, he become distracted from them. If he wishes to be stringent upon himself, he may. One whose heart is exposed, meaning that he is walking only in trousers and is bare above, which does not include a case where the collar is open, should not put on tefillin. Although he may recite a blessing when his ervah is covered, as is evident in siman 74, nevertheless it is not proper that his heart be exposed while he puts on tefillin.
