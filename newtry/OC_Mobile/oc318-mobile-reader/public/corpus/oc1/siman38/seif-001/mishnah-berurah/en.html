(1) Intestinal illness—and the same applies to one who drank a laxative drink.

(2) Exempt—because tefillin require a clean body.

(3) From tefillin—one who canceled the mitzvah of tefillin due to illness and thirty days passed during which he did not wear them: when he returns and wears them, he need not recite Shehecheyanu.

(4) Pain—even if he walks in markets and streets he is exempt, and it is forbidden for him to be strict upon himself in this except at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer if he knows he can maintain a clean body, as written below in seif 2.

(5) Exempt—lest due to distress he divert his attention from them. If he wishes to be strict upon himself, he may. One whose heart is exposed—that is, he goes in trousers alone and above that he is bare (and this does not include an open collar)—should not put on tefillin, for although he may recite a blessing since his hair is covered, as shown in siman 74, nevertheless it is not proper that his heart be exposed while wearing tefillin.
