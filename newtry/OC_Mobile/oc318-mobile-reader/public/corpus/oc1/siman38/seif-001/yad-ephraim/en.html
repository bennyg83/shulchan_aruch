In Magen Avraham, note 9, and the Rav Beit Yosef followed the Yerushalmi and wrote that the Rambam followed the Yerushalmi that one is obligated in tefillin, which is from the Torah—on this he wrote: one could say the Yerushalmi deals when it is possible, etc.; if so, it should not have omitted "when impossible" but exempt; rather, the Rambam holds one is exempt in every case and relied on what is written in the laws of Keriat Shema, as stated.
