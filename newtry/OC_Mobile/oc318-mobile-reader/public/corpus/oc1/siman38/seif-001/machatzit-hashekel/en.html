(Note 1) Even, etc.—as in Chullin 110: they say there, Rami bar Tamri, who is Rami bar Dikkulei, happened to come to Sura on the eve of Yom Kippur, etc.; they brought him before Rav Chisda, etc.; they saw he did not put on tefillin. "If so, why did you not put on tefillin?" He said: "Intestinal illness," etc.

One whose heart is bare, etc.—meaning: although his hair is covered, nevertheless he shall not put on tefillin.

And see siman 91 and siman 74, where it is explained that for Keriat Shema, if his nakedness is covered even though his heart is exposed he may recite Keriat Shema, but forbidden to pray in such a state—and see there the reason; and Shaarei Chayyim compares tefillin to prayer.
