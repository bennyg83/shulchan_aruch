Seif katan 1, even, etc. So it appears in Chullin, folio 110, where we say there that Rami bar Tamri, who is Rami bar Dikulei, came to Sura on the eve of Yom Kippur, etc. They brought him before Rav Chisda, etc. He saw that he was not wearing tefillin. If so, why are you not wearing tefillin? He said to him: I have an intestinal illness, etc.
One whose heart is exposed, etc. That is to say, even though his ervah is covered, nevertheless he should not put on tefillin.
See siman 91 and the end of siman 74, where it is explained that regarding Krias Shema, if his ervah is covered, even though his heart is exposed, he may recite Krias Shema, but he may not pray in such a manner. See there for the reason. The Sefer Chasidim compares tefillin to prayer.
