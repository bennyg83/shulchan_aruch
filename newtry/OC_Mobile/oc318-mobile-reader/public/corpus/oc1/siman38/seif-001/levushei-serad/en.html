Magen Avraham, note 1 permitted, as in seif 2—meaning there it is explained that if he can maintain himself at Keriat Shema time he puts on; but we do not rely on what seems to him that he can maintain himself, for Tosafot wrote Rabbi knew well to be careful, but for another person with intestinal illness we do not rely on his carefulness; and in seif 2 it deals with one who is healthy, therefore we rely on his carefulness. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote: only at Keriat Shema and prayer time, which is a short time, we may rely on carefulness of any person even with intestinal illness; and on this Magen Avraham wrote as in seif 2—and it is an abbreviation as I will write, hinting at what Magen Avraham wrote in note 2 in the name of Menachem Azariah, and there it deals with intestinal illness as I will copy there.
