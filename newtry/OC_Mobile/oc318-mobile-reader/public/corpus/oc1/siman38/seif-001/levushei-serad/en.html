Magen Avraham, paragraph 1: It is permitted, as stated in se'if 2. If he can restrain himself for the duration of Krias Shema, he should put on tefillin. One might have thought that a person suffering from an intestinal ailment is worse because his restraint is unreliable, as Tosafos says that Rebbe was exceptionally careful, whereas se'if 2 concerns a healthy person. Magen Avraham therefore teaches that for the brief period of Krias Shema and prayer we rely upon any person, even one with an intestinal ailment. His words "as I will write in se'if 2" refer to his own paragraph 2, where he cites Rema MiFano concerning an intestinal ailment.
