(1) [Seif 1] Intestinal illness—exempt from tefillin. Hagahah: even if he has no pain; even walking in markets and streets—exempt; forbidden to be strict on himself, only at Keriat Shema and prayer permitted if he can maintain a clean body, as in seif 2—Magen Avraham, note 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, letter 2; Razah, letter 1; Peri Megadim, Orach Chaim, letter 1; Siddur Beit Yaakov on who is obligated in tefillin, letter 1; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 10, letter 23. But in responsum of Menachem Azariah, siman 60, it seems even not at Keriat Shema and prayer he may put on when he has a time of fitness; and at his distress he removes them; and if compelled and did not remove hand-tefillin he did not transgress—Avnei Nezer, letter 1; and see below, letter 5.
