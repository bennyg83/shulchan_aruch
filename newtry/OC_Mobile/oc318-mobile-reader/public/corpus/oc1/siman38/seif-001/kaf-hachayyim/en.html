A) [Section 1] An intestinal patient is exempt from tefillin, proof that he does not even have grief and even walks in the markets and streets is exempt. And it is forbidden to be harsh on oneself, only during Kash and Tefal, it is permissible if one can present oneself in a clean body as in Sai' 2, MA SKA, Sob letter 2, Raza letter 1, Parmag 11 O' 1, Siddur B.I.
