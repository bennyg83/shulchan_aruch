4) “From then onward, etc.” This means on the second day, after sunrise, as stated in Yoreh De’ah. The Tur’s words must be explained accordingly. The Rosh and the Tur likewise explain the Rif’s words this way, as the Bach cites there; examine carefully. Olat Tamid and the Magen Avraham questioned this without cause; see there, for I have written briefly. The Magen Avraham writes that the mourner does not put them on until the new comforters leave; examine carefully. The Bach and Shach there rule that on the second day it is permitted even before sunrise. I will explain this in my work on Yoreh De’ah.
