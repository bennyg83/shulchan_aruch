Seif 4—one who puts on, etc. Kol Bo wrote in the name of the Rambam that it is forbidden to put on; and as in the Hagahah; but Beit Yosef disagreed and wrote one must certainly be careful, but one does not exempt him from tefillin for this; and as in Sukkah 26b, "Let us say," etc., and Rashi, s.v. she-ragilin, etc., and he strained himself to answer—and see Magen Avraham.
