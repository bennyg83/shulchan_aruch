Seif 4, one who wears, etc. The Kol Bo wrote in the name of the Maharam that it is forbidden to wear them, as written in the gloss. But the Beis Yosef disagreed with him and wrote that one certainly must be careful, but he should not be exempted from tefillin because of this. This is as stated in Sukkah 26b, shall we say, etc.; see Rashi, s.v. who are accustomed, etc. He forced himself to answer it. See Magen Avraham.
