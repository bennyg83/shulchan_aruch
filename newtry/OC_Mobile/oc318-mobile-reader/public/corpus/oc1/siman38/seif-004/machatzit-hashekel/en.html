Seif katan 4, from improper thoughts, etc. See there in Beis Yosef what he wrote, that he asked: from where did the questioner derive that he holds that a baal keri is forbidden, etc.? Perhaps the reason is because of improper thought. He answered that the questioner held that at the time of Krias Shema they can be careful not to think improper thoughts, and therefore he understood that what was taught, 'because they are accustomed to impurity,' means an old impurity, for presumably they had already seen keri. On this the Magen Avraham has difficulty: granted, the questioner initially thought that at the time of Krias Shema they could certainly be careful from improper thought. But according to the answerer, who says that the Mishnah is concerned for habitual conduct, end quote, he holds that even at the time of Krias Shema there is concern for improper thought, since habitual conduct is impossible without prior improper thought. If so, he should have said briefly that the reason is because of improper thought.
