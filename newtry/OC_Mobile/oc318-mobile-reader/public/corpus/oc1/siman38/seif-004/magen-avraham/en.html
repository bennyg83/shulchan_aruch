From thoughts. The Beit Yosef raised a difficulty from Sukkah, where children always remove tefillin because impurity is common for them, and Rashi explains that thoughts are common for them. The Gemara answers that it speaks of children whose wives are with them, lest they come to habitual intimacy. It must be said that because of a mere concern we do not forbid putting on tefillin; only when he certainly knows that he cannot avoid such thoughts is it forbidden to put them on.
