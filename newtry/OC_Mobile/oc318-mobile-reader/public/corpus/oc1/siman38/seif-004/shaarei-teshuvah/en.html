First—Av Beit Din. What he wrote regarding a close report: Shlah, in the name of Sefer Shevet Yehudah of Mahari Ayash in Yoreh De'ah, that the poskim did not reveal their view on the second day before sunrise; there are arguments on both sides, and they practice accordingly; I delayed on the second day from putting on tefillin until sunrise to escape doubt—see there. If his relative died on a festival, or he heard a distant report on a festival when he does not observe mourning and begins counting after the festival—Keneset HaGedolah wrote it is forbidden to wear tefillin on the first day; Birkei Yosef disagreed and wrote he is obligated; and even if it were doubtful, one does not nullify such a great mitzvah out of doubt—so too in Beit Lechem Yehudah, Yoreh De'ah, siman 388, disagreeing with Keneset HaGedolah—see there. Also what Av Beit Din wrote in the name of Maharitz, disagreeing with Birkei Yosef and Mishnah Berurah, and concluding: in my humble opinion, the day of burial, even if not the day of death, is forbidden for tefillin—and see there that he wrote this is implied from Taz and Mishnah Berurah, and Elyah Rabbah there, and in Shlah, also in the name of Sefer Derekh HaMelekh, not like Maharitz.
