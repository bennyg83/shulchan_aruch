First. See Be'er Heitev. Regarding a recent report of a death, Shaliach Tzibbur writes in the name of Shevet Yehudah by Rabbi Yehudah Ayash on Yoreh De'ah that the halakhic authorities did not reveal their view about the second day before sunrise. There are grounds for both positions. He personally acted by waiting on the second day and not putting on tefillin until sunrise, thereby avoiding the doubt; see there. If a relative died during a festival, or one heard a distant report during the festival, so that he does not observe mourning then and begins counting after the festival, Knesset HaGedolah writes that he is forbidden to put on tefillin on the first day. Birkei Yosef disagrees and writes that he is obligated; even if the matter were uncertain, one should not suspend so great a mitzvah because of a doubt. Beit Lechem Yehudah, in Yoreh De'ah, siman 388, writes likewise and disagrees with Knesset HaGedolah; see there. Birkei Yosef and Machazik Berachah also disagree with the ruling cited by Be'er Heitev in the name of Maharit Tzahalon. Machazik Berachah concludes: “In summary, it appears to me that on the day of burial, even when it is not the day of death, tefillin are forbidden.” See there, where he writes that this is also demonstrated by Turei Zahav and Magen Avraham. Eliyahu Rabbah rules likewise there. Shaliach Tzibbur also writes in the name of Derech HaMelech, contrary to Maharit Tzahalon.
