Scribes who write tefillin and mezuzot, their merchants, the merchants who supply those merchants, and all who are engaged in sacred work are exempt from putting on tefillin throughout the day, except at the time of Shema and prayer. {Rama: If they must perform their work at the time of Shema and prayer, they are then exempt from Shema, prayer, and tefillin, because one who is engaged in a mitzvah is exempt from another mitzvah when pursuing the other mitzvah would require effort. But if he can perform both together without effort, he should perform both. [Hagahot Ashiri in the name of Or Zarua, and Ran, chapter HaYashen]}
