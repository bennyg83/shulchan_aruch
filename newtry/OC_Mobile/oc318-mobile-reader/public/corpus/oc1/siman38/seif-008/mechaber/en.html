Scribes of tefillin and mezuzot, their agents, agents of their agents, and all who engage in heavenly work are exempt from putting on tefillin all day except at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer.If they needed to do their work at the time of Keriat Shema and prayer, then they are exempt from Keriat Shema, prayer, and tefillin—for one engaged in a mitzvah is exempt from another mitzvah if he must trouble himself for the other; but if he can do both at once without extra trouble, he should do both. [Hagahot Asheri in the name of Rashba; Ran, chapter HaYashen].
