(24) And their merchants: Even though they profit from this. This is specifically if their primary intent is to make them available to sell to one who needs them. But if their primary intent is only to profit, this is not called being engaged in a mitzvah, Magen Avraham.
(25) And the merchants of their merchants: Those who buy from the buyers to sell little by little.
(26) Need, etc.: For example, if a buyer comes who wants to purchase sifrei Torah, tefillin, or mezuzos, and the buyer wants to set out now by sea or in a caravan, and he cannot wait until the scribe or merchant fulfills one mitzvah that has come to his hand, such as putting on tefillin or Krias Shema, and the same applies to all other mitzvos. Therefore it is permitted to write and sell to this person, even though through this the time of the mitzvah will pass, Levush. See Biur Halacha.
(27) Then they are exempt, etc.: This speaks where he already began writing before the time of Krias Shema arrived. But once the time has arrived, it is forbidden to begin writing, as written in siman 72, seif 2; so wrote the Magen Avraham. However, according to what we initially described in the name of the Levush, where the buyer needs to travel by sea or in a caravan and cannot wait until the scribe fulfills Krias Shema and prayer, it is obvious that according to all opinions the scribe may do his work for him if he estimates that time will remain for him to recite. If he can recite one paragraph first, he should recite it.
(28) For anyone engaged, etc.: Specifically one who is engaged in a mitzvah, such as while he is wearing the tefillin or occupied with the needs of a lost object, such as spreading it out for its need or returning it to its owner, and the like. But while he is fulfilling a mitzvah, such as when he is already wearing tefillin or guarding a lost object that is already placed in his box, and the like, although he is fulfilling a mitzvah, he is not engaged in a mitzvah and is not thereby exempt from another mitzvah. See Biur Halacha, where I explained this further.
(29) If he needs, etc.: That is to say, even if through this the first mitzvah will not be nullified, and even if the second mitzvah is greater, since he already began to be engaged in the first one, Or Zarua and Ritva.
