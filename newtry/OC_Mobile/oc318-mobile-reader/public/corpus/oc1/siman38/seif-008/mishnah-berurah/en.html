(24) And their agents—even if they profit from it; and specifically if their main intent is to supply them to sell to whoever needs them; but if their main intent is only to profit, it is not called engaging in a mitzvah—[Magen Avraham].

(25) And agents of their agents—those who buy from the buyers to resell step by step.

(26) They needed, etc.—for example, a buyer happened upon him who wants to buy sacred scrolls and the buyer wishes to set out to sea or on a caravan now and cannot wait until the scribe or agent fulfills one mitzvah that came his way, such as putting on tefillin or Keriat Shema; and the same for all other mitzvot; therefore he may write and sell to him even if thereby the time for the mitzvah will pass—[Levush]; and see in Biur Halachah.

(27) Then they are exempt, etc.—and this applies when he already began writing before the time of Keriat Shema arrived; but once the time arrived it is forbidden to begin writing, as in siman 72, seif 2—so the Magen Avraham; however, according to what we described initially in the name of the Levush—that the buyer must cross the sea or caravan and cannot wait until the scribe fulfills Keriat Shema and prayer—it is clear that all agree the scribe may do his work for him if he estimates time will remain to read; and if he can read one section beforehand, he should read.

(28) For one engaged in a mitzvah, etc.—and specifically while engaged in the mitzvah, such as while wearing tefillin or while working on restoring a lost object, such as spreading it out for its need or returning it to its owner and the like; but while fulfilling a mitzvah, such as when already wearing tefillin or guarding a lost object already placed in his box and the like—even though he fulfills a mitzvah, he is not "engaged" in a mitzvah and is not exempt from another mitzvah thereby; and see in Biur Halachah where I explained further.

(29) If he must, etc.—meaning even if thereby the first mitzvah will not be nullified, and even if the second mitzvah is greater, since he already began the first—[Or Zaru'a; Ritva].
