(3) A person who hears and does not speak puts on tefillin; and likewise one who speaks and does not hear—Shaarei Chayyim there; Kenesset ha-Gedolah there; and he wrote that from his words it appears a deaf-mute, of whom the Sages spoke, who does not hear and does not speak, does not put on tefillin; and I saw in Constantinople some who put on tefillin and the Sages did not object. It seems Sefer Chasidim states an obligation—that those are obligated in tefillin; but a deaf-mute, of whom the Sages spoke, is exempt from tefillin since exempt from all mitzvot; nevertheless if he wishes to put them on we do not object—end quote; and so in Shakhnei ha-Gedolah in this siman, letter 4; and so in Or Tzaddikim. Avnei Nezer, letter 8; Shlah, page 40, 14; Shetilim Zeitim, letter 2.
