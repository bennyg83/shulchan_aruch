c) A person who hears and does not speak shall put on a tefillin, and so does one who speaks and does not hear, 36 ibid., and brought it as 13 ibid., and he wrote that his words are seen in the Harsh that the words of sages who do not hear and do not speak do not put on a tefillin, and I saw in Kustantina some of them who put on tefillin and did not wipe them with sages. And he saw that a number of Hassidim were brought, saying that they must wear tefillin, but a deaf ear, which the Sages spoke of, is exempt from tefillin, since he is exempt from all unleavened bread, and who, if he wants to put them down, does not have a brain in his hand, and so on.
