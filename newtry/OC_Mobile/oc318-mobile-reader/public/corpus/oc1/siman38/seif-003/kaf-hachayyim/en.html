3) A person who hears but cannot speak should don tefillin, and so should one who speaks but cannot hear. Sefer Chasidim there, cited by Knesses HaGedolah. Knesses HaGedolah writes that Sefer Chasidim means these people are obligated, whereas the deaf-mute discussed by Chazal (who neither hears nor speaks) is exempt from tefillin because he is exempt from all mitzvos. Nevertheless, if he wishes to don them, he is not protested; Knesses HaGedolah saw several such people in Constantinople wearing tefillin without rabbinic protest. So also write Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah 4; Olas Tamid; Eliyah Rabbah 8; Shaliach Tzibbur, page 40d; and Shesilei Zeitim 2.
