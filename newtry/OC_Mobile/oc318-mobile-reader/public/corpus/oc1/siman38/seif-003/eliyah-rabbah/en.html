[3] On the first day, etc.—and the same on the day of report of a close relative; so wrote Taz and Shakh, Yoreh De'ah, siman 388. If he heard afterward that he prayed the evening prayer—for we hold at the end of siman 375 that that day does not count—one may be uncertain whether he puts on tefillin on the second day, which is called "first" for him; and Taz in Yoreh De'ah, siman 402, seif 11, seems to me he puts on tefillin; and it appears on the burial day they do not put on even if he died yesterday, like close report—for they are like the Rabbis' view, like burial day when he died yesterday. In novellae of R. Yom Tov Tzahalon he wrote specifically on the day of death, which is burial day, they do not put on, for it is biblical; and the apparent view in my humble opinion is the main point; and so in responsum Mas'at Binyamin, siman 82, and Taz, siman 398—see there; and so Taz here somewhat. It seems one who died and was buried on Yom Tov puts on tefillin on the first day after Yom Tov, for he already practiced private matters; comforters also on Yom Tov, as below, siman 548. One who became a mourner on chol ha-moed—Magen Avraham, siman 548, wrote he puts on tefillin, for mourning did not take hold on chol ha-moed.
