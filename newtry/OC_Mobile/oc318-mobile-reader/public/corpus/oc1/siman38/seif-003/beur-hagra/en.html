Seif 3, and if the women, etc. For they are not careful to guard themselves with a clean body there. See Tosafot, Eruvin 96a, s.v. Michal, citing the Pesikta, etc., and it appears to explain, etc. Although the Gemara said that they did not protest, etc., and we rule like the opinion that optional leaning is permitted, as I wrote above in siman 17, the Beis Yosef was concerned for the words of the Pesikta. So too in the Yerushalmi, Berachos chapter 2: there we learned that women, slaves, and minors are exempt, etc.; but Michal bas Kushi, etc. Rabbi Chizkiyah said in the name of Rabbi Abbahu: the Sages protested against Michal bas Shaul, and the wife of Yonah was made to return. One may say that the Gemara also maintains this, but needed to cite it only according to the opinion that women are obligated, and rejected it because that opinion holds that it is optional and permitted. Rabbi Abbahu, however, maintains that although optional leaning is permitted, nevertheless here they are forbidden, as stated above.
