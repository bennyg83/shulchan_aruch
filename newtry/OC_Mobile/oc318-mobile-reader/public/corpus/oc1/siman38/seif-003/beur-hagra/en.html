Seif 3—and if women, etc.—that they are not diligent to keep a clean body—there; and Tosafot, Eruvin 96a, s.v. Michal, in Pesikta, etc., and it seems to explain, etc.; and although in the Gemara they said they did not object, etc., and we rule like the one who says they may rely on permission, as above, siman 17—it is difficult according to Pesikta, Beit Yosef. And so in Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Berakhot: there we learned, women, slaves, and minors are exempt, etc.; yet Michal daughter of Saul, etc.; R. Chizkiya in the name of R. Avahu: Michal bat Shaul—the Sages objected to her; the wife of Yonah was restored, etc.; and one could say the Gemara also holds thus, only they did not need to bring it except against the one who says women are obligated, and they rejected him because he holds like the one who says permission, and they are permitted; but R. Avahu holds: although they may rely on permission, nevertheless they are forbidden, as above.
