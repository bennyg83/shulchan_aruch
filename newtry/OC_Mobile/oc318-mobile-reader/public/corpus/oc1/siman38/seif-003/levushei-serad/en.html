Note 2: knows he cannot maintain—likewise Menachem Azarya, siman 60, intestinal illness: cannot guard from passing gas except when removing head tefillin; if he must also remove hand tefillin he will sometimes miss tefillin entirely; he concludes: although all guarding rules are strict for head like hand—see Magen Avraham, seif 3, Maggid—nevertheless by law hand is lighter than head; therefore for intestinal illness, being compelled, we permit wearing tefillin, and in distress he removes them; if forced before removing hand tefillin and could not be careful, no prohibition after the fact due to compulsion—see there at length.
