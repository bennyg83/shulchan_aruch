Paragraph 2: He knows that he cannot restrain himself. Responsa Rema MiFano, siman 60, discusses one with an intestinal ailment who can restrain himself only long enough to remove the head-tefillin. If he were also required to remove the arm-tefillin, the mitzvah might be entirely lost. Although the safeguards for arm- and head-tefillin are generally equally stringent, as Magen Avraham writes in siman 43, paragraph 3, the law of the arm-tefillin is lighter here. He may therefore put them on and remove them when distress begins; if he is unavoidably overcome before removing them, there is no after-the-fact prohibition because he was compelled.
