(10) Women—and a tumtum and an androgynous are obligated in tefillin out of doubt, like all mitzvot.

(11) That the time caused—because Shabbat and Yom Tov are not a time for tefillin.

(12) The women—see in the Peri Megadim that the same applies to slaves; and see in Tosafot Shabbat who wrote explicitly otherwise, and in Tosafot Yerushalmi.

(13) We object to them—because they require a clean body and women are not diligent to be careful.
