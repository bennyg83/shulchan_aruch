(10) Women: A tumtum and androgyne are obligated in tefillin because of doubt, as with all mitzvos.
(11) Caused by time: For Shabbos and Yom Tov are not a time for tefillin.
(12) The women: See Pri Megadim, who says that the same applies to slaves. See Tosafot Shabbos, who wrote explicitly the opposite, and see the sefer Tosafot Yerushalayim.
(13) We protest against them: Because they require a clean body, and women are not diligent to be careful.
