We object—Magen Avraham, note 3; and it seems slaves are not objected to because they can be careful; and this is because they are also not diligent, for they are exempt; and they are even worse than women, for a woman is believed regarding prohibitions and counted herself, but slaves in general are not—Yoreh De'ah, siman 1.
