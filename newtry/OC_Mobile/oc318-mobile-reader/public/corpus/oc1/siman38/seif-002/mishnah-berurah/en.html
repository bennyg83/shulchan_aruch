(6) One who is certain: This excludes where he does not know with certainty; certainly one should not cancel even communal prayer because of this.
(7) To pray: Specifically prayer, because he is like one standing before the King, and it is disgraceful to stand in prayer with the expectation that he will interrupt in the middle until the odor dissipates. But Krias Shema and its blessings are permitted, though without tefillin.
(8) At the time of Krias Shema: See below in siman 80 in Mishnah Berurah, for there everything will be explained with Hashem's help.
(9) And recites the blessing: He should recite Shema with them and afterward remove them. If he knows that he cannot restrain himself from passing gas except long enough to remove the shel rosh, nevertheless it is permitted initially to put on both. So wrote the Magen Avraham, but the Pri Megadim questions this.
