(6) One who knows—excluding if he does not know with certainty; then he should not cancel even public prayer because of this.

(7) To pray—specifically prayer, because he is like one standing before the king, and it is shameful to stand to pray with the intent that he will stop in the middle until the odor passes; but Keriat Shema and its blessings are permitted, though without tefillin.

(8) At the time of Keriat Shema—see below, siman 80, in Mishnah Berurah, where everything will be explained, with God's help.

(9) And he should bless—and read Shema in them, and afterward remove them. If he knows he cannot restrain himself from passing gas except when he can remove the head tefillin, nevertheless it is permitted to put on both initially—so the Magen Avraham; but the Peri Megadim questions this.
