(Section 2) "And if," etc. — only so that he may remove the head tefillin; nevertheless, etc. — Magen Avraham, siman 66 — thus it should read; and he means: although he cannot restrain himself until he removes the arm tefillin too, there is no need for that in a time of pressure, for the main concern is with the head tefillin because it has a shin; but the dalet and yod in the straps are not considered letters, as Tosafot Shabbat, folio 61 amud alef, wrote; and moreover the arm tefillin he can cover.
