Seif katan 2, and if, etc. Rather, only long enough to remove the shel rosh; even so, etc. It should read: Magen Avraham, siman, etc. He means to say that although he cannot restrain himself until he also removes the shel yad, we are not concerned in a pressing circumstance, because the primary concern is with the shel rosh, which has the shin. But the dalet and yud on the straps are not considered letters, as written in Tosafot Shabbos, folio 61a. Further, the shel yad can be covered.
