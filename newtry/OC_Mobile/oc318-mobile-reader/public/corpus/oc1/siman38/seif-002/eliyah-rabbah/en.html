[2] We object to them, etc. Thus wrote the Rambam. Arukh Chayyim raised a difficulty from Michal bat Shaul, whom the Sages did not object to; Beit Yosef and Acharonim strained to answer. It seems to me later generations were strict because they saw women nowadays are not so diligent; therefore they were strict to object to all lest mishap come from it.
