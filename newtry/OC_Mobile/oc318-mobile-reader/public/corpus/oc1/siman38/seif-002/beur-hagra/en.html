Seif 2, one who is certain, etc. As stated in Shabbos 49a, as above, and in the aforementioned Yerushalmi: there they said, anyone who is not like Elisha Baal Kenafayim should not wear tefillin. A proof is from the beginning of the second chapter of Berachos 14b, regarding Rav, who put on, etc., the messenger, etc. See what I wrote in siman 80.
