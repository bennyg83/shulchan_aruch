2) A person who wears trousers but is bare from the trousers upward should not don tefillin, because his heart is exposed. Sefer Chasidim 774, cited by Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Tur, siman 37; Magen Avraham 1; Eliyah Rabbah 1; Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 1; Machatzis HaShekel 1; Shesilei Zeitim 2; Pischei Olam 1. An open neckline is not included in this prohibition. Pri Megadim and Pischei Olam there.
