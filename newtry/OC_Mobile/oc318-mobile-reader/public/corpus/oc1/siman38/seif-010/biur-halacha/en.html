One who reads in the Torah is exempt, etc.—see Mishnah Berurah, note 33, that beforehand he is obligated; thus in the Gra's Beur, and the world is lenient in this; perhaps their reason is that they rely on siman 37, seif 2—and see what I wrote there in Biur Halachah; and see further in the Gra's Beur that from his words it seems even according to Shulchan Aruch, who does not distinguish regarding "his Torah is his occupation," we are lenient only regarding that he need not interrupt his study to put them on; but if they are already on him—that he wore them for Keriat Shema and prayer—or before he began to study, then they must be on him even during study and he should not remove them in the middle, similar to what Rama ruled in the Hagahah in the previous seif, that where without trouble he can do both, he must do both.
