One who reads Torah is exempt, etc. See Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 33, that before beginning Torah study he is obligated; this appears in Vilna Gaon. The common practice is lenient, perhaps because people rely upon siman 37, se'if 2. See what I wrote there in Biur Halachah. Vilna Gaon further implies that even according to Shulchan Aruch, which does not distinguish whether Torah is his occupation, leniency applies only in that he need not interrupt his study in order to put on tefillin. If the tefillin are already upon him (because he wore them for Krias Shema and prayer or before beginning to learn) he must keep them on even while studying and not remove them in the middle. This resembles Rema's ruling in the preceding se'if that when both mitzvos can be performed without added effort, one must perform both.
