Paragraph 9. Rambam omitted the ruling that tefillin writers are exempt from all other mitzvos. Beis Yosef relies upon the Yerushalmi, which obligates the writers, and the Mechaber exempts them only from wearing tefillin all day, not from Krias Shema. Rema disagrees and exempts them from Krias Shema as well; he understands the Yerushalmi to concern a case in which both mitzvos can be performed.
