Note 9: why Rambam omitted scribes exempt from all mitzvot—Beit Yosef wrote he relied on Yerushalmi that scribes are obligated; Mechaber ruled exempt all day except Keriat Shema time; Rama in gloss disagrees—exempt even from Keriat Shema; Yerushalmi only when both can be done—Magen Avraham's words are clear.
