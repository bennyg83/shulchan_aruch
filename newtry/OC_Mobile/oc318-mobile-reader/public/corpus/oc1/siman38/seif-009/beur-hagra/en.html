Seif 9—one who is distressed, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 2 of Berakhot: R. Yochanan in summer—his head was not steady, he would wear both; but in winter, when his head was steady, he would not wear except the arm-tefillin. And regarding a groom we say because he is preoccupied; only for a mourner and preoccupation of permission we say he should settle his mind—evidently whoever cannot is exempt.
