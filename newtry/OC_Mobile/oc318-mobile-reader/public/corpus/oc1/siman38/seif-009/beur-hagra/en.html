Seif 9, one who is distressed, etc. Yerushalmi, Berachos chapter 2: Rabbi Yochanan, in the summer, when his head did not trouble him, would wear both; but in the winter, when his head troubled him, he would wear only the one on his arm. Regarding a chasan we say this because he is preoccupied. Only with an avel and with preoccupation over optional matters do we say that he must settle his mind; but whenever he cannot, he is exempt.
