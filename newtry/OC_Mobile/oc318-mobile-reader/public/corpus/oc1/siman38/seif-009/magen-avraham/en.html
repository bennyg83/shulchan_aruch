"One who suffers distress." It implies even on account of cold, as stated in the Yerushalmi: Rabbi Yochanan would not put on tefillin on account of cold. And another was mentioned who did not put on tefillin on the day he came from a journey — see above, siman 27.

"Whose mind is not settled on them" — specifically when he cannot settle his mind (Bach) — see above, seif 5.
