Therefore Rama distinguishes—as in gloss—intent not to answer Rabbi Yitzchak on Rambam: in Darkei Moshe nothing; insufficient for Rabbi Yitzchak; if must trouble, exempt from Keriat Shema too; if no trouble except Keriat Shema, obligated all day in tefillin and all mitzvot; Magen Avraham: challenged Rabbi Yitzchak therefore Rama distinguishes per poskim though not "some say"—as Magen Avraham, siman 33, note 5.

Why omitted—baraita scribes exempt, etc.; Rambam relied on Yerushalmi: first Tanna obligates scribes in Keriat Shema, not prayer; R. Chiyya ben Akiva obligates even prayer—ruled like Yerushalmi, many scribes.

Rabbi Yitzchak wrote Rambam may hold both can be fulfilled—Magen Avraham: Maimonides holds even without trouble exempt.
