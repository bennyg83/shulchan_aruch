Seif 6, on Tisha B'Av, etc. Tosafot there. For they said at the end of Taanis that all mitzvos, etc., only regarding those that apply all seven days, and it is explaining this. Know that it is permitted to do work in a place where the custom is so, like an avel whom one does not ask what he will eat. Likewise regarding greeting, he may respond, as written in the Tosefta there, like after three days. This is the Rosh at the end of Taanis.
