Seif 6—on the Ninth of Av, etc. Tosafot there: they did not say at the end of Taanit "all mitzvot," etc., except those practiced all seven; and the commentators explain—you will know, for work is permitted where they practiced, like a mourner to whom they do not say what he will eat; and likewise greeting, he responds as in the Tosefta there, like after three. Rosh, end of Taanit.
