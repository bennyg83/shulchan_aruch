(5) [Seif 2] And if it appears to him he can maintain himself, etc.—and if he cannot guard himself from passing gas except by removing head-tefillin, nevertheless he puts on hand-tefillin too; and at his time of distress when he cannot remove it he did not transgress; and he removes if he continues to be compelled—Panim Chadashot in the name of Ramah of Pano, siman 60; Kenesset ha-Gedolah in Hagahat Beit Yosef—Magen Avraham, note 2; Avnei Nezer, letter 1; Machatzit ha-Shekel, note 2; Siddur Beit Yaakov on who is obligated in tefillin, letter 2; Shetilim Zeitim, letter 3; Petach Olam, letter 3.

(5) One who knows his stomach is impaired and knows himself he has intestinal illness, or will not restrain himself from passing gas—he wears tefillin for Keriat Shema and its blessings and the eighteen blessings of the Amidah; and after finishing he stands eighteen and removes them immediately—Kaf ha-Chayyim, siman 10, letter 28; and see below, siman 80, letter 1; and further siman 53, seif 3, and our words there, with God's help.
