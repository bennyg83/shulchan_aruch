5) “They are exempt because, etc.” Lechem Chamudot writes: “It appears to me that this applies specifically at the place of the wedding canopy, where intoxication and frivolity are common.” Thus far his words. Responsa Rama, siman 132, however, writes that the custom has become widespread for them to recite Keriat Shema and pray, and that they are obligated in tefillin, since nowadays tefillin are worn only during Keriat Shema. This implies that they are obligated even at the place of the wedding canopy. Responsa R’ Shlomo Levi and Knesset HaGedolah likewise imply this. The Magen Avraham is lenient in this matter.
