But on the first day it is forbidden to put on tefillin, and on the second day likewise he does not put them on except after sunrise; and some say that even before sunrise it is permitted to put them on. Nevertheless, on the first day after the burial he is obligated in prayer if the time has not passed; and likewise if he heard a report of a close relative he is also forbidden to put on tefillin on the first day (Mas'at Binyamin, and so it is implied from the words of the Ramban; see Yoreh De'ah, siman 383, seif 6).
