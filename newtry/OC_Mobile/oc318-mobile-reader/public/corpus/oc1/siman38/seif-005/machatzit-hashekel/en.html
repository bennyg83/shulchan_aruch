(Note 5) obligated—implies immediately; Yoreh De'ah 388: after sunrise—intending to challenge Rabbi Yitzchak's contradiction. Dispute of Rishonim: Moed Katan—R. Yehoshua: first two days forbidden; answer: second day permitted; Rif: if wore on second day, even if new faces come, does not remove. Some say second day permitted only after part of day because night not time for tefillin—part of day counts; some say night counts for mourning too.

Rabbi Yitzchak: although night is not time for tefillin lest one sleep in them, still forbidden for mourning; therefore second day not forbidden entire day even before sunrise.

Implies he may put on initially—even with new faces present at time of putting on—separate topic.
