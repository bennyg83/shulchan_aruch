Magen Avraham, note 4: let us say—Rashi thought they are accustomed to impurity, perhaps having seen keri; therefore he challenged: let us say R. Yehudah forbids wearing tefillin. The answer: concern for future keri. Magen Avraham challenged: the answer still is difficult—if concern is future, mere thought suffices—and it is difficult. Beit Yosef innovated concern for past keri—if so, thought alone should suffice—and it is difficult.
