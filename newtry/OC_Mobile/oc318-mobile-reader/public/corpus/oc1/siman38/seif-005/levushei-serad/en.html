Magen Avraham, paragraph 4: Shall we say that Rabbi Yose holds. Rashi explains the initial assumption to be that those accustomed to impurity may experience an emission because improper thoughts are common to them, though thought alone does not prohibit tefillin. Beis Yosef explains that the initial concern was that he may already have experienced an emission. Magen Avraham asks: the answer shifts to a concern about what may happen in the future; if so, improper thought alone should suffice. This requires resolution.
