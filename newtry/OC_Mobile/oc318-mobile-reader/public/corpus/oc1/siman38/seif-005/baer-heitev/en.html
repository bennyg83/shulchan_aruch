First. That is, on the day of burial; for on the day of death he is exempt from all mitzvot as long as he has not been buried, for then he is called an onen; and likewise if he heard a report of a close relative he is also forbidden to put on tefillin on the first day, like the day of burial—Taz, Yoreh De'ah, siman 382, seif kaf alef; and Maharitz in his novellae to chapter Eizehu Neshech, folio 12, wrote that we learn specifically that the day of death and the day of burial are exempt from tefillin on the first day; but when death was on one day and burial on another, he is obligated in tefillin even on the first day—end of quote.

Obligated. It implies that he is obligated immediately; but in Yoreh De'ah, siman 388, he wrote after sunrise—Magen Avraham and Elyah Rabbah. The Ari, may his memory be blessed, wrote that one does not put on tefillin all seven days of mourning—the head.

New faces. That is, new comforters. And the Magen Avraham ruled that if new faces came he does not put them on initially until they leave; but if he is wearing them he does not remove them—end of quote.
