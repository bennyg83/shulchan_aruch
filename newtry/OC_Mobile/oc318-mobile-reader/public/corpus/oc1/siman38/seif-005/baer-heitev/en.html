The first. This means the day of burial. On the day of death, before the burial, he is exempt from all mitzvos because he is then termed an onen. The same applies if he hears recent tidings of a death: he is likewise forbidden to put on tefillin on the first day, as on the day of burial. Taz, Yoreh Deah 402, seif katan 1. Rabbi Yom Tov Tzahalon writes in his novellae to the chapter Eizehu Neshech, page 12, that we rule that one is exempt from tefillin on the first day specifically when the day of death and the day of burial coincide. If the death occurred on one day and the burial on another, he is obligated in tefillin even on the first day of mourning; see there.
He is obligated. This implies that the obligation begins immediately, but Yoreh Deah, siman 388, states that it begins after sunrise. See Magen Avraham and Olas Tamid. The Arizal writes that one should not put on Rabbeinu Tam tefillin throughout all seven days of mourning.
New. This means new comforters. Magen Avraham rules that when new people arrive, the mourner should initially not put on tefillin until they leave; if he is already wearing them, however, he need not remove them. See there.
