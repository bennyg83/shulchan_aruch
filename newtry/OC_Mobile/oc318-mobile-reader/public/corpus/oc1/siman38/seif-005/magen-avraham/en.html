Obligated. This implies that he is obligated immediately. In Yoreh Deah, siman 388, however, it says after sunrise, implying that he puts them on initially. This also appears from the Tur and Rif. But in the Gemara and Rosh in Yoreh Deah it says that if new comforters came, he should not put them on initially until they leave, though if he already put them on he does not remove them, and this is primary.
New faces. This means new comforters. One might ask why a mourner is obligated in tefillin, since later we say that one who is distressed is exempt from tefillin. The answer is that there it is pain that comes on its own, whereas here he is distressing himself and should settle his mind, as stated in the second chapter of Sukkah regarding sukkah.
