"Obligated" — it implies he is obligated immediately; but in Yoreh De'ah, siman 388, he wrote after sunrise; and it implies he may put them on ab initio, as in Tur and Rif; but in the Gemara and in Rosh, Yoreh De'ah, it says that if new comforters came he does not put them on ab initio until they leave — only that if he put them on he does not remove them; and such is the essential ruling.

"New comforters" — meaning new mourners who came to comfort him. And if you ask: why is he obligated? But for tefillin — we say below that one who suffers distress is exempt from tefillin — one could answer: that applies to distress that comes of itself, but here he causes distress to himself; he should settle his mind, as we say in Sukkah, chapter 2, regarding sukkah.
