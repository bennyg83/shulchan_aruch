5) [Se'if 2] If it appears to him that he can restrain himself, etc. If he can restrain his flatulence only long enough to remove the head-tefillin, he should nevertheless also put on the arm-tefillin. If distress prevents him from removing the arm-tefillin, he has committed no prohibition; if the compulsion continues, he should remove it. Panim Chadashos in the name of Rema MiPano 60, cited by Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef; Magen Avraham 2; Eliyah Rabbah 1; Machatzis HaShekel 2; Siddur Beis Oved, laws of those obligated in tefillin 2; Shesilei Zeitim 3; Pischei Olam 3.
5) One who knows that his stomach is impaired and that he has intestinal illness or will not be spared flatulence should wear tefillin for Krias Shema, its blessings, and the eighteen blessings of the Amidah; immediately after completing the eighteen blessings, he should remove them. Kaf HaChaim by Rabbi Chaim Palagi, siman 10, paragraph 28. See below in siman 80, paragraph 1, and also siman 53:3 and our words there, with God's help.
