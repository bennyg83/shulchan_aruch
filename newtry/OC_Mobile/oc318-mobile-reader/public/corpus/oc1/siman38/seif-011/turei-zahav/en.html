Rather he should turn aside. The reason is that it is disrespectful to bare his head before him—as Rashi explained in chapter Chelek; but our teacher the Tur brought a teaching: what is his remedy?—his teacher should wrap first, etc.; and one can say accordingly the reason is that it is like teaching halachah before his teacher, for they would wear tefillin all day, and when the student removes first it appears he teaches that the time to remove tefillin has arrived; therefore there is a remedy when the teacher wraps first. What the Tur wrote, "and the students wrap"—meaning the student must do so when the teacher wraps first, lest the student wrapping appear as one who wishes to go to the restroom and return and wear them again. What the Tur wrote, "not fit to wear tefillin"—for one who reads—seems to apply when tefillin are on him all day specifically. I found written in the name of Maharar Shakna: when a student has no tefillin, he should not put on a shoe for his teacher or remove it for him where they do not recognize him, lest they say he is a slave—as in Yoreh De'ah, siman 242; therefore it is forbidden to remove before his teacher, for it appears he removes so as not to serve his teacher with shoe-tying and removal, etc. It is difficult: if so, where they recognize him it would be permitted to remove tefillin before his teacher—and this is certainly not so.
