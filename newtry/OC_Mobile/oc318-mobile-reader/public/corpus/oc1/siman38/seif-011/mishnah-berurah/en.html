(35) Before his rebbe: This means his primary rebbe, from whom he learned most of his wisdom.
(36) Because of his awe: That is to say, it is disrespectful that he uncovers his head before him. According to this, even if his rebbe has already removed his tefillin first, it is forbidden. If he turns himself somewhat to another side and is careful not to uncover his head before him, it appears that one may be lenient in every case. So wrote the Pri Megadim. See there further, for it implies from him that if he removes them close to dark, one should be stringent in every case not to remove them before his rebbe has removed his, because it appears as though he is ruling halacha before his rebbe that the time to remove them has arrived. See above in siman 25, s.k. 58, what I wrote there in the name of Pri Megadim.
