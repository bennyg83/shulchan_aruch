(35) Before his teacher—that is, his primary teacher from whom he received most of his wisdom.

(36) Out of awe of him—meaning it is disrespectful to bare his head before him; therefore even if his teacher already removed his first, it is forbidden. If he turns aside somewhat and is careful not to bare his head before him, it seems there is room to be lenient in every case—so too the Peri Megadim; and see there further that if he removes them near nightfall one should be strict in every case not to remove them before his teacher removed them, for it appears he teaches halachah before his teacher that the time to remove has arrived—and see above, siman 25, note 58, what the Peri Megadim wrote there.
