Before his teacher—from Rabbi Yitzchak's wording, even if teacher removed first, forbidden; Tur: if teacher removed first, student may remove—teaching time to remove arrived; Shlah in name of Gaon Maharash: removing tefillin prevents serving teacher—Yoreh De'ah 242—forbids untying shoe and carrying to bath lest called slave; with tefillin permitted all labors; but if teacher removed, student may remove lest called slave when teacher also has none—end of Shlah.
