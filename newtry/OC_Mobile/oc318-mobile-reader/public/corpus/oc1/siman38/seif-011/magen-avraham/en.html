Before his rebbe. Since the Beit Yosef wrote plainly, it implies that even if his rebbe removed tefillin first, it is forbidden. In the Tur there is an opinion that if his rebbe removed them first, he may remove them. The reason is that it appears like ruling halacha before his rebbe, as though he shows that the time to remove them has arrived. The Shelah wrote in the name of Maharash that the reason is that when he removes tefillin he prevents himself from serving his rebbe, for when tefillin are on his head he may do all services, but when his rebbe has removed tefillin, he too may remove them.
