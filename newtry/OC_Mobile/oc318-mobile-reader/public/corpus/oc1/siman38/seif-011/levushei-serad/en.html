Taz, note 4—and afterward he wraps, students too—meaning this "and afterward he wraps" is superfluous; it teaches that when the teacher wraps, the student is obligated to wrap too; otherwise it appears he says the opposite of his teacher—if the rabbi, after removing tefillin, wrapped them and placed them in the bag, he teaches his view that the time to remove has arrived because it is close to evening; and if the student removed and did not wrap, it appears he wishes to leave them on longer; therefore the student cannot suffice to remove but must also wrap as the rabbi did.
