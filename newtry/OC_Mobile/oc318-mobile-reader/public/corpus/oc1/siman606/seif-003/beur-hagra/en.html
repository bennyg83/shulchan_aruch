3 Tiknath 20. Be comforted, said Moses before the Almighty the Omnipresent, Rabsha'a, all this anger, why did you say, Behold, you have raised up under your fathers a culture of sinful people, 20 I learned from you that you said that the sacrifices of these sins are in their souls, the Omnipresent, I said in their souls, not in their ancestors:
