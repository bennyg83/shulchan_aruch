Se'if 3. The enactment, etc. Tanchuma: Moshe said before the Holy One, blessed be He: Master of the universe, why all this anger, etc.? He said to him: you said, "behold, you have risen in place of your fathers, a culture of sinful men, etc." He said to Him: from You I learned, for You said, "the fire-pans of these sinners against their souls." He said to him: I said "against their souls," and not against their fathers.
