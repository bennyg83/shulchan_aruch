3) There is one who wrote that when a person has not repented for the transgressions between himself and another person, even his transgressions between himself and Hashem are not atoned for, and he supported this from the Mishnah. I say that his Master is not pleased with this. Birkei Yosef, ibid. However, Yafeh LaLev, volume 5, os 1, wrote in the name of several poskim that as long as he has not appeased the other person, even the element between him and Hashem is not atoned for, see there. In my humility, I wrote in the sacred work Edus B'Yaakov, discourse 2 for Shabbos Teshuvah, that when he did not request forgiveness from the person against whom he sinned, there is reason to be concerned that the Attribute of Judgment will prosecute him on Yom Kippur, saying: This person is unconcerned about transgressions, for he did not request forgiveness from the other person; why, as it were, should You forgive him? See there.
