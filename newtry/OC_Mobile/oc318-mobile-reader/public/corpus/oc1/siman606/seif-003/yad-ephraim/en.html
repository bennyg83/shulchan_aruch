Subparagraph 10 Although they customarily do not wash all thirty, meaning, and if so, etc. See siman 131, se'if 1. So it should read. That is, there he wrote that on the eve of Yom Kippur we do not say Lamnatze'ach, even though on most days when tachanun is not said, Lamnatze'ach is said, and he counts it with Chanukah, Purim, and Rosh Chodesh, which are days on which eulogy is forbidden. However, all this is not by law but only mere custom. Perhaps this is what he means: and see siman 131, se'if 1, should be placed after the words "that it is a mitzvah to eat on it." About this he wrote, see siman 131, se'if 1, where it says that it has a somewhat special status, even more than other days on which supplication is not said, and it is comparable to Chanukah and Purim. One can also say he comes to bring proof from there: since it needed to count it regarding saying Lamnatze'ach, if so it is no better than Chanukah, and similarly Purim, regarding which some say mourning applies, as written above.
