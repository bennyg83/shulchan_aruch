Magen Avraham subparagraph 4 And he will be head of the yeshivah (Bach). I did not find this in the Bach; on the contrary, he wrote the opposite, that it implies from the gloss that if it is only for the benefit of the forgiver, he must forgive; see there. See Sefer Tosafos Yom ha-Kippurim, Pri Chadash, and Eliyah Rabbah.
