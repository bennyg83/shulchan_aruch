That a person should appease his fellow on Erev Yom Kippur. It contains four se'ifim.
For sins between a person and his fellow, Yom Kippur does not atone until he appeases him. And even if he vexed him only with words, he must appease him. And if he is not appeased the first time, he should return and go a second and a third time; and each time he should take with him three men. And if he is not appeased after three times, he need not [pursue] him further {however, he should afterward say before ten [men] that he requested forgiveness from him (Mordechai, Yoma; Maharil)}. And if he is his teacher, he must go to him many times until he is appeased. {Rama: And the one who forgives should not be cruel in withholding forgiveness (Maharil) — unless he intends [it] for the good of the one requesting forgiveness (Gemara, Yoma). And if [the offender] spread an evil name about him, he need not forgive him (Mordechai; Semak; Hagahos Maimoni, chapter 2 of Hilchos Teshuvah; Mahari Weil).}
