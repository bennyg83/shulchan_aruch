Se'if 1. Transgressions, etc. See Magen Avraham, regarding "at the time of forgiveness, etc." This is the Gemara in Yoma 86b: Rav Nachman said, here, etc.
And he should go, etc. See Magen Avraham, what he wrote in the name of the Bach. This is the Gemara there: "please bear, etc.; I have sinned, and it is straight, etc."
However, he should say, etc. Tanchuma: if he went to appease him and he did not accept him, what should he do? Rabbi Yosi ben Dormaskis said: let this sign be handed to you for all days: whenever you have mercy on your fellow, there are those who have mercy on you; and if you do not have mercy on your fellow, there are not those who have mercy on you. What should he do? Rabbi Shmuel said: he brings ten people, makes one row, and says to them, there was a quarrel between me and my fellow, and I went to appease him, but he did not accept me. The Holy One, blessed be He, sees that he humbled himself and has mercy on him, etc. See Magen Avraham subparagraph 2. And if, etc., this follows Rashi's explanation there, that Rav is different, and even the Rif agrees to the law.
And if he is his teacher, etc. The Rif there according to his explanation of what is written there, "Rav is different." See Magen Avraham. It is like Rav Chiya in relation to Rav there.
And the one who forgives, etc. End of chapter ha-Chovel, and there.
Unless, etc. There. He explains what is written there, "so that he should go and study, etc.," as being for the benefit of Rav, unlike Rashi's explanation there. The Bach already wondered about him: from where did he know to explain unlike Rashi?
And if, etc. Yerushalmi, end of chapter ha-Chovel, end of halachah 10: Rabbi Yosi said, that which you say applies where he did not spread an evil name; but if he spread an evil name about him, he has no eternal forgiveness.
