In Magen Avraham subparagraph 6: that he suspected his fellow. Note: Radbaz, part 3, siman 480, explained that it is like one who testifies about him alone, as with Tuvya sinned and Zigud was lashed. This is when he calls him this not by way of quarrel and dispute; otherwise it is not testimony, but mere degradation. This is easy to understand, and I wrote at length in a responsum.
