He may immerse, etc. See Mishnah Berurah. This means that even people who are not careful all year about immersion should at least immerse on the eve of Yom Kippur and be clean because of the holy day.
Because of a seminal emission - See the glosses of the Gaon R. Y. Pick, that those pious and men of deed who always customarily immerse for seminal emissions according to the ordinance of Ezra must be careful to urinate before they descend to immerse. In any event, a sick person or elder certainly needs to be careful about this, for if they are not careful to urinate before immersion, they will be impure when they urinate afterward.
