4) One who had relations with another man's wife has committed a transgression with two elements. The first element is between himself and Hashem, for he committed a severe transgression, and repentance and Yom Kippur are effective for this. However, there is a second element, consisting of transgressions between one person and another, for he causes the man's wife to become forbidden to him. Repentance and Yom Kippur are not effective for this until he has appeased him. Noda B'Yehuda, first edition, Orach Chaim, siman 35; Ruach Chaim, os 1.
