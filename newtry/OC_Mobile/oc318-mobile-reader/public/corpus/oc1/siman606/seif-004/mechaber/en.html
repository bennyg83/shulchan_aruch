One may immerse and receive lashes whenever he wishes, provided it is before nightfall; and he does not recite a blessing over the immersion. {Rama: And one need immerse only once, without confession, on account of keri [a seminal emission]; and the same applies that pouring nine kabin of water is likewise effective (Mahari Weil; Kol Bo; Tashbetz). One for whom a relative died between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur is permitted to wash and immerse on Erev Yom Kippur, for Yom Kippur nullifies the shivah [seven-day mourning] (Maharil, Hilchos Semachos); and even though the custom is not to wash the entire sheloshim [thirty days], a commanded immersion [tevilas mitzvah] is permitted (his own opinion).}
