Died. If he said to someone, "mamzer," he did not blemish his father's honor, for he can say, "I intended you," unless he said, "mamzer son of a mamzer." Yam shel Shlomo, Bach, Bava Kamma, siman 46; Magen Avraham.
His grave. He must go there barefoot. If it is more than three parasangs away, he should send his agent; Yam shel Shlomo. If he insulted him after death, he need not go to his grave, but asks forgiveness from him in the place where he shamed him; Kenesses ha-Gedolah, Magen Avraham.
