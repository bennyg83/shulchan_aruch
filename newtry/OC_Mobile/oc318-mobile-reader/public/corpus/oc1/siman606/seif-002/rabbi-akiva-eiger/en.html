Magen Avraham subparagraph 7 In the place where he shamed him. In Yam shel Shlomo, Bava Kamma chapter 8, siman 50, he disagrees, saying one must be more and more stringent, and must go to his grave, take ten people with him, and appease him. If it is more than three parasangs away, he should send his agent there, and he should join ten with him to ask forgiveness from him on behalf of the one who insulted him; see there. So it also implies in the gloss to the Shulchan Aruch, Choshen Mishpat siman 420, se'if 38.
