(12) If he died, etc. - This also refers to one who spread an evil name, for we say that if he were alive, he would forgive him.
(13) The one against whom he sinned - If he called someone a mamzer, some say that he also blemished the honor of his ancestors and must go to their graves. But many disagree, for he can say: I did not intend that the matter should relate to his ancestors, unless he called him "mamzer son of a mamzer." See Choshen Mishpat siman 420 regarding if he called him "wicked one son of a wicked one" or "thief son of a thief."
(14) At his grave - He must go there barefoot. If it is more than three parasangs away, he should send his agent there, and the agent should take ten men and go to his grave and ask forgiveness in the name of the one who insulted him. He should say: behold, I am the agent of so-and-so, who admits publicly that so-and-so sent me to say that he sinned against the God of Israel, etc. If he insulted him after death, he need not go to his grave, but asks forgiveness from him in the place where he shamed him.
(15) That I sinned against him - With such-and-such a sin, for here he must specify the sin. They respond to him three times: forgiven to you, forgiven to you.
