2) Ibid.: Transgressions between one person and another, etc. Maharash Garmizan wrote that regarding a transgression between one person and another that also contains an element relating to Hashem, such as when he disgraced him verbally, thereby transgressing "You shall love your fellow as yourself," and similarly in comparable cases, as long as he has not appeased the other person, even the element between him and Hashem is not atoned for.
