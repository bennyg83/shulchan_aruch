(ב) He tells a non-Jew and he does it — for it is shvut of shvut in place of mitzvah and permitted as above siman 307 s.k. 5. And nevertheless forbidden to bring the infant to synagogue through an unjoined courtyard even through a non-Jew, for they can circumcise him in his house, and many times they circumcise in the house when there is cold. And when need to take the infant from his house to an unjoined courtyard to the scalpel placed there — permitted through a non-Jew since there is no reshut ha-rabbim [Magen Avraham there]; and nevertheless better to bring the scalpel through a non-Jew to the infant [Machatzit HaShekel], for if he brings the infant to the scalpel he will need after milah to permit again shvut of shvut to return the infant to his mother — unlike when he brings the scalpel to the infant he leaves it there until after Shabbat [Machatzit HaShekel]; and see in siman 349 what we wrote on this:
