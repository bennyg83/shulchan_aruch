Accessories for milah that it is possible to prepare from erev Shabbat — do not override Shabbat; therefore if he did not bring a scalpel for milah from erev Shabbat — he shall not bring it on Shabbat, even in a place where there is only a d'rabbanan prohibition, for the Rabbis established their words in place of kares; and to tell a non-Jew to do it — if it is something that if an Israelite did it there is only d'rabbanan prohibition — he tells a non-Jew and he does it; and if it is something forbidden for an Israelite from the Torah — he shall not tell a non-Jew to do it (and see above siman 307): (.
