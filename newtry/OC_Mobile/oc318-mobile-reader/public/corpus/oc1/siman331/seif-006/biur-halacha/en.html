(ב) And see siman 307 — see in Mishna Berurah in what is written that some are lenient even in d'oraisa melacha, namely such as if the knife broke and needs fixing; and Magen Avraham's view is that one may rely on them regarding milah; and see in responsum Ketav Sofer that this is specifically when there is no other mohel, but if there is another mohel who has a fixed knife — even though he does not wish to lend it — one may not permit; and even when there is no other mohel, most poskim hold it is forbidden through a non-Jew; nevertheless Magen Avraham already ruled, but on condition that the knife was fixed from yesterday; but if he was negligent and did not prepare from yesterday — Magen Avraham himself does not permit fixing the knife through a non-Jew.
