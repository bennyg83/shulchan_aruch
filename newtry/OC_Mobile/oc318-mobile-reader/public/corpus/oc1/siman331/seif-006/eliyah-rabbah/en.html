[6] He tells Samaritans, etc. Magen Avraham wrote: see siman 276 seif 2 that some are lenient even in d'oraisa melacha; and it appears to me that in milah one may rely on them if impossible otherwise — until here; and it is difficult: behold he wrote there specifically where his body benefits from it, etc., and here his body does not benefit; however I placed there a hatchet; and nevertheless it was explained there that we do not hold like "some are lenient." Magen Avraham wrote: it is obvious forbidden to bring the infant to synagogue through an unjoined courtyard even through Samaritans, for they can circumcise him in his house, and many times they circumcise in the house when there is cold — until here; and one must say it deals where they circumcise in the infant's house; and it appears to me in a place where they practice leniency they rely on siman 325 seif 10 that permits telling Samaritans to bring beer because he can go there himself — all the more so here where human dignity is great to circumcise in synagogue, and we do not judge "possible" from "impossible"; and Taz siman 349 wrote: when carrying an infant to circumcise — Samaritans take the infant from the house, and so Samaritans bring into synagogue — only in the street Israelites pass to one another within less than four amot:
