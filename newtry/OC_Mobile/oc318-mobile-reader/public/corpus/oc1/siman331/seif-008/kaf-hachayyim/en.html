8) There: And suction is performed. This means drawing blood from where it is attached. Although this creates a wound because the blood is detached from its place only through suction, it is nevertheless permitted because of danger. Rashi on the Mishnah and Gemara there. See Yoreh De'ah in the preceding seif. If a mohel on Shabbos had sores in his mouth and could not perform suction, and another person came and performed it, the latter acted properly. A mohel who knew he could not perform suction but nevertheless performed the bris requires atonement. Admas Kodesh, Orach Chaim, siman 6; Zechor LeAvraham, volume 3, Yoreh De'ah, paragraph 130.
