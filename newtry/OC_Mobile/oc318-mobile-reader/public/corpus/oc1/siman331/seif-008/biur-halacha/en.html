Shirt — see in Mishna Berurah; and it is from Rashi and he copied it in Magen Avraham; and per this it is not in the category of danger in lack of a shirt, only that thereby he would need to circumcise him a second time d'rabbanan; but from Rashba and other rishonim it implies it is in the category of danger, and the practical difference from this is after milah whether permitted to bring him through reshut ha-rabbim when he has no other option:
