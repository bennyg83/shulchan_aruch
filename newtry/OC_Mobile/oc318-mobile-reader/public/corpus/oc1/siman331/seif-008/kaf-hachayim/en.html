(8) There. And perform metzitzah. Explanation: he sucks the blood from the place of connection; and even though through metzitzah the blood is detached from its place and it becomes making a connection — nevertheless permitted on account of danger — Rashi in mishnah there and in gemara; and see in Yoreh De'ah there mentioned seif: a mohel on Shabbat who had pimples in his mouth and could not do metzitzah — his friend came and they did metzitzah — he did well; and the mohel who knew he could not suck and circumcised — needs atonement.
