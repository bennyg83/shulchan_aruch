[8] Another courtyard, etc. Through a true public domain it is prohibited, as stated by the Magen Avraham. This implies that through a karmelit it is permitted, and so too it appears somewhat from the Beit Yosef. But in siman 301 I cited the Maharash as prohibiting even through a karmelit. The lenient view seems more reasonable, and so too it appears from Kol Bo, page 31. There is also some proof from siman 302 and siman 312, seif 1, where all rabbinic prohibitions are treated alike.
