And in these countries, etc. And in Agudah chapter Hader he wrote they practice burying hot water to bathe him, and he shall not wring a sheet for it is like laundering; or a non-Jew puts the sheet in the water; and if the hot water spilled — he tells a non-Jew to bring water heated for the non-Jew and he shall not tell a non-Jew to heat for him; Beit Yosef wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 264 in name of Rashbetz that an infant who was ill and recovered — they do not circumcise him on the fifth day lest they need to desecrate Shabbat on the third day, as written siman 248 regarding a ship; and Ra'avad wrote that according to his words even on the fourth day it is forbidden; and it appears to me permitted, and so the practice is clear in Constantinople; but on the fifth and sixth days they practice prohibition — end; and see in Shach and Taz there; and it appears to me that in our time when they do not bathe him — there is no concern, for it is not common at all that they would desecrate Shabbat for him; and also Ra'avad hesitates in this law; therefore one should not be lax in a mitzvah matter; and I do not know why he wrote according to his words even on the fourth day forbidden, for when they circumcise on the fourth day and the fourth day falls on Shabbat — there is no concern; but regarding a ship — forever there is concern for danger:
