And they bathe. And he shall not wring a sheet, for it is like laundering; or a non-Jew puts the sheet into the water; and if the hot water spilled — he tells a non-Jew to bring water heated for the non-Jew, and he shall not tell a non-Jew to heat for him — Beit Yosef wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 267 in name of Rashbetz that an infant who was ill and was not circumcised at its time and recovered — they do not circumcise him on the fifth day, lest they need to desecrate Shabbat on the third day of his milah; and also on the sixth day they practice prohibition on erev Shabbat; and see there Shach and Taz; and see in Knesset HaGedolah. And Magen Avraham wrote: and it appears to me that in our time when they do not bathe him — there is no concern for this, for it is not common at all that they would desecrate Shabbat for him; therefore one should not be lax in a mitzvah matter on erev Shabbat:
