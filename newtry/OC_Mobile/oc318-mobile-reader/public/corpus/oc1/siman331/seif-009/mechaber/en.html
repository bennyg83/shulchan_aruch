In the time of the sages of the gemara, if they did not bathe the infant before milah and after milah and on the third day of milah in hot water — it was dangerous; therefore it was necessary to write its law when it occurs on Shabbat; but nowadays they do not practice bathing at all, and its law is to bathe on Shabbat if they wish like the law of bathing for any person:And in these countries they practice bathing him before milah in hot water heated from yesterday, and after milah at Motzei Shabbat; and so if the third day of his milah was on Shabbat and they see there is need to bathe him — they prepare hot water while it is still day and bathe him on Shabbat; and all this is from presumption; but if they see there is reason to fear danger if they do not bathe him after milah — certainly permitted to bathe him and desecrate Shabbat for him like other illnesses with danger:
