There seif 9: in gemara times if they did not bathe the infant, etc. NB: behold regarding law if hot water spilled that he has to bathe him after milah — whether permitted to circumcise him then they will need to desecrate Shabbat afterward for hot water — Baal HaMaor and Ramban dispute and their words are brought in Ran chapter R' Eliezer of Milah and in Beit Yosef here: Baal HaMaor holds they do not circumcise him and Ramban holds we follow the present and the mitzvah does the preparation and afterward pikuach nefesh overrides Shabbat not the milah; and I already wrote much on this above. And behold Baal HaMaor brought proof from first chapter of Beitzah that Beit Shammai there hold slaughter and dig with a spade — Beit Hillel hold do not slaughter unless he had earth prepared from daytime; and they agree if he slaughtered he digs with spade and covers; and why not slaughter permissibly and afterward dig with spade — covering that deferred digging did not defer slaughter; only certainly we do not say thus; and likewise in milah. And Ran forced to reject this proof. And in truth it appears in my humble opinion to resolve Ramban's difficulty that their dispute depends on what poskim dispute siman 328 brought in Beit Yosef whether Shabbat is permitted for ill or deferred. And behold I wondered in my chiddushim on Beit Yosef on this and proved from Yoma daf 46 Shabbat is different from impurity and Shabbat permitted for public and ill on erev Shabbat; and therefore it works well: since this is the distinction between permitted and deferred that if impurity permitted in public — need not return to permission at all; but if deferred — must return to permission; if so likewise here: Ramban holds Shabbat is permitted for ill — therefore we properly follow the present now and circumcise; and if afterward need to defer Shabbat for hot water — what of it, this is real permission since it will be pikuach nefesh — real permission; therefore no proof from Yom Tov where regarding covering even if he slaughters what is permitted to dig with spade is only through positive commandment of covering deferring that Yom Tov prohibition — there positive commandment deferring negative commandment is only deferred not permitted; and this is clear and known, for if possible to fulfill both — must fulfill both; and there certainly only deferred not permitted; therefore all possible to return to permission we return; therefore do not slaughter since ultimately need to defer Yom Tov; but in pikuach nefesh which is real permission — and even though also there if find way of permission should not do prohibition as I wrote there from another reason pikuach nefesh was permitted on Shabbat; but since there is way of permission — no longer called pikuach nefesh at all and all that is not pikuach nefesh not permitted; but in positive commandment deferring negative commandment — if there is way of permission we do not consider there the positive commandment as positive commandment — behold ultimately it is mitzvah; and nevertheless forbidden to do through prohibition — proven deferred only; therefore forbidden to slaughter there; but in milah permitted since afterward will do through real permission; and Baal HaMaor would hold Shabbat deferred for ill not permitted — if so all possible to do permissibly we do; therefore he holds forbidden to circumcise on Shabbat and properly brings proof from Yom Tov forbidden to slaughter; and it works well as appears to me correct — examine well:
