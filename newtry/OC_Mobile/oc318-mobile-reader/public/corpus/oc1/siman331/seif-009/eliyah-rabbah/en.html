If the hot water spills or is spoiled, it is preferable to tell a non-Jew to bring hot water from a bathhouse or bakery where it had already been heated for him, rather than instruct a non-Jew to heat water on Shabbos. One must also take care regarding squeezing.
[10] [Levush] An infant who was ill and recovered. The Tashbetz prohibits circumcising him on Thursday, lest it become necessary to desecrate Shabbos on the third day after circumcision. The Shach at the end of siman 266 permits it, and the Magen Avraham agrees. In siman 248, seif katan 5, I brought support for the lenient view.
