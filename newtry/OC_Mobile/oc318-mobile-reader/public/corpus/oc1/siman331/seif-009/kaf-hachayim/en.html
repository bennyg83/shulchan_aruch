(9) There. And perform metzitzah. See in responsum Binyan Tzion siman 23 and 24 that he wrote metzitzah is specifically with the mouth and not through something else as the new doctors introduced; and in responsum Yad Eliezer siman 10 he permits sucking with a sponge, for it is better than metzitzah with the mouth, and even on Shabbat there is reason to permit — see there; and according to what doctors say now — on the contrary through metzitzah he may be endangered — we have only the words of Chazal; and even so it appears to me he should not suck very forcefully on Shabbat. Also he should rinse his mouth after metzitzah on the wound with vinegar he takes in his mouth — Tiferet Yisrael on mishnayot. Adam's sin was through sending the hand, for with hands he took and with mouth he ate — also in this mitzvah with hands they circumcise and with mouth they suck to repair this sin.
