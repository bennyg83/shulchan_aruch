"And born without a visible foreskin." I found written that it requires study why nowadays the custom is not to examine the child afterward. One must say as Bach writes in Yoreh De'ah, siman 264, that he must appear without a visible foreskin even when the organ is not erect. Accordingly, if he does not appear that way when it is not erect, the brit milah may be performed on Shabbos. This requires study whether one may rely upon this to permit an act involving the severe laws of Shabbos.
