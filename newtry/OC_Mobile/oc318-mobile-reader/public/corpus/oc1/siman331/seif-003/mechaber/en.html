An eight-day-old:' if his hair and nails finished [growing] — they circumcise him on Shabbat; and if they did not finish — even if he is doubtful seven-day versus eight-day — they do not circumcise him, and needless to say a definite eight-day-old; and if he is a definite seven-day-old — even if his hair and nails did not finish — they circumcise him [and see in Yoreh De'ah siman 266]:[]:
