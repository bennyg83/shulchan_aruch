(א) If his hair finished, etc. — for even though an eight-day-old in general is not a viable infant — through his hair finishing we say: this is a seven-day-old and delayed in his mother's womb [Magen Avraham]:
(ב) Safek seven-day, etc. — and likewise safek eight-day safek nine-day; and the reason in all this is we do not desecrate Shabbat from safek:
(ג) Definite seven-day — namely he had relations with her and separated until her pregnancy was recognized; but we do not rely to say presumably she conceived after night of immersion or from when she ceased seeing blood, for this is not clear proof:
(ד) And see in Yoreh De'ah, etc. — for there Rama's view is explained that safek eight-day or safek seven-day even if his hair and nails did not finish — circumcise on Shabbat in any case, for if viable for milah — he circumcises properly; and if not — he cuts mere flesh and there is no wounding concern since it is a non-viable infant. And behold Bach and Taz hold to rule like Shulchan Aruch; but Eshel Avraham brought in name of Sefer Lechem Chamudot that the practice is to circumcise safek cases; and also in Meiri he wrote so is the view of all geonim; and so Radbaz siman 32 — each river and its channel:
