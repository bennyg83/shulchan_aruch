If ground cumin was not available before Shabbos, see below, seif 6. Regarding the powder sprinkled after the bris as treatment, it appears immaterial whether one sprinkles it several times or, preferably, applies it all at once. Orach Chaim, paragraph 1, in the name of Eshel Avraham from Hagahos Kodesh. The earth into which the foreskin is placed must be designated before Shabbos, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, siman 265, seif 10; see there. If it was not designated before Shabbos, the foreskin should be cast into a place containing loose earth, or one may take earth that was designated before Shabbos for another purpose, as stated above in siman 308, seif 38, and below in siman 498; see there.
