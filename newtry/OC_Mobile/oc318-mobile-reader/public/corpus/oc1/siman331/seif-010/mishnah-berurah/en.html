(א) He shall not circumcise — for we fear perhaps he will ruin and find himself desecrating Shabbat not in place of mitzvah; and likewise if he never performed priyah — forbidden to perform priyah on Shabbat also for this reason. A mohel who comes and says he already circumcised once on a weekday — he is believed and need not bring proof. In Yoreh De'ah end of siman 266 he wrote in the gloss that l'chatchila good to be stringent that two mohalim not circumcise one milah on Shabbat — one circumcises and one performs priyah — rather the circumciser himself performs priyah; but many later authorities wrote one should be lenient, and so the practice in all Poland — always two mohalim, one cuts and one performs priyah, whether weekday or Shabbat; and in Chayei Adam kelal 149 he wrote that in any case three people should not honor — rather the one performing priyah should be the one performing metzitzah; but in Yeshuot Yaakov he rejected this and wrote metzitzah can be by another, for it is a separate matter:

(ב) On Shabbat — and regarding Yom Tov if second day of Diaspora Yom Tov — certainly lenient; and on first day of Yom Tov there are views among later authorities; but if he knows himself he can circumcise and there is no other mohel — certainly one should not be stringent:

(ג) Permitted to circumcise on Shabbat — even if there is another who circumcised many times:

(ד) Even, etc. — explanation: to exclude those who hold the father himself should not circumcise on Shabbat where there is another, because for him it is psik reisha de-nicha lei since he repairs his son — it teaches us we do not say thus, for also regarding others it is psik reisha de-nicha lei since he wants to circumcise him and for this he comes, and nevertheless the Torah permitted [Terumat HaDeshen]:
