And they place upon it cumin and aspalin (it is like a thick ointment called in Arabic marham — Rambam in commentary on mishnah) or bandage or powder of spices stopping blood — Yoreh De'ah there. And the reason that if they do not apply them the infant will come to danger — Levush. And if he did not have ground cumin from erev Shabbat — see below seif 7. And regarding powder they pour after milah for healing — it appears there is no concern to scatter several times; or better to apply once. Orach Chayim letter 1 in name of one of the great halachic authorities.
