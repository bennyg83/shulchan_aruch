Seif katan 10, "who has never performed milah," etc. And one reason for them is that he is still not expert in the skill of milah; and one could say that perhaps he will ruin it and desecrate Shabbos for nothing. This is unlike one who has performed milah or priyah once: that suffices, and we do not require that he establish proficiency three times, for if he were not certain in himself that he will certainly not ruin it, he would not put himself into a safek of Shabbos desecration. And as for what the Shulchan Aruch wrote, "even the father of the child" — one might have thought that regarding the father of the child, certainly his intent is to repair, and it is also a psik reisha that is beneficial to him, unlike another; so it teaches us otherwise; and see there.
