And see in responsum Chatam Sofer siman 158. A mohel who comes to circumcise on Shabbat and says he already circumcised once on a weekday — he is believed and need not bring proof. Shemuel is believed siman 19. And see in Yoreh De'ah siman 266 that two mohalim should not circumcise one milah on Shabbat — one circumcises and one performs priyah — rather the one who circumcises himself performs priyah; and see there Taz s.k. 11.
