(א) Androgynous — an infant who has male aspects like a male and female aspects like a female [Rambam chapter 1 Laws of Milah]:

(ב) And born at twilight — whether twilight of erev Shabbat or twilight of Motzei Shabbat — in both their milah does not override Shabbat and is deferred to the first day; and what is twilight — some say close to a quarter-hour [it is a temporary hour as we explained above siman 261 in Biur Halacha s.v. that it is erev Shabbat] before emergence of three medium stars — it is safek night; and a quarter-hour is outside safek; and if born then on Shabbat — count him for the coming Shabbat; but several poskim are stringent and hold from when the sun was covered from our eyes until emergence of three medium stars — all is included in twilight; and so Berkei Yosef that this practice spread in all cities of Eretz Yisrael; and so in Sefer Zekhor LeAvraham and Sefer Seder Zmanim; and even if he doubts whether born before twilight or during twilight — likewise Radbaz ruled to be stringent that it is deferred to the first day; and later authorities brought him; and see in Biur Halacha:

(ג) And born when he is already circumcised — explanation: even without erection he appears circumcised; but when he does not appear circumcised — only when his milah hardens does milah override Shabbat:

(ד) Born by caesarean section — his mother had difficulty in birth and they brought him out along the belly by medicine:

(ה) Two arlot — explanation: two giddin; and some say there are two skins one atop the other:

(ו) Their milah does not override Shabbat — and the reason for all these is safek; such as regarding androgynous because he is not definitely male; and likewise born at twilight lest it is not at its time; born circumcised — perhaps there is no pressed foreskin here and he is truly circumcised; and one who has two arlot — namely two giddin — also because we do not know which foreskin is primary; and per the second reasoning that it deals with two skins one atop the other — also the reason is we do not know the Torah's intent here; except born by caesarean section and non-Jew who gave birth — there the reason is they are not impure with birth impurity and several conditions are required as stated in parashat Tazria that when a woman is impure from birth her son is circumcised on the eighth:
