[5] He did not bring a scalpel, etc. Olat Shabbat challenged: bring the infant to the scalpel — there is no Shabbat prohibition at all, for a living being carries itself; and it is not difficult, for regarding a minor who cannot walk we do not say "a living being carries itself" as he himself wrote siman 308 seif 41. And regarding moving a knife after milah — Taz Yoreh De'ah siman 266 expanded; and above siman 309 that it is forbidden except to store in that room — permitted so he not refrain from circumcising; and they permitted the end on account of the beginning; and Magen Avraham permits all the time it is in his hand to move to whatever place he wishes, but after he put it down — forbidden to move it — until here; and per this when there is one mohel and perforce he must put down the knife so he can perform priyah — forbidden to move them; therefore good that he give to another person's hand, and in this manner it is in Yerushalmi chapter Lulav HaGazul that permitted afterward to take from his friend's hand and move the lulav; also it appears to me to be lenient when there is concern it will be lost — then since he returns and takes it in his hand they permit him even to carry to his house as above siman 308 seif 2 regarding other muktzeh; and further Rama in Yoreh De'ah implies in every case permitted to move since it was not designated for twilight because he needed it that Shabbat; and proofs Taz and Magen Avraham brought for prohibition regarding skewer — can be rejected, for a knife is different which is prepared to move it and it was known from yesterday he certainly needs it for this — examine well; and so in Sefer Nikudat Kesef there it implies lenient in every case; and see siman 493 to rinse a milah knife — forbidden (Levush Yom Tov):
