Seif 5, "and born without a visible foreskin." This follows Rabbah there, who says that we are concerned for a hidden foreskin, and the halacha follows Rabbah against Rav Yosef. "And one born by Caesarean section, and a non-Jewish woman," etc., "and one," etc. The ruling remains doubtful because Rav Asi's position differs from the first Tanna. We are uncertain whether Rav Asi had heard the baraita and nevertheless disagreed with the first Tanna, or whether he had not heard the baraita and would have accepted it had he heard it.
