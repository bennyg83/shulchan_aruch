(5) Mamzer — they circumcise him on Shabbat. In that passage there. And Shach brought there s.k. 17. And he wrote that so is in Maharil. And so Kesef Sheni on this siman letter 1. And regarding Karaites — Shach wrote there in name of responsum Rabbeinu Betzalel Ashkenazi siman 3 that Karaites nowadays who do not acknowledge priyah — forbidden to circumcise their children on Shabbat — see there; and so Petaḥ HaDevarim on this siman letter 1 in name of several poskim; and he wrote: and even though in our time our eyes see they do priyah and also bless at the time of milah — perhaps because they are subordinate to us that they have no mohel from the Karaites; but among themselves they do not do priyah and do not bless — see there:
