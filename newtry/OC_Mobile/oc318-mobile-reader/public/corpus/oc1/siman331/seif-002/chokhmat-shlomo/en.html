There seif 6, milah accessories possible from erev Shabbat do not override Shabbat, etc. NB: see in Magen Avraham s.k. 5 what he wrote; and so from what is written siman 491 in name of Yerushalmi. His intent is what is written there in name of Yerushalmi that if he left lulav on the ground — forbidden to move on erev Shabbat. And in Machatzit HaShekel it was hidden from him Tosafos end of chapter Lulav VeAravah s.v. "merely moving" who wrote even though fit for its mitzvah it is muktzeh since it has no vessel designation on erev Shabbat; and if so proven even if the law were permitted to take on Shabbat it would be considered muktzeh even while still using it since it is not a vessel — only gemara challenged since it is merely moving it is fitting the mitzvah should defer this prohibition; and if so requires study what was challenged siman 491: behold there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat — this was said only if while still permitted to use it the muktzeh completely passed; therefore when muktzeh returned there is no muktzeh for half Shabbat; but with lulav the muktzeh did not pass — even while using it has muktzeh law and muktzeh always applies [therefore it appears at night or by day not for mitzvah need one should not move it] — only all the time he takes it the mitzvah defers the muktzeh as gemara challenged Shabbos that moving is merely such; and to defer Shabbat only from another reason forbidden on Shabbat and Yom Tov which is only muktzeh deferring mitzvah for moving; but when mitzvah passed muktzeh remains and "no muktzeh for half Shabbat" is irrelevant — all Shabbat is muktzeh since it is not a vessel; and automatically no proof for knife — there it is truly a vessel designated only on account of monetary loss fear to use it; but when prepared for one use it is not muktzeh at all at once and muktzeh passed; therefore even after use muktzeh did not apply — no muktzeh for half Shabbat; and see in my new work on Orach Chayim siman 491 what I wrote on this and examine well, for they are clear matters with Hashem's help:
