(א) (s.k. 2) Baer Heitev, etc. And it is known they are less than a quarter-hour as written siman 498, for the measure of a mil is a quarter-hour and one-twentieth of an hour; and it is approximately a quarter-hour:

(ב) (s.k. 2) And Bach himself in responsum siman 154 wrote one-third of an hour; and it is possible he holds like those who say the measure of a mil is two-fifths of an hour — if so three-quarters of a mil is close to one-third of an hour:

(ג) (s.k. 2) For even regarding melacha, etc. — meaning: for even regarding melacha it is permitted on erev Shabbat to do melacha until the time of three-quarters of a mil before night, only adding a little time on account of Tosafos Shabbos as written above siman 261 — all the more regarding milah that it should be called day until that time:

(ד) (s.k. 2) To accept Shabbat from sunset, etc. — if so likewise regarding Motzei Shabbat — meaning: since on erev Shabbat they accept Shabbat from sunset of the sun even though their intent is only on account of Tosafos Shabbos and they do not consider it night — nevertheless it appears for them it is night; if so likewise regarding Motzei Shabbat — meaning: even though on Motzei Shabbat they pray only at actual night and melacha is forbidden until nightfall — nevertheless perhaps for milah one should consider it night to be stringent:
