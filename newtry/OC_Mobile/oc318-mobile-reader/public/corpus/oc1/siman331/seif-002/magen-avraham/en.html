Twilight. Bach wrote, and in Yoreh De'ah siman 266 I wrote that if he was born one hour of the twenty-four before emergence of three medium stars — he is circumcised on the sixth day; and after that it is a safek and he is circumcised on the first day; and even though in siman 261 I wrote one should be stringent to accept Shabbat from the end of hour 22 — for milah it is not so — end of Bach; and requires study, for he himself ruled in Yoreh De'ah three-quarters of a mil before nightfall is twilight, and it is known they are less than a quarter-hour as written siman 459; and it is possible he erred in haste and thought it was three mil; and the essence is as written siman 266 s.k. 9 in Yoreh De'ah that from sunset it is a safek, meaning the end of sunset, and it is three-quarters of a mil as stated siman 261, and it is approximately a quarter-hour before nightfall; and if born before that — they circumcise on the sixth day, for even regarding melacha which is stoning prohibition we rule thus as stated above; and so in Sefer Machatzit HaShekel in Maggid Mishneh chapter 5; and so Radbaz part 1 chapter 282; and if born on Shabbat a quarter-hour before night — he wrote: and it appears to me for halacha to circumcise him on Shabbat, but not for practice unless most sages of Israel agree on this, for I do not wish to rely on my view in severe Shabbat desecration; and it is implied from his words that his safek is because they practiced in his place to accept Shabbat from sunset — if so, likewise regarding Motzei Shabbat; but in our country sometimes they do not accept Shabbat until close to a quarter-hour before nightfall as written siman 261 — if so, likewise regarding milah one may rely on this to circumcise him on Shabbat, for all the great poskim hold thus:
