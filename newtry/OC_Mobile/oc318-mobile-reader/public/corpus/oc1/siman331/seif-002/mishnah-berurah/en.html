(א) He returns, etc. — for even though he finished cutting the foreskin and after the fact fulfilled milah even without these tzitzin — nevertheless all the time he has not removed his hand from the milah it is called l'chatchila and it is all one matter, for Shabbat was given to be pushed aside for it; and see in Chiddushei Ran who wrote that even if he already removed his hand from the cutting and began priyah — it is still called he has not removed his hand from milah, for milah and priyah are one matter [however when engaged in metzitzah he is uncertain there whether permitted on Shabbat to return to tzitzin that are not impeding, lest perhaps metzitzah is called only healing and not milah need — see there]; and per this it is obvious that even if the one performing priyah is another — nevertheless the first, while engaged in its mitzvah, is permitted to return and finish tzitzin that are not impeding, for we do not regard that he removed his hand since milah mitzvah is not yet finished:
