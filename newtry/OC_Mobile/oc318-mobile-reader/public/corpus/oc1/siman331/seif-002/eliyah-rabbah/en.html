[2] [Levush] Twilight, etc. Later authorities explained specifically when born a quarter-hour close to nightfall; but born before a quarter-hour from nightfall — circumcise on the sixth day; and so if born on Shabbat a quarter-hour before night — circumcise on Shabbat.
