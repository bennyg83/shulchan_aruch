He shall not circumcise. Avraham Tzvi; and see in Shevut Yaakov part 3 siman 25 that if there is no other mohel and he knows like himself he can circumcise — permitted; and what Avraham Tzvi wrote regarding two mohalim, etc. — see in Shaagat Aryeh siman 59 who expanded; and his view that on Shabbat forbidden for mohel to remove his hand once he began circumcising until he finishes priyah; but if it happened he removed his hand — permitted also afterward to finish; and Shabbat and Yom Tov equal in this — see there; and in responsum Noda B'Yehuda second edition siman 22 he wrote he was stringent on himself, thus: I do not accept being mohel on Shabbat because I do not perform priyah; but for myself I am stringent; Hashem forbid to rule for others; and he brought there Yesh Seder Yevamos who is stringent on this; and he wrote to defer; and concludes thus: see public practice in all Poland — always two mohalim, one cuts and one performs priyah, weekday and Shabbat — end; and see in responsum Rama; and see in Eshel Avraham and in Machatzit HaShekel from abroad found in Eretz Yisrael when there is milah on second day of Diaspora Yom Tov in Nachpeh Bakesef he wrote forbidden to circumcise the visiting guest when there is town resident for whom it is weekday; and Mateh Yehuda permits — see there; and regarding milah not at its time on second day of Diaspora Yom Tov see Noda B'Yehuda siman 434; and will be explained elsewhere in Yom Tov laws; and see in Tiferet Tzvi siman 7 he also wrote to permit in this that through two there is more caution and alacrity — cutter delays putting down knife and the like, etc.; and similarly they permitted in harvesting, etc. — see there:
