(א) Ground cumin, etc. — and it is still before milah as will be explained:
(ב) He shall not grind it — for milah accessories do not override Shabbat; perforce if he cannot chew it with his teeth — milah is deferred; and not specifically grinding which is melacha, but even to bring cumin through an alley not joined is also forbidden as above seif 6. And likewise he shall not say "I will circumcise him and when he needs spices afterward for danger I will grind," for pikuach nefesh overrides Shabbat — since now he knows it is impossible for him without spices after milah — better milah be deferred and he not bring himself to Shabbat desecration since it was possible to prepare from yesterday. And know that if it became known to him after milah he has no cumin — certainly permitted to bring even through reshut ha-rabbim and grind; and nevertheless if possible to change in all this without harm to the ill person — he must change as explained siman 328 seif 12:
