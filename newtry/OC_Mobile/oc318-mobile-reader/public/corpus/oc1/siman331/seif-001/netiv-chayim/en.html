(Magen Avraham s.k. 5.) His intent is what we hold. NB: and requires study, for if his intent is as his explanation — this reason of taste is irrelevant, for after he moved permissibly even though he moved as prohibition while muktzeh is in his hand he moves to whatever place he wishes — perforce it appears his intent: since after moving the vessel permissibly for milah mitzvah overriding Shabbat — he returns as we learned in Eruvin daf 45: all who go out to save — they gave them permission to desecrate Shabbat — they return to their original place; and that which we say there they would leave weapons in house near wall — that is because sometimes there is reshut ha-rabbim and liable for sin-offering; unlike muktzeh prohibition — all the time in his hand permitted to return wherever he wishes; however if he once placed in synagogue in guarded place — then not permitted to move it; and so Rama agreed Yoreh De'ah siman 266 and Taz and Shach and Maharshal in responsum and chapter Ein Tzedim siman 16; and so is public practice:
