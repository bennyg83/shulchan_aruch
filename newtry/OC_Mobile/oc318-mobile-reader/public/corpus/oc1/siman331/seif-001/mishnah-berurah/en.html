(1) "On Shabbos." The verse states, "And on the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be cut," from which the Sages derive: on the eighth day, even when it is Shabbos. [Gemara] (2) "And one performs metzitzah." Although metzitzah detaches blood from its place and would ordinarily constitute wounding, it is nevertheless performed because failure to draw out the blood poses danger to the infant. [Gemara] (3) "Cumin." Their practice was to place a type of ground spice upon the site for healing. The same law applies to placing an isplenis, a medicinal dressing, upon the brit milah site. [Mishnah there] See below in seif 7 regarding a case where ground cumin had not been prepared before Shabbos.
