Seif 4 permitted to tell a non-Jew to let blood. And it is from Mordechai's words because if you do not permit him he will do so himself, for a person is distressed over his property; and I question: what is different from a fire where they permit only to say "whoever extinguishes does not lose," but not to tell a gentile to extinguish; and on the contrary in Ran chapter Chavis the first answer is that by law it was permitted in a fire to tell a gentile to extinguish, for shvut in a place of loss is permitted like in a pipe — only they are concerned since he is distressed lest he do so himself; and see in Ran beginning of chapter Mi sheHechishich; and according to Ramban there who distinguishes between property in his hand and not in his hand, and according to Ran there who distinguishes between something sudden — see there; and according to those reasons here in letting blood it is similar to a fire and there is no difference between them, and requires study:
