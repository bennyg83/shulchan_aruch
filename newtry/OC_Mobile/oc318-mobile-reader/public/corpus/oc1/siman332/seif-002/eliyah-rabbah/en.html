(2) Permitted to tell, etc. Nachalat Tzvi wondered from siman 307 seif 5 that they permitted shvut only for a d'rabbanan prohibition in a place of mitzvah, until here; and in my humble opinion there is no difficulty, for here it is different as the reason is explained in Mordechai end of chapter Bameh Behemah that if you do not permit him he will come to do it himself. And behold Taz brings this reason and wrote on it these are his words: and according to this one must say that this of "one may run it" mentioned before is not in this matter that he fears lest it die, and therefore they were not stringent there, until here his words; and it is astonishing, for on the contrary if he is not concerned lest it die it would be fitting to be stringent, for he is not distressed to do a d'oraisa prohibition himself; and it is possible there is a scribal error in his words and requires study; and furthermore behold in Tosefta end of chapter 17 it is explicit that there is concern lest it die:
