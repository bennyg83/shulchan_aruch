(20) Melted animal fat. Either melted over a fire, or liquefied fat from the animal's intestines that has not yet congealed. If it congealed, Ran forbids it even after adding oil. Eliyah Rabbah rules that this is primary. I later found that Ramban, Rashba, and Ritva rule likewise and prove it from the Yerushalmi.
(21) And fish innards. That have dissolved. Clear oil made from a fish's eye socket or flesh is permitted even by itself.
(22) It is permitted, etc. Under the basic law melted fat would be permitted because it follows the wick well, but the Sages decreed against it because of unmelted fat. They did not extend the decree to a mixture, which would be one decree added to another.
