(29) In rags — this means coarse linen rags; and the reason is that the light does not catch well in them — see Peri Megadim. One does not light on the table with an old earthenware lamp because it is repulsive unless he kindled it with a flame; and if it is of glass or coated earthenware it is permitted, for it is not so repulsive. And for a poor person who has no certainty, it is permitted from what is left over from the Shabbat candle, which is obligatory [Peri Megadim]:
