(29) With rags. Meaning worn pieces of coarse linen garments; the flame does not catch them well. See Pri Megadim. One should not light at the table in an old earthenware lamp because it is repulsive, unless it was fired in a flame. A glass or glazed earthenware lamp is permitted because it is not so repulsive. A poor person who has no other lamp may certainly use it rather than forgo the obligatory Shabbos lamp [Pri Megadim].
