(23) The choicest. Olive oil follows the wick better than all others. If unavailable, there is a mitzvah to use other oils whose light is clear; they take precedence over a wax candle, and a wax candle precedes a tallow candle because its light is clear and beautiful [Mateh Moshe]. It appears that all depends upon the lamp. Our stearin candle, whose light is certainly clearer than wax and which one does not ordinarily tilt even on a weekday, is preferable to wax and perhaps even to all oils, since one will certainly not tilt it. See what we write below in siman 275.
