6) There, gloss: It is forbidden to use its light. It is likewise forbidden to study by its light, and the presence of another person to remind him does not help. Taz, siman 673, paragraph 4; Eliyah Rabbah in this siman, paragraph 3; Machatzis HaShekel, paragraph 1. Similarly, all cases permitted in siman 275, such as for a distinguished person and the like, are forbidden here. Even if he has no valid wicks or oils other than these, he may not kindle them. So write the later authorities.
