7) There, gloss: Some say that if there is one lamp made from permitted materials, etc. This is the ruling of Hagahos Mordechai cited by Beis Yosef, that whenever there is one valid lamp in one location, it will remind him and he will not tilt the other lamps in the courtyard or house; see there. We already wrote above in paragraph 2 that most halachic authorities dispute this, and that Tur and the Shulchan Aruch also reject it; see there. Levush likewise omits this ruling because most authorities forbid it. Bach and Nezer Yisrael also rule like those who forbid it, as stated in Eliyah Rabbah.
