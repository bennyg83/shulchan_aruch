Paragraph 7: With wax; meaning that he places it, etc. This means placing solid or melted wax in a vessel in place of oil and inserting a wick. It excludes wrapping wax around a wick, which se'if 7 permits, unlike the sages of Narbonne who forbid it. If one wraps wax around the wick and places it in oil, even the sages of Narbonne agree that it is permitted. See the Rishonim.
