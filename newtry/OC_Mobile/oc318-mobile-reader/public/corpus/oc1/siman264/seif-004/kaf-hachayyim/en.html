4) There, gloss: If he kindled using forbidden materials, it is forbidden, etc. Accordingly, if he has only that room, he must leave it. Rambam's words, however, imply that if he transgressed and kindled it, he is not required to leave. This is also implied by Rokeach, siman 45, who writes that one who transgressed and kindled it may use its light. Nehar Shalom, paragraph 1. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, writes the same.
