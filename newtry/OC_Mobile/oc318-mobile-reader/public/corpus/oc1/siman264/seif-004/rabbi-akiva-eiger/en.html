Magen Avraham, s.k. 12: Nevertheless, a wax candle. That is to say, if he has no other oils and has both a wax candle and a tallow candle, then even if we take into account the authorities who forbid wax, it would appear that one made of melted tallow is preferable, since with such a candle there is no concern that he might tilt it; its prohibition is only a decree on account of tallow that is not melted. Nevertheless, wax is preferable, because tallow is repulsive.
