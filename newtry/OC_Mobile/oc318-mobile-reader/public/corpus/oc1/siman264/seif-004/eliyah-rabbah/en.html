[4] To stiffen, etc. The Gemara's term is "lehakafos." Rashi explains it from the Aramaic translation of "the iron floated" as "vekafa." It appears that Maggid Mishneh in chapter 5 intended this when he says Rashi explained so; meaning Rashi explained the word lehakafos. Beis Yosef and the later authorities strained unnecessarily. See Bach and Taz's challenges to Beis Yosef: according to Rashi, the material lies beneath the wick, not around it. They overlooked Rabbeinu Yerucham, part 2, which explicitly supports Beis Yosef. I have abbreviated because we rule that the law depends upon intention; if his intention is to stiffen the wick, it is permitted even if both materials burn together.
