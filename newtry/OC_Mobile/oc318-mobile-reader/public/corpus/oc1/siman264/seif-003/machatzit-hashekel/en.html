Paragraph 3: Without a lamp; for example, drawing wine. This example is stated as a novelty, since drawing wine also requires a lamp. Magen Avraham in siman 263, paragraph 15, cites Maharil that lighting a lamp in a cellar in order to draw wine is a food-related need and one may recite a blessing over it. Nevertheless, it does not require such close attention that we should fear he might tilt the lamp. See Eliyah Rabbah.
