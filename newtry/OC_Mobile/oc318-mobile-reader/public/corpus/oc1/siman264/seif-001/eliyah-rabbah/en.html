[1] Between every lamp, etc. Bach is stringent even when a valid lamp stands beside an invalid one. Magen Avraham challenges him and writes that one may use the light, for it is no worse than the case of a single additional lamp in siman 276, se'if 4, or the shamash beside Chanukah lights in siman 673. One can distinguish our case: concern that he may tilt remains even though he can see by the valid lamp, because a person wants both lamps to burn well. In those cases, the law depends upon benefit; since he can use the other lamp, the added light is not considered benefit. For the practical ruling, see below.
