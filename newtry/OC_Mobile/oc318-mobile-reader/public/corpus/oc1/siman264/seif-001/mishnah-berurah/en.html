(1) It discusses, etc. The reason certain wicks and oils are invalid is that they do not burn brightly, and we fear he may tilt the vessel so its oil reaches the wick and burns well, thereby becoming liable for kindling.
(2) And hemp. Eliyah Rabbah writes that initially it is proper to be stringent with hemp when another material is available.
(3) That are forbidden. This includes both invalid wicks and invalid oils.
(4) It is forbidden to make use. Even a use requiring no close examination is forbidden, lest he tilt the lamp. A task requiring examination may not be performed on Shabbos by any lamp, even a good one, as in siman 275. Taz below in siman 673 writes that in our case it remains forbidden even when another person is present watching him, because avoiding tilting is impossible.
(5) It is permitted to make use. Magen Avraham explains this even where the lamps stand in another room: once one valid lamp is present somewhere, he will remember and not tilt elsewhere. Eliyah Rabbah says Rema means specifically in the same room. Biur HaGra, s.v. v'yesh omrim d'im, compares this to the present opinion concerning a Chanukah lamp, where use is permitted only if a valid lamp stands beside it, not in another room; he therefore understands like Eliyah Rabbah. Korban Nesanel is likewise stringent when it stands in another room.
(6) Without a lamp. Such as lying down in the room or drawing wine from the cellar, provided it is drawn through a vessel called a Hahn or lever in German, but not directly through the spigot.
(7) It is permitted to do. Eliyah Rabbah explains that at least one valid lamp must be in the house, which permits use even in another room. The other later authorities explain that according to this opinion it is permitted even with no valid lamp at all.
(8) After the fact. If one valid lamp and one invalid lamp stand beside him, Magen Avraham permits using their light even for a non-Shabbos need, since it is no worse than having only one lamp, as in siman 276:4; see there and Biur Halachah. This applies only to use after they were lit: because the valid lamp burns well, he will not tilt. It does not permit initially lighting a valid lamp together with an invalid one [Pri Megadim; Levushei Serad].
