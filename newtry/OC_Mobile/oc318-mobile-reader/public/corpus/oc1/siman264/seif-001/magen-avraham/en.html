"It is forbidden to use." Even for a use that does not require close examination, because one may come to tilt the lamp. See siman 275. "It is permitted to use." Even if the lamps are standing in another room, because once there is a valid lamp somewhere else, he will remember and will not tilt even in the other places. [Ibid.] "Without a lamp." For example, to draw wine from a cellar. [Ibid.] "After the fact." It appears to me that if there is 1 valid lamp and beside it stands a lamp that is invalid for use, it is permitted to make use of the light. It is no worse than having only 1 lamp, as stated in siman 276. Although Bach is stringent here, he follows his own reasoning in siman 673, where he writes that a shamash does not permit use of the Chanukah lights. According to our ruling there, however, it is clearly permitted.
