Paragraph 1: It is forbidden, etc.; that does not require close inspection, etc. See siman 275, where it is explained that even with valid oil it is forbidden to perform by its light a task requiring close inspection. The present ruling therefore concerns even a task that requires no close inspection. Taz below in siman 673, paragraph 3, writes that although siman 275 permits a task by a valid wick if another person is present, when the wick or oil is invalid, another person's presence does not help.
