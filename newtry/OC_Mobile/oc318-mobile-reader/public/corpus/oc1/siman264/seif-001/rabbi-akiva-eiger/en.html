Magen Avraham, s.k. 4: This follows his reasoning. Seemingly, it is not comparable, for there, since it is forbidden to derive benefit from a Chanukah lamp, even when there is another lamp he nevertheless benefits from the Chanukah lamp because of its added light. Here, however, since the valid lamp by itself would permit a minor use, and we are not concerned that he might tilt it in order to increase its light; that is, since a minor use does not require additional light, he will not tilt it; what difference does it make that the invalid lamp is also burning? He has a valid light that burns well, and he will not tilt it. Examine this carefully.
