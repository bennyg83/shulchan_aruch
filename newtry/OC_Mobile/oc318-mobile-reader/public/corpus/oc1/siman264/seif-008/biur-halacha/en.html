Most of what protrudes. Therefore, when Yom Tov occurs on Erev Shabbos, one may not light with pieces of cloth measuring exactly three by three tefachim. Once a small portion burns, it becomes a broken utensil, because it no longer has the status of a garment or utensil, and one may not continue lighting it, as stated in siman 501. This is permitted only if he folded the cloth into a wick before Yom Tov, thereby removing its status as a utensil. Magen Avraham, citing the Gemara.
