Paragraph 9: And leave it, etc. See the end of siman 273: in a place of great discomfort one need not recite Kiddush and eat beside the lamp, but may eat in the courtyard. Siman 263, se'if 9, likewise explains that the mitzvah of the lamp was given for enjoyment, not distress.
There is another reason, etc. These words are mistakenly positioned. They refer to balsam oil, for which Shulchan Aruch gives the reason that one may take some of it, etc.; Magen Avraham adds that the Gemara gives another reason.
