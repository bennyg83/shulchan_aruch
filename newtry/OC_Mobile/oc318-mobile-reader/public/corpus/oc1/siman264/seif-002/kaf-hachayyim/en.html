It also excludes the ruling cited in the name of the officer of Coucy, that an activity one can perform without a lamp, such as drawing wine from a barrel, may be performed by the light of these lamps. Many halachic authorities dispute these rulings, as Beis Yosef writes, adding that this is Tur's view; see there. This also appears to be Bach's view. See below, paragraph 7.
