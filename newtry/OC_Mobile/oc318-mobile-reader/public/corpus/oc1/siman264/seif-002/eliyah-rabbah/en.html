[5] And for this reason, etc. Beis Yosef writes that everyone permits this because reeds or straw are not fit to serve as wicks by themselves, so there is no decree lest one light them alone. Magen Avraham therefore says that even if one intended to thicken the wick it is permitted. This is incorrect. Beis Yosef writes that only in the name of Rashba and Semak; according to Sefer HaTerumah and Mordechai it is forbidden, because they hold those materials are fit by themselves.
