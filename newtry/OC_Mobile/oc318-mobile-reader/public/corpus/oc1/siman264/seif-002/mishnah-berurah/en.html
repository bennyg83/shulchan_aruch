(10) It is permitted. Even when he lights both together, since he does not intend to light the invalid material as a wick, no decree was made.
(11) Of salt. Because the salt draws down the oil so that it will follow the wick.
(12) Of a bean. To rest the wick upon. Tosefta, chapter 2, gives the reason that one does this so it will burn for a long time.
