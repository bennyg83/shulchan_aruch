On pepper and ginger, etc. — ginger is ingber and is a root under the ground; therefore, even though it is fit for eating, since it is not the body of the fruit one blesses borei peri haAdamah (Bach). Therefore it appears to me the same law applies to a tree called "acreitz," and so it is in Sefer Chassidim siman 22 that one should bless upon it borei peri haAdamah.
