16) Over arrope one recites SheHakol; this is straightforward. Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim. Arrope is made by pressing grapes and, after pressing, placing a certain known white earth into the grape juice for 1 hour or longer, so that the wine produced from it clarifies quickly and retains its sweetness. It is then heated until it reaches yad soledet bo temperature. Sherbet is made similarly. My view likewise is that one should recite SheHakol over it, and I believe this is the custom in Constantinople. Knesset HaGedolah, Responsa, volume 2, siman 1; Yad Aharon there, Hagahot Beit Yosef, paragraph 1. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 2, likewise writes in the name of R' Avraham Azulai that over arrope, which is congealed grape juice, one recites SheHakol. The same applies to cooked grapes called trankopit and to kamardin made from apricots; one recites SheHakol. See below, siman 204, paragraph 63. Know that some people of deeds do not eat the kamardin sold in the market, because it is known that market producers prepare large quantities together and may include worm-infested fruit. Flying insects or crawling insects may also develop in it. They eat it only after it has been soaked in water and strained.
