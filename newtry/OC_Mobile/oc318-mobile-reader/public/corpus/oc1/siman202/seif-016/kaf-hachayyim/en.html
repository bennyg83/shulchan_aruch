16) Over arrope, one recites Shehakol, and this is obvious. Beis Yosef in the name of Orchos Chaim. Arrope is made by pressing grapes and, after pressing, placing a certain white earth into the grape juice for an hour or longer, so that the wine emerging from them clarifies quickly and retains its sweetness. It is then heated until it is hot enough that the hand recoils. Sherbet is made similarly. My view agrees that one should recite Shehakol over it, and I believe this is the practice in Constantinople. Knesses HaGedolah, Responsa, volume 2, siman 1; Yad Aharon there, Hagahos Beis Yosef, paragraph 1. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 2, likewise writes in the name of R' Avraham Azulai that over arrope, which is congealed grape juice, one recites Shehakol. The same applies to cooked grapes called trankopit and to kamardin made from apricots; one recites Shehakol; see there. See below, siman 204, paragraph 63. Know that some people of deeds do not eat the kamardin sold in the market, because it is known that market producers prepare a large quantity together, including worm-infested fruit. Flying insects from the air or crawling insects from the ground may also form in it. They eat it only if it is soaked in water and strained.
