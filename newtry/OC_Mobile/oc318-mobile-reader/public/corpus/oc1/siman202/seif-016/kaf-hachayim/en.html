(16) On arufi — one blesses shehakol, and it is simple. Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim. And the matter of arufi is that they tread the grapes, and after treading them they put in the must known white dust for one hour or more in order that the wine coming from them quickly be preserved and stand in its sweetness, and afterward they heat it so that yad soledes bo. And they do the same with sherbet, and my view agrees to bless upon it shehakol, and it seems that in Koshta they practice thus. Keneset HaGedolah in responsum volume 2 siman 1; Yad Ephraim there in Hagahot Beit Yosef note 1; and so too Birkei Yosef note 2 in the name of Mahar"a Azulai that on arufi, which is grape water that froze, one blesses shehakol. And likewise cooked grapes called Trankovit, and so Kamerani wine that they make from muscatel — one blesses shehakol — see there; and see below siman 204 note 63. And know that there are men of action who do not eat the Kamerani wine sold in the market, because it is known that what is sold in the market they make much together and there are worms in them, and it is also possible that mosquitoes will form in them from those flying in the air or from those crawling in the ground — unless they soak them in water and filter them.
