(77) And ginger — it is what is called ginger.
(78) And everything similar — such as galgilin and citron.
(79) That it is not the way, etc. — explanation: and he eats them alone; therefore one does not bless on them at all, for there is no enjoyment in eating them when dry; and if he eats them with sugar and the like — one blesses borei peri haAdamah [Peri Megadim].
