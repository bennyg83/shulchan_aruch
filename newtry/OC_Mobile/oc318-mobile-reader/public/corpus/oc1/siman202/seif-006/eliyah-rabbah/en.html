[Levush] The main fruit, etc. — for we do not derive them from one hundred, but rather we consider them the main fruit — so is implied the view of R' Yitzchak and the Rosh. And it is difficult, for in the laws of orlah the Rosh learned pit from one hundred (Mlbushi Yom Tov), and see in Ma'adanei Melekh daf 44. I also require study, for in Tosafot daf 36 and Mordekhai it implies that one blesses even on walnut shells, even though we learn from one hundred; and it is possible that such is the view of the Rosh, but since they are bitter they do not consider it.
