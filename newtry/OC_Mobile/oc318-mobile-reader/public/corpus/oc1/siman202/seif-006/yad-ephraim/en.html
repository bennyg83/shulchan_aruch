Note 17 — borei peri haAdamah, for it is not primary fruit: such is the view of Rif and Rambam and Tosafot; and Rosh disagrees, that there is no proof from orlah outside the Land, because whoever is lenient — and therefore one blesses borei peri haEtz; and likewise Mordekhai — see there. Also in Arukh, entry tzalef, and what he wrote the reason — because it guards the fruit — this requires further study: what connection does guarding have to blessing? As Taz and Peri Megadim wrote regarding orange peel. And in Sha'agat Aryeh siman 523 he wrote the reason for Rif: if one eats capers outside the Land and blesses borei peri haEtz, there are two contradictory matters — see there. In truth, Ma'adanei Melekh already addressed this — see there; but in the aforementioned kontras I wrote that there is no proof from this, as Rosh wrote at the beginning of Berakhot regarding prayer — that we apply leniencies that contradict each other, for in prayer they were lenient; and likewise here, whoever is lenient. And I brought proof from that which they challenge: let Mar act like Beit Shammai; and likewise from what is written on page 40 regarding species of boxwood; and from the law of kishut that is not kilayim and one blesses borei peri haAdamah; and I also expanded there on the law of dates and in a responsum on this.
