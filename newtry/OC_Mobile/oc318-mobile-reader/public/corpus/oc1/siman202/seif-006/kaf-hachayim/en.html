(6) There — borei peri haEtz. — Fruit that grows in the Land of Israel in great abundance until they make from it a fence and barrier for gardens and orchards, and it is called Igo di Adam (and in Arabic they call it sabras), and the peel of its fruit is full of thorns, and likewise the leaves; and its planting is for one leaf in the ground and it is covered with leaves from here and from there, and when they take the fruits for another year a leaf grows there and fruits — one blesses upon it borei peri haEtz. And the time — for whenever the fruit comes from branches, per the geonim it is a tree; and for R' Yonah everything depends on whether one needs to sow it every year. Rav Pri Adamah volume 1 daf 24a and daf 25a, and Rav Kiryat Melekh Rav volume 1 siman 10 agreed with him — see there; and Beit Din in Birkei Yosef note 1; Ikarei HaDat siman 10 note 71; Chasam Sofer note 1. And if it is known that it did not complete two years of orlah — forbidden — Derech Mitzvotecha in Hagahot HaTur note 1. And likewise on pinionis de Hebron one blesses upon them borei peri haEtz and afterward borei nefashot. Pri HaAdamah there; Chasam Sofer there.
