{Rama: seif 2} And some say on carobs — Rama understood that the Mechaber who omitted this holds that on carobs and olives too one blesses from when they produce; therefore he brought the dispute that it is precise only from when they appear like chains, etc. And Rama did not see Sefer Bedek HaBayit, which wrote the opposite: that carobs from when they appear like chains — even though people treat them with shevi'it laws and they are considered fruit for shevi'it — nevertheless one may not learn for berakha, for we see carobs even after they appear like chains are bitter; therefore the Mechaber stated here anonymously on all trees from when they produce, provided they are not bitter, etc. — carobs and olives are automatically included among all things upon which one blesses borei peri haAdamah until we see clearly they are already fit for eating and were planted with intent for this; then one blesses borei peri haEtz; and all the time there is doubt one blesses borei peri haAdamah; and likewise on carobs even after they appear like chains — this is the intent of Magen Avraham note 6.
