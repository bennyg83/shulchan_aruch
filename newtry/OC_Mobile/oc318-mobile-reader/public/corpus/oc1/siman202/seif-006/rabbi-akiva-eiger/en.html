Seif 6. Over the caper husks one recites Borei Peri HaAdamah. If one recited Borei Peri HaEtz over them, he has fulfilled his obligation. This is so even according to Even HaEzer below, siman 206, who holds that if one recites Borei Peri HaEtz over something that is not the principal fruit, he has not fulfilled his obligation, contrary to Magen Avraham. Here, however, he has fulfilled his obligation, because according to Rosh the proper blessing over caper husks is Borei Peri HaEtz. Thus, because of the doubt, he has fulfilled his obligation. Even according to Rif, since they are considered fruit with respect to orlah in Eretz Yisrael, the matter is at least doubtful. We compare the law of blessings to the law outside Eretz Yisrael: just as we are lenient regarding orlah outside Eretz Yisrael, so too, because of the doubt, one initially recites Borei Peri HaAdamah. After the fact, however, if he recited Borei Peri HaEtz, he has fulfilled his obligation because of the doubt.
