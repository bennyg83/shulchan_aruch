On the leaves. For people plant them also with intent to eat from the leaves [Ri"v, Gemara].

Tamarot (the clusters on the caper bush). They project on its branch like the leaves of the willow.

Kaparisin (caperberries). It is a shell that grew around the fruit, like a shell that grows around thin nuts [Rashi].

borei peri haAdamah. For it is not the main fruit; and some wish to learn from here that a peel of fruit such as oranges that they candy — one blesses borei peri haAdamah; but some say that thus we say in the Gemara: when it comes to cooking, the peel falls from it, and also even if you take it, the fruit does not die — but this peel is from the body of the fruit; and further, regarding orlah it is fruit, as Magen Avraham wrote in Yoreh De'ah siman 294, and likewise in the Gemara: pomegranate peels etc., and the seeds are subject to orlah, and Tosafot wrote from here that one blesses on the seeds borei peri haEtz, as he wrote in seif 3 — if so, the same applies to peels; and some say regarding soft almonds in responsum 24, that there people did not plant with intent to eat them in their shells, for the fruit has not yet finished ripening — and one could say because it is not fit for eating without ladling; behold roses too are not fit for eating without ladling, nevertheless we have no concern, since the main fixing is through that, as Rav Eliyahu wrote siman 204 note 11 and see there; and further that nevertheless they are fit to eat them raw, only it is not so good, for also the inside of the fruit is not good without fixing and sugar mixtures — therefore it appears to me that one blesses borei peri haEtz, for also regarding kaparisin some say one blesses borei peri haEtz, and see seif 3.
