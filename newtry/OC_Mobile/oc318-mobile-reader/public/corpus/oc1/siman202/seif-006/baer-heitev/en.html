The caper husks. These are the peels of the fruit, like nutshells.
Borei Peri HaAdamah. Because they are not the principal fruit. From here Taz derives proof below, siman 204, seif katan 15, that one recites Borei Peri HaAdamah over orange peel preserved in honey. Magen Avraham, however, in this siman, seif katan 17, disagrees. He writes that the cases are not comparable: here the peel falls away from the fruit, whereas orange peel is part of the fruit itself and is fit to be eaten, although it is not especially good without preparation and the addition of sugar. Since the inside of the fruit is likewise not good without preparation, one should recite Borei Peri HaEtz. See there. Responsa Panim Meiros, siman 65, likewise proves that one recites Borei Peri HaEtz, contrary to Taz. I found that one should recite Shehakol over preserved orange peel, and this is the practice throughout the lands of Ishmael. So states the Baer Heitev before me. Eliyah Rabbah likewise rules that Shehakol is recited over preserved orange peel.
