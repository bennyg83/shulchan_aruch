Kaparisin. These are the peels of the fruit, like nut shells.

Borei peri haAdamah. For they are not the essence of the fruit; from here Taz brought proof below in siman 204 note 15 regarding orange peel that they spice in honey — that one blesses borei peri haAdamah. But Magen Avraham in siman 7 note 17 disagrees and wrote it is not comparable: here the peel falls off, but regarding oranges the peel is from the body of the fruit and is fit to be eaten, only that it is not so good without preparation and mixture of sugar; therefore one should bless borei peri haEtz — see there; and likewise in responsa Pnei Meiros siman 65 he was compelled that one blesses borei peri haEtz, unlike Taz. And I found that one should bless shehakol on candied oranges, and such is the practice in all the lands of Ishmael that are before me. (And likewise ruled in the book Eliyah Rabbah to bless shehakol on candied oranges.)
