The tzelaf [caper bush] {explanation: the tzelaf is a type of tree whose leaves are fit to eat, and it has upon it something resembling dates. The evyonos are the fruit of the tzelaf, and the kaprisin are the shell surrounding the fruit, like the shells around nuts} — upon the leaves, upon the temaros [shoots], and upon the kaprisin one recites "Borei Pri Ha'adamah"; and upon the evyonos, which are the primary fruit, "Borei Pri Ha'etz."
