Caper (meaning: caper is a type of tree whose leaves are fit for eating, and on its branches something like dates; avyonot is the fruit of the caper; and kaparisin are shells around the fruit like nut shells) — on the leaves, and on the tamarot, and on the kaparisin one blesses borei peri haAdamah; and on the avyonot that are the main fruit — borei peri haEtz.
