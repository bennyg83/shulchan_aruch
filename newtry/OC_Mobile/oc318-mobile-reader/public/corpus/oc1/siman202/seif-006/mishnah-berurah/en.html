(38) On the leaves and on the tamarot — for they are not important enough to be called fruit of the tree.
(39) And on the kaparisim, etc. — because it is not the main fruit. Nevertheless b'dieved if he blessed on kaparisim borei peri haEtz he fulfilled. And regarding peel of pomerantz that they candy in honey — there are opinions among the latter authorities whether to bless on them shehakol or borei peri haEtz and borei peri haAdamah; and therefore one should bless on them shehakol, and b'dieved if he blessed borei peri haEtz or borei peri haAdamah he fulfilled.
