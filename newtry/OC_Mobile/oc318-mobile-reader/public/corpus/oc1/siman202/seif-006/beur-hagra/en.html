Seif 6, The caper, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. vehilchesa, “and it was,” etc. The Rosh raises the same difficulty. It appears to me that at first glance one may answer the Gemara’s question, “Let the master act like Beis Shammai,” etc.: there it says that the teaching comes to inform us that the halachah follows whoever is lenient, etc. That differs from Beis Shammai, who is lenient only outside Eretz Yisrael. One must therefore say that the Gemara mentions only Rabbi Akiva because he is lenient, and his ruling is stated without qualification as the words of the lenient authority, not because of the rule that the one who is lenient in Eretz Yisrael, etc. This is the meaning of, “The halachah follows Mar bar Rav Ashi,” and not Rav Yehudah. See Yoreh Deah 294:3. Lechem Chamudos explains Tosafos’ question differently: since regarding orlah, etc. Meaning, because we are uncertain, we rule stringently in Eretz Yisrael and leniently outside the Land, and therefore the one who is lenient in Eretz Yisrael, etc., because a doubt regarding orlah outside the Land is permitted. The same applies regarding blessings, where we follow the lenient opinion because in cases of doubt concerning blessings we are lenient. As explained above, this is what is meant by following Rabbi Akiva, who is lenient.
