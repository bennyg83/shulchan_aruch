Seif 6 — caper, etc. See Tosafot there s.v. vehalakhta, etc.; and thus Rash questioned. And to me it appears that seemingly one could say: what does he challenge there "let the master act like Beit Shammai," etc. — behold he said there "it teaches us: whoever is lenient," etc. — not so for Beit Hillel who is lenient only outside the Land; and it must be said that therefore he said only like R' Akiva who is lenient, and his view is anonymously like the words of the lenient ones, and not on account of the lenient ones in the Land; and thus he wrote "and the halakha is like Mar bar Rav Ashi and not like Rav Yehuda"; and see Yoreh De'ah siman 294 note 3. And Lechem Chamudot explained to resolve Tosafot's difficulty, for his intent is: since regarding orlah, etc. — meaning, since we are in doubt and follow stringency in the Land and leniency outside the Land, therefore the lenient ones in the Land, etc. on account of their doubt outside the Land being permitted; and the same applies for berakha, for we hold like the lenient ones that doubt in berakhot is to be lenient — and as he wrote that thus he said like R' Akiva who is lenient:
