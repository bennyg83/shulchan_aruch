Ends. Of grapes. See Taz. Etz Chaim, seif katan 12, explains similarly: if they are already fruit, one recites Borei Peri HaEitz and does not need Shehakol. Unripe grapes differ because they are not yet the principal fruit; when they ripen they will become fruit. This will be explained further, with Hashem's help, in Magen Avraham seif katan 20.
