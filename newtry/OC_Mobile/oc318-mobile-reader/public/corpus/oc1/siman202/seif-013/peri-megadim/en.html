On the sugar cane — Taz. And in truth it is difficult, since these reeds were planted with intent thus and their way is to squeeze and to cook — if so, both according to Rashba and Rosh siman 11 and siman 61 — fit to bless borei peri haEtz. And behold if one squeezes those reeds — according to Rosh squeezing is not like cooking; Taz note 9 and he concluded: if it has tree fruit one blesses and also intends for this; nevertheless in coffee that they put sugar one blesses shehakol even though it is made for taste, it has no substance — Taz 1 and Peri Megadim seif 12 and it will be explained there. And on the coffee that Nimukei Orach Chayim 14:114 note 6 and Panim Me'irot vol. 2 siman 62 — and even though water of cooked items when cooked while standing — therefore Magen Avraham Rosh Chodesh note 6. And for medicine obviously for healing one drinks them shehakol for the water is primary — Peri Megadim Magen Avraham 9 will explain from sugar cane there.
