(66) Nougat — meaning that its growth is already finished and they split it and discard from it the shell that is like wood, and take the kernel and roast it in honey, and it is called in a foreign language nougat.

(67) One blesses on it, etc. — and even though nuts are customarily eaten raw, and if one cooked them in water they change for the worse — nevertheless when they fix it through roasting in honey they change for the better through the honey and it is like something that is good raw and cooked. And also it teaches us that we do not say here that the honey is primary and he should bless upon it the blessing of shehakol as the blessing of honey, as is explained similarly below siman 205 seif 1 in the Rama gloss — in this that the honey does not come for itself but to give taste in the nut, the nut is primary and the honey secondary, and one blesses borei peri haEtz; and the same applies to all kinds of fruits such as this [borei peri haEtz or borei peri haAdamah] that are customarily eaten only raw, and if one cooked them in water their blessing would change to shehakol — if he roasted them in oil or honey one blesses the blessing appropriate to them.

(68) borei peri haEtz — the latter authorities wrote that even if the nut is not whole but pieces and broken as is customary when roasting them — nevertheless its blessing did not change; however, at any rate we require that they not be excessively crushed and ground such that at least a little of its form is recognizable, for in a mass where its shape is not recognizable at all one blesses only shehakol.
