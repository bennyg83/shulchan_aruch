And if — Taz. His view, z"l, that he disagrees regarding fig beer, and so is found in Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba that whenever there is a primary and with it something secondary; however Magen Avraham note 2 did not explain thus (and Rashbam, Teshuvot, and Beit Yosef — Rashba); and see Levush and Ateret Zekenim 2, and he challenged from Yoreh De'ah siman 324 seif 9 (and there in note 9 and Shach 17 one can say specifically rice that follows after the water, but in Rosh it appears the reason is only where there are five species of grain); and the five species are significant — if so, even where there is no taste, as in Rosh Chodesh Taz note 3 that even if there is no taste, and it will be explained there — therefore one must say that for the matter of challah their significance does not help except only for the five species of grain; if so, all the more so for the matter of blessing it helps for the five species of grain; and I say that there is a blessing on the beer shehakol and on the wine borei peri haGefen — we follow after the majority; not so there; and apparently the reason is that majority is preferred to taste, for we stoned Reshafim on the majority, etc., and taste is like the primary, as Chazal said in one place. Nevertheless it is difficult regarding the concluding blessing per the view that it is abbreviated form of three from Torah, as Tur wrote in Rosh Chodesh (Magen Avraham 172:3 — if so, abbreviated form of three from Torah, and how can he exempt with Borei Nefashot, which is rabbinic, of the beer for abbreviated form of three of wine, which is Torah-level five species of grain)? Again I saw from that of Rosh Chodesh seif 9, 148 of the five species with other flour, and there is not an olive-bulk as Acharonim — one does not bless in Grace After Meals, and it seems even if the taste of grain is noticeable and felt; and Magen Avraham there 16 and Tur note 4 see there — if so, all the more so here where in Grace After Meals it is certainly Torah-level five species of grain; and therefore with a small amount of wine in other beverages, even if there is approximately an olive-bulk as Acharonim — behold it is explained in Terumot 12 seif 10 that for drinking we require a revi'it from its beginning to its end according to one view in stam, and Magen Avraham beginning of 10 note 1 and responsa Peri Megadim vol. 2 siman 27; and it will be explained in Beit Yosef and simply one can say that regarding blessing we follow after the majority, and whenever there is a primary and with it something secondary one blesses on the primary and exempts the secondary; and siman 204 seif 12 and it will be explained there.

And still — this requires further study; Peri Megadim siman 66 and Magen Avraham 16 — even in water, whenever there are not six parts water one blesses borei peri haGefen, and all the more so in other beverages whenever there is taste of wine; and to Taz in any case it is equivalent to water — and why did he write here "we follow after the majority" if we do not distinguish between that which mixed by itself and one who intentionally gives wine to give taste in water? Whenever there is taste of wine and it is one of five in water one blesses borei peri haGefen (siman 204 in Tur and Beit Yosef and Shulchan Aruch and Acharonim — one can say among those whose primary making is thus, see there); and because the matters are lengthy and to me, the poor one, the ways of wisdom are hidden from me — I said to shorten here, and it will be explained in Peri Chadash and in Peri Megadim siman 66 and seif 12 and Rosh Chodesh 61b and seif 9 further, and here one should not expand.
