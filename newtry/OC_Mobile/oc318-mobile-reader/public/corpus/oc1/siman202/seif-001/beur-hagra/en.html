Seif 1, Whether, etc. Yerushalmi, final chapter of Pesachim and in Shekalim. This shows that cooked wine and spiced wine are regarded as full-fledged wine, and one fulfills the obligation of the four cups with them and may recite Kiddush over them. See Tosafos, Bava Basra 97a, s.v. ileima, and Pesachim 109b, s.v. arbaah. See what I wrote in siman 272, seif 1.
Meaning, etc. As stated in the Pesikta, Parashas Yisro, on the verse, “In the third month.”
And if it became mixed, etc. Mishnah 44a: “Whatever is primary,” etc. See siman 204, seif 12. This does not appear correct, for we rule that any wine that cannot withstand three parts water is not considered wine. Thus, although water constitutes the majority, one nevertheless recites Borei Peri HaGafen. We see that in this matter we do not follow the majority. Nor is there proof from the Mishnah, for the Mishnah does not discuss a mixture at all, but rather one who eats each item separately and eats the subordinate item only because of the primary one.
