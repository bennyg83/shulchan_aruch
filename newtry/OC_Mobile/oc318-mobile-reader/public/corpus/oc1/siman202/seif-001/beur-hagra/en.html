Seif 1 — whether [raw or cooked], etc. Yerushalmi final chapter of Pesachim and in Shekalim, and from this it proves that cooked [wine] and preserves are complete wine and one fulfills with it the four cups and sanctifies upon it; and see Tosafot Bava Batra 97a s.v. elima, etc., and Pesachim 109b s.v. arba'ah, etc., and see what is written in siman 472 seif 1:

That is, etc. As written in Pesikta parashat Yitro in the section "in the third month":

And if it became mixed, etc. Mishnah Eduyot 1:1 "every [food] of which the main [ingredient is determining]," etc., and the matter is clear (Rambam Berakhot 12). But it does not appear [correct], for we hold "all wine that does not float three [measures of] water on it is not wine" — and behold it is mostly water, yet we bless borei peri haGefen; therefore we do not follow the majority in this; and from the mishnah there is no proof, for the mishnah does not deal at all with mixture but with one who eats each primary [food] separately and does not eat the subsidiary except for the sake of the primary:
