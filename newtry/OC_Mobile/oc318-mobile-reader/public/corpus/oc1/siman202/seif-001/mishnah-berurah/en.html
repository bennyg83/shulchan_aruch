(1) Tree fruits — whether of the seven species or not of the seven species.

(2) Except for wine, etc. — because it satisfies the heart and gladdens it, they established for it a berakha by itself.

(3) Borei peri haGefen — and even what oozes by itself from grapes without treading [Peri Megadim]. And if he erred and blessed on wine borei peri haEtz — see below siman 208 seif 14 in Mishna Berurah.

(4) Whether cooked — for through cooking the wine does not change for the worse. And there is no distinction whether he cooked the wine itself or cooked grapes and raisins in order to make wine from them.

(5) That is, they put in, etc. — and even though its taste and smell changed thereby from what it was before — nevertheless the wine did not change for the worse, and the wine is primary relative to the other; therefore one blesses on it borei peri haGefen.

(6) Honey and peppers — and even one-third honey and one-third peppers and more than this — nevertheless the wine is primary, for they come only to improve it and give it taste.

(7) In beer — and beer of all kinds are equal in this, except for types of beverages that impair and ruin the taste of wine as soon as they are mixed in so much that the taste of wine is lost — one blesses shehakol.

(8) If the majority is wine, etc. — as we rule: whatever is primary and with it secondary — one blesses on the primary and exempts the secondary; and whatever kind is the majority is the primary and the minority is secondary to it.

(9) One blesses borei peri haGefen — and this is when the taste of wine was not nullified by the mixture.

(10) And if the majority is beer, etc. — and this is not comparable to what we rule below siman 204 seif 5 in the gloss that even if there is in wine a little more than one in six against water — see there: there the way of diluting wine is in water, and therefore one cannot say the wine is nullified against the water mixed in them, for it all becomes one kind; not so with beer, for the way is not to dilute in them; and it is like two kinds in general — whichever kind is the majority is primary. And see in Biur HaGra that his view for the halachah is that also in our matter it is equal to diluting wine in water.
