Borei peri haEtz. And if it is fruit that one should be concerned it became worm-infested, one should not bless until one opens the fruit and sees whether it is fit for eating — Sefer Chassidim. And Shelah wrote specifically regarding a walnut; but regarding other fruits, even if it became wormy inside one should not bless in vain on account of its outer shell — it is better to bless when it is whole. (And see in the book Eliyah Rabbah that he brings several matters on the path of piety and ethics regarding blessings.)

Cooked. Even though regarding yayin nesech it does not become nesech, regarding blessing there is no distinction.

In beer (sheichar). Specifically beer of figs; but other beverages — as soon as so much is mixed in that the taste of wine is lost — one blesses shehakol; see Magen Avraham.
