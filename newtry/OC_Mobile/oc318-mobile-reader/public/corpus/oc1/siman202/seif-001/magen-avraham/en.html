Cooked. And even though regarding yayin nesech it does not become nesech.

Regarding beer. Figs [thus in the text] — his view is that Rashbatz specifically mentioned beer of figs, for it does not eliminate the taste of wine; but other beverages, as soon as so much is mixed in that the taste of wine is lost, one blesses shehakol. And see there siman 204 notes 5 and 6.
