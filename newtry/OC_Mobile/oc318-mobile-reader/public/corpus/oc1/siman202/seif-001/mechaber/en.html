The laws of the blessing on tree fruits. And in it
18 seifim. On all tree fruits one blesses initially borei peri haEtz, except for wine upon which one blesses borei peri haGefen, whether raw or cooked, whether it is made as preserves — meaning that they put in it honey and peppers.And if wine was mixed into beer, we follow the majority: if the majority is wine one blesses borei peri haGefen, and if the majority is beer one blesses shehakol (Beit Yosef in the name of Tashbatz).
