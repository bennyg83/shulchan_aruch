1) [Seif 1] Over all tree fruits, one recites, etc. R' Chaim Vital, of blessed memory, writes in Shaar Ruach HaKodesh, folio 9b: My teacher, of blessed memory, told me that a person's principal attainment of Ruach HaKodesh depends upon his intent and care with every blessing over enjoyment. Through these blessings, the power of the husks attached to physical foods, which cling through them to the person who eats the food, is nullified. Through reciting the blessings over them with intent, he removes those husks from the foods, refines his own physical substance, and becomes pure and fit to receive holiness. He strongly warned us about this. Shaar HaMitzvos, parashas Eikev, writes the same; see there. See further below, paragraph 24.
