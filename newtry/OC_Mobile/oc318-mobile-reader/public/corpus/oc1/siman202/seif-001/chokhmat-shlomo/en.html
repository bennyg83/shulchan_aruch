Seif 1. Except wine, over which one recites Borei Peri HaGefen. Note: See my addenda to the laws of Yom Kippur, siman 612, where I proved that regarding the abbreviated threefold blessing after wine, one recites it only if he drank the requisite amount in one continuous act, without pausing in between, even if the pause was shorter than the time required to eat a peras or to drink a reviis. See there for the reasoning. This requires study as a matter of practical halachah; examine it carefully.
