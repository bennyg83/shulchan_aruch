(1) [Seif 1] On all tree fruits one blesses, etc. — Maharhav z"l wrote in Sha'ar Ruach HaKodesh daf 9b, and these are his words: my teacher z"l told me that the essence of a person's attainment of ruach hakodesh depends on the intention of man and caution in all blessings on pleasures, for through them the power of those shells that cling to material foods is nullified and they attach themselves in the person who eats them; and through the blessings upon them said with intention he removes from them those shells and purifies his substance and becomes refined and prepared to receive holiness — and He warned us greatly about this — end of his words; and so too in Shem HaMeforash parashat Ekev — see there.
