Note 14 — also with intent the essence of planting is only for the caperberries; but at the time of planting he is not particular also about eating the leaves, for the tree does not spoil thereby — therefore borei peri haAdamah; unlike the reader in Rama and Tur, who is particular about eating the reader, for it spoils the tree — one blesses shehakol, as explained in the Gemara and Rashi. And boser in Magen Avraham note 4 is comparable to caper leaves — borei peri haAdamah; but the tree is not the essence of the fruit.
