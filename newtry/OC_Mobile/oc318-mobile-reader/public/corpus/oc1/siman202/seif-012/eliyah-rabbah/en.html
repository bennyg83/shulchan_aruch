[12] One blesses shehakol, etc. And Bach wrote: one scrupulous in his deeds should bless on them l'chatchila Borei peri haAdamah, and if he blessed Borei peri haEtz or shehakol he fulfilled — end of his words. And his words are not clear to me; also in Nachalat Tzvi he contradicts his words. However what Nachalat Tzvi ruled to bless l'chatchila Borei peri haEtz — from what Beit Yosef wrote siman 204, and Taz too ruled it is not so, for I already wrote Beit Yosef's words do not appear [correct], and Rambam and Rashi do not disagree; also they did not see the abridged rulings of Rosh and Rabbeinu Yerucham that I wrote above that they ruled like Rashi; however on mashed dates one can rule thus, and not for their reasons but as I explained that Rashi too admits; but for pavedli the law is like Levush and Rama's ruling; and what Taz extended on this lightly can be rejected — and one need not extend:
