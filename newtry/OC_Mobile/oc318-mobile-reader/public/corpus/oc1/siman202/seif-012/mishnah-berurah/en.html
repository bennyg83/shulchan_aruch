(61) That are good raw and cooked — and even if they are better when cooked, since when raw they are also good and many people eat them so.

(62) Such as apples and pears — that they call in German that those fruits are also good to eat when raw. There are some kinds of apples that are sour and are not fit for most people to eat when raw — only through cooking; one blesses shehakol when raw, and see below siman 203 Mishna Berurah note 5 regarding wild apples.

(63) And if they are not customarily, etc. — meaning that most people eat it when cooked.

(64) When raw, etc. — the reason: since the way of eating those fruits for most people is only through cooking, it is not called a primary fruit as such that it did not come into this category; and see in Eliyah Rabbah siman 205 and Chayei Adam that their view is that even if also good to eat raw, since the way of most people is to eat them only through cooking, one blesses shehakol.

(65) When cooked, borei peri haEtz — and the same law if it were the reverse, that the way people eat those fruits is only when raw, such as nuts and the like — he does not bless borei peri haEtz except when raw, and when cooked shehakol. And the concluding blessing Borei Nefashot even if he happened upon them among the seven species in this manner.
