(17) Fermenting wine — one blesses upon it borei peri haGefen, for they said: a person squeezes a cluster of grapes and recites upon it Kiddush of the day, and one sanctifies only on wine. Beit Yosef in the name of Rashba. Levush note 17. And see siman 472 seif 2. And likewise wine that flowed on its own, even without treading — borei peri haGefen. Kitzur Shulchan Aruch seif 8; and so too Avnei Nezer note 1.
