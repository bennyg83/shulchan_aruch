(80) borei peri haEtz — for it is fruit and people also regularly eat it dry. And one who has diarrhea and puts muskat nut into beer so it will stop — if he is not thirsty and drinks only for medicine, the muskat nut is primary and the beer secondary and he blesses borei peri haEtz, like regarding oil above in seif 4; and if he desires to drink the beer even without medicine, even though he puts muskat nut into it for medicine — the beer is primary and he blesses shehakol and exempts the muskat nut; and likewise regarding oil above when he desires to drink mei anigran and beer, even though he puts oil into it for medicine — he blesses on the beer alone if they are the majority.

(81) borei peri haAdamah — for it grows on the ground like reeds and people also regularly eat it dry.
