Muskat nut. What is called muskat nut is a complete fruit, and people are accustomed to eat it dry; and Levush wrote to compare to seif 14. And one who has diarrhea and puts muskat nut into beer in order to stop — it is considered like olive oil as written in seif 4. However, it appears to me that when he drinks for his thirst — even though he also puts muskat nut into it for medicine — nevertheless he blesses on the beer, and the same applies regarding oil — Magen Avraham. (And in the book Eliyah Rabbah he ruled that even when drinking only for medicine one blesses on the beer and exempts it, for the minority is against the beer; and Levush compared to the oil above.)
