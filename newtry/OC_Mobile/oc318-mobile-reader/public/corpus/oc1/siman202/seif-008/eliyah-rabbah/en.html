[Levush] One blesses on the bread, etc. — even with much oil and little bread, and even when he is concerned for his throat — so Bach proved from the Talmud and poskim; and Olas Tamid challenged him from siman 212 that if the bread is mixed in the main ingredient he does not bless on the bread — see there. And one who examines Bach himself will see that he himself raised this difficulty in this siman and in siman 212, and even so he did not budge from this ruling, for on account of a difficulty one does not reject a ruling — a difficulty can be resolved; and so I saw that Nachalat Tzvi ruled and resolved the difficulty that salted fish can eat without bread and enjoy it, only in order that it not harm his throat he eats bread; but oil alone he cannot eat and one who eats does not enjoy — up to here. And in my humble opinion, to resolve: since there is no way to heal through eating oil and bread, his mind is voided relative to every person and the bread is primary; and in this manner we say in the Talmud and poskim in many places, and so it appears from Rabbeinu Yerucham. Again, the book Magen Avraham was printed and I saw in it other matters, and our mishnah did not move from its place — and so is primary.
