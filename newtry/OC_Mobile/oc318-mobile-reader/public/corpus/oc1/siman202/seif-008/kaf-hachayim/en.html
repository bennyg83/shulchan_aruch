(8) And if one said borei peri haEtz Baruch Atah Heaven Elokeinu Melech HaOlam, or one said borei peri haEtz Baruch Atah Heaven Elokeinu Melech HaOlam sheBarah — Beit Yosef in chiddushim 21 wrote that he fulfilled. And they brought Petach HaDvir note 2 and wrote about it that if one said borei peri haEtz Baruch Atah Heaven Elokeinu Melech HaOlam — it is obvious he did not fulfill, for it is not understood and one cannot connect the blessing from beginning to end, and such a broken language — aside from not fulfilling the eighteen [blessings] — is close to saying the Name in vain; but when one said borei peri haEtz Baruch Atah Heaven Elokeinu Melech HaOlam sheBarah — which is understood from the blessing that one blesses the Holy One Blessed Be He — it appears primary that he fulfilled — see there. And they brought Sha'arei De'ah there; Orach Chaim there.
