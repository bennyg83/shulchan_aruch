Even though — Taz Berachot 38a and b regarding date honey and water of boiled vegetables — the view of Chiddushei HaRan that squeezing and pounding are like cooking, and he distinguishes between the way of boiling or squeezing — water like them, and there is no way to boil and squeeze there is not like them; and in my Chiddushim there I challenged: are vegetables not the way to boil them like hanging a basket on her, since it becomes sweat — even the way of cooking; and one must say that it is not in the way of their benefit and they were not planted with intent for this, and it is not literally sweat; and the language of Levush in siman 1 that it is called sweat — not so grapes and olives that are made for beverages; and likewise vegetables whose way is to boil them — also the beverage through boiling is like them; and likewise in the way to squeeze, etc. And what was difficult for this from the reason of the primary — one can say it is not in the way of their benefit as stated. And in Rambam chapter 8 of Blessings halachah 2 he wrote: one who squeezes fruits blesses shehakol; and in Beit HaGedolah — vegetables whose way is to boil bless borei peri haAdamah; date honey shehakol — those show that he holds like Chiddushei HaRan that he distinguishes whether their way is thus or not; and what he conditioned that one boiled them to drink their water — nevertheless there, and Magen Avraham Rosh Hashanah note 6, and it will be explained there; and he wrote date honey here to exclude from the view of Beit HaGedolah, and Beit HaGedolah did not include saying that even boiled dates one does not bless borei peri haEtz — to exclude from Magen Avraham, z"l, Ish Ribo brought in Chiddushei HaRan Berachot 38 that he holds in such a case borei peri haEtz — see there. And in Knesset HaGedolah he brought the words of Rosh against Rambam; and it is not clear that Rambam is like Chiddushei HaRan as stated — but Rosh holds squeezing and pounding are not like cooking and they are like sweat, not so cooking is not sweat; and likewise one does not boil borei peri haEtz, and through squeezing — even though their way is to squeeze — shehakol; in a manner that Rashba and Rosh disagree in two matters. And for Rosh — taste of Torah in olives and grapes, or in any fruit through cooking — behold squeezing and pounding and brine through salting is not literally taste in other fruits; and we already have lengthy discussion in the sugya of Chullin 120 and Pesachim 24; and it will be explained in chapter 5 of Pesach in the opening 462 regarding taste. And Bach wrote in Shulchan Aruch he did not decide; and per the general rule — view 1 in stam like Chiddushei HaRan — and all the more so it implies Rambam holds thus. And the Mechaber in siman 8 — date honey and likewise other fruits — seemingly this and all the more so that; rather to say in other fruits through cooking; and with this will be the intent of Ateret Zekenim that brought note 14 — in siman 1 stam, and in siman 10 he brought the dispute — understand.
