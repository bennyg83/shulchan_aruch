In Berachos 38a-b, regarding date honey and the liquid of cooked vegetables, Chiddushei HaRashba holds that squeezing and crushing are equivalent to cooking, and distinguishes between items ordinarily cooked or squeezed, whose liquid retains their blessing, and items not ordinarily prepared that way, whose liquid does not. I asked in my novellae: are vegetables truly ordinarily cooked? Rather, because otherwise the liquid is merely “sweat,” this must mean that cooking is a normal mode of benefit and the vegetables are planted with that use in mind. Levush similarly writes that it is called sweat, unlike grapes and olives, which are intended for liquid; the same applies to vegetables commonly cooked, whose cooking liquid is like the vegetables themselves, and to fruits commonly squeezed. Rambam writes that squeezed fruit juice receives Shehakol, but vegetables ordinarily cooked receive Borei Peri HaAdamah, and date honey receives Shehakol. It is evident that he follows Rashba's distinction between the normal manner and an unusual manner. Kesef Mishneh cites the Rosh's words on Rambam, but this is difficult, for Rambam appears to agree with Rashba, whereas the Rosh holds that squeezing and crushing are inferior to cooking. According to the Rosh, taste as substance is D'Oraisa in olives and grapes, or in all fruits by cooking, but squeezing, crushing, and brine produced through salting are not regarded as actual taste in other fruits. I have discussed this at length in Chullin 120 and Pesachim 24, and it will be clarified, with Hashem's help, in the introduction to Hilchos Pesach, siman 442. Bach writes that Shulchan Aruch did not decide the issue. According to the general rule, however, the first opinion cited without qualification follows Rashba, and all the more so because Rambam appears to agree. The Mechaber's wording in seif 8, “date honey and likewise other fruits,” is seemingly an unnecessary progression, unless it means other fruits produced through cooking. This may be the intent of Olas Tamid cited by Eliyah Rabbah, that seif 1 is unqualified while seif 10 records the dispute. Understand this well.
