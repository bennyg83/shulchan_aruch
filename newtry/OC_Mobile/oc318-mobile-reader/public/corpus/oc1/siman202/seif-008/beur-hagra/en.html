Seif 8, The liquid that flows out. This is unlike Behag. See Rosh there: “It is possible that the Gaon’s reason,” etc. Tosafos, however, writes plainly unlike Behag, and this is also the view of Rambam. The Tur therefore writes, “the liquid that flows out.” Beit Yosef.
And likewise, etc. There: “According to whom,” etc. This is simple, except, etc., as stated in the Mishnah there. See Tosafos there, s.v. hai.
