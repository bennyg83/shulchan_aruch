Seif 8 — on liquids that come out from squeezing fruits one blesses the fruit blessing; Peri Chadash in his book Mayim Chayim Chafetz Chaim siman 7; and see below siman 210 in the margin.
Taz note 14 — if so one should bless shehakol — this is astounding, for we do not find anyone who holds thus to bless shehakol, only that one should not bless borei peri haEtz but borei peri haAdamah; and see responsa Admat Kodesh siman 2.
