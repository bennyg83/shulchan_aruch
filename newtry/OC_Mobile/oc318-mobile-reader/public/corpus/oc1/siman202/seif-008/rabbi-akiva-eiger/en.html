Seif 8. Over liquids that emerge from squeezed fruit, one recites the blessing of the fruit. Peri Chadash, in his work Mayim Chayim, Orach Chayim, siman 7. See below, siman 210, in my gloss.
Taz, seif katan 14. “Therefore he should recite Shehakol.” This is astonishing, for we have not found anyone who holds that one should recite Shehakol. The ruling is only that one should not recite Borei Peri HaEtz, but rather Borei Peri HaAdamah. See Responsa Admas Kodesh, siman 2.
