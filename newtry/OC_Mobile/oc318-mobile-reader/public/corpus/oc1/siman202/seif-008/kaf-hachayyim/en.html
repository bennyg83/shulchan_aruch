8) If he said, 'Borei Pri HaEtz, Blessed are You, Hashem, our God, King of the universe,' or said, 'Pri HaEtz, Blessed are You, Hashem, our God, King of the universe, Who created it,' Beis Yaakov writes in handwritten novellae that he fulfilled his obligation. Petach HaDevir, paragraph 2, cites this and comments that if he said, 'Borei Pri HaEtz, Blessed are You, Hashem, our God, King of the universe,' it is obvious that he did not fulfill his obligation, because the words are unintelligible and the blessing's beginning cannot be connected to its end. Besides failing to fulfill his obligation with such a broken phrase, he nearly pronounces the Name of Heaven in vain. If, however, he said, 'Pri HaEtz, Blessed are You, Hashem, our God, King of the universe, Who created it,' the blessing makes clear that he blesses the Holy One, blessed is He, and the primary conclusion is that he fulfilled his obligation; see there. Sedei Chemed there and Orach Chaim there cite this.
