(45) Date syrup (zav from dates) — and the same law applies to what comes out by pounding and squeezing.
(46) On it shehakol — for it is like that which is written in the Torah "olive oil and honey" — meaning dates from which syrup comes out; but honey itself is merely perspiration and is not included in the category of fruit.
(47) On liquids that come out, etc. — such as apple wine and the like, whether it happened that they came out by themselves or whether they came out by pounding and squeezing — their blessing is shehakol, for it is merely perspiration; a beverage is called such only when it comes from olives and grapes alone.
