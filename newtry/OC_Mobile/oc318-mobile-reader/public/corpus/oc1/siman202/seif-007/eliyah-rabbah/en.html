[Levush] Since they are not, etc. — in Beit Yosef this reason is not mentioned; I also require study, for the main law that one blesses upon it shehakol — Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba. And in my humble opinion there is no proof from him, for it is possible that he follows his own reasoning that even on sweetened pits one blesses shehakol; but for the Rosh and his supporters who bless on the sweetened borei peri haEtz — it is possible the same law applies to bitter ones that were sweetened by fire; and per this we do not need the following the distinction that Levush divides in seif 5.
