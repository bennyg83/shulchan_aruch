If instead of 'Who creates the fruit' he said, 'Who forms the fruit,' or instead of, 'fruit of the ground,' he said, 'fruit of the earth,' although he was not permitted to alter the text, he fulfilled his obligation. The rule that one who changes the formulation established by the Sages for blessings does not fulfill his obligation does not apply here. Petach HaDevir; see there. Sedei Chemed, Asifas Dinim, section Berachos, siman 1, paragraph 35, cites this; Orach Chaim, paragraph 2.
