Dates that one mashes by hand, etc.: see Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote that for practical halacha we hold, etc., that even though in the Gemara they inferred from Rashi that trima is specifically when they are somewhat crushed: already Beit Yosef answered him at end of siman 204, see there, and so ruled Magen Avraham, Avodat HaRokeach, the Gra, and Chayei Adam, that even if they were completely crushed, if the essence of the fruit is somewhat recognizable their blessing did not change; and in truth apart from Rokeach's view brought in Magen Avraham, who stands in Rambam's method, I found more Rishonim who stand in her method: he is Aruch under trima, see there [and it appears Rambam copied his law from there], and the Or Zaru'a too transmitted for practical law; and so is Riaz's view in his halachic rulings; and since from all these it is proved explicitly that even if completely crushed their blessing did not change, we certainly rule thus for practical law, and especially since Avodat HaRokeach sides that Rashi too admits in law to Rambam. And what Mishnah Berurah wrote "only in pavedli," etc.: although Taz's view for practical law is that even initially one can bless Borei Peri HaEtz: Levush, Magen Avraham, Avodat HaRokeach, the Gra, and Chayei Adam all held for practical law like Rama's ruling that if completely dissolved and its form is not recognizable at all one blesses Shehakol [and Taz's proofs were already rejected by Acharonim: see Yad Aharon and Nahar Shalom]; and I found in Rosh HaYeshiva's novellae on the Rif that he too wrote that if crushed so much that their form passed one blesses Shehakol [and so is proved from Avudraham brought in Magen Avraham in siman 207 note 9, see there]: and although it is reasonable in simplicity that Rosh HaYeshiva holds so fundamentally, nevertheless one should not move from Rama's words, who decided that it appears to him fundamentally that if one blessed Borei Peri HaEtz he fulfilled:
