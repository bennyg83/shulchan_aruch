Dates that one mashed by hand and made from them a dough and removed their pits — nevertheless their blessing did not change, and one blesses borei peri haEtz on them and at the end the abbreviated form of three.And according to this, the same applies to plums called pavladli — one blesses borei peri haEtz on them; and some say to bless shehakol on them (Taz siman 89, Beit Yosef in the name of Tur). It is good to be concerned l'chatchila to bless shehakol; but if he blessed borei peri haEtz he has fulfilled, for thus appears the essence.
