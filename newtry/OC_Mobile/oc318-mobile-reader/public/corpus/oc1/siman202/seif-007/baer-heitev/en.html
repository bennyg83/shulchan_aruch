And this is specifically regarding berries such as pavedli that were cooked and completely dissolved; but regarding dates that were mashed, Terumat HaDeshen does not argue, and he holds like Rambam that one blesses borei peri haEtz — see Magen Avraham and Pri Megadim.

He fulfilled. Taz extended and raised that one who blesses l'chatchila borei peri haEtz on pavedli of plums or cherries has no concern and acts properly, since a blessing of borei peri haEtz has advantage over shehakol; and every day we practice when cooking apples until they are completely crushed that we bless upon them borei peri haEtz, as written in seif 12 "whether cooked," etc.; and certainly they are crushed by cooking, and there is no concern here for a blessing in vain — end of his words. And Bach ruled that one who is scrupulous in his deeds should bless on pavedli and the like that are completely crushed borei peri haAdamah; and if he blessed borei peri haEtz or shehakol he fulfilled; see Yad Ephraim what he challenged on Taz. (And see in the book Eliyah Rabbah that he challenged Taz regarding pavedli and ruled like the view of Rama.)
