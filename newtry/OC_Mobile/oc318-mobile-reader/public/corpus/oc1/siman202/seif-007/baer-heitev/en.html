Taz discusses this at length and concludes that one who initially recites Borei Peri HaEtz over plum or cherry powidl has no reason for concern and acts properly, since Borei Peri HaEtz has an advantage over Shehakol. Every day we likewise recite Borei Peri HaEtz over apples cooked until completely mashed, as stated in seif 12, “whether cooked,” and so forth. They are certainly mashed by the cooking, and there is no concern of a blessing in vain. Bach rules that one who is meticulous should recite Borei Peri HaAdamah over powidl and similar foods that have been completely mashed; if he recited Borei Peri HaEtz or Shehakol, he fulfilled his obligation. See there, and see Yad Aharon concerning his objection to Taz. See Eliyah Rabbah, who disputes Taz regarding powidl and rules in accordance with Rama.
