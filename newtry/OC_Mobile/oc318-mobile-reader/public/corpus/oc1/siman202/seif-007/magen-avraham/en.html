And some say to bless etc. It appears to me specifically regarding berries that were cooked and dissolved entirely, and so wrote Mahari"k shehakol; but regarding dates that were somewhat mashed he does not disagree and he holds like Rambam and Tur that one blesses borei peri haEtz, and likewise siman 204 note 11 that wrote even if they are very pressed — and Rama did not disagree; and also in Darkei Moshe siman 204 it implies that he holds like Rav Eliyahu; and also in Tur Beit Yosef possibly he concedes regarding dates that are somewhat mashed like Rambam; and also regarding berries that were cooked he wrote, and these are his words: that when they are dissolved entirely one blesses shehakol; and some say regarding spices that are ground in siman 203 seif 7, that there it is customary to crush and grind all spices — therefore they are considered as remaining in their substance, unlike another thing that is not customarily always mashed and crushed; and even though these distinctions are not clear, nevertheless it should only be a doubt and one blesses shehakol — end of his words; and in Rokeach he wrote, and these are his words: dates that are pressed, cooked — borei peri haEtz, and it is taryak etc. — end of his words.
