Even though the taste of the fruits entered, etc. This is the view of Rashba in Beit Yosef, and he wrote there the reason: since their way is to eat them in their eyes (as they are) and not to boil them or squeeze them; but something whose way is to boil it or squeeze its liquid — its liquid is like it; and if so, dried plums one should bless borei peri haEtz on the broth, for their way is through boiling:

And Rosh wrote that it is possible, etc. These are his words: Rosh — even though there is in it only the broth and the taste of the vegetables, one blesses on them as one blesses on the vegetables themselves; and some disagree regarding fruit water, for we say it is merely sweat, because a beverage does not have the taste of the fruit; and it is possible that if one cooked the fruit and the taste of the fruit entered the water, one blesses on it borei peri haEtz — end of his words. And Beit Yosef concluded that the water of boiled vegetables is like boiled vegetables, whether one cooked them in water or soaked them in water and did not cook them — if they gave taste in the water one blesses on them borei peri haEtz; and since he used the language "it is possible," it implies at first glance that there is doubt in the matter; and if so we should have to go out from the doubt and bless shehakol; and it is difficult, for Tur wrote in siman 204 these things as a clear ruling that on water in which vegetables were cooked one blesses as on the vegetables themselves, etc. — therefore it appears according to Tur that here too there is no doubt; and even though Rosh wrote the language "it is possible," one can say he did not intend doubt; and I have to settle why he wrote this, that he follows the method of Tosafot who wrote, and these are their words: water of boiled [vegetables] is like boiled [vegetables], and one blesses on it borei peri haAdamah even though there is in it only water and the taste of vegetables — one blesses on them as one would bless on the vegetables, even though we said above that fruit water is merely sweat — perhaps there is a distinction — end of his words; and they did not explain what the distinction is in this; Rosh wrote: perhaps this is the distinction; but in the essence of the law there is no doubt here, for certainly water of boiled [vegetables] is like boiled [vegetables], meaning fruit water like the fruits themselves; and for practical halachah one should bless shehakol on those whose way is not to cook them or crush them; but regarding something whose way is to cook them — borei peri haEtz; and one can answer on what is written in this siman that we do not follow after taste but after the majority — for here it is merely water and there is no significance against the taste of fruits, as stated — likewise; and see what is written siman 204 seif 2:
