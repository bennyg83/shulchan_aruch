On those waters, etc. — it implies that on the fruits themselves one blesses upon them the blessing appropriate to them; and so is proved in Magen Avraham in the name of Rashba; and therefore it deals with fruits that are good to eat whether raw or cooked — for otherwise we bless on them only shehakol, as explained below in seif 12, see there in Mishna Berurah; nevertheless regarding the sauce we do not say their water should be like them unless the majority of eating those fruits is through cooking — thus Shulchan Aruch's words must be explained; and clear proof for the matter: behold apples are in seif 12 that they are good to eat whether raw or cooked, yet Rashba wrote explicitly regarding their water that one blesses shehakol because their way is to eat them raw and not to boil — see there; and necessarily because regarding their water we require that most of them stand for that purpose specifically [and perhaps per Rashba even when their way is to eat cooked like raw equally he also admits to Rosh that it helps to bless borei peri haEtz, and they disagree only when they are good to eat cooked but their way is to eat only raw — unlike here; and see there in Rashba's novellae on the matter of water of boiled fruits in the entire sugya there, which implies somewhat like our words] — and know further that also Rosh, who holds that one always blesses on their water borei peri haEtz, means that we do not require that the majority of their eating be through cooking, but nevertheless we require that they be good to eat whether raw and whether cooked — for if they are good to eat only raw, even on the fruits themselves one blesses only shehakol when one eats them cooked, as below in seif 12 in Mishna Berurah. And therefore it is simple that on water of soaked apples [called in Yiddish kvas] one should not bless according to all opinions except shehakol — for the apples themselves are not customarily eaten when they are soaked, for they spoil thereby, and one blesses on them shehakol, and their water is no better than them; and apart from this reason there is another reason for its blessing shehakol — see in Magen Avraham in siman 207 seif 2, and so ruled Chayei Adam:
