There is room to question what is written regarding raisins that their way is thus, and Rashba agrees as in seif 11 and there is no doubt; and according to his view in note 8 regarding dried plums and so too Bach; however according to what Magen Avraham wrote in note 22 — specifically when the majority of their eating is for this they were planted, and dried plums — not so raisins, for they were not planted with intent for this but to tread; and so too in Levush siman 11, and likewise Ateret Zekenim note 15 for k'meitzemukin. And what is written regarding raisin wine — that Magen Avraham note 27 answered this and explained in one explanation, and it will be explained there. And Sheriyat Pirim wrote that there is not less than al ha'eitz — Ateret Zekenim note 14 that also in prohibitions we hold specifically al ha'eitz like cooked; therefore fruits whose way is thus and al ha'eitz — there is no doubt that borei peri haEtz. However regarding pickled — if pickled in vinegar in the measure that one puts on fire and it boils, even though their way is thus one can say one blesses shehakol, lest vinegar is not pickled in this measure — Eshel Avraham Yoreh De'ah 258:2 and Magen Avraham 29 in Rosh Chodesh 5 that he holds we follow after the majority, as written in note 9; but pickled in salted brine a great deal in the measure on fire — their way is thus borei peri haEtz and there is no doubt; and where their way is not thus — that is the dispute of Rashba and Rosh that pickled is like cooked, etc., as I wrote. And what is written regarding cucumber brine — possibly this is what Rambam z"l wrote in chapter 8 of Mishnah Berurah halachah 4, and that is that one cooks them to extract their water; and Magen Avraham in Rosh Chodesh note 6.

And behold a beverage in borscht that they make in the country of Russia, and also in our place from sivin and flour — shehakol, for wheat flour is shehakol in Rosh Chodesh 65. And those that they make marti ribin or called barkash — one who drinks it thus cooked is fit to bless borei peri haAdamah like vegetable water in Rosh Chodesh whose way is thus, etc., and pickled is like cooked — one can rely on what Magen Avraham wrote in Rosh Chodesh note 6 — such a case one should bless shehakol, and likewise dried plums if one separates the beverage one can say according to all shehakol; and what is written there in Magen Avraham note 22 from this.
