Shehakol. And Levush wrote regarding water that rises in siman 205 seif 2 that those waters — since most of their eating is by boiling — the water of their boiling is considered like them; not so regarding fruits that are not customarily boiled or squeezed but eaten in their raw state — regarding those we do not say their water should be like them — Beit Yosef, Rashba. However, regarding dry fruits such as apples and pears, berries, and plums — meaning that one blesses borei peri haEtz, for they are not customarily eaten except by boiling them and not in their raw state — and such is my practice, and such is the essence — Bach, Taz. And likewise ruled Pri Megadim that fruits whose majority of eating is in cooked form — certainly their water is like them: if they are borei peri haAdamah one blesses borei peri haAdamah, and if they are borei peri haEtz one blesses borei peri haEtz. And Magen Avraham is doubtful about this. And on coffee and tea one blesses shehakol — Perach Mateh Aharon, Chayei Adam siman 40; and likewise in responsa Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 5 that one blesses shehakol on coffee and tea; but in Hilkhot Ketanot siman 9 and in responsa Pnei Meiros siman 95 they wrote that by law one should bless borei peri haAdamah, only that the custom is to bless shehakol; see in the book Bnei Chiya and after him in Nahar; see siman 204 seif 7 as written.
