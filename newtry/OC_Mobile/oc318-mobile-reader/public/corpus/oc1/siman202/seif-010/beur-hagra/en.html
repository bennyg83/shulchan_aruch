Seif 10 — fruits, etc. And the Rosh, etc. As the first tanna said 39a "water of all of them," etc., and Rambam wrote in seif 8 on that which is there — there is no taste of the fruit in the beverage and it is merely perspiration; not so when they are cooked — and thus he wrote "and water," etc.; but Rashba wrote that there their way is not to boil them or squeeze them, unlike vegetables whose majority of eating is through boiling — Magen Avraham:
