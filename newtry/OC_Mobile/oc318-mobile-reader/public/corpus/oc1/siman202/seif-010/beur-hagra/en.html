Seif 10, Fruits, etc. “And the Rosh,” etc. This follows the statement on 39a: “The water of all of them,” etc. This is not comparable to what is written in seif 8 regarding, etc., for there the taste of the fruit is not present in the liquid and it is merely moisture, whereas when they are cooked the taste of the fruit is present. This is the meaning of, “and the water,” etc. Rashba, however, writes that there it is not customary to boil or squeeze them, unlike vegetables, most of which are eaten after boiling. See Magen Avraham.
Water in which they were soaked, etc. See Tosafos, 40b, s.v. verabbi.
