In Magen Avraham note 35 — it is close that they bless shehakol, etc. And in chapter 8 of the laws of blessings he brought in the name of Rema'h, who challenged from orlah; and what Magen Avraham wrote in chapter 8 per the words of Rashba that Magen Avraham brought — however one who examines Rashba's responsa siman 400 will see that it is not so, but rather to answer that it was fitting to bless borei peri haEtz since they grow on a tree; and he concluded there: nevertheless, say, since Scripture obligated them in orlah, etc. And in my Ohel Avraham Efrayim kontras I expanded and wrote to settle also what Rambam omitted — this law that peppers are obligated in orlah — and I brought the words of Tosafot in Yoma page 81. I also wrote per the words of Ritva that peppers are formed on the tree itself like resin blisters, and we hold that cotton of a shearer is not like a shearer; and see chapter 7 of the laws of orlah and chapter 7 of the laws of shemittah — I expanded on this and on Ritva in Eruvin page 20. And there is one who wrote that when wet one blesses borei peri haAdamah, for it is not considered tree fruit, but rather the Torah considered them to obligate from what is written "every tree of food" — see there.
