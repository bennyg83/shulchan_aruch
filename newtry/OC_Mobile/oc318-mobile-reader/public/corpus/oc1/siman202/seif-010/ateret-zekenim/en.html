Fruits that were squeezed — such as apples, pears, peaches, berries, and dried plums, and the like — even the Rashba agrees that one blesses on their water borei peri haEtz, because these dry fruits are not customarily eaten in their raw state but rather are boiled; therefore, according to all opinions one blesses on their water borei peri haEtz (my teacher in Bach), for such was the custom, and such is the essence (and see Magen Avraham note 2).
