And likewise those. It should read "and those," etc., as explicitly in Beit Yosef: the reason they were not planted with intent to eat them when soft; but a complete fruit such as peel of m'arnatzan one blesses borei peri haEtz, unlike Yerushalmi — see seif 6; but muskat nut is complete fruit as written seif 17 — see there and siman 203 seif 6.
