(69) A soft nut, etc. — that its growth is not finished and its shell is still not hard; they take it for preserves together with the upper shell and roast them so in honey.

(70) Nuss muscade — this is not the muscat nut mentioned in seif 17, for there it is complete fruit.

(71) One blesses on it shehakol — and even though through the honey the fruit was improved and is good for eating — nevertheless, since it is still soft and is eaten together with its shell, he does not bless on it except shehakol, for they did not plant them with intent to eat them when soft in their shells but to eat the fruit itself when they ripen; and as we hold below siman 204 seif 1 regarding soft almonds their blessing is also shehakol for this reason; and all the more so here, where without roasting it is not at all fit while still soft for eating — it is bitter and all its fixing is through honey. And behold, all this is specifically because the fruit is not finished and they were not planted with this intent; not so regarding complete fruit when it is not fit to eat raw except through roasting — certainly one blesses upon it its appropriate blessing [latter authorities].

(72) And likewise these, etc. — it should read "and these" etc., for this is also the Mechaber's intent.
