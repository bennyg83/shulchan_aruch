On pepper — Taz. Magen Avraham note 34. And what is written shehakol borei peri haAdamah should read and it was fit to bless shehakol, but since the minority were planted with intent to eat them wet, borei peri haAdamah — Abaye in the name of Rashba. What the Mechaber wrote in seif 16 is when one eats them in their eyes (alone) and nothing else; however with sugar and the like one blesses on ginger borei peri haAdamah, and on pepper in mixtures likewise — and Taz Rosh Chodesh note 2, and so wrote Levush, and it is simple.

Nougat that they make from sesame seeds with honey borei peri haAdamah, for sesame is primary; and behold like a whole walnut in honey siman 13. And papier-fish between from flour or crumbled bread — nevertheless flour is primary and for sticky in general honey is primary — siman 204 seif 12 and Rosh Chodesh 173d see there on this. However regarding what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 165 from this 28 as crumbled bread — if there is an olive-bulk by honey in those papier-fish — this requires further study according to him whether one should bless HaMotzi for it does not depart from the category of bread; and see what is written in Pri Chadash 49 and Taz in 168 note 6 — whenever there is doubt whether Mezonot or HaMotzi one blesses Mezonot and abbreviated form of three.

Those — when they compound nougat with sesame mixed in, requires further study; and possibly we follow the majority; and the rule is: except for the five species of grain we follow the majority; and what adheres to flour — all adhesion is nullified as siman 208 seif 3. See in seif 13 and 14 from this — see there. If in doubt borei peri haEtz or borei peri haAdamah — bless borei peri haAdamah; and if in doubt shehakol — bless shehakol as explained here and Magen Avraham letter 39.
