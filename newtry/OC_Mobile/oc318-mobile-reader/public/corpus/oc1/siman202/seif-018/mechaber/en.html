On pepper and ginger when they are moist — borei peri haAdamah.All fruits where one knows they are the main fruit — one blesses borei peri haEtz; and those that are not the main fruit — borei peri haAdamah; and if one is in doubt whether it is the main fruit or not — borei peri haAdamah; and if one does not know what it is — one blesses shehakol (Tur).]:
