Moist — borei peri haAdamah. And even though pepper is a type of tree, since they plant only intending they will dry and most eat them ground as spices and are not eaten alone except sometimes it is close to bless shehakol like Kor Mevasser at the beginning of siman 204 (and so Shabbat); except that since some are eaten in their moist state with intent for this they also plant somewhat — therefore one blesses on them borei peri haAdamah. And ginger is not the main fruit [Tur] — see in Bach from this.

And if he is in doubt. That is, after he learned; but one who did not learn should not eat until he goes to a sage to teach him berakhot (Maharam Chalavah in Gemara and Bertinoro and Beit Yosef seif 61 siman 204 in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot).
