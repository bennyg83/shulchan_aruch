(82) Moist, etc. — for ginger is not fruit but roots from the tree under the ground; and pepper, even though it grows on a tree, since they plant only intending they will dry and most eat them ground in spice mixtures and are not eaten alone except sometimes it is close to bless on them shehakol as Kor Mevasser at the beginning of siman 204; except that since some are eaten in their moist state with intent for this they also plant somewhat — therefore one blesses on them borei peri haAdamah.
(83) And that are not the main fruit, etc. — as above in seif 6; and see below at the beginning of siman 206 in Mishna Berurah.
(84) And if he is in doubt — that is, after he learned and cannot clarify; but one who did not learn should not eat until he goes to a sage to teach him berakhot [Gemara].
(85) borei peri haAdamah — for even if it is borei peri haEtz, b'dieved he fulfilled with the berakha of borei peri haAdamah, as below siman 206.
(86) What is it — meaning, perhaps it is a kind whose berakha is shehakol.
