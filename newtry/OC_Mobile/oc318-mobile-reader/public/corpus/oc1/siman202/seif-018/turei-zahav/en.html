On pepper. In Tur it is written borei peri haEtz, but Beit Yosef wrote in the name of other poskim that since people do not plant them with intent to eat them when they are wet but rather that they dry — therefore one blesses shehakol; and from this reason it is puzzling why one blesses on walnut soaked in honey borei peri haEtz, for we see that it is not good for eating without mixtures and certainly people did not plant it with intent to eat it by itself — this requires further study.
