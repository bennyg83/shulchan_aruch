And Rosh — Taz. Rosh is not in doubt, for Tur wrote in Rosh Hashanah, and "not so" regarding fruit water — through cooking more, etc. — behold Rosh distinguished, and the halachah is like the view of Taz, z"l: ends that are not the way to boil them and to pound them (as if he said to squeeze them) — shehakol like Chiddushei HaRan; and their way is to boil — borei peri haEtz; behold their way is to squeeze — he did not decide, for where it is not the way he decided shehakol, for it is not in the way of their placement and they were not planted with intent for this; but in the way to squeeze one can say it is sweat as stated. And Taz, z"l, challenged: why do we say water of boiled fruits — when boiled — in the way to cook them — all agree; and why here in seif 1 in the gloss wine mixed with beer — we follow the majority and not after taste, as Taz 1 wrote that there are two blessings — so too here, a majority of water and only the taste of water — one blesses shehakol; and he answered that water is not significant and we follow after taste; and when we follow the majority we consider beverage with beverage. And with this one resolves that of Rosh Chodesh 65 in the gloss, and Magen Avraham Taz — less than six parts water we do not follow the majority in water; behold in other beverages that are significant we follow the majority. But for what is written above in Taz 1 — one can say whether spontaneously or intentionally, and his intent is for this — there is no need to distinguish between water and other beverages; and it will be explained in the opening of 462. And Taz, z"l, in Rosh Chodesh 65 note 5 derived from this the law of vegetables that one cooked in vinegar or other beverage — one blesses on the beverage shehakol, from the reason that it is significant and we do not follow after taste except after majority, even with meat.
