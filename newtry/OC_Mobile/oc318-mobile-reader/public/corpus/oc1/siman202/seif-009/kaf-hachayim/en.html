(9) And if on fruit of the tree or fruit of the ground one blessed Borei minei mezonot — even though all things are called food, for "food" there includes everything — nevertheless for the matter of blessing it does not help except for dates and wine, for if one blessed upon them Borei minei mezonot he fulfilled. So too Beit Yosef in chiddushim, and Beit Din in Petach HaDvir note 12; Sha'arei De'ah there note 36; Orach Chaim there. But Rav Chayei Adam general 58 note 3 wrote that on everything if one blessed Borei minei mezonot he fulfilled except for water and salt — see there. And so Ben Ish Chai parashat Balak note 13. And see above siman 167 note 75. And this will be explained further from this below siman 206, with Heaven's help.
