Ends of grapes. And some say regarding boser in seif 2 that one blesses borei peri haAdamah — for boser is different, for it stands to make from it a beverage, for it changes for the better, namely wine that one blesses borei peri haGefen; therefore it is enough that we lower it one level and bless borei peri haAdamah; unlike ends of grapes that stand to make vinegar — therefore one blesses shehakol.

That the heat dried them. It implies from Rashi's words that the reason is because it changed for the worse — see Responsa HaRif.
