Inferior grapes. This differs from unripe grapes in seif 2, over which one recites Borei Peri HaAdamah. Unripe grapes are destined to be made into a beverage that improves through transformation, namely wine, over which one recites Borei Peri HaGefen. It is therefore sufficient to lower their blessing by one level and recite Borei Peri HaAdamah. Inferior grapes, however, are destined to be made into vinegar, and one therefore recites Shehakol.
