Grapes. This is not comparable to unripe grapes in seif 2, over which one recites Borei Peri HaAdamah. Unripe grapes are destined to be made into a beverage and thereby improve into wine, over which one recites Borei Peri HaGefen. It is therefore sufficient to lower their blessing by one level and recite Borei Peri HaAdamah. The terminal grapes discussed here, however, are destined to become vinegar; one therefore recites Shehakol.
