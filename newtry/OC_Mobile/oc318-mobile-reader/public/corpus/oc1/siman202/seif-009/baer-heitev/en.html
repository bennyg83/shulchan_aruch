Grapes. And Levush wrote regarding boser in seif 2 that one blesses upon it borei peri haAdamah — for boser is different, since it stands to be made into a beverage, and it changed for the better, namely wine upon which one blesses borei peri haGefen; therefore it is enough that they lowered it one level and bless borei peri haAdamah; not so regarding grape ends that stand to become vinegar — therefore one blesses shehakol.
