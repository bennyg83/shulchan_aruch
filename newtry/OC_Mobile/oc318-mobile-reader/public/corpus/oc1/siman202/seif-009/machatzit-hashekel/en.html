(note 9) Oil, etc. — however, from what Rosh wrote in siman 204, so it should be — meaning his words were brought in Beit Yosef siman 204.

Regarding that which we learned, daf 40b: on vinegar one says shehakol — Rosh in siman 123, and thus: and you will wonder, how do we bless on vinegar, and we said in Yoma that one who drinks vinegar in the Temple is exempt, and above regarding dry pepper we assign a berakha for the Temple? One answer: it speaks of one concerned for his teeth who puts much vinegar into soup and drinks it for medicine — vinegar is primary and along with the soup he has benefit and requires a berakha, as we say regarding olive oil that one put into anigran — end of his words. Behold he wrote "and puts much vinegar," etc., and Rosh compared it to oil by anigran; this implies that a small amount of oil or vinegar is nullified relative to the majority. And possibly it stated the ordinary manner, etc., that a small amount of vinegar possibly does not heal.
