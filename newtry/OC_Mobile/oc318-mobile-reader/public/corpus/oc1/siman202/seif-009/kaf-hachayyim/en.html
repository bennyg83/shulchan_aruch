9) If one recited Borei Minei Mezonos over tree fruit or ground produce, although all foods are called nourishment, since 'nourishment' is a general term encompassing everything, this is ineffective for the purpose of a blessing, except for dates and wine. If one recited Borei Minei Mezonos over them, he fulfilled his obligation. Beis Yaakov writes this in a manuscript, cited by Petach HaDevir, paragraph 12; Sedei Chemed there, paragraph 36; Orach Chaim there. Chayei Adam, rule 58, paragraph 3, however, writes that if one recited Borei Minei Mezonos over any item, he fulfilled his obligation, except for water and salt; see there. Ben Ish Chai, parashas Balak, paragraph 13, writes the same. See above, siman 167, paragraph 75. This will be explained further below in siman 206, with the help of Heaven.
