There, note 6 — that is, for R' Meir we consider an ailonit in her minority, etc. And his words are wondrous, for there the explanation is that an ailonit has no days of maidenhood, but immediately after two years of minority she has the status of an adult retroactively when she reaches the years of an ailonit. Also there is no distinction in the matter between R' Meir and the Rabbis, but rather there it speaks regarding the dispute of R' Meir and the Rabbis regarding a minor — whether she has a fine.
