The ends of grapes, etc. The Tur writes that Maharam of Rothenburg ruled that on them and on the liquid emerging from them one recites Shehakol. The Tur adds that it appears to him that one should recite Borei Peri HaAdamah upon them, like unripe grapes. Here, however, he rules in accordance with Maharam. It appears to me that there is a clear proof for this from Yevamos 80a, where Abaye states that an aylonis leaves minority directly into adulthood. According to Rabbi Meir, she is regarded during minority as though already grown, since she will never develop the usual signs. The same applies here. Since these grapes will remain as they are and will never ripen, their law is already that of their final state. They therefore do not possess the blessing of grapes at all, and only Shehakol applies, for they are destined to become vinegar.
