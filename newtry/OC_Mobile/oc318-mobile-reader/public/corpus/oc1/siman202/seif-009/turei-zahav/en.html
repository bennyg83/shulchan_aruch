Grape ends (sofei anavim), etc. Tur wrote that Maharam of Rothenburg wrote that one blesses on it and on what comes from it shehakol; I hold that one blesses on it borei peri haAdamah, like on boser. End of his words. Tur ruled here like Maharam. I wish to bring a clear proof for this from what we say in Yevamot page 80: Abbaye said, from her minority it emerges to adulthood — that is, for R' Meir we consider an ailonit in her minority as if she were an adult, since she will not bring the signs; and likewise, since these grapes — their end is like their beginning and they will never ripen — behold, the law is as after they are grown, and there is no blessing of grapes on them at all, and here there is only the blessing shehakol, for they are destined to become vinegar.

And likewise on withered ones (noveilot). See above siman 204 seif 1.
