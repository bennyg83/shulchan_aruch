Seif katan 2: Cooked. Although with respect to yayin nesech cooked wine is not subject to libation, the reason is that this is not the manner of libation and it is also uncommon; therefore, the Sages did not decree concerning it, as the Rosh and Beit Yosef wrote in Yoreh Deah, siman 123. See the Taz there. Nevertheless, it is certainly considered wine with respect to its blessing and Kiddush, as stated in siman 272.
