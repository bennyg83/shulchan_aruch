[Levush] Such as beer and the like, etc. — it implies that in all beverages the law is thus, and so it implies in Taz; and Magen Avraham wrote that specifically regarding fig beer the law is thus. And the reason they did not establish in His utterance the language of the mishnah "In ten utterances the world was created," and so it is in Scripture "And He said — all of them," is because Bereshit is also an utterance, as it is written "By the word of Heaven the heavens were made" — to teach about creation from absolute nothing, and it was in creation on the first day, as Rashi explained "Et — to include that everything was created on the first day"; therefore they established it in His utterance — Mlbushi Yom Tov. R' David Abudarham wrote: R' Asher asked why in all the blessings [we say] Bara and in this one Nihyeh — let us also say in this one "that everything created"; and he answered: since they established [this blessing] upon animals and the like, one may not say upon them Bara, for the essence of their creation was not for this — for Adam the first man was not permitted flesh for eating; and furthermore, since they established [a blessing] upon wine that soured and bread that molded, etc., for all these are not correction of creation but corruption and loss of creation — one may not say upon them Bara; therefore they established to say Nihyeh and not Hayah, and the discerning person will understand. Chochmat Menuch wrote that one must say Nihyeh with a segol under the yud and not with a kamatz, which is past tense, for most blessings were established in the present tense.
