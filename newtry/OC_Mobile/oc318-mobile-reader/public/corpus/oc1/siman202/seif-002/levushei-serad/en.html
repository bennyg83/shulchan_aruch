Note 3 — in fig beer. So is the source of the law in Rashba and Rama, who omitted it, for they hold it is not precise. And Shach emended in the margin the word "figs" — he holds it is specifically because other beverages lose the taste; and as we find in siman 204 regarding water: if there are six parts, even if the taste of wine is stirred, it is not a complete taste and is nullified; likewise in all beverages we follow their power to spoil and to lose the taste of wine, and even though it is stirred it is not a complete settled taste — and the difficulty of Taz note 1 is resolved:
