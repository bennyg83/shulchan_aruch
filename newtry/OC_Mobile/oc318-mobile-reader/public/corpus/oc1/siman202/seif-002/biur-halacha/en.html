And every other tree from when it brings forth, etc., like a kind of chains, etc. — see in Biur HaGra who disagrees with Shulchan Aruch's ruling and proved that all these signs were not mentioned in the Talmud except for the prohibition of cutting trees in shevi'it, but for blessing it is reasonable that we require specifically when they reach the season of tithes, and before that it is not considered fruit fit for eating; and so is also Pri Megadim's view — and per their words all the laws in this seif change, namely: for grapes we require specifically that the seeds be visible inside; for olives, when they bring a third; for carobs, when black spots are seen on them, which is a sign it begins to blacken; and in every species there is a special measure for this, as all is explained in Rambam chapter 2 of Hilchot Ma'aser, see there — and before they reach this measure one blesses only borei peri haAdamah; and there is support for their words from Rambam chapter 5 of Hilchot Shemittah and chapter 8 of Hilchot Berakhot, see there; and Acharonim already debated this. And behold per the Gra and Pri Megadim it is reasonable that even sweetening by fire does not help, since it is not considered fruit before it reaches the season of tithes, and one always blesses borei peri haAdamah; and one should practice thus in action, ostensibly, for through this one exits from doubt either way; nevertheless one who practices like the ruling of the Acharonim who followed Shulchan Aruch — one should not protest against him, for he has whom to rely on; however if they were not fit to eat except through pressing, one should not bless borei peri haEtz except when cooked or fixed by fire, as I wrote in Mishna Berurah in the name of Chayei Adam and Olelot Ephraim. And know that regarding apples their measure for the season of tithes is even when they are small and only when they become round, as explained in Rambam there — and if so here too the Gra and Pri Megadim admit to Shulchan Aruch's view that one then blesses on them borei peri haEtz; and if these small ones were fit to eat only through pressing and fixed through cooking, one also blesses on them borei peri haEtz according to all:
