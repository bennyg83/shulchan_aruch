borei peri haAdamah — for anything that grows on a tree and is not the main fruit — borei peri haAdamah; and see note 5 and Yoreh De'ah siman 44 that in their days grapes were large, but now a bean is larger than a grape, and possibly likewise the white bean.

Bitter. And if one sweetens by fire one blesses shehakol [L'v Chamisha Assar] chapter 3 and 14.

And some say, etc. And the s.v. wrote one should not learn this from shevi'it, for behold carobs even after they appear like chains are bitter and not fit for eating; therefore (one does) not bless on them borei peri haEtz; and so appears the view of Rif and Rambam and Ri"v; and to me it appears it should only be doubt and one should bless borei peri haAdamah.
