Boser — as long as it has not reached twice the white bean, one blesses borei peri haAdamah on it; from when it is twice the white bean and onward one blesses borei peri haEtz; and since we do not know the measure of the white bean, one always blesses borei peri haAdamah until it is very large; and all other trees — from when they produce fruit one blesses borei peri haEtz, provided they are not bitter or very unripe until unfit for eating even by pressing — for then one does not bless at all.And some say that on carobs one does not bless borei peri haEtz until one sees in them something like chains of carobs; and likewise on olives until the blossom grows around them — and before that one blesses borei peri haAdamah. And likewise is the essence (Tur).)
