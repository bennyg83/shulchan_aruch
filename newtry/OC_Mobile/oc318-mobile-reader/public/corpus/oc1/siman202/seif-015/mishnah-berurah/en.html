(73) On sugar — meaning that in their countries sweet reeds grow and they squeeze them and cook their liquid until it thickens and resembles salt [Rambam].
(74) shehakol — for it is not superior to date honey that we consider only as mere sweat, and its blessing is shehakol; and all the more so here, where they changed through cooking [Rambam].
(75) And likewise one who sucks — meaning that one sucks the sugar canes themselves.
(76) shehakol — also for the above reason, for the cane itself is merely wood and it is impossible to eat it except that there is sweetness in it; if so, one who sucks the sweetness is no better than the tree than one who squeezed that sweat and drank it. And it is written in Chayei Adam that likewise one who chews a root called Eckerich likewise blesses only shehakol, for it too is merely wood except that there is sweet taste in it. And know that although Shulchan Aruch ruled anonymously that on sugar and on canes its blessing is shehakol — truly there are opinions among the early authorities as brought in Tur and Beit Yosef, and from the doubt he ruled to bless shehakol, for through this one fulfills according to everyone b'dieved; however b'dieved if he blessed borei peri haEtz or borei peri haAdamah he fulfilled; and likewise regarding our sugar that is made from beets — also one should bless l'chatchila shehakol, and b'dieved if he blessed borei peri haAdamah he fulfilled; as I explained all of this in Biur Halacha see there.
