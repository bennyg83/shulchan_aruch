15) Regarding distilled wine called aguardiente, Mor U'Ketziah writes that it is superior to cooked wine, one recites Borei Pri HaGafen over it, and contact by a non-Jew prohibits it; see there. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 2, however, challenges him and writes that Tashbetz, Responsa, volume 3, siman 291, and Radbaz, early responsa, siman 299, explicitly permit distilled wine of our type after contact by a non-Jew. Regarding its blessing, it is extremely clear that one recites Shehakol, since it has changed entirely and has a new appearance, aroma, and taste. He writes that this is the universal practice; see there.
