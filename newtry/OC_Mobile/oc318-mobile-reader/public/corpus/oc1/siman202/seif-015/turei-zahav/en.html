On the sugar cane, etc. For one should compare it to honey that comes from dates, for sugar too comes out by squeezing those reeds and cooking their water until they are solid and resemble salt; and so too Rambam and Tur challenged on this, for one should not compare to date honey, for dates are fruit, etc.; but these reeds that are not fit for eating, and the main planting of them is with intent for the sugar — certainly this is fruit and one blesses on them borei peri haEtz — end of his words. And in Midrash Rabbah chapter 8 of Berachot he wrote, and these are his words: if these reeds were found in the land of Tur he would not have claimed thus, for in the place where they are found they sell from them to multitudes to fulfill their needs, and behold the water that comes from them is like other fruit water — end of his words; and behold, on the contrary, from this there is great support for Tur, for since they plant for this purpose it is fruit; and Beit Yosef praised the words of Tur; but nevertheless from doubt one should bless shehakol; and it appears simple that if he has before him another fruit he should bless on it and thereby fulfill also for the sugar cane, for the view of Tur is that it is primary:
