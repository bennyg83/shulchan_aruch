(15) On cooked wine called Agua Ardiente — the Magen Avraham wrote it is preferable to cooked wine and one blesses upon it borei peri haGefen, and touch of a non-Jew forbids it — see there. But the Mechaber note 2 challenged him and wrote that Rashbatz in responsum volume 3 siman 461 and Radbaz in Rishonot siman 499 explicitly permitted touch of a non-Jew in our cooked wine. And regarding blessing the matter is very simple to bless shehakol, since it changed entirely in appearance and smell and a new different taste; and he wrote that such is the custom of the world — see there.
