Seif 15 — on sugar, etc. But Tosafot there 36b s.v. birtiva, etc., wrote "and on his sugar," etc., borei peri haEtz; and likewise Rash and Rash and geonim and author of Halakhot Gedolot and Mordekhai to bless on sugar borei peri haEtz; and Rambam responded to them from date honey, and all the more so this since it changed by fire; and his view is like Rashba in siman 10; but Tur rejected his words and answered him that dates are fruit and they planted them with intent to eat them — therefore when they change, their blessing changes; and so all fruits except wine and oil; but not so these that are not fit for eating and the main planting is for sugar — and this is an exception; and in many places he rejected Tur's words, and his words are astounding; and also Taz challenged him; also Peri Megadim in name of Maharam challenged him; and the geonim's view is primary:
