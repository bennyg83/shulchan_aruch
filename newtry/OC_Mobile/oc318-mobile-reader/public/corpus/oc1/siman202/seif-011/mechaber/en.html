Water in which raisins or figs were soaked, or their cooking water — one blesses shehakol on them, and one also fulfills according to Rosh; but regarding their concluding blessing there is doubt whether to bless Borei Nefashot or the abbreviated form of three like Rosh. Therefore a God-fearer should not drink except within a meal, or should eat fruit of the seven species and also drink water so that he will need to bless the abbreviated form of three and Borei Nefashot. And if he drew off the water and separated it from the raisins it becomes wine and one blesses borei peri haGefen and the abbreviated form of three — provided the raisins have moisture such that if one would tread them their juice would come out; but if when pressing them nothing comes out, no moisture of juice — then no.
