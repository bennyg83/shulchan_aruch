If one recited Borei Pri HaEtz over wine, Magen Avraham, siman 208, paragraph 22, writes that he did not fulfill his obligation; see there. Rema MiPano, however, writes in Alfasi Zuta on the chapter Keitzad Mevarchin that if one recited Borei Pri HaEtz or Borei Pri HaAdamah over wine, after the fact he fulfilled his obligation; see there. We write the same below in siman 208, paragraph 77, in the name of several authorities; see there.
