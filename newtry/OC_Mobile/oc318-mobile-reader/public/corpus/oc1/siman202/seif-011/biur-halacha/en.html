Within the meal — meaning he will not need a concluding blessing, for Birkat HaMazon exempts him; but the initial blessing, which is shehakol, he will need according to all opinions, and even per Rashba — for otherwise what is his remedy [behold through this there will be doubt in the initial blessing]; and although in siman 174 the view of many poskim is that one need not bless on water and beverages within the meal — and therefore per Rashba ostensibly he would not need to bless on it an initial blessing — but in truth it appears that even per Rashba they have no status of beverage but as fruit sauce [and they are like fruits within the meal regarding blessing], and only because their way is to eat only raw he holds that when they are cooked one blesses on their sauce shehakol:

Fruit of the seven species — it appears the same law applies if one tastes a little of the raisins and of the figs themselves; and the Mechaber deals initially with one who drinks the water of raisins alone, as we wrote in Mishna Berurah; and what Magen Avraham wrote that it deals with one who eats the raisins with the water — his intent is not that he eats them together, for if so according to all he would not bless even shehakol, for the water became subsidiary to the raisins, as I wrote in Mishna Berurah; rather his intent is that it deals with when while the raisins were soaking in the water his intent was also to eat the raisins, and therefore it is the law of soup on the water of raisins; and so is implied from the conclusion of Magen Avraham's words there, see there; and so is proved in the Gra:

He should drink water, etc. — it deals with one who is somewhat thirsty for water; for if he does not want to drink at all one should not bless on the water, as I wrote below in siman 204 seif 7, see there:

One blessing that is a summary of the three, etc. — see in Pri Megadim who sides that one should bless the summary of the three first, for it is important:

And Borei Nefashot — and if he does not have these things to exempt the fruit water, the view of Kenesset HaGedolah is that one blesses Borei Nefashot and it suffices; and his reason — see in the article of Mordechai end of siman 208, what he wrote in the name of Ginat Veradim and in Nahar Shalom in this siman; and Magen Avraham doubts his law of Kenesset HaGedolah; and see at end of siman 208 it implies his view not to bless at all on this [from Pri Megadim]; and so implies somewhat in Beit Yosef and Bach that he has no remedy at all; however if he has good wine that he will drink and bless on the vine and include also on the tree — and although Beit Yosef and Levush wrote here that this is not a remedy, for one may not add to the wording of the blessing due to doubt, see there — nevertheless if he has another remedy of fruit and water mentioned in Shulchan Aruch; but if he does not have this remedy he may rely on Taz end of siman 208, who wrote that in a time of need one may add to the blessing; and so agreed in that Mordechai article; and so too Chayei Adam:
