For the Rosh. That on all of them if one said shehakol he fulfilled. However, if one first ate fruit and blessed borei peri haEtz — it requires further study whether one should bless again on the broth shehakol; therefore one should drink something else first in order to bless shehakol — Magen Avraham. And syrup of fruits that were pressed, such as ogirkas or kompost that is called zwiebel kraut — one should bless only shehakol, for they were not pressed to extract their water. Taz.

Drawn. Thus wrote the Rambam: specifically when the raisins are mixed with the water; but if the water was drawn from the raisins it is wine — end of his words. And Rama explained his words: that is, specifically when the raisins are mixed with the water and one eats the raisins and drinks the water — then it is like the broth of all fruits; but if it was drawn, etc. — meaning that his intent is not to eat the raisins at all but only to draw wine from them — then even if one drinks from the vessel in which the raisins are, one blesses borei peri haGefen. And one does not require drawing; and so is implied from all the poskim who wrote "raisin wine" and did not distinguish between drawn and not drawn — Magen Avraham see there; and likewise Taz — that after three days it becomes complete wine even if it was not drawn — see there.
