There is room to question. Meaning: on raisins and figs there is a concluding blessing of the abbreviated form of three, and there is doubt regarding their water whether we rule like Rashba and bless after them Borei Nefashot, for their water is not like them, since their way is not to squeeze or boil; or we rule like Rosh, that whatever has in it the taste of the fruit is like the fruit and one blesses the abbreviated form of three after them; and it is astonishing to me, for raisins — their way is to soak them in water and to extract their water and make wine, as is written afterward; and if so, also according to Rashba the concluding blessing after them would be the abbreviated form of three like the fruit; and further, that which he distinguished between if he drew off from them and it was wine, and if he did not draw off it was not wine, as is found in Yoreh De'ah siman 123 — without drawing off the wine from the lees it was not wine; and in truth there is a distinction between raisin wine and ordinary wine, as Maharal Chaviv wrote in responsum siman 41, which I brought in my work on Yoreh De'ah siman 123: that whenever one crushes raisins and puts water on them and it ferments it is called wine, even though the dry raisins are inside the water after they were soaked three days in water — specifically regarding wine made in a winepress, where there is no way to leave the grapes there with the wine, do we require drawing off; not so here, where the way is to leave dry raisins in a barrel and there complete wine is made — we do not require drawing off — end of his words; therefore it appears that what he mentioned here, raisin water in "there is room to question," is not the main point, but rather after they are three days in water and have moisture it is complete wine and is forbidden through contact with a gentile even though he did not draw off the water; but regarding fig water there is properly doubt, as it appears clear to me; and brine of fruits that were pickled, such as gourds called ogorkes or kompot, one blesses only shehakol, for they were not pickled to extract their water:
