Raisins, etc. Taz wonders that raisins are normally soaked in water to extract their juice. If so, according to everyone their after-blessing should be Me'ein Shalosh like the fruit itself. In my humble opinion, one may answer that nevertheless they were not planted with this purpose in mind.
[Levush] When the raisins are mixed, etc. Magen Avraham explains that this means he eats the raisins together with the water as a mixture, like soup. But if he drinks only the liquid, even if he did not draw it off, he recites Borei Pri HaGefen, as stated in Yoreh Deah siman 123.
