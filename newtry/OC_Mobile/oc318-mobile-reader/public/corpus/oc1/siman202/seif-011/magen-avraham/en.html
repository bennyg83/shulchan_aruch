And he fulfilled. For on all of them if he said shehakol he fulfilled. However, if he first ate fruit and blessed borei peri haEtz — it requires further study whether he should bless again on the broth shehakol; therefore he should drink something else first in order to bless shehakol.

He should drink water. For water of figs does not exempt them from the final berakha, for they are not fruit (Maharil in the name of Semak) — see siman 208 seif 13.

And Borei Nefashot. And in Agudah siman 149 he wrote to bless first Borei Nefashot and then me'ein shalosh; however it is possible specifically there where he ate fruit and a cooked dish and was unsure if me'ein shalosh exempts Borei Nefashot — therefore bless Borei Nefashot first; not so here where he drank water it is simple me'ein shalosh does not exempt them; and if he drank them alone he blesses Borei Nefashot (Maharam Chalavah siman 207) — requires further study.

And if drawn off. Rabbeinu Yosef z"l wrote: specifically when the raisins are mixed with the water; but if water was drawn off from the raisins — it is wine — end of his words. It appears the explanation: when raisins are mixed with water and one eats the raisins and drinks the water — then it is like broth of all fruits; but if drawn off, etc. — meaning he does not intend to eat the raisins at all but only to draw wine from them; or even if he drank from the vessel where the raisins are inside — he blesses borei peri haGefen; and so too in Mordekhai regarding vegetable water in siman 207 seif 2; and so in siman 472 seif 6 all poskim wrote raisin wine and did not distinguish between drawn off or not; and further Yoreh De'ah siman 123 seif 1 writes in the name of R' Levi Chaviv: if they make wine from raisins that they crush them and put water on them and it ferments — it is called wine even though the raisins remain in the water after drinking three days in water, etc.; and even though regarding wine drawn off is required for yayin nesech — nevertheless regarding raisin wine whose way is to leave raisins in the barrel and wine is made there completely — drawn off is not required — end of his words; and all the more so for berakha, for even complete wine one blesses borei peri haGefen even without drawn off; therefore it appears what he wrote is primary, as in siman 204 seif 6.

And one berakha me'ein shalosh. Even if three were not placed and four came — Yoreh De'ah siman 123 in Beit Yosef (Maharam Chalavah).
