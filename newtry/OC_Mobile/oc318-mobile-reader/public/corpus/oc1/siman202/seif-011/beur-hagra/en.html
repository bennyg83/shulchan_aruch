Seif 11, But regarding the blessing, etc. This is because the Rosh is uncertain about the matter, writing, “It is possible that if,” etc., as cited in seif 10. The same applies to soaking, since an item steeped in liquid has the same law as one cooked.
And if, etc. For then it is called wine regarding yayin nesech, as stated in Avodah Zarah 55a. See Yoreh Deah 123:7 and Magen Avraham.
