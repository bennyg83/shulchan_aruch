Fruit pits, etc. Whether apple seeds or the pits of any other fruits, as Tosafot and Mordechai write.
[Levush] As part of the principal fruit, etc. For we do not derive their inclusion from the word "et"; rather, we consider them part of the principal fruit. This appears to be the view of Ri and the Rosh. It is difficult, however, because in the laws of orlah the Rosh derives the pit from "et," as Malbushei Yom Tov notes. See Maadanei Melech, page 44. I also require study because Tosafot on page 36 and Mordechai imply that one recites a blessing even over nut shells, although they are included only by derivation from "et." Perhaps this is also the Rosh's view, and he excludes them only because they are bitter and therefore not significant.
