From when they produce fruit, etc. — even if it is very small — Bach and poskim; and regarding sweetened almonds, that one blesses shehakol below in siman 204 — Rashba wrote in responsum 428 that all other trees [are judged] from the body of the fruit that one plants them on account of it for food, but sweet almonds in their small state one does not enjoy from the body of the fruit but from its outer shell — see siman 320 seif 5.
