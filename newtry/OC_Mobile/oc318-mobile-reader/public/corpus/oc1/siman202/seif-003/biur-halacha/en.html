Fruit pits, etc.: see Mishnah Berurah in what he wrote that many disagree on this and hold one blesses on them Borei Peri HaAdamah; and one should study these fruits [called sapodilla] within which are found pits like actual almonds: meaning a hard shell above and inside a small sweet fruit like actual almonds: whether their law is also like pits in general, or that here all agree one blesses Borei Peri HaEtz, for this is not in the category of pits but fruit within fruit; and also certainly all plant them with intent for this; and see in Darkhei Chayim: requires study:
