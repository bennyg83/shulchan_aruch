Seif 3. He recites Borei Peri HaEtz. Even HaEzer rules that he recites Borei Peri HaAdamah, and Responsa Panim Meiros, part 1, siman 5, reaches the same conclusion. Peri Chadash writes that one should recite Shehakol, but I do not know why.
