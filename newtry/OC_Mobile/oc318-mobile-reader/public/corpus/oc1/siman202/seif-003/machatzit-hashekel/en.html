(note 3) Regarding beer of figs — Shach.

His view, etc. — one could say that in Tashbatz, brought in Beit Yosef, from whom Rama brought this gloss, it is written "beer of figs mixed with wine," and Rama holds that figs are not precise (davka), and so too Taz. But Shach holds that figs are precise (davka).

And see there siman 204, in Magen Avraham note 16 it implies on the contrary that Magen Avraham's view is that other beverages, even when they are six parts against the wine, do not eliminate the taste of wine. And he distinguishes between their wines, which were strong, and our wines — see there.
