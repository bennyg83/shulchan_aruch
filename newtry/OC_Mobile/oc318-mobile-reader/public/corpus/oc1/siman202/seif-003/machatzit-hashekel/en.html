Seif katan 3: Regarding fig beer. Shach.
His intent, etc. Meaning, the Tashbetz cited by Beit Yosef, from which Rama derived this gloss, speaks of fig beer mixed with wine. Rama holds that figs are not mentioned specifically, and this is also the view of Taz. Shach, however, holds that figs are specific.
See what I wrote in siman 205. Magen Avraham there, seif katan 16, appears to hold the opposite, that other beverages do not nullify the taste of wine even when they are six parts against the wine. He distinguishes between their wines, which were strong, and our wines. See there.
