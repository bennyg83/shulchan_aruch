3) One who has a fruit before him and recited Borei Pri HaEtz over it, with no other fruit before him, and afterward a more beautiful fruit arrived, should eat only the fruit over which he recited the blessing. Sefer Chassidim, siman 847; Yafeh LaLev there. Before the blessing one should intend that through this blessing the power of the husks attached to those fruits is nullified, as stated above in paragraph 1.
