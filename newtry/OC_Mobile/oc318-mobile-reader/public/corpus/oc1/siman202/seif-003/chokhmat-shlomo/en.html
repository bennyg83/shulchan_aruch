There seif 18 — on pepper and ginger when they are wet, Borei peri haAdamah. N.B. Behold the source of this law is in the Gemara Berakhot 35b, where Rava said there that on peppers one blesses borei peri haAdamah, and it challenged: did not Rava say dry pepper on Yom Kippur is exempt? And it answered: here it deals with moist.
