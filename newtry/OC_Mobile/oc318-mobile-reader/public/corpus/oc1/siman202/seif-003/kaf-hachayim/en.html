(3) One who has fruit before him and blessed upon it borei peri haEtz and there was no other fruit before him, and after he blessed another fruit came before him that is more beautiful — he should not eat except what he blessed upon. Shulchan Aruch siman 697; Yad Ephraim there. And one should intend before the blessing, for through this blessing the power of the shells clinging to those fruits is nullified, as written above note 1.
