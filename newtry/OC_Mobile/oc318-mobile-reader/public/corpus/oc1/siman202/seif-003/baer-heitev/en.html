Borei peri haEtz. Maharal Levi wrote in Gan HaMelech siman 244 that one blesses borei peri haAdamah; and likewise in responsa Pnei Meiros siman 65 that one blesses borei peri haAdamah or shehakol; see Yad Ephraim.

Shehakol. Regarding the Mechaber's words — he ruled that if they are sweet one blesses upon them borei peri haEtz; from this it implies it is fruit; if so, when one sweetens by fire one should likewise bless borei peri haEtz, as with almonds in seif 5. And one could say that when they are bitter they are not fruit, unlike almonds — Magen Avraham; and likewise in Levush.
