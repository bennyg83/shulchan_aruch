Borei Peri HaEtz. Rabbi Aharon HaLevi writes in Gan HaMelech, siman 30, that one recites Borei Peri HaAdamah. Responsa Panim Meiros, siman 65, likewise rules that one recites Borei Peri HaAdamah or Shehakol. See there, and see Yad Aharon.
Shehakol. One might ask: according to the Mechaber, who rules that when they are sweet one recites Borei Peri HaEtz, they are evidently considered fruit. Why, then, when they are improved by fire should one not likewise recite Borei Peri HaEtz, as with almonds in seif 5? The answer is that when they are bitter they are not considered fruit, unlike almonds. Magen Avraham. Levush writes likewise.
