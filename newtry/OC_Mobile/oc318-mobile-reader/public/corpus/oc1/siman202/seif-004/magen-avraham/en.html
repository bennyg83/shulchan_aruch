With bread. Bach wrote requires further study: why was it necessary to teach us this law, explained in siman 212? And Rabbi Yosef Yeres challenged Rif; and it appears we teach even one concerned for his throat who eats much oil and little bread: we do not say oil is primary; and so from Rif's wording; and it is difficult from one who eats salted fish with bread who blesses on the salted fish: requires further study: end. Therefore it appears: if he intends for medicine and eats only a little bread so it not harm his throat: it is not so that he blesses on the oil; and so Bach siman 212: see there; and because of this difficulty one should not innovate a new law, for many times poskim wrote a simple law; and that which the Gemara did not establish one who eats it with bread for medicine: that is because there is no medical practice thus, since it specified anigran and did not specify other beverages; and likewise if he drinks with other beverages for medicine, such as with beer or wine: he blesses borei peri haAdamah.
Oil primary. Even if the oil is the minority (Bach); however from what Rosh wrote siman 104 regarding vinegar it implies specifically much; and possibly it stated the ordinary manner.
Anigran primary. Even if it is the minority.
