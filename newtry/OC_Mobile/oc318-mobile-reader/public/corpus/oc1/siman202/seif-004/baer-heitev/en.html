Primary. If his intention is medicinal and he eats only a small amount of bread so that the oil will not harm his throat, he recites the blessing over the oil. The same applies if he drinks it medicinally together with another beverage, such as beer or spirits: he recites Borei Peri HaEtz over the oil. Magen Avraham.
