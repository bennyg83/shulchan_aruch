(note 4) borei peri haAdamah, etc.: and see Yoreh De'ah siman 44, which wrote that a small kidney is not treif until it is reduced in a large animal to the size of a grape. And in a small animal its measure is up to a bean (ful), and certainly the measure of a small animal is smaller than the measure of a large animal; and we see that a bean is larger than a grape, and one can say that grapes in earlier times were larger than beans. And therefore Magen Avraham wrote that possibly likewise the white bean in their days was smaller than a grape; unlike in this time, where it is larger than a grape. And with this one answers what Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote: since we are not familiar with the measure of the white bean, etc.: for seemingly why do we not know, do not our eyes behold? Rather he means that in this time, on the contrary, the white bean is larger than a grape, and we need to give the measure of the white bean in their days against grapes in their days; likewise we estimate now in this time according to that proportion. But we do not know the measure of the white bean and the measure of grapes in their days; therefore it is impossible to estimate according to proportion in this time.
