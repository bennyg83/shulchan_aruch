But — Taz, Levush, and so too in Darishah to distinguish between dates that one mashes by hand — the form of the fruit is somewhat recognizable, and therefore for Rambam borei peri haEtz; but povidla (compote) is not recognizable at all. And since the Gemara says trimah is permitted, shechar (date beer) is forbidden in terumah — if so, anything that one eats, even though it is not at all recognizable in its matter, remains; and terumah and blessing are alike — see Berachot 38a. And from that which is said there 57a — wheat flour borei peri haAdamah like olive oil — and he concludes there there is no other superiority; in any case we have seen that even when the form of the fruit is not at all recognizable, it stands in its matter; and likewise one who boils vegetables — in its matter it stands on daf 38b, and as explained in Shulchan Aruch in siman 205 Rosh Hashanah — clearly it stands in its matter; all the more so in povidla. And one must say that these are not the way to pound, and on the contrary they stand to be eaten thus; therefore if it changed — shehakol — not so olive oil and wheat flour per Rashi, and one who boils — their way is thus. If so, what proof does Taz bring from sky powders in siman 153 seif 67 — their way is thus — and as Magen Avraham wrote from him note 18, and so too in Tehillat Heaven siman 29; and there it implies that therefore in povidla he leaned to bless shehakol, because they also mix honey and spices, and there is a double degradation — they changed literally and also changed in taste; behold the fruits are completely melted and their form is not recognizable, and their taste is recognizable — Tehillat Heaven admits it is possible to bless borei peri haEtz. And yet, from Mishnah Yevamot — explanation that Rosh boiled borei peri haEtz even though it is not their way — there is some proof as Taz wrote; nevertheless one can say their taste is recognizable, not so povidla that mixes honey and spices as Tehillat Heaven wrote there. And behold, all this regarding the first blessing — there is a remedy of shehakol; and the concluding blessing is difficult: if trimah — dough compressed in the hand — borei peri haEtz, likewise the concluding blessing is the abbreviated form of three; and if shehakol — the concluding blessing is Borei Nefashot and borei peri haChadash, as explained in siman 208:13 that Borei Nefashot and the abbreviated form of three do not exempt one another with meat and the like; and there is a remedy to eat borei peri haEtz from the seven species and something that is shehakol, and afterward go out with the concluding blessing — Rosh Chodesh 208 and Tehillat Heaven 30. But if it happened that he has only this trimah — Tur abbreviated form of three from Torah — he should bless from doubt; and for us it is not doubt, as Magen Avraham 172 — nevertheless one should not bless at all from doubt, neither Borei Nefashot nor the abbreviated form of three. This requires further study; and see Magen Avraham note 11, and it will be explained there further.
