In Taz, seif katan 2. The same question applies regarding the anigron. Why are we compelled to establish the case as one in which the anigron itself requires a blessing, and consequently to explain that the person has a throat ailment? Let us instead say that the case is one in which the anigron requires no blessing, and then there would be no need to establish it as involving a throat ailment. For this reason Rabbeinu Yonah had to say that the Gemara merely stated the usual case.
