(4) A person who has one nut in his hand should not bless and afterward break the nut, lest the pit became wormy or rotten and he find he blessed in vain. Shulchan Aruch siman 699. And they brought this in Keneset HaGedolah in Hagahot HaTur. And he wrote that the same applies to every fruit regarding which one should be concerned it is not fit for eating — end of his words; but if it is beautiful and clean and there is no concern it is not fit for eating, it is a mitzvah to bless upon it while it is whole, and one need not be concerned about delay when cutting it between the blessing and eating, as written below siman 206 note 21 — see there. And fruit that requires peeling — one should peel it and afterward bless, on account of interruption; for even if he removed the peel, if the fruit itself is not yet broken it is called whole, and therefore one must be careful when peeling the fruit not to break it in order to bless upon it while it is whole. And likewise when opening fruit to see if it has worms, one must be careful to return and close it if possible so that it appear whole at the time of blessing. And see below siman 206 seif 3 and in our words there, with Heaven's help.
