Unripe grapes. See Baer Heitev. If one squeezed grapes while they were still unripe and made wine from them, he recites Shehakol. See Yoreh Deah, siman 123. According to Rabbeinu Meshullam, such liquid does not become prohibited through the touch of a non-Jew, because it is called water. Although Rabbeinu Chananel prohibits it because we are not proficient in determining what qualifies as unripe grapes, and the poskim agree with him, nevertheless with respect to the blessing one recites only Shehakol. Regarding the grapes themselves while still unripe, see what Yad Ephraim writes on this subject.
