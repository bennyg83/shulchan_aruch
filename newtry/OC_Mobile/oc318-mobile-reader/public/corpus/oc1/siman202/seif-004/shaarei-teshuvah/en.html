(Boser.) Baer Heitev and if one squeezed grapes while they are still boser and made wine from them, one blesses shehakol; see Yoreh De'ah siman 123, and according to Rabbeinu Meshulam it is not forbidden through contact with a gentile, for it is called water; and although Rabbenu Chananel forbids because we are not expert in what boser is — thus agreed the poskim; nevertheless regarding blessing one blesses only shehakol.
