Seif 6 — the caperim (kaparisin) with borei peri haAdamah — that if he blessed on them borei peri haEtz, even according to Even HaEzer below siman 206 that regarding something that is not the essence of the fruit — if he blessed borei peri haEtz he did not fulfill, and not like Magen Avraham — nevertheless here he fulfilled, for according to Rosh it is fit to bless on the caperim borei peri haEtz; if so, he fulfilled from doubt; and even according to Rif — nevertheless, since for orlah in Eretz Yisrael it is fruit — if so, it is like doubt; and we compare the law of blessing to challah — as we are lenient in challah, so too for blessing from doubt one blesses borei peri haAdamah; but b'dieved if he blessed borei peri haEtz he fulfilled from doubt.
