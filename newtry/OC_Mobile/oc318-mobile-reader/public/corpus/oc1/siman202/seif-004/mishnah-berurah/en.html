(26) As it is — without mixtures, lest he weaken the power of the oil.
(27) Then it harms him — and even if he is concerned for his throat he likewise does not bless, for oil as it is one does not enjoy from it.
(28) With bread — explanation: even though with bread the oil does not harm him and on the contrary he enjoys from this — nevertheless in any case he does not bless on it because bread is primary and he is exempt with the blessing of bread.
(29) And he exempts the secondary — and even if there was much oil it is likewise only secondary to bread; and the same applies when one eats oil to dip bread in it; but if he eats oil because he is concerned for his throat and only eats a little bread with it so that he can eat the oil with it and it not harm him — in this the oil is called primary and it exempts the bread that is secondary to it, and he need not wash hands and bless HaMotzi; and there are those who disagree and hold that even in such a case bread is primary [Ateret Zekenim].
(30) With beet juice (mei silka) — and the same law if they mixed it with some other beverage in a matter wherein he enjoys the oil through it and it does not harm him.
(31) Then the oil is primary — for it softens and heals the throat; and even though anigran is more than the oil, for we do not follow here after the majority of the beverage but whatever thing whose primary intent is primary for the matter of blessing and the second is exempt with his blessing.
(32) And one blesses on it borei peri haEtz — and after it the abbreviated form of three if he drank the requisite amount.
(33) Anigran is primary — and it is implied from the words of Taz that it discusses when the beet juice is the majority relative to oil and therefore oil is secondary to it; but Magen Avraham wrote that even when anigran is a small amount, also anigran is primary whenever it is not for medicine but for eating, and one blesses on the anigran and exempts the oil.
(34) shehakol — so wrote Rambam there and so is implied in Tur and Rash; and Magen Avraham wondered, for it is clear below in siman 205 that the blessing on beet juice is borei peri haAdamah; and the Gra reconciled it that here when they fix it for drinking behold it is like a beverage and its blessing is shehakol.
