Seif 4, He does not, etc. Rashi there, Rif, and the other poskim.
If he has a throat ailment, etc. See Rashi there, s.v. hachosheish. This is also the view of Rabbi Yosef and the Tur, that the law does not depend on whether his throat hurts, but on whether he puts in much oil or little. Behag, Rambam, Rashba, and the Shulchan Aruch, however, hold that the law does not depend on the quantity, but on whether he has a throat ailment, in accordance with the plain meaning of the Gemara.
Shehakol. Although on 39a the Gemara says that water in which beets were cooked has the same law as the beets, nevertheless, for drinking it one recites Shehakol, as Tosafos writes there on 38a, at the end of s.v. hai, and in s.v. yehei.
