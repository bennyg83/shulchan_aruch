Olive oil, if one drank it as it is, one does not recite any blessing upon it at all, because it causes him harm. And if he ate it together with bread, he does not recite a blessing upon it, for the bread is primary, and one blesses upon the primary food and thereby exempts the secondary. And if he drank it mixed with beet-water {which is called anigron}, in which case it does not cause harm — on the contrary, it is beneficial for the throat — if he is suffering from his throat, the oil is primary and he recites upon it "Borei Pri Ha'etz." But if he does not intend it for a remedy but rather for eating, the anigron is primary, and he recites a blessing only upon the anigron (Shehakol).
