Olive oil — if one drinks it as is one does not bless on it at all, because then it harms him; and if one eats it with bread one does not bless on it, for bread is primary and one blesses on the primary and exempts the secondary; and if one drinks it mixed with beet juice (called anigran) — then it does not harm, on the contrary it benefits the throat if one is concerned for his throat — then the oil is primary and one blesses borei peri haEtz on it; and if he does not intend for medicine but for eating, then anigran is primary and one blesses only on the anigran (shehakol).
