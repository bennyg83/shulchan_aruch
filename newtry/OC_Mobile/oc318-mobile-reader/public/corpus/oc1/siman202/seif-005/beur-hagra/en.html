Seif 5, Almonds, etc., when they are, etc. This is like radishes on 36a. Although regarding maaser we rule according to the opinion that both this and that are exempt, as stated at the end of the first chapter of Chullin, nevertheless, the Rosh distinguishes regarding blessings. See there. His words require study; see Maadanei Yom Tov. In any event, the law is correct, as with radishes.
When they are large, etc. As stated regarding maaser according to all opinions, and as explained above in seif 2.
And if one sweetens them, etc. As stated in Eruvin 28b: “Is there anyone who says,” etc. All this applies regarding blessings. See Tosafos there, s.v. ika beinaihu. The fact that they are fit to be sweetened is of no consequence regarding the blessing. See Rashi there, s.v. veyachol, “but,” etc. The same applies regarding the blessing, as explained above.
