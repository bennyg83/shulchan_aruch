5) There: One recites, etc. Every blessing must have a past-tense meaning, such as, 'everything came into being through His word,' rather than, 'everything will come into being through His word.' Likewise, 'Who creates the fruit' means, 'Who created,' like, 'Who forms mountains and creates wind.' Similarly, HaMotzi, 'Who brings forth bread from the earth,' means, 'Who brought forth.' Riaz, cited by Shiltei Gibborim at the end of the chapter Keitzad Mevarchin; Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur. See above, siman 167, paragraph 22.
