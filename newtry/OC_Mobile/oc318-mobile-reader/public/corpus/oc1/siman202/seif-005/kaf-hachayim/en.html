(5) There — one blesses, etc. — all blessings must have their meaning in the past tense, such as "that everything came into being by His word," and when one blesses shehakol — "that everything came into being by His word." And likewise Borei peri — its meaning is that He created, like "Who forms mountains and creates wind." And likewise HaMotzi lechem min ha'aretz — its meaning is that He brought out. Riaz and Beit Din in Sefer HaGan at the end of the chapter "How does one bless." Keneset HaGedolah in Hagahot HaTur. And see above siman 167 note 22.
