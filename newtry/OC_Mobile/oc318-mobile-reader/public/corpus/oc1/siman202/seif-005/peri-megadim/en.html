Honey — Taz. Rosh Berachot 38a — date honey is shehakol; and the Gaon said when mixed in water — behold only borei peri haEtz; and Rosh wrote "not so," etc., and Beit Yosef understood in Rosh that he truly holds squeezing and pounding are inferior to that which comes out by itself, but cooking is better than all; and therefore for us who ruled here that honey that flows by itself is shehakol — all the more so squeezing and pounding that are inferior to that which comes out by itself. And in seif 10 for Rosh — fruit water through cooking borei peri haEtz, and through squeezing shehakol, as Magen Avraham wrote note 23; and such is certainly Rosh — that squeezing and pounding in fruits are inferior to that which comes out by itself through cooking, for on daf 39 he wrote: water of boiled fruits — if one cooked, behold it is like fruits; and "not so" to what is written regarding sweat (38a) that one cooked in water, etc.; and above through squeezing — apple wine, etc. — clearly for Rosh squeezing is inferior to cooking, as Tur wrote in siman 205 and Taz Rosh Hashanah note 6. This requires further study — much of what is written there that apple wine, etc., comes out by itself through squeezing, and nevertheless the halachah is like R' Yehoshua that one is not liable for a fifth and it is inferior to cooking; but in Chiddushei HaRan on daf 38 it implies that squeezing is literally like cooking, and he was troubled from water of boiled fruits — when boiled — and why not apple wine; and he answered that there their way is to boil, not so apple wine; and Rosh does not distinguish between the way of boiling and squeezing — only that squeezing is inferior to cooking. And now, seemingly the Mechaber here in seif 10 ruled like view 1 in stam like Rashba — and that is the difficulty of water of boiled fruits, and he holds squeezing is like cooking; and in Rosh Hashanah 63 he ruled one who squeezes does not bless except shehakol — seemingly he seized the rope at both ends; and in our Chiddushim we expanded. We further wrote there regarding other fruits — reason and sense give that squeezing is more preferred than that which comes out by itself; and in dates their nature is that when there is a majority of fat in them, that which flows by itself is like the essence of the fruit — and this is the Gaon's claim, since he left off calling them dates and praised Eretz Yisrael in honey that flows — from which we learn this is the good and primary; but when there is not a majority of fat and one squeezes — it is not like it. And nevertheless we learned for Rosh: that which flows by itself, or squeezing and pounding, is not like cooking; and for Rashba squeezing is like cooking; and wine and oil even when flowing by itself — borei peri haGefen and borei peri haEtz, as explained in Ateret Zekenim 38a and the mishnah that one absorbs only on that which comes out, etc., whether by itself or by squeezing — see there. And see Peirush on Terumot in mishnah 3, detail 3 — date honey, vinegar of sithoniyot, apple wine, and other fruit waters — why did he specify; and in Rosh within the explanation of Rashi in this matter he raised this regarding his view that squeezing and pounding are not like cooking; and according to him in terumah, if one cooked them one is liable for a fifth on the water; but for Rashba he specified apple wine — similar to those that are not the way to boil — behold fruits whose way is to boil and to squeeze — a drink like them; and honey — to exclude from the Gaon and his claim that praised Eretz Yisrael; and vinegar of sithoniyot — not so, for that which comes out is the essence of the fruit, as R' Eliezer wrote, and Chiddushei HaRan, z"l, Magen Avraham note 20; and in our Chiddushim we expanded. And here there is no place to expand.
