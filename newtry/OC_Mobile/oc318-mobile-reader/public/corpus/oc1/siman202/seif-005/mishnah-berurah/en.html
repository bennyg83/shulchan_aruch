(35) One blesses borei peri haEtz — and even though on sweet small ones one blesses only shehakol, as below siman 204 — that case is different, for there it is not finished fruit and certainly they were not planted with intent to eat them before they are cooked; not so here, where when they grow large they will not be fit for eating at all — they were planted properly with intent to eat them in their small stage, and that is the completion of their fruit.

(36) And if one sweetens — for since they changed for the better through sweetening by fire, it is like other fruits that are not fit to eat raw and were cooked — one blesses when cooked borei peri haEtz, as below seif 12.

(37) One blesses borei peri haEtz — and likewise other kinds of fruits that are sour or bitter and not fit for eating at all, and one sweetens or cooks them — one blesses borei peri haEtz, for in truth it is fruit, only it is not fit for eating; therefore sweetening helps.
