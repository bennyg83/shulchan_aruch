Nachem. See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef writes that Knesses HaGedolah, Responsa, part 2, questions the comparison to Yom Kippur. Shibolei HaLeket writes that both on Tishah BeAv and in Birchas HaMazon one mentions the character of the day. Beis Yehudah, part 2, agrees with Rama that Nachem is recited in Birchas HaMazon even by one whose custom is to recite it in the Amidah only at Minchah. Eliyah Rabbah, however, inclines not to add it in Birchas HaMazon, by comparison to Yom Kippur. The established local custom should be followed, and one who does recite it should place it in the blessing concerning Yerushalayim without altering the conclusion of the blessing.
