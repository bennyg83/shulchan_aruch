1) [Seif 1] On Tisha B’Av, in the blessing Boneh Yerushalayim, one says Nachem, etc. Here in Jerusalem, the custom is to say Nachem in all the prayers, as appears from the wording of the Shulchan Aruch. Pri Chadash in the Likkutim; Birchos HaMayim, 41b; Birkei Yosef, entry 1; Shalmei Tzibbur, 132b; Zechirah LeChaim, entry 9. However, now the population has increased, and each congregation follows the custom of its place. For the reason, see above siman 468, entry 65, and see below entry 7.
