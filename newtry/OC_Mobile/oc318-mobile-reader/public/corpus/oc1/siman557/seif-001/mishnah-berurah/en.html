(1) On Tisha B'Av one says etc. From the Mechaber's terse wording it is implied that his view is that they say it in all the prayers, and so is the custom in Yerushalayim; but in our lands the custom is as the Rama wrote.
(2) In Boneh Yerushalayim etc. Because it is of that subject matter; and if one forgot, he says it in Avodah, namely before "VeSechezenah," and he need not conclude with "Baruch Menachem Tziyon," but rather "VeSechezenah Eineinu" through the end of the blessing.
(3) And Aneinu in Shomei'a Tefillah. That is, for an individual; but the chazzan says it as a blessing of its own with a concluding formula, between Go'el and Rofei. Now, regarding an individual, the Mechaber's view in siman 565 is that on the four fasts he says Aneinu in every prayer in Shomei'a Tefillah; but the Rama's view there is that an individual says it only at Minchah. And regarding the chazzan, the view of most poskim is that even on Tisha B'Av he should say it at Shacharis between Go'el and Rofei, as regarding other fasts.
(4) We do not make him repeat. Because on days when there is no Musaf offering, if one erred and did not say something related to the occasion, we do not make him repeat.
(5) In Birkas HaMazon. In Boneh Yerushalayim; and even though in prayer they customarily say it only at Minchah, nevertheless in Birkas HaMazon, where there is no Minchah, one should say it. And see Shaarei Teshuvah in the name of some later authorities who cast doubt on this ruling; and likewise in Beur HaGra it is implied that his view tends toward not saying it, for they instituted it only in prayer. However, according to all opinions, if one did not say it, we do not make him repeat.
