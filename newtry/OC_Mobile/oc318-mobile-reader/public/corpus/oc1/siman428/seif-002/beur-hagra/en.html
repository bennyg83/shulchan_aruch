Seif 2 — these days, etc. And sign for fixing. Three: always regular in simple and intercalated. Two and seven: cannot be regular in simple or intercalated; and five in intercalated. Five in simple cannot be deficient — behold seven types of fixing in simple and seven in intercalated. They also gave sign: Sunday of Shavuot is Sunday of Chanukah, sign "ki ner mitzvah" etc.; in complete year then Tuesday of Shavuot and Sunday of Chanukah is Purim day — only in simple deficient year.
