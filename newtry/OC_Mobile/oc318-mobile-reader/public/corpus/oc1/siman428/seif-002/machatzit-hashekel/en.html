(s.k. 2) Tammuz, etc. Marcheshvan Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat as must be — not as Shulchan Aruch Cheshvan gedu"a — then the sign would be on first day of Rosh Chodesh since Tishrei full; what Cheshvan sign from other months' signs refers to second day of Rosh Chodesh which is primary — examine and forget — must be Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat; then also Cheshvan sign on second day of Rosh Chodesh — so Taz.
