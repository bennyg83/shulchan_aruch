2) Rosh Hashanah does not occur on Sunday, Wednesday, or Friday. If Rosh Hashanah occurred on Sunday, Hoshana Rabbah would occur on Shabbos. If it occurred on Wednesday, Yom Kippur would occur on Friday. If it occurred on Friday, Yom Kippur would occur on Sunday. This is impossible, and therefore Rosh Hashanah does not occur on Sunday, Wednesday, or Friday. Once this rule is stated, it follows automatically that Yom Kippur does not occur on Tuesday, Friday, or Sunday, for if Yom Kippur occurred on Tuesday, Hoshana Rabbah would occur on Shabbos.
