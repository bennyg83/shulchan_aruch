8) [Se'if 2] These are the days on which Rosh Chodesh is fixed, etc. The order of the months is as follows: Nisan is always full; Iyar deficient; Sivan full; Tammuz deficient; Av full; Elul deficient; Tishrei full; Teves deficient; Shevat full; and Adar deficient. Rosh Chodesh Nisan is always one day; Iyar, two days; Sivan, one day; Tammuz, two days; Av, one day; Elul, two days; Tishrei, one day; Cheshvan, two days; Shevat, one day; and Adar and Adar II, two days. Cheshvan and Kislev are sometimes both full, and the year is called complete. Sometimes both are deficient, and the year is called deficient. Sometimes Cheshvan is deficient and Kislev full, and the year is called regular. When both are full, Rosh Chodesh Kislev and Rosh Chodesh Teves always last two days. When both are deficient, Rosh Chodesh Kislev and Rosh Chodesh Teves each last one day. When one is deficient and one full, Rosh Chodesh Kislev lasts one day and Rosh Chodesh Teves two days. Peri Chadash. This is based upon the words of the Tur and Levush. See there.
