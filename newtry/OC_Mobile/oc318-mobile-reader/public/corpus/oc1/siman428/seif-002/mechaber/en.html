These are the days on which Rosh Chodesh is fixed and not on other days: Nissan — Sunday, Tuesday, Friday; Iyar — Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat; Sivan — Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday; Tammuz — Sunday, Tuesday, Friday; Av — Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat; Elul — Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday; Tishrei — Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat; Marcheshvan — Monday, Wednesday, Friday; Kislev — Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Shabbat; Tevet — Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Shabbat; Shevat — Monday, Wednesday, Friday; Adar — Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat(and in an intercalated year, the first Adar — Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat, and the second — Sunday, Tuesday, Friday.):
