(2) Not Monday, Wednesday, Friday Rosh Hashanah — if Rosh Hashanah Sunday, Hoshana Rabbah Shabbat; if Thursday, Yom Kippur Shabbat; if Shabbat, Yom Kippur Sunday — impossible; therefore not Monday, Wednesday, Friday Rosh Hashanah; from not Monday, Wednesday, Friday Rosh Hashanah automatically not Sunday, Tuesday, Friday Yom Kippur — if Yom Kippur Tuesday, Hoshana Rabbah Shabbat.
