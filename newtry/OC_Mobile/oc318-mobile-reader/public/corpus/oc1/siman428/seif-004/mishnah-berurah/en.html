(א) Complete intercalated year — a sign that the hei of the mnemonic indicates Rosh Hashanah on Thursday and the chet indicates that year was deficient [Cheshvan and Kislev both deficient]; and Rama adds "and some say intercalated" — also a sign: the hei indicates Rosh Hashanah on Thursday and the shin indicates that year was complete [Cheshvan and Kislev full]; and meaning: when Rosh Hashanah on Thursday and the year intercalated, whether those months complete or deficient, they read Noach on Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan and automatically an extra Shabbat was added to the reading — therefore they read Acharei Mot before Pesach.
(ב) On Monday and Tuesday — meaning the first day of Rosh Hashanah on Monday or Tuesday.
(ג) Bet-gimmel the King — meaning Rosh Hashanah when they say "the King in judgment" when it falls Monday or Tuesday; pat Vayelech — language of patot, the pieces into which they divide Nitzavim and Vayelech.
(ד) When Rosh Hashanah on Thursday and Friday — when Rosh Hashanah on Thursday or Friday, thus in Tur and old Shulchan Aruch; meaning the first day of Rosh Hashanah on Thursday or Friday, for on the 16th it cannot fall, as explained.
(ה) Pekudei and Pesach — pekudei: language of command to read near Pesach; segoru: metzora musgar; manu veatzeru: Bamidbar where Israel were counted before Shavuot; tzomu: the Ninth of Av; tzelu: Vaetchanan; komu veteku: Nitzavim before the blowing of Rosh Hashanah. When the first day of Pesach on Shabbat, then sons of Eretz Yisrael who make Pesach only seven days read on the Shabbat after Shemini — automatically they have an extra Shabbat in the year without a parashah — therefore some divide even in a simple year Tazria and Metzora, and some divide BeHar and BeChukotai; and if intercalated they also divide Matot and Masei — Mahari Weil, Magen Avraham.
