(א) Complete intercalated year. Meaning when Rosh Hashanah fell on Thursday and the year was intercalated — then they read parashat Noach on Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan and an extra Shabbat was added.

(ב) The King. Meaning Rosh Hashanah when they say "the King in judgment." Pat Vayelech — meaning the word "patot" — the pieces into which they divide Nitzavim and Vayelech.

(ג) Pekudei — language of command, meaning they command to read near Pesach. Segoru — language of metzora musgar. Manu — meaning Bamidbar where Israel were counted before Shavuot. Tzomu — the Ninth of Av. Tzelu — Vaetchanan. Komu — Nitzavim before the blowing of Rosh Hashanah.
