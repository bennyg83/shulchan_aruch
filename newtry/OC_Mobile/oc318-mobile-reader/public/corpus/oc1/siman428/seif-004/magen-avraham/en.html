(א) Complete intercalated year. Meaning Rosh Hashanah fell on Thursday and the year was intercalated — then they read parashat Noach on Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan and one Shabbat was added.

(ב) Bamidbar Sinai before Shavuot. Because on Shavuot judgment is on trees — therefore they read one parashah after the rebukes so the year and its curses end; and why they read Atem Nitzavim before Rosh Hashanah.

(ג) The King. Meaning Rosh Hashanah when they say "the King in judgment." Pat Vayelech — meaning the word patot, the pieces into which they divide Nitzavim and Vayelech.

(ד) Pekudei and Pesach. Pekudei — language of command to read near Pesach; Segoru — metzora musgar; Manu — Bamidbar Sinai where Israel were counted; Tzomu — the Ninth of Av; Tzelu — Vaetchanan; Komu — Atem Nitzavim. When Pesach falls on Shabbat, then sons of Eretz Yisrael who read only seven days read on the Shabbat after it Shemini — some divide Tazria and Metzora, some divide BeHar and BeChukotai; and if intercalated they divide Matot and Masei (Mahari Weil part 2 siman 4, Tikun Yissachar, see there folio 89).
