Seif 4 — always, etc. Megillah 31b; see Tosafot there s.v. Moshe, etc. Sign for combined parshiyot: Vayakhel Pekudei; Acharei Mot Kedoshim; Tazria Metzora; BeHar BeChukotai — always combined in simple year and divided in intercalated except "some say" [Rosh Hashanah Thursday complete year Cheshvan and Kislev full] — then even in simple they divide Vayakhel Pekudei because Shabbat added before Pesach. Chukat Balak not combined except when Shavuot on Shabbat — parashah added [on Yom Tov no weekly parashah] — in simple and intercalated. Matot Masei not separated except complete intercalated year Rosh Hashanah Thursday — whether complete or deficient — then Acharei Mot before Pesach and Naso before Shavuot — one parashah missing — separate Matot Masei. Nitzavim Vayelech not separated except Rosh Hashanah Monday or Tuesday as Shulchan Aruch below bet-gimmel the King, etc.
