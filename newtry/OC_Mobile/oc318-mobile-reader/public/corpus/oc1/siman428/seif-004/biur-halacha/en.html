(א) Tzav before Pesach — because Tzav speaks of hag'alah of vessels for notar kodashim and hag'alah from chametz learned from Temple; also intercalated read Metzora before Pesach — written earthen vessel must break — like hag'alah — Avudraham.

(ב) And always read, etc. before Shavuot — so always break this parashah between BeChukotai with rebuke curses and Shavuot day of judgment on tree fruits — not read curses near judgment day giving opening to Satan to prosecute — Levush.

(ג) Bamidbar Sinai before Shavuot — sometimes even Naso before Shavuot when that year intercalated Rosh Hashanah Thursday — read Acharei Mot before Pesach [whether Cheshvan Kislev full or deficient] — then Naso before Shavuot — poskim. I copy before reader from Gra's explanation which parshiyot combined which separate. Sign combined: Vayakhel Pekudei; Tazria Metzora; Acharei Mot Kedoshim; BeHar BeChukotai — always combined simple year, divided intercalated except "some say" [Rosh Hashanah Thursday complete] — then even simple divide Vayakhel Pekudei — Shabbat added before Pesach. Chukat Balak not combined except Shavuot Shabbat — parashah added [no weekly on Yom Tov] — simple and intercalated. Matot Masei not separated except intercalated Rosh Hashanah Thursday — complete or deficient — Acharei Mot before Pesach, Naso before Shavuot — one parashah missing — separate Matot Masei. Nitzavim Vayelech not separated except Rosh Hashanah Monday or Tuesday as Shulchan Aruch [no difference intercalated simple complete deficient]. I also copy briefly Gra's main rules for fixing months: when Rosh Hashanah Sunday or Tuesday all months regular [one full one deficient Tishrei full Cheshvan deficient Kislev full Tevet deficient etc. until year end] simple and intercalated [intercalated first Adar also full per poskim]. When first day Rosh Hashanah Monday or Shabbat — cannot Cheshvan Kislev both regular — sometimes both full or both deficient simple or intercalated. When first day Rosh Hashanah Thursday intercalated year — cannot those two months regular — sometimes both full or deficient. When first day Rosh Hashanah Thursday simple year — cannot deficient year [those two months both deficient — sometimes Cheshvan deficient Kislev full or both full] — seven types simple [2 deficient 2 complete 3 regular 5 regular 5 complete 7 deficient 7 complete] and seven intercalated [2 deficient 2 complete 3 regular 5 deficient 5 complete 7 deficient 7 complete]. Also sign Sunday Shavuot Sunday Chanukah [after] and sign ki ner mitzvah veTorah or — if complete year Tuesday Shavuot Sunday Chanukah [so Magen Avraham]. Sunday Chanukah Purim day — only simple deficient year — end Gra; all rules proven Levush see there.
