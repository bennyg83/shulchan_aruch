5) There. Taanis Esther does not occur on Sunday, Tuesday, or Friday. Taanis Esther occurs on the thirteenth of Adar and Purim on the fourteenth. If Taanis Esther occurred on Sunday, Tuesday, or Friday, Purim would occur on Monday, Wednesday, or Shabbos: Monday follows Sunday, Wednesday follows Tuesday, and Shabbos follows Friday. Since Purim does not occur on Shabbos, Monday, or Wednesday, Taanis Esther consequently does not occur on Sunday, Tuesday, or Friday.
