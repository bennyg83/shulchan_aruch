Lo chachmo ki esa. This is the view of Rambam and Tur in the name of the Gaon; but Rashi and Tosafot wrote "were it not for anger, for He judges," and so in Minhagim, and so they practice in all communities in these lands. I wonder that Rama did not write here this matter, that they do not practice as Shulchan Aruch. Rambam wrote why they divide in these matters: because they are words of rebuke to return the people in repentance.
