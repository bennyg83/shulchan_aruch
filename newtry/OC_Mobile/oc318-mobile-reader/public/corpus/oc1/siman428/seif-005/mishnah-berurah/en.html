(א) They divide its sections as was done, etc. — and the reason: because they are words of rebuke to return the people in repentance — Rambam; and even if there are aliyot they do not divide the parashah more — Magen Avraham.
(ב) Once for the six weekdays — they divided it into six parts for the six days of action.
(ג) Lo chachmo ki esa — some poskim hold the word lecha hints "were it not for anger, for He judges" — and so they practice in our country — Acharonim.
(ד) From the end of the song, etc. — and where they add on Shabbat, they may add after HaZeiV LamedKaf from "Vayavo" and onward.
