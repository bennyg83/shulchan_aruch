(א) (s.k. 3) Complete intercalated, etc. — meaning when Rosh Hashanah on Thursday whether months complete or deficient they read Noach on Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan and Shabbat added; and since year intercalated they read Acharei Mot before Pesach.

(ב) Then they read Noach.

(ג) Meaning if Rosh Hashanah on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, or Shabbat they read Noach since some days of Cheshvan passed; reason Tzav before Pesach in simple year: written therein chatat and earthen vessel that boils must break, and if copper vessel scoured and rinsed — remembrance of hag'alah before Pesach of chametz vessels; in intercalated year when more Shabbatot and cannot read Tzav near Pesach they read Metzora before Pesach — written zav and earthen vessel the zav touches must break and wooden vessel rinsed — some remembrance of hag'alah; or "some say intercalated" — no choice must read Acharei Mot before Pesach.
