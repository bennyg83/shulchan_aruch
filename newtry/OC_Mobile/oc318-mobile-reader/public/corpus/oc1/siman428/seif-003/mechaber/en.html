A sign for fixing the festivals: aleph-tav, bet-shin, gimmel-reish, dalet-kuf, hei-tzadi, vav-peh — meaning: on Sunday of Pesach it is always Tuesday, and a sign: "on matzot and maror they shall eat it" — on Monday its weekday, on Tuesday its weekday, on Wednesday Rosh Hashanah, on Thursday Torah reading which is Simchat Torah, on Friday Yom Kippur, on Shabbat Purim that passed.
