Purim does not occur on Shabbos, Monday, or Wednesday, and Pesach does not occur on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday, etc. Once it is stated that Purim does not occur on Shabbos, Monday, or Wednesday, it follows automatically that Pesach does not occur on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday. Ordinary Purim is on the fourteenth of Adar. If Purim occurred on Friday, Pesach would occur on Monday; if Purim occurred on Monday, Pesach would occur on Wednesday; and if Purim occurred on Wednesday, Pesach would occur on Friday. Since Purim does not occur on Shabbos, Monday, or Wednesday, Pesach consequently does not occur on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday. Shavuos also does not occur on Tuesday, Thursday, or Shabbos, since Shavuos falls on the same weekday as the second day of Pesach, as explained below in se'if 3. Once it is stated that Pesach does not occur on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday, it automatically follows that Shavuos does not occur on Tuesday, Thursday, or Shabbos: Tuesday follows Monday, Thursday follows Wednesday, and Shabbos follows Friday. The reason Pesach does not occur on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday is explained in se'if 3: if it did, Yom Kippur would occur on Friday or Sunday, or Hoshana Rabbah would occur on Shabbos.
