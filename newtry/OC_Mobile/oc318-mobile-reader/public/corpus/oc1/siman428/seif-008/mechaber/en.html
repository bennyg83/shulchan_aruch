From the seventeenth of Tamuz onward, there are three "Haftarot of Rebuke," seven "Haftarot of Consolation," and two "Haftarot of Repentance." The Three of Rebuke are "Divre Yirmiya" (Jeremiah 1:1-2:3), "Shim'u Devar Adonai" (Jeremiah 2:4-28), and "Hazon Yeshayahu" (Isaiah 1:1-27). The Seven of Consolation are "Nahamu" (Isaiah 40:1-26), "Vatomer Tsiyon" (Isaiah 49:14-51:3), "Aniya So'ara" (Isaiah 54:11-55:5), "Anokhi" (Isaiah 51:12-52:12), "Roni Akara" (Isaiah 54:1-10), "Kumi Ori" (Isaiah 60:1-22), and "Sos Asis" (Isaiah 61:10-63:9). [The Two of Repentance are:] On the day of Tsom Gedalia at Minha we read "Dirshu" (Isaiah 55:6-56:8), and on the Shabbat between Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur we always read "Shuva" (Hosea 14:2-10)(Translator's note: often with the addition of Micha 7:18-20, and Ashkenazim add Joel 2:11-27). When Rosh Hashana falls on Monday and Tuesday, which causes there to be a Shabbat between Yom Kippur and Sukkot, we read Ha'azinu and the Maftir is "Vaydaber David" (II Samuel 22:1-51). And some say that when Rosh Hashana falls on Monday and Tuesday, and Vayelekh is between Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur, we read the Haftara of "Dirshu" then, and on the Shabbat between Yom Kippur and Sukkot we read "Shuva." {Rama: Gloss: and the custom is in accordance with the first version; and on other weeks of the year the Haftara parallels the Parasha (Tur). When two Parshiyot are read together, the Haftara is the reading for the second one (Mordekhai in Megilla ch. 3), except for Ahare Mot - Kedoshim, when the Haftara is "Halo Kivne Kushiyim" (Amos 9:7-15), i.e., Haftarat Ahare Mot (Sefer Minhagim)(Translator's note: that is the Ashkenazi custom, but Sephardim read "Ve'ata Ben Adam" (Yehezkel 22:1-16) for Ahare Mot.) On the Shabbat of a wedding, the custom is to read the Haftara of "Sos Asis," and none of the special Haftarot mentioned here is trumped by it, including the Four Parshiyot before Pesah; Rosh Hodesh and Mahar Hodesh; and Hanukka and Shira (Mordekhai in Megilla ch. 3, Tur, and Sefer Minhagim). However, all other Haftarot do get trumped by "Sos Asis." It has already been explained above in Siman 425 that we do not skip from one Prophet to another (and it is also explained there that the Haftara of Rosh Hodesh does not trump the special Haftarot mentioned here).}
