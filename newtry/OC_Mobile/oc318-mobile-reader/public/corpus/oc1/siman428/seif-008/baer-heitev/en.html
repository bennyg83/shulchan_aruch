(א) Shim'u. If he erred and read as haftarah on the first Shabbat the parashah of the day, he reads on the coming Shabbat Divrei Yirmiyahu and also Shim'u because they are adjacent — Tzitz Eliezer, Magen Avraham, siman 425. What is written in chumashim "Vayivrach Yaakov" for parashat Vayishlach is a printing error — it belongs to Vayetzei — Levush.

(ב) Kushiyim. And so wrote Bach and Mateh Moshe and Agudah, not like Levush who wrote to read the judgment — Bach already testified that in all communities they practice as Rama wrote — Magen Avraham. And see responsum of Mahari Weil siman 4 why specifically Chukat and Balak are combined.
