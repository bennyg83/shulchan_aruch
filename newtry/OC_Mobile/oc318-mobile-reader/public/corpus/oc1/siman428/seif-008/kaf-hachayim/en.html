(8) [Seif 2] These days Rosh Chodesh fixed, etc. — order of months: Nissan always full, Iyar deficient, Sivan full, Tammuz deficient, Av full, Elul deficient, Tishrei full, Tevet deficient, Shevat full, Adar deficient; Nissan always not Rosh Chodesh but day 1; Iyar two days; Sivan day 1; Tammuz two days; Av day 1; Elul two days; Tishrei day 1; Cheshvan two days; Shevat day 1; Adar and second Adar two days. Cheshvan and Kislev sometimes both full — complete year; sometimes both deficient — deficient year; sometimes Cheshvan deficient Kislev full — regular; when both full, Rosh Chodesh Kislev and Tevet always two days; when both deficient, Kislev and Tevet start day 1; when one deficient one full, Kislev day 1 Tevet two days — Peri Chadash; words of Tur and Levush see there.
