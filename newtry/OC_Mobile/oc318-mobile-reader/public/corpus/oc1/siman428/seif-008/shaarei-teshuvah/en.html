In Shulchan Aruch seif 8 they read always Shuva. See Sha'ar Ephraim gate 9 seif 28: some add from prophet Yoel, some add Micha "Mi Kel kamocha"; see Avodat HaRav: so in Prague; Dagul Merevavah brought Avodat HaRav — wrote did not know reason for both whys (meaning main reason of addition not to end "ufosh'im yikashlu bam" — one suffices); his view to rule: Vayelech Shabbat Shuva end Micha; Haazinu end Yoel — see there; word in context; all per custom.
