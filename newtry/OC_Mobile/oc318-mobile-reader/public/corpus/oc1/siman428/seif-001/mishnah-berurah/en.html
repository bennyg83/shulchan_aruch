(א) That we do not fix on them, etc. — see the reason in Mishnah Berurah.
(ב) And on the day Chanukah falls, etc. — behold in truth this is not so, for Chanukah can be on Thursday and Shavuot cannot, as explained; nevertheless in truth it is a printing error and it must read "on the day Shavuot falls, Chanukah falls" — meaning the Chanukah after it; but this is only when the year is regular, meaning Cheshvan and Kislev one full and one deficient or both deficient; but when both are full, the first day of Chanukah comes on the day after Shavuot — for example if Shavuot on Sunday, Chanukah on Monday.
