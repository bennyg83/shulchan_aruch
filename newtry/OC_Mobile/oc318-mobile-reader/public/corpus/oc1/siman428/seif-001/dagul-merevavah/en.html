(Mechaber) And because in Hoshea is end of this haftarah "ufosh'im yikashlu bam" and one does not end on that — therefore add verses. Avudraham wrote they add Micha "Mi Kel kamocha" etc.; Tosafot Megillah 31b proves they add Yoel "Tek'u shofar" etc.; therefore customs confused here and in chumashim sometimes one way sometimes another; Eliyahu Rabbah wrote in Prague they added Micha and Yoel — I did not know reason — smell of two whys; but Tosafot Megillah one may say per their view there they read Shuva Shabbat between Yom Kippur and Sukkot — then Yoel verses fit see Tosafot; we do not read Shuva only between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur as Rama gloss here — therefore it appears to rule: when reading Vayelech on Shabbat Shuva end with Micha — Micha and Hoshea prophesied same chapter Pesachim 87a; when reading Haazinu on Shabbat Shuva end after "ufosh'im yikashlu bam" with Yoel — parallel parashah "ya'arof kamatar likchi" "kesh'irim" "ker'vivim ali deshe" and Yoel "veyored lachem geshem morah" — as Tosafot there — not like Levush and not like Avodat HaRav; old chumashim Vayelech end Micha, Haazinu end Yoel; since no reason known customs confused — I ruled: Micha verses parallel Vayelech "vacharah afi" Micha "lo hachzik la'ad apo".
