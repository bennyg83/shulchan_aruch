And not Tuesday, Thursday, Shabbat for Shavuot. It is difficult, for afterward he wrote "not Tuesday for Chanukah" — it implies Chanukah is fixed and not like Shavuot; and how did he write afterward "on the day Chanukah falls, Shavuot falls"? It appears there is a printing error in what he wrote "on the day Chanukah falls, Shavuot falls" — rather it must read "on the day Shavuot falls, Hoshana Rabbah falls" — meaning Hoshana Rabbah after Shavuot.
