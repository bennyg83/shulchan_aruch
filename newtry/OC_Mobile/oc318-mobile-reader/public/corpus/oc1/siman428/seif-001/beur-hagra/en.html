(א) Seif 1 — not Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Rosh Hashanah 20a; Sukkah 43b; and see Tosafot in name of Yerushalmi that thereby all versions are explained.

(ב) Not Shabbat, Monday, Wednesday for Purim. Yerushalmi chapter 1, Megillah end halacha 2 — R' Yosi: it cannot fall on Monday nor Shabbat; can fall on fast day; Rabba: on Sunday fast day, on Shabbat fast day on erev; and this is proof for what Tosafot wrote Pesachim 58b s.v. ke'ilu; see Sukkah 54b; and Megillah mishnah there should read like others and Yerushalmi not like it — so we rule.
