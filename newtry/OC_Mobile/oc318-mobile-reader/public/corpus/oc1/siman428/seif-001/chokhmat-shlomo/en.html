Seif 1 — these days on which festivals not fixed, not Monday, Wednesday, Friday Rosh Hashanah, etc. See Magen Avraham: general principle see Yom Kippur and Shabbat not adjacent, etc. Reason: two sages in Gemara Chullin dispute — one vegetables, one Matya. I was asked by a man on what is said there that between them is Yom Kippur after Shabbat — for vegetables, at night he toils and brings — difficulty: if Yom Kippur after Shabbat, Rosh Hashanah erev — still vegetables concern per Rashi. I answered: Perishah felt in Tosafot novellae and wrote to reconcile; from his words what Rashi on Yom Tov means only for conclusion erev. I say per his way need not distance thus, especially say Rashi cited all for conclusion; in my view Rashi cited even for one sage — only "Shabbat after Yom Tov" is for conclusion; "Yom Tov after Shabbat" even for one sage because Gemara challenges Matya sage why not say vegetables and answers possibly in hot climate restore by hot water — Rashi: soak in hot water restore; a fortiori if already withered hot water restores — all the more if not really withered one day we are not concerned vegetables — certainly soak that day they restore and can rest another day; therefore for one sage unaware hot water restores, Gemara thought already withered two days hot water won't help — but Gemara knew restoration by hot prevents more withering — different from spoiling quickly; now all hold hot water view only Yom Kippur adjacent Shabbat — no hot water, wither two days hot water won't help afterward; here Yom Tov after Shabbat — Shabbat no hot water, vegetables wait more than full day and wither — no hot water remedy after spoiled; but Yom Tov before Shabbat — hot water on Yom Tov prevent withering — properly Yom Kippur after Shabbat for Yom Kippur no concern Shabbat no hot water — still toil at night and bring; for Rosh Hashanah and Sukkot erev possible hot water before withering. Rashi Yom Tov before Shabbat only conclusion; for one sage difference only Yom Tov after Shabbat or Yom Kippur before Shabbat; Gemara concludes Matya sage holds hot water helps even after withered — fits well — examine; this way more correct than Rav Perishah — see and understand.
