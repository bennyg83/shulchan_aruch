(א) These days on which we do not fix, etc. — the general principle: always see Yom Kippur not Sunday nor Shabbat so two holy days forbidden in melacha d'oraisa not be adjacent [Gemara reason Rosh Hashanah 20 — see there], and Hoshana Rabbah not Shabbat lest taking aravah be deferred — thereby know reason of whole seif: if Rosh Hashanah Sunday, Thursday, Shabbat, automatically that year's Yom Kippur Sunday, Tuesday, Friday; on Sunday and Shabbat impossible two adjacent holy days; on Tuesday impossible Hoshana Rabbah Shabbat. When Sunday, Tuesday, Friday Purim, first day Pesach Monday, Wednesday, Friday; when Monday, Wednesday, Friday Pesach, Shavuot Wednesday, Friday, Sunday — automatically Hoshana Rabbah that day [day Shavuot falls, Hoshana Rabbah falls] — impossible on Sunday for aravah, impossible Tuesday for above. Not Tuesday Chanukah — since we hold not Sunday, Thursday, Shabbat Rosh Hashanah etc., Chanukah cannot fall Tuesday simple or intercalated complete or deficient [can expand — see Levush]. Not Sunday, Tuesday, Friday Taanit Esther — else Purim Sunday, Tuesday, Friday; not Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat fasts Tammuz and Tisha BeAv — else Rosh Hashanah Sunday, Thursday, Shabbat — impossible. All this need not calculate in detail — know principle from Tevet onward months until Cheshvan regular: Tevet deficient, Shevat full, Adar deficient [two Adarim first full], Nissan full, Iyar deficient, Sivan full, Tammuz deficient, Av full, Elul deficient, Tishrei full [Cheshvan and Kislev vary sometimes both full, both deficient, or regular Cheshvan deficient Kislev full — can expand]; thereby understand Shulchan Aruch Purim day same as Lag BaOmer — nine weeks compressed.

(ב) These days, etc. — Nissan Sunday, Tuesday, Friday and not other days lest Pesach Monday, Wednesday, Friday; since Nissan known full, disconnects two days to next month — must fix second day of Iyar specifically Tuesday, Thursday, Shabbat; Iyar deficient — twenty-nine days disconnects one day below — must fix Sivan Thursday, Shabbat, Sunday, Tuesday, etc. all this way. Marcheshvan gimmel dalet vav aleph must read Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat — refers to second day Rosh Chodesh; since Tishrei Monday, Wednesday, Shabbat and full, Rosh Chodesh Cheshvan Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Shabbat; Kislev depends on Cheshvan full or deficient and which day fixed — can come six days not Shabbat — else first Chanukah day Tuesday impossible as above — Peri Chadash. Also wrote what they said Tevet can be Monday is intercalated year; Shevat Tuesday intercalated year; Thursday never — simple or intercalated; thereby understand whole seif.
