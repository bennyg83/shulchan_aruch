1) [Se'if 1] These are the days on which they are not fixed, etc. The general principle is that care must always be taken that Yom Kippur not occur on Sunday or Friday, so that two sacred days will not be adjacent, and that Hoshana Rabbah not occur on Shabbos, so that the mitzvah of the aravah will not be displaced. Magen Avraham, beginning of the siman. Regarding the statement that Yom Kippur should not occur on Sunday, etc., a reason for this is given in Rosh Hashanah 20a. See there.
