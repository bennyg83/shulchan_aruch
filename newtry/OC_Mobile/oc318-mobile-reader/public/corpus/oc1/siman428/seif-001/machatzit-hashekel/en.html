(א) (s.k. 1) The general principle, etc. — meaning this is the reason Rosh Hashanah is not fixed on Monday, Wednesday, Friday; from this follows fixing other Rosh Chodesh and festivals.

(ב) (s.k. 2) That Yom Kippur not be on Sunday. And why Rosh Hashanah is not fixed on the 16th — then Yom Kippur would be Sunday; nor on the 16th. And why Rosh Hashanah is not fixed on Thursday — then Yom Kippur would be Shabbat.

(ג) (s.k. 3) Adjacent to each other — they wrote the reason (and so Rosh Hashanah 20a) because of vegetables and Matya — meaning vegetables picked on weekday would lie detached two days and wither.

(ד) (s.k. 4) And Matya — meaning if, Heaven forbid, one died on Sunday of the two holy days, the corpse must remain two days, for even by gentiles burial is forbidden on Shabbat or Yom Kippur — disgrace and concern it will putrefy; and though these reasons are weak to defer Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur, rather these moladot we calculate as transmitted from Chazal is the average course, not the true course; and due to weakness of mind the true course could not be transmitted; Chazal in their wisdom saw through these deferrals that average and true courses meet; deferrals could have been in other months, but since we needed those deferrals for the above reason they fixed them for Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur to gain some benefit for vegetables and Matya — explained in Rambam chapter 7 Hilchot Kiddush HaChodesh and Levush; therefore specifically now we rely on the calculation transmitted; not enough when they sanctified by sight — Rashi's view Pesachim 58b, chapter Tamid nishchat; but Tosafot there s.v. ke'ilu disagree and hold even when sanctifying by sight they were careful two holy days not be adjacent; and one must say per commentator on Rambam chapter 7 Kiddush HaChodesh in name of Rabbeinu Chananel there was tradition among sages not to fix Rosh Hashanah on Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and the reason was not transmitted except to those sages appointed to study intercalation; for us the revealed reason in Gemara is vegetables and Matya — but that is not the main reason. Tosafot Sukkah also brought; also Chazal expounded "which you shall proclaim in their season" — "atem" lacks vav — we read "atem" even unwittingly and even intentionally their fixing is fixing — Rosh Hashanah chapter 2; nevertheless without sufficient reason they would not defer Monday, Wednesday, Friday — therefore one of the above reasons.

(ה) (s.k. 5) And also that Hoshana Rabbah not, etc. — and why Rosh Hashanah is not fixed on Sunday — then Hoshana Rabbah, which is the 21st from Rosh Hashanah, falls on Shabbat.

(ו) (s.k. 6) Lest aravah be deferred. Levush wrote the reason one does not beat hoshanot on Shabbat — d'oraisa prohibition; Sha'ar Ephraim siman 2 wondered — detached produce has no Torah prohibition; Sefer Avraham Zvi added to Rashi the mitzvah was shaking not hard beating — therefore wrote there the reason is Rabba's decree lest one carry; Sefer Avraham Zvi wondered: on erev we conclude Sukkah 43b aravah is not from Torah in Temple only, in provinces only prophetic foundation one day per R' Yochanan and R' Yehoshua ben Levi only custom — yet we teach if the seventh of Sukkot falls Shabbat aravah defers Shabbat in Temple since there it is from Torah; what of lulav — we teach only first day of Sukkot on Shabbat defers since then also in provinces from Torah — unlike other days even in Temple from Torah yet do not defer Shabbat; why aravah in provinces rabbinic yet seventh defers Shabbat? They answer to publicize it is from Torah in Temple since not explicit in verse — by deferring Shabbat they know it is from Torah. They challenge: lulav other days should also defer to publicize; they answer Rabba's decree lest carry; challenge: aravah too should decree for Rabba; answer aravah agents of beit din bring it (see Rashi and Ran) but lulav given to each; if so in provinces too should defer — Rashi: agents bring and beat, no Rabba concern, and to publicize d'oraisa in Temple. They answer we are not expert in fixing the month — Gemara end. Thus aravah has no Rabba decree. Rokeach: reason they would cry and plead for water in prayer on aravah day — impossible on Shabbat. From Rashba's novellae: nevertheless rabbinic prohibition of moving muktzeh (though permitted for mitzvah per Tosafot Shabbat 42b); and the mitzvah itself in provinces only rabbinic — sages found it hard to defer for muktzeh or not defer lest doubt it is not from Torah even in Temple — better defer fixing Rosh Hashanah when on Sunday so aravah not fall Shabbat erev. Sha'ar Ephraim's difficulty from Avraham Zvi that no Torah prohibition in aravah — all the more per Rashi they only shook — why say Sadducees deny aravah defers Shabbat; baraita: seventh of aravah Shabbat, agents brought aravah erev, placed in azarah; Sadducees took aravah and hid under stones — muktzeh, knowing stones not moved Shabbat — mitzvah nullified; baraita: because Sadducees do not admit beating aravah defers Shabbat. If no Torah prohibition why do Sadducees care about rabbinic prohibition and why think it does not defer Shabbat — Sadducees do not keep rabbinic laws. It appears for us who hold no Torah prohibition in detached produce, nevertheless Sadducees err to forbid the forbidden and permit the permitted — may hold psik reisha has Torah prohibition even per Rashi they only shook — for shaking no need to err Torah prohibition; certainly not from prohibition — they wanted to prevent sages fulfilling aravah Shabbat, holding their folly that aravah is no mitzvah; on weekdays they found no excuse; Shabbat stones muktzeh they imagined mitzvah would cease since sages would not move muktzeh stones — baraita reason Sadducees deny aravah defers Shabbat is not necessarily because Shabbat — even if Torah prohibition in beating, Sadducees hold no aravah mitzvah from Torah and all rabbinic mitzvot they do not keep — even on weekday no prohibition yet since for them no mitzvah, why beat; if no prohibition they do not care — also Shabbat why care since they do not beat; what of others — still difficult per Rashi.
