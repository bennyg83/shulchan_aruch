The general principle: always one must see that Yom Kippur not be on Sunday nor on Shabbat so there not be two holy days adjacent, and also that Hoshana Rabbah not be on Shabbat lest the aravah be deferred. And on the day Shavuot falls, Chanukah falls — thus it must read: on the day Shavuot falls, Chanukah after it, if regular or deficient; but when full, it comes the day after Shavuot — for example if Shavuot on Sunday, Chanukah on Monday.
