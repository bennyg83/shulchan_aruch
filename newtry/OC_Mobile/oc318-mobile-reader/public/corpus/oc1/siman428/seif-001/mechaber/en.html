The order of fixing the festivals and reading the parshiyot. Contains 8 seifim. These are the days on which we do not fix
the festivals: Rosh Hashanah' — not on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday; Yom Kippur — not on Sunday, Tuesday, or Friday; Purim — not on Shabbat, Monday, or Wednesday; Pesach — not on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday; Shavuot and Hoshana Rabbah — not on Tuesday, Thursday, or Shabbat; Chanukah — not on Tuesday; Taanit Esther — not on Sunday, Tuesday, or Friday; the 17th of Tammuz and Tisha BeAv — not on Monday, Wednesday, or Shabbat. Always: the day on which Purim falls is the same weekday as Lag BaOmer, and the sign is PLaG; and on the day Shavuot falls, so falls Chanukah.
