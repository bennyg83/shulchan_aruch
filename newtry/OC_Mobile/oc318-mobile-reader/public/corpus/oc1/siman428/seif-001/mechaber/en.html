These are the days on which the holidays may not fall: Rosh Hashanah not on Sunday, Wednesday or Friday; Yom
Kippur - not on' Sunday, Tuesday, or Friday; Purim - not on Shabbat, Monday or Wednesday; Pesach - not on Monday, Wednesday or Friday; Atzeret(Shavuot) and Hoshanah Rabbah - not on Tuesday, Thursday or Shabbat; Chanukah - not on Tuesday; Taanit Esther - not on Sunday, Tuesday or Friday; 17th Tammuz and Tisha b'Av - not on Monday, Wednesday and Shabbat; The day on which Purim is always the day of the week as Lag b'Omer, and the sign is P'LaG; and the day on which Chanukah falls will also fall Atzeret(Shavuot).
