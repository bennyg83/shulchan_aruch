Chanukah. It must read "and on the day Shavuot falls, Chanukah falls" — meaning on the day Shavuot falls, Chanukah after it falls, if they are regular or deficient; but when they are full, Chanukah comes on the day after Shavuot — for example if Shavuot is on Sunday, Chanukah comes on Monday. Magen Avraham: and Taz emends that it must read "on the day Shavuot falls, Hoshana Rabbah falls" — meaning Hoshana Rabbah after Shavuot erev.
