7) There. Chanukah occurs on the same weekday as Shavuos. This is the correct text. Chanukah following Shavuos occurs on the same weekday when the intervening months are in their regular or deficient arrangement. When they are full, Chanukah occurs on the day after the weekday of Shavuos. For example, if Shavuos occurs on Sunday, Chanukah occurs on Monday. Magen Avraham, subsection 1. This implies that when the months are regular or deficient, Chanukah occurs on the same weekday as Shavuos and therefore does not occur on Tuesday, Thursday, or Shabbos. Although the Shulchan Aruch states only that Chanukah does not occur on Tuesday, it can occur on Thursday or Shabbos when the months are full. The preceding Shavuos would then have occurred on Wednesday or Friday, and Chanukah would occur on Thursday or Shabbos. Eshel Avraham, subsection 1.
