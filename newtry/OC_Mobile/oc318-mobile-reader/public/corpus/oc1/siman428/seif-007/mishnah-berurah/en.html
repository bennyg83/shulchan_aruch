(א) Eight, etc. — meaning from "Vayamot sham Moshe."

(ב) One does not interrupt in them — to divide among two readers; the reason: there is a change from other Torah scrolls — Yehoshua wrote them; and even per those who say Moshe wrote them in tears, since there is change that they were written in tears, they are not divided like other Torah. He wrote in Tzror HaMor that the journeys in Ve'eleh mas'ei one does not interrupt — against the name forty-two — Magen Avraham.
