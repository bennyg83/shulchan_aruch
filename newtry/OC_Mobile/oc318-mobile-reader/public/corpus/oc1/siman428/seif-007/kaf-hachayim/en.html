On day Shavuot falls Chanukah as must read — meaning day Shavuot falls, Chanukah after if regular or deficient; when full, comes day after Shavuot — e.g. Shavuot Sunday, Chanukah Monday — Magen Avraham s.k. 1; implies when regular or deficient Chanukah like Shavuot and not Tuesday, Thursday, Shabbat; what Shulchan Aruch wrote not Tuesday Chanukah — comes when full; Chanukah after is Shavuot 4,6 and then Chanukah Tuesday, Thursday, Shabbat — impossible Sunday, Thursday.
