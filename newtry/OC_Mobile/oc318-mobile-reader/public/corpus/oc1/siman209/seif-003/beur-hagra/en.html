Seif 3, all, etc. That all blessings are d'rabbanan except Birkat HaMazon, as written chapter 1 law 1; and further we learned, etc., and there doubt, etc., and Tosafot 35a s.v. ela, etc., and s.v. lefanav, etc., except there 40 that me'ein shalosh is d'oraisa, and so there 44a, and Rashba; but Rambam's view is that all this is asmakhta, since initially he had to strain to learn from "kama" and "kerem," and likewise the baraita that derives from "kodesh chagigot" — from which we learn there is no d'oraisa in the seven species.
