1) [Seif 1] One took a cup of beer or water, etc. This is from the Rambam in chapter 8 of Hilchos Berachos, where only a cup of beer is mentioned; Maran added, 'or water.' Raavad there, however, disagrees with this principal law and writes that we consider only what one actually articulated. Bach similarly writes that according to Rashi, Rif, Rosh, Rashba, and most Geonim, he did not fulfill his obligation and must recite the blessing again. Taz, paragraph 1, Magen Avraham, paragraph 1, Shulchan Gavoah, paragraph 1, and Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2, rule likewise. Therefore, since there is a dispute and in cases of doubt concerning blessings we rule leniently, if he remembered within the time required for a short greeting after saying Borei Pri HaGafen, he should also say Shehakol Nihyah Bidvaro. He thereby fulfills his obligation according to most authorities, as stated in seif 2. If he did not remember within that time, he should recite the entire proper blessing in his heart.
