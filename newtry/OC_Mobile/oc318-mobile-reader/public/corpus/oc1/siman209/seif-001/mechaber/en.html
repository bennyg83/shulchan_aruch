The laws of error and doubt in the blessing over wine. It contains 3 seifim: If one took a cup of beer or water and began, 'Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe,' intending to conclude SheHakol, but erred and concluded Borei Pri HaGafen, we do not require him to repeat the blessing, because when he mentioned the Divine Name and kingship, which are the essence of the blessing, he intended the blessing appropriate to that species. Some say that if he took a cup of beer or water thinking it was wine, began, 'Blessed are You, Hashem our God, King of the universe,' intending to say Borei Pri HaGafen, then remembered that it was beer or water and concluded SheHakol, he has fulfilled his obligation. {Rama: All the more so, if he had wine in his hand and thought it was water, began intending to say SheHakol, then remembered and recited Borei Pri HaGafen, he has fulfilled his obligation, since even had he concluded SheHakol he would have fulfilled his obligation. [Tur]}
