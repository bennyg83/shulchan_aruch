If one took a cup of beer or water and blessed "Baruch atah Hashem Elokeinu Melech ha'olam, Borei Pri Hagafen," and within toch kedei dibbur he remembered that he had erred and said "Shehakol nihyeh bidvaro" — so that his utterance was "Baruch atah Hashem Elokeinu Melech ha'olam, Borei Pri Hagafen, Shehakol nihyeh bidvaro" — he has fulfilled his obligation. {Rama: And if others were also drinking and there was wine before them, and his intention was also upon wine — for he thought that there was wine in his cup and he blessed "Borei Pri Hagafen," and afterward it was found that in his cup there was water or beer — when he subsequently drinks wine he need not bless again, and he is covered by the blessing he made over his cup, even though it was in error; for his intention was to drink other wine as well, and he also exempted the others who were drinking there. Therefore his blessing is a valid blessing. (Responsa Maharil, siman 92; Beis Yosef, end of siman 206.)}
