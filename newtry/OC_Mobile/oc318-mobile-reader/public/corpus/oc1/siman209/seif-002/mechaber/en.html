He took a cup of alcohol or water and blessed, “Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, Who created the fruit of the vine” (Bore Pri Hagafen), and during the blessing he remembered that he had made a mistake and he said שהכל. Since his wording was “Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, Who created the fruit of the vine, שהכל” he fulfilled his obligation.Rema : if others were also drinking and they had wine before them, and his intention was also on wine, thinking that in his cup there was wine, and he blessed Bore Pri Hagafen, but afterward it turned out that in his cup there was water or alcohol, and then afterward he drank wine, there is no need to bless again, and he fulfills the blessing he made over his cup, even though it was a mistake, for his intention was to drink other wine also that others were drinking there. Therefore his blessing is considered valid. (Responsa Maharil, section 92; Be’er Yosef, section 216).
