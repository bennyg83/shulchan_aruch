1. Laws of Weaving on Chol Hamoed, and in it is 5 paragraphs. It is permitted to make a fish trap from willow
branches that is an amateur act but not weaving from threads (or sinews) as it is a professional act. Note (Rema): it is permitted to fix wicks for candles on Chol Hamoed because it is an amateur act (Nimukei Yosef Chapter Elu Meglachin (3rd ch.), Reb Yeruchom 54 Chelek 5, and Bais Yosef). ):
