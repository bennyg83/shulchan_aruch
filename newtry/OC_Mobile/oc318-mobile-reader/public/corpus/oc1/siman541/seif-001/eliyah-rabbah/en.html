[1] Fish nets, etc. Meaning for the needs of the festival (Rabbeinu Yerucham); and see siman 540, se'if 5, and see s.k. 11. The Rama wrote that it is permitted to prepare the wicks for candles on the festival; and that the Levush omitted this, it appears to me is because Beis Yosef wrote that there is a dispute about this, except that he concluded that they practiced to permit; and the Levush saw that this is not a widespread custom among us. And further, in Maharil there is one who preached that they should warn to make them before the festival, and likewise in the Agudah, chapter "These may shave." However, one may distinguish: that our making [them] from cotton is not [the same as] growing wicks. Still, it implies from Maharil and the Acharonim and Beis Yosef that there is no distinction.
