(א) Fish traps, etc. This means for the purpose of the festival (Rabbeinu Yerucham). See siman 544 seif 5 and Magen Avraham sk 11. Rama wrote it is permitted to fix wicks for lamps on the festival; the Levush omitted this — it appears to me because Beit Yosef wrote there is a dispute, but in practice they were lenient. See the Levush that this is not our common custom; and Maharil also ruled one should be careful to make them before the festival, as in Agudah chapter Elu Megalchin. However, our flax is not vine growth for wicks — nevertheless Maharil, later authorities, and Beit Yosef imply there is no distinction.
