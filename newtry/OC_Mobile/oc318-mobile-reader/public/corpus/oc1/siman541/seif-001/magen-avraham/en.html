Work of an amateur. And specifically when it is for the needs of the festival, as is written in siman 540, se'if 5. And this requires further study, for even a craftsman's work is permitted, since it is preparatory work for food needs, like an oven. And one can say that [items] are called preparatory only [when they are] something that prepares [food] for eating, such as an oven and a knife; but nets are not called preparatory.
