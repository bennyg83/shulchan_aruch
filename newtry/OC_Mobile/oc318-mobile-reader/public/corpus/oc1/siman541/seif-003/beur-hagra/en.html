Se'if 3: It is permitted to press, etc. And it is permitted to smooth, etc. And these are two explanations that Tosafot wrote, s.v. "kesachusei"; and see Beis Yosef in the name of the Semag; and as is written in chapter Tolin, "mechaschisu," etc.; and in Niddah folio 62, one must rub, etc.; and in Zevachim 94b, all washing, etc.; and see Rashi there; and Tosafot brought [this] under that lemma.
