(6) It is permitted to press linen garments, etc. See siman 302, se'if 5: there, regarding Shabbos, it is forbidden to do so to linen garments, and even to a shirt, if one intends thereby to whiten it, it is forbidden; and here it is permitted even when one intends to whiten it; and specifically when one needs it on the festival.
(7) With the hands, etc. According to what he wrote later in the gloss, this "with the hands" is not precise; for likewise it is permitted to roll them by means of a utensil, as is customary.
(8) Creases: that is, to pleat [them], which are called kraenzlin in La'az; and specifically when the creases do not spread out when one wears them, for since the creases are so strong, it is craftsman's work and forbidden even for the needs of the festival. And accordingly it is forbidden to pleat the collar that is called kollner with the wood utensils designated for that, for thereby it remains creased even when one wears it.
(9) With the glass: that is, with a round stone of glass; and it implies that this is considered only amateur's work. And in Chayei Adam he copied that it is permitted to smooth them with a utensil in their usual way; it implies that he holds that glass is not precise, and the same applies to other utensils that one normally uses to smooth; and accordingly it is also permitted to smooth with a smooth iron utensil that tailors customarily use to smooth, for it is all amateur's work and permitted for the needs of the festival.
