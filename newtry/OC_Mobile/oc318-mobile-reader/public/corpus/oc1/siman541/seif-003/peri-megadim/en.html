And what he wrote in the name of the Kol Bo: Eliyah Rabbah raised two difficulties. First: it implies from the Taz specifically that the permitted crafts, namely a craftsman [working] with a shinui, are permitted for others free of charge; but an amateur in his usual way is forbidden for others free of charge. And whence [do we know] that an amateur in his usual way is also called among the permitted crafts? And further: if [the case] is a matter of loss, as he wrote in letter 1, then why specify the amateur? Even craftsman's work is permitted, for it does not require a shinui, as is explained. And Maamar Mordechai answered this, and Eliyah Rabbah; see there. And see siman 542; and God willing there it will be explained further.
