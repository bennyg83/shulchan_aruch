It is permitted to press. For the needs of the festival; see siman 312, se'if 5.
Creases. Meaning, to pleat [them], which are called kraenzlin in La'az (Agudah). So Rashi explained. And Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote: if they do not spread out when one wears them, it is forbidden to make them. And if so, it is forbidden to pleat the collar that is called kollner with the wood utensils designated for that; see siman 519.
