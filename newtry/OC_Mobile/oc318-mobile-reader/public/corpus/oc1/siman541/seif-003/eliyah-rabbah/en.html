(ג) [Levush] Spreading [ropes], etc. This means rolling them (Magen Avraham in the name of Beit Yosef and Bach). That is, anything done with ropes before the festival is permitted even if they were not yet on the bed; the prohibition is only growing the ropes. On this one must examine the Levush, for his words imply forbidding even what was done before the festival since it was not yet on the bed. I again looked in Kitzur Piskei HaRosh and Sefer Tzida — they too ruled like the Levush. From this I learned the Tur did not write permission but Rashi's explanation; to him it is plausible it is forbidden. Possibly Beit Yosef and Bach agree, for they did not write explicitly but for Rashi. Shulchan Arukh may also be explained thus. Also R' Yossi whom Rabbeinu Yerucham cited — even sargining is forbidden, as ruled in Agudah. If so, why were they so lenient?
