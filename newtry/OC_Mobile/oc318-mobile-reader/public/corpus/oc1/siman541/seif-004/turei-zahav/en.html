It is forbidden to repair his torn garments. In the Tur it is written: and we do not say that it is a matter of loss because they would deteriorate if one does not repair them; for [something] is called a matter of loss only when the main thing is ruined, like one who overturned his olives, etc. But for a slight additional ruin it is not called a matter of loss. End of his words. From this it is implied that if they were torn and badly ruined, it is permitted to repair them, for it is a matter of loss; and this is a great loss, like the door-pivot in siman 540.
And likewise it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to repair them. The same applies to everything that they forbade on Chol HaMoed for a Jew, as is written in siman 543: whatever is forbidden to do, it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to do.
