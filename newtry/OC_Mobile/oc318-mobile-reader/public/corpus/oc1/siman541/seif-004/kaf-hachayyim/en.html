4) Regarding the decorative candles known as shtab-likht, which are shaped with craftsmen's tools and require great exertion, it appears that even according to Beis Yosef's permissive authorities regarding twisting wicks, because these entail great exertion it is proper to make them before the festival. It is possible that the law requires them to be made before the festival. Eshel Avraham, subsection 2.
