5) [Se'if 2] Beds may be laced. This means weaving ropes across the bed lengthwise and widthwise, provided this is needed for the festival. Certainly, if the ropes were woven into the bed many days earlier and became loose, they may be tightened. Beis Yosef in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham. If this is not needed for the festival, however, even tightening is forbidden. Even when needed for the festival, permission applies specifically when one did not deliberately schedule the work for the festival. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 4.
