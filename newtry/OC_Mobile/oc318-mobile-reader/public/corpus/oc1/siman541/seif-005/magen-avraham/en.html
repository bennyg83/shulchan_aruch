Who needs a garment. A baby born on Yom Tov: it is forbidden to make new clothes for him on the festival in order to circumcise him; for there is no [heter] on account of honor of the father, since one can wrap him in silk cloths (Rivash, siman 226).
Craftsman. Anyone who knows how to draw out a full needle [of stitches] at once, or who knows how to set the hem at the edge of his shirt, is called a craftsman (Rosh and Tur; see there).
