(13) One who needs a garment: a baby born on Yom Tov: it is forbidden to make new clothes for him on the festival in order to circumcise him; and it is not called for the needs of the festival for the honor of the father so that he may carry him to the bris, for one can wrap him in a piece of silk cloths. In Chayei Adam, klal 109, he was uncertain: one who needs to sew for the needs of the festival and has no threads with which to sew, whether it is permitted to spin threads for this; see there.
(14) An amateur and he is not swift: it is one sign, for an ordinary craftsman is swift [Gra]. And anyone who knows how to draw out a full needle [of stitches] at once, or who knows how to set a hem at the edge of his shirt, is no longer in the category of amateur but a craftsman, and his law is as below.
(15) And every person should be stringent upon himself, etc.: for most people can draw out a full needle [of stitches] at once, and they are in the category of craftsman.
(16) A recognizable shinui: that is, that it be recognizable in the sewing itself and not in some other respect. And know that whether for an amateur who does it in his usual way or for a craftsman who does amateur's work, it is permitted only when he does it for himself or for others free of charge; but when he does it for others for pay, it is forbidden even though it is for the needs of the festival, unless he has nothing to eat, as below in siman 542; and see Biur Halacha.
