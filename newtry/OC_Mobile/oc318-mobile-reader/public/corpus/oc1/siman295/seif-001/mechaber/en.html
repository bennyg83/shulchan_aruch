The Havdalah that the chazzan makes. And in it one seif:
And they say V'yehi noam and the order of Kedushah at length in order to delay the order of Kedushah — for then the wicked return to Gehinnom (Tur); and at a time when they do not say V'yehi noam, such as when Yom Tov falls during the week, they do not say the order of Kedushah but they say V'yiten lecha (Kol Bo).} The shaliach tzibbur makes Havdalah in order to discharge those who have no wine: {Rama: And the custom is to say and mention Eliyahu haNavi on Motza'ei Shabbat, to pray that he come and announce the redemption (Tur).) :
