The verse Vihi Noam should be recited while standing. Some places do not recite Vihi Noam in a mourner's home, but begin with Yoshev B'Seiser. See siman 296, seif 7. The verse ‘With length of days’ is repeated, because the Divine Name that emerges from it is thereby completed. See Tur, Minhagim. See Matteh Moshe, who discusses this at length and writes that the word bishu'asi contains two letters yud; when the verse is repeated there are four letters yud, corresponding to Yud-Hei-Vav-Hei when its letters are filled with yuds. I later found in Tashbetz that when this passage is repeated, its number of words equals the numerical value of the word kohanim, namely 130, because the Chashmonaean kohanim recited it in battle and were victorious. So also is it written in the siddur of Maharar Hirz. This is the expanded form of Yud-Hei-Vav-Hei when filled with yuds, as Shelah wrote; and the recitation of the psalm itself is counted as one. This resolves Maharar Hirz's difficulty with the Mordechai. Eliyahu HaNavi on Motzei Shabbos. For Eliyahu does not come on erev Shabbos. The Gemara also raises the question whether the prohibition of techumin applies above ten handbreadths; if the halacha is that techumin do apply, he could not come on Shabbos. We therefore say this on Motzei Shabbos. Levush. This is unlike Olas Shabbos, who was not precise. See siman 600, where we rule stringently regarding a distance of twelve mil. See there.
