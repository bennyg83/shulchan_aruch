Levush: Because it is, etc. The Mishkan was erected on that day, the eighth day. Mateh Moshe. The verse Vihi Noam should be recited while standing. The writings. In a mourner’s house they begin with Yoshev BeSeiser. Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah. Responsa Binyamin Ze’ev, siman 219, writes that in “Establish the work of our hands,” we pray that our handiwork proceed uprightly and that we not come to sin, so that we will not be judged in Gehinnom. The practice is to repeat, etc. The same applies when reciting this psalm on Shabbos or Yom Tov, in bedtime Shema, or when returning from the cemetery. Piskei Tosafos. See Yoreh Deah, siman 376, seif 4, that the entire psalm should be repeated. Nevertheless, one burdened by his business or studies may rely upon Piskei Tosafos, which writes that reciting the psalm three times is sufficient. After Tefillas HaDerech, one should recite the entire psalm seven times.
