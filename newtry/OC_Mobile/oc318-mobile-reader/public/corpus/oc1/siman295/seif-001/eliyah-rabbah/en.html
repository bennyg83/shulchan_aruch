[1] [Levush] Because it is, etc. The Mishkan was erected on that eighth day (Mateh Moshe). The verse Vihi Noam should be recited while standing (Kabbalistic writings). In a mourner's house one begins with "He who dwells in the shelter" (Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah). Responsa Binyamin Ze'ev, siman 219, explains "Establish the work of our hands": we pray that our deeds proceed uprightly and that we not come to sin, so that we not be judged in Gehinnom. The custom is to repeat, etc. The same applies when this chapter is recited on Shabbos and Yom Tov, in Shema before sleep, or upon returning from the cemetery (Piskei Tosafot). See Yoreh Deah siman 376, seif 4, that the entire chapter should be repeated. If one is occupied with business or Torah study, Piskei Tosafot writes that reciting the chapter three times is sufficient. After Tefillat HaDerech one should recite the entire chapter seven times.
