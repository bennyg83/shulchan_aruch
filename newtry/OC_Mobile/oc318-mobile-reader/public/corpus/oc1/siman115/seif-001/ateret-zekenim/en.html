There is a hint regarding the matter of binah: the acronym — in the heavens (bashamayim), wine (yayin), candle (ner), Havdalah; and the laws of Havdalah will be explained, God willing, in siman 297. Fifth: Hashivenu, Selach Lanu. And that which they enacted to say "Our Father" in the blessing Hashivenu and in the blessing Selach Lanu, unlike the other blessings — because the father is obligated to teach his son Torah; and in Selach Lanu — because we mention the father's mercy, as it is written, "Like a father's mercy on children," and He will forgive us (Mahari Mintz):
