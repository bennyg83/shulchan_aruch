1) [Seif 1] Because the superiority of man over the animal is understanding (binah), etc. And we recite Havdalah within it [the berachah of 'chonein hada'as'] at Motzei Shabbos and Motzei Yom Tov, because it is wisdom: for the fact that a person distinguishes between one thing and another is wisdom for him; therefore they established it within the berachah concerning wisdom. Tur. And there is an allusion to the matter: 'binah' is an acronym for Besamim, Yayin, Ner, Havdalah [spices, wine, candle, Havdalah]. Ateres Zekeinim, ot 1.
