1) [Seif 1] Because it contains only two verses. Another reason is stated in Taanis 27b: The Holy One, blessed is He, told Avraham, 'I instituted the order of the offerings for them. When they read it before Me, I regard it as though they offered them before Me, and I forgive all their sins.' The Shabbos Mussaf offering, however, does not atone, because all its offerings are burnt offerings. Tur. Tosafos gives another reason: on every Shabbos the haftarah must relate to the subject of the day. If we read the Shabbos offering, one would have to read a haftarah concerning the final passage, namely Shabbos, and all the haftaros would then concern the same subject. Beis Yosef.
