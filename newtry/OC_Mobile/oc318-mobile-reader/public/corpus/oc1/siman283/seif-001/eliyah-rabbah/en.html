(1) Because it has only two verses, etc. And furthermore, Shabbat occurs frequently and its offering does not require mention, which is not the case with Yom Tov which comes once a year (Tosefta). And Ran in the chapter Bnei HaIr wrote that on Yom Tov they interrupt and read what is relevant to the day; they also read the offerings.
