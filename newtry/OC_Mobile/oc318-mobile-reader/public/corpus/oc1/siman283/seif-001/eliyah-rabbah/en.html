Because there is no, etc. Another reason is that Shabbos occurs regularly, so its korban does not require special mention, unlike Yom Tov which comes only from year to year, as Tanya writes. Ran in chapter Bnei HaIr says that on Yom Tov we interrupt the regular cycle and read about the day's theme, so we also read the korbanot. On Shabbos we continue the regular Torah sequence rather than reading the day's theme, so we do not separately read its korban.
