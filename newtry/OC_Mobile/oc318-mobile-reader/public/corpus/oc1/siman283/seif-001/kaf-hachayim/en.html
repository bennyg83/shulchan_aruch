(1) [seif 1] Because it has only two verses. And another reason as stated in tractate Taanit (27b): Heaven said to Abraham, "I established for them an order of offerings; when they read them before Me on high, I consider it as if they offered them before Me and I forgive all their sins." And in the Shabbat Mussaf there is no atoning offering, for they are all olot — Tur. And furthermore Tosafos wrote another reason: because one must read the haftarah from the Prophets on every Shabbat relevant to the day, and if we do not read the Shabbat portion, one would need to read the haftarah from what follows it, and that would be about Shabbat matters, and thus all the haftarahs would be on one topic.
