**From the mouth** (Taz) — according to my opinion I follow his method in sign 4 at its end: that for the author, even if not obligated, nevertheless there is in any case a prohibition even when he knows; and Mishna Berurah adopted the stringency of both. And if so, what did Mishna Berurah write here "and even," etc. — when he knows: when he does not know it does not help what the pipe answers regarding amen ye'utoma; and when he knows, the prohibition alone helps — the reasoning of Elya Rabbah in the name of Rabbeinu Hai brought in Beit Yosef: since the congregation answers, he answers with them, for they heard the berachah; and similarly the author; and Mishna Berurah added that even if he did not hear at all from the berachah, etc. (and as Maggid Mishnah wrote in Beit Yosef, Elya Rabbah — not as Magen Avraham sign 17 wrote, and there it will be explained, with God's help). And if so, the Taz challenged him: that even after reduction, all who are known to have heard the berachah answer with them; and such we marked Elya Rabbah — there is no prohibition because of the reason of amen ye'utoma in such a case. However, one could say they did not touch this because of orphan amen and its branches, but only that one must answer amen immediately after the end of the berachah, as Mishna Berurah wrote in the gloss to seif 8 in the name of Radak and the author, and all agree to this. And now we inform from Rabbenu Yitzchak of blessed memory in his rulings siman 109 (Tosafos 119): all that finished from the mouth of the majority — then amen from those who extend is not from the berachah at all, and it is as if it already finished — and on what would he answer amen? And Eliyah Rabbah sign 19. And see Levush seif 5 who explains thus; and he also follows his method that he holds when he knows it is not permitted in chazarat hatfilah that is to fulfill many for the eighteen — Mishna Berurah permits in Torah reading and one who enjoys, as I wrote above; but Magen Avraham in sign 17 has another way in this, as written above nearby.
