They shall answer amen after every blessing and blessing. And one who answers amen after every blessing is as if he prayed twice; and they said: One who prayed and did not direct his intention and returned and prayed — it is assured to him that his prayer is heard (Beit Yosef in the name of Orach Chayyim). And one must be more careful with amen at "ha-El ha-kadosh" and "Shomea tefillah".

That he direct in his heart "it is true, the blessing," etc. Explanation: this intention he should direct in all eighteen blessings; but in the middle ones he should direct in amen two things: (1) the above intention, and additionally (2) he should direct to say "yehi ratzon" — that so may it be fulfilled before us and fulfill our requests that we asked of Him, all the needs that the shaliach tzibbur asked — here we hold that request and pray before the Blessed One that we spoke the words of the petitions to Him (the kabbalist Rabbi Hirz in the siddur). And one who answers amen should not raise his voice more than the one who blesses (beginning of chapter 3 of Berachot).
