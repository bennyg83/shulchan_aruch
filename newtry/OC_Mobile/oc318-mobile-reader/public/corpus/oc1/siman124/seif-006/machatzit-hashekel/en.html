(6) After, etc. — that he argues, etc. — for in seif 9 he wrote that one needs nine answerers, and here one suffices; and also that one is not only advice, as written "and it is good that there be one," etc.; and in Shenei Luchot HaBrit he requires nine, for otherwise his blessings are likely to be in vain and he prays for himself. Meaning: granted in seif 9 the chazzan already prayed in a whisper and does not pray in a loud voice except because of the congregation — therefore if there are no amen answerers, close to blessing in vain; not so here, the chazzan has not yet prayed in a whisper — therefore he must pray for his own need; what blessing in vain is there if there are no answerers?
