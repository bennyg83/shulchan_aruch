There — the Yerushalmi that said: one is obligated to hear the blessing or to know. Explanation: what the Yerushalmi said "one is obligated to bless" — the intent is not on the blessing itself that the chazzan discharges him, but rather that he is obligated to hear it in order to answer amen; and you might say how is it possible to interpret thus — for if so it should also mention that knowledge suffices even if one does not hear; therefore Taz wrote that knowledge is also included in the statement "to hear the blessing"; and on this he brought proof from the words of the Kesef Tzedek, who wrote: they are obligated to hear — although in truth one who knows suffices, although it is included in it.
