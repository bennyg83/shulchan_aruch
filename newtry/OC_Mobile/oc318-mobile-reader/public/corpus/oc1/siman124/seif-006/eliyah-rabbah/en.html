(ו) "The shaliach tzibur should not wait," etc. — and nowadays they wait for the rabbi of the city; and the reason seems so the congregation will not pray so quickly when they say anyway we must wait. And greater than this Magen Avraham wrote: even if there is no rabbi in the city, one should wait for the one praying word for word. I also saw in Knesset HaGedolah siman 90 on the law in the seif after this that one should not wait if there is a minyan there — this is his wording: and the custom now is to wait for the sage of the congregation and all the great ones of the congregation, and there is no burden on the congregation since they waive their honor — so far. And nevertheless it seems the rabbi should not prolong so much, as we find with R. Akiva who was brief; and all the more in a place waiting for the sage who has not yet come, he should hurry himself and be among the first ten. Sefer Chasidim siman 487 wrote: all he occupies himself with and can do so they will not mock him — that if he sits with eyes closed, etc., as a sage said to his disciples when one prays before the ark: if he shortens the prayer and cannot shorten, I make myself as when bowing in Modim, and I have not yet reached "Hear our voice"; and when he begins to repeat the prayer I go backward as if I finished my prayer so they will not mock me — and I have not yet finished until they already said Kedushah; and the Holy One knows I did not go backward except so they will not mock me, and I could not intend.
