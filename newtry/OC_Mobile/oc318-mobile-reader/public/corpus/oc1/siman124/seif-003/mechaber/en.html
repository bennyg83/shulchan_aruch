A congregation that prayed and all of them are expert in prayer — nevertheless, the prayer leader descends and repeats the prayer in order to fulfill the enactment of the Sages.And if there are individuals in the congregation who prolong their prayer, the prayer leader should not wait for them, even if they are the prominent people of the city; and so too, if there was a minyan in the synagogue, they should not wait for a prominent or great person who has not yet arrived [Binyamin Ze'ev siman 168].]:
