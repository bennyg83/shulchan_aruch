(ג) "In a loud voice," etc. — and there is no concern of small faith here, since they do so under pressure; and Magen Avraham wondered that in the Gemara at the end of Rosh Hashanah it implies even without pressure it is permitted, for we say: According to your words, why does the congregation pray? He told him: In order to arrange the shaliach tzibur's prayer — which implies that if not for arranging, the shaliach tzibur would be permitted to pray immediately aloud — so far. And if so, it is not correct to challenge the congregation, for the congregation does not cry out but listens to the shaliach tzibur; and the chazzan possibly truly repeats and prays quietly since it is not a time of pressure. Also what he challenged on what we hold in siman 100, that one need arrange only on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur — therefore according to Rabban Gamliel why does the congregation pray all year? — so far his wording. In my humble opinion it is not difficult at all: for according to Rabban Gamliel one truly must arrange all year, but we hold like the Rabbis that a proficient person does not discharge; if so we say one need not arrange them, and that the congregation prays is because he does not discharge the proficient. Further one may say: according to him all year it is not difficult, for it is forbidden to pray aloud; and the Gemara that says "in order to arrange," etc., is because of Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur when it is permitted to pray aloud — and with this his first difficulty is also resolved.
