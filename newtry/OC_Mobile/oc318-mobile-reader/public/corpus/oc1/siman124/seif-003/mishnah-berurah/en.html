(14) If there was a minyan, etc. — meaning: even if by this the time of Kriat Shema and prayer will not pass; for if not, even an individual must pray so the time does not pass.

(15) One should not wait, etc. — for since there are ten, the Shekhinah is there — upon whom should they wait further? And nowadays the custom has spread to wait for the ab bet din; the reason seems to be because today in Jewish cities the custom is to establish together with the ab bet din a time for study after prayer; and if when a minyan gathers they pray immediately, each will go afterward on his way and nullification of Torah will result from this. And establishing study after prayer is a great matter, as stated in Tur and Shulchan Aruch below siman 155 — see there; nevertheless they should not delay the time of Kriat Shema and prayer on account of this. Eliyah Rabbah wrote: the rabbi should advance himself to come to beit ha-knesset earlier so they will not wait for him.
