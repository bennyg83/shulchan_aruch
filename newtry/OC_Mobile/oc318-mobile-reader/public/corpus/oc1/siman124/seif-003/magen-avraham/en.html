The prayer leader should not wait. And now they have practiced to wait for the Av Beit Din, and it seems to me the reason is that most people pray at a brisk pace, and if individuals pray word for word they will not be able to say Kedushah with the congregation; therefore they wait. And therefore it seems to me that even if there is no Av Beit Din in the city, one should wait for one who prays word for word; but when he prolongs, one should not wait for him, as written regarding R. Akiva — when he would pray with the congregation he would shorten and ascend. And it is written in Shulchan Aruch siman 584 that if one must prolong and fears they will mock him, he may go to his place behind at the time the chazan begins, even though he has not yet finished his prayer, and return to his place and finish.
