For them. The present practice is to wait for the head of the beis din. If there is no head of the beis din in the city, one should wait for a person who prays word by word. If he prolongs his prayer, however, one need not wait for him. Magen Avraham. Sefer Chassidim 784 writes that if a person must prolong his prayer and fears that others will mock him, he may step backward when the chazzan begins, even though he has not yet finished his Amidah; he then returns to his place and completes it.
