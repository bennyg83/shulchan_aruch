On them. And now they practice to wait for the av beit din. And if there is no av beit din in the city, one should wait for the one praying word for word. But when he prolongs, one need not wait for him. Magen Avraham. It is written in Shaarei Chayyim siman 484 that if one needs to prolong and fears they will mock him, he may go after him at the time the cantor begins even though he has not yet finished his prayer, and return to his place and finish.
