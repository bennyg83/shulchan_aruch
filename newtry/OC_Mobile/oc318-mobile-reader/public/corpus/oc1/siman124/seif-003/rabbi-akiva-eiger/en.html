Magen Avraham seif yud-gimel. And Bach wrote to be stringent. I am in doubt whether this is specifically for those fixed in one synagogue for whom the repetition of the prayer returns on their account — it is as though they are obligated in those blessings. But if one who already prayed and heard the repetition of the prayer comes to a synagogue and hears again the repetition of the prayer, it is possible that regarding him it is considered a different blessing — and so it seems — and the matter requires resolution for practical halacha.
