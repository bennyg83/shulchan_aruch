(ס"ק ג) [א] In a loud voice, and there is no concern here of little faith if one raises his voice, etc. — as should be read. And meaning: here it states in Berachos 24b: one who raises his voice in his prayer — behold he is among those of little faith (Rashi: as though the Holy One Blessed Be He does not hear whispered prayer, and he raises greatly); one who raises his voice in his prayer — behold he is among false prophets (Rashi: as it is written "and they called in a loud voice"); and in the Gemara, etc., according to your words, etc. — the Rabbis and Rabban Gamliel disagree: the Rabbis hold the chazzan discharges only for one not proficient, and Rabban Gamliel holds he discharges even for the proficient; and on this the Rabbis asked Rabban Gamliel: according to your words, why, etc. — the proficient should fulfill through the chazzan's prayer in a loud voice.

In order to arrange, etc. — since the congregation prays in a whisper, the chazzan also prays in a whisper, and this is the arrangement with which he arranges his prayer, as above siman 101.

However, one need not arrange, etc. — meaning: since the reason is only because of arrangement, therefore all year, when one need not arrange, he is permitted immediately to pray in a loud voice. And meaning that for the Rabbis, the proficient one nevertheless must pray in a whisper and does not fulfill through the chazzan's prayer; nevertheless in this they do not argue against Rabban Gamliel.

Nevertheless, this requires further study for Rabban Gamliel, etc. — since one prays in a whisper only because of arrangement, and all year one need not arrange.

For nevertheless the chazzan, etc. — since he prays for many they are more concerned, and even a prayer that is not of the festival sections requires arrangement; and therefore Magen Avraham retracted from what he said initially, that even without urgency it is permitted — for without urgency one must pray first in a whisper in order to arrange, even all year.

As he wrote siman 139, that even where the chazzan reads from the Torah he should not read before the congregation unless he arranged the matters between him and himself first; and an unspecified statement implies even where the chazzan is accustomed — nevertheless the chazzan must arrange; and therefore one must say the congregation was stringent.
