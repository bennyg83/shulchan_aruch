(10) "shall focus his attention," etc. — see in the novellae of Ra'ah, who wrote—and this is his language: see in the language of Tosafos and Rosh that it implies explicitly that even where he did not finish his prayer he need not return to the beginning of the prayer, but returns only to Retzeh; nevertheless he does not return and hears from the prayer leader the entire prayer—see there. And in such a case it seems he interrupts immediately and does not finish his prayer, since his intention is to be discharged through hearing from the prayer leader, automatically what he finishes of his prayer is a blessing in vain. And in my opinion, in such a case that he need not return to the beginning but only to Retzeh, it is possible that he should wait until the prayer leader reaches Retzeh, and from there onward pay attention to the prayer of the prayer leader, and need not hear the entire prayer from its beginning to its end—end of his words. And see Ran and Milchamot end of Rosh Hashana in what they brought from the Yerushalmi, and one can distinguish.
