Siman 10, "one who forgot," etc. Tosafos 29b, s.v. "erred," etc. And it is implied from their words that we rule like Rabbenu Gershom all year, and see Mordechai end of Rosh Hashana; but we rule like the conclusion of the gemara at the end of Rosh Hashana that we do not rule like Rabbenu Gershom except on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur alone, and as Rif and Rambam and end of chapter wrote; nevertheless the law of Bahag is true, as is proven in the gemara there, and as Tosafos also wrote; and the language of Rosh at the end of Rosh Hashana is more precise, that he wrote: and even though he said below that Rabbenu Gershom did not exempt, etc., but, etc., even the expert, etc., and so in chapter "Tefilat haShachar," etc.; and it is implied from their words that Rabbenu Gershom did not exempt those in a city at all in the prayer of the prayer leader; but Rif wrote there: but in a city the prayer leader does not discharge them except until they come to the synagogue and hear from the prayer leader from the beginning to the end, and he brings Yerushalmi Rosh Hashana Tzippori in the name of R' Yehuda: the law is like Rabbenu Gershom in these shofar [blessings], and this is when they sat there from the beginning of the prayer; and so Rosh there in his language, and so the Hagahot, end of chapter 2, s.v. "such as," etc., and on Rosh Hashana and Yom Kippur, etc., and so there in Tosafos as well—see there; and so Ran; and he wrote: and proof from what is taught in the gemara—he said to Rabbenu Gershom: according to your words, why does the congregation pray? Thus they are discharged—those who are in the synagogue; and as I wrote in siman 591 that he does not discharge them except in the blessings of malchuyot, zichronot, and shofarot alone; and even so the Yerushalmi required that he hear from the beginning of the prayer, and this is what [the Shulchan Aruch] writes here "and hears from the prayer leader," etc.; and this is the reason of Ramah in Tosafos there that a complete blessing does not exempt him; and in siman 268; and it is possible because Maariv prayer is optional.
