(10) In the gloss — and likewise if it is a time of pressure, etc. — and if the hour is not so pressed, Lechem Chamudos in chapter Tehillim note 15 and Eliyah Rabbah note 3 wrote that the congregation should not begin until after the chazzan says El HaKadosh — see there; and Beit Din, Beit HaTev note 4. But Eliyah Rabbah note 4 part 3 wrote that even if the hour is not so pressed they begin immediately; and he wrote: as it seems so is practiced — see there; and likewise agreed Piskei HaDin note 1 — see there. And further it seems to give a reason: because since the whole congregation intends on him and they have not yet prayed quietly, it seems as if they say repetition before quiet — and we already said above note 8 in the name of the Ari z"l that one should not say repetition without quiet — see there. And further, since the congregation has not yet prayed quietly and repetition without quiet is ineffective, behold it is as one praying individually; therefore they did not practice thus except if time did not remain — all pray together and the chazzan alone raises his voice in the first three only, because the first three count as one until after El HaKadosh in order to answer Kedushah; and afterward he finishes quietly with the congregation, as in the previous note. However, what Eliyah Rabbah and Peri Megadim Eshel Avraham note 5 wrote that the congregation should pray with the chazzan word for word until Shome'a Tefillah and Modim and they quietly while the chazzan aloud — and so from Levush siman 233 seif 1, see there — from the words of the Zohar and the Ari z"l it seems this should not be done; since there is no repetition without quiet, and also since there are not nine intending for his blessings, this is not like only quiet prayer, and one who raises his voice in quiet prayer is forbidden because external forces attach — as written above siman 101 note 7; rather one should beware not to raise his voice except in the first three specifically, as explained; and this too is under pressure because of honor of the holy commandment that it not be nullified from them — investigate.
