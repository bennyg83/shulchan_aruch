One who forgot, etc. This is the words of Behag in Tosafot, and the Rosh brought proof for this from what Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel expounded: just as the prayer leader fulfills for one who is not expert, so he fulfills for the expert. Beit Yosef challenged: we do not rule like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel except on Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur; but the rest of the days of the year we rule like the Rabbis, that the prayer leader does not fulfill for the expert — and here we deal with the rest of the days of the year. Answer of the Rabbis: they agree to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel in forgetfulness and error — but not except one who did not pray at all. Challenge: Rabban Gamliel only exempted with those in the fields due to coercion — and thus the Rabbis who disagree hold that even in a case of coercion he does not exempt the expert, and forgetfulness is not better than coercion. Answer: in forgetfulness the reason was not due to coercion; rather, the rabbis of Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel did not obligate the expert not to rely on the prayer leader except for the entire prayer, but for one matter alone he may rely on the prayer leader, as long as he hears from the beginning of the eighteen [blessings] until the end. In Mordechai, last chapter of Rosh Hashanah, he wrote that for Behag we rule like Rabban Gamliel even on the rest of the days of the year. Afterward he brought in the name of Maharam: if one forgot a complete blessing, hearing from the prayer leader does not help him — and it appears that Tosafot, the Rosh, and Tur also intended this. That is what they wrote, to distinguish between one who did not pray at all — meaning, even in error of forgetfulness — nevertheless in this he may not rely on the prayer leader. In Tosafot at the end of Rosh Hashanah there is another distinction: one who hears from the prayer leader, all fulfill; rather, those who wish to exempt even without hearing — the Rabbis and Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel disagree. Here, in Yaaleh veYavo, all agree, since he wishes to hear from beginning to end.
