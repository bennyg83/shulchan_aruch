(Shulchan Aruch seif 11) — answers with them. Meaning: as explained in seif 8, that one must answer amen immediately when the blessing finished — for otherwise it is like answering amen without any blessing; here he says that if the majority of the congregation has not finished amen, it is fine, because answering amen is part of the blessing, and when one hears them answering amen it is as though he hears the blessing now.
