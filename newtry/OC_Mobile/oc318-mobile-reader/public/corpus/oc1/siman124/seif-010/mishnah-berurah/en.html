(מ) "he shall pay attention" — for although the halachah is decided that the prayer leader discharges only one who is not expert specifically, here is different because he prayed but forgot and did not mention [it]; therefore even though he is expert the prayer leader discharges him. And see in the later authorities who wrote that it is better he should return and pray by himself, for nowadays not every person can pay attention to hear from the prayer leader from beginning to end, and perhaps his heart will turn away and he will not hear from the prayer leader some words that are blocking in prayer. And Bach and Eliyah Rabbah wrote that if the prayer leader's way is to swallow words, by law it is forbidden to rely on the prayer leader but he is obligated to pray by himself.
