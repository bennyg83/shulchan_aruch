One who forgot and did not say Ya'aleh veYavo on Rosh Chodesh or Chol HaMoed, or anything for which one must repeat — he should focus his attention and hear from the prayer leader all eighteen blessings from beginning to end like one who prays for himself, and he should not interrupt and not converse, and he steps three steps backward; for since he already prayed but forgot and did not mention, even though he is expert, the prayer leader discharges his obligation.
