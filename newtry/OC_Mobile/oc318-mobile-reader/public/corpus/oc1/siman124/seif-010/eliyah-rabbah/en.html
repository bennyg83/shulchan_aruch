(10) "It is true," etc. — on every matter of blessing and blessing one should intend that he acknowledges and believes the matter of the blessing he blesses. And after questions and requests — such as amen after Kaddish, and the blessing "Rachem," and "haRachaman" in Birkat HaMazon, and after "Zochreinu" in Yaaleh VeYavo and similar, which are requests — one should intend "may it be His will" that they believe the requests of the one blessing. And the middle blessings of Shemoneh Esreh, and the blessing Retzeh, and Sim Shalom, and Shalom Rav, which are requests and are praises of the Holy One — one should intend in Atah Chonen: it is true that the Name, may He be blessed, bestows knowledge, and He will fulfill and believe the words of the requesters; and so in all according to their matter. Therefore one should intend greatly to hear from the shaliach tzibur whatever matter of prayer, request, and blessing he will intend; and in Shelah p. 254 it is explained at length.
