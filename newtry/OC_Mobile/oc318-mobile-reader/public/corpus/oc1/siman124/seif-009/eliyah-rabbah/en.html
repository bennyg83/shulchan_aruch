[9] After every blessing, etc. It appears that this is not limited to a blessing, but applies even after merely mentioning Hashem’s name, for this is the reason given in Hagahos Ashiri. I found the same at the end of siman 323. Binyamin Ze’ev writes that one should not add the word hagadol except during Aseres Yemei Teshuvah. Our practice is not to add it even then, and this is also implied by Kitzur Shelah, as Shelah writes there.
