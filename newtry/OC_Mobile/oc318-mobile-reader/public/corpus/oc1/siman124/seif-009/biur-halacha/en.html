(א) “if they prolong,” etc. — and if the minority who have not finished are not prolonging excessively, but the majority said it hurriedly, one must wait for the minority; but presumptively one need not rely on this [distinction] [Mordekhai, article].

(ב) "the one blessing need not," etc. — and see in Mishna Berurah regarding blessings through which one discharges [another's obligation]; and this is from Magen Avraham. And regarding the repetition of our chazzan there are views among the acharonim that even though we are all proficient, nevertheless the Sages already enacted [repetition] for us as well — as above in seif 3; it is possible this is included in an obligatory blessing. See in Eliyah Rabbah siman 128, and in Perishah, and in Peri Chadash, and Peri Megadim.
