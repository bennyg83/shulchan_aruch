In the Shulchan Aruch — a prayer leader who prayed, etc. And see in She'ilat Yaavetz part 1 siman 21, who wrote: a prayer leader who prayed individually and a congregation happened upon him and he is compelled to pass before the ark — he prays again quietly and afterward prays in a loud voice for the congregation, see there. And in Birkat Yaakov he wrote that his words are not compelling and his proofs are not decisive, see there; and so it appears as the essential ruling that he should not pray again quietly, since he already arranged his prayer once quietly.
