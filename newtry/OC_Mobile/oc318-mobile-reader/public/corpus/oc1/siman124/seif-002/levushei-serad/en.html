Seif kuf-hei — a pause (shehut). Explanation: although it deals with the hour being pressed that they fear the time of prayer will pass, nevertheless it is not so pressed — and therefore Maharil commanded that one of them pray afterward in order to answer amen, or they call one who already prayed; but if the hour is very pressed that they all need to pray immediately and there is also no leisure to ask one who already prayed — permitted.
