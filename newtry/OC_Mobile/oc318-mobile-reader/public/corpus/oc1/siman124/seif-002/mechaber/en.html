A prayer leader who entered the synagogue and found the congregation praying quietly, and he needs to pass before the ark — immediately he goes down before the ark and prays in a loud voice for the congregation,' and he does not need to go back and pray quietly.And so too, if it is a time of pressure — for example, he fears that the time of prayer will pass — he may pray immediately in a loud voice, and the congregation prays with him word for word quietly until after HaKel HaKadosh; and it is good that there be at least one person who answers amen after the' prayer leader's blessings [Maharil].]:
