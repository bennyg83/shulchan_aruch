The prayer. Meaning that they will not be able to finish all eighteen blessings within the time of prayer; see siman 232, seif 1.

Ha-kadosh. Meaning also the wording of the blessing "le-dor va-dor" until "ha-El ha-kadosh" he says with the shaliach tzibbur word for word. Bach in the name of Maharshal, and so too Eliyahu Rabba and Knesset HaGedolah. And if the hour is not so pressed, the congregation should not begin except after the shaliach tzibbur said "ha-El ha-kadosh." Levush, and so Eliyahu Rabba. And Sephardim practice all year in Minchah that the shaliach tzibbur with the congregation say the first three aloud until after "ha-El ha-kadosh" and the rest quietly. And the last three the shaliach tzibbur alone says aloud; see Radbaz part 1, siman 5, 94, and 100, and in Knesset HaGedolah siman 101.

The shaliach tzibbur. Eliyahu Rabba wrote that he disagrees with seif 4 if there are not nine directing intention, etc. And this is not precise, for here the shaliach tzibbur prays once. Magen Avraham.
