But in the days of our master the Arizal, of blessed memory, permission was granted to reveal, and he revealed the secret of the repetition: that its level is greater than that of the silent prayer, and both are obligatory, as explained in Shaar HaKavanos, the first discourse on the repetition of the Amidah, and in Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar Chazaras HaAmidah, chapters 1 and 2; see there. Therefore the Arizal, of blessed memory, during the repetition of the shaliach tzibbur, would close his eyes and listen and concentrate on the words of the shaliach tzibbur, as written in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 4a, and Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaKorbanos, chapter 2; and so wrote the Mishnas Chassidim in the discourse of She'eiris HaAtzilus, ot 1. Therefore one should never cancel the repetition, at Shacharis, Musaf, or Minchah, except specifically in a situation of duress; and so wrote the Ben Ish Chai there, ot 2; see there. And the order of the aspects of the tefillos I have already written in a responsum in my small holy work Be'er Mayim Chaim, with Heaven's help; take it from there.
