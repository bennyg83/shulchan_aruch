(ד) "and he must," etc. — for there is not among them one who can pray before the amud; for otherwise it is not proper that he pray for them in a loud voice, since he did not pray at first quietly for himself.
(ה) "and he is discharged," etc. — for he is discharged through it also for himself, for since for others he discharges, for himself all the more so; and there is no concern here of small faith or raising his voice in his prayer, since by force of pressure he does so. The later authorities wrote: if the prayer leader already prayed quietly and it happened to him to pray the repetition of the prayer leader for another congregation, he should not pray quietly a second time.
(ו) "time of pressure" — and without a time of pressure, many of the later authorities agreed not to do so, because the essential enactment was by law to pray at first quietly and afterward in a loud voice.
(ז) "that it will pass" — meaning that the prayer leader will not be able to finish all eighteen blessings within the time of prayer, whether in Shacharit that the time of four hours will end — for then he has no reward for prayer in its time, as likewise in siman 89 — and whether in Minchah that its time will pass entirely; so it implies from Peri Megadim, and see in Biur Halacha.
(ח) "and the congregation," etc. — and if the hour is not so pressed, the congregation should not begin until after the prayer leader says El HaKadosh.
(ט) "the Kadosh" — that is, also the wording of the blessing "from generation to generation" until El HaKadosh; they say with the prayer leader word for word.
(י) "and it is good," etc. — that is, when the hour is not so pressed but only that they fear perhaps the time will pass; but if the hour is very pressed, it suffices with what the children answer amen, or they invite one who already prayed that he answer amen if they can easily, and there is no need to be particular about this at all; and it is not similar to what he wrote in seif 4 that if there are not nine paying attention to the blessing of the prayer leader he is close to blessings in vain, for there is different — because he already prayed once quietly and he intends now only for the enactment of our Sages, unlike here where he prays only once.
