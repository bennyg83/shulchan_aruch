(ב) There — that if there is one who does not know, etc. — this is as to the simple meaning. And it is known that all words of our Sages, even though they contain wondrous secrets, they do not reveal them but clothe them in simple words; but in the days of our Rabbi the Ari z"l permission was given to reveal, and he revealed the secret of the repetition that its elevation is greater than quiet, and both are obligatory, as explained in Sha'ar HaKavanot drush 1 of repetition of Amidah and in Pri Etz Chaim, Sha'ar repetition of Amidah ch. 1–2 — see there. Therefore the Ari z"l in the repetition of the chazzan would close his eyes and hear and intend the chazzan's words, as written in Sha'ar HaKavanot drush 4 p. 71; and Pri Etz Chaim, Sha'ar HaKorbanot ch. 2; and likewise in Machazit HaShekel in the drush She'erit HaElef note 1. Therefore one should not nullify repetition ever — Shacharit, Musaf, Minchah — except specifically where there is coercion — so Ben Ish Chai there note 2, see there. And the order of the aspects of prayers I already wrote in responsum in Sefer HaKadosh Be'er Mayim Chaim — take from there.
