The prayer leader — Baer Heitev. And see in Shakh — if there are ten, even though some of them already prayed, since in any case there are nine who answer amen "God stands in the congregation of God," it is called a congregation, since there is a majority for whom they can spread a tallit for Shema and say Kaddish and Kedushah, see there. And see above siman 69 what he wrote in the responsum of Magen Avraham and what Magen Avraham wrote, that if there are there ten who have not prayed, the prayer leader begins immediately in a loud voice; see what he wrote in his responsum there.
