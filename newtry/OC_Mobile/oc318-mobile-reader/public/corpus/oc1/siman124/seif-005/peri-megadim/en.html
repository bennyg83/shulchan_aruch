One who forgot (Taz) — apparently his opinion is that through a complete berachah one does not fulfill from the shaliach tzibur; and this is specifically when he did not pray at all — that is, he skipped and did not bless one section; but if he skipped and did not mention ya'aleh veyavo and the like, he fulfills from the shaliach tzibur — and similarly understood Eliyah Rabbah sign 17, see there; and he wrote that it is astonishing. But truly in Beit Yosef he wrote plainly that through a complete berachah one also hears from the shaliach tzibur; and similarly Levush. And truly one could say the Taz's intention is not for this kavanah, but rather what he wrote: that forgetting is not better than coercion; and nevertheless for the Rabbis the shaliach tzibur does not fulfill — likewise in forgetting, to pray with the congregation silently — if so he fulfills from the shaliach tzibur; for this he wrote that one does not fulfill all who did not pray, even in forgetting; and specifically if he skipped a mention or a complete berachah — he hears from the shaliach tzibur. And Magen Avraham sign 17 for the law. And behold: or — it is for the Tur: even through a complete berachah one fulfills from the shaliach tzibur, from what is written in 268, that he wondered on Rabbeinu Moshe Gaon how one fulfills through a berachah similar to seven over seven berachot — if not so, it is not difficult for him even though it is a complete berachah; and nevertheless one could say there is no fixed berachah on weekdays. And from that case in siman 268 one may learn, as I will say.

**And the ruling emerged** for us from that in siman 108:7 regarding intentional: if he wants he prays a voluntary prayer; if he heard from the shaliach tzibur too, it is as if he prayed — since it is optional in his hand, as in 268 — Maariv is optional, see in Beit Yosef and Magen Avraham sign 15. But in doubt whether he prayed and heard from the shaliach tzibur — he does not fulfill, since he is obligated to pray again conditionally as in siman 107, see there — the shaliach tzibur does not fulfill him. And who will tell us to distinguish and say the Rabbis dispute Resh Lakish — he certainly did not pray, etc. — and if so it is reasonable, certainly that he does not fulfill through hearing the shaliach tzibur. See the end of the siman — it applies here.
