Magen Avraham seif kuf-gimel — according to your words, that R' Yehoshua said to Rabban Gamliel: according to your words, that you say the chazzan discharges even the proficient one, why [does] the congregation [pray], etc. — and Magen Avraham proves from this that there is no quiet in chazzan for those of little faith, for there is no reason except to arrange; and throughout the year one need not arrange; and afterward he wrote: nevertheless, difficulty, etc. — it contradicts the proof, for necessarily the chazzan is good to arrange throughout the year, and as in siman 139 regarding reading the Torah; and therefore it is not permitted without pressure because of arranging.
