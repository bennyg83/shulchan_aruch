After the congregation has finished their prayer, etc. And also the shaliach tzibbur repeats the prayer so that Kedushah will follow him; therefore an individual must not begin to pray unless he can finish before the shaliach tzibbur reaches Kedushah. And if he began and reached Kedushah together with the shaliach tzibbur, he should say Kedushah with him word for word (Tur). And he should also say with the shaliach tzibbur afterward the wording of the blessing "le-dor va-dor" likewise word for word until "ha-El ha-kadosh" (Maharshal). And so it is implied in his gloss on his customs in the fifth chapter of Pesach, siman 42. And so too if it is a time of pressure.
And it is good to listen when the shaliach tzibbur repeats the prayer, to hear and answer amen; and one should protest against those who raise their voices and say with the cantor the eighteen-blessing prayer (Tur), for three things these people do are forbidden. First, they recite a blessing in vain. Second, each person is obligated to be silent and direct his attention to the shaliach tzibbur's blessing and say amen; and now that they say the eighteen blessings with the shaliach tzibbur, they do not direct attention to the shaliach tzibbur and do not answer amen after him. Third, because he does not direct attention to the shaliach tzibbur, the shaliach tzibbur's blessing is a blessing in vain. And one should not say "Baruch Hu u-varuch Shemo" the extended form.
