**One should have kavanah** (Taz; Magen Avraham letter 2; Elya Rabbah and Levush) — another reason: because of kedushah, the repetition of the shaliach tzibur is required. This is for R. Yehuda bar Lakish in Berachos 21b and Rosh Hashana 34b, who challenges: according to your words, why does he repeat? It is difficult for R. Yehuda bar Lakish and R. Yochanan to answer; and if so, certainly the words of the Rambam of blessed memory were correct, as the author wrote in seif 3 — they do not dispute this. And Magen Avraham siman 59 sign 4 — when does the shaliach tzibur repeat the prayer; see there.

**And know:** that if someone has a doubt whether he prayed, and returns and prays conditionally as in siman 107 — it seems to me he may fulfill the obligation for many aloud if he prays conditionally (see seif 2), and this is no worse than one who already fulfilled and fulfills again; I have seen that this is an error — one need not make a condition at all, since he is obligated to repeat because of the enactment. But if we say so, we would say: one who doubts whether he prayed Shacharit on Shabbos or Musaf on chol hamoed has a remedy to be shaliach tzibur and repeat the prayer because of the enactment, and also fulfills from doubt — as stated. And similarly Efrayim Chayyim 189 seif 7. Incidentally I saw to comment a bit in 189:8 — if counting the omer in our times as deoraita, one counts without a blessing; and it is different in siman 67 where they enacted thus (Magen Avraham there). And if counting in our times as derabbanan, one counts without a blessing — one could say a doubt in derabbanan is lenient; there is bother; unlike saying amen which is required, and like the case of R. Papa — ask him; and without a blessing, as a doubt in a berachah is lenient — from there is the stringency of lo tisa; unlike when one blesses to fulfill others, there is no lo tisa here, and he is obligated in the matter. And one could say the rulings of the judge there in 189, and with God's help it will be explained there. However, this is certain: that even regarding derabbanan, anyone who is not obligated cannot fulfill — and with God's help in 189 it will be explained further.
