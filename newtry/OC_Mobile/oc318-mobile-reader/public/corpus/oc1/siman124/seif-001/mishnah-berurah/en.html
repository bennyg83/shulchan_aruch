(1) One who does not know — but one who is expert does not fulfill his obligation even after the fact through the prayer leader's prayer. And even one who is not expert does not fulfill except when there are ten in the synagogue.
(2) Pay attention — and he must understand in the holy tongue, for otherwise it does not help even though he hears from the prayer leader every word; but one who prays by oneself in the holy tongue, even if he does not understand the language, fulfills his obligation. And see above siman 101 in Mishna Berurah that at least in the blessing of the Fathers he should understand what he says; and see above siman 62 in Mishna Berurah seif kaf-gimel.
(ג) "and he does not interrupt" — that is, even if he heard from others during that time some Kedushah or Baruchu, and all the more so it is forbidden to answer "Baruch Hu u'varuch Shemo"; but amen to every blessing he must answer, and as below in seif 6, for that pertains to prayer.
