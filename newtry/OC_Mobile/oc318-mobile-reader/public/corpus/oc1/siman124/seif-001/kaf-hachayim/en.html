(א) [Seif 1] After the congregation finished their prayer, etc. — and if he finished his prayer quietly and the chazzan has not yet begun the repetition, it is a complete sin to speak between quiet and repetition — Gur Aryeh, Kavanat HaLev siman 8 note 21; in Beit Yosef laws of repetition of chazzan note 10; Piskei HaDin siman 111 note 3; Chasdei David note 6; Kaf HaChaim to Rachatz siman 15 note 52; and Piskei HaDin wrote there all the more so the chazzan is forbidden to speak between quiet and repetition — see there; and likewise Kaf HaChaim there. And so from the words of Sha'ar HaKavanot, drush 4 of Yom Kippur, who wrote on the verse "Heaven Hu HaElokim": it would have been fitting to say it after the quiet prayer of Ne'ilah and not after the repetition; and the reason we say it after the repetition is so as not to interrupt between quiet and repetition — see there. If so, it implies that since this verse must be said between quiet and repetition and nevertheless we do not say it in order not to interrupt — all the more so it is forbidden to interrupt with speech between quiet and repetition; and therefore it seems even to learn is forbidden between quiet and repetition — so Ben Ish Chai, Parashat Terumah note 10; and see our words above siman 53 note 16.
