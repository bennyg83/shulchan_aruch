Laws of the Conduct of the Prayer Leader during the Eighteen Blessings [i.e. Amidah], and the Laws of Answering Amen. Containing 12 S'ifim: After the congregation finishes their prayer [i.e. Amidah], the prayer leader repeats the prayer, so that if there is anyone who does not know how to pray [the Amidah], [that person] will pay attention to what [the prayer leader] is saying and fulfill [that person's] obligation through that. And that one who is fulfilling an obligation through the prayer of the prayer leader must pay attention to everything that [the prayer leader] says, from beginning to end, and may not interrupt and may not converse, and [that person] steps three steps backwards just like one who prays by oneself.
