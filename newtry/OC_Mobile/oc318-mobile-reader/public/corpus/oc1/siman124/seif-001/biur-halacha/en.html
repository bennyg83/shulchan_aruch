(א) "shall pay attention," etc. — Magen Avraham wrote in siman 591: if they say piyutim, he does not discharge one who is not expert, and the reason is that it is impossible to hear all the piyutim and it is like an interruption; nevertheless the prayer leader must repeat the prayer even while the piyutim are said, for sometimes one who is not expert will pay attention and be discharged thereby, only that it is unlikely. Peri Megadim.

(ב) "and is discharged through it" — and one who has a doubt whether he prayed Shacharit on Shabbat or Musaf on Chol Hamoed, as we wrote in siman 107, that he cannot repeat and pray because he cannot have this intention voluntarily — see there. Nevertheless he has a remedy to be the prayer leader and repeat the prayer for the regulation, and he is also discharged from the doubt. Peri Megadim.
