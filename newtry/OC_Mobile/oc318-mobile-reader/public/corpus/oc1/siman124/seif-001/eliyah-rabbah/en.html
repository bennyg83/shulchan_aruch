(א) "The chazzan shall repeat." This is the wording of Olas Tamid — meaning: even though some of them already prayed a bit with the congregation, since there is a majority who have not yet prayed with the congregation, and this is obvious; and so wrote in Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah — so far his wording. One may wonder at its simplicity, for I brought in siman 55 seif kaf-zayin the responsum of Radbaz that even if all did not pray with the congregation, since they all prayed individually he does not repeat and pray; and I looked in Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah and saw it should be explained when they did not pray at all, even individually — see there. And even though I wrote above that even for one who did not pray he repeats the prayer, it seems this is for us who read Shema even for an individual, as above siman 69; but Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah speaks according to Rambam, that only for a majority who did not hear do we read Shema — investigate. And it seems that even for us there is a distinction: if there is a majority who did not pray, the shaliach tzibur prays quietly and repeats aloud; but for an individual he does not pray quietly but aloud. And it seems that if the shaliach tzibur already prayed, he never prays now quietly — see Magen Avraham siman 69.
