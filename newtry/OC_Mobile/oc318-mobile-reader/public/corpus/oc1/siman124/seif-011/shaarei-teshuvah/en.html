The blessing — Baer Heitev. And the words "that they believe" is a scribal error; it should read "that they believe." And two Rishonim in the name of Mahari Molcho: one who happens to answer amen on two matters answers two amens, one after the other; and it is not comparable to Shema, for there it is on two matters, and it is preferable to say amen and amen, see there; and Efer HaChayyim siman 61.
