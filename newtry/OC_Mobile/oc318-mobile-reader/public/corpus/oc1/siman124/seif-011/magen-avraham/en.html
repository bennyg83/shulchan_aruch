One who hears is like the congregation. At first glance it appears he intended what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Avraham Chaim, and so wrote the Mishna Medura; and it appears to me it does not exist — for Avraham Chaim's intention was to resolve another word regarding the poskim's difficulty from the flute, and to distinguish between congregation and individual; but Rama is silent in Shulchan Aruch like other poskim that there is no distinction, and that he specified here congregation is because it refers above.

And likewise Kaddish — for only a thing one is obligated to say individually is called a blessing one is obligated in; if not, it is difficult how they fulfilled in Alexandria with Kaddish and Barchu.
