(11) "And I believe," etc. — therefore great is one who answers amen, etc.; and Perishah wrote the reason: because he accepts the blessing in amen — in it acceptance, in it oath, in it belief — as brought in Devarim Rabbah; which is not so in the one blessing — so far. And in Shelah p. 255 he concludes that one who blesses with great and mighty intention — the one blessing is greater.
