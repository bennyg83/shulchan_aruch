See in the responsum of Magen Avraham regarding people who study during the prayer leader's repetition of the prayer, or say supplications — if they intend at the end of the blessing to answer amen properly, one should not protest them; but in Sefer Vavei Ha'amudim he protested against them, and so it appears from what he wrote in siman 90, seif 18.
