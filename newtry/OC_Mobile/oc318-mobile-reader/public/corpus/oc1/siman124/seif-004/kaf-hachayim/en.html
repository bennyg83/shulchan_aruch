(ד) There — that if there is one who does not know, etc. — namely he does not know how to pray; nevertheless he understands what the chazzan says — Beit Yosef and Bach explained: namely he does not know how to pray from outside when he has no siddur in hand; but if he does not understand what the chazzan says he does not fulfill through hearing, as written in siman 193 — Beit Yosef, Levush, Bach; and Bach wrote there: but one praying himself in the prayer text, even if he does not understand what he utters from his mouth, he fulfills — see there; and see our words above siman 101 note 16 — investigate; 16 seif kuf-alef; Eliyah Rabbah note 2; Magen Avraham seif 2; Peri Chadash note 1; Eliyah Rabbah note 2; Razah note 2; Mishna Berurah note 2.
