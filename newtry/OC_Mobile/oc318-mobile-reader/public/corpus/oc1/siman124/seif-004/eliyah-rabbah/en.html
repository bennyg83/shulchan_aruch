(ד) "Until El HaKadosh," etc. — it is somewhat difficult, for above siman 109 seif 2 implies they should pray with the shaliach tzibur the entire prayer and the blessing Shome'a Tefillah word for word, and intend to reach Modim with the shaliach tzibur — strained to divide. And it seems it comes to inform us that they should say the entire text and Kedushah and Ledor Vador, etc., until El HaKadosh; and the same law that they pray the entire prayer with the shaliach tzibur; and so implies in Maharil, Laws of Prayer, source of this law; and so implies in Mateh Moshe siman 375 in the name of Sefer Chasidim — this is his wording: he shall say Kedushah aloud and all the prayers, and the congregation shall say quietly everything he says — Kedushah and all — so far; and so implies in Levush below siman 233. Lechem Chamudos wrote: perhaps he spoke only at a very pressed hour that if they do not begin they cannot finish before their time passes; but otherwise it is better that the congregation not begin until the shaliach tzibur says El HaKadosh — so far. And for what I will write in a note afterward, it implies even when the hour is not so pressed they begin immediately; and so implies more in Maharil there; and as it seems so is practiced — see above siman 109 and siman 233 more on this.
