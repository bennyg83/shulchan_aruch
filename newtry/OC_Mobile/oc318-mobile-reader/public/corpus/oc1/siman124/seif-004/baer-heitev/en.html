To be silent. See the responsum of Rama mi-Fano siman 102 on people who study during the repetition of the shaliach tzibbur's prayer or say supplications — if they direct intention to the end of the blessing to answer amen properly, one should not protest them; but in Vay Even Ha-Amudim he calls quarrel against them; see siman 90, seif 18.

Directing intention. From this it appears that one who does not hear is not counted in the minyan even though he is competent. Taz. And Pri Chadash siman 55 wrote that if he directs the blessings and when he sees them answering he answers with them — similar to waving the scarves from Alexandria of Egypt — for everyone they are counted; see there.
