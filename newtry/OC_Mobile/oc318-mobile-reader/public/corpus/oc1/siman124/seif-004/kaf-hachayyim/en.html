4) There: For if there is someone who does not know, etc. That is, one who does not know how to pray, yet nevertheless understands what the shaliach tzibbur says. Beis Yosef. And the Bach explained: that is, one who does not know how to pray by heart, when there is no siddur in his hand; but if he does not understand what the shaliach tzibbur says, he does not fulfill his obligation through listening, as stated in siman 193. Beis Yosef; Levush; Bach. And the Bach wrote there: but one who prays by himself with the text of the tefillah, even if he does not understand what he utters with his mouth, fulfills his obligation; see there. And see our words above in siman 101, ot 16, and examine this well. Taz, s.k. 1; Olas Tamid, ot 2; Magen Avraham, s.k. 2; Pri Chadash, ot 1; Eliyah Rabbah, ot 2; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, ot 2; Mishnah Berurah, ot 2.
