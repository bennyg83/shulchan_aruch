Taz seif kuf-dalet — a dispute — except in the repetition of the chazzan's eighteen [blessings], in which one is obligated. Meaning: this is an intermediate matter — because it is more stringent than other blessings, for there one is not obligated to try to answer amen, but here one is obligated to answer amen, as explained in seif 7; and it is lighter than one who is obligated in the blessing, for behold he already fulfilled. And since it is an intermediate matter, they dispute about it — one compares it to this and one to that.
