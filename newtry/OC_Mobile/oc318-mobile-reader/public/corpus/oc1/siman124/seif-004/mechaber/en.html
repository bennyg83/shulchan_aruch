When the prayer leader repeats the prayer, the congregation should be silent and focus on the blessings that the chazan recites and answer amen; and if there are not nine focusing on his blessings, his blessings are' close to being in vain. Therefore, each person should make himself as though there are not nine besides him, and focus on the chazan's blessing. (There are those who say that the entire people should stand when the prayer leader repeats the prayer [Hagahot Minhagim].)[ ]:
