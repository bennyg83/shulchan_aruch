He should not answer amen hurriedly (chatuf), etc. Meaning that one who reads the alef in shem Heaven leaning toward tzerei — "amen" — but it must be amen with patach, meaning with kamatz, as we read amen; and kamatz is also called patach, as Raavad explained on the verse "Heaven yimloch le-olam va-ed".

And likewise he should not answer amen cut off (katuft) by omitting the reading of the nun; and the same applies if one omits reading the alef or mem — but because many are not careful about reading the nun that it should be articulated, therefore Rashi mentions the nun.
