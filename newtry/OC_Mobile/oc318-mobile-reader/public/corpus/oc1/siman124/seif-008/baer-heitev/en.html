With chataf. Explanation in Shulchan Aruch; and likewise he should not say amen with other vowel points — shuruk or holam — as Knesset HaGedolah, for they do not convey the meaning of affirming things. Levush.

Obligated, etc. And the Bach was stringent even if he is not obligated, and even if he knows which blessing it is — if the blessing is to discharge the obligation of the many, it is not permitted for an expert unless in the blessing on the Torah and the blessing over enjoyment; see there.

Amen. And if the cantor prolongs with the melody of "va-yomru amen," he should say amen immediately, for the melody is an interruption. Yad Natan; see Magen Avraham.
