With a chataf. Meaning, with a chataf patach (Bach).

He is not obligated. And Bach wrote to be stringent — even if he is not obligated, even if he knows — if the blessing is to discharge many from their obligation, it is not permitted knowingly, except in the blessing on the Torah and blessings over enjoyment.

Immediately when it is finished. And if the chazan prolongs in the melody of "and let them say amen," they should say amen immediately, for the melody constitutes an interruption (Yesh Nochalin); and see above in siman 128 that prolonging the melody is part of the blessing; nevertheless it seems to me that if he prolongs greatly it constitutes an interruption.
