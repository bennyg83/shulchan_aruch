(ח) [Seif 2] A chazzan who entered the synagogue, etc., and goes down before the ark, etc. — so too Beit Yosef in the name of Kolbo siman 27, who wrote in the name of Rosh; but the Rav wrote in Pri Etz Chaim, end of chapter 2 of Sha'ar HaAmidah, that one should not say the Amidah of Shacharit and Minchah except first quietly and then say the repetition aloud — end quote; and so from the words of Sha'ar HaKavanot, drush 1 of repetition of the Amidah — see there; and likewise the Rav Kaf HaKohen in siman 109 from responsum of the Rav Ya'avetz: if he did not pray quietly and found a congregation that finished quietly, he cannot say the repetition, and this applies to both of them — for there is no repetition prayer unless quiet prayer preceded it; and this David the anointed one hinted: "I will give great thanks to Heaven with my mouth; at first with my mouth alone, and again afterward among the multitude we will praise Him" — end quote; and Rokeach brought this in note 2 — see there; and see our words above siman 25 note 75, where I wrote that anything not explicitly mentioned in the Talmud and over which the poskim disagree — the opinion of the kabbalists decides — see there; if so, all the more in this matter, where "Sit and do not act" is preferable.
