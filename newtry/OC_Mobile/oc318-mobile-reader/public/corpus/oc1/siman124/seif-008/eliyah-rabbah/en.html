(ח) "Close to being," etc. — this is Perishah's wording: close but not literally, for he already wrote at the beginning of siman 55 that if he began with ten and some left, the shaliach tzibur finishes. Also explained in Beit Yosef that even if some sleep or count rumors, even so the shaliach tzibur prays and finishes his prayer — so far his wording. And per this, the law is: one who speaks and does not listen joins to the minyan; and so wrote Beit Yosef, Shulchan Aruch, and Levush explicitly in siman 55; and in Beit Yosef, in the name of Agur more explicit, and not like Taz.
