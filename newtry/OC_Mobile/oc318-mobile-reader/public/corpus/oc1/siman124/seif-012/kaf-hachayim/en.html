(12) In the gloss — and if there are individuals in the congregation, etc. — the chazzan should not wait, etc.; and now they practice waiting for the av beit din; and it seems the reason is that most people pray hurriedly, and if individuals pray word for word they cannot say Kedushah with the congregation — therefore they wait. Therefore it seems that even if there is no av beit din in the city, one should wait for the one praying word for word; but when he prolongs one need not wait for him — Magen Avraham seif kuf-zayin; Eliyah Rabbah note 6; Sha'ar HaTziyun note 1; Razah note 5; chazzan siman 137 p. 74; Chasdei David note 2; Mishna Berurah note 13; and likewise Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahat Tashbetz siman 90: the custom now is to wait for the sage of the congregation and all the great ones of the congregation, and there is no burden on the congregation since they waive their honor — end quote; and they brought Eliyah Rabbah there, who wrote: nevertheless it seems the rabbi should not prolong so much, as we find with R. Akiva who was brief; and all the more in a place waiting for the sage who has not yet come — he should hurry himself and be among the first ten — end quote; and Chasdei David wrote there: if he prolongs excessively he should be concerned for honor of the congregation and command them not to wait for him — see there; and see above siman 55 seif 7 and our words there note 50.
