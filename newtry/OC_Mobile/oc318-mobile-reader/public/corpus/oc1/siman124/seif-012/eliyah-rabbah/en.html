(12) "And they rebuke him," etc. — Kol Bo wrote p. 9: Woe to them who speak idle words or jest during prayer, for we saw in our sins how many synagogues were destroyed because they treated them lightly — so far. Written in Sefer Vavei HaAmudim: it is fitting that every community appoint people over this who will watch with many punishments on speakers and shame him publicly. Shelah wrote p. 255: I saw among those who tremble at God's word that they place the siddur before them during the repetition of prayer, and their eyes and heart are there and they do not look outside — then they intend on every word. Olas Tamid wrote in the name of responsum of Menachem Azariah siman 102: one occupied in Torah even if work is not becoming is nevertheless not called a sinner — so far. One should beware of this, for Menachem Azariah wrote the reason that we hold ourselves proficient; and he concluded: see that we stopped ourselves as not proficient — that if we did not intend fully we are not fit to fulfill when the shaliach tzibur goes down — so far; and it was already explained at the beginning of siman 101 that we hold ourselves nowadays as praying without intention. And so above siman 1 I wrote one should not say prayers and supplications while the shaliach tzibur repeats the prayer; and so implies in Shelah there; and so in Sefer Vavei HaAmudim he calls a quarrel on this — see siman 90 seif 18.
