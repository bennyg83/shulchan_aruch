**One should not raise** (Taz) together — it implies equally; and therefore one may give support to the custom that the shaliach tzibur raises his voice more in chazarat hatfilah, so that those answering do not raise more. One who forgot Minchah on erev Shabbos — the first time the enactment is to hear from the shaliach tzibur as in siman 268, and the second time not; and see siman 108.
