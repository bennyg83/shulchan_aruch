(ז) "To be silent," etc. — this is not like Nimukei Yosef wrote that he should say with the chazzan until the end of the blessing; and likewise in Beit Yosef in the name of Avudraham that one should rebuke them even if they do not conclude blessings with the chazzan. However, one who did not pray with intention should say with the shaliach tzibur calmly without concluding the blessings, only intend and hear from the shaliach tzibur — even though I wrote siman 112 that nowadays we hold ourselves not proficient, i.e., stringently; but this is like a leniency. And further, if so the whole congregation would say with the shaliach tzibur and become confused. Avudraham wrote: every prayer is like three prayers — three times nineteen equals fifty-seven blessings: one who prays quietly, one who hears with the chazzan — hearing is like answering — and one who answers amen. And in Maariv, where the shaliach tzibur does not repeat the prayer, they enacted "Baruch Heaven leolam amen" — you find among them between mentions and amens thirty-eight; and nineteen of quiet — behold fifty-seven blessings in Maariv.
