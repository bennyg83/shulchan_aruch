(ז) There — and he steps three steps, etc. — to exclude Ravyah brought in Beit Yosef in his name who disagrees and says: just as the chazzan discharges him from prayer, so he discharges him from three steps; but it is not so — for what discharges from prayer is because hearing is like reading; but in steps certainly he does not discharge — as the Mechaber ruled; Peri Chadash note 1; and Kaf HaChaim acharonim as the opinion of our master z"l. Therefore one must bow and rise also in Avot and Hodah at beginning and end, as when he prays himself.
