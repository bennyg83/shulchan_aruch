(26) During the time, etc. — meaning: even if he wants to be careful at the end of every blessing to intend and answer amen. Shlah wrote: I saw among those who fear the word of Heaven that they place the siddur before them during the repetition of the prayer, and their eyes and heart are there so they will not look outside — and then they intend on every word.

(27) From resting — Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Kol Bo: Woe to the people who chat during prayer, for we have seen how many synagogues were destroyed on account of this sin; and one should appoint known people to watch over this.

(28) The children — and one must educate them that they stand in awe and fear. And the very young who run back and forth in the synagogue laughing — it is better not to bring them, for habit becomes nature, and also they disturb the congregation in their prayer. And besides all this, it is proper for the father who brings such young children to the synagogue to watch their clothes and sandals whether they are clean, so as not to cause stumbling in this for those praying within four cubits of them — see above siman 81 in Mishna Berurah seif kaf-gimmel.
