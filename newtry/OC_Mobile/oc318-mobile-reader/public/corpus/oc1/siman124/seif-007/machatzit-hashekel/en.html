(7) The chazzan should not wait on him, etc. — but when he prolongs, etc., as written regarding R. Akiva, chapter Ein Yaakov 4:41a; and Tur wrote and this is his language: that he did not multiply and prolong in supplications that he would say, and also did not prolong so much in uttering the words because of troubling the congregation, end of his words; and if so, one who prolongs excessively does improperly by troubling the congregation so much, and therefore one should not wait for him.

And he fears they will mock him, and thereby confuse him and he will not be able to focus — so it is there.
