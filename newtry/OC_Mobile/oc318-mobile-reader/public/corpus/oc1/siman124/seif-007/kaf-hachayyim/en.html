7) There: And he steps three steps, etc. This is to exclude Mahari ibn Ghiyyat, whom the Beis Yosef cited in his name, who disputes and says that just as the shaliach tzibbur exempts him from the tefillah, so does he exempt him from the three steps. But it is not so, for the reason he exempts him from the tefillah is that one who listens is like one who recites; but from the steps he certainly does not exempt him, and as the Mechaber, of blessed memory, ruled. Pri Chadash, ot 1. And the Acharonim ruled like the view of Maran, of blessed memory. And according to this, he must also bow and straighten in Avos and in Hoda'ah, at the beginning and at the end, just as one who prays by himself.
