(36) If he removed it. This refers to removing it from himself completely. If it merely slipped from its place but remained on his body, see the following s.k.
(37) And he returns and wraps himself in it. The reason is that by removing it he interrupted the first wearing. Some later authorities disagree and hold that if his mind was to return and wrap himself in it, he does not bless again. Therefore, because of doubt regarding blessings, if his mind was to put it back on immediately he should not bless again.
(38) If most of it fell. If only a minority fell, all agree that he need not bless again. If most of it fell, the Mechaber holds that he must bless again, for the mitzvah of wrapping departed. Some later authorities rule that if part remained on him and he immediately restores it, he should not bless because of a doubtful blessing.
