And returns — Taz: Beit Yosef wrote that Tur holds one returns and blesses like tefillin; Taz challenged — if so, most of it too is like tefillin. And proof from Taz who wrote Tur as 36 — the interruption in prayer is different, and most of it is not an interruption and he blesses afterward (although one must pass to their performance ab initio as in siman 25 seif 8 for duration of time — here it is impossible). However Beit Yosef brought only as we find regarding prayer — the difference applies likewise; one can say regarding blessing there is a difference. And what he wrote regarding tefillin "most of them" — if so for Beit Yosef it is not needed; and see Eliyah Rabbah letter 18. However for us, below in 25 seif 12, one does not bless even if all of them slipped off — Magen Avraham there letter 21: Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Shevut Yaakov 83 — he blessed and his entire tallit fell from his hand and he lifted it — need not bless. Simple from Berachos 66 — specifically lost or despised; and above we wrote that if its tzitzis tore it is comparable to there.
