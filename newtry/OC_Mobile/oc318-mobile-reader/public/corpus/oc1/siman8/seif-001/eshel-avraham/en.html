And he blesses. And that which we do not bless on every mitzvah—the Tur in Ramban, siman 189: every mitzvah that has no action, such as release of loans and the like that are entrusted to bet din; and likewise matters that depend on others' will, such as charity and the like; and likewise a mitzvah that comes through a transgression, such as returning stolen goods and usury; and priestly gifts as well not, for from the High Table one merits—unlike redemption of the firstborn and separation of terumah, where one does not bless on separating it but on the separation. And likewise on every mitzvah whose main mitzvah is not [the act itself], such as chalitzah and yibum whose mitzvah is procreation, but these mitzvos branch off from it; and court executions as well not, because Heaven does not desire the death of the wicked; and for the same reason one does not bless on mayim acharonim that comes from Sodomite salt; and one should not bless on distress—and see there at length. Magen Avraham, seif kama 4: The citation on the word "viyaamod" is a printing error; one must place the citation on the word "al rosho." And what he wrote "if so, when he wraps himself" etc.—one can say that he places most of the tallit toward his stature, for then the wrapping is in length. And see siman 11, seif kama 15.
