1) [Seif 1] He shall wrap himself in tzitzit, etc. Immediately after netilat yadayim—Tur. Magen Avraham, seif kama 1; Chayei Adam, klal 11, letter 1; Razah, letter 1—and the reason Machatzit HaShekel wrote is so that he wrap himself immediately in the mitzvah. And Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham wrote so that he not go four amos without tzitzit; and so Chayei Adam there. But before netilat yadayim one should not don tzitzit, because it is proper to bless immediately before performance—see seif 10. However according to us, who bless on a tallit gadol for wrapping and intend to exempt this one, there is no need except to don it first before netilat yadayim; indeed according to what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 4, seif kama 1, in the name of Sedeh Yehoshua, that one may not touch garments before netilat yadayim—if so he should not don the tallit until after netilat yadayim—Peri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, seif kama 1 (see Levush and in our words above, siman 4, letter 6). And even when he rises before daybreak he wraps himself immediately, and when day breaks he handles it and blesses, as stated in seif 16—Machatzit HaShekel. And according to us, when he blesses on a tallit gadol for wrapping he may intend to exempt this one, as will be explained there, be-siyata diShmaya; and although our custom is to lie in a tallit katan per the Arizal, as will be explained in siman 21, seif 3, be-siyata diShmaya—nevertheless a practical difference emerges: if it happened that one did not sleep in his tallit, or in places where one cannot lie in one's garments because of fleas and the like—and see Shaarei Teshuvah, letter 1.
