(1) He wraps himself in tzitzis — immediately after netilas yadayim, so that he does not walk four cubits without tzitzis.

(2) Standing — meaning that both the wrapping and the blessing are while standing. The wrapping, because it is written “for you,” and we derive a verbal analogy from the omer, which also is written “for you,” and regarding the omer it is written: “from the beginning of the sickle in the standing grain,” and we expound “in stature [bekomah].” And the blessing, because every blessing over mitzvos must be while standing. This is only lekatchilah; bedieved he fulfills both in any manner. And most of the later authorities agreed that lekatchilah the blessing must precede the wrapping — that is, at the time he holds it in his hand and wishes to wrap himself in it, for that is passing immediately to their performance. And one should not recite it while the tallis is still folded, for that is passing over and passing over.
