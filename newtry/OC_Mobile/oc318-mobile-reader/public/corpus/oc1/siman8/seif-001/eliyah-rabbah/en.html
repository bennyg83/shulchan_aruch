He should wrap himself, etc. This implies that one does not bless before wrapping, but at the time of wrapping, before he has wrapped himself fully, as with tefillin in siman 25, seif 8. In Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 88, he wrote that the common practice is to bless before wrapping; nevertheless, it implies there that one should not do so, but rather as we have said. Also regarding tefillin, the Bach wrote not like the common practice. The Levush wrote that one recites the blessing ‘betzitzis’ with a sheva under the beis, but the Bach at the end of siman 24 wrote with a patach under the beis. So too, in Sefer Orchos Chaim my teacher and master Rasham, of blessed memory, wrote at length with reasoned arguments that it should be read with a patach. This also seems correct, for we find most blessings phrased in the definite form, such as ‘upon the milah,’ and ‘Who gives the Torah,’ end quote. But he did not note that here the definite form is not applicable, since we do not have techeiles, as the Levush wrote. Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah also wrote that the practice of precise grammarians is like the Levush, and so is my practice.
