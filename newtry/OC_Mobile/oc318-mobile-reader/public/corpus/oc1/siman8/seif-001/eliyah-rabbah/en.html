[1] He shall wrap himself, etc. It implies one does not bless before wrapping but at the time of wrapping, before he wraps himself properly — as with tefillin, siman 25, seif 8. In the responsum of Beit Yaakov, siman 88, he wrote that people are accustomed to bless before wrapping; nevertheless it seems there one should not do so except as we say. Also regarding tefillin the Bach wrote not as people practice. The Levush wrote they bless on tzitzis with a shur under the beis; but the Bach at the end of siman 24 wrote with a patach under the beis. So too in Aruchos Chaim my teacher Rabbeinu Rashi wrote at length reasons to read with a patach. It also seems most blessings we find in established language, like "on the milah," "Who gives the Torah," until here — and he did not notice that here established language does not apply, since we have no techelet, as the Levush wrote. Also the remnants of Knesset HaGedolah wrote the custom of careful people follows the Levush; and so is my custom.
