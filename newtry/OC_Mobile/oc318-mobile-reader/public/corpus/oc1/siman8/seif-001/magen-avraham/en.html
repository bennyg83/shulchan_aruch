“He wraps himself,” etc. Immediately after netilas yadayim (Tur).

“Standing.” Meaning the berachah and the wrapping should both be while standing. This requires examination: Yoreh Deah 328:28 seems to permit blessing while seated — and that is a complete mishnah; one could say that separating challah is not so much a mitzvah act, for one only fixes his food, similar to slaughter, as I wrote in siman 585. Those who place the folded tallis draped around the neck on the shoulders do not fulfill their obligation (Mishnah Berurah). In writings it is stated that one should wrap down to the mouth, like Ishmaelite wrapping, for four amot, and afterward wrap down to the chest, front and back; so too the Levush — that the main wrapping is on the body — see siman 10, seif 10.
