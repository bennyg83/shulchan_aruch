“He wraps himself,” etc. Immediately after netilas yadayim (Tur).
“Standing.” Meaning the berachah and wrapping should both be while standing. This requires study: Yoreh Deah 328:28 seems to allow blessing while seated, and that is a complete change of subject—perhaps separating challah is not as much a mitzvah act, for one only fixes his food, similar to slaughter, as I wrote siman 585. Those who place the folded tallis draped around the neck on the shoulders do not fulfill their obligation (Mishnah Berurah). Some writings say one should wrap to the mouth like Arabs’ wrap for four cubits, then wrap to the chest front and back; so too Taz that the main wrapping is on the body—see siman 10:10.
