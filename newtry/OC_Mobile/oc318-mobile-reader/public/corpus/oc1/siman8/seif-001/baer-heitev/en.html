He should wrap himself in tzitzis. Immediately after washing the hands. Tur.
Standing. The same applies to the wrapping, that it should be done standing. In Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 29, he ruled that one recites the blessing after wrapping, unlike the common custom; see there. Yad Aharon and Bnei Chiya disagreed with him. Yad Aharon concluded that after one has placed the tallis upon himself, even if he has not yet placed the tzitzis in his hand but merely cast them behind him, he may recite the blessing; see there. See Rama MiPano, siman 102, and Responsa Darchei Noam, siman 3.
