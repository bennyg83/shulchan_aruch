He shall wrap himself in tzitzit. Immediately after netilat yadayim. Tur.

Standing. The same applies to the wrapping—it should be done standing. In the responsum of Beit Yaakov, siman 29, he ruled that one blesses after the wrapping, unlike the common custom, see above; and Yad Aharon and Bnei Chayya disagreed with him. Yad Aharon raised that after he placed the tallit on himself—even though he did not place the tzitziyot in his hand but only threw them behind him—he may bless, see above; see Rama of Pano, siman 102. And in the responsum of Darchei Noam, siman 3.
