(Seif 1) And he blesses standing, etc. In my view: see in the Magen Avraham what he challenged from challah, that one blesses seated. To reconcile: one may say the Sages enacted that regarding the matter of the mitzvah, so is the blessing — if the mitzvah is seated, the blessing too is seated; but if the mitzvah is standing, the blessing too shall be standing. In particular according to what the Radbaz said, brought in the Magen Avraham [siman 484]: where something is biblical and rabbinic together we are strict even on the rabbinic part lest they treat rabbinic laws lightly; so too here, where the mitzvah is standing — if he blesses seated they will treat rabbinic blessings lightly; therefore they enacted that the blessing too be standing.
