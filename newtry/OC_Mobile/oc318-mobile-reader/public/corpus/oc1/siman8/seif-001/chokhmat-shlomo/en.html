Seif 1. And he should recite the blessing while standing, etc. Note: See Magen Avraham, who asked from challah, where one recites the blessing while sitting. This is not difficult, for one may say that the Sages enacted that the blessing follows the nature of the mitzvah. If the mitzvah is performed sitting, the blessing is also sitting; if the mitzvah is performed standing, the blessing is also standing. Especially according to what the Radbaz wrote, brought in Magen Avraham, siman 184, that where a Torah matter and a D'Rabbanan matter are together, we are stringent even regarding the D'Rabbanan matter so that people not come to treat D'Rabbanan matters lightly. If so, here too, where the mitzvah is standing, if he blesses while sitting he may come to treat D'Rabbanan blessings lightly. Therefore they enacted that the blessing also be standing.
