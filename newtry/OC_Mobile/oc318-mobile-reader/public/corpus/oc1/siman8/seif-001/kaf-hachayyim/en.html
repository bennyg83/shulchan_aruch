1) [Se'if 1] He should wrap himself in tzitzis, etc. This should be done immediately after netilas yadayim. Tur; Magen Avraham 1; Chayei Adam 11:1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 1. Machatzis HaShekel explains that the reason is so that he immediately wraps himself in a mitzvah. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, writes that it is so he does not walk four amos without tzitzis; Chayei Adam there writes likewise. One should not, however, don tzitzis before netilas yadayim, because it is proper to recite the berachah immediately before performing the mitzvah; see se'if 10. According to our practice of reciting 'to wrap oneself' over the tallis gadol with intent to exempt the tallis katan, this is not necessary, and he could first don it before washing his hands. Yet Magen Avraham, siman 4:1, writes in the name of Seder HaYom that one should not touch his garments before netilas yadayim. Accordingly, he should not don the tallis until after washing. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 1. See Levushei Serad and our comments above in siman 4:6. Even if one rises before daylight, he should wrap himself immediately; when daylight comes, he should touch the tzitzis and recite the berachah, as stated in se'if 16. Machatzis HaShekel. According to our practice, when reciting 'to wrap oneself' over the tallis gadol, he should intend to exempt this one, as will be explained there with God's help. Although our custom, following the Arizal, is to sleep in a tallis katan, as will be explained in siman 21:3 with God's help, this ruling remains relevant if one happened not to sleep in his tallis, or in places where people cannot sleep in their clothing because of fleas or the like. See Shaarei Teshuvah 1.
