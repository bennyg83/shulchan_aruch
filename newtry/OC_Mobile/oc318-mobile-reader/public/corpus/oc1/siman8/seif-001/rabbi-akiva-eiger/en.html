Se’if 1: “And he should recite the blessing while standing.” See the Taz below, siman 643, se’if katan 2, who writes that he should recite the blessing after wrapping himself. Eliyah Rabbah writes that the blessing should be recited while wrapping oneself. In Responsa Shaagat Aryeh, siman 32, he writes that the blessing should be recited before wrapping oneself, when he is holding the tallit in his hand in order to wrap himself in it.
