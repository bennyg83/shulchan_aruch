One blesses standing, and all blessings over mitzvot must be recited standing, except for blessings of enjoyment; therefore the custom is to stand when reciting the blessing to sit in the sukkah, even though there is no proof for this—it is a reminder: in the verse “Heaven is forever; you shall stand,” the words “omer,” “tzitzit,” and “tefillin” appear. Or these words hint at the acronym “atzat le’alem”—seven matters done standing: “atzat”—that is, tefillin, tzitzit, and tekiah; “le’alem”—kiddush levanah, omer, lulav, and milah.
