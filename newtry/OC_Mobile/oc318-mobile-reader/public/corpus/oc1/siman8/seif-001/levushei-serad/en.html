{Rama: Magen Avraham, seif kama 1} After netilat yadayim. Apparently the reason that before netilat yadayim we [wait] was so that he could bless immediately before performance—see seif 10; and according to this his words apply only when lying naked—see siman 4, seif 23. And one can say another reason: because he may not touch his garments—see Magen Avraham, siman 4, seif kama 1, in the name of Sedeh Yehoshua; and although he wrote there that in the Gemara it does not imply so, but that touching garments is permitted—one can say that this law of the daily order is a stringency of the later authorities; and from this, by the Gemara's law they would don tzitzit before netilat yadayim when not lying naked. And it seems necessary that this is so, for these words of Magen Avraham were not practiced in the days of the Gemara—for otherwise this difficulty that Magen Avraham raised above on the daily order—from there there is also a difficulty on himself, namely on these words: how did he write "after netilat yadayim," when in the Gemara cited it is explained that he donned all his garments before netilat yadayim? If so, he certainly donned tzitzit first according to Magen Avraham himself in seif kama 13, who agrees with the writings that one dons the tallit katan under the garments; therefore it seems as stated, that his words here depend on the law of Sedeh Yehoshua, and in the days of the Gemara they did not practice this prohibition of touching garments, and therefore they donned tzitzit before netilat yadayim as well. And it seems for the same reason the author did not write these matters, because he follows his view in siman 46, where he brought the Gemara's words for halacha—it is clear he does not hold like Sedeh Yehoshua; from this he may don before netilat yadayim and bless when not lying naked. And one should not challenge the author in siman 46: how did he write there anonymously that one dons his garments before netilat yadayim and did not explain that when lying naked he cannot do so because of the tallit katan? For this is the author's view in seif 11, that one dons it over his garments; therefore even when lying naked it does not apply what siman 46 says, that one dons all his garments before netilat yadayim and the tzitzit truly afterward over his garments—and this requires study.
