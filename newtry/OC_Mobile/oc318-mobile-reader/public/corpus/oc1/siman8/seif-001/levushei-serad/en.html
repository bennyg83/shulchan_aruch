Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. After netilas yadayim. At first glance, the reason it is not before netilas yadayim is so that he can bless truly before performance; see seif 10. According to this, his words apply only when one slept unclothed; see siman 4, seif 23. One may suggest another reason: because he cannot touch his garments; see Magen Avraham, siman 4, seif katan 1, in the name of Seder HaYom. Although he wrote there that this is not implied by the Gemara, rather that it is permitted to touch his clothing, one may say that this ruling of Seder HaYom is a stringency of the later authorities. Accordingly, according to the law of the Gemara they would put on tzitzis before netilas yadayim when one had not slept unclothed. It seems to me that this must be so, for these words of Magen Avraham were not practiced in the days of the Gemara. Otherwise, from the very Gemara with which Magen Avraham above challenged Seder HaYom, there would also be a challenge against himself, namely against these words, for how could he write after netilas yadayim when in that Gemara it is clear that he put on all his garments before netilas yadayim. Thus he necessarily also put on the tzitzis first, according to Magen Avraham himself in seif katan 13, where he agrees with the writings that one wears the small tallis under the clothes. Therefore it seems as I wrote, that his words here depend on the ruling of Seder HaYom, and in the days of the Gemara they did not observe this prohibition of touching clothing, and therefore they also wore tzitzis before netilas yadayim. It seems to me that for this reason the Mechaber did not write these words, because he follows his own view, for in siman 46 he brought the words of the Gemara as halacha, showing that he does not hold like Seder HaYom. Accordingly, one may put it on before netilas yadayim and bless, when he did not sleep unclothed. One should not challenge the Mechaber in siman 46, how he wrote generally that one puts on his clothing before netilas yadayim and did not explain that if he slept unclothed he cannot do so because of the small tallis. This is not difficult, for the Mechaber follows his view in seif 11 that one wears it over his clothing. Therefore, even if one slept unclothed, siman 46 works well: he puts on all his garments before netilas yadayim, and the tzitzis afterward over his clothes.
