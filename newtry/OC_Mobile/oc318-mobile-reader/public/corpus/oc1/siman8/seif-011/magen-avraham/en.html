“And he should see them.” Some writings say specifically a small tallis under his garments—see there. It appears to me the tzitzis must be outside, unlike those who tuck them into the pockets—this is what the Gemara means, “there are no tzitzis unless they protrude.” Yet Tosafos Berachos 18a implies post facto one fulfills by tucking; one could answer that for the dead who are obligated it does not apply and “you have seen him” is different from the living; still if for the living one does not fulfill thus, a wicked person would mock the righteous—see siman 23:2. Therefore it appears to me that men walking among gentiles fulfill this way, but at the time of the berachah they should be visible for four cubits—see siman 2.
