Seif katan 12. Even though he already wore it. Do not say that since he already wore it, he should not bless “to wrap,” which is future language, but rather “on the mitzvah of tzitzis,” which is past language. See Taz, seif katan 7. Therefore Magen Avraham wrote that nevertheless he blesses “to wrap.” The simple reason may be according to the words of the Beit Yosef, brought in Taz, seif katan 9, that it refers to what he continues to fulfill afterward. Therefore the future language applies well, and “to wrap” is like saying “to be wrapped.” According to this, the Taz’s question in seif katan 9 against the Beit Yosef is resolved.
