{Rama: Seif kaf-bet.} Although he already donned it—do not think that since he already donned it he should not bless "to wrap," which is future tense, but rather on the mitzvah of tzitzit, which is past tense; see Taz, seif kama 7—for this Magen Avraham wrote nevertheless one blesses "to wrap," and the reason according to its simple meaning: one can say according to Beit Yosef brought in Taz, seif kama 9, that it refers to what he will fulfill afterward; therefore the language "to wrap" applies properly, and it would be as if he said "to be wrapped"; and through this the difficulty of Taz, seif kama 9, on Beit Yosef is resolved.
