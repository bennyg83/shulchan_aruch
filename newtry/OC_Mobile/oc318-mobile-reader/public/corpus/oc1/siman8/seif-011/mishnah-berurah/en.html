(25) Over his clothing. In the writings it is stated that the tallis katan is under his clothing. The Magen Avraham wrote that in any event the tzitzis must be outside, unlike those who tuck them into the corners. However, those who go among the non-Jews fulfill their obligation in this way. Nevertheless, at the time of the blessing they should be exposed for the time it takes to walk four amos.
(26) He should see it. As it is written, “and you shall see it and remember,” etc. Those people who place the tzitzis in their trousers not only hide their eyes from what is written, “and you shall see it and remember,” etc., but also disgrace the mitzvah of Hashem, may He be blessed, and they will in the future have to give judgment for this. What they say, that they go among the non-Jews, for this it would be enough to place the tzitzis inside the corner. If they had some gift from a flesh-and-blood king upon which the king’s name was engraved, how much would they adorn themselves with it before people always; all the more so with tzitzis, which alludes to the Name of the King of kings, the Holy One, blessed is He, as below in siman 24, seif 5. How honored is the person who bears His Name upon himself! Chazal said: one who is careful with the mitzvah of tzitzis merits to see the face of the Shechinah. It also implies from the verses that the Jews who remain at the time of the end will be distinguished by this mitzvah, as it is written, “Ten men from all the languages of the nations shall come and grasp the corner of a Jewish man,” etc. (Zechariah 8:23). Chazal said about this in Shabbos 32b: whoever is careful with the mitzvah of tzitzis merits that two thousand eight hundred servants serve him, etc.
