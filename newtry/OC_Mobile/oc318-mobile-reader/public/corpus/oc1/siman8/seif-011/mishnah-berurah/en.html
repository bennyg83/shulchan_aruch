And the Sages said: "He who observes the mitzvah of tzitzit, is justified and sees the face of the Shekinah." And from the scriptures, the Jews who will remain until the end of time will be distinguished by this mitzvah, as it is written: "And ten men from all the tongues of the nations came and held a Jewish man under their wing" etc. (Zechariah 8:23).
