{Rama: Ibid. seif katan 16}And it is not comparable to what is written in seif 14. That is, he stated anonymously in seif 14 that even if he did not interrupt in anything else after the blessing and removed and put on — he must bless — and this is because he did not have intent for this wearing at the time of the blessing. But if he removed in the middle of the day and returned and put it on immediately — even though he had intent for this at the time of the blessing — nevertheless from the time of the blessing until this wearing he interrupted. If so, the blessing cannot exempt this wearing, as I explained.
