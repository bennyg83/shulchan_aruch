There, se’if katan 16: “This is not comparable to what is stated in siman 14.” That is, the ruling in se’if 14 is stated without qualification: even if he did not interrupt with anything after the blessing, if he removed the garment and put it on again, he must recite another blessing. The reason is that, when he recited the blessing, he did not intend this later wearing. If, however, he removed it in the middle of the day and immediately put it on again, then even though he had intended this when reciting the blessing, the interval from the blessing until this wearing nevertheless constitutes an interruption. The original blessing therefore cannot cover this wearing. So it appears in my humble opinion.
