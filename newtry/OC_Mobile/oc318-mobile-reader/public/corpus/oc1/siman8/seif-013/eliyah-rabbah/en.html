[13] He must bless, etc. The Bach's view: even if other garments he intended to wear were before him at the time of the blessing, he must bless on what was brought to him since he did not intend for this — unlike fruits in siman 206 and slaughter in Yoreh Deah siman 19, where there is a fixed practice for slaughter and eating, which is not so for donning. But the Taz and Olas Tamid challenged him that slaughter and donning are equivalent and he need not bless. It is difficult: if so, even if it was not before him, if they bring him more of the same kind he need not bless, as the Rama ruled in Yoreh Deah there — even though the Taz wrote there on the Rama that Beit Yosef wrote Ran, Raavad, and Rambam hold like the Rosh, and perforce the Rama's words are lone against them, until here. I already wrote in Eliyah Rabbah that Machaneh Ephraim erred — Beit Yosef did not write thus; rather in slaughter there is a law of changing one's mind, as he brought proof there that it is unlike clearing the table where one does not repeat the blessing — see there; but regarding the law of siman 1 he did not write at all — see there I brought up per the Rama's words. Even so it is difficult the Rama should rule here thus — the main view is the Bach's; the reason may be said: in slaughter, as long as more is before him to be brought to slaughter, not so in tzitzis where with one garment the mitzvah is fulfilled — so also implies the Bach and Perishah to Yoreh Deah, siman 13. Magen Avraham wrote: initially he should not interrupt, for it is forbidden to cause an unnecessary blessing.
