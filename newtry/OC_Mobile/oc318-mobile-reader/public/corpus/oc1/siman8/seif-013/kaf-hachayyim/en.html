13) [Se'if 3] Small talleisim, etc. Ateres Zekeinim writes: The shoulder sections should be somewhat wide, and the garment somewhat large, so that it can cover one's head and the majority of his body from behind; otherwise it is not called a garment. Terumas HaDeshen, cited by Beis Yosef, writes: One who wears a tallis with a neck opening should make it large enough to cover his head and the majority of his body from behind. At the time of the berachah, he should wrap it over his head, cast it behind himself, and remain wrapped this way for at least the time required to walk four amos. He should then draw it forward over his head so that it hangs from his neck in the tallis's neck opening. Shalmei Tzibbur, page 32. See siman 16 and our comments there, with God's help.
