(Seif katan 13) And he should remember, etc. It seems to me that this is required, etc. What is written in the Gemara, Menachos 41b, and Rashi explains there: hanging and emerging slightly from the corner. If so, it refers to the required length of the threads. But the Magen Avraham explains that it refers to not inserting them into the corners, but leaving them outside. However, from the language of Tosafot, Berachos 18a, s.v. lemachar, where he wrote in the name of the Ritzba that in Menachos we say that at the time when the dead are dressed in shrouds we certainly place tzitzis for them on their tallis, for otherwise it would be mocking the poor; and in Maseches Semachos we find that Abba Shaul instructed his sons to remove the tzitzis from his tallis after his death. To avoid entering into a dispute, the Ritzba was accustomed not to remove them, but rather to tie them and fasten them within the corner, for if they are obligated in tzitzis, they have them, and if not, they are covered. Thus, even if you say that they are obligated, it suffices that they have them, even when inserted within the corner.
It does not apply to them, and you shall see, etc. For the reason that one should not insert them into the corner is in order to fulfill, and you shall see it and remember.
Therefore it seems to me, etc. Another explanation: see what I wrote in seif 3, where it is explained that the main matter depends on the time of wearing, and afterward the time needed to walk four amos.
