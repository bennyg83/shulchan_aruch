(Seif kama 13) "And he shall remember," etc. And it appears above that it is required, etc., as stated in the Gemara in Menachot 41b; and Rashi explained, and these are his words: "hanging and protruding from the corner something," end quote—and if so, it refers to the measure of the length of the threads. But Magen Avraham explained that it refers to not tucking them inside the corners but leaving them outside. However, from the language of Tosafot in Berachot 18a, s.v. "tomorrow," etc., who wrote in the name of Ritzba: in Menachot we say at this hour, when they clothe the dead in shrouds, surely we throw tzitzit on their tallitot—for otherwise one would mock the poor; and in tractate Semachot we find that Abba Shaul commanded his sons to remove the tzitzit from his tallit after his death. And to remove himself from dispute, Ritzba was accustomed not to permit them except to tie and fasten them inside the corner—for if they are obligated in tzitzit, they have them; and if not, they would be covered, end quote—thus even if you say they are obligated, it suffices with what they have even tucked inside the corner.

Its language is "upon them—and you shall see," etc. For the reason that one should not tuck them inside the corner is in order to fulfill "and you shall see" and "and you shall remember."

Therefore it appears above, etc. Alternatively, see what is written in seif 3, where it is explained that the essence depends on the time of donning, and afterward for the distance of four amot.
