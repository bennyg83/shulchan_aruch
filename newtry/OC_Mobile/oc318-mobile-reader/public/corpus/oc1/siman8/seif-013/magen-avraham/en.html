“It counts as an interruption.” It appears to me: if both rooms are one house it is not an interruption from room to room—as siman 178 and Eliyah Rabbah; if he left his house and immediately returned he must bless, for things that do not require a berachah afterward—change of place is an interruption—as siman 178. Maharam’s view appears to me: tzitzis is different, since it is still on him it is not a place-change interruption, unlike food where the first portion is gone; even though Taz wrote he presumably removed his tallis after coming home and thus did not change place after removing—as I wrote—Eliyah Rabbah compared this to blessings of Shema, but they are not comparable, for the whole day is one mitzvah context where interruption does not apply—see siman 104.
