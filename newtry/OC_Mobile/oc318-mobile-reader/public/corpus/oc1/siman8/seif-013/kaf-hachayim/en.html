13) [Seif 3] Small tallitot, etc. Etz Chayyim wrote whose language is: the shoulders must be somewhat wide and somewhat large so that it cover his head and most of him from behind—for otherwise it is not called a garment—until here. And the language of Terumat HaDeshen brought in Beit Yosef: the practice with a tallit that has a neck opening—one makes it somewhat large so that it can cover his head and most of him from behind; and at the time of the blessing he wraps it on his head and drapes it behind him and stands thus wrapped for at least the measure of walking four amos; and afterward he draws it from over his head to before him so that it hangs on his neck in the neck opening of the tallit—until here. Shaarei Tziyon, 32. And see siman 16 and our words there, be-siyata diShmaya.
