One who slept in his tallit at night must recite a blessing over it in the morning. Some say that he need not recite a blessing, and this is the primary ruling. It is good to touch the tzitzit at the time of the blessing. A person should look at the tzitzit. This provides a great benefit to the soul, helping prevent sin from coming to his hand and enabling him to attain great benefit. Rabbi Yitzchak Luria, according to the kabbalistic truth.
