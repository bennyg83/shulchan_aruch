One who sleeps in his tallit at night—some say he must bless, and some say he need not bless; and the latter is the essential ruling. It is good to handle the tzitziyot at the time of the blessing. A person must look at the tzitziyot; this is of great benefit to the soul, so that sin may not befall him, and also to attain great benefit (Ri'l in the Shulchan Aruch, as he holds).
