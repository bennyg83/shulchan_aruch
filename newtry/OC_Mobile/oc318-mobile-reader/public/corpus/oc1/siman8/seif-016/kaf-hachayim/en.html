And he stands thus wrapped, etc.—and specifically in a garment made like one square piece with the neck opening in the middle; if so, when he wraps in its length, his head and the width of his body constitute two tzitzis on this side and two tzitzis on that side. But if the garment is sewn—if so, when he wraps in its length around his head and the width of his body, then all four tzitzis come on one side, and it is not correct to do so; therefore he should don it in its normal way and not wrap with it at all on his head, and he should not bless on it for wrapping in tzitzit but bless on the mitzvah of tzitzit—Razah, letter 7.
