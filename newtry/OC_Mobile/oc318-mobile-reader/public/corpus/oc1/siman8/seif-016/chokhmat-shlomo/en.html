Seif 16. One who sleeps in his tallis, etc. Note: If at the time of the blessing he had in mind not to exempt the garment except until midnight or the like, whether he must bless afterward or not. See Magen Avraham, Hilchos Sukkah, siman 639, seif katan 17, where his view is clear that he need not bless again. But in Or Zarua I saw that he wrote that perhaps this is indeed so. Therefore, for practical law this now requires study.
