16) There: He should remain wrapped this way, etc. This applies specifically to a garment made as a single square piece with the neck opening in the center. When he wraps its length around his head and its width around his body, two tzitzis are on one side and two on the other. If the garment is sewn, however, then when he wraps its length around his head and its width around his body, all four tzitzis will come to one side, which is improper. He should therefore put it on in its ordinary manner, not wrap it over his head at all, and not recite 'to wrap oneself in tzitzis,' but rather 'concerning the mitzvah of tzitzis.' Shulchan Aruch HaRav 7.
