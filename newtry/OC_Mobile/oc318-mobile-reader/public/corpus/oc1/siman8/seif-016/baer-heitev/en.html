In the morning. The Bach and Taz disagreed and ruled that one need not bless in the morning {Rama: (since daytime clothing is obligatory even at night per the Rosh; if so, he never has a time of exemption at all—and likewise in Sefer Zichron to the Gaon Mahari HaCohen)}. Magen Avraham wrote that it is good to exempt it with a tallit gadol; and see Knesset HaGedolah, who elaborated. It is written in the derashah of Maharam Mintz the Elder: by day one should not remove the tallit katan from upon him, but only cover it at the time of sleep; and when he dons it again he need not recite a blessing—and by this one resolves all doubts.
