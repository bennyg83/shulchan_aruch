In the morning. The Bach and Taz disagreed and ruled that one need not bless in the morning, since according to the Rosh a daytime garment is obligated even at night, and if so there was no time of exemption at all; and so wrote Sefer Zikaron by the gaon Mahari HaKohen. The Magen Avraham wrote that it is good to exempt it with the tallis gadol. See Knesses HaGedolah, who discusses this at length. It is written in Derashas Maharam Mintz: one who sleeps during the day should not remove the tallis katan from upon himself, but should cover himself with it while sleeping, and when he wears it he need not bless; by this he leaves all doubts.
