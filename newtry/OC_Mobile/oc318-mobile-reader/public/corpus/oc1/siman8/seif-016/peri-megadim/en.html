It is good. See Taz, who concludes that one should not bless at all in the morning. This implies even though there is something like a double doubt: perhaps the halachah follows the Rambam that a day garment at night is exempt, and perhaps like the responsum of the Rosh; nevertheless, he does not bless. However, in the responsum of the Rosh he wrote that one need not remove it in the morning, which implies that if he nevertheless wants to, he may remove it in the morning, wrap himself, and bless. Perhaps it is forbidden to cause an unnecessary blessing. Regarding the blessing over Torah, there is no exempt time, yet one blesses every morning, because our intent is only to exempt a twenty-four-hour period; see siman 47, seif 12. Here he is wearing the tallis and one should not bless, it seems to me. There is no need to elaborate further.
