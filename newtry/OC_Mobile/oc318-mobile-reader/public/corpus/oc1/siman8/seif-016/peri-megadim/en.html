Good — Taz: And he concludes one does not bless at all in the morning; and it implies even though there is something like a double doubt — perhaps the halachah is like Rambam that day garment at night is exempt, and perhaps like Rosh's responsum — nevertheless one does not bless. However in Rosh's responsum he wrote one need not remove in the morning — implying that if he wishes, he may remove it in the morning and wrap and bless; and it is possible to cause a blessing that is not needed — forbidden. And the blessing on Torah has no exempt time and one blesses every morning — our intent is only to exempt from "above" — see siman 47:34, for here clothing is a tallit and one need not bless, in my view. And not to extend further.
