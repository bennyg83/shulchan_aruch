And it is proper. See Taz, who brought the second explanation of the Beis Yosef, that he was concerned for the view of the Geonim that perhaps we require wrapping like the wrapping of the Yishmaelim. The Taz was not satisfied with this, because Baal HaItur holds that it is not necessary; therefore he explains in the opposite manner, that Baal HaItur follows his own view and says that it is proper that one not have his head uncovered from a hat even while wrapped in the tallis, lest he remove it afterward, since wrapping is not necessary, etc. What he wrote in the name of Maharshal from the Midrash, ‘I did not trouble,’ see Eliyah Rabbah, letter 4, that it is comparable to ‘I did not trouble.’ Regarding standing, there is no prohibition; see there. Nevertheless, it is difficult, for it is forbidden to recite Shema with an uncovered head because of mentioning Hashem’s Name, and even to learn with an uncovered head is forbidden. See siman 74 in Taz, letter 2, and siman 91, seif 4, in Taz and Magen Avraham there.
