(Seif 10) If he wears, etc. In my view: see in the Taz what he wrote to reconcile the Beit Yosef's question from a convert — that initially the person is not fit, as with immersion of a convert, etc. In truth, after Mahari Chagiz, this is not comparable: for a convert it is impossible in any way to be fit initially, therefore it is considered "before performance"; not so here, for it is possible he will not wear it until after he washes his hands and blesses immediately before performance — and understand. It is correct and proper that the Beit Yosef challenged well; examine carefully.
