3) There: He should wrap himself in tzitzis and recite the berachah, etc. This implies that one does not recite the berachah before beginning to wrap himself, but while wrapping, before he is fully wrapped, as with tefillin. Eliyah Rabbah 2 writes that Responsa Beis Yaakov 88 implies this; see there. Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur, writes that after placing the tallis upon himself, but before taking the tzitzis in his hand (having merely thrown them behind him) he may recite the berachah; see there. Chida, Machazik Berachah 1, however, writes in the name of Shaagas Aryeh 32 that it is proper to recite the berachah before wrapping, when one holds the tallis and is about to wrap himself, because this is immediately before performance. He writes that this is the custom, unlike Responsa Beis Yaakov. Kesher Gudal 2:5; Maamar Mordechai 6; Ikrei HaDat 2:34 in the name of Shaagas Aryeh; and Tefillah LeDavid, page 5b, rule likewise, unlike Beis Yaakov. See also Zechirah LeChaim, Hilchos Berachos 9; Beis Yaakov 21 in the names of Shaagas Aryeh and Machazik Berachah, noting that Rokeach at the beginning of siman 361 agrees; Kerem Shlomo in the name of Shaagas Aryeh; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch 9:8; and Chayei Olam on Shulchan Aruch, se'if 1, who writes that this is implied by Magen Avraham. Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham 2, likewise writes that Magen Avraham implies this. See Pnei Yitzchak, maareches tes, paragraph 118, and Yafeh LaLev 1:3, who agrees and cites several additional later authorities. Shalmei Tzibbur discusses this at length and writes on page 32a that there is a straight path by which one can satisfy Taz and Beis Yaakov even while reciting the berachah beforehand with the tallis still in his hands: immediately after the berachah, he should place the entire tallis backward over his head and body at once. The berachah then unquestionably precedes performance. After putting it on this way, he should raise the corners over his neck and wrap himself in the manner of the Yishmaelim for the time required to walk four amos. He concludes: 'I have seen people of exemplary conduct among the prominent residents of Yerushalayim, may it be rebuilt and established, follow this practice.' Ben Ish Chai, first year, Bereishis 5, however, writes: 'After completing the berachah, wrap only your head in it, unlike those who consider themselves clever and first wrap their entire body. We have only the Arizal's words.' Nevertheless, the chassidim of Beis El, may their Rock and Redeemer protect them, who follow the Arizal, practice as Shalmei Tzibbur writes, because this matter is not stated explicitly in the Arizal's words; see Shaar HaKavanos, Derushei HaTzitzis. I, the servant, provided support for their practice in Responsa Be'er Mayim Chaim; see there.
