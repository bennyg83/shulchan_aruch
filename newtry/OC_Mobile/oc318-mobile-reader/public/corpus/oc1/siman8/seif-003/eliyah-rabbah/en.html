Its width according to his height, etc. The same applies if the length of the tallis is according to the height of the man, it is also valid and we have no concern, Bach. The shoulder sections should be made wide, so that the air on this side and on that side should not come and nullify it; so wrote the Agur and Mateh Moshe. The Bach wrote at the end of siman 11 that it is proper lechatchilah, for the choicest fulfillment of the mitzvah, to follow what HaItur wrote, that one should place the tzitzis through two holes and have them hang outside on one side. However, since it appears like arrogance, one should not change the visible tzitzis on the tallis he wears from the common practice. But on a tallis katan he should arrange it so, end quote. I likewise found that Maharai Luria, of blessed memory, did so. This means two adjacent holes like a tzeirei. See Magen Avraham, siman 11, s.k. 13.
