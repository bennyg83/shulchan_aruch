(5) Small tallitos — provided they have the measure that a nine-year-old child can cover his head and most of his body in them, and an adult of thirteen years would in any case not be ashamed to go out in it casually in the street, as below in siman 16; see there in Mishna Berurah.

(6) One fulfills with them — for nowadays all our garments are worn as coverings, and atifah is not alluded to in the Torah.

(7) Wrapped — in order to fulfill [the requirement] per the opinions that hold one specifically needs atifah (wrapping). And it appears that those who are accustomed not to bless at all on the small tallit, but only to exempt it with the blessing on the large tallit, need not wrap in it at all. And see Magen Avraham and Biur HaGra.[1]

(8) And afterward — for the essential stringency is at the time of wearing.
