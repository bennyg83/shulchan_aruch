(5) Small talleisim. This is when they have the required measure, such that a child of nine years can cover his head and most of his body with it, and an adult of thirteen years would at least not be embarrassed to go out with it occasionally in the street, as below in siman 16; see there in Mishnah Berurah.
(6) One fulfills with them. For nowadays all our garments are worn in the manner of covering, and wrapping is not hinted at in the Torah.
(7) Wrapped. This is also to satisfy the opinion of those who hold that actual wrapping is required. It appears that those who have the custom not to bless at all on the tallis katan, but instead exempt it with the blessing over the tallis gadol, need not wrap themselves in it at all. See Magen Avraham and Biur HaGra.
(8) And afterward. For the main concern is at the time of wearing.
