(e) Small tallits - and he who has a rule that a nine-year-old child should cover his head and most of his head with it, and a thirteen-year-old adult should not be ashamed to wear it randomly on the street, and as follows in the 16th sign, refer to the Mishnah Berura there.
(f) We go out in them - dahaydana all our covers through covering them, and wrapping is not a hint of an auraita.
(g) Wrapped up - in order to get out of the way of opinions that think they are actually wrapped up. And it seems that it is customary not to bless a small tallit at all, only to dismiss it with the blessing of a large tallit, there is no need to wrap it at all. And refer to Magan Avraham and the explanation of the Gra.[1]
(h) And then - the main thing is to be careful when wearing it.
