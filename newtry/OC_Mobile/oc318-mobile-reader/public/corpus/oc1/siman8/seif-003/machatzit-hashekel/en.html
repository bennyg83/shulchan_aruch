(section 3) [regarding] a tallis, etc. — see siman 27.

At all events, etc. — as it is written, “And all the peoples of the earth shall see that the name of Heaven is called upon you, and they shall fear you.” And it is taught: R. Eliezer says — these are the tefillin on the head. Therefore they must be dignified; however, the intention is that the tallis should cover the entire head as well as the tefillin, and the tallis should be like a roof over the tefillin — but nevertheless it should be visible from in front of him.

In Kiddushin 29b we say: R. Chiya praised Rav Huna before Rav Hamnuna, saying he is a great man. He said to him: when he comes into your hands, bring him to me. When he came before him, he saw that he did not cover his head with a tallis. He said to him: why? He said: because I have not married, etc. And on 8a it states, etc., that Rav Kahana would take a sudra at pidyon haben — for me it is worth five sela. And R. Eliezer — specifically R. Kahana, who was a great man and needed a cloak to cover his head; but an ordinary person, no.
