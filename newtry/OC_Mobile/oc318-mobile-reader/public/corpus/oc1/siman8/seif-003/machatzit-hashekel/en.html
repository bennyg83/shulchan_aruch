Seif katan 3. With a tallis, etc.; see the end of siman 27.
That in any event, etc. For it is written: “All the peoples of the earth shall see that the Name of Hashem is called upon you, and they shall fear you.” It was taught: Rabbi Eliezer says, these are the tefillin of the head. Therefore they must be exposed. Rather, the intent is that the tallis cover the whole head and also the tefillin, and the tallis should be like a roof over the tefillin, but nevertheless the tefillin should be visible from the front.
In Kiddushin 29, we say that Rabbi Chisda praised Rav Hamnuna to Rav Huna, saying that he was a great man. He said to him: when he comes to you, bring him to me. When he came before him, he saw that he did not cover his head with a tallis. He said to him: why? He said: because I am not married, etc. On page 8 it says that Rav Kahana took a scarf for redemption of the firstborn, saying: to me it is worth five selaim. Rabbi Ashi said: specifically Rav Kahana, who was a great man and needed a cloak to cover his head, but an ordinary person, no.
