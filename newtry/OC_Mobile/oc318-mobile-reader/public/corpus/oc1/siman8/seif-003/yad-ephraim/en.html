In the words “until they are all cut off” — it is not precise: if enough remains for a knot, even if they are all cut off it is valid; and if not enough remains for a knot, even if one thread alone was cut off — that is, if two heads were cut off, one on this side and one on that side — it is invalid, even per the Rosh, as explained in siman 12. It is possible that this is a textual error, and it should read that he holds they do not invalidate it even if they are all cut off; and he means that nevertheless it is valid if enough remains, and lest they break and not enough remains — we are not concerned. Unlike our view: if the heads were also cut, even if enough remains for a knot it is invalid, for we are stringent like the view of Rabbeinu Tam; therefore one should be concerned and require inspection. See there — in the Rosh’s wording that the Beit Yosef brought, who wrote as follows: furthermore, tzitzis do not break easily, and people are accustomed to repair them even before they reach invalidity, etc. — see there.
