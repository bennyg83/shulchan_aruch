He shall wrap himself in tzitzit and bless, etc.—it implies one does not bless before wrapping but at the time of wrapping, before he wraps himself properly as with tefillin—Eliyah Rabbah, seif kama 2; and he wrote that so it appears in Tur Beit Yaakov, siman 88—see there. And in sefer 11 in Hagahat Tashbetz he wrote that after he placed the tallit on him—but did not place the tzitzit in his hand, rather threw them behind him—he may bless—see there. However the Chida in Machazik Berachah, letter 1, wrote in the name of Shaagat Aryeh, siman 32, that it is proper to bless before wrapping, at the time he holds the tallit in his hand and wishes to wrap—for this is immediately before performance; and he wrote that such is the custom, and not as Beit Yaakov wrote. And so in his sefer 103, siman 2, letter 5; and so Maamar Mordechai, letter 6; and so Ikvei HaDat, siman 2, letter 34, in the name of Shaagat Aryeh—and he wrote that Rabbi Tefillah LeDavid on page 5:2b also holds thus, and not like Beit Yosef; and so HaZekan LeChayyim, Hilchos Berachos, letter 9; and so Beit Yosef Yashan, letter 21, in the name of Shaagat Aryeh and Machazik Berachah—and he wrote that so Rokeach wrote at the beginning of siman 361; and so Kitzur Shulchan Aruch in the name of Shaagat Aryeh; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 9, letter 8; and so Chayyei Olam on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, seif 1—that so it appears from the words of Magen Avraham—see there; and Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham, letter 2, wrote that so it appears from the words of Magen Avraham; and so Penei Yitzchak wrote; and Yafeh Lelev agreed in Chayei Adam, letter 3—and see there that he brought several later authorities who wrote thus. And see Rabbi Shaarei Tziyon who expanded on this and wrote on page 32:1a: there is a straight path before a person to satisfy also the view of Taz and Beit Yosef—even if he blesses first while the tallit is still in his hand, and that immediately upon the blessing he don the tallit entirely down his back on his head and body at once—for then he is immediately before performance without any hesitation; and after donning in this way he raises the corners on his neck and wraps like the Ishmaelite wrapping for the measure of walking four amos, etc.—and he concluded: and so I saw men of action in Jerusalem, may it be rebuilt speedily in our days, practice—until here. However the rabbi Ben Ish Chai wrote in his sefer Shana Aleph, Parashat Bereishit, letter 5, whose language is: after finishing the blessing wrap only your head in it, and not like those who are clever to wrap their whole body from the start—for we have only the words of the Arizal—until here. However the chassidim of Beit El who follow the Arizal practice as Shaarei Tziyon wrote, because this matter is not explicit in the words of the Arizal—as explained in Shaar HaKavanos, drashos on tzitzit; and I, the servant, relied on their words in responsum Be'er Mayim Chayyim—see there.
