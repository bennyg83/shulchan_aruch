Seif 3, "talleisim," etc. Although the text of the blessing is "to wrap," this is not exact but follows their custom. The Merciful One said "their garments" and "your covering," and every garment and covering is included. We say: a tallis with which a minor covers, etc., although an adult does not wrap his head and cover most of his body with it. We say "with which you cover" comes to include, etc., and do not say that the verse comes only for one who covers, etc.
"And it is good to place," etc. For this is the way of wrapping, as the Baal HaIttur and the Tur wrote: and the way of wrapping, etc.
"And he should stand," etc. And afterward, etc.; for the concern is only because of the blessing, since one is not obligated to wrap himself in a garment obligated in tzitzis, as written below in seif 17; see there.
