Abhat and refer to Mashat Binyamin SI MH road workers who place the tallit when it is folded and hang it around their necks on their shoulders in the matter that one end when folded with the tassel comes down in front of him from the right and the other end with the second tassel comes down from the left. Wrapping the tallit is a mitzvah that the tallit be on the head and not on their shoulders alone, and where the majority used to not cover their heads and are careful about the minority who cover their heads, they have to do according to their portion 3 Maravim P.S. And it is seen, that is, after the blessing while he is wrapped in it to pray, but when he blesses it and wraps himself in it immediately, then at that time he will cover his head with a tallit and not rely on throwing the tallit over his shoulders alone, and in this we should not observe the custom of the Marovim, which is a custom in error that they did not go out 18 Bracha Kmash in the name of the M.B. and K.C.
