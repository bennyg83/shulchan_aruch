For small tallitot, the shoulder straps need to be somewhat wide and the garment somewhat large so that it covers his head and most of his back; for if not, it is not called a garment of clothing called a sardakl. If it is open on both sides more than the majority visible to the eye, one may also recite a blessing on it to wrap oneself in tzitzit. One should not make slits in it below the belt, because of marit ayin.
