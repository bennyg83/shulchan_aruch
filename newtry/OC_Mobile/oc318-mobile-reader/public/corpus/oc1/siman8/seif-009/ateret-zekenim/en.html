Indeed, if he checked his talisman and placed it in a known place and found the same as they placed it in the case of a doubter, he checked it. It is proper that those tufts that come first to the side of the head should always be on the side of the head because they are raised in the holy place and there is no taking them down, and now the custom is to make a talit crown in order to recognize that it is the part of the head so that they do not change the top 'down' and the bottom 'up'.
