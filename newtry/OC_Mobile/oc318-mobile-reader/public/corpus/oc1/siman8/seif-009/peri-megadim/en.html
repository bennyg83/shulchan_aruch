He feels [the garment] — Taz: Here he understood from Beit Yosef that a mitzvah that has duration can ab initio be blessed after the act, and he passes to what comes after; and on this he said it is not necessary — I would say ab initio one must likewise pass to the actual performance. And this case is different, for he wishes to dress so as not to walk four cubits without tzitzis and his hands are soiled — impossible except afterward — and see in Magen Avraham letter 12. On clothing it is difficult, for he wrote impossible in another matter; and moreover that it has duration implies for its wording afterward, even if it has no duration — and we establish for immersion that there it is impossible in the act of the person, he is not fit at all; not so here where he waits until he washes his hands — and Taz wrote as I explained. And in Netilat Yadayim below, that one blesses before drying — there the reason is that most times his hands are soiled and it is forbidden to mention the Name; but a mitzvah that one can bless beforehand certainly requires it, even though it has duration. And above seif 1 — he shall wrap and bless before wrapping — see there.
