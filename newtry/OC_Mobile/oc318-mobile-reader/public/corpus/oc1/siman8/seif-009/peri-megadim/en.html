He understood from the Beis Yosef that a mitzvah that continues over time may be blessed over lechatchilah after its performance, and it is considered before what comes afterward. About this he said that it is not compelling, for one may say that lechatchilah even in such a case it must be truly before the performance. Here is different, because he wants to put it on so that he not walk four amos without tzitzis, and his hands are soiled, so it is impossible except afterward. See what is written in Magen Avraham, letter 12. The Levush is difficult, for he wrote that it is impossible otherwise, and further, that it continues over time, which implies that according to the other answer even if it did not continue over time this would be so. But this is not comparable to immersion, for there it is impossible otherwise, since the person is not fit at all, unlike here, where he can wait until he washes his hands. According to the Taz it works well, as I explained. Regarding washing the hands below, where one blesses before drying, there the reason is that most times his hands are soiled and it is forbidden to mention Hashem’s Name. But by a mitzvah where one can bless before it literally, he certainly must do so, even if the mitzvah continues over time. Above, seif 1, he should wrap himself and bless before wrapping; see there.
