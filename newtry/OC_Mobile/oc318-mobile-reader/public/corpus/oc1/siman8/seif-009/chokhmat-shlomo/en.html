Seif 9. Before he recites the blessing he should examine the tzitzis strings, etc. Note: The poskim wrote the reason: although with respect to concern for neglecting a positive commandment we are not concerned, for we place it on its presumption, nevertheless with respect to concern for a blessing, because of the severity of “You shall not bear,” we are concerned. It appears that this is specifically according to those who hold that “You shall not bear” applies even by way of blessing. Since it is a possible Torah prohibition, like the mitzvah of tzitzis, and “You shall not bear” is severe, we are concerned regarding the blessing. According to them, women are indeed forbidden to bless over positive time-bound commandments. But according to us, as the Rama wrote in siman 589 that the practice is that women bless on positive time-bound commandments, the reason must be that we hold that by way of blessing there is no Torah prohibition. Therefore even if it would be a blessing in vain, it is only D'Rabbanan. Since with respect to the possible Torah prohibition of going without tzitzis we are not concerned and rely on the presumption, all the more so with respect to the concern of a blessing, which is only D'Rabbanan, one need not be concerned. Therefore, according to our practice that women bless on positive time-bound commandments, there is room to be lenient and bless without examining the tzitzis. This can support leniency for one who is preoccupied and rushed and has no time, that he may put it on and bless in this way. So it appears to my humble opinion. Study this carefully. See the Taz, who distinguishes in this law, and Magen Avraham disagrees.
