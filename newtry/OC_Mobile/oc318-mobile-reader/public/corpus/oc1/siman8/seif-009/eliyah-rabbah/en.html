[9] One must have intent when wrapping, etc. And if he did not have such intent, it implies that he has not fulfilled the mitzvah properly—Bach wrote that the same applies to tefillin and sukkah. Although for other mitzvos one fulfills them even without specific intent in them, only that one performs the mitzvah for the sake of Hashem who commanded it. These are different, for regarding tzitzit it is written "so that you may remember"; regarding tefillin, "so that the Torah of Hashem may be in your mouth"; and regarding sukkah, "so that your generations may know." And the words of Olas Tamid on this can be easily dismissed.
