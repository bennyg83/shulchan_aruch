Seif 9, "before," etc. Although we follow presumption, as in Chullin 12, nevertheless since it is possible to clarify, as stated in Pesachim chapter 1: its presumption is that it was checked, etc.; for what practical difference, etc. And in Beitzah chapter 3 regarding checking an animal — although we follow presumption, as regarding cheeses in Yoreh Deah siman 81. That is for a common minority, as in Yoreh Deah siman 1; and all the more in a case like this, where snapping is common, we do not follow presumption, as the Magen Avraham wrote. His proof from mikveh in siman 201, end of seif 65, is not compelling, for there one can say: leave the impure person in his presumption, as the Ran wrote there. But his proof from Kiddushin 79b; see Tosafos there, gloss beginning "mi," and Ri explained, etc. We say there: until here Rabbi Nosson disagrees only, etc. Rabbi Yaakov holds it is not a presumption because sickness is common, even according to Rav; otherwise, let it challenge oppositely: leave the body in its presumption, as it challenges there; and even according to Rabbi Nosson bodily presumption is stronger than money. We rule like Rabbi Yaakov, as written in Choshen Mishpat siman 251, seif 2, in the gloss; and so we rule like Rav there.
