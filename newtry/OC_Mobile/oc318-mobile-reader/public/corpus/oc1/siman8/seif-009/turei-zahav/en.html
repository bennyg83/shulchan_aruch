“So that he not bless in vain.” Even so there is concern he might wear a four-cornered garment without tzitzis — nevertheless we rely on chezkas kashrus that it was valid, and the stringency of “do not go about” was only to require checking; see below siman 13 s. 1. One could say: we do not rely on chezkah when we can check, as Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De’ah siman 1 — one can answer: here is different, for he already checked the tallis and we only worry about later damage, so legally we may rely on chezkah. Accordingly what the Tur and Shulchan Aruch give as the reason “because of a blessing in vain” — one with many four-cornered garments need check only one of them since one blessing is for all — still one should do as Nimukei Yosef: the experienced, when they wrap, examine the threads, separate them well, and see whether a strand was uprooted; one must also look at the part of tzitzis in the tallis hole until the knot, which also often frays. See siman 13 s. 2.
