Although in any event there is concern for the prohibition of wearing a four-cornered garment without tzitzis, nevertheless we establish it on its presumption that it was valid; only because of the stringency of “You shall not take, etc.” did they require checking it specifically. According to this, one who wears many tzitzis garments need check only one of them, since one blessing covers all of them. But according to the concern that he may wear a four-cornered garment without tzitzis, he must check them all, and it is proper to be stringent. One must check above, from the hole in the tallis until the knot. Taz. The Taz wrote in siman 13, s.k. 7, that one who was called to the Torah and puts on a tallis need not check at that time because of the burden on the congregation; see there. One who is traveling and is afraid to check the tzitzis of his tallis lest it be invalid and he has no other tallis may avert his eyes and bless without checking. Beis Yaakov, siman 84.
