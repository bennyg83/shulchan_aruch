To annul [the blessing]. Even though without this one may fear concern about a prohibition—that he might wear a four-cornered garment without tzitzit—nevertheless we establish it by presumption that it was valid; they were strict to check it only because of the stringency of “do not explore after your eyes,” etc. Accordingly, one who wore many garments with tzitzit need check only one of them, since one blessing covers them all. But if one fears that he might wear a four-cornered garment without tzitzit, he must check them all; and it is proper to be strict. One must check upward from the hole of the tallit until the knot (Taz). The Taz wrote in siman 13, seif kaf-zayin: one who was called to the Torah and is wearing a tallit need not check at that time because of trouble to the congregation, see above. One who is traveling on the road and fears to check the tzitzit of his tallit lest they be invalid, and he has no other tallit, may close his eyes and bless without checking (Beit Yaakov, siman 84).
