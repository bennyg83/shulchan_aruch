Taz, se’if katan 9: The Ran writes likewise in the third chapter of Berakhot, “Mi SheMeito,” and Tosafot in Pesachim also write this. In my humble opinion, however, the cases are not at all comparable. There, regarding immersion, it is certainly altogether impossible to recite the blessing before performing the mitzvah. Here, by contrast, he can remove the garment, recite the blessing, and put it on again. This requires further study.
