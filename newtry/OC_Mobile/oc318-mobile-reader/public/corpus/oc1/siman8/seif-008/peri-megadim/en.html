The author slightly changed the language of Tur — implying his view is specifically because of a blessing in vain they were stringent; if not so, no. And according to his method in siman 13 letter 3, regarding Shabbat they were lenient and regarding blessing they were more stringent; Magen Avraham here letter 11 and there letter 5 according to his method — see there — and see in Magen Avraham from this. And from this he derived regarding many garments, etc. Similarly a silk tallit — there are those who practice to bless on wool; need not check the silk. Also from this he derived that even what is inside the hole one checks — not per Magen Avraham letter 11 according to his method, for it is made to tear — not so in a hole; compared to Taz, the seriousness of "you shall not bear" — therefore for several garments, for everyone need not check what is in the hole. But Eliyah Rabbah letter 10 in the name of Taz and Shelah — to check where it rests on the corners, for on the contrary there, if one thread tears it is invalid and it is usual to tear there — see siman 12, and so it is fitting, for there is the essence; not so the threads — most are valid if enough remains for tying. And what he distinguished between that case of Yoreh De'ah siman 1 seif 61 regarding slaughter — even for the author there one must ask him after slaughter the laws of slaughter and we do not rely on majority — if so here too he transgressed a positive command and cannot fix. And need not force to say for this matter presumption is better than majority; and similar to this we wrote in Magen Avraham regarding the eye — opposite presumption one is not believed regarding majority; one can say the opposite cannot be said lest it is from the minority — see Pri Megadim 14 siman 1 in Mishbetzos Zahav.
