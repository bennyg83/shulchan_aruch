One must separate them, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote that from HaItur in the Beis Yosef it implies that if he did not do so, it does not invalidate, end quote. Magen Avraham wrote that if separating them will cause him to miss praying with the congregation, he should not separate them, end quote. Mateh Moshe wrote that the word tzitzis is an acronym for ‘a righteous person should always separate his tzitzis.’
