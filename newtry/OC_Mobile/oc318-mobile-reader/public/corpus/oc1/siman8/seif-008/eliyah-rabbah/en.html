[8] One must separate them, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov wrote from the Ittur in Beit Yosef—it implies that if he did not do so it does not invalidate—until here. And Magen Avraham wrote that if he would be prevented from praying with the congregation while he separates them, he should not separate them—until here. And Mateh Moshe wrote: tzitzit—the initials [stand for]: a righteous person always separates his tzitzit.
