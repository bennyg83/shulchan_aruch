He should have intent when wrapping himself, etc. If he did not have this intent, it implies that he did not fulfill the mitzvah properly. The Bach wrote that the same law applies to tefillin and sukkah. Although with other mitzvos one fulfills the obligation even without having intent for anything except that he performs the mitzvah for the sake of Hashem, Who commanded him to do it, these are different: by tzitzis it is written, ‘so that you shall remember’; by tefillin, ‘so that the Torah of Hashem shall be in your mouth’; and by sukkah, ‘so that your generations shall know.’ The words of Olas Tamid on this can easily be rejected.
