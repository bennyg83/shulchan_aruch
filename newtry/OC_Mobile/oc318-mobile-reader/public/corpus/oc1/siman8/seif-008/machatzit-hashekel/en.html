(section 8) “Three,” etc. — he wrote in Hagahos Maimoniyos, end of chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah: yes, his fellow may [bless for him], even though his fellow is not now wrapping himself in a tallis.

“One who ties tefillin,” etc. — he brings proof from one who has already heard the shofar blast and now blows to discharge another after the other recites the berachah, when the other wishes to fulfill his obligation now. And so too here: one who ties [tefillin], etc., should not bless except the wearer — and likewise regarding the shofar.

And one must say that Hagahos Maimoniyos speaks, etc. — meaning: so as not to create a dispute between Hagahos Maimoniyos and Terumat HaDeshen. And all the more so for Terumat HaDeshen, for whom the matter is so plain in his view and he brought proof from it; whereas in Hagahos Maimoniyos he wrote the law in the reverse direction, even though Hagahos Maimoniyos speaks of one who is not proficient.

And in Eliyah Rabbah he wondered at Magen Avraham, who wrote: Hagahos Maimoniyos [rules] like Hillel, etc., regarding wrapping in tzitzis — even though this one fulfills the mitzvah himself, his fellow discharges him even if he is not now engaged in that mitzvah, except for Keriat Shema, the meal, and prayer; and if he himself does not know [the berachah], even for these three his fellow discharges him — end quote. Thus the beginning speaks of one who knows; for other mitzvos, even for one who is proficient, a fellow discharges him.
