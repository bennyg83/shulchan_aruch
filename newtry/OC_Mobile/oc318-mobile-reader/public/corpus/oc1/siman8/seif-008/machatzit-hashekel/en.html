Seif katan 8, three, etc. Hagahos Ashri, chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah, wrote so: his fellow can do it even though his fellow is not now wrapping himself in a tallis.
One who ties tefillin, etc. He brings proof from one who already heard shofar blowing and now blows to discharge another, that the other who now wishes to fulfill should bless. About this he wrote that one who ties, etc., should not bless; rather, the wearer blesses. If so, the same applies to shofar.
One must say that Hagahos Ashri is speaking, etc. Meaning, so as not to make a dispute between Hagahos Ashri and Terumas HaDeshen. This is especially so because according to Terumas HaDeshen the matter is so obvious to him and he brought proof from it, while Hagahos Ashri wrote the law in the opposite way, even though Hagahos Ashri is speaking of one who is not expert.
In the book Eliyah Rabbah he wondered about Magen Avraham, for this is the wording of Hagahos Ashri: such as Hallel, etc. “And to wrap in tzitzis: even though this person fulfills the mitzvah, his fellow can discharge him even if he is not now engaged in that mitzvah, except for Shema, Birkas HaMazon, and prayer. If he himself does not know, then even in these three his fellow discharges him.” Thus at first it speaks of one who knows, for with other mitzvos, even one who is expert can be discharged.
