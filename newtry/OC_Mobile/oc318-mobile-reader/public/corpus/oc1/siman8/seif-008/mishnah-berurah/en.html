(19) One should intend in wrapping — and this is apart from the intention one must have when wearing to fulfill the mitzvah of tzitzis. Bach wrote that the same law applies to tefillin and sukkah: although regarding other mitzvos one fulfills even if he did not intend anything specific in it, provided he does the mitzvah for the sake of Heaven Who commanded to do it — these are different. For regarding tzitzis it is written "so that you may remember," and regarding tefillin — "so that Heaven's Torah may be in your mouth," and regarding sukkah — "so that your generations may know." Nevertheless it appears that all this is lekatchilah, in order to fulfill the mitzvah in completeness; but bedieved, even if he did not have any of these intentions, but only to fulfill the positive mitzvah, he has fulfilled. And afterward I found in Peri Megadim in siman 25 that he wrote thus (and so it implies in Olas Tamid and in Biur HaGra, that even regarding tzitzis intention is not me'akev).
