(19) Compliment in wrapping it - and this is apart from the intention that it is necessary to be precise in wearing it to observe the mitzvah of tzitzit. And the Bah wrote that the law is in tefillin and sukkah: Even a mitzvah debshar is issued even though nothing is intended in it, but if one does the mitzvah for the sake of Hashem that he commanded it to be done, you are me. Devzitzit reads "so that you may remember", and in the tefillin - "so that the Torah of Hashem may be in your mouth", and in the sukkah - "so that your generations may know". Because in all of these, only to fulfill the mitzvah, he came out He wrote yes (and yes means always on the go and in the explanation of the Gra, that even in tzitzit there is no reference to delay).
