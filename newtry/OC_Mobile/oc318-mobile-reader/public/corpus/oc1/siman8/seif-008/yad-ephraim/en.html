That walking is an interruption — in my humble opinion one may say that there the matter does not depend on interruption, but only that we require immediacy, that there not be delay between leaning and slaughter; but regarding interruption we do not care at all, even if he conversed between them. Therefore they proved that the measure of delay is twenty-two cubits, for it is not called “immediately” — unlike regarding interruption, where one may well say that walking is an interruption like conversation, and in such a case.
