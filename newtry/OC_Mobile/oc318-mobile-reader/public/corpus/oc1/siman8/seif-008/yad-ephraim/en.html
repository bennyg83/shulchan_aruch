There, se'if katan 11: 'For it constitutes an interruption.' In my humble opinion, one can say that there the matter does not depend upon interruption. Rather, immediacy is required, so that there be no delay between semichah and slaughter. Interruption itself is irrelevant, even if he speaks between them. They therefore proved that walking twenty-two amos constitutes the measure of delay and is not called immediate. Regarding interruption, by contrast, one can properly say that walking constitutes an interruption just like speech and similar acts.
