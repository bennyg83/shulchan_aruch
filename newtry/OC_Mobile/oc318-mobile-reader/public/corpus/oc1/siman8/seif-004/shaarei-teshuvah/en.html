Tzitzis. Biur HaTaz — and some are accustomed to place the right side on the left shoulder, etc.; and Radbaz ch. 1 siman 471 wrote: those accustomed to wrap in a tallis like mourners' wrapping and afterward throw the two tzitzis to the left side so they are all on the left — two before him and two behind him — is incorrect, and especially on Shabbat since it appears like a mourner; rather he should throw the two corners, one before him and one behind him on the right, and likewise on the left; and wrapping around the neck is at the time of the blessing, etc.:
