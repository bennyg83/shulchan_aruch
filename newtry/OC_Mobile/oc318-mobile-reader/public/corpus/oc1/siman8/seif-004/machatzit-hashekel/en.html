Seif katan 4, and he should stand, etc. Specifically wrapping. For we bless “to wrap,” and regarding a mourner who needs wrapping, the Shas concludes that it is like the wrapping of Yishmaelites, namely below the mouth. But the Bach wrote that this applies specifically to a mourner, for we learn from Yechezkel, where the Holy One, blessed is He, told him when his wife died, “do not cover over the lip,” and we learn from this that another mourner does require covering of the lip. But regarding tzitzis, necessarily what we bless “to wrap” does not mean Yishmaelite wrapping. For how can we say “Who commanded us to wrap”? Where were we commanded regarding wrapping? It is written only “with which you cover yourself.” Rather, necessarily, what we bless “to wrap” means to cover oneself. In order to satisfy all opinions, he should stand wrapped for the time it takes to walk four cubits, and this suffices, as written in seif katan 5.
With a garment that they make, etc. Meaning what we call arba kanfos.
In its length, the long side is everywhere called length. The shorter side is called width. But if the garment is sewn, meaning what we call a leibl, see siman 10, seif 6. In any event, by a leibl, without this, most of it must be open.
Nevertheless, there is no concern. Meaning, even with a leibl, although wrapping does not apply to it, nevertheless one fulfills the mitzvah with it.
