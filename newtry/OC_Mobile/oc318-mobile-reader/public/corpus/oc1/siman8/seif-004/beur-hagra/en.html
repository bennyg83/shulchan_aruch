Seif 4, "he returns," etc. Midrash Abkir on "and the water was a wall for them": regarding the waters behind them, be careful, etc. See Beis Yosef and Yalkut, and Beis Yosef in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos in the name of the Yerushalmi: it would not enter your mind, etc. See below at the end of siman 17. In the Pesikta, cited in Yalkut Tehillim, siman 723, "all my bones," etc.: from behind and before me I cast two corners of the tallis when I stand in prayer.
