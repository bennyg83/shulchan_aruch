[d] And it is true, etc. Adi Zahav wrote and everywhere one should cover one's head from the beginning of the prayer to the end. And not like some people who bless the tallit and throw it behind them and about them it was said and you threw me after your corpse, so far. And the 28th wrote the custom of the humble that there should not be a moment in the uncovering of the head as long as you covered it, up to this point. And in the book of the sages it was written that one should cover a prayer of the head with a talit, up to this point.
