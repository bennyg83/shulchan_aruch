[4] And it is proper, etc. Adnei Zahav wrote that nevertheless one must cover his head from the beginning of prayer until its end — not like some who bless on the tallit and throw it behind them, of whom it is said, "you cast Me behind your back," until here. The Bach wrote the custom of the modest is that there not be a moment of uncovered head as long as the tallit is on him, until here. In Sefer Divrei Chachamim he wrote one must cover the head tefillin with the tallit, until here. It seems not that it must be literally covered, but spreading the tallit on the head above, against all the head tefillin. Darkei Moshe wrote that at the time of wrapping tzitzis it is forbidden to go with uncovered head according to all, and in Perishah he wrote that even while standing it is forbidden to uncover as long as he is wrapped.
