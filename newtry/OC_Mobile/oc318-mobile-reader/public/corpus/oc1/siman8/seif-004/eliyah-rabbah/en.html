And it is proper, etc. Adei Zahav wrote that nevertheless one must cover his head from the beginning of prayer until its end, and not like some people who bless on the tallis and throw it behind them, concerning whom it is said, ‘And Me you have cast behind your back,’ end quote. The Bach wrote that the practice of the modest is that there should not be even a moment with the head uncovered while the tallis is upon him, end quote. In Sefer Divrei Chachamim he wrote that one must cover the head tefillin with the tallis, end quote. It appears that this does not mean actually covered, but that he spreads the tallis over the head above, opposite the entire head tefillin. The Darchei Moshe wrote that at the time of wrapping in tzitzis one may not walk with an uncovered head according to all opinions, and the Perishah wrote that even standing uncovered is forbidden as long as he is wrapped.
