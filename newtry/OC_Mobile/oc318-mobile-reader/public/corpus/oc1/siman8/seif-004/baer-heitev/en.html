The tzitziyot. They are accustomed to make an atarah from a piece of silk as a sign that those tzitziyot that are before him should always remain before him, as we find: the plank that he placed in the north merited to remain forever in the north (Shelah); and in the writings of the Arizal it is written that the Arizal was not strict about this. And some are accustomed to place the right side on the left shoulder so that they are turned around, as is found in siman 41, seif 36. Magen Avraham.
