Tzitzis. The custom is to make an atarah from a piece of silk as a sign that those tzitzis which are in front of him should always remain in front of him, as was said regarding a board that merited to be in the north: it remains forever in the north. Shelah. In the writings of the Arizal it is written that the Arizal was not particular about this. Some have the custom to place the right side over the left shoulder so that they are wrapped around; and so it appears in siman 301, seif 30. Magen Avraham.
