They fulfill [the mitzvah] — Taz: In their days the custom was to wrap oneself, and therefore the blessing was fixed in the language of wrapping; and now that the custom is not so, nevertheless the wording of the blessing was not changed. See Levush: "wrapping" is not alluded to, etc. — an old tallit of a commoner, whether it is permitted for tzitzis — siman 147, Magen Avraham 5.
