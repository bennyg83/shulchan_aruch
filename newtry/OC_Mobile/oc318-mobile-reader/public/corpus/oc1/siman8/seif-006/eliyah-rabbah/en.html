[6] And the others answer Amen, etc. It is written in Hagahos Asheri at the end of chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah that if one wraps in tzitzis, his fellow may bless for him. In Terumat HaDeshen, siman 140, he wrote: one who ties tefillin on his fellow's head — presumably only the wearer blesses, until here. One must say Hagahos Asheri deals with one wrapping who is not expert in the blessing, until here the Magen Avraham's language. This is not correct, for Hagahos Asheri wrote after this "and if he himself does not know," etc. — the first clause seems to apply even to one who knows; and from Terumat HaDeshen there is no difficulty at all, for in truth presumably the one fulfilling the mitzvah blesses. Rather Hagahos Asheri says his fellow may bless — and this is clear.
