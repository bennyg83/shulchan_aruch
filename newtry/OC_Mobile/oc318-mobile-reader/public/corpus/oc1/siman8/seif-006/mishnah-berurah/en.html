(16) Rather, he wears it. The blessing is “to wrap,” because that is the wording of the blessing, like “to dwell in the sukkah.”
(17) And one should not change. This refers to our tallis katan, in which one does not wrap himself. But for a tallis katan in which one does wrap himself, as explained in seif 3, he may lechatchilah recite “to wrap.” After the fact, if he recited “to wrap” over any tallis katan, or recited “over the mitzvah of tzitzis” over a tallis gadol, he fulfilled his obligation. Know that one should not bless over a tallis katan unless its length is at least three quarters of an amah on each side, and the neck opening does not count toward the measure. Also, most of it must be open. Men of practice are accustomed to have an amah in front and an amah behind. See Mishnah Berurah and Biur Halachah in siman 16, where I wrote more on this matter.
