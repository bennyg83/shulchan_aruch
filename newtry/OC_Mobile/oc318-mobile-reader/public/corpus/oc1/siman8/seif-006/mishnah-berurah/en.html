(16) Rather, he wears it — and the blessing is "l'his'ateif," because the wording of the blessing is thus, like "l'shevet ba-sukkah."

(17) And one should not change — that is, regarding our small tallit, which one does not wrap in. But regarding a small tallit in which one does wrap, as explained in seif 3, one may lekatchilah bless "l'his'ateif." And bedieved, if he blessed on any small tallit "l'his'ateif," or he blessed on a large tallit "al mitzvas tzitzis," he has fulfilled. And know that one should not bless on a small tallit until it is at least three-quarters of an amah long on each side, and the neck-hole opening does not count toward [the] measure. And also that most of it must be open. Men of action are accustomed to [measure] one amah in front of them and one amah behind them. And see in Mishna Berurah and Biur Halacha in siman 16 what more I wrote on this matter.
