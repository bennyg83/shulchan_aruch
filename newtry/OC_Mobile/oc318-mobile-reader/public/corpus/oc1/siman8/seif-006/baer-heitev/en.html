Over the mitzvah of tzitzis. See Beis Yosef, who brings in the name of Mahara that he asked how we can recite the blessing “over the mitzvah of tzitzis,” since we hold that “over” implies something already past, etc.; see there. In Darchei Moshe he wrote a reason for the custom and concluded that therefore we bless with the wording “over,” which implies the past; see there. At first glance this is difficult, for in Pesachim folio 7b we conclude there that the halachah is “over the removal of chametz.” Rashi there explains that it also implies the future. See what the Rosh wrote in the first chapter of Pesachim in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel, what Tosafos wrote at the end of chapter Rabbi Eliezer DeMilah, s.v. Avi HaBen, etc., what Kesef Mishneh wrote at the end of Hilchos Berachos, Responsa Mahari Mintz, siman 110, and Responsa Maharil, siman 14.
