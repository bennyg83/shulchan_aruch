On the mitzvah of tzitzit. See the Beit Yosef, what he brings in the name of Mahara, who asked: how do we bless “on the mitzvah of tzitzit,” when we rule that “al” implies something already done, etc., see above; and in Darkei Moshe he wrote a reason for the custom and concludes: therefore they bless with the wording “al,” which implies something already done, see above. At first glance this is difficult, for it is stated in Pesachim 7b that we conclude in that passage that the halachah is “on biur chametz”; Rashi in that passage explains that “al” also implies something about to be done. See what the Rosh wrote in the first chapter of Pesachim in the name of R' Chananel; and what Tosafos wrote at the end of the chapter on milah, s.v. avi haben, etc.; and what the Kesef Mishneh wrote at the end of Hilchos Berachos; and in the responsa of Mahari Mintz, siman 110; and in the responsum of Maharil, siman 14.
