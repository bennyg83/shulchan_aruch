“But wears it.” The blessing lehisatef — the text of the blessing is like that of leisheiv basukah.

“For the mitzvah of tzitzis.” Darkhei Moshe wrote: the custom’s reason is concern for those authorities who hold one does not fulfill with such a small tallis because it lacks itufa; therefore they do not bless lehisatef, which implies fulfilling the mitzvah as instituted — only they bless that Heaven commanded us mitzvos tzitzis even though now they do not fulfill as instituted. With this one resolves that they bless “al,” which implies past [already commanded]. See siman 16 on tallis katan’s measure.
