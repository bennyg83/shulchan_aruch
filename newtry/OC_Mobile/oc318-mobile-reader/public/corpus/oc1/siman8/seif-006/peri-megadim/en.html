But. See Taz. Wrapping the body is also called wrapping, and ‘to dwell in the sukkah’ means to tarry there; that is the wording of the blessing. The Rav in the gloss said that it is good to bless over a tallis katan ‘over the mitzvah of tzitzis,’ unless it has four corners, as explained in seif 3. Even so, the custom now is to bless ‘over the mitzvah of tzitzis,’ because we do not distinguish; only over a tallis gadol do we bless ‘to wrap in tzitzis.’
