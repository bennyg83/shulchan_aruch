Rather — Taz: Wrapping the body is also called wrapping, and "to sit in the sukkah" is like "to linger" — and the wording of the blessing is so. The Be'er Heitev in the gloss said it is good to bless on a small tallit "on the mitzvah of tzitzis" if it does not have four corners, as explained in seif 3; nevertheless they now practice "on the mitzvah of tzitzis" because of language — only on a large tallit do they bless "to wrap oneself."
