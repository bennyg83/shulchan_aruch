Seif katan 6. He holds like siman 301, for there it is explained that one may go out this way into the public domain because it is the manner of wearing, and therefore one also fulfills the obligation of tzitzis this way, and there is no concern if they do so. Know that this “some have the practice, etc.” refers to a large tallis, for with a small tallis this does not apply. Although this siman discusses a small tallis, do not be troubled, for this is the continuation of Magen Avraham’s words: “it is written in the writings of the Arizal, etc.” refers to a small tallis, while “and according to the version, etc.” according to that version refers to a large tallis. On this he wrote “some have the practice, etc.” regarding a large tallis. Meaning, according to the words of the Arizal they practiced this way, for according to them there is no concern in placing the right side over the left.
