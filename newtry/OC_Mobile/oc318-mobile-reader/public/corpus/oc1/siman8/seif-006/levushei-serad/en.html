{Rama: Seif kama 6} Siman 301, seif 30. For there it is explained that one may go out thus in the public domain because it is the manner of clothing, and therefore one fulfills thereby the obligation of tzitzit as well, and there is no objection if they do so. And know that this "and some practice," etc., speaks of a tallit gadol, for with a tallit katan this does not apply; and even though this siman speaks of a tallit katan, do not be troubled—for such is the continuation of Magen Avraham's words. And it is written in the writings of the Ari z"l, etc., that it speaks of a tallit katan; and per the intended textual reading, etc., per this reading it speaks of a tallit gadol, and on this basis he wrote "and some practice," etc.—that is in a tallit gadol; and the meaning is: as the Ari z"l wrote, they practiced thus, for in his view there is no objection to placing the right side on the left—and this requires study.
