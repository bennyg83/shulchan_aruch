6) Before the blessing and wrapping it is proper to say the verses of Barchi Nafshi until "He inclines the heavens like a curtain"—Yad Aharon in Hagahat Tashbetz in the name of Mishnat Chassidim; and so in Beit Yosef Yashan; and those accustomed to say so should not say it before the blessing on the Torah, as the Chida wrote in Tzipporen Shamir, siman 2, letter 6, and in 103, siman 5, letter 31; and as Ruach Chayyim wrote, siman 5, letter 2.
