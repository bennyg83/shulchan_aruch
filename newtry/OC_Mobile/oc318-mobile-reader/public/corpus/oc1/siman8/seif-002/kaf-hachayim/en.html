And he blesses, etc.—that we do not bless on every mitzvah: Tur in Ramban, siman 189: every mitzvah that has no action, such as release of loans and the like entrusted to bet din; and likewise matters that depend on others' will, such as charity and the like; and likewise a mitzvah that comes through a transgression, such as returning stolen goods and usury; and priestly gifts as well not, for from the High Table one merits—unlike redemption of the firstborn and separation of terumah, where one does not bless on separating it but on the separation. And likewise on every mitzvah that is not the main mitzvah, such as chalitzah and yibum whose mitzvah is procreation, but these mitzvos branch off from it; and court executions as well not, because Heaven does not desire the death of the wicked; and for the same reason one does not bless on mayim acharonim that comes from Sodomite salt; and one should not bless on distress—and see there at length. Eshel Avraham, letter 1; Biurei HaGra, letter 2; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch.
