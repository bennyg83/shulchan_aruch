The order [is] as stated until the end of the section. For this reason it is not correct to don a tallit while it is folded and to drape it around the neck on the shoulders, even though in such a case one end, when folded with two tzitziyot, hangs before him on the right and the second end, with two tzitziyot, hangs on the left—even so one does not fulfill [the mitzvah] with it, because it is not a manner of wearing at all; and one who goes out with it on Shabbos is liable for a chatat (Meshevet Binyamin).
