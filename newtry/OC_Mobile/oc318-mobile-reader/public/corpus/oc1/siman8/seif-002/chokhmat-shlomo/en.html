(Seif 9) Before he blesses he should look at the tzitzis strings, etc. In my view: the reason the decisors wrote is that even for concern of nullifying a positive commandment we do not concern ourselves — we rely on presumption. Nevertheless regarding concern of a blessing because of "you shall not carry" we are concerned. It seems this is only for those who hold that a blessing can involve "you shall not carry"; and since there is concern of a biblical prohibition, as with tzitzis and "you shall not carry" — it is severe — therefore regarding blessing we are concerned; and according to them women are forbidden to bless on a positive commandment they are exempt from. But for us, per the Rama [siman 589] that the custom is women bless on positive commandments they are exempt from — the reason is that we hold that no blessing involves a biblical prohibition; and if so, even a blessing in vain is only rabbinic; therefore regarding concern of a biblical prohibition of going without tzitzis we do not concern ourselves and rely on presumption — all the more so for concern of blessing which is only rabbinic we need not concern ourselves. Therefore for our custom that women bless on positive commandments they are exempt from, there is room to be lenient to bless without examining tzitzis; and there is support to be lenient for one who is busy and rushed and has no time for the matter, that he may wear and bless thus — so it seems to my humble opinion; examine carefully.
