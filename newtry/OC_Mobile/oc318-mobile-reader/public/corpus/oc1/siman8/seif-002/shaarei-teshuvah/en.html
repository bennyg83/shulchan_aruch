Biur HaTaz — and what he wrote that they disagreed with Beit Yaakov is likewise explained in Sha'agat Aryeh siman 32, who wrote that when one comes to bless on the tallis he must bless at the time he holds it in his hand and wishes to wrap himself in it, not at the time of wrapping, for that is not "before the act" since the mitzvah of tzitzis already applied from when we bless "to wrap oneself in tzitzis," etc. In Shulchani Tzibbur he wrote regarding the blessing "to wrap oneself": according to Taz siman 642 and Beit Yaakov — it appears he holds that the essence of the mitzvah is called when he throws the corners behind him after wrapping; therefore it is good to bless close to the essence of the mitzvah, as Rashbatz wrote ch. 2 siman 477 regarding milah. Nevertheless one should practice that while the tallis is still in his hand he blesses and immediately dons it over the back on his head and body at once; and after he dons it in this way the corners go up on his neck and he wraps like Ishmaelite wrapping for a walk of four cubits — and thus practice the men of action of the Jerusalem community, etc.:
