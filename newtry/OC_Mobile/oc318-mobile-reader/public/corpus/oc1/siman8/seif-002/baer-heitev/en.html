And it is proper that he cover. He must cover his head from the beginning of the prayer until its end so that he prays with awe. See Radbaz, part 1, siman 196, and Devar Shmuel, siman 123. It is written in the kavvanos of the Arizal that he would cover the tefillin of the head with the tallis; see siman 27, that in any event he should not cover them completely. At the time of wrapping, he should cast all four tzitzis to the left side, like the wrapping of the Yishmaelites, for the time it takes to walk four amos. See Radbaz, part 1, siman 571; Halachos Ketanos, part 2, siman 63; and Yad Aharon. Those who place the folded tallis around the neck on their shoulders have not fulfilled their obligation. See Magen Avraham.
