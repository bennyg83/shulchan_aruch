And it is proper that he cover [his head]. One must cover his head from the beginning of prayer until its end, so that he pray with awe; see Radbaz, part 1, siman 196. And in Dvar Shmuel, siman 123: it is written that per the intention of the Arizal he would cover the tallit over the tefillin of the head; and see siman 27 that in any case he should not cover completely. At the time of wrapping he should throw all four tzitziyot to the left side, in the manner of Ishmaelite wrapping, in order to walk four amot; see Radbaz, part 1, siman 571, and in Shulchan Leket, part 2, siman 63, and in Yad Aharon. And those who place the folded tallit around the neck on their shoulders do not fulfill [the mitzvah], see Magen Avraham.
