“With a tallis.” Kavvanos writes that the Arizal would cover the tallis over the tefillin shel rosh — and see siman 27: in any case he should not cover completely. Kiddushin 29b implies an unmarried bachelor did not cover his head with a tallis even if he is a Torah scholar; 8a states that one who is not a Torah scholar, even if married, did not cover his head — see there.
