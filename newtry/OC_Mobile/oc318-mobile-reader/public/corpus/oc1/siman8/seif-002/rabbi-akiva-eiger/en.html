{Rama: Magen Avraham seif katan 2}That he does nothing but repair his food. Therefore also the blessing on slaughter — at least standing. Also regarding tzitzis — one who does not wear a tallit of four corners is not obligated to wrap in tzitzis; nevertheless, after wrapping, the positive commandment of making tzitzis applies to him, not because of repairing the garment, for there is no prohibition on the garment to go without tzitzis, but the positive commandment of making tzitzis is imposed on him — see below siman 13, and examine.
