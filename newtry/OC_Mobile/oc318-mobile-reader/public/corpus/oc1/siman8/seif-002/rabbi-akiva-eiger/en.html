Magen Avraham, se’if katan 2: “For he is doing nothing other than preparing his food.” According to this, the blessing over slaughtering likewise need not be recited while standing. Even regarding tzitzit, as long as a person is not wearing a four-cornered tallit, he is not obligated to wrap himself in tzitzit. Nevertheless, after he wraps himself, a positive commandment to attach tzitzit takes effect upon him. This is not merely an obligation to prepare the garment, for there is no prohibition inherent in the garment against walking while wearing it without tzitzit; rather, a positive commandment to attach tzitzit rests upon him. See below, siman 13, and examine carefully.
