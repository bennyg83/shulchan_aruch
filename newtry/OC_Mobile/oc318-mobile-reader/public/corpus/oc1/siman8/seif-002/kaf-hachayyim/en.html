2) There: And he should recite the berachah, etc. Responsa Ramban 189 explains why a berachah is not recited over every mitzvah. No berachah is recited over a mitzvah that involves no action, such as release of debts, or over matters entrusted to beis din. The same applies to matters dependent upon another person's decision, such as tzedakah; to a mitzvah occasioned by a transgression, such as returning stolen property or interest; and to gifts to kohanim, because they receive them from the table of the Most High. Pidyon HaBen and separating terumah differ: the berachah is not recited over giving them, but over the act of separation. Nor is a berachah recited over a mitzvah that is not itself the primary mitzvah, such as chalitzah and yibbum, for the primary mitzvah is procreation and these mitzvos branch from it. No berachah is recited over judicial executions either, because the Holy One, blessed is He, does not desire the death of the wicked. For this reason, no berachah is recited over mayim acharonim, which are required because of Sodomite salt; one does not recite a berachah over calamity. See the responsum at length. Eshel Avraham 1; Gra's commentary 2; Kerem Shlomo.
