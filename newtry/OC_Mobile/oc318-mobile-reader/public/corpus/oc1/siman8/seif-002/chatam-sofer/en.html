{Rama: (ibid.)} Comment 11 — as written in Yoreh Deah siman 1. In my view: although there it comes to remove a presumption of prohibition of eiver min hachai, as the Maharam of Padua wrote below, siman 337 in his name — nevertheless there it is a majority of commonly found experts, and most people inspect their homes properly; and majority is preferable to presumption of prohibition. Even so we say that the superiority the majority has over presumption does not help initially where clarification is possible; and so too here regarding tzitzis, where there is only presumption and no majority — one must clarify. Not so with treifos, where the majority are kosher — clear.
