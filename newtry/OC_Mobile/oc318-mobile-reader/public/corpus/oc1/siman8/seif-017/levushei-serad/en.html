{Rama: Magen Avraham, seif kama 16} Requires study. He raised two difficulties: one, that Terumat HaDeshen's conclusion is not so, but that one need not bless, as with slaughtering; second, that even in Terumat HaDeshen it is not entirely so, but according to Maharam—that interruption does not harm, and we require specifically distraction of attention; therefore Terumat HaDeshen holds that if he removed it, it is distraction. Walking to the house of study, prayer, returning home, and removing the tallit constitute distraction; but for the author in seif 13, walking or another interruption also helps; and one can say one need not bless except when there was no interruption—if so, what harm is there in what he removed? And on this he wrote that several times in Agudah, chapter HaTekhelet, whose language is: and regarding a silk tallit, etc.—behold they don it after removal—and this requires study.
