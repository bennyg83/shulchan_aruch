The custom developed to make an atarah from a piece of silk as a marker, so that the tzitzis that were in front remain forever in front, just as a board that merited being placed on the north side of the Mishkan remained forever on the north. Magen Avraham 6 in the name of Shelah. In the name of Shaar HaKavanos, however, he writes that the Arizal was not particular always to place the same side upon his head, as many people practice. This appears in Shaar HaKavanos, Derushei HaTzitzis, at the end of derush 2. See also Matzas Shimurim, page 24a; Nagid U'Metzaveh, laws of tzitzis; Shulchan Aruch HaRav 9; and Ben Ish Chai, Bereishis 8.
