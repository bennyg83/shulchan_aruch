And afterward he draws it from over his head and dons it; and they were accustomed to make a crown from a piece of silk for a sign that those tzitzis before him should forever be before him, as a plank that merited to be placed in the north—forever in the north. Magen Avraham, seif kama 6, in the name of Shelah. And in the name of HaKavanos he wrote that the Arizal was not strict always to place one side on the head as many practice—until here; and this is in Shaar HaKavanos in the drashos on tzitzit at the end of drush 2; and so Sefer Matzmiach Yeshuah, page 24:1a, Naggid uMitzvah in the laws of tzitzit; and so Razah, letter 9; Ben Ish Chai, Parashat Bereishit, letter 8.
