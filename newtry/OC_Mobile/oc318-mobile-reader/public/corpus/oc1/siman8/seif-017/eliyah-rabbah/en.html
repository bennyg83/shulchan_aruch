But if it did not fall off entirely, etc. The Beis Yosef learned this from the case where his tallis fell during prayer, in siman 97, seif 4. The Taz challenged him, saying that there, because of interruption in prayer, we say specifically that when it fell entirely and he needs a new wrapping it is an interruption; but here, even if most of it fell, he must bless. This also appears to be the view of Lechem Chamudos, folio 113. The same law applies to tefillin that shifted mostly from their place. If most of his tallis fell during the Shemoneh Esrei, he should return it, as below in siman 94. Alternatively, if all of it fell and others returned it to him, it appears that when he finishes his prayer he should feel his tzitzis and bless. Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah wrote that if one took the tallis to wrap himself, blessed over it, and it then fell entirely from his hand, he does not require another blessing, end quote. So too it is implied below at the end of siman 206 regarding one who took a fruit to eat it; see there.
