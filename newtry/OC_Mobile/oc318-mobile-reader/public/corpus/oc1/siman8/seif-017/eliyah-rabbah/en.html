[17] But if not all of it fell, etc. Beit Yosef learned this from one's tallit falling during prayer in siman 97, seif 4. The Taz challenged him: there, because of the interruption of prayer, we say specifically when all of it fell, for new wrapping is an interruption; but here, even for most of it one must bless — so seems Lechem Chamudos, page 113. The same applies to tefillin when most moved from their place; and if most of his tallit fell during the Shemoneh Esrei he returns it as below, siman 94; or if all fell and others returned it — it seems when he finishes his prayer he feels his tzitzis and blesses. The remnants of Knesset HaGedolah wrote: if he took the tallit to wrap and blessed on it and all of it fell from his hand, he needs no other blessing, until here. So too seems below at the end of siman 206 regarding taking fruit to eat — see there.
