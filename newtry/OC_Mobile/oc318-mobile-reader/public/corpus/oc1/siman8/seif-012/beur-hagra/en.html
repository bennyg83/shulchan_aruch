(א) Seif 12, "if there are," etc. In Menachos 43a: Rabbi Yehudah would cast, etc.; "when he changes," etc. The Rosh asked: if so, at night too he should bless. He wrote that one must say he did not remove the night garment by day. From here there is proof, etc.; see there, 34a.

(ב) "And if he wore them," etc. In the Tosefta, last chapter of Berakhot: an individual doing ten mitzvos blesses over each one; if he was doing one mitzvah all day, he blesses only once; if he stops and does, stops and does, he blesses over each one. So too regarding a perfumer's shop in Berakhot chapter 8, and with fragrant spices in the Yerushalmi concerning those busy and interrupted, cited by the Rosh there; and regarding slaughter in Chullin chapter 6, if he slaughtered a hundred, etc.; and similarly with blessings over enjoyment: he blessed on wine before the meal, and many like cases.

(ג) "And the same applies," etc. As stated regarding the students of Rav in Arvei Pesachim, and there regarding Ameimar who changed his mind, etc.; from there the Gemara challenges slaughter in Chullin chapter 6. In Chullin chapter 8: he blesses over each cup, etc. See seif 13 and seif 14.

(ד) "And likewise if he removed," etc. So wrote Terumas HaDeshen at first, but in the conclusion he wrote that it is unnecessary; see there, and Magen Avraham. So it appears from all the decisors who cited Maharam, who removed his large tallis and diverted his mind with other matters in order to bless on a small tallis; see Beis Yosef. See Maharil, siman 10. In the above Tosefta: if he was doing one mitzvah all day, etc., even though the first mitzvah was completed, and as written in Yoreh Deah siman 265, seif 5, and regarding slaughter, as above.
