Seif katan 12, and he blesses “to wrap,” etc. Tosafot, Sukkah 38, s.v. over, etc., wrote: explicitly in the Yerushalmi, etc., if he wrapped himself in it he says, etc., “to wrap in tzitzis.” The reason is that the mitzvah continues as long as he is wrapped and standing. Magen Avraham used the wording “and he blesses to wrap” although Shulchan Aruch did not mention the word “to wrap.” Perhaps Magen Avraham used the wording of Tosafot, or he used it for novelty: not only if it is a small tallis, where he blesses “on the mitzvah,” although he already wore it, because “on the mitzvah” can also imply the past, as stated in Pesachim 7 and Tosafot there in Sukkah; rather even “to wrap,” which certainly implies only the future as stated in Pesachim, one might think he should not bless “to wrap,” for he would be speaking falsely since he is already wrapped. Therefore it teaches that even so it is permitted for the reason Tosafot wrote.
