Required Taz and Eshel Avraham — for the explanation of Tur's words is that interruption applies to tefillin as to tzitzis if he removed [them] to go to the bathroom — also doubtful; and his inclination is that one need not bless on tefillin again; and unintended movement is different — and accordingly one would attribute a great error to Tur from Sukkah that Rava blesses when he exits the bathroom. But in any case it is difficult — if so, why is Tur not doubtful regarding tefillin as regarding tzitzis? We learn he knew from that [case] of Rava in Sukkah, and it is different there, as Darkei Moshe wrote in note 6, that it is forbidden to enter the synagogue in tefillin — that is interruption; unlike a tallit, even a large tallit which is proper to remove in the synagogue — nevertheless there is no prohibition, and therefore he is doubtful; unlike tefillin in the synagogue. And similarly tefillin in a place where one need not remove — interruption applies as to tzitzis; and my opinion is that one should not bless — and so Taz in note 12: doubtful blessings are ruled leniently — one should not bless when he removed with intent to return, even if he goes to the bathroom, and even if he has no small tallit on him; and I will explain further in seif 13.
