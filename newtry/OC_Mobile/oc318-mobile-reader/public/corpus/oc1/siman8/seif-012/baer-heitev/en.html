And if he interrupts between them. This means if he spoke between them, specifically where it was not needed for wearing it. But if he said, “Give me a garment to wear,” or the like, which is needed for the wearing, he need not recite the blessing again. Olas Tamid, and see Magen Avraham.
For all of them. This implies that he is particular only at the time of the blessing that he intend for all of them. But if he blessed with intent for one garment, and before putting it on they brought him another and he then decided to wear it as well, he must recite a second blessing over the second wearing. The Taz left this requiring study; see there. Olas Tamid ruled plainly that he need not recite the blessing again, and so wrote Perishah to Yoreh Deah, siman 19; see there.
And bless. Magen Avraham disagrees and rules that he need not recite the blessing if his mind was upon it.
