And if he interrupts between them. Meaning, if he spoke between them. And this is specifically when not for the need of donning; but if he said, “Give me a garment to wear,” and the like, which is a need of donning, he need not return and bless again. Peri Megadim and Magen Avraham.

On all of them. It implies that at the time of the blessing specifically he was particular to have intention for all of them; but if he blessed with intention for one garment, and before he donned it they brought him another and he changed his mind to don it as well, he must bless a second time for the second donning. The Taz left it in doubt, see above; and Peri Megadim ruled plainly that he need not return and bless again—and so wrote the Prisha in Yoreh De'ah, siman 19; see above.

And to bless [again]. Magen Avraham disagrees and ruled that he need not bless if he had it in mind.
