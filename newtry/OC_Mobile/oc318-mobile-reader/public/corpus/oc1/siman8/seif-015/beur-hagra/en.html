Seif 15, "if it fell," etc. As stated in Sukkah chapter 4: the Rabbis of Rabbi Eliezer, whenever they would touch, etc. The Rosh explained in the name of Tosafos, Rabbenu Yonah, and other decisors that it speaks where they slipped from their place.
"Provided that they fell," etc. The Beis Yosef learned this from what is written above, siman 97, seif 4; but the Nimukei Yosef before us wrote even if it fell from most of the body. See Taz.
