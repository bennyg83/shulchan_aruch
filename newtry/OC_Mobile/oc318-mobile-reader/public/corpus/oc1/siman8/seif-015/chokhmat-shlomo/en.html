Seif 15. If his tallis fell, etc. Note: If a small part fell off him and he put it back, and afterward a second small part fell off and he put it back, and so on until, when combined, it would be considered that all of it fell, one may question whether this is considered that all of it fell and he must bless, or not. It appears this can be resolved from Eiruvin 24a, where it says: if one cubit was breached and he fenced one cubit, one cubit was breached and he fenced one cubit, until it was completed to more than ten, what is the law, etc., and they said to us: new faces have come here. Here too, new faces have come here. See the sugya and Rashi, and we rule this way below in siman 358, seif 2. If so, here too, certainly if one wore a tallis without a blessing he must bless as long as it is still on him, as written above in Shulchan Aruch. Therefore, even though only a little fell and he put it back, since in combination all of it fell, it is considered that new faces have come here, like one wearing a tallis without a blessing, and he must bless. The same applies according to the Taz in siman 16 regarding a tallis split in two and sewn back together, that one side of the tzitzis was in any event nullified and one must make tzitzis anew according to the Beit Yosef. So too, if it split a little and he sewed it, then again another little part split and he sewed it, until in combination all of it had split and there was new sewing after the making of the tzitzis, new faces have come here and he must make anew the tzitzis on one side. The same can be learned regarding what is written in Shulchan Aruch, Yoreh Deah, siman 216, about one who vows from this house and it fell and he rebuilt it, that he may live in it. Likewise in Even HaEzer, siman 95, regarding the dwelling of a widow, that if it fell she loses it. It appears that the same applies if he rebuilt a little and again rebuilt a little, even though at first most of it remained and it was considered the first house; if by combining the small parts most became new, new faces have come here, and it is permitted for the vow prohibition, and the heirs are exempt from giving her this dwelling. So it appears to my humble opinion, clearly and correctly. From this one can learn for all similar cases.
