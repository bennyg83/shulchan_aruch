(39) From upon him. If others placed it back on him while he was unable to do so himself, he should handle the tzitzis afterward and bless according to the opinion that requires a blessing, but where there is doubt one should be careful to avoid an unnecessary blessing.
(40) At night. Since a daytime garment is exempt at night according to many opinions, the morning wearing is a new obligation and requires a blessing. But according to those who hold that a daytime garment is obligated even at night, there was no time of exemption. Therefore it is good to exempt it with the blessing over the tallis gadol.
(41) He should bless over it. This is where he slept in it at night and now wears it by day, but because of the dispute it is proper to exempt it with another tallis.
