(Lat) Unintentionally - even why Dafskinan Dabhasir Talito on the thought of returning it does not need to be blessed, it is even worse, Daha fell unintentionally and I did her mitzvah. And there is even a small tallit left on it, which is not enjoyable.

(M) One should bless - even if worn immediately. And if he falls from it in the middle of the 18th prayer, and others put it back on him - when he finishes the 18th prayer, he will use the tzitzit and bless.

(Ma) Even though a rifle fell - the 16th and Rabbi Eliyahu share this, and the Gra also supports the words of the 16th. And for all my Alma, if it fell over the whole body, even though it remained in his hand, one should bless, dazda la mitzvah; that the main mitzvah of wrapping is in the body. If one blesses the shawl, and it falls from one's hand before it is wrapped in it and picked up and worn, one does not need to bless a second time. And even then they are disqualified his tassels, and he had a cash tassel and repaired them immediately, he does not need to bless a second time; since he has not yet performed his mitzvah, he was not distracted [as in the Tefillin Lakman in the 25th section of the 12th section, see ibid. 12]. And whoever took a tallit to wrap himself in it and began to bless it, and before he finished the blessing they took it from him, and tefillin in front of him, can finish the blessing "which we sanctified in his mitzvot and commanded to put tefillin", and he leaves with this [Minor Laws, Mark 13, and the HH agreed with him on the halacha].
