(39) Unintentionally—even according to what we rule that when removing his tallis with intent to return it one need not bless; in this it is worse, for it fell off unintentionally and the mitzvah departed from him. And even if a small tallis remains on him, it does not help.

(40) He must bless—even if he wears it immediately. And if it fell off him in the middle of the Shemoneh Esreh prayer and others put it back on him—when he finishes the Shemoneh Esreh prayer he should feel the tzitzis and bless.

(41) Even though most of it fell off — Taz and Eliyah Rabbah disagree on this, and the Gra also sides with Taz. According to all, if it fell off the entire body, even though it remained in his hand, he must bless, for the mitzvah departed from it; since the essence of the mitzvah of atifah is on the body. If one blessed on the tallis and it fell from his hand before he wrapped himself in it, and he lifted it and put it on — he need not bless again. Even if then its tzitzis became invalid and he had ready tzitzis and fixed them immediately, he need not bless again; since he still had not fulfilled his mitzvah, he did not remove his attention [as with tefillin below, siman 25 seif 12; see there in Taz seif katan 12]. One who took a tallis to wrap himself in it and began to bless on it, and before he finished the blessing they took it from him, and tefillin are before him — he may finish the blessing "...Who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to don tefillin," and he fulfills thereby [Hilchos Ketanos siman 153, and Ohr HaChaim agreed with him for the halacha].
