(Seif kama 15) "If not," even though, etc. He need not bless—and this is when he was silent [without specific intent]. But if explicitly he did not intend, and afterward he slaughtered again, he reconsidered—and according to all he must bless.

Regarding circumcision, etc. This is not like Shach, who challenged Rama's words against themselves: regarding slaughter he ruled that even if he did not have explicit intent on the others but was merely silent, he need not bless on the others; but regarding circumcision he rules that if there are two infants to circumcise, when silent he must bless also on the second. And according to what Magen Avraham wrote to distinguish—one can say that regarding circumcision as well there is no fixed practice and they are not accustomed to bring more [than one at a time].
