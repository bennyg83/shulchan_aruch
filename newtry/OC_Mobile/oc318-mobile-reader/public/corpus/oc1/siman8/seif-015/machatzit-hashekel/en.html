(Seif katan 15) If not, even though, etc. For he need not bless, and this is when it was unspecified. But if he explicitly did not intend, and afterward he slaughtered more, he is considered to have changed his mind, and according to all opinions he must bless.
For regarding circumcision, etc. Not like the Shach, who challenged the words of the Rama against each other: regarding slaughter he ruled that even if he did not explicitly have others in mind, but only unspecifiedly, he need not bless over the others; while regarding circumcision he ruled that if there are two infants to circumcise, then when his intent was unspecified he must bless also over the second. According to what the Magen Avraham wrote to distinguish, this is understandable, for regarding circumcision too there is no fixed setting, and it is not usual to bring more.
