Seif 10. If he wears, etc. Note: See the Taz, who wrote to answer the Beit Yosef’s question from a convert, that at first the person was not fit, as with immersion of a convert, etc. In truth, with all due respect, they are not comparable. By a convert it was impossible in any way for him to be fit beforehand, and therefore it is considered before performance. But here it is possible that he not put it on until after he washes his hands and recites the blessing truly before performance. This is simple and correct, and the Beit Yosef’s question is sound.
