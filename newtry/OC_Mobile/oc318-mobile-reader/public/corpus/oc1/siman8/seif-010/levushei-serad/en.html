{Rama: Ibid} Siman 13 in the name of Rosh: one need not inspect even the strings—for we rely on its presumption, because people are not accustomed to invalidate it; he follows his view in siman 12, seif 1: even if all the strings snapped, if enough remains for a knot it is valid; therefore it is not common that it becomes invalid—unlike those who practice according to Rabbeinu Tam, as explained.
