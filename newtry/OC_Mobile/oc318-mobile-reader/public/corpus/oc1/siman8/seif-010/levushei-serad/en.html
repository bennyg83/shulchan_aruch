Ibid. Siman 13 in the name of the Rosh, that one need not check even the strings. For we place it on its presumption, since they are not commonly invalidated. He follows his view in siman 12, seif 1, that even if all the strings snapped, if enough remains to tie a bow it is valid. Therefore it is not so common that it becomes invalid, unlike according to our practice like Rabbeinu Tam, as explained.
