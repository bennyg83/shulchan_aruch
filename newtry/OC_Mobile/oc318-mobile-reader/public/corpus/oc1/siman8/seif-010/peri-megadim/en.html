If. See Taz. He raised a contradiction in the rulings of Shulchan Aruch: here he ruled that if he did not have initial intent, etc., implying even if some garments remained that he had in mind; while in Yoreh Deah siman 19, seif 7, the Mechaber ruled like the Semag that if some are still before him, he does not bless, and slaughter and tzitzis are similar. Why here does he bless again? As for what the Bach wrote, that eating and slaughter have a fixed framework, meaning that a person establishes himself to eat much or slaughter much, that does not appear correct, for slaughter is comparable to tzitzis. Regarding eating, ‘fixed’ is not applicable, meaning it has no fixed endpoint and one eating follows from another. It appears that the Taz agrees that if one already wore it and afterward more were brought, he blesses again; yet in Yoreh Deah 19, seif 6, with the same species he does not bless; see there. However, the Taz there wrote that we do not hold so; see there, letter 11, and Shach, letter 9, which is missing in some printings, and so is primary; see there. Nevertheless, what the Taz wrote from siman 206, that if some of the same kind is still before him, in the Levush below he wrote explicitly that even if it is not before him this is also so; see there, and see Eliyah Rabbah there, letter 9. See also Yoreh Deah there in Taz, letter 11, who wrote that according to the Rosh one can distinguish between fruit and slaughter, implying that by eating, even if the same kind is not before him, he nevertheless does not bless. This requires study, and with Hashem’s help I will explain further in siman 206.
