If Taz challenged the rulings of Shulchan Aruch that contradict one another — for here he ruled if he did not have intention initially, etc., and it implies even if there are some of those garments he had in mind; and in Yoreh De'ah siman 19, seif 7, the Mechaber ruled as Semag that if some still remain before him he does not bless; and shechitah and tzitzis are similar to one another. And what is written here that he blesses again, and what Bach wrote that eating and shechitah have kevi'us — meaning a person establishes himself to eat much and slaughter much — does not seem [correct], for shechitah is similar to tzitzis, and regarding eating there is no kevi'us — meaning it has no fixed time and one is dragged from eating to eating. And here it implies that Taz concedes if he already wore and afterward they brought him another, he blesses a second time; and in Yoreh De'ah 19, seif 6, from one species he does not bless — see there; except that Taz there wrote we do not rule thus — see there in note 11; and in Shach 9, in some editions it is not there, and so is the essential ruling — see there. Nevertheless, what Taz wrote from siman 206 — if there is still before him of that species — here in Orach Chayyim he wrote explicitly that even if none remains before him — see there; and see there note 9; and see there in Taz note 11 he wrote that for Rosh one may distinguish between fruits and shechitah — it implies that regarding eating, even if none of that species remains before him, nevertheless he does not bless — difficult; and I will explain further in siman 206.
