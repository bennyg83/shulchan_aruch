“He should handle the tzitzis.” The Beit Yosef asked: the rule is “before the act” — and he answered: nevertheless it is “before the act” in that he will fulfill afterward, and he brought proof from R. Yerucham on tefillin: if one forgot to bless before donning, one blesses as long as they are still on him. It seems this is unnecessary: “before the act” does not apply here, for that time he was not yet fit; so too Ran end of ch. 3 of Shabbos on eating: for immersion one blesses after the act because the man is not yet fit.
