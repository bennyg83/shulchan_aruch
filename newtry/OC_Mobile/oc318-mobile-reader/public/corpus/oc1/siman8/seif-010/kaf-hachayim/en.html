10) There. And it is proper that he cover his head with a tallit so that he pray with awe, for covering subdues a person's heart and brings him to fear of Heaven, as explained in the first chapter of Kiddushin. Bach; Olat Tamid, letter 3; Sh'tilei Z'taim, letter 5—and one must cover his head from the beginning of prayer until its end. Eliyah Rabbah, letter 4, in the name of Adnei Zahav; and likewise Shaarei Berachah, letter 7, in the name of the above and in the name of Sefer Mekor Chayyim; and likewise Chesed LeAlafim, letter 3—and when reciting the eighteen [blessings] it is good that he also cover his forehead and eyes if he is not before many people. Chesed LeAlafim there.
