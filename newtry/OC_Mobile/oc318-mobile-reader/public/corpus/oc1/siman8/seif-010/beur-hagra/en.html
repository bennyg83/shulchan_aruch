Seif 10, "if he wears," etc. From Berakhot 14b, as Tosafos wrote at the end of the gloss beginning "umanach," etc., and from the end of Menachos chapter 3, 36a, as Tosafos wrote there, gloss beginning "and when he reaches," etc. This is what he wrote: he should touch, etc.

"And recite the blessing." See Tosafos, Sukkah 39a, who cited the Yerushalmi that even after he has wrapped he can bless, since the mitzvah continues. Only when it is finished did they say at the end of Berakhot chapter 7 that he should not bless. Their intent is not that initially one should do so, for the Yerushalmi, last chapter of Berakhot, says: when does one bless over mitzvos? Rabbi Acha says before performing them; Rav Huna says at the time of performing them, and we rule like Rav Acha. Likewise regarding tefillin we say in Menachos chapter 3: from the time of placing, etc. Rather, this applies where there is no other possibility, as with lulav and here. In any event, in such a case even after the mitzvah was finished one could bless, as written in Berakhot there, since he was not fit; and in Pesachim chapter 1 and the Yerushalmi regarding lightning, cited by the Rosh in Berakhot chapter 9. "Or," etc., "and he need not," etc., as written in seif 12.
