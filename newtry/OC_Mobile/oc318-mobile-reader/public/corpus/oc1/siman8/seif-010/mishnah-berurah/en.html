(23) He handles the tzitzis — for through this it is considered somewhat as though he is wearing it then. And even though we maintain regarding all mitzvos that one must bless immediately before performing them, here it is different, because the person is not yet visible [in the garment].

(24) And he should intend to exempt — and such is the practice in our time: to bless on the tallis gadol and intend to exempt this tallis katan. And this is preferable to what some people do, who bless on the tallis katan and then immediately bless on the tallis gadol, thereby causing a berachah that is not needed. Furthermore, even if a long time passes between the tallis katan and the gadol, many times it is not appropriate to bless on it — because most of it is not open, or it is smaller than the measure explained below in siman 16, or he slept in it at night; as below in siman 16 in Mishna Berurah.
