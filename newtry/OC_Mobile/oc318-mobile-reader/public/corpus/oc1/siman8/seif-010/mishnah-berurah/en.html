(23) He should handle the tzitzis. By this it is considered somewhat as though he is wearing it then. Although we hold regarding all mitzvos that one must bless immediately before performing them, here it is different, because the person was not yet fit.
(24) And he should intend to exempt. This is the practice in our time, to bless over the tallis gadol and intend thereby to exempt the tallis katan. This is preferable to the practice of some people who bless over the tallis katan and immediately bless over the tallis gadol, thereby causing an unnecessary blessing. Moreover, even if much time passes between the tallis katan and tallis gadol, many times it is not proper to bless over it, because most of it is not open, or it is smaller than the measure explained below in siman 16, or he slept in it at night, as below in siman 16 in Mishnah Berurah.
