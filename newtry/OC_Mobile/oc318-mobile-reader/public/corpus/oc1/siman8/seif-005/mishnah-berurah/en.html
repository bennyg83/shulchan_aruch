(10) With tzitzis. With a sheva under the beis.
(11) With a tallis. The same applies to tefillin and to every blessing over a mitzvah.
(12) As one. This means that each one wraps himself in a separate tallis, but all the wrappings were done at one time, excluding after the time needed to say a short greeting after his blessing, which is an interruption, and each one must bless for himself. See below, siman 206, seif 3.
(13) And if they wished. From this wording it implies that it is better for all of them to bless. In truth, the opposite is so: lechatchilah it is better that one person bless and exempt the others, because “in a multitude of people is the King’s glory,” as below in siman 298, seif 14. However, the custom now is not so. Perhaps this is because not everyone is expert in intending to fulfill the obligation and to exempt others. See Shaarei Teshuvah.
(14) One recites the blessing. He exempts the others, even if they are expert, provided that the listener intends to fulfill his obligation and the one reciting the blessing intends to exempt him. More than this, even if the one reciting the blessing already fulfilled his own obligation, or is not wrapping himself at all, he may exempt others with his blessing, for all Jews are responsible for one another. However, regarding this there are those who say that he exempts only one who is not expert. Pri Megadim wrote: if several people bought new talleisim, made tzitzis on them, and did not recite Shehecheyanu, since they recite it at the first wrapping as explained in siman 22, although one can recite “to wrap” for all of them, the blessing of Shehecheyanu must be recited by each one himself.
(15) They should answer amen. Although in any event one must answer amen after every blessing, nevertheless for this blessing through which he wishes to fulfill his obligation, the obligation is greater, for by this he actively shows that he intends to fulfill his obligation with it. However, after the fact, even if he did not answer amen, he fulfilled his obligation, as below in siman 213, seif 2.
