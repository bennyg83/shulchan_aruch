{Rama: (Magen Avraham) seif kama 5.} He would remove it—for tzitzit is not an obligation of the body, but rather we bring ourselves under obligation to make a four-cornered garment of the minimum measure, and we fulfill the eighteen [blessings] through what one wears all day—that is the mitzvah's observance; but the body of the mitzvah is at the beginning of donning, after the blessing.
