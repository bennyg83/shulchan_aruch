“Tzitzis.” With a patach (Levush); Bach siman 14 wrote according to grammar to say with patach.

“Three.” Hagahos Maimoniyos / Hagahos Ashkenaz end Rosh Hashanah ch. 3 wrote that if one person wraps in tzitzis his fellow may bless for him; Taz siman 140 wrote that one who ties tefillin on another’s head presumably only the wearer blesses, etc.—we must say Hagahos Maimoniyos speaks when the wearer is not proficient in the berachah—see siman 585:2.

“And the others.” Accordingly the custom is that the shaliach tzibbur blesses aloud to discharge many (Darkei Moshe).
