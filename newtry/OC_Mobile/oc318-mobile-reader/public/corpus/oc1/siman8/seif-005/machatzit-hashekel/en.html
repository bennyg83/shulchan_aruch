(section 5) “And afterward,” etc. — for if he wished he could remove it, since one need not make for oneself a tallis that is obligated in tzitzis. Rather, one wears a tallis for the mitzvah of Hashem to make tzitzis in it; but we insert ourselves into wearing a tallis that is obligated in tzitzis. It is enough for him to wear it and stand for the distance of four amot, and afterward he may remove it. What he wears afterward is only habit, in order to fulfill “and you shall see it and remember,” etc. — as stated there.

“And for this reason standing is not required,” etc. — meaning: just as we require wrapping while standing, which we learn from the omer, so too we require shofar blowing while standing, for we also learn that from the omer, and there is one reason for both. Nevertheless we hold regarding the shofar that for the entire duration of the blowing one must stand — so why is standing not required for a tallis while it is on him? One must say: specifically for blowing, where the entire blowing is the mitzvah; not so here, where the essence of the mitzvah is at the time of donning and no more — as stated there in Terumat HaDeshen.
