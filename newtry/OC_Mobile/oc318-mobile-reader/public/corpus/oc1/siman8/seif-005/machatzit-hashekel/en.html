Seif katan 5, and afterward, etc. For if he wished he could remove it, since one need not make for himself a tallis obligated in tzitzis. Rather, if one wears a tallis, Hashem’s mitzvah is to make tzitzis in it. We bring ourselves into obligation by wearing a tallis obligated in tzitzis. It suffices for him to wear it and stand for the time it takes to walk four cubits, and after this he may remove it. What he wears it afterward is only an extension of the mitzvah, in order to fulfill “and you shall see it and remember,” etc. So it is there.
For this reason it does not require standing, etc. Meaning, just as we require wrapping while standing, which we learn from the Omer, so too we require shofar blowing while standing, for that too is learned from the Omer, and one reason applies to both. Nevertheless, regarding shofar we rule that throughout the duration of the blowing one must stand. Why do we not require by a tallis that as long as it is upon him he must stand? Necessarily one must say that only blowing is different, for all the blowing is part of the mitzvah. Here, however, the essence of the mitzvah is at the time of wearing and no more. So it is there in Terumas HaDeshen.
