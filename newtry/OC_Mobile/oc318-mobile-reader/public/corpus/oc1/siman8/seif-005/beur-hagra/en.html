Seif 5, "he recites the blessing," etc. Berakhot 60b, and in the Tosefta and Yerushalmi, last chapter of Berakhot.

"If two," etc. His words imply that it is more proper for each to bless for himself. This is not so; initially, specifically one blesses for all, as written in Berakhot chapter 8. So is the language of the Tosefta: if ten, etc., were doing one mitzvah, one blesses for all; an individual, etc. So too in Berakhot chapter 6 regarding after eating, etc.; and likewise the blessing of Hamotzi, Grace after Meals, and other blessings, as written there.

"And the others," etc. Initially; but after the fact, even if he did not answer, he fulfilled his obligation, as written in Sukkah chapter 3 and below siman 213, seif 2. There it implies that initially one should answer, and so too at the end of Berakhot chapter 8.
