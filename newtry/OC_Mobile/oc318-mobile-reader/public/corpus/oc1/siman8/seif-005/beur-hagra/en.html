(א) Seif 5, "he recites the blessing," etc. Berakhot 60b, and in the Tosefta and Yerushalmi, last chapter of Berakhot.

"If two," etc. His words imply that it is more proper for each one to bless for himself; but this is not so — initially, specifically one blesses for all, as written in Berachos chapter 8. So too is the language of the Tosefta: "Ten," etc. — they were performing one mitzvah; one blesses for all of them; an individual, etc. So too in Berachos chapter 6 regarding Birkas Hamazon, etc.; and likewise the blessing of Hamotzi, Grace after Meals, and other blessings, as written there.

"And the others," etc. This applies initially; but after the fact, even if he did not answer, he fulfilled his obligation, as written in Sukkah chapter 3 and as stated below, siman 213, seif 2. There it implies that initially one should answer; and so too at the end of Berachos chapter 8.
