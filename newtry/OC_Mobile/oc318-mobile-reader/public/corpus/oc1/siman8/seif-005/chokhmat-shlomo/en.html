(Seif 16) He slept in his tallit, etc. In my view: if at the time of the blessing his intent was that the garment is not exempted until noon or the like — whether he is obligated to bless afterwards or not. See in the Magen Avraham, laws of Sukkah (siman 639, comment 17) that plainly he need not bless again; but in Iggeres Azriel I saw he wrote that in truth yes; therefore for practical law it is uncertain now; examine carefully.
