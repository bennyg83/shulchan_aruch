And he blesses standing—Rabbi Shemen HaMaor wrote whose language is: behold, the author of Shulchan Aruch did not explain to us the law regarding after the fact—if he wrapped while seated, whether after the fact he must return and wrap standing. He answered: only Magen Avraham in seif kama 2, who wrote see what is written in siman 585—Peri Megadim wrote his intent is as there in seif 1 regarding shofar: if he blew while seated he fulfilled, and the same here if he wrapped while seated he fulfilled, and this is correct—until here. And so Chayei Adam, klal 12, letter 3: if he blessed while seated he fulfilled; and so Amudei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 9, letter 13; and so Tefillah LeDavid, letter 18: if he wrapped while seated and blessed while seated he fulfilled. And what Eliyah Rabbah wrote in seif kama 2—see Shemen HaMaor there, who rejected his words—see there.
