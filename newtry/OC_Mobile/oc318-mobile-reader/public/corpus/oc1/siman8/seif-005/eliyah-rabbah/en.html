[5] Wide for his height, etc. The same applies if the length of the tallit matches the man's height — it is valid and we have no concern, Bach. The shoulders should be made wide so air from this side and that side does not enter and nullify it — so wrote the Agur and Mateh Moshe. The Bach wrote at the end of siman 11: it is proper to do initially the mitzvah in the optimal way, as the Baal HaIttur wrote — fix the tzitzis in two holes and hang them outside on one side; but since it appears ostentatious, therefore do not change the tzitzis openly on the tallit one wears, unlike the world's custom; but on a tallit katan fix it thus, until here. I also found Mahari Luria did so — two holes close together like the vowel tzere. See Magen Avraham, siman 11, comment 13.
