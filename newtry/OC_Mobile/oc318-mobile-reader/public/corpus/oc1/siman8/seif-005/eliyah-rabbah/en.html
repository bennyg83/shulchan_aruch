And the others should answer, etc. Hagahos Ashri at the end of chapter 3 of Rosh Hashanah wrote that if one person wraps himself in tzitzis, his fellow may bless for him. In Terumas HaDeshen, siman 140, he wrote that one who ties tefillin onto his fellow’s head presumably should not bless, but rather the wearer should bless, end quote. One must say that Hagahos Ashri speaks where the one wrapping himself is not expert in the blessing, end quote, language of the Magen Avraham. This is not correct, for Hagahos Ashri wrote afterward, ‘and if he himself does not know, etc.,’ implying that the opening clause speaks even where he does know. There is no difficulty at all from Terumas HaDeshen, for in truth, presumably the one performing the mitzvah blesses; Hagahos Ashri merely says that his fellow can bless, and this is clear.
