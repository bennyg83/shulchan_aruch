Taz, se'if katan 9: 'At the end of the third chapter SheAchlu regarding immersion, etc.' This requires study, because the cases are not comparable. There, no alternative was possible; here he can wait and put on the garment after washing, so the principle that 'the person was not fit' does not apply. I saw that Mishbetzot Zahav seeks to resolve this; see there, but it still requires study. Perhaps the intent is as follows. At first glance, Beit Yosef implies that even after the time when he could have blessed has passed, he blesses over what he will fulfill afterward, as is shown from tefillin. Taz answers that this proof is unnecessary. Even if in such a case one ordinarily cannot bless over what he will fulfill afterward, here the person was then unfit; the time is therefore not considered to have passed, because he was unable to bless. He may properly bless over his later fulfillment of the mitzvah. Taz cites immersion to show that the time for the blessing is not considered to have passed when the person was not yet fit. The same applies here. This appears correct in my humble opinion.
