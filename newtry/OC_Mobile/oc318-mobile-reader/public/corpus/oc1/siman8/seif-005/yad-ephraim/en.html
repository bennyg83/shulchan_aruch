Taz, note 9. Section 3 — that they ate, regarding immersion, etc. — it is questionable, for the case under discussion is not comparable: there it is impossible in another matter, unlike here, where he can wait and wear other [garments] after washing — “a person is not fit” does not apply. I saw in Mishbetzos that he wants to reconcile this — see there — and it is questionable. It is possible that his intent is that apparently, per the Beit Yosef’s words, it implies that even though the time passed when he could have blessed, he blesses on what he will fulfill later, as he likened it from tefillin. To this he says that this is unnecessary: even if you say that in such a case one should not bless on what he will fulfill later, nevertheless in this case, when then the person was not fit, the time is not called “passed” when it was not in his power to bless, and he properly blesses on fulfillment of the mitzvah afterward; and for this he brings from immersion, that the time is not called “passed” for the obligation of the blessing, since the person is still not fit — and similarly here, as seems correct to me.
