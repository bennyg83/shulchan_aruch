On — Taz: See Levush 65, for they are concerned that perhaps the mitzvah specifically requires wrapping, and a small tallit such as this does not involve wrapping the head and part of the body — therefore they bless "on the mitzvah of tzitzis," praise for the past, that He gave us this mitzvah and we are not now fulfilling it. And the question of Mahari in Beit Yosef — "for the past" — is resolved as concluded in Pesachim. And even though in Talmud Bavli on biur there is a reason as Tosafos wrote there, not so here — it would have been better to bless "to dress" plainly than "to wrap oneself," but for the past we give praise; nevertheless that is only a reason for custom, but the essence is certainly that we establish wrapping is not alluded to, and one fulfills through it — for if not, from where would we innovate a blessing after the sealing of the Talmud? What the Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 8 — one should intend that He commanded us in order to remember all the commandments of God — Bach wrote that here, and in tefillin and sukkah (siman 625), we require that one also intend this, for in Torah the reason is explained; not so other mitzvos, that if one intended to fulfill alone he fulfills — see there. And here, if he did not so intend, he does not act properly — see there. Therefore for pidyon haben too, a reason is written — there are those who explain.
