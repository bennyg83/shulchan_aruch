Seif 7, "one must separate," etc. Like Rashi's explanation, and so wrote all the decisors, unlike the Baal HaIttur, who first wrote to separate the techeles to one side and wrote that it is proper to be precise as a remembrance of techeles; according to this one need not separate all the tzitzis strings. In the Tur and Shulchan Aruch it says "from one another," as the Tur wrote: they are called tzitzis because of separated strands, as it says, "He took me by the locks of my head"; therefore one must separate them, etc. So it also appears in the Gemara, which says: tzitzis is nothing, etc., as it is stated, etc., like the forelock of an Aramean.
