(18) One from another — each thread by itself, so they are not tangled one with another. And if one delayed coming to the synagogue, and thereby would miss praying with the congregation, Magen Avraham wrote that one need not be particular about this. And see Biur Halacha[2]. The Ari wrote: "tzitzt" is an acronym: a righteous person separates his tzitzis always.
