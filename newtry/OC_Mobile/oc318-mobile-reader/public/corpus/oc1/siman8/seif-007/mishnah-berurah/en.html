(18) From one another. Each thread should be separated by itself, so that they are not tangled with one another. If he delayed coming to the synagogue, and through this he will lose praying with the congregation, the Magen Avraham wrote that he need not be particular about this. See Biur Halachah. The Arizal wrote: the word tzitzis is an acronym for “a righteous person should always separate his tzitzis.”
