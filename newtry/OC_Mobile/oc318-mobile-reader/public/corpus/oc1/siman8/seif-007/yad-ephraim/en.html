Magen Avraham, note 14. It is like one who conversed in the middle of the meal, who need not bless; but if he conversed in the interim, the later authorities ruled that one must bless — therefore let every person take care not to converse in the interim, as written in siman 215 and siman 166, etc. — thus should read; and he means that there it is written not to converse so as not to cause a blessing that is not needed — all the more so here, where according to some authorities one should not bless at all; it is possible that this [word] is missing, and therefore “let him take care,” etc., in its place should come only what is written above in siman 215 and siman 166 — it should read at the end of this note: thus should read — it is forbidden to cause a blessing that is not needed, as written in siman 215 and siman 166.
