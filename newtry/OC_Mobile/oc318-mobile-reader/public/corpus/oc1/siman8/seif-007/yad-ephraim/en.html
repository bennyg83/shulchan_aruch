Magen Avraham, se'if katan 14. 'It is like one who spoke during a meal and need not recite another blessing. If, however, he diverted his attention in the interim, the later authorities concluded that he must bless. Every person should therefore take care not to speak in the interim, as stated in siman 215 and at the beginning of siman 166, etc.' This is the correct text. It means that those passages instruct one not to speak so that he will not cause an unnecessary blessing. This certainly applies here, where according to some poskim he must not recite another blessing at all. It is also possible that the words 'Every person should therefore take care, etc.' belong in their present place, but the references to siman 215 and siman 166 should be moved to the end of this se'if katan. The text would then explain that it is forbidden to cause an unnecessary blessing, as stated in siman 215 and siman 166.
