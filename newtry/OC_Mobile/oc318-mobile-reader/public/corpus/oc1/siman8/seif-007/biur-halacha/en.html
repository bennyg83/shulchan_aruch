One must separate, etc. — see in the Mishna Berurah what we wrote there, that if it was left, etc. The reason of the Magen Avraham is that today this is only a remembrance of the time of techelet, when by law one had to separate the techelet from the white. See in Biur HaGra, who challenges this; his view is that even today one must by law separate each thread from the other. Afterwards I found in Eshel Avraham that he also wrote like the Gra. If so, according to them it is possible that if the tzitzis strings are tangled one with another one may not bless on them; or it is possible that it is no worse than worn-out tzitzis — this requires study. In any case, to begin with one certainly must separate them if they are tangled before he blesses on them.
