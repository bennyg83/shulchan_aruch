(Seif katan 2) On Chol HaMoed, etc. Meaning, he acts because of uncertainty whether Chol HaMoed is obligated in tefillin. According to this, one must necessarily say that he does not recite a blessing over them. If he recites a blessing, how can it be said that he acts only because of doubt? He is not permitted to recite a blessing in a doubtful case, because that may be a blessing in vain. See Taz, seif katan 2.
