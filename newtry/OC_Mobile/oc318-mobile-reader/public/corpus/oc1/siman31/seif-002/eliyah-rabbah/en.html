However in Lechem Chamudos he wrote the explicit reason in Zohar that he holds Chol HaMoed is prohibited in melacha from the Torah, and we do not hold thus, as below at the beginning of Laws of Chol HaMoed, see there. Maharshal, first chapter of Yom Tov siman 3, found support that "the Lord's name is proclaimed upon you" hints to the shin of tefillin, for shin is the sum of the days of the year that one dons tefillin — for when you remove 52 Sabbaths and 13 festival days from 365, 300 days remain.
