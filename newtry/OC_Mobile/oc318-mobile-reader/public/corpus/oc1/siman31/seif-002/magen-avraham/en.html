On Chol HaMoed it is likewise forbidden. One who puts them on on Chol HaMoed does not violate bal tosif because he does not intend to add. Semak. Meaning, he acts because of the doubt whether Chol HaMoed is obligated in tefillin. See Eruvin there, siman 668, what I wrote in siman 29, and the end of siman 25.
They too are a sign. On Pesach, eating matzah is the sign; on Sukkos, dwelling in the sukkah is the sign. The dissenting opinion maintains that since labor is permitted on Chol HaMoed, there is no sign.
Aloud. It appears to me that one should not walk with them in the public domain, but should put them on only in the synagogue.
