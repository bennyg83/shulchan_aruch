On Chol HaMoed likewise forbidden. And one who dons on Chol HaMoed does not transgress bal tosif, since he does not intend to add (Semak) — meaning he does because of doubt whether perhaps Chol HaMoed is obligated in tefillin; see there Eruvin; and responsum 5648; see what is written siman 29 and siman 25.

They are also a sign. On Pesach — eating matzah; and on Sukkot — sitting in the sukkah; and the Rishonim hold that since melacha is permitted, there is no sign.

Aloud. And it seems one should not walk with them in reshut ha-rabbim, only don them in beit ha-knesset.
