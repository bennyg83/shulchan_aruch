And so is the practice, etc. It seems to me nevertheless that one who dons without a blessing on Chol HaMoed does well, since we hold everywhere that blessings are not me'akev; and the Zohar greatly distinguishes the penalty of donning them on Chol HaMoed; and the Rosh himself wrote in his conclusion, and one who blesses has not lost — meaning there is no obligation to bless, and he did not see the book of Zohar; and so too in Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 4 of Laws of Tefillin in the name of Semak that one should don them without a blessing, for one only transgresses bal tosif when intending to add, as above. And it seems to me furthermore that one who dons on Chol HaMoed should remove them before Hallel, and all the more so before Torah reading, for they come because of the festival and teach about the essence of the sign; not so on Rosh Chodesh, where the reason is not because of a sign but because of sanctity of the crown — therefore the remover removes before Musaf, as written siman 25; in my humble opinion this is correct. And the custom that Rama wrote — that one does not bless aloud — is astonishing: before Heaven, who divides between hidden and revealed?
