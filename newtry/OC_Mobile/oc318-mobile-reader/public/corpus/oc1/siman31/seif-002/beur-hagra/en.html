Seif 2 — "On Chol HaMoed," etc. Meaning on Pesach (matzah) and Sukkot (sukkah); and so too in Hilchot Gedolot and Sefer HaItim in the name of the Gaon; and so too in Zohar part 1, page 3.
"And there are those," etc. As written in Moed Katan 19a — if to don them after the festival, "one writes for himself," "one writes for others" — but there is no proof from this at all; Rabbi Meir and Rabbi Yehuda each per their reasons, for they hold in the last chapter of Eruvin that Shabbat and Yom Tov are time for tefillin, as written in Moed Katan there; and so the Rosh in Hilchot Ketanot rejected this proof; and so Tosafos Menachos 36b s.v. yatz'u; and see Tosafos Moed Katan 19a s.v. Rabbi Yosei; and in Eruvin 96a s.v. yamim; and from the Yerushalmi that Tosafos brought in Moed Katan there — and there is no proof at all, for there it is per one who says Shabbat is time for tefillin, as the baraita states there disputes on it — Rav: a person writes tefillin, etc., for himself; and he resolves the baraita per Rav; and the baraita is Rabbi Yehuda, who says one writes, etc., for he holds Shabbat and Yom Tov are time for tefillin; and see Nissim in that sugya there — and this opinion has no main basis at all, for that which Tosafos brought in Eruvin — its meaning is slightly from the sugya in the first chapter of Beitza regarding sending vessels between sewn, etc., and it says to include tefillin, see there — this is not so, for it is an error in the baraita, for in Yerushalmi chapter 1 of tractate Beitza it is explicit in the mishnah: even though not for festival need; and so must be in the mishnah above; and if so there is no meaning at all in that sugya above — see there.
