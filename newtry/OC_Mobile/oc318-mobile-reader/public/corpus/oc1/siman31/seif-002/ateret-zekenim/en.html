And there are those who say that on Chol HaMoed one is obligated in tefillin — a hint to the matter: "the Lord's name is proclaimed upon you" refers to the shin of tefillin; and shin is the sum of the days one dons tefillin in a year, for when you remove 52 Sabbaths and 13 festival days from 365, 300 days remain. If so, according to this calculation one must don tefillin on Chol HaMoed (Maharshal and Semag).
