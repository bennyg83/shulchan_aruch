On Chol HaMoed it is likewise prohibited to wear tefillin, for this very reason — that the days of Chol HaMoed are also a sign.There are those who maintain that one is obligated to wear tefillin on Chol HaMoed [Beit Yosef] in the name of the Rosh], and in all these lands the custom is to don them on the festival and bless upon them; only one does not bless upon' them aloud in the synagogue as on other days of the year.
