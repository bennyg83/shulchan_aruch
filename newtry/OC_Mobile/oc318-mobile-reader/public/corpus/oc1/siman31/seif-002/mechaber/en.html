On Chol HaMo'ed it is likewise forbidden to don tefillin, for this very reason — that the days of Chol HaMo'ed are also a sign. {Rama: And some say that on Chol HaMo'ed one is obligated in tefillin [Beis Yosef in the name of the Rosh]; and so is the custom in all these regions, to don them on Chol HaMo'ed and to recite a blessing over them — except that one does not recite the blessing over them aloud in the synagogue, as on the other days of the year.}
