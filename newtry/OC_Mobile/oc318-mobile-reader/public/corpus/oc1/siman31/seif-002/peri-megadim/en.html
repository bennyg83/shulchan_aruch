And so — Taz. He agreed not to bless on Chol HaMoed; and furthermore, if he blesses he appears to intend for the mitzvah, and perhaps he is not obligated and he also transgresses bal tosif — not so when he does not bless and dons from doubt, bal tosif does not apply, for all he does is from doubt — Magen Avraham note 2. And what he wrote to remove them before Hallel — now they are accustomed to remove after the Kedushah of the Shemoneh Esrei prayer; nevertheless one must intend to hear the repetition of the prayer. And Rabbeinu Tam tefillin one should not don on Chol HaMoed, for the Zohar greatly distinguishes the penalty; and Rashi tefillin suffice, and likewise there is doubt whether the law is Chol HaMoed exempt, and whether the law follows Rashi.

And behold Shabbat is a sign between Me and you; and tefillin: "and it shall be for you as a sign upon your hand" — meaning: thereby we are servants of the Lord, for a servant bears the king's seal; therefore on Shabbat there is another sign; and Yom Tov, even though "sign" is not written regarding Shabbat, it is called so; but Chol HaMoed is not called Shabbat — see Rashi Menachos 36b "they themselves are a sign," as it is written "for it is a sign" — and this is on Shabbat (Shemos 31:13, 17). And I, the layman who writes, were I not afraid I would say that what is written (Bereishis 1:14) "and they shall be for signs and for festivals" — "signs" means Yom Tov days are called a sign, and "festivals" means Rosh Chodesh, for Rosh Chodesh is also called mo'ed; and see Laws of Rosh Chodesh siman 419, see there in Tur and Levush; and I would say "and the luminaries shall be for signs" for Yom Tov that are called signs, and "for festivals" for Rosh Chodesh; and Rashi explained when the luminaries strike, etc. What Taz wrote — blessing secretly — Rabbi [Akiva Eiger] justified because of the dispute that some say to bless and some not, as in Shechem where they say secretly, see there; and Levush justified that we need to instruct that there is somewhat of a sign on Chol HaMoed, see there in Levush. If one may interrupt on Chol HaMoed between Shema and Shemoneh Esrei — see siman 25, Magen Avraham note 17, see there.
