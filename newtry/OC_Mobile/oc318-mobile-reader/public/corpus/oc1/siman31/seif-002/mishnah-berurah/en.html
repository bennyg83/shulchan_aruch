(6) They are also a sign — on Pesach, eating matzah; and on Sukkot, sitting in the sukkah; and the Rishonim hold that since melacha is permitted from the Torah, there is no sign.

(7) To don them on the festival — and removal of the tefillin should be on Chol HaMoed before Hallel. And now some people are accustomed to remove them after the Kedushah of the Shemoneh Esrei prayer; nevertheless they must be careful to intend to hear the repetition of the prayer.

(8) "That one does not," etc. — meaning: because some don and some do not don, or some do not bless — one should bless secretly so as not to come to dispute; and likewise one should not walk with them in reshut ha-rabbim to the synagogue; and the Acharonim agreed with Taz that it is better to don them without a blessing, since blessings are not me'akev and doubt of blessings is to be lenient; and in particular the Gra, may he rest in Eden, wrote that the view of the Rishonim has no main basis in the Gemara; and in any case regarding blessing one should certainly be stringent. Also before donning he should think in his mind: if I am obligated, I don for the sake of the mitzvah; if not, I do not don for the sake of the mitzvah — and thereby he fulfills the view of all: for even according to those who hold Chol HaMoed is not time for tefillin, he does not transgress bal tosif since he does not intend in donning for the sake of the mitzvah with certainty; and all the more so one need not be concerned for prohibition of disrespecting the sign of Chol HaMoed, for that too is only when intending for the mitzvah, as above. And Rabbeinu Tam tefillin one should not don on Chol HaMoed [Pri Megadim]. The Acharonim also wrote [and they are brought in Eliyah Rabbah, see there] that it is not proper that in one synagogue some don tefillin and some do not, because of "do not divide yourselves"; and one who does not don tefillin on Chol HaMoed but prays in a synagogue where they don — he too should don them, without a blessing; and a congregation that is accustomed to don tefillin should not change their custom.
