It is forbidden to don — see Mishna Berurah; and behold the source of the law — see below siman 308 seif 4 in Taz and Magen Avraham there. And behold in Chol HaMoed laws of muktzeh we generally are stringent that one may not move them except for their body or place; so appears in Birchei Yosef in this siman; however in Chayei Adam it appears that so they not fall — and likewise so they not be stolen — it is also permitted to move them; so appears in Pri Megadim siman 308 s"k 3 in Mizbeach, see there; and the entire law is explained in siman 308, depending whether donning tefillin on Shabbat and Yom Tov not for the mitzvah is forbidden; if so, according to what we bring below that the Gra is also among the lenient on this, automatically he is also lenient regarding muktzeh; therefore certainly one may be lenient in time of need, as I wrote inside.

It would be disrespect — see what I wrote in Mishna Berurah — that is, one who dons them, etc., is from Magen Avraham above siman 29; but it is not explained in his words whether there is at least a rabbinic prohibition; see Bach, whose words imply there is no prohibition in donning them even rabbinically; his intent is when donning not for the mitzvah, for otherwise anyway there is bal tosif; and from the Gra in his commentary siman 308 end s"k 7 it also appears that when not intending in donning on Shabbat and Yom Tov, rabbinically it is not forbidden to don them.
