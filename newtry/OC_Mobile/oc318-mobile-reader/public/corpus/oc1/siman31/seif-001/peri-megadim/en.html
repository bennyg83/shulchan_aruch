Dispute of the Rishonim: some hold matzah and sukkah are the sign; some hold specifically melacha prohibition is the sign; and some hold Chol HaMoed is prohibited in some melachot; some hold from the Torah it is permitted, only rabbinically forbidden on Chol HaMoed — see responsum 5648, Magen Avraham note 1; and Rabbi Akiva Eiger note 2, that the Zohar according to its view that Chol HaMoed is prohibited — see there what he challenged from Yoreh De'ah siman 265 that one should not remove tefillin until after milah, since milah is called a sign and tefillin a sign — on the contrary, one would disparage the sign of milah; one may say that on weekdays we don tefillin even though we have the sign of milah, as Levush wrote siman 29 that two witnesses we require, and it suffices; and on Shabbat there is milah and Shabbat — these are the words of Semag and Abudraham that he brought there.
