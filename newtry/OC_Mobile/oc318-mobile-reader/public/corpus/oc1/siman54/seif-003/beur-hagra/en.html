Seif 3: And some say, etc. This is not deemed a transgression, as Rashba writes concerning the passage at the end of the third chapter of Menachos, 'The tanna explained, etc.' They termed it a transgression only where one interrupts for a discretionary matter. Rosh likewise writes there that one does not return from the battle lines because of it. Although he writes that a prohibition nevertheless exists, and Tur and Shulchan Aruch rule accordingly in siman 25, there the reason is that he causes an unnecessary blessing, which does not apply here. See Beit Yosef there and Magen Avraham there, seif katan 17.
But between, etc. From that point it is considered as though Yotzer has begun. See below, siman 66. This also appears in the Midrash. See also siman 53, seif 3, and what is written there.
