Between Kaddish. See Mishnah Berurah, where I wrote that the interval between Kaddish and Barechu has the status of between sections. Eliyah Rabbah writes this, and Peri Megadim cites it. This is difficult, for Rama rules in siman 80 in the gloss and in siman 66, seif 2, that regarding tefillin one may at least recite the blessing between sections, as is also evident from seif 8 there; see the glosses of Rabbi Akiva Eiger and Peri Megadim, letter 4, there. Why, then, does Rama state that one may not interrupt for anything, when regarding tefillin, the very subject discussed immediately beforehand, one may interrupt? One cannot answer that Rama merely copied the wording of Or Zarua but did not accept it, because in the Or Zarua quoted in Darchei Moshe tefillin are not mentioned at all, only a tallis. Perhaps Rama states this according to the view of the 'some say' in the gloss there, rather than according to his own ruling. I later saw that Biur HaGra writes, 'But between, etc., for from that point onward it is as though Yotzer has begun.' This implies that from the beginning of Kaddish onward it has the status of the middle of a section. Everything is then resolved if we maintain there that interruption in the middle of a section is forbidden, as Rabbi Akiva Eiger and Peri Megadim hold. Yet Biur HaGra there, in seif 2, clearly indicates that according to Rama, who is lenient regarding tefillin, it is permitted even in the middle of a section. It therefore requires study why Rama is stringent here regarding tefillin. We must consequently rely on our first answer, that Rama here follows the opinion of the 'some say' there.
