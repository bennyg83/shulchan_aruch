Between Kaddish—see in Mishna Berurah what I wrote that between Kaddish and Barekhu the law is as between the chapters—so too Rabbi Akiva Eiger—and Pri Megadim copied this. It is difficult for me: for behold Rama ruled in siman 80 in the gloss and in siman 66, seif 2, regarding tefillin that one may bless at least between the chapters, as is also proven in section 8 of the gloss—see Rabbi Akiva Eiger's novelties and Pri Megadim letter 4 on that point. If so, why did he say that one may not interrupt for anything—is tefillin, which was discussed above from him, not permitted? And one could say that Rama copied the language of Or Zarua, and he did not hold so—for behold in Or Zarua brought in Darkhei Moshe, tefillin is not mentioned at all, only tallit. And it is possible Rama says this per Hagahot Maimoniyot's view in that gloss and not his own opinion. Again I saw in Be'ur HaGra that he wrote: but between, etc., for from there onward it is like the beginning of Yotzer, end quote—implies from the beginning of Kaddish onward it is like the middle of the chapter; if so everything is resolved if we hold on that view that in the middle of a chapter it is forbidden, as Rabbi Akiva Eiger's novelties and Pri Megadim state. However, in Be'ur HaGra on seif 2 it is proven from him that Rama, who is lenient regarding tefillin, permits even in the middle of the chapter.
