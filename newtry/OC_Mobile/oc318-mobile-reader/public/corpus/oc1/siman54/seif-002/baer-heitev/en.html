With praises. Our text reads, 'God, the great King, with praises.' The word 'b'sishbachos' is pronounced with a chirik beneath the tav, according to the Arizal. The Knesses HaGedolah likewise wrote this in the name of Radak. See above, siman 51, seif katan 1. 'Chei ha'olamim' is pronounced with a tzerei beneath the ches. Rabbi Shabsai wrote this; see Tosafos Yom Tov, Tamid, chapter 7. This is not the view of Maharal of Prague, who pronounces it with a patach. See Ateres Zekeinim.
