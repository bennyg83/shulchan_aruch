(2) "With praises"—this was his wording, but our wording is "Hashem, great King, praised with praises." The meaning is that after the prayer leader finishes Yishtabach with "Hashem, great King, praised with praises," no one should answer amen to that, for it is not yet the end of the blessing until he also says "Hashem of thanksgivings," etc., until "who lives forever." In the wording "who lives forever," some read it with tzere and some with patach; even according to that opinion, nevertheless one should say "the worlds" with a heh, for its meaning is that He lives in both worlds and rules in this world and the World to Come.

(3) For there it is—uncertain whether one may then interrupt to answer Kaddish and Kedushah, for it is one utterance until the end; see Chayye Adam, general rule 5, seif 13, that in any short blessing it is forbidden to interrupt in the middle, even for Kaddish and Kedushah—and there is room to distinguish; uncertain.
