"And what Magen Avraham writes," that one may therefore say that this was also Rambam's intent, etc. Meaning: Likkutei HaPardes likewise first states generally that after a verse or Mishnah one should recite Kaddish, and its general statement is clarified by what it says afterward, that after a Mishnah one should say a passage of aggadah in order to sanctify through Kaddish. Rambam's words can therefore be understood in the same manner. Although he writes generally concerning the study of Oral Torah, his intent is that a passage of aggadah should be said afterward. When Rambam writes "even with Midrash," etc., he means that even if one studies only that material by itself, Kaddish DeRabbanan is recited. All the more so, if one first studies Oral Torah and then follows it with words of aggadah, Kaddish should be recited.
