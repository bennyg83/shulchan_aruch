And what Magen Avraham wrote, "if so one may say that Rambam's intention," etc.—meaning, since also in Lekuttei Pardes he wrote at first in general that after a verse or mishna one must say Kaddish, and the general statement accords with his explanation afterward that after a mishna one says aggadah in order to sanctify—if so, Rambam's words are also explained thus, that although he wrote in general Oral Torah, meaning when he says aggadah after it. And what Rambam wrote "even in midrash," etc.—meaning that although one is occupied only with this alone, he says Kaddish derabbanan; and all the more if one studies Oral Torah first and says aggadic words after it, that he says Kaddish.
