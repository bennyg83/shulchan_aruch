Magen Avraham seif 3—that is, like Torah reading, etc. It seems to me this is a scribal error; what he wrote "like Torah reading" fell into this scribal error in Lekuttei Pardes in the printed edition, because there afterward he wrote "and likewise on Shabbat after they read the Torah," etc. It is possible it was written "and likewise in Torah reading on Shabbat after," etc., and they erred in the printing and wrote the words "in Torah reading" not in their place. And thus it must be—that is, in order to sanctify, etc.; or it is possible it should read that it is like Torah reading, meaning that this statement of Rabbi Elazar is as though he read Written Torah, that it is appropriate to say Kaddish after it, for one does not sanctify except at the end of a verse, etc.
