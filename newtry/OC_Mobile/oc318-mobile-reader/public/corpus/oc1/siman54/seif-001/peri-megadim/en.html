This one is before them (Turei Zahav). For it is adjacent, and therefore it does not open with "Baruch"; if so, without Baruch She'amar, even though he said pesukei dezimra, he should not say Yishtabach, for they are adjacent—and Magen Avraham wrote there. See Ateret Tzvi letter 2: there is a shortcoming, for he wrote only "without pesukei dezimra," see there. And pesukei dezimra are not an interruption between them, like "true and established" and "faith" in Shema, "let Him be praised" in Hallel—see Beit Chadash here.
