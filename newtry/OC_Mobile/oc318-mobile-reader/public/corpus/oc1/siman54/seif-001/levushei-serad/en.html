Magen Avraham, seif katan 4 'For Kaddish, see siman 53, seif 3.' Meaning, although siman 53, seif 3 states that specifically an individual should put on his tallis after Yishtabach, because an interruption after Yishtabach is less severe than before Yishtabach, since Yishtabach is the concluding blessing following Pesukei DeZimrah, whereas the chazzan should put it on before Yishtabach because he must recite Kaddish after Yishtabach, see there, this applies only when the tallis was brought to him before Yishtabach. If it was brought after Yishtabach, he should put it on, and he may recite several verses of Pesukei DeZimrah before Kaddish. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 3.
