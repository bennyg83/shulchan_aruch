And the wall was breached on Shabbat — meaning in its full [length] or more than ten amot.

To karmelis forbidden — see Beit Yosef who wrote this means forbidden to carry in the courtyard beyond four amot, but within four amot permitted even to carry from it to karmelis which is also like karmelis of water; Magen Avraham doubts this — perhaps the courtyard still lies upon it. Tosafos Shabbat 9 brought proof to permit, and so Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger and Nehar Shalom; in Avodat HaKodesh Sha'ar HaReshuyot din 1 it states explicitly forbidden, and so Tosafos there proved from Rashi 24 to forbid — see there; therefore it appears we should not be lenient:
