(Siman 2) Each one, etc. — specifically when the opening is sealed; Rambam disagrees only on the beginning of the seif:

(Siman 2) For even though the eruv, etc. — meaning: if we do not permit each only for itself, then why do we say once permitted for one — even though the eruv lies in another courtyard? In such a case it would be fit to forbid carrying even in the courtyard where the eruv is not placed since it cannot bring its eruv there; nevertheless since at the start of Shabbat it was possible to bring its eruv there, we say once permitted, permitted:
