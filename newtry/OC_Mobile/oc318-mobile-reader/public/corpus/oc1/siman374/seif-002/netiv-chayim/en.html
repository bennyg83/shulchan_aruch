Note: truly they initially explain thus; but afterward Tosafos conclude Yerushalmi does not hold thus — a roof protruding four tefachim, since it lacks partitions, is a makom patur; and they explain all these passages per Yerushalmi — thus even with less than four protrusion it is makom patur; but Rosh, Tur, and Shulchan Aruch ruled like Rashi's view — for them our roofs are karmelis even without four protrusion:
