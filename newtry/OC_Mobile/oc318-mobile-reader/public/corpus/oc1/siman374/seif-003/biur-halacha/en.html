(טו) And the small one forbidden, etc. — for it breached in its full [length]. The rule "full" applies where the courtyard lacks a strip of four [amot] or any strip from here and there [or one tefach per one view] — called "full" — forbidden if breached to a forbidden place; if breached to a courtyard they need one eruv for both; if the breach is more than ten amot it is also a breach and forbidden [Peri Megadim]:

(יח) And when walls of the small one protrude three tefachim, etc. — not precise — for even one wall suffices [Gra]; meaning even if only one wall protrudes three tefachim and the other was less than three, still forbidden — for what they said lavud on one side requires specifically a strip from here and there or a strip four tefachim wide on one side:
