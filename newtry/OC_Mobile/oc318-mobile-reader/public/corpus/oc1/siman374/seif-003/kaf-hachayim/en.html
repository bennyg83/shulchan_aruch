And it was sealed on Shabbat, etc. — but if sealed while still day, even if it reopened at night they are forbidden, for they never acquired an eruv at all — acquisition of eruv is at bein hashemashot, and here there was no eruv at bein hashemashot since the opening was sealed; and from nightfall they do not acquire eruv since we do not join at nightfall; and even if they joined before the Shabbat that passed and that eruv still remains, they cannot rely on it this Shabbat after the opening opened, since it was nullified while still day and does not revive at nightfall.
