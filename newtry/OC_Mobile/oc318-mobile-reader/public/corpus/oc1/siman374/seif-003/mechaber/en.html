A small courtyard breached before Shabbat' in its full [length] to a large one' and the breach is not more than ten amot' — the large one is permitted to take out utensils that remained in the house to its courtyard if it joined for itself; and the small one is forbidden to take out utensils that remained in the house to its courtyard unless they joined together; and this is when the walls of the small one enter into the large one; and when the walls of the small one protrude three tefachim from the length-walls of the large one — for if not, the small one would be permitted via visible from outside and equal from inside:
