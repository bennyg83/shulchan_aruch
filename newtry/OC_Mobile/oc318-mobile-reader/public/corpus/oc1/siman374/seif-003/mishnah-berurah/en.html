(י) That was breached before Shabbat — for if on Shabbat we say once permitted, permitted:

(יא) In its full [length] to the large one — meaning they were adjacent and a wall separated them and this entire wall was breached:

(יב) And there is not in the breach more than ten amot — for if more than ten we cannot say the small one is like the opening of the large one — an opening more than ten amot people do not make:

(יג) The large one is permitted, etc. — and the small one does not forbid it, for the large one has sides and the place of the wall breach of the small one is like its opening:

(יד) To take out utensils that remained in the house, etc. — for utensils that remained in the courtyard are permitted l'chatchila to take out even to another courtyard even without joining together:

(טו) If it joined for itself — but if it did not join, it too is forbidden:

(טז) And the small one forbidden, etc. — for its wall breached in its full [length] to the large one which is a forbidden place for it:

(יז) And this is, etc. — meaning: when is the small one forbidden — when its walls enter and protrude into the large one two or three amot like this:

(יח) Protruding three tefachim, etc. — meaning the place where the partitions of the small one enter is protruding from the length-walls of the large one on its side; and we require three tefachim — for otherwise we say lavud to the length of the large one and it is as if not protruding at all:

(יט) For if not so the small one would be permitted, etc. — meaning: if the walls of the small one did not enter and protrude into the large one, the small one would also be permitted, for though equal from inside — one standing inside sees all its length-walls equal with no wide wall to its sides — the breach is in its full [length]; from outside within the large one it appears to have remnants of wall on this side and that [the surplus the large has over the small would permit the small through them] — unlike when the walls of the small protrude into the large, where the remnants of the large wall behind the small breach are not relevant to it:
