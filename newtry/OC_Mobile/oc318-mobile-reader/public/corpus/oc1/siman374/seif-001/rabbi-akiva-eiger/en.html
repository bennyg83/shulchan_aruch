Magen Avraham siman 3 page siman 372 seif 2. In my humble opinion it is explicit in Tosafos Shabbat 9a in what they wrote "or," etc. — e.g. open to karmelis and larger than it — we find Eruvin 90: requires two amot; and one authority in a portico — how? Rashi there: in a portico more than two se'ah which is karmelis — in my portico certainly permitted, for no change in ownership; rather required in another's portico — though also reshus ha-yachid, nevertheless since forbidden to carry four amot because breached to a place forbidden to it — to another roof permitted to carry from it to karmelis; one can reject: only in a portico which is also full reshus ha-yachid and throwing into it one is liable — only because more than se'ah they forbade carrying; therefore comparable to this roof; but to another karmelis one could say forbidden to carry since the roof is full reshus ha-yachid; however in Tosafos there and in Perush Rabbeinu Tam: "they asked — required on roof and on pillar" — meaning a pillar nine high which is karmelis — they said on it: karmelis and reshus ha-yachid are required — meaning the roof is considered reshus ha-yachid in height because eruv of houses permits carrying in all of it — implying without that reason it would be permitted to carry from this roof to karmelis; likewise a courtyard breached to karmelis as I wrote:
