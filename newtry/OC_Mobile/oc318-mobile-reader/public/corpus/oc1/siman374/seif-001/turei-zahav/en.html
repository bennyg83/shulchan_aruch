Mahariv z"l wrote; and it appears to me to distinguish: if he broke its pegs the eruv was nullified; if he did not break its pegs — when he sealed his opening he did not remove his mezuzah, lintel, and threshold — he revealed he did not remove it for himself; the eruv returns to its place; thus the Talmud distinguishes Bava Batra chapter 1 daf 11 — end; but here we are not concerned with diverting attention, for if so when it fell on a weekday and was not repaired before Shabbat he certainly diverted attention at Shabbat entry; and likewise ships that were tied and permitted on Shabbat — certainly he diverted attention they would not be repaired on Shabbat — this only when the essence depends on the eruv act: since it was fit at that hour, even if later ruined, when repaired later we are not concerned; and the eruv returns to its place:
