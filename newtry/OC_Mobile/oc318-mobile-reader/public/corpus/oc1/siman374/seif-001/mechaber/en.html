A doorway or window in a place through which they had made an eruv became blocked on Shabbos. It contains four se'ifim.
If they made an eruv by way of a window or doorway between them, and it became blocked on Shabbos, they are permitted to make use [of the passage] by way of the top of the wall and its holes; and even if they made an eruv for the year, and the doorway became blocked on a weekday and was opened on Shabbos {and even if he blocked it deliberately (Agur)}, the eruv has returned to permit it. And according to the Rambam, it is permitted to carry only each one within itself, but from one to the other, no.
