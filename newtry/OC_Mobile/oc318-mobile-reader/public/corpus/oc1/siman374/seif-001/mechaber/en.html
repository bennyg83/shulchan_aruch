If an opening or window was sealed on Shabbat in a place where they had joined an eruv. And in it
are 4 seifim: They joined via a window or opening between them and it was sealed on Shabbat — they are permitted to use via the height of the wall and its holes; and even if he joined for a year and the opening was sealed on a weekday and opened on Shabbat (and even if he sealed it intentionally (Agur)} the eruv returns to permit it; and for Rambam it is not permitted to carry except each for itself, but not from one to the other:) חזר העירוב להתירו ולהרמב"ם אין מותר לטלטל אלא כל אחת לעצמה אבל מזו לזו לא:
