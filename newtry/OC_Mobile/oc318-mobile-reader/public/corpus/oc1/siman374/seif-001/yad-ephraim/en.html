There in Magen Avraham siman 5: but if it does not protrude four — not as written; meaning: if it protrudes four, which is an important place in its own right, the entire roof is as breached to karmelis; but if the protrusion is not four, since the roof itself is reshus ha-yachid — we say gud aciq from its six sides like a pillar — therefore Rashi 89 on visible partitions: all agree we say gud aciq on them like a pillar in Rosh Hashana which is reshus ha-yachid due to gud aciq; and since the roof itself with three partitions is reshus ha-yachid, the protrusion too is considered reshus ha-yachid — since it lacks four to be important in itself it is nullified to reshus ha-yachid and is like holes of reshus ha-yachid; and one could say: since here are only three partitions for the roof itself, forbidden to carry until repair in the fourth as explained siman 363 letter 7 — specifically when breached in the fourth to reshus ha-rabbim or karmelis — unlike here where the public does not control the roof as Tosafos there; also there is no karmelis since it is not four wide — makom patur; and breaching to makom patur does not forbid carrying; therefore Tosafos wrote: if the protrusion is four the whole roof is forbidden as breached in full to karmelis; but because the roof has only three partitions no repair needed unless breached to reshus ha-rabbim or karmelis — therefore when protrusion is not four, carrying on the whole roof is fine, also on the protrusion since considered reshus ha-yachid nullified to it or like holes of reshus ha-yachid even if not four wide; see 87b: they are like holes of karmelis — here beginning as karmelis the amah entering the city though lacking karmelis measure is called holes of karmelis; likewise here considered holes of reshus ha-yachid; see siman 321 siman 5 and what is written there:
