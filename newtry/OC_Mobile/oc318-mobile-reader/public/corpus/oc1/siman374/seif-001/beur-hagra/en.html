(ג) And for Rambam. As with the ships mentioned — when they were severed they became forbidden from one to the other; they rejected this because there it is different since they are not fixed and they come to carry from one to the other via the sea which is karmelis; Rambam follows his view as he explained in commentary on the mishnah and in his composition there — even though enclosed they lack a partition, but they are explained like Rashi: adjacent.
