Breached before Shabbat in its full [length] to a large roof — the reason a small roof is not permitted in the sides of a large roof is "visible from outside and equal from inside" as above seif 3 — for regarding the large roof we say gud aciq mechitzeta and it is as if it has sides; one could say that gud aciq applies to the large roof itself but not to permit the small roof in the sides of the large [Ritva — thus the question of Derishah is resolved, brought in Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger]:
