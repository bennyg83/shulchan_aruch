A small roof breached before Shabbat in its full [length] to a large roof — the small one is forbidden to raise onto it utensils that remained in the house; and the large one is permitted; and this is when the partitions of the house are visible to one standing on the roof; but if they are not visible — such as the roof protrudes over them — it is karmelis unless open to it from the house a window four by four:
