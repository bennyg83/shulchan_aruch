(כ) Small roof, etc. — but if both roofs are equal they forbid one another even if not more than ten amot, for they breached in their full [length] to one another:
(כא) That was breached, etc. — not precise; the intent is it was open to the adjacent roof; regarding roofs we do not deal with initially having a partition — he used the term "breached" following seif 3:
(כב) Before Shabbat — for if on Shabbat we say once permitted, permitted, as above:
(כג) In its full [length] to a large roof — meaning the entire small roof faced the adjacent large roof; also deals when they did not join their upper stories together; also the small roof does not hold more than ten amot — as above seif 3:
(כד) The small one forbidden to raise, etc. — for it breached to its fellow's forbidden domain:
(כה) That remained in the house — for utensils that remained on the roof are permitted to carry even to another's roof without any eruv, as above siman 372 seif 1:
(כו) And the large one permitted — to take out house utensils onto its roof, for the small roof toward it is considered like a mere opening since it is not more than ten amot:
(כז) Partitions of the house visible, etc. — that all their roofs were divided and not sloped; and when the roof does not protrude from the walls outward, the house partitions appear to one standing on the roof edge looking under his feet — then we say gud aciq mechitzeta and as if enclosed with partitions above around the roof; but when the roof protrudes from the walls outward the house partitions are not visible, we do not say gud aciq mechitzeta, and it is karmelis — forbidden to carry in each who lacks any partition, even utensils that remained on the roof, except within four amot:
(כח) That the roof protrudes — specifically when it protrudes four tefachim; but if it does not protrude four tefachim it is like visible partitions [Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah; see what we wrote above siman 345 seif 16 in Biur Halacha]:
(כט) Open to it, etc. — for they use it and the entire roof is considered holes of reshus ha-yachid; nevertheless this helps only to permit use on his roof with utensils that remained inside which thereby has reshus ha-yachid name on it; but to raise onto it utensils that remained in the house is forbidden in any case when they did not join together, since the partitions are not visible on it:
