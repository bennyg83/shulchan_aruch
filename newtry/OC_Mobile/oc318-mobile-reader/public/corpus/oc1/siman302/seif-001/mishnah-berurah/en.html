(א) New — called new if not yet used much so its newness still shows.
(ב) Black — but pale and red we have no concern; reason: on new and black the improvement by shaking is evident; therefore when particular, shaking is considered like laundering; but if not his way to be particular and sometimes wears without shaking — permitted to shake even l'chatchila.
(ג) Rain etc. — here no difference black and other colors unlike dew above where specifically when little one is not particular to shake except black; here water is abundant, way to be particular to shake all garments; also concern of squeezing.
(ד) New he is particular about — meaning presumably particular to shake because shrinks by water; even if garment old, beware shaking from water forcefully lest come to squeezing in that shaking [known when garment absorbs water it squeezes well by shaking] [Sh.A. Hagahot Maimoniyot, brought Ateret Zekenim].
(ה) Forbidden to shake — Rashi and supporters hold what Gemara says Rav Huna one who shakes tallit Shabbat liable chatas is shaking from dust and dirt absorbed in it, liable for laundering; specifically new and black; old or pale and red no concern as explained there, like above regarding dew; Rav's intent also specifically new and black; stated as prohibition to teach if not particular even rabbinic prohibition none.
(ו) Good etc. — see Ateret Zekenim wrote by law prohibition, many Rishonim like Rashi; nevertheless appears rely on first lenient view via davar she'eino mitkaven, especially sometimes kevod habriyot; good beware coming Shabbat morning to synagogue place hat and garments where guarded lest fall and dust and come to chillul Shabbat.
(ז) Permitted remove etc. — not like bleaching, feathers not embedded like water and dust but stand above; therefore permitted even new and particular.
