The laws of cleaning and folding garments on Shabbat. It contains 13 seifim: One
who shakes a new black tallit (1) from the dew on it is liable, for shaking benefits it like laundering — when he is particular about it not to wear it without shaking.All the more so it is forbidden to shake a garment that was soaked in water or upon which rain fell; and specifically a new garment he is particular about (Kol Bo). Some say (5) it is forbidden to shake a garment from the dust on it if he is particular about it, and it is good to be concerned for their words (Rashi and Or Zarua). But (7) it is permitted (4) to remove feathers from a garment on Shabbat (Or Zarua); and see above siman 337.
