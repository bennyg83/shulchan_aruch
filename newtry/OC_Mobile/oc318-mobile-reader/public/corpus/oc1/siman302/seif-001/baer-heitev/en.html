(א) New — that they have not used it much, rather it is still in its newness. Beit Yosef.

(ב) New — because it shrinks in water; this prohibition applies to all garments, whether black or other colors. Sefer HaZichronot; see Magen Avraham.

(ג) Dust — specifically black and new. Magen Avraham.

(ד) Feathers — and in Sefer HaZichronot he was concerned about the prohibition of tearing, a primary category of melacha; therefore one whose garments became entangled in thorns separates them discreetly and delays so they will not tear, and if they tore he is not liable for he did not intend. Magen Avraham.
