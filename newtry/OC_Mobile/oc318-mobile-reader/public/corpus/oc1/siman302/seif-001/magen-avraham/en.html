(א) New — that they have not used it much, rather it is still in its newness [Beit Yosef, Rambam].

(ב) New that he is particular about — because it shrinks in water [Kol Bo]; implies that stam new he is particular about it, even though regarding dew the Gemara says "and he is particular" etc.; water is different because it shrinks. In Sefer HaZichronot he forbids on all garments.

(ג) Garment from dust — specifically black and new as explained.

(ד) To remove feathers — and in Sefer HaZichronot page 53 he was concerned about tearing, a primary category of melacha; therefore one whose garments entangled in thorns separates discreetly and delays so they not tear; if torn not liable for did not intend; and see siman 317 s.g.
