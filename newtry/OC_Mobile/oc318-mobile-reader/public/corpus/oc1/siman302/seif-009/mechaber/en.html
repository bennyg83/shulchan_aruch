Permitted to put water on a shoe (39)(40) to rinse it; but to launder it — that is (41) rubbing this side on that side (42) — forbidden (16). But a garment that has filth on it (43) is forbidden (18) even (44) to rinse it (45), for that is its laundering; rather he wipes it with a rag (17) lightly and not with pressure lest he squeeze.And a garment without filth — permitted (46) to put on it a small amount of water (19)(47) and not much, lest he squeeze (Beit Yosef in name of Semag, Semak, Sefer HaTerumah, and Rosh ch. 1 of Yoma)) (20)(48). Some forbid in every case (Beit Yosef in name of Tur siman 334 and siman 319 and Tosafot chapter Kol Kitvei and glosses ch. 22).
