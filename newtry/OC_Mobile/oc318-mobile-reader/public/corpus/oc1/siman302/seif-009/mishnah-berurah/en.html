(א) Permitted etc. — introduction: a) Laundering primary melacha; bleaching wool flax all bleachable; includes flax in sun with water [custom]. Measure thread four handbreadths. b) Laundering derivative liable; squeezing part of laundering; laundering soft leather, some hard; difference leather complete laundering vs garment rinsing is laundering, liable launderer, language on white and colored [Acharonim]. c) Opinions on rinsing is laundering: some only with filth; darkened from wear not laundering unless actual laundering/squeezing; rabbinic decree lest squeeze; some even clean rinsing is laundering only if laundering manner not soiling manner like hands on cloth even much water — not rinsing is laundering; some stringent much water even soiling = laundering, should shake hands until little water then wipe. Now explain seifim:

(ב) To rinse — shoe has filth, water until filth gone = rinsing.

(ג) Rubbing etc. — like launderers; language implies stam laundering shoe no prohibition; see Biur Halacha many poskim prohibition even general laundering, only rinsing; wooden utensil no laundering concern; see s.k. 51.

(ד) Forbidden — liability too, laundering applies leather.

(ה) Filth — even saliva.

(ו) To rinse — forbidden put water at all even little.

(ז) This is laundering — garment rinsing is laundering, melaben category liable; shoe laundering applies but rinsing not laundering unless actual laundering.

(ח) Put water — rinsing not laundering except with filth; nevertheless much water forbidden lest squeeze; if intends launder even little forbidden [Sefer HaTerumah, Semag, Ateret Zekenim siman 301 s.k. 87].

(ט) Not much — if not particular about water leaves time with water no decree lest squeeze siman 301 seif 46.

(י) Some forbid — hold rinsing is laundering even clean garment; little water forbidden laundering; nevertheless if way of soiling permitted s.10 gloss; be concerned stringent Torah view.
