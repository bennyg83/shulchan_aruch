(א) One who gathers tufts — yavel language meaning growth; same law hard thin knots woven unintentionally like tuft; Acharonim wrote same forbidden remove shavings tailors call striegivan at start sewing to join pieces; possible liability too as artisans remove at melacha completion.

(ב) Because makeh bepatish — whoever does thing completing melacha liable makeh bepatish as artisan strikes hammer on utensil after completion to straighten crookedness.

(ג) When particular — removes intentionally to beautify not merely occupied; even if would wear without removing — liable, for here melacha more than seif 1; exempt only as occupied. Rambam: shakes new black tallit to beautify remove white-yellow hanging as artisans — liable makeh bepatish; if not particular permitted; particular here too intent to beautify like tuft.

(ד) Exempt — Sefer HaZichronot wrote prohibition; Hagahot Gedolot: flax seed or dish on garment permitted scrape; appears if new black garment not permitted scrape except not particular; if moist wipe with rag as below.
