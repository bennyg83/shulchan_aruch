(א) Sharp — mirror edge sharp made to remove dangling hairs weekdays; Shabbat concern forget act as weekday.

(ב) Fixed in wall — cannot cut with it also forbidden; sages did not distinguish metal mirror fixed or not.

(ג) Without etc. — glass mirror like ours; metal not sharp — not made to cut hairs, no decree; not concerned see hairs go to barber, remembers Shabbat.

(ד) This concern — see Yoreh Deah siman 156 how permitted look in mirror.
