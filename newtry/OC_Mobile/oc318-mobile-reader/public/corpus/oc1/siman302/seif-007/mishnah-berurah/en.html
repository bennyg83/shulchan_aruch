(א) On garment etc. — no difference silk flax wool.

(ב) Rub inside — hold garment inside against mud place rub until mud falls; though above s.305 sudar forbidden rub without inside/outside distinction — there intends whiten, here only remove mud [Gra].

(ג) Appears like laundering — softens garment, not actual laundering, no water.

(ד) Scrape fingernail — outside not bleaching; likewise back of knife, possibly even point [Beit Yosef].

(ה) But dry — scrape or rub even inside forbidden.

(ו) Tochen — melacha she'eino tzricha legufa still prohibition all; davar she'eino mitkaven permitted. Taz: only actual mud substance, scraping crumbs like tochen; only appearance cancel — not tochen; permit slightly soiled cloak no excrement dry scrape fingernail pray cleanly; to me requires study: though no tochen, intends beautify whiten no filth mark — like laundering; see Biur Halacha; even per Taz only fingernail or rub inside, rub outside forbidden all cases.
