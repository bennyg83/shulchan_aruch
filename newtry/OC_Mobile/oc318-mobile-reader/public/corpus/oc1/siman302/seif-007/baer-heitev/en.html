(א) With a fingernail — and likewise with a knife, even if blunt (Beit Yosef, Darkhei Moshe).

(ב) Tochen — it appears specifically when there is actual substance of mud on the garment; but if there is only the appearance of mud and he scrapes there to nullify the appearance, this is not similar to tochen; for this reason there is room to permit one whose cloak is slightly soiled and there is no substance of excrement and it is dry — he may scrape with a fingernail and remove the appearance in order to pray in cleanliness. Taz.
