(א) Mud on garment — see Mishna Berurah no distinction etc.; Bach ruled; Magen Avraham from stam Shulchan Aruch omitted Shulchan Leket Rabbenu Tam Beit Yosef; unlike above s.305 gloss soiled mud every matter repair, or stringent his view; Ateret Zekenim questions Magen Avraham; Gra proves we rule s.305 sudar forbidden rub — therefore our case all manners forbidden.

(ב) Tochen — see Mishna Berurah Taz permitted scrape cancel appearance only; to me not clear: no tochen, but scrape intends beautify garment no filth mark — not less sudar s.305 forbidden whiten bleaching; Gemara permitted scrape fingernail pass mud, here no mud intent only whiten garment — exactly s.305; Gra wrote permitted rub inside because mud on it intent mud not whiten; Taz no mud intent only whiten — forbidden; even other poskim Beit Yosef sudar permit rub only soften not whiten, unlike here beautify garment — all forbid; appears Rashi liable chatas shake tallit from dust — here liable chatas whiten; even not black new, dust generally does not spoil only black per Rashi; only new otherwise people not particular, here people particular any filth mark mud excrement — removing mark whitening included in laundering.
