One who washes his hands (21)(49)(50)— good (51) to dry them forcefully one against the other and remove from them the water as he can before wiping them on a cloth.And some wrote not to be concerned, for we do not say rinsing a garment is its laundering in such a case, for it is only (22)(52) the way of soiling (53), and so is the custom (Tur and Beit Yosef and Agur). Therefore permitted (14)(54) to dry his hands (23) on a garment into which a child urinated (24)(15)(55)(16) in order to nullify it, for that is only ordinary soiling (Tur); but (26) forbidden to put (17)(56) actual water on (57) the urine in order to nullify it (Hagahot Maimoniyot ch. 22 and Tosafot chapter 8 of Sheratzim).
