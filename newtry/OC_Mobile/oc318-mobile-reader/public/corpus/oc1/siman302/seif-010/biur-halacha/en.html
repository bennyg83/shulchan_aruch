Do not say etc. — even filth on cloth permitted since wiping soiling manner [Peri Megadim]. Yom Tov bathing child hot water weekday custom spread cloth under [windelin] in tub — cannot bathe small child otherwise; Yom Tov laundering prohibition soaking in water; permit if windelin clean laundered — first view no laundering; even stringent view clean possibly soiling manner; even if not soiling, clean certainly no intent launder, only spread under child — laundering melacha she'eino tzricha legufa, rabbinic prohibition for many poskim, child like sick without danger if accustomed bathing — permit rabbinic for him, therefore no decree lest squeeze since judged sick, provided not squeeze. Peri Megadim wanted permit pressing need even dirty melacha she'eino tzricha intent spread not launder — not clear dirty wants laundering, weekday intends squeeze after bath launder — intent also laundering not melacha she'eino tzricha; nevertheless davar she'eino mitkaven permit putting windelin in water even clean preferably kele'achar yad if possible.
