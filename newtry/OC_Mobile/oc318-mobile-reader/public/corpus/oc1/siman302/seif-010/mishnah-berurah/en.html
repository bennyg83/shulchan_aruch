(א) Good etc. — by law permitted both views in gloss wipe cloth.
(ב) Dry forcefully — also satisfy stringent view s.k. 39 all cases rinsing is laundering even soiling; if little water no laundering concern; not expert what is little — custom shake hands forcefully.
(ג) Way of soiling — hands dirty soil cloth; only cloth not particular about absorbed water; item usually particular weekday about absorbed water forbidden lest squeeze siman 301 seif 46; Magen Avraham; Peri Megadim ruled only much water, little like after washing hands no squeeze concern; Ateret Zekenim, Turei Zahav lenient hand drying; spilled table water all forbid wipe particular garment much water lest squeeze.
(ד) Dry hands — washed hands dry on garment.
(ה) Urine — with excrement forbidden even hand-drying; though soiling manner still laundering as garment dirtier; urine less repulsive, permit only intent nullify not launder.
(ו) To nullify — can pray bless; little urine as child in wrap not flowing to mother garment, little suffices hand-drying [Beit Yosef]; see siman 77 M.B. s.k. 87.
(ז) Actual water — even wash hands on them though pouring, no filth looks like laundering; not intent launder only nullify urine still psik reisha rinsing is laundering; good not take child in lap Shabbat unless cloth lest urinate [138]; per above garment with filth rinsing is laundering — beware not pour into soaking vessel unless water already above garments; careful not immerse garment; bathing child do not put garments in water; see Biur Halacha.
(ח) Urine — in garment; on ground permitted [Sh.A.].
