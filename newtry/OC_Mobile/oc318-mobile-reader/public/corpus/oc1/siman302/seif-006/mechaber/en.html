6. Mud on one’s leg (or on one’s shoes)(Beis Yosef and R' Yerucham) may be scraped onto a wall but not onto the ground, lest one will fill in crevices in the ground. There is an opinion that forbids scraping onto a wall as well. RAMA: there is an opinion that permits scraping onto both. (the Rosh). It is also permitted to remove it with a little piece of clay that is not Muktze.
