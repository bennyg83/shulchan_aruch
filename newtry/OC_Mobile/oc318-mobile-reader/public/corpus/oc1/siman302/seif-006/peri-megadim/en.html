(ב) what Mechaber s.9 and Yom Tov laundering laws explained later Magen Avraham.

(ג) I was asked on Yom Tov, when they bathe an infant in hot water and it is impossible to bathe him except by placing dry swaddling cloths in the tub — how is this done? Per the view that soaking is laundering, and especially linen; also, how is the permission of bathing done? Behold, bathing on Yom Tov is explained in siman 511 seif 2 and Magen Avraham there, and see there it will be explained. And regarding swaddling cloths — it is likewise impossible to bathe a small infant unless they place swaddling cloths under him in the tub. If they have swaddling cloths that are laundered and dry, one may say it is permitted to place them in the tub, for it is a manner of soiling, like the case of one passing through water with a towel, as is written in the present siman seif 4 and siman 328, and as we need to say in siman 326 ot 20 see there. And if he has only soiled ones, one may say nevertheless the infant is like a sick person without danger if he is accustomed to bathing, and like a very small child who needs bathing — this too is permitted to place, for a sick person without danger permits a d'rabbanan prohibition; and his soaking is his laundering — it is melacha; nevertheless one must say he is not doing this for laundering but only to spread under the small child — permitted; and see siman 328 seif 17. And it is good to do it with a slight change by a Jew: he places the swaddling cloths first and pours on them water changed from weekday use; and regarding drying them on Yom Tov by the fire — if the water was hot, it requires study; one may say there is no cooking after cooking — see siman 318 siman 66 and siman 318 in this; there is a dispute regarding moist food that cooled whether cooking applies to it, but nevertheless libun applies to it; and see Magen Avraham in Shulchan Aruch ot 57 and what he wrote there. And Magen Avraham ot 24 wrote further regarding swaddling cloths in this.
