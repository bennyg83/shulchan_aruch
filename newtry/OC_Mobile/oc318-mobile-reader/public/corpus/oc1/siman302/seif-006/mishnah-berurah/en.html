(א) Or on shoes — unlike Shulchan Aruch 138 no scraping shoe even with mud intent remove mud; as written there forbidden only back of knife scraping erases leather, unlike wiping wall/beam; Beit Yosef; Taz: iron before synagogue doors forbidden like knife back; Magen Avraham stringent even thick head; wipe wall/beam/stair notches; if wipe gently no concern erasing leather, especially wet mud.
(ב) Even wall — stone appears adding to building; his view wipe only beam or on stone; same wooden wall permitted.
(ג) On both — adding building not concerned field building; lest wipe in hollow and level — not concerned; nevertheless even per them beware ground not in hollow; halacha Taz rabbinic rely lenient both; some Acharonim stringent both; appears wall certainly rely permit as Mechaber, most Rishonim.
(ד) Little earthenware — all permit.
(ה) Fit to move — see below siman 308 s.7 which pottery fit to move.
