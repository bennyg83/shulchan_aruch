11) There, gloss: But it is permitted to remove feathers, etc. Sefer HaZichronos, folio 53, is concerned that this is forbidden. Knesses HaGedolah there; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 4; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 4; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 5. It is therefore proper to take care not to remove any feather from a black garment or anything else hanging from it. Ben Ish Chai there.
