(א) For Shabbat need — if not fold, regularly wrinkle.

(ב) Wear them that day — therefore forbidden fold tallit though tzitzit all day can wrap; since no intent to wrap forbidden unless place custom wrap tallit at mincha.

(ג) One person — not two, for two spread creases appear like repairing utensil; folding on stool law like two.

(ד) Not laundered — all not laundered hard, not quick to wrinkle, folding not considered repair enough to appear repairing utensil.

(ה) And white — colored, folding repairs more.

(ו) Nothing to change into — if has other garment for Shabbat even less nice not permitted fold; all the more no intent wear that day forbidden; as s.k. 13; nevertheless Yom Tov on erev Shabbat permitted fold tallit when removing if new white even no intent wear that day — when made eruv tavshilin else preparing for tomorrow forbidden Ateret Zekenim; Chiddushei Ra'ah proved even without placing eruv permitted if adjacent to Shabbat.

(ז) Permitted in every case — this fold has no permanence, no repair; permitted even without four conditions, even no intent wear that day.

(ח) His words appear — Acharonim ruled thus; one who wants be stringent not fold at all certainly better [Machatzit HaShekel]. Spread beds Shabbat night to Shabbat, preferably from erev Shabbat; do not spread Shabbat to motzaei Shabbat; if bed stands in house disgrace for Shabbat to stand thus — permitted spread called Shabbat need [Magen Avraham, Sh.A.].
