[9] In every manner, etc. Meaning even old garments and even with two people. Mordechai and Agudah write likewise, and this is primary. It implies this is permitted even when the garment is not needed that day. This applies specifically to garments worn on Shabbos, but if they were not worn on Shabbos, folding them is prohibited in every manner because it could have been done before Shabbos.
