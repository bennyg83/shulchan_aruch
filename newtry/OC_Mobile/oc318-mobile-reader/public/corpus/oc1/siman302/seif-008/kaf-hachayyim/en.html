Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, however, writes that the act is forbidden by strict law; see there. The later authorities likewise write that shaking off dust is forbidden. Chayei Adam, klal 22, paragraph 9; Ben Ish Chai, parashas Vayechi, paragraph 8; Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 80, paragraph 9. See above, paragraphs 1 and 2; the same applies here regarding dust. See Petach HaDevir, paragraph 4, who writes that if a person leaned against a wall and plaster dust adhered to his garment, he may strike the garment with a finger snap until it leaves. If, however, thick, wet plaster adhered to it and dried, one should be stringent, as stated below in seif 7 regarding dry mud; see there.
