A cloak after laundering becomes hard and they rub it with the hands to soften it (22) — permitted to do on Shabbat when he intends only to soften it (23); but a sudar is forbidden (24) because he intends to whiten it and it is like bleaching.(25) And hats (8) and other linen utensils — their law is like a sudar (Beit Yosef in name of Shulchan Leket).
