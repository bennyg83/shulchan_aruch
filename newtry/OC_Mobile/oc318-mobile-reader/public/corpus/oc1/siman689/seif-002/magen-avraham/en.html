Deaf person. The Bach ruled that one who hears from a deaf person has fulfilled after the fact, and so it appears in the Maggid Mishneh.
Minor. Although an adult too is obligated only rabbinically, this is not comparable to what is written at the end of siman 187, for there the minor can eat enough to be satisfied and bring himself into a rabbinic obligation, Re'em. This requires analysis, for Beis Yosef ruled at the end of siman 675 like the opinion of the Baal HaIttur, who validates a minor, while here he states anonymously and rules like the prohibiting authorities. It is forced to distinguish in this between megillah and Chanukah lights, and in the Ran there too it implies that they are comparable in this matter. See what I wrote nearby.
They do not discharge. This is not comparable to Chanukah lights, for megillah is different, since it is like Torah reading, Sefer Mitzvos Gadol. Meaning, she is invalid because of the honor of the congregation, and therefore even for an individual she does not discharge, because the Sages did not differentiate, Re'em. See what I wrote in siman 271.
If the woman reads. In Midrash HaNe'elam on Ruth it is written that she should not read for herself, but should hear from the men.
For she is not obligated. For he holds like the one who says that women do not discharge men; so it appears in Mordechai.
