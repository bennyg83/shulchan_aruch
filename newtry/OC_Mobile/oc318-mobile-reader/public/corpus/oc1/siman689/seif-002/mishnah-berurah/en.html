(1) (d) He has discharged his obligation - and it is necessary for the reciter to perform and the hearer to perform lawfully the rest of the matzot that one performs for his friend and as follows in S. 1736 CD4:

(5) Deaf — meaning one who speaks and does not hear at all; and even though generally we rule if he did not make heard to his ear he fulfilled as above siman 62 and siman 185, here regarding Megillah which is because of publicizing the miracle they were stricter — hearing in his ear is required, or because deaf is worse since he cannot make heard to his ear; and see Shaarei Teshuvah that specifically if he is completely deaf who does not hear at all — excluding if he hears when spoken to loudly. And all this is Shulchan Aruch's view; but many acharonim hold even if completely deaf, the listener fulfills from him; and nevertheless l'chatchila all agree they do not establish him to discharge the many. And know that one whose ears are damaged or who is far from the bimah and cannot hear well from the reader should be careful to read for himself from a kosher Megillah, or at least hold a chumash and the words he lacks say immediately from the chumash.

(ג) Or a minor — even though a minor is obligated at least rabbinically as above seif 1, nevertheless he cannot discharge an adult because regarding a minor it is two rabbinic levels (the reading itself is only rabbinic and the minor's obligation is also only rabbinic in all mitzvot), unlike an adult who is one rabbinic and one does not extract another rabbinic. Above siman 475:3 Mechaber brought some permit a minor who reached chinuch to discharge others in his lighting — same for Megillah; see Ikarei Dinim in name of Sema: in pressing need when no men expert in Megillah reading, a minor who reached chinuch may discharge them.
