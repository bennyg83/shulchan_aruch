b) There. And women — that they too were in that miracle. Megillah 4a. And meaning: even though Megillah reading comes from time to time and a time-caused obligation [women are generally exempt], as written in Kiddushin 29a, nevertheless here they are obligated since they too were in that miracle as written in scripture "children and women on one day." However there are commentators that "they too were in that miracle" means the miracle was done through a woman as Beit Yosef wrote. And see below letter 5.
