2) Levush: For she is not strong enough, etc. In Shulchan Aruch he wrote two opinions on this. Olas Shabbos wrote that perforce he holds that she does not discharge him; for otherwise it would be difficult why he ruled in section 3 that a tumtum does not discharge even one of his own kind because of doubt, since even women discharge. End quote. This is astonishing, for Beis Yosef explicitly wrote that even according to Rashi, that women discharge men, nevertheless a tumtum does not discharge even one of his own kind, because a recognizable status is required. Nevertheless, as a matter of practical law, the main view is like Levush, and so wrote Rama and the poskim. See siman 671, seif katan 6, where I wrote to distinguish between Chanukah lights, where women discharge, and megillah. It further appears to me that megillah is different, for a woman is not obligated in reading as men are, but in hearing, unlike Chanukah lights, where women and men are equal in the mitzvah.
