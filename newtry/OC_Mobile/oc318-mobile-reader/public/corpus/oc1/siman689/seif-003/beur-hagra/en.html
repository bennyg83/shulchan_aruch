Section 3: An androgynous person. This is from the Tosefta there. The Rambam omitted it, for he follows his own view that the halachah does not follow the Tosefta, since even women discharge men from their obligation. So wrote Kol Bo, and so wrote Tosafos 4a, unlike Beis Yosef and Magen Avraham. But why the Rambam omitted the case of one who is half slave requires analysis, according to what he wrote about freed slaves.
