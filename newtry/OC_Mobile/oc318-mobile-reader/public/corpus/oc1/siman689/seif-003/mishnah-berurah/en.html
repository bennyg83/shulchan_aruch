9) An androgynous person. He has male and female characteristics.
10) Discharges one of his own kind. For if this one is male, that one is also male; and if this one is female, that one is also female; and if we say that he is a being of his own, the second is also a being of his own.
11) But not one who is not of his kind. For perhaps this one is female and the second is male. Even according to the one who says that women discharge men, this is worse, for we require that the one who discharges others be recognizable as to whether he is a man or a woman.
12) A tumtum, etc., does not discharge even one of his own kind. For perhaps this one is male and this one is female, unlike an androgynous person, where all are alike in their kind. Likewise, one who is half slave does not have the slave side of himself come and discharge the other, who has a side of freedom.
13) Some say that even himself, etc. This does not refer to a tumtum, but only to one who is half slave and half free, because the slave side of him does not come and discharge the free side of him. All this is specifically according to the opinion that women do not discharge men, and a slave is similar to a woman.
