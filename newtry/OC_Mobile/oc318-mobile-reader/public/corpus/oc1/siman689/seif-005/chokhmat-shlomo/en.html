Seif 5: And if all know, each reads for himself. See Magen Avraham s.k. 10 who brought views whether another can discharge his fellow in Megillah with fewer than ten — see there. At first glance difficult: why does the Gemara in Megillah challenge from Megillah in its time with ten? It appears Rava's view: if all know, each reads for himself; if not, need ten. And per what Magen Avraham wrote, even if all know, discharge is only rabbinic — see there.
