Each reads for himself. It implies that he holds that an individual does not discharge his fellow except with ten. One must say that this is not comparable to shofar, for reading the megillah is like prayer, where we require ten. But in Rabbeinu Yerucham, section 13, it implies that even an individual discharges his fellow with megillah, even though the fellow is expert; see there, part 3. So wrote Avudraham, and so wrote the Bach at the end of siman 690 as obvious in the name of Maharil. If so, it is preferable that one read for all of them because "in the multitude of the people is the King's glory," as written in siman 98, section 14. In Tashbetz he wrote: each reads for himself if they wish; end quote, siman 220.
