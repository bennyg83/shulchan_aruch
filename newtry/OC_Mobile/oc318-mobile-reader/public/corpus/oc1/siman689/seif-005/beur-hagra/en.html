Section 5: A place. He holds like Rav Ashi, 5a, and he holds that it is indispensable. But if so, the halachah does not follow Rabbi Asi because of that statement of Rabbi Yochanan in chapter 2; see the end of siman 690. This is like what is written at the end of Rosh Hashanah: an individual who did not bless, his fellow, etc. See there in the Ran, who wrote there: however, this applies only when he knows; but when he does not know, his fellow discharges him, for there are three things in the Yerushalmi that are excluded from the general rule: Shema, prayer, and Grace after Meals. And we say in Berachos, chapter 7: two who ate, etc.; but one recites, etc.
