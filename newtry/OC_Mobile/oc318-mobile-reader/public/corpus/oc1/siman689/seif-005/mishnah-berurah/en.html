15) For himself. For an individual does not discharge his fellow except with ten. This is not comparable to shofar, for reading the megillah is like prayer, where we specifically require ten. Pri Chadash wrote that all this is only initially, but after the fact, if he intended to exempt his fellow, they have fulfilled, even though all of them know how to read. Magen Avraham inclines further that even initially it is preferable that one read for all of them because "in the multitude of the people is the King's glory." However, Eliyah Rabbah and Matteh Yehudah are of the opinion of Shulchan Aruch, that each should read for himself, and so Derech HaChaim copied.
