"Freed." According to Beit Yosef, even slaves who have not been freed, but who underwent brit milah and immersion for the sake of slavery, are obligated in reading the Megillah. Rabbeinu Shimshon disagrees. Re'em, in his novellae to Sefer Mitzvot Gadol, likewise writes that according to Sefer Mitzvot Gadol slaves are not obligated in reading the Megillah. Therefore it appears to me, without hesitation, that according to all opinions slaves who have not been freed are not obligated in reading the Megillah. My teacher wrote that even slaves who have not been freed are obligated, but they cannot discharge others. Nevertheless, the primary view appears to me to be that they are not obligated in its reading at all, as stated above.
