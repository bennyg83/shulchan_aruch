Freed. According to the Beis Yosef, even slaves who have not been freed, but who were circumcised and immersed for the sake of slavery, are also obligated in the reading of the megillah. Rabbeinu Shimshon disagrees with him. The Re'em, of blessed memory, in his novellae to Sefer Mitzvos Gadol, also wrote that the opinion of Sefer Mitzvos Gadol is that slaves are not obligated in reading the megillah. Therefore it appears to me without hesitation, according to everyone, that slaves who have not been freed are not obligated in reading the megillah. My teacher wrote that even slaves who have not been freed are obligated, only they do not discharge others; but nevertheless the main view appears to me to be that they are not obligated in reading it at all, as above.
