Seif 1: Converts and slaves. This is stated in the Tosefta, chapter 2 of Megillah. In our version the word “converts” was omitted, and that is a scribal error. See the end of the third chapter of Rosh Hashanah, 29a, regarding shofar.
Freed slaves. This is Rambam’s ruling. Although he holds that women are obligated and may discharge others because they too were included in that miracle, slaves are different. They are exempt because they were not included, etc. They are not comparable to women in this matter, unlike Tur and Magen Avraham.
And one educates, etc. The Jerusalem Talmud, chapter 2, states: Bar Kappara says that one must read it before men, women, and children, because they too were included in that miracle. Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi acted accordingly. He gathered his sons and the members of his household and read it before them. Hagahos Maimoniyos and the other poskim cite this. The obligation of children is only one of education, as with all mitzvos. See Tosafos, Megillah 19b, in the passage beginning “and Rabbi Yehudah validates,” where they ask, etc., and answer, etc.
