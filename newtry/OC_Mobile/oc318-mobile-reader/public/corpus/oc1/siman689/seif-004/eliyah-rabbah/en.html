4) Levush: And all the more so when he fulfills, etc. This requires analysis, for the language of the Tosefta is that he does not discharge one who is not of his kind, and so wrote Shulchan Aruch, which implies that he does not discharge even a woman. It can also be compared to siman 199, section 5, that he does not join with a man or with a woman. So too it implies in Kesef Mishneh, chapter 6 of Hilchos Berachos, that he is worse than women. However, Tosafos in Megillah, page 4, requires analysis, for it implies that he is not worse than women. Yet according to Rashi's explanation, that women discharge men and an androgynous person discharges men, perforce he is worse.
