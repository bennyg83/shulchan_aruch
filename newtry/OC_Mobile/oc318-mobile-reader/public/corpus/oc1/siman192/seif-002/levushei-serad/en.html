Magen Avraham s.k. 1 — and in Rashi in Chullin, in the incident of the Sadducee who dined with Rabbi — after the meal Rabbi said to him: a cup of blessing you drink, or forty gold coins you take. And Rashi explained that forty gold coins is payment, etc.; and see there, for Rashi's words in their place require explanation, and one should not elaborate.
