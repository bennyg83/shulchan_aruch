Chayinu. Eshel Avraham and Ein Ayah brought up per what Magen Avraham wrote that the custom is not to answer Amen, and he rejected the proof from siman 198 that one who enters is different, as Levush wrote; and also in Machatzit HaShekel he wrote that in our communities the custom is not to answer Amen; see there. And the one leading zimun certainly does not answer Amen after the words of those reclining Baruch she'achalnu, etc., since he himself also returns and says Baruch; and I already wrote thus regarding the prayer leader who says bar'chu — he should not answer Amen after the congregation's words Baruch Heaven haMevorach; and so too regarding Kriat HaTorah, see in Sha'arei Efraim sha'ar 4.
