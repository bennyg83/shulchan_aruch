(At the beginning of the siman at the end in Magen Avraham.) And all the more so that one may say until Al yechaser lanu — and it is written Bach, but this is not found in Bach; and the word Bach appears that one should cite before this; and this all the more so is from Magen Avraham's own words, and his intent appears: all the more so if due to some cause of compulsion one says birkat hamazon in our nusach until Al yechaser lanu and does not say haRachaman hu — for one may do so; and all the more so is from the short birkat hamazon, which also does not mention haRachaman hu — requires study. And see Bach — this nusach, and there it says lechem shova and not l'ratzon b'eretz Yisrael, etc.; and Magen Avraham omitted the word velo l'ratzon, for they are only for rhyme; but there b'eretz Yisrael emunah melech and the word ha'El is missing — see siman 189.
