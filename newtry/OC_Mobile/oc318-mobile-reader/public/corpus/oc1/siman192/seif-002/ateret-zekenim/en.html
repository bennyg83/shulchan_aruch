And he says Baruch atah Heaven, etc. It implies one should not say baruch Hu and baruch shemo, and Maharshal so ruled. However, in the Tur it implies one should say baruch Hu and baruch shemo. And my teacher in Bach distinguishes between whether he blesses with three or with ten — meaning, in a group of ten, since one mentions the Name, for they say Baruch Eloheinu she'achalnu mishlo, therefore one must say baruch Hu and baruch shemo, which is not so with three. And his words are words of reason; end of his words.
