Birkat hazimun with three or with ten. And in it 2 seifim.
If three were reclining' together they are obligated in zimun, that one of them says n'varech she'achalnu mishlo and they answer and say Baruch she'achalnu mishlo uv'tuvo chayinu, and he returns and says Baruch she'achalnu mishlo uv'tuvo chayinu b'eretz Yisrael emunah haZan et ha'olam, etc. And if they are four he may say bar'chu' she'achalnu mishlo;' but it is better to say n'varech so as not to exclude himself from the group. And if they are ten he must mention the Name, for he says n'varech Eloheinu, etc., and they answer and say Baruch Eloheinu, etc.; and one should not say n'varech l'Eloheinu' with lammed. And whether they are ten or a hundred or a thousand or myriads, thus they bless. And anyone who changes from this formula — such as if he says n'varech al hamazon she'achalnu, or if he says lemi she'achalnu' mishlo, or if he says in place of uv'tuvo mituvo, or in place of chayinu he says chaim — behold this is a bore (blessed in vain). And when they are ten, since they mention the Name, he may say n'varech Eloheinu al hamazon she'achalnu mishlo.
