There — and in His mercy He will grant us, etc., so it should read; and see in Bach, who brought the short nusach of Kol Boh entirely — and one wonders about him — see there. And I was astonished that he did not mention from the words of Darkhei Moshe siman 91, who copied the nusach of Kol Boh entirely and wrote on this: but I did not know who instituted it and when one should say it, for I did not find it in any other posek. And it was already explained above s.s. 187 that any change from the coin the Sages minted one does not fulfill; and it is possible that also Kol Boh did not write it to fulfill with it, but it is like a short tefillah that one who walks in places of wild beasts and bandits prays; and when he comes home he returns and prays as written siman 110. And it is possible the same applies to this short berachah — one blesses it in place of compulsion and afterward blesses birkat hamazon as established to fulfill with it — requires study; end of Darkhei Moshe's words. And nevertheless from Bach and Magen Avraham it implies that if he blessed the short birkat hamazon that the Gaon Ran instituted due to compulsion or b'dieved he fulfilled; and this Kol Boh is different, for the first berachah is not as established at all, and also the second berachah is mainly missing, as explained in Bach — unlike here, where per Ran one fulfills b'dieved or under compulsion, in my humble opinion.
