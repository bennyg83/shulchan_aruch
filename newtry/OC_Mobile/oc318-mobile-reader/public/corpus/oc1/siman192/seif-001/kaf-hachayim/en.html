(1) [Seif 1] If three were reclining they are obligated in zimun, etc. And it is written in Zohar parashat Balak daf 186 amud bet, and this is its language: at the time a person sits at the table, the Shechinah stands there and the sitra achra stands there. When a person invites to bless the Holy One blessed be He, the Shechinah is arranged above to receive the blessings and the sitra achra is subdued; and if a person does not invite to bless the Holy One blessed be He, the sitra achra hears and strives to have a share in this blessing; end of his words. And if the obligation of zimun is d'oraisa or d'rabbanan — see Petaḥ HaDe'ah ot 4, who ruled to say it is d'oraisa even with three — see there. But Yad Ephraim holds ot 1, who wrote that with three it is d'rabbanan and the verse is merely an asmachta; but with ten, where there is mention of the Name, it is d'oraisa — see there. And so too Ein Ayah siman 199 ot 5 in the name of Sefer Keriat Sefar, that with ten it is d'oraisa; but from the words of Ein Ayah there it implies that also with ten it is d'rabbanan — see there. And the practical difference: if they were in doubt whether they invited, they return to invite; and if they were ten, they return to invite without mention of the Name, because since there is a dispute, one should not mention the Name out of doubt per Chabad custom. And see Mishnat Chasidim ot 1.
