Ba'er Heitev and see in Mishna Berurah who wrote they are accustomed to stand when saying this verse; and likewise in Omek Berachah and in the gloss of his son, author of Shelah, who brought the Zohar and wrote that it is fitting and obligatory to stand for it — see there; and see in Shulchan Tzibbur in the name of Sefer HaKavanot that the time of wrapping in tzitzit at Minchah prayer is after Kaddish of Kedushah and U'va l'Tzion after the chazzan says V'ani tfilati, etc., so that he wraps in tzitzit at a time of ratzon — and wrote in Nagid uMitzvah that one should not cover the face with the tallit, also reveal the forehead from the tallit — see there. And see there it appears Birchat Shem at taking out the sefer Torah; and on the contrary, that is the primary time for saying it — except the Ari would say it even at Shacharit, and one who says it also on weekdays does not lose — see there.
