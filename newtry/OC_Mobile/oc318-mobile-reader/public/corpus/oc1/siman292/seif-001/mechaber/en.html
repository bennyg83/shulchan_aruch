The law of the Minchah prayer on Shabbos. It contains two se'ifim.
At Minchah {we say "Ashrei," "U'va LeTziyon," and "Va'ani Sefilasi," etc. (Tur)} we take out a Torah scroll, and three men read ten verses from the coming parashah. And even if a Yom Tov falls on Shabbos, we read from the coming parashah, and not from that of the Yom Tov.
