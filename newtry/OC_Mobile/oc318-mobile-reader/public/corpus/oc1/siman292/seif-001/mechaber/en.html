דין תפלת מנחה בשבת. ובו ב סעיפים:
1. The Laws Governing the Shabbos Afternoon Service, 2 Seifim: During Minchah [we say אשרי and ובא לציון and ואני תפלתי, etc] (Tur) we take out one Torah and read three aliyot totaling 10 verses from the next week's parshah. And even when Yom Tov falls on Shabbat we read the next week's parshah and not the one for Yom Tov.
