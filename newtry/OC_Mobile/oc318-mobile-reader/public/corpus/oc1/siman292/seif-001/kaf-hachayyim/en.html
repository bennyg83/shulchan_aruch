1) [Seif 1] At Minchah, Ashrei is recited, etc. The custom is first to recite the psalm Lamnatze'ach Al HaGittis Livnei Korach Mizmor, etc., the passage of the continual offering, and the order of Pittum HaKetores, as on a weekday, followed by Ashrei, U'Va LeTzion, and Kedushah DeSidra.
2) There: And U'Va LeTzion. This takes the place of the order of Kedushah recited every morning. Since on Shabbos and Yom Tov the Shacharis, Yotzer, and Mussaf prayers are lengthy, it was postponed until Minchah. Levush. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 74, gives an esoteric reason. The illumination of the Sefer Torah at Shabbos Minchah benefits the World of Creation. Therefore, before taking out the Sefer Torah, we first recite the Kedushah of U'Va LeTzion, whose purpose is to draw light into the World of Creation before receiving the illumination of the Sefer Torah. Through drawing down this light, we can afterward receive the higher illumination of the Sefer Torah; see there. On weekdays, the Sefer Torah is taken out before U'Va LeTzion because its removal then corresponds to a different aspect, as stated in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 48d; see there.
