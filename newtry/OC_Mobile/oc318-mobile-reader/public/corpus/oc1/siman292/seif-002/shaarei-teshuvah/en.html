Three men. Ba'er Heitev that Magen Avraham wrote in a place where there is no sefer Torah they do not say V'ani tfilati; and R' Akiva Eiger disagrees and wrote that because of not interrupting, etc., one should say V'ani tfilati before the Kaddish after U'va l'Tzion, etc.; and see in Yad Ephraim explaining Magen Avraham's words; and see in Shulchan Tzibbur who brought the language of Maharchu z"l and wrote it does not depend on sefer Torah — even one praying alone at home must say it; and it appears from his words one must precede saying U'va l'Tzion and the verse V'ani tfilati to taking out the sefer Torah; and his daughter in the name of Matteh Yehudah to say it even alone; and Shulchan Tzibbur wrote their custom is Torah and per the secret of the matter and also per the simple meaning — see there. And wrote in Ohr Chadash in the name of Maharil: when he would be late to arrive until they finished Torah reading, he would pray with the congregation and afterward read the portion — see there; and see Taz in the name of Beit Yosef that it appears "day of rest and holiness," etc., for it says they shall rest in it, etc.; and see Shulchan Tzibbur who wrote that it means what Yaakov said "and his sons shall rest in it," etc., and so it appears from Ohr Chadash — see there; but in the blessing of Yom Tov he wanted, etc., they say at Minchah "and they shall rest in them" as explained in siman 268 — see there; and see Shulchan Tzibbur it should be "as Your people Israel" per the secret as known to Yom Tov Katan; and Maharikash wrote if an error is found in the sefer Torah at Minchah they take out another — and likewise for two and five — see there; and Mishna Berurah wrote those accustomed to take out another even for mere adhesion — likewise on Shabbat at Minchah for two and five they take out.
