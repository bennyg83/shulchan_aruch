(א) (ו) Tzedekatcha — for Yosef, Moses, and David died on it; and therefore the custom is not to establish a derashah then, for when a chacham dies all derashot are void; and the Bach elaborated to prove that Moses did not die on Shabbat but on erev Shabbat and was not entombed until Shabbat at Minchah at a time of ratzon, as written in the Zohar; and we say Tziduk haDin on him at the time he was entombed. And some say they say Tzedekatcha to justify the judgment on the wicked who return to Gehinnom on Motza'ei Shabbat.
