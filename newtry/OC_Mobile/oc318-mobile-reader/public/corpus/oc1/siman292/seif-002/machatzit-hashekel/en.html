Three men, etc. There is no interruption here, because after U'va LeTzion they recite Kaddish, and after the Torah reading, before Minchah, they also recite Kaddish. If Kaddish were recited at the bimah after the reading, how could another Kaddish be recited immediately before Minchah? There would be nothing between the two Kaddishes. At Shacharis, Ashrei is recited after the Kaddish that follows the Torah reading, and this serves as an interruption. At Minchah, however, Ashrei was already recited before the Torah reading and is not repeated afterward. Therefore, no Kaddish is recited after the reading. Even at Minchah on a fast day, one might have thought that Kaddish should be recited after the three aliyos and before the haftarah, with the haftarah serving as an interruption before the Kaddish preceding the Amidah. Magen Avraham therefore clarifies that this is not the practice.
