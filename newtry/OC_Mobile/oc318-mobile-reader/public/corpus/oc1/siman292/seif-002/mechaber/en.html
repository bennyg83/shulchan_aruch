We say "Tzidkascha." And if a day falls [on that Shabbos] on which, were it a weekday, we would not say nefilas apayim [Tachanun] at Minchah, we do not say "Tzidkascha." {Rama: And the custom is not to hold a study session between Minchah and Ma'ariv (Tur; Zohar, Terumah, p. 279); but we say Pirkei Avos in the summer and "Shir HaMa'alos" in the winter, each place according to its custom.}
