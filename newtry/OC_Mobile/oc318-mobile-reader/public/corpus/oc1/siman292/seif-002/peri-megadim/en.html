They say — Taz and Bach in the name of Yesh Mochin answered: the view is erev Shabbat and the day of its departure was Shabbat — see there. What Taz wrote from Tosafot Ketubot 5a — see there; Tosafot brought from Torah as well in parashat Vayakhel "all who do melacha on it shall die" — and in siman 526 I wrote it appears the day is referred to and mechalelo — they expound mechol lo; and one may say from doubling the required gematria they expound as lo yechalel lo vaH. And I answered letter 9: the Beit Yosef's intent is all the language is in feminine majesty language and salvation and rest, and Shabbat is not mentioned at all in "Atah echad" — see there in Ohr Chadash letter 17 that the custom is to say Tzedekatcha standing since it is in place of Tziduk haDin; and some places do not say it in a house of mourning — Abudraham, see there. And in Tosafot in the congregation it is public — there is [reason] to say it — see there. But within the seven days one does not say Pirkei Avot for it is not from the order of the day — see siman 553 Tosafot. On Tisha B'Av one does not say Pirkei Avot and likewise for a mourner — see Yoreh De'ah 384; and perhaps likewise "Barchi nafshi" is not said — and it requires study what Magen Avraham will explain further.
