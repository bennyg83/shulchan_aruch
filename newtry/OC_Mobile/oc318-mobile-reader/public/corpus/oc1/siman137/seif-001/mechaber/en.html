How Many Verses Must Be Read For Each Aliyah. Containing 6 Se'ifim: On a day that 3
[people] read, we do not read less than 10 verses. [The frequent short introductory verse] "And [the the Master]' spoke..." is included in the count [of verses]. And if the topic ends in less than 10 verses, for example the parsha of Amalek [Exodus 17:8] which has only 9 verses, it is acceptable.
