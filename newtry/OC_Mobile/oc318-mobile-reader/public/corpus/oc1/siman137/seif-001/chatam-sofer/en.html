Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. “They are three men.” Note: This is why we say that the first or the middle reader is likewise praiseworthy. Without this reason, however, we would certainly say, even regarding several readers, that one ascends in holiness and does not descend. In Parashas Haazinu, the first three readers each read six verses, while the fourth, who is the distinguished middle reader, reads ten verses. The fifth then reads seven and the sixth five. This is considered an ascent in holiness relative to the first readers, who each read six verses, while the distinguished middle reader reads ten.
