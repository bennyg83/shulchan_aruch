(1) [Seif 1] On the day they read three — one does not read fewer than ten verses, etc. — chazzan who called priest for Shabbat Minchah three verses, Levi two, Israel three, and they returned the Torah scroll to its place: take out the Torah scroll again and read with Levi another three verses from where he stopped — Rabbi Mahari Zein in responsum Shaarei Yeshuah part 2 Orach Chayim section 3 siman 1, Birkei Yosef note 1, Zachor LeChayim part 1 note 60 and part 3 note 77; see there.
