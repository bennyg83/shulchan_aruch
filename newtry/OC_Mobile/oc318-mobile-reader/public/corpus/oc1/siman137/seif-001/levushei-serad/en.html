Magen Avraham note 1: for the middle one — per his words in note 2, by law one should fix reading four verses for the third because we ascend in holiness as on Chanukah, and they do not heed the importance of the first; only with three men ascending in holiness does not apply so much; therefore now that the chazzan reads it is like one man and four verses reach the third because of ascending in holiness. And what he wrote "for the middle" is an extra — it is not needed for the first not to read four, for he would transgress twice on ascending in holiness — for the second and third; rather even for the middle he should not read.
