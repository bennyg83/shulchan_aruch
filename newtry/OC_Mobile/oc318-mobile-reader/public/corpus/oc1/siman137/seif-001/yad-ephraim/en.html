Magen Avraham, siman 137, seif katan 1. “So as not to embarrass,” etc. This is likewise stated by the Perishah in the name of Maharshal. The reason appears to me to be that if the chazzan gives the extra verse to the first reader, there is no embarrassment to the others, because it is normal to give more to the first, who encountered the honor first. If he gives it to the last reader, there is likewise no embarrassment, for one may say that perhaps he erred and gave each of the others only three verses, and now he has no choice but to give the last reader four so as to complete ten verses. If he gives it to a middle reader, however, the last reader is necessarily embarrassed. According to this, Magen Avraham’s wording, “so as not to embarrass the last,” is precise.
