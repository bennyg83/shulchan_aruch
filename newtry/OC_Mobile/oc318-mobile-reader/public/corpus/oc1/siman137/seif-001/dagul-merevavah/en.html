Thus it is explicit in Kerisos 22b: Rava said, “From the words of both we learn that a definite asham does not require foreknowledge.” The words of Rambam, Mishneh Torah, Hilchos Shegagos 9:10, are puzzling, and what Kesef Mishneh writes—that it is a dispute between Rabbi Yehudah and Rabbi Akiva—is strained. He follows Rashi, which contradicts Rava above; see Tosafos Shabbat 72b s.v. man deamar. Magen Avraham attempts to reconcile the Tur’s words and holds there is no difficulty for the Tur from Rambam contrary to Rosh—but he wrote that Magen Avraham was unaware of Rambam’s words.
