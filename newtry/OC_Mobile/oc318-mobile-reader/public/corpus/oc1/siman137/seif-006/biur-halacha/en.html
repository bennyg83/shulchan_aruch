Or even two, etc. The same applies even if he added only one verse, when the subject concluded there and it was impossible to read more. This follows all the more so from the festival portions concerning the bulls, regarding which greater leniency was granted because no other course was possible. The Mechaber’s use of the term ‘two’ follows the wording of Abudraham, who cites a proof from the people of the maamad, concerning whom the Gemara discusses the addition of two verses because the subject concluded after those two verses.
