Seif katan 3: At Minchah, etc. With a mem, etc., and at Minchah of Yom Tov. According to this, it implies that at Shacharis of Yom Tov the law is like Shabbos, and one must return even if he omitted only one occurrence. This is unlike what is written in the Shulchan Aruch.
It should read “on Yom Tov,” meaning Shacharis of Yom Tov, and so is implied in Maseches Sofrim, chapter 12.
