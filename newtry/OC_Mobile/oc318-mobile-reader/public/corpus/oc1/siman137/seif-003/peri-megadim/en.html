Even — the Taz: it appears he did not see Yerushalmi pei 21 halachah 7 like Magen Avraham in orach 6, 46 — this year differs from Rabbi Meir Lublin z"l siman 85, see there. And one can say in Yerushalmi pei 21 halachah 7 it discusses one who skipped on Shabbat one portion and they read for seven men only 24 verses, and even if each read only three verses with skipping, etc. — nevertheless since he transgressed the Sages' enactment of 21 verses and also skipped, he must read three aliyot with blessing; not so when seven read 21 verses and only skipped one portion — one can say he does not bless on one verse. And this is the case of Yerushalmi pei 1 halachah 6 that wrote on Shabbat if skipped one portion he returns and reads and did not condition "two with him and blessing" — rather it discusses similar to weekday there where he read ten verses, the same on Shabbat 21 verses (in a country that finishes the Torah over several years); and in pei 21 it discusses they did not read 21 verses but if so it is not sufficient, and three verses with him, and must return in order seven men like weekday less than ten and skipped one portion — returns and reads in order three men as explained there in pei 1; and the Mechaber who wrote on weekday if he did not read ten verses he returns and reads — anonymously per his explanation on order of ten verses, while on Shabbat he explained "and two with him" when he read 21 verses; nevertheless in Magen Avraham orach 6 one can say reading is not less than three aliyot and no proof he blesses — therefore he wrote in letter 6 and it implies he blesses — see there explained.
