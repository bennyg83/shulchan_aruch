[3] And if not, etc. — the language of Massekhet Soferim end of chapter 11: he returns and reads ten properly per their view that one does not skip, until here. And although if he read ten even with skipping it suffices — that is b'dieved that he read ten verses — but whoever did not read ten verses that he must return and read in order; and so wrote Sheyarei Knesset HaGedolah. And now it is resolved that seif 3, if they read fewer than nine they must return — for since he must return and read those two verses that were not blessed after him in skipping, therefore they read all ten verses. And Magen Avraham forced himself to explain that they must return on the one who read fewer than three and did not come to this; and perhaps the poskim teach us this; see note 3 [2] and understand. However, if the third read only two verses, he reads from where they began another two verses.
