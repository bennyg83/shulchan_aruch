Shevisi Lord: Baer Hetev; I saw writings of holy Rav Zalman Lokower about menorahs of parchment illustrations placed in siddurim writing Shevisi four letters and names—the menorah called “Shevisi” so one remembers not idle chatter in prayer from fear of Name before eyes; his uncle Gaon Tevuos Shor said poetically “unless Shevisi and silence”—could fence silence without Shevisi—angered greatly because mostly not guarded properly—streets littered—like Rosh Hashanah 18b tallis from scrap parchment turned holiday—therefore abolish custom—elaborated pleasantly; large illustrated menorahs fixed in synagogue before pillar or glass seem fine—concern only small ones in siddurim as above—mostly leads to erasing Names improperly written—warned should shine like Zohar.
