The festival portions, etc. See Mishna Berurah regarding our ruling in the name of Eliyah Rabbah concerning the Mussaf offerings: if the essential obligation of the day was omitted, the reading must be repeated. Accordingly, it would seemingly be the same for the festival portions if the principal passage relevant to that Yom Tov was omitted. For example, on Sukkos they did not read the portion of the festival of Sukkos at all; rather, the five people called to the Torah read only as far as ‘you shall observe your Shabbos,’ and the omission was discovered after the maftir had read. They would have to reopen the Torah scroll and read the festival portion with a blessing before and after it. I also found that the author of Shaarei Ephraim, shaar 8, seif 39, discusses the matter in Pischei Shearim, though not in accordance with our view; see there. He writes that Sefer Shulchan Atzei Shittim also disagrees, and his own view is that, after the fact, if the omission was discovered only after the maftir had already begun, the maftir is not sent back. Instead, the portion is read publicly from the Torah scroll without a blessing.
