(4) [Seif 2] And one reads four, etc. — Rashal wrote in Hagahot: now that the chazzan reads, he should not read for the middle one four, lest the last or first be embarrassed; therefore Magen Avraham brought note 1. But Bach wrote that even now when the chazzan reads with each one, one may also call the third for four — whether with the first, last, or middle — not like Rashal; see there. And they brought in Shakhnei HaGedolah in Hagahot Tashbetz note 1, Orach Torah note 3; and Peri Chadash agreed note 2; and so is Yeshuot Yaakov's view. And so appears from Mechaber's words that there is no difference whether he reads himself or the chazzan reads for him; see Sefer A"R note 1 who wrote another explanation: l'chatchila call for one, and if not for two, etc., see there. We have only Mechaber's words and his view: whichever one the chazzan wishes, call him for four, and there is no stringency; and so is the practice.
