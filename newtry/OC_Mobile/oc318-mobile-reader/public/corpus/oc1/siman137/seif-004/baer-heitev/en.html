(1) And to read — the Taz brought that if one remembered after the final blessing and the shatz has not yet read after, then that one who lacked one verse should read one more verse and not bless the first blessing; rather he completes that verse and blesses the final blessing again. And if they did not remember until after another person read — the first leaves and the second reads with the first blessing at the beginning and begins retroactively from where the previous one began and adds as he wishes — it turns out all the verses were blessed before and after them, only what he read as two verses counts for nothing. (And in Eliyah Rabbah he disagrees with the Taz there.) Magen Avraham wrote that on weekdays he should read again three verses, for it is forbidden to add to the number of readers; but on Shabbat he reads from here onward three verses, and the same on Yom Tov — for some say it is permitted to add, therefore he should not read again — and so agreed in teshuvat Panim Me'irot siman 94.
