He must return and read. He recites a blessing before and after, as Shibolei HaLeket writes. It appears to me that on a weekday this same person should read three verses again, since it is forbidden to add to the prescribed number of aliyos. On Shabbos, however, another person should read three verses from that point onward. The same applies on Yom Tov, since some permit adding aliyos and most poskim agree; therefore, the first person should not read a second time. See siman 282, seif 5.
Fewer than nine. If the second reader repeated one verse already read by the first, they fulfilled their obligation. The proof is from the ma'amados, where only eight verses were read, as explained above. This is unlike Olas Tamid. See seif 6.
They must return. Meaning, the person who read fewer than three verses.
