(2) There, such as Parashat Amalek, etc. — it is written in Orach Chayim: Parashat Amalek has nine verses and they do not add above or below, because the Holy One Blessed Be He said: Amalek caused My name and throne to be lacking — so his portions are lacking. And in Yerushalmi it says: he made a severed act — so his portion is severed — Beit Yosef; and Bach brought it and wrote: it appears to me the question was on the elders and prophets who instituted reading Parashat Amalek on Purim — they should also have instituted to split one verse into two or more above or below so three readers would read ten verses; rather prophecy was to them because the Holy One Blessed Be He said, etc., end of his words. And one can say: this resolves for Samuel; but for Rav, who holds every verse Moses did not divide we do not divide, as in Megillah 22a — how could the elders and prophets split one verse into two? Rather it appears to me in Shulchan Aruch that the question is on Moses son of Amram: why did he not split one into two so there would be ten verses, since he knew they would later institute Megillah reading, as in Megillah 19b; rather because he knew in prophecy that the Holy One Blessed Be He said, etc.
