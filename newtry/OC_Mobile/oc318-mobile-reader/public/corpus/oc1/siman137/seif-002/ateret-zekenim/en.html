One who rises early to engage in Torah should dress himself fittingly to receive his God (Zohar Parashas Noach amud gimel). In Sefer Yesh Sachar VeTov it is good to stand when engaging in Torah so that he not fall asleep (Zohar Parashas Lech). Although he must rise promptly without laziness, as written in Sefer HaYirah and Sefer HaMusar ch. 4, nevertheless he should pause briefly and not stand up suddenly (Levush ch. 1 Berachos). It is written in Seder HaYom that upon rising he should immediately say Modeh Ani before You, living and enduring King, who has mercifully restored my soul within me—great is Your faithfulness—and hand-washing is not required for this even if his hands are dirty, for he does not mention the Name or an appellation.
