Taz note 1: included in this per the name "slick" [topic concluded]. But the reason that the topic alone concluded does not work for fewer than ten verses — only the reason of Orach Chayim works; therefore this is specifically in Parashat Amalek; but in another portion, even though the topic concluded, they add — only there is no such portion where the topic alone concluded except Parashat Amalek; therefore the tanna taught "we do not reduce" — only where the topic did not conclude, excluding Parashat Amalek; understand. Therefore he challenged: we find in haftarah that the reason the topic concluded alone works for fewer — why do I need the reason of Orach Chayim?
