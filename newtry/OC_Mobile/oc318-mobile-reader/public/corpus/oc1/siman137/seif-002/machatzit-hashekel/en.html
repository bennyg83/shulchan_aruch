And at first glance what he wrote: per north-south the middle is drawn, etc. — implies that therefore it is "before the the Master" more than other lamps, against Menachot there; and perhaps because the six are turned sideways, automatically the middle whose mouth was equally west is drawn more west than the others; but the main point is missing from Rashi's words.
