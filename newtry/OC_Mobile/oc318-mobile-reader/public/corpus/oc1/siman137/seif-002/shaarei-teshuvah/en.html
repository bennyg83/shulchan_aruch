Dawn: see Baer Hetev; Birkei Yosef writes our printed Zohar lacks this; manuscript Zohar p. 10 obligates death heavenward without washing even if not rising; if water distant some walk less than four amos—not clear to me—better run quickly not to delay ruach on hands. Shevus Yaakov III §8: people not careful walking four amos without washing—perhaps relied Rashba Berachos whole house like four amos—cannot rely except dire need; see Eshel Avraham subparagraph 4 and below siman 4.
