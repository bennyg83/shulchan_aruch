2) There: Such as parashas Amalek, etc. It is written in the Orchos Chaim that parashas Amalek has nine verses, and one should not add to it from above or from below, because the Holy One, blessed be He, said: Amalek caused My Name and My throne to be lacking, so too its parshiyos are lacking. And in the Yerushalmi it says: he did a truncated deed, so too his parashah is truncated. Beis Yosef. And the Bach cited it and wrote: it appears to me that the difficulty was upon the Elders and Prophets who instituted reading parashas Amalek on Purim, for they should have also instituted dividing one verse into two, either above or below, so that three would read ten verses; but a prophecy came to them, because the Holy One, blessed be He, said, etc. End of his language. And one can say: this is well according to Shmuel, but according to Rav, who holds that any verse that Moshe did not divide, we do not divide, as stated in Megillah, folio 22a, how could the Elders and Prophets divide one verse into two? Rather, it appears in my humble opinion, with the help of Heaven, that the difficulty is upon Moshe Rabbeinu, peace be upon him: why did he not divide one into two, so that there would be ten verses, since he knew that they were destined to institute the reading of the Megillah, as stated in Megillah, folio 19b? Rather, because he knew through prophecy that the Holy One, blessed be He, said, etc.
