4) [Seif 2] And one reads four, etc. The Rashal wrote in a gloss: nowadays, when the chazan reads, one should not read four for the middle [oleh], so as not to shame the last or the first. Until here. And the Magen Avraham cited him, s.k. 1. But the Bach wrote that even nowadays, when the shaliach tzibbur reads with each and every one, one may also have him read four, whether with the first or with the last or with the middle, and not like the Rashal; see there. And the Shiyurei Knesses HaGedolah cited him in the Hagahos HaTur, ot 1; Olas Tamid, ot 3; and so the Pri Chadash concurred, ot 2; and such appears the view of the Yad Aharon. And so is implied from the words of Maran, of blessed memory, that there is no distinction whether he reads himself or the shaliach tzibbur reads for him. And see the Eliyah Rabbah, ot 1, who wrote another explanation, that l'chatchilah one should read four for one [oleh], and if not for one, one should read it for two, etc.; see there. But we have only the words of Maran, of blessed memory, and those who agree with him, that for whichever of them the shaliach tzibbur wishes, he reads four, and there is no objection; and so the people practice.
