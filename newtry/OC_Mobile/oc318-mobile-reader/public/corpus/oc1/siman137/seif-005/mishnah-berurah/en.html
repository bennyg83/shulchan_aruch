(17) And he rolled the Sefer Torah. Many later authorities agree that “rolled” is not literal. The essential factor is whether he recited the concluding blessing. If he did not, then even if he rolled the Sefer Torah, he returns and reads the remainder without another opening blessing.
(18) From the beginning of the portion. It does not suffice to begin where he initially stopped, because since they did not read in the order established by the Sages, there was no valid reading. Nevertheless, the later authorities agree that if he merely omitted a verse he does not return, since these portions are read on their respective Shabbosos, as explained above in seif 3 regarding festival portions. However, this applies only if he omitted verses that are not part of the principal obligation of the day, namely up to “the stranger who resides among them.” If he omitted from the principal obligation of the day, Eliyah Rabbah agrees that he returns, as explained above in Mishnah Berurah seif katan 11 regarding the verses of the Mussaf offering.
