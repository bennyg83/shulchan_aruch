[2] [Levush] If the wind extinguished it, etc. So wrote Terumat HaDeshen, and the Taz wondered: if so, from light to light as well — why is it permitted even when moving a light that one wishes to kindle to a light from which one is kindling, and one must in any case leave it in its place as written in siman 675 — until here. In my humble opinion there is no difficulty, for even at the end of siman 675, if one holds it a short time and then places it, he has fulfilled; if so, since kindling accomplishes the mitzvah, even if it went out before he placed it, nevertheless when he returns and kindles it and leaves it a short time until half an hour passes, together with the first kindling he has fulfilled. It also appears to me that according to what the Levush explained the Gemara meaning of kindling from light to light — that one takes a burning light to a placed light and kindles it from it.
