Section 2: There is one who, etc. Sefer HaTerumah, and he follows his own view in section 1. But according to the first view in section 1, that degradation of a mitzvah is forbidden, this too is forbidden, for the mitzvos cancel one another, as Ran wrote, that for this reason even sacred use is forbidden before a Chanukah lamp. Although the reason there is degradation of the mitzvah, as written there in the Gemara, and is a lamp holy, etc., and is blood, etc.; see Ran, and above, siman 673, section 1.
