And it is permitted to kindle one from another. See Biur HaGra, who wrote that according to the first view above in siman 673, section 1, that even sacred use is forbidden by the Chanukah lamp, and they wrote the reason that it is not similar to what we hold, that one may kindle from lamp to lamp, because there all the lamps are one mitzvah, whereas learning is another mitzvah and they appear to cancel each other, in our case too it is forbidden. See Chemed Moshe, whose view is also that one may not kindle other lamps from the Chanukah lamp; see there.
