There is one who says that a synagogue lamp, a Shabbos lamp, and a Chanukah lamp are all mitzvah lamps, and it is permitted to kindle one from another. Gloss: The same applies to a lamp for Torah study or a lamp for a sick person who needs a lamp. Nimukei Yosef, Chelek. Regarding a synagogue lamp, see above, siman 154, section 14.
