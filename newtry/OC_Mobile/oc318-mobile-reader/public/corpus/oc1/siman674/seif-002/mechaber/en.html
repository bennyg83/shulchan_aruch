There is an [opinion] that says that the lights of the synagogue, of the Sabbath, and of Hanukkah are all mitzvah [lights] and it is permitted to light one from another. {Rama: RAMA: It is the same law for a light for Torah study or a light for a sick person who needs a light, (Nimukei Yosef) (and regarding the light of the synagogue see above 154:14).}
