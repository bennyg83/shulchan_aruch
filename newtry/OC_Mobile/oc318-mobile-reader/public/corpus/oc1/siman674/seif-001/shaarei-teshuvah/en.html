From one to another. See Baer Heitev. See Responsa Beis Yehudah, siman 18, who wondered about this, for in the Talmud it is proven that one may kindle from lamp to lamp, even a lamp of the mehadrin from the mitzvah lamp adjacent to it. One cannot say that he explains it as a lamp of this house, etc.; nor can one say that it refers to the custom in siman 671, that each member of the household kindles, for in the end it is included among the mehadrin; see there. In truth, the words of Rema are from Hagahos Maimoniyos and Mordechai.
