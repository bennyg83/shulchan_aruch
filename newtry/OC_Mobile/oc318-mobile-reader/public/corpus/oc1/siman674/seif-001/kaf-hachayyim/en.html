1) [Seif 1] One may kindle one Chanukah lamp from another Chanukah lamp. This applies, for example, from the second night onward, when one kindles more than one lamp in fulfillment of the enhanced practice, and one kindles one from another.
2) Ibid. One may kindle one Chanukah lamp from another Chanukah lamp. We are not concerned that doing so weakens it by drawing away some of the moisture of its oil, since the other lamp is also being used for a mitzvah. Levush.
