From one to another. All the more so, if the shamash went out, one may not kindle it from the Chanukah lamp. Maharshal wrote that in the synagogue there is no distinction between the shamash and the other lamps, for all the lamps inside it are called a mitzvah lamp. Therefore one should rebuke those who light their lamps by means of maidservants from the synagogue lamp, except on Motzaei Shabbos because of the dark alleyways, through which Shaul merited kingship. A Havdalah lamp is called a mitzvah lamp. But after he has recited Havdalah over it, the mitzvah has already been performed and one may kindle from it, for even a Chanukah lamp after its required time is no longer called a mitzvah lamp.
