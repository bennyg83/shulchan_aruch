From one to another. And all the more so if the shamash went out, one may not kindle from a Hanukkah light. Maharshal wrote that in the synagogue there is no distinction between the shamash and the other lights, for all the lights inside are called a mitzvah light; therefore one should rebuke those who kindle their lights by maidservants from a synagogue light, except on Motzei Shabbat because of the dark alleyways through which Saul merited the kingdom. A havdalah light is called a mitzvah light; but after one has made havdalah on it the mitzvah is already done and one may kindle from it, for even a Hanukkah light after its required time is no longer called a mitzvah light — end quote; see siman 554.
