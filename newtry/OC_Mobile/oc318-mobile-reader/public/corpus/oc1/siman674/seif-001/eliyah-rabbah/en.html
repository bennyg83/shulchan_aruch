[A] And there is Metirin, etc. And it is customary to make it strict not to light even a single candle. The main mitzvah is only one candle (Rama'), that is, one candle for all the members of one's household, and if the sun goes out, surely one should not light a Hanukkah candle. Even if a Hanukkah candle has been lit for half an hour, it is not to be lit from them.
