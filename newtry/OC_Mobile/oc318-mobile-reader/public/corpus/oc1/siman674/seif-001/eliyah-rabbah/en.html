And some permit, etc. The practice is to be stringent not to kindle even from one lamp to another, because the main mitzvah is only one lamp. Rema. Meaning, one lamp is enough for all the members of the household. If the shamash goes out, one certainly may not kindle it from a Chanukah lamp. Bach wrote that even after the Chanukah lamps have burned for half an hour, one may not kindle from them; see siman 672, paragraph 2.
