Se'if 1. Some have the custom. See Tosafos of Kiddushin, folio 81, s.v. sakva. And see in the Tur, siman 429, where he wrote in the name of Masseches Sofrim that students fast Bahab because of chillul Hashem for three days; and he wrote, and it appears to me that those three days he said after Pesach, as is customary in Ashkenaz, and likewise after Sukkos, because they are days of joy, and they based themselves on the verse of Iyov, "And it was when the days of feasting had gone round," etc.; and see Beis Yosef there; and so wrote the Tur the reason here; and so wrote the Rosh in the first chapter of Taanis; and see Magen Avraham.
And they wait. See Magen Avraham. And even though in Masseches Sofrim he wrote to fast in Nissan, [nevertheless we wait,] because we do not practice like Masseches Sofrim, who permits fasting in Nissan when they do not mention [it] publicly, as written in siman 429; and furthermore, we practice to fast publicly, and even according to Masseches Sofrim that is forbidden.
Immediately. See Magen Avraham.
And they make it, etc. Even though in the Tosefta of Taanis it is taught that they do not read the Torah on an individual's fast, a congregation that accepts a communal fast is called a public [fast]; and even though regarding she'elah he said at the end of the first chapter of Taanis that they are like individuals, that is [only] regarding changing the fixed form of the blessings of the prophets' ordinance; and similarly they formulated Aneinu as a blessing; the Rosh there; and see siman 566, se'if 2.
