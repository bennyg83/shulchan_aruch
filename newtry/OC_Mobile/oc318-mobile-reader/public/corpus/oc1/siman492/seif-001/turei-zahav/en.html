Immediately Monday and Thursday. In Mahariv in his Halachos, siman 14, he wrote that one should not fast Bahab in Cheshvan until seventeen days into the month, which is the time of the first rainfall, when they used to fast; and so wrote Ravyah, and so wrote Maharil. And in our times, when they begin fasting earlier, they lose nothing, and they are zealous and are rewarded. And if there is a milah on these fasts, see siman 568.
