(s.k. 2) And they say etc. As is written in siman 167, that one should not interrupt between Birkas Hamotzi and eating the Hamotzi.
That it is not considered an interruption. Since it is for the need of eating the apple. And as is written in siman 167, that speech that is for the need of the meal is not an interruption.
And likewise in the sukkah they recite the blessing to dwell in the sukkah, as is written in siman 167 in the gloss.
