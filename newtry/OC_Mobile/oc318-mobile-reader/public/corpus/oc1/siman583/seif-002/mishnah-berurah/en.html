(6) A sheep's head. And better still is that of a ram.
(7) To say we shall be for a head. And therefore if he has no sheep's head, he should eat another head of an animal or of a fowl.
(8) The river etc. And to say "You will return, You will have compassion on us etc." Because it is stated in the Midrash that Avraham Avinu went in water up to his neck when he went to offer him upon the altar, and said "Save me, for waters have come unto my soul," and we do this as a remembrance of the Akeidah. And it is good to go to a place that has live fish, as a sign that the evil eye not dominate us, and that we be fruitful and multiply like fish. And in the writings he wrote: a river or a well, and it is good that it be outside the city. And one should go on the first day after Minchah before sunset and say the verse "Who is a God like You etc."; end quote. And in some places I saw that when the first day falls on Shabbos, they go on the second day to the river. And it is possible that this is because the river is outside the city, and because of carrying, that they carry books and the like; therefore they go on the second day (Peri Megadim).
(9) Not to sleep etc. Because it is stated in the Yerushalmi: whoever sleeps at the beginning of the year, his mazal sleeps. And the Arizal said that after midday it is permitted to sleep, for the angel has already been awakened by the prayers and tekios. And the Bach wrote that the Maharam would sleep on Rosh Hashanah. And one who sits idle is considered like one who sleeps. And see Chayei Adam, who wrote that after eating one should set oneself to study; and if his head is heavy upon him, he should sleep a little if he cannot manage without this. And some are accustomed to complete all of Tehillim.
