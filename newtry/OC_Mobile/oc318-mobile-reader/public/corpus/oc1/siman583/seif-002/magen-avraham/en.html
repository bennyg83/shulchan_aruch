Sheep. And if there is no sheep's head, one should eat another head, because of the first reason.
Nuts. I heard that the large ones are called egosim and the small ones are called luzim, and so it implies in Midrash Rabbah on Shir HaShirim; but according to the second reason, both are forbidden.
The river. That has live fish in it (Minhagim and Matteh Moshe); see siman 497, se'if 2. But in the writings it is written: a river or a well, and it is good if they are outside the city; and one should go on the first day after Minchah before sunset and say the verse "Who is a God like You etc."; end quote.
And not to sleep. The Arizal said that after midday it is permitted to sleep, for the angel has already been awakened by the prayers and the tekios. And in the Bach it is written that the Maharam slept on Rosh Hashanah; and one who sits idle is considered like one who sleeps.
