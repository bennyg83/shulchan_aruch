There, s.k. 1. Apple etc. And what he wrote, "Rabbeinu Yeruham [mentions] a pashtida," is not understood. And perhaps it should say: and some eat a pashtida; that is to say, in order to exempt the blessings; see siman 168, s.k. 22; and in Birkhei Yosef 177, s.k. 3, in the name of the Hagahos Tashbetz regarding an apple pashtida; see there; and this requires further study. And perhaps it should say: and a proof from a pashtida; that is to say, that even according to the one who holds to be stringent to take out the stuffing and recite borei pri ha'etz, even though the apples in the stuffing are in honey in the manner of a pashtida, we are dealing with a case where the honey is secondary. But in my humble opinion there is no proof, for since his intent is to eat the honey as a hint to its sweetness, one may say that the honey is primary. But according to this there is also no proof from dipping the maror; and this requires further study.
