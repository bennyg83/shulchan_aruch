[1] Yehi Ratzon etc. Meaning that he should say: May it be Your will before You, Hashem our God and God of our fathers, that our merits multiply; and so for all of them. And so it is in the Kol Bo. And the Shlah wrote that it is a sign that a person should remember and be stirred to repentance and pray concerning this matter; end quote. And it implies that also on the second night one should do so.
