It appears to me that the same applies to whatever is called in other languages a plural form: each and every country should eat according to its language; and see Berachos, chapter 9, regarding a cat (shunra). One should eat fish to be fruitful and multiply like fish, and they are not cooked in vinegar (Abudraham); see what I wrote at the beginning of siman 597. Apple. And one should recite the blessing over the apple, for the apple is primary; for it is written in the Kesef Mishneh: they take the apple and dip it in honey, and he did not write that they eat honey with the apple, which implies that the apple is primary, like the model of dipping the maror (Maharil, Darkei Moshe); and Rabbeinu Yeruham [mentions] a pashtida; and some are accustomed to dip a slice of the Hamotzi in honey.
And they say. It implies that he eats first and afterward says [the Yehi Ratzon], so as not to interrupt, as is written in siman 167, se'if 6. And Mateh Tzvi wrote that he says first and afterward eats, and one must say that he holds that it is not considered an interruption. And likewise the Bach wrote in siman 605 in the name of the Tashbetz to say "this is my exchange etc." between the blessing and the shechitah, for this is called a need of the shechitah; and likewise in the sukkah they recite the blessing between the blessing and the Hamotzi. Nevertheless it appears to me that ideally one should eat before saying [it], for this is not such a need; and furthermore, even for a need one should not interrupt ideally, as is written in the gloss.
