(1) And when he eats rubia etc. And the same applies to whatever is called in other languages a language of increase: each and every country should eat according to its language.
(2) Yehi Ratzon. Before You, Hashem our God and God of our fathers, that our merits multiply. And Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of the Shlah that a person should be stirred to repentance when he says Yehi Ratzon, and pray about this with a whole heart.
(3) A sweet apple in honey. And the apple is primary, and one blesses over it and exempts the honey. And even though one eats the apple after Birkas Hamotzi, nevertheless one must bless over it. And some are accustomed to dip the Hamotzi in honey.
(4) That You renew etc. And he should say the request after beginning to eat, because it is forbidden to interrupt between a blessing and eating.
(5) A sweet year. And therefore some refrain from cooking on Rosh Hashanah vinegar dishes, borscht, and the like. And therefore those who eat fish as a sign that they should be fruitful and multiply like fish do not cook them in vinegar. And behold, all these matters are done entirely as a good sign; therefore it is obvious that one should be very careful not to get angry on these days, aside from the severity of the prohibition itself, so that it be a good sign; rather his heart should be joyful and confident in Hashem, together with repentance and good deeds.
