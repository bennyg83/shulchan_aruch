That is, that he eats first and afterward says [the Yehi Ratzon], so as not to interrupt; see Magen Avraham in Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah, who discusses at length whether one should recite the blessing over the apple or also over the honey; see there. And in Responsa Shevut Yaakov, part 2, siman 27, he wrote that because of this doubt they were accustomed to eat the Hamotzi with the honey and the apple together; see there.
Oil. The Maharshal received from his grandfather that he did not eat fish on Rosh Hashanah, in order to somewhat diminish his desire in some matter. And Abudraham wrote that one should eat fish in order to be fruitful and multiply like fish, and one should not cook them in vinegar.
