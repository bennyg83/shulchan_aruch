1) [Seif 1] A person should be accustomed to eat on Rosh Hashanah, etc. The woman should be diligent to change her clothing while it is still day and light the candles. Their laws are like those of every festival, as written above, siman 514, seif 11, and siman 263, seif 5, see there. She should arrange the place where the meal will be eaten, set the table, and cover it with a fine tablecloth before her husband returns from the synagogue, so that when he arrives he will find everything prepared and the table set, which is a good sign. Nevertheless, if circumstances arose and he arrived and did not find the table set, he must not quarrel with his wife, G-d forbid, or become angry, for on the contrary, anger is a most unfavorable sign. Rather, he should judge her favorably and not be resentful even in his heart.
2) Upon returning from the synagogue, before Kiddush, he should sit in his place upon a prepared bed and recite Patach Eliyahu HaNavi until "Arise, R. Shimon, and let new words be revealed," etc. He should then recite the verse, "But Noach found favor," etc., 12 times; then the verse, "Yitzchak sowed in that land," etc., 12 times, or 13 times in a leap year; then the verse, "For with You is the source of life; in Your light we see light," 10 times; then the verse, "Light is sown for the righteous," etc., 17 times; and afterward the verse, "And you shall say, Thus shall you say to the living: You shall have peace," etc., 10 times. He should then say, "May the year and its blessings begin," and begin Kiddush. Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Nitzavim, os 7.
