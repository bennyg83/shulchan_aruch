1) [Seif 1] A person should be accustomed to eat on Rosh Hashanah, etc. The woman should be diligent to change her clothing while it is still day and light the candles. Their laws are like those of every festival, as written above, siman 514, seif 11, and siman 263, seif 5, see there. She should arrange the place where the meal will be eaten, set the table, and cover it with a fine tablecloth before her husband returns from the synagogue, so that when he arrives he will find everything prepared and the table set, which is a good sign. Nevertheless, if circumstances arose and he arrived and did not find the table set, he must not quarrel with his wife, God forbid, or become angry, for on the contrary, anger is a most unfavorable sign. Rather, he should judge her favorably and not be resentful even in his heart.
