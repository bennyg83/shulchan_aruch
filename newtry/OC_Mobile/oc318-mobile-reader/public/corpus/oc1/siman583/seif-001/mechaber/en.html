Customary foods for the eve of Rosh Hashanah. Containing two sections: One should eat beans, leeks,
beets, dates, and pumpkin. And as one eats the beans ( rubiya ), they say: God, may our merits increase (yirbu)! Eating leeks ( karti ), they say: God, may our enemies be wiped out ( yekartu )! Eating dates ( tamari ), say: God, may our enemies disappear ( yetamu )! Eating pumpkin ( kra ), say: God, may our judgement be ripped up ( yikra' ) and may our merits be called out ( yikaru ) before You!Rema: Some have a custom of eating a sweet apple in honey) (Tur), and saying: May a sweet year be renewed on us! (Abudarham) This is what we do. Some eat pomegranates, and say: may our merits be as many as pomegranate seeds! And we are accustomed to eat fatty meat and all sorts of sweets (Mordechai on Yoma).):
