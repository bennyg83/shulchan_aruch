May our enemies be cut off. It is written in the Agudah: This is difficult, for if so, one should eat every type of food; if it is good, it should correspond to the Jewish people, and if it is bad, it should correspond to non-Jews. Rather, one can say that both rubia and leeks grow rapidly, and they are therefore a good sign. End quote.
To eat an apple. Maharil wrote that one must first recite borei pri ha'etz, because it is among the foods brought during the meal that are not eaten as part of the meal. The apple alludes to the well-known ‘field of apples’ according to Kabbalah. End quote.
