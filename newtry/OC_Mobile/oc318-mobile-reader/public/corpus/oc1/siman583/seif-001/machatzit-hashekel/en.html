Magen Avraham wrote: It appears to me that the same etc. And see Berachos, folio 56b: one who sees a cat in a dream: in a place where it is called shunra (with a ו after the ש), its interpretation is that a beautiful song (shirah) will be made for him. And in a place where it is called shinra (with a י after the ש), a bad change (shinui ra) will be done to him. Thus it depends on the language of the place and the country.
