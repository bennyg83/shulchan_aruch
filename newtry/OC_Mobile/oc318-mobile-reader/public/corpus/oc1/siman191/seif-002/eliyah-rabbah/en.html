(2) [Levush] and they did not permit, etc. And Ma'adanay Melech wrote the reason: logic suggests including the later in the earlier; to here. And to me it appears the Levush's intent is that in any case they need not have instituted combining specifically in the berachah of the land, but rather the permission was in their hands to combine the berachah of the land in Boneh Yerushalayim, for what precedes — the berachah of the land before Boneh Yerushalayim — is because it precedes in time, and even now one needs Boneh Yerushalayim more than the berachah of the land.
