(2) There. They abbreviate in birkat hamazon, etc. Shach Me'ubad wrote in Choshen Mishpat siman 37 that also one need not wait for others until they invite him — see there. But Magen Avraham s.k. 1 wrote that they are obligated to wait for zimun — see there. And in Biur Halacha, nowadays the custom is as Shulchan Aruch wrote in seif 2, that they say all four berachot and also say "HaRachaman Hu," etc., until the conclusion of "Oseh shalom bimromav," etc. And if so, automatically they are obligated in zimun as well if they are three or ten; and presumably anyone who hires workers hires with intent of the custom that they will invite and bless all four berachot and also HaRachaman, etc., until the conclusion of Oseh shalom, etc. And so is the practice of the people.
