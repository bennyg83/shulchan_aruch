They abbreviate in birkat hamazon. Also one need not wait for others until they invite him (Shach Me'ubad Choshen Mishpat siman 37). And it appears to me the beginning of Tur's words there, for the Sages were strict in zimun regarding the fourth berachah, etc. — meaning Abbaye answered Amen in a loud voice so that workers would stand; and necessarily he speaks where he was inviting them and they fulfilled through his berachah; therefore Tur took "in zimun." And if so, on the contrary it is somewhat proven from there that they are obligated to wait for zimun. And siman 110 seif 2 — Tosafot wrote: even though they are d'oraisa, nevertheless the Sages have power to uproot a matter from the Torah on account of preoccupation with birkat hamazon. And Rabbeinu Yonah wrote that there is no count of berachot from the Torah, and Bach wrote that this is Rambam's view; but many hold that the count of three berachot is from the Torah. And see siman 194. And it appears to me Rashi resolved this difficulty, who wrote as follows: that the berachah of the land and building Jerusalem are similar — meaning that what we expound from the verse that one bless on all three is not meaning that one must conclude on each one with a berachah, but from the Torah it suffices when one blesses on all three in one berachah; but the Rabbis instituted to conclude on each one. And regarding workers, on account of nullification of work, they instituted that HaZan bless separately and the berachah of the land and building Jerusalem in one berachah, since they are similar; end of Bach's words. And so too in the Gemara on daf 46; and see Rabbi Yehuda Leib siman 44 and Rabbi Shimon Chiyun siman 42.
