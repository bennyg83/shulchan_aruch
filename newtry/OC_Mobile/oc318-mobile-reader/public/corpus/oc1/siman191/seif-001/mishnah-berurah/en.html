(1) And include in it Boneh Yerushalayim — for even though we expound in the Gemara from a verse that one bless Heaven in birkat hamazon on sustenance, on the land, and on Jerusalem, it is not meaning that from the Torah one must conclude on each one separately with its own berachah, but from the Torah it suffices when one blesses on all three in one berachah; but the Rabbis instituted to conclude with a berachah on each one. And regarding workers, on account of nullification of work, they instituted that only HaZan bless separately, but the berachah of the land and building Jerusalem, since they are similar to one another, one combines them in one berachah [Magen Avraham in explanation of Rashi's words, and so too many later authorities for the halachah].

(2) At all — for this berachah is only d'rabbanan in general, and they uprooted it regarding workers, whether they bless by themselves or hear from another who invites and blesses birkat hamazon as established — behold they stand for their work when he concludes the berachah of Jerusalem. And regarding the berachah of zimun, whether workers are obligated in this or not — there are opinions among the later authorities; and according to what he wrote in seif 2, there is no practical difference for us, for certainly they are obligated. However, when a worker eats with other people and he finishes his meal first, it appears he need not wait for them if they do not wish to answer him, for in such a case certainly the employer is strict. And see below siman 200 in Magen Avraham s.k. 2.

(3) When they receive wages — and necessarily they must hurry the work [Rashi].

(4) Reclines with them, etc. — for presumably he waives for them.
