1) [Seif 1] Laborers, etc., recite an abbreviated form, etc. Tosafos in Berachos 16a explain the reason: although the blessings are of Torah origin, the Sages have authority to suspend a Torah matter because the laborers are occupied with the employer's work. Maran, of blessed memory, however, writes in Beis Yosef that the reason is that the number of blessings in Birkas HaMazon is not of Torah origin; see there. We already wrote this above in siman 187, paragraph 1; see there.
