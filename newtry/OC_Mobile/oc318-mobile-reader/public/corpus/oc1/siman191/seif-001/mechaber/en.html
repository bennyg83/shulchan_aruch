Whether workers are obligated to bless. And in it are 3
seifim. Workers doing work for an employer abbreviate birkat hamazon so as not to cancel the employer's work. How? The first berachah as established; the second they open with the berachah of the land and include in it Boneh Yerushalayim and conclude with the berachah of the land; and they do not say the berachah of HaTov VeHaMetiv at all. In what circumstances does this apply? When they receive wages for their work besides the meal; but if they receive nothing except the meal they eat, they bless all four berachot as established. And likewise if the employer reclines with them, even if they receive wages besides the meal, they bless all four berachot.
