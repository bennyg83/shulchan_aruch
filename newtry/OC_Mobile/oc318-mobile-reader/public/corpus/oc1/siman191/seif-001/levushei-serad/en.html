Magen Avraham s.k. 1 — also one need not wait. Tur there warns that workers should not cease even a little, for the Sages were strict regarding zimun of the fourth berachah. Shach Me'ubad challenged: what is "in zimun" that Tur said — it should have said that the Sages were strict regarding the fourth berachah. Therefore he reads "in zimun and regarding the fourth berachah," and he says two things.
