(1) [Levush] it is not written, etc. Ma'adanay Melech on daf 16 wrote as follows: aside from his honor he need not have done so, for we hold that the Sages have power to suspend a shev v'al ta'aseh prohibition; to here. And so wrote Meleches Yom Tov. And in my humble opinion, the Levush's intent is that they had power to suspend this on account of the berachah of Boneh Yerushalayim over the berachah of the land and not the reverse — that is, they concluded but did not permit combining the berachah of the land with it. And further, if they had wished to suspend a d'oraisa matter entirely, they would not have instituted more than one berachah and combined the other two with it; consider this.
