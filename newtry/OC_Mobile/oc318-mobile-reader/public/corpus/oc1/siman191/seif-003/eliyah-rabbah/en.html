(3) [Levush] because it burdens, etc. And above siman 63 he wrote the reason that it should be in a casual manner — one must say that in kri'at shema it is written "and speak of them," meaning make them fixed, which is not so in the synagogue, and this requires study. And perhaps the two reasons are one reason. Olas Tamid wrote, and it appears specifically l'chatchila, but b'dieved one fulfills, no worse than intoxication as above; to here. And it is somewhat difficult: if so, why did Chazal uproot a Torah matter in the berachah of Boneh Yerushalayim since they are preoccupied with the employer's work, as Tosafot wrote on daf 16 — would it not be more fitting to bless when they are occupied in their work, since b'dieved it is permitted? Magen Avraham wrote that even light use is forbidden to do. And Taz wrote not to engage in Torah at that hour, and not specifically birkat hamazon but the same law applies to other berachot and tefillah; and even if one engages in this mitzvah and engages in another mitzvah it is not proper — and this is the intent of the verse "And if you walk with Me casually," meaning in a happenstance manner.
