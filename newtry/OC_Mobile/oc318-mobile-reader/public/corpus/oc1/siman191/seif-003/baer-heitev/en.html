Melacha. Even light use is forbidden. And the same applies to all mitzvot — a person should not perform them and engage in another matter, for this indicates he performs the mitzvah without intent but in a happenstance and casual manner, as our Sages said: "And if you walk with Me casually" — meaning that even though you walk with Me, that is, perform the mitzvah, nevertheless it is in a happenstance and casual manner. And it appears to me that included in this prohibition is not to engage in Torah at that hour. Taz.
