(5) It is forbidden to do melacha, etc. — because it appears as though one blesses in a happenstance and casual manner; and even light use is forbidden to do; and all the more so one should not engage in a matter that requires his attention. And Taz wrote that one should be careful not to look even in divrei Torah while blessing birkat hamazon, for this indicates that birkat hamazon is for him only on the side of happenstance and chance; and not specifically birkat hamazon — the same applies when engaging in tefillah or in any other berachah. And this is included in the statement of our Torah "And if you walk with Me casually" — meaning that mitzvot should not be for us on the side of happenstance and chance in general.
(6) Above at the end of siman 183 — except there it speaks where he first began melacha, and here it speaks where he first began to bless; and it is the same.
