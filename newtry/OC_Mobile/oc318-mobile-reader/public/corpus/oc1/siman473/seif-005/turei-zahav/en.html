But not in their root. Mahari Weil wrote: meaning the small roots that split here and there, but the large root on which the vegetable stands is included in the stalk; thus far. And I have seen many people who at the time of eating maror take its vegetable, and at the time of korech take the stalk, and this has no reason or sense; for one should take the vegetable also at the time of korech, for he can eat well a kezayit from it, since it does not distress so much as the stalk, which has more strength.
