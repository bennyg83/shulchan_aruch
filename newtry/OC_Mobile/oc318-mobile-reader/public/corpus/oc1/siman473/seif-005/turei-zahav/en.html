Mahariu wrote: meaning the small roots that branch to here and to there; but the large root in which the vegetable stands is included in the stalk; thus I saw many people who at the time of eating maror take their vegetable, and at the time of wrapping take the stalk — and this has no reason or logic; for one should take the vegetable also at the time of wrapping, for he can eat well a k'zayit from it, for it does not distress as much as the stalk which has more strength:
