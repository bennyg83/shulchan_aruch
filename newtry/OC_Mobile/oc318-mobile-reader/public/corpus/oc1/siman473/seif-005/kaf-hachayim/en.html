He sanctifies standing — Seder HaYom; we are not accustomed thus unless Pesach is Shabbat because of Vaykhulu — as widespread in Hagahot, and so in Pesach Ma'uvin s"k 208; however per Arizal all kiddushim must be standing, Shabbat and Yom Tov, as above siman 471 s"k 62 — see there; and so Yafeh Lalev vol. 2 s"k 1: he begins Eileh mo'adei Heaven, etc.; if on weekday — see there; meaning: but if on Shabbat, first say Yom ha-shishi Vaykhulu, etc., then Eileh mo'adei Heaven, etc., then Savri maranan, then wine berakha and kiddush and sheheheyanu; and before all say Mizmor le-David Heaven ro'i, etc., as above siman 471 s"k 63 — see there.
