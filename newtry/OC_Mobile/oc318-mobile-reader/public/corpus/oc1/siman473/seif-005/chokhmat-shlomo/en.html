Seif 5 — These are the vegetables with which one fulfills his obligation, etc. Note: we read in Pesachim daf 39b: Rambach asked whether one fulfills with ma'aser sheni for maror, etc., until Rava said it is reasonable — matzah and maror with Yom Tov — see my explanation of this sugya in my books on Orach Chayim from the year 5622 in the laws of Birkat HaMazon siman 184, in my writings there daf 131a — see there and investigate.(There, seif 5 in the gloss) And if he lacks one of these vegetables he takes wormwood or another bitter vegetable, etc. Note: it appears clear to me that one may take radish called rettich in our country for maror, for it is explicit in Magen Avraham and Beit Yosef that tamcha is what they call maraytikh; and if in Ashkenaz they call krien rettich, nevertheless rettich too is so called; if so, both are called thus — only to distinguish they call one maraytikh and one rettich, but their root is one; reason supports this, for radish too is pungent like krien, soft at first and hard at end, a sharp thing like krien; and Rama's view is that otherwise one fulfills with any bitter vegetable, and Rashi in Chumash as Magen Avraham wrote. This does not contradict the mishnah — one may say: for matzah types where the Torah specifies importance, only these five count as bread; but maror is not specified in Torah what it is, therefore Rashi explained from Torah any bitter herb is maror, but the Sages enumerated those common in their place, soft beginning hard end — they found these and enumerated them. Similarly Megillah 10: why did the Sages enumerate these, etc. — they found these and enumerated, not only these; so too maror — they found these and enumerated; and if another similar species is found it is also maror; therefore Rashi: since Torah did not specify the species, whatever is a bitter vegetable is called maror — unlike matzah where Torah requires chimutz and it be called bread, and Torah specified bread is only wheat and barley — therefore for matzah we need specifically these; not so maror; therefore for rettich, which we doubt if in tamcha category, there is room to say perhaps in tamcha; and if you say not in tamcha, perhaps per Rashi and Rama one fulfills with any thing. The essential view: both krien and rettich are in tamcha category and are one species as explained — several elders told me they saw people fulfill maror with rettich; investigate.
