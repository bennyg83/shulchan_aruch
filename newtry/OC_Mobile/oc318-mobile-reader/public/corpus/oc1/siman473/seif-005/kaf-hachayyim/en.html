Knesses HaGedolah, Hagahos Beis Yosef. He writes likewise in Pesach Me'ubin, subsection 208. According to the Arizal, however, every Kiddush, whether for Shabbos or Yom Tov, should be recited while standing, as stated above in siman 271, subsection 62. See there. Yafeh LaLev, volume 2, subsection 1, writes likewise. He writes that when Pesach falls on a weekday one begins, 'These are the appointed festivals of Havayah,' and so forth. See there. When it falls on Shabbos, one first says, 'The sixth day,' and Vayechulu, then, 'These are the appointed festivals of Havayah,' then Savri Maranan, the blessing over wine, the blessing sanctifying the day, and Shehecheyanu. Before everything, one should recite the chapter of Tehillim beginning, 'A psalm of David: Havayah is my Shepherd,' as stated above in siman 271, subsection 63. See there.
