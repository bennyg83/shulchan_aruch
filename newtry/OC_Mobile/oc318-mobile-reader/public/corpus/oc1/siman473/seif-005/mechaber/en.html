These are the vegetables with which one fulfills the obligation: lettuce, endives, field eryngo, Charchavina , and maror. One fulfills the obligation with their leaves and with their stalks, but not with their roots. However, with the leaves, one does not fulfill the obligation unless they are fresh, whereas with the stalks, one fulfills it whether they are fresh or dry. But not if they are pickled, scalded, or cooked. All of them combine to make up an olive's bulk, which is their required measure. The primary mitzvah is with lettuce, and if one does not have lettuce, one should prefer the first over the next according to the order in which they are listed.' ((meaning types of bitter herbs) Rema: If one does not have any of these vegetables, one should take wormwood or another bitter vegetable (Agur). The charoset should be made thick as a remembrance of the mortar, and afterward one puts in it a little vinegar or red wine as a remembrance of the blood (Tur).
