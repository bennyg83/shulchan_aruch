And one recites Kiddush over it, etc. Before Kiddush, one should say this formula: For the sake of unifying the Holy One, blessed is He, and His Shechinah, through the interwoven Name of Havayah and Adnus, with fear and love, through the corresponding interwoven Name, and with love and fear, through the corresponding reverse interwoven Name, to unite the yud-hei with the vav-hei in complete unity, Havayah, in the name of all the Jewish people and in the name of every nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, and yechidah associated with the roots of our nefesh, ruach, neshamah, chayah, and yechidah, their garments, those close to them, and those joined to them, from the totality of Atzilus, Beriah, Yetzirah, and Asiyah, and from every particular aspect of Atzilus, Beriah, Yetzirah, and Asiyah within every partzuf and sefirah. We now come, with the help of Heaven, to fulfill all the mitzvos observed on this night, the night of Pesach, the first night of the Festival of Matzos. They are: the mitzvah of sanctifying the day over the first cup of wine; the first washing of the hands because of dipping karpas; eating karpas; the positive commandment to recount the Exodus from Egypt, as the verse states, 'You shall tell your child on that day,' and so forth; reciting the blessing of redemption over the second cup of wine; the second washing of the hands for the meal; the blessing over washing the hands; the blessing of HaMotzi and eating matzah; the positive commandment to eat matzah, as the verse states, 'In the evening you shall eat matzos'; eating maror to remember that the Egyptians embittered the lives of our ancestors in Egypt, and dipping it into charoses in remembrance of the mortar; wrapping matzah and maror together according to the opinion of Hillel the Elder; eating the afikoman in remembrance of the korban Pesach; washing the hands with mayim acharonim; the positive commandment of Birkas HaMazon and reciting it over the third cup of wine; reciting Hallel; and drinking the fourth cup of wine. May all these mitzvos rectify their roots in the supernal realm through that Hidden and Concealed One. May the pleasantness, and so forth.
