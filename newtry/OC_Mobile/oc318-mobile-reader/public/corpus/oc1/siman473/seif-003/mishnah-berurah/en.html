(א) Several cups — whether between first and second or after second cup, even during eating; but between third and fourth forbidden, as below siman 479.

(ב) Permission is in his hand — per Mechaber below 474 gloss: our custom first berakha on each cup — permitted to drink between cups only if at berakha time he intended to exempt all he would later drink [or wine was before him, automatically exempt if he did not explicitly decide not to drink]; if no intent to drink between cups and later decided to drink — must bless; forbidden, appears like adding cups since he blessed this cup like the four — as written siman 472 s"k 21 in Mishna Berurah.

(ג) It is fitting to be careful — and such is our custom [Darkei Moshe]:

(ד) Not to drink between the first and second [cup] — to be concerned for the opinion of someone who says (and it is brought in Beit Yosef) that what we permit to drink between the cups is only between cup 2 and cup 3, which is close to the meal or during the meal, for then one does not become intoxicated; but not between the first and second, so that one should not become intoxicated, and as it is forbidden between the 3rd and 4th:
