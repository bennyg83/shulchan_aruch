Magen Avraham seif kaf 8 That Yom Tov does not prepare for its fellow — it is not understood, for in any case on the night of the second Yom Tov too he will not eat it; and if he would eat it on the day of the second Yom Tov — only that generally he will not eat it on the first day of Yom Tov since he needs it for the night of the second Yom Tov; if so, what relevance is there, in the introduction that one does not eat roasted and therefore it is forbidden?
