He has permission to do so. According to the Rama's ruling below in siman 474 that our custom is to recite a separate initial blessing over each cup, unlike the opinion of Rosh, permission to drink between the cups applies only when he had the additional wine in mind, so that he need not recite Borei Pri HaGafen over it. Otherwise, it would appear as though he were adding to the cups, as the Tur writes in the name of Maharam of Rothenburg. Bach reaches the same conclusion even according to Rosh; see there.
So that he will not become intoxicated. This implies that one may drink other beverages that are not intoxicating. However, according to Mordechai, the reason wine is permitted between the first and second cups is that wine stimulates the appetite, so he will eat matzah with appetite. It is therefore possible that other beverages are forbidden between the first and second cups, because they do not stimulate the appetite. Nevertheless, no posek expresses concern about a prohibition in this respect. Wine alone required an explicit permission, so that it should not appear as though he were adding to the cups, and so that one should not say that wine is filling. See above, siman 471.
