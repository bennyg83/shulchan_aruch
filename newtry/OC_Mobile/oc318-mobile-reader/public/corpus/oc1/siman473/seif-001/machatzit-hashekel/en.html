(s"k 1) Sheheheyanu, etc. It should read: even if he blessed, etc., and did not bless on the second Yom Tov, etc.; Kaf HaChayim wrote: but he did not bless on the first Yom Tov night and blessed on the second night — he fulfills necessarily: if the first Yom Tov was weekday he certainly need not bless sheheheyanu except the second night; and if the first was Yom Tov, he completed with what he blessed on the second night — since the first was Yom Tov, the second weekday, he need not bless sheheheyanu on the second night for the second night; and the berakha he blessed on the second night ascends to complete the first day.
