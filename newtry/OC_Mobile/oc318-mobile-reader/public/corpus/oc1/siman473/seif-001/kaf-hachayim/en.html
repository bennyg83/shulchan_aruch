(1) [Seif 1] They pour for him the first cup, etc. And the cups require rinsing and washing, for they are cups of berakha — Drashot Maharil Seder Ha'agadah; Rokeach siman 283; and so Mahariv in the name of Maharai and Beit Yosef Darchei Moshe in siman 486 s"k 2; and Chayei Adam kelal 130 s"k 10 wrote: if they are clean, rinsing and washing between cups are not needed, only rinsing the first cup suffices — end of his words; nevertheless see above siman 183 seif 1 and our words there s"k 4, that per Zohar and tikkunim a Birkat HaMazon cup requires washing and rinsing even if clean — see there; nevertheless it is good to have a vessel full of water near the table to rinse and wash cups between cup and cup. Sefer HaMitzvot; Tosafot Chayim on Chayei Adam there s"k 34; and see further below s"k 142 and siman 479 s"k 1.
