(א) And he makes kiddush on it — before kiddush intend to fulfill kiddush and four cups, kiddush cup being one of four; some say "I am ready to sanctify and fulfill four cups" — careful not to say this after kiddush since he already blessed borei peri hagafen on the cup, only before kiddush; before Hagadah intend or say to fulfill telling the Exodus [Chayei Adam]. Sheheheyanu — before drinking; if not before drinking, bless whenever he remembers day one, even in the market, time is rolling; if remembered on second night after sanctifying the day, exempt with sheheheyanu on the cup after kiddush; if forgot sheheheyanu on second night's kiddush, even if already blessed first night, obligated whenever remembers through the festival until end of last diaspora Yom Tov; likewise other Yom Tov until festival end.

(ב) On Shabbat — permitted to say Hagadah from a book even alone; we are not concerned he may tilt, as explained above siman 475:9 — see there.

(ג) He says yaknehaz — meaning: first wine berakha and kiddush and ner, i.e., borei me'orei ha-esh and havdalah and sheheheyanu; do not bless on spices every Motza'ei Shabbat to Yom Tov; say havdalah seated like kiddush.

(ד) He completes, etc. — to make havdalah immediately when remembered he cannot, for havdalah requires a cup and the one who blesses must taste; during Hagadah forbidden to interrupt with drinking; from the Mechaber's wording "until he began Hagadah" implies if remembered before Hagadah he need not wait but immediately pours a cup and says havdalah berakha and drinks — however this is only per the Mechaber who does not bless on each cup; per Rama 474 gloss and our custom to bless each cup, he must bless borei peri hagafen — then it appears like adding cups, forbidden, as siman 472:7; therefore possibly even if remembered before Hagadah there is no remedy until completing Hagadah, unless at time of first cup blessing he will drink between cups as siman 472:7 — see Bi'ur Halakha. If forgot Hagadah at night — no compensation, for "because of this" when matzah and maror before you; if forgot kiddush at night — law as siman 471:8.

(ה) And afterward makes havdalah — bless borei peri hagafen on second cup and also ner and Yom Tov havdalah and drink; if remembered mid-meal he did not make havdalah, must interrupt immediately and make havdalah, not borei peri hagafen, for second cup drunk before eating exempts unless he then had no intent to drink during eating — then also borei peri hagafen; if remembered during Birkat HaMazon — Birkat HaMazon and havdalah on one cup; if not until after cup of Birkat HaMazon — wait until after Hallel and Hagadah, then havdalah on fourth cup; if not until after fourth — havdalah on fifth cup and must bless borei peri hagafen since he already removed his mind from drinking [later authorities].

(ו) And one should not wash, etc. — meaning: even those who all year wash before kiddush for the meal — for them kiddush is not interruption between washing and meal as explained siman 471 — nevertheless on Pesach night, where they interrupt much after kiddush in Hagadah and lose attention to guarding hands, all agree not to do so; even to wash so as not to need wash again after kiddush for karpas is also not proper per later authorities.

(ז) He washes a little — wrote "a little" to heed the reason Darchei Moshe brought: do not wash before kiddush lest it appear kiddush requires washing — therefore not a full washing.

(ח) But do not bless, etc. — even if full washing, careful not to bless al netilat yadayim, for this washing does not count for eating — berakha in vain.

(ט) Only another pours for him — if possible.
