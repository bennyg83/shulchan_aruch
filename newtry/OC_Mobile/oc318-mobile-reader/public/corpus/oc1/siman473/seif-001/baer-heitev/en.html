(א) Shehecheyanu. And if he did not say it on day one, he blesses on day two or on one of the other days when he remembers; and if he has no cup of wine at the time of Kiddush he blesses shehecheyanu without a cup, and it appears to me that even if he blessed initially and did not bless on the second day of Yom Tov of the diaspora he blesses all seven — Magen Avraham. And Kaf HaChayyim wrote it appears the same applies to Shavuot, even though it is only two days, nevertheless it has make-up all seven. And further it appears to me that if he forgot to say shehecheyanu on the first night and on the second night said shehecheyanu, he need not say shehecheyanu again based on the first night to complete it — for if the first day is holy, then he completed shehecheyanu on the second day, and if the second day is holy, behold he said shehecheyanu — Kaf HaChayyim, see there. And if he forgot to say Kiddush at night, his law is as above siman 471 seif 8, and so too Eliyah Rabbah seif 485. And if he forgot to say the Haggadah he has no make-up at all, as it is written "because of this" at the hour when matzah and maror are placed before you, and so too Magen Avraham below siman 485 see there. And they do not say "who performed miracles for us" because he is destined to say it in the Haggadah — Tur and Kaf HaChayyim:

(ב) On Shabbat. It is permitted to say the Haggadah from a book even though he is alone, and we are not concerned that perhaps he will lean, as written siman (479) [475] seif 9. Kaf HaChayyim:

(ג) He makes havdalah. And if he remembered in the middle of the meal that he did not make havdalah, he makes havdalah in the middle of his meal over the cup; and if his intention was to drink wine during the meal he does not bless borei peri hagafen, and if it was not his intention he must bless, and he should not wait to make havdalah until after the meal, for then it would be like adding to the cups; and it is better that he say havdalah and Hallel on one cup, for one cannot have two sanctifications on one cup — only Kiddush and havdalah are permitted, for both are one matter; and if he forgot to make havdalah until after birkat hamazon he says Hallel and havdalah on one cup, for one cannot [do so] on another matter. Magen Avraham, and see above siman 296:
