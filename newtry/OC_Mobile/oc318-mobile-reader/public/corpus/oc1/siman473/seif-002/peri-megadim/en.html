(א) And he does not Taz: and for the first three cups b'dieved it suffices with the majority of a revi'it that he drinks his full mouthfuls; and a cup of revi'it requires that he drink a full revi'it in order to bless the blessing acharonah — see siman 190 in Taz 3 and 486 1:

(ב) See in Tur what the Rosh wrote: even if he diverted his attention from drinking, he drinks between the first two cups and it does not appear as adding to the cups — even a berachah is difficult from what the Rosh wrote. In Pesachim 108a between 3 and 4 it appears as adding, for according to him there is no distinction between berachah or not — Magen Avraham 472 sign 4; and some write that it is forbidden to drink between 3 and 4 and also after 4 from custom — see siman 481; it would be for the Rosh to omit this reason, which is the main one. And Bach explained that even for the Rosh, whatever he says in a berachah is adding, and without berachah and without Haggadah it is not adding — if so, it requires study:
