(10) He drinks while reclining. And if he forgot and did not recline, see in siman 472, se'if 7, and in the Mishnah Berurah, s.k. 21.
(11) And he does not recite a blessing after it. "Al HaGefen," etc., even if he drank a revi'is, for he relies on what he will recite in Birkas HaMazon at the end; and further, behold he will recite an after-blessing at the end of the four cups; and in siman 474 it will be clarified well.
