(א) (s"k 2) And afterward, etc. Since there is a dispute in siman 499 whether one who makes havdalah during the meal must recite borei peri hagafen on the havdalah cup.

(ב) And if he did not have in mind to drink wine during the meal — in such a case all agree he must recite on every wine he drinks during the meal, all the more so the havdalah cup.

(ג) And one should not say havdalah and Hallel on one cup. To say Birkat HaMazon and havdalah on one cup is explicit in the Gemara daf 102 — forbidden because two sanctifications on one cup appear as if the mitzvot are burdens on it; Magen Avraham added the same for Hallel and Hagadah — why on one cup?

(ד) And if he forgot, etc. he says Hallel and havdalah on one cup; and likewise if he remembered during Birkat HaMazon that he did not make havdalah, he recites Birkat HaMazon and havdalah on one — what is the difference? Rather since Magen Avraham deals with whether to say Hallel and havdalah on one cup, he also cites here Hallel and havdalah.
