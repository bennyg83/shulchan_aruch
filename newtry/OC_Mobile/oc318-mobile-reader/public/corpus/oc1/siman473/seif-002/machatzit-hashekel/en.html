(s.k. 2) And afterwards, etc. Since there is a dispute in siman 299 whether, when he makes Havdalah during the meal, he must recite Borei Pri HaGafen over the cup of Havdalah.
And if his intention was not to drink wine during the meal, for in such a case all agree that he must recite a blessing over every wine that he drinks during the meal, all the more so the cup of Havdalah.
And one should not say that he say Havdalah and Hallel over one cup. For to say Birkas HaMazon and Havdalah over one cup is clarified explicitly in the Gemara, folio 102, that it is forbidden, because one cannot [place] two sanctities on one cup, for it appears that the mitzvos are upon him like a burden; and the Magen Avraham added that the same applies to Hallel and Havdalah: one should not say [them], for this reason, over one cup.
And if he forgot, etc. He says Hallel and Havdalah over one cup. And the same applies if he remembered during Birkas HaMazon that he did not make Havdalah: he recites Birkas HaMazon and Havdalah over one cup; for what difference is there? Only, since the Magen Avraham is discussing the matter of saying Hallel and Havdalah over one cup, he also mentioned here Hallel and Havdalah.
