[6] [Levush] The seven havdalot, etc. In Avudraham he explained: between holy and profane, light and darkness, Israel and nations — behold three; "between the seventh day and six days of work" is not counted but since we say it generally we say it here; between sanctity of Shabbat and sanctity of Yom Tov you distinguished — this is ochel nefesh permitted on Yom Tov, behold four; "and the seventh day from six days of work you sanctified" — this is the last Yom Tov of the festival separated from the six intermediate days — behold five; "and You distinguished Your people," etc. — are two distinctions between kohanim and levi'im and levi'im and Israelites; difficulty: where are the six intermediate days — strained to reconcile.
