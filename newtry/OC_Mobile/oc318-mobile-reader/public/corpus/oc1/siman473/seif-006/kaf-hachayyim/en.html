6) There. And one recites Shehecheyanu, etc. This exempts the matzah, over which Shehecheyanu is not separately recited. Rabbi Yosef Molcho in a manuscript responsum; Birkei Yosef, subsection 1. Therefore, when reciting Shehecheyanu during Kiddush, one should intend to include the mitzvah of eating matzah. See below, siman 643, se'if 1.
