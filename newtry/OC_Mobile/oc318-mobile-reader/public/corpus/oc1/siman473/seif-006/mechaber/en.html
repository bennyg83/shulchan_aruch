He washes his hands for the first dipping and does not recite a blessing over the washing. He takes less than a kezayit of karpas, dips it in vinegar, recites Borei Pri HaAdamah, and eats it, without reciting an after-blessing. He then takes the middle matzah and breaks it into 2. He gives half to 1 of those reclining at the table to keep for the afikoman, and they place it under the cloth. He places the other half between the 2 whole matzot. He lifts the plate containing the matzot and recites Ha Lachma Anya until Mah Nishtanah. {Rama: They should say it in a language understood by the women and children, or explain the matter to them. R' Yitzchak of London likewise recited the entire Haggadah in the vernacular so that the women and children would understand. [Kol Bo and Mahariv]} He then instructs that the matzot be removed from the table and placed at the far end of the table, as though they had already eaten, so that the children will see and ask.
