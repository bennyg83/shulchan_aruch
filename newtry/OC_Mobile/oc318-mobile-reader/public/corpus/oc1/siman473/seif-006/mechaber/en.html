Next, they take the middle matzah and break it into two pieces. One half is given to one of the participants to guard for the Afikoman, and it is placed under a cloth. The other half is placed between the two whole matzot. Then, the plate containing the matzot is lifted, and they recite Ha Lachma Anya ("This is the bread of affliction") until Mah Nishtanah ("Why is this night different?"). Afterward, one should command that the matzot be removed from the table and placed at the end of the table' as if the meal has already been eaten, so that the children will notice and ask questions.Gloss: The recitation should be done in a language that the women and children understand, or its meaning should be explained to them. Rabbi Yitzchak of London would recite the entire Haggadah in the vernacular so that the women and children could understand.
