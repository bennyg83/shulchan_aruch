(א) Less than a kezayit — from here it implies that something dipped in liquid requires washing even for less than a kezayit, even for plain bread; Mechaber wrote in siman 158 seif 3 in the name of some that washing is not required for less than a kezayit, and this is not as we wrote there in Mishna Berurah sk 20; and I already noted this in the second printing in the margins; but now I found a Tanna who supports my words — namely Rashbatz in Sefer Yavin Shemuah 4:35, who wrote regarding karpas that according to Rambam we require a kezayit, and he wrote it is reasonable that since washing is required, certainly a kezayit is required, like eating plain bread — it is reasonable he is not obligated in washing, for certainly they did not decree in such a case because of concern for terumah; and likewise for fruits in something dipped in liquid; and Mateh Yehudah brought it exactly as our words; and the law requires further study:

(ב) And he does not bless afterward — see in Mishna Berurah; and the Gra wrote in his beur that all this is according to Mechaber's view in siman 474 that he blesses only one berachah on the first two cups, and because Hallel and the Haggadah are not an interruption, therefore he does not bless birkat hamazon — for he relies on the berachah of karpas to eat maror without a berachah; but per the gloss and our custom that he blesses each cup and cup, one cannot rely on the berachah of the first cup because he interrupted with Hallel and the Haggadah; and if so one cannot say we rely in eating maror on the berachah of karpas, since he interrupted with the Haggadah and Hallel (only maror is exempt from a berachah because it is like things that come within the meal, which are dismissed by the berachah of hamotzi); and if so he is obligated to bless birkat hamazon after eating karpas, since it is not related to the meal that comes afterward — this is the essence of the Gra's words. And in truth Rav Magen Avraham was careful about this and wrote in siman 474 that each cup requires a berachah according to all, even for one who holds the Haggadah and Hallel are not an interruption, and because each mitzvah is separate therefore he must bless on each (and as Rif wrote at the beginning of his words in the name of the rabbis); and per Magen Avraham's words the Gra's words are rejected, and as Magen Avraham wrote, many Acharonim wrote above in siman 278 seif 6 except Taz — see there; and one may say this reasoning applies here too, that karpas and maror are two mitzvot and therefore one does not exempt the other; and the law requires further study:
