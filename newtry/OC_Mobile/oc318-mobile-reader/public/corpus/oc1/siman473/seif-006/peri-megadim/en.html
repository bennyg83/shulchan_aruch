He washes Taz: and see siman 158 seif 2 and Eliyah Rabbah there, and he explains well sign 11 in the name of Mateh Yosef who was very strict. But one does not bless al netilat yadayim on something whose dipping is in liquid, even on the night of Pesach — and see Levush here and Eliyah Rabbah in 158. And know that there are three reasons: (1) on account of residue of terumah, and this is the opinion of Rashi z"l — even though for fruits 43, nevertheless liquids are prone [to contract impurity] and become primary; they decreed more stringently; and therefore even a moist liquid also requires washing. And Tosafot Pesachim 116a s.v. kol: the reason is that it is forbidden to defile one's body; and therefore specifically when there is in the liquids a revi'it, the measure of defiling the body — Eshel Avraham, Taz chapter 4 halachah 2; and possibly the Sages did not distinguish. And Beit Yosef Orach Chaim 164 in the name of R' Yehudah: the reason is on account of cleanliness — Taz there sign 6; and in Sefer Rosh Yosef in Chullin we wrote: one who dips a radish in salt and it becomes water and dissolves — one can say its dipping is in liquid — Magen Avraham 462:7 and Kaf HaChayim seif 12 and Rambam z"l chapter 8 halachah 49. And see Darkei Moshe in 158: plum juice, since it changed, they bless Borei peri ha'eitz — it is not "something whose dipping is in liquid," and it requires study whether we say that for impurity it is a liquid, and likewise honey-water and beer of this kind; and that which Tosafot wrote that it is forbidden to defile one's body — one can see in Chullin 44 that it appears only a kohen is warned, and rabbinically from the Torah only for impurity of the dead, but an Israelite is not warned — see Chochmas Adam in four views. And Magen Avraham 489:4 — and it is simple that a kohen is warned rabbinically in the time of the Temple not to defile his body; nevertheless it is a mixture that is visible to Israelites. And behold on the night of Pesach, which implies in Levush that they bless al netilat yadayim for dipping in liquid — one can say that on the festival all Israelites are warned to purify themselves; and it is fine to say they are careful specifically on Yom Tov, and the same on every Yom Tov.
