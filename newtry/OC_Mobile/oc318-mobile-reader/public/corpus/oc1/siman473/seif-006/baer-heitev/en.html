(א) He washes. For anything whose dipping is in liquid requires washing; and Taz wrote: from here is clear proof against those who are not careful all year to wash hands for things dipped in liquid — how is this night different from all nights of the year? And if he comes to purify himself more on this night, at least he should be careful also on days of repentance; but here there is only lack of care and they do not scrutinize to equalize their measures — end quote. And Eliyah Rabbah in siman 85 wrote the world is accustomed not to wash and they have on what to rely, that Tosafot hold so, and so Magen Avraham siman 158 see there; and that they wash on this night is like one of the other changes made this night so that they will ask.
