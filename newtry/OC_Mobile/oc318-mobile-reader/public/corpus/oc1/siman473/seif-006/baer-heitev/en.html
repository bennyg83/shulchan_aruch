He washes. For anything whose dipping is in liquid requires netilas yadayim; and the Taz wrote: and from here there is open rebuke to those who are not careful all year to wash their hands for things dipped in liquid: what is different about this night from all the days of the year? And if he comes to purify himself more on this night, at the very least he ought to be careful also during the Days of Repentance; rather, there is here only a lack of care, and they are not precise to make their measures equal; end quote. And Lechem Chamudos in chapter 25 wrote that the world customarily does not wash, and they have upon what to rely, for Tosafos hold so; and so wrote the Magen Avraham in siman 158; see there. And that they wash on this night is like one of the other changes they make on this night so that [the children] will ask (Chok Yaakov).
Less. For if he eats a kezayit there will be a doubt whether he should recite an after-blessing (Tur); therefore if it happened that he ate a kezayit, he should not recite an after-blessing (Taz and Magen Avraham; Chok Yaakov).
For the afikoman. And the larger portion he should hide (Acharonim). Also it is proper to wrap it in a cloth and place it between the pillow and the cushion; and when he wishes to eat, he should take it out as it was inside the cloth and sling it behind him and walk about four amos and say: thus did our fathers walk with their kneading-troughs bound up in their garments (Maharshal). And see Chok Yaakov, siman 472, s.k. 2, for the reason for the custom that the children take the afikoman.
The plate. With everything that is upon it, and one need not remove the cooked dishes (Tur).
Ha lachma. And some say "Ha kolachma"; see the Magen Avraham. And Maharshal wrote in siman 88 to say it in a loud voice.
To remove them. And nowadays they are not accustomed to remove them, because there is no recognition for the children in this, for they know that these things were not placed on the table for the need of eating (Magen Avraham).
And they will ask. And he should tell them that they are not permitted to eat until they tell of the Exodus from Egypt; and therefore one should not place any matzah on the table until after the Seder (Maharil).
