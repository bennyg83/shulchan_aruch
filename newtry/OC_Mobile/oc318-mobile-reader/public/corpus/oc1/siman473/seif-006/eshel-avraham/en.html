That the women understand. In truth women are not obligated in saying the Haggadah, for it is a positive time-bound commandment; even though they were included in that miracle, that reason does not apply to a commandment according to the Torah, and it would be no different from sukkah, even though they too were covered by the Cloud. Rather, our obligation is to tell them of the miracles and wonders of Hashem; see the Taz in Yoreh De'ah, siman 253.
