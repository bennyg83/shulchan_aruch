In truth women are not obligated in reciting the Hagadah, which is a rabbinic mitzvah that women were also in that miracle — that reason does not apply to a Torah mitzvah; it would apply only to sukkah, though they were also covered by the cloud. Rather our obligation is to tell them Hashem's miracles and wonders; see Tur in Yoreh De'ah siman 253.
