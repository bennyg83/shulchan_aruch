(א) Less than a kezayit. For if he eats a kezayit there is doubt whether he recites a concluding berakha (Tur); therefore if he happened to eat a kezayit he does not recite a concluding berakha.

(ב) And he recites borei peri ha-adamah. Before dipping so it precede the act; the same for maror (Maharil); difficulty — the berakha refers only to eating, borei peri ha-adamah, and so for maror — requires study.

(ג) And he breaks it in two. For it requires lechem oni — a poor person's way is with a piece.

(ד) And he gives half. Meaning: the larger half (Maharil, Bach).

(ה) Under the cloth. Some place it on their shoulders in remembrance of the Exodus.

(ו) And he lifts the plate. And need not remove the cooked dishes (Tur).

(ז) Ha Lachma Anya. Biblical language, as written "so they will see the bread that I fed them," etc.; do not corrupt the formula to k'Ha Lachma or Ha Kolachma (Mahariv, Darchei Moshe, Mateh Moshe) — some do not want Ha Lachma because it is not the very bread Israel ate; proof from scripture "the bread that I fed" — yet that bread they already ate, only a sample of bread; here we bake new matzot — not fit to say Ha Lachma; therefore one who says Ha Kolachma or k'Ha Lachma did not lose.

(ח) And he commands to remove them. Now they are not accustomed, for children lack recognition — they know these were not placed to eat; in Gemara times they were accustomed to clear the table.

(ט) And they will ask. Tell them they may not eat until the Exodus is told; therefore no matzah on the table until after the seder (Maharil).
