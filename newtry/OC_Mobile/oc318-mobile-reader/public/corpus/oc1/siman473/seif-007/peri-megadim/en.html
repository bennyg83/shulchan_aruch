And not Taz. And we already wrote in the previous sign that one can say on Yom Tov a person is obligated to purify himself — they are more careful. That which is written in Teshuvat She'elah to a girl — according to Rashi and Rashbam z"l, and Eliyah Rabbah explained: (1) the way of freedom — they dip before the meal to extend appetite for food. (2) They need to say the Haggadah and tell of the Exodus from Egypt; therefore they dip before eating without lingering, likewise without eating. (3) The children will ask about the second dipping: why — did they not already dip in the first? And the answer: dipping 1 is as all year, and dipping 2 is on account of the mitzvah of maror. And Taz explained that the question is on dipping 1 and the answer is to instruct about dipping 2; just as the first is not for the need of the meal, so the second only on account of the mitzvah of maror — a rabbinic mitzvah, for dipping is certainly only d'rabbanan, and maror too in our time — Magen Avraham and Eliyah Rabbah there will be explained.
