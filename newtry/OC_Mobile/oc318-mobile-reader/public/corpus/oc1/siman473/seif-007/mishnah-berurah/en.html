(67) Immediately. That is to say, he should not wait in mixing the cup until he reaches "Therefore," when he must hold the cup in his hand; rather, immediately upon removing the plate from the table he mixes.
(68) The second cup. And it does not need rinsing and washing, for they already rinsed it for Kiddush.
(69) So that they ask, etc. That is to say, and by this he will be aroused to ask the remaining questions and astonishments that he sees on that night.
(70) And when the son or the wife asks. And the same applies if one Torah scholar asks his fellow: the one asked need not say "Mah Nishtanah."
(71) And he reads the entire Haggadah. And one should not say it while reclining, but only with awe and fear (Shelah). When he says "and we shall say before Him a song," one must say "ve-namar" with a segol vocalization, "ve-namar," which is past-tense language, for it refers to the redemption from Egypt (Acharonim).
(72) And likewise when he reaches "This maror." But when he reaches "the Pesach that our fathers," etc., he should not hold in his hand the meat that is a remembrance of the Pesach, for it is like one raising sacred food outside, for it appears from this that he consecrated it for the Pesach. The Mordechai wrote that one must say "and we shall eat there from the zevachim and from the Pesachim," for behold they eat first from the Chagigah, which is the zevach, but the Pesach is eaten when satiated.
(73) And when he reaches "Therefore," he raises, etc. And in the Shelah he wrote that also when he says the passage "And it is this that has stood for our fathers and for us" until "the Holy One, Blessed be He, rescues us from their hand," he should hold the cup in his hand, and then the bread will be covered when he holds the cup in his hand.
(74) With his finger. They customarily throw [the drops] with the finger, on account of "it is the finger of God"; not as is written in Hagahos Minhagim to throw with the small pinky.
(75) Sixteen times. Corresponding to the letters Yud-Vav of the Name of the Holy One, Blessed be He, Who struck Pharaoh.
(76) And the bread should be uncovered, etc. For therefore matzah is called bread of poverty (lechem oni), for they answer (onin) words over it.
(77) That he holds the cup in his hand. In order to say song over the wine.
(78) And then he should cover the bread. So that it not see its shame, as is stated in siman 271, se'if 9.
