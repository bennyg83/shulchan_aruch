(א) Why is this night different. One should not say "we are obligated to dip," etc., but rather "we dip," for it is not an obligation but only for recognition — Tur. When he says "and it is this that stood for our fathers," etc., until "the Holy One, blessed be He, saves us from them," he should hold the cup in his hand. And when he says "and we will say before Him song," it must be v'ne'emar with segol vowels (under the alef), which is past tense, referring to the redemption from Egypt that has passed. Bach, Rashal, Shelah:

(ב) For maror. But when he reaches "the Pesach that our fathers," etc., he should not hold it in his hand, for it is like raising consecrated things outside [the Temple], but nevertheless he should look at it. Maharil siman 193:

(ג) With his finger. Written in Hagahot Minhagim that one should sprinkle with the small finger, namely the pinky; and Magen Avraham wrote one should sprinkle with the ring finger, which is next to the small, for with the ring finger the Holy One, blessed be He, struck Egypt. And in Darkei Moshe he wrote one should take specifically with the index finger in remembrance of what is said "it is the finger of the Almighty," and such is the essence. Kaf HaChayyim:

(ד) In his hand. In the text of the berachah "who redeemed us" Maharil wrote: when Pesach falls on Shabbat one should reverse and say first "from the Pesachim" and afterward "from the sacrifices," for the chagigah does not override Shabbat, and on the second night the opposite — see there. And in Pesach Me'ubin siman 268 he wrote one should say everything in one text "from the sacrifices and from the Pesachim," and Acharonim agreed, such as Maharil, and so Bach. In saying Hallel one should not sit reclining but with awe and fear — Shelah. Levush wrote they are not accustomed to bless on Hallel since they divide half before the meal and half after. A blind person is obligated in reading the Haggadah, and when he reaches "this matzah" and "this maror" he does not say "this" but says "the matzah we eat," etc. — Kaf HaChayyim sk 39:
