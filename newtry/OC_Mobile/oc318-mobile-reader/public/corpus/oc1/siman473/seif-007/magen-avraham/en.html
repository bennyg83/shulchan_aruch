They mix, etc. He should not say "we are obligated to dip," etc., for it is only a recognition for the children; rather he should say "we dip" (Tur). When he says "And it is this that has stood for our fathers," etc., he should hold the cup in his hand and cover the bread until "and rescued us from their hand" (Shelah). When he says "Pesach," he should not raise the meat. One should say "and we shall say before Him a new song, Halleluyah," for it refers to the redemption from Egypt that already passed; and also some say that Moshe and Israel said Hallel, as is stated in the chapter "Arvei Pesachim" (Bach; Maharshal; Shelah).
With his finger. They customarily throw [the drops] with his finger, on account of "it is the finger of God"; not as Hagahos Maimoniyos wrote to throw with the small pinky; end quote (Darkei Moshe). And in Yalkut on Parashas Va'era he brings Pirkei DeRabbi Eliezer, that with the knuckle the Holy One, Blessed be He, struck Egypt, and upon this it is said "it is the finger of God"; and if so one should throw with the knuckle.
