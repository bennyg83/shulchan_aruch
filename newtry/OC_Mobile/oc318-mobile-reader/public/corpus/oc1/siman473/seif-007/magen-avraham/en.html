(א) They pour, etc. Do not say "we are obligated to dip," etc. — it is only recognition for children; say "we dip" (Tur); when saying "and it stood for our fathers," etc., hold the cup and cover bread until "Heaven saves us from them" (Shlah); when saying Pesach do not raise the meat; read venomar lefanav shirah chadashah haleluyah — on past redemption; some say Hallel was said by Moshe and Israel as in commentary Arvei Pesachim (Bach, Maharshal, Shlah).

(ב) With his finger. Custom to sprinkle with the finger — see there "it is the finger of Heaven" — unlike Hagahot Maimoniot to sprinkle with the small pinky — end Darchei Moshe; Yalkut parashat Va'era brings Perkei Rabi Eliezer: with the fist He struck Egypt — on this "finger of Heaven"; therefore sprinkle with the fist.

(ג) Sixteen times. Corresponding to the sword of the Holy One, blessed be He, called yud-heh-vav-heh (Darchei Moshe).

(ד) He covers the bread. Lest he see his nakedness, as siman 471:9; when Pesach is first day of Yom Tov say from the Pesach offerings and from the sacrifices — they did not offer the festival offering, for festival offering does not override Shabbat (Bach, Mahari"v) — see Tur.
