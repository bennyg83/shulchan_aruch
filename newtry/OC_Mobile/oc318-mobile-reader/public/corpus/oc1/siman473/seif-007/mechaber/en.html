One immediately pours him a second cup so that the children will ask, "Why do we drink a second cup before the meal?" If the son lacks the wisdom to ask, his father teaches him. If he has no son, his wife asks him. If not, he asks himself. Even Torah scholars ask one another, "How is this night different?" etc. When one begins Avadim, that is, l'Pharaoh, he returns the Seder plate with the matzos before him and recites the entire Haggadah. When he reaches "this matzah," he must lift it to show it to those reclining, so that the mitzvah becomes beloved to them. Similarly, when he reaches "this bitter herb," and when he reaches L'fichach, everyone lifts their cup in hand until the conclusion of Ga'al Yisrael. The bread should be uncovered while reciting the Haggadah until L'fichach, at which point one holds the cup in hand and then covers the bread.
