With the first cup, one should intend that the cup itself has the numerical value of Elokim, but one should contemplate its letter substitution, the Name Akdatam. The letters lamed, hei, and yud of Elokim are exchanged for the preceding letters, kaf, dalet, and tes, while alef and mem are not exchanged. The five fingers of one's right hand correspond to the five letters of Akdatam. The wine in the cup also alludes to the Name Akdatam, because wine has the numerical value seventy. Together with the five fingers holding it, the total is seventy-five, equal to Akdatam when its collective unit is included. When reciting Havayah in the blessing of Kiddush, one should intend the Name of seventy-two, corresponding to chochmah.
4) The second cup corresponds to binah within binah. One should intend the Name Akda'am as divided into two groups, alef-mem and gimel-lamed, because kaf-dalet-ayin has the numerical value of gimel-lamed. When reciting Havayah in the blessing Asher Ge'alanu, one should intend the Name of sixty-three, corresponding to binah.
4) The final two cups correspond to chesed and gevurah within daas. Therefore, one should not drink between the third and fourth cups, so as not to separate them. The third cup corresponds to the chasadim within daas. One should intend the Name Akda'am in its numerical calculation, which is seventy-four, and with the collective unit, seventy-five. This is three times the unification Yabok, meaning three combinations of Havayah and Elokim, each having the numerical value Yabok. One should also intend the Name of forty-five, corresponding to the chasadim within daas. The final cup corresponds to the gevuros within daas. One should intend the Name Akda'am in its numerical calculation, which is seventy-four, and the Name of fifty-two, corresponding to the gevuros within daas. Nevertheless, one should combine the two sets of intentions together for the first cup and then repeat both for the second cup, as explained in Sha'ar HaKavanos and Eitz Chaim, chapters 2 and 3.
