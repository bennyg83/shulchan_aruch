Magen Avraham seif kaf 14 Everything standing to be uprooted is considered as uprooted — it does not suffice, for even now when it is fully cooked it is considered uprooted; nevertheless the gentile did not now acquire it and did not become a robber on it, as is stated in Bava Kamma 117b, brought in Magen Avraham below siman 637; and if so, when the Jew uproots he is the robber and there is no change of ownership:
