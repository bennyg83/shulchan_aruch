(א) There. And he makes kiddush on it. See above siman 472 s"k 51 that we wrote the first cup corresponds to chochmah of binah — see there; on the first cup intend that the cup itself is gematria Elohim; nevertheless intend its exchanged aspect (meaning the letters of Elohim exchange with preceding letters per some, and some say they do not exchange) — the name AKDaT"M; the five fingers of the right hand correspond to the five letters AKDaT"M; wine in the cup also hints AKDaT"M, for wine is gematria 70, and with the five fingers holding it behold 75, count of AKDaT"M with the kollel; in the havayah of the kiddush berakha intend the name 72, aspect of chochmah.

(ב) And the second cup is aspect of binah of binah; intend in it the name AKDa"M divided into two mems gimel lamed (meaning AKDa"M is gematria gimel lamed); in the havayah of Asher Ga'alanu intend the name 63, aspect of binah.

(ג) And the two final cups are in the aspects of chesed and gevurah of da'at; therefore between the third and fourth one should not drink, so as not to separate them. The first cup is chesed of da'at — intend AKDa"M in its calculation, gematria 74, with kollel 75, which is also yud beit kuf (meaning threefold havayah Elohim, each gematria yud beit kuf); and the name 45, aspect of chesed of da'at. The last cup is gevurot of da'at — intend AKDa"M in its calculation gematria 74 and the name 52, aspect of gevurot of da'at. You should join both intentions in the first cup, then return to intend both in the second cup, as explained in Sha'ar HaKavanot and Pri Etz Chayim chapters 2–3 — see there.
