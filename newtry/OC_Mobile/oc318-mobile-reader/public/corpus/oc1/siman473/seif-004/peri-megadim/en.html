They are accustomed Taz: meat to roast on coals as a remembrance of the Pesach on a pomegranate spit; and because of the trouble they roast on coals, for also the Pesach if roasted on coals is fine — Eshel Avraham 5:280 chapter 8 halachah 10, Magen Avraham 8. And that which Taz wrote that one should not eat the meat on the first night — Pri Chadash agreed; and even if the meat is cooked, as long as it is a remembrance of the Pesach one does not eat on the first night except tomorrow if he wishes; and the egg is permitted to eat at night, for not only on account of remembrance of the festival offering do they cook an egg — there is also a reason of remembrance of mourning; and thereby he gave a reason for what the Mechaber wrote (Tur Rama): meat they roast and the egg they cook, because they are accustomed to eat the egg at night; if so, also fish and egg have no prohibition of roasted, as siman 476 seif 6; nevertheless they instituted on erev Yom Tov to cook the egg as well. And one can say for us: the festival offering of the 14th is optional and eaten cooked like all peace-offerings — Eshel Avraham 5:280 chapter 8 halachah 12 and Pesachim 70a; if so, the custom to cook the egg is a remembrance of the festival offering, etc. However Mishna Berurah wrote that they are accustomed to roast also the egg and one is permitted to eat the egg at night even roasted; but the meat, even cooked, they do not eat at night except by day tomorrow — see Eliyah Rabbah 86 seif 5: if they cooked a non-kosher egg with kosher eggs, see there; and wheat among them — 51 in Pesach forbids even m'b'eod yom until night if there are not sixty against the wheat, and if they are not peeled they are forbidden — see there Taz 10 and Shach 15:
