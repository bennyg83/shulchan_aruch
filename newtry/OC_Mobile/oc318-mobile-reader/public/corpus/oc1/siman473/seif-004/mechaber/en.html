A plate is brought before the head of the household containing 3 matzot, maror, charoset, karpas or another vegetable, vinegar or salt water, and 2 cooked foods, 1 as a remembrance of the Pesach offering and 1 as a remembrance of the Chagigah offering. The custom is to use meat and an egg. {Rama: He should arrange the plate before himself so that he will not have to pass over any mitzvah. Thus, the karpas should be above everything, with the vinegar next to it and closer to him than the matzah; the matzot should be closer than the maror and charoset; and these should be closer to him than the meat and egg. [Maharil]} The custom is that the meat should be a foreleg, and that it should be roasted over coals. The egg should be cooked; the same applies if it is roasted, and that is the custom in our city.
