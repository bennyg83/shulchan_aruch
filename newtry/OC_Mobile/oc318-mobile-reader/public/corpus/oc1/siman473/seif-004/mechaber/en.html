They bring before the head of the household a plate that contains three matzot, maror, charoset, karpas, as well as two cooked dishes—one as a remembrance of the Paschal offering and one as a remembrance of the festival sacrifice. The custom is to use meat and an egg. The custom is for the meat to be from the forearm (zeroa), and it is customary for the meat to be roasted over coals, while the egg should be cooked((and vinegar or salt water), Rema: The plate should be arranged before him in a way that he does not need to bypass any mitzvahs. That is, the karpas should be placed on top of everything, with the vinegar next to it, closer than the matzah. The matzot should be placed before the maror and the charoset, which in turn should be placed closer to him than the meat and the egg. (Maharil) (or roasted, as is the practice in our city).) ושני תבשילין אחד זכר לפסח וא' זכר לחגיג' ונהגו בבשר וביצה: הגה ויסדר הקערה לפניו בענין שאינו צריך לעבור על המצות דהיינו הכרפס יהא למעלה מן הכל והחומץ סמוך לו יותר מן המצה והמצות מן המרור והחרוסת והם יהיו יותר קרובים אליו מן הבשר והביצה.
