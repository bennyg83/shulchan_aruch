Three. As is written in Berachos 39b and Shabbos 116b: all agree regarding Pesach, etc.; and in the chapter "Arvei Pesachim," what is a poor man's way with a broken piece, etc.; and one still needs two whole ones for lechem mishneh, as is written there and there: all agree regarding Shabbos, etc.; and the same applies to Yom Tov, for on Yom Tov the manna also did not fall, as the Rif wrote in the chapter "Arvei Pesachim"; and so we say at the beginning of Yom Tov 2b: "And it shall be on the sixth day," etc., weekday prepares for Shabbos, weekday prepares for Yom Tov, etc.; and see siman 529, se'if 1; and this is the view of the Rosh at the end of Pesachim and Sefer HaPardes and the Mordechai. But the view of the Rif in the chapter "Arvei Pesachim" and the Rambam in chapter 8 of Hilchos Chametz U'Matzah is that one needs only two matzos; and so is the view of the Maggid Mishneh and many Geonim, and so is the view of Rabbeinu Manoach; and so the language of the Mishnah implies: they brought before him matzah, etc.; and likewise what is written "bread of poverty" implies so, for if not so this is not the way of poverty, since he has two matzos and also has half a matzah; rather, it is as above: he recites HaMotzi and Al Achilas Matzah over the broken piece and breaks both of them, and it is not like doing mitzvos in bundles since they are blessings, as Tosafos wrote there, s.v. "All," and they are not called bundles, etc.; see there; and so is the view of the Rashba in a responsum; and so is the main [ruling].
