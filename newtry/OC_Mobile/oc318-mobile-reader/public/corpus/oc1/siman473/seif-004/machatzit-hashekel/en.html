(א) (s"k 4) And karpas, etc. arduous labor; I heard from a great man who knew through investigation in medical books that karpas is what we call celery — through meat they improve; but if cooked alone without meat it would change for the worse; at least they would not improve — nevertheless one recites on it raw borei peri ha-adamah, as siman 200.

(ב) I would say, meaning: even if surprising that even without meat they improve through cooking, nevertheless since in any case it is also eaten raw, one recites when raw borei peri ha-adamah.

(ג) There is dispute — Tur and those who disagree; also one authority is unclear to me in Magen Avraham — apparently Maharil's father holds and doubts whether cooked is preferable even cooked without meat; for on maror too one recites borei peri ha-adamah — from the law in siman 475 seif 2: if he lacks vegetables for first dipping he takes maror and recites borei peri ha-adamah — see there.
