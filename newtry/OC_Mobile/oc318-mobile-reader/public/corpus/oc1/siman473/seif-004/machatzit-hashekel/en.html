(s.k. 4) And karpas, etc. Hard labor (avodat perekh). And I heard from a great man, to whom it became known through investigation in medical books, that karpas is what we call celery, which by means of meat improves; but if they cooked it alone without meat it would change for the worse; at the very least they would not change for the better. And in such a case one recites over it raw Borei Pri HaAdamah, as is written in siman 205.
It is fine; that is to say, even if it is surprising that even without meat they improve through cooking, nevertheless since in any case it is also eaten raw, when it is raw one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah.
For there is a dispute about it, namely Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah and those who disagree with them; and also one answer is not clear in my eyes in the Magen Avraham, for Maharil's father necessarily holds and is in doubt that perhaps cooked is preferable even if cooked without meat. That also over maror one recites Borei Pri HaAdamah is from the law written in siman 475, se'if 2, that if one has no vegetables for the first dipping he should take maror for the first dipping and recite Borei Pri HaAdamah over it; see there.
