(ד) There — Rama gloss: and nowadays one does not recite this blessing. And the reason — Tosafot wrote: because we see it every day, and all those sightings count as thirty days to thirty days — end quote — it implies that if it happened one did not see avodah zarah for thirty days he must bless. Olat Tamid ot 1. But Eshel Avraham ot 1 wrote that even one who did not see within thirty days does not bless, since most of the world sees — unlike Olat Tamid — see there in full. And Shachneh Gadol in his Hagahot Turei Zahav ot 1 wrote the reason one does not bless it now — because they rely on the poskim who hold specifically Mercurius and Adna — this avodah zarah is not prevalent — end quote. And it appears — since there is a dispute on this, and also many are lenient in these blessings as was written above siman 223 seif 1 in the gloss — therefore one should not recite this blessing except without Shem u'malchut — whether one sees the avodah zarah itself or a house of avodah zarah — and even if he never saw that place or that avodah zarah in his life:
