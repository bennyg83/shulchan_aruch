From the dust of the place, etc. Rashi's second explanation is that people take its earth to make mortar. Talmidei Rabbenu Yonah also write this, and explain the verse "I will sweep it with the broom of destruction" as meaning that it will be made into mortar.
[Levush] Alternatively, the word "or" is intended, etc. Maadanei Melech, Shiyarei Knesset HaGedolah, and Olat Tamid rule likewise. Nachalat Tzvi, however, agrees with the first explanation, and that appears primary, because the text of our Gemara is: "Blessed is He Who says and does, decrees and fulfills." I found the same in Baal Halachot Gedolot, Agudah, Kitzur Piskei HaRosh, Sefer Zikaron of Mahari HaKohen, and Abudarham. I found in Sedeh Yehoshua that one should also recite the verse "I will sweep it with the broom of destruction," but this does not appear from the poskim generally. Knesset HaGedolah, however, provides some support for his view.
