One recites. See the responsum in Yoreh Deah, siman 93. Derashos Maharash writes that one should not visit the same grave twice in one day. Writings of the Arizal. Reading the inscription upon a monument is harmful to memory, and one should recite Ahavah Rabbah until “with love,” which is highly effective against forgetfulness. See Eliyah Rabbah for several additional laws concerning this matter.
