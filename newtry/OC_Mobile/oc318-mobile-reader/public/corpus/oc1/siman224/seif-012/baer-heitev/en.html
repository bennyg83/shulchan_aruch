Graves of Israel. Even on one grave one blesses — Radbaz. But in Halakhot Gedolot vol. 1 siman 212 he disagrees and wrote that on one grave one does not bless with Shem u'malchut. Maharash wrote in his derashot: when he would walk on a grave he would say, May it be His will that the rest of so-and-so here be in honor and his merit stand for me. He also wrote: what they pluck grass or a pebble and place on a tombstone is only because of honor for the deceased, to show that he was on his grave.
