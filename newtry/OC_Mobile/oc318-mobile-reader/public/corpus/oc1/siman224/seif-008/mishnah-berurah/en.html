(11) And on kings, etc. — and it is implied in the Gemara that even a blind person, when he knows the king is passing, blesses; and see Peri Megadim who leans that if he is blind and never saw light from his days he blesses without Heaven's name and kingship.
(12) Blessed… Who gave of His glory, etc. — the rulers upon whom the king's yoke is not to change their words, and who judge and execute by law — one blesses on them. And on ministers whom the king appoints in each and every city — it is good and proper to bless without Heaven's name and kingship.
