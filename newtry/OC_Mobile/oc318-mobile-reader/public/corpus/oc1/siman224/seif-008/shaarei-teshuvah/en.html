Graves, etc. — Be'er Heitev; and see in Rashi Yerushalmi in the name of Mahari Abulafia, author of Chesed L'Avraham in his glosses in a manuscript: when he places his hand on the grave he should say the verse "And Heaven will guide you always… lie in peace and sleep in peace until the comforter who announces peace comes"; and when he places his hand he should intend in the verse "And guide you" that it contains fifteen words like the number of knots of the hand; and I heard one should place specifically his left hand and not his right hand — end of his words.
