(א) (s.k. 2) Per Rabbeinu Yehiel — the second text of Tosafot. And not specifically Mercurius — for likewise other avodah zarah one blesses Who gave long patience, etc.; and that the Gemara mentions Mercurius — because in that tanna's locale they worshipped Mercurius. And Bach explained why it mentions Mercurius — because that avodah zarah stands openly and they throw stones to it; therefore one blesses:

(ב) But other avodah zarah — their way is to set it up in their house of prayer, and one does not see the avodah zarah itself but the house of prayer in which the avodah zarah stands; and therefore one does not bless Who gave long patience, etc., but rather says: the house of the proud, Heaven will uproot — as was written in seif 11. But if one sees other avodah zarah openly — Bach concedes one blesses Who gave long patience, etc.:
