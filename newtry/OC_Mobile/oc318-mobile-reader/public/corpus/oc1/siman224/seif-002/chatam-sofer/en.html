(there) In Magen Avraham s.k. 6 "kings of Israel implies." Note well — see in Eliyahu Rabbah who rejected Magen Avraham's proof and leans not to bless; and see Birchei Yosef who adds: if the king is in a ship he should bless like a blind person — on this. And in my humble opinion one should not compare to a blind person — for at any rate between the king and himself there is no partition, unlike in a ship; and if they bless when passing before the king's palace — go out and see what the people are doing [from the Gaon, end of his words].
