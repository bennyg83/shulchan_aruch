One who sees a place from which avodah zarah was uprooted — if he is in Eretz Yisrael he says: Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who uprooted avodah zarah from our land; and if outside the Land he says: Who uprooted avodah zarah from this place; and in both he says: Just as You uprooted it from this place, so uproot it from all places and return the hearts of its worshippers to serve You.And if avodah zarah was uprooted from one place and placed in another — one blesses on the place from which it was uprooted Who uprooted avodah zarah, and on the place where it was placed Who gave long patience (Beit Yosef in the name of Tosafot Yerushalmi).'.
