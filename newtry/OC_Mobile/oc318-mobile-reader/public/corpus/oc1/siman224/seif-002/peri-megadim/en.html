One who sees idolatry and avodah zarah — as Rashi wrote, Second Temple specifically in Eretz Yisrael when the Temple stood — then one blesses on houses of the wealthy; if so, one should not recite this blessing except without Heaven's name and kingship; and similarly synagogues in their beauty — Baruch matsiv gevul almanah without Heaven's name and kingship.

Rambam, zichrono livracha, Hilchot Berachot chapter 10 wrote: Jewish scholars — Who gave of His wisdom to those who fear Him; Jewish kings — Who gave of His glory and might to those who fear Him; and in his commentary he wondered why in the Gemara he said Who apportioned, etc.; also what he wrote of might is not in the Gemara; and in Maharsha part 1: to those who fear Him — whoever fear of sin precedes his wisdom is sustained; and in kings — glory of Torah, etc.; and see Levush.
