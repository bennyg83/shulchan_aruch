7) There: If it is in Eretz Yisrael, etc. Mordechai writes that even when idolatry was uprooted in Eretz Yisrael, one says, 'Blessed is He Who uprooted idolatry from our land,' only if it was uprooted from all of Eretz Yisrael. If it was uprooted from only one location, he recites, 'Blessed is He Who uprooted idolatry from this place.' Darkei Moshe, paragraph 1, cites this. It is also implied by Tosafos at the beginning of the chapter HaRoeh in the name of the Yerushalmi. Regarding why Rema did not include this in his gloss to the Shulchan Aruch, Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 1, writes that the Bavli implies the blessing is recited even when it was uprooted from one location, as Bach writes; see there. See Nehar Shalom, paragraph 3.
