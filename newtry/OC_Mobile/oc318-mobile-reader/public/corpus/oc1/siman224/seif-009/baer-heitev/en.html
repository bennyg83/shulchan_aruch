Kings. If they do not go there with avodah zarah — see siman 218. The governors on whom there is no king's yoke to change their words, and who judge and execute by law — one blesses on them. And on the ministers whom the king appoints in every city and city — it is good to bless without Shem u'malchut — Knesset HaGedolah in the name of Radbaz and Magen Avraham. If one sees the king once — he should not cancel more of his Torah study to see him unless he comes with greater might and with greater honor — siman 218, Magen Avraham.
