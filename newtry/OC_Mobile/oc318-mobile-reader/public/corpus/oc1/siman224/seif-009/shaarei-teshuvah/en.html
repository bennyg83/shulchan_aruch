The same thing — Be'er Heitev; and see in responsa of Radbaz part 2 siman 296; and see in Yad Eliyahu siman 31 that he wrote from Magen Avraham's words in note 2 it implies his view is not to bless; and in my humble opinion there is room to distinguish; and see in Rashi Yerushalmi who brought Radbaz and Leket Yosher and his ancestor Mahara Azulai, zichrono livracha, that on other graves one blesses, and so he himself wrote; and see there in Rashi Yerushalmi in the name of Peri Chadash that if through his house window he sees the graves — nevertheless, per the view that one who did not go there for thirty days and now goes to the place itself should bless; and he brought proof from Maharitatz siman 89 and Tzror HaKesef of Mahara Gutteno who disagreed with him there. And if, Heaven forbid, a grave was newly added within thirty days — Maharikash wrote in the name of Radbaz to bless if certain, and in doubt not to bless; and see Radbaz part 3 siman 569 and in Rashi Yerushalmi he wrote that in Leket Yosher siman 212 he disagrees; and it appears according to what he wrote that perhaps it was not instituted for an individual, lest perhaps his time has not arrived and it is like rebels who raise, etc.; and Radbaz wrote that on one grave also one blesses; and the words "formed you" facing are difficult — end of his words; and it appears he thought Radbaz referred to one grave from the outset — therefore Radbaz also speaks only of one added, and "formed you" fits well since it includes all. However, because he wrote lest perhaps his time has not arrived, from this it appears his view is that even when one is added one should not bless.
