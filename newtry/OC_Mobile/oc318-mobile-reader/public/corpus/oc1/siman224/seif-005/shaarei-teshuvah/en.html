Kings — Be'er Heitev; and see Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 38 and what is written in Yad Aharon siman 225 and what he wrote there; and see in Avodah Zarah that he wrote there is doubt whether Rav Sheshet did not bless Baruch Who gave except like a greeting, etc.; and in Mishna Berurah he wrote that from the Talmud it implies he actually blessed; also that it was hidden from Avodah Zarah the text "through an opening" — Rav Sheshet said he blesses there's name; and Birkei Yosef wrote in the name of Mahari Yitzchak in a responsum in a manuscript: one who sees the king at sea in a small ship and he is covered and the king himself should not be seen but they know with certainty the king is there — he may bless; and proof from Rav Sheshet that it suffices with light and nevertheless he blessed; and it appears that specifically for this sight of the king the law is so, since the essence of the blessing is Who apportioned of His glory, and so it appears in the incident of Rav Sheshet; but one should not learn from this for other blessings that one blesses on seeing and not on knowing.
