5) If a spectacle of non-Jews occurs in your city, such as a joust or tournament, guard yourself from seeing it. The same applies to their dancing or anything connected with them. You will thereby merit to see the sport involving the Leviathan and the wild ox. If you hear a multitude of non-Jews celebrating, playing flutes, and rejoicing, sigh and say: Master of the worlds, Your people whom You brought out of Egypt sinned doubly and were punished doubly, etc. May it be Your will to rebuild Yerushalayim, Your holy city, speedily in our days, Amen. Bach in the name of Sefer HaEshkol; Ateres Zekeinim. Avodah Zarah 18b states that even one who attends a spectacle of Jewish people is included among those who sit in a gathering of scoffers. Magen Avraham, paragraph 3; Chayei Adam, rule 63, paragraph 17.
