Six hundred thousand, etc. This implies that he follows Rambam, who holds that one does not recite Chacham HaRazim over a single great sage. Beit Yosef and Bach labor over the reason, and Lechem Chamudot rejects their explanations. I am surprised, because I found this law and both reasons explicitly in Ramban's Milchamot Hashem. According to this, it also appears that the blessing over six hundred thousand is recited specifically in Eretz Yisrael.
