(ה) And if there occur in your city keniginot of gentiles, such as Turlnaga and Turnira — guard yourself lest you see; and the same applies to their dancing or anything of their rejoicing — for then you will merit and see the keniginot of Leviathan — end quote. And when you hear the sound of a multitude of gentiles celebrating and playing flutes and rejoicing — sigh and say: Master of the worlds, Your people whom You brought out of Egypt sinned doubly and were smitten doubly; a disgraceful measure was meted out to them; You destroyed their palaces, their Shabbat joy You brought to mourning, the joy of their lips You turned to mourning, their glory You spoiled, their splendor You cast from heaven to earth, their pomp You cut off, the joy of man you placed as their head; You subjected the exalted over them, their scorn You saw — and You, Heaven, are He forever; be not angry with us, and let Your wrath not smoke forever against the flock of Your pasture; may it be before You that You rebuild Jerusalem, Your holy city, speedily in our days, amen. Bach in the name of Sefer HaEshkol. And so in the first chapter of Avodah Zarah daf 18b — even one who goes to the keniginot of Israel — this is included among seats of scorners.
