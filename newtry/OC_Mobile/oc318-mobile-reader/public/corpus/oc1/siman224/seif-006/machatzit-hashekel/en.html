(s.k. 6) Kings of Israel implies in the Gemara even a blind person, etc. — and so we find regarding Rav Sheshet, daf 58a. And in Sefer Avot de-Rabbi Natan it is written, and it implies there from the language of the Gemara that he did not bless the blessing the Sages obligated, but rather blessed the king with the blessing of greeting, etc.; for there they say: when the king came, R' Shimon opened and was blessing him; he said to him: this Sadducee — for one you did not see you bless, etc. But in Ein Yaakov the version is: when the king came, R' Shimon opened and said Baruch; he said to him: and you did not see — how do you bless? — end quote:
