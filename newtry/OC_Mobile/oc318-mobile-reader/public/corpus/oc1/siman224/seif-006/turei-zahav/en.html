Who apportioned of His wisdom. This that is taught regarding Israel "Who apportioned" and regarding nations "Who gave" — I saw many versions and it appears the essence is: regarding Israel there is reason, for they cleave to Him, blessed be He, and whenever you say "portion" you must know from Whom it was apportioned — it turns out there is lineage of the portion to the One from Whom it was apportioned; unlike nations who have no lineage after the Giver, like every gift that has no liability on the giver but what he acquired he acquired and no more; therefore Scripture said (Genesis 25:6): "And to the sons of the concubines Abraham gave gifts" — Rashi explains there: impurity — he also gave them this with the intent I mentioned, that after he gave them this gift there is no longer cleaving to the Giver.
