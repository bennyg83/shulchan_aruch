(י) [Seif 3] One who sees wicked Babylon, etc. And this is when passing from Baghdad in Iraq, which sits on the Chidekel river, to go to the city of Hilla which sits on the Euphrates river — while still about two hours before arriving at the city of Hilla one encounters one place in the desert called by name, and there are ruins there, and they call it Old Babylon; and I remember when I was about ten years old when we went to prostrate on the monument of Ezekiel the prophet, may peace be upon him, on the Kevar river — which is about six hours from the city of Hilla to the Kevar river — we already saw that place of Babylon, and they told us that this is Old Babylon:
