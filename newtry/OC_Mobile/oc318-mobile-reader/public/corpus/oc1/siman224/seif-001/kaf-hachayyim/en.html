1) [Seif 1] One who sees Markulis. The idol's actual name is Kulis, not Markulis. It is called Markulis as an alteration of 'kilus,' for they call praise 'kilus,' while the Sages changed it to a term of disgrace meaning mockery and derision, because it is forbidden to mention an idol's name. 'Mar' indicates an alteration. Tosafos on Bava Metzia 25b, beginning with the words, 'like stones,' and Tosafos on Avodah Zarah 50a, beginning with the words, 'stones.' See Magen Avraham, seif 1. Markulis is constructed by placing one stone upon two others and throwing stones upon it. Rashi on Avodah Zarah 50b.
