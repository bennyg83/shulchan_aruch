(ב) Constantly. Even if one goes to another city, since he saw the like in his city — and the same applies to a child who grew up, that he need not bless since he saw in his youth — Magen Avraham. And Bach wrote in the name of Sefer Eshkol: guard yourself to see the keniginot of gentiles, and the same applies to their dancing or anything of their rejoicing; and if you hear the voice of rejoicing gentiles — sigh and grieve over the destruction of Jerusalem and pray to the Holy One blessed be He concerning it.
