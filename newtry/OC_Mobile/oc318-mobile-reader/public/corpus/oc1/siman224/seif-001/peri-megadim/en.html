Who apportioned — Beit Yosef gives two reasons: (1) souls of Israel are a portion of Heaven, unlike gentiles; and R' David Abudraham wrote: a portion like a pipe from a river that can be widened and narrowed — so Israel, and per their merit it increases and decreases, unlike gentiles who are cut off and uprooted. And Bach wrote in the name of Midrash Rabbah: for Heaven gives wisdom from His mouth, knowledge and understanding — to gentiles He gave human wisdom (in the seven wisdoms) and not Torah and its secrets; and to Israel a portion of everything — of Torah and its secrets. And Levush explained portion — for to Him they live; how many generations must come to the ultimate purpose of Torah — only a portion, unlike the seven wisdoms which are entirely a gift in their hands that they can reach its ultimate purpose. And Taz, zichrono livracha, explained that an unspecified gift has no liability, as explained in Choshen Mishpat regarding a sale with liability, etc.; and if so, like a gift — when the recipient receives it there is no connection and mingling of the recipient with the giver, for even if they take the gift from him there is no liability of the giver on him; so gentiles, unlike Israel — when they sin: "You forgot the Heaven Who formed you" and cleave to Him, blessed be He, per "Who dwells among them in their impurity"; and this is what is said regarding Abraham (Genesis 25:6): "And to the sons of the concubines Abraham gave gifts" — Rashi explains there: impurity — meaning the filth separated from him and remained in them and they have no connection at all with Abraham, unlike Isaac: "for you are called his seed, a portion of him"; and see Ra'avan there, and one need not elaborate.
