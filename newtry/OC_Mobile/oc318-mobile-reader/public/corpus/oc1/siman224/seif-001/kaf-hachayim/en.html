(א) [Seif 1] One who sees Mercurius. And the name of the avodah zarah is not Mercurius but Kulis; and that they call it Mercurius — this is an exchange of kilus, for they call kilus a word of praise, and the Sages exchanged it for a word of disgrace and mockery — praise — because it is forbidden to mention the name of avodah zarah; and mar is a word of exchange. Tosafot Moed Katan 25b s.v. ke'avnei, and Tosafot Avodah Zarah 50a s.v. avnei; and see Magen Avraham s.k. 1. And Mercurius is that they place one stone on two and throw stones upon it.
