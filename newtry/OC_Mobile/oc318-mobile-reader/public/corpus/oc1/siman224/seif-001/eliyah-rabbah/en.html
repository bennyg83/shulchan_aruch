[א] And nowadays, etc. So wrote Tosafot at the beginning of perek HaRo'eh, and Levush explained the Gemara teaches us even a minor who grew up does not bless. In my humble opinion the Gemara teaches us that even one who did not see within thirty days does not bless, since most of the world sees — and so Piskei Tosafot imply, unlike Olat Tamid.
