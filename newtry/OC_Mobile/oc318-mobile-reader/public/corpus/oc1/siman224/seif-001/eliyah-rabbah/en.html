And nowadays, etc. Tosafot at the beginning of chapter HaRoeh write this. The Levush explains that the novelty is that even a minor who grew up does not bless. In my humble opinion the novelty is rather that even one who personally did not see them within thirty days does not bless, because most people do see them. So appears from Piskei Tosafot, unlike Olat Tamid. For the distinctions among these laws, see Bach and the later authorities. They write that it is prohibited to watch non-mitzvah dancing and public spectacles.
[Levush] To our fathers, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov writes that this is the Gemara's text, but Rif, Rosh, and Shulchan Aruch read "to the righteous" instead of "to our fathers," which is more correct. Baal Halachot Gedolot and all the poskim have the same text. The Levush himself also uses it above in siman 218 seif 7. This requires study.
