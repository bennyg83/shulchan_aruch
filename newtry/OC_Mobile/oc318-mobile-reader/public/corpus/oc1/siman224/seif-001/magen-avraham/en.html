(ג) And nowadays. It appears to me that he comes to inform us — even if one goes to another city and sees avodah zarah, nevertheless since he saw the like in his city within thirty days he does not bless, as above; see siman 218 seif 13. And it is possible he comes to inform us that even a child who grew up need not bless, since he saw it in his youth. Bach wrote in the name of Sefer HaEshkol: Guard yourself from seeing the theaters of gentiles, and the same applies to their dancing or anything of their rejoicing; and if you hear the voice of gentiles celebrating and rejoicing — sigh and grieve over the destruction of Jerusalem and pray to the Holy One, blessed be He, over it — end quote.
