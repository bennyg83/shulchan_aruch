To those who fear Him — Be'er Heitev; and see Rashi Yerushalmi in all these mentioned that one blesses on seeing them even if he did not see them eye to eye but through a window where glass intervenes between them — he may recite the blessings of seeing; and see Dvar Shmuel siman 242 and Shevut Yaakov part 1 siman 126 regarding Birkat HaLevana through a closed glass window, and it will be explained in siman 227 there.
