3) There: Or other idolatry. Beis Yosef writes that this includes even a house of idolatry. Bach, however, writes that it applies specifically when one sees the idol exposed; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 7, likewise writes that it applies specifically when one sees it exposed; see there. Halachos Ketanos, volume 2, siman 177, likewise writes that the blessing is recited even over the building, contrary to Bach; see there. See the following paragraph.
