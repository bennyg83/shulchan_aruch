10) [Seif 3] One who sees wicked Bavel, etc. When traveling from the great city Baghdad, may God protect it, situated upon the Tigris River, toward the city of Hillah, situated upon the Euphrates River, approximately two hours before reaching Hillah one encounters a desolate wilderness containing ruins called ancient Bavel. I remember that when I was about ten years old, we traveled to prostrate ourselves at the grave of the prophet Yechezkel, peace be upon him, beside the Chebar River, which is about six hours from Hillah. We saw that place of Bavel, and they told us that it was ancient Bavel.
11) There: Who destroyed Bavel, etc. This is because Bavel destroyed our land. Maharsha, Chiddushei Aggados.
12) There: If he saw the lions' den, etc. See above, siman 218, paragraph 32.
13) There: Or the fiery furnace. This is the pit in which the furnace of Chananyah, Mishael, and Azaryah was heated. Rashi on Berachos 57b.
