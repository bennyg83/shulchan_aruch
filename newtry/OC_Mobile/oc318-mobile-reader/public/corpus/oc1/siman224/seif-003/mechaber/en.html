One who sees wicked Babylon says: Blessed are You, Heaven our Heaven, King of the universe, Who destroyed wicked Babylon. One who sees the house of Nebuchadnezzar says: Blessed… Who destroyed the house of wicked Nebuchadnezzar. One who sees a lions' den or the fiery furnace says: Blessed… Who performed a miracle for the righteous in this place. (And see above siman 218 seif 7.)(.
