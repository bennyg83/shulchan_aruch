On all the days, etc. Except for Shabbos. In Sefarad they are accustomed to say Selichos and supplications even on Shabbos; Tur. On Tzom Gedalyah, a woman who gave birth within thirty days is permitted to eat. Some are stringent and prohibit from the eighth day onward, but one who is lenient has lost nothing. If Tzom Gedalyah falls on Shabbos, it is postponed to Sunday.
We do not recite Kiddush Levanah, etc. Some say it is good to renew the moon before Yom Kippur so that this mitzvah will tip the scale toward merit; Maharam Yaffe. During those Ten Days one should be careful regarding bread of non-Jews, and if he cannot avoid it, he should make the oven acceptable by throwing in one chip of wood. See Yoreh Deah, siman 112, seif 15.
