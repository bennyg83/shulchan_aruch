Seif 1. In the gloss: We do not recite Kiddush Levanah until Motzaei Yom Kippur, etc. Marginal note: The custom is not to marry women until after Yom Kippur, and I was told that in Sefer Mateh Ephraim he brought this custom. The reason for this custom appears in two ways. First, since Chazal said that one who marries a woman has all his sins forgiven, if so, at the beginning of the year one should not make efforts for forgiveness of sins until Yom Kippur, when the Holy One, blessed is He, together with the essence of the day, forgives his sins. If he makes an effort at the beginning for forgiveness of sins before Yom Kippur, it is like raising a hand against the King. A support for this is what is written in Yerushalmi, chapter 8 of Yoma, regarding one who says, I will sin and Yom Kippur will atone, etc.: this person says to the King, You are not King. End quote. See Korban HaEdah and my novellae to Orach Chayim from the year 5609, in Hilchos Yom Kippur, siman 607, folio 54, and from there it is proven that Yom Kippur is considered like a king, and one who kicks against it is like one who kicks against the king; see there. So too here, one who makes efforts to obtain forgiveness at the beginning of the year before Yom Kippur is like raising a hand against the King. It resembles what Chazal said on the verse, And he raised his hand against the king, that he removed tefillin in his presence. That was raising a hand, for he showed that the time had arrived to remove tefillin before the king had done so. So too here. A second reason may be said: since Chazal said in Rosh Hashanah, Which festival is it on which the moon is covered? This is Tishrei. The kabbalists explained that the meaning is that above, the Satan covers the moon. If so, the moon is not revealed above until Yom Kippur. Therefore we do not recite Kiddush Levanah until Motzaei Yom Kippur, for then the moon is revealed above in its light. But until Yom Kippur, the moon above is hidden, even though it appears below. Therefore, since the moon is hidden above, it is like the end of the month, when one does not marry women. So too here: since the moon is covered and we do not recite Kiddush Levanah, we do not betroth a woman. These are the reasons as they appear to me, and it is very correct, with Hashem's help; examine it carefully.
