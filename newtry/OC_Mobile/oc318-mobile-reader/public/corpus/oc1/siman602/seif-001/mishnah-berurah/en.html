Cherem. In order not to awaken the forces of judgment at that time. Similarly, they do not impose oaths, because they are especially concerned about bringing the punishment of an oath into the world, Heaven forbid. It is possible that even if he is obligated to accept a cherem upon himself through reversal of the oath or the like, nevertheless he does not accept a cherem upon himself until after Yom Kippur. However, if he wants to, one may say that permission is in his hand; Pri Megadim.
And similarly they do not impose oaths. In any event, it is a mitzvah to judge in order to remove stolen property from his hand; and when there is judgment below, there is no judgment above.

For Kiddush Levanah is better when one is fragrant and in joy, and in the Midrash it is stated that on Motzaei Yom Kippur a heavenly voice goes out and says, Go, eat with joy, etc. But know that several later authorities agreed that it is better to fulfill the mitzvah beforehand, so that this mitzvah will tip him toward the side of merit.
