Perhaps even one who is obligated to accept a cherem upon himself through reversal of the oath and the like, see Choshen Mishpat, nevertheless does not accept a cherem upon himself until after Yom Kippur. However, if he wants, one may say permission is in his hand. What the Rav wrote, that on the Shabbos between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur they say Tzidkascha Tzedek: even when Erev Yom Kippur falls on Sunday, nevertheless on Shabbos at Minchah they say Tzidkascha Tzedek. For on a weekday as well, at the Minchah before it, they say Tachanun, as in siman 604, seif 2: one does not fall on one's face on Erev Yom Kippur, even in the dawn watch; see Levush. But at the Minchah before it they do fall on their faces. So wrote Eliyah Rabbah, siman 604, os 3; see there.
