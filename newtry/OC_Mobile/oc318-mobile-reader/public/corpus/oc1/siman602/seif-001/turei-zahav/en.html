"And they do not impose a cherem." This is in order not to arouse the forces of strict judgment at that time. Likewise, they do not administer oaths, because there is heightened concern that punishment associated with an oath may thereby be brought upon creation, Heaven forbid. But adjudicating monetary disputes between one person and another is certainly a great mitzvah. Levush writes likewise, because when judgment is carried out below, there is no judgment above.
