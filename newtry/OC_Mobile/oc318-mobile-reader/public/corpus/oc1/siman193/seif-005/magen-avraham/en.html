Until they said ha-zan. This requires further study, for below siman 200 it is written until baruch, etc.

Even if they ate afterward. For zimun already departed from them.

And even if they did not eat. This requires further study, for does not bread require a berachah in its place as written siman 178 and 184? And it appears to me that this is what he says: they must eat now a little in order to join for zimun. And per Rambam there, it must deal with one house from corner to corner [Beit Yosef]; and it appears to me that even if they left inadvertently per Rambam, nevertheless it is preferable that they return to their place rather than invite zimun here. And examine this: what he says "and even if they did not eat together" — meaning rather each one ate separately, that they did not establish and are not obligated in zimun as written seif 2 here — they are obligated since they already became obligated, as in Terumat Yisrael. However per Rambam this cannot be except b'dieved, for l'chatchila it is forbidden to eat until he blesses birkas hamazon.
