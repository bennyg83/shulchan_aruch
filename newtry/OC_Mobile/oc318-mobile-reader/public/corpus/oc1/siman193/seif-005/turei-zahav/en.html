They can never again invite zimun. So wrote Tur. And in the Gemara: if he invited zimun upon them in their places, zimun departed from them — Rashi: if there was enough in every group and in each remained enough for zimun, and these who separated needed to go to the market before the groups finished their meal — then these three must invite zimun; but if they invited upon them that these join with them for zimun as above, "let us bless" etc., and the group blesses and these were not there except enough to bless and he is blessed — and now they come to bless, they do not invite zimun, for zimun obligation departed from them and does not return upon them. And Tosafot wrote: if he invited upon them in their places — meaning each of the three ate and stopped for the two who were reclining with him until ha-zan — zimun departed from upon them, i.e., exempt from zimun and now permitted to divide — end of his words. Behold what Tosafot mean by "exempt" etc. — this is to exclude Rashi's view that they already invited zimun properly but have not yet blessed — then these three are forbidden to invite zimun since they already invited; not so per Tosafot's explanation: if he stopped for two and ate afterward — in this there is no prohibition if they wished to invite zimun on the second eating, only they are exempt from zimun. And Tur wrote like Tosafot's explanation but disagrees with him in what he wrote "meaning exempt from zimun" — which implies there is no prohibition if they wish to invite zimun on the second eating; and in this Tur holds there is prohibition if they wish to invite zimun, since they already fulfilled zimun at the beginning of the meal; and therefore he wrote they cannot invite zimun; and so in Shulchan Aruch.

And even if they did not eat together from when they joined. And it is difficult — behold bread requires berachah in its place; and it appears it deals when each ate afterward, only not in one group but this not in the presence of that; and Beit Yosef gave two answers on this. One: three groups ate in one house — it deals that this group ate in this corner and that in that corner; for even though afterward they gather in one corner, we have no concern — from corner to corner is not a change. Or: this is what he says — since these three came to join together and invite zimun, even though they did not recline together and did not eat together, since they convened to invite zimun they are not permitted to divide further; therefore they need further to eat together and zimun will apply upon them and again they are not permitted to divide — end of his words. Behold the first answer is like the answer I mentioned; and the second answer is puzzling in my humble opinion — for since they are not in a place where berachat hazimun applies, since they are not permitted to bless there, how can it be said they are obligated to eat in order to bless — we do not find such a thing, to eat in order to bless, since he does not desire to eat; and proof from what Mordechai wrote, whom Rama brought siman 197: the blesser must drink — and that means desiring to drink; it implies but not drink for the sake of the berachah.
