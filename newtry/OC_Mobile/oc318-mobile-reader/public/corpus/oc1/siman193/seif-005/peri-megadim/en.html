Taz — ostensibly three views here: from Beit Yosef it implies with zimun better that afterward each bless birkas hamazon for himself, as siman 183 seif 7; and Darkei Moshe here ot 3 wrote it is good to bless in a whisper nevertheless word for word with the blesser and at the conclusion hurry somewhat and finish earlier and answer amen (perhaps Beit Yosef does not require); and to Bach it is good not to bless at all in a whisper wherever there is zimun, and to hear from the mouth of the blesser all of birkas hamazon; and Darkei Moshe and Bach also have a leniency: if they are many, even if they can hear berachat hazimun — whenever they cannot hear birkas hamazon it is good they bless each by himself and again are not permitted to invite zimun; and to Beit Yosef in such a case they are obligated to invite zimun only when they do not hear berachat hazimun — then they are permitted to divide into threes; and Bach challenged from Berachot 45b that Taz brought: three invite zimun and bless birkas hamazon each by himself and fulfill both zimun and division of berachot — implies wherever there is zimun there is no division of berachot; and this is difficult also for Darkei Moshe; and Taz explained one answer for Darkei Moshe — end of his words; and for Beit Yosef it is somewhat difficult further: even for Beit Yosef division of berachot means the cup in birkas hamazon — when they bless each by himself without zimun, and birkas hamazon requires a cup even for an individual each blesses mouth and last berachah. And one could answer for the Mechaber in siman 182 seif 1: some say and some imply like the second view; if so, on the contrary when they do not invite zimun there is no cup; and further they invite zimun and each stands with his cup in hand and intends not to fulfill through the blesser's mouth — and there is zimun and division of berachot — requires further study.
