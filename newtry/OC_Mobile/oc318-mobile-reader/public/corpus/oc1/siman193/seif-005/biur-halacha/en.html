Each one for two — it appears that the same applies if one of them paused for two — we also say zimun already departed from him and only two remain here; and so I found in Pesakim of Riaz and in Ra'ah's novellae on Rif. However per what is explained below in siman 200 in Tur, brought there in Magen Avraham, it implies that one can join again with the two when they now ate together after they had not yet invited zimun; and one must say that for the first cases cited it deals when they did not eat now, but when they ate — likewise they join; and the law requires study.
