(29) Each one to two — see Biur Halachah.

(30) Even if they ate afterward — for zimun already departed from them through their having invited zimun upon them previously; and nevertheless in birkas hamazon they must bless from the beginning of the berachah ha-zan, as below siman 200 in the gloss.

(31) And they are not permitted to divide — since they now convened together and each one comes from a group that already became obligated there in zimun; and even though it was explained in siman 196 in the gloss that it is forbidden to separate from a group of three, for thereby they lose their zimun — if so each one should return to his group; one could say it deals here when their first group separated; or according to what is explained below siman 200 in Eliyah Rabbah and Shaarei Teshuvah that where there is coercion or monetary loss it is permitted for one to finish his meal before the two finish and bless by himself — it deals here also in such a case where these three were forced to hurry and finish their meal earlier; and therefore if these three chanced to meet together they invite zimun by themselves.

(32) And even if they did not eat etc. — and it is difficult, for we hold above siman 184 that birkas hamazon must be blessed specifically in the place where one ate; if so each one must return to the place where he ate originally. Some reconcile that here it deals when they uprooted inadvertently from their place, for in this we do not trouble him to return to his place per Rambam, as likewise there in seif 1; and so the Acharonim ruled there in a place of coercion; and in our matter too, since here he can invite zimun and when he returns there he will bless alone — it is like a time of coercion; and further one could say: what it says "and even if they did not eat together" — meaning rather each one ate by himself, that they did not establish and are not obligated in zimun as written seif 2 here — they are obligated since they already became obligated.
