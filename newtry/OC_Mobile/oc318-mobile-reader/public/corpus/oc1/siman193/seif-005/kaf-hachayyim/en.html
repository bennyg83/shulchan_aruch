5) There: If he does not understand, etc. This implies that in every case, if he does not understand, he does not fulfill his obligation by listening, even with three, where one discharges two, or with ten. This is also implied by Beis Yosef, siman 124; see there. Birchas Avraham, however, writes that this applies specifically when one individual discharges another individual. When one individual discharges a group, such as a prayer leader discharging a congregation or the leader of a Zimun discharging a group of three or ten, the individual discharges the group even if they do not understand; see there. Knesses HaGedolah, siman 62, Hagahos HaTur, Olas Tamid in this siman, paragraph 2, and Shulchan Gavoah, paragraph 1, cite this. One should rely upon this only to prevent them from entirely missing the blessing, as stated above in paragraph 2; see there.
