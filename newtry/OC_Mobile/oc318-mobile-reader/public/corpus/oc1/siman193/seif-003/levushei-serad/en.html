(Magen Avraham s.k. 5) He explains birkas hamazon. In the Mechaber's words one must certainly explain berachat hazimun — for in birkas hamazon each can bless in a whisper by himself, as explained in the previous s.k. in Magen Avraham; but Magen Avraham per his view explains thus, for he holds in the previous s.k. that they must hear all of birkas hamazon. And the main intent of Magen Avraham here is to bring proof for his words in the previous s.k., because this law in Shulchan Aruch is from the Talmud: on daf 50 Rava said, when we ate bread in the house of the Exilarch we would bless three by three, etc. — see there; it implies from Rashi that when they would invite zimun with ten, R' Gamliel heard — meaning all of birkas hamazon — see there.
