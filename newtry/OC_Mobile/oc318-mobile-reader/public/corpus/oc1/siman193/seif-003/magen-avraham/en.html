Not to invite zimun. And the reason is as he wrote, that he saw many great people who invited zimun in an akum's house and say "May the Merciful One bless the ba'al habayit," referring to the master of the feast — all the more so if each one eats from his own, for then even in a Jewish house they say "May the Merciful One bless us," etc. And so Levush and Maharil and Bach. And it is possible that if they eat at an inn for a day as casual wayfarers, that is not establishment in an akum's house and they do not invite zimun.
