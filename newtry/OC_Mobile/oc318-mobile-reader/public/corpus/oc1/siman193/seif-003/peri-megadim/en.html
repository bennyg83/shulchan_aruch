Then. Taz — behold in Darkei Moshe he wrote explicitly that twenty is better not to divide; and he brought Rashi Berachot 57 ot 1 on Mishnah "until ten if they wish," and on "six" he did not write "if they wish" — the reason: with six the choice is in their hands, and with twenty better not to divide since they mention the Name in zimun; ten is better with multitude of people like synagogue, see there; and there 57 ot 1 top of page "six may divide" — Rashi "if they wish" meaning without obligation only if they wish; nevertheless choice is in their hands; and therefore he proved there "Let us bless" is better — for if with six better not to divide only that they are permitted and there is no prohibition, how did he prove perhaps "Bless us" is better (so in Chiddushim; and now I saw in Eliyah Rabbah ot 3 he sensed this). And so too Rashba's words here in gloss "twenty may if they wish" — this is Rashi in Mishnah; and since he did not explain thus with six — implies with six choice is in their hands; and above where he explained "if they wish" to exclude that there is no obligation — and he says "and it appears to me" etc. that there is no obligation. And Levush's words are clear as his word: six divide if they wish, and twenty are permitted to divide; and in seif 2 as we said "permitted" is better not to divide — nevertheless it is difficult: Rashi in Gemara "six may divide — they are permitted" unlike the term "permitted" with twenty in Mishnah — opposite of what is said here. And Taz's view: since Mishnah 57 ot 1 does not say "six are permitted" and likewise with twenty — implies choice in their hands, see there. And what he wrote that there is merit in multitude of people over division of berachot — requires study, for certainly without that berachat zimun requires three. And Magen Avraham Tzedek ot 15 and Terumat Tzvi — requires further study on what Beit HaKnesset wrote from here; and see ot 4 Eliyah Rabbah on this.
