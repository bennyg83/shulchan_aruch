(25) If they were riding etc. — this seif is brought verbatim above siman 167 seif 12, except there it deals with discharging in berachat hamotzi and here it deals with joining for zimun, and the law is one for both; and I already explained there well in Mishnah Berurah, see there.
(26) "They walk and eat — no" — but those sitting in a wagon and eating together while traveling — see siman 167 what I wrote in the name of Magen Avraham who is uncertain on this, and Kaf HaChayim ruled that in this and likewise wherever there is doubt with three they invite zimun; but with ten there is concern for mentioning the Name in vain. And nevertheless it appears to me they invite without the Name, for b'dieved one fulfills with ten even if he did not mention the Name, as above at end of siman 192.
(27) Not to invite zimun etc. — the Acharonim disagree on this, and today the custom is that they invite zimun, and in HaRachaman they say "May the Merciful One send us abundant blessing in our going and our sitting forever," and we can say "May the Merciful One bless this ba'al habayit" — referring to the master of the feast. And all the more so if each one eats from his own, for then even in a Jewish house they say "May the Merciful One bless us," etc.; and if they eat at an inn for a day as casual wayfarers in an akum's house, it is possible this is not establishment and they do not invite zimun [Magen Avraham]; and it is good that they not establish then all three together and remove themselves from doubt [Peri Megadim].
