There — since their companions invited zimun, it appears to me this is specifically if they invited zimun before these three joined together; for then the one who left his place to fulfill alone — through zimun in this, the zimun that the three remaining in their homes invited applies also upon him and zimun departed from him; but if these three joined to one place and afterward the others remaining in their homes invited zimun — these three may invite zimun and zimun did not depart from them; and since there is here a place for them to invite zimun together by themselves, they separated completely from their group and the zimun does not apply upon them; and there is some proof from the gloss here; but if in every group there were four, it is permitted to separate and they did not stipulate that they would invite zimun first, as it appears to me.
