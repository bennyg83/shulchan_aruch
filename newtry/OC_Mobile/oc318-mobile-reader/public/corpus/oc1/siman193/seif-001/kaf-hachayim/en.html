(1) [Seif 1] Two who ate — even though in berachat hamotzi one discharges the other, etc. — and the distinction between the first berachah and the final berachah is because at the beginning of the meal when they recline and are fixed together their minds are to join; therefore each fulfills through his fellow's berachah. But at the end of the meal the fixation is nullified and they separate from one another; therefore each blesses for himself. And R' Yosef explained in the name of Rashbam: because birkas hamazon is d'oraisa, but the first berachah is d'rabbanan — we are not so stringent, and one fulfills through his fellow's berachah. Rosh chapter 3 "who ate." And so Tosafot and Mordechai and R' Yosef Beit Yosef. And Levush wrote we do not practice like the latter reason, for otherwise one could discharge his fellow in every berachah on fruits, whether first or final, since all are d'rabbanan; rather the first reason is primary — specifically bread that has fixation: if they fixed, one discharges his fellow; but not other species, neither in the first berachah nor the final — see there; and Perishah brought this, letter Alef. However Taz s.k. 1 wrote that even per the latter reason, who distinguishes between d'oraisa and d'rabbanan — one must distinguish regarding fruits, even though they are d'rabbanan, and each must bless by himself, as Rama wrote in siman 213 in the gloss — see there. And all this is per Rama's words in the gloss to siman 213 seif 1, who wrote that regarding fruits even the first berachah — each blesses for himself, for sitting does not help one discharge his fellow; but per Maran there, sitting helps one discharge his fellow in the first berachah also for fruits — see there. And see above siman 167 seif 11, and in our words there letter Tet and letter Peh; examine.
