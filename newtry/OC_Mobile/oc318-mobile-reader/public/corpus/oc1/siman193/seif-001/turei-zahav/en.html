It is a mitzvah to divide. The reason in Beit Yosef in the name of Rashbam: birkas hamazon is d'oraisa but the first berachah they were not so stringent, and one fulfills through his fellow's berachah. And Rosh wrote the reason: at the beginning of the meal when they are reclining and established together their intent is to join; therefore one fulfills through his fellow's berachah; but at the end of the meal the establishment is nullified and they separate from one another; therefore each blesses for himself — end of his words. And even per the first reason that he distinguishes d'oraisa from d'rabbanan — one must divide on fruits even though it is d'rabbanan that each must bless by himself, as written siman 213 in the gloss — see there.

If he understands lashon kodesh. Tur concluded and therefore women do not fulfill through hearing if they do not understand. Beit Yosef brings in the name of Rashal who holds women fulfill even if they do not understand; and even though we do not hold thus, nevertheless our women who do not understand lashon kodesh and most do not bless for themselves — nevertheless they may rely on Rashi; and even l'chatchila they fulfill through what they hear, for this is still better than not blessing at all, in my humble opinion.

Then they may divide if they wish. It appears from this that even though the choice is in their hands, nevertheless it is better not to divide because of "in a multitude of people is the King's glory," as Rama wrote afterward in seif 2; and it is difficult on this — for if so the tanna of the Mishnah should not have needed to say here "they are permitted to divide," for it is stated in chapter One Who Ate daf 53: the Sages taught — they were sitting in the house of study and they brought light before them; Beit Shammai say each one blesses for himself, and Beit Hillel say one blesses for all because of "in a multitude of people is the King's glory"; and Beit Shammai's reason is because of nullification of the house of study — for when one blesses for all they must all be silent and hear him and answer amen — implies that without nullification of the house of study even Beit Shammai agree we follow multitude of people and the King's glory, and the choice is not in their hands; and what is written here that they gave him the choice. And further why did Rama not write this here as he wrote afterward. Therefore it appears to me: specifically in seif 2 — that if they bless each by himself zimun will not be fulfilled at all — in this we say the mitzvah of zimun will be fulfilled and the berachot will be "in a multitude of people is the King's glory"; and likewise in chapter One Who Ate that we mentioned — the berachot were equal whether each says by himself or one says on behalf of all, for there is no relevance of zimun; therefore we follow multitude; not so here with twenty or six where there is zimun; and it is possible to make two zimun — here there are equal merits: if they all chance together there will be a greater multitude; and when they make two zimun there will be in each group somewhat of a multitude; and there is division of berachot from two zimun — here the choice is in their hands because the sides are balanced, as above; although Maharach z"l also copied in the name of Rama that better not to divide with twenty because of multitude etc. — in my humble opinion what I wrote is correct; and in chapter Three Who Ate daf 45: Judah bar Mareimar and Mar bar Rav Ashi and Rav Achi of Difti wrapped bread together — there was not among them one distinguished in wisdom and old age from the others; they sat and raised: that which we taught "three who ate are obligated to invite zimun" — this applies where there is a great person; but where they are all equal, division of berachot is preferable — bless each for himself. They came before Mareimar; he said to them: you have fulfilled berachah, you have not fulfilled zimun. And it is difficult: why would they say to bless each by himself — behold it requires "in a multitude of people is the King's glory"; and it appears that in truth this was their mistake in two matters: they did not know there is greater merit in multitude of people over division of berachot; and further that there is merit in zimun even if there is no great one among them — until Mareimar revealed to them both matters that there is zimun here even though all are equal; and then there is merit because of multitude of people; and this is preferable to division of berachot, as above.

And it appears to me likewise with six etc. Meaning: that Rashi wrote this regarding ten, and it is not precise.

They cannot hear from the mouth of the blesser. Meaning berachat hazimun — for if they fulfill birkas hamazon they need not hear but each blesses by himself in a whisper, so Beit Yosef; and if so those who can hear berachat hazimun certainly should not divide into threes; and even though in the Gemara that I mentioned nearby from the incident of Judah bar Mareimar that they said bless each for himself — it implies that if they invited zimun each would not bless for himself but hear the blesser — that is for the sages of the Talmud who could be careful to intend for the blesser's berachah word for word, and this is better; but we who cannot be careful in this — better each bless in a whisper, as written siman 183 in the name of Rosh regarding berachah of yotzer or; in my humble opinion. And further it appears to me one cannot prove from this that each should not bless birkas hamazon for himself — for in any case one must bless on a cup of blessing when they are in zimun; and if each blesses by himself each will bless on his own cup of blessing by himself; and on this he said division of berachot is preferable — that it is better each bless on his own cup.
