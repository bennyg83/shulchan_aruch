Mitzvah. Taz — per Darkei Torah's view, when one ate less than satiation he can discharge others; and we hold like the second view also that in the end zimun is nullified. And see Levush who proved from Mordechai for our practice that we hold regarding fruits one does not discharge his fellow even though it is d'rabbanan; and Taz explained there is no zimun on fruits. Eliyah Rabbah ot 1 likewise in the name of Maharitatz rule 38. However, for those who are lax in berachot, Taz on siman 213 ot 1 wrote it is better to listen to them; and even on the seven species where some say Darkei Torah — nevertheless it is preferable since they are lax in it.
