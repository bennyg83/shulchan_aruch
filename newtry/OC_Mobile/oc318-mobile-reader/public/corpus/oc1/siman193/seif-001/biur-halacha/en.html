And he must, etc. This is indispensable unless he knows that he at least heard the essential portions of the blessings, including their openings and conclusions; in that case he has fulfilled his obligation after the fact. Know further that if he heard Bircas HaMazon while dozing, he has not fulfilled his obligation. This appears from Biur HaGra.
He does not fulfill his obligation by listening. The Mechaber’s ruling concerns two people, as explained above. Regarding three people, whether one may discharge the other two is disputed among the poskim. Bircas Avraham holds that even according to the Mechaber, the restriction applies only where one individual discharges one other individual. In a zimun of three, where one discharges two, or of ten, where one discharges nine, one person may discharge the group even if they do not understand. Many later authorities cite this view. Bach in this siman and Levush in siman 199, however, imply otherwise.
