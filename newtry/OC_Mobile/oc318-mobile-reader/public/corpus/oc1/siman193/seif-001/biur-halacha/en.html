And he must, etc. — and this is to disqualify (le'akeiva) if he does not know that he heard at least the fundamentals of the berachot and their openings and closings; then he fulfills b'dieved. And know further that if he heard birkas hamazon while dozing he does not fulfill [as Mishnah Berurah wrote in Beur HaGra].

he does not fulfill through hearing — behold the Mechaber's ruling discusses two [who ate together], as stated above. Regarding three: if one can discharge two — there are opinions among the poskim; the view of Birkat Avraham is that even per the Mechaber this is only regarding one discharging one individual; but in zimun of three where one discharges two, or ten where one discharges nine — even if they do not understand, one individual discharges the many. Many Acharonim copied this, and Bach in this siman and Levush in siman 199 imply not like his words.
