Seif 1 — three who ate may not divide. Marginal note: I was asked if initially they sat to eat with intent that they would be permitted to divide — is it permitted to divide? I answered that it appears to me plainly permitted to divide, for behold Magen Avraham s.k. 7 wrote that the Gemara implies that where one cannot discharge his fellow in birkas hamazon they may not invite zimun; and behold to discharge one another we certainly require that their intent be to join together. And also below in siman 195 it states in Shulchan Aruch: such as they entered with intent to join; and see Magen Avraham there, whose view is that even in one house we require they entered with intent to join. Therefore even per Taz who disagrees there — it is only that we do not require explicit intent to join; but when they entered explicitly with intent to divide — certainly they do not join perforce, and there is no other opinion; and this is clear and plain in his words; and to minimize dispute is preferable; examine.
