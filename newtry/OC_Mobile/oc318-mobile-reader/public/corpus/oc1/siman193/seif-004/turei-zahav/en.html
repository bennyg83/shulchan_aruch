They are not permitted to divide. It implies that even if one wants to finish his meal before the others finish — since the obligation of zimun already applied to him at the beginning of his eating; and it appears this is specifically when they began together; but if one preceded a little in his eating and the second came afterward and also ate, the first is permitted to finish his meal first and bless by himself; and so in Tosafot chapter Three Who Ate: they gave their minds to eat — meaning they began from the outset to eat together, then the obligation of zimun applied to them; and "if they ate a kezayit" — meaning one began before his fellow by a kezayit — then certainly if they finished their meal together they must invite zimun, but they are permitted to finish one without the other and bless — end of his words. And it is puzzling to me that Beit Yosef did not bring this distinction.
