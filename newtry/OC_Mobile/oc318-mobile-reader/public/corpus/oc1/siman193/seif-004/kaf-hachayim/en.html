(4) there — but if he does not understand, etc. — this applies to hearing: he does not fulfill if he does not understand; but the one blessing himself — even if he does not understand what he utters from his mouth — fulfills. Levush; Eshel Avraham there; Shulchan Aruch HaGavohar letter Alef on siman 13; and so above siman 124 letter Dalet — see there. And even though from Rosh's words there chapter 3 "who ate" and R' Yonah there it implies that even the one speaking with his mouth must understand — one can say this is l'chatchila, but when impossible he fulfills — see there. However Eshel Avraham letter Bet wrote to distinguish: in lashon kodesh when speaking he fulfills even when he does not understand the language; but in hearing, where one must intend, we require specifically lashon kodesh and understanding; and in another language, even the speaker must understand — see there. And so in Tosafot Sotah 32a — see there. And so above siman 62 letter Gimel and siman 101 letter Tet Zayin — see there.
