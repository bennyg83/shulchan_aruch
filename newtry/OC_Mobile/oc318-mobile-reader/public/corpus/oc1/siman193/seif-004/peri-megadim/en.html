And it appears to me. Taz — his proof goes in ot 3, and Rashi in Mishnah there likewise; but above in Gemara he wrote with six "if they wish they are permitted," see ot 3 from this one could say between twenty and six, and examine this.

It is plain to me. That which we taught: six may divide without one of them having blessed — if one blessed for himself they do not divide; even though in siman 494 seif 61 two can invite zimun upon the three who blessed for himself — that is b'dieved, but l'chatchila not; and all the more so if one of them invited zimun with two others. And in this Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Tosafeta: twenty may divide provided there is not among them one who is exempt from zimun — for zimun is with three, or he blessed for himself; even though seemingly all the more so in such a case with ten they invite zimun upon him — nevertheless l'chatchila they should not do so; except I saw in Hadrashah he wrote that if he blessed for himself they invite zimun upon him, see there — and he understands from his words even l'chatchila. But if they invited zimun as three, even though they are obligated with the Name, the separation agrees with Beit Yosef that again they do not invite zimun with ten upon them; and it appears even if three each blessed for himself, per separation they divide among twenty in such a case — and to Beit Yosef all the more not. And I saw in HaPanim Me'irot part 2 siman 16 he raised what the separation wrote; and we already wrote b'dieved certainly so, and even three who each blessed for himself — seven invite zimun upon them from the reason that it is no less than the merit of vegetables as siman 196 seif 2 "ate"; but l'chatchila to be dividing among twenty in such a case one could say not, as Beit Yosef wrote, and Eliyah Rabbah in siman 494 and 196 will be explained further.
