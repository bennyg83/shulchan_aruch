[2] [Levush] women, etc. — and above siman 185 seif 3 one must say it deals with an ignoramus who understands lashon kodesh but cannot bless; however Bach wrote and the custom is like Rashi, that in all matters women are obligated they rely on what they hear even though they do not understand at all — until here. And so ruled in Sefer Birkat Avraham daf 265 and Rabbenu Yerucham daf 202; and see below siman 199 s.k. 3.
