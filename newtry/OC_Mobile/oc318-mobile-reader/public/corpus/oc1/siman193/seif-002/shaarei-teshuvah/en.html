In zimun. Be'er Heitev in the name of Bach, and in Lekutei Peri Chadash ruled like Rambam that one does not invite zimun on fruits of the seven species; and he wrote in Be'er Heitev that the custom is simply to fix several people on fruits of seven species, and likewise to eat pat haba'ah bekisnin, and they are not careful about this — see there.
