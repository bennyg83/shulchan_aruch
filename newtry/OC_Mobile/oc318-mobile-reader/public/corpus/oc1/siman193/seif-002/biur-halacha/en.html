It is preferable to invite zimun — see Mishnah Berurah, and also in Beur HaGra it implies that per what the Mechaber ruled earlier, that even if the third came at the end of the meal and established with them he joins for zimun — we have no source for this law [from Rav and Shmuel on daf 47a, for there the law is they are obligated since they were fixed together at the end of the meal; and likewise from daf 53 that Beit Hillel hold one blesses for all is no proof, as Rosh wrote there]. And this view has a place only per Rabbenu Yonah, who holds that only when they began together are they obligated to invite zimun; if so it is difficult for him from Rav and Shmuel cited above, and likewise from the incident of King Dinai on daf 48 and Or Zarua, who resolves that permission to invite zimun applies in all cases; and so too in Sefer Magen Giborim.
