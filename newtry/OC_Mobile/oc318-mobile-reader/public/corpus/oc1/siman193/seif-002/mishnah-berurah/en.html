(18) That the two established — establishment is called when they sit and eat at one table or on one cloth, even if each one eats from his loaf, as explained above siman 167 seif 11. And see above there in seif 11 in Beur HaGra that his view there is that a ba'al habayit with the members of his household who sat to eat — that is establishment, and they join even without one table.

(19) At the end of eating — the obligation of zimun applies to them through this, that they ate together at the end; and it is implied from this: but if they did not also finish eating together — such as one came to eat with two after they had already begun eating, and they also finished their meal not at one time — those who finished earlier may bless by themselves, for in any case we require beginning or end together. Nevertheless, if they waited after their eating and did not bless until the third also finished eating, they are obligated to invite zimun and are not permitted to divide; and even though they did not finish together — wherever if one would bring them something they could eat from it, as if they finished together — we judge them thus; and similar to that which the head of siman 197 wrote, see there.

(20) And nevertheless "if" etc. — it is like "but" (in the Mechaber).

(21) Without establishment — meaning they did not eat in the manner of established sitting and on one table; and even if they began eating as three from the beginning of the meal until its end.

(22) He is an attendant — whose manner of eating is without established sitting; therefore he joins in every case.

(23) "That they are permitted to divide" — meaning the three ate without establishment; and likewise with twenty or six — even though by law they are permitted to divide, nevertheless it is better not to divide.

(24) Better to invite zimun — see Magen Avraham who challenged that from many poskim it appears regarding three who ate without establishment that one cannot discharge his fellow in birkas hamazon, and automatically also for zimun they are not permitted; and see there where he elaborated on this; and even though some Acharonim rejected his proof for zimun, nevertheless his words are more plausible; and so Peri Megadim also wrote that in a place where one cannot discharge his fellow in birkas hamazon, if they can join for zimun — requires further study for practice; and he concludes that with ten where there is mention of the Name, perhaps it is forbidden for all to invite zimun when they are not obligated; and so it appears in Chayei Adam general rule 48 law 7.
