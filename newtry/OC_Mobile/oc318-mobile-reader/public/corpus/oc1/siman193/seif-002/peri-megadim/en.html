If. See Taz. Regarding what the Rama writes in the gloss, that the one reciting the blessing must intend to discharge the listener, the listener likewise must intend to fulfill his obligation. This applies even to D’Rabbanan blessings, as stated in siman 213, seif 3. See Magen Avraham, siman 60, seif katan 3, who writes that intent is unnecessary for a D’Rabbanan obligation. One must distinguish between personally performing a mitzvah and hearing it from another. This distinction does not appear from Rosh Hashanah 29b; see there. See also what I wrote in Peri Megadim there, siman 60.
