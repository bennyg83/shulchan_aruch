(s.k. 6) And the ba'al habayit hears. Or else because, etc. — end of Rashba's novellae on Berachot daf 43 on this incident that occurred in the house of the Exilarch, where many were reclining and they blessed three by three. From this flows the law that here, etc.

And the Gemara said: and bless with him ten by ten. And it answers: R' Gamliel heard and resented. And Rashba wrote, end of his words: since if R' Gamliel heard, they would prevent [the others] until he blessed with them, and it would not be hearing where the whole world makes noise; and it is better for them to divide. And even if he would not prevent them, since he resented, it is disrespect of R' Gamliel.

And great is human dignity that pushes aside, etc. — and this is rabbinic, end of his words; and he means: even though we say that [human dignity] pushes aside a Torah prohibition, we conclude that R' Yitzchak [bar Avdimi] [means] not the prohibition of "you shall not turn aside," and this is specifically a rabbinic matter. Therefore here, that one may not divide three by three in order to bless with God's Name — this too is only rabbinic.
