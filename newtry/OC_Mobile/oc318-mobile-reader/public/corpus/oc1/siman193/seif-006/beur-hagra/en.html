Seif 6 — three, etc. — so explained R' Yehuda on his sugya; see there.

And the same law applies, etc. — with this it is settled that Rava's words in the Gemara refer to three men.

Three, etc. — so explained R' Hai Gaon: three who came, etc., are not permitted to divide from their first group, so that zimun not depart from their group; and this is what R' Chananel wrote — and he wrote, etc., to exclude if they came from a group of four; and this is what he wrote "but," etc.; and Rashba wrote, and one must say his girsa is R' Yitzchak — and if they invited zimun, zimun departs from them; and Lamed Dalet for what is written "these three from villages," etc. — for there this three did not fulfill zimun; and see siman 194 seif 1.
