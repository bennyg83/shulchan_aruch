(6) there — since all became obligated in zimun — meaning: even though if one goes away three remain and can invite zimun, nevertheless he does not fulfill his obligation, for he too became obligated in zimun. Magen Avraham s.k. 3.

(6) there — six may divide, etc. — meaning: if one of them did not yet bless; but if one blessed for himself they do not divide. And even though in siman 194 seif 1 it is written that two may invite zimun upon the three who blessed for himself — meaning b'dieved; but l'chatchila not; and all the more so if one of them invited zimun with two others. And so the law regarding twenty who divide — if one of them did not bless for himself or invite zimun with others — from Taz letter Dalet.
