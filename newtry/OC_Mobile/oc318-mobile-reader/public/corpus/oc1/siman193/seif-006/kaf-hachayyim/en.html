6) There: For they all became obligated in Zimun. This means that although if one leaves, three will remain and make a Zimun, the one who leaves will not fulfill his obligation, because he too became obligated in Zimun. Magen Avraham, paragraph 3.
6) There: Six may divide, etc. This applies if none of them recited the blessing. If one already recited it for himself, they may not divide. Although siman 194, seif 1, states that two may make a Zimun with a third who already recited the blessing for himself, that applies after the fact, but initially they may not do so. This is certainly true if one of them already joined two others in Zimun. Likewise, the rule that twenty may divide applies only if none of them recited the blessing for himself or joined others in Zimun. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 4.
