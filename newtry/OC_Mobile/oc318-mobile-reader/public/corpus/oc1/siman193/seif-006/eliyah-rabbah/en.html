[6] it is preferable, etc. — Magen Avraham explained: they should eat a little at one table in order to become obligated in zimun; for otherwise they may not invite zimun, since each must bless birkas hamazon for himself. And he proved this from Rav's students who tore their garments; and it was difficult for them to invite zimun together and bless each for himself — but they may not invite zimun — until here his words briefly. In my humble opinion this is no proof, for he himself wrote above that they must hear birkas hamazon from the mouth of the one blessing when inviting zimun; if so they were in doubt regarding birkas hamazon. Rather the difficulty is: they should have blessed in a whisper and heard, as he wrote in siman 183 — but he does not hold thus; examine. Also what he inferred from siman 195 is no proof — one can say there he speaks from the side of obligation, but from the side of hiddur it is preferable, as Taz wrote.
