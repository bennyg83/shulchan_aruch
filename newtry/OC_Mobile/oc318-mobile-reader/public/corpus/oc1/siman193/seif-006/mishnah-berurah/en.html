(33) Zimun departed from them — and with this specifically if they did not eat afterward together; but if they ate afterward together they combine; and it is not similar to the case of seif 5, for there they did not invite zimun upon them previously [Chiddushei Rabbi Akiva Eiger in the name of Terumat Yisrael].

(34) Since their companions invited zimun — see Biur Halachah.

(35) And one came etc. — meaning one from the market ate a little at the end of their meal and joined with them; and the language "for every group" that the Mechaber wrote is not precise, for even for only one group — if so zimun departed from one and only two remain here.

(36) It is permitted to separate etc. — and it deals when all the groups were initially also in this house, only they did not join together; for otherwise each one must bless birkas hamazon in his place.
