Se’if 1: “One should strengthen himself… for the service…” As it says in Avot, chapter 5: “Be… strong like a lion to do [the will of your Father in Heaven].”
“Like a lion to rise.” Midrash Rabbah on Parashat Balak: “Behold, a people rises like a lion…”—they rise from their sleep like lions, seize the recitation of Shema, and proclaim the Holy One, blessed be He, as King; and their deeds are like lions, etc.
“So that he should…” Yerushalmi, Berakhot ch. 1: “The dawn would not awaken him; rather, David would awaken it,” as he said: “Awake, my glory… I will awaken the dawn.” The evil inclination would prosecute him; therefore it says “he should strengthen himself,” etc. See the Taz.
“In any case…” As it says in Berakhot 8a about “a favorable time,” and likewise in Avot: “sleep of the morning… removes a person from the world.” And in Avot d’Rabbi Natan ch. 21: “sleep of the morning”—this teaches that a person should not plan to sleep until the time of Shema has passed, etc.; and Rashi explains there on Avot similarly.
“I have set [Hashem]…” A great principle in the Torah, as in Berakhot 63b: Bar Kappara expounded…
“And among the virtues of the righteous who…” As it says: “Noach was a righteous man… he walked with God”; and likewise regarding Chanoch; and: “with whom my fathers walked before Him” (Bereishit 48:15)—this is the entire virtue of the righteous.
“For a person’s manner of sitting…” Immediately it will reach him, as in Berakhot 28b: “May the fear of Heaven be upon you…” and “If only you knew…”
“Nor is his speech…” Berakhot 61a: “A person’s words should always be few before the Holy One, blessed be He,” etc.
“How much more so when a person takes to heart…” Avot ch. 2: “Reflect on three things…”; and Chagigah 16a (R. Yitzchak) regarding one who transgresses; and Kiddushin 31a; see Rashi there s.v. “and not…”; and this is what it means: “He stands over him.” Also Bava Kamma 79b.
“Immediately… and shame…” Nedarim 20a: “It was taught: ‘in order that His fear be upon your face’… ‘so that you do not sin’…” etc.
“Always.” As explained above on “I have set [Hashem]…”
“And he should not be ashamed.” As it says: “Be bold as a leopard,” etc. See Tur. Here it says regarding service of Hashem; but in general they said: “the brazen-faced go to Gehinnom,” and in Chagigah ch. 2, “these are the brazen-faced of the generation,” and in Tractate Kallah, etc.
“Even in walking modestly…” Sukkah 49b: “He has told you, man…”—and it is a kal va’chomer, etc.; meaning: even if he is not ashamed, doing what he can in a concealed way is preferable.
“And when lying on his bed…” Meaning: even in this he should be modest, as in Shabbat 118b (“my roof beams never saw…”), and at the beginning of the fifth chapter of Yoma.
“And immediately… he should rise.” As it says: “Swift as an eagle to do [the will]…”
