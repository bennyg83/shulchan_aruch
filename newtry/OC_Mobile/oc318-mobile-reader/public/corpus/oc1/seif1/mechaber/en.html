Laws of rising early in the morning. It contains nine se’ifim:
One should strengthen himself like a lion to rise in the morning for the service of his Creator so that he is the one who awakens the dawn: Rema: In any case, he must not delay beyond the time of prayer at which the congregation prays (Tur). Rema: “I have set Hashem before me always” is a great principle in the Torah and among the virtues of the righteous who walk before God: for a person’s manner of sitting, moving, and conducting his affairs when he is alone at home is not like his manner of sitting, moving, and conducting his affairs when he is before a great king; nor is his speech and casual talk as he wishes when he is with the members of his household and his relatives like his speech in the king’s court—how much more so when a person takes to heart that the great King, the Holy One, blessed be He, whose glory fills the entire earth, stands over him and observes his deeds, as it is said, “Can a man hide in secret places and I will not see him?”—the word of Hashem; immediately fear, humility, awe of Hashem, and shame before Him come upon him constantly (Guide for the Perplexed, Part III, ch. 52). And he should not be ashamed before people who mock him in the service of Hashem. Even in walking modestly, and when lying on his bed, he should know before whom he lies. And as soon as he awakens from sleep he should rise promptly for the service of his Creator, may He be blessed, exalted, and uplifted (Tur):
