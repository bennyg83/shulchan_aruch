Seif 1 In Shulchan Aruch, "even in modest privacy" (behatznea lechet) would seem to mean that he should act modestly to fulfill what they said, "walk modestly with your God," etc.; see Sukkah 49b and Rashi there. But in Shlah, pamphlet Asarah Ma'amarot, it is also [said] when he sits at home and when he lies down, etc. It appears somewhat that he explains: even when he is at home in modest privacy, his "walk" shall be with the Lord his God, as it says in siman 2: even though he is in inner chambers he should not say "Who sees me," etc.; yet what is written here, "in modest privacy," does not sound like that:
