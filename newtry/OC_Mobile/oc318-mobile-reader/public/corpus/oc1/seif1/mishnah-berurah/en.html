(1) For the service of his Creator—for that is why man was created, as Scripture says: "Everyone who is called by My name, for My glory I created him," etc. And even if his inclination tempts him in winter to say: How will you stand in the morning when the cold is great; or tempts him in summer to say: How will you rise from your bed when you have not yet had your fill of sleep?—he must strengthen himself against it and not listen, and think in his soul: If he had to stand to serve before a human king, how careful and prompt he would be to rise early to prepare himself for his service; how much more so, and by an a fortiori whose a fortiori follows from it, before the King of kings, the Holy One, blessed be He.
