Taz seif katan 1 Meaning, the binding, etc. This is difficult, for this very law from the Magen Avraham was brought by the Beis Yosef in siman 653 and in Shulchan Aruch, and they wrote 'but he did not lift it to fulfill the mitzvah with it,' which implies that even if he bound it, it is permitted. If so, although here in the Beis Yosef it says 'and he did not bind it,' it appears to be a scribal error, and it should read: 'but he did not lift it.' This also follows from the phrase 'to fulfill the mitzvah with it.' This requires investigation. So too the Peri Megadim asked.
