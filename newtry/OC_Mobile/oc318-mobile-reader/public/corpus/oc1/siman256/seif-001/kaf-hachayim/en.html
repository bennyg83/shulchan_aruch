(א) [Seif 1] When Israel was in their settlements they would blow on Erev Shabbat, etc. — It appears that if one now wishes in this era to practice in his city to blow in order to separate the people from melacha, he is permitted; only what was discontinued was due to fear of the nations, and now it is not as in years past — in every city they may do as the ancient enactment, for even abroad it was thus, as Maaseh Rokeach chapter 5 of Maaseh Heaven din 18, see there. And in the sin of the other nations they have a great chain that strikes with a cane called "campana" that clangs and is heard from afar — why should Israel be less than them, not to enact in order to separate the people from melacha on Shabbatot and Yom Tov and to time the three prayers and the like as was in the Temple. Ruach Chaim, letter 1.
