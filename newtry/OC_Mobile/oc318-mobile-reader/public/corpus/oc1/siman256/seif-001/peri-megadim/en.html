(א) When they were — Israel in their settlements is the Mechaber's wording and Rambam's wording, chapter 5 of Maaseh Heaven 18: every province and town of Israel, etc. In the halachah 21, and 31 in what he wrote they blow, and Yom Tov Erev Shabbat they blow; and Magen Avraham that there are disputants in what he wrote they did not blow — and now the correct practice is to call barechu in Maaseh Heaven so they know that before that melacha is forbidden. Yom Tov Erev Shabbat: it is appropriate to advance calling barechu or accepting Shabbat while the day is great, as Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of siman 527, that close to nightfall it is forbidden by Torah law to cook from Yom Tov for Shabbat, for the reason of "since" does not apply; in Tashbetz Katan one should be careful to put into the oven that it be fit to eat from before the day at least like ben drusai, and as we need to say below in siman 299 in Magen Avraham on the laws of leaving food and insulating — on this see there.

(ב) And know that in the Gemara Shabbat 35b: one waits enough to roast a small fish or to attach bread in the oven; Rashi explained there is a wait until the faces scorch, for they would hurry to add from weekday onto the holy. Rambam translated there, and the separation see there. What the separation wrote — that faces scorch before nightfall — means before accepting Shabbat; there is a difference of opinion whether doubtful Torah law is strict: nevertheless it is certain except that one is not lashed for it; it is obvious that this is so even for one who holds it is rabbinic — nevertheless for karet one may say it is Torah law; and in any case one may say such a doubt was rabbinic, a doubt of lack of wisdom and knowledge was somewhat present, and I will explain further in siman 261. What he wrote — waiting enough to roast a small fish — he holds the measure; in Bach I saw written to say like ben drusai, and I did not merit to understand his words, for Rashi explained in Tosafot Shabbat there is no concern, as stated; and what is difficult, how does one ride on Shabbat — see Chayei Adam here; and nevertheless there, and in siman 254 seif 5, attaching when permitted is permitted, and in our ovens more than three meals is permitted; and Magen Avraham there note 16 and note 23 — in a manner we find to learn that all they said in siman 253 and 254 requires from before the day — in Tosafot Shabbat it is permitted, and in accepting Shabbat forbidden, as stated.

(ג) And in siman 261 it will be explained there how much is tosefet Shabbat and whether it is Torah law; and regarding Yom Tov too whether it is Torah law; and if one accepted Shabbat while the day is great, whether it is acceptance. Also this requires further study: the baker on Yom Tov for a non-Jew's bread and the like — whether it is permitted before nightfall without scorching faces from before the day, for perhaps they did not decree all those in siman 253 and 254 except on Shabbat where there is karet and stoning, not on Yom Tov where the prohibition is only a negative commandment — this requires further study now, and there. It will be explained in chapter 5 of Yom Tov more on this.
