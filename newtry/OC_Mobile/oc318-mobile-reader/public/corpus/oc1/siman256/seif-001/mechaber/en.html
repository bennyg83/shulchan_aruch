The six tekiyot that they would blow on Erev Shabbat. And it has
one seif: When Israel was in their settlements they would blow on Erev Shabbat six tekiyot in order to separate the people from melacha.And the practice in holy communities is that whatever is close to Shabbat, about half an hour or an hour, the shamash announces that they should prepare themselves for Shabbat, and this is in place of the tekiyot in their days; and so it is appropriate to practice in every place.
