(א) Close to Shabbat — meaning per Tosafot Shabbat "garments of Yom Tov"; Bach wrote half an hour close they announce. Magen Avraham wrote it is appropriate to practice sending a man two and a half hours before night to stop those engaged in melacha from their work; also shopkeepers should close their shops about an hour before Shabbat, for I have seen the great stumbling-block that sometimes one official comes and bargaining continues.
