Close to Shabbos. Malbushei Yom Tov explains this as close to the time one adds to Shabbos. Bach writes that the announcement is made half an hour beforehand. Magen Avraham writes that it is proper to send a person two and a half hours before nightfall to stop craftsmen from their work, and also to close the shops about an hour before Shabbos, because he saw the serious stumbling block that sometimes an important official arrives and business negotiations continue.
