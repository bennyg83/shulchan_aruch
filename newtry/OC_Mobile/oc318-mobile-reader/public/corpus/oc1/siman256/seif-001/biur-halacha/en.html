And it is in place of the tekiyot — see in Peri Megadim what he writes in the name of Rambam. And since it is in place of the tekiyot, therefore even when Erev Shabbat falls on Yom Tov one should also announce as in former times when they would blow tekiyot, as our sages expounded; see in Peri Megadim who wrote that on Yom Tov Erev Shabbat it is appropriate to advance saying barechu or accepting Shabbat while the day is still great, as Magen Avraham wrote at the beginning of siman 527, that close to nightfall it is forbidden by Torah law to cook from Yom Tov for Shabbat, for the reason of "since" does not apply. And in Tashbetz Katan they put food from before the day into the oven for Shabbat needs — one should be careful to put it from before the day enough that it be fit to eat at least like the food of ben drusai, see there.
