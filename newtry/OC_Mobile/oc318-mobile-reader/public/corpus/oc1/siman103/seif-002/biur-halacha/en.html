And see above in siman 85 — see in Mishna Berurah what he wrote in the name of the Bach. And apparently per this, if he paused in Keriat Shema in order to finish it entirely, and the pause was due to duress — such as he went to the bathroom to attend to his needs — we rule above in siman 65 that he returns to the beginning; the pause is not counted within the measure, only the time he went to the bathroom, for at that time he was not fit to recite, and also the time he was in the bathroom; but the time he went afterward from the bathroom to his house — it is possible we cannot combine, for then he was not truly under duress, for if he wished he could wipe his hands with earth and a clod and finish Keriat Shema, per what we rule that netilat yadayim was not established except in the morning, when a new creature is made — as above in siman 4 in Magen Avraham, note 1; and this requires study.
