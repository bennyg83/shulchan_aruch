(2) If one needs to pass gas from below and is suffering greatly, (3) (3) and cannot contain himself, he walks (4) four cubits backward and releases the gas, and waits until the smell dissipates from him, (5) and says: "Master of the worlds — You have created us with holes upon holes, cavities upon cavities; revealed and known before You are our disgrace and our shame, disgrace and shame in our life, worm and maggot in our death." (6) He then returns to his place, (4) and returns (7) to the place where he stopped.(8) And see above siman 85. There are those who say that all of this applies only when one is praying at home; but when praying with the congregation, which would be a great embarrassment for him, one need not distance himself backward at all, (9) and one should also not say the "Master," rather one waits until the smell dissipates from him, and so is the custom. (Terumat HaDeshen, siman 16.).
