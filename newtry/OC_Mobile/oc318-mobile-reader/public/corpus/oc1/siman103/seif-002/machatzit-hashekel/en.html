(Seif katan 2) Behind him, etc. For if he turns his face away, it will appear, etc. For this reason Beit Yosef writes that he should also not move to the sides. His words are: “It is evident that moving to the sides, since his face is no longer directed precisely toward the place where he began praying, resembles one who stops entirely, as though he does not intend to return to his prayer.”
