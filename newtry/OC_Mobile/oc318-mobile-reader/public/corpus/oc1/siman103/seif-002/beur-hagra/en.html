Seif 2: And he is distressed, etc. Rambam. See above, siman 92, seif 2, regarding one who, etc.; all the more so in a case such as this.
And he returns, etc. See Beer HaGolah. In our Gemara this is stated explicitly, and the Bach writes likewise. See above, siman 104, seifim 5 and 6.
Some say, etc. See Magen Avraham, seifim katanim 4 and 5. This also appears from the Gemara, for in the first case, in seif 1, he does not say, “Master, etc.” See chapter 3 of Berachos concerning one who enters a banquet, etc. It follows that this is disgraceful, and all the more so in a case such as this. Nevertheless, the primary ruling is as written in Beit Yosef, that it is very difficult to devise distinctions based on reasoning alone, etc.; see there.
