[3] And he says, “Master of all the worlds,” etc. This follows what Beit Yosef wrote, that we rule like Rambam against the Tur. The Gemara, however, clearly supports the Tur, that he first returns to his place and afterward says the prayer beginning “Master.” The Bach rules likewise, and this is the primary ruling, for I found it so in Semak, siman 57, Shibolei HaLeket, Sefer Tanya, and Shelah. As to why the Sages did not institute such a prayer for one whose urine is flowing, in siman 78, Rabbeinu Yonah answers that he was initially negligent, since urine does not ordinarily come so suddenly had he examined himself. The prayer beginning “Master” is an apology for duress, not for negligence.
