And he returns and prays: See Mishnah Berurah. Baer Heitev quotes the Peri Chadash as ruling that he must return to the beginning, but this quotation is not correct. The Peri Chadash follows his own view, ruling in siman 65 in accordance with the Rif that in prayer one returns to the beginning even without duress. According to our ruling, however, which follows the Rama there, the law accords with the Magen Avraham. [Maamar Mordechai and Magen Gibborim.]
