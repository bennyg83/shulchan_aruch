(1) The smell — for it is forbidden from the outset even in matters of Torah, as stated above in siman 79 seif 9.

(2) And he returns and prays — that is, to the place where he stopped, even if he delayed after the smell dissipated and through the combined time it amounted to the duration needed to complete the entire prayer. For had he wished, he could have prayed immediately once the smell stopped, and therefore not all of the interruption was due to compulsion, and he does not return to the beginning, in accordance with the ruling of the Rama above in siman 65. The achronim wrote that in this case one need not say the "Master" written in seif 2, for when one does not distance himself, his shame and disgrace are not apparent — similar to what is written in the Rama's gloss below in seif 2.
