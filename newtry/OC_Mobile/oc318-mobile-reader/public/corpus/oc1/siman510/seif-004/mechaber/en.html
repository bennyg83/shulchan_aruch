One may not hang a strainer on Yom Tov to filter sediment through it; but if it was already hanging, one may pour sediment into it to strain. One may use a ruse — hang it to put pomegranates in it, and then put sediment in it. {Rama: Rema: The other laws of straining on Yom Tov are the same as on Shabbat, as stated in §319. (Beit Yosef)}
