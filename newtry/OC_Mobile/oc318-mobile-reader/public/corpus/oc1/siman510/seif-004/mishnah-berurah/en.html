One may not hang. That is, stretching the mouth of the strainer over a vessel in a circle so that it becomes like a tent over the hollow of the vessel; and therefore they forbade it, for it is like a weekday labor.
To strain them. And this deals with a case where if he had done it from yesterday it would not have been so good; for otherwise it is forbidden because of selecting, as regarding mustard above in seif 3. And Peri Megadim wrote that through an alteration it is permitted in every case.
And he may employ a stratagem and hang, etc. Meaning: even if it was not hanging from before Yom Tov, he may employ a stratagem and hang it in order to place pomegranates in it at first.
To place, etc. And he puts pomegranates into it at first; but when he does not put pomegranates into it, the matter is evident that he is doing it because of the lees, and it is forbidden.
On Yom Tov as on Shabbos. He alludes here to what is explained above there in seif 10: that it is forbidden to strain water in a cloth because of laundering; and see there in Mishnah Berurah; and the same applies here.
