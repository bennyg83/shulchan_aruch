And he blows little by little and eats — meaning the seeds, after they were separated from the stalks and are still mixed with chaff, it is permitted to shake them from one hand to the other hand and to blow the chaff from them; and he should not blow much at once, because it appears like blowing for the sake of a weekday, but he blows little by little and eats immediately (R' O. bet).
