It is permitted to put dregs in it to strain them — even though it is a complete melacha, for on Shabbat one is liable for chatat on this, as stated in the Gemara; on Yom Tov it is permitted because it is ochel nefesh. And even though above borer through nafah and sieve is forbidden — there, because through these vessels the way is to do for many days, and it is like harvesting and grinding; but here, even though it is through a vessel, the way is to do it for that hour.
