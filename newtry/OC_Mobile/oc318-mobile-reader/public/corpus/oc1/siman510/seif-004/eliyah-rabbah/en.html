[ד] [Levush] It is permitted to select them, etc. Even to borer refuse from food, since it is one species and there is no refuse upon it — it is merely a general food preparation and there is no melacha upon it (Maharshal there), and so it is proven from Rama who wrote the law of nuts etc.; also the Shulchan Aruch holds that in all types of refuse it is permitted to select them; and Olas Shabbat and the Taz and Magen Avraham disagreed that nevertheless there is [a concern] due to extra exertion; and to my humble opinion, since it is only food preparation we are not concerned for exertion — for specifically where there is refuse upon it do we concern ourselves for this, and that is what he wrote 'and there is no melacha upon it.' I also found proof from Rabbeinu Yerucham who ruled like the Shulchan Aruch and also wrote the law of nuts — consider it. And furthermore it appears to me that possibly the intent of Maharshal and Rama and R' Yehuda is where refuse is great in quantity and few in exertion — then he says it is permitted with nuts to borer refuse, even though in other refuse one must borer the food, as I wrote in the name of Ran and R' Yehuda above. And behold Magen Avraham challenged from the end of siman 321 that one who peels garlic and onions is borer; and it appears to me there is no difficulty at all, for there it deals with one who does so in order to leave it — in this case here as well it is forbidden; but when one peels to eat immediately it is permitted even to borer the refuse, as one peels the shell. Yam Shel Shlomo wrote there that it is forbidden to leave legumes or other seeds in water so that the refuse floats to the top or the dirt falls to the bottom; and so it appears from Beit Yosef (Magen Avraham); and requires investigation — for possibly Rambam agrees when there is refuse only dirt, that then one takes the food from it and leaves the dirt below, for the essence of borer is only in refuse. Also one must examine in Yerushalmi that which we taught in Beitzah there 'he selects in his usual manner in his lap' — R' Chanina said this is Rabban Gamliel, for Rabban Gamliel says one may even rinse and scoop — it implies Rabban Gamliel does not disagree with the first tanna; and this supports Rabbeinu Yerucham whom Beit Yosef brought; and requires investigation regarding Rambam; however in siman 319 seif 8 it implies to forbid, and it is a mishnah on Shabbos daf 140 — and one can distinguish, requires investigation.
