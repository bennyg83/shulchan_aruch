[4] [Levush] It is permitted to select them, etc. Even to select refuse from food, since it is one kind and there is no name of refuse upon it: it is mere food-preparation and there is no name of melacha upon it (Maharshal there). And so it is proven from the Rama, who wrote the law of almonds, etc.; also the Shulchan Aruch holds that in all kinds of refuse it is permitted to select them. And Olas Shabbos and the Taz and Magen Avraham disagreed, that nevertheless there is a concern of extra toil. And to my humble opinion, since it is only food-preparation we are not concerned about toil, for specifically where there is a name of refuse upon it do we concern ourselves for this; and that is what he wrote, "and there is no name of melacha upon it." I also found proof from Rabbeinu Yerucham, who likewise ruled like the Shulchan Aruch and also wrote the law of almonds; consider carefully. And further it appears to me that possibly the intent of Maharshal and the Rama and Rabbeinu Yerucham is where refuse is great in quantity and few in toil: then he says that with almonds it is permitted to select refuse, even though with other refuse one must select the food, as I wrote in the name of the Ran and Rabbeinu Yerucham above. And behold Magen Avraham challenged from the end of siman 321 that one who peels garlic and onions is selecting; and it appears to me there is no difficulty at all, for there it deals with one who does so in order to leave it; in that case here as well it is forbidden; but when one peels to eat immediately it is permitted even to select the refuse, as one peels the shell. Yam Shel Shlomo wrote there that it is forbidden to place legumes or other seeds in water so that the refuse will float to the top or the dirt will fall below; and so it appears from Beis Yosef (Magen Avraham). And this requires investigation: for possibly the Rambam agrees when there is no refuse but only dirt, that then one takes the food from it and leaves the dirt below, for the essence of selecting is only regarding refuse. Also one must examine in the Yerushalmi that which we taught in Beitzah there, "he selects in his usual manner in his lap": R' Chanina said this is Rabban Gamliel, for Rabban Gamliel says one may even rinse and scoop; it implies that Rabban Gamliel does not disagree with the first tanna; and this supports Rabbeinu Yerucham whom Beis Yosef brought. And this requires investigation regarding the Rambam; however in siman 319, seif 8, it implies to forbid, and it is a mishnah on Shabbos, folio 140; and one can distinguish; this requires investigation.
