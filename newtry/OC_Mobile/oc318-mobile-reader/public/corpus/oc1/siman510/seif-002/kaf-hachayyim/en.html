7) [Se'if 2] One who selects legumes on Yom Tov. This refers to legumes mixed with dirt or with their chaff. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 3.
8) There. He selects in his usual manner, etc. When the food is more abundant, he removes the refuse from the food. Olas Shabbos, subsection 2.
9) There, in the gloss. If he wishes to eat it that day. This means that it is permitted even if he does not wish to eat it immediately. Its law resembles the permission to bake and cook for consumption that day. Olas Shabbos there; Magen Avraham, subsection 2. Magen Avraham writes that this is unlike Rashba, who forbids selecting for consumption later that day. See there. Yeshuos Yaakov, subsection 1, and Maamar Mordechai, subsection 1, write likewise. Maamar Mordechai disputes Taz, subsection 3, who agrees with Rashba, and Chemed Moshe, subsection 1, and Nehar Shalom, subsection 1, likewise dispute Taz. See there. Chayei Adam, rule 82, subsection 2, and Mishnah Berurah, subsection 7, rule accordingly. See Yeshuos Yaakov, siman 500, subsection 2, who concludes that the law of selecting on Yom Tov is more lenient than on Shabbos specifically when removing refuse from food. Regarding two types of food, however, the law on Yom Tov is the same as on Shabbos, and selecting is permitted only for immediate consumption, not for later. See there for the reason. Mikraei Kodesh there, Likutei Rima, subsection 2; Tosafos Chaim on Chayei Adam there, subsection 1; and Piskei HaShulchan on Kitzur Shulchan Aruch, siman 98, subsection 9, cite this.
