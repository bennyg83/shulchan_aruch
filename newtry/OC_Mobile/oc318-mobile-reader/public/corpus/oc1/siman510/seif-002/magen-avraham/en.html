(1) If he wants, etc. It is difficult: what does it teach us — that even plain preparation is forbidden to prepare from Yom Tov to a weekday, all the more so selecting; therefore it appears to me it must read: even if he wants to eat it on that same day. And this too is obvious: when he wants to eat immediately it is permitted, but even if he wants to eat it after some time on that same day — for on Shabbat in such a case he is liable for chatat, siman 319 — nevertheless on Yom Tov it is permitted, not like Rashba, who forbids selecting to leave for later that day; and likewise explicitly in Maggid Mishneh chapter 3, and in siman 506 seif 2.

(3) And lupines, etc. It requires further study, for Tosafos wrote so much per his view, that if the refuse is abundant it is forbidden to move it; and on this he wrote lupines are permitted to move. But Shulchan Aruch ruled like the Rambam, that always they are permitted to move, only there is a distinction in selecting; and if so, what is different about lupines and nuts from other species — for even with two types of food selecting applies, as written siman 319; and in Yeshuos Shlomo he wrote, and these are his words: indeed it appears it is not similar to selecting food from food — meaning because they are two species, or refuse and food; but here it is one species and there is no refuse on it, and in whatever way he fixes the food from the guard it is plain fixing of food and there is no melacha on it, end of his words. And it requires further study, for the Beit Yosef wrote in siman 321 in the name of Yerushalmi: one who peels garlic and onions is doing borer; and one may ask: since we are concerned for exertion, why is it not considered exertion regarding lupines? Therefore it appears to me that also with lupines he selects in whatever way is easier to select. The Yeshuos Shlomo wrote it is forbidden to leave legumes or other seeds in water so the refuse floats up or the dirt falls down — and this is the view of the Rav Beit Yosef, since they omitted it in Shulchan Aruch.
