(1) Selecting legumes — From here there is a difficulty on this view stated above in siman 506 seif 2 in the gloss that it is forbidden to take a pebble by hand, whereas here it is explained that on Yom Tov one may select in the usual way; and the Taz already hinted at this there, and likewise the article of the Mordechai and Nehar Shalom expanded the difficulty there, and likewise the Gra stood on this, and in Mishna Berurah there I explained the reason for this view — because regarding a pebble, even on a weekday the way is to take it without a vessel, and it is a fortiori; see there. And the Gra wrote to distinguish that the Sages did not permit selecting in the usual way except for legumes that are not normally prepared for many days, but not wheat that is normally prepared for many days [and I found some support for this in Beitzah daf 13, to distinguish between grain and legumes; see there in Rashi s.v. aval ketanos that wrote there are many places where they do not beat them together except for the amount of their pot, and the Ra'ah wrote there, and these are his words: that there is no way to prepare all of them as one except for the amount of his pot, end of his words — and it follows that selecting too is only for his day; and apparently there is proof for this also from the words of the Rambam chapter 1 halacha 7, that when he counted there also borer among the melachot the Sages forbade on Yom Tov, he mentioned wheat there; and one may reject that his intent there is through a vessel, which is forbidden, as the Shulchan Aruch ruled, but not by hand, in a sieve and winnowing basket — and wheat that he listed is not precise]. And he wrote that thus it is explained in Shmuel Kohen at the beginning of the chapter Ein Tzadin in the name of the Ramban; and therefore with grain it would be forbidden to select even by hand.

(2) If he wants to eat it on that same day — see Mishna Berurah; the reason is that it is like a baker and cook, and so is the view of Magen Avraham and other Acharonim in the name of the Maggid Mishneh, that borer is among the melachot fully permitted on Yom Tov, not like the view of the Rashba. And behold the Taz sides with the halacha per the view of the Rashba that only immediately is permitted but not to leave it, since on Shabbat one is liable for chatat, it is forbidden on Yom Tov; and the later authorities wondered at him [Peri Megadim, Beit Meir, Nehar Shalom, and others] — for per what we establish above in siman 319, that on Shabbat if one selects refuse from food even immediately he is liable — if so, necessarily, since the Sages permitted on Yom Tov to select refuse from food, it is because borer does not apply at all on Yom Tov, as it is like a baker and cook, per the view of the Maggid Mishneh [and the Rashba — it is possible he follows his view that holds even on Shabbat if one selects immediately he is not liable for chatat, as implied in Avodas HaKodesh]; and if so, even to leave it for later that day is permitted. However, what he raised that one must forbid because it was possible to do from the day before — apparently the law agrees with him in this, for thus it is explained in the Rama siman 495 seif 3 per the view of those who are stringent, that even for ochel nefesh itself, whatever is possible to do from erev Yom Tov is forbidden except through a change; and if so, in our matter, that which they permit refuse from food is specifically when he could not select from erev Yom Tov, and likewise in truth wrote the Chamed Moshe [except that he wrote immediately is permitted in every case like the Taz, and this is not understood, for on Shabbat refuse from food even immediately one is liable]. However, from the conclusion of the Shulchan Aruch and Rama it appears one may be lenient in our matter in every case; and the reason, it is possible in my humble opinion to say, that since through a change the Rama wrote there it is permitted, and here where otherwise they permitted only through a sieve and winnowing basket — one may say this is considered a change, for in the usual way is specifically through nafah and sieve, and even though this is not a complete change, for on Shabbat in refuse from food one is liable even with this, nevertheless for Yom Tov it is considered a change, and all the more so if one selects by hand, like what the Ran wrote at the beginning of the chapter Ein Tzadin regarding salt; see there. Afterwards I found that thus wrote Nehar Shalom and Shulchan Atzei Shittim, and likewise in Avodas HaKodesh to the Rashba it also appears this is considered not in the usual way; and likewise it is proven in the Meiri, who wrote on this matter that even if possible from erev Yom Tov it is permitted, and his view is explained in many places in his book that he holds like the view of those who are stringent mentioned above, that even for ochel nefesh it is forbidden d'rabbanan whatever is possible from the day before; and therefore, as we wrote, this is considered a change. The conclusion from our words: those accustomed to be lenient to select legumes, refuse from food, even though it was possible from the day before, have whom to rely on; and even in selecting to leave for another meal [or on erev Shabbat for Shabbat and through eruv tavshilin], and nevertheless l'chatchila it is proper in such a case, when it was possible for him from the day before to select the food from the refuse, for this is certainly considered a change, and as it seems the world practices.
