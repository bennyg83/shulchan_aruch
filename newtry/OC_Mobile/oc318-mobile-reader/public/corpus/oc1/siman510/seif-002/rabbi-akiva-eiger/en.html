Magen Avraham seif zayin: for he does not burn and there is no extinguishing; and because of hardening there is none per the poskim, for hardening is d'rabbanan — it may be said because of the joy of Yom Tov they were lenient, as they were lenient in extinguishing of metal; and per the poskim that hardening is d'oraisa, it must be said it deals with wood that is not psik reisha; and for the Rambam chapter 12 of the laws of Shabbat — even though it is psik reisha, nevertheless since he does not intend to make a vessel from it, there is no concern of hardening; see in Hagahos Maimoniyot there and in Magen Avraham above siman 318 seif 136, and Hagahos Maimoniyot there per the Ramban answered: mustard does not cause hardening. R' Akiva.
