[ב] One who wants to eat them, etc. Meaning even though on Shabbos there is permission only immediately, Yom Tov is different for it is like one who cooks and it is permitted the entire day — and this is from the words of the Maggid, who disagrees with Rashba who forbids even on Yom Tov; and the Taz agreed with Rashba because ochel nefesh that could be done from yesterday is forbidden, as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 506 regarding 'we do not sift flour' etc., and he wondered at the Maggid. And to my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all, for it is proven in siman 506 that Rashba holds that even when possible from yesterday it is permitted — for he ruled that one may sift flour; and that which we do not winnow is because it is for a long period; nevertheless in siman 695 it is explained that we hold thus only through a change is it permitted. Furthermore the Taz wrote that Maharil who wrote that one should not borer matza crumbs on Pesach, separating large from small — he holds like Rashba; and from Maharshal chapter 1 of Beitzah siman 42, when he disagrees with Maharil, the words of Rashba were hidden from him — up to here. One could say behold Maharshal there brings the words of Rashba, only he wrote that the Maggid's words incline [to leniency]; also the Taz's other words can be dismissed; nevertheless one should be stringent, for so it appears in Sefer Tanya and from Rabbeinu Yerucham daf 31; and likewise Magen Avraham s.k. 9 in siman 504 was stringent like Maharil.
