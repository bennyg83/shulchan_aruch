[2] That he wants to eat them, etc. Meaning: even though on Shabbos there is permission only immediately, Yom Tov is different, for it is like one who cooks and it is permitted the entire day; and this is from the words of the Maggid Mishneh, who disagrees with the Rashba, who forbids even on Yom Tov. And the Taz agreed with the Rashba because ochel nefesh that could be done from yesterday is forbidden, as Beis Yosef wrote in siman 506 regarding "one may not sift flour a second time," etc., and he wondered at the Maggid Mishneh. And to my humble opinion there is no difficulty at all, for it is proven in siman 506 that the Rashba holds that even when possible from yesterday it is permitted, for he ruled that one may sift flour; and that which one may not winnow is because it is for a long period. Nevertheless, in siman 495 it is explained that we hold thus: only through an alteration is it permitted. Furthermore the Taz wrote that the Maharil, who wrote that one should not select matzah crumbs on Pesach, large from small, holds like the Rashba; and from Maharshal, first chapter of Beitzah, siman 42, when he disagrees with the Maharil, the words of the Rashba were hidden from him; until here. This is puzzling, for Maharshal there brings the words of the Rashba, only he wrote that the Maggid Mishneh's words incline [to leniency]; also the Taz's other words can be dismissed. Nevertheless one should be stringent, for so it appears in Sefer Tanya and from Rabbeinu Yerucham, folio 31; and likewise Magen Avraham, s.k. 9 in siman 504, was stringent like the Maharil.
