That one does not make — Taz in siman 507 seif 5 and 506: shvus deshvus in place of a mitzvah is permitted; nevertheless it may be said specifically mitzvah of shofar and the like — behold joy of Yom Tov is possible without butter; no. But from Mishna Berurah in Yom Tov Sheni, a slight mitzvah — likewise this; and this is in Yom Tov Sheni there is a prohibition of positive command in any case (Tosafos positive and negative command in siman 461); and nevertheless lamed gimmel on shvus, since there is no kares and stoning — and this is where there is a slight mitzvah (however not so did R' Tzvi write Bava Metzia 90, Even HaEzer 5). Likewise Yom Tov — and it may be said Yom Tov positive and negative command, and Eshel Avraham. And in other prohibitions there is great forcing, or a slight mitzvah — it may be said permitted to tell a non-Jew; in Tosafos, and understand this.
