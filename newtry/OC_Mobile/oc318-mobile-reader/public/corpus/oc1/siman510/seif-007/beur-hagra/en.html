(1) Seif 7: It is permitted to salt, etc. — as written, 11b: a person salts, etc., and all the more so here where it is needed for everyone.

(2) And there are those who are stringent. There, for in the beginning they permitted; and see Hagahos Alfasi there s.v. u'mishum, etc. And see Ra'ah there, because there is no processing, etc., and this agrees with the first view; and one may also say that here, per all, it is permitted since he wants to eat them, as explained.
