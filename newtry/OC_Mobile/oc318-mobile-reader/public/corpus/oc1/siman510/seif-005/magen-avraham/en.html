Cheese. For curdling is like building, and it is possible to do it from yesterday, for aged cheese is better than new. And it appears to me that the main reason is that the usual way is to make it for many days, so it is weekday labor, as written in siman 504; and therefore the Gemara asks what differs from kneading, and does not ask what differs from cooking, only because kneading too is usually done for bread for many days; and they answer: since hot bread is better, it is permitted. And so it appears from siman 495, end of seif 1.
And one may not set [milk]. Meaning that one puts rennet into it, for that is selecting. And one authority wrote that one should not be stringent for others in this; and one may not rely on him against all these many authorities.
That one does not make butter. And even though same-day butter is better, nevertheless it is selecting and a great toil, and it is forbidden even through a non-Jew (there in the Rivash). And it appears to me that it is permitted to skim the cream floating on the surface of the milk that they call smetana, even on Shabbos; and when he reaches close to the milk he should leave a little with the milk, as written in siman 319, seif 14. And specifically when he needs to eat it on that same day; and if he does not need it, but only fears it will spoil and become ruined, it is permitted to do it through a non-Jew, as written in siman 307, seif 5.
