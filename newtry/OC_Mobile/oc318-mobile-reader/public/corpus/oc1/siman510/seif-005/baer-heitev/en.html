One may set [milk]. Meaning that one puts rennet into it.
Butter. Even through a non-Jew it is forbidden (Rivash). And the Taz is lenient through a non-Jew if he needs this for simchas Yom Tov; see there. And Magen Avraham wrote, and it appears to me, that it is permitted to skim the cream floating on the surface of the milk that they call smetana or ra'am, even on Shabbos; and when he reaches close to the milk he should leave a little with the milk, as written in siman 319, seif 4. And specifically when he needs to eat it on that same day; and if he does not need it, but only fears it will spoil and become ruined, it is permitted to do it through a non-Jew, as written in siman 307, seif 5.
