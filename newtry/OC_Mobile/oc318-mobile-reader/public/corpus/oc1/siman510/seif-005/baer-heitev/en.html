(2) Butter. Even through a non-Jew it is forbidden (Rivash). And the Taz is lenient through a non-Jew if it is needed for this on account of the joy of Yom Tov (see there). And the Magen Avraham wrote, and it appears to me, that it is permitted to collect the fat floating on the surface of the milk that they call s'ma'etene or ra'am even on Shabbat, and when it approaches close to the milk he should leave a little with the milk, as written in siman 319 seif 4. And specifically when he needs to eat it on that same day; and if he does not need it but only fears it will spoil and become ruined, it is permitted to do it through a non-Jew, as written in siman 507 seif 5.
