(1) One does not make cheese. He explains in the Gemara that cheese is better when dry and salty; and if so, he should curdle before Yom Tov.

(2) That one does not make butter from milk. The reason in Rivash is because of borer; and he wrote it is appropriate to forbid even through a non-Jew, for telling a non-Jew is shvus, even in a matter that has only a d'rabbanan prohibition, end of his words. And per what we establish in siman 342, that on Yom Tov they did not decree shvus on it, since its prohibition is only d'rabbanan and there is in it a slight mitzvah — here too it should have been permitted if needed for the joy of Yom Tov, and it should have been permitted through a non-Jew.
