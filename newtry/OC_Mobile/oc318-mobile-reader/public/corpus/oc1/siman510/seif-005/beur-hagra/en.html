(1) Seif 5: And one does not set, etc. Yerushalmi at the beginning of the chapter: How may one set milk on Yom Tov? If you say so, he also milks and sets from Yom Tov to chol; and see Rashi on Shabbos 95a s.v. hamachbetz (the one who sets milk in the stomach), and likewise the Rivash explained in siman 121.

(2) And the same law, etc. — like setting, which is borer, as on Shabbat there. And Tosafos there s.v. veharodeh: it may be said it is forbidden, etc., and the same applies here because it is for many days, as written in Yerushalmi on setting.
