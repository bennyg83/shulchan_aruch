On Yom Tov — {Rama: see there at (7).} And the reason is that it was possible to make the cheese and butter from the day before, for cheese from the day before is considered better than that of today; therefore there is a prohibition of borer in cheese and butter. Even through a non-Jew it is forbidden, for telling a non-Jew in such a case is shvus, even regarding a matter that involves only a d'rabbanan prohibition (Beit Yosef, Rivash).
