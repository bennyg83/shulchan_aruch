(Seif tet) To strain, etc. — as written seif 3, that Tosafos asked: what is different here, that here it is permitted to put into the hanging strainer to strain, and to strain mustard is forbidden? And it may be said: here it deals with if he were doing it from yesterday, etc.; and if so, necessarily in seif 3, if he had not strained the mustard from erev Yom Tov, it would be better as if he did it on Yom Tov, and therefore it is forbidden; and for this Magen Avraham intended in seif heh in what he wrote: and specifically when it was possible, etc.
