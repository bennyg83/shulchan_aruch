Similarly, loads that are normally carried on a pole should be carried on one's back; those normally carried on one's back should be carried on one's shoulder; those normally carried on the shoulder should be carried in front in one's hand, or a garment should be spread over them — and any similar change in how the load is carried. If it is impossible — such as when one has invited many guests and needs to hurry and bring things before them — one may do it in the normal way. This applies to carrying on a person, but on an animal one may not bring things at all.
