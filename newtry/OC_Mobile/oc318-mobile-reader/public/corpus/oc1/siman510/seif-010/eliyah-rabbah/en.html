[י] Cheese, etc. And Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote in the name of Tosafos that it is permitted, and Beit Yosef wondered at him that it is against the Gemara, that in Tosafos as well it does not appear — see there. And to my humble opinion it is clear that what Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote 'permitted' means d'oraisa, and he relied on what he wrote at the end 'from the Torah all ochel nefesh is permitted even when it could be done from erev Yom Tov'; and what Rabbeinu Yerucham wrote that it is better when it is a ben yomo means that one should not say it is not fit for eating when it is a ben yomo, like honey and date honey that he mentioned there that are forbidden d'oraisa — he says it is better; and the same law applies that from yesterday is preferable. Also what Beit Yosef wrote that Tosafos in chapter HaMitzniya did not mention — that which is taught 'he is lashed' refers only to kneading honey cakes; and it is possible that in another place they wrote thus, that in the Tosafos text of Rabbeinu Yerucham he wrote thus — one could wonder at one as great as he, for explicitly Tosafos wrote there on daf 55 thus; and likewise Tosafos in Beitzah daf 21 s.v. he'eil etc.
