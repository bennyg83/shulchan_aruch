One should spread a garment over them. And this deals with wine, for we do not decree lest he squeeze (Tosafos and the Baal HaMaor). And even though it appears from the Ran that even with wine it is forbidden, and it is permitted only with fruits, it appears to me that it is permitted with wine, for washing does not apply to it, since it is a manner of soiling (Yam Shel Shlomo). And see siman 319, seif 10, and siman 320, seif 15: a garment made to be spread over it is permitted even with water. We say in the Gemara: one covers with a lid; sometimes it falls and he comes to bring it; and it requires further study why the poskim omitted this (Yam Shel Shlomo). And for me it requires further study: what is wrong if he carries it? Is carrying not permitted on Yom Tov, and especially with a vessel that he needs? Therefore it appears to me that the correct text is as in Shabbos, folio 148: one covers with a lid; sometimes it [the cord] snaps and he comes to tie it. And Rashi explained: since you require him to cover, he ties it to the jug; and sometimes it snaps and he comes to tie it, and it is a permanent knot. This implies explicitly that now, when covering is not required, it is permitted to carry with a covering, for if it snaps he will leave it as is and will not tie it; and therefore the poskim omitted it, as explained; this is clear.
