(א) One shall spread a garment over them. And it deals with wine, for we do not decree lest he squeeze (Tosafos and Ra'avad); and even though it appears from Ran that even with wine it is forbidden and permitted only with fruits — it appears to me that it is permitted with wine, for washing is not applicable to it, for it is the manner of drinking (Yam Shel Shlomo); and see siman 319 seif 10 and siman 327 s.k. 15 that a garment made to spread over it, even in water, is permitted. We say in the Gemara: 'One covers with a stopper — sometimes it breaks and he comes to carry' — and requires further study why the poskim omitted it (Yam Shel Shlomo); and to me requires further study what is wrong if he carries it — is not carrying permitted on Yom Tov, and especially with a vessel he needs? Therefore it appears to me that the correct text is as it appears in Shabbos daf 148: 'One covers with a stopper — sometimes it breaks and he comes to tie it,' and Rashi explained: since you require him to cover it, tie it in a jug; and sometimes it breaks and he comes to tie it — and it is a permanent knot; it implies explicitly that now when he does not need covering it is permitted to carry with covering, that if it breaks he leaves it thus and does not tie it; and therefore the poskim omitted it, as explained — clear.
