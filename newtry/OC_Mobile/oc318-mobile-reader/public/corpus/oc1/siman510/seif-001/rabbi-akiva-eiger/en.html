Magen Avraham seif vav: and kindling too is not present, etc. — this is not relevant here, for plainly to kindle in order to roast at the beginning is permitted; and the words of the Ramban are on smoking fruits on coals, for it is not so much ochel nefesh, but permitted because it is not extinguishing, since in the end it kindles; in this the Ramban wrote it deals only with a bonfire made for cooking need, and examine.
