One may rub heads of grain. Meaning that by hand he rubs the wheat while soft, for that is threshing in an unusual manner; and one may crumble legumes, that is the pods, and remove the seed. And on Shabbos an alteration is required in rubbing, as written in siman 319; on Yom Tov they did not decree. But not on a board, for it appears like one acting for tomorrow's need, since with these vessels the usual way is only to prepare a large amount.
