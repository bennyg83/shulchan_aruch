One may rub heads of grain. Meaning that by hand he rubs the wheat while soft, for that is threshing in an unusual manner. And on Shabbos an alteration is required in rubbing, namely with the tips of his fingers, as written in siman 319; and on Yom Tov, when he wants to eat them, they did not decree. And this is what he wrote: in their usual manner on Yom Tov.
And one may crumble legumes. That is, the pods, and one removes the seed from them.
In their usual manner on Yom Tov. And some are stringent specifically with an alteration, with the tips of his fingers.
And even with a kanon and a dish. This is explained above in siman 319, seif 6; see there. And on Yom Tov they were lenient.
But not with a board, etc. For it appears like one acting for tomorrow's need, since with these vessels the usual way is only to prepare a large amount.
