One rubs — Taz; Rashi Beitzah 12b and in the mishnah 14b; but Rashba holds nafah and sieve on Shabbat — chatat, on Yom Tov forbidden; see letter gimmel. And it is possible from guarding fruit, and immediately — even with nafah and sieve, only that it appears for many days (Magen Avraham 4, and in Eshel Avraham letter dalet, and Levush in the gloss that with nafah it is forbidden on Yom Tov; and nevertheless in seif 2 he wrote leaving for later that day is permitted). And it must be said: distinguish between nafah and leaving for later that day — and this will be explained in Magen Avraham 2.
