(1) [Seif 1] One rubs ears of grain, etc. — meaning ears of wheat that one rubs in his hands when they are soft. And one breaks legumes — sword beans and those that release seed — for threshing ke'lachar yad, and even on Shabbat it is not d'oraisa but only d'rabbanan, and on Yom Tov they did not decree (Rashi Beitzah 12b; Beit Yosef; Taz seif kaf alef).
