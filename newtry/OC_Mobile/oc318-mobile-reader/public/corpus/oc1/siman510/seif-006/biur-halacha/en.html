The vegetable — see Mishna Berurah; and the Peri Megadim wrote: but to cut vegetables very thin is permitted on Yom Tov [and he means even though on Shabbat it is forbidden, as above in siman 321 seif 2] like grinding spices — behold, in their vessel like a fixed knife on a wooden board (which in Russia they call shpetkovnitse) — one may say it is forbidden because the way is to do for many days, end of his words.
