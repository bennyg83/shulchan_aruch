(1) Seif 8: Even though, etc., rather, etc. — as explained above, etc., the Rabbis say: How much, etc., and as explained.

(2) And specifically, etc., but from corner to corner. And Tosafos there s.v. hammevi, etc., and it may be said that there, etc., and it may be said why he explained, etc.

(3) Or from house, etc. — for on Shabbat it also deals with from house to house, and Lamed — in that house, as Tosafos wrote there that he cannot take out outside, and as written in chapter 5 (36); and here we learn: one lowers, etc. — there, what? Here we learn, etc. — thus even from house to house is dealt with there.
