[8] [Levush] That this is not similar to selecting, etc. It is puzzling to me, for in siman 319, seif 9, he wrote that one who puts [liquid] into a hanging strainer on Shabbos is liable because of selecting, and likewise the Tur wrote, and it is a Gemara in Shabbos, folio 138. And I have no doubt that Rashi's explanation in the mishnah at the beginning of the chapter of R' Eliezer regarding milah misled him here, for he explained that this is not the manner of selecting; but plainly he explained thus according to R' Eliezer, who said one may put into a hanging strainer on Shabbos; but according to the Sages, with whom we hold, that one may not put, certainly this is the manner of selecting. And likewise it appears in Tosafos, folio 134, s.v. "here," etc., and likewise it is explained explicitly in the Ran. And that which is permitted on Yom Tov: it appears to me as Tosafos wrote there, that if he had done it from yesterday it would not have been so good and strong.
