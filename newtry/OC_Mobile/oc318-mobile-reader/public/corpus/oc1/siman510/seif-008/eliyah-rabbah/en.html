[8] [Levush] That this is not similar to borer, etc. It is wondrous to me, for in siman 319 seif 9 he wrote that one who puts into a strainer on Shabbat is liable because of borer, and likewise wrote the Tur, and it is a Gemara in Shabbos daf 138; and I have no doubt that Rashi's explanation in the mishnah at the beginning of the chapter of R. Eliezer regarding circumcision misled him here, that he explained there is no way of borer in this; but plainly he explained thus per R. Eliezer, who said one may put into a strainer on Shabbat, but per the Rabbis, with whom we hold, one may not put, and certainly the way of borer is in this; and likewise it appears in Tosafos daf 134 s.v. hacha, etc., and likewise it is explained explicitly in the Ran; and that which is permitted on Yom Tov — it seems to me like Tosafos wrote there, that if he had done it from yesterday it would not be so good and strong.
