It is permitted in every case. Even a great amount, as written in siman 521. And Tosafos wrote that it is better to carry much together in order to minimize walking. And it appears to me that all agree about this, not like Rav Beis Yosef, who wrote in siman 323, seif 5, that there is a dispute about this. And what the Tur wrote, that minimizing the load is better, deals with when they carry through a merged alleyway where many people are commonly present.
