(3) There. In the usual way on Yom Tov — meaning without a change on his fingertips; and even though above siman 319 seif 6 he wrote that a change is required, that he rub specifically on his fingertips — there regarding Shabbat, but on Yom Tov they did not decree. And the Maggid Mishneh wrote chapter 3, and even though there are disputants, thus is the essence. And likewise the Ran, beginning of Beitzah — that per the view of some, since we say one rubs ears on Yom Tov, specifically through a change; but that is not the view of the Rif, that on Yom Tov it is permitted to rub even not through a change; see there. And the Beit Yosef wrote, and thus is the view of the Rosh in the chapter Mefanin, and so we record. And this is what he ruled here in Shulchan Aruch: one rubs ears, etc., in the usual way on Yom Tov; and likewise Rashal in Yeshuos Shlomo, beginning of Beitzah siman 38; and likewise the Bach, Taz there. R' O. A. — however Magen Avraham seif kaf alef brought the view of those who are stringent that the Ran wrote, and likewise R' O. A. brought the view of those who are stringent and wrote: and even though the essence is like the first reasoning, nevertheless if he can do so through a change it is good to be stringent and to be concerned for the other reasoning.
