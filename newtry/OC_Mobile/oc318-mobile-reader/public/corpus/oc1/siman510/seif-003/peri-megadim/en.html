No — the Taz, and likewise Magen Avraham letter 4 challenged thus; and in Olas Shabbat letter 2 he challenged thus; and in Yam Shel Shlomo chapter 1 of Beitzah siman 42 — see there; and in Eliyah Rabbah he reconciles that 'immediately' is not borer at all — see there; and if so in Magen Avraham it will be explained further. And that which he wrote on the words of Maggid Mishneh on Rashba chapter 3 of Yom Tov seif 16 is difficult — for in borer of refuse from food it implies even immediately by hand one is liable for a sin-offering on Shabbos; and nevertheless on Yom Tov they permitted a sieve and a winnowing basket on that day as well; and the matter is in siman 319 s.v. and in Magen Avraham letter 60; and see Olas Shabbat there and chapter 8 of Machatzit HaShekel in Maggid Mishneh — requires further study; and Rashba possibly disagrees on this and holds that refuse from food by hand immediately there is no liability for a sin-offering on Shabbos. And that which he wrote that the words of Rashba were hidden from him — see in Eliyah Rabbah letter 2 he challenged that Yam Shel Shlomo brings there the words of Rashba — see there.
