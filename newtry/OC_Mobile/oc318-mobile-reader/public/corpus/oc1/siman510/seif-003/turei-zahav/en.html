(ב) With charcoal of wood. In the Gemara we challenge: what is different from meat of udders that is permitted? And we answer: here there is extinguishing, here there is no extinguishing — meaning due to extinguishing the charcoal; but with metal extinguishing is not applicable, for charcoal is not made. And Ba'al HaMaor challenged on this — for behold every extinguishing that is for the need of ochel nefesh is permitted; what difference to me between kindling and what difference to me between extinguishing? Rather our version is: here it is possible, here it is not possible — meaning that mustard is only for the indulgent and is not equal for every person; and Ramban rejected his words, for certainly it is needed and equal for every person. And the difficulty of Ba'al HaMaor — its explanation is that sweetening mustard is a preparation and is not included in baking and cooking, rather like grinding wheat; and in the manner that they said in Avot: everything needs preparation — mustard needs sweetening, wheat needs grinding, even a person needs preparation; and because of this they forbade it — end of quote; and I did not descend to the end of his view — for if you compare it to grinding wheat, baking as well is preparation and even more than grinding wheat; and if so what difference is there in sweetening mustard? And in truth grinding wheat — the prohibition is not due to preparation, rather as Maharil wrote in siman 695; and the difficulty of Ba'al HaMaor — it appears to me the truth is like his version 'here it is possible,' only the explanation is in this manner — namely that sweetening mustard would be done from erev Yom Tov, which is not so with meat of udders that spoils taste; and so it is explained in Beit Yosef siman 511.
