With a coal of wood. In the Gemara they ask: what differs from meat roasted on coals, which is permitted? And they answer: here there is extinguishing, here there is no extinguishing; meaning because of extinguishing the coal; but with metal, extinguishing does not apply, for it does not become charcoal. And the Baal HaMaor challenged this: for every extinguishing that is for the need of ochel nefesh is permitted; what difference is there to me between kindling and extinguishing? Rather the reading is: here it is possible, here it is not possible; meaning that mustard is only for the pampered and is not equal for every person. And the Ramban rejected his words, for certainly it is needed and equal for every person. And the Baal HaMaor's difficulty: its answer is that sweetening mustard is a preparation and is not included in baking and cooking, but is like grinding wheat; and in the manner they said in Avos: everything needs preparation; mustard needs sweetening, wheat needs grinding, even a person needs preparation; and because of this they forbade it, end quote. And I did not reach the end of his view: for if you compare it to grinding wheat, baking too is preparation, and even more than grinding wheat; and if so, what differs regarding sweetening mustard? And in truth, grinding wheat: the prohibition is not because of preparation, but as written at the beginning of siman 495. And regarding the Baal HaMaor's difficulty, it appears to me that the truth is like his reading "here it is possible," only the explanation is in this manner: namely that sweetening the mustard should be done from erev Yom Tov, which is not so for meat roasted on coals, which spoils the taste; and so it is explained in Beis Yosef siman 511.
