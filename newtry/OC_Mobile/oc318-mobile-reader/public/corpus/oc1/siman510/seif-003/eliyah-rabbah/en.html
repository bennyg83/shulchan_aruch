[ג] [Levush] Even to move it, etc. Requires investigation — for the Shulchan Aruch ruled that it is permitted to borer the food, and Olas Shabbat was precise about this from what he wrote 'even though the food is greater' etc.; and he was not precise, for the Shulchan Aruch wrote thus explicitly prior to this; and also his challenge here, and what is weighed against fresh in siman 319 is for nothing — let one examine in Beit Yosef. Also requires investigation — for in siman 319 the Levush himself wrote two views on this and those who permit are many; and it is possible he holds to be stringent on Yom Tov more than on Shabbos regarding muktzeh, as he wrote in siman 695; and for the practical ruling I raised above to be lenient also on Yom Tov. And behold when the refuse is greater, only that in food there is more exertion — it is explained in Ran that one bores specifically the food; and in Beit Yosef siman 319 he brought the words of Ran mistakenly — see there and consider it; and likewise it appears from Rabbeinu Yerucham there, for he wrote that the refuse is forbidden to move; but I found in Tosefta [Beitzah 1:14]: Beit Shammai say if there were many stones upon the foods, he selects the foods and leaves the stones; Beit Hillel say he selects whichever of them he wishes — up to here; and it appears to me that it deals with stones that are many in quantity but few in exertion; for if stones are many in exertion, certainly for all poskim there is no permission to borer the refuse, unless we say they disagree with the Gemara — consider it, and requires investigation. And from the aforementioned Tosefta as well there is proof for the view of Rambam, that even when refuse is great in quantity it is permitted to move.
