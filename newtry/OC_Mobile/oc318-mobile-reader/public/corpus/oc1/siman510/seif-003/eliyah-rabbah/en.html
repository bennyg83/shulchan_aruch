[3] [Levush] Even to move it, etc. This requires investigation, for the Shulchan Aruch ruled that it is permitted to select the food, and Olas Shabbos was precise about this from what he wrote "even though the food is the majority," etc.; and he was not precise, for the Shulchan Aruch wrote thus explicitly before this; and also his challenge here, and what he deliberates in siman 319, is for nothing for one who examines Beis Yosef. Also this requires investigation, for in siman 319 the Levush himself wrote two views on this, and those who permit are many; and it is possible he holds to be more stringent on Yom Tov than on Shabbos regarding muktzeh, as he wrote in siman 495; and for the practical ruling I concluded above to be lenient also on Yom Tov. And behold, when refuse is the majority, only that regarding the food there is more toil: it is explained in the Ran that one selects specifically the food; and in Beis Yosef siman 319 he brought the words of the Ran mistakenly; see there and consider carefully. And likewise it appears from Rabbeinu Yerucham there, for he wrote that the refuse is forbidden to move. But I found in the Tosefta [Beitzah 1:14]: Beis Shammai say, if there were many pebbles upon the foods, he selects the foods and leaves the pebbles; Beis Hillel say, he selects whichever of them he wishes; until here. And it appears to me that it deals with pebbles that are many in quantity but few in toil; for if pebbles are many in toil, certainly for all poskim there is no permission to select refuse, unless we say they disagree with the Gemara; consider carefully, and this requires investigation. And from the aforementioned Tosefta as well there is proof for the view of the Rambam, that even when refuse is great in quantity it is permitted to move.
