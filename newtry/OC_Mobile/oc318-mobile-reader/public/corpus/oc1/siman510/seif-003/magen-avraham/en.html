(2) On coals. For it is extinguishing and this is not actual ochel nefesh need; and in the Gemara he asks: what is different from meat on coals that is permitted, even though it is possible to roast it on a spit? And it answers there: there is no extinguishing that ends up kindling, and kindling too is not present — it deals with when they roasted it in a bonfire made for cooking need, and therefore it is permitted. This is the view of the Rif, Rosh, Ramban, and Rashi explained: since mustard is possible from yesterday, for the way is to sweeten a lot in one day, it is forbidden to do it on Yom Tov. And the Bach wrote: if he was busy on erev Yom Tov and could not do it, it is permitted on Yom Tov; however in siman 504 seif 1 it implies forbidden with salt, and it is possible he says the same here, through a change.
