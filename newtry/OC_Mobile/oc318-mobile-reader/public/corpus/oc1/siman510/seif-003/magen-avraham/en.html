One may not strain. For it appears like selecting, in that he casts away its bran; but it is not actual selecting, since the refuse as well is fit to eat (Tosafos). And specifically when it was possible to do it from yesterday; see seif 4.
With a coal. For it is extinguishing, and this is not an actual need of ochel nefesh. And in the Gemara they ask: what differs from meat roasted on coals, which is permitted even though one could roast it on a spit? And they answer: there there is no lasting extinguishing, for in the end it kindles; and kindling too is not present, for they deal with roasting it on a fire made for cooking need, and therefore it is permitted. This is the view of the Rif, the Rosh, and the Ramban. And Rashi explained: since mustard can be prepared from yesterday, for the way is to sweeten a great amount at once, it is forbidden to do it on Yom Tov. And the Bach wrote: if he was occupied on erev Yom Tov and could not do it, it is permitted to do it on Yom Tov; however, in siman 504, seif 1, it implies it is forbidden, for the anonymous mishnah is that one may not crush salt on Yom Tov; and it is possible that here too he means it is permitted through an alteration.
Metal is permitted. For it does not burn, and there is no extinguishing; and see siman 334. And it appears to me that for this reason it is permitted to place a whitened spit into the beverage so that it will become sour, if it is fit to drink on that same day.
