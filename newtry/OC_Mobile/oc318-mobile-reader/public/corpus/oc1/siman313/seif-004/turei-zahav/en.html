And that which he forbids here even when there is a recognizable hinge — it appears to me this case is different in seif 3 regarding a reed mat, for there a recognizable hinge helps since there it does not appear like building as here — for it is not customary to build a reed mat in building; unlike here where it is one board and it is customary to build thus in a wall — therefore a hinge does not help; and in the Gemara they call this door "a widow's door":
