(א) A door made, etc. — meaning that since it does not have the fixtures of a door like other doors, it is like mere sealing and appears like building; and it deals here even with an opening made for frequent entry and exit:

(ב) From one board or that does not have, etc. — and Rambam's view is that it is forbidden only with two adverse factors — namely that it is one board and also has no threshold; and with an opening made for frequent entry and exit the later authorities side to rely on his view to be lenient:

(ג) And when they open, etc. — it is implied from this that it deals when it is not tied; but if it is tied and hung, even if it is dragged on the ground when he opens it — permitted, even if it does not have the fixtures of doors properly; and this is because here it deals with an opening made for frequent entry and exit:

(ד) Even though they remove, etc. — meaning that it is not tied at all; and even though at the beginning of seif 3 regarding a wide door that is also presumably made like other doors, and nevertheless we are stringent that it should be tied and hung intentionally — here it deals with an opening made for frequent entry and exit, as stated, and he holds that in this we do not require tying at all, as is written in s.k. 29 [Gra]:

(ה) And there is carving — meaning that the carving is considered like a threshold:

(ו) That their law is like a door — meaning that even though the boards are not joined and he inserts each board by itself — nevertheless it is not considered a door of one board:

(ז) Since they are not, etc. — we already wrote above that the later authorities side to be lenient even with one board, since it has a threshold and is made for frequent entry and exit:
