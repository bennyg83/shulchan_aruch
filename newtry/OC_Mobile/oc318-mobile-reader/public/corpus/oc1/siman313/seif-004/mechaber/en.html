A door made from a single board, or that has no lower threshold and when they open they remove it and uproot it — they may not lock with it even if it has a hinge.: But a door made from many boards is permitted even though they remove it when opening, if it has a threshold; and the same in a place where they lock with many boards and there is carving above and below into which they insert the boards, that their law is like a door, since they are not a single board for the entire door (Beit Yosef).
