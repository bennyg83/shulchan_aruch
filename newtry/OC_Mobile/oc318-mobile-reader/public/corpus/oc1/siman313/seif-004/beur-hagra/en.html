(א) Seif 4 "or that it does not have." As Rif and Rosh explained.

(ב) "Even if there is." Tosafos s.v. "door of" — for if not so, otherwise it is forbidden, and like the version "that is dragged"; but for Rif who reads "that is not dragged" — meaning even if it is not attached at all, and as stated "that one removes and uproots it" as stated above in the Shulchan Aruch — still the difficulty remains that a hinge does not help in such a case. But Rif goes according to his view that he explains specifically in a wide area that is muktzeh as stated above, but for constant entry and exit — meaning from his words that even if it is not attached at all it is permitted; and so too Rambam; and therefore specifically a widow's door; and Tosafos go according to their reasoning that it is not specifically muktzeh, and like Rashi; and as stated s.v. "ki peligei" and the Mishnah above that door etc.; and they did not go down to distinguish as Ra'avad distinguished as stated above:
