There that Tosafos beginning of chapter Kol HaKeilim wrote that it seemed to them that this is, etc. — so it should read; and the explanation of his words: in this law R' Eliezer holds we require tied and hanging because he holds it is forbidden to add to a temporary tent; and the rabbis hold one does not need tied and hanging, for it is permitted to add to a temporary tent; and like them Shulchan Aruch ruled; and we say there, in the Gemara that the incident of "they plugged the window" does not follow R' Eliezer — for there it was not tied at all; and Tosafos challenged — perhaps actually like R' Eliezer and there it is different because it was for a mitzvah; and as stated there in that incident that they also made from it because it was for a mitzvah. And they resolved — specifically from it, which is a light prohibition, they permitted for the sake of a mitzvah; unlike adding to a tent according to R' Eliezer which is like building — one may not permit for the sake of a mitzvah; perforce like the rabbis it is permitted even in a private domain — end of his words. And this is what Magen Avraham wrote "it seemed" — namely it seemed to Tosafos that their question was thus; but their conclusion is that it follows the rabbis and a plug is permitted even in a private domain — and all the more so for a mitzvah; and from this it is proven that it is permitted even after his death, since we see that even adding to a tent according to R' Eliezer — Tosafos seemed to permit in a place of mitzvah — only they resolved that building is a more severe prohibition; automatically per the rabbis who have no prohibition of building — permitted even though there is prohibition of fixing; at least this is not more severe than measuring and permitted in a place of mitzvah — so Tosafos Shabbos explained:
