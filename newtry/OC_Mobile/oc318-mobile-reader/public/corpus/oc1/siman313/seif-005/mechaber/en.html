A chest, box, and tower whose openings are from the side and have two hinges, one above and one below — if the lower came out entirely from its place, it is forbidden to return it lest he hammer; but if part of it came out he may force it until he returns it to its place, since the upper remained in its place he can easily return the lower. But when the upper came out it is forbidden even to force it to return it to its place.
