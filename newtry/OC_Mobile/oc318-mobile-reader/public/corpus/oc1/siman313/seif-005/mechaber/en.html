Regarding a carriage, box and closet that open from the side and have hinges on top and below, if the bottom hinge became dis- lodged altogether it may not be replaced out of concern that he will insert it firmly but if it became dislodged partially one may push it until it falls back into place. The reason is that since the upper hinge remained in place one can easily replace the bottom hinge. When the upper hinge becomes dislodged one may not even push it back into place.
