A chest, etc. All their doors had two ends — one above and one below — and one hole was in the threshold and corresponding to it a hole above in the lintel; and where the hole was in the middle of the opening of the chest there was no need to decree lest he hammer; nevertheless they decreed in the middle due to the side, and it deals with fastening in the first chapter of Beitza; and seemingly it appears that with our hinges made of iron there is no need to decree lest he hammer, for the hole is wide and impossible to hammer more; but in Mordechai chapter 3 of Eruvin it is somewhat implied that it is forbidden — it is forbidden to return a chest door, a decree lest he hammer; meaning, lest he place the hinges (meaning, connect them with nails), which is significant building; and it is shvus, see siman 314 seif 7:
