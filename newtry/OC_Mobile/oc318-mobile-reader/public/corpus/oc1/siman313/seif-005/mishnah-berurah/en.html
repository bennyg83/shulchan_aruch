(א) And one below — and there is a hole in the threshold into which the hinge enters, and corresponding to it above there is one hole above, and the upper hinge is within the hole:

(ב) Lest he hammer — forcefully with a knife and pegs so that it can no longer come out from there, and it is striking with a hammer; and even though the hole is in the middle of the opening of the chest, where it is not so relevant to decree lest he hammer — nevertheless forbidden in the middle due to the side; and even with our iron hinges it is forbidden to return the doors of vessels, for they decreed lest he see that the hinge is not inserted well and connect it with nails:

(ג) But when the upper one comes out — for through this it falls completely; also pressing it closed is forbidden like returning:
