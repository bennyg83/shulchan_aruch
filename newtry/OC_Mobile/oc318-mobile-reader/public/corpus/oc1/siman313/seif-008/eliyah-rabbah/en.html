(8) [Levushei Serad] Due to makeh, etc. I do not know why he did not mention due to building — for even though there is no building and demolishing in utensils, nevertheless if driven it is actual building and one is liable (Olas Shabbat); and it escaped him that so Rashi explained at the beginning of chapter Kol HaKeilim — Beit Yosef brought siman 308; and likewise Rashba wrote in Sefer Avodat HaKodesh daf 39; and Rosh; and likewise Ran explained there for one explanation; and one may also wonder — Olas Shabbat contradicts himself to what he wrote siman 308 seif 9 — examine. And for the law requires study — for Ran there for the second explanation wrote that whatever requires strength and craftsmanship is considered as making a new utensil and there is concern of building; and likewise wrote chapter HaBoneh and in Tosafos there; and so is implied in Semag and Semak and Agudah and Shiltei Giborim in the name of Riaz; and it appears according to Rashi and his supporters it is forbidden even when strength and craftsmanship are not required — rather whatever he is particular that it not be driven into the hole and he tightens with force — forbidden; and possibly this is Ra'avad's view whom Beit Yosef brought — requires study. Rambam wrote chapter 10 "One who returns" — door of a pit and cistern and loft — liable due to building — implied even when not driven, since doors are attached to the ground; and so is explicit in Eruvin daf 52; and for this it appears to me that the Maggid chapter 22 and Bach in siman 308 section 6 did not address this:
