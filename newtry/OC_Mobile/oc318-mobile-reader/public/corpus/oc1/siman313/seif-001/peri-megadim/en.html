(א) What the Mechaber wrote "and that he intend on it from erev Shabbat to close with it" — I have here one explanation and I will explain it there's help. The master and sighing Bach of blessed memory wrote that intent is due to prohibition of handling; and tying does not need intent; and he loaded this on Rashi Shabbos 126a (1) "he made" — namely that they forbade only when not tied — see there. And behold Rashi s.v. vehu concluded "therefore"; and likewise Rambam chapter 22 law 31 concluded "therefore"; and in Ran he explained fixed with the status of a utensil specifically, or that he used from before the day; and nevertheless he explained "between tied" — that even tying is not needed as Rashi explained; and whence do we derive this? Granted if intent — it works well when tied one does not need this since it is preferable to intent; but one can say Rashi's intent as Semag wrote in Choshen Mishpat 72 letter 78 "between" — and all the more so this that is not simple in the first; and in my small general principles letter 17 we wrote from this; and if so "between tied and not tied" is "not only this but also this"; therefore he explained that it refers to the first matter that he mentioned at the beginning the essence of the dispute and for this "he made." And behold Rashi there explains that partition does not apply at all to a temporary tent — and therefore he was compelled to explain in a permanent partition worse still; one can say per the rabbis "fixed" we require to exclude from building a temporary tent — and intent alone does not suffice; but Tosafos there s.v. hakol like a garment — where a partition permits there is a temporary tent — and adding a tent is permitted for the rabbis without any fixing; and here only muktzeh; and however it may be — in Shulchan Aruch intent — the reason is due to muktzeh; and according to this if it was a utensil — a board on which one sets a table whose melacha is forbidden — nevertheless for bodily need permitted — one does not need any intent; and at first glance it is implied like Ran and Rashi — for otherwise why was mentioned here "and that it be fixed" — that it does not depend on building but on muktzeh; and in Rambam he mentioned this chapter 22; and muktzeh its place is chapter 25; and likewise. And intent "to close with it" — I do not know except if he prepared for some matter — intent suffices. And see Levush here what he wrote; and even to add a little forbidden — I did not understand now; and in Magen Avraham letter 1 I will explain:
