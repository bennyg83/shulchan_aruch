(א) A window plug. And even on a skylight in the roof is permitted, for adding to a temporary tent is permitted (Tosafos): R' Yehuda haLevi deduced in his responsum from the words of Rashi, Ran, and Tosafos that specifically before the deceased dies it is permitted to seal the window so that tumah will not enter the house, but after his death it is forbidden to seal it, for it is like fixing, end of his words siman 34; and to tell a gentile to seal it is a rabbinic shvus, and see there at the end of the siman in the index siman 34 (so it is); and requires further study, for if so why did the poskim not write this novel law; and also in the responsum of Maharil siman 69, who wrote that it is forced to establish on Shabbat that which they took a chest and sealed the window as stated in the chapter Bnei HaIr — it is implied in any case that there is no prohibition to seal on Shabbat; and further, that Tosafos at the beginning of the chapter Kol HaKelim wrote that they thought this is a plug of mitzvah and therefore permitted, but for a secular matter forbidden; and since in the conclusion we rule that even for a secular matter it is permitted — a fortiori for one of mitzvah; and it is not fixing, for the vessels that were inside were already impure and it helps only for the future; and what was said before the deceased dies — we mean that they would fix so that no vessel in the house would become impure, for as soon as he dies the deceased will render impure all that is in the house; and it is clear to Rashi that adding a tent from the side is permitted, as is written siman 315, see there; except for that which we rule as forbidden — namely, to make a partition that permits; but to prevent tumah is permitted, and all the more so here, where it is only an addition of tent, which is permitted:
