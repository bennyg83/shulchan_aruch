Even if tied with a thin rope — it is implied that if it is not tied at all a knob does not help; and so it is implied according to my view in the Gemara where we say regarding "carried in his belt" — everyone agrees when it is not tied; when he had a knob tied and hanging — everyone does not disagree; for they disagree when it is not tied. And Beit Yosef wrote that it teaches us the strength of R' Eliezer — that even when tied he forbids whenever it is not carried in his belt. And it is difficult — he should rather have taught us the strength of the lenient view, namely that according to R' Yosi it is permitted even when not tied at all whenever it has a knob, for so we say the strength of the lenient view is greater; and furthermore the halacha is like R' Yosi.
