1) [Seif 1] A window stopper, etc. It is permitted even over an opening in a roof, because adding to a temporary covering is permitted. Tosafos; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 1; Eshel Avraham, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1. This means that the ruling does not apply only to a window stopper on the side of a building. Even covering an opening in a roof is permitted because it merely adds to a temporary covering. This is stated there in Tosafos.
