The laws of moving a door, window, and lock on Shabbos. It contains 10 seifim: A window stopper, such as a board or anything else used to close a window, may be used to close it even if it is not tied, and we do not say that this constitutes adding to a building, provided that one designated it before Shabbos for closing the window. A reed that was prepared to be used as a bolt need not be tied to the door. Nevertheless, it resembles building more than a window stopper, and mere designation from the previous day is insufficient; it must be prepared for this purpose. According to Rashi, this means preparing it as a utensil fit for some use, such as turning olives or cracking nuts. According to Rabbeinu Tam, it need not be fit for another use; once one prepared it, performed an act upon it, and designated it for this purpose, that is sufficient. {Rama: Through this it is considered a kli shemelachto l'heter and may be moved, as explained above in siman 308, seif 2. [Beit Yosef and Rambam]} A door bolt that is a peg inserted into the threshold below resembles building even more, and the foregoing is insufficient. One may not lock with it unless it is tied. If it has a knob at its head, meaning that one end is thick and fit for pounding, so that it resembles a utensil, it is sufficient even if it is tied with a thin cord that is not fit to carry it, even if it is tied not to the door itself but to the door bar, and even if the cord is long and does not hang in the air at all but lies entirely on the ground. If it has no knob at its head, then if it is tied to the door itself, a thin cord that is not fit to carry it is sufficient, even if it lies entirely on the ground. If it is tied not to the door but to the bar, the knot must be strong enough for the peg to be carried by it. Even then it need not hang, and it is permitted even if it lies entirely on the ground, because it is forbidden only when one removes it and places it in a corner.
