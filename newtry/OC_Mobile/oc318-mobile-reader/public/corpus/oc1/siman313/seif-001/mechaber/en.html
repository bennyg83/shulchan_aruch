Moving a door, window, and lock on Shabbat. And in it 10 seifim: A window plug, such as
a board or any other thing with which they seal the window — they may seal with it even if it is not tied, and we do not say that it is like adding to building; provided that he intend upon it from erev Shabbat to seal with it and acquires it — that he fixed it to be locked with it — he need not tie it to the door. And a reed that they fixed to be locked with it — he need not tie it to the door; and nevertheless it resembles building more than a window plug, and thought that he intended it from yesterday is not sufficient; he must fix it for that purpose. According to Rashi, this means he fixes it as a utensil fit for some use, that it should be fit to turn olives in it or to crack nuts with it. And according to Rabbeinu Tam, it need not be fit for another matter; rather once he fixed and did an action and prepared it for that purpose, it is sufficient.And thereby it is called a utensil whose melacha is permitted, and it is permitted to move it, as explained above siman 308 seif 2 (Beit Yosef and Rambam).} And a door bolt, which is a peg with which they lock and insert it into the threshold below — it resembles building more; this is not sufficient, and they do not lock with it unless it is tied. And how must it be tied — if its head has a glostera, meaning that it is thick at one of the heads and fit to pound with it so that it resembles a utensil — it is sufficient even if tied with a thin rope that is not fit to be carried by it, and even if not tied to the door itself but to the door bar, and even if the rope is long and not suspended at all in the air but all of it lies on the ground. And if its head does not have a glostera — if it is tied to the door itself it is sufficient, even with a thin rope not fit to be carried by it, and even if all of it lies on the ground. And if it is not tied to the door but to the bar, the knot must be strong that it be fit to be carried by it; and even so it need not be suspended, rather even if all of it lies on the ground it is permitted, for it is forbidden only when they remove it and place it in a corner.) ונגר שהוא יתד שנועלים בו ותוחבין אותו באסקופה למטה ודומה טפי לבנין לא סגי בהכי ואין נועלים בו אא"כ יהא קשור וכיצד יהא קשור אם יש בראשו גלוסטרא דהיינו שהוא עב באחד מהראשים וראוי לכתוש בו שדומה לכלי סגי אפי' אם קשור בחבל דק שאינו ראוי להיות ניטל בו ואפי' אם אינו קשור בדלת עצמו אלא בבריח הדלת ואפי' אם החבל ארוך ואינו תלוי כלל באויר אלא כולו מונח בארץ ואם אין בראשו גלוסטרא אם הוא קשור בדלת עצמה סגי אפילו בחבל דק שאינו ראוי לינטל בו ואפי' כולו מונח בארץ ואם אינו קשור בדלת אלא בבריח צריך שיהא הקשר אמיץ שיהא ראוי לינטל בו ואפ"ה אין צריך שיהיה תלוי אלא אפי' כולו מונח בארץ מותר שאין אסור אלא כששמטו ומניחו בקרן זוית:
