Window plug — see Baer Heitev; and R' Yitzchak wrote in responsa Beit Yehuda part 2 siman 29 agreed with Magen Avraham; also wrote that iron hinges and locks in the window plug are considered as attached, unlike the Taz; and thus agreed in Peri Megadim part 2 siman 93 and Divrei Shmuel siman 223 etc. see there.
