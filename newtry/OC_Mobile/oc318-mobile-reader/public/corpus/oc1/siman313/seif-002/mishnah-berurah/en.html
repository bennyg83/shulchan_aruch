(ב) But if he made, etc. — and if he made a handle for it [it is like the knob mentioned above, similar to a mortar with which one pounds spices; but above it dealt where the knob was in it from the outset, and here we deal where he made it by hand] — it is proven that it is a vessel, and it no longer appears like building, for it is not the way to sink a vessel in building to nullify it there; and since he did an action to make it a vessel — permitted, even if it is not tied so much, Beit Yosef; and he brought proof from Rif; and Gra wrote in his Biur that according to Rashi and Tur we do not find permission in a threshold that was hollowed out, even by making a handle:

(ג) A threshold that was hollowed out, etc. — and if from the outset he made a hollow in the ground where the head of the bar would be and he does not add a hole in the ground — there is no prohibition; and with this it is reconciled what in most houses they make holes in the wall next to the door on this side and on that side and insert the bolt there, which goes from one end to the other, and we do not say that it is like building — for what difference to me whether it enters in the wall or below in the ground; rather because holes in the wall were made from the outset for this — therefore there is no prohibition; and further it appears that since they make an iron ring in the middle of the bolt and hold it at opening and closing and pull the bolt — this is like making a handle for it, which is permitted even if not tied, even in a hollowed threshold [Taz and other later authorities]; and it appears that even according to Gra, who is stringent above regarding making a handle according to Rashi and Tur — one may be lenient in this because of combining the first reason of Taz:
