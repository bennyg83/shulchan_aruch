But if the threshold was breached, etc. — it appears to me specifically when initially the threshold was whole below, that the hole did not reach the end of the threshold and it became damaged and the hole was made to its end and thereby the bolt pierces the ground and makes a depression in the ground — perforce it is like building; and so is the language of Rashi who wrote "and he inserts into the ground," etc. — it is implied that he makes a new hole. However, if from the outset one made a hollow in the ground where the bolt's head will be and so it always is — that he does not add a hole in the ground — there is no prohibition in this due to appearing like building, for it is like the window plug at the beginning of the siman; and with this the practice in most houses is resolved — that they make holes in the wall by the door on this side and that and insert the bolt-bar there so it goes from one end to the other — and we do not say it is like building; for what difference to me whether it enters the wall or below in the ground — rather certainly as we said that from the outset holes were made in the wall for this purpose — perforce there is no prohibition. And furthermore it appears that since they make an iron ring in the middle of the bolt-bar and one grasps it when opening and closing and pulls the bolt-bar — this is considered as making for it a handle, which is simple in the Gemara that it is permitted even when not tied and hanging; and Rif explained that it refers to nikmatz — namely the threshold was breached:
