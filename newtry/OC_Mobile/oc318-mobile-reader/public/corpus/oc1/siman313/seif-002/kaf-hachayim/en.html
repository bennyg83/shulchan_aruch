(2) There. Window plug, etc. A house where someone died there — forbidden to close the window facing another house so that kohanim may enter — for it is fixing; and to tell a non-Jew to close it is a dispute among the rabbis. R' Yehuda HaLevi siman 34; Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahot HaTur. However Magen Avraham section 1 disagreed with him and wrote that it is permitted to close even after the deceased died and it is not fixing — see there. And Eliyah Rabbah in section 1 rejected Magen Avraham's words; and likewise some say in Hagahot HaTur that the essence is like R' Yehuda HaLevi — see Tosafos Shabbos. But Tosafos Shabbos siman 15 wrote that the law is with Magen Avraham. And likewise in responsa Beit Yehudah part 2 siman 29 he challenged R' Yehuda HaLevi and permitted plainly like Magen Avraham; and he brought from several later authorities who hold thus; and he also wrote there that hinges and iron locks on a door and window are considered attached — unlike Taz; and he wrote that so agreed Pnei Me'orot part 2 siman 92 and Divrei Shalom siman 223; and he expanded greatly and concluded that it is permitted for a kohen to close the opening even after death and even on Shabbat; and he should watch his doors that they be locked — none goes out and none enters until the deceased is removed — see end of his words.
