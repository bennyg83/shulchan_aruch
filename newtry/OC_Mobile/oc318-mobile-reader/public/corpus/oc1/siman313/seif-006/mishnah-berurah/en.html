(א) Of sections — meaning that it is made in sections for craftsmen to carry them on the way, and when they come to their place they return the sections together and set it:
(ב) Forbidden, etc. — a decree lest he hammer with pegs and nails, for it is completing the work and he is liable for striking with a hammer; and some say that since through this he makes a complete vessel, he is liable for building; and see in siman 314 seif 1 and in Gra there:
(ג) And if its way is, etc. — for then there is no concern at all lest he come to hammer:
(ד) That he not tighten — and loose, and not loose, forbidden:
(ה) And a cup of sections — there is a cup whose sections are removed from upon its foot:
(ו) To remove it and return it — when it is loose; and the reason is that it is not the usual way to tighten so forcefully that it would be considered like hammering; and therefore there is no concern lest he hammer; and there is one who says he holds that in any case its way is to tighten, and even when loose it is forbidden, like a bed of sections. Magen Avraham wrote that our cups made with grooves around [like those that have a screw near the leg] and tightened firmly — all forbid; and vessel covers — even if made thus — permitted, for there they are not made for permanence but only to open and close constantly; and so Taz; and even vessels that are forbidden to remove and return — nevertheless to move them is permitted, and they decreed only on a menorah whose way is to come apart, as is written end of siman 279, and anything similar; but a thing whose way is not to come apart — permitted to move:
(ז) And there is one who says, etc. — and l'chatchila one should be stringent like this view; but if it is for Shabbat need one may rely on the first view [later authorities]:
(ח) The law of the cup — and the same applies to the stand in the synagogue upon which books are placed, if it is of sections:
(ט) To be tightened — meaning to be tightened and inserted firmly; in this even Tur, who is the first view, agrees that it is forbidden:
