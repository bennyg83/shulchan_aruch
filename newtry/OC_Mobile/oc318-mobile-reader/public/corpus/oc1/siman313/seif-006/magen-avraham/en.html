(א) Liable for a sin-offering. Because he makes a vessel:

(ב) Its way is to be loose. Kolbo wrote in the name of Raavad: things that a person is not particular about — if it shakes within the hole it is permitted, for they decree only on a thing that needs to be held firmly with difficulty; but in Semag it states that specifically a thing that needs strength and craftsmanship is forbidden:

(ג) And a cup, etc., is permitted. For it is not the usual way to tighten it so much (Tur); and it appears to me that our cups made with grooves around and tightened firmly — all forbid; and some say regarding vessel covers made thus — there they are not made for permanence but only to open and close constantly, see siman 314 seif 10; and Beit Yosef wrote: loose and not loose are also forbidden; and it is implied from the plain meaning of the poskim that all these vessels are permitted to move, and they decreed only on a menorah whose way is to come apart, as is written siman 279; and the same applies to anything whose way is not to come apart — permitted to move:
