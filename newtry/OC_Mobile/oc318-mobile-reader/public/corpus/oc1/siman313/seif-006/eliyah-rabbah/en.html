[10] And a cup, etc. Meaning a cup that is removed from its stem and then reattached (Tashbetz). Agudah, chapter HaBoneh, writes that silver or wooden cups and a synagogue shtender made of sections may not be dismantled or reassembled if the sections fit tightly. Malbushei Yom Tov writes that vessels whose covers are screwed on may be opened and closed, as Taz and Magen Avraham also write, because they are made to be opened and closed constantly.
