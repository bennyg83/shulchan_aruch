(א) Frequently — meaning, and therefore it is not made to open except at rare times, for its way of entry and exit is not upon them; and it appears like adding to the building through locking it, unless in the manners explained here, for then it is recognizable that they are doors:

(ב) That he fenced with thorns — meaning that he sealed it with bundles of thorns made like a door, and sometimes he opens it and now wishes to lock it:

(ג) That they had a hinge — meaning that it is recognizable that they had a hinge:

(ד) Even, etc. — only that he tied and hung them — meaning that he tied and hung them there intentionally in order to lock with them; and therefore permitted to lock with them on Shabbat, for even when they are open they are dragged on the ground, and only when he locks them does he raise and stand them on the threshold; nevertheless, since they are tied and attached there intentionally and have the recognition of a hinge — it does not appear like building on this Shabbat:

(ה) High above the ground — meaning that they were tied and hung intentionally in a manner that even when he opens them they are not dragged on the ground; and even if they are high above the ground only like a hair's breadth [gemara] — the reason that again it does not appear like building in this locking, even if they do not have the recognition of a hinge, is since even when they are open they are attached and fixed there well and are not dragged on the ground:

(ו) And an opening made, etc. — since one uses it frequently, everyone knows that it was made for a door and does not appear like building:

(ז) And it is dragged — on the ground when he opens it; and behold from the Mechaber's language it is implied that in any case it is tied; nevertheless we require; but from the language of the gloss in seif 4 it is implied that he holds that even if it is not tied at all it is permitted if it has a threshold below; and one may be lenient in this [see Gra, who wrote that such is the view of Rif and Rambam, and especially since Rama ruled anonymously thus]:
