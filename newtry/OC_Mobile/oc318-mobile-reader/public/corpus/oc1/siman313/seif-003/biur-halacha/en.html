(א) That one does not enter and exit through it frequently — see in Mishna Berurah; and see in Tosafos Shabbos who sides that in an opening made for entry in less than thirty days — it is like an opening made for frequent entry:

(ב) High from the ground — see in Mishna Berurah; and behold in Nishmat Adam general 39 he expanded on this matter and wrote that what is stated in the Mishnah that we require they be high from the ground — there is a dispute among the early authorities in its explanation — see there well in all his words; and in truth his explanation that emerged as novel on this matter — and thereby the halacha changed in Chayei Adam — is something new; and almost all the early authorities did not explain thus — behold they are Razah and Ritva whom he himself brought who explicitly explained as we wrote in Mishna Berurah; and likewise Rashi on the Mishnah is also proven explicitly that he explains thus; and also Or Zarua copied it; and also Rambam in his commentary on the Mishnah is implied that he also explains that it be tied — and it is exactly like the explanation of Tosafos on daf 126b [and it is astonishing that Nachalat Tzvi wrote from Rambam in his commentary on the Mishnah it is implied as his words that when high from the ground it helps even if not tied at all — on the contrary it is proven there to the contrary at the end of his words — see there]; and what Nachalat Tzvi wrote from Rif and Rosh and Ra'avad and Shulchan Aruch — the plain meaning is as his words — in my opinion I did not see any proof for his words — for behold they all copied only the language of the Mishnah; and just as Razah and Ritva and the other early authorities explain the language of the Mishnah — so too their words should be explained; and from Rambam in his composition there is also not so much proof for one who examines; and especially in his commentary on the Mishnah it is explicitly implied that he explains it should be tied — and no support remained for his words except from the words of the Maggid and he is alone against all the early authorities mentioned regarding the halacha; and perforce I copied their explanation in our commentary:
