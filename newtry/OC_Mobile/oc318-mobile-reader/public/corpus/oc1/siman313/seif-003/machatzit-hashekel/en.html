(s.k. 3) And according to Rabbenu Tam, etc. And so too in siman 308 seif 10: on daf 127 it is taught: a reed that one prepared to lock with — Tanna Kama holds we require that it be tied and hanging at the opening; Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel says: prepared (meaning that he prepared and designated it) even if it is not tied; and R' Yochanan said: the law is like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel; and he challenged: but we taught: all vessel covers that have a handle are carried on Shabbat; and R' Yochanan said: and this is when there is the status of a vessel upon them; and with a reed the gemara proved that according to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel it is not required that there be the status of a vessel upon it; and how did R' Yochanan decide like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel; and he answered: R' Yochanan holds like Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel in one matter — that tying is not required — but in this he does not hold like him, that the status of a vessel is not required; and in this Rashi and Rabbenu Tam disagree: Rashi holds that "status of a vessel" means that it should be fit for another use, such as to turn olives with it or to crack nuts; and Rabbenu Tam explained that he prepared it and did an action with it and designated it for this, see there; behold the gemara equated the law of a reed with vessel covers, and for R' Yochanan, who requires the status of a vessel in vessel covers — the same in a reed; nevertheless the status of a vessel in any case is the same in both; and in siman 308 seif 10 it is written: a cover of a pit and cistern may not be carried unless there is a handle, for then it is proven that it is a vessel, etc.; behold he did not stipulate that it should be fit for another use; and therefore he holds like the view of Rabbenu Tam; and if so, the same in a reed, for their laws are equal; and therefore, even though here the Beit Yosef did not decide, in siman 308 he decided like Rabbenu Tam:
