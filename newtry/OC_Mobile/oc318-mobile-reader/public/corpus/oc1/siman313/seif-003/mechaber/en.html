A rear courtyard behind the houses that is not regularly used for entering and leaving: if one made a door for its opening, or hung a reed mat there, or fenced a breach with thorns in the form of a door, then if it has a hinge, meaning that the door is pierced and a pointed piece of wood is fixed into it so that it can be inserted into a socket and swing back and forth, or even if it has no hinge now but previously had one, it may be used to close the opening even if it drags on the ground, provided that it was tied and hung for use as a door. This is not considered building, because the fact that it had a hinge makes it recognizable as a door. {Rama: All the more so if it still has a hinge, provided that he does not return the hinge to its socket, as will be explained below in siman 519. [Beit Yosef]} But if it has no hinge and never had one, it may not be used to close the opening unless it is raised above the ground. An opening that is regularly used for entering and leaving may be closed with such a door even if it never had a hinge and it drags on the ground.
