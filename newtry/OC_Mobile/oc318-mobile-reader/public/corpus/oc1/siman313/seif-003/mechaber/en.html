A courtyard behind the houses that people do not regularly enter and exit — if he made a door for its opening or hangs a reed mat on it, and likewise a breach that he fenced with thorns like a door — if they have a hinge (explained: they pierce the door and set in it a single piece of wood in order to insert it in a socket back and forth), or even if they do not have a hinge now but they had a hinge — they may lock with them even if they are dragged on the ground, only that he tied them and hung them to lock with them, and it is not considered building since it is recognizable in them that it is a door, since they had a hinge.(And all the more so if they still have a hinge, provided the hinge does not return to its place as will be explained later siman 519 (Beit Yosef).} But if they have no hinge and never had a hinge — they may not lock with them unless they are raised from the ground; and an opening made for regular entering and exiting — they may lock with it even if it never had a hinge, and it is dragged.) או אפי' אין להם עתה ציר אלא שהיה להם ציר נועלים בהם אפילו הם נגררים בארץ רק שקשרם ותלאם לנעול בהם ולא חשוב כבונה שניכר בהם שהוא דלת כיון שהיה להם ציר: הגה וכ"ש אם יש להם ציר עדיין ובלבד שלא יחזור הציר למקומו כדרך שיתבאר לקמן סי' תקי"ט (ב"י) אבל אם אין להם ציר ולא היה להם ציר אין נועלין בהם אא"כ היו גבוהים מן הארץ ופתח העשוי לכניסה ויציאה תדיר נועלים בו אפי' לא היה לו ציר מעולם והוא נגרר:
