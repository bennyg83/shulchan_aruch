Unless Taz — Rashi Eruvin 101a in the Mishnah — Beit Yosef brought it; and as appears from Rashi who wrote "therefore" he mentioned it muktzeh — that one does not enter frequently and one is not concerned to make a door properly — namely the essence of the reason of prohibition: since it does not have the actual form of a door — it appears like building; and it does not depend on not entering frequently — rather it is the way of the matter; and if so a window plug — even closing for many days permitted according to Rashi; and these doors are worse than a window plug — and they are the halacha (see Tosafos Shabbos; and for what Rashi wrote unlike Razah — and one can further distinguish between a window and these — see Ramban); and a door in muktzeh — even for a near time forbidden; and in Ran he wrote due to muktzeh — requires some study see there; and according to Ra'avad — a window for a long time forbidden; and a door in muktzeh for a short time permitted — and they disagree on two matters; and the Mechaber ruled like Ra'avad of blessed memory; and he wrote this to exclude Bach who wrote Rashi also like Ra'avad; and Beit Yosef omitted from Rashi the word "these" that one does not enter always — behold Beit Yosef copied it above from him; and Rashi's intent: because they do not enter — not that it is worse — rather it is the way of the matter that therefore one does not make a door properly in its form; and when he closes it — it appears as adding to the building — see there in this.
