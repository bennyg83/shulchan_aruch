There in Shulchan Aruch "that they fix it for this" — it appears to me and this is also due to prohibition of handling; namely since they somewhat resemble building — intent does not bring it to the status of a utensil, since it is not the status of a utensil but the status of building — therefore greater preparation is required; in a bolt that more resembles building — tying is required to exclude from prohibition of building as Rosh wrote — namely that by tying it there it was prepared for this matter to lock the door and it is not like building:
