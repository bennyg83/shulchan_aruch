(s.k. 6) And on erev Shabbat he wrote "this that there is no joy," etc. — meaning Maharash gave proof from a fast that fell on the ninth of Av that one does not eat more than a minyan; and even though there it is possible to fulfill that the rest not eat meat and eat other things — nevertheless there is no joy except in meat; and on this he wrote on erev Shabbat one should doubt this — for there is no joy except in meat — meaning when the Temple stood; and on this Magen Avraham wrote he did not look in Sanhedrin daf 9 — meaning regarding ben sorer u'moreh that we say as long as he is whole there is in him because of joy; and these words of the Gemara were brought in Taz siman 552 on erev Shabbat.
