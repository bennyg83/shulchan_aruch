(א) If not, etc. — It appears straightforward per the Mechaber: if the fast obligation was already upon him and he wished to fulfill it on Erev Shabbat, he does not fulfill it thereby, since he did not complete it properly and must fast another day. Know that even per the Mechaber, who did not cite Rambam's view inside — even for the one who says it appears he holds this is not called completion at all in what he accepted Shabbat in Maariv prayer, etc.

(ב) And one says he need not complete — see in Mishna Berurah on this "one says" it is not correct to wait from when he comes from synagogue until nightfall if he wishes to eat and is delayed because of the fast. Know that from Raavad's words brought in Beit Yosef it appears that immediately when the sun set, and he already accepted Shabbat upon himself even though he has not yet prayed, it is also called completion thereby. It appears this "one says" and Raavad's reasoning, etc.
