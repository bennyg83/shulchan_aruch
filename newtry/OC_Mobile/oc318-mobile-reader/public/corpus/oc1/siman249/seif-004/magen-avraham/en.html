When they leave the synagogue. This is considered completion because he fasts until after accepting Shabbos. On a weekday, however, completion does not occur until nightfall, as Maharil states there. A fast for a troubling dream must be completed, as Darkei Moshe states there. It appears to me that one who fasts every Friday and completed the first fast must conduct himself this way forever. The same applies to one who observes the fasts listed in siman 579, as stated at the end of Yoreh Deah siman 402, and likewise to the eve of Rosh Chodesh when it falls on Friday. Rama’s wording implies that in a communal fast a stipulation is ineffective, since the matter does not depend upon the individual, as stated in siman 572:2. Although Maharil implies there that a stipulation is effective, that was because it was not a full communal fast. See Tur, siman 567.
