4) There: So that he will reach his home, etc. Many transgressions can result from this. When he arrives close to Shabbos, his host, or even those in his own home, may cook additional food for him and desecrate Shabbos. All the more so, he may be delayed on the road until it is actually dark, leading to numerous Shabbos violations involving carrying out, bringing in, leaving the techum, and the requirement that his animal rest. A person should therefore take all this to heart and not allow the evil inclination to entice him by saying, 'The day is still long and the road is good.' Chayei Adam, klal 1, paragraph 7.
