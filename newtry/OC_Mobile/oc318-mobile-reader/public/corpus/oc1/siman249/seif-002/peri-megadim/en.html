If he did not specify — Taz: ostensibly missing the word "not" in "unspecified" and the word "but" in "unspecified"; and his intention — since the Mechaber wrote "if he did not specify" and did not write "if he does not specify," etc. — implies l'chatchila it is fitting to accept unspecified and fast until nightfall; to this he challenged — why is it not better to specify first that he fast only until his departure from the beit knesset, for one view holds it is forbidden to fast on erev Shabbat (Peri Megadim siman 7); and even for Tur and Mechaber — the way of working people is to fast so they enter Shabbat with appetite; but to fast into the addition of Shabbat until nightfall — it is more fitting not to fast; and see siman 266 Magen Avraham seif 2 — he may eat immediately and we do not say he should eat at night with appetite; and one view holds he need not even an olive's bulk at night:
