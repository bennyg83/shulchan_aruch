It is forbidden on Friday to establish a meal and drinking feast that one is not accustomed to have on weekdays, even if it is an engagement meal, because of the honor of Shabbos, so that one will enter Shabbos with an appetite. And the entire day is included in the prohibition. {Rama: And a meal whose time falls on Friday, such as a circumcision or pidyon haben, is permitted as explained above, and this is the prevalent custom.} And to eat and drink without establishing a meal, even a meal of the sort one ordinarily eats on a weekday, it is permitted by the strict law to begin throughout the day; but it is a mitzvah to refrain from establishing a meal that one is accustomed to have on weekdays from the ninth hour onward.
