[4] Engagement feasts, and so forth. The same applies to wedding feasts, since they could have been held on another day. Magen Avraham resolves the ruling below in siman 444, where an engagement feast is considered a mitzvah feast even on Pesach eve, by explaining that there the engagement took place on Pesach eve. Becoming engaged then is permitted lest another precede him, and the same applies on Friday; he may therefore also hold the engagement feast. Here, however, where the engagement took place previously, the feast may not be held on Friday. It appears to me that even if he became engaged on Friday, when the feast can be postponed until the next day or another day, it is forbidden to hold it on Friday. The later ruling concerns a case in which postponement is impossible. The words of Olas Tamid here contradict his words there; see there.
