And there are those who say — Taz: the Mechaber concluded regarding a dream fast he completes, even though so too one who always fasts; one could say a dream fast is lighter, for he did not accept it at minchah; and Rabbenu Yerucham who wrote one view — in an individual fast one does not complete — nevertheless one could say regarding a dream fast I agree with the Mechaber that he completes, for on Shabbat itself one can fast, and also danger — perhaps it will not help to nullify the dream unless he completes; if so, there is no ruling in Rabbenu Yerucham against a dream fast; and therefore they certainly agree with the Mechaber to complete a dream fast on erev Shabbat; and it will be explained further in siman 288:

And what he wrote regarding yahrzeit — he was not permitted to say: since the first time it fell on a weekday and he did not consider whether it would fall on erev Shabbat — if so, when Shabbat falls in any case he would need release, per Yoreh De'ah siman 214 and responsum 62 on erev Shabbat; and therefore one should not say a communal fast proves, for l'chatchila they accepted explicitly when it falls on Shabbat they do not fast — but we learn the same regarding yahrzeit — it was not like a vow, for unspecified, as explained — if it falls on erev Shabbat he does not fast part of Shabbat. See Eliyah Rabbah siman 11 — he sided with the lenient like Taz — he did not lose; and if so, when it first falls on erev Shabbat it is fitting he stipulate not to complete (and Shelah wrote he has choice in his hand, and Aaron wrote he should stipulate); and when it later falls on a weekday he must complete; and it appears everything depends on his mind — if at first he wants even on a weekday not to complete, he is permitted, even though Aaron wrote to forbid on erev Shabbat — generations are weak. And responsa Chut HaShani siman 66 — I do not have it now; and perhaps they spoke only regarding unspecified — understand. And if it happened once on a weekday and he explicitly conditioned when it falls on erev Shabbat he will not complete — it is obvious he should not complete according to all; and see Eliyah Rabbah on this on erev Shabbat; and even Rashal agrees to this in Shulchan Aruch Nitzchonim gloss after what the Mechaber wrote regarding a dream fast.
