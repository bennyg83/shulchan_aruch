To fast every Friday. Bach writes in the name of the authorities and the Jerusalem Talmud that it is forbidden to fast on Friday, lest one enter Shabbos afflicted. He writes similarly in siman 686. This also appears from the Mishnah in the fourth chapter of Taanis, and is stated by Mateh Moshe in the name of Midrash Tanchuma, Bereishis, by Megillas Taanis, by Hagahos Maimoniyos in the laws of Purim, by the glosses to Semak, and by Sheiltos, Vayakhel. Rabbi Avin’s fasting every Friday must therefore have been because he was delicate, so that if he ate during the day he would not eat the evening meal with appetite, as stated in siman 470. This is the principal ruling.
