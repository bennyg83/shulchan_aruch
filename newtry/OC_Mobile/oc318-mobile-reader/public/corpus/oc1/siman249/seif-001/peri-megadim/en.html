It is forbidden to establish, etc. Taz and Rambam chapter 30 of Laws of Shabbat halachah 4, and Maggid Mishneh and Kesef Mishneh, and Maggid Mishneh who wrote: and to eat and drink without establishing a meal — even a meal one is accustomed to on weekdays — all day is permitted. The wording "even" in "even a meal one is accustomed to" means to eat without establishing a meal at all — without any establishment at all; even the accustomed is permitted and there is not even a mitzvah to refrain; but to establish the accustomed — there is a mitzvah. But the Mechaber's wording is difficult in what he wrote "even a meal one is accustomed to" — for this is difficult like Maggid Mishneh; and therefore Taz explains that even if he extended the established meal — it is not "accustomed," etc.; and he explained in Rambam's words a different explanation; and according to this, the leniency of establishing a meal without drinking is permitted all day by law, only a mitzvah from the ninth hour, etc.; and even a meal one is not accustomed to is permitted, and before the ninth hour there is not even a mitzvah.
