A) [Section 1] No more than 3 Parsots, etc., from the beginning of the day, are to be carried out in the Shash. And so per the DKK, an average person walks ten farsats a day, the walking time will be from the morning until about a third of the day, and two thirds of the day will be to prepare the needs of a meal. And the PZ means a model walking in a cart or riding a horse will not walk more than this lesson so that there will be time to prepare the meal. Whoever wrote it looks like Dadin walking on his feet, but when walking in a cart or riding a horse, the world used to make it easier and let them be better off being shoggin and not being mazidin. And M.M. should be careful not to leave until the evening, Efi, in a hurry to come to his city and his home, because many times those who desecrate Shabbat fail to come to the city until nightfall, but sit a horse or two far away from the city in a village, and he will not desecrate Shabbat.
