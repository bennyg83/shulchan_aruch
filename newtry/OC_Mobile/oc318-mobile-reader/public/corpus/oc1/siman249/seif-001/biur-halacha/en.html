One may not travel on Friday, and so forth. At first glance, it appears that when one travels for a mitzvah he may continue until close to evening, as stated in siman 248:1; see there. This is so even though there too he thereby loses Shabbos enjoyment, as stated in seif 2. Perhaps, however, our case is more stringent because he made no preparations at all. See below, siman 250, in Biur Halachah, where we will discuss this. Nevertheless, it appears that one may be lenient here and travel more than three parasangs if he estimates that he will arrive while much of the day still remains.
And he will be able to prepare, and so forth. See Olas Shabbos, who is stringent even when the traveler carries his provisions with him, for according to the second formulation of the Gemara the Sages made no distinction. This also appears from the Beit Yosef, whose words I cited in Mishnah Berurah, seif katan 5. Eliyah Rabbah, however, disagrees and inclines toward leniency in this case; see there.
