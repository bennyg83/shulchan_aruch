A) [Seif 1] Although it is established that things in writing you may not say them by heart—anything that is regular and fluent in everyone's mouth, etc.—but if it is not fluent in everyone's mouth, even though it is fluent in his mouth, it is forbidden to say it by heart. Darkei Moshe note 2 in the name of Or Zarua. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. However the Bach wrote that it is not specifically "fluent in everyone's mouth" but since it is fluent in his mouth it is permitted to say it by heart—see there; and so is implied from the words of Orach Chayyim page 5 as Beit Kenesset wrote letter 5, and so from the words of R' Yeshayah chapter 2 of Megillah as Eliyah Rabbah wrote letter 1, and so is the opinion of Maamar Mordechai note 2. Therefore one may be lenient where it is impossible so that he not cease from his studies.
