Siman 61 "although" etc.—Gittin 62a, Temurah 14b.

Every matter etc.—but the essence is as the Tur wrote in the name of the Rosh that only to fulfill the congregation's obligation, and so wrote Tosafot in Temurah there, and so wrote the Kolbo in the name of the Rif, and the Gemara is arranged at the end of Taanit after praying, and so wrote old Tosafot in tractate Yoma page 70, s.v. "and on Yom Kippur"—the reason is that the prohibition is only to fulfill the many's obligation, and they brought proof from that in Taanit that R' Yochanan said "and how can an individual…" etc., and so is the essence.
