[1] By heart etc.—lest they not notice missing and extra letters and divergent reading and writing, and for this reason the Ran wrote in the chapter "one who reads the Megillah" that things by heart you may not write them, lest one be uncertain that he did not understand the language, and when they are spoken only by heart one cannot establish them except from the mouth of a teacher who will explain the interpretation well; however for us it is permitted to write.
