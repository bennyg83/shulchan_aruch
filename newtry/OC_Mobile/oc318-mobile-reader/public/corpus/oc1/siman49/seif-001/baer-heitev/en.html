Everyone. If a passage is not fluent in everyone's mouth, it is forbidden to recite it by heart even if it is fluent in one's own mouth. Olas Tamid writes that if it is fluent in one's mouth, one may even recite it by heart to discharge others. Bach, however, writes that everyone agrees that one may not discharge others through a recitation by heart, even when the passage is fluent in one's mouth. See Magen Avraham. One should therefore be careful not to recite anything by heart except what is mentioned here in Shulchan Aruch. Magen Avraham. Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 175, permits reciting Tehillim by heart on Friday night. See there. Tosafos in Bava Kama states that a blind person may recite by heart because, “It is a time to act for Hashem; they have violated Your Torah.” Regarding reading the weekly portion by heart while the chazzan reads, I personally am careful not to do so, but I do not protest against others. Radbaz, part 1, siman 135. See Knesses HaGedolah.
