The Gra wrote that even to fulfill others' obligation one may say it by heart if it is fluent; but the Bach wrote that to fulfill others' obligation, even if fluent in his mouth he is not permitted to say it by heart—see Magen Avraham. One should be careful not to say anything by heart except what is mentioned here in Shulchan Aruch. Magen Avraham. And in responsum Chavat Yair siman 175 he permits saying Tehillim by heart before praying; and see Tosafot tractate Bava Kamma—a blind person may read by heart because of "a time to act for Hashem, they have voided Your Torah."
