But if it is not fluent in everyone's mouth, even though fluent in his mouth, it is forbidden—for what we say about tefillin that they are written by heart is because they are reviewed—also here so, for all scribes are accustomed in them (Darkei Moshe, Or Zarua)—and the Bach ruled as the Tur wrote that to fulfill others one may not say by heart, and so is in Taanit page 28 side 1; therefore they are accustomed that the congregation says parashat "Who is like You" and "the Omnipresent will reign" by themselves—because in their time the cantor would pray by heart and could not fulfill them with verses—and there are differing opinions, and see Avi HaEzri. Therefore it seems one should be careful not to say anything by heart except what is mentioned here in Shulchan Aruch. It is in Tosafot tractate Bava Kamma—a blind person may read by heart because of "a time to act for the Omnipresent, they have voided Your Torah." To read the parashah by heart when the shaliach tzibbur reads—I am careful but I do not object to others [Radbaz volume 1 siman 135], and see there that for one reason only Shema it is forbidden to read by heart [thus].
