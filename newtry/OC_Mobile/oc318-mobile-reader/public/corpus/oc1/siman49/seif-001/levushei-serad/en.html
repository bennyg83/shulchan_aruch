Magen Avraham s.k. 1: They are accustomed that the congregation says. That is, when they reach the verses that are in the order of prayer, the shaliach tzibbur is silent and the congregation says each one in his mouth, and they do not rely on the shaliach tzibbur—because even if the shaliach tzibbur has it fluent and says by heart, nevertheless since in everyone's mouth it is not fluent he does not fulfill their obligation per Beit Shmuel. And what he mentioned specifically these verses—even though there are many verses in the order of prayer—see Eliyah Rabbah at length.
