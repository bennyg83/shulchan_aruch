Regarding standing. Provided it resembles a settled halachah and contains nothing distracting. Nevertheless, one should not forgo communal prayer because of this. Certainly, if he fears that the prayer time will pass, he may pray.
Regarding, etc. The later authorities rule likewise.
To pray. Even if the prayer time will pass, when no one else is available to attend to the communal need. See above, siman 70. He need not pray Minchah twice as compensation for Shacharis, because since he was exempt from it according to law at the time of its obligation, no compensation is required at all.
