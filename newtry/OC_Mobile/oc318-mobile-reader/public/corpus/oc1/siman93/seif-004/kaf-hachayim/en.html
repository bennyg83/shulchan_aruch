(4) There — and one hour after prayer, etc. And if he can delay until fewer than ten remain in the beit knesset, it is better that he not cause the destruction of the building by removing the Shechinah — for he delayed until no place remained for the Shechinah to rest; and further, sitting in the beit knesset is a mitzvah in itself, as it is written, "Happy are those who dwell in Your house" — and from this they relied that a person must wait one hour before prayer and one hour after prayer: "Happy are those who dwell in Your house" — before prayer; "they will yet praise You, Selah" — after prayer. Seder HaYom, Eliyah Rabbah letter 1, Shaliach Tzibbur of Knesset Yisrael 72b; Chassidim LeMoshe letter 2, and he wrote: all the more so that the first ones not leave before the last ten, for that is the main cause of removing the Shechinah — so far.
