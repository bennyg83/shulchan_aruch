4) There: And one hour after prayer, etc. If one can remain until fewer than ten people are left in the synagogue, it is beneficial for him, because he did not cause the building to be demolished and the Divine Presence to depart. He remained until there was no longer a place for the Divine Presence to rest. Furthermore, sitting in the synagogue is a mitzvah in its own right, as it is written, "Praiseworthy are those who dwell in Your house." From here they found support for the requirement that a person wait one hour before prayer and one hour after prayer: "Praiseworthy are those who dwell in Your house" refers to before prayer, and "they will yet praise You, Selah" refers to after prayer. Seder HaYom; Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1; Shalmei Tzibbur, page 158b; Chesed LaAlafim, paragraph 2. Chesed LaAlafim writes: "All the more so, he should not be the first of the final ten to leave, for he is the primary cause of the Divine Presence's departure." End quote.
