From this I have a slight difficulty with what Ma'adanei Melech and Lechem Chamudos page 14 wrote per the view of the Rif and Rambam, that one must seek water for four mil even though prayer time passed — that he must pray two Minchahs if the time passed, for he is considered as having erred, since he is an oness — why, behold he is exempt by law; and one may distinguish, and it requires examination — and see above siman 70.
