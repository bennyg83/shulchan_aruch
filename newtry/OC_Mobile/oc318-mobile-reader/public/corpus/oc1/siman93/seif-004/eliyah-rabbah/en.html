For he need not interrupt, etc. If the time for that prayer passed, the Derishah in Yoreh Deah, siman 341, writes that he need not make it up during the following prayer. This is analogous to a mourner who did not pray Shacharis because he was an onen, who need not pray Minchah twice. The Taz there disputes this ruling. In Nekudos HaKesef, however, he upholds the Derishah’s ruling, because one who was exempt according to law need not make up the prayer. Magen Avraham and Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 70, rule likewise. This poses some difficulty for me regarding the statement of Maadanei Yom Tov and Lechem Chamudos, folio 14, that according to the Rif and Rambam one must travel four mil to seek water even if the prayer time will pass, and that if the time passes he must pray Minchah twice, since he is considered one who erred. Why should this be so? He was prevented by circumstances beyond his control and was exempt according to law. Perhaps a distinction can be drawn, but this requires study. See above, siman 70.
