Seif 4 — one engaged in, etc. Tosafos there on the passage beginning "R' Eliezer," in the name of the Yerushalmi and the Rosh there.

Explanation, etc. Tur.

And one whose view is, etc. And so in Tosefta Berachos chapter 2: writers of books, tefillin, and mezuzot interrupt for Kriat Shema but do not interrupt for prayer. R' [Yehuda HaNasi] says: Just as they do not interrupt for prayer, so they do not interrupt for Kriat Shema. R' Chiya ben Akavya says: Just as they interrupt for Kriat Shema, so they interrupt for prayer. R' Eliezer bar R' Tzadok says: When R' Gamliel and the court were in Yavneh occupied with communal needs, they would not interrupt so as not to remove [focus] from their hearts.
