Seif 4: One who is occupied, etc. Tosafos there, s.v. Rabbi Abahu, in the name of the Yerushalmi, and the Rosh there.
Meaning, etc. Tur.
Others explain, etc. So too, in Tosefta Berachos, chapter 2: Scribes who write Torah scrolls, tefillin, and mezuzos interrupt for Krias Shema but do not interrupt for prayer. Rabbi says: Just as they do not interrupt for prayer, so they do not interrupt for Krias Shema. Rabbi Chanina ben Akavya says: Just as they interrupt for Krias Shema, so they interrupt for prayer. Rabbi Elazar son of Rabbi Tzadok said: When Rabban Gamliel and his beis din were in Yavneh and were occupied with communal needs, they would not interrupt, so as not to divert their attention from them.
