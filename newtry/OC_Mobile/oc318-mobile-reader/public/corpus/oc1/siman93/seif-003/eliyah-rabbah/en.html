Nor from an unsettled halachah, etc. The same applies when one is occupied with communal needs in a matter that entails distraction. It is, however, permitted to enter a lavatory or bathhouse while occupied with an unsettled halachah. See Terumas HaDeshen, siman 41, who discusses the reason at length. Magen Avraham writes that if one was engaged in halachic analysis and the congregation began to pray, he should pray with them.
