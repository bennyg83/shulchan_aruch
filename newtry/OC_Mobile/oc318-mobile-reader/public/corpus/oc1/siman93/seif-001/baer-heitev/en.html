One hour. Terumat HaDeshen proved from the gemara that although wherever we say "hour" it means a short time, here it means one hour of the twelve hours in a day; however, this is for the pious, and for the rest of the people a short time suffices. Magen Avraham.

That he should have intent. The Arizal would pray within the book so that he would have very great intent. And everything is according to what a person feels in his soul. Magen Avraham.
