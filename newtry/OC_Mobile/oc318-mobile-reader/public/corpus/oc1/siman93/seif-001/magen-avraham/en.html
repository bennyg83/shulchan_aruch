One hour. Rabbeinu Yonah proved from the Gemara that although wherever the Sages say “an hour” it generally means a short while, here it means one of the twelve hours of the day. This applies to the pious, while for ordinary people a short while suffices, as written at the end of seif katan 20 of siman 90. The Zohar on Bereishis states that one should recite, “But as for me, through Your abundant kindness,” etc., before entering the synagogue.
So that he may concentrate. The Arizal would pray from within the prayer book so that he could concentrate intensely. See Pri Etz Chaim, Shaar HaKavanos. Everything depends upon what a person senses within himself.
