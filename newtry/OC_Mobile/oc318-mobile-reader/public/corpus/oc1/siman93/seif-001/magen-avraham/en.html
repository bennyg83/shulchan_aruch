One hour. Terumat HaDeshen proved from the gemara that although everywhere we say "hour" the meaning is a short time, here the meaning is one hour of the twelve in the day — this is for the pious; and for the rest of the people a short hour suffices, as written in siman 90, end of note 20. It is written in the Zohar, Bereshit, that one should say "and I, in Your abundant kindness," etc., before entering the beit habechirah.

In order to direct. The Arizal would pray from within the siddur in order to direct greatly (the kavanot), and everything according to what a person feels in his soul.
