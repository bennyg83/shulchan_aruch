[1] He should wait one hour, etc. But the Tur's wording is "wait a little," and one must say that specifically the early pious ones would wait one hour, and for us a little suffices; and so wrote Kesef Mishnah chapter 4 of Hilchos Tefillah and Lechem Chamudos page 40 and Lechem Mishneh; and the same law applies after prayer. And behold, in Lechem Mishneh there he deduced from the Tur and Rambam that specifically we require sitting a little — see there. And in my humble opinion it appears like the Rambam chapter 11 of Hilchos Tefillah and Tur siman 131 that sitting — the language of waiting — applies even if he waits while standing; and so wrote the Bach. But one may distinguish in his reason: here specifically we require sitting, for through sitting one settles more to have intent; and after prayer, when he sits, the prayer is not upon him as a burden, which is not so with waiting while standing. But there, his reason is that one should perform a mitzvah in the synagogue, and in waiting while standing there is also a mitzvah. Seder HaYom wrote: if one can delay until fewer than ten remain in the synagogue, it is better that he not cause the removal of the Shechinah — for he delayed until no place remained for the Shechinah to rest; and further, sitting in the synagogue is a mitzvah in itself.
