(1) One hour — Terumat HaDeshen proved from the gemara that although everywhere we say "hour" the meaning is a short time, here the meaning is one hour of the twelve hours in the day; however, this is for the pious, and for the rest of the people a short hour suffices — that he wait before he begins, which is the measure of walking eight tefachim, as written in siman 90, end of note 20; see there. Seder Hayom wrote: If one can delay until fewer than ten remain in the beit haknesset, it is good for him; see there his reason — also because sitting in the beit haknesset is a mitzvah in its own right.

(2) That he should direct — the Arizal would pray from within the siddur in order to direct greatly, also so as not to omit a point; and everything according to what a person feels in his soul.
