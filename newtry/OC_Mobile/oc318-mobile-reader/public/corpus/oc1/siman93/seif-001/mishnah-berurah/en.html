One hour. Rabbeinu Yonah proved from the Gemara that although wherever the Sages say “an hour” it means a short while, here it means one of the twelve hours of the day. This applies to the pious. For ordinary people, a short while before beginning suffices, namely the time needed to walk eight handbreadths, as written at the end of seif 20 of siman 90; see there. The Seder HaYom writes that if one can remain until fewer than ten people are left in the synagogue, it is good for him to do so; see there for his reason. Furthermore, sitting in the synagogue is itself a mitzvah.
That he may concentrate. The Arizal would pray from the siddur so that he could concentrate intensely and also avoid swallowing any vowel point. Everything depends upon what a person senses within himself.
