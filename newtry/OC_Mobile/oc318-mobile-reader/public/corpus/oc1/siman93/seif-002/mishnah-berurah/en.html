(3) With awe and humility — as it is said, "Serve Hashem with fear" (Psalms 2:11) — meaning prayer, which for us is in place of the sacrificial service; perform it with fear.

(4) Not from, etc. — meaning that it is a mitzvah from the preferred way that one should stand to pray only with awe and humility; but since not everyone can be careful to bring humility and awe into his heart, at least he should not pray from laughter, but rather from the joy of a mitzvah [Bach and Eliyah Rabbah].
