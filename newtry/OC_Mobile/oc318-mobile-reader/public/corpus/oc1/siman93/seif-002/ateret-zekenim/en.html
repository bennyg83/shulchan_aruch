One should not stand to pray, etc., except out of joy — such as words of consolation, etc. And so they practice at Shacharit near [the verses of] the Exodus from Egypt, which are joy and consolation; and at Minchah near Tehillah le-David; and at Maariv near the set readings before him, such as "for Hashem will not abandon His people," and the like. And one must stand in his place to prepare himself for prayer when the chazan begins "Tehillot le-El Elyon" (Maharil). And one who does not wish to sing with the shaliach tzibbur and wishes to pray with the congregation should wait here, as Maharam did regarding the krov'tz.
