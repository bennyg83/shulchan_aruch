He should not stand up from laughter, etc. Meaning, the ideal fulfillment of the mitzvah is to stand for prayer only with awe and submission. Since not every person is able to take care to instill submission and awe in his heart, he should at least not pray from a state of laughter, but from the joy of a mitzvah. The Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah writes: Concerning those who stand in the house of prayer and present themselves as tired and unable to stand, Scripture says, “You did not call upon Me, Yaakov, for you grew weary of Me, Yisrael.” He stands in the marketplace all day and does not grow weary, but at the time of prayer he grows weary.
