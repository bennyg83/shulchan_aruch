[2] He should not stand out of levity, etc. Explanation: it is an ideal mitzvah that one stands only out of awe and submissiveness; but since not every person can be careful to bring awe and submissiveness into his heart — in any case he should not pray out of levity, but out of mitzvah-joy. She'eirei Knesset HaGedolah wrote: those who stand in the house of prayer and show themselves as tired and cannot stand upon them — Scripture says, "You did not call upon Me, Jacob, for you wearied Me, Israel" — all day he stands in the market and does not pray, and at prayer time he prays.
