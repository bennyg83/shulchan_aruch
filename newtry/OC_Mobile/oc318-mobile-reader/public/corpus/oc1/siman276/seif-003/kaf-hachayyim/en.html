3) There: It is forbidden to everyone, etc. The Shulchan Aruch does not state here regarding kindling a lamp that after Shabbos even another Jewish person must wait the time required for the act to be performed, because it relies upon what is written below in siman 325 regarding a non-Jew who made a coffin, etc.; see there. The same law applies when a non-Jew drew water for a Jewish person or made a ramp by which a Jewish person could descend: after Shabbos they are forbidden for the time required for the act to be performed. This is demonstrated by Rambam's words at the beginning of chapter 6, and is obvious. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 1.
