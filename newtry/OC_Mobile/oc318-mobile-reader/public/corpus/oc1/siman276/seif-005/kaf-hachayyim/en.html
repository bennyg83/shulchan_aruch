5) There: It is forbidden to everyone, etc. The prohibition is rabbinic, because if benefit were permitted, the Jewish person might tell the non-Jew to kindle it. Chiddushei Rashba. Ran explains that the concern is that the Jewish person might accustom the non-Jew to doing so on another Shabbos. Mishbetzos Zahav there cites both explanations and writes that they appear to express the same reason as Chiddushei Rashba; see there.
