Se'if katan 2: And hired labor. The Rama's words, ‘or if he did so,’ etc., explain what he wrote first concerning whether a fixed price was set, etc. Alternatively, the Rama means that this applies whether the non-Jew lights it for pay or without pay; he then returns to explain that when it is done for pay, there is no distinction between contract work and hired labor. Gloss, seif 1: On his own initiative; when the Jew did not instruct him. Even though the non-Jew acts for the Jew, they did not trouble the Jew to leave the house, although he inevitably benefits; he is merely forbidden to make use of the light.
