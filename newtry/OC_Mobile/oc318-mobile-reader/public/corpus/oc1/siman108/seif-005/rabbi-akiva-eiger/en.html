Taz, seif katan 10. That at least in the second prayer he certainly intended it. In my humble opinion, according to Taz's own distinction in seif katan 9 concerning why one does not recite Havdalah in both prayers, as opposed to saying Mashiv HaRuach in both, the reason is that once he recited Havdalah once and rendered the day weekday, there is no further relevance to reciting Havdalah and making it weekday a second time. Accordingly, here, if he prayed the first prayer for his current obligation and forgot Havdalah, it is proper for him to recite Havdalah in the second prayer, which he prays as compensation, since he has not yet recited Havdalah. Havdalah is relevant even in a compensatory prayer; it is merely inappropriate to recite Havdalah twice. See what I wrote in my gloss to Magen Avraham, siman 294.
