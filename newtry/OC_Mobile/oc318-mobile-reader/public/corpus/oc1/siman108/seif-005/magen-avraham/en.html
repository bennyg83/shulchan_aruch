If he wishes. Therefore, one who was ill or imprisoned in a place that was not clean may, upon leaving, pray all the prayers he missed. Beit Yosef in the name of Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah. If he leaves prison on Rosh Chodesh, he says Yaaleh VeYavo in all of them, and the same applies if he leaves on Shabbos. Knesses HaGedolah.
And he should introduce a novel element. According to the Rama’s ruling there that the novel element must be new to him, this does not apply here.
