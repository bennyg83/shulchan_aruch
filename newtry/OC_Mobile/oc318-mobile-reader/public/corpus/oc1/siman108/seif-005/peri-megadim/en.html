And Bach wrote: for Shacharis and Maariv one needs innovation, since one is not occupied; but for Mincha one does not need innovation, for occupied in his business is like unintentional and does not need innovation. They brought Taz and Eliyah Rabbah 8 — they agreed to Shulchan Aruch.

Question — on Yom Kippur one forgot Mincha prayer until Maariv — what is the law to complete it in Maariv? Also if one forgot Ne'ilah prayer — what? And if one was intentional with Ne'ilah, or forgot both Mincha and Ne'ilah until night — what?

Answer — if one erred with Mincha on Yom Kippur, one prays Maariv twice of weekday, as in seif 10. And so wrote that the time of two prayers passed — meaning Mincha's time is all day; proof from what Tosafos wrote Berachos 26a s.v. miba'ya — Musaf has no teshuvah at night, and derive that the time of two prayers passed for Musaf and Mincha; and they hold there is completion only at the time of the prayer adjacent to it — necessarily, since its time is all day, two times have not passed. And Abudraham wrote, and Rashba — even though he holds there is teshuvah for two prayers, etc. — see there, letter peh, that he wrote; and further that there is no sacrifice at night (Taz brought letter 4). And this is difficult for him — for Rashba, at night the reason is insufficient, he should wait until Shacharis; therefore he said nevertheless they gave a reason for night — that whenever possible immediately, one can also afterward; unlike when impossible immediately, for at night there is no sacrifice — again they did not institute; therefore Mincha's time until darkening, two times have not passed, for Mincha and Ne'ilah are one time for them. See siman 233:11 — Mincha until plag and Ne'ilah at the head of the trees; therefore it appears two times passed; nevertheless after the fact he fulfills until night in Maariv, for it is the prayer adjacent to it. All the more if one forgot Ne'ilah — he completes at night with two of weekday. And if one forgot both, one can also say he completes in Maariv, even though in seif 4 there is no teshuvah — behold the main thing is two times passed; here two times have not passed. And if one was intentional with Ne'ilah, he prays as voluntary if he wishes, and does not need innovation, but he does not stipulate and is not obligated to do so, even though Rav and R' Yehuda disagree — see siman 623, halachah that Ne'ilah is only by day; therefore he is exempt, only permission of voluntary. And if one forgot Mincha and Ne'ilah, it is reasonable he prays obligation and afterward Ne'ilah and afterward Mincha in order (as in Shulchan Aruch), and it is possible since both times are equal, the frequent comes first; nevertheless Ne'ilah is later for us — incident 233.

See Tosafos Berachos 26a s.v. ta'ah — they wrote we did not find they instituted for two prayers; and for intentional, even adjacent, it implies that letter peh — the time of two prayers passed, he is called negligent for not remembering in two times (as in Beitza: for a year you are negligent). Therefore, like intentional, he ruled here voluntary without innovation; and why in Shulchan Aruch he ruled innovation — Pri Megadim Mishneh Berurah there, and here one cannot extend in this.
