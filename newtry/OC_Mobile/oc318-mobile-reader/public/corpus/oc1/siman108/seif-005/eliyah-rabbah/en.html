If he wishes to pray them, etc., and he should introduce something new, etc. Meaning that he prays Minchah, Shacharis, and Ma'ariv. In Piskei Tosafot, however, he disagrees and writes that in our times one should not pray them as voluntary prayers even if he introduces something new. The later authorities did not mention this. According to the Shulchan Aruch and Levush, it is obvious that the same applies to one who was sick or imprisoned and was unable to pray in prison; when he recovers or is released, he prays all the prayers he missed as voluntary prayers with an innovation. The Knesses HaGedolah wrote that it implies that if he recovers or is released on Rosh Chodesh, he mentions Ya'aleh VeYavo in all of them, and similarly on Shabbos. The Radvaz wrote that if the chazzan must pray two prayers, he should intend in his repetition aloud for the makeup prayer and to discharge one who is not expert.
