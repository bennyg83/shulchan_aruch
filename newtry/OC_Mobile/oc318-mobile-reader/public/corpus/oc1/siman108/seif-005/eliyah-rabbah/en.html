(5) If he wished to pray them, etc., and he should innovate, etc. — Explanation: that he pray Minchah, Shacharis, and Maariv. In Piskei Tosafos he disagrees and wrote that in our times one should not pray them as a voluntary prayer and innovate something in them — up to here, and the later authorities did not mention this. According to Shulchan Aruch and Levush it is simple that the same law applies to one who was sick or imprisoned who could not pray while imprisoned; when he recovers or is released he prays all the prayers he missed as a voluntary prayer with innovation. Knesset HaGedolah wrote it is implied when he recovers or is released on Rosh Chodesh he mentions in all of them Yaaleh ve-Yavo, and similarly on Shabbos.
