Therefore, one who was sick or imprisoned, and the place was not clean, when he leaves he should pray all the prayers he missed, and he should pray Ma'ariv, then Minchah, then Shacharis. If he left prison on Rosh Chodesh, he mentions Ya'aleh VeYavo in all of them. But if he left on Shabbos or Yom Tov, he waits until evening and prays, for we hold in siman 107 that voluntary prayers are not prayed on Shabbos and Yom Tov.
And it is proper, etc. See the Peri Megadim, who agreed that it is good for him to stipulate and say: if I am obligated to pray, this is for my obligation; and if not, it is voluntary. In this way he fulfills according to all opinions, for there are several opinions that hold that there is makeup for prayer even after several prayers have passed.
