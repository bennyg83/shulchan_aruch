(18) If one wishes — therefore one who was ill or detained in confinement and the place was not clean when released should pray all the prayers one missed: one prays the evening prayer, then the afternoon, then the morning; and if released from confinement on Rosh Chodesh, one mentions Yaaleh VeYavo in all of them; but if released on Shabbat or Yom Tov, one waits until evening and prays, for the halachah is in siman 107 that voluntary prayer is not prayed on Shabbat and Yom Tov.

(19) And it is proper, etc. — see in Pri Megadim who agreed it is good to stipulate and say: if I am obligated to pray, behold this is for my obligation, and if not, behold this is voluntary; thereby one fulfills according to all opinions, for there are several opinions that hold there is teshuvah for a prayer even for several prayers that passed.
