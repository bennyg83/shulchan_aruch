As a voluntary prayer. Therefore, one who was ill or imprisoned in a place that was not clean should, upon leaving, pray all the prayers he missed. Beit Yosef and Talmidei Rabbeinu Yonah. If he leaves prison on Rosh Chodesh, he mentions Yaaleh VeYavo in all of them. The same applies if he leaves on Shabbos, when he uses the Shabbos text in all of them. Knesses HaGedolah, Magen Avraham, and Peri Chadash.
