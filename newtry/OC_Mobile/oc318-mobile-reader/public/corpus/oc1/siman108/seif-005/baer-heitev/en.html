Nedavah. Therefore one who was sick or detained in imprisonment and the place was not clean when he left, prays all the prayers he missed (Beit Yosef, Terumat HaDeshen). And if he left imprisonment on Rosh Chodesh he mentions in all of them Yaaleh VeYavo. And the same if he left on Shabbos he mentions in all of them that it is Shabbos (Knesset HaGedolah, Magen Avraham, Peri Chadash).
