(5) [Seif 2] If he erred and did not pray — Maariv, he prays Shacharis, two, etc. — When the shaliach tzibur the next day prays two, he need not return and pray a third; rather he prays tomorrow two: one silently for the day's obligation and the second aloud he has intent for make-up prayers and to discharge one not expert — Radbaz part 1 siman 174, Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahos Tur, Olas Tamid note 5, Magen Avraham note 1, Eliyah Rabbah note 5, Shaarei Teshuvah note 8, Chayei Adam part 2 note 2, Peri Chadash note 2, and wrote the law is clear; Kaf HaChayyim siman 22 note 14 and wrote so is the main ruling, not like the Rav the proofreader on Shulchan Aruch page 171b who doubted this — see Beit Yaakov note 9, Raz note 6, Chayei Adam there note 3, Kitzur Shulchan Aruch siman 21 note 9.
