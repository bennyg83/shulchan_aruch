There is tashlumin only for the adjacent prayer alone. And the same applies if he prayed the prayer in between, only that he delayed the tashlumin and did not complete the tashlumin at the time of the third prayer, since already one prayer-time in between has passed (Maharam). And some say even if he did not pray many prayers, all of them have tashlumin; therefore one who was imprisoned or sick, when he leaves the house of prisoners or when he recovers, prays all the prayers he missed (R' Yitzchak, chapter Tefillas HaShachar, and likewise Beit Yosef that Rashba holds thus). And at any rate he must pray first the adjacent prayer—that is, if he forgot Maariv and Shacharis, he prays at Minchah the Minchah prayer first, then that of Shacharis, then that of Maariv. And so always, whether he prays in the usual manner or by way of nedavah and chiddush as mentioned later in seif 5—each time at any rate the adjacent prayer first.
