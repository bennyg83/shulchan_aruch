Seif 4, there is no, etc. At first glance this requires study. Why did the first statement not mention one who failed to pray Minchah, which would be a greater novelty? Alternatively, it should have mentioned only one who failed to pray Maariv. Rather, its meaning is as follows: if he failed to pray Shacharis, even Shacharis, nevertheless he prays only twice. Therefore it did not mention Minchah, since that would follow all the more readily. See Tosafos there, s.v. Taah, etc.
