Musaf has no tashlumin. For he cannot mention the korban of Musaf since its day has passed; and further there is no korban at night—as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashba; and on the first reason: if he completes the Musaf prayer he would say the Maariv prayer twice—as with all tashlumin one finds he would not mention the verses of the korban, for it may be said: since the essence of Musaf prayer has no supplication for mercy, only tashlumin in place of the korban—in this tashlumin is not applicable.
