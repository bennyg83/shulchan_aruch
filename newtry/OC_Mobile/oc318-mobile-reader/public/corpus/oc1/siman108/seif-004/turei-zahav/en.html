Mussaf has no compensation. He can no longer mention the Mussaf offering, because its day has passed, and offerings are not brought at night. Beit Yosef writes this in the name of Rashba. Do not ask why, when compensating Mussaf, he could simply recite the Maariv Amidah twice, as with other compensatory prayers, and thus would not mention the verses of the offering. The answer is that the essential character of Mussaf is not a prayer for mercy but a substitute for the offering. Compensation is therefore inapplicable.
