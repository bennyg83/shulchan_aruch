(א) The Samukha - the latter wrote that there is no benefit to supplementing the prayer of a supplement, that is, if one misses dawn on the day on which there is a supplement, he cannot complete it by praying two supplements. If he forgets Shacharit and prays Mossaf and the time for Shacharit has not yet passed, he will pray Shacharit. He who forgets and did not pray Shacharit and Mussaf and remembers the Mencha, will pray Mencha, followed by Mussaf, followed by Shacharit.
