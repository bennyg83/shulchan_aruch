The adjacent one. The later authorities wrote that Mussaf prayer does not help for makeup, meaning that if he missed Shacharis on a day that has Mussaf, he cannot make it up by praying Mussaf twice. If he forgot Shacharis and prayed Mussaf, and the time of Shacharis has still not passed, nevertheless he should pray Shacharis. If one forgot and prayed neither Shacharis nor Mussaf and remembered at Minchah, he should pray Minchah, then Mussaf, and then Shacharis; and if he prayed Mussaf before Minchah, he has fulfilled.
And not Minchah. The same applies even if he also prayed Minchah: he cannot make up Shacharis in the evening, for the rule is that makeup was instituted only during the time of the prayer adjacent to it, as the Peri Megadim writes.
