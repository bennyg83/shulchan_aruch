(16) The adjoining [prayer] — the later authorities wrote that the Musaf prayer does not help for completion — that is, if one lacked the morning prayer on a day when there is Musaf, one cannot complete by praying Musaf twice. If one forgot the morning prayer and prayed Musaf while morning prayer time had not yet passed, one nevertheless prays the morning prayer. One who forgot and did not pray the morning prayer and Musaf and remembered at the afternoon prayer — one prays the afternoon prayer, then Musaf, then the morning prayer; and if one prayed Musaf before the afternoon prayer, one has fulfilled.

(17) And not the afternoon prayer — the same applies: even if one also prayed the afternoon prayer, one cannot complete in the evening for the morning prayer, for the rule is they did not institute completion except at the time of the prayer adjoining it [Pri Megadim].
