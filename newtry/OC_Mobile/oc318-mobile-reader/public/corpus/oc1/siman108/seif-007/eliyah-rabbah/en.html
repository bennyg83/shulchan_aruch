It has no makeup, etc. For this reason the Nachalas Tzvi wrote that if one forgot Shacharis on Shabbos, he should not make it up with the Mussaf prayer, since Mussaf itself has no makeup; if so, it certainly cannot serve to make up another prayer. Rather, he waits and makes up Shacharis after Minchah. It seems to me that the same applies if he forgot and did not pray until after Mussaf, that he may still pray while the time of Shacharis remains, for Mussaf is not considered another prayer for this purpose. Even according to the poskim I cited above, that if he left the matter of prayer he may not make it up, nevertheless here, since he is still occupied with the Mussaf prayer, it helps for completion.
