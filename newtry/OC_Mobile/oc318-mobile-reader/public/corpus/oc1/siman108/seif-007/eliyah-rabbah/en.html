(7) It has no make-up prayers, etc. — For this reason Nachalas Tzvi wrote if he forgot Shacharis on Shabbos he does not complete it in Musaf prayer, since Musaf itself has no make-up prayers; if so certainly it does not complete; rather he waits and completes Shacharis prayer after Minchah. The same law applies if he forgot and did not pray until after Musaf — he is permitted to pray still while Shacharis time still exists, for Musaf is not like another prayer for this matter; and even per the poskim I wrote above that if he left the matter of prayer he does not complete — nevertheless here since he is still engaged in Musaf prayer the completion helps; see below end of siman 294 if he forgot Maariv on Motzei Shabbos.**** END BLOCK ****
