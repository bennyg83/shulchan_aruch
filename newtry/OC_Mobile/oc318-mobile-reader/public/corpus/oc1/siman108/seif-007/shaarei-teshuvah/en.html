The adjacent. Ba'er Heitev. And he wrote in Be'er HaGolah in the name of Shaarei Yeshuah to Mahari Weil, and he brought from siman 287 that Musaf prayer does not help for tashlumin; and see in Eliyah Rabbah that if one forgot Shacharis and prayed Musaf and the time of Shacharis has not yet passed, he should pray Shacharis—see there. And he wrote further in Be'er HaGolah in the name of Mahari Weil: one who forgot and did not pray Shacharis and Musaf and remembered at Minchah should pray Minchah, and afterward Musaf, and afterward Shacharis prayer. And if he prayed Musaf prayer before Minchah he has fulfilled; and see in Shiyurei Berachah that he retracted from what he wrote in letter 5 to disagree with Mahari Weil; and see in Eliyah Rabbah s.k. 6.
