Seif 7, and he need not, etc. Beit Yosef. This requires study. In what way does this differ from that stated in seif 5? Indeed, it follows all the more readily, since the Halachos Gedolos and several Geonim maintain that even after several intervening prayers there is still compensation. Even were this merely a doubt in halachah, he would not need to introduce a novel element, as stated in seif 11. This is certainly so here, where it is a law of the Gemara. See Bach.
