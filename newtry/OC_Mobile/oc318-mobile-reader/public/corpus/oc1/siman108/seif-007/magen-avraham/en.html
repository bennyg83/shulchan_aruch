He acted deliberately. An incident occurred in which a person came to the synagogue close to Maariv, descended before the ark, and prayed Maariv, although he had not yet prayed Minchah. It appears to me that this is not comparable to one who deliberately failed to pray, because the time for Minchah had not yet ended. One should not argue that he can no longer pray Minchah because he has already treated the time as night, as stated at the end of siman 30. Here it is different, since at the very least he may pray Maariv twice. It is specifically regarding Shabbos that we say in siman 263:15 that he may not do so. Furthermore, he may be regarded as having erred, because he thought it was permissible to pray Maariv first. It also appears to me that on Rosh Chodesh he should say Yaaleh VeYavo in the second prayer, since he has already accepted Rosh Chodesh upon himself, as stated at the beginning of siman 422.
