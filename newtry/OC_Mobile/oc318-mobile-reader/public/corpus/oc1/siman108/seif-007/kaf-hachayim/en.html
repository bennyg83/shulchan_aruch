(7) And know — that even ten who did not pray Shacharis or Maariv pray two and that suffices for them (I believe thus it should read, and what is written in books and that is not enough is a printing error, for he returns to what Magen Avraham wrote in the name of Radbaz that the shaliach tzibur fulfills through repetition, and comes to say not specifically the shaliach tzibur but the same applies to ten, and such is the continuation of the language as is clear to one who examines) in what the shaliach tzibur repeats the prayer for them. However certainly for ten who did not pray Minchah and pray Maariv twice, the shaliach tzibur may not repeat the prayer on account of Minchah, for they only enacted it in its time; that the shaliach tzibur repeats is not found for make-up prayers — this is simple; Peri Megadim Eshel Avraham note 1, and they must intend as written in the previous note.**** END BLOCK ****
