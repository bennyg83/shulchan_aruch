He prayed. An incident occurred with one who came to the synagogue close to Maariv and went down before the ark and prayed Maariv and had not yet prayed Minchah. It appears to me this is not called deliberate and it has tashlumin; and it appears to me that on Rosh Chodesh he mentions Yaaleh VeYavo in the second, for he has already accepted Rosh Chodesh upon himself. Magen Avraham. Peri Chadash wrote: it appears that what the Mechaber said above in siman 76—that one who read in a place where one should be concerned about feces and found it afterward must return and read—is specifically when the time of prayer has not yet passed, or in the compensatory prayer; and such as when he completed his prayer he still had opportunity to pray more, only that afterward he was distracted or compelled. But if when he prayed in the proper place to be concerned about feces, there is no further opportunity afterward to pray another prayer, it is "crooked cannot be corrected" and he has no tashlumin—for just as where we consider him negligent to require him to return and pray because he did not check, here too he is considered negligent regarding that he has no tashlumin. But regarding a drunkard one cannot say thus, for there a person is not fit until his wine passes from him—see there.
