Or. Was under compulsion. See the Taz, and so he wrote in Yoreh Deah there. See siman 93, Magen Avraham seif katan 5, and the Eliyah Rabbah there, seif katan 4, in the name of the Nekudos HaKesef, like the Perishah. It requires study whether this is comparable to what is below in seif 11, where a doubtful dispute is like a doubt whether he prayed, as with Krias Shema, etc., or whether many disagree with him and it is not considered a doubtful dispute in such a case. If so, he may not pray a voluntary prayer here without an innovation. If he wishes, he should pray voluntarily with an innovation, as in seif 5, and it is proper to do so, for there too the Bahag holds that he should pray makeup, only many disagree with him. So it seems to me.
