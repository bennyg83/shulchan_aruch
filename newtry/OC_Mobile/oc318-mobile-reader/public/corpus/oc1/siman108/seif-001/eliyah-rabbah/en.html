And if he reversed, etc. Meaning that he had in mind that the first prayer should be for the makeup prayer. The Levush wrote the reason: when there is something frequent and something not frequent, the frequent one comes first. This is difficult, for in Menachos 49 it says that the frequent one comes first only as a mitzvah, but not to invalidate after the fact. Therefore the Ma'adanei Melech at the beginning of Perek Tefillas HaShachar wrote another reason, so that one should not come to error and pray the makeup prayer even outside the time of prayer and of the adjacent prayer, and put it first when it is not the time of prayer. According to what I wrote in siman 89, that one may pray Shacharis until the time of minchah gedolah, this is difficult. It seems to me that the reason is that the makeup prayer is accepted only incidentally to the prayer that is now due, but when he prays the makeup prayer before the prayer that is now due, it is not accepted, since its time has passed. See below, seif 3. It also seems to me, according to sod, that one should not weaken the attribute that applies in its time by giving precedence to another attribute; understand this. The Magen Avraham wrote that one may not eat before he prays the second prayer, and if he began, he does not interrupt.
