(1) And if he reversed, etc. — Explanation: he had in mind that the first would be for make-up prayer. The reason the Levush wrote: frequent and infrequent — frequent comes first. It is difficult, for in Menachos 49a it says for a mere mitzvah frequent comes first but not to prevent fulfillment. Therefore Maadaney Melekh at the beginning of the chapter on Shacharis prayer wrote another reason — so that one should not come to a mishap of praying make-up prayers even not at the time of prayer and the adjacent one, and preceding not at the time of prayer — up to here. According to what I wrote in siman 89 that one may pray Shacharis until the time of Minchah Gedolah, it is difficult. In my view the reason is because a make-up prayer is accepted only along with the future prayer; but when one prays the make-up before the future prayer it is not accepted since its time has passed; see below seif 3. I also see a reason per the sod that one should not weaken the attribute that operates in its time to advance another attribute — understand.
