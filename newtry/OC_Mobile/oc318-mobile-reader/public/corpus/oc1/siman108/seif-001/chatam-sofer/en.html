Rather necessarily the Gemara holds that if so, what difference does it make if he distinguished in both of them — let it suffice that even in thought alone he does not fulfill his obligation; rather necessarily the Baraisa deals with one who intended the first for obligation and the second for make-up, and even so he did not fulfill his obligation; and one might think because of distinguishing we send him back, and it asks well and leaves it as a difficulty; nevertheless the Rif and Rabbeinu Hai hold that even though he intended the first for obligation, since he distinguished in the second, speech comes and negates his intent; but it is always proven that with thought alone he does not fulfill his obligation, like Rama:
