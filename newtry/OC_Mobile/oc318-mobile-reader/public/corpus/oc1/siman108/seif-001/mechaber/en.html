One Who Did Not Pray Due To A Mistake, Or An Extenuating Circumstance, Or On Purpose. Containing 12 S'ifim If one erred
or was forced [by circumstance] and did not pray the morning prayer, one should pray the afternoon prayer twice: the first is the afternoon prayer, and the second as a make-up. If one inverted [the order], one has not fulfilled one obligation in prayer for the prayer which is a make-up, and one needs to go back and pray it [again]. And the same law applies in every case in which one must pray a make-up prayer.
