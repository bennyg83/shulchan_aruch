(In Shulchan Aruch seif 3) Specifically at the time of prayer — Mishnah Berurah: meaning, if one did not pray the evening prayer and forgot until after one prayed — even if one prayed the great afternoon prayer and afternoon prayer time still remains for everyone in the world. Similarly, if one prayed the evening prayer at the beginning of the night — although the evening prayer [may be said] all night — one cannot complete, since one did not remember at the time of the adjoining obligatory prayer; this is proven from the words of Chiddushei Rashba at the beginning of chapter Taanit, brought in Beit Yosef. And what Magen Avraham wrote in note 9 — that case too refers to when one mentioned at one's prayer time the evening prayer for which one had not prayed the afternoon prayer.
