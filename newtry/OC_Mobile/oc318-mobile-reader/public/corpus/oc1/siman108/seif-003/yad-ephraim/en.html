In Shulchan Aruch seif 3—specifically at the time of prayer, etc.; see in Netiv Chayyim, who wrote that if he did not pray Shacharis (there [the text has] Maariv, and it is an error; and in Mishnah Berurah he copied as it is in error and must be corrected as stated) and it was forgotten by him at the time of Minchah prayer—even though he prayed an early Minchah, there is still time of Minchah prayer for the entire world; and likewise at Maariv, if he prayed at the beginning of the night, even though its time is the entire night—nevertheless he cannot complete, since he was not reminded at the time of the adjacent obligatory prayer—see there. And see in Eliyah Rabbah in the name of Malbushei Yom Tov that this means when he waited a long time after Maariv prayer, since he is not engaged in prayer it is not the time for tashlumin; and Eliyah Rabbah brought some proofs that the entire prayer time helps, and nevertheless concludes to be stringent and pray in it. And see in Ateres Zekenim who wrote one should not complete Shacharis prayer immediately after chatzos before the time of Minchah prayer arrives, etc.; and it requires study, for by law it is already the time of Minchah prayer after six hours, only we are not expert as written siman 233; but one may say that nevertheless, since he has not yet fulfilled, we do not call it prayer time in him; and one may also say that if we say it is prayer time, then again he must advance the obligation of its hour; and see above siman 88 and in Turei Zahav and Peri Chadash here and there; and the essence of the law is from the words of Rashba, and there it is implied as Turei Zahav wrote—see there.
