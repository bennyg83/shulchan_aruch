The questioner thought therefore the first did not count, because the first is Motzei Shabbos prayer and he did not make Havdalah in it — it is like one who did not pray; and on this he asked, for we learned if one forgot Havdalah we do not make him repeat it; and he concluded in the difficulty. On this Rosh wrote one may answer that even though it is like one who prayed, nevertheless because he revealed his intent, etc., not so when he did not make Havdalah in either or made Havdalah in both where there is no revelation of intent — he fulfilled.**** END BLOCK ****
