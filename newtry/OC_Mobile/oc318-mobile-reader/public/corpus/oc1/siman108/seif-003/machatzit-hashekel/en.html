(Seif katan 3) Shacharis twice. It appears to me that it is forbidden, etc., for the time of the second prayer has already arrived.
See siman 88 and siman 232. For we maintain that since Mincha is D'Rabbanan, even if one began in a forbidden manner he does not interrupt. Granted, above in siman 89, seif 5, there is an opinion that he must interrupt for Shacharis. That is because it is more stringent, for they based it upon the verse, You shall not eat over the blood, meaning, do not eat before you have prayed for your blood. This is unlike here, where he already prayed Shacharis before eating, and there is no issue of You shall not eat over the blood. However, he wrote in seif 3 that one makes up the prayer only during the time of prayer, and the Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of the Perishah as follows: he should not delay except the time it takes to walk four cubits or to recite Ashrei, and he should immediately pray the makeup prayer. If so, when he is engaged in a meal and does not interrupt, there is no doubt that he delays longer than this measure, so how can he make it up after his meal? In Lechem Chamudos there, he wrote from the implication of the Rashba's words as follows: if he left the matter of prayer and went away, he no longer has any connection to making it up. If so, where he sat down to eat after Shacharis, he removed himself from the matter of prayer, so how can he make it up after finishing his meal? The Magen Avraham does not hold that all this is only initially, but the language of the Rashba cited by Lechem Chamudos implies that it applies even after the fact; see there.
