Se'if 3: “Specifically during the time of prayer.” This means that the compensatory prayer should immediately follow the current prayer, allowing only enough time for his mind to become settled. This appears from the words of the Raavad and Rashba, and likewise from the words of the Rambam and Rikash. Pri Chadash writes the same.
Magen Avraham, seif katan 7. The same applies if he was released on Shabbos. This is difficult, for he must pray those prayers as voluntary prayers, and that is inapplicable on Shabbos, as stated above in siman 107. This requires study.
