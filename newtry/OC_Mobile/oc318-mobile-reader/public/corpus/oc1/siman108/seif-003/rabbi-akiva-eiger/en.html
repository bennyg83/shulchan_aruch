Seif 3 — specifically at the time of prayer; meaning, that it should be adjacent to it immediately, so that one's mind settles on it. So it appears from the words of Ra'avad and Rashba, and so it appears from the words of Rambam and Rokeach, and so too wrote Peri Chadash.
