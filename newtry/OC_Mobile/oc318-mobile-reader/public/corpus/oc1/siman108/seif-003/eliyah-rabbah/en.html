(3) He says Ashrei between, etc. — This is the language of Bach. Indeed what Semag wrote, that even when praying Maariv twice one must interrupt with Ashrei. The kabbalist Maharam Rekanati was very careful not to say Ashrei after Minchah prayer — up to here his wording. At first glance it is difficult, for if so when praying Minchah twice he would not interrupt with Ashrei since it is after Minchah prayer; even though Bach holds this is truly so, nevertheless he should have brought this proof above. And further it is difficult against She'eirei Knesset HaGedolah and Olas Tamid who ruled like the Levush that if one prays Minchah twice one interrupts with Ashrei, and they also cited Bach's words in the name of Rekanati in his writing and in his wording. Also below siman 234 I wrote one may pray eighteen blessings at Minchah and then Ashrei; and it is possible that even when completing Shacharis after Minchah prayer it is considered Minchah prayer — examine well, it is strained. I looked again and found in Rekanati on parashas Vayera his wording: indeed in Maariv prayer one should not say it because it would appear as separation, etc. — up to here his wording. It is clear explicitly that he speaks only of Maariv. It also seems to me one may interpret his words that he also holds like Semag and Shulchan Aruch that when praying Maariv twice one interrupts with Ashrei, because Ashrei belongs to the Minchah prayer one is completing; and what Rekanati wrote that one should not say it means in Maariv prayer itself, and he comes to give a reason why we say it in Shacharis and Minchah and not in Maariv. Also the sod that there should not appear separation may be intended in this way — understand; see below beginning of siman 132. Nevertheless one should be concerned and careful not to say it when praying Maariv twice; and furthermore I wrote above that Mahar'i so ruled in his writings siman 59. I also saw in Rivash's responsum there that he wrote some would object to saying Ashrei; Rivash himself did not rule for practice to say it, but wrote one should not protest those who say it. And in Tashbetz simanim 223 and 224 it seems to me they do not say it. Therefore it is appropriate to rule not to say it when praying Maariv twice; nevertheless one should wait the measure of walking four amos as above siman 105.**** END BLOCK ****
