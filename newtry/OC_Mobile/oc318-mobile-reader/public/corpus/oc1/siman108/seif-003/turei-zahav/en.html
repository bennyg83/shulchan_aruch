He should say Ashrei. The reason is in order to stand in every prayer out of words of Torah; and what Rama wrote, and likewise when praying Maariv, etc.—this too is from Semak; and one must give a reason why he skipped in between; and likewise if praying Minchah twice he should say Ashrei between one prayer and the next, for certainly there too Ashrei is required, for it is one reason. It appears to me to resolve: both are needed—for if he only cited "one who prays Shacharis twice," one would say explicitly Ashrei is required in between, since in any case he must say Ashrei before "lamenatzei'ach" at Shacharis; therefore he should say that Ashrei before the second prayer, which is for tashlumin; but in one who prays Maariv twice, where there is no Ashrei in any case, one would not conclude Ashrei in between—this teaches not; and automatically we know that also in one who prays Minchah twice Ashrei is required. And if he cited "one forgot Shacharis and prays Minchah twice, he should say Ashrei"—one would say specifically in this, since Ashrei must be said at Shacharis after the prayer before "lamenatzei'ach" and he did not say it, for he did not pray at all—therefore he says Ashrei between prayer and prayer at Minchah; but in one who forgot Minchah and prays Maariv twice one would not conclude Ashrei in between—this teaches; and if he only said at Minchah and Maariv separately, one would say at Shacharis too it is not needed, only that Ashrei between prayer and prayer and no more, since from Maariv we derive it—this teaches.
