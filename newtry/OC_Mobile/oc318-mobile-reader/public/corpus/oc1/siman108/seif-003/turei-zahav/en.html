He should recite Ashrei. The reason is that one should begin each prayer from words of Torah. The Rama's statement concerning one who prays Maariv twice is also from the Semak. Why did he omit the intermediate case of one who prays Minchah twice and should recite Ashrei between the prayers? Certainly the same reason applies. Both stated cases were needed. Had he mentioned only one who prays Shacharis twice, we might think Ashrei is required between the prayers only because he must in any event recite Ashrei before Lamnatzeiach at Shacharis, and therefore places it before the second prayer. We would not know that Ashrei is required at Maariv, where it is otherwise not recited. Conversely, had he mentioned only one who missed Shacharis and prays Minchah twice, we might think Ashrei is required because he missed the Ashrei ordinarily recited after Shacharis and before Lamnatzeiach, but not when he missed Minchah and compensates at Maariv. Had he mentioned only Minchah and Maariv, we might think that at Shacharis the single Ashrei between the two prayers suffices and no additional Ashrei is required. Therefore both examples were stated, and the Minchah case follows automatically.
