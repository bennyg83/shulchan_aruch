This—that one makes up a prayer he missed—is specifically in the time of prayer. For example, if he makes up Shacharis immediately after chatzos, before the time of Minchah arrives, this is not tashlumin, for now there is no time of prayer at all, neither of Shacharis nor of Minchah. And likewise if he erred and did not pray Minchah, he does not make it up except at the time of Maariv, not all night after the time of Maariv has ended.
