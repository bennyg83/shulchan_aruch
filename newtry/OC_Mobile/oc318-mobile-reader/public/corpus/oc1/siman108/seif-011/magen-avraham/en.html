As a voluntary prayer — because some say he need not pray again, for what does he gain by this, since he already prayed the eighteen [blessings]; and some say the first prayer is as if it did not exist, since he prayed on Shabbat the eighteen [blessings] without [the proper mention] — therefore he prays as voluntary.

The same applies if not [as above]. And it seems to me that if at night too it is Rosh Chodesh, according to all he prays, for he gains by saying Yaaleh VeYavo; and the same on the night of the second day of Yom Tov; and the same if he forgot v'ten tal umatar or mashiv haruach.
