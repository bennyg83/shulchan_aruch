Magen Avraham, seif katan 10. The correct reference is siman 263, seif 15. There it is explained that when the congregation has prayed Maariv of Shabbos, an individual may no longer pray Minchah in that synagogue. All the more so, once he himself has prayed Maariv of Shabbos, he may not afterward pray Minchah. On a weekday, however, since the Minchah and Maariv Amidahs are identical, there is no concern.
