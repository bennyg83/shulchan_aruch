(11) He wrote — Rama in Alfasi Zuta beginning of chapter Tefillas HaShachar: R' Yochanan said if one erred and did not pray Maariv he prays Shacharis twice; and it is reasonable to us only until four hours and no more. If he erred Shacharis he prays Minchah twice; here too until half of Minchah and no more — up to here; Machber brought note 2 and wrote on it that the implication of the poskim's words does not sound thus, and the general sugya is not so — up to here his words. Beis Din, Shulchan Aruch page 169b, and Zechor le'Avraham part 1 note 9, Ruach Chayyim note 2 explained their words: the book Alfasi — if he did not pray Maariv he cannot complete it after Shacharis prayer unless he prays Shacharis prayer at its fixed initial time, i.e., until four hours as written in siman 89 seif 1; but if he prays Shacharis after four hours he cannot complete Maariv after it; and likewise if he did not pray Shacharis he cannot complete it after Minchah unless he prays Minchah before half of Minchah, as Zechor le'Avraham wrote there. On this Machber wrote the implication of the poskim's words does not sound thus, etc., but whenever he can pray he can also complete before it the prayer that preceded it, even praying Shacharis after four hours and Minchah after half of Minchah.**** END BLOCK ****
