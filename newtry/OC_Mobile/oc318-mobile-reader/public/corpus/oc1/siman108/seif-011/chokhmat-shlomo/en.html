Seif 11. If he erred at Minchah, etc. Note: Some authorities maintain that he need not repeat at Motzaei Shabbos, because what does he gain by praying again without mentioning Shabbos? In my novellae to Pesachim 95b I brought a clear proof. The Sages explain that impurity was merely deferred for the first Pesach, so why should one who missed it not offer the second Pesach in impurity? At the first Pesach only a minority were impure and most who offered were pure, whereas at the second Pesach the majority of those offering may be impure, and communal impurity may be permitted or merely deferred. This shows that we evaluate the circumstances at the time of the original obligation, not the circumstances at the time of compensation, in accordance with the view that the second Pesach is compensation for the first. See there carefully.
