Of Shabbos, etc. For the sanctity of Shabbos has already taken effect upon him. The Levush wrote that the reason is that it is a denigration of Shabbos, but this is difficult from what I brought above, that even on Shabbos it would always have been proper to pray Shemoneh Esrei; the Taz also wondered about this. It seems to my humble opinion that this is what he means: since in any event one must mention Shabbos, as I explained, therefore one should not pray Shemoneh Esrei because of the burden. And if he prayed Shemoneh Esrei and did not mention Shabbos, that is a denigration of Shabbos, for he adds what is not normally said and does not mention Shabbos; analyze this.
