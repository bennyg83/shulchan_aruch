(11) Of Shabbos, etc. — For Shabbos holiness has already taken effect upon him. Levush wrote the reason is it is disrespect to Shabbos; it is difficult from what I brought above that even on Shabbos it is always fitting to pray the eighteen-blessing prayer, and Taz too was astonished. It seems in my humble opinion he says thus: since in any case one must mention Shabbos as I explained, if so one should not pray eighteen blessings because of bother; and if he prayed eighteen blessings and did not mention Shabbos it is disrespect to Shabbos — he adds what one is not accustomed to say, and of Shabbos he does not mention — examine well.**** END BLOCK ****
