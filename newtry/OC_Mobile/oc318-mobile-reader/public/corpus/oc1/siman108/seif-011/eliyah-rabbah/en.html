Two of Rosh Chodesh, etc. The Levush disagrees and says that in the second prayer one should not say Ya'aleh VeYavo, for there is no denigration of Rosh Chodesh. So ruled the Olas Tamid, who wondered about the Rama: how is this different from Motzaei Shabbos, where one makes Havdalah in the first prayer and not in the second? Therefore, he concluded, the primary ruling is like the Levush. He overlooked the words of the Darchei Moshe in siman 422, who asked this very question and answered that Havdalah is different, for after he has made Havdalah in the first prayer, he is standing in the weekday prayer, and therefore he should not make Havdalah in the second; but here, regarding the second prayer on Rosh Chodesh, he is standing on Rosh Chodesh. He also overlooked the words of the Levush himself in the following seif, where he distinguishes this way. Likewise, in Lechem Chamudos, page 30, he agreed with the Rama and wrote that such is the custom. So too ruled the Taz and Magen Avraham. However, Shiyarei Knesses HaGedolah and the Perishah in siman 422 agreed with the Levush.
