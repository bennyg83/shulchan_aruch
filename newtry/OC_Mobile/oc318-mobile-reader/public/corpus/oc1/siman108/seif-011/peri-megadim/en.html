Prays — Taz. Tosafot Berachot 26b s.v. ta'ah — a doubt of dispute is like a doubt of action, and its law is as in siman 107 — see there. In Shulchan Aruch seif 10 he ruled one returns and prays even though one gains nothing; and here in seif 11 it is doubtful. In Tosafot there, Rabbi Moshe of Altona deduced that both are equal — we wrote in our chiddushim: one can say Chazal instituted that one precede specifically the obligation of its hour first; and although if unintentional one returns, unlike when one gains nothing — one can say they did not institute completion. And this ruling applies to tal umatar or mashiv haruach as well.
