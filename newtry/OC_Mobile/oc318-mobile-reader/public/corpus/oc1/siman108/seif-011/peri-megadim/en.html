He prays. See the Taz and Tosafot, Berachos 26b, s.v. Ta'ah. A doubtful dispute is like a doubtful fact, and its law is as in siman 107; see there. In Shulchan Aruch, seif 10, he ruled that one goes back and prays even though he gains nothing, while here in seif 11 it is a doubt. In Tosafot there, Rabbi Moshe of Alavri inferred that both are equal. We wrote in our novellae that one can say Chazal instituted that the obligation of the present time must specifically be put first, and even if he erred, he repeats; but where he gains nothing, one may say they did not institute makeup. This ruling is relevant also to Tal U'Matar or Mashiv HaRuach.
