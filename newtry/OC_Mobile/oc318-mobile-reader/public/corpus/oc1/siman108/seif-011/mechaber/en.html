If one erred during the afternoon prayer of Shabbat and prayed the Eighteen [i.e. the weekday Amidah] and did not mention Shabbat,[immediately after the end of] Shabbat one prays [the Amidah] twice, and does not separate [Shabbat from weekday - i.e. insert "ata chonantanu"] in the second; and it should be prayed as a voluntary prayer and there is no need to innovate any [new] thing [into it]. The same applies if one did not mention "Ya-aleh V'yavo" during' the afternoon prayer of Rosh Chodesh.
