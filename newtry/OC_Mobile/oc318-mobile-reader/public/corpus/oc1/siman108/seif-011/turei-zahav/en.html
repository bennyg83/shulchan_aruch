He prays on Motza'ei Shabbos twice. There is dispute on this: Tosafos in the name of R' Yehuda hold he need not return for this, for he already prayed the eighteen, only he forgot it was Shabbos; and when he completes the prayer of eighteen and does not mention Shabbos, what does he gain by this tashlumin? And the scholars of Provence hold that what he prayed the eighteen without Shabbos is as if he did not pray at all, since he did not act properly; therefore the poskim ruled he should return to pray as nedavah and need not innovate, since there is rationale that he must return and pray from the side of law.
