He prays twice at Motzaei Shabbos. Tosafos cite Rabbeinu Yitzchak that he need not repeat, because he already prayed the eighteen weekday blessings, and repeating them without mention of Shabbos gains nothing. The scholars of Provence maintain that praying the weekday Amidah without mention of Shabbos is as though he did not pray at all, because he did not pray according to law. The authorities therefore rule that he should repeat as a voluntary prayer, and no new element is required because there is a substantial view that repetition is required by law.
