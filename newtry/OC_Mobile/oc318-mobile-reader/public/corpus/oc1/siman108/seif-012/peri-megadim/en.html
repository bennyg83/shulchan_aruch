An event. See the Taz. There is a textual error: 'brings' should read 'recognizes,' and 94 should read 104, seif 6. See siman 294, Taz, letter 3. The Pri Chadash wrote that in a makeup prayer it is not an interruption, implying that in the principal prayer it is an interruption. In the Levush it implies that even in the principal prayer it is not an interruption. If he erred inadvertently, he has the law of one who spoke, and he returns to the beginning of the blessing according to the view that it is an interruption; and according to the view that one who spoke intentionally returns to the beginning, the same applies here. As practical halacha this requires study.
