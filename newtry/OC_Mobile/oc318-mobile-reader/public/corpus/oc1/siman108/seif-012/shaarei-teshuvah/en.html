Ba'er Heitev; and see below siman 126 in the name of Rama MiPano siman 25, and Knesset HaGedolah agreed with him and Peri Chadash disagrees; and in Shiyurei Berachah he wrote that he saw in a certain great letter that brought Rashba in a responsum in a manuscript: one who did not mention Yaaleh VeYavo at Shacharis of Rosh Chodesh and was not reminded until he prayed Musaf and Minchah arrived—he should pray Minchah twice; and if he remembered at the time of Musaf he does not pray the tashlumin except at Minchah; and it is explained according to Peri Chadash; and if Rama MiPano had heard Rashba's rationale he would have withdrawn his view—see there. And regarding what Magen Avraham wrote that forgetfulness is compulsion, etc.—see in Vavei HaAmudim page 17 and in Sha'arei Ephraim siman 28 and in Shevut Yaakov part 2 siman 148; and in Be'er HaGolah he brought the responsum of Nechpeh BaKesef part Choshen Mishpat siman 23, and see there that they brought proof from Bava Kamma page 26; and in Be'er HaGolah he wondered that they did not mention the responsum of Maharam Mintz siman 25, who ruled that forgetfulness is unwitting on account of this proof—see there.
