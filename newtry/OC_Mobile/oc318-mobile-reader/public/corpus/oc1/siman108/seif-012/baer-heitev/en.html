Weekdays. This Beit Yosef learned from what we say that if he made Havdalah in both he has fulfilled—see there. And Taz challenged him, for there certainly the time of Havdalah is at that time, only that this man made Havdalah a second time without need; unlike something that has no relevance at that time, such as one who says on weekdays what belongs to Pesach—you have no greater interruption than this—and the ruling of this is uncertain, end quote. And likewise in responsum of Leket Yosher part 1 siman 200 he disagrees on this and rules they return him—see there. And it is possible to say it speaks of compensatory prayer, such as one who did not pray Minchah on Rosh Chodesh or on Shabbos or on other festivals who prays Maariv twice—in this matter it speaks: if he mentioned in the compensatory prayer Rosh Chodesh or Shabbos or the matter of other festivals, since this prayer comes to compensate for the day that passed, if he mentioned it is not an interruption.
