One who mistakenly mentions an occasion, etc., has not made an interruption. See the Mishnah Berurah, where we cited the Acharonim. Nevertheless, concerning a compensatory prayer, such as one who did not pray Minchah on Rosh Chodesh, Shabbos, or another festival and therefore prays Maariv twice, if he mentions Rosh Chodesh, Shabbos, or the other festival in the compensatory prayer, many Acharonim agree that this is not an interruption, because this prayer comes to compensate for the preceding day.
