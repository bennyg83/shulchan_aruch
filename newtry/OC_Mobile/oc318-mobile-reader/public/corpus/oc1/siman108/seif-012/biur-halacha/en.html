One who erred and mentions an event, etc., is not an interruption — see in Mishna Berurah what we wrote in the name of the achronim; nevertheless regarding a make-up prayer, such as one who did not pray Minchah on Rosh Chodesh or Shabbat or other festivals, and prays Ma'ariv twice and mentioned in the make-up prayer Rosh Chodesh or Shabbat or an event of other festivals — since this prayer comes to make up the previous day, many achronim agreed it is not an interruption:
