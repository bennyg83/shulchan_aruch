(12) Of Rosh Chodesh, two, etc. — Levush disagrees that in the second he should not say Yaaleh ve-Yavo, for there is no disrespect to Rosh Chodesh; so too ruled Olas Tamid, and he was astonished at Rama — what is different from Motzei Shabbos where he makes Havdalah in the first and not in the second; therefore the main ruling seems like Levush — up to here his words. It escaped me the words of Darkei Moshe siman 422 who raised this himself and answered that Havdalah is different, for after he made Havdalah in the first in weekday prayer he stands [in weekday status], therefore he has no Havdalah in the second; but here regarding the second prayer on Rosh Chodesh he stands [in Rosh Chodesh status]. So too it escaped me to him Levush's own words in the seif after this where he disagrees on this; so too in Lechem Chamudos page 30 he agreed to Rama's words and that the custom is thus, and so ruled Taz and Magen Avraham; but She'eirei Knesset HaGedolah and Perishah siman 422 agreed to Levush's words.**** END BLOCK ****
