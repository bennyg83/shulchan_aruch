12) Mussaf has no compensation; one does not compensate it, and no other prayer compensates for it. The same applies to Ne'ilah. Neither prayer interrupts the sequence for compensation: the missed prayer may still be compensated from Shacharis to Minchah or from Minchah to Maariv.
