To a loss, etc. This follows from what he asked at the beginning of siman 99; see there. The Olas Tamid wrote that it appears from Terumas HaDeshen, siman 5, that only where the damage is definite is it called compulsion, but if the damage is not definite it may be included in intentional neglect; this requires study. In my humble opinion, since the responsum there brought proof from one who loses a lost object in Perek Eilu Megalchin, it implies that even where the damage is not definite, it is comparable to compulsion.
