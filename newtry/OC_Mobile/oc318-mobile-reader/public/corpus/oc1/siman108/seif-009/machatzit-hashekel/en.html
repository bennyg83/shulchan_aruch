(Seif katan 9) It has no. For how can he read the korbanos when the time of Mussaf has already passed? Although in the makeup prayer he does not mention the korbanos, see what the Taz wrote on this in seif katan 4. But the other prayers are requests for mercy; although the prayers were instituted corresponding to the daily offerings, nevertheless no korban is mentioned in the prayer, only mercy.
