And if he did not mention Yaaleh V'Yavo in the first and mentioned it in the second — see in Mishna Berurah the reason; and see in Derech HaChaim who wrote that the same applies to one obligated to pray twice on the first day they begin saying tal u'matar [namely that he forgot to pray Minchah on the day before and must pray in the evening twice and mention tal u'matar in both], and he did not mention it in the first and mentioned it in the second — all its law is as written here; additionally he wrote there that if he did not mention it in the second he returns and prays as a voluntary prayer and conditions: if I am obligated to pray, let it be for my obligation, and if not, let it be for my voluntary prayer; and similarly in Shaarei Teshuvah in the name of Maggid Mishneh; and in Mishna Berurah I copied to exempt because I saw many achronim who hold like Pri Chadash, and one may do according to either authority:

But if he did not mention in both of them, etc. — the reason is that we do not sanctify the month at night, as below in siman 422. Also in the gloss in Kol Bo — except what he wrote of Rosh Chodesh twice — meaning, all that he wrote in this gloss is written in Kol Bo except what he wrote in the gloss that one prays two of Rosh Chodesh — this is not like Kol Bo, for Kol Bo holds that l'chatchila one does not mention Rosh Chodesh in the second prayer, and these four words in the first and not in the second; or what is written in Kol Bo is a scribal error; and similarly it is stated there in Darkei Moshe below in siman 422 in his citation there of the words of Kol Bo; and similarly it is proven there from the words of Darkei Moshe from what he disagrees with him there; and all this is not like Taz in this siman.
