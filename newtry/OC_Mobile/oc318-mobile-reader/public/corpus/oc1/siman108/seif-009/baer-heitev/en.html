Shabbos. Magen Avraham was unsure if he did not mention Shabbos in the second whether they return him—see there. And Knesset HaGedolah brought from there the Maharam of Padua that he did not fulfill his obligation and must return and pray, and likewise Taz—see there. But Eliyah Rabbah ruled he fulfills ex post facto, and likewise Knesset HaGedolah there and Peri Chadash.

Twice. Magen Avraham wrote: one who did not pray Maariv on Rosh Chodesh and prayed Shacharis twice, and did not say Yaaleh VeYavo in the second—they return him—see there. And see Maharsha and investigate. And Knesset HaGedolah in siman 422 wrote he is not obligated to return—see there. And likewise Peri Chadash.

In the second. He revealed his intent that the first was for tashlumin, as with Havdalah in the seif after next; and this is specifically when the tashlumin comes on account of a prayer that is not of Rosh Chodesh, then we can say as below regarding Havdalah—for since he did not mention Yaaleh VeYavo in the first, he revealed his intent that he prayed the first with intent of tashlumin. But if Rosh Chodesh was two days and he did not pray Minchah on the first day of Rosh Chodesh, when he prays on the second night of Rosh Chodesh two prayers, one obligatory and one for his tashlumin—if he did not mention in the first and mentioned in the second, it is obvious he need not return and pray, for here we cannot say he revealed his intent, and certainly he erred.
