Of Rosh Chodesh, twice. In siman 422 Beit Yosef copied from Kol Bo, and these are his words: One who did not pray Minchah on the eve of Rosh Chodesh prays Maariv twice and mentions Yaaleh VeYavo in the first and not in the second; and if he mentioned in the first and not in the second, or in the second and not in the first—the second counted for him, the first did not count for him; and if he mentioned in both he fulfilled; and if he did not mention in both he fulfilled—end quote. He compared it to the case of one who did not pray Minchah on Shabbos in siman 292, and it is correct; and likewise I found in the name of Chay Gaon—end quote Beit Yosef there. And it appears to the eye all the errors in this copy; and therefore the correction appears this way: and one mentions Yaaleh VeYavo in the first and second; and if he mentioned in the second and not in the first, the second counted for him, the first did not; and if he did not mention in both, or he mentioned in the first and not in the second, he fulfilled. And on the ruling of one who mentioned in the second and not in the first, the second counted for him, etc.—Beit Yosef wrote that he compared it to the ruling of one who did not pray Minchah on Shabbos and made Havdalah in the second and not in the first, where he revealed his intent that the first was for tashlumin and he did not fulfill; likewise here—even though if he did not mention Yaaleh VeYavo at Maariv of Rosh Chodesh he need not return, this is worse in that he advanced the tashlumin prayer before the prayer of the obligation of the hour; but what we wrote that he must mention Rosh Chodesh in both—in this it is not similar to what was stated, for certainly regarding Havdalah, which are not supplications but a mitzvah for its time, one cannot make Havdalah twice, this after that—for if he did once weekday, why do twice weekday? Unlike Yaaleh VeYavo, which is supplication; and we already hold "would that a person pray all day"—therefore he is obligated to mention the sanctity of Rosh Chodesh also in the second, as on Shabbos. And thus was Rama's correction in Kol Bo, and as copied here; and Levush wrote here to disagree with Rama in what he wrote "one prays Maariv of Rosh Chodesh twice" and distinguishes between sanctity of Shabbos and sanctity of Rosh Chodesh, and relied on himself in what he wrote in siman 422—and all this is not equal to us, for he corrected otherwise in Kol Bo as written there; and if so, where is proof from his own words? And truth guides his way as written, that there is no distinction between sanctity of Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh in prayer—as explained, it is clear that Rama's words are comfortable in halachah.
