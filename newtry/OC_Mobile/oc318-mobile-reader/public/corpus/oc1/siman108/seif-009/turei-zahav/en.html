Of Rosh Chodesh twice. In siman 422 Beit Yosef copied from Kol Bo: one who did not pray Minchah on erev Rosh Chodesh prays Maariv twice and mentions Yaaleh ve-Yavo in the first and not in the second; and if he mentioned in the first and not in the second, or in the second and not in the first — the second counts for him, the first does not; and if he mentioned in both he fulfilled, and if he did not mention in both he fulfilled — end of citation. Beit Yosef compared it to the reason of one who did not pray Minchah on Shabbat in siman 292, and it is correct; and so I found in the name of Chai Gaon — see there Beit Yosef. And it appears to the eye all the errors in this copy; therefore the reading should be thus: and he mentions Yaaleh ve-Yavo in the first and in the second; and if he mentioned in the second and not in the first — the second counts for him, the first does not; and if he did not mention in both, or mentioned in the first and not in the second — he fulfilled. And the law of one who mentioned in the second and not in the first — the second counts for him, etc. — Beit Yosef wrote he compared it to the law of one who did not pray Minchah on Shabbat, and distinguished in the second and not in the first, for he revealed his intent that the first was for make-up prayers; and likewise here. However what we said that one must mention Rosh Chodesh in both — this is not like what is said regarding Havdalah, for Havdalah is not mercy but a mitzvah for its time — it is not applicable to distinguish twice; but if he did Havdalah once, why do it a second time as weekday? Not so regarding Yaaleh ve-Yavo, which is mercy; and we already rule, would that a person pray all day — therefore he must mention sanctity of Rosh Chodesh also in the second, as on Shabbat. So was Rama's reading in Kol Bo, as copied here; and Levush wrote here to disagree with Rama in what he wrote, praying Maariv of Rosh Chodesh twice, and divides between sanctity of Shabbat and sanctity of Rosh Chodesh, and relied on what is written in siman 422 — all this is of no value to us, for he corrected differently in Kol Bo as stated there; if so Beit Yosef has proof from his own words, and truth guides his way as stated — there is no distinction between sanctity of Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh in prayer, as above. It is clear Rama's words are correct in halacha.
