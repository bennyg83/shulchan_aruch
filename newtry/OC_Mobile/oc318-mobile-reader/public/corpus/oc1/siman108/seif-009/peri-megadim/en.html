Of Rosh Chodesh — Taz. Levush emended thus: he mentions in the first and not in the second; and if he mentioned in the second and not in the first, the second counts, and he compared it to what is written for all cases. Eliyah Rabbah 18 in the name of Shach 23 and Perishah 23 — in Levush not to mention in the second Rosh Chodesh; and it appears on Yom Tov it is like Shabbat, that one prays both of Yom Tov; and it seems for Rosh Chodesh that if one does not mention yaaleh veyavo it is not disrespect — see Levush. And what the Taz wrote to distinguish, etc. — in Darkei Moshe 23 it is explained the reading is like Levush, and he disagrees — not like havdalah, which suffices once, unlike here one is obligated to mention in all prayers, and not because of mercy — see there. And Magen Avraham letter 13 — there is another distinction between Taz and Levush — see there.
