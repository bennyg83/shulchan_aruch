(6) In a case where he did not pray Maariv and prayed Shacharis and gave his mind in the shaliach tzibur's repetition for make-up prayers — he fulfills eighteen blessings — Birkei Yosef note 1, and in his book Kaf HaChayyim there note 15, Beit Yaakov note 10, Shaarei Teshuvah note 2, Kaf HaChayyim siman 15 note 56. However Eliyah Rabbah note 1 wrote Kaf HaChayyim that the listener and speaker must intend; and here certainly the shaliach tzibur's mind in repetition is not to discharge him in make-up prayers, for still a person cannot intend with the shaliach tzibur in silence as Tur wrote in siman 59 in the name of Rosh — see there. But if the shaliach tzibur intended to discharge him for make-up prayer and he intends with him the entire prayer in silence, it is possible even the expert fulfills after the fact — see there. If so, the shaliach tzibur must intend in his repetition to discharge this one's make-up prayers and also to discharge one not expert, and for the aspect made through repetition as cited in the previous note; and this one also intends for make-up prayers and also to fulfill eighteen blessings for the aspect made through repetition; and all this if the shaliach tzibur prays word by word aloud, for if not he cannot intend for him, as written below siman 124 note 56 — see there.**** END BLOCK ****
