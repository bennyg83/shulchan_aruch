The Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: therefore, if he also forgot Shacharis, he completes it, since the time of the adjacent Minchah prayer has not passed. It seems to me that his meaning is that if he forgot to pray Shacharis and Mussaf, he completes it after Minchah. This implies that he prays Mussaf before Shacharis, since Mussaf is still in its time, whereas Shacharis is only a makeup prayer. See siman 286, seif katan 7. In the responsa of Shaarei Ephraim, siman 28, it is written: if one forgot and did not mention the sanctity of the day in Shacharis, if he has not yet prayed Mussaf, he must go back and pray; but if he already prayed Mussaf, he has already mentioned the sanctity of the day and need not go back and pray Shacharis.
