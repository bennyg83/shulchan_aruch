The Malbushei Yom Tov wrote: therefore, if he also forgot Shacharis, he completes it, since the time of the adjacent Minchah prayer has not passed. It seems to me that his meaning is that if he forgot to pray Shacharis and Mussaf, he completes it after Minchah. This implies that he prays Mussaf before Shacharis, since Mussaf is still in its time, whereas Shacharis is only a makeup prayer. See siman 286, seif katan 7. In the responsa of Shaarei Ephraim, siman 28, it is written: if one forgot and did not mention the sanctity of the day in Shacharis, if he has not yet prayed Mussaf, he must go back and pray; but if he already prayed Mussaf, he has already mentioned the sanctity of the day and need not go back and pray Shacharis. See below, siman 422.
It has no makeup, etc. For this reason the Nachalas Tzvi wrote that if one forgot Shacharis on Shabbos, he should not make it up with the Mussaf prayer, since Mussaf itself has no makeup; if so, it certainly cannot serve to make up another prayer. Rather, he waits and makes up Shacharis after Minchah. It seems to me that the same applies if he forgot and did not pray until after Mussaf, that he may still pray while the time of Shacharis remains, for Mussaf is not considered another prayer for this purpose. Even according to the poskim I cited above, that if he left the matter of prayer he may not make it up, nevertheless here, since he is still occupied with the Mussaf prayer, it helps for completion.
