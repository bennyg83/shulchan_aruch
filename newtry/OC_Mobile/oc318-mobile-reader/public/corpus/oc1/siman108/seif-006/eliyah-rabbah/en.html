(6) Musaf, etc. — For there is no sacrifice at night, but all day until dark the time of Musaf prayer still exists; Malbushai Yom Tov wrote therefore if he forgot Shacharis he also completes it since the time of the adjacent Minchah prayer has not passed — in my view, up to here his wording. In my view his explanation is that he forgot to pray Shacharis and Musaf — he completes them after Minchah; and it is implied one prays Musaf before Shacharis since Musaf's time still exists, which is not so for Shacharis which is make-up; see siman 286 note 7. He wrote in a responsum (?) Shulchan Aruch siman 28: if he forgot and did not mention sanctity of the day in Shacharis, if he did not yet pray Musaf he must go back and pray; if he already prayed Musaf behold he mentioned sanctity of the day and need not go back and pray Shacharis; see below siman 422.**** END BLOCK ****
