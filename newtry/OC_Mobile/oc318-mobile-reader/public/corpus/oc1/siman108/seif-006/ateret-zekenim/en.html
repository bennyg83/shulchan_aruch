He went through the whole day and did not pray Musaf, etc. Nevertheless, if he did not pray Shacharis on Shabbos, it has tashlumin at Minchah, even though tashlumin is effective only in the prayer adjacent to it, as above in siman 286 seif 1. And all the more so he can pray Musaf even though he already prayed Minchah, as below in siman 286 seif 4.
