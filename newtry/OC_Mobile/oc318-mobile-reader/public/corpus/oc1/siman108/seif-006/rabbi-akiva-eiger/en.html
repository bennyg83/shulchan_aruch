(Magen Avraham siman kuf 16) That he say yaaleh veyavo. In Binyan Ariel he objected: since two-day Rosh Chodesh is only a doubt, therefore presumably if the second day of Rosh Chodesh he did not pray properly; and if the first day of Rosh Chodesh it is not proper to mention now yaaleh veyavo. In Tzelach Berachos perek 4 he wrote that on two-day Rosh Chodesh, how could witnesses come from Mincha and above — it is like a long day. However on two days of Yom Tov of the last diaspora — if on the first day he prayed weekday Mincha he does not pray Maariv twice — see there. In Tur Even HaEzer 483 Rosh Hashana he also wrote simply that on every Rosh Chodesh there was the enactment that they do not accept witnesses from Mincha and above. However in 484 Rosh Hashana he wrote the opposite — see there; and see in responsa Shey Limora siman 53. And see what Beit Yosef wrote siman 131 in the name of Shibolei HaLeket — it implies explicitly that also on Rosh Chodesh of the whole year the law is so, that when witnesses came from Mincha and above they practiced today holy and tomorrow holy.
