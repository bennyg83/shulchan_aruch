And if the reverse he did not fulfill. He learned this from one who forgot and did not pray Minchah of Shabbos, who prays Maariv twice, as written siman 287 regarding Havdalah: if he did not make Havdalah in the first but in the second he must return and pray a third time, since he revealed his intent that he advanced tashlumin first—the same applies here. And this is not necessary, for one could say that there he made an actual change in practice in that he did not make Havdalah in the first and showed the change that in the second he made Havdalah, and as Rif wrote there that he revealed his intent, etc.—unlike here, where he made no change except in intent of the heart did not intend properly.
