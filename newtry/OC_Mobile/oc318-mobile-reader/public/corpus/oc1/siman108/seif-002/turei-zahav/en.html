If he reversed the order, he did not fulfill his obligation. This was learned from one who forgot Minchah on Shabbos and prays twice at Motzaei Shabbos. If he omitted Havdalah in the first prayer but recited it in the second, he must pray a third time, because he revealed that he treated the first as compensation. The same was applied here. This is not conclusive. There, he made an objective change by omitting Havdalah in the first prayer and reciting it in the second, as the Rif writes, thereby revealing his intent. Here he made no objective change, and the issue is only incorrect intent in his heart.
