The first is counted and the second for make-up prayers, etc. — The reason the Levush wrote is because the Gemara [says]: frequent and infrequent — frequent comes first, and we derive from verses; however the Magen Avraham in the chapter on Shacharis prayer note 10 questioned this reason and wrote another reason, because there is concern that through this the enactment will be nullified and one will come to pray it even not at the time of its second prayer; but since even if he prayed first for the obligatory one he must return and pray that of make-up prayers, this negligence of praying it after its time and before the time of the second prayer will not come — up to here. Eliyah Rabbah note 1 also questioned this reason and wrote the reason is because make-up prayer is accepted only along with the future prayer; but when one prays make-up prayers before the future prayer it is not accepted since its time passed — up to here. In my humble opinion, with Heaven's help, to give a reason per the sod, following what the Rav wrote in Shaar HaKavanos in the discourse on changing the prayers page 59b: in Shacharis prayer the drawing down of the minds is in the inner vessels, in Minchah in the middle vessels, and in Maariv in the outer vessels; therefore if one prays make-up prayer before this it will not be aligned, since now it is in another aspect; but if he prays the prayer of that aspect that exists now, then the minds of this aspect will be drawn down; and all the more so he must draw down the minds of the aspect that preceded this, and then through this he will have an opening to pray also the prayer that passed, so the drawing down of the minds will be complete.**** END BLOCK ****
