If one erred and did not pray the afternoon prayer, one prays the evening prayer twice: the first for the evening prayer, and the second as teshuvah (make-up). If one erred and did not pray the evening prayer, one prays the morning prayer twice: the first for the morning prayer, and the second as teshuvah. After one says Yotzer and the Eighteen Blessings, one says Ashrei and then prays the Eighteen Blessings for the teshuvah of the evening prayer.[And similarly, when one prays the evening prayer twice because one did not pray the afternoon prayer, one says Ashrei between one prayer and the other prayer [Sefer Mitzvot Katan and Rivash siman 140].]:
