(Magen Avraham note 11) That one supposes forgetting — Mishnah Berurah: from what is concealed in Shulchan Aruch, and he wrote "either way the hour passed for him" — implies whether it was forgotten from him or compulsion. Similarly in Rama in Yoreh Deah siman 232 seif 13 regarding idolatry he wrote that supposing [forgetting] is called compulsion; but it seems more that it refers to the other dispute. And similarly one who was preoccupied with monetary need, etc., and thereby missed praying — implies that through one's preoccupation it was forgotten from him.
