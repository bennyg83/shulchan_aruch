(Magen Avraham, seif katan 11) Who thought that forgetfulness. Note: Since the Shulchan Aruch states without qualification, ‘Whether in this case or that case, the time has passed for him,’ it implies both where it was forgotten and where he was prevented by circumstances beyond his control. A similar formulation appears in the Rama, Yoreh Deah 232:13. Regarding this, he wrote that one who thought that forgetfulness is deemed a circumstance beyond one’s control. It appears more correct to me, however, that it refers to the other case: ‘Likewise, one who was occupied with the needs of his property,’ etc., and because of this he lost the opportunity to pray, meaning that due to his preoccupation he forgot.
