After he says the eighteen blessings he says Ashrei. And likewise when he prays Minchah twice because he did not pray Shacharis, he must also say Ashrei between one prayer and the next, even though he said Ashrei before the first prayer. And some say he should be very careful not to say Ashrei after he prayed Minchah (Maharam in responsa). Therefore he waits only the time it takes to walk four amos between prayer and prayer when praying Minchah twice. And tashlumin applies only to prayer that is a request for mercy; but for Kerias Shema and its blessings tashlumin does not apply—for if he does not recite Kerias Shema of Shacharis or of Maariv in their time, it has no tashlumin in the prayer adjacent to it, since once its time has passed its offering is voided and "that which is crooked cannot be made straight" (Maharam). There are those who wrote the blessings have no tashlumin (Beit Yosef, Kol Bo).
