Seif 2, after he says, etc. This is so that he may enter prayer from words of Torah. See Tosafos 31a, s.v. VeRabbanan. In any event, since he must say Ashrei, he should say it now. This is the meaning of “and likewise,” etc.: although he need not say Ashrei now, he should nevertheless say it as compensation for the Ashrei that he missed at Minchah, just as he compensates for the prayer itself. Strictly speaking, however, only the time needed to walk four amos is required, as explained above in siman 105.
