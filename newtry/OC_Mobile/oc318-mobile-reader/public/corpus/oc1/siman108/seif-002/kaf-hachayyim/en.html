Maadanei Yom Tov, chapter Tefillas HaShachar, paragraph 400, questions this and gives another reason: otherwise the enactment may be undermined, and a person may come to pray the compensation even when it is not yet time for the second prayer. Since one who puts the compensation first must repeat it, he will not negligently pray it after its own time but before the time of the second prayer. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, challenges this reason as well and explains that the compensatory prayer is accepted only together with the coming prayer; if one prays the compensation before the coming prayer, it is not accepted because its time has passed. With Hashem's help, it appears to me that a mystical reason can be given from Shaar HaKavanos, discourse on the changing prayers, folio 59b. In Shacharis the mochin are drawn into the inner vessels, at Minchah into the middle vessels, and at Maariv into the outer vessels. If one first prays the compensatory prayer, it is inappropriate because the present spiritual aspect is different. If he first prays the prayer corresponding to the present aspect, the mochin of that aspect are drawn down; all the more should the mochin of the preceding aspect then be drawn. This opens the way for him also to pray the missed prayer, so the mochin will be drawn in completeness.
