Seif 10, of the weekday. Gemara there.
If he did not, etc. There. Although the discussion concludes with a difficulty, since it does not say that the ruling was refuted, it remains halachah. Rosh writes this in the name of Rav Hai Gaon. Rashbam likewise writes in the name of Rabbeinu Chananel in Bava Basra 52b. Although Rashbam disagrees with him, we do not rule accordingly. Rif and Rosh resolve the difficulty. See Magen Avraham, and as written in seif 1.
