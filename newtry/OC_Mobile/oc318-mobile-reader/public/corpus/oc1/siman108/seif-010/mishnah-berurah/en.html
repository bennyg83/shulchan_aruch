(28) And one does not make havdalah — because havdalah suffices once, unlike Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh which require mention in all prayers; therefore he ruled in the previous seif that one prays the Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh [Amidah] twice.

(29) It did not count for him — for he needed to precede his obligation of the hour first, and he revealed his intent that he prayed the first for make-up; therefore he must return and pray once for make-up without havdalah. But if in his thought the first was for obligation and the second for make-up, and what he reversed regarding havdalah was by mistake and inadvertence without any intent, he need not return.

(30) And if one made havdalah, etc. — all this is when unspecified; but if it was explicitly in one's mind that the first was for completion, it appears one does not fulfill [Rabbi Akiva Eiger].

(31) One did not make havdalah, etc. — for one does not return because of mentioning havdalah in prayer, since one can say it on the cup as written in siman 298. If afterward one tasted before making havdalah on the cup — the halachah is that one must return and pray — nevertheless one's make-up prayer counted, since when one prayed make-up one was properly exempt from the obligation of the hour, for the cup was before him; only through error one became obligated.
