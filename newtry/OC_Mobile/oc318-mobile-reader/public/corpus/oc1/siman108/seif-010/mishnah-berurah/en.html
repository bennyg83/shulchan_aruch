And he does not make Havdalah. Because Havdalah is sufficient one time, unlike Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh, which must be mentioned in every prayer; therefore he ruled in the previous seif that one prays two prayers of Shabbos and two prayers of Rosh Chodesh.
It did not count for him. For he should have put the obligation of the present time first, and he revealed his intent that he prayed the first as makeup. Therefore he must go back and pray one prayer for makeup without Havdalah. However, if in his mind he intended the first for the obligation and the second for makeup, and the reversal of Havdalah was by mistake and inadvertence, with no intent at all, he need not go back.
And if he made Havdalah, etc. All this is where his intent was unspecified. But if his explicit intent was that the first prayer should be for makeup, it appears that he has not fulfilled, as the Eliyah Rabbah writes.
He did not make Havdalah, etc. One does not go back because of mentioning Havdalah in prayer, since he can say it over a cup, as written in siman 298. If afterward he tasted before making Havdalah over a cup, in which case we hold that he must go back and pray, nevertheless his makeup prayer counts for him, because at the time he prayed the makeup prayer he was properly exempt from the present obligation, since he had a cup before him; only through his later error did he become obligated.
