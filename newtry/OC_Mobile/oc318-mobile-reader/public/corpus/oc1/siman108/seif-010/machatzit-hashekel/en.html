(Seif katan 10) He acted intentionally, etc. For it is still the time of Mincha. This means that it speaks of places where they pray Maariv while it is still day. If so, even though he prayed Maariv, nevertheless it is still day. Therefore, although one who acted intentionally has no makeup prayer, that is specifically when its time has passed and he seeks to make it up with the adjacent prayer. This is unlike here, where its time has not yet passed.
As written at the end of siman 30, if one prayed Maariv while it was still day, he may no longer put on tefillin, for through his prayer he made it night, and night is not the time for tefillin. Likewise in siman 263, end of seif 6, a woman who lit the Shabbos lamp may not pray Mincha, for she has already made it Shabbos through her lighting. Tefillin and Shabbos are different, because since he made it night, if he puts on tefillin, or she prays Mincha, he or she is doing two things that contradict one another. This is unlike here, for even if you say that it is night, he is nevertheless permitted to pray Mincha as a makeup prayer; if so, these are not two matters that contradict one another. And although here, where he acted intentionally, he has no makeup prayer, and he must therefore be praying because it is still the time of Mincha, which is a contradiction, this is not so, for we require that the contradiction be recognizable. Here the contradiction is not recognizable, for who knows that he acted intentionally? Perhaps he acted unintentionally and is praying as a makeup prayer, even though in truth this is not so.
For he thought that it was permitted, etc. And one who says that it is permitted. He wrote above in siman 104, seif katan 6, in the name of the Taz, that this is called unintentional.
And it appears to him that on Rosh Chodesh, etc. As written at the beginning of siman 419, so it should read. Know that even according to what the Magen Avraham wrote, that since it is still day he is not praying as a makeup prayer but as a prayer in its proper time, nevertheless, in my humble opinion, he must join it to the Maariv prayer, so that it will appear as though it is for makeup. Otherwise it would be two matters that contradict one another, for then the contradiction is recognizable: he is not praying as a makeup prayer but as a prayer in its proper time, while he has already made it night.
