He does not make havdalah — because havdalah [suffices] once, unlike Shabbat and Rosh Chodesh where one must mention it in every prayer; as stated above, it is plain that this is not so.

It did not count for him — for he needed to precede the obligation of its hour first, and he revealed his intent that he prayed the first for make-up; therefore he must return and pray once for make-up without havdalah. And it seems to me specifically when he did so intentionally; but if he forgot to make havdalah in the first, he has fulfilled, for he did not reveal his intent, as was written in the previous siman.
