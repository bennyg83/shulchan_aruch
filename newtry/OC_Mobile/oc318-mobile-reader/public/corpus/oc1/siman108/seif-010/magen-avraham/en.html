And he does not recite Havdalah. This is because Havdalah need be recited only once, unlike the mention of Shabbos or Rosh Chodesh, which must be included in every prayer. This is obvious, unlike Olas Tamid.
It did not count for him. He was required to place the current obligation first, and he revealed that he had prayed the first as compensation. He must therefore pray one additional prayer as compensation, without Havdalah. It appears to me that this applies specifically when he acted deliberately. If he merely forgot to recite Havdalah in the first prayer, he fulfilled his obligation, since he did not reveal any contrary intention, as stated at the beginning of this siman.
