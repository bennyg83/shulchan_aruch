Nedavah. Ba'er Heitev: if he fulfilled on Shabbos, etc.; and see in Degel Machaneh Ephraim, who wrote that this means per the poskim that on Shabbos too one may pray nedavah; and sharpen in Beit Yosef siman 107; and accordingly Magen Avraham should not have copied this ruling; and it appears he holds thus, since there are opinions that one may complete several prayers and opinions that pray nedavah on Shabbos—therefore he relied on this to permit; but it requires study, since in any case he needed to consider it as nedavah to fulfill per the view that tashlumin applies only to the adjacent prayer; if so, on Shabbos it is forbidden to do so, since Shulchan Aruch adopts primarily the view that there is no nedavah prayer on Shabbos—and it requires study.
