Likewise, one who was occupied, etc., to avoid a loss. If the loss was not certain, the Olas Tamid writes that he is considered negligent, while the Eliyah Rabbah considers him inadvertent. It is therefore doubtful. It appears that he should pray conditionally: if I am obligated, let this be my obligatory prayer; otherwise, let it be a voluntary prayer. This is from the Peri Megadim.
Nevertheless, initially, etc. It is possible that one is not obligated to spend more than one fifth of his assets. This is from the Peri Megadim.
