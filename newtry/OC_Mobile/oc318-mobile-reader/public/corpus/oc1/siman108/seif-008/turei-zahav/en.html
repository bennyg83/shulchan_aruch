Two of Shabbos. The reason is that the sanctity of the Shabbos prayer has already come upon him; and likewise he must do so—all his prayers must be thus, for it is forbidden for him to pray on Shabbos without mentioning Shabbos; and nevertheless Rama ruled nearby likewise on Rosh Chodesh; and Levush wrote the reason that it would be contempt of Shabbos if he prays a weekday prayer; and nevertheless he distinguishes between Shabbos and Rosh Chodesh—and it is not correct, for Tosafos in chapter "Ma'alin" page 21 wrote that on Shabbos too it would have been law to pray all eighteen, only they did not burden him; and how could he write that the prayer of eighteen would be contempt of Shabbos? And it appears to me that even after the fact, if he prayed the second as weekday, he has not fulfilled tashlumin, for there is no prayer on Shabbos without mentioning Shabbos.
