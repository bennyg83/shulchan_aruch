Two Shabbos prayers. The reason is that the sanctity of the Shabbos prayer has already taken effect upon him, and he has acted accordingly. All his prayers must therefore mention Shabbos, for one may not pray on Shabbos without mentioning Shabbos. For the same reason the Rama rules similarly concerning Rosh Chodesh. Levush explains that reciting the weekday Amidah would demean Shabbos and therefore distinguishes Shabbos from Rosh Chodesh. This is incorrect. Tosafos in Berachos 21 write that even on Shabbos one should in principle recite all eighteen blessings, but the Sages did not burden him. How, then, can the weekday Amidah itself demean Shabbos? It appears that even after the fact, if his second prayer was the weekday Amidah without mention of Shabbos, he did not fulfill the compensatory obligation, because there is no valid Shabbos prayer without mentioning Shabbos.
