(Marginal note 8) And he should innovate, and according to what Rama wrote, etc. — it does not apply here; were I not afraid it would be [understood] that it is a printing error, and it belongs to the previous marginal note that he wrote: one who was sick or imprisoned, when he goes out he prays all the prayers — and that is through innovation; on this one should not ask what Rama wrote above in siman 106, that something must be renewed for him that he did not need before — for this does not apply here: how can something be renewed for him between each prayer and prayer, since he prays many prayers one after another? And according to what is printed, that it refers to what Shulchan Aruch wrote — it deals only with one praying one extra prayer as a nedavah; and although innovation does not apply here, his intent must be: this is not common, since he prays the nedavah prayer adjacent to obligatory prayer and make-up, and does not interrupt between them except the measure of four cubits or saying Ashrei — it is not common that something new happens to him in such a short time. If it already happened and something was renewed for him before he prayed the obligatory prayer but he did not mention that innovation in the obligatory prayer — he may not do so, for it is like causing blessings; for he may not pray nedavah except because something was renewed for him, and then through the innovation he may pray nedavah with all eighteen blessings. Since he could have mentioned the innovation in the obligatory prayer and did not need a nedavah prayer, he again may not pray nedavah because of that innovation; if so, necessarily something was renewed for him after he prayed the obligation — and this is not common as above.
