(Seif katan 8) And he should introduce a new element; and according to what the Rama wrote, etc. This does not apply here. Were I not afraid, I would say that it is a printer's error, and that it belongs to the end of seif katan 7, where he wrote that one who was ill or imprisoned, when he leaves, should pray all the prayers, meaning through introducing a new element. Regarding this, what he wrote fits well, that according to what the Rama wrote above in siman 106, he must have something new arise for him that he did not need beforehand. This does not apply here, for how is it possible that a new matter arise for him between each prayer and the next, since he is praying many prayers one after another? But according to the printed text, which refers to what is written in Shulchan Aruch, which discusses only one extra prayer that he prays as a voluntary prayer, even though a new matter does not apply here, one must say that his intent is that this is uncommon. Since he prays the voluntary prayer adjacent to the obligatory prayer and the makeup prayer, and he interrupts between them only by the time it takes to walk four cubits or by saying Ashrei, it is uncommon that a new matter will occur in such a short time. And if it already occurred to him, and a new matter arose before he prayed the obligatory prayer, but he did not mention that new element in the obligatory prayer, he is not permitted to do so, for this is like causing blessings. He may pray a voluntary prayer only when a new matter arose for him, and then, incidentally to the new matter, he is permitted to pray all eighteen blessings voluntarily. Since it was possible for him to mention the new matter in the obligatory prayer and he did not need a voluntary prayer, he may no longer pray voluntarily because of that new matter. Therefore, it must be that the new matter arose after he prayed the obligatory prayer, and this is uncommon, as explained.
