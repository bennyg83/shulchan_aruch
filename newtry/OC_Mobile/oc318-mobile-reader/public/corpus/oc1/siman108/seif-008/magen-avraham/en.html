Because he thought. Forgetfulness is considered compulsion. See Choshen Mishpat 421:10 and Mabit, volume 2, responsum 132. See also siman 527:7 and siman 538:1. In Yoreh Deah 232:12 a dispute is recorded concerning this. One must say that regarding an oath the ruling is stringent because of doubt. The Taz there, however, rules as stated here. This is also implied by the language of Rambam and Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat, siman 21. The proof of the Agudah, cited by Beit Yosef in Choshen Mishpat, siman 207, appears to me inconclusive. There, compulsion is of no effect, just as where one lent money against a field and stipulated that it be redeemed within three years, and an unavoidable circumstance prevented its redemption, the field nevertheless becomes absolutely his, as Beit Yosef writes in Choshen Mishpat there.
