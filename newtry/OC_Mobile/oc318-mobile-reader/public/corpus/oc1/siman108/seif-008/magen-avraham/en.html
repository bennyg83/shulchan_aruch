That forgetting is called coercion; and in Choshen Mishpat siman 381, seif 10; and in Mabit 102, siman 432; above in siman 527, seif 7; and siman 538, seif 1; see Even HaEzer siman 232, seif 2, where he wrote there is a dispute in this, and it must be said that regarding an oath one is stringent, but Taz there ruled as written here; and so in Choshen Mishpat siman 21, from the wording of Ramban and Rabbeinu Yerucham, and the proof from the Agudah — Rabbeinu Yerucham brought it in Choshen Mishpat siman 207. It seems to me it is not proof, for there coercion does not help at all, as one who lent on his field and stipulated to redeem within three years, coercion occurred and he did not redeem — that is absolute to him, as Beit Yosef wrote in Choshen Mishpat, seif 10.
