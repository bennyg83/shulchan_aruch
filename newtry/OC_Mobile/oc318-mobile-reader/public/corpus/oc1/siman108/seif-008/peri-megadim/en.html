Twice — Taz. And Magen Avraham letter 12 left this uncertain, and it implies somewhat his view that one fulfills; and so Peri Chadash simply — one fulfills if the teshuvah is weekday; and for the law, in doubt of dispute one can say one stipulates as in siman 107. And again I saw in Eliyah Rabbah 10 through innovation (Pri Megadim Mishneh Berurah in letter 3), and it is possible he did not consider it so much a doubt of dispute. And know: if one accepted Shabbat with a psalm or with Barchu and the day is still large, one did not pray Mincha — one prays Shabbat twice; Eliyah Rabbah letter 10 explains in siman 263:95. And still uncertain — one can say the first is Mincha and the second Maariv, since it is still day this is not teshuvah. If he did not mention Shabbat in the first, it is possible the Taz concedes he fulfills here; and if he prayed teshuvah of eighteen and mentioned Shabbat, simply he fulfills even in the Shabbat [prayer], as explained in siman 268:4. See Levush — what he wrote: when one must [pray] the first for Maariv, behold he accepted Shabbat, etc.; it implies if the first is teshuvah, one prays weekday; and one must say letter peh — it darkened and he had not yet accepted Shabbat at all — forced [explanation].
