Two. See the Taz. See the Magen Avraham, letter 12, who left this requiring study, and his view somewhat implies that one fulfills. So too the Pri Chadash ruled simply that one fulfills if the makeup prayer was a weekday prayer. As practical halacha, in a doubtful dispute one may say that he should stipulate as in siman 107. I later saw in Eliyah Rabbah, letter 10, that this should be done through an innovation; see what I wrote in letter 3. Perhaps he did not consider it so much of a doubtful dispute. Know that if one accepted Shabbos with Mizmor or Barchu while there was still much day left and he had not prayed Minchah, he prays two Shabbos prayers. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 10; this is explained in siman 263, seif 15. It still requires study, for one could say that the first is Minchah and the second Ma'ariv, since it is still day, and this is not makeup. If he did not mention Shabbos in the first prayer, perhaps the Taz agrees that he fulfilled here. If he prayed the makeup as Shemoneh Esrei and mentioned Shabbos, it is obvious that he fulfilled even in such a Shabbos prayer, as explained in siman 268, seif 4. See the Levush, who wrote that since he intended that the first must be Ma'ariv, he accepted Shabbos, etc. This implies that if the first were makeup he would pray a weekday prayer. One must say that his meaning is where it had grown dark and he had not accepted Shabbos at all, but this is forced.
