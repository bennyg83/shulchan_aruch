S.k. 2 — and so is the essential ruling; at first glance it appears he means the essential ruling is like Bach; and if so how does he bring from Rama's words proof to refute? And see below Magen Avraham s.k. 38 that Rama's words there are not like Bach; possible Taz proves that speech by way of hint is more lenient — for if we say it is a real hint considered like speech as Rama wrote, if so why permitted to say "one who appears healthy" etc. even though from this he understands etc.; since hint is like speech as Rama wrote — behold forbidden to say "it would have been right at dusk"; rather it must be that hint through speech is lighter; therefore he can say "one who appears healthy" etc. which is hint through speech; a real hint is forbidden since speech is forbidden; and like gentile where hint is forbidden like speech — therefore here too one can tell gentile "why did you not do" etc., for even on Shabbat itself to speak in hint language is permitted though hint itself is forbidden — this is his intent; and it requires study.
