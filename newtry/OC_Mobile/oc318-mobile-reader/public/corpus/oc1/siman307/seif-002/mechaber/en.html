Forbidden to hire workers and not to tell a gentile to hire workers for him on Shabbat, even if the Israelite needs that work only after Shabbat — for whatever is forbidden for an Israelite to do on Shabbat it is forbidden to tell a gentile to do; even to tell him before Shabbat to do it on Shabbat is forbidden; but permitted after Shabbat to say to him "why did you not do such-and-such on the past Shabbat," even though he understands from his words that he wants him to do it on the coming Shabbat.
