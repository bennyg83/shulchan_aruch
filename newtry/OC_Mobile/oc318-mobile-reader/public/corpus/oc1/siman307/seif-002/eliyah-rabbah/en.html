[5] Forbidden to hire, etc. — but in what is only a mere stringency permitted to tell non-Jews (Kenesset HaGedolah); requires study.
[6] And even to say, etc. — I wonder at the abbreviated Shelah printed in 533 that wrote: regarding improving candles or filling oil on Shabbos one must tell non-Jews before Shabbos — until here; and one must say he wrote thus on those who are lenient at the Shabbos meal; see siman 276.
[7] [Levush] To do this thing, etc. — meaning even melacha he does not need until after Shabbos (Malbushei Yom Tov); and so Rambam beginning ch. 5.
[8] But permitted, etc. — and Tur wrote "it appears somewhat to forbid"; and it is astonishing: why is this different from "all who extinguish do not lose" that we permit in 334 (Taz); and there is no difficulty, for there it is because of loss; and one may say there he does not speak to his face, but here it is like commanding this non-Jew; nevertheless for the law we do not leave Tur's "it appears" before Terumat HaDeshen, Semag, Smak, Hagahot Maimoniot who permit; and in responsum Maharam siman 559 some permit even before Shabbos to do on Shabbos; and though in 242 they rejected entirely — nevertheless here one may rely; and greater: responsum R' Yom Tov Tzahalon siman 244 permitted even informing him beforehand that telling a non-Jew is permitted and he understands — and permitted to tell him "you would not lose if I paid your wage."
