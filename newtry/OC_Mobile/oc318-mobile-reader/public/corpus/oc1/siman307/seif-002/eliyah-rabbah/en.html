[2] Or merchandise, etc. — Rambam's wording ch. 24 and Semag: one may not speak with his partner what he will sell tomorrow, buy, how he will build this house, with what merchandise he will go to such-and-such a place — all similar forbidden; and it appears to me he mentioned "partner" as the way of the matter, and the same for another, as Rama wrote seif 8; and in Kolbo he omitted partner — and it appears even if he did not say to another at all but only to himself, as implied by the conclusion of Shulchan Aruch and Levush and Maharash and Kolbo — only presumptively he says to another: why speak to himself? Magen Avraham s.k. 11 brings poskim that even "I will do" to one's fellow is permitted — and if so Tosafot's questions Shabbat 150a s.v. "what," etc., are difficult — see there and investigate.
