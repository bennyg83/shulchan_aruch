Even though he understands — Taz: from lighting one may say they permitted loss; nevertheless forbidden to say for tomorrow; for even though speech is forbidden from the verse or d'oraisa and "speak a matter," as in letter 1 — Torah permitted thought — Shabbat 103a (one may say "and speak a matter" is superfluous like 306); unlike telling a gentile, which is rabbinic shevut — stricter, for even thought is forbidden that he understand he will do on Shabbat — Rabbenu Bechor end of siman, Magen Avraham letter 31; and it will be explained there. And Rabbenu Bechor who was silent here may rely on what he wrote at the end of the siman — Tur's view; and perhaps he concedes here to Semag to say on a weekday a hint that he do on the coming Shabbat is more lenient than a hint on Shabbat itself. And even though one could distinguish — nevertheless for the law we do not leave "it appears" of Tur before Semag, Smak, Hagahot Maimoniot who permit; and in responsum of Maharam of Rothenburg siman 559 some permit even to say before Shabbat to do on Shabbat; and though in siman 242 they rejected entirely — nevertheless here one may rely; and greater: responsum of R' Yom Tov Tzahalon siman 244 permitted even if he informs him beforehand that telling a gentile is permitted and he understands from that — and also permitted to tell him "you would not lose if I gave you your wage."
