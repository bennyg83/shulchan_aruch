To a gentile. Forbidden to tell a gentile that he tell another gentile to do melacha for him — the Gaon R' Gershon z"l in responsum Chut Yair siman 49 and in siman 53 the view of Chut Yair is to be lenient and all the more so for shevut on erev Shabbat. Regarding a matter that is only a mere stringency it is permitted to tell a gentile. Radbaz. Magen Avraham:
