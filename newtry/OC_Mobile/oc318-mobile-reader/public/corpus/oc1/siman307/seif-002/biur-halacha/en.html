Forbidden to hire workers and not to say etc. — that is even if he does not fix the amount of wages and complete the transaction with them but the Israelite speaks with them about the matter of the work and that if they wish to earn for themselves — this too is forbidden [responsum Chut Yair siman 49 see there that he brought proof for this from siman 306 seif 6 that this too was not permitted except for matters of Heaven on erev Shabbat]:
