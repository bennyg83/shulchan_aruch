(10) Forbidden — for a person to walk in his affairs on Shabbat and even to speak of them, such as speaking with his partner what he will sell tomorrow or buy, or how he will build this house, and with what merchandise he will go to such-and-such a place — all this and similar is forbidden. Rambam ch. 24 halacha 1; and so in Semag. Beit Din, Acharonim vol. 2; and he wrote "partner" as the way of the matter, and the same for another, as Rama wrote seif 8; and in Kolbo he omitted "partner," and it appears even if he did not say to another at all but only to himself, as implied by the conclusion of Shulchan Aruch, Levush, Maharash, and Kolbo — only presumptively he says to another: why should he speak to himself?
