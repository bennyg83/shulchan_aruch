And likewise it is permitted, etc. The Olas Tamid wrote: this is surprising, for in siman 247 regarding a letter he ruled that it is permitted only where he fixed a wage, yet here he was lenient. He overlooked the end of siman 245 and the Shulchan Aruch, seif 5 there, that here too it is permitted only with a fixed wage. Even according to the Taz, who infers this here from the Semak, he overlooked the Shulchan Aruch there; and so wrote the Magen Avraham. However, this requires study regarding the Rama and Levush, who repeated it here. I saw that Malbushei Yom Tov wrote that the words of the Shulchan Aruch there escaped them. In my eyes it is astonishing to suspect the Rama of this, and moreover, why did he permit here without qualification? Therefore it appears to me that the Rama held that since the Semag and Mordechai permitted without qualification, this shows that it is permitted even without a fixed wage. It is not comparable to a letter, where it is recognizable in the hand of a Jew, as I explained at the beginning of that siman and in seif katan 3. The Semag may also be discussing where his garments are recognizable, while here the Rama is discussing siman 241; this requires study. But where he gives him money on erev Shabbos, it is obvious that it is permitted even without a fixed wage.
