(s.k. 10) To one's fellow, etc. — because with a gentile more: the gentile presumes he is hired as agent in the evening; if he has no need for him, the Israelite has no dealings with the gentile without cause.

For thought is permitted, as above 306 seif 8: they expounded "and speak a matter" — your Shabbat speech shall not be like weekday speech; Scripture forbade only speech, not thought — therefore permitted to tell an Israelite "as you see fit with me," etc. — only thought; the same telling a gentile — only thought.
