He invited guests — Beit Yosef wrote: what they practice that the attendant reads for the meal from writing is forbidden; though no erasing concern when the host reads, nevertheless decree lest he read, etc. — until here; and I say custom of Israel is Torah: accounts of mitzvah permitted, as 306 seif 6; we do not decree idle accounts because here it is itself a decree against account documents permitted for mitzvah; see there seif 12: communal needs permitted.

Sunken engraving — but protruding forbidden, lest he erase; even very high — do not distinguish, as 275.

On tablet and ledger, etc. — interchangeable with a document; but wall with document does not interchange, since engraved — Beit Yosef unlike Or Zarua.
