He invited guests. The Bach wrote that the practice for the attendant to read the meal list from writing is forbidden. Granted, there is no concern that he may erase unless the host himself reads it, but there is still a decree lest he read weekday documents. I say that the custom is proper, for they did not decree regarding the attendant from the outset, so that he not err and cause ruin and mishap, as in the incident of Kamtza and Bar Kamtza.
