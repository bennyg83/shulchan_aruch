The Bach also ruled that the practice is to be lenient, and the Responsa Masas Binyamin ruled likewise, permitting it for this reason even in a case of financial loss. It appears that the Responsa Rivash, siman 387, escaped his notice, for it states that even the authority who permits it for the sake of a mitzvah forbids it in a case of financial loss.
