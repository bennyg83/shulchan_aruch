There — and it appears from his words this is the matter in its own right: the Maggid Mishneh did not emend "great need" but "even slight need," meaning slight distress is permitted; but from Raavad it appears he emended "or great need," for he challenged Rambam: how does he permit via gentile — even he himself, etc., such as a pipe; and he did not answer that there it is great need; therefore the correct girsa is as Shulchan Aruch copied, that great need is required; and he already answered the Gemara on pipe that he will not come to repair by hand; see there; and afterward he brought that Rivash is stricter — therefore one may not permit except in great loss; this is the continuation of Magen Avraham's words, whose entire intent is to uphold the girsa in Shulchan Aruch that requires great need.
