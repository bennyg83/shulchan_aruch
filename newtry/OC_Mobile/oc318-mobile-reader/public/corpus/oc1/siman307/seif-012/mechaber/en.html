One who invited guests and prepared kinds of delicacies for them and wrote in writing how many he invited and how many delicacies he prepared for them — forbidden to read it on Shabbat, even if it is written high on a wall, because of a decree lest he read weekday documents — meaning bills of debt and accounts (and even to gaze at them without reading is forbidden) (Rosh and Ran, chapter HaShoel, and Tur); but if he carved a sunken engraving in the wall — permitted; but on a tablet and ledger, even if engraved — forbidden to read it.() אבל אם חקק בכותל חקיקה שוקעת מותר אבל בטבלא ופנקס אפי' אם הוא חקוק אסור לקרותו:
