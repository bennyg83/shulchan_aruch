One who invited guests and prepared various dishes for them and one had written down the amount of guests and the amount of food he had prepared, it is prohibited to read the list on Shabbos, even if they are written high up on a wall, lest one reads documents i.e. bills and expenses. If however it is carved into the wall it is permitted, but reading from a tablet is prohibited even when carved into the tablet.(Rema: It is likewise prohibited to read them with one’s mind.
