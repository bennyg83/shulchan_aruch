One who borrows something from his fellow should not say to him "lend me," for it implies a long term, and there is concern lest he write; rather he should say "loan me"; and in a foreign tongue where there is no distinction between "lend me" and "loan me," he must say "give me."And when he borrowed on Shabbat and does not wish to trust him, he leaves collateral with him, but he should not say to him "take collateral," for it is like a weekday act [(Beit Yosef in the name of Ohr Chayim); just as one does not borrow on Shabbat, so one does not repay on Shabbat (Ashiri siman 115).]:
