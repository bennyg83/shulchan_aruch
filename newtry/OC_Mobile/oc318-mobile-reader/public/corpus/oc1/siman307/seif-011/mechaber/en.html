One who borrows an item from another should not say to him, 'Lend it to me,' using a term that implies a long-term loan, because there is concern that he may write it down. Rather, he should say, 'Let me borrow it.' In a foreign language in which there is no distinction between the expressions for lending and borrowing, he should say, 'Give it to me.' {Rama: If he borrows on Shabbos and the lender does not wish to trust him, he may leave collateral with him, but he should not say, 'Here is collateral,' because that resembles a weekday transaction. [Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chaim] Just as one does not borrow in the manner of a loan on Shabbos, so too one does not repay on Shabbos. [Rosh, siman 115]}
