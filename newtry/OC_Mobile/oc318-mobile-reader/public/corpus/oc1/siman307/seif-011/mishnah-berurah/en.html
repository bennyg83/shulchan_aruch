(42) For a long term — stam loan is thirty days, and concern lest the lender write the loan as a record; but loan's term is immediate — no concern he will write. Where custom is stam loan collectable immediately as in our lands — permitted to say "loan me" [Magen Avraham]; see Biur Halacha — this is not clear.
(43) And there is concern, etc. — Peri Megadim in the name of Rashba wrote: in loan language it does not help even if he stipulates short term — the sages did not distinguish; Peri Megadim further wrote: even borrowing books, a mitzvah matter — also not to use loan language.
(44) Give me — and permitted also to conclude "I will return and give you."
(45) He leaves collateral with him — and specifically wine and oil and Shabbos need; but what is not Shabbos need forbidden to leave collateral on it.
(46) Thus they do not repay — meaning even if he borrowed wine and oil for a long term — forbidden to repay on Shabbos; just as in loan they decreed lest he write, so in repayment; Tosafot wrote if he says in language of return and not repayment — permitted, for he has recognition and this does not lead to erasing. And all this specifically food and Shabbos need; otherwise forbidden in every language [Acharonim].
