Permitted to tell them on Shabbat, etc. — not from Sefer Chassidim; and one who guards himself from weekday speech — "holy" will be said of him; men of deed are very careful not to speak even necessary matters except in the holy tongue. I received: one who visits his fellow on Shabbat morning should not say as on a weekday "good morning" but "good Shabbat" to fulfill "remember the day of Shabbat." Shelah. Acharonim vol. 3. And so Machberet wrote vol. 1 that this ruling entered the cave of the pious and they did not accept it — the pious master Shelah; and after him the pious master author of Daat Chochma p. 11b followed; and our rabbis said "the pious," etc., that according to Rashi's explanation and Shulchan Aruch's ruling even mundane talk is forbidden to multiply; also explicit in Zohar that it profanes Shabbat, as the rabbi Daat Chochma wrote there with good reason; all the more from this one will certainly come to forbidden speech; and a careful person will fence so he not be among Shabbat profaners, God forbid. The rabbi Daat Chochma argued at length on the custom to visit groups on Shabbat eve to friends, etc. — see there at length, words of the holy living. And see above letter 1 as the Ari z"l wrote.
