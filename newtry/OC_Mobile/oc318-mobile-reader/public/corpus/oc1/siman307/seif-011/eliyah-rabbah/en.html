[11] And proper to punish, etc. — Olat Tamid 306: only intentionally; unintentionally no monetary punishment; some say only day hire but fixed sum permitted — until here; I do not know his source; it appears he deals with fixing and forbidden because he takes from the Israelite's house — as at start of 244. I later saw Kenesset HaGedolah brought thus in responsa R' Eliezer Ashkenazi siman 256; but he concluded his heart was troubled; also Magen Avraham wrote in 292 it appears even with fixing forbidden; also what Olat Tamid wrote no monetary punishment — in R' Eliezer's responsum it is clear no punishment at all and he is believed without witnesses and excuses — see Choshen Mishpat siman 30 seif 24. Magen Avraham: it appears even designating a place is forbidden since merchandise is Israelite's; however if gentiles took from Israelite's house before Shabbat, even sailing on Shabbat — permitted.
