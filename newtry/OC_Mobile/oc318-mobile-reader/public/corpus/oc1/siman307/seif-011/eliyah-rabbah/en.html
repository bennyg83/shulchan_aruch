And it is proper to punish, etc. The Olas Tamid wrote in siman 306 that this is specifically where he acted intentionally, but if he was inadvertent one does not punish them monetarily. Some say this applies only to a day laborer, but where a price was fixed it is permitted. I do not know his source, for it implies that it refers even to where a wage was fixed, since otherwise it is forbidden even without taking from a Jewish house, as explained in siman 252, seif 2, and in the Magen Avraham there. It also appears that if the person erred and thought it was permitted, he is believed to say he acted inadvertently.
