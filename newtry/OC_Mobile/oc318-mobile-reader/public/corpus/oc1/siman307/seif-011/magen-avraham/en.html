For a long term — stam loan is thirty days; but loan is immediate; and if so where custom is stam loan collectable immediately as in our lands — permitted to say "loan me"; and it appears in Gemara only wine and oil and Shabbat need; but what is not Shabbat need forbidden to leave collateral; and see 306 seif 6: "and likewise orally," etc., he leaves a cloak — implies forbidden for other things except sacrifice or eating and drinking; and so Ran ch. 1 of Shabbat challenged: how permitted to seize movable collateral for her ketubah — behold he acquires on Shabbat, etc.

They do not repay — thus Rivash: forbidden to redeem the son, for it should only be repaying debt forbidden, as they said because of loans and repayments on Yom Tov — until here; and Tosafot ch. 5 of Bava Metzia wrote forbidden to say language of loan and repayment — only loan and return; and specifically food and Shabbat need; otherwise forbidden in every language; see 306 seif 7.
