Permitted to give a gentile money on erev Shabbat to buy for him, provided he does not tell him "buy on Shabbat."And likewise permitted to give him garments to sell, provided he does not tell him to sell them on Shabbat (Beit Yosef in the name of Semag);) one who hired a gentile to transport his merchandise and the gentile came on Shabbat and took it from an Israelite's house — forbidden, and proper to punish the doer (R' Yerucham ch. 12, Beit Yosef siman 306).
