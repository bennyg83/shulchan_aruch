That he not tell him buy on Shabbat. Beit Yosef brought the words of Peri Megadim ch. 6 and thus: and one opinion for all these that are permitted with the sun that all of them if he wants he does tomorrow if he wants he waits and the gentile does on his own understanding; but here it is impossible except tomorrow on market day — end quote. Beit Yosef wrote on this market day is not precise but the manner of speech was cited for even if it is not market day since he tells him to buy for him on Shabbat it is forbidden and this is the essence of the distinction for in those he does not tell him to do on Shabbat — end quote and it appears to me he cited market day for in this even if he does not mention explicitly that he buy on Shabbat only gives him money to buy for him unspecified and in that place it is not available to buy except on Shabbat which is market day as is customary in small towns — it is like mentioning Shabbat explicitly and in a place where it is somewhat available also on other days there is no prohibition unless he mentions Shabbat explicitly and it appears further that even in those they permit with the sun such as giving vessels to a gentile launderer there is prohibition if he gives him the vessels close to Shabbat and tells him "see that I need them tomorrow" for this is like saying explicitly he launder them on Shabbat and likewise regarding if he gives him money on erev Shabbat to buy and tells him he should go on his way tomorrow as I wrote. And it appears further in what they permitted if he did not remind him that he buy on Shabbat — nevertheless the Israelite should not benefit from him on that Shabbat for in any case he did for the Israelite and this is similar to a gentile who lit a lamp for an Israelite and even if he fixed wages for this it does not help for nevertheless he does the melacha for the Israelite and Semak wrote just as one does not send a letter by a gentile if he knows he carries it on Shabbat unless he fixes payment for him — so too one who gives him garments to sell implies that without fixing a price there is prohibition in every case like a letter:
