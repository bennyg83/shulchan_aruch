(14) From erev Shabbat to buy for him — and specifically when he fixed wage for this, for then he acts for himself; otherwise forbidden, as explained siman 245 seif 5; see Biur Halacha.

(15) Provided he does not say, etc. — and if market day there is Shabbat and in that place buying is only on market day as usual in small towns — it is as mentioning Shabbat day explicitly, whether he asks to buy for him or gives him to sell; and even from start of week forbidden; where somewhat available other days — prohibition only if he mentions Shabbat explicitly; and if the Israelite said "see that I must travel on motzei Shabbat" — also as mentioning Shabbat explicitly. Taz wrote: what they permitted if he did not mention buying on Shabbat — nevertheless the Israelite may not benefit that Shabbat, for in any case he did for the Israelite; though it deals when he fixed wage as above in s.k. 14 it does not help; but Acharonim and Turei Even disagree. Sefer Chassidim wrote: one should not dwell in a city whose market is Shabbat, for impossible not to sin; if market is not in his neighborhood — permitted.

(16) One who hired, etc. — for even though he fixed wage for this and did not designate that he transport on Shabbat, and the non-Jew acts for himself buying on Shabbat — nevertheless forbidden since he buys from an Israelite's house on Shabbat, as above 292 seif 1. And in our case where merchandise is Israelite's — even if he designated a place and left merchandise there it does not help.

(17) From an Israelite's house on Shabbat — but if he bought before Shabbat, even though he sails on Shabbat outside the techum — permitted since he fixed wage and acts for himself, as explained 292.

(18) The doer — according to his wealth in money or lashes if he has no money; and if he erred thinking permitted — they do not punish and he is believed he acted unintentionally [Acharonim].
