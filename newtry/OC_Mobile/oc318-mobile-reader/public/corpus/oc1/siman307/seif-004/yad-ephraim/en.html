Magen Avraham s.k. 10 — because it is for the sake of that melacha — see Tosafot Shabbat there, this wording appears and requires explanation; see in Eshel Avraham, Maharsha, and Maharam there what they wrote to emend this; and in my opinion there is an emendation and so it should read "for the sake of some melacha" etc.; and meaning regarding a gentile, since he surely wants to stand with him on the understanding he will do some melacha — therefore forbidden; and on this they wrote this applies also to an Israelite — presumably for melacha — and nevertheless permitted, for this is not reflection; and if so for a gentile too permitted.
