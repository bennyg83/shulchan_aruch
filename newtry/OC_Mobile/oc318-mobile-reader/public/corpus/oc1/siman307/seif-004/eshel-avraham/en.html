To buy — an Israelite and gentile who became partners to buy something from the royal house and the sale is on Shabbat — he ruled in responsum Beit Yehudah siman 44 that it is forbidden to command the gentile to buy it; and it does not appear from siman 266 see there, for there the gentile acts without command and the gentile does not restrain himself — unlike here, if there is no Israelite partner he does not buy at all — see there at length.
