There is — Magen Avraham letter 12 at length agrees except Taz distinguishes: what can be done now, such as partition of people — permitted to tell gentile to do so; see there; unlike if there were watchmen — only that speech not be weekday; permitted for tomorrow as in seif 7. And "some permit" telling a gentile d'oraisa melacha for mitzvah — do not challenge that telling a gentile has no prohibition except reshut — derive from "pursuing your affairs"; one may say hinting is also forbidden — Rabbenu Bechor seif 22; Taz letter 2, Magen Avraham letter 31 — as agent through hint; unlike "speak a matter" where thought and hint are permitted as 307 seif 7; see letter 1 at length.

The lay writer has a small question, which I present here, and by the way some details will be clarified. It is plain to me that telling a gentile is shevut on Shabbat even in what is only a plain prohibition — it resembles loading on a gentile's donkey — forbidden to tell a gentile to drive it; and Torah shevut di-shevut is stricter — one decree; it has karet and stoning; from this I say Yoreh Deah 297:4 and Even HaEzer 5:64 in Bava Metzia 1 — and it was resolved in study: in all prohibitions with a lav, telling a gentile is forbidden — we hold a gentile is not warned on castration and leaving eggs, etc. — therefore because of telling a gentile; all the more Shabbat shevut with a lav. Plain that in other prohibitions shevut di-shevut one may permit — certainly not asked; resolved only in lav, not shevut di-shevut. I was unsure in other prohibitions, positive command — whether telling a gentile is forbidden; Rabbenu Bechor in Yoreh Deah on lav implies positive command is lighter than lav — "a lav for a positive command is stricter" — we have no source to forbid; we derive that after six hours it is permitted to tell a gentile to buy for the Israelite with the Israelite's money, and to place chametz in gentile's domain until after Pesach — no agency for a gentile and it is his lav; telling a gentile on erev Pesach after six depends on dispute whether the gentile transgresses a lav after six or only the positive command of "it shall rest"; most agree even Rambam — only positive command; may we say in positive command they did not decree telling a gentile? Yom Tov and Yom Kippur are different — Shabbat is called, as Tosafot Bava Metzia write.

See in his holy words in Even HaEzer 5 letter 19: to say the halacha is not as Mishna Berurah 96 who ruled gentile agency for gentile — "also you" — from what is permitted to sell to a gentile though his agent will castrate, etc.; the student in Taharat HaDash siman 299 [and in Even HaEzer 9 the Maggid Mishneh is copied] — the reason is no barur even for a gentile; and we may say all the more we hold there is no agency for a gentile; it appears he is unsure only with combined reasons. Rabbenu Bechor in Choshen Mishpat ch. 88 s.k. 15 seized the Tashbetz's reason in the presumption that it is forbidden — there is gentile agency; "words are heard" does not apply; and this reason alone pleased him (Eshel Avraham ch. 8). If so, here in Even HaEzer where he ruled even for his agent and today's gentile — certainly they do not observe the seven Noachide laws at all and are suspect — certainly there is gentile agency for her — from "there is no agency for a gentile," not as Mishna Berurah; see Peticha Klalit part 4 and siman 48 Magen Avraham 8; and it will be explained in siman 348 in Taz and Magen Avraham; here we cannot elaborate.
