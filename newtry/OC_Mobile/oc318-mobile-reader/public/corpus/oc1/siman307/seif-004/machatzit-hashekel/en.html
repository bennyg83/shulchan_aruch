(s.k. 4) And likewise permitted, etc. — see 245, etc.; some say this law's source is Semag and Smak — they permitted only with fixing wage, but without fixing forbidden; if so why did he write here stam permitted, implying even without fixing? And therefore Magen Avraham: no need to infer from Semag and Smak, for anyway it is clear in the Mechaber's words 245: "permitted for an Israelite to give merchandise to a gentile to sell if he fixed wage only, that he not tell him sell on Shabbat" — until here; and in Sefer Acharonim he brought in the name of Mahari Weil who challenged Rama: why repeat, since clear from Beit Yosef 245 — see there.
