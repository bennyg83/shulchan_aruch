(s.k. 4) It is likewise permitted, etc. See siman 245, etc. The meaning is that Olas Shabbos asks that this ruling originates in Semag and Semak, and they permitted it only where the Jew fixed the non-Jew's compensation. Without a fixed payment it is forbidden. Why, then, does Rama write here without qualification that it is permitted, implying that this is so even if he did not fix the payment? Magen Avraham therefore writes that there is no need to infer this from the words of Semag and Semak, since it is already stated explicitly in the words of the Mechaber in siman 245: "A Jew may give merchandise to a non-Jew to sell if he fixed his compensation, provided that he does not tell him, 'Sell it on Shabbos.'" End quote. Eliyah Rabbah cites Minchas Yom Tov, who asks why Rama needed to repeat this ruling when it is already stated in this manner by Beit Yosef in siman 245; see there.
