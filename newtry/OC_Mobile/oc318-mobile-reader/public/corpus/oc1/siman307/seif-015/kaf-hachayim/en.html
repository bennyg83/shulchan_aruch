(15) Forbidden — to tell a gentile that a gentile friend should do melacha. Mahar Gershon Ashkenazi in responsum brought in Chavot Yair siman 49 and Nega. And Mahari Engel in Sefer Bnei Yehudah p. 67b agreed. Berkei Yosef vol. 5. And so in Sefer Shulchan Atzei Shittim siman 1: Israelite who tells gentile, and gentile to another gentile — is only one shevut. And so in responsa Shenot Chaim siman 38 and 175. However in Tiferet Yisrael on mishnayot Kelim Shabbat, on measure of telling a gentile siman 5, wrote it is shevut di-shevut — see there. And there is relevance to what is written below seif 5 that shevut di-shevut in a place of mitzvah or great need is permitted — see there. And if so, since most authorities count telling gentile to friend as one shevut — we rule thus; only if there are other sides to be lenient one may rely on those who permit; see Shadal Klalim part 1 letter 174.
