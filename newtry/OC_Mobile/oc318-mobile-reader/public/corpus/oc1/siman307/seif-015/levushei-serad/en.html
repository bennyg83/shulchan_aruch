Magen Avraham s.k. 9 — because he wants to know that melacha (Maharsha on Shabbat 120 — he emended that he does it according to that telling; meaning when the gentile does melacha he does it on account of that telling that at the time of telling he was ready to do immediately, only the Israelite did not want until evening; if so the Israelite's telling helps; unlike an Israelite worker who keeps Shabbat, for he does melacha not at all through Shabbat telling but through what they speak in the evening).
