(s.k. 15) There is no, etc. — because of loans and repayments, etc. — for they said Bava Metzia 75a and so Shabbat 148: members of a group who are strict with each other come to prohibition, etc., and because of loans and repayments on Yom Tov; Tosafot explained: forbidden to say language of thirty-day loan or repayment — only "loan me" and at repayment time "return" and not repayment; they fear saying "loan" since loan returns immediately and they fear immediate claim; therefore they say "loan" and automatically do not say "return" but repayment — through this they transgress in their language; from this it is proven language of repayment is also forbidden; if so redemption is forbidden, not better than repayment; and for redemption one cannot change the law; and perhaps even if one could change redemption language, since permitted only for Shabbat need as Magen Avraham afterward wrote — redemption though a mitzvah may be forbidden.
