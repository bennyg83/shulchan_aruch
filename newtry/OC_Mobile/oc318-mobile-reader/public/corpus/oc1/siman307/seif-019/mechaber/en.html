If merchandise is in danger of being ruined on Shabbos by rain or something else, or if a flood of water, meaning a stream or accumulation of water, is coming and will cause him monetary loss, or a barrel of wine has become damaged and its contents are being lost, it is permitted to call a non-Jew even though he certainly knows that the non-Jew will save the property. Regarding covering merchandise, fruit, or another item because of rain, see above, siman 338, seif 7. It is likewise permitted to say to a non-Jew, 'Whoever saves it will not lose,' just as in the case of a fire they permitted saying, 'Whoever extinguishes will not lose.' There is an authority who says that this was permitted specifically in the case of a fire. See also simanim 334, 305, and 336, seif 9.
