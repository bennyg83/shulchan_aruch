So in Shaarei Torah, Semag, Hagahot Maimoniot ch. 10; and the words are astonishing as plain — a whole mishnah: if the gentile lit by himself it is forbidden for the Israelite to benefit; and similar; as Gur Aryeh wondered klal 3 siman 21 and after him in Sefer 11 — see there with explanation. And therefore one should not be lenient except where the Israelite's body does not benefit, as Gur Aryeh wrote; or there is already fire and the gentile adds, as Sefer 11. Berkei Yosef vol. 6. And what Taz wrote vol. 2 on Tur — he inclined to leniency; Maharsham vol. 2 already questioned him — see there. Therefore in all these leniencies that Beit Yosef wrote to tell a gentile to wipe his nose so he understands to remove soot on the candle, and Kenesset HaGedolah Hagahot Tur vol. 2 to say the candle does not shine and the maidservant understands and cuts the wick, or when he wants a book where there is no candle to tell the maidservant to bring something and she lights and he follows — in all this one should not be lenient. And so Ikkarei HaDat siman 14 letter 100 in the name of Mahari Weil — see there; and so Kenesset HaGedolah in Hagahot Tur in the name of Shlah ch. Shoel that hinting on Shabbat itself for Shabbat need is forbidden for all. And so Magen Avraham s.k. 31, unlike Beit Yosef — see there; and see below letter 64. However only when saying "the house is dark" and the maidservant understands herself to remove soot — some permit, as written below letter 57.
