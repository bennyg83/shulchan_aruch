(68) Merchandise lost, etc. — and he concludes one may call a non-Jew — even if this requires Torah melacha such as taking from reshut ha-rabbim to reshut ha-yachid; and likewise muktzeh moving or reshut ha-yachid to karmelit — even to command non-Jew explicitly permitted per above s.k. 5 if great loss.

(69) Permitted to call, etc. — permitted because when a person is frantic over money, if you do not permit even these ways he will save himself and do Torah melacha.

(70) Whoever saves, etc. — to tell him in second person "if you save you will not lose" — forbidden [Acharonim from Shulchan Gavoah; and implied Yoreh Deah 221 seif 8].

(71) And some say, etc. — one who is lenient does not lose; and so the Mechaber rules below 334 s.k. 26; and to call non-Jew to show him and not tell him — all permit.
