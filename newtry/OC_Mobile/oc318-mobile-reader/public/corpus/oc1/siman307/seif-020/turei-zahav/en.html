Shoel according to R' Yehuda's method for we learned: an incident with a certain pious man that a breach opened in his field and he considered fencing it and remembered it was Shabbat and that pious man refrained and did not fence it and a miracle occurred and a caper bush grew for him and from it was his livelihood etc. — difficult why they called him pious in that he did not fence on Shabbat and further what did he consider for what doubt was there to advise this and it appears at first he also knew it was Shabbat only he considered fencing via a gentile as stated below regarding saving from loss and on this he remembered and said this is forbidden for it is Shabbat — meaning because of shevut of Shabbat and he refrained even on a weekday from fencing like R' Yehuda here and even though he did not do an act only there was thought — this was his piety that he judged himself thought like deed. And further it appears to me to say on Shabbat he was advising how to fence on a weekday and thought there is no sin and afterward he regretted since on Shabbat he thought about it and for this reason he did not fence even on a weekday so as not to benefit from this thought and this is the essence for so it is in Yerushalmi explicitly: wrote in 8 that forbidden to mention on Shabbat that one's beloved died:
