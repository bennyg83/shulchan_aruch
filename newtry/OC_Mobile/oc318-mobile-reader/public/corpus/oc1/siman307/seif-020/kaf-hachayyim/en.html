20) [Seif 3] It is forbidden to give a non-Jew money on Friday to purchase for him on Shabbos. The same applies if he gives him money on another weekday to purchase for him on Shabbos. Levush Serad. Even fixing his compensation does not help, because he explicitly tells him to purchase on Shabbos, as stated at the beginning of siman 247. See below, paragraph 25.
