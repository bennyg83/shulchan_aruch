Magen Avraham, seif katan 4. See siman 245, seif 5. The Olas Shabbos asks why it was not explained that this applies specifically where he fixed the payment, as in siman 247 regarding a letter, and as the Taz writes here at the end of seif katan 3. To this Magen Avraham answers that the ruling stated explicitly in Shulchan Aruch, siman 245, namely that a fixed payment is required, escaped his notice.
