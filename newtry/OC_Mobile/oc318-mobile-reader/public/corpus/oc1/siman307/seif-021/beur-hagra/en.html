Seif 21 forbidden to say. As closed muzzle, open threshing floor and treading in it Bava Metzia 90a and as Rashi explained there s.v. chasum and see there s.v. gentile treading etc. and so wrote Nissim Gaon there and so Rosh wrote not like Tosafot on erev Shabbat and even in gentile grain that is gentile's benefit — to the conclusion of Tosafot there s.v. chasum:

But. That it is like "muzzle your ox" from threshing floor for it is not written except "muzzle your ox and tread" certainly permitted and thus Nissim Gaon there and we learn for our cases where an Israelite tells a gentile "muzzle my ox and tread your grain" or "muzzle your ox and tread my grain" forbidden and one opinion for that which is taught in Mekhilta that in a gentile's melacha there is no concern of telling a gentile shevut for there he does not do for the sake of Israel etc.:
