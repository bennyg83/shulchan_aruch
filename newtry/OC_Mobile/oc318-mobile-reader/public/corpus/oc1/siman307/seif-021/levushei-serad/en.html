There — "and plain" — this is the matter in its own right: the view of a side of permission does not help except to tell one's fellow or gentile to do tomorrow, not to do on Shabbat. And that which seif 10 permits to tell one's fellow "guard for me fruit in your techum" on Shabbat itself though he cannot go there except through watchmen — there is another reason, and without the view of watchmen it is permitted since his fellow is within the fruit's techum and there is no Shabbat prohibition at all — permitted to command him on what is permitted on Shabbat; unlike all telling a gentile, forbidden because there is Shabbat prohibition in the matter, only the gentile is not commanded; unlike here where there is no Shabbat prohibition for the agent though there is for the sender — permitted.
