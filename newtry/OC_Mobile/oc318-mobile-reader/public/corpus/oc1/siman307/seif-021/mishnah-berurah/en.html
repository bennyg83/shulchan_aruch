(73) Forbidden to tell a non-Jew, etc. — since what he tells the non-Jew to do for himself he cannot do himself — it is included in telling a non-Jew shevut and forbidden even though the Israelite does not benefit at all from that melacha; nevertheless permitted to tell him to do melacha for himself, such as "take your meat and cook it for your need" — since it was never the Israelite's and the non-Jew does it only for himself, they did not decree telling a non-Jew.
(74) On Shabbos — see Acharonim who proved from Tosafot and Rosh that even on erev Shabbos forbidden to give a non-Jew and tell him to cook on Shabbos for himself.
