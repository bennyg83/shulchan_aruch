[16] And even if he already, etc. — and it is explained in Rashi that if there is need in the account it is also forbidden (Taz); requires study: I saw Ran permits explicitly, such as saying one who wants to build will spend so much; and first explanation of Rashi; and Rabbenu Yerucham Netiv 12 part 7 wrote similarly; since they are great Acharonim and did not mention Rashi's words to them — we heed them; also it appears to me to emend Rashi 150a: not "for you and not for another" but "for another" — somewhat implied; now Rashi also holds like Ran and Rabbenu Yerucham. Rambam wrote: accounts without need permitted — how many se'im in such a year, how many dinars at my son's wedding, such-and-such king's levies (Rokeach 133), etc., idle talk without need (until here); Maggid explained: idle talk is unbecoming for G-d-fearing; and below it will be explained forbidden for anyone to multiply idle talk on Shabbos — perforce "G-d-fearing" means they should not speak even on a weekday even a little more; but to multiply on Shabbos forbidden for everyone; and this is Beurei Rashal: even on a weekday not so proper for a Torah scholar — sitting of scoffers and neglecting study.

Rambam: accounts without need permitted — se'im, dinars at wedding, king's levies (Rokeach), idle talk; Maggid: unbecoming for G-d-fearing; below forbidden to multiply idle talk on Shabbos — "G-d-fearing" means not even weekday; multiply on Shabbos forbidden for all; Beurei Rashal: weekday improper for scholar — scoffers, neglecting study.
