I. See Taz. Even telling the non-Jew to ride is forbidden. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 11, for whatever a Jew may not do, even because of a D'Rabbanan prohibition, he may not instruct a non-Jew to do. See siman 305, seif 18. This accords with the ruling here that even where the act is prohibited only D'Rabbanan, the instruction is forbidden.
