S.k. 23 — Tosafot and Rosh wrote etc. — requires study, for Shulchan Aruch does not mention portraits; and in truth it requires study on Shulchan Aruch who brought "turn not to idols" on parables and fables; and on Shabbat 149 it is on portraits — only Rabbenu Yona wrote to forbid because of "a sitting of scoffers"; and it appears there is a missing word in Magen Avraham and it should read: and on Shabbat they say this reason on portraits, that even on a weekday forbidden to gaze at them; and Tosafot and Rosh wrote etc.; and see what is written on these words of Magen Avraham in responsa Beit Efrayim siman 15.
