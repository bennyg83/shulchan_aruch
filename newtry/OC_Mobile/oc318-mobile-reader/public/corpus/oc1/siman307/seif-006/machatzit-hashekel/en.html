(s.k. 6) To punish, etc. — and it appears even if he designated a place, etc. — meaning in such a case some permit 292: if he sold to a gentile from erev Shabbat and designated a place — nevertheless Rama concluded there to be strict as the Mechaber, that permitted only if the gentile removes it from the Israelite's house on his own close to nightfall, even if he designated a place; also according to Magen Avraham stated in the name of Hagahot Maimoniot the reason designation helps for those who permit to remove even on Shabbat since he designated — it is the gentile's courtyard and he acquired the courtyard from his own initiative; this does not apply here, for in truth it belongs to the Israelite — see there what Magen Avraham wrote on Hagahot Maimoniot.
