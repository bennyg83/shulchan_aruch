(s.k. 6) To punish, etc. It appears that this applies even if he designated a place for him, etc. That is to say, at the beginning of siman 252 there are authorities who permit a non-Jew to remove an item even on Shabbos if the Jew sold it to him before Shabbos and designated a place for it. Nevertheless, Rama concludes there that one should be stringent in accordance with the Mechaber's opinion, which permits it only if the non-Jew removes the item from the Jew's house while it is still day, close to nightfall, even if the Jew designated a place for it. Furthermore, according to what Magen Avraham writes there in the name of Hagahos Maimoniyos, the reason that designating a place is effective according to the lenient authorities is that, once the Jew designated a place for the non-Jew, it is regarded as the non-Jew's domain, and the place acquires the item for him before Shabbos. That rationale does not apply here, since the item actually belongs to the Jew. See there in Magen Avraham regarding what he writes concerning Hagahos Mordechai.
