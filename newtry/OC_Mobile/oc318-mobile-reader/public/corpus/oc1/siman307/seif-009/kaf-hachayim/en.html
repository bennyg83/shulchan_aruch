(9) Forbidden — to detain a gentile on Shabbat because of a debt; but he may say on a weekday to the judge why he did not detain the gentile on Shabbat. Agudah. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. And in responsum of Bach siman 146 he permits one who has a debt with an oppressor and on Shabbat learned the oppressor will leave the city and perhaps not return — permitted to go on Shabbat to receive from the officer that he pay him — see there; and they brought it in Acharonim end of this siman. Chayei Adam general rule 61 letter 3; nevertheless if possible he should do it through a gentile.
