[9] Provided he does not, etc. — and Taz and Olat Tamid and Magen Avraham: if impossible to buy except on Shabbat, such as villages where buying is only on market day and market is Shabbat — even stam forbidden; unlike Beit Yosef's implication. Sefer Minchat Kohen ruled like Beit Yosef if he does not say to buy on market day — which is as saying to buy on Shabbat and is not the essence; even stam forbidden. Magen Avraham wrote: the same forbidden to give him something to sell; so Masat Binyamin; and wrote on fourth and fifth permitted as 293 — until here; requires study: certainly forbidden even from the first day to tell a gentile to do on Shabbat, as Levush and Masat Binyamin and clear from Rambam and Tur who wrote stam forbidden before Shabbat; and from law of telling after Shabbat above; and from Maharam's responsum; and when impossible to sell/buy only on Shabbat it is as saying explicitly to them; unlike 292 seif 2 where he is not compelled on Shabbat; and in the letter at the start of 287; even so Rama was strict there. Taz: even for laundry given to gentile close to Shabbat, saying "see I need them on motzei Shabbat" — as saying explicitly he will launder on Shabbat; and if he gives money erev Shabbat to buy and says he will travel on motzei Shabbat — until here; plain; see 292 seif 2.
