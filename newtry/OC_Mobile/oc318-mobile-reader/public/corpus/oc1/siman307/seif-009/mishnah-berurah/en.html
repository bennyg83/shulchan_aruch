(39) To detach fruit, etc. — and it deals when he does not stand by the garden — otherwise the matter is evident through his standing that he wishes to do something on motzei Shabbat; as above 306 seif 1; see there in Mishna Berurah s.k. 1.

(40) Because the matter is evident — but within the techum the matter is not evident why he waits at dusk; and it is not similar to 306 seif 1 forbidding examining property such as going in his field to see what is needed tomorrow — even within techum forbidden, for there in every case evident he plans action such as near plowing, harvest, or weeding time — forbidden because of "pursuing your affairs"; therefore they also forbade in Gemara strolling at the city gate to exit after dark to the bathhouse near the city [their custom placed the bathhouse at the city gate at its end] — all for this reason; unlike our case where not evident except when waiting at end of techum — then it appears he waits to do on motzei Shabbat something forbidden on Shabbat; nevertheless forbidden only if his intent is truly for that; but if his intent is to wait in order to walk at night — permitted, for walking itself is not forbidden, as above seif 8.
