To detach fruits, etc. This refers to a case where he is not standing next to the garden, for otherwise the matter is recognizable through his standing there, that he wants to do something there on Motzaei Shabbos, as above in siman 306, seif 1; see the Mishnah Berurah there, seif katan 1.
Because the matter is recognizable. But within the techum it is not recognizable why he is waiting there. This is not comparable to what is written in siman 306, seif 1, that it is forbidden to inspect his property, such as walking through his field to see what must be done tomorrow, even if it is within the techum. There the matter is recognizable in every case, that he is calculating to do some work there, such as when it is near plowing time, harvest time, weeding time, or the like, and this is forbidden because of ‘pursuing your affairs.’ Therefore the Gemara also forbade strolling near the city entrance in order to go out after dark to the bathhouse nearby, for their practice was to place the bathhouse near the gate at the end of the city. All of this is for that reason. But in our case the matter is recognizable only when he waits at the end of the techum, for then it appears that he is waiting so that after Shabbos he can do something that was forbidden on Shabbos. Nevertheless, it is forbidden only when that is truly his intention; if his intention is to wait there so that he can walk afterward at night, it is permitted, for walking itself is not a prohibited matter, as above in seif 8.
