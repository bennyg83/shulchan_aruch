Because the matter is evident — and some say for what is written 306 seif 1: there it is within a field needing plowing and work — evident; and strolling at the city gate where the bathhouse is — evident and leads to suspicion (Maggid Mishneh ch. 24 in Tosafot, Ramban, Rashba, Rambam); requires study: in Eruvin ch. 3 they said explicitly even at end of techum the matter is not evident — if Torah scholar they say learning distracted him; if am ha'aretz — wine happened to him; and Rashi's reason for prohibition: whatever is forbidden for him to do on Shabbat, forbidden to invite himself to it — and likewise since forbidden to detach fruit, forbidden to invite himself; Tosafot answered since not so evident — permitted; but to wait at end of techum even if not evident they forbade because of "pursuing your affairs" as they forbade speech where there is no side of permission; Maggid Mishneh's words require study.
