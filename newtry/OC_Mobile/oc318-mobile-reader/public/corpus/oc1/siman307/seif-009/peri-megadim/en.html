Engraving — Taz: when there are two distinctions — engraving and wall does not interchange with weekday documents; high wall writing interchanges; engraved ledger also interchanges; and for what Eruvin wrote high is forbidden lest he erase — one may say distinction: for the attendant it is also forbidden to read though no erasing, as Beit Yosef wrote — lest he read weekday documents; see the sugya Shabbat 149a and Tosafot s.v. "forever," and Levush wrote on high no erasing concern — difficult from siman 275; also two may read — perhaps one reminds the other; weekday documents forbidden even for ten because of "pursuing your affairs" as Levush and Beit Yosef in Rashi ch. Mashilin; but Rambam ch. 23 — reason lest he erase. I thought to say he wrote bills of sale etc. — writing and erasing are only rabbinic, as 306 seif 11 in the gloss, Magen Avraham 325 letter 10 — perhaps the girsa on high is like Eruvin — there is Torah prohibition except erasing is not liable unless intending to write two letters — Orach Mishpat 81:5; without intent to write — only rabbinic; Shabbat 149a — for one who holds high two cubits, the same here; and it will be explained in Magen Avraham letter 17.
