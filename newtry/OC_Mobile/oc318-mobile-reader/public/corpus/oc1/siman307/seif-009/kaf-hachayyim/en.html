9) It is forbidden to detain a non-Jew on Shabbos because of a debt. During the week, however, one may ask a judge, 'Why did you not detain the non-Jew on Shabbos?' Agudah; Magen Avraham, paragraph 1. Responsa Bach, siman 146, permits the following case: a person has a debt owed by an oppressor, and on Shabbos learns that the oppressor will leave the city and might never return. He may go on Shabbos to complain to the government official so that the oppressor will pay him; see there. Eliyah Rabbah at the end of this siman and Chayei Adam, klal 61, paragraph 3, cite this. Nevertheless, if possible, it should be done through a non-Jew.
