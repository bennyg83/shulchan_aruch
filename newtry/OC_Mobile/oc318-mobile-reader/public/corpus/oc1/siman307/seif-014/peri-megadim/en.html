So that — Taz: see Bava Metzia 90a "fowl sleep in coops of Armenians" — Shabbat to after Shabbat could be done permissibly — they did not penalize; unlike castration forever impossible for an Israelite permissibly — they penalized; but one who crossbreeds via gentile — they do not penalize. Taz Yoreh Deah 297 letter 2: castration is recognizable — they penalized; unlike crossbreeding animals — not recognizable — they did not penalize; and in Beit Shmuel Even HaEzer 5 letter 17 in the name of Derishah: castration — they penalized; not crossbreeding tamei with tamei and pure with pure (since tamei does not conceive with pure; and to forbid the animal in what he does not benefit — one may say they did not penalize). Know therefore Rambam in practice: tamei with tamei is rabbinic — in truth crossbreeding is Torah, as Eshel Avraham Yoreh Deah 297:1; we already stood on this in part 6 at length; see there; here we cannot elaborate. And see Rambam ch. 6 Laws of Shabbat halacha 8 wrote explicitly: even though the Israelite told the gentile to do and is liable — nevertheless permitted to stipulate so they do — Magen Avraham there: we did not find this prohibition in the Gemara; see there.
