[14] [Levush] Or one who is distressed, etc. — implies bodily distress, not in loss; Magen Avraham lenient in great loss for shevut di-shevut — his words are not compelled, for he relied on one version of Ran ch. barrels against many forbidders; also Ran end ch. R' Eliezer on milah implies he does not hold that version; so in seif 19; so Peskei Rakanati siman 98.
