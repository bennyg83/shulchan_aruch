(Seif katan 14) To invite, etc. See what was written in siman 306, seif katan 15, concerning the phrase, ‘and likewise on Erev Pesach, etc.’ For we learned at the beginning of the chapter HaShoel: A person may borrow from another person jugs of wine, etc.; and if the lender does not trust him, the borrower may leave his cloak with him, etc. Likewise, when Erev Pesach falls on Shabbos in Yerushalayim, meaning that one has no lamb for his Pesach offering and goes to a shepherd to take a lamb from him on Shabbos, if the shepherd does not trust him, he may leave his cloak with him, and they calculate the amount after Shabbos. This implies that it is permitted specifically for something needed on Shabbos, or for a mitzvah of that day, such as the Pesach offering, but not for another purpose.
And this is also implied by the Ran in the first chapter of Kesubos. This is the correct reading. It is the objection of the Ritva cited by the Magen Avraham in siman 306, seif katan 15.
