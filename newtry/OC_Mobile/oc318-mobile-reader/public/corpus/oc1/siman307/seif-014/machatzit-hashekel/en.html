HaShoel one borrows from his fellow so much wine, etc., and if he does not trust him he leaves his cloak with him; and on Pesach eve occurring on Shabbat in Jerusalem (meaning he has no lamb for Pesach — he goes to shepherd and buys lamb on Shabbat) if he does not trust he leaves cloak and makes account after Shabbat — implies only what is Shabbat need or that day's mitzvah such as Pesach; not for another matter.
