And even to say before Shabbat, etc. — it implies even to hire workers to build on a weekday, which is shevut, is forbidden; and so R' Saadiah, R' Shimon bar Berach, Semag, Tur — telling a gentile on erev Shabbat even for shevut is forbidden — see there. But from Rashba siman 475 it appears permitted to tell a gentile on erev Shabbat to hire workers on Shabbat to build on a weekday; and so Rokeach siman 88 and Rivash siman 387 p. 297; and so R' Eliezer Ashkenazi siman 256 — so from Rambam's wording — see there. And so Radbaz ch. 1 siman 232: they forbade telling a gentile on erev Shabbat only when telling him to do melacha — see there. Acharonim vol. 3; and see there.
