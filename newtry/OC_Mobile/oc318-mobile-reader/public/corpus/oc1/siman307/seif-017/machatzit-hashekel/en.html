(s.k. 17) Sunken engraving — but protruding, etc. — lest he read weekday documents, for they decree since it may be confused with a document on a wall; if we permit reading from a wall he will also read from paper and document; but when there is another change — engraving is sunken — there are two changes: it is a wall and writing is sunken; unlike writing on a tablet which is merely inscribed — does not interchange with documents; but tablet and ledger though sunken engraving — decree lest he read weekday documents since only one change — sunken writing; unlike wall where there are two. And if writing protrudes on wall — one might think since two changes — wall and protruding writing, unlike document where writing is inscribed — nevertheless forbidden lest he erase; protruding can be erased unlike sunken; even very high, etc.; and that the Mechaber gave reason forbidding very high "lest he read" not "lest he erase" and "do not distinguish" — must be reason "lest he read" is stronger — forbidden in itself without "do not distinguish"; so Sefer Turei Even.
