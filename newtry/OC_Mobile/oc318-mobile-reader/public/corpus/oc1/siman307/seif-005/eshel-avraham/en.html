To do it — and for this reason he learned in responsum Beit Yaakov siman 15 merit for what they practiced that gentiles extinguish candles in synagogue, for extinguishing itself is only shevut, for the halacha follows R' Yehuda that it is melacha not needed for itself, and telling a gentile is shevut di-shevut; and even so it is forbidden to say on Shabbat to extinguish — because of a decree that a person is hasty over his money; and all the more on Yom Kippur when the gentile does it himself and for himself — and see Levush siman 334 s.k. 28 similarly; and in any case one can say at the start of his hire that if he extinguishes the candles he will not lose his wages; and further without this it is permitted, for whatever he does is presumably only because of fire danger and sacred writings — and with this even permitted explicitly to tell a gentile to extinguish; see Magen Avraham on the above siman s.k. 30 and Hagahot Rama.

Because of a mitzvah — on the first day of Sukkot a gentile brought an etrog from outside the techum per the community's directive, since their etrog was disqualified — see responsum Darkei Moshe siman 36 who distinguishes whether he is outside twelve mil or not; and see Mordechai chapter HaDar; and he concludes in any case that telling a gentile to bring it is certainly permitted; and see siman 495.
