A matter that is not melacha and is not forbidden on Shabbat except because of shevut — permitted for an Israelite to tell a gentile to do it on Shabbat, provided there is some illness, or great need, or because of a mitzvah; for example an Israelite tells a gentile on Shabbat to climb a tree to bring a shofar to blow a mitzvah blast, or to bring water through a courtyard that was not included in an eruv to wash one who is distressed; and some forbid.And below siman 686 he ruled to permit; and see above siman 276 seif 2 that some are lenient even regarding d'oraisa melacha, and see there seif 3.
