There s.k. 13 — and the reason it is forbidden Rashi explained etc. — meaning in that of "a person should not walk in his field" etc.; Rashi explained in Eruvin there because forbidden to invite himself etc.; and on this Tosafot answered because the matter is not evident etc. — meaning within the techum permitted to stroll for pleasure, only the prohibition from the place where he is, for in his place he is decided to invite himself after Shabbat to do some work forbidden on Shabbat — and this is forbidden because of "from pursuing your affairs"; but specifically where the matter is evident he invites himself for this, such as a field etc.; unlike when he waits at dusk to detach fruit — even though his garden and ruin are there, nevertheless not so evident that he sits there for this — therefore permitted, for this is called only reflection of heart alone which is permitted; but all this within the techum where there is no prohibition from walking — unlike at the end of the techum where there is no way to walk a great distance to stroll for pleasure — this is forbidden if he goes so much for a weekday matter, because it is written "if you turn back your foot from Shabbat" etc. "from pursuing your affairs"; even though from the place where he is the matter is not evident; and it can also be that sometimes he goes this way for a permitted matter — I heard from my teacher etc.; nevertheless since his intent is for a weekday matter it is forbidden from the aspect of walking for a forbidden matter; and that which they say in Eruvin there that at the end of the techum it is permitted — since they join eruv only for a mitzvah matter or matters of Heaven — and for this reason they did not challenge there from the Mishnah that one may not wait at the techum to bring fruit etc. — matters of Heaven are different; only that it is difficult: forbidden from preparing from Yom Tov to Shabbat even though he was silent and said nothing — nevertheless it is preparation; for in "one should not walk at the end of his field" etc. he is also silent and nevertheless forbidden, for it is like inviting himself; and likewise here it is like preparing from Shabbat to Yom Tov; and on this he answered well that here the matter is not evident that he acts for the city; therefore since he was silent it is not preparation — unlike "one should not stroll" where the matter is evident and it is invitation; but all this for eruv which is matters of Heaven — unlike where he wants to do his own affairs — otherwise forbidden at the end of the techum even if the matter is not evident, since in truth his intent is for his affairs — and on this it says "and if you turn back" etc.; and this is necessary in my opinion from Tosafot Shabbat 150b that without "one should not stroll" there would be no difficulty to him: what is different within the techum that permitted from end of techum that forbidden? — it must be they held within the techum there is no prohibition from walking at all as stated — unlike end of techum; and nevertheless it is difficult for them that of "one should not walk" which is within the techum — and nevertheless forbidden; and on this they answered that there even though there is no prohibition from walking, nevertheless the prohibition is from the place where he stands now, for the matter is evident etc.; unlike waiting at dusk for fruit — not evident; but for end of techum this distinction is not needed at all — for wherever the prohibition is from walking itself, even if one can hang it that he walks for a permitted matter, nevertheless since in truth his intent is not for that it is forbidden; and the words of Terumat HaDeshen who wrote the reason is because evident and will lead to suspicion — requires study.
