And there are those who forbid. They hold that a double D'Rabbanan prohibition was permitted specifically for circumcision, because circumcision itself overrides Shabbos. This requires study, for in Eruvin 91a we say that in a time of danger they would bring a Torah scroll up from a roof to a courtyard to read from it. One must say that it is not comparable to there, for there it involved the public's Torah reading, or that there was danger.
