[18] If already, etc. — and Rashi explains if there is need in the account it is forbidden afterward also (Taz); requires study: I saw Ran permits explicitly; Rashi first explanation; Rabbenu Yerucham as above; great Acharonim omitted Rashi — we heed them; emend Rashi as above; now Rashi holds like Ran. Rambam: accounts without need permitted — se'im, dinars at wedding, king's levies (Rokeach), idle talk; Maggid: unbecoming for G-d-fearing; below forbidden to multiply idle talk on Shabbos — "G-d-fearing" means not even weekday; multiply on Shabbos forbidden for all; Beurei Rashal: weekday improper for scholar — scoffers, neglecting study.
