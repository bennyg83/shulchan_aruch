Taz s.k. 8 — "except for waiting at dusk" — meaning in the Gemara this was not said regarding telling a gentile — in truth telling is forbidden even within the techum; only for waiting at dusk the Gemara said thus; and on this he wrote "likewise the reason because it is not evident," etc.; but telling and waiting are not dependent on each other — for although Abba Shaul said "all that I may tell, I may wait at dusk for," telling and waiting are dependent — waiting where waiting is forbidden, at the end of the techum where the matter is evident, is permitted only where telling is permitted; unlike within the techum where waiting is permitted because not evident — telling is forbidden and waiting permitted; this is the continuation of his words.
