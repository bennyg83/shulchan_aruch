(18) Life — I saw in Rashbatz ch. 2 siman 281 he forbade telling a gentile to bring objects house to house without an eruv even for mitzvah, even if he said so before Shabbat — see there. And he follows his view that they did not permit shevut di-shevut except for milah which overrides Shabbat, as 397 — not everyone agrees with Rambam — see there; but for us, who permit shevut di-shevut for mitzvah, it is permitted; all the more when he said so before Shabbat — some permit even not for mitzvah; and so Radbaz: permitted by law to tell a gentile on erev Shabbat to come play tomorrow — they forbade telling a gentile from erev Shabbat only for melacha; but the Jew himself is forbidden only by decree; and some permit telling a gentile to play on Shabbat — do not add; see there. And though Rosh brought in Beit Yosef 317 forbade thus for pigeons for an Israelite on erev Shabbat — we rely on those authorities to permit plainly from erev Shabbat for mitzvah; and even one who is strict on shevut di-shevut even for mitzvah should not be so strict from erev Shabbat; all the more here, for the Gemara wrote that in a foreign language on Rosh HaShanah they permitted telling a gentile because he can bring via partitions of people — see there. And see what is written in 295 in the name of Ritva and Rabbenu Yehiel that they did not forbid telling a gentile for shevut without action in the thing itself, such as carrying from reshut to reshut — see there.
