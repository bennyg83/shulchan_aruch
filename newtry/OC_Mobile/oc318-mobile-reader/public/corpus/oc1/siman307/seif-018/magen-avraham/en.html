To ask — thus Rivan ch. 9: to ask from a demon for healing — whatever is permitted on a weekday permitted on Shabbat — until here; it appears he inferred "for healing": for in ch. Chelek we learn one does not ask from a demon on Shabbat (Rashi: when they lose something they ask through demons — forbidden on Shabbat because of "pursuing your affairs"); R' Yitzchak: even on a weekday forbidden because of danger, and halacha follows R' Yosi; it implies even what is permitted on a weekday as in Yoreh Deah 179 s.k. 16 is forbidden on Shabbat because of "pursuing your affairs" unless for healing, as 306 seif 7; see there; Rabbenu Yehudah skipped "for healing" and gave a stumbling block to examiners.
