To inquire. This is the wording of Rabbeinu Yerucham, section 9: to inquire of a demon for healing, anything that is permitted on a weekday is permitted on Shabbos, as is clear in Perek Chelek. It appears to me that he was precise in saying ‘for healing,’ because in Chelek we read that one may not inquire of a demon on Shabbos, which Rashi explains as when something is lost. But for healing, since it is permitted on a weekday, it is permitted on Shabbos.
