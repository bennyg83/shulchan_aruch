[1] "I will do tomorrow," etc. — even for a mitzvah matter (Sefer Chassidim 266, Magen Avraham); I followed Kenesset HaGedolah and Olat Tamid; and so I followed in my small book Eliyah Zuta; but I examined Sefer Chassidim — it deals when he says he will sleep in order to write a Torah scroll tomorrow; I brought it siman 290 s.k. 2 — then there is no pleasure of sleep for honor of Shabbos; but saying he will do a mitzvah tomorrow — from where? And on the contrary it implies from 306 seif 6 that it is permitted; and so from what Kolbo wrote. Further Sefer Chassidim siman 262: one should not dwell where non-Jews' market is on Shabbos — until here; and one must say specifically when the market is in Jewish neighborhoods, for then it is impossible not to sin in speech; and further he wrote: forbidden on Shabbos to tell one's friends who died or are in distress.
