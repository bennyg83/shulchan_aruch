But one who does not enjoy it is forbidden to say it etc. — the wording "but" is not correct and he should have written "even one who does not" etc. and in Levush he translated "but one who does not" etc. forbidden to tell them etc. and in truth it is so in Terumat HaDeshen siman 61 and thus: however I saw many times that some people who gather to tell these rumors are not enjoying the abundance of these rumors but do so to please their fellows gathered with them — there is concern of prohibition for those who do not enjoy — end quote and it appears his intent is those who tell have no desire at all to tell only they intend to fulfill their fellows' will in this — there is concern of prohibition; but if they intend that they will have enjoyment from it there is no concern for in this they also have enjoyment in that their fellow enjoys from it — permitted as above — a correct distinction:
