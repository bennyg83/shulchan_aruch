(1) [Seif 1] "And speak a matter, that your speech on Shabbat," etc. — the Maharshal was very careful not to speak in a foreign tongue on Shabbat night or day except when he had to say some derashah in a foreign tongue so the listening public would understand. He was also careful not to speak idle talk or weekday speech even in the holy tongue; and so he practiced on all Yom Tov as on Shabbat. Shaar HaKavanot 74b. Pri Etz Chaim, end of ch. 21.
