Forbidden on Shabbat to tell any matter that distresses him (Sefer Chassidim 310): "I will do tomorrow" — even a mitzvah matter (266). Agudah wrote: forbidden to detain a gentile on Shabbat because of a debt; but he may say on a weekday to the judge why he did not detain the gentile on Shabbat. Forbidden to detain Reuven's property in Shimon's hand (Rashdam Choshen Mishpat 105).
