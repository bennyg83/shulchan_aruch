1) [Seif 1] 'And speak a word,' so that your speech on Shabbos shall not be, etc. My teacher, of blessed memory, was exceedingly careful not to speak a foreign language on Shabbos, either at night or during the day, except when he delivered a teaching that had to be given in the vernacular so that the listeners would understand. He was also careful not to engage in idle conversation or weekday speech, even in the holy tongue. He followed the same practice on every Yom Tov as on Shabbos. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 74d; Pri Etz Chaim, gate 18, end of chapter 21.
