Laws of Shabbat dependent on speech. And in it 22 seifim:
"Speak a matter" — one's speech on Shabbat shall not be like one's speech on a weekday; therefore forbidden to say "I will do such-and-such tomorrow" or "I will buy such-and-such merchandise tomorrow"; and even in idle talk forbidden to multiply.And some say that recounting rumors and novel Torah matters is pleasure for them — permitted to tell on Shabbat as on a weekday; but one who does not enjoy them is forbidden to say them so his fellow may enjoy in them (Terumat HaDeshen siman 61).
