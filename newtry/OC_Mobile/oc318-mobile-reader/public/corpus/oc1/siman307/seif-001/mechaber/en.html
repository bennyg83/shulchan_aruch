The laws of Shabbos that depend upon speech. It contains 22 seifim: 'And speak a matter' teaches that one's speech on Shabbos should not be like one's speech on a weekday. Therefore it is forbidden to say, 'I will do such-and-such tomorrow,' or, 'I will purchase such-and-such merchandise tomorrow.' Even idle conversation should not be excessive. {Rama: For people who derive enjoyment from relating reports and new matters, it is permitted to discuss them on Shabbos as on a weekday. But one who does not enjoy such conversation may not say these things merely so that his fellow will enjoy them. [Terumat HaDeshen, siman 61]}
