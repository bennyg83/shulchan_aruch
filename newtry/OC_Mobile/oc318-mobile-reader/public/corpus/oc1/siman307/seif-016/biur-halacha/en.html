And transgresses because of "do not turn" etc. — in the Gemara it states forbidden to look at portraits and Tosafot in Shabbat and Rosh wrote specifically at a portrait made for idol worship but for ornament it is permitted and so appears the view of other poskim [Avodah Zarah] and some are stringent also in this [Tosafot Avodah Zarah 50] and Magen Avraham wrote specifically looking is forbidden according to them but mere seeing is permitted for all:
