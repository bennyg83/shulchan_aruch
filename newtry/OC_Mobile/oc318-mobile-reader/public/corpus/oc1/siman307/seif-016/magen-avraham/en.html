And he violates. Tosafot and the Rosh wrote that he violates this specifically with an image made for idolatry; see there. But in Avodah Zarah 50, Tosafot wrote that every image is forbidden, except for the image on a coin, which he is used to seeing constantly and therefore there is no issue of turning toward it.
In the Holy Tongue it is permitted. For the language itself has sanctity and one learns words of Torah from it. A get may be moved even if written in a foreign language, because one can learn the laws of a get from it. So wrote the Darchei Moshe and Mateh Moshe. But the Shelah forbids, and so it appears from the Agudah; the Bach wrote that on a weekday it is permitted.
