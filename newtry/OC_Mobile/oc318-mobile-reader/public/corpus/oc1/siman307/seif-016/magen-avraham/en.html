Because of sitting of scoffers — and the same for going to theaters and circuses [types of amusement as Avodah Zarah 18] and other sports; and I do not know who permitted them on Purim — perhaps they relied on mockery in memory of Ahasuerus; and woe today it became unrestrained for some to go to theaters; Scripture cries "Israel shall not rejoice in gladness," etc.; also arousing evil inclination; and they said whoever scoffs falls to Gehinnom, etc., and afflictions come — "now do not scoff lest your bonds be strong" [see further in Gemara].

And he transgresses because of — Tosafot and Rosh wrote: only on an image made for idolatry he transgresses; but Avodah Zarah 50 Tosafot wrote even any image forbidden except a coin image permitted, since one is used to seeing it and recreation does not apply; the world practices the first view; and it appears mere sight is permitted for all — wedding 229.

In holy tongue permitted — the language itself has holiness and one learns Torah from it; therefore a greeting letter in holy tongue permitted to read — one learns language and Torah verses are written; but bills of debt and accounts — even for Rama forbidden even in holy tongue [Peri Megadim]. And a get permitted to move though in foreign tongue, for one can learn laws of gittin. Regarding reading newspapers on Shabbat — though Shevut Yaakov is lenient, many Acharonim forbid because of business news.
