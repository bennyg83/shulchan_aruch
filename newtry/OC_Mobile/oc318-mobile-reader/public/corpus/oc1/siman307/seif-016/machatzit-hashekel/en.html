(s.k. 16) He invited, etc. — Beit Yosef wrote: what they practice attendant reading for meal from writing is forbidden; though no erasing concern when host reads, nevertheless decree lest he read, etc. — until here; and I say custom of Israel is Torah: mitzvah accounts permitted as 306 seif 6; we do not decree idle accounts — all the more here which is itself a decree against account documents permitted for mitzvah; see there seif 12: communal needs permitted.

Accounts of mitzvah, etc. — in the Gemara forbidding it deals with a meal not of mitzvah but friendship; so Sefer Turei Even.
