(Seif katan 16) If he invited, etc. The Bach wrote, etc. Although the concern that he may erase, etc. For the Gemara gives two reasons for this law cited by the Mechaber. One is that he may erase some of the names of the people whom he invited when he sees that the delicacies he prepared will not suffice for all of them because they are too numerous, and he will therefore erase some names so as not to call those people. The second reason is that he may come to read ordinary documents.
