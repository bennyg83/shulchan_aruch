(58) Secular — and not included: Yosipon, genealogy books, and chronicles of R' Yehudah HaKohen and tribe of Yehudah from which one learns moral and fear matters — therefore even written in foreign tongue permitted.
(59) Because of sitting of scoffers — and the same for going to theaters and circuses [types of amusement as Avodah Zarah 18] and other sports; and on Purim only the mockery in memory of Ahasuerus is permitted [Magen Avraham]; and woe — today some treat going to theaters unrestrained; Scripture cries "Israel shall not rejoice in gladness," etc.; also arousing evil inclination; and they said whoever scoffs falls to Gehinnom; afflictions come — "now do not scoff lest your bonds be strong" [see further in Gemara].
(60) Not of your will — what you do from your heart's intent [Rashi there].
(61) And needless to say the printers — and book sellers who trade such matters and supply buyers to read them are included; see Machaneh Chaim responsum 1 who elaborated greatly; and they said one who causes another to sin is greater than one who kills him — killing is only this world, causing sin also the world to come.
(62) To read secular talk, etc. — and passion matters arousing evil inclination — all forbid even in holy tongue; and whatever forbidden to read forbidden even to move [Taz].
(63) But in holy tongue, etc. — the language itself has holiness and one learns Torah from it; therefore a greeting letter in holy tongue permitted to read — one learns language and Torah verses are written; but bills and accounts — even for Rama forbidden in holy tongue [Peri Megadim]. A get permitted to move though in foreign tongue, for one learns laws of gittin. Regarding reading newspapers on Shabbos — though Shevut Yaakov is lenient, many Acharonim forbid because of business news.
(64) Permitted — but Agudah, Taz, Beit Yosef, and many Acharonim dispute and forbid; and so Gra's view; therefore also greeting letter no difference between foreign tongue and holy tongue.
