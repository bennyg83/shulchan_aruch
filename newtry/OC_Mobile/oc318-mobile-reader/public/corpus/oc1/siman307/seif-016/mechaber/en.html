Figures of speech and parables of secular talk and passion matters — such as the book Immanuel and likewise war books — forbidden to read them on Shabbat; and even on a weekday forbidden because of "a sitting of scoffers," and he transgresses "turn not to idols" — turn not your mind; and in passion matters there is further concern of arousing the evil inclination; and one who composed them and one who copied them — and needless to say one who prints them — cause the masses to sin.And it appears to be precise that forbidden to read secular talk and war stories is specifically if written in a foreign tongue; but in the holy tongue — permitted; and so it appears to me from the wording Tosafot wrote in the chapter Kol HaKesavim; and so they practiced to be lenient in this.
