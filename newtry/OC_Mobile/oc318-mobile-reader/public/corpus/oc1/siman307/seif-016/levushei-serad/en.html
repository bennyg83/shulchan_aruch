Magen Avraham s.k. 11 — "perhaps without this" — requires study; if so how did the Mechaber copy Tur's wording forbidding "I will do tomorrow"? Perhaps Beit Yosef distinguishes between saying "I will do" which implies he prepares himself to do, and saying "I desire to do" which is only recounting that he longs for the thing but does not yet know if he will do — in this there is no contradiction from Rosh; but Magen Avraham does not want this distinction, therefore he wrote that Rosh forbids in every case.
