Magen Avraham s.k. 3 — forbidden to give etc. Hagahot Maimoniot wrote etc. — for if he tells him to buy on Shabbat, even in all those cases it is forbidden etc. — should read thus; and meaning Hagahot Maimoniot wrote it is not comparable to all those permitted bein hashemashot, for in all of those if he does etc. — see Taz; and therefore Magen Avraham wrote if he tells him to buy on Shabbat even in all of them — meaning in that of simanim 247 and 252 it is forbidden, for there too one must conclude it is shevut.
