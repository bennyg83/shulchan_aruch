(12) From erev Shabbat, etc. — and the same: even from the start of the week forbidden since he designated for Shabbat; and as above in seif 2; and even fixing wage does not help here — only mentioned "from erev Shabbat" because of the end: if he tells him "buy for yourself" — even from erev Shabbat permitted.

(13) But he may, etc. — source of this law learned from below siman 348 seif 4 regarding chametz; see Beit Yosef and Acharonim who tend there that even if he promises to buy from him afterward — also permitted since now he buys for himself.
