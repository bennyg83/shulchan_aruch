From erev Shabbos, etc. The same applies even from the beginning of the week: it is forbidden, since he designated him for Shabbos, as above in seif 2. Even fixing a wage does not help for this. It mentioned erev Shabbos only because of the latter clause, that if he tells him to buy for himself, it is permitted even on erev Shabbos.
But he may, etc. The source of this law is learned from what appears below in siman 448, seif 4, regarding chametz. See the Bach and Eliyah Rabbah, who incline there that even if he promises to buy it back from him afterward, it is also permitted, since for now the non-Jew is buying it for himself.
