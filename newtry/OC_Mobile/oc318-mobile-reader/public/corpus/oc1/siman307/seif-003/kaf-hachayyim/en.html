20) [Seif 3] It is forbidden to give a non-Jew money on Friday to purchase for him on Shabbos. The same applies if he gives him money on another weekday to purchase for him on Shabbos. Levush Serad. Even fixing his compensation does not help, because he explicitly tells him to purchase on Shabbos, as stated at the beginning of siman 247. See below, paragraph 25.
21) There: To purchase for him on Shabbos. If a Jewish person and non-Jew are partners seeking to purchase something from the king and the sale occurs on Shabbos, the Jewish person may not instruct the non-Jew to purchase it. Beis Yehudah, siman 44; Birkei Yosef, paragraph 7.
22) There: But he may tell him, 'Purchase it for yourself,' etc. He must specifically say, 'Purchase it for yourself.' Purchasing for the Jewish person is forbidden even without giving money. Eshel Avraham, paragraph 3.
