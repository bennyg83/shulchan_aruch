On erev Shabbat. And if market day is on Shabbat and it is not available to buy except on that day — forbidden. And the same when market day is on Shabbat forbidden to give him something to sell. And Taz wrote and it appears to me that in all these where they permit (with the sun) such as giving vessels to a launderer etc. there is prohibition if he gives him the vessel close to Shabbat and tells him "see that I need them tomorrow" for this is like saying explicitly that he launder them on Shabbat and likewise regarding if he gave him money on erev Shabbat to buy and told him he should go on his way tomorrow — see below siman 252 seif 2. And in 8 he wrote one should not dwell in a city whose market day is Shabbat for it is impossible he will not sin. And if the market is not in the Jewish neighborhood it is permitted as Keneset HaGedolah.
