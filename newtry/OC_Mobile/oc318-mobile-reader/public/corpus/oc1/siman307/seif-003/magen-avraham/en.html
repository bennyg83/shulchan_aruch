It is forbidden to give. This is the wording of the Hagahos Maimoniyos: it is forbidden to give money to a non-Jew on erev Shabbos so that he should buy for him the next day, on market day. This is not comparable to what is written in siman 252, where it is permitted, nor to siman 247, for there, if the non-Jew wishes, he need not do it until the next weekday; but here, it is impossible except on Shabbos.
