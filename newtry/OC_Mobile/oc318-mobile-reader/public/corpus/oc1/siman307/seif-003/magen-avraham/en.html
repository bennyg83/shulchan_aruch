Forbidden to give. And thus Hagahot Maimoniot: forbidden to give a gentile money on erev Shabbat to buy for him tomorrow on market day; and some say for what is written in 292 it is permitted; and in 287: there if the gentile wishes he need not act until weekday, but here impossible except tomorrow on market day — until here. It appears explicitly that even if he does not tell him to buy on Shabbat it is forbidden, since impossible to buy except on Shabbat; if he tells him to buy on Shabbat — forbidden even entire week, as Hagahot Maimoniot — similar to one who waits at dusk for weekday; see erev Shabbat. And the same when market day is Shabbat — forbidden to give him something to sell; so Sefer Masat Kohen; and wrote on fourth and fifth permitted as 307 and 292 (252). Sefer Chassidim: one should not dwell in a city whose market is Shabbat, for impossible not to sin; if market is not in Jewish neighborhood — permitted.
