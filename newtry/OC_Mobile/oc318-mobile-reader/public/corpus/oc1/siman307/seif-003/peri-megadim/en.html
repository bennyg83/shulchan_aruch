That he should not. See Taz. He distinguishes between an item ordinarily available every day, in which case there is no prohibition unless one says explicitly, “Buy it on Shabbos,” or says, “See, I am leaving on a journey on Motzaei Shabbos,” and an item available only on market day. In the latter case, even an unspecified instruction is forbidden, because it is equivalent to expressly telling him to buy it on Shabbos. It is forbidden to benefit from the item on that Shabbos, as stated in siman 276, Magen Avraham seif katan 1. Even when the melachah was prohibited only D'Rabbanan, one must wait until after Shabbos before benefiting from it. All the more so here. See siman 328, seifim 6 and 7.
