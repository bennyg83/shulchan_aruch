Only — Taz: distinguish between available every day — prohibition only if he says "buy on Shabbat," or "see that I travel on motzei Shabbat"; when not available except market day — even stam forbidden as saying explicitly to buy on Shabbat; and forbidden to benefit that Shabbat, as siman 276 Magen Avraham 1 — even rabbinic prohibition he must wait until evening; all the more 325 seif 6. And what is available only on market day when market is Shabbat — forbidden entire week as saying to do on Shabbat; so challenged Acharonim; and the essence is forbidden; and clear from Magen Avraham siman 292 s.k. 8. Taz: "he should travel on motzei Shabbat" — meaning the Jew wants to leave there on motzei Shabbat; if so he commands him to buy on Shabbat. There: "even if he fixed wage" — second view in 292 seif 4; see Magen Avraham. Magen Avraham s.k. 4: deals with 245 seif 5; Beit Yosef wondered why not explain only with fixing wage like 287 in the letter and Taz here end s.k. 3; Magen Avraham wrote it was omitted — clear in Shulchan Aruch 245 that fixing is required; 292 s.k. 8.
