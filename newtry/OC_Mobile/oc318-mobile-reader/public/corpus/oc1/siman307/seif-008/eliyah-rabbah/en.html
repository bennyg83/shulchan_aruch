[8] But permitted, etc. — and Tur wrote "it appears somewhat to forbid"; and it is astonishing: why is this different from "all who extinguish do not lose" that we permit in 334 (Taz); and there is no difficulty, for there it is because of loss; and one may say there he does not speak to his face, but here it is like commanding this gentile; nevertheless for the law we do not leave Tur's "it appears" before Terumat HaDeshen, Semag, Smak, Hagahot Maimoniot who permit; and in responsum Maharam siman 559 some permit even before Shabbat to do on Shabbat; and though in 242 they rejected entirely — nevertheless here one may rely; and greater: responsum R' Yom Tov Tzahalon siman 244 permitted even informing him beforehand that telling a gentile is permitted and he understands — and permitted to tell him "you would not lose if I paid your wage."
