Produce. See Taz. Magen Avraham, seif katan 12, likewise writes that even when the produce is within the techum, it is forbidden to say that one will bring it tomorrow so long as it is muktzeh. As the Rama writes in his gloss, the reference to “his fellow” is not exclusive; the same prohibition applies when speaking to oneself. See Magen Avraham, seif katan 11. With Hashem's help this will be explained there.
