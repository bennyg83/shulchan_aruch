He may tell his fellow "to such-and-such a city I am going tomorrow"; and likewise permitted to tell him "go with me to such-and-such a city tomorrow," since today he could go by way of watchmen; and likewise all similar cases where there is a side of permission to do it today he may tell his fellow that he should do it tomorrow, provided he does not mention hire to him; but in a matter where there is no side of permission to do it today, even if there is only a rabbinic prohibition — such as he has muktzeh fruit outside the techum — since it is impossible for him to bring them today, forbidden to tell his fellow to bring them for him tomorrow; and likewise forbidden for him to wait at dusk at the end of the techum so he may hurry at night to go there to bring them.And likewise he should not say "I will do such-and-such tomorrow" (Rosh, chapter HaShoel, and see above at the start of the siman).} But he may wait at dusk at the end of the techum to hurry to go there to guard them, for even today he could guard them if they were within the techum; and if he intended only to guard them, he may even bring them.'] אבל יכול להחשיך בסוף התחום כדי למהר לילך שם לשמרם שאפי' היום היה יכול לשמרן אם הם היו בתוך התחום ואם לא כיון להחשיך אלא לשמרם יכול אף להביאם:
