(30) He may say, etc. — for it is explained at the start of the siman that what is forbidden on Shabbat is forbidden to say one will do tomorrow; and here he teaches that though forbidden to go outside techum on Shabbat — nevertheless permitted to say "to city P I am going tomorrow," since on the going itself there is no prohibition except lacking watchmen; however forbidden to say "I will ride tomorrow" or go in a carriage called in foreign tongue "faherin," since his speech is the forbidden thing itself and counsel does not help — only "I am going tomorrow."

(31) Watchmen — meaning booths of guards whereby he may walk on Shabbat even many mil if embedded within seventy amot and four handbreadths, as 398.

(32) And likewise all similar, etc. — such as telling his fellow to bring him tomorrow from outside the techum fruit already detached, upon which there is no muktzeh prohibition; though impossible today because of techum and reshut prohibitions — nevertheless permitted to say since there is a side of permission — if there were partitions he could bring them.

(33) That he will do it tomorrow — and to do it today forbidden even to tell a non-Jew; and even after the fact if he brought from outside techum forbidden to use them, as below 325 seif 8.

(34) Outside the techum muktzeh fruit — Acharonim wrote: not specifically at end of techum — likewise within techum since impossible to bring because of muktzeh; but because of the end he mentioned thus: waiting at dusk is forbidden only at end of techum, as below seif 9.

(35) To go there to bring them — meaning those muktzeh fruits; but fruit not muktzeh — since permitted to tell one's fellow to bring after Shabbat — also permitted to wait at dusk for them [Gemara].

(36) And likewise he should not say, etc. — refers to what he wrote at first forbidden to tell one's fellow to bring tomorrow; and he teaches: do not say it is forbidden only when speaking with one's fellow and consulting, since he needs his speech; but speaking to himself without need — permitted? He teaches: since it is something forbidden on Shabbat, even speaking to oneself is called weekday speech and forbidden, as at the start of the siman.

(37) That even today, etc. — meaning the guarding itself is melacha permitted on Shabbat and he waits at dusk for what is permitted; and that it is outside techum — he could have gone via watchmen if he had them, as above.

(38) Even to bring them — meaning even if attached and he must detach from erev, since his main intent in waiting was initially only for guarding.
