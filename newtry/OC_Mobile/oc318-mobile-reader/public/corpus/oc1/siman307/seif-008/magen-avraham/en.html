"To city P I am going," etc. — Ramban and Rashba explained: tell him "to there I am going tomorrow — come with me"; if not, why does he say — plain? — until here the Maggid Mishneh ch. 24; it appears if he said "this thing I will do tomorrow" even where there is no permission today — permitted; and so from Rambam: forbidden to speak with partner what he will sell or buy tomorrow, how he will build — implies only when consulting and calculating what they will do; but to say "this thing I will do" — permitted; and so Beit Yosef citing Rokeach: he cannot say "I desire to ride tomorrow" because he cannot rent on Shabbat — until here; it appears to me it deals when he tells him thus so he will prepare a horse — and I am inclined that without this it is permitted; but Rosh forbids, as Rama in the gloss; and perhaps Maggid Mishneh also holds thus — see Tosafot; if so forbidden to say "I will ride tomorrow" — only "I am going"; and when asking him, ask if he will go tomorrow.

Fruit outside the techum — but within the techum, since permitted to wait at dusk as seif 9, permitted to tell one's fellow (Beit Yosef); and to me: then permitted to tell one's fellow to detach fruit within the techum — and this is plain forbidden, as seif 7: even to tell him "it would have been right with me at dusk" forbidden — the principle is included: all that I may tell I may wait at dusk, etc. — implying they are equal; it deals at end of techum because of techum prohibition they are equal; so Rosh, Remazim, Rivan; therefore I say he mentioned outside techum because the mishnah teaches it, and "forbidden to wait at end of techum," etc.; and plain even what has permission on Shabbat forbidden to tell gentile to do on Shabbat; it deals with 325 seif 8 and seif 10: even after the fact forbidden; Beit Yosef's words here require study; see there; also what he wrote via partition of people forbidden to take from reshut ha-yachid to reshut ha-rabbim — this is not as in 362 seif 5 and Gemara — it is a superior partition; and plain in Gemara permitted to say "bring me this thing tomorrow" — if there were partitions he would bring today; also what he wrote in Tosafot's name "to one's fellow" — not so, for they wrote thus on another law, as s.k. 7.
