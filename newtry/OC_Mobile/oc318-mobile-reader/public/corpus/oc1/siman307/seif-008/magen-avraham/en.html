I am going to such-and-such a city, etc. The Ramban and Rashba explained that he says to him, ‘I am going there tomorrow; come with me,’ for otherwise what is the teaching, since it is obvious. This is the language of the Maggid Mishneh in chapter 24. It implies that if he says, ‘I will do this tomorrow,’ even regarding something that has no permitted way to do it today, it is forbidden.
Fruits outside the techum. But if they are within the techum, since it is permitted to wait near the boundary for them, as written in seif 9, it is permitted to tell his fellow, according to the Bach. This does not appear to me, for if so it would be permitted to tell his fellow to detach fruit within the techum, and this is obviously forbidden, as written in seif 7, that even saying ‘I will do it tomorrow’ is forbidden.
