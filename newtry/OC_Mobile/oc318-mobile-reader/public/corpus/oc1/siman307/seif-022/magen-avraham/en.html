To tithe bein hashemashot — Rabbenu Yehudah challenged: behold we do not tithe, as 261; if demai — permitted even without being busy; see there he wrote even definite produce permitted to tithe even not for Shabbat need if busy; for us who hold they did not decree shevut bein hashemashot in pressing time — and to me no "busy" here, for he can tithe on Sunday; therefore Tur's intent is as mishnah ch. 7 of demai: if he forgot to tithe until bein hashemashot he may call the name; so Tosafot Sukkah 23a; and it appears only tithe of produce which is rabbinic or in exile; but grain, wine, oil which are Torah — we hold no selection and calling the name does not help; see Rambam ch. 1 of Terumot halacha 21, Kesef Mishneh, Yeshuot Yaakov Bava Kama ch. 5 on laws of selection siman 13, and investigate 331 s.k. 11.

Forbidden to hint — and the same forbidden to tell him something from which he understands he should do melacha (Peskei Rakanati siman 99); if so forbidden to tell gentile to wipe his nose so he understands to remove soot on the candle; only telling him something he understands he will do after Shabbat permitted as seif 7; but when he does on Shabbat forbidden — even if gentile did melacha himself for Israelite forbidden to benefit, as 325; all the more to hint — unlike Beit Yosef; see s.k. 14 in the name of Rambam: there it is not because of commanding gentile, only that he said he cannot read it.

In his own melacha, etc. — but if grain belongs to Israelite forbidden to tell gentile to clear it; and the same for all muktzeh — shevut di-shevut; see s.k. 21.

Standing at the herd — some say unlike 244: there he already called on erev Shabbat and it is as walking into his field to see what is needed; but here cheeses are not yet Israelite's; even if gentile does not make cheeses except on Shabbat, if Israelite accepts them as debt and if he does not accept he loses debt — permitted, as 244 seif 6; all the more here (Shach Bava Batra 55).
