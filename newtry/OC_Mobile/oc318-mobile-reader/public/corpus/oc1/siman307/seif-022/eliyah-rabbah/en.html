To his fellow, etc. This is the wording of the Magen Avraham: what the Bach wrote in the name of Tosafot, that ‘to his fellow’ is not precise, is incorrect, for they wrote that regarding the law in seif 7. In my humble opinion, it is the same matter, for the reason of Tosafot there is that since thought is permitted, it makes no difference whether it is a non-Jew or a Jew; and here too it should be so. This requires study.
