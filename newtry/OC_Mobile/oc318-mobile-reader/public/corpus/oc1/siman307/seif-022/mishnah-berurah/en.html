It is forbidden to hint, etc. This too is included in instructing a non-Jew, since through his hint the non-Jew acts on Shabbos. The same applies that it is forbidden to say something to him on Shabbos from which he will understand to do melachah. Therefore it is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to wipe his nose so that he will understand to remove the coal from the top of the lamp.
For his own work, etc. But if the grain belongs to a Jew, it is forbidden to tell the non-Jew to clear it away. The same applies to any muktzeh item, for it is a double D'Rabbanan prohibition and is permitted only for the need of a mitzvah or for someone somewhat ill, as above in seif 5. Magen Avraham; and see the Taz below in siman 308.
Until after measuring and calculating, etc. Meaning, before the measuring and calculation they are not his. If a non-Jew brought his objects on Shabbos into a Jew's house, even if they are forbidden to move and he brought them for the Jew, since they are in the non-Jew's hand the Jew may tell him to move them to another corner.
He watches and buys, etc. Meaning that even though the Jew stands there while the cheeses are being made and while the milk is being milked so that he can later buy them from him, nevertheless it is permitted, for at the time of the making the non-Jew acts for his own purpose.
And with intent to sell them, etc. Even if the non-Jew makes the cheeses only on Shabbos, if the Jew receives them for his debt and would lose his debt if he did not receive them, it is permitted, for at the time of making they are still the non-Jew's.
