Any rabbinic shevut is permitted during bein hashmashot for the sake of a mitzvah, for example, to tell a non-Jew to light a lamp for him during bein hashmashot, or, if he was preoccupied and needs to tithe, to tithe during bein hashmashot. {Rama: Anything that one is forbidden to tell a non-Jew to do on Shabbos may likewise not be told to him before Shabbos if he will perform it on Shabbos, except in cases expressly permitted above. One may not tell a non-Jew on Shabbos to do after Shabbos something that may not be done on Shabbos, unless the matter can be described in a manner that does not constitute a direct instruction to perform forbidden melacha.}
