Regarding the number of lines, these are the opening words of the lines: In the hand tefillin: “And Hashem spoke”; “this day”; “you are going out”; “to give”; “the festival”; “the Torah.” In the second passage: “And it shall be”; “the womb”; “with a lamb”; “saying”; “for He hardened”; “animal”; “as a sign.” In the third passage: “Hear”; “you”; “and it shall be”; “your heart”; “and when you walk”; “your hand”; “the doorposts.” In the fourth passage: “And it shall be”; “and with all”; “your soul”; “and you shall eat”; “among you”; “which Hashem”; “between your eyes”; “your house and your gates.”
In the head tefillin: First passage: “And Hashem spoke”; “from this”; “this.” Second passage: “And it shall be”; “every firstborn”; “a donkey”; “from Egypt”; “from the house.” Third passage: “Hear”; “to your children”; and some say, “your heart”; “upon.” Fourth passage: “And it shall be”; “grass”; “you shall not give”; “your children.” This is from Rabbi Moshe Isserles.
