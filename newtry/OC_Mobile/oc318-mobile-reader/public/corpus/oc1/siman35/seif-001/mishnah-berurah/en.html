(א) They practiced — per the kabbalah in the hands of scribes from person to person (Tur).

(ב) In counting the lines — and there is such kabbalah in their hands regarding the beginning of line-heads in hand and head [tefillin] as explained in Tur and Beit Yosef; scribes today are not meticulous about line-heads and change as opportunity arises; one who wishes to be meticulous should not stretch letters or shorten greatly to align line-heads, for it is not ornamental for tefillin, but a little is permitted (Pri Megadim).

(ג) And if he changed — whether in hand or in head [tefillin]; and if for hand [tefillin] he has only short and long parchment on which he cannot write seven lines unless he writes very fine script — it seems better to change the line count; see Mordechai in Hilchot Ketanot end of Hilchot Tefillin and it was cited above siman 32 that included in this is "and I and His oneness" — to write thick script in part so they will not be erased quickly; and we truly see sensibly that in fine writings that some scribes today are accustomed to, God forbid, there are many corruptions even at the start of their writing because they were not written properly due to fineness, and in most of them many tagin of sha'atnez getz are missing by law of Gemara; and some early authorities are strict about this even after the fact; one who guards his soul should not buy such parashiyot in his tefillin unless he checks them thoroughly from the start and knows who wrote the parashiyot — for such parashiyot are very common, written by youths who did not know the laws of writing sifrei Torah at all.
