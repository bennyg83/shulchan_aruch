"The eyes" — see Beer Heitev. And so is the view of the Arizal, like the Taz, as stated above; and see in the Tur in the name of Sefer HaHeichalot.

"Their body" — Beer Heitev. And in Shulchan Atzei Shalom he wrote that while still bowing head and body one may lift one's body and heels; and also regarding leaping he wrote that it seems one should not protest them, for thus the language "to fly," etc., implies; and so in Sefer HaKavanot the language of leaping is mentioned several times, and there is somewhat for you per the secret of the matter regarding ascents of the worlds — see there. Nevertheless it is obvious one should not do so in a place where their custom is not thus, lest they come to mock and one's reward come through his loss. And see above siman 96, note 3, what he wrote in the name of Magen Avraham and Peri Chadash regarding if it happened to him to answer kedusha and Kaddish; and see in Shulchan Atzei Shalom in the name of Maamar Mordekhai: if he did not hear kedusha and already heard Kaddish, and afterward it happened to him to answer kedusha and Kaddish — it seems better that he answer kedusha in order to fulfill his obligation of answering Kaddish.

"His feet" — Beer Heitev. And they wrote in the name of the Arizal, may his memory be blessed, that one must be extremely careful to pay attention to the feet at the time of kedusha, that both should be like only one foot, etc. — see there.
