And there is — Orach Tziyon: the custom of Ashkenazim is to raise their eyes upward and Sephardim their eyes downward; and the Taz explains upward and closed. But Bach wrote that prayer is different — unlike in kedusha, where their eyes are open; and so in Midrash Tanchuma thus in Beit HaMidrash. Magen Avraham, siman 56, note 1: amen yehei shmeih raba before kedusha; and Efrayim Zalman Margolios here — it is reasonable that if he began kedusha he should finish and not interrupt for amen yehei shmeih raba; and it appears he finishes kadosh three times until kevodo (siman 66, Magen Avraham 6), and afterward says amen yehei shmeih raba if he hears it then.
