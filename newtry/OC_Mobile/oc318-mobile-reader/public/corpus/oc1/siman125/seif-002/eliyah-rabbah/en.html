(2) "With the prayer leader, Nakdishakh," etc. — the reason in Levush: behold the prayer leader says it on behalf of the entire congregation in plural language; perforce one explains thus what Beit Yosef wrote — his reason in the name of the responsum of Rosh: one does not say a matter of kedusha except with ten, and one must understand the prayer leader and answer after him — end of his words. And with this the wonder of the Taz from siman 109 — that one should say with the chazzan Nakdishakh, etc. — is resolved; and consider it. The author of Halakhot Gedolot wrote, siman 13: even if there is a minyan besides him it is forbidden — even to learn by heart — unless at the time the chazzan prolongs in the melody during the melody and not when he cuts the letters.
