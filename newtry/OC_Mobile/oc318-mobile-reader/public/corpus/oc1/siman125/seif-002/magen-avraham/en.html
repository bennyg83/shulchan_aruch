"They shake their bodies." In Midrash Tanchuma, parashat Tzav: "and with two he will fly" — from here they enacted to fly on one's legs when saying kadosh [s.v.], and not like those who leap and jump. In writings it states: at the time of kedusha one steps three steps forward and remains there until falling on the face, and says Nakdishakh and we will exalt You and kadosh kadosh kadosh in a loud voice, and the rest say quietly word for word with the prayer leader, end quote. As Bach — it should read "and so is written by [your prophet]," etc.; and our version is "as is written by [your prophet]," etc.; and we see, we see, and our version has support from the Mechilta of parashat Haazinu that there should be only [the prescribed] words before mentioning the Name, as Moshe did in parashat Haazinu, and see there in Yalkut and in Tosafos Sanhedrin.

"Good [to do this]," etc. And the Arizal would warn greatly about this.
