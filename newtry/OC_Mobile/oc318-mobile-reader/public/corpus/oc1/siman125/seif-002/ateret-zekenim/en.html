It is good to direct his feet. And those who lift up their eyes to the heights of the synagogues have a time when Israel says, "Holy, and I look to heaven." It means that it should be seen against the Shemi' when saying holy. And indeed in the word holy, one must look to the sky, but those who say holiness a second and third time should say it with a bow.
