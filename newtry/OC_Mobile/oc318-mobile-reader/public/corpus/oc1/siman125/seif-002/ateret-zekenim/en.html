It is good to direct one's feet. One should lift the eyes upward; in Sefer Haichalot it states that at the time Israel says kadosh and gazes toward heaven — it implies one should gaze toward heaven when saying kadosh. Specifically at the word kadosh one should gaze heavenward; but the kedushah said a second and third time must be said with bowing, for the baraita teaches: whence that we say Avot... and whence that we say kedushah? As it says, "Ascribe to the the Master glory due His name; bow to the the Master in holy adornment" — thus a matter of kedushah is written in it, "bow to the the Master." Likewise "Blessed is the glory of the the Master from His place" should be said with bending the head, because after "glory due His name" it is written "bow." So I found in an old anthology in Tosafos — namely when he says Blessed glory he bows and rises at the Name; and likewise when saying yimloch he bows and rises at the Name (Maharil).
