The eyes — namely they should be closed, but not with open eyes (Taz).

Their body. Maharil, when he said Baruch and yimloch, would bow and rise at the Name — see Magen Avraham. And in the Shelah he wrote: I received that one lifts one's body and heels upward in Baruch and yimloch as in kadosh. It is stated in Midrash Tanchuma, parashat Tzav: "and with two he will fly" — from here they enacted to fly on one's legs when saying kadosh; and not like those who leap and jump (Magen Avraham).

His feet — in the secret of "and their feet were a straight foot." One must have kavanah in kedushah to sanctify Heaven, may He be blessed, so that kedushah from above rests upon him; and he should intend to fulfill the mitzvah "and I shall be sanctified among the children of Israel." And the Arizal would warn greatly about this.
