(1) [Seif 1] The congregation does not say Nakdishakh with the prayer leader, etc. The Tur wrote that in Ashkenaz they say a different formula: "We will sanctify Your name in Your world, as they sanctify it in there above, and so is written," etc. However, in Shaar HaKavanot in the prayer text, folio 51 side gimel, he wrote in the name of the Arizal: in the repetition one should say Nakdishakh and Na'aritzakh as the custom of Sephardim, and not as the custom of Ashkenazim who say "We will sanctify Your name," etc.; and likewise in Musaf one should not say Na'aritzakh and Nakdishakh as the custom of Ashkenazim, but rather "They shall give a crown," etc., as the custom of Sephardim — end of his words. And Eliyah Rabbah above in siman 48 — see there; and so too in Shaar HaKavanot in drush 3 of the repetition of the Amidah; Pri Megadim, Eshel Avraham, chapter 2 of the rest of the repetition of the Amidah; Magen Avraham in tractate She'erit HaEtzilut, chapter 2, ot 2. And the Tur wrote that this formula of "We will sanctify Your name," etc., has in it 14 words as in the verse "and one called," etc.; and Bach wrote that therefore it must say "and so is written by your prophets," and not "as is written by your prophets," etc. — for then there are only 13 words; see there. And if so, according to this, in the formula Nakdishakh it must say "as is written" — for if he says "and so is written," there would be 15 words until "and one called," etc. And see Magen Avraham, seif katan 2, who wrote that even if one says in the formula Nakdesh "as is written," there is support from the Mechilta of parashat Haazinu that there should be only [the prescribed] words before mentioning the Name, as Moshe did in parashat Haazinu — see there.
