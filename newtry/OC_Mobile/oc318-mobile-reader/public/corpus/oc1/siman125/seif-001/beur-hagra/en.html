Seif 1, "the congregation does not," etc. — like Kaddish, where one cannot [say it] with the prayer leader; only the prayer leader blesses, as written in chapter 1.

"And there is," etc., "and likewise," etc. — Sefer Haichalot; and so too Rashi, as it says "and the doorposts shook," etc.; and Magen Avraham in the name of Tanchuma.

"And one should not speak in it" — see above siman 56.
