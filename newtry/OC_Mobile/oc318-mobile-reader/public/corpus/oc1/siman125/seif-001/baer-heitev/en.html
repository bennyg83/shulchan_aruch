They are silent. Also for Kaddish — do not say it with the chazzan. And even to learn by heart is forbidden, unless at the time the chazzan prolongs in the melody during the melody and not when he cuts the letters. And it is not called kedusha except "We will exalt You," etc., "in their abode," etc., "and in Your holy words," etc.; but the rest of the text they add on Shabbat is not in the category of kedusha (Yesh Noflim). And see above siman 66, seif katan 17; and the Arizal wrote that one should say all of kedusha quietly with the prayer leader — see Sefer Nagid uMitzvah, folio 97 side b. On Shabbat they say as the matter stated in kamatz (Seder HaYom siman 475). And the prayer leader should finish "in their abode," etc., "and in Your holy words," etc., before the congregation begins Baruch and yimloch (Seder HaYom siman 476).

Kadosh. One who said kadosh kadosh kadosh while the prayer leader was still at "one called to the other" — has fulfilled. Leket Yosher, part 2, siman 163.
