"The congregation does not say [Nakdishakh with the prayer leader]," etc. — In Beit Yosef, in the name of the responsum of the Rosh, he wrote the reason: for kedusha is impossible except with ten, and so for Kaddish. And Beit Yosef wrote: and it appears one should do thus when the chazzan says "in their abode they praise," and they are silent until he reaches "in the house of My holiness," and then they answer; and so when he says "and in Your holy words" they are silent until he reaches yimloch. And it requires investigation — from what is written in siman 109, that if he began he says with the prayer leader word for word "We will exalt You" and "We will sanctify You," and therefore one might say that what he says with the chazzan is not called an individual in this. And it seems to me there is no difference whether he already prayed or not; and all the more in what Beit Yosef added regarding "in their abode they praise," etc. — I do not know harm in this. And so too Rama, siman 29: the common custom is that even an individual says the order of kedusha in Yotzer; and so "and in Your holy words" is written to say — what prohibition is there for an individual to say this thing that recounts what is written in the verse?
