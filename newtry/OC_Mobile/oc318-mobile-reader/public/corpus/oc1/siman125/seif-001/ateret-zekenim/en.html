The congregation does not say Nakdishakh with the prayer leader. The custom has spread that the congregation remains silent when the chazzan says Nakdesh or Na'aritzakh until he reaches Kadosh; then the congregation answers Kedusha. Similarly, when the chazzan says "in their abode they praise," the congregation is silent until he reaches "Blessed is the glory of the the Master from His place," and the congregation answers it. Similarly, when the prayer leader says "and in Your holy words," they are silent until he reaches "He shall reign," etc., and then the congregation answers (Beit Yosef and Rosh in responsa, klal 7 siman 19). In "We will sanctify Your name" there are 14 words until the beginning of the verse "and one called to the other," etc.; therefore it must say "and so is written by your prophets" — "and one called to the other and said" — and not in our formula that we say "as is written," for then there are only 13 words.
