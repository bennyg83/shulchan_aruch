In Magen Avraham at the end of seif katan 2; and in Tosafos Sanhedrin. N.B. — see there, folio 37 side b, s.v. michanaf; and in Yalkut Haazinu it states that Moshe Rabbeinu, peace upon him, said: it is enough for me to be one of the seven ministering angels who mention the Name after three words — and seven times three equals twenty-one. One must sharpen: why did he say specifically seven times? Rather Tosafos in Sanhedrin there wrote that each day they praise with one of the six wings, and on Shabbat Israel praises; therefore it is seven times three — and it is simple.
