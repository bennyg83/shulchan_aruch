"There is no [congregation saying with the prayer leader]" — Orach Tziyon in siman 109, seif 2; nevertheless there he agreed to Rabbi Bunim in the gloss that lekhatchilah one should not begin until after amen of the kadosh that one answers amen; and Magen Avraham there, to his view in note 9, that it is forbidden lekhatchilah to say kedusha with the prayer leader, and bedi'eved if he began there is no choice because of amen — see there. And see Levush the reason: how can they all say Nakdishakh, which implies they are saying to others — are not all also saying? Do not say when an individual began to pray he says with the prayer leader where there are many without him; but nevertheless, certainly without having prayed with the prayer leader it is obvious that even if there is a minyan without him he should not answer, for each one would do so — and Magen Avraham and Rabbi Akiva Eiger. And what he wrote from siman 59 regarding "in their abode" is also a [separate] matter.
