(1) "The congregation does not," etc. — Nakdishakh, etc., and likewise "in their abode," etc., "and in Your holy words," etc. So wrote Nachalat Tzvi; and in Beit Yosef it appears he is uncertain, and therefore he did not write it in Shulchan Aruch. And likewise the Taz ruled that they should say it; and likewise in Sefer Chasidim siman 499 it is implied that the congregation says it quietly; and see in Sefer Chasidim that it requires study to understand — and according to this one can explain; and consider it. Further, Sefer Chasidim wrote that it is forbidden for individuals to say Baruch kavod before the prayer leader finishes the word "and they said"; and likewise for yimloch it is forbidden before the prayer leader finishes saying it. Abudraham wrote: "We will sanctify Your name in Your world" and the correction in the lower [world] — "in their abode" — explanation: all four directions praise the Holy One, blessed be He, in whichever direction one wishes, for the glory fills all directions and they need not turn their faces, since they have faces toward every side.
