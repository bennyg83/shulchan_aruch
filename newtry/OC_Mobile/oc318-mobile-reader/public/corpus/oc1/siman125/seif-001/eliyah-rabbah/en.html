[1] The congregation should not, etc. The phrases Nekadishecha, etc., and likewise Le’umasam, in the words, etc., should be recited. Nachalas Tzvi writes this. Beit Yosef appears uncertain and therefore did not record it in Shulchan Aruch, while Taz rules that they should be said. Sefer Chassidim 809 also implies that the congregation should say them quietly. The passage in Sefer Chassidim requires study, and it may be explained accordingly. Sefer Chassidim further writes that individuals may not say Baruch Kevod before the chazzan finishes the word Ve’amru, and likewise may not say Yimloch before the chazzan finishes saying Leimor. Abudraham writes: “May Your name be sanctified in Your world, and the arrangement below corresponds to them.” This means that all four directions praise the Holy One, blessed is He, facing whichever direction they wish, because His glory fills every direction and they need not turn, since they have faces on every side.
