Nevertheless, when a mitzvah needed during Chol HaMoed is involved, one may instruct a non-Jew to perform it, since some permit this even on Shabbos. See siman 307, se'if 5. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur; Chayei Adam, rule 115, subsection 3; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 56, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1. This means that any skilled work which a Jew may not perform even when needed for the festival, as explained in the preceding simanim, may be performed through a non-Jew when needed for a mitzvah during Chol HaMoed. See Eshel Avraham, subsection 1, who gives a different explanation. The unqualified words of the aforementioned halachic authorities imply what we have written.
