Magen Avraham note 1 — siman 307 seif 5, for it implies telling a Gentile regarding rabbinic matters in place of a mitzvah, and likewise in melacha on Chol Hamoed, which is d'rabbanan; and likewise Beit Yosef siman 532 regarding cutting nails for a niddah, for even one who forbids permits through a Gentile since it is a mitzvah.
