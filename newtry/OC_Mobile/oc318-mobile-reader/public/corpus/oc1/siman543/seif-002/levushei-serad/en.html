Magen Avraham s.k. 1. See siman 307, se'if 5, for they permit telling a non-Jew regarding a shevus in a situation of mitzvah; and the same applies to melacha of Chol HaMoed, which is rabbinic; and so wrote Beis Yosef in siman 532 regarding cutting nails for a niddah: that even one who forbids [it] permits through a non-Jew, since it is a mitzvah.
