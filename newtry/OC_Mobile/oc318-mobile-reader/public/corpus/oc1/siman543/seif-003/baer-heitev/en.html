After. Meaning that he should tell him explicitly that he should do it after the moed. And the same law applies that it is permitted to give [it] to a Jew. Magen Avraham.
And he should count. And if so, when he gives his garments to a launderer, it is forbidden to count [them] (Magen Avraham). And see in Baer Heitev earlier, who left it as requiring further study, from what is written in siman 551 at the end of se'if 3: that the Rama there forbids giving to a launderer until after Tisha B'Av, and Magen Avraham wrote even if he says to launder after Tisha B'Av, end quote; [but] he was not precise, for there [the reason is] so that they not divert their minds from mourning.
It is permitted. Meaning he need not protest against him; see siman 248.
