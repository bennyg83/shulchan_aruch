That he do it. Meaning that he should tell him explicitly that he should do it after the moed; see Beis Yosef; and the same law applies that it is permitted to give [it] to a Jew.
And he should count. And if so, when he gives his garments to a launderer, it is forbidden to count [them].
It is permitted. Meaning he need not protest against him; see the beginning of siman 248.
