1) [Se'if 1] Anything one may not perform, one may not instruct, etc. Regarding this matter, our Sages treated Chol HaMoed like Shabbos and Yom Tov.
2) There. It is forbidden to instruct a non-Jew to perform it. Nevertheless, when a mitzvah needed during Chol HaMoed is involved, one may instruct a non-Jew to perform it, since some permit this even on Shabbos. See siman 307, se'if 5. Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Yad Aharon, Hagahos HaTur; Chayei Adam, rule 115, subsection 3; Mikraei Kodesh, siman 56, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1. This means that any skilled work which a Jew may not perform even when needed for the festival, as explained in the preceding simanim, may be performed through a non-Jew when needed for a mitzvah during Chol HaMoed. See Eshel Avraham, subsection 1, who gives a different explanation. The unqualified words of the aforementioned halachic authorities imply what we have written.
3) There. It is forbidden to instruct a non-Jew, etc. This applies even to an activity that is not a melachah, such as commerce. Since the Jew may not perform it, he may not instruct a non-Jew to perform it. Bach; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1; Mikraei Kodesh there, Likutei Rima, subsection 1. See above, at the beginning of siman 539, regarding which commerce is forbidden. See there.
