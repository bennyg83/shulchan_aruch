s.k. 1: Any matter, etc. And in Beis Yosef, siman 532. That is to say, from there there is some proof: for he brought in the name of Hagahos Asheri that a woman whose immersion occurred on Chol HaMoed is forbidden to cut her nails with a utensil; rather she should tell a non-Jew to cut her nails. And he concluded: even though telling a non-Jew is a shevus, in a situation of mitzvah they did not decree, end quote. And granted that we hold that she herself is permitted to cut them; and also according to Beis Yosef it is permitted to cut nails even not in a situation of mitzvah. Nevertheless, from the words of Hagahos Asheri, who forbids cutting even in a situation of mitzvah and even so permitted through a non-Jew, one may learn for our case [to permit] another melacha in a situation of mitzvah.
