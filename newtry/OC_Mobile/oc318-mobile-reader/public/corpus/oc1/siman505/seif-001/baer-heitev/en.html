(ד) Second day. And all the more so if it was milked on the Shabbat before Yom Tov — it is permitted on the second day in exile; the agreement of the later authorities, and so Magen Avraham; see there that he elaborated and raised that one should not deviate from Rama's ruling; therefore for an animal standing for slaughter and one milks into food — even a Jew is permitted to milk his animal; and if it is not standing for slaughter and he milks into food — it depends on the dispute written in siman 495 seif 4 whether there is muktzeh on Yom Tov; and for a gentile's animals, even if not standing for slaughter, it is permitted to milk for food; but to milk into an empty pot is forbidden in every case. And if a gentile milked into an empty pot — it is forbidden for him that day; and if it was milked on Shabbat — even for food and an animal standing for slaughter, as written siman 305 seif (2) [20] — it is forbidden on the first Yom Tov after it, but on the second day it is permitted. And a gentile who is suspected is believed in this matter, as written siman 573 seif 6 — end of Magen Avraham. And see siman 329 seif 33 whether it is permitted to suckle from an animal due to pain; and above siman 494 as mentioned.
