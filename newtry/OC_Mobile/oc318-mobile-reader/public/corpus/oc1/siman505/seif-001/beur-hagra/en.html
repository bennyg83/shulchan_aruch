(א) Seif 1, that it stands for eating. As with a hen at the beginning of Yom Tov 2a, and as written in end of chapter 5 of Shabbat regarding milking it, etc., and a hen, etc. — if not so it would be like selecting food from refuse. Ran and Shiltei Gibborim; it is surprising, for on Yom Tov it is permitted and even on Shabbat in such a case permitted; but Tosafot there already explained it is separating and because of muktzeh, not so when it stands for eating — food from food.

(ב) And such as when milk comes, etc., but, etc. But, etc. As written there because liquid that goes to food is considered like food, not so in such a case that does not need food; and Rashi there 144b s.v. into the pot, etc., and so Samuel there: a person milks a goat, etc., not its udder; and Rabeinu Chananel said squeezing a cluster only, for they said there R' Yirmiyah: like the dispute of the one who smooths, etc. Rabeinu Peretz: all agree, etc.; our books' reading and Rashi and Tosafot: all agree, etc., it is not food and this is not halachah — Tosafot strained themselves there s.v. all agree; but Geonim' reading and Rashba there and Acharonim agreed: all agree, etc., considered like food — it would seem R' Yirmiyah that squeezing grapes is nolad in bread, and Rabeinu Peretz holds there is no nolad in bread because fire burns it and it does not reach food; therefore it would be fit to permit, only they dispute whether liquid going to loss is machshir or not; likewise liquid standing to rinse is not like food and would be fit to permit, only they dispute whether it is machshir or not; therefore a small amount of liquid going to food, since it mixes in food, is not like food — see Rivash siman 121 — see there.

(ג) And an am ha'aretz who milks an animal. Standing for slaughter, or an am ha'aretz's animal that does not require preparation, as written in siman 498 seif 3. From the words of Hagahot Maimoniyot end of chapter 1 of Beitzah 40 — forbidden because it is nolad, since he milked into a bowl, as written at the beginning of Beitzah; and milk, etc.; and nevertheless into the bowl, etc.; even though in an am ha'aretz's case there is no concern for preparation, therefore with nolad there is no distinction, as written later siman 573 seif 7 and siman 517 seif 1. But Rashba permits — muktzeh does not apply, for behold even a Jew can milk by way of a pot, as with an egg permitted from a chicken standing for slaughter if not for preparation that from yesterday she finished for him; not so here, since a Jew too can milk into the pot it is prepared; and even though it stands for its milking — that is not so in an am ha'aretz's case as above, and one should not forbid lest he say to milk, for behold it is permitted to say to milk due to animal pain; Magen Avraham; and therefore he said there in Hagahot Maimoniyot: and if an am ha'aretz milked, etc. — permitted to mix like an egg; and so here, however, etc.; as written in chapter 3 of Eruvin and chapter 1 of Yoma; and even though Rashi and Tosafot disagree in chapter 3 of Yoma and chapter 1 in siman 515 like Tosafot who forbid — Tosafot wrote there lest he say; not so here that one cannot compare except to what falls by itself, as written seif 2. The words of Hagahot Maimoniyot require further study that he considered it nolad; dates and pomegranates prove it is forbidden to squeeze them, and if they came out by themselves they are permitted where there is no concern lest he squeeze, such as those standing for eating; and likewise olives and grapes crushed from erev Yom Tov; and the reason forbidding milking into a bowl is not nolad but mafrek; and likewise squeezing forbidden on Yom Tov and Shabbat, as Rashi wrote there and explained; and proof from what is written at the beginning of Yom Tov chapter Liquids that they flowed — what is the reason, etc.; and if it were so, it would not be needed, for behold it is forbidden due to nolad; see Tosafot there s.v. gezeirah; and one could wonder, etc.; therefore it may be said, etc. Also because the am ha'aretz did melacha on Yom Tov — forbidden even in rabbinic melacha like beyond the techum — nevertheless here it is permitted l'chatchila to tell him to milk.
