[1] [Levush] for it is muktzeh, etc. Meaning: [the milk] is nolad from muktzeh, as the Hagahos Ashri and Avodas HaKodesh wrote; and if so, even according to those who say in siman 495 that muktzeh is permitted, this is forbidden, for it is like nolad; and not like the Magen Avraham. And the view of Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah and the Taz is that the Rashba permits even [when the animal is] designated for milking; and the book Avodas HaKodesh escaped his notice. And specifically when they are particular not to eat the milk as it is, but leave it to collect a lot to make cheeses (Taz).
