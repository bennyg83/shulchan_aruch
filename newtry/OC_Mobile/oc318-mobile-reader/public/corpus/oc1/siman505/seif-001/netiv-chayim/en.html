(Magen Avraham note 1) And for one who permits muktzeh, all is permitted. It appears to me: the rabbis challenged this from Maharsha's reasoning, for nevertheless he removes from his mind and it is nolad from muktzeh; in my humble opinion Maharsha wrote this per an initial thought but in conclusion he does not hold this view — rather for one who permits muktzeh, even if the hen stands to lay eggs, nevertheless it did not leave the category of food — examine well.
