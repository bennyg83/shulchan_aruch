For eating. See Baer Heitev; and see in Chut HaShani, siman 31, who wrote that two things are required: designated for eating, and specifically into a pot; and our milking, which is into a bowl, is forbidden on the first day of Yom Tov as on Shabbos, and like the ruling of the Rama as a matter of law and not as a mere stringency; see there. And see in Responsa Mahari Halevi, brother of the Taz, regarding milk that was milked by a non-Jew with a Jew watching, on the first day of Yom Tov.
