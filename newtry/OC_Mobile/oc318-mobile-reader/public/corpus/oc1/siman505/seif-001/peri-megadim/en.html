Standing. Taz in Shach 64 brings Rif; and in 67 he brought Rashba's view that it is forbidden (to squeeze) a cluster of grapes into a pot, see there; and in Beit Yosef and here Rif ruled anonymously that on Yom Tov a cluster into a pot and a goat standing for slaughter into a pot are permitted; and if so, likewise on Shabbat a cluster into a pot is permitted and a goat is forbidden, for on Shabbat a goat is forbidden, etc. And see in Pri Megadim there in Taz 6, see there. And what he wrote to raise eggs — some say chicks like those without a male, only Spanish, etc.; nevertheless all that gather many together to sell — that is muktzeh; and likewise a goat for milking — all who milk and eat the milk immediately — we are lenient rabbinically and it is not muktzeh; Magen Avraham 1; and see there it will be explained further.
