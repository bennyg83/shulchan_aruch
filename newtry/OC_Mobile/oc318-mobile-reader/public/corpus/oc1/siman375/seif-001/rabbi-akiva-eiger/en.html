Even though for this one pouring and for that one throwing — height of ten [tefachim], and through pouring nevertheless Rav's law is both are forbidden; and in Netiv Chayyim they strain on this not for need. Nevertheless it is difficult to me in that [sugya] in Eruvin ch. 4 — it appears for the courtyard, etc., and why is it permission of both of them? For to the courtyard it is only through throwing and to the balcony throwing of distance and pouring — therefore they give to the courtyard even though Tosafos wrote that for the conclusion according to Rav there is neither through throwing nor pouring nor through pouring are both equal — meaning according to Rav we do not follow ease more, for whatever is difficult for both they are equal. But according to Shmuel we follow ease more — therefore for this one pouring and for that one throwing they give to this one pouring; so too for the courtyard more at ease, for for this one throwing and for that one throwing and pouring; and from Rashi's language that throwing of distance is not so difficult as throwing of height — therefore throwing of distance with pouring is equal to throwing of height alone; but here in Tosafos s.v. pit it is explained that on the contrary throwing of distance is more difficult and afterward they pushed off that they are equal; but nevertheless height is not difficult from distance — if so it is difficult.
