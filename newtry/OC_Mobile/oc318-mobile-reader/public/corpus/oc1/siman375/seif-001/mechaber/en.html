What things present in a courtyard do not restrict. Containing 4 sections A balcony that serves as a
passageway to upper stories opening onto it, standing in the courtyard, accessed by a ladder, with the upper story residents descending from it to the courtyard and passing through to the public domain — they do not restrict the courtyard residents, since a ladder has the status of an opening, making it like two courtyards with an opening between them, where if they wish they may make a joint eruv and if they wish each may make their own eruv — provided all the balcony residents make their own eruv among themselves so that the permitted foot remains in its place.
