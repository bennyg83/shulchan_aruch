1) [Se'if 1] A gallery. It is long, and many entrances of upper stories open onto it. Rashi, cited by Beis Yosef. Rabbeinu Yerucham describes a gallery as follows: Houses surround a courtyard, with upper stories above the houses. A gallery runs in front of the entrances to the upper stories, and all the upper stories open onto the gallery just as the houses open onto the courtyard. A ladder in the courtyard leads up to the gallery. All those who live in the upper stories descend by that ladder into the courtyard and leave through the courtyard into the public domain. Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 2.
