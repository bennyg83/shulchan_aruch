3) There. They do not prohibit, etc. This discusses a gallery ten handbreadths high. If it is not ten handbreadths high, everything is considered one courtyard, and the gallery residents prohibit carrying for the courtyard residents. Rashi and Tosafos explain the Mishnah this way, and the ruling is obvious. Bach; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 1.
