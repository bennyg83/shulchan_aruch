They are not high. And also for the balcony they are not ten [tefachim] high, for both of them are at ease; and Beit Yosef wrote that even though it is four [tefachim] from the balcony both are forbidden, for the members of the balcony can use it through throwing; but in the Tur and poskim it is implied that any less than ten is nothing and they give it to the members of the courtyard if it is four [tefachim] from the balcony or ten [tefachim] lower than it, for a person does not forbid his fellow through airspace, as is written siman 376 seif 2; and it is possible that Rambam's view is also thus, only his way is to be concise; and see siman 372 that if one through pouring and one through throwing — height of ten [tefachim], and through pouring both are forbidden, and so in the gemara and in the gloss; and requires further study that the Rosh in ch. 4 s.v. did not write thus: a pit in a courtyard — if it was full of tevel fruits that are forbidden to move on Shabbat, behold it and its embankment are like a rock and count for the balcony as was said; but full of water — forbidden lest he fill from it and lack ten [tefachim] (Rambam) (and see siman 372 seif 16):
