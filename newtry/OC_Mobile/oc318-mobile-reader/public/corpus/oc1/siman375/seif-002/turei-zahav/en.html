If they did not join together, etc. The rule of this seif is that whenever for this one usage is at ease and for that one difficult, it is not called only permission for the one whose usage is at ease and the other cannot protest against him at all; but if both are equal at ease — such as both using through the opening — difficulty applies to both and they forbid each other; but there is a dispute in the gemara what is called at ease and what difficult — meaning whatever requires throwing, meaning that place is ten tefachim higher than him and he must throw there, is called difficult; and so pouring — meaning that place is nineteen tefachim lower than him and he must pour there; and if less than nineteen tefachim both in throwing and pouring it is not called difficult; and also called difficult even if less than ten tefachim, such as if very far in continuing distance from him four tefachim; but if one through throwing and one through pouring — Shmuel holds that even though both are difficult, nevertheless pouring is not as difficult as throwing; therefore it belongs to the owner of pouring who is at ease; and Rav holds that ease is called only for one using through an opening; but here we are not concerned for ease at all and pouring and throwing are equal and forbidden, and so is ruled; and in the Tur it is written: and if it is not ten tefachim high for the members of the courtyard and from it to the balcony four — there must be a yud in place of a lamed, and so emended Maharshal z"l, and it is straightforward and there is no need for what Beit Yosef strained to explain; and what is written here "and if they were distant from the balcony four tefachim or more" even though ten tefachim high from the courtyard into ten for the balcony — meaning that each has for the courtyard owner companionship through throwing and for the balcony owner through distance of four tefachim, and for height there is no companionship through pouring since it is within his ten — and therefore forbidden since both control it; and if there were two companionships for the balcony owner — meaning above ten and distant — it appears the courtyard owner is permitted in the mound and the balcony owner cannot forbid him because a person does not forbid through air, and it will be explained elsewhere in siman 376 seif 3. And in the Tur it is written: but all whose usage is more at ease than his fellow, they give it to the one who is at ease, etc. — end of his words; and Beit Yosef wondered, for behold this language is Shmuel's words regarding for this one pouring and for that one throwing, and we rule like R' Papa as was written; and it appears since the Tur already wrote the law of pouring and throwing that it is forbidden, perforce one explains this phrase on the matter that for this one there is companionship and for that one there is not — all this is the Tur's method; but Rambam does not hold thus, and it is clear from his language that it is the language of Shulchan Aruch in this seif aside from Rama's glosses — for at the outset where they are not ten tefachim high it is forbidden for both even if distant four tefachim from the balcony, since he did not mention at the outset the distinction of distant or near; and so too Beit Yosef and Machatzit HaShekel; and he also did not mention there the matter of height of the balcony from the mound at all — all these matters according to his view that the balcony has more relevance to the mound than the courtyard has to the mound; therefore whenever there is elevation for both — for the courtyard within ten and for the balcony near, less than four tefachim — straightforward they forbid each other; and even if the courtyard has elevation and the balcony companionship, and so if both have companionship they also forbid each other; but if the balcony has elevation and the courtyard companionship — then there is permission for the balcony, meaning at height of ten and they are not distant four tefachim; and Rambam did not mention at all the matter of the mound lower than the balcony ten or more; and Rama brought into Rambam's words from the Tur's method — namely within ten for the balcony in a pit that is ten tefachim high; and if so also at the outset where they are not ten tefachim high one must emend also lower than the balcony less than ten — then only equal for both;
