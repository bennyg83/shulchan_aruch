A person may wash, etc. This applies even when there is no pressing circumstance. Some say that it applies only in a pressing circumstance, or when water is not available within a distance of four mil. See Responsa Maharshal, siman 94. However, if he washed his hands for the purpose of eating, a stipulation is ineffective, and he may not interrupt between washing and the meal. Rema MiPano writes that even for another eating occasion, a stipulation made at the time of this meal is ineffective. This requires study, for what is his source?
