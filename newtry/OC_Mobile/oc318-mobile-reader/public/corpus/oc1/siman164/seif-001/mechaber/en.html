That one may make a condition regarding handwashing. 2 sections: A person may wash his hands in the morning and stipulate that the washing covers him for the entire day, even not in a time of pressing need, provided he does not have a lapse of attention regarding them (Tur). If water is readily available it is good to wash again, but he should not recite a blessing. {Rama: (and does not soil them) Rema: The stipulation is only effective for washing that is not needed for eating, similar to the morning handwashing; but if one is washing for the purpose of eating, a stipulation does not help for that washing. (Beit Yosef in the name of Ri)}
