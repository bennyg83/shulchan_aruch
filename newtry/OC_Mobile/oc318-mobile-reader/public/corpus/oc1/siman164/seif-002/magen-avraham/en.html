And he remembered that he touched. The same law applies if he diverted his attention from guarding his hands; he must wash, as stated in siman 170, seif 1, and at the beginning of siman 475. Rashba writes similarly in Toras HaBayis. If he touched while the piece of bread was still in his mouth, it appears to me that he may not swallow until he washes his hands. See siman 163, seif 2.
And he should recite the blessing over washing the hands. Maharshal, in Chullin, chapter 8, siman 41, writes that he should not recite a blessing unless he went away and was absent for a substantial interval. See there, and see siman 170.
