2) There: And he stipulates concerning them, etc. Sefer Damesek Eliezer, folio 325c, writes that this stipulation does not mean a verbal statement, but that he takes care not to render his hands impure or dirty. Shiyurei Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot HaTur, paragraph 1, cites him and writes that this does not appear from the Tur's words in the name of Rabbeinu Peretz, the Rashba, and the Mordechai; see there. Olas Tamid, paragraph 1, similarly writes that one verbally stipulates that this washing will serve for the entire day and that even when he later wishes to eat, he will not wash again. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 1, however, inclines to say that thought suffices. Peri Megadim, Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 5, writes that this depends upon the dispute: according to the Rashba, thought suffices, while according to the Ra'ah, one must specifically verbalize the stipulation; see there.
