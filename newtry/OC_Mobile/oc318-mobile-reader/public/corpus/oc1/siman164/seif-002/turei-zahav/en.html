And he shall bless on bread. In Beit Yosef he brings thus in the name of responsa Ramban, and wrote that for this reason we wash and bless on Passover night on the first dipping, and return and wash and bless on the second dipping, etc.; therefore it is astounding to me: since we do not bless on Passover night on netilah, as below siman 473, why did he rule here in Shulchan Aruch to bless? And it is further difficult: if you say he requires a berachah because of touching covered places, all the more so he should require one if he goes to relieve himself during the meal—and this is not so, for he ruled in siman 7 that one does not bless on bread even if he wishes to pray. Again I saw in Rashal that he wrote ch. 25 that one should not bless on this netilah, for we did not find that they enacted a berachah in the middle of a meal, etc.; see there where he elaborated. And as above, this is the main point; and it is clear from Rama's words at the end of siman 178, who wrote that relieving oneself is not an interruption in the meal, and he did not mention that one should bless on hand washing.
