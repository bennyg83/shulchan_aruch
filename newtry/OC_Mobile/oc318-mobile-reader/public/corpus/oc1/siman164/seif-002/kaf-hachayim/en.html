(2) There [one conditions upon them, etc.]. He wrote in Sefer Damascus Eliezer, page 310a, that this condition does not mean in his mouth and on his lips, but rather that he should be careful not to contaminate them and not to soil them. And Keneset HaGedolah brought this in Hagahot Taz note (1) and wrote on it that it does not appear so from the words of the Tur in the name of Rif and Rashba and Mordechai; see there. And similarly Avnei Nezer, note (1), that he conditions verbally that this netilah shall be for the whole day, and also that when he wishes to eat he shall not return and wash again; end of his words. However, see in Rabbi Akiva Eiger, note (1), that he sides to say that thought suffices; and Mishnah Berurah, note (5), wrote that this depends on the dispute—for Rashba, thought suffices; for Ramban, it must be specifically verbally; see there.
