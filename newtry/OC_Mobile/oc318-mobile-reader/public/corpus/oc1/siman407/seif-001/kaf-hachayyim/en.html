1) [Se'if 1] And the like. This includes the cases stated in se'if 3, as well as one who goes out to testify regarding the new moon when the month was sanctified on the basis of sighting. Gemara.
2) There. In every direction from that place, etc. If these two thousand amos overlap the techum from which he departed, he may return home and then has two thousand amos from his home in every direction, as stated below in se'if 2. See there.
3) There. In every direction from that place, etc. This applies although he has already performed that mitzvah. The Sages instituted this allowance because if he were not permitted to move from his location, he might refrain from going out again in the future, and that mitzvah would be neglected. This is implied by Rosh Hashanah 23b and by the following se'if.
