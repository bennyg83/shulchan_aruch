It is forbidden to begin eating half an hour near the time of evening Keriat Shema; and if he began eating after its time arrived he interrupts and reads Keriat Shema without its blessings and finishes his meal and afterward reads it with its blessings and prays:But he need not interrupt for tefillah since he began eating; but if he did not begin eating — even though he washed hands — he must interrupt (Ran first chapter of Shabbos); and) if there is no time to pray he interrupts even for tefillah (Raavad in Hagahot chapter 3 of Hilchot Berachot):.
