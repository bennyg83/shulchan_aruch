And even though he eats etc. — In this he wanted to resolve one difficulty of Tur — for obligatory Keriat Shema must be before eating:

As Rashi wrote — meaning Rashi wrote at the beginning of tractate Berachos to settle why we read Keriat Shema before tzeit hakochavim — because we rely on what he reads Keriat Shema on his bed; and Keriat Shema they read in the synagogue is only to stand in tefillah from words of Torah — until here Magen Avraham; nevertheless it is difficult for Rashi — since he fulfills only with Keriat Shema on his bed how is he permitted to eat before; and like Tur's difficulty — except necessarily Rashi holds that in such a case it is permitted since anyway he read in the synagogue and fulfills with what he reads on his bed; however below siman 267 Magen Avraham wrote: and nevertheless those accustomed to pray Maariv at tzeit hakochavim while still day — when half an hour near the time of Keriat Shema arrives they are forbidden to begin eating for they do not fulfill at all with the Keriat Shema of the day — end quote; and this is the opposite of what he wrote here — necessarily in siman 267 it refers to the opinion of Rabbeinu Yehuda who permits eating before reading Keriat Shema at the time of tzeit hakochavim since he already read at least while still day; and he brought proof from Rav; and Magen Avraham attacks him that his proof is not proof; and therefore he wrote that if they are accustomed to pray at tzeit hakochavim Maariv while still day they are forbidden to eat half an hour before tzeit hakochavim — and this is per the custom of the place of Rav Beit Yosef — that on weekdays they read at the time of tzeit hakochavim for we hold like the Sages as appears from what is written in siman 233 seif 61; and so Magen Avraham inferred the language those accustomed to pray Maariv at tzeit hakochavim while still day etc. — implies on weekdays they pray after tzeit hakochavim and therefore are forbidden to eat half an hour before tzeit hakochavim for they do not fulfill at all with what they read by day since they hold like the Sages; and here he wrote according to what appears from Rama's words siman 233 that also on weekdays they read while still day; and because we hold like R' Yehuda (even though Magen Avraham there note 6 wrote those accustomed to pray Minchah and Maariv consecutively must hold like the Sages — at minimum they hold like other tannaim that one fulfills Keriat Shema before tzeit hakochavim as written there) — only Magen Avraham wrote one should be stringent to read on his bed or after tzeit hakochavim; and since for us it is only a stringency for we hold like R' Yehuda therefore it is permitted to eat before the reading. And according to this — per the ancient custom now that they pray Maariv on weekdays after tzeit hakochavim and on erev Shabbos pray with the congregation by day — we hold as Magen Avraham wrote siman 267 that half an hour before tzeit hakochavim they are forbidden to begin eating.
