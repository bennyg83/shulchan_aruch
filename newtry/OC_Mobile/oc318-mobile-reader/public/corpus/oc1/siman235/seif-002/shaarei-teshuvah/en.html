Without blessings — Baer Heitev; and see in Sha'agat Aryeh siman 3 who expanded and concluded that a God-fearing person should be careful to read all three paragraphs of Keriat Shema after the emergence of the stars, even though he already read in the synagogue from the midpoint of Minchah and upward. And they wrote in the name of the Ari z"l that he was careful not to pray Minchah except with sunset; and even if it was Erev Shabbat nevertheless he did not omit to say it aloud but would say it quietly and afterward Erev Shabbat aloud; and further they wrote in his name regarding the three watches, etc., that one should pray all three prayers at the time when he is closer to day than he is close to night, for all three prayers must be in the day; and Keriat Shema he reads after the emergence of the stars; and Shlomei Tzibbur wrote that thus it is explicit in Zohar Chadash parashat Bereishit and in other places in the Zohar; and if so how did he do two leniencies that contradict each other — it must be said that he relied on Rabbenu Tam that they were lenient in prayer and he read Keriat Shema afterward; and those accustomed like the custom of the Rav z"l to pray Ma'ariv while it is still day — nevertheless on Motzei Shabbat they delay Ma'ariv to add from the weekday onto the holy with Erev Shabbat.
