And if — Taz; in Teshuvot HaRashba siman 1 — three hours in 233 in the Levush: seasonal hours and they calculate from sunrise until the beginning of sunset; one could ask somewhat; and from Magen Avraham there — three; and Keriat Shema with its blessings is not a blessing in vain ab initio. And what he challenged on the Mechaber — behold the Mechaber's view implies that the essence of its time is specifically from the emergence of the stars and he reads in the synagogue with the blessings and does not intend to fulfill with it; and at the emergence of the stars he intends to fulfill like the view of R' Yitzchak literally; and so too in the Levush; and blessings their essence was not enacted for Keriat Shema since they do not recite 'Who has commanded us' for Keriat Shema; nevertheless I do not know this intention — what does it detract and it does not harm at all; and let him read if its time is conditional as they say its time is before the emergence of the stars — good, and let him read afterward in its time — and there is no two permanent offerings here and the like. And what he wrote to rely on Keriat Shema on his bed — one could say l'chatchila it is fit to read before eating even though he is not liable death since he read in the synagogue as written in letter 2 and as Magen Avraham wrote letter 4 — nevertheless l'chatchila it is fit to read the obligatory Keriat Shema immediately before eating; and as Magen Avraham wrote letter 2; and Eshel Avraham letter 3 — see there.

And know that per the one who says Keriat Shema's time is from the emergence of the stars — behold before then no; if he doubts whether he read in its time and he knows he read in the synagogue — whether he returns and reads or not; and in our novellae at the beginning of Berachot we were in doubt on this — one could say in Beit HaSho'evah it is not a doubt but certain he does not fulfill, for it is not the time of lying down at Hashkiveinu; and therefore it said from the hour the Kohanim, etc., at their terumah — one could say there at Hashkiveinu it is not a doubt and the sky will be purified from the light of the sun — Iyun Yaakov there; and Shabbat 35 — bein hashmashot — R' Yosi: Kohanim are forbidden — implies somewhat that it is a doubt; and it is possible to load on their terumah the known verse in d'oraisa that doubt is like certainty only that one is not lashed as written in Tosafot HaRosh — from this Keriat Shema there is no doubt without it and the time of lying down applies; if so there is no safek safeka and he returns and reads; nevertheless one could say for us there are several tannaim who hold its time is before and one could say likewise to precede — there is no tannaitic dispute. Nevertheless for Keriat Shema on his bed one could say he reads only the first paragraph; and per the one who says two paragraphs d'oraisa this is our safek; and in the Yerushalmi beginning Berachot he sought to resolve the safek of one who read Keriat Shema — he returns and reads from the baraita: one who reads before then does not fulfill — and it is not a safek; and see Chiddushei HaRan beginning Berachot they brought; and in our novellae we expanded and its place is not here in this. And regarding what the Rashba wrote that for prayer they were lenient so much — Beit Yosef in his name in 233; and this requires further study — for Ma'ariv prayer is optional and d'rabbanan one could say thus; but Keriat Shema is d'oraisa and one could say those who pray Minchah until evening and Keriat Shema sometimes while the day is still great — they hold that the time of Keriat Shema is split and Minchah prayer they were lenient also because of the burden on the community. And regarding mentioning Egypt at night it helps; and what he said parashat Tzitzit in the synagogue — even though he is obligated to mention Yetzi'at Mitzrayim at night all the days of your life to include the nights — and it is a verse from ab initio — we wrote elsewhere it is not a full derasha; and if so in Beit HaPesach it will be explained further regarding the Haggadah.
