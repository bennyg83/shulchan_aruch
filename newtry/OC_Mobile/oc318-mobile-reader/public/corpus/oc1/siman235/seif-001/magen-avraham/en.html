And he reads without blessings. And he should not rely on Keriat Shema on his bed — one reason because he reads it after eating and drinking and obligatory Keriat Shema must be read l'chatchila before eating; and further because he does not intend to fulfill his obligation thereby; and siman 60 seif 4; and nevertheless if he reads the first two sections since he mentioned yetzi'at Mitzrayim in the synagogue (Tur); and the world now practices like Rabbeinu Tam that they fulfill with Keriat Shema they read in the synagogue even though it is a bit while still day; but every person should be stringent for himself and read on his bed all of Keriat Shema — at least two sections — and intend to fulfill thereby; and in summer he should be careful to read it after tzeit hakochavim; and even though he eats before he fulfills as Rashi wrote; and see siman 267; and the ancients were accustomed to read immediately at tzeit hakochavim; and see seif 2:

They advance much. It is written in Terumat HaDeshen that they were accustomed to advance three or four hours before night and he was greatly pressed to settle the custom and could not; and from Bach's words in siman 261 it implies they practiced as the one who says from sunset it is night and plag haminchah is an hour and a quarter before sunset; and when we calculate a temporal hour it is in summer approximately two hours before night; however we do not hold so for on erev Shabbos they do melachah until half an hour before night approximately; and therefore even b'dieved if he prayed at the aforementioned time he must return and pray; but Keriat Shema it is plausible for most poskim that we require specifically after tzeit hakochavim; and therefore it does not appear arrogant if he returns and reads; it appears to me that one accustomed to read Keriat Shema and pray with the congregation Maariv at night — and erred and began the blessing of Keriat Shema with the congregation while still day — should finish them so as not to bless a blessing in vain; and at night he should read Keriat Shema without its blessings and pray even though he does not connect geulah to tefillah — this is preferable so that he pray at its time; and so too siman 89 that it is not permitted for him to read Keriat Shema before its time because of connecting geulah to tefillah of Shacharit; and a fortiori Maariv — see siman 236 seif 3:
