Small stars. Baer Heitev; and this requires further study from what Magen Avraham wrote in siman 562 that small stars do not help; and see there that he emended 'large' in place of 'small'; and see in Shaarei Tzedek in the name of Mateh Yehudah who explained until the small stars that emerge after the prayer are visible — that is, the medium ones — meaning that at the beginning of their emergence they appear small and afterward those same small stars themselves appear larger to our eyes, and after they grow other small stars emerge; and so too in Be'er HaGolah that the small stars here mean those after the medium ones about which they said in chapter Bava Metzia that it is certainly night; and in siman 562 it deals with the medium ones at the beginning of their emergence which are small; and with this he resolves the words of Shach in Yoreh De'ah siman 262 from what Peri Chadash wondered in Likutim — see there; and these three stars for Keriat Shema we do not require consecutive ones but even scattered ones help; and see siman 493.
