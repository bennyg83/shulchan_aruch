Three small stars. Beit Yosef in the name of Terumat HaDeshen wrote medium ones as stated in the beginning of Bava Metzia; and since not everyone is expert between medium and small ones one must be careful until the small ones are seen — end quote; and in siman 562 regarding Ta'anit the Rama wrote medium ones — and it must be said that for Ta'anit they did not burden so much.
