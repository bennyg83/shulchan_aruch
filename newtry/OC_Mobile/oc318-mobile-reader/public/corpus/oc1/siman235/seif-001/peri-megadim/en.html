Three stars — Taz; in Avodat HaKodesh siman 1 the Maggid emended between large and medium; and it is possible to resolve that on Shabbat 35b not large ones visible by day and not small ones visible only at night but medium ones; and it implies a small star — even 2 or 3 are not visible except at night; and for this reason that we require three small ones is because of a fence — lest if three medium ones come they err with two and say they are small, and a doubt in Torah is stringent; therefore they made specifically three small ones. And one could ask: on Ta'anit they did not burden — unlike if one is not expert between large and medium it is fit to be stringent on Ta'anit as well even though it is d'rabbanan — one does not apply doubt leniently in lack of wisdom except for experts in medium ones; and in d'oraisa they made a fence and not there; and it wrote on Ta'anit they did not burden — even on Yom Kippur d'oraisa — possibly they did not burden as long as there are three medium consecutive ones that appear as one, for then it is Tosafot Terumat HaDeshen 284 — for the Rambam says so; our master Magen Avraham z"l in 493 letter 2 — small ones that one is not expert (it is the language of R' Yitzchak brought in Beit Yosef and there it says between large and small); and presumably Ran does not disagree that we require consecutive ones because of Tosafot — evidently medium consecutive ones are Tosafot; and see Levush in 493 that then it is certainly night — meaning even for one who does not understand — and it is Tosafot; and Eshel Avraham part 5 Ma'adanei HaShulchan letter 4 — three medium ones; and part 7 Ma'adanei Terumot did not write medium ones — and I do not know why; and if because Shabbat involves karet and stoning they were stringent to say a doubt in stoning is forbidden until there are medium ones — unlike terumah where death is involved, all the more a doubt in stoning is permitted only d'rabbanan they did not be stringent likewise — this is confused for several reasons; and further in part 9 Ma'adanei Tum'at Met 12 the Peri Chadash did not agree to that version that the Kesef Mishneh brought in Asham Talui — and there where it is established, etc., its place is not here; and if so it will be explained elsewhere. Magen Avraham 472 letter 1 — medium ones but small ones do not help — Taz is wrong and it must be said but large ones do not help; but one could say with difficulty less than three small ones nevertheless Taz is wrong; and Magen Avraham here letter 1 — and if so it will be explained there.
