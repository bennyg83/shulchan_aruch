The time of Keriat Shema at night is from
the emergence of three small stars; and if it is a cloudy day he should wait until the doubt clears from his heart; and if he read it before then he returns and reads it without blessings; and if the congregation advance to read Keriat Shema while still day he reads Keriat Shema with them with its blessings and prays with them; and when its time arrives he reads Keriat Shema without blessings:However he should not return and pray at night even though the congregation advance much before night — unless he is accustomed to other piety and hasidut — for then it does not appear arrogant that he returns and prays (Mordechai beginning of Berachos and Hagahot Maimoni chapter 2 of Hilchot Tefillah and Terumat HaDeshen siman 61):.
