As Magen Avraham wrote what Olas Tamid etc. — on the contrary etc. — for Tosafos in Berachos daf 26b wrote on that which is taught: Yaakov enacted Maariv tefillah as it says 'and he encountered the place and lodged there' — and encounter means only tefillah; and so they state: lest one say in personality in Berachos chapter 3 — when he reached Charan he wanted to return; he said: perhaps I passed a place etc. — apparently there it is proven he prayed Maariv by day since he already prayed and his intent was to return (unless the sun set not at its time as they expound the verse when the sun came) — and it is difficult on the mishnah in the first chapter that says it is not time of tefillah until tzeit hakochavim; and according to what we explained that we hold like R' Yehuda who says until plag haminchah — it works out; and well is our custom that on the contrary it is good to pray a bit while still day — end quote. And Olas Tamid wrote that necessarily it must be that Yaakov also said the blessings of Keriat Shema by day — for we hold like R' Yochanan that also in Maariv one must connect geulah to tefillah — only Keriat Shema he read at night; and Olas Tamid wrote on this that necessarily Tosafos do not hold so — for if so there would be no difficulty etc.:
