1) [Seif 1] Three small stars; according to the strict law, medium stars suffice. This is as stated in siman 562. Since we are not expert, however, small stars are required. Large stars are ineffective because they are visible even during the day. Beis Yosef and Magen Avraham in the name of Rabbeinu Yonah. Regarding Rema's statement in siman 562 that medium stars suffice for a fast, Taz, paragraph 1, writes that one must say the Sages did not impose such a burden regarding a fast. Alternatively, since Krias Shema is of Torah origin, the Sages were slightly more stringent than with a fast, which is rabbinic. Mateh Yehudah, paragraph 1. This will be explained further below in siman 562, with the help of Heaven.
