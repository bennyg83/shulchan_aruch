(3) Maariv tefillah is not like Minchah tefillah — for Minchah tefillah is strict judgment and therefore its essence is standing which is in the world of Atzilut. But Maariv tefillah is lenient judgment and therefore we say therein individual Keriat Shema with its blessings which are the secret of the palaces of the world of Beriah as is known.
