(Note 3) They advance etc. And when we calculate temporal hours it is in summer approximately two hours — meaning the time of plag haminchah which is an hour and a quarter by parable — if the day is long eighteen hours then the measure of an hour is an hour and a half and a quarter hour is a quarter and a half; if so the measure of plag haminchah is two hours less half a quarter hour — that is an eighth of an hour; and when we add to them the measure of four mil from beginning of sunset until tzeit hakochavim which is an hour and a fifth according to Magen Avraham's words in siman 233 note 3 (and this is certainly an hour of proportional hours and not a temporal hour for this depends on equal motion and the wheel between long or short day) — if so combined it is more than three hours before tzeit hakochavim:

But Keriat Shema etc. — This is a separate matter and refers to Rama's opinion who wrote however he should not return and pray because it appears arrogant; but he agrees to Rabbeinu Yehuda that Keriat Shema he reads again after tzeit hakochavim and we are not concerned for arrogance — for this he gave a reason: since for most poskim we require specifically tzeit hakochavim; even according to R' Yehuda that after plag haminchah it is night for tefillah — nevertheless for Keriat Shema that is written uvshachbecha and we require time of lying down he must wait until tzeit hakochavim according to most poskim:

And a fortiori Maariv. For in Shacharit according to all one must connect geulah to tefillah; and in Maariv there is a dispute of R' Yehuda ben Levi and R' Yehuda — R' Yehuda ben Levi holds tefillot they enacted in the middle — meaning all three tefillot of the day should be in the middle of the two Keriat Shema of Shacharit and Maariv; and if so he prays Maariv before Keriat Shema and its blessings and does not connect geulah to tefillah; but we hold like R' Yehuda that also in Maariv one must connect geulah to tefillah:
