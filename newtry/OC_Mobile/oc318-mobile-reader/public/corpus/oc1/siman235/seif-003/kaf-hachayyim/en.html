3) The Maariv prayer is unlike Minchah. Minchah represents severe judgment, and therefore its essential component is Shemoneh Esrei, which is in the world of Atzilus. Maariv, however, represents softened judgment. Therefore, during it an individual recites Krias Shema with its blessings, which correspond to the chambers of the world of Beriah, as is known. Shaar HaKavanos, folio 52b; Pri Etz Chaim, gate 15, chapter 5.
