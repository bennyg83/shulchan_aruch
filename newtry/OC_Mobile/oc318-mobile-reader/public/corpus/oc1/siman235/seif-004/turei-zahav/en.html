Such as one who is drunk, etc. Beit Yosef in the name of the Rashba wrote as follows: even close to sunrise literally — since there are still people sleeping; but one who transgressed intentionally and delayed — no; and even as negligent also; and even immediately after dawn — forbidden; and like the incident of R' Gamliel who said to his sons: if dawn has not risen you are obligated to read — behold if it rose not — and they certainly imply they were not fully inadvertent but negligent because they were drawn after banquet matters — end quote; and these 'negligent' that he said are not truly negligent Heaven forbid — for they were Torah scholars as implied in the Gemara — rather he said the opposite of fully inadvertent — that they were inadvertent close to intentional in that they continued all night and did not read and suddenly dawn came; and that which is in the Gemara regarding the pair of rabbis who became drunk at the wedding of R' Yehoshua ben Levi's son — they came before R' Yehoshua ben Levi; he said R' Shimon is fit to rely on him in a time of pressure — and we consider them as compelled and they read before sunrise; and we do not say they are not inadvertent since they were drawn after banquet matters like the sons of R' Gamliel — it appears to me that the sons of R' Gamliel were not drunk only that they were at their banquet; and these rabbis were drunk at the beginning of the time of Keriat Shema — for otherwise they would not be compelled since they continued in prohibition after the banquet; and it is known that whoever increases drinking becomes drunk; but certainly they became drunk before the time in permissibility — therefore it appears to me that if a person became drunk after the beginning of the time of Keriat Shema he is not called compelled and he has no measure to read except until dawn rises and no more.
