Such as — Taz; he brought up that whoever began in prohibition no longer reads after dawn, for he is not compelled but negligent — meaning his beginning was in negligence even though at the end he is compelled; and it is lighter than intentional as the Rashba wrote; and even negligent — meaning not fully intentional; and regarding the blessings he also does not say; and Tosafot 55 seif 7; and in Magen Avraham os 8 and 99 — Magen Avraham letter 3 disagrees with the Taz and holds even if he began in prohibition he is called compelled — see there; and Tosafot 101 seif 8; and in Avodat HaRodesh here — and he reads like one who reads in the Torah without blessings.
