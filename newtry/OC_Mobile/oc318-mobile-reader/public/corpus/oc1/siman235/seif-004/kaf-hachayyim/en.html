4) Regarding tzedakah, one need not give before Maariv as before Shacharis, because this is a time of severe judgments, as indicated by the intentions there. See Birkei Yosef, paragraph 1, who writes that this means its intention is not like at Shacharis. Nevertheless, it still helps and protects from harmful forces even if given at night, and he brings proof from the Yerushalmi; see there.
