(א) Rather — Taz challenged Rambam's explanation: why does the Gemara need a verse; one may say since he holds gorem lemamon is as money, likewise for unavoidable loss — otherwise per those who hold not as money, whence this; and also for seif 4 since it returned to sight, etc. And his challenge on "in his city is stringent" — selling is difficult for it is not his; and giving to another non-Jew does not suffice especially per Rama; Magen Avraham note 1 will be explained; see Mishneh Lemelech and Levushei Serad's answer regarding kingdom law — see there at length.

(ב) And behold the Mechaber brought the first view in unspecified form — only theft, not negligence like Rambam and R' Yitzchak; and he did not decide regarding unavoidable loss — thus after Passover one may say it is a doubt for leniency; within Passover a Torah doubt; if the loss is only from negligence within Passover — double doubt; all the more for first view in unspecified form; and to accept l'chatchila — one should not create double doubt with hands — Magen Avraham siman 467; Levush may be explained thus — see there.

(ג) I was asked: non-Jews place flour with a Jew in a house, pay fixed weekly rent, and the Jew has liability on Passover — how should they conduct? I answered: sell to them — he has no other remedy; some say semag siman 62 one may benefit a person without his knowledge and make him an agent; or the depositor gives permission to his seller; or deposit with another non-Jew who accepts liability as Magen Avraham note 1; and the place is rented to him — not chametz rent — siman 448; here more lenient; see Magen Avraham further. Our flour is wheat for preserves and full chametz — nullification does not help since it is not his.
