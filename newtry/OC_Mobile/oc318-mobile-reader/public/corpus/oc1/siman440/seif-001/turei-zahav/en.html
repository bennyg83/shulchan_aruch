Beit Yosef brought two views — Rambam and Raavad: Rambam holds if the non-Jew is forceful it is as his own and he must destroy, explaining Rava to sons of Machoza — warriors' chametz is stringent since if stolen they will compel payment though payments were not by law but under duress — nevertheless called their chametz; Raavad holds liability from duress is not liability obligating biur; Rava there was legal liability from kingdom law to support soldiers guarding the city. Challenge on Rambam: why does Gemara need verse to teach where liable it is as his own — per view gorem lemamon not as money we need to teach regarding chametz anyway forbidden; per view as money — why need verse for duress; further challenge on Rava — why say burn, sell suffices; why give to another non-Jew — does not help, especially per Rama; why Rava said "in your city from your houses" — should say briefly "in your city warriors' chametz"; for Raavad — give to another non-Jew and exempt from king's law; for Rambam — even giving to another non-Jew he will compel payment — difficult; Gemara "accepted liability" does not imply Jew accepted liability for duress — how bring proof from Rava; thus one who rules like Raavad did not lose due to these difficulties.
