Seif 1 — if he is liable for it. Meaning he accepted explicitly, for we hold there is no law of guardians for a non-Jew. And even if he accepted explicitly to be obligated in payment — some say for negligence it helps without kinyan but for theft and loss kinyan is required — see Gilyon Choshen Mishpat siman 291 s.k. 27 and Machaneh Efraim Hilchot Shomrim siman 8.
