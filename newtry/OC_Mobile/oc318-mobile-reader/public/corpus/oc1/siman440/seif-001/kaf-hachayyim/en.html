1) [Se'if 1] If one is liable for it, etc. This is taught in Pesachim 5b: The verse states, 'Leaven shall not be seen by you.' You may not see your own, but you may see that of others or that which is consecrated. One might infer that one may hide it or accept deposits from a non-Jew; therefore, the verse states, 'It shall not be found in your homes.' The Gemara subsequently explains that the latter case is where one accepted liability for it, whereas the former case is where one did not accept liability for it. See there.
