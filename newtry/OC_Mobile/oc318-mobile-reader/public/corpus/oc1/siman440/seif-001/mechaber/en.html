The law of a non-Jew's chametz deposited with a Jew. It contains four se'ifim.
A non-Jew who deposited his chametz with a Jew: if [the Jew] is responsible for it against theft and loss — whether it is in his house or anywhere within his domain — he is obligated to remove it. {Rama: And even if he in turn deposited it with another non-Jew (Hagahos Alfasi, first chapter of Pesachim).} And some say that even if he is only an unpaid guardian, who is liable for negligence, he is obligated to remove it. And even if he is not legally responsible for it, but he knows that the non-Jew is forceful and will compel him to pay if it is lost, he is obligated to remove it; and some disagree.
