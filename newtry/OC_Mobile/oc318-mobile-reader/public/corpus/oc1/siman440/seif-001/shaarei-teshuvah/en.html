See Baer Heitev; see responsum Sha'agat Aryeh siman 87 and Avnei Nezer that nullification and hefker do not help for others' chametz for which he accepted liability — this does not remove bal yera'eh; no remedy except burning or designating a house or partition ten handbreadths before Passover. He wrote in siman 79 and 80 — if he did not make this remedy until within Passover — forbidden in benefit after Passover; but if he made the remedy before six hours even for non-Jew's and there is a positive commandment of tashbitu from six hours — nevertheless although he transgressed the positive of tashbitu — permitted in benefit after Passover — see there extensive cases siman 83, 84, Beit Efraim siman 41, etc.
