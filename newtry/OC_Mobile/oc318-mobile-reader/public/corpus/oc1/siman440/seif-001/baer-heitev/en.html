(א) He must destroy it. And if he transgressed and did not destroy it — Magen Avraham wrote: if the non-Jew comes after Passover he may give him the chametz; nevertheless some say it is forbidden from this reason to give it to him lest the non-Jew return and sell it to a Jew and the Jew stumble on chametz that passed over Passover — see there. And Kaf HaChayim disagrees and wrote he may say to him "here is yours before you" — see there; and see responsum Shevut Yaakov chelek 2 siman 66.

(ב) Another non-Jew. And if the second non-Jew accepted liability for theft and loss — permitted, Magen Avraham. And Kaf HaChayim disagrees and is stringent — see Peri Chadash. And if he deposited it with its owner — permitted; and likewise if the non-Jew placed his chametz in a non-Jew's domain and there a Jew accepted liability — agreement of the Poskim.

(ג) And there are those who disagree. And b'dieved one may rely on the first view. (And likewise Kaf HaChayim agreed there.) And if he is liable by law of the kingdom he is obligated to destroy according to all — Kaf HaChayim.
