And some disagree. See Baer Heitev. His intent is that although one should not rely on the dissenting view regarding a violent non-Jew, even after the fact, there is nevertheless room after the fact to rely upon the first opinion, which holds that only liability for theft and loss creates the relevant obligation. Chok Yaakov explains this clearly. This is how I answered those who asked me about Baer Heitev’s words. See Shaagas Aryeh, siman 88, concerning one who accepted liability only for negligence. Although he must remove the chametz before Pesach, if he transgressed and failed to remove it, the chametz is permitted for benefit after Pesach. See there.
