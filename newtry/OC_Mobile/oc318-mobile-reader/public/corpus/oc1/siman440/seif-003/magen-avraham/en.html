(ב) And even with a separating cloth. And even though if one eats meat and one milk at one table it is permitted with a separating cloth — here since chametz is significant it is impossible that a crumb of the non-Jew's not mix into the Jew's food and it is forbidden; and Acharonim agreed that even if he has no acquaintance with this non-Jew and no concern he might forget and eat from his chametz — likewise forbidden, for perhaps a crumb of chametz mixes by itself into the Jew's food. And all this when they eat at one time; but when the Jew is not eating he may leave the non-Jew to eat at his table only that the Jew should clean the table well after the non-Jew eats so no crumbs remain; and he should be careful when the non-Jew leaves his house to take all his chametz and leave nothing — see below siman 88 and siman 423.
