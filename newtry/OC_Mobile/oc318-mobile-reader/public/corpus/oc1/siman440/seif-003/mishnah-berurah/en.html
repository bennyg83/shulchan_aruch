(א) There is nothing to do with it – yours do not see but you see others. And he will bring his food to the darkness (as he felt in his hand, and that he was forced to do so) that he was under the hands of the High Priests, and he was guarded:

(ב) To raise him with him, he will not be able to eat together for the sender who will not interfere with Israel’s food:

(ג) And even with a separating cloth — and even if he does not say the berachah with the kohanim it is forbidden, for obviously a berachah le'vatalah is forbidden — Torah said to bless to kohanim not Israelites, as in previous note; likewise Eliyah Rabbah note 4. And all this when eating at one time; but when not eating he may leave the non-Jew to eat at his table only he must clean the table well after the non-Jew eats so no crumbs remain; and beware when the non-Jew leaves his house to take all his chametz and leave nothing.
