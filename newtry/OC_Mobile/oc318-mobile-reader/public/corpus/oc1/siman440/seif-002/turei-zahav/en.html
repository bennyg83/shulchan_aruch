And he must make, etc. — and although he did not accept liability, if he accepted liability partition does not help; even designating a house does not help — Rambam and Rashi and R' Tam; R' Tam holds even with liability designating a house helps, and partition helps; without liability no partition needed. Why then does designation help with liability — why did Rava say "in your city from your houses" — should say designated place; one may say he truly told them to destroy from their designated houses and take to warriors' place. R' Tam — with liability must designate explicitly; if only "here is the house" since he accepted liability he would have to pay if he did not designate — when he clarifies we say it is clarified, for we do not say birur in a d'oraisa matter.
