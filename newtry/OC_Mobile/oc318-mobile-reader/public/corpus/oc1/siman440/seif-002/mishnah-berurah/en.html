(10) He is not required to destroy it. During Pesach itself, however, one should not initially accept a deposit of chametz at all, even without responsibility and even without payment. Later authorities.
(11) Such as a ger toshav. This means a non-Jew who resides in your land and is subject to Jewish authority. One might have thought that his property is treated like yours and that you must remove his deposit from your domain. The ruling teaches that this is not so.
(12) A partition. A curtain does not suffice, because people pass beneath it.
(13) So that he will not forget, etc. Although other prohibitions of eating and benefit do not require a partition, chametz is different because people do not ordinarily avoid it throughout the year, and we are concerned that he may come to eat it. A partition is therefore required as a reminder. Covering the chametz with a vessel does not suffice, because he may remove the vessel for use and expose the chametz. Covering with a vessel was permitted in siman 446, seif 1, only when chametz is found in one’s home on Yom Tov, when a partition cannot be made, and only for a short period, since it will be destroyed that night.
(14) A non-Jew who placed, etc. This teaches that even when a non-Jew placed chametz there entirely without permission, the same concern applies as with one’s own chametz or a non-Jew’s chametz deposited in one’s care. Before Yom Tov a partition is required. On Yom Tov, when a partition cannot be made, it must at least be covered with a vessel, because here too we are concerned that one may eat it.
(15) If it is before Yom Tov, etc. Since a partition can then be made, it must be made. The same applies on Motzei Yom Tov, meaning the first night of Chol HaMoed. Likewise, if the non-Jew places the chametz there during Chol HaMoed, a partition ten tefachim high is required even when the Jew knows that the non-Jew will return for it after a day or two. If he knows that the non-Jew will return and take it that same day, one may be lenient and merely cover it with a vessel.
