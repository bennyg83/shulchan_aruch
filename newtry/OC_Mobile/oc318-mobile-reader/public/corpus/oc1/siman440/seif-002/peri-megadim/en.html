And one must — Taz: Rashi and R' Tam Pesachim 6a disagree on two matters: Rashi — with liability designation of a corner does not help (Magen Avraham 1); without liability no corner needed but partition rabbinically for seven days for recognition; covering a vessel Rashi answered for one day or Yom Tov when partition impossible; R' Tam — designating a corner with liability permitted, for non-Jew's chametz in his house with Jew's liability is permitted; partition is as his house — before the non-Jew owner; without liability covering a vessel suffices even seven days. They also dispute explanation of designation — Rashi "here is the house" without liability; Tosafot — corner specifically. Taz's "he must pay" should read "must destroy." Halachah follows Rashi as in Shulchan Aruch — see Ran and Bach on Rashi s.v. deposited and accepted liability — see there.
