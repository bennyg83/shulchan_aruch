Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. Even if the non-Jew arrives during Pesach, it is permitted. In my opinion, since the Jew transgresses bal yeraeh when the non-Jew arrives during Pesach, the Jew may not leave the chametz for him to take. Although this is not ordinarily classified as deriving benefit, the Jew must destroy it in order to remedy the prohibition. Bal yeraeh is a prohibition remedied by a positive commandment, as in Pesachim 95a. He must therefore burn the chametz and reimburse the non-Jew.
