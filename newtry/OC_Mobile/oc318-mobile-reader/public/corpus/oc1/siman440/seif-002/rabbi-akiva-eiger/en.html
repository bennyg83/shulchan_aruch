Magen Avraham s.k. 1 — that even if the non-Jew comes it is permitted on Passover; in my opinion: since the Jew transgressed bal yera'eh when the non-Jew came on Passover he may not leave it for him to take; although not called benefiting — nevertheless the Jew is obligated to destroy to fix the negative commandment, for bal yera'eh is transformed to a positive as in Pesachim 95a; thus he must burn and pay the non-Jew his money.
