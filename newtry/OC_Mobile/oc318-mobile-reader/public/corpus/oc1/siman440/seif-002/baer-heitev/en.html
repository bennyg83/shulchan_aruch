A utensil. Although it is possible to make a partition with a sheet, it is preferable to cover the chametz with an overturned utensil rather than use a sheet, because a sheet moves back and forth. Magen Avraham.
But if it is before Yom Tov. The same applies during Chol HaMoed. A partition should be made even if the non-Jew intends to take it the next day. If it was left there for only half a day, one may be lenient and cover it with an overturned utensil. Magen Avraham. Chok Yaakov writes that if the Jew is not required to compensate the non-Jew, he should destroy the chametz, and there is no need to trouble oneself to make a partition during Chol HaMoed. See there.
