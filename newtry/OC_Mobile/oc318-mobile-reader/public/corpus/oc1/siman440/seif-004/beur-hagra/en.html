(א) Seif 4 — a Jew who deposited. As written "there are those who say" in seif 1; but a Jew, etc. s.v. over; and although Tosafot there 2 s.v. shekoneh wrote "the whole sugya," etc. — they wrote only regarding a Jew's chametz, and so is implied there from the challenge from the mishnah to Rava "we learned a Jew," etc., and it does not say the mishnah is when he accepted liability.

(ב) He transgresses regarding it. Apparently proof from the mishnah "a Jew who lent to a Jew," etc., and we establish it even though we deposited with him — specifically when he said from now; and even though one may reject that the mishnah is when he did not accept liability, similar to a Jew who lent, as Tosafot wrote — nevertheless since the Gemara was forced and did not establish, etc.
