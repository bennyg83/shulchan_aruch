(א) Thus Taz in the fourth distinction — R' Moshe z"l like Rabbi, and Tur ruled like Rabban Gamliel, and if he nullified he need not [check] more, or a small loaf. And the third distinction — they agree b'dieved without nullification like the Sages per R' Meir and one community and two checked [houses] — for Tur, distinction in one case or one-and-one; and for R' Moshe z"l there is no distinction; and the Mechaber might rely on what is written in Yoreh De'ah, Magen Avraham (5), Eshel Avraham — for Rabban Gamliel it is difficult for the Rabbis: he left ten and found nine — checking is required; if he even found ten, the entire house requires checking — I would say since we were already concerned perhaps he hid; not so if he left and found as he left; and furthermore that here even if he nullified he must check after the tenth (per Tur's view in 431 one might say that one who checks until he reaches a sela):

(ב) And what Taz wrote from captivity — I did not understand, for captivity is rabbinic, for from what did he establish it on presumption as explained in many places; and for R' Moshe z"l, in rabbinic [law] even in one case it is permitted — nevertheless captivity has a trace of d'oraisa; and from terumah one might say nowadays it is rabbinic, or per one view terumah of fruits outside the Land is rabbinic, and in d'oraisa requires study — for it is impossible to be lenient from doubt. And dry in dry — why permitted in majority and from rabbinic [law] we are stringent in one case for one person requires study in this. Chok Yaakov (7) — and Rama said four — the Mechaber: one community chametz and two houses checked — likewise two communities chametz and one matza; and the truth is as Peri Chadash wrote per Tur's view — two cases and two communities, one chametz and one matza, and two houses one checked and one not checked, even of one person it is fine — not so for R' Elazar z"l, two houses checked and one community chametz and he nullified — one might say for tum'ah; and even for one person we rely to be lenient — if so, specifically one community; but two communities, one chametz and one matza, for there are two entrances it is impaired — we do not say to be lenient except one checked and one not checked — see there in Peri Chadash. Furthermore Peri Chadash wrote: one community chametz and two houses, one checked and one not checked, and we do not know about them for non-sacred food to be lenient with nullification; and if doubt whether in general nullification is not required — here a double doubt — for from what is written from two communities, one chametz and one matza, for there is only one entrance — Maggid Mishneh Yoreh De'ah 111 — per the Mechaber's order; and the law of the four is explained in Baal HaMaor, and he explained doubt in such a case; and the Rabbis in tum'ah — a double doubt does not help; here in chametz they agree like Rashi's explanation; and however, even without nullification for its mixture — see there; and it is not as if.
