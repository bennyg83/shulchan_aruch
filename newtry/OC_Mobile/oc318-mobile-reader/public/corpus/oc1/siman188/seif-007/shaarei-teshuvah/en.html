The meal—see Ba'er Hetev; and likewise Rabbeinu Yonah in name of Sha'arei Yeshuah in manuscripts that even though Magen Avraham in siman 695 wrote that Mordechai's opinion is to say Al HaNissim—we do not rule so, for even without praying Rosh holds and our custom is we follow the present etc.—see there. And Magen Avraham there wrote: he blesses first and afterward prays—to remove himself from the dispute—see there. And see Eshel Avraham: if he did not pray—even though the congregation prayed—he mentions; however if he prayed Minchah and began to eat after the congregation prayed Maariv on Rosh Chodesh while still day—he does not mention that of Rosh Chodesh. And the same conversely: if the congregation prayed on the evening of Rosh Chodesh while still day and he began to eat after Maariv—even though he did not pray Maariv—he mentions that of Rosh Chodesh; however if he has not yet prayed Minchah he does not mention that of Rosh Chodesh since he is about to pray weekday Minchah—and the same on Motza'ei Rosh Chodesh he mentions that of Rosh Chodesh in birkat hamazon if he did not pray Minchah even though the congregation prayed Maariv while still day there.
