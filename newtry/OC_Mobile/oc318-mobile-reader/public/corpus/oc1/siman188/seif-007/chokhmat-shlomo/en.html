There, seif 7. If Rosh Chodesh falls on Shabbos and he mentioned Shabbos but did not mention Rosh Chodesh, etc. Note: This is the ruling here, and we find the same in several places in Shulchan Aruch: in Hilchos Yom Kippur regarding mentioning Shabbos in the Haftarah at Minchah and in Ne’ilah, and in Hilchos Shabbos, siman 284, regarding mentioning Shabbos in the Haftarah on Rosh Chodesh. This all requires study in light of Zevachim 91a, where the Gemara says: Did Rosh Chodesh affect only its own Mussaf offerings and not the Mussaf offerings of Shabbos? It likewise says: Did Rosh Hashanah affect only its own Mussaf offerings and not those of Rosh Chodesh? This proves that the sanctity of one day can affect even an offering that is not uniquely its own when that offering occurs on that day. Accordingly, when Rosh Chodesh falls on Shabbos, Shabbos should suffice for Rosh Chodesh as well; and when Yom Kippur falls on Shabbos, Yom Kippur should suffice for Shabbos as well. There is a similar difficulty from Pesachim 59a, concerning Rav Safra’s question. The Gemara answers that the case concerns the fourteenth of Nissan falling on Shabbos, so the fats of Shabbos may be offered on Yom Tov. Yet the offering is ultimately a weekday offering. What difference does it make that the day is Shabbos, when this is not a Shabbos offering? One cannot answer that the Pesach offering is superior to Shabbos because it overrides Shabbos, for then the Gemara need not have said that the fourteenth fell on Shabbos; it could simply have answered that the Pesach offering is superior to Shabbos because it overrides it, and therefore is not included in the prohibition against offering a weekday burnt offering on Yom Tov. Necessarily, that explanation is insufficient. What, then, is gained by the fact that the day is Shabbos if the offering is not a Shabbos offering? It must be that Shabbos affects even this offering, as stated in Zevachim. If so, why should we not say the same regarding Bircas HaMazon, the Haftarah, and the Amidah? This requires study. Examine it carefully.
