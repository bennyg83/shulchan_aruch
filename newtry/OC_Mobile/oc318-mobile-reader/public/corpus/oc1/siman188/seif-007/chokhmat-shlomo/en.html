There in seif 7: if Rosh Chodesh fell on Shabbat and he mentioned Shabbat and did not mention Rosh Chodesh, etc. NB: So it is here, and so we find in several places in Shulchan Aruch Laws of Yom Kippur regarding mentioning Shabbat in the haftarah at Minchah and regarding Ne'ilah prayer; and likewise Laws of Shabbat siman 484 regarding mentioning Shabbat on Rosh Chodesh in the haftarah. And behold there is study on all this from the Talmud Zevachim daf 91, where it says: is Rosh Chodesh effective for its own additional offering but not for Shabbat's additional offerings? And likewise it says there regarding Rosh Hashanah. If so, it is proven that one holiness is effective even for what is not its own day, if it occurred on its day; if so, here too: if Rosh Chodesh occurred on Shabbat, Shabbat is effective also for Rosh Chodesh; and likewise Yom Kippur on Shabbat—Yom Kippur is effective also for Shabbat. And likewise difficult from what we learn in Pesachim daf 59 regarding the fourteen [of Nisan], etc., and the answer: the additional offerings of Shabbat are offered on Yom Tov—and it is difficult: behold in the end it is a weekday offering, and what of it that the day is Shabbat—behold this is not a Shabbat offering; and to say Pesach is preferable to Shabbat since Pesach defers Shabbat—if so he need not say on the fourteenth that fell on Shabbat, but should say simply that Pesach is preferable to Shabbat since Pesach defers Shabbat and is not at all a weekday offering on Yom Tov; perforce this does not help; if so, what of it that the day is Shabbat, since this is not a Shabbat offering? Perforce it is proven that Shabbat is effective also for this offering, as stated in Zevachim above; if so, it is difficult why for birkat hamazon, haftarah, and prayer we do not say so—and requires study; examine well.
