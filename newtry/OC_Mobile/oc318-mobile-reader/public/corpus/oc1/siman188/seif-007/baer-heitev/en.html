Shabbat. If forgot Shabbat alone does not mention Rosh Chodesh in blessing; however when opens haTov vehaMetiv returning to beginning all mention Rosh Chodesh. On Shabbat Chanukah if mentioned Chanukah and not Shabbat—returns to beginning and does not mention Chanukah for not obligated to mention Chanukah. Magen Avraham.
