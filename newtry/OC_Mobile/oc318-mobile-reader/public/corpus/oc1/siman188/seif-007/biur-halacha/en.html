Between at night—even though regarding prayer we hold we do not return him if he forgot yaaleh veyavo on the night of Rosh Chodesh, here we return him; but to add a berachah even at night as well [Tur]; see Rashba at the end of ch. Tefillat Hashachar what he wrote on this.

Says Blessed Who gave, etc.—see Shaarei Teshuvah in name of Birkhei Yosef; likewise Peri Megadim doubts—see there; nevertheless for the law it appears one should rely on the many who wrote explicitly that the opening must be with Name and malchut: they are Shibolei Leket in Laws of Rosh Chodesh, Piskei Riaz to Berakhot—see there, and likewise Rabbenu Yerucham in Laws of Shabbat; and it is proven from Tosafot Rabbenu Yehuda what he wrote in the reason he does not conclude—because it is like berachot on fruits and matzah that are without conclusion; and it implies in any case he opens with Name and malchut; so too Shitah Mekubetzet there—see there; and aside from this there is another band of Rishonim who hold for the law that it is a complete berachah even regarding conclusion like Shabbat and Yom Tov [and from this certainly all the more that it must be with Name and malchut in the opening]: they are Behag, Rokeach, Raavan, Eshkol with a variant in the Gemara, Tosafot Rabbenu Yehuda, Or Zaru'a in name of Yerushalmi [and in Tosafot Rosh he also sides thus, and likewise Tur that his father Rosh agrees for the law though in Rosh he did not decide explicitly]; and though we do not practice with conclusion, at least the opening must be with Name and malchut, since among all those who hold there is no conclusion there is not one who wrote explicitly that the opening of the berachah is also without Name and malchut; on the contrary many poskim who uphold their method regarding conclusion wrote explicitly that the opening of the berachat Rosh Chodesh is with Name and malchut, as stated; and likewise Av Beit Din agreed for the law, and Maaseh Rokeach, Shulchan Aruch of the Gr"a, and Chayei Adam.

And one should not say it in vain—see Magen Avraham who wondered on this; in Sefer Bigdei Yeshu'a they settle it: here regarding yaaleh veyavo his intent is to fulfill the lesser obligation of berachah, and in this there is concern of berachah levatalah—see there that he brought support for his words.
