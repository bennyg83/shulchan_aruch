(25) He says Baruch shenatan—see in Biur Halachah where we clarified in the name of most poskim that also that one must be with Name and malchut.
(26) Because he is not obligated etc.—for specifically on Shabbat and Yom Tov when one is obligated specifically to eat bread—and if so the obligation of birkat hamazon is fixed on those days—therefore the Sages enacted to delay also to mention a kind of the event as in prayer; but on Rosh Chodesh—even though it is forbidden to fast—nevertheless he is not obligated specifically to eat bread, and if so there is no fixed obligation of birkat hamazon on it—therefore the Sages did not enact its mention of a kind of the event to delay in birkat hamazon; and therefore if he forgot he need not return; and Magen Avraham wrote that likewise on Yom Tov beyond one meal by day and one meal by night that he is obligated to eat—if he ate and forgot to mention a kind of the event in birkat hamazon he need not return as on Rosh Chodesh, except when he did not eat the meal at night that he is obligated to eat two by day—as below siman 599—then even in the second meal he returns [Peri Megadim].
(27) And Chol HaMoed its law is like Rosh Chodesh—for the matter that he need not return; and likewise for the matter that if he remembered before he began haTov vehaMetiv he says Baruch Atah Heaven Elokeinu Melech HaOlam asher natan mo'adim l'amo Yisrael l'sason ul'simcha and he does not conclude as on Rosh Chodesh.
(28) He does not return—as in every Rosh Chodesh when he forgot; and even though on that day it does not suffice without eating bread—that eating is because of Shabbat and not because of Rosh Chodesh, for eating bread on Rosh Chodesh there is no obligation—and that of Shabbat behold he mentioned.
(29) And if he forgot Shabbat—as it should read; and so is the text in Shulchan Aruch of the Tamid offering; and if he forgot Shabbat alone certainly he need not include also Rosh Chodesh in this berachah since he already mentioned Rosh Chodesh in yaaleh veyavo; and in any case if he did not remember until after he opened in haTov vehaMetiv and returns to the head of birkat hamazon on account of the mention of Shabbat—then he must say again also yaaleh veyavo, for the first birkat hamazon was entirely nullified.
(30) And he does not conclude with that of Rosh Chodesh—as above that there is no conclusion on Rosh Chodesh; and even though here he mentions the Name in the conclusion anyway because of Shabbat—nevertheless it is not fitting to mention, for the mention of the Name is about both of them; and in any case for the law the Acharonim conclude he mentions in the conclusion also Rosh Chodesh—for the reason that anyway many poskim hold that also on every Rosh Chodesh one must conclude in the berachah as on Yom Tov; and even though we do not practice like them because of concern for a berachah not needed—here where anyway he concludes in the berachah on account of Shabbat it is fine to say mekadesh HaShabbat veYisrael veRoshei Chodashim.
