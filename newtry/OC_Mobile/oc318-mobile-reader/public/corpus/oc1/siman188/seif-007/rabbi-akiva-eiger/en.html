Seif 7. Its law is like that of Rosh Chodesh. See Lechem Chamudos, who writes that Chol HaMoed is likewise treated like Rosh Chodesh in this respect, and that before beginning HaTov VeHaMeitiv one must recite a separate blessing, even though the poskim did not explicitly mention it. See there. Or Zarua, cited there in Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah, writes likewise. He adds, however, that one should not say, “Blessed is He Who gave Yom Tov,” because Chol HaMoed is not called Yom Tov. Rather, he should say, “Blessed is He Who gave days to Israel for rejoicing and gladness.”
