Seif 7: its law is like Rosh Chodesh. See Levushei Serad that also in this Chol HaMoed is like Rosh Chodesh—that one must mention in a berachah by itself before he began haTov vehaMetiv even though the poskim did not mention it so—see there. And likewise Eshel Avraham there in Shakhnehag writes only that one should not say Baruch shenatan Yom Tov for Chol HaMoed is not called Yom Tov; rather he should say Baruch shenatan yamim to Israel for joy and gladness.
