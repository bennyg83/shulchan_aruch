To Israel, etc. Lechem Chamudot wrote: in Tur he wrote leYisrael and Acharonim followed him, and not so in Talmud and Rif and Rambam but le'amo Yisrael, until here his language. And so it appears to me to practice le'amo Yisrael—for so I saw in Knesset HaGedolah, Semag, Semak, Raavan, Rosh, Agudah, Rabbenu Yerucham, Sefer Tzeda Laderech, and R' David Abudraham; and likewise I found that Agur quoted Tur itself; and likewise written in Kitzur Piskei Rosh that the Tur compiled; and per this, perforce there is a scribal error in the Turim before us and the word le'amo was omitted, or he relied on the law of Shabbat that he wrote earlier.
