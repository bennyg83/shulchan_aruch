Mentions—Taz. In Beit Yosef there is omission in the print and its law is to make havdalah first with "who distinguishes" (mention of Shabbat unlike the text) in birkat hamazon etc. and he raised the ruling that one mentions both (thus one who did not pray Maariv; but if he prayed Maariv even though he did not make havdalah—like Rosh Chodesh and Purim that if he prayed Maariv he does not mention that of Purim and Rosh Chodesh—Magen Avraham siman 695 s.k. 9). And it appears even per his words specifically if he mentioned Shabbat first he mentions afterward that of Rosh Chodesh and similarly; unlike if he erred and mentioned that of Rosh Chodesh first—how will he mention Shabbat afterward for they contradict each other—for he made Rosh Chodesh now it is not Shabbat? And kiddush Yom Tov and havdalah of Shabbat—for thus is kedushat Shabbat vs. this; and siman 684 in Taz s.k. 4 regarding frequent and infrequent if one acts—infrequent does not precede; and so here; and it is possible there is no mentioning God's Name in vain as Magen Avraham s.k. 11 wrote; and it will be explained there further. And Magen Avraham s.k. 18 has a different path in this and it will be explained there.

And if his meal extended until Maariv and Yom Kippur—whether he did not mention Shabbat or Yom Tov until he opened haTov vehaMetiv he does not return to the head—for we do not decide from doubt—Magen Avraham s.k. 18; if so doubt of berachah to be lenient. And if he remembered before haTov vehaMetiv he says Baruch shenatan Shabbatot limnucha without Name and malchut; but one may say in this it is fitting—Levush z"l to rely on him that he says with Name and malchut for he holds on Rosh Chodesh so—and it is like a doubt according to what is written in the previous s.k. And it will be explained in Magen Avraham s.k. 10 further. Magen Avraham s.k. 17—in Levush it should read "but" see there.

What is written ro'einu zonenenu—both with cholam: the Name-title "shepherd" [ro'eh] with necessities bread zonenenu with cholam; permitted fruits and they request thus parnasenu with necessities vechalkelenu with luxuries; or chalkeil with necessities as "and Joseph sustained [vayechalkeil] his brothers" etc. However we say ro'einu with cholam and afterward in this request zonenenu with melafumim—eating, livelihood, clothing, and other things; and we sustain etc. See Ma'amar Nisyon in root kul language of burden or order—they request orderly livelihood not many times and sometimes none; and Magen Avraham s.k. 2 wrote there on this. And in Sefer HaMagid we wrote in Tehillim 23 "Heaven is my shepherd I shall not lack" when Heaven ro'i as father to son in reduction and not luxuries as Taz wrote ro'einu means necessities alone as pavement "bread with salt and water" siman 170 s.k. 2—then I shall not lack anything in the world—understand.
