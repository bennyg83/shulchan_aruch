If Rosh Chodesh falls on Shabbos, etc. The Tur writes: “My brother, Rabbi Yechiel, wrote that some disagree with this ruling, and I incline toward their view. His proof is from a day on which one is obligated to recite four prayers. Here too, since he cannot avoid eating, he must repeat Bircas HaMazon.” The Tur therefore rules here unlike Rabbeinu Yonah. It appears that his reason is that even on Yom Kippur one is obligated to mention Shabbos in Ne’ilah only initially. After the fact, if he did not mention Shabbos in Ne’ilah, he likewise does not repeat the prayer for that reason, as Beit Yosef writes in siman 623 that Hagahos Mordechai records a dispute concerning this matter. In cases of doubt concerning blessings, the ruling is lenient. Rabbeinu Yonah, however, holds that there too he must repeat the prayer.
