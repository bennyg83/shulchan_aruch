If Rosh Chodesh fell on Shabbat etc. In Tur it is written, and my brother R' Yechiel wrote there are disputants in this and to this my opinion inclines; and proof is from the day one became obligated in four prayers—so too here since it does not suffice without eating he must return—end of his words. Here he ruled not like Mahari; and his reason appears: also on Yom Kippur one is not obligated on Ne'ilah to mention Shabbat except l'chatchila; but b'dieved if he did not mention Shabbat on Ne'ilah likewise he does not return on account of this; and as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 482 that there is dispute in this in Hagahot Maimoniyot and doubt of berachot to be lenient; but Mahari holds also there he returns.
