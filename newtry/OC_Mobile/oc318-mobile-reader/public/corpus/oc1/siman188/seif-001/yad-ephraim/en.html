Magen Avraham s.k. 14: and also requires study; and see siman 188 s.k. 18 as it should read; and the intent is in seif 8 there in name of Terumat HaDeshen that he should not include on account of doubt even though he does not add Name and malchut—and this answers his difficulty that he challenged: what harm is there when he also says veRoshei Chodashim; and see below on daf 88b printed in margin of Even HaEzer (and what is written there siman 94 is a scribal error and the matters belong here) who challenged Magen Avraham from words of Hagahot Maimoniyot that Beit Yosef brought etc.; and the reason of the matter is explained in Terumat HaDeshen siman 144 that Beit Yosef brought in siman 208 etc.—see there; and in Machatzit HaShekel it appears he did not notice the scribal error in Magen Avraham—for if he had noticed, behold Magen Avraham himself showed the place to siman 208; and what Machatzit HaShekel wrote "I did not find this reasoning"—it appears he means it was not mentioned only in Hagahot Maimoniyot alone and he abbreviated since his intent was on the main source of the law in Beit Yosef; and see Ateret Zekenim from words of Levush and Levushei Serad on this and Eshel Avraham; and see Birkhei Yosef what he wrote on this. And newly printed Sefer Machatzit HaShekel wrote that what Magen Avraham wrote "and see siman 101" means to say the word veRoshei Chodashim is not considered an interruption—see there; and this is astounding—for what concern of interruption applies here when today is Rosh Chodesh and there is no interruption here; and all the more in berachat haTov vehaMetiv which is a berachah by itself d'rabbanan and he interrupts in it with the word Amen. And also in what he brought in Even HaEzer from siman 208 he wrote that there too without reason it is as Terumat HaDeshen wrote—that there is regarding including in the middle (and so Rabbeinu Yonah to distinguish in this etc.—see there); and all he wrote is because he did not notice the scribal error in Magen Avraham as stated; and also what Machatzit HaShekel wrote a scribal error in Hagahot Maimoniyot is not clear in my humble opinion.
