It appears in Taz—commentary on our custom that they hold Abaye and Rav Ashi do not disagree in Berakhot 45b but rather each one acted as he did, and so Beit Yosef and Perishah. And in responsum of Rashi siman 44 he also wrote that nowadays there are no workers etc.—see there. He also wrote that one should not say Boneh Yerushalayim Amen immediately but should wait a bit and then say Amen so it should not appear that Amen is part of the conclusion of the berachah. And this is within kedei dibbur as speech is judged; but one should not pause a long time for then it is an orphaned Amen—see siman 124 in Magen Avraham letter yod-daled. And see Darishah on this. And they answer Amen immediately after Yerushalayim.
