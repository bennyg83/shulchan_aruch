There in seif 6: he erred and did not mention Shabbat. NB: I was in doubt: if he blessed birkat hamazon on Shabbat and does not remember whether he mentioned Shabbat in it or not—must he return or not? And behold the place of this doubt per Shulchan Aruch Laws of Rosh Chodesh siman 422: in doubt whether he mentioned yaaleh veyavo on Rosh Chodesh he need not return, because he does not pass thirty days without mention; and Magen Avraham wrote there that from this, likewise in doubt whether he prayed Shabbat he need not return and pray again—only he pushes off later, for Shabbat is different since it is a berachah by itself; but in birkat hamazon there is no berachah by itself, only a mention like yaaleh veyavo in prayer on Rosh Chodesh; therefore we say presumption that he mentioned, since he mentions so every Shabbat; and therefore even per Taz who disputes there, and likewise Magen Avraham, this is specifically if it is not literally from thirty days to thirty days, such as Rosh Chodesh where the matter is not so recognizable and perhaps it left his heart that it was Rosh Chodesh; but on Shabbat, which is very recognizable, as is known: when Shabbat is upon him—and the same here—one can always say presumption that he mentioned Shabbat; study well and requires study for the law now.
