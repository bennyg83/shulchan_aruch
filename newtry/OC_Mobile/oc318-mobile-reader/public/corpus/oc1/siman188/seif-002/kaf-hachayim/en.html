(2) [Seif 2] Rama: It appears specifically when blessing alone, etc. So too Moreinu z"l in Darkei Moshe ot 2: this distinction is to settle the custom that they answer this amen aloud—see there. But per Maran z"l there is no distinction, and whether blessing for himself or to discharge others he must say it quietly. So too Bach—see there; however there in Darkei Moshe he gave another reason for the custom to say it aloud: specifically in their times when workers went to their melacha there was concern they would treat it lightly, unlike now—see there. So too Bach, and Magen Avraham brought it s.k.1. Nevertheless Bach concluded it is good to say it calmly and not very loudly, for there is concern of treating lightly—see there. And so Taz s.k.1: one should not protest those who do not practice saying it in a whisper—see there.
