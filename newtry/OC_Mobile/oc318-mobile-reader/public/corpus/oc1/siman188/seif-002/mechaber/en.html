This Amen should be said quietly, so that people do not notice that HaTov veHaMeitiv is not Torah-level and come to treat it lightly. {Rama: Rema: It seems this applies specifically when one is blessing alone and no one answers Amen after the other blessings; but when there is a zimun, others answer Amen just as they do for the earlier blessings, and even though the one blessing also answers, it is not so noticeable since others are answering along with him. This is the custom in these lands to say it aloud, even the one blessing himself when there is a zimun, and perhaps this is the reason.}
