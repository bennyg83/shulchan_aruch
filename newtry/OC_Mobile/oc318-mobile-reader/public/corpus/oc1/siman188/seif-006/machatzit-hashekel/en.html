(seif katan6) And he does not, etc: and that, etc. that he mentions - meaning Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: if Yom Tov fell on Shabbos he mentions that of Shabbos in Yaaleh VeYavo even though he already mentioned Shabbos in Atah bechartanu.
Since he is about to, etc: meaning Rabbeinu Yonah concedes; and if Rosh Chodesh fell on Shabbos he does not mention Shabbos in Yaaleh VeYavo as written there - since he already concluded that of Shabbos he is not fit to mention it again, unlike Yom Tov that fell on Shabbos - for when he says Yaaleh VeYavo he has not yet concluded and is still about to conclude with that of Shabbos; if so he has not yet removed himself from mention of Shabbos - therefore he should mention Shabbos in Yaaleh VeYavo; and here is similar to Rosh Chodesh that fell on Shabbos: even though he did not conclude, nevertheless since he already finished Retzei he already removed himself from mention of Shabbos; and even though he will say haRacheman He will bequeath to us the day that is entirely Shabbos - behold all these haRacheman are only a mere custom; and from the words of the Gemara it suffices in blessing of HaTov VeHaMeitiv; and per the Midrash one says three expressions of recompense and three expressions of goodness and no more - and the rest is a mere custom.
