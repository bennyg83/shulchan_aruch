(s.k.6) And he does not, etc.—and that, etc. that he mentions—meaning Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: if Yom Tov fell on Shabbat he mentions that of Shabbat in yaaleh veyavo even though he already mentioned Shabbat in Atah bechartanu.

Since he is about to, etc.—meaning Rabbeinu Yonah concedes; and if Rosh Chodesh fell on Shabbat he does not mention Shabbat in yaaleh veyavo as written there—since he already concluded that of Shabbat he is not fit to mention it again, unlike Yom Tov that fell on Shabbat—for when he says yaaleh veyavo he has not yet concluded and is still about to conclude with that of Shabbat; if so he has not yet removed himself from mention of Shabbat—therefore he should mention Shabbat in yaaleh veyavo; and here is similar to Rosh Chodesh that fell on Shabbat: even though he did not conclude, nevertheless since he already finished retzeh he already removed himself from mention of Shabbat; and even though he will say haRachaman He will bequeath to us the day that is entirely Shabbat—behold all these haRachaman are only a mere custom; and from the words of the Talmud it suffices in berachat haTov vehaMetiv; and per the Midrash one says three gemulim and three hativot and no more—and the rest is a mere custom.
