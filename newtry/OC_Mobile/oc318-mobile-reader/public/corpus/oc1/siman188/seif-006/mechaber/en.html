If one forgot to mention Shabbat, one says: Baruch Atah Hashem Elokeinu Melech HaOlam asher natan Shabbatot limnucha l'amo Yisrael b'ahavah l'ot ul'brit, Baruch Atah Hashem mekadesh HaShabbat. If one forgot to mention Yom Tov, one says: Baruch Atah Hashem Elokeinu Melech HaOlam asher natan yamim tovim l'Yisrael l'sason ul'simcha et yom chag ploni hazeh, Baruch Atah Hashem mekadesh Yisrael vehaZmanim. If Yom Tov falls on Shabbat, one says: asher natan Shabbatot limnucha l'amo Yisrael b'ahavah l'ot ul'brit, viyamim tovim l'sason ul'simcha et yom chag ploni hazeh, Baruch Atah Hashem mekadesh HaShabbat veYisrael vehaZmanim. All these blessings include God's name and kingship. This remedy of a supplementary blessing applies only if one remembered before beginning HaTov veHaMeitiv; but if one did not remember until after beginning HaTov veHaMeitiv, one must return to the beginning of Grace After Meals.
