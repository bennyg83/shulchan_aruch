(s.k.4) The formula, etc. on Shabbat uveneh—meaning they do not wish to say uvaneh Yerushalayim, for building of the Temple does not override Shabbat; and therefore it is foolish, for we say in Sukkah daf 41 that future building of the Temple overrides Yom Tov and Shabbat; and at night—thus Rashi: and if it is difficult, etc. that there is no building of the Temple at night, etc. this refers to building built by human hands; but the future Temple that we await—its building and completion He will reveal and bring from Heaven—end of Rashi. And he also wrote in Beit Shmuel mentioned that in any case one can say the formula of berachot is so, as in Yerushalim—see there.
