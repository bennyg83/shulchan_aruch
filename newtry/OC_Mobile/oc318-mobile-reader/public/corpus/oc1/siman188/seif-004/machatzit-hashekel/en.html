(seif katan4) The formula, etc. on Shabbos uveneh: meaning they do not wish to say uvaneh Yerushalayim, for building of the Temple does not override Shabbos; and therefore it is foolish, for we say in Sukkah folio 41 that future building of the Temple overrides Yom Tov and Shabbos; and at night - thus Rashi: and if it is difficult, etc. that there is no building of the Temple at night, etc. this refers to building built by human hands; but the future Temple that we await - its building and completion He will reveal and bring from Hashem - end of Rashi. And he also wrote in Beit Shmuel mentioned that in any case one can say the formula of blessings is so, as in Yerushalim - see there.
