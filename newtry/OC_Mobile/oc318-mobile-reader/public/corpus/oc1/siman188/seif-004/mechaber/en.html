The text of this blessing opens with Rachem Hashem Elokeinu or Nachameinu Hashem Elokeinu and concludes with boneh Yerushalayim or menachem Tzion b'vinyan Yerushalayim. One should not change the text between Shabbat and weekdays, as the same text is recited both on Shabbat and on weekdays. {Rama: Rema: Some say one recites boneh berachamav Yerushalayim, and this is the custom. (Mordechai, end of Berakhot)}
