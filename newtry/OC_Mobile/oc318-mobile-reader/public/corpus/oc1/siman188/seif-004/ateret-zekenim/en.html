And some say we say Boneh Yerushalayim b'rahamav and so is custom. It appears the reason: since he began with mercy he concludes with mercy—for conclusion resembles opening; not as all in Him who say one should not say because written Zion in judgment shall be redeemed—and they erred; specifically redemption shall be in judgment; but to be built and established in her honor more than First Temple is with mercy.
