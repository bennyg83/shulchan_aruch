The formula, etc. Mahari Giinzburg said Boneh Yerushalayim beRachamim (38 [letters]); and in Bereshit Rabbah parashat Ekev that Rav R. Yitzchak son of Rav z"l would conclude Blessed Heaven Heaven of David and Boneh Yerushalayim—and see ch. 2 of Beitzah: Beit Yosef wrote one should say zonenenu with shuruk in the meter of shovenu Elokei yisheinu as Shlomo wrote; and Radak that now it is not the Name-title but he prays that He will sustain us; and also ro'einu should be explained per this as "pasture us" with missing vav and shin for resh; and in winter days I heard that grammarians say so—but in all books it is written ro'einu with vav—end of his words. And in Darkei Moshe he wrote in name of Or Zaru'a: some say ro'einu—see there Heaven is my shepherd I shall not lack; therefore one should not depart from the custom—and so Maharam; but Mateh Moshe wrote to say on Shabbat—and so in Shlah and Mordechai; and even though it is forbidden for a person to request his needs on Shabbat—here it is different because the formula of berachot is so (Tur).

The formula from Shabbat. And those who say on Shabbat uveneh Yerushalayim—it is a foolish custom [Beit Shmuel end of the book].
