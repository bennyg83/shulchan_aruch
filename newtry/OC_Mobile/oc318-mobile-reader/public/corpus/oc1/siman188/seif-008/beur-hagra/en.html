Seif 8: A meal, etc. Tosafos in Berachos there, s.v. “if he wishes,” etc., and “a meal,” etc. In a case of doubt, the ruling is lenient. See below, siman 291, seif 5. This follows Rabbeinu Tam’s interpretation that bread is not required for the third meal. According to those who require bread for the third meal, however, its law is the same as that of the other Shabbos meals.
