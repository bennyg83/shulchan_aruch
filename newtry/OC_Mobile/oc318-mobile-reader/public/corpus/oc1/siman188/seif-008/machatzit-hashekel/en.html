(seif katan8) To the head, etc. are considered as one: meaning the difficulty should not arise: that which we hold in siman 422 if he forgot Yaaleh VeYavo in prayer - if he is accustomed to say supplications after his prayer and remembered before he uprooted his feet - he does not return to the beginning but returns to Retzei; if so, why is blessing of HaTov VeHaMeitiv worse than saying supplications after his prayer that he should not return except to blessing of Rachem which is the first blessing in which it is fitting to mention, like Rosh Chodesh where he returns to Retzei; therefore he wrote that all three blessings are considered like one blessing - therefore he must return to the beginning of Bircas HaMazon.
However only to HaTov VeHaMeitiv - for blessing of HaTov VeHaMeitiv is not considered as one with the three blessings before it - for afterward in Yavneh they enacted it.
