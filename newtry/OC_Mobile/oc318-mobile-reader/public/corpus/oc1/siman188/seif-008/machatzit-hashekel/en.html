(s.k.8) To the head, etc. are considered as one—meaning the difficulty should not arise: that which we hold in siman 422 if he forgot yaaleh veyavo in prayer—if he is accustomed to say supplications after his prayer and remembered before he uprooted his feet—he does not return to the beginning but returns to retzeh; if so, why is berachat haTov vehaMetiv worse than saying supplications after his prayer that he should not return except to berachat Racham which is the first berachah in which it is fitting to mention, like Rosh Chodesh where he returns to retzeh; therefore he wrote that all three berachot are considered like one berachah—therefore he must return to the head of birkat hamazon.

However only to haTov vehaMetiv—for berachat haTov vehaMetiv is not considered as one with the three berachot before it—for afterward in Yavneh they enacted it.
