Seif 8. Its law is like that of Rosh Chodesh. If one ate the second Shabbos meal close to nightfall, forgot to mention Shabbos, and did not remember until after reciting Havdalah, one may say that he does not repeat Bircas HaMazon, since he has already recited Havdalah. See above, siman 118, seif 11. Even if he remembered before reciting Havdalah, the author of Kisei DeHarsena, siman 87, holds that he does not repeat Bircas HaMazon.
