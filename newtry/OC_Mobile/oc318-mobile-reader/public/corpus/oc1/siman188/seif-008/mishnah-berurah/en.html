(31) Its law is like Rosh Chodesh—that he does not return if he began berachat haTov vehaMetiv, because some say the third meal does not require bread; and even though their words are not the essence as the Mechaber wrote in siman 291 seif 5—nevertheless one should be concerned for their words not to enter into doubt of berachah in vain. And in the third meal on Yom Tov for Darkei HaEmunah he does not return—for for Darkei HaEmunah it is only optional as above in s.k. 26; and in any case if he remembered before he began berachat haTov vehaMetiv—even in a fourth or fifth meal on Shabbat and Yom Tov he says Baruch Atah Heaven Elokeinu Melech HaOlam asher natan Shabbatot limnucha etc. or asher natan yamim tovim etc. as one says on Rosh Chodesh—even though he did not need bread at all that day.
