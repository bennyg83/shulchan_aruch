On Shabbat its law. And on Yom Tov specifically one meal of night and one of day (Teshuvot haBatat siman 110)—see siman 529.

Its law is like Rosh Chodesh. Even though l'chatchila he is obligated to eat bread as written siman 291—nevertheless since some say he is not obligated to eat bread—and if so he is not obligated to return—and doubt of berachot to be lenient; and if he ate a fourth meal on Shabbat he does not return to the head (Tosafot)—it implies from their language that if he did not open in haTov vehaMetiv he says Blessed etc. like Rosh Chodesh.
