Shulchan Aruch seif 6: he says Blessed are You, Heaven. Even though if he had mentioned them in their place there would not be [a berachah formula] with Name [and malchut]—because when he mentions it for the adjacent berachah behold they are included in the berachah that has malchut in its opening and conclusion; for opening and conclusion of the first includes all of them. But since he finished Boneh Yerushalayim and says it as a berachah by itself it is not adjacent to its companion since it is only incidental—see Beit Yosef.
