(8) There in Rama: but I have not seen it practiced. And also Tosafot Berakhot 49a wrote that some practice saying malchut in Boneh Yerushalayim—see there. And all the more in yaaleh veyavo, which is a matter by itself; therefore one should not protest those who practice so. Also Levush omitted this Rama. Magen Avraham s.k.2. And so Taz s.k.2: one should not skip the word melech in yaaleh veyavo—see there. And so Av Beit Din ot 3, and Maharikash z"l: specifically in those that are from the formula of the berachah there is stringency; but in yaaleh veyavo which is not from the berachah formula one need not be stringent; on the contrary if he omits it, Heaven forbid, and does not say as in prayer—it appears he rejects this because of that, Heaven forbid, until here; and Magen Avraham brought it in Hagahat Taz.
