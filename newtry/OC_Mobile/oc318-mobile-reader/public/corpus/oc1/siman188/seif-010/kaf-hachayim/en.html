(10) There: He opens with Racham, etc., or nechemenu, etc. And in Edom they practice whether weekday or Shabbat to say Racham, and in conclusion say Blessed are You Heaven Boneh Yerushalayim beRachamav, and one should not change at all to say Menachem Tzion in Boneh Yerushalayim—for perhaps this too would be a conclusion in two. And so Maharshal who received from his rabbis. Bach. Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahat Beit Yosef ot 6—and likewise from words of the Ari z"l that there is no difference in this berachah's formula between weekday and Shabbat—see in Shaarei Mitzvot parashat Ekev and Shaar Hakavanot siman 61 and Know ot 3, see there. And so in siman Hakavanot of Rashash z"l: beginning of berachah Racham and conclusion Boneh Yerushalayim, whether weekday or Shabbat. And so is widespread custom; and see below ot 12.
