(s.k.10) And Rosh Chodesh its law, etc.—this is Rambam's opinion, for we say we do not decree a fast on the community on Rosh Chodesh, Hanukkah, and Purim; and if they began (meaning they decreed some days of fasting and it did not enter their mind that within those days Rosh Chodesh, Hanukkah, and Purim would occur, and they began to fast on weekday—even one day)—they do not interrupt and fast also on Rosh Chodesh, Hanukkah, and Purim. Rambam wrote: likewise Rosh Chodesh its law is so—that if they began they do not interrupt; apparently he holds the obligation of eating on Rosh Chodesh is not more than on Rosh Chodesh; and likewise here.

See there siman 270 that nevertheless l'chatchila it is a mitzvah to eat bread on Rosh Chodesh.
