Blessed are You Heaven Elokeinu Melech haOlam Who gave roshei chadashim, etc. In Shulchan Aruch he wrote one should say Blessed Who gave roshei chadashim, etc.—it implies he does not mention Name and malchut; and Knesset HaGedolah wondered to know the reason and agreed with Levush—see there. And I say: if Shulchan Aruch intended this reason—that on Rosh Chodesh it is optional whether to eat, we rely on Raavad whom Tur brought, that these berachot are without Name and malchut since they are not fixed. And in Rashi for what I explained above in the words of Rif and Semag and Kol Bo—that in the opening there is no Name and malchut in any of them; but I toiled and found in Agudah, Rokeach, Semak, Rabbenu Yerucham, Shibolei Leket daf 22, Abudraham, Sefer Tzeda Laderech, Shaltiel Gibborim, and a tanna—all wrote like Levush; and they are worthy to rely upon.
