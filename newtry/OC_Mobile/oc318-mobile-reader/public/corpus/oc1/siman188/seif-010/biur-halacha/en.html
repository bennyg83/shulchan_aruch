Mentioning Shabbat in birkat hamazon—see Mishna Berurah. Nevertheless if he forgot to mention and remembered before opening haTov vehaMetiv—it appears to me he does not bless Blessed Who gave, etc., per what Magen Avraham wrote siman 471 s.k.14—see there; for also the Mechaber in this siman who rules we follow the beginning of the meal—this too is doubtful to him; certainly one does not bless from doubt any berachah with Name and malchut—and we wrote this per our ruling above that on Rosh Chodesh too one must bless with Name and malchut; however per what other Acharonim wrote that also on Rosh Chodesh, and likewise the third Shabbat meal—even when he blesses intentionally—he does not mention Name and malchut—in all this there is no practical difference: certainly even now it is permitted to bless without Name and malchut.
