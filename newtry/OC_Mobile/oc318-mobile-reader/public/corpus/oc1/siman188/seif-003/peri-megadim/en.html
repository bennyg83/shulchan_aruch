Opens—Taz. That which they conclude boneh berachamav Yerushalayim even though it is written "Zion shall be redeemed through justice"—that Israel should do judgment between a person and his fellow and do charity as he wrote "and its captive through righteousness"—end of his words. And Shulchan Shelomo wrote that what is said "through justice" is the redemption, but what will be built is more honorably than the first through mercy—see Taz.
