One should not mention, etc. Rama wrote: and some say that even when saying yaaleh veyavo he should not conclude melech chanun verachum but should skip the word melech—and this is sound reasoning; but I have not seen it practiced, until here. And Levush whom he omitted—it appears because he left them the custom of Israel Torah; unlike what I now saw some people changing our custom; and likewise Taz and Magen Avraham wrote to say it because it is a matter by itself. It also seems to me there is another reason not to say melech in Boneh Yerushalayim—because it is an adjacent berachah; and therefore since they mention it in Shemoneh Esrei which is also an adjacent berachah, they did not want to change in birkat hamazon lest they err and not say it in prayer too, thinking they skip it because it is an adjacent berachah; and truly in Tosafot daf 49 it is explained that the reason of not equating earthly kingdom, etc., is not accepted at all; and likewise Rashba whom Beit Yosef brought. And what Rashba challenged—behold they mention melech many times in adjacent berachot, thus they are not considered kingdom—to answer: Boneh Yerushalayim is different, for one must mention heavenly malchut since he mentioned earthly malchut—lest he err that this is considered complete kingdom and say kingdom even in other adjacent berachot; but in yaaleh veyavo which is not common he will not err; and moreover they know it is not considered kingdom from what they mention it in Shemoneh Esrei in an adjacent berachah.
