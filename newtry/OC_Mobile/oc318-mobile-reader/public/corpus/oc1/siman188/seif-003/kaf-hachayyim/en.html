5) [Seif 3] In the third blessing, one must mention the kingdom of the House of David, etc. See above, siman 187, seif 4, and our words there, paragraph 20, with the help of Heaven.
6) There: One who says, 'And Your kingdom and the kingdom of the House of David,' etc. Rather, he should say, 'And the kingdom of the House of David, Your servant, Your anointed.' Tur; Bach.
7) There: Likewise, one should not say in it, 'Our Father, our King.' Rather, he should say, 'Our Father, our Shepherd.' Levush; Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 5.
8) There, gloss: I have not seen the practice follow this. Tosafos in Berachos 49a likewise write that some practice mentioning kingship in Boneh Yerushalayim; see there. All the more so in Yaaleh VeYavo, which is a separate matter. Therefore, one should not protest against those who follow this practice. Levush also omits this gloss. Magen Avraham, paragraph 2. Taz, paragraph 2, likewise holds that one should not omit the word 'King' from Yaaleh VeYavo; see there. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 3, writes the same. R' Yaakov Castro, of blessed memory, likewise writes that concern applies specifically to words that are part of the blessing's text. Yaaleh VeYavo is not part of that text, so there is no concern. On the contrary, Heaven forbid, if he diminishes it and does not recite it as he does in prayer, it appears that he rejects one because of the other. Yad Aharon cites this in Hagahos HaTur.
