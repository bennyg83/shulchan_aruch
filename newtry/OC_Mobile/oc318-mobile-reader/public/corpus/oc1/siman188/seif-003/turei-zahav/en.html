But I have not seen practiced so. In my humble opinion I have seen a sound reason for this: that which was fitting to mention malchut shamayim in this berachah is only because he mentions malchut beit David—it is fitting to mention there also malchut shamayim; but it is not proper conduct to equate them together—that is, to say adjacent to malchut beit David malchut shamayim; and so in Asheri alongside it—and so he wrote: rather they did not wish to mention malchut shamayim adjacent to malchut beit David—end of his words. And so in Tur siman 189; but far from it there is no impediment—that is, in that matter where he mentions a request for malchut beit David one need not mention adjacent to it a little; therefore one should not say Avinu Malkeinu which is also adjacent to him; for what is written "and on the great house" etc. is one matter with malchut beit David for this depends on that; but in El melech chanun etc. it is far from it and not of this request; and if so they should have enacted malchut shamayim in another place far from it—for thereby we gain nothing, for berachat haAretz also they did not enact except they relied on what the adjacent berachah was, and the first berachah has malchut in it; and the same in berachat Racham—except it was fitting to mention it again adjacent to malchut beit David and to request on it, only it is not proper conduct; and far from it there is no need; and if one mentions it there is no lack of honor above; and nevertheless they enacted in berachat haTov vehaMetiv to complete corresponding to this—to teach that it was fitting to mention adjacent to malchut beit David—as appears to my humble opinion.
