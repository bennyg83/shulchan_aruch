(3) Any other kingdom—that is such as that which the Mechaber concludes below:

(4) And one who says etc.—it should read therefore one who says—and so is in Tur:

(5) Avinu Malkeinu—rather he should say Avinu ro'einu; and even though he does not say it together literally with malchut beit David, nevertheless since it is in one berachah it is not proper conduct to mention malchut shamayim alongside malchut of flesh and blood.

(6) But I have not seen etc.—and the Acharonim wrote a reason for this: since it is a matter entirely by itself they were not strict about this and say it as arranged in the prayer of eighteen—therefore one should not protest those who practice so.
