Seif 8: its law is like Rosh Chodesh. And if he ate the second meal close to nightfall and forgot to mention Shabbat and did not remember until after he made havdalah on Motza'ei Shabbat—it appears he does not return and bless since he already made havdalah—see above siman 118 seif 11. And even if he remembered before he made havdalah—the opinion of Ba'al Kisei DeHarsana siman 87 is that he does not return and bless.
