(12) He says it therein—for this mention and likewise in yaaleh veyavo are requests for mercy; they fixed them in berachat Boneh Yerushalayim which is also a request for mercy, and not in berachat haAretz which is thanksgiving; and if he mentioned them in berachat haAretz he did not fulfill and must return and mention in Boneh Yerushalayim.

(13) And afterward yaaleh veyavo—because Shabbat is frequent and we hold everywhere frequent precedes; and b'dieved if he switched he certainly fulfilled.

(14) In yaaleh veyavo—for he already mentioned Shabbat in Retzei—why should he return and mention it again.

(15) In Retzei veHachalitzeinu—for they enacted for it a separate formula yaaleh veyavo—why should he double it twice.
