On Shabbat one adds Retzei veHachalitzeinu, and on Rosh Chodesh, Yom Tov, and Chol HaMoed one says Ya'aleh veYavo. If one of these falls on Shabbat, one says Retzei veHachalitzeinu and then Ya'aleh veYavo, and one does not mention Shabbat within Ya'aleh veYavo, nor Yom Tov, Chol HaMoed, or Rosh Chodesh within Retzei veHachalitzeinu.
