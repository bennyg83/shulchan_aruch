And it requires study—for Rosh's words contradict each other; and it is possible that in Bava Metzia he relied on another reason he wrote there; and it appears to me that wherever the dispute of the Sages is on his side we hold like the Sages, but in ch. Makom sheNahagu the dispute is not on his side but only after several mishnayot—therefore it is called a complete Stam and preferable to the Sages; and in Niddah Rosh brings proof to amplify the matter as stated.
