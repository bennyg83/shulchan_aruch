If—Taz. Likewise Levush seif 8: who will say the Name returns etc.—see there. And in siman 482 he wrote the cantor that if he did not mention on Ne'ilah in the middle of prayer Shabbat he does not return—behold an individual who did not mention at all does not explain what his law is (see Perishah and what Eshel Avraham wrote). And Taz holds simply that on Ne'ilah even an individual who did not mention Shabbat at all on Ne'ilah does not return for it is l'chatchila, and as Beit Yosef wrote there that some dispute. And Magen Avraham on siman 482 s.k. 2 deduced from language of Shulchan Aruch with the words "in this"—specifically in the middle of prayer; but if he did not mention at all he returns on Ne'ilah; and so is Beit Yosef's opinion on siman 482 who wrote regarding the cantor: if he did not mention in the middle one may rely etc.; but if he did not mention at all he returns there. And Darkei Moshe printed in Florence brought this there and so wrote Magen Avraham there. And here Magen Avraham wrote s.k. 12 and 11 and it will be explained there.
