(seif katan9) He is not obligated, etc: one can say fruits - even though it is forbidden to fast on Rosh Chodesh - nevertheless bread that he is obligated in Bircas HaMazon he need not eat on Rosh Chodesh.
And from the Stam, etc: meaning in Sukkah folio 27 we learned: R' Eliezer [says] fourteen meals a person is obligated to eat in the sukkah in seven days - one by day and one by night; and the Sages say there is no fixed measure except on the first night of the festival we derive fifteen fifteen from chag hamatzot: just as there one night is obligatory and from then on optional - so too in the sukkah. And Tosafos challenged: behold we say in Berakhot that if he forgot Yaaleh VeYavo in Bircas HaMazon on Yom Tov he returns to the beginning - for it does not suffice without eating bread; and it should be said that in Berakhot where they say he returns - it refers only to the first night of Pesach and the first night of Sukkos; but from the unqualified wording of Rabbeinu Yonah it is implied that he holds on Yom Tov whether by day or by night, and also on the last day of Yom Tov he returns; and the difficulty of Tosafos is difficult for him; perforce one must say he holds like the answer of the Ran that Magen Avraham brought.
And in the sukkah however more than a kebeitzah: meaning except for one night he may eat an olive's worth of bread whereby he fulfills the obligation of eating bread on Yom Tov; and he need not [eat] in the sukkah - for until more than a kebeitzah is called casual eating and he may eat outside the sukkah - unlike one night where he is obligated to eat in the sukkah and must eat more than a kebeitzah; and in the sukkah - or on one night, since he is obligated to eat in the sukkah, even a kezayis is called kevi'a and it is forbidden to eat outside the sukkah; therefore he fulfills when he eats a kezayis in the sukkah (and so we hold in siman 639 seif 3). They also wrote: if rains descended they may eat outside the sukkah - and on one night he must eat in the sukkah.
And Rabbeinu Yonah chapter 3 who ate, etc: know that Rabbeinu Yonah did not write their words to settle Tosafos's difficulty mentioned - but from their words perforce Tosafos's difficulty is settled; and that Rabbeinu Yonah wrote: in the third meal if he forgot Retzei he does not return - even though we say on Shabbos and Yom Tov it does not suffice without eating - that is specifically two meals, since he must make Kiddush and there is no Kiddush except in the place of a meal - therefore he must eat; but the third meal where he does not make Kiddush he need not eat bread - now; if so perforce Tosafos's difficulty is settled: that the first night of Pesach and Sukkos is different because their obligation is D'Oraisa, unlike other Yom Tov meals where the obligation is only D'Rabbanan because of Kiddush in the place of a meal.
And see Ra'avad seif 5 that some hold so - that he wrote in name of the Geonim: if he drank a cup of wine he fulfills Kiddush in the place of a meal; but in the Gemara we say it does not suffice without eating - and it implies bread that he is obligated in Bircas HaMazon; and how did he give the reason because of Kiddush in the place of a meal - here even with a cup of wine he fulfills.
