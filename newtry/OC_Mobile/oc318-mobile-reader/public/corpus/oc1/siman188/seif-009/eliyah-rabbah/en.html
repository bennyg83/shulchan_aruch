These matters when he remembered, etc. Levush Yom Tov wrote in name of derashot of Maharash: if he did not finish all berachot of Boneh Yerushalayim—for example he did not say Yerushalayim amen—he says retzeh and finishes Boneh Yerushalayim amen; and see siman 482; and likewise implied a little in Baal Halakhot Gedolot there.
