(9) [Seif 4] Formula of this berachah, etc. In Tur he wrote: and concludes Blessed are You Heaven Boneh Yerushalayim. And in Darkei Moshe end of tractate Berakhot he wrote that Maharam would say Boneh Yerushalayim beRachamav. However Kol Bo and Avnei Choshen wrote on Maharam's words above that it is not an exact formula, since Yerushalayim is built only in judgment, as written: Zion shall be redeemed through justice. And Bach in Beit Yosef wrote that if he said beRachamav they do not return him—see there. It follows that Maran z"l's opinion is like Tur, Kol Bo, and Avnei Choshen—that one should not say beRachamav but only Boneh Yerushalayim, and so are his words here in Shulchan Aruch. So too Maharikash, and Birkhei Yosef brought it ot 1—see there. And so in his order Simchat Regel—see there. And so the Gr"a ot 4, Maaseh Rav ot 88, Beit Yosef Even haEzer 5, Chasam Sofer ot 8, Ben Av Chai parashat Chukat ot 16. However Moreinu z"l in Darkei Moshe ot 1 wrote the custom is like Maharam—for what is written in judgment is redeemed does not mean except if Israel judges true judgment between man and his fellow, then Zion is redeemed, as written: and her captivity through righteousness, until here his words; and so are his words here in Hagahat Shulchan Aruch. And so Levush in seif 2, Mateh Moshe siman 405, Bach, Etz Chaim ot 1, Knesset HaGedolah in Hagahat Beit Yosef ot 1—and wrote: and so is primary, and so is practiced—see there. And see further below ot 13 and examine.
