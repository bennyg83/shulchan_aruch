Drying. Because the water on his hand did not become impure.
A revi'is. The Maharshal, Bach, and Taz wrote that specifically with immersion one does not require drying, but with washing, even with a revi'is, it does not help at all. See Knesses HaGedolah. One should not dry his hands on his garment, for it is harmful to memory; Tashbetz and Magen Avraham.
