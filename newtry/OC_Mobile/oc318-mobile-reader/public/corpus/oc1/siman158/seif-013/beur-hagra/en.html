Seif 13, one who immerses, etc. Tosefta at the beginning of the second chapter of Yadayim. The Beis Yosef wrote: it appears to me that the reason drying is required is because of tamei water, unlike immersing, where there is no tamei water, as written above in siman 162, seif 1. This is what he wrote: and the same applies, etc., and as written there: and so, etc., for one who washes, etc. This too is for the same reason, that there is no tamei water, as above. A proof is that second waters are not required, as written at the beginning of the second chapter of Yadayim: if one washed his hand, etc., until he washes with a revi'is. Tosafot in Chullin there and the Rosh there explained that this means even if they came from remnants of purity, for with one hand it helps if the rinsing is like two rinsings; but a revi'is always helps and does not require two times. See siman 162, seif 4. This is the wording of the Tosefta there: one who washes his hands should not say regarding the first waters, behold I am washing the tamei ones; and if he did so, he must dry his hands. But one who immerses his hands need not dry his hands. This requires study. Perhaps instead of tamei ones one must read: halves, as Rabbeinu Shimshon wrote at the beginning of Yadayim, that one who washes half his hand and then the other half must dry his hands.
