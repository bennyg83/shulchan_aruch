(46) Without drying, etc. — thus it is in the Tosefta; and Beit Yosef wrote that the reason is because in immersing there are no impure waters here at all, and therefore drying is not required; and from this we learn that the same applies when pouring a revi'it at once on both hands, or on each hand by itself — for we also hold the waters are pure [as below siman 162] — drying is not required. Rashal disagrees with this, for he holds that the essence of drying that they enacted is because of disgust, as above; and if so, what difference is there whether a revi'it at once or not — only in immersing they do not require drying, for immersion of the hands is like a Torah purity of immersing the body, and there certainly drying is not required; therefore also in immersion of the hands they did not enact drying — and in his words Bach also wrote in siman 165, and likewise other Acharonim agreed. And even in immersing, where drying is not required by law, Magen Avraham wrote that one whose intent is repulsive upon him — there is [reason] because of disgust and he requires drying.**** END BLOCK ****
