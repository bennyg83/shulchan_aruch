13) [Seif 4] If one eats something dipped, etc. For so it is stated in Pesachim 115a: R' Elazar said in the name of R' Oshaya, anything dipped in liquid requires netilas yadayim. Rashi, of blessed memory, explains that netilas yadayim is required because of the liquid, since hands have secondary impurity, and anything that disqualifies terumah renders liquids primary impurity. The Beit Yosef and Taz, paragraph 4, cite him. See below, paragraph 25. See the Beit Yosef, who cites a dispute whether one recites a blessing over this washing. He therefore rules that one should not recite a blessing, because a doubt concerning blessings is treated leniently, and he rules so here in the Shulchan Aruch. The later authorities write the same. The unqualified wording implies that one must observe all the laws of netilas yadayim for liquid just as for netilas yadayim for bread, except that one does not recite a blessing over washing for liquid because a doubt concerning blessings is treated leniently. Whether one may wrap his hands in a cloth and dip the food is discussed below in siman 163, seif 1.
