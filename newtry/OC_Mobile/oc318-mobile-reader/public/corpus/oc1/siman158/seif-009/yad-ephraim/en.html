(There, s.k. 16) The text should read: specifically regarding HaMotzi, if he forgot, he does not recite the blessing after eating, unlike, etc. That is to say, there he was truly obligated to recite the blessing, and since he did not do so, once it was pushed off, it was pushed off. By contrast, here, sometimes he is not fit for the blessing, and in such a case the blessing is displaced from its place. Therefore, even though there is room to recite it after he cleans his hands, and even so it will be before its performance, nevertheless, since in most cases it is impossible for it to be truly beforehand because his hands are not clean, we are no longer particular at all that it be somewhat before the act, before its completion. Even where his hands are clean, they did not distinguish. According to the words of the Tur, this reasoning works better as well, as I wrote above.
