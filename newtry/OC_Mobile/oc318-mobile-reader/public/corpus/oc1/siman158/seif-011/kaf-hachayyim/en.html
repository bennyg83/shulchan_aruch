11) There: Less than an olive's volume, etc. If he eats more than an olive's volume, or exactly an olive's volume, he should wash his hands because of the doubt but should not recite a blessing. Levush, seif 2. This follows the view of Maran, of blessed memory, and those who agree with him. We already wrote in the preceding paragraph that the later authorities are stringent even for less than an olive's volume; see there.
