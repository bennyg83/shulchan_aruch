And if he did not divert his attention, etc. It appears to me that Rama was troubled by the fact that we rule in siman 159, seif 13, that intention is required only initially. Here, the first washing is no worse than a washing performed without intention. He therefore writes that the same rule applies here: so long as one did not divert his attention, he should wash again without a blessing, because the first washing is effective at least to prevent him from reciting another blessing.
