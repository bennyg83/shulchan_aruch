And the custom is, etc. Tosafot there, s.v. al, etc., according to the first reason there. So too they wrote in Berachos 51a, s.v. initially, etc.; also, etc., according to the second reason there. So too they wrote in Sukkah 39a, s.v. before.
And if, etc. Hagahos Ashri at the beginning of Chullin, s.v. and it was not omitted, etc.; however, etc., as he explained in Berachos. That is to say, as written at the end of the first chapter there, s.v. when not, etc.: wherever, etc. But the Rambam in chapter 11 of Hilchos Berachos wrote that he does not recite a blessing afterward, as stated there, 51a. What Hagahos Ashri distinguished between blessings over mitzvos and blessings of benefit is not implied there, for he brings proof from one who immersed and ascended, and it is rejected only because initially, etc. The proof of Hagahos Ashri is from what is written in Berachos there, that one recites the blessing of Yotzer Or after Shema. This is not decisive, for the blessings of Shema were not instituted over Shema, as the Rashba wrote. This is also the view of Tosafot in Pesachim there, s.v. at the time, and in Sukkah there; see there. But according to the first reason, he can recite a blessing, as written in Berachos there, and this reason also applies to lulav. See what I wrote in siman 651, and see Magen Avraham. In the gloss he ruled according to both reasons and leniently.
