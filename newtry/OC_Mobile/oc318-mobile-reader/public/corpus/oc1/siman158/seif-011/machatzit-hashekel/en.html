(S.k. 11) He need not wash, since he does not touch, etc. For this reason the Taz wrote in s.k. 9 that when dough is cooked in water, even if there is broth there, the dough does not require washing, since the way is to eat it with a spoon and it is not likely that he will touch it.
See what I wrote in seif 4, s.k. 5, regarding something whose normal manner is not, etc.
