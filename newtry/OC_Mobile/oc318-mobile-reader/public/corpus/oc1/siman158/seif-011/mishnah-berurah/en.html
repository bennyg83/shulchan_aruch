(39) Prior to performing it. Meaning before and immediately adjacent to the performance of the mitzvah. This is derived from Rashi.
(40) Because at times his hands are not clean. For example, he left the lavatory or touched covered parts of his body, so that he is unfit to recite the blessing before washing. For this reason, the practice developed to recite the blessing after washing in every case.
(41) The second waters. Beit Yosef cites this in the name of Rabbeinu Yerucham, who writes that his teachers practiced this way. Shibolei HaLeket cites Rabbeinu Meir, of blessed memory, who would recite the blessing after washing and before drying, as stated below, and this is the common practice.
(42) Because drying is also, etc. This is another reason for delaying the blessing. Even after washing, it is still considered prior to performance, because drying completes the washing, as explained below in seif 12.
(43) And it is considered prior to performance. Even when one pours an entire reviis at once, in which case the Mechaber holds that drying is entirely unnecessary, as explained below, the Sages did not distinguish. Magen Avraham and other Acharonim.
(44) He recites the blessing afterward. This follows the first reason given by the Mechaber. It differs from other blessings over mitzvos, regarding which we rule that if one did not recite the blessing initially, he may not recite it afterward, since once the opportunity was deferred it remains deferred. Here, however, he was sometimes unfit to recite the blessing beforehand because his hands were not clean. Therefore, even if he has already dried his hands, he may still recite the blessing. If he has already recited HaMotzi, however, the poskim write that he may no longer recite Al Netilas Yadayim. Taz is stringent and holds that after drying he may no longer recite the blessing. Although in practice one should not follow this view, since many Acharonim agree with Rama, one must nevertheless be very careful initially. Some people say “Raise your hands” and prolong the matter, delaying the blessing Al Netilas Yadayim until after drying; they act improperly.
