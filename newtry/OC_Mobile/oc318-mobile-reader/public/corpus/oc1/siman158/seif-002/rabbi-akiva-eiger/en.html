Magen Avraham, seif katan 8. And fish blood. Magen Avraham assumes as obvious that fish blood does not render food susceptible to impurity. To me this requires considerable study, for even fish fat renders food susceptible, since the entire fish is regarded as water, as stated in Zevachim 22a, that one may immerse in the eye of a fish. Rambam and Shulchan Aruch rule accordingly. Since it is considered water, it should likewise render food susceptible to impurity.
