(9) Less than the volume of an egg, etc. The reason is that, regarding tumah, food measuring less than an egg does not contract tumah D’Oraisa and therefore will not become tamei through contact with hands. It may therefore be that the Sages did not decree washing for such an amount. Alternatively, since regarding Bircas HaMazon it is considered eating, for we rule that Bircas HaMazon is recited over an olive-sized amount, it may likewise be considered eating regarding washing, and the Sages did not distinguish within their enactment. He should therefore wash without reciting a blessing, since the blessing does not prevent fulfillment of the washing. Know that if the food measures an egg even without its shell, all agree that he must wash with a blessing. Dagul Merevavah and other Acharonim.
