(S.k. 2) And it is not, etc. For one must recite HaMotzi, as below in siman 168, seif 6. The Beis Yosef was uncertain about the matter, for he first wrote that since the Rambam wrote in the language of the Shulchan Aruch, that anyone who eats bread over which HaMotzi is recited requires netilas yadayim, therefore even with pas haba'ah b'kisnin, if he established his meal upon it so that he must recite HaMotzi, he should also require netilas yadayim. He concluded: However, it is difficult in my eyes that with one and the same bread, sometimes it would require netilas yadayim and sometimes not. Perhaps it is not called bread over which HaMotzi is recited unless it is bread over which HaMotzi is always recited, even when he does not establish his meal upon it, etc. However, I found in the Rashba, etc., and from his words it implies that if he establishes his meal upon them, he requires netilas yadayim. End quote from the Beis Yosef.
And so the Rambam wrote, etc. That is to say, what was uncertain to the Beis Yosef in the language of the Rambam was obvious to the Magen Avraham, that the language indicates that even with pas haba'ah b'kisnin, if he established his meal upon it so that he must recite HaMotzi, he requires netilas yadayim.
