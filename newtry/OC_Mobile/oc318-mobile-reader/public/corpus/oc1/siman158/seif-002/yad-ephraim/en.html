(S.k. 2) There is proof from what the Beis Yosef wrote in siman 167, close, etc.; so the text should read. His words are astonishing, for the Beis Yosef wrote that from the words of the Rash it implies that regarding something whose beginning was dough, it depends on the law of HaMotzi. Nevertheless, he wrote that there is room to be uncertain, perhaps specifically with bread over which HaMotzi is always recited; and that case whose beginning was dough, he holds that according to the Rash one always recites HaMotzi over it, and therefore it also requires netilas yadayim. By contrast, genuine pas haba'ah b'kisnin, since if he does not establish a meal upon it he does not recite HaMotzi, perhaps it does not require netilas yadayim even if he established a meal upon it. Also regarding the Magen Avraham, who brought proof from the Rambam, this requires study; see Beis Yosef.
