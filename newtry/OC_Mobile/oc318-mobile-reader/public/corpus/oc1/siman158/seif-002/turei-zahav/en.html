Washing is not required. But if he established a meal upon them — Beit Yosef wrote opinions both ways whether washing is required as practiced in Chullin; and he concludes it is implied from Rashba's words that washing is required, and if so certainly one must bless; and Levush wrote he washes and does not bless — it appears to me he should bless. And there is proof from what Beit Yosef wrote in 168 near the end in the name of Rosh on dough whose end is sufganin and whose beginning is dough — and thus: and he blesses upon it Hamotzi and it requires washing and Hamotzi, end of his words. And here none disagree; only Beit Yosef discusses this — and it is astonishing that he did not bring this here.
