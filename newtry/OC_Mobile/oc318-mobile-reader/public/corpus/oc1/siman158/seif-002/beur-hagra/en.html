Seif 2, there are those, etc. Because of uncertainty. This is from the Rokeach. His uncertainty is because in the second chapter of Sukkah they say: behold, an egg-size requires washing and a blessing, and they likewise seek to prove from there in chapter 25 regarding a cloth. However, one can say that perhaps Rabbi Tzadok follows his own view, for he maintains regarding Birkas HaMazon like Rabbi Yehudah, and the same would apply regarding washing. But according to us, since we rule like Rabbi Meir that an olive-size is sufficient, the same applies regarding washing, as written in seif 3. Alternatively, one can say that even according to Rabbi Meir, since less than an egg-size does not contract tumah, the Sages did not decree netilas yadayim, even though for itself it receives tumah with any amount, as Tosafot wrote in Sukkah 26b, s.v. they took, etc., from the Mishnah at the beginning of the second chapter of Taharos. Nevertheless, since according to the Torah it does not contract tumah, as written in Shabbos 91a, s.v. if, and Pesachim 33b, s.v. when. As to what the Magen Avraham wrote, that Tosafot and the Ran in the second chapter of Sukkah disagree with this, meaning the Tosafot there, s.v. they took, and so the Ran wrote there, I do not understand it; for with terumah, certainly even any amount requires washing, since hands are second-degree tumah and disqualify terumah.
