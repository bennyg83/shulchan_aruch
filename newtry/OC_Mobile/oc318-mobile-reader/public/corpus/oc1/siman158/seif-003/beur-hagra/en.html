Seif 3, if, etc. See the Magen Avraham, that there is a textual error in the Rokeach and one must read: less than an egg-size. His proof is from what is written in Pesachim 116b: he washed his hands for dipping, etc.; it is not difficult because of matzah, for matzah suffices with an olive-size and does not require washing. This is unlike food dipped in liquid, which becomes tamei with any amount according to the Torah, as Tosafot wrote there, folio 14a, s.v. for there is, and even according to Rabbeinu Tam's explanation, nevertheless it is because of the liquids themselves. But Tosafot there, s.v. requires, etc., wrote: it is not precise, etc. This is what the Magen Avraham wrote: and in Tosafot, Pesachim 116. It is not compelling, for Tosafot did not wish to force an explanation that throughout the whole meal he does not eat an egg-size of bread. But the essential halacha is that the measure for netilas yadayim is an olive-size, since it contracts tumah with any amount, except that they decreed only regarding something for which Birkas HaMazon is required, just as they did not decree regarding fruits or refreshments when he does not establish his meal upon them. Thus it depends on Birkas HaMazon. Therefore in Sukkah there they linked washing and blessing, and at the end of the eighth chapter of Berachos they supported them from one verse.
