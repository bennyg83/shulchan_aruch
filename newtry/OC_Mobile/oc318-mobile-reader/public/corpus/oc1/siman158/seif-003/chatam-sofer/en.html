In the same passage, “which they consecrated.” Note: In my humble opinion, butter is not considered a liquid according to any view, even if it later melts. Once the curd, which is milk, was set aside, the designation of milk ceased from it. The verse, “She opened the skin of milk and gave him drink,” which implies that milk is a liquid, refers to milk in its original state. Once its components have been separated, however, the designation of liquid ceases from it. Independently of this, I also concluded in a responsum that whenever butter was congealed and then melted with the intention of placing it into food, it is a liquid that comes for the sake of food and is treated as food, since from the outset it was made to be added to food. This is contrary to Taz below, at the end of siman 109, and Magen Avraham, seif katan 10. I explained the matter there at length. See also Mishnah 6 of Machshirin, mishnah 5, which states specifically that whey renders seeds susceptible to impurity, but not butter. See Chullin 114a.
