(S.k. 3) Less, since it does not receive food impurity. This means according to the Torah. Although it receives impurity D'Rabbanan, nevertheless they were lenient with it regarding netilas yadayim, for the law of netilas yadayim is only D'Rabbanan. So wrote the author of Tosefes Yom HaKippurim in tractate Yoma. Others disagree with this and hold that even less than an egg-bulk, although it does not render others impure, it itself receives impurity according to the Torah, and if so it requires washing. Since this depends on great authorities, the Beis Yosef wrote that he should wash his hands but not recite a blessing.
