(Magen Avraham, s.k. 4) For with matzah an olive-bulk is required. That is to say, the Rokeach wrote there that one should wash his hands for dipping, whereas for matzah, with which he fulfills the obligation with an olive-bulk, he does not require washing, for less than an olive-bulk does not require washing. On this the Magen Avraham wrote that it is a scribal error, and the text should read that less than an egg-bulk does not require washing.
