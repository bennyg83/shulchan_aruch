That is dipped. See Baer Heitev. Birkei Yosef writes that fruit submerged in water to clean it and then removed and eaten is likewise considered food dipped in liquid. He proves this from charoses, citing Maharaz Guta. A holy authority likewise wrote in a manuscript responsum that vegetables washed to remove dirt are considered food dipped in liquid. This is also evident from Responsa Radbaz, siman 83; see there.
