4) There: Even if he knows of no impurity upon them. The Rambam writes in chapter 6 of Hilchos Berachos: even if his hands are not dirty and he knows of no impurity upon them, he may not eat until he washes both hands. The Beit Yosef cites him; Olas Tamid, paragraph 2. One who has a wound on one hand and can wash only the other hand nevertheless recites the blessing over netilas yadayim, as we wrote above in siman 4, paragraph 7. So too writes the Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham to this siman, paragraph 1, and Ben Ish Chai, parashas Tazria, paragraph 13. See below, siman 162, seif 10.
