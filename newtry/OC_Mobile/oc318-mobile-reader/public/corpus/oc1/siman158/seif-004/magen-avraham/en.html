Something dipped. Something not ordinarily dipped in liquid does not require washing, because his unusual practice is disregarded relative to ordinary people. One who dips his finger in liquid and sucks it need not wash. Radbaz, part 1, siman 78.
Liquids. Even when they touch something of the second degree, they themselves become first degree and render the food second degree. See seif 7.
Bee honey. This excludes date honey. Bircas HaZevach, chapter 3 of Menachos, page 31, writes that established halachah is that congealed honey is not a liquid, and therefore one need not wash for a confection. Radbaz, siman 101, writes likewise, whether it congealed through cooking or through cold. If it has only thickened slightly and still flows, washing is required. At the beginning of chapter 3 of Taharos, Rash explains that only when it is sufficiently wet to moisten another object is it considered liquid. If it has congealed so much that it cannot moisten another object, it is considered food. He also cites a Tosefta there: honey flowing from its hive contracts the impurity of liquids; if one designated it as food, it contracts the impurity of foods. Accordingly, there is no concern at all regarding honey used in confections, since it is intended for eating and is not considered liquid. Rambam rules likewise in chapter 1 of Hilchos Tumas Ochlin. So too Shabbos 19 states that honey was originally food and remains food. See Bava Basra 72 and Tosafos there, who write that oil, blood, and milk that congealed are not liquids. Kesef Mishneh implies that even if they later melt they do not regain liquid status, except for wine. See there in the name of Tosafos to Pesachim 14.
Blood. It was mentioned unnecessarily, since consuming blood is prohibited. The blood of fish and grasshoppers is not considered a liquid. Perhaps the case concerns one who consumes blood for medicinal purposes in a life-threatening situation. Lechem Chamudos, seif katan 41 in chapter 25, writes that people customarily do not wash and have authorities upon whom to rely, since Tosafos hold this way. Go and see what the people do. See siman 473.
