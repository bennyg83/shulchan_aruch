In one — Taz brought Rashi Pesachim 116a. The examiner in Levush will see he holds four explanations: Rashi — liquid wine and oil for terumah, and dipping touches his hand, and because of terumah they decreed the other five liquids; they did not decree on fruit — first liquids, etc.; Rashi contaminates liquids first; Hagahot Ashri — R' Yerucham: liquids receive without hechsher and become first-degree, therefore washing; R' Yehudah — cleanliness; Tosafot — lest he defile his body; Beit Yosef seems R' Yerucham and Rashi are one — simple meaning: thing dipped in liquid, fruits etc., stringent because of proximity to terumah since there is liquid too, like bread per Tosafot for Rashi; no need to say "as if wine and oil." See letter 5 on Levush, Eshel Avraham letter 9. For Rashi — berachah because of proximity to terumah (Rashi wrote vegetable and terumah in vegetables — possibly only maaser rabbinically; even eating what has no terumah or maaser, all whose dipping is in liquid requires washing).
