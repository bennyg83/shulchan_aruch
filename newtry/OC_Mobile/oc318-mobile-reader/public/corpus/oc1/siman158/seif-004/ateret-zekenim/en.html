When one eats soup with a spoon, however, it is possible that the law is like drinking liquids, since one is not likely to touch it. What is written in seif 5, that a cooked wheat dish requires washing, may refer to eating it without a spoon. See below, siman 163, seif 1: even if one dips only the tip of the vegetable, he nevertheless washes without a blessing. The Tur writes in the name of Maharam of Rothenburg that nowadays we are not required to do so. One who wishes to satisfy the doubt should bring himself into an obligation of washing for another reason, such as by urinating and rubbing the area. Maharshal likewise writes in Responsa, siman 86.
