Seif 4, in one, etc. For they convey tumah as liquids, as written in the eleventh chapter of Terumos, where their mnemonic is given, etc., and in the sixth chapter of Machshirin.
And it did not dry, etc. For otherwise, even though it became susceptible to tumah, it does not become tamei with respect to chullin, as written in the Mishnah in the second chapter of Chullin: one who slaughters an animal, a wild animal, or a bird, and no blood came out of them, etc. The reason is specifically that no blood came out, etc. In the Yerushalmi, in the eighth chapter of Berachos, Rav Huna said: there is no netilas yadayim except for bread alone. Rabbi Hoshaya taught: anything that has the dirtiness of liquid. So too it is evident in the chapter Arvei Pesachim, for if it were because of susceptibility to tumah, what does it mean there when it says: derive from this regarding charoses, etc.? Behold, it can receive susceptibility to tumah with any amount, for even through the hand it can receive susceptibility to tumah, as written in Chullin 118b according to Rabbi Yehudah and Toras Kohanim. Necessarily, Rav disagrees only regarding the hand. And in the Gemara there: about what do they disagree, etc.; and there, 128b: if the animal was slaughtered, etc.; and there, 128a: the milk of a slaughtered animal in villages, etc.; and in many other places.

Without a blessing. Tosafot there, who maintain that nowadays this washing is not required. But all the Geonim and most of the poskim agreed that even nowadays it applies as with bread and requires a blessing, and this is the primary ruling. See Tur, siman 473. Tosafot's proof that this washing is not because of sanctity but so that the foods and liquids not be disqualified, from the fact that the Gemara challenges: but he did not touch, is not decisive. One can say that it initially assumed that they did not decree unless he touched the place of the liquid. What they wrote, like that case in Chullin 33a, means that it says: behold, it came out, etc., why are hands second-degree, etc.? But if they would make a third degree in chullin, washing would be required; and there it is so that the food not be disqualified, and here too. They also brought proof from the order of reclining in the sixth chapter of Berachos, that he washes one hand, and there it is so that the liquid not be disqualified. All this is not decisive, as Rabbeinu Yonah wrote, that he recites the blessing: Who commanded us, for He commanded us regarding cleanliness, and all this is included in the mitzvah to heed the words of Rabbi Elazar ben Azaryah. It is all the more so from bread, where the reason is only because of a concern for terumah.
