If one eats something that was dipped in one of the 7 liquids (mnemonic "yad shachat dam") and doesn't dry it off, even if his hands won't touch the wet place, he needs to wash without a blessing {Rama: [that is: wine, honey, oil, milk, dew, blood, or water] [even if only the tip of the fruit or vegetable was dipped he needs to wash without a blessing].}
