That is dipped. Something whose normal manner is not to be dipped in liquid does not require netilas yadayim, for his view is nullified relative to all people. One who dips his finger into liquid and sucks it does not require washing; Radbaz, volume 1, siman 78, and Olas Tamid; see there. See also Mateh Yosef, volume 2, siman 18.
Liquids. For the hands are second-degree impurity and render liquids first-degree impurity, and the liquid then makes the food second-degree impurity.
Wine. Vinegar is included in wine; Mateh Yosef there.
Honey. This means bee honey, not date honey. If one mixed one of these seven liquids with other liquids, such as bee honey with date honey and the like, we follow the majority. If the majority is other liquids, he does not require netilas yadayim; if it is half and half, he requires netilas yadayim; Mateh Yosef, volume 2, siman 18. Regarding honey preserves, and similarly any liquid that was cooked, there are many opinions as to whether it is considered a liquid or not. See Magen Avraham, Taz, Birkas HaZevach, Radbaz, Maharit, and Yad Aharon. In Responsa Mateh Yosef, volume 2, siman 18, he concluded as the halacha that if it is a clear broth, whether cooked by itself or cooked with another type, it requires washing. If there is no broth there, but it is congealed and dry, it is food and does not require washing. If it is congealed but not dry, so that it is still recognizable, if he intended them as liquids it requires netilas yadayim, and if not, it does not require washing and is nullified. With water, even if it is congealed and dry like stone, if he intended it as a liquid it requires washing. If they are cooked with another type and there is no broth there, but liquid is absorbed within the food and the whole becomes one body, even though there is moisture sufficient to wet something else, it does not require washing. Pickling brine is obviously a liquid and requires washing. End quote.
Oil. Olive oil, but not sesame oil; Ginas Veradim, Orach Chaim, klal 1, siman 29, and Mateh Yosef, volume 2, siman 18.
Milk. Whey is like milk. This is after they make cheese, and afterward milk remains mixed with food floating on top; that remainder is called whey; Mateh Yosef there. Sour milk called yogurt requires netilas yadayim; Mateh Yosef there. See Yad Aharon.
Blood. It was mentioned incidentally, for it is forbidden to eat blood, and the blood of locusts is not called a liquid. It is possible that it speaks of one who eats blood for healing and pikuach nefesh; Magen Avraham.
Blessing. For it is only because of cleanliness. In Lechem Chamudos he wrote that the world has the practice not to wash, and they have what to rely upon, for Tosafot hold so; Magen Avraham. See Ateres Zekeinim. In Mateh Yosef, siman 18, he wrote that one who is lenient is included among those who demean netilas yadayim, who are uprooted from the world and liable to excommunication. Therefore, a God-fearing person should be careful in the matter and should not be concerned for the bad custom; see there.
The vegetable. The same applies if he eats with a spoon: he requires washing for something dipped in liquid. However, if the dipping is in a liquid that was cooked, even according to one who is stringent to wash his hands even for cooked liquid, if he eats with a spoon one should not be stringent, for we do not make two stringencies. Knesses HaGedolah and Mateh Yosef there; Olas Tamid.
