Seif katan 13. “And even if he washed.” Meaning, if so, it would be appropriate to recite the blessing over the first washing, since he at least intended it as a washing. Now that he wishes to eat, he could recite the blessing over that first washing. To this Magen Avraham answers that this is not so, for Tosafos, cited in seif katan 8, hold that no washing at all is required. It is therefore considered as though he had not intended it as a washing at all.
