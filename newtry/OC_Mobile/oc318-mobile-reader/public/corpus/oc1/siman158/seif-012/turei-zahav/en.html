Also that which is dried, etc. — according to this reason it is difficult, for there are many washings that do not require drying, as written in the next seif; but per the first reason that Tur wrote — because his hands are not clean — it is settled. And that Rama wrote: if he forgot to bless until after drying he blesses afterward — it is implied, but not after Hamotzi. And thus Rashal wrote in Pesachim ch. 2: if he forgot to bless until after drying — I wrote in Pesachim ch. 2 that he can bless before Hamotzi and not afterward, end of his words. Behold, his proof is hidden from me; but according to what appears, much more likely to my humble opinion: after finishing fulfillment of the mitzvah — that is, drying — he should not bless, for all mitzvot that require a berachah — one transgresses if he blesses after performance; and certainly in a mitzvah that has duration in its fulfillment — then even if he forgot before performance, there is still time while he is engaged in it; not so if the duration of its fulfillment has ended — he should not bless. And therefore Tur wrote that he blesses before drying, and did not write even after; and proof from what is written in siman 167 seif 8: if he did not remember until after he finished his meal — he does not bless Hamotzi. And one can say this case is different, since he has not yet eaten — this is not yet eating; it is possible the hands are pure without touch, as Beit Yosef wrote in siman 164 in the name of Kol Bo: if he placed his hands in the finger-partitions he need not wash afterward; and if so, he should have blessed, and it commanded him to eat with pure hands — the mitzvah of washing already passed. And one can say different from what Rama wrote in siman 172 regarding expelling liquids and blessing — there he remembered while the liquid was still in his mouth, as written there.
