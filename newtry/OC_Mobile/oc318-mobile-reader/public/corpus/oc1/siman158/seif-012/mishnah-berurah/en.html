(45) Tamei bread. Beit Yosef writes that this is because the first water poured on one’s hands becomes tamei through contact with the hands, as explained below in siman 162. Although he pours second water to purify the first water, as stated there, initially he must remove the first water completely by drying. Maharshal writes that the primary purpose of drying is cleanliness, because when one’s hands remain wet from the washing water it is repulsive. This also appears from Rashi in Sotah 4a. Magen Avraham cites Tashbetz that one should not dry his hands on his shirt, because this is detrimental to memory. See Peri Megadim, who is uncertain whether this applies specifically to one’s shirt or to all one’s garments.
