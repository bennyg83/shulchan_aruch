Fourteen. These are the seven liquids whose mnemonic is yad shachat dam. See Taz. I explained his disagreement with Levush in letter 4. Levush holds that the requirement for food dipped in liquid is a safeguard for terumah, specifically wine and oil. Taz holds that the decree applies to all seven liquids because they are especially susceptible to impurity without prior preparation and become first degree. The reason may be terumah of fruit dipped in liquid, or the ordinary liquids themselves: because they are especially susceptible, the Sages required washing for them without invoking wine and oil. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 9, and Kesef Mishneh, chapter 7 of Hilchos Berachos, who cites the wording of Tosafos and Rashi. See what I will write, with Hashem’s help, in letter 6. The phrase “bee honey” excludes wasp honey. Ordinary “honey” means bee honey, and it is sometimes explicitly called bee honey. A substance whose very name requires an additional qualifier, however, is never included without that qualifier. See what I will write, with Hashem’s help, in Magen Avraham, letter 6.
