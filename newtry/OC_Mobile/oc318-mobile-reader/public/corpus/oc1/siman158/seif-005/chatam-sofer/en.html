There, seif katan 12. In Tosafos, s.v. kol shetibulo. Note: In Tosafos, s.v. issurei, they wrote that one might have thought, etc., because according to the conclusion they hold that intention is required, as is evident from the passage in Berachos. The Gemara, however, gave a stronger answer, namely that he had diverted his attention. Furthermore, according to the view that less than an egg-bulk does not require washing, the Gemara there cannot be referring to eating matzah, for an olive-bulk suffices for matzah, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Rokeach, contrary to Tosafos.
