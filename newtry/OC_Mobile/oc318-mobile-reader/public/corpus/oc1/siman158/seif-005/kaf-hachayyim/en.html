5) There: He should recite the blessing over netilas yadayim. The word netilah contains the letters of tal, numerical value 39, and NeHi. One should intend to draw the shefa from the three upper letters, Yod, Hei, and Vav, whose filled-out value, using alefs, is 39, to the final Hei, which is Malchus standing in NeHi. The initial letters of al netilas yadayim spell ani, whose numerical value is 130. The five Havayah Names also have the numerical value 130. One should intend to cause the value of 130 to prevail over the poor one and to sweeten it through the five Havayah Names. The Rashash, of blessed memory, in Sefer Nehar Shalom, folio 15b in the new printing. See below, paragraph 78; siman 160, paragraph 82; and siman 162, paragraph 2.
