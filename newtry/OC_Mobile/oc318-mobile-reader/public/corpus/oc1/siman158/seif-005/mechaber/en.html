One who washes his hands for fruit is among the arrogant. {Rama: This applies specifically when he washes them as though there were a halachic obligation to do so. But if he washed them for cleanliness because his hands were not clean, it is permitted. [Hagahot Semak]} Regarding roasted meat, there is an authority whose words indicate that even though its juice is still moist upon it, its law is like fruit. A cooked dish of wheat kernels, when they are dry, likewise has the law of fruit.
