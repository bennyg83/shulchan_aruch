[11] [Levush] One should wash, etc. People have adopted the practice not to wash at all. Malbushei Yom Tov. Lechem Chamudos writes likewise. Sheyarei Knesses HaGedolah writes that in other countries the practice is to wash. Mateh Moshe writes that one who is careful about this will merit abundant good, and Shelah, page 81, strongly cautions that this practice should be observed.
[12] One who washes his hands for fruit, etc. The same applies to meat, cheese, or vegetables. If he dips them in liquid, or they were soaked in water and the water is still upon them, washing is required. Kol Bo, page 16.
[Levush] His hands are dirty, etc. The same applies if he touched covered parts of his body. Bach.
