Fruits cooked in water — there are opinions among the Acharonim whether they require washing; therefore it is proper to be stringent when eating them with a spoon if there is liquid touching them; and in pickled foods [such as okra and the like] it is certainly proper to be stringent to wash hands if they were not dried from the water on them. Bach wrote: one who eats lekach to sweeten the drink and dips the lekach in vinegar — this is not a thing dipped in liquid, because the moisture is from the grain and is fruit juice; and even if the vinegar is mixed with water, nevertheless it is minimal and washing is not required, end of his words; it is implied from this that if the water is the majority [by gradation] it is included as a liquid.
