(34) In a wilderness. This too is because of danger, if he were to go searching for water. The wilderness is mentioned separately to teach that an ordinary wilderness is always considered a dangerous place.
(35) And he has no water. Even if he knows that by going back one mil he will find water, he is not obligated to do so.
(36) He is exempt from washing his hands. Nevertheless, he must wrap his hands in a cloth, as explained below in siman 163.
