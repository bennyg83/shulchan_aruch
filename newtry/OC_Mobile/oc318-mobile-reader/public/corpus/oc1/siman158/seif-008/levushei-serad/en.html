Taz, seif katan 7. “You will not find honey.” This proof is valid only after he established that no distinction is made here on the basis that an action nullifies an earlier action. Otherwise, one could say that honey was counted among the seven liquids only where it was crushed but not afterward cooked, so that no act was performed to nullify it. Alternatively, Taz may hold that transforming a food into a liquid is itself an act performed to make it into food. Examine carefully.
