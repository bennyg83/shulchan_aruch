And meat. See Taz. The juice that emerges from meat after roasting contains no blood and has the law of fruit juice; it neither renders food susceptible to impurity nor conveys impurity. See Magen Avraham, letter 10. If a small amount of water became mixed with fruit juice, see Rambam, chapter 16 of Hilchos Tumas Ochlin, halachah 4. According to Rambam, if the water itself was impure, the mixture is impure; regarding other liquids, the majority determines the status. Raavad disagrees. See Eliyah Rabbah, letter 17, who cites the Tosefta in chapter 2 of Machshirin as quoted by Rash there. Examine it carefully. This distinction likewise has practical application here. With Hashem’s help, I will explain it in Magen Avraham, letter 10.
