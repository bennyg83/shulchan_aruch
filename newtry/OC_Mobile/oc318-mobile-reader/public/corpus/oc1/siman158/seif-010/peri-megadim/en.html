One who drinks. See Taz. If one touches the liquid with his hand, such as when drinking directly from a river, he requires washing, unlike Magen Avraham, letter 11. This is as Beit Yosef writes in the name of Ran, cited in letter 9. It follows that one who eats honey by dipping his finger into it requires washing. This seemingly implies that both hands require washing, analogous to one who drinks from a river. It may be, however, that a person drinking from a river scoops the water with the palm of one hand rather than immersing the whole hand, and therefore at least the one hand with which he drinks must be washed, including all its fingers. See Magen Avraham, letter 5, who writes that one who sucks liquid from his finger does not require washing. This requires study. See Toras HaBayis, page 109, and Bedek HaBayis, page 28. There is no need to elaborate.
