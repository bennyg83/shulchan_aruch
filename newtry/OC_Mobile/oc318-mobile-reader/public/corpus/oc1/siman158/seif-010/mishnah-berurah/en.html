(37) Whose required amount is a reviis. This means one reviis for both hands. Below, in siman 162, seif 4, I cite the Acharonim who write that one should use no less than a reviis for each hand, because when washing with less one must be careful about many details, and not everyone is proficient in them.
(38) “Full handfuls of goodness.” Nevertheless, initially it is preferable not to do this for the sake of wealth, for that would be serving in order to receive reward. Rather, one should do everything for the honor of Hashem, and the reward will come of itself. One who is careful about this but does not become wealthy is prevented by his other deeds. Acharonim.
