Magen Avraham, seif katan 11. “They did not decree.” Meaning, since an ordinary drinker does not require washing of the hands, the Sages likewise did not decree regarding one who drinks directly from a river, because that is uncommon. This is the meaning of his concluding reference to what he wrote in seif 4, namely in seif katan 5, that the Sages did not decree concerning an uncommon occurrence.
