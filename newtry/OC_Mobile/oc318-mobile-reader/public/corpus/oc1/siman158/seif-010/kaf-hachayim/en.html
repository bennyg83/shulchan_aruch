(10) [Seif 3] If one eats less than a kezayit, etc. — thus in Shulchan Aruch according to what Beit Yosef wrote in the name of the Rokeach siman 483, that for less than a kezayit washing is not required. However Lechem Chamudot in Chullin ch. Kol HaBasar s.k. 72 wrote that a mistake fell in Beit Yosef's copy, and because of this mistake he wrote as halachah in Shulchan Aruch — but this is not so; and it must be said that for less than a kebeitzah washing is not required, and he wrote that thus is it in the Rokeach, and concluded: therefore one should wash without a berachah on any bread, whether little or much, until a kebeitzah — when then he is also obligated in the berachah of netilat yadayim, see there. And likewise Magen Avraham s.k. 4 s.v. ve'im. But Matteh Yehudah s.k. 3 wrote to settle the words of Shulchan Aruch and concluded: even though Lechem Chamudot and Magen Avraham disagreed with Shulchan Aruch regarding less than a kezayit, and hold the law is like less than a kebeitzah — that one washes and does not bless — we have only the ruling of Shulchan Aruch in practice, and such is the opinion of Bach, and one who wishes to be stringent should examine. And likewise Levush: for less than a kezayit, for everyone washing is not required. And likewise Taz s.k. 3. Yeshuot Yaakov in Hagahot Beit Yosef. Birkei Yosef s.k. 4 wrote that thus we record; however Ateret Zekenim s.k. 4 wrote that one should be stringent and wash without a berachah. And likewise Peri Megadim in Eshel Avraham s.k. 1, in Mishbetzot Zahav s.k. 2, in Nefesh HaChayim s.k. 2, in Derech HaChayim s.k. 2; but Beiur Halacha in laws of netilat yadayim s.k. 1 wrote like Birkei Yosef, and wrote that thus is the custom of the world, and not like Ateret Zekenim and Peri Megadim — only one who wishes to be stringent should examine, see there. And it appears: since this law is in dispute, even though Beiur Halacha wrote that the custom is not to wash for less than a kezayit — it is correct to be stringent and wash in order to satisfy all opinions. And in any case, people are careful not to eat less than the measure so as not to eat without a final berachah; and if so, all the more so with bread, upon which there are several intentions, as written in the previous siman — and if so, who would dare in his heart to eat less than the measure without netilat yadayim and without Hamotzi?
