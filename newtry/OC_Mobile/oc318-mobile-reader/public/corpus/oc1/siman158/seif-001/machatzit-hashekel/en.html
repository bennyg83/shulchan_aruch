One must, etc. See siman 4 and the Beis Yosef there, that one should not distinguish between morning washing and washing for a meal. It is explained there that one first takes the vessel with the water in his right hand, gives it to his left, and pours from his left onto his right, and afterward onto his left.
(S.k. 1) He should wash, etc. Because of the trace of terumah, etc. For with terumah, second-degree impurity makes third-degree impurity. Since they decreed regarding the hands, which are busy, that they have the law of second-degree impurity, if they touch terumah they invalidate the terumah by making it third-degree. Therefore they required washing even for chullin in order to purify the hands, and through this those who eat terumah will also be careful.
