His hands. One must wash the right hand first, so that the left serves the right; Recanati, Parashas Eikev; Magen Avraham. The Baer Heitev that was before me wrote regarding the Magen Avraham that he erred in this; see there. But he himself erred and did not see the Recanati itself; see there.
He establishes. If he establishes his meal upon it, so that he must recite HaMotzi, he should wash without a blessing, or first eat other bread; Shelah and Levush. But the Taz and Magen Avraham ruled that he should wash and recite the blessing of netilas yadayim; see there. See Sefer Dammesek Eliezer, folio 318b, and Responsa Mateh Yosef, volume 2, siman 18.
