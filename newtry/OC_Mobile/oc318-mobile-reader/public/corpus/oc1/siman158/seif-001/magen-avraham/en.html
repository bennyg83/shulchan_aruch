One should wash the right hand first, so that the left serves the right. Recanati, Parashas Eikev. See siman 4, seif 10, and Beit Yosef there. He should wash his hands. Although hands have the status of a second degree of impurity, and a second degree does not create a third degree in ordinary food, the Sages nevertheless decreed washing because of the concern for terumah.
And he does not establish a meal upon it. If he establishes his meal upon it, so that he must recite Hamotzi, there is a dispute whether he must wash. He should therefore wash without a blessing. Shelah. Levush writes that he should first eat ordinary bread. I do not know who disputes this. Although Beit Yosef initially expresses doubt, he concludes in the name of Rashba that washing is required, which indicates that after raising the question he considered the matter resolved. Rambam likewise writes at the beginning of chapter 6 that anyone who eats bread over which Hamotzi is recited must wash. Bedek HaBayis and Mishmeres HaBayis explicitly write that washing is required when one establishes a meal upon it. He should therefore recite Al Netilas Yadayim.
