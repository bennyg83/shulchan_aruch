When one comes to eat bread that we bless on it "HaMotzi" , one should wash their hands, even if one does not know that [your hands] have any impurity, one should bless: "al n'tilat yadayim" /"regarding washing of hands". But bread that we do not bless "HaMotzi" on it, for example, little rolls, , or bread that comes as dumplings , and one does not establish a meal with them, one does not need to wash their hands. {Rama: (explanation: satorti in another language) (explanation: bread that is made with sugar, almonds, and nuts)}
