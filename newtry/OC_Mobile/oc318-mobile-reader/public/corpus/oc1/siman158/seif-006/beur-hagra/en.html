Seif 6, one who drinks, etc. That is to say, even though we say in Berachos 43a: they brought, etc., Tosafot already answered there and in Chagigah, as above in seif 5. So too in Yoma 30a: the Sages taught, the halacha, etc.; Rav Chisda said, etc. Rashi already explained there, s.v. not, etc.: nevertheless, etc. The Rashba, however, requires him to wash one hand based on the above. Although in Yoma there it says: if he spoke with his fellow, etc., Terumas HaDeshen already guarded against this and said that what is stated, there is no difference, etc., applies only to urinating, that is, only to the latter clause, and when he washes, etc. But in truth he washes only one hand, unlike the Raah, who requires one who drinks in a fixed manner to wash both hands based on the above, and he wondered at the Rashba; but his words are not understandable. Therefore the Rashba wrote that this baraisa can be rejected, for perhaps washing is required there because of droplets, since it is referring to urinating. All this appears forced to Rashi, that the first clause of the baraisa should differ from the latter clause, and all the more so to say that the latter clause does not refer back to the first.
