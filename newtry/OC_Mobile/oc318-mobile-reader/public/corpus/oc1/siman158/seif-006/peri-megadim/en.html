Washing. See Taz, who cites Rabbeinu Yerucham. This is not an improper combination of two contradictory leniencies. One may say that the primary law follows Rashi and Tosafos, while regarding the blessing we are stringent and do not bless because of the severity of uttering Hashem’s name in vain. See siman 215. Nevertheless, I do not understand why Taz did not explain like Magen Avraham, letter 6, following Tosafos: since the concern is that one’s body not become unfit, liquids outside the seven do not require washing, and no blessing is recited. According to Rabbeinu Yerucham, by contrast, even other liquids should require washing. See what I wrote in letters 4 and 5. This requires study. According to what I wrote in letter 3, Magen Avraham, letter 6, is understandable. There is no need to elaborate. In siman 473, letter 7, he cites Tosafos as saying that the reason is cleanliness, while Tosafos in Pesachim 115a writes that it is lest one render his body impure. For that reason, nowadays no blessing is recited.
