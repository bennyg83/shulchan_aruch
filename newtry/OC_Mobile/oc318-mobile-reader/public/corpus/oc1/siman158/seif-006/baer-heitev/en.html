One who drinks. The reason is that he never touches the liquid. According to this, one who drinks water from a river with his hand would require washing, but we have not seen people practice this way; Knesses HaGedolah and Radbaz. The Magen Avraham wrote: To me it does not appear so, for since they did not decree regarding one who drinks, he never requires netilas yadayim; see there. The Taz wrote that one who eats honey requires washing, for he is likely to touch it with his hand, to dip into the honey and eat. See Radbaz, volume 1, siman 78; Halachos Ketanos, volume 2, siman 128; Responsa Panim Meiros, siman 68; and Mateh Yosef, volume 2, siman 18.
