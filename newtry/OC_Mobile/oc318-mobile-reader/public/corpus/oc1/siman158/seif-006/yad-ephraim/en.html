(In Taz, s.k. 12) And because of this the Tur wrote that he should recite the blessing beforehand, and did not write even afterward. It appears that his intent is that certainly, if we say that he already completed fulfillment of the mitzvah with drying, it is obvious that he may not recite the blessing at all afterward, even after the fact, as he brings from siman 167. There is room to say that he should recite the blessing after drying only if you say that the mitzvah continues as long as he has not eaten, as the Taz wrote afterward. And one should not say that here is different, since he has not eaten, etc. For if so, there is no room to distinguish between before drying and after drying, since both are after the beginning of the act and before the completion of the act. If so, the Tur should have written that since before eating it is properly called before performing it, etc. One may also explain the Taz's intent in writing, therefore the Tur wrote, not as bringing a proof, for one could reject it and say that the Tur speaks initially, and then we require before drying, whereas if he forgot before drying, perhaps he may recite the blessing afterward. Rather, the words of the Taz are explained smoothly: therefore the Tur wrote, etc., meaning, not as you think, that the Tur wrote before drying only regarding the initial practice, but after the fact one may recite the blessing even after drying. Rather, it is not so; after drying, even after the fact, he may not recite the blessing. Therefore the Tur wrote before drying, etc., because afterward he does not recite the blessing at all.
