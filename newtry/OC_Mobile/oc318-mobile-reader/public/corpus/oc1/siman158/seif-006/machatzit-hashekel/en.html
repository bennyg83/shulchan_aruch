(S.k. 6) Liquids, for even, etc. That is to say, although one who washes his hands for fruit is considered among the arrogant, as written in seif 5, since according to the Torah one need not separate terumah from fruit, and since washing was instituted because of the trace of terumah, therefore with fruits, where terumah is only D'Rabbanan, they did not institute washing. Nevertheless, if the fruits, even if they are less than an olive-bulk, are dipped in liquid, they require washing. For specifically with bread they required washing only because of the trace of terumah, but because of chullin it would not have been necessary, since hands are second-degree impurity, and a second-degree impurity does not make a third-degree impurity with chullin. By contrast, with something dipped in liquid, washing is needed because of chullin alone. For we rule that whatever invalidates terumah, namely second-degree impurity, if it touches liquids, the liquids become first-degree impurity. Therefore, when he touches the liquids with his hands, which are second-degree impurity, the liquids become first-degree impurity, and the liquids will make the food second-degree impurity, for a first-degree impurity makes second-degree impurity even with chullin. Therefore washing is required.
