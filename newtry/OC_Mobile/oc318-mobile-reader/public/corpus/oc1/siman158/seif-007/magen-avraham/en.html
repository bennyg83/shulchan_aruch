(ב) Not intending to eat. Such as when they were soiled and he washed them and did not intend to eat then, and changed his mind to eat and did not divert his attention in the interim — the first washing does not count for him, for it was not for holiness at all. Rama ruled that he washes without a berachah, because for those who say one need not have intent, his first washing counts for him and it is not relevant to bless upon it, since he did not intend at all for washing — similar to a menstruant who fell into water, or one who threw a knife to stick it in the wall and it went and slaughtered — one may not bless. And even if he washed for a thing dipped in liquid, according to Tosafot washing is not required at all, as written s.k. 4; therefore he does not bless. And even on the washing now he does not bless, lest his first washing counted.
