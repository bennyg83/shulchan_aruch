(28) There is an opinion, etc. This follows some of the poskim mentioned above in paragraph 20, who hold that there is no obligation today to wash for food dipped in liquid. According to them, such washing does not suffice for eating bread, because it was not performed for the sake of an obligatory washing.
(29) Not in order to eat. For example, his hands were dirty and he washed them without intending to eat, but afterward decided to eat and had not diverted his attention in the meantime. All the more so, the first washing does not suffice, because it was not performed for the sake of sanctity at all.
(30) And if, etc. This refers to the entire seif.
(31) He did not divert his attention. If he diverted his attention from guarding his hands, all agree that he must wash again with a blessing, because the first washing no longer suffices at all.
(32) Without a blessing. Several poskim hold that regarding washing for ordinary food, even if one did not intend the washing as netilah, he fulfills his obligation provided that he did not divert his attention from the time he washed. Their position is explained in Beit Yosef, siman 159, and Shulchan Aruch there, seif 13; see there. He should therefore wash again without a blessing, lest he already fulfilled his obligation with the first washing. No blessing may be recited over the first washing, even if he decided to eat immediately afterward, because that washing was not performed as netilah. Acharonim.
(33) If he touched, etc. This applies even to food dipped in liquid. Mateh Yehudah, and so appears from Biur HaGra; see there.
