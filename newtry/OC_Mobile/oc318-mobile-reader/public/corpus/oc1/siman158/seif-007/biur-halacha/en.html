And if he did not divert his attention, etc. Even if several hours passed before he ate bread, it is evident below, siman 164, seif 1 and the gloss there, that by law he must wash his hands again, and consequently must also recite the blessing, even though he knows that he did not divert his attention from the time of the first washing. This is because he did not stipulate at the time of washing that it should remain effective for later. Although the source there proves only that the washing does not remain effective for the entire day, it is reasonable that it likewise does not remain effective after a lengthy interval.
He should wash without a blessing. See below, siman 159, seif 13, in Mishnah Berurah, that when he has no water he may rely on the first washing.
