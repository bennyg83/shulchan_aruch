Eliyah Rabbah wrote in the name of Knesset HaGedolah, etc., to kindle after Ma'ariv; so too in Shevut Yaakov; afterward he brought Magen Avraham's words and in the name of Knesset HaGedolah; one who examines Shevut Yaakov will see Eliyah Rabbah's words are reversed — there he brought Magen Avraham and wrote on this; however in Knesset HaGedolah it is written "and in my choice, etc., I returned to my custom to kindle after the evening prayer," and such is the essential law — certainly the frequent comes first, as one can do both, as Tosafot wrote on Shabbat 23 — see there; see below siman 684 in Magen Avraham and Peri Chadash there; see what Yad Ephraim wrote there; see in Eshel Avraham here in the name of Shav Yaakov on this; see Machatzit HaShekel who showed room for four views in Maharash's conclusion siman 2, and Machatzit HaShekel concluded it seems one who kindles before going to pray Ma'ariv has not lost, for there is concern that until he prays Ma'ariv the kindling time — half an hour by Gemara law — will pass; see there.
