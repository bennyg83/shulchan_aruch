1) [Seif 1] One may not kindle, etc. Rather, one kindles at the end of sunset. At that time people are passing to and fro and see the lamps, and the lamps also shine, so there is publicizing of the miracle. This is unlike during the day, for what benefit is there from a lamp in broad daylight? Levush.
2) Ibid. Rather, at the end of sunset. This means at tzeis hakochavim, as written in siman 562. Bach; Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahos HaTur, os 1; Magen Avraham, s.k. 1; Eliyah Rabbah, os 1; Maamar Mordechai, os 1; Chayei Adam, rule 154, os 18; Derech HaChaim, os 2; Ben Ish Chai, Parashas Vayeishev, os 7. It is implied that one should not kindle during bein hashmashos, and there is no basis here to apply the rule that a doubt in a rabbinic matter is treated leniently. Eshel Avraham, os 1; Tosafos Chaim on Chayei Adam there, os 24. If one is uncertain whether tzeis hakochavim has already arrived, he should kindle, for it is better to be slightly early than late. Chayei Adam there.
