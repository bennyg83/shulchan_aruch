Se'if 1, one does not light, etc., not, etc., and not, etc. This is the language of the Rambam, except for the word 'end,' which the Mechaber added from the words of the Tur, as written below. This also appears to be the view of Baal Halachos Gedolos. The Rashba and Ran wrote that it is permitted to light earlier; see there, for he proves this, and see there and there at length.
The end of sunset. So wrote the Tur, and so wrote the Rosh in chapter 1 of Taanis regarding what is written there, 'Any fast on which the sun did not set,' etc., and as written in Berachos: and incidentally he teaches us, when do kohanim, etc., as taught in a baraisa, 'and the sun shall set and he shall be pure' - the setting of his sun, etc. But in the Rashba and Ran it is explicitly implied that it is the beginning of sunset, though it is implied there that this means the second sunset; see there. See the Mordechai at the end of chapter 2 of Shabbos, who wrote that the language 'from when the sun sets' proves that it is the beginning of sunset, as written in chapter HaPoalim, 'from when they whiten until they whiten,' etc. This is the main view. See what I wrote above in siman 261, se'if 2, at length.
