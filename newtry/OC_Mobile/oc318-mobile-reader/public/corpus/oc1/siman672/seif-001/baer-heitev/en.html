Until there is no more. The Shiyarei Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that every individual should light after the Maariv prayer, and immediately when its time begins he should not eat and should not learn until he lights; if he began, he stops. So too it is written in Responsa Shevus Yaakov, part 2, siman 40; see there. And the Magen Avraham wrote that one who has not prayed Maariv lights first, for not so much time is required that we should be concerned that perhaps he will forget Maariv. So too the Kenesses HaGedolah wrote that he practiced this way.
