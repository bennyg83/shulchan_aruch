The end of its sunset. This means tzeis hakochavim, as written in siman 562; Bach. In Maseches Sofrim, chapter 20: The mitzvah of lighting it is from when the sun sets, and if he lit it by day, one may not benefit from it and one does not recite a blessing over it, for so they said: one does not recite a blessing over a lamp until one benefits from its light.
