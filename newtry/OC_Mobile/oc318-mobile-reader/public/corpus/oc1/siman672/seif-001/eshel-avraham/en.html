Not later. See Ba'er Heitev s.k. 2 what Magen Avraham wrote. And in Shav Yaakov siman 22 he raised another reason: since Ma'ariv's time is the entire night and we are not concerned for forgetfulness; and moreover we see that even in the synagogue, while there is still much time, they kindle before Ma'ariv on erev Shabbat — yet the aforementioned Gaon struck a spark and three wondrous reasons emerge from him. And since the time of Keriat Shema and kindling come together, certainly one must give precedence to Keriat Shema which is d'oraisa; and also that the frequent comes first; see siman 684 that even in rabbinic matters the frequent comes first; and some say that in siman 678 Hanukkah precedes Kiddush because it is possible with bread. See there that he rejects all the reasons with a spirit of the times — certainly one prays before kindling on erev Shabbat.
