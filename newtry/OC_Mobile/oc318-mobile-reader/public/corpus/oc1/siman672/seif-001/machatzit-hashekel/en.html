(S.k. 1) End, etc. As written in siman 562 regarding completing a fast, that the end of sunset means tzeis hakochavim.
In Maseches Sofrim, etc. And one does not recite a blessing, etc. But if he is preoccupied, when it is permitted to light while it is still day from plag haminchah onward, this means with a blessing, as written in siman 679 regarding erev Shabbos. So too wrote the Pri Chadash.
