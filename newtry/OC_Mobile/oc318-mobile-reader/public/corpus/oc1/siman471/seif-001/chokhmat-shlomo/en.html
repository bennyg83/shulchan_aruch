Seif 1 — forbidden to eat bread from the tenth hour, etc. NB: it appears what Rama permits to eat cooked matzah is if less than kezayit — then since cooking nullifies from bread category and there is also no kezayit — therefore all agree one does not fulfill; unless they are large that each has kezayit alone — then we do not require bread character; if so perhaps one fulfills — therefore not permitted to eat all day; and understand; so it appears correct to me.
