Tenth. That is, immediately after nine hours, which is one quarter of the day; for at nine and a half hours is the time of Minchah Ketanah; and at the beginning of Arvei Pesachim it says one should not eat near Minchah, and that means near Minchah Ketanah. And the Ran wrote that one should be stringent from the time of Minchah Gedolah, that is, from six hours onward; and so wrote Maharil; and so ruled the Rama below in siman 639, se'if 3, in the gloss; and see Chok Yaakov.
Vegetables. And the same law applies that meat and fish are also permitted; and the Tosafos in Eruvin, folio 25, wrote that raw vegetables stimulate the appetite, but cooked ones satiate even with a light amount (Magen Avraham). And Chok Yaakov wrote that there it refers to a fixed manner of eating, that is, when one uses them as a relish with bread; but vegetables alone are permitted to eat, whether raw or cooked.
Much. And he should not drink so much that he becomes satiated, for it certainly spoils the appetite for food, and the senses themselves testify to this (Taz).
