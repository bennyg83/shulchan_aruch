To eat bread. That is, even rich matzah, as below in se'if 2.
From the tenth hour, etc. That is, from the beginning of the fourth hour after midday; and even if he began to eat, he stops, since he began in prohibition.
A little fruit. And the same law applies to meat, fish, eggs, and the like; but of the five species cooked in fruit juice it is forbidden, for they are filling.
Or vegetables. Whether raw or cooked.
A delicate person, that even if he eats a little, etc. And as above in siman 470, se'if 3; except that the delicate person there is one who even if he eats in the morning cannot eat at night with appetite, whereas here we deal with a delicate person who, if he eats from the third hour onward, will no longer be able to eat at night with appetite.
Because it is filling. And it is reasonable that less than a cup, or at least less than most of a cup, is not filling, and is permitted.
Much wine, etc. And two cups of a revi'is, or most of them, should be considered "much"; and [less than that] is permitted.
Stimulates, etc. And nevertheless he should not drink so much that he becomes satiated, for it will certainly spoil the appetite for food, and he may also become intoxicated and nullify the mitzvos of the night.
