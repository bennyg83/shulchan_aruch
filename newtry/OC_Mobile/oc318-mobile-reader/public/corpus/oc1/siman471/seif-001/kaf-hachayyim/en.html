1) [Se'if 1] It is forbidden to eat bread, etc. This includes even matzah ashirah, as stated below in se'if 2. Chametz bread may not be eaten from the beginning of the fifth hour, as stated above at the beginning of siman 443. Matzah with which one can fulfill the obligation at night may not be eaten at any time on the fourteenth, as stated below in se'if 2. See there.
2) There. It is forbidden to eat bread, etc. This applies even to less than an olive-sized quantity. Mishbetzos Zahav, subsection 1; Pis'chei Teshuvah.
3) There. From the tenth hour, etc. The tenth hour itself is included in the prohibition. After nine hours have elapsed, eating bread is forbidden. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Peri Chadash; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1. These are proportional hours, as Rambam writes in his commentary to the Mishnah. Peri Chadash; Machatzis HaShekel, subsection 1; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1. Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes there that whether the day is long or short, eating is always prohibited from the beginning of the final quarter of the day until nightfall. See the gloss above in siman 443, se'if 1, and our words there in subsection 21.
