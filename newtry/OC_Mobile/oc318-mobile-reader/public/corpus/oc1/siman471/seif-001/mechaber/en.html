On Passover Eve After the Tenth Hour It Is Forbidden to Eat Bread. Containing 3 paragraphs: It is forbidden to eat bread from the tenth hour onward, so that one will eat matzah with appetite. However, one may eat a small amount of fruit or vegetables, but should not fill his stomach with them. One should not drink a small amount of wine, because it satiates, but if he wishes to drink a large amount of wine, he may drink, because when he drinks a lot it stimulates the appetite for food. {Rama: (And if he is sensitive such that even eating a little harms his appetite, everything is forbidden.) (Rabbeinu Yerocham)}
