Rich matzah. That is, kneaded in fruit juice; therefore it is permitted to eat, since one does not fulfill with this the obligation of matzah at night, as explained in siman 462; and see there in the Mishnah Berurah that even if it was kneaded in water and some fruit juice was mixed in, one likewise does not fulfill with it as matzah, so long as the taste of fruit juice is discernable in it; and if so, regarding erev Pesach it is permitted to eat, for it is likewise rich matzah; and see there in se'if 4 in the gloss, that in these countries we do not practice kneading in fruit juice.
