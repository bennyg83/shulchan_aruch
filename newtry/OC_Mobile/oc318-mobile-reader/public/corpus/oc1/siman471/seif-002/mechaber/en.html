A minor who does not know what is told at night about the Exodus from Egypt may be fed [matzah] (Terumas HaDeshen, section 7). Some have the custom not to eat lettuce on the eve of Passover in order to eat the bitter herbs with appetite (Tashbetz 455, section 3). Similarly, on the first day of Passover [they refrain from lettuce] in order to eat it on the second night with appetite. Similarly, some have the custom to minimize eating matzah on the first day for this reason (Kol Bo). Some are even more stringent not to eat fruits in order to eat the charoset with appetite, but one need not be concerned with that custom. Some are stringent not to crumble or break the matzos on the eve of Passover so as not to come to eat from them (Mahariv)}
