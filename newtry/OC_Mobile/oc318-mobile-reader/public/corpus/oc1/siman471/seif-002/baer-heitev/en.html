(א) Rich. And if it was kneaded in fruit juice with water as well, it is still rich matzah and one does not fulfill the obligation with it — Chayei Adam and Magen Avraham — see there; and see above siman 462 seif katan 3.

(ב) Day 14. But on the night of the 14th it is permitted — Ran, Bach, and Kaf HaChayyim and Or Zaru'a. And in Shachnahag he wrote that many are accustomed not to eat from Rosh Chodesh erev Pesach. And Magen Avraham wrote that doubled and puffed matzah one may not eat on erev Pesach all day, lest it be matzah — see siman 461.

(ג) And a minor. And the same law applies to a minor girl. Magen Avraham.

(ד) For appetite. And this custom has no reason. Eshel Avraham.

(ה) And kneaded. But crumbled and cooked it is permitted to eat and one blesses haMotzi — nevertheless, for it is a pot-cooked dish and dissolved — Maharil, Magen Avraham, and Kaf HaChayyim.
