Se'if 2 in the gloss: To reduce eating matzah on the first day, etc. And it is written in the Igros of the Ramaz regarding the third meal when the first day of Pesach falls on Shabbos: on Shabbos, and if you ask, it is preferable for us that it be like erev Pesach because of the doubt of our second day of Yom Tov; and nevertheless one should eat some little fruit, enough for a blessing, and not a great amount, because of the mitzvah of eating matzah with appetite on the night of the second Yom Tov; see there. And it appears that one who never forgoes making the third meal with bread is permitted, and he should eat a little, and he should also take care to begin early; and see above, siman 444.
