3) There. From the tenth hour, etc. The tenth hour itself is included in the prohibition. After nine hours have elapsed, eating bread is forbidden. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1; Magen Avraham, subsection 1; Chok Yaakov, subsection 1; Peri Chadash; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Ma'amar Mordechai, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1. These are proportional hours, as Rambam writes in his commentary to the Mishnah. Peri Chadash; Machatzis HaShekel, subsection 1; Eshel Avraham, subsection 1. Shulchan Aruch HaRav writes there that whether the day is long or short, eating is always prohibited from the beginning of the final quarter of the day until nightfall.
