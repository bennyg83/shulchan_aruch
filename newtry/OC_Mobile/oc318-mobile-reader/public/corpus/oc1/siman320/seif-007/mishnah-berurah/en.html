(א) And boiled — meaning vegetables and the like that were boiled before Shabbat and their liquid remained in them:

(ב) If for themselves — meaning he squeezes the beverage floating on them and absorbed in them in order to correct their bodies for eating alone and he does not need their liquid, like the manner to squeeze vegetables called salads after they soaked them in water — therefore it is not included in extracting at all, and even though he squeezes into the bowl and the beverage does not go to waste, nevertheless since he does not intend for the beverage it is not included in melacha; but in olives and grapes forbidden in every manner because most of the world squeezes them for their liquid:

(ג) Rather, etc. — and likewise permitted l'chatchila to squeeze from fat absorbed in them if he does so because he cannot eat them because of much fat in them, so wrote Derishah and Chayei Adam lenient for another reason that fat he separates is also food and is like separating food from food:

(ד) Into a bowl, etc. — and specifically for Shabbat need, but forbidden to squeeze in every manner for Motzei Shabbat need [Gemara]:

(ה) Into a pot, etc. — for it is not worse than a cluster that is permitted as is written seif 4:

(ו) That there is food in it — and likewise into morayis which is food, but into beverages — forbidden:

(ז) Forbidden — and even though other fruits permitted to squeeze even for their liquid as above in seif 1, here is different for there was no beverage on the fruit liquid and it is like separating food from food, unlike here where there was beverage upon it before it was absorbed and the name of squeezing applies; nevertheless chatas liability there is none according to this view who holds that from the Torah one is liable only for squeezing olives and grapes alone, as is written at the beginning of the siman:

(ח) And Rabbeinu Chananel, etc. — behold R' Chaim disagrees in two matters with the first view who holds that if he needs their liquid he is liable even in pickled and boiled, for wherever he needs their liquid forever he is liable even when squeezing into food, even though all poskim disagree on this who hold beverage coming to food is like food as is written seif 4, and the halacha is like their words, and therefore the Mechaber mentioned their view in his plain language; nevertheless he also brought R' Chaim's view to teach that it is good to be stringent like his words, and one who is stringent — blessing upon him, as is written in responsa of Rosh brought in Beit Yosef:
