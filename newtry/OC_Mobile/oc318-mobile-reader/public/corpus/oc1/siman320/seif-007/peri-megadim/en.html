And according to Rabbeinu Tam — other fruits. Scribal error in Shulchan Aruch — Rabbeinu Tam, for he holds it is considered like food only unripe fruit forbidden for him like olives and grapes; and Rabbeinu Chananel must hold the halacha is not like Rav and Shmuel — only like R' Yochanan Shabbos 145a that even into the pot forbidden; and even on Yom Tov forbidden — unlike "there is no establishing" the words of Rav and Shmuel on Yom Tov — behold on Shabbat forbidden (on Yom Tov even melacha that is permitted like borer and the like in siman 495); and if so difficult on the Mechaber here — he brought Rabbeinu Chananel's words — it implies on Yom Tov also there is no halacha that permits (see Tosafos 145a "and R' Yochanan"); and on Yom Tov stam that permits — other fruits and Magen Avraham there sign 2. And one may say Rabbeinu Chananel forbids because of borer in two types of food — Magen Avraham 319 seif 16; and on Yom Tov borer permitted; and in truth requires study, for one may say beverage to food is considered like food — meaning in a cluster it is not borer of one species; and in pickled vegetables for their liquids when squeezing to eat immediately — and in Taz sign 4 — if after time in pickled vegetables forbidden, for it is two species; and for Magen Avraham 7 it is reversed and Peri Chadash. Understand and requires study. And what he wrote "likewise" is difficult — as written nearby one may say this that pickled vegetables are stricter because it is borer; and proof that the Mechaber wrote pickled vegetables for eating permitted — all the more from grapes we learn as we say; and see that our master z"l hinted here that pickled vegetables that came out by themselves permitted — meaning according to R' Yehuda that pickled vegetables also liable for chatas — nevertheless what came out by themselves one may say permitted; and grapes that came out by themselves forbidden — all the more not for eating forbidden for Rabbeinu Chananel — see Rif.
