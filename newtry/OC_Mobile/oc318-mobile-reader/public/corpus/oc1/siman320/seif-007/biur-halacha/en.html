(א) And for R' Chiya, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah what we wrote that all the poskim argue on R' Chiya; and this is regarding the second law concerning a beverage that comes to food; but regarding his first law that he rules that in pickled vegetables and blanched vegetables there is liability of a chatas when he squeezed them for their water — many early authorities hold like him, namely Rashba, Rokeach, Or Zarua, and Yere'im according to Ra'avad; and their reason is they ruled like R' Yehuda, for we rule like him against Rav and Shmuel who hold pickled vegetables and blanched vegetables — when he squeezed them for their water he is liable; and the reason of Rif and his supporters is explained in Rif. And know further that even according to R' Chiya who holds like R' Yehuda — nevertheless he admits other fruits are permitted to squeeze for their water l'chatchila; and as stated above in seif 1 [and therefore the Mechaber did not mention at all in seif 1 this view regarding other fruits even as a mere stringency]; for so it is stated explicitly in the R' Chiya we have: they squeeze l'chatchila in pagim and perishin; and therefore his reason is because he holds pickled vegetables and blanched vegetables are fit to be squeezed, unlike other fruits; or because of what is absorbed in pickled vegetables — there is beverage upon it beforehand, as is written in Mishna Berurah. And behold in the gloss of Semak he proved from this that in pickled vegetables and blanched vegetables even in squeezing fruits it is forbidden for their water; and in truth from R' Chiya's explanation the opposite is proven; and therefore the reason is as we wrote, and we already explained at length above in Biur Halacha, see there:
