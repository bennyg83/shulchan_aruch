Pickled produce (meaning fruits and types of vegetables placed in vinegar and salt so they will not rot) and boiled greens — if for themselves, that he does not need their water and does not squeeze them except to fix them for eating — even if he squeezes into a bowl that has no food in it, permitted; and if he needs their water — permitted to squeeze into a pot in which there is food; but if there is no food in it — forbidden.
