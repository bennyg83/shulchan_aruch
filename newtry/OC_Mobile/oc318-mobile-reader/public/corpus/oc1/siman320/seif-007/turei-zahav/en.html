And for Rabbenu Tam, etc., even if he squeezed into a pot, etc. For he holds we do not rule like R' Chiya who said one milks into a pot that has food in it; rather even on Yom Tov forbidden, as all this is in Rif and Rosh; only they disagree, for we rule like R' Chiya that one milks on Yom Tov into a pot that has food, for a beverage that comes to food is like food; and one may wonder on Shulchan Aruch that he did not rule here, and in siman 495 regarding Yom Tov he ruled like Rif that permitted to milk into food and did not bring at all the view of R' Chiya and Rabbenu Tam who forbid; and if so, here too l'chatchila they squeeze pickled vegetables and blanched vegetables to extract their water into food; and what Rama wrote here "and according to his words one who squeezes a cluster into a pot is also forbidden" — astonishing language; for what also that he says it is a fortiori — behold grapes are stricter according to his words, for even when they emerge by themselves they are forbidden, unlike pickled vegetables and blanched vegetables; and if so plainly forbidden even into a pot; and it appears to me he did not say "also" except regarding Shulchan Aruch's words — meaning according to his words that which is written in seif 4 that permitted to squeeze a cluster, etc. — is according to his words; only there is also a dispute, and whoever forbids here certainly forbids there; and in truth Rama did not need this, since we already rule leniently as stated:
