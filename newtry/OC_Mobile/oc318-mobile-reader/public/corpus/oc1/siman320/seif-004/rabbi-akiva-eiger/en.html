There "and place the vessel upon it." And so in the Gemara "and he places the drawing vessel upon it" — it implies that spreading a cloth alone there is no prohibition, only on account of the drawing vessel that is on liquids and the cloth becomes soiled from its liquids from it. However it is difficult, for we challenge on it and what is different from a funnel? And see Tosafos who challenge from Rav's statement in chapter Chullin; and behold there no drawing vessel on it is mentioned at all.
