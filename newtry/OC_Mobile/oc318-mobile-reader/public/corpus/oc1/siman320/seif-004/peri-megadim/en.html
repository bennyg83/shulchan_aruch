Bach wrote if they put; but from comparing to snow — and so Magen Avraham sign 5 wrote; and Taz challenged that certainly to put l'chatchila on Shabbat so they split is forbidden rabbinically, for it is like threshing k'lachar yad; and if so the grapes are forbidden when they split seif 61 — one who cooks even in Tiberias hot springs, etc.; and the grapes are recognizable — then what comes out even in a thousand is not nullified; and there is a dispute regarding snow — there only coals of water or nolad, and whatever is mixed permitted — unlike here.
