(4) [Dress] Permitted to squeeze them, etc. And there are those who forbid, and likewise to squeeze apples (Dress of Yom Tov); and likewise Riaz in Shilta Gibborim chapter Keilim forbids even to dip in it; and Magen Avraham at the beginning of this siman wrote: these pears called pears in Ashkenaz language — forbidden to squeeze them because in Ashkenaz countries they were accustomed to squeeze them for beverage; and it is possible that if the whole world had many they would also squeeze; and it appears to me to squeeze lemons and apples into a beverage or into food — plainly permitted for all of them; and even not into a beverage or food one may permit regarding lemons, for Rabbeinu Yeruham wrote that thus they are accustomed; and so it appears to me from Sefer Tanya and Shibbolei HaLeket that apples are inferior and forbidden even for those who permit regarding lemons — see Magen Avraham at the beginning of this siman; and if the apple is cooked — permitted to squeeze it, for one squeezes all food from it (Taz).
