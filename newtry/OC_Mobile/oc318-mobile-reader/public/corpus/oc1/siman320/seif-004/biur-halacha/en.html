(א) Into a pot, etc. — And the same into a bowl; and even though from Rashi's words it appears that in a bowl, even if there is cooked food in it — also forbidden because it is not evident that he fixes the beverage [so is Rashi's explanation and so in Sefer Mateh Yehuda and so I found 'iri, unlike some Acharonim who did not understand Rashi's words thus]; from the words of all the early poskim — namely Bahag, Rif, Rosh, Rambam, Semag, Semak, Tur, Rabbeinu Yerucham, and Me'iri — it appears there is no distinction at all between a pot and other vessels if there is cooked food in them; and that which the Gemara distinguished between a pot and a bowl is because a pot by default has cooked food in it and a bowl by default does not have cooked food in it:

(ב) For it is a beverage, etc. — From Shulchan Aruch's language without specification it appears he holds for the essence like the view of the author of HaHashlamah brought in Beit Yosef that even in a cluster designated for beverage — also permitted; and so ruled Me'iri:

(ג) That comes to food — But into a vessel that has beverage in it — forbidden, and there is also liability for this — so Acharonim and Shach, and plain:
