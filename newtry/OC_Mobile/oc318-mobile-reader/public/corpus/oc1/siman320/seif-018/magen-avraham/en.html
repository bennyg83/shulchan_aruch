(א) Psik reisha. Rosh wrote chapter 17 of Shabbos that even for the Ikkur who holds psik reisha de'lo nicha lei is permitted — nevertheless in other prohibitions he concedes it is forbidden, from what they challenge end of chapter 14 of Zevachim and behold he extinguishes, etc., see there; and the Gaon challenged in Sefer Beit HaZahav in name of Maharal of Prague: what proof does he bring from the questioner, for we conclude for R' Shimon it is permitted, and also in chapter HaBoneh the Rosh brings proof from this for the words of the Ikkur, see there what he wrote; and in my opinion he wrote nothing, for the Rosh brings proof from it that he holds that even in all prohibitions we say thus, and it appears to me to reconcile: for we rule the halacha is not like Shmuel as Rambam wrote chapter 16 of Moed Katan, and as is evident in Menachos, and it is difficult why in truth we do not rule like him, and one must say because of the questioner's difficulty regarding extinguishing even though it is psik reisha de'lo nicha lei and permitted per the Ikkur — nevertheless specifically regarding Shabbat where melacha requires intent the Torah forbade, but in other prohibitions forbidden; therefore he properly brings proof from the questioner:
