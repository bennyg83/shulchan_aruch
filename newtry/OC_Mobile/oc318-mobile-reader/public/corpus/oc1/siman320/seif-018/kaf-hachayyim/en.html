This does not resemble an item that will later become permitted, which could be left until after Shabbos and is not nullified even in a thousand. That rule applies when the forbidden substance first exists distinctly and afterward becomes mixed with permitted food. Here the liquid was never recognizable; as each small amount emerges, it mixes with the liquid that emerged the previous day. Beis Yosef in the name of Mordechai; Taz, paragraph 3; Magen Avraham, paragraph 5. Accordingly, if the grapes lie separately and the wine flows down an incline into wine already present before Shabbos, all the wine may not be used on Shabbos because it is an item that will later become permitted. Before mixing, the newly emerged wine existed distinctly and was recognizable. Sefer HaTerumah, siman 223.
