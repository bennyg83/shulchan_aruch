(r) There: nullified in the wine in the vat. And it is not comparable to davar sheyeish lo matirin that one can wait until after Shabbat, for even in a thousand it is not nullified — that is when the prohibition is in existence at first and afterward mixed with the permitted; but these were never recognizable except little by little as they come out and mix with the liquids that came out from yesterday. Beit Yosef in the name of Mordechai, Taz s.k. 3, Magen Avraham s.k. 5; and therefore if the grapes stand by themselves and the wine drips from them on a slope and descends into wine that was already from erev Shabbat — all the wine is forbidden to rely on it on Shabbat because of davar sheyeish lo matirin, for before they mixed it was in existence and recognizable.
