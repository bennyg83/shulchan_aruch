Ran and other poskim explained that there are two types of squeezing. One is because it is a toldah of laundering, namely whitening — therefore the prohibition is only in water but not in other beverages; and for halacha Tur and Shulchan Aruch ruled in siman 319 that wine is like other beverages; only here Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Ran that wine launders. And it appears to me to distinguish that white wine launders like water, but red wine does not launder — on the contrary it reddens the garment. And further, squeezing is also due to being a toldah of dosh and threshing, and the prohibition is only when it is pleasing to him in what emerges from it, like in dosh and threshing; except per the other view that here it is forbidden in any case d'rabbanan even when not pleasing to him; and therefore if beer or other beverages that do not launder spilled on the tablecloth on the table and he wishes to clean it — in any case he should not draw up from there into one palm and pour outside, for even though he will throw it to waste — nevertheless here at the time he draws into the palm he commits a full prohibition and it is a toldah of threshing; and in this all agree to the prohibition, and it will not help that he afterward pours the palm outside — this is crooked and cannot be corrected; only with a knife there is permission to push off on the tablecloth and scrape by means of the beverage that spilled on the tablecloth and let it flow to waste — only he should not scrape with force, for otherwise he commits a d'rabbanan prohibition at least per the other view; and one must warn in what he can warn.
