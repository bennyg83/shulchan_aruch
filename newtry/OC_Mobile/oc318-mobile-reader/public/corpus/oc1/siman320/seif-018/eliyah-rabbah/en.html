[20] [Levush] And it is not comparable to laundering, etc. Malbushei Yom Tov objects that the comparison to laundering in the sponge case is unclear, for Rashi explains wiping as wiping a board. Perhaps the author understood it as wiping a garment, but then the source for saying it is unlike laundering is even less clear. With due respect, squeezing in this context is prohibited because of laundering, as explained at the end of seif 15. I also found Mordechai, chapter Tolin, explaining that one wipes wine and oil absorbed in a bowl and in a garment.
