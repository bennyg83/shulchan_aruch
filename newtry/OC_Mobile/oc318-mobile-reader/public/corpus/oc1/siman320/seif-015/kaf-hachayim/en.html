(o) [Seif 2] Olives and grapes that were crushed from erev Shabbat, etc. Explanation: even if he put them for drinking; and all the more strawberries and pomegranates — see there or 16; and Bach wrote it deals when they were crushed well so they lack nothing except crushing; but if they lack — the liquids coming from them are forbidden; and so Olat Shabbat there; and this is according to Tur who wrote they were crushed well; but according to Shulchan Aruch even if they lack crushing — permitted, as written above siman 252 or 59 that there is a difference between Tur's view and Shulchan Aruch's view — see there; and nevertheless l'chatchila one should be stringent like Tur's view:
