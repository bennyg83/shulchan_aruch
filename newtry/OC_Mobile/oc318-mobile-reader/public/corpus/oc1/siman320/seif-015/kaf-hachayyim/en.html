15) [Seif 2] Olives and grapes that were crushed before Shabbos, etc. This means even if he placed them there for drinking, and certainly applies to mulberries and pomegranates. Olas Shabbos, paragraph 10. Bach writes that they must have been thoroughly crushed and require only grinding; if they still require pressing, the liquid that emerges is forbidden. Olas Shabbos there writes the same. This follows Tur, who writes that they were thoroughly crushed. According to Shulchan Aruch, however, they are permitted even if they still require pressing, as stated above in siman 252, paragraph 59, where the distinction between Tur and Shulchan Aruch is explained; see there. Bach's question why Shulchan Aruch omitted the word 'thoroughly' is therefore unfounded, since Shulchan Aruch follows his own position stated in siman 252. Nevertheless, one should initially be stringent like Tur.
