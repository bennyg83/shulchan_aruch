(א) Lest come to squeezing — lest he squeeze the cloth from water absorbed in it through the vessel, and one who squeezes a garment is liable on account of whitening; and it deals when the barrel was then empty, for otherwise spreading a cloth itself is forbidden on account of squeezing concern even if he does not place a wet vessel on it:
