(א) That they were crushed — Tur wrote that they were crushed well, and his intent is we require that they be ground thoroughly; and as he himself wrote above in siman 252. And the Mechaber intentionally omitted the word "well," for he follows his view there that even when lacking grinding the beverages that emerge are permitted — as Gra and Ma'amar Mordechai wrote there [and Bach wondered in vain at Shulchan Aruch]. And for this reason he also omitted in Shulchan Aruch what Tur brought in the name of Bahag regarding a cask that they beat with sticks — for b'ein he wrote this according to his view that forbids with crushing alone all that lacks grinding, see there in his words; unlike the Mechaber. And for the law we already wrote above in siman 252 in Biur Halacha in the name of Acharonim that one should not permit with crushing alone all that lacks grinding — like the view of Tur and Bahag:
