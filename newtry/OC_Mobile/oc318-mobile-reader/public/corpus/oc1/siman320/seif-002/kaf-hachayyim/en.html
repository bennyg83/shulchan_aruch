2) There: Olives and grapes may not be squeezed. This constitutes extracting, for which one is liable as a derivative of threshing. Rambam, chapter 21, halachah 12; Levush; Olas Shabbos, paragraph 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 1. One who squeezes raisins is likewise liable for threshing. Chayei Adam, klal 14, paragraph 2. Squeezing them into food is permitted, however, as stated below in seif 4.
