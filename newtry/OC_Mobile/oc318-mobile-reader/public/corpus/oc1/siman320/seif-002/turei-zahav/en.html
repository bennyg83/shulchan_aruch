Maharach z"l wrote: it appears to me that even if he put whole grapes on Shabbat inside the wine and they split open on that Shabbat — also permitted, just as permitted to put on Shabbat snow or hail into the cup, as is written above in this siman, end of his words. And in my humble opinion not so, for if so it would be permitted even l'chatchila to put grapes there like snow and hail — and this is certainly a full prohibition due to squeezing, which is a toldah of dosh, as Rashi explained in the chapter of the barrel; and one could say it is like k'lachar yad since he does not thresh with his hands — for even k'lachar yad is permitted only in a place of distress like that case of milking a goat; and further, behold even when it emerges by itself it is forbidden due to the prohibition of a toldah of dosh, even though hands did not touch it; rather the law here is like one who cooks on Shabbat, and permission is only b'dieved when it is nullified against the permitted — and this matter is not comparable at all to snow and hail below. And Mordechai wrote: that which it is nullified here and it is not Dishil'am — even in a thousand it is not nullified — that applies to a prohibition that was initially recognizable b'ein; unlike here where as soon as it emerged it became mixed and nullified in the permitted:
