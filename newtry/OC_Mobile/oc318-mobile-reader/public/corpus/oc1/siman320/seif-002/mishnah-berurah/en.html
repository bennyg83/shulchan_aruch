(א) That were crushed from erev Shabbat — for since they were crushed the beverage will flow by itself, and there is no longer chatas liability even if he squeezes by hand, and therefore they did not decree on beverages that come out:

(ב) Nullified — and even though after Shabbat it will be permitted for him and we rule that davar sheyeish lo matirim even in a thousand is not nullified — that is in a prohibition that was recognizable initially in the substance and afterward became mixed, unlike here where little by little as it comes out it mixes immediately and is nullified in sixty in the permitted wine that was already in it, and the name of prohibition does not apply; therefore if the grapes sit by themselves and wine flows from them in a slope and descends into wine that was already there from erev Shabbat — all the wine is forbidden to rely on it because of davar sheyeish lo matirim, for before it mixed it was recognizable [Sefer HaTerumah siman 223].
