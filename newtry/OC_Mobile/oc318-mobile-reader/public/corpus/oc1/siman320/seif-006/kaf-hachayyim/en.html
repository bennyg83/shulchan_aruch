6) There: Mulberries and pomegranates may not be squeezed. Some people who possess many mulberries or pomegranates squeeze them for juice as they do olives and grapes. If other people had such quantities, they too would ordinarily squeeze them for juice. Therefore, one who squeezes them for juice causes his intent to give them rabbinic beverage status, and we do not say that his view is disregarded against that of everyone else. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there.
