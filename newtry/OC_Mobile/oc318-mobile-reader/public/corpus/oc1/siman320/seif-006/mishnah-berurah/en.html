(22) To squeeze lemons — and in our language citrons, meaning even for beverages, and even though the manner on weekdays is to squeeze them, since there is no manner at all to squeeze them for beverage need but to dip food in it — it is like other fruits as above in seif 1; and even if the manner on weekdays is to squeeze them into water where they put sugar to drink for pleasure — nevertheless the world practices leniency to do this on Shabbat, and possible because it is forbidden only when the manner is to drink the squeezed fruit liquid also without mixture of another beverage, or because it is forbidden only when they squeeze its liquid alone and afterward mix them, but if the practice is to squeeze its liquid into another beverage to give taste — permitted [Beit Yosef and Taz]. And according to this nowadays where it is known they fill a barrel for hundreds to drink with punch in some places — great doubt whether permitted to squeeze them on Shabbat into beverage, for possible it is comparable to berries and pomegranates since the manner is to squeeze them in vessels by themselves, and therefore one must be careful to squeeze beforehand on the sugar by itself, for it is beverage coming to food; and likewise to squeeze onto a dish and on the food — obviously permitted as is written seif 4.
