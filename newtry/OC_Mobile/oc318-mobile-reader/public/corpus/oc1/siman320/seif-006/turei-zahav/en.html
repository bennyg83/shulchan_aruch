Permitted to squeeze lemons. Beit Yosef wrote that in Egypt they practice on Shabbat to squeeze lemons into water in which they put sugar to drink for pleasure, and we have not seen anyone protest against them, etc., end of his words; and he expanded somewhat on the reason for the permission; and the essence is as Beit Yosef afterward wrote in the name of Rashal — another Acharon's view that everyone knows he does it to sweeten food and not for the need of beverage, etc.; and the same here he does not intend except to give taste to the wine. And regarding squeezing apples it appears to me that those who squeeze in order to soften the apple in the manner they do when the apple is hard — permitted, for it is like sweetening that we mentioned nearby regarding pomegranates; and even though it is stated in the Gemara that there is apple wine — if so one should decree even in this lest he squeeze to extract its wine, like we decree regarding pomegranines for Rashi above; and so Beit Yosef compared them above in this siman; nevertheless it appears we do not decree regarding apples, since we already saw in the Gemara chapter of the barrel that it depends on local custom whether they make beverages from fruits, as we say: are the places of the Arabs comparable to the house of Menashe? Their intent is nullified, etc.; and so Beit Yosef established for halacha as he wrote: however, this applies when the manner of the people of that place is to squeeze to extract its water, etc.; and if so in our country where we have not heard at all of making apple wine — we do not decree for it; and we say without specification squeezing apples is to soften; and even though he drinks what emerges from it — he did not intend in the squeezing for that, since it is not designated for that; and if the apple is cooked and he squeezes that liquid emerge from it — plainly permitted, for he squeezed all the food from it and not to extract its water from it, as appears correct to me:
