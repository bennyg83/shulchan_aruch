To squeeze lemons — See in Mishna Berurah what we wrote that this is even for beverages; behold the source of this law is from responsum of Rosh brought in Beit Yosef: and you asked regarding squeezing lemons — it appears they are included in other fruits that are permitted to squeeze, for it is not the general manner to squeeze lemons for the need of beverage but for the need of food, and permitted to squeeze them on Shabbat, end of his words; and therefore he means: since the custom is not to squeeze them for their water — therefore one who wishes to squeeze them for their water is permitted like the law of other fruits; for certainly Rosh did not need to permit squeezing them into food, for even a cluster of grapes is permitted to squeeze into food, and all the more so lemons that are like other fruits, end of Sefer Mateh Yehuda; and so it appears from Magen Avraham and other Acharonim that Shulchan Aruch's intent is even for beverages. And know that what we wrote at the end of our words that permitted to squeeze into cooked food and on top of food — this is that in this one need not be concerned for the stringency of R' Chiya that we wrote regarding a cluster of grapes; for per the first resolution of Beit Yosef that I brought in Mishna Berurah — in every manner one should not be stringent regarding lemons:
