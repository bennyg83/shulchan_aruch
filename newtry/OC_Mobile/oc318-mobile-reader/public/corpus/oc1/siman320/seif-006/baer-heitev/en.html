Lemons. Since it is not the general manner to squeeze them for the need of beverage but for the need of food — it is like other fruits, see Magen Avraham seif 1. And even if they practice squeezing it into water to drink it — permitted, since it is not the manner to drink it alone — Beit Yosef. And pickled lemons in salt — their law is like pickled vegetables and blanched vegetables, see seif 7; and regarding squeezing apples it appears to me that those who squeeze in order to soften the apple in the manner they do when the apple is hard — permitted, for it is like sweetening as is written in s.k. 2; and even though he drinks what emerges from it — he did not intend in the squeezing for that, since it is not designated for that; and if the apple is cooked and he squeezes that liquid emerge from it — plainly permitted, for he squeezed all the food from it — Taz, see there:
