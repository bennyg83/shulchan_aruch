Permitted — other fruits. According to his view in sign 1 that other fruits for beverages forbidden — only stam for seasoning; likewise what they squeeze into water to give taste in water — it is in other fruits like for seasoning; unlike grapes — even into water to give taste and the like for other beverages there is chatas liability; and the same applies to strawberries and pomegranates — thus forbidden; only in other fruits permitted — this they did not consider beverage. And apples permitted, etc. — from the flow of his words it appears since what Magen Avraham sign 1 wrote: even Beit Yosef in his book Aruch intended that place alone is forbidden like the Sages of R' Eliezer in maintaining thorns, etc. — see there; and if so — even in one country they make wine from apples — forbidden there to squeeze apples even to give taste in beverage — forbidden; other countries permitted; and in Magen Avraham it will be explained further. What the Mechaber wrote seif 4 — into the pot that has stew — there is no stew in it; although he squeezes to eat — forbidden because of mar'it ayin: one who sees will say for beverage he needs it; and therefore the Gemara mentions a bowl — although they do not drink in a bowl — against Rashi and Rif; and in pomegranates it also appears thus: whatever has no food actually inside — forbidden — Magen Avraham 16:
