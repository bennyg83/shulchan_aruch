To eat. Automatically it is like borer — food from refuse — Tur. And the same applies to other fruits that are not fit for eating — forbidden to squeeze them for their water — Magen Avraham. And Taz wrote that according to this, if he wishes to eat immediately that thing into which he squeezes the unripe grapes — permitted, as above siman 319 regarding borer, see there; and so Elyah Rabbah wrote that one may permit to squeeze for the need of that meal, see there. And Magen Avraham wondered at them: is one permitted to do melacha in order to eat immediately? And so Hagahot on chapter 7 of Shabbat. Rather the reason regarding borer in his hand to eat immediately is not called borer, for it is the manner of eating; and therefore in a cannon and in a serving dish forbidden to separate even to eat immediately.
