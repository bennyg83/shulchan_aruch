This is like borer food from refuse [Tur], and according to what is written siman 202 and Yoreh De'ah siman 44 that we are not expert which is called unripe — therefore Rama wrote as long as it is not fit for eating it is forbidden, and likewise in other fruits that are not fit for eating forbidden to squeeze them for their liquid; Yerushalmi wrote there is to permit to squeeze for the need of that meal as is written siman 319, etc., see there, and it does not appear precise to me, for is one permitted to do melacha in order to eat immediately? And likewise Shevut Yaakov chapter 7 of Shabbos; rather the reason in borer by hand for immediate eating is not called selecting, for it is the manner of eating, and likewise the reason in cutting for immediate eating as is written siman 321 seif 12, see there in Beit Yosef; therefore in a bowl and spoon forbidden to select even for immediate eating, and likewise refuse from food forbidden, all the more forbidden to squeeze unripe grapes — know that Ran wrote the reason milking a goat is forbidden is on account of borer food from refuse — is it permitted for immediate eating?
