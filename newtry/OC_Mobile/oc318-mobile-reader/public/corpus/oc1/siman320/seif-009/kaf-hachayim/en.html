(i) There: gloss: and in a place where they were accustomed to squeeze some fruits, etc. Although the plain words of our teacher z"l appear that the prohibition is only for the people of that place and not for the whole world — nevertheless since we find a source for this law from Beit Yosef's words, and there he explained: if it became known to us that in some place they squeeze it for its liquid — forbidden to squeeze in every place, as we find regarding pomegranates — if so one must explain the gloss's words thus; and if there is a place accustomed to squeeze — its law is like strawberries and pomegranates that it is forbidden for the whole world; and the language "and in a place" is not precise — necessarily it is so, since he concludes and wrote its law is like strawberries and pomegranates; and it is proven that just as those are forbidden for the whole world, so too for all other fruits — what is the difference; and not like Magen Avraham s.k. 1 who wrote that our teacher z"l disputes Beit Yosef and holds the prohibition is only for people of that place who were accustomed to squeeze — it does not exist; and the essence is Beit Yosef's view and likewise the gloss's view. Mateh Yehudah or 2 — see there; and likewise Chida or 3 greatly challenged Magen Avraham's words here and concluded the essence is like Bach; and so Rama holds, and it is evident from itself since he wrote its law is like strawberries and pomegranates — see there. And these pears called in Aramaic (and in Arabic language anjas) — even Magen Avraham there wrote forbidden to squeeze them because in Ashkenaz countries they were accustomed to squeeze them for beverage — see there; and likewise apples forbidden to squeeze because in Ashkenaz countries they fill many barrels from them for beverage, as Chayei Adam general 14 or 3 wrote; and all we wrote is according to those who permit squeezing other fruits; however we already wrote in the previous or that l'chatchila one should be concerned for those who forbid, and one should not squeeze any fruit anywhere for beverage purposes — see there:
