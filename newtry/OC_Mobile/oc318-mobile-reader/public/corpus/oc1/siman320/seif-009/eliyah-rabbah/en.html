[12] [Levush] The status of liquids, etc. This gives me some support for ruling that meat soaked for twenty four hours in water that then congealed is considered pickled, unlike Rama in Yoreh Deah siman 104 regarding goose fat that hardened and is not treated as pickling liquid. Here there is still moisture, which has the status of liquid for every purpose. Yet this requires study because Maggid Mishneh, chapter 21, and Ran at the end of chapter Bameh Tomnin imply that whoever permits placing hail in the sun likewise permits congealed fat, while whoever prohibits fat because of nolad likewise prohibits hail. Magen Avraham also implies this. Shulchan Aruch and Levush, however, cite two views regarding fat in siman 318, seif 16, while here they rule simply to permit, implying they consider this case more lenient. Perhaps they discuss snow mixed with water, though the wording does not imply so. The primary law of meat soaked in liquid will be explained in Yoreh Deah. See Shach, siman 69, seif katan 11.
