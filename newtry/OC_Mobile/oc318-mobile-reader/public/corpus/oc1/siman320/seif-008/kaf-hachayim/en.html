(h) There: and all other fruits permitted to squeeze them. In Beit Yosef he brought a dispute in this: for Rif and Rambam and Rosh and Tur it appears that in other fruits one may squeeze l'chatchila even for their liquids; but for Rashi and Tosafos and Hagahot Semak — only to squeeze for seasoning permitted, but for their liquids forbidden — see there; and from the Mechaber's words stam it appears that even for their liquids permitted to squeeze; but Bach wrote one should not rule leniently on any fruit to squeeze it for its liquid — unlike what Shulchan Aruch wrote stam; and also in Hagahot Shulchan Aruch he followed him that it does not exist; and thus the whole world is accustomed to forbid every fruit to squeeze for its liquids — end of his words; and they brought Magen Avraham s.k. 1; and wrote: certainly where there is a custom — custom applies; but nevertheless where he does not squeeze except to dip food in it — permitted, for it is not called beverage, as Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket and Rosh — see there; and so Rabbi Zalman end of or 1 that the essence is like the first view; but in a place where they were accustomed to be stringent — one should not change. And what Taz s.k. 1 distinguished — that even according to Shulchan Aruch who wrote "and all other fruits permitted to squeeze them" — meaning stam; but when intending to extract beverage from them — forbidden — already Chida, Nechmadot, and Mateh Yehudah or 1 challenged him — see there; nevertheless for practical law: although the essence is like the first view which is Shulchan Aruch's view and those who agree with him, and only where there is custom to be stringent one should not change as Rabbi Zalman wrote — nevertheless l'chatchila one should be concerned for the view of those who forbid; and so Chayei Adam general 14 or 3, Ben Ish Chai Parashat Yitro or 3:
