(14) [Dress] The Rosh forbids, etc. Maharshal and Bach from siman 326 forbade to warm in the hands after netilat yadayim even though he did not intend to warm the water; and Taz rejected their words and permitted because it is impossible to be careful, for the whole land is full of snow; and in my humble opinion even so it is not proof for my humble opinion, for there it is in a place of yad soledes bo because of bishul, and it is also like one who washes his hands; and further in Shilta Gibborim end of chapter Bameh Madlikin it is explained that one who permits to warm in the sun permits also in this — therefore one may be lenient unless it is possible without exertion.
