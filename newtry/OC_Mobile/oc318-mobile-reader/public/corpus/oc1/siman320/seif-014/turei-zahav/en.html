Urine in snow. Tur wrote its reason that he compares it to trampling with the feet that one tramples and is not concerned. And Rosh wrote that it is not comparable to trampling with the feet, for through urine it certainly dissolves, end of his words; and Rashal wrote there is some proof to forbid, for in siman 326 we forbid to warm hands after netilat yadayim even though he did not intend at all to warm the water — it is considered psik reisha; and the same here, end of his words. And according to what is stated nearby, trampling is certainly psik reisha, and even so they did not decree; so too here, since it is impossible to be careful because the entire land is full of snow; and some say regarding warming hands that it is possible otherwise — they decreed regarding it; so it appears correct to me:
