11) There, gloss: But it is permitted to suck, etc. Although nursing directly from an animal is forbidden, as stated in siman 328, seif 33, this is because it is the ordinary manner. Here it is unusual, so the Sages did not decree against it. Beis Yosef in the name of Shibolei HaLeket; Magen Avraham, paragraph 2; Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 4. Taz, paragraph 2, writes that Shibolei HaLeket contains a scribal error, but Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 2, correctly notes that he was imprecise, because its words refer to nursing from an animal. Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 6, likewise challenges him; see there.
