And other fruits. Beit Yosef holds that Rashi and Tur dispute on two matters: for Rashi — two baraitot regarding other fruits: for seasoning permitted, and from it strawberries for seasoning forbidden (Rashi's explanation: liable for chatas like R' Yehuda; and for Rav Dimi only olives — one may say it is all one decree; and pickled vegetables for Rav and Shmuel and R' Yehuda for themselves permitted — one must say fruits are inferior more); and for beverages of other fruits also forbidden; and for Tur — other fruits for beverages permitted; and there is a dispute regarding pickled vegetables in Taz or 12, or there beverage is upon them as Magen Avraham 12, or pickled vegetables are like strawberries and pomegranates and for seasoning strawberries also permitted — only olives forbidden — unlike R' Yehuda in pickled vegetables, etc.; and we establish like R' Pinchas there Shabbos 144b and it is not one person and one place as the Rav wrote in the gloss — Magen Avraham 1; and Taz challenged that we should not devise answers for what was not said in the Gemara — therefore he explained that all agree Rashi and Tur permit for seasoning in strawberries and forbid beverages in other fruits; and the baraita deals stam, etc.; however what Shulchan Aruch concluded fits well here — what he wrote in seif 6 regarding lemons is difficult; and Eliyah Rabbah 5 — requires study, it will be explained there.
