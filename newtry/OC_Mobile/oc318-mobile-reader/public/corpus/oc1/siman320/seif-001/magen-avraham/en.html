(א) From the Torah one is liable only for treading olives and grapes alone (Rambam) on this siman: and in a place, etc. At first glance requires further study, for Rashba wrote and if we knew that in some place they squeeze one fruit it is forbidden to squeeze them, as we find regarding pomegranates that are forbidden to squeeze them because of Beit Menashe who were many, and as we are concerned for the custom of Arabs regarding maintaining thorns in a vineyard, end of his words — if so it implies that if in one place they practiced squeezing it, it is forbidden in the entire world to squeeze, and why did Rama write and in a place, etc., it implies that in that place it is forbidden but in other places permitted; but it appears to me that Moses is truth and his Torah is truth and Rashba's words are puzzling, for he argues extensively in the Gemara according to R' Elazar and we rule like the Sages as is written Yoreh De'ah siman 276 seif 4 that specifically in that place where they maintain thorns it is forbidden to maintain them and in other places permitted — if so the law is likewise; and on Shabbos daf 92 they say one of the men of Hutzal who carried a load on his head is exempt even though the people of his city do so — their intent is nullified relative to every person, and likewise siman 286 seif 6 regarding meat, and Tosafos wrote and R' Levi said regarding thorns on Arabs there if there were for the entire world a majority of camels like among Arabs they would maintain thorns, and likewise if there were for everyone many pomegranates like Beit Menashe they would squeeze, and therefore if Beit Menashe were a place they would be considered significant, end quote, and nevertheless in the Gemara it is stated explicitly that Beit Menashe was not a place but one man — if so why forbidden to squeeze pomegranates, and one must strain to say the questioner and answerer are divided on the reality as Rashba wrote, and these are Rambam's words chapter 21: forbidden to squeeze berries and pomegranates since some people squeeze them like olives and grapes, and other fruits permitted to squeeze since they are not fit for squeezing, end of quote, and likewise Rif, and he also brought what R' Chaim said — if so one must say R' Chaim meant thus: since some people squeeze them and he considered them as well, they become beverage — know that otherwise why does the baraita say and those of Beit Menashe, etc., rather perforce the reason is he considered them alone is not effective unless some people also squeeze them, and even if not a complete city; and only in a matter that if there were for the entire world many fruits they would squeeze them, but otherwise even in one place they say their intent is nullified, and therefore in other fruits permitted, and if so these pears that they call pears in German are forbidden to squeeze because in the land of Ashkenaz they practiced squeezing them for beverage, and possible if there were for the entire world many they would also squeeze, and Rash wrote beginning of Kilayim in name of Yerushalmi that if in one place they practiced maintaining zunin and the manner is to transport them from place to place — forbidden to maintain them in the entire world, see there — if so likewise here, and Bach wrote forbidden to squeeze except to sweeten the fruit but to their liquid forbidden, as is written seif 7, and likewise Semak, and so the world practices, end quote, and certainly where practiced it is practiced, and nevertheless it appears to me where he does not squeeze except to dip food in it — permitted, for it is not called beverage, as Beit Yosef wrote in name of Shiltei Giborim and Rosh, see seif 5 seif 6:
