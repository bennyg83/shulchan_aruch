1) [Seif 1] Olives and grapes may not be squeezed. By Torah law, one is liable only for pressing olives and grapes. Shabbos 145a; Rambam, chapter 8, halachah 10; Magen Avraham at the beginning of the siman. One is liable even for squeezing them by hand into a bowl; the law does not apply only to pressing them with a utensil, as Rambam writes explicitly there; see there. Eshel Avraham at the beginning of the siman. One is not liable unless the liquid squeezed measures the volume of a dried fig. Rambam there. See below, siman 368, seif 3, regarding this measure.
