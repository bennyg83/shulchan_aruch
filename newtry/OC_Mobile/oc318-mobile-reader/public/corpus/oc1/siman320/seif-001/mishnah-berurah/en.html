(א) Forbidden to squeeze them — and one who squeezes them is liable on account of extracting, which is a toldah of threshing:
(ב) And see above siman 252 — for there it was explained regarding loading a beam by day on olives and grapes, and see there what we wrote in Mishna Berurah s.k. 42:
(ג) Forbidden — a decree lest he come to squeeze l'chatchila:
(ד) Except for eating — that is, he gathered them for eating, and the reason: since most of them are for squeezing he is pleased with the beverage that flowed, and lest he reconsider regarding them for squeezing:
(ה) Forbidden to squeeze them — even though they stand by default for eating, since some people squeeze them like olives and grapes the rabbis forbade them on account of olives and grapes:
(ו) For eating, etc. — for he is not pleased with what flowed, and there is nothing to decree lest he squeeze; and if he did not intend at the time of gathering neither for eating nor for beverage but stam — forbidden, for they are considered standing for beverage as long as he did not gather them for eating:
(ז) Permitted to squeeze them — because they stand for eating, and no one needs beverages from them, there is no name of beverage on what comes out of them, and it is like one who separates food from food; and even though he had in mind regarding them in his squeezing for beverage — his intent is nullified relative to every person:
(ח) And in a place where they practiced, etc. — it implies that in that place forbidden and in other places permitted; but if the manner is to transport the beverage of the fruits from place to place — forbidden in the entire world, and all this is according to Rama; but Magen Avraham elaborated on this matter and his conclusion is that if we knew that in some place they practiced squeezing it for beverage, and even those who practice this are only some people because they have many fruits and not a complete city — because of this it is forbidden to squeeze in every place, and likewise several Acharonim wrote. Magen Avraham further wrote that specifically in a matter that if there were for the entire world many fruits like these they would squeeze them, but otherwise we say their intent is nullified; and see in Be'ur Halacha what we wrote on this, and these pears that they call pears in Ashkenaz German are forbidden to squeeze because in Ashkenaz lands they practiced squeezing them for beverage, and possible if there were for the entire world many they would also squeeze; but fruits whose manner is never at all to squeeze them in any place — permitted to squeeze them even for beverage as above, and know Bach's view that forbidden to squeeze any fruit except if his intent is only to sweeten the fruit, but for its liquid forbidden, and he wrote such is the practice — and see in Be'ur Halacha that we explained that by law one should not be stringent in this except in a place that practiced to be stringent one should not be lenient, and likewise Magen Avraham's view and several Acharonim, and even in a place that practiced to be stringent in other fruits — that is specifically when they squeeze for beverage sake, but when they squeeze in a bowl to dip food in it — permitted, for it is not called beverage in this; and in olives and grapes forbidden even in this manner:
(ט) Their law is like berries and pomegranates — and apples today too their law is like berries and pomegranates, for there are places that practiced squeezing much from them; but if the apple is cooked and he squeezes so liquid comes from it — permitted, for he squeezes all the food from it:
(י) Permitted to suck, etc. — because there is no manner of squeezing in the mouth, and anything whose manner is not thus they did not decree on it; and even though forbidden to nurse from an animal even in the mouth as is evident below siman 328 seif 133 — there nursing from an animal is not a complete change [for the manner of all living things is to nurse], but here since there is no manner of squeezing at all in this manner they did not decree:
(כ) And all the more in other things — such as one who put meat in soup or bread in wine and sucks them, for otherwise there is no chatas liability in their squeezing, for they are like pickled and boiled below in seif 7, and likewise permitted according to this view to suck berries and pomegranates in the mouth or sugar cane:
(ל) And the like — meaning even other things explained above, and the reason of this view is that he holds this matter is not worse than nursing from an animal, for there too it is a change and nevertheless the Sages forbade; and see in Ahavat Chesed who wrote that in olives and grapes whose squeezing is d'oraisa it is correct to be stringent from sucking even in the mouth like the first view, and in other things one should not be stringent, and it appears even in olives and grapes one should not be stringent except when it is the manner of nursing alone — that is, he sucks them and does not place them in his mouth; but if he places them in his mouth and sucks the beverage and throws the pits outside — for all it is the manner of eating, and so appears from Peri Megadim:
