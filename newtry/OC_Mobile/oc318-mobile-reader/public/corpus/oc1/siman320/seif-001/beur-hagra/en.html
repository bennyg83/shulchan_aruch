(א) Seif 61 "and berries," etc. "if," etc. Like R' Yochanan, for R' Yochanan ruled like him — Ri'f and Shulchan Aruch. But it is not necessary that R' Yochanan's view is according to his method that he holds in Beitzah because of liquids that seeped, for he does not hold hazmana, and the view must be switched as stated at the beginning of chapter 19, and that is the one of the barrel as Rashi explained there; and see Tosafos in Shabbos there s.v. "and R' Yochanan." But we rule like the hazmana of Rabba as stated below — halacha is like Rav in these three, whether etc.:

(ב) "And the rest," etc. There "one squeezes," etc. And even though Rashi and Tosafos there wrote that specifically to sweeten — meaning for the first wording; but for the wording of Abaye because it is a thing etc. — this reason went away; and what is forbidden to squeeze pomegranates — it appears that at first glance one could say why does Ran need to say halacha is like stam, etc., for who disputes him? This is explicit in a braisa; but not regarding pomegranates, and it must be that the first tanna holds that pomegranates everywhere their way is to squeeze; and what is stated "and in a house," etc. is another tanna; and this is what he said halacha is like this tanna, and nafka minah for other fruits if they practiced in some place; and to the conclusion halacha is not like him except in his place specifically as stated above; but Rashba wrote that from what is stated "for we reckon them," etc. — specifically berries and pomegranates that some squeeze them for beverage; but in the rest their view is batel before everyone; and he wrote that thus wrote Rabbenu Yitzchak and Ramban and likewise Ri'f and Rambam and Shulchan Aruch; and he wrote there, and even though Abaye and Rava dispute this reason, and wrote because it is a thing that is not, etc. — nevertheless it does not impose upon him according to Ran; and he holds there the reason that we reckon them — only specifically in the view of Rav Chisda; and the mishnah holds from another reason; and Ri'f and Shulchan Aruch ruled like Shmuel, even though Ulla and R' Yochanan dispute Shmuel; and that braisa of squeezing they establish specifically for R' Yochanan, as Rashi wrote s.v. "and the rest," etc. — nevertheless since the stam of the gemara says "and let it also be R' Yochanan," etc., and it is implied that the stam of the gemara holds thus, and likewise Rav Nachman ruled thus, for he said halacha is like him:

(ג) "And in a place." There 144b — Arama: halacha is like, etc. "anything that goes," etc. "forty" — if they practiced all the people of the place, certainly forbidden; and even though the rabbis dispute R' Elazar — this is in other places; but in that place they concede to him, as Rambam wrote and Shulchan Aruch Yoreh De'ah siman 396 seif 14, and Magen Avraham:

(ד) "But if." And proof from that they did not mention apples even though in their days there was apple wine for medicine as stated in Avodah Zarah:

(ה) "And all the more so." Meaning pickled and stewed, for there is no chatas obligation in them:
