(א) Permitted to squeeze them — See in Mishna Berurah what he wrote that even when he squeezed them for the sake of beverage — and even though Taz agreed to Rashi's view that specifically when he squeezed them without specification and we can say his intent was to sweeten the fruit and not for the sake of beverage; and he explained that this is the view of all the poskim that Beit Yosef brought who permit to squeeze them — in truth Shulchan Aruch's view is not so, since he permitted in seif 6 to squeeze lemons; and Peri Megadim already challenged this; and also one who examines the chiddushim of Ramban, Rashba, and Ritva will see plainly that the view of Rif and the Geonim who permit to squeeze them is even when he squeezed them for the sake of beverage, as the plain meaning of the Gemara; and automatically Semag, Semak, Rosh, and Tur who copied Rif's language — their intent is also thus; and see there, in the chiddushim of Ramban and Rashba that they agreed thus for halacha and wrote that this is also the view of Rabbeinu Yonah [and the difficulty mentioned in the words of Rashi and Tosafos that according to what the Gemara says, since he designated them they are a beverage — if so even in other fruits too — they already answered in their chiddushim, and Ramban wrote in his chiddushim: turnips that he squeezed, etc.; rather what can you say, etc.; Rashi explained, etc., and this is not clear, etc.; and this is that we say rather like R' Chiya — meaning this is the reason we are concerned for the house of Menashe bar Menachem because this one practiced like his custom and designated them; and like R' Chiya who said turnips that he squeezed invalidate the mikveh through a change of appearance — and they are not fit for squeezing, for the majority of the world does not squeeze them; except since there is a minority that squeeze them and this one practiced like the custom of that minority and squeezed them for beverages and designated them — they are beverages; and if in other fruits where no person squeezes them for beverages — even though this one squeezed them, his intent is nullified relative to every person; and this is correct; and so it appears from the words of our great master z"l, end of his words; and Rashba and Ritva also wrote thus; and all this was hidden from the eyes of Beit Yosef and Bach, who strained themselves to resolve Rashi's difficulty per the view of those who permit; and Magen Avraham in s.k. 1 inclined on his own to this reasoning; and the difficulty that Bach brought from the gloss of Semak — one can resolve it simply, as we will write below]; and also in Kolbo it appears permitted in other fruits even to squeeze them for the sake of beverage; and also Rabbeinu Yerucham — even though he himself wrote at first the two views — nevertheless for halacha it appears he holds like the view of those who permit, since he himself ruled at the end that permitted to squeeze lemons; and we have no view of those who are stringent except Rashi, Tosafos, and the gloss of Semak. The conclusion from all these: since the view of Rif, Rambam, Semag, Rosh, Tur, Rabbeinu Yonah, Ramban, Rashba, Ritva, and Kolbo — all adopted to permit in other fruits even when he squeezed them for the sake of beverage — we need not be concerned for the view of those who are stringent from the perspective of law, for they are individuals relative to them; and also it is possible that according to Rav Pappa's resolutions even Rashi and Tosafos agree, as the poskim wrote — if not in a place where they practiced to be stringent like the view of Rashi and his supporters — one should not be lenient in this; and as Magen Avraham wrote at the end of s.k. 1:

(ב) And in a place where they practiced, etc. — See in Mishna Berurah what we wrote in the name of Magen Avraham: specifically in a matter where if the entire world, etc.; but otherwise we say their intent is nullified — and this is even in this place itself — according to what Tosafos hold; and so it appears in Machatzit HaShekel. However, the essence of Magen Avraham's law is not clear, for it is possible that per the conclusion of designation we no longer need all this; also for Tosafos I again found in Machatzit HaShekel that he challenged this; and even though they resolved somewhat — nevertheless it is not compelled at all for the law; and in particular since the entire source of Magen Avraham is from the words of Tosafos; and one who examines the chiddushim of Rashba and Ritva on that which "are the places of the Arabs" proves that they do not hold like the words of Tosafos; and apart from all this, from Rambam's words who wrote that the reason for figs and pomegranines is since a portion of people squeeze them like olives and grapes — it appears that for this reason alone there is to forbid in every place; and this is since he designated them and squeezed them for the sake of beverage; and in any case in this place where they practiced to squeeze — certainly one should not be lenient in any manner; and so it appears from Rama's conclusion, as appears correct to me:

(ג) Its law is like figs and pomegranines — And regarding apples it appears from Beit Yosef that its law is also like pomegranines because there are places that squeeze them for beverage; but from Biur HaGra it appears he holds apples are comparable to other fruits [however his words there are somewhat difficult to understand, for the proof Beit Yosef brings for his words from Avodah Zarah daf 40 where it states that R' Chiya had pain in his intestines and they brought him apple wine of the Land of Israel aged seventy years and he drank and was healed; further it states there that a certain man in the Land of Israel had three hundred barrels of apple wine — evidently their custom was to squeeze apples extensively; and he mentioned pomegranines — all the more so apples, see there in Beit Yosef; that proof itself Beit Yosef brought the opposite — that he brought proof that for healing it is not called a beverage since they did not mention apples [for the prohibition of squeezing] even though in their days there was apple wine for healing as stated in Avodah Zarah, end of his words; it appears he holds this wine was made only for healing — and from where to say thus, for it is a great strain to say three hundred barrels of wine were made only for healing — requires study]; and so Taz in s.k. 5 holds comparable to other fruits, and his reason is because in our country we have not heard that they make apple wine [and his intent is we have not heard at all in our time that they make apple wine in any place; for if they made it in another country — behold it is explained in Beit Yosef that for this reason it is forbidden throughout the world; and Taz was assisted in his words from Beit Yosef, see there; and so he sided in Mordechai in explanation of Taz's words; and see in Peri Megadim]. But Chayei Adam wrote that today the custom in Ashkenaz countries is to fill barrels from them by the thousands for beverage; and if in other countries there were apples in abundance like there — they would also make beverage from them; and therefore forbidden to squeeze them, end of his words; and so it appears one should not be lenient in this in our time, since the custom is to squeeze them; and as Magen Avraham was stringent regarding pears, and Taz too would agree if he saw thus in his time:
