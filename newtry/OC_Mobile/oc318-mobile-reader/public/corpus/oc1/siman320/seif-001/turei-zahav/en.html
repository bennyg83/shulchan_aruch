(א) And all other fruits, etc. Beit Yosef wrote that there is a dispute among the poskim in this: for Rif, Rambam, and Tur it appears that in other fruits one may squeeze l'chatchila even for their water; but Rashi and Semak in the gloss wrote specifically to squeeze in order to sweeten them; and therefore Beit Yosef challenged: according to those who permit even for their water — from what do we establish the prohibition of squeezing pomegranates due to that which R' Chiya said regarding a mikveh: turnips that he squeezed and put into the mikveh are invalid, since he designated them — they became a beverage; if so, in all other fruits too we should say thus, since he designated them to extract beverage from them — forbidden; and one could answer that according to Rif, who did not explain R' Chiya's reason due to designation — it works well that we do not hold this reasoning, since he designated them; for since the majority of the world did not designate them — it is difficult; for if so Rif argues on R' Nissim in what he said: the halacha follows R' Menashe, for behold tender leaves — are they the majority of the world? And for Rif there is no answer to this difficulty, for we do not find an answer in the Gemara except from that reasoning of designation; and we have no power to answer what was not explained in the Gemara; and if so, since you will say Rif holds like Rif — he should not have ruled like R' Nissim who said the halacha follows R' Menashe; and Rashi was careful about this and said that even per the reasoning of designation it works well that they did not permit in other fruits except to sweeten them and not for their water; and if so Rif and his colleagues remain with the difficulty. And it appears to me that just as for Rashi in any case there is an advantage for pomegranates over other fruits that in pomegranates it is forbidden when he does not wish except to sweeten — we decree lest he extract beverage — and in other fruits we do not decree, as Rashi stated explicitly — so too Rif holds this reasoning that pomegranates are stricter than other fruits; only they did not touch it due to a decree but because in other fruits we say without specification they squeeze — we say without specification he intends to sweeten; and even though he benefits from what flows from the liquid — nevertheless his primary intent at the time of squeezing was only for the sweetening produced through the squeezing, which is the manner of squeezing other fruits that are not designated for liquids; but in olives and grapes their default is for liquids — and therefore forbidden even when it emerges by itself; and in figs and pomegranates the matter is balanced and their default is not decisive — therefore one does not squeeze l'chatchila, and if it emerges by itself — permitted; but in the case where one wishes to squeeze other fruits and reveals his intent explicitly that he squeezes for the sake of the liquids that emerge — certainly forbidden according to his words; and this is that which R' Chiya — for one who is precise in his language who said "turnips that he squeezed and put them into the mikveh" and did not say "and they fell into the mikveh" like that case where wine fell into it; rather certainly R' Chiya deals when he intentionally squeezed the turnip in order to put them into the mikveh and revealed his intent that he designated them — they invalidate; and the same in other fruits; and this is the essence of the answer that they establish it like R' Chiya — namely just as in turnips we follow what he designated, and this is if he designated them explicitly — so too in figs and pomegranates presumably, unlike other fruits that are like turnips and permitted without specification; and if so there is no distinction in law at all between Rif and his colleagues and between Rashi and the gloss of Semak; and nevertheless there is a contradiction in the gloss of Semag in Beit Yosef's citation, for that which it wrote "even though the water is not from their essence," etc. — its place is not where Beit Yosef brings it, but rather on liquids that emerge from pickled vegetables; and what it says "all the more so squeeze" — its meaning must be "all the more so squeeze fruits"; and this is plain; and the plain meaning of Shulchan Aruch emerges well: all other fruits are permitted to squeeze — namely without specification; but when he intends to extract beverage from them for the need of the beverage — forbidden; and so Ra'avad regarding meat that has gravy in it, as is written in the next seif, as appears correct to me:

(ב) But permitted to suck, etc. And so Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Rashal for the reason that there is no manner of squeezing in the mouth, and anything whose manner is not thus — they did not decree regarding it and it is not a toldah, end of his words; and he wrote there: but nursing in the mouth l'chatchila forbidden — as it appears to me this is a textual error; and those who forbid is Ra'avad in Beit Yosef who wrote that a person should be careful not to squeeze meat absorbed in gravy for the need of the beverage that emerges from it; and meat that one puts in gravy or bread that he soaks in wine and returns to his mouth and sucks the liquids — forbidden, and I am concerned for him due to a chatas; and all the more so he should not suck grapes in his mouth, end of his words. And in my humble opinion this matter is similar to the law written in siman 319 seif 16 — namely the view of Rosh that one may drink through a cloth, etc.; just as there there is no prohibition of borer in what he does with his mouth, so too here there is no prohibition of dosh in what he sucks with his mouth; what difference between the melacha of borer and the melacha of dosh?
