Even for a rabbinic prohibition, sixty is required, and we do not say that each first portion becomes nullified as it enters. Mishbetzos Zahav, paragraph 3. Since we do not know how much wine will emerge from the grapes, the wine in the vat must amount to sixty times all the grapes, as stated in Yoreh De'ah, siman 98, seif 4. Otherwise, it may not be drunk on Shabbos.
