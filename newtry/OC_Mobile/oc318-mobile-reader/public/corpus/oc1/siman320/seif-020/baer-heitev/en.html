Dyeing. And Radbaz siman 131 wrote that it is an excessive stringency, since it is the manner of soiling and this is not the manner of dyeing, see there and in Knesset HaGedolah. See siman 302 seif 10 that some forbid laundering due to the manner of soiling — all the more so dyeing, for every dyeing is thus; therefore one should not be lenient — Magen Avraham, see there. And it appears to me that with a red garment all the more so forbidden to wipe it, for he repairs it — Magen Avraham, see there. And see siman 328, Semak, and siman 301, Semag (and in Eliyah Rabbah he sided to be lenient in red and even in white when impossible in another manner, see there). One who dyes a thread whose length is four handbreadths, or a thing from which it is possible to spin such a thread — liable; and specifically a dye that endures; but a dye that does not endure at all — such as if he passed wax or tar over iron or copper and dyed it — exempt, for behold you remove it temporarily and it does not dye at all; see siman 301 seif 4.
