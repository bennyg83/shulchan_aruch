One who dyes a thread four handbreadths long or something from which one can spin such a thread — liable, and specifically permanent dye; but dye that is not permanent at all, such as one who applied rouge or saliva on iron or copper and dyed it — exempt, for you remove it temporarily and it does not dye at all.
