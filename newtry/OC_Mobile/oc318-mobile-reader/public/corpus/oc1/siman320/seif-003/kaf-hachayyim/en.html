3) There: Olives and grapes may not be squeezed. By Torah law, one may squeeze other fruits to extract their juices, because no fruit juice has the status of a beverage except that produced from olives and grapes. The juices of other fruits retain the status of food, and separating food from food does not constitute extracting. Shulchan Aruch HaRav there. The Sages nevertheless forbade squeezing certain fruits because some people squeeze them for their juice, as explained below.
