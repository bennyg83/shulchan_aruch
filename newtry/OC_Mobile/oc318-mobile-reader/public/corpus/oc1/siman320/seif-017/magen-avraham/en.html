Decree lest he squeeze. Requires study, since a sponge is made for that — why decree lest he squeeze, as is written seif 15 and siman 301 seif 41; and further it is puzzling, for in the Gemara they challenge regarding a sponge, and we rule like R' Shimon that davar she'eino mitkaven is permitted, and they answer it is psik reisha — if so the reason is not on account of decree; and requires study, and Rambam and Rashi wrote when it has a handle-hold then possible without squeezing, but Ra'avad wrote it is never possible to wipe without squeezing except since it has a handle-hold even though he squeezes it is like rinsing from a flask of water, but when it has no handle-hold forbidden to squeeze, end quote, and requires study according to him, since wiping without squeezing is impossible — why did they permit wiping with a towel as is written siman 301 seif 8, and one must say specifically in a sponge wiping without squeezing is impossible, see siman 302 seif 12:
