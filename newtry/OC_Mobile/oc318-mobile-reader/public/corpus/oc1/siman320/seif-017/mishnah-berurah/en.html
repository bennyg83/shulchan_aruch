(א) Sponge — it is a kind of wool made for wiping the sea, or as the Ikkur explains as below, and by nature it draws water and beverages into itself:
(ב) One does not wipe with it — the table and bowl:
(ג) Except if, etc. — Rashi and Rambam explained when it has a skin handle-hold that one holds it, possible to wipe without squeezing; but Ra'avad wrote that nevertheless impossible to wipe without squeezing, nevertheless permitted because since it has handle-hold it is like a flask full of water one rinses water from:
(ד) Lest he squeeze — Be'ur HaGra wrote it is a textual error and must read on account of squeezing, for we say in the Gemara it is psik reisha because it is impossible not to squeeze when he holds it with his fingers without a skin handle-hold, and likewise Magen Avraham:
