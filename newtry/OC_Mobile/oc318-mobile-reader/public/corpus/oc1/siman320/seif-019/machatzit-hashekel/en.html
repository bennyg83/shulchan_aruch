(s.k. 19) Decree, etc., and further, etc., sponge, and behold davar she'eino mitkaven is thus. And that which we taught daf 143 one may move wool of wheels because it is animal food — sponge: if it has a handle-hold one wipes with it, if not one does not wipe — the Gemara challenges the first clause permits moving wool, etc., perforce like R' Shimon it comes, for if like R' Yehuda it would be muktzeh, for it is like an animal that died on Shabbat that is muktzeh since yesterday it was not standing for dogs, and likewise wool of wheels since yesterday they were attached for eating they were not standing for animals, and the latter clause forbids sponge without handle-hold — perforce like R' Yehuda, for for R' Shimon it should be permitted for the squeezing is davar she'eino mitkaven and permitted for R' Shimon, and they answer: always R' Shimon, and nevertheless sponge without handle-hold forbidden for it is psik reisha and R' Shimon concedes. But Ra'avad, etc., but when it has no handle-hold forbidden for then it has no name of vessel. And Rashi and Rambam explained when it has a skin handle-hold one can hold it and wipe without squeezing, but Ra'avad wrote that nevertheless impossible to wipe without squeezing, nevertheless permitted since it has handle-hold it is like a flask full of water one rinses water from it; but when it has no handle-hold forbidden to squeeze, end quote, and it implies there that even when not sitting in water forbidden, see seif 15:
