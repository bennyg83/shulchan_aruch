It appears that even if whole grapes were placed in wine on Shabbos itself and split that Shabbos, the wine is permitted, just as one may place snow or hail in a cup or bowl on Shabbos below in seif 9. Bach. This implies that placing them there initially is permitted, like snow and hail in seif 9, and Magen Avraham there writes the same. Taz there, however, disagrees with Bach and writes that this is absolutely forbidden, because squeezing is a derivative of threshing and is forbidden even when performed unusually. The permission applies only after the fact; see there. Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 4, rules the same. Eliyah Rabbah, paragraph 5, and Tosefes Shabbos, paragraph 8, however, challenge Taz's words; see there. Chayei Adam, klal 14, likewise rules that it is permitted initially.
