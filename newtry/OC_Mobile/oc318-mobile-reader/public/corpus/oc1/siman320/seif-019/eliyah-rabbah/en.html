(19) A decree, etc. Requires study: since a sponge is made thus — why do we need to decree lest he squeeze, as explained seif 15; and further one may ask: in the Gemara [Shabbos] daf 143 it says it is psik reisha — if so the reason is not because of a decree (Magen Avraham); and it appears to me the first difficulty is no difficulty, for even though it is made for this and one need not squeeze — nevertheless it is squeezed between his fingers; and in such a case he wrote himself siman 302 s.k. 27; and in the second difficulty one may say the baraita deals when the sponge is moist, but when the sponge is dry and wipes something moist — it is not psik reisha; and with this it appears to me to uphold the version in the Mishnah: and the Sages say in any case one wipes; and Tosafos there rejected this version, for behold it is psik reisha; and according to what I wrote it is fine — requires study; and one should not say: if so Taz did not forbid — for it is not psik reisha — one may say they do not distinguish between sponge for squeezing and sponge for wiping; and it does not appear correct to him the version mentioned above — requires study; see siman 302 s.k. 22; and more it appears: in Rambam chapter 22 he wrote first the law of sponge and concluded one does not cover water barrels — a decree, etc.; and he used the Tur's language in sponge as well, and it is easy to understand:
