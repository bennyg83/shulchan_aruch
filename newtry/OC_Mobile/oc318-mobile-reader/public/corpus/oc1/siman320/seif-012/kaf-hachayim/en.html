(l) There: in the gloss: and all the more so in other things. So Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket that there is no sucking of meat and bread, and one who sucks in his mouth does not have beverage from fruits or grapes through the manner of sechita at all; although in the name of Hagahot Maimoniyot chapter 21 he wrote one should be careful not to squeeze meat absorbed in soup for the sake of beverage coming from it; and one who puts meat in soup or soaks his bread in wine and returns it to his mouth and sucks the liquids — forbidden, and I fear for him from a chatas; and all the more he should not suck grapes and the like from his mouth — end of his words; and this is what our teacher z"l wrote in the gloss: and there are those who forbid sucking with the mouth, etc.; and our teacher z"l's view in Beit Yosef appears like those who permit, as Birkei Yosef wrote in Shevitat Asor or 1; but Bach wrote in the name of Maharshal one should distinguish: in soup absorbed in meat or wine in bread — since there was already beverage upon them — therefore it is close to chatas liability and forbidden to suck; but beverage coming from grapes before there was beverage upon them, such as one who sucks grapes in his mouth and discards the pits — this is not threshing but thus is the manner of eating grapes and permitted by law; nevertheless one who is stringent — good for him — end of his words; and Bach wrote on it: thus we rule that in meat and bread forbidden and in grapes permitted; but Magen Avraham s.k. 4 wrote from Rama's words it appears other things are lighter than grapes; and he wrote: and so from Rashi's explanation — see there; and so Olat Shabbat or 9 to be stringent specifically in olives and grapes; and so Eliyah Rabbah or 3 — see there.
