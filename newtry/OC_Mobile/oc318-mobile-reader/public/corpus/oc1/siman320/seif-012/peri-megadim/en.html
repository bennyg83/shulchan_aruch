And Taz wrote: when beverage spills on the cloth in the spoon — forbidden to draw and afterward pour outside, for he ruins what cannot be repaired (Eliyah Rabbah sign 23: ultimately he does not want it — there is no dash here — see there; and possibly because of mar'it ayin rabbinically forbidden: one who sees will say he wishes to drink the beverage he draws in the spoon — forbidden; and possibly this is Taz's intent); and Eliyah Rabbah 22 in this: beverage absorbed in the cloth — forbidden to scrape with a knife, for the place where none fell there will stain like laundering seif 12.
