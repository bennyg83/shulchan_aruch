(12) [Dress] The law of beverages, etc. From this it appears to me slight proof regarding meat salted from time to time in water from which the hide peeled — it is like pickling and not comparable to what Rama wrote Yoreh De'ah siman 104 regarding goose fat that hardened, that it does not have the law of pickling; for here in any case there is moisture that is like the law of beverages for everything; but requires study, for it appears in Maggid chapter 21 and Ran end of chapter Bameh Madlikin that one who permits to place hail in the sun permits also regarding fat that congealed; and one who forbids regarding fat because of nolad — the same applies to hail; and so it appears in Magen Avraham; however from Shulchan Aruch and Dress who wrote in siman 318 seif 16 that there are two views regarding fat, and here they concluded to permit — it appears he holds to permit here more; and it appears to him that here it deals when mixed in water — not so there — requires study. And the essential law of meat that was pickled will be explained in Yoreh De'ah — see Shach siman 69 s.k. 11:
