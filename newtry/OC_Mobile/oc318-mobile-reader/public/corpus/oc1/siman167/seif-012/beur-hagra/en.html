Seif 12: If they were, etc. This is the Baraisa cited above. The same applies in a comparable case. Specifically, one who rides while traveling has the status of one who walks.
But, etc. This is stated in the Baraisa there, and as explained above, only when they sat down does their fixed gathering take effect.
Since, etc. As stated there, establishing a fixed meal requires that they say, “Let us go,” etc. Furthermore, the wording implies that they sat in one place. This is why the Mechaber writes, “and they eat scattered about.” This is an additional reason, because two conditions are required, as stated at the beginning: “and they said, ‘Let us eat,’” etc., and “since,” etc. It is possible that the Shulchan Aruch inferred from this in the preceding seif and wrote, “unless,” etc. This is not conclusive. It is sufficient that they not be scattered and that they establish themselves together, for a fixed place in our practice is no less effective than it was in theirs, as explained above.
