Since they stood, etc. Meaning, this is considered like sitting, and it helps because they said, let us eat, and thereby prepared a place for their eating, as above in seif 11.
But if they are walking, not. Meaning, the statement they made initially, let us eat, does not help, because walking cancels the fixed setting. If they are sitting in one wagon and said, let us eat here, and they eat together, the Magen Avraham is uncertain whether they combine. Although generally one traveling in a wagon is like one walking, nevertheless since they are all sitting in one group, perhaps they combine. The Eliyah Rabbah also inclines to be lenient. On a ship, even the Magen Avraham clearly agrees to be lenient, for it is a complete fixed setting. The later authorities wrote that a waiter serving at the meal fulfills his obligation with the HaMotzi blessing if he hears and intends to fulfill his obligation, even though he constantly goes and comes and is not fixed at the table, because that is his manner of eating.
And if they were eating, etc. The text should read, and similarly if, etc. This refers to the earlier case, where they said, let us eat here, and sat to eat. Even so, it does not help if they were scattered, for we require that they be in one place, as stated by Maamar Mordechai.
Where they did not fix a place and are eating, etc. This is one matter, meaning that they did not fix a place at the time of their eating, but ate scattered, one here and one there; therefore it does not help even though they initially said, let us eat here.
