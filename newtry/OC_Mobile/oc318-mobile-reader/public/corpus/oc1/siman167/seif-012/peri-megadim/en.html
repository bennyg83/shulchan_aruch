If they recline—Taz worked on this and Shulchan Aruch wrote two or many; and so Tur and Levush the reverse—and if many or even two—not both this and that; for in zimmun three one is motzi as in siman 193 and two are not motzi—and here even two; and Taz's language can be said he refers at the end and specifically they reclined—for in zimmun of three we do not require reclining—Eshel Avraham 12; nevertheless for us reclining seems not required in zimmun for here establishment of place is required—see siman 193 seif 3—if so Levush acted well for for us it is not both this and that in reverse.
