And they said, let us eat. Although above it was written that speech does not help unless they sat at one table, that applies in a house. In a field, where there is no table, designation by speech is a fixed setting even without a table.
But if they are walking, not. Meaning, saying so does not help, and so it appears from the Gemara. It requires study whether, if they sit in one wagon and said, let us eat here, and they eat together, they combine. Although we rule that one riding is like one walking, and the same applies to one sitting in a wagon, nevertheless, since the three are sitting in one group, perhaps they combine. This requires study. On a ship it is clear that it is a complete fixed setting. It appears from Shiltei Gibborim that a waiter, even though he did not establish himself at the table, fulfills his obligation with the HaMotzi blessing; see what I wrote in his name in seif 11 and at the beginning of siman 199.
