If they were riding and said, "Let us eat," then even if each one eats from his own loaf and they did not dismount from their animals, they are considered joined (for purposes such as zimun)—since they are standing in one place. But if they are eating while walking, they are not considered joined. And if they are eating scattered and separated in a field—even if they are all eating at the same time and from the same loaf—since they did not establish a place and are eating while dispersed, they are not considered joined....
