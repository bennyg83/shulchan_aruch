Seif katan 5: And on Shabbos, etc. This applies specifically when he still holds the cut portion attached to the loaf. If he cut more deeply, then even if lifting the remaining loaf causes the slice to rise with it, this is ineffective.
And so it appears below in siman 168. Meaning, Magen Avraham there, seif katan 4, cites the Rokeach that if one has two halves of a loaf, he may join them with a piece of wood and it is called a whole loaf even on Shabbos.
If leaving only a slight natural connection sufficed for Shabbos, what novelty would there be in the Rokeach's ruling? According to Magen Avraham, joining the halves with wood is no worse than a natural connection in the bread itself. In truth, this proof can be rejected.
