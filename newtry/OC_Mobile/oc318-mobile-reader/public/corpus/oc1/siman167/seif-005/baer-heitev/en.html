To the altar. Therefore, one may not kill a louse on the table. Sefer Chassidim; Magen Avraham.
End of siman 170. Although we do not customarily eat salt because our bread contains salt, it is nevertheless a mitzvah to place salt on the table. The Arizal likewise wrote that according to the hidden dimension of Torah, salt is required even with fine bread. Beis Yaakov, siman 165, writes similarly. The kabbalists wrote that one should dip the piece over which HaMotzi was recited into salt three times.
