He should not break (the bread) until they bring before him salt or a condiment (Rashi explains: anything that is eaten with bread) with which to dip the piece of bread. But if the bread is clean (i.e., of fine quality) or seasoned with spices or salt like ours, or if he intends to eat dry bread, he does not need to wait. {Rama: Rema: Nevertheless, it is a mitzvah to bring salt to every table before breaking the bread, because the table is likened to the altar and the eating to a sacrifice, and it is said, "On all your offerings you shall offer salt" (Beit Yosef in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket). And it protects from calamity (Tosafot and Hagahot Ashri, chapter "Keitzad Mevarchin", and see the end of Siman 170).}
