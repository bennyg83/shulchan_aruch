See Magen Avraham, s.k. 32, end of s.v. vehalachta. Marginal note: Berachot 46a; see there. R' Avahu knew this of R' Yochanan—that the host breaks and the guest blesses, as concluded at the end; if so, what was his thought initially that he gave R' Zeira to break? Rather, perforce because he wished to give honor to R' Zeira; but when R' Zeira did not wish to accept, he returned and broke himself. And understand.
