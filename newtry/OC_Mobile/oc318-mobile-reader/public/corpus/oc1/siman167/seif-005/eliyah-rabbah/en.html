[5] Both his hands etc.—and if he has a sleeve he must remove it. Shelah wrote: intend in berachat hashulchan more than other berachot for food and drink bring a person to coarseness and haughtiness; therefore he must reflect so his heart be drawn to desire of Heaven blessed be He and not to desire of food and drink. Maharil wrote Shabbat laws: always when he mentioned Heaven in berachat hamotzi he raised bread with both hands upward until he finished mentioning Heaven and lowered as at first; on weekday he raised bread and on Shabbat both.
