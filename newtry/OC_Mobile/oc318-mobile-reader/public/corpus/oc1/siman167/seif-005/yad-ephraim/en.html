In Magen Avraham note 2—this is for the other explanation etc.—meaning for one explanation that Tur holds thus—this means the place where bread baked beautifully; and for other explanation it means from place that precedes in baking; and so per text of where cooking preceded—meaning there it precedes in cooking, and it is one matter with place that precedes in baking; and on this they divided—some say it is the upper side etc.; and again he wrote and Rashi wrote etc.—because edges of bread precede in cooking over middle of bread; and on this Magen Avraham wrote and in our bread he shall cut above—it seems to me he does not mean he shall cut only above but rather regarding what Rashi wrote that he cuts from edges—it implies that alone suffices; therefore he said in our bread he cuts also above to fulfill the views above; and perhaps a word is missing and it should read and in our bread he shall cut also above.
