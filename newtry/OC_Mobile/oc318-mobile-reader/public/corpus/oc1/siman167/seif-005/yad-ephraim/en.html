Magen Avraham, seif katan 2. This follows the other explanation, etc. Meaning, according to one explanation the Tur means the place where the bread baked well; according to the other, the place that bakes first. According to the reading “where cooking precedes,” it likewise means the place that cooks first, and this is identical with the place that bakes first. They dispute which side this is, some saying it is the upper side, etc. Magen Avraham then cites Rashi, because the bottom edges of the bread cook before its center. Magen Avraham writes, “With our bread he should cut above.” It appears to me that he does not mean that one should cut only above. Rather, because Rashi's statement to cut from the lower edge implies that this alone suffices, Magen Avraham says that with our bread one should also cut above, thereby satisfying the earlier opinions. Perhaps a word is missing, and the text should read: “With our bread he should also cut above.”
