There Well baked. The Taz’s wording is missing a phrase, for this is a third reading: “where its baking was completed.” Meaning, the entire circumference of the bread is not completely uniform, for there is a place where the fire had greater effect and there it is harder, while elsewhere it is softer. This is the meaning of “the place that is well baked” in the Shulchan Aruch, as Magen Avraham writes in seif katan 1. This is evident to one who examines the Beit Yosef.
