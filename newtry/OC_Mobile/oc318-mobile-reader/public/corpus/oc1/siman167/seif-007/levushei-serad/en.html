There And Rashi wrote. He brought this to explain the Rama’s words, “our bread,” and I explained it above in letter 4 in the manner that the Taz interpreted it. His intent is that Rashi wrote to break only from the edge because their bread baked equally on both sides, whereas regarding our bread one should also cut from the upper surface. This is the continuation of his words. I already wrote in letter 4 what is difficult about this; see there.
