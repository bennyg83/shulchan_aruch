There—that borei implies past and not that we bless borei nor do we say she-bara—for certainly past tense; because we must use present tense—for truly this is certain that also borei is past tense; and that we do not say she-bara which is past tense—for all agree because we use borei which is scripture's language as Tosafot wrote there; and always in berachot must be past tense. And afterward Magen Avraham wrote that in Rashba's chiddushim he wrote in name of Yerushalmi that even Beit Shammai hold to say she-bara—they dispute only on fire (and what is before us only as typo and should read on fire) which is not renewed each year; but on matter that is renewed even Beit Shammai concede one blesses borei which is present tense; and nevertheless our Talmud is primary; and below siman 204 note 114 he brought words of Chokhmat Shlomo and hinted here, see there.
