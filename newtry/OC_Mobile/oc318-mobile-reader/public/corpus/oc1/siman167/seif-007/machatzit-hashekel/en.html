Seif katan 7: For every, etc. One must exert himself to eat two kezaysim together: one kezayis of the HaMotzi bread and one kezayis of the mitzvah matzah. Since the throat can contain exactly two kezaysim, he must exert himself to swallow them together. Otherwise, he could have made the HaMotzi portion smaller than a kezayis and thereby swallowed it easily together with the kezayis of matzah.
