To discharge the listeners. And the listeners also intended to fulfill their obligation through his blessing.
And afterward he dried, etc. The same applies if the entire washing and the blessing of al netilas yadayim were also afterward; he still fulfills his obligation after the fact, for all this is not an interruption since it is a need of the meal. The text used this wording only to teach that if Yaakov recited the blessing of al netilas yadayim at the time that the other person blessed HaMotzi, he did not fulfill his obligation, for one must understand and hear what the one blessing says and not interrupt with other words, and only then hearing is like answering. See below, siman 183, end of seif 6. Because of our sins, many stumble in this on Friday night when the head of the household discharges his family with Kiddush.
And he blessed al netilas yadayim, etc. Although we rule that hearing is like answering, and the Magen Avraham already ruled above in siman 158, seif 11, that if before blessing al netilas yadayim he blessed HaMotzi, he can no longer bless al netilas yadayim, here is different because he had not dried his hands before HaMotzi and the mitzvah was not yet completed. But if he had already dried as well, he could no longer bless al netilas yadayim, since he had already fulfilled his obligation with the HaMotzi blessing.
