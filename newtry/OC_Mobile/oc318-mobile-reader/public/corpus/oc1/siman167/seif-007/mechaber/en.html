If Reuven was washing his hands for eating, and Yaakov was reciting the HaMotzi blessing with the intention to fulfill the obligation on behalf of the listeners, and afterward Reuven dried his hands and recited the blessing over washing hands (Al Netilat Yadayim)—this is not considered an interruption, and he fulfills his obligation with Yaakov’s HaMotzi blessing and does not need to recite the HaMotzi blessing again.
