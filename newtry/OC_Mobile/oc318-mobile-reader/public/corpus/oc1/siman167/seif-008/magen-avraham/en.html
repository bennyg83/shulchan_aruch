He blesses. On what he wishes to eat now.
He does not bless. This is unlike netilas yadayim in siman 158, seif 11, for there he was initially unfit, whereas here he was initially fit to bless, and once it was deferred, it remains deferred, as stated in the Gemara. The Beit Yosef wrote in the name of Kol Bo that if he can eat, he should bless and eat a little. It seems to me that it is good to do so, since the Rashba wrote that according to the Raavad he must bless even though he completed his meal; see the Beit Yosef in siman 172. It is therefore good to remove oneself from dispute. See what I wrote there.
