I do not know how one could move from what is explicit in the authorities because of an implication in the Rambam. The Prishah writes that the Rambam need not disagree with all those great authorities; giving food to a person is certainly no worse than giving food to an animal, and if one says to give food to a person who needs it, even a wealthy person, this is a mitzvah and precedes feeding one's animal. So too it appears from Shibolei HaLeket, Taniya, and Avudraham. At first I thought to establish the law that one must return and bless, but because of doubt I investigated further and found that the Rosh, Agudah, Raavan, and Semak imply that anything needed for the meal is not an interruption. Therefore one should not return and bless.
