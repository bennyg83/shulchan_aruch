He blesses. On what he will eat afterward, for merely because he ate until now without a blessing, should he continue eating without a blessing? This is from the Gemara.
He does not bless. A blessing over benefit is like other mitzvah blessings, which are recited only before performance and not afterward, unless it is a mitzvah on which it is impossible to bless beforehand, as written in siman 158, seif 11. Nevertheless, if he can eat a little more, it is good for him to bless HaMotzi and eat.
