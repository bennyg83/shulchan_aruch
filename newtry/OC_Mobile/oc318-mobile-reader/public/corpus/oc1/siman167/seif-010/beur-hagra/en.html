Seif 10: If, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. veRabbi Yehudah, etc.
Or, etc. This is stated there in the Gemara regarding Bircas HaMazon, and the same applies to the blessing of HaMotzi. It follows Rabbi Meir there, since the Yerushalmi rules like him, and Rabbi Yochanan likewise holds like him. Although the Gemara rejects this and says, “Rabbi Yochanan said to him, etc.,” that rejection itself is overturned in the conclusion, as stated there: “It was necessary, for I might have thought, etc.”
Malkah, etc. This follows Rabbi Yehudah, whose view is accepted as halachah. Some texts explicitly read: “And according to Rabbi Yehudah, who says, etc.” See Rosh there.
