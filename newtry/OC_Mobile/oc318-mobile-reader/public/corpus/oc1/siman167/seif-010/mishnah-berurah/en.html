In place of the HaMotzi blessing, etc. Meaning, although bread is an important item, nevertheless after the fact it helps if he blessed Shehakol, and all the more so regarding any other item, as stated below in siman 206, seif 1.
He blessed Shehakol. Meaning, he said the standard formula of Shehakol, or even if he did not mention it at the beginning at all but only said the Aramaic formula invoking the Merciful One, he fulfilled his obligation, since it contains the Divine Name and kingship; the term Merciful One stands in place of the Name, as they called Him in Bavel, and the term King is kingship.
Of this bread. If he said King, Master of this, and did not mention bread at all, it is possible that he fulfilled his obligation since the bread lies before him. See Biur Halacha.
He fulfilled his obligation. After the fact, but initially both formulations are forbidden. Although blessings may be recited in any language, that applies where one does not change at all from the form of the blessing. Here, however, he changed from the formula established by Chazal in the holy tongue; nevertheless, he fulfilled his obligation after the fact since he at least mentioned the Name, kingship, and the content of the blessing. According to what the Mechaber rules below in siman 214, the case here is where he said King of the universe, Master, etc. See what we will write there in Biur Halacha. It appears from the Shas that the same applies if he said, Blessed is the Omnipresent, King of the universe, Who created this bread, that he fulfilled his obligation.
