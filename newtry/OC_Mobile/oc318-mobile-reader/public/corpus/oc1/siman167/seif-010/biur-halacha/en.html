In place of the blessing HaMotzi, etc. If one recited Borei Minei Mezonos, many later authorities hold that he fulfilled his obligation after the fact. Eliyah Rabbah, citing the Derishah in siman 168, writes that Borei Minei Mezonos is a general blessing like Shehakol and covers every food, since all foods provide nourishment, except water and salt. Chayei Adam likewise rules this way in principle 58; see Nishmas Adam there.
Berich Rachmana, etc. Taz below, siman 219, cites Rabbeinu Yonah that one must also say Elakana. Other poskim did not cite this as practical halachah, implying that they hold that since Rachmana serves in place of the Divine Name, one Name is sufficient. It is obvious that according to both Rav and Rabbi Yochanan, if one says, “Blessed is Hashem, King of the universe,” he has certainly fulfilled his obligation, for he has mentioned both the Name and kingship. See Rashba in the chapter HaRoeh. This phrase does not appear in the Yerushalmi text before us.
Who produced this bread. See Mishnah Berurah. This follows Hagahos Ashri and other poskim, who write the ruling only as a possibility. Beit Yosef writes that since the doubt is D'Rabbanan, one has fulfilled HaMotzi after the fact. Yet according to Rambam, who requires that one at least mention the subject matter of the blessing, here he has mentioned it only by allusion. Where do we find such a blessing? I therefore copied only the cautious wording of the Rishonim on this matter.
