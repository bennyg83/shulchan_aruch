Drinking is permitted before one's animal, as we find with Rivkah, who said to drink and then that she would water his camels, as stated in Sefer Chasidim. This now explains Rashi on Chukas, that from the command to give drink to the congregation and to their animals we learn that the Holy One, blessed is He, has compassion on their property. Otherwise one could ask that perhaps it teaches that they should water their animals first. There is another answer, but this is not the place to elaborate.
