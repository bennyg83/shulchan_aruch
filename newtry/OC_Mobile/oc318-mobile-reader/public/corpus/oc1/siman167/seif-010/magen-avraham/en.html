Merciful One, King. If he said, King, Master of this, without saying bread, he also fulfilled his obligation, as stated by the Beit Yosef and Bach. But it requires study, for in siman 214 it is ruled that if he said King and did not say of the universe, he did not fulfill his obligation. If so, here too he must say King of the universe, Master, etc., and so it appears in the siddurim.
