Even on Shabbos. This is implied by the Gemara, since they asked regarding the HaMotzi blessing over matzah whether one may bless for others, implying that on an ordinary Shabbos and Yom Tov it is obvious that one may not bless for others, as stated by the Beit Yosef. The reason is that eating on Shabbos and Yom Tov is not an obligation on the person except for his enjoyment, for one who suffers from eating is exempt. If so, if he does not know the blessing we also say that he should not benefit, since it is forbidden to benefit from this world without a blessing. This is unlike matzah and the wine of Kiddush, which are obligations upon the person, and for which he must distress himself to eat; they are like mitzvah blessings. See siman 188, seif 7.
