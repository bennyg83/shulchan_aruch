Even on Shabbat, when one is obligated to eat bread, his friend cannot recite the HaMotzi blessing for him if he is not eating. It is not permitted for him to bless others, even if he is not tasting the bread himself, except for the HaMotzi blessing on matzah on the first night of Pesach and the blessing over the wine for Kiddush, both at night and during the day. Gloss {Rama: Rema: One should eat the piece of bread that was broken for the blessing before eating any other bread, and it should be eaten with appetite, as it enhances the mitzvah. (Agar and Nishmat Yisrael in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket).}
