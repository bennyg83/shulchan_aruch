Seif 20: Even, etc. This follows from the Gemara’s question there, etc., and as written there, “Rather, the blessing,” etc.
Whether, etc. See below, siman 273, seif 4 in the gloss, and siman 484. They themselves recite Bircas HaMazon, and if, etc. However, he may recite other blessings for them, even the blessing over vegetables. The Tur likewise writes this in the name of the Rosh. See there.
