[20] In one place, etc. The author of Malbushei Yom Tov wrote that this requires study — those sitting on a wagon while it is moving and they said "let us eat" — if we say the wagon combines, until here are his words. In my humble opinion I bring proof from what I wrote in siman 2 in the name of the Taz that one sitting on a wagon is like a rider; if so, whenever the wagon is not standing they are like riders traveling, for there is no keviut (fixed residence). See later siman 94 seif 4. Again the book Magen Avraham was printed and he wrote that since all three sit in one association one may say they combine — this requires study. On a ship it is simple that there is full keviut, and the sun even if he did not fix himself at the table is included in the motzi — until here. See above at the end of siman 89 and siman 94 that wagon and ship seem equal and one may distinguish. It seems we may be lenient on a wagon too when he intends — for b'dieved in every case they fulfill the obligation, and also because it is a rabbinic matter.
