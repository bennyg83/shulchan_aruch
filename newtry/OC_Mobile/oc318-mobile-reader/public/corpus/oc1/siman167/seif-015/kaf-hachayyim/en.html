Rokeach in the name of the Yerushalmi; Beit Yosef. The Rama, of blessed memory, writes in the gloss that one should give each person an olive's volume, implying that he rules according to the opinion that the HaMotzi portion must be an olive's volume. This also appears at the beginning of siman 475, which requires an olive's volume for HaMotzi, and it must be eaten continuously without interruption; see there. Olas Tamid, paragraph 11, similarly writes in the name of the Shelah that before speaking one must eat an olive's volume, for less than an olive's volume is not considered eating; see there. Shaar HaMitzvos, parashas Eikev, likewise writes that the HaMotzi portion must be an olive's volume; see there. Magen Avraham, paragraph 7, writes the same and says that one should initially be stringent; see there. So too the Bach in siman 475; Yad Aharon in Hagahot Beit Yosef to this siman; Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 2. This applies when one intends to eat more, in which case he should initially eat an olive's volume continuously after the blessing of HaMotzi. Regarding the blessing itself, however, even less than an olive's volume requires HaMotzi, as written below in siman 168, seif 9. Dagul Mervavah writes the same; see there. It is obvious that Dagul Mervavah refers to the initial preference, since his comment addresses Magen Avraham. After the fact, once one has recited the blessing and tasted even a small amount, if he then interrupted with speech or the like, he need not repeat the blessing. This is unlike Beis Menuchah, paragraph 16, who discusses the matter at unnecessary length; see there. See below, paragraph 45.
