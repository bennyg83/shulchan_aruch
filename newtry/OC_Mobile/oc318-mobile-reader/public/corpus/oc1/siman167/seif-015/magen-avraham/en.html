It is permitted to give. One might ask, in siman 206, seif 2, it is written that it is forbidden to interrupt more than the time needed for speech. One can answer that this is part of the tasting, since he blesses on behalf of all. However, the Yerushalmi states that Rav would taste with his left hand and distribute HaMotzi with his right.
That he should eat. And they should wait until he eats.
