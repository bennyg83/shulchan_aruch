The participants at the table are not allowed to taste until the one who breaks the bread tastes first. However, it is permitted to give each one their portion before he eats. (Tosafot and Mordechai, chapter 3 of "Sha'alu"). If each person is eating from their own loaf, and not all are dependent on the loaf of the one breaking the bread, they are allowed to taste before him. But if it is Shabbat, there must be two loaves of bread in front of the participants, aside from what is in front of the one breaking the bread.
