19) There: He must give from the portion, etc. Meaning, the primary mitzvah is to give each person bread from the portion he cut, rather than from the loaf that remains. One must take care with this at a large meal. Mateh Moshe and Maharil; Magen Avraham, paragraph 6. The words of the Arizal, of blessed memory, in Shaar HaKavanos, folio 72b, however, imply that one should cut from the loaf over which he recited HaMotzi and give to the others, rather than giving from the portion he cut for himself; see there. Although that passage discusses Shabbos, there is no distinction in this respect between Shabbos and weekdays. If one were required to give everyone from the first portion, it would have said that he should cut a large piece, take an olive's volume from it for himself and an egg's volume for his wife, and then distribute to the other diners. Instead, it implies that the first portion should be solely for him. For the others, he returns and cuts a separate portion for each person from the loaf itself over which he recited HaMotzi.
