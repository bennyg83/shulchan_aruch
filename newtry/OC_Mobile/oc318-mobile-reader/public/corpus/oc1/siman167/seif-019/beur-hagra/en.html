Seif 19: But for minors, etc. The statement there concerning his sons and household members refers to minors, for the concept of education does not apply to adults. This is further evident from the Gemara’s statement there, “Come and hear,” etc., concerning his sharecropper, who is included among the members of his household. The law is not limited to household members; it applies equally to others. The Gemara merely described the usual case. Beit Yosef.
