He cannot bless. Even if the listener cannot bless by himself.
The HaMotzi blessing. The same applies to every blessing over benefit. This is because only with a mitzvah blessing are all Israel guarantors for one another, and when his fellow has not fulfilled the mitzvah it is as if he himself has not fulfilled it. Therefore he can discharge him even though he himself already fulfilled the mitzvah. But regarding blessings over benefit, although the one deriving benefit is obligated to bless, since it is forbidden to benefit from this world without a blessing, nevertheless he has the option not to benefit and not to bless. Therefore, one who is not benefiting is not called obligated in this blessing. See below in siman 197, seif 4, in the Mishnah Berurah, regarding Birkas HaMazon.
But for minors, etc. Even ordinary minors, whose education is not legally incumbent upon him, may also be blessed with when they wish to benefit and do not know how to bless themselves; all the more so when they are members of his household. But for adults, even members of his household, he may not.
