HaMotzi. With the letter heh before the mem; if he blessed Motzi, he fulfilled his obligation.
And if many are reclining. Meaning, if those present wish to fulfill their obligation through his blessing.
They should direct their hearts. Since one wishes to fulfill his obligation through his fellow's blessing, he must set his mind to hear it, and then every word he hears from the one blessing is as if he said it himself.
And they should answer Amen. Through answering, which affirms the matter, it is considered as if he blessed himself. Nevertheless, after the fact it is not indispensable, as below in siman 213, seif 2.
And the one blessing should intend, etc. Answering Amen is also part of the blessing. Although he already blessed himself, nevertheless when they answer Amen to it, the blessing is more significant; therefore initially it is proper for him to intend to fulfill the Amen response of the one answering. See Biur Halacha.
He should leave a space. Meaning, he should pause slightly so that one of the two same letters not be swallowed under his tongue. The same applies between any two identical letters in blessings.
