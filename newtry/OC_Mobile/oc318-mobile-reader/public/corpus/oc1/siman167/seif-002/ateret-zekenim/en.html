One recites the blessing HaMotzi, etc. The meaning is that one says “HaMotzi” with the letter hei. Although when produce first emerges from the earth it is not yet bread but grain, we nevertheless say “bread,” because in the future the Holy One, blessed is He, will bring forth actual cakes from the earth. Toldos Yitzchak, Parashas Behar. It is possible that this is also why we say HaMotzi with a hei rather than Motzi. According to everyone, Motzi implies the past, whereas HaMotzi may imply the future, for the reason stated above. See Responsa Maharam Mintz, siman 74, regarding why we say “HaMotzi lechem” with a hei but do not say “HaBorei peri hagefen”; see there. Regarding bread, we bless “from the earth,” because the intent is Eretz Yisrael. Regarding fruits, however, we bless “of the ground,” which includes even land outside Eretz Yisrael. Bach; see there.
