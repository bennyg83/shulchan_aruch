Seif 2: And if there are many, etc. Yerushalmi, chapter 3 of Berachos: Rabbi Abba bar Zemina was serving before Rabbi Zeira. He poured him a cup. Rabbi Zeira said to him: Take it and recite the blessing, and have in mind that you will drink another cup, for it was taught that an attendant recites a blessing over each cup, but not over each piece of bread. He replied: Just as I have in mind to discharge you through the blessing, so you should have in mind to discharge me through answering amen. Rabbi Tanchum bar Yirmiyah said: The Mishnah also indicates this, for one who performs an act without intent does not fulfill his obligation, and one who hears from one acting without intent does not fulfill his obligation. The reason is that amen is included within the blessing. See Rashi, Berachos 47a, s.v. ad, etc.
He should give, etc. See Tosafos there, s.v. vehilchasa, etc., and as stated in the Gemara, 15b.
