HaMotzi. What the Beit Yosef wrote, that there are variant readings, requires study, for at the end of daf 52 it is stated explicitly that one must bless for what has passed, and so too in Eruvin 19a. Perhaps there is a scribal error in the texts, and they wrote a form meaning brings forth in place of a form meaning brought forth, confusing the letters. For this reason one should bless with the proper past-tense pronunciation. The Chok Yaakov wrote to bless with a different vowelization because all blessings are in the present participle, like borei. But the Gemara states explicitly that borei can imply the past; nevertheless, we say borei because it is the scriptural wording. So wrote the Shiltei Gibborim, and so wrote the Knesses HaGedolah in siman 202. The Chok Yaakov also wrote in the name of the Yerushalmi that even Beis Shammai disagreed only regarding fire, but regarding something renewed each year one blesses borei.
