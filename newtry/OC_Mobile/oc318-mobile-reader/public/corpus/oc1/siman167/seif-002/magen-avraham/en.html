Hamotzi. That which Rabbenu Yehuda Shlita wrote, that there are variant readings — requires study; for we explicitly state at the end of daf 52 that one must bless on what has passed, and likewise in Eruvin daf 19a. And it is possible there is a textual error in the versions, and they wrote "mefik" in place of "dafik," and mem was exchanged for aleph. And from what reason may one bless "n'hi-ye" in his utterance with kamatz under the yod? And in Choshen Mishpat he wrote to bless "n'hi-ye" with segol, because all blessings are present tense like "borei"; but in the Gemara it is stated explicitly that "borei" indicates the past; nevertheless we say "borei" is biblical language. And likewise in Shevuos chapter 66 the word "n'hi-ye" is past tense, and likewise in chapter 25 siman 242. And in Even HaEzer he wrote in the name of the Yerushalmi that even Beit Shammai disagree only regarding fire, but regarding something that renews every year they bless "borei," etc.
