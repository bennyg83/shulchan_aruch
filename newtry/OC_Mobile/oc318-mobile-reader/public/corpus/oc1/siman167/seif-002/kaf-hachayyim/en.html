2) There: At the place where it is well baked. Maseches Derech Eretz Rabbah, chapter 6, teaches: a person should not break the HaMotzi bread from the soft place, but from the hard place. The Beit Yosef cites this. The Levush writes the same, as do Magen Avraham, paragraph 1, and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, paragraph 2. Even one who prefers the soft place should break the bread only from the hard place. Although we generally say that a preferred food takes precedence, this case is different because we seek to demonstrate the bread's desirability. Halachos Ketanos, volume 2, siman 188; Beis Menuchah, paragraph 14.
