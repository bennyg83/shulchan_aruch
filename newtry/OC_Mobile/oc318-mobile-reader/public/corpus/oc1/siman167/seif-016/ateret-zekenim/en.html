The one who breaks bread is not permitted, etc. So wrote the Tur, and so Rashi explained in the Gemara, for we rule that the blessing must be completed before breaking bread; Amen belongs to the blessing. The Rif's version is: "The one who breaks bread is not permitted to taste," and the Beit Yosef wrote likewise in the name of Orchot Chaim. It appears that "to taste" was not exact; rather, even breaking the bread is forbidden until Amen is completed.
They customarily do not feed an animal, beast, bird, or non-Jew from a piece touched by the piece of hamotzi (Kol Bo in the name of the Radak). So too wrote R. David Abudarham in the name of Sefer HaMinhagim. But we have neither seen nor heard of anyone careful about this, and the matter has no basis at all (Beit Yosef).
