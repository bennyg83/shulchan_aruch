And he should not speak. Until he swallows the slice. However, if he spoke while still chewing, he need not return and bless, as stated by the Shelah. See the end of siman 210, that one who merely chews does not need a blessing; if so, the essence of the blessing is on swallowing, and this requires study.
Regarding relevant matters. Even if it is not needed for the HaMotzi slice itself, but only for the needs of the meal, as stated by the Beit Yosef and Bach.
Food for the animal. This is considered a need of the meal, for it is forbidden to taste before giving food to one's animal, as stated in the Gemara. But regarding drinking, a person comes before the animal, as we find with Rivkah, who said, drink, and I will also water your camels, as stated in Sefer Chasidim.
But afterward it is not considered, etc. The requirement to wait until salt is brought before each person, as written in seif 5, is only initially, for initially they too are forbidden to interrupt. Regarding what is stated in siman 174, seif 8, that with wine during the meal each person blesses for himself because the throat is not clear and they cannot answer Amen, and it does not say because they would interrupt between the blessing and drinking, the meaning is that all should taste immediately after the blessing, one after another. However, Tosafot in Pesachim 101, s.v. and Rabbi Yochanan said, wrote explicitly that if they interrupted, they must bless again. So wrote the Raavan, siman 181, and so the Rosh and Mordechai wrote explicitly. The Lechem Chamudos wrote that Tosafot hold like the Rokeach, but this is difficult.
