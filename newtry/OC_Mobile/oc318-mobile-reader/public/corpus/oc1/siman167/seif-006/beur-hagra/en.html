Seif 6: Matters concerning, etc. So explain Rambam and the Tur.
Give so-and-so food, etc. Rambam, following the reading of Rif and the other poskim in the Gemara: “Take, wrap.” This is why the Shulchan Aruch writes, “matters concerning, etc.,” as explained above.
Nevertheless, etc. This is implied by the Gemara, which states that he need not recite another blessing, etc.
And this, etc. His proof is from the end of chapter 3 of Eruvin: “Give it to a child,” etc. Beit Yosef and all the later authorities already disagreed with him, because there the adult will not drink, whereas here, since they will eat afterward, the speech constitutes an interruption between the blessing and eating. It appears that his proof is from Pesachim 100b: “Rav said: They fulfilled their obligation of Kiddush, but did not fulfill their obligation regarding wine,” specifically because a change of location is an interruption, even in the middle of a meal, as stated there, since it is like a diversion of attention, as Rosh writes there; and Rabbi Yochanan says, etc. Tosafos do not agree, as they write there, s.v. veRabbi Yochanan, etc.; likewise Rashbam, s.v. yedei yayin, etc. See seif 7. Nevertheless, there is no proof from Rosh and Mordechai, for they wrote only regarding one who recites Kiddush over bread. See there, unlike Magen Avraham.
