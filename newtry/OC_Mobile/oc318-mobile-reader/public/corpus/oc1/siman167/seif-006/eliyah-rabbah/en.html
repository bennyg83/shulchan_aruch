[Levush] and it is possible, etc. The phrase dry bread implies that only where he intends to eat dry, unclean bread does it require salt, but in the first case, clean bread such as ours, there is no mitzvah at all. From the Rama it implies that the mitzvah applies to all cases, and so I found explicitly in Mateh Moshe. Perhaps the Levush's further statement, that the verse says, etc., also refers to the first case. From the Rama it implies that he is permitted to wait, whereas from the Levush it seems possible that there is an obligation with clean bread. What is said in Berachos, that this does not require waiting, refers after he has already blessed HaMotzi. See Responsa Beis Yaakov, siman 168.
