With idle words. The same applies if he recited another blessing, as in Re'em, siman 4. I found in Baal Halachos Gedolos that if one answered Amen after his own blessing, it is as if he spoke idle words. Birchas Avraham wrote that if he paused but did not speak at all, he need not return and bless; see below, siman 206, seif katan 4. Olas Tamid cites the Shelah that one must eat a kezayis before speaking, and that if he placed the HaMotzi slice in his mouth and is chewing it, he may not speak until he swallows; if he transgressed and spoke before swallowing, he need not bless again. I checked the Shelah and found only the latter part. The Magen Avraham cited it as well, and his view inclines even to require a new blessing. Birchas Avraham writes plainly that the blessing is only on the purpose of the act, namely swallowing after chewing. This is somewhat difficult for the Shelah, who seems uncertain. As to the first point, that less than a kezayis is not considered eating, I do not know its source.
