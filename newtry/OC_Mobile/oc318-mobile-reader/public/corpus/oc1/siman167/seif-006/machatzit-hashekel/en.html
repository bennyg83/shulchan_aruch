Seif katan 6: From the slice. Meaning, the primary mitzvah, etc. This is why one may cut a slice larger than an egg. On Shabbos, even when eating alone, this is permitted, because he does so only on Shabbos and it is therefore evident that he acts out of affection for the mitzvah of the Shabbos meals and not out of gluttony. So states the Gemara.
Accordingly, at a large meal one should cut the HaMotzi slice sufficiently large to distribute from it to all the participants.
