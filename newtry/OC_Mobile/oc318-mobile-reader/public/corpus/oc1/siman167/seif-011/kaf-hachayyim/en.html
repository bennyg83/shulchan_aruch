11) There: So that if he holds the portion, etc. On weekdays, Maharil, of blessed memory, would cut a ring-shaped cut, leaving the center attached. He would break the piece from bottom to top. On Shabbos, he customarily used a thin semolina loaf for breaking bread. Since it was thin, it did not require a preliminary cut. He placed his eight fingers on its upper surface and his two thumbs underneath, recited HaMotzi, and at the conclusion of the blessing bent it from below upward until it broke.
