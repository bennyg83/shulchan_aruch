Two or many. This language was used because with Birkas HaMazon the law is that specifically three join for zimun and discharge one another, while two do not initially discharge one another. But with HaMotzi, even initially, two may have one discharge the other. The reason is explained below at the beginning of siman 193 in the Mishnah Berurah.
They reclined. That was their manner of eating in a fixed way, leaning on their left side on a couch, as Rashi explains.
With the members of his household. Because they are drawn after him, since he reclines; therefore they join with him even by mere sitting, as stated by the Beit Yosef.
It is considered a fixed setting. Therefore, even if each one eats from his own loaf, they combine, as stated in the Gemara.
And even for a group. Meaning, not a host with the members of his household, for for them even in the time of the Gemara this was like reclining, as above.
And for us even, etc. The text should read, and even for us. Meaning, one should not say that since our sitting is like their reclining, then in a place where reclining was not required for them, such as where they fixed a place for their eating by saying, let us eat here or in such-and-such a place, and so too a host with his household, for whom they did not need reclining, perhaps for us even sitting is not needed. Therefore it teaches that without sitting it is not called a fixed setting.
At one table, etc. See what is written in Biur Halacha in the name of the Gra.
