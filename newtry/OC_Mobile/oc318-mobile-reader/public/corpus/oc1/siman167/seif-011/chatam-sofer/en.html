There, in the Mechaber, seif 11, our manner of sitting. Note: According to Rabbeinu Chananel, cited by the Rosh, this is the law in principle. The Rosh asks: if so, what uncertainty did Rav’s students have? It appears to me that this itself was their uncertainty, namely whether haseibah means reclining on couches, as it does throughout the Talmud, or merely preparing to eat. The Gemara proves from the contradiction between the Baraisos that fixing a location at a specified place constitutes establishment, and consequently the haseibah of the Mishnah means merely preparing to eat at one table. Thus, there is no difficulty against Rabbeinu Chananel. If anything remains difficult, it is Tosafos’ question, s.v. heseibu, etc., because the questioner evidently had not considered Tosafos’ answer, whereas according to Rabbeinu Chananel that answer is in fact the correct conclusion. This requires study.
