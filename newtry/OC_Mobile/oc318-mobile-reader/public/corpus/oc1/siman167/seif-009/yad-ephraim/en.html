Magen Avraham, seif katan 19. “Wine during the meal” means a case where not everyone presently wishes to drink, for otherwise it would be wine after the meal, since everyone would turn their attention to drinking. It must therefore refer to a case where everyone still wishes to eat, but one person now wishes to drink. We say that the others should not intend to fulfill their obligation through his blessing, because they would then have to answer amen while their throats are occupied with eating. Magen Avraham therefore asks that since they do not intend to drink immediately, they should in any event not fulfill their obligation through his blessing, because they would interrupt between the blessing and drinking. He answers that although they do not wish to drink now in the ordinary manner, they will each immediately taste a small amount from the cup over which he blessed, one after another.
