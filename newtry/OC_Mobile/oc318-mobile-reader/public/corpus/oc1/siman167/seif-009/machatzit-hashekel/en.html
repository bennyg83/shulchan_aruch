Seif katan 9: Before, etc. As written in siman 651 regarding taking the lulav, where one must recite the blessing immediately before performing the mitzvah. That is accomplished by taking the lulav but not the esrog and then reciting the blessing, or by one of the other methods explained there. One should not recite the blessing before taking even the lulav, because that would be too far in advance of the mitzvah. The same is stated above in siman 25, seif 8, regarding tefillin: one should recite the blessing after placing them but before tightening them, not before placing them at all, for this reason.
