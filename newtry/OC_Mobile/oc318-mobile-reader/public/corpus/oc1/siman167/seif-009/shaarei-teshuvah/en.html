For eating. See Baer Heitev. See Magen Avraham in the name of Sefer Chassidim, that regarding drinking, a person takes precedence. See She’eilas Yaavetz, who writes that even when one is not in his own home, he may not taste food until he first gives food to his animal. One who raises a cat is not obligated to feed it first, although it is nevertheless proper to do so; see there. Birkei Yosef, siman 154, writes in the name of his grandfather that one must be careful to instruct that food be given to chickens in the morning, lest one violate the prohibition against causing suffering to animals, as is known from an incident that occurred in the time of the Arizal. The incident from the Arizal’s time is mentioned in Sefer Chareidim, whose author lived in his era. See Yad Ephraim regarding one who interrupted between the blessing and drinking by instructing that his animal be given water. He also offers a basis for why some people are not careful about this; see there.
