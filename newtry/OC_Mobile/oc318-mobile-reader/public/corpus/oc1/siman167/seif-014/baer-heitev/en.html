Breaks the bread. A groom breaks the bread on his wedding day even if someone greater than he is present. Beit Yosef; Magen Avraham.
To him. If the Torah scholar grants him permission, there is no concern. Knesses HaGedolah. Bach writes that one should be stringent and that a Torah scholar should not grant permission to an unlearned kohen. Magen Avraham rules similarly.
To give him precedence. By doing so, one merits long life. See Olas Tamid and Be’er Sheva, page 23.
Breaks the bread. The custom is to honor a guest. This is likewise implied by the Gemara, where Rabbi Abbahu honored Rabbi Zeira by allowing him to break the bread. Magen Avraham.
My teacher. The custom is to request permission from a kohen before requesting permission from a Torah scholar, since there is a mitzvah to give the kohen precedence. Nevertheless, merely saying “with permission” is ineffective unless the kohen actually granted him permission. Magen Avraham.
