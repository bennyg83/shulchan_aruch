If there are many participants at the table, the greatest among them breaks the bread. Gloss And if there is a host among them, he is the one who breaks the bread, even if the guest is greater. {Rama: Rema: If they are equal and one of them is a Kohen, it is a mitzvah to give him precedence. But if the Kohen is an ignoramus and the other is a Torah scholar, the scholar takes precedence. And if the Kohen is also a Torah scholar, but of a lesser level than the other, it is preferable to give him precedence, but it is not an obligation (see also Siman 281 and Mordechai, chapter "Bnei HaIr", and Beit Yosef in Siman 135 in the name of the Rashba). [And the one reciting the blessing should begin by saying, "With the permission of my teachers and masters."] (Or Zarua and Beit Yosef in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket).}
