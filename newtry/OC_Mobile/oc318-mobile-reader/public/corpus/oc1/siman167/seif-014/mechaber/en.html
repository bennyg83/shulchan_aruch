If there are many people reclining at the meal, the greatest among them breaks the bread. {Rama: If they are equal and one of them is a Kohen, it is a mitzvah to give him precedence. If the Kohen is an am ha'aretz and the other is a Torah scholar, the Torah scholar takes precedence. If the Kohen is also a Torah scholar but is of a lesser level than the other scholar, it is preferable to give the Kohen precedence, though there is no obligation to do so. See above, siman 201; Mordechai, chapter Bnei HaIr; and Beit Yosef, siman 135, in the name of Rashba.} If the host is among them, he breaks the bread even if the guest is greater. The one reciting the blessing should first say, 'With the permission of my teachers and masters.' [Or Zarua; Beit Yosef in the name of Shibbolei HaLeket]
