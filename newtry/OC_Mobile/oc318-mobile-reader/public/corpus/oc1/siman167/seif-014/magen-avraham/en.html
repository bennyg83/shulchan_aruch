(א) Great in everyone. It means that the wedding was carried out on the day of his wedding, even though he was larger than him

(ב) Please give him first. You should not be promoted:

(ג) Good to advance. A long time in this:

(ד) B was done. And he made an honor to grow up with a beautiful eye, because he does not give him a great slice that will be sufficient for the whole of the feast The Holy One is a sign of the Heaven’s glory:

(ה) With the permission of my teacher. And the practice is to take permission from a kohen (Levush); and so it is correct, for there is a mitzvah to give him precedence—if so, it is forbidden to bless without his permission. And so is established in the rhyme Nodah l'Shimcha; nevertheless it does not help for one who says with permission if the kohen did not give him permission.
