(ה) With the permission of my teacher. And the practice is to take permission from a kohen (Levush); and so it is correct, for there is a mitzvah to give him precedence—if so, it is forbidden to bless without his permission. And so is established in the rhyme Nodah l'Shimcha; nevertheless it does not help for one who says with permission if the kohen did not give him permission.
