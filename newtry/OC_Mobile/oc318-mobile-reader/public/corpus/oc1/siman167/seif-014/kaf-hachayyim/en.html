If one recited the blessing over a loaf intending to cut it on one side, but was unsuccessful and had to cut it from another side, Riaz holds that he must repeat the blessing. Before repeating it, he must say regarding the first blessing, 'Blessed is the Name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever.' Shiltei HaGibborim, chapter 3 of Berachos, cites him; Knesset HaGedolah, Hagahot HaTur. Olas Tamid, paragraph 2, however, writes that this requires practical study. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 4, likewise writes that according to what is written below in siman 206, seif 6, it is obvious that he need not repeat the blessing. This is true not only according to the Rama's words there, but even according to Maran's view; see his full discussion. See above, siman 140, seif 3, and what we wrote there in paragraph 15, and examine carefully. Birkei Yosef, paragraph 12, cited by Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 22, writes that one who recited HaMotzi and then dropped the portion from his hand should break another portion and repeat the blessing. We do not rule this way, because the matter is disputed in siman 206, seif 6, and we rule that a doubt concerning blessings is treated leniently. Rather, he should break another portion without a blessing, since he intended to eat more from this bread.
