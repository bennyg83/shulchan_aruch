"The bread." If it has a handle-like projection, it should be removed. Hagahot Minhagim. When he says the Divine Name he should raise the bread, and on Shabbos he raises both loaves. Maharil; Magen Avraham. "With bread." These are: "You shall not plow with an ox and donkey together," kilayim, leket, shikhechah, peah, bikkurim, terumat maaser, maaser sheni, and challah.
