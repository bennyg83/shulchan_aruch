There “That baked first,” with a dalet. Meaning, from the place that begins baking first. This is Rashi’s explanation. His intent is that even according to this reading, the explanation is the same as according to the first reading: one should not break the slice from its middle, but from the place where the crust forms, for that is where the baking begins. Within the crust itself, however, there is no distinction, because all its sides bake at the same time. On this the Rama wrote that our bread does not bake equally on all sides, and therefore one should break it from the side, etc. Yet this is difficult, for in siman 254 it is explained that even bread in the time of the Gemara had one side that began baking first. See there, and see the later authorities concerning this.
