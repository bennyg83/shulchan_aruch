Seif 4: He should give, etc. Yerushalmi, end of the first chapter of Challah: A person performs ten mitzvos before eating a piece of bread: because of “you shall not plow,” “you shall not sow,” “you shall not muzzle,” leket, shichechah, peah, terumah, maaser rishon, maaser sheni, and challah. It appears, however, that there is a scribal error, and it should read there: leket, shichechah, peah, terumah, maaser rishon, terumas maaser, maaser sheni, challah, bikkurim, and tzedakah. Rabbi Yitzchak, when he would come to eat bread, would spread out his ten fingers and say: Behold, I have fulfilled ten mitzvos.
Therefore, there is, etc. Tur.
And the yud, etc. Tosafos in the aforementioned gloss.
