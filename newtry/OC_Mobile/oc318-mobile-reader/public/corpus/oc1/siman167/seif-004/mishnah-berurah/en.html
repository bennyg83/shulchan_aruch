That depend on bread. For a person performs ten mitzvos before eating a slice of bread, as stated in the Yerushalmi at the end of the first chapter of Challah: when plowing, do not plow with an ox and donkey together; when sowing, do not sow your field with mixed species; when threshing, do not muzzle an ox while it threshes; leket, shikchah, peah, terumah, first maaser, second maaser, and challah.
