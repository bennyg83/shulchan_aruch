17) There, in the gloss: Until after the blessing, etc. The reason is that Shabbos requires lechem mishneh. Although cutting between the blessing and eating could constitute an interruption, since it is not a complete interruption and is done in honor of Shabbos so that the loaves remain lechem mishneh, the Sages were lenient.
