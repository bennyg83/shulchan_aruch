Seif 1: One breaks bread, etc. Rambam and Tur, in accordance with the latter explanation of Rashi there. Likewise, Maseches Derech Eretz, end of chapter 6, states: A person should not break bread from the soft place but from the hard place. Derech Eretz Zuta, chapter 6, states there: When one comes to break the loaf, he breaks it from the well-baked portion.
And with our bread, etc. This requires study. What distinction is there regarding our bread? The words of the Taz are astonishing.
And he should cut, etc. This follows the second explanation of Rashi there, that it finishes baking earlier at the bottom, etc. Rashi and Riva disagree regarding this in Shabbos 20a. One who fears Heaven should satisfy both opinions.
And he should cut, etc. Tosafos, Berachos 39b, s.v. vehilchesa, etc. The phrase “and afterward he breaks it” means that he separates it completely, but the initial cutting is done beforehand because of the concern for an interruption.
And it must, etc. See Beer HaGolah. This accords with Rabbi Yochanan.
And after, etc. This is the Gemara’s conclusion there.
And he should not break, etc. In chapter 7 of Berachos: The host breaks the bread so that he may distribute generously. The Yerushalmi, cited by Rokeach, states: What is the proper size of the piece? Some say an olive-sized piece, and some say less than an olive-sized piece.
Nor a piece, etc. At the end of chapter 6 of Derech Eretz: A person should not hold an egg-sized piece of bread in his hand; if he does so, he is regarded as ravenous. See chapter 6 of Berachos and chapter 15 of Shabbos.
And on Shabbos, etc. Tosafos in Berachos, in the passage cited above.
Nevertheless, etc. It is considered whole, as written in the laws of washing the hands above. However, because of the concern that the entire loaf might break and he would then lack lechem mishneh, he should not cut it initially.
But on Shabbos. As stated in Berachos and Shabbos there.
Or on a weekday, etc. The objection applies only when he does not eat from an egg-sized piece, as written in siman 170, seif 7.
