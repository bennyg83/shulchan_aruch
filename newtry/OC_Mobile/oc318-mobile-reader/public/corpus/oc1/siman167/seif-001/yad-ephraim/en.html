In Shulchan Aruch seif 1—in the place where it baked well, etc.—this is per Gemara's text as Rashi wrote, or text of where cooking preceded; and nevertheless he explains it is in the place where it baked beautifully as Darkei Moshe wrote; and per this text there is no distinction at all between our bread and theirs, since in any case the upper side or its edges is baked more beautifully than its middle which is soft, and one may cut either from above or from the edges. Unlike the text of nikrim or where cooking preceded whose explanation is from the place where baking begins—then there is distinction between upper and lower for there are views which cooks first; and if so it is hard why he ruled anonymously like the other text; therefore Rama wrote and in our bread etc.—meaning Shulchan Aruch refers to their bread where there is no distinction in beginning of its baking between upper and lower as Taz wrote, and it comes only to exclude the middle of the bread; and if so since he took in the place where bread baked beautifully, automatically the middle of bread is diminished; but in our bread truly there is distinction between upper and lower as Taz wrote, and it comes only to exclude middle of bread; and if so since he took place where bread baked beautifully automatically middle is diminished; but in our bread truly there is distinction between upper and lower and if so one must be concerned for the other text that we follow beginning of baking, and since there are views which of them precedes, he must cut from both.
