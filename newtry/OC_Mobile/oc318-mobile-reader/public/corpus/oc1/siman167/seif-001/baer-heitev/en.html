Well. Meaning in the hard place and not in the soft place. An old person who has no teeth should cut from the soft place. Halachot Ketanot II siman (182) [188].
Like a slice. It appears to me that even with a slice he should not cut completely before the berachah, so that it be larger; and so is the practice. Magen Avraham.
A kezayit. At the outset one should do so. The main mitzvah is to give each and every person a kezayit from the slice that he cut, and not from the remaining bread. One must be careful about this at a large meal. Mateh Moshe, Mahari Weil, Magen Avraham.
