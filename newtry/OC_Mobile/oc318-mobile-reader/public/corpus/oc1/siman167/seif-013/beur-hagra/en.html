Seif 13: Where, etc. Two people regarding Bircas HaMazon have the same status as without reclining together, as Tosafos writes on 46a, s.v. im, cited above. The Gemara there says, “Rather, conclude from this,” etc., and its statement that he fulfills his obligation refers to after the fact, since we rule that it is a mitzvah for them to divide. This is also evident from the Gemara’s question, “What does this teach us?” etc. Sefer HaRecanati writes in the name of Riaz that this is likewise the view of Rabbeinu Chananel. The Rosh, however, does not agree. Rashi also follows the Rosh and holds that he did not fulfill his obligation because they did not establish themselves together. According to them, one must distinguish between a case where no fixed meal was established and a place where zimun is not recited. Nevertheless, the view of Rokeach and the other poskim is primary. The same applies to blessings over fruit, even though there is no zimun for fruit; rather, it is initially forbidden. See siman 205.
If, etc. Berachos 47a: “And the halachah is,” etc. The same applies to HaMotzi, as stated there on 46a: The one who breaks the bread recites the blessing. See Tosafos there, s.v. lo, etc. The same applies to all blessings, as stated on 43a from the wording of the Mishnah, etc.
