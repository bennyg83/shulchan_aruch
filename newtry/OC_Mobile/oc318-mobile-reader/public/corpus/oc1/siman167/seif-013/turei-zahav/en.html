If the one reciting the blessing intended, etc. Meaning, after the fact they fulfilled their obligation, since the requirement that each person bless was imposed only initially. Beit Yosef writes similarly in siman 213 in the name of a Responsum of Rashba regarding a zimmun over fruit. The wording of the ruling here is not satisfactory. Why mention the intent of the one blessing and the listener, which is required everywhere, even when they recline? It should simply have said, “After the fact, they fulfilled their obligation.” Beit Yosef states that this appears in Sefer HaRokeach, but I did not find it in our version of the Rokeach.
