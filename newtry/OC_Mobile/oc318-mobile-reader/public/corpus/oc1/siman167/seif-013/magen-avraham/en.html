They fulfilled their obligation. It requires study that the Beit Yosef did not cite the Rosh, who wrote that if they did not recline, although hearing is like answering, and even if he answered Amen, he did not fulfill his obligation. So too wrote the Remazim, Rabbeinu Yerucham, and the Piskei Rekanati, siman 80, that according to Rashi, if each one sits alone, even if the listener and the one reciting intended, he does not fulfill his obligation with his fellow's HaMotzi blessing, because they must be fixed together. According to Rabbeinu Chananel it is not so. The Rosh already wrote that Rashi's explanation is primary. There is no proof from the responsum of the Rashba cited by the Beit Yosef in siman 213, for there, although there is no zimun for fruit, they had nevertheless established themselves together and fulfilled their obligation after the fact. But where they did not establish themselves at all, they did not fulfill their obligation. This also appears from his novellae, where he asked that we rule that one who heard and did not answer fulfilled his obligation, and he left the matter requiring study. This implies that here even after the fact he did not fulfill his obligation. His question can be answered as in the Yerushalmi, that Birkas HaMazon was excluded from this rule, and the same is true of HaMotzi.
