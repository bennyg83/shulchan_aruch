Malbushei Yom Tov also wrote that we see daily practice initially in this manner regarding the blessing on wine during the meal. So too wrote Lechem Chamudos, who cited Tosafot in Pesachim regarding Kiddush. But this is astonishing, for Tosafot later write that regarding wine they did not fulfill unless they drank, since otherwise it is an interruption. This supports the stricter view that the listeners should not interrupt before they themselves taste.
