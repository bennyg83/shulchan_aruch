If he intended, etc. Meaning, after the fact he fulfilled his obligation, for they required each one to bless only initially. See Magen Avraham, who left the law of the Shulchan Aruch requiring study, because some early authorities hold that without a fixed setting one cannot discharge another even after the fact. However, in Beur HaGra he ruled that the words of the Shulchan Aruch are primary. Know that this law also applies to Birkas HaMazon where they did not establish themselves together, whether two or three; if one intended to discharge his fellow with Birkas HaMazon, he fulfilled his obligation after the fact.
