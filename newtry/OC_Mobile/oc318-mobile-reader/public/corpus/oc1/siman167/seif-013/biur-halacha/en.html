If the one reciting the blessing intended to discharge them, etc. Taz asks what novelty is taught specifically here, since even when they established a fixed meal together, the one reciting the blessing must intend to discharge them and the listeners must intend to fulfill their obligation. See Eliyah Rabbah, who answers that perhaps when they established a fixed meal together, although the listeners still require intent to fulfill their obligation, the one reciting the blessing need not explicitly intend to discharge them, because it is presumed that this is his intent once they have established themselves together. This is analogous to the chazzan who blows the shofar, as discussed below in siman 589. Other later authorities likewise give this answer.
