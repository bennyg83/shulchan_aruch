Seif 4. And he blesses Birkat HaMazon on the cup. NB: see in Magen Avraham sk 7 who wrote that one who is not careful to bless all year on the cup — also now he should not drink from it. And I was asked how is the law regarding a cup of wedding blessings if they feast on Motzei Shabbat — whether it is permitted to drink from the wedding blessing cup. And it appeared to me that it is forbidden to drink from it, for even though at wedding blessings they are accustomed always to bless on the cup, nevertheless since with it there is also a cup of Birkat HaMazon and regarding the Birkat HaMazon cup the custom is not to drink from it — if now he drinks from the wedding cup and not from the Birkat HaMazon cup, he comes to treat lightly the Birkat HaMazon cup; therefore he should not drink from it at all — so it appears to me. And one who acts otherwise — we do not rebuke him; and so in my opinion.
