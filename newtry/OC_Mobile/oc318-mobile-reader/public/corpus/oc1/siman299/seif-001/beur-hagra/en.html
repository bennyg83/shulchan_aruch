Seif 1: It is forbidden, etc., until he must interrupt. Pesachim 105a.
Even drinking. Tosafos there, in the passage beginning “but.”
And some say. This follows Rif’s explanation there, according to which the statement that Shabbos establishes a fixed meal applies from the onset of twilight. Baal HaMaor explains that this is the context of the statement, “This applies only to Kiddush, etc.” Rif’s own words, however, do not imply this. Were that his intent, why would he need to say at the beginning of the chapter, “This applies only to Kiddush, etc.,” and then reject the statement, “just as one interrupts,” etc.? See there. See Rif, whose text follows the reading of the Geonim, “See whether the day has darkened,” etc. Although Baal HaMaor and Rosh ask why Shabbos should establish the produce for tithing merely because one may not repair it, Milchamos answers that since one may not separate the tithe, eating must also be forbidden. Otherwise the separation would not appear as an act of repair. Furthermore, the delight of Shabbos begins at that time, as stated in Berachos. See there.
