Magen Avraham, seif katan 1. See siman 261. Meaning, there it is explained what is called doubtful darkness. But it seems more likely that this is a textual error and it should say: see siman 271. He is alluding to his words in seif katan 10, where he wrote that at the beginning of siman 299 it implies that even in doubtful darkness one must interrupt. His intent there is as the Magen Avraham wrote here in seif katan 3, that from the fact that he wrote, “and some say this applies only,” etc., it implies that in this matter all agree, and they disagree only about definite darkness, whether one must interrupt. On this the Magen Avraham wrote, “and so it appears in the Gemara, that Havdalah establishes,” etc., meaning regarding the prohibition to begin. Thus the Magen Avraham disagrees with the Taz in seif katan 1.
