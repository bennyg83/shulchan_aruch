One who makes Havdalah over wine at his table — even if he made Havdalah before he washed his hands — exempts the wine [drunk] within the meal, so that no [separate] blessing need be recited over it. And some say that it is not exempt unless he washed his hands before he made Havdalah. {Rama: And if he made Havdalah first, he must recite after it the after-blessing "Al Hagefen" [the abbreviated three-fold blessing] (Tosafos; Mordechai, chapter "Keitzad Mevarchin").}
