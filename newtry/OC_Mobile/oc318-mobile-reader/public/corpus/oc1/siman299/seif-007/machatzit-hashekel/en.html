(Seif katan 7) And he recites Birkas HaMazon and may drink, etc. Meaning, even though this is before havdalah.
And see there, that according to Rabbi Yosei it is permitted. Meaning, the Beit Yosef cites there the Rosh, who wrote in the name of Maharit against the Rif. The Rif wrote on the baraisa in Erev Pesachim 102 that when a group was reclining and the day became sanctified, Rabbi Yehudah says they interrupt, take one cup for Birkas HaMazon, and a second for kiddush; Rabbi Yosei says they continue eating until dark, and when they finish they take one cup for Birkas HaMazon and a second for kiddush. The Rif wrote that although we do not rule like Rabbi Yosei at the beginning, since we rule like Shmuel that one spreads a cloth and recites kiddush, nevertheless we rule like the end of Rabbi Yosei's words. Thus if the day became sanctified after they finished eating and they want to recite Birkas HaMazon, they first bless over one cup for Birkas HaMazon and over a second cup for kiddush, as cited above in siman 271, seif 6. Maharit objected that this is understandable according to Rabbi Yosei, who permits continuing the meal and therefore permits eating before kiddush; for him the cup of Birkas HaMazon belongs to the meal and may be drunk before kiddush. But according to us, who rule like Shmuel that one must interrupt and spread a cloth, how can he drink from the Birkas HaMazon cup before kiddush? The Rosh agreed with Maharit. With havdalah, however, all agree, since we rule like the first opinion in seif 1 that even once it is certainly dark he need not interrupt eating. It is therefore just like kiddush according to Rabbi Yosei, and one may drink from the Birkas HaMazon cup before havdalah.
And it appears to me that one who is not careful, etc. Although even for one who is not careful there is still a mitzvah, since for him it is not an obligation, we cannot say that the Birkas HaMazon cup belongs to the meal so as to permit him to drink it before havdalah. See Sefer Tosefes Shabbos.
