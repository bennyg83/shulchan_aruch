Yalkut Reuveni on the Torah, Kuntres Acharon, folio 6a, writes in the name of Sefer HaMaggid: It is proper to recite Havdalah immediately when a person leaves the synagogue, separating ourselves from those forces before they come to rule over us. In places where Havdalah is recited in the synagogue, this is even better, because they are given no opportunity to rule over us. For this reason the Sages said that one who tastes food before Havdalah dies of askara, meaning that through that food those forces attach themselves to him and remove his soul through askara. These are his words. This applies specifically when Maariv is delayed until three stars are visible. Where the prayer is recited early for some reason, it is more appropriate to delay Havdalah. Yafeh LaLev, paragraph 1. One should recite the liturgical poems arranged for after Shabbos until the time for Havdalah arrives, in order to drive away the external forces. Ruach Chaim, paragraph 2.
