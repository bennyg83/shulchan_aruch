[10] [Levush] He may recite Havdalah at the end, etc. Although we rule that Havdalah may be recited until the end of Tuesday, not including the end itself, Terumat HaDeshen, siman 154, explains that this limit applies where eating is possible. Here, where it is impossible, it is permitted. This rejects Olat Shabbat's understanding that Levush holds the endpoint is included. Some difficulty remains, however, because Levush appears to contradict himself in siman 581, end of seif 1, where he writes that Havdalah cannot be recited then.
