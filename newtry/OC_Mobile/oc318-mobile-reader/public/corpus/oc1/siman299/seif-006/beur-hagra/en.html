And some say. Like the second version. The Ran wrote that he had only the first version in his text, and there he read “the entire day.” At first glance, according to the Rif’s ruling, Havdalah is not recited on the night after Tishah B’Av that fell on Shabbos, as written in the above Tosafot, s.v. Ameimar. If so, that which is written in siman 291, seif 3, follows the first opinion. But when you examine the Rif there, there is at first glance a difficulty, for he wrote in the incident of Ameimar, “learn from this,” etc., “the entire Shabbos,” etc., and if it occurs for him until the end of the third day, he recites Havdalah. Rather, he holds like the view of the Baal Halachos Gedolos, brought by the Ran there and by the Rosh at the end of Taanis regarding Tishah B’Av that falls on Shabbos, that one recites Havdalah on the night after Tishah B’Av. And although, etc., “the entire day” and no more applies only where it was permitted to eat, etc.; see there. He learned this from the words of Ameimar, who said, “learn from this,” etc., as above.
