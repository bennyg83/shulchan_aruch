Seif 6. And he did not make havdalah on Motzei Shabbat; but on Motzei Yom Tov he has no compensation — so too at the end of Sefer Lashon Chachamim testifies that the rabbis of Prague agreed thus; and in my humble opinion one may still say that nevertheless he has compensation on the first day after Yom Tov, for per the view of the first authority he makes havdalah only each first day and no more — the reason that day follows night — likewise on Yom Tov so it is; therefore one may say even per what we hold he makes havdalah until the end of day three, and for the reason that it is after Shabbat — one may say this is not applicable to Yom Tov; nevertheless it appears to me also the foundation regarding day is not because of after Shabbat but because day follows night, and proof appears for this, for all agree the reasoning applies for day one like in Motzei Shabbat — from what Rosh wrote chapter 4 of Berakhot in the name of Rabbenu Yonah: if he expects to have a cup tomorrow and erred and did not make havdalah in prayer — he does not return and pray; and so in Shulchan Aruch above siman 294 seif 2; and behold Rosh in Pesachim holds he can make havdalah until the end of day three — why did he specify specifically that he expects to have for tomorrow; and likewise the difficulty on Shulchan Aruch's ruling; and what I wished to say above siman 294 in the margin — since he does not expect to have for tomorrow it should be permitted to eat, and the reason to return and pray is not decisive for me, since the poskim wrote in their language that the reason for the prohibition of returning and praying — therefore it is possible where he does not expect a cup for tomorrow that it is permitted to eat, he does not return and pray; and for this one may say the reason: if havdalah on the cup would be in its time — namely on Motzei Shabbat Kodesh or the day after, which is like Motzei Shabbat Kodesh — in this he erred and prays; but where he does not expect a cup for tomorrow, even if he will have a cup on day two or three, since it is not the primary time but only because it is called after Shabbat — in this he returns and prays; and it is possible this is the intent of Ma'amar Mordekhai there; therefore the law appears that if he forgot on Motzei Yom Tov to make havdalah, in any case he can complete havdalah the next day; examine. But one standing opposite part of what Beit HaGolah wrote — behold the reason of Magen Avraham who makes havdalah is that he makes havdalah at night of Motzei Shabbat; but if he did not make havdalah at night, since he tasted he no longer makes havdalah — and this is what the Mechaber wrote "and some say," etc.; from this it implies the primary time of havdalah is only at night of Motzei Shabbat; for if we say day one with night of Motzei Shabbat are all one matter to distinguish regarding taste — end.
