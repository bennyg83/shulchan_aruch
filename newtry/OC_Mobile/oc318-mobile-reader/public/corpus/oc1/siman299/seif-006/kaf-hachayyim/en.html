According to the Geonim in Shaarei Teshuvah, printed at the end of Neharos Damesek, siman 80, folio 6 at the end of the third column, however, it appears that even water should not be drunk before Havdalah over a cup; see there. Midrash Talpiyos, branch of Havdalah, folio 118d, similarly writes: It is forbidden to drink water before Havdalah, and one who does so will die of askara. This applies also during twilight after Shabbos. If he was eating the third meal at his table and the meal continued until the time fit for Havdalah, however, he may drink. These are his words. We wrote the same above in siman 291, paragraph 17, in the name of the Arizal; see there.
