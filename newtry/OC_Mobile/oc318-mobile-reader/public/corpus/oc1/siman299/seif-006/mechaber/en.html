6. If one forgot and did not make havdalah on Saturday night can make havdalah until the end of Tuesday. There are those who say that we' do not make havdalah except for all day Sunday and no further. And specifically Boreh Pri HaGafen, and מבדיל בין קודש לחול. However, over a candle and spices we do not make a blessing except on Saturday night. There is one who says regarding that which we established that one who ate something can still make havdalah, this applies if he makes havdalah at night after Shabbat. However, if one did not make havdalah at night, upon eating he can no longer make havdalah. RAMA: The essence is like the first opinion. And one who fasts three days and three nights hears havdalah from others. And if there are no others near him, he can make havdalah in Shabbat during the day, then drink and then afterwards accept the fast upon him. See Siman 553.
