One who forgot and did not make Havdalah on Motza'ei Shabbat makes' Havdalah until end of day 3; and some say he makes Havdalah only each Sunday and no more; and specifically in Borei peri hagafen he makes Havdalah between holy and profane; but on candle and spices he blesses only on Motza'ei Shabbat; and some say the reason we rule "taste" applies only when he tasted before Havdalah on the cup.
