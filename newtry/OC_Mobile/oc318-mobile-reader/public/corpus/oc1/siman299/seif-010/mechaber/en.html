It is forbidden to perform any melachah before one makes Havdalah; and if he made Havdalah in the [Ma'ariv] prayer, it is permitted, even though he has not yet made Havdalah over the cup. And if he needs to perform melachah before he has made Havdalah in the prayer, he says "Hamavdil bein [kodesh lechol]" without a blessing, and then performs melachah. {Rama: And likewise women, who do not make Havdalah in the [Ma'ariv] prayer, should be taught to say "Hamavdil bein kodesh lechol" before they perform melachah [Kol Bo]. And some say that all of this is with a full-fledged melachah, such as writing or weaving; but the mere kindling of a lamp, or carrying from one domain to another, does not require this [Rabbeinu Yerucham]. And from this the custom has spread to be lenient, that people light lamps as soon as the congregation says "Barchu"; but the primary [view] is like the first opinion. And some say to draw water on every Motza'ei Shabbos, for the well of Miriam circulates among all the wells on every Motza'ei Shabbos, and whoever encounters it and drinks from it will be healed of all his ailments [Kol Bo] — but I have not seen [anyone practice] this custom; and see above, siman 263, [regarding] one who adds from the weekday to the holy, whether it is permitted to tell another, after he has made Havdalah, to perform melachah for him.}
