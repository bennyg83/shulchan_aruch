(Seif katan 8) Havdalah is delayed. See siman 272, seif 9, and siman 551, that it is permitted, etc. Meaning, there is another remedy: he can recite kiddush himself and give others to drink. The Rama wrote similarly in siman 591 that because we are stringent not to drink havdalah wine from Rosh Chodesh Av until Tisha B'Av, he should give the havdalah cup to a child. The Rama also wrote above in siman 242 that if one vowed from wine, he recites kiddush over wine and gives others to drink. Although the Magen Avraham above in siman 272 disagrees with the Rama, his main reason there is concern for the Geonim in siman 271, seif 14, that if the one making kiddush did not taste, he did not fulfill the obligation. That applies specifically to kiddush, not havdalah, for there in siman 271 it is explained that with other matters requiring a cup, the Geonim agree that another person's tasting suffices. However, according to the Taz in siman 271, seif katan 18, the distinction is that kiddush has an asmachta according to the Torah to sanctify over wine, and havdalah also has an asmachta. According to the Levush there, however, it works somewhat, for he says kiddush is according to the Torah and supported by a verse; one may then rely on the view that havdalah is D'Rabbanan.
