It implies in the Rosh, chapter Arvei Pesachim, that if he had wine during the meal, even though he recited Birkas HaMazon without a cup, nevertheless he need not recite an after-blessing over the cup of Havdalah, for Birkas HaMazon exempts it, since it is all one drinking. Although in siman 174, seif 6, it is written that even if he did not have wine during the meal, he need not recite an after-blessing, here is different, because the wine does not come for the need of eating but for the need of a mitzvah. This is not comparable to kiddush at the end of siman 272, for kiddush is for the need of the meal, since kiddush is only in the place of a meal. So it seems in the view of the Tur. See what I wrote in siman 174 regarding other beverages. But it is difficult, for the Rosh wrote regarding the four cups that if it is so that an after-blessing is required when he has no wine during the meal, then even if he has wine, it should not exempt him; see there. If so, the same should apply here. It is forced to say that since it is a mitzvah, it is different.
