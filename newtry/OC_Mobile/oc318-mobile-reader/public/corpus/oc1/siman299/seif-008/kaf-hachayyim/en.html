Mechilta similarly expounds zachor and shamor to mean: guard Shabbos as it departs, like a person who does not want his beloved to leave him as long as he can delay the departure. Hagahas Yafeh Nof writes that Maharsha interrupted a wedding meal, went to the synagogue, and afterward returned. It is possible that regarding a meal he followed the opinion introduced as 'some say.' Magen Avraham there.
