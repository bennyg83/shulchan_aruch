3) There. An item whose species grows attached to the ground, etc. Even when most residents are non-Jews and the non-Jew performs all the melachos for non-Jews, the item is nevertheless forbidden here because of muktzeh. Olas Shabbos, subsection 1; Erech HaShulchan, subsection 1. Regarding an item forbidden because of muktzeh, there is no distinction between an act performed for a Jew and one performed for a non-Jew. The only distinction is that when it was performed for a non-Jew, one need not wait after Yom Tov for the time required to perform the act, as written above, siman 515, se'if 2. See there. Even if it is uncertain whether the item was gathered or trapped that day, it is forbidden, as written there, se'if 3. See there.
