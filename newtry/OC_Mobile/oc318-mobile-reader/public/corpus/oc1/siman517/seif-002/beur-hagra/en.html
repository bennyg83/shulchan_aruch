Se'if 2: A Jew etc. did not do well. For purchase on Yom Tov is forbidden except without fixing a price, as written "one may not fix a price," and there at the end of chapter 3; provided he does not mention to him the sum of the purchase, and there 37a; and whatever is forbidden is forbidden through a non-Jew, even Chol HaMoed which is rabbinic, as written in chapter 2 of Moed Katan; and telling [a non-Jew] is a shevus even from before nightfall, from the Igros of chapter 1 of Shabbos.
But if, etc. Since it was brought for a non-Jew, like the turnips of Mechoza, and there it was without a money purchase; and even though here he transgressed a shevus, as written in the Yerushalmi, chapter 5 of Beitzah, and the Rif and Rosh brought it there; and all who did [the act], whether inadvertently, etc. See there.
But if he is accustomed, etc. As the Rosh wrote at the end of chapter 16 of Shabbos, se'if 12, regarding one who gathered grasses, etc.: if a Jew is crafty and is accustomed to do his affairs by craftiness through a non-Jew, it is certainly forbidden, as written in Bava Metzia 90 regarding those oxen that Arameans stole, etc.: he said to him, craftiness, etc.
