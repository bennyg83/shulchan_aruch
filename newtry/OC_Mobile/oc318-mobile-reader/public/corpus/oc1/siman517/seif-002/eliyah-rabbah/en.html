Whose majority are Kutim. That is, that most of those who buy bread and flour are Kutim (Taz); and it appears they learned from what is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 1, se'if 4, that one requires a majority of Jewish slaughterers; and according to this everything depends on the majority of those commonly found, and this requires further study. The Maharil sealed the flour on erev Yom Tov with Kutim, and once he found the seal spoiled and forbade taking the bread, end quote; and that is [a case] where there was a Jewish majority. It appears to me that if a Jew bought flour or fish from erev Yom Tov and left it with Kutim without a seal, and on Yom Tov he sees that it is good and fine like at first, it is permitted, for we are not concerned that the Kutim exchanged it if he does not benefit from the exchange.
