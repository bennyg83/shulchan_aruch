[ב] Most of them are Kutim. That is, most of those who buy bread and flour are Kutim (Taz), and it seems they learned from what is written in Yoreh De'ah siman 1 seif 4 that most slaughterers must be Israelites; and per this everything depends on what is commonly found, and it requires study. Maharil sealed the flour on the eve of Yom Tov among Kutim, and once the seal was defective and he forbade buying the bread — up to here; and we were most of Israel. It appears to me that if an Israelite bought flour or fish on the eve of Yom Tov and left it among Kutim without a seal, and on Yom Tov it looks as good and fine as at first — it is permitted, for we are not concerned that Kutim exchanged it if they do not benefit from the exchange:
