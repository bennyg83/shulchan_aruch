12) There: He forgot and ate a kezayis, etc. Ran, citing Raah, writes that the amount is a date-sized portion, like Yom Kippur. The Rosh, however, reads that if he forgot and ate a kezayis, he has lost his fast; Tur likewise rules. Shulchan Aruch therefore follows the Rosh and Tur. Levush and Bach rule likewise, and this is the consensus of the later authorities.
