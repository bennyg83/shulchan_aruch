Or Taz; see siman 522 Taz 3 and Magen Avraham 16 and Taz there; here according to his method; therefore he ruled lenient in part that if he said “this day” and suffers greatly and fasts until after six and a half hours he prays Minchah Gedolah and says Aneinu without the two words “fast of our affliction”; see siman 562; borrow and fast the next day; in Geonim's ruling there in 522 siman 1 that in Maariv too one does so and fasts the next day — vow of Monday-Thursday for the whole year, for one cannot borrow only one day after, unlike vow of ten days on Monday or Thursday — then one does not borrow the next day but the following Monday or Thursday, as written later. See Turei David in the name of responsa of Rosh that Monday-Thursday in Maariv means the second day after; so what is written there Monday Thursday half a year — one does not borrow, for mercy days are not borrowed; see Magen Avraham note 7 and see there it will be explained.
