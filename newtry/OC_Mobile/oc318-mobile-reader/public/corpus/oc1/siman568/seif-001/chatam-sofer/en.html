In Magen Avraham, s.k. 10: on a communal fast day the practice is. Gloss: And according to what the Shach wrote there, that they redeem on the night of the entry of day 31, then he should make the meal on the night before the fast and redeem at the time of the meal, for that is greater publicity, as Maharai wrote there. And perhaps it is not proper to fix the meal on the night that belongs to the day of the fast. And afterwards I found in Birkei Yosef in Yoreh De'ah, siman 305, s.k. 14, that they practice thus in Egypt, and it implies even on a communal fast; see there in s.k. 16.
