He completes. In Responsa Panim Hadashos, part 2, siman 9, he concluded regarding one who erred and did not know that this day was Tishah B'Av and ate, that he should fast the tenth day and observe on it the five afflictions; but he should not cancel the mitzvah of tzitzis and tefillin, even though the Magen Avraham wrote that one who did not fast on the Tenth of Teves has no remedy; for that case is different, since it is proper to fast the ninth and the tenth; see there. And see Responsa Noda BiYehuda, siman 10, which concludes regarding one who accepted the fast until he studied a chapter and forgot and recited a blessing over bread, and before he swallowed it he remembered the fast: he should swallow, and it is not comparable to what is written in chapter 5 of Terumos that he should spit it out; see there, since here there is concern of a blessing in vain.
