And forgot. Even if he intentionally ate a lot, he should not eat more. Bach; Magen Avraham.
A kezayit. It appears to me this is specifically if he ate it at one time; but if he delayed in between and waited more than the time of eating a pras, they do not combine. Regarding drinking, its measure is a cheekful, as on Yom Kippur; see siman 612.
Another day. If he accepted upon himself to fast two consecutive days and forgot and ate at night, he fasts two other days. Kol Bo; Beit Yosef siman 572.
They are stringent. The reason is that some say that even on this day he is permitted to borrow the fast and repay it; therefore, when he ate, he lost his fast and is obligated to fast another day. Thus they are stringent like both views: he must fast that entire day and also another day. But on a fixed fast, when it is forbidden to eat, he does not need to fast another day unless his intention is atonement for his sin and inadvertence. Terumat HaDeshen; Magen Avraham.
