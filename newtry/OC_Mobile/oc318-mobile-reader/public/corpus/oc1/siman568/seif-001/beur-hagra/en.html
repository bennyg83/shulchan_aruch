(א) Seif 61, vow, etc. Yerushalmi Nedarim chapter Konom halachah 1: Rav said one may fast for hours; Rav said a person loans his fast and repays; Shmuel said to him: is this a vow? It is a vow to fast; if he forgot and ate an olive's worth he lost his fast. R' Ba in the name of the rabbis there when he said an unspecified day — but if he said “this day” he fasts and completes. Rosh explained because on an unspecified day one loans and repays, unlike “this day”; and what was said there after Rav's words, etc.; and this is what is written here, and the same if he was a Torah scholar who does not loan and repay, as in Gemara chapter 1 of Shabbat and chapter 1 of Taanit, and all the more for a communal fast which is only that day; therefore “a person loans his fast” — emphasize “his fast,” etc., and likewise a known day as Rosh explained in Nedarim and Hagahot in Shabbat that “this day” is in such a case; so Tur, and you have no “this day” from such a case; Rosh in Shabbat explained he vowed to fast this day, see seif 62.
