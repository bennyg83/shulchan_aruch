(In Shulchan Aruch seif 6) nevertheless l'chatchila, note: but Rosh in Taanit simanim 9 and 13 that one does not say on an individual fast; so in Remazim; what Tur wrote in siman 563 explains Rosh Chodesh that he should say in the [Musaf] prayer of a fast “I am on an individual fast” because it addresses also the public; Shulchan Aruch took the law from Mordechai Avodah Zarah. From there there is no proof he wrote so according to Rav who holds there is communal fast in Babylonia, and above it explains Rav Sheilah how to act — say I am on an individual fast; but we hold like Shmuel there is no communal fast in Babylonia — this law of saying on six for accepting individual fast is not needed, as Rosh brought proof from the fast of Monday-Thursday that they do not mention even l'chatchila individual fast. It also seems to bring proof from Nedarim 18: unspecified vows in Judea are permitted because they are accustomed that priests are there — if one said “herem” unspecified he meant herem of priests not herem to Heaven; Rambam chapter 2 of Nedarim, and so anything similar in vows we follow people's language; but in Tosafot 14 s.v. every fast, etc., it says one should read “I will be on individual fast”; Beit Yosef did not mention Tosafot's words; in truth there is no proof from there, for Tosafot explained there is no communal fast in Babylonia because they had much rain — implying other lands have communal fast, but not Rosh and Rabbenu Yerucham's reason because they have no prince; if so, perhaps communal fast was accepted. Therefore there is no doubt perhaps communal fast was accepted — therefore l'chatchila one should say “I will be on individual fast.”
