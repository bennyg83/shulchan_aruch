1) [Se'if 1] If one vowed to fast this day, etc. One accepted a fast at Minchah on Wednesday, thinking the next day, Thursday, was Erev Rosh Chodesh, and later learned it was not Erev Rosh Chodesh because Rosh Chodesh would occur on Shabbos. He does not require annulment, though it is preferable to annul the vow. Zera Emes, Responsa Orach Chaim, siman 83; Machazik Berachah, paragraph 1; Shaarei Teshuvah, paragraph 1.
