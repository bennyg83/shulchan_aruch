11) There: He forgot and ate a kezayis, etc. This applies only if he swallowed; if he merely tasted, it does not. That is, even if he vowed simply to fast one unspecified day, if he merely tasted but did not eat, he fasts and completes that day. It appears that "tasted" means he tasted up to a revi'is and expelled it, as stated in the preceding siman; otherwise it is eating. Bach; Ateres Zekenim. Maamar Mordechai, paragraph 2, disagrees with Bach and writes that "tasted" here does not mean tasting and expelling. It means that he only tasted a small amount, less than a kezayis; he then completes his fast. See below, paragraphs 13 and 14.
