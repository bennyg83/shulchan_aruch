(S.k. 5) For a matter, etc. And nevertheless he must fast part of the day, etc. It is clarified in Beis Yosef that the reason is that through this he need not annul his vow, and it is permitted to eat at a seudas mitzvah and repay on another day; for in this, that in any event he fasted part of the day, he has somewhat fulfilled his vow that he accepted upon himself yesterday.
And see siman 225 regarding a bar mitzvah meal, in what manner it is called a seudas mitzvah; see there in Magen Avraham, s.k. 4.
And siman 558, etc. Magen Avraham wrote there that a meal they make on the night before the wedding, in a place where they do not send sivlonos, is not called a seudas mitzvah.
It is stated at the end of Berachos: whoever benefits, etc. It appears that the view of Magen Avraham is that for this reason it is called a seudas mitzvah. But in Responsa Chavos Yair, siman 70, it appears that he holds it is not a seudas mitzvah. And see there, for he elaborated on the details of a seudas mitzvah: what is called a seudas mitzvah or not.
