(36) Or on Rosh Chodesh. It appears that one who fasts a dream-fast on Rosh Chodesh Nisan or on Rosh Chodesh Av need not sit a [makeup] fast for his fast, for there are those who say that it is a mitzvah to fast on them, as is written in siman 580.
(37) Or on Erev Yom Kippur. And specifically these days; but other days on which one does not say Tachanun, such as the month of Nisan, Lag BaOmer, the 15th of Av and of Shevat, and between Yom Kippur and Sukkos, one need not sit a [makeup] fast for his fast (Peri Megadim).
(38) And see above, siman 288, se'if 4. There the law of sitting a [makeup] fast for his fast is clarified.
