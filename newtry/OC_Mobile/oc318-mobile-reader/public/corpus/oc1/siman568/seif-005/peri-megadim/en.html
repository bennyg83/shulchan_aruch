And likewise. See the Taz; see siman 559, se'if 9: the same law applies here regarding yahrzeit; if a son was born to him and the bris milah falls on the yahrzeit, he does not fast; or if he wishes, he fasts until Minchah Gedolah. And see siman 551, letter 12: there regarding meat and wine; but regarding yahrzeit one may say it is specifically one to whom a son was born and it is his festival; but a sandek and a mohel should fast on their yahrzeit. And possibly if there is no other mohel in the city, he should perform the mitzvah and eat at the meal if it is by day; and this still requires further study for now. And in this it appears that he should fast; and regarding annulling his vow if he afterwards becomes a mohel, see regarding this for one to whom a child was born.
