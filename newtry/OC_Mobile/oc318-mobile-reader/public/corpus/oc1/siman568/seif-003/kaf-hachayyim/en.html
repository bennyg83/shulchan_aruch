3) Whenever fewer than ten people fast on Erev Rosh Chodesh or the like, no Torah scroll is taken out and the shaliach tzibbur does not say Aneinu in the repetition, even in Shome'a Tefillah and even at Minchah. Zera Emes there, siman 86. An ancient pamphlet of novel rulings from the early sages of Yerushalayim, dating from the nasi's time in the year 5269, states that individuals were designated to fast in response to a threatened calamity. The nasi agreed that the shaliach tzibbur should say Aneinu in Shome'a Tefillah because it was not a communal fast; they should not take out a Torah scroll in the morning, but should take one out at Minchah, and Aneinu in Shome'a Tefillah should be recited as at Shacharis. The same pamphlet states that when the official decreed against wine in Yerushalayim, they fasted because it was a communal affliction. Machazik Berachah, paragraph 3; Zechor LeAvraham, paragraph 400. See above, siman 566, paragraph 29, where this law is explained. Consider carefully.
