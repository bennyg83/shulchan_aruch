(26) One who vowed to fast, etc. And the same applies to one who became obligated to fast by force of teshuvah.
(27) He may postpone them, etc. That is to say: even though the vow was during the summer days, nevertheless it is permitted to postpone them, since he did not stipulate that the fasts be during the summer days.
(28) Until the winter days. See Magen Avraham: that is specifically when he was forced by circumstance and cannot fulfill immediately; but otherwise he is not permitted to postpone them, for we are concerned lest he die, unless it is a short time until the winter days. And so Machaneh Ephraim ruled in Hilchos Nedarim, siman 15, that he must fulfill immediately; but if the one who vowed himself says that it was in his heart not to do them immediately, it is obvious that he is believed regarding what is between him and his Creator.
(29) And he may borrow, etc. That is, in what is stated above in se'if 2, that one borrows and repays.
