Mahari Weil siman 5, brought in Beit Yosef — this is Rambam's method who ruled like R' Meir in Nedarim 63 and as Abaye established there that the anonymous mishnah is R' Meir, as in Shulchan Aruch Yoreh Deah siman 220 in his name; but all poskim disagreed, as Rosh and Ran there that halachah is like R' Yehudah, and so ruled in Shulchan Aruch there and Choshen Mishpat siman 43 paragraph 28, and Orach Chayim beginning siman 528 in the gloss, and Even HaEzer siman 126 seif 7 in the gloss; and this is what the Rav wrote "some say," etc.; so too in Turei David siman 294 and Maharil siman 31; Maharil also wrote proof from chapter 1 of Megillah that if not for connecting redemption to redemption it would be reasonable to do in the first [Adar], except he wrote one may say there it is because we do not pass over mitzvot; in Turei David he also brought this proof and wrote because we do not pass over mitzvot, and the same we do not pass over, etc.; he wrote there later "I saw," etc., and these are Maharil's words there and he did not heed his words. But all their proofs do not seem right to me, for from Nedarim there is no proof, for there they disagree about people's language — in Nedarim we follow people's language, and so in documents; but here the essence is fasting in both months; for Rabbenu Gershom one need not establish Tosefta in Megillah 6b except regarding order of portions, but otherwise he agrees to Tosefta that all mitzvot practiced in the second are practiced in the first; so too in Megillat Taanit chapter 2 which lists Megillat Taanit of Adar — it is written there there is no difference between first and second Adar except reading Megillah and sending gifts to the poor; so I saw in Magen Avraham who brought this proof; although he wrote Rabbenu Gershom says all mitzvot practiced in the second are not in the first except eulogy and fasting forbidden in both — we hold like Rabbenu Gershom; nevertheless he said except eulogy and fasting, meaning all mentioned in Megillat Taanit refers to that, and the same for fasting as in the Gemara there is no difference except order of portions that must be near Nisan; so in the Gemara "and the same for eulogy," etc., refers to all prohibitions of that month; Tosafot there s.v. R' Elazar, etc., 14 and 15 in first Adar — although forbidden in eulogy and fasting, one need not make feast and joy; at first glance it is difficult, for we derive prohibition of eulogy and fasting from feast and joy; but per this it fits that the reason eulogy and fasting are forbidden in the first is not because the verse forbids only in the second as "the second," but because they are written in Megillat Taanit as in Megillat Taanit and Gemara there 5b, and it is written there in Megillat Taanit there is no difference between first Adar, etc., as above; afterward it is written they write in a document "first Adar," etc., as in end of chapter Konom — plainly documents are a different dispute. Second proof they brought from Megillah that Rabbenu Gershom's reason is because of redemption, etc. — is not proof, for certainly per their view who hold one month alone it is proof, but the Gemara there proves as written they practice in both, since it challenges Rabbenu Gershom's reason and does not challenge Tosefta who practices in both — we learn the presumption is to practice in both, only after they learned from each year it is practiced in only one; as written "if the second were announced," etc., it challenges: granted, etc., but, etc.; therefore they need not in each year, whether R' Yehudah or Rabbenu Gershom, except to exclude Tosefta who should not practice in both — to exclude first or second we do not learn from Scripture, only for this because we do not pass over, and for that because "the second" is written; but Magen Avraham's proof from "if the second were announced" does not seem different because "the second" is written — on the contrary from this it would seem to practice in the first; but what I wrote is a clear proof, and what Magen Avraham wrote from end of chapter 2 of Taanit regarding Nikanor day and Turinus day is a clear proof, and from what is written it was needed only for an intercalated month — although the essence is like Rashi's second explanation there and Tosafot there explained; nevertheless from not answering otherwise we learn it was needed only for an intercalated year — we learn it applies in the second too, as Megillat Taanit states explicitly, and so Mahari Weil there that Maharil so ruled, and he ruled so; see Shach Yoreh Deah siman 220 note 8 that many later authorities hold like Rambam and wrote that in Nedarim too one should be strict in both months, all the more here where it is the law.
