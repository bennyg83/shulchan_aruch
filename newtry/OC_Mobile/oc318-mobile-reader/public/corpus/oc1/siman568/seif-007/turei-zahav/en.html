In second Adar. For Rebbi Meir and Rebbi Yehudah disagree: Rebbi Meir holds that unspecified Adar is the second, and Rebbi Yehudah holds that unspecified Adar is the first; and the Rambam ruled like Rebbi Meir; and "there are those who say" is the Rosh, who rules like Rebbi Yehudah; and in Terumas HaDeshen, siman 294, he decided like Rebbi Yehudah; consequently he should fast the first, for the reason that we do not pass over mitzvos.
