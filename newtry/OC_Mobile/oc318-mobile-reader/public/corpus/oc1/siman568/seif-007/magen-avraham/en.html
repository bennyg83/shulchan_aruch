In second Adar. Like one who vowed until Adar — if he knew the year was intercalated he is forbidden until second Adar, and the same here he knew (Beit Yosef, Mahari Weil). It is difficult, for Rabbenu Yerucham wrote Yoreh Deah siman 220 note 8 that for Rambam this is the law, but most poskim hold even if he knew it is intercalated it is until first Adar; moreover it is resolved if the first year was not intercalated and he began fasting in Adar — then like a vow; but if the first year was intercalated and he comes to consult which Adar to fast — how is it done? In my humble opinion bring proof from Taanit 18a for an intercalated month — Rashi explained the year was intercalated, etc. — implying explicitly practiced in second Adar; they also said there 2b that Turinus day and Nikanor day are all practiced in second Adar — otherwise it would immediately challenge from Purim, see there; so too yahrzeit day one practices in both, and Turei David brings proof to practice in the first from chapter 1 Megillah that Megillah reading in the second is because connecting redemption to redemption is preferable — implying if not for that reason they would do in the first, see there; in responsa Maharil siman 31 he rejected this proof because in Megillah presumably they would do in the first because we do not pass over mitzvot — therefore in Gemara it implies if not for the verse they would do Purim in both, see there; perhaps days written in Megillat Taanit were practiced in both, as somewhat in end of Megillat Taanit “there is no difference between first and second Adar except Megillah reading,” etc. — implying all days written in Megillat Taanit were in both; also 14 and 15 are forbidden in eulogy and fasting in both — therefore by law one fasts in both; but since yahrzeit is only custom one need only do as initially accepted; but one who vowed to fast on his teacher's death day and the like must fast in both as above, see Pri Megadim siman 502 in Shach and Taz and in Knesset HaGedolah in the name of R' Shlomo HaLevi note 16 wrote one fasts in the first, and in Eshel Avraham siman 512 wrote if father died in first Adar he fasted in a plain year in Shevat and Adar, end — in my humble opinion for all mourners he need not be given Kaddish only once, etc., as Mateh Moshe in the name of R' Shlomo Luria for one who does not know father's death day — clarify one day but do not exceed saying Kaddish; one whose father died on first day of Rosh Chodesh Kislev and next year Cheshvan is short and Rosh Chodesh Kislev is only one day — requires study when he fasts if on 29 Cheshvon which is day before his Kislev Rosh Chodesh, for always the second day is primary for counting seasons from the second, or we say at least Rosh Chodesh is upon him and he fasts on Rosh Chodesh; in Even HaEzer siman 126 there are views whether thirty days count to intercalated month or the coming one — it seems if the first year was short he fixes 29 Cheshvon, for truly in Gemara Nedarim thirty days count to the intercalated month (see siman 55 seif 10); but when the first year was also full he must fix the day on Rosh Chodesh, and therefore in later years even if short he fixes on Rosh Chodesh as a vow following people's language as Maharil and Mahari Weil, see Pri Megadim Yoreh Deah siman 220; if he died on first day of Rosh Chodesh Adar he fasts next year on first day of first Adar Rosh Chodesh and not on 29 of it, for first Adar always stands in place of second Adar.
