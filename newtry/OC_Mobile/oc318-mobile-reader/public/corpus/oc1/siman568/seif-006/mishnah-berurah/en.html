(39) There is no communal fast in Babylonia. And the same applies throughout all of chutz la'aretz, as is evident below in siman 575, se'if 10.
(40) Therefore an individual who accepted a fast upon himself: we are not concerned, etc. From this wording it is implied that in Eretz Yisrael, if he accepted upon himself an unspecified fast, we are concerned lest he accepted upon himself a communal Fast, and he must cease while it is still day and is forbidden in melacha. And perhaps nowadays, when it is uncommon to Fast the communal fasts clarified below in siman 575, se'if 3, presumably his intention is for a mere unspecified fast; and this requires further study. And see below there, se'if 10, what is written on this matter.
