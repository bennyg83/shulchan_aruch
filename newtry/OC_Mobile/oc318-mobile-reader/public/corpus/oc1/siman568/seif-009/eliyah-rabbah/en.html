[Levush] Twelve peshitim, etc. However, coins are not the same from one time to another, as stated by the Malbushei Yom Tov. The Magen Avraham at the end of siman 334 wrote that one peshit is one and a half Polish gedolim. Here he wrote that a wealthy person should give according to his wealth. The Rama wrote: It appears that this is specifically where he accepted upon himself an ordinary fast, but if he accepted it by way of a vow, he must fulfill his vow. The Levush, however, appears to have omitted this intentionally, in accordance with the reason he wrote, that acceptance of a fast is like a vow to charity.
