10) There: But if he vowed to fast one day, etc. This means that he initially accepted a vow to fast one day and later, at Minchah, accepted that fast for the following day. Despite the later acceptance, it is not called a vow for "this day." Beis Yosef, citing the Rosh. Therefore, if he forgot and ate a kezayis on the fast day, although he accepted it the previous day at Minchah, he may eat that day and fast another day, because his original vow did not fix a particular day. If he did not previously accept a general one-day fast but at Minchah accepted a fast for tomorrow, it is called a fast for "this day," as stated in the following se'if. Even if he ate a kezayis, he must therefore complete the fast and need not fast another day, as stated above, paragraph 7.
