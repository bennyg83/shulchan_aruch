He may postpone them, etc. The Magen Avraham asked: He should be obligated to fast immediately, lest he die before then, as written in Yoreh Deah, siman 219, that one who vowed to buy a house must buy the first suitable house. All the more so where he is obligated to fast as repentance, for perhaps he will die without repentance. If the time is short, however, we are not concerned that he may die. One may answer that a winter fast is also suitable for him, like a house, where he need not buy one that is not suitable.
