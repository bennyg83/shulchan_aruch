(S.k. 10) For they did not, etc. And so it is implied in siman 572, se'if 2, which wrote: if they decreed a communal fast and they began to fast and Rosh Chodesh, Chanukah, Purim, or chol hamoed coincided with it, they fast and complete. And behold, on these days the meals are seudos mitzvah, and even so they must complete, since they decreed a communal fast.
And see what I wrote in siman 559, s.k. 11, regarding a communal fast that was postponed.
And this requires further study when they fast the Twentieth of Sivan, etc. However, before the meal, etc.; and so it is implied from what I wrote above in the name of Terumas HaDeshen. That is to say, s.k. 5, where he wrote: and nevertheless he must fast part of the day, etc. Even though there the reason is as I wrote above, that through this it is somewhat called that he fulfills his vow that they accepted from yesterday and he needs no annulment; nevertheless it is required that there be upon him somewhat the name of a fast. And through this, that he does not eat until he goes to the meal, it is more recognizable that until now he fasted and now he will eat for the sake of a mitzvah.
