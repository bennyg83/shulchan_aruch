Or I will fast Mondays and Thursdays all year. It is difficult for me: here he said that in this case one cannot borrow even when distressed; and above, siman 552, se'if 10, if it falls on Erev Tishah B'Av then he borrows his fast and repays, even though he accepted upon himself a fast of Mondays and Thursdays all year. And in truth there is a dispute among the poskim whether we say even regarding "this day" that one borrows and repays, as is clarified in Beis Yosef. It appears to me as the Rama wrote here, that a loan does not avail so long as it is "this day"; and that [ruling] above in siman 552 is not the law, but rather specifically where he fasts up to a large measure [of the day] and afterwards borrows and repays, as I wrote above according to the view of the Geonim that the Rama brings; and also here it avails if he fasts part of the day and afterwards borrows and repays, even on "this day," as appears to me according to my humble opinion. And what he wrote that Monday and Thursday are called "this day" and a loan does not avail regarding them: it appears that this is specifically when he wishes to fast on another day in place of Monday or Thursday, for we say that he accepted upon himself specifically Monday or Thursday, which are days of mercy; but if he wishes to borrow from this Monday onto another Monday, or onto another Thursday, that is, after they complete the number of days of his vow that he set for himself, he may properly do so, for it is then like an unspecified day in this regard.
