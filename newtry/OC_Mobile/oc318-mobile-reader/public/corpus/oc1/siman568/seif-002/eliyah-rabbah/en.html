He forgot and ate an olive's bulk, etc. It seems to me that this applies specifically when it was at one time, but if he delayed in between and waited longer than the time required for eating a pras, the amounts do not combine, as stated by the Magen Avraham. I likewise found this in the Rivash, siman 287. Even if he did this intentionally several times in the day, he has not lost his fast, for in any event he is fasting, except that he committed a prohibition by intentionally eating half a measure. The Magen Avraham wrote that with respect to drinking, its measure is a cheekful, as on Yom Kippur; see siman 612. In my humble opinion, since regarding eating we hold that the measure is an olive's bulk, even though on Yom Kippur the measure is a large date, the same applies to drinking, whose law is as above, siman 272, seif 13. If one accepted upon himself to fast two consecutive days, and he forgot and ate on the intervening night, he fasts two other days, as stated in Beit Yosef, siman 562.
