[2] He forgot and ate an olive's worth, etc. It appears to me only once; but if he waited in between more than eating time of a piece it does not combine (Magen Avraham); so I found in Rivash siman 287; even if intentionally many times a day he did not lose his fast, for he is still fasting — only he transgressed eating half measure intentionally. Magen Avraham wrote regarding drinking his measure is full cheekfuls like Yom Kippur, see siman 612, end of his words. In my humble opinion, since for eating we hold an olive's worth, even though Yom Kippur is with a large date — the same for drinking, its law as above siman 572 seif 13. If he accepted to fast two consecutive days and forgot and ate on the middle night — he fasts two other days (Beit Yosef siman 562).
