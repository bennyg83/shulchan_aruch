(S.k. 2) A kezayis, etc. See siman 612. And see there that regarding drinking, whose measure is a cheekful: as to combining, there are two views whether we also require within the time of achilas pras, or its measure is like the measure of drinking a revi'is.
For the Ran, etc. With a koseves; and less than this he has not lost his fast; since on Yom Kippur the measure of eating to make one liable is a koseves. And granted that we do not hold like him in this, and a kezayis suffices; nevertheless in this we learn regarding drinking from Yom Kippur that a cheekful is required (and we also learn the time of combining from Yom Kippur).
