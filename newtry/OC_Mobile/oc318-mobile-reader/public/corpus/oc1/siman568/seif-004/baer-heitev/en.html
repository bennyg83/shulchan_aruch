To forty. Those who fast three days and three nights are accustomed to fast two more hours on the night of the fourth [day], so that it be by way of hours a hint for "Return, O Israel, unto Hashem your God"; and those who eat at the beginning of the night have not lost out, for it is the numerical value of kindness (Mateh Moshe). And one who fasts two days should fast one hour at night and also cease one hour while it is still day, for it is like twenty-seven fasts: the first day is one fast, and afterwards each hour counts as one day. And "hour" is not precise, for part of an hour is like the whole. Magen Avraham.
Teshuvah. And nevertheless it is better that he fast spaced [days], so that at every time his heart be humbled and his sins be before him always. Magen Avraham.
Yom Kippur. And he should begin to fast after the 15th of Av, and he should not fast on Shabbos or Rosh Chodesh. Olas Tamid; Magen Avraham.
