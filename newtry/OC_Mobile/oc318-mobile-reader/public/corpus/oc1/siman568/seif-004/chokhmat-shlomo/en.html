(There, se'if 4.) There is one who says that two consecutive days and two nights should be equated to forty non-consecutive fasts. Gloss: Behold, I saw in the newly printed sefer Responsa Neta Shaashuim, siman 11, that he wrote that "non-consecutive" is not exclusive, and the same applies even to consecutive [fasts]; see there. And with due respect, the words of the Rambam escaped his notice; for he wrote in Hilchos Teshuvah, chapter 5, as follows: And when one is accustomed to interrupt his day and night, and is accustomed to fast two days and two nights, the later authorities wrote that they are like forty days that are not consecutive; and I received that they are like twenty-seven consecutive [days], etc.; end of the wording relevant to our matter. If so, it is explicit that "not consecutive" is essential; for he adds that it is like twenty-seven consecutive, which implies that corresponding to forty it is specifically not consecutive. And there is no leisure now to elaborate further; examine carefully.
