That two days, etc. Those who fast three days and three nights are accustomed to fast two more hours on the night of the fourth [day], so that it be by way of hours sixty, a hint for "Return, O Israel, unto Hashem your God"; and those who eat at the beginning of the night have not lost out, for it is the numerical value of kindness (Mateh Moshe). And at the end of Rosh Chodesh HaKatzar he wrote: Moreinu HaRav: those who fast two days should fast one hour at night and also cease one hour while it is still day, for it is like twenty-seven fasts: the first day is one fast, and afterwards each hour counts as one day; and the thirty-fourth hour, for part of the hour is like the whole.
