[Levush] And some say, etc. The Magen Avraham wrote that he was not precise, for the Terumas HaDeshen did not write this except according to the Or Zarua, who holds that even this day may be borrowed and paid back, and only a fixed day is forbidden, such as the day on which his father died. But according to us, that even this day may not be borrowed and paid back, this too is included in that rule. This also appears from the Malbushei Yom Tov, as will be explained nearby. However, in Mateh Moshe, siman 773, he also ruled like these 'some say.' It also seems to me that his reasoning is that since the Beit Yosef cited the Terumas HaDeshen mentioned above, even though he had previously ruled that this day may not be borrowed and paid back, it implies that he holds that nevertheless Mondays and Thursdays may perhaps not be comparable to this day. Moreover, it would have been proper to rule that even this day may be borrowed and paid back, since the Raavad, Rashba, and Terumas HaDeshen rule this way, and so Rabbeinu Yerucham, page 163, ruled in the name of Tosafot. Although the Beit Yosef was stringent regarding this day, nevertheless regarding Mondays and Thursdays one should not be stringent. I also wonder about the Terumas HaDeshen, siman 275, who analyzes how the Or Zarua contradicts the Yerushalmi, and did not see the responsum of the Rashba, siman 246, who resolves the Yerushalmi with a good explanation. See there, for I have abbreviated. It is also possible that the Beit Yosef agrees that since he fasts part of the day, it is permitted even on this day, and so it appears from the Taz.
