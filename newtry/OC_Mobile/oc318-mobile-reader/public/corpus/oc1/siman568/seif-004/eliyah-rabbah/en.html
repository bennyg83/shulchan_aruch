[4] [Levush] Some say, etc. Magen Avraham wrote and was not precise — in Tur haDeshen he did not write so but according to Or Zarua who holds even this day one may borrow and it is forbidden only on a fixed day such as father's death day; but for us even this day is forbidden to borrow — if so this too is included. So Magen Avraham, end; so implies in Malbushei Yom Tov explained below; however in Mateh Moshe siman 573 he also ruled like “some say”; it also seems he holds since Beit Yosef brought the mentioned Tur haDeshen although he ruled earlier that this day is forbidden to borrow — it implies he holds nevertheless on Monday and Thursday perhaps it is not like this day; moreover it was fit to rule that this day too is permitted since Raavad, Rabbenu Gershom, and Tur haDeshen ruled so, and Rabbenu Yerucham p. 163 in the name of Tosafot; although Beit Yosef was strict on this day, nevertheless on Monday and Thursday one need not be strict; I also wonder on Tur haDeshen siman 295 debating Or Zarua how he contradicts Yerushalmi — he did not see responsa of Rashba siman 246 who answers Yerushalmi well, see there shortened; perhaps Beit Yosef concedes since he fasts part of the day it is permitted even this day, as Taz implies.
