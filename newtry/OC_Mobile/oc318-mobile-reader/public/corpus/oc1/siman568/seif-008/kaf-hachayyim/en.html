8) There: Or if he was the shaliach tzibbur, etc. It appears that on a communal fast, even if he ate, he may say Aneinu. As stated in siman 565, he can truthfully say "on this day of the fast," because the Sages instituted fasting. On a private fast, however, although he must complete it, if he ate the amount that would have destroyed his fast had he not vowed this particular day, he should not say Aneinu. Nahar Shalom, paragraph 1; Mishnah Berurah, paragraph 3. See above, siman 565, paragraph 6.
