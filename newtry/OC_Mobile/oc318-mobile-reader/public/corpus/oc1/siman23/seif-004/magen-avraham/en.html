In Yoreh Deah, siman 351, Shulchan Aruch writes that tzitzis are placed upon the tallis of the deceased, yet here he rules that it is forbidden to enter a cemetery or come within four amos of a deceased person while wearing visible tzitzis. This follows Rosh's first explanation. Why, then, does he write the opposite here? A distinction can be suggested, but it has no firm basis in the poskim. This requires study.
