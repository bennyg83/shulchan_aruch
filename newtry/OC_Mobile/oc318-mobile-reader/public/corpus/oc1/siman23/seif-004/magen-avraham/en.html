Where they are accustomed. It implies where they are not accustomed to remove them, there is no concern if pallbearers wear tzitzis. It requires investigation — for Yoreh De'ah siman 351 writes they place tzitzis on a deceased's tallis, yet here he ruled it is forbidden to enter a cemetery or four cubits of a corpse with tzitzis — this is like the first view of the Rosh; why did he write here the opposite? One may distinguish — but poskim do not make this primary; it requires investigation.
