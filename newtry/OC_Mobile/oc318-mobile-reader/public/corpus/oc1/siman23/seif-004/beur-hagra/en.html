Se'if 4, "in a place," etc. Menachot 41a — "at that time," etc. Tosafos Bava Basra 74a, Pesachim 40b, and Niddah 61b — from here proof that the dead have tzitzis; likewise in midrash. They strained to find a reason for their custom of removing them — see there. And although we say in the last chapter of Berachos there, "one who carries," etc. — although they have tzitzis, nevertheless there is lo'eig lerash, since they are exempt from mitzvos, as Shabbat chapter 2, one version; likewise Rosh in Menachot there. Some explain "at that time" — when they bring him to the grave we throw it on him because of lo'eig lerash: those who carry him have tzitzis and he does not; when they place him in the grave they remove them. Likewise Tur Yoreh De'ah siman 351 in the name of Razah; accordingly Shulchan Aruch wrote here "in a place," etc. His words require investigation: first, we do not rule like this explanation, as Tur wrote there in the name of Ramban — there is no doubt; rather they bury in a tallis with tzitzis, as Shulchan Aruch there. Moreover, if the dead have tzitzis, there would be no prohibition in tzitzis — and by our custom there is no prohibition at all — contradicting all the above; Magen Avraham also wrote thus.
