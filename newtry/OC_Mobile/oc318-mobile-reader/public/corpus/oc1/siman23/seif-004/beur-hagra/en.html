Seif 4: In a place, etc. Menachos 41a states, “At that time, etc.” Tosafos in Bava Basra 74a, Pesachim 40b, and Niddah 61b write that this proves that the deceased are buried with tzitzis, and the Midrash says the same. They strained to explain the reason for the custom of removing them; see there. They further write that although the Gemara at the beginning of the third chapter of Berachos says, “He raised them, etc.,” even if the deceased have tzitzis there is still mockery of the deceased, because they are exempt from mitzvos, as explained in the second chapter of Shabbos, 30a. Rosh writes the same in Menachos there. Some explain that “at that time” means that when they take the deceased to the grave, they place tzitzis upon him because otherwise there is mockery of the deceased: those carrying him have tzitzis and he does not. When they place him in the grave, they remove them. Tur in Yoreh Deah, siman 351, writes this in the name of Baal HaMaor. Based upon this, Shulchan Aruch writes here, “in a place, etc.” His words require study. First, we do not rule according to this explanation, as Tur there cites Ramban that there is no doubt in the matter and that one buries the deceased in a tallis with tzitzis. Shulchan Aruch rules this way there. Second, according to this explanation, if the deceased has tzitzis there is no prohibition, while according to our custom there is no prohibition at all. This contradicts everything stated above. Magen Avraham likewise notes this.
