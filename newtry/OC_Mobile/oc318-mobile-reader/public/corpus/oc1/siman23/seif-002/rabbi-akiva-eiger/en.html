Levush himself sensed this difficulty, and wrote one may distinguish: there nothing was done in the sechach itself; but here the main tying is in the tzitzis themselves — but this is strained; it requires investigation. See Taz above siman 12, se'if katan 3; in Turei Zahav's question at the end it is also explained that if the tzitzis themselves are disqualified, repair does not help — for example if they tore beyond kedei akirah, tying a thread does not help, for it is ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy — see there.
