And it did not help. For even if they tie them, the mitzvah of tzitzis is not voided, as siman 10. Moreover, according to their view that tzitzis become void by tying — when they return and untie them, it is ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy (Beit Yosef). Levush wrote there is no ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy here, as siman 626: if one removes the roof over the sukkah it is not ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy — see there. In my humble opinion, no comparison — there the disqualification is not in the sukkah itself, only in what prevents it; when removed it is valid. But here the disqualification is in the garment itself, as siman 10, se'if 6 — see there and Beit Yosef; see Sukkah 33, and Tosafos Berachos 18 — "a deceased's tallis: one may tie and fasten them inside the corner; if they are obligated in tzitzis, behold they have tzitzis; if not, behold they are covered." It implies that even when tied, the mitzvah of tzitzis is not voided; and perhaps when tying two to each other it is different.
