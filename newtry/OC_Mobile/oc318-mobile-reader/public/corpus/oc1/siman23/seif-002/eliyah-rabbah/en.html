[2] And it did not help, etc. See Levush, what he asked on Beit Yosef; in Eliyahu Zuta I settled on the correct view — see there. I again saw that Malbushei Yom Tov also agreed with Beit Yosef — his wording: if this is not compelling, he already compelled from another side that tying does not void, from siman 10, se'if 3, as Levush began; he already rejected that it is unlike hanging, etc.; he also compelled that otherwise one wears a four-cornered garment without tzitzis; and in this he nicely distinguished tzitzis from sukkah — so far his wording. Mordechai, Laws of Tzitzis, in the name of R' Yitzchak — his wording: when I die I command my sons to tie tzitzis to the garment corners so they stand ready to untie the tied garment — behold they hang on their own, and this is not proper practice — so far. Bach explained Yoreh De'ah siman 351: he meant to tie the two tzitzis on two corners to each other — implying disqualification by tying; difficult for Beit Yosef and Levush. Perhaps it is different there, for they do not stand to untie while not alive; nevertheless they wrote "this is not proper practice" — requires investigation; Magen Avraham did not address all this. Bach wrote there: it is good to tie them to each other or cover them under the corners — implying a living person should take to heart not to do so. However one may push from Semak, who wrote there that then there is no lo'eig lerash: either way, if obligated they have tzitzis; if not, they are covered — so far; consider well.
