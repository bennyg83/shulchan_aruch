Likewise, a tallit katan worn beneath one's garments involves no prohibition of mocking the destitute, and one may enter a cemetery wearing it because it is covered. Beit Yosef; Bach, who writes that this is the custom; Perishah; Ateret Zekenim; Maamar Mordechai, ot 1. Birkei Yosef, ot 1, writes likewise in the name of Tiferet Shmuel, page 40d. It is surprising that he cites this law from Tiferet Shmuel without mentioning that it appears in Beit Yosef. Shaarei Teshuvah, ot 1, cites him. See our words below, siman 24, at the end of ot 4.
