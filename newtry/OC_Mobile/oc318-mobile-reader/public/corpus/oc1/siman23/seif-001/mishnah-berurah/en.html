(1) Lo'eig lerash — it appears as if scoffing that they cannot fulfill the mitzvos.

(2) For themselves — for then their custom was that their garments were made with four corners.

(3) They are covered — such as one who hides the tzitzis of the tallis gadol under the corners of his cloak; and for the same reason, a tallit katan worn under one's garments has no lo'eig lerash, since it is covered — unless one goes without the outer garment; then they are forbidden until tucked into the corners [from Pri Megadim it seems one should be stringent regarding tallis gadol even when covered, since it is designated for prayer; but Beit Yosef does not imply thus, and Derech HaChayim wrote plainly that when covered it is permitted].
