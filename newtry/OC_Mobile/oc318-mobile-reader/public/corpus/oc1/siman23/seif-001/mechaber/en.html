The laws of tzitzis in a cemetery. Containing 4 se'ifim: It
is permitted to enter a cemetery while wearing tzitzis, provided they do not drag over the graves; but if they drag over the graves it is forbidden because of lo'eig lerash (mocking the poor). When is this said? In their times, when they attached tzitzis to garments they wore for themselves; but we, who intend them only for the mitzvah, it is forbidden even if they do not drag — and this applies when the tzitzis are visible; but if' they are covered, it is permitted.
