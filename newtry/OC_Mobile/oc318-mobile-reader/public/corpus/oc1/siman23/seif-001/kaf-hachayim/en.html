(1) [Se'if 1] It is permitted to enter, etc. But if they drag over graves it is forbidden because of lo'eig lerash, etc. The reason: souls of the dead stand over their graves, as Sha'ar HaMitzvos, parashat Vayechi, and Sha'ar HaGilgulim, introduction 37, folio 73a — otherwise one could not distinguish Ben Tzitzit HaKaseh (covered or uncovered), for in any case the dead person is covered in the grave; rather because of the soul standing above the grave, as stated. See Shetili Zeitim, letter 3.

(1) If one teaches Torah at a grave for elevation of the deceased's soul — there is no lo'eig lerash, for he acts in his honor. See Tosafos Kamma 17b, s.v. sheheshivu yeshiva, etc.; Pischei Teshuvah siman 45, letter 4; Yoreh De'ah siman 367, se'if 3.
