Magen Avraham, se'if katan 1. Ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy — see there. Meaning: Levush there holds if one made the sukkah before removing the roof it is invalid; but if there was a gap when making the sukkah and later they closed it because of rain, it is valid when opened — and no ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy, since its beginning was in validity. The same for tzitzis that from their beginning were made in validity — no ta'aseh velo min ha'asuy.
