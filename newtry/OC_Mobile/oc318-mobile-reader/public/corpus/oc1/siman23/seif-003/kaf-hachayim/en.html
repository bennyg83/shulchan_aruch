(3) [Se'if 3] One who enters within four cubits of a corpse, etc. Even a small grave — lo'eig lerash applies, for perhaps it is the soul of a great person (Or Zarua, letter 3; Yad Aharon siman 45 in the name of Maharit Tzahalon; Pri Megadim, Orach Chayim, letter 2). He wrote: it implies a woman's grave — for in her lifetime she too was exempt — there is no lo'eig lerash; we are not concerned for gilgul into a male — so far. Pischei Olam, letter 4. However Mishneh LaMelech, Hilchot Evel chapter 3, wrote that regarding a woman too lo'eig lerash applies — see there; they cited Menachem siman 45, letter 3. See Pischei Teshuvah siman 45, letter 3: Maharit Tzahalon also wrote there is lo'eig lerash for a woman, but Or Zarua did not bring all his words, causing Pri Megadim to say the opposite of Maharit Tzahalon — see there.
