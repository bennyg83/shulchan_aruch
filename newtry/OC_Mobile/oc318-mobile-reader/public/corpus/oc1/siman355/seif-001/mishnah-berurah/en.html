(א) gezuztra — meaning a panel extending from the wall of the house that was adjacent to the sea or river, the panel above the water with a hole in its middle and a window of the house open to it; and when one fills water through the hole he carries from the water which is karmelit [as above siman 345 seif 14] to reshus hayachid — therefore one must make a partition ten tefachim high all around, for now everything opposite it until below is reshus hayachid; and even though the partition is suspended and does not reach the water it is lenient that they were lenient regarding water and we say extend the partition downward:
(ב) one may not draw from it — to bring into the house; and likewise to place on the gezuztra is also forbidden, for the gezuztra is forbidden, for the gezuztra is considered reshus hayachid since the window is open to it, as above siman 345 seif 16:
(ג) or one shall make, etc. — meaning one need not make specifically around the entire width of the gezuztra but it suffices when one makes partitions around the hole which is four by four tefachim wide, for any partition made for less than a four-wide space is not a partition:
(ד) below attached to it — even though still far from the water; and all the more if one makes the partition of ten tefachim below near the water it helps, for we say extend the partition upward to opposite the gezuztra:
(ה) on top of it — for here too we say extend the partition downward; and some Rishonim disagree and hold that specifically when one makes partitions below the gezuztra do we say extend the partition downward, but not when one makes on top of the gezuztra; and l'chatchila certainly one should be careful about this:
(ו) to pour from it — meaning through the hole onto the river and sea; for even though what is poured goes outside the partition of the gezuztra it is nevertheless permitted; and even if it is adjacent to a ditch that is reshus harabbim as explained siman 345 — nevertheless since his intent is not to take them out to reshus harabbim it is permitted; and see in Biur Halachah:
(ז) cannot draw — for the water is karmelit and the ship is reshus hayachid:
(ח) four by four — tefachim:
(ט) they were lenient on a ship — meaning that in the gezuztra mentioned earlier one specifically needs partitions ten tefachim and the leniency is we say extend the partition downward to the water; but on a ship they further lenient that partitions are not needed at all, but we say bend the sides as if there were partitions and afterward we say extend the partition downward to the water:
(י) when it is within ten tefachim — meaning the height of the side of the ship protruding above the water was less than ten tefachim, but the depth of the ship inside was ten tefachim high, for it is reshus hayachid; therefore one must make outside a four by four panel as stated [for if not ten deep also inside the ship it is karmelit and permitted to carry from it to the sea and water to it without any repair]:
(כ) ten high, etc. — meaning when he draws he must raise the water somewhat above the sides of the ship which are more than ten, which is a makom patur; and if so he carries from karmelit to reshus hayachid through a makom patur; therefore the Sages were lenient that it suffices to put out a ledge of any size outside the ship for mere recognition:
(ל) and makes a hole in it — and the Acharonim agreed that in the ledge one need not make a hole since it is only for recognition, and one may draw even not at the place of the ledge:
(מ) may pour, etc. — meaning he is not obligated to pour his waste [meaning rinse-water of cups and bowls and the like] within the four by four panel he made to draw water through it, but may pour on the sides, etc.; and in this there is no difference whether the side of the ship is ten tefachim above the water or not; and even if he had no special four by four panel and also did not make a ledge at all — nevertheless permitted to pour his waste directly on the side of the ship since there is only its force:
(נ) on the sides — for if he pours somewhat far from the side it is throwing from reshus hayachid to karmelit literally; but this is specifically when the side is not ten high above the water; if it is ten high and he does not want to pour on the side, e.g. they are foul and smell would enter the ship and he wants to pour far to the wall — permitted provided he at least makes a ledge for recognition:
(ס) to the sea — and the same that he may place bones and peels on the thickness of the top of the side and they fall to the sea [Acharonim]:
(ע) and see below — for there it is written that not in every karmelit is its force permitted — see there:
(פ) unless they are tied — for when they are not tied, even though they are adjacent, they are made to drift apart and karmelit separates between them and we are concerned lest it fall and come to carry from karmelit to reshus hayachid; for this reason even two ships belonging to one person are forbidden; and some permit if they belong to one person, for we are not concerned lest it fall, etc.; and only when they belong to two people are they forbidden when not tied, for since they are made to drift and separate an eruv does not help if they make an eruv between them. It is stated in the Gemara that tied counts even by a rope provided the rope is strong enough to hold them so they do not separate:
(צ) one to the other — and they must not be four tefachim apart:
(ק) ten high above the water — for then permitted even if not tied, because he carries from reshus hayachid to reshus hayachid through air of a makom patur; and even though extending is forbidden in one balcony even above ten as written end of siman 347 — here it deals with two balconies one opposite the other, for even below ten it is only a d'rabbanan prohibition and above ten fully permitted to extend; or it deals with throwing:
(ר) and they need to make an eruv — this applies also to the permission of ten high even when not tied; and in Sefer Beit Meir he disagrees on this, and his opinion is that when not tied, since they stand to separate, even if they are ten high, if they belong to two people they are forbidden to carry from one to the other and an eruv does not help:
