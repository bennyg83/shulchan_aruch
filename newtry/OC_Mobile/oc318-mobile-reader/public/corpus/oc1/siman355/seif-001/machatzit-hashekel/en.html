And see siman 393 seif 2: that when the window is open within three to the gezuztra it has the law of holes of reshus hayachid as explained above siman 345 seif 17 regarding a ledge; unlike when there is no window open, for then it is karmelit even if ten high and four wide — nevertheless since goats break through beneath it, it is like a reed planted with a basket on its head, for even on top it is not reshus hayachid, as Magen Avraham wrote siman 345; and although above the water this gezuztra has no breaking of goats but only breaking of fish — and breaking of fish is not called breaking, as stated in Shabbos daf 101a — that is specifically when he already made a partition for the gezuztra; but without a partition we do not say extend the partition downward even though there is only breaking of fish; and even according to what Shulchan Aruch ruled like the Rambam that on a ship we do not require a partition and a four-wide panel suffices — and it appears to deal even when a window is open to it from the ship which is reshus hayachid, holes of reshus hayachid — nevertheless they are lenient on a ship since one cannot do as one does in a house, as Shulchan Aruch wrote; and it is impossible that without a partition of ten, even for fish, we would not say extend the partition downward — how did they permit on a ship? One can say that by Torah law it is permitted to take out from karmelit to reshus hayachid, only d'rabbanan it is forbidden; therefore on a ship where one cannot make a greater repair they permitted with a bare panel even though there is breaking of fish (and see Tosafos Shabbos daf 101a s.v. and he threw, etc., as Maharsha wrote according to his opinion); and permitted to carry from karmelit to karmelit, so it should read.
