seif 1 — and the practical difference when within ten tefachim. And this is Rambam's words chapter 15 laws of Shabbos; and see Maggid Mishneh who holds for Rambam switching domains through a makom patur is forbidden even if the ship is ten high above the water — it needs a four-wide ledge; however Tur ruled that to switch through a makom patur is forbidden — nevertheless here he ruled like Rambam; it must be as Beit Yosef wrote: nevertheless since it is a permitted matter, permit permits; and I am very astonished how we find our hands and feet entangled in the Gemara's sugya 100b: according to R' Huna who said, etc. — nevertheless when switching through a makom patur it is permitted if the sides of the ship are not ten high — the ship itself is karmelit; and if ten high — what of it, for the water is not ten deep and he carries from karmelit — nevertheless when he raises the water to bring to the ship he passes them through above ten from the ground of the water which is a makom patur — for R' Huna who holds from the ground is washed; and if so when carrying to the ship he switches domains d'rabbanan through a makom patur — a ledge of any size helps all the more. Therefore it is proven like Maggid Mishneh's approach according to Rashba that even in such a case a four-wide ledge is needed; and for us it must be that Rav Huna's ledge of any size helps because a makom patur automatically helps a ledge of any size; but when filling from karmelit and carrying through a makom patur a ledge of any size does not help; and for this they properly challenge sometimes when there are not ten and he fills from karmelit and even though he carries through a makom patur nevertheless a ledge of any size does not help; but for Rambam and Tur requires further study: and behold not only in the reason of the matter do we require for R' Huna a ledge of any size for recognition — meaning not everyone knows the water is ten deep; if so certainly when surely there are not ten in the water a ledge of any size helps since he carries from karmelit to reshus hayachid through a makom patur; but also according to those who do not hold like the first explanation — it appears his words are torn from the baraita in the sugya R' Yehuda: deep ten and not ten high, etc.; and Rashi and Tosafos explained: if ten high — R' Yehuda holds permitted to switch d'rabbanan domains through a makom patur; and he holds even a ledge of any size is not needed; if so it is difficult for R' Huna who holds from the ground is washed — also when not ten high to permit without a ledge according to R' Yehuda, since the ship itself is ten high; if so what difference between the ship ten high above the water or not — if we permit when ten above water and recognition is not needed, for even per the world's mistake that water is not ten deep and they carry from karmelit — nevertheless there is no prohibition since he carries through a makom patur — automatically also when not ten above water it is so, for even per the world's mistake that water is not ten deep and they carry from karmelit — nevertheless he carries from karmelit through a makom patur since he passes them through above ten from the ground; and I do not know the difference between ten high and not high; therefore it must be not like the first explanation but recognition is because not everyone knows the law that from the ground is washed — therefore there is properly a difference: when ten high, even per the mistake that from water is washed, nevertheless he carries from karmelit to reshus hayachid through a makom patur; but when not ten high per the reason from water is washed they think he carries from karmelit to reshus hayachid not through a makom patur. And with this the difficulty I had is resolved in what they ruled in Shulchan Aruch's gloss: two boats one next to the other forbidden to carry, etc., unless tied or ten high above water — for this reasoning is hidden at first glance from R' Huna's words who holds from the ground is washed; if so when the Mishnah teaches two boats tied may carry from one to the other — if not tied, even though enclosed they may carry from one to the other — how do we find we require tied? For according to him when carrying from ship to ship through air of a makom patur he carries since it is above ten from the ground; and per the above it is resolved: nevertheless not everyone thinks from the ground is washed — and it is comparable to carrying through air of karmelit. (And in truth according to this it is difficult Maggid Mishneh's approach who holds for Rambam permitted to switch d'rabbanan domains through a makom patur — from where does Rambam learn that a ledge of any size is needed? For this cannot be implied from R' Huna — perhaps only in reshus harabbim recognition is needed because they do not know from the ground is washed and automatically it is not through a makom patur — perhaps a ledge of any size is not needed; and one can say Rambam learned thus from what he holds for the first Tanna and R' Yehuda that even ten high they do not carry — here R' Yehuda and the Rabbanan argue as Tosafos wrote; and Rambam holds nevertheless for the first Tanna a ledge of any size helps; nevertheless our difficulty above is not resolved thereby — this suffices to challenge R' Yehuda's words regarding no ledge of any size for recognition rising well — the above distinction: when ten high it is clear it is through a makom patur; and when not ten high they think from water is washed and there is no air of a makom patur; but in the Gemara's challenge to R' Huna: sometimes there are not ten — here it is difficult: since when there are ten in the water why does recognition of a ledge of any size help — behold even per the mistake that from water is washed it is carrying from karmelit where a ledge of any size does not help — nevertheless they were lenient in recognition all the more since in truth he carries from a makom patur; only there is concern for marit ayin — recognition helps; if so even when water is not ten deep — nevertheless according to truth he carries permissibly switching d'rabbanan domains through a makom patur — at least with recognition of a ledge of any size; and regarding concern of mistake recognition also helps as with ten deep; if so what difference ten deep or not — behold by law permitted to carry; and regarding mistake there is no difference ten or not — requires further study.
