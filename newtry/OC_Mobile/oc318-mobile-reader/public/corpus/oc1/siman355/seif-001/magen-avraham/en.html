(א) and a window open. For otherwise it is karmelit and permitted to carry — in siman 346 seif 2; and see siman 393 seif 2.

(ב) made for it a partition. Ten high — see siman 357.

(ג) around the hole. For we say extend the partition downward.

(ד) to pour. And even though it is adjacent to a ditch that is reshus harabbim, nevertheless since his intent is not to take it out to reshus harabbim it is permitted (this applies to the Mechaber's words below: ledge of any size and makes a hole in it, etc. — see Eliyah Rabbah and Turei Zahav); and makes a hole in it; and in Sefer Raavan in Eruvin he wrote that one draws even not opposite the hole — and so is primary.

(ה) on a ship. Because the water is karmelit and the ship is reshus hayachid.

(ו) within ten tefachim. Meaning above the water; but the partitions of the ship are ten high; and if the partitions of the ship are not ten high it is karmelit and permitted to carry from it to the sea and from the sea to it (Rashi) since it stands in karmelit — see siman 345 seif 6 and siman 266 seif 2 what is written.

(ז) air of makom patur. And even for those who forbid in siman 346 end of seif 1 — here since there is a ledge of any size there is recognition and it is permitted.

(ח) may pour. And the same to throw bones and peels (Raavan).

(ט) tied. For when they are not tied an eruv does not help, for karmelit separates if they drift; and since they are fit to drift it is forbidden; and even if they were severed on Shabbos we do not say since it was permitted it remains permitted, since karmelit separates (Maggid Mishneh chapter 3 and Tosafos Shabbos daf 101) — see siman 365; but at the end of chapter 1 of Eruvin Rosh wrote that when severed on Shabbos they are permitted — requires further study; and it is possible he holds enclosed means enclosed by a partition as Maggid Mishneh for Rambam — see siman 374.

(י) one to the other. And they must not be four tefachim apart (Yerushalmi).

(כ) high above the water. For he carries from reshus hayachid to reshus hayachid through a makom patur; and even though in reshus harabbim it is forbidden in one balcony as written end of siman 346 — in karmelit it is permitted; and see Shiltei Giborim, and it appears there that if they belong to two people and did not make an eruv it depends on the dispute written siman 346 end of seif 1; and so Beit Yosef.
