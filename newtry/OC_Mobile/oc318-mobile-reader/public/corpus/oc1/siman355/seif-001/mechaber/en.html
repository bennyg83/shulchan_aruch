The laws of gezuztra and bathroom. And in it are 5 seifim: A gezuztra (meaning a panel or a structure protruding from the house outward) that is above the water and a window open to it from the house — one may not draw water from it unless one made for it a partition all around, or one makes the partition around the hole through which one draws by way of there, and it must be four by four; and there is no difference whether one makes it below attached to it or whether one makes it on top of it; and since one made a partition, they are permitted also to pour from it; and similarly one traveling on a ship cannot draw unless he makes a panel four by four, makes a hole in it, and draws by way of there; and he need not make partitions for it, but we say bend the sides and extend the partition downward — they were lenient on a ship because one cannot do there what one does in a house; and this matter is when it is within 10 tefachim; but if the sides of the ship are ten high above the water, he puts out a ledge of any size, makes a hole in it, and draws by way of there, for behold he draws through the air
of a makom patur, and a ledge suffices for recognition; and one may pour his water onto the sides of the ship and they descend to the sea, for since he did not throw them directly to the sea but they come by their own force — its force in karmelit they did not decree.(And see below at the end of siman 357.
