(א) gezuztra. See Eruvin chapter 7 seif 2; and indeed in the Mishnah it is not there and the window of the house is open to it; and Rambam in explanation of the Mishnah and Hagahot chapter 15 — another explanation: a narrow panel with a hole from which they draw from a spring or sea which are karmelit — one may not draw from sea and spring to the gezuztra itself unless they made a partition for it — meaning the gezuztra itself is at least reshus hayachid, as Tosafos Eruvin chapter 6 seif 2 s.v. gezuztra and chapter 9 seif 1 s.v. in partitions wrote that breaking of fish is not called breaking as Shabbos 101a seif 1 — and the panel is reshus hayachid whenever it has four by four width; and perforce this panel is ten high on the wall, for otherwise it is karmelit if within ten of the sea — from water is washed; if so it is difficult — behold on a ship in such a case Rambam wrote there halachah 22 that above ten it is through a makom patur — unless we say on a ship they were lenient as they were lenient there that partitions are not needed — strained; but Taz explained that the window of the house is open to the gezuztra and it is holes of reshus hayachid, as Tosafos Eruvin chapter 6 seif 2 s.v. gezuztra in the second version and so daf 89 seif 2 s.v. in partitions — see there; and what is written: a ledge protruding to reshus harabbim within ten — meaning within ten in the air of reshus harabbim — adjacent to the window of the house within three is required, as Magen Avraham siman 353 note 4 — within three is required to be holes of reshus hayachid; and so Magen Avraham in siman 345 note 2 — even within ten holes of reshus hayachid are like reshus hayachid; and automatically here in gezuztra above ten no partition is needed, for it is through a makom patur like on a ship; and requires further study in all this; and in Levush he wrote one may not draw from it through the window — implying water to the gezuztra one may draw; and why — behold they are holes of reshus hayachid; and it appears he explains the window is far three and the gezuztra is karmelit and one draws water to it only not through the window as stated; and Tosafos Shabbos 101 regarding breaking of fish — breaking is not called breaking.

(ב) And according to my common understanding it appears the Mechaber intended here a special intent, for in the Mishnah there between whether made from above or below — and Rashi explained above on the gezuztra and below beneath it adjacent to it — this is preferable and all the more the below; and above on it must say extend; and the partitions are not visible below, as Tosafos s.v. whether, etc., and whether between — to teach there is no plainness in the first version this and all the more that; and Rambam's explanation from above beneath the narrow panel and below in the water also is so, for extend upward is certainly said and in water even extend downward is said — they leniently lenient in water; or breaking of fish does not nullify — nevertheless partitions are needed and a panel alone does not help — understand. But Taz explained the window of the house is not adjacent within three but more; and the gezuztra itself is karmelit; and even above ten it is karmelit like a protruding roof siman 345 seif 16, as Rosh and Tosafos Eruvin chapter 9 seif 2 s.v. in partitions — as karmelit; but above ten whatever has four by four (and air of karmelit is not above ten); and if so water to gezuztra certainly one draws from karmelit to karmelit as siman 346 seif 2 — only to permit filling water to the window of the house through the gezuztra a partition is needed; therefore above a partition is plain from gezuztra to window of the house permitted; even below beneath it and a panel protruding from all sides — like a protruding roof where partitions are not visible — it is karmelit; here it teaches even if one makes partitions around the panel and the panel protrudes more — nevertheless permitted here; and we say from reshus hayachid to reshus hayachid through karmelit is permitted — all that has no resting in karmelit as siman 334 note 2 in Taz as Peri Megadim wrote there — specifically reshus harabbim not karmelit; and so the Mechaber wrote inversely that there should be whether whether there is no plainness in the first version; and Levush's words are clear as snow in what he wrote one may not draw through the window — that the gezuztra itself one may draw in every case for it is also karmelit; Peri Megadim note 1; and if so there will be explained further; and see Sema siman 72 notes 7 and 8 that the Mechaber certainly was precise to take whether whether there is no plainness in the first version this and all the more that; and see what he wrote in small general rules at the head of the book — see there; and all this for drawing from karmelit — from a pit ten deep which is reshus hayachid as siman 345 Magen Avraham note 10 — one law for them — understand this.
