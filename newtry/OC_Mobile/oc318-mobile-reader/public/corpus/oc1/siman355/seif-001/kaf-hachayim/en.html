(1) [seif 1] gezuztra, etc. Meaning a panel extending from the wall above the water with a hole in its middle and a window open to it from the house; and when one draws water through the hole he carries from the water which is karmelit to reshus hayachid — therefore one must make a partition all around, for now everything opposite it until below is reshus hayachid, for we say extend the partition downward — Taz s.k. 1.
