This means from the water, which is a karmelis, to the balcony, which is also a karmelis because it does not open onto a window, as stated above in siman 346, se'if 2. See there. It is, however, forbidden to carry from the house to the balcony or to the water, for that would constitute carrying from a private domain to a karmelis. Tosefes Shabbos, subsection 1, writes that even if the balcony is more than ten handbreadths high, it is a karmelis, and it is permitted to carry from the water to it. See there. Machatzis HaShekel writes likewise. However, we already wrote above in siman 345, subsection 93, in the name of several authorities, that although it is four handbreadths wide, since it is more than ten handbreadths high it is an exempt area. See there. According to their opinion, it is permitted to carry from it to the water or to the house, and one may rely on this in pressing circumstances.
