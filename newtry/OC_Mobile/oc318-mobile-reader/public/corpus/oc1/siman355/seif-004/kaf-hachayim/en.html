And a window open to it, etc. — for if not it is karmelit and permitted to carry from karmelit to karmelit — Magen Avraham s.k. 1; meaning from the water which is karmelit to the gezuztra which is also karmelit since it is not open to the window, as above siman 346 seif 2 — see there; but from the house to the gezuztra or to the water is forbidden, for it is carrying from reshus hayachid to karmelit. And Turei Zahav wrote note 1 that even if the gezuztra is above ten it is karmelit and permitted to carry to the water — see there; and so Machatzit HaShekel. However, we already wrote above siman 345 note 93 in the name of several poskim that although it is four wide, since it is ten high it is a makom patur — see there; and if so according to their words it is permitted to carry from it to the water or to the house, and one may rely on this in a time of need.
