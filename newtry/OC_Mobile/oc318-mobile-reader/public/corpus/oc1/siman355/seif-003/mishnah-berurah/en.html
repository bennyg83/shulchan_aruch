(א) which is karmelit — meaning that even though the ditch is ten deep, since it holds more than two se'ah and was not enclosed for dwelling, the law of karmelit applies — he takes out from reshus hayachid to karmelit:
(ב) it is not permitted, etc. — rather we require the partitions reach the ground:
(ג) but by a panel — or reed, or the excrement falls on the sides of the wall and afterward rolls and falls in the ditch, as above:
(ד) above ten — from the ground of the ditch; and the reason is air above ten in karmelit is a makom patur; when he relieves himself he takes out from reshus hayachid to karmelit through a makom patur:
(ה) and see above siman 346, etc. — meaning at the end of seif 1 there are those who forbid taking out from reshus hayachid to karmelit even through a makom patur; and see in Biur HaGra who expands on this, and his opinion is that even if we say in d'rabbanan domains through a makom patur is permitted — that is specifically when resting in the middle on the makom patur; but not when not resting; and similarly Sefer Maamar Mordechai siman 353 — his opinion is also like the Gra; and he wonders at the first "some say":
(ו) excrement from erev Shabbos — and it deals when he relieves himself directly onto the excrement; and even though it afterward falls from the side which is karmelit it is permitted:
(ז) the excrement is a makom patur — for presumably it is not four wide; and although generally we rule less than three height is nullified to the domain in which it stands — and excrement presumably is not three high — here it is different, for excrement in the bathroom certainly the masses do not tread on it; therefore it is not nullified to the domain at its side and is a makom patur:
(ח) and according to what, etc. — here the Rama begins to explain another law about a bathroom that belonged in the ditch area below to two people who did not make an eruv — he says that here the permission written initially that excrement is a makom patur does not apply, since the domains themselves are reshus hayachid — there is no makom patur in reshus hayachid:
(ט) forbidden to relieve oneself there — it deals when the seat-place of the bathroom belonged to each alone, for a partition separated between them, except the ditch beneath belonged to both; therefore forbidden, for he takes out from his domain to partners' ground; if the seat-place also belonged to the partners there is no taking out from domain to domain and it is permitted [Beit Meir and Chiddushei R' Akiva Eiger — see there]:
(י) as was explained seif 2 — meaning repair of a panel or reed; but repair of a suspended partition does not help here until it descends below:
(כ) as below siman 357 — seif 3 in the gloss explains that in an uncombined courtyard according to all its force is permitted:
(ל) that they forgot and did not make an eruv — or he had no possibility to make the repair mentioned [Chayei Adam]:
(מ) for the importance of human dignity — for the Sages permitted where there is no Torah prohibition:
(נ) from the paddle wheel to the sea — but from the ship to the sea [without the repair mentioned in seif 2] not, for he takes out from reshus hayachid to the sea which is karmelit; and if he cannot except for some reason unless he relieves himself on the ship it is permitted, for the importance of human dignity [Acharonim]:
