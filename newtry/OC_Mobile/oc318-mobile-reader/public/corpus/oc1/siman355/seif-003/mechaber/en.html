If the bathroom protrudes outside the wall of the city and excrement falls into the ditch around the city that holds more than two se'ah, which is karmelit — it is not permitted by means of a suspended partition, for they permitted that only regarding water; but by means of a panel it is permitted, for its force is permitted in every karmelit.And some say that if the bathroom is above ten it is permitted in every case, for he takes out from reshus hayachid to karmelit through a makom patur; and see above siman 346 that some disagree. And some say that if there was excrement there from erev Shabbos it is permitted in every case, for the excrement is a makom patur for the masses do not walk on it (Mordechai, chapter HaZorek). And see above siman 345 that some disagree and hold that there is no makom patur in karmelit. And according to what was explained above in siman 345 that there is no makom patur in reshus hayachid according to all — a bathroom standing between two houses is forbidden to use there if they did not make an eruv together and did not make for it a repair from erev Shabbos, as was explained in seif 2 — then it is permitted, for it is only like a courtyard that is not combined; as below siman 357, and in siman 376 seif 4 (Rama's own opinion). And all this is l'chatchila; but b'dieved if they forgot and did not make an eruv it is permitted because of the importance of human dignity (Hagahot Mordechai and his own opinion). Those who travel on ships are accustomed to relieve themselves from the paddle wheel to the sea, for since the paddle wheel is not ten high, even though it is four wide, it is only karmelit and he takes out from karmelit to karmelit (Beit Yosef in the name of Orchot Chayim).
