Magen Avraham s.k. 7 — since there is a ledge of any size there is recognition and it is permitted. And I wonder — what does the sugya challenge R' Huna: sometimes there are not ten and he carries from karmelit to reshus hayachid — for R' Huna who holds from the ground is washed automatically it is through a makom patur and a ledge of any size helps like for us when the sides of the ship are ten above water — and more than that, for always it is a makom patur since from the ground is washed; and requires further study; and similarly I question Rama's ruling that two ships ten above water even when not tied require eruv together — for this reasoning is hidden from R' Huna from the ground is washed and always the sides of ships are ten high — if so how do we find what is taught: if severed they become forbidden — requires further study.
