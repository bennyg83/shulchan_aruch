And a window open to it from the house — for through the window open to it from the house the gezuztra becomes reshus hayachid like the house, as written above siman 345 seif 16; and if so, when one draws from the sea or river which is karmelit and raises to the gezuztra it is taking out from karmelit to reshus hayachid and forbidden unless one made a partition, for through the partition it becomes reshus hayachid until the water and it is taking out from reshus hayachid to reshus hayachid and permitted; and an eruv is not needed since there are no residents, as above siman 354 note 14.
