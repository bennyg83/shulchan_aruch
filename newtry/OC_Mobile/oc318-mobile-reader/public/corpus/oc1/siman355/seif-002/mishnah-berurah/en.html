(א) on the face of the water — meaning adjacent to the sea or river:

(ב) by a suspended partition — ten tefachim high even though it does not reach the water, as above:

(ג) made to permit — to relieve oneself there on Shabbos, such as he makes it below around near the water; what is made above for privacy is ineffective:

(ד) a panel or reed less than three — meaning less than three adjacent to the hole, for then it is lavud and holes of reshus hayachid which is reshus hayachid to reshus hayachid; and even though it falls from there afterward below which is karmelit — its force in karmelit they did not decree:

(ה) the panel broke on Shabbos, etc. — meaning it broke on Shabbos itself so it cannot be repaired; therefore permitted to relieve oneself there for that Shabbos:
