A bathroom on the face of the water is permitted by means of a suspended partition, provided that it is made to permit; but one made for privacy of the bathroom alone does not help; and if one made a panel or a reed less than three such that the excrement falls onto it before falling into the water, partitions are not needed, for it is its force in karmelit and it is permitted; and the same applies if the bathroom is made such that the excrement falls on the sides of the wall and afterward rolls and falls below in karmelit; and if the panel broke on Shabbos it is permitted because of the importance of human dignity.
