2) There. And a window of the house opens onto it. Through the window of the house that opens onto it, the balcony becomes a private domain like the house, as stated above in siman 345, se'if 16. Consequently, when one draws water from the sea or river, which is a karmelis, and raises it onto the balcony, one transfers from a karmelis into a private domain, which is forbidden. This is permitted only if one made a partition, for through the partition the area down to the water becomes a private domain, and one is then transferring from one private domain to another, which is permitted. An eruv is unnecessary because there are no residents there, as stated above in siman 354, subsection 14.
