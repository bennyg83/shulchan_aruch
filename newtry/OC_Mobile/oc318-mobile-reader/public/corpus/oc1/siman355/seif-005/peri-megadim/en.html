General intent of our master from what Maggid Mishneh wrote chapter 15 laws of Shabbos halachah 22 in the name of Rav Hai Gaon that drawing through the ledge from there — one cannot learn at all; for in a ledge all the more too a hole is needed in the ledge for recognition; and all whose sides of the ship are above ten no hole is needed like Rav Huna Shabbos 100b seif 2 — see there; and Rav Hai Gaon — wide four refers (and below ten or according to us even above ten as we must say below in Magen Avraham note 7 — if so) — there a hole of something is needed in the panel in its middle and we say bend the sides — two leniencies on a ship; and there one must draw specifically through the hole as if within the sides and reshus hayachid is the sea, etc.; but above ten permitted for Rambam and the ledge is only for recognition — no hole needed at all and one draws on the ledge; however Maggid Mishneh in his own reasoning wrote that since Rambam did not mention at least making a hole within the panel something — he answered that since it is written at the end: for behold through a makom patur he draws and Rambam did not write: for behold through air, etc. — learn he comes to teach through a makom patur within the hole of the ledge, etc.; and automatically at the beginning in four by four too a hole is needed; and now whether four by four and whether a ledge all the more a hole is needed; and Taz challenged this that this is not Rambam's intent in what he wrote for behold through a makom patur — rather air of a makom patur and there is no hole in a ledge all the more; and similarly at the beginning in four by four he omitted that there should be a hole in its middle and certainly we say at the end; and what he wrote for Bach that it did not accord with Maggid Mishneh's opinion somewhat scribal error in printing and his intent that it did not accord with Maggid Mishneh in the name of Rav Hai Gaon; but Maggid Mishneh's own reasoning certainly thus — for Rambam in a ledge all the more needs a hole in its middle; and so our Rabbi explained. And know further for Rambam there is no makom patur in karmelit, as we say found its kind and shake as siman 345 seif 19 in the gloss; and Taz properly challenged him: this hole in a ledge all the more — what does it help; if we say bend and holes of reshus hayachid like reshus hayachid and extend to water — if so how did he say through a makom patur and it is from reshus hayachid to reshus hayachid; and if we do not join it to the ship it is air of karmelit even though it has no four by four — nevertheless the side of the ship is ten high in summary — air of karmelit until end of ten is all found its kind; only above ten when he raises the bucket it is a makom patur — meaning air of a makom patur not through a makom patur — this is Taz's intent; and if so in Magen Avraham it will be explained further.
