(א) if there were two, etc. — I will explain briefly: a house and upper story above standing by the river, each having a gezuztra protruding to draw water from the river; if each made partitions around as explained in seif 1 both were permitted on Shabbos each to draw in his own even without eruv; if they repaired in partnership only one gezuztra as explained — whether for the upper or lower — they are considered like two people dwelling in one courtyard and both are forbidden to draw through that gezuztra since both domains control it unless they made an eruv; when the two gezuztras stand one above the other not four tefachim apart in wall thickness so use is comfortable; but if four apart the lower cannot use the upper except by throwing the bucket in air four tefachim — such extreme use is not considered to forbid one's fellow even without eruv:
(ב) offset a bit, etc. — meaning even offset; and all the more if standing one opposite the other evenly:
(ג) and it is not distant, etc. — for at four distant in wall thickness they no longer forbid each other, for she cannot use except by throwing the bucket in air from afar which is not considered use to forbid:
(ד) more than the height, etc. — for regarding height we are not strict even though thereby forced to use the bucket by throwing upward which is difficult use, since he does not use through air in width his use is considered to forbid:
(ה) and all the more if both made for the lower — for this use is easier for the upper since it is by descent:
(ו) the other does not forbid her — meaning even if accustomed to use her fellow's on weekdays she does not forbid her, for essentially the domain is not hers unless they explicitly agreed each uses the other's — then they need eruv [Peri Megadim]:
(ז) even if the other did not make, etc. — and all the more if each made for herself — each permitted to use her gezuztra without eruv; but some say all this is specifically when the gezuztras are offset and they need not use one in the other's; but if the upper is directly opposite the lower and when she needs to draw water her bucket descends through the hole of the lower gezuztra and there is not ten tefachim air between both heights — both forbidden to draw [Rambam]:
(ח) if they made in both, etc. — meaning that although above we said if they repaired for one in partnership both forbidden to draw there without eruv — that is specifically if they repaired only one and the second made no partition at all; but if they made repair in both in partnership each is permitted in her own since each removed herself from her fellow once she made repair for herself [and what they made in partnership — whatever reason they had]; and all the more if they made only in one in partnership and the second made a partition for her gezuztra alone — certainly each permitted to draw without eruv, for in this certainly she removed herself from her fellow:
