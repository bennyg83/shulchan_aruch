And a window open to it, etc. — and if there is no window open to it and also the width of the gezuztra is not four by four, it is a makom patur, as above siman 345 seifim 11 and 19, and permitted to carry from it to the water or to the house. And if it is permitted to carry the water from the sea to the gezuztra which is a makom patur and afterward bring it into the house — see above siman 346 end of seif 1 in Shulchan Aruch where a dispute is brought; and we wrote there in note 15 that if it rested in a makom patur and afterward one carries it to another domain one may be lenient — see there. And all this if the gezuztra is above ten; but below ten see above siman 345 seif 19 in the gloss that if it is in karmelit its law is like karmelit; and we wrote there note 22 that one should be stringent although there is a dispute — see there; and see there note 120 — examine.
