If there are two balconies, one above the other, [or even] a bit offset but not four [horizontal] tefachim away from each other, whether they have ten [vertical] tefachim between them or more, if they jointly made walls for the top one [in order to be allowed to draw water from below the balcony], it is forbidden for either of them [to draw water] until they make an eruv. This is even more true if they jointly made walls for the bottom one. However, if one made walls for his balcony independently, the other does not render it forbidden, even if it doesn't have walls. Similarly, if they both jointly made walls for both, one balcony's existence does not render the other forbidden for use.
