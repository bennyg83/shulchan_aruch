If there were two gezuztras one above the other, offset a bit from one another but not four tefachim distant from opposite it — whether they stand within ten of each other or there is more than the height of ten amos between them — if they made for the upper one a partition in partnership from both of them and did not make for the lower one at all, both are forbidden regarding it until they make an eruv; and all the more so if they made for both for the lower one, both are forbidden until they make an eruv; but each one that made alone in its own does not cause the other to forbid it, even if the other did not make in its own; and similarly if they made in both in partnership from both of them, one does not forbid its fellow.
