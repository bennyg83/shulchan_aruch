And a window opens onto it, etc. If no window opens onto the balcony and the balcony is also not four by four handbreadths wide, it is an exempt area, as stated above in siman 345, se'ifim 11 and 19, and it is permitted to carry from it to the water or to the house. Regarding whether it is permitted to carry water from the sea to the balcony, which is an exempt area, and then bring it into the house, see above in siman 346, at the end of se'if 1. The Shulchan Aruch cites a dispute in this regard, and we wrote there in subsection 17 that if the object comes to rest in the exempt area and one subsequently carries it into another domain, one may be lenient. See there. All this applies when the balcony is more than ten handbreadths high. If it is less than ten handbreadths high, see above in siman 345, se'if 19, in the gloss, where it is stated that if it is in a karmelis, its status is that of a karmelis. We wrote there in subsection 31 that one should rule stringently in this manner, notwithstanding the dispute in this regard. See there. See also Olas Shabbos, subsection 120, and examine the matter carefully.
