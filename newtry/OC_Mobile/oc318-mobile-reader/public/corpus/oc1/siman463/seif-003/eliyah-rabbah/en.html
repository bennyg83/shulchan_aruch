[3] [Levush] made from roasted grains, etc.—even the flour itself dried. Beit Yosef siman 461 cites Semag, Hagahot Maimoniyot, R' Yitzchak forbidding; Agudah and Kol Bo too. If placed b'dieved in food Magen Avraham wrote not to forbid, for Rashi and Rambam permit l'chatchila—end. Requires study: Riaz in Shiltot wrote from this one permits even roasted grain flour; do not challenge from 39a forbidding Mar Zutra, for 40a Rav Papi and Rava permit, and Rashi's two explanations imply one case—so Semak.
