(s.k. 3) This too, etc.—for roasting sheaves, etc.—even though in sheaves they only permitted from fruit juice:

Not to make the mixture. Meaning not to mix fruit juice with flour because we are not concerned, etc., that Rava who permits and vitika holds fruit juice leavens; nevertheless permitted since not only hardened. If it were full chametz with karet one should be concerned though uncommon; but since only hardened rabbinically, therefore no concern for uncommon moisture—especially for us who hold hardened is d'rabbanan as siman 442:
