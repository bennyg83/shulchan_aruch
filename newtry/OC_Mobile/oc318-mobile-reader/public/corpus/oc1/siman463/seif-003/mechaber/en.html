Karmel that they flash in fire and grind—one may not cook its flour in water, lest it was not roasted well in fire and will become leavened when cooked. And likewise when they crush new pots (meaning they put flour into the food to thicken), one may not cook in them, but only baked matzah that they' returned and ground.
