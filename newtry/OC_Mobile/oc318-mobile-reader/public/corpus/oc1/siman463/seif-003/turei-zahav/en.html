But only baked matzah. Tur wrote and in Gemara Rava said even baked matzah forbidden where workers common, etc.; Beit Yosef wrote no question, for Gemara has two versions and Rif and Rosh ruled like latter version; certainly Tur did not mean to challenge one version against one who rules other; rather his intent: since first version Rava said improper to permit where workers common i.e. Exilarch's house lest confusion with flour, and second version Rava himself did so where workers uncommon—if so why say Rava does not distinguish whether workers common—no legal difference, only whose action; wherever not evident to make dispute, certainly better to say so. And explain regarding lentils and legumes—for truly flour and more similar to five grains than baked matzah; Tur says even baked matzah stricter where workers common; though dispute on this, regarding lentil flour certainly concern, and we rule all legume and lentil flours forbidden—even whole forbidden lest confused with grain as 453—how much more here—as I see it:
