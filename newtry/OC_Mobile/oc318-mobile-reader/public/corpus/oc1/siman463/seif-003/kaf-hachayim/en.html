(3) [Seif 2] Made from oil and salt, etc.—we do not forbid for salt being like water mixed with oil since minimal and form changed without power with oil; but real water we concern in any amount; possibly also much salt we concern. Ran ch. 21. Beit Yosef 462. Taz this siman s.k. 1. Magen Avraham s.k. 1. Chafetz Chayim (2) R' Ovadiah (2) Chok Yosef (2) R' Zalman (4). From Ran's words that salt is like water—even in salt one cooks water, its law is like water as above 462 (75)—still permitted as minimal; so R' Zalman. However Maharitva on Pesachim wrote: vitika permitted established with oil and salt, fruit juice does not leaven—one view: mountain salt; salt from water is like water forbidden; another view even mountain salt dissolves, but here minimal for flavor no concern; others: every salt case; here flour and oil cooked first then salt—like baked, no leaven—correct to Maharitva—end; Mishna Berurah in Biur Halacha brought; for practical law good l'chatchila follow Maharitva—add salt after food cooked:
