Well. Specifically in karmel the Rambam is concerned about this; but regarding roasted flour the Rambam ruled in ch. 5 that it is permitted to cook l'chatchila. However the Mechaber concluded here only baked matzah, etc., implying he holds roasted flour is forbidden in every case; see Magen Avraham who wrote: it requires study whether if placed b'dieved in food they forbid because of this concern, for Rashi permits l'chatchila; and there is a difference regarding matzah kneaded with such flour and baked, as in siman 459, then placed in sauce—nevertheless it appears one should be stringent with matzah since matzah separates between flour and fire—end of his words. Chafetz Chayim wrote that in roasted karmel one should forbid even b'dieved; but in roasted flour, since per the Rambam it is permitted even l'chatchila, one may rely b'dieved to permit benefit or leave until after Passover—so it appears.
