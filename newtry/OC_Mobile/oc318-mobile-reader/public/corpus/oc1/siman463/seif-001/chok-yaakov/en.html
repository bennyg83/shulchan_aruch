It is permitted to parch two ears [of grain] together. And Beis Yosef wrote, and these are his words: It is a dispute of Abaye and Rava in the chapter Kol Sha'ah, and we hold like Rava who permits; end quote. And so wrote the Rif and the Rosh. But it is somewhat difficult, for one who examines there in the sugya on folio 40a concludes that Abaye retracted and agrees with Rava that in such a case it is permitted, for the moisture drains away in such a case and it does not become chametz; and they disagree only regarding roasted grain that dried out inside the pot, and we hold like Rava who permits even in such a case. If so, the posekim ought to have written this novel ruling that in such a case it is permitted; and in truth Halakhot Gedolot cites only the latter dispute. And this requires further study.
