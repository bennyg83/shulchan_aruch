However Magen Avraham there letter (6) distinguishes fermenting and later fermenting—so Peri Chadash. For practical law do not be lenient to eat except minimal water nullified in sixty and later fermentation like that case 462 (3); possibly even without long time permitted if not changed—even minimal water and long time do not be lenient unless great need—one may argue.
