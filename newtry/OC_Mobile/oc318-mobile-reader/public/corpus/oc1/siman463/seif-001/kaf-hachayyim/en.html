1) [Se'if 1] It is permitted to singe two ears of grain, etc. This is permitted even according to the custom to be stringent about kneading and smoothing dough with fruit juice, as stated above in the gloss to siman 462, se'if 4, and below in the gloss to se'if 2. See below, subsection 7.
2) There. It is permitted to singe two ears of grain, etc. One may likewise fill a pot with ears of grain and dry them in an oven. We are not concerned that the heat of the fire might draw liquid from one ear, which could fall upon another and cause it to become chametz, because this liquid is classified as fruit juice and does not cause leavening. The Gemara; Eliyah Rabbah, subsection 1; Shulchan Aruch HaRav, subsection 1; Mishnah Berurah, subsection 1. Regarding whether this is also permitted in a place whose custom is to be stringent about fruit juice, Eliyah Rabbah and Shulchan Aruch HaRav, there, and Chok Yaakov, subsection 3, permit it. Only Eshel Avraham prohibits it, as stated below in subsection 7. See there.
