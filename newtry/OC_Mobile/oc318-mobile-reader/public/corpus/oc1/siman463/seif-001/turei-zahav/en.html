From oil and salt. The Ran writes: We do not forbid it on account of the water mixed with the oil, because it is a small amount, its form has changed, and it has no potency together with the oil. With actual water, however, we are concerned even for the smallest amount. It is also possible that if there is a large quantity of salt water, we are concerned. End quote. The reason we are concerned for actual water and do not say that it is nullified in the oil, as we say regarding wine in siman 462, seif 3, is that there it was nullified before Pesach, whereas here it was made on Pesach.
