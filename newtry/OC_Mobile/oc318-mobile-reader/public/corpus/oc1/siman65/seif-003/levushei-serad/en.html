There — regarding one who needs to relieve himself. Raavad is more lenient: even if the person was not fit at the time of the pause he need not return to the beginning; and "water dripping on his knees" — all that he prayed before the pause is also invalid, since he was (reciting Keriat Shema on the road of his walking and came to filthy alleys — see there) — he needed to relieve himself at the time he prayed; and we hold: one who needs to relieve himself may not pray.
