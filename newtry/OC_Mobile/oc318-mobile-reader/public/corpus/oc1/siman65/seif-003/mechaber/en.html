If one recited the Recitation of the Sh'ma [on one's own] and entered the synagogue and found the congregation reciting the Recitation of the Sh'ma, it is best that one should recite the entire Recitation of the Sh'ma along with them, and they will receive a reward like [when one] reads the Torah. {Rama: Rem"a: But one is not obligated [to do so] except for the first verse, as was explained. [Beit Yosef]}
