[3] For example, from Barukh she-amar, etc. — and study is needed: behold, Beit Yosef brought this from Terumat HaDeshen siman 63, and there it implies that specifically in Keriat Shema and its blessings one may not interrupt, but from Barukh she-amar until Yishtabach one may interrupt; and thus it appears in Lechem Chamudot page 20. And Beit Chadash is more lenient and disagrees entirely with Terumat HaDeshen and concludes that even in the blessings before Keriat Shema, even in the middle of a blessing, one is obligated to interrupt and recite a verse; and thus I practice — until here. And Taz disagrees with him regarding the blessings of Keriat Shema — see there; and thus it appears to me for practical law.
