(3) There — gloss: and there are those who say that if he was under compulsion and paused the time to finish it all he returns to the beginning, and such is the custom; and thus ruled Levush, Beit Chadash, and Magen Avraham note 2, and Razah, as our teacher the Rema wrote. But Pri Chadash raised, as Maran wrote, that even if the pause was because of compulsion he does not return to the beginning — see there; and thus too Eliyahu Rabbah letter 1 — see there; Siddur Beit Yosef letter 4; and thus also ruled Maran below siman 78 — see there. And such is the essential law; and what our teacher the Rema wrote, "and such is the custom," refers to the Ashkenazic custom, and it is simple. Regarding prayer it will be explained in its place, siman 104 seif 5, with God's help.
