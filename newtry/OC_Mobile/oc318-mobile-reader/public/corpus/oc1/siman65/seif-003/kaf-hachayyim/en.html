And some say if he was coerced and interrupted long enough to complete the entire thing he returns to the beginning, and such is the custom; and so ruled Levush and Bach and Magen Avraham comment 2 and Turei Zahav according to our teacher's words. But Peri Chadash raised according to our teacher's words that even if the interruption was due to coercion he does not return to the beginning — see there; and so Erech Lechem letter alef — see there; Siddur Beit Yosef letter dalet; and so also ruled our teacher below siman 78 — see there. And so is primary; and what our teacher wrote "and such is the custom" — this is for the Ashkenazic custom, and it is simple. And regarding prayer it will be explained in its place siman 104 seif 5 with God's help.
