Seif 3: It is preferable, etc.; but, etc. Rokeach writes that he should recite all of Keriat Shema. Beit Yosef explains that this is only the preferable course, as Behag writes. It appears more likely, however, that Rokeach holds that one must recite it all, for in the Mishnah they said that he contemplates in his heart the entirety of Keriat Shema. Although the Gemara says, 'Prayer is different, for it does not, etc.,' they nevertheless required him to contemplate all of Keriat Shema. Furthermore, in the conclusion it answers, 'Rather, Keriat Shema and Birkat HaMazon, etc.' The words of Behag require examination.
