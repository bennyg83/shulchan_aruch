Seif 3 — it is good, etc., but, etc. — Rokeach wrote that he should recite all of Keriat Shema; and Beit Yosef explained: that is, in the manner of what is better, as written in Beit Yosef (Geonim). And it appears more that he holds one must recite all of it, for in the Mishnah they said one contemplates in one's heart over all of Keriat Shema; and if in the Gemara they said prayer is different because it lacks, etc., nevertheless they required contemplation over all of Keriat Shema. And further, in the conclusion they answer "only Keriat Shema," and in Birkat hamazon, etc.; and the words in Beit Yosef (Geonim) require study.
