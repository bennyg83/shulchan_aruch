Shulchan Aruch seif 1 — due to coercion. This is Rif's view that there is no distinction between coercion or not; rather there is a distinction between prayer and Keriat Shema, Hallel, Megillah, and tekios — in prayer he returns to the beginning and not in all the others. And Tosafot and Rosh whom Rema brings hold prayer and all others are equal: if he waited long enough to finish the entire thing, the same law in Tosafot Berakhot 13 and this is their language: Rabbeinu Tam would wait during "oseh pelle" in order to answer Kaddish, and he would hold that even if he waited to finish the entire thing he does not return to the beginning, end quote — it seems he means Rabbeinu Tam holds like the first view here; but according to the second view he returns to the beginning; and this is not like Magen Avraham. And at first glance one may say Magen Avraham does not explain Tosafot's words as I explained because he follows his reasoning in siman 53 comment 5: the dispute here whether he returns to the beginning is only in Keriat Shema; but in the blessing of Keriat Shema all agree he does not return; if so what did they write "and he would hold," etc. — therefore to explain Magen Avraham: Tosafot speak lekhatchilah — Rabbeinu Tam would wait for Kaddish and would hold it is permitted to wait lekhatchilah even to finish the entire thing; but what they concluded in Tosafot "he does not return to the beginning" does not mean so. And further Magen Avraham in siman 53 speaks only that in rabbinic matters we are not stringent like R' Yochanan and Rosh as Darkei Moshe wrote siman 422 brought in Magen Avraham there comment 5; but Tosafot and Rosh themselves are stringent even in rabbinic matters — requires further study. Nevertheless for the law he wrote well: because one who waited for Kaddish is not considered coercion for all Magen Avraham's rulings comment 2 — examine.
