Seif 1 — one who recited it out of order, etc., and interrupted, etc. — note: behold, regarding the law whether it is permitted to interrupt in the middle of the blessings of Keriat Shema to say the thirteen attributes with the congregation, see what is written on this in the omissions to the composition Mei Niddah, Laws of Niddah siman 199, to Rabbi Kalonymus of Regensburg; it is still in manuscript, page 120, where he wrote incidentally on this that it is forbidden to interrupt — see there and examine well.
