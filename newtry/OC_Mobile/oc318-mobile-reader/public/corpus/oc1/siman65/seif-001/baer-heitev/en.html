Tarried — ab initio it is forbidden to interrupt in order to tarry, even in the blessings of Keriat Shema; see above siman 53. However, to answer Kaddish and Kedushah it is permitted to tarry even the time to finish it all; see siman 66 seif 9. Magen Avraham.

Under compulsion — it implies that even under compulsion from robbers or other compulsion he returns to the beginning; thus too in the responsum of Maharam Mintz siman 85. But Beit Chadash wrote: specifically when the person or the place is not fit to recite in it — that is compulsion; but under other compulsion he need not return to the beginning. And Magen Avraham wrote: it seems to me that in prayer, if he tarried the time to finish it all, even under other compulsion he returns to the beginning, for would that a person pray all day long. But in Keriat Shema he does not return unless the person or the place is not fit — see there.
