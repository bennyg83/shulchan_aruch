[1] And there are those who say, etc. — Beit Yosef wrote that thus wrote Rashbam in the name of Raavad — until here. And it appears one must say Rashba, for thus I saw in the novellae of Rashba, chapter "One whose dead," in the name of Raavad; but I wonder, for in Sefer Temim De'im siman 200 he brought this view of Raavad in the name of Me'or and challenged it and agreed with Rif, which is the first view. Tosafot wrote: it is very difficult — the congregation waits and is silent until the prayer leader finishes; except, however, if they do not wait the time to finish it all from beginning to end there is no concern, and we do not calculate from where he stands to its end — until here. And Magen Avraham wrote: even not because of compulsion it is forbidden ab initio to interrupt, even in the blessing of Keriat Shema; however, to answer Kaddish and Kedushah it is permitted to tarry even the time to finish it all.
