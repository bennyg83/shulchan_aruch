Se'if 1 in the gloss — he returns to the beginning. In the Yerushalmi, beginning of chapter 2: he interrupted a third and returned, and interrupted a third. And the commentator explains: the question was whether the waitings combine — if among all of them he waited long enough to complete the entire [Shema]. If we require that he interrupted in one utterance long enough to complete the entire [Shema]. Another question there: Keriat Shema and its blessings — is it one [unit], or its blessings and not it, or its blessings and not it — and he did not resolve it.
