[The Laws of] One Who Entered a Synagogue and Found the Congregation Reciting Keriat Shema, or Who Interrupted at the Time of Recitation — containing 3 se'ifim.
One who recited it out of order — that is, he began to recite and paused, whether through silence or through speech, and returned and finished it — even if he' tarried the time to finish it all, he has fulfilled his obligation, even if the pause was because of compulsion.And there are those who' say that if he was under compulsion and paused the time to finish it all, he returns to the beginning (Tosafot and Rosh, chapter Mi] she-meito, and Tur). And such is the custom. And we calculate the matter of tarrying per the one reciting and not per most people (Rashba, beginning of chapter Hayah korei). And thus it is below in siman 104.
