That they recite Keriat Shema — and the same law applies to anything else the congregation says, such as Tehillah le-David: one recites with them, for such is proper conduct (Tosefta and Shiltei Giborim in the name of a Gaon).

He should not interrupt — and the Bach rules that one interrupts even in the middle of the blessing of Keriat Shema to accept the yoke of the kingdom of Heaven with the congregation.
