Krias Shema. The same applies to any other passage that the congregation recites, such as Ashrei. He should recite it with them, for this is proper conduct. Tanya and Shelah.
Interrupt. Bach rules that one interrupts even in the middle of the blessings of Krias Shema in order to accept the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven with the congregation. Taz rules that during Pesukei DeZimrah one should interrupt, but during the blessings preceding Krias Shema one should not interrupt at all for the first verse of Krias Shema. Rather, he should then recite those words in the melody in which the congregation is reciting Krias Shema. See there. Peri Chadash likewise writes that one who rules that during the blessings of Krias Shema one should recite the first verse of Krias Shema with the congregation, following Bach, deserves rebuke.
