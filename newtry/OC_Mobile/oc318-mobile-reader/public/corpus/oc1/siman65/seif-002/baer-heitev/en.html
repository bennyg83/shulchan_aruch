Keriat Shema — the same applies to anything else the congregation says, such as Tehillah le-David; he recites with them, for such is proper conduct. Tanya and Shelah.

Should interrupt — and Beit Chadash ruled that he interrupts even in the middle of the blessings of Keriat Shema to accept the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven with the congregation. And Taz ruled that in psalms of praise one may interrupt, but in the blessings before Keriat Shema one may not interrupt at all for the sake of the first verse of Keriat Shema; rather then he says those words to the melody as the congregation says Keriat Shema — see there. Thus too Pri Chadash; and he wrote that one who rules in the blessings of Keriat Shema to recite the first verse of Keriat Shema with the congregation, as Beit Chadash holds, deserves rebuke.
