(8) He recited Krias Shema. Certainly, if he has not yet recited it, he must recite the first verse. He should not, however, intend thereby to fulfill the positive mitzvah of Krias Shema, since he is not wearing tefillin on his head and he will also be reciting it without its blessings, as explained above at the end of siman 46 in Mishnah Berurah. See there. It appears to me that even if this occurs before Bircas HaTorah, he may recite it, since he is not saying the verse for the purpose of Torah study. He should be careful, however, not to recite more than the first verse.
(9) When they recite Krias Shema. The same applies to any other passage the congregation recites, such as Ashrei or Aleinu. He should recite it with them, for this is proper conduct.
(10) The first verse. He should also recite, "Blessed is the Name of His glorious kingdom forever and ever." The Gra's view in his commentary is that he must recite all of Krias Shema with them. During Pesukei DeZimrah, however, it appears that one should not be stringent beyond the first verse, since the Mechaber in any event is stringent regarding interruption during Pesukei DeZimrah, as explained in the following ruling.
(11) He should not interrupt. The later authorities write that during the blessings Baruch She'amar and Yishtabach, and during Pesukei DeZimrah, one should interrupt in order to accept the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven with the congregation. During the blessings of Krias Shema, however, and likewise between their sections, one should not interrupt at all. Rather, he should recite those words in the same melody in which the congregation is reciting Krias Shema.
