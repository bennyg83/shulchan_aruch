If one recited Keriat Shema and entered the synagogue and found the congregation reciting Keriat Shema, he must recite with them the first verse, so that it not appear he does not wish to accept the yoke of the Kingdom of Heaven with his fellows. And the same applies if he is in the synagogue saying words of supplication or verses in a place where it is permitted to interrupt. But if he is engaged in a place where it is not permitted to interrupt — for example, from Barukh she-amar and onward — he should not interrupt; rather he should say the words he is saying at the time the congregation says the first verse, to the congregation's melody, so that it appear he is reciting with them.
