According to the need of the matter, etc. It implies that he holds that melacha on Chol HaMoed is according to the Torah, except that they handed it over to the Sages. And the Bach ruled like the Ramban, that whatever is not a need of the Moed and is not a davar ha'aved is forbidden according to the Torah; see there. And in siman 538 it implies that Beis Yosef holds that melachos of Chol HaMoed are rabbinic. And in any case its punishment is great, for we say: one who desecrates the moadim is as if he worships idolatry; and Rashi explained that this means Chol HaMoed. The Tanya wrote: one is obligated to honor it with food and drink and with clothing like other Yom Tov; end quote. And the Maharil wore the kuta and matron of Shabbos. And in the Mishnah at the beginning of chapter 4 of Sukkah we say: the Hallel and the joy [are] eight days; and see there. And in siman 188, se'if 7, it implies that eating fruit is sufficient; nevertheless it appears to me that l'chatchilah there is a mitzvah to eat bread; see siman 664. A place where they customarily were lenient even regarding something that is not a [davar ha']aved, and a sage came and forbade them even a davar ha'aved: another sage may not permit it for them (Maharashdam in Yoreh De'ah, siman 120; so it is).
