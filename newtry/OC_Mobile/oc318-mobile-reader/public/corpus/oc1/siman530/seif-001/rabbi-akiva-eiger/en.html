Magen Avraham s.k. 1: that the melachos of Chol HaMoed are rabbinic. And so it is implied at the end of siman 531, in that he ruled like Rav Ami to be lenient against Rav Huna. See the Tur there; and in siman 539, se'if 6, where he ruled the unresolved question to leniency, that they dig deep up to seven; see Beis Yosef there.
